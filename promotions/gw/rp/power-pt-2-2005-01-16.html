Date: 01-16-05 The room smells of stale cigarettes and left over food from a few weeks before; it smacked her in the face when she walked in. How can anyone live this way? She had hoped the bed was not in the same condition as the rest of the apartment. Surprisingly, the bedroom was immaculate. Now he lay on the bed, beads of sweat dripping down his beautiful form. Red with exhaustion and nerves, he rests his head on the wall and scratches his chest. Only a sheet separates his manhood from the cold night air that drifts into his apartment from the open window adjacent to him. He closes his eyes and smiles; how could he be so lucky? She was still there, only feet from him in the bathroom! Happiness, content, and confidence swirled deep inside of him. Meanwhile, in the bathroom, Aradia finishes lacing her corset. She turns and looks herself over in the mirror. Her make-up is smeared among beads of their sweat, while her hair is thrown about in what can only be known as the “just-been-fucked” style. As she dabs at the sweat and make-up with a towel, she laughs silently; she sure knows how to pick them. Once again, an inexperienced boy with better moves than most “experienced” men. Who would have known when she pulled that boy from the warehouse, that he’d be so good? She grabs her duster and pulls her make-up bag from inside and quickly reapplies her face minimally. As she presses her lips together and blows, she studies her darkened form in the mirror. Blackened eyes and lips accentuated by red blush resting lightly on her face. She reaches back into her duster and pulls out a hair tie. She pulls her long raven hair back; leaving two chunks in the front, and then ties the hair at the top of her head. Putting the duster back on, she studies herself in the mirror once again. A sly smile creeps upon her face as she grabs the wallet from the counter. “Hey! Are you almost done in there!” I’m ready for another go!” The voice from outside startles her at first, but she quickly regains her composure. Temptation grabs at her, calling her to return to the young stud for another go. Temptation waits, however, and she shoves the wallet in her pocket. She turns toward the window once again and makes her way towards it. Then she stops, a contemplative look on her face. She opens the medicine cabinet. She sifts through various over-the-counter drugs: Aspirin, Tums, and some No Doze. A container of acne cream nearly topples over one of the glass shelves, but she quickly catches it. Behind it she finds a package of condoms and a Polaroid of a naked middle aged man. One of her slender eyebrows arc and she looks back at the door in confusion. Silently and without question, she sets the acne cream back in its place and continues to search for any other goodies. She finds exactly what she was hoping for: A bottle of pain killers and some Valium. Snatching it quickly, she shoves it in her pocket, but not without noticing the bottle of Vagasil behind them. She steps back for a moment and stares at it in wonder. What the hell is he doing with Vagasil? Suddenly there is a knocking at the door and she turns quickly, startled. “Hey, are you almost done in there!” I’m ready!” Panicking, she quickly and quietly shuts the medicine cabinet and darts for the window. She throws one leg and then the other through the open window and lands on the fire escape. As she makes her way down the wet and slippery metal staircase, she hears the bathroom door fling open. She then hears the man scream in anger and she looks up to see his head out the window. “Where the fuck are you going!”” he yells after her. All she can do is laugh menacingly as she jumps over the railing and falls several feet down onto the street below. An impulse to run crosses through her mind, but she shrugs it off and begins to walk down the street. Her black combat boots pound on the thick, slippery cement as she rounds the bend to find herself looking at the main road once again. She sticks out her hand to flag a taxi which slowly approaches her. Somewhere in the distance, a metal door creaks open rapidly and slams against a brick wall. Knowing her escape will be narrow; she quickly hops into the taxi and shuts the door. The man races around the corner just in time to see her blow him a kiss goodbye from a distance. He falls to the ground, defeated, still unaware of his lost wallet and medication. As the taxi continues off into the distance, Aradia pulls the wallet from her pocket and counts the cash she has gained. Later in the evening, she’ll clean her finger prints from the wallets she pocketed and then plant them at various locations around town, keeping the cash. Pleased with her earnings, Xiomara puts the wallet back in her pocket. She turns to the window, peering through the glass with indifference. The golden lights above reflect in the rain puddles that line the street and then shine upon Aradia’s face. Her blank face peers at the homeless and the prostitutes. How is her life any different, seducing men for their wallets? She snarls and scratches at her wrist quickly. For a moment, her hand reaches for her dagger, but realizing her surroundings, she stops. Instead, she focuses on whatever she can, just as long as she can keep her mind off of everything else. She takes in the city life which exists outside of her own bubble, existing inside the back of a yellow cab. To those outside, she is just another passenger, one higher and of more importance. But to herself, she is just as bad as they are.
