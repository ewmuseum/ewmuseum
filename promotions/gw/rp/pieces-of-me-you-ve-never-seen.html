Last Week’s Evo Before Shadow’s match, after her confrontation with Dawn “looking for a savior in these dirty streets” Shadow slammed the door of Soma’s locker room, not caring whether or not she disturbed the occupant within. Right now, she was fiercely angry, and she didn’t care who knew it. “Soma! Are you here?” There was nothing but darkness all around her, darkness and silence’which irritated her immensely. A moment later, a quiet, deep voice answered. “Yes, I’m here.” He appeared out of the shadow with a serious look on his face. She looked up at him, a hurricane brewing in her green eyes. “Who the fuck does Dawn McFarlane think she is”! She spent the entirety of her WMW career ignoring me, and pretending like I was completely beneath her notice, and now she has the gall to act like this?!” Soma stepped closer to her, looking her over critically as he always did’each examination scientific and thorough, but at the same time, she could sense his desire radiating from him, as hers did from her. Soma let his hand brush through her hair as he took it off her shoulder, letting her realize he meant her no harm, but saw something in her. His small smile turned into a very cold face. 'Maybe she doesn't see you as under her now. Maybe she sees you like the immortal you are. This is how I see you.” Soma sniffed the air, for a minute looking away from her but then with the same dominant eyes as before. 'Is there something different about you?' A slight smirk came over Shadow's face as she looked at him. 'You told me to come to you when I was ready to learn, did you not?' She held up her hands. 'Here I am.' Soma's eyes turned blood red and he grabbed her pulling her close to him with a slight grin once again on his face. 'Are you sure you want what I have to give? I smell my blood within you but I need to be certain.' She growled in a rather feral manner under her breath, a slightly off-color reaction to his pulling her closer, but one that cropped up within her like wild instinct. 'And what test must I past to show you that I'm interested?' Soma tilted his head to where his neck was almost to Shadow's lips. 'My blood does flow in you but not enough. I think you know what must be done.' Shadow was practically overwhelmed at this close proximity by the essence of him, which she'd been trying to get out of her head for two entire weeks now. But now, the pull was overwhelming- she unsheathed her fangs reflexively and bit into his neck almost lovingly. As the blood started to flow into her mouth, she made a quiet, almost overwhelmed noise- at no other time had she been so completely at his mercy as she was now. Soma closed his eyes from the pleasure of the bite, running his hand through her hair. He held her to his neck, having never felt the bite of a vampire; the feeling almost overtakes him before minutes later, he pulled her off the wound she made. 'You and I are forever linked now. Your lessons will start now and you will gain power unlike you have ever imagined.' Shadow was still rather taken aback by the situation. She paused for a few moments, wiping the last drop of blood from her lips and licking it off her fingertip. A smile came over her face. 'I'm honored to be considered your student.' Her voice dropped almost to a sultry purr, but the sentiment behind it, at least, was sincere. Soma looked down at his chest as blood almost paints the tattoo. He looked at Shadow with a devilish smile on his face. 'A lot comes with being my student Shadow; like you may call me Leinad. I also will expect more from you than just being my student.' He looked her over once more the smile seeming to also show the lust that was more than apparent in his eyes. A smirk traced itself over her features. 'Like...?' Soma's smile, the largest it had been in a while seemed to take on the full heat of his lust and the look in his eye was horribly striking. 'In becoming my student you have become Eve to the shadows of man. We will keep each other 'company' if you will for now. But who knows about the future. You, could be the Black Madonna to a child of the oblivion.' An eyebrow arched across her forehead. 'Now there's a thought I'll have to get used to...' She smirked slightly, realizing quite suddenly that she may have gotten herself into something that she's not quite prepared for. But, showing that would have been quite out of character for her, and she didn't intend to do so. As if her thoughts were made clear he kissed her gently, wrapping his arms around her waist, his body warm against hers. 'These things come with time. Right now your training is more important. When each of your new abilities shows themselves, I shall teach you to master it. Do you have any questions?' She smiled slightly, shaking her head. 'For now, I'm quite content to follow your lead.' For the moment, she looked up at him with a measure of trust- something that's not all too common for her. And for a moment, Dawn's words ran through her mind, but she smirked, slightly smugly at that.
