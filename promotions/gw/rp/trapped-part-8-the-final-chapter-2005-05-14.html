Trapped (Part 8): The Final Chapter?

14 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14th May 2005 Evil? It is not as clear cut as it is in the movies. All the movies Hollywood produces there is a clear divide between who the good guys are and who the bad guys are. There is no in between ground in the movies. This is so the audience know who to cheer and who to hate. This is true in wrestling today, it was not as clear cut as it was in the 80’s but there are still the good guys and the bad guys. So which am I? Most people would say I am the good guy, but I would not agree. You see every single one of us is a mix of good and evil, if we only were good or if we were only evil then there would be an imbalance. That is why no one can be truly good or evil. I try to lead a good life but I am sure there are times when I have done something that to many people would be seen as evil or wrong. For example leaving my mother to go pursue my dream career that could be seen as heartless and evil. The reason I talk of “evil” is because of the last 3 to 4 months where there has been one man who stands in the way of me finding my family, Mike Biddle? Biddle can be seen as uncaring, heartless but is he truly evil? Even though he has put me through hell the passed few months I still don’t see him as evil. The crowds at the arena obviously see him as the bad guy and me as the good guy in this whole debacle but I understand the guy is only human. I still think of him as an arrogant prick who is the scum of the earth but he obviously has his reasons. He obviously values wealth over any sort of morals and that is his choice. At first I didn’t think he realised that Wood was keeping my family, I did actual believe him at first when he said he had no idea what I was talking about but then I looked him in the eyes and I could see the lie. That is what infuriates me the most that he could see the torment I was going through yet he did nothing to help me because he valued the money more than my happiness. Again his choice but the wrong one. Now I have him backed into a corner, no escape, if I climb that ladder at Wrestlecade and garb that belt the game is over he has to tell me where my family is. This match at Wrestlecade is not about the title for me, it is about my family, sure it would be nice to be a champion again but I want my family more and that is what will drive me to victory? There is of course the possibility that neither Biddle nor myself come out the victor in this match after all it is a triple threat match and the third man is none other than Corvus. Corvus is a powerful man there is no doubt about that, he goes into this match with only one thing on his mind and that is walking out the GW Platinum Champion. He has nothing else riding on this match except the championship. Corvus does not care for my situation and in my opinion that will be his weakness. He does not know how desperately I need this victory, he does not know how much I need to win this match. He may think my mind is fogged with thoughts of my family but the truth is my family is what will keep me going. All Biddle has going for him is the thought of losing all the money Wood has given to him, now that is a distraction. I know all 3 of us will put are all in to this match to come out the Platinum Champion but don’t forget I am going into this match with more than just the championship on the line. I am going into this match to win my life back and come Wrestlecade that is exactly what I am going to do? I looked at the clock ticking away on the wall to the right of my steel bed. It was 4:46 in the afternoon and I had been waiting for Dr. Wood the passed 15 minutes. He was running late which was unusual for him, to give him something he was always punctual but not today. What was keeping him? I stood up and began to pace up and down, the only sound I heard now was the sound may bare feet made as the hit the steel floor. I stopped and turned towards the two way mirror where I knew Wood’s people were watching me. “Where the hell is he?” I asked impatiently. “Who?” One of Wood’s male workers replied over the speakers. “The pope, who do you think I mean moron” Wood, where is Dr. Wood?” I said sarcastically. “He is erm”,” There was a pause some muffled speaking heard as the worker conversed with his fellow workers. “He is stuck in traffic, he said he was about 5 minutes away, so pleased just be patient Mr. Myers.” “Patient” Patient?!”!” I exclaimed, “I have been stuck in this steel box for nearly 4 months now and you expect me to be bloody patient! Does Wood not realise that I need to go to the gym to train for my match at Wrestlecade?” That is when it hit me. Of course Wood didn’t want me to go to gym because he didn’t want me to train, because he didn’t want me to win at Wrestlecade. If I won at Wrestlecade he would have to tear up that contract and he would lose me. He wanted me to be unprepared for the match so that Biddle or Corvus could take the victory and leave me stuck in here where he can continue driving me insane. Well I was not going to let me happen? “Open this door!” I yelled now standing at the door of my cell. “We can’t do that Mr. Myers. You will have to wait for Dr. Wood to come.” “Do you think I am an idiot” I know Dr. Wood is in the building , he is probably watching me on CCTV right this minute. Now you either open this door or you get Dr. Wood down here right now, either way I am getting out this room in the next minute.” “How are you going to do that Mr. Myers?” Came the worker’s reply over the speakers with an arrogant tone. I moved over to the two way mirror and placed my hands on the mirror feeling it for weakness I felt a slight give as I brushed my left hand over a position on the mirror. I smirked. “How safe do you think you are behind that mirror?” I said still with the smirk on my face. “You couldn’t.” “Oh yeah?” I took a step back keeping my eyes on the position that my left hand I just felt. With all my might I launched a straight kick right into the mirror. I took a step back and the mirror stood in front of me, still complete. There was laughter from the other side of the mirror. I simply looked up and shook my right hand index finger at them as the spot I had just hit shattered into a thousand pieces and fell to the floor. The laughter instantly stopped. “Now are you going to get Dr. Wood or am I getting out of this room through there.” “He is on his way.” Came a very scared reply. With in an instant the door slid open and in stepped Dr. Wood, unaccompanied this time. He had a very stern look on his face as he moved over to me and looked at the shattered glass on the floor. “What are you doing Adam?” Wood asked still looking at the shattered glass. “Getting your attention, worked didn’t it?” I flashed a sarcastic smile Wood’s way before I moved over to the wash basin and splashed some water in my face. I turned to Wood, “So when am I going to the gym?” “In a minute,” Wood stated, “but first about your match at Wrestlecade.” “What about it?” I questioned as I grabbed a towel and dried my face. “I don’t agree with the stipulation between you and Biddle.” “Well that is too bad because Biddle agreed to it, so you have to abide by it.” “I am not sure I do.” Wood said bringing back that arrogant smirk of his. “What do you mean?” “Well, Biddle is not necessarily one of my workers; he is a man I hired as an independent agent. So although he agreed to the stipulation doesn’t mean I have to go with it because he is not one of my workers.” “Oh you are going to abide by it.” I stated not rising to Wood’s attempt to get me angry. “And why is that?” I moved up close to Wood so that we were toe to toe. “Because when I win Wood there is not a man or woman alive that is going to stop me from leaving this place.” Wood just stared at me neither of us blinking. Wood finally broke the stare and laughed. “Okay fine Adam but that is only if you win.” “Oh I will.” I smiled as I moved over to my hanging bio-suit, “Now if you would direct me to the gym that would be great.” As I slipped on my suit I looked at Wood and I could tell that I had got him. He walked out of the door and I quickly followed placing my mask on just as I stepped outside of the door. I looked at Wood who pointed down the hall not saying a word. He looked like a defeated man. “See you later Wood.” I smirked behind my mask as I walked down the corridor. As I reached the gym door I looked back at Wood. He still stood in front of the door with his head down deep in thought. At that moment in time I would have loved to known what was going through his mind? Dammit! Why did Biddle agree to such a stupid stipulation? If I lose Adam then I lose the contract and my company is back to square one again! I can’t let that happen there has to be something I could do, something I could change. There isn’t though, all I can do is watch the match and pray for Biddle or Corvus to win. The door slid shut behind me as I reached into my inside jacket pocket and pulled out my mobile phone. I looked down the corridor to make sure Adam had disappeared. I punched the number in for my P.A, Cindy. The phone rang for what seemed like forever before Cindy answered. “Hello, Dr. Wood’s office, Cindy Watts speaking how can I help?” Cindy said with a bright cheery tone. “Cindy?” “Yes?” “It’s Dr. Wood.” “Oh, hello sir.” “What took you so damn long to answer the phone?” “I was just typing up some reports sir.” That was a plain face lie, I knew exactly what she was doing. She was talking to her boyfriend in Germany over the internet, topping up my phone bill. She thinks she can get away with it but if I ever find the need to fire her I had one good reason. I decided to let it slide and let her go this time. “Okay that is fine Cindy but I need you to send for a limousine to go pick my daughter up and then come pick me up. Now I am going to be about 10 minutes so when I step out of this building I expect to see the limousine waiting, got it?” “Yes sir.” “Good.” I said as I disconnected the call. I took a deep breath and walked down the corridor towards the exit. “Doctor Wood”!”!” Someone shouted after me. Reluctantly I turned around to come face to face with one of my workers. From his name tag his name was Gary and looked like he was about to wet his pants in fear of me. “Yes?” I said impatiently. “Erm’err’erm?” “Spit it out!” I exclaimed feeling agitated by his presence. “What do you want us to do with Adam Myers?” “What do you mean?” “Well he is becoming more and more erratic. His behaviour is all over the place. I mean you saw what he did to the mirror, how can we control him?” “You can’t.” I laughed, “Let Myers do what he wants. Let him wear himself out, less chance of him winning at Wrestlecade right?” “Right.” Gary weakly smiled back at me. “Now go fix that mirror.” I said waving the boy away. As quick as a flash of light the boy was already halfway up the corridor as I turned around and went through a set of doors into the office area of the building. The phones were ringing and my associates were frantically answering them and blaring out the same old sale pitch I had used for the company for 15 years. I gave a few of the workers a friendly nod as to give them some glimmer of hope in their pathetic lives. I placed my sunglasses on as I stepped into the outside world. The sun was shining and the birds were singing, as I had asked in front of me was my limousine with the engine purring away. I opened the door and stepped into the back. In the back sat like a good little girl was my beautiful daughter Helen. Her bright blue eyes lit up as she saw me and gave me a huge hug. Her blond curly hair wafted in my face as I returned the hug. “Hi daddy.” Helen smiled. “Hi sweetheart, you had a nice day.” I asked as I closed the limousine door. “Yeah, I learned about the Civil War today in class.” “That’s great.” I beamed like a proud father. “How was your day, daddy?” “Eventful.” I put on a fake smile, hiding the worry. “Where to sir?” Came the drivers voice over the intercom. “Mike Biddle’s residence.” I replied. Smoothly the car moved away as I kissed my daughter on the forehead. I slowly opened my briefcase and brought out a contract. A contract that showed I had rights to Adam Myers. A sick feeling filled my stomach? One day this contract would be null and void and Myers would be gone, I knew that but would it be sooner rather than later? It all depends on Myers at Wrestlecade, and if I know Adam, defeat is not an option.
