Crappy Hype

4 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1.4.06 We open to a wrestler in the Global octagon. He's struggling to get to his feet as sweat drips down his face, stinging his eyes. The edges of the scene are fuzzy, in a haze. 'Your eyes are heavy. The air is thin. Your heart pounds so hard that you're sure it's going to come out of your chest and onto the mat right in front of you. ' The wrestler attempts to pull himself up with the aid of the ropes, but is met with a thudding blow to the back. We can not see the attacker, but the effect is obvious as the wrestler drops to the mat and screams in agony. 'Your will quickly fades away as the pain engulfs your senses. You have no defenses. You are helpless' The wrestler is grabbed by his long blonde hair and peeled up off of the mat, where he is met with a spinning back chop to the side of his head. Again, we only see the hand and arm of the attacker and the wrestler falling to a heap on the mat. He clutches at his neck as the pain sears throughout his entire body, and he silently prays for mercy. He fights his way to a knee and whips his hair back. We quickly zoom straight into his eye and we see how he sees. We hear the heart pumping and the gasping breaths. We hear the roar of the crowd, and we feel the pain searing through our body. We slowly stand on weakened legs, barely able to keep our balance. We turn slowly. Ace Cash stands before us with a cocky sneer on his face. He charges, but we are too weak to defend. He's in the air and his knee is flying towards our face. We hear the sickening crunch of our nose breaking and we are rocketed to the mat, where our head bounces viciously. All is hazy, as if we were dreaming. The crowd suddenly seems to fall quiet and our eyes slowly shut, but not before we look up and see Ace Cash standing over our prone body with a smirk on his face. He says something that we cannot quite hear right before all fades to black...
