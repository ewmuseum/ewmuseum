Trials and Tribulations

28 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/28/05 “Death does not concern us, because as long as we exist, death is not here. And when it does come, we no longer exist.” ? Epicurus “Is everything set to my exact specifications?” Soma asked as he was walking into his backyard early that morning. “Yes Mr. Soma.” The backyard consisted of seventy acres of Texas wilderness. He began to walk deep into the woods as his new assistant Daria followed at a distance. He smiled at the girl’s fear. He looked back at her with a look of assurance that he would not be making her a meal anytime soon. She was wearing a black pinstriped suit and leather boots. Her jet black hair came down to her shoulders and was neatly combed to cover the left side of her milky white face. Soma loved having her around, not for anything physical but having the smell of youthful blood in his nostrils. He kept his stride long that she almost had to keep at a fast paced walk to keep up. “I want her to have to work for this victory. I want her opponents to realize this is to the death.” “Yes Mr. Soma.” Soma looked up at the sun as it warmed his body with its rays. He stood there for a second letting the experience take him over. He finally attained the next form in his evolution. He was now ready. His mind thought back to Shadow. Was she to be the one to carry his next child? Would she be able to go through the training that she was about to endure? How will Virgil and Dawn effect her decisions? Would she turn on him? Would she? “Mr. Soma, are you ok?” Soma looked at Daria and smiled pulling him out of his trance. “Yes I’m fine. It’s just I appreciate the sun more than most. It gives me hope that I will achieve my destiny. But let us get back to the situation at hand. Her strength and her natural abilities should have increased by now so have each of her opponents injected with the blood of Maximillian’s heart. He was rather strong and I think it will be good for them as they might go insane.” “Yes Mr. Soma.” “As for her final opponent, he is another matter. He has not eaten for days and he should be just right for tonight’s festivities.” “Yes Mr. Soma.” He stopped walking and turned to face her. The girl froze with a look of fear in her eyes. They differed much in size. He stood there in his cream colored mandarin collared linen suit. He just watched her as she looked at him still not moving from fear. “I like an assistant with personality. You are not a robot. You are mortal. Act as such.” “Yes. Mr. Soma.” He smirked and shook his head. The girl had much to learn but she would learn. She would become Shadow’s guardian. One day she will not need one but Soma was the only vampyre who did not need a daylight guardian. They followed the trail passing various metal cages with all kinds of sounds coming from each. “Do you think Ms. Shadow will get all the way through Mr. Soma?” “I hope so.” “What happens if she doesn’t?” She says in a quiet voice. Soma looked at her admiring her boldness, his red hair swaying in the wind. “Then she will die and my search will continue.” There was silence for a couple of minutes before Soma finally stopped. He looked at the woman that walked behind him once more. “Go and see to my alterations and make sure everything is ready for tonight. If there are any flaws you will be terminated.” “Yes Mr. Soma.” “No I think you to realize if anything at all goes wrong, I blame you. And then your life is forfeit.” “Ye’Yes sir.” The girl walked off her pace somewhat quickened by the last words of her employer. He was alone for the first time in while. Silence. The rays of the sun playing in between the clouds and trees like small children. This is what he wanted to bring into the world. It was a feeling of serenity and peace. It is all Soma ever wanted but knew could first only be obtained by destruction. This night was important because if Shadow was victorious this night, she would one day be suited to be his mate, and their child would bring true peace through utter destruction. The idea made him lust for her that moment a feral growl escaping him as if his inner beast would soon be let out. The day for him and Shadow was a long time coming, but a small twisted smile came to his face as he saw and felt her thoughts. He knew of her feelings for Dawn and Virgil. He saw other developments he could dabble in until his time came. Soma thought about how easily Virgil joined Rex Deus. Did he realize Soma’s true ability? Could he sense a potential rival or threat? Either way, Soma would keep him just close enough to be able to kill him if he decided to become a turncoat. As for Dawn, she was unique in her own way. He sensed the tinge of vampires in her and yet not their essence. He would love to stain his teeth with her blood. He would love to show her the ways of oblivion and then have her be Shadow’s closest confidant. The more he thought about it, it seemed as if destiny were forming all these unique creatures around Shadow and not himself. For only a moment, fear struck him before his thoughts were interrupted. “You really think this Shadow will survive the trials you have set for her.” He looked over his left shoulder seeing a lean, feminine figure, covered by the shadow of a nearby tree. The shadow was to dark for the sun to penetrate through. He shrugged off the woman’s comment with a smug smile. “You wouldn’t be here if you thought I believed otherwise. You must really fear her.” “You don’t realize the chain of events that you want to bring about. You think you do but you have lost reasoning. This won’t only affect those mortals. This will affect us all; you, the vampyre nation, your vampiric kin, me, and your precious Shadow, “Her name is Lia. It is a name you need to remember. And from the glorious ashes of this dismal world, a great empire will rise, one created by the child by two pure sources; her being mortal will give the child a soul, her being turned into a Cainite, having given her training under the watchful eye of the Tzimsce promised to make her ready to receive the blood of a vampyre. She has blood from both races of the monsters you and I created.” The figure was quiet for a second. “I see. I finally see. You want to die. You have finally had enough. You’d risk killing us all so you can end?” The silence was for minutes. Soma looked away from the sun and turned his back to female. “You have been around as long as I have, if not longer. You’ve never been tired” You never wanted an end? You all may think I’m crazy, but I have invested a lot of time and money into the prophecies, of man, vampire, and vampyre alike. We all know there is an end, a final jihad, a war to end all wars. I am the storm before the hurricane. You fear the end. I’m looking to a new world. You only care about yourself and the petty lives of children who hate their creators. If I have to die?.if millions have to die?..then so be it.” His ears lightly heard a drop of water hit the ground. It was a tear from the darkness. “You’ve gone mad Leinad and you must be stopped.” “Maybe so”.but you can not do that on your own and you have not brought anyone with you.” “I’ll always love you.” “Tis a shame I can not say the same.” As soon as the darkness appeared, it was gone. He was once again alone in the wilderness. He sat there in silence, thinking of the words that now rang in his head. He looked up to the sky. “What must be done, will be done.” “I know indeed what evil I intend to do, but stronger than all my afterthoughts is my fury, fury that brings upon mortals the greatest evils.?- Euripides His hunger was at it’s height that night as the red moon rose from the wilderness behind Soma’s large estate. He stood at the rear entrance of his mansion looking at the moon. Shadow stood beside him very quiet looking at the moon as well. He could feel her nervousness, and a part of him was nervous also. Something didn’t feel right; he could sense it in the air. “Tonight is the first of many trials that I will lay before you Lia. Be on your guard. You will face great challenges. I have faith that you will overcome. There has been a couple who were thought to possess what I see in you, and they have all met their demise. ? Shadow looked at Soma, crossing her arms. “I look forward to the challenge.” At Soma’s feet laid a rather large, old looking ebony colored chest. He bent down and opened it. They both looked inside to see a bundle of silver stakes, a shotgun, seven small vials with an unknown, red liquid in them, and a katana. Soma started to pick up each weapon and looked up at Shadow, her green eyes fixed on each weapon as Soma explained them. “This first weapon should not be of any surprise, these silver stakes were melted down from a silver cross from the Hierarchy Cathedral in Rome. These stakes will paralyze our kind but nothing more. The next weapon is a little unique. This shotgun was made by Malachi and Samuel Grimm, brothers who have made it their life’s work to eradicate supernatural beings. This automatic shotgun holds seven rounds. This particular model shoots off phosphorus rounds. It’s like shooting sunlight into a vampire. Do not worry; your armor will make sure you stay safe enough, unless they get through it. Make sure they don’t. These vials are synthesized vampyre blood; they should help you with stamina throughout the night. Be careful though, you have a limited number and you have a long night ahead of you. And finally the last weapon, this katana was forged by Orion De Salvo, one of the world’s greatest blacksmiths. As a young vampyre he crafted the spear that would one day kill the Christ. It is said that Orion’s weapons kill the body and the very soul of a creature. This katana is the greatest weapon in your arsenal. It is sharper than any weapon you have ever used. Be mindful of your surroundings, keep your senses sharp, and keep your mind open. Do you have any questions?” Shadow stood almost in a trance at the katana. It looked so plain and yet held so much power. She reached down and closed her hands around the hilt of the katana. She shook her head, as to answer Soma’s question. He smiled as he turned to face Shadow face to face. He leaned in, and lightly brushed her pale skin with his lips. He whispered in her ear. “I have great faith in you. Don’t prove me wrong. If something goes wrong I will be there to aid you.” “Thank you.” He nodded slightly as she began to pick up each weapon and fasten it to her armor in its designated place. She began to walk the path into the wilderness and Soma watched her in silence. When she was finally out of sight he pulled out a cell phone. “Let the test begin.” 'It is change, continuing change, inevitable change, that is the dominant factor in society today. No sensible decision can be made any longer without taking into account not only the world as it is, but the world as it will be.'-Issac Asimov It had been almost an hour and she hadn’t seen anything. She knew she should have been at least attacked by now. She also knew this is what Soma would want her to be thinking. It was like he was mentally making her do his will. Either way she still had not seen anything in these woods. She looked up at the moon, tonight was special. The red moon, which she had learned in her studies, was the blood moon to the vampyre nation. It heightened their abilities as well as made their lust for blood even stronger. She stood there in its glow her armor covering her from her throat down. The armor had been specially made to fit her perfectly. It had been made of a special fabric that could not be torn into easily. She could tell it was made by Soma’s corporation. Anything he had a hand in left his aura on it. She could feel him watching him, his eyes looking down on her. Even when he didn’t mean to he had an air about him that made him seem superior to others. She looked back toward the large mansion, which looked like a model from where she stood. As she began to turn around she heard a small sound come from further down the path. She looked into the direction in which the sound came from. From her distance she could only make out a small shadow but it seemed to be getting larger, and was moving at an alarming rate right in her direction. She went to pull the katana from its sheath as the creature jumped from about ten feet away and landed on her. It was a male of average height, pale skin, and at the moment foaming at the mouth on top of her. She kicked the creature in the groin and pushed it off her. He backed away slowly and she jumped to her feet pulling the sword from its sheath. They stood not moving for a few minutes, both catching their breath. The male began to growl at her and lunged again. She sidestepped the male and swung the katana catching him in the throat, ending his life with a cut Agra. The body burst into ashes and she continued her walk down the path. “Is this the best he is gonna do” Really Leinad I thought you would send better.” She gave a slight smirk. “Actually the creatures you face will get harder Lia. That was just the first of several. He is what happens when a vampyre bites a human. They are like the ghouls of our vampire cousins except they can create as well, bringing creatures that are far worst than them.” She stood there shocked as she heard the voice of Soma coming from a pin that was placed on her armor. “Two way radio, very smart Leinad.” “Thank you.” She continued down the path now her green eyes constantly checking around her. “So you are just watching me aren’t you?” She said into the pin. “For now’yes. I’m also here to answer questions and give you background information on the creatures you come in contact with.” “Oh lovely.” She said with some dry humor. “I wouldn’t be so sarcastic; here comes your next opponents.” Between all the talking she hadn’t been paying attention and two females stood in front of her. She prepared for an attack but the two females just stood there. She looked at them; the first had brown hair that was in a ponytail. Her eyes were, as Shadow knew all her opponents eyes would be, crimson. She wore a black leather jacket, a pair of jeans, and black boots. The second female looked exactly like the other female but was dressed entirely different. She wore a fencing uniform. “You’ve got to be kidding me.” “No, there is no joke in these two. These twins are the children of a powerful vampyre lord. They are said to be unstoppable when it comes to swords.” “Fine. They want swords, I have something for them.” Shadow sheathed her katana quickly and as the twins charged her, she pulled the shotgun out and shot both vampyres in the chest. “Bravo Shadow, I didn’t know if you had it in you to do that.” She picked up the two swords, carrying them with her down the path. She had made it through half of the wilderness and had only had to face three very easy trials. It all seemed too easy to her. As soon as she had that thought she heard something growl. The growl sounded more wolf like as they got closer. She drank three of the vials, and as she prepared once again to fight a large werewolf walked from out of the darkness. She dropped the two swords she had acquired, holding her own sword with both hands. The wolf stood about eight feet tall. His fur was black with spots of brown in it. His eyes were the same crimson as each of the vampyre she fought. She knew if she did not act quickly she would not be able to act at all. She rushed the animal, trying to push the sword into his chest. When she got close enough, the wolf grabbed her, arms first and tossed her into a nearby tree. As her body came into impact with the tree she dropped the sword. The animal wasn’t finished he picked her up and threw her into another tree. She was dazed from being tossed around but she slowly tried to get up. The large wolf caught her before she made it all the way up and kneed her in the stomach. He slammed his fist into the lower part of the back sending her into the dirt once more. “Get up wyrm. It is time for you to meet your end.” The large wolf spoke with a hint of joy in his voice. “I’m not done yet. You underestimate me and you will suffer for that.” She spoke as she spit blood from her mouth. She began to stand up; this time when she stood she looked the werewolf eye to eye. Her eyes burned red like a fire. She rushed the wolf taking him off his feet. They wrestled on the ground for a few minutes before they pushed away from each other, and both of them jumped to their feet. He growled at her and she growled back. She pulled a silver stake from off her belt and stood ready. He lunged at her once again, and as soon as he landed on her she drove the silver stake into his chest as hard as she could. The wolf let out a wail as its body became limp. “Are you trying to kill me”! That was a werewolf. You said nothing of making me fight a werewolf.” There was quiet from the other end of the radio. “Hello” Leinad?” “Your radio won’t work anymore. I’m guessing it was smashed in your fight with Azar over there.” The voice was not familiar to her. She spun around and saw three males standing there in black suits with red bands on their left arms. “Who are you?” Shadow asked as she picked up her katana. “We are servants of the vampyre council. We are called the Crimson Knights. We were sent to kill you.” Shadow smiled, her eyes still a dark crimson. “Many have been sent to kill me and they have all failed.” The man smiled as well. “We are not your ordinary assassins.” “Ordinary, maybe not, but you are all foolish for coming here.” The man turned at the sound of another male’s voice. Soma had appeared behind the three males holding a sword. “Leinad, it has been a long time.” Soma stood there, his face showing nothing but contempt for the vampyre. “Gaetan, you will leave here now or die. You know you can not defeat me. None of you can.” Gaetan smiled, looking over his shoulder back at Soma. “You’re right. We shall leave, but remember this Leinad. You started this war, but we shall end it.” The three men returned into the darkness quietly, their forms becoming as shadows and then disappearing into thin air. “Who were those men” And why did they have a werewolf with them?” Soma sighed. “That was Gaetan Trask, head of the Order of the Crimson Knights. The Order was founded to stop potential threats to the vampyre nation.” “But why were we attacked by them. Aren’t they your people?” “Yes but they see the future I have dreamed as their end. Now they want me, and anyone associated with my ideals dead.” “I could have taken them, why did you interfere.” “Because I trained them. You would have been killed. The next time you and Gaetan meet you will be ready I assure you. Let us go inside, your test is over, you did far better than I hoped. Tonight you killed a werewolf single-handed. And he was a Crimson Knight. Bravo.” “Thank you.” “You’re welcome. Now let us go, we have much to discuss.” They began to walk the long path back toward the mansion. They both walked in silence. “We are the product of 4.5 billion years of fortuitous, slow biological evolution. There is no reason to think that the evolutionary process has stopped. Man is a transitional animal. He is not the climax of creation.'-Dr. Carl Sagan
