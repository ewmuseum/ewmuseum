[:Reload:] Return to faith...

11 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-11-06 [OOC: good god I'm rusty] `=`=`=`=`=`=`=`=`=`=`=`=`=`=`=`=`=`=`=`=`=`=`=`=`=` “It can be said that true wisdom comes but from the mouths of babes” Those of innocence speak only the truth, as they do not know any better. Heed their wisdom.” With only a few days left we find ourselves in the presence of one man, Mr. Kris Kanton. He stands alone on a windy day in Louisville, KY. Waterfront Park is his location as he stares off across the Ohio River. The sky is gray with tinges of white, as the sun cannot be seen. In this location we find ourselves standing at the riverbank by the statue galleria which is on a higher plane than the surrounding green field. Shoes shuffle across the gravel as couples walk by talking and laughing. Their laughter cheers Kanton up for only a moment until he realizes that he no longer shares in such human contact. His smile fades as he looks to the field at his right. Many kids play a fun game of pickup football. The weather has been nice this year and because of that people have been able to enjoy some of the nicer parts of the city for a bit longer of a time. The wind picks up for a moment as Kanton slides the zipper up on his black running jacket. Many things cross the mind of the man we accompany. Thoughts of doubt cloud his mind whenever he contemplates his return to Global Wrestling. It has been a few years since his last time in the Global Arena. He had almost returned about a year and a half ago before signing with Four Winds Wrestling? a thing that part of Kanton regrets. He had changed during his time there? he grew bitter and cold. Just then Kanton’s thoughts cleared as he heard two voices that almost seemed directly behind him. “You ask him?” “No, you want the autograph” you ask him?” “But, he is so mean now” I don’t want to make him mad.” Kanton sighed deeply at the sound of this? even his own fans, the kids who he cherished as fans the most, had turned on him. Kanton turned around and eyed the shocked kids. Obviously they hadn’t realized that he had heard them. “Can I help you two?” Kanton asked the stunned kids. When taking notice of them you can tell that one is the older brother to the other as they both share similar characteristics are, but one is older than the other. The wind picked up again as the children’s jackets ruffled in the harsh cold wind. It was starting to get late in the afternoon and it was getting cold. Kanton saw the younger of the two children holding a what seemed to be a posed black and white eight by ten photo of Kanton from the last time he was in Global. “I’m sorry to bother you sir” it’s my brother, but don’t worry. I’m taking him home now.” The other brother responded to Kanton who was obviously taller than him. The younger brother, who seemed around eleven or so gulped and didn’t even say a word. “Hey, you” I take it you want me to sign that?” The keep turned white as if he was afraid Kanton was going to give him his signature move, the Fear Effect. Kanton only laughed and reached for the photo. The younger child handed it to him. With a grin Kanton pulled a sharpie from his jeans pocket and signed the photo. After handing it back to the kid the kid began to speak.” “You don’t seem as mean as you were on TV. You act like a good a nice guy” like you used to be.” “I’m just me kid...” “You weren’t you for a while. We didn’t know who you were anymore.” Kanton thought about this comment for a moment. It was true? towards the end there he wasn’t himself anymore. Just as he had thought earlier. “Don’t worry” I think I’m myself again.” “C’mon” mom is going to worry where we are.” With a wave the older brother pulled his younger brother away. With a wave Kanton watched as they began to walk off, but before they got very far the older kid turned around. “Hey, we really glad you’re back. Survive?” “If you can kid” If you can?, replied Kanton as the older kid turned around with a smile. A warm feeling returned to Kanton just then? It was a feeling he hadn’t experienced in a while. He was excited again? the hunger to battle had returned, as he could hear the ring calling to him. It was a new day? a new chance? and a new Kanton? Actually? the return of the old Kanton. Later that day Kanton boarded a plane from Louisville International Airport to London, England. During his trip Kanton returned to writing in his journal? something he hadn’t done in some time, but he felt the urge to return to the practice. What follows are the contents of that entry. Just days before Reload? Just days before his return to Global. +~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~+~~+ My good friend, It has been a while since my last entry? a really long time actually. It wasn’t intentional, but I was lost for a while. I think I have found myself once more though? I have some news! I have returned to the ring? but not to 4WW! Rather, I have returned to my old home of GW? Interesting eh? Last time I wrote to you I had retired, but times change, and I cannot ignore the call of the fans. I have returned to what I once was? I am now a renewed faith as even my fans have begun to forgive me. I can’t begin to explain how great the feeling is? I already have goosebumps. I make my debut in Manchester, England at GW’s Reloaded? in of all things, a giant cluster match. I, along with eighteen other men, am going to vie for the number one contendership to the Platinum Title. Most all of my opponents in this match are unknown to me? with the exception of one man, Kyo Kushinaga. A few others I have run across in my past, but they don’t hold the history that I do with Kyo. Multiple times I have faced defeat in the face of the Deathscythe Outlaw. I will do what I can to ensure that it won’t occur again this time. This is my new shot? might even be my last shot. I will write again with news in a few days, but until then wish me luck. Hopefully I won’t need it. Lights? Camera? Action? Survive? If you can. Your friend, Kanton
