Questions un-asnerwed. Part one

22 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-22-04 Ely sat there in the empty Dark Horse salon.. Empty all save the bartender and a few waitresses getting ready for the night. Ely sat in his corner smoking a cigar and slowly drinking a Smirnoff Ice; Black. He sat there thinking of recent actions and going ones? The loss he had with Briggs was not his first. The team they fought was a good team. But what was weighting heavy on his mind was a few recent actions? Race traitor Makonie called him. Braxton slowly turned the bottle in his hands staring into the milky white substance in it. Braxton had always been a more in depth looker at people? The outside was just a shell. All it was; was what carried a personas sprit. Thought Ely. A Person was not what they looked and appeared always. But what they did, how they lived, how they acted, and how they loved. But then again, Ely pondered, a Persons looks was just as important in some cases as the spirit it self. “You know Jack” Ely said breaking the silence. As the bartender Jack Wilde stopped what he was doing and turned at the sound of his name. Jack walked over to the table and sat down across from Ely. “Yeah Ely” Jack asked. “Maybe it was my time I spent working a lot with Talon Eagleclaw. But his family and Now I believe a person is more then what the look like” But then again that is very important to. ? Ely said. Almost in a sort of day dream. Jack scratched his head and looked at Ely, Now the world knows that Bartenders are the poor mans Psychiatrist. And Jack in his time had heard a lot. “Ely. Does it matter” Ye4ah I think it does. Not to the effect if every one is a perfect picture of humanity? But if they take care of there self’s. Live their life well, And Try and be a good person. But you can tell when a Person has confidence in there self.. They way they dress and take care of there self. IT complicated? But it is a total package. But I do know this.. The inside of a person is far more important then the outside.” Jack said and rose to his feet. “Thanks Jack. You’re right, as usual. Guys Like Makonie.. They piss me off they make me angry at things they say and believe. Now if he wishes to believe that stuff so is it. But who is he to judge me” Brick is my friend and has come to my aid many a time. Brick is a honorable and good man. ? Ely stated slamming his fist down on the table to emphasize his point. “Eye. Brick is a good man. I have known him for a while. He is a Good friend.” Jack said. The conversation ends as the door to the Saloon burst open and in walks an enraged Christina McCoy. “How dare he” HOW DARE HE!!!” She screams as she heads for the office. Jack and Ely look at each other and Jack goes back to work as fast as his old self can. Ely gets up and cuts her off before she gets there. “Chris.. what is the matter?” Ely asks. Christina states at Ely for moment. And though not many things scare Ely the look on her face made him want to run as far away and as fast as he could. “That” Train.. Guy. AARGH!” She Screamed. Ely tilted his head confused. “He screamed at me. HE” I thought he was going to hit me!” Christina said. “What” Going to hit? you? But he did not?” Ely asked. “NO.” Christina replied. “But for acting the way he just did I suspended him for a week.” “Hum” Well Okay. You have to do what you have to sometimes.. Umm Christina. I was wondering?” Ely question trailed off as Christina cut him off. “Yes Ely.” Ely started into her pretty blue eyes. The same eyes he had met five years ago when she was at the age of sixteen.” “Well?” Christina pressed Ely for a answer.” Ely stood there not really knowing what to say to this woman.. He wanted to ask her to go on a date with him but, “Well I was wondering, if I could get a match this week. I was hoping to get a hold of Makonie.” He asked instead of the one he wanted to ask. Christina looks a little doubtful about the question but nodded to her self more then anything. What was that.. Ely wondered. Could she her self like him the same as he did her? “You got one. Did Logan Not tell you?” She asked. “Ely. Umm No. He and Jesse went fishing and camping.” Ely responded looking dumbfounded. “Well this Monday you’re facing Mark Makonie. After what he said last week, I figured you be wanting some retribution.” “Makonie Good.. Real good’Thanks. ? Braxton said. Christina started to head away but stopped as Ely called he name again. “Chris. Any word from Bubba?” Ely asked. Christina lowered her head? “No, Nothing. Not since I got that tape” She said taking a deep sighing breath. “Well I have sent Talon the tape” If any one can figure something out from it then he can.” Ely said reassuringly Christina smiled and ran to Ely and hugged him. Ely lost for what to do hugged her back’Again he had her in his arms. Smelt her hair. It always smelt like French Vanilla. Christina looked up and just as she was about to say something? the door burst open to the Saloon.. Christina and Ely turned to see standing there the large Indian known as Talon Eagleclaw. Talon staggered out of the bright light causing him to be shrouded in shadow and fell to his knees. Blood pouring down his head. A waitress screamed and the two ran to Talon. “Talon! What happened” Who did this!” Ely shouted at his friend. “Found Bubba. I know” ? Talon said as he slipped into unconsciousness. “Ohh my god.” Christina said. Ely leaped to his feet as the sound of tires peeling in the gravel out side was hear.. Ely raced to the doors to see a black car pulling off, the license plate missing. Ely raced to his truck. “DAMN!!” Ely cursed as the tired of his truck had been flattened. Ely Stormed back in to the Saloon. :The cops are on there way Ely.. So is a ambulance. Did you see who did this? ? Christina asked. “No. All I saw was a plane black car with no license plate. That was it. My tires were popped, keeping me from chasseing them. Who ever did this knew what they were doing. This Christina was a message. ? Ely said sourly. “Who would do this?” Christiana asked. “I don’t know, but Bubba has his fair share of enemies. Who ever it is don’t want him found though that is for certain.” Both of them looked worriedly down on Talon as the sirens sounded in the distance. The police were on their way. Only things Ely could think of though was who did this and how was all this linked? Some one had an answer and he would find it. --------- To be continued ------------
