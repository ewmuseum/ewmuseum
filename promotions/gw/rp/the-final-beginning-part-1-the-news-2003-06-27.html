The Final Beginning. Part 1 - The news

27 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27/6/03 With everything he had done in life, he had set out to be the best and running Global Incorporated was no different at all. His attitude towards it was the more that he put into it, the more he would be able to get out of it in the end when he finally sold his half of the federation to the highest bidder. And he knew that one day that high bid would come and it wouldn't matter what his the guy's plans were for GW, he would sell it to him anyway. What was the point in keeping something that you knew you had done all you could for? None. But that time wasn't yet for Salaok. Not but a long shot, even though the bidders did continue to knock on his door and make attempts at buying out his half. But there was still more to be done, that was as long as he could blind side McCoy and not let his little daughter get in the way of what could possibly be the one thing that funds their retirement. Butt he day had come to an end and now he sat in the drivers seat of his newly refurbished hearse. After Holmes had set it on fire a month ago, the car had never been the same so Salaok did the only thing he knew how when it came to fixing things. Throw heaps of money at someone to do the job and make sure that they do it the way they are meant to. The lights of the cars passing him by on the other side of the road brought brief relief from the dull darkness which had fallen over the city as the day time had lost its last bit of sun and the night sky had invaded for the next fourteen hours or so. Leaning over he presses the cigarette lighter into the dash and while it heats up, he pulls a cigarette out of his packet and shoves it in his mouth without a care. The knowledge that cigarettes would some day kill him just didn't bother him any more. It once had an effect on him but the knowledge that he was going to die one day no matter what made him think they he might as well enjoy himself. And that was something that he had surely done over the years. Money beyond most man's wildest dreams, a castle instead of a house in which to live in, the knowledge that he had killed people and always gotten away with it, but more importantly than that, he had a family. A wife and a son who loved him no matter what he did. Home wasn't too far away now and as Pantera's 'Great Southern Trendkill' rumbles through the car, he drifts away into thought. Click The cigarette lighter pops out and he quickly reaches over to grab it. As he does the car swerves slightly but it is nothing to worry about as small animals never did much damage to the car and that is what he expected that small thud under the wheels to have been. The glowing red lighter touches base with the cigarette and the smoke begins to riffle up into the air and surround his head. He breathes in deep through the cigarette as the red ball on the end of the white paper grows and moves up the cigarette. He sighs as he breathes out and lets his body relax. Soon he would be at home with Lisha and Darrell and have the cooks have his meal brought out to him with a beer or a glass of red wine. He couldn't help but think of the talk he had had with Derek earlier in the day and how someone who had been with him in the fed could ever want to leave. Sure they had never really gotten along int he ring, but the respect between the two men was on such a high level, that Salaok seemed somewhat depressed about his request. Could this be a sign to Salaok? Was it also time for him to move on? In his mind it didn't seem like it was, and his body felt as good as it ever had. No, the time to leave GW for Salaok wasn't yet. Finally the car rounds the entrance to the driveway of the castle grounds. So hard to believe that something so big and so grand could only be an hours drive out of the city center. But then again, anything was possible in this world. If Salaok had learned anything in this lifetime, that was it. The entrance lights were on and it was always a grand site, even for Salaok who saw it every day of his life. The flames shooting up from the edges of the driveway as you move past the sensors and the big flood lights pointing up from the ground at the house casting shadows on it in different directions making it seem even bigger than it already was. With the window down and the air pushing his hair back, he takes a deep breath of the fresh night air before shutting the window as the car begins to slow. As the car comes to a complete stop at the front of the castle doors, he sits in the car for a moment, just happy to be home but with no idea of what will great him once he steps foot inside. From the outside, everything looks normal but on the inside a hell like no other had engulfed one of the family and as Salaok gets out of the car he has no idea about it. Each step he takes without caution as he had walked the 47 steps day after day and knew exactly which one's you need to watch out for due to the cracks near the edges. It had always been the case that steps 19 and 32 were harmful and that is probably the reason that he had never done anything about fixing them. It was only a danger to those who didn't know the place well enough to step there. And if you didn't know the place well enough, then more than likely you weren't wanted there anyway. As his hands reaches out to grab the door handle, the door opens, seemingly by itself. But that myth is soon dispelled as Salaok is greeted with a huge hug from Lisha who is smiling despite the fact that hell has been going on inside of her mind since earlier in the week. But finally it was time to come out with what she knew. Grabbing his hand, she leads him over to the sofa in front of the fireplace which was burning brightly. Her grin from ear to ear seemed to confuse Salaok, but it didn't matter as he smiled at her. He loved her and every time that he looked at her, he was reminded of that fact. Sitting down on the sofa beside him, she looks him square in the face.'Sal, I know your probably wondering what on earth is going on, but you have to trust that my excitement isn't for nothing.' Raising an eyebrow and tilting his head slightly to the side, he looks at her, trying to work out what she is about to say but there is no hint of what he was about to unload on him. Within a few minutes his entire life would again be turned on it's heel but it would be good, just like the first time was. Only think is, will he think so? Lisha continues... 'Sal, we're right financially aren't we?' A nod is his answer as he still continues to think about what she is about to say as she continues to speak with her angelic voice. 'I know. Well I went to the doctor today.' Salaok cuts Lisha off before she even gets a chance to finish what she was saying and Salaok couldn't have been further from the real situation. 'Are you sick? Holy shit. Look, we will get the best doctors and make sure that nothing goes wrong.' 'Sal, does it look like I'm sick?' Salaok stops and thinks about what he is about to say because knowing that Lisha is female and saying that she does look sick when she's not could put a huge whole in the nights activities and cause the silent treatment for the next day at least. 'Umm, no.' 'Good answer. Now will you let me finish what I was going to say before you interrupted?' Again a nod is the response Lisha receives as Salaok goes back to trying to think about what she has to tell him if she isn't sick and has been to the doctors. Just as he starts to form an idea, Lisha spills the beans. 'Sal, you're going to be a father again. I'm pregnant.' The look that came over Salaok's face is one that's hard to describe. It's that mix of being really happy and yet really surprised at the same time which only makes Lisha think the worse as his smile seems fake. 'You're not happy are you Sal?' Quickly answering to put any misunderstanding out of the way, his voice lifts with the joy, which he feels inside. A joy which had been absent for a while now. 'What? Not happy? I'm fucking over the moon babe. When did you find out?' 'Well I took a piss on a stick test on Monday and finally went to see the doctor about it today and she confirmed what I thought.' Lisha has lost Salaok with the 'piss on a stick' comment and it shows in his face as confusion replaces the joy. But Lisha is quick to answer the unasked question before Salaok's mind begins to wander into strange thoughts. 'A home pregnancy test you fool.' 'Oh. I knew that. So when's it due?' 'Well Sal, I didn't ask. I wanted you to be there when we here all the details and everything. OK?' Although not the most exciting thing in the world, Salaok knew that it was the least he could do for her, seeing as how she would be the one doing the large scale of the work over the next nine or so months. Salaok looks into the fire with a smile on his face and sits back into the chair, putting his hands behind his head, looking rather happy with himself. 'Does anyone else know?' Salaok asks, in an attempt to keep the conversation going. Lisha seems kinda hesitant to answer at first but then finally says it after a few seconds of silence. 'Yeah, Eve knows. She came with me. And I think she told Justin.' There was always something that had to ruin the moment. Having Eve know wasn't the problem and he was actually glad that she knew, butt he fact that his brother in law knew about it, made him annoyed. 'Are you sure he knows?' 'Well he's kinda throwing us a dinner tomorrow night to celebrate it with friends.' Salaok's hands fall to his lap and his head rocks back onto the headrest of the sofa. His chest expands and then falls as he lets a huge and loud sigh out of his system, which is clearly done to let Lisha know that he's not happy with it. 'Do you know who this idiot is going to invite?' 'Well I spoke to him not too long ago on the phone and he said it would only be those who you are close to. So that's not too bad is it?' 'I guess not. As long as he doesn't get up to anything because I don't trust that freak.' Choosing not to say anything, Lisha just sits there and like Salaok, looks deep into the fire place through the jumping flames. Flames which flicker and dance all over the place, giving life to the otherwise rather dark and lifeless room.
