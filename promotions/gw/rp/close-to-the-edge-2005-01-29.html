Close to the edge

29 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29th January 2005 Anger? It is a horrible emotion but it is an emotion which as of late seems to be clouding my mind. We often hear stories of how anger led someone to the brink of their sanity. We often hear stories of how a cheated wife or husband becomes so infuriated that they destroy everything precious to their partner because their anger takes control. Anger is a powerful emotion and once you are in its grip it is hard to break free. Everyone has that button that when it is pressed nothing seems to matter apart from the anger. Some people are able to hide their anger and simply grit their teeth but that is not true anger because everyone can hide a little anger. However when hat specific something gets under your skin, no gritting of teeth will save you because once that fuse is lit there is no stopping the explosion. Indeed anger brings out the worst in people and at the moment it is bringing out the worst in me? What is making me so angry, you ask? Well it can all be summed up in two words, The 138! Now this tag team have only just arrived in the GW but already they have got under my skin! You may think that this is all down to that match El Serpenti and I had against them at last week’s Evolution but it isn’t just that! Let me cast your mind back to the beginning of this year, with a GW Pay Per View called Reload. I was having a great title defence against Joshua Curtis when out of nowhere comes those 2 idiots Damien Ekx and Rafe Corvin! They attack Curtis and then run off, letting us finish the match with which I came out the victor. Now this sounds great to you doesn’t it? I came out still the GW Cruiserweight Champion, so I should be thrilled right? But I wasn’t! I pride myself on having honourable matches each time I go out to that ring and what The 138 did, disgraced a match which up until they interfered was a classic Cruiserweight match. However after my match my anger subsided, I thought they were just trying to make a name for themselves here in the GW, so I let it go. Then our match happened last week, when they cheated again! Not only did the referee not see it but they also got the victory because of it! You might think I am just bitter because I lost but I wouldn’t care if I lost as long as I lost in an honourable way. So what am I going to do? Well you will have to wait for Evolution for that answer. I also have a match at Evolution with 3 up and coming stars of the GW, which I look forward to and hope that these superstars are more honourable than The 138! “Say that again?” I was pacing in Peter Willow’s office with Peter sitting at his desk watching me pace up and down. Pete had discovered who Richard Wood’s was and had spent the passed half an hour explaining to me. Richard Wood is a man who I met at the end of last year. He was kind enough to aid me in getting to the birth of my daughter in time as I was thousands of miles away from my wife. As we travelled together however I couldn’t help but think that I had heard of him before and the name bugged me all the way through Christmas and New Year. I finally asked Pete if he knew who he was; Pete had heard of the name before also but couldn’t place him so looked into it. Recently he found out and that is what has led to this scene in his office. “Richard Wood, is the head of a company called B.T.I or to give its full name Bio-Technology Industries.” “So?” “B.T.I is the company that bought out the corporation that made your suit. They then liquidised the company as they believed the suit had no future.” “Why not?” I asked still confused about the whole story. “Well apart from you the only people who knew about your suit were the executives of the corporation. You were sort of a trial run but no one else knew about it, so no one knew what a success it had been.” “But surely they saw me on television, in my matches in the GW?” “The corporation had already been liquidised by then and any files on you had been destroyed because no one wanted B.T.I to know about you.” “So does Wood know about me or not?” I asked as I sat down. “To be honest Adam, I don’t know.” Pete replied rubbing his forehead worryingly. “The way he was asking me those questions though, he was a little too interested in the suit.” “It is worrying.” “Why” He can’t do anything can he?” “Well technically since he bought out the corporation, he technically owns that suit, which means he technically owns you!” I stared at Pete whose face was full of worry, I reflected the same look behind my mask. “How can he own me” You can’t own a person?” I exclaimed feeling my throat tighten with worry. “Well you know that contract you signed with me, years ago?” “Yeah” “Well you read all the clauses didn’t you?” “Yes.” “Well do you remember clause 3B?” I fell back on the chair my right hand placed over my mask in remembrance. “Clause 3B,” I sighed as the sentence appeared in my head, “The company holds the right over the under signed and at any time can request the under signed to participate in company events or experiments.” “Exactly,” Pete said holding his head in his hands and looking down at the desk, “I never thought that clause could affect you as apart from the executives no one knew about you or the suit. However if Wood does know about you then he can call you in at any time.” “But we don’t know if he does know about me do we” I mean he could just have been interested in my suit because he owns a technology company.” I was standing and pacing again, I turned to look at Pete and he looked at me in a way that told me I was clutching at straws. “I’m sorry Adam but if he does know then you have no choice.” “Dammit!” I yelled as I smacked my fist against his wall. “This is not what I need at the moment!” “Yeah I know I saw last week’s Evolution.” The mention of last week’s Evolution made my blood boil. “It wasn’t right Pete! You know how much I pride myself in my matches!” “I know it is hard to take Adam but can’t you just forget about it. It was one match.” I smashed my fist against the wall again. Pete jumped back in his chair as I turned and stared at him even with my mask on he could tell how furious I was. “One match! One match Pete! Do you remember Reload” You remember what they did to my match? They made my match a disgrace because they wanted some more time in the lime light!” “Calm down Adam! I am not the bad guy here, turn your attention to your match at next Evolution! If you want to make up for your loss at last Evolution then show these up and coming stars why you are the GW Cruiserweight Champion.” “I am concentrated on next Evolution Pete! I am concentrated on my match but I am also concentrated on The 138. They think they can walk around and interfere and cheat in any match they like! Evolution is when I get even Pete!” “But how Adam” You are just one guy, you are a great fighter that is for sure but 2 against 1 are not fair odds!” I leaned across the desk so I was face to face with Pete. “There is always a way Pete! There is always a way!”
