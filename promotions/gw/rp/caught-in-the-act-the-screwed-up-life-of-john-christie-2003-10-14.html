Caught in the act: the screwed up life of John Christie John Christie Global Wrestling Promo #650 Date: 10/14/03 Location: John's Home A slight drop of blood oozed out of the small hole and he inserted the fine, heroin filled needle into his arm. A ray of sunlight bounced off the needles tip as it went in, John pushed the top down slowly, his facial expressions showing that it was a very pleasurable experience. He gasped as all the heroin left the needle and was replaced by a small amount of blood, he then layed back quickly as the drug took effect. He smile and sniggered as he looked at the ceiling, hoping it would come crashing down onto him. Sarah walked into the room, and was stunned by what she saw, the secret was out. Sarah:.....WHAT THE FUCK IS THIS?! OH MY GOD, YOU'RE TAKING DRUGS!! John:.....mmmm, heh heh heh, mmmmm aahh. Sarah:.....thats it John, I'm leaving! He used to be on top of his game, voted wrestler of the year at one point, when he had held the POW World Heavyweight title for the longest period of time ever, 2 years, 3 months and 20 days. Back then he was known as 'The Drunken Clown', the shit he got away with was unbelievable, but he all it took to ruin his amazing and fantastic career in pro wrestling was a huge slip which one him a match he was meant to lose, screwing up the whole storyline. A high incident match for the title he had held for perhaps for too long, they had a main event fued going on, The Drunken Clown vs Anthony. It was badly made scaffolding, up at the lights they were fighting, with a stiff right fist The Drunken Clown went down. As he got up Anthony produce a scaffolding pipe, he had pulled it off, little did he know this was one of the pieces that kept it together. He took a swing at TDC but missed, so far all was going to plan. The Drunken Clown grabbed it and to a wild swing, just as planned. Anthony was meant to duck, but something broke underneath him, diverting his attention from what was going on in the match. The Drunken Clown accidently hit the number one contender around the head, causing him to fall from the scaffolding, and fall a 120 foot drop, almost from the roof. He was rushed to the local E.R where he quite nearly died, if he had fallen a couple more feet to the side he would have been killed, either instantly or about 2 to 3 hours after. For this The Drunken Clown was fired, after nearly killing one of the top stars in POW, and completely fucking up the storyline and the match. Back in the present, John was laying on the floor almost completely motionless, Sarah was nearly in tears. After 5 minutes John got up, and said anything he could to make it better, he knew in his head that he was going to have to kick his habit. Not many people are this strong, John has to kick a drinking habit and a drug problem all at the same time. She made him promise on their marriage, he wasnt going to do it again, and if he did, certainley not any place where he is likely to get caught. He wanted to do this for himself, though, he wanted to get his career back on track. John went into the back yard and lit up a cigarrette, this was the only thing keeping him sain now. The wind blew harshly, it seemed to be coming from all directions. John shivered slightly and then walked back inside to find Sarah waiting for him in the living room. John:..... ....what? Sarah:.....what do you mean what?! Look at this mess, your fucking needle on the floor, ash from your cigarrette scattered everywhere, used spoons and lighters. Im not cleaning this shit up! John:.....I can't either Sarah:.....why the fuck is that? John:.....because im kicking a drug addiction Sarah:.....DON'T YOU MAKE THIS A JOKE JOHN! I'LL WALK OUT OF HERE AND REPORT YOUR FUCKING ASS TO THE POLICE! John:.....don't be stupid Sarah! We both know the police only arrest law breakers if they are black. Sarah:.....you racist fuck! That shit aint' funny John! John:.....then why are you smiling? Sarah:.....look, just clean this up or I'll make you eat the fucking heroin! John:.....ok! Sarah:.....by the way John, how long had it been going on? I just want to know John:.....about 3 months Sarah:.....all that time! You could of died, I would of found out exactly why if you had! John:.....yeah, well now you wont be so shocked! Sarah:.....I'm warning you, if you take even one more hit, I'm out of here ok?! John cleaned up the mess and went out to clear his head. He saw the state of his town, having more whores than a dutch brothel living in the same town as you wasn't easy to do. John walked all over town and saw what a shit hole he was living in, he wanted to move but he hadn't got his first paycheck yet. But he wasn't going to complain about it, he was just going to take it, maybe he would explode one day and get alot of overness for it. Who knows? He was working in major fed at the moment, and he wasn't going to fuck it all up by complaining about something so trivial as a fucking paycheck. This was his life, he went from jobber to champ to main eventer to wrestler of the year to loser with a drug problem and an alcohol problem. He just wanted his life to get back to normal and then again he didnt. His fucked up life gave him a sort of strange yet sadistic edge when he was in character, but outside of the ring he was depressed and had too many problems to get back to the great wrestling status he once had. Hopefully this new fed could help him out, hopefully his problems would go away, hopefully he wouldn't need to hope anymore.
