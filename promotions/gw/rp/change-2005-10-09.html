Date: 10-9-2005 The air was think. Tough to breath with all the smoke and smog filling the sky. Raining down to the people who were walking the dark streets. Seemingly the dark atmosphere wasn’t nothing for one girl. Yes, it was my time. My time to drape amongst the ever so changing world that was underneath my feet. The air was cold in this early October. Halloween not to far away as when the kids would be coming out. I never had a good Halloween. Mine was rather’well to say the least, nothing. I was never allowed to go out after dark. It was, “To scary” for me. Even when I was old enough to drive I was told that it was unsafe to go out after dark. But when I turned eighteen, the days of parents telling me differently was over. My life was mine when that day came. Drinking, drugs, sex, whatever. If I wanted to do them I was going to. But a lot of things, a lot of bad things have happen in the month of October? “And a lot of more will happen to. ---------------------------- October 9th, 2005 11:30 PM “Give me your money’NOW!” The streets of Dallas. Cold and wiry none the less. Many bad things can happen when the sun goes down. Like kills, shooting, and even what is happening right now. “Ple’Please don’t kill me.” Cries the little old lady who in all seriousness shouldn’t be out here at this time of night. “Shut up!” Screams the angry young teen. Searching through the purse that she gave him, tossing out all the little tissue and useless crap that didn’t need his attention, the man grabs her wallet. Tan with a little bit of flower little pictures in the seems, he gathers up a few hundred dollars that were stuffed inside the pocket. “Is this it?” Responds the young man. “Yes?”, responds the old woman. Oh there was more but for some reason she wasn’t telling him. Why you ask? Well because it was in a place that no man other then her husband had been in, in her bra. The young man launches the purse to the ground in a heavy toss before grabbing at the woman’s shoulder, pressing her back against the wall. “Are you lying to me old woman. Because if you are I swear you will die right here in this god forgiving place.” Responding the crazy young robber. “I SWEAR’I DO SWEAR!” Scared out of her mind or what little wind she had left. The young man took his fist and launches it right fore her face, slapping her like he would some slutty hoer outside baby dolls, a local Dallas strip joint. He didn’t carry a gun. No. His weapon of choice was a long slicing blade that could cut anything in half. She began to cry out, screaming for her life before a noise came from the alleyway. “Who’s there?” Trying to talk over the screaming. Nothing. Pure nothing from the dark alley. “HELP ME PLEASE!” Kicking the old lady right into the side of her old crippling body, “Be quiet or you really will need help.” Turning back towards the alleyway, towards what was really feeling like hell, the bobber began to ask again. “Someone there?” Awaiting an answer. ??So you like taking from others huh?” “Who’s there?” Responding to the female like voice that came from the depths of hell. The voice was low. Traveling towards his ear like a train going down the tracks. Blasting him back just a few feet. The old woman began to get up off the ground, holding her worn out gut like it was falling off her body. She was spitting up blood and for that the voice quickly began to change. ??So you like to see blood coming from old bodies huh?” “Show yourself or I’ll be forced to take my anger out on her some more. I swear to you I’ll kill her.” Screaming to the dark like he could see her but we all knew he was as blind as a bat. ??Go ahead. I don’t care what happens to her. He has shown me that you have no guts to finish the job?” Walking closer towards the light as the voice responds. By this time, thirty minutes later, the voice was so close to the light you could smell the body odor coming from the female. The robber was beginning to worry himself like he was a man waiting on death row. Pulsing his blood throughout his body like it was a Nascar race. He wasn’t going to take it no more. No more being scared at a shadow in a dark place. Turning around and taking the grandma up off the ground, he gathered her in a chokehold and held her there with the knife against her throat. ??Games are not my style. He says that if you give in to much for the weak then you will be a failure to the world you create. So I say kill her?” “I’m warning you I’ll do it. I swear. Show yourself?”, shaking several time with the knife. I think it was time. Time to show the world who was in hell. Who was running the show. And who’s voice, evil as it sounded, who this really was. A black leather suit came walking out from the shadow, carrying what looked to be a slapper on a stick. Her eyes were focusing on the man’s eyes, not drifting away at all with every single step. The face was familiar, so familiar. And he might of not known who I was but he knew what my intentions were. “Who are you?” Shaking says the young man. “I’m the person that she use to be. I’m the opposite of weak. I’m the girl that use to be the push over. I’m everything that he has shown me not to be.” Walking towards him with my head looking right at him. You could feel the tension in the air. Almost like we were two countries getting ready to fight in a war. Like the feeling in World War Two. Ready for battle at any given moment. “You stay away. She won’t die if you come closer.” Holding the knife even closer to her throat, making a little spot of blood come from the broken skin. “This world is cold and depressing for some. For you, the world must be dry and shaky. Braking away from what you need right?” reaching just a few feet from the young man. “Well let him show you a better way to live. Let him make you stronger. Let him wiggle into your body like a disease.” “Let who?”” Confused at the words that were coming out of my mouth. “The man who saved my life and the man with all the answers. He can show you the evil that lurks in the corners of the world. Let him show you the ways to make a better life.” Walking towards him and grabbing the coat of the old lady as she shakes in his arms. “Lady, you are crazy you know. I’m not letting anything change me. Not your man and not you, you female bitch?”, yelling at me with his rough and acting strong voice. Bitch? Is that me. Yes it is but it is far from what I use to be. Letting the coat go from my grasp, still standing there in the dark, looking up at him with my cold dark eyes. Feeling the weather go even colder on my body. It was time. It was time to show him what he changed me to. Reaching towards the knife and grabbing it like my hand was already there. The old lady fell to the ground in shock, rolling away as he struggles to get the knife free. It wasn’t going to happen. Twisting him around, pressing him right into the center of the wall were he slams his head right into the corner. He went down all the way into the wet water that was lurking underneath us. His light, being shut off as the old lady cries out for my help. “Please help me..”, reaching out with her hand. Tossing the knife to the ground and reaching out to the old lady, taking her hand and pulling her up to my level. “Thank you pretty thing. How can I ever thank you?”” “How can you thank me?” Responding to her question. “You can thank me by dying off. You cry because you are weak. I am strong in this world and you hold a spot in the chain of life that I could use. So if you want to thank me, you can die.” Her eyes, I will never forget, changing from the pretty blue to a scare dish white. Walking backwards from her, I could feel the blood running deep in my body. Another man feeling my wrath. Another person feeling my anger. You see’him changing me was worth it. Him changing me made me become what I was meant to me. And after this Monday’GW will understand that change is necessary.
