Date: 01/09/06 Prelude: It took quite some time to make a clear decision on where Shane's future would lie.... but EW defied odds and proved that they were the overall most worthy company to sign the major talent thats been plaging the comptetion in Fedwars. It goes all the way back to the days of Eternal Wrestling where Shane made the biggest impact in Fedwars history... he rose from a lower card rookie into one of the biggest stars in the industry as we know it. The talent Wethers showed in EW was so strong he was even successful in fending away the very powers of the Owners, Cecil Cassidy and the Infamous owner of Bio-Tech, Jax (Joshua Xian). In Cecils attempt to aid Shane's hate-fuelled brother Silus, and help him on his way to destroy Shane... Cecil found that destroying Wethers was never an option. The mind games, the savage attacks, the ambushes, all of them failed and worked against Cecil Cassidy. Even Jax, with half of the roster under the influence of the Bio-tech chip that he planted underneath their skin to brainwash them, couldn't put a stop to Shane's growing reputation of being indestructable. Over the years it has become a Wethers trademark, and it is still very much talked about today. Now Shane has entered EW he vows to bring the same talents with him that made him one of the highest rated stars of Fedwars in history. Forget all the big names that have circuled the depths and the heights of Fedwars, its time to witness the 'New Era' once again. It's time to see the ability that many agents described as 'world shaking', 'record breaking' and 'title-taking'. The Demonic Daredevil has now to make his arrival felt in the EW and leave a mark that will scar the EW for its entire life. This scar will live deep in the flesh of EW and will make it the most distinct company to ever grace this planet. Now is the time....Now is the Time for the Eternal Punk to take EW from a 'future favourite' into a 'first wonder of the world'! The time is here....the time is now! _____________________________________________________________________________ ::The scene begins in the late evening. Shane Wethers is sat in an outside bar in central Chicago, wearing his usual attire consisting of black baggy jeans with a chain hanging from the pocket, a black t-shirt that says 'The Eternal Punk' in bold blood red letters, illuminous yellow nike air trainers and a long black leather trench coat that almost covers his feet. Shane's bleach blonde spikey is in the usual style and Shane wears his usual gucci sun-glasses to protect his over-sensitive eyes. He is sat in the bar next to a close friend, Sean Oldfield. Sean is a smartly dressed business man, currently working as a full-time lawyer, and one of america's most successful. Shane sits back on a blue chair with a pint of lager in his hand, whilst Sean sits back with a drink of martini and lemonade, admiring the quiet atmosphere:: Sean: I'm glad you got the chance to come here with me. I hate working away from home, its almost like you get settle somewhere and before you know it you gotta get moving again. ::Sean takes a drink from the glass and places it back down on the oak table infront of them.:: Sean: I think I've spent more time travelling then I have living at home. Maybe I should leave it all and become a Gypsy. ::Sean and Shane laugh lightly:: Shane: I dunno, I quite like getting away. I mean we spent so long at home in england, then we move to california and as if it couldn't get any better, we are taking small vacations. I know it ain't exactly hawaii but hell, I could definetly get use to the different lifestyles out here. ::Shane takes a deep breath, breathing in the musty air of Chicago:: Shane: ...Besides, I gotta pick that money up from my dumb-founded cousin. Then I won't have any reason to be aqquainted with those organised-crime freaks. The only thing that'll be organised is my foot in there ass if the piss me off. ::Sean shakes his head and laughs, amused at Shane's zest:: Sean: Well I'd concentrate more on making a debut in EW... you haven't done yet. People are waiting for your appearence. If you don't make your debut soon you wont get to face Stormbringer in the next iPPV! ::Shane sits back in his chair as a cocky grin appears on his face, almost as if he was plotting something within the dark reaches of his mind:: Shane: Don't you worry about that... I have it all taken care of. ::Shane looks across at Sean:: Shane: Bobby Snorebringer has gotta be the most BORRRRRRIIING guy I have ever seen. Doesn't the guy have anything better to say rather than talking about his half-witted butler? damn.....if the guy keeps this up Eldarado will be makin' a comeback! why do these sick freaks have to go round hand in hand on national television? they may aswell be known as fudge-packers annonymous. 'Fudge packers' because they are both a set of bent losers and 'Annonymous' because no one knows of, or gives a tiny rats ass about these two twaddle-talking pickledicks! ::Sean coughs and takes another sip from his glass. He acts as if he's about to ask a billion-dollar question:: Sean: Erm.....What about his challenge? will you accept it? ::Shane frowns and stares into space, suddenly a sadistic grin appears on his face as Sean watches on rather concerned:: Shane: I've always said that if someone interfere's in my business then they will pay the ultimate price. Bobski interfered in my business and sadly for him, he will havta pay that very price. Don't think he can hide...i never forget a face and i can remember both of his. He's a real action man, crew cut, real scars and no testicles. I'm already visualizing the duct tape over his mouth. The guy is truely depriving a village of an idiot. I'd like nothin more than to represent EW in a iPPV. Bobski can ever bring his butler with him, i'll beat both their asses. They are like a set of bitches ::Sean shrugs and scratches his head:: Sean: Well they both wear skirts...sure they aren't lesbians? ::Shane and Sean fall into laughter as they lean forward and finish their drinks. Shane stands up, pushin the chair under the table:: Shane: man, I better go collect that money before those dumb asses raid another depositary store. ::Sean grins and nods his head:: Sean: Sure thing man, you got my number. Just gimme a call when you've done and I'll meet you. We can pick up some beers and maybe a pizza. ::Shane nods his head and feels his stomach rumble:: Shane: Yeah...yeah thats a good idea. I aren't gonna be too long anyway, I'm pretty hungry. Sean: Ok, later man. ::Shane turns and walks across the street towards another joint on road. The streets are becoming darker as the sun begins to descend over the horizon. The streets are now pretty busy as people rush back from work, or out for a night of socialising. Shane turns right at a small junction, walking onto a small street. This street in particular is pretty quiet, surrounded by detattched houses in long rows. Shane walks over to the pavement on the left and towards one of the houses, he follows the footwalk to the front of the house where there are stairs leading down to what looks like a basement. He suddenly hears footsteps behind him, he stops and turns around sharply..but there is no one there, just the odd car that rolls by. Shane continues to stare back over his shoulder for a while before continuing to walk. He walks down the stoney steps and towards a blue wooden door at the bottom of the stairs. Shane knocks on the door, you can hear whispers from inside the room, suddnely a voice can be heard . It sounds like a mans voice, he coughs to clear his throat:: Voice: Ahem....er....who is it? ::Shane shouts back:: Shane: It's me....Shane. Let me in. ::the whispers continue, as if there's a discussion taking place:: Voice: Er....what's the password? Shane: hmm...let me think, is it 'open the door before I beat you like a bitch' ? ::There is sounds of the door being unlock, suddenly the door opens and a tall, muscley guy stands infront of him with a suit on. The guy is well built and has the shape of a big bodybuilder, he is bigger than Shane and Shane is 6'3. The guy is unshaved and is dressed like a cocktail waiter, but not the sort you'd want to meet on a cold day. The guy looks Shane up and down and opens the door wide for Shane to walk in. He looks outside the door to make sure no one was following as Shane walks inside. The basement is pretty clean, with white painted walls, a large metal table and some chairs a bar with many different liquors, beers and spirits. At the table sits three people, each wearing a different custom designed suit. They All acknowledge Shane's presence, one of them stand up. He has a cream suit, a black shirt and a cream and red striped tie, he holds a cigar in his left hand.:: Guy: Shaneo! where'sa you been cuz? don'ta you-a know how much i missed ya? Come give yer cousin Huey a hug! ::Huey leans forward to hug Shane, but Shane steps back:: Shane: Huey! What the hell you doing fool? get off me before bitch slap yo' ass. ::Suddenly the two guys at the table and the bar man reach for guys and point them at Shane. Shane stops point blank , but Huey turns aroud to face the guys, raising his hand:: Huey: Hey!!! leave it out guys... this is family ova' here'. ::They all put there guns away and quietly sit down:: Huey: Shane, let me introduce you to Frankie....he runs the mob in New York. ::Frankie sits at the table with a grey suit, which almost matches his hair. He has a face like a bag of chisels and doesn't smile. Huey then points at a guy who looks to be wearing a white skirt, a blue blouse and make-up. Shane looks disgusted and lost for words:: Shane: I-i-i-is that guy wearing L-l-lipstick? Huey: Yeah...that's Carlos, he runs the mob in San Francisco. ::Shane nods:: Shane: Yeah, it figures. ::Huey then points to the large barman stood behind the bar:: Huey: I believe you've already met Francesco. Shane: Yeah. ::Huey looks Shane deep in the eyes:: Huey: Now Shaney... I should let them blow your brains out since you ran me out of my business...that being my protege', the NIWC. But seen as you are still my cousin and I love Auntie Miranda, I will let it go this time. ::Shane frowns:: Shane: Auntie Miranda died. ::Huey looks shocked, he falls to the floor as he puts his hands to his face and shakes his head in despair:: Huey: oh no...Auntie Miranda was the only one who understood me... ::Shane grins and mutters to himself:: Shane: Auntie Miranda died when we were 4. ::Huey looks up at Shane:: Huey: She did? ::Shane nods his head:: Huey: oh..well er.... ::Huey gets back up:: Huey:....so you're here for the money, right? ::Shane nods again:: Huey: Alrite...alrite... ::Huey reaches inside his expensive suit and pulls out a massive wad of cash:: Shane: It's about time. Huey: Wanna stay for a drink? ::Shane looks at Huey as if he's crazy. He grins sarcastically:: Shane: oh yeah sure I wanna stay with the mafia, who take part in organised crime and are wanted by nearly every federal bureau in the country. ::Huey cackles:: Huey: Ah Shane-o, you amuse me. We have everything under control, you see. We have someone that the police want more than anything... Shane: The Pretsel king? Huey: No Shane: Doctor Donut? Huey: No!....Bin Laden! ::Huey leans over the table and picks up a small device that looks like a remote control. It is black and has a few buttons on it. He presses the central red button and a large screen descends from the ceiling. The screen shows live access of Bin Laden.:: Shane: What the f.....? ? ? Huey: This is our headquarters in downtown Detroit. Bin Laden's being held captive in a small garage where he will listen to Britney Spears on repeat play 24/7. Shane: Wow...they can do that? ::Huey nods his head:: Huey: Yeah, they're doing it to hostages in the armed forces. It's a sort of torture device. After that we will force him to perform swan lake on national t.v, wearing only a skimpy leotard. Shane: Yuk! Huey: Yeah, and if he doesn't do what we tell him we will cut his balls off, give him titties and send him to costa rica for prostitution. ::Shane looks on in total disbelief:: Shane: Damn.... it must suck to be him right now. ::Huey nods his head and watches the screen:: Huey: Yeah...you could say that. ::All of a sudden, the sounds of swan lake begins and Bin Laden looks up at the screen:: Bin Laden: I will get you damn americaaaaaaans! Shane: Wow. Huey: Uhuh. Anyway Shane, wanna stay for a game? ::Shane looks down at the playing cards on the table:: Shane: Sure...i'm good at this game. ::As Shane walks over to the table to reach for the cards, Big Francesco runs over to him and taps him on the back:: Francesco: Tag! your it! ::The four mob members run around the room:: Huey: C'mon Shane! your it! you gotta catch us! ::Shane shakes his head and frowns:: Shane: What??? ....yor freaks are too stupid to be true. I ain't tagging any of you bitches, how about I play kabaam? It's where I shove my foot up your ass and it goes kabaam! ::The four gang members stop and pull out there guns once again, pointing it at Shane:: Shane: Ah stop with that damn gun thing for hells sake! they aren't even real anyway! ::Francesco points the gun against Shane's head:: Francesco: What makes you so sure? Shane: Erm...the way that is say's 'Toyworld' on the side of it. Francesco: oh yeah. ::Shane uses his quickness to pull Francesco's arm away and throw him over Shane's shoulder. Before any more trouble breaks loose, a whole raid of police burst through the door:: Police: FREEEEEEZE! Shane: What the..? Huey: huh? Francesco: Pigs? ..mmmmm...bacon....mmmm Mcbacon burger. ::The police inspector walks over to them:: Inspector: This place has been reported as a scene of organised crime..........and the possession of drugs which we will smoke and snort later on. Now don't try run because there's nothing funnier than 5 junkies bouncing of the walls like pinballs whilst we sit down and have a smoke. ::Shane frowns:: Shane: I'm no junkie you dohnut eating rectal wart! ::The police walk in the room and arrest all of the mafia members, including Shane:: Inspector: I'll be the judge of that.......haha, I've always wanted to say that. Take them away boys ::As the police begin to take them all away under arrest, Huey looks back at Shane who is in handcuffs:: Huey: Shane!?!?! you set me up! ::Hueys face disappears behind the wall as the police walk him back up the stairs:: Shane: What the hell is going on? ::The scene fades as Shane is taken away::
