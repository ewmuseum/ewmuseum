Tails From Tha Hood: Confidence Jay Quickness Global Wrestling Promo #1193 Date: 4/20/04 Location: Apt. (WrestlerCade Rp. I) Confidence (Kon'fe-dens) n. 1 Trust in or reliannce upon something or someone. 2. Assurance. ...Ready or not here I come, you can't hide. WHY! Cause Im find you, and pop you homie... The scene opens up in Jay Quickness little apartment in the Bronx. His house is decorated all nicely with pictures of his family and his dogs. He also has titles of old federations hung up on the wall. Jay Quickness is laying down on his black leather couch with a smile on his face and a blunt in his mouth. He takes 3 quick deep pulls and closes his eyes. ...GCW...GCW...thats where it all happen. That's where Jay Quickness made a name for himself, He won the GCW Hardcore Championship. I remeber that day like it was yesterday.... Victor (Vik' ter) n. 1. One who wins any struggle or contest. The scene opens up in a hotel room. We see Hood Hefner and GMac both sitting down on the wooden chairs. They both have two topless beautiful women dancing and grinding all over them while sitting in the chair. Hood Hefner then suddenly jumps out his chair and grabs his black duffle bag that has been laying in the corner. He places his duffle bag on the wooden table, and opens the duffle bag. His hand goes in and pulls out the GCW Hardcore Championship. GMac stay's in the chair while the other girl comes to him and also dance for him. Hood Hefner sit's back down in his seat with his title slung over his shoulder. The girls both notice that Hood Hefner has his title and both leave GMac, and begin dancing towards Hood Hefner. Hood cracks a smile and begins to smack the girls butt's with the opposite end of the title strap. 'Yo Hood, what you doing homie?' GMac say's. 'Im doing me. Any way it's about time that 2nd blunt get's rolled up.' Hood Hefner say's with a smile on his face. 'Oh yeah, let me go do that.' He say's while he takes all the supplies needed out of his pocket. 'Yo...Homie, I could not belive that those cat's actually thought they could beat me. I mean I told them I was gonna leave the Wachovia Center with that title, I tell no lies.' Hood Hefner say's while he smack's a dancing ass. 'Yea! you did tell them, they just didnt listen.' GMac say's while he lights up the blunt, that he just finish rolling. 'I mean not only did I beat one person to become the GCW Champion. I beat Omen, Falcon, Nemesis, and MTMT ass, to become the GCW Champion and the 1st person to hold gold. I can't say that it was easy for me, but I can say that I did come out with the 'W'.' Hood Hefner say's while he recieves the blunt from GMac. 'I cant lie homie, I thought MTMT was gonna win tha match. But then he dedicated a song fo' you, that's when I just lost all hope in him.' GMac say's while he begins to snicker. 'Yah!, that was a little wierd. MTMT singing to me, talking about 'I make him sick'. Nigga I dont care if I make you sick, you make me sick. Singing to another man, what kind of shit it that? I cant believe MTMT said he almost won tha title. neva that!' 'I can tell you what kind of that that is, that's some.....' Gmac is stoped by Hood. ...RING....RING.....RING... 'Dont even say it.' Hood say's. 'Say what?' GMac replies. 'We all know what you were going to say, But guess what it's my turn. hit that beat nigga.' Hood Hefner say's while he passes the blunt to Gmac, who begins to bang on the wall to make a beat. ...RING....RING...RING... Jay Quickness finially opens his eye's and clears his head. He takes a couple of pulls from his blunt and picks up the phone. 'Hello...Hello...Iz anyone there?' 'Jay Quickness!' 'Yea! thatz me. Who iz dis.' ..Laughter on the other line... 'Yo, who diz and whatz so fuckin funny?' 'Let's just say you got something coming to yah young man.' 'Really, well bring it on homie.' 'In due time.' 'Well until then you can kiss my fuckin azz.' ...Click... Jay Quickness grabs the remote from the counter and turns on his sterio onto some hott instrumentals. Jay Quickness takes a couple of pulls of the blunt and begins to nod his head to the beat. Jay Quickness begins to think to himself. 'Can I do it? I did it before, but the real question is can I do it again? Like the litte engine said 'I think i can, I think i can.' No forget about that shit, I know I can. I can win this match at WrestleCade 4 I have no choice but to win. This Six Man Cruiser Eliminator, I will win it and receive a cruiser title shot on eVo. and I will become the Cruiser Champ, and that is where it all start.a That's when it will all be let out. I will let the HOOD out in Global Wrestling.' 'So Roz Blayze, Max Roswell, Rich Sherwin, J. C. Diamond and 'Amazing' Mike Storm get ready cause I am ready to prove what this yungsta can do.' -To be continued.
