Visit To An Old Friend (Part 1)

6 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/6/03 This Becomes My Pistol This Becomes My Dagger This Becomes Your Future Unseen War Your Weapons Are Useless Drop The Gun The Golden Gun Its Like Bringing A Knife To A Gun Fight -Norma Jean He is seen moments later the confrontation with Salaok. He wears his GW Cruiserweight Championship Title across his shoulder proudly. He wears it like a badge of honor, because in his mind and others, a title in the Global Wrestling means more than a badge of honor. It shows talent, heart, and effort. And having a title shows the world that he is indeed a superstar and he is a contender here in Global Wrestling. He has made something of himself here and his name, oh, for it will be lifted up high and glorified in the heavens above. For everybody will remember the long battles with Jay Robson. For everybody will remember the trickle of blood that fell from Silent Wind’s face. And not one person will ever forget him’for his journey in becoming a legend’lies in the near future. He walks down the hallway. He walks down a flight of stairs and opens a door at the bottom to the outside. He reaches in his pocket and pulls out a set of keys and dangles them around in his hand and grabs a particular one. He continues down the sidewalk until he reaches a black 2003 Corvette that has purple interior. He doesn’t even bother opening the door, he just hops over the seat and starts it up. He puts a CD in and blasts it. Memphis Will Be Laid To Waste by Norma Jean plays in the middle of the song. He smirks and bops his head softly and pulls off into the road. 8 Hours Later The black Corvette pulls into a paved driveway through an enormous gate. He slows down and puts it in park. He opens the door and hops out of the car and shuts the door. He twirls his keys his finger and puts them in his pocket. He slowly walks up in front of the house. A house? It is more like a mansion. It is white and black. There are pillars and hedges surrounding the house. He walks up to the front door. He rings the doorbell and clears his throat. Seconds later, a Japanese man opens the door. The man has died red hair and is wearing baggy black jeans and a bright red shirt with no sleeves that reads: “X-Avier Al-X-Ander”. The man is fairly built for his small size. He is a couple inches shorter than him. He is probably around 6 feet tall. The man’s face lights up with excitement? “Sage”!” “X! Nice to see you.” They both shake hands and exchange smiles? “Shit man, I’ve been watching you a lot lately. You have been on a roll!” “Yeah, all that hard work paid off.” “Apparently so, you even look more intimidating then you did in the XWF.” “Well Salaok has put me through some very intense training. To strengthen my body and my mind.” “Well that’s good to hear.” The man nods his head? “What a gentleman I am’come on in.” He nods and steps inside the house. He follows the man down a hall into a room. He takes a seat on a big striped couch and the man sits in an old fashioned rocking chair. “I seen you beat Jay Robson a couple weeks ago for the Cruiserweight title. Congrats man.” “Thanks, I figured I would walk out with the title around my waist. Hell, I told everybody I would. But they didn’t think it was possible because Jay has been flawless over the past couple years. But now, it looks as if Jay isn’t so flawless, is he?” “He looks naked without that title. It was the only thing holding him together.” “Don’t get me wrong, Robson is an amazing competitor. He gave me a lot of problems in the ring, but when it came down to it, I was the better wrestler.” Xavier nods his head in agreement. “And when you and The Nevermore busted Silent Wind open last week, man, that sure as hell brought a smile to my face.” “Yeah, me too.” Xavier shakes his head side to side and smiles? “I never would have thought my former tag team partner would be wrestling in the Global Wrestling and actually beating the hell out of the Heavyweight Champion of the World. Hell, nothing against you Sage but, I never even thought you would have won any titles there.” “Well, this is the 2nd title I’ve held. I beat my current stablemates, Salaok, Quartz, and Jay Vyper in a cage match a couple months back to win the Platinum Title.” “You beat Salaok and Quartz?” “Yeah.” “Then why aren’t you heading for the Heavyweight Championship” You beat Salaok, Quartz, Jay Robson, and you beat the living hell out of Silent Wind a couple weeks ago. You should?” He cuts Xavier off? “Because in The Nevermore, we aren’t greedy. I would be nothing without Salaok and Quartz’s training. We aren’t selfish, we are a family.” “So what are you going to do then” What’s your next step?” “A handicap match this Evolution against Jesse Briggs, Ely Braxton, and Brick.” “They are all versing you?” “No way! Salaok owns half of the GW, so it is going to be the whole entire Nevermore against those 3 goofs.” “Well, like I said, you are on a roll and you are beating some of the best names in the business. Now you can add Brick, Braxton, and Briggs to your list.” “That’s what I plan on doing.” “Just like the good old days when we defeated Devastation and Driscoll for the Tag Titles’remember that?” “How can I forget” We gave them The Kiss Of Death DDT and pinned Devastation for the win.” “Yeah’exactly.” “You know what” Would you like to come down to meet The Nevermore with me this week?” “Me?” “No, the girl next door.” They both chuckle? “Sure, that would be an honor man.” “You never know, we might actually have a spot in The Nevermore for you’and anyways, I might need a manager.” Xavier’s eyes light up? “You mean?” “Yes, that is exactly what I mean.” “So I get to?” “Yup, be in The Nevermore as my manager.” “And I get to meet?” “You’ll get to meet Salaok, Quartz, Jay Vyper and Johnathon Davis.” “So I can meet Lisha?” He slaps his forehead. “We’ll see” They both enjoy a laughter than felt as good as winning the XWF Tag Team Titles as the?
