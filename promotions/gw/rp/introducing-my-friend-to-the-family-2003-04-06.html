Introducing My Friend To The Family

6 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/6/03 He is seen wearing black jeans and his black “The Nevermore” shirt. His hair is combed and tightly tied in a ponytail and his white goatee is freshly trimmed. He carries a black gym bag and wears his GW Cruiserweight title on his shoulder. Behind him is Xavier Alexander, his old tag team partner from the XWF. Together, they single handedly changed the XWF forever. Xavier is wearing black track pants and his red “X-Avier Al-X-Ander” shirt. His bright dyed red hair stands up and his bright red goatee falls towards the ground. He reaches the door of the arena where Evolution will be held. He throws the door open enough so Xavier can make it through. They both enter and walk down a blue hall. They both reach a door that has a black and red sign on it that reads: The Nevermore Xavier’s eyes light up and he smiles. “This is it. The Nevermore stands behind that door.” He nods and puts his hand on the door handle. He twists the handle and pushes the door open. He walks in the room. Xavier fixes his hair so that it is perfect and follows him. They both walk inside. Jay Vyper is in the right corner, he is putting on his kick pads. The massive Quartz is in the other corner speaking with evil and sophisticated Salaok. The door shuts. Salaok and Quartz turn around and see him and his friend. Salaok points towards Xavier? “Who is that, Sage?” “He’s my former tag team partner from the past. His name is Xavier Alexander.” “Nice to meet you guys!” Xavier extends his hand out towards Salaok. Salaok stares at his hand? “Nice to meet you too.” Salaok turns to Sage, leaving Xavier’s handshake attempt lying in emptiness? “Why did you bring him here?” “I would like him to be my manager. Xavier and I have been through a lot together and he knows me more than anybody in the world knows me.” “That’s great, but why is he here?” “He wanted to meet The Nevermore.” “Well he just met us, he can leave now.” Salaok turns his head towards Xavier? “Nice meeting you, have a nice day.” “Salaok, I just wanted to say that I admire your work.” “Good, you should. I’ve been in this company for many years and I deserve respect and admiration. I’m glad to hear that you do.” Salaok turns his head towards Quartz and starts to speak. Xavier runs towards Salaok and taps him on the shoulder. Salaok’s face drops in anger and turns suddenly towards Xavier. “Can’t you see that I’m busy” We have a match this week against 3 of the toughest men in this business, and all you can do is bug the living hell out of me.” “Come on Xavier, lets go before you start to piss him off.” He opens the door for Xavier. But Xavier refuses and turns towards Salaok. “Salaok, I am an amazing wrestler. I’ve worked with Sage for many years and I’d like to be apart of The Nevermore.” “Ok, just fill out an application and put it in my application box.” “What application box?” Salaok points towards the garbage can. Quartz and Vyper lift their heads and laugh. “Son, this isn’t a job. This is a life style. You can’t just want to be apart of something in life unless you are for it. That is why we took Sage and Vyper in. Because we realized that they had nothing except the love for delivering pain and accepting responsibility. And Xavier’that’s your name right” Xavier shakes his head? “If we think you are good enough and live the life, then we will find you.” Xavier’s smile turns into a frown. “But what about if I am one of the best wrestlers in the world?” “Including if you are one of the best wrestlers in the world.” “I get it, you just don’t want me to come into the picture and to take over your place in GW. You don’t want me to beat your?” He cuts him off? “X, you don’t want to do that bro!” “Sage, shut up man! I’ve listened to you for years and look what happened. You went onto big and better things and I stayed home. I don’t want to be home anymore, I want to be in the ring.” “Well maybe you should go back to the XWF.” “Well maybe I’ll just kick your ass.” Xavier pushes Salaok. Salaok does not even move an inch. Instead, Salaok laughs in his face, which just frustrates him more. Quartz and Vyper stand up to watch the action. “Boy, you can’t hurt me even if you tried.” Xavier punches Salaok in the face. Salaok’s face flies to the right and a trickle of blood slowly drips down from his nose. Salaok slowly slides his wrist off his nose and wipes up a fresh drip of blood off from his nose. “You see boy, that was an enormous mistake, possibly the biggest mistake of your life. You just drawn first blood, but there is a difference. Why” Because my blood will dry and my cut will heal, but with you, if you touch me one more time, your blood will never dry and your cuts will never heal.” “We’ll see about that?” Xavier throws another punch towards Salaok’s face. Salaok catches the punch and twists his arm until it pops. Xavier falls to his back and clinches his arm. It is definitely broken and his elbow can now bend a way that normal people’s elbow can’t. Quartz laughs and picks up Xavier over his head in a gorilla press. “STOP!” “Sage, choose.” “Between what?” “He drew first blood. Choose.” “Choose if you save him or break every bone in his body?” “No, you choose’friend or family.” He pauses for a moment. “SET HIM DOWN!” Quartz sets him down on his feet. “Thanks Sage. I knew you would choose me over these?” He kicks him in the stomach and delivers The 8th Deadly Sin to his former Tag Team partner. He stands up and is greeted with pats on the back from Quartz and Salaok. “Family.” Quartz picks Xavier up by the back of his throat and lifts him high in the air and gives him a Reverse Chokeslam. Xavier’s face bounces off the cement floor and his blood scatters all over the place. Quartz picks him up by his red hair and stands him up with one hand.
