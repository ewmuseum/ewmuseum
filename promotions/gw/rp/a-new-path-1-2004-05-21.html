A new path#1

21 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/21/04 Silence. I walk in the dark streets of oblivion in silence. Looking for the one who can start me on my new path. A path that shall take centuries to master but in the end shall lift me up in the eyes of the powers that be?. Babylon. It has been many years since I have come to this place?.The place where mankind attempted to reach the heavens’The place where mankind wanted to touch God almighty’The thing I search for has made its home here?. The Hierarchy Monastery. It is said that here one can find peace?.Redemption?.faith?.I stand at the gate and look at two monks who stand there. Both look back at me and somehow know who I am?.what I am?.and yet they show know fear, no hint of it. “Please enter in peace my son. We have awaited your coming. Enter, he is waiting for you.”The man said with a calmness that made me quite eerie. “Who is waiting for me?” “The one they call Revelation. For he shall start you on your path.” I walk into the dark monastery. To my left and right large corridors leading off into the darkness that seems to continue forever. I walk down the hall that is in front of me, hearing sounds of faint chanting and also faint screaming. For the first time in over a thousand years I actually feel fear. My eyes and my body show hints of the emotion that has been left dormant in me for ages. I look down the long hall and in the distance and in a faint light I see a type of pulpit with three chairs and a man sitting in each chair. The chair on the left sits a man of medium stature. He sits there cross-legged. He has long black hair and is wearing a monks robe. His face cannot be seen. The man in the chair on the right seemed much older, long gray hair and beard, his eyes a very pale blue. He is dressed in an ornate rabbi’s uniform. Black hat, shawl, and suit. The man sitting in the middle chair was the largest of the three hovering over the others like a dark messiah. He had long brown hair and blue eyes. A tattoo of a Celtic cross covered the left side of his face much like the cross that covered the right side of mine. He wore a Catholic priest uniform. I walk up to the pulpit and fall to my knees still to afraid I sit there shaking. “Do not be afraid Leinad Soma. There is nothing to fear. We welcome you as long as you are not looking to make your meal of our monks.” The youngest of the three said. “What brings you here my son” What has made you come across the world in search of we three?” The eldest speaks “It is said that redemption can be found by anyone as long as they truly believe that they can be redeemed”.A tortured soul can be brought back to the light’But what if the body is soulless? What if the body doesn’t want to return to the light only clean up the darkness he lives in?.I who have lived for an eternity?.I who have laid waste to countries, killed thousands, made humanity run in fear, and even hunted down the powers that be?.seeks now to purge the darkness and make it pure as the light. I?.Leinad Soma seek to change my ways?.” Finally the man sitting in the middle stands and begins to walk over to me. He is more massive than I expected. He stands over seven feet tall. I sit there looking up at him in awe, not knowing whether to stand and fight or to try and run. I sit there to scared to move. He finally makes his way over to me and in one solid kick sends me flying a few feet back. I immediately stand fangs bared and lunge at the large man. He quickly grabs be around my waist as I grab him around the throat. He smiles as he unhinges his hands from around me but only to use his arms to overpower me then lifting me and slamming me into the hard stone floor. I sit there seemingly helpless and as he walks over to me once more I give him a leg sweep. He falls and I jump on top of him growling, fangs bared and seeming to have a long awaited meal. He looks up at me with even a bigger grin. “Do you understand now Soma” You can never truly change your ways. Like a wolf can never change the fact that he is a wolf or a scorpion the fact it’s a scorpion. It was not meant for you to change what you have become only the evil that is inside of you. No creature in this world supernatural or not is truly evil. You are a minister of death, a knight of the eternal. You didn’t have to come all the way here to know that. Everything that has come under your grasp has died?.friends, family, co-workers, etc. But you have also been granted the power to bring back things in your image.” The man lifts me off of him and stands up and sets me down on the ground. He bows to me and goes and sits back down in his chair. “I am the one they call Revelation. You want to purge your darkness” The only way to do that is to destroy all those who are evil. Those who corrupt this world with their authority. You alone can’t do this but you will need the help of another. One who was trained by the same man as you and holds powers to match your own?.seek him out for he shall be lifted up resurrected and shall help in your path of death?” Revelation’s toned grew darker and darker but I understood what he was saying. I am a creature of nature and the only way I can change myself is through the mindfulness that I must be what I am?.A monster. I shall be a monster to all those who are monsters to others?.In my kingdom of the night I shall torment those who torment the innocent. But to find the one who was taught by the same teacher as me?.unless he means?..oh no? “Revelation sir”. Do you mean to tell me I am suppose to get the aid of one of those who died at the hands of my cohorts and I? How can that be if he is dead?” “Soma you ask silly questions”.you know what must be done?.now leave and begin your path of death?..you are now The Minister of Death, go forth and begin your teachings?.” I bow to the three men and begin to leave. I understand what I must do and now comes the task at hand?..finding the one who can aid me in my new journey?.I leave the monastery?.in silence.
