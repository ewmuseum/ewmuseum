No Place Like Home Episode #6

No Place Like Home Episode #6 J. C. Diamond Global Wrestling Promo #1167 Date: 4/16/04 Location: Martin Residence, Miami NO PLACE LIKE HOME- BEYOND THE CURTAINS [Scene: Inside of the Martin Residence. Ynonick is to the right/center of the living room. Chris is next to him as they try to figure out why the television is broken. Jayson stumbles into the living room.] “I can’t believe this,” explains the owner of the house- Chris. “I just bought this television like two months ago!” Their was a lot of anger and hate running around in that house hold today- Jayson tried to stay away from it, yet he had brought himself into that living room for some odd reason. Today was just like every day in the Martin Residence, father and son are doing the usual. The mother of the household, Crystal was no were to be found, and it was kind of starting to get to Jayson. What was going on with Crystal? Was she hiding something from this family? Did she want a piece of J.C but couldn't think right? Only time would tell the truth behind the myth. “It’s those god damn Chinese workers!” snapped J.C, leaning back on the couch. “They don’t know how to make shit, I am telling you.” Tired, confused and his eye‘s barley open, J.C leans his head back against the white couch, he soon feels the soft velvet press against the back of his head. “Damn those workers are really worthless,” shrugs J.C. And now, there was complete silence in the house. His afro was completely drenched in sweat, that had been caused from the heat stroke that nearly killed him last night in his sleep. Jayson had realized that night that the Martin family did not use AIR CONDITIONING. The whole right side of his face had a large red print on it, mostly because he had slept on his hand all night. That whole last night, there was a lot of yelling coming from inside of Chris’s bedroom, and the funny thing about it was, they never fought, well that is all Jayson would know. Looking back on this situation, J.C knew that there were plenty of family troubles coming from inside of the Martin Residence. It was almost towards noon, but still Jayson hadn’t wakened up completely. Last night he had taken to many hits of Marijuana which made him act a bit strange during the dinner. He had the munchies- BAD. The Martin’s had no clue what Jayson was doing, they just thought he had some hunger problems- or something of that nature. Ynonick on the other hand, had realized that he had became a man, after he smoked a fatty bowl with J.C. In Jayson’s eyes, this kid was not a child anymore, he was a man, and he had step out of the youth into manhood. This day was very special for Ynonick; it was his first day after becoming a man. Jayson knows how that feels, he remembers the day he turned into a man- well not really, and he was to fucked up that day to remember. With the sun now, glaring down on his un-shaved face, and on his dirty afro, he quickly jumped up on his feet; Chris and Ynonick were still trying to figure out the television. Ynonick jerked his head to the left, looking up at Jayson; he spoke the first words. “Do you have a solution to our problem Jayson?” Ynonick asked, very silently. Standing there, having no clue what the hell they were talking about, Jayson just nodded his head back and forth very slowly. Chris cringed for a few seconds before saying his sentence. “What is it then Jayson? What is your solution?” “Ugh,” gasps J.C, “Sleep!” After Jayson had finished his sentence he laid his head back on the couch. Letting out a very large moan, he fell deep back to sleep. [Scene Fades] ----------------- [Scene: Inside of the Martin Residence. Jayson is still sleeping on the couch. Ynonick, Chris are both outside doing some work. The sound of a large crash is heard.] He had attended on getting some sleep that day, but the fact that this family never sits down to rest, had made it hard for J.C to do so. There was nothing that he could do, he didn’t pay the bills on this house, he didn’t do shit, besides living in the Martin home on a free ride. Jayson groaned and lifted his head up- trying to wake up was the hardest part. Jayson rolls over and stares up at the tall ceiling, glad to know that the loud noises outside have stopped, and the fact now remained, he had time to relax. Dreaming of doing something dramatic to bring the ratings up on the NO PLACE LIKE HOME show were starting to flutter in his mind- well sort of. He continued to look up in the sky, lying on that velvet couch in the living room was prefect. Not a breath of air stirred the flimsy curtains besides the open windows as Jayson heard some yelling coming from outside. At that split second, Jayson lifted his head up, realizing that there was something BEHIND THE CURTAINS of this whole situation. He hadn’t put his finger on his tongue yet to figure it out. But soon, he would find the truth behind this ordeal. J.C tried to get up, but a deep cramp seized and he laid back down, shivering. “What the fuck is going on outside,” Jayson muttered to himself, trying to ask himself a question that he couldn’t answer. Jayson shook the pain off- well for a split second. J.C was stupider than he’d thought. Maybe it was true, Marijuana does kill brain cells, and this was just the first step of Jayson’s downfall. His eyes were very stricken, but he took a second to take a deep breath before trying to get back up on his feet. Everything around him felt a little dizzy, and the sounds of the screaming coming from outside were becoming a little louder- not by much. Jayson rose gracefully to his feet; yet, the pain was still there for another second before he brushed it off. Laying there on that worthless couch was not as comfortable as everyone seems. The thing was a piece of shit. It was nothing more than just a couch you find in the dump. Gently, he made his way towards the front door. His eyes glistened as he reached out and grabbed the door handle. He tried to concentrate on pulling the door handle, but his mind was in a different place. “What is with me today, the first minute I am totally awake- and the next, I am dead asleep on the couch,” Jayson sighed as he pulled the white door open. Outside of the house, Jayson noticed a familiar car. The camera’s were right behind, and during this point, Jayson had totally forgot about the show that was being taken place in the front of his own eyes. He glanced down the sidewalk, seeing Ynonick standing back as two familiar people quickly exchanged words. Jayson sneaked towards the garage- being unseen. His eyes were wide open now, his brow lowered to a point that it couldn’t become any lower. He listened in as Chris and Crystal exchanged words. “What is wrong with you Chris?” screamed the mother, Crystal. “You are so fucking stupid!” That was the first time Jayson ever heard the mother cuss. “Everything is about me you stupid bitch!” responds Chris, firing back some cuss words of his own. That was the first time Jayson ever heard the father cuss. Looking around with a sign of disbelief, Jayson realized that Ynonick, the son was deeply broken down in tears. He was sitting down on the bench, hands between his legs, his head on top of his legs as he started to shed a few tears. The Martins had no idea that J.C was listening in on this. This was a historic event for Jayson, not once has he ever heard a CHRISTIAN family use as much cuss words as they did, it was kind of like a drunken sailors convention. Timing this whole ordeal, Jayson stared at his watch. This whole thing was blown out of proportion, but he didn’t want to interfere on the conversation- no not because it was wrong to do so, but it was getting some good airtime. “You say that now Chris, but I am not the one that is not obeying GOD! THE FATHER OF THIS WORLD,” Crystal warned- snarling at her husband. Chris shook his head, not knowing what she was talking about. His voice sounded hallow as he proceeded to blow this conversation at its highest point. “I have always loved GOD! You are the one that is making an ass out of yourself in front off our child, and above of all things- on this television show!” “WHAT!?” There was no stopping these two lovers. “I AM NOT MAKING AN ASS OUT OF MYSELF CHRIS! YOU ARE THE ONE WITH THE DRUG PROBLEMS,” Crystal crackled. “DAMN YOU CHRIS.” Drug problems? Hate? Pain? What the hell was this family talking about? Just listening in on this conversation, made Jayson a little confused. He was the only one- well the only one that he could think of that had DRUG PROBLEMS in the Martin home. Crystal has Chris confused with Jayson- well he hoped not. “I really wonder what is going to happen next,” Jayson said patiently- looking deep into the camera lends. Knowing that this was a situation that he had faced plenty of times in his life. J.C remember the times when he was having problems with his former and now decease girlfriend, Daisy. They were in love- no doubt about it, but troubles just like the Martins were the ones that caused sudden downfalls of their relationship. Sure they weren't married like the Martins, but he knew what these two individual's yelling at each other were going through. It’s one thing for Jayson to jump off the roof of the house and make a big scene out of it. It is also alright if he wants to spicing up the show by giving Ynonick his man hood, but it is not alright for two people like Chris and Crystal to break up because of Jayson’s drug problems. OH WHAT THE HELL, JAYSON DOESN’T GIVE A SHIT ABOUT THESE PEOPLE. It is a show, and they have to realize the turn’s and twists of it. Jayson could see that Crystal was starting to burn a hole into Chris’s chest with her deep dark stare. Chris tried to fire one right back at her- but his was weaker than hers. “DRUG PROBLEMS?” Chris shouted from the sidewalk, his eyes lowered now. “I have never once used drugs in my entire life! God would disown me for that. What is with these people and GOD and CHURCH? This was the turning point of the argument. Lifting her short, thin hand up, she tossed Chris what looked to be Jayson’s bag of Marijuana that he lost. What a coincidence. Holding onto the baggy now, Chris studied it, he looked at it, felt it, and realized that this was not his. “This isn’t mine Crystal-” Each member of the family stood motionless- and above all things silent during those first few seconds. The tables were turned now.... “JAYSON!” The whole family screamed at once. [The camera’s fade to black, Jayson turning around and high tailing it towards the side of the house.]
