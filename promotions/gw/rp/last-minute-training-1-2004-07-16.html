'Last Minute' Training #1

16 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 16th 2004 A red Ford Mustang pulls up at the gates of Bristol High School. A number of he students passing through the gates of the school on their way home, stop and look at the car as the engine revs. Their attention is also to the small men inside, The Flying Vergomovs. “Igor, is this the place?” questions Constantino Zergolov as he sits at the wheel and peers through the glass and towards the large grey building behind the eight foot high wire fence. “What colour are the uniforms?” groans an under-the-weather Arefiev, as he lies back in his seat, a red baseball cap pulled down over his eyes. Constantino’s attention turns from the grey building and the large school crest on the wall, towards one of the pupils. He stares at a tall blonde that walks past. Here attention has not been drawn to car, as she passes very close to the doors. “Blue blazer” White shirt? Red tie? And VERY short grey skirt!” chuckles the driver as he checks out the teenage girl. “Yeah! We’re here!” moans Igor, “pull into the car park.” “No shit!” replies Constantino as he playfully pushes Igor head and laughs. “Don’t!” complains Igor as he struggles to push Constantino away. As he does, the car swings into motion and turns sharply into the car park. Igor is tossed around in his seat. Constantino pulls the car to an abrupt halt in a space. Igor is thrown forward into a sitting up position. He strikes his tag partner in the chest. “Do you have to drive like a prick all the time?” grumbles the passenger. “Nope,” smiles Constantino, “just when you’re hung-over!” He laughs. Igor flashes Constantino a sarcastic smile, as the driver of the Mustang jumps out of his seat, and stretches his body, as he yawns in the car park of the high school. Constantino Zergolov is wearing baggy blue jeans, red trainers and a white t-shirt. As he finishes stretching and yawning, he pulls open the backdoor of the car, and pulls out a blue sports bag. “Hurry up!” yells Constantino. Igor mutters something and falls out his door. He is wearing a red baseball cap, navy t-shirt and baggy dark jeans. He reaches into the back of the car and produces a matching sports bag to that belonging to Constantino. “You sure you’re in the right shape for this?” questions Constantino. Igor nods, as he scratches at his arms. “Fair enough. You’re call. But if you are hung-over on Monday, I will personally kill you. Ely Braxton and El Serpentio won’t have a chance!” “El Serpenti,” mumbles Igor. “What?” Constantino leans in as the two men slowly start out towards the doors of the school. Igor clears his throat with a gentle cough. “El Serpenti, not El Serpentio.” “Oh okay. Anyway, whatever his name is. I want you at one hundred percent to beat these fools. They are Bubba’s cronies. That means he is going to be watching with special interest. We need to put on a good show. And he needs to be sure he has made the right decision in hiring a couple of five foot nothings.” Igor grumbles. “Seriously. Don’t grumble at me, Igor.” “Igor” is said with a sarcastic undertone. “This is a huge chance for us. Not everybody gets a chance to really impress the boss on their debut.” “Con.” Igor cuts his tag partner off mid-thought. “Yeah”!” “Remind me again, why I thought it would be a great idea to come to this high school in the morning?” questions Igor. Constantino smirks. “Dude, its three thirty. The kids are going home from school. It isn’t morning. Where were you last night?” Igor grumbles. “Its afternoon?” Constantino nods. “Ugh!” sighs Igor, “I was in the Darkhorse and this blonde lady said she knew me as a superstar. You see, I’m finally being realised as a superstar. I knew this was the right move for us.” “Hold up! She said she knew you”! We haven’t appeared on television or anything.” “I know. I know I was saying hello to Maverick and introducing myself, and then she just came over and we got chatting?” “Gentlemen!” The attention of the new GW superstars is suddenly taken by a large, black, burly man standing in front of the double door entrance to the school. “I assume you too men are the great Flying Vergomovs”!” enquiries the man, who is clearly at least a foot taller than the two circus performers turned wrestlers. “Hi!” Constantino waves, as the pair make their way onto the steps leading to the entrance. “I’ll finish that story later,” whispers Igor. “I don’t think I want to know,” replies his tag partner. “Gentlemen!” The pair reach the top of the steps; they stand face-to-chest with the large man. He is wearing a navy tracksuit, and carrying a red clipboard. “My names is Coach Mark Jackson. I teach here at Bristol High School and I am also the wrestling coach. I would prefer if you addressed me as sir, Mr. Jackson or Coach in front of the boys in the sports hall.” “No problem, Mark,” smiles Constantino. Mr. Jackson does not seem amused. “So what are you’re plans for this afternoon” I was hoping to warm the guys up and then you can teach whatever secrets you feel ready to let go, and won’t jeopardise your careers.” The coach chuckles as he looks down on the pair. The two men have puzzled expressions on their faces. The teacher turns on his heel and pushes through the double doors. Constantino spins and thumps Igor in the chest. “I thought you organised this?” whispers Constantino with a snappy undercurrent. “I did!” pleads Igor. The teacher disappears from view so the pair raise their voices back to conventional levels. “You obviously didn’t organise it very well. He thinks we have secrets!” Igor groans, and scratches is head while holding his baseball cap. “What are we supposed to do Igor?” Igor shrugs. “We have to pretend we have secrets, I suppose,” suggests Igor. “Oh, what a fucking unbelievable idea!” Constantino storms off and then turns back. His face has turned an unhealthy colour of red. “WE CAN PRETEND WE HAVE SECRETS”!” Igor shrugs. “IGOR! WE HAVE NEVER STEPPED IN A WRESTLING RING BEFORE!” “Dude, can you not shout” He might think something is up!” “OH I’M SORRY! IT’S NOT LIKE HE WON’T FIND OUT LATER!” “Con, seriously” CALM DOWN!” As Igor speaks, the entrance to the school is open again, and Mr. Jackson sticks his head out. “Problem gentlemen?” The Flying Vergomovs just stop and stare at the door. Constantino’s red face is quickly replaced with a very white look. The two men swallow hard. The silence extends. “No!” Igor speaks up. “No. There is no problem. Just a bit of disagreement about our session.” Mr. Jackson nods. “Well, when you are ready, if you would like to follow me.” The Flying Vergomovs nod and make their way towards the doors. Constantino steps through first, followed quickly by Igor. Coach Jackson steps forward at a quick pace. The two wrestlers walk behind. “What are we going to do?” whispers Constantino. “I don’t know,” replies Igor. There is silence as the three men walk down a winding hall. “I have a plan!” exclaims Igor. “Oh great!” sighs Constantino. “Just go with it!” snaps Igor. Mr. Jackson turns round. “Did you say something?” asks the wrestling coach. The Flying Vergomovs are silent. “No?” The teacher leans in towards the pair. “Yeah. Actually I did.” Igor steps forward. Mr. Jackson has a puzzled look on his face. “Walk with me, Mark.” Igor steps forward. He motions for the teacher to walk along side him. Constantino follows at least thirty feet behind. He looks around the halls and waves at small groups of girls as the pair in front walk. “As you may,” begins Igor, “or may not, know, Constantino and myself are a little rusty in the ring.” Mr. Jackson nods. “Anyway, we had maybe hoped to use this session as a chance to get ourselves prepared again for our Global Wrestling debut. We had hoped to possibly join in your training session with the teams and just be part of the class, and maybe if you want, we could try to teach them a couple of our moves, but only if they are really ready.” Mr. Jackson nods, but not as much as before. “Monday night, we will make our debut on television in the western world. And it is a really big deal for us. We want to make the best possible impression on the staff and owners of the company. We want them to be really sure that they made the right choice in hiring us as a tag team.” “Okay.” “So we want to join in the training session.” “Okay,” nods Mark Jackson. He stops in his tracks. “But haven’t the GW given you training time in the ring?” Igor nods. “Yeah, they have. But we want to make the best impression possible. We want to be something unexpected. We want to shock Ely and El. We want to surprise the whole locker room with our style, and to achieve that, we can’t be seen training in front of them. They would what we are about.” “Okay,” agrees Mr. Jackson. “This is your big chance, and you want it to be really big!” “Exactly.” The coach rubs his chin as he ponders the suggestion. The silence extends between the two men. Down the hall, Constantino is standing looking at some pictures on the wall. Every so often, he glances towards the two men. “I don’t know,” exclaims Coach Jackson. Igor’s hopeful expression quickly drops. “The boys,” continues the teacher, “they seemed really excited about being taught by a pair of professional wrestlers.” “I understand,” sighs Igor. There is a quick silence. Igor picks up his head. “But?” his hopeful expression returns as he reveals his plan, “this way the team will get to not only be trained by professional wrestlers but actually train with professional wrestlers.” Mr. Jackson continues to rub his chin. “Okay. I will take the session and treat just like the other students.” Igor smiles. Mr. Jackson smiles. Igor looks at his partner and smiles. Constantino smiles. Mr. Jackson turns and begins to walk down the hall. Constantino catches up with his tag partner. “The locker room is at the end of the hall. Hurry up! The teams are already in the sports hall!”
