The Final Beginning. Part 4 - The Last Supper continues

29 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29/3/03 So far, all wasn't going too badly with the dinner, despite of course the fact that the hatred in the room was as clear as the full moon which rests in the night sky outside giving life to the normally dark plains of the castle grounds. But still Salaok's eyes continued to move from Justin, to the empty seat which had a empty plate put in front of it which Salaok knows must be some sort of hint at who this person was. Why an empty plate? But now Salaok's attention was at least divided among another position and that was straight down at his food. He was a big man and food was one of his loves, which would explain the cooks being hired to look after the cooking. Quartz was much the same as Salaok with his attention drawn in three different places. His food, Vincent and Rose. His eyes moving from each one and the fact that he happened to be seated directly opposite Rose wasn't helping the matter at all. Their past was not clear to anyone who wasn't Rose or Quartz but everyone knew that something had happened in the past. Each time Rose caught Quartz looking at her, he wouldn't look away straight away, he would keep eye contact and then slowly look back down to his food or over to Vincent. Clearly there was a look of unease on Rose's face because of Quartz, or was it simply because of the situation in which she found herself. Two men in whom meant a lot to her in the past hated each other with such passion that even her presence wasn't enough to make them see past it. The first meal was quickly out of the way and the waiting staff came out and removed the plates, including the blank one in front of the empty seat which again drew Salaok's attention to it. 'It's bothering you isn't it Salaok?' Justin's voice was the sound the broke the silence and all eyes turned straight to Salaok for the response and the silence appears again as Salaok stares straight at Justin when suddenly Johnathon speaks up. 'Who is the seat for Justin? I know that you're wanting to say something about it, otherwise you wouldn't have opened your mouth. It's bugging you not being able to say anything more than it's bugging us.' 'You may be right there, but this way Johnathon, I hold the cards.' Justin's response was exactly how he had wanted this little situation to go and he grinned at Johnathon which did nothing more than further annoy the Scarecrow. As the tension in the room continues to build, the second course of the meal is brought out to the table and again, the plate is left empty in front of the free seat. Small conversations go on between the smaller groups at the table and Salaok catches a glimpse of Rose passing something to Quartz across the table while Vincent isn't looking. A small piece of people which Quartz holds under the table and reads. With every word, his eyes seem to grow and then he finally looks up at Rose with disbelief and she nods to him before Vincent turns around and begins to talk to her again. Salaok looks over at Quartz who feels Salaok's eyes burning a hole in the side of his head and looks at him. But Salaok's attention is soon diverted from Quartz as two figures are seen walking down the stairs together. Two small figures, that of children. Salaok's son Darrell, and Justin's son Emmanuell walk down the stairs together. They had been close all the time, despite the war between the two fathers. Darrell sees his father and runs up, jumping up onto his lap and grinning at Salaok while Emmanuell, who is a year younger than Darrell, walks up to his father and stands next to him, resting his head on Justin's shoulder. The feeling in the room changes as soon as the two boys make their presence felt, although Salaok still glares at Justin and Justin returns the favor. Darrell begins to pick some of the food from off of Salaok's plate, although Salaok never even notices. Suddenly the sound of a window shattering is heard and everyone looks at it. At first a small hole in the window and then it gives way all at once, letting the entire thing fall to the ground. No one has a clue as to what is going on but out on the hill, there stands a figure, small, but strong looking. From his seated position, Salaok can't see who it is and so puts Darrell down and rushes to the window to peer out into the moon lit night. Quartz joins Salaok and they both see the same thing. Mani Shigeru standing on the top of a small hill with a sniper gun in his hands and from over the top of the hill, a larger figure appears and stands behind Mani. The larger figure strikes Quartz's heart as soon as he sees him for it is none other than Silent Wind. 'MUMMA!' Darrell's cry was loud and full of fear, which instantly drew Salaok's attention. Lisha was bleeding from her stomach. Lisha had been shot. The baby? 'Sage, get Darrell and my nephew up stairs away from all windows.' Salaok's order was direct and Sage followed the order straight away, picking up Darrell in one arm and then Emmanuell in the other. Salaok rushes over to Lisha who hasn't made a single sound, but her eyes are full of tears that are yet to spill. Johnathon is already on the phone calling for help and Grendel and Kullypso have straight away gone and found some cloth to put over Lisha's wound. Salaok looks Lisha in the ear and sees the strength in her as she bite down on her bottom lip, not wanting to make a sound. Not wanting to have let them win. Salaok holds her tight in his arms and hears her whisper something. 'Our baby?' 'Don't think about that right now. Don't worry, everything will be alright.' Salaok's response was as calming as he could possibly make it but the cracking in his voice was clear to everyone. Grendel passes a piece of cloth to Salaok and he holds it down on the wound while laying Lisha down onto the ground. Quartz stands at the window and looks at the two men in whom he hates and the one in which he gave life to. Quartz catches Mani's mouth as he says something and lowers his head. Reading his lips, Quartz seems confused. To Quartz it looked as if Mani had just said 'sorry Lisha' and now he was turning and walking away. As soon as Quartz sees Mani and Jude begin to walk away, Quartz burst for the door and not waiting to open it, flattens the thing with a huge shoulder and keeps running. A tear finally falls from Lisha's eye and runs down her cheek. The ambulance would arrive soon and everything would be OK. Or at least that is what Salaok kept telling himself. Lisha looks up at Salaok with her wet eyes wide open and she takes a deep breath before putting her hand on the side of his face again and lets out three small words that strikes at Salaok's heart like a million knives. 'I love you.' Salaok looks up to the heaven above and shuts his eyes tightly and takes a deep breath of his own before lowering himself down and kissing Lisha ever so gently on the lips and responding. 'I have always loved you Lisha, and I will be damned if I will let them take you from me. Be strong my love. Be strong.' With that, Lisha smiles and slowly closes her eyes. Her breathing continues but at a slightly higher rate now as Salaok has to change the cloth on her wound as it had become completely saturated with blood. He couldn't loose her. She was the strength that kept him going. Her and Darrell were his entire life and the new baby was to become a part of that. But now the idea of losing his unborn child through murder was making his blood boil and was clearly getting to him. He looks over to his childhood friend in Grendel, trying not to let him sees the tears in his eyes. 'Ring Diasporo and have him meet me at the hospital. Tell him there is a five million dollar contract waiting for him.' Grendel nods in response to Salaok's request and now the contract out on Mani and Jude was going to be final. Five million dollars was now the price on the heads of the two men who has shot his wife. Clearly things had gone far past personal now and what would become of it was unknown to all. Things had never gotten to this stage before and it struck home with Salaok the most. Johnathon continued to stand there, simply looking on, knowing full well that there was nothing else he could do. From the corner of his eye, Johnathon sees Justin, Brian, Vincent and rose all walk out of the door but no one is concerned with them except for Salaok who screams out after Justin. 'Not even your Lord all might can save you from my wrath Justin. For this you will die and burn in hell with me.'
