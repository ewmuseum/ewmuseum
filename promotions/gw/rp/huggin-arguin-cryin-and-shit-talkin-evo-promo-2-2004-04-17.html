Date: April 17 It was going to be one of those nights. Two hours earlier, Miranda had escorted two V.I.P.s to the upstairs suite of the Palace Hotel and Casino. It was the end of her first year as a bouncer, but she’d been in Vegas all her life, and she knew these men even before they introduced themselves; the man on the left, with the bulging muscles and bald head was Tony Grivaldi, hired gun, muscle’whatever you want to call it. The man on the right, Don Vito Manelli, owner of more than three casinos on the strip, not to mention this hotel Miranda was working in. Now she was escorting a third man, one Johnny Franklin, local card shark and all around hustler. Miranda hated the guy, naturally, because he often took an easy score away from her. But she had no idea what she was about to get herself into. Stepping through the door into the suite, she announced Mr. Franklin’s arrival; at the same time, two masked men pushed past her and into the room’and she caught the glint of gunmetal. Miranda: “Holy shit! Duck!” Corny? Yes, but it got the message across! Miranda spun away from the door, yanking Johnny back with her, but too late. CRACK! CRACK! The gunshots about made her deaf as a doornail, as she felt Johnny’s body go limp in her arms. Slamming against the wall, she watched his body flop to the floor, two good-sized holes in his chest, blood oozing like a broken dam from the wounds. Miranda: “FUCK ME!” Two more shots and she poked her head around the door. Don Manelli was ducked behind a thick table, Grivaldi lying dead in the floor with half his head missing. The two men continued to stand bravely just inside the doorway. Time to earn her paycheck! A shiver running up her spine in adrenaline rushed fear, Miranda spun back into the doorway, her right foot catching masked goon number one right in the back of the head! Goodnight! His partner crumpling to the floor, masked goon number two spun, his gun coming around at Miranda’CRACK! Flinching, Miranda closed her eyes just as blood and skull bone pelted her face. Gross! But just like that, it was all over. Miranda slowly opened her eyes, leaning against the door jam and breathing unevenly as her eyes settle on three dead bodies’luckily not one of them was her. Welcome to Vegas? --- Miranda sat up out of bed with a sudden gasp, sweat glistening on her naked form as she quickly looked over the dark room, spotting her sleeping sister across from her on the other bed. Shivering a moment, Miranda finally laid back down on the bed, catching her breath. Miranda: “Fucking hell’I hate that!” Kali: 'Nightmares again?' Kali’s voice was quiet as it rose above the slight darkness, her gown covered body buried under the mess of covers on her bed. Miranda blinked, looking back over to her sister again. So much for not waking anyone up! Miranda nods quietly, her breathing a bit slower now as she pulls the covers up over her form, shivering slightly.
