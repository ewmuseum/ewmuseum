Coup d'etat (Enfilade)

7 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/7/05 Click... Click... Click... The sound of heels on concrete beat like a snare drum down the concrete sidewalks in cadence. While the series of clicks had a rhythm to them, the woman they were attached to had one of her own. Confident and defiant she walked down the path. Behind her could be seen a car, still as the sky with its headlights on. A closer look would reveal that the front right tire had been flattened and the front left was going down. She turned around and took a long hard look back at her damaged car... 'That's perfect, I don't even know where I am...' she said as she turned back and kept walking down the concrete path. The streets were eerily quiet... not a car or person in sight. This part of the neighborhood seemed to be full of run down warehouses and old apartment buildings. She looked down the street through the maze of streetlights and saw what appeared to be a scrap yard of some sort. Hopefully she could find some tires, or at least someone who could get her a ride to some tires there. Click... Click... Click... She stopped in her tracks, and spun around. Her long blonde hair spun majestically, like an angle across her blue eyes as she peered into the darkness. She paused without making a sound looking around her... she knew something was out there, but where... and what? Maybe it was just a stray alley cat... or maybe it was a mugger. Click... Click... Click... The clicking sound grew closer and closer as she balled her hands into fists. The clicking cadence matched her heartbeat as she located where the sound was coming from. Peering deep into a black alley way the dim light reflected off his black jacket... 'I see you... if you're planning on mugging me, you're in for a big surprise buddy.' She said confidently as the man stepped into the light. 'Don't worry Gunner... I don't need your money. I have enough of my own.' The man said as he stood underneath the dim street light. A pair of black sunglasses shielded his cadet blue eyes from her sight. But she didn't need to see his eyes, Gunner Addison already knew the name. 'So this is why they call you The Midnight Messiah? You walk around in alleyways and stalk girls? I'm not some little girl Zenith... I'm in the Hall of Fame you're forgetting. If you plan to take me alone, you might need to rethink your strategy.' Gunner Addison said as Zenith looked blankly in her direction. With the sunglasses, she couldn't quite tell what exactly he was looking at. With those words Zenith held his right arm into the air and snapped his fingers. Behind him the footsteps of an army could be heard. Gunner looked behind Zenith as they walked closer and closer. Behind him was a 7 foot tall monster, as well as two others. Stepping into the light it became clear who they were... Demitri Miaskinov, Jason LeMaster, and Vivica J. Valentine. 'You were saying?' Vivica J. Valentine said with a grin on her face as she locked eyes with Gunner Addison... 'I was saying... I'm glad you came along, I was just thinking that this was going to be boring.' Gunner said as she put up her fists ready to fight. 'Good... I'm always up for a party.' Vivica said before she charged out at Gunner Addison. Vivica leaped into the air with an outside crescent kick, but Addison ducked under and grabbed Vivica by the hair and threw her into the nearby light post. Vivica hit the post and fell to the ground holding her arm as she got up on one knee. 'So what do you want from me Christian? You gonna beat me up to send a message to KPJ? What a message you're going to send... it takes me and three of my friends to beat up your girlfriend.' Gunner Addison said as Vivica got back up to her feet and started shaking her arm. 'Actually... I'm not here to hurt you, I'm just here to get you. I didn't expect you to lay down like a nice pussy cat, so I brought some kitty tamers. You can go the hard way, or you can go the easy way.' Christian said as he looked over at Vivica. 'If there is one thing I'm not... it's easy.' Gunner replied. 'I didn't expect anything less.' Zenith said as Jason ran out from behind him towards Addison. Jason went to slide and take out Gunner's legs soccer style, but she jumped over him with ease as Vivica charged. Vivica stopped quickly to let Jason slide by, giving Gunner the opportunity to do a roundhouse. Vivica slapped her leg away and Gunner was grabbed from behind by the monster Demitri. Demitri squeezed Gunner, trying to make her pass out. Her face scrunched up as she tried to break free. Demitri snarled as he applied more and more pressure, but suddenly Gunner's leg shot up backwards and the pressure ceased. Demitri ground and fell to the ground slouched over with his legs together in pain. Gunner fell down to one knee and Vivica charged once again. Vivica threw a punch and it was knocked aside. Gunner tried to counter with a right hook, but Vivica dodged and caught her right arm and throw her over her shoulder. Gunner hit the ground and swept Vivica's legs out from under her. Gunner got up and suddenly Christian Zenith buries his fist into Gunner's abdomen. Addison stumbles back in shock as LeMaster walks up behind her... his walk turns into a run as he goes to hit her with a double ax handle. Gunner falls to her knees instinctively, making Jason jump over her. Unable to control his momentum, Jason charges at Zenith who side steps him. Gunner leaps up with a superkick that hits Christian in the jaw. Zenith stumbles backwards and Demitri grabs Gunner from behind and pulls her arms behind her back for a free shot by Vivica. Valentine walks up to slap Gunner, but suddenly Addison uses Demitri's body weight to throw him over her shoulder into Valentine. Both crash into the ground and just in time for Gunner to grab a charging Jason LeMaster and throw him into the light post. Jason falls down holding his head, Demitri doing the same. Gunner Addison begins to breathe harder as she looks over at Christian Zenith grabbing his jaw slightly. 'So... this is all you got?' Gunner said between deep breathes. Zenith just stared back at her without saying a word, unrattled. Behind her Vivica J. Valentine rose to her feet and pulled something out of her back pocket. 'Time to put you to sleep kitty.' Vivica said as she whipped out a gun quickly and fired. The white dart went speeding through the air and hit Gunner Addison in the side of the neck. Addison fell down to one knee and looked up at Vivica J. Valentine who watched with a smile. 'You bitch...' Gunner said as she fell to the ground, limp and paralyzed. 'I know... but if I wasn't, you would've beat me.' Vivica said as Demitri slowly makes his way up to one knee, holding his head. Demitri was noticeably shaken but Jason is still on the ground, bleeding from his head as Christian looks down at him. Zenith shakes his head before he looks over at Gunner Addison on the ground... 'C'mon... shake it off guys, we have work to do.' Hello? Hello Mother Leopard I have your cub You must protect her But that will be expensive Ten-Thousand cola nuts Wrapped in brown paper Midnight behind the box I'll be the hyena you'll see The dinging of an elevator door resonates down the burgundy carpeted hallway. The cream colored walls were decorated with fancy gold and glass lamps and an embossed floral pattern. The doors to the elevator opened and a rectangle of light hit the wall adjacent from the elevator. The silhouette of an individual grew larger and larger against the rectangle until they came into clear view. They wore a pair of khaki colored pants and a black hooded sweatshirt which concealed their face. The elevator doors shut as it speeded away, leaving the hallway to return to its dimly lit existence. The hooded one walked down the hallway slowly, looking at each room along the way. The slow, yet meticulous pace of the individual became eerie yet calm. Suddenly the faceless person stopped in front of a door labeled 317 and dropped to one knee very quietly. In a stealth-like fashion, the individual pulled what looked to be a small silver key out of their pants pocket and slid it underneath the door. The individual then left the same way they came... with an assassin like silence. They opened up the staircase door and closed it behind them with a nearly inaudible click. Their footsteps echoed through the stairwell before they stopped 2 floors below where they started and slid inside the door, into another quiet hallway. With one motion, they pulled a phone from their belt loop and flipped it open with a beep... 'The key is in place, it's showtime.' A woman's voice said before she flipped it up and back onto her belt loop. With that she pulled off her black hoodie and tied it around her waist. A smile drew across the face of Vivica J. Valentine as she stepped back into the stairwell and sprinted down the stair case to the ground floor. One the other end of the page Christian Zenith flipped his phone shut as he leaned back in his wooden chair with his feet propped up on the desk in front of him. Zenith took a look behind him where Gunner Addison laid unconscious and gagged, tied up in thick chains and a padlock. Such an elaborate wrap that even Houdini couldn't have escaped from. Laying one cell phone on the ground, Christian picked a similar looking one up next to it. As he flipped it open a picture of a sky appeared on the screen before he started scrolling through the names. Finally, he stopped on a name that read 'KEN' and dialed, waiting as the phone rang... 'Oh hey Gunner, where have you been? I've been calling you for hours...' the sentence trailed off the lips of Ken Partridge Jr. as he didn't get a response. Something inside him told him that something was wrong for a while now, and this only solidified his intuition. 'Partridge... your partner has left the pear tree. They say that a bird in the hand is worth two in the bush, but I need to satisfy my appetite. There is a scrap yard near the end of town to the West, you'll pass Gunner's car on the way there. Meet me at the train tracks, behind the scrap yard if you ever want to see your precious again. You'll find everything you need on the inside of your hotel room door, I'll be waiting for you.' With a click it was over. KPJ's brilliant blue eyes narrowed as he place the cellular phone back into his pocket. His hands balled into a fist as he looked down on the ground in front of his door and saw a small gold key, glittering under the ceiling lights. Valentine. At his feet, laid a GW Hall of Famer... and at this moment he thought to himself about all the Hall of Famers past, present, and future that will be doing the same thing. Christian rose from his chair and walked over to Gunner Addison and looked down at the beautiful 6 foot tall, blonde hair, blue eyed beauty. 'I bet you're wondering how all this happened aren't you?' Christian said as he turned his back and began walking towards the front of the huge moving truck in which he and Gunner now was in, parked in a dark alley, 'Well as you've already witnessed... Miss Valentine is quite a shot. You should see her with a silence sniper rifle and a scope. She had no problems taking out both your front tires. As for your cell phone, we stole it from your hotel room last night... I'm sure you realized it was missing but thought nothing of it. In reality, it's on purpose was to call Ken unsuspectingly... we were quite pleased when the lack of it forced you out of your car and into the streets, made our task a bit easier.' Just then a knock, three times on the back of the truck interrupted Zenith. The door opened to expose Vivica J. Valentine and a blue dodge ram directly behind her. Vivica jumped up into the truck and looked down at Gunner Addison, all locked up. She looked up at Christian... 'Ken has the key... slid it under the door, never saw a single person in the process.' Vivica said. 'Good... Jason and Demitri?' Zenith questioned. 'Dropped them off at the local urgent care... Jason needed some stitches and Demitri has a mild concussion. They told me they'd be released tomorrow, they should be fine by Reload.' Vivica answered. 'What did you tell them?' Zenith asked, with curiosity. 'Bar fight, with each other,' Vivica said as she laughed a little bit, 'they said they see it all the time.' 'Don't surprise me in a redneck hell hole like this, we better get Gunner where we need her, I'm sure Partridge isn't too far behind you.' Zenith said as he picked up the chained Gunner Addison and threw her over his shoulder. Vivica followed while Christian walked over to the back of the Dodge Ram and dumped her in the bed. Valentine opened up the driver's side door with a smile and said... 'Let's go, I can't wait to see the look on his face.' A 20 foot high chain link fence separates the Dodge from towers of sheet metal and debris. Flood lights high over the scrap yard shine down on what's below. Vivica J. Valentine looked up at the flood lights squinting as Zenith opened the tailgate on the back of the massive pickup truck. In the bed of the truck, Gunner's eyes were closed as she lay limp under the flood light. Zenith picked her up over his right shoulder and carried here away from the light, towards two pairs of train tracks. Christian threw her down perpendicular across the tracks with authority. She lay face-up and helpless ontop of the tracks. Vivica walked over next to Christian... who looked down at his watch. 'What time is the train supposed to run by?' Vivica questioned. 'Over the past 2 weeks, it's crossed here consistently at 1:13am, on the dot.' Christian answered. 'And the time?' Vivica added. 'It's 1:10am.' Zenith said with a villainous smile across his face. 'What if he don't make it in time?' Vivica responded. 'Oh don't worry... he'll be here.' Christian said taking his eyes off the watch and looked down the train tracks towards where the train will be coming from. On a dark street, a car whizzes past a group of thugs hanging out on the corner. Inside, the driver Ken Partridge Jr. exceeds speeds of 100 mph on his way to the scrap yard at the end of town. He swerves in and out of the shoddy 1am traffic on the streets. Smoke billowed off his tires as he flew around the corner, almost hitting a nearby light post. The end of his car fishtailed back as he slammed down the gas pedal, narrowly avoiding a car parked on the side of the road... 'That was Gunner's car...' KPJ said as he flew down the street. Up ahead, he saw flood lights beaming on to what appeared to be a scrap yard. His eyes narrowed and his engine revved as he drag raced time down the dimly lit street, lined with abandoned buildings infested with vermin. Zenith looked directly into the headlights of the on-coming car as it ramped over the curb and into the grass, about 40 yards from where Zenith stood. Christian looked down the train tracks, and saw a light coming around the corner. KPJ jumped out of the car and looked into the cadet blue eyes of Christian Zenith, who stood confidently on the train tracks. 'What have you done to her Zenith?!' KPJ screamed as he ran towards Christian. 'Exactly what's about to happen to you!' KPJ stumbles sideways as Valentine pounces back up to her feet. Vivica tried to do a roundhouse kick on KPJ, but Partridge catches her in mid-move and throws her hard into the ground. KPJ looks over at Zenith as he begins moving towards him... 'Get your lap dog off of me Zenith, and fight me like a man.' KPJ screamed as he charged towards Christian. The train continued to rumble down the tracks, blowing his horn vigorously in hopes of getting Zenith to move... 'You can fight me, or you can save your girlfriend... I assure you that you can't do both.' Christian responded, raising his voice over the oncoming train. It was then when KPJ noticed Gunner Addison shackled and unconscious on the tracks. KPJ looked at the oncoming train grow closer and closer... 'I assure you... I can.' KPJ said as he charged at Zenith. Christian braced himself as Partridge tackled him to the ground. Zenith kicked the franchise off of him and got back up to his feet to be met with a hard right, making him stumble backwards. The train's rumble turned into a roar as Zenith tried his best to fend off the attacks from KPJ. Punches came flying in from seemingly every direction. Suddenly Zenith caught KPJ's fist and was hit in the back of the head by an enviguri. Christian fell to the ground as KPJ ran towards Gunner Addison on the ground. 'I'm coming!' Ken screamed over the roar of the train, just a few feet away from Gunner's helpless body laying on the tracks. With an unparalleled burst of speed, KPJ sprinted towards Gunner Addison as her body began to shake from the tremors on the track. The trains horn blared as KPJ scooped up Gunner Addison and leaped for his life. The barely struck the feet of KPJ as he barely dodged the freight train with his life. He laid stunned in the grass for a few seconds next to Gunner Addison, with the adrenaline pumping through his veins. Out from his pocket he pulled out a key, and unlocked the padlock which secure the chains Gunner was wrapped in. Addison's eyes opened slowly as she looked up at her Superman. Still dizzy from the tranquilizer, she was happy for the image to focus to that of Ken Partridge Jr. as the train rumbled past them a mere two feet from where they lay. Weakly with a smile Gunner Addison sat up and wrapped her arms around KPJ, and he rubbed her back. She could feel his heart pounding, the comforted each other... but KPJ knew it was only a matter of time until the train passed with Zenith and Valentine on the other side. Christian sat in the grass on the other side of the train watching it pass by in disbelief, Vivica walked over to him. Zenith looked down at his watch when Vivica sat down beside him. She looked over at him with confusion... 'I don't believe it! What went wrong?' Vivica questioned. 'I took the fall too early, the train is running 2 seconds late.'
