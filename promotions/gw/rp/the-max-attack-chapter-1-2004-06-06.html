The Max-Attack: Chapter 1

6 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 6. The scene opens back up to the hotel room Roswell is in. Everything is calm but then from outside Roswell hears screaming and crying. Roswell slowly gets up from his chair and opens the curtains on the window. Roswell looks out and sees ladies and other people laying on the ground with a blue creature flying in the sky above them. Roswell quickly closes the curtains and runs out the door not taking a key or anything. Outside Roswell runs towards a lady who is laying on the ground with a baby in her arms. Roswell helps the lady and baby up and runs to take them onto a nearby bench. Roswell then runs back to the creature and yells at it. “Leave all these people alone! They didn’t do anything to you!” From the sky a low voice is heard. “What are you going to do about it, MAX!” “How do you know my name!” “Oh I don’t know! I’ve kept about 10 newspapers from last week and you’ve been on the cover of 5 of them!” “Well at least I help people unlike what you are doing right now.” “Help people, YEAH RIGHT! All is what you do is fake worthless crap.” “WHAT! Ok now you crossed the line!” “Oh wow I crossed the line, what you going to do about it!” Max suddenly picks up a huge rock from the concrete and throws it at the creature. The creatures turns away and looks back but gets smacked in the face with the rock. The creature falls to the ground holding his face. Roswell walks over to the creature and looks down at him. The creature suddenly gets up like nothing happened and kicks Roswell straight in the balls. Roswell falls to the ground holding his crotch area but gets punched in the face. The creature picks up Roswell with one hand and punches him yet again making him fall back to the ground. “Have you had enough!” “Never. Roswell never gives up!” Roswell suddenly starts holding his head then the creature in front of him flies back and hits a metal pole. Roswell starts holding his head again and starts looking in horror. Suddenly inside his head pictures of a car crashing into a semi are shown. Roswell then sees a pictures that is inside one of the cars. Roswell sees his dad! Roswell then opens his eyes and looks at the fallen creature. “I think that was a flashback!” Roswell then flips up onto his feet kind of dazed. Roswell then walks over to a telephone booth and walks inside it. Then there is a loud shriek from the telephone booth. The door of the booth opens and Roswell walks out but has a red and black mask on. He is wearing a red and black suit with a red spider like design in the middle. “What happened to me! I thing this is my Max-Attack version of me!” Max-Attack walks over to the creature but before he can do anything the creature quickly flies into the air holding his rib’s. The creature glides away in the air but yells something out to Max. “I am The Blue Bat, and no one messes with me!”
