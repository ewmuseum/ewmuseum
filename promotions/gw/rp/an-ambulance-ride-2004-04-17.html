An Ambulance Ride.

17 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 17. (#2 for Evo) “Uh Oh.” I said. “This is bad.” the police officer said. “We have to goto the zoo immediately or else Roz is going to kill my mother!” I said. We walked out of the back alley and back onto the sidewalk thinking how we were going to get there with no car. We walked for a few minutes until a taxi stopped right beside us. The drive inside rolled down the windows and called over to us. We turned and saw him with a smile on his face. “Looks like you guys need a lift.” the driver said. “Yeah, actually we do.” I said. “Hop in.” he said. Me and the officer walked onto the curb of the street. The officer went in the front where the driver was and I went into the back. After we got in and shut the door the card started moving along down the street. The car stopped at a red light as the driver turned towards me and started talking. “So, where you guys going?” “The zoo.” the officer said. “The zoo it is then.” he said. The light switched to green as the driver kept going down the street. The taxi stopped at a big brick wall that had a sign on it that said, “The Zoo.” I took out my wallet and handed the taxi driver 5 bucks as me and the officer got out of the car. We walked up to a little counter with glass on it with a lady standing behind it. I handed the lady 10 bucks as she gave me and the officer a ticket each. “Thank you for coming.” she said to us. “No problem.” I said back to her. Me and the officer walked through the door and into the open zoo. We looked around and saw little children playing and petting some animals. It makes me remember when my mom used to take me here when I was a kid. It used to be so fun. I can’t wait to have children of my own so I can bring them her so they can have fun. We walked down the sidewalk of the zoo for a bit. We walked past the lions, bears, elephants, and rhinos and then got to the Tiger’s cage. “Hey, what’s that over there?” I asked the officer. I pointed to the tigers cage, it had a little white note hanging off the bars of the cage. We ran over to the cage and grabbed the note. I opened it and started reading it aloud. “So, you’ve finally found me, well, not me but your mother. Haha, well, your probably wondering why I said not me but your mother. Just look inside the cage and take a look at your dead mother.” it read. I dropped the note as the wind took it. Me and the officer looked inside the cage and saw my mother laying on the hard cement. There were 2 tigers sitting beside her. My mothers eyes were closed and it looked like she wasn’t breathing. I started banging on the cages as the tigers looked up and ran to the cage. Me and the officers backed off. “How are we going to get her?” the officer asked. “We have to get some staff that work here to open the cage, tame the lions and get my mother out of that dangerous place.” I said. A staff member walked by us and I poked him on the shoulder. He turned around and looked at me and the officer. He started laughing. “Aren’t you guys a little too big to be here in the zoo?” he asked us. “My mother is in this cage and I need someone to get her out.” I said. “Oh my god, are you serious?” he asked. “Dead serious.” I told him. “We need some more help here!” the guy yelled out. A whole bunch of workers came running to where we were standing. They stopped and they were out of breath. “What did you need?” they asked. “This man’s mother is in the tigers cage, someone tame the tigers and he can get his mom.” he said. Some workers opened the cage with a big key as a whole bunch of them ran at the lions and sat them down. I ran with the officer to my mother and picked her up off the ground. She was still breathing. I ran out of the cage and layed her down onto the soft grass. I checked her heart and she was still breathing and everything. I tried to wake her up but she didn’t wake. “We need some help here!” I yelled out. A few minutes later a few paramedics came running to where we were sitting. They had a stretcher and everything. They picked my mom up and put her onto the stretcher and rolled it away to the entrance of the zoo. I ran after them. They put my mother into the back of the ambulance but I got there in time. “Hey, stop. That’s my mother!” I said. “Oh well then you should come along.” one paramedic said. Me and the officer hopped into the back of the ambulance. We sat down on the carpet that covered the whole floor of the ground. My mother was breathing harder meaning she was waking up. Suddenly, my mother’s eyes opened as the scene started to fade to darkness.
