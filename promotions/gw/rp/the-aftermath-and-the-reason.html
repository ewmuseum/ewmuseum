The aftermath and the reason

Date: ... He left the church the same way in which he knew he was going to be. Alone. Despite knowing that Gabriel would struggle to fathom the idea of Salaok seeking his council, he knew that one viewing of Salaok's tortured soul wouldn't be enough to convince this former Knight that it was time to leave those walls. Walls that were binding for him. Safe for him. Safe because his beliefs were within those walls and outside of them lurked too many temptations that might show Gabriel that the way of his new Lord Jesus weren't the only ways. Wanting nothing more than to remind Gabriel of the Void in which to be apart of his life and hopefully draw upon that memory to attain a powerful ally in the world. Salaok had gone there with the intent of proving to Gabriel that he was a changed man, which in deed he was. However, it wasn't the change that he showed to Gabriel as should he had seen that Salaok had gone further than before, than gabriel's help would have been shut off at the gates to that church. As his feet pound the ground beneith him andthe distance between himself and the church grows, he feels the eyes of The Master of the Void perring down on him. Watching him. Seeing him. For that was the difference between Gabriel and most men as Gabriel would not only watch you, but would see you. Would see the thoughts and the feelings in which you caried with you all of the way and it was this thought alone that made Salaok wonder if the coat of deception had been broken? The time that had been spent in the church had been a whole afternoon and yet Salaok hadn't exploded into his usual state of rage which must have appeared strange for a man who has known Salaok for such a long time. However, the man in which knew Salaok was not the man who he had spoken with today for that was a man of the Lord. The man in which Salaok had known and been convinced to be apart of The Void in the first place was anything but that. A man who had been respected by everyone who had ever been willing to open their eyes and see the world the way it truely is. However, far to many people in the world today shut off what they can not fathom and instead go along their merry ways believing that everything that they believe to be true and not willing to accept that for once they could indeed be wrong. Salaok used to be one of those people. The only correct way in life was to stand with The Lair and all those who were foolish enough to stand against such a force in the world weren't living their lives the way in which they should. Anyone in whom was willing to stand against the Lair was an enemy even if their were a dear friend. Much like Gabriel was to Salaok. A friend. There wasn't many of them left in the world that were alive and so for those few who were still around, their guidance and their words were more important than anyone would choose to believe. Even The Forever Knights words of wisedom. Words that had sunk deep into Salaok's mind, despite that fact that they weren't the words in which Salaok was wanting to hear. He had gone to The Chuch of Gabriel in hope to find an ally once more, but instead found a man who wanted nothing more than to sit on the side lines and watch the team as a spectator. That was not the man in whom Salaok had known the better half of his life. The Gabriel, the Master of the Void, The Forever Knight was the man to take control of a situation and make sure that it was done the way in which he intended. But now he simply is willing to let God devine way take control of what ever is happening, willing to believe that what happens is indeed for the best for everyone. Well it's not the best for everyone. Infact it's far from the best for Salaok to have this new generation of Nevermore fail. His money and his power was enough to allow him to do almost anything in the world, but this control of Global Inc was something that had to be done through sheer display of power and brutality as the Nevermore has always functioned. However, Gabriel was willing to simply let his faith guide him. Faith in the cross. ---- He had been left for dead. Hanging there on the cross, looking at the rising sun, burning into the back of his eyes and the blood draining from his body. His father and brother-in-law had simply nailed him up and left him to die alone. But he would not be alone for a long time. Like always, his brother seemed to know when something was wrong and always seemed to be there. Salaok’s eyes watched on as the brown station wagon moved up the hill. There was no road where this car was going, but that didn’t seem to matter. It ploughed though everything in it’s way until it finally reached the peak where Salaok and his cross stood above all. But that was some time ago now. Three or so month back now. Times had changed. Salaok had changed. His brother however was as he always was. He would appear and then disappear without any thought. Johnathon had saved Salaok and now had left him to be on his own again. However Salaok wouldn’t last long on his own. He now sits in a padded cell, his hair messed and becoming more and more white. Whether it be the stress or the drugs that they were feeding him here, something had changed the color of half of his hair. Something at the same time was messing with his mind. The constant laughter coming forth from the cell seemed to cause worry among the staff members at the ward. Salaok had never been normal, and his time in the mental wrd was proof of this. Sitting in the corner, huddled up into a ball, he would spend his days. The time that he was allowed outside into the sunlight he would refuse. Happy just to sit. Sit and stare and the blank wall in front of him as he would repeat the same things day after day. Day after day he would talk about Lisha and Darrell. His family that was a family no more. Lisha was gone and Darrell was being looked after by Sage. Salaok was alone and right now that was the best thing for him. If someone was to see him like this, they would believe he had lost his marbles. But in truth?..he has. Two nurses walk down the hall, each one with a note pad under their under and dress exactly the same. Working in these places didn’t do a lot for one’s individuality nor their dress sense. But for some unknown reason, this was the profession that they chose and now as they walk past the doors, they make ideal chit chat. The weather, who’s sleeping with who, that TV show last night. However their annoying ramblings are soon silenced as a deafening roar comes from Salaok’s room. Looking at each other, they move towards the door which is heavily bolted and with warning notices to the staff plastered to it. This was no place for two young women to be going into, and yet with his roars only becoming louder and full of what one could only take as pain, the natural sense of a human being’s caring nature begins to take a hold of them. The blonde, who was slightly taller than the red haired girl reaches for her keys and begins to move them towards the locks, only to have her hand grabbed and forced away. “What in the hell do you think you are doing?” Question’s the red head with almost fear in her voice. The blonde simply doesn’t understand the situation and shrugs while answering. “I’m going to go and check on him.” “Are you stupid” Do you know who this is?” The blonde grins and unbuttons the top button of her shirt while raising an eyebrow. “Oh course I know who it is. Salaok Davis is in there. Big time sports entertainer. He’s just retired from the job and now he’s in here because he’s having withdrawal from the attention. That’s all.” Not fully being able to understand how this blonde girl was so stupid and ignorant, the red hair chick takes the keys from out of her hand before beginning to tell her work-mate the real story. “You are only partly right. Yes it’s Salaok Davis. Yes he used to be a big name on TV. No it’s not withdrawal from the attention he’s lacking. He sat in the same room as his pregnant wife as she was shot dead by his own half brother. He sat by the bed side as she and the unborn baby died and to add salt to his already wounded mind, his brother-in-law and father crucified him and left him for dead.” The blonde girl suddenly moves away from the door a bit not realizing that Salaok had been throw so much. And then she questions, “Crucified?” “Yes. They actually nailed his hands to the wooden cross. You can’t see it now because of the straight jacket, but both of his hands have giant holds in them. The blood and scar tissue had already begun to fuse around the giant nails by the time he was taken down by his older brother. Doctors spent days fixing his mangled fingers and attaching nerves endings but nothing was done to plug up the wholes.” In hearing this, the blonde walks up to the door and looks through the peep hole only to see Salaok where he always sat, in the corner, rocking back and forth in his straight jacket. “He looks to be in pain and those roars don’t sound good either.” She says as she backs away from the peep hole. The red haired girl is about to say something but as her mouth opens, another one of Salaok loud roars riffles out into the still and stale air. His roars seem to be agitating the other inmates who also begin to make noise and bang against the doors. With all of the commotion in the ward, it isn’t long until the red lights start to flash and the sound of thunderous footsteps comes bounding down the steal stairs. One by one, guards come running down into the wing with their batons, guns and anything else that they could find drawn out and ready for the worst. ---- Darting from left to right, up and down, his eyes moves frantically as he checks to see if the coast is clear. It had only been a few days since he had escaped from the Mental Ward which had cost innocent people their lives and the hunt for his head was still a big topic on the news. Why is it when someone well known does something, they instantly become a target of hatred and spread all over the papers making sure that everyone would know who they are even though they already do. And yet should someone unknown do the same thing, the scale of interest would be no where near the amount. In this world today, in order to get your fifteen seconds of fame, you must first be famous. Diving into the ditch on the side of the road, he hides as a black car speeds past. The make and model didn’t even register into Salaok’s mind which was strange as he had always been a big fan of automobiles. However, the speed that this machine had been travelling and Salaok’s want to not be seen were the major reasons for not having the time to take notice. As the sound of the car distanced itself from Salaok, he raises his head to look over the edge of the ditch at the house a few hundred yards away. But this wasn’t just any kind of house, this was more of a mansion. A mansion that Salaok knew all too well, but also knew that this would be one of the places they would come to look for him, if they hadn’t already been through the place. However, everything was quiet. No cars parked out the front of the building, no police officers walking around eating donuts and surprisingly, not a single journalist in the grounds. Running his worn hand backwards through his jet black hair, he thinks to himself for a brief second before jumping to his feet and not worrying about what may be hiding behind the trees, around the corners or worse, inside his own house. Technically, the house now belonged to Darrell, as most of the world had heard Salaok was dead, so what had become of the place was unknown. Would the government that Salaok had fought against for so long have taking the place away from Darrell and put into a trust fund for him along with the rest of the money’s which was left? Or would have Eve and Justin have laid claim to the place until Darrell became old enough to handle such a thing? What ever had happened, Salaok was now back to reclaim what was rightly his and to begin his reign with the aid of The Lair once again. Like a blur, the speed of his movements carry him quickly into the long grass which surrounded the ground of this monstrous castle styled building. As the grass brushes past his legs and soon, right up to his waist, the wind blows his hair back and he leaps into the sky. Sailing straight over the border fence, Salaok lands and tumbles along the ground. He was home. Walking towards the front doors which had seen Justin Marks walk through and into his house with Brian and Vincent Welch on the last night he was there. On the night that Lisha was murdered. On the night that he lost his unborn child. He stops and looks at the house from a small hill and realizes exactly what he can see. From where he stands, he is able to see straight through the window and see the dinning table that they had all sat at that night. But more importantly, through the window, the seat that Lisha had been sitting in was dead center. This is where Mani Shigeru and Silent Wind had stood that night and shot his wife. This is where two fellow Harvesters did the unthinkable. This is where Salaok’s half brother killed his wife. Although wanting to, no tear runs down Salaok’s face as it is overcome with the hatred and mixed with the insanity which would soon follow at the hands of his father and brother-in-law. But now was not the time to be thinking of such things, for if everything in the world had even a small piece of goodness in it, Darrell would be home and Salaok would be able to see his son again. See the son that had first caused him to mellow, but now the idea of his son is what drives him to insanity. Drives him to want to protect those who are still close. Drive him to reform that which had once dominated. Drive him to find the remaining members of The Harvesters and The Lair. With each step, he arrives that little bit closer to finding out what had happened in the past months and the little bit closer to finding out if all of this calm was merely a trap. The sound of movement causes Salaok to freeze and duck into the grass like a tiger before it pounces for the kill. With his eyes peering through the grass and popping up for a spilt second to see over it, Salaok waits to see what is about to discover him. The grass begins to part off in the distance ever so slightly but it is moving at a rapid pace. Too fast for a crawling human. An animal? Before Salaok even had time to contemplate fully, it was on top of him. Literally. His huge Great Dane had pounced on him, not knowing at first who it was, but as soon as he does, the licks start up. The giant tongue from the giant dog begins to lap Salaok’s face and it’s tail wages intensely. All though it is merely only a trivial thing to Salaok, even this brings a smile to his twisted face as he grabs a hold of the dog and rolls over, playing gleefully with his long time pet. For what would seem like ages, Salaok lay in the grass playing with the dog and everything bad in the world was forgotten for that time. But all good things must come to an end as the dog in his playful mood, suddenly jumps up and runs off towards the house. Disappointed at the abrupt end to his fun, Salaok gets up and looks at the parting grass which is caused by the dog as it bolts towards the front doors where a small figure stands calling it. Darrell? His son, standing in broad daylight calling for his dog on the front door step and all Salaok can do is freeze. Relief seems to take over his body at seeing his son is all right. Relief which would soon turn into something completely different. Fear. It was only ever for Darrell and Lisha that Salaok had ever felt that emotion and never as much as the last day Salaok was in that house. But now that feeling returns as a man walks out of the front door behind Darrell. A man who’s hands bare the same tattoos as his. Chris Davis, his father and Darrell’s Grandfather walks out of the house, stands behind Darrell and puts his hand on the young boy’s shoulder. Although Darrell hasn’t yet seen Salaok hiding in the grass, Chris has, and the eyes lock instantly as hatred fills the air over the many yards between them. This had been the reason for Chris Davis's death. Chris was the one who had put Salaok in the ward. Chris was the one who had taken everything Salaok owned as his own. Chris Davis had failed to protect Darrell. Chris Davis was now dead and Salaok was the reason. His search for alliance in the church with Gabriel hadn't gone as planned but as the past had shown, Salaok would do anything to get what he wanted. The line was too far behind him now to simply stop and walk away and live a normal life. No, this time, things would be bigger than before. This time he would seek out enemies and have them by his side for the purpose of caos. He would have Gabriel walk next to him stride in stride but deception was the game now. A game that focused on the mind. None better for the Harvester of Minds to be apart of.
