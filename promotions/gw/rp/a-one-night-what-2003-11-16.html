A One Night WHAT?!!

16 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/16/03 People wake up in strange places all the time. But why is it that they never remember how they get there? There’s no way that people can just appear someplace and not move themselves to some remote spot. They have to physically carry themselves there or have someone do it for them. Unfortunately, Johnie doesn’t remember either way happening. “Ughhhhh....” Johnie rumbles from underneath an over fluffed pillow as he pulls his head from under the covers. Lifting his arm to check the watch that’s still on his wrist. “11:24” it reads in highlighted jade. Johnie groans loudly as he starts to stir and get up from what he called his bed last night. His eyes attempt to focus on something around the room and the first thing he sees clearly is the light pink blankets on the bed. “The fuck?” His right hand shuffles through his hair as he looks around trying to find something familiar. Instead he sees clutter picture frames of faces he’s never seen. Pink feather boas, body frame mirrors, weird stuffed animals and fluffy things that he can’t even begin to describe. What the fuck is going on?! Where am I?! Johnie tries to lean backwards on the headboard behind him, but he’s being weighted down by something on his lap. A jolt of surprise appears in his eyes as they turn down towards his waist that’s still covered by the blankets. Slowly he lifts the covers and he suddenly sees a puff of matted blonde streaked hair. He sighs and drops his arm across the neck of this resting woman that had apparently allowed him to stay the night. “Oh, boy...” This stirs the figure lying beneath him and the lady raises her head from his hip to roll over to her side. The sun reflects off her thin, rosy cheeks as they crease together to form a slight smile. “Good morning,” she coos with a hit of southern drawl as she pulls herself up a little higher on the bed. Her eyelids peak open slightly, but not enough to show much of what’s behind them. “I wasn’t sure when you’d get up.” She glides her slender hands up and down Johnie’s chest and caressing his chest as he leans against the headboard. Johnie thumps his head against it as he stares towards the opposite wall covered with more no named pictures of whatever she might call home and a large poster of Justin Timberlake. “Where am I?” he mutters faintly. But instead of just himself hearing it, she tilts her head up to him showing off her soft eyes. “What?” Her voice carries a slight inflection to it. Stern, yet hurt. “Nothing.” Johnie tries to cover up what he said, but it doesn’t seem to work. She pulls herself up from the bed and sits up next to him, covering herself a bit with a scattered sheet. “No. You said “Where am I?”” Johnie’s cheeks flush up a bit, but not too much from the sleep creases still on his face. Nevertheless, he knows he’s been caught. “I heard you. What do you mean?” “I don’t know.” There’s no point in hiding it now. “I just don’t know where I am right now.” He knew it sounded bad, but he had to say it anyway. He sighs hard as she sees her eyes droop down a bit towards the bed. “Its obvious of what happened, I know. I just don’t remember much of last night.” “That might be true.” She chuckles and smiles a bit as she lifts her head back towards Johnie. “You were a little drunk last night. So was I. But what do you remember about last night?” Johnie leans back and stares at the ceiling as the memories of last night roll through his mind. It comes in bits and pieces as he sees himself at some bar as he leans a beer bottle to his mouth. He takes one last swig and slams the bottle down and heads back towards the rear door heading out. With a little extra effort, Johnie nudges it open and heads out into the alleyway. Going towards the parking lot, he hears a scuffle behind him as several men surround 2 guys who are standing beside each other. Closer and closer the men inch towards the duo and all try to attack them at once. The pair is able to fight some off one by one, but eventually they’re held back and pinned down by their assailants. Johnie stammers his way over towards the men and attempts to intervene. “Hey. What’s going on here, fellas?” Johnie mutters out towards the one standing closest to him. He scoffs him off which causes Johnie to reply the same again. This time, it draws the man’s attention. “What does it matter to you, man?” he says. “Yeah. This is none of your business.” Another one echoes in reply. Johnie continues to step forward to the skirmish and goes to get a closer look at what’s going on. But as he steps further, one of the others step outside the pack and shoves Johnie back a few feet. “Get the fuck outta here! This has nothing to do with you, fuckhead.” Johnie simply glares back at him in anger and steps toward him again. “You touch me again...and I’m gonna throw you a beating. You get me?” The rest of the gang laugh at that remark as three of them step in his direction. “Oh we get you, little man.” The goon says to Johnie. He has a slight height advantage to Johnie, but not much to it. “But we wanna teach you something first.” The same guy takes a swing at Johnie, but Johnie anticipates and locks his shoulder up with his arm and pulls back to hear a pop. The goon leans forward and Johnie knees straight between the nostrils. The other two charge him, but Johnie rolls the limp guy at one of them, tripping him up as he spears the second guy into the wall causing him to crack his head and neck back into the bricks. With the other men distracted, the guys pinned down shove and swing their way out of the group and get out from their attackers with ease as they toss them out of the way. As Johnie turns around he sees the two men approach him wincing a bit from the attack, but still with grins on their faces. “Nice work man.” one says as he wipes some blood off his lower lip. His long hair has gotten a little messed up, but still in pretty good shape. “Good timing there. We would’ve had them in another minute or two, right Jay?” The other stands a bit taller and more confident than his accomplice. He runs his fingers through his blonde locks and rubs a firm chin. “He does do good work. What’s your name, kid?” Johnie looks back at the pair a bit groggy, but still firm in himself. “Johnie.” “I’m Ben and this sorry excuse is Jay.” the long haired one says a bit hoarsely. “Fuck you, man.” Jay scoffs as the two laugh at themselves. He tilts his head to the side as he looks at Johnie and sneaks out a small smirk. “So, do you think the kid can hang with us for the night?” The trio laugh and hoot as they head back inside the bar and Jay orders a double shot for the three of them. Johnie fades back into reality as he sticks his left foot through his pants leg and nearly tripping over in the process. While he leans forward, he catches an eyeful of the girl’s shapely figure as the sun shines through the lavender curtains covering the window. He also notices the roundness of her breasts as she pulls her head through the neck of a white t-shirt. The t-shirt just covers over her tight blue panties and trails down to her well-formed thighs and calves. As she turns toward him, Johnie looks back toward the floor and picks up his dark blue Tommy shirt and tosses it over his neck and shoulders. “Well, I’m outta here.” Johnie turns towards the door and is able to open it a crack before... “Wait...” Oh god no... She comes toward him and tries to pull him back inside, but Johnie won’t budge. “You can stick around. There’s no hurry to go.” Johnie rolls his eyes and leans himself back towards the door a little more. “No. I think that I’d better go. I’ve stayed too long already.” Johnie moves closer to the knob, but she slides herself in front of it and angles herself to the side. “There’s no reason to leave now. You could stick around.” She bites the bottom of her lips as her eyes follow her hands going across Johnie’s frame and over his abs. “Maybe have a little more fun later on.” Johnie’s eyes stare up at the top of the door frame snickers a bit and shakes his head a bit. She can’t take a hint, can she? “I dunno. I was supposed to head out and meet a few people today. I can’t stick around for too long. I’ve been asleep too long already.” Her eyes turn up towards his face and her seductive smile turns into a pouty frown as she hears him say that. She leans back a bit and lets him get to the door handle and turn the knob. But before he can walk out, she pulls her hand up to the side of the door. “Well, could I at least get a rain check” Maybe give you my phone number?” she giggles with a bright smile. She doesn’t get it. Jesus... Johnie gently lifts her arm off the door and slides out of into the hallway and steps out. “Maybe some other time. Right now I gotta run here, darlin. You understand, don’t you?” Hopefully “Maybe.” She leans against the open door and intently looks Johnie up and down as he starts to turn away. “I guess I’ll see you around campus, won’t I?” Aw, shit. “Maybe.” Johnie turns tail and heads towards the end of the hall as the elevator doors slide open for him. Stepping one leg in, he waves back at the girl happily. “Later honey.” The doors seal shut on Johnie’s face and the elevator starts chugging down. Back in the room, the girl steps back into her room and closes the door softly. She glides towards her desk and picks up a slip of paper. Upon picking it up, she smiles intently. On the slip of paper, the words say why she’s grinning so much: Johnie’s Cell....
