The Silent Angel of War Pt.2

9 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 9th, 2005 “Why” Why did he do this?” These words spoken only in Jesse’s head. If looked at his eyes are glazed over with a tiredness notion, but showing no signs of illness or depression. Instead, he showed a comforting front, almost? too comforting. “Theirs gotta be a way out. I’m not sitting in here and dying. This will not be my bed of sorrows, or my bed of woe.” No action still, only the still outline of Jesse in his darkened padded room. The room didn’t disturb him or it’s entirety, no he was more worried of his survival. It wasn’t the fact that life could end so quickly for him, but more so end before it should. Sitting their in a room, with all his conscious working, was not a way he wished to part the world he’d began to love again. “I plead to god, and I plead to my guardian angels, but now I realize those prayers” are worthless.” Spoken outwards toward the room, the words dwindle in the air before finally fading out of their echo. Plating his arms on the floor he pushes himself up, but he can’t stand, and crumbles back to the floor. “I use to wonder if their was a god” or even someone above looking out for me. Now, I know if there is one, that their a prick, and the only thing they want is to torture an innocent soul. This may be my hate talking, but I damn well feel it’s true. Fuck you god? fuck you.” Closing his eyes, he rests his head back on the wall as he folds his legs and places his arms on his thighs. Not to long after, the jackal fades into his own fantasy world, where he can forget this nightmare he is living through. *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** “I have plans for you. Big plans.” Claimed TBC, the Blue Cougar as Jesse better knew him in his earlier career of wrestling. Smiling TBC shoved a piece of paper in front of Jesse at the table he sits. The room their in almost a perfect square, and only 10x10 square feet. It’s walls covered with a kitchen tile type texture all the way to the ceiling, which was completely white. Their was nothing but two chairs, and the table for furniture. “You want me to hunt down this person” Can’t do your own dirty work anymore? Have to resort to others doing it for you eh?” Smiling with disgust Jesse does, but it is soon wiped away with a back hand slap from TBC. “Don’t try to play your mind games Jess. I know you too well” I’ve just simply figured I can use my experiment in good use. Instead of dumping you in the ground after torturing you, I’ll instead lead you to the places and people I need forgotten.” His eyes sharpen as his head rises up to look at TBC. By forgotten he means kill or destroy, and by using him, he will leave no traces of evidence. An ingenious plan that needs not much figuring out besides? what if he’s to escape while doing these missions? “If for whatever reason you try to escape, your love” will die.” Now his sharpened eyes burst open to life. Did he mean Julie? Was his wife was still alive? After so many years how could she survive the torture? What was this, the truth, or a lie? All these thoughts flooded in without warning, and Jesse became too full of emotion to think. “WHERE IS SHE!”” Standing up Jesse reaches for TBC, but is denied touching him as he is restrained with stun guns. Behind him stands two men, both muscular and crew cut, almost navy seal like. While staring at TBC as he hunches over and the tears of pain begin to split in the corner of his eyes and speed down his cheeks, he asks once more. “Where” is she? Tell me your not lying!” Almost afraid to hear the answer that he is, Jesse looked down realizing it may just have been a tickle to get him rolling with thoughts, as TBC had always been good at doing. Finally sitting down after letting the jolt of electricity surge through his body, he leans forward and slams his fists on the table as he looks up at him about to ask again. “I’m not” She’s alive, and if you succeed then you will be allowed to see for yourself how true my words are. However, fail? and you’ll slowly witness her die.” His hope drowned with the words that TBC spoke. “Was this possible?” was all that Jesse could think. His eyes pondered with his mind, as they gazed at one object but were visualizing something entirely different inside. “So you ready to get down to business?” TBC looked at Jesse not really waiting for an answer, but more of some kind of motion that he was willing to fight for his love, as so many cartoon heroes do, and what TBC knew Jesse would as well. Standing up, his notion was answered to an exact, and their Jesse followed TBC out the door, with the two bodyguards behind them. *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** “I never liked to kill things” Never, but it never bothered me in anyway to do so. I wasn’t dissatisfied with my ruthlessness, because what I killed was animals in which would die eventually. But a man, that is entirely different.” Squish, swish, squish, swish? The sound of the water is heard being disrupted by Jesse as he makes his way through a small but very deep puddle. Heading towards a lonely wooden cabin summerhouse, Jesse suddenly stops. “How do I know this isn’t a set up” How do I know he’s not trying to lie to me? What if this is all just to set me up for jail, like I did to him? Ah damn it Jess? shut the hell up, and do your damn job. If this isn’t right then so be it, but it’s the only way I’ll know if she’s still alive.” Doubts flooding in his mind, but are canceled out quickly over his ultimate goal to see his ex-wife again. Shaking his head to clear his conscious as if it can be rattled and fixed so easily, he stares ahead at the summerhouse. The front porch has a nice swing on it, with an American cherry wood structure for the house. A beautiful work of art to say the least, with its European style design and refinished wood staining to make sure nothing discolors. “Rich” I suppose anyway. So maybe he’s trying to get his hands on money, eh, that must be it. For all this experimental testing he’s doing on me, a pretty penny is needed.” Hesitating no more, he heads out of the puddle wearing a pair of black and green army pants, and a black t-shirt, with his traditional mask on. Walking up to a window he took a peek through the window. “Nobody there” hmmm, looks like a living room, maybe go around the back.” Walking around the back of the house, he sees theirs no gates, no plants, just a back porch, with a screen door, and inside that screen door when entered was a kitchen. Creaking the door open Jesse slips inside the kitchen and begins to slide through the kitchen being aware of all his surroundings. Grabbing a knife off the kitchen counter, he slips through the door into a hallway, and begins walking down it. Their in the living room, now sits a man, a man Jesse had not seen when he looked through the window. “Just do it” It’s for the better? just do it.” Slipping up next to the chair, his hands trembled, but he steadied himself and put the knife quickly around the chair and about the mans? throat and slit it. “God have mercy on my soul?” Looking down and seeing that in front of the chair was a sleeping child. A nine year old boy whom was completely unaware that her father or grandfather, whomever had just been slayed, was now gone. “Oh god-? Turning around quickly Jesse hurried out the house the same way he had come into it. Nearly falling off the porch as he began to open the door and head that way. His body finally stopped, and he fell to the ground, his eyes weeping with tears, and his arms trembling as some blood was left on his fingers from when he cut the throat. “How could I” Now that boy? he’ll grow up like I did. No mother, no father, all alone. How can I do such a heinous thing like that, and not realize what was done to me in my past and how I felt? Damn it? I have not only betrayed my beliefs, but I’ve stabbed my self in the back for not doing what was right and standing up for me.” Breaking down right there in the dirt, on his knees, as he began to convince himself that his life had now been ruined and soiled by his own betrayal. From a close distant a voice could be heard as feet stomped their way near him. “Get him” Put him down, so we can take him back.” As he went to look up he was punched in the side of the face and then shot in the back with a tranquilizer gun. Looking up as his view began to fade, his grogginess took over and Jesse was unable to see his culprits face, but did hear his last words before the effects kicked in fully. “TBC will be glad to know that the test was passed” lets get going.” *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** “Wakey wakey!” TBC slapped Jesse’s face twice to wake him up, and he did so, as the groggy eyes soon widened and then narrowed just as quick as they adjusted to the light. They were back in the white room. However this time, Jesse was chained to a chair, an iron cast chair that was made similar to that of an execution chair. It had the braces for the wrists and ankles, but also for a cap for the head. However this one used chains instead of leather straps, and had two chains crossed over Jesse’s chest to prevent him from coming forward. As well his thighs we’re tied down so that he couldn’t use his leg strength to break free. “You passed.” The words slurred through Jesse’s head a she awoken and finally began to blink in a conscious and normal state. Responding ever so slowly? “Passed what?” A grin crossed TBC’s face, an evil, daring grin, that made you feel cheated out of something, and in this case, it was the truth. “You passed my loyalty test.” Standing up TBC paced his way around the table as he began to explain. “See” I didn’t know if you could be trusted. Our past has some contradicting notions of that Jess, and I just couldn’t take a chance putting you in a situation where if you fucked up, I’d get screwed, or that you’d try to escape.” Disappointment flushes over the face of TBC as he sees Jesse’s expression to this truthful foretelling. Smacking Jesse with the back of his hand, he knocks the spit out of his mouth and sends it to the wall. “Don’t you ever” ever? show me disrespect. If anything, you should worship me for setting that up. You see, that person was a doll, and that child was a paid accomplice. None of it was real? Be happy for that.” Closing his eyes Jesse began to fade from his reality, not really caring anymore if life was an option or not, or if Julie was alive. All he wanted was peace and to be left alone, where he could hear the silence, and become part of it. “I realize you’ve been internally confused by my actions. That was apart of the thrill ride I set up for you. So you’ll have some time to grieve, but not long. In six hours you’ll be awoken from your holding room, and taken to my first assignment. I’ll give you the papers to read before you head off on the trip.” TBC begins to walk out the door, but as he reaches for the door knob his smile returns to his face and he turns around part way to say one last thing. “Oh” and this time, don’t get all worried about what’s right and wrong. Just do it, or it may cost you your life.” *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** “Things never change. People are still backstabbers, and enemies are still our worst nightmares. People try to hide the truths with blatant lies, saying they’re not afraid. I myself have been victim to this. It’s very true that I’m afraid, but” I thrive on that fear. It gives me adrenaline, and makes me want to prove to myself those fears are conquerable. Forget what people say about politics and what they think they’ll be able to do when they hold a title, see? theirs one thing that can be done, and should be done, especially when your in the situation where fears controls things. That is? not give up. I may have given up some hope, but I’ve not given up on the base mind frame in me of how things I do will go down. I still control my destiny, TBC controls shit? He may have my wife, he may not, either way, and his day of suffering is coming closer. His guilt will be his worst enemy, and when it arises to him that he has failed in his proclamation of torture to his past enemies, then he’ll fall to pieces. He’ll fall far? until theirs no way to stand up again. This, is why I still continue. No matter what I do, no matter how hard I wish for my life to end, this peace will console me, and make me realize what is my true path? That path, is to never? give? up.?
