Max gets caught. But not for long.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today “MAX!” yells the voice of Tyler. Tyler is seen walking down the hallway of the Sears Tower. He looks everywhere, even out the window onto the street. Security guards start walking towards Tyler grabbing him by both arms. Tyler looks at the guards with a really confused look on his face. “Come with us.” says one of the guards. “Why?” ask Tyler “We’ll tell you when we get there.” The guard smiles. “When we get where.” “When we take you to the boss.” Two other guards pick up both of Tyler’s legs and they start carrying him down some stairs. They stop at a door that reads Janitors Closet on it. The guards open the door and inside is a huge room with a really big round table. The guards put Tyler down on a chair as they walk over to a guy sitting in a black leather chair. “Where am I!” yells Tyler “Don’t worry. Were not The Gang from H.E.L.L.” “How do you know that group?” asks Tyler. “They’ve been wanted in every state, continent, city.” “They are?” “Yes. And we want to put an end to it.” “Well, everybody does.” “Well, you see I know where this max guy is. We have security cameras in this whole tower on every floor. And we caught who took Max, and we know where he is.” “Take me to him.” “Well, its not that simple.” “Why not?” “They have 10 guards by the doors with big huge machine guns and knifes.” “I see.” “Yeah.” “Well, tell me where they are. I’m going machine guns or not.” “I’m afraid I can’t let you do that.” “Just tell me where they are.” The guy takes out a tape from underneath the table and pops it into a VCR. Suddenly a picture pops up onto the big screen TV. The picture starts playing as Tyler is watching closely. The video stops as Tyler rushes out of the room not saying a word. Tyler starts running down the hallway and down 2 flights of stairs. He stops behind a chair as he looks at the 10 guards covering the door. Tyler closes his eyes and starts thinking about something. He opens them and rises to his feet. He mouths something and then starts spinning really fast. He starts going forward and goes right through the guards into the room. He sees Max in a chair tied up and something in hiss mouth. “I’m sorry I have to bust your party.” says Tyler. Tyler then feels a chill going down his back, and turns around only to be punched in the face by one of the guards. Tyler falls to the ground as the guard picks up the machine gun he had. He points it at Tyler’s head. Tyler starts dripping sweat from his forehead. Max quickly tries getting out of the ropes, and does. He runs and dropkicks the guard to the ground. He picks u p the machine gun and smiles. “Anybody who wants to die, say Roswell.” Nobody answers as Max starts shooting at the guards. Half of them fall to the ground and the other half run at Max. Tyler flips onto his feet and takes them all out with a clothesline. Max throws the gun out of the opened window and goes after the main leader. Max takes the leader and picks him up with 2 hands. He throws the leader out of the window as screaming is heard. There is a big thud, and Max runs over to help his friend Tyler. One huge guard walks over and takes Tyler off is feet with one single punch. He then goes for a punch on Max, but misses and gets kicked into the brick wall, cracking it. “Good one.” says Tyler. “Thanks.” Tyler and Max stand up with lots of bodies on the ground again. They walk over to the window and look at the leader below, but he isn’t there. Max jumps out the window and so does Tyler as the scene goes to black.
