Every Father's Fear

19 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Every Father's Fear Judgement Global Wrestling Promo #439 Date: 07.19.03 Location: Shreveport, LA What in the hell am I doing? I lied to a 4 year old, I told her I would be back tomorrow and it has been three days. I can't face her like this, I mean I came here to get to the top and I have done nothing, but fall farther into the hole. First match I failed, second match I failed and I even had someone there to help me. Maybe I should give this dream up just drop the whole ordeal and get back to my family...if they'll have me. The cell phone sat there screaming my name to call my wife and daughter. It's been three days and I have to do something. I told Lyndsi to tell her tomorrow, but I knew I couldn't make it due to my schedule. I'm back in town now to get my head on straight, but does she even want to have anything to do with me. My daughter or my wife, I left them. Judgement gains the courage and just snatches the phone pressing '2' key on the phone and hitting the dial button. Judgement sits eagarly in the seat of his 1969 Chevelle gripping the steering wheel as he anticipates an answer, but is only getting continuos rings. 'Hi, you have reached the Wood residence. If you'd please leave your name, number, and a brief message I will get back to you as soon as possible.' The answering machine plays. 'Beep' Judgement pauses and takes a deep breath, 'Hey baby, its me. I just wanted to call and see if you'd want me to come over or do something later. Give me a call later, I love you and give Michelle my love.' Judgement lowers the phone clicking it off and tosses it into the passenger seat. Judgement then brings his feet into the car and slams the door shut. He starts the car and pulls it into gear. He looks both ways across the street and pulls into oncoming traffic. It's a lonely drive for Judgement all he has is his mind to think. 'I wonder where they are? Where could they be, I mean she doesn't work I send her enough money every week and month to pay for everything. Maybe Michelle joined a dance class or something. Maybe they knew it was me and just didn't want me anymore. Maybe she has someone knew and I'm out of the picture and she doesn't have the heart to tell me.' Judgement thought to himself. Judgement slams the gas pedal down trying to fume off when the sound of 'Crawling' by Linkin Park comes over his cell phone. Judgement slows and and reachs over trying to keep his eyes on the road. His eyes get big as it reads, 'Willis-Knighton Medical Center'. Judgement hesitates at first, but then answers, 'He..hello?' 'Mr. Wood?' A woman on the other end asks. 'Yes, is there something wrong?' Judgement asks with concern. The woman sits on the other end for a breif moment, 'Sir, are you driving in a car or doing anything that you can stop?' Judgement quickly slams on his breaks and turns into a parking lot in the middle of traffic. He turns off the car and puts the phone back onto his ear. 'No, not anymore.' Judgement says quickly with even greater concern now. 'Mr. Wood, your wife, Lyndsi, and your daughter, Michelle, have been in a fatal car accident and are currently in ICU. Lyndsi is in stable, but Michelle is in critical condition. We think it would be best for you to come see both of them, incase any changes happen.' the woman says in saddness. Judgement stands with nothing to say. Tears slowly begin to fall down his cheek and then they just pour down like rain. Judgement just sits with the cell phone to his ear as the woman trys to communicate, 'Sir? Are you OK? Sir...' Judgement lowers the phone to his waist and clicks it off. He throws it into the passenger seat. He stands with the door opened on his car and his arms sprawled across the top and his head pressed on the top of the car. He stands as tears fill his eyes and fall to the top of the car like a thunderstorm. Judgement slowly raises his head and then he enters the car as rage overcomes him. Judgement starts the car and slams his foot onto the gas not looking anyway, with no care anywhere near him. He flys down the highway until he reaches the interstate and he gets on the on ramp. He continues on the gas and he flips on his emergency lights. The speed-o-meter reads 85 and quickly climbs to 130 and continues to go up, until he gets on the off ramp. He takes a right and turns down a frontage road towards the hospital. Judgement switches off the car after parking and takes off running towards the hospital. The eletrical doors slide open and Judgement runs to the elevator. He gets in and goes to the 4th floor where the ICU is located. After going through the elevator he goes to the desk to a secretary sitting doing some paperwork. 'Yes sir, can I help you?' The women asks politly. 'Yea, I just received a phone call and was told that my family has been in a fatal car accident and I want to see them now!' Judgement demands to the women. The women shakes her head, 'Sir, I'd be more than happy to let you, but it is past visiting hours. You'll have to come back at a later time.' Judgement shakes his head no, 'Where are they? They are my family I have the right to see them!' 'Sir, I am only doing my job. I don't make the rules, but I am going to have to ask you to leave and come back at a later time.' the women asks nicely. Judgement looks for the entrance to the ICU room and he catchs it and starts towards it. The secretary stands up from her chair staring at Judgement as he walks. 'Sir, your not allowed to be in there! Sir!' the secretary screams. The hospital is all staring at the women screaming at Judgement. The lady then grabs the phone and lifts it to her ear, 'Security..' that is all that can be heard as you get focused back on Judgement and her voice fades. Judgement enters the ICU room as he presses the button. He passes by the sink to wash his hands and steadily looks for his wife and daugther. He goes three beds down and notices a group of doctors. A doctor approachs him, 'Sir, you can't be in here.' Judgement pushes past him and slings open the curtain to see his daughter and wife both lying in beds. Both having tubes and IV's hanging out of their bodys. Respirators on his daughter and his wife struggling to open her eyes. His daughter struggling to stay...alive.
