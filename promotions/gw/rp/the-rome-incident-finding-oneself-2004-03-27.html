The Rome Incident - finding oneself

27 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03.27.04 The sunlight breaking through Ice's hotel room window was enough to wake Ken from his slumber. His baby blue eyes fluttered to life as the radiance kissed his face. He pulled his hand over them to block out some of the illumination before beginning to stir on the sofa in Josh's hotel room. Letting out a yawn, he pulled himself upright and began to look around for his watch, which he finally found in the clean glass ashtray on the end table. He slipped it on and stood to his feet, stretching... allowing his black wife beater to rise and display his chisled stomach in the glimmer making it's way in from between the curtains. He pulled the tanktop back down over his white Adidas warm-ups, which are pulled a little lower than usual around his hips. He begins to shuffle through one of his black Samsonite suitcases and comes out with a large fluffy white towel, throws it over his left shoulder, and begins to walk into the bedroom. He opens the door to find Ice asleep in a recliner next to the bed that Josh is still dozing in. A small smile runs across Ken's face. 'Just like I left you,' whispers Ken. Feeling better about the situation as a whole, Ken decides against an immediate shower and instead walks back into the living room area, where he tosses the towel on the sofa and puts on his pair of white Adidas cross-trainers with black trim. As he's finishing tying his shoes, he looks up to nitice a not sitting on the coffee table. When he's completed his task, he reaches across the table to grab it. 'Went to the gym. Be back in time for us to make our late lunch date to go over the Rumble. Call if you need anything. -Ace' After reading it, Ken places back on the coffee table and begins to think about the events that took place the day before, wishing he could take back some of his actions against Ace. He knew that Harding had a point, and a very valid one at that. The three of them were going to end up facing off at The Global Rumble. On top of that, each of them would be taking on the entire Global Wrestling roster for the most part in that match. Training was crucial. Ken realized that Ace wasn't as close to Josh as he was and that he most likely overreacted. With that thought, Ken stood up and made his way out of the room and to his room... heading outside for a brisk morning jog. On the way down he checked his watch. It was 6:37 AM, so he looked to be in good shape to get a little training done considering their lunch wasn't until 2:30. He first stopped by his room to brush his teeth, then it was to the elevator and down to the first floor. 6:42 AM The brisk Spring air carried a touch of Rome's dew on it as it whipped Ken slightly in the face. He breathed in the smells of Italy... the ocean, the people, the food... and smiled again. It wasn't often that he got to pause for a few seconds, but when he did, it was definitly worth it. Once his moment was up, he shot off like a dart. His jog had immediatly turned into a run... almost a race... a race against himself. And, seeing as he knew he would be alotted plenty of time to do so on this 11 and a half mile journey to the Colisseum, he began to think. A lot of his past came to his mind. Shawn... how he'd taken Ken under his wing and began his training when Ken had nothing. How he'd taught Ken the fundamentals of our business on both the physical end and the business end. He thought about past organizations. His in-ring debut against 'The Creep' Chris Carson. Shawn sitting in the front row with Ken's parents with a proud smile on his face as a young 16-year-old 'Y2KPJ' Ken Partridge Jr won with Sweet Ken Music. His first title win, capturing the RWWF Television Championship in Los Angeles, California, over The Undertaker in a thrilling ladder match. His warm blood forming a crimson mask over his face as he stood atop the ladder with the title held high. He remembered his time training with Dean Malenko and Chris Benoit in Tampa, Florida, at Dean's Malenko School of Wrestling. His mind flashes forward to his first ever World Title win. Him winning a battle royal and later going on to defeat both Triple H and Chris Benoit in a steel cage match to be crowned the first ever Universal Hardcore Wrestling Federation Heavyweight Champion of the World brought another smile to Ken's face. The fact that he was the only man with the honor of being the UHWF Heavywight Champion only made the smile warmer. 6:57 AM Italy truly was beautiful. The water, the greenary, the outside villas and cafes... it was all so gratifying. As Ken ran around a curve taking him between two cafes, one serving crepes and the other, begnets, an entirely new stream of thoughts entered his mind... what was he running from? Was he running from the thought that had been haunting him for the past two days, his face on the body of a lifeless Josh? ... Perhaps... But, why was he running from it? What did it mean? It finally hit him like a slap in the face with an iron glove... If Josh were to have passed away... a piece of Ken would have decepated as well. Josh was like Ken's family. Brock and Ace were also, but Josh was like Ken's younger brother... and he was Ken's best-friend. Friends... Something Ken had been running short on as of late due to simple business decisions. 7:01 AM The air was getting a bit chillier, but Ken was fine with it. He preferred it to be brisk over anything else... especially when he was training. He had been running flat out at this point for 19 minutes and he was feeling great. Known around locker room for his cardiovascular regemin, Ken was just getting started. He felt the blood running through his legs as he began to pick up his speed... he was making it Clash of the Titan's Colisseum inside of an hour. 7:14 AM Ken began to realize more and more that The Global Rumble was his chance to gain back those steps that he'd been missing since his return to the ring around a month ago. This was his opportunity to prove that he hadn't turned into politics, glitz, and glamour... but that he could still do what needed to be done between the ropes. This was his opportunity to get a shot at the Global Wrestling Heavyweight Championship of the World at the biggest event of all, WrestleCade. He would win that title, be the champion Ice or anyone else, and he didn't care who he had to go through to get it. His co-horts would surely understand. It was business, and they all four want to be the best in the business. 7:23 AM The Colisseum was finally in sight, and it was a wonderous scene in and of itself. The sweat was beginning to pour off of Ken in buckets as the sun began to bear down on him. His hair was no longer mussily spiked, like it's normally seen, but rather soaked and drooping. The muscles in his arms, legs, and abdomen glistened as they ripped through every pump, step, and hop. Ken could feel his adrenaline... he could taste. Who needed a drug when you could have this... pure and uninhibbited... for free. The smile on Ken's face widened... he could feel it again. A taste of his old-self. 7:29 AM Ken's heart was racing and his hands were slightly shaking as he finally came to a halt at the steps of the Colisseum. 'Forty-seven minutes... that beats my top time,' Ken muttered through his pants for air, all the while a smile still tattood to his face. He stood there for a minute, catching his breath and gazing at the slpendor of the Roman Colisseum. It's architecture... it's tradition... it was beautiful. It just a mere matter of happenings, Ken would bee inside... in the middle of the ring... doing what he's known to do best... winning. 'Guess I need to get a shower, check on Josh, and eat some breakfast before I get ready for lunch,' Ken said, still smiling. With that, he turned around and took off again back to the hotel. Ken was back... he was back.
