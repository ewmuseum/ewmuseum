Out to dinner

25 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/25/04 One night before one the biggest pay per views of the year, Master of the Mat, Donny and Alexis enjoy a nice night out together just the two of them. 'Alexis I been thinking, about us, about lots of things really, and I wanted your opinion on something' 'What about Donny?' 'Well you know, me and you get a long so well, and honestly you know how much I love you, I was thinking if you would agree to it....that we move in together in fact I was thinking about buying a house in Dallas so we wouldn't have to do as much traveling you know?' 'Awww Donnykins, yeah I would love to move in with you! I'll have to ask my Mommy and Daddy first if it's ok' 'Ummm but you're old enough to make that decision by yourself. I mean you stay over at my house almost everynight of the week, I'm sure they know that right?' 'Not really, they think I been staying the night at a girl named Jennifer's house.' 'What? Why? Nevermind' 'Donny, you still have to meet my parents yet.' 'True, you still have to meet my Mom to.' 'My Daddy isn't always nice to my boyfriends though' 'Why is that?' 'I'm not sure, maybe getting caught making out with them on the couch could have something to do with it.' 'Well um yeah that could present a problem' 'Donny I was doing some thinking my self.' 'Yeah?' 'Yeah, I was thinking of what you would do if you won Master of the Mat?' 'Well, I'm not sure. I would probably have to beat the holy shit out of who ever would be the Global World Champion. Josh or Reno.' 'Josh is a dirtball and Reno is a meanie!' 'I agree,all of TUE seems to think their shit doesn't stink. And the likes of Josh, well he smells like shit.' 'Who else do you think is a meanie in GW?' 'Train is a real meanie. If you have to face him tomorrow night I hope you hurt him' 'I would like to more than hurt him. He's a snake, a cheat, a crook and a pure asshole' 'Wow you really don't like him' 'You're right I don't. I think he brings shame to the Platinum Title. If your a title holder in GW you should be a good role model for kids and anyone who watches. He's not a good role model at all. He's a pure jerk who will do what ever it takes to get ahead and that also means losing a match on purpose so I wouldn't have a chance of beating his ass to cost him from moving up in the Master of the Mat Tournament.' 'He's a loser' 'Yes he is, a big one at that. You know there is someone else I want to eventualy beat the hell out of. Mark Bishop!' 'He is a jerk, always picking on me, and saying mean things about me and he's said mean things about you too' 'Yeah for starters him messing with you. Then on top of that saying that I was too drunk to wrestle. I have some news for that asshole, I've never been drunk while wrestling, and soon very soon he's about to find that out for himself.' 'Donny I love you' 'Alexis I love you too' As the two exchange their vows of love, their dinner finally comes. Donny is having a large New York Strip Steak, Baked Potato, and Applesauce, while Alexis has a Kids Meal Cheeseburger. The two love birds begin to enjoy their meals as the Master of the Mat Tournament draws to a close tomorrow night, and the notion that anything can happen in Global Wrestling.
