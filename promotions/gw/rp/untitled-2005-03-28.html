Untitled roleplay

28 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3.28.05 I know this is short, but this rumble thing isn't for me at all. I put this up because I know we're all expected to at least put something in the form of a role play. ------------------------ (A black Armani blazer flies through the room and we hear an angry Ace Cash.) Ace: Global Rumble!? Global fucking Rumble!? Joey! Bro... you gotta be kidding me. (A voice is heard over the speaker phone in Ace's study.) Joey: Sorry, Ace... that's what they booked. I know you were hoping for a singles match, but that's not what McCoy booked. Ace: Wanted? Bullshit, Joey. I'm Ace Cash... you hear me!? ACE.CASH. How the hell do you look at a guy like me and think that a Global Rumble match suits me? I'm a star, Joey, not some jaybro that these monkeys can treat like this. I deserve a singles match, I SHOULD have a singles match. Joey: Well, Ace, it's booked. You don't want to no show it, because you'll lose your spot. Just show up and eliminate yourself or something. Ace: What the hell do I pay you for? (Ace angrily smacks the phone off the desk and it bounces off the floor. We hear the annoying beeping noise that the phones make when that happens)
