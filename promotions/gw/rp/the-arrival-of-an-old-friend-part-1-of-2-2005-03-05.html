The Arrival of an Old Friend (Part 1 of 2)

5 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03.05.05 'How was your flight, Amigo,' asked Ken, dressed in a black Giorgio Armani suit that sported a four-button jacket and a powder blue silk tie, to a somewhat jet-lagged Josh, dressed in a pair of worn Versace jeans and a black sleeveless shirt with word 'Punk' scrawled across it in rhinestones. 'Long and tiring,' he responded with a yawn. 'It's damn good to be here, though.' 'We're so excited that you're going to be watching the in the front row instead on pay-per-view in some Japanese hotel room,' exclaimed a very happy Gunner Addison, who was dressed in a beautiful and tight white backless Donna Karan dress and clinging to Ken's arm. Walking next to them with a full-size plastic Santa Claus under his arm was the mountainous Bill Moresi, dressed much like Ken, but instead opting for a cream color suit with a traditional two-button jacket and no tie. The foursome definitly looked stellar. 'I still can't get over that thing,' said Ken in reference to Saint Nick. 'I found it in an Americana Shop in Tokyo amd had to get it,' Josh responded. 'I figured you'd appreciate the humor in it.' 'If he doesn't,' laughed a quiet Bill, 'he may get a lump of coal in his stocking.' The rest laughed along as they exited the terminal of New York's LaGuardia International airport and were met by a long black stretch limosine that had been awaiting them. 'Aww,' joked Josh with a smile on his face, 'you didn't have to do this little ol' me.' 'I didn't,' responded Ken quickly with a slight hint of seriousness in his voice. 'Man, I take this thing to the grocery store.' They all shared another laugh before Bill spoke through his own chuckles. 'You think he's joking,' laughed Moresi, 'but, Josh, you don't know how funny it is seeing a World Champion in an Armani suit getting out of a limo carrying a bag of produce and a case of Diet Sprite.' 'That may be true,' retalliated a laughing Ken, 'but it's probably not nearly as funny as looking at a six and a half foot tall three hundred and seventeen pound full-grown monster of a man dressed in Armani and holding a plastic Santa Claus underneath one of his arms.' 'Ouch,' laughed Gunner while the entire group laughed along. 'You got burned.' 'That's all good and fun,' responded the Goliath, 'but where is old Kris Kringle sitting?' 'Well,' said KPJ with a bit of caution in his voice, 'I need to get back on his good list, so why don't we let him sit back here with us so he can have a few cocktails.' By this time, the driver had finished loading the bags of the former Global Wrestling stand-out into the trunk and was holding the door open for the four laughing celebrities. Bill placed Santa in first before stepping back and allowing Ken to help Gunner in before accompaning her himself. Josh entered next, per Bill's request, who entered last. Once inside, the four were still sharing chuckles and jokes as Ken unbuttoned his jacket and took it off. 'It's a long way to Detroit,' said the Global Wrestling Champion, 'we may as well get comfortable for the ride.' 'Comfortable or not, you look like a Champion,' chuckled Bill, 'and that's what counts.' 'Well, so do you,' said KPJ, returning the chuckle, ' and you will be after High Octane.' 'Yeah,' chimed in Josh. 'I'm really looking forward to that Tag Team Championship Gauntlet Match and seeing the two of you with those Titles.' Ken laughed somewhat adherantly under his breath. 'I'm definitly looking forward to it more than my World Title Match. There may be a helluva a lot more talents to contend with, but I'll have Bill beside me and I won't be fifty-feet in the air. Kind of makes you breath a little differently. But, I'm really looking forward to adding my first Tag Team Championship to my resume and I'm really excited about my partner being you, Dozer.' 'Same here, Champ,' thanked Bill, in his own way, at least. 'It'll be nice to have some gold again. Speaking of which, Youth, I heard through the grapevine about your Tag Team Championship victory with Hiroshi. Congratulations.' Ken, Bill, and Gunner all begin to applaud playfully as Josh holds clasps his hands together in victory. 'Thank you! Thank you,' responded Josh. 'I must say that it is nice to finally carry a piece of tag-team gold.' 'Well, I'm proud of all of you,' said Gunner with a sense of softness in her voice. 'Just the fact that the three of you have made it this far... win, lose, or draw, you've all made great accomplishments.' Ken smiles at the love of his life and gives her a quick peck on the lips in gratitude. 'Okay,' broke Ken. 'Enough of this mutual admiration, let's get comfy and watch a movie. We don't want to be exhausted when we get to Detroit, and once we're there, we've got plenty of time kiss one another's asses.' The limo bursted into laughter again as Ken pushed a button on a tiny remote, prompting a plasma screen to come to life and a DVD to begin loading. He and Gunner cuddled next to each other as Josh put his arms behind his head and leaned back to relax and Bill stretched out his long muscular legs and unbuttoned his jacket. Troy began to play on the screen as Ken looked over at his exhausted best-friend and brother, who was already asleep, and then to his partner and confidant, who had already begun to meditate. He snuggled closer to Gunner and watched the opening credits to the movie as steady mode of reality settled into his mind. In just a few short day, he and Bill would be doing battle much like the Roman and Trojans, and it wouldn't stop until only one side was left standing.
