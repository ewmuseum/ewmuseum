Deep in the Heart of Texas... (PPV#1)

26 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/26/04 Ah nothing like the sound of a wooden bat hitting a baseball squarely on the sweet spot. The King of Hardcore decided taking some batting practice while in San Antonio would be a good stress reliever. Just a few days away from High Noon and McNasty is already feeling the pressure as now he doesn't face one fierce Superstar, but two. Gonzalez and Drachen both have been making some big waves of late in GW, and Donny knows it. When it comes to dealing with adversity, Donny knows all about it and has conquered it many times, much like the homerun swings he's putting on the fastballs being hurled at him by a humanless machiene. As the round comes to an end Donny gets out of the cage and is approached by a pre-teen boy. The boy looks up at him with eyes of amazement that a GW Superstar is there at the same place he is doing the same thing he wants to do. Donny being the nice guy he is, says hello to the boy, the boy smiles like a kid on Christmas day. The responds with happiness, 'Hi Mr. McNasty, nice hitting in there, you sure crushed that ball.' Donny looked back him with a smile was flattered by the boys comment. 'Thanks kid, What's your name?' The boy had his smile grow even larger he stuck out his hand for a handshake and told Donny his name 'My name is Jason, Mr. McNasty, Do you like baseball or something?' Donny looks at the young man and motions for him to sit down on a bench next to him for he sees this kid likes to talk. 'Well Jason it's nice to meet you, Well I love baseball. It was one of my favorite sports ever since I was a kid. Me and my brother Charles used to play all of the time. He was the base hit guy, and I was the homerun guy. When we played other kids from school, we were always fought over who would play on what team, usually we tried to be a package deal you know. Obviously Jason you seem to be a big baseball fan, who is your favorite team?' The Boy almost immediatley answers 'Well of course I am a Texas Rangers fan, I just can't believe they traded A-Rod, but they're doing good without him. What about you Donny who is your favorite team?' Donny replies with a smile,Well that would be the Pittsburgh Pirates. I have been a big fan of them for a long time. Sure they haven't had a real good season in a long time, but they're still my team which I am sure you can relate to.' the Boy looked at him in agreement. Donny looked at young teen boy, and knew what he would say next would definatley make his day. 'Hey Jason, if your not doing nothing Monday night and if you want, I can get you and your family some good tickets to High Noon.' Jason looked at Donny with a bright light in his eyes. 'Oh my God that would be so awesome!' Donny quickly made a phone call to the arena ticket office, Yeah hi, this is Donny J McNasty from Global Wrestling... Yeah, hey I am going to be having a family come up to the will call window for some tickets, give them four tickets to the front row behind the announcers table...Ok thank you.' Donny puts his cell phone away, and of course Jason can see the he's getting four front row tickets to High Noon all because of Donny J McNasty. 'Thanks Mr. McNasty, your just as cool in person as you are on TV. This means a ton'. Donny just simply replies 'Don't mention it' The two shake hands as they both go their seperate ways, Donny is happy knowing he just brightened a kids day, and Jason is happy knowing he is going to High Noon for free. Donny just proves that you can't forget about the young fans.
