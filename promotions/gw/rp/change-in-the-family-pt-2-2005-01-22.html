Change in the Family (pt 2)

22 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1:22:2005 'Why are people always taken from you so abruptly?' ---- 10:35pm. Me, my wife Cindy, our uncle Johnny, and our friends Billy, Heather, Troy, and Maggie, are sitting in the living room watching a movie. A knock at the door is heard. 'I'll get it, better be that damn pizza man, he's running late,' says Troy, standing up and jumping over the back of the couch to answer the door. As soon as the door opens, a gunshot rings out, followed by a thud. Everybody scrambles out of their seats as three men barge through the door, adorned in black coats, white bandanas over their mouths, stocking hats on their heads, and a frantic look in their eyes. Before anybody can do anything, two men pull guns and point them at me, Johnny, and Billly. The tallest man has his gun pointed straight in my face, while the shortest is alternating between Johnny and Billy. The third man steps over Troy's body and raises his gun to Johnny and just stares at him. I glance at Cindy, who is standing next to Maggie at the end of the couch. I want to do something to protect her, but I can't move. I couldn't protect her even if I could move. I hate feeling helpless. Something hits the couch, jolting it just a little. Maggie screams and starts crying. The supposed ring leader of the group points his gun down at the ground, apparently where Troy is laying, and pull the trigger twice, then yells at us. 'Nobody try anything stupid and this will be clean. You fuck with us and we'll make this place messier than a butchershop!' The man with his gun pointed at me just stares straight in to my eyes. I do my best not to show him any fear, but while doing so, I see my wife's hand slowly move for the small wooden chair we keep at the table next to the couch. 'CINDY NO!!!!!' I scream, right as she goes to slam the chair over one of the men's head. The man standing in front of me turns, so I dive on him, trying to knock the gun from his hand. The man Cindy hits falls down, dropping his gun. I try to stop him, but the final gunman, who is still standing, fires at my wife. Once, twice, three times, four, five. I drop my guard and lay there in shock. I just watched my wife get pelted by bullets. The attacker I tackled pushes me off, grabs his gun and presses it against the back of my head. No time to think. BLAM! I snap awake. I'm laying in bed, cold sweat pouring down my back. My eyes dart rapidly around the dark room as my hand finds its way to my wife's body next to me. She stirs and whispers to me. 'Are you ok baby?' 'Yeah, just had a nightmare.' 'Well, try to go back to sleep. We're going to the family lawyer tomorrow to go over Grampa's will.' 'I know hun. I'm gonna go check on Damon.' I get out of bed, put my slippers on and step out of the dark bedroom. I walk down the hall, my fingers sliding along top of the molding. At the end of the hallway, I step through the door into my son's room. I quietly step over to his crib and peer in at him. He's sound asleep. While looking down at my son, my mind drifts away from the dream. My mind is set on rewind, and I'm going through the days backwards. Every moment of happiness with my lovely wife and first born son. Damon's birth. Nine months of ups and downs with Cindy. The news that Cindy was pregnant. All the way back to when we were teenagers. I look up from my son, and my attention is drawn to the mobile hanging above his crib. It was made by a friend of the family's. It has the basic baby toys and such on it, but in the middle hangs places for four pictures. One picture in particular is what I'm looking for. Cindy's senior picture from high school. Right after she graduated, she moved to Chicago to go to college. I, however, stayed in Detroit. I was in classes during the day, working in the evening, and training at night, so it goes without saying that I hardly got to see Cindy. So whenever I missed her, I would look at this very picture of her. I carried this picture in my wallet for around six years. Every time I needed to calm down, I would look at this picture and it's like she's there with me, soothing me. When Damon was born, I knew it was time to pass the picture on. I've since replaced that picture with one of my son and his mother, the two most important people in my life. Damon stirs a bit below me, snapping me out of my daze. I look down to see his bright brown eyes staring up at me. I lean down to pick him up and he smiles at me. A few seconds later, I'm holding my child in my arms, trying to put him to sleep. But something in the back of my mind is still going. That dream. It was too real. Not so real that it happened, but it was so intense, so immensely vivid, that I was wrapped up in it entirely. It was so real that looking back, it feels more like a memory of what really happened, much like hearing about Grampa Cruse's death, or that night on the highway when the black Lincoln shot my rear window out. With all of this happening at once, I almost feel like I'm trapped in some movie about the mob. We've had a patriarch killed. I had my car shot at while on the highway. And now I've had a dream about my house being intruded, one of my friends being killed, my wife getting shot, and myself having a bullet forced through my skull. But in this case, there is no Al Pacino. No Robert DeNiro. No Marlon Brando. Not even a Joe Pantoliano. This is my life, not some damn movie with a bunch of Italians out shooting each other. But why? Nothing makes sense right now. All I know is that I need to do something. But what? Sleep. I need sleep. I'm getting so stressed out that I'm coming up with conspiracy theories against myself. I lay my now sleeping son back in his crib, take one last look at the picture, lean in and kiss him on the forehead. I walk back in to my bedroom where I kick my slippers off and curl back up in bed next to my wife. I drift to sleep. ---- *six hours later* Cindy and I are in her Lexus IS300 going down I-275. We are following my parents on our way to their house so they can watch Damon while Cid and I visit her family's lawyer. 'I'm so glad Johnny let Mom and Dad borrow his Viper,' I say. 'Me too. I know your dad is having trouble getting the Chevelle running again, and your mother's Benz is in the shop.' 'Yeah, I love how our families have seemed to blend together,' I add. 'Well, I think they wanted us to be ha........' Cindy starts to say before being interrupted by a large BOOM. I see car parts ahead of me on the highway flying everywhere. A wheel and tire bounces off the road in front of me and I swerve to miss it, but it clips the front driverside headlight of the Lexus. Suddenly, it hits me. That wheel had a snake's head emblem in the center. 'FUCK!!!!' I scream, slamming on the brakes. I get out of the car and run over to the now burning mess of black and lime green car. Johnny's car. Blown up, now on fire. 'MOM!!!! DAD!!!!!' I scream, seeing if they can hear me of the roaring fire. I try the door handle, but the immense heat from the fire has virtually molded it to the door panel. I can't see anything inside the car because the thick, black smoke drifting up from under the hood and floating backward on the wind. Cindy comes running up with her cell phone at her ear. I assume she's on the phone with 911. I never find out for sure, because the thought that I just lost my parents in a car explosion, as well as extreme smoke inhalation, caused me to pass out on the side of the road.
