In disguise

3 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Jul 03, 2003 Ely Braxton leans against a wall in the crowded airport of Las Vegas. Ely is barely recognizable, He is dressed in a leather jacket, Black leather pants and a black Mesh shirt. His black hair slicked back and he where’s a slim par of black shades. An unlit cigar hangs from his mouth. Tourist and Airport workers walk past steering well clear and a few un-identifiable whispers are spoken here and there. Ely sighs and looks at his watch.. and waits. His wait is cut off as he is approached by two armed security guards. One liking more like the stay puff marshmallow man then a security officer. The other more like a stick. ~Ohh great Abbot and Castillo are gona beat me up and haul me off~ Ely thanks to his self. The thought causes a chuckle to escape from his lips, not to the liking of the guards. ~SHIT!~ He curses to hisb self. “What so funny pal?” Asks the fat guard in a very high voice for such a big man.. Its all Ely can do not to laugh. “Nothing, I was just thanking that you guys looks like some guys I know. I thought they wanted to play a joke on me. But now I get a closer look, I see you guys are not them though.” Ely responds rather quickly. Both guards look at each other for a moment. The small one turns his gaze back on Ely. “Okay. That aside, We would like to know what your standing here for” You have been here in one spot, for a four hours.” The little guard replies and a surprisingly deep voice. “I am waiting on a very important person. Last I check that is not a crime boys.” Ely responds a bit agitated. “Don’t go getting smart on us now.” Says the fat guard. ~Okay I will get dumb then. Moron.~ Ely thanks but refuses to say. Instead he nods his head yes. Last thing he needed was a situation right now. “That’s better. And no it’s not against the law to wait for some one. A few workers thought you looked suspicious.” Explains the small guard. “Understandable, Thanks for doing your job and keeping people like me safe.” Ely says trying to make the pair leave. “Good.” Says the small guard again. “Yeah, Don’t go causing any trouble. I have my eyes on you pal.” Says the Fat one. ~Ohh god! Now I am scared.~ Thinks Ely “No problems from me.” Ely says however. “Good job in the last fight Ely.” Said Christina McCoy as she walks up beside him. Ely spins to see the small brunette, standing there dressed in a pink and blue power business suit. At the sight of her? his demeanor changes. “Ahh thanks Chris.” Says Ely. “Lets get going” Ely reaches out and takes her two bags, and both start walking away. “Hay, Now I know where I have seen that face before!” Says the fat guard, causing Ely to freeze in his tracks. “Yeah” That’s they guy fighting in that PTC tournament!” Says the smaller guard. “Hey Mister! MISTER!!!” Screams the fat guard. “Lets get out of here”. As fast as well can? says Ely in a hushed tone.. Christina nods and they head away from the guards in a brisk walk.. “What’s up with the Terminator look?” Ask Christina. “Long story Chris. I will tell you as son as we are away from here. ? Says Ely. The two round the corner soon vanish into the huge crowd The two guards round the corner and look out into the crowd of people. “Damn it Bob! We lost them thanks to your fat ass. Probably was not Angelus Sorrow anyways!” Says the skinny one outraged. “It was him! I know it was!” Replies the fat guard. Both guard walk away arguing with each other. Another large man rounds the corner in a business suit. He stops and takes a deep breath. “Damn it!” Are his only words? ~~~~~~~~~~~~ “You FAILED!!!” Shots a male voice from a darkened side of what appears to be a office. The woman Jade Dragon stands in the center of the only light source in the room. “I am sorry. Ely has gotten better then the last time I seen him fight.” She says. “You knew the bargain. Your daughters life is now forfeit!” Says the man in a hostile tone. “OHH GOD NO! She has done nothing to deserve that. Please” Please one more chance?” Jade pleads, tears falling down her cheeks. “Fine. One more chance that is all you have. Ely Braxton is not to survive his fight against Angelus Sorrow.” Says the man “Thank you. Thanks you boss.” Says Jade wiping away the tears. Door opens behind here and Jade heads to it. “Ohh Jade. Do not fail me again.” Says the man as a lighter flickers. Then a red glow of a cigarette or cigar is all that’s seen. Jade hand slides inside her sleeves. ~No Jade. He is the only one who knows where Carrie is. One day though one day.~ Jade exits the room fire in her eyes. All that is seen is the red glow in the room. “Hello. May I speak to a Lacuna Debris please.” Sounds the voice of the man in the room. “Yes. I will hold.” ~~~~~~~~~~~~
