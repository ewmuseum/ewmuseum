The room almost reminded me of a crypt. Black marble walls. Huge matched columns trimmed in gold. In front of me, set back in the marble, a large fireplace, the flames of the fire slowly licking away at the wood, caressing it, consuming it, eating it. Above the fireplace, the TUE coat of arms was etched into the wall. The plush black leather chair was comfortable. The tumble full of Crown Royal much more comfortable. Sitting next to me in his own black leather chair was Deacon, who had taken to spinning the chair around and around on its pivoting base. I picked my drink up and took a slow sip. “So, how’s things with Rhaiza?” I hadn’t seen much of the couple since before the somewhat fuzzy trip to Rome. “I don't know, I mean... I'm having fun, if that's what you mean.” He didn’t even pause his spinning to respond. “Well, how serious are you two” Have you gone “there” yet?” “Bitch please. I’m there so much, they named a fuckin” sandwich after me.” More and more spinning. Then he stopped and got this misty look in his eye. “Yep. The Deactacular Supreme.” We looked at each other and started laughing. Deac then resumed his spinning. In the background, I could hear the slow measure steps of combat boots on the tiles floor. Maverick was approaching. I gestured to Deacon to stopped spinning, but he ignored me. Reno came around from behind me and stood in front of the fireplace looking at both me and Deacon. “Gentlemen,” he spoke softly. I nodded my head slightly to him. He turned his hard gaze to Deac. “Deacon.” It was more of a statement than a question. Spin. “What?” Spin. “Deacon.” Spin. “I said “What?”” Spin. Maverick sighed and lashed his leg out, his foot catching the side of the chair and stopping it in mid-spin, causing Deacon to spill out onto the cold, hard floor. I couldn’t help but laugh. Neither could Reno. “Oh, hardy-har-har motherfuckers. Sure, laugh at the black man when he’s down. I see how this shit is. The two white motherfuckers decide to pick on the black man.” Deacon slowly gets and glares at both of us. “See, I don’t have to take this shit. I’ma call the NAACP and JOHNNY COCHRAN, goddamnit!” D turned his attention to Reno. “I believe I'ma kick yo ass one day, you don't be laughin' at none of my jokes or nothin'!' I keep laughing, the thought of Deac and Reno getting into a scuffle entering my mind. Deacon and Reno both give me an odd look, staring at me laughing at them. “The fuck you laughing at?” Deac asked. “Sit down, Deacon,” Maverick interrupted. Deac slowly spun his chair back to facing the fireplace and sank back into the leather. I could hear him mutter, “Fuck boy.” Reno shot Deac a half-hearted harsh looking before picking back up. “Anyways” Sorry for dragging you here but we've run into a hitch. It would seem that one of our usual shipment handlers has taken matters into his own hands. Normally, this is a good thing... it reduces the amount of risk we have to take. The problem is, he's also taking the profits into his own hands. This needs to be dealt with... and we need to make sure that this doesn't happen again so making it visual for other handlers is a must.' “So, we send our boy Z to go tighten his ass up. What’s the big deal?” Deac was pushing and pulling with his foot, turning his chair in quarter turns, antsy to get back to spinning. “You not coming tonight” What’s the matter, Deac? Hot date?” “You thought I didn't when I did” Shit, I've got a reservation and a Deactacular Supreme on pre-order.' Me and Deac started laughing again. Reno cocked his head to one side giving us a “What did I miss?” kind of look. I held up my hand and shook my head, telling him not to ask. “Don’t worry D,” I tell him. “We’ll have ya back before it gets cold.” 'Mmm, yes. Quite. The culprit is James Frock. Luckily, he's on the Eastern seaboard so it won't take too much time to get there and back. Shall we?' I raise a questioning eyebrow to Reno. “You’re coming with us?” 'Yes; you two shouldn't have to do the dirty work alone. Oh, and there's one other small issue. The shipments he handles are arm shipments... and he has a small crew that is pretty loyal. Expect them to be heavily armed.' “You bring the Kevlar, Z?” “No, why?” “Cause I done seen this fuckin movie. The black man is the first to die. I ain’t playin” that shit. Uh-uh, no way, no motherfuckin how.” “You don’t have to bother with the Kevlar tonight, gentlemen. The shipment they are carrying contains copkillers.” “Fucking great,” me and Deac mutter at the same time. “Let’s go then.” Me and Deacon get up and follow Reno towards the exit. I reach out and grab Reno by the shoulder. Deacon looks back at us and I nod at him. “Go on ahead, D. I need to confer in private with Mav.” “Oh sure, leave the black man out once again. I see how you fuckers operate.” D walks away muttering something to the effect of looking up Johnny Cochran’s phone number. I couldn’t really make out the rest as he continued down the hall. “Is this the same Frock who operates out of North Carolina?” “One and the same. Why?” “And we’re flying there. Aren’t we?” “Yes. Taking the private jet. Why?” I go over to the small bar at the far end of the room and retrieve three fresh tumblers. From my pocket, I remove a small vile. From the vile, I pour a healthy dose of white powder into each tumbler. Discarding the vile, I pick up the bottle of Crown Royal and fill the tumblers up, swirling the glasses so that the powder dissolved. I handed one to Reno and picked the other two up. I started back down the hallway at a quick pace, Reno at my heels, in order to catch up with Deacon. When he was in sight, I called out to him. “Hey, D! Get over here!” “Oh” Now the white mastas need the colored help. Yessir masts, what can I do for you mastas?” “Can the shit, D. Before we go, a drink.” I handed one of the glasses to D, who snatched it up greedily. I lifted my glass. “To the echelon.” “The echelon,” Reno and Deacon echoed. Deacon raised the glass to his lips, throwing his head back and downing the alcohol in one shot. I dumped mine onto the floor, and motioned Reno to do the same. “Damn, that hit the fuckin spot. Before we jet, I gotta take a royal sized piss.” Deacon pushed past us and went back down the hall. Reno shot me a look. “What the hell was all of that about?” I held up my hand to Reno, all five fingers extended, moving each on down as seconds ticked by. In the background, a loud THUMP could be heard. I walked down the hall, Reno following me. Right in front of the door to the bathroom, Deacon had collapsed onto the floor, a thin line of drool already running out of his mouth. Reno gave me another look. “Care to explain?” “Have you ever rode with Deacon on a plane before?” Reno shook his head. “No.” “Trust me. This is the best way. He really hates flying.” ***** I shuffled through the cards in my hand. Across from me, Reno stared over the top of his cards, trying to figure out what move I was gonna play. I stared Maverick in his eyes before showing my play. “Got any threes?” A wide grin broke across his face. “Go fish.” Damnit. I took my cards and adjusted them accordingly. Out of the corner of my eye, I noticed Deac move slightly. I ignore it though, because he had been tossing slightly the entire ride. When a loud groan came out of Deacon’s mouth, I knew the game was up. Reno looked at me warily. “What’s wrong?” he asked. But before I could answer? “Son of a bitch! Goddamn, what the fuck happened?” I looked up at Deac from my cards. “I don’t know, D. We did the drink, and you fell over. We’ve been waiting on your sorry ass to wake up since.” “That’s some weird fucking shit.” Deac shook his head, trying to get the cobwebs out. Then he started to take notice of the surroundings. “Wait just a goddamn minute. Where the FUCK are we?” Mav looked over at me. “Care to answer that question, John?” “Why don’t you sit down, D?” “No, I don’t think I’ll fucking sit down, Z. Where the fuck are we.” “Umm, I would say somewhere over the great state of Virginia.” “We’are’over’Virginia?” he paused slightly. “How” are? we? fucking? over? Virginia?” “Because we’re in the TUE private jet.” Slowly, D’s eyes start to get wide. His mouth opens and closes, but no words come out. His head moves from left to right, searching for a way out. Oh shit. He scampered for the door, stumbling as he ran, bumping into white leather chairs, pounding fists into the windows. He lunged for the door, but fell short as a grab his ankles and starting pulling him back away from the door. I wrestled him to the ground, forcing him to the ground. Damn, trying to hold D down was like trying to nail Jello to the wall. It’s fucking near impossible. I looked over at Reno who still was sitting in his chair, cards in his hand. “Uh, Maverick?” Deacon lashed out, and I rewrestled him into a new position. “Yes, John?” “A lil help here?” Reno gave me a look of contempt. “You seem to be doing fine.” “Just bring me the fucking duct tape then.” ***** Ten minutes later, Deacon sat somewhat calmly in one of the plush white leather chairs. Across his chest were about sixty of so criss-crossings on gray duct tape. I sat back down at the table with Maverick and we picked up our game of Go Fish. Reno was fixing to make his first play when Deacon interrupted. “This is some fucked up shit! I tell ya'll what, If ya'll let me go right now I promise not to shove my feet up your asses and stomp around wearing you like a new pair of house slippers!” I set my cards down and picked up the roll of duct tape again. “I knew I forgot something.” I tore off another piece about six inches long and leaned into close to Deacon’s face. “Don’t you do that, John! I swear, you do that shit and I will fuck you up so bad that even Vic won’t mmph mmphmm mphm mphm” Deacon said, his words turning into mutters behind the piece of tape. I put the tape back down and picked up my cards again. “Where were we?” Reno looked at me from over his cards. “Got any twos?” “Go Fish.” ***** An hour later the TUE jet screeched to a halt on the runway just outside of Wilmington, North Carolina. Me and Maverick were still sitting at our table. We had abandoned Go Fish and were playing War. Deacon stared at us with hate-filled eyes. Once we were okayed to get up and walk around, I went over to Deacon and ripped off the piece of tape from his mouth. “THAT’S IT GODDAMNIT!!! I’MA GET JOHNNY COCHRAN ON YOUR COLLECTIVE BITCH ASSES!!!!” I looked over at Maverick, who was slowly shaking his head. Slowly, I put the tape back over Deacon’s mouth. “I’ll come back when you’ve calmed a bit.” Deacon strained against his restraints as I followed Maverick out the jet in order to prep everything for later that night.
