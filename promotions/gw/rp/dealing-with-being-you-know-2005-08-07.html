Dealing with being...you know

7 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08-07-2005 Sweat poured in streams and rivulets down Dawn’s nearly bare back, sliding over the myriad of cuts and abrasions she’d suffered in her motorcycle accident just over two weeks ago. The damaged skin was finally starting to heal, which meant her entire back itched as though she’d been attacked by a swarm of mosquitoes’but her pride was a long way off from healing. She hit the bag three times with her left hand, still favouring and protecting her right that had been injured in the accident and again on her sojourn across the western states. In frustration she drove her right elbow into the bag, catching it as it swung left with a rising kick that left an imprint of her long leg in the bags side. The custom helmet she’d had designed for her had absorbed all of the accidents impact, protecting her face and neck from any damage, which made the sweat trickling down her cheeks and off the point of her chin for of an annoyance then anything else’but one that made it increasingly difficult to focus. With a wordless snarl she snapped a straight right hand into the bag, driving it hard away from her’and immediately hopping back from the bag, cradling her hand and cursing a blue streak. “Mother fucker!” she howled, wringing her hand. “Stupid, stupid, of all the branless’agh!” Normally Alex would have joined in from the bench to one side of Dawn’s gym, either teasing Dawn for her slip or asking after her health. Today though there was no Alexis, in fact she had been absent from Dawn’s training and swimming sessions since Emoticon had outed Dawn’s feelings on TV. Feelings for another woman. The very thought elicited another growl from Dawn as she peeled of her gloves and chucked them hard across the room, storming over to where she’d had a simple sink installed. “You’d think I’d been accused of murdering children the way she’s treating me.” She grumbled to herself. A quick turn of the cold faucet and she washed the stinging sweat from her aching hands, the abused skin there quickly going numb from the icy water. Cupping her hands under the faucet, Dawn splashed the water back up onto her face and into her hair, reveling in the sensation of the cold water running down the skin of her neck and shoulders. A tired sigh escaped her lips as she ran her fingers up though her hair, her arms then falling limply to her sides as she stared into the mirror over the sink. Her own emerald eyes stared back at her, the same water dripping from Mirror-Dawn’s hair as was dripping from her own’but she couldn’t help but feel as though she almost didn’t know the face in the mirror anymore’didn’t know what she was doing with herself at all. “I never expected this?” She murmured, reaching out one hand to touch Mirror-Dawn’s face. Dawn and Lia had been no strangers to each other when Dawn first came to Global, in fact the vampiress had managed to pick out Dawn from under the mask of Nariko. They had first met in WMW, although it had not been under the most auspicious of circumstances. I will say there is a clearer sky at night as before when I actually thought you were valuable to me and my cause I started losing matches but now that I’m free from you there is nothing stopping me from rocketing to the success that I feel I deserve! What’s more is that I found someone who could fill your shoes capably being the dominant female in the Kindred and if you haven’t met her, I’m sure you’ve seen her in the back. Her name is SHADOW! One hand went to Dawn’s face, the memories of the night Corvus embarrassed her on national television flooding back to her in full force. Her shoulders shook a little as she repressed a small sob, refusing to shed anymore tears over what had happened that night’it had been hard enough to struggle to hold back tears as she’d slunk out of the ring past Shadow, wishing she could just disappear and get out form under the other woman’s mocking gaze. They’d never stepped into the ring against each other in WMW, not even once had the ring marquee read “Dawn McFarlane vs Shadow” despite the fact that for months all she had thought about was punching Corvus? new companions fangs down her pretty throat. Then Shadow had left WMW and Dawn felt she could finally breathe again, move out from under the “Shadow” that had been plaguing her. Dawn snorted, looking up at her reflection again. “Which was ridiculous of course. The only thing holding me back in WMW was fear and their terrible business sense.” Things in Global had been very different of course, Christian Zenith had forced both women together, making them put aside their differences to work for something they both wanted’to dominate. Working together had shown Dawn pieces of Shadow she’d blinded herself to in WMW, had forced her to admit that the other woman didn’t want to ruin her life’and she had taken some solace in finally finding a woman in the sport she could confide in. And had never seen where that was leading. “Now it’s all I can do not to think about her?” She whispered, her reflection gazing fearfully back at her. Unwilling to see anymore Dawn turned from the mirror to head upstairs, picking up a towel to dry the sweat from her face and hair as she went. The TV was on when she arrived on the main floor, Dawn never had much use for satellite television but Alex seemed to have taken to it with gusto. Reclining against the door frame of the living room, Dawn watched Kim Possible for a few minutes before speaking. “I thought you were an outside person.” “Gah!” Alex started up from the couch, looking wide eyed back at Dawn. “Jesus Dawn you scared the shit out of me.” “Yeah I’m sneaky, like a ninja.” Came the sardonic reply. “How come you’re spending so much time in front of the TV all of a sudden?” Alex shrugged uncomfortably, settling back onto the couch. “There’s nothing else to do’I can only swim in the pool for so long before I get bored of it.” Dawn winced a little, the sting of Alex’s words biting into her. “And I suppose I’m too boring to spend time with anymore.” There was an uncomfortable pause as Alex struggled for words, trying to find a way to say what was on her mind. Dawn cut her off, pushing herself away from the door frame. “I’m not going to bite you, unlike some of the people living here. But you do what you want to do, I’m sure Virgil would love to take you shopping.” Her voice had more bite then she intended, but there was no way she was going to apologize in her state of mind. She turned quickly on her heel and took the stairs two at a time, not interested in hearing what Alex had to say in reply. She paused at the landing of the second floor, staring for a long moment at the oak door there, one of the only facets of the house she hadn’t replaced or upgraded. Reaching out to run a hand down the worn wooden frame, Dawn fished a key out of her pocket, unlocking the door and quickly darting inside to close and lock it behind her. The warm, earthly smell of her fathers study was a welcoming sensation, and Dawn pressed her back against the door as she took it in, trying to will some of the pain from her mind and her body. The study was unchanged, always exactly as she left it when she finished dusting each week. Dawn avoided this room most times, finding the memories of her beloved father too painful to bear’but there was something here she wanted’maybe even needed. Quickly crossing the old carpet, Dawn stepped up to an ornate wall cabinet, pulling the doors open and taking a step back in anticipation. Crystal decanters and bottles gleamed in the study’s warm lighting, the liquids contained within reflecting gold, amber, silver and a myriad of other colours found in very few types of drink. James McFarlane had been an avid collector of high quality alcohol, although he drank it only sparingly, preferring to save it for special occasions. Dawn had her first taste of “real” alcohol, as her father called it, when she was 17 and the subsequent coughing fit had left her gasping for breath for what felt like hours. Now she removed one of the decanters with trembling hands, the amber liquid inside sloshing around as she set it on the floor, reaching up to retrieve a matching glass before she plopped down onto the floor next to it. “I’I can’t help it daddy.” She whispered, staring into the decanter. “I never meant to be this way’I never chose to be like this?” She closed her eyes, trying to fight back the tears she felt were coming, but when she reached for a comforting spot in her mind she found’Lia. The cool firmness of Lia’s embrace was still fresh in her mind, and almost against her will Dawn could remember the feel of the other woman in her arms, the almost fragile strength she felt whenever she was close to the vampiress. Dawn forced her eyes open again, staring back at the bottle that had nearly ended her life. In WMW she had hit rock bottom, feeling she had no place to go and no one to turn to. The drink had been an escape for her then, a nightly indulgence that dulled the pain and killed the unpleasant memories. She’d never dared to drink in her father’s study however, always feeling the shame of her addiction any time she even thought about what lay beyond’so she jumped from seedy dive to biker bar, from trendy club to irish pub’anyplace that would help her ease the pain for a little while. She could feel that pain again, the stirrings of new hurt she’d found when she saw the distance in Lia’s eyes. She knew. Dawn had tried to hide her feeling, had even tried to hide them from herself’but in the end it had all come to naught, and here she was again? ??back at the beginning.” She murmured, still unable to take her eyes from the bottle before her. With a trembling hand she unstoppered it, holding the neck just under her nose to breathe in the heady aroma. The old need seemed just as strong to her, a near physical pain in the pit of her stomach that cried out for the object before her’but it was dulled some, dulled by the memory of what had come next, the spiral her life had become. Marcus had come then, freeing Dawn from her addiction, but trapping her in a seemingly endless spiral of assault and abuse’that ended only when Corvus had stepped in to rescue her, which of course had begun the spiral she was just completing now. The need for drink was not only dulled by memories of Marcus, there was a feeling of reluctance in her, a sense of trepidation that didn’t make immediate sense....until she could picture Lia again. “I’d be hiding again wouldn’t I?” Dawn whispered, trying to set the bottle down with shaking hands. “I wouldn’t be honest with my feelings for her’I’d be hiding from myself’again?” She set her jaw, pushing up to her feet and slowly replacing the bottle back into her fathers cabinet, closing the door and locking it firmly this time. She rested her forehead against the doors, closing her eyes for a moment and pretending’just for a second, that the smell of the study was the familiar smell of her father. “Thanks Dad’I needed this talk.”
