The Final Beginning. Part 9 - Wake up

30 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30/6/03 Salaok drops to his knees next to the bed. ”I will never stop loving you Lisha. Never.” Lisha had finally said her last words and Salaok closed his eyes tightly, trying to hold back the emotion but it was of no use. She was gone. The love of his life had passed away and there was nothing he could do about it. Unlike all of the other problems in life which he had been through, there had always been something that he could do about it, but this couldn’t be change. Although, there was a time, that Salaok was helpless to do anything and the feeling of helplessness brought it all back. Back to a place that it had business in being. The present. ---- Laughing as they run towards the house with their mother in toe, a young Salaok and Eve never would have seen it coming. Salaok was only in his mid teen and Eve was only twelve. But what they were about to go through would make them both grow up faster than they ever should have had to. Their father had locked the door behind them as they had gone out to the movies as he wasn’t wanting to be disturbed while doing his work. So when Salaok knocked on the door and there was no response, there wasn’t anything strange about that because their father often went to bed early so he could be up first thing in the morning to begin his usual training of the students. The students in The School of Pain that is. A school that he and his best friend Pit had created. A school that Salaok himself had attended. Johnathon wasn’t home, nor would he be for a long time as he was serving yet another stint at juvenile jail. Cheryl Davis begins to riffle through her bag for her keys and then finally finds them, inserting the small brown one into the lock on the door and turning the handle. The warmth that the family had been expecting didn’t hit them, but instead a stone cold feeling blew past them as they stood in the doorway. ”I bet your father has left the window open again.”She muttered as they walked into the house and turned the lights on. Well the place was no different to what it normally would be. Everything was in the right place so there hadn’t been any troubles while they were away. But then again, they had only been gone four hours, what possibly could have the man of the house gotten up to in that time? Salaok heads straight for the stairs and leaps up them three at the time, in a rush to see his father and tell him all about the movie. ”Don’t wake your father if he’s sleeping. You know how grumpy he gets.”And boy was that ever correct. Chris Davis, although a master in the combat world, not the best when it came to normal house hold living. But then again, it was something that everyone had become used to over the years and Salaok had never knows him any other way so the gruff man that his father was, was seen as the way that all father’s were. He finally reaches the top of the stairs and creeps towards the study door which is slightly ajar and peers through the gap. Nothing. His father wasn’t there, or at least not in his line of sight and so with great effort to be quiet, Salaok pushes on the door with his shoulder and peers around it. No, he was right the first time, his father wasn’t in there. ”Dad?” Salaok whispers in his search for his father. Salaok moves away from the study door and walks towards his parents bed room and again whispers ”Dad? You there?” Again Salaok gets no response and so he pushes the slightly opened door open that little bit more and pokes his head through the gap. There was his father. Asleep just as his mother had thought he would be but Salaok had to go up and see him. And to go and tell him about the movie. Salaok knew that his mother had told him not to, and he knew that most of the time, his father would get annoyed but this story was just too good to let wait. Trying not to make a lot of nose as he walked across the room towards the bed, Salaok doesn’t want to wake his father suddenly. No, he would rather shake him gently until he wakes up. The same way his father had done for him every morning in order to get Salaok up and off to the School with him. Although he was fifteen years old, Salaok still acted very close to his father and at times treated him like his best friend. So as Salaok edged across the room, he began to smile and felt a laugh coming on because he could just see his father’s face as he wakes him up, being full of surprise. Finally after almost a minute of travelling over a few feet, Salaok reaches the bedside and looks at his sleeping father. His eyes closed but sleeping peacefully he was not. His face contorted and seemingly full of pain. Salaok grabs his father’s shoulders ever so gently and shakes him lightly. His father doesn’t stir and so Salaok shakes a little harder. Again, no response from his father. Standing upright and letting go of his father, Salaok crosses his arms over his chest and huffs, annoyed at his father’s deep sleep. ”Dad?” Salaok says in a low volume but much like the shaking, it gained no response. ”Dad, wake up.” This time Salaok’s voice was a lot louder than before but still not as loud as he would normally speak. This nothing. All he wanted was to talk to his father and share something good with him but his father just wouldn’t wake up. He drops down onto the bed, making sure that he rocked the bed as much as possible and awaited the response but still none was forthcoming. Salaok grabs his father hard by the shoulders, now annoyed at his refusal to wake. Shaking him lightly at first Salaok builds up the intensity slowly and increases the volume of his calls of “Dad”. No matter how hard Salaok shook or yelled at his father, he just wouldn’t wake. Fear began to sink into the teenager as he shakes his father harder than before now. The panic becoming evident in his voice and gaining the attention of his mother and sister down stairs. ”Dad, wake up. WAKE UP DAD. FUCKING WAKE UP!” Salaok was now yelling at his father as tears began to run down his face and his father simply didn’t wake up. Cheryl runs into the room and sees what’s going on and then sees the look on her husband’s face. The stone white skin tone and the marks around his neck. Chris Davis was dead. Salaok collapses next to the bed to his knees with his arms over his father. ---- Salaok raises his head from the side of Lisha’s hospital bed with tears in his eyes. ”I’m sorry.”
