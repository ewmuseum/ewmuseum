The Cure For the Common Curse - Change

31 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5.31.03 (DZ) May 30th, 2003, A limousine The scene fades from black, showing the cityscape of Dallas, Texas. Cars line the freeway’s, crowding and slowing everyone down. Tonight’s the big night, Global Wrestling’s Dead Zone Pay Per View. Men will rise and men will fall, men will prove themselves and men will prove nothing. Time will only tell where all of the Global Wrestling Roster will be placed; whether they will come out on top and prove themselves worthy to be called a Champion or whether they will be pinned and suffer the agonies of defeat. Tyler Pierce plans to be standing amongst the Champions even on this day, the day of his curse. With a smirk and a cold glass in his hand, Tyler sits in the limo slowly moving down the freeway towards his hotel. He’s dressed in the usual black suit with black tie and blue dress shirt, his eyes no longer covered by the sunglasses that protrude from his jacket pocket. He has a cell phone in one hand, his eyes scanning the small screen as he waits. With nonchalant movements, he lifts the phone to his ear and pauses, waiting for the other end to be picked up. “Yeah, this is Tyler.” There’s a pause as the other end speaks. “Yeah man, good luck tonight. Look, everything’s set, right” No complications?” Another pause. Tyler smiles and glances around the limo. A duffle bag sits on each side of him, one carrying the Global Wrestling insignia; one however, does not. “Yeah, I’ve got everything with me.” Tyler continues but suddenly stops, listening to the other man for a moment. He nods in agreement. “I know” leave it in the car. Don’t worry? Alright then, I’ll see the guys there.” Tyler’s attempted riddance of the phone call is quickly avoided by the man on the other end; Tyler nods, his eyes shutting for a second as the man speaks, the words obviously reaching Tyler. “I know. I’m beating it this year” I’m going to come out on top this time.” Within seconds, the two have said their goodbyes leaving the identity of the other man a complete secret. Tyler takes a sip from the glass and sighs, closing his eyes as he enjoys it. A voice rings out from the other side of the limo. “Tyler, um, I don’t want to be an ass but do you really think you should tempt the fates by drinking?” Tyler smiles and glances up in your direction, his eyes obviously focusing on the other man in the limo. “Let me worry about that” I’ll tell you the same thing I told him, I’m not letting it get to me this year? besides, there’s nothing to worry about.” “Nothing to worry about?” The vaguely familiar voice rang again, its emotion pouring forth in true concern for Tyler. “Over the last few years this date has brought you nothing but bad luck because of that kinda shit! Now tonight, you have a chance to change the date’s meaning. You have this Championship tonight” and you can win it! Don’t you under?” “SAM! Chill man” it’s ginger ale. Do you think I’m foolish enough to be drinking today? I mean, damn. How much of a fool do you take me for? Tonight means more to me than anything’my brothers are there. I know my parents won’t be but my brothers are? and that’s a start. I’m going to prove that I’ve changed tonight? I’m going to win that match, Sam. You’re my best friend? you know me. I want this so bad I can taste? so bad that I can feel the desire pulsing in every blood vessel. ? Tyler continued, his emotion driving the speech. “This belt isn’t that important; I could care less to be wearing this belt. The improvements I’ve made over the last year” That’s what’s important? that’s why I’m riding in this limo tonight on my way to the arena. Yes, I want the championship? I ache for it? and I plan to earn it, but it’s not the championship really? it’s what it stands for. It means I’ve changed, that I’ve beaten my curse? and that I can rise above any obstacle. Tonight, I’m facing GW veterans and rookies. As a rookie myself, I plan on coming out on top? beating legends and former world champions, beating newbie’s and unexplored fresh meat. I’m going to win tonight, Sam? I’m going to win.” The worlds are filled with emotion, enough energy and feeling that it seems to be pouring from every orifice of Tyler’s body. The scene pans slightly, now showing Sam, Tyler’s best friend from high school, college, and today. Sam nods a few times and glances out at the great metropolis that is Dallas, the tinted window not obscuring the view enough to be distracting. “I’m just worried man.” “You’re gonna jinx me if you start saying shit like that. Everything will be fine” nothing can go wrong today. I’ve worked too hard for it.” “Nothing can go wrong” It’s thoughts like that that will jinx you faster than anything, man.” Sam’s comment draws a smirk on Tyler’s face. He lifts the glass to his lips again, sipping at the ginger ale as he stares through the tinted glass. Dallas passes by slowly, traffic clearing up slightly. “So how long have you been training for tonight, Ty?” the tone in Sam’s voice has lost momentum, his curiosity fueled by the sheer lack in conversation. “Physically” the last year.” Sam glances at Tyler, his eyes shifting down and then back up. “Mentally” since Will died, right?” Tyler never looks from the window, his eyes transfixed on the world passing outside. Slowly he places his glass on the bar and speaks, his words strong and firm? yet full of emotion. “I still take part of the responsibility for what happened to Will” I take more for Ed. The fact remains, I’m as ready as I’ve ever been, Sam. Tonight, I’m not fighting for me out there. I’m fighting for Ed? and William. Those guys mean a lot to me and tonight, I’m going to dedicate the match in their honor.” Sam nods and glances towards the window again, his eyes finding the same spot that Tyler’s have been staring at. Sam’s voice rings out again, a bit more of a lighter tone this time? “So what’s with the second duffle bag, man?” Tyler grins slightly and cuts his eyes towards Sam, a certain gleam of mischief in his eyes. “Sorry, Sammy, I can’t tell you.” “Aw, c’mon man. You know you can trust me” I’ve never let you down before, what’s the difference?” Sam pleads, pure curiosity driving him forward. “Look, I really can’t say, Sam. A lot of shit is going down in Global Wrestling” and it’s best for right now if no one leaks that others know about it. Ya know what I’m saying?” Tyler glances at Sam, the mischief escaping and quickly being replaced by a serious tone. Sam nods and leans back in his chair. “Besides, it’s the first time I’ve seen you in a year man. I don’t want to talk business” once we get to the hotel I’ve got to show you the new Civic.” Sam lifts a brow, gazing towards his friend. “The new Civic” What happened to that beautiful Vette of yours?” A smirk crosses Tyler’s lips as he glances out towards the city again. “It was totaled.” The words come out quickly, a matter of fact tone to it. Sam blinks, actually quite surprised by the news. “Are you serious, man” That was one hell of a beautiful car.” A chuckle escapes the throat of Tyler Pierce as he takes a sip of his ginger ale. “It’s one hell of a beautiful mass of twisted metal and glass now.” Sam shakes his head, sitting back. “Well, man, how’d it happen?” Tyler glances across at his friend upon hearing the query? his mind flashes back: Tyler flashes back, watching the night as the images pass before him; first the boredom in the car, then the traffic in Dallas. He remembers the sound of his horn as it stuck; the sound of metal slamming into metal as the man crushed him in from behind. He closes his eyes, remembering how he reacted? how he put the man in the hospital. “Let’s just say” one wrong move that started a domino effect.” This does not answer Sam’s question but he knows better than to touch it again. The scene changes to get a view of the limo as it pulls into the parking lot of the Adolphus hotel. It slowly moves around the circular drive and pulls to the front of the hotel; within seconds, the prompt service of the hotel valet has opened the limo door for Pierce and his companion. Sam is the first to step out, dressed sloppily in pair of jeans and an old AC/DC screen t-shirt. He reaches inside and pulls out a blue duffle bag, one unlike either of Tyler’s. He glances at the nearest attendant and drops it with him and pats him on the back. The man glances at the bag and smirks, grumbling something about how much he hates his job. Tyler is next to step from the limousine; he however, fits the elegance of the situation. Tyler straightens his tie and suit as he stands; the impeccable taste in the suit says it all. The suit, a black Giorgio Armani with blue and gold accents, is one that truly compliments Pierce as it was tailored for him by the tailor in Boston; complete with the coat of arms cufflinks, tie clip, and dark sunglasses, he captures a style few can. Tyler nods to the attendant that Sam picked out and places a tip in his hand and tells him which room the bag belongs in. Sam stands before the hotel, looking up at the building as the limousine pulls off. Tyler places a hand on his shoulder to grab his attention; both men begin into the building when “Mr. Pierce!” is heard from behind them. Both men turn, the frame freezing and then panning around, everything motionless until the pan has completed at which point, everything begins to move again. Alexis Steel, one of GW’s staff of reporters, has begun her approach towards Tyler and Sam. Sam grins and leans towards Tyler, whispering? “Who’s the bitch with the nice a...” Tyler nudges him in the gut with his elbow, quickly cutting him off before taking a step forward and extending his hand towards Alexis. “Global Wrestling sent me here to see if I could get a few words with you.” Alexis is quick to the point although, there’s a lack of a business quality to her that relaxes the situation. Tyler extends his hand, offering to shake? when she extends hers, he lifts her hand to his lips and kisses the back gently. “I’d be glad to give you an interview Miss?”” Alexis blushes and glances down, attempting to hide a grin. “yours” er, Steel. Alexis Steel.” Alexis shakes her head and regains her composure although, she doesn’t retake her hand. “I’m a reporter with Global Wrestling. Is this a good time, Mr. Pierce?” Tyler smiles and glances back at Sam, thoughts racing through his mind. He glances at the young reporter and smiles, “Please, my friends call me Ty.” Alexis smiles and glances at her hand again, her cheeks returning to their reddened state as she withdraws her hand at a snail's pace. She continues to smile, staring at him for a moment. A flash hits her as if she remembers why she came here in the first place. “I” oh! I was wondering if we could?” Tyler cuts her off. “Name the time and place, Miss Steel.” Alexis grins a bit. “What room are you in?” Tyler smiles and motions towards the notepad she holds in her hand. He speaks calmly, correcting her, “I mean” what time would you like to do the interview?” Alexis turns a fire red and glances down. “Oh! I?” Tyler hides a smile. “How about in thirty minutes” Let me get settled in and we’ll get started. There’s a restaurant downstairs? I’ll meet you there.” Alexis nods, not saying anything as she’s still extremely embarrassed by her mistake. Tyler turns and glances at Sam, who is all grins. They walk into the hotel as Alexis begins fanning herself with the notebook she holds? the scene fades to black. Tyler’s hotel room is laid out the same as last time but now, Sam sits in front of the large television, his eyes glued to the screen like a dead fly to fly-paper. Tyler walks into the room, still dressed in his immaculate suit. He smirks a bit and walks to the bed, picking up his briefcase. “Sam, you sure you don’t mind this?” Sam finally shows a sign of life as he glances back at Tyler, shaking his head. “Nah, I don’t mind at all, Ty. “Sides, she’s hot” I understand.” Tyler smirks as he places the case on the bed; he opens it and flips it up revealing the inside. Papers are strapped into the case at the bottom. One side contains a pair of brown envelopes, the top one is marked “GW Contract.” The one underneath is covered and can’t be read. Papers and printed schedules rest aside that and beside that, a notepad and pen. Securely fasted to the roof of the case is a small calendar and a list of phone numbers. Tyler speaks as he unsnaps two latches at the top, the roof falling flush with the bottom of the case to reveal a small laptop concealed inside. A flip phone is fastened next to that and a small, silver plated 9mm handgun on the opposite side. The handle is engraved with the same coat of arms that marks his cufflinks. “It’s not about that, Sam. This is business” exposure. To get the big matches, the people have to know who I am. The people know who I am by seeing me in matches like tonight? and by doing publicity shit like this interview. GW books by ratings? and if I draw attention, then I’ll draw ratings. If I draw ratings, then I’ll draw the bigger matches. You get me, Sam?” Sam nods, having not even listened to Tyler as he spoke. He’s returned to the screen, watching the dark match that Tyler fought in just two weeks ago. “When in the hell did you learn how to do that?” Tyler glances at him as he speaks, shaking his head as he realizes he hasn’t been listening to a thing he says. Tyler fastens the compartment back, reconciling the cell phone, laptop, and handgun. He lifts the briefcase and walks towards the door. “I’ll be back later, Sam.” With this, he exits the room and closes the door behind him. He turns and starts walking towards the elevator. A man in a black suit and earpiece stands at the elevator. He nods to Pierce as he approaches and presses the button. “Thanks, Toby.” Tyler continues speaking to the man as they both step on the elevator. The door closes, Tyler speaking as it does so. “Sometimes people change” and sometimes, they don’t.”
