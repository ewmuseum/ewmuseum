Crimson Rain

23 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5.23.04 A room of traditional Japanese design, dimly lit with candles hanging in an intricate pattern about the ceiling. Rice paper walls painted with ancient calligraphy, held together with oak and surrounded in brick. The traditions of Japan meet with the marvels of modern architecture. The floor is a hard wood, stained in a very light cherry; in the center of the room is a low dinner or meeting table, currently holding sake and four small containers - one for each guest. Two Japanese women service the room, preparing it for the upcoming meeting. Each woman is dressed in the traditional Geisha outfit, a customary service uniform for very formal gatherings. An older gentlemen walks in, his hair dark and graying, somewhere between young and old, bold and wise. Ah, to be in the middle of perfection, with one foot in youth and one in wisdom. The older man wears a Kimono with line after line of Kanji scrolling down everywhere, a black lotus emblem behind white characters on the back. Could this be a signification of his alliances? The Nakazura Family of the Japanese Yakuza, marked by the black lotus - kuroii hasu - held a high place in society until recently when they were targeted and taken down by an unknown entity. Nakazura Gohei Routaika, or elderly master of the Nakazura family, Gohei called upon his nephews, Ichiro and Jushin Nakazura to unmask the identities of this new 'American Assailant.' The Echelon Gohei Routaika now seeks to strengthen his organization within the Yakuza ranks; his intention? To gather support for his family and lineage so that extinction is not on the horizon. Routaika of four Yakuza lineages were gathering with the Nakazura Routaika, Gohei, to discuss the events taking place in their native Japan. Daimura Routaika Yamajin Routaika Debuhiretsukan Routaika Their arrival would bring an amazing surplus of arms into the area, tightening security to the point where even your teeth would feel crowded. Some had already been placed in the area, occupying certain rooms and watching from the balconies of the hotel. Others took to acting as street urchins, keeping a sharp eye out. Two of the three Routaika had already arrived hours before and remained under strict watch, their lives in danger from this new foreign force. A final Routaika, Yamajin Yukio, would arrive via helicopter in a short while. Nakazura Routaika, Gohei, had been the first to arrive, seeing to the preparations of the grand meeting. -- Clouds were forming in the sky. It looked as if Hell had opened up in the middle of the Heaven's above, corrupting the beauty in all directions. Slowly what was once a joyous blue dimmed and changed pigmentation, altering to a light purple and then darker. Finally it was consumed by blackness. This of course spawned more corruption, spreading continuously from morning to mid-day. A breeze blows, carrying upon its shoulders a chill with enough foreshadowing to sever a man's spine with convulsions. For May, the air has an uncommon temperature. People walk slowly about the streets, dressed in light coats atop their normal Summer apparel. The streets bustle as normal, the cold not really affecting the Mr. Kidake pedestrians? daily trudge through their grimy existence. The lights of the city hum to a dim brilliance before fully illuminating, shedding light upon this dark day. Footsteps. Yes, there were lots of footsteps. But there was a set in particular that make a click with each and every step. Click. Click. Click. Click. These steps seem to be amplified, echoing off the sides of the buildings and bouncing to the opposite side and back again, to and fro, to and fro. Between the tightly packed buildings on either side of the busy market area, pedestrians and shoppers swarmed through a myriad of shops and stands, purchasing small items. Each person had their own goal and path, each one hustled and bustled, maneuvering in and out of the crowd to get to their next destination. Everyone was polite and quick to carry out their errands. All but one who seemed to ignore everyone before him. Straight. He walks perfectly straight through the crowd, a light black leather trench coat hanging to his calves. Despite the unnaturally dark day, he wears a pair of dark sunglasses over his eyes. His hair has been tied back, held in place by a hair-band as dark as his jet black hair, obfuscated by the multitude of strands. A goatee on his face and a business suit underneath his trench coat. His hands are shielded by a pair of black leather gloves; one hand carries a black briefcase, the other a simple black umbrella. The suit is of Mandarin style, giving him a modern business look. A pair of boots adorn his feet, the tips armored in a silver material. He walks ... Click. Click. Click. Click. Several people move from his path, quoting a quick 'Suimasen!' at best. His lack of respect for the numerous others wins him none in return. A third click enters into the mix as he taps his umbrella's tip once onto the stone street before him with each stride. Click. Click. Click. Click. Click. Click. The man stops and lifts his left arm and pulls back his sleeve, eyeing the sterling-silver time-piece attached here. June 16th, 2004 - 1:00 pm Mt. Kidake, Japan - Kotai Market Place On his left, an open fish market receives patron after patron; the smell of rotting aquarius animal overtaking the perimeter. To his right, Ouji no Ryoji; an infamous hotel. Rough translation? Hotel of the Prince. Or The Prince's Hotel. Reserved for an elite customer base that includes foreign investors, the Japanese business elite, Japanese government officials, and Yakuza figure-heads. The day grows darker. The rain will come. Crimson rain, pouring from the heavens as Le Diabolique bathes in the blood of the innocent, his sickle tainted with the blood of an Assamite, his grin full of desolation, and his eyes void of compassion. Click. Click. Click. Click. Click. Click. Using one against the other; that is the true sign of stratagem. Brother against Brother. Directly, it had been impossible but this way. Today, it would become even more than ever dreamed. A crack; the sound of thunder slamming about in the obelisk inspired sky above. The clouds light up with hints of red light visible from the ground. The storm had been brewing all day, awaiting the perfect moment to strike; to let itself be known to the world below. The man in black takes a sharp right and is met by a second man, this one dressed similarly but without an overcoat and in a more traditional collar and color of dress shirt. The man holds a briefcase in his right hand, his left has been tucked into his jacket pocket. The duo walks under a canopy and are quickly greeted by a member of the hotel staff. 'Konnichiwa!' Is all that is spoken from the Japanese doorman as the two walk past him and into the double doors, the scene fading as the sounds of a helicopter are heard in the distance. -- Championships are a truly amazing thing. Tag Team Champion, Maverick Reno takes on World Champion, Josh in a non-title match. Doesn't that sound great? Please, allow me to explain. You're walking down a street and you see 'World Champion takes on one half of the Tag Team Champions' written on a billboard; what do you think? That the World Champion has been put into a squash match, that the tag champion is just being given a shot to show his talent before he's once again reduced to the lowly world of mid-card events. Titles can be deceiving. Bubba McCoy, CEO and President of Global Incorporated. What does this mean to you? It means that Bubba McCoy controls and determines everything that happens within the walls of Global Incorpora-WRONG! Bubba McCoy is a shell of a once honorable man. Christina McCoy, daughter of Bubba McCoy and acting President of Global Incorporated. What does this mean to you? That Daddy's little girl is following in his footsteps? That Daddy's Little Girl is keeping the family business up and running it until Daddy can return? Again, deceiving. The McCoy's may hold the name-sake to Global Incorporated, but they are not in control. No. They are merely puppets in a game of cat and mouse, being pulled along by a piece of string like a dog chasing a bone. The Echelon pulls the strings behind this Kabuki theatre. Josh stands tall and proud with the big golden strap about his waist; he knows that he is on top of the world. He's making the big money, getting the rankings, the fans, the women. Josh is living in a world of obfuscation; a world before the facts. Josh has not beaten all that Global has to offer; he's barely scratched the surface. Soon, Josh will know the truth... he will know that that title he holds is nothing but writing on paper. He will know that being a Champion involves much more than holding the title... it involves being the best. That's something that Josh simply cannot claim to be. soon, he will realize that his championship means nothing ... that it's merely a paper title. -- Two Geisha's drop into the lowest possible bow, one on either side of the Japanese style table. Black and white lotus hang and decorate the room, giving a very loyal front for Gohei Nakazura. Guards enter the room from two doors, one on either side. Each guard holds an Uzi, fully loaded with extended clips. Two guards feature a tattoo on their right hands; the tattoo of the Kanji for mountain. Yamajin Routaika enters the room, a black business suit adorning his form. He's as Westernized as they come in Japan and believe me, they can become very Westernized. He walks towards the table, his eyes shifting over the sake. Another pair of guards, these featuring the Kanji’s for loyalty and village. They take their place on the north wall, preparing to keep their Routaika safe from harm. Daimura Routaika enters the room, adorned in a formal Japanese kimono featuring the insignias of his family; he appears old yet wise, his face worn with sands of time. The third pair take the East wall, the Kanji’s for wealth and nobility bore into their hands. Debuhiretsukan now appears in the room, dressed also in a kimono featuring his family's insignia. He is not a small man, weighing all of four-hundred pounds. He lowers himself slowly to the ground, resting at the eastern side of the table. Both Yamajin and Daimura take their places, one on the northern side and one on the western. Gohei Nakazura, Routaika of the Nakazura lineage now appears dressed in a kimono bearing the black lotus. He kneels and the four men bow to one another, Gohei lowering just slightly more than the others. He begins by addressing the other men, speaking in their native tongue and translated for our enjoyment. 'Thank you all for joining on this occasion.' The three Yakuza Routaikas' nod slightly to Gohei, motioning that he should continue. 'I have called you here so that your eyes may open to the arising problem of a conflicting organization that has been opposing the Yakuza. As you may know, it has been focusing most of its resources upon the Nakazura lineage but that may change; the power and spread of this organization could threaten every family within a month's time. It is not often that the Yakuza is threatened but of this, I am truly concerned.' A moment passes before words are spoken. Routaika of the Daimura lineage speaks, his hands slowly lifting the cup of fresh Sake from the table. 'Have you attempted negotiation with this foreign entity, Nakazura-San?' He sips of his sake, his eyes closing to enjoy every last drop. Gohei looks towards Yamajin and then back to Daimura, his expression losing some of its composure, 'Negotiation? They have slaughtered my entire organization, Daimura Routaika! Negotiation was attempted in the beginning before the murders, assassinations and bombings! We Nakazura's have little left.' Debuhiretsukan holds a hand into the air, silencing Gohei quickly. 'You speak irrationally, Nakazura. You speak as if this were a personal affair rather than business. Do you truly believe that this American entity can not be dealt with through our channels? Surely you are not so brash...' 'Brash!” I have seen so many of my lineage die! The only surviving full members are myself and my nephews, Ichiro and Jushin. You speak of negotiations, they speak only in clips and ammunition. I do not care how this ends, I just want it to end! The Nakazura can only take so much before we fall.' The tone in Gohei's voice is becoming desperate, his hope of seeking aid in his battles slowly growing dim. Yamajin slowly places his sake cup onto the table and glances up at Gohei, 'This Echelon; you say that their methods are direct and personal? Do you truly believe that these attacks on your family and organization were purposefully directed at you or your family in some way?' Gohei nods and speaks again, his tone calming quickly, 'My nephews, Ichiro and Jushin, worked for these men in the west. They were a part of this organization until the lotus lead them to a separate path. They split ways and at first, there seemed to be no turbulence. The Echelon was quiet until, as they say, it erupted. There was a personal vengeance placed upon them as they were marked as traitors by their former allies. It is for this reason that I believe these attacks to be personal; I believe that they are seeking alternative ways to strike at my nephews. It is for this reason that I call upon you, my brethren. I need your aid in this fight! In this blood war!' The room falls silent, Gohei Nakazura, Routaikan of the Nakazura Kazoku having finished his plea for assistance. The three Yakuza elders are silent, sipping at their sake, giving one another glances. Finally, Yamajin speaks again. 'You come to us, Routaikan of the Debuhiretsukan lineage, the Daimura lineage and the Yamajin lineage for aid. Have you not sought the same from the Yakuza's council?' The question is presented curiously with more a confirming tone than one of true curiosity. 'No. I thought it wise to speak with the Yakuza families in my own territory before involving the Yakuza council. I felt that seeking aid from them would be a sign of weakness within our prefecture.' Yamajin nods and glances towards his two counterparts. 'That was wise of you, Nakazura Routaikan. Debuhiretsukan, Daimura and I had already convened on this matter before our arrival here today but we felt the desire to hear your plea before we made our decision final.' Gohei looks up slowly, his eyes pleading for mercy. 'We have given our aid... but not to the Nakazura lineage.' There's a look of shock on Gohei Nakazura's face as Yamajin spills the proverbial beans on the matter. A pair of double doors open behind Gohei and he turns, his face telling the story of a million tragedies. Click. Click. Click. Click. Click. Click. The man in black has entered the room at the right moment, making for a dramatic entrance. Yamajin Debuhiretsukan and Daimura stand and bow to the man and promptly receive a bow in return. Gohei sits on his knees before the table, still in shock at the events that have transpired. The man in black's accomplice walks towards the table and bows again to the four before placing his briefcase upon the table. He steps backwards to stand aside hitodekuroii (The man in black.) 'Konnichiwa, Reno-san!' The voice of Yamajin rings forth, welcoming the devil into the nest. Gohei's mind reels with thoughts, betrayal sinking in. Ichiro and Jushin had warned of the Echelon's treachery and their ability to assault you at any turn but until now, Gohei had no idea how true it was. Maverick Reno had somehow garnered the backing of the Debuhiretsukan, Daimura, and Yamajin lineages all while assaulting the Nakazura lineage. 'Konnichiwa, minasan.' Reno spouts off, a devilish smile gracing his lips as he glances at Gohei. 'Ah, Uncle Gohei! How are you and those marvelous nephews of yours? Reno is almost cut off as Gohei stands, demanding an answer from his fellow Yakuza Routaikas'. 'What is he doing here?' Gohei spits out, his formal demeanor fading away into a rage filled tantrum. It's not long before Gohei's tantrum is answered by the aiming of uzis. Two men take aim at Gohei Nakazura while the other four aim at the Nakazura guards on the south side of the room. Reno smiles and places his briefcase and umbrella onto the floor. He holds up his hands, motioning for all to calm a bit. The guns are lowered slightly, the tension still remaining even when Gohei's question is answered. 'Your nephews betrayed a union in America. They return here and bring this betrayal with them, leading a powerful adversary into our nest. They brought shame upon themselves and the Nakazura lineage! Then, they endangered the rest of us! We had two options: Go to the council and appear weak or accept the business offer and become even stronger. I believe you can see our decision. We are truly sorry that it had to happen this way, Gohei.' Debuhiretsukan's words are not soothing to Gohei Nakazura. 'You're all traitors! If you think that I will stand back and allow this man to destroy our prefecture, then you have another thing coming. I plan to go before the council with this information.' A loud sigh is heard. Gohei turns and looks at Maverick who stands now, his arms behind his back, his hands clasped together. -- Ah, the governing body. It's a great institution, yes? In every shape and form of life in this world, there is some type of government body. Of course, there are the countries and their governments. Then there are companies and their board of CEO's. There's the educational system, complete with administration and 'higher-ups.' The military, complete with ten rusted old generals who last experienced foreign diplomacy, Hitler style. Fear. Fear controls every aspect of life. National governments; why do we have them? Fear of anarchy. Companies with their boards; why do we have them? Fear of failure. The Educational system and their administration; why do we have them? Fear of the individualization of education. The American Family; the American nuclear family. Even that has a governing body of some kind. Ah, good old mom and dad. What would they say now, huh? Maverick Reno's father was a stern man, full of wisdom, strict sterility, and impatience. His mother was your average house wife; ah, growing up in Canada in that decade. But what of today's youth? What of the American Youth? Would their parents be just the same? Would they be proud to call their son, 'Son?' or would they simply sigh and turn their backs, shaming them even more for stepping off the well-marked path. Ah, isn't it grand? The birds are singing, the sun is shining, and somewhere, some time, there's a mother crying because she never sees her son... her daughter. There's a father who sighs and glances at a photo, wondering, wishing; 'What if?' No. There are no, 'What ifs' in parenthood... there's only disgrace and shame. It comes in every youth's life; some more than others. The youth eventually realizes what they've thrown away however and they strive, fight, and scamper to retain some of that respect and love that they once felt as a child. The bust their asses just to be able to walk into the house and see that same expression that mom gave when they brought home their first 'A' on that math test. It never comes. It never will. That's life. That's the American way. The youth simply don't understand that no matter how hard they try, no matter what they become, it is not enough. It's not what 'daddy' or 'mommy' wanted. It's not good enough. You're simply not good enough. Only when you stop caring what 'mommy' and 'daddy' think do you become a man... or woman. Only then can you call yourself a champion. -- 'I told our noble friends that you may wish to walk upon that desolate path... they insured me that upon hearing of our arrangement, you'd come to terms. It would seem that you have chosen... a darker path.' Reno speaks, each passing word deeper than the last. Gohei glances around, knowing that he is fully outnumbered. A shot. Who fired this shot? Ah, good question. Do I care? Not one damned bit; I do know that blood splattered onto my face though. I smiled; like crimson rain falling from above, the gun flash, a flash of lightning in the sky. The sound; the crack of thunder above. The smoke, the clouds turning, being consumed by hell's brimstone. Gohei staggers back now as uzis are lifted all around the room. Nakazura drops to one knee, grabbing his shoulder where the bullet remains and biting down on his bottom lip, the pain pulsating through his entire upper body. Shots ring out from all sides of the room; five men fall, not counting Gohei. Both members of the Nakazura who were guarding Gohei hit the ground, riddled with bullets compliments of the other Yakuza families. Three others hit the ground, the Nakazura guards having sprayed the room. Debuhiretsukan has been hit in the arm by a stray bullet... blood soaks the kimono. blood fills the cheeks. Anger and rage fill the face. Debuhiretsukan draws a magnum from his sash and aims it at Gohei, his hand shaking with anger and pain. The hammer draws back and a round is pulled into the chamber. Reno's hand is placed atop the gun before pushing down, lowering it. Debuhiretsukan looks up at Maverick and nods. The guards from his lineage help him up and escort him from the room quickly; the other guards follow their respective Routaika. Maverick's cohort follows the group calmly, leaving Maverick alone with Gohei. Gohei Nakazura is kneeling in the center of the room, his face showing the pain surging through his body. Maverick sighs and walks slowly about the room, his eyes following the decorations and feng shui. 'You did a great job, Gohei.' Nakazura glances over at Maverick. 'You have two fine nephews. You tried to bring the entire Yakuza down on my back. Your family put up a fight for the most part... we were pleased with the challenge.' Reno continues, pacing slowly about the room until he's behind Gohei. He continues walking, talking. 'But to every war, there comes an end. To every battle, there is a victor. To this one, my dear... uncle... Gohei... I am the victor.' Gohei sinks down, sitting on his lower legs, his hand pressing against his shoulder now. Gohei breathes heavily, his kimono slowly dying, pigmenting itself red with the steady stream of blood. Reno reaches inside of his trench coat and pulls a single glock out and puts it on single shot. He stops in front of Gohei and looks at him, his eyes calm and tranquil... Gohei's finding serenity. 'It'll be quick, Gohei. One shot... you'll never feel a thing.' Reno presses the barrel of his gun against Gohei's forehead forcefully. With his other hand, Reno pulls a pair of black roses from his coat and offers them to Gohei. Slowly, Gohei extends his hand and takes the roses, pressing them against his chest. Maverick smiles and nods. 'I wish I could promise you that their deaths would be just as quick... but alas, there's I will savor.' Words are more powerful than any sword. What could be worse than killing a man? Telling a man that his only living relatives, his nephews who he loves dearly, are going to die in a cruel fashion just moments before you pull the trigger. ... yes, I'd say that's worse. It wasn't really a moment where I decided to pull the trigger; I just remember looking down at Gohei and speaking of memories and examples. The next, I remembered the splatter of blood onto the wall and floor behind him. Blood coated my glove and the barrel of my glock; skull fragments shattered, the sickening crack as the hallow tip blew past his frontal lobe's protective cap. After the shot... there was silence. I had not moved although, with the pull of a single finger, I had ended another life. Gohei Nakazura lay on the floor in suite 4 of Ouji no Ryoji in Mt. Kidake, Japan. Gohei lay near the center, the shattered remains of his skull and brain matter stained the carpet for a good ten feet until they reached the wall. Blood had splattered onto the wall as well, the hallow tip doing its job. Gohei Nakazura was dead, slain by the Assamite covered sickle in my right hand. In the adjoining room, Yukio Yamajin was making a call to Ichiro Nakazura. All would be known soon... but we wouldn't wait around for Ichiro and Jushin to arrive. No, this sight they must enjoy first. There time will come. For death comes for all; for them, it waits... laughing. smirking giggling it waits. I wait. I'm coming for you I had cleaned my face off and made the final exchange with Yamajin; a few billion for their help and for future services. A few more connections would hurt no one... important. The rain now comes down heavily over the city of Mt. Kidake, the streets flooding with beautiful transparent puddles. The many patrons of the Kotai Market Place had since scattered, giving way to the merciless downpour. The sky above had grown even darker, as black as night. Maverick lifts the umbrella and opens it above his head before stepping out onto the street. Slowly, he walks down the walkways towards the way from which he came, water splattering from atop the black umbrella like the blood from his face. The storm had come... Its aftermath would be the part to worry about.
