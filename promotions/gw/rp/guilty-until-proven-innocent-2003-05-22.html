Guilty until proven Innocent

22 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/22/03 It has been almost two months since the Global Wrestling community last saw or heard from Sean Price, but the mark that he left on the company is still very much alive. As a member of the Mile High Club, Sean Price was one of the most feared men in the business. As a singles wrestler Sean piled together a record that is almost unmatched in professional sports. The former All American was in the company for less than 6 months, and in that time he held three titles, two of which he held until the day he was forced to leave the company due to family issues. But now, not more than two months later, Sean Price has returned to take care of some unfinished business. As the scene fades in we open in a cold and dark office. We see a brown desk in the center of the room with the name tag “il Capo” neatly positioned in the center of the desk. Behind the desk is a black leather chair facing the wall, we can see that a man is sitting in that chair but we can not make out who it is, as the top of his head is the only thing visible from the current angle. Suddenly the door swings open and in walks Sean Price, all decked out in a black suit. Price walks over to the desk and looks directly at the chair for a moment, waiting for the man on the other side to speak. “Take a seat Sean.” Says the man who’s face is kept a secret. “You know Sean, it was not more than a month ago that I contacted you about becoming a member of my organization. At first you were a little skeptical, but as time went on you saw the potential that this organization had for you, and the potential you had for this organization. And now here we are. You sitting in my office a day after you were called out on national television by some lone ranger. Now this lone ranger claims that you are responsible for the current state that GW Owner Bubba McCoy is in.” The man is suddenly interrupted by an angry Sean Price. “But boss you know I had nothing to do with Bubba’s health situation. Sure I hate the ugly bastard but what would I poison him for when I could just kick his ass in the ring like I have done time and time again?” Replies Price “Don’t get me wrong Sean, I know you are innocent. In fact I know how you can prove your innocence. You just need to trust me.” “I think by now if I don’t trust you then we are in a serious problem. But go a head, let’s hear what you have to say.” “Before we discuss this Sean, go and make sure the office door is shut.” With that Mr.Price gets up and walks over to the door and shuts it gently, the camera suddenly fades to black leaving the discussion in the office and not allowing anything to be heard from outside of the office. ****Two Hours Later**** The door to the office opens and Sean Price exits with a stern look on his face. Sean slowly makes his way out of the building and towards his car. A man gets out of the passenger side of the black BMW and opens the door for Price. Price gets in and then two body guards get in after him. The body guard from before shuts the door and then walks around the car and gets into the passenger side again. The car starts up and then takes off out of the lot and onto the highway. The scene reopens inside the car where we see Sean Price sitting there with a drink in his hand, he takes a sip and then returns the drink to its resting place atop the napkin that is placed on his knee. Suddenly his cellular phone begins ringing. The body guard sitting across from Sean picks it up, flips it open and begins talking on it for a moment until handing it to Price. “It’s for you boss.” “Thanks.” replies Sean with a smirk across his face. “Hello Sean, Sean are you there?” replies the man on the cell phone. “Yeah I’m here. Who is it?” “It’s your lawyer, Preston. Preston Banks.” “Is there something I can help you with Mr.Banks?” “Well there is something that I need to discuss with you. You see you are scheduled to face Ely Braxton next week at GW’s PPV. However you are not under contract and will be getting paid no money for that match or anything else while in the GW.” “Thanks Preston, how very astute of you to notice that. I guess that’s what il Capo is paying you all that money for”. Price pauses for a moment to think, he takes out a piece of paper from his pocket and scans it over for a second. He hands the piece of paper to one of his body gaurds and orders him to hand that piece of paper to the driver. “Driver that is where I want you to take me.” “Yes sir Mr.Price.” replies the driver. “Ok Preston, here is what I want you to do. I want you go to GW Headquarters, talk to Salaok or talk to Logan Creed whichever one you can get ahold of. And tell them that unless I get a contract within the next week, I will not be fighting at the PPV.” “Yes sir, right away Mr. Price.” “Good man Preston.” Sean hangs up the phone and puts it back into his pocket. The camera fades out as Mr. Price sits back and closes his eyes in an attempt to get some sleep before arriving at his destination.
