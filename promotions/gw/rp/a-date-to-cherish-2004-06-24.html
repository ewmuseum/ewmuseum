A Date To Cherish

24 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/24/04 I place a napkin on my lap, I don’t want to stain the suit that Soma paid for me. He said it was “top of the line”, so I definitely don’t want to spill anything on it. I love this suit. He had it custom made to fit me. I guess guys at my age don’t have my body. Soma told me not to worry about comfort, because it’ll be the most comfortable thing I’ve ever wore. He wasn’t wrong. The pant legs dangle from my knee caps and the sleeves on the coat hang from my shoulders down. This is definitely the most comfortable things I’ve ever put on. I look into her eyes. They seem to sparkle, like the diamonds on the belt that Soma bought to match a pair of jeans he bought me. He told me that looks have a lot to do with business. That, you could be one of the worst business men in the whole entire world, but if you have on the right clothing, that can change in an instant. Soma is opening my eyes to many things around me that I’ve never seen. When I was alive, the first time, I had a little bit of money. But there was no way I intended on buying fancy outfits or anything of that nature. I spent it on more important things, like stereo systems or heated blankets for my king sized bed. You know, the things you see in the mall when you are walking through and say to yourself that the cost of that Playstation 2 game or that compact disc player is way too expensive. Those are the things that I’d just shrug my shoulders and end up putting on my card. I figure the more expensive the item, the better the quality. When you make a salary like I did, you don’t worry about anything at all. Bills, are just bills. You hire somebody to pay them for you. I didn’t even pay attention to what I was paying. I just let somebody handle it for me. When you have enough money, you get lazy. But when you are wrestling guys like Chris Camero and Jay Robson, every single second you get to relax, you do so. You kick up the chair and take a nap. Or you put on your favorite CD and just zone out. When you give it everything you have almost every night of the week, nobody even tries to call you lazy. But you are. And no matter what, you hate to admit it. She is wearing a red dress. Not a dark red, but more of a rose red. The straps on her shoulders are skinny. She looks incredible. I can hardly take it? Her hair is tied back and her black framed eyeglasses sit on her nose. I try to think of something to say, but the words just won’t come out. I begin to wonder how she got here to New York. So’I ask her? “So umm’how did you get to New York?” I ask over the table while she is sipping her red champagne. It’s a little darker then her dress. She sets it down in front of her. “Master Soma told me to drive to the airport, that it was important and catch the next flight to New York. He said that I wouldn’t be disappointed. So, I caught the next flight yesterday and well’here I am.” Such a remarkable story. Remind me to buy Leinard Soma another thank you card. “So, he pretty much had you fly down here just so we can still have our date?” “Yeah. He did that.” Remind me to put $10 in his thank you card. “Wow, I can’t believe it. I was really kicking myself in the head when I thought we were going to miss it.” “Yeah, I was feeling a little sad when I found out that you and Master Soma flew to New York for the week.” “But now, you’re here, so there is nothing to worry about. How long did Soma say you are in for?” “I’m supposed to go with you to Crossfire. He told me that he can arrange things with Bubba McCoy so that I can be backstage in the Global Wrestling locker room.” “Yeah, Bubba owes us a couple of favors for all that we’ve done for him. We are going to be fighting a handicapped match against four men at Crossfire.” “Pardon me, but, I have never watched wrestling. Are you really good?” Now is my chance to shine. “Well, I’m a former GW Heavyweight, International, Cruiserweight, and Platinum Champion. Actually, I’ve won the last two twice. I’m known as “The Harvester of Tears”, which is an elite group of members from The Lair. I’m a legend in the Global Wrestling.” “So you and Master Soma work together then?” “Now we do. He used to be my worst enemy. He’s done a lot of bad things to me.” “Master Soma did” Like what?” I don’t want to tell her that Soma killed me. It would probably change every single thing that she thinks about him. “You know, the one time I let him borrow one of my DVDs and he returned it, and it was scratched and everything. I couldn’t even play it.” “Ohh.” She looks around, confused like I’m some kind of idiot. I put my head down, feeling like an idiot. “So, you probably heard a lot about me. I actually haven’t heard a lot about you’other then how you started working for Soma. So, why don’t you tell me some things about you?” “Well?” She rolls her eyes and smiles? Like an angel. “Now I’m going to school.” “Really” That’s fantastic. What are you going for?” “I want to be a lawyer, so I can put a stop to a lot of the criminals out there. I heard a lot about the evils of the world from Master Soma. He kind of made up my mind after awhile. So I figured, since he helped me with college and helped me get off the streets, the least I can do is help him in some way.” “That’s very honorable. So, how much do you have left?” “Oh, this is only my third semester. I live at Master Soma’s mansion, I work during the days but if I have a class, Master Soma lets me take off. The work isn’t that hard though, just keeps me busy I suppose.” “I see.” “Here is your food, sir.” I look up at the waiter as he places a plate full of Chicken Parmesan in front of me. And he puts a plate of spaghetti in front of Cherish. They look scrumptious. My Chicken Parmesan is covered in a thick, rich blanket of marinara sauce with a helping of buttered noodles on the side. Cherish’s spaghetti has chunks of sausage and meatballs in the mix. Cherish picks up her fork and digs in’I hate to interrupt her. “I’m sorry, but umm’I don’t know if you are, well, umm’I’d like to say a prayer before we eat.” “Of course.” She sets her fork down’I close my eyes. “Lord, we thank you today for having the chance to eat at this great restaurant. I thank you for Cherish’s safe travels to New York. I thank you for Leinard Soma and all of his good deeds he’s done. I ask you to nourish our bodies with this remarkable food. In Jesus name I pray?” “Amen.” She finished my prayer. She is perfect. She is flawless. She is? “I’m starving.” “she is starving. She picks up the fork and twirls it in her spaghetti and takes a bite. I cut the chicken on my plate and take a bite” A LITTLE LESS THEN AN HOUR LATER I scrape up the last bit of marinara sauce and finish my dish, pretty much around the same time Cherish does. I pick the napkin up off of my lap and wipe my mouth off. I already know without looking that I had none on there, but Soma told me to make sure I’m clean and tidy. “That was really good.” “Yeah, it was. Thank you for bringing me. It really means a lot.” I stand up and stand beside Cherish. I hold my hand out to help her to her feet. She grabs it with her fingers and stands up. I push her chair underneath the table for her. “I’m such a gentleman. “Thank you.” I guide her to the door, but turn around and throw $100 on the table, by my own calculations, that should cover it. Soma gave me cash tonight instead of his card. Works for me. I meet up with Cherish by the door. I grab her coat from the coat rack and I put it over her shoulders and just stand there, staring into her eyes. I have to almost smack myself in the face to stop. I put my arm around her shoulders and open the door for her. We step out onto the curb and hop into the waiting limousine that Soma had for us. I let her go in first, because I’m such a gentleman. “Thank you for joining me tonight. I had a lot of fun.” “I had a lot of fun too.” My cell phone rings’great, perfect timing. “I’m sorry.” “That’s fine.” I take out my cell phone from my coat pocket. I turn a little to the side and answer it? “Sabbot.” “Tell her that you rented Ghost.” “What?” “The movie Ghost, with Patrick Swayze and Demi Moore. You know, the old classic. It’s her favorite, tell her you rented it.” “But I didn’t.” “I told you that I’d take care of you Sabbot.” “Alright, thanks.” “No problem.” The other line disconnects. “So’what plans do we have for the rest of the night?” “Well, don’t laugh if I tell you, okay?” “Of course not.” She giggles. The little school girl giggle that sends a chill down your spine. “I rented the movie Ghost. You know, the one with Patrick Swayze and Demi Moore.” “Are you kidding” That’s my favorite movie?” “No way, it has always been a guilty pleasure for me.” “Yeah, me too. I love that movie. What’s your favorite part?” “Well”.you know, probably the uhh, well’its so hard to say. Everything in the movie is so good.” She giggles again? Remind me to buy him a Hallmark card. I need the most expensive one. The more expensive, the better the quality. “I definitely agree. But my favorite is when Demi Moore is making a pot and Patrick asks if he could help. He ruins it and then’well, you know what happens.” “Of course I do.” I swallow hard, hopefully I don’t reveal that I have never even seen that movie before. We arrive at the hotel. I open the door and hop out. I stand face to face with the limo driver. I keep forgetting that he is supposed to open the door for me and everything. Oh well, I brush him off with a smile. I walk to the other side of the limo, a long walk considering it’s the biggest limo that I’ve ever seen. Sometimes I take Leinard Soma’s money for granted. I open the door on Cherish’s side and grab her hand as I help her out of the limo. I’m such a gentleman. 12 MINUTES LATER I open the door of the hotel room. I let Cherish walk in first. She takes the coat off of her shoulders and I grab it from her and I hang it up in the closet right by the door. I look inside the room and its lined with lit candles. The perfect romantic scenery. I know without asking that this room wasn’t like this before I left. Soma has to be up to something. Remind me to buy a red bow to put on the front of his thank you card. “Wow, I can’t believe you went through all this trouble. You went out and rented Ghost, you had an amazing dinner planned for us and this. This is just too much.” “Lets just say that it’s a special night for a special woman.” I grab a hold of her hands and stare into the stars in her eyes. I swear, I could do this for hours upon hours upon hours. “You’re so sweet.” She leans her neck over and kisses me on the cheek. I smile, the biggest smile I’ve ever smiled. “I don’t know about you, but I’m in the mood for” A million things run through my mind right now? “Some Patrick Swayze and Demi Moore. “So am I.” She laughs, I let go of her hands. I’ve rather die. I walk over to the DVD player and pop in Ghost. The movie begins to start. I cough in my hand, slightly. Enough to dim the lights with a little bit of magic. I look over and Cherish is lying on the bed. I don’t know if I shoulder lie next to her or what, so I sit in the chair beside the bed. “I don’t mean to interrupt you or anything, but can you lie down by me” It’s not comfortable on such a big bed by myself.” “Umm’sure.” I’m glad she’s smooth enough to make up my lack there of. I lie beside her as the movie starts, she crawls up to me and rests her head on my chest and wraps her arm around me. She throws one leg over mine. She watches the big screen television. “this is the greatest date ever. Remind me to do something better for Soma then get him a thank you card.
