----(My Death To My Resurrection)---- Match RP

2 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-2-05 My Match RP... Chapter 1: ----(My Death To My Resurrection)---- My first chapter in many of my so called life, and my mistakes may vary like my life does each and everyday it passes. I often sit and ponder what if and what if not. Really though is that possible to change anything at all? We all are outsiders to ourselves as we see it, we are shells of the new fabs and the new everything's that pass in months. I as a child often engaged into this irritating activities just to be shunned by my peers because of jealously and greed. My life is often shameful do to my acts of violence towards others as this, I am a wrestler. I am apart of a barbaric form of ending my problems and causing others pain like my own. I have a lot of pain, and It often buries me into my own self endured hell that I can't fight out of. Often I give into it's pleasures and get burned by it's evil deeds it seduces me into. I often ponder the idea of what if I wasn't this awful person that everyone has etched in their minds of me? What 'IF' doesn't cut it does it? I often ask questions yet nobody ever answers them as if I am not here at all really, as if I am this whisper in your ear just telling you all the secrets you should have never known. Awful Me...... I often look in the mirror and my reflection is none that I know, what I see is madness and all that I want to know of this human being is when he will stop, but then I remember....it's me. Awful Me....... When is it a good time to plead my case, and then have you look away as you have so many times I have asked for your attention but no...I am not worthy of your time or your credits to my name? I am nothing more then your whipping boy and your toy. Don't believe my hype, or actions believe what you will, because your will is what is going to be broken. Awful Me Deal With The Passion Of My Agony. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ I often ponder about life. What is life? Is there really a lil thing called destiny or is It just rubbish to get me to go to church. I haven't went to church since I was a little boy, A child. A child who had dreams, I was thinking as a child I'd become a football player. I dreamed of becoming a doctor, sometimes even dreamed of being Hulk Hogan. Those where just dreams though, and dreams never come true. Life is just full of misery and my misery is the Most hateful of all. I often am told that everybody will have there day to die, but really My day has come, but I am still here. I have lost so much, and gained very little. I have become a stone with no feelings, and if feelings I do have...I don't see them. I take a look back in my past when I was nine years old. The day was like no other it was supposed to be My 9th birthday. The day was sunny, it was the last day I remember ever watching the sun set with peace in my heart and my soul. I had very little to no family, my father was a lawyer. He often would do cases against mobsters, but I was never afraid of him doing so...I never gave it a thought. My mother was a stay at home mother, she was full of life and beauty. I loved them both with all my heart or what I had left of a heart. We decided to just go out in the country my father seemed a bit concerned about leaving the house, he seemed like he didn't want to. My mother made him, she told him it was my birthday and every boy should have a birthday. We drove out into the country with me being nine years old I had no idea how the world worked. After eating dinner we opened gifts, they got me a picture frame of all of us together, it wasn't the greatest gift in the world. 'Wow, a picture of all of us together! Thanks Mom, thanks dad! I love you!' I said. 'No problem son, happy birthday.' My father said with a twinkle in his eyes. I at the time didn't think much of the picture, what I really wanted was a bike a nice new bike. My old one was very rusted and trashed. Before I could give another thought my father pushed me out of site of the road, a car came zooming by and all I heard was gun fire all I saw was bullets, all I saw was death. My mother had a dozen bullets in her, she wasn't full Of life any longer...she was dead. She laid in a puddle of blood My father I could hear With my tiny ears grasping for air. I crawled over to him, he looked me in the eyes he was Crying as I was. I could barely get the words out of my mouth, all I could do was cry all I could do was watch. He with whatever power he had left in him....he touched my face and mouthed he loved me. He couldn't get the words out, but as he said it he stopped moving and my world/life was over. The car stopped, as I heard the doors slam. 'He's dead Joey, no worries about you's going to jail no more.' Says the man 'Find the kid, we can't have any survivors...heh.' -Says Joey. I heard my name, I saw the shoes I ducked under the car and then when they came on my side again I darted off again to the woods grasping the picture of my family with the strength I had left.. They never saw me.....I never saw me. Till then till now I been walking the streets trying to put my life back together. My fault line is here in this city. I lost my life at nine years old, I died that day. I told myself that they would pay for there sins, the wrongs would be put to right. I dawned the paint that covers my face, making me a monster in every way. My halo was forever gone, and my sins forever forgotten as now I was stealing to get by, and killing to survive. I threw away hope and faith, because really if God did care about me in any way, he would have saved my parents and not taken away the only two people I loved in my life, but he did. I hate him...I hate you all. After that day, I hid...I hid from the police because after my parents where killed they stuck me into a foster home, the father beat me. I often was beaten because I didn't do everything right, I learned to read out of focus and determination. After running away from my foster parents I roamed the streets to get away. I saw things no kid should ever see, the life styles of those who are reckless. I saw gangs kill gangs, and homeless stealing to get by. The system did me no justice, as nothing ever did. At the age of 18 I dawned the paint that made me a monster, I lived my own life finally....I had resurrected from the dead. I stole my attire and decided that hell on earth had came...it was judgment day for my soul, as it was for the people who killed my family! I walked into the big warehouse where these men worked, it took me weeks to find information on the street about these men but when it finally came time to strike I was the first to do it. I sat and perched in the rafters of the building, my hair long, my muscles bulging. My life was meant to be at this point I was going to have my revenge at last. Joey walked out in the open, it was just him and I. 'What was that?' Joey comments about the noise in the rafters. 'It's your watcher from the heavens, ready to judge you for your sins..' I replied. 'Wha...who goes there's?!' In his Italian accent he asks. I studied his exits and his weapon choices before I made my move towards him, I had been dreaming of this day as it seemed like forever, my justice was about to be done. 'Your Dark Angel Mr. Big, your Dark Angel...here with a message...' I said as I moved closer from attacking him. I could jump very easy and not hurt myself from this high in the air, as you could finally hear rain fall in the background. God must of known that my justice was near and wanted the tone to be set, as my intentions where clear, death was coming for this man. He started to get panicked as he looked around for me in the rafters, I made it a bit easier for him, as I floated down from the rafters like a fallen angel. AS I landed bending my legs to take the fall without hurting myself, I looked up at him as this look of fear took over his eyes, he had no idea what to make of me. 'Who...who are you?' He asked with a panic tone. 'Your judge, jury, and executioner.' I said with an angered tone to my little twist of my black pipe in my hand. I stood up completely holding my black pipe out showing him that I meant what I said, as he looks around for an escape but I was in the way of his only exit. He went to charge at me as I tripped him to the ground, and pointed my pipe at him as if it was a sword to his neck. I pushed my pipe into his neck choking him as he kicked away at me to get away, as I let go knowing how easy it was to finish this man off. 'Let me remind you of who I am, my name is Rafe Corvin...you killed my family...you killed me...now I killed you!' I said even more angary then ever. I had this man, as he was scared out of his wits, I had no idea this is how retribution felt like. He came at me again trying to hit me as I moved with every blow he threw at me, as if I was apart of the wind he could not connect with. I knee'ed him into his ribs as he backed off and grabbed his ribs, I walked towards him with nothing but an ice cold stare into his eyes, as everything I saw was his fear and his life flashing before my eyes much like mine did so long ago... 'AH, please! Please stop! I give up!!!' He cried. I struck him with my pipe as he laid limp on the floor, he was out maybe dead I didn't care. I felt more numb then before, killing this man didn't do anything for my pain...maybe it was because I didn't kill him that made me feel more numb. I called the police as I left the scene of the crime, my crime. I sat up on the roof top next to the warehouse watching the police bring out Joey Mussolini to his new home a jail cell. As i read in news reports later, all he said for the next two years after going to jail was that an Arch Angel has visited him with intentions of killing him. Why didn't I kill him? Because now he will live his life in pain and torment. He will go crazy much like myself, he will sit for the rest of his life wondering when I might return...to finish what I started... This Has Been My First Chapter to my story, this is my First installment of my own sick twisted reality...deal the deck...
