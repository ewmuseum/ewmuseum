The Struggle Within (Part 2)

Date: Today Weakness. That’s why I’m here. Now don’t get me wrong, it is not because of my own weakness that I am here, it is because of his weakness. His inability to do what his mind knows he must do is what has brought me here. His poor mindset and his desperation created me. I was brought here because of everything that is wrong with him, and I am here to be everything that is right. I am not a monster. I am not a demon. I am no plague or virus. In fact I am the exact opposite. I am a vaccine. I am not the problem, I am the answer. I am not the enemy I am the savior. I am here to do what no one else will do, I am here to do what I want to do, and I am here to do what I need to do. Fushichou no Nazo paced around the small apartment complex. The place was a wreck and Nazo himself did not look so well. His body was slumped over as he walked, and his hair was matted and clinging all over his face and neck. He did not seem well, almost as much of a wreck as the room. The room truly did look like a hurricane had roared through it. Almost as if Ivan himself had stormed through this room only. Papers were scattered across the floor, glass was shattered all over the place, the television set was knocked over, not to mention a pile of clothes scattered everywhere and the many other cleanliness nightmares that were scattered around. Nazo did not seem to mind the mess, he didn’t seem to mind anything. He continually paced around the apartment. His body was slumped over, his hair as mentioned a complete mess, he was not at all the proud man like he had seemed on Evolution. He finally began to stop pacing, but as he did so his breathing began to increase, in both volume and intensity. His strange behavior did not stop there however. He began to speak out loud, to an audience not there to hear his words. “You think you can control me, but you can’t,” he screamed. “I am in control now! There is nothing you can do!” He slumped down to the ground. He rested his back against the wall, and buried his head into his hands. He seemed to be in a lot of pain, but it was strange there was nothing around that could be hurting him, so it seemed. He began to pull hard on his hair, he was grunting slightly now, there was definitely something upsetting Nazo, but the source of his discomfort was unnoticeable. Suddenly, his breathing began to slow. His body began to regain its normal proud and tall stature. He began to calm down a bit, releasing his head and hair from his hands, although some strands of his own hair did fall to the ground afterwards. He stood up, and began to brush himself off. His face was still covered with the shaggy hair that has been accustomed to this mysterious man. He never seemed to wear the Samurai style mask that he wore inside the Octagon outside. However, that did not allow a better look at his face. He was now standing, he seemed to be back to normal. However, once again he began to speak to an audience that was not quite visible. “I told you, I told you that you couldn’t do it. I’m in control, and now I’m going to prove it to you. I’m going to teach you a lesson.” Nazo walked out of the small living room space of the apartment. He stepped over gigantic mounds of paper, garbage and clothes as he did so. However, he merely ignored all of that. He walked directly towards the kitchen counter area. He reached across and rummaged through a cupboard. He hid his actions, but soon after a few seconds of searching he apparently found what he was looking for. He emerged from the cupboard with a large knife. He had a sadistic look on his face and did not look at all stable. He glanced around the room smiling as he walked back towards the wall. He sat down and slumped against the wall with a loud thud. The pain that he should have felt did not seem to bother him however, and with the sick look on his face, it looked like that would feel pleasant compared to what he had in mind for someone. He began to hum a tune to himself, and began to carve into the wooden floor at the same time. He seemed delighted as he defaced his own floor, from the motion of the handle of the blade it could not be made out what he was writing in the floor but he was writing a significant amount. “I told you, I told you a million times. You’re not in control and now you’re going to pay for it. You’re going to hurt because you didn’t listen to me. You’re going to bleed because of it. I’m sick of these constant fights, I’m sick of these constant struggles. I’m done with you, I’m going to prove to you that I am the dominant one.” Nazo began to laugh hysterically. He then seemed to be finished writing his message on the ground. He then raised the knife, placed it against the flesh of his own forearm. With one swift motion he slid the razor-sharp edge of the blade across his skin. An intense pain rushed through his entire body, and his adrenaline began rushing. However, he did not show the slightest sign of discomfort, and stayed silence. He began to shake slightly, maybe the pain was affecting him after all. He laughed slightly as the blood began to pour out of the open cut. The blood ran straight out as Nazo held his arm above the carving job he had just done, allowing the blood to run all over the cracks of the message. “It is done, now see what I have done. And relish the control I give you, for it is all that you will have for a long, long while.” His body began to shake violently now, seemingly not from pain, but from something else. He was spasming quite violently before his head reared back, and he seemed to fall backwards onto the ground. Finally, Nazo sat back up. However, he now wore a confused expression through his matted hair. His blood was all over the place, his chest, his arm, his pants, everywhere. He became aware of the wound once again, like it was something new to him. He examined the wound, and let out a squeal of pain as he touched it. He then noticed the knife on the ground beside him. “What the hell is this all about”! You bastard!!” The target of Nazo’s screaming was invisible and most likely non-existent, but that did not stop his anger. Finally, he took notice of the pool of blood on the ground. He took his hand and smeared most of the pooling blood out of the way. The blood stained his hands, but that was not on his mind right now. He looked down at the floor. He saw the engravings, some of the cracks filled with blood, others not. He began to read the message that he wrote out loud. He seemed like he had never seen it before. Like he had entirely forgotten that he had written the message only minutes ago. “Your struggle is futile. Your pain is endless. I am in control, and I will always be in control. I am here to do what you cannot, because you are too weak to do what your heart desires. Enter me, the solution to all of your problems. I’m here and I’m in control, so you better get used to it and get ready to live with it. Good night once again my friend” Nazo paused the second he finished saying that last word. However, his body began to shake once again, and this time instead of spasming like he was experiencing a seizure his body reacted differently. His muscles trembled slightly. His eyes began to flutter, but suddenly his entire body went limp. His vision went black, and he blacked out. His body hitting the floor with a loud thud. His body slumped over the message, the pooling blood covering the side of his body, and the hair on the right side of his head. It was a terrible sight to see, a man laying helplessly unconscious in a pool of his own blood. Blood, that was drawn by acts done by himself, acts of self harm. This man truly wasn’t stable, and there was something amuck. But, what could it be?
