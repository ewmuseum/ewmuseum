Later that day...

5 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/5 Later that day... After a state of TUE affairs meeting with Il Capo the black Porsche Carerra GT of Deacon Holmes can be heard roaring down the highway. For brief moments in time it can be seen as well, that is, if you tape it going by with a video camera and then watch the tape in slow motion. And with the proper equipment you may even be able to tell what type of car just zoomed past you at over two hundred miles per hour kicking up dust and debris in it’s wake and quaking the very ground beneath you as it passed. Inside the vehicle sits Deacon Holmes, who is giving the gearbox the workout of a lifetime while bobbing his head to “Ghettomusick” by Big Boi. Also inside the vehicle is John Zero, who sits next to him stoically in the passenger seat. Suddenly a puzzled look flashes across his face and he begins to reach for the volume control on the radio only to have his hand slapped down by a vigilant Deacon Holmes who must’ve seen the attempt from out of the corner of his eye. “Now what did I tell you about touchin” a black man’s radio!” You know that shit ain’t right John, you don’t see me moseyin? around snatches cowboys? hats and whatnot! You’d understand if you saw what happened to this one white dude I saw. He turned the channel on this guy’s radio and three guys jumped him, then they made him strip buck naked in the street and duct taped him to a street light. Shit was fucked up, it was rainin? and everything... he was hairy too, must’ve hurt when somebody ripped all that shit off him.” Deacon shouts over the music while continuously shifting his eyes back and forth between John and the road. “I’m serious man, and then this other time...” He continues only to be abruptly halted by Zero, who has turned down the radio anyway. “Now Deacon, before you regale me with anymore of your tales of black men and their radios, I’d like to backtrack to just a moment before you turned up the radio and cut off our conversation.” “Oh, my bad, I thought we were done. Were you going to say something?” Holmes inquires, turning the music down even lower as Zero’s mouth begins to open. “Well, as a matter of fact I was.” John states before clearing his throat. “Ok, better. Now as I was saying... “You stole a WHAT!”!”!”” Zero exclaims, uncharacteristically raising his voice. A sheepish look grows in the eyes of Deacon Holmes as he stammers a bit before spitting out “I... I... I couldn’t help it man! It was just sitting there, ripe for the takin” and shit man! I was uh... seasonin? tha day! Yeah! ...I think that’s what they call it.” Deacon shrugs as he tries to explain himself. John looks up through the transparent sun roof while shaking his head as if to say “why me” before releasing a sigh. “We’ve really got to work on controlling your impulses.” His tone becoming more exasperated by the second he continues, “So where is it?” “Well, that’s another thing Z.” Holmes begins, scratching his head nervously. “I stashed it in one of those warehouses you own. I mean... is that ok?” “Take me there.” John demands before asking “And what in the hell did you plan on doing with it anyway?” Zero continues with a confused look on his face while shaking his head in disbelief, causing Deacon to look around nervously before answering “I don’t know, take it on joy rides and stuff. Guess I never really thought it through.” “Impulse control Deacon, impulse control.” Zero says dryly while leaning back into his seat as Holmes hangs a hard right off the exit ramp. After a few more sharp turns that cause the tires of the vehicles to all scream out in protest simultaneously before once again aiding in the effort to propel the car at rocket speeds. They pull up to the gated entrance of the warehouse and Zero punches in a few keys on the digital keypad causing the large metal gates to slowly pry themselves apart and allow the two men to enter. Once inside Holmes and Zero hop out of the car and enter the warehouse on foot. They step into the dark room and stand only a few feet away from the doorway, which is dimly lit by the inkling of sunlight peeking in from the open door as opposed to the rest of the room which is completely pitch black. John walks off into a dark corner in order to fish out a light switch. Suddenly the entire warehouse is illuminated and both men turn to find a gigantic green army tank sitting smack dab in the center of the room. A speechless John Zero’s eyes bulge out of their sockets in perfect timing with his dropping jaw before looking over at an esteemed Deacon Holmes who stands cross armed looking on with an ear to ear grin while nodding his head in appreciation.
