The Streets of Dallas: Pt. I

13 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 13, 2004 ** Time after time, the 'sinful' of this world are persecuted and punished for what others believe to be 'un-natural' or 'not right.' Yet, no one ever lets the thought of 'what if they deserved it?' cross their mind. No. The focus is never placed on why the action happened in the first place, but instead the focus is placed on 'how can we hurt this criminal in the same capacity as the deed they have committed?'** **Well the simple fact is that some people just deserve what ever comes their way. In many cases people bring trouble upon themselves, and then look for the easy way out when they get what was coming their way anyways. I am a deliverer of messages. Sometimes I have nothing but kind, wholesome messages to pass on, and other times my messages involve pain, and suffering, in order to make sure that they are fully received** - THE STREETS OF DOWNTOWN DALLAS - .:We are getting a shot of a dark, dreary alleyway. In this alley we see a dumpster on one side. The dumpster is overflowing with inhumaine garbage, and we can even see groupings of maggets crawling around in certain points, as well as steaming fluids leaking from the bottom, simmering in the humid Texas night:. .:As we peer further down the alley, we see a pile of cloths and blankets that appears to be moving, it might very well be a human being. We continue to stare at the lump leaning against the brick wall on the left side of the alley, when all of a sudden, a door swings open next to the pile of blankets:. CREEEEEEEEK! SMASH! .:After the rusty door swings open, we see that there is in fact a homeless person underneath the blankets and cloths. The scruffy-bearded homeless man pops his head up, and just as he does, a trench-coated, face-painted Takahashi Jetsui steps from the door way and into the alley. Takahashi takes a breath as he steps into the alley, and then looks in disgust at the homeless man on the ground:. HOMELESS MAN: Spare some change, pal? .:The homeless man says in a scratchy voice, as Takahashi retrieves a cigarette from an inside trench coat pocket and lights it with a silver zippo lighter:. TAKAHASHI: People like you make me sick. What? You want a couple bucks so you can get loaded? Why don't you get a job? How come you leech of others, and make your way from alley to alley, in search of ignorant fools to plead your case to? Huh? HOMELESS MAN: Hey, I don't want any tr- TAKAHAHSI: WHY IS IT that every where I go, whether it be northern Japan, a British suburb, or right here in the 'good ol'' U - S of A, there are always disgusting street peddlers looking for hand-outs? HOMELESS MAN: Hey, I just asked if you could spare anything. Anything at all. My family is hungry, my dog is dying, I - I have no legs, PLEASE HELP ME! .:The homeless man begins to cry at the feet of Takahashi, grabbing on Takahashi's trench-coat with his dirty hands, which are covered by gloves with holes cut in the fingers. Takahashi places the cigarette between his lips, and lifts the homeless man off the ground:. TAKAHASHI: You lied to me. HOMELESS MAN: Wh- what do you mean? TAKAHASHI (Removing the cigarette from his mouth): You told me just now, that you had no legs. I look down, and I see a det right there. They are philthy, they are disgusting, but there they are, you legs. HOMELESS MAN: I'm sorry, look, just leave me alone, I HAVE POWERS! Woosh! Habbabla! Eek-On! .:As the homeless man begins to show signs that he may be a schizophrenic, Takahashi takes a puff of his cigarette, and with a billow of smoke pouring over his lips, he throws the cigarette to the ground:. TAKAHASHI: You're a crazy man now, are you? Well, I've always been a believer that less homeless schizophrenics would be better for the good of our society. .:Takahashi takes the man by his shirt, and throws the man very hard into the wall of the building on the other side of the alley:. THUMP! .:Takahashi then proceeds towards the homeless man, who seemed to have hit the brick wall very hard, as he is now shaking rather violently, and it appears as though his eyes might be rolling in his skull. Takahashi crouches down to the fallen homeless man's level:. TAKAHASHI: Look at what happens when you lie to me. Not only do lazy, worthless people like you already make me ill and nauseous, but then you lie right to my face. .:Takahashi begins fixing the homeless man's rags that are covering the man's upper body, almost in a sadistic fashion:. TAKAHASHI: And people are going to start realizing that you simply don't play games with Takahashi Jetsui. Now, have we learned our lesson, you philthy urchant? HOMELESS MAN: Gargle, grrrrl. TAKAHASHI: Now, doesn't it feel good to almost be out of you misery? I'll allow you to try to keep up your tongue because in the mean time I have some things I need to take care of. .:Takahashi pats the homeless man on the shoulder, and then turns to walk away as the homeless man struggles to catch a breath through a series of gurgles and gasps. Takahashi walks through a puddle in the middle of the alley way, and as he passes the disgusting heap of garbage in the aforementioned dumpster, is shoots the can a digusted look, as the steam from the piles visibly seeps into the air. Takahashi then exits the alley, and turns the corner into the thick Dallas, Texas night:.
