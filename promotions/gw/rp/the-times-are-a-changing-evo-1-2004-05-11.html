The Times are a changing Evo #1

11 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/11/04 Donny J McNasty arrives in the Drunken Leprechaun early in the morning after a long night at Evolution, he has to be tired, he had a big match and on top of that he had some civil court proceedings to deal with. There have been some other developments as well, Saturday night Donny was nowhere in Altoona or Dallas, He was in Cleveland Ohio, in fact he was at the WMW Pay Per View High Stakes, paying Noah Hanson a visit. What transpired, well it’s simple, he showed up and made his presence known, after Hanson retained his WMW Ace Superior Title, the lights went out and Donny knocked him out, took his title belt and ran off. Donny hasn’t said much to anyone about it really other than to Harv and Charles. Donny of course had other things on his mind? Last night he had two huge events about to transpire for Donny, each one could have had an impact on his career. First there was the big ten man Battle Royal match for a championship no one knows what it is in GW. Donny faired pretty well, he was the last one out. He was kinda pissed, but after having time to think about it, he relaxed. Sherwin fought one hell of a match and well in the eyes of Donny, he’s a pretty good guy. Donny understands the virtue of patience. It may seem like somewhat of a bad thing that Donny didn’t win the mystery title, but it was also a hidden blessing. As he was on his way home on the plane last night, he received a phone call from Christina McCoy. Donny was really curious as to what this could be about. Normally McCoy doesn’t talk to him it’s normally her assistant, then she told him the good news. Donny J McNasty takes on American Youth Josh for the World Championship next week on Evolution. Donny has earned a shot at GW’s most coveted title. Many a men cry tears, sweat and bleed to get that belt, and Donny is given an opportunity of a lifetime. This is his chance to shine like the sun. If that wasn’t enough, VCPPT lost their case against Donny and Judge Ed Kotch ruled they must now pay Donny 5.4 million dollars. Knowing Donny’s mind, one can only imagine how this money is going to be spent. Needless to say though, the worry is over for Donny when it comes to those midgets. Now he can set his sights on bigger and better things like winning the World Championship in GW. There is only draw back in his life right now is knowing that Noah Hanson has put a large bullseye on Donny’s back. That’s ok though, he’s ready for it and knows when push comes to shove, GW is the best federation out there today. You can just see it happening before your eyes, a war is about to erupt and Donny J McNasty is on the front lines? The times they are a changing?
