Rhetorical Lifestyle (ReLoad Hype)

2 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 2, 2006 My life, at the moment, was like one of those rhetorical questions people ask. They never make sense, or if they do, they are just dumb. Why even bother answering? Well, up until recently, I never bothered to pay attention to such questions, until I was faced with something like it, in my own life. Would you rather be living in a place where you have everything you could ever want, but be unhappy, or be happy living in a place where you've got nothing? I had to ask myself this question just recently. Why? Well, because at the age of 23, I finally managed a way to get out from underneath the proverbial rock that was holding me down... My parents. More specifically, I’m talking about my father. Once living in the 'comforts' of 'home' in Athens, Greece, I was basically a slave to my father. Pushing me to do everything he never had a chance to do in his lifetime. His steroid abuse got the best of him, and his one and only shot of becoming a competitor in the Olympics for Greece went down the drain. Thus, once I was born, it gave him a new goal in life. Live his dream through me. This was good for him, until I got old enough to realize what he was doing. Then I found something better, more fun, more entertaining. I found the sport of wrestling entertainment. You know the 'fake' stuff as some of those people call it. Sports entertainment is ultimately what has brought me to the United States. Going from a stable home, but wanting to leave, to living in a run down apartment, but being as happy as can be. So what if Primetime Central's GTT5 didn't go as well as I wanted it to. Losing in round 2 was nothing to be ashamed of for a rookie such as myself. I knew something would work out for me. Not only did I have my talents backing me up, but a major wrestling corporation sponsoring, training, and helping me out as well. It is only a matter of time now before my phone rings with the next opportunity for me. Only a matter of time. -------------------------------------- My cell phone rang. 'Hello?' I answered. The person on the other end responded. 'Bubba McCoy from Global, huh? It's funny; I swear I was just thinking about my next big opportunity coming my way.' I laughed. 'Can you read minds?' McCoy responded, and I laughed. 'No, I don't think misspelling words all the time constitutes mind reading. Sorry. But hey, if you are calling in hopes of me joining Global. I'd love to.' I said back as the conversation continued...
