Don't you remember anything ?

19 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/19/03 Angus swings open the stiff door and hops into the motor vehicle. He waves to his crowd and gives a cheeky grin, emphasising his bushy goatee. The motor starts, and he revs the car up. Another look at the crowd, and he accelerates into the distance. The crowd disperses as the vehicle is no longer in sight, except for one fan, one single man stands all alone. The camera zooms in to see the faded pair of blue jeans riding over the top of a pair of black Chuck Taylor Converse sneakers. A plain white T-shirt, covering a toned, muscular body. Long, shoulder length, blond hair half covering the man's face, though it is already know that the man is Ice. The signature look gives it all away. Ice stands there with a cocky smile across his face. Thoughts of Angus the Bull's words are still fresh in his mind, as Ice turns and heads over to his black Ford F-150. As Ice walks to his black Ford F-150 he begins to talk to himself. 'Silence is vital? What matters isn't what is said before the match, rather who comes out the victor after the match. You are superior to me in everyway? Jesus son, who in the hell has been feeding you all this garbage?' Ice stops as he reaches his vehicle, and turns around to face the camera. He gently leans backwards, placing his forearms on the hood of his black Ford F-150. Ice looks down at the cracked pavement, and then up at the bright blue sky. He gazes for what seems like forever at the slowly passing, white fluffy clouds overhead, before finally breaking the silence. 'Blurring and stirring the truth and the lies, so much that you don't know what's real and what's not. That is all you are doing Angus. I think you need to lay off those funny cigarettes. They are eating away, and killing any cells you have left in the peanut rolling around inside your head you call a brain. Because, silence may be a virtue, and it may be vital, in your opinion, but your lack of intelligence behind your words, has shown me just why you keep so silent. It's not because you would rather do your talking in the ring. It's not because you believe that actions speak louders than words. No, it's because everytime you open your mouth, you sound like a complete and utter moron. Which doesn't help you one single bit. I already knew you were a moron so this is nothing new to me, but you, waltzing in here, and trying to come off like someone important, then sounding the way you do, it really doesn't help you out at all. So buddy, pal, amigo, let me give you a little advice, until you can think of something just a little more intelligent, and something that will help you get that recognition you are in so dire need of, I would suggest that you just keep your mouth shut, and leave the trash talking, to those who can do it well, and back it up. Like myself for instance.' A cheesey, and cocky smile grows across Ice's face, but just as quickly as it appears, it fades away, and a stern, focused, and intesense demeanor engulfs it. 'Now, back to the important stuff. Who in the hell has been feeding you all that garbage? No I'm not talking about your horrible breath, or your atrocious smell Angus. I'm talking about the nonsense that is spewing from your mouth. No, no Angus, again I don't mean your breath, I mean your words. You are better than me in everyway? Yet you agreed with me and said I had no equal? Again I must suggest that you stray away from those funny cigarettes, they really are effecting your mind, well what is left of it after your 'mental problem'. Let me allow you to be in on a little secret Angus.' Ice leans forward and curls his index finger, signalling for the camera to come in closer. Cautiously the camera approaches Ice. Ice looks to his right, and then to his left, as if to make sure no one else can hear his little secret. Ice whispers at the camera. 'Angus, it's not a mental problem that is stopping you from becoming better.' Ice smiles and then yells at the top of his lungs. 'IT'S BECAUSE YOU ABSOLUTELY SUCK!' Ice leans back on the hood of his black Ford F-150, and begins to chuckle at himself. '...You see Angus, there is nothing wrong with you and your mental abilities. Well, nothing more wrong with your mental abilities. You just set yourself too high of a standard to reach. I mean, come on! You actually think that you have the slightest chance of defeating me?! Yeah, yeah, yeah, sure you could get lucky and score a pin on that nobody Christopher Canada, and be declared the winner of the match, but would you be that much of a coward, that much of a pathetic loser, to actually consider it a victory of me? I would surely hope not. But you see, that is if you should happen to get EXTEREMELY LUCKY! Now back to reality and what is going to occur. You are going to walk down the aisle to your girly music, and the fourteen year old girls are going to go crazy. Christopher Canada is going to walk down the aisle to his crappy music, and no one is going to even notice that he is there. Then my music will hit, the lights will go down, and I will make my entrance to the ring. The place will go crazy, and I will soak up every single minute of it. Then when I'm done listening to my millions, and millions of Icicles, I will take out Christopher Canada. Then I will turn my attention to you, and whoop your ass, like the red-headed step-child you are. Plain and simple. Even someone with your lack of intelligence can understand something that simple.' Ice looks down at his right hand with a confused look. With his left hand he scratches the top of his head, and with his right hand he then extends his index finger, his middle finger, his ring finger, and his pinky, while mouthing something to himself.. Ice then nods his head and looks back up at the camera. 'Yeah, I think that's everything.' Ices turns around and walks over to the driver side door. He opens it, and hops inside. The engine starts, and it's loud hum echoes through the camera's microphone. The black Ford F-150 begins to pull away, but suddenly stops and the driver side window rolls down. 'Ah yes, I knew I forgot something. Angus, you know me pretty well I would say. And I know you just as well, if not better. So let me remind you of something. All the talking is over. All the slapstick humor, it's done. Game time is upon us, and you, of all people should know what that means. If you don't remember, this Friday night on Overload, I will remind you, that when that music hits, and the lights go down, there is no turning back, and there is no escape. When you walk down that aisle and you step into that ring, everything you have ever known, it won't matter. It won't make one single bit of difference. Because once you step through those ropes, you are no longer in your element. You are no longer surrounded by the world you know, and the world which comforts you. This Friday night, I will remind you, that when you are in that ring with me, you are in MY WORLD, and in MY WORLD, no one has the tools to hang with me. In MY WORLD I HAVE NO EQUAL! If you haven't figured it out by now, this Friday night it will hit you like a ton of bricks, as the lights go down. The temperature in the arena will drop, and a cool, cold chill will hit your body. The hairs on the back of your neck, and on your arms will stand on end. The lights will turn back on, and you will be standing face to face with your fate. You will be standing face to face with me. And when you look deep into these cold, icy blue eyes, you will realize just what you have gotten yourself into. You will realize that fate has come to serve it's cold harsh punishment onto you, as I get the pleasure of putting you on ICE!' The black tinted window of Ice's black Ford F-150 rolls back up, and the vehicle speeds away. The camera watches until it can no longer be seen. The scene fades to commercial.
