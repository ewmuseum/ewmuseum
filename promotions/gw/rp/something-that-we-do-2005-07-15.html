Date: 7/15/2005 Something that we do I remember well the day we wed, I can see that picture in my head! I still believe the words we said Forever will ring true... June 16, 1967; 1923 hours Mexico City 'There's no empty table, Miss...' said the maitre'd. 'We'll let you know as soon as one becomes available. You can sit over there if you wish to wait.' 'How long?' the young woman asked anxiously. 'I'm due back at work in 45 minutes, and I'm absolutely famished. Please, is there any way I can place an order to go?' 'I'm sorry, Miss,' said the maitre'd in a voice that carried overtones of sympathy; but a clear indication that in a choice between helping her or keeping his job, he chose the latter. 'I will let you know soon as a table is free, that I--' 'Sir... bring her here. She may join me.' The maitre'd turned toward the occupant of the closest table. 'Are you certain, sir?' he asked. 'Time's wasting,' came the quick reply. 'The lady has to be back at work in 45 minutes. She may join me.' 'Very well, sir,' said the matire'd, bringing the young lady over to the table. 'You're too kind,' said the young woman in a voice that rang with gratitude. 'There should be more people like you.' She smiled. Love is certain, Love is kind, Love is yours and love is mine. But it isn't something that we find, It's something that we do... The young man gave a light chuckle. 'Heaven forbid... how could I compete against myself, when I know all my best secrets?' She giggled as the waiter came over to her. 'Please excuse me,' she said to her host as she gave the waiter an order for a Cuban sandwich with a bowl of ham and bean soup. Turning back to her host, she smiled pleasantly. 'I'm Lupe. Lupe Maria Juarez. You may call me Lupita if you like.' He smiled and shook hands warmly. 'Mateo Ramirez,' he said, introducing himself. I'm studying... the media entertainment business.' He didn't dare say he was studying to be a Lucha Libre wrestler... for his name and face to be known before he even officially broke into the business, was to doom himself to perpetual mediocrity. The Lucha Libre mask was as much a protection of anonymity for the beloved stars of the wrestling ring as it was a tradition of Mexico. 'I major in mathematics, and I put myself through school working as a secretary,' said Lupe. 'My employer is not unkind, but lack of punctuality is the worst affront to his sense of duty.' She laughed lightly as the waiter brought her soup. 'Then I am only glad I could save you from his displeasure,' said Mateo like a true gallant. 'A shame he does not know how to treat such a fine lady.' Lupe blushed lightly. 'Outside of that one trait, he treats me well enough. I am not so timid as to remain under his thumb if he were cold and callous.' 'As well such a beautiful young lady should not be,' said Mateo, taking a bite of his meal. As he turned his hand to put his fork back to his plate, a deep sparkle of purple flashed from a ring on his hand. 'Pretty ring,' said Lupe, smiling lightly. 'An amethyst?' 'Not a real one, but yes,' said Mateo. 'My birthstone. My birthday is February 11th.' Lupe dropped her spoon into her soup bowl, and blushed as she carefully picked it back up. The soup was thick enough as to not have spilled onto her clothing. 'Please pardon me,' she said sheepishly. 'Of course,' said Mateo. 'Did I surprise you?' 'Actually yes,' said Lupe. 'That's my birthday as well... February 11th... the year 1947.' Now Mateo did a double-take. 'No...' It's holding tight, It's letting go, It's flying high and laying low. It lets your strongest feelings show, And your weakness too... Lupe looked up at him and the expression on his face spread to hers. 'You... too?' 'Indeed yes,' Mateo smiled. '1947 as well.' The waiter brought Lupe's sandwich as she finished her soup and beamed at Mateo. 'As many people live in Mexico City... the odds of meeting someone born on your birthday... month, day, AND year...' 'You think it's a good omen?' Mateo said, still with the same charming, gallant smile that was setting Lupe's heart aflutter. Lupe lowered her head to half-hide her gentle smile. 'Perhaps...' her tone carried clear overtones of a subtle flirt. 'Please, I must do something to repay your kindness. I get paid today; I can settle the bill--' 'Out of the question,' said Mateo with a firm shake of his head. Just as quickly his tone turned gallant again as he reached for her hand. 'If you wish to repay me... join me for dinner tomorrow. The Casa D'Oro.' 'Casa D'O...ro?' Lupe's mouth gracefully dropped open, and she put a hand up to cover it. 'Can you afford the prices--' He chuckled lightly. 'They're not as high as some people are led to believe,' he said. 'Besides, to bring such a fine lady there is recompense enough in itself.' At the end of the meal, Lupe gave Mateo a business card with her address and phone number. 'I'll see you tomorrow,' she smiled. Mateo gently lifted her hand up to his face and kissed the back of it. 'Until then, fair lady,' he said with a small bow of his head.' July 14th, 2005; 1255 hours GW Commissary, Las Vegas It's a little and a lot to ask, An endless and a welcome task! Love isn't something that we have, It's something that we do. Teresa could recite the story by heart now, she'd heard and then re-told it more than enough times... and it always made her heart feel warm and fuzzy. The lingering smile on her lips betrayed the joy of recalling how her parents met, and how her father courted her mother like a true Don Juan. 'And yet, she has nothing but contempt for her mother now,' thought Noi as she turned a couple of knobs on the radio station's control board to make sure the signal was broadcasting clearly. 'Sad, that she and her mother don't even speak now... and yet she can recall her mother's courtship with such peace and pleasure.' Juan held Teresa's hand lightly; his thumb lightly sliding back and forth along the ridge of her hand. He was able to recite much of the tale himself with surprising accuracy; most likely having heard it from Teresa more than enough times... So why did they seem to treat each other so... professionally? We help to make each other all that we can be, Though we can find our strength and inspiration independently! The way we work together is what sets our love apart; So closely that we can't tell where I end and where you start... Exclusivity had its privileges... and it had its fringe benefits. Noi had been fortunate to strike the agreement with the Mexican Luchadores. There was just... something about these husband-and-wife warriors that caught her interest... the way they told their back-story piece by piece had piqued her curiosity. They'd been trained well by their mentor, Jos? Sanchez, on proper microphone work as well as flawless wrestling technique... but having conducted all the major interviews with La Herencia de Herederos had one side-effect that Juan and Teresa hadn’t counted on. Noi was observant. She watched, and she LISTENED. The biggest scoops came to those who spotted what others didn't... both in what they saw and what they heard. And Noi didn't care how staunchly conservative and traditional these two were in their lifestyles. Mexicans were nothing if not passionate and deep in their love. And she didn't see it in Juan and Teresa. At first it was just something she chalked up to the excitement of the next match, or their preoccupation with telling the latest bit of story... but over time it became more... and now it was a preoccupation with HER. Noi saw less passion, less depth of love, in the displays of affection between Juan and Teresa than most American couples give on their first date. Young men carrying condoms with them on visits to singles clubs notwithstanding, it was a maddening puzzlement on how a couple married for twelve years could be so much like sticks in how they held hands... how they held each other... how they embraced... how they kissed each other. A follow-up comment by Juan pulled Noi out of her train of thought. 'It's one of the most beautiful stories I know,' he said. 'If only more people in the Western Hemisphere could speak of such a truly romantic courtship... two people falling in true love... finding a soul mate in each other. Despite our falling out with Lupe, she was a lucky woman... very lucky. Perhaps that's why Teresa holds such animosity for her now... that in the end, Lupe forgot how truly lucky she was. She found it... and Teresa found it.' He leaned in to give her a small buss, just as Noi jotted down some notes, and Teresa giggled lightly. 'Yes, I did,' she said, returning the buss. It was beautifully timed. The exchange was done just as Noi's head was turned right to her notepad. But Noi's peripheral vision was keener than most. 'Did they just turn their cheeks to each other??' It gives me heart remembering how We started with a simple vow; There's so much to look back on now, And still it feels brand new. We're on a road that has no end And each day we begin again! Love's not just something that we're in, It's something that we do. Juan and Teresa had always kissed Yugoslav whenever Noi saw them kiss at all... but this was almost... formal. Perfunctory. And the timing wasn't lost on Noi, either. The fact that she'd spotted the little cheek-turns made her sure that they'd kissed just as she turned to her notepad, for a reason. 'What are they trying to hide?' 'In some ways, the story sounds fairly similar to how the two of you met... at the cantina,' said Noi, trying to get back to the subject at hand. 'In October of 1990.' They gave each other a long glance before turning back to Noi and nodding in unison. 'Yes... perhaps in some ways, it was,' said Juan, offering a small smile. 'History repeating itself, perhaps?' He turned to Teresa and winked lightly... but she lowered her head and carefully withdrew her hand from his. Her attempt to disguise it by using the hand to scratch her other arm was a very poor attempt at deception. We help to make each other all that we can be, Though we can find our strength and inspiration independently! The way we work together is what sets our love apart, So closely that we can't tell where I end and where you start...! 'Okay, enough,' Noi reminded herself. Time to get to the real meet of the radio segment. 'And now, tomorrow night, you have your chance to make the most important history of all repeat itself,' she said, smiling encouragingly. Their heads snapped toward her an instant, and their hands clasped firmly together again as the fire flashed in their deep brown eyes. 'Yes...' said Teresa. 'It took many years in the Mexican indy leagues and in their CMLL league... it took our share of dues here in the States... but it will all be worth it if we win.' Her eyes started to glisten with moisture. 'It will all be worth it...!' Juan rubbed his wife's hand lightly, and this time she responded with a gentle squeeze. 'Our most difficult fight... but for the greatest reward. For Teresa and I to be Tag-Team champions is the greatest honor this sport may bestow on us. Too many times, in Mexican Lucha Libre, I had to hold a Light-Heavyweight championship of some sort on my own... because my wife was not allowed to don a mask and join me in the ring. But now... she is at my side. Now, we fight for something that will be OURS, instead of just mine. Finally, we'll share the bounty... and it will be complete. Something we earned together. Something we will defend together. Something we will dedicate to the spirit of Mateo Ramirez... together.' Teresa lightly laid her head on Juan's shoulder as he spoke, and she sniffled briefly. 'It will be for you, Pap?,' she said softly. 'How do you feel about Brick and Train?' asked Noi. 'They're champions for a reason,' said Teresa. 'They won the tag-team gauntlet at High Octane. They were the best of all the tag-teams that night. We fought our hardest back then, but it was not to be. Juan and I weren't ready yet. But the time between then and now, I feel has made a difference. This time, I know we are ready. To be the best, we have to beat the best... and we are determined that tomorrow night at Evolution, the fans will say we were the best.' Teresa's voice became more passionate as she spoke... her words filled with conviction. The husband-and-wife Luchadores were a team with a mission... and they were so close to the pinnacle and success, they could smell its enticing aroma. It pulled them toward it; drew them with irresistible force... and why should they want to resist, for that matter? 'So you don't hold any animosity toward Ruckus, Inc?' inquired Noi. Teresa sounded surprised. 'Of course not,' she said. 'We hardly know them well enough to feel animosity. We will fight them like tigers, and we are determined to defeat them... but not out of any grudge. This all about one thing-- they have something we want. Something we hunger for. And that hunger will not be sated until we get it. Brick and Train are tough, tough men. But that makes the fruit of success all the sweeter. Far better to defeat a strong enemy than a weak one.' Love is wide, Love is long, Love is deep and Love is strong. Love is why I love this song... And I hope you love it too! 'And with that,' said Noi with a slight trace of regret, 'We're out of time for now. 'Folks, you've been listening to the weekly GW Radio segment, with special guests Juan and Teresa Ramirez, La Herencia de Herederos. Will history repeat itself again for the Ramirez family? Have the Heirs finally found their rightful Inheritance? Tune in tomorrow at Evolution.' Juan smiled at Noi as all three of them doffed their headsets. 'I like the analogy... indeed it fits. The Heirs have truly found their rightful Inheritance... it is what we are all about. And we will not rest until we claim it.' Juan and Teresa joined both their hands in a firm clasp... though it lasted only a few seconds. It was to draw strength and inspiration from each other as a team... nothing more. As they left the studio, Jos? Sanchez and Melina looked up and smiled at them. Jos? had been reading aloud to Melina, as part of her studies. The child was reading on a first-grade level now, and could speak a few basic English sentences with less difficulty. Melina beamed proudly as she demonstrated her progress by reading a short passage aloud from the book, and both Juan and Teresa applauded lightly as they took their turns giving their daughter a hug and a kiss. Noi's brow furrowed even as she offered a polite clap and smile for Melina. The show of respect for Juan and Teresa by congratulating their child was something she was able to produce reflexively as a matter of simple etiquette. But inwardly her mind was churning. 'Let's get some lunch,' suggested Teresa. She kneeled down and spoke to Melina inquisitively. '?Deseas alg’n almuerzo?' (*Would you like some lunch?) '?S?, Mamacita, por favor!' Melina responded, nodding eagerly. (*Yes, please, Mommy!) It took less than ten seconds to decide on where to go, and they started to head out of the studio. Noi took a moment to touch Jos? Sanchez lightly on the arm and lean in to him, indicating she had a private question for him. I remember well the day we wed, I can see that picture in my head. Love isn't just those words we said, It's something that we do... 'Are they always... so distant with each other?' she asked him in a very quiet voice. 'They just both kissed their daughter more meaningfully than I've seen them kiss each other.' Jos? smiled and made a small dismissive gesture... but there was no mistaking the nervousness in his voice, or the shifty look in his eyes, as he answered... and Noi was positive that he meant for her to catch it. 'My wife and I are more open in our displays of affection, I'll admit,' he said to her. 'But that's just us.' Noi whipped out her notepad and jotted down some notes furiously. She was starting to skirt very close a piece of back-story that Juan and Teresa must not have wanted her to find... and either they were deeply concerned that she'd caught the clues... Or they had no idea that she was getting this close. There's no request Too big or small; We give ourselves, We give our all! Love isn't some place that we fall... It's something... that we... do!
