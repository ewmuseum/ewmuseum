Death Omen IV

22 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 22, 2004 Prologue: What if all hope ran short? What if every last drip of energy you had was drained from you? Could you muster the fact of being a prune? Could you remain a bag of dried up bones? Some people say death is the same thing. Others expect another live afterwards. One man has experienced such and his name is Jamester. In the early days of wrestling he was known as The Kid, the underdog. Everyone wanted to beat him down and take him out for he was a rookie, but no one could for they’re cockiness had become they’re main reason for loss. Jesse Jamester proved many wrong. He proved the generation of extreme wrong when he took a fatal fall from the rafters and went through 23 tables. With three broken ribs, a cracked shoulder blade, and three misplaced discs in the neck Jesse looked to be on the shelf for life. However the kid fought back with a passion and desire, and even though still injured went on with his career proving many doctors wrong. In early 2003 Jesse had been the abuser of drugs, alcohol, and other illegal goods. His abuse of these good became the reason for him being put in a rehabilitation center. Though rough he had felt it was a relaxing place after the first three weeks until he was examined after a self-suicide attempt. Jesse had been diagnosed with cancer. A section by his heart and in his left lung had the cancer cells and the surgery would be near impossible. However Jesse had gone into surgery to do the impossible, and prayed for the best. Now after again almost dying from an alcohol overdose, Jesse questions his life again. He’s not sure whether or not he’s able to keep going, but he knows one thing is for sure, his wife is watching over him. Story Continues: ’I keep hearing the name Deadman being heard… People ask how do you prepare for a fight with a Deadman? I am not dead… just different. People think that death is the mere absences of life, but it’s not that. It’s more of a new beginning, a fresh start to once be you again. I can’t seem to realize how anyone can confuse me and the dead, but I’m definitely not dead.’ Jesse stood there in the hospital room with his blue jeans and black shirt that had a flaming skull with wings of fire across the chest on. He was waiting to be released when Julius came in, his cousin who’d done a stint in Lights Out Wrestling with him. ’Hey Jesse, how are you feeling man?’ boasted Julius as he hugged his cousin and shook his hand afterwards. ’Not too bad, I could take you on right now if you wanted to,’ laughed Jesse jokingly with Julius. Well, I came to pick you up from the hospital, they wanted family to do it, and I told them I could. So you ready to go?’ asked Julius. ’Yeah lets go bro,’ said Jesse in a stern and yet patient way right as the two left the hospital and got in Julius’ truck. As the truck went down the road Jesse pondered away at the outside where snow fell again freely as it did so many times up in Calgary. Thinking to himself Jesse pondered about his match and remembered the questions a reviewer asked him just days before while in the hospital. ’Jesse, what happened after you loss the World Title Match against Rokku? Everyone thought you were well… really dead, or missing. Can you comment?’ ’I’d lost a lot of energy. When I came to it, I knew I’d lost and I wasn’t willing to accept that. It was quite a thing to feel really. I’d lost this part of me that was my life, and so as any person would do, I faded away for some time, reflecting.’ ’Jesse, some say you have thoughts of leaving Global Wrestling? Is this true?’ ’No its not true. If anything I’ve thought of signing a new deal with GW to stay here longer. Global Wrestling is my home, and I won’t be leaving until I feel like it.’ ’What about Ice? We saw here tonight he beat you, will that be a problem when you face him again if you shall do so?’ ’Look in my eyes… Does it look like a problem for me to rip that man apart after what he did to me? No it isn’t and it won’t be if we step in the ring again. Ice is going to get a dose of reality next time we meet, because I’m going to hit him so hard he’ll be OUT COLD!’ ’Last question Jesse. In the last few weeks some would compare your abilities to a cliff, they just keep going down. Do you agree and why would that be if you can answer?’ ’Maybe it’s because I’m doing drugs… Or possibly because you people never get off my back… However that’s not the reason, and the answer isn’t involved within the wrestling company, it’s personal. So I won’t answer it… Sorry, now good day.’ Jesse just sat there and kept driving watching out the window remembering the memories. The ground passed by so quick as the truck drove, yet the memories didn’t. Reaching into his left pocket Jesse pulled out his wallet and opened it up looking at a picture of his daughter, Samantha. Her blue eyes, long brown hair, and bliss smile of what true happiness was a reminder of the woman Jesse married and had her with. It was a pleasurable memory nonetheless. Putting the wallet back in his pocket after removing her picture he held the picture in his hand and kept looking out the window as the trees and sky went by, just thinking of her. *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** The doorknob wiggled once then again, and finally the door opened to show Jesse entering his new hotel room. The perfume-like air was a breathe of sweetness to him as he walked in and dropped his stuff only a few feet from the door. Walking in he saw a dozen of roses lying on the table in the living room of his sweet. As he walked over he noticed that they were black and dead, yet thought it was because they’d been there for a while. However a note next to the roses had his name written on it, “Jesse Fristik”. Picking the note up he tore it open and began to read it. His eyes wondering the lines quickly, his face became confused and then with a sigh of either frustration or relief hit him. He dropped the letter on the table and went over to the kitchen. Opening the door of the mini-fridge he looks inside for something to drink, but then the phone rings. Ring… Ring… Ring… Ring… Finally he picks it up. ’Hello?’ ’… (Pause) did you read the letter?’ ’Yes, who is this?’ ’… Do what it says or she will be hurt?’ ’Who will be hurt?’ ’… (Breaking up of the phone and then a new voice speaks) DADDY!!! HELP ME!’ ’You sick bastard! Who is this!!! You better not hurt her or I’ll…’ ’Or you will lose her… So do what it says… (Hangs up)’ Jesse just stands there with a blank look on his face as he drops the phone on the floor and the beeping noise on the connection ending is heard. Dropping down to his knees he grabs his hair and begins to cry from what was just heard. (To Be Continued…)
