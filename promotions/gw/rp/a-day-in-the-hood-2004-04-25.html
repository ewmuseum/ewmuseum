A day in the hood!

25 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A day in the hood! Jay Quickness Global Wrestling Promo #1221 Date: 4/25/04 Location: Apt./Street (WrestlerCade Rp. II) The scene opens to Hood Hefner in his apartment. The apartment is nicley decorated with pictures and replecas of titles that he won in other federations. Hood Hefner is sitting down on the couch listening to some beat's and freestyling while he smokes a blunt. Hood Hefner rasies the volume to the radio. 'Aight, here we go…we gon shoot to these young cowards bleed, so when you see this kid spittin shells, it aint gonna be sunflower seeds. Your rims are too small; roCs are too little, you be talking to them chicken heads like Dr. Dolittle…I barely reload, 'I got hoes', and slugs that'll hit you in different 'Area Codes'…Now calm down homey, I aint Scarface, I'm that quiet dude, that creeps behind Tony…I bang for fifty thousand, I'm the kid involved with more projects then New York City Housin'…I get in these girly's hineys, cause they ain't seen the Jordans on my feet since the early 90s… I keep rocks cleana, I be busted on these chick's faces till they look like they covered in Noxzema…I still got this game all set, and I won't till my paycheck looks like the national debt...' The cordless phone which is resting right next to him begins to ring. Ring...Ring Hood Hefner lowers the music, picks up the phone and begins to speak. 'Yo, what up. Who dis?' Hood Hefner say's. 'What up Hood. This is GeeMac.' The other person on the line say's. 'What's really good my nigga?' Hood Hefner say's while he takes a couple of pull's from his blunt. 'Yo, I seen that nigga on the block. That nigga that you have been looking fo'.' GeeMac tell's Hood. 'Word, this nigga is back! Yo, let's go get him.' Hood Hefner say's with a angry tone of voice. 'No doubt, Im come pick you up in tha van. He now's how your whip looks already.' GeeMac, tells Hood Hefner. 'No doubt, Im get ready. One.' Hood tells GeeMac. 'One!' GeeMac replies. Hood Hefner hangs up the phone, and get's up from the couch. He quickly walks to the bathroom and grabs something from the back of the toilet, it's a 9mm pistol. He walks back to the living room, sit's back down on the couch, put's his blunt in a ash tray and begins to clean the 9mm pistol. He takes out the clip, check's if it's loaded, put's the clip back into the gun and cock's it back. He lay's it on the table picks up his blunt from the ash tray, lay's back and continues to smoke while he closes his eye's. ~5 Minutes Later~ The intercome begins to ring, startling Hood Hefner. He quickly get's up from the couch, grabs his pistol and walks to the intercome and answers it. 'Who?' Hood Hefner ask's. 'GeeMac, let's go do this Hood.' GeeMac answers. 'Yeah...Yeah Ima be down there in a second.' Hood Hefner answers. Hood Hefner rasies the pistol and stares at it. He put's it on saftey and threws it on the couch. He grabs his coat from the coat rack, and rushes out of the apartment locking the door. The scene changes and we are now infront of a red brick building. Hood Hefner see's GeeMac, he walks up to him gives him handshake and they hug each other, for they have been friends for years. GeeMac and Hood quickly jump into the van and skid off. While in the van they begin to speak until they get to there destination. 'So where you seen this nigga at?' Hood ask's. 'I seen him in his block, he came back. I think he thought you forgot about it.' GeeMac explains to Hood. 'Yeah, well he got something else coming to him.' Hood tell's GeeMac. 'Why are we doing this again?' GeeMac ask's Hood. 'I lent the nigga a grand, he neva paid me back. Instead he left the block fo' a couple of month's and he thinks I forgot about it. I want my mothafu*king money or else there gonna be problems.' Hood explains to GeeMac. 'Well it's time to put in that work Hood.' GeeMac tell's Hood. 'You got that shit right, my nigga!' Hood says. The van stops at the end of the block, GeeMac points him out for Hood Hefner. Hood Hefner spots him and his eyes just open up in excitment. Hood Hefner begins to look in the car for something to beat him up with but has no luck. 'What you looking fo'?' GeeMac ask's. 'Something to hit this nigga wit.' Hood say's. 'Oh, check under your seat.' GeeMac tell's Hood with a smile on his face. Hood hefner put's his hand under his seat and begins to feel around, he grabs what feel's like a bat from under his seat, he takes a look at the bat and notices it's his 'Peace Maker!' (Bat). Hood hefner eye's open up. He quickly get's out of the van and begins to walk to the guy who is standing infront of a store. Hood begins to yell out to him. 'YO....WHERE MY MONEY AT HOMIE!' Hood hefner yell's at him. 'OH, SHIT. I GOT YOUR MONEY, LET ME GO GET IT.' He say's. 'Nah, it's too late homie.' Hood say's. The young man tries to run, but is stoped by the 'Peace Maker!' connecting with his back. He fall's on the floor and pain and is screaming out, Hood Hefner begins to laugh and continues to beat him with the bat. The owner of the store comes out hearing all this screaming and yell's at Hood. 'OH NO. Get this away from my store, I am calling the cop's!' The owner yell's out. 'Shut up, and get your ass back in tha store!' Hood yells at the owner. GeeMack pulls up with his van and yell's out the passenger window. 'Let's roll.' GeeMac tell's Hood. Hood Hefner check's the young man's pocket and pulls out a knot of money from his pocket. He runs towards the van, opens the door. and they skid off quickly, leaving everyone that witness that in the street in complete disbelief. -Scene Changes
