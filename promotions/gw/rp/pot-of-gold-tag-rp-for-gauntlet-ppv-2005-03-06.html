Pot of Gold (Tag RP for Gauntlet/PPV)

6 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/06/05 (Tag RP for PPV gauntlet) March 6, 2005 1042 hours Olympiad Gym, Detroit 'It's an Irish folklore tale,' said Stuart LaPortia, as he assisted Jos? Sanchez in putting Juan and Teresa Ramirez through their workout regiment. Husband and wife were at rigorous calisthenics, having finished the stretching and deep breathing exercises that made up the warm-up portion of the routine. 'You won't find much discussion of Irish folklore tales in Mexico, except among university professors and scholars,' said Teresa, grinning slightly as she pumped out squat-thrust repetitions beside her husband. The two huffed and puffed intermittently from the continuous motion. 'If you ever visit the United Kingdom, it's one of the most oft-told legends you'll hear,' chuckled Stuart. 'The tale of the Irish leprechaun... magical faerie folk barely knee-high to a full-grown human. Very secretive and shy, sworn never to be seen by mankind if they can help it... but they say that every leprechaun has a personal treasure trove of gold coins kept in a small pewter pot. Woe betide the mortal who steals a leprechaun's pot of gold... and yet the common folk of Ireland always kept their eyes peeled for clues on how to find them.' 'And what does that have to do with High Octane?' asked Juan; cheeks turning light rosy from the pumping of blood in response to the squat-thrust exercise. 'Gold, as in the gold centerpiece of a championship belt,' said Jos? with a small grin as he called time, and Juan and Teresa came to a rest in sitting positions on the mat. 'Why they put us in the championship tag-team gauntlet match, I don't know,' said Juan, carefully dabbing perspiration off his forehead with a towel. 'We're still very new in GW, and have scarcely introduced ourselves to the fans.' 'Booking agents and administrators always have their reasons,' said Stuart, and smiled and grunted at the thought. 'Sometimes you're just cannon fodder intended to make the reigning champions look good. Sometimes an extra slot is needed to make it look like the winning team worked longer and harder for their win. Sometimes it's just because you're on the roster, and they want to put everyone in... even the newcomers. And sometimes--' Stuart paused to look at Jos?, who nodded sagely-- 'sometimes they simply think you can handle it, and do well, even when you're still new.' 'I know that philosophy is rarely a thought, much less an option, in Lucha Libre,' said Jos?, signaling for a round of rope-skipping. He handed a leather skipping rope with ball-bearing grips on each end to Juan and Teresa. 'Just another example of how American professional wrestling is different.' 'Still have quite a bit to learn, I see?' said Stuart. The thought made him have to chuckle a bit. 'That's why we have you... and Jos?,' said Teresa, smiling as she and Juan commenced skipping their ropes in near-perfect unison. 'We didn't expect to simply waltz right into GW and start-- what's the term? Owning the competition. Even long-time vets can have to start over.' 'And so we have,' said Juan, nodding sagely as he gave a small smile. 'So no, we didn't expect to be put into the tag-team gauntlet match with so few matches under our belts, and the fans still getting to know us.' 'Now that you know you're in it, I trust I know how you intend to wrestle in the match?' asked Jos? a little sternly. 'Like any other match,' said Teresa more seriously. 'As if it could be our last. Giving it all we have.' 'And more,' admonished Jos?, looking his prot’g’s in the eye. 'High Octane, as they call it, is a special card meant to be bigger and better than the weekly Evolution shows in the Global Arena. That's why the federation travels about the country for these specials while the weekly 'House Shows' are always in the same place. At High Octane, you'll see everyone pushing themselves above and beyond their normal limits as you'll have seen them in the tapes you've studied, and when you've seen them perform at the arena.' 'A pot of gold would be all the incentive that the tag-teams need to do that, of course,' said Juan, taking in steady breaths in between skips of the whirling rope. 'Just like in Lucha Libre.' A nostalgic smile came to Teresa's face. 'Papa certainly wrestled the match of his life... that night when the cantina fight happened.' Her expression grew somber at the thought of it, although subconsciously, she continued the strenuous regiment of rope-skipping repetitions, still in almost perfect unison with Juan. Jos? marveled at the sight of it, and Stuart did too. The two Ramirez spouses did a perfect tandem, whether wrestling in the arena or working out in the gym. The timing, pace and coordination of their seamless tandem was astonishing. Perhaps being husband and wife, and knowing each other so well, contributed to it, thought Stuart. 'He was thirty-nine,' said Teresa, continuing her train of thought. 'And he had the chance to win that title for a third time. Some people questioned whether he still could do it... if he still had it. For a few hours, that night, at least... everyone was made a believer.' Jos? called time, and then patted Juan and Teresa on the shoulders. 'Now it's your turn,' he said. 'Just like Mateo did, that fateful day in April... High Octane is your chance to win the tag-team championship... take a huge step toward fulfilling your mission... the vow you made after you brought Melina home from the hospital.' Teresa suddenly bit her lip at the mention of Melina's name. 'Should we call the sitter?' she asked, suddenly a bit apprehensive. Jos? started to make a reassuring gesture, but it was silenced by Juan's hands on his wife's shoulders. 'We need to borrow your cell phone for a minute, Stuart,' said Juan politely. 'It won't take more than that.' Stuart handed Teresa his cell phone and waited patiently while she checked in with the babysitter. Reassured that her daughter was fine, and telling the child that she loved her (in Spanish, of course... the little girl hadn't really picked up and learned any English in her short time in America), Teresa clicked the phone off and returned it to Stuart. 'Let's hit the exercycles,' said Jos?. '5% increased resistance today. In honor of the goal you're pursuing... and how hard it is to reach.' 'Bring it on, Jos?,' said Teresa defiantly, and she and Juan both smiled tightly. 'What do you call Detroit, Stuart? The Automotive City? A little ride like this should be right up their alley.' 'That's the spirit,' said Stuart, nodding and smiling as the two spouses hit their stride pedaling on the cycles, almost as if competing against each other. 'Salaok and Frost,' began Jos?, in his mentor-like tone. 'Frost is the daredevil, the technician... he's the one who will take risks to get the advantage. Salaok is an extremist... he'll pull out all the stops. Anything goes with him. Remember the Glass Dungeon match he wrestled with Christian Zenith.' 'Barbaric,' grunted Teresa; grimacing at the thought of the match. She and Juan had watched it, and were profoundly glad they had put Melina to bed first. The thought of letting a child her age watch that kind of savage behavior... 'Zenith will be pairing with Vivica Valentine,' instructed Jos?. 'They don't care about the fans, they don't care about the rules, and you can believe they don't care about you. Watch your back, Teresa... the way you and your sister watched each other's backs at the cantina that night.' Jos? mentioning the cantina wasn't lost on Do’a Teresa... he gave the example not only to emphasize the need for she and Juan to watch each other's backs... but also to rouse her ire again at an unpleasant memory... push her into an even greater burst of effort than was her norm. Both Teresa and her husband began grunting intermittently as the readings on their cycles pumped up noticeably. '138,' said Jos?, continuing his mentor-like instructions. 'Damien Ekx and Jonathan Drake. Gothic-like punk-rocker wrestlers. They'll look to take you out early if they reach you in the gauntlet order. The key is to outlast them.' Juan shuddered at the thought of punk-rock mixing with Lucha Libre... he and Teresa were conservative traditionalists in their lifestyle. They didn't allow Melina to have her ears pierced; just as Teresa and her sister weren't allowed to have theirs pierced until they were 17. 'Ken Partridge, Jr. and Moresi,' said Jos?. 'Partridge is the reigning champion of the world in GW. Don't stand toe to toe with the man. Use all the space available. Keep mobile. You'll be at home in the air.' Juan and Teresa's faces began to gradually turn florid from the efforts of their joint pedaling. The readings on their cycles were nearly off the scale. Jos? discreetly reached out and ramped up the resistance another 5%. The two Ramirez spouses didn't even seem to notice or feel it. 'Jesse Jamester and Jesse Briggs,' said Jos?. 'Jamester's the all-around athlete. He can take the punishment, and he can dish it out. He can stand toe to toe with you, or dance around the ring with you. He can brawl or wrestle. Briggs is a near-unknown. Combine your attacks on Jamester. Wear him down little by little. Soon as he has to tag out, go all-out on Briggs. We'll see what he's made of.' Stuart called time, and had to smile in surprise at how Juan and Teresa stopped pedaling at the exact same time. The mileage readings on their cycles were identical down to the thousandth of a second. 'I think we just showed them all what YOU'RE made of,' he said, nodding his head up and down slowly to show he was impressed. El Cometa Carmes? and Do’a Teresa smiled at each other and gave each other a hug. Damned if they didn't feel they had just chased down and caught their leprechaun. All that remained was to make it hand over that pot of gold.
