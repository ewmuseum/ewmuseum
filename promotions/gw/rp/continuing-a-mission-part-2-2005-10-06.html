Continuing a mission part 2

6 October 2005

Date: 10-6-2005 Octorber the 28th, Five days later Maine? A place I had never seen before. A place that whenever it is talked about is talked about it’s cold nature. But it was more then that I guess. “MAINE STATE HOSPITAL” Those were the words that I read on the outside sigh. The place was huge. White brick walls with a fence all the way around it. The structure was perfect in a way that you were not going to break out. I for one knew that the only way I was going to get in was to dress the part. A solid white dress, something I was not conferrable with wearing. Walking into the building, seeing the several security men standing around the area before walking up to the information desk that was in front of me. “Hello. How can I help you?” The lady asks. “I need to find out if I can see Mr. Crisp?” Responding to her question with a bit of softness. The demon was asleep. For once I was back in control but for how long? That was the question. “Sure. I just need you to sign a few papers.” Replying as she hands me a few things to sign. I wasn’t sure who this guys was. I never had met him before. All I knew was he was family. He was my uncle. Signing the papers before handing them to her. “Ok. Just follow me and I’ll take you to his room.” She responds. Walking down the long corridor, passing a few of the rooms were the crazy, insane people were staying. Some of them were in straight jackets, some of them were strapped to the beds, and some of them were just to crazy to even move is seems. Walking a few move yards down the hallway before coming to room twenty-three. “Is this it?” Asking her with my heart racing. “Yes it is. You will get ten minutes with him and then visiting hours are over. And remember, don’t look at him in the eyes. Good luck.” She says as the security officer that was walking with us opens the door. The room was wide open. White walls covering my eyes all around. “Who are you?” His voice says, raising up his head from the bed that he was sitting on. “I’m Elia’Elia Smith. I’ve come because I n?”, quieting down as his voice takes over. “I know why you’ve come. You’ve come because you are haunted to right?” Turning around to view me in my entirety. His eyes, glowing with a little red in them. Shaking a little but it wasn’t cold that was making him. “Yes. How did you know?” Responding as the door shut on us. “Because he knows. He can feel the demon in you. He can feel how weak you’ve become because of him.” Lowering his head once more. Walking closer to him as that I could get a closer look at him. He had sweat dripping down his face. His clothing, that of what you get in a place like this. White top with a pair of white pants to go with it. “I need to know who and how to beat this demon. Can you help me?” Asking with a feeling of being scared in my voice. “Who” You ask me as if he is processing me as well. You see Elia, I’m no longer Johnny Crisp. I’m a man that he wants me to be and the only way I can get rid of him is to be in a place like this and that is the only way you can defeat your demon as well.” He says, raising his head up once again. I wasn’t going to handle that answer. I was going to be normal again and if it meant going to hell and back to defeat this demon alone, then so be it. “I refuse to do that. I refuse to let him win. That is why I can to you thinking you might have some type of help for me. Now can you help me or are you to scared to even help yourself?” Forgetting the feeling of being afraid in my gut. I was angry now and I knew he could feel it to. “Afraid” I’m not afraid. I’m just here because I’m scared for everyone around me. But if you want help’leave.” Turning away from me with that response. Though my time was coming to an end. As I began to respond, my grasp on my reality was beginning to end. Hearing his voice inside of me as I stood there. “What are you doing Elia?” The demon inside of me response. “Go away!” Screaming out loud, making my uncle turn back around to me. “You weak little bitch. You will not get rid of me. No one will!” Screaming as my right to run was over. He was in control. He was in control of everything now. My eyes began to go red, looking right at Johnny Crisp as he presses up off the bed and began to walk towards me. “Elia?” Asking as he gets closer to me. “Elia?”, he says to Crisp. “She is gone and so are you.” His eyes began to go wide. Looking right at me as the door opens up and the security officer speaks. “Ok your time is up.” He says. “I’ll be back for you Mr. Crisp. I’ll be back soon’very soon.” The demon says, walking away from him like he was no one. All I could do was stand there inside of my own body and listen and watch. I wasn’t in control anymore. It was as if I was there but not there. ----------------------------------- Saturday, November the 5th Two days before Evolution It was dark. The area was like what you’d see if you were dreaming. Standing around as the ground was in terrible condition. Water was everywhere as it had rained just hours before. I had no idea where I was or what I was doing here. The cold air slung into my body, making me shiver inside. “What have you brought me here.” Asking him from deep inside. “Because this is were it all started and this is were he is.” Responding to me. It all started? Waiting for someone? Who could we be waiting for? My eyes kept looking around. Seeing the homeless men and woman laying underneath their little cardboard boxes down the alleyway. Walking just a little ways I felt a sudden chill go from behind me. It was as if another demon so to speak was entering the picture. Turning around to see one man walking out from a doorway. “He’s here?”, the demon says out loud. He was a little ways away. His power, his breath could be felt inside the darkness that we were standing in. The demon began walking us up closer to me. Closer and closer till we were just inches away from him. So it begins? Jake was looking around, but there was little light in the alley. The edges were providing all sorts of dark hiding places for anyone to hide in. It was seeming that Jake was felling something from one corner, and he began to look in that direction. “Who’s there?” I stood in the dark shadows of the depressing dark alley, in my tight black leather outfit, not revealing myself to the tiny light that was illuminating from above... “Someone who has been waiting here for you.” The mind knew the voice, somewhere in the dark recesses of familiarity and dismissed nuance he knew the name matching the voice. He couldn’t bring the information forward though, and so he stepped forward to force the issue. 'Don't be shy big man, come on out and say hello.' The invite was pleasant to me, revealing the dark shades of my face to the shock of Jake. 'Shy is not in my nature Mr. Hix. Though many other things are...' 'I take back the big man comment.' The feeling, a cold wind rushing through his body as though it were paper-thin. The tingling at the base of his spine became a crackling surge of energy lighting his inner self aflame with warning of what was here before him. For her? She is nothing more than a young woman, troubled perhaps, but? Jake forced the feelings away, looking at Elia as he came slightly closer, within a step of grabbing her if it was needed. He never knew what would be thrown at him from his enemies, and there were enemies. Jake remained coy and playful, 'As for the other things in your nature, perhaps the dark alley is not the best place for such a conversation Ms. Smith, or can I call you Elia?' 'Elia...?' Pausing for a moment as a gust of cold heavy wind blew through our hands and hair. I wasn't in control anymore. All I could do was stand there and listen to the things he was saying. 'For you Mr. Hix, you may call me Ms. Smith. Though deep down you may know me in a much different way.' Jake tried to pierce the mysterious veil surrounding the small woman in the shadows. He felt something, but he couldn't quite place it. She was something more than he had thought, but was it damned or divine? 'Well, I have to admit that whenever we meet I feel some sort of...connection to you Miss Smith. Of course, sometimes you blame bad Thai food, or something else seems wrong. You've...changed...since I first joined Global. To what do I owe this mysterious pleasure?' Moving up a little closer, caming fully out of the darkness that was filling up the space between me and Jake. Eyes, watery blue with a little touch of red, were locking on to Jake like they were two arms. 'Why am I here? Why am I standing in front of you in this deep deserted palace of darkness that surrounds us, waiting for you?' Elia, or so it seems to Jake and it would to everyone else, took a deep breath, 'I'm here to ask you for an answer. An answer that only you will know...' Jake raised his eyebrows as a little smirk crossed his lips, 'You'll have to forgive me Miss Smith. You see, I haven't had dinner tonight, and so I don't have a nice little fortune cookie saying to shoot back your way. I could make something up, be quick and deep in my hazy yet striking observations. Of course, what would the point be? If you have a question, ask it and I shall answer if I am able. If you have some other need...' Jake looked her up and down, then his own striking blue eyes met her own, 'Well, seems a little early in our relationship for that, but at least calling you Miss. Smith would be a bit kinky.' Elia's eyes that the demon was looking out, going glossy a little, caught the eyes of Jake going back up from her small feet. Her heart, racing with the beat of the night, licking her lips toward Jake's foolish comments towards her. 'Your comments, as kiddish as they seem, are not wanted. Nor is your invitation for bodily touching either. You see Mr. Hix, your mind is what is needed and all it's needed for is the information it holds.' Stepping back into the darkness that had reclaimed her body, Elia's voice remains the focal point for this depressing and awkward encounter. 'And the information it holds about one person.' 'Touch?.' Jake shrugged as he nodded at her. 'Women have sought after me for many things, but never been so blunt as to look for me for 'my mind'. I think I’m flattered Miss Smith. What can I help you with, since there is obviously some mental marathon you feel the need to run through.' 'First and foremost Mr. Hix, just like the freezing...cold...night that has been so brutal to shadow us tonight, your mind has the same qualities. It has the something that I desire more than anything and for that very reason Mr. Hix, our relationship will be more different that the soulless, deceased, females that you infest in your tiny word.' Brushing her hair back, making her eye connection with Jake to remain the center of this little chat. 'You Jake, the information that I require you will give me.' Remaining inside, filled up to the top with the pleasant flying creatures of the night going around my head. 'For weeks now Mr. Hix, I've been hearing the same name over and over in my head. Trying to figure out what it is. listening to the name and all I keep hearing is the name Thomas...' Jake's eyes narrowed slightly, his face slackening as the reaction took physical control before he could stop it. The name couldn't mean anything to her because no one knew. She was throwing something out to him, trying to bait him. 'Well, maybe the rumors about the voices in your head are true. I'm not sure what I could do to assist you.' Trapping myself off from the words that Mr. Hix was tossing out to her, blocking the shadows that were being cast down from the surrounding walls of the buildings around her. 'Mr. Hix...' Pausing for a brief moment. 'Your knowledge is far too great for you to deny the name. Even I, a lonely soul that is being compressed by things that are not of my will know that you understand me. Why do you deny me the information...' Jake's entire body relaxed, a natural reaction for him whenever danger was present. He would relax, ready for the fight he expected to come, but here the battle had nothing to do with the bulging muscles on his body. Jake's face was washed over in a dark threat, 'I don't know who you think you are little girl, but sometimes looking in dark places can bring on unexpected consequences. Whatever you're thinking, do your fractured sanity a favor and move on.' 'Unexpected consequences...' It was weird. All I could do was scream out, trying to get the attention of Mr. Hix but it was to late. I was beginning to lose all control of my speech, my mind, everything. Elia Smith was beginning to fade out forever and I could do nothing. The black covering shadow that Elia's body was hidden in lit up like Christmas. The eye color, the watery blue, was suddenly changed to a cloudy white, outlined by a fiery red that was taking over. Standing there, the demon fixing those eyes, not on the eyes of Jake's anymore but on a name that was printed on a wall just yards away. 'Mr. Hix...you have nothing to fear. I'm not here for action. I'm here for a deeper purpose. As for who I am Mr. Hix...' Walking up towards Jake just a few feet. 'Maybe you should be asking Thomas...' 'Fear wasn't my concern Miss Smith.' Jake stepped forward, he could feel something emanating from her, like waves of heat washing over him. 'I have no fear in this world, or the next. However, for someone who insists I refer to her as Miss Smith, you seem particularly interested in parts of my life that are of no concern to you. While you may need a therapist, I’m not one. We’re not in group therapy either, this isn't a group circle, and you seem to be the absolute last person to speak with about anything of...substance.' 'Group circle...' The demon says or shall we say, Elia Smith who in all of this I was finally finishing the transformation. 'Mr. Hix, my concern isn't for YOU.' My words strike the very heart of Jake. '...But of the world that you have lived in. I do not do this by choice Mr. Hix but I do it because it's of the greater importance. You see, I don't require answers for myself. I require answers for a greater purpose...and a purpose I'm sure you can understand.' His eyes narrowed dangerously, 'You know, we could banter back and forth all night long, and still get nowhere. What is it you want Smith?' 'In due time Mr. Hix...in due time. But I believe out time right now is up. But I will leave you with one lasting question. How far down does the Thomas name go...' My lips, slowly peeling up on each side as it was forming a smile. My eyes, lowering down, taking me off guard of Jake before taking a few steps towards him, walking right by him and towards the nasty and breezy night. Jake grabbed her arm as she tried to pass. The world shifted. The entire landscape before his eyes was replaced by memories flashing through his mind. Demons and devils, slaughter and suffering, and through it all he finally recognized the feeling he was experiencing. Jake came back, shaking away the assault of memories and experience as he looked down at Elia, 'You aren't Elia Smith. You're...something else...something else entirely. No wonder you cling to the darkness.' My eyes wondered to the face of Jake once more. This time...almost looking deeper inside his soul. Trying to flesh out the real story that he was hiding. 'Mr. Hix...sometimes the answers that you seek are laying in the past. In due time all your questions will be shown to you. In due time...' Pulling my arm away from the warm grasp hand that Jake was clinging to. The long journey was ahead of me and the darkness was beginning to over take me. Walking right into the cloudy fog was was coming up, turnign around to see what little bit of Jake was left. Jake Hix watched as the darkness enveloped the slight, almost frail body containing the power wrapped within withering flesh. 'So, it begins in darkness and an unspoken truth. Oh, I know you darkness, I know where you come from monster. The hour has come round at last, and I stand before you on the road to Bethlehem.'
