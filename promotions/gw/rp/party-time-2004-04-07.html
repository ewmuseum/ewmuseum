Party Time!

7 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/07/04 There is a party happening tonight at the Drunken Leperchaun, it's Karoke Night, and up on stage right now is Donny J McNasty singing his rendition of one of Kiss's greatest hits 'Calling Dr. Love'. He's not too bad at it either, he's not Gene Simmons, but he sounds good in his own way. McNasty is in a great mood, He and Brick defeated the team of Mark Makonie and Train and he won the Drinking Tournament. How much better could life be for the King of Hardcore? There is one thing that hasn't been explained, the mysterious envelope that was given to Harv during the last Evolution from the Tag-Team White Rose. What business could they want with McNasty or vica versa? He hasn't mentioned to anyone since he got back late Monday night. McNasty makes his way off stage over to the bar, Harv is behind there as usual. McNasty orders up his usual Guiness Irish Stout. It almost begs the question, Does McNasty have a blood alcohol level or should the question be, Does McNasty have a alcohol blood level? If so what happens when McNasty has more blood than alcohol on his system? I guess those are thing's we'll learn over time. McNasty and Harv begin to talk. 'Donny, have you heard anything from Arik Idol about the other night?' 'No, but I can almost guarandamntee he has a hangover from hell' 'Ha ha no kidding. I kind of like him, he's a straight laced kind of guy' 'Yeah it's something you don't see much of late, he's a good guy, he's got a bright future' McNasty takes a large gulp of his Guinness. 'Harv did you see on TV some wacked out Midget group is trying to sue me in Civil Court for the other night on Evolution.' 'Ha ha, yeah, I think it's funny you have the lollipop guild suing you' 'That's funny,' McNasty now does his best impression of the Lollipop Guild from Wizard of Oz. 'We represent the Lollipop Guild, the lollipop guild, the lollipop guild, and were going to try and take Donny J McNasty to the cleaners because he made fun of us' Harv is laughing to the point of tears. 'I just don't get it Harv, someone must have pissed in their Cheerios or something. Vertically Challenged People are People Too, can kiss my white irish american ass and like it.' 'That's the spirit' McNasty takes another large gulp of Guiness, as Harv proceeds to ask him a more serious question. 'Donny, what was that envelope from White Rose the other night' 'Nothing really, it's just some old business that needs to be looked into' 'You sure? I mean do you need a lawyer or anything like that type of business.' 'No no, I have it being taken care of, in fact Monday night at Evolution in my interview I will address it then' 'Alright, well who are you having interview you?' 'The ever so beautiful, Alexis Steele' 'Nice choice if I do say so myself' 'Yeah, if things go right, she'll find out why I am the King of Hardcore' 'That's what I'm talking about' McNasty chugs the rest of his Guinness down and the man running the Karoke machine says it's time once again for Donny J McNasty to come up and sing. This time he'll be singing, 'All Apologies' by Nirvana in memory of Kurt Cobain who died 10 years ago this past Monday. The lights in the bar begin to dim and the lighters of the patrons come on. All gets quiet as McNasty begins to sing.
