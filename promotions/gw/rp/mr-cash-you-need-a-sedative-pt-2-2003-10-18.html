Mr. Cash, you need a sedative (pt.2)

18 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.18.03 Lisa Lind looks in the mirror of the faculty bathroom, mad at herself for letting Ace run away again. She slowly applies war paint to her face, some of it dripping down onto her nurses uniform. 'He can't run forever..I will find him. And when I do...' Lisa says, an evil grin coming across her face as she examines the needle. Lisa shrieks laughter as she shoots out of the bathroom, in the search for Ace. *** Out of breath and sweating, Ace creeps along the wall of the hallway, attempting to find another exit out of the hospital. He can still somewhat hear the janitor bellowing out his terrets version of 98 Degrees songs, almost positive they will give him nightmares for weeks. Ace has no doubt the nurse is still searching for him, so he wasn't going to make himself too easy to find. Crouching low to the ground, Ace rounds a corner and enters what appears to be an office. A startled old doctor looks at Ace with his hair all messed up with dried blood. He's standing by a copying machine. 'Sir! Can I help you?' The doctor says, concerned. Ace is peeking out the glass of the door for the nurse. After a few seconds of searching, and being assured she's not around, he turns to the doctor. 'No, I'm fine.' Ace says. 'Umm..okay. Hey, would you mind helping me with this copy machine?' He says, looking at the cophy machine with a frown. 'I'm afraid I'm confused.' 'Uhhh..sure.' Ace replies, wondering what kind of doctor couldn't use a copy machine. 'What's the problem?' 'Well, I don't what to use to make copies.' The doctor replies. Ace taps the copy machine, a confused look on his face. 'This thing right here, bro.' Ace says. 'Oh, I know that..I just don't know what to use?' The doctor replies. 'Perhaps if I put a soda on it, it will give me copies of my papers?' 'Wait a minute...a soda?' Ace says, not believing what he's hearing. 'What the hell, dude...you put paper in this thing...and it makes copies.' The doctor throws his arms in the air, a smile on his face. 'Who'da thunk it!?' The doctor says laying the paper down on the machine. 'Psh..paper! Oh, I'm so rude.' The doctor extends his hand, and Ace shakes it. 'I'm Dr. Summers. I do open heart surgeries.' The doctor speaks with a large smile. !!?!?!??!?!!? 'You do..what!?' Ace says, in genuine fear of this man even being a doctor. 'Open...heart...surgery.' The doctor speaks to Ace as if he were a two year old. 'Where I cut open a patients ch-....Hey, I like your make up.' 'I'm not wearing any make-up, chump.' Ace replies. 'No, no, not you...Lisa.' The doctor replies, pointing behind Ace. Ace's eyes bulge, and he whips around just in time to see Lisa charging him with the needle, screaming, the warpaint on her face. Ace narrowly dodges the needle, and Dr. Summers takes the needle right in his arm. 'Oh my....' Dr. Summers mumbles as he slowly falls to a heap on the floor. Lisa quickly grabs another syringe from her utility belt and throws it at Ace, who is now running away. Ace screams in terror as the needle embeds itself into the wooden door inches from his head. Ace whips open the door and begins to run down towards the exist that Lisa was once guarding. Lisa is screaming at the top of her lungs in an Indian wail. 'AYAYAYAYAYAYAAYAYAYAY!!!!!' Lisa screams, throwing needles down the hallway at Ace. 'What the hell is wrong with you!?' Ace screams back at Lisa. Suddenly the noise stops and Ace comes to a slower pace, turning around. Lisa is nowhere to be found. There are needles everywhere, but no sign of her. Ace looks around in a panic, walking backwards towards the exit. 'I've gotta get outta here.' Ace mumbles to himself as he pushes the exit open.
