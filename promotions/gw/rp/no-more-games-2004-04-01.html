No More Games...

1 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/1/04 'I'll be damned if I shed blood for nothing...' Train makes no attempt to hide the anger in his voice as he slowly and intensly speaks those words into the cell phone at his ear. The city of Chicago, trees, people, buildings, all cast a brilliant reflection on the windsheild as Train sits behind the wheel of his rented Escalade, one hand on the wheel, the other holding the phone while his foot, atop the gas pedal, gradually makes it's way to the floor. No doubt he should be relaxing today. He deserves to. Hell, after what went down in Rome just a couple of nights ago, he probably deserved a purple heart pinned to his chest. But he's got two important tasks to take care of today... And one of them he wanted taken care of now...right fucking now. Train tilts his head to the left as he drives, bring on a slight twinge of pain. The damn stitches under the bandage above his left eye seem to catch the material if he moves too quick. And, of course that's why this shit needed to be resolved. Train had indeed blead on the streets of Rome. That fucking idiot had damn near killed them both with that bullshit 2x4 he set on fire like some kinda moron. Yeah, that's a slick idea when you're right next to the gas tank of a car. Now, even though Train won the match, he would've normally felt the need to settle another score with the son of a bitch for that shit. However, now he knows just how quickly his transition to the wrestling world is begining to take hold. The opportunity for retribution has landed right on Train's doorstep. Payback is just a few days away at the next Evolution. But who gives a shit? 'Whip his ass...again? Hell yeah.' That's what he'd have been thinking a few weeks earlier. Oh, but what a difference a month makes. Instead of thinking how much ass he can kick...he only wonders who's ass is worth kicking. It ain't about volume no more...it's all about quality. 'Do you fucking hear me Jerry?! Huh? Man, I don't care what that bitch said! Are you my damn agent or not?! Oh, you are? Well then act like it! Stop being such a little ho' when you talking to her son. What? Man, fuck her! I don't care if her name is Hatfield or McCoy, that bitch owes me. I was the one that put on the show last night. Bet you the buy rate went up at least half a damn point when that fucking car blew up dogg. So now, what do I get for making that show worth watching? Not a damn thing! Wha... Yeah I know I got booked for Monday. What's the problem? What's the problem?! Did you see the booking?! Yeah, that's right Jerry... Me and Makonie versus McNasty and Brick. Now let me ask you something Jerry, in your capacity as my agent.... How many times am I gonna have to hand McNasty his punk ass on a silver platter before I stop getting dealt small Irish potatos up in this bitch?! Clash of the Titans...what's that, like the tenth or twelth time I done broke a piece of my shoe off in his monkey ass?! Now, why am I having to do that shit again? See, if it had just been me and Brick, one on one? Hey, it would be all good right now, you feel me? Not that I give a shit beyond getting my check, but the fans want that. They want it. I want it. I owe that chump an ass whuppin' for opening his mouth about me. But more than all that? He's a former champ Jerry! I beat his ass one on one then I get myself lined up for a title shot. And that's why that ho McCoy didn't book it. She knows what it's gonna mean when I finally get my hands on that muthafucka and leave his ass looking up at the lights! She knows she's gonna have to deal with me. Won't be no more putting me off. Won't be no more trying to ignore, booking me on on the bottom of the cards. When I leave my boot print on Brick's punk ass she knows I'm headed to the main event and she won't have no choice, but to deal with me! Now she don't really wanna do that, but I don't too much give a damn what she wants. Let's get the game plan straight right now Jerry. You gonna get that bitch on the phone. You gonna let her know that after me and Makonie leave Brick and McNasty laying flat on their backs there ain't gonna be no more bullshit. You let her know she's gonna give me Brick one on one. Then you let her know once I beat his monkey ass she's gonna give me the title shot I've earned! You let that ho' know there ain't no use in protecting any of her golden boys. I don't care if it's Ice. I dont' care if it's Zero. I especially don't care if it's that twerp Deacon Holmes. You let her know that I didn't come all this way just to let some little bitch from some fucking trailer park stop me. You tell her if she insists on trying to rob me of what a brotha's earned? Hey... I done had muthafuckas try to rob me before. They all thought they was gonna end up on easy street after they took what belonged to me. But all they got was a one way trip to intensive care. You let that bitch know I ain't playing no more games dogg. If she doesn't start showing me the respect I deserve? You let her know...that shit that she suspects, but can't prove I did to her show a few weeks back?! Me and the M.O.B.? We gonna make that shit look like exactly what it was. A joke. But next time?! That shit ain't gonna be funny...cuz we ain't gonna be playin'. You tell her that Jerry...before the bitch gets hurt.' As Train snaps the phone shut and tucks it into his jacket pocket he finds himself wishing for one thing. Wishing...that it didn't all come back so easily. The rage. The since of entitlement. The feeling that the world owed him something. The desire to do whatever it takes to collect. After all he'd done to change his life...he wished it wasn't so damn easy to go back to being who he was. To being that Thug. But more than all that. He wished he didn't like it so fucking much. Whatever. It was time to complete the second task of the day. It was time to make the pick up....
