A Sign From The Heavens...And of Love?

4 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-4-2004 Snow begins to accumulate as Pendragon scales the mountainside. Although it is surely cold, he sweats from the heat of his own inner fire. His cloak blows back from his body in the winds that whip about the high altitude, yet he smiles as he goes higher, approaching the hole that the meteor left in the side of the mountain. Each step he takes, the wind blows harder, seemingly trying to hold him back and keep him from his prize. The light never leaves his cold blue eyes as he presses on, his mouth turning down into a grim frown. Soon, it seems that he fights for each step that he is able to take towards the crater? “Indeed. It is true then that other forces than those of the Light have a hand in this business. Darkness does indeed grow strong in this time where heroes are few and far between. “Twould seem that everywhere I go there is someone or something trying to stop me. And yet here, as in other aspects of mine life, I shall not be stopped.” And so he trudges on, the relentless wind pounding against him’Harder and harder it blows, until he is finally only crawling. He digs his hands into the deepening snow to keep from being blown completely off of the mountain. Closer and closer he comes to the crater. No longer smoking, the largish whole is a pitch blackness in contrast to the blinding white of the snow. Closer and closer, until finally, he leans into the hole, and with both hands pulls out a basketball sized meteorite. Staggering with the weight of the thing, he stands and holds it overhead with both hands. A white lightning bolt streaks from the sky and strikes the rock, illuminating Pendragon in a blinding flash. The wind stops howling, finally giving up the fight with the Lord King? Pendragon: INDEED! “TIS A GLORIOUS DAY INDEED! THANK YOU!!!! The scene begins to fade as Pendragon wraps the meteor in his black cloak”. ************FLASH************** Once more, the smithy. Sitting on the small porch, Yoshindo carves some bit of wisdom into the hilt of a katana sword. He looks up as cries of awe begin to sound through the village. With a throng of onlookers following behind, the Lord King strides to the blacksmith’s shop. Yoshindo gets to his feet and walks out to meet Pendragon. Pendragon’s eyes blaze as he unwraps the meteor. Yoshindo and the crowd gasp simultaneously as the rock is revealed. Pendragon holds it overhead to the “oohs” and “ahhs” of the crowd. Yoshindo motions for him to come inside the smithy and the two of them leave the crowd outside. Inside, the two men stare at the meteor, in all its glory. Set upon a table, the table seems almost to groan with the weight? Yoshindo: It is certainly dense enough to be steel of a high grade? Pendragon: This rock holds mine sword. We must free it! Yoshindo: Indeed. This may be a sword the likes of which shall be forever unmatched? Pendragon: We should get to it then? Yoshindo: Stoke the fires? Pendragon removes his mail shirt, and is replaces it with a leather apron and thick leather gloves. Yoshindo doesn’t remove his shirt, but he does don the apron and gloves. Pendragon shovels some coal into the furnace and works the ventilation lever? Yoshindo: You are certainly big enough for the job? Pendragon merely laughs as he continues with the ventilation, growing the heat of the flames within the furnace. At last, Yoshindo nods and Pendragon goes to a tool rack and grabs what appear to be a huge pair of tongs. He grasps them with both hands, opens them, and picks up the meteor. He walks it to the furnace, the cords standing out in his neck, and places it in the flames. As time passes, the meteor begins to glow red. Finally, Pendragon removes the meteor and places the whole thing on an anvil. Yoshindo gives him a hammer, and the two of them begin to hammer at the thing, each hammer fall echoing through the smithy? Yoshindo: We have to get through this outer layer of crap. Inside is where the treasure lies, if indeed there is a treasure contained in here. Seems an awfully large meteor though for just one sword. Pendragon: If it is meant to be, then it shall be? The hammers continue to fall and small pieces of the trash rock fall from the meteor. Smaller and smaller it gets, and the gleam of steel is still not revealed. Finally, with a grunt, Pendragon brings the hammer down and the meteor shatters, revealing a round metal object. He drops the hammer again but the thing doesn’t even budge? Yoshindo: My god! This is a titanium alloy. Radiation treated, this may be the hardest piece of steel I have ever seen! Stoke the flames, my boy! This is going to take some heat! Pendragon works the lever as Yoshindo places the ball into the flames. Hotter and hotter the flames burn, and finally the ball turns red. Pendragon raises his eyebrows, asking “Is it time yet?”, but Yoshindo shakes his head. Finally, he nods. Pendragon pulls the ball from the flames and sets it upon a huge flat anvil. Again, they trade hammer shots. This time, the steel doesn’t shatter, but rather seems to flatten. It alternates as such for some time’Time in the furnace, time under the hammer. Each time, the steel becomes flatter. Finally, Pendragon grabs tongs and begins to fold the molten metal. With each fold, the two men hammer the steel, melding it together. Sweat stands out on both men at the painstaking labor, yet both men’s eyes gleam with the thought of what this piece of steel shall become. Pendragon: Thirty folds. Thirty folds will be enough? Yoshindo: I have never made a sword of steel folded more than even ten times! Pendragon: then we shall both accomplish a first this day? Yoshindo’s eyes widen at the brash statement. Steel folded thirty times is unheard of! Not since the master, Masamune, has a sword of such caliber even been tried, let alone completed! And yet Pendragon means to go on with the madness. Each time, as the steel becomes less malleable, it goes back into the furnace. Each time it comes out, it is hammered flat, then folded, then hammered together. The bit of steel becomes smaller and smaller as the two men fold and hammer, hammer and fold. At last, this first task complete, the two men look upon that which they have toiled to make. A small steel box. Steel folded thirty times to create an ultimate density. A box of meteoric titanium. A sword in the making. Pendragon looks at Yoshindo but the man shakes his head? Yoshindo: That is enough for this day, son. We have toiled long into the night. The village sleeps, and will sleep even easier if we stop the infernal hammering. Pendragon looks as if he is about to plead with the man, an almost insane desire shines through his gaze as he looks upon the steel. He averts his eyes, nods, and a smile comes to his lips? Pendragon: It is well, then. I am a bit tired my ownself, and I think that a bit of rest might be in order? Yoshindo: Come’we shall go to my house. Yoshindo leads Pendragon out the back door of the smithy and into a flowing garden. Bridges span ponds here and there. It is a much more extravagant version of Mr. Miyagi’s house in the Karate Kid. That isn’t what Pendragon sees, but you folks do. What else have you to compare it with? Pendragon stops and leans over the railing of one such bridge, and smiles? Pendragon: I thought thee a sword maker, not fish grower, Yoshindo! Yoshindo chuckles and shakes his head? Yoshindo: Of all people, you should know that a true warrior practices many things. I am a creator. I create swords, trees, and fishes in my garden. Sword making is a love, a passion, and my vocation. In here is my vacation. My home. Here I can relax, and take in the beauty of other things that I have made with my own hands? Pendragon nods and continues walking towards a small house in the distance? “Indeed. A man might lose himself in this beauty. Mine whole life, each thing of beauty I create is either destroyed or is taken from me. My parents were taken at an early age. I have never been one to have friends, and now I am without mine sword, Excalibur. Some people misunderstand the sword. They wish to view it as a weakness. Throw taunts at me as if I love the sword. As if an inanimate object is capable of love. They all have it wrong. The sword is a token of the past. The history of mine name is wrapped in that sword. To take it from me is a direct affront to mine nobility and honor. The fools understand honor not. My strength flows not from the sword, but is rather honed by the sharp will of Pendragon’s past. The sword carries a bit of each generation. The blood has never thinned, and I am here, undimmed from mine family’s past glories. That is what the sword is. Mine history. It belongs in this time not, just as I do not belong here. And yet, as it goes on, so to do I. The sword is like a rock’Immovable. Obstinate. Uncaring, unfeeling. It is the heart that wields the sword which matters. When I kill, I kill with mine heart. Not the sword, not mine hand. Enemies fall because they need to, and I kill them with mine heart. And yet, people make a mockery of me. Excalibur is the last of its kind. Never again shall there be a sword so grand, and never again shall the imprint of such a strong line leave its indelible mark upon an object; sword or otherwise. As it is the last, so too am I the last. The last Pendragon. Some of mine line have known peace. Some have ruled nations and prospered, had sons and then faded into the past. I have known war. Battle is what I know. What is love” I have known it not. Barely I remember the love of mine father, and the faint traces of it that I have in mine heart warm me on cold nights. Yet, I am the last, and I fear I am destined to remain as such. Never have I known the tender love of a woman, nor hoped to.” They reach the house, and as they enter, a rueful expression crosses Pendragon’s face, and he chuckles a bit? Yoshindo: What’s funny, young master? Pendragon: Oh nothing? The two men enter and strip off the aprons. Yoshindo disappears as Pendragon removes his boots and sits on the floor. Yoshindo soon reappears with two glasses. One he hands to Pendragon before he sits. Pendragon takes a drink and stares into the glass? Pendragon: Hast thee known love, Yoshindo? Yoshindo: Of course! Who hasn’t? The softness of the woman, her heart?.her soul. Why? Pendragon: I know not what love is’I only know how to destroy. I have never been much of a builder? Yoshindo: Umm’err’Venn, you have, um, HAD a woman, haven’t you? Pendragon laughs out loud and takes a drink? Pendragon: Aye’Wenches are an easy thing to get, yet love eludes me. Monday, I fight a man whom love seems to drive. Drachen. And love is strong, yet I know mine will is stronger yet, and I will wrest the gold from his hands as I wrested the meteor from the mountain. It simply will be. Yet, I feel some reservations. The man fights for love, and for redemption, yet Monday, I shall take it all away from him. He speaks of honor, yet he is really only a child. He knows not honor. Yet he knows love. A decent man, and GW has seen fit to offer him up as a sacrifice to the Lord King. But benevolent as I am, it is an offering I cannot refuse. The gold, thee see’I must have the gold. It is mine by right, as all that I wish is mine by right. In front of his wench, the GW roster and the rest of the world, I will be forced to destroy this man. It is what I am bread to be: a destroyer. I love peace, and someday, I would like to have a place like this to escape to. Now though, it seems that a battle is always looming on the horizon for me. Freeze. Jetsui. And now Drachen. They are all weak in their way, but only one tries to make himself better. And Monday, he shall learn that to be a better loser, he shall be crushed by the Lord King. He simply stands in the way and knows not when to move. A shame, as there is such promise there? Pendragon pauses, his eyes burning, and looks up a Yoshindo. A single tear drips from the old man’s eye? Yoshindo: All things serve a purpose. What you take from the lad on Monday will only make him hungrier. In the end, he may come to thank you for teaching him ambition. He may even come to love you. I pray for you, my son. You walk a fine line between being a wonderful man and being a monster. You wish to gain the world, and I am sure that you will get what you wish for. I only hope that fate doesn’t bend you to the beast. You are the last warrior of the White, and decency cannot afford to lose her most powerful ally?. ~TO BE CONTINUED~
