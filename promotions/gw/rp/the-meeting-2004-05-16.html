The Meeting

16 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 16th, 2004 - DOWNTOWN DALLAS - .:We catch Takahashi Jetsui walking down a beaten up sidewalk on the left-hand side of a cracked, and preactically un-drivable road. Pot-holes litter the sad-excuse for a road as the dull, yellow glow from a couple odd street lights give the road an orange-shade as Takahashi proceeds down the street. On both sides of the cracked up road are equally cracked up buildings, that look as though they should have been condemned some time ago. As the trench-coat clad Takahashi walks down the sidewalk in his usual calm manner, and emotionless painted face, we can hear various noises coming from the buildings around Takahashi. Amongst the humid Dallas, Texas air, a sudden noise from the building that Takahashi is walking in front echoes through the street:. ARGGGH! SMASH! .:These noises are followed by what sounds like the collision of human skin on human skin. With each THWAPPING sound of skin on skin, the slap quickly turns into a scream from what seems to be a woman's voice. After a couple more THWAPS, followed by screams, the slapping ceases, and we only hear the apparent women sobbing, while a male voice shouts:. MALE VOICE: WHEN I COME HOME FROM A LONG DAY OF SUPPORTING THIS GODAMN FAMILY, I EXPECT SOME GODAMN DINNER ON THE TABLE! WHAT ARE YOU? FUCKING INCOMPITENT?! .:The male voice quickly no longer persists, and shortly after it stops, from the same house exits a man in a tank top with blood covering his hands. The man stumbles onto the front steps of the three-story low rise building and into the streets. Once out on the sidewalk, standing next to Takahashi, the man lifts a bottle in a paper bag to his lips and takes a long swig of the liquid inside. After chugging the liquid, the man wipes his hairy arm across his mouth, lets out a loud, gurgling burp, and scratches his large, round stomach with his bloodied hand:. .:The profusely sweaty man attempts to stumble down the sidewalk past Takahashi, but on his way through Takahashi's path, the fat, sticky man bumps into Takahashi, and immediately falls to the hard pavement, smashing his glass, paper-bagged bottle in the process. After a couple of stunned minutes, the visibly drunken man slowly makes his way to his feet, and looks down at the brownish liquid, amongst a group of scattered shards of glass. The man stares at the puddle of alcoholic fluids and jagged pieces of glass, the man turns to Takahashi:. MAN (Heavily slurring): You fucking moron! Look what you did! .:The man (barely standing, swaying back and forth as a matter of fact) points at Takahashi, while at the same time getting very close to Takahashi's face. Takahashi's facial expression finally changes as he is bombarded with a steamy, raunchy smell exiting the drunk man's mouth. Takahashi's face cringes as the man gets closer in, still pointing:. MAN (Heavily slurring): What are you going to do about what you just did boy? Or what, do you not understand the language that is coming from my mouth? TAKAHASHI: I understand perfectly, the words that are coming from your mouth, and I intend on walking in that direction, as soon as you are done here. .:The drunk man suddenly gets a look of confusement on his face, and takes a step back:. MAN (Heavily slurring): Well, burp! That wasn't exactly...the answer I was...blurb! Looking for. You know what, as a matter of fact... I think I'm going to kick your ass right now, nipper boy! TAKAHASHI: Before you go on and make such a hostile action towards me, I'm going to go ahead and let you in on a little something I know, a little something I know about men like you. You people, you hit those weaker than you, and get a rush of adrenaline out of it. Like you, for example, abuse your wife. She is nothing more than an outlet for your anger that has been building up in you since you were very young. You drink that alcohol, and suddenly feel that need for the rush of inflicting pain on another human, a weaker human, so you immerse yourself in the hate that constantly boils inside of you. .:The man, now even more confused-looking than before, lets out another gurgling belch, and again points in the direction of Takahashi:. MAN (Heavily slurring): So why the hell are you telling me this boy? You scared, you little pussy? TAKAHASHI: I tell you this because, I simply know the feeling. I may not fit into the same category as your slobbinly kind, but none the less, I FEEL the invigoration that you feel every single time that your hand makes contact with a woman's face. I FEEL the surge that you feel with every degrating obscenity you shout at your victim. But there is only one difference between us. MAN (Heavily slurring): Blurp! And what the fuck is that? TAKAHASHI: You will come back here in the morning with your head throbbing uncontrollably, and your stomach in an enormous knot from the drinking you have done the night before, and you will feel remorse for the actions you took on the previous night. You BEG for forgiveness, you PLEAD for acceptance. Me, I feel no such need to repent. .:The drunken man is now absolutely baffled, and at this point, if he wasn't so intoxicated, would most likely be scratching his head in an attempt to comprehend what is being said:. MAN (Heavily slurring): Well there sure were a whole lot of big words in there, so I'm not completely sure why I shouldn't just fight you right here! .:The man takes a very off-target swing at Takahashi, who quickly moves out of the way of the attempt, and allows the large man to hit the pavement. As the man hits the ground, every part of his body shakes upon impact, with waves sent across every section of fat on his body. The man hits his head on the ground very hard, and is knocked unconcious. At this point, Takahashi kneels down beside the man, and reaches into the pocket of the man's worn-out denim jeans. Takahashi retrieves the man's wallet from the pocket of the jeans, and proceeds on his way down the street in the direction Takahashi was previously going in:. .:Takahashi walks down the street, and after a few minutes finally stops on front of a building with a flickering neon sign over the front doors, and tinted front display windows. Once Takahashi stops, he reaches into the inside of his trench-coat pocket, and retrieves the small piece of paper he received from the Tranvestite a few hours previous. The sign on the building reads 'JOE'S BILLIARDS,' and after a few moments of holding the piece of paper, Takahashi enters the apparent pool hall. Takahashi steps through the front door of the small, one level building, and quickly become emersed in a sea of inch-thick cigarette smoke. Two pool tables lie to the left side of Takahashi, and on the other side lies a bar. At the bar, sitting in the thick fog are three Asian men, two puffing on cigarettes, one puffing on a cigar. The three men look at Takahashi, before getting up, and making their way towards Takahashi. The man in the middle appears to be the oldest, as his hair is graying, and he is developing a hunch:. ELDER MAN: Osashiburdesunee. .:Says the elder man, placing his cigarette in his mouth and bowing while saying it. The elder man straightens back up, fixes his suit jacket, and blows out a ball of smoke after bowing:. TAKAHASHI: I am not so glad to see you, Master Hikawa, as I wonder why you have tracked me down after so many years. .:The elder man, now revealed to be Master Hikawa, cracks a small smile, and takes another puff of his cigarette:. MASTER HIKAWA: Ah, I am glad to see you still refer to me as master, Jetsui-san. You and I have spilt a lot of blood on the same grounds, this pleases me that you still refer to me as master. TAKAHASHI: Yes, well, I am a busy man, so why don't you get to the reason you summoned me to this piss-smelling rat hole. MASTER HIKAWA: He, he, he, your fire still burns I see? Well, I have heard as much, but words tend to lose their original purpose when travelling so many steps from Dallas to Hiroshima. Jetsui-san, I have called you here for a very important reason, the reason being that I feel you are losing your way. You are turning careless as you immerse yourself in the ways of the western world. Public fighting, Jetsui-san? You are turning a profit from the beatings you place on men, and you are losing your soul because of it. TAKAHASHI: Soul? You dare summon me to talk about losing one's SOUL? Old man, you are the last one to be talking about the loss of SOUL. After all, you oganized the Hikawa Clan in the name of your fore-fathers. A murderous clan that tought its followers to kill and steal from the innocent, and set fire to their villages. You lead us in this pillaging, and now you stand before me, acting as though you are concerned for the welfare of MY soul? MASTER HIKAWA: You have become foolish Takahashi! You are using sporting arena's for the purpose of your evil doings. Millions have their eyes fixed on your every move, and are paying their money to watch you go down the path to self-destruction. As your teacher, I must advise you to stop this follishness! .:With the arguement heating up, Takahashi reaches over and grabs a bar stool, which he then tosses into the shelving behind the bar. The shelving holding up numerous liquor bottles, and keeping up a mirror behind it, shatters into several pieces. After the action, Takahashi still stands before the three men, who are doing nothing:. TAKAHASHI: I will not stop until my rage has been satisfied! I will not cease until I have shown everyone else what it feels like to go through what I have! You can't stop me, no one else can stop me! I fight for my own purpose, my days of fighting for YOUR purpose are over and done with. You are no longer my master, you are now NOTHING to me, and how dare you call me a FOOL. .:Takahashi pushes the old man in front of him to the floor, at the same time causing the other two men to be sent to the ground as well. Takahashi then reaches into his coat pocket, and pulls out his zippo and a cigarette. Takahashi places the cigarette in his mouth, and lights it. After lighting the smoke, Takahashi peers into the flame with wide eyes:. TAKAHASHI: You have come to save the soul of the wrong man, Hikawa. My soul was poisoned a long time ago, and especially now, there is no point in turning back. .:Takahashi stares at the flame a moment longer, and then tosses the zippo over the bar, into the pile of liquor and glass he created moments before. This almost instantly causes a fire to ignite, and Takahashi slowly exits the burning pool hall. In a sea of even more smoke than before, with the other three men scurrying to exit out the back door, Takahashi walks out the front, and into the humid Dallas nightlife:.
