Angel Standing By

6 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04-06-05 “When I last left the cameras, I had just told Tommy what he thought was the truth behind my disappearance seven years ago. Not to say that I didn’t tell him the truth, I merely told him a fragmented version of it. I told him what he needed to hear so that he would leave and never return. I did this not to betray him, but to ensure the safety of us both. I should have known better, though. Tommy wasn’t one to just back down, so instead of getting on the plane he did just the opposite. If I had only taken some other measure, perhaps he would have left. Maybe if I would have just told him everything” but I couldn’t risk it. Besides, hindsight is always twenty/twenty. But, ugh, I’m getting way ahead of myself. See, in order to understand everything in full, in order to understand the events of this past week, I have to take you back? way back. I’m talking seven years back, to the very reason I left Pittsburgh in the first place. The only problem is, I’m afraid. For once in my life, I am afraid, and it pisses me off. If I tell you what happened to me, you have to keep your word to never, ever repeat it. This could cost me my life and, contrary to popular belief, I value that above all else. I’m here to ask you for help. Whatever happened between us still stands, because that can never die. But I know you value my life just as much as I do?” He stared back at her, speechless, his round golden eyes filled with hurt and betrayal rushing through his veins. Words could not fall from his lips; he was shocked. It had been seven years since he last saw the face of this angel and that last glimpse had left a knife in his heart ever since. Even looking at her now, he could still see the tears streaming from her eyes as she looked at him, speechless as he is now. Her eyes had said enough, though. The thousand words she had wanted to scream at him cut into his flesh through those brown orbs of hers. At the moment, they were placed across from each other, he behind his desk and she standing in the doorway. There had not been a knock or any other sign of her entrance. Only her last words had informed him of her appearance and now he sat, baffled, staring into the face of this lost beauty. “Are you listening to me?” Her words sliced through his thoughts and brought him to his senses. Regardless of how much joy currently inhabited his being, he could never show it. He would not show it. Their eyes met, a decade of emotion rushing between them. Speech was impossible at the moment; his throat was chocking all of his words. Instead, he cleared his throat of the invisible debris and responded to the woman’s inquiry. “Yes, I am listening.” He paused, looking down at his hands, which were neatly folded together. With his next line calculated, his steady gaze caught hers once more. “My dear, I am sorry to hear about Tommy’s death, but you?” “He’s not dead?” she cut him short before he could finish, knowing what his last words would be: some kind of lecture about her actions and disappearance and false explanations to provide her with some reason why he could not help her. All the acting I the world could not hide that it was only his pride stopping him. “He’s not dead,” she repeated and then continued. “He’s only missing. They have yet to gather enough evidence to say he is dead.” The man shook his head. “No, my dear, but the case was practically closed when it started. The general conclusion for such an incident would be death. No matter how much denial you may be in?” Once again, she was swift to interrupt. “No! You aren’t listening to me. It’s not as though I would expect you to, you never did. I know he isn’t dead. I saw him with my own eyes and I have enough personal evidence to make that conclusion.” “What evidence is that, my dear, woman’s intuition?” The man chuckled at his own joke as she allowed a heated glare to befall him. Intimidated by her size and overall aura, he ceased his joviality and continued. “Regardless of how you feel, or what you “know,” you must realize that you have not seen him since and, for all you know, he could be dead.” A sigh escaped her pale lips, a palate usually painted black, but currently left barren of false color. In fact, her entire face lacked its usual complexity. It was quite apparent to him, at that moment, that she had spent the last few days in rush and confusion. He knew the clock was ticking, but he refused to tell her that he knew more than she thought. “They wouldn’t kill him,” she finally states, appeasing the man’s interest. “They’d rather just dangle him in front of me like a screwed up game of cat and mouse. They’re trying to lure me in and I can’t have that. My life is at a point where I can’t have these things haunting me anymore?” She stops talking and puts a hand to her forehead, her thoughts suddenly overwhelming her. “Ah, yes, of course” your career? and that title you carry with such pride.” His sarcasm leaks through every syllable and she looks at him in disgust. He only shakes his head and looks back down at the desk. “Look, after what you’ve done to me, I know that things can’t be the same and I’m not exactly sure that you and I can work together on this?” Her eyes widen in anger and she looks at the man with utter disgust. Flames burn in her eyes as she balls her hands into fists; she enters the room and then shuts the door. “You listen and you listen good” she says, finally finding words through her anger. She points a finger at him, her aggression shaking throughout her entire arm. “You are not innocent in this situation and you damn well know that. It was your actions that prompted me to leave. Your pride can’t cover that. I know that somewhere under that business suit and hard shell you feel guilt for everything you said and did to me.” She stops, looking him over and for the first time since entering his office, she realizes how much older he looks. The gray of time has swept over his former black tresses and wrinkles litter his tanned complexion. She shakes this digression from her mind and continues. “It’s not as though you would admit that, though. You were always a proud man. You wouldn’t help me then and I don’t even know why I’m asking you for help now.” She pauses to look at him, her aggressive look suddenly shifting to despair. “I guess things have just gone too far. I tried to do everything alone, but it just isn’t possible. Things have gone too far” and you’re my only hope.” She laughs, realizing how outrageous her next statement will appear. “I need you.” His eyes widen. In all his life he never thought he would hear these words from anyone, especially her. They look at each other, years of vacant compassion suddenly being exchanged between them. He slowly gets to his feet and looks the woman over and realizes how well she has matured. She was so much more stunning off the television than on and he was proud of this. Streams welled in his eyes, but he put up the dam to stop them. Finally he speaks. “You were always such a stubborn girl. If only you had realized that those were the words I had longed to hear all this time?” A small smile crossed her lips and she shrugged, still trying to organize her thoughts. The past few days had been a whirlwind of emotion and fear and it was oddly comfortable to be standing before this man. Shaking her head, she glanced out of his office window and was greeted by a view of the city that has plagued her dreams for years. “I know” but you must realize the hell you put me through in all of this?” she looks back at him, hoping that he may finally admit his faults. He nods and frowns slightly. “Yes, I realize that. My actions were not always called for, but you know my beliefs. I had no other choice, my career, my life; my everything would have been destroyed. You know that. It was a hard circumstance?” “Bullshit, you know it didn’t have to be a public matter.” “It would have been, my dear?” ??well then you know why I had to leave.” He slumps slightly, knowing he is defeated. “Yes, yes I know why you had to leave. But it still hurt, Radie. The scandal, and everything that followed it was rough.” He chuckled. “You were always such a problem. Your behavior, your band, everything?” They share a knowing glance, hers more pained than his. “Please, just skip the drama. I need to know if you’re going to help me.” She looked at him, her eyes pleading with him even though her face wore a different expression. He looked back at her, afraid to hurt her, but knowing that he would have to. “Radie, I’m sorry to tell you this, but I just can’t. Things are complicated in the office right now and if I help you with this, matters could get worse?” he pauses, his heart smiling even if her face showed so much pain and disappointment. His next words would mean the world to her. ??and besides, as you know how our saying goes, the rose is always more beautiful when observed alone.” Currently looking down at the floor with tear filled eyes, Aradia looked back at the man, her eyes wild with any emotion possible at that moment. It was code, one they had both agreed upon nearly a decade ago in case anything was amiss in the office. What could the matter be? She looked around, trying to figure out what could be out of place, but with the seven years since she had last seen his office, she did not recognize a thing. Then it dawned on her, there was only one thing that could be amiss. They were being watched. Suddenly anxious, she ran a hand through her raven hair. She flashed him a glance, an inquisitive one, and he nodded, knowing that she understood. He was proud to know she had not lost her intelligence. Breathing heavy, her mind began to spin. How did they think to come here? Did they not know this was the last place she would turn for help, or did they only realize that this was her only option? Whatever the case, her situation has worsened. Then his mouth moved without words and revealed everything. “They’re recording everything.” Putting a hand to her face, she felt as though everything were falling apart around her. Not only was Tommy in danger, but now this man was too. She would never forgive herself is something happened to either of them. Backing up slowly, she looked into his face and frowned. He sensed her fear and realized that he had given her the wrong impression. There was still hope, but how could he let her know without setting them off? Settling with his only option, he could only pray that she was still intelligent enough to understand code. “My dear, I am sorry to inform you of this news, but let me assure you that you have nothing to fear. Everything will be fine. I understand your pain. I know it. I know everything? With the emphasis of this last word, Aradia suddenly understood. Somehow, he knew everything. He knew every word she would have spoken to him only moments before, when she entered his office. This, was a massive relief, but there was still a problem: who will she turn to for help” “It’s alright, dear. I understand that you are scared. You’re strong, though. You must remember that. All your life you’ve been a fighter, but you’ve always doubted yourself. You can work through this on your own?” he pauses, praying that this clue will set her off. ??and if all else fails, there’s always Nathaniel.” She turns to him, still shocked that he would drop her so many hits in such an obvious fashion. But, then again, it is not as though whoever is listening would know their codes. They planted them when she was only a child. By saying she could always talk to Nathaniel, he was insinuating that there was still hope. There was still someone she could go to. “Stay with the federation. It will keep you safer than traveling alone. I know your anger and curiosity, I know you want retribution, but you can’t risk everything.” He stops, looking at his watch. Frantic, he looks at her once more in the eyes and smiles to her. “You should go. I’m so glad I got to see you once more. I’ve missed you.” She looks back at him, looking into his golden eyes with care. Slowly, she leans forward and kisses his forehead. Mumbling a thank you through a shortage of breath, she turns to exit the office, knowing that this is probably the last time she will ever lay eyes on him. She can’t continue to put him at danger and there is a good chance she already has. Just by being here, she has taken the risk of putting him in mortal danger. He knew this when she entered and she realized that now. If he was not satisfied with this risk, he would have asked her to leave. Suddenly, she felt more appreciation and respect for him than she ever before. She felt compelled. Before exiting the office, she stopped in the doorway and stood there for a moment. “I love you, daddy” Those final words touched both of them as though it were the first time they were said. In fact, it was the first time. He smiled and watched her leave, knowing where his fate would lead him. This was a sacrifice he was more than willing to make. He owed her that much. As she walked down the hall back towards the street, a man in a dark black overcoat rushed past, bumping shoulders with her. Turning, she meets his gaze and recognized it. Shuddering at his criminal smile, she stopped and watched him as he turned from her and entered her father’s office. Knowing from her father’s hidden messages that he was alright with what was to come next, she allowed a chocked sob to escape her throat before slowly exiting towards the street. Meanwhile, in his office, her father looked on as the man in the trench coat looked on at him. Both speechless, her father only smiled as he watched the man reach inside of his coat and pull out the shiny silver pistol. This was the sacrifice he had been prepared for all day and thankfully, he would be able to divert their tracks for at least a few days. Standing out on the street, Aradia listened closely, but heard nothing. Several minutes passed as she stood outside her father’s office, awaiting any sound at all. Concluding that the man had taken silent means to do his deed, she turned and began down the street, remembering every fond memory of the father she was about to leave behind. Suddenly, a gun shot rang through the block as startled screams emerged from behind closed doors. Putting a hand to her mouth, Aradia stopped in her tracks for a moment before realizing exactly what just occurred. Suddenly, fear overtook her once more and she began to run away down the street, unsure of where she was headed, but knowing she had to get away from the office. She had to get away from this city. She had put everyone at danger. Tommy may still be alive? “but her father was dead. ~*~*~*~ Inside of her father’s office, the man in the black trench coat lay motionless on the floor, blood forming in a pool around his lifeless corpse. Her father stood over him, a small handgun in his grasp. Shaking his head, he quickly wiped the gun off with a handkerchief and threw it at the intruder’s feet. Knowing his time was limited, he quickly made rounds through the room, collecting all of the man’s recording devices and throwing them into an open briefcase, which was already full of manila files. Closing the briefcase, he quickly picked it up off of the desk and made his way towards the door. If he could not help his daughter directly, then he would help her from a distance. As long as she thought he was dead, then she could not interfere. Looking down at the man’s form, her father snickered and began to exit, but without having the last word. “You’ve fucked with the wrong family.”
