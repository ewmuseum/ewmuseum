Ready For Action

22 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22/08/04 The scene for today is the interior of a lovely little caf?, some way away from the confinements of the GW Arena. Given something of a French decore to make the place seem more attractive to the lower-class citizens of the world, we find Rich Sherwin seated inside the establishment with the goon that he'd saved at an earlier moment in time. The two of them were seated at a table, drinking coffee with vastly different expressions on their faces. For the goon, it was a case of being completely scared and worried about when it was going to be OK to leave... and for Sherwin, it was the sweet satisfaction of knowing that intimidation upon the normal line of humans was not something that he lacked. Eager to find out a little more about everything, Sherwin opened conversation with the best opening that he could think of. '... Ok, so I've given you all the time in the world to compose yourself and think about everything that you're going to want to say. The only thing now is for you to spill everything about what happened to me... why exactly it happened to me because I don't see that whole; match excuse as being the cream of the shit... AND, I'm still waiting for you to tell me where this GW Arena is. I don't want you to hold my hand there... just tell me and I'll be on my way.' Sherwin sounded calm but his words echo'd deathly sentiments that nobody could possibly fathom. The goon gulped loudly and took a mouthful of his cup of coffee to wash it all down. He looked from side to side in the room before turning to Sherwin and beginning to give him the information that he so kindly requested. '... Ok,' the goon began, breathing loudly before composing himself once more and continuing, 'The fact is that you were taken for a number of reasons. Yes, one of the reasons was the fact that you'd lost that ultimate battle with Juan Gonzalez but the line of story stretches even further than that. To be honest, the boss has had his eye on you for quite a long period of time. You've been fighting for long enough... and to be honest, you've never really delivered into anything that would be respectable. I mean, you talked the talk almost all of the time... but when the push came to the shove, either you were never in the mood to give us anything... OR, you just weren't good enough to give us anything. You following what I'm saying so far?' The goon stopped for a few moments to look at the reaction that was forming on the face of Sherwin. For some reason, Rich seemed to agree and understand everything that was being thrown in his direction. The reason for such a reaction was unknown but for the time being, it looked as though both of these people were on the same page... or at least somewhat close to being on the same page. 'So, you took it upon yourselves to look over small portions of my career and take me away because I wasn't exactly doing as best as I could be? What about J Shit Diamond? What about the other people that were doing even worse than me? You're making it sound as though there was nothing I could do properly. In fact, you're making it sound as though everything that I did manage to win... I only won because of some stroke of luck or some element of surprise.' Sherwin informed the goon, his own tone of voice beginning to get deeper and more serious but his calm attitude for the moment remaining intact. Being in such a public place, one of the things Sherwin didn't want to do was cause any trouble with a ruck... at least, not until he knew a lot more about everything that went down. 'Well, to be honest... you were in the middle of the road. You were neither excellent; nor excessively bad. Regardless of what would have happened to you with regards to improvements, you were one of the only subjects that would have seen substantial benefits. Too many of the people that boss wanted to see just wouldn't have improved enough for any substantial difference to be seen. It seemed as though a lot of the prospects were at their physical and mental limits already... or very close to being at such a point. You though, you were neither too close nor too far from the limits.' the goon said aloud, almost impressed at the picture that he was painting for the subject of his boss. He let a few seconds slip pass for all previous information to settle before continuing, 'Unfortunately, there were two problems with the entire project... both of which would probably explain your current resentment toward the boss and his people. You see, the first issue was the whole part about you being alive. We couldn't operate on you unless you were one of the dead... and the second problem was your memory. You see, even now, we don't have the technology to manipulate your brain to produce false memories and everything until the point of your death was a memory. With those still intact, immediately after you came back, you took it upon yourself to wreak havoc and destruction in our central lab.' The goon became a little more relaxed as his tale began to unfold. He started to lose the element of fear and his tone of voice began to stabilize, moving from one that was shaky... to one that was completely understandable. Sherwin, for the better part of things, was just trying not to explode with the same rage that he did when everything started. Even though he was highly appreciative of these gifts and enhancements; nobody had given him the option of throwing away his life for something that could have been nothing more than a huge gamble. He felt the urge inside his heart, burning as though a towering inferno was bursting through. 'I still find it hard to understand why you took this decision upon yourself... and didn't just give me the choice of choosing. What stopped any of you from just asking if I wanted to be involved in your pathetic frankenstein project? Come to think of it, what gave you the right to treat me as though I was a test subject?' 'We did this because it would have been the first step in a long line for our family. As you were a successful subject of our creation, we were intending to spread the wealth across our men and have every single one of them evolve to the state at which you currently are. Sadly, with your uncontrollable rage... you destroyed every little piece of data that was left. You trashed the project within fourty-five seconds of returning to this world.' The goon stated, getting the actual objective of the project out... something that Sherwin had absolutely no idea about. 'What would you have done if I died? What would you have told the world if a GW athlete had died? Would you have even said anything?' Sherwin barked, the consequence of this tragic action now rearing a head that was uglier than even he would dare imagine. The goon looked down at the table and shrugged his shoulders, a reaction implying that they probably would have just thrown away the body and let the authorities find something that was even worse. Sherwin pushed himself away from the table and began to pace away, turning around to come back before turning away and doing the same thing over and over. '... Should I just assume that you would have left me to rot in some horrific location?' he yelled, forcing the civilians in the cafe to remove themselves from their tables and exit the building. 'In the event of your ultimate demise... we would have simply done something to make everything Ok. You can't expect us to simply leave a tattered body for somebody to find. You can't expect anyone to just suddenly find a body that had been stolen from the cold storage of a morgue. This isn't the movies of some silly television show where something is found and only a few questions are asked. If they'd found your body in a such a condition... the damage to everyone would have been titanic.' the goon tried to argue, attempting to reason with Sherwin with a solution to what may have happened if everything did go wrong. Unfortunately for him, Rich didn't care a single bit about the good or the bad of this event any more. As far as he was concerned, these people had tried to play God with his life. Sure, they'd been able to come through this time... but what about anyone before him? Had there been people in the exact same situation? People who were killed for the sheer benefit of a cheap enhancement? 'You've told me more than I would ever care to know about this... sick and disgusting project that you put me through. I don't care about the past anymore, you've left thoughts and impressions that I'm never going to be able to leave behind. You have caused more grief to me than I could ever imagine. Not satisfied with the idea that I was dead... now I have to live with this, THIS... bullshit. Look, I only need to know one more thing from you... just one more thing. Where the Hell do I have to go to find this GW arena? I'm supposed to be there for something of a re-debut. I'm supposed to be there to take on a couple of people that were... promoted with me from some other organization.' Sherwin said to the goon, his own tone of voice slowly starting to simmer down, the ideas and concepts behind this entire ordeal beginning to eat away at his mind. 'As far as I remember, the GW arena isn't all that far away from here. If you wait outside for a cab, I'm sure you'll find somebody that could take you straight to the location that you're looking for.' the goon responded, backing away from the table before removing himself from his chair. With this moment of mental insecurity, could this second be the only opportunity that this pathetic little man has for an escape? Could this be the only way that he will now see the light of tomorrow? The goon turned and began to walk away but the pounding of a fist against a table caught his attention. '... Now that you've introduced me to such a high level of pain, isn't it only fair if I do the same to you? Perhaps even give you a little more to show you how much I care?' Sherwin said, his tone of voice dropping to something that was sadistic by all means of the word. The expression on his face changed to something that was truely representative of insanity. The only problem facing him now was the condition of his mind when Evolution came to pass? Knowing that he had to do this match-thing, would he be able to keep himself calm and composed? Or would the beast from within strike out at his opponents? One way to find out... stay tuned~!
