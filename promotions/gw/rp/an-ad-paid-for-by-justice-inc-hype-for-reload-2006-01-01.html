An ad paid for by Justice Inc!!!! Hype for Reload

1 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/1/06 [The ad opens up with the rather sizeable, bright, orange letters “Justice Incorporated”, closely followed by their popular (at least to themselves) slogan “Justice’for a price” in fancy joined-up lettering, all of which on the backdrop of a super-imposed ten dollar bill.] Cheesy voice over guy: Justice Incorporated. Justice’for a price! [The ad then cuts to our two loveable superheroes in what appears to be the offices of their headquarters. Warrior is half-sat on a desk and Blind Rage is stood right beside him.] Blind Rage: I’m Blind Rage? Warrior: And I’m Warrior? Together: We’re’JUSTICE INC!!!!! [The ad cuts to Blind Rage walking in some undisclosed park on a glorious day.] Blind Rage: If you’re having squirrel problems? [The ad cuts to Warrior in the same park kicking a red-furred squirrel up the arse with venomous force and great delight. After doing so he turns to the camera, raises a thumb and produces and incredibly cheesy smile. The ad then cuts to the docks were we see Warrior walking along a pier on a dull and dreary day] Warrior: Or if you’re having pirate problems? [The ad cuts Blind Rage whacking a pirate around the back of the head with a plank of wood. After doing so he looks at the camera, raises a thumb and produces an equally cheesy smile. The ad cuts back to the Justice Inc office. Where we see Blind Rage and Warrior in exactly the same positions as we saw them in before.] Together: Then call Justice Inc TODAY!!!! [A phone numbers appears on screen.] Together: On [detail withheld]...Thats [detail withheld] [The ad cuts to, well, another ad]
