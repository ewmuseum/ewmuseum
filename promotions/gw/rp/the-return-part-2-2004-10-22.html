The Return: Part 2

22 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22nd October 2004 Choices... Humanity's primary distinction is the freedom to choose. We as human beings do not have a fixed nature, or essence, as other animals do; each person makes choices that create his or her own nature. Choice is therefore central to human existence, choice is inescapable, even the refusal to choose is a choice. In everyday life we have to make choices, how we dress in the morning, how we go to work, and how we interact with people. However our freedom of choice does entail commitment and responsibility. Because we are free to choose our own path, we must accept the risk and responsibility of following our commitment wherever it leads. The point of me rambling like this is because over the past month I have had to make choices about my life. These choices only had 2 paths, there were no intertwining roads, it was one way or the other. The first path led to nothing, nothing but a dark depressing place, a place where there was no escape. The second path led back to the GW, a place of hope and prosperity... As you already know I chose the second path, however I was only a few steps away from taking the other. For over a month I lay in bed simply staring at the ceiling letting the world close in on me and not caring. I let my wife become distant from me, it almost destroyed my marriage but I neither had the energy nor the drive to do anything about it. I have never in my life felt as hopeless and empty as I did over that month. It took me to the brink of my sanity and it is a place I never want to go back too. Nevertheless I have to face that possibility I mean there is going to be a time when I become injured again perhaps even worse than I was this time round. I need to realise that it is not the end of the world, that it is not the end of my career, and that I will fight on. We have a very limited time on this place we call earth and we cannot spend it threating and worrying about what is going to happen tomorrow or the next day or the day after that, it may sound cheesy but we have to live each day as it comes and that is what I intend to do. For example in a few days time the GW approaches Dark Rituals, an event with specialised matches that will test the GW superstars to their very limits. I am taking part in a match titled Eternity's Purgatory, a match in which I have to not only pin an opponent but also make them submit as well. Now this is going to be a tough match there is no doubt about that but I have come a long way over the past month and I don't intend going home from Dark Rituals without my Crusierweight Title... 'I am standing outside the gym now honey.' I was on my mobile phone to Rachel, who since our reconciliation had become my motivator. She would ring me up every half and hour making sure I was serious about returning to the GW. She knew I was serious and knew when I had set my goal there would be no stopping me reach it, however she felt it was her duty as my wife to keep me going. 'Okay sweetheart,' Rachel said with a satisfied tone, 'Now you go in there and you show Peter that you are serious about this. He has lost a lot of faith in you since the incident with the Vergomovz, he thinks you have given up all hope. So show him that you mean business, you take care honey and I will see you later tonight. Love you.' 'I love you too gorgeous, I will see you later, bye.' I disconnected the call and placed the mobile back in my kit bag. I began jogging on the spot, trying to pump myself up. The truth was I had been stood outside the gym the passed 20 minutes. I had tried many a time to go in but every time I reached for the handle to open the door I felt my body freeze. I knew how ridiculous I was being, it had only been a month since I had been in a gym, yet here I was standing outside my local gym in the freezing cold October air frightened as a kid on their first day of school. The look of disappointment Pete had given me at the hospital only a few days ago was repeating over and over again in my head. I hated that image, I despised that image, Pete was my best friend and I had treated him like a piece of shit I had found on the bottom of my shoe. He had every right to look at me like that, he had every right to hate my guts, but he had given me this chance so obviously he must have had some faith in me. I couldn't disappoint him not again... Defiantly I pushed the gym door open and stepped in. I walked passed the weight machines with my head held high; I had a purpose in my step as I pushed through a second set of doors into the fight training area. As the doors slammed behind me I took a look around, the place was brightly lit and crowded with fighters, spookily it looked like the academy I had visited when I first started my journey back when I was 18. I saw Peter standing at the ringside looking at me with a face like stone. I chose to ignore his stern look and walked over giving a few nods to some of the fighters I knew in the gym. I eventually reached Pete and stood next to him. Now Pete stands at about 5 foot 8 whereas I stand at 6 foot 1, now you would have thought I was the bigger man but not that day, Pete even though considerably shorter than me seemed to stand tall. 'Hey Pete, how is it going?' I said trying to lighten the mood and sticking my hand out, Pete didn't greet it with his. 'Listen Adam!' Pete said in a very stern yet angry voice, 'I am here today as a favour to your wife, Rachel, who seems to believe that you have made a miraculous recovery and have become responsive emotionally again...' 'Yes I have Pete, I...' Pete held his hand up as if to say he hadn't finished. 'Forgive me Adam when I say it is hard to believe that this is true.' 'But it is true Pete...' Again the hand went up. 'Over the past month Adam, you have not contacted me once. In fact the only person I have talked to is Rachel who every time I spoke to her was always on the edge of tears. Nevertheless she always made excuses for you like you weren't up to it or you had just step out. All absolute bullshit reasons but I didn't want to test Rachel as I didn't want to add more pressure to her life. Do you even realise what you put Rachel through?' 'Yes I do and I will never stop apologising to her for it. You know I would never intentionally hurt her Peter, I love her.' 'I know the old Adam Myers would never hurt Rachel but the Adam Myers I saw at the hospital, I don't know.' 'Come on Pete can't we just go back to the way we were. I don't like this atmosphere between us.' 'Now let me make this crystal clear for you Adam,' Pete said stepping forward and looking at me still with the same stern look, 'I am not here as your friend or as your lawyer, in fact any connection me and you have is none existent until you prove to me that you have changed and that you are the Adam Myers of old.' 'Well I am ready to prove myself Pete, where do you want me to start, weights, cardio?' 'The ring!' Pete said pointing to the ring situated next to us. 'The ring?' 'You not ready?' Pete said with a told-you-so look on his face. 'Just sudden that is all.' 'You know what is my favourite memory of you Adam?' 'What?' I said hoping it wasn't the night in Mexico where I had got so drunk I was convinced I was a Mexican Bandit. 'It was the first time I saw you in that ring at the academy back in England. You were only a kid of 18, with no wrestling experience, however when that trainer told you to step in that ring and face that mountain of a man, you had no fear. You dropped your kit bag and stepped in that ring. Sure you got the shit kicked out of you but you showed guts that day. You showed that you were willing to keep going because you wanted to succeed so much. That is what I want to see today Adam, I want to see that willingness to succeed. Can you show me that Adam? Can you?' There was a moment of silence where Pete just stared at me and I stared back. A small smile crossed my lips, of course Pete couldn't see it behind my mask but I simply dropped my kit bag and slid into the ring. I bounced up and down preparing myself; I looked at Pete and nodded, still with the same stern look on his face he pointed behind me. I swivelled around just in time to see a huge fat arm charge me down to the ground. It wasn't long till I bounced back up and finally saw my 'sparring partner' a huge man weighing in at possibly over 400lbs, he was obviously a brawler by the force he hit me with the clothesline. I called him on to charge again because no brawler can resist a charge. He charged again but this time I was able to duck and smash him across the back of the head with a sidekick. This caused him to stumble but he turned around in time for me to give him a standing handstand flip kick right onto his chin. He stumbled back to the ropes and quickly I followed up with a simple running missile dropkick sending him flying out of the ring. I fell to my knees it had been a while since I had a match and my stamina was something I needed to work on. I looked across at Pete who was now smiling from ear to ear. Behind my mask I smiled back. 'Now that is the Adam Myers of old, although the old Adam Myers wasn't that unfit. That is obviously something your trainers will need to work on. Speaking of which...' Pete turned to the doors just opposite the ring. The doors swinged open and out stepped my old trainers Juan Diaz from my Mexico days and Bruce Uemura from my Japanese days. My smile grew broader as I looked at Pete again. He was still smiling and obviously knew what I was thinking; he winked and gave me a nod. Diaz and Uemura made their way to the ring as they did I heard Pete say... 'Gentlemen, make this man a champion!'
