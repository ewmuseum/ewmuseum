Salvation

19 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/19/04 Dressed in black leather pants, black leather boots and a black leather vest without an undershirt 'El Mariachi' rode down highway 77 from Desoto heading back towards Dallas, Texas on his 2003 Harley Davidson Screaming Eagle Deuce at a speed of 70 MPH. 'Believe in my heart and confess with my mouth...I never knew it was that simple to fix things in my life...had I only known...' Juan Gonzalez is caught up in an uproar after a revival service that he attended last night in Desoto. At this service the GW wrestler gave his live to God. A pivotal point in 'El Mariachi's' life that now seems to have affected him deeply. 'Everything that I've ever done...it's been foregiven...all the murderers, the lies...the fornication...' At the service Juan gave his life to Jesus and it seemed that it not only affected his personal life but also his profesional life. At the service 'El Mariachi' planned to use his time to be the best person he could be, to be a living witness so that others could see him as giving his life over to God, the problem with that was that 'El Mariachi' was a bad guy on television and he had to find a way to make himself go over as a good guy. 'Will the fans support me? What can I do? How will I do it?' 'El Mariachi' pulls over to an old rugged gas station and removes his black helmet, revealing his new wrestling mask, a solid black mask with a cross on the back. The area is dead, nothing but dessert with one human being in sight, an old overweight man that looks like he lived during the Civil War. 'We dont like ya kind here boy...we aint to kind on them Mexaykans!' 'I'm sorry sir, I just want to get some gas...' 'We aint got nothing for that there stolen bike ya got...who you stole that from?' 'It's something that I bought sir...with my first paycheck from Global Wrestling...' 'Oh so you one of dem rasslers huh, dem luchy-dores, dem high fly-n mexaykans?' 'I guess you could call it that...' Just then the old man breaks a beer bottle over a bar and charges at Juan. 'I dont wan't any trouble sir...' <bThe old man lunges at Juan and misses, falling on his face. Just then a beautiful young woman runs out, her face is smudged in dirt, her shirt is torn and shes wearing some cut off pants that have been converted to shorts and an old pair of brown boots. 'Daddy! What are you doin? I'm sorry sir...my dad, he's drunk...' 'I can tell, I'm sorry mam, I didn't want any trouble...I'll be going now.' Gonzalez makes a move to leave but gets stopped by the lady. 'No please don't go...stay please...I'll give you gas for free. I need your help...' 'With what, what can I possibly do?' 'Get me out of here...as far from here as you can...my dads crazy he gets drunk every day, my brothers are just as bad but they're at work right now. I'm practically a slave, cooking, cleaning...please!' Juan stares at the beautiful girl and motions for her to hop on, he revs up the bike and spins dust all over the place as both drive away in the bike. 'So what's your name?' 'Lucy Beaman...' 'Pretty name.' 'What's yours...' 'Just call me Juan.' Lucy holds on to Juan Gonzalez as if he were her saviour, as if he had saved her. They ride off down high 77 headed towards Dallas, Juans a new man and he seems to have a new woman.
