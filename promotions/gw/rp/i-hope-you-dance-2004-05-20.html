I Hope You Dance

20 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/20/04 It's a juicy afternoon in the great city of Dallas. Juicy is a strange way to describe Dallas but indeed it is. J-Gonz rolls his chair around looking at the crime below, the gangs raping girls, the kids doing drugs and the bum stealing from the gas station. It's a great city for J-Gonz knowing that he is running the biggest crime network in the city, since the days of the KKK. J-Gonz turns to face his secretary Maria De Luz, wearing a white suit with a red vest and a red bow tie and a large cigar in his mouth. Gonz runs his hand over his shaved head as he nods and the cigar drops ashes. 'Global Wrestling is yet to find out really who J-Gonz is mammi, they have seen an idiot running around trying to fit in and I've gotten laughed at, I've gotten booed and I've gotten thrown around, but it's going to change and it's going to change now!' Clearly not knowing what J-Gonz is talking about Maria pulls out a note pad and crosses her leg tugging at her long black silky skirt. 'Senor Gonz, you always seem to intrigue me, let me take some notes down as to what exactly you mean.' J-Gonz nods. 'You see I tried to hide from what I was, I tried to gain respect from the stupid Americans and I tried make a mark but now I realize that I can't be that trash they want me to be, I'm me, I'm El Mariachi!' 'Si, the most dangerous man I've ever met...' 'Well I was taken as a joke, one that really had no purpose here in Global Wrestling and at one time I was going to leave, just go to Michigan and run my game from there. But then it hit me, I'm one bad mother...you know where I'm going with that. I'm a dangerouse man and I need to show people that!' J-Gonz moves away from the desk walking around his penthouse office in his white suit and looking out the window at the city below. Maria still writing down notes waits as J-Gonz continues. 'The crime in this city is run by me and is controlled with by me, if someone wants to kill someone they ask for permission, if someone wants to sell drugs they ask my permission! I'm J-Gonz and I'm dangerous!' 'That you are Mr. Gonzalez, but tell me what are your plans?' Gonzalez goes over to a chart that has a plan layed out. Two charts depicting the crime rate in Dallas, another a picture of the Global Wrestling Champions and finally a picture of a money bag. 'I need to find a way to have this crime, get me title shots and in turn money! We're losing profits and I need to get some gold around my waste. Drugs and hoes can go a long way, but I'm in the wrestling biz now and gold is what talks.' Maria moves away from the seat and closer to the chards scribbling down the notes as quickly as she can. Gonzalez moves his cigar out of his mouth and smashes it against the wall. He fixes his suit and claps his hand summoning in a very large man. Covered in tatoos the Mexican man walks in sporting a black tank top and a pair of dress style khakis. 'This is my plan Maria! This is what is going to get the job done! Paco! You see Global Wrestling wants to tame the lion in me and has not let me break out of my shell. They want to make me TV friendly but that friendly nature is over! I'm Mexican, and I'm bad! I have the accent that I've hidden, I have the cholo look and I'm gonna get down...esse! I'm gonna dance! I'm gonna smoke and I'm gonna cheat!' Paco crosses his arms and nods in appreciation. 'So look at it like this esse, I'm from the dirty south and I'm gonna bust em up esse! Donny J and Brick won't know what hit em homes! And Train...well Paco will take care of him! Look at it like this esse, Train...I hope you're ready to dance homes, cause este tigre loco is gonna go rolling homes!' Paco nods as the scene fades to outside the office buidling, the crime infested city run by J-Gonz.
