After the brawl

15 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/15/04 It was bedlam down in the arena. A brawl had broken out between three men: Brick, Deacon Holmes and Train. Road agents, security, referees and even Global Wrestling superstars had rushed down near the end of the program to break up. Before long, the entire area outside the ring was filled with staff looking to separate them. Eventually, they would be separated and dragged to the backstage area, but not without shouting threats and obscenities at one another. Ely Braxton, Jesse Briggs and Logan Creed had dragged Brick off, while the Upper Echelon and the MOB dragged Deacon and Train, respectively. * * * It was the second edition of Deaconpalooza, that rare edition of Evolution when Deacon Holmes' constant grandstanding more or less dominated the whole show. Earlier in the evening, Deacon defended both his titles in what were more or less squash matches designed to boost his ego. Deacon's final stunt of the evening involved him announcing his retirement, his self-induction into the Global Wrestling Hall of Fame, and return to active competition--all within the span of twenty minutes. Of course, the party was brought to a halt by acting GW president Christina McCoy, who promptly reminded Deacon, 'you know as well as I do that you may not, I repeat, MAY NOT, nominate and place yourself into the hall. That's done by the people.' There was no problem getting the people behind him, as Deacon did have a rather impressive fan base. The fans chanted 'LET HIM IN, LET HIM IN!' Christina then reminded him that he also needed to be nominated for induction into the Hall by a member of the roster, one of his peers. She then mischievously hinted that there was someone he could ask. Deacon referred to his stable mate, the current International champion John Zero. But Christina had someone else in mind: Brick. Brick was out there because, for one reason, of a casual announcement that Deacon had made. It concerned him being in the running for the lead role in the upcoming Luke Cage movie. Now there was certainly no love lost between the two. Theirs had been a very intense feud that had run almost a year. But this recent announcement had really gotten under Brick's skin. This was about more than just ego. In Brick's mind, Deacon had intruded on something personal to him. For years, Brick had been trying to get involved in the making of the Luke Cage movie. He'd been a fan of the character since he was a kid. Now, just after hearing he was in the running, Deacon made his announcement. And it wasn't even a big announcement, either. It was just something he said, casually as an aside. And that was what really got to Brick. He let Deacon know as much when he was in the ring with him. Then, just as something was ready to happen between the two, Train showed up. Most was what he said from the stage was a tirade insulting Brick. This was the last thing Brick needed at that point. Just as he was ready to take his anger out on Deacon Holmes, here was Train throwing gasoline on the fire. Much of what Train was saying about him made Deacon laugh. But then something changed. In the middle of insulting Brick, Train started to insult Deacon. His tirade went on until Brick finally cut him off and challenged him to come down to the ring. Train instantly answered the challenge and made his way to the ring. But just as Brick was ready for a fight, Deacon leapt to the outside and dropped on top of Train. That’s when the brawl started. * * * “Punk motherfucker!” Brick shouted as Ely and Jesse dragged him through the backstage area, doing their best to restrain him. “Who, Train or Deacon?” “Both!” It was right then that Christina walked right up to them. She was already on her way back to the arena to preside over the contract signing between Ice and Josh. “Brick, are you okay?” “Yeah, I’m okay. I’d be a lot damn better if I were stomping Deacon and Train into wine.” “I should have prepared for something like that. Damn, that Train is a troublemaker!” “This is a surprise to you?” “Look, Brick, you need to calm down.” “No, what I need to do is beat the shit out of Deacon and Train.” “You already did that.” “No, I didn’t. I was stopped before I could. Next time, it’ll probably take more than the whole staff to stop me. Maybe the whole staff and the National Guard!” “Look,” she said, “I have to go and take care of the contract signing between Ice and Josh. Ely, do you think you can take him down to the Dark Horse to cool off?” “Yeah, sure. I got a bit o’ coolin’ down to do, myself.” “We all do,” Jesse said. “Fine. I’ll see you all later.” As Christina walked off to the arena in order to maintain some order and to moderate over an important part of the Wrestlecade card, the four men made their way to the exit. It had been a long night for all of them and they were now looking to unwind. And if a night at the Dark Horse didn’t do it, nothing would.
