The Chronicles of Pendragon...Part 2: Clubbing? Wenches....

11 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-11-2004 The Lord King. Teamed with a wench. Seems that fate is indeed a cruel muse to deal with. All of the macho ideals of medieval chivalry that our King holds to would certainly be more of a hindrance in the ring with a female as a partner. This was indeed a problem. One man believed he knew the solution to this particular, “dilemma”. “The Bastard” Ben Davis. As one of Pendragon’s only friends, this fellow has a special interest in seeing his pal save face and claim victory in all the things that he does. Just cause he is a bastard, it doesn’t mean that he wont take care of his friends! “Hmm?” Always calculating, “The Bastard’s” mind was hard at work, trying to come up with the right answer. He sits alone at a bar, drinking a Coor’s Aspen Edge. Low carb lager for this kid’It’s all he drinks as of late. Used to be an import kind of fellow, but after this extremely light beer, he lacks the stomach for such brews. As he takes a pull from the bottle, the man in the gray sleeveless “Everlast” weightlifting shirt scans the bar and his eyes light up?. “This would be prefect! Oh my! My friend could learn the art of dealing with the ladies from a first hand pro!” He nods to himself as he sets the bottle on the bar and drops a five beside it’Meaning to or not, as he leaves he exclaims out loud “HOT DAMN! I’m a friggin GENIUS!!” The few patrons of this early hour kinda stare at the insane bit of outburst from the pony tailed fellow, but soon he is out the door and their drinks call them back to reality? *********FLASH*********** Thy Lord King’s master bedroom. Looking a bit ill at ease, Venneman Pendragon sits on the edge of his bed. Clad in his golden mail shirt, sword belted to his hip, he is every bit the avatar of a medieval king. The troubled look upon his brow is not customary, nor are the grunts and sighs that come from his large walk in closet? “What is it thee seeks, my friend” Why dost thou vex mine closet so, Benjamin?” So that is what is going on??The Bastard? is in the closet’But why? “Damnit Venn! Aintcha got any regular clothes” We have gotta get out and work on your interpersonal skills, my man! And all you have in here is countless pairs of leather pants and innumerable types of armored shirts’Hell, what the Christ are these things?””” What appear to be swimming trunks fly from the closet and land on the bed’These are no ordinary trunks though. These are made of a very fine laced chain mail. Ouch. Seems like these would pinch the scrotum to say the least’Pendragon picks them up, but before he can reply, bright orange arm band floats fly from the closet and land in his lap. He turns a bit red, but speaks? “Ahem’Were man made to swim, he would come equipped with scales and fins. I have neither, but I do have the common decency to cover mine manhood when I take a dip?” “And the damned arm bands?” Comes the reply from the closet? “I am able to swim not?” “Christ man! We’ll have to work on that too!” The rummaging goes on as Pendragon pushes the mail trunks and the arm beds beneath the bed. Finally, Ben comes out of the closet. In his hand he holds a white shirt upon a wooden hanger. Rather than buttons, it has a leather lace that runs up the front, and the sleeves appear to be the billowing variety. “This is the best I could do, man. This might actually pass for a modern day shirt with your build. Take off the armor and put this on?” He hands the shirt to the befuddled looking King, and crosses his arms? “Is all this really necessary” I have never in mine life had a tough time adapting when the battle haze drops’Everything is just?.Cold.” His eyes plead with the smaller man, but Ben shakes his head? “You are teamed with a female, and they just aren’t the same species as you, pal. You gotta know how to treat em to get what you want from em. In your case, you want a victory. I can show you how to go about doing that. My victory is a bit different than the one you are thinking of, but they can be hand in hand if you learn to do things right. It’s all about how you speak?” Pendragon shakes his head, but he changes shirts and shrugs?“Alright’Where is it that thee and I are to go in search of mine interperternational skills?” Ben slaps his head??Interpersonal. And we are gonna hit some clubs my boy!” Pendragon shudders?“I have been hit with clubs on more than one occasion’I would rather it not happen this night.” Ben can’t help but chuckle a little. It is really like this Pendragon fella is from another time??Clubs are places people go to hang out, Venn. We are gonna visit these places. Hit some clubs means that we are gonna go to some nightspots. It doesn’t mean a beat down or a clubbing, although clubbing is another term?” Pendragon just stares at his friend?“Thou art quite the strange fellow with thy weird and nonsensical talk?” “Never mind man. Let’s go’Oh, and the sword is a no-no in clubs.” Pendragon looks a bit unnerved by this talk. Ben just stares at him and finally the King unbuckles his sword and places it upon a mantle at the foot of his bed? “I shall return, mine most obliging ancestors?” He plants a kiss upon the hilt, rises and strides from the room. Ben shakes his head and laughs, and then follows his friend. *********FLASH********** The shot switches to what is indeed a club. Ben and Venn are leaning on the bar, drinkless? “What service is this” Yonder barkeep needs to hasten to out beverage needs?.Now!!!” Pendragon exclaims? “Calm down, buddy. You just gotta wait your turn. Don’t be so nervous?” He says this last with a chuckle, and Pendragon glares at him. Ben smiles and at last, the bartender comes by? “I’ll have an Aspen Edge”.And my friend here?.” “Rum. Strongest ye have. Make it a double”.” The bartender leaves and soon comes back with the beer and a glass filled with an amber liquid. “It’s Bacardi 151. Strongest we have”.” Pendragon glares at the man??My lord? he finishes before scampering off. Pendragon smiles and smells the liquid. Ben stares at him and shakes his head? “Fella must have just known royalty, eh buddy?” Ben asks with a sardonic smile? “Aye. Mine fame is widespread, is it not?” Pendragon smiles and knocks the drink back with a fluid motion. Rather than a grimace at the harshness of the alcohol, Pendragon smiles and puts the glass on the counter?“An excellent mix. Why, “twould nearly rival mine home label?” He looks around as the warmth of the rum seeps into his belly, giving him almost an instant calm, as strong alcohol can do. “So this is where I am to learn the magic of the wench, my friend?” Ben shakes his head as he responds??First off, wench just isn’t gonna cut it, bro! Ladies don’t like to be called wench. In fact, there are a few words that will mean instant turn off if you call them as such?” Pendragon chuckles?“If not wench, what then” Lassies have always responded to such speak’And what are these other words to avoid?” And then, under his breath?“Wenches not liking to be called as what they be?” Ben looks around and lowers his voice??Alrighty, man. Avoid these words’Bitch. Dyke. Butch. Cunt. Especially that last one. Nothin gets ya smacked quicker than callin a bitch a cunt’Er’A female a cunt. Stick to words like baby and beautiful if you have to liken them to something. And much of the art of speaking to women is in the tone and how you carry yourself. You gotta be confident?.” Pendragon cuts him off with a question?“A cunt” What is that? I do not know the word!” “Just know that you don’t call em that.” “Ok’Carry on?” “Right. Be confident. I don’t think you’ll have a problem with that. Do not however, command them. Ladies do what they will. You have to coax them to do what you want. Observe, my man?” Ben takes a swig from the beer, looks down the bar to where two fairly beautiful women sit. The kind of woman that men are afraid to approach because they just KNOW they are gonna get shot down. One blonde, one brunette. The good ones always travel in pairs. He motions for the bartender??Say man, what are the ladies at the end of the bar drinking?” The bartender looks and smiles??The blonde is sipping on Stolichnaya, and the brunette is keeping it simple. Captain Morgan’s and diet coke.” Ben smiles??Excellent. Make up one of each drink. Give me the Capn’s and Coke, and my man here the Stoli.” The bartender goes to his task and Ben looks at Pendragon??You get the more sophisticated one. Just wait here till I give the signal. Watch how I carry myself. Learn. When you get there, stay kinda quiet and observe some more?” The bartender comes back with the two drinks and Ben grabs one and smiles at Pendragon as he saunters down the bar. Head up, shoulders wide, he seems to know what he is doing. He reaches the women and seems to introduce himself. The three share a laugh and Ben hands the drink to the brunette. He leans on the bar, and the two women chuckle some more. He glances at Pendragon and the blonde woman nods her head. Pendragon takes this as his que and starts over with the drink? To himself, Pendragon mumbles lowly?“Confidence. Call them not wenches. Be funny’Good Christ! So many rules to this engagement!” He reaches the woman and smiles. He bows slightly and hands the drink towards the blonde?“My lady’Thy beauty is rivaled only by the openness of thine smile’I hope thee will accept this small offering from thy humble servant.” The blonde’s mouth opens slightly and she smiles, taken by his debonair and accent. She sips at the drink and smiles warmly??You have chosen well?” Ben is flabbergasted too. This wasn’t what he expected. Pendragon stands there pretty relaxed however, and hides the determination not to “fuck this up” with a wry grin. He raises his hand to the barkeep and soon a double rum sits before him. Ben takes a pull from the beer and smiles at the brunette??I find it hard to believe that the two of you are here alone! And even more hard to believe that we are the first fellas to talk to you this whole night!” The brunette frowns??It’s always like this when we go out. Either people leave us alone, or we get hit on by creeps who are looking to get laid?.Like we are pieces of meat.” “Meat. Yeah, me and Venn here know all about that’Aint that right, Venny?” Pendragon knocks back his second double and smiles. He answers not, however. The name irks him to no end, and “The Bastard” knows it? The blonde looks at Pendragon’s empty glass??What was that, if you don’t mind a girl asking?” Pendragon clears his throat???Twas a strong rum, my lady. More potent than the liquid that thee ingests’Fit only for a wench?..Ahem’Aye. A bit more to mine taste than vodka.” Ben almost laughs and the woman regards Pendragon with a slight frown? “Whatever you say, big guy?” And then, under her breath, “Big bastard almost called me a wench’wench” Who talks like that?” Pendragon shifts a bit uncomfortably. He doesn’t like to cow tow to anyone, even when the most precious box in all the world would be on the line. Ben and the Brunette watch as he settles himself and finally Ben speaks??Heya ladies’Whattya say we hit the dance floor. He may not look it, but Venn there can really shake it when he wants to?” The women nod and head to the floor. Ben walks close to Pendragon and whispers to him??If you can dance with em, you got em! And I’ve seen ya fight. You should have no problem with this?” “Aye. Especially if the music be a waltz!” “I don’t think that is what is playin old buddy.” And so they walk out onto the floor and a dance mix fills the air. Neither man really likes the music, with Pendragon preferring a more classical sound and Ben liking Rock music, but they make the best of it. Immediately Ben dances in close with the brunette. Pendragon kinda moves his arms in rhythm with the music, and the blonde chuckles and dances over to him. Hey, he is a pretty good looking guy, if a little strange! Finally, the four of them are dancing, well, the three of them dance, Pendragon is still pretty stiff. From across the floor, however, someone roars?.?WHAT THE FUCK?””!!!” The ladies gasp in surprise as Pendragon and Davis see what appears to be an angry group of about 8 guys? “Who the fuck are they?” Ben asks, then cracks his neck? “Those two are our ex boyfriends’And looks like they brought some buddies?” Chuckles the brunette?. Pendragon’s easy expression leaves his face and a sterner one replaces it?“Thee damned wenches get a kick out of this sort of thing, eh” Fighting for yonder maiden and all? HOGWASH! This is dangerous, and those men know not who they call out now! And for what? For thee? Teases and sluts? Whores be the term? CUNTS!!!”””” Even Ben can’t believe this but he laughs out loud as the blonde slaps Pendragon. Pendragon smiles and turns to the men, his hand dropping to where the sword hilt would be’Nothing there today, however? “Good CHRIST!!!” He exclaims as the mob moves in’Ben shrugs and looks at his friend... 'Good Christ is right! Why is it always a brawl when you are around, big un? Shit!' He starts to unbutton his shirt, waiting for the shit to hit the fan... ~TO BE CONTINUED~
