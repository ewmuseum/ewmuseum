Brick confronts Christina

25 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/25/04 Christina McCoy was under a lot of stress. She had to deal with a lot of things. The Upper Echelon was still running rampant. WMW was still causing trouble. The World Champion was being a pain in her rear end. And to make matters worse, yet another troublemaker in GW, Train, appeared on WMW Burnout, seeking to sign a contract with the company. On top of all that, she had High Noon coming and she wanted to 'Shelly, I'm going to be very busy this afternoon. Cancel all my meetings and inform anyone who calls that I'm not to be disturbed. Is that clear?' 'Yes, Miss..excuse me. You can't go in there!' 'Too damn bad,' exclaimed a deep male voice, 'because I'm goin' in, anyway!' 'Brick,' Christina sighed. Immediately, the door to Christina's office burst open and in stormed Brick. Immediately, he walked up to the desk and leaned over towards Christina. 'We need to talk,' Brick growled. 'Now.' 'Gee, Brick, thank you ever so much for barging into my office without an announcement or an appointment and invading my personal space. I do so appreciate the utter rudeness and lack of consideration for proper protocol.' 'Don’t hand me that shit, Christina. You're the last person in here to be talking to anybody about rudeness. Especially after what I've heard.' 'Shelly, will you excuse us for a moment?' 'Yes, ma'am.' Shelly then discreetly closed the door. 'Well, Brick, now that you have my attention, what can I do for you?' 'I'ma just come out an' say it. I think it's extremely fucked up how you did Ely. If you weren't cool with him and Gunner being together, you should have just been upfront and said so.' 'Listen, Brick, whatever business Ely, Gunner and I have is between us.' 'Yeah, well, Ely had too much respect to come up here and tell you to your face just how fucked up you were being to him.' 'Nevertheless, it's not your business.' 'Excuse me, but when I see my boy sitting at the bar in the Dark Horse, getting wasted almost out of his damn mind, I consider it my business! That shit you pulled on him was fucking childish and fucking unprofessional!' 'Excuse me, but who the hell are you to lecture someone on being childish and unprofessional? All the times you've gone crazy in the ring and outside the ring over the years?' 'Look, Christina...' 'Miss McCoy.' 'Look, Christina, I know you're under a lot o' stress cause of TUE an' Train an' the Elite an' this bullshit with, and maybe part of it has to do with your menstrual cycle but that ain't no excuse for you to take it out on Ely, especially after all the times that cat has stuck by your family all these years.' 'You know it's funny that you should come to me on Ely's behalf. Word is, you're not too thrilled about he and Gunner being a couple.' 'Yeah, part of me wasn't. But when Ely confronted me about it, I was honest. But we're cool. And she's the other reason I'm up here. And, for the record, Ely didn't flip when you were drooling over Jin Akiyara. And don't deny that you weren't, either.' 'Look, Brick, I have a lot of things on my plate. I really need some peace and quiet. And you need to get ready for your match at High Noon. You are in the main event after all. Or would you like yet another loss to Deacon Holmes on your record?' Brick let out an angry grunt. “Fine. I’ll go, but remember what I said.” Brick then began to walk out of the office when Christina stopped him. 'Brick? You’re right. I have been acting rather over the top. Maybe it is my time of the month, but you have to understand. Running this company is starting to take its toll on me. I’m just under a lot of stress and I’m taking it out on the people who care about me.” “Look, we all know you’re under a lot of stress, but you’ve got to chill out.” “Maybe you’re right. Thanks for being straight with me.” “No problem.” Brick then left the office and Christina McCoy leaned back in her chair and sighed. She had the whole world on her shoulders, or at least it seemed.
