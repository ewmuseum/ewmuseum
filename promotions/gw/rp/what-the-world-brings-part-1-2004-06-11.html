What the world brings (part 1)

11 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/11/04 His hands shaking as they bring a cigarette and lighter towards his mouth as he rests up against the outer wall of his Nevermore Mansion. The day was finally over and he had once again returned to the place in which he had stayed isolated for the past year. Only this time, thing would be different. The thrill of the kill shock his body. But it was not just any kill. Salaok had finally done what Quartz was meant to have done when Salaok was still a young teenager. Chris Davis was finally dead. Or at least Salaok thought so. The thought only now enters his head that he didn’t stay around long enough to find out if the bullet had actually killed his father. Not that going back there now to check on his was an option, but the thought did pass though The Harvester of Mind’s head. The bullet had blown Chris’s skull apart, so there was no logical way that he could have survived such a shot. The barrel pressed firmly under his father’s chin pointing towards the roof, which is exactly where the blood, brain and skull fragments were left behind. Resting with his back against the dark bricked wall of the house in which crime paid for, he takes a drag from the cigarette and then after the smoke has filled his lungs and began to diffuse into his blood, he lets it all trickle back out of his nose. The night air was chilling, to say the least and as the fog slowly rolled in over the low laying hills and tree tops, it was clear that inside the house would be a much healthier place to be, but not before the cigarette had been smoked. The cold air brought back the memories as the only way to not feel the cold, or anything for that matter was to block it out with something else. That was something his father had thought him in the School of Pain. The first thing that you learnt in fact. But with the memories that he brought back to life in his mind, it only brought back other pain. Pain that didn’t need to be resurrected, but while he lived in this house, it would never go away. Not that he wanted their memories to go away, just to merely not be so overpowering to the point where he can do nothing but sit and think about them. No, these days he had gotten back in control of his life and although the memories either bring a smile or a tear to the Harvester’s face, he was able to control them now. Control that which had destroyed him and tortured his soul every day for the past year. With defiance, he closes his eyes and lets the memories engulf him, daring them to try take control of him again. They begin their attack like they always did. They begin with Lisha. The song of the bullet breaking through the glass of the window in the dinning hall is how it would always start and then the image of Lisha laying on the floor with a single bullet wound to the midsection. Her face, shocked at first but with time, the shock turns to a look of worry and then to one of calm. If only that image could be the finally image that invades Salaok’s mind, but it never is. Everything that happened that night comes flooding back. ---- 'Sage, get Darrell and my nephew up stairs away from all windows.' Salaok's order was was direct and Sage followed the order straight away, picking up Darrell in one arm and then Emmanuell in the other. Salaok rushes over to Lisha who hasn't made a single sound, but her eyes are full of tears that are yet to spill. Johnathon is already on the phone calling for help and Grendel and Kullypso have straight away gone and found some cloth to put over Lisha's wound. Salaok looks Lisha in the eye and sees the strength in her as she bite down on her bottom lip, not wanting to make a sound. Not wanting to have let them win. Salaok holds her tight in his arms and hears her whisper something. 'Our baby?' 'Don't think about that right now. Don't worry, everything will be alright.' Salaok's response was as calming as he could possibly make it but the cracking in his voice was clear to everyone. Grendel passes a piece of cloth to Salaok and he holds it down on the wound while laying Lisha down onto the ground. Quartz stands at the window and looks at the two men in whom he hates and the one in which he gave life to. Quartz catches Mani's mouth as he says something and lowers his head. Reading his lips, Quartz seems confused. To Quartz it looked as if Mani had just said 'sorry Lisha' and now he was turning and walking away. As soon as Quartz sees Mani and Jude begin to walk away, Quartz burst for the door and not waiting to open it, flattens the thing with a huge shoulder and keeps running. A tear finally falls from Lisha's eye and runs down her cheek. The ambulance would arrive soon and everything would be OK. Or at least that is what Salaok kept telling himself. Lisha looks up at Salaok with her wet eyes wide open and she takes a deep breath before putting her hand on the side of his face again and lets out three small words that strikes at Salaok's heart like a million knives. 'I love you.' Salaok looks up to the heaven above and shuts his eyes tightly and takes a deep breath of his own before lowering himself down and kissing Lisha ever so gently on the lips and responding. 'I have always loved you Lisha, and I will be damned if I will let them take you from me. Be strong my love. Be strong.' With that, Lisha smiles and slowly closes her eyes. Her breathing continues but at a slightly higher rate now as Salaok has to change the cloth on her wound as it had become completely saturated with blood. ---- Standing there against the wall, the cigarette almost now burnt out as he stands there like a statue as the pain and suffering begins to extend it’s vile hand and grab a hold of Salaok’s heart. That night in his mind was far from over. No, for now there was still the hospital and the contracts that have still yet to have been finished. Salaok’s body visually shakes as the scene in his mind changes. ---- “NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO.” Salaok head falls into hi hands, which are propped up by rests his elbows in his knees. But the doctor hasn’t finished with the news. “There is something else you should know Mr. Davis. The child that your wife was carrying was not yet matured enough to survive without your wife’s body.” The news was devastating and for the first time in years, Salaok felt a tear run down the side of his face. Salaok had lost his beloved wife and unborn child and there was nothing he could do about it. Everything else in his life he had been able to control or pay people off in order to fix, but this, this was different and Salaok knew it. The doctor, although not wanting to say a word, speaks up. “Mr. Davis, you will be able to go in and see your wife in a minute and be with her for her passing if you so wish.” “I do.” That same word that he had spoken on their wedding day to start their life together, would be the same word that would allow him to see his wife for the last time. The doctor gets up and goes back into the room as Salaok sits there in silence. No one says a word as that tear finally reaches his chin and then with no where else to go, drops to the floor. The small splash can easily be heard in the silent deathly atmosphere. At that exact time, Jackson Diasporo walks into the waiting bay, knowing full well what has happened and knowing that Salaok was going to give the order. Salaok slowly turns his head to look up at Jackson and through his tear blurred eyes gives Jackson a look, which he understands. With a voice, wrecked with emotion, Salaok speaks the words of the order. “Mani must die. I will double your contracted amount for what he has done to this family.” That was all. The contract had just been doubled and for that amount of money, Jackson would be able to assemble a team that would hunt down the forgotten Davis brother and destroy him for his crimes against the family. Jackson nods and replies. “It will be my honor Salaok. It will be my honor.” Salaok nods before standing up and walking towards the door of the operation room where Lisha lies. As his hand reaches out and presses on the door, he stops and turns to Grendel. “Tell Sage to bring Darrell to see his mother.” Grendel doesn’t say a word, only a nod and a quick movement away down the hall as Salaok pushes the door open. He steps into the room and there she lay, awake now and changed into clean hospital clothes as all of the external bleeding had been contained. There was nothing to show that Lisha wasn’t going to pull through except for the knowledge that the internal bleeding wouldn’t stop and her body wouldn’t take the blood on. Her face was that of an angel. Although on death’s bed, her face was always strong and beautiful. She would never let anyone other than Salaok know that she was hurting, but even now, she stays strong for her husband. He moves over to the side of the bed and grabs a hold of her hand as she smiles. It wasn’t a forced smile, but one that was heart felt and one that she was glad she could give him. “I’ve excepted it Salaok and you must now do the same.” Her voice of calm reason was one thing that Salaok could not bare to do without. He brings her hand up to his lips and plants a soft kiss on her tender and now pale skin. “I will never accept what they have done to you my dear.” “In the end Sal, you will see that everything that has ever happened, happened for a reason. Every action has it’s purpose whether you like it or not Salaok.” Salaok lowers his eyes, not worthy of being in the presence of such a woman. Although not a lot of strength is left in her body, she nudges Salaok’s chin with her hand to make him look at her. “Make sure that Darrell remains safe at all times. And in doing so, I will make sure you are the same. I will watch over you Salaok and be there when you ask for guidance. When you need me, just call out my name and I will be there.” “You didn’t fight this did you” You gave up back at the house.” Taking Salaok’s hand in both of hers, she holds them tight. “I didn’t give up Sal, I accepted that which had been given to me.” As soon as Lisha finishes the door opens and Sage walks into the room, behind Darrell who still has tears streaming down his face. “Mumma!” Darrell runs across the room and throws his upper body over the bed. Lisha’s hands let go of Salaok as she wraps her arms around Darrell’s body and squeezes him with everything she’s got. She looks up at Sage and smiles.“Thank you Sage. Now make sure you look after my boys now.” Sage stands there silent as a tear runs down his cheek before opening his mouth. “I will Lisha. And thank you.” Lisha nods in understanding of what Sage meant and then turns her attention to Darrell in her arms. In the calmest voice she could possibly muster, she begins to explain things to Darrell and Salaok can do nothing but stand there in silence and in awe of this beautiful person who he is about to lose. “Darrell my dear I have something to tell you. I’m going to be going away to a place you will never be able to go. My life is coming to an end, but your life is only starting. Remember everything I have told you and you will grow up to be a great man.” Darrell, now completely overwhelmed by the hurt inside his little heart, talking through his cries. “I don’t want you to go Mumma. Why do you have to go?” “I’m dying, Darrell. The doctors can’t stop the bleeding inside of me. But don’t worry. I will be all right. Wipe away those tears and be strong for me Darrell. I need you to be strong.” Darrell’s cries don’t stop, however they do reduce in volume as he tries to be strong. But for a child losing his mother, there is nothing in the world that is harder to try and be strong through. “I don’t want to be strong Mum. I just want to be with you.” “I know dear, but you can’t be with me. One day we will be together again, I give you my word on that.” Suddenly Lisha visibly shivers as her body begins to go cold and she grimaces with the pain, which luckily Darrell doesn’t see, but Salaok and Sage certainly do. “I will forever be with you in your hearts as long as you remember that I love you.” With that said, Lisha closes her eyes and lets one last breath exhale from her lungs as Salaok drops to his knees next to the bed. “I will never stop loving you Lisha. Never.” ---- Those words were true. Salaok had never stopped loving Lisha and never would. Nor would he forget the faces of those who had done this unspeakable act. Lisha had been the mother of The Nevermore since it’s early days and had taken care of everyone and everything that she could. And there had been times where both Silent Wind and Mani Shigeru had felt the caring hand of Lisha and yet they were able to stand there so cold and shoot her without a second thought. And a second of thought is all that Salaok can manage before the next wave of pain hits his fouled mind, although, this one far less painful now that the years have past and the truth has been found. ---- “Dad” You there?” Again Salaok gets no response and so he pushes the slightly opened door open that little bit more and pokes his head through the gap. There was his father. Asleep just as his mother had thought he would be but Salaok had to go up and see him. And to go and tell him about the movie. Salaok knew that his mother had told him not to, and he knew that most of the time, his father would get annoyed but this story was just too good to let wait. Trying not to make a lot of nose as he walked across the room towards the bed, Salaok doesn’t want to wake his father suddenly. No, he would rather shake him gently until he wakes up. The same way his father had done for him every morning in order to get Salaok up and off to the School with him. Although he was fifteen years old, Salaok still acted very close to his father and at times treated him like his best friend. So as Salaok edged across the room, he began to smile and felt a laugh coming on because he could just see his father’s face as he wakes him up, being full of surprise. Finally after almost a minute of travelling over a few feet, Salaok reaches the bedside and looks at his sleeping father. His eyes closed but sleeping peacefully he was not. His face contorted and seemingly full of pain. Salaok grabs his father’s shoulders ever so gently and shakes him lightly. His father doesn’t stir and so Salaok shakes a little harder. Again, no response from his father. Standing upright and letting go of his father, Salaok crosses his arms over his chest and huffs, annoyed at his father’s deep sleep. “Dad?” Salaok says in a low volume but much like the shaking, it gained no response. “Dad, wake up.” This time Salaok’s voice was a lot louder than before but still not as loud as he would normally speak. Still nothing. All he wanted was to talk to his father and share something good with him but his father just wouldn’t wake up. He drops down onto the bed, making sure that he rocked the bed as much as possible and awaited the response but still none was forthcoming. Salaok grabs his father hard by the shoulders, now annoyed at his refusal to wake. Shaking him lightly at first Salaok builds up the intensity slowly and increases the volume of his calls of “Dad”. No matter how hard Salaok shook or yelled at his father, he just wouldn’t wake. Fear began to sink into the teenager as he shakes his father harder than before now. The panic becoming evident in his voice and gaining the attention of his mother and sister down stairs. “Dad, wake up. WAKE UP DAD. FUCKING WAKE UP!” Salaok was now yelling at his father as tears began to run down his face and his father simply didn’t wake up. Cheryl runs into the room and sees what’s going on and then sees the look on her husband’s face. The stone white skin tone and the marks around his neck. Chris Davis was dead. Salaok collapses next to the bed to his knees with his arms over his father. ---- If only Chris Davis, the father of Salaok had really been dead that day, then today never would have happened. For today, Salaok had killed his own father, for real. Today Salaok finally stands alone. Dropping what is left of the burnt out filter of his cigarette, Salaok opens his pain stricken eyes and looks out onto the world that had caused him so much torment over the years. Pushing himself off the wall and moving towards the front doors of this mansion, he utters that words that The Lair had been waiting for him to say. “This world will pay once more for what it has done to me. And I will take great pleasure in it this time.”
