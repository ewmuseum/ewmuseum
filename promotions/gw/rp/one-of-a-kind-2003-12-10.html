One of a Kind

10 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
One of a Kind JJ Jarone Global Wrestling Promo #722 Date: 12-10-03 Location: Tokyo, Japan 'Alright people step back, step back. Let the man through!' Jarones agent yells. Here I am trying to maneuver myself through the compacted crowed in the lobby. I've just arrived at the Tokyo Print-Casiday, headquarters for press. I'm set to do a conference in a good ten minutes, but with the crowd trying to get hold of me it's making it a little difficult. When your a superstar like myself you really can't use the whole The crowd forced me from making it on time excuse. After a few moments of fighting with the multiple Asian folks here in Tokyo I finally arrived in the press room. After brushing my suite off I head for the podium ready to receive questions. There was only one problem... 'Mr. Jarun, Mr. Jarun, I ask quick question?' A man says with horrible English. I can't understand a lot of these people in here. So I step aside for a moment and shrug my shoulders peering at my agent. A lean towards him whispering, trying to keep it down, fearing the ladies and gentleman could possibly hear me. Chris, what in the hell are we doing a press conference in Tokyo for? I must have left that question out on the plane ride over here last night.' My agent Chris Rock peers at me, I know what he's thinking. He could never really hide laughter all that well. His lips scrunch together, and his face gets red. Knowing any minute this man can just explode in laughter. Finally after collecting his thoughts, Chris came up with an answer for me. 'Well Jarone, not all these people in here translate that bad. Infact a lot of the press here speak English pretty fluently. Anyway, just answer his question. Incase you did not get what he was saying, he wants to ask you a quick question... Mr. Jarun.' Chris replies in laughter. At this time the man asking the quick question is fluttered, and a little confused. 'Mr. Jarun, Showdown in Tokyo, only days close. What you have instore?' The man asks while pulling out a pen. 'Well I am one of the newest additions to the Global Wrestling roster. The past month I have busted my ass to get where I am now. not only beating the six foot six beast Jesse Jamester for the GW PrimeStar Championship, but also proving myself in the Lions Test match. What I have instore for Showdown in Tokyo is going to be something no human has ever seen before for JJ Jarone. There is a survival match with teams and captains. I have been to the gym every damn day for the past three weeks to prepare myself for this match. I promise ass out there who will be watching, and attending the match that Jarone is gonna serve a good ol' ass whoopin' when I am tagged in. It's time for Jarone to step up to the plate ya see. I'm a swing for that long home run, and anyone who feels they need to test my skill, then put up or shut up.' Yea I know them are some pretty strong words there, but I have to let the people know now I'm not one to lay down, I'm not one to give up. I believe in one thing, if you first fail, try, try, try, and try again. But I wont have a problem, and I wont fail. I know most still look at me as a rookie here in the business, but as long as I know I've busted my ass for five years to get this far, that's the only thing I need to push me to continue on. I take a quick sip of my water, then placed it on the podium top. After shifting the GW PrimeStar Championship over my shoulder, another question is shouted, this time with a more understanding of the English language. 'JJ Jarone, Jonny Ringo stated there was some heat backstage with you and himself at the Showdown in Tokyo pre-show. What kind of rivalry has worked up, if any?' A man in a blue suite, wearing a top hat says. 'Well to be honest Jonny Ringo is just sticking that big nose of his in business where it just shouldn't be. The man has the audacity to speak of me as a rookie here the Global Wrestling. I'll give the man props for carrying a four win and zero loss record, but homeboy I'm also pushing a one-hundred percent winning streak here in GW. Basically to put it out there, Ringo is the type of man who will do anything, and everything to get your attention. It's like them little boys at the suprise birthday party looking out the window awaiting to give the que when the birthday boy arrives. He yell's, HE'S HERE HE'S HERE! Everyone gets ready then you hear, JUST KIDDIN'! A short chuckle usually follows. Ringo made a statement that I have a big mouth, and little talent.' I couldn't help but to spread the laughter there. After letting out the laughter I scratch my chin and continue. 'Ringo, Ringo, Ringo... You just don't know. Who in the hell are you inerpret this statement. Do you know me? Did you ever step in the ring with me? So you happen to know I beat the hell out of several GW superstars in the Lions Test match and came up damn near victorious. I've been ambushed, and spit on in the backstage area's, but did that keep Jarone down, oh hell no. Boy check yourself before you step in the ring with me, cause Ring-o will bet wrapped around the ring-pole.' I took another sip of my water with a grin ear to ear on my face. Another man stands up form the back and begins to speak. 'What about your feud with Jamester?' I was waiting for that question. And believe it or not I've been trying to come up with a reasonable response, and still haven't found one. 'Jamester is a six foot six beast. I would have never have thought of myself and him in the ring. I fear none, but this man did put a little sweat on my chest when I first stood toe to toe with this man. Look at me I'm 5'10 and weigh 212 pounds. Jamester is 6'6 and damn near doubles my weight. But I did not let that slow me down in the ring. We've met three times in the ring as of late, and all three times I've whooped his ass. The first match is the Lions Test match, sure I was defeated after a cheap shot over the ropes. The second match is the PrimeStar Championship match. I beat his ass, and some punk from the back comes to the ring and cracks Jamester with a sledge hammer. Jamester may have thought I had something to do with it, but in his mind he's all wrong. The third would be again the PrimeStar Championship. Where I did what most thought I couldn't do again. Beat the hell out of him, and carried on the PrimeStar Champion. Our feud surely has thickened, and I don't mind it. But for him to come out and say I have no talent is like saying a giraffe has no neck. You know a giraffe has a lot of neck, and you know Jarone has a lot of talent. Some may call me conceded, I'm just being me, the only person I know how to be.' With that said I took one last sip of my water, and prepared myself for the last question. This time a woman stands up in the crowd, wearing a long red dress, and a mic in hand. 'Ever thought of retiring?' Whoa, thats a blow. I never had that question pop up in a cod before. I guess you can say I wasn't prepared for that surten question. I knew what I had to do, answer it, and answer it well. Out of the side of my eye I could see Chris grin knowing I really didn't know how o answer this question. 'Yea I thought of retiring multiple times, but never went through with it. SO Ringo, Jamester, and the rest of the Prime-time Central region, don't get your damn hopes up, 'cause I'm a be here for a while. I know most like to hang up there tights and return. Well, when I retire it won't be anything like that, I'll be gone for good, maybe putting on a staffer position. But like I said, I'm a be here for a while so don't expect to see Jarone hanging up them tights for a while. And if any superstar disagrees with me, then I challenge them to lace my boots.' Chris shoves his way through the crowd and up to the podium trying to calm the crowd. 'Alright people please no more question, Jarone must leave now. Please step back, and let the man through.' With that said I grabbed my title and headed towards the door. On my way out a little boy stoed me with a pen and photo of myself performing the frog splash in hand. 'Jarone, can I get your autograph?' 'Sure kid.' I grabbed the picture with a grin and signed the picture. Then headed out the door.
