Cheating Destiny

17 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 17, 2004 The morning sun shone in through the blinds, it was striking Chris in the eyes. As the clock buzzed several times until the mans open handed fist struck the annoyance and silenced it. Still half dazed he began to move about in his bed, until successfully freeing him of the heavy covers. Still not fully awake he finally sat up in his bed wearing only shorts, after promptly dressing himself and proceeding to the living room. But as he made it to the leather couch and finally sat back, he turned his head and opened his eyes. Only to notice a red blinking on his answering machine. 'Ah damn...' Chris sighed. He got up and struggled to the machine, not even lifting his feet he pressed the key. The tape began to run. A nice woman's voice began to play... 'Hello sir, sorry we missed you but your flight has been rescheduled due to technical difficulties, your plane leaves at approximately 9:00 AM. Thanks for your cooperation and sorry for the inconvenience.' The tape winded down and Chris saw the clock, 8:16. 'Holy crap,' this was a complete wake up call as Chris ran to his closet, grabbed his bags and started cramming clothes in it so fast it could have broken a record. He packed three suitcases and took off downstairs to his vehicle. At this point he was in a dead sprint. He jumped into his car and threw his cases into the back-seat. As he started the car he caught a glimpse at the time, 8:24. He sped out of his driveway and after fighting morning traffic, and almost hitting a couple of other cards. He parked spread out in two handicap spots. He jumped out, locked his door and grabbed his cases as he pushed through the crowds of people he looked up at the airport clock, 8:47. Whew, he had made it with time to spare. As he ran down the airport hall he made it in line ... no ones else was standing there. He extended his hand to hand her the ticket. He reached into his pocket to pull it out and ... it wasn't there! He had left it while in his hurry. 'O my god, okay look just let me on the plane I really need this I've got a deadline to meet.' 'Sir, I'm sorry but I just can't allow that. We have another plane leaving in an hour, after you land you'll have to catch another plane and that should get you to where you need. The tickets for this deal are also relatively cheap.' 'I guess I don't really have a choice, give me the tickets.' Chris said as he dropped his credit card to the desk. After purchasing the tickets he sat in the waiting room what seemed like an eternity. The children all were playing with the toys that were in the corner. They were whining. and yelling, and just being pests. After a while they finally announced they were boarding his plane so he grabbed his bags and proceeded the loading ramp. 'Sir' a man said, 'You cant carry on your bags, this plane is non carry on, I'll have to take them.' 'No, No, NO!! There is no way in hell that you are going to take away my only luggage, after all the crap you people have put me through, No Freaking way!' After a long argument the airport worker managed to convince Mr. Steel that he wouldn't be allowed to board if he had his bags. Everything was going exactly to plan, well besides the being late, them taking his bags, and switching his flight. Besides those everything was in the clear. After he finally got off his plane, he was leaving and went to pick up his bags, sure enough, as he suspected the airport had screwed him once again, they only successfully got one suitcase here. Angry he decided to just leave and give them his number, maybe they would find his bags, but most likely not. He had no car, no money, and was angry as fuck. He sat on the curb of the hustle and bustle city. He decided to find what the airport had actually sent correctly. He clicked both snaps on his bag, and opened the case. He started digging around seeing what he had packed in his hurry, he held up a pair of medium tight fitting shorts, 'Ah my old wrestling shorts, this takes me back.' At that point, as he was holding his shorts in the air. A logo on a building had caught his eye, It had a globe shape on it and had the initials G + W on it. 'Global Wrestling huh?' Chris thought. It seemed like fate of destiny, he could continue his plane trips and risk more mishaps, or he could go in and fill out a application. It seemed like a sign, riddled with thoughts he mentally began to list out the positives and negatives of each. But his legs seemed to move without him, as he walked off across the street and into the building.
