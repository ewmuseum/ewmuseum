The Telling Truth

25 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09.25.05 09.13.05 | Virginia Mason Hospital, Seattle | 11:34 AM 'Michael!' Mindy Mercer ran through the hallway of the clean hospital, in search for her injured cousin. Markus trailed close behind, although being a giant compared to Mindy, he certainly didn't have as much endurance as his female companion. 'Michael?!' the young lady shouted again, a panicked look in her eyes as she frantically ran about. 'Michael?! Where are you?!' 'Slow down, hun,' Markus pleaded, but it was no use, as even the large man's booming voice soon became little more than a whisper to Mindy's tiny ears. Before Markus knew it, Mindy was well ahead of him and soon out of his sight. 'Dammit...' When Markus found his girlfriend, she stood in place with her hands covering her mouth. Beyond the doorway Mindy remained in, lay her cousin. The one doctor in the room had just finished up his work and was taking his leave, allowing Michael to rest peacefully in bed. Mindy never saw her cousin in such shape; in fact, she'd never seen him in a hospital bed ever. 'Michael...' Bravely, Mindy took a step into the room. Markus slowed his running to a stop as he caught up to the Global World Champion, watching her closely as she moved to her cousin's side. 'Michael...' she whispered again, to which the older Mercer blinked his eyes open. 'Nngh....' 'Mikey!' Mindy exclaimed happily, throwing her arms around her cousin's neck. 'Are you alright?' Michael groaned at the physical contact before his aching throat mumbled out, 'Of course I'm not fucking alright.' Frowning, Mindy stopped her hugging of her cousin and stood upright. 'I'm so sorry.. if I knew you were hurt that badly I'd have never left you here. Forgive me?' Michael chuckled. Mindy was apologizing, and for what reason, the older cousin had no idea. 'Forgive you? Er... kay?' Smiling, Mindy hugged her cousin once more. 'Bah, just don't do that,' Michael sighed in disapproval. What was Mindy to do? She was so sure everything would go right at Whiplash, and for the most part, it did. She retained her World Title, got her revenge on Dawn McFarlane, and was the first World Champion in months to make a successful title defense. For most people, that would have been everything. Unfortunately, her cousin lost. Again. Therefore, everything was not alright. In fact, as far as Michael was concerned, nothing went right at Whiplash; and the aftermath that led him to the hospital was just the icing on the cake. 'I'm sorry..' Mindy meekly apologized once more, not wanting to worsen Michael's condition. She tried to think of anything, just anything that could possibly cheer him up. 'Hmm... ooh, I know what'll make you feel better.' Quickly, Mindy reached into her pants pocket, and pulled out a small, stuffed teddy bear. 'Mister Bear will sing you a song!' 'No...' Michael's mind screamed. 'No, please, no.' Too late. In her best Claudio impression, Mindy began to croon her favorite Coheed And Cambria song. 'Good eye, sniper! Here I'll shoot, you run! The words you scribbled on the walls, with the loss of friends you didn't have! I'll call you when the time is right- Are you in? Or are you out? For them all to know the end of us allllll!' Mindy moved the bear's limbs in a dancing manner, trying frantically to get her cousin to crack a smile. It did not work. Instead, Michael's hands shot up to cover his ears. Mindy completely ignored the look of displeasure on her cousin's face, too into her singing to note Michael's look of pain etched in his features. 'Byeeee byeeee beautifullllll... Don't boooother to wriiiiite.. Disturbed by your words, and they're calling all cars: Face step, let down... Face step, step doooown!' 'Stop it!' Michael cried, babbling anything that would get Mindy to stop the madness. 'Stop it, I can't breathe, I'm gonna throw up!' Sorrowfully, Mindy whimpered and cradled her teddy bear close to her chest. 'You don't like mister Bear?' 'Want the truth?' 'Uh huh.' 'No.' Mindy gasped. 'Awww.... okay..' Sniffling, Mindy concealed her stuffed animal from her cousin's sight. Michael moaned in relief, and could rest easy a little while longer with the assurance of having to deal with no more squeaky singing. 'You don't need to be here anyway,' Michael shrugged, 'not like I'm spending my life in this hospital or anything.' 'I know, cuz..' Mindy frowned, 'but that doesn't mean I'd want you to have to stay here all alone.' Michael let the remark sink in. Alone... alone.. yeah, alone. And there was one big reason why Michael was alone. 'Yeah, well... not much I can do about that, now.' Mindy blinked. It took her a minute to realize what Michael had meant, but before long, she understood all too well. What was worse, however, was that in the same breath, she noticed the two identical rings on Michael's right hand; one on his ring finger, the other on his index finger. Mindy was naive, yes, but it didn't take much for her to understand the meaning behind that other ring. 'Erm...' Mindy was speechless. Should she mention it, and risk being verbally berated by her cousin for doing so? Or should she merely leave it alone, allowing Michael to only bring it up to himself, possibly leaving him saddened in the process? With a dying grumble, Mindy decided that perhaps silence was the best policy. However, to Michael, it wasn't. 'What's wrong with you?' the injured man questioned, prompting a look of worry on Mindy's face. As if she needed to be worrying anymore than she already was. 'I, uh...' Luckily for her, when she thought she was going to be put on the spot, her boyfriend stepped in. 'She was just nervous about this affecting your Television Title match next week, that's all.' '...' Michael relented from singling Mindy out, in favor of taking in the news given to him just then. Television Title, eh? 'Hm... these fuckers really want me to keep losing, huh?' Markus sighed. Just when he thought he'd have made Michael at least somewhat happy. 'Um, what do you mean?' 'The only reason I can think of bookers putting me in a title match after a loss would be because they want me to keep losing. Really, it's not like I earned a title shot or anything, is it?' Markus rolled his eyes. This was not the conversation he expected. 'Why question it, Michael? A shot is a shot.. this would be your chance to shut some people up.' 'I don't care about that anymore!' Michael shouted, taking Markus by further surprise. 'God... I just don't give a shit about wrestling right now.' 'Really? Now that's a first.' If Michael wasn't lying down and recovering from his injuries, he'd have been Markus' ass in an instant for that one. 'The fuck did you just say?' 'Er, I dunno,' Markus choked out nervously, 'what did I say?' 'Guys,' Mindy cut in, 'just forget it.. I don't want us to argue, okay?' Solemnly, she concentrated her focus on her cousin. 'Are you going to be alright?' Michael looked his cousin dead on in the eyes, and there Mindy found something that she didn't see often in Michael: it was a look of concern.. a look that seemed just as uneasy as the heart that continued to beat beneath skin and bone of the older Mercer. 'I'll try... that's all I can promise right now.' 09.20.05 | Truth Or Consequences, New Mexico | 12:35 AM The beats to the music playing on the radio to Mindy's pink lamborghini died out as she and Markus scampered from the car. Her and her cousin took a disappointing loss to Divine Darlings that night, and not being one to try and push Michael to be angrier, she thought it best that she leave Michael to himself so he could concentrate on Emoticon later that night. She hadn't the slightest idea how the result of that match was going to play out- much less what would happen afterwards. 'You think he won?' Markus asked his girlfriend, who quietly walked up the front steps to her home, keys in hand. Mindy gave the question some honest thought. It wasn't like her to doubt her cousin's ability, but from what she had seen of him in the tag team match that night, even she had to admit that Michael's heart didn't seem to be in the right place. 'Eh... it doesn't matter what I think. Let's just head inside, okay?' Mindy kissed her boyfriend softly on the lips, which quieted the large Markus enough for the roar of a car engine to be heard zooming past them down the street. Mindy turned to see what car it was; under the glimmer of the streetlights, Mindy pointed it out easily. 'Michael...' The car made a U-turn on the dead end street, coming back to park just outside of the house. As Mindy stated, it was indeed Michael inside the car, but the kind of Michael that stepped out from the vehicle was not one she expected. He was smiling. Not a full on happy smile, but it was a smile. A start of something good, maybe. Of course, the smile wasn't without its source of happiness: a leather strap that took its position neatly on Michael's right shoulder. The Global Wrestling Television Title. 'Looks I caught you guys just in time,' he said, obviously exhausted. Still, a slightly happy Michael was better than a completely unhappy Michael. 'Wow...' Mindy stammered, floored by the surprise of seeing her cousin with some gold of his own. It reminded her completely of Michael's days as SAWF World Champion.. but given such trying times, this meant so much more. 'You did it!' With a small shrug, Michael nodded his head. 'Yeah.. I would have tried during the tag match.. but I guess I was too busy laughing at how the number of lesbians in Global is now higher than the number of title wins I've had in Global. Figured that I'd have to regulate that shit.' Mindy's eyes widened. That bubbly spark to her green orbs had returned, proving that maybe things would get better after all. 'Glad you won, Mikey,' she smiled, running back down the front steps to give her cousin a big hug. 'Are you feeling any better?' Michael shrugged, gently patting Mindy on the shoulder. 'Nothing will make me feel better,' he said softly, 'but at least this gets my mind off things.' 'Eh, that's a start.' Mindy turned her attention back to the front door, fumbling the key into its lock, the door opened with ease and Mindy stepped inside. She wandered happily into the home, placing her duffel bag on the living room couch, when she heard something odd eminating from the kitchen. Mindy glanced in the direction of the noise, and there she saw something shocking: a dark figure making its way out of view, down the hall. Mindy froze in shock, staring in fright as the silhouette immediately popped back out into view, walking around and paying no mind to the little girl standing in the front door. 'Oh gods...' The World Champion backed away from the door, turning to the two men before her. 'What is it?' Markus asked, to which Mindy gulped and stammered a response. 'It's.. there's someone in the house.' Michael didn't waste any time jumping into action; he practically dove headlong into the home, and it didn't take him long to spot the intruder walking through the halls. Quietly, he followed the man down towards the the direction of the bedrooms- namely the bedroom of Michael's daughter. The man opened the door, and Michael calmly followed inside. He didn't let the man get any farther into the room than the doorway, before Michael grabbed the men firmly around the neck, putting him in a rear naked choke. One moment later, Michael had found out who it was he was choking. 'You motherfucker...' Michael growled, 'if you do not get out right now, I will fucking kill you, you hear me?! I'll ki-' 'Daddy!' That one word that came from one tiny little's girl's voice was enough for Michael to lose all of his anger. He threw T'sao Liang to the ground, allowing himself to look at the rest of the room. Inside, Michael's daughter Gabriella sat watching television, and she was sitting in the lap of her own mother, and both women stared directly at Michael- one of them with a decisively colder glare than the other. 'it's about time I talked with you, Michael.' Michael's jaw dropped. The Television Title that seemed glued to his shoulder had sank to the floor at his feet. The Chinese man calmly rose to his feet, turning to smile at Michael who remained completely helpless, not knowing what to say. How could he? What could he say in this situation? 'You see, Michael,' Liang spoke plainly, 'I figured I made my goodbye message last week somewhat... hm, what's that word... ambiguous. That was silly of me. Now, my friend.. it's time for me to tell you the truth.' Michael couldn't believe what he was hearing or seeing. After all of the inner doubt that his wife and daughter were still alive, they sat in Michael's own home without a scratch on them. In fact, Gabriella seemed perfectly happy. Silently, wordlessly, something in Michael Mercer's head told him that he did not want to hear the truth.
