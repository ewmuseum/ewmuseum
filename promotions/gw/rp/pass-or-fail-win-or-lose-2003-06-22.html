Pass or fail....win or lose...

22 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/22/03 Life throws you many curve balls, you can either swing for the fences or stand there and watch them go by. For some, watching is much easier, but for others, namely Sean Price, it’s the challenge and the attempt to make something out of nothing that makes him wake up each and everyday. For Sean, every day is a curve ball, everyday is another test and as we all know there are only two outcomes to a test’pass or fail. Lose or win, beat or be beaten. All of these choices ring through Sean’s mind each and every time he gets in the ring and looks across at his opponent. Last week it was Xodus, this week it is one of Sean’s stablemates and friends Deacon Holmes. In a sad attempt to break up what Salaok sees as a threat he has pitted two members of GW’s most powerful stable against each other. You would think that something like this would infuriate the TUE but instead it only makes them stronger and more aware that their very presence is what causes Salaok to lose sleep at night. (As the scene fades in we see Sean Price relaxing by his Olympic sized pool in his Atlanta mansion. The sky is clear the clouds a puffy white with a bright shining sun beating down on the ground below. Price is wearing a pair of tan shorts that are frayed at the bottoms, a white t-shirt and sandals. One of his body guards walks over to him and hands him an open bottle of Corona with a piece of lime inside. Sean takes the bottle and than takes a sip from it before gently placing it down on the table next to him.) “Last week I was given a test when I was asked to face the tag team of Xodus and at the same time find someone to take the place as my partner while Tyler was off winning the cruiserweight title. Not only did I win the match but I found my partner. John Zero, the newest member of The Upper Echelon and most importantly one of the scariest men I have ever met.” (Sean slowly gets up from his seat with Corona in hand and then takes a walk along side of the pool.) “This week is yet another test handed down to me by Salaok. He believes that by forcing me to go into the ring against one of my friends that it might cause a rift in the stable that has threatened his power since the day we announced our existence. But you see what Salaok doesn’t know is that unlike the Nevermore, we are not driven by greed, there are some things more important to us than winning, one of those things is friendship.” “Someone once said, chose your friends wisely because they will be the ones there at your funeral. Salaok for your sake I hope you did that because the way things are going if the stress from us constantly succeeding at every road block you throw up doesn’t kill you, then we will.” “You are some what like a teacher to us, granted we don’t learn anything from you, but then again how many people actually learn something from their teachers and not life experience. But none the less you are like the teacher in that you administer the test, and like all tests two people are graded, those given the test and those who give it. We, and by we I mean the TUE have passed the tests you have given us in the past and we will continue to do so.” “The only difference Salaok between you, and a real teacher, is that with every test we pass, you look worse and worse in the eyes of the rest of GW. You see with most teachers a passing grade is a good thing, but with you, it just shows that each and every time we are one step ahead of you, and that much better than you.” (A confident smile comes across the face of Price as he realizes that the TUE is indeed Salaok’s worst nightmare.) “There is an old saying, “beat’or be beaten” you have proven so far that beating us is impossible, so I guess your only option now is to sit back and watch as your little world becomes unraveled at the hands of the TUE.” “What you Salaok and what everyone else has seen up to this point has been nothing but the TUE warming up and scouting things out. We have yet to scratch the surface and we have yet to show you exactly what we are capable of and what we will eventually accomplish in our time here. Just sit back because you are all about to witness the power of the TUE.” “Just a few hours from now I will be getting in the ring against Deacon Holmes, not at my wish and certainly not at my approval but I am doing it nonetheless. Salaok you may think that you own us because you can put us in matches such as the one I am involved in, but the truth is, you own nothing but this company. Not the wrestlers in it.” “Salaok you may think that this company is nothing without you, but the fact is, this company is nothing without its wrestler’s. That most importantly includes the TUE. You can be the owner all you want, sit in your nice office and sign bullshit match after bullshit match, give title shots to undeserving people in your stable, but without a solid federation of wrestlers you will have no matches, no card, no ratings and with all of that”.no money.” “It is only a matter of time before you get the best of yourself, as easily as the TUE can put you out of a job, you can do it to yourself that much faster. So remember that next time you decide to sign a match such as the one involving me and Deacon. There is nothing stopping us from walking out just like there is nothing stopping us from walking in. You may think that a contract, a piece of paper is your proof that you own someone but the truth is, a contract is nothing more than a piece of paper with a few signatures on it. You can’t force anyone to do anything. You can keep thinking that but the TUE will be a constant reminder of how wrong you are.” (As the scene fades out Sean gets up from his chair and walks inside the house, he turns around and looks out through his sliding glass window, he smiles at the camera and then continues on his way through the house.)
