The Cell: Inaugurate (Ch.1)

25 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 25th, 2004 Prologue: It’d been a year since he’d entered the cell of a real prison. A whole year… and now it’d be another three months before he’d get out. Under the conditions he was given, no family member, work related person, or friends could bail him out, that leaving him with no options but to stay in the penitentiary of Texas. Texas was known for the number of killings it’d let during the early 1990’s, where the death penalty was being enforced by the law to let people who’d committed more serious crime, to be able to move up the chain and be executed quicker. This wasn’t news Jesse was worried about though. He knew he’d only committed a minor act of breaking the law, but what he did worry about was being stuck in a prison where some of the most dangerous men on earth were. Could he get hurt? Could he die? What happened if he dropped the soap? Was Jesse as safe as he could be? Or was this possibly the end? Story Begins. Buzz. Buzz. Buzz. The buzzard beeped three times before the gates opened and the prisoners were allowed out of their cells to eat. Each man stepping out and making a straight line as guards patrolled them from the sides. Walking a straight line the mass majority of well over a thousand men had begun to make their way to the cafeteria for the food that became their life. The significant quantity of orange made the penitentiary look as if a body of orange ocean water. There in the back of the line stood Jesse, for he’d been the last to enter the cell and would be the last to serve. The chain of prisoners was like a weave of history of the prison, for each one showed a distinct feature that represented their age, health, and their culture. Some in groups, others by themselves, but no matter what it was, they were here in Dallas, and were being 0charged for criminal acts. Making their way down the steps the prisoners slowly went down into the cafeteria where they’d eat their lunch. Jesse leisurely walked into the cafeteria trying not to make a scene. In prison, no matter who you were you were a marked man. The fact that you may have killed someone sends shockwaves through the walls, and being a celebrity didn’t make that news go any slower. In line Jesse grabbed his plate and then his food, before walking by the inmates in front of him who were chatting. Sitting down at a table at the far end of the room that was nearly rusted, as the tables were made from steel to last over a longer period of time, he began to eat. Sure enough though his peace and quite would be interrupted. A small man with bony hands, as well body shape, sat down and began to eat opposite of Jesse. He had multiple tattoos on his arms, and showed a Mexican heritage via his skin tone and his facial structure. The man’s eyes began to wonder from the hands of Jesse up the arms and finally to his face. Jesse stopped eating and wiped his lips clean with a napkin. ’Excuse me, may I help you?’ stated Jesse wondering what the man wanted. ’… … … You really don’t remember me do you?’ responded the man. Jesse stared at him for a moment. It was not a familiar face to him, but Jesse tried to put on a convincing mask of a wrinkled remembrance and half smile. The man snickered and knew Jesse didn’t have the slightest clue who he was so he ended the suspense and said his name. ’Richard Lorenzo… Remember now?’ declared Richard as he now watched Jesse’s face light up in enthusiasm. ’No way it’s you! I… ah... Man it’s been so long. What happened to you?’ retorted Jesse in a blissful disposition. ’Well… After the trial they told me I’d come down here and work as a guard. I got in a fight with one of the inmates, self-defense on my part mostly, and I accidentally killed him,’ Richard answered with disgust filling in his eyes, ’What about you?’ Staring down at his plate, Jesse picked up his fork to eat. Half way to his mouth Richard grabbed Jesse by the hand and shook his head in a disapproving nature. ’It can’t be that bad man… Tell me,’ Richard said wanting to know. Jesse looked up at him, eyes squinting as they held back his tears, and his face drooping over the grief that the memories brought him before saying why. ’I… assaulted an officer and am here for three months.’ Yet that didn’t satisfy Richard, because Rich knew he wouldn’t have done such a thing unless it was attached to something else Jesse was hiding. Richard stood up and told Jesse he’d see him later as he put his plate in the dishwater and strolled out of the cafeteria in a hurry. Moments later Jesse followed the same steps Richard had done and left the cafeteria quickly to escape the hellhole they ate in. The Elite of the Yard It was two o’clock in the afternoon, the ‘recess’ for the inmates, as some would call it. In the yard it was a typical set up, the fences with barbwire, the steps, the walls, the basketball court where many guys played, and then a setup of weights to the one corner of the yard. Only difference was the concrete wall that surrounded the outside of the fences that had dogs between them. Checking the area out you could notice who was with whom, for the whole yard was divided into sections of where people could go and such. The Latinos, Nazis, Japs, Thugs, and then a group of elite where the top notch men were patrolling the whole yard. This particular group of elite was different then what one may think they are. The leader sat on the concrete steps at the top, being just a small old man, he looked like a wolf with his white hair that was jelled back and the widows peak making his face all that much more frightening. Sitting just below him was another man, a little larger than he was but not a muscle head, as you’d think. This man looked somewhat like him, but without the white jelled hair. Instead he fashioned a scar on his left cheek that went down under his chin. Having short curled brown hair he represented a young leader, possibly a brother or son of the leader. To his side was a large bulky African American who wore a cut off shirt and was completely bald. You could notice him as the muscle but by his decent it was wondered if he was a paid bodyguard or just a friend. Playing cards below the big African were two of your average Joes, the thick headed type who always did things wrong. They both sported short hair cuts that were black and spiked in odd ways, as if greased up like thug type rats. Both showed resemblance that signaled they were family. Finally below them was the last man in the chain… Richard Lorenzo. Ole’ Richard was a Mexican, without doubt his use for the group was negotiation and if Jesse knew correct, Richard would be wanting to get Jesse in the Elite. Jesse avoided being scene and stayed near the weights as he had forgotten about his match with Juvian in Global Wrestling this upcoming Sunday, April 25, 2004. Grabbing a hand bar he put two 35-pound weights on it and began to curl it with one arm. After ten reps he did the other arm to loosen up his arms. Yet somehow out of all the people to be lifting, Richard from the top of the concrete steps spotted Jesse. The elite, all of them, including the leader followed Richard, with the leader being the man right behind Rich in order to show his dominance as the leader, went from the steps to Jesse in a matter of minutes. The sections quickly cleared to the side for the Elite group of men. They walked straight through the basketball court where the game stopped abruptly for them. It was as if they were the president in his limo for everybody stepped onto the side of the street to gawk at what was taking place. There the Elite group stepped up into the weights area, and everyone was quite. The only thing you could hear was the gritting of the teeth of the leader before he spit out some tobacco juice at the side of his feet. Staring at Jesse the leader extended his hand in a gesture of friendship. Jesse accepted and smiled. ’I’ve heard a lot about you Mr. Jamester, and I’d have to say I’ve been looking forward to meeting you,’ stated the leader as he shook Jamester’s hand, ’My name is Curt, but everybody calls me the C-Wolf. I heard you have connections to the outside…’ ’Yes I do. Why do you ask?’ wonders Jesse replying with a raised eyebrow. ’Oh… I have my reasons. Let’s talk…’ Jesse and Curt walk with the rest of the elite back up the concrete steps and sit leaving the yard to go back to normal. However, not one pair of eyes don’t move from the sight of Jesse Jamester sitting with the Elite. A fresh piece of meat getting a shot to run with the big boys, it was rarely heard of, and the chaos that would follow was exactly why. (To Be Continued…)
