The Meeting

26 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 26th, 2004 If you've ever seen it, you know that from the rooftops, Dallas looks peaceful. Quiet, almost. Practically serene. From the vantage point of the Crane's Perch, that's exactly what you're supposed to see. It enhances the feeling of safety and calm, making the exclusive lounge a true retreat. But nothing felt safe or calm to the owner of the Perch, and the Lion's Den beneath it. He could feel the storm brewing, practically see it on the horizon. He knew that not even this place, designed explicitly as a sanctuary, would remain safe or calm for long. Soon, no place would. As Akodo Rokku looked out over the city, he had to wonder how much crime went by each night, completely unnoticed. Or worse -- ignored. It brought him some measure of comfort that he was finally doing something to make a difference. In Global Arena, at least, things were going to change. But that peace was a small comfort in the grand scheme of things. We sacrifice what little peace we now have to achieve a greater peace in the future, he thought. We no longer have the option of failure. The telltale ding of the private elevator brought Rokku out of his reverie. He stepped back from the window just as the elevator doors slid open, revealing the men inside. 'Ah, konbanwa,' said Rokku, striding across the rooftop to the elvevator, 'Ichiro, Jushin. Glad you two could make it.' The monochromatically dressed brothers Nakazura stepped out of the elevator, the belts of leather and gold they each had over one shoulder standing in stark contrast to their suits -- black and white, respectively. Rokku clasped his hands in front of his chest and bowed slightly at the waist. The brothers returned the gesture -- a sign of respect among equals. 'We weren't about to miss this meeting,' said Ichiro, 'after all, we wouldn't be much of a team if you called us together and we didn't show up.' 'Plus,' added Jushin, 'nothing good was on TV, and we've already done more than our fair share of sparring today.' 'Glad to know I'm so high on your list of priorities, Jushin,' said Rokku, 'and by the way -- nice jewelry. This meeting isn't meant to be that formal, though.' 'We thought that if we left them at home, either the Sons or the Syndicate would probably try to steal them,' said Ichiro. 'This way,' said Jushin, glancing at the title and patting it appreciatively, 'if anyone wants them, they have to come through us -- plus, I hear gold like this is a like a magnet to the ladies. And I don't know about the rest of you, but I plan on visiting the club downstairs when we're done here.' Rokku chuckled. 'A man after my own heart. Right this way, gents. Everybody else is already here.' Rokku lead Ichiro and Jushin through the maze of curtains and free-standing walls that split the Perch into modular 'rooms', making it customizable to its visitors' needs. 'No! NonononoNO!' Ichiro and Jushin's eyebrows raised at the sound of the scream. 'Was that?' started Ichiro. '?Akiyara?' finished Jushin. Rokku just chuckled, and pushed aside a curtain. Jin Akiyara looked down at the controller in his hands dejectedly, as Sakura Minako high-fived Yoshiko. On the giant plasma screen television in front of them, flashed a message: Seung Mina Wins! 'A can't believe she beat me again,' grumbled Jin, glancing over his shoulder at the arriving trio, 'Rokku, this woman must be some kind of cyborg.' 'You don't have to tell me that, said the Steel Lion, 'I can't even beat her with Ivy.' 'I call next game,' declared Jushin. 'No fair,' pouted Sakura, 'I wanted next game.' 'You can have your turn,' said Jin, handing the controller to his girlfriend, 'watch out for her Jushin. She does mean things with Nightmare.' Jushin reached for Yoshiko's controller, only to see the screen go black. All eyes turned to Rokku, and the remote control in his hand. 'Sorry folks,' said the Steel Lion, putting the remote down on top of the X-Box, 'but now that everyone's here, we've got to put the games on hold. It's time for business.' Yoshiko looked up from the couch at Rokku, with big, puppy-dog eyes. 'Just one more game?' she asked. 'Aww, sweetie,' said Rokku, leaning down to her, 'no freakin' way.' With that, he wrapped his arms around her waist and slung her up onto his shoulder. 'Follow me, folks,' said Rokku, marching off. Yoshiko simply looked at the group and rolled her eyes. As they filed off after the Steel Lion, Sakura caught Jin glancing sideways at her with a contemplative smile. 'Don't even think about it, Akiyara,' she said, 'we haven't been together that long.' Sighing disappointedly, Jin fell into step behind the others. *** After a minute or two of following Rokku through the twisting maze of sliding walls and billowy curtains, the group came upon two burly men standing in front of a white door. 'Evenin' Larry, Carl,' said Rokku, finally putting Yoshiko down and slashing his keycard through the reader next to the door. 'Evenin' sir,' both guards responded, as the door slid open with a hiss. Rokku nodded, and stepped through the door, motioning for the others to follow him. 'Welcome to Sanctuary,' said the Steel Lion, as the others took in the room. It was completely white, from ceiling to floor. In the center of the room was a long black table, surrounded by comfortable looking chairs -- with a notebook computer at each seat. 'This will be our staging area,' continued Rokku, 'the walls are steel-reinforced concrete, under a layer of lead, under a layer of plexiglass, and everything's completely soundproofed -- not even the high end laser microphones can pick up sound inside this place. I knew as soon as it was made clear that the Echelon was back on it's feet that we'd need a place like this. In here we can make plans without having to worry about them peeking in on us.' 'Impressive,' said Ichiro, 'you've really taken the initiative here, Lion.' 'I figured as the de facto leader of this group, that was kind of my job,' said Rokku, 'Now please, everyone take a seat.' Once everyone had chosen their seat, Rokku hit a few keys on the notebook in front of him. Immediately, several windows opened up on the screens in front of everyone present. 'This is the hard information that we've dug up on the Echelon so far. And frankly? well a lot of it's gibberish to me, so I'll let the lady who speaks legalese handle this part. Sweetheart?' Yoshiko smiled demurely. 'It's about time, Lion,' she said, her smile quickly turning into a glare, 'I thought you were going to spend all night parading around like you did everything by yourself.' 'Perish the thought, pookie,' said Rokku, grinning across the table at her. Yoshiko just rolled her eyes. 'Okay,' said the Silken Crane, 'I did a little research, and here's what we know. Echelon Inc. is, for lack of a better word, huge. They've got their fingers in all sorts of pies; you'll see a list there on your screen. It's not really important exactly what they're into, it's more important how much of it and where. You'll notice that they've got branches spread out all over the globe. Combine all that with the resources of their sister company SomaCorp, and we are basically dealling with some serious shit here people.' 'I knew the Echelon was dirty,' said Jin, going over the list incredulously, 'but I didn't think they were this dirty.' 'Well, start thinking it,' said Rokku, 'the Echelon is very powerful and they're just about everywhere. The scariest thing though, is that their interest in Global Wrestling seems to be entirely personal, rather than financial -- which means they're not coming from a sane place, and will be willing to take serious losses before they finally pull out.' 'We already knew the Echelon was going to be a problem,' said Ichiro, 'is that all your brought us here for?' 'No,' said Rokku, 'but first things first, I wanted you all to be 100% aware of what you were getting into. When I originally proposed the Legion, TUE looked to be on the ropes, if not completely out of the picture. With them standing tall, this is a whole new ball game. As dangerous as a group like Ice's Elite is, they don't have the resources and the will to hit us where ever it'll most hurt just about anywhere on the planet. TUE does. So, knowing this, does anyone want to back out now?' Rokku's gaze swept over the table, but nobody moved. No hands were raised, and nobody spoke. 'All right,' said the Steel Lion, 'issue one, then: security. We all have loved ones that aren't directly involved in this war that the Echelon will almost definitely target. That's why I had Jin bring you here with him, Sakura-san. Alone, you would have been a prime target.' Sakura nodded mutely, and laced her arms around Jin's. 'My family should be safe,' said Yoshiko, 'there's no way the Echelon will want to bring the leverage of Kakita Industries to bear on them.' 'There's Uncle Gohei in Tokyo,' said Ichiro, 'but I imagine he'll be perfectly capable of handling himself.' 'I should definitely contact my sister in New York,' said Jin, 'she could be in serious danger.' Rokku nodded. 'My mother in Kyoto's been targetted by the Echelon before,' he said, 'I put security on her then, but no reason not to up it. Okay, Yoshiko, got all that?' Yoshiko's fingers were flying across the keyboard at blinding speed, and she nodded an affirmitive. 'Aeris Akiyara in New York,' said Yoshiko, 'Maiko Akodo in Kyoto, and Gohei Nakazura in Tokyo -- just in case. I'm calling in a few favors, they should have full security details monitoring and protecting them within twenty minutes. Daddy's going to want to have words with me for hacking his mainframe later though, I'm sure.' Ichiro raised an eyebrow. 'Yoshiko-san,' he asked, 'is there anything you can't do?' 'Cook,' she immediately replied, 'when you grow up rich, someone else always does that for you.' Ichiro chuckled. 'Okay,' said Rokku, clapping his hands together, 'the security issue's taken care of. Next: plans for High Octane. I've got a few surprises in mind I'd like to discuss with all of you?'
