Remorse Gabery Breed Global Wrestling Promo #805 Date: 1/28/04 Location: Places I used to run through my life. Now I’m slowly walking it. The pain has inserted. It’s like a disease you know? I really didn’t even wake up on time this week. My kids just kept jumping on me….Hell I was almost late for my own mom’s funeral. I feel like a true dip shit… I just sat there. On the couch and watched her lie there motionless. That was the first time I’ve never not seen her move. She used to move around in her sleep too. She was the busiest woman I had ever met. They said a car hit her, and the car got away. Nothing was left. No one saw it. No tire tracks. Not a damn thing. I can’t believe the world’s gotten so bad that you can barely walk across the fucking street to get some Advil. I turn on the TV and hear all this crap. It’s getting useless to watch the news. Hell, the stock market has to do violence as well. When something good happens, stocks go up. When something bad happens, they always go down. It’s like a damn puzzle. What the hell can I do? I’m just a human. I’m the evolved species of a Neanderthal. Great. I fee like nothing any more. I can’t cry because there is nothing to cry about. I can’t share my feeling because I have none. Hell has frozen over, and I am the solution to it. Are people that eager for me to die? Is this what I deserve. For all the sins I’ve committed? Taking away my mom was too far. Sure, I could of gotten beaten up here and there. I might of fell down the stairs, or maybe even get arrested for DUI. I don’t care what the hell it might have been, but killing was never the solution. I can’t believe I’m saying that. I’ve killed how many people for money? Then again it was survival. People will never learn what I’ve been through. Running numbers 16, and killing at 18. I felt as if though I was on top of the world. A pistol of any kind made me happy. I thought I could go around and shoot everyone…but I never though of anyone shooting back. Nobody did, but I think God did. I got the message. I just found a new love. A new addiction, wrestling. He doesn’t like it. Every thing I’ve loved has been taken away from me. No he won’t take my loved ones. My mom was my mom. That’s all I can tell you. She was the mother you always wanted, and though she didn’t like the idea of me having kids, she kept my life because she took care of them. I knew I had to grow u p, but look at me now. I’m making thousands while others are in school studying. I love wrestling. It’s the truth. There’s nothing to like about it. Good money, fine dining, and nice women. Every man’s dream…it doesn’t matter who you are. It’s a great thing to have, and I’m just about there. So whom do I have to blame for? Not myself. My actions are the ones I chose quite nicely. Survival is what I said and I’ll say it again. It was survival from the beginning of the existence and to the very end. I have someone in mind to blame, but this would start one of the biggest wars in history. Do I really care? The answer is simply no. I don’t feel to love right now, and I don’t think I am going to be giving any love. My final adversary is no other then God himself. It’s time to bring out the big toys. No guns, no nothing. I still believe in him, but I think he’s sticking his nose into someone else’s business. They say he’s everywhere, and I do not like that at all. I have a bone to pick with him, and it’ll start this very instance. No one wants to wake up on a sunny day. Who would? Nobody is the answer. Gabery lies with that idea and sleeps on his bed. Though as soon as he moves, he gets a sudden jolt. He’s forced to open his eyes and looks around. He blinks around as the crust falls from the corner of his eyes. He gets up and throws the sheets off of him and has a stare around. Jacob stares at him from the corner with a smile. “Hey dad,” he says and walks towards the bed, and takes his climb clinging onto the sheets and climbing aboard. “What’s up?” Gabery looks at him as if it’s the first time he’s seen him. His senses hit him like a truck, and he lets out a lifeless smile. “Hey Jake,” Gabery replies and takes a look around the room, and sets himself up against the wall of his bed. “Is your auntie home?” Jake shakes his head a couple of times before he stops, “She’s still in Ok-la-homo.” Gabery lets out a chuckle, ‘It’s Oklahoma smart one.” Jake shrugs and then crawls towards Gabery, “Some kid at school called me four eyes…” “You don’t even have glasses,’ Gabery quickly responds taking him into his lap. “I know,” Jake says talking across to a wall. “They said I’m a four eyes and that you’re a drunky.” “Who said this?” Gabery asks with a stern look across his face. “Brian Magenta,” Jake replies and looks up at his dad. “He’s such a loser. He threw glue at me, so I pushed him onto the ground. And, he said his dad could beat you up.” Gabery’s eyes look around the room as if someone is staring him. He feels something, but can’t sense it. “Why didn’t you fight this week?” Jake asks as he rocks back and forth. “Grandma,” is Gabery’s response as he sits there. “When is she coming back?” Jake asks quickly. A minute passes as he stares at the wall. “Never,” he says as a tear slides down his cheek and hits the top of his son’s head.
