Surreptitiousness... thy name is Holmes!

1 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/1 The dark sky above gives off a foreboding rumbling sound before being illuminated for a split second as a bolt of lightning slips through the clouds and crackles on it’s way to the ground. The sky splitting bolt of pure electricity seemed to act as the signal for the weather’s little game of Trojan horse. The thick gray clouds inhabiting the sky simultaneously break open, sending countless millions of their precipitation soldiers spilling toward the ground below in a torrential downpour. Bolts of lighting continue to rip the sky asunder as a whirling wind picks up, whistling through the air as it passes. Splitting off in the opposite direction of his partner John Zero, Deacon Holmes is preparing to make his way into the base. He starts off trotting along the fence, heading to his left toward the corner of it. Once halfway there he looks over his shoulder to see Zero already out of sight as he is on way to shut down the power in order to make way for Holmes? infiltration. Having reached the corner, Deacon then begins moving right, down toward the front of the building, but still off to the left as there are three men standing in front the building wearing raincoats and brandishing assault rifles. A lone hummer is parked next to the men toward the left, most likely full of more guns and ammo to use in case their current armaments run out. As Holmes gets closer he slows down so as not to make much nosie and begins cutting inward toward the hummer. Still out of sight, Deacon crouches down on the far side of the vehicle and takes a quick glance at the LCD screen attached to the inside of his wrist. He then looks to his right and leans forward in order to see around the corner of the building. A large fenced in area juts out from the left side of the building containing several ventilation ducts. This is to be Deacon’s entrance route. Growing increasingly anxious due to being in such close proximity to armed guards, Holmes begins rocking back and forth slightly. “Deac” You there?” comes through Holmes? earpiece in an exhausted sounding voice broken up by deep bated breaths. “HELL YE...” begins Deacon exasperated before realizing the situation he’s currently found in and quieting down a bit. “I mean hell yeah I’m here, what’s taking you so damn long!” What tha hell have you been doing runnin? marathons and shit, why are you out of breath?” he continues. “Thirty, seconds...” replies Zero, still out of breath. “Thirty seconds!” What tha hell you talkin? bout John?” inquires Deacon, still somewhat aggravated by the wait. “Thirty seconds, you’ve got thirty seconds to get through that ventilation duct before the blades start spinning again in there!” replies John, this time more in control of his breathing. Holmes? eyebrows shoot up into his hairline and he shakes his head from side to side in disbelief before muttering “Fuck!” to himself while violently nodding forward as though he were head butting someone. Then as if lightning had just struck his body, Deacon shoots up from the ground and flies across the soggy grass of the facility’s lawn, causing a rooster tail of water to form behind him. Puddles are dispersed into the air as his feet slam down into the ground they occupy along the way. A flash of lighting illuminates the area once more and out of the corner of his eye one of the guards thinks he sees something fly by. He begins turning to investigate but then shrugs it off, figuring it to be nothing more than just a low flying bird being pushed down by the wind. After hearing “Twenty seconds Deac!” come through his ear piece Holmes turns on the jets and runs so fast it would seem as though he’s about to start hydroplaning. Squeezing out his last bit of speed, Deacon runs harder than ever before, pumping his arms and legs as fast as he can. The sound of several small puddles being splashed through in rapid succession is now loud and clear to the guards around the front of the building. The commanding officer signals for them to follow him as he high tails it around the corner. In the distance Holmes hears the guards coming up behind him as he approaches the ten foot high fence surrounding the ventilation ducts. Deacon leaps in the air and then clings to the chain links of the fence like a monkey covered in rubber cement as he scales the slippery surface. “Come on Deac, ten seconds left!” blares through Holmes? earpiece as he nears the top of the fence. Once there, Deacon flings himself over the top legs first and plummets to the ground almost entirely horizontal. He then recovers in mid air and lands in a crouching position, slightly sinking into the soft muddy foundation under flat surface of evenly clipped glass. Stopping for a moment, Holmes looks over his shoulder before exploding from his crouching position and dashing toward the wall. Once there he plants the sole of his right foot and uses it to push himself upward, then quickly presses his left against the wall and pushes off once more. At the height of his wall assisted jump Deacon latches onto the edge of the indented space where a ventilation grate sits. “Five seconds Deac!” rings through Holmes? earpiece with a sense of urgency in John’s voice. Deacon quickly pulls himself up onto the ledge and slides the grate, which is only a little smaller than he is, upward and slips inside, allowing it to slam shut behind him. Once inside, Deacon takes off running with his head slightly bowed in order to keep from knocking it against the top of the tunnel. At the center of the long tunnel three gigantic blades sit motionless. Knowing there can’t be any more than three seconds left on the clock Holmes uses every bit of his four flat speed to get to the fan blades. Noticing them begin to move, Deacon leaps forward into the air and dives in between two of the blades that are slowly beginning to spin. After colliding with the floor of the cylindrical ventilation duct Deacon slides forward a bit and rolls onto his back breathing heavily as he stares blankly at the ceiling while realizing just how close he came to being in separate pieces. After a few moments he hears “You still alive Deac?” in his earpiece and responds “Yeah... somethin like that” he groans as he begins to sit up, studying the fan blade which is now spinning at full speed and considering what might have happened to him had he been just one second too late. Suddenly his eyes grow wide and pan from left to right nervously before he frantically pats each of his limbs, making sure each of his appendages are still in tact. After checking everything else he reluctantly reaches for his crotch. Fearing the worst he slowly reachers down into his pants. Leaving his hand there for a moment he exhales yet another deep breath, this one of relief more so than fatigue. Holmes then raises his arm up into the air and bends it inward across his face so that the LCD sleeve attached to his forearm is at eye level. After confirming his position Deacon slowly rises to his feet with nothing but an empty tunnel ahead of him. He moves forward with gusts of wind from the gigantic whirling fan blades behind him blowing his dreadlocks in a myriad of different directions, causing them to dance in the churning air like snakes being played out of a basket by a charmer’s flute. Still somewhat shaken by his near limb losing experience he radios Zero while approaching the opening at the end of the duct. “Remind me to ask Capo for a raise when we get outta here.” “What the hell are you talking about now Deac?” inquires John amidst the sound of several gunshots. “A raise damnit! I almost got castrated in here a few minutes ago, I’m gettin” paid extra for this shit!” “I think between that computer you blew up and all the cigars you steal out of his office you two are even.” “What!” I didn’t even touch that computer, you’re the one who gave him that disc!” “But who stole it?” “Ya’ll told me steal it! Fuck this man, we’re switchin! Next week... I’m workin” with Hughes!” exclaims Deacon as he reaches the end of the tunnel. At the end of the duct Deacon drops down onto an elevated platform held in place by some elaborate ductwork. Below there is a fence standing about fifteen feet high that spans the length of the entire room, dividing the long narrow room in two. Both sides of the room are more or less the same, except the far end doesn’t have a labyrinth of ducts running through it and in it’s place there is an opening leading into a dimly lit hallway. Still crouching atop the shadowy platform Holmes surveys a single guard walking a simple patrol route, M-16 gripped firmly in hand. He watches as the guard walks in through the hallway, checks both ends of the room, left first then right, and exits again into the hall only to return minutes later. After watching this repetitive routine and seeing the guard step into the hall once more, Deacon leaps from the platform and dives forward, extending his body in order to grab onto the fence during his descent. As soon as they make contact with the cylindrical steel bar running across the top of the fence Holmes? clamoring fingers wrap around it as the rest of his body swings down. Due to his forward momentum Deacon slams hard into the fence, rattling it loudly and potentially alerting the guard who must be on his return route by now. As a result of the impact Holmes also lost grip with one of his hands, along with all the wind in his body. Deacon struggles to scale the rest of the way over the fence but is finally able to hoist himself over the top and land crouching on the other side. Realizing that the guard can’t be too far away Holmes dashes across the room, passing the hall on the way, while pulling his hood over his head. He then dives into the darkened corner on the right side of the room. Once there he huddles up against the wall and bows his head down in order to more closely blend in with the darkness. The guard returns, and true to routine heads toward the left side of the room. As the guard approaches the wall at the left end of the room Holmes explodes from the corner and shoots across the room unseen and into the dimly lit hallway like a bat out of hell. Deacon quickly moves along the dim corridor with sparse lighting attached to the walls in random positions. Hearing the guard’s footsteps echoing through the hall behind him Holmes ducks inside an alcove hidden in the darkness and waits for the man to pass. Deacon grows more and more anxious with each echoing footstep growing louder and louder as the man approaches. Holmes reaches into the dual holster at his back and draws one of his two custom made desert eagles, grabbing the gun by the handle first, then, after second though, decides to hold it by the barrel. As the guard passes Holmes reaches out from the darkness and throws an arm around the man’s neck from behind, then, before he has a chance to yell anything that might alert a nearby comrade, knocks him unconscious with the butt of his gun. Allowing the limp body to slump in the shadow blanketed alcove, Deacon slips back out into the hall. Tip toeing down the corridor, Holmes presses up against the walls and slowly makes his way to a set of rickety metal stairs situated to the right of the dead end hall. After peeking around the corner and into the hallway the stairs lead into Deacon scurries up the short flight of stairs and into yet another hallway, this one a drastic change. Well lit, painted walls as opposed to the plain concrete of the previous corridor, and carpeted floors. This looked more like an office building than any military installation or secret lab. Holmes creeps silently through the hallway ducking under large panes of glass that make up the upper part of many of the walls. Although many of the rooms are empty, Deacon takes no chances, stealth is a must. Checking his LCD map once more he looks toward the end of the hall which leads into a wide open foyer. Deacon surreptitiously makes his way into the large foyer illuminated by only a single light hanging from the ceiling and hides behind one of the many black marble pillars that line the east and west ends of the room. The entire floor of the room is made of black marble as well except for a large insignia done in red toward the center of the floor. To the north end of the room a single door stands guarded by a man holding an M4. The door leads into an elevator, which according to Holmes? LCD blueprints will take him up to the third floor, which is where the terminal room should be. Creeping from pillar to pillar, Deacon finally stops at the final one, closest to the guard. Holmes peeks around the corner from his shadowy hideout and watches as the guard steadily scans the room from left to right over and over looking for something out of place. Deacon grabs one of the pistols from his holster and removes a single bullet from the clip, he then leans out from the pillar just enough to have his right side exposed. Once in position Holmes flings the bullet into the air and across the room, causing it to strike the ground and roll into the center of the floor. Wondering what that noise was the suspicious guard wanders toward the center of the room to inspect. Seizing the opportunity, Deacon immediately shoots from the shadows and runs straight for the elevator. Fearing the guard could turn around at any moment and pump him full of hot lead, Deacon repeatedly mashes the up arrow on the control panel while nervously looking over his shoulder. After a moment the doors slide open and he quickly steps in. Before even bothering to press number of the floor he’d like to go to Holmes smashes his fist into the “close door” key. He then presses “3'. The elevator glides up through the shaft effortlessly, quickly passing the second floor and abruptly stopping at the third. Deacon steps out into yet another hallway, this one longer, and lined with closed doors. A quick glace at his map reveals a door to his left to be the terminal room. Holmes quietly approaches the door and just as he goes to reach for the knob it begins to jiggle. A rush of anxiety flows through Deacon’s body as he darts from one position to the next looks for somewhere to seek cover from whatever may be behind that door. Finding no place suitable to stash himself Deacon’s adrenaline filled body instantaneously switches from flight to fight mode as he draw both his pistols from their holster. The door slowly creaks open and the man on the other side is met with a double barreled hello that echoes through the hall and give much of the walls inside the room a temporary red paint job that drips down onto the floor and stains the carpet. Holmes then steps inside the room and immediately shuts the door, locking it as well. A large computer terminal resembling something from the first coming of the super computer takes up a large portion of the room. Deacon walks up to it and beings looking for a way to eject the disc. Temporarily mystified by all the blinking lights and buttons, Holmes finally locates the proper switch and ejects the disc. After depositing it inside his pocket he prepares to make his escape. Zero was to create a grand distraction, enabling the two to them to walk out untouched amidst all the chaos. “It’s time to get ghost John.” he informs his partner in crime. “I haven’t picked up that ebonics dictionary yet Deac, you’re gonna have to put a little more suburbs in it than that for me.” retorts John. “Umm, how’s this... It’s time to get tha fuck outta here!” exclaims Holmes, preparing to make an expedient exit into the night. “Oh, well why didn’t you say so” One distraction coming up...” states John matter of factly. Suddenly the ground beneath Deacon’s feet begins to shake as a deep booming sound pulsates through the air. Holmes moves toward the window to see that one of the buildings across the way has erupted in flames and that the sky is filled with stone debris from the building. “And they say I’m always blowin” stuff up...” Deacon mutters to himself as he slides the window open and gets ready to vacate the premises. Just before hopping out the window and onto the fire escape Holmes spots something out of the corner of his eye, blueprints. He walks over to a desk and momentarily studies them before realizing he has no clue what the hell he’s looking at. “Ah well, you look important, so I guess you’re coming with me.” he explains to the blueprints as though they could understand him. He folds the large sheet of paper and tucks it away in his waist band under his hoodie and then hops out the window and begins descending the fire escape. Below him throngs of soldiers rush toward the burning building as a single body is seen flying across the field toward the gate running full speed. “Damn, John got that get down.” he remarks to himself while taking note of John’s surprising speed. Approaching the bottom rung of the escape, Holmes drops down onto the ground, which is still soggy from the heavy rain, and also sprints across the field toward the gate. Once there he hops over, kicking off one of the bars for extra height, and makes his way to his black Porsche Carerra GT Turbo, where John is already waiting. The two men quickly hop inside the car and speed off, tires screeching as they go. Inside the car Deacon notices that the right sleeve of Zero’s jacket is ripped and that his forearm is bleeding. He decides to pay it no mind though as it can be discussed later, after it’s certain that they’ve made a clean getaway. As the car flies down the highway appearing to be nothing more than a black blur to opposing motorists the rain begins to pick up once again.
