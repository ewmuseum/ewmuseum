Unexpected dinner guest

25 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/25/04 It was another Sunday night at Coretta Randall's house. 'I can't believe you went there with Deacon Holmes,' said Kyle as he and Brick set the table. 'Implying you had sex with someone's girlfriend, I mean, that's kinda going out there, isn't it?' 'I don't think so,' Brick replied. 'Besides, remember how he disrespected Monica? He didn't have a girlfriend then. The most I could do then was just kick his ass. At least, the most I could do without getting a life sentence.' 'I still think it was kind of crass,' said Monica. 'Throwing that pair of thong underwear at her and saying she left it in your hotel room? That was pushing it, even without the broken condom remark. Besides, you know you only did that for Gunner.' 'I did it for both you and Gunner. Deacon needs to be taught a lesson and I ain't done by a long shot.' 'Don't get me wrong. Deacon Holmes isn't one of my favorite people, either, but I still don't think you should antagonize him like that.' 'Jamal!' Coretta called from the kitchen. 'Yeah, Ma?' 'Come and help with the food.' 'Ma, I'm setting the table. Why can't Monica help?' 'Because Monica is pregnant. You're not. Unless you have yet another revelation you'd like to share.' 'No, no. I'll be right there.' Brick then immediately headed into the kitchen. 'Is this what you need me to take into the dining room?' 'Not just yet. You and I need to talk.' 'About what?' 'What is this foolishness you've got going on with Deacon Holmes and his girlfriend?' 'What do you mean?' 'Don't try to play dumb with me, Jamal. I know you too well. And I think this game you're playing is a dangerous one.' 'Yeah, well, I can't think of anyone who deserves this more than Deacon.' 'Be that as it may, that doesn't mean you should. Mark my words, this is going to get worse before it gets better. Now, take the chicken into the dining room.' 'Yes, Ma.' As Brick and Coretta carried the dinner into the dining room, he spied Kyle and Monica on the couch, kissing. Coretta cleared her throat. 'Oh, sorry, Mom,' said Monica as she and Kyle quickly rose off the couch. 'Mmm hmm, that's why y'all are havin' twins!' 'Oh, Jamal, hush! Monica, you are a married woman, not a sixteen-year-old. You don't have to apologize for showing your husband affection, even if it's in my house.' Just then, Kyle and Monica went to help put the food on the table. 'So, Jamal, you ready for Master of the Mat?' 'As ready as I can be. I get through this, I get that much closer to becoming Master of the Mat. It's the one title in Global Wrestling I've never won.' 'Not true. What about the womens' title?' 'Real funny, Monica.' 'So, who else is in the match?' 'There's Trey Reed, John Zero, Ely Braxton...' 'Aren't you and he friends?' 'Yeah, it's cool.' 'So, who else?' 'There's this new cat Dagoth Kinslayer...' 'Dag-what?' 'That's what Aaron said.' 'And the sixth man?' 'Oh, the sixth man is...' Suddenly, there was a knock at the door. 'Who could that be?' 'I'll get it.' Brick went to the vestibule and opened the door, and who should be on the other side, but... Train. Needless to say, neither man was thrilled to see the other. 'AW, HELL NAW!!!' both men cried out in unison. 'What the hell are you doin' here?!' 'What the hell are you doin' here?!' 'This is my mother's house and I asked you first!' 'Jamal, is something wrong?' Coretta asked as she walked up to Brick. 'Ma, go back into the dining room.' 'Oh, you're Brick's mother?' 'Jamal, isn't this...?' 'Yeah, Ma, this is Train.' 'My son often talks about you.' 'Yeah, I'll bet...uh, I mean, I can imagine...' 'So, how can we help you?' 'Tellin' him to go to hell is a good start,' Brick mumbled. 'I'm sorry?' said Coretta. 'Nothing, Ma.' 'Well, ma'am, I just happened to be in the neighborhood when my car broke down.' 'I thought you lived in Chicago.' 'I'm visiting relatives. Anyway, I tried to call AAA, but my cell phone isn't getting a good signal. So, I came to see if I could use your phone.' 'What?!' 'Certainly.' 'WHAT?!' 'Jamal, he needs help.' 'What he needs is a foot in his...' Just then, Coretta cut him off with a slap on the back of his head. 'OW!' Train couldn't help but snicker. 'Won't you come in?' 'Thank you so much, Mrs. Randall.' As he walked in, Train then shot a sly smirk Brick's way. 'The phone is right here.' 'Thank you.' Brick then walked back into the dining room, keeping an eye on Train. 'Brick, is that who I think it is?' 'If you think it's the other pain in my ass, it is.' 'What's he doing here?' 'He says his car is broken down and he needs to call AAA. Your mother told him he could.' 'I know this can't make you happy,' said Kyle. 'That's an under--' Just then, they heard chuckling coming from the living room. 'Is he laughing?' 'What could be so funny?' Then something started to dawn on Brick. 'Aw damn, the baby pictures!' Brick rushed into the living room, where he saw Train laughing while perusing a framed baby picture. It was of Brick, when he was 10 months old. He was wearing overalls and a sky blue T-shirt. 'Oh, Brick,' Train said, 'you were so cuuuuute!!' Brick rushed over to Train, snatched the photo out of Train's hands and placed it back on the shelf. He then leaned closer towards Train. 'My mother does not like people touching her photos,' Brick growled through gritted teeth. 'You invadin' my personal space, dawg.' 'What did AAA tell you?' asked Coretta. 'They said they'd be here in a half hour.' 'Well, this may seem unusual, just having met you, but would you like to join us for dinner?' Brick quickly turned to his mother when he heard this. He mouthed 'No' and made various gestures, suggesting that he didn't want this. 'Nah, I couldn't impose. I mean, this looks like a family thing and, besides, with all the beef that's gone on between your son and myself...' Just then, Train took a whiff. 'Is that fried chicken?' 'Yes, it is.' 'Well, I guess I could stay for a little while.' 'Jamal, you wouldn't mind making an extra setting, would you?' 'No, Ma, I wouldn't.' Brick then begrudgingly walked into the dining room and took out an extra plate and set of utensils. 'Are you sure there's enough?' 'Oh, of course there is. I always cook extra, just in case. Monica, Kyle, we have a guest for dinner. This is...' 'Train.' 'Is that what your mother named you?' 'No, ma'am. It's Anthony. Anthony James.' 'Anthony, this is Jamal's sister Monica and her husband Kyle.' Train shook hands with Monica. As he did, he looked her up and down. 'Very nice...to meet you, I mean. Yes, very nice.' Right away, Kyle stepped in and took Train's hand. 'So, you and Brick are in the same match at Master of the Mat, huh?' 'Uh, yeah. You got a hell of a grip, dawg.' 'Excuse me?' 'Sorry, I mean, you've got a heck of a grip.' Train then walked to his seat, whispering, 'Got a grip like a little bitch.' 'I think it would be a good idea for our guest to bless the food tonight.' Upon hearing this, a look of apprehension crossed Train's face. It had been a while since he'd been in church. He hadn't even seen 'The Ten Commandments' in years. So, saying grace seemed like a challenge. 'Anthony, will you do the honors?' 'Umm...uh...everybody, uh, bow your heads...dear Lord...thank you for this evening...and for this company and the hospitality they have shown me...I must say I feel blessed to be here and to know my numerous defeats of Jamal in the ring doesn't mean we can't be friends outside of the ring. I ask that you bless this food, the ones who made it and the ones who partake of it. Amen.' 'Amen.' Brick fought every urge to strangle Train right there on the dining room table. Train eagerly stood up to fix his plate. 'Oh no, you're a guest in my home. Allow me.' 'Ma, I'm sure Train would like to make his own plate. I mean, he has two...' 'Jamal, Anthony is a guest.' 'Yes, Ma.' Meanwhile, Train held his plate. 'Yes, I'd like that piece, right there. Oh and some macaroni and cheese would be nice. Oh and definitely some collard greens and yams.' 'How many times did you beat my brother?' 'Twice, but it's hard to count.' 'Yeah, but I'm the cat who stopped your undefeated streak. Let's not forget.' 'Boys, don't discuss business at the table.' 'You're absolutely right, Mrs. Randall. You know, Brick, what you're doing to Deacon and Rhaiza is just shameful. I would have thought better of you.' 'I told him the same thing.' 'Oh, I wholeheartedly agree.' 'Oh, is that right?' asked Brick. 'Tell me, Anthony, how did your mother feel about you killing a cat on national TV? Oh, and what about that brawl you were in with those Dallas Cowboys? And the time you sided with a Nazi?' 'Can we please change the subject?' 'Yes, let's.' 'Sorry, Ma.' 'Sorry, Mrs. Randall. Monica, is it? I don't mean to be rude...' 'Ha!' '..but you seem to be packing that food away.' 'Well, I'm eating for three these days so...' 'Oh, you mean you're...' 'Having twins, yes.' 'Twins. You sure don't look it.' 'Um, thank you.' 'Yeah, definitely not.' Now it was Kyle's turn to fight down murderous urges. 'You know, I think I heard a truck pulling up. That might be AAA.' 'I didn't hear anything.' 'I think I heard it, too' said Brick. 'Why don't we go and check?' Brick and Kyle swiftly went out into the vestibule. 'I'm gonna kill him!' Kyle whispered. 'Not before me, you're not. What the hell was Ma thinking, inviting him to dinner?' 'Maybe she did it to teach you a lesson.' 'Well, she could have just told me to go out back an' get a switch off a tree! It would have been less painful! OK, let's go back into the dining room.' As they re-entered the dining room, Train's chair was conspicuously closer to Monica's. 'Ahem!' said Kyle. 'Oh, I was just showing Monica my niece and nephew.' 'They're adorable.' 'Well, I know you're definitely going to have some beautiful babies.' 'Yes, we will,' Kyle said, placing particular emphasis on the word 'we'. 'Pretty mother...' Just then he felt Coretta's eyes on him. 'To be. Pretty mother to be. May I have some more macaroni and cheese, please?' The rest of the half hour just seemed to breeze by. AAA had sent a tow truck and it finally arrived. Now it was time for Train to leave, a time Brick thought couldn't have come sooner. Train, however, wasn't leaving empty-handed. He had 'Well, it was so nice meeting you, Mrs. Randall. Thank you for everything.' 'It's nothing. 'Ma!' called Brick. 'Who took the last chicken leg?' 'I just gave it to Anthony.' 'What?! Ma, you know that's my favorite piece o' the chicken!' 'Jamal, could you walk our guest out?' 'Gladly.' Brick and Train walked to the door. 'You know, Brick, you got a nice family. Nice mother. Fine sister. Although her being your sister, I didn't think she'd be interested in pretty boys like that cat she married.' 'Even if my sister weren't married, there's no way in hell I'd let you anywhere near her.' 'Aw, Brick, I'm hurt. Really. Oh, an' you ain't gotta worry 'bout me attackin' your peoples. If I got a problem with you, I'll take it up with you. Just so you know. See you in Toronto.' It was then that Train took his leave and walked out the door and over to the tow truck. As he got in on the passenger's side, he looked back at the house and waved goodbye to Brick, smirking as the truck drove off. 'Well, Jamal, you managed to survive tonight. It wasn't really that bad, was it?' 'I think I left something in my car,' Brick replied. 'I'll be right back.' Brick went down the walkway and walked to his car. After getting in, he closed and locked all the doors. He checked to see if all the windows were rolled up. They were. He took a deep breath and screamed 'MOTHERFUCKER!!!' at the top of his lungs. Brick then got out of his car and walked back to the house. 'Did you find what you were looking for?' 'No. I guess I was mistaken.' 'Believe it or not, Jamal, I had that young man join us for dinner to help you.' 'Help me, Ma? How?' 'Building tolerance.' 'And what about you, Monica?' 'I'll admit I was a little uncomfortable, with him hitting on me.' 'You could tell?' 'Oh, he was so obvious. And speaking of obvious, what about that 'Yes, we will' mess?' 'Oh, so I'm supposed to just let some roughneck hit on my wife?' 'Like I would have given him the time of day. I've had enough roughnecks. You're the man I married.' 'Like I would have let him anywhere near you,' said Brick. 'Well, Jamal, look on the bright side. At least it wasn't Deacon Holmes.' Brick then leaned toward Monica. 'That's not helping, Monica. Ma, could you make me a plate? I got a red-eye flight to catch.' Inwardly, Brick thought, 'Train better pray we don't get to face each other at Master of the Mat.'
