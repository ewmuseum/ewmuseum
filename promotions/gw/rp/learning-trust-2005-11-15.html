Learning Trust...

15 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/15/05 What do you do when you find out that the people that are suppose to be working with you, hide things? Your friends, people who claim to be your brothers, your lovers. Well if your Danielle Northsky you go on a warpath. Danielle was in no mood to play around this week. She got the match she wanted and it would serve Josh and Gray right by the fact that they had to fight in the match, also. However, they both knew she wasn't' happy with them. Danielle had decided that avoiding Gray and Josh was going to be the best for their health. She went to the gym for a work out. Dressed in Sweatpants and a tank top she was ready to do some good strong workout, But she had decided it wouldn't be ring or machines but something to get out a little anger, As she fixed her gear on she stared at the bag hanging in front of her. A loud Caw was heard she turned to see her Angel sitting on his Perch. “You know Angel, if it wasn't for the fact I have you, I think I would of killed Josh and Gray. Hell lets add Ukyo and Blaise to that list. I know damn well they all knew about it.” The bird tilted his head as she spoke to him, his eyes focused on her, showing the intelligence of Danielle's Feathered companion. Danielle proceeded to take a stance in front of the bag. As she snapped her foot forward the impact of it to the bag caused it to jump and start to swing. Angel fluffed his feathers out with sound of the impact. Danielle waited for the bag to swing back towards her and as it did she released a series of kicks and punches with the timing of someone that has all the time in the world. Someone that has a lot on her mind. Someone that is planing to really get even. The sweat forming on her brow, matted her hair to her forehead. She grumbled to herself a little bit as she started to show a little sign of being fatigued. “DAMN IT! They are not going to tire out as easy.” Danielle let out another kick with the force of the first one, but this time the bag didn't go any where. She stumbled, fully expecting the bag to move freely. A head popped out from behind the bag as she regained her footing before she fell. “You are going to hurt yourself working out alone, you know that.” It was her boyfriend, Joshua. Danielle looked up at him, with fire in her eyes she sent another kick to the bag just missing his head. He pulled his head back just in time to ensure that his nose wasn't stung. “Should I take this to mean your still pissed at us?” Dani snapped back at him. “Take it for what you want to. But the door is that way.” Danielle turned away from the bag and walked towards a mat laying out on the floor. She goes into a full straddle split, and starts to stretch her legs even more. Josh walked away from the bag and headed over towards the mat also, but staying out of range of her current weapons. “Look we didn't tell you because, we knew you would try to kill them.” Dani snapped at him again. “But it took someone like Dawn MacFarlane to tell me. My dignity, hell OUR dignity was taken from us for a few minutes for a practical joke... and you didn't want to tell me?” Josh shrugged his shoulders at her. “Maybe she was playing a mind game with you. Telling you that, once she figured out you didn't know about it. To throw you off your game for the night. It looked like it might of worked.” Danielle Pulled her legs in and came to her feet quickly. “What the hell is that supposed to mean” I gave him my all thank you! He just got one up on me. Damn, it wasn't like I was against some rookie, for some rookie match. It's the second time I faced him, and he's gotten me both times. He's a good wrestler. One day I'll get him. Right now, no. Right now I’m the rookie having to learn from my mistakes.” Danielle's face said it all Josh was treading on dangerous ground with the subject he was heading at. “Josh: Look I am just saying, I don't trust that woman thats all. Why was she in the room” What the hell is she doing with my brother?” Dani smiled. “In all honesty, if she's doing anything with your brother, it's not your business. Just like it's not his business what you and I do. But, tell you what, you go try to put on a Corset with wings attached. You have hair even longer then hers, so get your hair stuck and TRY to get it undone with out any help. Lets see how far you get. She admitted to me that she was looking for me or Ukyo. She wanted a woman's help.” Danielle turned to walk away again, only to walk into what seemed to her a wall.... a large Human wall at that. Her eyes traveled up to meet Gray's steel eyes staring back at her. As she did Angel started to scream at the top of his little lungs. “You know you should warn people before sneaking up on them.” Danielle moved to comfort Angel, who was unsettled by the arrival of Gray. As she did she noticed he wasn't the only one there, Blaise and Ukyo were standing by the door of the gym. Ukyo seemed to be chuckling a little maybe at the vision of Josh in a corset, or maybe because of Danielle's run in with Gray. But Danielle really didn't care. As she walked by them. Angel looked at Blaise, and then scurried to the other side of Danielle's body. Angel appeared to be trying to keep an eye on him at the same time. The look of slight confusion on his face and in his eyes. Dani looked Blaise right to the eyes. “One day you will tell me what you truly are Blaise. Because your obviously not who you say you are.” Blaise smiled at her. “I've never told you what or who I am, Sirius Star.” Dani was unsettled a bit by his comment. Danielle walks out the room and heads down the hall way to her room. As Angel moves down her arm and onto her hand, she opens her bed room door, steps in sideways, and then closes it behind her. Placing Angel on what looks to be a home made play gym for him of PVC piping, wood blocks, and toys. Angel happily starts to tear open a small pi’ata sitting on one of the many perches. While he mumbles softly to himself. “At least someone is happy tonight.” Danielle moves to take out her Journal. November, 8th 2005 Just my luck while working out everyone would try to join me. I can't get a moment's peace at all. Things just are not fair. What do you say or do when no one tells you everything. And even the person you thought you could trust you can't? It isn't fair anymore. If I was to go with Angel's reaction to Blaise, he is night and day. Ying to the yang. One moment Angel wants to play with him, the next Angel is acting like he does around Gray. Talking about a cross breed. What do you do when there is no one to talk to. It took Dawn to tell me about the Video, not Josh, not Ukyo, not Gray, and definitely not Blaise. But someone that could very well have her name at the top of her hit list after the Platinum Title Convergence. Danielle sets her pen down moves towards Angel, as she does a shadow catches her eye. “What the fuck?” Danielle Moves to the window, a shadow can be seen moving in the darkness of the back yard. Danielle runs out of her room. “Josh! Gray!” All four that had been there before were sitting around the bar in the kitchen. They appeared to be having a little chit chat session. Josh turned to answer her first. “Whats up” Something wrong?” Danielle looks at him and then moves to open the back door, the lights in the back yard spring to life flooding the back yard. “Someone was just back here. He was looking in my window!” “If someone was back here the lights would of came on, and if they touched the windows the alarm would sound... Are you sure?” Gray tried to state matter-of-factly. Dani spun her head to look at him and snapped, as was her temper to do right now. ? I know what I saw old man, don't try to play that game with me. There was a man back here, looking into my window.” Josh tried to add in something. “I didn't feel anyone.” “That doesn't mean anything. You guys are getting lazy. As you already know, half of our potential enemies are not going to be detected by mundane things. And some could even get by your empathy, Mr. Brand.” Blaise looked out to the back yard, almost as if he was staring into nothing. Danielle knew what he was doing she had seen it before. “Nothing is out here right now. But doesn't mean there WAS nothing here. There are faint traces of something. Nothing that could show there was a major threat. But... You have to remember the Order always has someone watching someone like you.” Danielle turned and looked at him. “Someone like me” Explain please.” Blaise just looked at her, and just smiled. “Later. Right now, we need to talk.” The five of them walked back into the main part of the house, Danielle kept looking over her shoulder she knew someone was there. Some how some way, even if they didn't see the person. ~~Back in the bed room ~~ A faint light could be seen, accompanied by a whispering voice. A voice that was soft and kind to the point that Angel moved closer to the edge of his perch. It looked like he was trying to get as close as possible to the light. “Almost too close my little feathered friend. She senses more then she lets on to the others. That Sense could serve her well.” Angel snuggles into the light. His head down almost as if someone is giving him head and neck scratches. “You know she is truly amazing. Sirius Star, it a true throw back to the Virtues of Heaven. One day she will embrace her gifts. I hope I will be there to see it. But until then I need you to keep watching her for me. She really trusts you. She tells you more then she would ever write down or tell even him. I know others will come looking for her. So for now you are my eyes and ears.” The light fades Angel almost looks disappointed as he is left in the room by himself again. He slowly raises his head and ruffles his feathers. He quickly starts preening them one by one. ~Back in the living room~ “Alright, I'm getting tired of having things hidden from me. I'm only a few days younger then Josh. I may be the youngest in this house, but I'm not exactly a kid.” Dani started off pretty much telling it like it was already. She was tired of all of it. “You guys are hiding shit from me. I don't appreciate it. Like this video thing. Why would you not tell me about that?” Josh goes to answer her. “Because we've all seen how you take things like that. As it is, you went and demanded a match against people that according to Gray we don't' really want to face just yet.” Dani shrugs her shoulder. “So” Why should we wait to pay them back? All three of them. Grest, Dermont and Lanza. I don't care who they are. Hall of Famers? But hall of famers where?” Dani was set on this path. Gray spoke up. “They're geniuses in and out of the ring. They're trained by the Keeper himself.” That took Dani back a bit. But she answered confidently. “So what” I don't care who trained them. I'm trained by you and the Keeper's brother in law. I don't care about them. They ruined that night even more. I just had to find out about it late! Hell, from what I finally saw, they were looking for Blaise for one of their jokes. He's been with them before. He's wrestled with them before, right?” Blaise was leaning against the wall, watching the interaction between them all. “Yes, I have. I'm the one who warned Gray about them. He then got in contact with Annie and got the necessary videos and that. One thing that'll be going for you all is that they're rusty as hell. They've not really been in the ring other then the one or two matches here in over a year.” Dani's hand were wild, flailing as she spoke. “Then it's settled. I mean you two have beaten two of the top teams in the fed already. And I'm ready to beat the hell out of Grest. You take the two idiots, I'll take the clown. No worries.” Gray sighed out loud. “Will you stop that?” Dani was getting madder. “Have you all lost your balls or something” It's a challenge, maybe. But damnit, it's a fight we need to do.” Josh suddenly got up and came over to her. “Calm down, okay?” “I don't want to calm down. I want to get it all out! You guys hid stuff from me. You hurt me. You showed that I have to have some distrust in you! I grew up with you two! Blaise and Ukyo, they don't know me. But you two...” Josh's head just went to where he was looking at the ground. The shame flooded his face at those words. “And then I always get this bullshit: 'someone like you'. What the hell do you all mean with that” I'm a person you know, not a fucking lab rat!” Blaise was smiling. He seemed to do that a lot. “What the hell are you smiling about?” He went to a smirk. “You've got my cousin's fire in you.” “Oh” Who would that be?” “The Lady of Secrets herself, Maeb Fey McClancy-Childress.” That comment took Dani off guard. She had remembered meeting her once in the academy. She remembered being able to almost see her spirit as she just spoke, let alone walked around. Dani had nothing to say on that. “You have heard the theories about what you are, Dani. Correct?” Dani just nodded. “You're something that's not expected. You're an anomaly. That's what you are. An Angel of Virtue should not be born to human parents. Most of the angel hybrids we deal with in the Order today are the half breeds. Gibborim or Anakim. Giants. Huge people, seven feet tall, big. Most of them we use fighting to train them. Wrestling, Martial Arts... it's a legal way to keep their prowess up. But you, you're not a giant. You're showing signs of being a true throwback to your angelic ancestor himself.” “Morning Star.” “Yes, his real name Kokabel, which means “Star of God.” The royal astrologer if you will. He fell with two hundred other angels to take human wives and husbands.” That took Dani off guard. “And husbands?” Blaise came off the wall, walking over towards her. “Ukyo already told me that she told you part of what happened up in Alaska. That was a female angel, Zuphlas. A surprise, but it makes your circumstances a bit more understandable. If the dogma that all angels are male is true, you'd have the wrong equipment. But it's apparently not. She knew your great grandfather so many times removed. Confirmed that he was let back into Heaven after defeating some great enemy. But she warned us that they might be after you soon.” Josh glared at Blaise for telling her these things. “But when it comes down to it, we don't know fully what's going on. You've manifested twice... twice. Both times we don't know what happened. You've not fully been open with us on what's happening with those things.” Dani looked down at the ground at that one. “I think I've actually manifested more then that. I just don't remember them fully. Sometimes I dream of flying in my sleep... and then I get scared when I look down at the ground and then I wake up. Whatever is happening when I do that, I don't know.” Blaise smiled again. “Dani... I know you say that you're not a lab rat. But would you be willing to submit to a little experiment?” She looked a bit confused by that request. “Uhm... I guess so” What does it entail?” “These three staying here and you going down the road to my place. There is something there I need you to see. I want to see the reaction.” Dani nodded. “Okay. Let's go then.” She was still in her workout gear, but she didn't care about that. Blaise headed to the door and opened it up. As Dani walked out, he nodded at Ukyo and then headed out behind her. The two were walking down the sidewalk together. Dani looked a bit uncomfortable as she did. Blaise just had a stride to him, his hands clasped behind his back. “Blaise?” “Yes?” “Can I ask you a question?” “Yeah” But not just yet.” That set Dani off a bit, as she walked a little behind him. Her training said she was in an unfamiliar situation and that she needed to be ready for anything. As they approached the house that he shared with Ukyo, he didn't head to the front door but the side yard gate. He opened it for her, and let her walk in first. “Please, head on over to the side over there. Where the garden is.” She did as asked, and as she approached it, she noticed the stunning rose and lily bushes that were apparently hiding something. She stopped before she got to close to it. Blaise kept walking in, towards the center. As he entered into a certain point, to Dani it appeared as if his position shifted slightly. She shook her head to try to get her vision straight. “You're seeing what you are seeing for a reason. There are four plates here marking this ring of tiles as a magic circle of protection. This place is a sacred haven inside of everything else. Come on and step forward.” Dani took a step forward and it felt as if the night chill disappeared fully. Suddenly she felt as if in the height of spring, instead of the middle of autumn. “It's warm here?” Blaise smiled. “Yes. Part of what protections I put on my tree here. Now, for the first thing, I'm going to give you some information that even Joshua doesn't have. It's time we fully introduced ourselves to each other...” Dani looked a bit dumbfounded. “My name in this world is Blaise William Todd McClancy. I've fought under the names 'Hazard' and 'Tetsuo Oni III' before.” She grinned. “I know this. Ukyo talks about you all the time.” He nodded. “But just like you have another name, so do I. But my name has two sides to it. I'm the son of Conner McClancy, the bearer of the Mantle of King Elatha of the Tautha De Dannan. That is to say that my father bears the immortal spirit of one of the Faery folk named Elatha. My name to his kind is Prince Turenn MacElatha. Just like him, and my cousins Sean and Fey... I'm a wood spirit. Similar to what the Greeks called a Dryad. But we're from...” “I've heard it called Tir Na Nog by Annie before.” “Yes, same place. Annie is actually a water sprite of sorts. As is her handmaiden, Kaylee.” “Okay. So this would be your tree then” I thought Dryads had Oaks.” Blaise laughed. “Yeah, they do when born over there. I was born in a little yellow house on the Valley of Fire reservation. I was born over there. This Yucca Tree is native to this area. So I'm tied to it. Kinda strange.” “So being a nature spirit of the trees, that's why Angel sometimes wants to be around you.” Blaise nodded. “Yes. But there is also the side of my mother, a Native of this area.” “Okay?” Blaise looks up at her as he steps closer to his tree. “Let's just say that on that note, I share something in common with Gray.” At that Dani stopped in her tracks. “You're...” “I guess I share stuff from each of you. I'm a half breed like Josh, into music. Like you, I'm a throwback. Like Gray I've an animalistic side. My grandfather is a 'Wendigo' according to him. However, due to my celtic blood I'm a major throw back. He's said that due to the white fur, and green eyes I meet all the traits of something called a White Howler. I've never gone to anyone else for a second opinion.” Dani was getting interested. “Why would that make you a throwback?” Blaise looked at her. “They were wiped out hundreds of years ago by the other Tribes. All but a few who hid in the new world with the two garou tribes here.” “Like the Wendigo.” “Yes. Exactly. And the mixing of the Tautha blood from my father, and the blood of my mother made something that isn't supposed to be. Not unlike... you.” She was beginning to feel something here. He reached out and pulled her closer to something, near his tree. “Now, for the experiment. You see that hanging in my tree?” He pointed to a bundle that looked like the feather wand that her grandfather had made her from her and Morning Star's feathers. “That is a wingtip, smoked and mummified by me. That wing tip belonged to a man who once owned me to a point. When I broke away from him... we fought not once, but twice. The first time I took that from him.” Dani couldn't help but look at it. The feathers were stark white, where hers were ashen with white highlights. “His name was Zadikiel, the Arch Angel of Destiny.” She was almost speechless, but managed to ask a quick question. “What about the second time?” He reached out and pulled a cover off of something that sat vertical in the ground, in front of the tree. Hidden from view was a gold colored blade of a sword. The pommel was also golden colored for the body of what looked like a female angel, her breasts an obvious design to give more of a grip on the handle. The wings of this angel went up and down, up the blade. Currently with the blade point in the ground, the angel was upside down. “OH, it's beautiful!” It was the long sword, the flame tongue of the archangel. “That night, I took more then this from him. But the other thing I took isn't physical. But it's the only thing I know of in this area right now that's of your world.” She began to reach out. “Can I touch it?” Blaise backed off a bit. “By all means...” And she did... And that's all it took as suddenly the sword began to glow with a light about it. Dani's eyes went wide as it did... she didn't remember the rest of it well. ~~ Back at Dani's house.~~ The balcony from her bedroom, three people stood there talking. One of them using Dani's telescope to view the scene going on over in the backyard of the other house. “HOLY SHIT!” It was Josh, as he was leaned over the powerful telescope that Dani used to look at the stars at night. Ukyo commented next as Gray looked on, using his self enhanced vision. “Looks like the Experiment was a success. ~~The bushes nearby~~ A dark figure looked on, as he saw the view in front of him... A smile, teeth so white they would be considered perfect. “So beautiful...” ~~Back to Dani~~ Blaise looked on as he took in the view. Dani Nothsky... Sirus Star stood there as she's meant to be seen. Her ashen wings spread out and high above her head, a good fifteen foot wingspan of ashen gray feathers, not unlike that of certain types of Owls. Dani was laughing, for once she wasn't afraid to let her wings out... Blaise walked over to stand in front of her. “Well, I can only return the favor. You have graced me with your true self.” At that Dani gasped a bit, as she found herself looking into the same green eyes, but not the same face.... But she wasn't afraid. She smiled. ~~End for now~
