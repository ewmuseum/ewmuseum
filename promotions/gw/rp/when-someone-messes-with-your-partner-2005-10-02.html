When someone messes with your partner...

2 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/2/05 Brick, needless to say, had not been in a good mood the past two weeks. The previous week, on the Evolution after Whiplash, Brick made his return to Global Wrestling as Christian Zenith's mystery opponent. The match went back and forth until the rest of the Hunting Grounds interfered, making it a four on one mugging. The attack escalated when his partner Train headed for the Octagon, before being attacked from behind by Tessa Windsor and Dawn McFarlane. Eventually, Emoticon and Ken Partridge, Jr. got involved, and together the four cleared the ring of the Hunting Grounds. But the war was far from over. This past week, Brick was booked in a singles match with Dawn McFarlane. He had an advantage, but a miscalculated dive off the top rope was ultimately his undoing. A Dragon's Fang was the ultimate clincher that gave her the victory. He'd underestimated Dawn. He'd gotten cocky and he'd gotten sloppy. It was a mistake he couldn't afford to make again. Earlier in the show, Zenith had announced that he was placing a special bounty on Train's head: his shot at Shadow's International Title to the individual who took Train out. Throughout the night, many had attempted to collect. One person did: Mike Biddle. Train had barged onto the set of Marvelous Matters in an attempt to call Zenith out. It turned out to be an ambush. By the time Brick got to the scene, the ambulance was already leaving the arena. Brandi Sights, Global's new on-site director, stood watching it drive away. Brick walked up beside her, not really acknowledging her presence. If there was one thing Brick hated, it was seeing his friends and partners being carried off. 'It's ironic. Before this happened, I was chewing Train for possibly ending Zack Black's career and now I'm watching him being driven away in an ambulance, his own career in jeopardy. Damn Biddle! Damn Zenith!' Brick didn't answer. He merely stood, his fists clenched and trembling, a scowl fixed on his face, as he watched the ambulance drive away. 'Brick?' Suddenly, Brick walked over to a stack of empty crates. Without warning, he kicked them over and around. 'GODDAMN MUTHAFUCKIN' HUNTING GROUNDS!!!!' Brick roared before storming down the hallway. 'Y'ALL ARE GONNA FUCKIN' DIE!!!I'MA FUCKIN KILL EVERY LAST ONE O' YOU PIECES O' SHIT!!!!' Brandi had been witness to Brick's intense moods for years. But she very rarely saw them up close. This was one of those rare occassions. Immediately, she ran up to Brick, trying to calm him down. 'Brick, please!' 'I'ma kill them, Brandi! Every last one of them! Biddle, too! Him an' his Back Alley Bitch!' 'Look, Brick...' 'Don't give me 'Look, Brick', Brandi! Biddle put his head on the chopping block tonight after what he did to Train!!' 'You and Train used to hate each other's guts. And now you're the best of buddies all of a sudden?' 'It's like Bogart said in The Maltese Falcon. When someone messes with your partner, you're supposed to do something about it. It doesn't make any difference what you thought of him. He's your partner and you're supposed to do something about it.' 'Actually, he said 'when someone kills your partner'...' 'You know what I mean. Where's Biddle?' 'He's down at ringside. Back Alley Brawler is in a TV title match.' Right away, Brick headed towards the arena, but Brandi immediately stepped in his path. 'Brick, no!' There were two places you did not want to be when Brick was on the warpath: in his sights and in his way. 'Brandi, do not make me move you.' 'Look, there's been enough chaos already!' exclaimed Brandi. 'The last thing I need is you pulling that Stagger Lee shit!' Brick responded with a frustrated grunt. 'Brick, none of this was your fault. You do know that?' Brick sighed and bowed his head. 'I should've been here for him. I wasn't there for Aaron when the Nevermore took him and tortured him. I wasn't there where Donny got jumped. I still haven't found who did it. And now Train...' Brandi walked up behind Brick and placed a hand on his shoulder. 'Brick, I can assure you, Zenith and Biddle aren't gonna get away with this. Not if I have anything to say about it.' Brick started to walk away, then he stopped and turned back to Brandi. 'Do something, Brandi, or I will.'
