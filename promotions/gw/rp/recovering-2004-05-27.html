Recovering

27 May 2004

Date: 5/27/04 It was no longer a question of if J-Gonz would be ready to go in his match, it was a question of when he was in the match. The news had leaked to Gonzalez that the match had been changed to a triple-threat match after the unfortunate accident of Rich Sherwin. Not only had the match been changed to a triple threat, the Television Title was on the line and J-Gonz knew that it was his first shot at stardom. Beep...beep...beep...J-Gonz rested in his room as Chocolate and Paco stood at his side wondering if their boss was going to be okay. The room was adorned with a late Renaissance feel to it. Flowers flood the bed side of J-Gonz, mostly the white rose that is so famous and loved by J-Gonz. 'Chocolate, do you think he'll be ready to go on Monday when he goes into the ring, I know he's the boss and he's one touch Mexican, but he lost a lot of blood.' 'You worry to much Paco, it's el jefe man! This loco once fought two elephants in Mexico just to thatch his roof, so you know he's one loco man.' A cough from the bed startles the two men as J-Gonz shifts to his side and then manages to pull himself up. His face looks bruised from the knife cut that the midget inflicted. J-Gonz looks around trying to figure out the room and his exact location. 'Have I died? Is this heaven?' Chocolate moves closer to J-Gonz as Paco circles around to face him. 'Jefe, are you okay? You're at home, you passed out the other night and we brought you home and called Santa Maria. She's been looking after you.' 'Paco...lean in brother, I gotta tell you something...' With a surprised and saddened look Paco leans in...SMACK! 'The next time I tell you not to take me to a hospital don't listen...' 'But jefe...' 'No buts! You two knew I was in bad shape and you didn't even take me to the hospital!' 'You said...' 'I don't care what I said. Chocolate go fetch my cane ese, Paco get my suit! Daddy's gotta take care of some business.' The two men disappear as J-Gonz manages to crawl out of the bed in his blue pajamas. His face looking swollen he stares into the mirror observing his injuries. 'Man I hope this doesn't keep the mami chulas from digging me. What makes this worse is I got a date tomorrow night with a fine mamasita!' Chocolate returns fist with the cane folowed by Paco. J-Gonz disappears into the bathroom and then returns looking like his usual self adorned in the white suit, the purple bow-tie and the purple vest. His hair is slicked back as he twirls his cane around. 'Boss have you heard about the match change?' 'What match change?' 'Your match, it's no longer just against Jonathan Drachen!' 'What? What do you mean, did they throw an elephant into it too?' 'They made it a triple threat match...' 'For the title homes! Chocolate, tell him who it's against.' 'Si jefe! You're gonna be in a bad match homes, Donny J. McNasty and Drachen.' 'Oooh...is that what you guys expect me to say? Do you expect me to give them putos credit? C'mon ese, I'm El Mariachi, yo soy J-Gonzalez! I make the ladies scream and the kids dream. I'm what people want to be ese!' 'We just thought you should know boss.' 'Yeah yeah. Did you guys do what I told you to do?' 'The thing...' 'Yes Paco, did you guys do it?' 'Yes boss, we took them out and I don't think that they'll be coming back anytime soon.' 'Did anyone recognize you guys?' 'No boss...' 'And?' 'Yes boss, the title is going to be on the line, it was vacated after our friends little accident.' 'Si'moan! That's how I like it and that's how I do business. Ted DiBease couldn't buy the World Title, but this Mexican cholo can steal the gold! Ariba la raza! Lets go celebrate!' 'Oh no you don't!' The three men turn around to find Santa Maria holding a syringe in her hand smiling at them. 'You're in no shape to be going out tonight! Get in that bed....hey get over her J-Gonz! Get over here!' The scene fades out with an image of J-Gonz butt in the air with Santa Maria holding the syringe smiling.
