Working

5 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/5/03 Save for the light of the computer screen, Christina McCoy’s office is extremely dark. Work is being done. Files are being reviewed. The only problem? Christina McCoy isn’t the one doing the work. Sitting at her desk, perusing personnel files is the Global Wrestling World champion, Brick. He finds the one file he’s been looking for: Deacon Holmes. “Ah, here we go.” Brick then proceeds to click the Print icon. Suddenly, he hears the door opening. He looks up to see Christina McCoy, who is surprised to see Brick at her desk. “What are you doing in my office?” “Actually, it’s Bubba’s office and he told me I could drop by any time.” “I doubt he meant that you could break in. Why are you sitting at my computer?” “How’s Ely doing?” “He’s gone to round 2 of the UTT. Don’t change the subject.” “I needed to get some’information.” “What kind of information?” Christina asks as she walks towards the computer and gets a look at the screen. She then turns to Brick. “Brick, what are you planning to do?” “Nothing.” “Nothing” You break into my office, pull up personnel info and then expect me to believe you’re not planning anything?” “I just want to send Deacon a little message.” “Damnit, Brick.” “Look, he started this whole damn thing!” “What are you, eleven?” “You know how that little bastard gets to me!” “So why do you let him”!” “I don’t back down from anybody, least of all some little punk bastard who was still playing Power Rangers in the schoolyard while I was in training.” “Look, you need to keep focused, or you’re going to lose that title you worked so hard and long for.” Christina is right. Brick has worked hard to win the title and he’s going to have to work even harder to keep it. “So, what’s this “message” you’re planning to send Deacon?” Just then, Brick’s cell phone rings. “Yeah” That’s good. No, don’t. I’ll be right down.” “Who was that?” “One of the guys in the Video department. I asked him to do a little favor for me.” “What kind of favor?” “I had him make a tape of my run-ins with Derek Draven.” “Is that the “message?”” “Yes, it is. Because Deacon needs to be educated on just what happens when you piss me off. Maybe I should have included the run-ins with Southern Comfort.” “I’d rather you didn’t.” Before Brick walks off, Christina adds, “Brick, I mean it. Don’t let your personal feelings cloud your judgment.” “I’ll try,” says Brick as he walks off.
