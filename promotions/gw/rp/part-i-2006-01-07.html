Part I

7 January 2006

Date: 1-07-06 November 2nd, 2005? It was going to be greatest night in my career? I was going to reclaim what was stolen from me? “The 4WW North Wind Title” And finally put a certain spoil king in his rightful place? Six feet under with the worms and maggots? “But” But that wasn’t what happened? “On that night” That night would be the start of the rest of my life? ==|Part I: The Reawakening|== The scene opens in a pitch black room. Power was out which explains the room’s current condition. Suddenly, the room’s power mysteriously starts up? The lights flicker on along with the computers and the other electronics in the room. With the power back on the room’s identity was revealed. The room was a cold and creepy research lab that looked like it came right out of a Resident Evil game. Who knows what kinds of experiments have been committed here. But it’s obvious that they weren’t for the sake of mankind. There stands a tall large reinforced door in the far back of the lab. It must be at least four or six sheets of steel within the door. Whoever ran this lab made it clear that they didn’t want anybody entering or escaping. What exactly was behind this door? The scene changes to a faintly large room that was behind the reinforce door. The room was lit up by a single dim crimson spotlight. Underneath the spotlight stands an incubator that can contain anything large. The incubator was protected. It was reinforced with fire-resistant glass that can withstand almost the same kind of the heat that the sun gives off. Why did this incubator need this special kind of glass? There was something inside. But it wasn’t a something. It was a person? Who would be in this incubator? Behind the glass a naked man whose body was covered with many scars. His face couldn’t be made out due to the lighting of the room. The protection even continues in here too. As it was seen that the man was attached to multiple tubes that was injecting God knows what into his veins. What ever was entering inside him was doing its? job by keeping him in a deep sleep. Why was it vital to keep this man in this condition? Who exactly was this man that was so tightly locked away like a prisoner? “Meanwhile” The scene switches to a dark room that was somewhere near the research lab. Within the darkness there was some light coming from the front of this room? The light was from a computer screen. In front of the computer sits alone a mysterious person that was working on something. The person’s identity couldn’t be made due to the person sitting in the shadows. Only thing that was known about the person was that the person wore glasses that gleamed from the light of the screen. Who was this person? The screen shows the diagram of human body with clear viewing of the veins and organs. Mysterious person types something into the computer. The computer screen now shows a black box that reads: >>Project KAGE activation indicated SEDATIVE FEED ? AUTOMATIC SHUT OFF I.V. FEED ? AUTOMATIC SHUT OFF MUSCLE RELAXANT ? AUTOMATIC SHUT OFF Mysterious person checks the room. The person sees that no one was in the room. The mysterious person looks back on the computer screen. What was this person working on in secret? The scene changes to the research lab’s back room. The lights within the incubator mysteriously turn on. Still the man’s face couldn’t be seen. But it was revealed that the man was sound asleep in some kind of coolant fluid that filled the incubator. The man’s eyes starts moving around within his closed eyelids. He was dreaming? In his dream he sees nothing but darkness. The sound of his slow heartbeat was faintly heard within the darkness. Something was seen far away in the darkness. And it appeared that this thing was fast approaching. His heartbeat begins to go faster? As the thing was getting closer? His eyes move around more. Faster? Closer? Cracks start mysteriously appearing on the incubator’s glass. The man’s long raven hair with grey streaks flares up. As a pulsing crimson glow forms around the man’s body. The coolant fluid starts bubbling due to this. Faster? Closer? Waves of energy soon take form around the strange glow. What was happening to this man? Was he dying? Or was it something else? His heart stops beating... As the man and the incubator returns to their normal states. But his eyes were still moving around. In his dream he sees that the thing has mysteriously disappeared. Or so he thought? The thing rapidly soars high up. As it was revealed to be a great red dragon with black hair that runs down its? long scaly back. It lets out a mighty roar that echoed throughout the darkness. This mysterious dragon stops flying. The dragon glares down with its? golden slit eyes. It was like staring into the face of something pure evil. But not? The dragon swoops down. It soon lets out another mighty roar. The dragon opens its? mouth revealing its? many sharp teeth. As it looks like it was about to sneak its? teeth into something. The dragon disappears from view? As the man sharply opens his eyes that was glowing dark crimson. The incubator explodes! The room was covered in broken glass and coolant fluid. On the floor the man lies motionless. Was he dead? A cough was heard? The man shows sign of life. He staggers up. But he immediately falls back down. As his legs were completely numb from sleeping too long. This didn’t stop from trying to stand up. With the use of the room’s sidewall he manages to get back on his feet. He was panting heavily while leaning against the wall. Ignoring the fact that he was standing on broken glass. As well ignoring the many tubes that was still attach to him. The man has awakened? But who was this man? He grabs hold of himself and shivers with his head down. As he was freezing cold. But who wouldn’t be cold after being locked inside of an incubator filled with coolant fluid? The question still remains thou? Who was this man? The man slowly lifts his head. As it becomes clear who this man was. The scar over one eye. The brown eyes that was full of fire and rage. This man was no other then a black bearded Kyo Kushinaga who was better known as the Deathscythe Outlaw. Where exactly has the Outlaw been? How did this happen to him? Why did this happen to him? And who was responsible for this? All these questions have to be answered at another time. As this concludes the first part of Chapter 0 of the Resurrection Saga. Be sure to check out Part II?
