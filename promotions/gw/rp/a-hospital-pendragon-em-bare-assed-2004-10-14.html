A Hospital? Pendragon Em-Bare-Assed?

14 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-14-2004 Still looking a bit worse for wear after his battle with The Nameless One, Pendragon strides confidently out of the turnstile hospital doors. His time at Our Sisters Of Mercy hospital was not a pleasant time for the Lord King’What happened before was even less pleasant... ************************FLASH************************** In his dressing room following his main event matchup at Evolution, Pendragon sprawls on his couch’What the hell? A chain mail couch? Yowza’Anyhow, he sprawls there with an icepack held to his forehead, a bit of blood still crusted on his mail shirt. A knock at the door breaks the silence and Pendragon moves the icepack to reveal a face that is a mix of both elation and pain. He had beaten back the demon tonight, but he felt as though he had to walk through hell to do it. With an annoyed glance at the door, he answers in his strongest voice of command? “Go away.” Just as quickly, he sighs and places the bag of ice back onto his face. The knock, however, will not go away. Pendragon removes the bag once more and glances at the door. A “What the hell?” look is on his face as he answers it once more?“I command thee away, whelp! Thy Lord King is busy?” The knock comes again and Pendragon’s frustration boils over. “GOOD CHRIST!!!” he bellows as he tosses the bag onto a grand looking wooden table and storms to the door, a bit wobbly on his feet. Propped next to the door is the dark blade Lucien which he picks up and belts on before he opens the door? “This had better be important?” He glares into the face of a cowering looking young fellow. The fellow quivers a bit, and doesn’t speak?“Have thee no tongue” What is it that thee would chooseth to disturb mine meditation for?” Pendragon’s eyes burn and his hand drops unconsciously to the hilt of Lucien. With a squeak, the young fellow pushes a sealed envelope into Pendragon’s free left hand. Pendragon stares incredulously at the man who then turns and bolts down the hall, bound for who knows where? “That fellow has a lot to learn of being a man?” he mutters this to himself as he shakes his head and closes the door. Slowly, trying to be steady, he walks himself back to the chain mail couch. With a fluid motion he unbelts Lucien and leans it against the arm of the couch before slowly lowering himself down into a sitting position. With a sigh he looks down at the envelope, muttering to himself, something about “feeling like an old man”. The envelope is unmarked and he smiles as he unsheathes Lucien and slices open the top of the letter? “Worlds largest letter opener, here?” He chuckles softly at his own wit, however weak it may have been. He gently replaces the sword and pulls the folded sheet from the envelope’He reads to himself? Pendragon- You are fast becoming a valuable asset, both to this company and to the world in general. You are hereby ordered to Our Sisters of Mercy for a full checkup. We can’t have you being injured’There are plans for you. Management As Pendragon reads, his eyes grow large’He crumples the paper in a massive hand and looks up, his eyes ablaze?“How dare they order the Lord King about! Why, the local apothecary can give to me all that I need to make mineself better. This is ridiculous!” The paper, now crushed very tightly, is thrown across the room. With a chuckle, Pendragon lies back down on the couch. With a sigh, he realizes he has left the ice bag laying upon the table. With a groan that speaks of some pain, he rises, pads over to the table and grabs it up? “Damnit?” He smiles as he realizes he is talking to himself?“So long as I answer not, I am still sane?” He shakes his head?“But what happens when thee answers?” With a jolt he looks around, hoping nobody heard him actually have a conversation with himself before he laughs again’After all, he is the only one in the room. With a smile on his face, he hobbles over to the couch, lies down and places the bag over his eyes. After a few minutes, another knock at the door, this one a bit more powerful’Pendragon groans but he doesn’t move the bag this time before answering? “DAMNIT! Can thee not allow a man any rest” Leave me be! Good Christ! Thy Lord King commands it!” This is followed by some soft mumbling on the other side of the door. Suddenly, the door is thrust open and the young fellow leads to burly looking men in white suits. Pendragon jumps up, shaken? “Cripes! Someone did hear me! And now yonder brutes have come to take the Lord King away!” He looks around like a cornered beast. He looks longingly at his blade, propped against the couch. Though it is just across the room, it might as well be miles for all the good it will do him as the two orderlies step towards him, one of them holding a syringe. Pendragon holds his hands out towards the two?“Can we not just speak this whole thing out” Surely this is a mistake!” “No mistake, My Lord. You coulda made this easy by just goin down for your checkup, but you had to play harass on the company. They wish to protect their investment?” one of the orderlies smiles as he speaks, and Pendragon cocks his head? “Yet, “tis I am the Lord King’None own me, nor have I been made by any other than mineself’Investment” I am no investment?” The two men draw closer as Pendragon backs up, prepared to fight even though he is battered and exhausted. As they get near, though, Pendragon sighs?“Alright. Peacefully. Let us get this over with.” As they look at one another warily, he walks between them towards the door. One of them nods and the other sticks him with the syringe. The second fellow catches him as he falls into unconsciousness. “Damn but this bastard is heavy! And loony too. We oughtta be taking him to B Ward?” The two orderlies laugh, but the nervous looking young fellow clears his throat. “He is only to be treated for his injuries. This is the only way he will ever go to a hospital. And don’t poke fun at him when he wakes up. At full strength, he would break the two of you over one knee?” The two glance at the smaller fellow, mockingly?.“And don’t forget his sword, less you wish to see him go berserk at the hospital.” One of the men bends down and grabs to sword, taking a moment to bow to the small fellow. He does this as the other drags Pendragon from the room, his hands in the golden clad fellows armpits?””. And when he woke, he found himself in Our Sisters of Mercy Hospital?. *********************FLASH**************************** White light illumined the room, as Pendragon lay motionless on the bed. His blue “robe” is covered by an equally blue blanket folded down to reveal’TA DA! A blue sheet. He looks fairly peaceful, and the only attachment is the heart monitor attached to his finger. He even smiles a bit as he lies there. His eyes flutter briefly before he slowly opens them. He stares straight into an overhead fluorescent? “Bob” Have I died? Bob? Is this the light of which I have heard so many tales? Bob? Where are you?” He remains motionless as he eyes adjust to the light, and when at last he turns his head and sees a conglomeration of mostly dark monitors he gives a start? “What sort of witchery is this?” He rolls from the bed and pads barefoot across the floor. At the end of it’s tether, the heart monitor dislodges from his finger and the one monitor that was active gives a shrill BEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE! sound. Pendragon gasps again but he continues to the closet door, passing a mirror along the way. He glances in, passes by, but comes right back to it? “Good CHRIST! The whole world could see the Lord King’s behind in this skimpy attire! Who would clothe me so!” The door pushes open and Pendragon glimpses a nurse before bounding back into his bed. She looks at him, his face coloring?.Hey, kings can be modest too! With a shake of her head she picks up the heart monitor. She shakes it at him and speaks as if speaking to small child? “Now, now, now! We thought something had happened to you in here! You cannot just go ripping this off! Why, you could have been dead for all we knew! Promise me you will not do it again?” She crosses her arms and stares at the massive fellow sitting on the bed. His face is stone as the embarrassment has faded, and he says nothing? “Come now’You are gonna be a good little boy, aren’t you?” Pendragon cocks an eyebrow at the young nurse’Obviously he is in the wrong place! “Art thee insane as well as empty headed” I be no child, witch! Art thou here to prep me for whatever alchemy thy’Doktors’Have in store?” The word doctors comes difficultly to him, with his emphasis on the “k” sound. The nurse’s smile could make even the hardest man run screaming for the hills; condescending and knowing at the same time? “If you are good for the doctor, you can have a lollipop afterwards, Venn?” She moves over and places the monitor back on Pendragon’s fingers. His mouth works, but no words come. Such audacity to refer to him by name, and such foolishness to treat him as a child. What kind of hospital was this? With a smile and a pat to his head, the nurse leaves and Pendragon is left alone with his bare ass. He looks confused as the door opens again to admit a man who wears the white coat of one of the “doKtors”, and a mask as though he were a court jester? “Are you gonna be a good boy for Dr. Littlefield?” asks the strangely clad man, and Pendragon only gapes?.The fellow had to have the wrong room! “IS EVERYONE INSANE!!!”””” Pendragon shouts, and the doctor just laughs at him’Because, in fact, there is only one person there who is insane?
