The Quickness and The Dead(meat)

18 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/18/04 'Train.... Train.... Train, can you hear me?' Train's eyes opened and the world seemed covered with frosted glass. The glare of the lights were blinding, refracting in unnatural ways as the world struggled to come into focus. Where he is...what just happened to him... Who all the voices around him belong to... All questions Train can't answer as he lies on the concrete floor of the Overload's interview set. The signals to Train's brain are still scattered. Only one thing is perfectly obvious at the moment... judging by the dull ache above his right eye...and the river of warmth flowing down the side of his face, Train knows, whatever happened, he's bleeding. It's the only fact of the situation he can assertain at the moment. The rest of it? His head is too cloudy to put that together yet. Funny. He cant' remember what he knows must have happened just a minute ago, but he can remember everything that's lead him to this point. He could remember what he had to do to earn his way back in the M.O.B and why he was willing to do it. Jamester was a smug son of a bitch. He owed Train a thank you. Instead the muthafucka' thought it was all good to just get up and start showing off for the crowd. Like Train was just some kind of fucking punk or something. Like it had all been in the bag for him. Trick. He thought he was that damn man...until Train made his punk ass eat concrete. That's what the punk got. Train hands him the match on a silver platter and he wants to front like he's bad ass supreme. The only reason he won is because Terrelle Greeniso wanted him to. They called him the Godfather for a reason. Train wasn't nobody's puppet, but Terrelle had been pulling the strings on that match. The thing about gangsters...real gangster is that nothing is ever personal. So when Terrelle called Train back after that first phone call at Train's house on that cell someone left for him to find, Greeniso wanted to know what Train meant by him having to make a choice. Because the shit with Train dropping Makonie wasnt' personal he was able to lay it out without having to deal with a bunch of bitchy whining about betrayal. He just had to tell the truth. Makonie was weighing down the M.O.B. and Train wasn't down if that type of shit was going to be allowed. Train was too busy trying to build a career to deal with all that bullshit. Train knew the type of power the M.O.B. could have in GW. With Train and Greeniso putting their connections together, they could be unstoppable. Terrelle, maybe not legitimate, but definitely a business man, heard him loud and clear. The choice was made in his head...but there was one thing. He wanted a gesture from Train. A show of loyalty. A sacrafice. If Train's career was as important as he claimed Terrelle knew Train would be chomping at the bit to take on the new PTC Global Champion. No reason he shouldn't be. With Jamester's losing streak in GW Train was favored 3 to 1. All the marks had been betting right along that line. The whol betting operation could take a bath on this one. Without a sacrafice that is. Anybody who'd bothered looking back at the tape could see... Train had Jamester dead to rights. He had him up...the hammer was cocked... Jamester was about to catch a 'Hollowpoint' in the dome...right until Jamester...escaped? No. Until Train let him go. Train let him go and took the Jwister and the pin. All to prove he was down for The M.O.B. Terrelle knew Makonie would have never done that. Nor would he have ever Made Terrelle the Money Train did by fucking up the spread on the betting by losing to Jamester. It was a hard choice. Giving up the biggest win of your career. But Train was as much a business man as Greeniso. If the M.O.B. had his back he had the back up to make it in the long run. That's what business men do. They think of the future. Another shot at Jamester would come and he'd show them who the better man was. In the meantime Train would just have to settle for 10% of the take at the betting parlors, which just happened to be 10 times his check from GW, even if he had won. That and having the Greeniso's watching his back. Any business man knows a good deal when he hears it. Dinkler had almost fucked it up for him though. He knew if Christina had found out she would have found him in breech of contract and he'd have been out on his ass. Dinkler doesnt' know how close he came to throwing the Train off the rails.... ~~~~~ April 12, 2004 - Evolution Steven Dinkler is head down the hallway on his way to the ring. His walk is steady until he begins hearing a familiar voice coming from one of the conference rooms down the hall in front of him. Threw the slightly cracked door he can hear a conversation. Dinkler creeps closer, finally recognizing the voice that caught his attention...as that of Terrelle Greensino. Terrelle: and that's why I'm offering you this position within our... organization. You see, by your actions tonight...by your sacrifice, you have proven to me and my associate that you're everything I figured you were. What you've done tonight, by giving up an opportunity that I know you coveted in your heart you've not only proven yourself to me...but you made me a hell of a lot of cash through my betting operations. And now that you've proven yourself? As a part of this organization, you will never be asked to sacrifice anything again. As a matter of fact, now that you've proven your loyalty, you'll get every opportunity you've ever dreamed about and more. You now have the full weight of The M.O.B. behind you. You're now...a Made Man. And... as your present for passing the test? I'm gonna give you what I know your really want. I'm going to let you eliminate one of our members...so you can take his place. Dinkler is chomping at the bit as he creeps up closer and closer to the door, his tape recorder running He gets right up to the door, listening intently as the conversation comes to a halt, waiting for the next words to come. But the only thing that comes is a gigantic hand out of the doorway, quickly wrapping itself around Dinkler’s throat! The door comes all the way open and the body wielding the hand steps out into the hallway backing Dinkler into the wall...the hand owned by Michael Greensino! Michael: Didn't nobody ever tell you'se it ain't nice to listens in on other people's conversations?! Get outta hear you little son of a bitch! Greensino shoves Dinkler to the side, sliding him down the wall and to the floor! Michael: This is M.O.B. Business. Michael stalks back into the conference room and slams the door shut behind him. ~~~~~ If only poor Dinky had know he had about the story of the century. If only he had know Train had been the recipient of that little speech. Fuck 'em. Close but no cigar. The M.O.B. was all about business that night. The put Makonie in the hospital. The handled business in the backstage. GW had a few good prospects for the M.O.B. Terrelle wanted to offer a couple of these kids the chance of a lifetime...a chance to get Made. Of course not everyone was smart enough to see the opportunity. Jay Quickness had the opportunity to join up...but instead of do the smart thing he... That was it! Quickness!!! 'Cocksucker....' mumbled Train outloud, his voice broken, struggling to escape from his battered body. 'Is he going to be alright?' asked Christina McCoy, with a half hearted air of concern. 'I think he'll be ok.' said the trainer as he moved the tiny pin light from eye to eye in front of Train's face checking for pupil reaction. The world was starting to come back to Train now, the fog slowly begining to clear. It had been Quickness. That little bastard. haha... The little punk had some balls on him. He turned down the M.O.B. and Train and Micheal Greeniso had to teach him a little lesson about not taking advantage of an opportunity. He had taken his beating like a man. And now he'd gotten some payback with that...that... That's right.... That bat. Muthafucka... Train could almost respect the shit. He really was M.O.B. material. Or he had been. Now the only thing he could be was an example of what happens when you fuck with the M.O.B....when you fuck with Train. The world began to come into focus as the throbbing in Train's head subsides. Now that Train remembers what the hell happened he chuckles softly to himself. It was the surprise that got to him. Not at being attacked. No, he had basicly expected that. Only he figured it would be Ice doing the hitting. After seeing the way Jamester got his ass handed to him during and after the match he surely had to know this wasn't gonna be some fucking cake walk. He had to know Train was no joke to be laughed off. He knew the type of cat Ice was. He wasn't the monster everyone made him out to be, but he wasn't no joke himself. Anybody with the world title over their should wasn't to be laughed at. Now, as he lay here, Train could only laugh at how much wrestling was just like the streets where he made his bones long before GW was a thought in his mind. When you're the big man in the streets it only means one thing. Half the time you dont' get shit from cat's who's your size. Naw... the trouble comes from them boys who ain't even a quarter of it. The big boys ain't got shit to prove. It's the little guy that needs to make a name. Train had expected differnet,but this was a good lesson. Ice didnt' have shit to prove and if he did he was gonna do it in the ring. That was tight with Train. Quickness was the young buck who thought he was a hard rock. He was the cat that needed to be dealt with. Suddenly the crowd jumps back as Train bolts up, sitting and moving his now clear eyes around the room. 'Whoa, Train hold on...' the Trainer says as Train slowly makes his way to his feet. 'You need to have that cut looked at.' 'Well...lucky us... you're ok.' says Christina in a half sigh of disappintment. 'We have an ambulance waiting to take you to the hospital.' Train slowly brings his hand to his face, searching for the source of the warmth. He feels the stinging above his eye and wipes some of the blood from the side of his face onto his hand. He can only stand and look at it for a moment...knowing what it means. Quickness had drawn first blood. That meant when Train was done... the hood hefner was going to need a transfusion. 'Save the ambulance' Train stated simply, not even shooting her an eye. 'Bring it to Evolution. Quickness is gonna need one.' Train snatches the gauze out of the trainer's hand and applies pressure to his wound as he walks away. 'And you might want to call one for Ice too. When it's all done he'll probably need one.' The trainer looks like he wants to follow after him, convince him that he needs a doctor, but Christina holds him back. 'No.' She says. 'He claims he's such a tough guy.... Now let him prove it.'
