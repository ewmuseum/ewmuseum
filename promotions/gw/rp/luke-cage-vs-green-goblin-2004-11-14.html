Luke Cage vs. Green Goblin

14 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/14/04 Outside on the steps of a courthouse stood members of the press. According to an article by Daily Bugle reporter Ben Urich, Norman Osborn was in fact the infamous Green Goblin, a murderous supervillain who had faced Spider Man numerous times. Among his many victims was Gwen Stacy, the daughter of late police Capt. George Stacy and the love of one Peter Parker alias Spider Man. Normally, Norman Osborn had red hair and blue eyes. But this time, he had long black hair with a goatee and his eyes were soulless white orbs. His lawyer was a young man with spiky blue hair. 'Don't worry,' said Osborn's lawyer. 'All you have to do is choose your words carefully and...' 'Gwen,' 'What?' 'It's just like with Gwen, at the bridge,' Osborn muttered. All of a sudden, the limo came to a quick halt. Then the limo tilted forward, causing Osborn and his lawyer. The chauffeur, meanwhile, was terrified by what he saw through the windshield. It was Luke Cage: Hero for Hire, holding the limo up with his bare hands. After the article was published, Goblin attacked the Daily Bugle building and tried to kill Ben Urich. With Urich at the time was Cage's girlfriend Jessica Jones, who was carrying their child. Fortunately, both mother and child survived the attack, but Cage still wanted justice. 'OSBORN!' roared Cage. 'You think you can mess with my family?!' Cage then turned the limo on its side. He was ready to go all they way to get his vengeance. Meanwhile, inside the limo, Osborn scrambled for his briefcase. Suddenly, who should swing by but Spider Man. But this Spider Man looked a bit more robust. 'I came here, 'cause I was worried Osborn would attack innocents,' Spidey said, in a deep voice with a Texas accent, 'not 'cause I thought you'd attack Osborn!' Then without warning, two flaming pumpkin bombs were hurled out of the car. Spider Man caught one with a web and hurled it into the sky. 'This is why ya don't fight idiots like Osborn in broad daylight!' Meanwhile, Cage had caught the other pumpkin bomb. 'Aw man, I'm gonna lose my shirt.' The pumpkin bomb exploded in Cage's hand. He survived with not so much as a scratch on his body. However, his clothes were considerably damged, but not so much as to eliminate modesty. 'Wow,' said Spidey. 'Unbreakable skin.' 'You need unbreakable pants,' said Spider Man. 'I've heard that before Suddenly, Osborn flew out of the limo on his Goblin Glider, wearing his Goblin mask. Spider Man shot a web-line at the Glider, attempting to follow him. Unexpectedly, Cage grabbed Spidey's leg and swung him back, causing the Goblin Glider to crash. Cage then rushed over to the Goblin and unleashed a severe and merciless beating. Cage's fists met Osborn's body with a fury. Any normal man would have been killed by Cage's blows, but Norman Osborn was the Green Goblin. When it was over, Cage held an unconscious and bloodied Osborn up by his necktie. 'Mr. Cage, a word please?' 'Why did you attack Osborn?' 'No comment' was Cage's only reply. ---- Brick woke up on his couch, his copy of issue 5 of Marvel Comics' The Pulse resting on his chest. It had all been a dream. 'I love this comic,' Brick said.
