A Blast from the Past (Part I)

9 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/09/06 'We have nothing to fear..but fear itself'- Sir Winston Churchill Prelude: Alot of things took place in the illustrious passed of Shane Wethers, deceit, bretrayal and consipiracy were whirling Shane around in a tycoon of destruction. In his EW days, Shane's brother Silus attempted to frame him for an expired visa, but that was not all that Shane's evil sibbling had up his sleeve. He, with the help of Cecil Cassidy planned ways to destroy Wethermania. On top of all that, Shane was in a mid-heat battle that will remain in the memory of all former EW fans, Bobby Stormbringer was number one on Shane's hit list. Today we look at those passed time and learn a little bit more about the invicibility of Wethermania. Interlude: After his first day as a free man after been released from the cold shallow cells he has spent the last week in, Shane has finally be released. Even though the troubles of sleeping in a cold cell may have left his mind, still the troubles and anger of being wrongly accused of taking part in mafia-related acts, have turned Shane's world upside down. He now has built up a wall of anger in which he has to battle each and everyday of his life.. yet, with that aside he still has to concentrate on one of the biggest matches of the Era, when he faces Bobby Stormbringer at the Eternal Wrestling PPV. But still bigger obstacles lay in his path prior to the big event. . Wethers will rise up and hope to defeat Stormbringer and Dexter Thomas in the triple threat this week. Things are heating up even more as all Shane' appearances and Epics have seem to be plagued by the message above which clearly says 'We have nothing to Fear but Fear itself'.....Where are the messages coming from? what does it mean? are Resurrection behind this? only one way to find out! and that will be this FRIDAY! *It’s the break of dawn and the sun can be seen rising just above the horizon. Still there are many dark shadows that cast down on the ground. It’s a very quiet morning, not even the morning bird to sing or the sound of a postman delivering letters. Instead the whole area is filled with peace and quiet. In the neighbourhood stands an expensive red brick house with two floors and a fair sized garden. Inside the house are two people sat at a table in a kitchen, a man and his wife talking over a morning coffee. The man is dressed in a navy blue suit, a light blue shirt and a long green striped silk tie. The wife is wearing only a peach cotton dressing gown and red slippers. The kitchen is big and has yellow and turquoise wallpaper, the cabinets are made out of fine oak and the fridge is large with a water cooler fitted into the door of it. The man and wife begin to talk* Wife: Sean, we have to be careful. We have someone who is still considered a mafia member, what do you think this is gonna do to your career? You’re a highly respected Lawyer and Attorney, if the police find out that your helping out a Mafia Don, then you’ll be locked up. You are putting yourself in jeopardy here. *The man (who we now know is Sean) takes a drink of his coffee and studies the situation. He looks dazed and confused, trying to make sense of it all* Sean: Amy, Shane is like my brother. I have known him since we were younger, I know for a fact he has nothing to do with the Mafia apart from the relation ties. He's already said he doesn't want any part of them. He hate's Huey, he hates all this mafia crap and all he wanted is to get the money that Huey owed him since he was 18! He’s a main target, who knows what he’s capable of doing. He has no other family, his grandfather was the last of kin and he did last year; he was the sort of guy who used to take me and him to football matches when we were younger, he then gets stitched up for acts of terrorism terrorism last year, he finds his Elektra used to have a relationship with the Silus, the same brother that tried to kill him, and now he's been connected to the mafia. What happens next huh? I’ll tell you what happens next, Dorkeus Omega, or whatever they call him nowadays, he starts mouthing of like an disrespectable little yob and all he’s doing is writing his own death warrant because Shane won’t put up with it. If we let Shane go now then who knows what he’ll do, he is a danger. *Amy looks up and sighs* Amy: That’s right! He’s a danger to us all, the children are bound to be frightened and it’s putting your career on the line as I have already said. I really care about Shane, but isn’t there any other way we can go about this? *Sean shakes his head and looks down deep into his coffee, as if he is trying to find an answer in the bottom of the coffee mug* Sean: If there were then I’d be looking into it by now! But there isn’t, all I know is that he’d do the same for me if I was in his position. He needs us right now, he’s lost. All he has inside of him is anger and I’m one of the only people that can calm him down until Immortals. He’s fighting away these demons, but there’s only so much a man can take. *Just then a door opens to the far left of the room. A tired Shane Wethers walks out looking rough. His hair is messed up and he is wearing blue dressing gown, he walks over to the sink and takes a glass from the rack, he fills it with water and then picks up a packet of aspirin from the side of the cabinet. He takes two out and swallows them with water; he then puts the glass in the sink. He stands for a minute looking out of the window and running his hand across his rough beard that has been growing due to not having chance to shave after the hectic week. His face tells the tale of total depression, looking like a lost child trying to find his way out of a maze. Amy and Sean watch on at him. Amy gets up and takes some pancakes and treacle syrup out of the microwave, she walks up to Shane.* Amy: Good Morning, Shane. Looks like someone is in need of a good breakfast to rebuild them. *Shane looks over at her and forces a smile upon his own face. He nods and takes the plate from her, he sits down at the table. He begins eating away at his pancakes as if he is trying to fill a massive hole in his soul. Sean watches on while looking at his paper, he glances up every so often before speaking* Sean: Did you sleep well? *Shane keeps eating, as if it would take a world war to interrupt him from eating. He then puts down the pancake that he was eating. He looks up at Sean whilst wiping his hands on a serviette.* Shane: Nah. Not really, but thanks fer' yer' concern. *He continues to eat once again, wolfing down the pancakes like he hasn’t eaten properly in twelve days (which he actually hasn’t)* Sean: The police say that they need you to answer more questions about the Incident. Also, stormbringer was talking trash about you on television last night. *Shane eats the last of his pancakes and he walks over to the sink, running the water on his hands to try and remove the grease. He then turns the tap back off and dries his hands with a blue and white striped tea towel. Shane leans against the kitchen cabinets and looks over at Sean* Shane: I know, I saw it. Why would I give a damn about Stormbroomer and his two little side kicks? Wow, he’s a tough guy talking all this gothic nonsense about how he’s is god n' he's gonna do this and that. What the guy doesn't understand is, all this gothic crap he talks is just a fairy tale, he's part of that fairy tale. Only problem is nobody can quite tell if he's a dwarf or a goddamn donkey. *Sean closes his paper and folds it in half, he then stands up* Sean: Look, I’m sorry I reminded you about that idiot. *Shane shakes his head* Shane: No, you didn’t remind me of him. I was looking at the cereal box and it reminded me of him. Sean: What? The one with the monkey on the front? Shane: No, the Lucky Charms, The one with the Leprechaun on the front. You see, he’d be the luckiest guy alive to get through the match on Friday in one piece. This is the perfect match to mark the start of a new era and I ain’t laying down for nobody. Woohoo, Bobby may have beaten Ordicus but I ain't Ordicus, I’m “The Eternal Punk”, The New Era, The Eternal punk Shane Wethers, I am All that and a whole lot F’n more! I hope he is spending more time training for the match than he is talking in this useless morbid rap, because I’m not gonna go light on him, this isn't over just an EW title. This is over the simple fact that I don’t like the guy, in fact I pity his stupid, unwise, uncanny, fairytale remarks. The way he thinks that this Friday he can rely on all the fairy dust crap to save him? just because he can trash talk using nothing but cussin? Well if that’s what he thinks then he’s in for a surprise because I’m ready and waiting. *Shane turns away and looks out of the window, the whole room becomes quiet again. Amy glances over at Sean and shrugs. She then walks over to Shane* Amy: Shane? I think that you should lay low from the media for a while, you need to concentrate on your match. The public may not take too kindly to having a 'rumoured' mafia don in the area/ *Shane turns around and looks at Amy with a big frown on his face, he laughs as if she was joking* Shane: Give myself up? Give myself up?” Oh yeah, like I haven’t had enough things go wrong for me this week, I’m sure not gonna lay low just because that dope-ass cousin of mine has some issues. Anyway, I sat in bed watching Omega's useless waffling last night, him talking about all this kanoodling crap as if the pickledick thinks he can intimidate me. No one has ever or will ever intimidate me. I was so bored that all of a sudden I had the mad urge to watch the Ultimate Warriors wrestling classics. *Shane walks over to the table and picks up his plate, he returns to the sink and gives it a rinse under the tap with a bit of soap. He then puts it on the rack and walks towards the door he walked out of earlier* Shane: Thanks for letting me stay; I really appreciate it right now. But I’m gonna have to get going, there’s a lot of work to be done. *Sean walks over to him trying to get him to stay* Sean: Why go now? Where else are you going to go? The Mafia will be waiting at your house, they’ll be waiting for you. You maybe able to take out one or two of them, but you know the mafia...they hunt in groups. Shane: So? Do i look bothered? I’m just gonna try sort out this whole situation. I may even be as lucky as to come across Omega. I don’t expect him to run like a little girl, he doesn’t know the meaning of fear but then again, he doesn’t know the meaning of many words. I don't know what makes him so dumb but it really works. *Shane leaves the room, leaving Sean and Amy in the kitchen with not very much to say. Shane goes into a bedroom where he has spent the night and closes the door behind him. The scene ends and goes to an advert of Shane Wethers, showing his career in lots of different feeds, holding many titles. Then it shows Bobby Stormbringers victories in many different feds* “Omega versus Wethers in a match to defy the ages. This one is personal, Shane Wethers is out to avenge the criminal acts of the EW Rat. They are set to go face to face real soon...but First they're going int a tag match.....Wethers and Stormbringer, one-on-one! Don’t miss this match, this Friday at EW Legends! No souls will be spared!” *The camera’s cut back to the room where Shane had previously entered. The door opens and Shane walks out wearing a grey addidas tracksuit and white trainers. He is carrying a sports bag in his left hard, he walks into the kitchen where Amy and Sean are stood watching him* Shane: Thanks again for everything. *They walk over to him Shane shakes hands with Sean and Amy hugs him and kisses him on the cheek* Sean: If you ever need anything then we are here for you Shane. *Amy nods and agrees with Sean* Amy: Yeah, take care Shane and’be careful. *Shane nods and smiles, he then turns away and walks through the hallway to the front door. He looks back at Sean and Amy, hesitating as if he has something to say, but he changes his mind and he leaves. The sun has now arisen and it is a little bit lighter. The air is quite cold as you can imagine, approaching the wintertime. Shane walks down the street and jogs to the nearest bus stop, just about a quarter mile away from the house. He waits for a couple of minutes until a silver bus approach, it says Palm Beach on the sign above the window screen. Shane pulls his anorak up from his tracksuit, just to try hiding his identity for the time being. He sticks his hand out as the bus pulls in. He walks up the steps of the bus and hands the money to the bus driver, he keeps his head down and speaks in a lower tone than usual.* Shane: One to Palm Beach centre please. *Shane feels the eyes of the bus driver upon him, yet he remains calm and does not look up. The bus driver looks away and prints his ticket on the teller. Just as Shane is about to walk away the bus driver speaks* Driver: I know who you are’Shane. *Shane stops and bites his tongue he turns back round to face the driver, the driver is a shot black man, possibly African, he seems very friendly. The driver laughs* Driver: Don’t worry, I won’t tell anyone. Actually, practically everyone is on your side, we know you haven’t done anything wrong. I just hope you find the scum that has set you up like this. It’s a conspiracy. *Shane looks at him with a plain look* Shane: A conspiracy? Your damn right it’s a conspiracy, and d’ya know what? I LIKE conspiracies’in fact, I’m good at starting them as well as ending them. *Shane turns away and walks to the end of the bus, this time more eyes are upon him. He keeps on walking, trying to act calmly and not give anything away. But the people know, they aren’t fools, they know exactly who he is. Shane chooses to sit on the side seats. As he sits down the bus begins to move and he is sat with a young child who is with his mother. He has a wrestling figure in his hand, it looks like the Shane Wethers figure. The child stares at Shane and moves over to him. Child: I see dead people. *Shane looks on wide-eyed in disbelief. Shane shakes his head and begins to wonder if this day can become any more stranger. After a while the child sits next to Shane again.* Child: Your gonna beat Bobby the Pickledick. *Shane cracks a smile and begins to laugh at what the child just said, as the mother walks over to her child and grabs his hand* Mother: Daniel! Would you stop disturbing people! *the mother looks at Shane* Mother: I’m sorry sir, he sometimes gets a bit bored with these long bus rides. I’ll have to stop him from watching wrestling. Last week he called his father a Huckleberry. We wouldn’t of minded so much but we don’t know what on earth a huckleberry is! *Shane begins laughing again. Feeling the first bit of humour he has been feeling all week long. He can’t believe that the lady doesn’t recognise him. He feels a bit of DDP’s advice working because the lady not recognising Shane is a good thing!* Shane: Don’t worry about it. A Huckleberry is a? *Shane thinks of a good meaning for the word, untrue but still worth saving the kid from no supper when he gets home* Shane:?really really.....er...nice person. *The lady begins to look relieved* Lady: Oh, well in that case I guess my husband and I are indeed huckleberries. Cheerio! *She walks away with her child who is laughing now and waving to Shane. Shane grins. The bus finally comes to Shane’s stop of Palm Beach. He stands up and walks towards the double doors, waiting for them to open. The Bus Driver looks at him* Driver: Good luck Shane. *Shane looks at the driver as the double doors open, people walk through the doors* Shane: Don’t worry, It’s Stormbringer who is gonna need the luck, and if i find out he's behind all this, then he’s gonna need all the luck he can possibly get. *Shane steps off the bus and walks down the road. He passes an Electronics shop that has televisions in the window. The news is on and there is a female reporter talking* Reporter: “After a big week, EW looks in danger. The news of Shane Wethers being arrested rocked the world and now with co-owner Cecil Cassidy having recieved the blame in hiring criminals to work for them, things are beginning to look bleak for the industry. There has already been complaints of attacks on some other members of EW by Wethers, which has done no good to the Eternal Wrestling's image too. At the moment, Shane Wethers is currently wanted by the polcie. Shane was last seen at the house of Lawyer Sean Oldfield, whom..i can reveal exclusively, has disappeared from his home literally minutes ago. His wife was not hurt but in a state of panic. Now police want to interview Shane Wethers, linking him to the disappearence... *Shane walks away looking shocked, swearing and cursing. He carries on walking until he see's his californian house, he carefully sneaks in unseen*
