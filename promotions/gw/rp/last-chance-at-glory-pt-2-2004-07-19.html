Last Chance at Glory Pt.2

19 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 19, 2004 Prologue. Ever get that feeling at night… that eyes are upon your every movement. That the focus you see is only an imagination of what someone else wants you to believe. Have you ever seen a ghost, or lost your nerve in the dark and let your imagination run freely? Fear will do that to a person. Man or woman, we are all alike when fear is involved. You dread your own existence, you fear every fragment of danger, and in the very darkest of times, you wish for your own death… That is how severe fear can be. The ultimate power of darkness, it surges through us like the blood in our veins, not changing ever, just growing more rapidly than life. Creeping inside of us until those unexpected times where your hit with your uncertainties of malevolence. This is when your fear of life, your fear of belief, and your fear of the truth, become void… It’s scary to know theirs powers like this in the world. But what’s even scarier is the fact that people can conquer this fear and make out with a strong force. To conquer fear is almost impossible, so to do such, gives those who can a whole new purpose and reasoning behind their life. They control aspects in which others have never even contemplated. Just imagine, if you could use that control to overcome your worries, your doubts, and those uncertainties that frighten you. The strengths, the positives, the abilities, would be… Unbelievable. Meeting New Friends. ‘What is this? A twenty-two?’ asks Jesse as he stares at the gun in his hands and the desk manager walks over and looks at it. ‘No, that’s a Russian shot gun, one of a kind… Interested in buying it?’ replied the man, not realizing Jesse’s sarcasm when he said twenty-two. I turned and looked around in the place, it was quite a gun shop. Five aisles full of guns on racks that could be taken off to look at with ease, of course the safety locked on each. Then another three aisles of ammo and supplies for the avid hunter and gunman behind them I noticed while walking up the side of the front desk. Trophy heads plated the sides of the walls from the hunters who’d caught big game like a bear, cougar, and a buck. ‘I’m looking for two hand guns. Something nice, very easy to handle, and not too trigger-happy, you know? I don’t want a hole in my leg or foot now,’ I said as I waited for the man. He shuffled around behind the desk looking for what I asked for. A plump fellow, not much life I suppose, as he wasn’t wearing a wedding ring or band, and barely took care of his face from what I could suspect. Acne all over, rough beard growing, and shaggy hair on the side of his head but none on top letting the light shine off his cue ball dome. ‘Ah, here we go!’ he shuttered in a coughed voice. Their he set in front of me a box, and then removed the lid to expose two hand guns, silver barrel, 24 round clips, with extra clips in the box as well. ‘How much?’ I questioned knowing nothing was free and nothing was cheap. ‘$300 for the box,’ he stated as if he knew I wouldn’t buy it had it been that much and smirked as he began to put the lid back on. ‘I’ll take it,’ I muttered and placed three single one hundred-dollar bills and a ten on the desk. The man didn’t even need to count; he knew exactly how much it cost and grabbed the change and gave it to me before I walked out of the store. As I walked out a car pulled up in front of me. Three men jumped out and walked past me, and then the fourth, the driver stepped out. For some reason his face caught my attention, as if I knew him, and then I realized I didn’t but it was an eye catcher. Long blonde hair, a blonde goatee, hazy blue ice eyes, and a muscular tone… He reminded me of an old wrestler I knew named Jason Snow. He stepped up to his hood and lifted it, looking down inside he shook his head. ‘What’s wrong?’ I verbalized as he kicked the bumper in frustration. ‘Damn car is dying on me… I just got it fixed a week ago, but the part must be bad or something cause it’s acting up again,’ he stated in a pissed off way as if he wished the car would blow up. ‘Oh, well do you know how to fix it?’ I asked as I put the box in my truck and looked at him waiting for an answer. ‘Only thing I know is it’s not working, I’m not a mechanic, only know how to change breaks and tires, but besides that, I know shit,’ he replied with a tone I felt I knew very well, as this happened to me a lot when I wrestled with jobbers. ‘Well how about I follow you and if you break down I can give you a ride?’ I offered, as I thought hey, I’m not doing anything else why not give him a ride. He said sure, and I got in my truck and waited till his friends came out. They got in the car and we got on the road. Low and behold, the car broke down as the guy suspected. He got out and we discussed how we’d do this. His friends sat in the bed of my truck as I drove and he was the passenger. We drove for a while before he began to speak, ‘I can’t put my finger on it but don’t I know you from somewhere?’ he said as I hoped he wouldn’t. ‘Eh, maybe, not sure… You live in Texas your whole life?’ I replied trying to maybe push him away from the ‘me being a wrestler’ speech. ‘Yes I have. Oh now I remember, you’re Jesse Jamester on that wrestling show Global Wrestling right? I catch it sometimes, real busy, but if it’s on I’ll watch it,’ he claimed as if he were a big fan but didn’t want to pressure me. ‘Yeah, I wrestle their. Just moved so I’m looking for a new home down here in Texas to stay at for a while, close enough to the Global Arena that is,’ I stuttered trying not to sound desperate for a home. ‘Well, I can give you a bed to sleep in and a roof over your head, if you wish to abide by some rules,’ he followed up as I finished my sentence. ‘Sure, what would they be?’ I wondered, as I knew it had to be something big had he wanted me to abide by rules and such. ‘Oh, just wait… I’ll tell you once we get their.’ A New Home. Slowly we rolled through an iron gate into abandoned loading docks. There the truck stopped, before them was none other than a giant warehouse. The man signaled to go in and so Jesse followed up through the door that began to open as the tires scattered the gravel beneath the truck. Pulling up in the doors closed behind the truck and the room showed only one light in the middle. It was completely empty and right then Jesse gulped as he pulled the box out and grabbed one of the guns and pointed it at the man. ‘You set me up didn’t you?’ Jesse yelled as he pointed the gun at the mans chest. The man smiled, and then extended his hand in a shake, ‘My name is Jeff Michaels, and I own this place. If you would, please get out of the car so I can show you where you’ll be staying.’ Jesse was shocked. Where couldn’t he stay? The place was so empty that it made feel Jesse uneasy, but as well it seemed like the arena after all the fans had gone home. Something Jesse knew well as he had sat in the fans seat and waited for every fan to leave so he could gaze upon the ring. Stepping out he followed Jeff’s orders and followed him to the middle of the warehouse. There the man waved his left hand to one of the three men near the door. Jesse couldn’t make out what was by the door but it seemed like a phone or something. BEEP! BEEP! BEEP! A buzzing noise began to sound after three warning beeps. The floor beneath their feet began to sink into the ground and slowly decline downwards. Jesse stood staring at Jeff as he stood still as ice. Finally it stopped, and a source of light was let in as a door was opened from what seemed to be a wall. Following Jeff through what had to be an underground home, Jesse looked around at the things that were built. There was a mini-hospital, a storage room, a stairs that led down even further, and then an office with no door but glass windows. Walking into it Jesse stayed silent and stood as Jeff sat down behind the desk. Waving for Jesse to sit too, he pulled out a folder and tossed it on the table. ‘This is my underground home Jesse. I’ve lived here for over 15 years… I let others stay here under conditions you can find in that folder,’ stated Jeff as he pushed it closer to Jesse. Opening up Jesse saw pictures of what looked like a company of fighters. Their were pictures of men with chains and brass knuckles, bats and 2x4’s, and other objects used to bring pain. Finally coming upon a text document Jesse read it and his eyes grew. ‘Yes Jesse, I want you to live here, but only if your willing to fight and earn some money, as well help me make some. I’ll pay for your food, I’ll pay for your clothes, and I’ll let you sleep in a room we’ve set up for our fighters of your choice, as long as you can make me money by fighting,’ offered Jeff as he turned in his chair and stared at the calendar on his file cabinet. Reading more Jesse couldn’t tell if he should take the offer or not, but then as he read on he saw a piece of information that really got him. When you sign this contract you oblige to not hold any of the staff responsible for your mental or physical problems, though they will be attended to, you leave no accusations on the table that we are the people whom are responsible for your fights to the death. Death, how odd was it that this was what Jesse mostly wanted yet at the same time feared beyond glory itself. With a smirk he grabbed the pen and signed and handed the contract to Jeff. For what reason he did not know, he just wanted to live somewhere, and as a great wise man said once, ‘Desperate men, do desperate things…’ ‘I’m glad you wish to become a fighter Jesse. It tickles me to know I’ve signed a fighter whom can bring the fight. So I want to wish you luck against Sage Sabbot on Evolution, as I knew all along you’d be facing him, and lets just say it’s your try out,’ muttered Jeff as Jesse got up, leaving Jesse to think of what he means. It was all a setup that Jesse knew, but now he was going to be tested in what he wished not to be tested on, a wrestling match. This was not how Jesse planned on using his efforts to get ahead. No, he admired Sage for his hard work and glory in GW, as well admired the fact that the man was one of the single greatest wrestlers of global wrestling’s history… But now, he was being told he had to KILL Sage. ‘So you’re saying I have to-‘ began Jesse but was cut off quickly. ‘Yes, that is exactly what I mean. Prove me right in this decision, and give me something I can look forward to. Got it?’ Jeff demanded but in a quite voice. He proved he wasn’t a violent man when one-on-one, but when watching matches, when seeing brutality up close and personal, that was when a real man was revealed, and that’s what Jesse would wait to see. Nodding Jesse turned and walked out the door where one of the three men who was in the back of his truck showed him to his room. It was now or never… I have to prove to Jeff that I can be what he expects, even though I wish not to harm Sage. He’s a caring man, he knows what life beholds for him and others. Wiser than many would think so, Sage is one man you can’t just walk into the ring with and expect to beat. Technical, powerful, and very agile, that was what kept Sage from being beaten so easily. That is why I have to be on guard… But how can I deal with death like this? I’ve never taken a man’s life, at least not under my own conscious, and now I’m being ordered to prove myself worthy… What if I fail? What if he believes I held back? Or what if… No, this couldn’t be the doings of my father could it? Warrior’s fight for pride, Men fight for money, Boys fight for revenge, But lions fight for the right… Expect the Unexpected.
