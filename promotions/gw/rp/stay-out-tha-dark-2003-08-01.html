Stay Out Tha Dark

1 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/1 Down a long and lonesome highway a powerful engine can be heard roaring it’s way down the road. Housed inside of a black Hummer with dark tinted windows it propels the hulking vehicle east, towards the docks. Inside the vehicle sits a raven haired Sean Price and next to him, Deacon Holmes. Who is currently battling to stay awake with his eyes cracked open ever so slightly and a blank expression on his face as he leans back into the butter soft leather seats of Price’s ride with “Criminal” by Eminem blasting. Windows tinted on my ride when I drive in it, so when I rob a bank run out and just dive in it, so I’ll be disguised in it, and if anybody identifies the guy in it, I’ll hide for five minutes, come back, shoot the eye witness, fire at the private eye hired to pry in my business. Die bitches, bastards, brats, pets, this puppie’s lucky I didn’t blast his ass yet! If I ever gave a fuck I’d shave my nuts, tuck “em between my legs and cluck, you motherfuckin” chickens ain’t brave enough to say the stuff I say to just tape it up! Both men are clad in all black, causing them to blend with the interior of the vehicle... and the night. Sean looks away from the road momentarily to find Deacon Holmes passed out beside him with his hands tucked inside the large front pocket of his hoodie and the hood pulled up over his head, which is pressed up against the window. Price shakes his head in silent disbelief before giving Holmes a backhand slap in the chest. Just hard enough to pry him from the arms of sleep, apparently it is an unhappy journey. “What tha fuck man!”” exclaims Holmes while stretching. “Wake up, there’s plenty of time to sleep when you’re dead... and you’re a long way from nap time.” replies Sean. “I can’t help it, this shit is boring man! I feel like we been ridin” forever!” explains Deacon, who’s eyes are now wide open and red. “Thirty minutes.” retorts Sean “Like I said, forever. And why is it so damn hot in here!” What you got the AC on? Hell?” complains Holmes, causing a slight chuckle to come from Price. “This shit ain’t funny Sean, fuck that, I’m openin” a window and goin back to sleep! Wake me up when he get there man.” continues Holmes, who then rolls down his window and leans back over to the side. Seemingly, he falls asleep instantly. “They grow up so fast.” mutters Sean to himself, shaking his head once more with a slight grin. He then reaches over and hits Deacon in the chest once again, this time harder. “Damn man I just closed my eyes, what now!”” Inquires Holmes in an aggravated tone. “We’re here.” states Sean while pulling into a parking space inside of an empty lot with a beat up green and white sheet metal sign reading “Emerson’s Southside Docking.” The vehicle rolls to a stop, parallel parked across two or three parking spaces. The lot is empty, save one other vehicle, making the manner in which Price parks irrelevant. Deacon rolls up his window as Sean cuts off the lights and both men hop out then walk to the back. Price removes a large black duffle bag from the back. “Need anything?” he asks before closing everything back up. In lieu of a verbal response, Holmes simply unholsters one of his twin custom desert eagles and taps his chest causing a knocking sound that informs Sean he’s wearing a vest. “Alright then, let’s hurry up and get in there so we can hurry up and get outta here.” Sean picks up the bag and begins walking toward the boat filled docks with Holmes at his side. Across the parking lot a small office can be seen with a light on. Beyond that are seemingly endless hallways with huge boats for walls and the sky for a ceiling. The two men continue on across the parking lot. “So how was she D?” asks Sean with a grin on his face after nudging Holmes with his elbow. “How was who?” inquires Holmes, pretending not to know what he’s talking about. “Debra, Zero’s assistant. Don’t toy with me man, you know what I’m talking about. It’s not like I didn’t see it happen.” replies Sean, knowing Holmes is gonna have to give up the information now. “Alright, alright, but a gentleman never kisses and tells...” replies Holmes straight faced, causing Price’s eyebrow to raise slightly out of confusion. “Good thing there aren’t any of those here. Let me put it this way, she looks good but fucks ugly.” “Ooh, nice combination.” says Sean. “My favorite, closely seconded by a big ass and tight jeans. So anyway, what exactly are we here for?” asks Deacon while cocking his guns. “Mr. Emerson owes Don Salamone a substantial sum of money. The Don runs some of his “business” through these docks because with boats coming and going all the time it’d be hard to tell which ones have drugs in them and which ones are just fishermen. Well it came to Don Salamone’s attention that lately Emerson has been shaving extra money off the top for himself, dropping Salamone’s profit margin, and royally pissing him off.” explains Sean causing Holmes to nod. He then continues “So we’re here to make sure it never happens again.” “So in other words, this is a hit?” questions Holmes. “More or less, a lot more than less... but don’t worry about it. I’ll handle that part, you just make sure he meets me at the edge of the docks in row D. Now let’s hurry up, he probably knows someone’s coming for him, we’ve gotta catch him before he makes a run for it. He owns tons of island property and is probably cleaning his safe out right now.” retorts Sean. He and Deacon then pick up their pace and begin jogging toward the end of the parking lot, splitting off along the way with Holmes heading toward the small lighted office with the silhouette of a figure quickly closing up and Price jogging out into the docking area. As Price trots out of view, Holmes closes in on the small office, Emerson’s silhouette still shuffling around the inside busily. Deacon presses up against the side of the building and begins gathering his wits. After a few moments the lights go out and the door slowly swings open. Out backs Roger Emerson holding a medium size steel box and waving a can of gasoline all around, spilling the contents all over the inside and outside of the office. Apparently he plans on ducking out of town with all of the money he’s embezzled from Don Salamone and is burning down his business in an attempt to either fake his own death, or just scam the insurance company into giving him some extra money to lay low with. Just as he removes a match from his pocket and prepares to strike it out steps Holmes from around the corner. Clad in all black, Holmes remains unseen by Emerson until he’s within arm’s reach. Stay out tha dark! “Cause if I catch you when the sun is down... Run it clown, come up off that, or I’m gon gun it down. Holmes grabs the man by the collar and forces him up against the door of the building, causing him to drop the match along with the steel box. “So much for a clean getaway huh?” growls Holmes as the two men begin to struggle. Emerson places one of his feet behind Holmes and pushes him away causing him to fall to the ground, a move that usually would have failed against Deacon. Holmes? expected response would be to become enraged but in this case he smiles to himself as Emerson takes off running toward the docks. “Idiot.” Deacon whispers to himself with a smile as he opens his hand to reveal a small silver key, most likely belonging to the metal box. He then kicks up and quickly kneels infront of the box, just as he suspected, the key opens the box and reveals stack and stacks of large bills. “Hot damn, looks like this trip just got worth while!” exclaims Holmes as he re-closes the box and stashes it in the shrubs outside the office door. That in order, he then returns his attention to Mr. Emerson, who is already halfway toward the docking area. When in doubt, however it’s gon go it’s gon be that. You see that? This shit’ll finish you dog, believe that. Where we at? Do you value your life, as much as your possesions? Don’t be a stupid nigga, learn a lesson. By the time Holmes gets to the start of the wooden planked docks Emerson is closing in on the middle area, in which he can go in one of four directions. Noticing the sign hanging above the center of the row marked “C” Holmes raises his gun and fires a round that travels not too far off of Emerson’s left shoulder. “Turn right Mr. Emerson” says Holmes in his regular tone, knowing the man is much too far off to have actually heard him, although he does indeed turn right in an attempt to escape Holmes. Seeing that his idea worked perfectly causes Holmes to grin. He decides to give it another go and runs over to the right just in time to catch Emerson starting across the center of row “D”. I’m gon get you either way, but it’s better to live. Let me get what’s in yo socks, cause it’s better to give. Than receive, believe what I say when I tell ya. Don’t make me put ya somewhere that nobody can smell ya. Holmes takes off down the row toward Emerson and fires another shot off, this one going wide right and striking the hull of a nearby boat. Seeing this Emerson turns left and continues one, toward the end of row D. With Holmes hot on his tracks he is now running faster than ever. As he approaches Sean Price, who’s waiting at the end of the row near the water, he continuously yells for help until he realizes that Price has a silenced 9mm pointed directly at him. He attempts to stop in his tracks but slips on the wet wood and falls to his back. He quickly pops up to his knees and turns around to make a break for it only to be shot in the back of the leg by Sean Price, dropping him to his knees once more. Seeing that Sean has the situation under control Deacon stops running and begins walking. Emerson is too busy trying to get up to realize that Price is now right behind him and once he does it’s too late. He turns around slightly and has his temple met with the butt of Price’s gun, knocking him unconscious. “Damn, now that’s cold!” exclaims Deacon, who is now right alongside Price. And when the lights is out, they don’t come back on. This ain’t a flick you in, you ain’t that strong. Knew it was wrong, but you asked for it baby. Oh you’s a big nigga, ski mask for it baby. “So now what do we do with him?” inquires Holmes while watching Sean walk over to his duffel bag. “Don’t worry about it, I’ll handle everything from here.” says Sean while removing a few items from the black bag. Including a roll of black tape, and a cinder block with a length of rope tied to it. Price then tosses Holmes the keys to his Hummer. Deacon turns and walks away with the faint sound of silenced gun fire at his back followed by a loud splash. He begins running back toward the office in order to retrieve the metal box full of cash that he stashed by the door before Sean catches up.
