She's The Blade: Prologue part one

13 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 13, 2005 She was a stunningly beautiful woman. Her long brown hair hung down to her ample buttocks and her hazel eyes made her seem more innocent than she truly was. Her name was Lisa Jameson and the tabloid media had taken to calling her “The Gold Digger” due to her peculiar habit of marrying elderly men that were close to becoming deceased. She had become a billionaire after five such marriages. Lisa had become sick and tired of the constant attention that the tabloid media had been paying to her and decided that the best way to avoid further problems would be to take a sixth husband, only this time it would have to be someone more her age. The problem was that no one of her age truly interested her. She was 27 years old and all the men of her social class were playboys that wouldn’t think of settling down with her. She sighed as she sat down in the living room of her Los Angeles penthouse. With nothing at all to do she decided to switch on the television. She flipped through the channels until she happened to come across HBO’s broadcast of Global Wrestling’s Evolution. She was in no way a fan of the sport of kings but she stopped on this particular program because she recognized the man that was being interviewed by some woman with pink hair. The man was none other than Mike Biddle. Lisa knew Mike by reputation only and she had managed to catch the occasional glimpse of him at a few parties in Beverly Hills. She knew he was arrogant and self centered but she also knew that he was somewhat more mature than the other twenty-something playboys that plagued Beverly Hills. She watched intently as Mike went on and on about how great he thought he was. In fact, his oratory had caused her to giggle. It was then that she realized that Mike Biddle was the perfect fit for what she desired. Later That Same Week... William Bastafar was furious. His anger showed in his face and especially in the barely controlled rage that escaped through his Scottish accent. He paced back and forth with the aide of his black mahogany cane that was tipped off by a shimmering golden skull. In front of him sat Mike Biddle. Mike rolled his eyes in dismay after every sentence that William completed. “You’re not performing up to par lad,” William began, “oh sure you make it to the very end of every match but you’re the one that either gets pinned or is forced to submit! You’re not conditioned properly, you need to expand your training immediately so that you can fight to the bloody end and come out victorious every time.” Mike rolled his dark blue eyes once again. “I’m fine William,” Mike said, “like I told you, I let Mindy win and Corvus used an illegal hold. So technically I haven’t lost.” Mike rose from his seat on the couch and walked towards the bar to make himself a martini. William saw Mike walk towards the bar and reached over with his cane and thumped Mike on the head. Mike turned around and looked at William in disbelief. “What the hell did you do that for?” Mike asked as he rubbed the top of his head. “Because liquor is part of your problem!” William exclaimed in an admonishing tone, “Instead of training you sit around on your duff and drink martinis all day. Well no more, you’re going to go jogging this very instant! Come now lad, go get changed and I’ll meet you outside.” Mike mumbled to himself as he made his way to his room to change into a white t-shirt and his jogging shorts. A short time later, after he had properly stretched out, he stepped outside his home and found William protruding though the sunroof of his limousine. In his hand William held a mega-phone. William made a motion that signaled for the limo driver to begin to drive off. “Come now lad,” William said into the mega-phone, “try and catch the limousine.” “Damn it,” Mike said under his breath as he took off after the limousine. Mike jogged after the limousine for what seemed like hours. Every time he got close to the vehicle William would either order the driver to speed up or he’d throw something at Mike. It was an annoying exercise and Mike could feel himself become fatigued. He tried to stop and die on the side of the road but every time he slowed down William would threaten him by saying that if he stopped he would have to spend the rest of the day with Carlos Fraga, a fate that Mike thought to be far worse than death. Mike continued the punishment and began to promise himself that he’d either fire William or not lose another match. As he was lost in thought he didn’t notice a sporty red BMW pull up beside him. The driver was a beautiful brunette that stared at Mike intently as he ran. She honked the horn and quickly gained his attention. Mike looked over at her and she winked at him before she sped off into the distance. Mike recognized the woman as Lisa Jameson, the Gold Digger chick that had been marrying all the old guys in the neighborhood and taking their fortunes. He came to a stop as he tried to take in what had just happened. Why would some woman that was into old men wink at him? Did this mean he was getting old? It couldn’t be, he was younger than her by at least a year or two! “Blast it all lad!” said William over the mega-phone, “Stop being a lazy sod and get back to work this very instant or else I’ll be forced to back up over you!” Mike didn’t say anything, he simply started jogging again. However, now he had Lisa Jameson on his mind and he wanted to know why she’d even be interested in him. -The End for Now-
