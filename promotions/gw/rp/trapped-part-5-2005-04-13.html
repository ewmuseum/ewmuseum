Trapped (Part 5)

13 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13th April 2005 Time? We are all ruled by the clock, our day is planned out due to how much time we have in our day. Most people have a set time of when to eat, when to sleep and when to wake up. Jobs are always described using time, for example most jobs are 9 to 5 jobs. The job of a fighter has no time limit, we are always training or always have the job on our minds. I suppose that is why I have always found the aspect of time and people working to the clock slightly strange. However, recently even though I am not ruled by the clock or time for that matter I have began to lose all aspect of time. I suppose that is what happens when you are locked up in one place for such a long time. Days fall into each other, weeks and months do not have any meaning anymore. It is just one long never ending day for me? Do you know that feeling where you are not sure whether you are a sleep or awake? That is how I have been feeling since I was torn from my everyday life. There is not a moment that passes by that I don’t think about the outside world, about what people are doing that day and the freedom they have to do it. Whilst they live their dreams I live my nightmare. When I think of the outside world it is not long until I come crashing down to the reality of my situation. Nearly 3 months I have lived in the same damn steel box waiting for that scum Richard Wood to come waltzing through those doors with a smug look on his face. He knows I hate him and in some strange way I think he gets off on it. He probably believes that the angrier I am the better I perform which is utter rubbish. I know what I am like when I am angry and I sure don’t perform any better, my judgement is impaired, my reactions are erratic and sometimes I can’t control myself. This is the reason I have been performing so badly at Evolution and the GW events. I am just constantly angry, usually I could switch my anger off but this situation has clouded my mind. However finally some of the clouds are beginning to part and I see a way out of this place, and this way goes by the name of Mike Biddle? Biddle knows something and I am sure of it, the passed few weeks he has been acting so strangely. He is always around wherever I go. I might sound paranoid but my questions will be answered at the coming Evolution when I appear on his joke of a show Marvelous Matters. Talking of this coming Evolution it seems I have a second chance to become a GW Champion again, as I have a second shot for the Television Championship against Mindy Mercer. Mercer has done incredibly well and put on a great fight against Aradia Xiomara last week at Evolution. She has defended the TV title on every Evolution show thus far and has had a great run of success but that run ends at Evolution. I have nothing against you Mercer, I think you are a fantastic fighter and athlete but I need that title more than you at the moment. I need some hope, I need to know that I can still fight and that I can still win. I am desperate for gold and I will stop at nothing to have that TV gold around my waist. Evolution for me is where problems will be solved and hope will be gained! “This is ridiculous!” I screamed yet again in the face of Richard Wood. I was pacing up and down my steel room as Richard Wood and a group of scientists who stood behind him, watched me from the corner. “It is the rules, Adam.” Wood said holding his arms out to show it was out of his hands which I knew it wasn’t. “Then tell me how come I was never told of this rule before?” I asked. “Because it was never necessary before.” Wood smiled. “You mean you just don’t want me to do it?” I exclaimed. “Partly yes but it is more that we want to protect the programme.” “What do you mean?” “Well if you do this interview with Biddle then it is a possibility that you could tell him what goes on here.” “So?” “Well then our competitors will know what we are doing and begin their own programmes, which we don’t want because we don’t want any competition at the moment.” “Fine, I promise I won’t tell Biddle about the programme but I am doing the interview.” “Not possible, Adam.” “What?” I questioned feeling my rage build. “You are a risk Adam, I know you hate being here and any chance you got to jeopardise the programme you would take it.” I moved towards Wood and the scientists. The scientists crouched in fear whilst Wood tried to stand tall. I was again in the face of Richard Wood, I was so close I could see his pupils dilate with emotion. “You’re right Wood, I hate being here, I hate everything about this place and maybe I will tell Biddle about the programme or maybe I won’t but I am doing the interview either way.” I moved away from Wood with my back to him and for the first time in 3 months a smile crossed my face, finally I had my own way, finally I had got one up on Wood or so I thought. As I sat down on the edge of my bed and looked up at Wood he was still smiling. He adjusted the cuffs of his suit as he talked. “Well I have to say Adam, I am disappointed. I came in here to tell you some good news that we were nearing the end of the programme and that you would be released soon but since you are not willing to co-operate I guess you will have to remain here indefinitely.” “Don’t lie to me Wood.” I said knowing he was lying, “You have no plans of releasing me. You get too much sick pleasure at watching me go insane in this place.” “Oh really” Well if you don’t believe me then I will have to show you some proof won’t I?” Wood looked at me knowing that would spark my interest. “What?” I asked trying to contain the emotion in my voice. “You’ll see,” Wood smirked, “Charlie tie his hands and follow me.” I looked around for the big mountain that goes by the name of Charlie but couldn’t see him. Suddenly from within the middle of the scientists Charlie emerged like a mountain over hills. He has a piece of rope in his hands as he moved over towards me. “Hands out.” Charlie demanded in his deep voice. I stuck my arms out and quickly Charlie grabbed my hands forced them together and tightly tied them together. “Up!” Charlie again demanded. I rose to my feet and Charlie grunted pointing towards the door. The scientists had disappeared now and already standing outside the door was Wood. With Charlie behind me I made my way through the door and Wood began to walk down the long corridor. The corridor was brightly lit and the light reflected of the white walls. It felt like I was walking to my death that I was walking my green mile. I stopped my train of thought before I gave myself a panic attack. We passed empty rooms like mine, obviously never used as I was the only one to sign the stupid contract. When we reached the end of the corridor Wood stopped outside a door and quickly punched a few numbers into it a keypad. The door slid open and Wood pointed towards it. “In you go then.” Wood smirked. “Why” What’s in there?” I asked taking a step back and feeling my heart race. “Evidence that I was telling you the truth.” With a deep breath I stepped through the door and as I turned to face Wood the door slide shut behind me. What I was facing was a staircase; lights were dimly lit all the way to the top. With another deep breath I made my way up the stairs. My mind raced wondering what awaited me at the top of the stairs. As my mind continued to race I felt a warm sensation on my right arm. As I looked I noticed that rays of sunlight were shining through a window. Suddenly my mind stopped racing and I simply stood there enjoying the light warm my pale skin. Of course I have never truly felt the sun on my bare skin as I have never been out of my suit when I am outside. But this ray of light on my arm represented what I longed for, freedom. I shook my head to snap myself out of my trance as I continued up the stares feeling more relaxed. As I reached the top another steel door awaited me. Within seconds it slid open and I stepped through into a new room. This room like the corridor was well lit and everywhere was a clean white. “Adam?” Came a familiar voice. At first my ears didn’t believe it but my eyes confirmed it as I turned around and saw, stood behind a wall of glass was my best friend Peter Willows. His eyes looked red raw and he looked untidy with the top button of his shirt undone and his tie untied. However all that didn’t matter as a huge smile was on his face and I returned it with a huge smile of my own. I ran up to the glass wall so that I was now facing my friend on the other side of the glass. “Pete!” I exclaimed, “It is so good to see you!” “You too Adam! I have been worried sick, I haven’t slept for weeks.” “I can tell, you look horrible man.” “I know but I have been trying to work out how to get you out of here.” “Have you heard anything about Rachel and Emma?” I asked with hope. Pete looked down at the ground trying to avoid eye contact with me. My heart sank as I tried to fight back the tears. “Pete” Please tell me something. I just need to know where they are. Please Pete.” “I’m sorry Adam, I have tried everywhere but I have no idea where Wood is keeping them.” Pete replied trying not to cry. “You’ve got to keep trying Pete, please for me. I don’t know how long I am going to be in this place. I don’t think they are ever going to let me leave.” “I’m sorry Adam, I’m so sorry.” Pete mumbled before he burst in to tears. “Hey, hey, Pete don’t cry.” I tapped on the glass trying to support my friend. “If I hadn’t got you to sign that stupid contract, none of this would have happened!” “Pete, I signed that contract, I read through it and I signed it. None of this is your fault okay. Come on buddy I need you to be strong for me whilst I am in here. I need you to keep fighting, I need you to find Rachel and Emma for me, and if you do tell them I love them and that we will be together soon. Okay?” “Okay.” Pete replied wiping away the tears. “I am relying on you Pete.” “I know, listen Adam they said we only had a couple of minutes so I better go but I promise I will not stop until I find Rachel and Emma. I won’t let you down.” “I know you won’t.” Pete flashed a smile and I flashed a smile right back. With that he ran out of the room, as I watched him go a new sense of hope filled my body. I put my head against the cold glass as for the first time in a long time my mind was clear of worry and only one thing was on my mind? Evolution!
