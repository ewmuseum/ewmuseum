Revenge-Part 1

24 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24/10/04 He felt it, the pain that went through his body when he was killed after assassinating the Caesar. It was the worst thing he had ever felt in his life, it was excruciating. He would never have to go through it again though. For he was immortal, made so by the devil himself. Dagoth gave himself to the devil for the chance to avenge his family, and now he had been turned into just another pawn in the great game of Lucifer and God. He was the Devil’s assassin. He appeared to Dagoth only when he needed a job done, and when he appeared, he made sure that Dagoth would keep his part of the bargain. A man of his word, the one they called the Kinslayer did the Devil’s bidding, but grudgingly. He was an immortal, and he thought that no one should be able to control him. He was right. He had killed hundreds of people in his life as General of the Hun army. He had killed Romans, Ostrogoths, Visigoths, Vikings, and even the Huns too. After the betrayal by Attila and the assassination of his family, it was all that he had in his mind. Ur, the name the devil had taken when walking the earth, had told Dagoth that there was something important that they needed to discuss, but first of all he needed to do a “little job” as he liked to call it. Last time it had been an Italian priest, this time it was a nun, who was growing to be the next Mother Theresa, and of course, the Devil wouldn’t want that would he? No, it was Dagoth mission to either turn her or kill her, but Dagoth already knew how it would end. People like these woman and the priest before her were not afraid of death, because they knew where they stood, they never faltered, only the weak. That was the problem with humans, or it should be said, mortals. They would always be the Gods little toys or the Devil’s little toys, but an immortal like Dagoth could walk the earth without those problems, at least until his job with the devil was done. His black hair moved around as the wind flowed through it. It was dark, although it was never really dark in Las Vegas. The light of the neon signs made it look like it was still day. The rain didn’t keep people from walking around either. They were there to have fun and a little rain wouldn’t keep them from it. Dagoth walked in between the mortals trying not to draw attention to himself, he was not in the mood. The bone medallion hung around his neck, his only tactile memory of his wife. The sword on its sheath, the hilt glittered in the night. The gold growing more beautiful because of the illumination. He was huge, and had a sword, even though this was Las Vegas, he always got weird looks. He finally got to where he was going; he had left the lights behind him and gone through a dark alley until he found an abandoned house. When he finally entered he was greeted by his master? for now. Ur was sitting there on a sofa; the only light was coming from a single candle in a table besides the sofa. He sat there looking at a piece of paper, Dagoth didn’t know what it said, and he didn’t care. Ur finally looked at him and a sickening grin finally appeared on his face. “It’s a great night for death isn’t it?” asked Ur still smiling. “Every night is great for death,” answered Dagoth getting in front of him impatiently. “What is it that I have to do this time?” “You know what you have to do; your target is Mother Josephine. This is the address where you will find her. Try to turn her first, although I know it is an exercise in futility. She?” “I do not need to know what she has done to you or what she has not done. I will do your dirty work simply because you gave me immortality and I gave you my word. Now, what did you have to talk to me about?” Asked Dagoth impatiently, almost at the door already. “This,” he said as he threw the paper to Dagoth. Dagoth caught it and instantly knew what it was. GW Presents Dark Rituals and in one of the matches, Dagoth Kinslayer was circled with a red pen. Ur wanted an explanation, and Dagoth had to give him something, it didn’t matter if it was simple, just a reason. “None of your concern,” he answered trying to leave the place. “It does concern me Kinslayer. I own you!” The Devil stood up anger now showing on his face. Dagoth turned around, his face filled with frustration and anger. He looked straight into the Ur’s eyes and stood his ground. It is not everyday that you lock eyes with the Devil himself? as the priest had called him? Ill Diavolo. “I am only your servant for two more jobs Ur. I suggest you use them wisely, because you won’t find another immortal like me,” Dagoth said as he left the place and threw the door shut. His eyes were turning red, and he was about to do something stupid, but he didn’t want to fight the devil? yet. After he was walking away from the house he heard the worst scream of terror you could ever imagine. “Be careful what you wish for Kinslayer!” The voice then stopped and complete silence surrounded Dagoth as he walked back to the busy streets of Las Vegas. He saw a couple of hookers showing their “merchandise” to people in cars looking for a good time. Dagoth went through them and one of them came towards him. Dagoth stopped and looked at the woman. She came closer to him and started rubbing his chest. “You want a good time big boy?” she asked him as she took her top off to show him her breasts. Dagoth was outraged. He would always remember his wife and would never go as low as what this woman was asking. He took out his sword and put the edge directly on her neck. She was in shock, it took some time for it to sink in, she was in danger. “Err... please don’t kill me!” she begged of Dagoth. But his heart was as hard as stone. He took the sword and swung it horizontally. The head of the woman was on the air and fell to the floor. A pool of blood formed around the head and where the head was severed. Dagoth kept moving ignoring the looks of shock and terror from the other prostitutes. They were screaming and crying but they wouldn’t get closer to the head or the body of the nameless prostitute. He walked through the streets of Las Vegas looking for his target’s home. It was important that he complete his mission because it was his path to freedom. His eyes still burned with anger, even an hour after he had killed a prostitute on the street. He knew that he had over reacted, but he wouldn’t admit it to himself. He finally found the place. A small house that seemed like it was a hundred years old. He opened the gate to the front porch only to hear police sirens and cars screeching in the distance. He had heightened hearing so they were still a little off. He opened the door to the house and went inside. He didn’t need to be careful, he was immortal. He walked towards the back of the house after searching the living room and the kitchen and finding nothing. He looked in the first door to his right and found the bathroom. It was empty. He continued on to the door on the left and found a small room with a bed and everything very cleanly organized. It was probably the guest bedroom. He was getting closer and closer and he knew it. When he opened the next door, he found a big bed and a small woman of nearly 80 years of age kneeling in front of a shrine filled with figures of Jesus, saints and archangels. Now what he had to do was simple. He grabbed his sword and unsheathed it. “Mother Josephine,” said Dagoth pointing at her with his sword. She looked around surprised. “You” you? who are you?” She asked clearly very frightened. “I am a messenger. I bring you a message from your enemy, from the Prince of Darkness. He wants you to choose. Either you change yourself and swear to work for him or quite simply” you die,” he said looking deep into her eyes. “I would never work for the Devil!” She screamed trying to get up. “Good choice,” he stabbed the sword into her heart. The look of shock still in her face, but he knew she was better of without. The cops would be coming soon; he heard their sirens in the night. He cleaned his sword on the bed sheets and left the house. They would never be able to find him by looking for fingerprints, so it didn’t matter, and if they did find him. What could they do anyways? He was immortal, and there was nothing they could do. Not even with their guns. Dagoth Kinslayer was unstoppable; the only one powerful enough to destroy Dagoth was the Devil himself, the one that gave him immortality. Soon he would be free though, and there would be no Ur looking after him every single time he wanted some of their dirty work done. He would no longer be a pawn on their game. He didn’t care how that war ended up, it was not his problem. His problem was getting revenge on Attila the Hun. And he would get his revenge. Soon.
