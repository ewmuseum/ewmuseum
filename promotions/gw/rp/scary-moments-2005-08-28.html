Scary moments

28 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-28-2005 Monday Night after Evolution 10:35 PM... “I finally won.” Racing down the hallway towards the locker room area where my stuff was, I could still feel the sweat that was dripping down off of my hair. The smell of pride was draining from my body and deep in my mind, I knew nothing could ever make this day worse’though I could be wrong. As I got to the door, the very same door I was dressing in before the match, I could heard a voice inside the room talking. Turning the door knob a little and pressing it open just enough to see a face. The one person I didn’t really want to see tonight. My night was set. I was going to go out and celebrate my victory and all he represented was hurtful feelings. “What are you doing here?” Saying in an unsure voice. His image was different then the way I’ve seen in in the last two years. Wearing a blue suit that went well with his black dress shoe’s. He fit the image of a business man. That is what was scaring me right now. “Hey baby.” He says, walking over to were I was. Pressing his body into mine, feeling the warmth and hairy body that he had, I could feel something he was worrying about. “I need to talk to you about something.” He says, pulling back from me in the seat he was sitting in. “Why so important to come up here and tell me?” Grabbing the towel that was on the bench next to me. My hair was sweating like a damn rainfall. Pouring water down off of it, almost enough to fill a damn little small pond. Turning back to my Uncle who was just a little under stress from him being there which gave me the clue something was up. Twisting back around to see his face before dropping the green towel to the bottom of my feet. “Elia, you know I love you right?” Watching as I gave a small nod. “Well we need to talk about what’s going on with you as sort of a father and father talk.” “What’s there to talk about” I mean, I’m a grown up now. I make my own choices.” Saying to him with anger that ran off my lips. This was my life. I wasn’t about to have anyone try and run it for me. No one did for my mother’s life. Tossing the hair out of my eyes. No one in my family has tried to run it so why try now? “NO! I will not take advice from anyone on this matter. I am perfectly fine. See I even won out there tonight.” Reaching over to grab a hold of the red and white bag that I had brought. “Elia, sometimes I might not always do the right things for you or me for that matter but by god, you need to talk to someone. You’re crazy and you know it.” He says, standing up and out of the chair that he was propping his body into. “NO I’M NOT CRAZY!” Throwing the bag clear across the room in a fit of anger. “Now can you please leave me be?” My uncle’s eye told a story. Deep with red marks and water that was on the brink of getting out and draining all over my skin and face. Watching his body get up, going towards me once more with a strange looking facial expression. “Listen Elia, I really wish you’d think about this. I’m not trying to make you change your life but rather then go see a doctor.” Watching his pledging with me with every ounce of love that was in his veins for me. “Uncle, I’m not telling you how to run your gym so why are you trying so hard to run my life?” Walking through the locker room to the bathroom. “Because when your mom and dad didn’t continue trying to get you to come back then you became my responsibility.” He says. “Besides, if you won’t listen to me now before I’m gone, who are you going to listen to then?” I was never one on goodbyes but when my uncle does die then I don’t think I can say it and go talk the way I do with someone else. He was right, they did stop trying to get me to come back. That was wrong of them but life does go on. I don’t find new parents rather I become a woman and create one of my own. Pulling off my wrestling top, sliding off the top of my chest and over my breast, I began to pull on the knob and began to get the shower on. “Elia?”, my uncle says, return before hopping into the steaming, burning red hot shower I have going. Inside the shower the steam was overloading. Rushing up the tips of my nose like it was cocaine. My eyes began to water even more, the water dripping down all the way to places that no other man has seen in a VERY long time. My body was breaking up from the long hours of wrestling and other things. Other things meaning my social life though that wasn’t anything special. It didn’t take me long to get in and out of the shower. Washing off the rest of shampoo that was in my hair. Turning off the water, pulling the shower curtain open to revile a sight that wasn’t there before. On the mirror in the bathroom there was some wording on it that were looking really fresh. “He is evil. He wants to hurt you.” Grabbing the towel that I had placed in the towel holder, I couldn’t but know who this was that wrote it. Shutting the curtain for a second till his words, rippling out that brought back the very fear I was hoping to end. “Hello again Elia.” A voice says from behind the curtain that I had open but now was close. Pulling it back to revile as well, the red eye monster. His eyes, they seem to be growing even more red with everything that has been happening. He still made me feel fear but for some exotic reason, I was beginning to feel safe and secure around him now. “I don’t have time for your games.” Backing up a little right into the bathroom sink. “Games” I don’t play games. I tell the truth and he is trying to hurt you.” Stepping out of the bath tub, the red eye monster begins to glow almost around his entire body. “Hurt me” That is the man that made me who I am. How could he hurt me?” Saying without a smile, a frown, or anything. His eyes were warm feeling. Almost as if he was trying to look into my soul. My heart rate was beginning to raise, my body even began to sweat again. I knew something was wrong as he took a few more steps towards me. Grabbing my wrist, it was as if I was on fire. Being burnt to a crisp and having to drop down to at least one knee. I felt like screaming at the top of my lungs, feeling the pain almost take out my entire body. “PLEASE STOP!” “You will listen to me?”, His eyes glowing even higher . “Elia, you ok in there?” MY uncle says. My thoughts grew for him to come in. Maybe he would see his giant, this monster, and help me see the light of day. “Please let me go.” Not able to even talk any high then I did before my head fell down deeper towards the floor. “Listen good Elia, you will go in there and take him out. Take the very man that offense you, calls you a lair, and tries to force you to go do something you don’t want to do.” He says. “You will do it or I will do it myself.” My heart began to race and I knew the only way I was going to get out of this was to do what he says. Shaking my head up and down in the yes answer as I felt the pain beginning to slowly slip away. He was winning at whatever game he was wanting to play and I was the pawn for his vicious games. Falling back towards the bathroom counter, slamming into it like a stack of bricks, I just knew that my uncle would hear. “Elia, are you ok in there?” Screaming a little louder this time. “Y’Yes?” saying as blood began to run from the back of my head. He was gone but I could somehow still feel him. Pressing up off the warm floor of the arena, grabbing the shirt that I had brought with me before rubbing that wound off with the towel. How was I going to do this? I love this man so much yet I didn’t want anything else to happen to him. Continuing to place my clothing on, reaching over for the knob before coming out. As I did, I saw my Uncle’s eyes wide open. He Didn’t look the same. He was stiff, hard as a rock, and a slightly different color. “Uncle?” Asking and afraid to even walk any closer to him. There was a deep down feeling in my gut about this. My eyes began to water up with tears, rushing over to him and fighting the fear that I had in my body. As I grabbed him he was cold as an ice cube. The worst thoughts began racing through my head. Was he? “No he isn’t Elia but he will be if you don’t go through with what I ask you.” Turning around to the bathroom door to see the monster once again. “Leave him out of this. He isn’t trying to harm anyone.” Pleading with every tear that ran down my eyes. I ran right for the monster, grabbing him by the neck but only slamming right into the door. I felt my entire body go numb, bouncing off if it like it was a trampoline of some sort. Falling down to the floor, I saw nothing but the ceiling above me. The room began to go dizzy and my mind began going in circles before the colorful room began to turn into total darkness. Pitch black with no sound at all. ******** 2 hours later My head hurt bad. Like I had got hit my a damn bus or something. Opening both of my eyes, I quickly and promptly began to check out were I was. Looking at the walls, the ceiling, everything, it was all different to me. I was back inside my house. Trying to get up out of the bed, all I could feel as pain in my lower back. “AW!” Screaming out in a soft voice. “Your awake.” My Uncle Johnson says, walking in with some medicine. “How did I get here?” Asking, grabbing my head a little. “I drove you remember?” He response. “Here take this.” Was it all just a dream or did it really happen. I took the medicine from my uncle and sat up just slightly enough so I could get a better drink. Tossing the pills in my mouth and taking a drink I knew things were ok. No matter what I wasn’t going to do it. Watching as my Uncle kept walking towards the door before looking back at me. “Get some rest. We’ve got lots to work on tomorrow.” He said as my eyes began to get heavy. I felt sleepy again. Trying to kept my head up and off the bed. Leaning back on the pillow I still kept asking the same question, was it real or fact? Did he really try to hurt my Uncle or did I see something that wasn’t there? My eyes, closing once again before the room began to go black. Before I was fully asleep, deep in the light I could hear one last thing before falling asleep. “Ok, lets get her into testing?”
