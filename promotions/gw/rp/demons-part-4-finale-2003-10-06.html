'Demons', part 4 (finale)

6 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 6th, 2003 Kakita Yoshiko could taste her own blood. It was one of many sensations she'd gotten used to in the days since she'd been taken prisoner. The cold feel of the concrete, the darkness of her prison, the rawness of her wrists an ankles where she had strained against the manacles holding her fast, all of these had become her world. But worst of all was the throbbing. The steady throbbing pain in her neck, where that monster had fed from her again and again, never more than just a little, just enough to remind her that she lived because he wanted it, that she belonged to him. Yoshiko laughed out loud, and tossed her bedraggled hair back. He was doing it. He was really driving her crazy. How could she let him do that? She was a Kakita. She was better than this. She couldn't let herself think those thoughts, couldn't allow herself to break. Not for him. She closed her eyes, and tried to meditate. When she opened her eyes, she wasn't sure how long she'd had them closed. She'd obviously fallen asleep. Her vision was blurry and her head felt light -- had Michaelson fed on her again while she'd been sleeping? The very thought made her skin crawl. 'Hey.' Yoshiko looked up from the floor, and her eyes widened. There he was, blonde hair, green eyes, gorgeous smile, rippling muscles? 'Rokku,' whispered Yoshiko, choking back a sob, 'are? are you real?' 'Real as a rose, and twice as pretty, ' said Rokku, grinning, 'c'mon 'shiko-chan, I'm getting you out of here. Can you walk?' 'I, I think so,' Yoshiko stuttered, 'but the chains?' 'Not a problem,' said Rokku, producing a key from his pocket. 'I took this off of 'dad'. He put up a hell of a fight for it, though.' 'So, you mean he's?' 'Dust in the wind,' replied Rokku, as he unlocked Yoshiko's shackles. 'Here, let me help you up?' Yoshiko took Rokku's hand, and he helped her onto unsteady feet. Hesitantly, she took a step? and her legs gave out on her. She stumbled, and pitched into Rokku, who caught her and held her close. 'Rokku, I?' she began, looking up into his eyes. 'It's okay, Yoshiko, you don't have to?' 'No, I do,' she said, biting her lip hard to fight back tears. 'Your father he? he did things to me. Fed on me, tortured me, tried to break my mind. But? but I never gave in. Because I knew. I knew you'd come for me. I knew you would set me free.' 'Yoshiko?' 'Let me finish,' said the Silken Crane, as tears rolled down her soft cheeks. 'God, I hate this. You must know I do, right? I hate having to be rescued, I hate having to be weak like this. I swore to myself on my father's grave that I'd never be weak again. I hate this so much? but it's okay. It's okay, Rokku. Because it's you. I can let myself be weak with you. I? I can?' Yoshiko wrapped her arms around Rokku's neck and pulled him into a deep kiss. Their tongues danced against each other, and she let her eyes fall closed, just enjoying the feeling of his lips against hers, the sensation of his hands roaming over her body, exploring her. The kiss went on and on, and slowly, Yoshiko's head stopped swimming. And she found herself wondering something. Why was Rokku so cold? Slowly, she opened her eyes. And found herself looking into the eyes of a monster. Shrieking into his mouth, Yoshiko tried to push away, but cold dead arms held her fast, and cold dead lips stayed locked to hers. His fangs extending, the monster bit her lip and redoubled the force of his kiss, turning it violent. Yoshiko's shrieks turned to sobs, and finally the monster broke the kiss, licking her blood from his wide, satisfied grin. 'I guess the little cocktail I slipped you in your sleep wasn't strong enough, sweetness,' said Michaelson, his cold hands sliding down her back and roughly grabbing her ass, 'it's such a shame that it wore off before we could get to the real fun.' Yoshiko just stared at him numbly. How could he? no, how could she! James released Yoshiko from his arms, and she stumbled back, slumping against the wall with a blank expression on her face. The vampire watched with amusement as she slid to the floor, her empty eyes staring out at him but not really seeing him? 'Well,' said James, his twisted grin plastered to his face, 'it took you long enough. You get it now, don't you sweetcheeks? Even if he *does* come, you can't be saved. Everything that's happened here will be with you forever and ever and ever?' Yoshiko's hands slowly rose to her face, the heels grinding into her eyes and her fingers tangling in her hair. Michaelson's grin only widened, and he walked away laughing while silent tears rolled down Yoshiko's face. *** This was it. Rokku placed his hand on the white-wrapped bundle on his back and nodded slightly, then slammed the door of his Vehement closed. The Steel Lion marched towards the warehouse, his eyes locked in an iron glare. One of the guards outside yelled and reached for his gun, but before it cleared the holster a shuriken was embedded in his throat. The remaining guards opened fire, and Rokku rushed towards the building, twisting and jerking to avoid bullet after bullet at the last instant. Shuriken flew from his hands, dropping several more guards, and distracting them long enough for Rokku to drop into a dead run, sprinting hard and hurling himself through a ground floor window. Rokku hit the floor running, as shattered glass rained down around him. Three shuriken to the chest dropped the first interior guard he saw, and a kick to the knee followed by a blow to the skull dropped the second. 'Yoshiko!' shouted Rokku, 'Yoshiko, where are you?!' Frantically, the Steel Lion searched the warehouse, tossing everything he could see. He knew there must be more guards inside, who were regrouping to take him out -- not to mention the guards from the outside who would be storming in soon. He had to find Yoshiko and be sure that she was safe -- and he had to do it fast. When he found her though, huddled in a corner and just? staring, he worried that he might already be too late. 'Yoshiko,' he said, crouching down and taking her by the shoulders. 'Yoshiko-chan, snap out of it! It's me. It's Rokku. I've come to get you out of here!' Yoshiko's eyes suddenly snapped into focus. She looked at Rokku's face, and his steel-hard eyes softened. Then she slapped him. 'Get away from me,' she screamed, 'you aren't real! You're just a trick! You're never, ever, ever real!!' Rokku staggered back to the center of the warehouse. What the hell had that monster done to her? Familiar, grating laughter began to echo throughout the warehouse, and Rokku knew he'd get his answer soon enough. 'Come out, monster!' screamed the Steel Lion. 'Come out and face your demons!' Michaelson's laughter grew louder. 'Is that what you are,' Michaelson's voice echoed all over the warehouse, leaving Rokku unable to pin it down, 'my personal demons, given flesh? You look to me more like a little boy playing dress-up. I mean the hair, the outfit, the mask -- what, are you supposed to spook me?' 'Come on out,' growled Rokku, 'and we'll see if I can't show you fear.' Again, the vampire's laughter rang out. 'That's rich. You think you're ready to really fight me? Prove it. Boys!' The front doors burst open, and guards came pouring in. They were soon joined by the remaining guards from within the warehouse, forming up to take Rokku down. 'Damn it, dad,' grumbled Rokku, reaching into his large coat pockets, 'I don't have time for this.' For a moment, everything was still. The guards ready to pull triggers at any moment, Rokku with his hands in his pockets. With lightning speed, Rokku pulled a pair of plastic half-gallon jugs from his coat, and hurled them at the guards. The guards immediately opened fire, but Rokku cartwheeled out of the way, throwing a pair of shuriken at the jugs, causing them to rupture and spray clear liquid all over the guards. Without thinking, the guardsmen continued to fire -- and ignited the gasoline that had covered them. As the guardsmen screamed and flailed, setting more and more of the warehouse on fire, Rokku walked back towards Yoshiko. And there he was. His bare chest rippling with muscle, his face twisted into a confident sneer, and a six-foot metal staff held cockily under one arm. James Michaelson. Rokku's father. And the most twisted monster he'd ever faced. 'Good job there, son,' said James, 'I really didn't think you had a move like that in you. I'd almost say you earned the right to take your little lady friend home safe and sound -- but I've grown so attached to her. She tastes so exquisite?' 'Shut up,' growled Rokku. 'You either get out of my way, or you're not walking out of here.' James chuckled. 'That's the way you want to play it, eh son? All right then. Consider this your personal Thunderdome, boy! Two men enter --' Rokku finished his sentence for him. '--One man leaves.' James hurled himself through the air, swinging the staff over his head. Rokku rolled out of the way as James came down and the staff struck concrete, cutting a deep furrow into the floor and sending chips of the stuff flying into the air. Rokku was on his feet in an instant, and his katana practically leapt from its saya, slicing the air where James had just stood. The vampire thrust the staff at Rokku's knees, but the Steel Lion leapt, and came down on the staff's tip. With lightning speed, Rokku slashed the blade across the vampire's bare chest, leaving a deep cut. James roared and yanked up on the staff, sending Rokku sailing though the air. Before Rokku could right himself, James leapt after him, driving the staff down onto Rokku's ribs in mid-air, and sending the Steel Lion down to slam into the concrete. Rokku hit the floor hard, but managed to dodge another downward staff strike from his father. Catching the vampire's legs between his, Rokku pulled James off balance, crashing him face first into the floor. Seconds later, the two men rolled away from each other and spun back onto their feet, putting several feet of space between them again, and staring each other down. At that moment, the warehouse's fire suppressant system came on, raining water down on the two combatants. 'You're starting to lose your disguise there, son,' said James, as he and Rokku circled each other. 'It looks like dress-up time is over, 'cause your roots are showing through!' Rokku ran a hand through his hair, and it came back slick with black. The dye was starting to run from his hair, returning it to its natural blonde. 'Shut up and fight!' shouted Rokku, snatching a handful of shuriken from a pouch on his belt and hurling them at James. The vampire smirked, striking the throwing stars out of the air with precise lightning-quick staff strikes. Rokku came in directly behind the shuriken, thrusting his katana towards James' heart. The vampire spun, dodging the thrust and driving the butt of the staff into the small of Rokku's back. 'What I don't get,' said James, as he parried Rokku's overhead swing and clipped him in the jaw with a snap of the staff, 'is why you're still fighting. You saw her, son. I broke the girl. She's of no use to you anymore -- nothing you know of her is left in that shell. She's little more than a toy for my amusement now!' 'I refuse to believe that!' shouted Rokku ducking under a swing of James' staff and driving a boot into his stomach. 'Kakita Yoshiko is stronger than you can ever imagine, 'dad'. I love her with all my heart and soul. That's why I know the worst you've done is stall her for a while -- she'd never fall over for a thing like you.' 'Really?' James jabbed the end of the staff into Rokku's chest, the inhumanly powerful strike sending the Steel Lion flying across the warehouse to land on his back. 'Well,' continued James, wearing a thoughtful expression, 'she sure didn't seem so strong when I was drinking from her. Ooh, or how about when I drugged her and convinced her I was you? Or better yet, when I was kissing her! My hands all over her body? she seemed pretty ready to 'fall over' then. So what do you think about that?' This time, it was Rokku's turn to laugh. He didn't move, just let his laughter ring out, echoing over the sound of the water and the fire and the moans of the wounded. Slowly, he stood up, looked at Michaelson, and smirked. 'I say you know nothing, father.' Rokku untied the rope holding the long bundle to his back, and grabbed it, pointing it at his father. 'You can taunt me all you want, you can hit me, you can torture me, and you can do the same to her, because you can never do it. You can never, ever break us.' 'Really,' said James dryly. 'And why's that?' 'Because we have something you can never, ever take from us, something that you can't possibly understand.' 'What,' said James, chuckling, 'that thing you've got wrapped in cloth? Some kind of anti-vampire superweapon, is that the special thing you've got?' 'No,' Rokku said, and then he hurled the bundle with all his might. It sailed through the air, the rope uncoiling from around it, the cloth slowly unfurling, revealing its contents. It was a sword. A katana of blue-tinted steel that tumbled through the air, gleaming in the light of the fire. And landed in the outstretched hand of Kakita Yoshiko. 'What we've got,' said the Silken Crane, throwing her sodden hair out of her face to reveal an expression of absolute intensity, 'is Honor.' Together, Crane and Lion screamed, their twin kiais shaking the vampire to his very foundation. As one Rokku and Yoshiko charged, raining down sword strikes that even Michaelson's unnatural reflexes were hard-pressed to keep up with. 'You think this matters?!' shouted Michaelson, as he drove his staff into Rokku's jaw, shattering the steel mempo, and sending the Steel Lion tumbling to the ground. 'You're still nothing to me, both of you! Just happy meals with legs!' His eyes burning with rage, Michaelson raised the staff high, ready to crush Rokku's skull. And the tip of Yoshiko's katana burst from his chest. 'A happy meal never bit back, baijii' spat Yoshiko, holding her blade fast. 'Goodbye, father,' said Rokku, springing back to his feet, and separating James' head from his shoulders with a single clean strike. An expression of absolute shock was on the vampire's face as his head hit the floor -- then it, and the rest of his body, crumbled to dust. Rokku took a deep breath, and sheathed his katana. Then he looked at Yoshiko. She was bloodied. Bruised. Soaked to the bone. And she was the most beautiful woman he had ever seen. Without a second thought, he took her in his arms and kissed her like he'd never kissed anyone before. To his pleasant surprise, she returned the kiss with equal passion, pulling herself close to him, like she was trying to make the two of them become one. Finally, they broke the kiss, and looked deep into each other's eyes. And one of the main support beams of the warehouse came down in a hail of ash and flame. 'Lets get the hell out of here!' shouted Yoshiko, grabbing Rokku's hand and turning to run. 'Wait!' shouted Rokku in response. Yoshiko stopped and glared at Rokku. The Steel Lion peeled off the long black coat, and held it for a moment, looking at the golden lion mon stitched onto the back. Then he threw it into the fire. The demons of his past were dead as of that moment -- it was time to look toward the future. Rokku felt Yoshiko squeeze his hand, and he turned away from the flames. Together, they ran from the burning building. Together, they ran towards the future.
