The Max-Attack: Chapter 10

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today Max starts running down the hall even faster and stops at the door he came in before. Max walks into the room on the other side of the door and hides beside it. The Blue Bat comes down the hall and into the room only to be attacked by Max with a hard kick to the face. Max jumps onto The Blue Bat and starts giving him punches to the nose and face. The Blue Bat recovers from the attack and kicks Max straight in the balls. The Blue Bat gets up from the punches Max gave and starts giving punches of his own to Max. The Blue Bat takes Max up and gives him a side kick straight to the jaw of max. Max falls to the ground holding his jaw and screaming in pain. The Blue Bat ignores the screaming and keeps on punching and kicking Max, who is still laying on the ground. The Blue Bat walks over to the big glass window in the room and smashes it with one single punch. The Blue Bat jumps out of the window, and is out of sight. Max gets up from the hard concrete ground and sees that the window is broken. Max walks over to the window and looks into the light windy city. Max sees a black dot in the sky moving really fast. Max also sees that the black dot drops something onto the streets below. The thing that it dropped was a tiny little ball, and when it hit the ground, a police car and a bench blew up! Max remember that The Blue Bat drops little balls, so Max jumps out of the window and flies after him. Max pushes his body really fast making him fly faster. Max sees that The Blue Bat has slowed down because he doesn’t know Max is behind him, and is only a couple feet away from The Blue Bat. Max tries going faster and jumps at The Blue Bat in mid air and grabs onto his legs. Max also hits a mid air kick taking them both out of the air. Max gets up from the middle of the street and takes The Blue Bat with him. Max punches The Blue Bat again and takes him into a old factory. The factory is really dark so max doesn’t know where he is walking. Max puts down The Blue Bat and starts stomping him on the ground. Max runs towards a hard wall and does a backflip off it catching The Blue Bat in a modified head scissors. Max gets up and starts laughing at The Blue Bat. Max kicks The Blue Bat one more time before leaving for a different part in the factory. Max walks up a flight of stairs and sees that The Blue Bat is still laying on the ground not moving. Max walks into a room that has a chair right by the window. Bright lights are coming from the window, notifying the sun is still out. Max walks over to the chair and sits on it, looking at the windy city. “Chicago is an awesome place to be in” says Max to his self. All of a sudden, from behind Max comes The Blue Bat with a shovel in his hands. The Blue Bat whacks the lights out of Max taking him off of the chair. The Blue Bat starts hitting Max repeatedly with the shovel making his whole body limp. The Blue Bat backs out of the room leaving Max lie on the ground, as did Max before. The Blue Bat hops down from the stairs and goes to walk out of the factory, but stops right before the door. The Blue Bat gets a sick smile on his face and walks back to where Max was laying. The Blue Bat takes a little ball out of his pocket. The Blue Bat backs up and throws the ball into the room. He starts running but is not fast enough and gets some of the explosion too. A big fire starts inside the room Max is in. Max is heard screaming trying to open a window to get out but can’t and drops to the ground because of the fumes from the fire. From the outside of the factory, fire fighters are seen getting hoses trying to stop the fire. A couple walk into the factory looking for the burning room. They see it and rush up there trying stop it. The fire fighters put out the fire, but Max is not in the room! The fire fighters have a confused look on there face but then see the window is open a wind is blowing into the room. “He must’ve jumped or something” says on of the fire fighters. Police officers start pouring into the factory and walk by The Blue Bat who is laying on the ground. The police officers take The Blue Bat up and sees that a smile is on his face. The officers takes The Blue Bat out of the factory and into a police car. The car drives away and all of a sudden Max is seen sitting on the very top of the factory look down on the people. Max jumps off the factory and flies away, now knowing The Blue Bat is n jail yet again. SCENE FADESMax starts running down the hall even faster and stops at the door he came in before.
