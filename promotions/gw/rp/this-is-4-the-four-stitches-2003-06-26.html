this is 4 the four stitches.

26 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: june 26th, 2003 My first pay. “Congratulations on the victory over Ace Cash, the crowd loved your performance,” he told me. I knew that already though. I knew that the fans would love me the second I stepped into that ring. They chanted it, “Reed! Reed!” As I pulled out a couple simple moves. Toe hold-to-reverse suplex-to-springboard elbow drop, shooting star DDT from the middle rope. Yawn, yawn, easy stuff. They gave me my first pay in bills, instead of a check. Almost an “under the table” thing, since I didn’t sign the big contract I was looking for until Sunday. They’re giving me more than some chumps who have been around here for years. Not surprising. But, before I get too full of myself, not all things went as planned last week. Monday night, I saw an old friend from three sixty wrestling. His name, he says, is Judgement. Judgement is mad at me, and on Monday night he sliced my eyelid by sending me through a car window. “Hi Judgement,” I say, offering my hand. “Nice seeing you here.” “RRRAAAARRRGGGGHHHHHHHH” he yells, and grabs me. And that’s what led to my four stitches. And I’ve been out of the hospital since about one in the morning, Tuesday. I’ve been too busy to think about them though; I already used almost all of my money for the week. For one, I bought a plane ticket and flew into Houston. Then, with more of my money, I purchased a much nicer apartment there, one with an air conditioner. Then, I celebrated my victory for this coming up Overload against Tyler Pierce with Ronny just yesterday. “But, you don’t face him for another two days Trey,” he explains. I sip my drink, “Ronny. I’ve been in Global Wrestling for almost two weeks now; I’ve studied the tapes, I’ve watched the matches and I’ve heard the interviews. This match is going to be just as easy as the green dreaded pretty boy last week, it’s like butter on toast. It is a given that I will get the victory over Tyler Pierce this Friday.” He sighs. “Trey, this isn’t NEW. Just because you took a win over a jobber doesn’t mean that Tyler Pierce will be the same. The guy is established in Global Wrestling, you can’t expect to go in and just do away with him like that.” Ha. That’s the thing about Ronny, he doesn’t know who he’s dealing with. I mean, sure, we are two good friends...but he doesn’t understand the depths of my skills, he never has. This isn’t NEW, I know that. This is GW, the chance I have to make myself into something more than just a “great.” I am going to be the one, I am going to shatter all the records here, make Hans Krueger and Dafoe and Sabbot and anyone else look like fools. That’s why this isn’t NEW, that’s why I am going to be the best. And then the dinner ended. Up until now, I haven’t had much time to concentrate on Judgement and Tyler Pierce. The moving, the paying, the traveling and site-seeing. The not-training, the not caring all together. But now, I have all the time in the world. My eyes are closed and I’m laying on my bed looking at nothing. I take my finger and softly run it across my left eyelid. The stitches won’t be removed until Saturday night, so I have to be careful this Friday to not get them ripped out. This means that Tyler Pierce can’t touch me, and I’m okay with not allowing him to have a chance against me. Maybe this will be the week to make a point, to make everyone know what I am here for. You see...The critics, when I first debuted in the states, used to compare me to a kitten with a ball of yarn. I slowly, methodically, stalk around studying the federation, looking at the layers of it. (GW has its bad guys, good guys and its no names...it has ownership by the Nevermore and by the McCoys.) Unleashing my claws, I rip into the yarn, and play around with it. Eventually tossing it to the side after running through things, I move on to another. But things are different, this isn’t NEW; it’s not EGWA, NEGWA, FWF, 360 or any other of the various. It’s a bigger ball of yarn. And I’ve changed. I’m ready to be more patient, more attentive. I’m ready to change things this time, to assert greatness. No more mistakes, no more drugs, no more blowing all my money. But first, I have to get a cigarette in me.
