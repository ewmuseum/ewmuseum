But little did I know that another surprise encounter awaited me? ==|Part IV: The Bullet That Came Between Us|== The scene reopens in Yosho’s office on the 27th floor of this unknown facility. Kyo glares at the man. “Who the hell are you?” The man in white sheathes his long sword after he wiped it clean. “I’m Mother’s son, Aion.” “After the Anti-Christ?”” Kyo still kept a tight grip on the shotgun. Since he still didn’t know what this man’s intentions were. As he felt a great energy coming from him that almost sent chills down his back. But he keeps his composure. Aion nods. “I see that you know your religion.” He soon noticed Kyo’s hostile behavior toward him. “You can lower your weapon. I’m not going to hurt you.” Kyo pumps the shotgun with a grin. “You’ll forgive me if I don’t.” Aion shrugs. “As you wish?” He kneeled beside Yosho’s corpse. As he yank a necklace off the corpse’s severed neck. “I believe this is what you were asking for.” He tossed the necklace to Kyo. Kyo catches it with one hand. And see that it was in fact the necklace that Yosho took from him when he captured him. He slips the necklace in his lab pants for safe keeping. “Why did you kill Yosho?” “Isn’t this what you wanted” Didn’t you want Yosho to die a miserable death?” Aion stood up. He soon nods his head. “Oh I see?” He chuckled. “You wanted to be the one to kill him. Sorry about that but Yosho lived up his purpose to us.” Kyo glares at him. His grip on the shotgun got firmer. He had a hunch that these two were allies of some sort. But why would Aion kill Yosho? “Mother and I never trusted him to begin with. We were just using him to get to you.” “What?” Aion rests his sheathed sword over his shoulder. “Mother wants you to join us.” “But why me?”” Aion nods. As he begin walking around Kyo with calmness. “You have exceptional skill and power that would be much vital to our cause.” “And let me guess” With my help you’ll rule the world with no problem, right?” Kyo grinned. Was this guy actually for real? He sounded like a corny villain from bad comic book. Aion shakes his head. “Oh no” Ruling the world is for mortals.” He stopped. As he look at Kyo with fire in his eyes. “But creating a new world is for Gods.” “You can’t be serious.” Aion walks to the large windows in the office. As he check out the view of a city that shined brightly in the night. “I’m very much serious. This world is on the verge of destruction anyways. And it’s no surprise since this world is overrun by these vile mortals.” “So, you want to wipe out all the mortals and create a Utopia for special beings like us.” Kyo sighed. Did this guy get his material from a comic book or what? I mean come on this was just sad to listen to him. Aion nods. “Exactly?” He looked at Kyo by his reflection on the window. “Will join Mother on her cause?” This perhaps was one of the most common causes in the history of all things evil. But did Aion actually tempt Kyo on joining Mother with his words? Kyo closes his eyes. “This world is may not be perfect but it is pretty decent place.” He opened his eyes. As he glare at Aion. “So I guess I’m telling you that you can go to hell.” Kyo tightened his grip more on the shotgun. Aion shook his head with his eyes closed. He sighs. “You’re such a disappointment. All that power but no will to use it.” Aion laughed. “I mean the power that you use to have.” Aion turns around. As he unsheathe his sword. And swing it across without a second thought. Glass shattered! Kyo witnessed the sphere that contained his Shadow Powers get destroyed by Aion’s sword attack. Black vapors seeped out of the shard of the sphere. The vapors soon take form of the spirit of Amatsu Mikaboshi that was better known as the Japanese God of Evil. It roars at both men before it disappeared. Aion sheathes his sword. “You could’ve been a God.” He glared at Kyo. “But you choose to be a stupid mortal just like that stupid mortal you fell in love with.” Aion’s words triggered a vision in Kyo’s mind. Kyo sees that he was facing the back of a woman with long hair that blew in the wind. His vision ends. As Kyo anger filled his eyes realizing that he just insulted the woman that still love to this day. Kyo goes to shoot Aion. Aion stopped Kyo with a swift swing of his sword. His sword cut right through shotgun. But Kyo released his grip on the weapon before his hands were slashed off by the attack. Kyo flips backwards. He landed some feet away from Aion. As he pull out two handguns. And aim them at Aion. But Aion once again stopped Kyo with a swift sword of his sword. His sword cut off the guns barrels. Kyo throws the unusable guns at Aion. Aion slashed the guns into more pieces. As Kyo use this chance to strike Aion with a spin kick. But Aion avoided it by stepping to the side. Kyo starts fighting Aion bare fisted along with bare feet. Aion maneuvers through Kyo’s assault. It was as if Aion could read Kyo’s moves. And he was? Aion soon decided to fight back with a vicious one palm thrust to Kyo’s chest! Kyo was thrown back. But he catches his bearings. He dropped down to a knee. As he cough up blood. “I think it was a mistake fighting this guy without any powers.” “Don’t tell me that you’re finished already.” Kyo looks at Aion. But he saw that he wasn’t there. He soon senses that something was fast approaching him above him. He looked up. And see that Aion was soaring down at him with a sword attack. “Damn!” Kyo flipped away. The floor shatters! Aion stood up from the crater that was made from his aerial sword attack. He brushes himself off with a grin. “I missed, eh?” He acted too mellow for a killer. Just what kind of person was Aion really? Kyo landed far away from Aion. “Just how powerful is this guy?”” He bumped into something. As he sees that he bumped into a tall metal lamp post. Kyo pushed off lamp cap. And decide to make it his weapon against Aion’s sword. “Let’s go.” Aion smirks. As he lunge at Kyo. And swing his sword. But Kyo blocked the attack with the lamp post. They were soon engage in combat. The sounds of metal hitting metal echoed the office! Kyo mysteriously release his weapon. Aion looked at Kyo with puzzlement. As he goes to end the fight with a stab to Kyo’s heart. But Kyo stopped him with kicking up his weapon before it touched the floor. It hits Aion in the chest. Kyo caught it then followed through with a hard swing. Glass shatters! Aion stumbled back. He felt a trickle of blood dripping from his cheek. As the light bulb that was still lamp shattered on impact opening a small cut. Aion watched Kyo swing the weapon. It was like Kyo was taunting him to attack. He decided to accept Kyo’s challenge with a hard swing of his sword that released a strong energy blast. Kyo went to defend against it. But the blast struck him hard in the chest. As he get thrown into the back wall of the room. He slumped down from the imprint that he made on the wall. Kyo falls to the floor on all four. He coughed up more blood than before. “This was all fun.” Aion sighed. “But alas, all good things must come to an end.” His sword began to glow a dim violet color. “Farwell Kyo...” He swung his sword releasing a great blast that was swirling right at Kyo. “TORUNE-DO MURASAKI!” Aion’s energy attack gets closer to Kyo? Kyo stayed in the same position. It was like he had fallen unconscious in this position. Was that case? Was Kyo finished? The energy attack was about to strike Kyo. But Kyo suddenly lifted his head revealing that his eyes glow a dark crimson color. The scene switches to outside of the facility that revealed to be a tall building. As Yosho’s office blew up with great force! The scene changes to the Yosho’s office. As the office looked like a total war zone. Everything was destroyed? Possibly no one could have survived that wicked explosion. Could they have? Aion stood unharmed at the large hole where the windows use to be at. But there was no sign of Kyo in the office. What happened to him? Did the explosion vaporize him into nothingness? A cold wind blew? “Are you all right, Lord Aion?” A dark figure kneeled far behind Aion. It was like this figure appeared with the wind. Who was the figure? And what sort of connection did this figure have with Aion? Aion rests his sheathed sword on his shoulder. “Yes.” “Did you kill him?” “No. He managed to escape.” Aion grinned. “It appears that I underestimated him.” “Do you want me to go after him?” “No, I want Sniper to retrieve him.” Aion looked out at the bright city. “He has my permission to use lethal force if the target becomes hostile.” Who was Sniper? Did Aion just sign Kyo’s death warrant by sending this Sniper character after him? “Yes, my Lord.” A cold wind blows again. As the figure was gone... “It appears that you’re a not mortal.” He unsheathed his sword halfway. Aion checks his sword. And saw that there were visible cracks on the blade. “You’re something else...” He laughed. What did Aion mean by that? And where was Kyo? “Meanwhile” The scene switches to the facility’s parking area. There a couple of common cars parked here. But there was one this particular car that roof was dented beyond repair. All the windows have been shattered. It looked some heavy landed on top of the car to cause all this damage. What could’ve done this? There were some traces of blood on the side of the car’s roof. As there was a blood trail that started at the car. And it continued on further away from the car. Blood trail ends outside of a small outdoor security office. The scene changes to inside the office. There lied a guard on the floor. It appears that the guard has been possibly knocked out by someone. This someone was none other then Kyo himself. As he sat in fold chair treating a deep cut that was on his side with the office’s first aid kit. This would be a job for his regenerative power. But remember that his power was remove then destroyed. There was a question that needed to be answered. How did Kyo get from the rooftop to the parking area? And what caused the explosion at Yosho’s office? Kyo starts thinking back to that exact moment of time? “Moments Ago” The scene changed to Yosho’s office. Aion had just fired the Torune-Do Murasaki at Kyo. It looks like the blast was about to strike Kyo. But Kyo underwent a strange transformation. His long raven hair has turned dark crimson. He soon lifts his head up to reveal the black markings on his face along with his crimson glowing eyes that were slit like some kind of a reptile. Aion looked at Kyo with shock. “What’s this!”” Kyo roared at Aion like a mighty beast. His left hand was gathering an unknown violent power. “KOKYUU RYUUJIN!” He thrust out his left arm. As he fires a great blast at Aion’s Torune-Do Murasaki. Don’t know if it was Aion imagined it. But he saw a mighty spirit of a dragon form around Kyo’s energy attack. It roars with great rage before it along with the attack collided against Aion’s attack. Both attacks soon engaged in a vicious power struggle. It was a back and forth. But the attacks couldn’t last any longer in this. A bright spark was seen between both attacks? The entire room exploded! Kyo had jumped from the top floor during the explosion. While Aion had put an energy shield around himself with the use of his sword that had taken great damage for doing so. Kyo had returned to normal. But he was now falling to his death. He needed to think fast before he became a stain on the ground below. He decided to will the wings that his beloved gave him. The wings didn’t come out. He tried again. Still no wings sprout out. It appeared that Kyo was dead. He was getting closer to the ground? Suddenly, a strange idea entered Kyo’s mind. Closer? Kyo guided his hand over to a wound on his side that a big shard of glass stuck out. He got this wound within the explosion. As he yanked it out. He gathered blood from the wound. And smear the blood on his forearms. Closer? He didn’t know how. But he started doing ninja hand signs with the use of both hands. He executed the hand signs like he a pro. Closer? “Blood Wings, release!” Wings of red appeared on his forearms. But did he make it in time? The wings slowed him down. But it wasn’t enough. As he still fell fast. He saw that he was heading toward the facility’s parking area. Kyo turned over on his back. Glass shattered! Kyo crash landed on a car’s rooftop breaking the windows in impact. The wings vanished without a trace. Kyo lied on the dented roof motionless? Was he dead? He soon stirred with a grunt. As he show signs of life. “Present Time... The scene switches back to the security office. Kyo had finished patching himself up. As he lean back in the chair. Kyo begins to wonder what happened up there. How did he know how to make those hand signs” And how did he know that move? He never was taught that move along with those hand signs. It was like something inside took control of his body. But the biggest mystery that he wondered was what that transform he underwent was. He checks his face on an off computer screen. As he seen that his face returned to normal. But still he wondered about that transformation. Since he thought that his powers were gone. Well something powerful was going within Kyo. But he decided to think more about this after he was long gone from this place. Kyo exits the security office. But he stopped. He sees that many lasers were locked on him. As it was revealed that many guards armed with assault rifle that were equipped with lasers were aimed at him. It looks like that Kyo was pinched. Suddenly, a pink mist lingered before the guards? The guards soon mysteriously falls to the ground after they caught whiff of this unknown mist. Kyo blinks. “What the hell?” He saw that the guards were all asleep. As he realizes the mist was a strong sleeping powder. Kyo started to wonder who did this. Since he knew that only one person could do this. But that person was supposed to be dead. Or did that person really die that night? “It looks like that I made it in time.” Kyo looks around. But he soon saw a whirlwind of cherry blossom petals appear behind him. The whirlwind disperses. And revealed that Kairi Silverwolf was alive along with well. “Kairi” Is that really you?”” He reached out to touch her to make sure that this wasn’t some kind of a sick trick phantom up by Aion. Kairi leaps over to her master. Kyo caught her in his arms. She embraces him. As she was glad that he was still alive. “I told you I would always have your back, Master.” She winked at him. Kyo blinks. “But I think you were killed by that explosion.” Kairi laughs. “Master, if I could survive years of your intense training then I think I can survive a simply explosion.” “I’m just glad that you are you all right.” “Me too?” Kairi smiled. More guards approaches? “I would love to continue this reunion but we need to roll now.” Kairi nodded. Kyo soon broke into a car. He begins hotwiring a car for it to start. And it does. Kairi entered the car. As they both drove off with much haste. The guards continues running after them on foot. But they stopped when the car entered the long bridge that led from the facility to the main city. It appeared that the guards were about to enter their cars. But they stopped. As they see a man in street clothes mysteriously appear at the start of the bridge with a highly advanced sniper rifle in his hand. This man was probably the one that Aion called Sniper. Sniper drops on one knee. He aimed the gun down the bridge. There was no scope on the rifle. As he only used his own sight for sniping. What was Sniper planning on doing here? Don’t tell me that he was going to shoot Kyo from here. Kyo had to at least one hundred meters away now. The scene changes to the inside of the car. Kyo looks at Kairi with shock. “You’re telling me that I was in Japan all this time! I can’t believe that the main headquarters of The Organization was here the entire time.” Kairi nods. “We’re in the outskirts of Tokyo. This place doesn’t even exist on the Japan’s map. So you know that something sinister was going on here.” “How were about to find this place then?” Kairi leans back in her seat. “I had help.” “Help from whom?” Kyo looked at her. The scene switches back to the start of the bridge. Sniper fired a single shot! The scene changes to the inside of the car. Kairi’s wolf ears suddenly sprouted out from her hair. As if she heard something fast approaching them. Glass soon shatters! The scene switches to the outside of the car. The back window had shattered from great force. As the car swirls side to side before it crashed right through the metal rail on the bridge. Car splashed hard in the water below! The car begins sinking? The scene changes to the start of the bridge. Sniper stood with a grin on his face. “Yeah, I’m the King.” As he knew he hit his target dead on. All the guards look at Sniper with amazement. As they have never seen anything like that before. But who hasn’t? The scene switches to the crash site. As the car continued sinking? Was this the end of Kyo? Will Kyo end up being with Mother and Aion? Afraid that all these questions must be answered at another time. Be sure to check out Part V.
