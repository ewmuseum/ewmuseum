The Killer

29 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-29-05 'Well well well,' muttered the man in the third floor hallway of the expensive Italian hotel. 'I guess I shouldn't be surprised that you are here, but I would have thought Michael to have more sense than this.' Mindy glared up in defiance at her father, as he attempted to poke his head inside the hotel room and look around. 'Where is he? I know he's here with you, now where is he?!' Mindy was in disbelief. She paused for a moment, trying as hard as she could to keep her composure, as she calmly answered her father. 'Okay, first of all, calm down. Take a deep breath, eat a doughnut or something. Two, leave Michael out of th-' 'This isn't some kind of joke!' Luis snapped back. 'You run away from home, and you expect me not to be worried? My nephew promised me he would return you to me the moment he got in touch with you, and of course he took advantage of my trust in him. He is involved in this as much as you are, now where is he?!' Luis' wish was promptly granted. Michael left the steamy bathroom only wearing his tee shirt and a pair of boxers with a yellow smiley face on the front, and the caption 'Smile, Ya Fuck!' on the waistband. He obviously wasn't expecting his uncle to be at the front door, and he blinked in shock at the initial sighting of him. 'Uhhh... hey.' 'You...' Luis snarled through gritted teeth, as he pointed a shaky finger at his nephew. 'You were told to bring my daughter back to me. Why did you not do as you were told?' Michael hesitated. 'Because... I don't answer to you?' 'Well if you don't mind me asking, who do you answer to then?' Luis grew impatient, as he remained out in the hallway, arms folded and right foot tapping the carpeted floor. Michael's sense of humor soon started to fade, as his eyes remained on his uncle. 'I do mind you asking, actually. I mind a goddamn lot.' 'Mikey, calm down and head off for a second,' Mindy interrupted, trying desperately to keep things from getting worse. 'Please, this is between me and you, dad. I'm here, I'm the one you were looking for, you talk to me.' Michael couldn't believe it. Mindy didn't have to take the verbal bashing she was bound to get from her father, but she was willing to take it anyway. She had just earned a whole new level of respect from her older cousin, and therefore, he stumbled off to find a pair of pants. 'Who the fuck do you think you are disobeying me, Rosa? I am your father, and you will do as I tell you, whether you disagree or not!' Mindy simply shook her head. She was so angry, so disgusted... she just couldn't bring herself to make a level-headed remark in response to her father's anger. He used the silence to his advantage. 'Get your things, and get them right now. We are leaving. And you can forget about wrestling, and you can forget about seeing your cousin ever again, because it's never going to happen, do you understand me?!' Mindy refused to cry, not wanting to give her dad the satisfaction. Instead she merely stood in the doorway, refusing to budge to her father's threats. He yelled at her yet again. 'What are you waiting for? Get your damned things!' Luis stared at Mindy, and did not break his eye contact once. Mindy had to do something that didn't involve giving in to her father's orders. She thought and thought, before finally... 'No.' Luis laughed, as if Mindy had actually told him a joke. 'Excuse me?' he inquired. 'I didn't hear you.' 'Yes you did,' Mindy murmured, leaving Luis even more impatient than before- if that was even possible. 'You are going to get your things,' he stated, 'or I'm going to go in there and get them for you.' Mindy still did not move. Slowly, Luis took an imposing step forward. 'Get out of the way, Rosa.' 'I said no.' That did it. Luis grabbed his daughter by one of her black and pink pigtails, and threatened to drag her aside by it alone. At that moment, every single terrible thought gained from her father flashed through her head. 'The sight of you makes me sick.' 'You being a wrestler pains me. If you think you can really accomplish anything by getting beaten up, you might as well not consider yourself my daughter.' 'You are going to get your things, or I'm going to go in there and get them for you.' And without the actual act, Mindy could feel the stinging sensation of Luis' hand slapping the taste out of her mouth. He hadn't done it at that moment, but it was a feeling she had endured more than once before- and if she didn't do something soon, it would probably happen again. 'Fuck!' Suddenly, Luis stumbled back in amazement as his daughter struggled in his grasp, quickly breaking free of him before she shoved him back as hard as he could. He smacked the back of his head against the unforgiving wall behind him, before his eyes once again were locked onto Mindy's. For a moment, Mindy felt as though the push had knocked some sense into her father, but before long that thought was out of her head. Luis approached once more, and soon forced Mindy to retaliate with a loud slap, sending her father reeling. Before he could even realize what hit him, Mindy was all over her own father with punches and kicks. Every once of fury and sorrow was unleashed at that one moment, as her dad crumbled to the floor in a surprising amount of pain. He was bewildered that his own daughter was actually doing such a thing to him, and briefly so much as wondered why she would attack him. Soon, however, the message was all too clear for him. And if Michael hadn't seen the fight and pried Mindy off of him, it probably would have been the last message he'd ever gotten at the hyperactive rate she was on. 'Let go of me!' She kicked and thrashed, but Michael refused to let go. He held on tightly, before backing her into the hotel room and quickly getting in front of the doorway. He took one look at the man who found himself busted open on the floor, and noticed the regret in his eyes. It wasn't enough to forgive him for the torture he put his daughter through over the years, but perhaps it was a start. After a couple of deep breaths, Mindy sank to her knees and could only bring herself to do one thing- cry her eyes out. 'Oh sweet chocolate Christ, don't do that,' Michael groaned sadly, before he slowly closed the front door and placed a comforting hand on Mindy's shoulder and giving her a hug. He had no doubt in his mind that Mindy would be able to defend herself at Clash of the Titans- the real question, though, was if she was really thinking about that anymore. All in all, though, Mindy could only bring herself to think about one thing. 'Gods... I can't believe it. I should have done that much, much sooner.'
