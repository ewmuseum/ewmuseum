The Rome Incident - Finding Ace

25 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03.25.04 'Dammit, where in the hell have you been?' A furious Ken Partridge Jr is screaming at the just arriving Ace Harding in the hotel parking deck as Ice and Josh look on. 'Here we are in the middle of a fucking crisis and you're nowhere to be found.' 'Calm down, Ken, damn... I'm here now. How's Josh,' Ace asks calmly. 'Oh, Josh is just wonderful. All fine and dandy. He only OD'd on Rohypnol. Why in the hell are you just now showing up, Ace?' Josh lowers his head a bit at the comment. However, Ken doesn't notice that his words just stung his best-friend violently. 'I just got the message, Ken. Shit! I got here as soon as I could. The nearest gym's like 30 or 40 mi...' Ken cuts Ace off before he can finish. 'GYM?!?! YOU WERE AT THE FUCKING GYM?!?!' 'Well, how in the hell else do you figure I'm going to prepare for The Global Rumble,' Ace continues. Ken's eyes turn more violent. 'Not by getting your head smashed in with a freakin' con-chair-to and leaving me to get ambushed.' Ace finally starts to lose it some with the last comment. 'Leaving YOU to get ambushed? Where the fuck were you when The White fucking Rose attacked me, huh? I was left a little unconscious you sanctimonious holier-than-thou son of a bitch!' With that Ken slaps Ace across the face. 'Watch your fucking mouth,' Ken insists. Ace and Ken both lunge at each other with fists blaring, causing Brock and Josh to get between them. The remaining two Elite members are doing their best to calm KPJ and Harding down. Finally, the commotion stops some with Ice's aggressive words. 'Stop it, both of you! We all have enough to worry about without this kind of bullshit! Ken, you know just as well as Josh and I do that there's no way Ace could have predicted this was going to happen. He was training, preparing. Hell, whether you want to admit it or not, the three of you ARE going to be in the same ring at the Rumble... AND... on top of that, after one of you wins it, the two of us will have to face off for my title.' Josh chimes in, causing everyone to finally calm down completly. 'Look, I'm sorry for what I did, but we've got more to worry about here. This is a test... a test between friends... between teammates. Now, I know that we won't have anything to worry about until the end of the match, but what happens then, what happens when it's every man for himself?' Ken speaks quietly and calmly. 'Then it's time for the best man to win and the rest of us to stand behind him.' Ken places his hand out and it's met by Josh's and then Brock's. Ken's eyes meet Ace's, and you can tell that there is still left over hostility between the two from past organizations and former feuds. It's almost as if the two are glaring directly into one another's thoughts and minds. Finally, Ace places his hand in with the rest, he and Ken still staring at each other. 'Let the best man win.'
