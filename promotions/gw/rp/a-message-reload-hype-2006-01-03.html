A message... [Reload Hype]

3 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-3-06 Images of football games with the WFA logo in the bottom corner flash across the screen? Images showing the Hammer Brothers dominating the offenses of the league and destroying player after player? Names flash by with injuries and the severity of them?. The cameras zoom out and the images play on a sub screen next to bald man in a suit. The Name Drake Astor appears below him for a moment, and then fades out. Drake: In sports today, the newly formed WFA have released its two star rookie defensive outside linebackers of the Hawaiian Pirates franchise. All reasons are still unclear, but one thing has been stated is that the injury list they have cause was to damaging to the league. Pirate’s fans are very disappointed in the loss of the two powerhouses. WFA owner Bobby Devante has not yet released an official statement, but is expected to do so in a few days. The screen pauses and a man dressed in a Black suit with a blue silk shirt walks in front of the images. The man holds in his hand a remote control. Man: Hello my name is Quinton Spikes, but you can call me Mr. Spikes. I am the agent and manager of the Hammer Brothers. To brutal for sports they said. To violent on the gridiron they say. Well sport is war. And soon this destruction shall be unleashed on the teams and fighters of Global? As Quinton says this, the image behind him, is replaced by the Hammer Brothers Logo? And from each side of the screen walk the Hammer Brothers; Gimley and Gorn. Spikes: January 12, 2006. Time for a new beginning in Global. Time for a reload of the game? This game is about to get a lot more brutal then you can imagine? Boys? Tell your victims at Reload what time it is? The Hammer Brothers: It is time to? Gimley: To Smash and Bash!!! Gorn: TO CRUSH YOU!!!! The Hammer Brothers: IT IS TIME TO HAMMER YOU TO GOO!!!! The two smash their fists into their hands as they have a sick smile and laugh as Quinton smiles and nods. He then takes a few steps closer to the camera. Spikes: At Reload a new era will come, a new day will arrive, and it is time for the dawning of the Hammer Brothers. As he says Hammer Brothers fire comes from the right side behind Gorn, and ice from the left side behind Gimley, at the same time. Spikes: Get ready Global? It’s Showtime?. Quinton Spikes then points the remote at the camera and the screen winks off and the Global logo flashes and then fades out.
