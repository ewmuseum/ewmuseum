Date: 07.22.05 07.18.05 (8:16 PM MST) 'You know, you're birthday's in a few hours, right?' asked Markus Riley, watching his girlfriend from the rolling chair he was sliding along the floor in. Across the pink decorated bedroom lay his girlfriend Mindy, resting on her comfortable bed while holding on tightly to a purple stuffed teddy bear. She was wide awake, which was obvious when she responded to her boyfriend's question. 'Yeah?' she started confusingly with feigned interest; with how up and down things had been recently, it was tough for her to remain happy without the fear of something messing it up. 'It can't be the eighteenth, already, can it?' Markus smiled, contrary to the fact that Mindy's back was turned to him. 'If my shaky track keeping of the days hold true, yes, it can.' Mindy sighed inwardly; she didn't want to be bothered, but it didn't appear as though she had a choice in the matter, especially when the felt the bed shift from the added weight that sat down over the edge of it. 'Things won't be happy all the time, but I guess I just oughta enjoy my moments while they last.' Mindy tried her best to form a smile, and rolled onto her other side to face her boyfriend. The reaction Markus gave seemed to prove that Mindy's false expression had worked its magic enough. 'Wow... and I haven't even had time to plan a party.' 'Parties are overrated,' Markus informed calmly as he leaned to his side, placing one arm over and on the other side of Mindy in the process. 'You're gonna be twenty, soon, babe. Parties aren't necessary beyond the teenage years, I mean, unless you're going drinking or something.' He stopped his speech to favor looking at the innocent nineteen year old's beautiful green eyes. 'And you don't seem like the drinking type, so...' Mindy counteracted Markus' praise with a peck on the tip of his nose. 'A party wouldn't hurt, though.' Markus blinked in surprise from the unexpected cute little kiss he had recieved, letting that carry a smirk across his lips. 'Hey, if you need a party, look no further. I'm enough party for a whole boatload of people.' Mindy laughed; not one of her high spirited- and high pitched- giggles, but a low, relaxed laugh. 'I don't doubt that.' They looked into eachother's eyes for a long time. And then a longer time; so long that it felt like time had actually stopped just to let them enjoy gazing at one another without allowing too many breaths of life to be wasted on mere gazing. Eventually, Markus decided that perhaps more than staring contests were in order: the timing felt somewhat appropriate, given their recent draws together from everything gone on in their lives, which would all culminate in three and a half hours when Mindy's teenage life had passed on to true adulthood. That thought in mind, Markus gave himself enough self-assurance to lean down and kiss his girlfriend passionately on the lips. It had been a long time since they had shared such a moment together, and they both seemed to sense that. They were in eachother's arms in a true moment of love; where love transcended its worded meaning and took on a level that could never be expressed through letters. Markus loved Mindy. And Mindy loved Markus. All was right with the world. Or not. 'Sorry..' Mindy pulled away slowly, trailing a black nail-polished finger over the corner of her bottom lip as she spoke, 'I just... I'm not sure if I'm really ready for this... I'm, uh...' 'It's alright,' Markus cut her off, sitting up as he adjusted the ever-present hat on his head. 'I know this whole thing is kinda.. for lack of a better term.. fucked.' That was the best term he could come up with? 'Wh- what do you mean?' Mindy was nervous, of course, but she wouldn't have put her feelings quite as close to the perspective Markus was seeing them from. In fact, she didn't seem to fully grasp what Markus had meant; she did get the right idea on part of his mindset, but not nearly the whole thing. Of course, however, she became silent so that she allowed Markus to elaborate. 'Well, you know, you got the whole.. innocent thing goin' for you, and I'm just the corruptive boyfriend. I'm not the most straight-edge person on the block, and the only thing you get high off of is gummi bears and sugar packets. I'm sure the last thing you want is...' Mindy frowned. 'Well... I can't say you're completely wrong.. but you're not entirely right, either.' The sugar driven girl thought of a way to put things without being too... dirty sounding, which considering its Mindy Mercer who Markus is dealing with, took quite a bit of time. 'Yeah... I am afraid. But the fact that I haven't been intimate with you isn't because you seem to think you're a bad person.. I just... I have so much on my mind right now, ya know? And I mean this stuff isn't making just my head hurt, it's making my heart hurt, too, hunny bunny.' As if the words weren't enough, Mindy expressed the importance of that sentence by rubbing her forehead, immediately followed by clutching her chest. And suddenly Markus got hit with a case of 'self guilt' for yet another reason. 'Yeah...' with a deep exhale, Markus flopped onto his side and hugged Mindy gently, pulling her back against his chest. 'I'm sorry.. I keep trying to remind myself how much you've been going through, and just wish there were more ways to be there for you than just saying 'I love you' twenty thousand times. It's just.. tough when you're afraid you're pushing yourself too close to someone.' A pause. 'I told your cousin this already, and now I'm telling you.. I just want the opportunity to make things better.' Slowly but surely, Mindy nodded her head against Markus. 'I understand.' ---- 06.15.05 (5:00 PM CEST) 'I won't be surprised if I never see you again after today,' spoke a mild mannered woman, as he looked sorrowfully into the eyes of her daughter. 'You don't even have to believe a word I say. Whether you trust me or not, though, I'm just going to give you the truth.' Mindy let the words sink into her mind, but showed no other confirmation that she was listening. The only clue Arielle had to having her daughter's attention was the fact that Mindy's eyes seemed to be pleading wordlessly, desperately for some answers. Mindy couldn't care enough about truth anymore; the word 'truth' had been twisted around so many times to fit her naive mind that truth was nothing more than a joke. If there was anything Mindy knew to be the truth, it was the fact that she didn't know whether to be sad that her parents had covered so many things up from her, or how much she had once believed all of it. Arielle wanted to wait until she knew Mindy was listening before she began her attempt of rationalizing things, but she soon realized that she would get no words from her daughter; therefore, she started anyway. 'Your...' immediately things started off shaky, as she tried to find the right word, but failed. 'Your father and I, we never knew your parents personally. We only saw on the news that a little girl had survived a car crash with her mom and dad here in Verona, a few blocks away from this house. Your parents....' Mindy's eyes narrowed as Arielle trailed off once again. The middle aged woman noticed the glance she had recieved, and swiftly regained her composure as best as she could. 'Well, I guess you know what happened to your parents then, and I don't need to get into that. Anyway, naturally we were upset about it, but neither Luis or I had acted upon it at that moment... not until we found out who the others involved in the car crash had been.' Mindy tilted her head slightly. She tried to think where her mother was going with things, but it was impossible to think straight much at all. Her cousin and her boyfriend wouldn't have flown all the way to Italy for no reason at all; what they had planned downstairs, only the Lord above knew about. How Mindy could get her mind on track thinking about things like that, was something that she was yet to figure out. 'Luis' brother and sister-in-law were driving the car that killed your parents,' confessed a near teary-eyed Arielle, although Mindy didn't fully comprehend the meaning behind the sentence until her 'mother' had finished, 'more specifically, Michael's own mother and father.' 'We, of course, took you in from the orphanage you had been left in, and tried to get in touch with Michael's parents. Unfortunately, although we knew them to be a rather well-mannered pair, they weren't very willing to admit what they'd done.' Arielle paused momentarily to wipe a swelling tear from her right eye. 'I guess if you were responsible for killing two people, it's easier to pretend it never happened than to confess to it. Your fath... Luis didn't like the fact that his own relatives could be so ignorant, and... well, that leads us to now.' Mindy's face took on a look of disgust. She knew her dad was a scumbag, but she never would have truly thought he would do something like this... 'Michael's parents were kept alive by some friends of your father's, and some people he knows are just... dangerous. Needless to say, I don't think they were left in the greatest conditions, if they're still alive, that is.' All Mindy could do was shake her head. Just shake her head, and wish that everything she just heard was more fabrication of the truth. Unfortunately for her, the knot in Mindy's stomach told her that something like this just could not be made up. 'I'm sorry,' her mother continued still, 'I'm really sorry I didn't say anything... I hate to admit to it, but I was just too afraid of what would happen if I told anyone. God, I'm so sorry...' Upon the sight of tears coming from Arielle's eyes, young Mindy leaned forward and gave her a comforting hug, even though she wasn't exactly head over heels for her mother's apathy before that day. 'At least she didn't kidnap two people and take their lives away...' ---- 'Why... how, why would you do it?' Michael was shaking with so much fury, so much thirst for justice, he could barely form the sentence that left his lips at he stared at his uncle with little- if any- compassion in his eyes. Markus stood and watched helplessly; now that he was assured Mindy was safe, his reason for being there had pretty much been finished. He wished he had the strength to move to the stairs and run up and see his girlfriend, console her, promise her things would be okay... but he had a feeling Michael didn't want that. Therefore, in fear of causing notice towards himself, he simply stood as relaxed as he could, watching what was happening right in front of him. Michael, on the other hand, couldn't remain so still. He turned his back to the two men, stumbling a couple steps in the direction of the front door. Luis prayed that Michael was going to simply leave, but there was no way this was going to be solved so easily and he knew it, and the thought had soon left his mind as quickly as it approached. Of course, Michael stopped in his tracks, brushing back his slick black and red hair with his hands before he looked over his shoulder at his uncle, still bound to the couch. 'I...' Markus winced with every attempt Michael made to calm himself; it hurt watching the man this upset, especially considering Michael was usually so low-key... for a Mercer, anyway. Low-key or not, however, this didn't make for a good situation. Markus sensed something dreadful quickly approaching. Tired of remaining silent- no matter how much he was unable to it- Michael made his way back over to Luis and sat down on the coffee table in front of him. 'Where are they?' he asked, not hurriedly, but with enough meaning behind his voice that along with his question, he'd swiftly assure punishment in response of an answer he did not want. Luis recognized the threat in a heartbeat, and not being one to play stupid to the point of stupidity, he quickly shot back an answer. ---- Markus Riley and Michael Mercer stood across from eachother in the kitchen. It wasn't the ideal place to discuss how to handle another man's life, but considering it was the only real spot in the house where they could chat without anyone hearing, neither man had much of a choice. 'What are you thinking?' Markus choked out without really paying much attention to what he was saying. Michael, of course, didn't take the question so lightly. 'The fuck do you think I'm thinking?' Markus stammered indifferently. 'I, I dunno...' Michael grit his teeth in annoyance, and reached over his shoulder. There, he grabbed a handle on a large butcher knife in the wooden cutlery set behind him and slid it out. 'I think I'm gonna kill him.' Markus' eyes widened- and rightfully so- at the response. Yet at the same time, he was so taken aback by what was said, that he almost burst out into laughter. 'You're kidding right?' The brown eyed Mercer simply twirled the knife handle in his hand and shook his head. 'Nope. As funny as that is, I'm not.' Michael looked out towards the living room at the other end of the kitchen, and sighed. Sitting in that living room would be a man he once had no business with. They were family, and that was that. But now... 'Trust me.. I am not kidding.' 'Michael...' Markus looked into the eyes of his friend- if he could indeed still call him that- and sighed. Michael almost seemed just as displeased. 'What?' Markus took a deep breath. 'Look... I know you're upset. I don't know what to say to you other than I'm sorry. But you can't just kill him.' That remark seemed to spark something in Michael's mind; before Markus knew it, Michael had rushed up to him, and slammed the knife inches past Markus' body and into the counter he stood in front of. 'Give me one good reason why not.' 'Do you really want to become worse than him?' Good point. Seeing the reason in Markus' words, Michael backed away. He took a couple breaths, and glanced back at the living room in the distance. 'You came all the way here. Why?' Markus thought about the question, but he didn't have to think about if for too long. 'Dude, I heard the message left on your machine, and the first thing that popped into my head was...' Markus trailed off, noticing the weird look Michael was giving him for some reason. Sighing, Markus' tone of voice was raised slightly, emphasizing the meaning to what he was saying. 'I love your cousin, man.. like me or not, I really, really love Mindy. And I would do anything to make sure she was safe.' Michael smirked. Of course, it was only logical that he perfectly understood what Markus meant. After all, the last time Michael felt so much love for someone... 'Jaden.' 'Well, she is safe, you have that,' Michael nodded. 'In fact, perhaps you should go upstairs and check on her... get her out of here.' Markus tilted his head ever so slightly from bewilderment. 'Wh- what?' 'You heard me,' Michael answered swiftly, 'take Mindy back home and stay with her. I don't want her to have to see or hear any of this.' The gentle giant gulped, and sulked out to the living room to make his way for the stairs. 'Then do what you have to do, Michael,' Markus said as he passed, 'I just want the opportunity to make things better.' Both men exchanged somber looks, but Michael refused to comment anymore. He had enough with talking. Markus took the hint, and made his way upstairs. He and Mindy consoled eachother, held eachother, and together they had left the house. Whatever happened beyond that, only Michael and his aunt knew. And of course, his uncle. No one could forget about him. ---- 07.19.05 (12:02 AM MST) That was when Mindy woke up from her brief slumber, and was glad to have remained in Markus' arms for those few hours. Smiling at the warmth from her boyfriend's clutches, Mindy wriggled around just enough to look over at the clock, and her smile expanded instantly. 'Mew...' Her first moments out of teendom seemed interesting... not that welcome of a change, really, but then again, it didn't take much more indication that her teenage years had more or less sucked. Maybe Mindy would find herself on a different stroke of luck? 'Happy twenty.' Mindy gasped and looked down, surprised to find her boyfriend awake with her. She wasn't the only one who was having bad memories. Giggling, Mindy poked Markus' ribs and rolled onto her other side, facing him through the darkness that covered the room. 'It's twenteen. I'm twenteen now, hunny bunny.' 'Twenteen?' Soon, it was Markus' turn to start laughing. Upon noticing that Mindy was still poking him, he began prodding back before he eventually rolled over her and pinned her hands down. 'Just can't let the whole innocent vibe go, can you?' 'Nope.' Before Markus could think about how to respond to that, Mindy leaned up as much as she could and planted a kiss on Markus' lips. Markus felt as though he should say something, but finally let it fall by the wayside to continue the kiss. By the time they did break apart, however, Mindy did her own explaining. 'But it's time I let you help make things better.' They kissed again. 'I love you, sugar cookie.' 'I love you, too, hunny bunny.' Mindy was officially a whole year older, and having that fact to wake up to the next morning, she would hope to gain a year's worth of maturity. Sure, however, Mindy would have to do that growing on her own; she found it convenient that she would find herself challenging Train for the Global World Championship that week, in what was without a doubt set up to be Mindy's most important match of her short career to date. Mindy had no idea what was going to happen, but she knew that come Saturday, it would be her top priority to win. No longer was she a child, but she was responsible for herself, just as her cousin Michael was responsible for himself. And whatever he did that day in her old home, it would come with consequence to him. All Mindy needed to think about was happy thoughts- Markus. The Global World Title. And most important of all: a new life. Because she was twenteen, and responsible twenteen year olds were one of a kind to say the least.
