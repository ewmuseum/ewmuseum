Date: 12/5/04 THE WRESTLING INSIDER 'Under Zenith?' by Tom Wells Hype, the four-letter word that has changed the very foundation of the wrestling industry. For the most part the 20th century, the wrestling industry was a fan-driven industry. Impromptu matches and special unexpected appearances is what kept the fans coming back. Most fans came to shows expecting the unexpected, and walked away in shock and a smile. Then Vincent Kennedy McMahon ushered in a new era of wrestling known as 'sports-entertainment.' The wrestling industry began a profit-driven adventure, while the fan-driven industry was forced wayside by the sheer power weilded by the conglomerate known only as WWE. The surprise and shock that made wrestling great, was replaced with heavily anticipated debuts, and commercial marketing along side sales-driven pre-planned, not to mention heavily advertised, 'special appearances.' Together, they became known as hype. Hype has been known to make or break the careers of ultra-talented superstars whom either thrive or fold underneathe the big arena spotlight. Kurt Angle, The Rock, Randy Orton, and even Brock Lesnar's short stint were successful in backing up the huge hype that WWE put behind their arrivals. While on the other hand... Sean O'Haire, Nathan Jones, and Mordecai just couldn't fill the shoes they were given upon arrival. That brings us to our next case, which takes us to Texas... the home of Global Wrestling and one of their newest rookies, Christian Zenith. Much in the same way Brock Lesnar, Nathan Jones, and Kurt Angle did... Christian Zenith made his hype with his persona. A millionaire from Chicago, Illinois with somewhat of a criminal background. He was tried for the murders of several people... under the name Die Entremaschine, which is a German word meaning 'The Reaper.' After Christian Zenith was found innocent, another man stepped forward claiming to be Die Entremaschine, thus taking Christian's head off the chopping block. Many say that there is nothing money can't buy in Christian's case, including an innocent verdict in his trial. It was common knowledge Christian Zenith was a huge name in the underground scene in Chicago, whether the man who stepped forward was the real murderer is still debated today. Christian Zenith, with extensive training in Vale Tudo and an IQ of 114, made his Global Wrestling debut at the Maximum Carnage PPV, where he respresented his home country of Germany in the main event. Zenith's main event debut fell short at the hands of Venneman Pendragon though, and Zenith was handed his first taste of defeat. In his second match, he made quick work of another up and coming star named John Winters. But from there, the only highlight in Zenith's short GW career was winning the TV Title from Venneman Pendragon, and then losing it back a week later. A series of defeats has show that Christian Zenith of GW might be the next casuality of the 'hype era' of wrestling. This is just another lesson to all the up-and-coming superstars up there, don't let the promoters make you bite off more than you can chew, or your career may be permanately associated with failure, like Christian Zenith is surely about to become.
