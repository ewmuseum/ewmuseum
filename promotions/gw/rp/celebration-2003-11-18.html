Celebration

18 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-18-03 The air in the Dark Horse Saloon was thick with excitement. As usual, people were dancing and drinking and having a good time. Kid Rock’s “Feel Like Makin” Love? was playing on the jukebox. Three men, in particular, were soaking in the ambiance. Three men who often mapped out battle plans and drank over discussions about life. These three men were Brick, Jesse Briggs and Ely Braxton. Brick had a glass of ginger ale, Jesse had a beer and Ely had his usual Texas mudslide. More often than not, these men came to the Dark Horse to ease their minds or to let off steam after a disheartening setback. Tonight, they were here to celebrate. After two weeks of the Upper Echelon getting the better of them, they finally got to leave TUE laying unconscious while they raised their arms in victory. This was due to the arrival of one Jesse Jamester, who had earlier in the evening been cheated out of the Primestar title by Sean Price. “Gentlemen, to Jesse Jamester!” Brick exclaimed, raising his glass. “To Jamester!” Jesse and Ely cried in unison. “And to finally getting TUE!!” “Hell yeah!!” “Say, where is Jamester, anyway” He ought to be here celebratin? with the rest of us.” “I invited him to come along, but he told me he had to take care of some business.” “Ah, well, at least he came when he did.” “And I’ll be damned if it didn’t feel good. You can’t imagine how good I felt spearing Deacon’s sorry ass to the ramp and then giving Zero that Bricklayer right on the ramp.” “Well, not half as good as Jamester must o” felt layin? Price out like he did.” “Yeah, by the way, you sure Akodo Rokku ain’t pissed with us for bringing Jamester onto our team” I mean, he was on Team Lion.” “Yeah, well, J.J. Jarone was supposed to be part of our team and I didn’t get pissed when he joined Team Lion. Besides, I’m pretty damn sure he’s got other stuff to worry about.” “You mean what’s goin” on with Ice an? his lady Yoshiko?” “I knew Ice was a sick motherfucker, but for him to do the shit he’s doing to her?” Just then, Brick noticed Jesse and Ely staring at him. “What?” “Well, some o” the things he’s doin?, you did to Christina back when she was datin? Dane Rennier.” “Well, that was different. Rennier left me for dead and she was involved. Besides, she and I have put that behind us. Besides, I never kept her for long, not like Ice is doing. And Yoshiko ain’t got nothin” to do with it.” “Well, she is Akodo’s lady,” said Jesse, “an” Akodo did kill Ice an? Star’s baby.” “Well, that was an accident.” “I don’t think Ice cares,” added Ely. “Yeah, well, somebody might have to set his ass straight and if Akodo doesn’t do it, well then, it might just have to be up to me.” “This like when you thought Terminator needed his ass kicked?” “Yep, and he did. Just like Ice needs his ass kicked.” “Well,” said Ely, rising from his seat, “I better be headin” out. Christina might need me.” “Mm-hmm.” “I better head out, too.” “Yeah, I got a recording session in a few days, so I’d get back to new York. Later.” “Later.”
