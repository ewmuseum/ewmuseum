Streetz are Watching Pt.II

3 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Streetz are Watching Pt.II J. C. Diamond Global Wrestling Promo #1086 Date: 4/3/04 Location: Streetz of NYC [The screen is pitch black. All of a sudden a loud boom is heard and a still frame of a teen getting punched across the jaw is shown as the chatter of onlookers is heard in the background at the fenced in basketball court that we saw earlier on. Another loud boom is heard as a still frame flashes up on the screen and the young teen JC Diamond is being held by two other teens as he is being punched in the face. The boom is heard again as a still frame of a teen being slammed on the concrete face first is seen. Another still flashes up on the screen of JC slamming one of the teens face first into the fence. Another boom is heard as a new still flashes up on the screen of Vince Jones being speared onto the concrete of the basketball court. One last still flashes up on the screen that shows JC on top of one of the other teens pounding him into the ground in fury. The still slowly fades out. The camera slowly fades back into the fenced in court in New York City during the spring, the same court we saw the young JC at earlier on. The camera slowly pans around the scene on the bright and sunny spring day and catches sight of the large, black thug leader named K-9 and his fellow gang members all helping up a battered, bruised, and beaten young version of JC who has a bloody lip and spits out a bit of blood on the concrete. He grabs his side in pain and looks down at the other teens that were looking to gain a spot in the gang in disgust. A few of them stir and try to continue on with the fight that obviously just ended but they are too much in pain to put up any more of a fight at all. One of the teens rolls over and looks up at JC in fury.] Teen: You…son of a b*tch! [JC hears this and breaks away from the grasp of K-9 and kicks the teen in the side in fury. The teen winces in pain and grabs his side. JC turns away and K-9 and the other gang members begin patting him on the back and look at him with satisfied eyes. K-9 laughs to himself and looks at all the rest of the teens that are laid out on the concrete groaning in pain.] K-9: Well, it looks like we got ourselves the newest member of the clique here. Now what did the Big Dawg tell all you b*tches before? Only the best of the best makes it in the EDW. JC showed that he had what it takes and he’s in. Now all the rest of you cats get up and roll the hell on! [All the teens continue rolling on the ground in pain and agony after the free-for-all they had to try and get in the gang. K-9 watches them all in fury as they struggle to their feet and try to leave. He turns to his gang members and begins barking orders.] K-9: Hey guys! These fools ain’t movin’ fast enough for me here! They stinkin’ up my court! Get rid of ‘em while I talk a lil with V.J! [The gang members nod and they begin yelling, rounding up the teens, and begin beating them mercilessly as they force them out through the gate of the fence and toss them out. K-9 laughs to himself and looks down at JC.] K-9: You took care of this sh*t like a champ, JD. So, how does it feel to be apart of the clique? [JC clutches his side in pain and looks up at K-9.] JC: It feels…damn good! Now when the hell we gonna be bustin’ some heads or somethin’? [K-9 shakes his head and laughs outloud.] K-9: You’s a headbusta, huh? Well, don’t worry kid. You wanna bust some heads you gonna get your chance to bust some heads. That ain’t a problem. Man, when I first saw you I thought there was somethin’ special about you. There’s somethin’ different about you, J.C. You keep up the good work and you’re gonna go a long, long ways, kid. Just think about it like this. Now that you down, things are gonna start changin’. You gonna be seein’ this city in a whole new way here. You gonna be a king of these f*ckin’ streets. Nah mean? Look around. We run all this sh*t. We… [K-9 pauses and notices that JC’ attention has wavered and he looks down at JC in fury.] K-9: Hey! Look at me when I’m talkin’ to you! What the hell you starin’ at anyways, huh!? [K-9 turns around and he spots a beautiful young black girl with long brown hair that’s standing at the fence peering in and looking right at JC. K-9 nods and cracks a smile and looks back at JC once again.] K-9: Oh, looks like JC spotted’ some candy and the candy got some eyes for him. Sh*t man, you betta holla at that playa before it gets away. Gotta catch ‘em while you can around here in NYC, son. [JC shakes his head no.] JC: Nah man. I’m tryin’ to do my thang with the clique right now. F*ck that ho! She ain’t important! [K-9 shakes his head and laughs to himself.] K-9: Man, get the hell over there before I have to whup your b*tch ass and embarrass ya in front of the chick! JC: Yeah, yeah, yeah. I got this. I got this, mane. It’s all good. I’ll catch ya lata, Big Dawg. K-9: Peace. [JC turns and walks towards the fence where the girl is standing and she looks at him half in amusement and half in shyness. JC reaches the fence and she quickly turns and trots away. JC watches her in confusion as she runs off down the street giggling.] JC: What the hell was that all about? Damn, trick! [K-9 shakes his head and laughs to himself.] K-9: Told ya so. Next time you know to listen. The Big Dawg knows what’s up, J…J…J… [All of a sudden JC snaps back to reality and awakens from his daydream. He is seen leaning against the fence that we last saw him leaning against earlier on in the dark of night on a lonely street in New York City. He has his arms folded and he turns around and catches sight of a black, stretch limo that is sitting in front of him in the street with the passenger door held open by the chauffeur. Daisy is seen sitting inside of the limo looking directly at him beckoning him to get inside.] Daisy: J, come on. Hop inside. [JC looks on at her confusion as he approaches the limo.] JC: What the hell are you doin’ here’? This ain’t our limo! Where the hell’d you get this whip from? Daisy: Well, I…I kinda had a talk with Reno Banks. And he sent this limo by the pad to pick me up so I could find you. He really wants to talk some business with us, J. [JC slams his hand against the limo in fury.] JC: You what!??? Daisy: Yeah. I called him up and we had a little discussion about you; but it was for the good of your career. You’ve been slippin’ out to this spot every night now. [JC looks at her in rage and just shakes his head.] JC: What the hell were you thinkin’, huh!?? You know he and JC haven’t been seein’ eye-to-eye since the sh*t that went down a while back. You ain’t plottin’ sh*t, are you? [JC glares at her and shakes her head no very quickly.] Daisy: No! No! That’s not the case at all, J! JC: Then what the hell is it, huh!? We don’t do business with Reno Banks! You know that!! We talked this sh*t over time and time again! [Daisy nods in agreement and looks at JC rather uneasily as a result of his rage.] Daisy: I know, I know; but just hop inside and I’ll explain myself to you on the way. You need support. You need a plan. JC: Plan? Oh, J. Diamond got plans. Don’t worry ‘bout that. You remember what happened at the latest Overload? Well, JC think it about time we get goin’ with round two since it seems like the message didn’t get across the first time around to these GW cats! Daisy: I know, I know, but I really think we should give Banks another look, J. Come on. Let’s go. Everything will be explained along the way. [JC lets out a loud grunt of anger and hops inside the limo and folds his arms across his chest. The chauffeur closes the door, makes his way around the limo, hops inside, and quickly drives off down the road as the camera fades to black.]
