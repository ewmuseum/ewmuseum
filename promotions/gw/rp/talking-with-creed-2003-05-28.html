Talking with Creed

28 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/28/03 “Any change, Doctor?” Logan Creed asks over the phone. “Nothing yet. So, he’s still stable” Okay, well, call me if there’s any change.” Creed has been worried for months on end now. It’s been two months since Bubba McCoy fell into a coma. Since then he has taken over as acting President, helping to keep order in Global Wrestling and to keep Salaok and the Nevermore in line. Suddenly, a sound stirs his attention. It’s the sound of Brick bursting into his office, pissed off. “You know what that son of a bitch did”!!” Brick roars. “I’m so sorry, Mr. Creed. I can call security.” “That’s okay, Roberta.” As Roberta closes the door behind, Brick continues to fume and breathe heavily. “You’re pissed about something, that’s a given.” “He actually sent front row tickets to my friends and family!” “Salaok?” “Who do you think” You know, this shouldn’t surprise me! He’s a punk bitch who can’t fight his own fights! They all are! Him, Quartz, Kullypso, Scarecrow, Sabbot! Sabbot wouldn’t have even gotten this far if it wasn’t for the Nevermore! He got the Cruiserweight title because of the Nevermore! He kept the Cruiserweight title because of the Nevermore! He got the International title because of the Nevermore! And now?” “Look, calm down, Brick! I know you’re pissed, but you gotta think! This is just what Salaok and Sabbot want! They want you to be pissed off! They want you to lose focus! They want you to lose this match!” “And that’s another thing,” says Brick. “What the hell is the idea of you putting these stipulations on the match” Making it a Cell Match with the winner being crowned only through submission?” “Yeah, and?” “And” Creed, you know I never finish a match with making cats tap out? I knock them out with the Bricklayer?” “Well, you do know some submission holds, don’t you?” “Yeah, I know some submission holds?” “Well, I made this match a submissions match to show what you’re capable of. To show that you’re more than some trashcan swinging homeless bum, like Camero called ya.” Brick remembers that night. He remembers Ely Braxton having to hold him back to keep him from going back out into the arena and beating the hell out of Camero. “Besides, instead of just beating on a member of the Nevermore, you get to make one squeal like a pig.” “Hold up, man. I ain’t into that “Deliverance” shit. But making somebody in the Nevermore scream in agony? Making one of them tap out? The more I think about that idea, the more I like it.” “I thought you would.” “Still, there’s my family to consider.” “I’ll have security beefed up around them. Your family’s gonna be fine.” “I hope so. And Salaok better hope so too. Because I ain’t the only one between the two of us who has family. He might want to ask himself if he can really afford to have Lisha go missing again.” “Brick, don’t even think about it.” “Fine, but he’d just better not forget who he’s dealing with.” “In case he does, you’ll remind him at Dead Zone.” “Damn right.”
