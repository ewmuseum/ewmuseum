Fairy Tales Can Come True

3 June 2004

Date: 06.03.04 FAIRY TALES CAN COME TRUE Ken stirred in his bed a touch at the sound of what seemed to be footsteps in his New York City penthouse apartment. He rolled around on his oversized waterbed to look out of the large window that served as the wall to his twenty-fifth story home, only to see that a storm had moved in and the rain had begun to fall. A flash of lightning illuminated the room and, while only for a second, Ken could have sworn that there was someone else there. The outline of their body lurked in the dark as one does in the horror movies of the past and present. With another flash of the electrical energy, Ken's suspisions ere proved correct as a young woman stood in his doorway. Ken sat, staring, awaiting his eyes adjustment, and when they finally did, he couldn't believe them. He quickly reached to the lamp on his left bedside table and turned it on. The light gave a dull glow to the room and revealed the beautiful face of Gunner Adisson. Soaked from head to toe and dripping wet, KPJ couldn't help but notice how extrordinarily radiant she was, even with her mascara running from the tears that swelled in her eyes. He quickly shot up out of bed and rushed over to hold her. He held her in his arms, warming her shivering body with his as she cried on his shoulder. 'I -- I can't believe it's you,' Ken exclaimed as he stroked the hair on the back of her head. 'I can't beieve you're here.' 'It's been to long,' she managed to work through the sobs. 'It's been way to long and I miss you so much. I made a mistake... I should never have left.' Ken pulled her away from him and looked into her gorgeaous green eyes. She really did get prettier every day. Even now, standing soaking wet in a white untucked button up, which her white spaghetti strap undershirt was showing through due to the rain, and a pair of blue jeans, she really was the most amazing thing that he'd ever seen. She was so amazing in fact, that Ken wasn't even thinking about the imported Italian carpet that she was drenching with water. 'I made the mistake, Gun,' Ken began. 'I was the one who was never there enough, and I swear to God if I could od it over again I would treat you how you deserve to be treated.' Her lips began to tremble as half of a smile etched across her face. 'Why can't we,' she asked. 'Why can't we do it over?' With that phrase, she put both her hand on Ken's face and pulled herself up for what was the most spectacular kiss that either had ever experienced in their lives. It seemed that all things on the planet earth had stopped and the rest of the universe to it's lead to soon follow, and for that one point in time... it was just the two of them there... in that moment. She pulled away slowly, both with butterflies in their stomachs and fireworks in their heads. 'I have some thing to tell,' Gunner started before she was cut off. 'No,' rebutted Ken, 'I do. Gunner there's something that I've regretting never telling you to this day, and if another goes by with me letting out, I may well die.' Ken released Gunner and took her by the hand before continuing on. 'Gun,' Ken began, extremly nervous as to the proceeds that were placed before him, 'I love you.' It was as if a ton had been taken off of his chest and now he could say everything that he had ever wanted to. 'I've loved you since the first time I saw you. You... Gunner... you are the most remarkable things that's ever happened to me and I would gladly give up everything just to have you back. I want you to know that you... you mean more than any of those things to me, Gun... and... and... well... I love you.' Gunner stood staring at Ken with a smile on her face and fresh tears in her eyes. 'Ken... I lo----' 05.47 AM 'Dammit,' Ken shouted into his apartment's early morning air. This was the eigth time in as many mornings that he'd woken up from that dream. However, this time, just like the last had... and the one before that... and so on and so forth, had felt more real than any of the others. He missed Gunner, he made no bones about that. Nor did he hide it, as both Josh and Ice knew that he'd nothing but long for her since the two had parted ways. A smell was filling the room now... Orange Pekot tea. That was Gunner's favorite for so long after he introduced her to it. Ken's eyes widened as he thought of this and he darted into the kitched, praying that by some work of God, she would be standing there, nursing a tea bag in one of his glass tea mugs. All he found was loneliness. Loneliness and his automatic coffee-maker which he'd forgotten that he'd set to brew the tea at 05.30 this morning. He shrugged his shoulders and took in a deep, sorrowful sigh before reaching up into the cabinet in front of him and pulling out one of the aforementioned mugs. Usually, the aroma of the tea as he poured it gave him the gradual awakening that he was fond of. However, on this morning he was already wide awake with the memories he'd retained of himself and Gunner running back and forth in his mind. He looked around his large apartment which many said was perfectly designed with a mix of traditional and modern designs, and he took a drink of his tea. He had so much. So many expensive things graced his apartment... yet it still felt so... empty. He knew why... and he also knew that there was no way to fill that void that was resting inside of him. So he did the same thing that he always did. He finished his tea while reading the copy of the Wall Street Journal that was delivered to his door every morning, hopped in the shower, brushed his teeth, and ran out the door and down the stairs to his vintage '67 Corvetter 427 Roadster, which would, in turn, take him to the gym to begin his training for the day. If he couldn't get Gunner Adisson out of his head, he was going to preoccupy himself in the meantime.
