Quick Job/Fallen Memories

8 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/8/03 Old memories come back to people in many ways. Certain songs hit the brain in such a way it’ll spark the old times. Or even movie lines for catch phrases you and your friends laughed about for hours on end. They can even hit when they are walking down a random sidewalk and come out of nowhere. In most ways they are welcomed openly for the good times and resent for they can’t be relived. Other ways, you’d rather keep them out of your head. Prefer to forget them and never speak again of it. Unfortunately, those are the memories that seem to stick with you the most. They are easier to remember for some reason. To taunt you when you least expect them. Never leave you alone... Johnie Ringo hears the clicking of people walking around the campus of the University of Tulsa, Oklahoma. The walkways aren’t as crowded as they usually are during school hours, but it is a weekend. He looks up and finds himself surrounded by several very large, beautiful white buildings reaching for the skyline in magnificent fashion. Cracking his neck as he tilts it back forward and heads towards a large cathedral type building at the end of the walk as it branches out towards it. Scattered students are walking in and out of the structure. Johnie grabs the arm of a man with his attention to the stack of books in his right arm. As Johnie twists him around, the books fly out and scatter over the concrete. Instantly the man raises his arms in the air in retreat. “AH!! What do you want?” Johnie can see the fear in his eyes, despite the thick lenses and dark, messy hair hiding them. “I don’t have any money, so there’s no use mugging me.” Calmly, Johnie tries to explain his situation. “I’m not trying to mug...” “HELP! HELP!! HE’S MUGGING ME!!” No such luck. But as he attempts to get away, Johnie tightens the grip on the boy’s arm which drives him to his knees. In doing so, the passer-bys turn into witnesses of an assault of a fellow student. Johnie looks around and grimaces back into the boy’s face. “I’m not mugging you, jackass!” So much for pleasantries. “I want to ask a question. I’m looking for someone.” Seeing promise as the boy looks up to him with sadly, Johnie loosens his grip and helps him back to his feet. His lip quivers as he stares back into Johnie’s dark sunglasses. “Wh-wha-what do you want then?” Moron. Johnie sighs heavily as he lets go of the boy’s arm so he can pick up his scattered books. “I told you,” he moans contemptuously. “I’m looking for someone. He was said to be around here today.” “Wh-wh-who are you looking for?” He’s still shaken up, but not as bad as before. At least he has the mugging thing out his head...maybe. “Luther Treadwell. He’s about 6 foot, blonde hair, moderate build.” Trying the a business tone, Johnie leans against the stone wall. “You seen him?” “Ye-ye-yeah.” the boy stutters as he stacks his last book and wraps his arm around it. “He’s up in the computer lab. About 3 rows back.” That’s all he needed. “Cool.” Johnie turns and heads to the glass doors, but gets stopped by the boy yelling back at him. “Hey!” Looking down at the ground, the boy stutters again. “Wh-wha-wha-what” “T-T-T-TODAY JUNIOR!” Johnie’s patience is reaching its leash. “Wh-wha-what do you want to see him for?” At this question, Johnie simply scoffs and turns away from the boy as he throws the glass door open and heads up the stairs towards the computer lab. “Fuckin dork.” As he heads up, that dork looks back up at him in wonder as to what just happened. Shaking his head, he continues on his merry way. Looking back down at his stack of books he stops... “Where did...”” ************************************************************************ You never know how easy it is to get into school facilities until you try it. Johnie headed straight for the lab and blended right in with everyone else. I guess its easy to mix in when you look just like everyone else. Just another student on his way to check e-mail and whatever. Johnie peeks through the thin rectangular glass window in the door and sees his target. Its easy to pick a target when he’s pretty obvious. He fit’s the profile perfectly. Spiked blonde hair, sunken brown eyes, and one of those ass-chins. Easy to pick out. Johnie slides through the door carrying a thin red book labeled “The Deeper Reaches of the Mind” in his left hand. He guns right toward Luther, pulling back the chair right next to him. Leaning back, staring at the screen, he doesn’t even notice Johnie yet. Until Johnie leans over in his direction. “Psst! Luther...” Luther stops typing at reluctantly looks in Johnie’s direction. “What?” “Hey! What’s up, man” I have a question for ya and I need some help.” Might as well play the fool. It usually works. Johnie glances over at the book covered in starts above Luther’s mouse and continues in a low voice. “You might not know me, but I sit behind you in Astrology and I’m having troubles understanding stuff for the test coming up. I was hoping you could help me.” Luther looks back at Johnie with a surprised brow and mutters back. “What test”! I didn’t know we had a test coming.” Oops. “Well, it’s a surprise exam,” Nice cover. Pfft. “And I was, uh, talking to the professor between classes the other day and I kinda got it out of him. Its on Monday.” “AW SHIT!” he lets out as a low roar. “What’s it on” We just had a test last week!” “Well, uh, it’s a remake. Everybody fucked up on the last one that he wants a redo.” Luther rubs his neck rapidly in the fear of failing another test. He’s buying it! “Come on. Let’s go out into the hall and talk about it. I hate bugging other people in here.” Good line. If the lab was more than a quarter full. But it works none the less. Luther gets up in a cluttered rush with Johnie and they head towards the door. As they get into the hallway, Johnie leads them off to a less traffic area off to the left and stops. Turning around to face Luther, he talks normally. “Ok. You ready to talk?” “Yeah.” Dummy! With that Johnie slams Luther’s face directly into the plaster wall. Instantly hearing the crunch, Luther grabs for his nose and opens his eyes widely. Continuing, Johnie jabs him in the side of the ribcage and slams his elbow into his jaw. Luther stumbles backwards covering his face and trips over into the hallway beside the lab. Johnie pulls him to his feet and thrusts into his abs and using Luther’s reaction, he shoves him through the hip-high window that separates the lab with the hallway. Lying half-conscious on the ground, Luther gags hard as blood gushes from his mouth and nostrils. Johnie leans on the window casing and looks down on him. “Next time, try to be on time with your shit. My employer said that you were overdue.” He shoves off the frame and starts walking towards the stairwell as he yells back at Luther. “By the way, good luck on that test!” As Johnie hit’s the pavement, he looks out around the campus and smiles. Until he catches a glimpse of something in the distance. That smile turns to a hard frown too quickly as he storms off into that direction. ********************************************************************* Skelly Stadium. The venue for the football legends of the Tulsa alumni. Where the ghosts of the gridiron still haunt and scrimmage in hopes of immortal glory. Screams and cheers still echo from the game played just a few hours prior to Johnie’s arrival. If only... It’s the only thought rolling through Johnie’s head. The “What if” questions are hard ones to answer when you have no idea what could have been. As Johnie sits on the bench, he stares out on the torn sod before him. Rolling his eyes over each line one by one. Then suddenly, a uniformed player leaps into the air and snatches the football from the air. Upon landing, he runs routes to stop at a point to turn around. Johnie looks again and the field is covered in gold and white uniforms. Each of them doing pulling drills at their spots. One right after another. Slowly a smile returns to his face. “That could be you in a few years, son.” A voice echoes from Johnie’s left as he spins his head to its origin. There he sees Hurricane head coach, Steve Kragthorpe decked out in his practice apparel. His balding hair sticking in all directions, but he still holds the fire in his eyes. “Just imagine, you busting through the lines as you head for the end zone looking for glory. All of it could be yours.” Johnie looks straight at the running back position as the offense lines up for a dry run. Seeing the mock lineup and snap, he can feel the ball being swept up into his waiting hands as he rushes out towards midfield before turning around to do it again. “Its gonna happen for you Johnie,” Coach continues. “Trust me. Your scholarship’s gonna be waiting for you when you graduate next year.” He walks over to Johnie and slaps his hands around his shoulders, grasping hard. “Its gonna be waiting for you, and we’re gonna be waiting for you. Trust me...” A scowling smile peaks across Kragthorpe’s face as he chuckles to Johnie. Those words echo into his ears over and over and over again. They never came true. And just as the scholarship faded, so did the vision. So did the staduim. All of it sunk away from him. ******************************************************************* “HEY!” The voice yells out in front of him, causing Johnie to snap his head up from the wooden counter. “You want another?” The bartender stares back into Johnie’s sunken eyes in disapproval. “Well?” Johnie just looks back at him drowsily with his head half sagging towards the bar. Still he musters enough stability to answer back solemnly. “Sure.” The bartender turns around to the ice chest behind him and pulls out a dripping Bud Light. A hiss swishes though the air as he tilts the beer to the bottle opener. As he slaps the beer down before him, Johnie pulls a couple singles and hands them over to him. “Thanks.” Its no Corona, but it’ll have to do. The bartender returns the greeting with a stifled smile and heads back to the opposite side of the bar, wiping his hands with the white towel over his shoulder. As the bartender heads over to the opposite direction, Johnie lifts the bottle up to his face, he chuckles to himself. Amazing what a fake ID can still do for you. As Johnie throws back a swig, he looks around and quickly loses his grin again. Sees the plain wood walls and thinks sadly to himself. Is this what I have left? TO BE CONTINUED...
