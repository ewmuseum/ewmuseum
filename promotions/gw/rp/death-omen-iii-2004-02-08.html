Death Omen III

8 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 08, 2004 Prologue: In the spirit of battle the man known as Jamester lost to Rokku. What intensions he had of winning were thrown out the window after a vicious fist nailed him in the face. However now, a new dawn is approaching and the man known as the Jackal who reigns from Calgary, Alberta, Canada has come back and has something to say. As the PPV went off air the night afterwards, a story so unknown yet so wanting to be uncovered was. The truth of what really was behind the man Jamester after his death… Story Continues: The lights of the arena began to slowly fade away. As the seats were now nearly empty, and the ring was being fixed, the Jackal sat in the stands pondering with a white towel over his long hair letting only the wet tips show beneath. Jesse had given a horrible performance at Global Reload, and he himself knew it. From the bottom of his heart he knew he’d never done so bad in his life like he had there in the squared circle on that given night. Whispering under the towel words could be heard, to whom they were directed no one knew, but Jesse spoke, and he was there to listen as well. ’I don’t know where I went wrong… I’d faked my own death, I’d lied about myself and family, and I’d changed my appearance to be this monster only to get a shot at the World Title. What did I do wrong? I should have won. It must have been my heart. I had the wrong intentions, and in my heart I could feel it leading up to yesterday night. Not every time you wrestle do you win, and last night I’d felt no feeling but failure in the black heart that had taken over me. But that can’t be it… I know I’m better than Rokku, I just know it!’ Standing up he pulls the towel off his head and drops it on the floor revealing his face. The once pale look is now a peeling of paint and makeup mixed to form a mask of shades for the skin. His hair and goatee are also back to normal, both being brown as they were originally, and his arms and body now back to a tanner state, Jesse is once again looking normal. He walks out of the arena doors only looking back to see the ring being covered as the staff shut the lights off. *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** ’It could be any day, or any night… I could take that pressure, that guilt, and turn it into fuel. That fuel would take me from the dark places of my life and send me into the world of light. It’s always easy to wash away your problems… I did it for seven years of my life, and now, I’m feeling the effects. I’m feeling the consequences of my once desired love, alcohol. I could never have lasted through those tough times with my wife’s death without them, it’s just too bad that I can’t go back and change it all…’ Easing out of the chair he sits in, he walks slowly as if in no hurry, goes to the kitchen and gets a bottle of Jack Daniels. He comes back to the living room only to sit back down where he sat before, and gaze at the bottle of alcohol. ’Who’d think that this was anything more than a beverage? It’s just water with some mixed fruits and sugar or something like that… It’s so unknown to those drunks that walk the streets that this right here is the only reason I had a bad life. If only they knew what I knew, then I’d be able to change everything I’ve ever done…’ Repeating Jesse just sits there talking to himself, in a state of deep depression, he has yet to see the light outside for nearly a week, and his body reeks of odor. Jesse is being eaten away from the inside out. As he goes to open the Jack Daniels, the door bell rings, possibly the saving point of what may lead Jesse into the dark places he once was at. Hiding the bottle he stands up and struts the door quickly, turning the brass knob and opening the oak door to reveal a woman on the other side of his screen door. Long blonde hair with brown streaks from her natural color blending in, and her eyes a misty green. Her chin pointy a bit, letting her face be shaped into somewhat a heart. It’s as if Jesse is looking into a photo album, for the woman he sees has struck him in the heart before he even asked her name. ’Juli… Julie?’ he stuttered; yet the woman did not answer to the name. She turned to him and smiled before speaking, ’Hello, my name’s Charlotte and my car broke down just outside down the block. I walked hear since no one is answering their doors down this whole street. Do you think you can give me some help…’ Jesse’s face a stunned complexion, for he’s lost in a memory of his wife. Shaking the cob webs lose he finally answers with a yes and grabs his coat before walking out to help her. *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** Later that night Jesse is parked upon a hill with a beautiful evening sky perched above him. In the passenger seat is the woman he’d met earlier. The two seem to be in a great mood and have gotten to know one another. Jesse kisses her on the cheek, and then glances over at the window looking outside. ’When I first saw you Charlotte, I’d been reminded of the woman I’d married some years ago by the name of Julie. She’d been my pride and joy for a good five years, before being murdered… However, your face has revived me of that past experience and I live here now thinking of her sitting beside me in your body… Doesn’t that sound weird?’ Charlotte smiles as she says, ’Yes it is… But love is nothing to be forgotten or misunderstood… Maybe it’s just because she didn’t want you to be alone anymore, so she sent me to you… Whatever it is, I know I could be happy with you for the rest of my life. You’re my soul mate Jesse, and I love you.’ ’That’s so strange because Julie said that to me the day she died… and- Jesse stops to look at his mirror, but as he does everything changes. The sky changes from beautiful to disaster in mere seconds, as it appears to be a dream that was so ripe and happy that had just been crushed with the massive fist of steel. Jesse turns around to look at Charlotte, but she is no more alive, yet has a cut across her neck and is covered in the blood that has poured from that wound. Panicking Jesse tries to open the door of the car but can’t, it’s jammed. Looking back at Charlotte his eyes begin to swell with tears and he begins to break down. He cannot imagine having to climb over her body when he once was kissing her in that very seat and thought he’d found true love. Trying to get out of the car again he elbows the window to try and break it, but it will not budge. The car begins to coast towards the edge of the hill. Jesse slams his fists off the dashboard and screams “HELP!” but no one can hear him. He slowly closes his eyes as the car goes over the edge of the hill rolling off an edge and dropping into a lake of large mass. The car begins to fill with water, and Jesse slowly drowns away… ’Doctor… We’ve got something! He’s back with us!’ states a voice above the fallen body of Jesse in a hospital room. Jesse can barely move with his body being connected with tubes from every thing around him and his head being in a holder to keep it from moving. Jesse just lies there as the doctors do their work. *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** A day after what seemed to be death for Jesse, he is now able to speak, yet is still hurt and in pain. ’Mr. Fristik, you wished to speak with me?’ stated the doctor as he entered the room with his white long trench coat like shirt on, and a pair of glasses that slid to the tip of his nose. He peered through them just barely looking at Jesse waiting for an answer. ’Yes… (Gulps for air as his throat is sore) I wanted to ask you what happened… to the woman I was with?’ The doctor looks unsure of what Jesse is saying. He looks at him closely, and then responds, ’What woman? We found you Jesse in your house, with a 6.0 alcohol level; you’d passed out because of the alcohol and were revived twice in the hospital. If it wasn’t for your cousin Julius, you’d be dead… But a woman, their was not another person anywhere in the house when the paramedics found you. Maybe it was a dream you had.’ ’Thank you doctor… that’s all I wanted to know.’ The doctor leaves Jesse alone and that is when Jesse closes his eyes and repeats the words My eyes stray to awake this morning, it's like a sickness without you, by my side you sit but you are nowhere to be found. Your eyes approach me, I glance up and we see eye to eye, I know your the one, I hope you feel the same. Your laughter a breath of fresh air, Your hair is like an angels wings so bright and live, Your voice a whisper of wind as I admire you, I cry in joy letting tears run down my face, Are you my true love? ’I will always remember that poem that I wrote for you dear. I know you love me Julie… But I have to move on, I will always love you in my heart, and when I wrestle it is for you. As it will be when I face Ice for my title this Monday, I will fight for you Julie, but you have to let me live my life with peace, or I’ll end up giving up on those dreams you helped me pursue. Please just let me go.’ With that said Jesse closes his eyes, with tears glistening form the corner of his eyes, and he goes to sleep with a bliss smile on his face and the tears of joy he cried for her still on his face.
