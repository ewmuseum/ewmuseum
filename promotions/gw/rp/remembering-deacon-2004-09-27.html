Remembering Deacon

27 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/27/04 For the past year or so, Deacon Holmes had been the biggest thorn in Brick's side. The intensity of Brick's feud with the young superstar had often reached many heights, including the recent animosity involving Deacon's girlfriend Rhaiza. Now Deacon was gone, indefinitely suspended. 'So, Deacon's finally out of your hair, huh?' 'Looks like it,' Brick replied, with a smile. ---- It all began with an interview on GW Overload in June of 2003. Deacon had just come into the company and was a promising young superstar. Many had compared him to Brick. When asked about his views on the superstar, his response was less than flattering. One night, on the Evolution before Whiplash, Holmes had a match with Roz Blayze. Brick had come out before the match to sit at ringside with the commentators in order to get a good look at youngster. Much of the match, Deacon spent showing off, almost as if he were challenging Brick. Brick remained cool throughout the whole thing. Then, sometime before the match ended, the Nevermore swarmed the ring and attacked the Brick took it upon himself to drive the Nevermore, cleaning house with a steel chair. After the Nevermore retreated, Deacon got in Brick's face, causing an argument which escalated into a shoving match. When Brick went to leave the ring, Deacon slapped him in the back of his head, further provoking Brick's wrath Then came July. Brick had gone into June, finally winning the World championship after four long years of hard work. The Evolution after Whiplash, he had gone down to the ring to be recognized for his accomplishment. Who should then come out to disrupt the celebration but Deacon Holmes. Rather than resort to violence, Brick told Deacon that he was glad Deacon decided to show up. Brick referenced the incident at the end of Whiplash where Deacon soiled himself. Brick offered him a present to cheer him up. The gift turned out to be a pack of diapers. In September, Deacon had pulled an elaborate prank on Brick which involved knocking him unconscious, dressing him up in drag and having him dropped off at an alternative lifesty. But Brick would get his revenge the following week: then-acting president Christina McCoy appointed him as a special guest referee in a match where Deacon would defend the two titles he held at the time in a triple threat, two falls match. The first fall would dtermine the Primestar champion. The second would determine the Platinum champion. Thanks to Brick, Deacon lost both titles. That resulted in a Streetfight for the World title match being booked for the week after. By chance, Deacon won, becoming the youngest GW superstar ever to win the World title. Their animosity reignited on the second edition of Deaconpalooza when Deacon made an announcement involving the Luke Cage movie in development, which Brick had been vying to be cast in the title role. Deacon casually mentioned that he was up for the role. This caused Brick to come out anf confront him. A moment later, Train got involved, resulting in an intense three-way brawl. For the next two months, theirs was the hottest rivalry in Global Wrestling. After Dead Zone, many thought the Brick/Deacon rivalry had ended. Not so. Deacon had decided, one day, to berate Gunner Addison. Unfortunately, two of the men in her life were Brick and Ely Braxton. Braxton wanted to tear Deacon apart, but Brick attempted to calm him down, citing the fact that he and Deacon were tag partners in a qualifyinng match for the Master of The Mat tournament. The next week, Deacon berated Gunner yet again. When the match came, Deacon was assaulted backstage by Ely, costing them the match. The following week, Deacon blamed Brick for the assault, when Brick blamed Deacon's mouth. Then, unexpectedly, he produced a pair of black thong panties which he claimed belong to Rhaiza. In the weeks that followed, Brick had proceeded to toy with Deacon's mind with thinly-veiled comments, an audio tape depicting what sounded like Brick and Rhaiza engaging in sexual intercourse and a woman who looked suspiciously like Rhaiza visiting Brick in his locker room. Deacon constantly demanded a match with Brick at Whiplash, but Bubba refused him. Finally, Deacon took a stand and attempted to hold up Evolution until Brick came down and faced him. What came later was a brawl between Bubba McCoy and Deacon, then a brawl between Deacon and Brick, ending in Deacon's indefinite suspension from Global Wrestling, the latest in a number of blows struck against the Upper Echelon. ---- 'Aren't you a little upset that Deacon made it to the Hall of Fame before you did?' 'Not really. Yeah, sure, I could have made it in if I'd retired. But I've got something Deacon didn't.' 'Yeah? What?' 'Longevity. Every cat that said they'd take me out, I've outlasted them. I outlasted Jason Hughes. I outlasted Mark Makonie. I outlasted Stecchino and Price. I outlasted...we don't have enough time to go over the cats I've outlasted in GW.' 'Mmhmm. So, you're facing John Zero at Maximum Carnage, huh?' 'Yeah, him and one of the Vergomovs.' 'You need to be careful. Deacon was one of TUE. He might use that match to try to take you out, especially since you got Deacon suspended.' 'I didn't get Deacon suspended. Deacon got Deacon suspended.' 'Yeah, because of the mind games you were playing. You think TUE is gonna let that slide?' 'I don't give a fuck what they let slide! Zero wants to try me, he knows where I am.' Meanwhile, on the TV, a commercial for McDonald's Chicken Selects had just come on. 'Mama is enjoying her Chicken Selects!' said the woman in the commercial. 'None for you! Touch my chicken, you're grounded for life! Whoa! Are you getting close to my chicken?! Mama loves you, but you need to back away from my chicken! Beep beep!' 'Who writes these commercials?' 'I know, right? This one is just a Paul Mooney joke waiting to happen. 'Back away from my chicken'? What kinda bullshit is that?' 'And the one where that woman threatens her grandmother? Is McDonald's not aware that Black people do eat at their restaurants?' 'Aaron, Black people went to see 'Soul Plane', ok?' 'Good point. We've gotten too complacent.' 'Let's just change the channel.' Aaron pressed the channel button, only to see the same commercial. 'My chicken!' 'Change.' '...but it won't save you money on car insurance.' 'Change!'
