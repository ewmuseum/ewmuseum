Screw the Meek

11 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04.11.04 'Bull-fucking-shit! I don't give a damn... he can kiss my ass!' Josh and Ice's heads turn in time to see the martini glass that Ken Partridge Jr just tossed shatter on the wall. He was livid, and to be honest, he was scaring them a little. Neither Josh nor Ice had ever seen their friend and cohort act this way. Their entire career's, Ken had been the calm and collective one, and now, with one small hair, the came's back had finally broken. However, as fearful as they were, they would have been spewing lies had either man said that they didn't like it. 'Monday,' screamed Ken, 'he's got until the match on Monday. If he can prove to me he still belongs here, that's fine. Mark my words, though, if that son of a bitch makes me think for just a fraction of a second that he's trying to fuck me, I'll snap his damn neck.' Another glass thrown by Ken breaks on the wall, this time a highball, still half-full of scotch. 'Dude, cool off, alright,' proclaimed Josh cautiously. 'i'm sure Ace has a perfectly good excuse for not...' 'For not fucking calling any of us back in a week,' questioned Ken, cutting off his best friend. 'No, Josh, there is no excuse... no reason! He doesn't want to train or work out with me, that's fine. He doesn't want to go over the match with me, that's fine... but have the spaldings to tell me that you don't want to.' Ice finally stirs, picking up a few pieces of shattered glass from the bar and tossing them in a trash can. As he looks up, he snags Ken's bottle of MacAllen and studies it for a few second. 'You know, Ken,' chimed Ice, 'You've always had good taste. Good taste in women... in cars... in clothes and style. Good taste in liquor.' Ice begins to pour the perfectly aged Scotch over three icecubes in a new highball glass. 'You would think with all of that good taste, you'd be meek enough to ponder about whether or not Ace had a serious problem, like an accident, perhaps, and that's the reason he hasn't gotten in contact.' Ice puts the punctuation on his statement by finishing of the drink with a splash of soda, a lemon, and a lime. 'Of course,' says Ice as he hands Ken the drink, 'that's just my opinion.' Ken takes in some of the Scotch and 7 before calmly responding. 'Fuck the meek. They can inherit the Earth... simply because those of us who are elite will be inhabiting the heavans,' quips Ken with an arrogant smirk sprawled across his face. 'Oderint dum metuant.' 'Let them hate,' begins Josh, 'so long as they fear. Latin... nice touch.' 'So much truth in that one statement,' responds Ken, 'and so many thought that Caligula was just a bumbling drunk.' Ken's words brings a chuckle through the room as he finally takes a seat, followed swiftly by both Ice and Josh. 'Even if I do go into this thing alone, it's not like I have anything to be worried about,' remarked Ken cockily. This Kosidlo cat should be cake... and Idol, well, once I take out that Josh-kid and it's one-on-one, he won't stand a chance.' 'That's the Ken I look to for a partner,' returned Ice. 'Screw that humble bullshit, you know you're damn good... one of the best... hell, we all are... that's why we're here. There's no shame in proclaiming it.' 'We all proclaim it, Brock,' said Josh. 'Some of us just prove it in the ring.' 'Well, I'll prove it both on the mic AND in the ring,' exclaimed Ken. 'And I'll begin doing so at Evolution.'
