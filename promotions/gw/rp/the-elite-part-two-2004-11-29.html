The Elite (Part Two)

29 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.29.04 The rain was beating down viciously at New York's JFK International Airport as the bright yellow texi pulled up to the front terminal. Ken Partridge Jr tosses a handfull of twenties at the cabbie in the front seat and hopped from the door and into the downpour without even thinking about his change. Ken ran to the doors as the happy driver sped away counting his tip. His eyes were dancing throughout the soaking crowd in search of a long mane of beautiful blonde hair where they finally found what they were searching for. His run quickly turned into a sprint as he pushed innocent bystanders to the side in his quest. As he reached her, he spun her around as she gasped before laying her eyes on Ken. He took a moment to take in Gunner's face before he quickly moved in a laid his lips on her, the rain pouring down both of their faces as the crowd observed in near silence lovers' first kiss. 'Don't go,' Ken spoke as he pulled away. 'I don't want you to go. 'Wh-What,' she asked somewhat out of breath. 'Ken, I have to go.' 'I need you to stay,' he continued. 'I don't know what's going on in my mind and my soul, but I know it involves you. I know that I can't see me without you and that if you left me now, I would die. I -- I didn't know what I was going to say when I found you, I still don't... But, please... Stay.' She looked up at him with tears in her eyes and a smile on her face. She would have had to admit, the kiss took her completly off guard, but she couldn't get it out of her mind as the two now stood face to face, both with tears in the eyes and streaming down their faces camofauged only by the torential downpour that was enveloping them. 'Stay,' rang out a voice in the crowd. 'Stay,' came another... and another, and another. She started laughing, fighting the tears as she did so before reaching up and embracing in what was now their second kiss. Rarely is a second, or any kiss as powerful as the first. Let alone the rarity of a second surpassing the first. This kiss, however, left all others far behind. One could hear God and his choir of angels cheer as the heavens were opening up with the cleansing surplus of cold drops that covered them. The crowd erupted into applause and whistles around them as the two melted into one another forgetting everyone and everywhere that they were. In those moments, they knew only each other's hearts and souls. 'How could I forget,' responded Ken. Gunner threw her arms back around Ken's neck and kissed him gently before the two were interrupted by the voice of Bill Moresi coming from the other side of the room. 'That duck smells like it's ready to me.' The two looked upon one another and shared one more quick kiss. 'I love you,' Gunner whispered. 'I love you,' Ken responed as the two broke their embrace and Ken picked up the duck, allowing Gunner to lead the way into the hungry crowd of friends and family that were now awaiting them in the dining room of Ken's apartment.
