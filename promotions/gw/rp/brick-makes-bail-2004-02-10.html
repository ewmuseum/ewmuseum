Brick makes bail

10 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/10/04 Brick had been sitting in the holding cell for about an hour. It began after the end of the Terminator-Mark Makonie match. Brick was appointed the special guest referee. It was a difficult undertaking, seeing as both participants were men he’d had issues with. His issues with Makonie were more recent and as personal, but not in the same way. Whereas his issues with Terminator stemmed from a year-old feud, his issues with Makonie stemmed from something deeper. They stemmed from a more timely issue: the issue of race. Granted, it wasn’t the first time Brick had to deal with a GW superstar who made prejudiced comments. It was just the first time in two years. Brick’s relationship with Makonie has been decidedly hostile since Makonie’s arrival in GW. It began with the incident on the 1-26-04 edition of Evolution and escalated at Global Reload. The aftermath of the match was the next and logical step. It was such an intense clash that it took armed policemen to finally bring things to order. Brick could at least take comfort in one fact: the Dallas police had wisely put Makonie in a different holding cell. Just then, a police officer came with keys. “Congratulations. You just made bail.” “Who posted my bail?” “That pretty little boss o” yours.” Brick figured it had to be Christina coming to bail him out. It would either have been her, Ely, Jesse, or Logan or he would have had to wait until someone in New York could wire him the bail money. While he was relieved that he didn’t have to spend another moment in jail, he just knew that Christina was probably going to read him the riot act for yet another brawl. As he walked out of the station, he saw Christina McCoy leaning against her limousine, her arms folded. Brick walked up to the limousine. “Thanks for bailing me out.” “Just get in, Brick.” Brick could tell by the tone in her voice that this was going to be a long ride. As the limo drove off, there was a still silence between Brick and Christina for about four minutes. “So how was your evening?” Brick asked. “Since my dad disappeared, I have been doing my damnedest to ensure that Global Wrestling runs like a well-oiled machine. So I think you can understand why this upsets me. It’s bad enough that I had to deal with Kid Flash acting like such a jackass and damn near assaulting me, not to mention the new guy Train making a mess out of the Donavan Savage-Kid Wikkid match, now I find myself having to bail one of the company’s top superstars out of jail. Not to mention the way you tried to alter the events of the match in your favor. Throwing the chair” “Christina, please!” “Excuse me?” “You knew I’d screw Makonie over! That’s why you made me the special guest referee in the match.” “I figured since you didn’t like either Makonie or Terminator, that you’d call the match down the middle. And quite frankly, I don’t appreciate you accusing me of doing otherwise.” “Well, I’m on better terms with page these days than I am with Makonie. At least, he never called me “coon” or told me to go back to Africa. And if you didn’t want this kind of trouble, maybe you shouldn’t have hired the son of a bitch in the first place.” “Look, I already told you at Global Reload, I hired him before I learned he was such a racist pig.” “Uh-huh.” “I did!” “He didn’t list any interests in his job application” You know, lynching, cross burning?” “Brick, just stop.” ??Brick, just stop?, my ass, Christina!” Brick roared. “You heard what that son of a bitch said to me at Global Reload! You heard what he said about me! About my people!” “Brick, look. Makonie as a person disgusts the hell out of me. But it’s not helping matters any with you attacking him like you’ve been.” “He’s been pretty much calling me “nigger” since the day he walked through the double doors! You tell me what I’m supposed to do!” “Look, for now, let’s try to deal with it, calm’oh never mind. I forgot who I was talking to. Just try to stay out of trouble unless he provokes you.” “Yeah, whatever.” Christina sat back in her seat and sighed. “It’s going to be a long ride,” she thought to herself.
