Trustworthy

6 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/06/05 For Danielle the week passed wasn't a bad one, it was just a busy one. She had no one to really blame for her loss then herself. And she was accepting on that. Between the photo shoots for the pay per view and the show, Danielle hadn't really had time to breathe. Josh didn't help it any, Danielle was sure she knew what he was up to. But, just as he was getting ready to do it, he ran out of the building so fast that he never finished. She was still waiting on the tape from the Pay per view, but it had not arrived yet, which was odd. Gray never took this long to get a copy of a match for her. Something was up, but she couldn't quite put her finger on it. Journal Entry: We just got back, the Pay per view went well, even though I lost my match and my title to him. But then again I guess I can not blame anyone but myself. My mind wasn't fully on the match with what happened up at the dome, over looking the Northern lights. Josh dropped to one knee started to ask me something but then got up and ran outside. Did he change his mind? He really didn't say much about what was going on. If he only knew how frustrating that was for me. And now I think Blaise,Gray, Ukyo and Josh are all hiding something from me. There was something about being up there, it was like a presence that I just couldn't put my finger on. But almost like I was being watched the entire time I was there. I guess I should be use to that feeling. But as I told Angel, things were different up there. Ukyo is suppose to be coming over for some girl time. I think I will talk to her for a little bit and see what I can pry from her. I hate feeling like a outsider looking in. Especially when this most being kept from me is about me. Why is it that no one seems to talk to me about things, but yet they seem to demand I tell them everything. I believe the path of truth goes both ways. Maybe if they talk to me about things then I would talk to them. Right now the only one i seem to be able to talk to is Angel. Other things I share with Josh, but the things I put in here are the secrets I share with Angel also. But no one else. Angel is the one that knows what led up to my last Manifestation the day dream. I haven't shared that with anyone and don't plan to either. My Dreams don't always stop they are the same ones over and over again. Just wish they didn't have to deal with flying. The knock on her bedroom door made Danielle put her pen down. “Come in.” The door opens, and Jessie, one of their two housekeepers enters. Jessie is the younger one that Dani likes, even though she likes to take long looks in the direction of her boyfriend.. “Ms. Northsky, your guest has arrived.” Danielle nodded her head and stood up, quickly trying to recap her bottle of ink. However her co-ordination wasn't up to par with that task, and she ended up knocking it over and spilling it some on the rug next to her desk. Giving a slight disgusted look at the new spot on the rug. ?CRAP!? Jessie pulled the towell from her apron and started to come over to try to mop the mess up. “No, no. It's ink. It'll only smear it. Please, go tell Ms. Ukyo I will be there shortly. And please call the carpet cleaners, before this stain sets.” Danielle slid the still damp journal into the desk drawer, leaving it open to dry but out of sight to prying eyes. “Yes, Ma'am. I'll have them here shortly.” Jessie quickly turned to leave the room, As Dani walked over to her dresser and took out a quick change of clothes from her nightie that she was still wearing. A quick pair of sweats and a t-shirt quickly made her more presentable to have a guest over. She quickly looked over at the black splotch that had penetrated the rug. ? I can't believe I did that.? She headed down to the bottom floor of the three story house, to the small snack kitchen. Sitting there already was her friend, Ukyo Kushinaga. Ukyo already had a cup of tea and a muffin in front of her for a breakfast with Dani. Dani poured herself some of the coffee from the freshly made pot and then pulled up into the seat next to her. “Dani, you seem distracted.” Danielle turned her head to look at Ukyo. “That isn't the word for it, but it works. I know Josh was up to something but if it was what I am thinking... Oh, I don't know. It's been on my mind now for over a week. And I can really use it to blame myself for my loss to Ambrosi up in Alaska.” Ukyo tilted her head towards Danielle. ?What do you think he was up to?? Dani sighed. “Only a question that he had for me that never got asked.” ?You think he was going to ask you something?? Danielle just shook her head yes, but that is all it took. “And I knew all to well what that question would have been. But it just didn't happen.” Ukyo smiled at her. “How can you sit here so Calmly” Danielle chuckled to herself a little bit. ?Who said I am being Calm? I am a nervous wreck!? Danielle laughs loudly. ?But there was more then that up there, it was just something distracting, too up there. It was like I... like there was something that was watching me. Just call it a gut feeling thats all. ?A feeling like you were being watched? Why didn't you bring this up while we were up there?? “Yeah just a little bit. Did you guys see anything or hear anything, I know you have a little more of ways of getting information then I do. Then again Gray doesn't talk to me, and I don't think anyone truly knows what is going on.” “Well” Ukyo looks to be concerned about letting the cat out of the bag. ?You and Blaise, when you ran off, did you see or talk to anyone?? Ukyo just nods her head slightly. “We're not up there anymore. So it should be safe. Look, there was someone up there. It was, it wasn't a big deal just someone that knew you were there. We talked to them, and they said they'd watch. Nothing more.” Dani's concern showed on her face. ?So who was up there?? “Another like you, at least in a way I guess. She came to us, when you arrived. She sensed you. Blaise is looking into the who's, whys and what's of everything, still. But the name she gave us other then what the local Natives up there called her was Zuphlas. Angel of forests. She claimed to have been up there since your great grandfather had fought with another angel. She really didn't have much else for us.” Dani perked up with some interest. ?She? An angel that was a she?? “Yes, well at least her form had tits, you know.” Both chuckled for a bit, but Dani stopped suddenly. ?Like that is going to help me, any. I am still in the dark about what is going on. And where the hell is this week's tape? Gray usually has the show tapes for me the next morning. This is almost a week now!? Ukyo tries to hide the fact that she's working with the boys to keep her from seeing the full show's results. She is a quick thinker though. “I think there was a problem with the recording of the show, osmething about tropical Storm messing up the truck.” That seems to ward Dani off a bit. “Yeah heard that they had been up to something. I know that our rental car had to be defrosted after one of their pranks. They're really coming across as retarded or something. Actually that's probably not fair to retarded people.” Ukyo laughs a bit and adds in her comments. ?Yeah, you should of saw the tag team that got Tared and Feathered!? Danielle almost spits the mouthful of coffee, ?What?? Tar and feathered?? “Well Coke syrup and down feathers. But Tropical Storm definitely became the most wanted tag team and not in a good way. Add in Fred Grest making Dawn MacFarlane's clothes pink, and his prank on Elia Smith, they made for some wanted posters being made up.” ?Sounds like it... wait tropical Storm... weren't they a tag team like years ago? Haven't you guys had to deal with them before?? “Yeah they were hired for Global a few weeks back. Right after you were. We've not had much with them, but Blaise's cousins have had lots of dealings with them. They're the ones that used to sell that poster of Fey McClancy naked, and the sex tapes that Sean and his wife made when they were dating.” Dani shakes her head at those comments. ? Looks like Josh and Gray have their work cut out for them. I heard about them when I was in school. They were quite the trouble makers in their day. “And they've not changed. They were hired for Concession stand work. But Grest decided to challenge them to a prank war of some type. From what I understand they had been after Blaise the whole show. But seems to keep missing him. Instead another new team, the Hammer Brothers took some great offense to their first prank and got involved in a few instances with them.” “Lucky for them I am sure Blaise would of killed them.” “If he didn't, I would have.” Ukyo smiled as she sipped on her tea. Jessie walks in and interrupts them. ?Excuse me Miss Northsky?? Danielle turned around to see Jessie Standing there with the phone in hand. ?Yes, Jessie?? “The rug cleaner can be here in 30 minutes he said until then keep the spot damp.” “Okay, thank you. Do whatever he needs. Paying him to clean it will be cheaper then replacing that rug in this house.” “Yes, Ms. Northsky.” Jessie turns and leaves the area, talking with the man on the phone to end the call with him. Dani turns her attention back to Ukyo. ?So, you want to go swimming?? “Sure. Let me go get my bathing suit on and I'll be right back. I brought it over in my bag. I was expecting as much.” Dani nods at her. They had taken that to be part of their work out routine. Dani especially started insisting when Blaise let it slip about the Occupational therapy Ukyo was trying to avoid with her neck injury. She watched as Ukyo finished her tea off, and then got up while she grabbed her bag. “Only if life was just simple.” Danielle set her coffee cup down and turned to head back to her room to get into her bathing suit. About half an hour later. Dani and Ukyo are both out back near her pool. Dani is wearing her white thong bikini, the one that Josh really likes. The reason why we point this out is that Ukyo, taller, and more statuesque, with a body to die for also, is wearing a rather unflattering one piece suit in a blue color that matches her hair right now. Dani puts her towel down onto one of the chairs and takes a deep breath. ?So, wonder what Global will have for us this week?? At that, Ukyo places her towel down and then without any emotion says. “Well you've got Jake Hix for the second time. I know you almost beat him a few weeks ago.” Dani smacks her forhead. “I can't get any rest huh? Sometimes I wonder if they book some of my matches, trying to make sure I'll loose to these big guys.” “Well, any consolation... if you beat him this time you'll be the number one contender for the World Title.” Dani is almost floored by that. She was still legally a rookie in the sport. She hadn't been wrestling professionally more then 3 years. She had actually only about 2 years total so far. But while some always said she was a natural at it, she still hadn't fully grasped her rapid rise in the last few feds that she had been in, and then the last few months in Global after her slow start. However, Dani had to start thinking the question: Was she ready for a chance like that. “Shit! I didn't need to know that.” “I'm sorry.” Ukyo looked concerned to her. She reached out and put her hand onto the smaller, in some ways, woman's shoulder. Dani's hand went up to clasp her friends. “It's okay. Just don't know what path I'm taking sometimes. It's been enough of an adjustment in taking on a full time boyfriend. I don't ever want to do anything that would jeopardize my career, but I also want to be like every other woman out there. It's a bad spot to be in.” Ukyo nodded. “You can say that again. I've got my own ambitions and all that, but I also find myself wanting to be a mother, especially now that I have twins to help take care of. I just hope that everything works out and one day I give Blaise another child or two.” ?Two?? Ukyo smiled. “Yeah. He's already produced one pair of twins. But apparently in his kind twins are very common. And since I'm basically the Japanese equivalent of his, it'd make sense. His cousins were both born in two sets of twins. His dad and uncle are twins. And his daughters were twins. He's the only 'only child' born to his family for generations.” “That's scary to think of. Especially with the pain invovled.” Ukyo smiled. “Well according to those in the Order, most of us who share the blood of the immortals feel minimal pain or no pain when giving child birth. We went through our pain when we awakened. That's how we get rid of the curse of mortality put down on humans.” Dani's head swam with that information. But she wrote it off as fact coming from Ukyo. She knew that Ukyo had taken to studies on the many subjects since her injury, let alone the fact that she was a trained hunter and guardian in the Order, like her boyfriend. ?That's nice to know. But while you're being open, can I ask you something?? Ukyo dipped a toe into the pool to test it for proper temperature. ?Yes?? ?Why the hell do you always wear such unflattering clothes?? Ukyo just smiled at her and dove into the pool. She popped back and swam over to the side that Dani stood on. “Well, to answer your question. Because I really don't see why I should. I know that the dress I now wear out to the ring is getting some fans. But I've never understood that. I don't have your looks, so...” Dani gimmaced at that comment. “Oh, come on. You made the cover of FHM:UK for their wrestling issue. I think that's the only time I've seen you wear anything resembling what Josh would call 'sexy' on a woman.” “Only time I wear thong outside of bedroom, too. Just not my cup of tea. The modeling was fun for a moment. But after seeing what Sean's wife and you go through for it, I don't think it's a career path I'd take.” Dani knew that it was time consuming. “Well, you know, FHM has been bugging me about doing their year recap issue with some new shots. They said they'd come here to do them. You were in FHM:UK about the same time. You could always come in with me and give it another try. They'd probably take that shot if given the opportunity.” Suddenly Dani's mind jumped back some. “Wait, only time you've worn a thong outside of the bedroom. Okay, that might have qualified as too much information.” Ukyo smiled at her and then flipped back into the water and started to swim a bit. She popped up again and looked over at Dani. “That is where our cultures differ, Dani. If you asked me some things, I see nothing wrong with you knowing them. While over here, people are so secretive. It breeds mistrust in your whole nation. That's why the Order attracted me so much. They don't follow that same pattern of mistrust amongst each other. Blaise has always been very open with me and not kept secrets from me.” Dani thought about a few things and then fired back. “Yeah, but they seem to think they can keep things from me.” Ukyo smiled. ?Dani, can I ask you if you've asked Blaise for any updates on anything since you've come to know him?? Dani shakes her head to the negative, not giving a verbal answer. “Then maybe you should.” ?Why should I have to ask?? ?Because sometimes, there are people who don't want to know. The only way to let someone know which type you are, is to let them know. If you don't ask him, he'll assume safe and not tell you. If you ask him, he'll tell you. But just remember, sometimes he's sharp with his comments that could be the truth. Be ready for him to be honest with you. He was taught by the Keeper, himself. The Keeper let's Blaise still use the moniker of Tetsuo Oni when he wants to. He's the Keeper's chosen one. But in that, he's not only learned the fighting arts from him, but the fact that truth should never be sugar coated. So be ready.? Dani nodded in affirmation to that. “Okay, I'll remember that.” Dani slid into the water to start the work out with her friend. The End, for now.
