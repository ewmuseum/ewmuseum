Is Trey Reed a pimp or does that nigga sell drugs? (Part1)

23 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-23-03 “You gimme your number..I call you up, you act like your pussy don’t interrupt, I don’t have no trouble with you fuckin me’but I have a little problem with you not fuckin me” This was it’things were about to get hoppin down here in SoCal, where the drinks are bad and the bitches are even badder. I looked at Trey Reed’and Trey Reed looked back at me with a look of confusion in his eyes as I pushed the door open to my hotel room. Darkness? I squinted my eyes a little bit trying to focus as I felt around on the side of wall for the light switch. “Jay..” Said Trey from the entrance to the room as I continued to try to locate the switch “Yea’Man” I replied not giving any thought to what I said because I was paying more attention to the wall than Trey “So’what are we celebrating again?” I stopped looking for the switch and turned around to Trey’and saw a outline of his body as a light behind him obscured his face’I stood there trying to come up with a reason to why we are celebrating “Uhh..because we can?” I said back to him in a questionable manner, shit I didn’t know why we were celebrating, it just sounded cool at the time. I turned back around and continued my hunt for the light switch. What the fuck? The son of a bitch is hidden I thought as I put both hands on the wall and felt around’at about this time the light flipped on and I glaced back to Trey who was still standing in the entrance of the door but his hand was on the switch as he looked at me with a smirk on his face “Found it?” He said to me as I stood there looking like a jackass with both hand on the wall. Trey walked into the room looking around like a kid in a candy store “Wow..man’this is nice” He exclaimed as he looked around at the red satin sheets on the beds, the big screen tv, and the bottles of liquor lined up in a nice row on the cedar bar in the corner of the room, this caught his eye I think because this is the first place he headed as I watched him walk across the white carpet to the bar’the place was pretty big, I thought to myself as I looked around a crystal chandelier that hung down from the golden painted ceiling , a big leather wrap around couch right in the middle of the room, a few stupid paintings on the royal blue walls and most importantly a white fridge which I hoped was filled with thirty packs of Bud Light like I told the people to put in there “Yea I know” I said to Trey but my baby blue eyes were focused on the wood handle of the fridge as I walked up to it’someone was gonna get an ass whoopin if my beer wasn’t in there I thought as I pulled the door open, the cool air hit my face in a rush’and there it was my treasure’ice cold beer’four thirty packs stacked one after another, fuck yea I said to myself as I ripped the top box open and pulled out a can of beer and cracked the top on it. I looked back over to Reed who was still inspecting the bottles. “Trey” I hollered, as I looked at him over the door of the open fridge awaiting a response “Yeah?” He said to me as he held a bottle of Hennessey in his hand looking at the label “You thirsty man?” I said to him with a bit of excitement in my voice because I was ready to get fucked up “Yea sure what do you got?” He questioned to me still looking at that bottle “Ice cold muh fuckin beea” I shouted to him trying to sound cool like one of those beer guys at the baseball games. Trey looked up from the bottle and looked around the room scanning for my position, he found me behind the fridge sticking my head up over the door and he nodded at me “Na dude’I think im gonna break into some of this” He replied to me as he held up the bottle of Hennessy so I could see it gleaming out of his hands “Alright cool” I exclaimed to him as I closed the door of the fridge with my Doc Boot and a beer in my hand, I jumped over the back of the couch and plopped down on the soft black leather. I took a quick drink of my beer and look down at a coffee table on top of it were three controllers which I figured went to the tv, the massive sound system it had built up around it, and the third I didn’t know what the hell it went to. I grabbed the controller in the middle with my free hand, pointed it at the tv and pressed the on button, the cd player on top of the tv came on in glowing green and read “track 1” I didn’t know what was going to play but I pressed the volume up anyways and the music blared’Mike Jones Automatic Freestyle *They call me a pimp * Cuz I walk with a strut *I fucka..I fucka up sluts that try to fuck me for bucks *Im Mike Jones the onea onea one and only *Can’t nobody call me *Now that im hot everybody wanna be my homie Damn..this is tight I thought as I slowly bobbed my head to the beat stopping every once and a while to get a drink of beer, Reed must have heard it to..well’I know Reed heard it cuz it was really loud, the speakers were pounding as I looked back at Reed and he looked back at me with a some what look of horror. After seeing his face I turned the music down and looked back up at him “Haha..what?” I asked him laughing as he stood there with the bottle of Hennessey in one hand and a shot glass in the other “What kind of music is this?” He asked me in a concerned voice and a confused look on his face “Rap I believe’chopped and screwed” I said back to Trey sounding like I really knew what the fuck I was talking about. But He just nodded his head and looked up at the cd player “Oh’ok”.whatever the fuck that is? He replied to me trying to play the subject off. He walked around the the other side of the couch and up to the tv and changed the song “Du..Dude..what are you doing” That’s party music man?.” I said to him in a non serious pissed off tone of voice, Trey dropped his shoulders and turned around and faced me as he raised his arms in the air “Jay’I don’t see a party here” I interrupted Trey in mid sentence “No..you don’t see a party’yet’the night is young..im sober?” “I said as I pointed at Trey” “Your sober’the bitches ain’t here yet...the bitches are sober’the bitches like party music” I exclaimed to Trey as I was now standing on the glass coffee table explaining to this guy how to mack hoes and what does he do? He turns his back on me and puts it on Marvin Gaye’Marvin Gaye?...are you fucking serious? I said to myself as he turned back around smiling ear to ear “Now that’s real music” I stood there on top of the coffee table with a look of shock on my face as I watched Trey close his eyes and sway back and forth to the rhythm of the music’enough is enough as I shouted out “TREY!” Reed snapped out of his own little magical world and looked up at me on the table’as he did *DING *DONG The door bell’our eyes lit up and we both looked towards the door’but I stopped and hopped off the table as Trey headed for the door wanting to get the first glimpse of the girls I have lined up but I shouted out to him again “TREY!” He stopped in his tracks and looked back at me “What?” He said to me in a rush as he tried to get to the door “Turn that shit off’girls can’t take that shit..just like I can’t” Trey shook his head and waved me off as he continued to head to the door “Girls like that music’it will get you laid” What what? I said to myself as I looked at him with a blank stare “Hey..I don’t need music to get me laid..I can get myself laid” I hollered out to Trey and I hit the power button on the cd player turning it off and started in a dead sprint to the door.
