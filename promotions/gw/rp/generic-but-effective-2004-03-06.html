Generic but Effective

6 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03.06.04 Getting out of the shower, I look through the two intersecting walls of my bedroom, forming a large and ongoing window overlooking the entire city. New York really is the most beautiful city in the world... and I would know... I've been to all of them. Chicago, L.A., New Orleans, Montreal, London, Glascow, Mexico City, Tokyo, Detroit... you name it... I've worked it. Hell, I've probably lived it. That's one thing that a lot of these younger guys in Global Wrestling don't realize. In this day and age... somebody's been there before you and already done it. They want to be great? Well, they can acheive that. But if they want to be original it's going to be a hell of a lot harder. Roz Blaze... a big time fan favorite playing to the crowds... smiling for the babies and strutting for the ladies. Give me a break. Roz doesn't have the originality of a two year old. I've been there... I've worked to please those pieces of trash that fill up our arenas night after night. It got me absolutly nowhere. Someday, the kid'll see it. He'll realize that dancing in circles for these nobodies is only gonna give him carpet burn. As for right now... well, he seems to constantly be in the wrong place at the wrong time. Poor, poor Roz. Perhaos I'll have to knock a little sense into him at Evolution. There are others as well... these that claim to be dark and mysterious... living in the inner recesses of their minds... then going out after the show and partying it up at the local hot spots. This pack of benevolent sacrifices know absolutly nothing about what true mental suffering is all about. They don't know what it's like to be captured in the confines of your head. That deep dark prison that Sigmund Freud defined as 'our greatest gift and our greatest fear.' It's pitch black in there... completly desolate... you're alone... and cold. I've been there all to many times. I usually head there at some point during a match. That state of being where you feel nothing. You're numb... cold. You no longer care about your well-being or the well-being of your opponent. You put it all on the line with considering any of the outcome. It's not a fun place to be... they can simply ask those that I've admitted to that state of being. Then there's Max Roswell. The boy that 'defeated' me for my Primestar Championship. It's so simple to defeat someone when they're not there. I have business with this punk that will be settled after Evo. There's just something about a little Jumping Bean bouncing around that couldn't put a sentence together if it were a two-piece puzzle. I've faced him before... I'll sure I'll face him again. I've beat him before... now I'm going to break him. He doesn't know what it's like to be in that dark place. To be completly broken down... mentally, physicaly... BROKEN! His neck... his back... his arms... his legs... his spirit... his body... his mind... ALL... OF... HIM! He plays to the fans... and they eat it up... they love him. They love hims in a way simlar to the way they loved me. There's a difference though. I never loved them back... I never really cared what they thought. My one purpose wasn't to please them... it was to scrape... pull... drag myself to the top of this business in any way plausable. Max... he loves them. He just wants to please them. He doesn't realize yet, that they're not going to carry him anywhere. He doesn't realize, yet, that it's going to be them on his back... not vice versa. At Evolution... everyone in the back... Roz Blaze... J.J. Jarone... and ESPECIALLY Max Roswell will learn these lessons... curtosy of the innovator of fucking greatness. At Evolution... there's going to be a clinic... put on by none other than The Elite.
