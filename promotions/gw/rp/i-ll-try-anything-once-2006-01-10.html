I'll Try Anything Once

10 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 10, 2006 When you aren't used to the fortune and fame, it can really get to you. Unfortunately for me, I had yet to achieve the fortune, and the fame wasn't even there, yet, but I was already getting tired of having my picture taken. I had signed with Global less than a month ago, and already they are trying to get my picture. I haven't even wrestled a match there yet, let alone do anything to warrant this kind of attention. GTT5 wasn't even that great for me. Obviously when you sign with one of the big 10 in PrimeTime Central, however, you've become someone, or at least are labeled someone at the start. I wasn't sure if I was ready to be all over magazines, and have my name printed on t-shirts, and all that. I wasn't even positive I ever wanted that to happen. I only came to America to live my dreams, and those dreams don't happen to include merchandising rights for any federation, or anything like that. I just want to wrestle and have fun doing it. Winning in the process? Why the hell not?! Thus brings you somewhat up to speed on my situation. Well, other than the fact that the multitude of questioning I had been getting lately was having me question a few things about myself. Was I living enough? Was I actually enjoying my time in the states so far? Couldn't I be doing a little more than I was? True, I hadn't done much more than sit around my apartment, and go out when necessary, but I was about to live my dream up in Global Wrestling starting in just a couple of days. But much like my first match in Global, I wanted to try other things for the first time. Yes, for the first time. I had never participated in a match of this size of any sorts. Athens Underground only had a couple more people in it than just this one match has total. The most people I had faced at one time were three, and that seemed tough enough. Four would be insane, but then you throw nineteen people into the same match? It almost seemed like too much to handle. I liked the sound of that! -------------------------------------- What's the first thing you think of when you want to get a little bit crazy? Well, for me, never actually having a drop of alcohol in my life, up until this point, seemed like something I needed to fix. So getting a little bit crazy for me then was to get into a bar and drink myself silly. Little did I know at this point was what the effects would be like on my personal being. Though, how it would affect me later wasn't any care of mine. I just wanted a drink. 'What will it be son?' The fat man behind the bar eventually asked me. He seemed as if asking me was a mistake, like the bodyguard checking IDs on the way in had made a mistake. I looked around, and kind of shyly asked. 'What would you suggest?' I looked around after my question. 'I've never drank before.' I said under my breath as my cheeks turned red, or I could feel them turn red. As if it wasn't bad enough I'm sure a couple folks around me had heard of my lack of experience in the drinking department, the bartender sounded an alarm. I was unsure what this was about, until he grabbed the mic. 'Hey everyone, who wants to welcome this young man to the world of drinking? He says it's his first time, and he has no clue what's good! Let's pop this guys drinking cherry!' He exclaimed as laughs were heard, and dirty smiles were plastered all over their faces. I wasn't so sure about this, to be honest. I had never heard of anything like this happening in Greece at bars. Then again, I had never been to any bars in my life until now. Yes, one of those pathetic things about my life I wish had been different. 23 years of age and had yet to experience being drunk. Well, until now. It wasn't long until a couple of guys, easily looking like they were in the Army, or some branch of the United States military, and they pulled a couple bills from their pockets. They didn't even say anything to me, but instead grabbed me and took me back to the bar. There they ordered what sounded like three or four drinks. I was too scared to say anything. I wasn't sure what to expect. When I finally looked back at the bar, though, four shots of differing smells and colorations were sitting in front of me. The two military looking guys had big smiles on their faces, and it was if a crowd had gathered around me. 'Just do it!' Was yelled, from what sounded like directly behind me. I picked up the first shot, clear in looks, and smelled kind of decent. 'Well this one doesn't seem like it'll be that bad.' I said aloud as I downed it. The air was directly knocked from my lungs in reaction to the shot going down. I'm more than positive I made a funny face as a few laughs were heard. It tasted like raspberries and rubbing alcohol. 'That was the girls shot there buddy. Bacardi Raz. That's nothing like the next few.' One of the military guys had said to me before slapping me on the back. 'Now drink up young'n, you are going to get fucked up tonight!' With that said, the crowd around me erupted. It was a mystery to me why these Americans where so anxious to see a person who had never drank before get, well, 'fucked up.' Without hesitation, however, I picked up the next shot, darker in coloration and smelled worse than the first. I downed it, with the same reaction as before, only worse. I could almost feel the liquid coming back out of my mouth, but it hit my throat a second time, and went back down. My throat now burnt, and I had tasted two of the worst things in my life. 'What the hell was that?' I asked, short of breath. A laugh came from the other military man. 'Bacardi 151 son. Good stuff, huh?' He asked. I did nothing more than shake my head. Without wasting time, though, I grabbed the next shot, which looked and smelled similar to the last, but I knew it wasn't going to be good. I shook my head and thought twice about shooting it before changing my mind both times. Both military men beside me laughed before I finally took the shot. This time it stayed down, but it damn near knocked me on my ass. I coughed a couple of times too. 'Black liquorish?' I asked. 'I swear I tasted a little bit of that shit.' I said again, gaining my composure. 'That there was a Wild Fuck. Jager, Wild Turkey, and Rum. Not that you know what any of this is, and yeah, the Jager tastes like the liquorish. You should remember it for later though, if you remember anything.' The bartender said, getting as much of a kick out of this as everyone else who had surrounded me. Though, at this point, I was starting to get a tingling feeling throughout my body, and all the people around me were making me want to drink that last shot. I picked it up, and without a care in the world, threw it back. Big mistake! This clear shot was something I'll never forget. The burn in my throat. The inability the breath at all for at least 5 seconds. The running to the bathroom because I thought I was going to puke. As I made my way to the back of the bar, I heard one of the military men yell. 'Everclear! Never take shots of it! It'll fuck your shit up big time! Remember that!' I had made it into the bathroom, but lucky enough for me, I had lost the feeling of needing to puke. I was sure that was going to come later. And as it turned out, I wasn't going to remember anything from that point on, until the wee hours of the morning. -------------------------------------- My cell phone was ringing, I thought. It felt like a dream, but it seemed so real. My eyes opened, and everything was a blur. My head was pounding. My stomach was turning. What had I done? I tried looking at the clock on my bed side table, but the closest thing I could make the blurs out to be was, well, not ever really a time to begin with, but if I had to guess, it was somewhere close to 5am in Michigan. I frantically searched for my phone, barely moving my body any, and finally I had grabbed it. 'Hello?' I answered, though I'm sure it was far from being able to understand. 'This is Kaden.' I would reply back to the one on the other end. 'Do you know what time it is?' I asked. Obviously the person hadn't. 'It's like, well, if I could read the clock I would tell you. It feels like, really early!' I exclaimed to the other end, with frustration in my voice. There was then a time of silence. I kind of picked my head up off of my pillow, but only for a second because my head hurting made it impossible to hold like that. I opened my eyes a little more, but everything was still a blur. 'Tryna! What the hell are you calling me for? I thought you wanted nothing to do with me after I broke up with you?' I said back, wondering why my ex-girlfriend from back in Greece was calling me at 5am. 'How can you still want me? I had to break up with you to come to America, you know that! How'd you get this number anyway?' I asked, while thinking of what time it was there in Athens. It must have been somewhere around 2pm or so. 'Of course my mother gave it to you, she loves you.' I commented with a sigh following. A few moments went by while I obviously let her explain something to me. 'Yes, if I were still there, we'd be together, but I'm not still there. I'm sorry.' I wasn't really sorry, though. She was a beautiful young woman, but she was just too annoying. 'Sorry Tryna, it's really early, and I've got a hangover, yes, a hangover! So I'm going to go.' I then hung up the phone, and threw it to the floor beside my bed. I then heard a noise. A movement in my bed that was not my own. 'Hey there you, could you keep it down, we're trying to sleep here.' A female voice said. I, despite all pain involved, sat up in bed, and opened my eyes. I rubbed them really fast to clear the up, and I noticed I was lying in bed with a girl on either side of me. Not to mention the fact that neither of them were that great looking either. Not bad, but not great. It might have been the darkness of the room too, but I'm positive these were not the greatest looking women I've ever seen. 'Whoa.' Was all that I could say? The sad part about this is, though, I was too lazy to walk to the living room to sleep on the couch. Despite not wanting to keep between these two to sleep, I still laid back down, and attempted to get back to sleep. As I began to nod off, though, I heard my phone ringing again. It was too late; I was nearly gone once more. And as I drifted off, the thought that went through my head was never thinking this is where my night of trying new things would have lead me. I'll try anything once, and maybe I'll do it again as well. I sure hope this hangover doesn't affect my wrestling, though.
