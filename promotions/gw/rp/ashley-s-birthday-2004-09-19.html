Ashley's birthday

19 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/19/04 It's a rare and beautiful thing when you can find a decent parking spot in New York City. This day was no exception. The 30th of June was the day that Ashley Lillian Foster turned one year old. And on this day, her godfather Jamal Randall a.k.a. Brick was searching, frantically, for a place to park. 'Come on, come on, there's gotta be at least one empty space that ain't next to a hydrant,' he muttered as he began to drive. As he was coming towards an intersection, the light turned green. Just as he got to it, a pedestrian walked right in front of Brick's car...and stopped. Brick came to an abrupt halt. Just then, the man resumed crossing the street. 'Yo, what's your problem, man?!' Brick roared at the man out his window. The man then turned back his head and shouted 'Man, suck my di?' He immediately stopped himself when he saw who was behind the wheel. “Aw, damn!” 'Lucky I don't get up out this car and beat your monkey ass!' Brick yelled at the man. 'Hey, yo, I'm sorry! I ain't know it was you!' He then ran the other way as Brick drove off. Brick then sighed and drove for about 10 or 15 minutes. Not a decent parking space in sight. Meanwhile... The party was in full swing. Parents, grandparents, uncles, aunts, cousins had all come to celebrate Ashley's first birthday. 'I don't understand where he could be,' said Aaron, checking his watch. “There’s no way he’d miss this,” said Felicia. “He’s probably just looking for parking. You know how hard that is in New York.” “Maybe you’re right. I’ll try to hit him on his cell.” Aaron reached into his pocket and pulled out his cell, hitting a number on speed dial. Brick’s cell phone went off to the tune of “Ruff Ryder’s Anthem”. “Yeah?” “Brick?” “Hey, Aaron. I’m just looking for a parking space.” “Yeah, Felicia thought that might be it.” Aaron looked off and saw the birthday girl in her pretty little white dress, walking towards him. “Hey there, Princess.” He knealt down and picked her up in his arms. “Is that Ashley?” “Yeah.” “Let me talk to her.” 'Ashley, it's your uncle Brick.' Aaron then put the phone up to Ashley's ear. 'Hey, Ashley! Happy birthday!' Little Ashley's face brightened up upon hearing her godfather's voice. 'I'll be there in a little while.' 'OK, sweetie, let me talk to Uncle Brick. So, hey, man, how soon do you think you can get here?' 'Soon as I find a space.' Brick then looked out and saw what had been eluding him for the past 15 minutes or so: a parking space. “Oh, yes! Finally!” “You found a space?” “Yeah, I think this cat's coming out. Excuse me, are you coming out” OK, thank you! I’ll see you in a little bit.” “Was that Jamal?” “Yeah, he was looking for parking, just like you said.” “What can I say” It’s a gift.” Minutes later... Everyone had gathered around the table as Aaron sat at the head of the table with Ashley on his lap. Felicia brought out a chocolate frosted cake with one lit candle in the middle. 'HAPPY BIRTHDAY TO YOU! HAPPY BIRTHDAY TO YOU! HAPPY BIRTHDAY, DEAR ASHLEY! HAPPY BIRTHDAY TO YOU!' 'HAPPY BIRTHDAY TO YA HAPPY BIRTHDAY TO YA HAPPY BIRTHDAY! HAPPY BIRTHDAY TO YA HAPPY BIRTHDAY TO YA HAPPY BIRTHDAY! HAPPY BIRTHDAY HAPPY BIRTHDAY HAPPY BIRTHDAY HAPPY BIRTHDAY HAPPY BIRTHDAY HAPPY BIRTHDAY!' 'Now, blow out the candles, sweetie.' Ashley turned back and looked at Aaron and then blew at the candle. With an assist from Aaron, the candle went out. 'Yaaaaaay!'
