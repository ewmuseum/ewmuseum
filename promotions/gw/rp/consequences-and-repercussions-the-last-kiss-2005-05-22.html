Consequences and repercussions: The last kiss.

22 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May, 22nd 2005. 'Consequences and repercussions: The last kiss.' ---- 3:06 PM. May 22nd, 2005. Seattle, WA. I had hardly stepped through my front door when the fight started. Standing in my living room with a bag in one hand and my keys in the other I fell victim to a verbal ambush from my lovely girlfriend with a less than chipper attitude. 'You've got some explaining to do.' Raki said with a leer, her pretty face looking as angry as I've ever seen it. I forgot about the cleavage popping out of her black shirt and the way her jeans squeezed her attractive figure and my mind instead focused on the scowl she had across her features. 'No kiss to welcome me home?' I asked with a seductive smile, tucking my keys into the pocket of my A&F barstar jeans, my vaunted charm fell flat, suddenly I had less smooth moves than that drunk Irish guy I had met in West Virginia, the RV salesman, what was his name again? Oh yeah, DJ; a redheaded alcoholic with a tendency to scare women off with his breath, at this moment I felt comparable to him. 'Where shall we begin?' Raki asked, folding her hands in her lap. 'At the beginning?' Came my response, my fears masked by levity. 'Don't even try to play your games with me Josh, I'm not taking it, not today.' She shook her head for emphasis, as if she needed to, stubborn as that woman was I had known the second she was angry that my best bet was to run away screaming yet for some reason I wasn't running, oh the things I do for love. 'What's on your mind, dear?' I asked, dropping my bag onto the floor and pulling off my black hoody to expose the gray 'grunge movement' t-shirt I wore beneath, I flashed her my brightest smile to no avail, I should probably be running by this point. 'Don't try to persuade me with your sweet talk, that isn't going to work, not this time.' Her tone bore a hint of anger to it, between the tone of voice and the sparks flashing from her eyes I got the idea that she wasn't exactly ecstatic at this moment, go figure. My black Chuck's padded across the floor as I dropped onto my leather couch, leaning lazily against the armrest I glanced back up at her. 'What's up, Raki?' I asked, a look of bewilderment across my face. 'You tell me.' She said, her voice even sharper than before. 'Tell you what?' I inquired again, still unsure as to what she was talking about. 'I watched Atlantic City and Wrestlecade Josh, I saw the show, what was Ryusaki talking about?' Her dark skin was red with anger as she spoke. 'You know it's been a long weekend, Seattle. to Las Vegas, Las Vegas back to Seattle, I really don't have the energy to argue with you.' I recited in rebuttal to her question, it was a cop out...and she knew it. 'Don't try to play that game, I already warned you about playing games, I'm not going to take it.' Her mind was made, as was her point. Raising a hand to my face I brushed away a lock of shaggy black hair and pressed lightly against a bruise on my forehead, I winced in pain, stuck in thought for a moment before dropping my left hand back onto the couch and answering. 'Ryusaki was just...' I stopped midsentence, my golden tongue failing me. Isn't it typical? I can entertain 20,000 people every night yet can't say the hardest words in the world to my girlfriend. 'Josh, what was he talking about? He said you were a father, he said he had pictures to prove...something.' Her eyes were accusing as she looked at me and I couldn't return her gaze, choosing rather to turn my head and stare down at the pale blue carpet in our Seattle flat. 'Do you want answers?' I asked, my voice low and distant. 'Yes, I want the truth, Josh.' Raki answered in a demanding tone. 'You can't handle the truth!', I yelled, snapping my head up a cheesy grin across my face. I thought the easiest way to distract her would be with humor...sometimes I should keep my brilliant ideas to myself. 'This is no time for joking around Josh, I want answers and I want them right now...' Raki snarled, her lips curled in anger, her eyes flashing with rage, I paused momentarily thinking of a rather amusing joke about PMS before biting my tongue and looking for an answer as requested. 'Well, how much do you know, Ra?' I asked in a flat voice, hiding all of the emotion that dwelled within. 'As much as everyone else who watched Wrestlecade but obviously not as much as Ryusaki and that is a problem. At the Atlantic City show in Core he said that you were a father, Josh. You! A father?' She shook her head seemingly disappointed and angry at the same time. 'It's a long story, Raki, a long and painful story.' I said, with a grimace. 'I've got nothing but time.' She answered with impatience. How ironic. I sighed as I began my story. 'It all started in February of 2000...' ---- Her name was Rosa Castillo, she's the loving wife of my friend and mentor Jose Castillo, the man who taught me the art of lucha libre and led me along in my career, mentoring me to success and serving as my backbone oftentimes. She was a brown eyed Mexican beauty of 27, with a gorgeous body and soft skin, her hair smelled like vanilla and her lips tasted like cherries. Looking over at her I smiled, her eyes were closed as she slept, a cover pulled over her nude figure. Her skin was pressed against mine, she slept facing me, her right arm thrown over my torso holding me close. Jose was gone, to the bar again to celebrate the graduation of many students in his school of Lucha Libre. Not being a drinker I had opted to skip the cantina and stay home, little did I know how I would be celebrating my graduation: a night with Rosa Castillo, a fitting reward for any great task. I watched her breathe, her chest heaving beneath the sheets, her warm breath on my neck, her lips parted almost as if they were smiling despite her state of unconsciousness, I wondered what she was dreaming of and I hoped that it was me. Raising a hand to her face I gently caressed her cheek, smiling as I leaned in and kissed her softly as she slept. I rested my head against hers, sighing in exhausted satisfaction. I had hardly closed my eyes when she awoke, feeling her move I glanced over at her, peering into her eyes. She yawned and stretched her arms squeezing me close with the arm that was locked around my torso, I stroked her chin gently, kissing her again as she awoke. 'What time is it, Josh?' Rosa asked in a voice marked by sleep. Somewhere around 3 A.M.' I estimated. 'I should go.' Rosa answered, as she pulled the covers from her body. My eyes drifted to her figure again as I spoke. 'There's no need to be in such a hurry, you had a 15 minute cat nap, sure you aren't up for another tumble?' She smiled, laughed at the term I had used and then answered me. 'Josh, I would love another tumble as you so delicately put it, however, Jose will be home soon, I don't want to think of the consequences if he catches us.' 'Jose would never hurt you Rosa, he's a better man than that.' I said with the utmost confidence in my instructor. As I evaluated his character I almost felt a tinge of remorse for what I had just done with his wife, glancing back at Rosa's unclad body the remorse was quickly forgotten. 'I know that Josh, and I'm not worried about him hurting me. What worries me is what he would do to you, not physically but to your career. He has great influence in Mexico and could easily ruin any chance that you may have at being a Lucha Libre legend.' Her tone was sincere, her voice painfully truthful. She raised a hand to my face and rubbed it across my chin, she smiled and gave me a wink as she slid off of the bed and placed her feet on the floor below. 'I'm sorry that this night had to end, but rest assured, it won't be our last night together.' Rosa promised as she pulled on a pair of black shorts. 'I'll hold you to that promise.' I answered with a grin, watching as she shoved her head and arms through a white tank top. She chuckled, approaching the door she opened it but paused before making her exit. 'Goodnight Josh.' She turned, blew me a kiss and then pulled the door shut behind her. I smiled as I slumped back against the pillow, I ran my fingers through my sweaty hair as I stared up at the ceiling. Tonight was a good night, the first of many. ---- Raki shook her head in confusion. 'Wait, you tapped Rosa Castillo? Josh...how could you? Jose Castillo was your hero! He's been a major piece of the puzzle that you put together to reach greatness!' I hung my head in shame, refusing to look into Raki's angry eyes. I know, I know, don't give me this guilt trip, Ra, believe me, I've put myself through this a thousand times before.' 'I'm assuming that you wouldn't have told me about Rosa Castillo unless it had some connection to what Hiroshi Ryusaki said?' Raki asked in accusation. 'Yes, she's the mother.' I answered my voice low and cold. 'Yet now she's dead.' Raki's voice was quickly becoming livid. 'She died with Jose in that car wreck, and now what? Now your going to tell me that their son, 4 year old, Espen Castillo isn't really their son after all?' 'Yes, Jose isn't the father, I am. How many Mexican children have you seen with blue eyes, Ra?' I buried my face in my hands, stricken with guilt and remorse. 'You're the father...are you going to allow someone else to raise your child, just because you aren't man enough to admit to your own mistakes? Is that it Josh?' Raki's tone wasn't only one of anger but also of disappointment. 'No, Raki, I know what I have to do...' I responded, tears running down my face. 'Good, and maybe while you're growing up you'll learn how to make a relationship work.' Raki's voice was cold and hard, it matched her heart. 'Don't do this to me Raki, please, don't do this to me.', I pleaded, my eyes full of sorrow. 'You did it to yourself Josh, I didn't tell you to sleep with another mans wife, this is your fault Josh...all your fault.' Raki stood to her feet, turning on her heel as she walked to the bedroom. 'I'm sorry Raki, I know I should have told you but this isn't an easy thing for me to admit to myself, much less to anyone else.' I called after her, watching as she tromped away. 'Ever heard of a little thing called honesty Josh? It does wonders for relationships.' Raki snapped in biting sarcasm. 'Raki, what do you want me to say? I was 18 and stupid, I made a mistake.' I choked emotionally as I stood to my feet and followed her into the bedroom. 'Yeah? Well what's your excuse for not telling me? You're 23, stupid and made another mistake? Enough is enough Josh, all you ever do is make mistakes.' Raki's words were aimed directly at my heart and they met their mark with ease. 'I'm sorry, Raki, I wish you could feel how sorry I am.' My voice was cracked and trembling, the words fell off of my tongue. 'You know, if it helps, I'm sorry myself, Josh. I'm sorry that I wasted eight and a half months with such a loser. I thought you were someone Josh...I thought maybe you were something special. You had the sweetest words, the most romantic ideas, you seemed to be Mr. Perfect. I guess that was just a disguise and once the mask came off Mr. Perfect turned out to be a foolish little boy that doesn't have the heart to accept the consequences of his own actions.' Raki didn't look at me as she spoke, nor did I look at her, choosing instead to revert my gaze to the floor. I dropped into a seated position on the bed, burying my face in my hands as my heavy heart forced me to bow my head in shame. 'What do you want me to say Raki? Do you want me to say I'm sorry? Do you want me to acknowledge that I made a mistake?' My questions went ignored as she shrugged her shoulders. 'What do you want to do, Josh? We both know that it's always been about what you would rather do. Everything, everything Josh, every single thing...is about you.' Her words were cruel and to the point. 'Raki, I...' My words were interrupted as a pair of my own jeans hit me directly in the face before falling to the floor, raising my eyes I saw Raki standing in front of the closet in our bedroom, one by one she was pulling articles of clothing off of their hangers and throwing them to the floor. 'Ra, what are you doing?' I asked, captured by confusion. 'I'm helping you Josh, I'm helping you pack your things so that you can move out.' Her words hit me in the chest and I slid off of the bed and fell to my knees, looking up at her, begging for mercy. 'Don't do this, don't do this to me...please...' My eyes were pleading, yet she failed to notice. 'I've already told you Josh, you were the one that did it to yourself, this is your fault...don't even try to make me feel guilty for doing the right thing.' She said, as she continued to discard items from my closet, choosing to deposit them on the floor in a heap of designer clothing. Tears rolled from my eyes as I stared at her, her features were cold, far from the beautiful young woman I knew and loved, our eyes met for a moment, mine flooded with tears as hers flashed with sparks of anger, then she turned her head away in obvious disappointment. 'Who are you, Josh?' Raki mused, her eyes now focused on the floor, her anger slowly fading. 'What...what are you talking about?' I stammered, as I looked up at her, our eyes met again and this time she held my gaze. Something in her eyes had changed, the anger had vanished and had been replaced by sorrow, and the weak and bitter look of a woman betrayed. 'I thought I knew you Josh, you were so sweet, so kind, sure, you were rough around the edges but you were a diamond that could be polished...or so I thought. I never expected this from you Josh, I never thought you would hide something from me...lie to me...' Raki shook her head, her eyes full of stunned disbelief, the anger was slowly dying away as reality set in. 'I'm sorry, Raki, I wanted to tell you...' I whimpered, bowing my head in humble submission. 'No you didn't, you didn't want to belive it, you were afraid to face the hauntings of your past. You wanted to pretend nothing had happened and it would all go away, now you're paying for it Josh.' Raki said, the edge gone from her voice. She knelt down in front of me and reached down, taking my right hand in her left, with her right hand she pulled my chin up so that our eyes met. 'I love you Josh, I love you so much.' Raki gasped painfully as she looked deep within my soul. 'I know you made a mistake...but it was a tragic mistake.' 'Don't do this Raki, I love you...' I winced, between clenched teeth and a multitude of tears. I know, I know you do, but this isn't right.' Raki shook her head slowly before forcing a weak smile onto her face, leaning in she kissed me gently on the lips. I closed my eyes, drifting away to heaven for a moment as I returned her soft embrace, my hand squeezing hers. She pulled away as I opened my eyes, the kiss had been sweet albeit brief and somehow I knew it was a moment to remember, it would be our last kiss. 'Josh, I love you, but we both know...this is the end for us.' Raki patted my hand softly as she spoke to my breaking heart. 'It's just...it's too much Josh.' I tried to speak but the words could not escape my lead tongue, somewhere along the way they were trapped behind my trachea, refusing to leap from my heart and cut into the dead Seattle air, as a response I only nodded, slowly but fervently. 'I'm going to leave you alone for a few hours Josh, but when I come back...I want you out of here, out of my apartment, out of my life.' Raki said gently as she rose to her feet. I closed my eyes as she walked away, her footsteps led her through the bedroom door and into the living room, there was a brief moment of silence before I heard the doorknob turn and then the sound of a door closing behind her. I sighed, bowing my head in deep remorse, she was gone and it was all my fault. ---- 5:12 AM. May 23rd, 2005. SoHo, NY. The sound of a ringing doorbell snapped an exhausted Gunner Addison from her sleep, she shook her head wearily, listening again as the doorbell rang for a second time. 'Ken...' She mumbled, poking the Superstar in the ribs in an attempt to awake him. 'Mmmm?' Was all he could muster from his dazed stupor of sleep. 'Ken, wake up, someone is at the door.' Gunner mumbled, barely awake. 'Who would be at the door at this hour of the night?' Ken asked, lazily, not wanting to crawl out of his warm and comfortable bed. 'I don't know, but you should go check.' Gunner prodded Ken, as he reluctantly kicked back the covers and slid out from beneath them clad in only a pair of silk boxers, pulling a t-shirt over his torso Ken glared at the digital green numbers from his alarm clock - 5:13 AM. 'I swear if it's another fan that found my address...' Ken mumbled with a yawn, his idle threat falling only upon his own ears as Gunner stole his spot in the bed. Ken opened the door to their bedroom and flipped the light switch on, illuminating the stairwell, Ken squinted his eyes, blocking out the bright lights as he grasped the guardrail and descended the stairs. His black and red hair was a mess, his baby blue eyes filled with the evidence of sleep, yet who could blame him, he hadn't been expecting company. Ken stopped at the bottom of the stairs, crossing the floor and pulling open the door, he wasn't afraid of a burglar, he knew he could easily take care of them, what brought fear to Ken's heart was a messenger, someone who would bear bad news. Since Josh's incident in Rome, Ken had feared for his young friend's safety and often prayed himself to sleep with his brother in arms on his heart. Ken opened the door... ---- ...and there he stood. My best friend in the World, my partner, my brother, the one and only: 'Superstar' Ken Partridge Jr. Ken was a far cry from his usual GQ self, dressed in only scooby doo boxers and a plain white t-shirt, Ken's eyes were fixed on me, they met my eyes before sweeping my whole body and stopping at the suitcases that rested by my feet. 'Josh...' Ken began, suddenly awake. ...what's going on, are you okay?' I shook my head, as I stepped inside, dropping my suitcases on the floor. 'No, I'm not okay.' 'What happened?' Ken asked, in legitimate concern, as he closed the door behind me and waved me into the living room. 'I don't know, Ken...I don't know...' I replied in a state of emotional exhaustion. We left the suitcases behind as Ken led me through the living room and into the kitchen, he pointed to a bar stool and motioned for me to sit, I obliged. Ken in return, leaned back against the counter his eyes studying me. 'Tell me Josh, what exactly are you doing here in the middle of the night? Why aren't you in Seattle, where's Raki?' Ken asked with a puzzled expression. 'Gone.' Was the best answer I could provide. 'She's gone, she's out of my life, Ken.' 'I'm sorry to hear that...' Ken answered with the deepest of sympathy. 'Are you going to be alright?' 'I don't know.' I answered, raising a shaking hand to scratch at the whiskers that had began to emerge across my face. 'Ok, just sit back and relax, I'll stir up some coffee and then we'll kick back and you can tell good ol' KPJ what happened, deal? Ken smiled, as he looked at me, his smile brought a rush of relief. 'Deal.' I said with a weak nod. <>'Ken, who is it?' Came a voice from the bottom of the stairwell, as the ever lovely Gunner Addison emerged wrapped in a silver silk robe, her blonde hair falling in all directions. 'It's Josh, he decided to drop in on us.' Ken answered with a smile. Gunner's eyes fell upon me as a smile came to her face. 'Hey hun, what brings you all the way from Seattle in the middle of the night?' I glanced up at Gunner, wishing I could return her smile, I gave it a shot but failed miserably so I opted to nod instead. 'Hi Gun, it's...it's quite the story, really.' Her eyes studied me for a moment as she approached Ken and I. 'Must be quite the story, you don't look exactly enthralled, what's up Josh?' Gunner asked, knowing me all too well. 'I had just asked him the same thing, we're going to discuss it over a cup of Joe.' Ken said, as he began the task of making coffee. 'Are you okay, champ?' Gunner asked, with a friendly smile and a sisterly hug. 'I...I don't know Gun...I really don't.' I answered in painful honesty. She pursed her lips, her face taking on a look of concern as she shared a look with Ken, each seeming to be able to read the others mind. 'What happened Josh?' Gunner asked, as she pulled up a stool across from me. 'Everything.' Was the best answer I could provide as I shook my head. 'C'mon Josh, you're going to worry us sick, what's going on?' Ken asked as he finished his duties with the coffee machine and turned his focus back to me. With the eyes of both Ken and Gunner locked onto me, I bowed my head, finding it hard to raise my eyes to meet theirs. 'Raki and I broke up, she told me to leave...we're through, I had nowhere else to go. I'm sorry for waking you...' I started to apologize but Ken quickly cut me off. 'Don't be sorry Josh, I'm glad you're here, you don't need to be alone, not at a time like this.' Ken said, concern emitting from his voice. 'Yes, we're both happy to see you, even if it is on such an ugly occasion.' Gunner echoed. 'Josh, you know that our home is your home, you're more than welcome to come here at anytime, and even if that time isn't so pleasant...so be it.' Ken said in kindness. 'You guys are the best.' I answered, raising my head and flashing a weak smile at them. 'This isn't just our home Josh, it's yours too.' Gunner said, reaching over and taking Ken's hand in hers. 'Welcome home, Josh, welcome home.' Said Ken, a twinkle of joy in his eyes. I was touched as I looked across the bar at the beaming couple, this was home... Home, sweet home.
