The Tragedy of Mike Storm Part I

8 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Tragedy of Mike Storm Part I Mike Storm Global Wrestling Promo #2083 Date: 06/08/05 Location: Dallas, Texas It has been a long time since the fans and even employees of GW have heard the name 'Amazing' Mike Storm, in fact it has probably been nearly as long as the name has been thought of by anyone in association with GW. Mike Stom has come and gone in GW, without leaving much of an impression, and without much true success. Only brief time of glory for Storm in GW was early on in his time there, when he held the Cruiserweight title. This was all a long time ago though, Storm's GW career since then has been less then memorable to say the least. Now Storm making his return to GW, as a man with something to prove. With the man finally realize his potential, or will this be another failed and forgotten run..... only time will tell. Scene: It is the lobby of a hotel, the lobby of a rather nice hotel at that, it is in a suburb of Dallas, Texas. GW personality Steven Dinkler is in the lobby talking to the female receptionist. After a few moments he thanks the receptionists and motions for the camera to follow him as he heads towards the stairs. He walks up two flights of stairs, and then at the top of the stairs he turns left. He passes by a few doors, and then stops in front of room 350. Steven Dinkler turns and looks into the camera. Steven Dinkler: I am here outside the hotel room of former, and now returning GW competitor, 'Amazing' Mike Storm. The deal for his return was recently announced, and it came as a surprise to many in the GW world. Now there is much that the fans and others want to know about what is going on. So now I will get the first words with Storm since his signing was announces. Scene: Steven Dinkler turns his back to the camera now and proceeds to knock on the door to room 350. After a few seconds pass the door is opened, and standing there is an older and rather short man, he is balding, the only hair remaining is gray and around the sides of his head. The man wears a white shirt, it his short sleve, and had had a collar. It is one of those polo shirts with the three buttons, the symbol of the company that made it on the left side. The man also wears a nice pair of khakis with dress shoes that match well. He looks up and down at Steven Dinkler, as if sizing him up. Then he speaks softly. Older Man: Are you from GW? Scene: Steven Dinkler nods yes, with a slight look of confusion on his face. The man steps aside and uses his hand to point Steven Dinkler in. Dinkler walks in to find a rather large man sitting down in the room at the table. He holds a glass filled with a brownish liquid, next to the glass is a bottle of Wild Turkey whiskey with a little bit missing. The man looks up at Dinkler, then kicks the other chair lightly, from under the desk, offering the seat. Dinkler now sits down hesitantly, seeming very confused about what was occuring. The older man sits down on one of the two beds in the room. The large man sips the whiskey before clearing his throat to speak. Large Man: You look, a little confused Mr. Dinkler. Steven Dinkler: Well I was expecting to speak with Mike Storm, and I have no idea who either of you are. Large Man: Mike Storm is on his way, but you are going to speak with me, you can simply call me Soultaker, I am a close friend of Storm's. Steven Dinkler: Soultaker, I have heard the name before, you use to be a world champion, I think it was in a place called SWF. Soultaker: Hmmm, good to see I have not been completely forgotten. However my career is long over, I am retired and probably never coming back. Steven Dinkler: Well is Storm going to be here? Soultaker: Soon enough, he is on his way back from Mexico, working his last engagement there. Anything you need to ask though, you can ask me. Steven Dinkler: What is your interest in Mike Storm resurrecting his career in GW? Soultaker: Apart from being his life long friend, I am his new agent. I am responsible for his return to GW. Storm had a falling out with his old agent, among a plethora of other personal problems he has faced, and I am the only person he can trust. Steven Dinkler: Well Mike Storm had a few unsuccessful runs in GW, what is it about this time that you think will make things any different? Soultaker: There is a lot of stuff about Mike Storm that no one out side of those few people close to him knows. Storm has always kept his personal life a secret. However I have spoken to him and he has given me the okay to explain on some of the things that have, and things that still do haunt him, it also explains where he has been and why he has been gone. Where would you like to begin? Steven Dinkler: Well where has he been and why has he been gone? Soultaker: Well Storm had some personal demons, and had some troubles with a few things, but at about this time last year, Storm's life was finally coming together. He was working in Japan, and was about to go one on one for one of the biggest titles in Japan. He was engaged to be married to the woman whom he had a three year old son with, the woman who he had been on and off with for years. Everything seemed right in his world, until that day. Scene: Soultaker pauses and sips his whiskey once more. Steven Dinkler looks on seeming very interested, as Soultaker refills his glass. Then he continues. Soultaker: It was the week that he was set to go one on one for the title, his fiancee and son were coming to watch Storm hopefully realize his dream. Storm was unable to pick them up himself because of his trianing schedule, so he arranged for them to be picked up. Then Storm received word in the middle of his training session that there had been an accident, and both his fiancee and son were killed in. Storm's life quickly fell apart, he no showed the match, and when he was back in the United States, he went on a drinking binge and eventually attempted to commit suicide. He was ordered to see professional help, which is where our friend Doctor Alexander Warren comes in. Scene: Soultaker holds up his glass and points to the older man who still sits on the bed. Dinkler looks at the man. Steven Dinkler: So is Mike Storm even emotionally able to compete, is he stable enough? Scene: Doctor Warren looks over at Soultaker as if requesting his approval, Soultaker nods and sips his whiskey once more. Doctor Warren stands up and paces as he speaks. Doctor Warren: When Mike Storm came to me, he was basically a wreck, and he was very prone to violent outbursts. After a few months we were able to fine something that was the ultimate therapy for Storm. That was a return to the ring. So he did so, he has been wrestling in Mexico since February. His style has changed though, Storm lives now only for his sport. He has become more violent in the ring, more daring even. He does moves now that he would have never attempted in the past, and his extreme tendacies have shown through. He has become the most well known names in Mexico once again. Now he is so dedicated to this sport, it really is his only reason to continue living. Soultaker: You see Mr. Dinkler, I know many may question the work ethic of Mike Storm, and the legitimacy of his return. Well there is no denying the talent of Mike Storm, he is in the best shape of his life, and now has a fire lit underneath him like he has never had before. This time around the people are going to see what Mike Storm can do. He looks to recapture some of the brief glory he held hear in GW when he was a cruiserweight champion. Scene: The phone rings, and Soultaker rises to answer it, as Doctor Warren takes his seat on the bed once again. Soultaker speaks briefly on the phone and hangs it up. Soultaker: Storm has arrived and he is waiting for us in the lobby. Come on, we have said enough for now, Storm wanted me get through this part since he felt unable to speak of the incident anymore. The rest you can ask of him yourself. I am sure you have much to talk about with him, but first we are gonig to go eat, once Storm is officially set to return, you will have your chance. Now let's go. Scene: Soultaker opens the door as both Dinkler and Doctor Warren rise up. They head the door of room 350, and Soultaker follows them, closing the door behind him. Fade to Black
