He flipped another switch inside before closing the pack up. “Bomb’s all set.” From my position on the ground, I saw a red light come on the laptop out of corner of my eye. “Aight, Deac. Get the hell outta there before Vincent’s boys get there. Don’t want you getting it early in the fight.” “Yeah, yeah, fuck you too.” I got off the ground and cleared the distance to the Vanquish, jumping in, foot on the clutch, cranking the engine and sliding off in a cloud of dust. I worked on the gearshift, going in and out of gears, negotiating turns, and slamming my way through traffic. On the laptop, I saw that the bogus timer was running. If they disarmed the timer, the bomb would still blow by ways of the tripwire. If not, there’s always the handy dandy remote trigger. I kept the windows down, listening for the telltale signs of an explosion. Nothing. Two minutes passed from the time I left the grassy knoll till I arrived at the back of the Vincent Estate. Deac was already there, waiting. “What took you so fucking long?” he asked, a playful scowl burning on his face. “Well, I couldn’t just turn the corner, now could I?” “You still could’ve gotten here sooner.” I give Deac one of my blank stares. “Quitcherbitchin”.” D stares back at me, his jaw slightly loose. “Quitcherbitchin?” QUITCHERBITCHIN?! Oh, hell naw I know you just did not tell me to quit my bitching. What the fuck, man. Here we are, trying to save your damn chick, and you tell me to shut the fuck up? Man, I should just go home and go back to bed. This shit ain’t worth it.” “And miss the chance to repel down this wall?” I asked him, holding a rope out to him. He snatches the rope out of my hand. “Damn you and your sneaky white boy tactics.” I tossed him a black satchel and threw another one over my shoulder. We went up and over the wall without a hitch, landing at the base of the fifteen-foot brick wall. I turned and looked at Deac. “Okay, here’s the game plan. I want you to take those explosives and plant them in the basement of the house. While you do that, I’m gonna go find Vic.” “And Vincent?” I look at Deac, the world turning red, a cold hard stare on my face. “Motherfucker is mine.”
