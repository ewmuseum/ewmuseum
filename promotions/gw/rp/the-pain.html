Date: Unknown Home wasn’t a place he visited that often anymore, and so they decided to meet somewhere else. Outside, as the cold winds carried dust from the dry landscape across the empty downtown streets he was watching for his visitor. The message had put him into a very dark mood, and when he looked like he was brooding and angry people tended to run away from him. The cup had no handle, and when he lifted it to take another gulp of the Kenyan roast his hand was burned slightly. He simply kept taking a big drink of the coffee with cream and Sugar In The Raw. He set the cup back down. The pain reminds you that you’re still alive? From around the corner, he came walking out. The black trench coat and his long black hair were flowing out behind him as the wind seemed determined to whip him relentlessly as he cross Elm Street. No one else was outside, preferring the interior of Caf? Brazil where they were protected from the weather. Xavier Black stepped over the black, wrought iron fence. He pulled the steel chair out from the round table, turned it around and sat down. “I told you never to say that name to me again.” Jake said flatly as he stared at his brother. Xavier shrugged, “Youngest siblings are often the most rebellious because they’re allowed to get away with the most.” “I’m not in the mood for this.” Jake said threateningly. He didn’t want to keep rehashing what they had done in Japan, “What does that son of a bitch have to do with us anymore” He’s in the wind.” “Not anymore.” Jake looked up from behind his wall of hair, “What do you mean?” “We kept looking for the artifact, the bottle. Pitt swore he had a lead or two to find it. He thought he found some proof that Veston had sent it back to a friend here in the States. Pitt tracked him down, and he found the bottle.” Xavier was smiling coldly. “So we have the bottle, and if the shipment comes into Houston as planned then we’re all set. Once Robert is able to translate everything we’ll know how to beat Sid, but there’s something else.” “Veston?” Jake said hopefully. “Veston.” “Tell me.” “The guy working with Veston was someone he knew since he was a kid. Jamal immigrated here, and they stayed in touch.” Xavier remembered all the pressure they had put on Jamal. “We kept tabs on him, and finally figured out what was going on. Veston had shipped it to him to store, and he seemed like the only one who could have hidden him.” “Where’s he at?” Jake didn’t care about the details. He just wanted to know where the man was hiding. “We haven’t confirmed he’s there.” “But you have an idea!” “I do.” Xavier looked around briefly, “And to be sure, I already have SSR looking around for us. He’ll verify that he’s there, and if he is then he’ll call you. After Fusion, you can make a trip back East.” “Back East where?” “Fine, be anxious if you want. They have arranged an extended stay at a hotel in Herndon, Virginia. The Hyatt right by Dulles. He’s got a suite there, and if he is there then you can walk right in and say hello. There’s something I want to know Jake.” Xavier was eyeing his brother intently. “What’s that?” “What are you going to do if you find him?” Jake thought about it. If they hadn’t sniffed out Veston’s plans in Japan, then he would have killed them all. Veston was a sellout in the worst way. He had betrayed everything he had ever known, and Jake wanted to know why. “When I find him, I just don’t know what I’m going to do. I can think about it here, and speak logically about what should happen. I can speak rationally about my anger toward him, but none of that matters. I can’t say exactly what I would do if and when I stare into his eyes Xavier.” Jake finished his coffee. He threw the cup aside, watching the white porcelain shatter into pieces on the dirty concrete of the patio. “Did I tell you about the dreams Xavier?” “No, what dreams?” Xavier saw the distance their in his eyes. “I keep seeing John in my dreams Xavier, like he’s stalking me for not finishing this already. He keeps attacking me, coming after me and yelling at me. I can’t shake him, but that’s not the part that bothers me.” Jake stood up. Xavier was worried about his brother, “What is?” “In the end, when he’s choking me with those slick, blood covered hands around my throat I’m almost gone. I have that one last sight before the darkness closes in around me forever.” Jake stepped right next to his brother. He put his wide hand down on his shoulder, “That’s when I realize I’ve been looking into my own face Xavier. I keep seeing myself. I don’t think I’m ever going to get past all this’never.” Jake walked past his brother, pushing open the swinging gate with a creak. He walked off into the dusty air, ignoring the wind as it bit into his barely protected side. The pain reminds you that you’re still alive?
