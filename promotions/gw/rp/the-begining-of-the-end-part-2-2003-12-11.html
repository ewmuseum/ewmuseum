The Begining of The End ( part 2 )

11 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/11/03 The car stopped, and as it did, I rapidly opened the door, and ran as fast as I could from it. I stopped myself at the front desk, frantically asking where my love had been placed. The old lady who waited on me, was slow with her response to my question, and I quickly became irritated by her. I was a hysterical mess to begin with, and now this lady was taking her sweet time, as the woman I love was somewhere in this hospital, lying in agony, or possibly worse. I stood impatiently looking side to side over and over, and tapping my fingers on the desk top. Finally I could take no more. ' WILL YOU HURRY THE FUCK UP AND TELL ME WHERE SHE IS?!' The old woman was taken back by the anger and force behind my words, I could tell by the way she softly gasped, and leaned back when I spoke. My words however did do the trick, as he hurrily thumbed through the files. Finally she pulled out a file, and briefly read it over before looking up at me. ' Sir, she is on the sixth floor, the intensive care unit. You will have to register up there, to be able to visit her.' I lowered my head, and took in a deep breath, before rushing to the elevator and making my way up to the sixth floor. The elevator trip was short, and before I had time to gather my thoughts on what I would say, how I would react, the door opened. I walked out of the small elevator and was greeted by Star's sister Lara. She grasped me tightly in her arms and began to sob uncontrollably. I wrapped my arms around her, but held back my emotions. I let Lara cry for a few moments before I gently pulled myself, and pushed her away. She began to wipe her eyes with a small tissue she pulled from her left pant pocket. ' Where do I sign in at, and how soon can I see her?' Those were my only words. Those words were hard enough for me to get out of my body, let alone try to say anything else. And quite frankly the only thing I wanted to do was see her. Lara pointed me in the direction of another desk. I walked over with her, and signed in. Together we walked down a long corridor, with glass windows, peering into each occupants room. At the end of the long hallway was where we stopped. Lara and I stood there together looking in at the lovely woman we both cared about lying in a bed, with tons of tubes and wires sticking into, and out of her at various parts of her body. I took in a long, deep breath. Lara patted my back before leaning in and whispering something into my ear. ' I'll give you two some time alone.' I nodded, and she walked back down the long corridor. I could hear her begin to sob once again. I watched her walk away until she entered back into the waiting lobby. I then turned, and braced myself for what was to come next. Slowly, step by step, I walked into her room, and made my way over towards her bed. My left hand shot up and brushed down the front of my nose and over my mouth. I took a seat next to her, and gently took her hand and began to caress it with my hands. I wanted to speak to her, tell her how I felt, and what I was thinking. But I was unable to spit the words out. My mind and my body would not allow me to utter a single word. So for two hours I sat there, holding her hand, and watching her fight for her life. Lara eventually showed up, and wanted to spend some time with her sister. The walk out of the room, and back down the long hallway felt like walking down death row. There was just that feeling to it, that being there was not right. The sliding door slid open, and I made my way back into the waiting lobby. I turned and and gazed back down the long, endless hallway, and my eyes began to swell with tears. Just as I was about to begin a sob session of my own, a soft, warm hand slid across my shoulders. Slightly startled I turned to see, her smiling at me. ' Everything is going to be ok.' I smiled back at her, and nodded in response. ' Thank you.' She shot me a confused look, and cocked her head to the right. ' For what?' I smirked to myself, and shook my head. ' For everything.'
