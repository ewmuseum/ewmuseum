Changing the Game

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ok... She want's to play? Let's play. That was the extent of any thought that was breezing through Train's mind at the moment. 'Fucked' by KRS-One was pulsing through the system in the Navigator as it glids smoothly down the Dallas Street. Considering all that was around him, all the agression might seem miss placed to anyone that was privy to the anger pulsating through his mind at this moment. Donny J. McNasty? Still digging out that piece of Train's shoe that got broke off in his monkey ass. Christina McCoy? Jumped up and got smacked down with an injunction. Untouchable. That's what Train was. He'd gone into one of the biggest pay per view events of the year and handled his fucking business. Piss on anyone who says otherwise. The haters couldn't help, but tell the truth. Train was on a roll. Hell, it's even a nice day out, although it's quickly turning to night. The sun, now and soft orange, turns the sky along the horizon into a rainbow of pink and purple as it prepairs to withdraw from the Texas sky. Normally he would be at home chillin'. Probably taking that customary nap you take on club nights so you still have energy when you go out to celebrate. That was the plan. It had been all good not an hour earlier. The win, the pissed off boss.... How could it get better? That is until he got the call. Until he found out the booking for Evolution. Little bitch. She thinks she's fucking slick. Like Train wouldn't know what the deal was. What she was trying to pull. He could smell this one from a mile away. She thinks if she gives her boy the home field advantage he's just gonna wipe Train out. Fuck all that. It was time for Train to be the Dogg he is. Time to go pull out the hose and go mark his territory. And his territory? Where ever the hell he needed to be when he needed to be there. The Navigator slows and pulls into a gravel parking lot in front of the shit hole of a building. The engine and the music shut off a split second later and Train hops out. The door slams and the electronic beep of the car alarm engaging cuts through the air. Train looks over to the large sign at the entrance of the parking lot to confirm that he was in the right place. Yup. 'The Darkhorse Saloon'. Time to let 'em know. They wanna play games? It's all good. Train plays for keeps. It's a scene right out of a Sergio Leone flick as Train pushes the swinging doors of the saloon in, his muscular frame silohetted against the sunset. The whole room seemed to pause for a moment to stare at the figure in the doorway. If there had been a tumbleweed blowing through that shit would've been tight. But, from the looks of this dump, they couldn't even afford a regular weed. The dark, wooden floor boards creek under Train's feet as he steps into the room, the saloon doors flapping like a loud pair of birds wings behind him. The place seemed somewhat dim and dingy in his mind. No doubt this was a damn redneck magnet. Don't matter though. If Train had to fight up in this bitch Monday, this joint was under new management as of now. Train quickly scopes the room and makes his move. He saunters up to the bar and motions over the grizzled looking character behind the bar. 'Let me get a Michelob,' Train says plainly. 'Bud or Miller,' the man grunts back. 'We don't do that fancy pants shit here.' 'Oh... My bad, I didn't know Michelob was so exotic.' 'Miller or Bud young buck?' 'Fine. Let me get a Miller Genuine Draft.' The bartender has this place down to a science, quickly reaching into the cooler just below the bar and retrieving a bottle of MGD. He effortlessly pops the cap off the ice cold bottle, water condensing on the side as the cool mist drifting up from the open top, and slides it to Train. Train doesn't need an invitation. He quickly grabs the bottle and takes a few sips of his beer. Train scopes the scene again, getting a lay of the land. As he looks around he spots something interesting. Taking another sip of his brew Train walks towards the middle of the bar. Small, circular tables fill in what would normally be a large, open space between all the boothes that line the walls. Along the ceiling large wooden beams cut a large, square grid pattern over the bar. Train pushes one of the little tables off to the side as he looks up to middle square on the cieling. This is what caught his eye. Just inside the middle square another square, this one made ow what looks to be steel or iron, is built into the cieling. Train just stares at it for a while before looking down at the space the metal square encompasses. Train walks the square as he sips his beer. The man behind the bar looking a little irritated at the disturbance of his floor plan. 'Son, what in the blue hell are you doing?' he questions gruffly. Train just turns back at him for a moment, cutting his eyes before going right back to what he was doing. 'First off, I ain't yo son, son. Second... Is this it? This the spot where the cage goes? Where is it?' 'What cage?' the older man responds. 'There ain't no cage there.' 'Oh, no shit?' Train says consciously letting his irritation shine through. 'No shit.' 'Uhuh... It used to be here, right?' 'Used to be. Not any more.' 'Man, I don't know why you just tending bar in this dump man. Hell, you oughta be President with that talent to state the obvious and shit. So what, they just slide into these grooves in the ceiling or something?' 'Sounds about right.' Train takes a few more sips of his beer. He starts to bounce on the balls of his feet like a boxer. It's no surprise that he draws a fuew stares for the other patrons. Even the barkeep, who's been doing his best to ignore Train (and about everyone else from the looks of it), has no choice but to pay attention. 'What in the hell are you doing?' the barkeep bristles. 'Getting to know the space. Nothing you need to be worried about.' Train responds in a tone indicating his dissatisfaction with the old man's attitude. 'Well I think it is my business when you're in here jumping around like a retarded jack rabbit. Do you know what you look like?' Train stops and turns to him, meeting his eyes with an icy glare. 'I know what you're gonna look like in a minute. Hamburger. Shut the fuck up.' Train states plainly. 'You're gonna have a hard time with that...son. Seeing as I could just call security and have them toss your ass outta here.' the old man spits back defiantly. 'You do you playa. but I gotta warn you. You do you what you gotta do and I'm gonna have to do the same. I don't give a damn if this is McCoy's shit. Fuck that Braxton chump too. I ain't leaving till I know exactly what I'm looking at on Monday. Period.' There is a sudden realization for the grizzled barkeep, the wave of knowledge sweeps over his face as he breaks out in a chuckle. 'Hahahaha.... Oh, I get it. So you're him. That fella that thinks he's a piece of public transportation. What's it... Bus... Trolley...' 'It's Train muthafucka and keep talkin' shit. You'll find out the hard way why they call me that when I run yo punk ass down.' 'Yeah, that's it. Train... Hell, I should've remembered. You been quite the topic of discussion around here son. Christina's looking for your head on a platter. And Braxton? hahahaha... I wouldn't wanna be in your shoes there tough guy. The way you been treating that girl? You ought not have done what ya' did. Ely is the man to cut it off and by a new platter to put it on.' 'Oh, he said all that?' 'Oh no. No, he said a hell of a lot worse. Says he wants to have a 'friendly talk' with you before hand commences to whuppin' your ass. Now I only seen that one time before when Ely was mad. That boy spent a week in the hospital after that conversation was over with. You in for a world of hurt boy.' Train quickly closes the distance and slams the beer down on the bar, sending the foam boiling over the lip of the bottle. 'Let me tell you something you redneck son of a bitch. You call me boy one more fucking time and I'm gonna start callin' you ho'. That way I got a reason to smack yo bitch ass like you owe me money! Punk! As for conversatin', fuck all that! Actions speak louder than words chump! Fuck Braxton! I don't give a fuck what he has to say, but you can tell that muthafucka that I said.... 'Just keep running your mouth and in a few minutes you'll be able to tell him in person.' a woman's voice, not yelling, but speaking loudly enough to be heard interjects. Train stops midsentence, rolling his eyes, a sneer forming on his face the instant he hears her. He knows exactly who it is. This bitch here... She's one of them that's always got to have the last word. Wasn't no doubt she was smart, but at the same time she's about the dumbest person around. Her problem? She thinks she knows it all. Worse yet in this situation? She thinks she understands the opponent she's locked in this battle of wills with. Wrong. She hasn't got a clue. Come Evolution she's going to learn though. Everyone is going to learn. Train equals street... Street equals Train... Street does not equal stupid. Train turns and his eyes meet with Christina McCoys. Her tableau is at least as impressive as Train's was. She stands in her tailored business suit, hands on hips, flanked on each side by to large and rather surly looking bodyguards. Despite being cut off at the knees at about every turn by this 'common' street thug over the past few weeks, she still stares at Train with a smug grin on her face. She knows she has the upper hand this time. And, the way she's set it up, it's not going to be any different come Monday. Christina strolls over slowly, confidence echoing in every tap of her high heels on the wooden floor. Train turns to her, though his eyes vove around the room. The wheel is almost visible as it turns in his head, calculating his odds in this confrontation. Two bodyguards trailing close behind Christina... plus the bartender, probably got a bat or something under the bar... mutiplied by all the evil eyes shooting at him from about everyone one of these other drunk rednecks in the joint... Nope. Not the time or the place. Bitch probably thinks it's gonna be just the same come Monday. Well, she can think what she wants, but she's gonna find out the truth... Train's got somethin' fo' that ass. Christina finally stops about 5 feet from Train, who finally turns his full attention back to her. 'Why are you here? Can't you go anywhere without harrasing someone or another?' Christina inquires, the smug grin still firmly in place. 'I ain't harrasing nobody. I'm just trying to get some stuff cleared up.' Train responds firmly. 'Like what?' 'Like the fact that you ain't fooling me with this little match you set up from Evolution. I know what you trying to pull.' 'Is that so?' 'Damn right. I'm like 2-Can Sam up in this joint. I always follow my nose. But see, instead of Fruit Loops, you know what I smell? A fucking set up! You think I don't know what's going down. You think I don't know what you want to go down in this Bar Cage Brawl thing you got planned? I mean shit, it ain't like it was hard. See I had to ask myself. What's up with this shit, you know? Here I am, 2 matches, 2 wins. Ain't nobody even come close to beating me. I been whuppin ass on a regular since day one. McNasty stepped up twice, got beat down twice. Hell, ain't nobody even heard from Kid Wikkid after I broke a piece of my shoe off in his monkey ass.' 'Any other promotion on earth, you leave that many bodies on the ground and they start talking about setting you up some high profile shit, with some high profile cats... Some damn main eventers. Oh, but not me. Naw... Instead here I am put in a match with some 'never was' that ain't hardly did shit since I been in this joint. Add to that shit that this is all supposed to go down in your bar. Yeah... All that shit's real convenient. See at first I was just thinking I was being paranoid and shit. Till I heard about yalls little...relationship. Yeah... I heard. Braxton gets to be your punk ass knight in shining armor while you stack the odds against his opponent to help ol' Ely get back in a title hunt while you set me up to lose. Damn... Guess bangin' the boss has it's benefits, huh?' The sound of flesh on flesh crackles through the air as Christina's hand connects with the side of Train's face. His head snaps to the side and freezes there. It would seem to be a losing exchange, but the two combatants in this little battle have changed positions. Christina's face is now the one distorted by a mean snear while Train's is occupied by a sly grin. 'You son of a bitch. How dare you...' she says in a mixture of anger, hate and embarassment. 'Brotha hit a little nerve, did he?' 'Don't you ever speak to me like that again. Or about Ely like that again. He's a good friend. One of the only ones when I needed one and we are just that... friends! And he hardly needs me to stack anything to kick your ass.' 'Yeah, whatever... Bottom line of this shit is... You know why ain't neither one of yall gonna be able to get over on me? Cuz you think you know me. You think cuz you know where I come from that you know how I must think. Let me be real clear. You don't know shit. You don't know shit about me, and you don't know shit about where I come. See you think you dealing with some little small timer who got busted every ten minutes. But see I ain't the petty criminal you think you got on the line. I'm a Thug. The thing about a thug is... Just when you think you know the rules? I change the fucking game. You can tell yo' friend that jsut happens to be a boy... I'll be seeing him Monday. haha.... I know you think you slick and all, but ahhh.... You gotta ask yourself....' Train turns back to the bar and turns up his bottle of beer, finishing it off in a few gulps. He slams the empty back down to the bar. he slowly strolls up until he's parralel with Christina, each looking in the opposite direction. 'Who's really setting up who?' With that Train strolls out the door as Christina cuts her eyes suspiciously at the question while the scene fades to black.
