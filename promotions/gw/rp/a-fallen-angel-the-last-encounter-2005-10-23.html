A Fallen Angel: The Last Encounter

23 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 23, 2005 Mike Biddle’s recent encounter with Jake Hix landed him in the hospital. After Evolution Mike was airlifted to a hospital in Los Angeles, luckily the result of the beating was only a few bruises and a wounded ego. Biddle was kept overnight and released the next afternoon. Before his release Mike learned that he was to face Hix at Dark Rituals with an International Title shot on the line, the very title shot Mike had already earned the night he collected Zenith’s bounty. Mike knew that his chances of beating Hix in his current condition were nonexistent, however, there was something he could re-acquire that would tip the odds in his favor: his lucky wrestling gear. His lucky wrestling gear was the very gear he had worn the night he beat Fathom for the PTC Extreme Championship, he had also worn the gear during other highlights of his storied career. To make a long story short, Biddle was undefeated while wearing his lucky gear. The only problem was that the gear was at Hillary Watson’s apartment in West Hollywood. She had asked for the gear as a memento of their relationship a few months back, Mike was intoxicated at the time and agreed to let her keep his sacred garments. So when Mike left the hospital and got into the limousine that was to take him home he ordered the driver to first take him to Hillary’s apartment. Within the hour Mike found himself standing at the front steps of Hillary’s apartment building. He pressed his finger to the button next to her name and waited for her to buzz him in. Much to his surprise she didn’t bother to ask who it was over the intercom. He adjusted the collar of his red polo shirt and entered the building. When he got to Hillary’s floor he found her waiting for him in the doorway. She wore a Red Sox jersey, blue jeans, and her white purse was slung over her shoulder. A look of shock had formed on her nymph like features when she saw Mike walk down the hallway, she was expecting someone else. “You’re not Charles,” she observed with a small frown. Mike smirked, “That’s the best complement anyone has ever given me.” A disgusted grunt escaped her full, pouting red lips. “Seriously,” she said, “what the hell are you doing here” I told you I didn’t want to ever see you again after the way you broke my heart.” Mike’s dark blue eyes rolled in extreme annoyance as he listened to this. He just wanted his lucky gear back, it’s not like he actually came here to see her. “I’m not here to see you,” he admitted, “I just want my lucky wrestling gear back.” “Fine,” Hillary shot back, “will it get you to leave?” Mike nodded. “Good,” Hillary said as she unlocked her door, “Charles will be here any minute and he’ll get mad at me if he sees you here.” “Why the hell do you waste your time with him?” Mike asked as he followed Hillary into her apartment. Hillary grabbed Mike’s gear out from the cabinet by the door and slammed the fabric into his chest. “Unlike you he actually cares about me,” she said through gritted teeth. “Riiight,” Mike replied as he walked out the door. He turned to thank her for her cooperation but found that she had closed the door behind him. Mike shrugged his shoulders and left with his prize. A smile was plastered on his face as he felt that his victory was now 100% guaranteed. ***** It was Wednesday, the day that Hillary always helped her elderly neighbor, Mrs. Duran, do her grocery shopping. Normally Hillary would show up at Mrs. Duran’s apartment early but today she didn’t come over at all. By two o’clock Mrs. Duran became worried and she decided to go down the hall and see if Hillary was alright. Mrs. Duran rapped lightly on the door. “Hillary?” She called. She waited a few moments before knocking harder. This time when she knocked the door opened, she didn’t find this to be the least bit strange. Thinking that Hillary was home Mrs. Duran entered the apartment to look for her. The old widow’s first thought was to check the bedroom. Perhaps Hillary was sick in bed. Mrs. Duran quietly opened the door so that she wouldn’t disturb Hillary. She peaked inside the room and what she saw caused a blood curdling scream to emanate from her throat. ***** West Hollywood Police Detective Harry Hagen sat at his desk in the middle of the police station and smiled. “Only a few more days,” he thought to himself, “and I can finally go on vacation.” Hagen was an older man in his mid-fifties. Despite his age he still appeared to be as fit as a man half his age. He had a strong weather beaten Germanic face to go along with his bald head and gray goatee. Harry was so lost in thought that he didn’t notice that his boss, Lieutenant Dan, approach his desk. The Lieutenant cleared his throat and caught Harry’s attention. “Hagen,” Dan said, “I know that you’re supposed to go on vacation but?” “But what?” Harry cut Dan off. “There’s been a murder,” Dan replied, “and I think the case might interest you. You see, her name is Hillary?” “I don’t want to hear it,” Harry interrupted again, “I haven’t had a vacation in fifteen years.” “Alright then,” Dan said as he walked away. Suddenly a light of recognition flashed in Harry’s eyes. “Wait a minute Dan,” Harry called, “what was the victim’s name again?” “Hillary Watson,” Dan said over his shoulder. As a fan of professional wrestling Harry instantly recognized the name as Mike Biddle’s former valet. “I think I’ll be postponing that vacation,” Harry said. ***** Harry arrived on the scene almost instantaneously. At this time he had only one thought on his mind, to talk with the woman that had found the body and find out if she knew more than she was letting on. He found her seated in her own apartment, tears streaming down her wrinkled face. The tears steamed up the lenses of her glasses. “Mrs. Duran,” Harry said to the old widow, “have you seen anyone, anyone at all, come or go from Miss Watson’s apartment?” The widow nodded but didn’t say anything so Harry had to prod her further. “Who did you see come or go from Miss Watson’s apartment?” “T-two men a-at different times,” Mrs. Duran said through the tears. “The first was that awful Mike Biddle that always broke that poor girl’s heart. He came over and went inside with her. I never saw her after that.” Harry cringed as he wondered if Mike Biddle was truly capable of murder. “You mentioned two men,” Harry said, “who was the second man?” Mrs. Duran let a small smile form beneath her veil of tears. “That wonderful Charles that treated Hillary so well,” she said, “Hillary didn’t buzz him in so I did. He came and knocked on her door and didn’t get an answer.” “When did all this happen?” “Mike Biddle came at about 3 o’clock and Charles came at about 5 o’clock,” the old widow replied. Harry made note of all this in his small notebook. “Thank you for the information Mrs. Duran,” Harry said as he rose to leave. “You really helped me narrow down my list of suspects.” As Harry left through the apartment door he found the junior detective, Sam Higgins, waiting for him. “So who is on top of your list of suspects sir?” Higgins asked. “Mike Biddle,” Harry answered as he left to go have a drink and plan the next stage of his investigation. -To Be Continued-
