A Harsh Lesson In Reality Part 1

3 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/3/03 **Everyone goes through life hoping to be the best, hoping to be the smartest. And in this world that we live in today children are brought up with the idea of being number one engrained in their minds from day one. No one wants to be second best, no one wants to be wrong. Because with that comes humility. Some have better ways of dealing with humility than others. Some, like Ely Braxton chose to deal with that humility by fighting. Since the beginning of time people have said that violence isn’t the answer. But the truth of the matter is that in some cases violence is the only answer, the only option to a question that is so obscure to begin with. In the United States there is a saying “Innocent until proven guilty”, this statement although tested to its most extreme lengths has stood the tests of time and has been upheld everywhere. Everywhere except the Global Wrestling arena, where two weeks ago Ely Braxton accused Sean Price of attempted murder of the company’s president. Sean ofcourse being the innocent man he is denied anything involving Bubba’s sudden illness and from that came the match that took place last night. Sean Price vs Ely Braxton in THE PIT, a bloody and hard fought match, but with all matches there can only be one winner, and that man was Sean Price.** (As the scene fades in we see Sean Price walking around outside of his Atlanta mansion talking on his cell phone. Price is wearing a plain white t-shirt and a pair of red and gold shirts and flip flops. His jet black hair is free flowing, blowing around as the winds suddenly begin to pick up. Suddenly two men wearing black suits, sun glasses and ear pieces walk out from the back door and down the deck steps towards Sean. While on the phone Rice hears a twig in the grass snap and Sean abruptly turns around to see the two men standing within arms distance of him. Sean quickly takes the phone down from his ear and covers it with his hand.) “Is there something I can help you gentleman with?” remarks a slightly shaken up Sean Price. “Yes sir, there is someone on the phone that refers to themselves as C.M. Shall I tell them that you will call them back or just hang up?” “You tell C.M that I will call them back as soon as I get the chance. I am a busy man you know.” “Yes boss.” “Besides I have a meeting with Il Capo in 20 minutes, I don’t have time for C.M’s crap.” (With that Sean repositions the phone against his left ear and tilts his head slowly in that direction. He walks towards the front of his house and waiting there is a black BMX with tinted out windows. A man gets out of the passenger side door, opens the back door and allows Sean to get inside. Once Sean’s body is completely in the car the man shuts the door and than gets back into the passenger seat of the car and the car starts up and takes off.) **15 minutes later** (The same black BMW as before pulls upto a big office building. The driver of the car pushes alittle button that activates an intercom. The driver speaks into it for a moment and than the huge metal door directly infront of the car gradually begins to open. When it has opened to the appropriate height the car passes under the door and into the small parking garage. When the car reaches its destination inside the garage 6 body gaurds come out one of the doors. One of them opens the back door to the car and allows Sean to exit the vehicle. The 6 body guards escort Sean to the top floor of the building via elevator. When they reach the top floor Sean exits the elevator and walks a short distance down the hall where an door marked Capo lies. Sean opens the door and steps inside to see a figure sitting in his big leather chair on the opposite side of the desk. We can not make out the figures image because of the poor lighting in the room.) “Take a seat Sean.” The voice echos throughout the room. A moment of silence fills the room but then the cold, hard voice begins to speak again. “Sean do you know why I have called you here this evening?” “Not entirely but I have a pretty good idea.” “Well Sean, first let me congratulate you on a job well done. Your match with Ely Braxton was a successful one. Maybe now he will think twice before challenging stepping in the ring with you. Regardless of his motivation.” “Braxton just needed a harsh lesson in reality. And that is exactly what I gave him.” “That’s right, you did. Now to the topic at hand, do you have the stuff with you?” “Actually I don’t have it in my possession right now. It was given to one of the other associates so that they can take a look at it, see what they can learn from it. But I can assure you that you will have it within the next 24 hours.” “Ok, that sounds good. Have you had our lawyer take a look at it” To iron out all the details and make sure everything is ok?” “Yes, he took a look at it right after I did. He seemed to be ok with everything as am I, and as I am sure you will be as well.” “Sean, haven’t we learned anything from our experiences together” You know that when it comes to me, and what I do, there is nothing to be sure about. I come and go like the wind. What you think you may know me for may all just be a facade. Remember that.” “Will do boss.” (The scene fades to black as Sean Price and il Capo continue their conversation.)
