A Night To Remember

15 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/15/06 There it was again... Was it possible? The thought lingered in J-Gonz mind, he wasn't sure if what he was seeing was really there. It flashed again and now J-Gonz was positive he wasn't imagining things. He leaned over his bed and grabbed the black hand-gun that he kept near by. Beside him Rasberry rustled around in the bed. He slowly climbed out and followed the light that had been bothering his mind. The light appeared to be coming from some kind of flashlight. It wasn't too bright, but it was obviously a light. 'I know I havent gone crazy...' The light trailed down the stairs, J-Gonz followed it holding the gun up waiting to shoot if necessary. Finally the light stopped and disappeared. 'Am I seeing things? I know I just saw a light.' As J-Gonz turned again to head back up the stairs he saw the light. This time it was bright and lit up the entire lower floor. J-Gonz shielded his eyes from the immense light, and then as fast as it came it disappeared. J-Gonz dropped to the floor. Finally feeling that the room was safe he crawled back up the stairs and back into bed. No idea what the light was or what it mean, but J-Gonz decided that it was better to rest. --- 'You ok boss?' In his office J-Gonz looked around feeling somewhat out of place. With him were his two men, Paco and Chocolate, the two men could sense something was wrong with the Television Champ? 'Que pasa esse?' 'Look boss, I know it's been a stressful week, business is down, the cops are asking questions and now you've got a match against KPJ. We can wipe him out esse, we can make sure he doesn't make it to the match boss.' 'That's ok boys.' The two men just shrugged and waited to see if anything else was going to be said. 'Boys, I want you guys to take control for a week. I need sometime off. I'm going to take a week off from the club and turn things over to you.' 'Boss?' 'It's ok Paco, nothing wrong esse. I've got a big next three weeks coming. You see boys, I've got a business deal with Train that we need to work out, I've got this Master of the Mat Tournament and then I've got the final match with Rich Sherwin.' 'Boss, if those guys are a problem we could take them out.' 'No!' The words came out louder then J-Gonz had intended and he could tell that the two men knew it. 'Listen boys, you two take care of the club. Take care of the business and make sure that everything runs smooth. If the cops stick there noses in our business again, don't hesitate to put em down homes.' The two men weren't exactly sure of what to say, they just nodded and watch as J-Gonz got up from behind his desk, grabbed his coat and hat and walked out. Not even J-Gonz knew where he was going, but he knew he had to go. The door to the club shut behind J-Gonz and he starred out into the crime infested street of Dallas, partly run by him. As he walked down the street his phone chirped... 'Mariachi.' '....' 'This Monday?' '...' 'I've taken care of her. She's been paid.' '...' 'It's a go then.' Hanging up the phone J-Gonz looked up with a smile. What a few minutes ago had been a look of doubt had turned into a look of glee. As he walked down the street his mind raced from thoughts of Train to KPJ. Mostly his upcoming match, the biggest match of J-Gonz career. Not only was his Television Title on the line, but also his place in the Master of the Mat. This match would send him into the semi-finals and to the PPV. J-Gonz had no doubt proved his worth in Global Wrestling, holding the Television Title for almost a month, the longest reigning champion was no doubt under pressure. 'Ken...you kept your end of the deal two weeks ago, and I thank you for that. Now this week you have to keep it again.' Around him Dallas had become his territory. J-Gonz had not only risen as the biggest crimelord in Dallas but also as themost wealthy. He controled 'El Dorado', the biggest night club in Dallas, he controled Gonz. Enterprises, the ring that managed the largest drug trafficking agency in Texas. His success outside of the ring was due in part to the fact that he had kept business and wrestling seperate, but now as he walked down this street he thought about his decision. Monday night, business would become wrestling, and wrestling would become business. At Evolution, J-Gonz would mix the two and he wouldn't be doing it alone. 'It's going to be a night to remember!'
