Ahhh....shit

19 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5.19.03 Not many people know what happened to Ace Cash a few weeks ago. He had finally made his triumphant return to the GW after being put in exhile! And then...he disappeared again. Where was our resident green haired luchador? In trouble? Arrested? Stranded? Murdered!? 'Get me the hell out of this thing or I'm gonna kill somebody!!' Ace is pissed as he pounds on the door of the port-a-potty. His friends from college thought it would be fun to lock him in one and ship it off to the middle of nowhere. Local authorities have just busted the deadbolt off of the door with an axe, and Ace burst out of the thing. 'JESUS HUBERT CHRIST! What took you people so long! GOD!' Ace brushes at his blue silk muscle shirt, a look of disgust on his face. All he remembers is is friends telling him there were naked girls in there. It was the night before Wrestlecade, and Ace decided to throw a big party with all of his old college drinking buddies. But what happens when you combine college, drinking and Ace? TROUBLE. Ace didn't notice the Global Wrestling cameras there until just now. He's infuriated! 'Oh no! Get them out of here! AHHHHH!!! My reputation is ruined!' He charges the camera man, covering the lens on the camera and we hear a thunk sound as the screen turns to fuzz. ---- ---- Ace Cash paces around his living room on his cell phone, talking to Salaok. He's just called him and told him, and Salaok is pissed off that Ace disappeared. 'No! I swear, it was beyond my control.' He really doesn't want to have to tell Salaok what happened. Fortunately, he payed off the GW camera crew to get rid of that video tape evidence so no fans would see. It does pay to have a multi-million dollar inheritance sometimes. 'What was wrong? Ummm, well, see, it was ahhh......eeeerr...family...family problems. Yeah! Real bad problems at the re-union before Wrestlecade. See, ahhh, the old people wanted mashed potatoes, but we didn't bring any. Yeah, big riot. It was nuts! Dentures flying everywhere, I think somebody even broke a cane over somebodies head! Yeah... it was violent for sure.' Ace smiles at his wit..or lack thereof. Salaok doesn't buy a word that he just told him, but goes along with it anyways, just glad to have Ace back. 'No, I'll be ok. 20 showers laters, and I still can't get the smell of wrinkle cream off me.... but I'll be allright. Yeah, I'll talk to you later. Bye.' Clicking the phone shut with a sense of relief, Ace plops down onto his couch. He picks up the remote and clicks on his TV. It's been a while since he watched the Primetime Central programming, so he flips over to GW's channel to see what he's missed. He sees the ever familiar face of GW's main reporter, Stephen Dinkler. They are broadcasting a GW News Update. 'Earlier this afternoon, local authorities in Denver, Colorado discovered GW superstar Ace Cash locked inside of a port-a-potty! Word has it that Ace attempted to destroy this footage I'm about to show you, but we have it here for you all to see.' 'AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHHHH SHHHIIIIIIIIIIIT!'
