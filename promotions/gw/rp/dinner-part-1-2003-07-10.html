Dinner Part 1

10 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/10/03 Outside a sign glows in the night sky. The sign is black with neon red letters that read ' PIZZA HUT'. Evolution commentator Brandi Sights and her cameraman walk into the resturaunt and pan around the building. The tile floor looks freshly mopped, the brick walling looks like it had just been washed, however the wood panelling still looks a little dusty and a little dirty. The hostess, who is a tad bit shorter than Brandi, with short brown hair, wearing her Pizza Hut uniform which consists of black shoes, black pants, and a maroon shirt with the Pizza Hut logo on it, walks forward with a menu in hand. 'Can I get you a table?' 'No that's ok, I'm looking for someone, and I'm not quite sure if they are here yet or not. But thanks anyways.' Brandi begins to stand on her tip toes as the hostess walks away. Brandi then begins to walk into the dining room of the resturaunt. Sitting alone at in a booth wearing a pair of blue jeans, a white hooded sweatshirt, with pink letters across the chest spelling ' Abercrombie & Fitch', and with her black streaked blond hair resting on her shoulders, in loose curls is Star. Brandi and her cameraman make their way over to her booth. 'Mind if we have a seat?' Star looks up and rolls her eyes. 'Sure, you're going to anyways. However I am waiting for someone and would like you gone before they arrive, so hopefully we can make this quick.' 'Waiting for someone? ICE? I would love to speak to Ice when he gets here if that is who you are waiting on.' Star lets out a deep breath and her face shows that she is slightly annoyed by Brandi. 'No, I am not waiting for Ice, however...nevermind.' A waitress walks over with three glasses of water and sets them down on the table. She walks away without asking if any of them want something to eat or another drink. Star takes a small sip from her glass of water before continuing to scan over the dimly lit room. She then looks down at her watch briefly, and looks back up at Brandi. 'Now if you don't mind, I'm expecting an important guest, so if you could please excuse yourself.' Brandi. gets up and leaves. The scene fades to black.
