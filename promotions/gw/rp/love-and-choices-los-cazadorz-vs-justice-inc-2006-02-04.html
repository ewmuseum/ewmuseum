Love and Choices [Los Cazadorz vs Justice Inc.]

4 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-4-06 It had been a long time sine the last day he saw her, held her, smelled her hair. Serpenti sat at his home, resting and thinking. The house was almost a exact replica of his home in Spain. The recent training gym was a new addition. One addition was seen from the roof where Serpenti now sat. A neat little spot recreated from the home in Spain. Serpenti found it as a small boy; save there he had the view of the ocean, Here the sea of lights that was Las Vegas. Serpenti watched as his new partner and old friend, trained against two men, His dojo from England and Japan recreated here in the United States. But watching Jago Tamura train was not why he had climbed to the top of his home. The thoughts of two women mixed in his mind. One was a dangerous beauty; known as Kitty Macintyre. The other; was a beautiful treasure known as Teresa Ramirez. Serpenti though he had lost all hope of finding Kitty ever again. One night she had vanished. Teresa had left back home to Mexico after an accident that injured her Husband, His friend; Juan Ramirez. Gaspar Roca sat and looked to the stars. “Teresa is a taken woman Gaspar. It is not right to covet your friends wife.” Serpenti thought. “Kitty, who knows” Did she really love me?” Serpenti let out a deep sigh as he tried to replay the images in his head. He was sure Kitty had just left the office of Brandi Sights. But when he got there he lost her in the crowd. A crack across his head though woke him from his daydreaming. Serpenti rubbed his head and looked up to see a small monkey sitting on the roof above his head, the monkey was holding a stick in his hands and looked down at Serpenti... Eulb Yeknom a small white spider monkey with a hint of blue though out his fur. But this was no ordinary Spider Monkey. It was a Luca Spirit Guide. Most Luchador warriors had them. How Serpenti acquired this one, he had no idea. Yeknom reached out and smacked Serpenti again. “That’s for staring, You would think you never seen me before!” Eulb Yeknom exclaimed pointing a finger down at Serpenti. “Well, I have not seen you in months, then when I am sitting here thinking about things, you pop up out of thin air. How do you expect me to look” Serpenti explained as he rubbed his head a bit more. “Yeah well I’ve been ummm, tied up.” Yeknom said. “Took a bit of work to get free.” “What do you mean tied up?” Serpenti asked as he stood up. “Don’t want to talk about it. Let’s just say my trip to Japan did not go well Reason you have not seen me since then.” Eulb Yeknom said shaking his head and hanging it low. Serpenti climbed down from his roof as Eulb Yeknom hoped and followed. Serpenti landed and headed to the living room of the house, three large couches sat in a semi circle facing a large flat screen television. A few tapestries on the wall of ships and the ocean. A few swords hung here and there. A small wet bar sat off to the side next to the sliding glass doors Serpenti walked though? A few bookshelves with books in it and a few well-placed statues and trees completed the room. Serpenti went to the bar and grabbed out a bottle of rum and walked over and sat on the large tan couch. +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ Jago Tamura blocked each blow from his two opponents Hatori and Hanzo. Two Ninja’s from the Blood Claw Clan. Jago finished his sparing session and the three bowed to each other. “Your skills continue to improve Hanzo and Hatori.” Jago said removing his veil. “Thank you Master.” The two said removing there own veils. The two looks exactly alike except one had a strange tribal tattoo around his right eyes and down his right cheek. Jago walked over and took a glass and dipped it into a large silver jar with red and black designs. Jago drank the liquid down and started to wipe his mouth when he noticed though the window, Serpenti climbing down from the roof, with a small White and blue haired monkey. “Should be in here training with me. Not playing games with his pet.” Hatori, Hanzo. Take a break. I will return.” Jago said as he placed his glass down. “Yes Master.” The two said with a bow as Jago stormed out of the room. +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ Serpenti looked at the bottle of Rum for a moment then took a big drink. When finished he offered Eulb Yeknom a drink. “No. Not today.” I came because you are in distress.” Eulb Yeknom said as he scratched his chin. “HA!! Me in distress” No Amigo. I am afraid not. I am perfectly fine!” Serpenti said taking another drink of the Rum. “No. You forget I know your thoughts. You are tormented by the love of two. It is a dangerous thing Serpenti, Do not be fooled or crush you it will.” Eulb Yeknom Serpenti shook his head and then drank down more Rum. “You know Yeknom, Okay so I love to women” But one I can never have. One I don’t know if she even wants me. I don’t know why she left or what happened. So I have a big heart, and a fondness of females. What is wrong in that!” Serpenti said trying to stand up? Look back at the now empty bottle of Rum. “I don’t know who your talking to Serpenti, But how do you expect to win our next battle in the octagon, when your drowning your self in a bottle!” Roared Jago Tamura. Serpenti rolled his head over to look at Jago and took a deep breath. “I have you to know I am more then capable of drinking while fighting Sinor.” Serpenti said slurring his words “After all” I am? pirate!” Jago eyes narrowed and a slight smile appeared on his face. “Very well. Prove it to me you dirty pirate. Join me in the dojo!” Jago said as he turned and began walking down the hall. “OHH!! You do not believe me Amigo! I” I? I show you? Just who is da boss!” Serpenti said as he staggered forward. Eulb Yeknom just slapped his face and shook his head. Yeknom ran past Serpenti causing him to trip and fall. Serpenti looked around and pulled his self up. Serpenti staggered over to the wall and grabbed a rapier from it tossing the scabbard aside he continued down the hall. “JAGO!!!! Lets play swords!” Serpenti shouted as he continued to slowly stagger down the hall. The small monkey ran into the Dojo and slid to a stop seeing Jago with a Katana drown and ready to face his partner. Yeknom know this would not end well and if his charge perished he would likely be sent off to clean the Lucha locker room. A small shudder ran down his spine at the thought. Before he could do anything the door flung open sending Eulb Yeknom flying across the room landing in a cart of padding. Serpenti stood their sword drawn. Staggering into the room. Jago readied his self for attack as Hatori and Hanzo watched from the far side of the room. “On guard Amigo!” Serpenti shouted. Jago flashed his blade and slid in back into his scabbard and tossed the blade aside. “No blades Serpenti. We wil not be able to face Justice Incorporated with swords.” Jago said. “Fine, I shall show you” “Serpenti started and then fell forward and laid still. The sound of snoring quickly filled the room and Jago shook his head. “Hatori, Hanzo... Grab Serpenti and take him to the garden. Our friend needs a bath.” Jago said. The two ninjas bowed and did what they were told quickly and silently. ? A drunken Pirate is one thing, a unconscious one is a different matter.” Jago said as he walked over to a shelf and pulled out a large bottle, Jago sniffed it then held his nose. “Yep this is it.” Jago walked to the sliding doors as Eulb Yeknom slid in front of him. Eulb Yeknom took a big breath and held up his small hands. “Jago stop.” Yeknom said causing Jago to stop and look down and then look at the bottle in his hand. “No, This is not intoxicating, its is the reverse. So you eater said my name Monkey, or I have finally lost my mind to the madness.” Jago said as he kneeled down to Eulb Yeknom “No. I spoke. Hopefully I won’t get reassigned.” Yeknom said with a sigh. “Well then little one” You have some explaining to do.” Jago said. +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ “Ohh my head” What hit me?” Serpenti said as he rubbed his head and stared at the ceiling. “A bottle of rum.” Jago said walking to look down on Serpenti. “You... You hit me with a bottle of Rum?” Serpenti asked. “No you hit your self.” Now if you do not mind? Get dressed and meet me in the garden for some tea.” “What day is it?” Serpenti asked as he slowly sat up in his bed. “Saturday” You decided to sleep for the past two days.” Jago said as he walked out the door. Serpenti staggered over and put on, a pair of white cotton loose pants and a matching long sleeved shirt, and his white cloth mask. The mask was open at the top allowing his long black hair hanging loose. Serpenti slipped on a pair of white house slippers and headed to the garden. “Sunday?” Serpenti asked his self as he shut the door. +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ Jago sat on the ground in the garden. His legs crossed and a small square table at his side. On a small stool Eulb Yeknom sat smoking a small pipe. Serpenti slowly walked up seeing Eulb Yeknom smoking he stopped. “Well take a picture if you are going to stand and stare.” Eulb Yeknom said sending Serpenti’s Jaw dropping open. “And please close your mouth.” “You are speaking with Jago present.” Serpenti said shocked. “It is okay Serpenti” Jago said. ? We have talked about a great many things as you slept off the rum.” Serpenti nodded and took his seat. ? But what about?” Serpenti Asked. “I have permission since you two are tag team partners!” Eulb Yeknom said with a smile. “The council also has ties in Japan, Both have always been linked. Japan And Mexico. But I digress. I am not the guide for Los Cazadores.” “Well. That is good, At least one person will not think I am crazy when I am talking to you.” Serpenti said sitting down on the other side of the table. “Umm Hum. Well now that that is over” We have one day to train tomorrow. But tonight Serpenti, we meditate and drink Green Tea.” Jago says pouring hot steaming Green tea into two large cups. Serpenti Nods and picks up the cup. “Very well, I guess a night time ritual would be good to have, but in the morning we wake before sunrise.” Serpenti says. “Why is this?” Jago asks. “For my Sunrise ritual. We drink juice and watch the sunrise and stretch in the cool morning air.” Serpenti says with a smile. Jago nods in agreement “Very well. You partake in my Night time Ritual and I shall partake in your Daytime Ritual” The two begin to drink there tea but Eulb Yeknom taps his stick on the table. “Before you both start drinking and meditating. I need to inform you both of something very serious.” Eulb Yeknom says. Jago and Serpenti look at Eulb Yeknom, waiting for him to finish. “There is a force that the counsel sees as a dire threat in the world of Global. A man known as Darth von Doom has arrived.” Eulb Yeknom says and lowers his head. “Von Doom” Is that not the name that those two locos mentioned last week?” Serpenti asks. “I believe it was. Who ever this von Doom is” We shall battle him in due time. But now? Tea!” Jago says and the three begin to drink their tea and meditate while pondering their thoughts. +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++
