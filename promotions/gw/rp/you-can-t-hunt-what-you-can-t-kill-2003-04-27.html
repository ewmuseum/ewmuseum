You Can't Hunt What You Can't Kill

27 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/27/03 Now Listen To The Clever One Who Speaks Before His Thoughts Are Done Some Things Are Better Left Unsaid His hands run through his pure white hair. In his eyes are a reflection of the past. A young boy, running through a field. He runs full speed. The sweat, oh, it pours off his forehead. The blood rushing up to his face. And the adrenaline, oh the adrenaline flows through his veins like no other. He is fueled by this aggression. An aggression and a desire to be something that nobody would ever let him. He wanted to be something’but society began this revolution to hold him down towards the Earth, keeping him from soaring in the stars along with guys like Michael Jordan, Hans Kruger, and Bart Starr. He attempted to shoot towards the stars, hoping to land among them’to survive with them. Then, all of his hard work would pay off. But instead, he shot towards the stars and landed on the moon. For he, he is one of a kind. His style, no man or woman, animal or plant can compare to his style. He is cocky, because he deserved to be cocky. He grew up from birth wanting to be something more then just an average Chinese child who was raised to own the farm after his father departed. But instead, he attempted to be something. And he was successful. He is the GW Cruiserweight Champion. He is The Soul Survivor. He is Sage Sabbot. “I looked all around me. I noticed God has handed down skills and abilities to people that they have not worked for and deserved. I became jealous’I envied being like them. I thought to myself, these people did not have to work for what they want, why do I have to work for what I want” Maybe God will bless me with these treasures later on in life. But then I questioned the theory that maybe the man up above was just a myth and a rumor. That people believed in him, just to have an excuse for the world’s problems. People prayed to ask for these treasures for free. But my prayers, they lied in the determination that I had to succeed. I did not stand back and let some fake God hand me my fortune and destiny. I grasped that opportunity in becoming something greater then what I would have been early. I trained and practiced to be a martial artist. Instead of playing games with my childhood friends, I stayed home and studied the arts and went on jogs and lifted weights. I had nobody to help me. I had no God, I had no mentor, I had no family. They wanted me to be something I refused to be’they wouldn’t accept me for who I wanted to be, and now, now they are gone. And I look at them like I looked at them before. I stand before their graves in disgust’for I am not their son. I was a slave to their labor. And now, now that they can not see me, I hope they look down from the supposed Heavens that they destined themselves to be in and be ashamed. Now that I am who I wanted to be since childhood, I want them to realize that they never took any part in this success, that I accomplished this by myself with no help. For I survived’I survived’I survived.” No tear is shed from his eye. Just pride, pride from himself’because he deserved it. “The only thing I can ever thank my parents for, is not drowning me in luxury. I am glad I am poor, for I don’t take anything for granted. This title that I hold so tightly in my arms, I’ll never let it go. The International Title match I was granted’not granted, I mean worked for, will not be taken for granted either. This is my first opportunity to hold the International Title. And it will not be flushed down the drain like others have. This is an opportunity that Salaok gave me for accomplishing what I have accomplished so far. Just because I am a member of The Nevermore, does not mean that I was thrown into the match. I was thrown into this match because I deserved it. Throughout my life, I have worked hard for what I wanted. And now, now this hard work has paid off. As I climb the ladder of success in the Global Wrestling’I smile. Because it feels a hell of a lot better to accomplish something then to have things handed to me.” He reaches his arm out in the darkness around him. The light turns on above his head and his albino tiger, Brylou, is sitting beside him. He reaches down and pets Brylou’s head. The massive tiger slowly growls, a growl that is enough to send an adult running in fright. But he sits down without fear and pets his head some more. “Well, Brylou came along for the ride through this journey entitled, life.” He smiles and pets Brylou on the top of his head. Brylou just sits back and enjoys the head massage. He closes his eyes and attempts to go into a deep tiger sleep. “Guys like J.J. Dynasty are the guys that disappoint me. He had everything he wanted, then he wanted more. He gluttoned himself over materialistic things. He bathed in money and stardom. But he handed that over’should I pat him on the back” Or should I sit back and watch his life corrupt because he can’t survive without the help of The Mile High Club or somebody to watch his back. J.J., Jason, Dynasty’it doesn’t matter if you are the IceMan or the 5-Star Superstar. You gave me the fight of my life only a couple months ago. And we took the spotlight. And at Wrestlecade my friend, we will do it once again. You can change your views and your lifestyle all you want, it isn’t going to help you at Wrestlecade. Revenge is sweet, and so is success. I can taste it on the tip of my tongue. Dynasty, you can be an assassin, you can be somebody clothed in luxury, it doesn’t matter what you are. Because in my book, you are only one thing and one thing only. You are just a victim to fall to the 8th Deadly Sin. At Wrestlecade, history will be made as I am crowned with the International Title. And a halo of pain will be blessed upon your head. This is not a normal match. This is a title match at Wrestlecade. It is an honor to be invited to be in it. And I am glad that you are in the match, because I can prove to the world’that the only man that can beat me has left the Global Wrestling when he was J.J. Dynasty. Jason, you are nothing special in my book. Hell, you haven’t even qualified to be anything in the Global Wrestling. You beat me a couple months back, but the favor will be returned in the ring. You think that you are so evil and nefarious? Well you are stepping into the ring against me. The most nefarious man to ever step foot into the Global Wrestling. You are an assassin? It is your duty to eliminate people from this earth? Well my friend, try to eliminate me at Wrestlecade. Why? Because you can’t’you can’t hunt what you can’t kill. I can already smell your blood and I am on your trail. The time is ticking further and further until I feast upon your weaknesses and boast in glory for the International Title will be strapped around my waist once and for all. And then, then I will lift my head towards my family in the false heaven they believed in and laugh’laugh because they couldn’t contain me when I was a child. And I’ll laugh because nobody can contain me now.” He pauses and takes a deep breath to relax his thoughts? “I have beat guys like Jay Robson and guys like Roz Blayze. From legends to rookies, I have defeated them all’all except the few that have avoided me. But why avoid somebody who is only 6’2 and 215 pounds” Do I send a sharp prick of fear into your hearts? Probably so. At Wrestlecade, a man will enter the ring along with me’but we will not be seeing eye to eye. He is not my companion, he is my opponent. He is a man that I have full respect for and a man that I pat on the back. For he paved the way for guys like me, guys who are getting into this business and attempting to make something of themselves for themselves and nobody else. The Tragic Hero’a man who I look up to. A man who I respect more then almost anybody in the world. But that respect will be dropped for 1 match. Where his blood will stain my clothing, where we will fight for what hangs high in our minds’gold and glory. And when it is done, the better man will win. Christopher Camero, the “Canadian Icon” Christopher Camero. An icon in Canada, an icon in America and an icon in China. His has led the Global Wrestling world for quite some time now. I remember sitting back, watching guys like Jean Claude Dafoe, Hans Kruger, Mark Kullypso, Christopher Camero and many others battle it out for a future. They were all just starting in this business, and they are now all veterans of the game. And nothing can take that away from them, they have earned it. But Camero, you are just like the old Jason Dynasty, you consider yourself something more then what you are and ever will be. You are just a normal man’now, the GW Heavyweight Champion of the World. You are another man to knock down the ladder, that is all you are Camero. Sitting back at home when I was a child, I used to jog and do pushups and I would get physically weak and tired and think about giving up. But I pushed myself harder and harder just by thinking that one day I’ll get my opportunity to face guys like you and win at the end of the match. Those are the dreams that I wished I had when I was a child. And those dreams will finally come true. Wrestlecade was an event that I watched when I was at home when I didn’t even think wrestling was for me. And I watched guys bleed and die for the Global Wrestling Federation. And it was my dream to bleed on the same mat as these men once did. But now, now I will shed blood, but it will not be my own. It will be Christopher Camero’s blood. And my goal is not to just win the match, it is to take Chris? ego and make him regret lacing up his boots for the first time. And after Wrestlecade, when I put him through this torture and pain’it will be the last time he will lace up his boots.” He pauses and pets Brylou. 'What I do have, is what I worked for. And that, that doesn't even mean much to me. I don't need money to have happiness. I will be happy when I win the International Title.' He reaches in his pocket and pulls out a couple $100 bills and throws it on the floor. 'It all means nothing to me. Keep it Camero, keep it Dynasty, keep it Frost, and keep it Welch.
