The Formation

26 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/26/05 They sit there with their heads held high. Proud. This is the beginning. They want to be apart of this regime, but it isn’t for everybody. United as one, we dream of venturing into the future and being one step ahead of everybody else. The truth is, not everybody will make it. But they are young prospects with big hearts. We don’t want that. “When Orthane and I created this, we sat down and knew that we weren’t capable of doing this on our own.” Salaok walks back and forth in front of the line of recruits. They sit on the floor because its cold and wet. If they can’t handle that, they won’t be able to handle our newly found group. He walks past his brother, his own flesh and blood. He doesn’t look him in the eyes, I told him not to. Don’t take it easy on him, he has to earn it too.. that’s the method behind my madness. “We knew that we’d have to have some of the strongest members in the world to even survive on the underground circuit of groups.” I step in from behind the shadows and butt into the conversation. “We decided that we’ll take from the streets.” “And take from the ones who own their own home and operate four apartment complexes while working forty hours a week for their hundred thousand dollar paycheck.” “For nobody is different. Living in poverty or living in riches, they all came from the same place. As long as they possess the strength and are ready to unite together as one, we’ll take them.” I look across the line of hope filled line as Salaok walks the opposite side. We can see their faces, but they can not see ours. We are in a small basement of an abandoned building. One light bulb attempts to fill the entire room. It tries, but it doesn’t succeed. It flickers on and off, we can’t afford pyrotechnics yet. “Stand up.” Salaok motions for a man in front of him to stand up. The man rocks on his heels and stands to his feet. He stands face to face with Salaok. He looks him up and down and takes a step back. “So tell me, why do you want to be apart of this?” “Well, sir..” “Don’t call me sir. In this group, there is no leader. Orthane and I are the beginning, only the beginning. And we’ll continue until the end.” “I’m sorry.” Salaok stands in front of him and shakes his head. “Too soft. Second thing you’ve ever said and you’re apologizing. You’re too weak, soldier. Where do you live?” He swallows hard.. “I don’t got anywhere else.” “So you’re a street dweller then?” “I suppose you can say that.” “Well, if you are homeless, you dwell the streets, right” So that makes you a street dweller.” “Then yes, I’m a street dweller.” “Now street dweller, why do you want to be apart of this group?” “Well, I don’t have much else.” “.. you don’t have much else?” “I don’t have a home or a family. I don’t have a job or anything. This is my only hope.” “We aren’t a charity organization. We’re not welfare. We won’t provide you with food stamps. We don’t operate a soup kitchen. We’re very selective, soldier. That’s how we run. Do you think you can keep up with our pace?” “I’m sure I can try.” “Well, what street do you live on, street dweller?” “Market and Kerry. There’s an alley that r..” “Then go the fuck back there, we don’t need your weak ass.” I butt in, grabbing a handful of his dirty and old jacket and pushing him backwards. I watch as the man turns around and runs away out the door. I’ve never seen anybody run that fast in my entire life. He’s out the door, probably crying his way home. Good, maybe it’ll teach him something. You see, I’m no as compassionate as Salaok. Well, there isn’t much sympathy in his blood, but there is none in mine. Zero tolerance, that’s how I was born. And that’s how my group will survive. “Anybody else?” I scream into the crowd. “The door is right behind you, we don’t want you if you are as pathetic as that guy. If you can’t handle today, you aren’t going to handle tomorrow. If you can’t push yourself, we’ll push you. But don’t waste my god damn time. We don’t need you. I’d rather have five strong members in my army rather than fifty weak ones. If you are scared, get the fuck out of my building.” I give them a few seconds, looking through the crowd. I look to the right, and slowly move my eyes towards the left. I hear some scattering around on the other side, somebody on the right side of the room turns around and runs out the door. I giggle to myself. And yes, I find weakness hilarious. “Anymore want to follow him?” I look around. “Good.” We work together, Salaok and I. Everything from having each other’s backs to finishing each other’s sentences. A well oiled machine of hate and tragedy. “I take it the rest of you are going to be staying then” Good. Like Orthane said, don’t waste our fucking time or you’ll regret it.” There are about eight people in the formation now. Soldiers, ready to fight. Soldiers, ready to stand by our side. “Now, for your first test..” Salaok motions for them to stand up and he turns around. He waves his arm for them to follow. He continues along his way without saying one word. So powerful, so intimidating. .. they follow. I follow behind them, there’s no turning back now. Moments Later As we reach the opposite side of the building, a red stage curtain is set up, blocking off one corner of the room. Only Salaok and I know what lies behind it, we look over at it, then at each other with a grin. We know what’s going to happen before we even do it and it doesn’t scare us one bit. We both turn towards the ones who seem to bite their fingernails to the knuckle. They stand there, in a line, waiting for something to happen. They see the curtain and they know something is behind it, but they don’t know what. .. And they are too afraid to ask. Down the line, I look at each and every one of them. And right then and there, I seem to know which ones are going to last. Salaok turns around and faces them. The ringleader of this masquerade. “Are you ready?” Some nod their heads, others stand in silence. He turns around and walks over to the curtain and waves his hand. Showcasing his work. He slowly walks backwards and draws the curtain. Her eyes slowly open, looking towards Salaok. She squints, perhaps the light is too bright. Her eyes, non-adjusted at this moment. Or perhaps she is so close to wanting to see the men who are doing this to her. She attempts to shriek in fear, but the cloth tied to her mouth just won’t allow it. She turns around and nudges her boyfriend, his head hangs towards the ground and his five o’clock shadow is about to turn to six. Unconscious, a defect in our system. A defect in his system. He wouldn’t last. Tied up to the same chair as him, she nudges him harder, but he won’t wake. She screams.. louder than before. And I don’t think I have ever laughed so hard in my entire life. Salaok places his finger over his lips and shakes his head at her.. “There’s no use in screaming, nobody can hear you.” She sobs, like a little baby. But she is further away from birth as can be, but even closer to death. Sitting in her torn clothing, drenched in urine and blanketed with dirt. She bitch smells. Wretched, she smells. Perhaps a bloodbath can sooth my nostrils. “There’s no use in crying, little one. Everything will be alright, if you allow it.” Salaok walks away from her, she tries to get out of the ropes, but they are tied well. Too well. Boy Scouts of America can’t tie knots this well. Her cluster and clatter awaken her boyfriend from his slumber, I shake my head, hoping it was everlasting. But that just adds to the fun and excitement. Salaok stands in front of the recruits. I turn around and lock the door behind me. They all look back, there’s no turning back. I place the key inside my front pocket, nobody has the testicular fortitude to take it from me. I make my way and stand by Salaok.. “When Salaok asked if you were ready, nobody said anything. So we’ll take it that you are. This.. this is your first lesson.” I reach into my overcoat and pull out a handgun. I hold it in front of my face for everybody to see. Slowly, I walk it down the line. And then, I turn around and show our guests it. They both scream as loud as their mouth gags allow them to. I laugh at their stupidity. Why scream if it does you no good? You’ll only lose your voice and that won’t be any good once you need it. I turn back around towards the recruits. “This is a Bersa Thunder 380 with a hammer action. Made in the good ol” US of A. .38 special caliber and only weighs 15 ounces. Such a small weapon for so much damage. Easy to carry and comfortable to shoot. Uncomfortable to be shot by. It holds a maximum of ten bullets.” I lock and load the magazine cartridge into the chamber and aim it at the man’s head in front of me. I focus in between his eyes and I can see the beads of sweat dripping down his forehead. I pull it back and hold it in my hand. “But today, we’ll only need a maximum of two.” One of the recruits dry heaves, but nothing comes out. That’s because he’s hollow and shallow. What a fool, pathetic. At least release an acidic discharge or you’ll make a fool of yourself. I point the gun towards his head.. “One more of those and I’ll be using three.” He stands up straight. What else could he do? I turn around and look at Salaok, he nods his head of approval. I turn around and walk over to the lovely couple. Within every step, the smell grows. Apparently sitting in the same spot for two days must be uncomfortable. I bet they are hungry. Speaking of which, I’m in for a cheesesteak after this is over. I pet the girl’s hair in my hand. She starts screaming bloody murder but it sounds like a mouthful of shit. I wretch her hair back in my hand and she stays quiet and still. Her eyes widen and glisten from the tears she has cried. The male, the damned boyfriend, he decides to get all wild and tough. A vein protrudes from his neck and his face turns tomato red. I look at him and laugh. I find humor in weakness, I told you “Oh be quiet, I’m not going to hurt you. Instead, you’re going to hurt each other.” He continues to scream and I backhand him with the butt end of my pistol. If the inside of his mouth is bleeding, there is no way to spit out the blood because of the gag. Therefor, he is forced to swallow his own blood. Thinking of that, I laugh even harder. “Now, listen closely. I’m going to make you both an offer. And it is an offer that you won’t be able to refuse. But first, you have to be quiet. And if you aren’t, I’ll shoot you directly in the nose, right here.. right now. There is no point in yelling, the nearest resident lives a quarter mile down the road. And now even a banshee can not scream that loud. Now, do we have ourselves a deal?” They both look at each other and nod their precious heads in agreement. So I untie their gags and watch them drop to the floor. I look over to the boyfriend and he spits out a mouthful of blood. Who could have guessed. The girl lowers her head, not wanting to look at us. And the man looks directly in my eyes with fury and rage. I can see the fire in them and I love it. We should recruit him. “Who are you?” “The future.” “You aren’t anything. You are just pathetic pieces of shit. You aren’t the future.” I walk up to him and smirk, directly in his face. I wave my pistol.. “If you open your mouth one more time without permission, you won’t have a future. You read me?” He nods his head. “.. like a book.” The man looks up. “Say it. Say.. “like a book.” “Fuck you.” I pistol whip him again in the mouth. He turns around and spits out a tooth. The woman screams at me. I place the nose of my gun to her forehead and she quiets down pretty quickly. I take it away from her head and point it at the guy. “You apparently didn’t learn the first time. Say it.” “.. like a book.” I try to keep serious, but I laugh. He seems to get aggravated by my sense of humor, or his own, whichever frustrates him the most. “What do you want from us?” “We want to play a little game.” Salaok tells them. “Why.. why us?” “Because you’re special. Very special, actually. You love each other and care for each other. That’s why we chose you.” “There are other people who care about each other, why didn’t you choose them?” “Because not every couple gets trashed at Damon’s bar and grill on a Wednesday night.” “What do you want us to do” Don’t kill us. We didn’t do anything. Please.” “Oh, don’t worry honey. We aren’t going to kill you. You’re going to have to decide that.” “What do you mean?” “Plain and simple. At least one of you will die tonight.” The girl gasps for a breath. Apparently it took the air right out of her. She seems to choke on something, but it was just a loud whine. She lets it out and starts bawling her eyes out. “But you will choose which one of you two will go. And if you can not choose, you will both die. You will decide amongst yourselves. You have five minutes to figure things out. Enjoy.” Salaok closes the curtain and I motion for everybody to walk to the back of the room. Everybody moves towards the back. Half of them look as if they are going to vomit, the other half, calm as Hindu cattle. They stand in the back. “Line up, soldiers. If you think this is bad, you’ll never make it to the future. The world is taking a wrong turn. So we’re planning on turning around before anybody else so we are ahead of them all. There is no such thing as peace and harmony. No such thing as happiness and truth. Welcome to the new generation. “Our question to you is, will you be beside us?” “We don’t need you, you’ll need us. But we aren’t going to take just anybody. This isn’t the NBA draft. We don’t have to pick anybody here. We’ll choose who stays and who leaves at the end of the night. Until then, enjoy the ride.” We pause for a few moments and I check my watch. A couple minutes left to spare. A couple minutes until they make their decision. “Three minutes sounds a lot better than five. I’m too impatient. Lets go.” Such a heartless man, I love it. He turns around and walks back to the curtain. Everybody follows. He pulls the rope and the curtain draws open once again. This time, they are both crying. “Have you made your decision?” “Time is up for your thinking and one of your lives. Which one is it going to be” Who dies here tonight?” “I’m going to..” “NOOO!” She screams.. “Don’t listen to her. Kill me. Leave her go, please.” “NOO! PLEEEEASE!” She screams even louder. She begs. She pleads for the life of her lover. “Well, which one is it going to be” Both of you have to agree on it.” “Let him go.” “No, honey. No.” He looks up at me. “Don’t lay one finger on her. You can kill me, but don’t touch her.” “Well, it seems that you both can’t seem to decide. So, we’ll just have to kill you both.” They both cry. They lower their heads onto each other’s shoulders. She kisses his cheek, perhaps for the last time. He kisses her forehead.. “I love you.” He says to her in a soft whisper, trying to choke back enough tears to escape those words from his mouth. She can’t seem to hold back the tears long enough to say it back. “Please, go home. We’ll always be together. I love you.” “I love you too, but I’m not going to let you die. We’ll die together before I watch you die. I can’t live without you.” I grow impatient. I walk behind them both. Their heads, still clinched to each others. Whispering words of compassion time and time again. I point the gun to the back of his head and she screams. I pick it back up, she stops. I point the gun to the back of her head, tied up and nothing he can do, he just cries and screams. They both can’t seem to want the other to die. Even to save their own lives, they can’t seem to decide. How pathetic.. .. and how compassionate. I point the gun down towards the knot on the ropes, holding them both together and down to the chair. I pull back the hammer and shoot towards the ground. Blasting a big hole in the knot, it comes unloose. They untie and the first thing they do is turn towards each other and give each other a hug. Yes, I’m a heartless bastard, but I’ve spared their lives. I’ve given them a new hope and they came through this obstacle because they loved each other. “You both passed the test. Congratulations.” Instead of growing angry, they don’t seem to care. They love each other and I don’t believe they will ever stop hugging each other. I reach in my pocket and take out the key. I grab the man’s hand and place it in it. “You’ve won the key to this locked room. Feel free to leave.” He grabs her and they both run towards the door. He fiddles with the key a little and unlocks it. He opens the door with his shoulder and storms down the hallway, holding her hand with every step. The stomps seem to echo throughout the hallway walls until they eventually die. The only thing tonight that died. I look towards the formation. They stand there, two of them are crying and another runs towards the door. I pick up the pistol shoot him in the knee. He falls to the ground. I smirk, an evil grin. I walk towards the door. I walk towards the fallen soldier. The thief who stole my time and wasted it. I stand over him. He is crying, louder than the couple. And I laugh. “What did you think you were going to do” Run home and call the police? Did you actually think I was going to let you run out of the room? I gave you your time to walk out and you did not do it. So now, you will pay the price.” I point the gun towards his head and pull the trigger. It fires, thundering a hellacious sound throughout the room. Blood pours from his head and I take a step back because I don’t want it to stain my shoes. I turn around and face the formation. “Some of you have just witnessed your first murder. If you stay with us, then you will witness many more. We aren’t a gang, simply because we aren’t sloppy. If you turn your back on us, we will find you. We are organized, with only two members, we can do whatever we want. Your time is now, you can go home or you can stay. You decide and that’s all. But let me set this straight, if you say any word to anybody about what you have just seen tonight, we will kill you. So leave if you will, for this will be your only chance to ever again.” I didn’t expect anybody to leave, especially after just seeing what they saw. But every single one of them except one man left through that door. I watch them walk out and then I turn my attention towards the one who stayed. He smirks, an evil grin. He must be a Davis. “Welcome, Jonathan. Welcome.” He pats him on the shoulder and closes the stage curtain. We turn around and step over the man’s dead body and walk through the door, together. United. “Good show. Very good show.” Jonathan Davis says with a smirk. Heartless, he’ll fit in well. Our first member. Nevermore
