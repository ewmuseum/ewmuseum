Training Yet Again

8 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: )April 8(For APRIL 17) You know I never knew that there was such thing as a great e-fed but I proved myself wrong. You see Global Wrestling everybody knows is the best fed in the world. In Global Wrestling I will do some accomplishments. First beat The Creeper then go for maybe the Platnium Title or Cruiserweight title. In GW I will try to be the best guy in the fed which probably will never happen but you never know. There will be some ups and downs but I will manage it. But outside GW I have a real life, training other wrestlers, maybe capturing bad guys. But GW is my life and no one and I mean no one will take that away from me. Roswell starts walking down the street in a slow pace waiting for traffic to go by and keeps on walking. Finally he reaches the building he goes to every day, “Larry’s Training Center.” Roswell slowly walks into the building out of sight. Inside the building a brown walls with little rocks coming out to poke. There is very soft carpet under the feet of Roswell. The brown counter takes up most of the space, workers are working as hard as they can. Roswell glares over at the clock. 5:00 the brownish black clock reads. Roswell: Just in Time. Down a long narrow hallway there are 5 doors going along it. They are numbered 1-5, Roswell quickly runs into the 5th one. Inside there are bear white walls but still the same carpet. Roswell who is carrying a black gym bag sits down on a brown stool. A man walks out from behind a curtain, dressed in black gym clothes. Larry: Roswell, you just made it. Larry walks over to Roswell and pats him on the back. Larry sits down and starts to talk to Roswell. All of a sudden a 19 year old flies through the door with papers in his hand and a back pack on. He quickly gives the papers to Larry as he puts on his reading glasses and starts to read the papers. Larry: So your wrestling name is Fang? Fang: Yes sir I wrestle under that name. Larry: Well you will be wrestling Roswell, he is in Global Wrestling. Fang: Global Wrestling! I love that fed, I read about it everyday! Roswell opens the sport bag he brought and brings out a black and red singlet, black elbow pads, black knee pads, and black lace boots. Roswell takes off his normal shirt and pants and puts on the singlet and all that other crap. Fang quickly opens his back pack and brings out black baggy pants, and boots. Roswell and Fang both walk back behind the curtain that Larry came out of. All of a sudden Lifestyles of the rich and the famous by Good Charlotte blares through the little room and Fang quickly runs and slides into the ring. He goes to the top rope and raises his fist. He then goes by a man and asks for a little microphone. Fang: First I want to thank Roswell and Larry for letting me do this. “Save Me by Remy Zero is heard and Roswell walks down the little ramp and slides in to the ring. He goes up top and raises his hands. He does that to every turnbuckle and then stops. Roswell and Fang shake hands and then lock up. Roswell who is taking advantage pushes the kid backwards. Roswell bounces of the red ropes and hits a big leg drop. 1............................... 2............................... THR...NO! Roswell picks Fang up and hits a overhead belly-to-belly, Fang who is laying on the ground slowly gets up and gives Roswell a low-blow! Fang hits a spinning heel kick and covers Roswell. 1.......................... 2..No! Roswell quickly gets up and kicks Fang in the gut, he brings him up and hits him with a vicious sit-out powerbomb, Roswell who is wasting no time jumps to the top rope and leaps off with a Shooting Star Press. 1.................................. 2....................................... 3................................ DING DING DING! After the match was over Roswell walks over to Fang, he seems to be hurt. Roswell trys to lift him up to his feet but then Fang low-blows Roswell making him fall to the ground, he picks up Roswell and hits a powerful punch straight to Roswell’s temple. Larry runs down the little ramp and holds back Fang and starts checking on the fallen Roswell. SCENE FADES.
