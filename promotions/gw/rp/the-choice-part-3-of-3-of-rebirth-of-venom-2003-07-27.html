The Choice part 3 of 3(Of Rebirth Of Venom)

27 July 2003

The Choice part 3 of 3(Of Rebirth Of Venom) Venom Alexander Global Wrestling Promo #472 Date: 7/27/03 Location: unknown (the scene opens in a dream Venom finds himself in a world of wonder. A place he has never seen before. Where everything looks to good to be true.) “Who are who?”Asked Venom, in a quite tone voice. “I’m someone who know very well, someone you are keeping inside of you.” Explained the unknown figure, who’s face was covered up with some kind of cloth. Venom tries to make his way to the unknown figure, but some kind of shield is blocking him off. “What the hell. Why can’t I see your face? Is it like messed up or something.” Venom just looks and wonders, why the figure’s face is covered up. “If I show you my face Venom, then what you been wondering all this time would be a waste for you and me. How, should I put it, I am your thought. For days now, you have been a nervous wreck after you wreck. In where you girlfriend was killed when you went down the band. On top of that, you lost a best friend Cobra as well. You feel that you are helpless now, that you are afraid you ego will take the best of you. You have a choice to make. Do you really want to release that ego from the past? Where people have hated you cause of it.” Venom sits down, realizing what his thought was telling him was true. He has been a nervous wreck since his best friend and girl died all in less than a montht. “You know, the ego in which I had in the past doesn’t affect me. If I do get that ego again I will rest ashore I will have control over it. Make sure it does not affect my relationship with Roz, as well as my fans out there, that support me each time I step in a GW ring.” “What about Jay Vyper? He is your friend you be jealous since his return.” Replied the figure. “I respect Jay Vyper in and out of the ring; I’ll admit backstage at GW all the focus is on Jay Vyper. I know why, he is one hell of an athlete he is the reason why I am here today. No matter what, if he is still part of the nevermore, sure they are the enemy but the friendship will never change.” A teary eyed Venom walks up to the shield and draws his hand back and gives it one hell of a punch to give it a crack. “I see you made your choice Venom, you angry shows me that you alter ego, in which you called yourself Mr. V.A, is dead. “What you are talking about?” As a puzzled Venom looks on. The figure steps out of the force shield, and takes off the cloth that’s been hiding his face. “Holy Shhhh….” Venom in shocked walks backwards and trips over his feet, as the figure reveals himself. “I know Venom; it’s hard for you to look at me this way. You have to live the past behind and move on you success is close my son. Remember, Cobra told you so.” The figure was, Venom’s father, who past away when he was still a kid. Venom walked up to him to give him a hug, but the his father disappeared. Venom wakes up from the dream, and looks down beside his bed and finds a photo, of him and his dad, it was taken before his father past away. Venom grabs it, looks at and sheds a tear. “Father, thank you for making my choice a lot easier. That was a talk I needed, instead of some stupid therapist, who was waste of money.” Venom puts the photo away in a safe place. Hours later, he heads off to find Roz at his home recovering. “Roz, how its going, here lately I’ve been thinking of a lot of stuff, you probably know how my ego is, well I had a long talk with a special someone, and there is no need to worry, the Venom Alexander that stands before you. Is the Venom that will never ask for respect, the one you see is the Venom that will earn respect, by putting on a performance week after week. And Roz when you return, will we make our way to the top being one of the greatest tag teams of all time. Roz, looks at Venom, he is glad that Venom isn’t going to change for no one. Venom leaves Roz house, and heads back into his dodge and drives away. He knows that Cobra, and his girlfriend are still with him in spirit. That he finally realizes he has to earn respect from everyone, not demand it. The scene fades away.
