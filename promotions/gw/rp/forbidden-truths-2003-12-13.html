Forbidden Truths

13 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-13-03 -Start of Video Footage- “The sight from here is so unbelievable to any eyes. It’s amazing to see such a piece of art with your eyes that seems so unreal, yet you can touch.” The reflection of a great lake, much bigger than a pond but not bigger than lake Erie or anything like that which is also surrounded by trees and dirt, just reflects is beauty as the sun rises. The birds fly by in the sky above the astonishment, letting it become even more of an attraction. On one side is a long dirt road that extends around a corner where trees and grass grow. As it turns around a house, wooden by default, but built by man for sure, stands and shows it’s untouched marvelous site. It has a dock that leads out only a little into the lake, letting the person who wishes to take a day off go out and fish or rowboat. “I can only remember the good times from this lake. It was my heart for so many years. Swimming, fishing, and so much more were done in the lake. It’s a wonder why I haven’t been back here more often.” Upon the door of the wooden house is nothing but a doorknob, no decorations, no window, just a doorknob, but such a beauty of a door for it’s made of the American Cherry tree. Opening it I walk in. Not caring if I awake some dead thoughts in my head or bring back the glory days of life in its roots. “I can only remember why I loved it so much. My dad, he was my favorite person in the world. He’d sit in the living room and tell me all these farfetched stories of great fishermen, or even of great sea hunters. Then I’d go out and try and act like them. I always took him serious and told him my adventures afterwards. It was great…” The windows in the living room are bay windows made to see the lake to the outside. From here we’d hang bird feeders on the outside and watch humming birds come and eat. “You never really know what will happen in a place like this. I mean, when I first lived here it was peaceful. Then when I came back after ten years of being away, some drunks owned it. Big differences huh?” “Defiantly. So what brought you back here this time? Tomorrow you have a match with near the whole Global Wrestling roster, yet you bring me out to this house for what? There has to be some reason Jesse.” “Yes there is. I’ve been sick of being in the city. Everything’s so loud. I’m a just plain sick of it. Why do people have to be so ignorant or so stupid to yell and fight in a city? You have to have your beeping and your honking of every car during red lights, no one says hi, and it’s plain sickening. You need to get away. And anyways… I wanted to show you something… Something a little bizarre, shall I say so myself.” I got up and lead him to the door that lead to the basement. No one would understand why, but in this basement my life began as a wrestler, and so for, I want to share it with all. “See those walls right there?” I said as I pointed to the walls with chains on them and a pair of handcuffs at the end of the chains. The room quite the same as it had been years before. Some blood on the cement was stained into cracks that could not be reached, and on the left a mirror was where there was broken glass also covered in blood, however this blood was my own, and it was quite fresh. “Yes that one. I use to tie myself upon that wall for days. I was fat once, and so I tied myself up until I was starving, and I’d have my friends bring me water. I didn’t really have friends, they were people who just thought I was weird and so wanted to watch me, but it was the same thing really. So I got a drink every day or so, and then after maybe a week or two without food I’d ask for my mother to come unhook me. She’d wonder how it happened, and I’d say I was messing around. She was stupid but she was a great mother. Anyway, as I grew up I found that this was not the only reason I was in the basement a lot.” “Then why were you in the basement a lot? Does it have to do with that personality split thing you told me about?” “Yes… Exactly. As I grew up it wasn’t really common, so I tied myself up to prevent harm from anyone. I knew what I was capable of, and that’s why I was so sick and deranged… I’ve killed people you see. But my other half, the dominant half, he doesn’t know. He’s in control until he sleeps, and then that’s when I take over. You know how some people have sleep walking problems? Well to Jesse that’s just his other half waking up. I know that you’ll turn this video in but don’t worry people know about me. I’m fine, but Jesse won’t be.” “So what do you want to do next? ”Well…” Then as I turned my back to him I grabbed the glass and swung around lodging it into his neck, cutting his throat, and then with my hands covered in blood I wretched the glass across his face as many times as I could. Not for the fact that I wanted to hurt him, but to get the thrill I needed, to get what I wanted out in the open. I wanted to be free! And finally I did. Standing up I gripped the camera, and looked into it’s lens, my face a crimson mask, as my hair leaked the blood I had been scavenging in. I swept it back with my hand, and laughed into the camera only before shutting it off. -End of Video Footage- “And that’s the footage of you only 3 months ago… So my question to you is, are you better?” “Yes,” replies Jesse with a blunt expression. “Then I shall have to check with my superiors, but Jesse… You’re not looking better. You’ve grown stronger, how do we know you’ll take the medicine, or even behave yourself after so long of being in the real world alone?” “You don’t, and that’s why you have to trust me. I don’t remember ANY of that footage sir, but trust me, I’m not going to act like that, and I will for sure take the medicine,” claimed Jesse now. “Good to hear that. You may go to your room now.” Jesse stands up from the chair and walks to the door. He opens it up and leaves the room going down the hallway. As he reaches his door a guard opens it for him, and he walks in and smiles. Not just some normal smile. But a sick, twisted, evil smile that only comes from a man who is possessed.
