Stitches

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Deacon Holmes is seen perched upon a small wooden stool inside of a plain white room complete with no window. Just four walls, one of which having a door located at the center of it is directly behind Holmes. There is also a small counter with rows of metal handled drawers and a few glass cylinders resting atop it, each holding something different. Cotton balls and popsicle sticks are just some of the things held in these jars. There is also another man in the room. He stands a lanky 6'1', and wears a white coat that comes about an inch pas his knees showing a pair of black slacks and a pair of Nike walking shoes. He stands to Deacon’s right and deftly works a needle and thread in and out of the open wound that crosses Holmes? brow. With each insertion and removal of the needle Deacon’s eye twitches as he remembers the beating he took on Monday. First the high angle chokeslam off the turnbuckle, then the huge inverted DDT, and finally the two sit out powerbombs from the top rope that it took to beat him. Any other man would be glad to have had it take so much just to put him down for three seconds, but not Deacon Holmes. With each twitch Holmes grows even madder. His eyes burn with fire as more and more veins burst from his body. Deacon Holmes is fuming. He breathes heavily through his nose and tenses up even more as Dr. Parker puts in the final three stitches. A large vein develops in his neck and he begins to shake. Just when it seems he’s about to explode a loud smack is heard causing him to lose his entire train of thought and his head to rock forward. He steps off his stool and turns around to find all 6'6' two hunred and seventy pounds of his uncle CW behind him laughing his ass off. Dr. Parker cuts the loose thread and leaves the room looking somewhat frightened. He closes the door firmly behind himself. CW: Told ya he was gon give you some act right boy! Should’a had yo punk ass in tha gym instead of havin that wild ass three day party and settin fires in abandoned warehouses. Deacon Holmes: Not now unc. CW: Oh don’t go tryin to get all sensitive now, I told you from tha start that guy was gonna give you a run. That’s what you get anyway for burning down that chump’s hideout. Deacon Holmes: I said not now! Do you no longer understand english Clifford!” CW: Clifford? Oh I got your Clifford right here, soon as you get big and bad enough feel free to come and get some! Deacon Holmes: Be serious, I’m not scared of your decrepit retired wrestler ass. I know it’s hard to sit back and watch me seeing as how I’m better at 18 than you ever were, but at least you can win vicariously through me. CW flashes a toothy smile before suddenly snatching the stool that Holmes once sat on and hurls it at him. Holmes ducks causing the stool to shatter upon contact with the white painted brick wall, he then charges his uncle taking him to the floor with a high single leg takedown which he then floats over into a cross knee hold. CW grunts in agony before chopping Deacon in the throat causing him to relinquish the hold and roll over coughing. CW gets to his feet and begins to stomp Holmes leaving black footprints all over the back of his white tanktop. CW: Come on Deac?, I thought you were better than this. Holmes quickly rolls over and catches CW’s right leg and wraps both of his around it before using his right arm to trip CW’s left leg sending him to the ground. Holmes keeps the leg scissors on CW’s right leg and begins to torque the ankle, causing him to write in pain. Deacon Holmes: Had enough old man? CW struggles to find a way out before the pain becomes too much CW: Alright damnit! I give up! Deacon immediately lets go of the hold and both men roll over and climb back to their feet. CW does it much faster than Holmes and ceases the opportunity to get a cheap shot in. He rushes Holmes who’s back is turned pushes him to the wall before ramming his head into it which reopens his stitches. Holmes feels the slow trickle of blood from his brow and checks it with his hand. As he stumbles about CW locks in a full nelson. With CW being 6'6' and Holmes being only 5'11' CW rather easily lifts Holmes off his feet in this manner leaving Holmes? feet kicking air. Finally they find the wall which he uses to push himself off of causing both men to hit the floor with CW’s 275 pound to break the fall but he holds on. Now using one of his arms to choke Deacon who flails his free arm while rolling left and right looking for a way out of the chokehold. He then rolls backwards causing the toes of both his feet to hit the ground. Deacon Holmes: 1!...2!...3!...Alright, let me g... Holmes collapses and goes dead weight. CW lets go of the hold and rolls Holmes off of him before getting back to his feet. He leans on the counter with one hand as he removes a cigar from his pocket and lights it. After a few puffs he looks down on the ground at Holmes who is still lying there unconscious. CW walks over still disgusted at his loss and kicks him in the ribs. CW: Wake yo ass up! Plenty of time to sleep when you’re dead! The room’s door slowly creaks open as Dr. Parker returns looking down at a clipboard with some paperwork attached to it. Dr. Parker: Alright guys, now all that’s left to do is fill out these few... Dr. Parker stops dead in his tracks and looks around the room to find a shattered wooden stool piled up against the wall, CW leaning against the counter smoking a cigarette, and Deacon Holmes, who is once again bleeding, slowly crawling to his feet with the help of the wall. He turns a bewildered eye to CW. Dr. Parker: What tha hell went on in here!” The stool’s broken, he’s gotta get stitched back up, and you’re smoking! Where do you think you are!” This is a hospital! This isn’t Compton! Now put that damn thing out! NOW! CW gives the doctor a smug look as he puts out his cigar on the counter and then gets in his face CW: Listen doctor in case you didn’t see, my nephew over there is bleeding. Now unless you wanna bleed too I suggest you get to work with that needle and thread! CW steps out of Parker’s face and helps Deacon to his feet. He walks out the door under the watchful eye of Dr. Parker. Halfway to the door he stops abruptly and jumps at the doctor causing him to jump back. CW: Ya see that D? He flinched like a little bitch! Now hurry up and get sewed up, I got a little surprise for ya when we get back to Orlando. CW walks out the door laughing as Dr. Parker goes in the drawer and grabs a new needle to stitch Deacon up with. He begins, Deacon’s eye starts to twitch.
