The long road home. Part II.

24 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 24th, 2004. I groaned softly as I collapsed onto the hunter green sofa that sat in my hotel room directly across from the Television. 'No more pizza.' I said to myself as I reached over to the table in front of me and flipped the lid to the box shut covering the remaining three pieces. I pushed away the remnants of a twenty-four ounce Mountain dew that rested near the box as I snatched the remote from the floor. Stretching out across the couch my bare feet hung over one arm of the couch and my head rested on the other. The dead TV showed my reflection as I lie stretched across the couch wearing only a shimmering pair of silver gym shorts and no other attire clinging to my body save for the cross pendant around my neck. Reaching up with my left hand I playfully tugged at my messy black locks before resurrecting the TV with a simple push from my right index finger. The TV buzzed to life quickly as the colors danced beautifully across this twenty-seven inch, flat screen, high definition marvel. As it came to life it was on channel twenty…a channel, which I recognized as a local news program. On a whim I decided it was best to leave it there to catch up on the latest in Nebraska news… I found it strikingly odd that I appeared to be the latest in Nebraska news. Sure enough it was a picture of yours truly that sat in a small picture box on the right side of the screen on KHOL news. Below my picture the words 'American Youth' Josh, were written and this seemed to be all too interesting as it commanded my full attention. 'Also in today's news, Professional Wrestler and fellow Nebraska native Josh made a return to his home state. A news anchor said dryly with all of the raw emotion usually reserved for a strangers eulogy. The man looked to be quite the bore as it was, sitting there in his black suit, white shirt and red tie, with his black hair neatly parted. Sitting at his side however was a rather attractive co-anchor that looked as if she were in her late twenties. Blonde hair, glasses, and a neat black two piece business skirt, she had a rather serious tone about her…yet a sensual demeanor as well. 'That's right Aaron' She began acknowledging what her colleague had just said. 'Global Wrestling has decided to make it's first ever trip to Nebraska and it's only fitting that Global Wrestling's only Nebraska native will headline that show. For more information we'll go live to Dani Cade on location.' With those words spoken the cameras cut away to an attractive young brunette in her early twenties standing in the parking lot of Memorial stadium wearing jeans and a soft white T-shirt. 'Thanks Aaron and Colleen.' Said the attractive young lady with a pause before continuing on. 'Normally when you come to Memorial stadium it's to see the Husker's take the field and compete against another collegiate team. However that wont be the case this upcoming Monday because it's here at Memorial stadium that Global Wrestling will hold an event, and not just any event but…their biggest event of the year. With a few seats added to the field the stadium now has the capacity to hold eighty-two thousand people, and it comes as no surprise to the Global Wrestling executives that this show is already sold out. As the attractive young lady ended her part, the cameras cut away to Global Wrestling spokesman Chris Hanover. Mr. Hanover is standing outside Memorial stadium as cameras flash away wildly and he speaks in his professional tone, wearing a gray business suit and black shirt beneath with his short blonde hair combed neatly. I watched him with an eyebrow raised due to curiosity. 'This is no doubt the biggest event of the year for Global Wrestling. We travel from place to place on our Pay-Per Views so the odds of watching a GW Pay-Per View in person without having to travel a long distance…those odds are pretty slim.' Said Mr. Hanover as he continued to speak into the cameras. 'I also believe that this is the first event we've ever held in the heartland and I'm certain that this is our first show held in Nebraska. We're really breaking into virgin territory here and we've had an overwhelming rush of interest in this Pay-Per View, not only for it's magnitude but for it's location as well.' Mr. Hanover said as they cut back to the young lady in the parking lot as she stood next to a large Global Wrestling production truck. 'A Wrestling event of this proportion has never been held in Nebraska which is yet another reason why the hype for this show has the fans buzzing with anticipation. Not only will the stadium be packed with the residents of Nebraska but the Global Wrestling faithful have traveled far and wide to see this show.' The young reporter said as the cameras once again left her and focused on an aging gentleman in his fifties as he stood in front of the camera wearing a white T-shirt and a red Husker's baseball cap that shadowed his weathered face. Below his image was a caption reading 'Ted Armon, Wrestling fan.' 'I think it's great for the state of Nebraska to be able to hold an event like this.' Ted stated rather nonchalantly. 'This is the biggest event of the year for Global Wrestling so it's going to be the best show of the year. I bought my tickets the very second I heard that they were going to be in Lincoln.' Mr. Armon said with his a thrilled sound in his voice. The cameras left that Wrestling fan and went to another, a younger man, roughly in his early twenties with a shaved head and a Boston Red Sox T-shirt on, below his face a caption read 'Joey Mivera, Wrestling fan.' 'I came all the way from Boston to see this.' Joey said with his accent proving his Boston roots to be true. 'It's going to be an extravaganza that wont soon be forgotten. I bought my ticket the very day they went on sale and my friends and I piled into the car and took a ride down here to Lincoln because this is a show we can't afford to miss.' As Joey finished what he had to say the cameras cut back to the young reporter, Dani Cade, as she now leaned against the front fender of a Global Wrestling production truck. 'The show is still several days away but the entire state is about to explode with excitement as they'll be able to witness the first ever Global Wrestling event in the state of Nebraska. And with the main event on that card featuring a fellow Nebraska native…I think we all know who we'll be cheering for.' The lovely young reporter smiled into the cameras her dazzling smile briefly captivating me until the scene cut away from her and back to the studio where the two anchors sat. 'In other news today…' The boring male anchor began before being cut off by a simple press of the power button on the remote control as the TV returned to it's deactivated state. Standing to my feet I couldn't help but smile as I thought of my name echoing throughout Memorial stadium with over eighty thousand strong shaking the stadium with their thunderous roar. It would be a moment etched in history…a man who had distanced himself from the fans would suddenly find himself the recipient of cheers as he took on the role of the hometown hero, it couldn't have been written more amazingly had it been a Hollywood production. The only thing missing…was the storybook ending. A storybook ending of a hometown hero making his triumphant return, an ending that showed the underdog defying the odds and achieving his dream, an ending that would crown 'American Youth' Josh as the champion. Perhaps it was time to ink that chapter into the pages of destiny… It's often been said how the stars control our future, yet the only stars that control me are the ones that shine brightly in my eyes as I lustfully stare at the World title. Walking slowly across the floor of my room I stepped into the spacious sleeping quarters, throwing back the silk curtains I gazed out the window of this famed Embassy Suite's hotel room. From my eighth floor vantage point I could easily look across the city where only a half mile away sat the famed stadium I had grown up to cherish. Every Saturday meant Nebraska football…where our heroes took the field. This Monday would be different because two gladiators would step into the ring with an ultimate goal…to walk away as the World Champion by any means necessary, yet only one would gain success on that mission. In the most unlikely circumstances I had reason to believe that it would be I that walked away as the World Champion. 'American Youth' Josh…the scrawny punk kid from Nebraska. The next World Champion. It was time to close this chapter…for I had another to begin as soon as I became World Champion.
