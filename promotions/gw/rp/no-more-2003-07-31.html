NO MORE

31 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/31/03 'A COLD STAR PRODUCTION' ( in colaboration with HBO and Global Wrestling television) A picture of Ice running on a treadmill is showing in a flashing strobe light effect. Metallica's 'There Is Only One Guarantee' plays in the background. The picture of Ice on the treadmill flashes from Ice on the treadmill to Ice lifting dumbells, to Ice wrestling in the ring, some of the in ring footage is Ice getting beat down, and some is of Ice in control, handing out the punishment. If I could have my wasted days back, would I use them to get back on track? Stop to warm at karmas burning or look ahead, but keep on turning? The footage changes to Ice sitting alone in a locker room with his head in his hands. Then as the music continues to play it changes to Ice pacing thoroughout the back stage area of the Global Arena, walking through airports, and doing personal appearances. Do I have the strength to know how I'll go? Can I find it inside to deal with what I shouldn't know? Ice frantically doing bench press after bench press rep flashes on and off the screen. Ice slams the bar on the rack and sits up as the flashing screen stops. His face flushed red, sweating seeping from his pores. Ice lifts his right hand and slowly pulls the strands of blond hair out from in front of his face, and stares intensley into the camera lens. The music stops. 'The silence ends. As my grandmother lies on her death bed I have taken it upon myself to be by her side and keep to myself. I let the travesty that occurred on Evolution slide. I let those who wander about this company looking to make a name through me, and my abscence do what they feel they need to do. But now I am back, and there is hell to pay.' An evil sneer grows across Ice's calm solemn face. 'I've been around for a couple months, and have bided my time, and played by your rules. I played the game the way you wanted me to play it. I did what you asked me to do. I whored myself out for absolutely nothing. And it took this reality check to make me realize it. It took this slap in the face by life to show me just what I have become, and just how much I hate it. Well NO MORE! ...................................' Ice pauses. He looks down and to the right at the ground beneath him. His vision doesn't change as his voice secretly seeps from his mouth, and over his lips. 'Deacon Holmes, you escaped on Evolution, because of your little bitches coming to your rescue. Because of that spotlight stealing son of a bitch Brick. Well again I say NO MORE!......NO MORE sitting back and letting the powers that be, and the so called superstars of this company screw me out of what is rightfully mine! NO MORE staying in the back letting those who are undeservant take the spotlight that I DESERVE! You see the facts of the matter are this. Since my arrival I HAVE YET TO BE DEFEATED! Yet week after week the BITCH MCCOY still overlooks me, sends out her goons to hold me down, and keep me from doing the unthinkable, the unwanted. My unbeaten record should speak for itself. My unbeaten record should prove my deserving of much better things. But still I am not being noticed. Still I am sitting here and waiting for the powers that be to recognize me. NO MORE!' Ice violently snaps his head back flipping his long blond hair out from in front of his face. 'At this upcoming pay-per-view, I show the bitch, the powers that be, and the so called superstars of this company just how much I deserve the spotlight I seek so much. I'm not going to walk into Master of the Mat to win that stupid tournament. I'm not walking into MOTM to win a stupid cruiserweight title belt. Ms. McCoy, I suggest sending out a memo to your roster, and your precious staff, because I am putting everyone on notice. I don't care if you are a wrestler, a cameraman, an announcer, or the freaking janitor of the arena. If I feel like hurting you, I WILL! It starts with a ladder match, and ends with an Iron Man Match. One by one, BRICK by BRICK, I will eliminate this entire roster if I have to! You think my words are just a pharse. You think I'm just talking to hear myself talk. Well then step up, and see if you can put that first blemish on my record. See if you have what it takes to step out of your comfort zone, and step into a new world.' The scene cuts to black.
