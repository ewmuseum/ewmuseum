The Journey (Part 10/Joshua's POV)

2 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-02-2005 Early Afternoon Its the day after New Years and all through NYC not a creature was stirring not even a...ok you get the idea right? Well folks its time yet again to pay a visit to “The Big Apple”, New York City and today was kind of a weird day because a lot of people were suffering from hangovers from the day before so a lot of doctor’s appointments were being scheduled because of the epidemic that was sweeping the city. Of course in one part of the city that wasn’t the case... “The Residence”, that is what a lot of people call the home of one of the most prolific stables in the history of Professional Wrestling or Purowresu as they call it in Japan, The Connection live, breathe, study and workout for their matches and any other activities that they need to do. Some say that they are hermits but that’s not necessarily true but when you go by the schedule that they have at times it doesn’t leave a whole lot of time for fun and games to be had because they always believe that they have to stay at the top of their game and that can be a blessing and a curse for the group but when you have some of the accomplishments that they do you can’t afford one off day in a year because there are and always will be those whom want to knock you off of your perch every single day that you exist! Take today for example: Now most wrestlers when they have about a week or so to prepare for a PPV take a couple of days off and chill out with their friends and family but not the subject of this story and you know who he is.... Joshua L. Curtis, The “Golden Boy” of professional wrestling as some have dubbed him is in the “Dungeon” or as he likes to call it “The Torture Room” because he puts his body through so much abuse that some people have questioned whether the young man from London, England is in fact sane but you only have to back to about 15 years ago when Joshua had a match in Budokahn Hall against one of his mentors in Japan, Jushin Liger and he nearly shattered his leg to win his first ever Junior Heavyweight Title at which point the fans went nuts and not because he was hurt but because he was willing to put his livelihood on the line and there were people that actually chanted “*** ****!” when Joshua got up and walked on his jello leg and PINNED Liger for the title! That match made Joshua a legend in Japan and a lot of fans still believe if he had stayed he would have replaced people like Muta and Pogo as icons and some actually hold him to those pedestals now! I know you want the point of this right? The point is that Joshua was willing to torture his own body to become someone and to change the face of Pro Wrestling today but I’m digressing aren’t I.... As you travel into the recesses of the residence we find Joshua wearing nothing but a t-shirt, sweatpants and is in his bare feet punching one of the hardest and most abrasive hevy bags that he could find and is continuing to punch it with the oly observer being the reflection in a cracked mirror that that he puches every time he screws up on set, move or whatever he is doing...heck if he messes up on his tax return he goes and puches it!(Although now Ami does them for him so :) ) *This is for jumping me from behind at DR and this is for being born Adam!* Joshua thinks to himself as he spin kicks the bag so hard that it nearly breaks the chain! *Good* Joshua thought *mission accomplished* as he finished his thought he looked above him and about 10 feet above him was the bar that he used for chin ups and sit ups! I know what you’re thinking *How do you use that bar for sit ups?* am I right? Well when he goes to use the chin up bar he first gets a chair to boost him up to the bar and then kicks the chair away after he has hold of the bar. To make things interesting he does the chin ups overhand and underhand 25 each and then somehow gets the energy to flip up so that his feet catch the bar and then he does something like 30 sit ups that way! Generally this part of the exercise takes about 45 minutes because he usually is so beat to hell from the chin ups that he takes his time doing the sit ups but he gets tehm done and then its on to the push ups after he flips himself down on the ground so that the first thing that hits is his knuckles and then proceeds to do 60 push ups(20 palms ,20 knuckles and 20 claps) all in succession! Is he nuts for putting his body at risk like this? Yeah but also don’t forget this is your first time ever seeing the regimen that he puts his body through every day to succeed in the business that is Pro Wrestling. After about....oh 3 hours total Joshua decided to go up to the main floor to go use the bathroom to take a shower and believe me when I tell you that when the gang saw him after doing his usual workout routine they knew that he put his body through hell to get his body ready for his match at the next PPV against someone that he doesn’t really consider a nemesis but a pain the neck nonetheless in Adam Myers for the Cruiserweight title. “Ah that feels good!” Joshua says to himself after he turns on the water in the shower ad does his best to wake up from the beating his body took in the basement. You may or may not realize it but Joshua does this so much to himself but his body never really gets used to it and there have been times when Zack has pleaded with him to not do this to himself but Joshua is a stubborn one and insists that its only to try and keep himself focused on his goals because if he slips one day then people start to forget him and the impact that The Connection is going to make in Pro Wrestling someday. Has Zack tried to play that off? Oh a hundred times or more but Joshua continually performs the ritualistic torture to his body that scares Ami, Tina and Zack to death! Why didn’t I mention Michiru? because of the fact that she does the same thing with Joshua many days in a year because she wants to become the tops in other countries like Japan, England, Australia and others. Michiru wants to learn about the business from Joshua and the others so she goes through the different training regimens that Joshua does with him to learn the price that is to be paid for success and he’s willing to teach her. Does Ami understand that? Yes she does and she watches Joshua teach Michiru the basics as well as some of the submissions that he learned to help her learn about pain tolerance so that when she gets in those situations it takes longer for her to have her will broken to even THINK about submitting! Oh yes submissions is all about how much “Will” or how far your believe your body can go before you either blackout or tap out. When Joshua wrestled in Japan and trained with Liger at first he tapped in 5 seconds from a leg grapevine but now unless you really kick the tar out of him it takes a while! When Joshua got done taking his shower he went upstairs to the bedroom that he and Zack shared and grabbed his notebook before getting changed in the big stand in closet that they have and then went downstairs to the study where Ami had only been a day earlier and looked over the notes that he had on Adam from watching the tapes that a friend of his gave him to study and before shutting the door he actually announced that anyone interested in seeing what he does the week of a big match can come into the study with him. Ami and Michiru obliged and, even though Zack basically already knew he went in anyways because this was a rare day that Joshua allowed people into the study when he was going to look over notes but how else was Ami and even Michiru going to learn about the in and outs of what Joshua does for a living? When the trio went into the room Michiru closed and locked the door before taking her seat next to Ami in the Study and just so that everyone could see the notebook Joshua made sure that he sat at the table , which still contained the kettle of tea that Michiru had from when her and Ami had it the night before! “OK gang” Joshua began “this is one of the notebooks that I keep on the different wrestlers in different territories. Zack knows I keep like 350 of these at the moment and if anyone needs to borrow any of them tell me who you are facing cos I either faced him/her or have a report on them sotwed away for reference....yes Michiru?” Joshua asked as the auqamarine-haired girl raised her hand to speak. “Is it true that one of his finishers is called ‘The Cure’?” Michiru asked and Joshua nodded before speaking again. “Yes it is my dear and honestly not having it slapped on me I couldn’t begin to tell you what it feels like nor *Lord willing* will I ever have to. I hear its a painful move and that’s why I left this space blank under the ‘description’ subheading. It is also known that he has a girlfriend and honestly I can give two craps what her name is but I’m sure I could find an equalizer for her if I need to.” Joshua said somewhat mischievously and both Ami and Michiru smirked at the thought the thought that they shared with Joshua. “I know you don’t have his notebook here with you but what about Christian Zenith?” Zack asked and while Joshua was willing to answer the question he was annoyed that Zack broke a rule when they have a group study session. “Well honestly as far as he goes I think he’s a little bit of a punk but other than that a decent human being...just not the type I want hanging with us y’know?” Joshua replied and Zack nodded as Ami raised her hand to ask a question “Yes Ami” Joshua replied and the young prodigy cleared her throat before speaking. “What of Vivica Valentine? Isn’t she like Zenith’s girlfriend or something? Ami asked and Joshua realized where that was going... “I believe so. DO take care of that little pest for us ok?” Joshua asked and both Ami and Michiru smiled! Somehow I think they got plans for the young rook in due time! As the study session ended the familiar purr of Joshua 1996 Dodge Viper ‘s engine could be heard in the garage amd when the clatter of a pair black high heels walked up the steps and the front door opened. “Hello?” See you in part 11 Au Revoir
