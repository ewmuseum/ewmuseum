The Silent Angel of War Pt.3

23 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 23rd, 2005 Sitting there against the wall with his hands on his knees, and his head slouched down. It was another day of misery and despair for the one Jesse Jamester. He’d nearly lost himself after committing murder to a fake doll, that he thought was real, but in reality was a set up. His eyes had begun to sink into his head, and leave a cast of shows from there pivoting dark craters. The blue eyes were now darkened to deep almost evil water like mercury blue. His beard now grown in full, with it hanging down from his chin a good four or so inches, and his hair out of control even in a ponytail. His face was thinner, but not because of starvation, but depression. Giving up is not an option. I’ve vowed not to, and I won’t? but being torn from these memories on the inside is killing me. If I don’t gain some control, I may as well just die from the suffering if not this hell hole. His eyes closed as they normally did, as this place had nothing to look at but the padded walls, and they only made him go mad. I hesitate when thinking of my escape? I know she’s not alive, she can’t be? I’d seen her in the casket, her body was prepared and al. There is no way she can be alive, and I know TBC well enough to believe this. Forget fighting for vengeance. Just get yourself out of this place before it consumes you Jess? Talking to himself was now a mere reality for him. Without anybody else, his mind and self had split in some ways and he’d began to ask and question himself of his methods and thoughts. The brink of insanity was near, as Jesse’s mind was traveling to the edge of it, and reality was in no way, shape, or form going to stop him. If I keep thinking about it? I’ll only talk myself out of it. I have to stop this now. Come on Jess, think of better things. Think about your daughter Samantha and your cousin Julius. Those are happy thoughts? come on. If I keep thinking of the nightmares in my life, I’ll only drive myself to relive them. This was a common process for Jesse, his mind bobbled the thought of death and the horrible memories of his past, but in no attempt did he try to stop them before. He had always been reminded to get out of the shadows by people close to him like Samantha and Julius, and now, it was finally kicking in that he had to do that to himself now. The thoughts of Samantha and her new dog? Awe the times. I can faintly remember her face. It’s been so long since we last talked, and now she’s almost nine years old. What a way to be a father, not seeing your child for five years. She probably has forgotten me already. Not that I’m really any type of father as is, but eh? I’d like to try and change that if I ever get out of here. It wasn’t fun being tamed. The jackal, as he was referred to in the ring, was a fairly well drawn out name for the mindset of Jesse. At times he’d be a happy, charming, youthful man, while at others he’d be ruthless, uncaring, and saturated with rage. It was like his mind gave an on/off switch, and it’d flicker each way whenever it wanted to. In reality it may sound superior as it allowed him to do what others couldn’t, but in his head, it was a demon locked up that one day would be his ticket into hell. *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** A cast iron object slammed across a wooden desk, shattering its top. The man sitting next to the table had nothing to say, he was sitting still just staring on as the fist rose again and this time stopped and positioned itself vertically so that the knuckles laid across the top of the table and imprinted themselves as the upper hand applied pressure. <>“You do realize, this is your last call right” You fuck up again and the boss will have your head on a plate. I don’t want to see you worrying about anything when your doing your assignment, you get it done, and fast. That is all you should worry about.” A dull impression sat upon the face of the man across the table. He brushed his lengthy brown hair back with his hand as he took a breath and just stared up the man. “So what does that matter” I’m going to die here anyway. My as well give someone a little satisfaction before I leave this hell hole eh?” The man turned, and moved his cast iron fist with him. As odd as it was, the hand did not seem to suppress his life style. He was not a broad man, but surely large in muscle at the pectorals. His head was hairless, as was his face, but his eyes glistened a bright baby blue, before his face opened your imagination to the fear this man could bring, when thought of in dreams. He showed no aspiration for sympathy for the man he had talked to and instead, left the room grumbling in disappointment. I’m not playing their games without some proof. And without that I’m just going to make them want me gone, and out of their whole experiment. Yeah sure it’ll probably not happen, like their going to just LET me walk away. Pfft. All I want is to get some choice in the things I do, and maybe, escape the horrific scenery that is to come. THUD! The noise echoes through the hallway, and into the room. Shoes slap the tiled floor as they enter the room. A blue shaded crew cut, almost spiked, but too messy to be, and a face that shows genius and psychotic intensions to it’s fullest extent. The man places his hand on the man in the chair, and smiles as he looks across the room. “You know what makes us different Jesse” It’s our intentions. Between us, we have a lot in common, but our intentions are far different from one another. You want to be something, while I just want to make money. See while your becoming that something you wish to be, I’m using you to gain the money I want through your success.” Jesse’s eyes refocus towards TBC. His face much different than last time he’d seen him, probably surgery, but that wasn’t what Jesse was focusing on. It was his eyes. The deep blue cold hearted eyes that showed no mercy as you could see right down into him, and see he had no soul. “Wait” So every time I wrestle, you make money?” A slight snicker develops from the silence of TBC. He turns and puts both hands on Jesse’s shoulders now as he stands behind him and keeps staring ahead. “Your getting it now. You’re my stock Jesse, and every time you go out there and win, you go up in value. The chemicals I’ve paid for and put in your body, are now making you the most indestructible man in all of wrestling, and you know what?” He bends over the shoulder of Jesse and whispers in his ear. “Theirs nobody who will stop me.” His breathe foul, a garlic and onion blend that makes your eyes water, but to Jesse it was natural to smell, since he’d had to live in a room that smelled much the same. TBC wasn’t a man that Jesse ever trusted, but now, he had to, as he was afraid what would happen without the chemicals that TBC was putting into him. “What if I stopped wrestling” What if I got hurt? Then what? how would you obtain your money?” Tightening his grip on Jesse’s shoulders TBC’s smile is wiped clean with these questions. He brings his head back up and stares at the wall as he replies. “Well, that won’t happen. You can’t be injured” Not physically anyway. You’re bones are like steel. You’re a prot’g? of Superman, you could say.” Jesse stared down at the floor, not realizing any of this, or for the most part not being able to cope with the fact that he was some test rat. “So are you saying” You don’t have a back up plan? You’re only plan as of now is to USE me into killing these people for you, and wrestling to make you money?” TBC lets go up Jesse’s shoulders and walks around the table slowly, as he does he speaks in a low tone, almost a whisper, but too vocally direct to be one. “I have other plans, but they are none of your business. As of now I have you wrapped around my finger so much, I could make you bark like a dog if I wanted to. Eh, why don’t I prove it” Bark? Come on, bark now.” Glimpsing up at him with an eyebrow jetting up like “what the fuck?” Jesse just stares at him and wonders if he’s serious. Then smiles and spits on the table as he holds his head up high. “I’m not barking for anyone” especially you, bitch.” Pulling something out of his pocket, he tosses it on the table does TBC. The thing he tosses on the table is pictures, some are upside down, some aren’t, but those that are have writing on the back. Jesse picks one up and looks at it? “Julie?” His jaw drops somewhat, as he leaves it open after saying her name in a questionable manner. Turning it over he reads the text. “In my heart I never thought I’d be left like this” No body even knows I’m here. How does ones life end up like this?” Tears swell in Jesse’s eyes. He knows how it feels to be alone, how everything seems to go a lot slower, and time doesn’t pass on normal minutes, but instead on hour-minutes. Every detail in your life runs through your head, until you don’t know why your still alive, because you think you’ve died already. To him, he had just seen a reflection of his life through the text, and because of that, he had become saddened. “Is she still alive?” The words escape his pursed lips almost too faintly to even hear. Yet TBC knows what he says, because somehow he knew he’d ask it. “Yes, she is alive.” A tear drop fell onto the back of the picture, smearing the writing just a bit. Looking up at TBC Jesse’s eyes glistened with sorrow and mercy. As he began to say it, TBC answered. “After this mission, yes.” With that Jesse nodded, knowing TBC wouldn’t budge on any offer. Picking up the rest of the pictures he looked through them, letting his tears fall freely. *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** I cope with things differently than others. It’s not that I’m strong in my mind, I just understand a bit more than most. When some people think their in trouble, they panic, or try to hold it all in. But that’s not something I do. I can’t panic? I have nobody to give myself to and complain. So if I panic I only put myself in a place I don’t want to be I don’t know if that’s just my way of thinking or if everyone thinks like that, but I’ve come to see people go insane as they try to figure out simple things. I just know that I have a goal, and I’m going to accomplish it, so my decisions reflect on the best path I can take to get to that goal. In the wrestling ring, I see myself doing things before I do them, and they normally pan out just as I seen them. Again I can’t explain it really, I guess it’s one of those things you feel when you’ve surpassed some type of barrier in the profession. If not, I’m still blessed I have it, and I’ll use it until I can no more. Sitting here I talk to myself, questioning a lot of things, and discussing subjects with myself. It’s weird I suppose, because people don’t normally do it, but in some absence of the mind, anything can be weird, if not looked at with an open mind. As the clock ticks down, I have to wait, it’s almost time for me to face Christian Zenith at Evolution on Monday Night? I can’t really judge him as much as I want. I’ve wrestled him once, and I was severely tired, but I know it wasn’t him who defeated me, but Frost who took that skateboard and busted it over my head. So Zenith will definitely have another thing coming if he thinks I’m not coming full throttle to prove why I am the International Champion. Though, I can’t say anything until the time comes. It’s pure speculation on what will happen until it does. So until I see Zenith, I’ll just sit here and think of all the things I mostly desire, and then I’ll clear them of my mind and try to realize how petty they really are compared to life itself. I have a lot of thinking to do? now that I realize where I stand in this whole fiasco. Lying on the floor he stares at the ceiling and looks up at the cracked white paint on it. Patterns of many things come to mind, but one that sticks in his head is the face of his wife Julie, and the thought of whether he’ll ever see her again.
