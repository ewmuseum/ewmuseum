The Time Is Coming

8 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5.8.03 May 8th, 2003, Logan International Airport The scene opens slowly; people stand all around the terminal as a line begins to form across the way, the flight’s seats opening up. The scene pans over the hundreds of facing waiting to board the plane that will take them to their final destination. The fight attendants and check’ins stand near the entrance; a couple of security guards stand near as well. Another pair of security guards takes someone behind a nearby screen as part of their random seed screening system. The scene pans back slowly, showing a busy walkway a few feet in front of the seating area, and then another seating area. This is also full as the scene pans, showing the desk near the entrance, a large window from which we can view the plane on the outside. A Boeing 747 is outside the window, a Delta Air symbol on the side of the plane. Seated next to the window, Tyler Pierce awaits the boarding call. He’s dressed in a black suit as usual with golden cufflinks, black tie and a blue dress shirt. He leans back in his seat while others watch the planes outside, he fools with the laptop inside of his briefcase; not the same one as we’ve seen before, a simple briefcase/laptop combo. After all, wouldn’t want to be caught with a weapon inside of an airport now would we? Tyler Pierce sighs a bit, his eyes grazing over the other patrons of this terminal waiting area? this place where frequent flyers come to wait for their next commute. He glances back towards his screen, a smirk on his face. Frequent flyers who have to use public flights, that is. Normally, Tyler would never be in a place like this? with his father’s business and Il Capo, he’s not been on a public flight in a little over a year. Today’s a different story. He closes his eyes for a moment and sits back, thinking of the events that took place yesterday, leading up to his being in this pit with hundreds of other plebeians awaiting the slow ass service of Delta Airlines. May 7th, 2003, The Harbor Side Inn of Boston Tyler lays in a large bed, the comforter strewn about but covering him. He lays on his back, his eyes closed and his arms folded beneath his head. A young woman lies beside of him; a woman with milky white skin of porcelain perfection. She has an arm draped over his chest, her raven black hair strewn about her shoulders and face, preventing her face from being shown. Suddenly she leans up and starts to kiss Tyler on the cheek and neck and squeezes him again. The situation is obvious and in no need of an explanation. It really makes you want to hate the guy gazing at young boy’s wet dream. She’s one centimeter shy of being a super model and about one inch short of being a clich? American Sex Symbol; a cross over between glamour model and girl next door, a true beauty. Tyler glances to his side at the nearby clock; 12:43 in the morning. They’d had a very long night, not that Tyler would ever complain. A quick dinner, three separate clubs in Boston, and finally back here for what would be one hell of a night cap. Of course, the final stages were in effect now, starting with breakfast. Or lunch, as it would be. She rolls over to the side and takes the sheet with her, keeping herself covered as she stands and walks across the room. The scene pans back, showing the luxurious hotel room, clothes scattered in a near straight line from the doorway to the bed. A knock is suddenly heard at the door as the young woman steps into the bathroom. Tyler, with a grown, sits up and pulls a nearby robe on. He stands, stretching his arms up into the air and sighs, completely waking to the new day only after half of it has passed him by. “Room Service.” Tyler groans, hearing who’s at the door. He quickly ties his robe and makes his way to the door, opening it quickly to find a small German woman standing here, a cart in the hallway behind her and a bundle of sheets and towels in her arms. She smiles at Tyler and glances down ? she must be in her sixties, her age just beginning to show but with a certain look of wisdom about her. Tyler shakes his head “Can you come back a little later” I’m a little busy right now.” The woman nods and starts off, blabbering on and on to Tyler. “Right, Sir, I’ll come back sir. Thank you sir, sorry for bothering you.” Tyler sighs and closes the door, locking it once it closes. He turns, leaning back against the door and closes his eyes ? he glances around the room and then right at his briefcase. He walks towards it and pops it open, quickly snatching his cell phone from inside. With a quick flip of his wrist and the press of a few buttons, he lifts it to his hear and mouth. The ringing commences. “Hey, Capo, it’s Tyler. I was just calling to check on the flight to Dallas?” There’s a pause and then a crinkle in Tyler’s brow as he glances down. He sighs. “A problem huh” How are we supposed to get to Dallas if the plane’s not picking us up?” There’s another pause as Tyler listens to the man on the other end of the phone. “Oh, Preston’s booking other arrangements” What kind of arrangements?” There’s a sigh as the man on the other end begins to relay the information to Tyler. He nods and goes to the briefcase, typing into his calendar. “Right” The 8th as 2 o’clock? Delta Airlines?” A sigh escapes Tyler but he doesn’t argue, he simply nods. “I really hate commercial airlines, ya know?” There’s another pause as the mumbles are heard, the other man speaking on the other end of the conversation. Tyler nods and glances at the laptop again. “Right, I understand, Capo. No problemo. See you in Dallas” four hours before Evo, right?” With a quick nod, Tyler presses enter on the laptop and closes the monitor down. He glances back towards the bathroom door as the sounds of a shower starts up. “Ok, Capo. Well, I’m gonna get going. Something just came up” yeah, might be coming up wet in a few minutes. Talk with you later. I’ll keep you updated?” There’s a quick pause. “right, bye?” With this, Tyler goes to the door and steps inside; a moment passes and you see his robe fly threw the open door and the scene fades to black. May 8th, 2003, Logan International Airport. Tyler opens his eyes suddenly, glancing up as he’s tapped on the shoulder. The camera pans up to show the beautiful woman who was in the hotel room the day before. She smiles at Tyler and leans down to kiss him quickly on the lips. He returns and glances around. “Boarding yet?” She shakes her head no and takes a seat next to him. The scene pans out, now revealing two men seated nearby, both in black suits. Who is Il Capo? What is this all about? Why guards for Tyler and who is he meeting four hours before Evolution on the 9th? Only time will tell. The scene fades to black. May 8th, 2003, A Boeing 747 heading for the Dallas/Fort Worth Airport A bright sun is blaring through the windows of the large airliner. A movie is playing in the top, “The Recruit” starring Al Pacino and Collin Farrell. The woman is asleep, her head resting quiescently on the shoulder of one Tyler Pierce. Tyler’s staring down at his laptop, a pair of headphones on connecting him directly to it. He seems to be examining a biography as he has many times before; he’s scrolled down however, enough so that we can’t see the images at the top of the page. Tyler smiles and scrolls up quickly, revealing a picture of his Evo opponent, The Ladies Man, Jay Robson. He closes the laptop and it powers off as he does so; he places it at his side and leans back in the seat, watching the movie that plays before the entire first class cabin. Thoughts pulse through the mind of Tyler, the movie fading into the background as he watches: “Frost and Jay Robson. I know they’re going to be working together and that adds for a whole new level of difficulty. It’s hard enough to consider a four way, having to watch your back for every second, but when you have two guys who will simply not be going at each other” ? Tyler shakes his head and glances up at the flight attendant as she walks by, his eyes wandering down to a steady point, staring at she walks away. “Mm” what I’d do to that.” The thoughts continue. “Sean Price and I alone against a tag team” That’s not going to work, it’s almost like a handicap matchup. We’re going to have to bust our asses just to stay alive if it stays this way? there’s no way around it. We’ll either have to work together, or take the loss. ? There’s a pause as Tyler gets engrossed in the movie, his eyes focusing on it for a few minutes. “I don’t lose well. I don’t care what happens on Evo, I’m not losing that match” if I have to defeat both men at the same damned time I’ll do it. TLM? Derek Frost. They may have the respect and time in Global but I’ve got something more than either of them will ever dream to have ? talent.” Tyler shifts a bit and rests his head back against the seat, closing his eyes. The scene fades to black. May 9th, 2003, just hours before Evolution at the Adolphus Hotel The scene opens, the lights fading up. Tyler Pierce stands in the main room of his suite, wearing nothing more than a robe. He’s on his cell phone, pacing back and forth. “The limo will be here in exactly one hour, right?” There’s a pause as Tyler glances towards the suit hanging near the full sized mirror inside of the cabinet. “Right. Preston, everything is set for tonight” The limo? Capo’s set up?” Another pause and nod. “Great. Well, I’m going. Catch you la?” Preston obviously cuts Tyler off, causing a smirk on The Prodigal’s face. “Look, I’ll deal with all three of them if I have to. But don’t you worry your little head on it” You just worry about arrangements, dealing with the legal bullshit, and making sure we have a ride back and a bottle of champagne in the limo for the victory toast. Got it?” Tyler nods and glances around the room. “Alright. I’m out. Catch you at the arena.” Tyler smiles and walks to the closet, grabbing his suit off the hanger. “Tonight’s the big night, boys. Tonight, we’re going to cause more than a few ripples. It’s time for a fucking explosion?” The scene fades to black as he speaks. “Tonight, we’re taking the reigns and proving to everyone” things are not as they seem. Power is only an illusion held by men with positions, power comes with force? power comes with me. With us.” The scene fades up again as Tyler steps out of a limo at a large building. He has a briefcase in hand, guards surround the area. Another limo pulls up directly behind him, another has already been parked in front. A bodyguard steps out and goes to open the door. The scene pans around, showing Tyler again. He slips a pair of Armani shades on and adjusts them, a grin on his face. We see another bodyguard opening the third limo door. “Tonight’s gonna be fun.”
