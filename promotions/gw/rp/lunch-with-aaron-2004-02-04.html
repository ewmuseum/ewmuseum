Lunch with Aaron

4 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/4/04 It was a Saturday afternoon. Often on Saturday afternoons, Brick and Aaron would get together and have lunch. Either they’d have Chinese or they’d eat at their favorite soul food restaurant. This Saturday, it was Chinese. Brick ordered a pint of chicken and broccoli with some fried dumplings. Aaron ordered a fish dinner. Brick often reached for napkins with which to wipe his hands. “So, how are you gonna deal with this Makonie cat?” asked Aaron. “How do I normally deal with a cat who disrespects me?” “Oh, right. I forgot who I’m talking to. Still, I wish I could come and kick his ass with you. That “coon” and “go back to Africa” bullshit. Not to mention that comment he made about picking cotton.” “You should have heard what he said that made me throw him out that window. Could you pass the soy sauce, please?” “Let me guess. He said “nigger”.” “Among other things. So how’s the album looking?” “Pretty good. We managed to finally get that last track down perfectly.” “Which one was it again?” “It was “Hotel California”.” “Ah.” “So why did Christina hire somebody like him, anyway?” “Hey, this is the company that gave the Nevermore paychecks, ok” She hired him because of his talent. At least that’s what she told me. I don’t see her hiring somebody because they don’t like black people.” “Unless you’re a recruiter for the police.” Brick and Aaron laughed at the joke. “You know why I think he’s coming after you.” “I think me throwing his sorry ass out a window has something to do with it.” “That might be part of it. I mean, obviously, this guy is a racist. That whole tirade he went on at Global Reload said as much. But I think he’s looking to make an example. He’s looking to prove that his color is what makes him the best?” “What makes him perfect.” “Exactly, and to do that, he has to target the most visible superstar of color, that being you.” “Yeah, that’s kind of what I was thinking. Of course, Deacon’s right up there with me. Even if he is a little piece of shit.” “Yeah, but he hasn’t accomplished what you have. First Black United States champion, first Black tag team champion, first Black International champion, first Black Platinum champion, first Black World champion. Of course, Makonie’s gonna want to make an example out of you. “He ain’t the first cat to want to make an example out of me. He is, however, the first to want to do it because I’m Black.” “It’s no different than when Hitler entered his Aryan “supermen” in the 1939 Olympics in Berlin.” “Yeah, and we know what happened there.” “That’s right, and just like Jesse Owens beat the Aryans, you have to beat Makonie the same way. Prove him wrong.” “Aaron, that’s just what I plan to do.”
