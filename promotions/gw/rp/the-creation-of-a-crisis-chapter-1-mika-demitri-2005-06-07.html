The Creation of a Crisis (Chapter 1: Mika & Demitri)

7 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/07/05 'The line dividing good and evil cuts through the heart of every human being, and who is willing to destroy his own heart?' - Alexander Solzhenitsyn WHACK! The croaking of a chain swinging back and forth in the darkness settles in like a metronome of violence. Setting the tempo for the next lash, only to start anew. His eyes fixated on the swinging mixture of nylon, sand, and steel. WHACK! A roar of thunder echoes around the room with the impact of a balled fist on nylon. Once again the metronome sets in... more rapid than the last. With the pace quickening his heart races to keep up, and his breath becomes audible. WHACK! Grinding his teeth as the nylon swings away from him, he tightens his fist waiting for it to return once again. Rattling chains announce that the bad had reached it's apex. A growling noise escaped his mouth as he roared once again, slamming his tightly balled fist into the black bag. WHA-POP! Sand began to trickle down onto the concrete floor. Staring at the hole he had created, he started to regain his regular breathing pattern. Beads of sweat dripped down his forehead and was already glistening off of his bare chest underneath the dim lighting. Reaching over to his left hand, he yanked at the velcro strap which fasted his black lifting glove to his wrist. As he pulled the glove off, the squeaking of a door hit his ears from behind him. His eyes glanced over to catch a silhouette standing the doorway. With a flick of the wrist, the lights quickly turned on... buring the man's eyes which had grow accustomed to the darkness after what seemed to be hours of hitting on the punching bag. Once the burning subsided, the man fought through the blur to catch a glipse of the figure in the doorway. A pair of cold, blue eyes looked back at him, slightly draped by jet black bangs which barely hung down over his forehead. 'So this is where you've been hiding?' Christian Zenith asked while he approached the mountain of a man standing in the room, next to a gaping shell of what used to be a black punching bag, 'Jason asked me where you ran off to.' 'I had some thinking to do.' Demitri responded while pulling his right lifting glove off of his hand. 'Well try to think a little less next time... that equipment isn't cheap. My punching bag expenses shouldn't be higher than my grocery bill, they're supposed to last more than a week.' Zenith reponded looking at what had become a familiar image of the past couple weeks, damaged training equipment next to Demitri Miasnikov. 'It won't happen again Christian.' Demitri responded with a slight snarl to his voice, 'This is always a tough time for me. My sister Mika was killed this week 6 years ago. You know that.' 'I couldn't give less of a damn about that bitch...' Christian reponded before he turned his back to walk out of the room, 'I guess in the same way I should thank her, I wouldn't exactly be who I am today if it wasn't for the important lesson she taught me about trusting people. But if she were still alive today Demitri, make no mistake about it... I'd kill her myself.' Christian didn't see the look on Demitri's face when he left the room... but he knew very well what it was. In the end Demitri didn't blame him. He'd told Christian a long time ago that if he ever hurt Mika that he'd send him back to his rich ass parents in a box. If only Demitri had known at the time, that it wasn't Christian that he needed to worry about. ---- Shot through the heart and you're to blame Darlin' you give love, A bad name FUCK YOU! ---- Date: April 22, 1999 Time: 10:12pm Central Time City: Chicago, Illinois High above the city, clouds floated rapidly by the half-moon... making a flickering effect down on the mirrored towers below. The winds howled through the city like a wind tunnel. Some even said if you stood in the right place, you could hear the lake singing. That singing sounded more like a roar on thing night, especially to the ears of a girl lost. Her dark brown hair blew in the wind as she wrapped a cream colored sweat jacket tightly around her, covering her black tube top. Taking a whiff into the sky, the smell of rain filled the air... it was only a matter of time until the April showers washed away any chance of getting home she had. Looking around, she wandered how she had gotten to this part of the city... thought for sure she could make it home from there. The thought of turning around and going back to where she started crossed her mind several times, but it was too late. Dead End. That's where she found herself staring over the shores of Lake Michigan. She had always found the lake to be soothing, but this time... it was nerve racking. With a strike of lightning over the beautiful body of water, water began to trickle down from the sky. Faster and faster the drops started to fall, she could hear the drops bouncing off the concrete. Surveying her surroundings, the only shelter she seemed to find was what appeared to be some kind of meeting hall. Listening to the music, she sprinted towards it trying to avoid the on coming downpour. Cracking the door open she looked inside at what appeared to be a party. 'Celebration' was playing around the fancy meeting hall, and everyone appeared to be in suits and dresses. Sneaking into the door she tried her best not be be spotted, but it was too late. 'I take it you're here with the Zenith Party?' A tall balding man with glasses asked her. She looked up at him confused before taking off her wet sweat jacket and hanging it on a nearby hook. Not knowing what to say... she just blurted out an answer. 'Yes.' She responded looking around feeling out of place in her black tube top and tight blue jeans. 'Can I have a name please?' The man asked her, 'This party is of course reservation only.' She paused for a minute in shock... 'Jessica Martin.' She said, trying to act as collected as possible. 'I'm sorry, there is no reservation for Jessica Martin.' The man said. 'That can't be right! There must be a mistake on the list!' She exclaimed, doing anything she could not to get sent back out on the cold, wet, and lonely streets. 'There are not mistakes on the list.' The man explained, 'I suggest you leave before security kicks you out ma'am.' At his command soon two security guards approached the young lady. As she was being pushed out the door a teenage boy around her age came along wearing a black dress shirt and black slacks. He looked at the situation with confusion... 'What's going on here Andres?' He asked with curiosity. 'We have a party crasher sir.' Andres responded, 'We didn't find her name on the list.' The teenage boy looked over at the girl with his cadet blue eyes, his black hair parted down the middle... his bangs falling on either side of his forehead. Stopping in his tracks at the sight of her beauty, he began to wonder what such an amazingly beautiful girl was doing at his party. He could see a look of depression across her face. 'Let her stay until the rain stops.' He said forcefully. She brushed herself off as security backed away. 'As you wish Mr. Zenith.' Andres responded as the girl walked over to him. She seemed a bit shy to talk to him, he was obviously important in some way. Zenith looked into her emerald green eyes as she walked towards him, blushing in his presence. 'So what's your name?' Christian asked the young lady. 'My name is Mika...' she said softly with a smile, 'Mika Miasnikov.' 'Welcome to my party Mika... there is a buffet table across the hall over there if you'd like to grab you something to eat. I'm just going to let you know... I don't know you from any homeless person out there on the street Mika. If you dare do anything to insult anyone here or disturb things, you'll be handled.' Zenith said in a very serious tone. 'You don't need to worry about me...' Mika said to Christian, 'I'm harmless really. Do I look like I could really do any harm? Plus I'm sure we'll have some time to get to know each other... I know I'd like to get to know you better.' 'I wish I could tell you more Mika, but this is just a party for a promotion I recieved recently. Maybe one of these days I'll let you know a little bit more.' Christian responded. 'I'd like that... but for now, well... can I at least you know your name?' 'My name is Christian... but my friends call me Chris.' Zenith reponded, 'I have to return to my party... are you going to join me?' 'I wouldn't miss it for the world.' ---- An angel's smile is what you sell You promise me heaven, Then put me through hell Chains of love, Got a hold on me When passion's a prison, You can't break free Whoa! The rest of the night seemed to go perfectly from there on. Christian was mesmerized by Mika's beauty, and Mika was so fascinated by Christian's mystique. He'd found out that Mika was the middle child and had an older brother named Demitri and a younger sister named Eva. Her family had moved to Chicago from the Soviet Union after the end of the Cold War. It was amazing how much they had in common, Christian had never met anyone like her before in his life. So beautiful, intelligent, and fascinating. His years growing up under a constant eye had always prevented him from going out and meeting many new people whom his father didn't know. But of course, his father was too busy to attend the party and his mother had gone to bed early. Something always seemed to come up when it came to him. One of these days he'd earn his parents respect. At the closure of the party a limo awaited outside for Christian, with a driver sent by his father. 'Do you need a ride home?' Christian asked Mika standing outside the limo. She backed away a bit and shook her head no. 'Surely... you're not going to walk home are you? These streets are dangerous you know. There are gangs and all kinds of people out walking these streets.' Christian insisted, but Mika didn't know where to go. 'What about your parents and your brother and sister? I'll take you home.' 'I can't go back home...' Mika responded, 'They don't want me back.' At that moment Christian thought of his sister Tera who'd ran away a couple years back and how she must feel the same way. His mother still went out looking for her all the time, he knew that couldn't be true. 'Nonsense... just take the ride.' Zenith said, 'They're your family after all, they're supposed to love you no matter what. Right?' Mika didn't say a word about her situation before she slowly walked towards the limo and sat down inside. On the way she had told him where her family lived, but never why she left and felt she couldn't return. They had talked about stories of their childhood, how they were raised and some of their most tragic moments. When the limo finally arrived at Mika's families house, the lights turned on inside. Her mother came out along with her old brother as she got out after Christian. Zenith could tell by the looks in their eyes that they had been very worried to death about her. As her brother walked down the staircase, Christian was taken by surprise when Mika wrapped her arms around his neck and kissed him. He'd never felt anything like it before in his life... it was amazing, beautiful, and powerful. A smile crept across her face as she waved goodbye and walked away. Demitri walked up to a still semi-stunned Christian Zenith... 'You must be Demitri.' Christian said, looking up at the taller Demitri. He was build like a mountain, so strong that he would be intimidating to most people... but not to Christian. Zenith was fearless as the man looked into his eyes. 'If you ever dare think about doing anything that will make my little sister cry... you better never see me again, or I'll send your rich ass back to your house in a box.' Demitri said staring down at Zenith. 'You don't need to worry about that... she'll be fine with me around, plus... I can handle myself.' Christian said with an intense looks in his eyes before he turned around and headed back into the limo. Once he got inside and closed the door, he reached down into his pocket only to discover a piece of paper with Mika's address and phone number on it. ---- You paint that smile on your lips Blood red nails on your fingertips A school boy's dream, You act so shy Your very first kiss was your first kiss goodbye Whoa! It wasn't long before their families took notice. While Mika's family was estatic... The Zenith Estate was very reluctant about the sudden appearance of Mika in Christian's life. Ulrich especially had become very weery of the girl and kept close tabs on her while she was in the manor. Under strict supervision, the hostility towards Mika finally started to east up... and talks between Mika and Christian began to become serious. As Vice President of Zenith Corporation, Christian was able to support Mika on his own at the age of 17. There had been talks of engagement and even starting a family. Meanwhile, the Miasnikov family had grown very close to young Christian. Demitri especially, who had learned a lot from Christian about various styles of self-defense and martial arts. The two had even begun taking classes together in martial arts and were on a strict fitness program. Everything seemed to be going fine, until one day: Date: November 14, 1999 The glass doors of the building are pushed open, and a shadow is cast across the marble floor. Click Click Click The sound of his footsteps echo around the incredibly acoustics of the room as he makes his way up to the counter. Behind the counter 3 tellers stand, with big smiles as the man in a black leather jacket and a pair of sunglasses approaches the one in the middle. A younger woman, around 24 or 25 years old with short blonde hair and hazel colored eyes. The man's jet black hair slightly covers his sunglasses, in which the teller can now see her reflection. The man reaches into his back pocket and slides an ID across the the woman who reads it with a smile and hands it back. 'How may I help you today Mr. Zenith?' The woman smiled back at Christian, who began to jot down the amount he wanted on a sheet of paper so the others in the bank wouldn't hear. He slides the paper across the counter and the woman begins to type in his account number for his withdrawl. 'I'm sorry Mr. Zenith, are you sure this is the right account number?' The miss behind the counter asks. 'I'm positive.' Christian says as he investigates the room. 'This account is empty sir.' 'Empty? What do you mean empty?' 'Your balance is zero.' The lady replied. Christian took off his sunglasses and stuck them in his pocket, making sure that his eyes didn't decieve him. 'That's impossible! A million dollars doesn't just disappear!' Christian exclaims. 'Of course it doesn't sir...' The lady said as she began to type. Christian's fingers began to drum on the counter as he waited, 'All the money was withdrawn earlier this morning by a Mika Miasnikov, who has access to this particular account.' 'You have to be fucking kidding me...' Christian said as he crushed the pair of sunglasses in his hand. 'No... I'm not... I'm sorry.' Zenith snarled and threw his crushed pair of sunglasses across the bank before he sprinted out the door and jumped back into his car. Christian sped down through the streets in his brand new 2000 Trans Am. Weaving in and out of traffic, Zenith rushed in a panic through the streets. Suddenly Zenith skids to a stop in front of the Miasnikov house and sprints up to the door and kicks it open. Inside sits Demitri and Eva. 'Where in the fuck is Mika?' Christian screams looking over at Demitri. 'She left here this morning and never came back.' Demitri responded looking up at Christian before rising to his near 7 foot tall frame. 'She stole every cent I had in one of my bank accounts Demitri.' Zenith exclaimed, 'Do you have any idea how much money that is?!' 'I can imagine.' 'A MILLION DOLLARS DEMITRI! FUCKING GONE!' Christian screamed in Demitri's face, 'You will fucking find her and get my money back. And tell her this thing between me and her is OVER. No one touches my hard work and my money Demitri... NOBODY.' 'I'm sorry Chris.' 'You have no reason to be sorry Demitri...' Christian said before he pulled the door open with such force, it slammed against the inside wall, shaking the small appartment, 'But you bet your ass she has a reason to be sorry.' Christian spent the rest of the day trying to track down Mika Miasnikov, as did her family with no luck. A runaway... just like before. Now, she was a million-dollar runaway... and Christian Zenith had his first taste of betrayal. The next few months wasn't any easier... Christian had to earn all the money from that account back and never fully gained back the respect that his father once had for him. The hunt for Mika Miasnikov never really ended until the headlines of the morning newspaper on June 12, 2000... 6 months later. ---- Shot through the heart and you're to blame You give love a bad name I play my part and you play your games You give love a bad name Shot through the heart and you're to blame You give love a bad name I play my part and you play your games You give love a bad name You give love a bad name ---- DEAD WOMAN FOUND June 12, 2000 By Jeffery Montgomery At around 10:50pm last night Darren Brooks came home to find the body of his fiance, Mika Miasnikov, dead on the floor of their Northern Chicago apartment.
