Can't think of one

19 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 19 **BOOM** The house blows up with pieces of shattered glass and wall fly everywhere! Father Clark watches on as the house burns to the ground. He waits for 1 minute to see if Roswell and his mother are alright. Quickly father Clark runs to the carnage and starts to move broken pieces of the wall away. Father finds a wood table in bad shape and is hoping for Roswell and his mother to be underneath. Finally from the big oak tree Father Clark spots two figures walking over to him. Father Clark: Hello! Is it you Roswell? From dark black smoke the figures start to mover closer and faster. Finally they reach Father Clark and a sigh is heard. Father Clark walks over to the people and gives them both a big hug for relief. Roswell: Father, yes we are alright but I think I’m hurt. A shard of glass hit me in the side of my chest. I was lucky to survive. Roswell’s Mother: Where am I dear? Roswell: I think she lost her memory. Roswell, Father Clark, and his Mom start to walk down the damp street, From the sky it starts pouring rain. Roswell quickly takes off his jacket and puts it over his Mom. Roswell then picks up his mother and starts to run. Down the block Roswell and Father come up to a big blue house, they walk up the slippery stairs and open the door. Inside Roswell walks over to a blue leather couch and lays his Mom down on it. Roswell: Let her sleep, she’ll need it for Monday. Father Clark: Why for Monday night? Roswell: Oh, I didn’t tell you guys but I got you and Mom tickets to see me wrestle against, Ace Cash, Xavier Hunter, and Uncle Sam. Father Clark: OH NO! Sorry Max but I can’t come on Monday night, I have to do confessions at the church that night. Roswell: Awwwww cra.......crud! I really wanted you to come. Father Clark: Well I could maybe skip these I do have another priest for backup. Roswell: That would mean a lot to me. Roswell and Father stand up off the chair and stretch their arms, and legs. Roswell whispers in Father’s ear and they both walk into a bedroom and close the door. **THE NEXT MORNING** RING RING RING goes the clock on the dresser. Roswell quickly gets up from a master bed and puts pants and a shirt on. He opens the door quickly and runs down the stairs, he sees his mom still laying on the couch. Roswell opens a cupboard and grabs a Froot Loops box and puts it on the counter top. He takes a bowl out of the white dishwasher and a spoon. He pours some Froop Loops into the bowl and walks over to the large wooden table. He starts gulping down all of it and within 5 minutes he finishes. Father walks into the room and asks why Roswell is rushing to finish breakfast. Father: What’s the matter? Roswell: I have to go to train with Larry and other guys. Roswell grabs a jacket and puts on his black sandals and walks out the brown door. Outside Roswell is seen opening the door of a car and taking a black sport bag. Roswell slams the door and starts running down the now dried up street. A couple minutes later Roswell walks into the large blue building. TO BE CONTINUED
