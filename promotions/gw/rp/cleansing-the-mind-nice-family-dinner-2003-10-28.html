Cleansing the mind/Nice family dinner

28 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/28/03 A shower is sometimes believed to cleanse the heart and soul as well as the body. All the pain and troubles that may be bothering the mind. Only it doesn’t work every time. Johnie Ringo wished it were true in his case, but it only brings more thoughts. Both of his hands are pressed against the shower wall as he leans his weight on it, letting the water splash across his face and trickle down his body. He droops his head downward to let the water go all up in his hair and over his shoulders. As he does this, his eyes hang open. He breathes deeply as it echoes in the porcelain shell. He leans his hand down on the knob marks “H” and rotates it to the right. Instantly he winces at the pain, but he soothes quickly back into it and adjusts to the warmth. Steam starts to rise up from the floor and surround the entire shower. The haze envelops Johnie whole and after a little while, outlines of bodies are seen walking past him. Laughing begins to resonate and distort all around him. Soon, he isn’t even in the same shower. Johnie turns his head left opening his eyes and sees guys drying themselves off not very far away from him. Behind them, guys are snapping towels at each other and laughing their heads off. He laughs and grins along with them. Turning his head right, other guys are showing as well. Telling stories and jokes to one another as they soap up their heads under the metal spray showers. “So you’re telling me that Lena...Lena Eastman was all over you and shit?” One says to the guy on his right. “Yeah, dude,” he scoffs. “She was like “Oh, Trey! Why should you be with Nicole when you can have me?” Everybody hoots and whistles at this strong answer. His buddy only nudges Trey’s shoulder and points at him. “Come on man, you gotta be shittin” me. Which, incidently looks a lot like someone washing a battleship with a baby wipe. “I know you’re holding out on me, man.” Johnie hangs his head down as a slight grin grows on his face. Shaking the water off his face, he looks back at the behemoth with genuine distain in his eyes. “Come on. Do you really think that I’m just gonna shoot my mouth off and tell you?” “Yeah!” Johnie shakes his head around as he turns the rusted knob on the shower to off. Nearby his towel hangs on a hook out of the water’s reach. He grabs it, rubs his face in it roughly before tossing it over his right shoulder. “Well I guess you got me there.” With that, Johnie pulls the towel off his shoulder and starts snapping it at Wes? flapping thighs. The gentle giant turns the corner and gallops away towards the rest of the crowded lockers. As Johnie comes out of the shower hole, he runs into a wide red jacket. As he bounces off he sees the wall of a man that stopped him. “Still can’t help messing around, can you Ringo?” says the horseshoe-haired goon. Johnie looks up earnestly at the man standing before him. A toothy smile comes across his face as he leers back at the straight-faced visage before him. “Well I gotta get my exercise somewhere, coach.” “I thought you would’ve burnt it out on the field today. But I guess these guys are just too easy for ya.” The coach grumbles down to his subordinate kid.
