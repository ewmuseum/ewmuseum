The Call Of The Ring Never Fades

16 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/16/2005 Jake was smiling. For a strange reason he had never felt closer to his little brother. The man had done plenty of dirty deeds in his life, and had even been investigated for the supposed rape of a competitor, made the same woman his slave and ended careers on purpose more than once. All of that had been in pursuing his dream, but he remembered how many times he had questioned his own commitment. The Fallen Angel never had those questions. He was pursuing a very specific, moral purpose with no doubts. Sure, some would call him evil or a vigilante, but he had always been the man with a singular purpose in those days. Jake remembered how he seemed different right before he took the hiatus from the business. He never imagined it was because he was doing something like this with his life. The legacy of the Hix Family was not all positive. They ran through a rough industry, hung out with rough people and had ended up in all manner of dangerous situations. Of course, they were able to handle themselves just fine’most of the time. Jake rubbed his eyes, then began re-reading the information. Something would reveal itself in these texts’he was sure. The story continued, and he knew that his brother was never going to be able to let go of the career. None of them could? . . . . . . . . . Xavier woke up in a daze. Sleep had not come easily, and Xavier was still not sure of himself or his ideas and plans. He wanted to make sure that he left his mark on the world, even if the world would never know about it. Xavier stumbled into the bathroom and turned on the water in the shower. It would take a few moments for the water to heat up, so Xavier turned on the faucet and cupped water in his hand. Xavier brought the water to his mouth and used it to rinse. Xavier put a generous amount of toothpaste on his toothbrush and turned it on. The electric toothbrush made quick work of the bad taste in his mouth. Xavier finished brushing and spit out into the sink. He rinsed off his toothbrush and turned around. Steam was just beginning to come out from atop the enclosed shower. Xavier stepped into the shower and enjoyed the feeling of the hot water washing over him. The heat soothed his muscles after the intense workout he had gone through the night before. Xavier still didn’t know what he thought about continuing his wrestling career. Too many demons seemed to e chasing him down, and he wanted to get away from it all for a little while to sort things out. The death of his friend, his missing brother and everything else made his mind a mass of confusion. Xavier had gone down to a local gym under the direction of General Skandar Akbar. Akbar had never made it to the big time, but he was a very successful promoter for a long time in the Texas area. Now he ran a wrestling school and staged small shows all across Texas. Akbar may have been best known for Devastation Incorporated and also for training several current stars in the business. Xavier was able to work out in the ring, go through some moves with the trainees, and use the gym. The workout had been the first in far too long inside the ring. Xavier lifted weights, ran, kept up on his martial arts and even did some aerobic exercise. All that meant very little in the long run because once you were inside the ring an entirely new element took over. Even people in supposedly great shape could blow-up very quickly inside the ropes. The ring put stress on you that was too difficult to define. There was the workout of running the ropes, lifting weights in the throws, working on balance and precision while moving and of course the basic wear and tear the moves caused. Xavier had enjoyed getting back into things, but he wasn’t at his full capability. He realized he was barely half way up to being where he needed to be. Xavier finished washing up and stepped out of the shower. He made his way back tot he sink and turned on the hot water for the faucet. Xavier put some shaving cream into his small bowl and used the boar’s bristles brush to apply the cream to his face and head. The brush made sure the cream was applied evenly while helping to massage the skin. Xavier used his Mach 3 razor to quickly remove the stubble from his face and head except for the thick goatee around his mouth and chin. He had let the hair grow nice and long to frame his face. Xavier rinsed off the shaving cream and few stray hairs that always clung about with warm water before applying his aftershave lotion. The cool lotion eased the burn as Xavier wrapped a towel around his waist. Xavier walked out of his bathroom and made his way out his front door to retrieve his daily paper. Xavier had never been a big fan of the paper. Normally he preferred to get his news from CNN or one of the local stations, but his new activities demanded even more attention to the happenings going on around him. The cool air met Xavier’s still moist skin and sent a chill through him. Xavier leaned down and picked up his copy of the Dallas Morning News. The door across from him opened and Mrs. Angela Johnson stepped out onto the landing. Angela looked over at Xavier with a smirk, “Good morning Xavier. Just out for the morning paper?” “Yes ma’am. Wanted to have a nice read before I got going for my day.” Xavier explained. Angela gave Xavier a noticeable up and down. She had never hidden her desire for Xavier, but there was something about her that Xavier simply hadn’t liked from the beginning. She seemed like someone bringing a lot of baggage with them. “Try not to lose the towel Xavier, you never know who could be watching.” Angela said before licking her lips. “I’ll keep that in mind.” Xavier went right back into his home as he tried to escape the situation. Xavier walked into the kitchen and got a Hansen’s Energy Drink from the refrigerator. He took the drink and the paper back to the kitchen table. Xavier sat down and began reading through the articles as he popped open his drink. Much of the same stuff he had been seeing for a while with a few interesting items thrown in here and there. Xavier flipped to the fourth page and a story caught his eye. Xavier read through the story quickly. Police responded to a call early yesterday morning in the 1400 Block of Oak Cliff Avenue. An altercation at a private home has led to the arrest of four suspected drug dealers. According to eyewitness accounts the situation began when a local resident, Gina Hernandez, began an argument with her fianc? Eric Turner as he tried to pack up his belongings. Mr. Turner was seen preparing to leave the premises. Neighbors called police, but before they could arrive two cars pulled up to the house. Four men entered the residence and a larger fight ensued. The police arrived shortly thereafter, and heard gunshots from inside the house. After a brief but tense standoff police arrested the four men and discovered three bodies inside the house. The third body’s identity has not been released yet pending notification of the family. Lieutenant Dan Wolf with the Dallas Police department attributed the violence to drugs. “Mr. Turner was a known drug dealer, drugs were found on the premises, and the entire episode seems to be related to the ongoing drug wars between competing factions.” Dallas Police had no further comment. Xavier read the article once again in disbelief. He had convinced him to leave town and find a new life, but his own life came back to bite him. Xavier was comforted by the facts of the case. Despite the fact that his life had ended, Xavier thought he had found the right path in the end. Xavier continued sipping on his drink as he finished the rest of the paper. Xavier knew he had a lot of work to do if he was going to reach his now firm goal. There was good work to be done, there were problems to be corrected and Xavier was ready to get revenge for all his friends and family who had been wronged by the drug world. Xavier had the next step in the chain. The next step would touch the heroin connection because the chain was easier to find and follow. Javier Garcia was the man who supplied the heroin at the next level, and he was receiving his supplies from someone named Jorge Mexia in Laredo, Texas. Xavier was going to have to wait on some firm intelligence from the sources “Bulldog” Hargis was working on. There would most likely be a trip south of the border required as the plan was developed. Xavier knew that operating beyond the border would mean certain death, The cartels in Mexico had seemingly complete freedom to operate however they needed to in its goal to achieve their ends. The politics involved were meaningless when it came down to the decision of men to violently take action that injured and endangered the civilians around them. Xavier knew if the cartel had any idea what he was doing there would be a small team of young men dispatched to ensure his plan never came to fruition. The organizational chart for this cartel was difficult to penetrate and document. If enough intelligence could be gathered to document the entire system then Xavier could pick and choose one or two areas to strike the supply chain. Xavier knew a small gain for the people could mean more than anything else he would do. Xavier had come to the realization that each operation would save at least one person, but in reality it would save dozens of people who would not be addicted by the first taste. Xavier finished his morning drink and paper. Xavier went to his bedroom and got dressed for the day in blue jeans and a button up flannel shirt. Xavier slipped on a pair of black cowboy boots he had bought a few weeks earlier. He was still working on breaking in the leather for maximum comfort. Xavier stepped out into the cool air and made his way to his Excursion. Xavier finally had something pulling at the corner of his lips. He once would have called it a smile, but this was something different. Xavier had a cruel smirk on his face knowing the darkness that would be coming. Javier Garcia was operating out of Austin, and Xavier hadn’t gotten to see UT in a long time. Xavier realized that passing the time as he waited for the next operation was one of the most difficult things in the entire project. Xavier had spoken to “Bulldog” Hargis earlier, and they had decided Austin would be the next staging area, but it would take some time to get everything ready. Xavier was spending his days doing research through police reports, intelligence reports, and looking through online reports of crime throughout Texas. Xavier had pieced together more information about the drug trade using these sources and mapping everything out than he thought possible. Consistent reports identified the high drug traffic areas in the major cities, the indictment reports showed key players, and then the police and intelligence reports segmented the different groups in the trade. Xavier was in his study looking over the information with a revered silence. He now had the information required to take action. Xavier wondered as he looked at the information about the ability of the DEA, FBI and local police agencies. If he had put this together through paying informants, using police reports, newspaper articles and some intelligence reports from other agencies then certainly the law enforcement agencies could do it. The very fact that they should be able to do it and so far had not been able to was a testament to the ineffectiveness of the system. Xavier knew he would have to get things taken care of along with the few dedicated individuals Hargis had identified to assist them. At most, they would be a strike force of four people including Hargis and himself. Xavier looked over the charts and realized this was a test of pure and simple will. Xavier had made the decision to act, and the consequences be damned. Xavier had a match that night on a wrestling program being run in Midlothian, Texas. Xavier needed to keep his skills sharp, and he had agreed to take a match as The Annihilator under a mask to conceal his identity. Xavier walked away from the room and his mind was at an easy peace. He had made his decision and mentally he was prepared. Xavier ignored the burning in his throat. He ignored the sour feeling in his stomach dismissing it as heartburn, but there were other forces at work inside him. Xavier made it to the arena early. He wanted some time in the ring to test out the ropes and mat. He had seen too many people injured by not knowing the ring they would be working in, and he refused to be one of those statistics. Xavier found his way into the building past the only security guard and found the ring already set-up. Xavier dropped his bag on the mat outside the ring and slipped under the bottom rope. The ropes were tight and pulled a little too tight for Xavier’s preference, but he could deal with that. Xavier ran the ropes getting used to the feel then did a few back bumps to judge the mat. It was hard and firm, and he realized the big bumps would hurt inside this ring. Xavier leaned back in the corner. He had a lot of history to live up to inside the ring, and even more than that he had bigger expectations of himself than anyone else had of him. Xavier was imagining being in Madison Square Garden in front of thousands of fans rather than the few hundred who would be in the small arena tonight. Xavier was ready, and he didn’t care if it was a win or loss so long as he got some of the ring rust off by working an actual match. He had already made a deal to do at least one match a month to stay current, and maybe a few more matches for fun during the week along with training would make him smooth again very soon.
