Saga of the Dark Blade: Part 4...Jenean

19 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-19-2004 Few trees dot the harsh landscape as the shot pans down into a shot of a land green and lush with healthy grasses. Mountains rear their jagged peaks in the distance like a hideous mouth ready to chomp down upon whoever has the misfortune of coming near. A lone horseman trots a fiery eyed white warhorse through the tall grass, leaving a path in his wake. The beaten down path stretches back as far as the eye can see, and as he comes closer, we can get a good look at the man. Regal appearing in a shirt of golden mail armor, a black sword rests on his hip. Occasionally the man looks around warily, his hand dropping to the hilt of his blade in a practiced fluid motion. Venneman Pendragon. The Lord King of a forgotten land in a distant world. Even riding as he is, he has a dangerous look about him. His eyes are cold blue, and burn with a fervor that one might mistake for a touch of insanity’One might be right with that assumption. Seriousness of purpose defines the man, and it always has. Single minded, when he sets his will towards a goal, normally he attains it; whatever the cost? “Times change, no matter what. People stay the same. “Tis the way of the world. The harsh reality of that, is this: sometimes people are left behind. As times change, people adapt, but their simple natures stay the same. The only thing one can do is put thy head down, keep thy seriousness and thy wits about thee, and tarry forth’take thy Lord King for example?” ***********FLASH************** A bar’A very drunk Pendragon. Two things one should never see together. Pendragon capers here and there in the memory, before finally slumping into a corner booth. A blinding flash of white light hit’s the screen, and a man in white sits with the Lord King. “I remember the first time Bob revealed himself to me’Bob’The Creator. I had been adrift in a sea of dissent’My seriousness, once honed to a knife edge, had left me’I had lost the great blade Excalibur. What was I without that ancient talisman” If only I knew’would have saved lots of trouble. The Creator, God if you will, revealed himself to me in that bar’Only I saw him. He does not go for publicity. Bob told me’Bob is what he likes to be called’Bob told me that it was time I fulfilled my purpose. I was to be his weapon against evil in the worlds’This world and those that border it closely. The seriousness of the calling he had in mind for me was far greater than the petty glory I had sought for mineself in the past?.” *********FLASH*********** “And that brings me here’The beautiful plains. Glory and honor used to be my credo. Beneath those words I conquered those who were weaker than I, and the multitudes in that number were great. No regard I held for the honorless, yet there was one piece missing from mine equation”Seriousness. I thought that I was serious about mine fate. I thought I controlled mine destiny. How foolish I was. The more I relied only upon mineself, the more of a joke I became. Mine blade, Excalibur, stolen and sold on the black market. Mine reaction? To lament the loss as if I had lost mine strength. I hid mineself in the bottle. The vessel I had been made to be was weakening. I could not climb from the abyss on mine own. I had lost the seriousness of purpose that had driven me to great heights.” Pendragon reigns in Xerxes, the warhorse, and peers into the distance, blue eyes blazing? “It has returned”.” He states this as a fact to himself before looking around. With a chuckle he continues on. In the distance, smoke rises, and he makes a b-line to the source of it. A look of urgency is writ upon his face as he and Xerxes pick up the pace to a faster trot. “God’s Hammer. I am to be the weapon of Bob’What purpose could be more serious than that” What cause greater? “In my name shalt thee conquer??He said that to me. Glory and honor would be heaped upon mine name’A just price to pay for mine services he called it. He set me an early task to gain focus. The dark blade, Lucien. Hidden by the ancients until the chosen day, the sword would be wielded by either an agent of the light, or of the dark. That man is me, and for an instant I teetered on the edge’Goodness and honor to one side, darkness and blood to the other. I chose the light. This pleased the Lord Bob, and he set me another task’To gain honor by defeating a former PTC Global Champion and, well, a has been. A serious endeavor for a man bred of seriousness. This would be mine most difficult defense as yet, and though trivial to mine Lord Bob, the Television title is quite precious to me. Do not misunderstand; battle has always been second nature to mine hands, yet the challenge before me now was the greatest I had ever faced in the Octagon. Mine thought, if mine thoughts are mine any longer, was that the Hammer would be unleashed upon a stage that the world would watch. GW. Finally, I would earn the respect of the masses. Along the way, darkness might rear it’s ugly head before the Lord King’It would be lopped off.” Pendragon peers ahead, blue eyes showing a touch of worry. This was serious business. To fail Bob this early? How could he face the Lord God if he did not at least SHOW for the tournament that had been ordained for his unveiling? “If only mine way to the Arena would remain traversable’That smoke up ahead vexes me?” He nudges Xerxes up to a gallop and the warhorse responds with an attitude of “About damned time” as he races through the grass, pulling ever closer to the smoke. Pendragon has never been a complex man. See the enemy, engage, defeat, enjoy the spoils. Sometimes he fought for a cause, other times he merely fought for his own glory, his own honor. Seriousness oozed from his very pores as he lived his life in two worlds? “I was not meant for this century. I get not the humor, and people mistake mine serious attitude for aloofness. Solitude has lived hand in hand with the Lord King for as long as I remember. I get not the jokes that people make, oft times at mine own expense. It seems that the only modern speech that reaches me is insult. I do know when I am insulted’People believe me to be all chivalry and pomp??Tis not the case, and I pity the half man who would believe these things to be true.” Pendragon chuckles out loud as his blonde hair blows back from his brow in the wind created by the gallop. Xerxes crests a rise on the plain, and far ahead, a vast port stretches along water as blue as the sky. The mountains have drawn ever nearer and the horse carries Pendragon through a wide pass. The smoke comes from a large vessel in the water. The damage remains to be seen, but the ship is indeed engulfed in flames? “THAT is serious. That ship is mine passage to see that the former champion Jamester and the upstart Sherwin are taught to respect those who are their superiors, both in humanity AND in combat. I seek not their respect’I seek his salvation. These men are wrapped in shrouds of darkness though they know it not. Hellish darkness await those who do not find the way, and I wish that not upon mine worst enemy’Even those enemies who fight merely to say they fought’Even those enemies who cannot see that they have lost before they step before me. I pity the foolish, and mine serious nature will see the King to victory. Pretty words and jests hurled at the King shall deter me not’Yet fools named Jamester shall be hurled BY me’And lest we move too quickly, let us not forget Captain Retirement’Retirement might be the right choice, Rich’Aye, Pendragon remembers grade school insults as well?” He chuckles at the last, amused by his simple wit, but the look of seriousness quickly returns’Pendragon never fit really well in grade school either’He has always been one to leap from mood to mood. Psycho? “The time for honoring thyselves”.It ends’I jest not. It is not in mine nature. I speak truth. I speak the word as was handed down to me by Bob and mine forebears...” (Yep. Psycho.)?Xerxes seems to fly as he races to the port, buildings and faces streaking by in a blur? “Now if only I can find a new ship’Mine appears to be?””..Broken.” Overhead, a huge jetliner flies by, and Pendragon looks up with a seeming look of wonder writ upon his face? “A signal! Bob speaks!” Seriousness“Psychosis. Is there really a difference when one boils away the edges” It’s just a damned jet! ~FINE~
