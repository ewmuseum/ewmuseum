Art of War

10 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3.10.04 Introduction to Arik Idol Anybody that even casually knew Arik Idol knew not to disturb him while he was in his basement. Why? Because he would rip your head off. Whenever Arik went down into that basement, the rest of the world had no meaning to him. He was busy, and the rest of the world could go piss up a rope as far as he was concerned. He would be studying as many wrestling tapes as he could possibly get a hold of. Arik almost owns literally every showcase of Japanese Strong Style wrestling ever taped. Some people like to call themselves perfectionists, but they can't even hold a candle to Arik. His work ethic resembles that of a machine more than a human. Every move he does in the ring is in as perfect of form as possible. They're crisp, quick, and effective. He has to be the best, because he will have it no other way. Most people would be pretty happy with second place... Arik isn't. In fact, second place is the absolute worst of them all, because that means you had made as far as you could before being the best, and you blew it. That was just unacceptable. Arik had always kept the the volume off also. He didn't care about the announcers and the fans... he studied the wrestling, the form, the technique, the precision. There was nothing he didn't notice. He was and is a true student of the game, and one day, he'll be a God of the sport. Some would call Arik's work ethic a bit obsessive, if not totally crazy. In the ring, Arik is absolutely vicious. There's just no dodging around it. Every move he does has a certain snap to it that just makes it that much more aggressive, and that much more painful. Something was fueling him, but what? Is it possible for one human being to be so focused on one goal that he doesn't acknowledge the people or enviroments around him? Is it possible that he would be willing to maim and destroy anything that was foolish enough to get in his path? Arik's goal is to be the absolute best wrestler to ever step foot in a Global Wrestling ring. He has put in the work, he has put in the time, and he has paid his dues. It's his time now. Arik Idol's time has come to reign supreme over GW... and there's not a man alive that can stop him. ----------------- Chapter 1 Art of War Like the great leaders of the past, I too will soon dominate the GW. I am a finely tuned athletic masterpiece with one goal, and one goal only. I don't set the 'small step goal' bullshit. That's for the weak. My goal is to be the absolute best in the GW. To dominate anybody foolish enough to attempt to stop me. I've trained far too hard and far too long for my goal to be denied to me now. Like a one man army, I will tear through the GW roster one by one, and take out every single person that dares to step up. My cell phone rings, but I ignore it. I can't be bothered with things like that right now. I'm preparing. Preparing for what, you may ask. I'm preparing for the war that I am about to begin both with myself and with the GW roster. I pack my gear into my travel bag, along with my bare necessities. This will be my first match with GW that I consider 'real'. I was involved in October of last year, but the place was a mess. I had left shortly after I arrived. But now, with things in order, I've returned, and I'm more than ready to compete. Somewhat like a soldier going to war, I sling my bag over my shoulder and exit the room. My butler, Jameson, meets me at the bottom of the stairs. His suit is in perfect condition as usual. 'Sir Idol, the car is waiting,' he speaks with a distinct British accent. I still hadn't grown accustomed to having Jameson wait on my every need. He had been another 'gift' of sorts from my Father before he disappeared. He gave me his talent agency, the ridiculously luxurious house that I live in, and Jameson to aid me. I try my best to be polite to him, but it takes all my being not to fire him. I don't fire him, because I know that's not what my Mother would want. She had known Jameson for years. 'Thank you, Jameson,' I said as I patted his shoulder. 'I trust you'll watch over the house while I'm gone?' 'Of coure, Sir Idol' 'Excellent to here, Jameson.' I said as I exited the house without saying good bye. As I said, the house is ridiculously luxurious considering that I live alone... with the exception of Jameson, of course. I suppose most people would see me as a 'crazy'. I've had girlfriends, of course, but none of them stick around. Every single one of them said I spent too much time studying my wrestling tapes, and not enough time with them. I guess some would say that I'm obsessive. I don't see that to be the case necessarily. I see my diligent studying and my constanst striving for perfection as admirable traits. I want to be the absolute best at what I do, and I know I can be. It is necessary for me to study the warriors of the past, so I can better understand their mistakes and their flaws. With enough work, and enough dedication, it is my belief that I can be flawless- physically and mentally. With enough work and dedication, I can become an unstoppable machine. The wrestling world will bow at my feet in awe of my prowess. They will bow at my feet in awe of my presence, in my aura, in my very being. With enough work and dedication, I can be a God. The Emperor of the wrestling world, if you will. Most will tell you that my goal is ludacris, and impossible... but they're wrong. Soon, very soon, I will prove them wrong. Because soon... I will become a God.
