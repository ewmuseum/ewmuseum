Date: 7/18/03 A gloomy grey sky morning fills the picture as the scene opens. Ice is shown sitting on a chipped green bench, with his elbows resting on his thighs, and his head in his hands. The darkened color of his blue shorts, and the heaving of his uncovered upper torso is seen vividly, as Ice catches his breath, while the sweat rolls off of his body. Ice slides his hands down his face, and slowly sits back, into an upright position. Ice breathes in deeply, then slowly exhales, and turns in the direction of the camera. Walking into the picture at that very moment, wearing a long elogant, off white skirt, and matching top, is Star. Her long blond hair sways side to side, along with her gorgeous hips, as she makes he way over to Ice. A small smirk grows across Ice's exhausted face as his beautiful blond counterpart takes a seat next to him on the green park bench. The camera inches in closer and closer as Star begins to speak to Ice. 'Only three more miles back to the gym. Then you are done for the day.' Ice nods his head up and down in response to Star's comments, and stands up. Ice takes off down the side walk in a dead sprint, as Star watches on. Ice turns the corner, and Star turns around as Ice fades around the buildings. Star walks down the sidewalk towards the camera, and past it, to a black Ford Expedition. She opens the door, hops into the large vehicle, and drives off. The scene fades to black. ________________________________________________________________________ The scene fades back in with Star stepping out of the black Ford Expedition she was seen leaving in earlier. She turns and walks around to the back of the truck, to see Ice jogging his way into the parking lot. Ice jogs to the truck, past the truck, and into the building behind the truck. Star follows him into the building. She pushes the metal bar extending from the two brown metal doors, and enters. Ice is standing, with his arms behind his head, catching his breath, just on the inside of the door, and is almost hit when Star opens it. 'Very good honey, now lets see what that scale says.' Star walks down the small narrow hallway, and takes a left, entering another room. Ice drops his hands from behind his head, and sighs as he follows the path. Ice enters the room, slips off his black Converse sneakers, and steps out of his navy blue gym shorts. Ice in his black Hanes, boxer breifs, steps onto the scale. He looks down to see what it has to say. '240' Ice steps off of the scale, and Star rushes over to him with a gigantic smile on her face. She leaps in Ice's direction and wraps her legs around his waist, and arms around his neck. Repeatedly she kisses him all over his face as Ice turns his head from side to side, attempting to miss the onslaught. Star hops down off of Ice and gives him one last kiss. Ice shakes his head, and smiles at the girl's actions. 'Now can I shower?' Star smiles and nods. Ice turns and begins to walk out of the room, but stops as Star slaps him on the left ass cheek. Ice turns around to see a devilish smile on Star's face. Ice winks and the two leave the room. The scene fades to black.
