The Big Dawg's Contract

14 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
14 August 2006 [Taken from Global News] As many people who listen to online rumors may know, Jake Hix’ contract is set to expire soon. There is already a rift in Global management on how to handle the situation of the current Global Heavyweight Champion of the World. Currently, Jake is the figurehead of Global, but many feel he and The Syndicate are more trouble than they are worth. There is a growing call to let the superstar leave and focus on other talent. While this is a minority opinion, there are several key backers of this plan who feel perhaps the Big Dawg is uncontrollable. Of course, many more members of Global Management and the Board of Directors feel letting the Big Dawg leave would be the worst result for the company. They have been pushing for a long-term deal, yet no contracts have been signed as of yet. Jake Hix has a distinguished career with Global. In addition to his accolades within our company, he also is a PTC Infinite Gauntlet Champion, 4WW Holiday Show Rumble Champion, placed 4th in the Tournament of Champions and has continued to make waves outside of Global. Recently, Jake Hix joined his brothers as a lumberjack at the Atlantic Wrestling Club’s Fresh!West Special on August 1st. He used the platform to eliminate both members of the Furious Fists of God from the Ruby’s Surf City Diner Match. While this is a shocking appearance, I am in receipt of reports that Jake Hix and Dawn McFarlane were out together at a nightclub. While many wonder if he is simply keeping in touch with a colleague, Dawn’s appearance was reported by one observer to be “a scantily clad whore looking for a party.” This reporter has to wonder what Lia thinks about these developments. An additional source pointed out that there are two other more promising possibilities. Jake Hix has been recruiting talent to come to Global and he might have been inviting Dawn back since she is no longer the GCW Champion. But… Considering Dawn’s outfit, the more likely scenario may be that Dawn is recruiting Jake by any means necessary for GCW’s roster. Another steal away from Global would be a crippling coup for GCW to pull on Global, and with Jake’s contract coming up for renewal this would be the appropriate moment. Considering last year’s departures, Jake Hix’ recent loss of his good friend “Sensational” Stephen Reed and his brothers’ hinted departure it may be that Global’s Heavyweight Champion is considering a move out of the company. When asked for comment, Jake’s representatives simply gave a very terse “No Comment.” We’ll be following developments in this story very closely. Noi Tsunami. ---- 14 August 2006 When approached for comment, AWC Fresh!west Entertainment Manager David "Pearl" Harber refused to confirm or deny reports that Hix had signed a pre-contract agreement with the hotly-backed promotion. Speculation is rife over whether the GW Heavyweight champion was paid to appear at AWC's Huntington Beach event.
