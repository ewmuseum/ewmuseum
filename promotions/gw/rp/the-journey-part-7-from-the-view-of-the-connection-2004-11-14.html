The Journey: Part 7/From the view of 'The Connection'

14 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-14-04 Early Evening Well folks this is astonishingly enough part SEVEN of our little trip into the lives of the members of the tag team, stable, whatever you want to call it in “The Connection” and the good news is that its not going to end anytime soon! Its about 6:45 EST in New York City and while a good portion of the city is getting ready for the evening news we find that some people that live in a residence on the outskirts of Time Square are getting ready to have a very special time in the home on 451 Evergreen Avenue? Now you may wonder WHY 6:45 is a special time huh? Well at this time every week on Sundays the family that is the “Connection” gathers around and has a dinner together. Why? Because this is how the unit stays united in its quest and just because it is something that was a part of Joshua’s upbringing in London, England and its something that he feels is necessary to the survival of the group as well as his own personal self! On this day Joshua is at work in the kitchen making sure that he has everything just right for the salad and main course because well’tonight Joshua has something special planned for the meal! See its been officially about 3 years outside of wrestling that Joshua has been dating Zack and while we all know that in the country that IS the United States of America a gay marriage is outlawed Josh could give two fucks because of how much he cares for him. To Joshua he’s living a dream and no one will wake him from it because to him life is too blasted short to have someone tell HIM that what he is doing is a sin or that he needs a woman in his life! Why? THAT IS WHAT DROVE HIM TO MEN IN THE FIRST FUCKING PLACE! Sorry had to get that bit of immaturity out of my system. Shall we continue? So dinnertime came and Joshua, dressed in a white dress shirt, black dress pants and matching black socks came out with the food and set in on the table and when he called out “DINNER!” they call came coming out! (pardon the pun but it couldn’t be helped) “Wow something smells good! Brother did you cook tonight?” was the first thing that was said by the younger sister of Joshua Curtis and the “leader” of the group cleared his throat before speaking to his younger sister. “Well Ami” Joshua began “tonight we are going to have our Thanksgiving dinner early and that’s mainly because of the fact that in a couple of weeks I won’t be able to have it due to the schedule they got me on this week coming up with the big match in SiT for Global Wrestling and plus I thought that we should just have a really nice dinner compared to the charcoaled mishap we had 2 days ago.” Joshua said jokingly referring to the dinner in which Juri kind of burned a ham! “HEY!” was all you heard when Juri came walking out with Tina behind her and apparently she didn’t take TOO kindly to the reference about the mishap. “What happened Juri-sempai?” Ami asked and rather than hear Juri be a-cussin? Tina decided to take control of the conversation so that there weren’t any problems. “Two days ago we were supposed to have a honey ham for a big dinner and somehow the thing burned! Juri swears that she was there checking on it and that much IS true but how the hell it burned really IS a mystery to all of us still! Joshua knows it was an accident but for a couple of days anyway he doesn’t really want the stove to be used except for a special occasion with tonight being a good example.” Tina explained as they took their seats at the table and one noticeable vacancy was puzzling to a lot of people. “Where’s Zack?” Ami asked quizzically and got shrugs from Juri and Tina before Joshua spoke up “Zack is a little under the weather tonight. He thought that his headaches were gone and went out with me to get the stuff for dinner tonight but he got violently ill in the store and had a “blackout” if you will. I quickly had paid for the stuff we needed and then went about getting him to the car before he got sick again.” Joshua explained with a hint of sadness as this unfortunately is how his mother back home in England sometimes got and he never knew how to help but he does the best that he can for Zack because without him Joshua was only a fraction of what he could be with him! As dinner progressed it was noticeable that Joshua was looking lost without one of the few people that mattered in his life and that’s not to say that the rest of the faction don’t matter because they do but fact remained that Joshua had his beloved on his mind for a good portion of the day after the “seizure” as Joshua referred to it when he first came home with Zack. Poor guy was broke out in a cold sweat and Joshua was essentially a leaning post for him, which he didn’t mind at all and when they got to the bathtub believe me when I tell you that Josh didn’t want to leave but the man DID need his privacy! Well after much prodding from the gallery Joshua went and excused himself from the table before leaving and heading upstairs to the bedroom where Zack laid peacefully asleep and Joshua couldn’t help but let a weak smile escape him as he looked at Zack lying there and while the urge was great Joshua held back on kissing his lover and just gently stroked his face and ran his fingers through the long, black hair that he had. “Oh honey why does the good Lord have to be so cruel to us” Why can’t it be me with the problems and then you can be the healthy one?” Joshua said to himself as something entered his mind as he sat there pretty much helpless to the problems that face him? It was almost like a flashback of when Joshua went and had that sit down with Ken Partridge Jr. Arguably that event helped shape Joshua’s fortunes in the fed known as GW because as he will openly tell you the meeting with Ken came when Joshua was at a low point and when he was down on himself. Joshua was almost ready to give up on himself but when he talked to Ken things changed. When Ken had a lapse during the conversation Joshua was worried about his friend and he has told Zack openly that he still does worry about him and Zack supports Joshua because Zack doesn’t want Joshua to feel like he can’t talk to people that aren’t him or the Connection! When Joshua snapped out of his gaze it was because Zack had woken up and pulled Joshua on top of him all the while kissing him full on the lips and when Joshua broke the kiss he was winded! “Sorry about that but something seemed to be bothering you so I thought maybe that would help keep your mind at ease.” Zack said with a half wicked grin on his face! “Oh it got my mind off of all the bad things and made me want something else! Problem is the girls are home so we can’t right now.” Joshua said pouting before claiming a seat Indian style next to Zack and all the while admiring what belongs to him! “Want me to go get you some dinner hun?” Josh asked and Zack nodded as Joshua went down the spiral staircase to go get Zack some dinner and to say that Zack appreciated it would be an understatement! The rest of the night for the two was spent in the bedroom just talking and cuddling which was something that admittedly they didn’t do much of anymore but that was only because of the schedule that Josh has been on! Joshua loves to be with his family and I know that it seems like a lot of the time he is with Zack but truthfully Zack’s health is of pretty high importance to Joshua also and that’s because a family member that Joshua didn’t know too well but cared for all the same died from some complications a while ago and Josh treats Zack just like a family member just slightly different because he’s DATING him! As we leave the house for now we see Joshua Curtis fast asleep in his lover’s arms with his head gently resting on Zack’s chest knowing that while this week they will be together at Evo next week Joshua has to go into a match that could be career shortening but we will cross that bridge when we get to it? Au Revoir darling!
