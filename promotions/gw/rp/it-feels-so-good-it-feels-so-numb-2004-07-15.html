It Feels So Good, It Feels So Numb

15 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07.15.04 Everything... he had lost everything. In one flail swoop, Ken Partridge Jr, the man heralded across the nations as 'Superstar' had lost all that he had. Leinad Soma took his International Championship from him in what Ken considered one of his poorest efforts to date. Couple that together with the fact that Soma is one of the best in the profession and Ken's loss was a given. He felt naked now... whenever he went out to that ring and that strap wasn't around his chisled waist... he felt nude. He was beginning to crave it... like Gollum did with the one ring. That title was Ken's 'precious,' and he, like Gollum, was willing to do whatever it took to get it back. It had come to consume and take control over him. His desire for it was the thing guiding him now. But there was a balance. The beauty by the name Gunner Addison. She was his calming factor. Only she wasn't by his side now. She stood with Ely Braxton, a brash redneck with no class whatsoever. She too was gone... and she had been for some time. However, whatever thoughts Ken had of winning her back had subsided since he'd lost his belt. There was only one ray of hope now. The Master of the Mat tournament. This Monday, he held redemption in his hands as two birds could be slain with one stone. Not only would he advance to the Master of the Mat tournament, but he would win the GW World Television Championship in the process. ____________________________________________________________ The Elite's lockerroom looked as if it had been ransacked when Josh entered it. Broken lamps, glasses, and vases were lacing the groung while furniture was upturned and tables were shattered. 'I can't believe this,' shouted an angry Ken Partridge Jr. 'It's gone... my International Championship is gone.' Josh took an immediate sigh as he released his fighting stance upon seeing his teammate. 'What do you mean it's gone,' questioned Josh. 'Somebody took it?' Kenn looked up at 'American Youth' with rage and anger in his eyes, now knowing for the first time that he was there. 'You weren't even watching, were you,' accused KPJ. ' You have absolutly no idea what happened, do you?' The World Champion began to back off a step as Ken approached him. 'What do you mean, man?' 'That explains why you WEREN'T THERE FOR ME!!!' Ken looked as though he was going to lunge after Josh, but his restraint was still great and he knew it wasn't Josh's fault that he'd lost his title. Instead he pulled his hands top his head, grabbing his beautiful hair, and kicked at a half broken vase with a loud yell. 'Leinad Soma took my International Championship from me, and you were nowhere to be seen,' reasserted Ken in a disappointed tone of voice. 'Ken,' began Josh, 'Bubba called me into his office after we saw Gunner. He said that he had to talk to me about a pressing matter. He kept me in there during your match. I didn't think you'd need me. I figured you'd beat Soma hands down.' Ken calmed down a good bit as the acceptance of the facts that had occured settled in. 'I know it wasn't your fault, Josh,' sighed Ken with a softness in his voice. 'I just can't believe I let it slip through my fingers like that. Especially after seeing Gunner for the first time in... well... forever. It wasn't supposed to happen like that. I was suppose to go out there and defend my title, winning the match in grand fashion as she watched from a monitor in the back. Then, after, she was supposed to come congratulate me with champagne and hugs. Instead, I come back through the curtain with my head down and nobody to console me.' Just as Josh is about to offer words of solace, there's a knock on the door. He looks at Ken, who begins walking past Josh to open the door. 'What did McCoy want to talk to you about,' he asked. 'Just issues on the Master of the Mat title match,' responded Josh. Ken nodded as he opened the door and came face to face with Gunner Addison. She immediatly lunged up and hugged him for a few short seconds before pulling away. 'I'm so sorry about what happened tonight with Soma,' she said. 'Thanks,' responded Ken shyly. 'Look, I don't have much time,' Gun rushed, 'Ely will be finsihed talking to Bubba soon. You're match next week, against Juan Gonzalez to advance to the Master of the Mat... well, it's now for the Television Championship. I thought that might cheer you up.' Gunner began to walk away quickly before turning around and looking back at a hightened Ken. 'Oh,' she winked, 'Go get 'em, Tiger.' Ken smiled as Gunner walked away, as a whole new beast had been born withing him by the events that had occured in only sixty seconds. His mind finally became clear and he turned to Josh. 'Redemption, my friend, is mine.'
