Hunting the 'hunter'

9 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/09/05 City: Las Vegas, Nevada Place: LAS - Las Vegas Airport Time: 7:03pm A sea of humans file out of the airplane, as they make their way through the tunnel they can be compared to sheep going to slaughter. Everyone pushing to go further and faster, without knowing where they're going or why they want to. Only to find out when they reach their destination, that maybe... just maybe they shouldn't be so eager to push. A crowd stands around tunnel, some holding signs and others holding tape recorders. They are certainly awaiting the arrival of Global Wrestling superstars, more than likely Ken Partridge Jr. in particular. As the people begin to file out, a man in a red Chicago Blackhawks ballcap and a blue jean jacket glances at the crowd gathered as he walks over to luggage claim. Just another face in the crowd, he continues on through the airport as a shower of cheers erupts behind him. A vibration in the pocket of the man's blue jeans startles his attention. He reaches deep into his pocket and pulls out the blue lit Motorola flip phone. He flips it open and places it against his ear... 'Hello.' The man said, confidently as he stopped in front of a gift shop. 'Hey Jason, how did the flight go?' A woman responded on the other end of the line. 'It went alright... in-flight movie kinda sucked.' Jason answered with a chuckle. 'It happens... so I take it you got the seats?' She asked intently. 'Yeah... it wasn't a problem. The people with the seats behind Ken Partridge Jr. weren't in any hurry to get to Las Vegas. I paid them double what they paid for their plane tickets and it was a piece of cake.' Jason responded with a smile before he ducked into the gift shop. 'Anything interesting?' The woman asked. 'Not much... seems Ken Partridge Jr. isn't that rattled. He said that Zenith is his prey and that everyone knows the last thing you ever want to do is make a match personal. He said that Christian made a huge mistake in doing so. Not only that, he said Zenith's failure to kill him was from lack of experience and on and on and on...' Jason said as he took a look at postcards. There was a long pause on the phone as the woman on the other side started to write down all the information that Jason just told her. 'Are you there Vivica?' Jason asked... 'Yeah... I'm here, just getting everything down on paper... I think Christian will have some fun with this.' Vivica said with a smile as she sits in a hotel room somewhere in Las Vegas. The lights are dim as she scribbles on the hotel stationary under a dim desk lamp. 'Anything else?' 'Well... I put a tracer on his carry-on bag. But that bag could've been any one of them I suppose, he didn't have a clue... his attention was on other things. If I heard them call him champ one more time I think it would've made me vomit. All their talking about revenge and redemption... seems Ken Partridge Jr. is quite the artist as well, he drew a flattering picture of Zenith on the way here.' Jason said looking at other souvenirs in the gift shop. 'Don't worry about the tracer... we don't need it.' Vivica said with a chuckle, 'Seems like Ken is doing all the work himself. Can't get the match out of his mind, anything and everything he's got is on Zenith now. Everything seems to be going as planned, no further actions seem to be necessary.' 'Yeah... he barely even acknowledged Jackson's existence.' Jason said as he left the gift shop, 'I don't think he has a clue.' 'Great.' Vivica said with a smile as she scribbled more information down on the stationary. The door to the hotel room opened and Christian Zenith stepped in. Zenith hung his black custom suit jacket up on the hangar. Underneath Christian wore a black suit vest and a sleeveless white dress shirt with a black tie. Vivica looked back at Zenith with a smile... 'I got to go Jason... Christian just walked in, I'll relay your information to him.' Vivica said before she hung up the phone. Christian walked over to her, he blue eyes sparkled when he flipped on the light switch. Christian grabbed the paper from her hand and glanced it over... 'So I'm his 'prey' now...' Christian said looking over the paper, 'he don't seem to be doing much about it. What exactly does it take to be a hunter? A hunter doesn't stand around and wait for his meals to come to him, a hunter doesn't make idle threats to his friends when he thinks his opponent isn't watching. Those are the characteristics of a meal.' 'A hunter keeps his eyes on the prey at all times and doesn't let him go until he knows the job is finished, a hunter doesn't make threats when his prey isn't around. I took the fight to Kenny boy and I took his girlfriend. Partridge had no choice but to come after me... and all this time he thinks I tried to kill him. In all reality... his death was nothing more than a plan B.' Christian paced as he continued, 'Plan A was that Kenny boy would go save his girlfriend and all would be right in the world. But he took the gamble, he had more than a minute to save his girlfriend and waited until the last 3 seconds to do it... 3 seconds that he shouldn't have had in the first place. That's not hunting, that's stupidity.' 'Yeah,' Vivica replied 'he knows what you look like and drew a perfect picture of you LeMaster said. I take it that in doing so Ken thinks he knows everything about you.' 'If he knew half as much as he thought he did, he'd know that I'm everything but inexperienced. I've been doing this kind of stuff since I was 16 years old... doing it legally is the only thing that's new to me. The law changes but the rules stay the same. I'll do anything it takes to win... he won't. That's why a month ago Mr. Partridge went on that losing streak, and it's about to start over again. I'm not the one who has made a mistake... I'm not the one who has taken my mind off one of my opponents.' Christian said as he sat down on the bed, 'This match isn't just about Ken Partridge Jr... no matter what his friends tell him. They can call him 'champ' all they want to... he isn't champ yet. And as long as I'm breathing, it isn't going to happen anytime soon.' Just as the words escaped his mouth, Vivica's cell phone on the desk began to ring. She picked it up and took a quick look at the number. She looked over to Christian and held the phone in front of her... 'It's for you.' She said before flipping on the television, and watching an episode of CSI. Christian took the phone and looked down at the number before he flipped the phone open. Putting the phone against his ear he responded... 'What's up?' He said before he listened curiously. 'Partridge just arrived, I was just talking about it with Vivica.' Christian listened for more information... 'Oh, KPJ thinks I've screwed up and he's out for blood now... I think it's down to me and you now.' Zenith said as he stood up once again. 'May the best man win then... and one last thing... Jackson, don't you dare underestimate me or I will break you and our little puppet KPJ.' Zenith said as he flipped his phone shut and threw it back onto the bed. Thoughts of victory ran through the mind of Christian Zenith as he looked back at his own reflection in the mirror. A crooked smile crossed his face, and looked back at him in the mirror... 'Please toy with me Kenny boy... cause I'm the one pulling your strings.'
