Kakoriaphobia.

25 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 25th, 2004. Four twenty six AM, and here I am wide-awake. My eyelids refuse to entrap my vision any longer as I gaze about looking for any perception of monumental proportion. Sleep wont answer my beckoning and my body refuses to concentrate its efforts on rest. It's just as well that I can't sleep, I don't really need another night of haunting dreams, a night of dreams where both Amber and Alexia inform me of their undying love. The dreams are without a doubt wonderful. However awaking from those dreams is what rips my heart out. So as insomnia takes control I let my body and my mind run wild. My body clad in a pair of black Adidas Gym shorts with the white logo on the right leg and Nike 'Respect' air trainers of the same color. My legs pump hard on the treadmill that resides in the Global Wrestling gym. My eyes are closed, as my hands are free of the bars my body desperately pushing to keep up with the inhuman speed of the treadmill as my mind races even faster. Opening my eyes I snare the white bottle of water off of the drink holder on the treadmill. A quick drink is all I need as I squeeze the bottle and blast it into my mouth. Another squeeze of the bottle hits my sweat covered brow, and a third squeeze my bare and sweaty torso. Replacing the drink to where I found it I surge on, looking down at the clock again I realize I've been steadily keeping this strenuous pace for approximately twelve minutes and forty six seconds. Closing my eyes again my tired body keeps going as if I'm a human version of the energizer battery. With my eyes closed and no light penetrating my eyelids my mind begins to race once again. My mind moves with the speed of lightning as painful memories that seem to be etched onto my heart carved on my soul and burnt into my cerebral cortex resurface. As images of Amber, Alexia, Tommy and numerous other painful moments of reminiscence flash before my minds eye I squeeze my eyes closed even harder almost as if it would help block out the pain, but it is to no avail. 'What is wrong with me?' I audibly asked myself not expecting an answer from the empty gym which is nothing more than a forest of exercise equipment and drink machines. Was I Manic-depressive? A mood disorder characterized by mood swings from deep depression to great elation. My mood's had been changing greatly from day to day and even moment to moment but the moods always seemed to stay in a fairly low state, meaning it was unlikely that I was manic-depressive. Perhaps, Mania? A disorder characterized by an abnormally high level of activity, irresponsible behavior, and a feeling of elation. The sufferer has also been known to experience delusions of grandeur. That couldn't be my problem I wasn't anywhere close to feeling elated. Paranoia? A delusional disorder where a person may feel he is being persecuted or have delusions of grandeur, as well as feelings of excessive suspicion and hostility. Was this my problem? It almost seemed like it but I knew that even as marked of a man as I was I knew whom I could trust and whom I could not. And honestly there were a lot of people I put my trust in. Or was it schizophrenia? A group of psychotic characterized by loss of contact with reality, disturbances in thinking, perception, emotional reaction and it's often accompanied by bizarre behavior. Hallucinations in the form of voices or vast delusions are common place as well. Could that be it? It was extremely, extremely close. I had somewhat lost touch with my reality, my thoughts truly were a bit disturbed, my emotions were flaring out of control and my behavior seemed far more bizarre than usual. Automatism? A state in which the sufferer carries out actions without realizing what he was doing. No, that wasn't it. No, no, no! In fact I had to be completely honest and admit to myself that I didn't know what was wrong with me. I was shaken, I was an emotional wreck. I was self-loathing, brooding and frighteningly dangerous. It's as if I was sitting on the proverbial edge and as I overlooked that edge it was almost like I was prepared to fall. But when or if I fell would it be because I was pushed…or because I jumped? Amidst all of these pondering my right hand reached forward and I opened my eyes searching for the button to bring this treadmill to a stop. Finding the button I turned the treadmill speed down quickly before it finally reached a dead stop. Hitting the power button with my right hand and snatching up the bottle of water with my left, I stepped off of the treadmill and onto the concrete floor of the gym. I knelt down my right knee on the concrete as I once again took a long drink from the bottle of water. Glancing around the gym I looked for what other equipment could be of use to me. I was physically and mentally exhausted from running. When I ran it almost seemed as if I was running away from my problems…perhaps that explains my long obsession with the treadmill. Standing to my full stature of five foot ten I shook my shaggy black locks trying to rid the sweat that had crept onto it. My bluish gray eyes swept across the Dead Sea of gym equipment looking for anything to catch my eye. That's when I saw it…a punching bag. It seemed the perfect thing for me at that moment. A chance to take out my frustrations with violence and sheer physicality. I made my quick steps across the floor of the gym the patented 'shox' in my Nike's made for a comfortable walk across the concrete. Stepping around a weight bench and a cluster of free weights I made my way closer to the punching bag. The big black bag was hanging there on its chain waiting for me. It was waiting for my abuse as it hung patiently. I approached it with a grim look of frustration on my face. I stepped close too it and eyed the large bag over. Satisfied with it's quality I looked around for a pair of gloves. I spotted a shelf full of gloves nearby. There were gloves of all types of various natures. Walking to the shelf I looked them over before making my selection. The pair I chose were black with red trim. They weren't boxing gloves but rather the style I was used too, a padded glove that only went so far as to cover your knuckles. I had used this type numerous times in training and competing in Taekwondo. Pulling the gloves on I strapped them up. A quick jab at the air proved the gloves to be satisfactory as I turned to face the punching bag. As I stared at the black bag it was almost ominous and challenging. It glared at me from it's black leather surface as it challenged me to move it. I did so with a quick right handed jab. It swung back on it's chain as it rushed me looking for retribution. With my left I jabbed at it again as it rocked back only to come forth. Catching the bag with my gloved hands I steadied it. Closing my eyes I stepped back. As my eyelids closed the bag was still visible only it began a metamorphous. It quickly transformed into Deacon Holmes. I fired blindly at the bag with a round kick and I heard the chain clank as my foot slammed into the leather. The bag rocked back and forth before I opened my eyes and steadied it yet again. As the bag steadied I closed my eyes once more, slowly the bag transformed yet again this time with the face of Jin Akiyara staring back at me. With my eyes closed for a better sense of balance and direction I whipped around with a spinning outside crescent kick and felt the satisfaction of a perfect shot as the side of my foot drilled the bag in the precise location I had targeted with closed eyes. Steadying the bag once more I closed my eyes a final time. But this time it wasn't the face of Jin Akiyara or Deacon Holmes that rudely greeted me, instead it was a trio of faces…Roz Blayze, K.C. Jacobs and Tommy Michaels. Taking a step back I fired a wild Tornado kick slamming into the bag with a high rate of impact. The bag flew about unpredictably due to the power I had nailed it with. I took two steps back and dropped to my knees as the faces of my enemies came rushing back through my mind. I knew that I was a man without fear…but if there was one thing that had potential to scare me it was losing. Losing… That's when it hit me, my eyes sprung open, I stood to my feet at full attention…I knew what my problem was. It wasn't a severe mental disorder. And yes perhaps I did have a few emotional issues but those weren't my problem. Instead my problem was all too simple. I had kakoriaphobia, or in more simplistic terms…the fear of failure.
