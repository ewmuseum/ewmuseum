A dark union(preview)

29 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/29/03 Quiet. The darkness had covered the Zero estate like a blanket. Camera’s everywhere. Almost the best security system money can buy. Almost. Scaling one of the walls with ease and not being detected by any of the camera’s, I walk around the grounds. I look around awaiting the sound of dogs or something to attack me. Nothing. I make my way to the main house, looking at all the trees and smaller houses on the grounds. At night the grounds remind me of Versallis. Beautiful. I pull out a vile and pour it on the back door knob. It falls off rather quickly as the bluish liquid burns through it. I start to open the door when I hear a rather low growl. I turn and see a German Shepard looking at me growling, his hair standing straight up, showing his teeth. And saliva dripping from his mouth. I stare at the dog. My eyes start to glow a dark purple. The dog yelps and walks off. I smirk as I walk in the back door. Voices. Screaming voices. I stand in the dark room and listen to the voices. “NEVER EVER SAY HER NAME!” I hear one of them yell….I stay hidden as to not become part of whatever is goin on in there. A Struggle. I hear a struggle start between the men. Oh the sweet sound of violence. The sweetest sound I have heard in a long time. Shots fired. I smell blood. Someone is down. The smell of mortality draining away. I hear a door a door slam. Someone has either walked in or left. “I need to step out for a while. Look after Deacon for me.” She looks over at Deacon, as he continues to kick Matthew, screaming profanities. “I will.”</blue> I move into the room unnoticed where there are two standing there, a man and a woman. I think to myself what I should do now. Should I introduce myself? Should I sit here and stay quiet? I sit there pondering. What I should do? I lie in wait. I wait for the one they call John Zero.
