Calm before the storm

2 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/2/04 Earlier that night, Brick was feeling rather disappointed. He'd heard that at Crossfire, he'd be facing Wrestling Midwest Heartland champion Umbrage. Both wrestlers were two of the best examples of hardcore wrestlers in each promotion. However, they would not be facing each other in a hardcore match. They would be facing each other in a standard match. This did not sit well with him. Brick felt that he needed to be competing in a more no-holds-barred type of match to make the proper impact. He had campaigned with Bubba and Christina to change the match into a hardcore match, even going so far as to request that he and Umbrage face off in the Pit, the most brutal match Global Wrestling had to offer. Unfortunately, at least in Brick's mind, the match stayed as is. Brick was still disappointed until his friend Donny McNasty gave him a few words of wisdom: 'It ain't the type of match that makes it hardcore. It's the wrestlers in the match.' These words were what spurred Brick to make his match the best that he could. And he did, finally defeating Umbrage by countering an attempted hurricanrana with the Bricklayer. Now Brick was taking a shower and enjoying his victory. A few minutes later, Brick headed out of his dressing room and into the backstage hall. He then saw a stage hand 'Say, man, you seen Bubba McCoy or Ely Braxton? I need to talk to one o' them.' 'You mean you haven't heard?' 'Heard what?' 'Mr. McCoy and Josh were just attacked during the contract signing.' 'Bubba was there? What happened?' 'Well, Mr. McCoy had just heard that Ms. McCoy was giving Maverick Reno a shot at the World Title at Master of the Mat V. Anyway, he objected to Reno getting the title shot. Then a brawl broke out. Then next thing anybody knows, Ms. McCoy 'She did what?!' 'She left the ring with Reno. She's apparently joined TUE.' This news didn't exactly make Brick feel comfortable. With Christina on their side, TUE now had even more power. And this was never a good thing. 'Mr. McCoy!' Brick looked to see Bubba McCoy walking slowly in his direction. Walking close behind him were Ely Braxton and Jesse Briggs. All three men were rubbing various parts of their body, showing the fight they had just been through. Immediately, Brick ran towards them. 'Bubba! Bubba, I just heard what happened. Are you--?' Bubba said nothing. It was as if he couldn't. The look on his face suggested a broken heart. 'Bubba?' The GW owner merely walked past him, not saying a word. All Brick could do was watch him walk away. 'Been like that since we left the ring,' said Jesse, as he rubbed the back of his head. 'We tried to help 'im out, but he just shrugged us off.' 'Can't be easy having his own daughter turn on him.' 'An' to hook up with Reno, of all people!' spat Ely. 'Shoulda ripped the sorry little bastard a new hole right there in Bubba's office! If it weren't for Bubba, I would have!' 'I'm just sorry I wasn't out there to help.' 'Yeah, where the hell were you, anyway?' asked Jesse in an accusatory tone. 'Hey, man, I just heard about it, okay? Plus, I just got out of the shower.' 'Uh-huh.' 'Lay off o' Brick, Jesse. He had a pretty rough match against that guy Umbrage.' 'Not as rough as your match,' Brick replied, pointing to the welts around Ely's neck. Ely had been in the opening match, facing Juri Hyobanshi. It was a grueling match, with the psychotic young girl dominating the match. The match ended in a DQ for Ely after the referee caught him with a steel chair that Juri had brought into the ring. While he and the ref argued the matter, Juri came up from behind and wrapped her steel chain around Ely's neck and began to strangle him. He was aided by his girlfriend Gunner Addison. Juri left the ring immediately, laughing all the way. 'Yeah, I know. Next time I see that little...' 'Easy, man. Let's worry about that later. Right now, we got a bigger problem.' 'Yeah,' added Jesse, 'Christina joinin' up with the damn Upper Echelon.' 'We all know Josh and Partridge won't be their only targets.' 'Yeah. Each one of us has a bulls-eye on his back. I know Christina's still pissed at me for choosin' Gunner over her. And Gunner might get some, too.' 'Yeah, and in addition to the fact that I got in Christina's face on your behalf, I don't think she ever really quite forgave me for that one incident back in January 2001.' 'You mean the time you an' Ruckus Inc.--?' 'Yeah. Bottom line is we've all got to stick together and we gotta stick by Bubba. Because Bubba is gonna need all the help he can get. That includes us, that includes Donny and it might include...' 'Wait, you're not thinkin' o' goin' to...' 'I just might.' 'But I thought you hated him.' 'Not as much as I hate the idea o' TUE having more power in this company than they already have. Besides, they've got their sights on him, too. Listen, let's just head to the hospital and see how Donny's doing. He's had the roughest match out of any of us.' 'I'm gonna go check on Bubba, first. Then I'll go get Gunner an' we'll meet ya there.' 'I'll come with ya,' said Jesse as he followed Ely. 'See you guys there.'
