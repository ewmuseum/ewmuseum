Pull Harder On The Strings Of Your Martyr (Chasing Immortality)

15 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/15/05 Prelude: Claiming Immortality ---- The heat of 1,000 suns beat down upon him. Glaring lights obstructing his vision, making his new situation even more precarious than he figured to be in. Desperately he tried to break free, moving his arms and legs violently. Ever muscle in his body began to move, looking to exploit a weakness in his current position in order to free himself. Bright flares shot out from all around him, a thousand voices screamed in unison into a deafening roar. ONE! He tried shifting his weight to take the burden off his shoulders. His resistance seemed futile through, he has bound onto his shoulder blades pinning him hard against the surface. He could barely see beyond the constraints of his own body, the fact that a mixure of blood and sweat began to pour into his eyes certainly didn't help his cause. Helplessly he listened as another smack on the ground raised the volume of his surroundings ten fold. TWO! 'I won't let it end this way.' He said to himself as he collected all he had left in his body for one last effort. With that, he braced his hands firmly against the ground and pushed off with the adrenaline pumping through his veins. Another smack on the stretched cloth beneath him was not met with the same reaction as he sat up and glared at his nemesis, now on her hands and knees just a couple mere yards away. Wiping the blood, sweat, and tears away from his battle stricken face... his body running on fumes when he painfully reached a verticle base. One look into his cadet blue eyes sent her backing up away. Whether from fear or intelligence, it didn't really matter. She wasn't the one he was after, she was merely in his way. Her eyes suddenly shifted away from his, and he stopped momentarily sensing something coming from behind him. Suddenly he was crashed into from behind, sending him forward into the girl in front of them. Crushed against the padded steel post he momentarily lost track of his senses. Feeling as if he'd just been hit by a truck he slowly backed away from the post. He watched as the woman who had just tried to defeat him fell limp down onto the floor. Grasping his chest in pain, he tried to turn around but to no avail. He was suddenly grasped around the throat from behind. It wasn't a truck that he was hit by... it was a Train. Anthony James to be specific. 'The Hollowpoint...' he said to himself, quickly hooking his leg inside of that of Train's seconds before his target tried to lift him into the air. A second effort by James seemed useless as well. He knew what he was doing afterall... and he'd had the man known as Train well scouted for years. Trying to take him off guard once again, Train releases the hold. Then his instincts took over. Quickly he turned around just in time for Anthony to get a good look at the intensity in his eyes before getting a boot to the stomach. The thousands of voices began to grow louder in volume once again. This time it wasn't in ectasy... this time it was in desperation. 'It's over.' he said aloud before picking Train up off the ground. The man held him upside down before he lept forward... violently smashing the face of Anthony James into the ground. A smug sense of satisfaction came over him as he rolled over Anthony James and looked into the eyes of a defeated man. This was a day he'd dreamed of for years. Vengeance, vindication, revenge... a rose by any name would still be as sweet, but not like this. ONE! His cold cadet blue eyes glaced over at the woman who still lied seemingly unconcious by one of the eight corners. TWO! Underneathe his body he could feel Anthony James struggle to move, his effort was fruitless in the end. THREE! A roar of disapproval sounded along with the chiming of a bell. The man looked down at Train with a glare before pushing his aching body up to his feet. Everyone around him seemed heartbroken, what a beautiful thing to behold. A fine tribute to his dominence this was. Nothing could hold him back. No one could keep him down. This honor was now his and his alone once again. The best of the best. The king of the mountain. The... 'Winner and new Global Wrestling World Champion... Christian Zenith.' A chuckled escaped his lips as he looked down and the bloodied terrain around him. Covered by a crimson mask from ear to ear. He smiled as he could sense the hostility in the air. It wasn't a fight... it was a massacre. He'd slaughtered their heroes, but it was something the world better get used to. Afterall, it was the beginning of an era. The Zenith Era. ---- Chapter One: Not Enough ---- The night was crawing to a close, but the buzz was just beginning. Outside the confines of the arena in Arkansas... reporters, photographers, journalists, and wrestling enthusiests shoved up against the steel barricades constructed outside the backstage exit. They all pressed their way towards the front for a shot at the exclusive photo and the exclusive interview with the new Global Westling World Champion. Excitement filled the air as the door opened, sending everyone into a roar of questions. Demitri Miasnikov lead the way out, dressed in a black leather jacket and black jeans... he was followed by Jason LeMaster and Vivica J. Valentine. An thunderstorm of flashbulbs ignited as Christian Zenith emerged from the arena, stitches and all with the GW World Title over his shoulder. His cadet blue eyes shielded by black sunglasses, surveying his enviornment. They all wanted to try all wanted to talk to him now... now that he was champion again. He found it quite sickening as he passed, watching the very same people who'd declared him overrated months before pleading to talk with him. Vivica J. Valentine looked back at Christian while they made their way towards the black limo that had pulled up to take them to their next destination. 'You're not going to talk to these jackasses are you?' Vivica said to Christian, but was almost lost over the crowd. A smirk crossed his usually stone cold face. 'It's quit disturbing the thought process of man. A couple months back I was washed up, I was a has been. I had nothing on the likes of Mindy Mercer and Train. Now look at them. They beg and plead to me like I am a God... just like I had told them all along. There is nothing more I would like to do than drop them an 'I told you so.' But they don't deserve it. These assholes don't deserve the attention of a man as great as me... or a woman as great as you, or individuals as great as Demitri and Jason. We are above this Vivica... look at them. That is the face failure, I refuse to grace them with my presence. It's time that they repent for their sins against their God.' Christian Zenith said as he paused in front of the opened limo, staring out at the masses swarming upon his presence like zombies towards a meal. With that Zenith stepped inside the limosine and took a seat, the door shutting quickly behind him. 'Where is the celebration?' Jason LeMaster said with a smile across his face looking back at Christian Zenith who continued to glare out the heavily tented window. 'It's coming, but I do not think it is what you expect it to be.' Zenith replied, never moving his head away from the chaotic scene outside. 'What do you mean?' Vivica asked now intrigued, expecting completely to go back to the Manor and pop open a bottle of champagne. 'We're going back home, to Chicago.' Christian said, 'There are some friends there from a long time ago that need a little visit due to our good fortune. After we have a little party with them, we may go back to my manor and celebrate with a bottle of champagne.' 'What friends?' Demitri asked questionably. 'You'll remember them when I reintroduce you to them. They're very close with Mr. James.' Christian Zenith took his eyes off the outside of the limo for the first time, taking off his sunglasses and looking back at Demitri, 'I looked into his eyes right before I defeated him. He was weak... he was helpless. There was nothing he could do to stop me. There is nothing anyone could do to stop me. All this time I had figured that Anthony would be a worthy adversary... but turns out when you take away all the weapons and throw civilized rules into the mix, he's no better than anyone else. Beating him wasn't satisfying, it wasn't enough to compensate for the hardships that were caused to us by them. So now... we move down the food chain, and we prove a point.' 'Which is?' Vivica eyes Christian Zenith suspiciously. 'We're the best for a reason... and it's about time we teach the world what it means to be the best, one pathetic jackass at a time.' ---- Chapter Two: The Wrath Of Zenith ---- Click... Click... Click... The sound of footsteps resonated off the tall metal walls. Seemingly they came from every direction, but they grew louder and louder as the closed. Under a bright light, 3 men sat... blind-folded, bound, and tied. Silently they tried to wiggle loose, but they knew they were being watched. Closer and closer the footsteps grew as beads of sweat trickled down their foreheads. 'Who the hell are you?' One of the men yelled, breaking the silence. There was no answer. Click... Click... Click... 'What the fuck do you want from me?' The man still demanded an answer, but once again with no results. The shadow of a man cast upon them as they baked under the intense spotlight from above. Under the spotlight Christian Zenith's eyes sparkled like a clear river as they navigated across the three men who sat bound and helpless before him. His face cold and emotionless as he stood before the blindfolded men, arms folded in a position of authority. He wore his trademark black suit jacket with twin cold chains connecting the two sides. Underneath he wore a black suit vest over a white sleeveless dress shirt and a black tie. He was recognizable on sight due to his attire these days... which can both be a curse and a blessing. Quietly Vivica J. Valentine, Demitri Miasnikov, and Jason LeMaster approached from behind the men. A good look around the place brought back memories of a time when things were nothing like they are now. The boxes in the abanndoned warehouse were still stacked up to be used as a stage... and the improptu sound system still seemed to be in place. Although more windows had broken out since then and the loading dock door in which they made their grand entrance in before was no longer operational. Obviously a few animals had turned the warehouse into their home, with pieces of trash on the cold concrete floor throughout the massive vacant facility. Vivica, Demitri, and Jason patiently looked at Zenith... waiting for approval. 'Take off the blindfolds.' Christian Zenith ordered, looking back at the five men who lay before him. As their eyes adjusted Zenith began to pace back and forth in front of them, 'Welcome back to the warehouse... it's a shame two of your friends couldn't make it. Seems as if they're gone out of their way to start trouble with more than just myself. If you miss them, you will be with them soon enough.' 'Man didn't you learn your lesson the first time?' One of the men commented. 'Apparently not.' Zenith stopped pacing, focusing on the one man with the big mouth, 'What brings you here today is an even that happened a couple years ago. You guys may remember it... I was released from the hospital after being shot and I was having a little get together with a few of my people. The three of you, along with two friends, found it necessary to show up and crash my little party. At the time you thought you were something. Now look at you... tied up and helpless, an ironic physical display of the actuality of this circumstance. Running your mouth like you can stand up to me. Pathetic. The three of you were nothing more than pawns for Anthony James, playing you like a fiddle to do the work he wasn't compitant enough to get done himself.' 'He didn't make us do shit!' One of the three men yelled, 'We did it on our own!' 'Don't interrupt me while I'm speaking pawn.' Christian Zenith yelled as he drew Ares out from the inside of his coat jacket, 'How dare you speak at me in such a demanding manner. I've brought you here for you to repent for your sins against myself and my brothers and sisters. Do not hunt what you cannot kill. I watched in sheer ecstacy as your hero Train screamed out for his life. I watched in satisfaction as he looked up at me, limp and helpless covered in his own blood. I looked down upon him a winner as I took from him what he and his followers cared about most. I walk as a God amoung men... I have walked too far and too long to be defied by 2nd tier thug with an attitude problem.' With that said Christian Zenith placed Ares up against the front of the big mouth's skull. He watched as the man closed his eyes and squirmed, trying to get away from the instrument that lie in Zenith's hand. The spotlight from above feels like the surface of the sun as it beats down upon them. His friends watch silently, wishing they could do something to help... 'I would like you to apologize for your actions against your master.' Christian Zenith said staring into the man's eyes and possibly his soul, 'Tell me you're sorry for being such a fucking coward.' The man sat silently as Christian's finger grew itchy on the trigger. Zenith could feel his heartbeat accellerate and his breathing growing heavier and more rapid. He controlled the fate of this man's life in his hands... and he loved it. The man knew he was in danger, but like a deer in headlights he froze. Not knowing what Christian Zenith was capable of. A few moments ago he was eating dinner with his family, away from a life of crime for about a year. Now he sits in an abandon warehouse with a gun pointed to his head, next to two men he hasn't even seen in well over a year. Suddenly Christian smacked the man across the face with a shot that echoed across the room. Wincing in pain the man looked up at Christian. 'I'm sorry... ' The man said under his breath, 'I just want to see my family...' 'I cannot hear you pawn.' Christian Zenith responded. 'I said I'm fucking sorry.' The man shouted out at The Midnight Messiah. 'To who?' Zenith responded as his eyes narrowed. 'To you.' 'You mean you're sorry master?' Christian said watching the man contemplate what to do under the light, 'Don't you have a family at home? A family who cares about you and wants their daddy back? Two children watched as four guys lead by Demitri over here kicked down the door to your home and dragged you out. Your girlfriend and two children cried, pleading with them to leave you alone... having absolutely no idea what's been going on over the past couple years. Didn't you see the looks on their faces? You wouldn't want anything to happen to them would you. Hurting children is by no means something I do... but if they're genetically connected to scum like you, they might deserve to get wiped off the face of the earth. You wouldn't want anything to happen to them would you?' 'No...' 'How do you think your wife is going to take the news that you were found dead in a garage with three bullets in the head because apologize correctly?' Christian Zenith said, 'I'd horribly reconsider the way you speak to me if I were you. I believe you horribly underestimate the impact I could have on your life and the life of your family.' 'I'm sorry...' The man hesistated, nearly in tears thinking about his family, '...master.' A chuckle escaped the lips of Christian Zenith as he pulled the gun away from the man's head. You could see the relief spread across his face as Zenith concealed the handgun back inside of his jacket. Turning his back to the man he began to pace again. This wasn't the challenge he'd been wanting either. Tasks like these had become mundane over the past few years. This wouldn't satisfy his thirst for more. 'Knock them out.' Christian Zenith said as the eyes of his hostages grew wide. Vivica, Demitri, and Jason all rose their revolvers simultaneous and crashed them into the back of the skulls of the three thugs in front of them. With a crack that could be heard from blocks away, the men's heads hung limply in front of them. They'd all suffered a concussion... but nothing two nights in the hospital wouldn't fix. The sensation for more itched deep within Christian Zenith's body once again. As much as he hated Train, his defeat didn't feel as good as he thought it would. He needed something bigger... something for the World to remember him by years after he was gone. He wanted to be worshipped as a legend, the status of an immortal. Reaching on the floor Christian Zenith picked up a bottle of champagne and looked over at Vivica, who stood between Demitri and Vivica. 'So who's next?' ---- Prologue: The Truth ---- Christian Zenith walked through the backstage area of the PTC Super Series I PPV, his eyes navigated his surroundings. There was a scavenger hunt match going on and he knew that the people involved would spordically show up. Lia was in the match, and as much as he may have wanted to give her a hand she'd requested to do it alone... but he also had to give Dawn McFarlane an escort. You never know what kind of whackos are running around backstage in a place like this. XUW had a match here tonight as well, so he could run into anything from bong smoking hippies to zoo monkey's turned professional wrestlers. The place was a zoo alright. Dawn McFarlane's match against Jake Hix was fast approaching, and she couldn't be found. Christian Zenith turned the corner looking for his teammate and partner in crime. There she was talking to a man... wearing a rather odd choice of clothing. He looked like a character straight out of some Japanese cartoon... or a video game. Before he got the chance to walk up to Dawn McFarlane, she had turned around and started heading towards him. As Dawn walked towards Zenith, Christian got a good look at the man she was speaking to as he seemed to be thinking to himself. A character for sure he seemed to appear, but he looked vaguely familiar. Suddenly it his Christian Zenith like a ton of bricks when he looked into the man eyes before he turned around and began to walk in the other direction, it couldn't be could it? It was! How easy that was... it was amazing that no one had seen it before. He chuckled to himself as Dawn McFarlane approached. 'Are you coming to ringside with me for the match?' Dawn asked of Christian, who broke his attention from the man who was walking away and looked into Dawn McFarlane's eyes. 'Yeah,' Christian Zenith asked, 'What was that all about?' 'I'll tell you later.' Dawn said, 'We have a match to get to.' Even though he asked he knew what it was about. It was about the same thing it's always been about. The only question that crossed Christian Zenith's mind was if Dawn knew that. Christian Zenith thought to himself about the situation as he followed Dawn McFarlane down the hallway. This just may be more of a challenge than he previously thought... now the he knew the truth. The one thing that kept crossing Christian Zenith's mind was that things were just about to get a little more interesting in Global Wrestling.
