Boiling over

2 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/2/02 “THAT MOTHERFUCKER!!!” Brick roars, pacing around the holding cell. “Will you calm the hell down, Brick”!” says Ely Braxton. “It’s been a bad night for all of us!” “And Bubba and Logan call *me* hot-headed!” adds Jesse Briggs. This was true. For Brick, Briggs, and Braxton, it has not been the best of nights. Tonight, Brick was cheated out of victory by Salaok and the Nevermore. That meant automatic suspension until Salaok decided he wanted him back. Just as the Nevermore started to close in on Brick, out came Briggs and Braxton to help out. Eventually, a brawl between the two sides broke out before the police rushed down to the ring and broke them up, arresting Briggs, Braxton and Brick. Then Salaok sadistically offered a solution: The three of them face the Nevermore next Evolution in a Handicap match. If they win, the charges get revoked and Brick's suspension will be lifted. Then Salaok took special pleasure in taunting Brick about kidnapping and torturing his best friend Aaron Davis last year. This especially angered Brick, but he was essentially powerless, as he was handcuffed and held by the arresting police officers as they dragged him, Briggs and Braxton away. 30 minutes later? Brick is now suspended from Global Wrestling. He’s bloodied, bruised and mad as hell. “Salaok’s gonna pay for this! Him, Quartz, Vyper and Sabbot, they’re all gonna pay!” “Well, we got a chance,” says Briggs. “Next Evolution, it’s gonna be the three of us beatin” the shit outta the Nevermore, so we gotta keep cool heads.” “Jesse’s right,” adds Braxton. “We gotta remember what’s at stake here. It can’t be personal.” “Can’t make it personal”! That punk motherfucker put us all in jail!! Don’t tell me it ain’t personal!” “Yeah, well, you know how Salaok works the best out of any of us,” Braxton replies. “You know what a twisted, calculating son of a bitch he really is. You know how he uses emotions against people. That’s why he kept bringing up your old buddy. So going into next week’s Evolution, we can’t have any personal baggage. We gotta keep it strictly business.” “It ain’t gonna be easy. I don’t win, I’m outta the company. That messes with my livelihood. I take shit like that personally. Plus, if we ain’t around to stand up to Salaok, who will?” “Aw, will you shut the fuck up!” says a voice on the other end of the cell. “Excuse me?” says Brick, looking in the direction of the voice, which belongs to a rather burly Hell’s Angel. “What are you, deaf” Did I stutter? I said shut the fuck up!” the Hell’s Angel replies, with a couple of his friends chiming in. “You know, y’all really do not want to fuck with either of us right now.” “Oh, and now why would that be?” “Because, considering the mood we’re in, especially our friend here, we’re more than likely to kick your asses,” replies Braxton. “Oh, is that right?” asks another Hell’s Angel. “Yeah, that’s right!” adds Briggs. “Well, looking at your friend there,” says the head Hell’s Angel, pointing to Brick, “I’d say you boys got a bit of disadvantage.” Brick then rises out of his seat and walks right up to the head Hell’s Angel. “Aight, dog, listen up, cause I’m only gonna say this one more time. My boys and I are having a bad night. And even if we weren’t having a bad night, we ain’t cats you wanna fuck with, anyway. Now I may not look like it, but, even bloody, I can still beat your fat ass. So could either o” these two.” “Oh, that right” Well, we saw you boys on the TV before you got here. You weren’t beatin? much o? anything. In fact, you might as well go an? sit your asses down before we make you out like the pussies you really are!” “Oh, really?” “Yeah, really!” “Well, you probably saw that if it wasn’t for those punk ass cops, we’d have beat the Nevermore’s asses, and we can still beat yours!” Then the biker gets in Brick’s face. “Or you could wind up like your buddy Aaron.” Just then, Brick’s expression turns extremely grim. His anger really begins to boil over. Briggs and Braxton notice it, too. Then it happens. Brick lands a right to the biker’s gut and then a left to his jaw. “Ah shit!” Braxton exclaims. Soon, the other bikers start to join in. Pretty soon, Braxton and Briggs follow suit, helping out their buddy. The numbers are daunting, but the three men face the odds. They’ll have to. Because next week, they have to face similar odds when they face the Nevermore.
