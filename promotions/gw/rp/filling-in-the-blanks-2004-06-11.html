Filling in the Blanks

11 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06.11.04 “I love you too, sweetheart.” Los Angeles, California ? The Home of Scott Pierce September 28th, 2002 ? Approximately 8:00 pm. The scene shows Scott Pierce, a young man in his mid-twenties with blonde hair and blue eyes, dressed in a pair of khaki shorts and a dull blue sports shirt. He’s walking through his home from the front door, his hands full with mail. “Sweetheart, who’s Gina?” Scott asks in passing, his wife, Alicia, standing over a fish aquarium, checking the water. She glances back quickly, smiling as her eyes glance over her husband. She’s about 24 with raven black locks that cascade to her shoulders; she’s wearing a white blouse and jeans. She turns back to the aquarium and her fish before answering, “My cousin, Gina. Gina Palierie. Short red hair” I’m sure you met her at the wedding.” Scott nods, having already shuffled further into the stack of mail. He leans against the wall and then smiles, glancing up at his wife. “Ah yes, Gina. How is she?” “She’s fine. Well how am I supposed to know, you have the letter!” Alicia shoots a playful glare back at her husband who jumps and hides behind the turn of the wall, quivering. “Don’t hurt me, miss Alicia!” He screams, his voice more playful than anything else. A smile graces his lips, unable to hold it in. She walks over slowly, slinking to him almost. Slowly, Alicia slips her arms around Scotts neck and turns, pinning him against the wall with her body blocking him in. She grins and kisses his lips once playfully and then again. “Mmm, but hurting you is half the fun.” Scott slips his arms around his wife’s waist and leans in to kiss her, the situation growing less innocent by the moment. “MOMMY! MOMMY! Jessie threw Jeanie into the toilet!” Scott smiles, the voice of his daughter Brittany calling from another part of the house. Alicia puts her head down, resting it on Scott’s chest before looking up and turning her head in the direction of the young voice. “Jessie. Are you trying to flush Jeanie again?” Alicia calls out, as if this is an ordinary happening? and it probably is. She glances at her husband again, smiling softly, “See what happens when we seduce one another” We end up with more of them.” “Noo-oooooo!” A young boy calls out. Alicia smiles and glances up at her husband. Their romantic moment shattered by their children. It has become a common ailment for these two but has yet to truly cause any problems. Alicia leans up and kisses Scott’s lips quickly, “Meet you at 9:45 after I put them to bed?” “White wine, candles, and a hot tub. I’ll meet you there, gorgeous” Scott winks and Alicia grins before turning and walking down the hallway after her children. Scott watches her walk away, his eyes moving lower and lower to finally focus on her bu.. a knock comes at the door and Scott jerks his eyes up quickly. “I’ll get it sweetheart.” Scott calls out before walking towards the door. Still sorting the mail, Scott reaches forward and grabs the doorknob. Slowly, he turns the handle and pulls the door open. Scott offers a smile to his guest even before he realizes who it is. “J” John?” An even larger smile lightens Scott’s face as he opens his arms to embrace his old friend. Standing at the door is John Russell, dressed much more casually than he would have been a few years back. He’s dressed in a pair of jeans and a black GW t-shirt. The two men embrace, John yet to speak. Scott again offers his voice to the ears, “John! What brings you out here?” “Does a man need a reason to come visit an old friend?” John spouts off, smiling in return. Scott walks in, John right behind him. “So it looks like you’ve been doing well for yourself since you left the business.” “I’ve been running a training school.” Scott replies, motioning for John to take a seat in his living room. “Wrestling?” John glances towards Scott, his smile peculiar. Scott shakes his head and motions towards the refrigerator, “Nah, kick-boxing. Been training a few guys for the circuit lately as well.” John nods before Scott continues, “Can I get you a drink, John?” John shakes his head, “No, Scott. Thank you.” Scott nods and turns to open the refrigerator. “I have to admit, Scott.” John says, standing and picking up one of Scott’s kick-boxing trophies. Slowly, John approaches Scott, his eyes studying the plaque on the wood.??my visit here isn’t 100 percent social.” Scott turns and glances at John who is now less than two feet away, a large bronze and wood trophy in his hands. “What’s going on, John?” Scott asks, growing more curious by the moment. He drops the final ice cube into his glass of water, his eyes moving from John’s straining expression to the large instrument in his hands. “Tony sends his regards.” John mutters before pulling back and slamming the trophy against the side of Scott’s head; Scott flails to the side, plummeting towards the floor. Glass shatters and water spills everywhere, ice cubes sliding across the floor Scott grabs the side of his head, blood dripping to the floor where the blunt object made contact. Scott grabs the glass and slings it up into John’s face, causing it to shatter. Scratches cover John’s face and he steps back. Scott spins and kicks John’s legs out from under him, knocking him to the ground. Continuing with the offensive, Scott slams his foot down in an axe kick onto John’s chest. “John, what is going on!”” Scott pulls himself up, blood draining down the side of his face to drench his shirt. Scott reaches for a butcher knife quickly, his old ring-injury slowing him down. John pulls a gun from his back and aims, fires. Blood splatters onto the refrigerator and Scott drops to the ground. Scott clenches his eyes and slowly lifts his head, looking up. Standing in the door way is his son, Jessie. Jessie’s holding a sketch pad and a bucket full of paints and brushes. Art and drawing is his obsession when he’s not terrorizing his younger sister. Scott’s eyes grow wide in fear, his worst nightmare being created around him. Another shot rings out. Jessie falls backwards onto the floor, paints flying up and splattering the wall. Green, blue, yellow, black, white? Red? That’s not paint. A scream rings out as Alicia runs down the hallway towards her fallen child. She drops to her knees and picks up his lifeless body, clutching him in her arms. Scott grunts the pain and glances back, the bullet wound having entered in his upper thigh and exited from behind. John aims a little lower this time and blasts a shot out, shattering Alicia’s knee with a single well-placed bullet. Alicia falls forward onto the ground, still holding her child. Tears flow freely as does the blood from the wound in her leg. John walks to her quickly and grabs her by the hair; he jerks her to the center of the room and hurls her down to the floor, forcing a tremendous impact. Another shot and then a fifth. John puts a hole in Alicia’s other leg and then in her hand. Scott’s vision grows blurry and dark, losing consciousness slowly. Alicia has already passed out, the pain too much for her to bear. John walks to the hall and glances back at Scott before grabbing Jessie’s shoe and dragging his corpse to his mother’s side. “Now, where is that little girl of yours, Scott?” John speaks, glancing down the hallway at the closed doors. “She’s not here” ?Scott manages to get out, tears blurring his speech and his vision even more than it was already. “She’s at a friend’s house.” John smiles in Scott’s general direction then points at set of plates on the dining room table. “So it took four plates for three people?” John shakes his head, “Tsk tsk.” He walks down the hall suddenly, kicking in every door along the way. Scott drops his head, trying to collect himself through the dizziness and pain. “Come out, come out, where ever you aaaaare.” John’s voice echoes throughout the home. Finally he kicks in the right door and grins, his eyes returning to Scott who drops his head, his pain complete. “There you are.” John walks into the room and grabs Brittany about the waist. He lifts her up and carries her into the room, her golden hair tumbling about her head. Tears pour from her eyes, screams from between her lips. John tosses her atop her mother and pulls his gun again, taking aim. “Tell your little girl good night, Scott.” Before Scott can protest or speak, a shot rings out. Another shell hits the floor. The little girl falls forward atop her mother’s bosom. Scott buries his face, the tears flowing without end. John sighs and grabs a bottle of brandy from a nearby shelf and pops the top. He drinks a mouthful and then another before emptying the bottle atop Alicia and her children. “Why!” Why did you do this? What .. what have I done?” John ignores Scott’s screams as he flips over a silver lighter and drops it atop the brandy soaked bodies. The liquor ignites instantly, the clothes burning quickly after this. Smoke begins to below up, the room turning gray and hazy. The heat from the burning bodies becomes overbearing, the stench even worse than any heat. Flesh. Bone. Muscle. It’s all burning. A life is burning. Three lives have been lost. Umpteen shattered. John walks to Scott and drags him within view of the bodies, propping him up so that he can’t turn away from the image. John pulls his gun and fires four bullets, one into the joints on each of Scott’s four limbs. Blood pouring, Scott’s head drops back against the wall. The last image he sees before he goes black is movement. His wife has woken up? Another tears pours from Scott’s eye, the look on his wife’s face before she drops back onto the floor, the fire eating away at her. When one person dies, families suffer. When a family dies, entire communities suffer. They say that the only way a person forgets is if that person stops caring. But to love is to always care; does this mean that those who love and care, and devote their lives to someone or something are meant to lead the most tragic and painful lives? ? Possibly. Scott woke up a week later. He had been in and out a lot but up until this point, he had never been fully aware. Slowly his eyes adjust to the light and move to scan the room. Deborah Pierce stands above him, a look of hope in her eyes. Scott smiles slightly, his face badly singed. “Mom” I had? I must have had an accident.” Scott manages to speak, his voice raspy and weak. Deborah leans down and gives him a gentle hug, a hug with enough love to make the sun rise and set for the next millennia. “Thank God, you’re alright, Scott. We’ve been so worried.” Deborah squeezes out between sobs, Scott smiles, resting his head back on the pillow. He closes his eyes slowly, seemingly at peace. “Where’s Alicia?” Scott’s voice lifts again and everyone freezes. Deborah slowly lifts her head to stare into her son’s eyes. She’s speechless. “I want to see her. Is she here?” Silence. Twisted cruelty. Scott was forced to witness and live through the deaths of his entire family; his wife, his daughter and his son. Now, through psychological defense, he’s going to be forced to live through it again. But what should they do? Should they simply tell him what has happened? Should they allow him to know the facts of what transpired or should they wait; should they let him recuperate first? It’s painful enough knowing what has happened; having to look into this man’s eyes and tell him that everything he knew and loved so dearly for so long has been taken away from him ? that would be a Hell only lesser than the Hell that Scott must face alone when he hears the news. Deborah turns to her husband, speechless. Mark takes her in his arms and looks at Scott. “Scott. Maverick is here” and ? he wants to talk to you.” Scott is confused, his eyes following his Mother and Father as they walk towards the door of the hospital room. “Why won’t you tell me where Alicia is” Why? what has happened? Was she in the accident too?” Scott’s mind races, his emotions hitting new highs as his parents simply walk from the room, “DAMNIT! TELL ME WHERE MY WIFE IS!” Mmmm, fate you are truly the concubine of Lucifer himself. Scott rests back in the bed, his body not strong enough to keep up with his emotions. The door remains open after they have left. Voices are heard in the hall before Maverick walks into the room and closes the door. He walks to Scott’s bedside and sits on the edge of the bed, speaking softly, “How’re you feeling, old man?” “Like shit. Where’s Alicia, Maverick” Scott is to the point, although he’s beginning to feel the consequences of being so active, so quickly. Scott rests his head back on the pillow, his mind racing as his body comes to a screeching halt. Maverick takes Scott’s hand in his and holds it tight before locking eyes with his long time friend. “Scott, your parents have asked me to come in here and tell you what I’m about to tell you. We were afraid that you’d not remember the incident” Scott, you’ve been through a lot.” Scott’s eyes begin to close slightly but then open fully again. “Someone broke into your home” and assaulted your family.” Scott’s eyes grow wide again, adrenaline pumping and replenishing his body. “There were shots fired” and the home caught fire.” Scott interrupts, either to protect himself or out of shear hope, “Jessie and Brittany. Alicia. They made it out” they’re in the hospital too?” Scott speaks as if coming to a realization; if only it were so. Maverick glances down and speaks, unable to look his friend in the eyes any longer. “You were the only person to make it out of the house.” Silence is wondrous. Like the calm before the storm. The sound of the trigger being pulled before the bullet fires. The sound of a fire before it gets out of control. Like a battlefield the day before war is declared. A heart before it stops beating. ? Scott’s stopped beating when I told him that. Scott closes his eyes and slams a fist against the side of Maverick’s face. Reno hits the ground, surprise overcoming his face. “YOU FUCKING LIE!” Scott erupts, screaming at the top of his lungs. Reno pushes himself up slowly, his face turning a different shade. “What sick minded son of a bitch would tell someone that!” Tell me where the hell my wife and children ? then get the hell out of here!” Classic denial. Well, violent denial. Reno stands back, watching his friend as he erupts and argues the truth. “WELL!”” The door opens and Kyle walks in, his face showing an expression of true concern. Crying can be heard from the hallway. Kyle speaks quietly, trying to speak to his brother, “Scott, calm down.” Kyle insists, speaking to his brother. “He’s no lying to you.” “HE IS! And now you are! Kyle, this is not funny! I want to see my wife! I want to hold my son! My daughter! Where are they!”” Scott demands, his eyes searching the room. A nurse comes into the room, needle in hand. She presses the tip again the IV going into Scott and smiles down at him, his mind racing even more. “This will help you relax, Scott.” Reno states as he leans down, speaking to him. “Scott. I’m sorry” so very sorry that this happened. ? Scott clenches his eyes shut, tears welling up. He grabs Reno by the arm and? “Maverick.” The Echelon Tower, New York ? Reno’s office. June 07, 2004 ? Approximately 10:00 pm Maverick snaps out of his state and shakes his head. He sits in his chair, facing the large window that acts as one of the walls of his office. Outside, New York City can be seen in all its glory. Reno turns in the chair; he’s dressed in a mandarin dress shirt, dyed black, and a pair of dress pants. His hair hangs loosely about his shoulders. Standing at the door is John Zero. “Mav, everything alright?” John questions the blank expression and the delay in Maverick’s reaction. Maverick nods slowly and motions towards one of the comfortable leather recliners on the opposite side of Reno’s desk. John walks in and takes a seat, settling in. Reno’s voice is calm and collected; you’d never know what he was just thinking. “I’m fine, John. How about you” How’s Victoria?” “She’s fine, Maverick. As fine as she can be, that is.” John responds, his mind reeling as Victoria once again dominates. “But I came to speak on another topic, Mav. Elite. It seems that we never get rid of those who oppose us.” “Mmm, this is true, John.” Maverick smiles and picks up two glasses. He drops a few cubes of ice into the glasses and picks up a bottle of scotch, pouring a glass for both of them, speaking as he does so, “But they are simply a nuisance. Nothing that we can’t deal with” their reach is only inside the arena.” John speaks again, his tone casual, “Have you heard that Soma has returned?” Maverick nods, swirling the scotch in his glass around, “He stands against us” with Sabbot at his side.” “I’ve heard this rumors but only until now knew of their merit. Two men such as these stand against us, John” they could be a worth adversary.” John nods and reaches in to pick up his glass. “But do not fear too much. Sage Sabbot is as weak now as before; he can and will be taken care of if need be. Soma is a special case due to certain circumstances that I’m sure you’re aware.” “Then we must take special measures to rid ourselves of this proverbial thorn.” John smiles midway through, his tone continuing without much change. “Any ideas what these measures might be?” Reno nods slightly and motions to an opened package in the floor. “The Magnoris and the Temporal Manifest. Two books of arcane origin. Let’s just say that I plan to find and play with every weakness Soma has. When we are done with him, John, he will not be worth the dirt from which he came. Reno smiles and leans back in his chair, John taking the lead in this conversation. John nods and picks up his glass. “A toast, Maverick?” Reno nods and lifts his glass as well, holding the two together. “To the health of our enemy’s enemies.” “Kanpai, John. Kanpai.”Reno spouts off. The scene fades to black with the clink of their two glasses. I’m Coming For You
