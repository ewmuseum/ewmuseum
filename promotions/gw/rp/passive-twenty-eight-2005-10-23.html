Passive (Twenty-Eight)

23 October 2005

Date: 10/23/05 Prelude: The Perfect Storm ---- CRACK A flash of light ignites the darkened sky, shaking the earth with it's might. Raindrops the size of dimes continued to drop from the sky, bouncing off the sheet metal surroundings of the Chrysler Crossfire. From inside the Crossfire, it sounded almost is if it were under heavy machine gun fire. Unfortunately this wasn't some human who could stop at any time, it was mother nature at its finest. The pitch black vehicle rounded the corner, it's bright headlights tearing through the darkness and the terrential downpour from the heavens. Zenith Manor North is what the gate read. Hessitating for a bit, the gates opened for the Crossfire slowly. The sensors were rampaged by violent winds and ambushed by the near hurricane conditions. The Windy City... the old cliche never fit better as it did at this exact moment in time. The black luxury car made it's climb towards the three story mansion the laid behind the beautiful landscape. Almost hauting the woods seemed at this time of night. Another blast from above scared a small raccoon that stood on the side of the road, forcing it to retreat back into it's sanctuary. The Chrysler plodded on through the night... and almost zero visibility as broke out of the woods and into a opening which showed the three story mansion in all its glory. Slowly it came to a halt as it rounded a circle path that lied in front of the massive structure. CRACK You could feel the foundation of the manor tremble under the heavens wrath. Reluctantly the driver's side door of the Crossfire opened, and a black open heel shoe clicked on the wet concrete. The form of an umbrella bursted out of the door, and slowly she followed behind. Slamming the door shut behind her, the woman looked towards the entrance with anticipation... 'I hate this weather...' She muttered under he breath as walked up the concrete steps towards the manor. Wearing a pair of black designer jeans and black baby doll Tee which read 'Killswitch Engage' in white text over a blue flower convered in stick pins, she didn't show a hint of the storm. Only a couple drops of splatter showed on the black leather bomber jacket she wore over the ensamble. Reaching up and pulling back on the umbrella, it closed as she made her way under the ledge covered the glass entrance to the lobby. Grabbing the gold door handle, she pulled back and entered the luxurious establishment she was quite proud to call home. A familiar man appeared in her creulean blue eyes as she escaped the storm... 'Welcome back Vivica,' Marques, one of the housekeepers spoke as he walked into the room from the attendants wing of the manor, 'Is there anything you need tonight?' 'Yeah...' Vivica said sighing as she grew comfortable in her dry surroundings, 'But it's something you can't help me with.' ---- Chapter One: Scars Run Deep ---- CRACK The light flashed across the room, where a man sat alone in the darkness. Dim lights flickered in response to the apparent power surged caused by the bolt of lightning. His eyes navigated the darkened room, staring out of the force of nature that knocked on his doorstep. After a life of living in the Windy City you'd think these storms would become mundane... but to Christian Zenith, they were a thing of beauty. Their force and power are far beyond that of any mortal, who all run in fear of its might. Artistic and wreckless, each one was it's own beast. Beating off the window like a war drum, Zenith's cadet blue eyes pulled away from it's grace and concentrated once more on the task at hand. He stared coldly across the nightstand, reaching out for a small metallic object which glistened through the dark underneath the dim lights. Grasping it he looked down at the throwing knife he now held in his hand, running his thumb across the blade to check the sharpness. CRACK Illuminating the room once again... a series of scars appeared on Zenith's right leg just below the seem of his boxer shorts. They appeared to be notches, four vertical lines with a diagonal slash through them. 5 sets there were with 2 extras by themselves below, embrossed above the skin in an off-colored scar. Christian laid his hand with the throwing knife on his leg, staring down at the scars through the darkness... each one with its own purpose. A reminder of the past... reminders of things most of the time he'd rather not admit to. Each individual memory reinacted briefly in his head as he counted them one by one. With a deep sigh he lifted up the knife, looking calmly down at his leg before speaking aloud. 'Twenty-Eight' With those words he buried the blade of the knife into his leg, parrellel with the two notches on their own. Pulling back on the knife, a wound began to open up and the blood tricked out of the slice. Not a single hint of pain crossed his face as he watched with what appeared at first to be a sickening fixation. Finished with the cut, he slowly pulled the blade out of his skin... covered in red. Looking down at twenty-eight, Christian dropped the knife back onto the nightstand. Reaching next to him on the bed, he grabbed a gauze covering and a roll of medical tape. He quickly treated his wound. Placing the gauze over the cut and wrapping the medical tape around his leg to keep it secure. Another notch on the list, another lesson learned. They say you learn something everyday... something are just more painful than others. Getting up off the chair, Christian grabbed a pair of black slacks that laid on the bed. Quickly he put them back on, one leg at a time like everyone else... whether he liked it or not. With the sound of a zipped he was set, but thorougly unprepared for the bang of his door hitting the wall. Looking back quickly, he saw Vivica J. Valentine shut the door behind her. Obviously frusterated by the events that occured earlier in the day. Christian might have found the perfect storm intriging, but to Vivica Valentine, it was just another fucking annoyance to go along with her day. 'She's not there.' Vivica said to Christian as she hung her black leather bomber jacket on the coatrack just inside the master bedroom, 'She hasn't been there either, none of her things have even been touched in the compound. I'm sure no one has even been in the sanctuary for weeks. This isn't right Christian, it just isn't normal.' 'Either is being a vampire...' Christian responded to Vivica, flustered at her inability to find anything productive on Shadow's wearabouts. It became quite obvious to Christian that when Shadow didn't want to be found, she wouldn't be. 'Aren't you the slightest bit worried? She's you're friend too you know.' Vivica said as she sat down on the master bed, 'No one has seen her in two weeks now, not a single person. Just because she's a vampire doesn't mean something couldn't have happened to her. We're her friends Christian, she depends on us to help her. What are we doing though? Not a damn thing. What if someone got ahold of her? Like a vampire hunter or something? Those exist right? I mean it could fucking happen you know.' 'She wants to be alone Vivica, is that so hard to understand?' Zenith reponded, hoping to end Vivica's attempted guilt trip. 'She had her time alone... not showing up to an event is not like her at all.' Vivica responded, 'Even you know that. Either something has happened to her or Ambrosi is hiding something. It's pretty apparent by last week that Emoticon don't know what's going on. What if she doesn't show up again at Dark Rituals? Are we just going to sit around and do nothing about it until someone finds her ashes somewhere in the Czech Republic?' 'We have more important things to worry about at Dark Rituals than Lia...' Zenith looked over at Vivica, trying to calm her down, 'She's a big girl, she can handle herself. It's not like she got hit by a car or anything like that. Shadow lets me care about myself, and I let her handle her own business and I'm fine with that. What do you want me to do about it? Burst into The McFarlane Estate and interrogate Virgil at flamethrower point?' Vivica's reaction was completely expected, Christian had just given her an idea. That was never a good thing. As much as he wanted to do his own thing and worry about turning Anthony James into The Human Torch, he couldn't shake a sneaking suspicion about Lia either. It was true it wasn't like her to just up and not show up. Christian Zenith got up off his chair and walked towards the window. Looking down two stories below, he watched as the rain battered the fall leaves covering the ground. The storm calmed him. CRACK The lightning flared and Zenith looked out into the woods that lead almost as far as the eye could see. Behind him Vivica waited impatiently as he thought to himself. The scar on the back of Christian's shoulder quickly reminded Vivica of the last time she was so frusterated trying to find someone. That time it was for a completely different reason. It wasn't to find someone who she cared about... but to find someone who ended the life of someone she cared about. It was hard to believe that it had almost been two years ago, but life had changed so much since then... ---- Chapter Two: Memoirs Of Former Glory (Apocalypse Please) ---- Almost Two Years Ago... The moon hangs high over the night sky in Chicago, Illinois. The clock strikes midnight, the church bells swing back and forth singing their melodic song over the third largest city in the United States. Thunder rumbling could be heard in the distance... but there was not a cloud in the sky. Down below, on the streets, a rumbling could be felt. The rumble grew louder, like an oncoming stampede. Excitement filled the air, the word was out, anticipation was enough to make a grown man bow to his knees and beg. Suddenly, they came from every direction. An Urban stampede, hundreds of young men and women stormed the streets in a mad sprint. 'HE'S BACK! HE'S ALIVE!' joyful exclaimations could be heard in the midst of the pack. The tidal wave of humanity swarmed a nearby abandoned warehouse like a pack of jackals on a fresh kill. People pushed and shoved to fit through the two entrances on opposite sides of the building. The inside of the warehouse was dark and dank, shattered glass littered the floors like a carpet being crushed under the feet of the South Side Riot. The crowd huddled close to a massive stage-like structure made out of old used crates. The vermin scattered in fear for their lives, some would never get the chance to escape, being crushed under the feet of an unknowing soul. Masses continued to pack the warehouse just to get a glimpse of greatness. It was then it started. The sea of people erupted into a roar that could be heard across Lake Superior that night when they heard the sound of 'O Fortuna' through a series of large impromptu speakers strung about the facility. Although a vast majority of the crowd didn't know a lick of German, chants of 'Au’er Uns! Au’er Uns!' quickly grew contagious. Most of the people didn't have a clue what they were saying, but it really didn't matter. Two spotlights from the back 2nd floor balcony of the warehouse popped on pointing directly at the structure of crates. Suddenly two black banners fell from the ceiling, each sporting a white circle at the bottom, which contained a black 'Z' intertwined with a silver upside down crucifix. The mass of human emotion tried to shove their way up to the front, showing a force of loyalty that hadn't been seen in Chicago since Prohibition. A metal lifting garage door began to open behind the crate structure, much to the delight of the congregation. An abandoned parking lot was on the other side of the door when it opened. The lights from the parking lot silhouetted 4 people as they approached the warehouse. Slowly, the 4 made their way into the warehouse side by side to the deafening roar of their over joyed audience, surrounded only by their visible breath in the cold January air. The light shined down on a man, his black hair partially covered his cadet blue eyes, which burned brilliantly under the heat. His emotions were cold and understated as he acknowledged his crowd with a single right fist high into the air. The crowd repeated, all thrusting their fists into the air in approval. He wore a black three piece custom fitted black suit. On the jacket two gold buttons on each side were laced with a 14k gold chain, underneath the jacket was a black suit vest. A white dress shirt, black tie, and black slacks completed the outfit. Christian Zenith lowered his right arm holding his shoulder with his left arm, in pain... but his face stayed strong, bold, and intense. He looked to his right, where Vivica J. Valentine was by his side. She nodded with a little smile on her face. She pulled a piece of her blonde hair out of her face, you could see the adrenaline rush pumping through her veins. She loved being on stage, to her there was no greater rush than being in front of a crowd. She sported a black leather bomber jacket over a Black tube top, and a pair of black bondage pants from a local Hot Topic. Demitri and Jason looked into the crowd on each side of Christian and Vivica, dawned in all black. Jason LeMaster smiled a little bit back at the crowd whose roars overcame the blasts of 'O Fortuna' which played amazingly well for how old the former intercom speakers were. Demitri stood tall and firm, like a brick wall, looking back at the crowd. He slowly looked over to his left back at Christian and Vivica. Zenith put his arms in the air... 'Stille!' Christian shouted across the sea of man that stood infront of him. The loyal hoard of followers who grew quiet at the command. 'I have gone through more trials and tribulations than their so called hell, and I have returned as an immortal! As I laid on my back on the unforgiving streets of Chicago... I looked up at the coward who dare try to strike me down in vein. I saw no other than Lady Death... Die Erntemaschine, the reaper of souls. Through her greatest efforts, I stand here before you today not as a dead man... but as a man reborn! Like a pheonix rising through the ashes I have returned to all of you.' Christian Zenith stated to the roars of approval from the crowd. 'No man nor God may claim my head nor my enduring soul... and Die Erntemaschine will be punished for her sins against humanity. The lady reaper means something different to each of us. To some she stole our family, to others she had stolen loved ones and ripped apart the lives we used to know and love without as much as a second thought. With such cowardice she rampaged our lives, not even brave enough to show her face to those she claimed as her victims. Now is not a time for mourning... now is a time for vindication. Now is a time to stand up as one and make the lady reaper repent for her sins!' The crowd cheers in agreement with Zenith as he looks over his followers. 'Stand with me Chicago, and follow my lead as we stand defiantly in the face of lady death. No longer will you hide in the shadows in cowardice. No longer will you take the lives of our loved ones and feed upon our misery. No longer will you be able to hide behind the facade of Die Erntemaschine. Many of you know her as the bitch who nearly ruined your life...' Zenith paused, 'I know her as the bitch the ruined my family. Take heed to these words as I speak to you today, lady death is Tera Elise Zenith... Die Erntemaschine is of my own blood. It seems there may only be one Zenith standing in this world when the dust clears. Unfortunately for big sister... I am the alpha male, and now WE SEEK VENGEANCE!' The roars of the crowd quickly turned into screams of terror as gunfire echoed through the warehouse. Christian Zenith looked around in confusing as his followers rushed for the exits. Hysteria and chaos consumed the room as people ran for their lives from the group of hooded individuals who continued to fire into the air. The four person army atop the stage of crates jumped off onto the floor, where the congregation continued to scatter like cochroaches under an apartment light. Screams and terror consumed Christian's surroundings as he glared at 5 men wearing ski masks standing in the middle of the ruckus, enjoying every minute of it. As the room cleared the men soon noticed the foursome that later became known as The Hunting Grounds standing before them. 'What's the matter cracker?' The man said pointing his gun sideways towards Christian Zenith and his entorage, 'Do you want to die tonight?' Zenith reached for his gun and the other 4 men pointed their guns at Vivica, Demitri, and Jason. Leaving them surrounded, and doomed to certain death if they tried to retaliate. Much to Zenith's shagrin... he was helpless. If was a lose/lose situation, and he had much more on his plate than to get back at 5 thugs with guns. 'Does this look like the north side to you?' The man said, 'Get the fuck out of here you piece of trash. Don't you know who this belongs to cracker?!' 'Yeah I do...' Christian Zenith said pulling his hand out from inside his jacket to show he wasn't going to grab his gun, 'Do me a favor will you jackass? When you see Anthony James, tell him after I kill Die Erntemaschine that he better not go anywhere alone. Cause if he does, and I find him... his ghetto ass is going to be next to her.' 'Get the fuck out of here!' A man in a mask yelled as he smacked Jason LeMaster with his gun. Vivica J. Valentine sneered but Christian Zenith held her back as she tried to get him back. 'Take that damn traitor and that whore with you, we're trying to clean up the South Side.' Demitri Miasnikov helped the bleeding Jason LeMaster back up to his feet, and with a lasting glare... Zenith and Valentine lead the way out of the warehouse. Neither Christian nor Vivica would forget that night... it was the night that would change their lives forever. The next morning they took off for the road, Christian left his vice president in charge for an extended period of time but never let the public know. They would travel the country looking for Tera, and finally found her in New Mexico outside of Las Cruces. They would bring Tera to justice to meet her fate. Anthony James luckily never did meet Christian Zenith, until now. It was about much more than the World Championship or some petty grudge. It was an insult to his pride as a man and a leader on the line. It was the final crusade of the man known as The Midnight Messiah. ---- Chapter Three: The Estate Of Affairs ---- Tick... Tock... Tick... Tock... Tick... Tock... The clock slowly ticked the time away, it was almost 10:30pm and Alexis couldn't wait for the vampires to awake. As unsure as she may have been about Lia and Virgil, boredom had taken its toll and she'd love to talk to just about anyone at this point in time. Nothing good was ever on television and the phone calls had stopped coming since Lia had taken off. Virgil just didn't seem the same either since Lia didn't show up at Evolution. He seemed even more irritable than normal... so Alexis paid extra close attention to everything she said. Her eyes watched as the second hand moved melodically around the dial... 'Twenty-five, twenty-six, twenty-seven, twenty-eight...' Alexis drummed her fingers on her desk as she counted along with the clock. Suddenly, the door to the McFarlane Estate opened and she almost jumped out of her chair. Was it Lia? Did she finally come home? Or maybe Dawn was finally back from her night on the town? 'Hey Alexis.' Vivica J. Valentine said as she followed Christian Zenith into the room, who looked around suspiciously at the estate almost completely covered in black drapes. It was a house for vampires alright... not even an ounce of daylight could make it into the room. 'Vivica!' Alexis Key responded with an enegetic smile as Vivica approached her desk, 'This must be Christian! I've heard Dawn and the others talk about you, it's nice to meet you. What brings you here tonight? This You did know that Dawn stepped out for the evening, right? No one is here except for me and a few others. You can stay and keep me company if you'd like until Dawn gets back though... if gets kind of boring around this place when no one around. ' 'I'm not here to see Dawn...' Vivica says to Alexis, who seems to be overly bubbly on this day because of her complete lack of human activity, 'I'm here to see Lia, is she around?' Alexis' cheery disposition quickly grew to grim disappointment. She didn't know exactly what to tell Vivica about Lia... or Zenith either for that matter. If she told them she wasn't there, they might get worried or upset. Then again she couldn't lie to them... she hadn't see Lia in weeks. 'She's not here...' Alexis said reluctantly, 'I don't know where she is... she stepped out quite a while ago and just never came back.' It didn't take much for Christian Zenith to notice the reluctancy in Alexis' voice. Either she was scared of the consequences or she was hiding something. But.. why would she hide where Lia is from them? They were her friends... right? 'Where is she?' Christian Zenith sternly asked Alexis, who looked up in confusion. 'I just told you...' 'Tell me where she is Alexis, are you hiding something from me?' Christian's eyes looked down at Alexis who looked around the room, trying to take the pressure off of her and try to get Zenith's attention on something else. 'I swear to God...' 'You swear to God?' Christian Zenith closed in on Alexis who got out of her chair and backed up against the wall in fear. Slowly Christian walked over towards Alexis who had her back to the wall... with no place to go. Vivica tried to turn away as Christian moved in closer. She didn't agree 100% on what Christian was doing, but usually he was right about these things. She did respond like she had something to hide afterall. Instead of being forced to watch Vivica had broken into the habit over the years of acting like she didn't notice. Alexis took a big gulp as she looked into Zenith's cadet blue eyes in terror. 'Yes... I do.' Alexis responded after a short stutter. 'As far as I'm concerened...' Zenith looked almost as if he was staring into Alexis' soul, 'I am God.' 'Don't flatter yourself...' A voice spoke from behind Christian and Vivica. The sudden involvement made Alexis breathe a sigh of releif. Zenith looked around and saw Virgil Ambrosi standing right behind him. Their eyes locked for what seemed to be hours before Virgil finall spoke again, 'She don't know any more than you do Zenith so leave the girl alone.' 'Then I'll take it that you do.' Christian Zenith responded looking into the eyes of the vampire as he turned around, letting Alexis crawl away and out of the room. 'I don't.' Virgil responded, 'Lia left here a couple weeks ago, I fully expected her to be at Evolution. I was just as surprised as you were when she didn't show up.' 'Aren't you concerned?' Vivica chimed in from behind Virgil who shot a quick glare back at her. 'To be honest with you, a little bit.' Virgil answered before looking back at Christian Zenith, 'But I don't seem to be pinning girls who don't know a damn thing about the situation against walls. The exit is behind you, leave... Dawn isn't here.' 'Can we help you?' Vivica asked, 'We have tons of money, and we can get our hands on just about anything we wanted to if you needed it.' 'I don't need your charity.' Virgil answered, 'I have faith that Lia is going to show up when she's ready. If it's any consolation to the two of you, I'll let you both know the second I find out anything about where she is or what she's doing.' 'Thank you.' Christian Zenith looked back at Ambrosi with an icy stare. He knew that Virgil didn't know anything... he was telling the truth. If Virgil was hiding Shadow he's confident enough in himself that he didn't think Christian could get past him or anyone else to get to her. He would've told him. With that in mind, Christian walked around Ambrosi towards Vivica who waited on the far side of the room near the door they came in. 'Oh and one last thing...' Vivica stated before she walked out the door, 'I hope you kick Danielle Northsky's ass at Dark Rituals, that bitch annoys me to no end.' ---- Prologue: Twenty-Eight ---- Christian Zenith and Vivica J. Valentine walked out the door towards the exit of the McFarlane Estate. The black curtains lining the windows actually gave Zenith a comfortable feeling of home, he could get used to a place like this... give or take the headstrong vampires that seem to be lurking the corridors. On the way out the exit... Christian Zenith took a look at the clock the hung on the wall, that day had gone by faster than expected. 10:28pm The number immediately registered in Christian Zenith's mind. His mind flashed back to the Zenith Manor, as he reached down and rubbed the gauze and tape that still remained wrapped around his right leg. Twenty-Eight: Losing Lia when she quite possibly needed him most.
