Instillment of Fear

23 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 23rd/04 - HALLIDAY PUBLIC PARK: DALLAS, TEXAS - .:A woman is speed walking through the park. The night is very humid, as the trees and plants surrounding the area sit still, due to there not being a breeze to be found in any close parameters. The woman is wearing a set of head phones, and is clad in neon, sequin-matching spandex. As the woman power walks along through the park, she reaches a bench:. WOMAN: AHHH!! .:The woman lets out a blood-curdling shriek into the sticky, humid Dallas air, as when she reaches the bench, we see that a man is sitting there with his legs crossed. The man is clad in an all-black outfit, and appears to be smoking a cigarette. The man has his head slightly tilted downwards as he puffs on his cigarette, and continues to puff away as he speaks:. MAN: What happens to be the matter my darling? Did I spook you? .:The power-walking woman is now breathing very heavily at the spook from this man loitering on a bench in the park in the middle of the night. The woman holds her hand to her heaving chest and tries to choke out a word or two:. WOMAN: I, I, I- was....just, I mean, I'm-.... .:The man finally chuckles to himself, and leans back, all the while keeping the smoke in his mouth. Once the man is leaning back, arms up on the bench, we see under the sheath of cigarette smoke that the man is Takahashi Jetui. Takahashi continues to chuckle to himself while removing the cigarette from his mouth:. TAKAHASHI: Something seems to have your tongue my lady, why don't you let me reach down there, and check out the situation? .:Takahashi takes one last drag of his cigarette, and flicks the remaining tobacco onto the ground, before standing up and reaching for the woman:. WOMAN: AHHHHH! OH MY GOD! .:Takahashi grabs the woman by her shoulders as she frantically struggles to retrieve something from the fanny pack connected around her waist. Takahashi moves with the woman, causing her to stumble backwards and fall to the ground:. SMASH! .:The woman's walkman falls from her side and hits the ground, shattering into several pieces, as Takahashi continues to persue her. Takahashi is slowly moving towards the woman, almost stalking her as she crawls backwards on the pavement:. TAKAHASHI: Don't be scared, I don't want to hurt you, do you think I want to hurt you? WOMAN: P- please, d- don't hurt me, I'll give you my things, just PLEASE don't hurt me! TAKAHASHI: Why do I scare you? I mean, sure, I've done some things in my life that, well, many people wouldn't very well agree with, but you have to understand, it's all out of the good of fun. Is it my face paint that scares you? WOMAN: I..... I don't know (shrieking and sobbing)! .:The woman has tears running down her face, and the few street lights in the park cause the tears on the woman's cheek to glisten. Takahashi crouches down beside the woman and begins to feel up her leg. Takahashi takes in a deep breath of appeasement as he does this action:. TAKAHASHI: Mmm, I feel the terror that runs inside of you, and to be quite honest, I am loving every minute of it. As your heart races faster, mine tries to keep up, as the adrenaline pumps through my veins. You are frightened, and it is not because of my face paint, you are scared because of the threat I am posing on you right now. It is not that I am an infamous murderer, in fact you only know me as a stranger, yet when I first moved towards you, you screamed. I did not pull out a weapon, nor were my movements necessarily threatening, yet you still felt... COMPELLED to react in a scared manor. .:The woman begins to cry even harder as the minutes go by with Takahashi crouching in front of her, rubbing her leg as she sits on the ground, seemingly fearing for her own well-being:. TAKAHASHI: But that is what I am all about, my dear. You feel it, others have felt it, and I know that if Jesse Jamester has felt the fear in his heart when looking me dead in the eye... wellhe will soon have reason to fear me. Soon, Jamester will be just like you, my dear, he will tremble at the touch of my fingers, he will cringe at the thought of what I can do to him. Soon enough, after I put the man through three stages of Japanese Death Match hell, he will have reason to tremble in fear in my presence. .:Takahashi ceases rubbing the woman's leg, and stands up from his crouching position. Takahashi then takes in a deep breath of the warm Dallas air, and brushes off his trench coat. Suddenly a man comes running up from a dark corner of the park, and stands behind the woman on the ground:. JOGGING MAN: Hey! What are doing to this woman man?! .:The man also appears to be a jogger, as he is wearing a pair of spandex biking shorts, and has a bottle of water hooked into a pouch on his hip. The jogging man appears to be apprehensive about approaching Takahashi, but tries to help the woman up while confronting the woman's would-be attacker. Takahashi stand before the two joggers in a fairly calm manor all the while:. TAKAHASHI: I'm simply have a natural conversation with one of my fellow human beings. Does this pose a problem, super hero? Or are you just looking for an innocent one night stand on this bomby night? JOGGINNG MAN: Hey, why don't you just get the fuck out of here. You have obviously already frightened this woman! TAKAHASHI: So be it. .:Takahashi calmly turns around with his hands in his coat pockets, and walks off into the humid Dallas night, with the moonlight beaming down from the skies. This leaves the two joggers confused, standing in a Dallas park in the middle of the night, scratching their heads, as it would seem an absolute positive that one of the two was going to be attacked by the face-painted stranger they had just encountered:.
