Red Eyes!

7 August 2005

Date: 8 - 7 - 2005 Thursday March 12th, 1975 5:30 PM at night? “Huh?” Says a young girl. Her eyes were bright. Forcing your vision to be blurry and hard to read. As though a light was shining through them like it was something you couldn’t see through. Her hair was the same way, wavy with every string. Bouncy as the wind blew through it. She was a queen so to speak and she knew it. “I said can you please move.” Says a very rough voice fellow who stands there right in front of her with a bag in his hand. His body language told exactly who and what he was. He was a man who was barely making it in the world. Dressing with a suit with holes in the sides of the jacket, infesting with a smell that would even kill the worst fart that was let out. It was as if a rat had passed on and fell in the pocket of the suit and just rotted away for years. “Well I beg your patron. I was walking here first so you please move.” Says the strong looking woman with the wavy hair that bounces in the wind. “No you move.” Response the man with an even stronger voice. “No you move NOW!” screaming out at the top of her small little lungs. The woman was seemingly not going to take anymore, raising her hand and extending it towards the gentleman, knocking him right across the shoulder that to the eye was hit with such baby force you could have had a little twelve year old to hit harder. “Ouch?” Laughing with passion on his face. “Oh you think that’s funny huh?” Anger pouring out of the woman as she watches on. “Yes miss but next time, please hit me so I can just move in laughter next time?” Patting the woman on the shoulder before continuing. “Yes please.” Getting a small grin on her face as well, she just shakes her head from side to side, regretting what she had done just seconds ago. Tossing her hair out of her face once again, batting another smile towards him before she brought out her hand. “I’m Sara.” Sending a greeting towards him. “I’m Bobby.” Responding with a handshake of his own before losing the grin and trading it in for a small sexy lift of the lip. It was weird. Anger, hate, and screaming was quickly turning around to be happy, pleasant, and gift full for even them. ************************************* Monday April 20th, 1990 12:34 PM in the afternoon? “Mommy?” “Yes?” “I need you?” The sounds of a beautiful young woman, about six years old came to life. Wearing a pretty blue outfit with flowers all of it with her hair just a little blonde then anything. Her eyes sparkling with a dance that not even an Indian could do. The mother got up off the chair, racing into the other room to find something I bet she would never want to find. “What in the heck happen here?” Screaming as if something was wrong and, it was wrong. “I don’t know?”, frowns that little girl like she was still the princess. Standing with her was a very evil little child. Eyes glossed over with a caressing red fade look. Hair waving up and over, almost in a Mohawk look. Clothing hanging half way on as he looks right at the little girl and mother’s way. “He did it?”, pointing towards the devil’s evil son. “How dare you try to blame this on him. He’s not even over here.” Says the mother, grabbing the girl away from the stain that’s on the floor. “But he did?”, crying to her as if it was life and death. The little girl looks back, looking right at the boy. Her eyes got bigger as she went through the set of door, disappearing from anyone’s eyes but the mother’s. *********************** Tuesday May 30th, 1997 7:00 PM at night? “Somebody help me!” The tree’s were speaking with every little bit of wind that was racing through them. Crossing the wood with each powerful blow. Arching them to the point of breaking. The dirt, flinging upwards in the air into little ground tornado’s, crossing the trail that the young girl was walking. It was the same girl that you saw at six years old. Yet now she was a little more grown up. Long straight blonde hair that was pouring down every inch of her back. Gathering dirt and grass that was blowing towards her with her step. Walking a little more, trying to take little baby steps before the unthinkable happened. “AHHHHH!” Screaming the little girl. A broken tree branch that had been covered by leaves and dirt was sticking up a little bit, causing her to trip all the way down a long hill. Bouncing off the ground like a twig that was being restless. As she bounces down the hill, the girl’s arm smashes into a rock, breaking it cleanly at the elbow joint of the arm. “HELP ME!” Screams the girl as she grabs her arm, not able to bend it at all. Her eyes, filling up with water as she took shelter in a tree that had been already pressed down from the wind. Hiding out of sight, out from the wild that she was lost in. All she could do was cry. No one there to help her, lost from the pack that she was here with. Night was here and it was never friendly. “MRS. JOHNSON?”, screams the girl. “KATIE! SARA! ANYONE!” But as she screams for anyone, something that she hadn’t scene in a long time pops up out of the corner of her eye. Reddish, and a set at that. Closely together and very deeply looking. They were there, almost looking right at her. She screams out loud, causing the remaining birds that were there to fling up off the tree’s into the air. Scooting back and out of the tree’s cover, the red looking things came closer and closer. She kept on backing up, trickling right next to another tree, out from behind anything that could keep her from being none seeable. The red looking dots got closer to her till they began to look like something she never would of even dreamed of. “WHO’S OUT THERE?” Eyes. They did look like eyes. The form of a face came next, hair on top of the head and they kept on staring at her. The girl began to scoot a little up, trying to get closer this time to seeing who it might be. As she did, an arm grabbed her by the shoulder, causing her to jump almost up the hill she had rolled down. “Mrs. Johnson. God am I so glad to see you.” Say’s the little girl. Pressing up to her feet and grabbing the woman by the neck. “Where have you been?” Anger pouring out of the woman with her words. “Long story?”, says the girl. As she and the woman began to walk back the other way, she quickly began to turn back, looking for the pair of red eyes but all she saw was nothing. They were gone. She stopped for a moment, trying to look deeper before a voice came out of the woods. “I’ll be watching?” ******************************* Sunday August 7th, 2005 11:00 PM at night? “Come on Elia. If you can’t get your head in the game then you should just quit.” Screams an old man. Elia, you all know her. Global Wrestling’s star or hopefully as she would say. The gym was the usual. Her uncles but her uncle was no where to be in sight. Wearing her wrestling gear for the first time in a long time here, Elia squares off against one of her long time enemy’s here names, Patrick Ransom. He was tall, built to the bone and very athletic. He was the type that you wouldn’t want to be in bed with if you were Elia’s size. “I’m trying but I can’t seem to get it down.” Elia says, dropping her head for a moment. “Well I guess that losing streak that you are on isn’t coming to an end at Master of the Mat is it.” Shaking his head after that one. “Guess we better just call Fred Grest and tell him to take the win huh?” Oh that was seemingly all she needed to here. Running at Patrick with full speed, looking right at his shoulders before locking up with him. Elia, sending a hard kick to the gut of Patrick, reverses the lock in to a side headlock, twisting at his head. “That’s it, now lock in the other move.” Screams the coach. But it wasn’t going to happen. As Elia began to reverse the lock again, that left an opening for Patrick to reach down, grabbing the leg of Elia and bringing her up and over with a sidewalk slam, driving her back into the mat. “GRRRRR!” Blows out the coach as he tosses the towel he was holding in his hand. Elia slams her arm on the mat, not crying but in frustration of the failure she was having. As she did, she caught the glimpse of a man walking towards the locker room. “It couldn’t be?”, proclaims Elia for a moment. Rolling towards the ropes and flopping right out onto her feet, she begins to walk towards the door. Pressing down gently with every step from the pain she had endured from Patrick today. Turning the corner, she saw a man standing right by her locker. He wore a pair of raggedy torn clothing, dirt infested, and smelly to the core jeans and a shirt. It was a wonder at how he got in here and not scene. Elia began to creep towards him, trying not to let him hear her but it was to late. The man began to turn around, blasting out the red eyes that he wore ever since she was even born. “What the hell?” Says Elia. Her jaw at the floor and her hand trying to cover it. She couldn’t believe it. The same kid that was there with her when she was six. The man eyes that were there when she was in the words at age thirteen. And now, the same eyes return. She couldn’t move from her spot, trying to run but it was of no use. “Who are you?” Says Elia, doing the only logical thing to do. “You don’t know?”, replies the red eyes that felt as if they were covering her. Elia just shook her head in a no answer, trying to make them go away like they had in the passed. The red eyes then began to move closer, taking every step with procession and perfection. Letting tear after tear go down her face, Elia just stood there on the floor, trapped between the lockers and the wall behind her. “Why are you here?” Says Elia. “To make you see the light.” Responses the man with the eyes of the devil. “What light?”, Elia says, grabbing her mouth and holding her hands there. “The light of evil.” Evil? The man says it with passion. As if he lived the life everyday. Elia’s eyes grew bigger. Concerned at the one word that came out of his lips. Evil? That wasn’t something she wanted it seemed. Elia taking a step back and hitting the cold and bumpy wall that was behind her. “You see Elia. I know you have evil deep down inside you. Covering that heart that you claim to have loved for so long. I can see it, I can feel it, and you know it’s there.” The man says to Elia, caressing her arm with an evil stroke. “But why have you chosen me” I’m just a lonely country girl. Why have you followed me through the years?” Crying out to him for answers. “I knew you were evil before you even were born. You knew it when you saw me that day in the doctor’s office. You felt it in the woods taking over, and now were you stand you can feel the urge to cause so much trouble it’s making you scared. The man breaths towards her, breathing all the way down her neck towards her chest that was pumping extra fast right now. Elia suddenly didn’t feel fear. She felt betrayed from this all. Not from the way she made herself feel but the way she lied to herself in the passed. Looking down at the face, the red eyes mostly, Elia pressed her chest out a little more, closing her eyes as she did. “I will not listen to you anymore. I’m not evil, I’m my mother’s daughter Elia Smith. I am my father daughter and I will always live on the side of good. I wish for you to go away. God away’GO AWAY!” screams out Elia as she opens her eyes. Looking around, mostly in front of her, all Elia saw was an empty room. The man was gone. No one else was there as Elia began to step forward towards the middle of the hallway of lockers. Thank god she was alone again. Elia ran for her locker, grabbing her things that she needed and running for the door before she head a small evil laugh coming from the very back of the locker room?.or was it. “I’ll be here’I’ll always be here.” says the evil man. Elia then runs out of the locker room, towards god knows were.
