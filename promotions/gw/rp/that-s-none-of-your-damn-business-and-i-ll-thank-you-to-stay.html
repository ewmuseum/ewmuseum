That's none of your damn business and I'll thank you to stay out of my personal affairs.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Saturday Two silhouettes can be seen stepping into a large pitch black room from an illuminated hallway for a split second before blending in with the darkness blanketing the room. After a few moments a light flickers on and the large room is revealed to be practically empty, with two walls covered completely by wall to wall mirrors, and the other two being used to hold rather extensively stocked weapon racks. Several ceiling fans slowly begin whirling their way to life over the hard wood floor, which is for the most part covered in a thin firm padding that couldn’t possibly offer much more give than the floor itself. The two silhouettes that were seen entering are exposed as none other than TUE cohorts Deacon Holmes and Maverick Reno. Deacon clad in baggy black mesh shorts with an elaborate design consisting of a fading red to white spider web covering the shorts and a black widow sprawling across it, his Nike Shox Air Genocides which he endorses, and a plain white tanktop that allows his tattoo menagerie to show. Reno is clothed in heavyweight black woven karate gi pants held up by a thick deep purple belt. His upper body is covered by a plain black tank top and his hair held by in a pony tail by a thick purple rubber band. In his hand Reno clutches a black duffle bag, and allows it to dangle at the full length of it’s handle strap just inches above the ground while watching Deacon as he attempts to figure out the in wall disc changer console in a corner of the room. Shaking his head slowly with a faint smile as a result of Deacon’s bemused clamoring for buttons to press, Reno gently sets the back down against the floor and slowly unzips it as though he were a surgeon employing a scalpel in order to split a patient’s chest open. Just as the bag’s entrails are exposed the low frequency thumping rumble of powerful bass rhythmically rocks the room like a baby in it’s crib as “Flipside” by Freeway begins to blare, effectively shattering the silence of the room like a cinder block through a pane of candy glass. In the process, the loud music also manages to garner Maverick’s attention, who looks across the room toward the console at Deacon, who seems to have taken to dancing rather than stretching. Reno seemingly pays him no mind and continues on about his business as he removes a roll of black tape from the bag and proceeds to wrap it in strands around his hands and wrists. Once adequately covered, he casually sever’s the remaining strand of tape from the roll and drops it back into the back. Next he reaches back into the bag and removes a small black remote, which he aims in Deacon’s general direction and with a single press ruins his entire mood as blaring hip hop is replaced with soft classical music laden with pianos and violins. This causes Deacon to immediately direct his attention to Reno, whom he neglects to hide his dismay from as he begins staring a hole through him as he stretches. Stopping mid exercise, Maverick gives Deacon a bewildered look as though he hasn’t a clue in the world as to what the problem could possibly be before continuing on with his stretching exercise. Holmes fumes silently all the while tapping his foot in mute rage, you can just sense an f word coming on though judging by the look on his face. Just as it would seem Deacon was apt to explode Reno ceases his stretching and resumes a normal posture before asking “How can you concentrate with that loud racket on” If music soothes the savage beast, that racket must surely incite it’s rage.” Deacon scoffs at Reno’s remarks then inquires “So I guess we should just thrown on some powdered wigs and critique post modern art then shouldn’t we?” aggressively, to which Maverick simply slits his eyes before retorting “I’m merely trying to broaden your horizons Mr. Holmes. When’s the last time you read a good book?” Holmes gives Reno a look that couldn’t possibly suggest any phrase other than “bitch please” before quipping “I don’t read anything unless I’m on the cover or there’s a centerfold.” and closing the statement with his trademark mega-watt smile and an erect middle finger. Reno returns the gesture with a flat smile that soon fades before kneeling down in front of his bag and removing two black masks commonly used in fencing and tossing one Deacon’s way. This gesture spoke louder than any verbal response as Maverick fit his mask neatly over his head then nonchalantly made his way over to the weapon rack behind him and removed a six foot bo staff. Turning back around to face Deacon, Reno puts on a little exhibition of his prowess with the weapon while spinning the wooden pole fast enough for it to whistle as it cut through the air. Then, nestling it in between his shoulder and neck, Maverick extended his free hand out toward Holmes and gave him “come here” gesture before informing him “Whenever you’re ready Mr. Holmes.” calmly. Completely and utterly unimpressed, as well as still somewhat pissed off about his music being turned off, Deacon simply rolled his eyes before turning and walking to the matching weapon rack across the room. Holmes removes a six foot bo staff of his own and balances it against the wall while fitting his mask on, all the while grumbling to himself “Shit, motherfucker wanna turn off MY music when I’M tryin” ta get crunk...” Once his mask is on, Deacon takes his staff in hand and turns his attention to Reno. Holmes points the pole at him like a batter trying to intimidate a pitcher before adapting an unconventional stance that immediately shows off his lack of experience with the weapon. Both men stare each other down from across the room, their breaths beginning to slow, before simultaneously breaking out into a full on sprint toward the center as if on cue. Due to his advantage in the speed department, Deacon is able to close a little more of the gap than Reno and is able to strike first, raising his stick high overhead like some sort of caveman and leaping into the air without breaking stride. With Deacon high overhead as a result of his awesome leaping ability, in order to block his downward vertical blow Maverick takes a wide grip on his staff and raises it up over his head, extending as far as he can so as to meet the blow before it has a chance to build up much more velocity. A loud resonating clap can be heard as the two wooden poles slap together. Deacon’s momentum still carrying his staff down against Reno’s, Maverick narrows his grip and slides his hands together to the point that they’re touching while angling the pole down and to the left in order to parry Holmes? attack in that direction. Deacon staggers to the side as a result and finally regains his balance just in time to look up and find Maverick Reno’s bo staff coming crashing down directly on top of his head. At the last possible moment, Holmes proves that speed can make up for a lack of skill at just about anything by just barely managing to dodge the attack by weaving around it like a snake. Before Deacon can even mount any offense to counter Reno’s miss he’s met with a firm shot across the ribs as Maverick angles his grip and cracks Deacon across the mid-section. Holmes stands doubled over and once again regains his wits just in time to dodge an overhead slash from Maverick, but this time he avoids it by rolling backwards in order to create some distance between he and Reno. The two men briefly relax their stances before exchanging a quick nod and getting right back down to business. Maverick approaches with a vertical strike while starting up a conversation in the process. “So what is all this I here about you storming out on Rhaiza a few days ago?” he asks casually while attempting to deliver a blow that could possibly leave Deacon unconscious which he merges with a quick horizontal swing that manages to knock Reno off balance, causing him to lose control of his pole and stand holding it arms crossed at his side and off balance. “Nothin” man...” Deacon begins as shuffle steps forward raising his staff into the air in an attempt to bring it down on Reno, only to have him quickly regain his balance and apply a roof block by raising the staff horizontally over his head. Maverick immediately segues his roof block technique into a forehand diagonal strike as Holmes continues his sentence “She was just prying into my business about my parents and shit.” while switching his grip in order to fend off Maverick’s attack and stepping outside laterally to the left. Deacon displays his lightning quickness by removing his force and continuing to move around Maverick to the left then using centrifugal force to trip him up and cast him down onto his shoulder on the floor. At the instant Reno makes contact with the floor he looks up wide eyed through his mask at Holmes in surprise as a result of his maneuver. This doesn’t last long as Deacon is soon continuing his attack, slamming his stick into the ground just inches outside of where Maverick was rolling to escape, then missing by mere inches again on the other side as Reno narrowly escapes once more. Finally Maverick manages to wrap his legs around one of Deacon’s to jerk it out from under him and uses his staff to take out the other, sending Holmes to the floor as a result. Maverick then rolls backward onto his feet just in time to lock engage in another brief mask to mask stare down between he and Deacon, who’d kicked up just as he got to his feet. “I was under the impression that you two had grown rather attached to each other. Is this not so?” Reno inquires whilst quickly leveling his staff with Deacon’s head and charging forward with a barrage of attacks. Maverick’s first strike is horizontal and parallel to the ground, it cuts through the air with a sharp whistling noise punctuating the swipe that Holmes just barely manages to duck under while spinning against the grain as he slides to the right. This miss does little in the way of deterring Reno who comes right back with another horizontal swipe at Deacon’s head, this one coming quickly from the other side in a backhand type motion. “No it’s not like that...” Holmes starts while fending off Maverick’s second strike by holding his out vertically and away from his body in order to parry Reno. “We are close, but there are just certain things I don’t like to talk about.” Deacon continues while still trying to escape Maverick’s vigorous attack. Without stopping Reno fluidly shifts to an altered grip and attempts the same type maneuver but this time directed at Deacon’s legs as he sweeps across at a forty five degree angle while still somehow managing to keep up their conversation in a casual tone. “I never knew one’s parents could prove such a controversial topic. What exactly was the problem with her inquiry?” Maverick asks while recovering from his missed sweep at Holmes? legs with a powerful thrust to the chest that stops Deacon dead in his tracks momentarily after jumping over the sweep. Absorbing the blow and then spinning with a high arching shot directed at Reno’s head, Holmes replies “I don’t have any.” as Maverick narrowly dodges losing his consciousness as a result of the blow by ducking under the out of control swipe. Deacon recovers and goes right back at Reno with a vicious overhead attack after shifting his grip below the center of the staff making his swing look something like that of a caveman. Reno employs a roof block holding his staff horizontally up away from his head and repulses the attack, which only seems to give Holmes more motivation to attack as he repeats the maneuver but this time with much greater force in hopes of crushing his defense. “So are you implying that you were hatched from some sort of egg?” asks Mavericks, somewhat confused as he blocks Deacon’s second identical attack in a row. Hoping the third time will be the charm, Holmes attempts the same attack once more, adding even more power this time by leaping into the air while performing the maneuver. Deacon’s staff is met by nothing but thin air as Reno runs under the third blow. “Frankly that’s none of your damn business...” starts Holmes as both fighters quickly turn to face each other. “And I’ll thank you to stay out of my personal affairs... but I think you know what I mean.” Reno answers the question inaudibly with a simple nod before being rushed at by Deacon. Holmes spins while taking a swipe at Maverick’s head but nobody is home as Reno saw it coming and managed to dodge the attack. Countering Deacon’s attack, Maverick uses his staff to trip Holmes as he passes. Deacon loses his balance and as he hurtles toward the ground his staff is flung from his hands due to being taken off guard. Holmes crashes into the ground hard and watches for a moment as his staff rolls away across the floor before rolling over to find Reno standing over him preparing to attack. Maverick jabs at the ground repeatedly but each blow fails to hit it’s mark as Deacon continually rolls from side to side. Suddenly, Reno abruptly halts his thrusting attack as his attention seems to be drawn away from Deacon and toward the room’s entrance. “Speak of the devil...” he says while removing his mask. “Were your ears burning?” Maverick inquires, still speaking to someone out of Deacon’s view. Curious as to who Reno could possibly be talking to, Deacon rolls over onto his stomach and looks over toward the door to find Rhaiza standing there nervously giggling as Reno’s remarks not knowing quite how to respond to him. Holmes quickly hops to his feet and removes his mask as Rhaiza approaches him. Deacon looks over his shoulder to Reno who gives him a nod and remarks “Saved by the bell.” before collecting Deacon’s mask and walking over to the duffle back he’d entered with to pack them away. Holmes then turns his attention back to Rhaiza as she stops in front of him. As Deacon begins to open his mouth to ask her what she was doing there Rhaiza cuts him short with an apology. “Listen, I’m sorry about earlier...” she begins before stopping abruptly as Reno exits the room, giving them both a two finger wave as he walks past. “I know that you’ve got a lot of things that you like to keep private, but sometimes it just bothers me that there’s so much you keep from me.” she continues, looking dead into Deacon’s eyes as his chest slowly heaves and contracts as a result of sparring with Maverick. The two young lovers stand face to face in silence, with Rhaiza staring sheepishly at Deacon’s stoic facial expression. Several moments pass in utter silence before Deacon finally looks right into her eyes with a harsh facial expression and coldly says “Look, I didn’t wanna talk about this back at the house, and I definitely don’t wanna talk about this now.” With that, Holmes calmly walks right around her and toward the door but it stopped mid step by the sound of Rhaiza beginning to tear up. In his head he’d love it if he could just keep walking, but it was as if there were a thousand clamoring hands pulling him back and ripping at his insides if he resisted. Something had changed inside of him. He was beginning to have feelings, and he hated that. Two months ago he would’ve been able to walk right out of the room without even so much of a second thought. Two months ago no one else mattered, any woman he became involved with he immediately chalked up to his burgeoning fame and would discard them on a whim, but not now. The idea of leaving Rhaiza behind crying in his wake ate at him. He wanted to keep going, but he couldn’t. Walking out of the room had suddenly become as hard as walking over quicksand. Deacon grimaced as he found himself frozen in place with his back turned to his weeping girlfriend. In his nineteen years no one had ever meant anything to him. Placing value on or even trusting others had always been regarded as setting yourself up for disappointment to him, and with good reason. He’d never had anyone, and years of fending for and basically raising himself had taken it’s toll. Deacon had become cold, incapable of feeling, or so it would seem... He’d really just been hiding them all deep down inside so as to protect himself from the world. Being uncaring meant being invincible, no one can hurt you if no one can get to you. But now here he was, feeling and vulnerable as ever. He tried with all his might to just push it all aside and keep on walking, and just when it seemed like he’d finally locked his emotions up for good they all came rushing back like a tidal wave to the sound of Rhaiza’s voice. “Why are you being this way?” she cried, stopping Deacon in mid step once again. A cold chill shot down his spine and he cringed at the thought of what he was becoming. He shook his head slowly while gazing up into the sea of lethargically rotating fan blades on the ceiling. With a sigh he once again tried to continue toward the door but was stopped this time by a hand entangling itself within his own. It was soft and delicate, yet strong enough to stop him from leaving. Drawing his attention down from the ceiling, Deacon found himself looking into Rhaiza’s tear filled emerald green eyes. This would prove to be his kryptonite as he felt every wall he’d put up over the course of his nineteen years on this earth crumble into a miserable pile of debris. He felt empty inside, and could do nothing more than stare into her eyes as his wits had long left him. Rhaiza tried to muster a smile but could only manage a quickly fading flat attempt at one. She rubbed her thumb over the top of Deacon’s hand in a circular motion while wiping the tears from her eyes, her full lips quivering as she tries to speak. “You’re the best thing that ever happened to me...” she starts before being interrupted by having to fight back more tears as Deacon wipes one from her cheek with his thumb. “I just wanna know where you came from, is that so wrong?” With that, Rhaiza closes the small gap between she and Deacon by pressing her body up against his and wrapping her arms around his body. While gently caressing the curvatures of the muscles in Deacon’s back with her hands she also places her head against his chest and listens to his once rapid but now slowing heart beat. Gently wrapping his arms around her, Deacon finally finds the strength for words. “It’s ok...” he began reassuringly. “It had nothing to do with you, it was me.” he states, prompting Rhaiza’s head to lift up from his chest slightly in order for her to look into his eyes. With a mischievous smile, Deacon then continues “I tell ya what, to show how sorry I am and make up for this whole mess... I’m taking you out to dinner tonight.” Rhaiza’s eyes beam and her entire face lights up as an excited smile flashes across her face. “Really?” she asks anxiously. Deacon simply replies with a nod, causing her to become extremely excited and tighten her grip on him, pressing their bodies even closer. “Someone’s getting a little something extra for desert tonight.” she whispers in his ear seductively before once again letting her enthusiasm take hold and spouting off “I can’t believe you’re taking me to a Japanese steak house!” Deacon’s facial expression quickly becomes one of a dumbfounded nature as he cocks an eyebrow and shoots back “Japanese steak house?” as though he has no clue what she’s talking about. Rhaiza’s smile quickly fades and her voice begins to shake a bit as she replies “Yeah... you said you were going to take me to one of those restaurants where there make the food right in front of you. I thought we were going to Kyoto’s.” Deacon’s smile grows a bit wider and he appears to be relishing every moment before responding “Kyoto’s” Girl, I was talkin? about Subway!” With that he break’s their warm embrace just in time to duck a playful attempt at a slap from Rhaiza. Her next attempt is blocked and returned with a love tap from Deacon, who exclaims “Gotcha!” before turning tail and running for the door laughing hysterically with his girlfriend hot on his tracks.
