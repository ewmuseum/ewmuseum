The changing of the tides #1

16 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/16/04 Silence. I walk into the Global Wrestling head offices in silence in the natural attire a large white cow boyish styled hat that covered half my face, long white mandarin collared trench coat, black shirt with priest collar, long white suit coat and pants, and black leather boots. I walk up to the desk where a secretary is talking on a phone. She looked up at me and went back to her conversation, seeming to pay me no attention. In the old days I would have killed her on the spot for such insolence, now I stay call and politely get her attention once again. “Excuse me miss, I’m hear to see the McCoy’s. I have an appointment. Tell them a mister Leinad Soma is here to see them.” She rolls her eyes at my comment and tells the person she is talking to that they have to be put on hold. Its sad to see people in this day and age hating there job. But one must do what they have to do in order to survive nowadays. The whole world has become a jungle not like in the old days where there was a specific distinction between a jungle and proper society. But through the years the world got tougher. Through the years people became harder and had to go through harder things in their life. And with such things even those who seem to live in the shadows had to accept change and go with it. My kind especially, they came up with the idea called “The Masquerade”. Hiding ones self of what they truly are. They put themselves in clans with those vampires who had the same attributes as each other. They waged war on those who didn’t join their cause. And rhen another faction was made, a faction that vowed to not care about the Masquerade. A faction known to me only as the Black Hand. For I am old enough and powerful enough to not take any side in their mediocre games. I lived before their time and I shall continue to live well beyond it. I look again at the lady at the desk who so hates her job and I pity her’But she is only behaving how society has made her. “The McCoy’s will see you now, right this way.” She stands and begins to lead me to a boardroom. I follow her slowly, looking at the walls and seeing pictures of the champions of old. I stop and see a picture of Jean Claude Defoe. I slowly begin to walk again seeing pictures of great men like Hans Krugar, Brian Welch, Silent Wind, and Jonathan Davis. Seeing these men and the greatness that they inspired made me wish for having been able to see those times in GW. But those times would soon come back. Through the dark clouds of war I could see images of a time where GW would be even greater than it is now. The young lady finally stops and opens a door and I catch up to her and walk in. The is a table that could easily sit twelve people and at the head of it sat Bubba McCoy and to his right his daughter Christina. “Come in Mr. Soma, do have a seat.”Bubba said with a smile on his face. “Why thank you.” I say as I sit down cautiously not so much with paranoia but as a man who knows the ways of business. “Leinad on behalf of GW I want to thank you for bringing back my father. You and Sabbot did a very noble thing by helping him out of where he was.” “There is no need for thanks Christina. It was wrong for what was happening to him” so I intervened?.as well did Sabbot. “If I may ask where was he when you rescued him?” “That’s a secret between Myself, Mr. McCoy, and Sage Sabbot”.I’m sorry but I think you have the wrong idea about why I am here. I didn’t come here to have a conversation I came here to do business. Business with you Mr. McCoy and only you. I didn’t think your daughter would be present.” “WHAT!” She was infuriated I could tell but that was no matter to me. She begin to yell of how she gave me my contract she was the one to give me a chance in this federation and she is right. But everyone must start out somewhere so that they may evolve beyond those that brought them in or taught them. “I will have you know Leinad I could fire you just like that you are not that precious to be kicked out of the fed that my father started and I have worked so hard in. I’ll have you know”.” “Christina if you want to fire me do it, but as I said I’m not here to go through this right now I’m here to conduct business. Yes you brought me in and gave me a chance but since that day I have evolved into something so much more. You say you work so hard in this fed. I have news for you’so do I. If you worked so hard in this fed why is TUE running rampant, why are great men being killed in the ring” These are all questions people are looking to you for answers. Can you give them any?.I would say not.” “YOU SONOFA?” “Christina could you give me and Mr. Soma a moment alone please?” “What?” “Please just a few minutes dear.” As Christina McCoy leaves the room angrily, Bubba and I sit there in silence. For a brief moment we stare each other eye to eye. Business man to business man. I begin to speak but Bubba beats me to the punch. “She has quite a temper sometimes. She means well though. Thank you once again Mr. Soma for bringing me back to her and to this company. It’s good to be home.” I smile at the sound of hearing someone thanking me for a good deed. It was so long ago since those words “Thank you” have graced my ears and someone meant them. “Your most welcome Mr. McCoy. Please call me Leinad since YOU are my boss here.” “Well if I’m to call you Leinad, then by all means call me Bubba. I’m glad we are having this time to talk. I would like to know what your goal is in GW this time around.” “Well’first off you realize I own a large share of GW and I would like to side that with your share”.just yours not your daughter’s. I don’t really trust her much?.never have for some reason. As for my plans here I plan on becoming a champion. None like you’ve ever seen. With men like Sabbot at my side and a few others I have been meaning to talk to. My plans are to start a revolution. There are so many bad guys here and for the longest time they have had the upper hand now the tides are changing?.now the sides are becoming more equal?.men like Venneman Pendragon are coming to surface?.guys like Train, Josh, KPJ and others have come to the call and for those who have been fighting this fight for a while like Brick now have aid in this battle that has raged before my time in GW. I have been told of the days of The Nevermore’hell I almost joined them. I was apart of the disease known as TUE in the beginning. Now I realized I was wrong for a lot of things I did while I was here. And I have had many friends hurt in the time of my leave and return?.Rokku?.my dearest of enemies?.The Souls of the Black Lotus?..all great men’all the same enemy’TUE’I have come back to rid you of this problem. Politically with our shares combined we strike a formidable blow when in comes to industry?.and with my plans already in motion I shall be putting together a group like known could ever dream?.Those are my plans and goals Bubba.” Bubba sits there quietly thinking while keeping his eyes on me?.The eyes of an old warrior not yet defeated. The eyes of an old samurai dedicated to his ways and tradition. The eyes of a man who has seen the gruesome parts of war?.and the war that is about to be waged in his own company. “I was hoping your ideas were gonna be similar to mine. And they are for the most part. I was hoping you’d help get rid of the menace known as TUE so I’m glad that is what you are intending on doing. I know of some guys in this fed that may want to help you in your aid of ridding this place of TUE. I’ll talk to them and send them your way. And we also should keep in touch Leinad I see great things in your future and hopefully they will all come true.” I nod and smile I begin to stand when Bubba stops me and urges me to sit back down. I sit down in the matter that I did the first time?.cautiously “Leinad, you do realize that TUE is coming after you soon don’t you?” “Yes I do. Reno will not rest until he has my head on a platter or he is dead. And since he thinks nobody can take him down”.i’m a special case?.since I was born dead and well I like my meals extra rare’having a pulse?” “You know you will most likely lose going against him head on?” “Bubba I have laid plans within plans within plans when it comes to this subject. I understand how the world works”..i’ve been on it long enough to see the changing of the tide’this is no different. Reno is a powerful and smart man I give him that?.but I have had greater men as my main course. When the time is right that will be dealt with, that I can promise you.” “We shall see Leinad.” “That we will Mr. McCoy.” I stand and walk over to him and shake his hand and walk out the door in silence.
