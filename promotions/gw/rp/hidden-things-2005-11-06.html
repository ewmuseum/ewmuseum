Hidden things

6 November 2005

Date: 11/06/05 A few moments caught in time, a few moments that can make everything seem so real and make you want to violate that which you have sworn to be and to do. The way others treat those around them, the way they seem to think that everything is a big f'ing joke. Josh sat in a chair in his room that held all of his other things that didn't need to be mingled with Dani's yet. A TV screen was live in front of him, replaying things from Dark Rituals up in Alaska, the last show that Global had put on. First is replayed the scenes that unfolded that night. But nothing about how the 'man', Virgil Ambrosi, pinned Dani to take away her hard fought for and won Platinum Title belt. But more along the lines of how two miscreants joined together with another and formed an alliance to ruin everyone else's time that night, or at least try. Fred Grest has a history in Global of being some sort of comedy bit. A good laugh for the crowd to use after they've had to deal with some of the competitors who see this is not a game, but as a career. While there are people like Josh who like to have some fun with their work, there are things that cross the line. And seeing the replays of the event afterwards, where the privacy of not only himself, but of his girlfriend, Dani Northsky violated for a good 'laugh' was enough to make Josh forget about being the fun loving man. His girlfriend.... Maybe he was just angry at himself for failing to bring his own 'gumption' up enough to ask her what he wanted to ask her while they were up in Alaska. While they were standing there under the Aurora Borealis... when he wanted to ask her to be his wife. He had permission from the man who had been a father to her for the last few years, since she'd been found in the orphanage. Her Maternal Grandfather had told him that it was good. And then he'd given Josh the very engagement ring that had be recovered from her mother before they had been put into their final resting. He'd actually given him more. The while wedding set that Danial and Lynn Northsky had used to seal their love. Josh had originally intended to use the ring that his mother had given him, the one that she and the father he shared with Gray had used. But this was something he couldn't pass up. He watched again as the scenes of those two idiots, Jaime Dermont and Miguel Lanza went across the screen. As they were shown doing their dirty work in the production truck and then the world saw Dani's naked form in the shower... and then him coming in while she was in there. While only a few short minutes were all that were shown, it was enough... “We've got other things to worry about. Those two will get their comeuppance when it's time. In the meantime, you need to get your head on straight.” His brother, Gray had come in behind him. He'd actually been waiting for him to get there for a while. “Why do you say that? It's not like we've got anything else going on for us right now.” Gray tossed a folder down on his lap. “Wrong. We've got a match this week.” Josh didn't bother opening it. ?So?? ?So?? Gray mocked him, patronizing him. “The tag team scene here isn't healthy. It's sick, just like it is in every other damn federation out there. And our contract, as we have found out, doesn't really play well when we are split up. What's the use though? They promised that we'd get new champions soon. Started a tourney, then canceled it. We've beaten the former champions. We beaten everyone else EXCEPT these two idiots. Maybe if we take the fight to them, we can get what we deserve! We beat the last champions, and then they up and quit? And we didn't get what was ours? We should be the top, but instead they have rammed the tag team scene down the crapper.” Gray shook his head. “And this week you can think about our opponents will be thinking when they face us then.” “Who are they? Joe and Bob off the street? There aren't exactly tag teams lining up to get into Global.” “How about the champions before the last ones? Train and Brick, former world champions and all that. Big time players. Ruckus, Inc is what they go by.” Josh pulled up the folder and look in it at the announcement. “Oh, big deal. The walking gangster stereotypes. I'm impressed... not.” “They're former tag team champions.” ?Okay, so we fight them, win and then what? Are they going to go... 'Hey, Josh and Gray. You've beaten our last two championship teams now... here you go with your nice shiny new belts. Fat chance in that.? Gray dropped a stack of papers down onto the table in front of the small television. He then bent down and picked up a box of things and dropped them down onto it also. “Get your head on straight first. Tropical Storm are not people to just jump on and try to ride them out. I was hoping that I'd never have to get to that point with them as it was.” Josh looked up at his brother's look of resignation on his face. ?What? They're just a couple of idiots!? “You can keep thinking that. But I know differently. They're the ones who wrote the book on tag team wrestling that the academy follows. Along with a few others.” Josh's jaw drops open. ?They're members of the Order?? Gray shakes his head. “No, but at one time with a third person they formed a stable known as Oni No Kage who combined with two others to form the first incarnation of the Order. Their good friend in life was with them. Don't take them lightly.” Josh scowled at his brother's refusal to just put them over as idiots. ?And just who was this friend that kept them around? Who would put up with them... at all?? Gray gave him a stern look. ?The Keeper of Secrets, himself. In this box and in that stack of papers is a copy of the entire history of the team that's been known as Tropical Storm and Hit N' Run. Read it, get to know them. But follow my warning, my little brother: impulsive behavior when it comes to this will only get you more and more pissed off as those two idiots show you how the game is really played. They're crafty. They're pranks were so much more genius level, and innovative then Grest's. But sometimes, they bite off more then they can chew. But they really love it when others do the same thing with them. Learn about them first. They're time will come. A pack of wolves might know that it can take down it's prey, but they still watch it, study it first and figure out any habits it has that they can exploit.? Josh looked at the stack of papers, and the box of tapes. ?You just want to hear me say thank you, don't you?? Gray laughed at him. “Yeah, but it can come later on. I’ve been called up to the 'rez' by Billy Sly Fox. I'm running up there. You study. I've got a DVD in there with the last few matches of both Brick and Train too. Watch that one mostly for right now. It'll hopefully be less brain numbing then some of Dermont's and Lanza's stuff. I'll probably be overnight.... so don't wait up for me.” Gray didn't wait for an answer. He looked at the watch on his wrist as he then walked out of the room, leaving his brother behind for the time being. A few hours later with Gray Gray brand pulled himself up the steep face of the cliff with the resolve that he was known for. He wore no safety gear or anything else, and was shirtless in the desert sun. His muscles rippled as he finished coming up, pulling his almost three hundred pound frame up onto the plateau. When he was up onto his feet, he pulled his shirt from his back waistband on his jeans and wiped the sweat from his brow. He looked out and saw a small cottage sitting there, with an old, looks like the early 70's, Jeep CJ-5 4x4. In fact it looks like the last time it was even washed was the last downpour this are of the desert gets. He heard a slightly familiar voice speaking to him. ?I know what you're thinking, Gray Brand. Why did I have you climb the cliff up to my house when you could driven your fancy new truck up the back road, right?? Gray tucked his shirt into his front waist band, letting it dangle like a loin cloth. “No, I was actually wondering how good a car mechanic you are if that thing is still running properly.” The old man, wearing old black jeans, a black sabbath t-shirt and a black felt top hat with a feather based hat band on it, walked out of the open door to the cottage and stood there. “You're not at all like my grandson. Then again I can attribute that to his mixed heritage. You are full blooded, to a point.” The old man, barely able to walk steps out into the sun further. His face shows the signs of being through quite a few season changes in the desert. “I can only hope to learn from you, Great Sly Fox.” The old man scowls. ?BAH! I'm no great one. I'm just to onry to die and leave these people to their own orchestrations. I'm no shaman or wise man. So why do you seek me out?? Gray walks up to him and then bows down onto one knee. “I was directed to find you, Sly Fox, but the wise man of the Sioux named Screaming Eagle. The grand father of Danielle Northsky, Sirius Star. He said that you could help me with my lessons that are needed for me here.” The old man smiled a grin of quite a few missing teeth. What teeth were still in his head were yellow with bad maintenance and what would probably a long life of smoking the Native made cigarettes. ?Ah, I see it now. You are lost, and you need specific instructions. Who is your tribe?? Gray answers. “I am of the Saquamish. An offshoot of the Salish people from the Puget Sound area of modern day Washington State.” The old man chuckled. “Wrong tribe.” Gray looked up at him. “I've never been taken into a Cairn. I've never been with my kind in any great number. Your grandson is the first I've seen of my kind outside of the Order's academy system in the Northern States.” The old man nodded to him. ?Good, it's usually hard to fight the allegiances that some of our kind take. Do you know what Tribe's blood runs in you? Wendigo or Utkena?? Gray almost blushed at the question, because he knew the answer would not fit either. “Neither of those. I am of the children of Fenris.” The old man took a step back with that knowledge. ?How?? “We don't fully know how. When the white man came to our area, almost two hundred years ago, some of them were immigrants from the Scandinavian countries. On top of that, there have even been ancient Viking longboats found in the bays of the sound. There are many theories, but none really proven. But any of them could have brought the blood with them.” The old man smiled his toothless smile. “Yes, that's true. So, you come to me to learn how to control that which you are afraid of. That which you have given up a family: A wife and children, for? Good luck. But I respect the wise man who sent you, so I will not turn you away. I just wish Blaise would have been a bit more forthcoming in why he wanted to get in touch with me. He's always so secretive anymore.” Gray kept his head bowed. “Now get up. By the way, the name is Billy. I don't like to answer to anything else. And I hope you don't mind drinking or smoking. This new fangled world is just not working into the ways that I've ingrained into me over the last 70 years. That's for sure.” The old man put out his hand as Gray came up to his feet. Gray then moved to grasp his hand and shake it. A few hours later with Joshua Brand At the same time that his brother had been meeting his teacher in the ways of being that which he ultimately was, Josh had decided that the tapes and that were enough. He left his room on the second floor and worked his way down to the first floor where the kitchen was. He looked outside and saw Dani and her friend, Ukyo, sitting out by the pool in the afternoon sun. Josh sees Jessie, the housekeeper that works for them as she's stirring a pot of something that looks to be destined for dinner tonight. ?Hey, Jessie. What's up?? He watches Dani and Ukyo as she answers. “Not much, Mr. Brand. Ms. Northsky and her friend are talking out there. It appeared to be mostly female topics. And if you go to your bedroom, please check to make sure the carpet cleaner isn't interrupted. Ms. Northsky spilled a bottle of her ink this morning and he's trying to get it out of the rug.” Josh nodded his head. ?Ah, okay. I didn't even notice anyone else in the house. Is he here?? “Yes.” Josh decided that was kinda funny, he headed back towards the hallway on this floor that connected to the master bedroom that he and Dani shared. He stepped in and took a survey of everything. Over near Dani's desk was the black spot on the rug. Foam came up from it as the chemical re-agents worked. That would explain why he'd not heard the machine that sat next to it yet. He then looked over and saw the man standing there, next to Dani's Macaw. He appeared to be talking to it gently as he looked outside at the two girls lounging around the pool with a definite intention. Josh tried to read the man as his talents would allow, but he couldn't get anything. “Oh, I'm sorry. I didn't know they had called for a cleaner today....” Josh tried to play it off that he'd accidentally come into the room. The man, Hispanic looking, wearing the jumpsuit for the cleaning company turned around and smiled at him. “Oh, no problem, sir. I've got the spot soaking. India ink isn't easy to get out of the rug.” “Well you'll be happy to know that she uses a natural ink. Shouldn't be as hard then. It's mostly made with the ink of cuttle fish or squid.” The man nodded at him, but absently looked back out to the patio area. Josh was confused inside his head though. He couldn't feel anything from this guy, at all. “Hmm, looking at the chicks outside huh? I guess that would be a perk of working a job like this. Being able to see all these pretty ladies.” The man smiled. “Yes, the Oriental girl is very pretty. Shame with the hair dye job though. Makes her stand out. Then again, maybe I could find I'd like that type of thing.” The man smiled at him, wide with perfect teeth. “Yeah, she is. Her husband lives up the street. He's a bit bigger then me. Half Irish, bad temper.” ?Husband?? “Yeah.” “That's a shame.” The man looked to decide that he'd better stop and moved over to check the stain on the carpet. ?Although the other girl looks familiar. Has she appeared in any magazines?? Josh should have expected this question. “Yeah, she was in FHM last year. Main story and cover.” ?Ah, that's where I recognized her. Pretty lady too. But she's your's right?? Josh didn't like his tone. “We're dating.” The guy nodded and started to use a scrub brush on the stain to try to lift it out of the rug. He looked to have started to ignore Josh as he did. Josh just walked out of the room after a bit. He didn't see the two pairs of eyes that followed him. One from the carpet cleaner, the other from the bird that stood on top of it's cage.
