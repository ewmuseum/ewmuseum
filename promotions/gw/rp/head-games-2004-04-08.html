Head Games...

8 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/8/04 'He's inside your head.' That's what the whispy, but familiar voice blew into Train's ear as he sat watching the tape. The voice was one from the past. One that had guided him a long time ago. It occured to Train for a moment that he must have done the right thing headed back to the old gym just before Clash of the Titans. He hadn't heard Isaiah's voice in a long time. He had for a while after Isaiah left him, but the more and more he tried to forget what happened the night Isaiah left the more and more feint his voice had become. Until finally...he couldn't hear him at all. But now it was starting to creep back...all the lessons learned. As the 19' tv he'd set up in the downstairs workout area of his warehouse apartment played one of his next opponent's greatest hits Train chuckled quietly to himself. It was funny that in the midst of all this world class equipment and his newly renovated facilities the only thing he could think about was the old gym. That old, dingy building where he'd learned so much. The sounds of bodies pushing themselves to the limit as they scratched and clawed to prepare themselves to reach some lofty goal they'd long dreamt of. The musky smell of the sweat that perculated as all who'd trained along side Train pushed themselves to the brink of exhaustion and beyond. It seemed now that Train had returning and opening the doors to the gym Isaiah had released his spirit. Now everything he'd been taught was returning to him. As was his teacher. And, as usual, he wouldn't be quiet. Really it was a good thing. Train had forgotten one of the most important lessons he'd ever learned, but the voice reminded him. He was right. Jesse Jamester was inside Train's head. Not hard to figure out how he got their either. The screen was lit brightly with images of the new PTC Global Champion going to work on a variety of different opponents. Watching him could be intimidating for anyone. He was quick for a big guy. Agile. The technical skills were solid. No doubt he had an all around game. It was no wonder he was able to win that title. It was no wonder had a few knots tied in him about this match. Fuck McNasty. Fuck Brick. Fuck Makonie. Fuck Christina McCoy. If Train puts a Hollowpoint through the Global Champion's dome? It might not be that Global title, but a belt was coming his way. Melt Ice. Put Zero into the negatives. Put Deacon on the deck. He could do one or all those. He didn't know which one he'd get to do, but whichever one, the chance comes when he leaves Jamester on his back, staring up at the lights. But as the pictures of Jamester working his quick, cruiserweight like offense flowed in through his eyes, he know that wasn't gonna be no walk in the park. But still... Every offensive scheme has a defensive set to counter it. Problem was Train wasn't sure what the hell it was. At least not until Isaiah whispered in his ear. No doubt Jamester was gonna be running and guning. Trying to put this thing away early. Hoping the star power slash experience factor would psyche him out. But Train knew better than that. Experience is great. A big name makes you money. But in a fight, it's about two things. Offense and defense. And it wasn't your body you were defending. It was your head. He hadn't thought about the day he learned that in years.... ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ April 20, 1995 'The Bottom's Training Facility' is as full of as much hustle and bustle as the rest of Chicago today. The place is packed with people, all bodies in motion with heavy and speed bags being pounded, ropes being jumped, weights being forced up and dropped down. They are the sounds of personal battles being waged as those training her push themselves to levels they have yet to go. But there is an odd, akward sound. It's the sound of frustration and irritation. The sound of anger at the situation and the inability to do anything about it. The sound sweaty flesh hitting the hard, parkay floors. The muscular yet slinder man, dressed in loose, leather like pants with flames going up the legs, now thoroughly in control of the ring continues to bounce energeticly on the balls of his feet while the the young man he was sparing with goes over the top rope and hits the ground with a resounding thud. Half the gym stops to see what's going on as Isaiah, with his ever present jogging suit and imposing figure, slowly walks over to his young pupil, shaking his head. Isaiah had been training Tony for a while now. It had been two years a couple of days ago. He would never deny how proud he was of Tony. The old man, a gristled, former pro wrestler, had come back to the neighborhood after traveling the world. After he'd captured a legion of fans, won numerous titles and, most importantly to him at the time, a hell of a lot more money than he had growing up as a kid in this same southside Chicago neighborhood. Back when he'd been in 1 bedroom hovel, sharing the space with his mother, father and 4 brothers and sisters because it was all the family could afford, he could have never predicted the way his life would turn out. From that half condemned hell hole to the best hotels and suites int he world. From being a second class citizen that only paid lip service to that 'justice for all' slogan they loved so much to being a respected athlete in a world that appreciated warriors. Isaiah had been a gladiator and it provided endless pride for him. His heyday was long gone, but no matter how many years had past, the title belts he's won hang in the gym office, gleaming like the day he'd won them. He'd been blessed and he knew it. He also knew he couldn't just take so many blessings without returning a few. Not without giving some kid the same opportunity he'd blessed with. Or at least the confidence to go out and make his own way in the world without becoming another statistic in the plight of black men in America. The second Isaiah layed eyes on Train he knew he was exactly the kind of kid that needed to be blessed. Not two years ago he'd walked in just a small, loud mouth punk with all the heart in the world, but no ability to back it up. The first time he'd seen Tony he was pressed up against the juvenile detention van that delivered him to the gym. He still wasn't sure what Tony had said to that hick gaurd to push the man to beat him, but he knew two things about it. One, Isaiah had come out just in time to save Tony from the beating of a lifetime. Two, the second he looked into the kid's eyes...all the grit, guts and determination shining through the fear, he knew he had what it took. He knew he had what it took to become a real man, making his way in the world the right way. He knew he had what it took to be a warrior. He knew he had what it took to be a champion. Unfortunately Tony wasnt' showing right now as he lay half sprawled on the gym floor. It wasn't his physicality that was lack anymore. The dreads had gone from the little sprouts sticking out of his head to hanging down around his ears. And In the two years Tony had been training, compelled to stay off the streets, he'd gone from 5' nothing to two 5'9' and packed on 60 pounds of lean muscle. His agility and endurance had increased ten fold in the time he'd been training. No, this was all about the mental. There was no reason Train shouldn't have been able to take down the sparring parnter Isaiah had lined up for him today. the kid was about the same size and agility as Tony. But he had a style Tony had never seen before. His name was Yugi Kansaki... The Wildfire. He was a student in the dojo of one of Isaiah's old buddies from Japan. The high flying, high speed, martial arts style of this visitor from the orient had Tony dumbfounded. Isaiah had figured it'd be a challenge, that's why he lined up this little session while the Yugi was in the states on a tour of the independent circuits. However...it shouldn't have gone anything like this. Tony was the younger man, only 16 (though by the still loud mouth you would have figured he was going on 30), but he had the advantage here. The fight was in his hood and this gym was his block. He was just a fast and likely stronger. Tony should have spent the sparring session coming to realize that. Instead he spent the majority of his time getting peppered with swift kicks and a flurry of offense resembling a pinball game as it came bouncing off the top and middle ropes. Tony had spent so much time trying to analyze his opponent's style he'd forgotten to execute his own. Tony had dropped his guard. He'd let The Wildfire get inside his head. Tony finally rolled over and to his feet, half stumbling and angry. The ice bucket that sat by the ring full of ice for those training to cool off and get some water became a target, Tony booting it halfway across the gym with a clamor, spreading ice everywhere. 'FUCK!!!' Tony claimed as he kicks the bucket. 'You feel better about getting your ass handed to you now?' Isaiah calmly inquired. 'Whatever man...' Train quietly growled as he rolled his shoulder, trying to work out the pain there from his nasty landing. 'Whatever my ass boy. You're letting this opportunity slip right through your fingers.' 'Opportunity? Opportunity to what, get my ass kicked?' 'Boy... This boy train's with a buddy of mine in Japan. A buddy that just happens to have thick ties to 3 big promotions out there. I called in a favor getting this kid over here for you today. I told him I had a real prospect for them as soon he got that GED he's working on. Now, this kid is headed back to Japan tommorow. Now just imagine what could happen if he goes back to my buddy and let's him know that you pinned him. This is what we been talking about. You getting away from this neighborhood. Not having to work some halfass, rinky dink job to get buy. Touring, building a name for yourself and making descent money. It's all right here and you're letting it slip away.' 'Man, what the hell you want me to do?! Shit, Bruce Lee over there doing some ol' 'Enter the Dragon' type shit. What am I supposed to do?!' 'You could do what I've taught you to do. You could wrestle your game instead of his. You could kick him the hell out of your head!' 'Man, he ain't my head, aight?' 'Oh, he's not?' 'Hell no!' 'So you haven't hit him with one suplex this whole match cuz why?' 'Cuz he won't let me inside! And when he does I'm the one that winds up on the mat. I don't know man, I don't think I'm ready for all this.' 'hahaha... Yeah, I gotta hand it to the kid. He's done a job on you.' 'Man, what the hell you talking about?' 'I'm talking about you not wrestling your type of match. Down in the trenches, using your powere and low center of gravity like I taught you.' 'I just told you I get caught when I go inside.' 'Maybe you should try it when he's not expecting it instead of on his schedule.' 'On his scheldule?' 'Let me ask you something. In boxing....what's the point of jabbing?' 'What? I don't...' 'Don't tell me you don't know. I know damn well that you know, we've wathed a million fights right in this gym. What is the point of jabbing?! I mean they're not usually hard punches, they don't usually ring a man's bell. So, what's the point?' 'What doess boxing have to do with this?' 'Boy, just answer the damn question.' 'Man...to keep the other dude off you.' 'Thank you. It's not meant to knock your opponent out, it's just meant to keep him at bay.' 'Yeah.......so?' 'So you're just standing there in the ring letting that kid jab you the whole match. Those kicks? They're not stunning you. Best I can tell they're not even really hurting all that much, but he's still snapping them out there to keep you at a distance. Instead of getting inside you're just standing out there letting him pop you.' 'Hell, even when I get inside he's ready.' 'Of course he's ready because he's letting you in.' 'Letting me in?' 'Is there an echo in here? Yeah, he's letting you in. It didn't seem funny he was constantly kicking and hitting the ropes, moving away from you and then all of the sudden he's just open for you to run up on him? Then, when you do, you wind up on the mat?' 'Man... Isaiah you keep saying all that... What the hell am I supposed to do about it?!' 'You can do what I told you and shake him out of your damn head! Stop letting him run his game on you. He's using those kicks to push out to the outside. He wants you out there so he has room to get a running start so he can hit you with that high flying, bouncing off the ropes stuff. And all the time you spend on the outside the more time you give him to work.' 'So?' 'So get up on his punk ass boy! Stop letting him put you out where you're at the point of his foot. This kicks ain't hurting you, put your arms up, block and push you way in. When you're in stop waiting to see what he's gonna do. Lock his ass up and hit him with that T-Bone or Northern Lights. When he tries to hit the ropes follow him in. He can't get up any speed if you're right there to greet his ass everytime he turns around. When it looks like he's wide open, back off and make him come to you. When he wants you on the outside go inside, when he wants you inside stay outside. When he wants space crowd him. When he wants to be up on you back back out. You do that you throw off his game plan. You throw off his gameplan you throw off his offense. You throw off his offense you throw off his timing. You throw off his timing your throw off his defense. And I ain't really talking about the defense for his body. It's like I told you a million times. Wrestling happens half in the ring and half in the head. It don't even matter if he was better than you in every way, which he ain't. But even if he was, if you throw off his defense you won't only be out of range of his offense, you'll be inside his head. Now... get your ass back in there. Get him out of your head and get in his. If you can do that... you'll see you ain't gonna just be inside his head... Your foot'll be inside his little ass.' Tony stood for a few moments, letting the idea permiate him. Embolden him. Isaiah could see it in the kids body language as his posture slowly straightened and the confusion that had been written on his face turned to a smirk. Train turned and climbed back into the ring, Isaiah knowing the whole time that this was about to be a different match than the one earlier. The Wildfire was still bouncing on his feet, going through some quick katas and kicking drills. But Tony didn't seem to care. He just strolled right up to the japanese import, who up to a few minutes ago, was bouncing him around the ring like a basketball. Isaiah face carried its usual stern visage, but inside he was smiling. It was about to get good. The Wildfire was hardly intimidated. He just returned Tony's smirk with one of his own. 'They say...you...good. I no see it.' stated The Wildfire as plainly as possible in his broken English that was descent for someone who hadn't been speaking it long. Inside Isaiah's smile grew wider. He knew what the young Japanese man was trying to do. He knew he'd been in his opponent's head earlier and he was just trying to stay there. But he didn't know Tony. Isaiah did though. He knew that wasn't going to throw him off...as much as it was going to piss him off. 'What?' asked Tony, his tone and his voice reading shock. 'You no understand word...coming out of me mouth? I say, they say you good. I no see.' 'Oh, hell naw.......... I just know yo' little non speakin' ass ain't trying to talk shit to me in a language you can't even speak.' 'I speak ok. You hear what I say. You do nothing.' 'I do nothing? Oh for real? Cuz see....' Train says just before rearing his hand back and letting it fly. WHAP Tony's hands weren't the biggest in the world, but it would be easy to think otherwise when looking at the red outline left behind after catching the side of Wildfire's face flush. Seeing how it snapped Wildfire's head to the side, sending him stumbling backwards, it was understandable that the slap, so loud, caught the attention of everyone in the gym, bring all other activity to a screeching halt. All eyes snapped to the ring as The Wildfire quickly regains his footing. He quickly shoots his eyes back to Tony. Even though it comes out in Japenese there's no need for interpreter to understand the intent of the phrase he utters in Tony's direction. Suddenly the Wildfire bursts into a conflagration, exploding with a flurry of punches and kicks. Most in the gym can only snicker, Tony seeming to run from the action. But they none of them were close enough to see. There was no fear in his eyes. He just waiting for The Wildfire to make a mistake. The flurry continues, some hits connecting, but bouncing away uneffectively as Tony continues to evade and block, bearing up under the onslaught. Suddenly Wildfire has had enough. With lightning quick speed he charges the far ropes. If he'd been going any faster he would have left a trail of smoke like some looney tunes cartoon. Wildfire is likely invisioning the spectacular manuever he's going to hit his upstart oppenent with as he turns and rebounds. Unfortunately for him there's no doubt that his vision gets blurry as soon as he turns for the return trip. Tony has heard the instructions of his mentor well. Wildfire can't run more than 2 feet before he runs smack dag into Tony who has charged into the ropes right behind him. There is no time to react. The sensation he'd felt heading towards the ropes is the wind in his face. The sensation he'd gotten after coming off them is the hard canvas nearly breaking him in two after Tony tosses him halfway across the ring with a viscious T-Bone Suplex. The crowd Ooo's and Aw's as the Wildfire hits the mat hard. But Wildfire's tough. Despite the pain he rolls back to his feet. Train moves in, but quickly starts catching alternating left and right kicks to the side of his arms and The Wildfire decides to work the body. The loud slaps of the impacts ring through the gym, filling Wildfire with confidence. It only takes a few good connects to tell it's time to finish Tony off. He hops back, and then leaps forward, connecting a superkick to Tony's jaw. At least, that was the idea. Instead, he quickly finds himself on the mat again, but there's no bounding back to his feet this time. Not with his knee filled with pain after Tony counters, grabbing his kicking foot and whirling around underneath the exposed leg, twisting it with the Dragonscrew Legwhip. Now the only one smelling blood in the water is Tony. He gives Wildfire no room to breathe, taking to handfuls of hair and yanking him ruthlessly to his feet. But Wildfire is hardly done. He genuinely catches Tony off guard, opening up on him with resounding knife edge chops to his chest. Tony is rocked back on his heels, stunned. One time... Two times... Three times... Hell no, not again. Tony sucks up the pain and barrels through the forth chop like an on coming train. Tony suddenly spins hips to expose his right side to Wildfire. The subsequent chops from Tony rattle off rapid fire like an automatic machine gun, rocking Wildfire with 5 stiff shots. Wildfire has only half felt the last one before he's doubled over with a sidekick to the gut. There's nothing the visiting wrestler can do to free himself when he finds his head locked between Tony's legs, now staring down at the mat. He plots his last ditch effort, feeling the powerbomb coming. It's a devestating move, aside from the pause the aggressor has to take at the top of the move before slamming his opponent back to Earth. That's where Wildfire plans his escape, at the pause. He's swung up with a vengance onto Tony's shoulders...but there is no escape. The pause never comes. The whole gym fills with a unified gasp as what looked to be a powerbomb is snapped straight back in one fluid movement, Tony falling to his back and The Wildfire shot throat first down onto the top rope. Wildfire is launched off the rope down to the mat, clutching at his throat, doing whatever he can to open up his airway again. Tony cares nothing of his struggles though. He simply hooks the leg and counts the 1...2...3. The ring quickly fills with trainers, attending to the downed wrestler, simply on loan from the orient. Even Isaiah charges in pulling Tony aside as the others attend to The Wildfire. 'Boy, what in the hell was that?!' he demands to know. 'I call that shit the 'Streetsweeper' man! That joker's bad, right?!' Tony inquires excitedly. 'Hell yes, it's bad boy! Have you lost your damn mind?!' 'What?! What's the problem?!' 'What's the problem?! Look!' The forcefully turns Tony towards the mob scene of trainers trying to help the young man laying on the mat still clutching his throat. The trainers nod to Isaiah, letting him know that he's going to be ok, but that doesn't change the old veteran's outlook. 'Boy, if you use that in a match I garuantee you get disqualified. Look, you did a good job, but you need to put that move way down in your bag of tricks. I really think you need to go with that inverted suplex into the frontface DDT I showed you.' 'The Hollowpoint.' 'What?' 'The Hollowpoint. That's what I named that move. You like the name?' 'Boy...why does everything you do have to be named after a gun or a bullet.' 'Just cuz I don't run the streets no more don't mean I ain't gonna keep it real.' 'Mmmhmmm... Just keep it real with something besides that Streetsweeper garbage. ....... ....... Good job.' ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ The tape in the vcr stopped at the end and begun to rewind itself. Train had missed most of it reminiscing about a day he hadn't thought about in years. But that was ok. The past was more helpful than that tape anyway. Jamester was no longer in his head. But he had a new idea now. He remembered an old weapon in his arsenal. Isaiah had been right about The Streetsweeper. It had gotten him disqualified from every match he'd used it in during his short tour in Japan after he had gotten his GED. The Wildfire went on to make a pretty good name for himself, despite ducking Tony, then being billed as 'Big-T'. The move was deemed illegal in Japan and people bad mouthed Tony for using it. He came to figure out years later that the reason his tour was so short is that no one wanted to be booked against him because of that manuever. It was maligned. But more importantly...it was also feared. It had been a lot of years since those days. When he flew across the ocean with the desire to see the world he escaped from the streets that had held him. At least for a little while. It didnt' last. He hadn't planned it. When he was fighting just the way Isaiah had taught him, making a little money touring the independants he'd always thought he would be able to keep his promise to Isaiah. He thought he would be able to leave the streets alone. Problem is the streets wouldn't return the favor. They pulled him back the night...the night... The night Tony died....and Train was born. He quickly shook off the ghosts that were trying to drag his memories down a dark path. He switched back to the task at hand. Jamester. Beating the PTC Champion and getting his first title shot. And now, he had what he needed. The game plan. The mindset. The knowledge to get behind his lines and into his head. And the secret weapon. In any other promotion it would probably get him disqualified. Oh, but this was Global Wrestling now. Damn near anything goes. So that meant one thing. The game was all new and he could do whatever the hell he needed to. No doubt Jamester was scouting Train the same way Train was scouting him. He'd know The Hollowpoint was coming so he'd probably come in a flack jacket to ward it off. But his trip to the past had made that ok now. If The Hollowpoint missed there was another option. A blast from the past. Not Tony with The Streetsweeper, but Train... with 'The 3rd Rail'.
