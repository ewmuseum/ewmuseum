Maybe they aren't right

18 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/18/04 The night sky looms, dark and ominous over the city of Dallas. A single security light, is the only light that shines upon the small hotel parking lot from which we are located. A dark shadow walks across the dark, and eerie parking lot. Slowly, methodically, one long stride after another, the figure stalks its way towards the camera. Finally the figure reaches the light. The dark and ominous figure, as it reaches the light, is revealed to be Ice. Ice stands under the glimmer of the light, in a pair of black Converse sneakers, black wind pants, and a black silk button up shirt. Ice's jet black hair is pulled back into a low pony tail, which rests gently on the back of his neck. Ice slowly raises his right hand, and motions for the camera to approach with the curl of his right index finger. Slowly the makes its way towards Ice, until it is close enough to Ice's liking, and he holds out the palm of his hand, telling the camera to stop where it is. 'Self doubt..' Ice says. 'The one issue that I've had a difficult time dealing with as of late. The only flaw I've been able to find in myself.' Ice takes in a long and deep breath. He holds in the breath for a few moments before slowly releasing it back out into the dark night sky. Ice turns his head away from the camera and looks up into the security light for a moment, before slowly lowering his head back down, and turning it back towards the camera. 'Since my debut into this business, I've had to scratch, fight, claw, and battle for everything I've ever gotten. Now I've made it to the top of the mountain. I have finally reached the one goal I've worked my entire life to achieve. I AM THE GW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION!' An arrogant smirk glimmers across Ice's face as he says those words. That smirk instantly fades away, and is replaced just as quickly as it appeared by a dark and sadistic snarl. Ice's eyes, more specifically his evil glare, pierce a hole through the camera lens. 'Now that I've achieved that goal, I've been living off of my reputation. Yeah. I admit it. I've grown soft. I've let things that normally wouldn't effect me, effect me in the worst ways. I've let punks who I would have destroyed in less than a minute, get one up on me. But more importantly.........I've forgotton just who it is that I am.' Ice says in a anger filled tone of voice. Ice raises his right hand once again, and pulls the hair band off from around his pony tail, letting his hair fall free. Ice shakes his head from side to, which causes his hair to partially cover his face. 'This Monday night. Train. This Monday night is where it all begins again. I've realized the error of my ways. I have seen my flaw. And Monday night, Evolution, I will show you, and the world, just who it is I REALLY AM!' Ice tilts his head back slightly, as he stares down at the camera. 'Train, you said on Overload, that I don't deserve the fear that is felt by my opponents when they step into the squared circle with me. You make it seem like you are not even worried to be walking down that aisle, to step into the ring with the very best in this business. This Monday night, I GUARENTEE YOU WILL LEARN THE REASON FOR THAT FEAR!' Ice's head lowers once again. Violently Ice flips his head back, flipping any strands of his long, jet black hair out from in front of his face. Evilly Ice glares into the camera. 'This Monday night, at Evolution. Your music will hit, and you will walk down that aisle. You will step into that ring, and you will wait for the VERY BEST IN THIS BUSINESS! As you wait, the lights will go down. My music will hit. A cool chill will fill the arena. The hairs on the back of your neck, and the tops of your arms will stand on end. It will send shivers up and down your spine. I will walk down that aisle, step into that ring, and look you straight in the eyes, and see the fear in your soul. You will look into these cold blue eyes, and see nothing but your fate. It will be at that very moment, you will realize just who in the hell I AM! It will be then, you realize, that when you walk through those ropes, you are in MY WORLD! And in MY WORLD, there isn't a single soul alive, who has what it takes to hang with me.' Ice pauses for a moment, as a sick and sadistic smile crawls across his face. The security light begins to flicker, as the wind picks up and begins to howl through the black night sky. 'Train, this Monday night, you will find out, just what a REAL MONSTER IS. Because when Evolution is over and done, you will be put on ICE!' The light begins to flicker faster and faster, and the wind begins to pick up and become faster and faster, the howling becomes louder and louder, until the security light bulb finally bursts, and everything goes black.
