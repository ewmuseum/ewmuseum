Don't Let Her Slip Away

3 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 3rd, 2003 I'll Just Sit Back Here And Watch It Is What I Do Best Out Of Every Guy In The World You Said I'm Not Like The Rest Then I Won't Be Like The Rest With the paper in his hand, a tear falls from his eye. He takes a deep breath, but it is stolen by a mouthful of pants. He wipes off the tear, but more fall onto his wrist. His tears, they sink into the pores of his skin, only reviving more. He throws his head back, his chin shaking with pain. He crumbles the paper up in his hand’so tight, the ink almost bleeds out of the paper. He throws it onto the ground. He rests his face on his hands’weeping over his problems. Each tear, they slowly ease the pain. Time heals all wounds son. Time heals all wounds? “From the moment I saw you, a chill ran down my spine. I knew, I knew that you were the one.” The paper begins to unfold, while he rests his head on his hands’catching every tear. “These years spent, watching every move you made and listening to every word you said. These years, they blew up right in my face. I love you’I hate you’I’d love to hate you, but I can never hate you’because I love you. But this love I have for you, it doesn’t mean anything to you. But to me, if you loved me, it would mean more then gold.” He speaks to himself, trying to talk his problems out to himself. The paper unfolds, and so it reads: Sage, I’m astonished to hear what you said about me. I never in my wildest dreams thought that anybody would ever care about me so much. I am honored to be the one that you think about when you go to sleep and the one you think about when you wake up. But it can’t work out Sage. I am 16 and you are 18, I am too busy with other things in my life. Anyways, I don’t normally date anybody anyways, friend or not. I’m sorry if I hurt you. But it is just the way it is. I can’t be your girlfriend and I can’t be with you. You have your future ahead of you, and we will have to break up. But there is one thing we can be, we can be friends. Relationships end, but friends last forever. Please don’t be mad at me Sage. I love you, but only as a friend. You are a great guy, a guy who cares and a guy who has actual emotions and feelings. Sage, you are something different then every other guy. You will be perfect for someone someday, but not me Sage. I’m sorry. Love, Jessica His tears evaporate into the heavens above’but his heartache stays with him. He lifts his head’his legs continue to shake. His eyes, pitch red from the crying he has done. “If I am such a wonderful and special guy, then why won’t anybody accept me for who I am” Simply because society frowns upon guys like me. And society accepts guys who have money. They accept guys who have great looks’but why wasn’t I blessed with these things? God, oh dear God, you have put me through such torture in my life. My father, my father died’and you couldn’t save him. I followed you for such a long time, and you did not bless me one bit. After all my troubles following you, you could have at least helped me through one of these things. If I’m such a special and great guy, then why don’t I have somebody to hold? Why can’t I speak the words, “I love you” to anybody? God, oh dear God, you have dealt me a bad hand in this game called life. And I wonder, I wonder why I continue to live on this one way life filled with agony and defeat.” He pauses and thinks about the whole situation’another tear falls from his face. “I have enough friends, I have Brylou and Xavier. I don’t need anybody else. I have a place inside my heart that is empty. And it is a place that I’ve lost when my father passed away years ago. And it is a place that I wanted to fill with memories and love. Jess, dear Jess, why” Why can’t you accept me when anybody else won’t? Of course, I’ll be perfect for somebody in the future’but the future, that is wonderful’how am I supposed to know when that is?” He gets off his chair and picks up the piece of crumbled paper. He holds it in his hand’wishing he could put it close to his heart and smile. But he doesn’t do too much smiling these days. He is on his own’he survives, because he dedicates his life to being the best he can be. But it is hard to be the best he can when he has nobody or anything to help him along the way. This love he has for her’it doesn’t come once a year’it happens once in every life. He pleads with himself’he can’t let go of what he has for her. He can’t stop caring and feeling how he feels about her’even though there will never be a future. “So we are friends’is that what I want?” He shakes his head side to side while wiping off a tear that slowly falls from his cheek. “It is not what I want. You try to cheer me up, but the words you’ve spoken at best are worst then teenage poetry. Relationships end’only if you want them to. I wanted us, us to be together forever. But you, you were all caught up in everything else when I was caught up in you. I seen everything you are. And all you seen of me is merely a silhouette of my love. Oh Jess, oh love’do you know how hard it is to continue with your life knowing that you can never succeed with what you want to succeed” Do you know how hard it is going to be to look into your eyes and try to smile when inside, inside I am crying. Jess, I can’t live life without you.” ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- In his heart are the memories. He let her slip away from his grasp. She let him slip away from his desire. His heart was broken, and it remained broken from that day. Nobody has yet to fill that place in his heart. The empty spot that has been missing since the death of his father. His new family, The Nevermore, is the only family he has ever had. The Harvesters have accepted him when nobody else could. And after all that they have done, that missing spot still is empty. He stands in front of her grave. A tear falls from his cheek. He bows down on one knee. He kisses the red rose and closes his eyes, pinching off the remaining tears, letting them fall from his eyes’fall from his troubles. Reviving the pain and memories, they are all he has left. She is gone now... “Jess, you once told me that friendships last forever and relationships break up’but you never said what happens if you left me forever. But what will last are the memories. They will last forever in my heart.” A tear falls from his eye and lands on the bright green grass on her grave. “I shouldn’t have let you slip away. But what was I to do” I was confused and I was young. I still loved you, but being with you was only a dream’because it would have never happened. I blame myself, not you, because I never stuck with it. I was stubborn and I only wanted you. I was greedy because I wasn’t with you. I let you slip away from my grasp Jess, I let you slip away from my grasp.” He tosses a crumbled paper onto her grave. The paper is yellow and has stains on it. A small wind slowly opens the paper. He turns around and walks away, raising his head towards the sky and blows one last kiss towards Jess. One last kiss’for the memories. The paper opens up and it reads: Jessica, I’m sorry I let you go. If I only knew, then I would have never left you. These years were filled with everything I’ve worked for in my life. I am finally accepted, but the spot in my heart is still empty. Jess, dear Jess’if only you were here, then you’d finally be proud of me. You would see where my hard work has taken me. And maybe things would have been different. Maybe things would have changed. But the word “maybe” is just a word people use when they dream. I’ve dreamed about you for all of these years and never realized the word, “forever” until now. Memories that I have with you’they last forever. I love you and thank you for the memories Jess. Love, Sage Tse Ling Never let the ones you love go away. Hold them tight. Even if they are only your friends, cherish their friendship, because it is better then nothing. Jess couldn’t be with me, so I was greedy and didn’t feel like talking to her anymore. But her friendship will never be forgotten, and neither will the memories.
