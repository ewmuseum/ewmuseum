Back to a Beginning (Part 1)

22 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 22, 2004 [Parked in the white concrete colored driveway, just in front of the two-car garage, connected to the deep majestic blue painted two-story Greensboro home of Mackenzie Geller is Mac’s jet-black Dodge Durango. The tailgate of her Durango is completely popped open. Around the garage is a white concrete sidewalk to match the driveway’s coloration, leading up to the front door that is slightly ajar. Inside the residence Mackenzie’s slender figure leaps from nowhere, her feet landing on the floor, at the base of the staircase. Mac is sporting a black spaghetti strap top, displaying her mid drift and a black belt, lined with rootbeer bottle caps, along the top of her slightly baggy plaid (black and red) pants. In her right hand she’s got a black duffle bag that she drops at the base of the stairs.] ?Where’s my baby?? Mac’s tone becomes small and meek, totally gentle. [At the sound of her voice a small black kitten quickly darts out from behind the black leather couch. The black fuzz ball scurries up to Mackenzie jumping at her and throwing his small body against her shoes. Crouching down to the floor Mac pats the kitten on the head, and then picks his whole body up, cradling him in her arms as she stands up.] “No, you can’t come with me this time baybba,” Mac taps him on the nose with her forefinger. “I’m sorry. You just stay here and be a good baby Axelkins for Uncle David, okay? Mommy promises to take you on the road trip next time.” [A small mew comes from the baby in response. Axel rubs his fuzzy head against Mac’s petting fingers as the front door fully opens. Mac looks over to the door, as it’s being shut behind a tall, well-built man, Mackenzie’s friend, David. Striding into the room, David wears faded blue jeans, a slightly off colored, and worn in white muscle tee, with a plaid button up shirt, unbuttoned and hanging open.] Mac: “Hey Daviekins. Thanks so much for watching baby Axie for the weekend while I go to New York,” she smiles at David. [David makes his way from the entranceway towards the stairs where Mac stands, rubbing Axel’s small and soft kittie ear in between her thumb and forefinger. The kittie tilts his head, against her finger, purring into her hand.] David: “No problemo, messed up my entire party hardy weekend, but I’ll deal,” he smiles at Axel, avoiding Mac’s icy stare. [Mac snuggles the kittie against her cheek, covering his ears.] “Don’t listen to Uncle Davie baybba. He loves you lots and couldn’t wait to kittie-sit you.” [David gently kissing the kittie on the top of the head, getting an undersized meow back and then he follows by smiling at the kitten, patting his hand down the kittie’s back, receiving a small but steady purring sound. After a moment, David wetly kisses Mac on the cheek, making a loud smacking sound as he does. Mackenzie allows him, but doesn’t look all too happy about it.] Mac: “Better be careful, I can already tell he’s going to be vicious. A killer guard kittie. Totally.” David: “Oh yeah? Maybe he gets it from his mommy.” [David smirks at her, getting the ice stare again. He ignores her stare and holds his hands out for the kitten.] “Come on now. Give me the little ankle bitter already.” [Mac smiles, reluctantly handing baby Axel over into David’s outstretched hands. Kenz leans her head down close to the kittie’s forehead and Axel, in turn, bumps his forehead against hers, giving her a “kittie kiss.”] David: “Ah. He likes you.” Mac: ?Of course he does! I’m his mother figure!? David: “Okay, okay. Don’t become Mommy Dearest on me now.” [Mac mouths “Sorry” as she gently hugs David, being careful of the baby kitten in his arms. Mac can’t resist petting the baby’s fuzz ball body once again, and then finally she pulls herself away and grabs her duffle bag from next to the staircase.] “Alright, the kitten food is in the pantry, in the kitchen. I’ve got soft bits and hard ones; I like to mix them together for him so he gets a variety, you know? So he’s not bored of his food. His food dish, water bowl, and litter box are also in the pantry. He’s beginning to be able to find them all himself, but sometimes he needs help. He can make it up one or two stairs by himself, but I don’t recommend taking him up there unless you’re bringing him back down. So he doesn’t get stuck up there, obviously.” [Mackenzie is rattling on and on as she slowly backs her way towards the front door, making very slow progress. David’s right eyebrow is slightly raised, he is looking at her, looking down at Axel, blinking, and looking back at Mac. Axel meows up at David softly, blinking as well.] “Also, he’s afraid of the dark; so you’ll have to take bring him to his food before bedtime. He gets a spoonful or two of tuna into his food every night before he has to be in bed at eight thirty. Usually he sleeps under the covers with me, but I’m not sure if he’ll like you that much right away. If he doesn’t want to sleep with you then his bed is in the corner of the living room.” [Finally Mac is only a small step from the front door. She’s got a firm look about her, as she’s racking her brain for something she might have forgotten. She slings the duffle over her shoulder, and reaches her hand back to the doorknob.] “If you have any problems call my cell, it’ll be on no matter what and I’ll check in every night. AND! No parties, no girls, and if you teach him any naughty words I will wash your mouth out with Dawn dish soup mister.” [Kenz pulls at the doorknob, allowing the door to swing open behind her back. She kisses her hand and blows a kiss to David.] “Thanks so much David. I owe you.” [Then kissing her hand once again, she blows a kiss to Axel before she swiftly walks out the door, letting it fall shut behind her.] “Axel, man, your mommy’s got some major issues.” [Dave shakes his head slowly, looking at the closed doorway as he slides a single fingertip over the tip of Axel’s nose. The kitten looks up at Uncle David with big, bright honey colored eyes and meows in agreement.] * * * [Just outside the house Mac throws her duffle into the back of her Durango and slams shut the tailgate. She moves around the rear of the vehicle and makes her way into the drivers seat. The Dodge starts up with a roar and Mackenzie is in reverse, going out of the driveway and then up the street. Mac lets out a soft sigh.] ?Home sweet hell. New York. What was I thinking?? Her words are quiet, almost thoughts forming in her mouth, instead of her mind. [Shaking off the thoughts of her old New York home and her old wretched life Mac reaches her hand out to the volume dial on the car stereo. Linkin Park’s premier CD, “Hybrid Theory” comes blasting through the speakers all around the car. Setting her cruise control, Mac begins her ritual jamming to Linkin Park, speeding past multiple cars on the interstate, and scaring little old ladies with her head banging as she drives. As she’s driving through Virginia she listens to both Linkin Park CDs, and a couple other random songs from her favorite CDs.] * * * [Before long Mac has already driven completely through Virginia, passing through Richmond. Having already made this trip many times, she doesn’t notice much anymore. She puts her Durango on cruise control and floats through her own mind, listening to music, and enjoying her time alone. After the end of one of her favorite songs on the radio Mac reaches over to the passengers side seat to grab her cellular phone. She turns down the volume on the radio before she dials David’s cell number.] ?Hello? What are you guys up to?? [There is a muffled noise from David on the other end.] ?Yeah, I’m in Maryland right now. Only a couple hundred more miles to go. How’s Axel? You fed him already, right?? [A barely audible “Yes” comes through the cell. Then there’s more muted speaking.] “What? You took him for a walk!” David..I know you better be kidding.” [After a moment David’s laughing can be heard from the other end. Mac is not amused.] “Damnit David. I’m getting ready to stop and eat lunch. Call me later.” [Mac hangs her phone up and tosses it back to the passenger’s seat as she pulls into the parking lot of Wendy’s to grab some lunch.] * * * [With a small half smile Mac lets her mind wander back to the not so good old days. Taking the interstate exit towards New York, she slips the Evanescence “Fallen” CD into the stereo, turning it down low. Her mind floats back to the one man she ever let herself trust.] “Evan. He found me in that miserable bar. Stopped me from giving up on myself. On life. Well, what little of a life I did have. He gave me my real life. Gave me this life. He took me from one hell and put me into another hell, underground. A more gentle hell, where I could be as much of a devil as everyone else.” [She’s remembering the underground fighting tournament Evan trained her for and then introduced her to. Being one of the only women entered into the competition was tough enough, especially when all the other women hardly made it past their second matches.] “I could have taken it,” she shakes her head, still regretting that semi-final match. [A devastating two-man attack, from behind, on Kenz before her semi-final match prevented her from going on to the finals in the tournament. The attack itself didn’t, but it wounded her enough to not have enough left to beat her semi-finalist opponent. Though, she did try. Of course, she could hardly fight back, but she always had enough left to kick out. Shockingly, her opponent showed enough mercy and sympathy to just knock her out after a few pinning attempts.] “Bigots.” [Mac shakes her head, letting go of her hate for those two men. It was a good four or five years ago. Still, her mind is wandering over her matches in the tournament. Every win. As her mind is drifting through her memories, and through her past Mackenzie passed the bright green interstate sign “Welcome to New York.”] “Finally,” she breathes in deeply, feeling relieved. [Getting off the highway and heading into New York Mac releases the cruise control and let’s her mind come back to reality. She drives her way through the cities back to her old place of residence, located in a rough part of a New York town. She sighs, pulling in front of the decaying apartment building and parking against the curb.] “Oh, how I hate this place,” her voice is that of revulsion as she bends her neck to the side, looking out the window at her personal hellhole. [Slowly she steps out of the Durango, before shutting the driver’s door Mac yawns a bit, stretching out her whole body. After a minute she commences walking around to the other side of the vehicle and steps onto the sidewalk, and looks up at the five-story brick building. Her gaze falls onto the third story, where her home used to be, all those years ago. Her face cringes in pain, remembering coming home to this every morning after a nine-hour night at the bar. Hesitantly, Mackenzie takes steps towards the building dilapidated entrance. She pushes open the two large wooden doors, tarnished by time and neglect. Mac steps into the lobby, looking around the room like she’d just traveled back to four years ago, the place was virtually untouched. Unchanged, and still as nasty now as it was then, if not more. She stood in the doorway, in shock.] “Wow,” the word came to her, under her breath. [Before she can prevent herself, Kenz is already making her way up the stairs from the lobby to the first floor. From the first floor stairs she makes her way to the second and then finally she’s only one step away from the floor of the third story. Moving down along the hallway Mac halts in front of her old residence. Mac slowly knocks a few times on the small timber door. After a moment it opens with a loud groan from the hinges.] ?Hello?? In the doorway is a meek looking young woman in worn jeans and a plain t-shirt. “Hi. Erm..sorry. I just, well, I used to live here. I was just taking a trip down memory lane, I thought maybe it would be empty.” “Oh. Well, come on in.” [Hesitantly Mac steps a few feet into the apartment, moving away from the door as the young lady closes it behind them.] “I’m Anne,” the woman holds her hand out and shakes Mac’s. “Mackenzie. Most call me Mac. Feel free,” she forces a smile for Anne, then lets her eyes shift to look around the room. “Wow, this place was a lot worse when I moved in here.” Anne: “Ah, I can imagine it was about the same as when I moved in. I dabble in some interior design, so I completely revamped the place,” she waves her hand in a motion around the room. ?Can I get you something to drink?? [Mackenzie is lost in her mind once again, she’s looking around the place, remembering the way it used to be. Anne’s voice is faint as she’s hearing it in the back of her mind. On one half of this small four-walled room there is a shaggy brown couch, a matching rocking chair, a tiny television, and a few baby toys here and there. On the other half of the room is the small tile-floored kitchen. There is only one hallway that leads to a tiny bathroom and another undersized bedroom. After a few moments she snaps back to reality.] Mac: “Huh? Oh, no no. No thanks. I can’t stay long. Being back here, no offense, but it gives me the urge to vomit.” Anne: “Completely understandable,” Anne lets out a small, genuine laugh. Mac: ?How long have you been living here?? Anne: “A couple years. It was just me for the first few, but now I’ve got a one year old.? Mac: ?How’s the rat population”? Anne: “Less, but still holding strong.” [With a laugh they both smile at each other, sympathizing with each other having to live in this. In the background there is a small cry from another room in the small apartment. Anne holds up a finger to Mac.] Anne: “Oh, feeding time. Just a second.” [Anne turns to exit the room, leaving Mac standing awkwardly in her living room. Listening to another small cry Mac’s mind drifts to Axel. She feels a throb of pain in her stomach at the thought of a baby living in a place like this. Reaching into her pocket, Mac pulls out a small wad of money, a couple twenties. Maybe a hundred dollars at most. She takes a step towards Anne’s purse, but before she can place the money inside Anne comes back into the room, holding her small bundle.] Anne: “Sorry “bout that. This is Alexander,” she smiles at the baby, showing him to Mac. Mac: “He’s very adorable. I remember a lady across the hall had a baby when I lived here,” Kenz looks down at the baby for a moment. “I really have to be going. Thanks so much for letting me look around my old place.” Anne: “Oh, no problem at all,” Anne moves to open the door for Mac. [As her back is turned, Mac slips the money into an open pocket of Anne’s purse and then follows her to the door, stepping out with a smile.] Mac: ?Thanks again. [With a nod, the door closes and Mac makes her way back to the Durango. Going back down the three flights of stairs is like Mac letting go of something, knowing she’ll never again have to ascend or descend those stairs or go back to that life. Climbing into the driver’s side of the Durango Mac takes one last look at the apartment building before starting the vehicle up again and driving off to her next destination.] To Be Continued..
