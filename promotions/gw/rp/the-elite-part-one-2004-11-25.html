The Elite (Part One)

25 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.25.04 As had become the annual tradition in the past few years, Ken way playing host to a slew of friends and family in his penthouse apartment for Thanksgiving for yet another year. To set the scene, most of the food was finished cooking, including the turkey and the cornish hens, and the main thing that Ken was now waiting for was the duck. He was in the kitchen while it was finishing up while his family and friends congregated in the remainder of the apartment. Laughter filled the air, along with the music of bands such as Paul Oakenfold and Darude spilled from the speakers strategically placed throughout the apartment accompaning the BOSE stereo that hung on the wall. Ken was quietly sipping his scotch in the kitchen with a smile on his face. He was pleased that things were going so well, as usually by this point in the day a family member, namely one Aunt Cathy, had taken it upon herself to sping Ken and the rest of his friends into a spiralling vortex of pissed off that could only be rivalled, perhaps, by an prgy between Sadaam Houssain, Osama bin Laden, and the Bush twins. She did so through insults, referring to them and their business as being filled with nothing but 'uneducated brutes' and 'steroid abusing ogres.' Yet, Ken couldn't remember the last time he saw large and fat green guy running around the ring talking like Austin Powers and tagging with a talking donkey. He must have forgotten her invitation this year... pity. He peered briefly out of the kitchen door to see his mother, Connie, engaged in a delightful conversation with both Bill and Josh, all with smiles on their faces. It made Ken happy upon seeing this due to the fact that Josh and his parents weren't really all that close. Hell, they no longer even spoke to each other anymore, let alone spent the holidays together. Therefore, it was always a comfort to see that his family had indeed 'adopted' Josh in as their own, and he had done the same with them, in much the same way the bothe he and Ken had done as 'brothers.' Ken loved the fact that he referred to his mother as 'mom.' Bill was a similar story in that he had only recently had a falling out with his family. So silly dispute between him and his father in which his dad was still angry with the fact that Bill had taken the past of a professional wrestling king as opposed to following in his footsteps and going into the courtroom as a lawyer. All this, even after Bill passed the bar after graduating from Harvard Law some years ago. Ken once asked him wy he didn't go on to practice. Why he didn't take the easy money with some big law firm as oppose to getting the hell beat out of him every night for what started out as little or no pay. Bill's response... the courts were filled with 'overeducated pussies' and 'cocaine abusing idiots.' That, Ken thought, was hillarious. His eyes quickly shot to another area of the room where his very close friend, Ceci Braden, an amazingly beautiful musical artist of Pacific Islander decent, was carrying on a casualy with Gunner Addison, the amazingly beautiful woman with whom he'd fallen in love. Ceci brought her eyes up, making contact with Ken's before delivering a smile and a playful wink. Many at one time thought that the two of them were meant to spend the rest of their lives together. This was true, but only in another sense. Ken and Ceci were together as in item at one time. He was her agent, they had just started Nameless Records, and they were very much in love. Until Gunner came along, it was the best relationship that either had ever had. The two were... the two are soulmates. Plutonic lifemates, as Ken likes to put it. She completes him and he does the same for her. Neither can see their lives without the other in it, nor do they want to. The friendship they share is very special, their bond unbreakable, making their relationship difficult to understand by most. It was too powerful for them to foul it by being an 'item,' as they knew they were meant for others. Ken for Gunner and Ceci for Haley. They were both overjoyed that the other had found true happiness and Ken was eternally grateful that Gunner truly did 'get' the realtionship between the two of them. The fact that it was now fully plutonic and that there were no romantic feelings attatched was something people couldn't usually grasp about he and Ceci. He flashed a smile back up at Ceci before she was distracted by Haley, her girlfriend, walking up to her and Gunner. This prompted him to re-enter the kitchen. The duck was finally finished and, as he pulled it out of the oven and placed it on the island, the door opened, allowing Gunner to step into the kitchem and throw her arms around him in a long embrace followed by a long, sweet, innocent kiss. 'Do... do you remember our first kiss,' she asked. 'Of course,' he said in response, 'how could I forget?'
