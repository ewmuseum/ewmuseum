Regret (part 2) *sucks*

Date: 7.11.03 I don't know what it was about this roleplay, but I just couldn't get a feel for it. I promise the next one will be waaaaaaaay better. -Josh Here's a little recap, since these are so far apart. Regret Ace Cash Promo # 1 for GW Dead-Zone It was the happiest day of Ace's young life. His best friend had messed up real bad when he let lost this girl. Ace couldn't help but stare into her eyes for too long. It seemed to weird her out a little at first. 'Are you ok, Ace?' She would always ask, a look of concern on her face. Ace always felt like such an idiot when she said that, because he didn't realize until just then that he'd been starting at her. 'Yeah, I'm fine. It's just you're beautiful is all.' Ace replied, a meek smile on his face. 'I can't help but stare sometimes.' 'Ooooh, Ace. That's so sweet.' She would reply, cuddling close to him on her couch. There was no happier place for Ace to be at that point in his life. Having the girl of his dreams with him was all he ever wanted, and now he did. His friend Joe Leone had really messed up when he lost her. She was perfect. Ace can still remember to this day every little detail about that night. He remembered the perfume she was wearing, sometimes he swore he could still smell it. He remembered the way she was wearing one of his shirts. Every little detail to the night. It had been simple enough, just a movie at her house. But Ace couldn't help but feel as if it were something much more as he sat closer to her. Love That word kept rushing into Ace's mind! He couldn't control it. He was too young to be in love.. wasn't he? That night seemed to go by in a flash. It ended far too soon for Ace's tastes. He was more than a little dissapointed as she led him to her door. Why? He wasn't quite sure, he supposed he just didn't want the night to end. She stopped him at the door and looked up into his eyes, with a smile on her face. 'I had fun! That movie was kind of lame, but it was better with you here.' She says, rocking back and forth on her feet. She's nervous. Did he have fun, she didn't know. Will he kiss her? 'Yeah, me too.' Ace replies, not quite sure if she even really liked him all that much, 'I...uuuh..had fun.' The akward silence started right about then. Should he just go before it gets even worse? Should he apologize for being so stupid? What should he do!? 'So, did you study for that Chemistry test tomorrow?' Ace says, trying to ease the mood, but she only laughs at him. 'No. Did you?' She asks. Did he not want to kiss her? Was she not good enough? Oh, she hoped not! Enough of this. She couldn't wait anymore. 'Ace.. why don't you like me?' She asks, lowering her head a little bit. She didn't want to make eye contact with him, just in case she began to cry. 'What? Why did you ever think th-' Stopping in mid-sentence, Ace sees a tear running down her face. Smooth move, asshole. Now you're making her cry. You're an idiot!! Ace reaches and gently brushes the tear off of her cheek, and tilts her head up to look at him. There are those eyes again! Her beautiful brown eyes, he couldn't stop staring. Ace slowly leaned towards her, his face coming closer to hers. His lips... meeting hers. Ace had always heard that the best way to know if you were in love was by the first kiss. And now Ace knew he was. He started to pull away, but went forward again, kissing her again before leaving out the front door, heading to his car. He sits in the seat for a little while before starting the car- smiling. This was a very special girl. He wasn't going to let her pass him by, he thought as he started his car and drove away from her house. The girl? Tiffany Jo Murray. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- How fast things change. Back then it was so much simpler, so much easier. Tiffany would later become Ace's manager in his wrestling career, and they would spent several happy years together. It was't until about a month ago that they had a single fight. Ace doesn't even remember what it was about, all he knows is that she's gone. She left back for Proctor, Minnesota, leaving him in San Antonio to live in a house they bought together.. except now he was all alone. Everywhere Ace goes, there are pictures of him and Tiffany. He can't stand it as he walks around the house with a cardboard box, putting all the pictures in it. He misses her. He places a few pictures in the box before coming to his favorite. That was a good day. She looked so beautiful, we were in love. The picture was taken in Proctor, Minnesota. Both Tiffany and Ace are wearing heavy winter coats, standing in front of Tiffany's mothers house. Ace is hugging Tiffany from behind, his arms around her waist and his chin on her shoulder. Her head is leaning to the side, resting against his. Both are smiling. So genuine. So true. So... real. Ace holds the picture in his hand, and it starts to shake a little bit. He did that from time to time. It wasn't long until a tear gently rolled down his cheek. STOP IT! Get a hold on yourself, Ace. Stop this crying bullshit! You're too strong for that. Ace squints his eyes shut hard and hurls the picture into the wall, glass shattering. Why did I have to be so stupid? Why'd I let her leave!? In all the time he was with Tiffany, he had felt many emotions. Lust, love, honor, pride, loyalty- just to name a few. But this was new for him. It was a feeling he had hoped he would never have to experience from his relationship with her. Things used to be so happy...so simple. All there was... was happiness. Now, there's only regret. And now for Part II.. Regret (part two) Ace Cash Anybody who's anybody knows about The Royal Flush down in San Antonio, Texas. Perhaps the hottest night cl.ub in existence, but very exclusive. There is a very select A-List for the club, and you didn't get in unless you had some high status in the area.The Royal Flush, owned by former pro wrestler, Wild Card, is underground and has some real badass bouncers at the front doors. There was a small office above ground with windows overseeing the parking area for the club members. And that's where the owner, Wild Card (real name: Lance McBride) stands. He often stood in that very spot, watching people enter and exit the club, associating the faces with names, and statuses. There were very few people that Lance felt the need to watch out for when they came around the club, mainly because of his security. But when that green limo rolled into the parking lot, Lance nearly shit himself. Ace 'Shit!' Lance yells to himself. Every single time that limo has pulled into his parking lot, something bad happens. It's not like Ace tried to start trouble or anything, it just seemed to follow him wherever he went. Lance paces around the room, mumbling to himself as he looks out the window at the approaching limo. He stops as it parks and sees the chauffer open the back door, as Ace Cash steps out, dressed very sharply, as usual. But another man follows him out. 'Oh sweet Lord..' Lance says in disbelief. Joey Hunter. Ace's little brother, and definently his worse half. Unlike Ace, Joey went into the 'family business' of oraganized crime, after his father, Benny Hunter. Joey is a small guy, but he's got a mouth that tends to get him into a lot of trouble. If it weren't for his backup and his mob connections, he'd get his ass kicked daily. Another man steps out after Joey. A distinctively tough looking guy. (think Vin Diesel) Josh Swanson. The enforcer of this little trio. Josh is the muscle that backs up Joey's mouth. Everybody in town knows not to mess with Joey or Ace because Josh will rip you in half. Lance can't help but get a little nervous everytime these three are together, because it usually causes problems...lots of problems. ------- Chapter 2 A small smile forms on Ace's face as the familiar lights of The Royal Flush shine over his face. It's the first smile he's showed out of a ring since Tiffany has been gone. There was always a certain amount of comofort whenever he came to The Royal Flush. He didn't want to go tonight, but his brother and best friend dragged him there. He was starting to feel glad that they did though. Maybe this is exactly what he needed. To get out his house and go back on the hunt for another girl. He just felt wrong doing it, and didn't know why. 'Whooo!!! Looks like we're gonna have some fun tonight, boys.' Joey yells over the music. Joey always was the one to try to lighten a mood. He felt it to be his duty to cheer his big brother up. He knew that it was a hard time for him, and he wants to be there to help him out. 'Alright, drinks are on me tonight, Ace.' Joey says as he leads Ace and Josh towards the bar. Ace surveys the club as they walk. It's kind of a mix between a bar, nightclub and underground shoot fighting club. There was a rather large place set off to the far end of the club, where there was a ring. They'd hold tournaments and what not, where it was basically a fight to a knockout. In fact, Josh was the reigning champion of the league. Josh had always been a badass. Since he was really little, he would always come around Ace's house and hang out with all of Benny Hunter's associates and friends. He was always very interested in the Italian underground, though he had no Italian blood in him at all. He started doing small jobs for Benny when he was only 16. He was very developed for his age, and was very strong. Benny used him as an enforcer of sorts. Anytime something went wrong, he'd send Josh out to crack some skulls. And it was very well known in The Royal Flush that if you were in the opposite corner of Josh Swanson, you weren't walking home on your own power. Ace has always respected Josh for everything he's dealt with in his life, but really doesn't approve of his lifestyle. Josh is Ace's best friend, and trained him in a few forms of Martial Arts. 'So, Ace! Whadya think of all the lovely ladies we got out here, tonight?' Joey urges, trying to get Ace to do his usual scoping. Ace looked, but had Tiffany on his mind, which was very evident to Joey. 'Come on, bro! Let it go. She's gone, it's time for somebody new.' says Joey, 'C'mon, just take a look out on the dance floor. There's gotta be somebody there that you like.' 'Hey, lay off. Let the man grieve for a while.' Josh voices in his deep grading voice. 'He was with her for a long time.' 'It was just some stupid broad!' says Joey, not realizing what he had just gotten himself into. Ace lunged at Joey right about then and firmly gripped a hand around his neck. 'A stupid broad!? Who the fuck do you think you are?' Ace yells, anger flashing. Josh is there in a flash to pull Ace off of Joey. Joey sits in his chair, holding his neck, coughing for breath. 'Damn dude, what's your deal?' He didn't know. Anger was something that the fun loving Ace Cash rarely displayed. But something has snapped inside of him as of late, and it's making him do things he normally wouldn't. It's showed both in and out of the ring. Josh looks at Ace semi-startled at the outburst. 'Are you ok, bro?' Josh asks. Is he? Is this just a phase of breaking up with his girlfriend or is it something much more serious? 'I don't know...' Ace says slowly, 'I just don't fucking know anymore.' -------- Chapter 3 Let me see that sexy body go bump, bump, bump! Ace recites the lyrics in his head as he watches out on the dance floor. Joey was out there with some girl, and Josh was standing off in a corner, with a cigarette hanging out of his mouth. It had been about 45 minutes since Ace's hands were gripped so tight around his little brothers neck. He still didn't know what came over him, but it scared him. He had lost so much control since Tiffany has been gone. Ace ponders all this as he sips on a beer he is holding when he notices a very attractive woman walking from the dance floor in his direction. Her red tank top and short shorts are the first thing that Ace notices. She has a very intruiging walk, that immediately got Ace's attention. He eyed her up and down several times, just to make sure she was real. 'Hi, I'm Tia. You want to dance? You look a little down?' Joey sees as Tia approaches Ace and energetically encourages Ace to go with her. He gestures wildly with his hands, flashing thumbs up and making fists. 'Yeah, sure.' Ace followed her out on the dance floor that night, not really thinking much of it. But as soon as she put her back to his chest and started to dance up on him, he knew that the party wasn't going to end at The Royal Flush. She wanted him, and she wanted him bad. They didn't dance for very long before a group of guys came up and pushed Ace. The one who pushed him faded back into the group of three. The guy in the center was definently the leader of the pack. He was a fairly big sized guy, though he looked like a bit of a hick, he was definently a tough son of a bitch. 'Why are you dancing with my girl, son?' The leader asks with a snarl. Of course, Ace's smoothness came into play right around then, and he went into Ace Cash mode. 'Your girl?' Ace asked looking over his shoulder, 'I don't see any livestock here...' Ace smiles, and mockingly says, 'son.' The leader of the trio laughed at Ace. 'Oh, so your'e a funny one, huh?' He says, lifting a strand of Ace's hair, 'What's this bullshit? Green braids? Are you some kind of a hom-' A booming voice was heard just then that made the leader jump back. 'Hey! Is there a problem here?' Josh. He had seen the group get up from their table and come at Ace. He brought Joey with him. 'No, no problem. Just a friendly discussion.' The leader responds. 'Friendly discussion? It looks to me like you are about to fight my friend here.' Josh replies back, staring the leader down. 'And what if we were?' He responds back, his buddy taps him on the shoulder hard. 'Matt, that's Josh Swanson...the fighter fromt the pit here.' He whispers at the leader, Matt. 'Oh, so you''re Josh Swanson? I've heard a lot of hype about you, son. But my daddy always taught me never to believe the hype.' Matt says, inching forward. A smile came across Josh's face just then, knowing what this was going to turn into. 'Well, if you boys are looking for a fight with my friend here..let's at least make it fair. There's three of you...there's three of us. Sounds like a fit to me.' Josh says moving about 2 inches away from Matt. Matt threw the first punch at Josh. It caused Josh's head to turn to the side slightly. But he just smiled. He turned back at Matt and grabbed him by the shirt and headbutted him about as hard as he could. Matt fell to the ground, and then proceeded to throw a punch at each of his friends, knocking them out. Matt got back to his feet and pulled out a pistol on Josh as he moved closer to him to finish the job. In a flash, Joey opened his leather jacket and had a piece held to Matt's head! 'Do you know who the fuck you're messing with? That's Josh Swanson, and I'm Joey Hunter!' Joey screams 'J-Joey Hunter?' Matt asks cautiously. 'Yeah, that's right. Joey motherf*cking Hunter! And let me tell you something. You don't ever pull a gun out at one of my friends because I will put a bullet in your head just like that! Give me that gun.' Joey demands. Matt immediately handed over the gun. 'Now, put your hands behind your back. NOW!' Matt quickly obliges and puts his hands behind his back. Immediately after doing this, Josh threw a punch dead center in his face, knocking him out. 'Ahh, guys? You think maybe we should get out of here now?' Ace questions. 'Yeah, good idea' Tia says. The four of them barely got in the limo before they heard the sirens. ----- Chapter 4 The phone ringing woke Ace up almost immediately. He'd been waiting for Tiffany to call him for the longest time, and he had trained himself to wake up at the slightest noise just in case. He grabbed the phone on the second ring. 'Hello?' Ace says into the phone. '....Hi...' It's Tiffany, Ace could never forget that voice. 'Tiff, where are you?' Ace asks. 'My sisters.' She answers back. 'When are you coming home?? I really miss you around here.' Ace asks with genuine emotion. 'I've been giving that a lot of thought, baby. And I really want to try and make this work. I just need to know that you're done with all the gambl-' 'Who is that, baby?' Tia asks, rolling over in Ace's bed. Ace will never forget the pain that went through him when he heard the connection on the phone click off.
