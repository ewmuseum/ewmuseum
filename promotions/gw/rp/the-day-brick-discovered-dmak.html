Brick and Aaron had just come off the Q train at Union Square. They headed up the stairs toward the exit near Virgin MegaStore. A quartet of middle-aged men were standing by the exit, singing a capella. They stopped for a moment to listen to the men sing 'Wonderful World'. 'Nothing like the New York Subway system for untapped talent, huh?' 'Yeah, I remember this is where I discovered DMAK.' ---- It was a cold afternoon in February of 2002. Brick had had some business in Manhattan, and he was headed back home to Brooklyn. Taking his car would have been warmer, but he hated driving through Manhattan. So, he decided to take the train from the West 4th Street station. When he went down in the station, he noticed a crowd gathered. As he got closer, he saw four young men in the center, singing a capella. It was a known fact that New York City subway stations held some of the greatest untapped talent there was. Brick was experiencing another example of this fact.
