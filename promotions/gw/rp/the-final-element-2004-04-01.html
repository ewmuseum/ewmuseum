The final element.

1 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 1st, 2004. The day seems like a dream…May 23rd 2003. It was a lovely day in Colorado I had just returned from a tour of Japan, the sun was shining and the birds let their presence be known as their joyous songs filled the air. The sun beat down at a pleasant eighty degrees and I was even in great condition, which was almost surprising considering the things I had done to my body in Japan. That was the day my phone rang and my ex-girlfriend excitedly brought me the phone with the news that Global Wrestling had expressed their interest in me. Only two days later I fulfilled a longtime dream and scrawled my name on a white piece of paper that would sell two years of my life to Global Wrestling. I had reached the highest point of my career at that time. An independent star and international phenomenon I was praised by my peers amongst the Wrestling circuit. Friends had congratulated me; foes had even wished me the best as I embarked on my difficult journey to reach the top of Professional Wrestling. I had trained for a year and Wrestled an additional two years to reach this level and I certainly didn't intend to fail once I had landed in the top tier. The talent that surrounded me was quite stunning and oftentimes I was seen as the perennial loser or underdog…oftentimes I still am the underdog. On May 25th, 2003 my life changed forever as I signed my contract and took one small step for Global Wrestling and one giant leap for my own personal gain. It was all a blur as my first major league contract had sent me reeling. I was content and happy as I worked harder and harder to gain recognition amidst my fellow Wrestler's. Yet the harder I worked the more it seemed I was ostracized for various reasons. Perhaps it was my over abundance of desire for success, my drive for stardom or my insane tendencies for risk taking. I had joined Global Wrestling to do exactly what the marquee said…Wrestle. I began my journey by showing my superiority on so many different levels. High flying, technical, hardcore and martial arts maneuvers that would make Bruce Lee cream in his pants with jealousy. But for so long it seemed as if I simply could not catch a break. The more I worked the further I sunk in the proverbial mire. I paid my dues and earned my spot yet still the bigger stars were credited for their success while I was shoved back into the shadows. The spotlight was a stranger to me as I constantly lurked in the shadows looking for my breakout moment. I took every chance I had to explode but instead of exploding I evolved. I didn't become an overnight success as many before me had done, instead I ascended the ranks pushing myself to heights that were unseen. Yet when it all came down to it…often my hard work wasn't enough. It didn't matter that I could put on a great Wrestling match with the Easter bunny and a drunken Oompa-loompa, what mattered was the fact that in the three ring circus we refer to as Wrestling I was merely a sideshow. A sideshow freak…but I changed all of that at Clash of the Titans…or did I? Blazing through the competition I came out as the surprise victor even going so far as to eliminate my partner in crime, Ken Partridge Jr. But mere hours after I had earned the shot I have worked for nearly a year, news involving my shot surfaced. That news was hard news to bear…I had another obstacle to overcome before chasing the highest prize this industry has to offer. The Global Wrestling World title seemed to be eluding me at every turn as I was booked in this match with Jesse Jamester my hard earned and well deserved World title shot up for grabs. Losing was not an option that I could afford. Ten months of hard work…injuries…mind numbing pain…and endless desire, these things would not be squandered in one night. My long quest would be fulfilled one way or another because I couldn't lose…failure was unacceptable. But to beat a man such as Jesse Jamester was an arduous task. I was faster and far more technically proficient but my game plan was lacking something. High flyer versus brawler…I knew that I must bring another element into play and that would of course be my skills as a martial artist. When he went to brawl I knew I must combat his deadly attacks before I could go to the top rope or take him down to the canvas. When he came out swinging I must have an answer ready. Of course I could always just assume that my skills are fine the way they are…but that would be a foolish assumption. Instead I decided to brush up on certain skills…skills that would be detrimental in my battle with Jesse Jamester. It was time to let 'the way of the foot and open hand' shine through. Of course when you translate 'the way of the foot and open hand' from English into Korean you get one word…Taekwondo. I had begun Taekwondo several years ago and quickly became a devout practitioner of the sacred battle art. I had seen far larger men taken down to nothing courtesy of the effective maneuvers utilized in Taekwondo and the Hapkido self defense that I had been instructed in. It was Taekwondo that strengthened my resolve to become a Professional Wrestler as it gave me both courage and intensity. So when I see an obstacle such as the one that is set in my path it would only make sense to return to my beloved martial art, so that is exactly what I had done. My instructor of years gone by was no stranger to this area yet he diligently taught at his small school in Nebraska. He did however recommend me to a friend and fellow instructor, a man that both he and I could trust…Master Ju Kim. Master Kim stood before me in this empty dojang marching back and forth in front of me quietly as his mind raced undoubtedly conspiring torturous methods of training that would fill my body with pain and flush any weakness from my system. Standing at 5'10 his height was identical to mine so he was the perfect sparring partner. His DoBok consisted of a white V-neck tunic bearing his name etched on the wide lapels in Korean writing. His pants were black the same color of the belt that was tied expertly around his waist…a belt that bore five marks labeling him as a fifth degree black belt. His appearance was only made more impressive by his uniform as the man stood there a thin goatee ran down his chin resting under his bottom lip, his clean-cut black hair was moist with sweat from our tedious workout that had already been in progress for over an hour. At the age of forty-seven Master Kim was experienced far and wide in this art and his expertise shined as I was subject to his instruction. As he paced about in thought I stood at attention my DoBok, which is of course Korean for uniform bore some similarities to his yet it was quite different. Instead of the black pants I had the traditional white with black around my ankles and wrists. My name was etched neatly across the lapels of my uniform and a black belt was neatly tied around my waist. The belt bore two gold marks recognizing my rank as a second-degree blackbelt. My physical appearance was vastly different to Master Kim's as I stood there my bluish gray eyes staring straight ahead, my jaw set and my shaggy black hair dampened with sweat. After what seemed to be much pacing Master Kim finally stopped, pausing for a second he walked slowly across the concrete floor of the Dojang and picked up an unmarked black gym bag. Returning as quickly as he left he dropped the gym bag at my feet. 'Put these on.' He said softly as I quickly proceeded to unzip the bag. Upon discovery of what was in the bag I pulled out the contents which just happened to be pads specifically designed for sparring. He reached in at well his iron hands pulling out a pair of white shin pads and red gloves. I also pulled out shin pads of black and gloves of the matching color. Inside the bag I noticed two sets of headgear as I tugged one out. I stopped as Master Kim's voice abruptly ceased the proceedings. 'No headgear.' He stated firmly yet in a quiet tone. I couldn't help but smile as I zipped the bag shut once more before pulling the shin pads on. The gloves quickly came on as well and as I raised my eyes Master Kim stood before me wearing his gloves and shin pads. He simply nodded at me as he swept the gym bag away with the side of his bare foot. I stood across from him ready to do battle with my superior and I could see he was anxious to spar as his brown eyes sparkled, gleaming with untold wisdom inside. 'Kyung neh' He said using the Korean phrase for bow. I bowed to him and he quickly followed up his first command with a second. 'Joon bi.' Was his second command as I stepped out into my ready stance. I anticipated his next command, which would of course be to commence sparring. Slowly the Korean term for begin rolled off of his lips. 'Shi jock!' He snapped as we each exploded into action. Ever the impetuous one I was the first to strike, lashing out with a high right-legged round kick which was promptly blocked. He fired at me with a round kick of his own that I blocked as well but he quickly switched feet catching me hard in the ribs with a round kick from his left leg. 'Your Wrestling ability is impressive, but can you fight?' He said as he spurred me on. I quickly fired off a spinning outside crescent kick with my right leg and he hopped back avoiding it. 'You came to me because your opponent will surely do battle with his fists, will you be ready?' He inquired as he tested my reflexes with a quick punch to the face that I blocked. I returned his shot with one of my own as I threw an inside crescent that was ducked before turning quickly into a spinning sidekick which he narrowly avoided. 'Can you go blow for blow with this man known as Jamester?' He demanded as he let loose with a flurry of shots, firing off two round kicks that were blocked before catching me in the stomach with a wheel kick. He came in again with a round kick but I was quick to jump back and leap up with a jump kick, catching him off guard and nearly blasting my instructor in the face before he could slap my foot away. As I landed I quickly fell prey to a hard sidekick to the ribs which was quickly carried over into a hook kick as I felt his bare foot smack me on the back of my skull. 'Are you entering this contest of skill with the set of mind to win, or just not to lose?' He shouted as he threw a jumping front kick at me which I narrowly avoided. I knew his questions were only to test me and make me think as I answered his inquires each time with a retaliation blow. My answer to this question was a knife hand strike that he blocked before I spun around into a spinning sidekick catching him in the stomach and sending him staggering in reverse. 'Will you become the victor or will you fail in your conquest?' He demanded of me as he fired a mid section punch which I blocked before whipping around with a heel kick that he barely ducked. In my haste to land this blow I had failed to keep my arms up and protect myself. Before I could even fully realize what the white blur streaking towards my face was his foot collided with the side of my head as I went crashing to the ground via a hard round kick! My body slammed down hard on the concrete floor as he stood over me. A smile danced across his lips as I groaned in pain. Looking down at me he reached forth his gloved hand and helped me to my feet. I slumped over trying to regain the breath that had been driven from my body. Master Kim however seemed to be all the more amused at this scenario. With a broad grin across his face he slapped me playfully across the back. 'I certainly hope you haven't planned prior engagements tonight because we're going to be here a while.' He softly stated. 'Yes master.' Was all that I could muster between painful breaths. Perhaps the training that would continue nonstop was rigorous and painful but it would all be beneficial in the end. Because tonight I would add the final element to my game plan…the final element that would lead me to destiny.
