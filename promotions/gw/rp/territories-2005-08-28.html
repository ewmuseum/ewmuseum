Territories

28 August 2005

Date: 8/28/05 Josh didn't know if he should be... well, he didn't know what he should be feeling right now. The events of last week were still catching up to him. Mostly those outside of the ring. Dani was still being tight lipped about what had happened in her room when Josh and Kaylee had gotten there. Josh knew something had happened. He had almost heard her calling his name about the time they were assuming it had happened. But he'd not listened and a few hours later they found her, unconscious on her floor in only her towel. Yeah, he had a habit of drifting off lately, the dreams had to be getting stronger. But there was one thing that Josh really didn't like about it all.. the smell that permeated the room, the smell of Sulpher. I couldn't have been from the irrigation system. Weeks here in Vegas, it had never made it up to her suite. Something wasn't going right, and Kaylee had agreed with Josh. Shortly after Dani had left, Josh had gotten one of his old books out and looked up what they needed. Kaylee assisted with getting the required stuff, basically pure sea salt and they had conducted a very simple ritual to protect her place. Kaylee, being a rep of the company that was paying for their current living arrangements, also managed to ensure when house keeping would be going to the room, so that it could be renewed each time when Dani wasn't there. Some others things, Dani didn't even notice, such as Kaylee managing to find a Native 'souvenir' store down on Freemont street that actually sold smudge pots and wands. They managed to get Kaylee to give it to her as a gift so that Dani could carry out her normal spiritual things. Dani took the gift with a non-feigned concern. It was as if she knew exactly what she'd do with it, because she was keeping stuff from them again. That was the one thing that made him sometimes want to just take Dani over his own knee. Even though he was only a few days older then her, she was sometimes too independent for her own good. .... Josh took a mental break for a moment, and adjusted the sound of the music that was playing in his ears as he walks northward on the Las Vegas Strip. A town that never sleeps, a city that the life becomes more abundant once the sun goes down. His long raven black hair is pulled back into on neatly done pony tail. His skin, face and build is much like Gray's and his father's build was, as far as Josh knows. From what Gray has told him he is a spitting image of their father. All but his eyes are the only thing that give him away; soft and green like his mother's. He is dressed from head to foot in black, his favorite color. His mind full of things, but mostly on a young girl that at his young age, he's sworn himself to. Even if he has to wait for a long, long time for her. He walks just to walk tonight, with maybe a few stops for himself; no meetings, no interviews to get to. But there is one thing he wishes he did have, his driver's license. Not that is wasn't his fault he didn't have it to begin with. Doing over a hundred in a sixty on Interstate-18 down the mountain side, inin his friends brand new corvette, wasn't the smartest thing in the world... but it was fun. That was him He liked to live in the edge, he liked the rush. Sometimes he could show his true side, that of someone who calculates every move if he can, but that usually got caught up in the want to be spontaneous and impulsive at the same time. He looked up and saw his destination, The Stratosphere Hotel and Casino. At the top was a roller coaster he was in the mood to just have a little fun. Danielle refused to even go to the top to the observation tower, the view was much like the one from the space needle in Seattle, breath taking. It was also the one thing in this town that reminded him of home. The Stratosphere was a direct mock up of the Space Needle in Downtown, Seattle. He walked, listening to his music as he passed the Treasure Island, it's long standing show of Pirates and Privateers dark for a few months now, now nothing much happening. Beyond that, Caesar's, Bellagio and then the Strat was but a few short blocks after that. Being the tallest building in Vegas though, it stuck out already at him, beckoning him to his few moments that he could claim to being something like home. Josh couldn't say he wasn't happy with the outcome of last week's match, winning against the tag champions was a big boost. Especially for him, when it was realized that the move his brother always teased him about, the modified strangle hold gamma, managed to get a tap out. It was a good thing for them, for not only did they win, but it was Josh who took the victory lap on it. But he felt bad for Danielle, and her match with Biddle, how ever close it was to a win for her, she still lost it. And, lost it due to a rookie mistake. Sometimes she forgets that she's only been in the business for a little over a full year outside of the school. Then again her rapid rise to success in other places didn't prepare her for some place like Global. He knew she was beating her self up for that one. Extra hours in the gym, and more watching the tapes to see where she made her mistake. so she could work it out. All business for her, never much fun, then again her idea of relaxing was taking a tent and sleeping bags from the back of her truck and setting up camp some place. Josh thought back to the first day he saw her come into the school. A scared, shy young woman clinging to her grandfather like a child clinging to her fathers coat. begging him not to leave her again. It was also the first time he had ever seen him, the Keeper himself, and his first wife. There was something about them both, that just seemed to have a relaxing effect on her. So after about an hour meeting Danielle was escorted into her first class by the Headmaster, a man who's now the Keepr's brother-in-law. That first class was Gray's, the gaggle of girls in the corner of the class room just giggled at her as she took her seat, quietly. She waited her turn as the rest clumsily went through the moves that Gray had shown them, when it was her turn, she stepped up and showed everyone why the Keeper, himself, had placed her in this class, her moves where fluid almost flawless, text book moves. Shutting up the gaggle of giggling girls,and locking her into Josh's heart forever. Josh himself was a casualty of Danielle's charms in the training ring. As would be most men that were alive from the waist down. Josh chuckled to himself as he thought about the day that Danielle bested him, as mad as he was at himself for falling for it, he took Gray and the Headmaster's lectures of “keep your mind on what your doing in the ring, and I was distracted is an excuse” with a grain of salt. But the sense of values that they taught at the school were above and beyond anything Josh had seen in the ring yet. The “No Interference” rule was the hardest one to hammer into the heads. Or as the Headmaster would say, “Don't let 'hem get a chance to have an excuse, as to why you beat their arse.” But to Josh, it was all a daily normal thing for him. His mother had taken him when their father had died, and moved back across the water to the big city of Seattle. He had been 11, and Gray was 18 and getting married in a few weeks after graduating from High School. Once over in the city though, Josh came to find out more about his mother then he had in his younger years. As a kid he grew up in the halls of the Order. He was there when the new Lady took her spot next to her husband for the first time, he was there when his first wife passed away. His mother kept the going ons in the Order quiet from him. But Josh's own personal life style kept him on top of things. He knew about things long before his big brother did. In fact, he didn't even know if Gray knew what had really gone on with even himself when the Order offered him a spot within their group. When his brother reached the age of majority, and started to change, it was Josh that went to their... no, His, mother to have her help him. But he would never admit that to his brother, he let's him keep thinking the Order came to him. Josh had his little secrets and he liked it that way. There were things about him that no one else, except maybe one, would ever get to know. However, one thing Josh didn't like was this man named Jaymes. There was something that just irked him about that guy, but Josh couldn't put a finger on it. For one Jaymes was way to eager to get close to Danielle. And way to eager to point a finger as Gray. in blame to what is happening to her. Josh was glad that his mother was only a phone call away, getting the information he needed to protect Danielle even with out her knowing about it was easy. After they did the salt ceremony in the room, even Kaylee said that she felt unwelcome in it. Kaylee was a pretty neutral to everyone, which meant that it would keep the real nasties, as Josh called them, out and away from her. When they went to inform Annie as to what they thought had happen, Josh wasn't so surprised at his big brothers reactions. The first one was to drag Danielle in and make her tell the truth. This was pointed out as not so good idea via Lady Leanan, do to the fact you cant make someone tell you what they don't want remember. And, his second reaction was him offering to camp outside her room every night just in case she needed protection. This was also was pointed out as not a good idea to have happen, because it would let whom ever was up to no good, know they were on to them. Both responses left Gray mumbling to himself. And that is when Jaymes stepped in... the slippery... slimy snake of a person that the girls seem to turn into goo over, and the men just hated by looking at him. Jaymes volunteered his services to assist her and maybe to try to bring some new light to things. His being almost the same nation as Danielle, knew the old rituals that could call forth the right ancestors to help find out what was going on with her. But, it would needed to be done on scared lands. Normally this would be a problem, but we're in the middle of the South Western Mojave Desert, with a strong tribal heritage already. In fact there was the perfect spot right in Down Town, Las Vegas. A big thorn in the side of the normal, non-Tribal, leadership and commerce groups here was the fact that there was a 20 acre spot right in the middle of everything that was tribal land. Not only was it Tribal Land, but being a cemetery with hundreds of Paiute buried there, it was holy land for them also. Jaymes had called for the ceremony to be held at dusk, a few days later. When Annie conceded that it might be a good idea, but making sure that she didn't know too much about it at all, he left to go get things set up. Leaving Josh with a unsettling feeling about him, even more so then ever. Although it was passed off as a he was just reading the mercenary part of the man, which just no matter what showed on him. “He's a specialist, Joshua. He's not with us, but there are very few experts on tribal history and legends out there. Especially ones who have been involved in it at long as him. We pay him in things that he can't get elsewhere, and he works for us,” is how Annie had explained it to him. Jaymes was a greedy sort, and was only out for himself. But, he was working for the Order, well paid, so there was no worries. But, that is exactly what worried Josh, Jaymes was a mercenary's, working for whom ever paid him more. And who was to say that was not happening now. All Josh knew was Jaymes needed to be watched closely... Very Closely. As he got closer to the Tower that was his destination, something else caught his eye. A sign that said “Diversity” on the top. A tattoo parlor he'd heard about on his local radio station, Xtreme Radio X107.5. He stepped in to have a look around. He knew he was going to be in the need of getting another tattoo as soon as his back healed, so he figured he better start looking around. There was a lady standing at the front desk, well tattooed and, pierced more times then Josh cared to try to count. She greeted him with a friendly smile, first thing he noticed was that her front teeth had been cut. ?Greeting Sir, can I help you?? the lady breaks into his concentration. Josh removes his headphones from his ears, dropping them to his shoulders, before answering her, ? Just looking around, going to be shopping for a new one here soon.? She stays polite, especially with the prospect of a sale coming up, ?Okay sir, feel free to browse if you have any questions anyone can help you. Anything in particular you looking for?? Josh looks up at some of the samples on the wall, being a typical male, he stops at some of women in various states of undress. He then looks at her to respond, “A full one for my back.” The lady smiled at him, he was talking more her speed of money spending. She got up to see if there was anything she could help him with, ?You have something in mind or what?? Josh flipped through the hanging slates of samples, paying attention to them suddenly, ? Kind of. But, it's more of a personal design then anything else.? Josh reached into his pocket and pulled out a piece of folded paper, and handed it to the lady. She unfolded it and looked at the hand drawn picture for a few seconds, “Wow! That's pretty. But I can tell you now that something this elaborate and the size you want it is going to take a while and it is going to cost. I'd guess: some place in the two grand range, and most people prefer a few settings.” Josh nodded his head, at her, ?That's to be expected. I've already got a few on me. But I recently had an accident that caused some pretty bad scaring on my back. I'm going to want to cover them up. I've heard Nevada has limits to how long a seating can be as it is, correct?? She answered, still looking down at his drawing, “Yes, no more then 4 hours every 48. I'd expect that to be at least 3 seatings, if not 4 or 5. Depends on how well the ink takes and if there are any complications. Scar tissues is sometimes harder to get the ink to set into.” He answered in a way that kinda passed her off, “To be expected. Thank you” He grabbed the paper from her hands, then turned and walked out the door again, heading for his original destination, only now about a block away. As he starts to cross the road one last time he hears the sound of Queensryche's “Sign of the Times” play out from his belt. His cell phone is going off. He pulls it out and looks at the screen to see who's calling, and his eyes roll as he flips it open. He spoke into the phone, “Yeah, big brother, what do you want? You know I was guaranteed a bit of time alone tonight.” A muffled man's voice from the other side of the phone can be heard but not really made out to what is being said. Best described as the voice of the teacher in Charlie Brown cartoons. Josh's responses come quickly as he's told something that sounds like it's upsetting him. ?He is doing it tonight?? The voice speaks up again, with more of a commanding voice to give Josh the idea that he is being TOLD. ?What the hell is the rush I thought it took time setting stuff like this up? What time is he planing on it?? The voice makes a few more comments before Josh interrupts one more time. “Doesn't give much time for me to get there, luckily I am not to far away I still don't like him, but this is for her. So I'll be there with bells on.” Josh folds his phone back up and places it back in his jean pocket, his face twisted in displeasure as he looks at the top of the Stratosphere tower and says something, as if to the tower itself... “Later.” Josh then looks around and sees the Porte Cochier of the Paris Hotel and Casino. He heads right for it, for the army of taxi cabs that wait for people leaving the casino on a 24/7 schedule. The driver of one of the cars acknowledges the man trotting towards him and opens a door. As he does, he asks, ?Where to sir?? Josh sits right down on the chair as he gives his destination, “Down town, Paiute smoke shop. Near the old cemetery.” The driver double takes on him for a second, before closing the door, “Sir just so you know it closed about an hour or so ago.” Josh leans into the lights from above a bit, to show his features to the man, “I know, just meeting some friends there.” The Cabby isn't going to argue much with a very native person asking to go to tribal land. He walks around the car and climbs into the driver's seat. He turns on the car and hits the button that puts his car lights showing that he's with a fare and starts the meter. Josh sat back and relaxed a bit, since the taxi drivers here are probably the only thing that was much better then Seattle's drivers. None of them here will drive the wrong way down a one way street, yelling out the window at people to get out of the way. All the while, their passengers white knuckled and holding onto the 'oh-shit' handles in the back. Not fifteen minutes later, the cab was arriving at the gates to the Paiute Smoke Shop and the small contingent of tribal locals that called this spot home. It was basically a mini casino that sold cigarettes with no taxes, two trailer parks and a huge cemetery that had been used by their people for centuries at least, if not longer. The driver gave him a warning about walking in this part of town at night as he took Josh's money. Josh just nodded his head and walked over the the group of people that where standing at the gate to the cemetery. As he got closer he could make out Dani standing in the middle of the group with Gray, Annie, Kaylee and Jaymes flanking her. All of them looked more like they were on guard then standing with friends. Gray spoke up, trying to break the obvious tension going on, “Didn't think you were going to make it little brother, you know if you had your license still you wouldn't have to rely on cabs to get every place.” Josh just shrugged and walked over to Danielle who was standing in a strange mixture of lights. One from up in the sky, the other from a barely glowing street light above her. The effect didn't change the fact that she was a beautiful, statuesque woman. She was dressed casual, jeans and t-shirt. Her long hair pulled back to her normal squaw braids. But Josh was concerned with the look on her face, she looked more like she was being forced then anything to come here. “You okay? You don't have to do this, you know,” Josh said under his breath to her. She glanced up to his eyes, looking to take some comfort from his presence, “It's okay. We need to do this before more things start happening. I'm tired of being scared.” At that, Jaymes took out a set of keys and unlocked the large master lock that held a chain around the gate and fence. The others followed the man to the center of the grave yard, where they saw that he had already set up the equipment he would need. What looked like a small alter with a couple of other implements was sitting there. The full view came into place as Jaymes lit a couple of lanterns that were stored there. “You 4 can hold out over there. I need only Ms. Northsky over here,” Jaymes said, motioning to an area that was outside of the markings he had done on the ground, “This is going to take a few moments. It shouldn't be too bad though.” Josh put his headphones back onto his head, part as a way to pass the time and part as a way to show disrespect for the man who was the master of ceremonies right now. He watches as the man took a small clipping of Dani's hair and put it into a small bone made bowl. Mixing it with a few powdered items and a liquid, he mixed it in and then started his chanting. Josh wasn't familiar with the Sioux languages outside of their songs, which were, admittedly, the same in most of the nations. He heard the dull sounds of the man as he started his chanting. He looked quickly over at his brother and the other two. Gray was yawning, obviously as bored as he was. Kaylee was looking around, the ever vigilant guardian of her Mistress. Annie was looking on intently, with a confused look on her face. Josh knew that Annie didn't know much about what was going on, and that was his guess as to why they were so confused when it came to Dani. They were admittedly 'mushrooms' when it came to native myths and such. Mushrooms in the line that they had always been kept in the dark and fed 'crap'. . It appeared to be going very slow and drawn out, Josh swore he dozed off while he was standing a couple of times. He was getting ready to speak up and say what he thought of everything, when he suddenly heard Dani scream over the sound coming from his head phones. He snapped into the mode to look around, Kaylee was in a ready stance. Not a fighting one, but one that would make her ready for just about anything. Gray had gone into a fighting stance, and his face was showing signs of fighting off the change that would make him something else. Annie was staring at something intently. Then Josh looked over and noticed the form that was but a few score feet from Dani and Jaymes. A creature that looked like a spider, with a bed of tentacles coming off of it's back was breaking it's way out of the ground. “What the fu...” was all Josh got out before he started to move towards Dani. Jaymes spun around and put his hand up to him, ?Don't you dare break the line. We're safe as long as we're in the circle. You break it, it'll allow it to enter!? Josh stopped, and looked at Dani, as she was beginning to squirm. Jaymes had his other hand on her shoulder, forcefully holding her down in place. “Don't even show any fear. Don't move...” were the instructions he head James say to her. Josh turned off the CD player, leaving his headphones on. He wanted to be able to hear, but give the indication that he couldn't still. The first thing he heard normally again, was his brother, sniffing the air. Then it him him also. A smell he'd only touched upon recently, in Dani's room... Sulpher... They were downwind from the creature, and it was stuck on the other side of the circle as Jaymes had called it. “These demons seem to have a vested interest in this ceremony. They're going to keep the ancestors away from us for now. This ceremony can't finish tonight,” Jaymes said, turning his head over his shoulder to speak directly to Lady Leanan. Annie started to mumble something in her native Irish, and her hands started to move, but Jaymes put his hands up to her also, “My dear, may I remind you that you're not Native. You could make matters worse if you continue.” He then turned his attention back to Dani, putting his hand back onto her shoulder, and holding her in place, “I'll take care of this.” Dani didn't say a word to him, just nodded. Josh had never seen her so scared before. Josh watched, as Jaymes unbuttoned his thousand dollar jacket and pulled out what could only be described as a wand with a bird's skull upon it, and a set of feathers. The man then pulled something off of a pouch on his belt and opened it by pulling off a cork. It was a ceramic made bottle. He poured the powder out a bit, to get the flow going, and then tossed the flask at the... thing. As soon as it hit the back of the creature, it broke and the powder contained within went up in a cloud of dust. Jaymes then started his chanting again, using the wand to emphasize his words. Quickly, in a flash of light the creature disappeared... Josh stood there, frozen to keep things from getting worse, but he watched the man who had just done the 'banishment' for any tell tale signs. “It's gone,” Jaymes suddenly said, mostly to Dani who was on her knees, next to his makeshift alter. Jaymes put his hands out and helped Dani up to her feet. “Thank you, Jaymes,” Dani said to him, looking up to his eyes. Josh instantly wanted to run over and punch the man, but he knew that wouldn't get the reaction that he would want out of Dani. ?What was tha', Jaymes?? Jaymes turned around to address the lady. “That was a servant of Ilya, the demon of Chaos. It would appear they have a personal association with this. This is not good. I will need to do more research before we try something of this again,” Jaymes said, his hand going to Dani's shoulder for comfort. Josh watched as that hand went from her shoulder to her lower back. Annie gave the answer that Jaymes wanted, “Yes, by all means. I'm not one for combating demons I know not much about. I'm glad to have an expert on this subject here. If there is anything the Order can help you with, let me know. Especially resources.” “I may need access to your libraries. That is all I would need from you and the Order,” was Jaymes' answer. Dani's eyes were darting back and forth, looking for anything else that could happen tonight. Josh thought on it just a bit and realized what had exactly happened in her room. He gave a knowing look towards Kaylee, who nodded to him. The smell of something Infernal still hung in the air with the stench of rotten eggs. Josh's mind began to wonder on it all, he didn't know how long. He snapped out of it when he heard Jaymes mutter some other words towards Dani. ?Ms. Northsky, I may need to address you with some questions also. Directly, would you be available to answer them as I do my research?? Dani nodded her head, “Yes.” Then Josh's blood began to really heat up as he saw Jaymes take both of Dani's hands and hold them up in front of him, clasping them inside of his own, “Well then, I'd have to say that is good news. What would you say to dinner? Maybe next Tuesday Night, I know this night restaurant in town that would be the perfect environment for our discussions.” Dani visibly began to blush as she looked at the man that looked like the native version of Fabio, “Yeah, sounds like a plan.” Josh didn't like that answer one bit. His fists clenched, involuntarily. “I would advise now, it'll be a formal dinner. If you need any assistance in making the accommodations for that, just let me know,” Jaymes said as he then handed Dani a card. Jaymes then turned to everyone, “Now, you'll all excuse me. But I must get started while I have time. Lady Leanan knows how to contact me if you have any questions.” Jaymes then immediately turned and started to leave. Josh muttered under his breath, “How about this one? What are you up to, mercenary...” Gray put his hand on his Josh's shoulder. “It'll be okay bro. Once this is all done, he'll be gone. Just remember, don't do anything stupid that would hurt Dani, too.” Josh remembered that his brother's hearing was much better then his own. “All I know is that this guy is shady, and makes used car salesmen and lawyers look honest. I don't trust him and you can't make me,” Josh said, glaring in the direction of Jaymes. Kaylee then spoke up, as she had moved to Josh's side, “We'll make sure we double up on the defenses on 'er room. I'll convince me Mistress to also enlist the aid of some of 'er servants. This area has Ravens and Crows just as much as her homeland. We'll keep an eye on things.” “That's not what I'm worried about. I'll see you guys later. I've got my own date with a location that I'd better keep tonight,” Josh said as he started to walk off, leaving the others. Gray shook his head. Kaylee looked confused, ?What's his problem?? Gray answered her as he scanned the area, “He's seeing the green monster here. He thinks someone might be moving in on his territory.” Kaylee was still confused, “But Dani has said that she isn't looking for romance. She's all business at this moment.” “He's keeping his hopes up she'd change her mind. But he's hoping that she doesn't change it for someone else, like Jaymes,” Gray said as he watched his brother walk away. “Jaymes Tirawa, would not step into anything like that. The man has his price, and it's being paid. He's not changed that price since the days of the first European Settlers, I don't see him changing it now,” Kaylee observed, innocently. ?He's immortal?? Gray suddenly asked? “No, he's not a primal. He's just very long lived. But the measures at which he keeps his longevity include rare materials that can only be obtained from the other side of the veil, such as primal elements and oricalcum metal. We're his best source for them, so if he were to mess up anything with the Order, he'd eventually find himself growing old, just like everyone else. Don't fret. Hopefully your brother is only seeing envy. I would hate for him to ruin this entire mission.” Gray hears her words, but his mind is itself wondering, as he looks in the direction of the woman he's here to train and protect. ~end~ ~for now~
