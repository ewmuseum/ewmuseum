Return of the Lackey

30 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 30, 2005 Mike had finally completed one of the most important steps to reclaiming his fame and fixing his small problem with the ladies. Becoming the number one contender to Global Wrestling’s Television Championship had put his name on the tip of just about every woman’s tongue and also proved that his fifteen minutes of fame weren’t up just yet. The fact that Partridge was also the World Champion brought Mike an even greater deal of attention and his career had no where to go but up once he defeated Ken. The Four Corners match on Evolution gave the possibility of Mike improving his standing within the ranks of Global Wrestling by giving him the chance to defeat the current Cruiserweight Champion, the prot’g? of “The Ladies Man” Jay Robson, and the up and coming Mindy Mercer. Once the dust settled Mike would be the last man standing; thus giving a message to all of Global Wrestling that he IS Mr. Main Event and if they cared for their health they’d try to avoid being booked in matches against him. ***** Mike relaxed in the plush leather armchair in the living room of his Beverly Hills home. It was the first time in the last few months that he had truly been at ease for he was now finally comfortable. He knew now that he hadn’t lost his touch and that he could once again have any woman he wanted...well except for Mindy Mercer but it was only a matter of time before she came around. His dark blue eyes began to feel heavy and he could feel a deep sleep coming on. Just as he was about to close his eyes his descent to the dream world was interrupted by the sound of the James Bond theme as it blasted from the pocket of his dark blue jeans. With a sigh he reached down and pulled the obscenely expensive phone from his pocket and answered it. “What?” Mike lazily said. “Lad,” said the light Scottish accent of William Bastafar, “I need you to come pick up my bloody dumb grand son and me from the hospital. It seems that my limousine driver drove over some nails on the road and popped the damned tires!” “Get a taxi,” Mike yawned. “No,” came a mortified gasp from William, “I will not accept a ride from some non-English speaking barbarian with a cheap automobile that has a vomit stained interior. You have nothing else to do today lad, so get off your lazy bottom and get over here now.” William hung up the phone after he finished his sentence. “Damn that evil Scotsman!” Mike exclaimed under his breath. He rose from his comfortable position on the chair and walked towards the door that led to his garage. As he made his way to his destination he readjusted the officially licensed “Mr. Main Event is Marvelous” t-shirt that bore his resemblance underneath the words to a more comfortable position on his body. Mike flicked on the light switch once he entered the damp garage and he looked over his choice of vehicle. At this juncture in time Mike only had two vehicles stored at his home: a silver Aston Martin V12 Vanquish and a white Blazer. Mike chose the Aston Martin; the car was quicker and turned more heads than a boring Blazer. He entered the vehicle and sped off towards the hospital that Carlos had stayed at during his recovery from the coma caused by 4WW superstar Fnord in an impromptu extreme match during Mike’s PTC Extreme title reign. It didn’t take long for Mike to reach the hospital, and when he reach his destination he found William Bastafar standing beside a wheelchair bound Carlos Fraga. Mike came to a screeching halt in front of them and motioned from the to enter the car. Carlos had to squeeze in the back with his folded up wheelchair and William sat in the front, directly in front of his grandson and his seat was pushed back as far as possible; effectively squishing the immobile Carlos. Carlos was a short and chubby Spaniard boy with a dark complexion that caused many to confuse him for a Mexican. Carlos was also extremely stupid and fit the role of Mike’s idiotic comic relief perfectly. As Mike glanced in the rearview mirror and got a good look at the dolt for the first time in months a cunning plan sprang to life within his mind. “William,” Mike said, “when will he be able to walk again?” “Another week or so,” William replied. “Good,” Mike said with a smirk. In the backseat Carlos said nothing, he wanted to ask why Mike was looking at him so strangely and if he could have a cup of hot cocoa once he got home but his throat was so dry that he couldn’t speak. Carlos thought he knew what was going on and he had the feeling that it involved him getting another beating like the one he got from Fnord. He sighed internally and began to wish that he had stayed in Boston with his mother. He hoped Mike didn’t ask him to do anything stupid since he knew he couldn’t say no since his Grandfather would be more disappointed in him than he already was and that was the last thing he needed. When would it all end he wondered as the Aston Martin sped off into the distance. -To Be continued-
