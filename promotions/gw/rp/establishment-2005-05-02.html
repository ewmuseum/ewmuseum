Establishment

2 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05-02-05 It was once written that every man has to go through hell to reach paradise’what then of the man who finds paradise in hell? --- I stared into my hands. Analysing the intricate contours of the dry, cracked skin around my fingers at where everything had almost slipped away from me. Clenching my fists I brought them to my face, closing my eyes and exhaling softly as though in prayer. Saturated in darkness I sighed and extended an arm before me, punching the Power button on the TV/VCR. As light fizzled into the room with the accompaniment of static crackling I sat back and watched as the tape automatically played. Placing a hand on my leg I gently rubbed the source of much of my troubles. An intense training session at the close of last year had left me with torn muscle damage to my hamstring. Any hope of wrestling was dashed, and life spiralled into a quagmire. As the fans yelled and cheered on the tape I sneered begrudgingly at the two men exchanging blows in the ring. The expressions told so much; on the outside a manifested hatred for their respective foe, but on the inside shone a happiness I longed for. To return to the ring and to the sport I loved. Never had an injury niggled for so long, and how I loathed it so. My reputation was among other worries slipping through my fingers. I felt tainted watching the Global wrestlers before me, at how they would think of me. I cared simply for the lack of respect I was sure to be shown upon my eventual entrance to the federation. Perhaps they didn’t care; maybe they thought highly of me’whatever the case I would go in with guns blazing. Win or lose, I had cultivated a range of manoeuvres that would see my mark left on any opposition. My staying power in any federation was not just rooted in my presence, but the fact I did what no other could. Sure there had been wrestlers as big and bad as me before, but none of them could compare to my style. Ivan Stanislav could laugh hard and lead armies, Meanstreak was crazy and threw a lot of vending machines around and John Mammoth could kill an opponent in one punch, albeit that punch took half a week to connect’but none of them possessed the level of athleticism I portrayed for much of my career. Be it soaring off the top rope, drawing blood and guts in a hardcore rumble or showcasing my innovation I was what the people wanted: something different. With my leg healing it wouldn’t be long before the Murder Show resumed its course and Eternal Damnation was established as the new threat to the company. While I absorbed everything on the television screen, my attention was not fully focused on the first opponent I would face in a long time. From what I saw of Train I knew I could expect less than what was brought by the likes of Violence Jack in Rush Pro Wrestling. Elsewhere in my mind the trials and tribulations of a possible business venture lurked in thought. During rehabilitation I had learned of some valuable real estate coming to auction belonging to a former “acquaintance”, two properties actually. One of which I gained a vested interest in, the other I would never make the mistake of stepping in, and they were the studio flat and the small office building formerly of one James Carlisle. Perhaps my greatest nemesis beyond the mat, Carlisle was the reason behind my existence today? The subject of Carlisle was a sensitive subject with me and most knew it. The man that slaughtered my family in the name of the almighty dollar, Carlisle was a nail in my side later in life during the most prestigious moments of my career’when I was graced with the PTC Global Title. During a rather vulnerable period of my life the filthy loan shark made it known that an old debt was still to be paid, and that I, Angelus Von Kelsig, son of Jack Kelsig was the man to pay it. Fortunately I defeated Jesse Jamester to claim the Global Title and paid off my father’s debt, closing that fateful chapter in my life. As time passed though I began thinking vengefully towards Carlisle’a man who had taken my family and now a vast amount of money that would have been mine. With this newfound hatred for the man, it was on the eve of my acceptance to Global that I lurked in the shadows and with little more than a sharp breath and a tight noose took Carlisle’s life from him. Vengeance had been served on some level, but now’now I saw an opportunity to take full revenge on my tormentor. In so many years I had never seen myself doing anything other than wrestle. I surprised myself when I decided to follow up this property for sale and was even more surprised to discover the estimate of its worth at auction wasn’t beyond what I could afford. Being incapacitated with my injury I felt rather than risk any further strain to bid over the telephone. The competition was tough but by a margin of $25,000 I acquired what would come to be known’as the Slaughter House. The business of sitting out with an injury and investing my time and money into a project such as this may seem trivial to anyone taking interest, but that is why being Hessian is so much fun. Nothing is what it seems. Jesse Jamester has a habit of telling people to “Expect the Unexpected”, yet if that were so then the unexpected would surely become the expected’ergo the element of surprise is lost. With myself, it’s simply a case of wait and see. For my plan to take off I would require the assistance of someone whom I could trust in a business sense. Very few people met this requirement, and those that did I knew wouldn’t let me down. As I sat contemplating my options, I reached for the telephone and slowly dialled, wondering exactly just what I was doing. On the one hand the plans I had for the short term would ensure my reputation as a sadistic evil bastard would flourish into something darker, while on the other I also realized I had a handy little hobby to pursue should I ever step away from the ring. I smiled knowing this, knowing that soon I could wrestle simply for the love of it, without the dependence of money. I was well off after a successful stint in OSW, and to this day the odd royalty saw my bank balance increase that little bit more. “Hello?” came the voice on the other end of the receiver. I snapped from my little daze and took a breath. “Hello, Bruce.” I replied, knowing that he would recognize the deep voice from our encounters in the ring on previous occasions. “Well, do my ears deceive me or has the great Angelus Von Kelsig graced me with a phone call” Finally decide to pledge your allegiance to the Sect?” Bruce joked, and from his tone I could tell his views hadn’t changed much. “Actually I have a business proposition for you.” I cut straight to the point, avoiding any idle chitchat. “I’ve only recently bought myself a little property in Detroit and I have high aspirations for it. You’ll find it very interesting I’m sure, as will you find the deal I propose to you-?” “Violence Jack and Hessian, business partners?” Bruce cut me off. “This must be an interesting venture indeed’what kind of establishment will this be?” “Well’firstly are you willing to invest upwards of $600,000 if this appeals to you?” I said, making it clear to him that what I wanted would come before what he wanted, even if I needed him. “It would have to be irresistible to me Von Kelsig. But please go on.” “Oh, I think you’ll find it’s right up your alley, Shanahan. You’re going to love this?” And so I divulged my plans for the Slaughter House to him. If anyone would appreciate what I had in mind, it would be someone as cutthroat and bloodthirsty as Violence Jack. My diabolical scheme would soon be realized, and only the most unfortunate would know of it. Train would only discover the top of the iceberg, neither he nor anyone else exploring what lay beneath the murky depths’until I let them. To unleash my wrath beyond the ring unto those who posed no realistic threat to me would be to take a path I could not return from. To feed my bloodlust beyond those I wrestled in order to vanquish them, and make a profit from it. Of all that I had been through over the years, finally everything would happen at once. It would see the culmination of capitalism and violence in the midst of the ominous Global federation. Now was the time for me to re-establish myself in the food chain, to return to healthy physical competition and to begin climbing the business ladder. On Evolution I would establish myself as the threat I had been described as and thought of by the roster, and when I wasn’t in the ring I would be working to make my establishment something to be proud of. Once again I would delve into the depths of human depravation and come out smiling. No one would know of my intentions, for after all these are my thoughts, and my thoughts alone... The story my fists will tell’will be an entirely different, bloody epic.
