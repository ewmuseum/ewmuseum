If you do not enjoy this type of writing, you've been warned ahead of time. ~En Route~ ~Earlier this week~ The Metallic Blue Toyota Highlander whipped down the back roads of Las Vegas, with a complete disregard for any posted speed limits. At this time of night, it didn't' matter much though. Metro was concerned with the Las Vegas Strip, especially at night. The heat of the day rendered the normal hours of any other city completely inhospitable. At night, that's when everyone went out, that's when most of the relations time with the tourist public was meant to happen. The outlaying areas were mostly industrial, support for the decadent core of the city with a buffer of residential communities to keep the tourists from seeing what it took to keep everything going. Josh was actually quite the expert driver. He'd won a few trophies at the Bremerton and Everett raceways back in Washington State, the place that they'd be heading in just a few days for Global's Whiplash Pay Per View. But right now, he was on a mission. It didn't matter to him that he'd not had a license to drive a car in years after his incident with some of King County's finest while on a two lane road, drag racing down the side of a mountain... This was a matter of saving Dani. That love of cars and the faster side of Seattle would do him, and her well here. However, it wasn't doing his fully aware passenger any good. As Josh got to the point where he was passing the Air Force Base testing range, Dani started to stir and wake. It was still in the 70's, but would soon be rapidly approaching the low 90's that were the norm for September. A car, what looked like a rental jeep, pulled up to the location of the tent setup. Dani's truck was sitting there and everything was in the place it was left in when Josh had retired for the evening. Two people got out of the rental car, Gray Brand and Kaylee. Kaylee though looked a bit different. Instead of her normal blonde tresses, they looked to be dyed green in color, yet still looking natural. ?Alright, where are you, you little shit?? Gray yelled out, trying to draw attention to the fact that they were there. ?JOSHUA!? He yelled out again when he didn't get any answer. ?Yeah? What do you want old man?? Came a voice from the tent. ?Get your ass out here!? ?Give me a moment, will ya?? ?No, I said get your ass out here!? ?Alright!? At that, the tent flap moved and out walked Joshua Brand. However, how he walked out actually caused his brother to turn around. Kaylee's cheeks got some color in them, as she looked at Josh, and her eyes moved... down. “What? You said to come out now. You didn't even let me toss a pair of shorts on.” “Son of a bitch.” Gray started to walk back to the rental jeep. Josh turned to Kaylee and pointed to his own hair. ?What happened?? ?Ah, nothing. This is my natural color. Because it's unsettling for some, my mistress usually uses her glamories to hide it. I can't do it myself. She called me right before she left and filled me in on the details. She's en route to Texas now. So until she gets back I'll need to kinda stay out of the limelight or blame it on a pool with too much chlorine. Is Dani okay?? Kaylee tried looking around her, into the tent. Dani stepped out, with her shawl wrapped fully around her this time. Unlike Josh, she was going to give Gray some modesty in return. ?Yeah, she's fine. How did it go with my brother?? Kaylee smiled. “Fortunately, Archons can only handle being in our plane for about 2 hours total. And that's if they find a body to use. Basically every time it re-possessed him, I pulled him back under the water until it sensed that he might die. It would leave, I'd let him recover and try to fend it off. If it got to him again, I just drowned him again. Worked like a charm.” ?Drowned Mutt, huh?? She caught herself as she almost blurted out in laughter. “Yeah, I'd hate to have to try to use that tactic on something that doesn't have their natural healing rates.” Gray walked up with a couple piles of clothes in his hands. He handed one to his brother, slapping him in the chest with them. “Get dressed... get covered up.” Josh smiled at him. ?What? Jealous?? Gray turned to only look his brother in the face. “Hardly... Hardly.” Gray then walked past him and headed over to where Dani was standing. “Here, I used the spare key to get you some clothes too after Annie told us about your poor dress.” Dani spoke softly to Gray, but her eyes were on Josh. “Thanks, Gray. I do need something more then my panties to walk around in.” Gray looked over at his brother, following her eyes. “I take it things are changing? He had better of taken good care of you.” Dani blushed a bit, but she answered him. “Yes... I think they are. You don't have an issue with that do you? And yes, he took very good care of me. I don't think I would have made it without him being here.” Gray gave her a knowing look, having been fully briefed on the situation by Kaylee and Annie. “Well, as I understand it, there is an Order doctor waiting for you at the hotel. After that, you've got the next two days off. Then we fly up to Seattle for you to face Elia Smith again.” “Oh, goody. Cowboy Barbie wants to play again. Fun.” “Yeah, but I just got word that the house should be ready when we get back. No more hotels.” Dani looked happy at that notion. “Good.” But Gray noticed her look in Josh's direction. ?I take it that we might have another storage room, there?? Dani blushed and just looked down at the sand. Gray reached out and pulled her chin up with his big hand. “He'd better take good care of you. If this is the path you're set on taking now, then so be it. But remember, things are going to be happening quickly. You've already caused quite a stir in the Order... be careful... Sirius Star.” Dani nodded at him, even though she didn't quite grab it that he'd called her by her actual Native name there. Sirius Star, named for the brightest star in the constellation of the Great Bear. Gray left her standing there, giving her time to turn around and move back into the tent to get dressed. His brother still stood there, naked as a jaybird, holding his clothes. Gray grabbed him by the arm. “You know what, you little...” Josh smiled at him, he knew his brother wasn't mad. However, he feigned being angry that his brother would grab him like that. ?Know what?? Gray backed off a bit and let his arm go. “Don't get pissy with me.” Josh chuckled under his breath. “Little bastard...” “Nope, they were married...” Gray put a stop to the jokes. “Just remember... you done good. Looks like you could consider it that you did very good from the looks of it... Corporal.” Josh hesitated for a moment. “Corporal? I'm not up for review for...” Gray slapped him on the shoulder. ?Review, shem-view... you done good. You took to the fight, and even beat at least one on your own. Then you helped pull her through it. I put you in for it, and Annie co-signed for it over the phone. Consider it a field promotion. Increase in pay from our sponsors begins immediately. Oh, and you got the next two days off. I'd advise you NOT to mess anything up in the meantime. I'm heading up to Seattle early. Your new mission, whether or not you care to accept it is to take care of that young lady every waking moment you have over the next two days. Got it?? Josh smiled and then half saluted him. “I got it. And I must say, it's a mission that I already accepted weeks ago.” “I know. I'll start to help pack up, get dressed and then help out.” Josh broke away and walked back into the tent, where Dani was. Gray reached down and started to unhook the small kerosene powered stove to get it ready to put away. ~End... for now~
