Stitches BloodAngel Global Wrestling Promo #2833 Date: 10/26/2006 Location: Lexington, Kentucky. University of Kentucky Medical Center Daily Missive to the Masses™: 9mm is a pussy round. And with those words sending their truthful message deep into our hearts, we slowly commence with the story of the day. Cue up Edward Lambert, not sitting in his office as we have seen him before, instead we see him sitting, shirtless, on a hospital bed. His scarred and tattooed frame sits gingerly on the bed, almost as if he were afraid of breaking or toppling it from where it sits. You would think that with having seen the inside of so many hospitals in his career that he would have learned not to worry about the bed, but ah well… such is the life of Edward Lambert. “So tell me Doc, what is the prognosis this time?” Standing across from where Edward is sitting is a man dressed in a lab coat with his back turned to the camera. Currently his attention is focused on a set of three X-rays of the human body. It would be safe to assume that those X-rays are of Edward Lambert, especially since each of them bears his name across the bottom of them. “Well Mr. Lambert, those ribs are bruised, you already knew that. The gash on your forehead is impressive, and congratulations, your knee has been wrenched again. I don’t suppose you are willing to take about two weeks or so to let them all heal utterly are you?” “No.” “Well then, they are as good as can be expected.” “That’s really not news Doc.” “Then would you like to know that you are now our most constant client? That over the years you have spent more time in my hospital than I myself have?” Edward shrugs his massive shoulders and grins wryly, “That’s supposed to be good news?” With an angry grunt the doctor slaps the chart down on the table beneath the X-rays. “What else would you like me to tell you? That you need stitches to close your forehead? We both know that Mr. Lambert, we both know that you are going to require stitches and bandages, and ice, and actual rest to heal. You aren’t getting any younger and your returning to this punishment is asinine in a way that would make Super Dave Osbourne proud.” Edward glances at the doctor and smirks. “Is that a business as usual speech Doc?” “I don’t know Mr. Lambert,” The doctor in the lab coat sighs and shrugs his shoulders. “What would you like me to tell you?” “Something other than what you always say.” “Mr. Lambert, no, we’ve known one another long enough to be on a first name basis haven’t we?” “I suppose so.” “Well then Edward, unless you look forward to spending your twilight years as a cripple then you need to stop. Stop the insanity, the bleeding, the injuries, just stop all of it. It’s killing you. When you retired, I had hoped that we would never meet again, but with this move, you getting back into the ring it just brings it all back up again. What will it take to get you to stop?” “A frontal lobotomy.” “As your Doctor Edward, I have to tell you that not only should you stop, but you NEED to stop. You are thirty six?” “Yes.” “And how many years have you been doing this?” “I’ve been wrestling for thirteen, if you mean how long have I been doing the injury track, then… just over eleven.” “Exactly. And what do you have to show for it?” “Pardon?” “Exactly what do you have to show for it? Scars? Nagging injuries? Ribs that you refuse to give time to heal? A brother in a vegatative state? More medical insurance claims than the entire state of Rhode Island? What is it that you want? You want to turn yourself into a Martyr?” Edward sits on the bed for a moment before shaking his massive head. “You don’t get it either.” “No Edward, you don’t get it!” The doctor turns abruptly. “Edward, maybe you don’t listen to doctors, maybe you don’t listen to your family, maybe you don’t listen to the world you live in or anyone in it, but start listening to yourself. Start listening to what your body is telling you. Do you wake up in pain?” “You know that I do.” “Do you wake up coughing and craving a cigarette just to clear your throat?” “Yes…” “Do you wake in the middle of the night and have to take medication for pain?” “Nope.” “Wait… what?” “I don’t take medication for pain any longer Doc. It doesn’t work anymore.” “Are you insane?” “No. Painkillers don’t work anymore. Tylenol, Vicodin, Asprin, Loritabs, Perkesets, none of them work anymore Doctor. It’s not that I even really want them to work either.” “You don’t want them to work?” “Nope.” “Why the hell not?” Edward’s grin is reminiscent of that of a shark. “Because while some people have a happy family to show their accomplishments, and some have proud parents, or hoards of legal representatives to prove their worth, I have pain.” The doctor stares at him for a moment and blinks rapidly. “That’s fucked up.” “We live in a fucked up world Doctor, now fix me.” With a heaving sigh, the doctor reaches into a plastic box on the table, pulls forth a hooked needle and surgical twine and steps in front of Edward. “Would you like me to numb it up?” “Not really, as I said, pain doesn’t bother me much anymore.” Nodding, the doctor inserts the needle into the skin beside the gash on Edward’s head. No emotion shows in the eyes of the patient as the painful work goes on. As the stitches come closer to the end, the doctor shakes his head sadly. “How many more times will we have to do this Mr. Lambert?” “However many it takes Doc.” “You know that this will only end up one way don’t you?” “Oh yes Doc. I know exactly how it will end.” “Doesn’t that frighten you?” BloodAngel shakes his head. “No, but it should frighten several other people.”
