[:: ----- ::] ==|Part I: The Aftermath|== The scene opens within the secret headquarters of NEST where a metal door slid open, as a mysterious group of six stepped through and began walking down the long corridor that were before them. Two NEST scientists are talking while they walked down the corridor. They stop walking and talking at the sight of the mysterious group. Both scientists quickly move out the group’s way. The group didn’t even pay the two scientists any mind when they passed, as they just continued walking down the corridor. Scientist looks at his colleague with great fear. “I thought each of them were away on separate assignments for Lord Aion.” “It’s no doubt that they finished their assignments without even breaking a sweat.” His colleague had a nervous look that was accompanied by sweat that ran down his face. “No one except for our Lord can match their superior power and skill.” Scientist nods. “With them here it must mean that Lord Aion has another assignment for them.” He looked at the group. “I surely feel sorry for whomever they have to deal with.” “I hear ya.” ---- It’s obvious that these guys were nowhere from being human, as they were full-blooded demon warriors. The sinister group was the infamous Mother’s Children and these were its? members: Volt, a warrior that controls electricity with his own will. He have been given the nickname The Lightning Emperor. His choice of weapon is a guitar that shoots out strong bolts of lightning and amplifies his powers to great levels. It’s said that he kills with great elegance and style. Bash and Smash, warriors that are brothers and have the ability to make their bodies as strong as iron. They have been given the nickname The Iron Brothers. Their choice of weapons is their bodies of course. They’re strong and brutal separately, but it’s said that they’re even stronger and more brutal when they’re together. Widow, a warrioress who’s touch is poisonous and has the ability to summon spiders to do her bidding. She have been given the nickname The Spider Lady. Her choice of weapon is her lips since that where’s her most deadly poison are stored. It’s said that she seduces her prey before killing them just like the black widow. Tsunami, a warrioress that controls water with her own will. She has been given the nickname The Water Maiden. Her choice of weapon is a trident that unleashes strong geysers when thrust into the ground. It’s said that she has killed many with towering waves that can easily wash away entire countries. Moon, a warrioress that controls air with her own will, but she also controls another strong power that no one except for Aion have seen. She has been given the nickname Divine Sword. Her choice of weapon is twin katanas that shoots out sharp air currents that cut can anything into ribbons. It’s said that she’s blind, but despite this handicap she is an incredible swordswoman whose sword technique is second only to Aion’s. ---- Mother’s Children soon arrived at a closed metal door. The metal door slides open where Aion’s enforcer, Zel greeted the infamous group at the other side of the door. He wore bandage on his right cheek that hid a nasty scar that he sustained when Kyo struck him in the face with his gun not too long ago. Zel steps aside while he looked at them with seriousness. Mother’s Children enter the new area that looked to be a briefing room. All of them take a seat at a long table except for Moon who sat on the floor far back from her group members. The Iron Brothers are chatting and laughing while looking at Zel. Zel raises a brow on his serious face. “Is there something on both your minds?” Smash nods with a smirk. “We were wondering how you were feeling?” “How I was feeling?” Zel stared at them. “Yeah, we’re just concern after we heard that you got your ass kicked by that little weakling, Kushinaga.” Bash started laughing along with his brother. Zel sharpens his eyes in anger and responded with a deep growl. Volt lets a little laugh as he played a few notes on his guitar. Tsunami shakes her head at her group members? immature behavior. Widow doesn’t even pay them any mind, as she was more focused on making herself more attractive. Zel turns his head away from them. “He got lucky. It won’t happen again.” The Iron Brothers laugh even harder. “I’ll put money on that!” Bash pulled out money from his pocket. Smash does too. “I’ll take that bet!” Zel growls once more. “Even the weakest of things can put up a fight.” Moon cleaned her katanas in turns. Zel and The Iron Brothers looked at Moon curiously. “But he isn’t weak.” Mother’s Children and Zel turn to Aion’s voice and saw that he along with Mother entered the room. They rise from their seats and saluted their leader. Aion takes a seat at the front of the table while Mother stood at his side, as Mother’s Children soon sat back down afterwards. Aion leans back in his seat. “I know that you all are tired of raiding temples, destroying shrines, and killing off Shinto Priests and Priestesses.” He looked at them with a smirk as he forward in his seat. “That’s why I think you’ll enjoy this new assignment.” “I don’t care as long as the assignment guarantees lots of blood and death.” Smash popped his knuckles. Bash nods with a dark grin. “Yeah, it has been a while since we had a real fight.” “Then this assignment is for you all.” “The Deathscythe Outlaw, right?” Moon held up one katana in front of her serene face. Aion smirks. “Well informed as always, Moon.” He looked at his group with seriousness. “That’s right. I want you all to hunt down Kushinaga but I want him brought back here alive.” Volt scratches his cheek while he had a curious look on his face. “Isn’t he the one who fights in that promotional league?” “He’s the same.” “I think he’s pretty cute.” Tsunami slightly blushed. Smash scoffs. “I thought you said that we would enjoy this assignment.” He looked with great disappointment. “You’re having us go after someone who plays grab ass every week.” Bash crosses his arms. “And everyone knows that wrestling isn’t real.” Aion closes his eyes and grinned with amusement. “Your fellow member, Sniper said the same thing too before he was sent to a permanent timeout.” “Sniper was a damn fool who allowed his obsession for killing that worm to get the better of him.” Smash still looked disappointed. “He should’ve been dead a long time ago.” Tsunami nods. “Sniper wasn’t too bright.” Volt plays a soft tune on his guitar. “Yeah and he didn’t have any style.” “Nevertheless, you should never underestimate your opponent.” Moon sheathed her swords one at a time and gently lied them down on the floor. “If you do then you certainly fall before your opponent in defeat.” “However, this isn’t your run in a mill opponent especially since he’s just like us.” Aion brought his hands together and rest his elbows on the table. “What! You mean he could be a ?”” Volt’s eyes quivered in shock. “That’s utter bullshit.” Smash glared. “That weakling couldn’t be possibly like us.” “It is true, my children. I’ve seen it with my very own eyes.” She looked at them with a smile on her face. “He is one of you.” Widow gives Mother a nasty look. “And we’re supposed to believe your words, the words of a human.” “I was there too which means I saw it too unless you’re calling me and Mother lairs.” Aion opened his eyes and revealed his frightening eyes that flared crimson. Widow has nervous look that was accompanied by sweat that ran down her face. “No, I was just saying?” “I believe that Widow is trying to say that we need proof of this.” Moon approached the table with her dull colored eyes on Aion. “You must admit that is a bit farfetched to believe.” “So, you all need proof?” Aion looked at each member with seriousness. “Ok then.” He pushed a button on the arm of his chair. “You come in now.” Mother’s Children have curiosity on their faces. The metal door slides open where a NEST scientist entered the room. Mother’s Children stared with curiosity. “I’ll like you all to meet Dr. Jennifer White.” Dr. White bows before Mother’s Children. “It’s honor to meet you all.” She stood up and looked at them with a smile on her face. ---- Remember her? It should since this is the same woman that helped Kyo escape from The Organization’s Headquarters in mid-January. She was supposedly a scientist for The Organization, but guess this proves that her allegiance was really with Aion and Mother. ---- “And how is this human going to prove that the bastard is one of us.” Bash stared at Dr. White with unimpressed look. Dr. White pushes her glasses up to her eyes. “With this.” The metal door slides open again where a dark figure entered this time. It stands behind Dr. White, as it stared at Mother’s Children with one eye that glowed red. Mother’s Children look at the figure with confusion. Dr. White grins. Kyo looks at the ceiling through the lingering smoke in deep thought. “What the hell happened last week?” His cigarette falls out of his mouth when he gasped after he suddenly felt a sharp pain in his head, as random visions of deaths and carnage were entering his mind in a blur. Kyo favors his head when the visions ended. Sephiroth nudges his nose under Kyo’s arm with a soft whimper. The third crew member stares at him with an awkward look. “Why are you staring at me like that?” The other crew members and the man look at Kyo in the same way that the third crew member was. Kyo closes his eyes with a modest look on his face. “Uh, you looked like someone I know.” He realized that the others didn’t see what he saw. “Thanks for the bike.” Kyo put on his shades. The garage door slides up where Kyo rode out of there like a bat out of hell. Kyo quickly rides down the street, maneuvering his way through traffic, but he soon stopped at a red light. While he waits for the light to turn green, he looked around the popping area, but a look of surprise appeared on his face again when he saw that some of the civilians had the appearances of demons and angels. “What the” What the hell is going on here?” He made the same discovery that the other civilians didn’t see what he saw. His thoughts are broken by loud honking from many cars that was behind him. “Hey the light is green, asshole!” Kyo resumes driving where he passed a small gathering people that stood outside of an electronic store. They’re watching the news on the many television sets that were in the front window. The news was reporting the strange phenomenal that occurred in Tokyo last week. Kyo’s thoughts are broken by the sound of a train riding on the bridge above him, but that also triggered a clear vision on what happened to him. ---- “You won’t get away with this.” Kyo glared over at him while he felt his bones cracking from the Ghost Man’s tight bearhug on him. “I don’t understand you. He quickly dove down to the train where he placed both feet and hands on the train’s nose. Kyo was trying to stop the train, but the train wasn’t showing signs of slowing down. He slammed both feet into the tracks and tightened his grip on the train’s nose. Kyo was wincing in slight pain from feeling the tracks shattering apart on his legs. Some of the passengers that were in the first cart were watching in shock while others were actually praying to the angelic looking being to stop the train. Blood began gushing out from Kyo’s hands in reaction to how tight he held onto the train. In what appeared to be a large halo soon formed behind his wings. Kyo roars out even louder, as his strength and speed was increased dramatically. “We’re slowing down!” The train was getting even closer to the bummers! The passengers look on with great fear, as they were bracing themselves for certain impact. Kyo crashes hard into the bummers where he and half the first cart were hanging off the tracks! Passengers are screaming! Kyo sharpened his glowing violet eyes and started lifting the train back up on the tracks. Once the train was on the tracks, he began pushing it to a safe distance from the end. The passengers realize that they were still alive. “He saved us! The angel has saved us!” The passengers start celebrating. Kyo removes his blood covered hands from the train’s nose, as he took a couple of steps while he panted heavily. His inferno disperses and it was revealed that he returned back to his normal state with his clothes torn. “It stopped?” Kyo smirked slightly before he collapsed on the tracks, as smoke rose from his body from what appeared to be extreme heat. ---- “Kyosuke!
