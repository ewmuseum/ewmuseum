The Journey: Part 22/From Joshua’s POV

4 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-4-2005 8:30 in the morning Its early morning in the NYC and most everyone in the populace that is to be at work on this day is and those that don’t have to be are out roaming the streets looking for something to do ,which considering this is New York City ain’t as hard as one whom has never been there would think. On this particular morning people are out and about and one of the big topics of discussion is the young man formerly from London ,England and now a legal resident in the United States ,Joshua Curtis. Ok let’s clarify: Curtis has been a legal resident for years now but he wasn’t born in the United States and it took him a couple of years until he earned his citizenship in the country. It had been a dream of Joshua’s to become a legal citizen of the United States since he was asked out of IWF’s Takedown many years ago and he had to fight with the Government of the United States to get what he wanted and when he got it he was VERY happy because for him it was ,as he put it “One dream down and one to go”. So why had Curtis become a “hot button” topic as of late? That would be a good question with all things considered that have gone down in his life at this point in time? First off Zack returned in his life and that was a HUGE boost for the young man formerly from London ,England because ,truth be told ,Joshua missed Zack and it was apparent when would go into his locker room before. Joshua ,whom is usually a talkative person by nature ,was more treserved with talking to people within the fed known as “Global Wrestling” but now that he had Zack back it made him open up a lot more than he used to! There is a theory that when Zack returned that Joshua was “awakened” and he was going to be able to focus on his job in the ring a lot more and that has officially become evident with the recent stroke of good fortunes that Joshua has had’ok so he “lost” in the tag match recently when he ,Zack and “a certain young man from the SoHo district” went up against Christian Zenith ,his girlfriend Vivica Valentine and ICE ,but in a way HE didn’t lose the match and he reminds people all the time about whom it was that on his back when the three count was administered and that was in fact KPJ! That brings us to the other hot topic of the people on the street and that is this “offer” that Joshua has made to the two power factions in GW in “Hunting Grounds” and “The Elite?” Why would Joshua make an offer to those two particular factions? Good question and its one that in all reality Joshua could answer in this way only: Joshua’s desire to belong in GW is fueled by wanting to be associated with the winning side of a war that is about to begin in GW. The catch: Curtis knows that his chances are slim at best in getting in with either faction but that only makes him more willing to try. If Curtis gives up on his mission it would be like if Heero never acted on his emotions and that is what Curtis has ,had and forever will do to help him remain true to himself. Has Curtis minded getting attention as a wrestler in GW? No because of the fact that he was ,and in his mind still someone that GW can look to as a man that wants to be a leader and not a follower. Joshua has wanted nothing more in the past couple of months since we began this “Journey” than to be accepted and for the most part the people that truly matter ,the fans of GW ,and they have done such with open arms and know that Curtis acts only on what he feels they want him to do’right now what it seems they want him to do is not get stepped on anymore by factions like “Hunting Grounds” or even “The Elite”. So where is Curtis today at this hour? Well Joshua decided to take a drive from the hustle and bustle of New York City and went out into the rest of the state and specifically a cottage where a friend of his was waiting but fear not ,for Joshua wasn’t alone on this day. Did you really think Joshua was going to leave without SOMEONE with him? Joshua knows better especially after the problems that he had with Zenith already and apparently the “American Youth” has become quite angry at Curtis over comments that he made. If Joshua didn’t speak his mind on different topics though then the fans whom adore him would think he was a product of “The Powers That Be” and Joshua knows better than to conform to what THEY want! So whom did Joshua take on this excursion? Well of course you know he took Zack with him and he even brought Quatre ,but this trip he brought the “Braided One” in Duo Maxwell and where they were going is somewhere special. HIS name is Ian Lane ,a huge 7 foot 2 inch man that weighs about 295 pounds but one of the gentlest giants in the industry of Pro Wrestling ,just don’t get him mad or you get the wrong end of a snap powerbomb that he has perfected and its one of the most devastating moves in wrestling history! Ian was born in Salt Lake City ,Utah and met Joshua through a friend of his in a promotion in Japan and the two struck up a friendship that is very much like that of Joshua/Zack except Ian has a girlfriend and Joshua adores her because she treats Ian as a person and not a freak like most people that meet Ian would. Ian lives in the cabin outside of the city with his girlfriend and when Joshua pulled up the Pontiac Firebird that he had gotten as a present from Duo a few years ago he turned and addressed all that were there. “Ok guys” Joshua started “this guy that you are about to meet is a good friend of mine and please no “Green Giant” jokes Maxwell! He got that enough when he began wrestling in Japan and I had to stick up for him more times than you got twists in that braid of yours on your head!” Joshua said pointedly to the young man from Sand Diego ,California and Duo gave the “Scout’s Honor” symbol before speaking. “Josh” Duo started “I promise you that I will not act up around Ian and I respect all of your friends you know that. I know that you want us to meet a lot of the people that are in your life and this one is really important to you ,so its with that I say let’s go!” Duo commanded and Joshua couldn’t help but smile as he knew Duo meant what he said and said what he meant and when he saw Ian he knew better than to start busting jokes on him! Ian Lane ,the 7 foot plus friend of Joshua was waiting at the door of his has cabin when Joshua went to knock and opened it before any contact was made. As he opened the door Joshua shook the man’s hand and made his way into the house along with his friends and as they walked in and turned to face Ian Joshua opened his mouth to speak. “Ian ,my good friend its been too long hasn’t it?” Joshua said and Ian smiled before speaking to his former employer. “Yes it has Joshua. The last time we were together I was your bodyguard and not to mention one of your young prospects when it came to the in ring wrestling and my girlfriend was going to be hired as a manager for me in your company but something happened and we both know that it wasn’t your fault.” Ian explained when Duo came from behind him and extended his hand and that was when Joshua realized that he had forgotten to introduce everyone there! “Oh I’m sorry Ian” Joshua began “I almost forgot to introduce everyone here. The young man with the braided ponytail there is Duo Maxwell and to my right is my partner ,Zack Johnson and to my left is a young friend of mine named Quatre Raberba Winner. We are a part of a faction that has begun to surface in GW and well I think we might need some assistance in educating people and I couldn’t think of any bigger help than yourself Mr. Lane.” Joshua explained to his friend ,whom by now had already shaken Duo’s hand after the introduction and then proceeded to hoist Quatre up on his shoulder with ease’suffice to say Quatre needed to change his underwear after he got down later! “Mr. Curtis’it would be an honor to come to work for you again ,BUT I do have my girlfriend and I don’t know if?” Ian was cut off by the presence of the aforementioned girlfriend ,whose name is Haley Martin ,and she seemed intrigued by the idea of her boyfriend going back to work for Joshua. “If you are worried about me having you go to work for Mr. Curtis don’t. I talked to Joshua before he came over and proposed the idea of having you go back to work for him and we agreed that it would be good for you to go back to the ring because there haven’t been a lot of offers for anything long-term elsewhere.” Haley pointed out but she wasn’t quite done “And besides hun the next PPV for the company that Joshua works at is coming up and we all know the pay day that will be fetched win or lose because of the magnitude of the show.” Haley said with a smile and that was something that Joshua loved about her. The fact that she could light up a room with her smile and she was ALWAYS good for a “pick me up” as far as personality went! How Joshua didn’t meet these two sooner was beyond him! During the time that Joshua ,Duo and Zack were talking with Joshua’s friend Quatre decided to do some exploring in the house and during his explorations he stumbled upon a microphone that was plugged in and he decided to do the one thing that no one in the faction knew that he could do’in his language of Arabic no less! He started singing! Now it wasn’t the fact that he was singing that shocked Joshua but here’s a fact that not many ,if any people, knew: Quatre forsake speaking Arabic when he moved to moved to the Untied States and the fact that he was doing it now surprised a lot of people especially with the culture ,as unfair as it is, that we are living in! When Quatre finished singing Joshua walked over and whispered something in his ear that made the blonde Arabian smile and what it was he wouldn’t tell anyone but everyone watched as Quatre walked back over to the group and apologized profusely for using the equipment and even saying anything in his language but everyone just congratulated him on doing it and staying true to himself. When Quatre started speaking again in English there were people actually disappointed because they wanted to hear Quatre speak his language more often but he told them that if he did it more often that he felt his support from the fans would go away. “Now ain’t that the biggest bunch of bull shit I ever heard in my life!” Yelled Duo and everyone turned to the young soldier as he let out the first expletive in a long time from any member of the group “Look Q you know that the American fans are very loyal to those they care about. They would LOVE to hear you sing in your own language I think and if you think differently then let’s test it. On a GW broadcast one day I want you to walk out to the ring in your ring attire and start singing just what you sang a little bit ago. If they boo you I’ll cut off my ponytail and if I win’well nothing happens but only because I want you to show America the “Real Quatre?”. Duo told the Arabian and after consulting with Curtis there was one last thing that could be heard from Quatre before they went to go get dinner for the night? “YOU’RE ON!” Au Revoir!
