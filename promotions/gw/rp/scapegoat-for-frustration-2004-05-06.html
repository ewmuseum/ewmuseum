Scapegoat for Frustration

6 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/06/04 Scapegoat for Frustration Revenge was always one of my stronger methods of retaliation, seeking out an individual who had done something that damaged me in some way, shape or form. I was never good at being the sole aggressor, the single individual who wanted to run across the playground with some kind of meanstreak. I never knew whether this was down to some kind of personal preference or whether I just lacked the actual motivation to be half as bad regularly as I was when I desperately needed to get even with someone or thing. Until recently, I never thought I would have the same desire to extract revenge that I had back in the day when I was beaten and thrashed on a regular basis. Not even people like JC Diamond and 'Mr Xtreme' had really taken my anger to the limits, despite the way that they screwed my career over. However, with all of that aside... with everything thrown deep into the depth of my mind, never before had I found myself in a situation where my career... and perhaps even my life could have been over because of one thing that somebody felt like doing. Never before had I been in the emergency room of a hospital because some bastard wanted me out of the picture, be it for reason a, b or c. Now though, not only had I been into that emergency room with all kinds of fears and concerns about my life and my career... but I left that emergency room with a goal, a life aim that would never be able to rest until the storm had passed over the heads of every living member of this planet... and then, when High Noon came to pass and I was able to discover the culprit, the goal had gained concrete foundations, it had been cemented. Juan Gonzalez was going to pay dearly for running both Ryan and myself off the road like that, he was going to pay for not only making sure that I didn't make it to High Noon... but he was going to pay for the accident that led to me being stripped of the Television Title. ... However, with the way that my life was nothing like a fantasy tale; nothing like some beautiful, mythical legend that always had a happy ending, this story didn't quite have the same level of ease and comfort. No, instead of being allowed to simply rocket back ahead of schedule into a contest with this sick, sadistic bastard... I was going to have to make do with a couple of people to make scapegoats for this unlawful occassion. Instead of being able to take that Juan Gonzalez by the neck and snapping his limbs like twigs, I was going to have make do with venting a degree of frustration on both Mike Page & Vegas of this team that were called 'Lost Cause'. Don't get me wrong or anything, these aren't exactly the people that I really would have liked to get my hands on at the peak of my rage and frustration... but anybody to pound was somebody worthwhile to pound. Jamester, Josh... whoever, if you were in my way and if you wanted to get something around me, you were going to be looking straight into the eyes of 'hatred' itself. Heh, I can remember the day that we were released from that Mexican hospital... I can remember it like it was yesterday. Although, in all truth and honesty, it was only a few days ago that we were actually released from this place... but despite that, I think this is going to be something that hangs around in my mind for quite a few years at the very least. The level of media and law enforcement attention that surrounded both Ryan and myself when we walked through those doors, it was both immense and somewhat scary at the same time. Never before had we been surrounded by so many people... be it because they wanted to get a statement from us or wanted us to press charges against the bastard that had done this to us. 'Mr Sherwin, sir, is there anything that you would like to say to the world about your almost life threatening accident?' 'Mr Sherwin, is there anything that you'd like to say to the people at home about how you feel now that you've been able to walk away from one of the scariest accidents in recent history? 'Mr Sherwin, are you going to press charges for this attempt on your life? We know who executed the plan and if you wish to proceed with legal action, we'll be more than happy to take this person away.' The questions had come thick and fast, at a pace that I almost lost track of completely at times. Waving my arms about, trying to ward off these predatorial creatures, I managed to get to a car that had been waiting to pick me up and get me back across the border and into the United States of America. The door was opened for me by one of the Mexican officers, who told me that if I didn't say anything now, he'd assume that I wouldn't press charges and consider the case closed. 'Why would I want to press charges against the fellow?' I told the mystified officer, his facial expression displaying a level of confusion and amazement that I hadn't seen for many, many years. I knew that with the help of the police, I was never going to get the level of revenge that I wanted to extract personally. So what, I could have laughed all the way to ga-ga town as Juan was thrown behind bars and sent to become the bitch of some homosexual criminal on Death Row called Bubba Jones. That'd have been the easy way, that'd have been my choice if I wasn't so God damned angry about this whole situation... about this whole 'accident'. Closing the door and looking at the front of the vehicle that I'd climbed into, the driver looked into his mirror and stared at me for a few moments, the look of bitterness and the expression of anger prompting him to speak quickly and clearly. 'Where to?' he asked, trying not to sound like a bag of bones. '... The United States of America.' I responded, giving no indication of an airport that I wanted to go to. Afraid to ask any questions, the driver nodded his head and turned the ignition on inside his vehicle. Pressing his foot down on the accelerator and placing the vehicle into gear, he began to drive off, slowly easing through the rabid crowds of people and reporters before taking off down an otherwise empty street. I knew who was going to pay for all of this in the end and I had an idea as to how I wanted this revenge to be taken... Lost Cause though, they just happened to be a couple of people who were in the wrong place at the VERY worst of times. :: End ::
