May have to take drastic measures

29 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-29-04 Brick was on the warpath. He had just gotten through losing a match to Corvus via disqualification after repeatedly hitting the Dark Prince with a steel chair. He then went on to hit Corvus' wife Shadow, newly admitted into Global Wrestling, with the chair. Before the match, Brick had gotten some information about the night Donny McNasty was attacked. A make-up artist named Marcy had mentioned that she saw Corvus hanging around near the arena entrance while Donny was in the ring. When Brick questioned Corvus, the Dark Prince was tight-lipped. This and the teamup on him by Corvus and Shadow led to his assault. Then, suddenly Salaok appeared on the Globaltron, taunting Brick by revealing that he planted the story about Corvus to instill false hope in Brick. As Brick stormed backstage, Bubba met him and kept step with him. 'Brick, I'm sorry. I thought her story was legit.' 'Where is she?' Brick growled. 'Damnit, Brick, don't do anything stupid! You're already pushin' it after what you an' Train did to Adam Grey!' Brick found Marcy at the make-up chair putting some finishing touches on Noi Tsunami. Right away, he darted towards her and grabbed her by the arm. 'Brick, don't!' 'Why did you lie to me?!' he roared in the frightened make-up artist's face. 'How much did he promise you to lie to me?!' 'Please!' she cried, ready to burst into tears. 'HOW MUCH?!' 'He said he'd kill me if I didn't tell you that story about Corvus! That's the truth, I swear!' Brick immediately let go of the woman. Right away, Bubba placed a hand on his shoulder. 'It's okay,' said Bubba, trying to calm Marcy down, 'it's okay. We understand.' As Marcy walked away sobbing, Brick stood, trembling with anger, his fists and eyes clenched tight. 'This is it, Bubba,' Brick growled through clenched teeth. 'I'm tired o' that motherfucker playin' games with me.' 'OK, Brick, calm down...' Right then, Brick exploded. 'Calm down, my ass!!! He keeps crossin' the fuckin' line with me, Bubba! I ain't takin' his shit no more!!!' Brick then continued storming down the hallway. Bubba followed close behind. 'I see Train's havin' his effect on you.' 'Yeah, well, you're the motherfucker who put us together in the first place.' 'That was to defuse one intense situation, not to cause more. Beatin' up Adam Grey...' 'I told you he had it coming!' 'Takin' that chair to Corvus an' Shadow like you did...' 'I'm gettin' tired o' the bullshit, Bubba. And cats are gonna know that, real soon.' Brick walked off, leaving Bubba behind. 'And just what do you plan to do?' Brick stopped and looked back at Bubba over his shoulder. 'Believe me, Bubba. You don't wanna know.' ---- 'I was wondering when you'd come see the twins,' said Monica as she led Brick down the hall to the twins' room. The room was painted in coral. 'You have my nephew in a pink room?' asked Brick, looking around the r. 'It's not pink, it's coral.' 'It's pink!' 'Don't shout.' The babies right away 'Hey. Mommy's here and look who she brought. It's uncle Jamal.' 'I can't believe how big they've gotten,' Brick said, as he looked down into the crib. He then reached down to pick little Randall. 'And especially this one. You and me are gonna be wrestling pretty soon. Yes, we are.' Randall giggled. 'See, they love you.' 'Yeah.' As much as he enjoyed his niece and nephew, however, he still wasn't completely happy. 'Wanna tell me what's wrong?' asked Monica. 'Not really. 'You might as well. I'll get it out of you, and if I don't, Mom will.' 'Fine,' Brick sighed. 'It's Salaok, it's Clash of the Titans, it's Train being my tag team partner, it's the fact that I'm no damn closer to finding out who the hell attacked Donny.' 'I know. I saw what Salaok pulled on Evolution. You must have wanted to kill him.' 'Still do,' he replied. 'The problem is Salaok is in the main event and he might just win the World title. Now, I could go through the Rumble, coming out the winner. If he wins the World title, he and I can finally face each other one-on-one. Plus the chance to take the title away from him would make it all the sweeter.' 'Right.' 'On the other hand, do I really want Salaok to win the World title? I mean, he's been wanting to get it back around his waist since he came back. Keeping him from winning it could be the perfect payback.' 'Well, shouldn't you be worried about the rest of the roster? I mean, you won't be the only one. Win the rumble and then you can worry about Salaok.' 'You know, you're right. But still, I need to deal with Salaok. I'm thinking, I might have to do something extreme.' 'Extreme enough to make you and Ma ashamed of me.' 'Jamal, you're not gonna do something like you did with Christina McCoy, are you?' For a while, Brick was silent. 'Jamal?' 'I've said too much, already.'
