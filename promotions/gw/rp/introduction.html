Date: Today Beep? beep? beep? Hospital beds, wires, nurses, machines, hoses, sensors, needles, IVs, doctors, white walls. Beep? beep? beep? This is what I woke up to. I didn’t know how long it had been since my eyes last opened, but I did know that it had been too long. My eyes were sensitive to the light, my nose sensitive to the smell of morphine and other drugs that polluted the air. My fingers and skin were sensitive to the sensors and wires scattered across my body. My mouth was sensitive to the dry taste that had been dry for much too long. My ears were sensitive to even the slightest beep or tick. Beep? beep? beep? The hospital was alive, not only with movement. It seemed to have a life of it’s own. Everything seemed to have it’s own job, it’s own place in this miniature world. Beep? beep? beep? The doctor hurried to my bed. Someone must have informed him that I was awake, because there wasn’t much of a look of shock on his face, when he saw my eyes opened. As my body began to slowly remember it’s former strength, I found my hands feeling around the bed. Reacquainting themselves with the touch of the real world. I could tell that it had been a very long time when I saw the reflection of my own face in the doctor’s glasses. I hardly recognized myself. I touched my fingertips to my face, I ran them across my chin and jaw line. What normally would be a smooth, freshly shaved surface, was now covered in a tangle of facial hair. Beep? beep? beep? The doctor began to check the many monitors and machines that were hooked up to my body. After about three minutes he seemed to be satisfied with the results and pulled out a flashlight for further inspection. He had yet to say a word. Beep? beep? beep? The doctor began to check the many monitors and machines that were hooked up to my body. After about three minutes he seemed to be satisfied with the results and pulled out a flashlight for further inspection. He had yet to say a word as he examined my pupils with the flashlight. Nothing would have made my happier then to ram the flashlight up that doctor’s ass, the light burned the shit out my already sensitive eyes. Beep? beep? beep? He finally decided to speak. “How do you feel?” he asked me. “Like I got hit by a Mac Truck and I’ve been knocked out cold for the last year of my life,” I answered as truthfully as I could. “How about you, how do you feel doc?” I asked with a sarcastic tone in my voice. He ignored my sarcastic question and just continued right on with the conversation. Finding out what he wanted to know only. “You’re not too far off from the truth,” came his reply. “Do you feel dizzy, nauseous, sick?” “Nope, I feel just fine doc. What do you mean I’m not too far off from the truth?” I asked. “First of all, do you know who you are?” Of course I know who the fuck I am. What the hell was this guy’s problem. I just want to know why the hell I’m here, and how I got here. I opened my mouth to reply to his stupid question, but not a sound came out. As stupid as that question was, I was dumbfounded by it. For some reason the visions of my dreams came back to me. Visions of ancient samurai warriors heading into battle against their enemies. Visions of samurai training in the early morning sunrise. Samurai being tattooed, meditating in silence with only the accompaniment of their gods. I wasn’t the same anymore. Something had changed, something I couldn’t explain. Suddenly, I didn’t know who the hell I was anymore. Beep? beep? beep? And so the doctor took my silence as a cut to continue talking. But I could not listen. I could only watch his lips move in confusion. I was in a different place, a different world. And the truth of the matter was I didn’t have a fucking clue what was going on. My memory ran blank, it was like looking at a blur. All I could seem to remember, were the images of my dreams. It was like watching a broken movie, it did nothing to help my cause. What the hell is going on, I need to get out of here. But something had changed in me in those months of slumber. I had changed, and even though I didn’t know who I was, I knew that these images from my dreams had something very important to do with what was going to happen next. I looked at the doctor, and something clicked in my head. I didn’t ask any more about myself, or my condition, my next question was very simply. “Where are my release forms?” Beep? beep? beep? I’m done the forms, and they are taking the wires off of me now. In a few hours I will be home, wherever the hell it is that home exactly is located. All I know is that I’m back, and it will soon be my time for vengeance.
