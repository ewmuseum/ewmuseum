The Enforcers - I

26 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05.26.05 'You and me,' started Elite muscle Bill Moresi, 'we got this.' 'Are you kidding me,' rebutted Ace Harding. 'Bill, after we win this match and begin our march to the Tag Team Titles, The Elite's gonna' be shining like never before.' Bill and Ace, the two that were now known as 'The Enforcers' of The Elite, had just finished a heavy workout and sparring session in the Underground Gym of Ken's nightclub, The Elements. As the two began their walk to the showers, both toweling off their sopping wet heads with smiles on their face, they continued forming the bond that had begun so many years ago and was relit when Harding returned to the Global ranks just a few weeks earlier. 'You've gotta' go easy on me, though, when you hit that Article 92,' said Bill, working a kink out of his left shoulder with a half grimace on his face. 'Easy,' responded Ace with a laugh. 'Were you thinking about taking it easy when you dropped me with that Bulldozer Powerbomb of yours?' Bill immediatly began laughing at Ace's response. 'Fine,' he said, 'so long as we don't take it easy on those four cats come Evolution.' Ace gave Bill a brotherly slap o the shoulder. 'No worries on that one, Billy,' Harding began. 'Not many teams have the chemistry of the two of us. I mean, how many do you know of that link power, aggressiveness, and pure technical ability together like me and you? Add in the fact that we both know we've got each other's back, and we're golden.' Bill nodded in agreeance with Ace as the two reached a bench, where they both sat to take a breather before hitting the showers. Moresi had draped his towel over his head, still dripping sweat down his perfectly chisled upper torso and stopping on his black training pants. Harding was holding his around his neck as his head lean back. The sweat was causing his newly short hair to glisten and was causing his soaking black wife-beater to stick to his defined body. 'God, it's good to be back,' exclaimed the master of the Article 92, looking up at the ceiling and catching his breath. It was still difficult for Ace to imagine that he'd been away from the sport for a year before his return. Even harder than that was the fact was that the reason he'd left was due to the fact that he'd tapped out in an 'I Quit' match at WrestleCade, to Ken Partridge Jr nontheless. Harding knew there was no shame in losing to Ken, and that everyone was bound to lose a match. Hell, it wasn't the first strike on his record. However, it was difficult to realize that he'd tapped out for the first time in his career... the only time in his career. Watching Ken trap Ice in The Epidemic, the exact same move, and causing him the same fate resounded it in his mind. He'd lost his return match, if you want to call it a loss, when all hell broke loose. When he was called upon by Ken, Josh, and Bill to rejoin The Elite and take on Salaok, Frost, and Ruckus Inc., he wasn't there for them. At least, he wasn't in his eyes. And, while Ace wasn't unconfident, the overbearing shadow of ring-rust had settled into his mind. He didn't want to let Bill down. He didn't want to be the reason that the two of them lost at Evolution. His training had intensified since his return. Everything that he'd done before he was no doing plus half. A hundred laps in the pool had turned into a hundred and fifty, and an hour and a half on the treadmill had now become three. His life was totally devoted to wrestling once more. He had no wife and no children. The only family and friends that he had were his fellow teammates in The Elite. Three men that trained just as hard as he did and cared just as passionatly for the business. He wasn't wrestling for himself anymore, he was wrestling for them. 'It's damn good to have you back,' responded Moresi, who was in effect looking at the ground. The relationship between himself and Ace had always been a strange one. They met, initially, as enemies. Two men with two different goals. Bill's was to protect Ken, and Ace's was to destroy him. However, over time, all three had grown into friendship, one that would go on to include another friend of Ken's in Josh. Bill's main motive since returning had always been to be there for Ken, but it had since changed. Since his days as The Bulldozer, Moresi had always been a beast of a man that could rampage through his opponents, leaving them scattered like Romans at a colliseum. He'd tore through the ranks of each fed that he was in through brute force mixed with an astute technical understanding inside the ring. However, upom his retirement, he grew into a different being. Bill had become a much calmer man of reason... a wiser man, even. After becoming an owner and CEO, he'd made a full transition into a pure mind of the business. He knew the ins-and-outs like no other and could not be rivaled when it came to an inside knowledge of the squared circle. All of this made him a much stronger... a much more dangerous man upon his return to the side of Ken and The Elite. Much like Harding, the only family that Bill had anymore were his 'brothers' in The Elite. He'd no other call to life at the moment and he found solice in the fact that each of the other three took as much pride and love in the sport as he did. He wrestled, now, for The Elite. The two men, seemingly reading one another's minds, through their hands out to be met by a hard cup of trust and friendship. 'I'm as confident as I've ever been,' declared the man formerly referred to as Bulldzer. 'That makes two of us,' resounded Harding. The two got of the bench and made their way into the facilities showers to clean themselves up. Afterall, Ken was throwing a monsterous 'Elite Reunion Party' at The Elements tonight, and they all had to look their best.
