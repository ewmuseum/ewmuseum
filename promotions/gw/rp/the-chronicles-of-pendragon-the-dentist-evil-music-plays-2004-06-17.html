The Chronicles of Pendragon...The Dentist? *Evil music plays*

17 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-17-04 Pendragon had spent some time in a bit of a haze. A haze of pain. “Twas a tooth, you see. An infected one. Not normally one to admit to pain, this was driving him batshit! After Overload, he strode confidently from the set after a decent interview, and the pain that had been dogging him for the past week seemed to have vanished” “Seems that strength prevails over medicinal magic after all?” The statement, made only to himself, makes him smile. He likes it when he is able to overcome adversity upon his own. He strolls past a water fountain, stops, and looks at it, cocking his head sideways? “Magic at every turn “twould appear. This one has always fascinated me.” He leans down and presses the button and PRESTO! Water splashes from the fountain and he laughs. Damn’All this wonder over a water fountain. He shakes his head in amusement and bends down to take a drink. As soon as the mechanically cooled water passes his lips he staggers back from the fountain, a hand going to his cheek? “Good Christ!” he screams with a growl. He rubs at his cheek, but the pain is evident. “YO VENNY!”“And who should appear” “The Bastard” Ben Davis. Pendragon’s only “friend”. “Whatcha doin man?” He strides into the shot, dressed in a red tank top, jean shorts and a pair of Nike sandals. He stares questioningly at Pendragon for just a moment before shaking his head in acknowledgement? “I told you you oughtta see a dentist, man! You didn’t listen’Always with the questions?” ************FLASH************** Pendragon and “The Bastard” sit in what appears to be a room in a castle’high stone ceilings, tapestries here and there?.Old wooden furniture?.NO TV!! “Dude’You have GOTTA step into the new century!” Ben chuckles as he says this, but Pendragon looks on seriously? “Dost thou meanest the fifteen hundreds?” His eyes widen as he looks at “The Bastard”. Ben just laughs and shakes his head? “Hell no! The new millennium! The TWO THOSANDS!! What the hell are you talking about?” “My apologies, mine friend?” He grabs at his cheek???Twould appear that mine body is betraying me, and I am quite unnerved by the situation?” For the first time, Ben looks genuinely concerned?“What’s the matter, Venn?” Pendragon shakes his head and leans back into the chair? “A tooth. Such a small thing to bother a King. My damned tooth is killing me!” He continues to hold the cheek’Ben laughs? “A TOOTH?”!! You are complaining about a tooth!”” Damnit man! Make an appointment with a doctor!!” Pendragon looks at Ben as if he is a crazy man?“I have already been bled three times this week’Medicine has done nothing for me. The doctor lacks the correct remedies?” Ben stares at Pendragon, unable to believe what the man is saying?“You mean, you let someone bleed you rather than callin a friggin dentist” You got issues, dude?” “Bleeding is a most sophisticated form of medicine. It is said to let out the evil blood that causes most physical maladies!” He smiles at his knowledge, but Ben just stares? “A dentist would have either pulled the tooth or capped it. Damnit man! All you have is a friggin cavity!” Pendragon’s eyes grow wider with each word? “Anyone else, and I would break thee in two for speaking so to thy Lord King. But as it stands, mine tooth is killing me and you are the only man who sees fit to associate with me’LORD! THIS DAMNABLE THING IS KILLING ME!” “Just have em bleed ya again’I’m sure that’ll take care of it?” He rolls his eyes as the scene cuts back to GW headquarters? **************FLASH*************** “I told you bro! You gotta see a fuckin dentist! I already gotcha an appointment?” Ben laughs a little and claps Pendragon on the back’Pendragon winces in pain? “Damnit’But this hurts’I give up’Take me to yonder potion maker?” “He’s just a dentist?” Ben shakes his head and the two men head for the nearest exit. They push through into sunlight and both climb into Ben’s waiting Mustang?. The scene abruptly switches to a waiting room of what appears to be a Dentist’s office. Ben sits, idly flipping through a copy of “Molar Today”, while Venneman sits, stiff, looking a bit worried. Finally, the receptionist calls out? “Pendragon” Venneman Pendragon? You’re next! The doctor will see you now!” At the words, Pendragon’s hands clench the armrests of the chair he sits in. Ben looks up, puts the magazine down and stands. He grabs Pendragon by the arm and practically drags him through the door. “Thy Lord King appreciates this not, Benjamin’I go along only because I trust thee. Mark mine words, however’This had better work?” “Fear not, my man! This guy is gonna hook you up! You are gonna feel like a new man when he gets done with your mouth dude! Trust me!” Ben says as he flashes his winning smile. He leads him to the place where the dentist does his work, and Pendragon almost balks when he sees the chair and all of the implements. Pictures of teeth and happy people decorate the walls. Pendragon slowly relaxes at the warmth the room exudes? “So’My friend’How many times hast thee been to this tooth-wizard?” Pendragon asks, a little more confident? Ben laughs?“Oh, I have always had perfect teeth. Mom took lotsa good vitamins when she was growin me. I’ve never been to the dentist’Kinda freaks me out!” Pendragon looks incredulous but the doc comes in before he can say anything. Pendragon stares at the man in the white smock. A smallish man, he is clean shaven, balding and of about forty years of age. He actually looks happy, something rare in dentists these days? “You must be my newest patient! Your friend here has told me lots about you!” Then, almost as an afterthought, “My Lord?” Pendragon doesn’t notice the pause, but merely takes the title as a matter of course. Just because this man wields tools of the apothecary, it makes him no better that a mere peasant? “Thee looks nothing like I had imagined. Wear is thy cloak” The cobwebs? Where dost thee make the magic to make cursed pains vanish?” Pendragon asks, awe in his voice. He has never been completely comfortable with magic he does not understand. He stares at the stark whiteness and sterility of the room, the clean implements, and the dentist laughs? “There is no magic here, son! Err’My Lord! Only tools of the trade?” Pendragon looks on with blank eyes?“Alright’The magic comes out of the wall. See there where the cords run from my’Wands?” Pendragon looks at the wall and smiles. He visibly sighs? ??Tis a good thing’I am not one to trust mine welfare to just a man. Though I fear magic, I respect it. And I respect those brave enough to wield it. You may see my tooth, spell maker?” Pendragon sits down in the chair, and Ben turns to leave? “HALT! Where art thee off to, Benjamin?” “Why’Umm’I thought I’d go talk to that receptionist!” “Nay’Thee must stay here with thy King. What would happen if this man were to force me beneath a spell” Someone must be close to keep the King protected from such maladies!” Ben sighs, but Pendragon’s eyes almost seem to plead. With a grunt, Ben looks around and spots a red plastic chair by the door. He looks at the dentist and he nods his ok. Ben flops down into the seat, and Pendragon’s eyes show great relief. The doctor steps over and moves his light over Pendragon’s head? “Alrighty’Let’s get started! Lean back’I need to see that tooth?” Pendragon leans back and the doctor gently grabs the king’s jaw and Pendragon opens. He stares in for a minute and shakes his head?“That is a nasty cavity”.err’Malady you have their, my King’It is gonna have to be drilled. We’ll fit ya with a nice cap. Now’We need a mild antisthetic?” The doc grabs a mask and places it over Pendragon’s mouth and nose. Pendragon holds his breath but the doc gives him a stern look. At last, he breathes it in and almost immediately a stoned look drops over his eyes. “What amazing colors I see! Ben! Dost thee see this?” Ben laughs, and shakes his head? “It’s just the drugs man’You are feeling what normal people call “high”. Let him work.” The shot stays on Ben who watches the happenings. A loud drill is heard, and Ben’s eyes get wider? “Good GOD! Should there be that much blood?” He exclaims as the drilling continues. Finally, Ben can take no more. He is not a big fan of blood ya see’He puts a hand on his stomach and bolts from the room. The shot stays with him, all the way outside. Once in the fresh air, he bends over and takes in a few nice breaths. “Ahh’that was a close one. But LORD! The blood was awful!!” He exclaimed, talking to himself?“don’t worry. It’s cool so long as I never answer myself!” Again, to himself??DAMNIT!” A roar comes from inside the office, a roar of pain and anguish. It’s Pendragon. Ben looks back at the building and a grimace crosses his face? “Ahh SHIT!” Again, to nobody but himself’He looks at the camera and shakes his head? “Would you shut up! I have things I gotta deal with! Number one being a fella who is six five and scared of a damned DENTIST! I didn’t ask for this!!!” He runs back in after his latest exclamation’Made to himself? “FUCK!!!” ~FINE~
