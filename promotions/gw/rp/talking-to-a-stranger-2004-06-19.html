Talking To A Stranger

19 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 19 The scene opened up into the inside of Jesse Jamester's house. I was sitting on the couch, quietly, because Jesse had gone into the kitchen to get some food. I guess he was hungry, I was a bit hungry too, but not that much. Jesse walked into the room, with two plates in his hands. In one there was some chips with some dip in the middle of the bowl. In the other one, it looked like Dill Pickle chips. He set them down on the coffee table, and I reached a chip. I threw it into my mouth, as jesse sat down at the couch that I was sitting on. He reached for a few chips, and we started talking. 'So, that match on Evolution, was pretty good. We didn't win though, but we gave them a pretty big chance for the match. I cannot believe they won, it was so stupid, they rolled me up and one.' said Jamester. 'Yeah, I can't believe they won. That sucks, our first match as a tag team, and we lost. That's pretty bad, but what does it matter?' I said. I reached for another chip. Then something started beeping, from the pocket of my pants. I grabbed my beeper out, and looked at what it had said on it. It didn't say anything, but yet my beeper was still going. I tried hitting it, but it kept beeping, it was really pissing me off. I threw it at the wall of Jamester's house. It bounced off, and stopped beeping. I got off the couch, and walked over to where the beeper was lying. I picked it up, and put it back into my pocket. I walked back, and sat back down on the couch. Suddenly, my beeper started beeping again. I got off the couch, and ran out of the door. Suddenly, I was outside. I threw my beeper at the ground, it broke into a few pieces. Jamester had followed me outside. He was looking at me, like I was a really crazy person. 'Stupid beeper.' I said. 'No kidding, I hate when people beep you, but nothing shows up on the beeper. It is so stupid.' he said. When I through my beeper, I threw it onto the street. Suddenly, I heard beeping coming from it, and it was still broken in lots of pieces. I walked onto the street, but no cars were coming towards my name. I picked up my broken beeper, and it was beeping. I threw it at the ground again, but this time it ddn't stop beeping. I left it onto the street, and went back into Jamester house. We sat back down on the couches. But, suddenly, I heard beeping coming from far away. I looked out the window, and saw that the beeper was flashing, but still broken. 'What is wrong with that?' Jamester said. 'I don't really know. Its never done this before.' I said. 'I guess I should go, I have a lot of stuff to do today.' I said. 'Ok man, see you later.' he said. I got off the couch, and walked out of the door. I walked out onto the street, and walked over to my broken beeper. I picked it up, but this time it had a number on it, but no name. The number was [detail withheld]. I have never seen that number before in my life. I looked at the beeper again, and saw that it had an adress on it too. I looked at it, and then started walking down the street, to the adress it gave me. Obviously, it was in this town, who would phone me from out of town? *30 Minutes Later* I had come face to face with a building, I had never seen bfore, and it was at the end of the town. I walked up the stairs, and knocked on the door of the building. I waited a few minutes, and then the door opened, but no one opened it. I walked inside, the room was pitch black. The door closed by itself, I looked around to see if anybody was there. I walked around the house, but no one seemed to be in the house. Suddenly, I heard a big bang, on the floor above me. I was frightened, I didn't know what this place was, or who lived in it. But whoever it was, was trying to phone me, and tell me to come here. 'Who is it?!' I yelled into the house. There was no answer. It was no use yelling again, because no one was in the house. I saw some staiurs, that led to the up stairs of this house. I started walking up them, every one creaked when I stepped on it. I got to the top, and saw a room with some candles lit in it. There was a rocking chair in the corner of the room, rocking back and forth, and the man was whistling a tune, that sounded familiar, but I couldn't figure out what it was. 'Who are you?' I asked the man, that was rocking back and forth on the rocking chair. I started walking into the room, where the man was rocking. I kept moving closer, but I'm not sure who, or what was on that chair. I got to the chair, I was sweating. I turned the chair around, but nothing was on it. The window was open, which was probably making it rock, and maknig me scared. I walked out of the room, and saw that there were other rooms in the up stairs too. I walked into one, but it was the bathroom. I walked out, and into another one. I saw a desk in the corner of the room. There was a man sitting o a chair, behind the desk, with a cigar in his mouth. He was looknig sraight at me, but I couldn't see his face cause of the darkness. 'Who are you, and what do you want with me.' I said, to the guy. 'I'm not here to hurt you, I just need to tell you something.' the man said. 'What is it, because your scaring the shit out of me right now.' I said. 'I just need to tell you, ggggget ouuuut.' he said in a low voice.' I started running. I didn't care what that man had said to me or anything, I was running for my life. I ran down the stairs, and then down the same hallway I walked through. I opened the door to go outside, but it was locked. I looked back and saw that the man, had turned into something, but I don't know what. The thing rushed towards me, as the scene started to fade.
