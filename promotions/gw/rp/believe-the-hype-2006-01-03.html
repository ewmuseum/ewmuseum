Believe the hype.

3 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 3rd, 2006. 'Believe the hype' - Reload The camera zooms in and there he is in all the glory a suburban youth could ever wish for. His shaggy hair is split into two seperate colors, half-black/half-white. He wears a pair of ragged jeans, a white t-shirt with 'MAE' written across the front in pink and a pair of pink Chuck Taylors to finish off his ensemble. As the camera closes in on the unmistakable image of 'American Youth' Josh he speaks. Josh: They say I'm getting soft, they say I'm getting weak, they say I'm not the same man I used to be. And I say? Bitch, please! Josh smiles broadly. Josh: The critics say a lot of things, but actions speak louder than words, and when I step into the octagon with Mike Biddle I'll let my actions do the talking. Josh tosses his hair as a gleam of pride blazes from his bluish gray eyes. Josh: I try not to make things personal, after all its usually just about the gold and glory, but this time? Its about so much more than that. Mike, I want that International Championship...and I want to break every bone in your body in the process of obtaining it. Fade to black.
