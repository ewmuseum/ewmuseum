Weekly BLOG

8 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/8/2004 I had received a lot of emails from my fans out there in Global Wrestling land this week and many of you were happy to see a look into a wrestlers point of view on life and work. This week I’ll cover my view of some recent events like the big Tag Team match from Evolution, the big mystery title match and much more. I am sure most of you who read this seen the match on Evolution Monday night the Tag Team Invitational Match to secure the #1 contender spot for the Tag Team Titles… Well it was one hell of a match. The only problem I seen was that me and Charles didn’t win the match. That’s ok though. The McNasty Boyz will eventually be the number one contenders and WILL win the Tag Team Titles. It’s like I said before, all things happen when they’re supposed to. It’s not a big deal to me right now, and Charles isn’t really all that worried about it. Eventually one thing is for damn sure. Were gonna get payback on Black and White’s punk asses. So as all of you heard during the week, there is going to be a huge Battle Royal, with like 7 other Global Superstars Monday Night. It’s actually a surprise to which title it is, which is interesting in it’s self really. I mean the element of surprise is always interesting you know. Personally, I don’t care what the title is, if I win it, great and I will defend it to the best of my ability and I will represent it well. If I don’t win it then it’s another thing that I will fight for. I’m here in Global Wrestling number one, to beat some ass and entertain the fans, and number two win some damn championships. I may be one to not gloat about my accomplishments, but don’t get me wrong, it would be some serious fun to win some of the Gold flying around here and set my name in stone forever. As many of you know, my Brother was right in saying that my Leprechauns had a chance of losing that match Monday night. Well you know how Leprechauns get, they get all drunk and the next thing you know they forget all about the rules and then chaos ensues. Oh well, I am really not at all worried about the court proceedings, personally I just see this whole thing getting tossed out. Let’s look at the facts, I’m not trying to deface midgethood, I just used it for my best interests as dressing them up as Leprechauns. I mean come on, I am paying these people well to be my Leprechauns. So they have a tendency to start a fight or two, big deal. This business is a lot being able to defend yourself and well my paid Leprechauns can do just that. You know you the fans like them as well, so if you’re their Monday night at the Global Arena, cheer my Leprechauns on. It’s time to wrap this snapshot into my life. Sunday is Mother’s Day and be sure to at least call your Mother and tell her you love her. I will be seeing my Mother tomorrow before I fly down to Dallas for Evolution Monday night. Me and Charles will probably be treating her to dinner or something like that. Our poor Mother, she put up with so much with us two when we were kids. I would get into that now, but I do have some business to attend to at Drunken Leprechaun. Those stories will be for another time. Take care ladies and gentleman, remember Evolution is Monday night on HBO… Until then have a great week.
