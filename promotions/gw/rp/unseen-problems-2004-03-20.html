Unseen Problems

20 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/20/2004 It had been quite a weekend for Chris Steel. Ever since attempting to change his fate by going back into the wrestling scene things had been great! Many opportunities had arose for the young superstar. He had been approached by many feds looking for talent. Each with their own unique qualities. But something was wrong, he couldn't make his choice, these federations practically threw themselves at him. With any luck he could avoid a dark match and be signed right in. On one hand he had heard great things from some friends in GW who had quickly rose to top notch fighters, but for some reason he hesitated, not because he wanted too, but because once he had opened a federations can of worms, there was no looking back. He had to really want it, and he thought he did, as he kept telling himself over and over again. He felt an overwhelming sense of nervousness, every federation said he had what it took and that, after years of hiatus that he still had more skill than some of the current applicants who had been signed, he was a diamond in the rough, gold in a copper mine, for some reason he had what it took. It was in his genes. One week earliar he had just been sitting around his rag tag house, with dirty clothes and dishes laying everywhere. And now, the spotlight had been pressed on him to make a choice, but choosing one would make him the center of attention. And that was one thing he didn't need. He was already on bad-terms with some people and being on TV would certanly be impossible to keep avoiding them. But Chris had already pondered this and decided that it was a risk he was willing to take. After all, how would anyone be able to get to him in the safety of his own breed? Wrestlers and fighters by nature. Once he had sided himself by the federation strongest fighters he would be free from harm. This state of mind made Chris realize that by joining a federation he wasn't only gaining a job, but freedom from harm. This hurried his decision and he had a federation in mind, a federation that had all the modern day wrestlers wants, good staff, good shows, and a long standing history. Yes, he had finally chose his federation. He reached into his backpack, put on a hat, although the logo could not be seen. He rummuged through his gear until he found a small business card. He picked up his cell phone, and dialed the number. On the other end a man picked up the reciever. 'Hello?' Chris said. 'Is this Global Wrestling?'...'Good, I have some news your going to like.'
