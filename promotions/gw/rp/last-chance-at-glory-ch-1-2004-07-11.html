Last Chance at Glory (Ch. 1)

11 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 11, 2004 Prologue. It’s been a long time since I’ve sat down and thought of my past. Most likely it’s because of all the dark doors that reopen when I think about it. So many times have I stumbled across matters that break me down and eat me alive on the inside... I wish to just hide them behind a locked door and throw the key away, but in my mind nothing stays locked but the feelings I think I control. Theirs many twisted and deranged thoughts that surpass my mind day in and day out, yet I’ve learned to deal with them and live life as normal as I can. People rant about me being someone different than they are, someone who they cannot relate to, and someone that either scares them, or is their hero. I believe these people don’t know the true ‘someone’ I really am. They don’t know the maniacal fiend inside of me. The behemoth of rage I carry in my heart to win the gold I love oh so much. Or the hate I possess for men who have backstabbed me, disgraced the name of wrestling, and who have made me feel disgust at them for their lack of commitment. The fans, they don’t know me well enough to judge me… Twelve years is not long enough to tell if a man is a man worthy of holding the gold, or a man worthy of jobbing to the champion. To me, the only way to judge is to feel the scorch of the one being judged. If your mother ever told you don’t judge others by how they look, then you know exactly what I mean. Victims of unknown evaluations are the stepping-stones to drunk drivers and armed robbers. So to judge me by my size, by my titles, or by my tattoos, would make you guilty of unneeded criticism. Just put yourself in that state of mind. A thousand hidden eyes watching you around the clock, judging your every movement, predicting your next step, and laughing at your every fall. It’s something that would scare the tar out of a smoker’s lungs. However just as bad as it is, it becomes something that also makes champions. Some people thrive for perfection, and by being pressured to win or to do something it pushes them in the direction they wish to go, to the top. For me… The top can be found at the end of the Master of the Mat Tournament. The Beginning. Shattering across the rooftop, the rain pours down from the heavens. Still air flows from the inside out, as a calm man sits upon a wooden oak chair near the window. Peaking out his eyes glisten a navy blue, as his facial expression shows a serene mood. His long hair tucked back in a ponytail that hangs over his right shoulder, like a defined poet. Exquisite facial hair comes to life upon his chin and around his mouth, like a forest that was trimmed to illustrate it’s beauty. Without a blink he turns his face so only have can be seen through the window, and stares now at the wall. Upon it are multiple pictures of his wife and him through their marriage, holidays, pleasant memories, and other various times. He smiles a half smile, in spite of the memories, he holds too much in him to give her a whole smile, for it hurts to do such. ’… I sometimes wonder, whether or not the after life is as good as this life.’ Speaking finally he spurts those words to himself, and just lingers with his lips barely open now. An almost saddened face is what you could make out of him, even though no expression is depicted. A man with so much is he, but drained of hope, heart, and desire. ‘Because if it is, I don’t want to come back… I’ve never seen something as deserted, or empty as I am. Mirrors don’t lie, I’m getting to that time where the only reason I’m alive is because I’ve got something to hope for to come to me. Yet even hope seems so far away…’ Leisurely he stands up, and paces across the room to the closet. Grabbing a blue jean jacket with the white ruffle around the neck, he opens the front door and heads outside wearing that and black jeans. Their in his drive way sits his baby, his black dodge truck. Getting in he starts the engine up and takes one look at the house… ’Now I’ll just dream that what I’m doing ends up being the right choice.’ … Before he puts the drive in reverse and backs out of the driveway and then heads off down the road. A New Beginning ’You really wish to do this Mr. Jesse sir?’ ’Yes… I have to sell the house.’ ’How much would you like the price to be for it?’ ’Well to cover all the bills I owe and such, I’d say… 3 Million. That decent?’ ’The house has stood for over 120 years Mr. Jesse, it could go for more… With only the little touches you’ve done for it, I’d say it’s easily worth 3 million, but why are you selling it?’ ’Too many memories… I can’t get a good nights rest in that house.’ ’Ah okay, I understand. Things like that are hard to overcome in life. So will you be able to put this sign up or shall I?’ ’I’m going back their now with the U-Haul and I’ll put it up and pack my stuff. Here’s my cell phone number incase you wish to reach me for any sales negotiations or good news. Any bad news, and I’ll be coming back here to meet you personally.’ He smiled and then wrote the number on a piece of paper and handed it to the man. Getting up from his chair he walked out of the room content with his decision. Too long had Jesse wept over his wife Julie, and now it was time to move on and start a new life. ’It feels great to finally move on. After all these years I can move on with my life, and possibly do something I want with the money I get. If it all pulls through, this time next year I’ll be sleeping in a hot sun ray bath down in the Bahamas thinking about my next career move with the GW World Title over my right shoulder and the PTC Global Title over my left. Ah… I can feel it now. I’m not a greedy man, I’m just one that wants to be remembered. If their was any other way, I’d do it in a heartbeat.’ So after selling his house, and packing up his belongings, only the ones he really wished to have, Jesse drove out of Calgary, Alberta Canada in search of the Dallas, Texas, and the home of the Global Wrestling Arena. What is Home? ’Home isn’t just another apartment you rent to live in, nor is it a place you’d sell for cash… My home, Alberta, Canada is one of a kind. I’d lived in that house for 14 years… It was more than another house, it was where I grew up, it was my life.’ Even though he sold his home, he knew in his heart it was where he grew up and where his initial home would always be. No matter how many people said it wasn’t his home anymore, Jesse knew that their was no other place he could call home than the place he had lived with his wife for six years and produced their first and only child, Samantha. ’Some would say what I do is unethical, the way I taunt in a match, or how I strive to go the extra mile even if its at the risk of the other competitor. But to me, it’s a way of life. If I have to live through my earnings, through my wins, then I’ll do whatever it takes to win. Because at the end of the day, the pay check I receive is one thing that will last me the week, but in the other hand, the wrestling legacy I love so much, the dream of being that top athlete in this sport… That also is a gift you cannot get without a worthy resume.’ Finally Jesse arrives into the Dallas, Texas where he searches for the Global Wrestling Arena. After thirty minutes of searching he finds it, the only building with a parking lot the size of Rhode Island. ’Winning the Master of the Mats tournament… That’s not something you get by just winning a single match and saying you’re the best, it’s a test of courage, heart, determination, stamina, and endurance to prove you’re the worthy man of winning the World Title.’ Parking his truck in the parking lot he gets out and looks to see his U-Haul still attached. Walking in to the Global Wrestling Arena he checks-in at the first level and then gets a pass to go upstairs and meet with an administrator. ’Hello Jesse! How are you today?’ Says a voice as Jesse comes walking out of the elevator into a personalized Administrator room. The walls are a covered with a shiny wooden pattern that was made out of American Cherry, vases of flowers and plants everywhere, a huge desk lies in the middle of the room with a gigantic painting on the wall behind it of a famous painting. ’How may I help you Jesse? Our superstars are always given the best in Global Wrestling so I wish to know how you may need our services.’ ’Well… I just sold my house, well put it up for sale, and I’m looking for a new home down here in Dallas, closer to my love, the Global Wrestling company.’ ’Great! So you’ll be able to get here faster and spend more time sleeping than traveling. I gotcha… Well, I know a good place only about 10 miles away. It’s where most famous people I know live, and it has some very nice mansions-‘ ’Famous people and I don’t mix, sorry… I’m a rowdy person; you don’t want me being arrested again now do you? So what about like a ranch or something? Have any of those near by?’ ’Heh, okay I understand. Yes, theirs quite a few ranches around here too. Here, I think I just saw one in the paper today. Check this out…’ Handing him the paper Jesse glances at it and checks the place out. Then he looks at the price, ‘$250 Grand.’ He rubs his goatee in thought, should he check it out, or should he save the money and wait for a better offer. ’I think I’ll wait and see what else comes along. For now… I’m going to go rent an apartment where I can stay a few weeks at until I see where this sale goes. Okay? Thanks for the help though, it was much appreciated.’ They shake hands before Jesse leaves. Though it may have been a great deal, it wasn’t something Jesse was looking for. In his mind he wasn’t sure he could stand living in Texas. It was so hot… Everything was so dry… And he’d always been one that’d call Canada his home. So, instead of buying the place he returned to his truck and sat down in the drivers seat staring outside for a little bit. ’… Some say that home is where the heart is. I say that home is where you wish to live, yet a place you can leave at any given time and come back to knowing it’ll still be the same. Dallas doesn’t seem to be the type of place for this Jackal. Maybe… I should stay in Canada? Eh forget it. I have to go somewhere and relax.’ With that he starts up the engine and leaves the Global Wrestling Arena’s parking lot. M.O.T.M. Can it be Beaten? After checking into a motel, and parking his U-Haul in his parking lot, Jesse went out to get some pressure off his mind as he had always done before big matches. How else would he do this, but by going to the Gym. However the gym he was going to was a Muscle and Fitness gym, one he could not find in Canada. So it was new to him and as he stepped through the front doors after registering, he found a place that looked like paradise. Barbells, discs, pipes, wires, and a door that said Jacuzzi, this gym had EVERYTHING Jesse thought, and after being a constant flyer and not lifting weights for a long time, he knew very well that his endurance wasn’t up to speed, and so that was his first thing he’d work on. ’This tournament… MOTM, it’s the biggest thing I’ve ever came across. From the time I started wrestling till now, I’ve never faced a feat such as this. The challenge is immense, the stage it’s on is huge, and the effort you have to put forth is well worth the prize you earn… A world title shot. I’d been in Global Wrestling for almost nine months, and now I could have my second and possibly my last chance, at real gold.’ He’d lost the a shot he earned in his first month in Global Wrestling when he lost to Akodo Rokku after winning Showdown in Tokyo. The defeat crushed him to the ground, but now, he was back, and without a doubt in his mind a stronger, healthier, faster opponent than 6 months ago. ’I’m going to win this… and then I’m going to prove to the world that I deserve it. Along the way though, I’m also going to show the world what monster really lives inside the other face of my demented Jackal Soul.’ … …… ……… ’Expect the Unexpected’
