Date: 7/24/03 The black Mitsubishi Eclipse glides effortlessly through the giant black gate that is the opening entrance to the Zero Estate. John Zero pilots the car around the large circle drive, past statues and shrubbery to the curb near the front door. Both car doors swing open, John Zero and Deacon Holmes exiting the car. Both men walked into the front room of the dark house, Zero looking around, puzzled. “Debra!” he shouted, waiting for the reply to come. “Debra?” Holmes asked, confused. “Yes, Debra. My assistant.” “Mhmm. And she would be here this late because?” “Because part of her contract states that she is a live-in assistant.” “Right.” “No. Its not like that. Well, she wants it. I could care less.” “Mhmm.” “Shut up.” “I didn’t say anything.” “Just”.” Zero’s voice trails off as he notices a third man standing in the shadows. “You’re losing it, John,” the man says quietly. “If you say so, Matthew.” Holmes looks between Zero and the man named Matthew. Zero stands with his feet apart, one fist clenched, the other a flat edge, ready to pounce. Matthew stood with a similar stance, but looked tense, nervous almost. “You two know each other?” Holmes asked. “We’re old”..business acquaintances, you could say. Right, John?” “You could say that. What do you want Matthew?” “Always to the point, John. A few days ago, you and Mr. Holmes here relieved my employer of a briefcase, and I have come to return it to its rightful owner.” “I can’t give you what I don’t have, Matthew.” “Where is it then?” “I’m afraid that I can’t tell you that, either.” Matthew pulls out a gun, moving it from Zero to Holmes and back to Zero. “Where’s Debra?” Zero asks. “She’s nearby. She’s safe.” “You know what I’ll do if you hurt her.” “I know. She’s not another Lily, is she John?” “NEVER EVER SAY HER NAME!” Zero exclaims, causing both Holmes and Matthew to jump. “You don’t have a right to say it,” he seethes quietly. “Ooooh. You done pissed him off, Mr. I have a gun. I seen what this motherfucker can do when he’s not pissed. I hate to see what he’s gonna do to you now that he’s pissed. I’d run if I were you.” Holmes laughes. Matthew cocks his head to one side, and fires the gun, the bullet striking Holmes in the chest, on the bulletproof vest, causing him to fall to the ground.. In the time it took Matthew to fire the weapon, Zero lunged forward, tackling Matthew to the ground, the two professionals rolling around struggling for the weapon. Holmes leans up slowly, picking himself up. “Motherfucker just shot me. I’ma whoop your ass.” Holmes starts over to the two struggling men. In a desperation move, Matthew jerks the gun and fires again, catching Holmes in the chest again, dropping Holmes once again. Zero hits Matthew in the arm, sending the gun flying across the room. Matthew lashes out with a side kick, which Zero catches, twists, uses to throw Matthew to the ground. Unfortunately, the gun is nearby, which Matthew snatches up quickly. “Motherfucker,” Holmes moans, as he gets back up. Not missing the opportunity, Matthew shoots him again in the chest. “That’s it goddamnit! I’ma lay right here till you assholes get done. Shootin” a brotha in the chest, three motherfucking times, shit ain’t funny. Assholes.” Matthew whirls around, pointing the gun at where Zero was standing, only to find him gone. “Pussy,” Matthew whispers. “YOU PUSSY! Running away from me. You always were a pussy, John. You?” Matthew’s voice becomes garbled, as blood begins to flow freely from his neck. Zero appears from the shadows behind Matthew, a pen dripping with blood in his hand. “You can get up now, Deac.” “You two done?” “He’s done, yeah.” Holmes gets up slowly, looking at the body on the floor. “Ohh. He dead.” “Yeah.” Holmes limps over to where Matthew’s body lay. “Motherfucker.” Holmes starts kicking the side of Matthew’s body. “Shoot a brotha in the chest, three goddamn times.” Holmes pulls out his silver Desert Eagle. “I’ma fuck you up!” He shoots Matthew in the chest three times. “How the fuck does that feel” Huh? Huh? Huh? Motherfucker.” John watches as Holmes continues to wail on Matthew. Faintly, some sobbing can be heard. Zero walks over to the closet door, opening it, revealing Debra, sobbing, in tears. “You okay?” Zero asks. “Yes sir, I’ll be fine.” Zero nods. “Are you hurt?” “No sir.” “You can call me John now.” “Yes sir, I mean, John.” “I need you to do something for me Debra.” “Yes?” “I need to step out for a while. Look after Deacon for me.” She looks over at Deacon, as he continues to kick Matthew, screaming profanities. “I will.” “Good.” Zero walks over to Holmes, pulling him off from Matthew. “Deac, I need to step out. Need some time to myself. Look after Debra, will you?” Holmes looks over a Debra. “Yeah man, whatever. Hey, what we gonna do with the body?” “Leave it. I’ll handle it when I get back.” “Aight man.” Zero walks out the door, getting into the Eclipse. Pulling out a black cellphone, he dials a number that he memorized the moment he heard the woman say it. He listens to the clicking of the phone connecting as a female voice is heard answer it. “Violet?” he whispers. Lily lived in a duplex not too far from the college campus. The outside was not too bad looking, a beige stucco surrounded by green bushes. She answered the door on the first knock. “I hope you don’t mind if I dressed a little down today.” She was wearing a dark green sweat suit, except the top was more of a jacket with a white tee shirt underneath. Her hair was pulled back into a loose ponytail and she wore very little makeup. She was out of the neck-brace and most of the bruises were gone. “You look better than I do,” I said, gesturing to my attire of faded jean and a black tee shirt. The inside of the house was small, but big enough for one, maybe two people. There was a tiny kitchen, a dining area, a living room, a bedroom that she had converted into an office, and stairs that lead to what I assumed was her bedroom and bathroom. “I took the liberty of picking up some movies and ordering some Chinese take-out. The guy should be here in a minute,” she said. We made some small talk about the weather and what we planned to do on our upcoming break from school. Lily planned on going out of state to visit her folks. I said I didn’t know what I was gonna do. The delivery guy arrived and we proceeded to gorge ourselves on the food that Lily had ordered. We had abandoned the dining table and sat on the sofa, watching movies while we ate. The hours and the movies passed, going from comedy to thriller and from romance to action. The sun began to set, bathing the room in soft shades of red and pink. The last movie was rolling its credits when the last of the stars began to shine. Lily was pressed close to me, her body under my arm and her head resting on my chest. I turned off the TV with the remote. Turning slightly, I tucked my other arm under her legs and lifted her off the sofa. Mounting the stairs, I climbed up to the second floor. Sure enough, there was a loft bedroom. I took Lily over to the bed and laid her down. I leaned over and kissed her forehead. “Goodnight,” I whispered, turning to leave. “Don’t go,” I heard Lily say. I looked back and saw her staring at me. “Stay with me tonight.” “Where?” I asked. She slid over and pointed to the spot next to her on the bed. Hell, why not. I took off my shoes and lay down next to her. She moved closer to me and rested a hand on my chest. She kissed me on the cheek. Lily was soon asleep. I stared at the ceiling, listening to her breathing. Soon, I was off to dreamland. I awoke to find Lily staring at me. Big green eyes studying every inch of my face. Searching for some greater meaning of who I am. Good luck, girl. I don’t even know who I am anymore. I started to get up but she stopped me. “Where you going?” “Bathroom. Where is it?” My voice came out hoarse and strained from the night’s sleep. Lily pointed to a closed door. I got up and went in, shutting the door behind me. Turning the cold water on, I splashed it on my face, shaking the final remains of sleep away. Looking around, I found some mouthwash to get rid of my foul morning breath. I went back into the bedroom. Lily was sitting on the edge of the bed. “Come here,” she said in a whispered tone. I walked over to her. She reached up and took my hand, rubbing her cheek on the inside of my palm. She did this for several seconds and stopped. Getting up, Lily wrapped her arms around my neck, drawing me close to her. She brought her head towards mine and kissed me. We stood like this for what seemed an eternity. Bodies pressed together, arms encircling one another. Oblivious to the whole world. Lily pulled away and broke the kiss. I stood there breathlessly. I could’ve died happy. Still looking at me and taking several steps back, she took off the matching dark green over-shirt and pulled the white top that was underneath over her head. She stood there before me, half-naked, tan-body glowing in the pale morning sunlight. Walking towards me, her bra-less breasts slightly swaying in time with her movement, Lily reached up towards my neck again. I took a step back. “Are you sure about this?” I asked. “I was sure the day I met you. I knew then that you were really the one for me, not that bastard Bryce. I was always afraid of what would happen if I left him. I’m not afraid any more. All because of you being here for me when I needed it.” I thought of Bryce. The look of surprise I caught on his face before the bomb blew. Bryce, the little clumps of bloody flesh in the front yard. The hunk of Bryce on my windshield, lying there as worthless as he had been in life. I smiled and embraced Lily, holding on to her tightly. We made love that morning. Not the high pace stripping of clothes, fast and furious pumping, over and done with in five minutes flat with time left to smoke a cigarette shit that you see on TV and in the movies. No, this was special. It was slow. Savoring every moment, every inch of flesh. Bodies moving in time with one another. Pleasure and ecstasy on both Lily’s face and mine. Oh, to be here forever. But all good things must come to an end.
