The Past And The Present

28 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2.28.04 'There are only three sins - causing pain, causing fear, and causing anguish. The rest is window dressing.' -Roger Caras --- July 30th, 2003 - Dallas, Texas 27 Griffin's Way - Reno's Dallas Office The scene opens to show Jason Hughes standing outside of the building; he's wearing a black business suit and a long black overcoat. He has his hair tied back and is wearing a pair of sunglasses. A limousine waits in front of him and after a moment, a voice calls from inside. 'What are we waiting for?' 'They said someone was coming down to get me.' The scene remains the same for a moment with Hughes casually taking in his surroundings. Finally, a pair of men in suits come out accompanied by none other than John Zero. The trio make their way towards Hughes who turns, nodding as to acknowledge Zero. 'Ready, Mr. Hughes?' 'I'm always ready.' 'Then follow me.' The four men walk up the steps outside of the building and through a set of glass doors. The scene transitions, switching to an office. The room is very modern; glass and steel. The desk however is a solid piece of Mahogany with the top inset and a solid slab of black and blue marble lain in as the table top; a truly beautiful piece. A knock comes at the door and from around a corner, Naria Xiou appears and within the half-second, has the door open. 'Gentlemen. Please have a seat and Mr. Reno will be out shortly.' Zero nods and motions for Hughes to sit in front of the desk. Zero then takes a seat next to him, the two guards behind them both. 'Reno huh? Who woulda thought he was the one behind The Echelon. Of all the people I met in Gw...' Zero nods and glances towards Hughes. 'Who is better than the least suspected, yes?' Hughes nods and reclines in the plush chair, his eyes checking out the very sterile room. A sound can be heard and both look to their right where the wall is nothing but a large mirror; suddenly, it opens in the center, a seem that could not be seen before, and out steps Maverick Reno. He walks towards the desk, his eyes downcast on a folder in which he holds in his hands. He wears a pair of black dress pants and Armani wingtips along with a red silk shirt and black tie. He sits at the desk and places the folder down. He glances at the two men and nods. 'Gentlemen. Mr. Hughes; very glad you could make it today.' Jason Hughes simply nods and leans forward. 'So what're you offering, Maverick? And what is it you want me to do?' Reno smiles and leans back. 'I like your priorities, Jason. First you ask the salary, and then you ask the job. It's very simple; I've assembled a group of the best, the brightest, and the most ruthless and you, my friend, fit into that elite category. I need a man who's not afraid to get his hands dirty; someone not afraid to handle the gritty part of the business. Someone with enough creativity to scare others away from screwing us. John, Deacon, Sean, Tyler and I have tried our best in this realm but I believe it's time we called in a true expert.' Hughes nods and glances at his fingernails with a grin, obviously putting up a front. 'I see you only settle for the best, Reno. But now my second question.' Reno nods and turns the page in the folder before spinning it around to face Jason and then sliding it to him. 'I believe you will find that satisfactory.' Jason picks up the folder, his eyes scanning over everything. 'The details you can read for yourself. Basically, the offer is three million flat; of course you get the perks of belonging to the most elite group in the world. Then of course, there's the bi-monthly salary for your services when called for. Of course, we'll provide all transportation as well as complete security twenty-four-seven. Is it a deal?' Jason flips the page, continuing to read the details. 'A dental plan? Damn, I'd be a fool to turn this down. But what about advancement?' 'We'll discuss that when the time comes.' Jason nods and puts the paper down; he signs his name and slides it across the marble top to Reno. 'Then we have a deal.' Reno smiles and nods his head. 'How does it feel to sell your soul?' 'I feel heavier... about three million heavier.' Reno smiles and reaches inside the desk and pulls out a metal lock box. He slides it across the marble top and then tosses another velvet box on top of it. 'In the metal lock box you'll find your pay. In the velvet box, the key and combination; you'll need both to get inside it. I'm the only other person with access. Also, inside the lock box is an ID swipe; there's no information printed on it and the encoding is the most advanced. Welcome to the Echelon, Mr. Hughes.' 'Thank you, Maverick. Now what of my first assignment?' 'A simple one really. It would seem that Justin Rydell, normally a solid informant, has been more of an informant that he needs to be. Seems he may have leaked information to another. Make sure it never happens again... and that he learns his lesson in the process. His info is on the last pages of the folder.' Jason nods and stands; he extends his hand as Reno stands and both shake. John Zero stands and the two shake hands as well. The two bodyguards step in behind Jason Hughes as he walks out of the room, Zero and Reno remaining behind. The door closes behind the bodyguard duo and the newest member of the Echelon. 'I believe we've made a good decision, Maverick.' 'I do too, John. He'll make a great addition to the council as well as keep away a lot of our information leaks. I hear he's quite good at making a statement.' The scene fades to black. --- August 1st, 2003 Channel 9, Dallas This is a Channel 9 Breaking News Report! A male reporter appears on the screen in front of a warehouse in downtown Dallas, Texas. The area is taped up tightly and police are keeping a strict perimeter. 'Chris Rex with Channel 9 News on the scene in Dallas Texas. Behind me is the old Dallas Printing Press warehouse; before moving to a new location, this is where most of Dallas's newspapers came from. And for the first time in nearly five decades, this building has made the news. We are moments away from Police Chief Dannon's official announcement.' The scene cuts away and then returns to show a police officer standing at a podium. 'Ladies and gentlemen of the press and of Dallas. Although I will not distribute all details of today's events, I am prepared to explain the situation. Earlier today, we received an anonymous call regarding the warehouse behind us. When a patrol car arrived and investigated, what they found was horrendous. A single victim appears to have been executed in a very sacrificial and detailed manner. We are unable to divulge any information regarding identity or cause of death, I will tell you that this is unlike any homicide I have ever seen after twenty years on the force. More will be released as it is uncovered. Thank you.' We cut back to Channel 9 reporter, Chris Rex. 'I've just received word that a video tape has been submitted to channel 9. What you are about to see could be very graphic; parents be advised.' The scene cuts to a low-quality home video of what appears to the same warehouse in the early hours of the morning. The cameraman, unknown, walks inside where fog lights have been set up so that the scene may be told. The floor is covered in a pool of blood, the old printing presses showered in the crimson liquid. Clothing, ripped and torn, lay about the floor as well. A man's body can be seen hanging from between the rollers of the printing press. Blood has soaked the remaining paper in the machine. Another machine seems to be running; the camera advances towards it, careful not to touch any of the blood. Paper is being printed on with a mysterious red ink; on the paper are the words: 'Information can be a deadly thing.' The scene cuts to static. --- 'And that's what happens when you fuck with us. You're made an example of. Most of the time when a person is used as an example because of something they've done, they're the last example made. In our line of work however, you never really get credit for exemplifying this person. I guess that's why we've made so many people out to be examples. So why do we bother with examples? We figure that eventually, they'll get the hint and if not, then it's atleast a more creative way of doing it than putting them in a cement block and letting them sink to the bottom of a river or lake, right? That old clich? of going to sleep with the fishes was over done years ago; when you mess with us, we never fit a clich?... we make you wish we had.' June 12th, 2003 - Approximately 8:00pm London, Ontario, Canada - Estate of Maverick Reno The scene opens to show the home of Maverick Reno, nestled in the hills of a vacant part of Ontario; a place where the snow falls continuously but it's never a problem. A blanket of white is the norm, covering the land for miles and miles; as far as the eye can see. A dense evergreen forest lay in the distance; that too covered in a beautiful blanket of white, powered purity. Nearly everything is blanketed... excluding the circle drive of the illustrious Maverick Reno. On this night, the drive has been thoroughly cleared by a scraper and the heating pipes built into the concrete. Numerous vehicles are parked about the circle drive, all attended by men in black suits... and fur parkas. Their breath can be seen floating in the lit night air about their faces. The first vehicle, a stretch limousine furnished by the Echelon. The second, an 03 Ashton Martin, also partially furnished by the Echelon. The third, an Eclipse Spyder. The fourth: a black Expedition complete with 22' chrome spinners; the fifth would be your average hummer (atleast, from the outside). The Echelon fully united for perhaps the first time outside of the Global Arena. The scene transitions to a large rec room inside of Reno's home; a room complete with a small home theatre, numerous recreational games such as pool, card and chess tables, and even a small room for boxing, sparring, wrestling, or fencing. On one side of the room, a large double oak door had been propped open and inside, seated in a semi-circle of leather recliners, were the elite. From left to right: Deacon Holmes, Jason Hughes, Leinad Soma, John Zero, Tyler Pierce, Sean Price, Tony Stecchino. Empty seats were scattered about on the sides of the gentlemen and some between. The men's conversations were abundant, having all been in the room for some time. Obviously, this was months before the example execution of Tony Stecchino. Moments pass and the conversations continue; one in particular draws interest. 'So what do you think of the group, Sean?' 'Well, I have to say, uh, we're a diverse crowd.' 'Hah, no shittin'. I think we've got something good here, though. I mean look to our left, man.' The two men, Tyler and Sean, look to the left at the others in the group. Deacon is seated on the arm of one of the chairs, chatting with Zero and Soma. Hughes is somewhat involved and Tony Stecchino... the only man to their right, is staring off blankly for the moment. 'We have former GW stars... an expert thief, or so it would seem, an assassin, a ... pro Mafioso, an ex football star - you - a multi-million dollar business man in myself, and a man who.. er. Well I use the term man 'lightly' I guess. He's more of a monster but ... most definitely an asset.' Sean nods a few times and leans back, putting his arms behind his head. 'Not to mention... this is going to make us all filthy rich... Yeah yeah... I know. Some of us already are. But damn, filthy rich AND untouchable. Now this is the life.' Tyler nods and sits back. 'The Echelon, Sean. To the Echelon...' Tyler stands and raises his glass before everyone, catching everyone's attention. 'TO THE ECEHELON!' With this, everyone simultaneously and almost all in unison, throws back their glass and kills off their remainder. Reno's voice comes in right after the toast and the camera pans quickly to show him standing at the entrance, Naria Xiou at his side. 'Gentlemen... The Echelon is more than a group. We're more than filthy rich; money is only a minute part of the benefits from this great union of the elite. We're more than untouchable... We, gentlemen, Members of the Echelon, are Gods among men!' The group again raise their glasses as Reno walks in, Naria refilling each man's glass. She steps off to the side and smiles. 'And our might shall be known by all.' The scene slowly fades to black. 'A history scattered with victory and triumph, pain and hatred. Discrimination towards all and mercy towards none. That is what the Echelon has progressed through.' February 24th, 2004 - Approximately 1:00pm London, Ontario, Canada - Estate of Maverick Reno The scene opens to show the office of the one, Maverick Reno. A marble floor is the foundation of this most exquisite room; cherry stained book cases line the walls, the backs of which appear to be tempered black glass. Marble columns stand, pressed against the walls and tightly tucked between the bookcases so as not to obstruct movement in the room. A large picture window consumes one wall, the light from outside illuminating most of the room. The bookcases encircle the bottom and sides of this window, it's curtains, a dark red with black tassels, have been tied off to the sides to allow in the much needed light. Through the window, one can see the beautiful lawn of the Reno Estate; completed by the landscaping that has been done. Although right now, covered in a blanket of snow. On the opposite side of the room, a tall duo of cherry stained oak doors. Carved into the oak, exquisite designs have been carved. Celtic roses and crosses intermingling with Japanese Kanji for the words: 'Pride' 'Honor' 'Triumph' and 'Victory.' The handles are ivory and golden, accenting the room perfectly. To each side of this, a scroll hangs, each carrying different markings. The white scrolls travel down the walls until just above the marble floor, adding to the markings on the door. Kanji's have been scripted in beautiful calligraphy upon these scrolls, there meanings unknown to most viewers. Above all of this, mounted and lain into the wall is a coat of arms; The Echelon's coat of arms. Another source of light comes from above, a large stained glass sky-light in the design of a red horizon silhouetting the Ankh of Reno adds to the exquisite design of this most beautiful office. In the center of the room, a semi-circular desk made of Cherry stained Oak and processed marble, complete with ivory and golden accents. On one side of the desk, a golden pen set and a small stack of papers. On the other, a flat screen monitor complete with keyboard and mouse with a single half-inch in diameter cord running down and into the floor, carefully fitted to the desk's leg. The monitor, keyboard, and mouse, obviously run to a server or computer somewhere in the building. On the opposite side of the desk, you can see the disk drives built into the side of the desk. A mixture of Latin phrases and Kanji's have been carved into the edge of the oak and then filled with silver, creating a truly elaborate finish to the desk. A large black leather chair has been placed behind the desk, it's frame built of the same Cherry finished Oak. On the opposite side, two leather chairs framed in the same cherry stained oak. The master and commander of this exquisite office? Maverick Reno. He sits in the large, plush, leather chair, his eyes fixated on the computer screen. He's adorned in a blue silk shirt and black dress pants, a black tie and silver tie clip. His hair has been tied back into a tight pony tail; the look and atmosphere give a truly ominous and powerful feel to the man. Or is it simply that this man truly is ominous and powerful? 'Come in.' His voice rings forth, answering the knock that came at his door just moments before. The right-hand door of the duo swings open and in steps Naria Xiou. She smiles at Reno and bows her head in a respectful manner. 'You wished for me to inform you when Mr. Pierce had arrived. He is here sir, shall I send him in?' Reno sits back in his chair and nods, his eyes drifting away from the screen. He hits a few last keys and nods again. 'Yes.... yes, send him in. Hold my calls for the next hour or so; I wish for our conversation to be without interruption.' 'Yes, sir.' With this quick exchange, Naria steps to the side and motions towards one of the desk chairs. Footsteps and then the image of Tyler Pierce. He nods to Naria and then looks towards Maverick; he opens his arms, his alabaster suit adding a strict contrast to the darkness of the room. He grins a cocky Grin. 'Mav; how are ya?' Reno stands and extends his hand as Tyler takes a few steps forward to shake. Naria closes the large oak door behind her. 'There've been worse times, there've been better times. Have a seat old friend.' The two men take their seats, facing each other across the cold desk. 'So what's up? You didn't just call me in here to chat. You've got something on your mind and I want to hear it.' 'You're right, Tyler. You're one of The Echelon's trusted advisors, yes?' Tyler nods and leans back in his chair. 'I'd like to think so.' Reno shakes his head yes for a moment then cracks his neck as he begins to speak, the pop louder than it should be. 'I've got Jesse Jamester in a few days and it seems as if he plans to make the night interesting. I don't mean that he should lie down; I'd be insulted by that. But there are a lot of orchestrated plans in place for evening and I'd hate for him to screw with any of them.' Tyler nods and holds up his hand. 'You're worried that he might mess up the night how?' 'Not in the ring. In the ring he's mine, hook line and sinker. He's too... cocky to be anything more than a nuisance. I know what he's all about, I've seen a few of his matches. His got talent but then again, so did Robson. So did Almasy. Look at what happened there. Robson was out in less than a minute once I finally hit him. Almasy, I put out. It took a bit more effort but I still put him on the mat and that's where he remained. That's the same thing that will happen to Jamester. I don't care what he says about me, towards me, to me. That's what will happen.' 'Eh, Isn't that a little cocky, Mav?' 'No. That's simply confidence. Confidence in my abilities. Confidence that there's no way my winning streak will end. Confidence that I am the best to step into the ring and the best to walk out. No, I'm not cocky. I just know what I'm talking about.' Tyler nods again and opens up a small box on Reno's desk. He pulls a cigar from inside then lights it quickly before puffing on it a few times. 'So... you what? You're worried that he might piss you off?' 'No. I'm worried that he might get overzealous. Think himself a bit better than he is or worse, become desperate in the knowledge that his loss is eminent. I want you and Naria to stand watch over all Echelon activities on the 29th, Tyler. I trust both of you and I want both of you there. Also, I want security doubled around the VIP room. We can't risk any flaws in our plans and you know that. It's too risky.' Pierce again nods, inhaling a deep breath from the cigar then exhaling it to the side. 'You always have the best cigars, Mav. But no problem; Naria and I can stand watch. Between the consiliu in action and the advisors on watch, nothing will get by us. Not even a flea will be able to sneak past our eyes that night.' Maverick smiles and leans back in his chair. 'Also, except for when they have to be, I don't want the consiliu out and about. It's too risky. We need to keep the group tightly knit for protection purposes that night. Also, if they are out, make sure security is nearby as well as watchers. We can't let anything go wrong. Security needs to be top notched.' 'You know, this is a huge event. We can't protect everyone at every turn.' Reno nods and sighs. 'I know this. But we'll cover most of our ground and keep any attacks from being too serious. This is war, Mr. Pierce. In war, it's not the battles that count as much as who pays in the signing of the treaty.' Pierce smiles and nods. 'I understand. As long as we can keep everyone and our perimeter secure, we'll be fine. Also, should I have the van prepared?' Reno shakes his head no and glances at the paperwork on his desk. 'Naria has already dealt with all of the preparations.' Tyler again nods. 'And what of mole?' Reno smiles and nods again. 'Naria again. She's taking care of those as we speak in fact. I believe Melissa is helping as well.' Tyler taps the ashes from the end of his cigar, letting them fall into the ashtray on the edge of the desk. 'So everything really is in order. Oh... what of the production truck?' Reno nods quickly and leans forward. He opens a drawer on his side of the desk and pulls out a manila envelope. He tosses it to Tyler and speaks again, his voice calm and planned. 'Give this to Eric on the production staff complete with the link information when it's ready. Sorry, Tyler. We will have to leave you at the arena just long enough to have that part done. We'll make sure a sedan and two of our best guards are waiting with you though.' 'It's no problem, Mav. You sure Eric will do it?' Reno nods. 'We have an agreement. I give him the tape or link when I hand him the grand, he plugs it into the Globaltron, no questions asked. Yes. He'll do it... he's helped us out in the past.' 'Good, atleast he's reliable.' 'Very.' Tyler reclines in his chair, his eyes feasting on the beauty that is this office. 'So are the boys ready? John, Deac... yourself?' Again, a smile graces the face of Maverick Reno. He leans forward and nods again, his eyes moving slowly to meet with Tyler’s. 'They're in the best shape of their lives. Even with the recent gold loss, they're doing marvelously. I don't think anything can stop them right now. Deacon has a new vigilance... John is the same old Renaissance man you don't want to fuck with. And me? Hah! I have a streak that I don't plan on letting break, Tyler. I'm going in to win and walk out a winner. When this is over, I think I'll have my status back as the unbreakable, Maverick Reno. You know what I want and I can get it. I look at the two men in the battle for the world title... and to be honest, it sickens me. Akodo is like a bad American Anime. He has all the make up of a Japanese clich? but he's as American as Apple Pie and the girl next door. I don't know what to make of him, really. The only thing purely authentic about him is Yoshiko.' Tyler laughs a bit, shaking his head. 'Hilarious, Mav. Absolutely hilarious... Mav, man. I know what you're saying and I agree one-hundred-and-ten-percent. But he is quite the athlete. Look at he and Ice's I qui...' Reno shakes his head and pulls out a disk from his desk. He inserts it into his computers drive and spins the monitor. Within a second, the infamous 'I Quit' match begins. 'You mean that sham? I'm not impressed with that and you know it, Tyler. Yes, it took a lot of endurance but did it prove anything? No. It was nothing but two men seeing how much they could hurt each other. Not an ounce of talent was needed in that; add one woman they both love into the match and they both would have lost. Understand what I mean? It's all about perspective and leverage.' Tyler nods, Reno's words making sense on some levels. 'Ice. That's an interesting story. We save his career in Global Wrestling, pay everyone of that man's fines, sponsor him, and even get the McCoy's off his back, take him under our wing and what does he do? He takes a shank to our ribcage, that's ribcage. That's what he does. Ice is a fool if he thinks he can just walk away from The Echelon. No one walks away from the Echelon. He'll soon know this... as will the Nakazura's. Stecchino betrayed us and look at him. Or rather, look at his plot in Italy. These people, Tyler, they've got to learn that The Echelon does not play these games of petty differences. If you have a problem with us, then your ass had best be covered pretty damned quick. The Nakazura's will get what's coming as will Ice although, he owes us quite a bit more.' Tyler again taps his cigar, letting the ashes fall into the ashtray. He glances up at Reno as he nears the end of his monologue on Ice and the Nakazura’s. 'Do you think Sunday will be enough, Mav? I mean, you just said it. He owes us a lot.' 'Oh Sunday is just the beginning; just a sign of the times, my friend. A sign of the times. He was wise in forming the Elite. He has a bit of protection on his back now; it makes him a harder target. But any man marked by The Echelon has no protection, they simply endanger everyone around them. Ice will learn that soon enough.' Tyler grins and leans forward. 'Coming for him?' Reno grins a bit and leans back in his chair; he kicks his wingtips up and onto the edge of the desk, his eyes searching the stained glass ceiling for any flaw, finding none. 'Oh yeah. Him... Ichiro and Jushin... Akodo... Jamester... Almasy. All of them. I'm coming for all of them.' Tyler smiles and sits back in his chair. 'God. It's good to be on our way to the top of the food chain again, isn't it?' Reno nods, his eyes continuing to search the ceiling. 'We never left the top, Tyler. We just let those below us have hope for awhile. It makes it more fun when you can squash their hopes and dreams in one foul swoop. Oh yes... so much more fun.' The scene slowly fades to black, the room submerging into the emptiness of the screen. The Ankh of Reno fades up, a classic mark of his influence. Words slowly fade in around that... I'm coming for you.
