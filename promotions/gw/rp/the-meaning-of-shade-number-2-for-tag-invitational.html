The Meaning of 'Shade' (Number 2 for Tag Invitational)

Date: Two Years Ago and Today -Two Years Ago- We open to a plush, silk and velvet style room, the kind of room where the furniture is so rich you’re afraid to breath on it for fear of loss of value. Standing in the center of the room is Miranda, dressed in a gray, one-piece suit, gray boots with soft padding, and a headdress that hides her hair from view. Before her stands a man dressed in Armani from head to toe, his eyes settled on Miranda with an intense gaze, his mustached lip quirking slightly as he speaks. Man: “You understand that if anything happens to you’you’re on your own, yes?” Miranda: “I’m aware of the risks, Mr. Sienne.” Sienne nods thoughtfully as he looks Miranda over from head to toe for a few silent heartbeats, before stepping back from her and rounding the large mahogany desk behind him, sitting down in a large leather chair and leaning back. Sienne: “The payoff is ten grand, like I said. That should cover the debt owed to this casino and get yourself and your sister out of trouble. If you fail this, Miranda, I will take it out of Kali’s hide. Am I understood?” Miranda nodded once, but inside her head her anger flared. Her “parents- had lost a ton of money here, but just like them, they didn’t feel the need to pay it, leaving Miranda and Kali’once again’in a world of trouble. Her parents were influential in this city, but she could care less. They knew what would happen, and they didn’t care’much as Miranda didn’t care what she was about to do to get herself and Kali out of trouble. Miranda: “I understand, Mr. Sienne. Time is running short, Mr. Yokuto will be arriving at his hotel shortly’and I should be there to await him.” Sienne: “Yes. For reasons unknown to me, I’m going to tell you good luck. Now get going.” Miranda nodded and turned on her heel, walking through the side door left open for her and into the a dark and damp tunnel way. Miranda reached up and placed the jet black mask across her face to hide her features as she walked, following the secret tunnel towards its destination’two floors below the room where she was about to do something that only three months ago horrified her beyond belief. -Flash Forward To Now- Miranda tossed a little, causing Allie to awaken slightly and look at her, noticing the sweat beginning to form on Miranda’s brow. Thanks to her having family on the NYPD’and being rich to boot’Allie had been able to keep Miranda and Kali out of jail, and made sure Nik would either stay there, or get sent back to Vegas permanently. But ever since coming to bed, Miranda had been moaning and tossing in her sleep, leaving Allie wondering if she should wake her or just ignore it and go back to sleep. Allie: “Mira” Baby, are you ok?” No response as Miranda seems to settle again into the bed, a muscle in her jaw flexing unconsciously. -Two Years Ago- The door opened, and in stepped her target. She eyes him from head to toe, taking into mind his short, 5’4? frame, but the part that caught her eye the most was the glint of metal against like coming from the right sleeve of his cheap, black suit. Gun, no doubt. She remained quiet in her hiding place, lost in the dark shadows of the corner of the room, simply watching and waiting for the right moment. Never face on a gun user head first, that point had been in drilled into her head over the last ten years of instruction my Master Staz, and she wasn’t about to forget it. Her life, and her sister’s life’were on the line here. He crossed the room towards her, and it was now or never’she would prefer never’but, in this case’it’s her life, or his. She moved, catching him off guard with a sidekick that sent him to the bed! But fuck! The gun is spring loaded, and before she can move to finish him off, the barrel of the .45 is already tracking her! BAM! BAM! Too close! Miranda hits the floor in a roll, drawing a long dirk from her sleeve. Yelling outside the door’fuck! BAM! BAM! Two more shots that miss, and she’s on her knees’drawing her arm back? -Today- Len: 'Kali, c’mon? There’s no need to punish yourself like this?' Len’s voice was a soft plea as he kneeled down before Kali, who sat in the middle of her branched off room within the condo. Dressed in baggy martial arts pants and a tight sports bra, she sits in a FULL split! Positioned around her in burners are several sticks of incense, all lit and delivering the sweet smell of jasmine to the room. Her left eye is swollen, and black, as well as her neck plays host to a deep, black bruise in the shape of a hand. Kali: 'I was stupid, Len, and now I must atone, or I’ll be in no shape to fight this invitational?' Len blinks, and tilts his head. For someone so beautiful, she is easily one of the oddest people he knows? No one is perfect, hmm? Len: 'Atone for what?! The only person who did wrong was Niklaus!' Kali: 'I let him in? I trusted him? I lied to Mira by assuring her I could refuse him? I made Mira do things she never wanted to do again, just to save me? It’s my fault Len; and unless I atone for it, we won’t be able to work together, we’ll lose? And we CAN NOT lose!' Her final sentence was filled with fear and anger both; evident in her eyes which were now welling with tears. Len sighs, and slowly shakes his head. Len: 'Kali? Mira isn’t angry with you, she’s happy that you’re alive now? You haven’t ate in almost two days, you need to?' Kali shakes her head. Kali: 'No. Not until she comes and tells me to get up. It’s her I owe, it’s her who can release me from my fasting; until then, I will sit here, and punish myself. Now please leave, you don’t wanna see my reminder of my mistake.' Len’s eyes narrow slightly and he watches Kali questionably? She turns her attention to her wrists and begins to undo one of the leather bracers covering it. A sickening feeling comes to his stomach as his eyes settle upon the heavily scarred skin, a few straight slices, while some seem to be characters, ritualistic lettering of some kind? Len: 'Kali? What the fuck?' Kali: 'We all have our way of dealing, dude? don’t interfere?' Len groans and his brow furrows as Kali brandishes a small razor from beside herself. He reaches forward to grasp her hand, but she quickly retracts it and growls up at Len in a menacing manner. Len: 'Kali! Don’t! You’re too beautiful to harm yourself like this! It isn’t necessary! Give me the razor?' Kali: 'You’ve no right to tell me what is right in wrong in my life? Now get out?' Len starts to argue, but Kali huffs, and turns her attention away? He then shakes his head, deciding this a much easier task for Miranda to handle herself. With a huff of air, he climbs to his feet and turns from the room, coughing at the thick smell of the incense before moving to search for Miranda. Kali sighs, and turns her attention to her wrist. Pushing the razor into the now bleeding skin, she carefully begins to carve a small rune, the pain evident in her cry-stricken words? Kali: 'I’m sorry, Mira? Let me be reminded of my stupidity. I’m never gonna fail you again?' -Two Years Ago- Miranda: “Fuck! Fuck fuck fuck!” Miranda stood on the balcony, looking downward at the passing cars some eighteen stories below. Inside’Yokuto lay on the floor, his hand still clutching his gun. But’it would serve him no longer. Embedded deep in his throat was the dirk, and the dark stain of crimson still pooled around the dirk’s blade and onto the floor. But that part was over with’it was the door cracking under the strain of being bashed by the police on the other side of the door that had her attention now! Miranda: “Why”! Why the fuck did I do this? God’I killed a man’what the fuck is wrong with me!” Snarling, and crying at the same time, Miranda leaps up onto the railing of the balcony. If she was scared of heights, she wasn’t showing it! Ignoring the death fall below her, she leaps upward and grabs the edge of the balcony above her’slip! Holy shit! She gasps, digging her fingers into the concrete and holding on for dear life! She strains her muscles and lifts herself upward, just as the door below finally gives way’yes, she gets away’but once you’ve done a “job- in this town’you’ll be expected to do it again, sooner or later’and those days will haunt forever. -Today- Miranda suddenly shot up out of bed, crying and sweating profusely. Allie darts up, immediately wrapping her arms around Mira with a look of concern. Mira draws in a deep breath, shivering from head to toe, melting into Allie’s arms. Allie simply strokes Mira’s hair, letting the other woman calm down. Mira shudders again, the finally relaxes as both women slowly lay back in the bed, with Allie propping up on an elbow to look into Mira’s eyes. Allie: “I don’t know what kind of dream you were having, baby, but you were all over the bed with it.” Mira: “My”.my past, is all, sweets’I’they come back to haunt me from time to time.” Allie nods, but before she can speak again, there’s a light knock at the door. Before either woman can ask who is it, the door opens slightly, and Len sticks his head in. Allie starts to complain but stops as Mira sits up, her eyes focused on the expression Len wears. He wastes no time filling her in. Len: “Mira, I’m sorry’I tried to stop her, but she said only you could?” Mira’s eyes widen, and she’s out of the bed before Len can even draw his next breath to speak. Ignoring her stark nakedness, and Len’s suddenly wide eyes, she shoves him aside and bursts through the door, heading right for Kali’s bedroom! Mira: “KALI! KAAAAAALI!” Suddenly, from behind the closed door of her room, Kali responds in a pained voice. Kali: 'I..I um, I'll be ready, don't come in! I might be like, naked!' Like that was gonna work. Miranda kicks the door open, and the scene unfolds before her. She looks down at Kali’more specifically at her bloody wrists, and her entire body goes tense as she raises her eyes to Kali, her lip quivering with both anger’and sadness. Mira: “Wha’you’you lied to me?” Kali lowers her hand, lifting a small cloth to wipe at the tiny, bloodied carving on her wrist... Kali: 'No, I said I'd not try and take my life... This is only another reminder... I..I failed you, Mira! And like, I hafta atone, I hafta!' Mira: “BULLSHIT!” Mira rushes forward, grabbing Kali by the wrist and yanking her upward! Mira stares at the rune on Kali’s wrist, then throws her arm down, letting go and stepping closer, towering over her sister! For two years she’s dealt with the inner demons, and dealt with Kali’s as well, seeming as if the entire weight of what little they had sat squarely upon her own shoulders. Mira: “Failed me” (Grabs Kali’s wrist again) THIS is fucking failing me, Kali! Like, what did you think, I’d be happy about this? God damn it, Kali’why don’t you just use the fucking thing on me?! Because all you’re doing with this bullshit is cutting at my heart!” Mira shoves Kali semi-hard’but aimed correctly, so that Kali just ends up sitting on her bed. Mira lowers her head, clenching her fists as two figures emerge from behind her, Len and Allie. Mira: “It’s not enough I have to fight myself’you’ve got to go and make things that much more difficult. Like facing off in this Tournament isn’t test enough. No, you have to go and like, pull your shit again. I don’t know what to do with you anymore, Kali?” Kali lowers her head, swallowing lightly. Following a light sob, she starts to speak, her hands trembling noticeably. Kali: 'J..Just forgive me? I..It's heavy on me, Mira! You know my ways, I..I can't help it, it's a reminder, so I won't do it again... Everything on here, all these reminders... I've yet to do them twice... I'm sorry, it's just, trying to keep us together, because I always mess up...' Mira: “The only time you mess up is when you to do stupid things like this?” Mira sinks to the floor, resting her hands in her lap and closing her eyes. Mira: “It’s not keeping us together’every time you do this, you know’it pisses me off. It makes me think’that you do it to hurt me.” Kali: 'NO! That's so not it! It's to hurt me! it makes me learn!' Kali whimpers out her plea to her sister, not even caring of the other two witnessing this. In case you're wondering the disease in her mind, it's an ancient ritual done by barbaric tribes, ritual scarring. Done for rewards and punishment, to make visible the deeds of tribe members... Somewhere along the way, Kali sank into the idea... Kali: 'Please Mira... Please?' Mira raises her eyes to Kali’of course she’s going to give in. Kali is the only family she has. But this has got to stop somewhere’and now is the time. Mira: “If this happens once more, Kali’you are on your own’I can’t’I can’t deal with this anymore.” Kali slowly nods, closing her eyes. Kali: 'I..Help me seek counseling? I don't want you to leave... I'll die without you... Like in the ring sis, we so can't get it done alone!' Miranda stands to her feet, and walks to Kali, sitting down on the bed next to her sister. The events have the past few days have awoken the nightmares again, days past when she was dark inside. You see’after that first night, it became a little easier’and little less horrific. Death just became something that happened, and how she survived and stayed out of trouble with the law’well, if you know anything about the Mafia, then you know the answer to that. But over all that, Kali still remained the most important person in her life, and she always will, even over Allie. That’s just the way it is. Mira: “We’re gonna win this tournament, and after that’you, me, Len, and Allie are gonna go to Florida. I think it’s time I kept a promise I made a long time ago, and hopefully that can be the start to easing this need of yours to feel pain.” Kali's eyes suddenly widen, and she turns her creepy gaze to Mira... Kali: 'Dude, no frickin' way! The beach?!' Mira: “Yeah’Miami Beach, sun, fun, and like’bodies to die for!” Mira winks, knowing full well that Len and Allie stand in the door, as they turn to look at each other. Allie smiles, and turns back to Mira and Kali, who now embrace is a warm hug of each other. Sisters are sisters, I’ve said it a million times. And Shade’well, let’s just hope the guys who have signed up for this Tournament don’t awaken that side of Miranda’because, well’the last thing GW needs is a half the roster looking for their body parts! Gotta go! See ya at the tournament!
