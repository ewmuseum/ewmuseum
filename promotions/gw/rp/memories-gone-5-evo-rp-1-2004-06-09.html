Memories Gone #5 (Evo RP#1)

9 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 060904 I saw on the sandy beach, waves rolling under my feet. In the distance, the sun had begun to set, pale yellows changing to bold oranges and reds, streaking the sky with violet. In the background, a child laughed, half in focus, half out. Movement off to my right. Instinct took over. Rolling backwards, my right had coming up, gun in palm, centering on a shapeless form. My eyes uncertain by my instinct sure. Pull the trigger, it said. Do it. Survive, Do it. Live. Do it. My index finger caressed the trigger, pilling it back. The click of the hammer locking back. Eyes focusing. A child, with dark hair and the permanent tan of a person with Asian heritage. Gut feeling, my child. Instinct too far to stop. Darkness. Silence. ***** I startled out of my slumber, jerking my head back. Victoria’s hand fell almost casually off of its resting spot on the back of my head. I stared at it for a long moment. How had it gotten there? I could not remember placing it there. Turning my head, I stared at Victoria’s soft facial features, locked in her half eternal slumber. I stood up quickly and made my way to the security room of the Echelon Tower. ***** The soles of my shoes squeaked and slide underneath the tiled hall that the Security Room was located. The door looked like any other, solid oak, with a brass nameplate fastened to one side with black letters that simply stated, SECURITY. Above the doorframe, a camera peered down at me. I tried the knob first, but it was locked. I raised my fist and pounded on the door. “Open up!” I yelled at the door. “Who is it?” a ruff voice came through the wooden door. Aiden de Sarret. “It’s John. Open the goddamn door!” “Now, John. You know that I can’t just open up this door. You’ll need to step back, let us visually verify that it is you. Then, take your security card and swipe it through the lock. Then we will consider letting you in.” I patted my pockets looking for the card. “Aiden, I don’t have my fucking card. Can’t I just step back and let you ID me from there” “No can do, Johnny-boy. Go get your card. Whatever it is, can fucking wait.” Alas, poor Aiden, it cannot. I stepped back into full view of the camera, looking up, watching the lens twist and turn, focusing on my image before flipping them the bird with my left hand. With my right, I reached to the gun holster under my left arm, jerking the glock free, firing off three consecutive rounds. One to each hinge, the other to the door lock itself. I lunged forward, thrusting my left foot at the door, watching it fall into the room. I landed on top of it in a kneeling position. Aiden de Sarret stood on the other side, a pump-action shotgun in hand. He lowered the barrel so that it rested on my inner shoulder, near the neck. My gun hand was not idle, however, the barrel of my glock buried into his stomach. “Now why would you go and do something like that, John?” he asked me dryly. “Because Aiden, it couldn’t fucking wait.” He sighed heavily before lowering the shotgun. “Well then, you might as well come in. And you’re buying us a new fucking door.” “Yeah, whatever. Bill me.” ***** “There! Right there!” “Where?” “There, you blind fuck. Rewind it, play it again.” There were three of us hunched over the small television and VCR. Aiden, myself, and another guard whose name I had already forgotten who was operating the machinery. The guard backed the tape up slightly and let it play. “Pause it! Now goddamnit!” I yelled at the guard, who did as he was told. In the still frame, between the tracking bars you could see Victoria’s arm raised, hung in midair, caught by the wonders of the pause button. Hastily, I hit the eject button and went for the tape. Aiden snatched it out of the VCR before I could though. “What are you doing, John?” “I need that tape. I need to take it to the doctor so that he can see this. It’s important.” “This tape isn’t leaving the room, John. It contains images of the Echelon Tower that are classified to the general public, including certain levels of our staff. If this tape leaves this room, it would be over my dead?” He didn’t get to finish the coy saying. The glock in my right hand, the barrel shoved in between his lips stopped him. I could hear the audible clicks of safeties being clicked off as guns were drawn. My left hand retrieved the second glock under my right hand. “You see, Aiden, this tape is coming with me, regardless of whether I have to kill every goddamn person in this room. And yes, it is that important to me. What you may fail to realize at this moment is that the woman lying in that room is my fianc’e. And she is carrying my child, goddamnit, so show just a little fucking compassion before I repaint this room in your fucking brains, okay?” “Gentlemen,” the word came softly, from the new opening I had made. My attention was drawn to it, as was Aiden’s, who still had my gun shoved in his mouth. Maverick stood at the doorway, light shining in from around his dark figure. “Is there a problem?” As he stood there, I noticed that one of his guns was drawn and pressed against his leg. “I need this tape, Mav. If I don’t get it, there will be hell to pay.” “What’s on it, John?” Reno asked quietly. “Victoria moved last night. She lifted her arm and put it on my head. This tape has the evidence. I want to show the doctor.” I told him, trying to stay even-voiced. “Aiden?” Reno turned his steady gaze to the head of TUE security. I carefully pulled the gun from in between his lips. “The doctor isn’t cleared for the tape.” “What about just that particular section?” Reno asked. “He would be, yes.” “Copy it, just the one part. With appropriate lead-time, say five minutes before the movement and five minutes after. That okay with you, John?” “That’s all I need.” “Good. And the door?” Reno gestured to the door propped on the nearby wall. Aiden and myself exchanged glances. “It’s already being handled, Mav. John here is taking care of it.” I nodded. Reno shook his head slightly and continued down the hallway. Aiden handed the tape off to the same guard who had been operating the VCR. “You heard Mr. Reno. Copy the scene, five minutes on either side.” Aiden turned and looked at me. “You really would’ve killed us all, wouldn’t you?” “Every last one.” “Good man. I would’ve done the same.”
