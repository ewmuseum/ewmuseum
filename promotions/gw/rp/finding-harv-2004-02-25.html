Finding Harv...

25 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/25/04 (It's late at night and McNasty recieved a phone call on his cell phone from Harv to come down to the bar immediatley. McNasty has been there for a little while now, getting Harv untied cleaned up a little bit. Harv is clearly in pain, but Harv is a tough guy, who has been hurt before. McNasty knows that Train has elevated this to a whole new level and there is no signs of it coming down anytime soon. McNast is obviously angry, but when you look into his eyes it's beyond anger. He's beyond being enraged, in fact McNasty has a look in his eye that it's not about who wins or loses at High Octane it's about how much pain he can inflict on Train. Right now McNasty doesn't even care if Train were to die at Hight Octane, this is now personal. McNasty's thoughts have always been, don't mess with his family or friends. McNasty views Harv as family.) McNasty: Harv I am going to take you to the hospital. To avoid anymore trouble we'll just tell them you hurt yourself pretty bad skiing or something... Harv: Good idea... McNasty: So Train told you he was sending me a message huh? Harv: Donny, this guy is out of control and he needs to be stopped. He has no direction in his life. The asshole smelled like a bong after a Gratefull Dead concert. McNasty: Harv, train is going to pay for what he did. You can count on that. It was one thing to mess with the Boss... Hell I've done that before, but to mess with someone who I consider to be family carries a severe punishment. Harv: Donny I am going to tell you this with all honesty, Who gives a shit if you win the match on Sunday. Who cares, the wins will come when they're supposed to come. On the other hand, I can see the look in your eyes, Train has made you snap. You have no more regard for his existence as a human being. If you get disqualified tearing him apart I am sure Christina McCoy will forgive you and thank you. Donny, Train doesn't respect women, he doesn't respect you, he doesn't even respect himself. It's time to give him an attitude adjustment. McNasty: Harv, I don't think there is anything anyone can say or do right now, I am on the brink of destroying someone else. Fuck calling the Police, I am the Law, and Train won't fuck around no more. McNasty: Lets go get you fixed up then Harv. Then when we get back, we'll talk business.... (As they get up to leave, you can just tell that McNasty means business. Train has it coming for him as well. This bitter fued is building up more and more pressure with each passing day. There isn't much time left before High Octane, come Sunday night, the cloth roof of the Silverdome is about to be torn off when The Nomad Donny J McNasty and Train collide for what's shaping up to be the future power struggle in Global Wrestling)
