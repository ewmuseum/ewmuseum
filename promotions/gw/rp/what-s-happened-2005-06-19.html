What's happened?

19 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/19/05 Introspective ~A few weeks ago.~ ~The Trump Marina Hotel.~ ~Outside the North Crystal Suite Room~ The white doors and hallways are spotless, as they'd be expected to be. Expensive paintings and subdued lighting, with some potted plants on tables as the view goes down the hallway. A lone figure walks down the hall, towards the door to the north tower suite. This figure is obviously a man with his strong chin, Adam's apple and short cut hair in a small afro look. The African-American though is also a person with some strange tastes. This morning he's wearing a female cut business suit in blue, with a matching chiffon scarf wrapped around his neck. The purse and manner in which he walks belays much about this 'man'. This person, known in the wrestling world as Kristal, real name Kristopher, or Kris to his/her friends is the agent of one Danielle Northsky. Dani and Kris met when he was a road agent for the now defunct CCWA, where Dani got her first pro start just a few short months ago. When that federation closed, Kris stayed on with Dani and her two friends and assumed the role as their agent, and thus the main person who deciphers the deals that the shady characters in the wrestling business would try to pull on them. Kris stops at the door and knocks on it with a light tap from his knuckles. He hesitates a few short moments and then tries it again. This time he includes a small call into the door, almost a whisper, ?Dani?? No answer comes forth. Kris looks at his watch on his left wrist and then knocks one more time. ... ... Nothing. Now he opens up his purse, and pulls out a blue card that holds the Trump Marina logo upon it. He slides it into the slot on the door and the lights above the it then blink green and a 'click' can be heard as the door then pops open just slightly off it's latch. Kris pushes the door open and then steps in. The shades in the mostly white colored room are drawn, making it very dark. The jacuzzi tub is empty, radio and TV are both off. Only a pair of blue jeans thrown nonchalantly onto the couch shows that really anyone has even been staying here. That is, until you get to the bed. The bed is a rumpled mess, with pillows and blankets strewn about. Only the sheet remains on it, and one pillow. That pillow is actually covering the head of the person laying in it, with a huge mass of raven colored hair coming out from under it. A lone shapely leg, up to the hip, protrudes from one side of the sheet, the rest of it covers the occupant most completely. “Alright, Dani. Sleeping time is over. Time to get up and face the day. Just last night you made good on your promises to the fed, but this morning there have been changes. Big Changes, and you need to be awake for your update.” A faint mumble comes from under the pillow. Kris walks over and slaps the covered area right next to the exposed hip. This actually generates some movement from the person sleeping and a request. “What time is it,” Dani asks. “It's about 11 am,” Kris answers. “I just went to bed at...” Dani starts to say. “You left the party at about 3:30. Which is a good thing, because at about 3:45 the local police showed up. Something about at least one underage person drinking at the party,” Kris interrupted. “I'll be 21 in a few weeks. And I was only drinking wine coolers,” Dani finishes off that statement. Kris reaches over and pushes a button which causes a 'whirring' noise to be heard as the shades in the suite all open in unison, flooding the room with the bright sunshine from the outside. Dani visibly recoils even under her own personal shading from the bed. “Well you weren't there, and everyone else was of age when they got there. Like I said, it's a good thing. However, we need to talk,” Kris says as he heads over to the in room refrigerator and pulls out a few items from it. He quickly begins to mix them together. ?I guess I have to get up. Who's with you?? “No one, I'm alone,” Kris finishes the concoction and puts it up on the table top. At that, Dani starts to slide out of her cocoon of linen and satin. Her hair, disheveled at it's best, and her face with a scowl upon it. She's wearing only a belly baring babydoll shirt and a pair of white panties, the only other thing she appeared to have been wearing when she got back from this party were the jeans that are laying on the couch 20 feet from her. “My head hurts...” “You drank about 20 wine coolers alone last night. Come drink this, it's V8 mixed with a few things. It'll kill the headache.” “Thanks.” Dani stands up and takes the cup with the mixture in it. She starts to drink it and her nose wrinkles at it. But like a good girl, she finishes it off and then puts it down. “Okay, now I need a coffee to get the taste of that out of my mouth. That's nasty.” “Good medicine never tastes good, I'm sure your uncle taught you that.” Dani looks up at Kris with that, and finally notices that Kris doesn't have his/her usual go lucky face on. ?What's up?? Kris sighs, audibly and then he walks over to the chair and sits down. Reaching into his purse, he pulls out his I-Pac PDA and then starts to tap onto the screen with it's stylus. ?Where are Josh and Gray?? Dani asks. “They're on their way home, after I talked to them.” ?What?? “The fed is paying for them to go home. And paid them for their services up to date. I'll get to it, but you might want to sit down.” Dani walks over to the couch and sits down on it, moving her jeans out of the way. “Okay, you're not booked this next week. So you can take a little break to think about things.” “What? What about my title shot? I beat Smith last night.” “You're not booked. But your title shot doesn't mean much now. That division was dissolved. The title is still there, but it's now officially something for people without anything else going on to toss back and forth. It's not even a newby title anymore.” ?What?? Dani was now getting a hint as to just why Kris wasn't happy. He wasn't a paid member of the federation, but they hated dealing with him, since he was a signed agent to the three of them. “You're not booked this week, and when you are booked... I might as well tell you... Josh and Gray can't come out with you anymore.” ?Why not?? “They're not longer under a developmental deal.” “But, they're supposed to be a tag team eventually. They've got crap for a tag division as it is...” “They no longer have a tag division.” ?Since when?? “This morning... Just like the belt you were promised a shot at is no longer a division.” ?But... my developmental deal says that... Does the boss know about this?? ?His signature is on the memo's. But the announcement is being made by the one head booker we've butted heads with already. I don't know. However, I also don't know what's going on. It's almost as if after the huge success at the show last night, someone is really wanting to make a few statements. But I have other news... It appears that the PTC organization is reviewing their membership also. I'm getting that from other sources. There are a few violations that they've been tagged with, fines and such, that have gone unpaid.? Dani looked down at the table, and then shook her head. ?They've sent Gray and Josh home?? “Said they'd pay for it if they left on the first flight possible. I advised them to take it. I'll be heading back to Seattle here soon, as I've got to meet with the Lady soon to give her an update. I'll catch up with them there. However, I'm really thinking things here are going south really quick.” Dani got a scowl on her face. “After all the work I've done for them, I get this. I even started their night off for them. I beat Elia Smith and showed how much better we were then them.” “No, you showed how much better YOU were then them. Remember that. I think this is coming from the lack of a payday for them from your FHM deal.” “Why would they get anything from my FHM deal? I own my name. My real name is Daniel Northsky. It's not like they have to license it, and I started talks with them while I was still in the CCWA. They had nothing to do with it.” “And thus none of the 30,000 you got for it is going to them.” “But I did the commercial for them, promoting it and such. People will tune into the fed's show to see ME! Are they going to throw that away? They won't even sign me to a full on deal, it's still supposed to be a developmental one.” “You're right. And the developmental contract I negotiated for you had both Josh and Gray supported on it. Now they're not here. I'd cancel your contract too, as your agent, but I don't want to make waves if you don't want me to.” Dani stands up and starts to slide her jeans back on. As she does, she reaches into the back pocket and pulls out a leather wallet. She drops it onto the table and a few loose pieces of paper fall out. “You have two more nights here, paid for by FHM. Take care of yourself. Keep your eyes on things though. I'm going to head back to the school and the main offices. Just don't do anything rash without talking to me first.” “Yeah, I'll be good. I've got to just go parade around in a bikini for a few meetings and that, and do a few radio shows. I'll probably head back to the 'Rez' to go visit family for a few days too, since I'm not booked.” ?You know they're not booking you to try to force you to sign a regular contract that'll give part of your extra earnings to them, right?? Dani doesn't answer that one. ?You take it easy over the next few days, maybe a week. I'll be in touch, okay?? “Yeah.” Kris didn't appear to want to hang around too much. He got up and left Dani alone in the large hotel suite. Dani sat there, looking down at the table. She reached out and took her wallet into her hands and started putting the loose papers back into it. One dropped out onto the floor. She reached down and picked it up. Upon it was the logo of Global Wrestling! She looked at it, not understanding how she got it. Then she saw the name on it.... Logan Creed, Talent Relations Department. She barely remembered talking to a small man, with his accent and other quirks, the night before at the after party for the show. In fact, he was part of the reason why the 20 year old had some drinks the night before. But why would she have his card? It had a cell phone number written in on it too, not just his business hard line. She put it into the back of the wallet and then stood up, intending to head out to get something to eat quickly then come back and get ready for her business today. She now had many things to think about... Decisions to make. ~A few days later~ ~Black Hills, South Dakota.~~ ~Rosebud Lakota Indian Reservation~ Out in the backfield behind her parents house, we find Dani working on brushing down a painted stallion of a horse with a large white star on his forehead and a blonde mane. As she's brushing down the side of it's neck, a young man comes running out of the house with something in his hand. ?Dani! Dani!? ?Right here, Jimmy. What's up?? The young boy runs up to his elder cousin and hands to her a flip cell phone. Dani opens it and then looks at the screen and the missed call notification going to a 206 area code... Seattle. “Either Gray or Kris called me. Thanks, I'll take care of it.” She pats her cousin on the head and he goes running back off. Dani puts the brush for the horse back onto it's hanger on the wall and then starts to walk down a small path that appears to lead down to the pastures that the horses run in. She pushes the numbers on her phone to call back to the selected number and then puts the phone to her ear. ?Yeah... Kris?? A few second later. ?They said what? They want what?? A bit of a wait again, as Dani starts to look very upset. ?That's...that's... bullshit!? Dani isn't normally one to curse, and Kris on the other side would really be taken aback from it. Dani listens for a bit more while Kris explains things to her again. Then she makes on final statement. “No... It's a developmental contract. They've already violated it with the release of my registered valets who were there on the same contract. My answer is that I'd rather work in the CCWA again, if it was open. Forget it. I'm not re-negotiating to a full time employee. They can't have the money from my own deals that I worked on, and they're not getting anything else. They can shove it so far up their backsides that they choke on it. I'm done.” She stops and listens again. ?I know Kris. I'll call you back later, okay? I'm not in a good area for reception. I'm heading into town later and I'll give you a call from there. But I'm not going to deal with this anymore. Especially after finding out about the other stuff. I have no reason to continue there. I don't take well to broken promises. I've kept mine. I expect the same amount of professional courtesy. I'll talk to you later. Go ahead and deliver the word to them. I have another call I think I want to make first. At that, Dani hangs up the phone and then crosses her arms, as she begins to think. She then reaches into her back pocket and pulls out her wallet. As it had been put a few days before hand... she takes the business card out from the back of the stack of other card size things in it... She looks at it and then sighs out loud. With the thumb on her hand, she dials the number quickly and then returns the phone to her ear. The phone appears to ring quite a bit until finally she starts to speak into it. ?Mr. Creed?? A bit of a wait. “Yes, this is Dani Northsky...” She lets him answer. ?Yes, that Dani Northsky. Am I interrupting something?? Wait... ?No? Good, as I understand it, if I'm remembering our talks from a few nights ago right, you're the second man behind Mr. McCoy for hiring people, right?? Wait... ?Why do I ask? Well, what would you say if I said that as of 5 minutes ago I was a free agent?? She pulls the phone away from her ear as a loud noise comes forth from the phone. As the noise dies down, she returns it. “Yeah, I said I'm a free agent. I've decided to not renegotiate my developmental deal with them.” She waits... ?Yeah, I'm at home now. Use this number. I could be in Las Vegas at any time. However, I'd need you to talk to my agent too. ? She waits and gets a slight smirk on her face. “Yes, that agent.. the black man who carries the purse and thought you were cute when he came and got me out of the party. Kris represents me and my two brothers in arms, Josh and Gray. They were released by our former home a few days ago.” She stops as the voice on the other side talks to her. ?OH! You want to talk to him right now? I don't have 3 way calling on my phone. Let me text you his number so that you have it in some form of hard copy. Alright?? She quickly hangs up and then her thumbs go all over the small phone as she sends off the text message back to him. She sighs again as she finishes it and get the confirmation. ?Have fun, Kris. No moss growing on this stone. I've got to deal with the two weeks minimum no compete, and then I'm free to hope I've found someplace that women in the ring are appreciated. Not someplace where if you're not one certain woman, you should just be wiggling your ass in the ring and not worrying about it all. It's not like they can't deny that I'm already better then the bottom of their roster. I embarrassed Cowboy Barbie a few nights ago. Elia Smith is considered to be high ranking on their contenders for their little TV title... and I've already given her a wrestling lesson. I just hope they're not all hype like... Never mind, I'm going to forget about them. I don't care. They're nothing to me. They don't repsect their talent, nor their fan base. They don't even have a number one contender for their big belt. Oh, wait, they do... and she's the only woman who's allowed to compete openly with the men. Screw them and the high horse they ride upon. I'm in this for competition. If they want to see my tits, they can go buy a magazine. They want to see me wrestle, they can turn on the TV.? Dani turns and starts to walk back up to her family's ranch house. Her Indigo Ink colored Toyota Highlander sitting in the driveway next to about 4 large pickup trucks. ~Earlier this week~ ~Interstate 76 & 70~ ~Outside Denver, CO~ ~Late at Night~ A small group of cars is driving down the freeway, one of them an off blue colored (Indigo Ink) Highlander. It's going down the road as a black haired woman is fumbling with a map and a few other things inside. She's apparently using her phone, set to use speaker phone as she's driving. Yes, Kris is on the other end of the line. ?Alright Girly, those two are in Vegas with me now. I've got the rooms ready. Where are you at?? ?Just outside of Denver. About another 12 hours of driving from the looks of it. Do you have anything for me? Any idea what's going on?? “Yes, you're booked for a match.” ?What?? ?Mr. McCoy was all to happy to get you signed. He's already booked you for a match. Emoticon is his name. He's a Frenchman who thinks he's a knight of some sort. He's very much into the ladies. He's a good wrestler from the looks of it. I'd not discount him. I've got tapes and everything ready when you get here. Are you driving through?? ?Yes. If I get tired, I've got a pallet in the backseat. I'm not going to be late or anything. I've got things to get ready. Since Global is set in one city, and only travels for bigger events, did you get what I asked for?? “Yes, there are some nice apartments and that down Henderson way, looks like a half hour drive. There is also some good stuff up north of here, outside the desert near the Paiute reservation.” ?Did you just say the Paiute Reservation?? “Yes, I did.” “Oh my...” ?What?? Kris asking her with a sense of urgency. “Nothing much. Just if you remember, the Lady's cousin is from that reservation. I think I'll be making some calls when I get into town. I'll call you back if I stop to take a nap. Just in case I hit a wi-fi hotspot before then, upload the videos of this Emoticon guy to your personal space. I'll snag them if I see them. I might stop for a cup of coffee too.” “I gotcha, girly. I can tell you one thing, this place has a LOT more morale in the locker room. I'm glad you kept that business card from the cute little dwarf man. I'd expect Ms. Sights to also be on you like fly on a turd when you get into town for interviews and that. I've pulled it all so that you can get at least a day of rest after you're scheduled to get here tomorrow. So use it. In the meantime, I've got a couple of rooms at Suncoast Casino. It's not a really big one, but they had a room on this short notice, it's not on the Strip, so it's a bit more out of the way.” “Not a problem. Just make sure everything is in order.” “Already done that, girly. Oh, and just so you know, it's not a developmental deal either. It's a full blown contract. Payday is here...” “Good, I'm going to sign off so I can pay more attention to the idiots on the road.” ?See you tomorrow, girly. Viva Las Vegas and Viva Global!? The phone signs off and Dani hits her radio to switch it back to her cd-player instead of the phone. She then looks up and sees the road sign saying how much longer till Grand Junction. From there, it's two more states to her new home. She'd be there soon. Time to make an impact. ~End... for now~
