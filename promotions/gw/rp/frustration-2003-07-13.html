Frustration

13 July 2003

Frustration Sergei Strakhlzhenski Global Wrestling Promo #421 Date: 7/13/03 Location: Frankie's Apartment in Ft. Lauderdale, Fla Sergei is lying on his weight bench at Frankie's Ft. Lauderdale home pumping weights. He has 1500 lbs on the bar as he struggles through 10 reps. He sets the bar back on the cradle and sits up, grabbing a towel and wiping the sweat off of his brow. He stands up and walks over to the sliding glass door, leading from the gym to the living area and walks over to the bar, grabbing a bottled water and swigging it down. As he turns to go to the shower, the doorbell rings. He sets the water down and walks over, grabbing a baseball bat. He was not going to have a repeat of Derby, England, he would make sure of that. He looked through the peep hole, seeing a man in a black business suit standing there, apparently alone. Sergei smiles. Batter up, he thinks to himself, as he opens the door and reaches out, yanking the man inside, quickly closing it again behind them. He puts the bat underneath the man's chin, choking him. 'Who are you and vy are you here,' he asks, squeezing the bat across the man's throat. 'I... I...' the man gasps, but can't get past that. Sergei eases up the pressure so the man can speak. 'I'm from the CIA, I have information on your brother.' Sergei releases the man, who slams into the wall, sputtering and coughing. 'Vat about my brozer,' he asks. 'Speak qvick, or I vill kick you out.' 'We found the wreckage, but there were no bodies,' said the man. 'Not even the pilot and co-pilot were found. Our best guess is that they parachuted out at the last minute.' Sergei frowned. That meant the Physician of Lublin didn't die in the plane crash. It meant that all of this had been a ruse to fool the Mossad. It meant Igor had been two steps ahead of the Mossad when he left, knowing they would come after Sergei. 'Is zer anyting else,' asked Sergei. 'No,' said the agent. 'That's all we have so far. We haven't been able to track where they bailed out. I could have been before they got anywhere near the point of contact. In fact, that jet could have been a drone. We just don't know, and Polish Intelligence isn't exactly forthcoming with the information.' Sergei shook his head. Polish Intelligence wouldn't release any information on this. For all Sergei knew, they had hidden Dr. Greiber away somewhere in Europe to keep the Mossad from getting to him. He smiled to himself. Touche, he thought, thinking of his brother. You outsmarted them all. Now you have to get Greiber hidden away, and I think I know exactly where. 'Don't vorry about zis,' said Sergei. 'I vill get in touch vis Polish Intelligence and clear zis up.' 'Suit yourself,' said the agent. 'I'm just the messenger, not the boss.' Sergei nodded and opened the door, letting the man out. He turned and walked over to the bar, taking another swig of his water. He welled up with anger as he thought of his brother pulling a fast one on the Mossad. It was funny, but it did deal with a war criminal, one that has been protected by Russia since the fall of the Third Reich. He slammed his fist down on the bar and threw the bottle of water against the wall. Now he would have to arrange a trip to Europe to see if he could pick up the cold trail of his brother. Dr. Greiber had to be brought to justice. He had told his brother this. It was the cost of Sergei's silence. So far, Serge had kept his part of the bargain. Igor had not. Sergei heard a key in the door, then heard the door open. Frankie stepped in and looked around, smiling at Segei. That smile turned to a frown as she saw the watery mess on her wall, and the angered look on Sergei's face. 'What's wrong, honey,' she asked, careful to keep a neutral tone. 'Igor is alive,' said Sergei. 'Not only zat, he fooled zi Mossad and everyvon else. He vas never in zat jet.' 'He wasn't,' asked Frankie, surprised. 'Then where?' 'Zat is vat I have to find out,' said Sergei. 'I vill find him, and I vill bring Dr. Greiber to justice. I told Igor I vas not playing around. I kept my part of zi bargain... now he has to keep his.' 'Sergei, honey, what does it matter to you,' she asked. 'From all I've heard, the man had nothing to do with what went on in Lublin. It was his adjutant. My dad showed me the reports.' 'Vy vould he show you zi reports, Frankie,' asked Sergei, suspiciously. 'Because I asked him what this was all about, and I pushed him into a corner on it,' said Frankie. 'You forget, I was there when Igor spilled the beans. I have leverage too. Now answer my question. What does it matter to you if the man goes to justice. He's old, he's going to die soon anyway.' Sergei smiled. He liked how resourceful Frankie was. She could act like a kitten one moment, and a tigress the next. It always kept Sergei guessing, which made this whole relationship with her that much more exciting. 'No von has been able to find zis adjutant,' said Sergei. 'Visout him to corroborate zi story, it leaves Dr. Greiber as zi only suspect. Justice must be served, Frankie. For zi sake of zi people zat suffered at zi hands of zi Nazis.' 'Okay, if you must do this, I'll help,' said Frankie. 'I'm in this as deep as you are, whether you like it or not. Besides, I'm marrying a Jew... that has to count for something.' Sergei smiled and walked over to Frankie. 'I accept your help, Frankie,' he said. 'Ve vill follow zis trail togezer.' 'For now, though, I want you concentrating on this match you have coming up,' said Frankie. 'You got lazy in the PTC Tournament and let that little blue man beat you. We're up for another championship, Sergei, and you're going to win this one.' Sergei nods. 'Yes, I vill vin zis von. It is zi International Title. I vas custom made for me.' 'That's right,' said Frankie. 'So lets get to work. I want that strap around your waiste as soon as possible.' They walk out into the training room, leaving the worries of the world behind. They could worry about Igor and Dr. Greiber after they handle Stan Stone.
