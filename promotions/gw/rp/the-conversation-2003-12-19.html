Date: 12-19-03 “It’s been nine months, Jesse. Why can’t you just let it go?” Veronica, what a beautiful woman she is, but she’ll never understand what it means to be me. I have a code that I’m forced to live by, a code that those before me handed down. I don’t have a choice in the matter, I must have retribution! It’s out of my hands. “Darlin” you just don’t get it, do you?” I reply as I twist the lid off of my last jar of shine. “You just don’t seem to understand that I gotta show him that I’m better than he is. That he can’t just walk around like nothin” ever happened. It ain’t the way of my people.” Veronica’s face contorts in an angry protest of misunderstanding. She takes a few steps back away from me, maybe she thinks I’m walking down the same road as before, but she’s wrong. I know what I’ve got to do, I accept what I did in the past, but the two aren’t the same, not anymore. Just as she’s about to walk out the door I say something. “Don’t go.” The desperation in my voice gives me away. She turns from the door to face me, and I continue. “I need you, darlin”, you’re all I’ve got to keep me sane. You actually think I’m gonna go back to bein? the bastard that I was?” “It’s possible.” I can see the frost coming from her mouth as she speaks those words of ice. My mind races for an instant, how could she seriously think that? It’s easy I suppose, cause I have done a lot of terrible things in my day. They tell me a leopard never changes his spots, maybe I haven’t either. “Look, this thing with Brick. This street fight, I gotta do it, just to show him I’m an honorable man now. Just to show him I ain’t afraid of him, and that I won’t back away from a fight, even a forgotten one. I asked Christina McCoy to make this match so I could end it between me and Brick, and that’s just what I’m gonna do!” “Oh really” You’re actually going to go to an old gas station and wale the hell out of that man because of what happened between the two of you almost a year ago? My god, Jesse! Just when I think I have you all figured out, you go and do something UTTERLY STUPID like this!” I see the tears welling up in her eyes, and I notices her chin begin to quiver. She thinks I’m actually going to try and hurt Brick, and she’s right. I’ll whip him into a bloody pulp if that’s what it takes to win the match, but after the final bell has rung, I’ll put down all my weapons and walk off. “I ain’t out to kill him, you got it all wrong. I ain’t even out for revenge. All I want is for that man to respect me, and after Evolution, he’s gonna do just that. He might win the match, hell he might even break me in half, but one thing’s for damn sure. He’ll remember it for a long time when I’m done fightin”. A long time.” As she looks at me with utter terror and anguish riddled across her angelic face, an old flame reignites. The fire of my anger burns once more. I thought I had destroyed it, I thought maybe that with her help, I had let go of my old hatreds, but I haven’t. It’s apparent to me now, that the leopard really can’t change his spots. But does that still make me an evil man? No. Just because I feel this rising within me once more, doesn’t mean I have to turn it on those I care for the most. I’ll use it as my weapon, and break those who stand against me with it. Somehow, I got lost in my thought, and she sees it written all over me. My muscles have tightened and my fists have clinched. God only knows how my face has twisted, the anger warms my heart. Makes me feel alive again, I like it, but I have to control it. For her sake. “I’m’I’m sorry. I got lost in my own thoughts. I know this is scary for you Veronica. I know how you feel. You ain’t gonna lose me, not unless you walk out that door right now. If you do that, then I just might fall back into that abyss you pulled me out of, but if you stay. If you help me, then I can manage this. Then I can stay on the right path and be the Rampagin” Redneck I was before. I just let my anger lick me, it beat me good a few times, but you can help me manage it. I know you can, so just stay. Stay with me.” That’s all I’ve got left to say, what else could be said? She’ll stay, or she’ll go. I don’t really have any control in the matter, do I? I look at her, our eyes meet. My heartbeat quickens with the pure electricity that runs through my body as I look into her soul. She feels it too, her face begins to flush and her resolve fades. She runs over to me, wraps her arms around me. We embrace, and I know I have this thing beaten.
