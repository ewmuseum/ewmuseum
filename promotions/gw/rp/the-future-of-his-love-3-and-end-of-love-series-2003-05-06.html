The Future Of His Love (#3 And End Of Love Series)

6 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/6/03 The hands he has held. The mistakes he has made. They are all memories, memories because they have faded from his heart. They are used every now and then, to learn from his mistakes. Another broken heart, or another chance at love? Temporary or Together, Forever and Ever? Relationships are a confusing thing. It is not a one-way love, because a one-way love will never work out. He had Jess, he loved Jess with all his heart and gave her everything he owned. He offered her his love and his life, but she did not want to accept it. A relationship is nothing without love from both parties, and that my friends, that is the most important thing. Love from one party is called lust or a desire to have something you’ll never have. It makes him think’the next love, the next heart, should he care? Or should he just give up and not care? Should he risk his heart and fall for somebody and let her break his heart again, or just stay out of relationships. Love for awhile and end up in heartache or forget it all together? Don’t Cry Because You Broke Up Smile Because You Once Had Love Many people have told him that, but he blocks it out of his head. “If you only stood in my shoes.” He’d reply to their opinions. But their opinions didn’t matter to him, because in his mind and in his heart, that is where his future would lie. If he didn’t believe, nothing would happen. But he stopped believing because he got tired of getting hurt. When he stopped believing, the pain went away. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- He sits down in the theater, the play is about to begin. Romeo and Juliet, a William Shakespeare classic. He takes a seat, awaiting the performers to start the most brilliant piece of work ever made. He is wearing a black and purple suit. He is very clean shaven, looking his best for tonight. A beautiful girl stands beside him. She wears a red dress that hangs down to her ankles. She has beautiful strawberry blonde hair that is slightly curly. Her eyes are blue, they are eyes that you can see from a mile away. “Excuse me.” He looks up at her. His jaw drops at her amazing beauty. He stumbles around with his words? “Umm, yes ma’am?” “Is this seat taken?” “She speaks with an Australian accent, an automatic heart stealer. His heart would sink if it weren’t for the heartache he once felt over this gender. “No, it is all yours.” She smiles and takes a seat. She places her purse on her lap. “I am always worried whenever I come here by myself. That I’ll never find a seat.” “Then why do you come by yourself?” “Nobody else feels the way I do about these plays. They mean a lot to me, because I can relate to the stories and the emotions they express are the ones that I’ve felt previously.” “Sorry to hear that.” She looks up in astonishment? “Why?” “Because most playwrights only write about sorrow and sadness. They use these plays to express their opinions, hoping to relieve tension between things in reality. And to feel the way these plays feel, is nothing less then sad. So again, I’m sorry.” Her jaw drops this time? “That is brilliant, are you a poet or an English major or something?” “I’m just a Harvester.” “Oh, that’s nice. You own a farm then?” “I used to own a farm, now I am a Harvester.” She chuckles because she doesn’t realize what he is talking about, but he doesn’t lead onto what his meaning of a Harvester is. “Then what do you harvest?” “Tears.” She chuckles? “I don’t think I understand what you mean.” “I’m a professional wrestler.” “Then why are you so poetic?” “Poetic” I just reword life in my own words instead of anybody elses. I express how I feel, no matter what the consequence is. The worst punishment is death, but all must die sometime. Being alive is just stalling the process of life.” “It seems that you wrote Romeo and Juliet.” He chuckles? “Don’t give me that much credit.” “Well I am giving you credit for being a smart man.” “Well thank you ma’am.” “Call me Michelle.” “Nice to meet you Michelle. My name is Sage.” “That is your wrestling name right?” “My wrestling name is Sage Sabbot. My real name is Sage Tse Ling.” “That is a nice name. What is your favorite food?” “A fresh garden salad, from back home in China.” “You are from Chin?” “I know what you are doing, but I can’t do this.” “What do you mean?” “You are trying to get to know me. Please don’t, I don’t need anybody to care about anymore.” “Why?” “What do the words, Together Forever and Ever, mean to you?” “Umm’getting married?” “People don’t understand me. They don’t accept me.” “Maybe it is because whenever you first talk to them, you tell them you don’t care.” “I don’t care because nobody accepts me for who I am, instead of what society smiles upon and what they see on the outside.” “They probably don’t accept you because they don’t know you.” “And then they’ll get to know me and break my heart?” “Whatever, you are an asshole and you don’t understand females at all. No wonder why nobody accepts you.” “You are right, I don’t understand them. That is why I dislike risking pain and sorrow over a mind game called love.” “Maybe you should take a chance.” “I’ve taken chances all my life. There is no room for love in my busy schedule. I have all I need, and this missing spot in my heart, it will continue to be missing and it will be filled with evil and violence’making me more deadly and nefarious. The world, oh, it is pitiful and pathetic. And it starts with people like you. You are accepted because you are beautiful and society loves beautiful people. But I, I am the ugly duckling of the world. But I still bleed the same blood you do. And that doesn’t mean anything to anyone, because this world is disgusting. And I’m glad I live in my own world, with my own species called The Nevermore. A group that doesn’t take crap from anybody. And a group that doesn’t accept less then the best. I don’t need your love or your problems in my life. I can survive on my own. I can survive on my own. I won’t take anymore chances of getting hurt anymore. The pain and sorrow doesn’t compare to what can be love.” “I’m glad you can, I’ll take my own chances by sitting with another person.” He smiles, but inside, inside he feels like an asshole. But The Nevermore showed him to not care about that and to show no mercy. Show no pity on anybody, and that is what he has learned to do, and ended up loving it. Sage ended up having no love in his life. But he isn’t the only one, many people around the world end up having no love. But there is life past that’friendships and dreams can replace that love. And accomplish your dreams and don’t settle for less. Sage gave up on his love and he didn’t start believing in what the future may bring him. Sage has a life to live and doesn’t need to concentrate on love. But others, others strive for it. Hopefully you all, you all will find the one you love and cherish it and never let it go’because many never will.
