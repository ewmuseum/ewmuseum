Dark Horse Saloon

22 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 22 The scene opened up to the exact same sidewalk me and Jamester were walking on before, but this time, we stopped to rest on a little bench that was on the side of the road. Me and him were thinking of ideas, of where to go, because it was so boring where we were right now. I was sitting on the bench, but Jamester was sitting on the grass beside the bench. He was leaning on a a stop sign pole. I was pretty much laing on the bench thinking of what to do, then something came to me in my head. I got off the bench and was now standing up. 'I know something we could do.' I said. 'Hit me.' Jesse said. 'We could drive to the GW Dark Horse Saloon. There's always people in there, and I hear it gets pretty awesome in there.' I said. 'Sure, that's a great idea.' he said. He got off the ground and me and him starteed walking back to my house to get my car. We got there as me and him walked up the driveway and into the open garage. There was one police officer in the garage looking around to see what those robbers did, or if they did do anything. I got into the drivers seat and started the car. I backed out of the driveway and unlocked the car as Jesse got in. I started driving. *15 Minutes Later* Me and Jesse had just arriving in the little parknig lot for the Darkhorse Saloon. We got out of the car as I locked the doors so no one would try to break into it. Me and Jesse walked to the front door of the saloon. They were the old time western doors that were two wooden doors that you just push. I pushed both of them as we walked inside. We saw a whole bunch of people in there, and the one man standing out of the rest, was none other than former GW World Champion, Brick. 'Hey Brick.' I yelled across the room. Brick turned around, he was talking to someone tah twas sitting in a booth. At first it looked like he didn't notice me, but then he looked at Jamester and smiled. He walked over to us. I had never seen him before in real life, so it was a pleasure. 'Hey dogs, sup?' he said to us. 'Nothing.' both me and Jamester said at the same time. Brick walked away from us and back to the man he was talking to in the booth. Me and Jamester saw a big counter with a bartender behind it. No one was sitting in the stools in front of the bartender, so me and Jamester walked over and sat down beside each other. The bartender walked up to us, probably was going to ask what we wanted to drink. 'What can I get you two?' he asked us. 'I'll have a beer.' said Jamester. 'I'll have the same as my friend.' I said. The bartender walked away from us and then walked back with two bottles of beer in his hands. He handed them to us as we started drinking them. We looked around and saw that there were people partying everywhere. People were playing pool, and even someone was on the mechanical bull. I saw that there was a remote on the coutner, so I grabbed it and started clicking down each channel. 'What do you want to watch?' I asked Jamester. 'Doesn't matter, I think theres a football game on right now.' he said. I started flipping the channels until I got to channel 400. This television had BELL Express Vu, so the sports channel was on channel 400. Sure enough, when I got to the channel, there was a football game going on. It was the Chicago Bears v.s. the Denver Broncos, and Denver was winning 14-7 with only a few minutes left in the game. I put the remote back onto the counter and started watching the game. Suddenly, all of the people that were in the bar came over and started cheering for Denver, but not me, I was cheering for Chicago. 'Who are you cheering for?' I asked Jamester. 'I'm cheering for Chicago, but nobody here seems to like them.' he said. 'I do.' I said softly so no one would hear. 'Cool, I guess we have alot of stuff in common.' he said. Me and Jamester kept cheering for Chicago, and I guess it was good luck because they came back with two touchdowns to win the game 21-14. Everyone was so pissed off, except for us. The game ended and me and Jesse finished our drinks. I left mine on the counter and so did Jamester. Me and him looked around and saw Brandi Sights sitting on a chair, with a margarita, but she wasn't talking to anyone. Me and Jesse walked over to her as she looked up, she smiled at us. 'Hello Jesse, hello Max.' she said. 'Hi.' both of us said. 'What you guys up to?' she asked. 'Nothing, were trying to find something to do, but there really isn't anything to do.' we said. Me, Jesse, and Brandi kept talking for a bit. Me and Jesse got some chairs and set them beside Brandi so we din't have to stand the whole talk. We kept on talking as the scene started to fade to darkness.
