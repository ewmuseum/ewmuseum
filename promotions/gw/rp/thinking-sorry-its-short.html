Thinking... (sorry its short)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: hour before deadline :D 'god damn its deafening, wish you'd shut up about everything.. the future is x rated...' ------------- I sit in my locker room, on the black leather couch, thinking about the match I have to fight in a few hours. I pick up the paper and read it again, making sure I haven't misread it the other couple dozen times I looked at it. Main Event Frost Vs Ben J. Pailthorpe Guest referee TLM What happened on Evloution last week surprised us all when in the midst of a huge brawl Derek Frost destroyed BJP after fighting by his side against The Nevermore. Two former Young Bloods will now stand toe to toe in the ring and the man who has to keep control of the match is none other than another YB member in TLM - Jay Robson. Will Jay take sides of his two best friends or will he call it down the middle and hope they work things out on their own. This match could spell the end of a Young Blood revival and all will be watching to find out what happens. Damn, I have read it the same way every time. How did this all happen? Just a couple weeks ago I was an emotional wreck because my wife was missing. Now I know she's safe with her father, and I'm fighting one of my former best friends with another good friend as the referee. I run my fingers through my usually spiked blue hair, leaving it messed up atop my head. Four hours til my match starts. I'll get dressed half hour earlier, get myself emotionally ready after im physically ready. Ben, why are we in this match tonight? I want you to prove to me that I can trust you again. My water bottle is laid across the seat of the couch next to me, so I pick it up and down half the bottle in a few drinks. Ben, why didn't you have the guts to face me one on one after you and Jay cowardly attacked Hans and me? Jay came forward, after a month of me rehabbing my arm, and got his ass handed to him. Now WHY wouldn't you face me? YOU were top of the fed! World Champion! Where was I? Stuck in the mid card ranks. Now honestly, WHY could you NOT fight me then? Instead you made me wait to get my match with you. Ben J. Pailthorpe vs Derek Frost. I wad the paper up and throw it against the wall. Not only that, but the other part of the match. Special guest referee. TLM Jay Robson. A picture in a frame shines under the lights, between the flaps of my bag. I pick it up and look at it. No! I can't let this crap get in my way. I do NOT need to sit here and look at this picture. I toss it to the side and take one more glance at it. Me, Cindy, Jay, Johnny, Hans, Ben. All of us on the stage of the old GWF entry way, with the pyros shooting off behind us. I need to stop thinking about the past and worry more about tonight. Ok, so not WORRY about tonight, but get my mind set. Shit, I can't. What will Jay do in this match? Is he going to call it down the middle? Is he going to help me out here? No, he wouldn't do that. If he helps either of us it'll be Ben. They've been in more stables than just YB together. Those two helped kill the YB except for me, I was, and in one way or another still am, the Lone Young Blood. If I have to fight BJP and TLM, I can't win. I can put up a hell of a fight, but it is impossible to fight a referee and win. I get up and walk to the bathroom door, then lean against the door frame. I have to get ready. I need to shower and get dressed. I'll call Cindy. She can tell me what to do. Cindy is a Cruse, she will know exactly what I need to do. With that, I walk into the bathroom and slam the door shut.
