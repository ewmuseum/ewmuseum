Date: unknown .:: ::: ::. Part I Across the Universe Marianne Key was slightly uneasy. Her parents? northern Washington ranch was home to plenty of animals, but never one quite so strange as her cousin Alexis Key. Or, that is, what Alexis had turned into. Marianne remembered, when they were younger, that she and Alexis had often played in the stables together, standing on the backs of horses, balanced as though they were circus performers. As they’d gotten older, though, that had turned into Marianne wanting to play with makeup and talk about boys all night, and Alexis curled up in the corner of the hayloft reading a book. Marianne found it slightly odd that they had been so similar at age six and had turned into something so different. A few days ago, Alexis had showed up at the front door of the ranch, panting and completely exhausted. It seemed, upon questioning, that even Alexis didn’t know how she’d gotten here, aside from hawking something or other to get bus fare into Washington. Things had seemed fairly innocuous at first, but Alexis had acted so strangely. Alexis had always had a special relationship with her Aunt Viv and Uncle Owain, but she’d seemed oddly happy to find out that they were in Europe on an extended business and family-visiting trip. Most of the Key family still lived in England, save the occasional strangeness that Edred and Owain, brothers, had presented in leaving for America. Alexis had adapted remarkably well, though, helping Marianne tend the animals and do the chores around the house, and Marianne had begun to enjoy her company. That was until Alexis started asking her strange questions. Marianne found it disconcerting, but somewhere in her big heart, she found a way to explain it’Alexis was obviously scarred pretty badly from losing both her mother and her father in accidents. Marianne didn’t know how she would have held up under those circumstances, so even if Alexis was a little strange, Marianne still admired her greatly. Perhaps most strangely of all, Alexis had acted so? girlish. Marianne had never thought of Alexis as masculine by any stretch of the word, but Marianne knew that she herself was feminine almost to the point of ridiculousness’and from her pink-swathed bedroom, that could easily be proven. But from the beginning, Alexis had been on about some man that she was journeying cross-country to meet. She’d been in Cleveland, and decided to surprise him by dropping everything, even her school-work, to hitch across the country to Napa Valley to meet him at his college graduation. Then, there came the big surprise’Alexis asking Marianne’s help with a makeover. There had been plenty of times when Marianne, in the spirit of feminine teenage sniping masquerading as “help” had asked Alexis if she could to a makeover on her; Alexis had surprised her this time by jumping into the subject wholeheartedly. And perhaps most alarmingly, in a rather bubbly way. If you’d asked Marianne to pick one word to describe her cousin, it sure as hell wouldn’t have been “bubbly.” But, she had agreed. With Alexis? insistence, they had gone shopping in town’a two hour drive. Then, Marianne had helped Alexis with makeup application tips. Finally, they had cut Alexis? hair short, and were in the last phase of the process now’dying it a deep, rich wine-red. At least all those years of being a high school priss were paying off if she could help her cousin, but Marianne knew that something was up. Alexis was supposed to leave tomorrow, and Marianne wasn’t letting her leave without an answer. Marianne perked up slightly as the blow-dryer turned off. Alexis stepped out of the bathroom with a towel wrapped around her shoulder, and her newly cropped hair hanging down just to her chin. “How do I look?” she asked, smiling at her cousin. Marianne smiled a bit uneasily. “Great, Alexis?” “Good!” Alexis interrupted, “I’ve got to finish packing before tomorrow, gosh, I’ve got so many things to do that it’s insane.” She started rummaging around in her bag, seemingly rearranging things in it, but Marianne figured it was just trying to buy time and get her out of the room. She took a deep breath. “Alexis” I want to know the truth, okay? I know the guy in California is a lie, and I’m not here to tell you that you’re wrong for doing something, but what’s wrong with you? You come here and ask me to turn you into a totally different person than you are and? I just don’t believe your reasons. I’m sorry.” Alexis looked up sharply at her cousin, and for a few moments, Marianne thought that she had said the wrong thing. Her lip began to tremble’Marianne was rather sensitive, and she hated making anyone mad. But then Alexis relented with a long sigh. “Do you really want to know?” Marianne nodded. “Yes” I’m worried about you.” Alexis sighed, crossing her legs underneath her Indian-style. “Fine” don’t say I didn’t warn you. You remember that accident that killed mom and dad?” Marianne nodded again. “Well” write that off,” Alexis said, looking critically up into her cousin’s eyes, “Mom and dad died because they got killed hunting vampires.” Marianne’s mouth dropped into an “O” of shock. She shook her head, her curled brown hair tossing from side to side. Alexis held up a hand. “Yeah, I know” just please, wait until I’m done. I found it hard to believe too, but that was until I got involved with The Den myself. They paid my way through college, took me in as an honored trainee because of my parents? work? but then I found out that they’ve got closer connections to the vampires they hunt than they want us to know.” Alexis paused, then shrugged. “They felt like I was onto them, I guess. Put me on suspension and then planned to give me a hearing to see whether or not I was ready for a return. I bolted’they were either going to brainwash me or kill me, so I left Cleveland. Since then, they’ve been on my tail, and I’ve been hitching and running across America in the same pair of clothes” ran out of money just outside of Ohio.” She paused, looking down at the carpet. Marianne finally found her words, but her voice was shaking. “And California?” Alexis looked up at her, a sudden hard determination writing itself through the now-redhead’s blue eyes. “California is where the answers are. That’s where I first realized that something was screwed up with The Den, and I’m going back to Napa Valley to a house we burned down trying to kill two vampires. We missed them, but our leader just let them get away’there has to be a reason why.” Marianne drew in a gasp, and grabbed Alexis by the wrist. “No! That’s dangerous!” Alexis shook her head. “Let me go, Marianne, you’ve got to let me do this.” “Alexis, I can’t,” she said, her Pacific Northwest twang coming out quite strongly, “You’ll get yourself killed, by either The Den or those” those THINGS!” “They won’t be able to recognize me anymore, and you helped me with that. That’s why I came to you, I knew you’d be able to help.” Alexis pulled her wrist away, and Marianne stood up, in desperation. She darted over to her desk and grabbed her cell phone. Alexis whipped her head around. “What are you doing”!” she snapped. “I’m calling momma and daddy,” Marianne said, a determined look in her blue eyes, which were shining with frightened tears. “No. Listen to me. Put the damn phone down and listen to me.” Marianne dropped it on the ground, with a clatter. “This is for ME. I didn’t want to tell you the truth because I didn’t want to get you mixed up in it. Now that you know, you better learn how to keep your goddamn mouth shut. Viv and Owain don’t need to know what happened to my parents, because the family would never be the same. And you’re NOT going to tell them, okay?” Marianne nodded weakly, her shoulders slumping in defeat. Alexis stood up, shouldering her bag. “Now that you know” I’m leaving early, alright? The sooner I get to Napa Valley, the better.” Alexis didn’t even give her cousin time to say goodbye before she was out the bedroom door and down the stairs. Marianne turned dumbly to the blinds, pulling them down to look out, and watching her cousin walk down the driveway at a rapid pace. She wanted to call after her and try to make her stop, but there was nothing left to say, not even goodbye. Down on the driveway, a triumphant smile graced the newly makeup-adorned face of Alexis Key. This was exactly what she needed to help her out’a little refresher stay in a real bed, a new look, and a little bit of money to get her on her way. So, she’d lied to her cousin. Alexis had pick-pocketed the bus fare to get here, and somehow convinced her cousin to help her out with the money to get to Napa Valley’her flight left at 4:30 this morning, and she was going to spend some time in the airport terminal, hijacking people’s wallets. Of course, perhaps the worst part was that she hadn’t needed the money from her cousin at all, considering how good Alexis had gotten over the past few weeks at forcing other people to pay her way, without even knowing it. If there was one thing to be said about Alexis Key it was that she would survive, no matter what it took. And because of her keen instinct, she was jumping a flight to Napa Valley tonight, back to the ashy ruins of Gideon de Medici’s mansion, and the last ounce of truth she had known in her life. If she had to start from there and rebuild everything, so be it. .:: ::: ::. Part II Questions He didn’t even have to answer it’he was a cornered buck, with wolf’s paws on its throat. She forced his head to the side, a smug look coming over her face. “You’ll make a fine meal, then,” she purred, running her tongue over his neck. He squirmed, but the cold press of gun-metal in one side of his throat more than outweighed the need to escape the vampire fangs that pressed in on the other side. When she bit down on his skin, he only experienced pain for the first moment’then a heady rush of pleasure swept over him, ecstasy he had never before experienced. “No! You’re not supposed to feel this way!” his mind shouted at him. But it was all a weak reminder under the fingertips of this beast of the night. Lost in the nameless pleasure of what she would have called The Kiss, he couldn’t even feel his life draining away. He started to slide down the wall, and then, it was only her deceptively strong arms that held up his weight. At the sound of a helpless mew from his lips, she pulled away, smirking down at him, a droplet of blood falling from her left incisor onto her bottom lip. “And now that you know what pleasure really is” I’m afraid you don’t deserve to have it.” BANG! The slayer dropped to the ground, his throat essentially in ruins. Emotionlessly, Shadow dropped the gun, and turned to walk away. Hunger sated, she had no use hanging around, and no use keeping the gun either’she’d long ago used her powers of Vicissitude to remove her fingerprints. Plus, the pistol had been silenced’so much for the intelligence of the Society of Leopold, not realizing how THAT could get turned around on them. She smirked’this had been all too easy, just a game of bait and switch. Naturally enough, she’d known the little parasite was following her since she left the airport, but it had been too much fun to act like she was an unaware whelp, freshly sired, and then turn it around on him. Once again, someone’s perception put them into her hands at exactly the wrong time. Once again, the huntress had played her game to perfection, and walked away victorious.* The memory cascaded over Shadow gently, like falling feathers. She looked down into the sink of her hotel room, watching the clots of gore fall into the sink and wash away. Along with it went the memory of this nameless slayer who had attempted to make her the prey of some futile, pointless hunt that he had engaged in for no other reason than “cleansing the earth” or whatever it was those Society of Leopold assholes believed in this day and age. In all honesty, she wasn’t too concerned about it. One less human on this planet was just one less idiot on this planet. Despite the fact that she needed them for blood, she could still at least realize that one day, she would be able to reach Azhi Dahaka, and step beyond the boundary of needing blood to sustain her processes. That was all aside from the point’she had other concerns right now. There was Global, of course, and the match with Soma, and the Extreme Title defense at PRIME’s next show as well. Somewhere in the back of her mind, there was also Virgil. But, so long as she had so much work on her plate, and so many more chances to press buttons and test limits, both her own and her opponents? alike, it was much easier to keep from being unhappy. Perhaps it was a human trick, but it was one of the only trickeries they used to lie to themselves that actually worked. .:: ::: ::. Soma, Soma, Soma? she couldn’t get him out of her head. No matter what she did to try and remedy that this week, she was still waking up with Soma stuck right inside the front of her skull. Even if she had gone so far as to bash her skull repeatedly into a wall, he still would have been there. Of course, that would have necessitated some reconstruction work once the damage was done, and she liked her face well enough that she’d rather practice her medical arts on someone else. She realized full well, of course, that the biggest reason Soma was on her mind was because of the vial of blood hanging around her neck. She kept it close to her at all times because? well, there was really no because. There was no way to put a name to why she did so, only that she felt she had to. Now, she pulled it off, and held it up to the light. Same as always, the blood shifted back and forth in the tube, kept liquid by some strange enchantment’it didn’t feel warm anymore, though, and she wondered if it would still have its potency, even without its original temperature. “No,” she chastised herself, “Do NOT let yourself even THINK about how it tastes, because that’s exactly what he wants.” Even as she noted that, however, she realized that she had no clue what Soma wanted. He considered himself her better, that much had been clear ever since she had first encountered him in the hallway. The disdain which he had heaped upon her had seemed much more natural than anything else he could have said. Nobody expected two vampires from such different backgrounds to get along. It seemed to simply be a law of nature that beasts would be wary of one another, especially when one was new to the territory. Of course, the problem with that was Shadow’s great delight with flaunting every single more she could find, especially mores that dealt with vampire society, and what she viewed as its silly little hierarchies. She would bow and scrape to no one, no matter how old, unless he was her sire’and her sire, of course, was long dead, as was the domitor that she was supposed to be serving. That left Shadow somewhat adrift in life, given the chance to make her own path, and that was something she had taken advantage of. There was no one on this entire earth who could say that they had in any way dominated her or held her down. No one. Even as she thought that, she realized that there were exceptions to the rule. Certain periods in her life had been weaker than others, and she’d found herself underneath someone else’s boot. At least it was reassuring to note, then, that she had figured out their game and managed to somehow overturn them, and negate whatever influence they might have had. It usually had cataclysmic results’she could look at her now-shattered marriage with Corvus, to point out just one example; then again, what did she care? As long as she still had her autonomy, she was happy. She frowned, though, as the situation with Soma came to mind, and frowned for several reasons. First of all, she hadn’t figured out Soma’s game, had she? No, and she had absolutely no clue where to start. Second, if she wasn’t careful, if she didn’t play her cards right, she’d lose her autonomy, and it was always when someone tried to strangle Shadow’s potential that she became the most useless. She couldn’t let that happen to herself, not right now, not when she’d come so far, and finally gotten back on the right path again. The second incident with Soma was more disturbing than the first, if only because it seemed like he knew her weaknesses, and preyed on them. The seduction had been almost too well-played for it to be sincere, yet at the same time, there were cues from him; you couldn’t fake lust, not when acute sensory cues were involved. If he had faked it, he was the most talented fake she had ever seen. What’s worse was that she had allowed herself to reciprocate. She still remembered how it felt to be drawn into his strong arms, and the way his kiss tasted’she didn’t WANT to remember it as fondly as she did, because, when it came down to it, it was just subterfuge. She didn’t know what he was covering for, but he was covering for something, and he knew exactly how to get her to do what he wanted. The only problem was, she didn’t think he’d realized her stubbornness. She hadn’t quite made up her mind whether she trusted Soma just yet, and from the way he acted, she’d have been quite surprised if he expected her to. There was no way in hell she bought the innocent act a week after he’d been accusing he of being too human for his taste. Blood, blood, blood. His blood. That’s all that she could think about. Would it make him happy if she just downed it, drank it without thinking twice? Would he have been angry to know that she still had it on her person, and was still weighing the options? The lure was almost too powerful to deny. He’d not said a word about wanting to harm her’of course, if that’s what he’d had in mind, he wouldn’t have said a word. But there was something that kept sticking in her mind. ??When you think you are ready, drink the blood and come find me.?** But of course, he’d said that before there was the matter of this match. A match that made no sense to her. She had encountered her share of strange beings with strange ideas of attraction and commonality before, but never beings that changed their minds so quickly from disdain to raging lust to battle so quickly. Unless he was just as confused as she was. But that was unthinkable, wasn’t it? A being as self-assured as Leinad Soma being uncertain about something? She shook her head slightly. Still, the idea of just drinking it kept coming to mind. She couldn’t shake the powerful urge, and she realized that more than likely, it was his blood, his powerful essence, that had been making her think about him so much lately, that had made her insane for him. At the very least, perhaps if she drank the blood, she would be able to resume normal thought patterns. Plus, she had to admit, she was a bit selfish about it. It was powerful blood, and Shadow was a most curious creature. The idea of drinking it to see how it would change or strengthen her in the ring was almost too tempting to pass up. After all, she did have an Extreme Title defense coming up against PRIME’s Nova, and she’d like nothing more than to have even more of an advantage when she stepped into the ring with him. Call her a bad person with a nasty penchant for vengeance’you’d be right. Shadow stood up, stalking into the bathroom. It was now or never. Decision time. The contents of the vial were either going down her throat, or into the toilet. She was tired of being caught in the middle about Soma. She was tired of never knowing whether or not she was going to get turned on at the next moment. She was tired of that mattering’somehow, some way, she had to come up with an answer, right now. It was either tell Soma indirectly to go fuck himself, or figure out what he had to teach her that was so important. The choice was nearly impossible to make. .:: ::: ::. Shadow shifted on the couch so suddenly that Damek and Jirina leapt to their feet. She calmed them with a glance, however, and they sleepily slumped back to the carpet. Whatever else came in the way, Shadow had suddenly realized the truth’this match wasn’t anything to Soma, other than a test. Soma was pressing her buttons, testing her mettle? seeing what she had in her. She looked at the empty vial on the table, and a smirk crawled over her face. Determination carried one a long way, and Shadow had yet to meet anyone in the ring with as much grit as she had. If this was a test, she would be the one passing, no matter the expense to anyone involved. .:: ::: ::. *Beginning flashback scene taken from my Extreme Title defense RP entitled “What You Can’t Kill” **Quote from Soma taken from the Soma/Shadow in-card last week entitled “An Offer You Can’t Refuse .:: ::: ::.
