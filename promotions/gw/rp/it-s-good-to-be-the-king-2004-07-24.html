It's good to be the King

24 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/24/04 Silence. I sit there in the silence of my new office,and i'm not in a good mood.This maybe different than what most people think is. I'm cleaned up from my beatings, now adorned in my black armani 'Soma Collection' suit trying to dress to impress to put me in a better mood I had the office done to have a large black marble desk and floor.The desk shaped in a reversed cresent and up against tinted windows to each side. The wall right behind him is different weaponry, and the finest peice i've ever mad to right arm, my black onyx handled thai chi sword. The table could easily be for to people with cameras and tv screens everywhere. There are chairs in front of my office for when I have private meetings. As I sit there in my office going over documents and such a light tap is heard. Mortals and their constant disturbances, I prend not to hear it. A few moments later I heard the tapping' 'Who's that tapping at my chamber door?' 'What?' 'Nevermind. Come in.' My attempt at the joke upsetting me, my eyes peer to the door seeing who is gonna come through. One of my three new assistants and what looks like a lawyer. The side of my face without hair in front of it gives a gentle but smug smile. I stand and fold my arms as the man makes it to my desk. I open my arms gesturing him to sit and her to leave but softly. Watching her leave, making my day better, but then back to this 'man' made it worse. He sits and then I sit trying to be informal as one when having a bad day, but then I perk up and I smile at this putrid man of lies, but I smile and talk to him</> 'Good day to you sir. My name is Leinad Soma, the new owner the St. Francis foundation and you are?' 'I'm Sam Hyatte, one of the men on the board of directers, I was chosen as representive on behalf of them to talk about how we shall run the company.' He speaks as if he practiced in the mirror each word, each phrase, each syllable, so he could stand before me sweating and trembling like some kind of pig. He looks as if he is they type of guy that people steer away from because he is repulsive looking in his suit that is black and white with old brown shoes. No self respect for himself and no taste. I look at him wanting to show the disgust I feel in his presence but something stops me and I calmly retort.' 'What makes you think you guys are staying here?' 'Well Leinad...' 'Call me Mr. Soma' 'Yes, whatever, like I was saying...' 'No, not whatever you call me Mr. Soma or your ass is fired right now!' I look at the man with anger in my eyes but I close my eyes and take a deep breath and once again smiling.' 'Look here Sam, I bought out this company, the whole thing, including your job. Do not come up here asking how 'we' are going to run the company. But none the less you can keep your petty jobs....but you will be merely share holders and you'll still be getting your check.' 'You can't do that our contract clearly states....' 'I want you to watch something.' He begins to speak again and I cut him off with a hand gesture. I pick up my phone and talk for and then hanging back up. I give him the full Soma smile, sadistic and filled with imagination. Within seconds of the call all thre of my assistance standing there. The first is wearing a tight white top, showing more clevage than it should with a black skirt. She has long black hair and brown eyes. Her skin is almost as pale as most vampires.She was medium height but knew she worked out in a gym, could't be older than twenty. The second assistant had on a black suit that made her look more like an executive than an assistant, her creamy peach colored skin against her red hair and her blue eyes made me smile even more, her holding a plate of fruit. The third was strikingly beautiful. She had smooth brown skin, with matching brown eyes. She has black shoulder length hair and is wearing a red casual business suit. The man watched one of the ladies slowly walk over to his general direction as the other two sit on the edge of my desk. I, grab my sword and unsheath it while he watches the pale one move closer to him. I begin to walk around the desk and as I do another girl comes from under the desk wiping her mouth and walks out casualy. I finally get around the desk so I can watch this man's dream becomes a nightmare. 'Do you like the suit Sam? Do you like the woman undressing in front of you. You see this suit i'm wearing? It's worth more than your brand new lexus. It is made by Versace and only the elite can even look at the set. What my lovely assistant is showing you is the lingerie department. The feeling of the clothes is for the best feeling we can provide through this portion, or should I say this foundation's new job is. You know Sam, you could be part of the family. Would you like that being apart of the Soma family?' At hearing what I was going to turn this foundation to, he stopped looking at the pale girl, and looks me directly in the eyes. I peer into his mind, I already know what he's going to say. 'Mr. Soma, you can go to hell! I'll call a lawyer and we will sue you for everything your worth. You heartless bastard, we were coming up with cures for certain diseases. You can't think about any but the welfare of your corporations.' 'I understand your mad Sam but don't be rude....I don't like rude people, anyway the offer still stands. I'm not looking out just for me....it is my destiny to take over all. ' The man looks at me the same way I looked at him earlier but this time i'm smiling at him back. He spits on me. A mortal man spit on me..I smirk and have a little twich . 'I told you there is no reason to be rude, but since you have not learned that I shall teach you.' 'Fuck You!' Fuck me?! I lunge at the manand land on him at such a way I stabbed him through the chest with the sword. Once we were on the floor I kept stabbing him, after about five minutes of stabbing him I stood up, took off the blood soaked suit coat. And sat back down at my desk. he looks at the three ladies 'Well...' The ladies jump off the table, two of them get under the table while the red head straddled me and fed me fruit. 'It's good being the king...' I sit laid back in silence.
