Trapped (Part 4)

5 April 2005

Date: 5th April 2005 I wandered lonely as a cloud That floats on high over vales and hills, When all at once I saw a crowd, A host, of golden daffodils; Beside the lake, beneath the trees, Fluttering and dancing in the breeze. Continuous as the stars that shine And twinkle on the milky way, They stretched in never-ending line Along the margin of a bay: Ten thousand saw I at a glance, Tossing their heads in sprightly dance. The waves beside them danced; but they Out-did the sparkling waves in glee: I gazed and gazed but little thought What wealth the show to me had brought: For often, when on my couch I lie In vacant or in pensive mood, They flash upon that inward eye Which is the bliss of solitude; And then my heart with pleasure fills, And dances with the daffodils. Daffodils by William Wordsworth I have always loved that poem, it is so vivid and beautiful yet it is simply describing a flower. My mother often read it to me and when I closed my eyes as a child it felt like I could see what Wordsworth was seeing as he gazed out onto the lakes. But what has this got to do with my current situation I hear you ask? Well ever since I have been locked up in my steel box and times have become dark, I always recite this poem in my head. It is a release for me, it takes me away from all the drama in my life, and it takes me away from the thought that I have not seen my family for nearly 2 months. It takes me away from the fact that for 2 months I have been going through idiotic tests. Most importantly it takes me away from the fact that at this moment in time I see no escape. You may have noticed my moods at the GW shows, that I seem distant and distracted. I have always tried to maintain a sense of professionalism in my work but recently it has become impossible. The GW is the only outlet I have for my frustration; it is the only thing in my life that I can actually control. Yet I have had a constant run of failure, I keep on losing and I don’t know why. Even with all the experiments going on I still have access to a gym and still train. I need to win and I attend to at this next Evolution. My current run in this Platinum Series is terrible, every single Series match I have had has been a loss. This time round I face a man who is more in love with himself than anyone else, Mike Biddle. There is something about Biddle that for me just doesn’t add up but I don’t know what it is. I seem to see him more and more nowadays backstage and always seem to bump into him. Something is going on but what? “Take a deep breath in’good’and out.” I was lying in the doctor’s office on his examination table as he pressed his hands against my rib cage as I took in a deep breath. I had taken a few rough knocks in the rumble and Wood decided that I should be checked up not that he was worried about me but that fact that he didn’t want a damaged “product”. Wood was in the doctor’s office with me looking smug as always as he sat back on the chair next to the doctor’s desk. I stared at the ceiling wondering if I simply knocked the doctor out would I be able to escape. I soon stopped wondering as I noticed Charlie hovering behind Wood cracking his knuckles. “Are you having any difficulty breathing?” The grey-haired Doctor Wright asked me as he looked down. “No.” I replied with a sigh and looking at him menacingly. Doctor Wright was a kind looking man and if circumstances were different I would probably be more helpful in his questioning. But he worked for Wood and so to me he represented my torment. “So what is the verdict doctor?” Came Wood’s voice from behind. “There doesn’t seem to be any broken ribs and his lungs sound very clear.” Wright said as he turned around to face Wood. “Will he be able to compete?” “I don’t see why not. He is just badly bruised and a simple bandage around his lower rib cage should heal them pretty quick.” “What about experiments?” Wood asked as if it was the most important questions in the world. I looked at Wright hoping that he was about to say that I was not to be experimented on anymore. However a quick glance at Wood and Charlie told me the answer as they stood in a very threatening manner. “Erm’he will be fine to be experimented on, Mr. Wood.” Wright replied nervously. “Excellent doctor, well thank you for taking the time to give Adam the once over.” Wood said as he slipped a hundred dollar bill inside of the doctor’s lab coat and patted him on the back. Wright made his way out of the office not before giving me a quick glance. The look on his face said it all. He looked at me as if to say sorry but I had no forgiveness for him. He was like everyone else in this place, all controlled by the dollar. The door shut air tight as to keep the room sterilised. I was about to get up as so to be dragged back to my steel room but I was held down by Charlie who placed his huge paw like hand on my chest and pushed down hard. Wood then moved over so he was over my head and looking down. He smiled menacingly. “Don’t get up Adam, we are not finished yet.” “What?” I exclaimed feeling the panic in my voice. “You didn’t think you were here for a simple check up did you?” “To be honest with you’yeah I did actually.” Both Wood and Charlie chuckled which infuriated me as I tried desperately to break free from Charlie’s grip. “Settle down Adam,” Wood ordered, “You are not doing yourself any favours.” “What the hell have you got planned Wood?” “Well you see Adam, the past few weeks we have been testing the suit’s abilities haven’t we?” “Yeah, so?” “Well we haven’t really been testing the person inside the suit have we?” “You mean me?” I asked already knowing the answer. “Yes Adam, you!” Wood grinned, “You have worn this suit or versions of the suit for the passed 14 years and for 9 of those you have pushed the suit more than any of the other prototypes and that is why you were chosen for this programme.” “What the hell you getting at Wood?” “What I am getting at Adam is the fact that it dawned on me yesterday that although the suit keeps you alive, it does not make you the fighter you are. You are the one who makes you a fighter.” “Well that is obvious Wood. I trained for years to become the fighter I am today.” “And that is why our next experiment does not involve the suit per se but more you.” I felt fear flood through my body. “But hang on isn’t that breaching the contract. You can experiment on the suit and me in the suit but not just on me.” I said trying to control the fear in my voice. “That is why the experiment still involves the suit Adam and therefore does not breach our contract.” “So what the hell does this experiment involve?” “Combat.” Wood smiled. “Combat” What do you mean combat?” “You and the suit.” “Huh?” I exclaimed the fear now being replaced with confusion. “You’ll see Adam, now it is time to rest. You have got a busy few days ahead.” Wood looked over at Charlie and nodded. Charlie brought out a syringe from his jacket and before I could even struggle he jabbed me in the right arm with the needle and injected me with some fluid. My eyes became blurry as I looked at Wood who was walking out. He was about to open the door when he turned back to me and grinned. “Oh by the way good luck at Evolution!” As he opened the door I began to slip into a deep sleep. The last thing I heard was Wood’s mobile phone ringing and him answering. “Hey there, you ready for Evolution?”
