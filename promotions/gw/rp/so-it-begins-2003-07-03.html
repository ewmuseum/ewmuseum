So It Begins

3 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
So It Begins Jin Akiyara Global Wrestling Promo #440 Date: 03 July 2003 Location: Inner Thoughts Grateful Satisfying Allured My first night at GW’s Global Arena conveyed a wide range of emotions for my eyes and ears. I heard all types of squabble, toil, and turmoil. I saw antics that ranged from clear lunacy, to sadistic plans. There was even an intruder backstage. Where I was limited. Sure I was told I could go anywhere. But anywhere meant only within the first few levels. The GW security guards dared not let me go any higher and the public levels. The office levels and the upper echelon was just a tad bit out of my reach. But maybe that’s a sign of why I am here. Maybe being restricted to the bottom levels is a sign that I’m also to be restricted at the so called “rookie level”. Which embodies both pay and competition. I’m sure I’ll work out of it though. I’m here to work. I’m here to have fun. But after what I saw on Evolution, I’m wondering just how much fun I can have…before it gets too sick. Oh well, the best and worst is yet to come. My best will always be surpassed by my efforts the next day. My worst will never be remembered. I’m here to stay. In GW. In Dallas, Texas. In my new home. It is to my best benefits that GW is fixating on Dallas, Texas. At my young age, I cannot afford to travel the world with them. Even if they do pay me a hefty sum, to partake in their adventures, the cost of living will be too unbearable for me, and my sister. Aeris Akiyara But enough of that. I need my rest now. I need all the time I can get to rest and train for my very first match. Yes, you heard right. My first match. I’ve been slated to face Ace Cash and Kid Flash for the last spot in the GW’s Overload Pay Per View Cruiserweight Title Match. I’m shocked. I’m shocked that I would even be put in such a high-priced match. Surely there must be someone else. But who am I to argue against business? This is their business, and my business is to entertain. The fans will walk out the winners of that one. As for me? I’ll do what I will always do. I’ll give the fans and the fighters what they crave. A good show. Entertainment means you give it your all and the only reward you look forward to is the cheers. It doesn’t mean you lay down and take the beatings as if you couldn’t do anything more. I’m not here to lay down. I didn’t get employed to be a stepping stone. I was employed because Christina McCoy saw something in me. And for the next few years, or how ever long it takes, my sole job will be to prove that to you. With each match, with each show, that passes by, my efforts will be to make the world realize what she saw. They will even be to make myself realize. I’m not sure what she saw. I look into the mirror and all I see is a slim, decent-built, nineteen year old kid. No Experience. I’ve got very little experience, save for the training gyms in Hawaii. My only true wrestling moment, was that tournament held in Ikari’s Gym in Hawaii, to graduate. But this is the big business now. I saw that last Evolution. I saw what the “real” business is like. If my fate and destiny are to be drowned here in these waters, so be it. I won’t be one to complain. Not now not ever. So what was it that she saw? Maybe she saw an opportunity to drag in more audience. Maybe she saw some potential in me, which I hope to have. Whatever it was, I’m sure next week on Evolution, we will all find out. Ace Cash Kid Flash Two men whom I know very little about. Being the new kid on the block, it gets very hard trying to know just whom everybody is. I’ve heard Kid Flash was an extreme talent throughout the Japanese Islands. His style of wrestling is mix of many sorts. He’s exploited Japanese, Mexican, even the so called American Art of this sport. And Ace Cash? Well he remains a mystery. I hope I will remain a mystery to them. Well I have a week to prepare for the match that will either make or break my career. Everyone talks about their first chance for gold as their big break. Everyone talks about the time they became the champion as that one big match. They even talk about defeating that special someone. But no one ever talks about their first match. Intriguing. No one ever talks about the first match they had, when their stomachs were filled with jitters. Why is it? Why? Was it because they knew they would stay employed? Was it because they were already looking past the first couple of steps? Or maybe it wasn’t too important to them. Whatever it was, they failed to realize the importance of such a thing. Your first match tells the world who you are. Your first match reminds you why you are here. Whether it is being fed to the lions, or helping some poor co-worker get his dues, it makes you. The world locks its eyes on you in that first match. The fighters in the back, and around the world, look to see how and who you would be. Will you be a poor sap, easy pickings? Or will you be that special someone? This Friday marks my first chance to show the world who I am. The overused excuse of ignorance will little by little no longer be valid. With each show that goes on, the people will know more and more who Jin Akiyara is. Ace Cash and Kid Flash will be the first two. But there is also the matter of Shawn Fox. A man who was a World Champion is his former place of business. He’s new to GW as well, but he is not new to this business. When I saw him in Christina’s office, it became very apparent that he knew the wrongs of this business. It also became apparent that he does not live for the fans, or for the thrill. He lives for himself. His sole purpose is the defeat the challengers so he can say I won. Me? I will win every time no matter what. So now I have three faces to deal with starting next week. But I am confused. Christina said we would face each other. Yet Evolution advertises Ace Cash versus Kid Flash versus myself. Whatever the case is, it will not matter. Shawn Fox will get his opportunity sometime down the road. Everyone will. I may only be a kid to everyone else. I may only be nineteen years old, but I made it here. And if that may not be enough for everyone, it is enough for me. Shawn Fox will do better to adjust his anger and brutal emotion to someone else. He faces Judgment, another fighter I know little about. But I am sure it is someone who will give him a better fight than I will. At least until I am ready. But I will train and train until I am ready. Not only for him, but for everyone else. I have a lot to prove in the coming weeks. No better time to start than now. The chance is there. The opportunity is there. Now all I have to do is grasp it by the horns and not fail. I will not fail I will succeed The road is now paved But the bricks aren’t golden, or yellow They’re red and black Red with blood, and black with turmoil Everyone walks the road Few make it all the way Now I walk the road Already I have an obstacle But they will not stand in my way for long I’m the Silent Angel I’m the one you fear to be I’m the one the masses yearn to see I’m Jin Akiyara And I will win Because that is fact They will lose Because I will not be denied
