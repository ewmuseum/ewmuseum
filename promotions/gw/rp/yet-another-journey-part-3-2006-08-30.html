Yet Another Journey: Part 3

30 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30th August Memories... Some can be good and some can be bad. Personally apart from a select few, most of my memories are being sat in my room and watching the kids in my neighbourhood running around playing and laughing. However as I said there are a few good memories, like my wedding day and me getting into the GW. We can't ignore our memories, they are the things that make us remember how we got to where we are. No one likes to be forgotten, in fact it is a common fact that being forgotten is the thing that people fear most when they die. People like to think that they will be remembered for the all of eternity, that is why during life people strive to leave their mark on history. All it takes to be a memory is to touch someone’s life, no matter if it is a stranger in the street or a family member, if you make the right impact, you will become a memory that will last an eternity and in that sense you will become immortal. Many fighters strive to be such a memory and many have achieved this dream. I am one of those fighters who strive to make a mark in this world. However for now I am about to face an old memory that I fought I would never have to face… Last time I left you I had just arrived back home in Lancaster, England. I was back there after my wife had requested me that I go find my mother. The reason for this was because me and my wife are expecting a baby and Rachel did not want our child growing up without knowing their grandmother. This little journey of course coincided with my training for my match at Whiplash, a match where I face 7 other up and coming GW superstars all wanting the same thing, the GW Cruiserweight Championship. However I have not let this journey stop me from training, although this journey is important, the match at Whiplash is more important to me. To hold that GW Cruiserweight Championship and be announced as champion is what I have dreamed of ever since I started. I know that it will be a up hill struggle, an Elimination gauntlet is never easy and especially when there are 7 other people wanting the same thing as you. So what will make me stand out from the rest? Simply the fact that I want it more! Now my opponents can say they want it more all they want, but I have gone through hell to get to this point in my life. My opponents may have had tragic lives, abuse, murder etc but I have not just decided to become a fighter because I want to get back at society. I became a fighter because that is all I ever wanted to become and it is a simple as that. However I digress, before I return for Whiplash I have some unfinished business in Lancaster… “Listen to me sweetheart, I am on the street now.” I was leaning on a wall just at the beginning of my street. I was on my mobile talking to Rachel. It was good to hear her voice but the real reason I was calling was to avoid the inevitable. “That is good Adam, I am so proud of you.” “How are you feeling babe?” “A little sick but that is common. I got my first ultra-scan in a couple days, you are going to be back for that right?” “Oh without a doubt!” I said reassuring myself as much as Rachel. “Good, well babe I better let you go. Remember I love you and good luck!” “I love you too! Bye!” Reluctantly I hung up and placed the phone in the jacket I had put on to disguise my suit. I pushed off the wall and looked down at my old street. It hadn’t changed it looked exactly the same from when I had left it. The houses were the sort of houses you would see in a child’s movie, everything house was perfectly built. It sent a shudder down my spine, although the outside of the houses were perfect, there was at least one house on this street that’s inside was a long way from perfect. I tried to take a step forward but my body froze, I couldn’t move! I was going against my will, I wasn’t doing this for me, I was doing this for my family. “Come on Adam, you have got to do this.” I said to myself felling my heart pound against my chest. “Don’t let this control you! No fear remember? No fear! Take the risk!” I clenched my hand into a fist and took a deep breath and began to walk down the street. Each window I passed I took a look in and saw families playing games or huddled up sat in front of the television. The sun had been shining all day but now as I approached my old house, clouds began to gather and cover the sun. It was if the weather was in tune with my emotions. A few more houses down I reached the end of my old drive. At first I continued to stare down the street not willing to turn around and face my past. Again I clenched my hand into a fist and turned. There in front of me now stood my old house, not a thing had changed. I looked up to the right at my window, where I spent most of my childhood staring out at the world that would never except me. I took a step back onto the drive that I had driven off 9 years ago. I took another step drawing closer to the door, my heart continued to pound faster and faster. After a few more steps I was there, staring at my old wooden door. I lifted my arm and with a gulp knocked on the door. My first instinct was to quickly turn and run but there I stood as to my horror the door slowly opened. I closed my eyes and as I opened them to my surprise at the door stood a complete stranger. It was a woman of about 30 years of age with long brown hair and a child like face, she was wearing a flowing red dress. She looked at me surprisingly with a smile on her face. “Hello.” I said breaking the silence that had felt like forever. “Hi, Adam right?” My eyes widened in shock behind my mask, the woman obviously sensed this and beckoned me in. “No need to be so surprised we have been expecting you, come in.” Reluctantly I stepped back in the house. To my amazement again the hall way had changed completely. No longer was the walls covered in clean white paint and sterilised film, it was painted a warm red and parts were covered in hanging pictures of the woman and a man who I presumed was her husband. “I guess it has changed quite a lot since you were last here?” “Yes it has…I am sorry but how on earth do you know me? Who are you?” I exclaimed getting to the point. “Oh sorry, my name is Emma Blume, I bought this house a couple of years ago from your mother with my husband Matthew. The reason I know you is because your mother, Laura, told me all about you and she said that you may return one day.” “I see but why would she tell you all about me?” Emma quickly opened a draw from a side table in the hall and pulled out an envelope. “Because she wanted me to give you this.” She said as she handed me the envelope. I took the envelope, on it my name had been written. The envelope was sealed, which I quickly ripped open. I pulled out a folded piece of paper, as I unfolded it I already knew what it was. “I will just go into the kitchen, take your time.” Emma said as she left me alone. I looked at the letter my hands shaking as I read it… Dearest Adam, I write this 7 years to the day that you left this house. I hope and pray that one day you come back to this house and find this letter. Everyday I have wished for you to return home to me. I even expect you to come through that door right now as I write this letter but the realist in me says that you won’t. When you left I tried to track you down but every time I thought I had something I lost you again. After 4 years of searching I gave up, some how I knew you were safe and that you were happy and that made me happy. However I will not lie to you Adam, even after 7 years I am furious, there were words said in that last conversation that really hurt me. I still hear them when I go to sleep echoing around my mind. The point of this letter Adam is not to make me feel better or to make you feel better it is to let you know that I am still around. I will still be living in Lancaster and I have given that lovely woman Emma my contact number in case you ever do return. If it is not too late Adam then Emma will contact me and I will meet you at Lancaster Park near the pond. We have much to discuss… Yours lovingly, Mum I folded the letter back up and placed it in my jacket. I moved towards the kitchen already know what my decision was going to be. Emma was standing at the counter in the kitchen looking sombre, she looked up at me and I simply nodded my head. Instantly she reached for the phone. “Thank you.” I said to her as I turned around and exited the house. As I stepped back onto the street I sighed and walked off the street. I got in my car and was at Lancaster Park in 5 minutes. I settled myself down on the bench in front of the pond. The park was pretty quiet, not even the ducks on the pond were making a noise. I sat there for another 10 minutes before I heard a familiar voice from behind. “Hello Adam.” I quickly jumped up and turned around. There standing in front of me was my mother. The 9 years had not been kind to her, her hair was grey and she had wrinkles all over her face. I couldn’t help think that I had done that to her more than the passage of time. I just stood there not saying a word. It was if someone had removed my voice box. I simply gestured for her to sit down on the bench. She moved over slowly and sat down staring over the pond. I sat down. “So you finally came back.” My mum said sounding disappointed it had taken so long. “Yeah,” I said in an uncaring tone, which I didn’t mean to. “I thought it was time that we talked.” “You thought it was time? YOU!” My mum said becoming angry for a second then calming down. “That is rich Adam, 9 years no contact and you choose this moment to decide you want to talk.” “Yes mum. It has been 9 years, 9 years where I have been living my life and working hard to achieve my dream. 9 years where I never had the option of coming back home! I am sorry it has taken so long but if you do remember you told me never to return!” I said feeling myself become angry. “I didn’t mean it!” “Of course you meant it mum! You only regret saying it, that doesn’t make it any less meaningful! You never wanted me to become a fighter because you thought it was too dangerous! Well here I am mum, 9 years on and I am still standing!” “Yes but 9 years where every night I have been worried sick! Wondering if you were lying dead in some gutter in god knows what country! I bet you never thought about me once! I bet when you closed the door, you forgot all about me!” “What rubbish! I thought about you every day, wondering if you were okay! You’re my mother for gods sake, just because I hate you doesn’t mean I don’t care for you!” “So why did you never call? I am sure they had phones wherever you were!” “Jesus Christ! We are going around in circles mum! Remember you never wanted to speak to me again! And don’t say you didn’t mean it because you did! I don’t know why you even wrote that letter probably to make yourself feel better!” “No not at all!” “So why did you write it then?” “Because I missed you!” My mum’s last yell stopped me in my tracks. I realised we were now both standing screaming at each other. My mum took a deep breath. “Oh dear, this isn’t going too well is it?” “No.” I admitted. “Oh Adam, why did we get like this?” “I don’t know mum, I really don’t know.” “Was I not good to you?” “You were but you were too good. You smothered me mum, you would never let me try anything.” “I just wanted you too be safe.” “But you couldn’t always protect me. I felt like I couldn’t breathe, you knew how much I loved fighting.” “But it was so dangerous.” “And it still is! But I have become good at it and for 9 years I have stayed alive and I got to say mum I have never been so happy.” “That is good Adam.” Mum said as she placed a hand on my shoulder. “So do you understand why I did what I did?” “Yes I do, you could have done it better but I understand. However you have to realise I will never stop worrying about you, that is my job as your mother.” “I know mum.” I said as I grabbed her hand on my shoulder. “I know.” “So was there another reason you came over here to see me? Emma sounded like you had something else to tell me.” “Oh yeah! Your going to be a grandmother!” “What?!?!” And that was it. We sat there for hours talking about everything I had done and seen, talking about my marriage to Rachel. Talking about everything up to my match at Whiplash. I had got my mother back and finally she understood why I did what I did. Now my mind is clear, now my mind is ready… Whiplash here I come!
