Is It a Dream? An End Approaches....

26 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-26-2004 “The Old Wizard knew nothing about the talisman I am meant to seek. And now I find myself on the edge of a great ocean. An ocean where I thought there was to be desert. And how shalt I cross this body” I only hope that the answer comes?” The waves crash in on the rocky beach as Pendragon sits, sharpening his sword upon a flat stone. Daylight fades to dusk, and Pendragon gathers dry wood with which to build a small fire. He scrapes flint to steel and soon has a small blaze going. He lies back on the sand and looks at the sky as dusk deepens to the purple hue of nightfall, and finally the black of night. At last, he dreams? ***********FLASH**************** Bodies litter the battlefield. Knights, slain in battle, lie cooling in the sun, while their squires stand by weeping. In the distance, a huge army prepares yet another wave of attack, and my heart weeps. Such good men, wasted because of such an evil invader. With a look over mine shoulder, I see a huge castle sprawling in the distance, it’s battlements covered by archers. To my right and left stand the heavy cavalry, and behind me stands ready the pike men’Footmen have always been rewarded with disdain, but it is the infantry that wins wars, not the horse. Good for fear are the horse, but the dirty work always falls to the infantry. “Who’s thoughts are these” I feel like I am watching a dream through someone else’s eyes! This is truly an oddity of feeling. Who am I?” To my right, a voice??My Lord King’What are your orders?” Orders. Aye’The last of mine house shall be today. This army is too great. The numbers overwhelm. And it shall go down in the history books that Xavier Pendragon, high seat of his house, and King of the Land, shall let his kingdom go to the ruiness hands of Akumancha’Akumancha and his puppets, the Freeze? Stand ready for the next wave, men! Perhaps we will once more see the inside of yonder walls! FOR HONOR!!! The shot pulls back to show the two armies, as the Freeze advances. The faces of the knights of Pendragon are grim with the thought of their own impending deaths, but the king sits tall in his saddle as he watches the impending doom? With a cry, I lead my men forward’Forward into the valley of death. If only there were those who could remember. Perhaps a song would be sung of mine deeds long after these dark times have passed us by. As the bloodlust falls, things seem to slow down and take on a surreal vision. Here and there, swordsmen fall, but I pay them no mind. I am bent upon death, and it is death that I deal. Excalibur is a blur in mine hand, and men fall amidst piles of their own limbs; their own entrails. Warm blood steams in the blasted unnatural cold, and we press on. Foe after foe is felled, but where one is hacked, three rise to take their places. The odds are too great. It looks as if the end comes now’But what an end it shall be!!! GLORY!!! FOR HONOR!!! The few remaining men charge together, one last show of unity in the battle. Though they deal more death than is due them, soon, their horses are cut down, and the men as well? Without mine steed, I fight on foot. Against a hundred I fight; two hundred?.A thousand men or more thrust at me on this bloody field’This field where I see mine breath in the dead of summer. Each stroke of mine sword fells an enemy, and yet there number never lessens, it only seems to grow. Tirelessly I swing Excalibur, spurred on by both bloodlust and the clinging that mortal men have to tenuous life. I see the end as it comes, and finally the army becomes to much. I am overwhelmed. The hoard closes in on me, and soon, I feel swords pierce mine body. First at the thigh, then the shoulder’At last, through the heart I am stabbed, and I fall to the ground. As darkness descends, I look to the sky as the marching army passes, and I see a kindly face staring down at me with great sadness? “Merlin! Oh what a hand fate has chosen to deal me! And yet, I only hope mine death was a worthy on!” I say, and he smiles ruefully at me’I can feel my life force slipping from me, and a single tear falls from the wizards eye and lands gleaming upon my cheek. I see it all with a detachedness I have never known’I look for the light, yet there is no light. Only dark’Only the chill of forever night? You have done your best, my king. But the line must be preserved. Forebears of the past, I pray you! Give this great man the honor he deserves?.The rest his soul needs?.Forever shall Pendragon live. At last, Excalibur is taken from mine bloody grasp as I exhale mine breath for the last time upon this earth?.. *****************FLASH***************** With a start, Pendragon awakes’He runs his hand over his body, checking for the wounds that killed his father, Xavier. When he is satisfied that he is unharmed, he sits up and stares at the horizon as dawn begins to break. Pendragon, on the ocean. Pendragon, of all worlds and visions?. “And what does the dream signify” Anything at all? Nothing? I lived my father’s death. I saw things through his eyes in a battle I could not watch as a boy. And at the last, there was Merlin, and I felt the hope that coursed through my father at that moment. Merlin was like a father to mine father, and yet I have never wholly trusted him. I know not if “twas he that has instilled me with this power I have, this magnetism. Whether he put it there or no, the fact remains that it is. Things in the other world affect this world’That is a certainty. Yet do things here affect things there” Does even time move at the same speed? In my showdown with Freeze, will the outcome be reflected directly here? And what if I fail? I am a man among men, and yet I partner with a boy’Roswell’Against two men’Freeze; Jetsui. And a third in Akuma. The mirror of Lord Akumancha. And battle does draw nigh. Stolen has glory been from my hand, but not on the night when I meet mine enemies face to face. That night shall glory be unleashed upon the whelps who would try to hold the Lord King beneath the yoke of obscurity?” As the sun rises, a ship can be seen approaching in the distance. With many masts, it appears to be a galleon of archaic design. Still far out on the water, its flag is not able to be seen. From the east it comes, and the sun follows close behind it. Pendragon smiles, an eerie smile. Twisted between mirth and irony, it almost appears to be painful. “And is if by fate, it appears that my way has become clear. For ill or good, that ship shall be mine chariot over this water. Each step takes me to my destiny, and I have no fear. Is it that mine entire life is preordained” Do I even have a say? History will remember me by mine actions. If I am meant to be remembered, thus shall it be. But fate comes not to me’I go forth to meet it on mine own terms’Many roads there are to the map of a man, and many ends. But each end starts one new beginning, and the end to this short journey, fittingly enough, may come at High Noon. May the gods smile upon me, and protect the son of honor as they see fit?..” Spurred by this, Pendragon strides into the water. The waves engulf him and soon he swims with powerful strides into the deep blue ocean. A bee line he makes, straight for the large vessel? “Give me honor...Immortality’Or give me a death worthy of mine name. Let the name Pendragon be upon a thousand tongues or more. If this is mine fate, so be it?”
