The age of Blood #1

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Silence. Sitting in the cold desolate building that is Soma Corp’s main offices in New York, I ponder of the future. I look to what has become of the harsh reality that is my life….doomed to walk this world forever in an endless cycle of nothingness. I stand from my desk and look around the dark room. Contemplating to myself is this what I want out of my eternity….fame, fortune, the high life? Over four hundred years I was no more than a common slave, a fighter for others, one of the most viscous men ever to walk the face of the earth. Now look at me. A business man, a common débutante. I pick up one side of the desk and flip it over. The high priced computer as well as some very expensive office supplies smash into the ground. I kick the computer and help it shatter into a couple of pieces. After the computer smashes there is a knock at the door. I sigh and look at the door wondering who would dare knock on my door at this time of night. “Enter.” “Soma….what have you become?” I try to look at the figure at the door but even with my eyes I can not penetrate the darkness of the shadow looming in my doorframe. “I have become powerful. I have become one of the wealthiest men in history. I have become…..” “You have become nothing but a fake. You have lost your way. You have not truly become what you are destined to do. Your power is lacking young one….” “Young one? Who are you to call the likes of me young? I should tear you limb from limb for such an insult.” “Ignorant boy…. You have no concept of who I am do you? I am older than you can imagine. Older than you and even the ones who bore you. Tear me limb from limb? You think you have enough power to destroy the likes of me? You think your power can withstand hellfire?” His voice echoed in the large dark office and for the first time in a long time I felt an emotion long thought to have been destroyed in me…. Fear. I feared his words and feared him more than anything ever. His eyes through the darkness began to glow a very dark blue. He chuckled a bit and moved closer, but the closer he got to me the darker the room got. He reached out and grabbed me, his power so strong it dropped me to my knees. I tried to look up at him but couldn’t. I stood there kneeling as if bowing to him. “The times have begun Soma….you shall be tried and tested and when it is all said and done you will realize your worth. You shall take the blood of the innocent. You shall be once more the most feared man to walk the earth. But this can only be if you listen to me, be my student. What say you?” “I shall be your student and you shall be my teacher.” “Excellent, let the games begin.” The figure slowly walked back out the door and closed it behind him. The times are changing…..The age of blood has begun.
