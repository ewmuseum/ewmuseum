Boys will be boys.

3 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 3rd, 2004. The eyes of 'American Youth' Josh were locked on a drink as his bluish gray eyes stared down into the water sitting before him in a glass. The cigarette smoke that hung in the air stung his eyes, forcing them to water, yet he had no other choice but to suffer through the invisible cloud of cancer that haunted the bar. Josh was there against his own will because he's not one to frequent bars…or any other social function for that matter. If had his way he would probably be working out, training, or doing something that involved Wrestling. He had practically abandoned what small social life he had for his career, yet American Youth's longtime friend Ken Partridge Jr. had decided to remedy that. So he shoved Josh into his car and escorted him to this seedy strip club…sure it was a nice place…if you enjoy mingling amongst people but we all know that Josh does not. In fact Josh's life away from the ring, the gym and all other facets of the Wrestling world, primarily consists of writing poetry, playing the acoustic guitar and a nice healthy dose of self-loathing. Such is not the case on this lovely Dallas night. Sitting at a table in the both surviving members of the Elite look as if they have been cut out of a GQ magazine. Ken was sitting across from Josh wearing a Royal blue Banana republic button up shirt, a pair of black Versace slacks, and Chippewa boots. Sitting across from KPJ the only way to describe the style of American Youth was, 'punk meets prep'. With his shaggy black hair scattered about in messy locks, a silver ring was in his left eyebrow, and he bore a pained expression across his face. His black T-shirt bore the word 'iconoclast' across the chest in silver type. This T-shirt however was covered partially by a gray John Varvatos shirt that was completely unbuttoned leading down to a pair of tattered Ralph Lauren jeans and black Converse sneakers. Resting on a leather cord, a silver cross, a symbol of Josh's devolving faith hung about his neck. The night was still fairly young as a clock hanging on the white wall glowed the numbers '12:48' at both men. 'Come on…admit it, you're having a good time!' Ken said as he leaned across the table and playfully punched Josh on the arm. Glancing up at him Josh shrugged his shoulders before hesitantly nodding. 'I've had worse…' He mumbled as Ken laughed at Josh's attempt to deny that he wasn't at least somewhat enjoying tonight. 'So your enjoying it, eh?' Ken asked with a raised eyebrow. Josh shrugged yet again. 'It's not productive but it's alright.' Ken once again broke into laughter at the sight of his little buddy complaining about not getting something accomplished. 'Josh, the point of tonight isn't to get anything accomplished…it's to take a minute to relax, to have some fun. And pal…if you don't relax soon the pressure is going to get to you.' Ken warned Josh in a brotherly manner. 'The pressure has already gotten to me Ken.' Josh said rubbing his temple with his left hand almost as if a headache had suddenly stuck his cortex with a violent rage. Ken didn't respond verbally but rather looked at Josh with a raised eyebrow. Ken didn't need to do anything more than that; two men who were like brothers could often finish the others sentence or communicate with such body language. Ken's face clearly read 'do tell', and Josh obliged. 'It's just…I have everyone breathing down my neck.' Josh groaned with a scowl on his face. 'That's not what's bothering you though…' Ken stated, reading his best friend like an open book. 'No, no, it's not.' Josh admitted. 'Then just what is?' Ken inquired of the World Champion. 'Maverick Reno.' Josh slowly confessed. 'Mav? Why does he bother you?' Ken Partridge Jr. asked with a slightly confused look on his face. 'He did something that no one else has done since I became World Champion and only one other person has done since my return…he put my shoulders on the canvas, the man beat me Ken.' Josh groaned despondently in a worried tone. 'Are you kidding me?' Ken gasped. 'Maverick Reno couldn't beat you fairly, he had to resort to pulling a foreign object out just so he could hang with you.' 'Maybe so…but it's not like it's something we haven't done countless times, Ken.' Josh pointed out, recollecting the numerous times they had cheated their way to a victory. 'Right you are…' Ken hesitantly admitted. 'Plus, Maverick Reno is a legend…the guy is a former World Champion, and I've done nothing to offend him…the only reason he has for chasing after me is for my World title.' Josh gulped, legitimately worried for the first time in his young career. But of course with a man like Maverick Reno hunting your head, who wouldn't be worried? 'Come on Josh…you’re the Champion for a reason, it's not as if that title fell into your lap accidentally.' Ken said firmly trying to bolster the spirits of his partner in crime. 'I understand that, but really…is there anyone who can beat Maverick Reno?' Josh asked with a worried expression on his handsome features. 'Zero did…and then I beat Zero, and the last time I checked…you beat Me.' Ken said, flashing his million-dollar smile and mustering a slight chuckle from Josh. Sure, it hurt Ken to admit that Josh had indeed defeated him…it was nothing personal but rather about pride, and with an ego the size of Ken's it was hard to admit defeat, yet when his brother was in low spirits…it must be done. 'Maybe your right.' Josh said with a slight sigh. 'Perhaps I'm blowing this out of proportion.' 'Of course you are, you’re the World Champion, you can handle Reno.' Ken told Josh attempting to provoke him to anger against Maverick Reno. It was rather odd…Ken knew exactly what to tell Josh to throw fuel on the fire, and that was good because often times Ken was the only one Josh would listen too. Looking up at Ken, Josh couldn't help but smile, spurred on by the confidence KPJ had displayed in 'American Youth' Josh. 'We can't keep our minds occupied with Reno or Zero anyway.' Josh thoughtfully mumbled. 'Of course not. There are women to be chased!' Ken exclaimed while using his eyes to undress the cocktail waitress that walked by their table. 'Actually I was thinking more along the lines of our tag match this week.' Josh said, also taking a nice look at the passing waitress. 'Right…that.' Ken nodded still soaking up the beauty of the waitress with his baby blue eyes. 'We've got Brick and McNasty…that wont be an easy task.' Josh warned as he recalled his title defenses against each man and just how close he came to losing both rounds. 'It never is.' Ken nonchalantly shrugged, seemingly not worried in the least, yet that's what made these two friends so different. While things didn't bother Ken, everything seemed to eat Josh up from the inside. Losing was a cancer for him because he would doubt himself for months, he would question his abilities and self-loathe to the point of suicide. Perhaps one of the reasons Ken is so supportive is the fact that 'The Rome incident' still haunts his memory. 'Brick is a former World Champion, and I had to use a pull of the tights to beat him. He's big…he's a big guy, I can't throw him around.' Josh said, stroking his chin in thought as he plotted his strategy. 'Josh…' Ken started with a big grin on his face. 'You can hardly throw anyone around!' Ken laughed as Josh smiled in return. It was true…with a body of two hundred and one pounds and two inches south of the six-foot mark Josh wasn't known for being a power Wrestler. 'Touch?.' Josh said with a playful salute. 'But what I'm saying is…he's hard to take off of his feet.' 'The last time I checked…he had knees.' Ken said with a smile obviously confident in his technical prowess and the ability to take the legs out from under Brick. 'I was thinking the same thing.' Josh said with a nod. 'And McNasty?' Ken questioned. Being the in-ring veteran, Ken was often the one to advise Josh on what strategy to employ for his own individual matches or even for their tag matches. Yet Ken had limited experience against McNasty whereas Josh had taken him on man to man with the World title hanging in the balance. 'He's not as big as Brick…but he's not as slow either…or as dumb.' Josh warned. 'When he's sober.' Ken said with a chuckle. Josh simply shrugged…McNasty had nearly taken his World title so he had no reason to put the Irishman down. 'Well Ken…you do recall the move I used to defeat him don't you?' Josh asked forcing Ken to close his eyes in thought. 'A superkick!' Ken blurted out as he suddenly remembered. 'That's right…the Sweet Ken Music.' Josh said with a grin, proud of gaining victory over McNasty with Ken's signature move. 'That was impressive.' Ken admitted with a smile. 'Now I just wonder what strategy Brick and McNasty will employ…' Josh mused lost in deep thought. 'Josh…' Ken interrupted. 'Shut up!' Ken blurted out with a laugh. 'We came here for a night off…to relax, not to worry about Wrestling.' Ken said still chuckling. Josh smiled slightly before taking a drink of his water. 'Your right, your right.' Josh admitted. 'Let's just have a good time…or as good of a time as you can have in this lyceum of immaturity.' Josh grumbled, and as soon as the words had split his lips it was evident that he had already shoved Wrestling to the back of his mind. 'Yeah, well…' Ken began before the voice of the house announcer spoke loudly over the microphone, cutting Ken off. 'Gentlemen please welcome…Lollipop!' He exclaimed excitedly as 'Sex and Candy' by Marcy's playground hit over the speakers. What greeted the eyes of Josh and Ken was a sight rarely seen this side of heaven. Stepping onto the stage in a pair of go-go boots and a classic Catholic Schoolgirl outfit consisting of a white long sleeved shirt and a short flannel dress she wore a pair of black rimmed glasses. Her hair, which was, pulled back in pigtails showed where she had earned her name, as it was naturally blonde yet streaked pink and baby blue. Raising his eyebrows Josh grinned broadly. 'Now…that is one lollipop I'd lick the red off of!' Josh exclaimed with an excited tone. Ken's response was a simple smile…maybe his little buddy was going to be all right after all.
