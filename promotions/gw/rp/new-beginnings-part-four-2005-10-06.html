New Beginnings (Part Four)

6 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.06.05 'I don't know,' exasperated a timid Josh. He was looking at himself in a full-length mirror at Ken's tailor's shop. Josh wasn't thrilled at the sight of himself in a Versace made tuxedo, but Ken thought that the tailor-made arrangement looked great on his black, blue, and silver headed friend. 'What are you talking about?' question Ken. 'You look like a million bucks, Josher.' 'Ken,' began Josh in retort, 'I agreed to be your best-man, not to wear a tux.' Ken laughed a bit under his breath. Unlike Josh, Ken looked completely at home in his tuxedo. The jubilation on his face was ever present as he stood in material that looked asthough it were made for him. 'I hope to hell that you didn't think you were gonna' be standing beside me in a pair of tattered jeans, a t-shirt with a tux printed on it, and those ratty old Chuck Taylor's,' chimed KPJ. 'Ooh,' began Josh with an enthusiastic look on his face, 'that's be great, Ken. Let's do that, it'll be fun.' 'You're serious, aren't you?' he asked with only a hint of surprise in his voice. A stunt like that is definitely not something that Ken would put past Josh to pull off. 'Why not?' 'Because it's my wedding,' laughed Ken. 'Josh, you'll have it on for a couple of hours, tops. Long enough for the ceremony and for pictures. Then you can rip the damn thing off.' Josh looked back at himself in the mirror and turned to look back at Ken. 'I guess you could afford to rip it off,' he joked. 'Huh?' 'Come on, Ken. I saw your contract the other day when you were going over it for renegotiation. Why didn't you ever tell me you were pulling that kind of cash?' 'How did you think I afforded everything, Josh?' asked Ken in a bit of a defensive tone. 'I just know that's a lot of bread, Amigo. I don't think Brock was even getting paid that kind of lettuce when he was there. I know I sure as hell wasn't.' Ken just stood slack-jawed before giving a bit of a chuckle under his breath and turning his face to a half-smile. 'This is a joke, right?' Josh simply glared at Ken through the mirror. 'I can't believe this,' began Ken with a mixture of hurt and anger in his tone. 'My best-friend... my best-MAN is giving me grief about how much money I make.' Josh opened his mouth as if to say something, but shut it quickly as he thought wiser of it. Ken never noticed, he was too busy shaking his head and pacing now. 'I didn't negotiate that contract, Josh, my lawyers did. As far as the sum goes on my yearly wage, that's the exact dollar ammount that McCoy offered over the phone. I never ever asked for any set dollar ammount. I just wanted to wrestle again... with you, and Gunner, and Brock. I can't help that I got paid damn well to do it.' Josh couldn't stand it any more. He turned quickly facing Ken with a smile on his face before his mouth opened in laughter. 'Dude,' he started, 'I was just joking with you. I don't care how much money you make; you know that. Besides, no matter how much money you havem, you can never call yourself the longest reigning Global Champion of all time.' Ken simply stared at Josh like an orgutain would a NASA SuperComputer. 'You son of a bitch,' Ken said, finally laughing along with Josh. 'What in the hell made you think that would be funny?' Josh simply shrugged and made a sour face. 'You know me and my weird sense of humor.' 'Yeah, well,' said Ken before coming to a pause. 'You can boast the longest title reign all you want, Josher. Come The Night of Champions, I'll come one-step closer to something that you never acheived.' Ken stuck his tongue out at Josh mischeiviously and strode over to check out a new pair of gorgeaous platinum cuff-links. 'What?' asked Josh, 'the Grand Slam? You can have it. Who wants that much baggage anyways?' 'Jealous?' questioned Ken as he looked up from the links and made eye contact with Josh. 'Maybe a little.' The two shared a quiet laugh as Ken walked over to his best friend and smiled. 'You look great.' 'Thanks,' Youth responded, 'but you're gonna' have to do some serious bribing to get me to wear this thing.' Ken laughed a little under his breath as a light-hearted smile crossed his face. His eyes were telling a different story, however. In them, Josh could see that there was something more on his mind. Before he could even question Ken about pre-wedding jitters, KPJ had already beckoned over his tailor and whispered something in his ear, sending him away before turning his attention back to Josh. 'Josh,' he began, 'you're the best-friend a guy could ever hope to have. You're somebody that, well, that's more than a brother to me. I could never thank you for everything you've given me and everything we've shared and nothing I could do, say, or offer could ever extend my gratefulness. But, there is something I want you to have.' It was at this time that the tailor returned with a briefcase in hand that Ken had in the car on the way. Josh's curiosity had been somewhat peaked, but seeing as Ken was a businessman on so many fronts and levels, thought little of it. KPJ spun the combinations into three sevens on either side and popped the case open. Josh could not yet tell what was lying in wait, but it positively shone upon the face of Partridge. 'There are only two fo these in existance,' Ken continued. 'Mine's the only waist they've ever garnished. It would have been my honor to have dropped it to you years ago.' With that quick and defining statement, Ken handed Josh one of the most immaculate Championship belts that he'd seen in his long career. The pale gold of it shone gorgeaously in the light as the aroma of the leather strap sought it's way to his nose. The belt was heavy, as well, weighing around twenty or so pounds. 'I can't take this, Ken,' Josh protested. 'This is the old PHWF Grappling God Championship. It means so much to you.' Ken smiled and pushed the title back into Josh's chest as Josh attempted to hand it back to him. 'I've still got the original, but you're friendship means more to me than any title I could ever claim. Please,' Ken begged,' I want you to have it.' Josh looked down at the strap and then back up at Ken before placing it back in the briefcase and embracing his commerade in arms. Tears began to fill both of their eyes as the gesture of Ken was more than moving. 'There's something else,' Ken broke as they pulled away. 'I want you to be the first to know. I mean, Gun doesn't even know, yet. But, I've been thinking a lot about my future, lately. I guess it's to be expected. I want to start a family with Gunner. You know, the whole nine yeards... three kids, a dog, and family bar-b-ques. Wrestling just doesn't fit into it anymore.' Josh's jaw dropped. 'Are you saying what I think you're saying, Ken?' 'Probably,' he answered. 'I'm not one hundred percent sure of retiring, but I can't keep wrestling forever. My body can't keep taking it,' he laughed. 'I just wanted to let you know that you've been my greatest opponent and rival, my greatest teammate and partner, my greatest working experience, and my greatest friend.' Tears began to fill Ken's eyes again. 'Thank you, Josh. Thank you for everything.' The two shook hands and quickly pulled into a hug. It was amazing how real life was finally beginning to take over Ken's perception. For so long he'd lived, eaten, slept, drank, and bled the business. Now, he was a fan again... a fan that was getting to live his dream. A dream that would never die.
