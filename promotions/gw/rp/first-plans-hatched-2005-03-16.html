First Plans Hatched

16 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/16/2005 Coffee’I need Coffee. Jake made his way to the kitchen and turned the pot on to brew. The change to the timer was necessary. Besides, he might not get any sleep this evening. His brother’brothers’whatever they became started off like little kids on all things. A vision of right and wrong they would pursue. He just didn’t understand how he could have changed that much in such a short time period. The beast he was to become was frightening. There had been so much rage and hatred locked with love and honor’sacrificed to save a life. Then reborn, and then reborn again. Visions of fire danced in his head. Nothing had been settled. Maybe he was deluding himself. Perhaps things would never be settled. He would not allow himself to think that way though. He had to protect the future. Fire’Hospitals’Pain’Sorrow? The feeling of those emotions and memories burned through his mind. Much was left. The coffee was finished. He poured a cup over two ice cubes, waiting for it to cool enough so he could gulp it down. He needed the caffeine, fake energy fueling his quest for truth. . . . . . . . . . . . The drive down to Austin was something Xavier always liked. Austin was a great party town thanks to many reasons. The state capital always drew powerful people who liked to flaunt their wealth and power. More fuel was always added to the fire thanks to the sixty thousand college students inhabiting the city going to the local community colleges or UT. Xavier wasn’t sure what they would find in Austin. He was going to stop in at the furnished apartment that had been rented by the small corporation Hargis had established. Xavier would be meeting with two other men taking residence in the three bedroom apartment. Xavier had spent several years in Austin wrestling for a small startup when he was only nineteen years old. Xavier and his brother had both worked as a tag team while living in a small apartment on the wrong side of town. The problems they had faced didn’t matter to them because they were doing what they loved. The experiences they had gained in the Austin Wrestling Association had taught them more than all their training combined. The veteran of the organization was Michael Borne, and he taught them how to string all the moves together inside the ring to tell a story. Borne had never made it as a wrestler, but he had discovered along the way he was an excellent teacher of the art. He taught the moves, the storytelling, the character development, the ring psychology and more than anything else he taught everyone respect for the business. Xavier had been taught the art of tag team wrestling for the first time in the AWA. He learned how to sale teamwork, deviousness, double-teaming and perhaps the most important thing he ever learned was how to develop a story line that drew in the fans. The Black Brothers were doing a great job in the AWA. After the first year of steady work they had captured the AWA Tag Team Championship from the reigning champions. The promoter had a great idea to further develop story lines and give each brother a chance to test themselves as singles wrestlers. Xavier and his brother Jason were being focused on more and more. They were both entered into a tournament to crown a new AWA Television Champion which seemed comical to them both since they were never on television. Xavier ended up beating his brother in a long, drawn out battle by nailing him with a big Russian Suplex after his brother missed a Lariat. The Russian Suplex would become his trademark move, but the defeat was not something his brother had enjoyed at all. The story line continued developing as Xavier became both the Television Champion and one half of the Tag Team Champions. Eventually, the jealousy led Jason to turn on Xavier beginning a feud over the Television Championship. Jason and Xavier met in a steel cage match that was a big hit locally. The AWA combined the feud between the brothers with the visit of several wrestling legends including Terry Funk to sale out one of the larger arenas in the area. The AWA was going to become a decent federation in the state. Jason and Xavier didn’t disappoint in their match. Jason pulled out everything he could to defeat his brother, but Xavier was just too strong to be defeated. Xavier kept coming back time and time again. Finally, Xavier picked Jason up onto his shoulders atop the ropes and dropped him back onto the mat with a top rope Samoan Drop. Xavier followed the move up with a Moonsault off the top of the cage. Xavier remembered the pain that had been shooting through his body as he rolled around on the mat after nailing his brother with the high-risk move. Xavier’s vision wasn’t just blurred, the shooting pains had blacked it out for a few seconds and he tried to recover. John Mercer had slipped out from under the ring then. Mean John Mercer was one of the league’s bad guys, and he took the chair in his hands and slammed it over Xavier’s head. The assist led to a huge Power Bomb off the top rope, and Jason joined forces with Mean John Mercer, bringing the AWA Television Title with him. The night had been a huge success, and both Jason and John had assisted the AWA World Champion Big Jake Reed in defeating Terry Funk. The die was cast with the evil triad running roughshod over the league. While all this had been going one Jason and Xavier were going separate ways in real life too. They had begun running in different circles, which led to Jason getting an apartment of his own. Xavier remembered those days fondly. Now he was coming to Austin to continue his quest. The drive down was met with the angry storms of nature. The dark clouds and cruel sky shot down bright arcs of electrical hell as the lightning illuminated the stinging rain slamming into the world. The drive was simple and plain as always. Xavier finally arrived in the capital around ten that evening, joining the rush of Friday night party seekers attacking the night despite the weather. Xavier drove past the capital building, enjoying the brief glimpse of majesty in the massive domed structure that signaled the world that government was here. Once the signal alone had given hope to the world, but now the signal seemed warped to say enter here all yea who wish to suckle the fat of the land. New images would have to be erected in the world to show that times had changed. These images would be darker, crueler than the politically correct monuments of the past. The new words were written in blood of the martyrs who made the sacrifices. Xavier proceeded past the office buildings and state buildings to the downtown party area where the residence was going to be located. He followed 8 Street down to the address he had written down then rechecked the address. The block had been converted from a warehouse to six large town houses. Xavier parked in front of the town house next to the garage entrance despite the sign informing the world parking was prohibited. He stepped out into the rain and walked up to the red door. Xavier knocked loudly on the door to ensure that whoever might be inside would hear him. The door was pulled open a few seconds later with the security chain still attached. Hard brown eyes looked out at him, “Yes, can I help you?” “My name is Xavier Black. I’m looking for Sean King.” Xavier looked right back into the hard eyes with a hard stare of his own. Xavier’s eyes communicated more than the eyes looking at him. There was more emotion, more pain and more confidence than the brown eyes could ever muster. The door was close so the security chain could be removed. The red door came open all the way and Xavier saw the man standing in the doorway now. He was six foot tall and a very solid wall of two hundred and thirty pounds of muscle. The man offered his hand to Xavier, “I’m Sean. Good to finally have you here Xavier. I’ll open the garage so you can pull in.” Xavier looked over at the garage door, seeing it would be tall enough for his Excursion. “I’ve got a big SUV, will the garage be big enough?” Sean laughed, “Plenty big, trust me.” Xavier nodded and went back to his vehicle. Xavier checked the street, and when it was clear he pulled out into the street. Xavier backed into the open garage as Sean watched the street. Xavier was able to back straight in, and when all the way inside he saw there was still plenty of room before he would reach the back wall lined with shelves. Sean stepped inside the garage and hit the button to close the door. Sean walked over to the Excursion as Xavier stepped out, “Like I said, plenty of room.” Xavier saw the garage was huge. There was enough room for at least three of his vehicles next to the gym on the far end of the garage. Xavier could see the garage was outfitted like a professional garage with enough tools to do anything needed. There were also six motorcycles parked between the gym and the Excursion. Xavier looked around, “This place is packed, and I’m just in the garage. What is the rest of the house like?” “Hargis doesn’t disappoint at all. This is his retirement home though, so I shouldn’t be surprised.” Sean said as he opened the back of the vehicle. Xavier thought he had misheard the last part, “This is what?” “Hargis” retirement home.” Sean explained. “When his wife died he bought this little place down here to retire to. It is close to the college and he got to spend time with his sons when they were in college.” “I thought that?” Xavier shook it off, “Never mind. Let’s see what upstairs looks like so I can get a drink and relax.” Sean led Xavier up the first set of stair and pointed at the floor, “Second floor has the entertainment. Big screen television, entertainment center, bar, pool table, darts and a small kitchen. This is where we hang out on the down time.” The second floor reminded Xavier of a bar. The floors were hardwood with dark carpeting in several areas. There was modern artwork spread throughout the floor to showcase the room and added accents. Xavier continued following Sean up to the third floor. This floor was much brighter with white walls, beige carpeting and much brighter artwork. “This is the place we spend most of our time. There are two offices, a meeting room, a formal dining room and the big kitchen.” Xavier was impressed without having even seen the floor except this small glimpse. Hargis had obviously been hiding some of his life. “Come on and we’ll get you settled in your room.” Sean led Xavier up the last set of stairs. “Per Bulldog’s instructions we’ve left the master bedroom open for you. You have a private bathroom just like Bertrand and I.” Sean led Xavier into the wide hallway on the top floor. Sean walked straight ahead to the black double doors. Sean opened them and led Xavier inside. The room was huge with a four poster king size bed on the far wall, a sitting area next to a fireplace on the right and two doors on the left. “Over there is your closet and your bathroom. Bertrand and I are on the next floor working on some plans for one of the drug labs we found. Get freshened up and come on down.” Sean said before leaving. Xavier walked over to the first door with both his suitcases and opened it. Inside was a huge walk in closet. The left and right had two poles for hanging clothes running down each wall while the wall straight ahead only had one pole set high up for any longer items. Xavier turned around to see shelves and shoe racks along with a chest of drawers set in the wall. The entire closet was empty and bare except for the wooden hangers on the poles. Xavier quickly unpacked his clothes, hanging them up or putting them in the dresser as needed. Xavier pulled off his tee shirt and threw it in the laundry hamper next to the chest of drawers. Xavier peeled off his hoes for the shoe rack and the dirty socks followed his shirt into the hamper. Xavier picked up a fresh white tee shirt and then took his toiletry kit with him to the bathroom. Xavier was blown away by what he saw in the bathroom. The marble floor was white and pure as it led to a huge Jacuzzi style bathtub. The white marble should have been cold, but he found it quite warm. The two sinks and toilet were done in twenties style which Xavier liked. The entire bathroom was far too expensive. Xavier knew Hargis had received a large life insurance check and a large settlement check when his wife was killed by a police car on a high speed chase, but he didn’t think Bulldog Hargis would spend it on things such as this. Xavier walked over to the white sink and used a bar of Ivory soap to clean the oil from his head and face. Xavier dried off with one the plush towels before putting the white tee shirt on over his jeans. Xavier slipped on a pair of black house slippers from Hush Puppies that looked like corduroy wrapped shoes. Xavier left the posh pad to find his two new companions on the third floor. The third floor was very serene, quiet all around. Xavier was reminded more of the nice office buildings downtown than he was of a home on this floor. He headed down the hallway looking to his right and left as he went. The first window on the left showed him a large office with dark wood furniture. The next office on the right showed another office with black furniture. At the third window Xavier saw a large room filled with cherry red wood furniture dominated by the large conference table running down the center of the room. Sean was in the room with another man Xavier assumed was Bertrand. Xavier walked in to see them working on a projection on the screen from a laptop. Xavier recognized the picture as architectural plans, “Hello gentlemen.” Sean and his companion looked up at Xavier. “Xavier, glad you could join us. Let me introduce you to Mark Bertrand. He’s the last musketeer so to speak.” Mark stood up and approached Xavier. Mark was shorter than Sean, probably five feet nine or ten inches tall, but he weighed the same as Sean if not more. His thick frame showed off his commitment to bodybuilding that had rewarded him with muscles that could be even stronger than Xavier’s own. Xavier and Mark shook hands, and Xavier was impressed by the firm grip that confirmed he was stronger than Xavier, “Good to meet you Xavier. I hear this entire idea was yours to start.” “An idea born of years of loss and frustration.” Xavier said back with a small grin, “Good to know there are others who are willing to help out. What building are you looking at?” Xavier walked over to the projection. The architectural plans showed the second level of a two level building. The structure looked industrial in nature judging by the concrete beams and the emptiness. “This is the architectural plan we were able to find at city hall for a building in the industrial district. It’s located near several chemical factories, and we believe it is one of the drug labs being used by Javier to manufacture and process his drugs. We know Javier does a lot of business in marijuana, heroin and for the college crowd drugs like ecstasy and methamphetamines. This lab is probably responsible for most of the ecstasy and methamphetamines being brought into UT.” Sean explained. Bertrand pointed at the picture, “This is actually the second sublevel of the building. The building serves as a warehouse mainly. Perfect cover because boxes move in and out all day, and they sometimes hold chemical shipments for the other companies in the area.” Sean leaned back, “The two sub levels we believe are split. Sublevel One handles the production of the methamphetamines and ecstasy while Sublevel Two is a greenhouse for growing the marijuana. Distribution is handled easily enough because shipments go in and out all the time.” Bertrand took over again, “We know Javier has some routes he uses to send the drugs out. We suspect him of buying the protection to continue running his operations here in Austin. The college kids buy his product, he sales to the kids in high school, middle school and even grammar school.” Bertrand’s venom could be heard in his words, “He imports the heroin in from Laredo along with cocaine. He has several networks set-up, but we know for fact he ahs burned two of them when any part of the operation was compromised. Javier knows that he will last longer and make more money if he doesn’t lock himself into one network.” “He’s running into resistance from another drug ring that is dealing in marijuana, ecstasy and methamphetamines, but they don’t seem to be dealing heroin or cocaine. That hasn’t slowed them down, and one of the funny things is that the new crew has been very successful by partnering with key distributors.” Sean looked over at Xavier as he hit a button on the laptop, “The new crew is very cruel though.” A picture of a young man came up on the screen. The grainy, black and white photo looked like it had been scanned out of a newspaper. Sean was looking at the young man with a sad face, “This is Roger Denton. He was a graduate student in the law program, but last year he was killed in a most despicable manner. The papers kept most of the details secret, but Roger was tortured before being killed.” “Roger was skimming some of the profits from his drug trafficking, and when his business partners found out they were not pleased. The new crew is from the Russian Mafia. Roger has a younger sister Kristy who’s a Sophomore. From the accounts we have pieced together and trust what happened is dastardly to say the least.” Bertrand took over as pictures from the crime scene were shown, “They cut both of Roger’s Achilles” tendons to start. He couldn’t move after that, and the cuts on his body are superficial made with razors. The pain was made worse by the urine all over his body.” “Roger was forced to watch as his sister was gang raped in front of him. The coroner’s report shows she penetrated by men, objects and finally a knife in every orifice. This was not discovered until later because they took the sister’s body with them. The sister was found almost two months later.” “Roger was ultimately killed when he was stabbed in the abdomen then hog tied. He struggled to get free as he slowly bled to death. The message in the drug community was clear. If you deal with the Russians, don’t play with the numbers. Since this incident, they have increased their market share and there has been no more violence against the dealers.' Xavier snorted, “Of course not, why try to steal when you make plenty of money from what they give you and you know the consequences.” “Exactly.” Bertrand said with great disappointment in his voice, “The young people they use to deal at the college seem too concerned with the money to worry about the problems they have and are causing.” Xavier sat down in one of the leather chairs, “So the Russians are moving in on the Mexican Cartels and their delivery networks. What do we know about the Russians?” Sean turned to face Xavier, “Not much at all, but don’t lose focus. We can’t try to deal with both sides. We’ve got a lot of intelligence on the Mexican Cartel, and Bulldog is coming in next week to give us all the rest of the information on the Laredo connection. First we finish off the Cartel, then we can explore other opportunities.” “A guy can dream can’t he?” Xavier raised his eyebrows over and over jokingly. They all shared a laugh. Sean clapped, “Of course, but the first dream can be realized quite soon. Are you hungry?” “Starved to be honest.” Xavier could feel his stomach seconding his statement. “Well, Bertrand is an excellent cook. We made a deal that he does all the cooking and I clean up the mess he makes.” Sean explained quickly. “Let me get you a plate of food while Sean goes over all the work we’ve done so far. We have some good plans in the works right now. You’re going to be the last player we need to get things done.” Bertrand smiled as he left the room. Sean stood up and walked around to the whiteboard. Sean began going through the information, “Okay, Javier is basically untouchable, but his operations are vulnerable. Beside the location in the industrial district he has several locations through the city where the cocaine and heroin is brought in. We’ve identified two of the locations, but we think there could be as many as four others.” “The two we identified are located within legitimate businesses. One is part of a mom and pop grocery store that has been in the community for over thirty years. The family that runs the business is related to Javier through blood so they won’t turn on him. The other location is a restaurant downtown that is next to an empty building he also owns.” Sean pointed out the locations on the map before moving on, “These two places are on the campus at UT. One is a dormitory that is coed, and the next is one of the fraternity houses. Javier’s point man is a young man who graduated with a Masters Degree in Business Administration from UT ten years ago.” “Ely Kutcher worked his way through college dealing for Javier. He expressed interest in helping Javier enhance his business, and Javier saw the need for growth. Together they modernized and enhanced the operation to the point it became the model adopted by the Cartel throughout the state.” Sean hit a button on the computer that brought up Ely’s picture, “Ely has of course moved on to the more administrative side, and Vince Temon has taken over as the street man for the Cartel. Vince uses several lieutenants, but he likes to be personally involved in the business.” Another button brought up another picture on the screen. The triple picture showed Vince with two other men on the screen inside a restaurant. “These are Vince’s two campus connections. Michael Starkos and Carter Hayes are both Juniors at UT. Michael is studying to be a lawyer, and that means he has a long time left in the school. Carter is studying to be a Doctor, and he also has plenty of time left. They are members of one of the fraternities, so their connections in the school are impressive to say the least.” “Michael is the smarter, more devious player. He handles the heroin, cocaine and methamphetamines. He also does as much ecstasy as Carter does even though Carter is supposed to be the point man for the marijuana and ecstasy. Carter uses the parties and friends he makes through the frat to sale, but he’s not overly ambitious.” Sean moved to a single photo of Michael Starkos. “Michael is the one we feel is the biggest threat.” Sean sipped his scotch and soda. “Michael seems to have mapped out his career plans already. He’s going to become a lawyer so he can continue to work for the Cartel. Through a bug we placed in his room we found out a lot of what he thinks.” Sean sat back down and looked at Xavier laughing, “The guy is dumb enough to speak about taking out Javier to his roommate. We could send a copy of that tape to Javier, but that would just get one person killed. The landscape wouldn’t be changed at all. Michael wants to become the Cartel’s next hotshot lawyer, and his experience is going to get him that job easily.” “We can disrupt some traffic, probably take out some major dealers, but we were waiting on you to get some more ideas of where you want to go with this entire operation. The landscape is open, and the sooner the better.” Xavier smiled at Sean, “You two guys have done a great job here. Let me review all the information you have put together and I’ll have some ideas. The entire operation here has to fit into a larger overall operation. Dallas gave us a taste, but there was no real action taken on our part and we netted four of the enforcers out of luck. If we intend to cause harm I think we’ll be able to in a way never thought of by strategists worried about law enforcement and their operational restraints.” Sean rolled back to the red cherry filing cabinet. He opened the drawer and pulled out several thick files. “We’ve amassed a lot of information through our surveillance, the files we got through the Freedom of Information Act and from Bulldog’s contacts. It may take you some time to go through it all.” “Time I have, but patience I don’t. I’ll go through this information as quickly as possible, and soon enough we’ll have some real work to do.” Xavier assured Sean. Bertrand returned with a plate filled with food, “Here you are. I made green beans, gouda infused mashed potatoes and chicken cordon blue. I hope you like it.” Xavier looked down at the food with a hearty grin, “This looks amazing! Where did you learn to cook?” “I am an only child, so my mother taught me everything she could as did my father.” Bertrand laughed, “I feel I’m very well rounded thanks to them.” “We’ll leave you alone for awhile to review all the information. We also have everything soft copy in the laptop if you prefer.” Sean explained as Xavier opened up the first file. He scooped up a forkful of mashed potatoes as he read. Sean and Bertrand exchanged a quick look, and realized Xavier was focused on the task as much as they were. Xavier had gone through thousands of documents and reports in the ten hours he had been up. There was an exceptional amount of information to go through, but he had enjoyed seeing everything they had on the drug trade in Austin. Xavier had been intrigued by several of the players in the trade in and around Austin, Texas. Michael Starkos seemed to be the key as far as Xavier was concerned. He was someone positioning himself to be one of the next major players in the game, and his education was proving to be a well-rounded one. He had street level smarts from years of dealing and his difficult childhood in Oak Cliff, he was a quick study in school on the Dean’s List and he was becoming more capable thanks to self defense training. Xavier had read some opinion notes by Sean and Bertrand that indicated he was going to be the next great Cartel lawyer, but Xavier thought he saw another level to the young man’s viciousness. Michael Starkos had seen every layer of the business, and his ambitions would go beyond being a simple lawyer. Michael wanted to be the next great kingpin for the Untied States. He wanted to be the man in charge living a life he would have only dreamed possible as a child. Xavier looked at the notes and history and saw a calculating man who was not timid about using violence when he felt it might be necessary. There were several noted incidents of Michael handing out a beating to customers who had not paid their debts to him in a timely manner. Michael also seemed to be branching out in his influence. Xavier found it extremely enlightening that Michael had invested a large portion of the money he had made to purchase a controlling interest in a struggling strip club in Austin. Michael had begun using the strip club as a distribution point for some of his drug dealing which had gained even more customers. Javier had bought out the club from Michael at a nice profit for the young man because he wanted his focus to be on campus. The story seemed to e a footnote, but to Xavier that explained the type of man being dealt with. Michael was the rookie in management training, so making sure he was damaged by the operation would have farther reaching effects. Some of the other players were of equal interest to Xavier. Natasha Warren was a young woman noted in the files as well. She was one of Michael’s business partners in the entire operation. Natasha had begun an escort service that offered young men with enough money full service from beautiful young coeds. Xavier also assumed rightly that she was distributing drugs to customers who might want a little something extra along with the women. Natasha’s operation had been successful enough for her to expand to general trade in the city with the blessing and protection of the Cartel. Xavier had also reviewed the security arrangements for Javier Garcia. The man wasn’t paranoid about security, he brought the idea of paranoia to an entirely new level. Javier used contracted security firms to provide rotating security in addition to his own personal guards. He would not be someone they could get close to because his paranoia also ran to those he met with and spoke to. Xavier also liked something about the plant in the Industrial Sector of town. The plant was one of at most eight major centers, and Xavier thought it was the likely supplier of the lion’s share of the Cartel’s methamphetamines, ecstasy and marijuana. Xavier made a note on his open spiral notebook for the number one target to be the plant. Xavier also wanted to remove the future threat he saw in the continuing development of Michael Starkos. He needed to remove all of these pieces to make a real mark, and if there were some extra pieces that could be hurt then all the better. “Bulldog” Hargis was currently following down some information regarding the ultimate target for the operations. Depending on what was developed about Carlos Delgado they would adjust the operational targets as needed. Carlos Delgado was the current ruling member of the Cartel’s ruling council. Carlos was a piece of work, and he had only recently come to power after a power struggle inside the Cartel that was still unclear to Xavier. No matter, they would use anything he found to their advantage. Xavier realized his involvement in this operation would surely be as a shadow figure while Sean and Bertrand ran the game. Xavier was still a celebrity, and his time spent several years before in Austin all but guaranteed someone would recognize him. Xavier began writing down the overall picture of his operational plan, making notes and questions as he went along to firm up the points. Xavier was quickly running through the ideas as his pencil flew across the pages. Bertrand and Sean both woke up at eight o’clock when their alarms went off. Bertrand was the younger looking of the two, and his slight French accent had made it easy for him to seem a foreigner. That helped put people off, and his enrollment at the University of Texas at Austin was simply a formality 8under his assumed identity. They both showered and dressed quickly then headed down to the third floor to get breakfast before leaving. Both men saw the light still coming out from beneath the door to the conference room. “He still up?” Sean asked surprised. Bertrand looked at his friend, “I guess so. You think he tried to read all the files in one night?” “Bulldog did say he was quite determined about this situation.” Sean shrugged his shoulders, “Maybe we’re finally going to swing into action.” Bertrand sighed, “I hope so! Going through Economics and Calculus again are driving me crazy!” Sean and Bertrand walked over to the door and opened it. They saw Xavier writing on one of the white boards surrounding the table. Looking around, they saw Xavier had been a busy man as he made notes everywhere, Xavier finished up his note then turned around, “Hey guys, what’s up?” “We just got up to go do a few things.” Sean said as he looked more closely at the writing on the wall, “Bertrand has early classes today. I was doing a little reconnaissance on Michael Starkos today to firm up some of his habits.” “I was going to make some breakfast before we left. You look like you could use some food after how long you’ve been up. Would you like me to make some for you?” Bertrand put his backpack down on the table. “Sure, sure.” Xavier nodded as he continued making notes on the white board. Bertrand grabbed the empty plate and bottles of beer from the table. He carried them to the kitchen with him. Bertrand walked into the large kitchen and felt immediately at ease. He felt as if he was back home in the kitchen. The kitchen had black linoleum and black tiling around black wood cabinets and granite counter tops. The appliances were all stainless steel, and Bertrand headed for the refrigerator after dropping the dishes in the sink and the bottles in the recycling bin. Bertrand pulled out the eggs, sausage patties, hash browns and milk. Bertrand quickly went about preparing a hearty yet simple breakfast. He turned on the skillet on the counter. The skillet was built into the top of the counter. The large cooking area allowed Bertrand to cook everything at once in one location, and the skillet was coated with non-stick metal to ensure easy clean-up. Bertrand threw everything on the skillet. While Bertrand waited for the heat to begin going to work he put twelve slices of bread into the toaster. The entire process was practiced and easy for him. Spices went into the eggs and hash browns while the milk went into a small glass for himself. Bertrand put all the ingredients away as he sipped on the milk. The food cooked quickly as always. Xavier and Sean came walking into the room. “We’ll go over everything tonight. The plan will be simple, but it requires one of us to be very visible on the campus. That visibility will be dangerous.” Xavier explained as he walked over to the glass topped wrought iron table. Sean walked over to the refrigerator, “Do you want something to drink?” “Do you have Hansen’s Energy Drink?” Xavier asked, his eyes wide with hope. Sean shook his head, “No, none of that. We do have Red Bull though.” “I’ll take one of those then. I need to go by the store to pick up a few things for myself later today.” Xavier leaned down on his hands as he rested his elbows on the table. Sean pulled out the Red Bull along with a Code Red Mountain Dew for himself. He returned top the table as Bertrand pulled out three black plates. The toaster popped up all twelve pieces of bread perfectly toasted. Bertrand put butter on each piece of toast, then stacked them on a smaller back plate one on top of the next to help the butter melt. Bertrand loaded the plates with the eggs, browns and sausage then carried two of the plates to the table. Bertrand made another quick trip to get the last plate, toast and silverware. They all sat at the table, eating silently and quickly. Xavier’s mind continued to work over the plan as he ate, using the calories to fuel his intense thought. Xavier smiled as chewed the sausage. The plan would work. Xavier had taken a hot shower and cleaned up. His long night had followed a very long day, and so he washed away the entire dirt of the day with the washcloth and soap. Xavier shaved off the stubble from his head and face except for the goatee surrounding his mouth. Xavier stepped out of the enclosed shower onto the marble floor. Xavier found a large white bath sheet that he wrapped around his waist. A white terry cloth robe was hanging on the wall, so Xavier put the thick robe on around his muscular frame. Xavier walked over to the full-length mirror on the wall. He had been working out like a maniac while taking every supplement in the store. The benefits were obvious enough for him as he put on more muscle mass. Xavier’s chest was thick now, his arms like thick tree branches and his legs seemed carved from stone. He ran his hands over his bald head sending the water still resting atop it down his neck to the terry cloth robe. The image staring back at him was strong and sure with determined blue eyes that radiated an uneasy calm. Xavier pulled his after-shave lotion out of the medicine cabinet he had filled earlier that day before sleeping. Xavier poured the white lotion into his hand then ran it over his head and face. The soothing balm moisturized his skin while relieving the fire left after shaving against the grain for maximum smoothness. Xavier walked out into the main room and crossed the carpeted floor to the couch by the fireplace. Xavier sat down on the couch and spread himself out to relax. The workout in the garage gym had helped him work off some tension, and now the hot shower had succeeded in soothing his muscles. Xavier stood up and used his towel to dry off completely. He returned to the bathroom and hung the towel and bathrobe on the hooks. Xavier got the lotion from the medicine cabinet. He used thee lotion all over his body to soften his skin and help keep it moisturized. Xavier wasn’t the biggest fan of the lotion because it felt a little strange to him, but his agent was right that it kept his skin looking great. Xavier didn’t want to end up looking like a wrinkled raisin in the sun. Xavier put on boxers, black jeans and a black tee shirt. Xavier took his socks and boots with him to the couch. He put on the heavy boots and went downstairs to see if Sean and Bertrand were home yet. Xavier heard some noise coming from the second floor. Xavier went down the next set of stairs and saw Sean and Bertrand over at the pool table. Xavier walked over to the bar as they seemed to be at the end of their game, “Morning guys!” “More like good evening Xavier.” Sean laughed. Bertrand lined up his shot and sunk the one ball into the corner pocket, “Late evening as a matter of fact. Of course, that means you’re on the right schedule for most of the work we’re going to end up doing.” Xavier opened the refrigerator behind the bar. He pulled out the bottle of Coca-Cola and popped the top using the bottle opener, “Yeah, after I finished up my plan last night I slept like a log. I feel a lot better now, and I think we’re going to be able to make some moves very soon.” Bertrand sunk the four ball, his last solid on the table. The cue ball banked off the side and came around right in front of the eight ball in perfect position. Bertrand moved around to get lined up, “And now it is time for Sean to once again lose. One day when your testes finally drop and you’re ready to be a man you can come back to try and win for a change!” Bertrand pointed to the corner pocket and then shot. The eight ball rolled into the pocket smoothly as Bertrand raised his arms into the air, “I am the king of the world!” Sean shook his head, “Too much Titanic for you Bertrand.” “Whatever! Hey Xavier, would you like to play?” Bertrand taunted Xavier by pointing at him with his stick. Xavier smiled back at Bertrand, “Sure, why not?” Xavier walked out from behind the bar and went over to the pool table. Xavier set his bottle down on one of the tall, round tables then went about racking the balls. Xavier finished quickly then nodded at Bertrand. Xavier went over to select a stick as Bertrand lined up his break. Bertrand hit the cue ball with a loud pop sending the white ball into the racked balls. The cue ball hit with a loud crack that sent the balls flying in all directions. Bertrand looked over the table like a predator as one of the solids went into a pocket. Xavier found his stick and tossed it back and forth from hand to hand. Xavier watched as Bertrand went about his shots very carefully. Bertrand put in three straight balls before a slight miscalculation sent the fourth ball off the side of the pocket instead of into the pocket. Xavier took a final sip of his coke, “Shame Bertrand, but good try.” Bertrand simply snorted in reply. Xavier moved over to the table and lined up his first shot, which was a straight shot into the pocket. Xavier sunk the ball easily, and drew the cue ball back for a second shot on the next fourteen down the line. Xavier sent the ball sailing down the table gently, it tapped the ball into the corner pocket and continued on, banking off the wall to be set-up for another shot. Xavier continued moving around the table like a professional, intent on each and every shot. Xavier was moving with great skill and precision. Sean saw the change in demeanor as he moved around the table. Xavier was not shying away from anything, and his movements were so controlled he reminded Sean of an animal on the prowl. Xavier was moving like a lion on the savanna, king of his domain and master of all he surveyed. Xavier sunk each of his balls except the thirteen. Xavier had a great shot lined up for the thirteen, but there was not an obvious way to line up the final shot for the eight ball and sink the thirteen. “I believe your luck has run out my friend.” Bertrand smiled at Xavier. Sean agreed with the appraisal, “Well played, but you seem to be in a pickle there Xavier. Of course, Bertrand will miss another shot and then you will be victorious.” Xavier looked at both men with an amused frown, “Bertrand and Sean, would you rely on a man holding a gun on you to miss the shot, or would you try to disarm or kill him to save yourself?” “That is a very different situation than what you find yourself in here.” Sean countered Xavier’s harsh words. Bertrand nodded his head; “This is simply a game of billiards that we are playing. There is no need to read any deeper into this than we already have.” Xavier walked around to the other side of the pool table. He could not strike the thirteen directly from his new angle. Xavier looked at Sean and Bertrand, “You must make moves to win. If you play the game only not to lose then you shall profit nothing. We are already playing a new game through our current actions, and even more importantly we are playing to win.” “Try new strategies, new approaches and in the end you always play to win because playing it safe in this area will only lead to the same results we have faced the entire time we have been here.” Xavier explained. Xavier hit the cue ball down the table to the far end. The ball bounced off the wall and then traveled to the side wall then hit the other short wall near the pocket. The cue ball hit the thirteen and sent it into the pocket. The ricochet sent the cue ball back to the center of the table. “You see gentlemen, we aren’t going to play it safe and risk nothing. We will use our new approach to circumvent the blocks the police have seen, and then we will win this battle. Our battle may well turn the tide of the war.” Xavier said as he walked around to the side of the table again. “Not always the same thing.” Sean said. Xavier shot the eight ball into the side pocket much harder than needed as he showed how much power he had, “Ad so this game ends, but I think it’s time to begin a new game gentlemen.” Xavier looked back at his two companions, “We embark on something that endangers us all, but that danger only makes the entire affair more appropriate. Now that this game is at an end let us begin a new game.” Xavier set his stick down on the table. Xavier walked over and picked up his coke. He sipped the sweet, caffeine laced liquid. “How about we begin making plans to handle the operation I have planned?” “You’re finished with the plan?” Sean was surprised. “Yes, I am.” Xavier’s voice had a level of surety and confidence they respected. “I think we need to begin right away.” Bertrand set his own stick down, “Show us.” Xavier smiled, “Perhaps we should eat a good dinner as we discuss the plan Bertrand” If you cook then I will finalize the presentation. What do you say?” Bertrand was smiling broadly now, “Perfect! We just need Sean to run to the store and grab a few items for me.” “Okay, sounds good.” Sean was in agreement as well. Xavier looked over at Sean, “Can I add a few things I need to that list as well?” “Why not?” Sean shrugged. “I’m going anyway.” Xavier was cleaning up several of the points and drawing he had made on the white boards. Xavier wanted everything to be perfect for the presentation he planned on making to everyone. They had gotten a message from “Bulldog” Hargis that he would be arriving in town that evening to join them with news of what was happening in Laredo and Mexico. Xavier knew the final plans would soon be in motion. There was a coldness to his thoughts as he planned out every detail on the boards. He had to have a sound plan to ensure it would work, but more than that he needed a good presentation to capture their spirits and hearts. While Xavier was working in the conference room, Bertrand was busy cooking up a fine meal in the kitchen. He had decided to go all out for the meal this evening since a plan would be presented and Hargis would be showing up later. Bertrand had already made the dessert. The crust was simple and made with gourmet chocolate to add amazing flavor to a simple ingredient. He put cream cheese into the food processor then added four eggs with two extra yolks. Sugar and freshly squeezed lime juice also joined the mixture. The food processor was turned on to mix everything together. Bertrand watched as the processor attacked the mixture. He saw it was blended now. He stopped the processor and pulled out the blades. Bertrand made sure the crust was well pressed into the mold then poured the lime cheesecake mixture over the crust. Bertrand shook the mold briefly to ensure it was even then set it in a large roasting pan. Bertrand filled the pan up halfway with hot water from the large coffee carafe he had filled previously. The entire roasting pan went into the oven to cook. Bertrand had taken the finished cheesecake and put it into the refrigerator to cool for the meal. The dessert would be topped with fresh whipped cream as it was served to the men in the conference room. Bertrand took the potatoes out of the oven and put them into the refrigerator beside the dessert. Bertrand had four massive steaks on the gas grill built into the range. He had marinated them for an hour before beginning the cooking. Bertrand had also make a wonderful cantaloupe soup that featured thin slices of honeydew melon and water melon arranged in a star pattern on top of the puree. Bertrand also was baking some potatoes that were almost done. He would use them to cook twice-baked potatoes with garlic and cheese. Bertrand was not completely decided on what vegetable to cook, so he had decided to cook a nice medley of vegetables Julianne and cooked in a butter sauce. Bertrand used red peppers, yellow peppers, green peppers, squash, and zucchini for the medley. Bertrand enjoyed taking the time to slice up all the vegetables with his sharpened blade. He was an excellent knife fighter, and the vegetables he cut were simply an example of his skill. Bertrand turned the steaks on the grill then pulled out the cookie sheet with small foil wrapped cloves of garlic. He unwrapped the garlic then cut it up. The garlic went into his garlic press then into a large glass bowl. He used four garlic cloves for the recipe. Cheddar and Swiss cheese joined the garlic along with dashes of seasonings to taste. Bertrand retrieved the potatoes from the refrigerator. They were cut in half then scooped out into the bowl leaving only the potato skins. The entire mixture was quickly folded together then put back into the potato skins. The filled skins were placed on a cookie sheet then loaded back into the oven to continue cooking. Bertrand was beginning to move even faster as he cooked. The vegetables were thrown into a skillet with butter and lemon. Downstairs a key turned in the lock. “Bulldog” Hargis had arrived in town from his travels up from Laredo. His face was drawn and tired as he stepped into his house carrying two briefcases. Bulldog walked up the stairs to the second floor where he found Sean at the billiard table alone. Sean was taking shots, playing pool by himself as he was the shooter for both sides in the game of eight ball. Bulldog set his briefcases down against the wall, “Hey Sean! I always thought you were the kind of guy who liked to beat himself!” Sean looked up and saw Bulldog. Sean flipped him the bird as he put the cue stick down, “Bite me Bulldog. You want a beer?” Bulldog walked over to the table, “Yeah, that sounds good. If you haven’t finished all my Sheiner Bach just pop the top on one for me.” Sean walked over to the bar and slid open the refrigerated metal box. Sean pulled out a Sheiner and took the bottle cap off with a bottle opener. Sean handed the beer to Bulldog over the bar. “Thanks!” Bulldog took a long sip of the beer. Sean had a smug look on his face, “Well, I was always taught to respect my elders, and they don’t get too much more elder than you.” “Funny, very funny guy.” Bulldog sipped his beer as Sean stepped out from behind the bar. “Well, since I’m such an old man you grab those two briefcases and carry them upstairs to the office for me.” “Bertrand is cooking dinner for our meeting with Xavier right now.” Sean explained as he picked up the briefcases, surprised by how heavy they were. “Meeting?” Bulldog was surprised. Sean tilted his head, “Well, he didn’t just come in and go to sleep. He spent all night going through files and coming up with some ideas on how to approach the situation. He said that he wanted to know what intelligence you may have gathered in Laredo, but that he didn’t think it would affect the planning.” Bulldog hoped it meant Xavier had finally left all his qualms and moral questions at the door. It sounded like business was about to pick up, “Good, very good. So what do you think of him?” “I like what I’ve seen so far. He is definitely an intense kind of guy, and he already has a plan for us. How did you hook up with this guy?” Sean and Bulldog were headed up the stairs. “I know his family, and have known them for a long time. You know them too.” Bulldog wondered if Sean had recognized Xavier black at all yet. Sean couldn’t remember where he might have seen Xavier before, “Don’t recognize him at all. Where do I know him from?” “Don’t worry about it. We’ll talk later.” Bulldog replied as he stepped onto the third floor. He could smell the wonderful aromas coming from the kitchen where he knew Bertrand would be cooking up a wonderful meal. Bulldog walked into the kitchen as Sean went to drop off the briefcases. Bertrand was still hard at work on his meal. “Bertrand, it smells absolutely wonderful my friend! What are you creating tonight?” Bertrand wiped his hands on a towel he had on his apron, “Bulldog!” They quickly shook hands then Bertrand turned to point out the food. “I am making some wonderful steaks for us. I think you’re going to enjoy the meal I make tonight. We’re going to eat in the conference room, and it should be ready in just a few minutes.' “Great, I’m starved after the long trip.” Bulldog left the room without distracting the cook any further. Bulldog went to get ready for the meeting, knowing the information he brought with him would result in some changes they wouldn’t be ready for. The meal was laid out on the conference room table atop thick, padded cloths made to withstand the heat of the pans. Bertrand served everyone the steak, twice-baked potatoes, vegetable medley and rolls. They all drank according to their own tastes, and Bertrand was disappointed only he and Bulldog took advantage of the wine he had selected to accompany the meal. Xavier waited until the meal was winding down before he began going over the plan he had developed to handle the Cartel operations in Austin. Xavier’s presentation was clear and concise. Each person present would stop the presentation to ask questions, give suggestions or clarify information. The plan was simple and effective. Bertrand would play a large part in the play, and financing would need to be secured, but even that was part of the plan to make sure the money needed could be secured. Everyone listened intently as the final pieces of the plan were relayed to the team. The men around the table looked at Xavier and each other. “I’m going to go and get the dessert for us. We can discuss it over some cheesecake.” Bertrand stood up and began collecting the dishes. He carried them away. Bulldog looked up at Xavier, “There are some points in your plan that may need to be adjusted based on the information I just got from Laredo.” Xavier sat down at the table to listen as Bertrand returned with the cheesecake. They all agreed the cheesecake was some of the best they had ever had. The plan was finalized based on the information they received from Bulldog.
