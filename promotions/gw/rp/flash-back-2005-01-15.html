Flash Back

15 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/15/05 Tribulations of Drake, Continued? -Flash Back- Drake sits at his window looking to Melissa’s apartment across the alley. The lights are out and no one appears to be home. Another storm has hit Los Angeles on this night. He watches raindrops cascading down the window keeping himself occupied. We focus on the window and things behind us start to lighten up and voices gradually increase. Pulling back we find ourselves in Drake’s house where he rests on his recliner covered in a warm blanket and Katie next to him trying to nurture him back to good health. Katie: C’mon drink it. Drake: (Pouting) But I don’t wanna. Katie: Do you wanna get better or not? Drake: Well yeah but? Katie: Than drink it! He moans. Drake: You could have at least gotten a different flavor. Katie: Well this is all they had. Drink it! He slowly and reluctantly raises the glass to his lips and takes a small sip. Immediately his cheeks suck in and eyes squint. Seconds later he returns to normal. Drake: Oh I hate lemon! Katie: You may hate it but this will make you better. He sighs. It almost seems like he would prefer the cold over the drink. Katie: Now remember Drake you have to drink this while it’s warm. And don’t even think about pouring it down the drain! This brings a smirk to his face. Katie: Drake! Drake: I won’t. She picks up the remote beside him and clicks off the television. His eyes become wide and he begins to pout wanting her to turn it back on. Katie: Now I got the humidifier on and its nice and warm and cozy so I expect you to get plenty of rest tonight. Drake: Can’t I at least watch TV until I’m done drinking this? Katie: No because then you’ll stretch it into a couple of hours and that’s not the way to do this. And I’m sleeping with my door open tonight so I better not see that television go on, we have to get you better. She leans in and kisses him on the forehead. Katie: love you Jonathan. Drake: Love you to Katie. Katie stands and heads for her room. Katie: Good night. Drake: Night. She disappears into the darkens of her room as Drake looks to his thera-flu and sighs. When he looks up we immediately jump back to the present just as he looks back out into the night. He now has a petite smile on his face. Moments later? Welcome to the bedroom. This room isn’t quite as plain as everything else. Multiple duffel bag and weapons are organized and fill most of this room but as with the rest of the apartment, no decorations or even a bed. Drake enters and flashes from the storm reveal glimpses of his toned body. He climbs on to his hammock that hangs in one corner of the room resting his feet high. After covering up, he pulls Katie’s necklace out of his dark pants and stares into it releasing a deep sigh. After several minutes of gazing he finally reaches over and hangs it onto a single nail in the wall. “I promise we’ll be free of this chaos someday Katie’I promise?” He closes his eyes to rest as we push in on the necklace while the wall lights up with several more flashes of the storm.
