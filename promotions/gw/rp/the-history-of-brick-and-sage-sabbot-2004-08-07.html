The history of Brick and Sage Sabbot

7 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/7/04 Somewhere in New York City... Children play on the sidewalk. A man complains about pigeons dropping their waste on his car. And Brick trains in his personal gym for his big match on Evolution. He faces a man with whom he had an intense rivalry in the year before, barring his rivalries with Salaok and Deacon Holmes. He faces the Harvester of Tears. He faces the Soul Survivor. The Warlock. The New-Age Lazarus. Brick faces Sage Sabbot. ---- Date: May 2003 It was a month after Wrestlecade 3. Brick had defeated his longtime adversary Salaok. Now, Brick was free to pursue what had eluded him for so many years: the Global Wrestling World Heavyweight champion. When Chris Camero left the company, the title was vacated. So, a tournament was held to determine the new World champion. The final two contestants would face each other in the main event at Dead Zone. The two final contestants would turn out to be Brick and Sage Sabbot. At the time, Sabbot was a member of the Harvesters. They would face each other in a Hell in a Cell match, where one could only win by submission. In an attempt to stack the deck against Brick, Salaok mailed front-row tickets for Dead Zone to Brick’s family and friends. The logic was that Brick would be so worried about his loved ones being harmed, that he wouldn’t be able to concentrate on the match. Also, his loved ones would experience of seeing him possibly getting beaten to within an inch of his life. Brick managed to tip the scales in his favor a bit by barricading the door to the Harvesters? locker room. This, however, didn’t last very long, as the Harvesters managed to break free. Salaok had yet another idea to stack the deck against Brick. He had the Cell doused in gasoline and set on fire. Brick was already having problems breathing, considering how long the match had gone on and the fact that his nose was broken. But now the cell was on fire, and the fire was using up oxygen. At the end of the match, Brick suffered a humiliating defeat in his hometown of New York City, when he tapped out due to Sabbot dislocating his shoulder with a crossface. Afterwards, Brick popped his shoulder back into place by slamming it against a turnbuckle and showed sportsmanship by handing Sabbot the belt. He then attacked Sabbot and hit the Bricklayer on him on top of the belt. It was Brick’s message that this matter was far from over. ---- Date: June 2003 Brick was looking to get back into the World title picture and pay Sage Sabbot for his loss at Dead Zone. Unfortunately, Salaok had tried to place obstacle after obstacle in Brick’s path, but to no avail. Brick won a shot at the World title in a triple threat match facing Derek Frost and Quartz. Now, there was no keeping Brick from finally winning the title. His match with Sabbot was part of a double main event with Salaok vs. Derek Frost in a retirement match. This time, he and Sabbot would face each other in the most brutal match GW had to offer: The Pit. The Pit was fifteen feet deep, with concrete walls and a hard iron floor. Weapons dropped down at random. The only way out was through two large iron doors which would only lock and unlock at the command of a computer. The only way to win a match in the Pit is either by submission or knockout. Brick finally had payback and also finally achieved his dream. He was finally the World champion. After that match, Sabbot left the company. ---- Date: July 12, 2004 Master of the Mat, one of the company's biggest pay-per-vie events of the year, was fast approaching. Brick was competing in one of the qualifying round tag team matches. His opponents: Jesse Jamester and Sage Sabbot. His partner: another of Brick's rivals, Deacon Holmes. Brick's alliance with Holmes was a tenuous one, given their genral dislike for one another. In addition, Holmes had taken to verbally abusing a friend of Brick's, Gunner Addison. Brick had done everything in his power to ensure that everything went smoothly. Unfortunately for both Brick and Holmes, Gunner's boyfriend was 6'6' and 295 lbs. of short-tempered Texas cowboy named Ely Braxton. Right before their match, as Deacon made his way to the arena, Ely ambushed Deacon and beat him bloody, threatening to kill him if he ever disrespected Gunner again. Caught off guard by Ely's actions and outnumbered, Brick was easy pickings for Jamester and Sabbot. ---- Date: July 26, 2004 Brick had won the first lottery match in the beginning of the Master of the Mat card. He then went on to compete in the semi-finals against Sabbot, Venneman Pendragon and World champion Josh. Brick was still tired from the lottery match, but in the end, it took Pendragon, Josh and Sabbot combining their forces and triple teaming Brick to eliminate him from the match. ---- Now, Brick faces Sabbot yet again in their first singles match in slightly over a year. Who will emerge triumphant? Will history repeat itself? These are questions Brick hopes to answer on the 9th of August.
