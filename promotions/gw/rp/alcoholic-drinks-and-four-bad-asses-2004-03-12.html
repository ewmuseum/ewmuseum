Alcoholic drinks and four bad-asses

12 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/12/04 The air in the Dark Horse Saloon was thick with excitement. As usual, people were dancing and drinking and having a good time. “Cowboy” was playing on the jukebox. Four men, in particular, were soaking in the ambiance. Four men who often mapped out battle plans and drank over discussions about life. These four men were Brick, Ely Braxton, Jesse Briggs and Logan Creed. Brick had a glass of ginger ale while Ely had his usual Texas mudslide. Jesse and Logan each nursed a bottle of draft beer. This night, their topic of conversation concerned one particular topic: the M.O.B. On the last edition of Evolution, Ely faced Train in a Bar Cage Match. Christina McCoy booked the match after Ely requested it due to the current drama between Train and Christina. Ely had the upper hand at first, but eventually Train wound up winning the match. Almost immediately afterwards, Mark Makonie came out of the crowd and attacked Ely. Joining in unexpectedly was Train. Given Makonie’s blatantly racist views, such an alliance seemed very unlikely. Nevertheless, there they were. Joining in were Michael and Terrelle Greeniso, also known as the Syndicate. “How are you feeling?” Many of Ely’s body parts showed the marks of battle. Bandages covered the parts that were ripped by “Better than I was before. Thanks for comin” to my aid, buddy.” “Hey, you would have done the same for me. In fact, you have done the same for me.” “We both have,” added Jesse. “Bunch of punk asses, all four of “em!” Logan spat out. “You’re telling me. Wasn’t enough for the sumbitches to get me while I’m down. They gotta handcuff me to the damn cage. Next time I see “em, these cowboy boots are goin” straight in their asses!” “I definitely want to get my hands on that little sell-out bitch Train!” “I know. And Makonie calls me an” Ely race traitors?” “You gotta be one self-hatin” motherfucker to be black and hook up with a Nazi! I’ve met some Uncle Toms before, but that shit was just’just’I can’t even describe it!” “Easy, Brick, easy.” “Fuck easy! I take this shit personally!” “I hear you!” “All the bullshit I had to deal with last month because of Mark Makonie, and that ignorant bastard Train wants to join forces with the motherfucker!” “I thought that righteous indignation sounded familiar,” said a familiar sounding female voice. The four men turned around to see Christina McCoy walking towards the bar. “Hey,” said Ely as she walked up. “How are you feeling?” “Better than he was before,” said Brick, Jesse and Logan in unison. “Ah, go to hell, all o” ya!” “I’ve got some good news.” “Christina darlin”, just tell me this good news involves me beatin that sumbitch Makonie bloody.” “Not just Makonie, but all of MOB. Next Evolution, there’s going to be an eight man elimination tag match. On one side it’ll be them and on the other side, you guys and Donny McNasty.” “That’s good news,” said Ely. “Wait up, Chris. What’s McNasty got to do with it?” “I think he still wants to even the score after losing to Train at High Octane.” “Well, that’s fine with me. I can relate to wanting to beat Train’s little sorry ass.” “Me too,” said Ely. “I figured as much.” “Still, I got a feeling those assholes are gonna try something.” Brick then pulled out his cell phone and dialed a number. “Who you callin?”” “You’ll see. Hey man, how you doin?” Listen, something’s going down here next Monday. I might need you to show up, just in case. Yeah, I know you’ve been looking to m=come back, so this might be the best opportunity. OK, I’ll call you if I need you there. OK, peace.” Brick then hung the phone. “Who was that?” asked Christina. “Back-up plan.”
