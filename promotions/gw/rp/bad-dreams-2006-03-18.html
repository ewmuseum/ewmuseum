Bad Dreams

18 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 18 Its been nearly six months now. Six months since Rogan and his associates, the backbone of DK Enterprises, found a loophole in their contract and backed out of DK Enterprises, sending Dylan’s empire spiraling into bankruptcy. Six months since Dylan’s son, David, suggested that his father return to the squared circle. Six months since Dylan began to get into the best shape of his life, then after doing this he flew out Tampa, Florida and moved into Quinn’s Tampa condo so that he may train at the AoA fitness center with Harley Klein, that mean ass bastard broke Dylan’s wrestling style everyday for five and half months, and rebuilt it every night, weeding out Dylan’s weak spots, ridding the Killer of his technical weaknesses. Now, Dylan was a member of Global, and it was here that Dylan planned to finally win a World Title, even if that meant going through Jake Hix’again. He’s home now, Dylan that is, it’s been five months since that Killer has even stepped into his Dallas, where his wife, Kaylee was so eagerly awaiting him. Her husband had melted off a lot of that fat that had slowed him down over the years by training for his in-ring return to professional wrestling. There were, of course, a lot of people who doubted the fact Dylan was going to be able to hang with the younger guys, but those were people who had never since perform before, those were people who hadn’t seen him train for nearly six months straight, these people were going to be proven wrong. The foyer of Dylan’s home was a grand one, decorated by his wife and one of her girlfriends (who happened to be an interior decorator). It was brightly lit through exquisite all white floor lamps that pointed straight up at the high angled ceiling, but yet dark, almost gloomy pieces of art hung from the high white-walls. It was an odd yin-yang sort of mix of bright lights meet dark pictures, Dylan liked it, no matter how much the artwork had cost him. Two crimson colored rectangular ottomans sat pressed against the far walls. The ottomans were intended for people to sit on while they took off or put on their shoes, but they both had become a form of a coat rack over the years, Dylan didn’t mind, and it used to drive Kaylee crazy, now Kaylee didn’t care either, her coat was tossed over the ottoman off the left. Dylan quietly closed his thick oak door and crossed the foyer to the ottoman on the right. The Killer set his bag down on the ottoman, and proceeded into the dining room, which was just through a door on the right. The door swung open easily and Dylan peered inside? No Kaylee. Quickly Dylan backed himself back into the foyer and dashed up the flight of stairs that led directly into the hallway of their master bedroom. In the hallway Dylan could see that the door to the master bedroom, his bedroom that he shared with his wife, was cracked open. Dylan cracked a wicked sort of grin and tiptoed to the door. Listening with the skills of a master spy Dylan slid his body against the wall, listening the sounds coming from his bedroom. They were sounds that echoed there many a times. Kaylee’s low moans and breathless panting filled Dylan with a passion he hadn’t feel in years, but that passion turned into a burning rage as he realized that the person making his wife, the mother of his only son, moan and pant wasn’t him. Dylan’s bright blue eyes blazed with the fires of hell as he swung his bedroom door open so fast and so hard that he nearly tore it from its hinges. In the middle of his bed was his wife, as it should be, but on top of his wife something that should not be. It was something that caused Dylan to turn so furious that it must have affected his eye sight because the digital clock next Dylan’s bed was blurred beyond being even close to legible. Jake Hix, Dylan’s long time friend/rival was mounting Dylan’s wife. But all Dylan could do was quake with anger, quake with rage, quake with a sudden lust for blood. Without missing a beat the man that retired Dylan before he could obtain the World Championship turned his head and shot Dylan a wicked sneer? “AHHH!!! YOU MOTHER FU”.” Caught himself as his senses shot back to life, Dylan up on Quinn’s couch, breathlessly trying to catch his breath. Dylan slowly opened his eyes to find that he was still in Tampa. He was still in Quinn DeMarco’s condo. It had been a dream. “A fucking’dream. A fucking nightmare.” Dylan corrected himself breathlessly. The Killer turned his body and sat in the middle of the couch, slowing his breathing and wiping the sweat from his head. Dylan rubbed his eyes, then rolled his hands to temples and massaged his temples, massaging away the images of his horrific nightmare. Dylan’s sleepy mind began to slowly speed and his own thoughts began to travel to fast for him. “Aren’t all dreams suppose to symbolize something, isn’t that what Quinn used to say” Yeah, that sounds like Quinn, but I don’t believe in that crap. Well, for not believing in it seems to have left one hell of an impression upon me. It was just one of those nightmares, what guy hasn’t had a nightmare of his wife sleeping with a friend of his? Well, what could this dream have symbolized? I don’t know? Wait maybe it’s suppose to represent? No it doesn’t mean anything, it was just a very surreal dream. Bullshit, you know what it means! What then?! What does it mean?! You know!” Dylan shook away the thoughts that clouded his mind. “Tylenol, I need Tylenol.” But Dylan didn’t get up to get the Tylenol, he just sat on the couch trying to think of anything, he just wanted to be, he just wanted to exist long enough until he returned to the ring. Until then, maybe he should see a psychiatrist. Dylan The Killer Knight
