'Shadows over Jerusalem'

30 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'Shadows over Jerusalem' Derek Draven Global Wrestling Promo #1075 Date: 3-30-04 Location: Jerusalem 'Have you ever felt alone?' 'Really ever been alone...' 'I have..' I'm always alone... 'In my mind...' 'I rely on my recollections of a happier time,if there ever was one..' 'In my recollections I can recall a hoard of demons..' 'Prepare...' 'Cause now my demons are yours...' (As the scene fades in from the sun we come down to a desert.A lone man in torn ragged clothing with shoulder length dirty blonde hair and a two day stubble of a beard. As the man walks in his ragged white shoes across the scorching desert sand he keeps looking toward the sky) 'Whats a matter?' He asks looking deep into the clear blue of the sky above.. 'Having trouble..' The man continues his walk up a mound of sand.Nothing seperates him from his goal now... (cut from image into darkness) He is a shadow... A dark shadow figure... A shape if you will.. a figure that represents what you fear... Your deepest fear.. Evil... in it's absolute purest form.. All of GW's greats will tell you,This man is no game.. The teachings of Salaok and Quartz have got him here.. (from the darkness we come back to the desert.The man on the mound looks down upon a primitive appearing city.A slight gust of wind blows sand in front of his face as his hair covers his eyes.He descends downward toward the city..) (Back into the darkness..) This 'shape' if you will embodies the purity of what you can find on the other side of good. This mans return to GW has set of some sparks.. Brick..Roz Blayze..Terminator.. Fairly well to say the last three that will remember how truly evil this 'shape' can be. Hangings to abduction.. Anything to get ahead. Anything to feel in control over his enemies... Some will see him as powerless without the Nevermore.. The smart ones will see him as more dangerous.. Much more dangerous. (back into the city) 'I see what you do to your supposed Holy Land..' (the shape walks into the crowd of the city as they begin to scatter and talk in tongues..) 'See..they fear me' 'Where are you when they need you' 'Your supposed to be a higher power...' 'The great almighty' (The man walks through the city.Some men gather together street side prepared to take defense.) 'Great people of god i mean you no harm' (there fear is transparent through there bodies.They fear this man.Knowing nothing of him,just that his presence is one of the unholy) 'I'm not to be feared' 'Follow me like you follow him..' 'I'm you key to salvation!' (no one dares to move) 'Well whats a matter with you people..' (nothing still as some women whisper to eachother) 'Hopless..' 'Thats what all of you are' 'He is never coming..' 'Believe me,i've been in presence of a true power...' 'You all..have no clue' (the shape walks freely down the road.Directly into the middle of a huddle made up of thousands of these people...Holy Land people in the land of God himself.The shape just walks freely..not a care in the world.Just enjoys his sounds...a free roaming televangelist for his religion) 'Do you know what evil is?' 'He allows you all to fight..to kill' 'You call this his love?' 'He's not up there!!!' (pointing towards the sky) 'This doesnt bother you?' (the man stares in the eyes of some of these people,forcing them to move backward in fear..) 'You call this man God' 'I call him nothing' 'A no-one' 'He leaves all of you abandoned,to fend for yourselves!' (The shape walks out the huddle) 'Yet,you still,all of you still pray to this man...' 'Is it a sense of security?' 'Whatever he gives you is false..' 'Just like the teachings of his son.' 'The man they call Christ' 'He was only a prophet people' 'A liar' 'A fake' 'A nothing,like his father' 'yet to cling to him around here is to live' 'My presence here...though it may change nothing,will haunt you forever' (alot of people go here seeking christ,only to find the sad despair that his land,is nothing but a poverty stricken madhouse.Full of good and evil,the true battle.But for the shape the desperate search is finally over.He has finally found himself.) ~screen transition~ (In the dark,wet alleyway..the shape awakens..alone and in the ragged torn clothes that he wore in his dream.The man lay asleep leaned against a dented tin trash can overflowing with waste.Awakend by a revelation he looks startled as he looks around him in wonder.Recovering from his complete revelation) 'So thats what god is....A shadow over Jerusalem' 'Well...it's time to continue upon my way.For I have the meaning..' ~fade out~
