Finding the Past (Part 5)

13 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09.13.04 Gunner Addison was laughing with the Global Wrestling office's receptionist over a story he had just told Gun regarding himself, his boyfriend, and a vacuum cleaner. Gunner, looking as gorgeaous as ever, attempted to cover her mouth to keep in her outburst, but to no avail. 'I'm sorry, Rich,' Ms. Addison told the receptionist, 'but did he really try to put it in the vacuum cleaner?' 'God's honest truth,' laughed Rich. 'I didn't think he was ever going to get the damn thing out of there either.' The two remained laughing. 'What did you say his name was again,' questioned Gunner. 'Ryan,' responded Rich. 'You should have heard him. The damn man speaks Japanese and I've never heard so many foreign curse words in my life.' 'Oh well,' laughed Gunner, 'at least the gerbil's alright.' At this point, Rich had stopped laughing and was looking down the hallway that lead to the Global Gym very interestingly. 'Now that's a sexy man,' said Rich. 'I thought he couldn't get anymore attractive when he had that long blonde hair, but since he cut it off I'm just about ready to show him what a great piece of smoked Gouda with a trick jaw can do.' Gunner turned to look down the hall only to stop laughing herself as her eyes met orbs the color blue that only existed in the sky after a brutal storm had subsided and the sun had returned. standing across from her, some twenty feet or so, was an awkward feeling Ken Partridge Jr. 'Yeah,' she said without allowing her eyes to leave Ken's, 'you're right. he is gorgeaous.' Realizing that a thick chemistry was happening, Rich decided to leave the moment to the two and exit himself. 'I've got some boys to xerox, Hun. Call me later.' As the doors closed behind Rich, Ken finally began to apporach the woman that was the love of his life before he let it slip away. As he approached, the butterflies in his stomach began flying frantically, giving him the feeling that he was at the very top of the first hill of a large roller coater. To be honest, it was a feeling he'd missed. 'Ken,' announced Gunner first as she threw her arms around his neck. 'It's so good to see you. I'm so glad you're okay. I went to see you in the hospital, but you were still in your coma. My God, I'm so glad that you're alright.' 'Thanks,' replied Ken with a blush as he hugged her back. 'I'm feeling fine. My memories a little fuzzy, but the doctors say it'll come back in time.' Gunner pulled away, gazing into Ken's eyes deeply with a serious and worried look on her face. 'So the rumors are true. You really are suffering amnesia?' 'Just a mild case,' responded Ken as he squeezed her upper arm gently. 'There are just a few things I'm still having trouble remembering. But, like I said, it'll all come back.' 'Well,' began Gunner as she looked down at the floor. 'What... what do you remember?' Ken reached out to her face and pulled it up so that her eyes met his again. 'Everything about you... about us,' he said with a genuine smile warm enough to melt the ice caps. 'Well, almost everything.' 'What do you mean,' responded Gun. 'Well,' blushed Ken, 'I thought I remembered how beautiful you were, but I could never envision a beauty like this.' Gunner blushed as the front doors swung open again, revealing none other than Ely Braxton, standing with an irate look on his face. He walked over to Ken and Gunner, giving Ken a vicious stare-down the entire time. 'Gunner, we need to go,' said Ely without removing his eyes from KPJ. As Ken was about to tell an uneasy looking Gunner goodbye, Ely cut him off. 'I'm glad to see you came out of the wreck alright, Ken. Nobody deserves to go through what you did,' said Ely, surprising Ken and Gunner both. 'Just be careful from now on, alright.' Ely extended his hand, once again surprising the other two as Ken reached out and shook it. 'I'll be in the truck,' finished Ely to Gunner as he walked back out of the doors. The two stood in shock and disbelief for a short period of time before Gunenr broke the silence. 'I really do need to run, Ken.' 'I need to go, too,' responded Ken. 'Got a match to train for.' 'It was so good to see you,' began Gunner as she headed out the doors. 'Hopefully, we'll bump into each other again soon.' 'Yeah,' trailed Ken as Gunner exited. 'Hopefully... very soon.' His love is his strength.
