Brick's announcement

7 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/7/04 It was a Friday afternoon at Coretta Randall's house. Already there, sitting on the couch, were Brick's sister Monica and her husband Kyle. Coretta was 'What's keeping him?' 'I don't know. I just got off the phone with him. He said he'd be here in a little while.' Just then, the doorbell rang. 'I'll get it,' said Monica, attempting to get up, but Kyle stopped her.' 'Oh, no, you don't. You need to take it easy.' 'I'll get it.' Coretta answered the door to see Aaron, Felica and Ashley. 'Hi, Mrs. Randall 'Do you know why Brick called everyone over here?' 'No, he just said it was something important. Didn't say what it was.' Then the doorbell rang again. 'Hey, Ma,' said Brick, kissing Coretta on the cheek and walking into the living room. 'Don't give me 'Hey, Ma'. What's this all about?' 'Oh, good, you're all here.' 'Yes, we're all here. Now would you please tell us what's so important that you have us here?' 'Ok, here it is.' A slight pause. 'I'm thinking about running for Mayor next year.' For a moment, everyone was silent. And then, it happened. Everyone started laughing. The laughter didn't start as just a few titters and snickers, building up to loud guffawing. It went straight to the guffaws. 'No, I'm serious,' he said over the laughter. 'I'm thinking about it.' Everyone was still laughed. 'It's not funny.' Just then, the laughter started to die down. 'You're not kidding, are you?' asked Monica. 'No, I'm not.' 'You're actually thinking about running for Mayor?' Aaron asked. 'Yes.' 'Jamal, honey, what do you know about politics?' asked Coretta. 'You've never held a public office.' 'How many cats has that stopped? Did it stop Jesse Ventura? Did it stop Arnold Schwartzenegger?' 'Well, Schwartzenegger's different,' said Kyle. 'Voters in New York aren't that starstruck.' 'Well, we did put Giuliani and Bloomberg in office,' added Aaron. 'Giuliani, at least, held a public office.' 'And Bloomberg?' 'Good point. But still, don't you have enough stuff on your plate without running for public office?' 'Now I didn't say I'm actually going through with it. I said I'm just thinking about it. And I'd think I'd have your support. That at least one of you would believe in me.' 'Listen to me, Jamal. I have always supported you. Everyone here has. But you have to admit that this is a bit daunting. I mean, talking about politics over dinner is one thing. But actually going into politics...' 'Well, Ma, you know me. I'm always one to go against the odds.' 'That is true. If you really are seriously considering this...' 'I am.' 'Then I'm with you.' 'Me, too.' 'We all are. Just promise us you won't call yourself Brick if you decide to run.' 'Promise. Mayor Jamal Randall...nice ring to it.
