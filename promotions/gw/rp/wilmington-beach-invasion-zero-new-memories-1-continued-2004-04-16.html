The scene pans out as Deacon speaks. 'Sorry, can't play anymore. Good thing to, cause I was whoopin' yo monkey ass!' Now we see that another hand of cards is facing away from him, the same rat from before behind them. Deac stands and grabs his flashlight. He quickly climbs the ladder and stops at the top, looking at the manhole carefully before reaching into his satchel. He pulls one of the miniature charges out and presses it against the underside of the manhole. 'Juuuust incase the shit hits the fan early.' Now with a quick upward thrust, Deacon pushes the manhole cover up and off of its resting plate and crawls out, staying as low to the ground as possible. Back inside, John is still talking and Frock is still bored to tears. 'Ann and me, we were afraid that after the surgery, I wouldn't want to anymore. But I sure shocked her I think. I'm friskier than ever. And now we don't have to worry about it if I don't...' 'Mr. Griffin! Can we PLEASE get on with this?' James interrupts him in the knick of time. Naria smiles and glances at her watch. 'Yes, Brian. We have to get you back home before Ann starts wondering where you are. Let's get to the papers?' Naria says, speaking softly and hinting that they've stalled long enough. We zoom out suddenly, passing through the window to see the building outside and the three people on the inside, talking over the desk now as John begins to explain the papers. Deacon is crouched below the window. He reaches inside his satchel and pulls out two more charges, putting one in the grass and tossing another, letting it attach to the top of the fence nearest his position. A guard slowly walks into view from behind the building. Deac suddenly presses his back to the building and stops moving, hoping he's not spotted. The guard drops a cigarette on the ground and presses it out with his foot. In his hand, he holds a small automatic weapon. 'Fuck.' Deacon blurts out at the lowest possible level. The guard starts walking again, slowly towards the fence. The camera changes to show a closer view of this guard. He's not the most intelligent bloke in the bunch; with a crew cut and a 5-o'clock shadow, he stands around 5'10. A sound is heard in the distance and he turns quickly, looking directly at the window and Holmes' position... but Holmes is no longer there. He shrugs and turns, walking back behind the large building. Slowly the scene pans around to show Deacon around the corner, crouching with a seagull in his arms and his hand over it's beak. 'Damn, you're a seagull not a fucking stool pigeon!' The scene changes now as the same guard from before slowly approaches the dock, a curious look on his face. He mutters, just loud enough for those nearby to hear. 'What the fuck...?' Only one other guard can be seen and they both rush to the docks. The barges have all floated away from the docks now and it's finally caught the attention of these two guards. One of them walks to the end of the dock and grabs the rope which is now floating idly in the water. He glances back at his associate and speaks. 'These were cut, Jim.'The guard turns away and looks out at the barges again as his partner grabs a remote radio and presses the talk button. 'Yeah, we might have a-' And this is all that's heard inside as Reno grabs the man from behind and with one quick jerk, separates the man from his life. He falls to the side and into the water, causing a moderately loud splash. The radio hits the deck. The second guard stands and looks at Reno, surprise on his face. Quickly he aims his gun and pulls the trigger. Nothing happens. The scene fades and it shows the same guard, kneeling at the end of the pier looking at the rope. The frame freezes and then slowly pans around to show Reno underneath the pier. He reaches up and gently presses in the catch on the mans gun, letting the clip drop from the underside hold directly into his own gloved hand. He drops back under the water and swims away. The scene transitions back to the present. Reno holds up the clip and tosses it into the water before approaching the guard. The guard is silent and frightened, not quite knowing what to do. He begins to open his mouth to call for help but Reno grabs his face, forcefully covering his mouth with his cupped hand. He presses a single finger to his own lips, signaling for the guard not to speak. With a quick movement, his hand moves from his mouth to his throat, clenching him to prevent him from speaking. He forces the man to the dock and glances around, making sure that no one can see him. He quickly ties the nearby rope to his neck and with one quick jerk, shoves the unconscious man into the water, the rope keeping him underneath the dock and face down in the water. The first guards body is still floating. Reno quickly grabs an empty crate and tosses it on top of the guard, blocking him from view and explaining the splash that was heard before in one simple gesture. Footsteps... Reno quickly drops off the side of the dock and lowers himself silently into the water, not letting himself be seen. Another guard walks onto the dock and right by the fingertips that hold Reno in position at the side of the dock. The scene zooms out slightly to show the entire dock, Reno hanging in the water at its side. The guard stands above, staring out at the beautiful ocean. Another sound. The guard turns and aims his gun and is shocked by what he sees. 'What the fuck' A seagull flutters towards the dock, it's beak taped shut with duct-tape. The bird is panicking as it tries to free itself. The guard gets out a knife and slowly approaches the seagull when Reno emerges and grabs the guard about the neck with the strap of the man's own weapon. He jerks back and pulls him into the water, a hand over his mouth so that he can't speak. The camera moves, showing the water from above. Murky with tints of blue and green. ... red. Reno emerges slowly and sits on his knees, his eyes focusing on the duck. A grin forms on his face as he looks around. 'Didn't get all the duct-tape off, Deac?' The scene transitions. Deacon is slowly moving along the side of the building, sticking to the shadows. A guard walks nearby and he drops down, ducking behind a dumpster. The guard keeps walking, having no clue that Deacon was even there. With a quick movement, Deac throws a charge up and into the dumpster. He gets to his feet again and keeps moving until he finds a side door and uses it, slipping inside the building. The inside is rather large, filled with crates on the concrete floor, ship repair equipment to one side, nets hanging from the walls, and no trucks in site. Deacon nods and speak quietly. 'Mav was right. They did clear the place out.' Deac runs along the wall and jumps up, catching the ledge of an low-second floor balcony. He pulls himself up quickly and rolls onto the level, looking up at the ceiling above his head. He lifts his head and looks around before standing fully. No one is in site. He tosses a single charge onto an empty crate in the center of the room. Quickly, he tries the door but with no luck. It's locked. 'Aw, fuck. Had to go and lock Deacon out, didn't ya?' Within moments, Deac has the lock picked and he's in the room. Once inside, he sees his goal - a large overly secure safe. Deacon smirks and approaches it slowly. The sound of a boot hits the concrete floor. Deac draws both of his chrome Desert Eagles, complete with ivory grips, and aims them directly at the sound of the noise. A guard stands there, a look of pure anguish on his face. His gun slowly drops to the floor soon followed by him. Standing behind him is Maverick Reno, billy-club in hand. He smiles at Deac and drops the club, pulling out his own twin glocks. 'Get to work, Deac. Naria sent the second signal. You have about five minutes then we need to get the fuck out of here.' Deac turns around and puts on a pair of latex gloves and begins his 'operation' on the safe. He mumbles to himself as Maverick closes the door and takes his position, guns poised and ready to blaze away. 'Damn, always rushing a mother-fucker.' --- Three minutes later.--- 'Alright, let me get this shit in the... holy fuck that's a lot of money.' Deac spouts off suddenly, getting the safe wide open. Inside are stacks and stacks of 100, 500, and 1000 dollar bills. He quickly begins pulling the money out and into a waterproof black bag. Once the money is deposited in full, Reno grabs the bag from Deac and pulls the string, making sure it's completely closed up. 'Alright, the black sedan is still out front. You need to get into the backseat and stay down low. If all goes to plan, you should be able to ride right out of here with John and Naria.' Deacon nods and quickly bolts for the door. Maverick hooks the bag to his side and exits behind him, four guns heavy. The scene changes back to John, Naria, and their unsuspecting pawn. James is signing paper after paper, tired of listening to the explanations and stories given by John. Finally he restacks the pile and hands it to Naria. 'Here. That's it right?' Naria and John look at one another and slowly stand to shake hands with James.'That's it, sonny. We sure do appreciate your hospitality. You've been an awful good sport considering. John speaks slowly. A confused look forms on the face of Mr. Frock. 'What do you mean? A good sport, considering?' Asks a now confused and somewhat tense James Frock. Suddenly the door to the office swings open and a guard is standing there. 'Mr. Frock, we have a problem.'Frock looks up, somewhat peeved and now even more tense. 'What kind of problem?'Frock blurts out, not so sure as to what's going on. 'The safe's alarm has been tripped. They delayed it but it did sound. We're cleaned out!' Frock quickly stands and runs out of the room, following the guard. Naria and John look at one another and stand quickly, making their way towards the front entrance. Frock enters a small room in the building where a large safe had been placed. The safe stands, completely empty before him. Atop the safe is a single black rose. 'FUCK!' Exclaims an angry James Frock. He pushes past the guard and grabs a shotgun from his office as he passes through, heading towards the entrance. John and Naria are right at the exit. Guards have stopped them from leaving the building at this point, all aiming small arms at them. The ruse is, however, not up. Frock screams out, demanding an answer from the two con-artists. 'Where is my fucking money!?' John quickly raises from his hunched over position and grabs the end of his cane, drawing a sword from inside and swing it about, taking two guards down and disarming another with the final strike. He drops the wooden sheath quickly and draws a gun from Naria's purse, ripping the side off quickly. He aims the gun directly at Frock, his blade pressed to the neck of a guard. 'Our money, actually, James.' Realization sinks in. The black rose, the zoning issue, the fact that everything has just happened directly under his nose. James Frock is not dealing with a small time criminal. James Frock is the small time criminal... and he just stepped out of his league. Time stands against James and he knows it. He must speak now... it's too late to turn back. 'The odds are against you right now. Five to two... and only one of you is armed. Now where the fuck is my money!' BOOM - ah, good old Deac. Five explosions sound suddenly, the building shaking and rattling about. James looks out the nearest window and watches as a piece of fence flies by. The guards all panic and jump, giving Naria and John the chance they needed. John quickly jerks the blade, slitting one of the guards throats and twirls it, shoving it into a second guard. Naria jumps and turns in midair, slamming a heel into the side of one guards temple, knocking him to the ground with a thud. She drops to the ground and slams an elbow into the gut of another guard. She lifts her knee into his forehead and he hits the ground with a thud. John spins and catches the final guard... SMACK. with the blade, severing his ear from the side of his head. Another quick swipe and the guard falls to the floor, hitting his knees first and then falling to the side. John turns. He had heard something smack and the sound of a yelp but he hadn't caught what it was. Naria lay on the floor, a shotgun pressed against the back of her head. James stood there, another gun focused on John.'You fucks! Do you think I'm fucking kidding you! I want my damned money back!'John glances down at Naria and drops his gun, his blade still in his hand. 'The blade too, Brian... or whoever the fuck you are.' John complies and places the blade on the ground slowly. Suddenly five silenced shots ring out. James twitches with each, his face telling a story of pain, hatred, and death. Death's sickle strikes again... five spots of crimson form on the front of James' shirt, quickly joined by a creamy silver liquid. John sighs a sign of relief as James drops to the side, his head smacking into the floor and causing a sickening crack. Blood trickles from between his lips. Standing in the doorway that leads to James' office is Maverick Reno, twin glocks in hand. 'Looked like you could use a little help, John.' John is quick to grab the weapons and toss them to Maverick. John grabs Naria and pulls her over his shoulder, carrying her out of the building. 'We have no time for small talk.' He yells out, 'Deac! Start the car!' The three quickly make their way to the vehicle and get inside. Before them, the building remains in one piece. Surrounding it, flames and smoke. It wouldn't take long for the police to arrive... There exit is quick. A black van pulls up quickly and men in blue suits rush into the building. The sedan pulls away and starts moving down the road. In the rearview mirror, you can see men carrying bodies from inside and placing them in back of the van. A moment passes and the van pulls off. The entire area is cleaned of any evidence within twenty minutes. Every body is removed. Within the hour, the Echelon has replaced documents at City Hall with the documents that John and Naria had James sign; they're official and carry the signature of James Frock. It gives ownership of the building, the barges, and everything else included to John Zero. Within two days, a replacement is found for James Frock... his name is Mark Postan. The papers are quickly signed to his name. The fires are explained as, 'malfunctions with a fuel canister and a misplaced cigarette.' The Echelon never visited Wilmington... 'See, that's the different between TUE and MOB. You look at a group like us, and then a group like the MOB. At first you see a lot of similarity; parallels here and there. But then you look deeper, closer. There is a difference. Style, finesse, critical thinking, intelligence. That is the difference. The MOB uses pure brute force to get what they want; they harass the weak, those unable to defend themselves. We, on the other hand, have much bigger fish to fry. Simply breaking the door down and pointing a gun would not work for us; no. No, we have to use much different means to convince those we work with. Be it visible or be it psychological, we know what to do and how to do it. We know where to go and when to go there. We know how business is done and we know exactly what we're doing.'
