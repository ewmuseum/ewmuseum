Rage|Power Pt. 1

13 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01-13-05 “Rage” Power?” The music pounds throughout the warehouse; a large crowd cheers and stomps along to the beat, losing themselves in the music. The darkness of the warehouse keeps the anonymity of each individual; no one feature on any one person is entirely distinguishable. Not even the red and white flashing lights from above help reveal the identity of these people. In this place known for drugs, scandal, sex, crime, and violence, darkness becomes a friend. That is why she is here. For Aradia Xiomara, darkness is not a friend, but a very intimate lover. She embraces it, allows it to consume her very being. She sits alone, in a large black chair, completely comfortable and content. While the music moves the people dancing and stomping, she allows it to move her in a different way. The adrenaline of the music pumps through her veins, a rush of anger and calm fusing and intertwining like lover’s fingers inside her soul. Tonight is not a night where she needs to dance in order to feel the anger and hate. She can feel it on her own. In the darkness of the warehouse, in her own corner, she watches the crowd. Between her fingers, she twirls a dagger, which cannot be seen by anyone else. The point of the blade presses into her forefinger and then slips to the next. Bored with her little game, she brings the dagger to her temple and rests the point against it, then continues her twirling. The blade winds strands of her long black hair around the dagger and so she twists it the other way to release her nightshade locks. As she closes her eyes, a small smile creeps across her blackened lips. She shivers and the smile quickly goes away. Her dark brown eyes open to find a man standing across from her. Squaring her eyes, Aradia looks the man over in judgment and decision. Six foot, at least, with shaggy black hair that falls in strands around his beautiful brown eyes. He wears a black dress shirt, completely unbuttoned to expose his toned physique. She squirms in her seat and then smiles lightly at the man. Slowly, she brings the dagger from her temple and drags it along her cheek, until it reaches her blackened lips, careful to not catch the blade on her silver lip ring. Aradia sticks her tongue out slowly and then drags the blade along it. The man looks at her, desire wild in his eyes. Aradia smiles, a pool of white emerging from behind the wall of black. She stands, tucking the dagger into the fold of her black peasant skirt, shredded to reveal her back cargo pants underneath. The man’s gaze falls from her mysterious and exotic face down her body to the red corset she wears over a barely-there black tank top. He stares as she gets closer, leaving her coat and belongings behind her on the chair. The red lights strobe quicker as she presses up against him, one hand falling onto his bare flesh. With her finger, she traces a line down his torso and then loops her finger into his pants and pulls him closer. Dominance is obviously part of her game. Her raven hair falls around her shoulders; she whips it to one side and presses her face against his. Their black strands mesh and she slowly presses her lips against his, two sets of black mixing to become one. She pulls away and their eyes meet. His black eye make up runs in black streams down his face, smeared into beads of sweat. Her make up, however, is perfect. Black eyeliner drawn heavily around her eyes, with black tears drawn down to her dark red cheeks. Now closer to her, the man can see just how exotic she is, in her own American kind of way. Her skin, while pale, is smooth and clear. Her face and ears are littered with piercings: one in her eyebrow, one in her nose, one in her lip, and one in her tongue. She was careful to make that piercing known when she dragged her dagger across it. Her ears are a mess of piercings, all different colors, shapes, and sizes. Around her neck she wears a black chocker with red beads tied in sporadically. He was mesmerized by her beauty. By no means is she a small girl, her muscles are well defined and she’s slightly taller than most other girls, but her beauty lies in her confidence and appearance. There was something mysterious about this girl and he was determined to figure it out. Suddenly, he found himself being manhandled. She grabs onto his dress shirt and swings him around, pushing him back into her chair. Still in shock, the man backs up as she climbs on top of him, looking at him with anger and hate. Pushing his hair to the side, she kisses his forehead forcefully and then kisses him on the lips again. Pulling her coat off of the arm of the chair, she smiles at him one last time and walks away, leaving him dazed and confused. He brings a hand up to his forehead, where a black heart-shaped print is now stamped. She puts her black leather duster on as she walks away, her boots stomping heavily on the floor. Quickly she opens her hand and sees the wallet she grabbed from his pocket. Another, more malicious smile creeps onto her face and she tucks the wallet away into her coat pocket. As she begins to exit the warehouse, another man catches her eye, standing by the door. A typical new face, unsure of his surroundings, huddled in the corner with a drink, but beautiful in his own right. His hair is cut short and dyed fire engine red, spiked and tussled in every direction possible. The black t-shirt he wears clings to his body, showing every muscle and bringing out his most attractive features. On top of this he wears a mesh long sleeve shirt. But leather pants? She still finds him attractive regardless. At least the pants aren’t skin tight. She stops and looks at the man and bites her lip, feeling for her dagger. A new song comes on and she begins to move a little, her skirt flowing around her in waves of black. Finally, his attention turns to the dark beauty before him. They lock eyes and she squints, smelling his fear. As usual, she would take the initiative. She quickly moves forward and grabs him by the arm. Alarmed, he drops his drink and it shatters on to floor. Another smile crosses her lips as she pushes the doors open and drags him out of the warehouse. As the doors close behind her, the music continues, but no one seems to notice the man searching frantically for the beauty who just so happened to escape with his wallet. No one can notice much of anything, because darkness is everywhere.
