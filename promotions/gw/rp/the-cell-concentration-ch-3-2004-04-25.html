The Cell: Concentration (Ch.3)

25 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 25, 2004 Prologue: Given heroin, Jesse had a choice to make; a choice that may alter his life completely as did a sentence did a long time ago that was spoken from a doctor. This choice though was one Jesse could control; unlike the words he heard that were stated as were and could not be changed by Jesse. So what could Jesse do? Would he do the right thing? Or would he end up doing something he’d later regret? Story Continues. Jesse pulled the needle out and tossed it into the conditioner bottle. He didn’t have the guts to do it, whether those guts were good or bad, he didn’t care, because all he knew was he wasn’t doing the drugs. Lying down he thought out loud about life in general, and how it’d become a mockery of the old days. Everyone thought life was so easy back then, with the work being so less complicated, and you could never get in trouble for throwing a fist. Yet people thought wrong. The older days of life when Jesse grew up were over, and they were rough. Some people couldn’t live without knowing that their cornfields were plowed and their garden was protected from the poor neighbors. Life in the sixties wasn’t easy nor was it fun as a child. All Jesse could remember were hippies and the Beatles, some of the most memorable things to him that he hated the most. Maybe that’s why he didn’t turn out like all the rest of the people he knew his age. A free spirit, but then again why be a free spirit when you could live free and do what you want, yet still be under guidelines? It’s odd how so many can think one thing, and then one person comes in and gives their opinions and throws the whole place into chaos. Just like Juvian Ramorez and what he thought of Jesse. Everybody before Juvian had believed they figured the man Jesse Jamester out, even though he’d proved them all wrong this man, this young kid so to speak, Juvian Ramorez, believed he was going to stop Jamester. To Jamester that was funny, yet at the same time a challenge in its own self. When someone is that determined to prove you wrong they usually have something up their sleeve, and that was what Jesse wanted, a little bit of controversy. When he got warmed up for a match Jesse just wanted to show he had the juice to beat the crap out of someone, because through all his mental sick images he produced he knew he could and would in his heart. A twisted man with the desire of a young child wanting to be the very best at what he did on a sports team would be the easiest way to describe Jesse. Yet Jesse was thirty-five and had more desire than a young kid, for this wasn’t just about wrestling, it was about his life. To be beat by someone like Juvian was an insult, not only that but like a plague being spread around by kisses. It was just uncommon and would never happen, and so as Jesse laid their he knew this would be the end of his little joy trip, and that come Monday April 26, 2004, Jesse Jamester would have to unleash a new person upon the one Juvian Ramorez. Distorted Beliefs Inside! I will cry like never before, for when I beat his skull into the ground his face will be disfigured past the recognition of knowing who he was. When I snap I’ll see myself in a mirrored sense stabbing the inner demons with the sword of hope, until finally… I’ll sacrifice my own life to be born again. Giving into what has got me Bleeding, claustrophobic, scarred Severed me from all emotion Life is just too fucking hard SNAP! Your face was all it took Cuz this need ain't doin' me no good Fall on my face, but can't you see? This fucking life is killing me! It’s okay they say, I’m fine like this, though I don’t know how that could be. Inside my head I sit and breathe and ponder and wonder where I’d be. Theirs a life on the outside of the ring, but beyond that door I cannot see. Without a pair of eyes to know what that life is like I fear my existence and stay where I’m comfortable. Life’s too short for me to care now, so I’ll just ram the door down with my opponents skeleton before showing him how to kick his own ass, and prove he’s no match for a man in a mask… Tearing me / INSIDE Crippled from what I once thought I knew life could be sweet but never thought so nice as this. As I asked upon a windy day to a neighbor who wanted to be alone all day; How do you be all you can be, when you don’t know if you’re whole anyway? His answer was unclear, and as the wind blew so did his words out of his ass, I smiled and said, okay thanks, and ignored him as I went to the shed. Six hours later I had buried his ass, he’d proven to be a whole lot of bullshit after the first few swings, and so it proved to be, he was nothing but a worthless man in a skin he never deserved. Too far gone, I'm catatonic Leaving you to criticize Empty shell and running naked All alone, lobotomized Back and forth between my hang-ups It isn't easy to be hated Where do ya go? What do ya do? Simpleton, impromptu - crazy eight I never cared - not once - gotta get away Tripping would be the lose term of what I’d done. I wasn’t going insane I was having fun. If they couldn’t understand that then who were they to question me? I’m not a psycho, I just think blood should be the only color of the rainbow. Splash some here, and then some there, until it’s exactly how it was when I hit that old lady with my car and she went through the windshield. Given the death of any man, I’d be able to tell you a flaw within their hands. Whether it be their instincts, phobias, wants, needs, or even their life, each one proved to be a unworthy of being alive, and so when I ended them… I made a life that would return to this world in hope of changing. Tearing me / INSIDE I wasn't promised anything You keep mocking me But you will never again Before you know it - after you're gone As I would say on any normal day, this is the new me, and this is what I will be. Call me a prick, call me a dick, I’m just changing to fit in with your friend who wouldn’t care a lick. Spit on me, spit on you, it’s a chain of what happens and you’ll die if you don’t know how to guide your way to the end of it. GONE! On April 26, 2004, Jesse Jamester will be reborn. Expect the Unexpected, for this is a new era in his realm of wrestling… (To Be Continued…)
