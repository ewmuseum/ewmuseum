Quest

1 May 2004

Date: May 1 The scene opened up to my empty bedroom. I was sitting on my bed, wiping the tears away from my face. I looked at the photos one more time and then dropped them to the floor. They were really thin paper so they kind of floated to the ground. I’ve never been so miserable in my life, except for the time, when I was about 5, when my mother told me that my father had died. I didn’t know that till I was 5. Suddenly, in my head, a whole bunch of photos started coming up in my head. T was of the car crash. The car just drove straight into a semi, my father was in the car. Another came in of Roz kidnapping my mother, and sending her to the tigers cage. I started to sweat. Another came in of my mother saying her last few words, before she died. All of them started flashing in my head. “These demons, they are controlling me!” I thought. All the images kept spinning around in my head. I started sweating even more. I wiped my forehead, I haven’t had a flashback since I was 8. I didn’t even know what was happening, I closed my eyes and saw all of these evil images. I opened my eyes, the images faded away from me. I just sat there and wiped the sweat off my face. “I can’t get them out of my head!” I said again, but said it louder. The images came back into my head. I don’t even know how they keep popping up in my head. It feels like someone did something to me. I had all these fears I didn’t even know where to start. They were controlling me and I didn’t even know what to do about it. I clenched my fists and got off the bed. I started walking around my bedroom, still sweating. I needed someone to help me, someone to help me overcome these fears, someone to overcome these demons. I needed someone who could help me with all of this. And I think I just found it. “This man will help with every thing. He will help me overcome everything that has been bothering me. He is like a messenger from god, who was sent to help me with all of my fears and demons.” I said aloud. This man, he was charged for something he did not do. He was charged for killing his girlfriend, but he didn’t do it. The police took him into jail, and he is still there. This man has been in numerous federations, mostly the big ones. This man was against Tristan Belmont in the LOW World Title match. He came so close to winning, but did not pull this off. That’s when he left to come here, come here to Global Wrestling. He has been a big star here since he got here, even got a chance at the GW World Title. That man was... “I was finally going to let out who the mystery partner was. He was going to help me overcome everything.” I said. That man was, Jesse Jamester. My mind switched to some pictures of Jesse when he was in LOW. When he was in all of his feds. He won most of them. But, I needed to find him, I needed to talk to him just to see if everything was going alright. “Where would he be?” I asked myself. I got off the bed once more and walked out of my bedroom. I was going to goto the jail Jesse was being kept inside. I was going to talk to him. We were going to be partners, and we were going take the GW Tag Team Titles from a whole lot of other people. Or teams. I walked down the steps as they creaked after I stepped on each one. I walked out of the door and out into the world, for my quest to Jesse Jamester. I walked down the little sidewalk as the scene started to fade.
