Thug 101

25 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/25/04 Train sits at the open window, blowing the blunt smoke out into the empty alley. Even through the haze he intentionally brought on through the weed, meant specifically to dull the doubts and reluctance, nagging thoughts of his 'new life' still swirled through his mind. He knew what he was about to do and couldn't help, but remember that this was exactly the reason he'd come to Global...to avoid the shit that was going to have to go down for him to stay in Global. The thoughts pissed him off honestly. It's not like this was anything new. Some prick tries to play hard ball, he handlse it. Simple as that. Why should he be mad at himself? He didn't start this. That dumb McCoy bitch stepped outta line and he put her back in. Simple as that. It was supposed to be anyway. Now this McNasty moron had taken it to a place it was never meant to go. Even all the destruction on Evolution was inside the confines of the arena. Now? Now it had gone to where Train had wanted to avoid. It had gone back to the street. Stupid bastards. Why couldn't they have pulled this blackmail shit with someone who would just lay down and take it? Why did they have to try this with someone who knew how to nip the shit in the bud? Someone like Train? Fuck it. Wasn't no sense in whining about it. Train made his play, the shit didn't work out right, now he had to clean up the mess. And it was definitely as simple as that. He should be happy. The shit was ridiculously simple. It was pretty obvious these motherfuckers didn't know how to play this game. Hell, they had called right from the bar. Who the fuck does some stupid shit like that?! Everybody knows you don't call from the spot! You find a pay phone or get a cloned cell... that was Thug 101 right there. The name of the bar had shown up right on his caller ID. Didn't take but two seconds to type that shit into the computer and come up with an address. It could only have been easier if the idiots had given him their home addresses and social security numbers. Once he found it Train had done a quick surveilance run by he bar. The sign on the door said they closed at midnight. As Train took another toke, holding in the thick smoke, he saw it was 11:55 now. It was all going according to plan. The gloves were on so there'd be no finger prints and he was almost surprised at how easy it was to get in. The tactical knife slid easily between the window and cill, barely knocking off any of the chips of worn paint. It only took one smooth flick of the wrist pop the lock on the inside of the window and Train was in. He climbed down into what, according to the stand up urinals, was the Men's bathroom of the Drunken Leprechaun. The blunt was pre rolled so all he had to do was snap the Zippo open and he was business. 'Alright, closing time!' Train heard the husky voice call out in the front room. That was his cue. Train quickly put the blunt out and placed it into a small plastic baggy before putting the baggy in the pocket of his black leather jacket that matched the rest of his outfit. He had noticed the Glade airfreshener on the window cill as he climbed in so he grabbed it quickly and filled the air with 'Meadow Mist' before closing the window and steping into one of the stalls. Train puts his feet up on the urinal just as the bathroom door swings open. 'Closing up, time to go home', the same husky voice stated plainly. The man waits for a response, but of couse none comes. Click. Out go the lights and the door closes with a thud. There's enough light coming through the window to manuver safely as Train steps down and out of the stall. Train stands in the dark, the organish illumination from the street light outside shooting in through Train's entrance to the building. Outside he could already hear the engines starting, preparing to pull away. Train checked the time again. 12:05. So what? Another.....20 minutes or so? Yeah, the staff should be gone. The only one left would be the manager. Now if McNasty was gonna call right from this dumps phone, the manager must be this Harv cat. The one who thinks he can just gamble and never have to pay up. Mmmmhmmm... This was gonna be a lesson learned. ~~~~~20 minutes later~~~~~ The Drunken Leprechaun had probably never heard bounced like it was doing now. The bass from 'Damn' by The Youngbloodz rattled the whole place coming from the stereo underneath the bar. The lyrics said it all for the man who now sat there at the half darkened dark wood counter. 'If you don't give a damn, we don't give a fuck! If you don't give a damn, we don't give a fuck! If you don't give a damn, we don't give a fuck! Dont' start no shit, it won't be no shit! Dont' start no shit, it won't be no shit! Dont' start no shit, it won't be no shit!' Train knew it must've been a hell of a sight for poor Harv. He knew it was him from the name tag. He knew it was a surprise from the look on his face. Imagined being in a building where you've probably done business for years. You know it like the back of your hands. Been there alone at night a million times. Now all of the sudden, in a building that was supposed to be empty you're unexpectedly summoned back into the room to the trully unfriendly sound of one of Lil' John's biggest hits. It's gotta suck. As Harv stepped into the room Train sits at the bar, using the drink gun he has pulled over the counter top, shoots ice and then coke into his glass. Train tosses the hose back behind the bar and pours a generous slug of Crown Royal into the coke. Harv- Hey! What the hell are you doing?! Train looks at him puzzled for a moment. Harv looks as though he's going to demand clarification again, but Train throws up his hand, motioning for him to wait a moment. Train reaches over and grab a small remote control. The music dies down to a level more accomodating to conversation. Train- What you say? I couldn't hear you over the music man. *Harv walks over to the end of the bar* Harv- We've been closed for half an hour! I don't know who you are, but... Train- You don't know who I am?! Maaaaaaan... Now that hurts dogg. I thought we was friends and shit man! I mean that shit really stings. Harv- Look, pal... You may think this is funny, but I'm not laughing. Now, I'm giving you to the count of ten to get the hell out of here before I call the cops! *Pause* Train- You really don't know who I am do you? You an unobservant motherfucka son. Tell you what there Harv... You wanna call the cops? You do what you think you got to do son. But understand this. You pick up that God damn phone this friendly conversation is going to the next level...down. Harv- Oh, is that so? Thinking about it Train already knew what was going to go down. He knew Harv was going to pick up, not only the phone, but bat right next to it underneath the far end of the bar. Hell, he didn't really blame him. This is his house. It's been violated. That snotty little bitch McCoy had done the same thing to Train. That's what started all of this. She'd come into his home, disrespected him. He had no choice but to check her. Now Harv would have to do the same thing in the Leperechaun that Train had in his home. Problem was... Harv was having his space violated by something he wasn't prepared for. A man who'd boilded violating people into an art form. True to Train's prediction, Harv moved behind the bar and picked up the bat. He slammed the harnded wood club down on the dark bartop. Harv- Like you said, do what you think you have to. It was all confidence making up his voice in that statement. Harv wasn't some little punk, easily intimidated. Train respected that in away. But it pissed him off more than it impressed him. Harv had to know Train wasn't playing games. Whether he recognized him or night when you clear out the building and you come back in somebody's playing their own music and helping themselves to your shit you should know instinctively that man is nothing to be played with. Now this son of a bitch was going to stand there and talk shit? Motherfucker.... Somewhere inside Trained grinned a little when Harv picked up the phone to carry out his threat of calling the police. Train watched as the confidence wained sharply. The moment the phone cord whipped up and revealed the clean cut that seperated the reciever from the base. Train- Yo boy McNasty... He got you into some serious shit here man. I'd start looking for a better class of friend if I were you. But see, I know you didn't really know what you was getting yourself into so I'm gonna ask you one time, and I do mean ONE time real nice... Where's the tape? Harv- Tape? Train- Did I stutter? Oh and not the tape you had hooked up to yo little security cameras that I'm sure you thinkin' is gonna ID me for the cops. No, no, no... I already got that one. Naw, I'm talking about the tape from Evolution. I know you have it... Where is it? *Pause* Harv- ahahahahahaha.... Holy shit. So you're that Train kid, huh? Boy you know, you actually scared me there for a second! hahaha... Man... So what are you doing here, huh? What? Are you gonna put me in a little arm bar? Drop toe hold maybe? Get the hell out of here before I introduce you to my buddy Mr. Bat. And I can already tell Mr. Bat? He don't like your attitude. Train- You're reading this shit all wrong son. This is your one and only shot to get out of this shit without an ending that requires a wheelchair. Harv- Oh really? Well, seeing as I'm the one with the bat... if there is a tape, give me one good reason I should hand it over to you? Train- Muthafucka... You keep playing games I'ma give you 15 of 'em. Harv- haha... 15? Train- Yeah... Train stands and pulls a Glock 9mm from his waist, simultaneously pulling back the barrel, loading a round into the chamber. Train- 14 in the clip, 1 in the pipe. Do the math bitch. That was it, the moment Train was waiting for. Used to be he lived for that moment. That second when the mighty fall. When the cat who thinks he's got it all under control crumbles inside. Train hadn't even raised the gun towards him, but Harv was crumbling. No matter how he tries to remain calm, the panic in his eyes speaks louder than he can drown out. Harv- Whoooooooa... whoa, whoa, whoa, whoa... Ok...ok, this... this is... Look, this is going way outside... I mean.... You're taking this wrestling stuff a little far aren't you? Train- First off, drop the bat. *Harv Reluctantly tosses the bat away.* Train- Second of all... You got this shit twisted. See, this ain't about wrestling no more. Naw, this is about muthafuckas over stepping their bounds. See Christina McCoy, the one yo boy McNasty wants you to give that tape to? She over stepped her bounds. See you don't just come up in a man's home and disrespect him. I had to let her know she wasn't gonna get away with that shit. But see, that didn't have shit to do with that little bitch buddy of yours. He should've kept his nose out of it. See if he wanted to settle the score with me for whuppin' his monkey ass back in that tag match, he should've been a man about it. He should've just waited for his little shot at the pay per view. If he really thinks he's all that he should've handled his muthafuckin' business like a man, toe to toe! Oh, but no. Naw, he thinks he's gonna turn me into some little bitch. He thinks he can hold this fucking tape over my head and I was just gonna roll over and take that shit?! This ain't prison and my name ain't Tjaun muthafucka! It's Train you son of a bitch! Naw son... Yall is the ones that took this shit beyond wrestling. Yall's the ones that wanted to play. Only problem is... My name is Train and I ain't the muthafucka to be played with. Harv- Look, just walk out of here right now and I won't call the cops. You can still avoid going to jail here. Train- I'm the one with the gun and you think you gonna give me ultimatums? Harv- Look pal... If you're half as smart as you think you'll walk away. There are businesses up and down this street still open. If you pull that trigger they'll all hear the shot. Train- Wow.... That's a good point. Train reaches behind his back again, retrieving a silencer which he twists onto the barrel of the gun. Train- Good lookin' out dogg. No more snappy conversation son. Where's that God damn tape? *Pause* Harv- I don't have it. Train- You don't have it? That's real funny cuz that ain't what Donny boy said. Harv- Yeah well... I did have it but I sent it to that McCoy lady already. Train- You sent it to GW? *Pause* Really? So how'd you get it there? I mean McNasty called me about 9. Ain't no messengers running and the fedex guy ain't come over here at that time of night unless he's ready to knock a few back. Harv- I... I... Train- Muthafucka!!! They say when the end of your life is coming you see it flash before your eyes, but Harv had no such experience. Train was across the room too fast for even a flash. No, the only flash was the one of light after the butt of Train's Glock connected with his temple. There wasn't even time to brace himself and now, as he lay sprawled on the floor of his own bar, he could only assume the warmth he felt dripping down his face was blood. Train- You think this is a game?! You think this is a fucking game?! You think I'm playing with yo bitchass?! Muthafucka... Train's left hand locks on to a tuft of hair on the back of Harv's head, yanking the half stumbling man to his feet. With a ferosity not displayed in the ring as of yet Train shoves Harv through the set of swinging double doors by the bar and leads him back into the kitchen area. Train whips his head around searching for something as Harv tries to focus his blurry eyes. It doesn't take long before finds what he's looking for. Train marches Harv over to the stove and knees him in the gut. Harv drops to his knees, clutching his stomach, looking as though he may throw up. Train twist the knob on the front burner of the gas stove. flame shoots right up. The flame's light illuminates most of the dark kitchen and reflect in Train cold eyes. Train had forgotten how easy this was. How easy it was for him to cause pain. Deep down it had always scared him. Not the pain he caused...but how much he enjoyed causing it. The buzz was starting to wear down. The mix of adrenaline and not knowing where to get the good shit, being so new in town, Train needed another toke. He calmly reaches into his jacket pocket and pulls out the little baggy. It doesn't take a second for Train to free the half smoked blunt from it's cell. Train quickly puts the blunt over the flame and reignites the cherry. Soon the tip of the blunt is glowing red, smoking billowing into the air as he takes a toke. Slowly the high creeps back and Train is ready for the next step. Train tucks the Glock behind his back, and, with the blunt between his lips snatches Harv back to his feet again. Before he knows it Harv's face is uncomfortably close to the burning eye. Train- I heard your name is Hardcore, right? Harv- Y...yeah.... Train- Well, muthafucka your new name is gonna be french fry if you don't tell me where the hell that tape is! Harv- OK!!! Ok, ok, ok... L...l...look... The tape... it's in the safe underneath the bar! Train- Underneath the bar? Harv- I swear to God! I swear to God... please....please man don't do this. I'll get it for you right now, swear to God. Train- Son, if you're lying to me... Harv- No, no... I swear I'm not lying. Please.... There is a tense silence for a moment. Suddenly Train shoves Harv back towards the door as he pulls the Glock out again. Train- Move. Slowly. It's hard looking over your shoulder while heading forward. Even when you know where you're going, in a tense situation you're so busy concentrating on what's behind you, you have a hard time knowing what's in front of you. Train knew that's why Harv almost tripped 3 times on the short trip from the kitchen back out to the front room and the now infamous bar. No doubt Harv was feeling unlucky right at this moment, but frankly he was quite lucky. McNasty had tried to play Thug games with a true O.G. If it had been some amateur instead of Train there's no doubt Harv would have had a bullet in the back of his head by now with all the herky jerky shit he was doing as Train followed behind him. McNasty had put his friend in danger, not knowing who he was playing with. Luckily, in the midst of an 'unfortunate' situation, he had been playing with a professional. Finally they at there destination. Both men were behind the bar as the stereo continues to play in the background. The track on the cd Train had moved to the next track, the evil sounding drone of 'Eye for an Eye' by Mobb Deep drones in the background, adding to the tension. Harv stood in front of the small metal safe beneath the bar while Train places himself about 5 feet behind him, blocking the only exit to the bar enclosure besides leaping over the bar itself. Harv slowly kneels in front of the safe. The second his hand touches the safe's dial, he feels a cold, circular piece of metal press itself to the base of his skull. Train- Now... If you smart you probably got some heat in there. But I'm letting you know right now. You do something stupid, you're headed for a closed casket service. Harv- Take it easy. There's no gun. I'm gonna reach in real slow and get your tape. That's all. Train- It better be. Harv turns the dial. The tumblers slowly fall into place and the safe is open. True to his Harv, very slowly reaches into the save and retrieves a mini DV tape. Harv holds it up and has it snatched from his hand. Train- Where the copies at? Harv- No, no, no... I swear, there are no copies. That's it. Please... I...I... I was just doing a favor for a friend, ok?! I don't have any copies. That evil drone seems to be amplified by his terror. Train had to catch himself. He knew what the next move should be, his finger caressing the the trigger. No. Fuck this was too easy. Too easy to fall right back into this shit. Too easy to fall in love with the rush. The point was to escape this. His finger eased off the trigger. Train- Close it and stand up. The relief is palpable, cutting the tension with a knife. Train reaches up and ejects his cd into his hand, bringing the situation down another notch. Harv slams the safe shut and spins the dial for good measure. Train has already stepped out from behind the bar and grabbed the bat. As Harv raises up to eye level the Glock is tucked, but Harv's own weapon still looms in the midst of an unresolved situation. Train approaches slowly. Train- Give me your wallet. Harv- Huh? Train- Don't do it. Don't fucking play. You heard what the fuck I said. Harv knows the right thing. He reaches into his back pocket and tosses his wallet to Train. Train tucks the bat under his arm and starts going through the leather binder. Finally has what he was apparently looking for... A driver's liscense. Train- Damn... That's a nice picture. You probably the only cat in the world with a good DMV picture man. 2120 Walnut Creek Avenue.... That your house? Harv-(irritated) Yeah. Train- Tell you what. I'm just about out of your hair here. Only, there are a few folks I don't think need to know about this little situation. Christina McCoy...and the cops. Now if word of our lil' meeting here tonight should visit them? I'm coming to visit you. We clear? Harv- Crystal. Train- Cool. Train tosses the wallet, all it's other contents still in place, back to Harv who tucks it back into his pocket. Train- Aight, one more thing dogg. See, yo boy McNasty tried to use you to deliver a message to me. Now I'm gonna return the favor. Harv- Fine. What do you what me to tell 'em. Train- Sorry dogg, it ain't that simple. Harv- What are you talking about? Train- I'm talking about actions spitting game louder than words. *Pause* Put you hand on the bar. Harv- Ahhh, c'mon man! You said we were done! Train- I said we were almost done. See I can't walk out of here without making it clear a brotha don't play. Now back in the day leaving your brain on the floor just a second ago would've been my version of a telegram. I know you're just stuck in the middle of this shit and all, but the game is the game and it don't change for players just cuz they don't know the rules. Your hand on the bar...or your head on the wall. It's on you playboy. The inevitability of it all makes Harv's head sink, his eyes focusing on the floor for a moment. But it quickly rises back to Train's eye level. The defiance written on Harv's face garners a small grin from Train. He's got heart. I can respect that. Harv rolls up his sleeve and puts his lesser used left hand on the bar. Train moves up. Train- You tell yo' boy McNasty to remember two things. Keep your nose out of my business and your hands off what belongs to me. And my business? It belongs to me! Harv braces himself. He already knows it's coming, but there's really no preparing... WHAM/SNAP!!! The bat sails through the air and makes contact, the snapping of bones sickeningly obvious. Harv screams, but only for a moment. Train quicly reaches over the bar and grabs another tuft on Harv's head. The hair sails down towards the bar top, Harv's skull following and landing with a loud thud. The impact demonstrates the principals of physics. Inertia and returned force. Harv's head bounces violently off the bar and sends his body barreling backwards into the shelving behind the bar. Bottles fall and toppel to the ground right after Harv. Train tosses the bat back behind the bar. He quickly heads back towards the mens bathroom to turn his original entry way into an exit as the scene fades to black.
