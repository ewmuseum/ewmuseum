Path to redemption

1 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/1/05 With each drop from the heavens above, his hair becomes that little bit heavier, the ground becomes that little bit muddier, and the air becomes a little bit colder. However, it doesn't matter, for nothing ever stops Salaok from making his treck to see his loved ones. His family who had left him behind to face a world full of corruption, pain and death. But he for one could never complain about the world turning into such a state, because he was one reason for it getting that way. The Lair was all powerful throughout the late ninties and the first parts of the twenty first century, However, recently their power had fallen away do to members, or at least former members of the assosiation had turned their backs on The Lair and fought against them. There had only been two deaths during this war, but it was enough to cause such pain and suffering that it put one of The Lair's high ranked members out of commission as there was nothing he wanted to do more than to sit in his house and let the world go by without him. Not anymore though. That had had passed and Salaok had grown but not in the ways in which the world might have hope he would have. So many people thought the death of his wife and child would end up showing Salaok the path that he has chosen to be apart of is clearly the wrong one to be one. However, he's too stubborn for that. He had chosen his path, and now he would live it out till the very end. An end which he knew would be one that would be as bloody as the way in which he had lived. As he walks through the rain, his hair now completely drenched, pulls down and forms a blanket over his face of dripping black mess but it doesn't bother him. Nothing seemed to bother him. Each step he takes brings him one step closer to his family. A family he hadn't seen for so long, and longed to hold them once again. However, in this place of grey and lifelessness there would be no holding of his family. No touching of skin. No smelling of sweet perfume or the sound of laughter from the youth. The only laughter here would be that of the owls watching down on him. The only smell would be that of the dirt under his feet, and the only touch of skin he would feel was when his hands would cover his face as his tears fell like the rain. For each step that Salaok takes, brings him one step closer to the graves of his family. Lisha Davis, his beloved wife, best friend and mother of his child. Darrell Davis, his son, his future, his hope. Both were gone and as Salaok arrives at their sites, his face goes cold and no emotion is shown. If only he hadn't let this happen, he would have been able to excape from this world of crime and murder like he had wanted to, but no, now he was stuck in it for life. Just as his family were stuck in perfectly matches coffins six foot underground only two feet away from each other. Kneeling between the two of them, his pants becoming covered in the mud in which they rest as his hand reaches up and touches his forehead. The world, 'Ateh' rolls off his tongue before his hand moves down and touches the bottom of his ribs and lets, 'Mulkuth' billow out into the cold winters air. As his hand comes up to touch his right shoulder, a tear drips down his face and mixes in with the billions of drops of rain which scatter over everything they touch. His hand finally reaches his shoulder, swallowing his releases the word, 've-Geburah' and then drags his hand over his chest to the other shoulder and speaks, 've-Gedulah' as forst spews forth from his lungs. Finally, clasping his fist into a ball on his chest, he speaks softly and sounds almost defeated. 'le-Olahm, Amen.' Salaok remains on his knee in the mud as his eyes move from the rain drenched ground to that of his wife's tombstone. Although only a piece of stone placed on top of the grave site to mark who lay beneith, it acts more like a visual representation of the decease for loved ones to talk to. An act in which Salaok really had no problem with doing as he inhails a massive breath, trying to gain the strength to talk to the one who had made him strong for so much of his life. 'Lisha!' The word seemed to come out almost as a relief to be able to speak her name in such a place as it all flowed out with his breath. He seemingly waits for a moment, hoping that she would respond and place her hand on his shoulder in a comferting mannor. And yet there was none. Merely the sounds of the trees as the wind pushes them around like a bully to a smaller kid in the playground and the never ending impacts of the rain falling from the skies only to shatter on the ground. Tilting his head back to look up at the sky above, Salaok closes his eyes tightly as he attempts to gain control of his emotions and show strngth like he had prmosed to Lisha he would do. 'I've tried to do as you asked Lisha, but there are times when it all catches up to me and no matter what I do, I see you around be. I walk down the stairs at home and I see you waiting for me at the bottom with a smile to greet me and a voice so angelic that I feel as though I too have died. And I wish for that every day if I knew that I would be able to hold you in my arms again, and yet I know that can not happen as I have unfinished business here my darling. Not that when i die, I will be able to go where you are for my life has been one that can not be forgive and made up for no matter how many times I try.' Stopping silent, as if listening to someone, Salaok stares straight ahead and nods. As much as I want to see you my love, it's time for The Nevermore to rise again and this time there will be no power to stop us. My father is gone Lisha! I Infally did it. It was him all along who had caused The Lair such troubles but that will not happen anymore as he is six foot underground much like most of his students. I have heard that a few of them remain, but they will be dealt with in time.' No longer looking pained, Salaok seems to have taken great pride in the announcement of his father's death to his beloved wife like it is a huge achievement. Which for The Lair it would have been had they not just think that it was an act of Salaok's now uncontrollable rage. That was something that The Lair were going to need to get used to now, his time after his wife's death was enough to destroy Salaok's control as he sat int he mental ward for months on end. 'I saw Mani twice since I last came to visit you Lisha and I think now I am beginning to understand what you said to me. It was a while ago now when The Harvesters all split in different directions and we begun to attack each other but you remained strong as you always would and as we all sat around the table with our hatreds in hand, you spoke to us. You said that this war of The harvesters would make us stronger in the end, and I believe now you are right. I looked into my brothers eyes for the first time in over a year at my father's funeral and I think it's time to allow himself to be redeemed and for me to accept the hand which I have been dealt. You knew this day would come and that your death would somehow help that cause but I would give it all back to have you again. You accepted your death before most of us even knew that you weren't going to make it and for a breif moment as I looked at you on your hospital bed, I wanted to hate you for it.' Turning his eyes back up to the sky with a small smile pressed onto his lips. 'I could never hate you Lisha. One day my dear, I will redeem myself to the eyes of the world and join you in heaven. I hope.' ---- Everything was grey. It was just a continuation of grey no matter where you looked. Just like the mental ward had been white everywhere you looked. Only difference was, it was Salaok that was being held in this place of pain and torment for Salaok was too cunning to ever be arrested. However, the man who he was here to see about had been arrested more times than he has fingers and toes. Reaching into his pocket as he walks down the grey halls, over the grey floor, Salaok holds onto his wallet tightly, knowing just how much money was in there and knowing that every cent would be needed for what he was about to do. Turning the corner and coming to the desk in which he is needing, he pauses for just a split second which could have been taken as Salaok having a second thought. That never happens though. He comes to his decision and that's final. No one questions his choice, including himself. The conversation with the officer was meanless and full of words that Salaok simply doesn't find a need to pay attention to. However, the words he did hear would be like music to his ears. Chris Gothic Bail Released into your control. And then what would follow would change things forever, as Chris Gothic was eventually brought out to see who had bailed him out. Shock was written all of Gothic's face as it was not the Davis he had expected to see when he was told about this bail attempt. Ever since his first meeting with Chris Davis when he bailed Gothic out of jail in order to work for Freedom, Gothic had known about Salaok. Known about the war between the father and the son. Gothic, afraid of nothing walks straight up to Salaok and as Salaok turns they fall into stride, walking side by side for the first time ever. 'So why didn't your father bail me out this time?' Gothic asks, sounding suspisious of Salaok's motives, only to have a responce as cold as ice be fired back at him. 'He's dead.' Silence followed as the two men walked out of the grey and into the daylight, neither knowing what the other is thinking, and yet neither wanting to ask. 'According to one of the officers, this is your last chance to redeem yourself Gothic so in return for getting you out of there, I want you to come work for me.' Telling Gothic the terms of his release after Salaok has actually released him, doesn't give the young man much of a choice and yet still he had to ask why. 'And what does working for you get me?' 'For one, you'll never end up behind bars again as you'll have The Lair's protection and lawyers as long as you remain one of us.' Salaok answers back as he walks past another guard in the grounds who looks at him with despise before he then continues talking to Chris. 'However, there is something that must be said before you get in the car and drive away from this place forever.' 'Go on.' 'You may not have asked for this favor, but it's been given to you anyway. As long as you respect us and work for us, there will be no problems. Betray my trust young Gothic and you will not live to tell your story.' His warning firm and yet seemingly mocked by Chris. 'If you say so.'
