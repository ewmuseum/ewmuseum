Starting the Day

10 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/10... Saturday Deacon Holmes is seen slowly lurching across his frigid living room’s white shag carpet in a pair of boxers. He makes his way to the air conditioner each arm rubbing the opposite one for heat the whole way. After adjusting the temperature Holmes moves over to the window. He stands there for a moment allowing the warmth of the sunlight to wash over his body as he stretches before pulling the window’s dark curtains closed to block the brightness of the day. Holmes then shuffles over to the kitchen still attempting to escape the chill in the house. Once there Holmes opens the door and squints as the light shines in his eyes. He scans the shelves and then removes a St.Ides Special Brew, takes a sip, and then goes over to his coffee table to sit. After taking the bottle down halfway Holmes pushes it aside and puts his head down resting it on his arms. Just then the door busts open and there stands a jubilant CW clad in a loud Italian shirt, white slacks, and leather sandals, smoking a cigar as usual. He walks in shouting salutations and immediately goes to open all the windows Holmes had just closed before taking a seat with him at the coffee table. Holmes raises his head and surveys the room to find the windows once again allowing light into the house and CW puffing away on a cigar, he shakes his head before burying it once again in his arms. “I should have never given you a key.” comes out muffled by Holmes arms. He looks up again only to be met by CW’s extended middle finger and a puff of smoke blown directly into his face. “Asshole” he mutters with his head once again resting on his arms. CW gets up and walks into the kitchen to put a pot of coffee on, “Hung over again huh?” he inquires. “No...” CW turns Holmes an awkward eye. “Yes, but I paced myself this time.” Holmes adds. CW looks at him even harder. “Alright, alright, I didn’t, but you had to see the clientele I had in here last night.” Holmes gushes. “Really now?” says CW as he nudges Holmes causing him to uneasily shift in his chair. “Oh yeah, the chickens were definitely clucking.” he replies. CW places a cup of coffee on the table and slides it over to the dreary looking Holmes. “Drink up” he spouts off as he slaps the table to get Deacon’s attention. “And what was that shit about on Evolution” Burnin cars and such, what tha hell’s your problem?” CW rattles off. “I know you got a grudge against that Leviathan but Salaok too!” What were you thinking!” You’ve just gotten into a whole world of shit. “You remember what you used to tell me” A man’s the last thing you should fear, and I know Salaok is the man who signs my checks but he pays me to perform, not to like or respect him. I could give a fuck less how mad he gets. Bastard shorted me last month anyway...” Holmes states matter of factly. “So you burn his car?” asks CW. “Hell yeah, the only reason I’m not the best yet is cause I haven’t been killed yet and he’s got the nerve to short me on my check” Shit, he had it comin.” spouts off Holmes. With that said he downs the rest of his coffee and returns to the kitchen to scour the cabinets for food. CW chimes in. “Well on Monday night you’re gonna have it comin from all directions. Leviathan’s gonna be after you right along with Salaok, and I doubt you’ll find help from anyone.” “So what.” bellows Deacon from the kitchen searching the fridge now. “They know who I am and that’s all that matters, I’m D.Holmes, the D before Holmes stands for deez nuts and they can get each one, for free so feast up.” CW looks on in awe of his nephew’s arrogance, he sees flashes of himself before the retired from pro wrestling. “Oh yeah, them and that big redneck, shit Sage too if he wants it... I’m down to win those belts but I know he’s gonna fuck me the first chance he gets so I’m not gonna give him one. I’m gonna be watching the clown in my corner just as hard as the two nimrods that’ll be on the other side of the octagon and if he tries me I’ll bust both his lips that Nevermore shit just makes me sick. There is absolutely no doubt in my mind that when they all come together they are by far the biggest bitch menagerie ever assembled on the face of the earth. You’d think one of them would figure out that when you follow the pack you wind up dead last. And as far as Leviathan goes, how’s he gonna get there” I destroyed his car the same way I destroyed him after our match at Wrestlecade. What’s he gonna do walk? He’d better start now!” CW stands up and walks into the kitchen and begins to laugh. “I thought that ought to get you fired up, now hurry up and get some shorts on, we need to get to the gym. You’ve got two matches so that means I’m goin double time on your ass today, don’t think you gon get no breaks cause of that hangover neither!” Holmes looks at CW in disgust and shakes his head on the way to his room to get dressed. Just then two voices are heard echoing from the hall. “Deacon” Deacon?” Two girls walk out half naked covered only partially by bed sheets. They enter the front of the house to find Holmes on his way to the bedroom and CW leaning on the counter smoking a cigar. Deacon turns around to face CW, “Unc, meet the twins Lauren and Ashley” Holmes says as he walks past the two girls on the way to his room. They stand there half naked smiling and whispering about how much CW and Holmes look alike. “So you two are what kept my nephew out so late huh?” CW puffs his cigar as the two girls just stand there giggling like so many Lizzie McGuires. Holmes returns and ushers CW out the door, “I’ll see you two later...” he says to the girls as he flashes a smile on his way out the door. “And have somethin cooked in here when I come home!”
