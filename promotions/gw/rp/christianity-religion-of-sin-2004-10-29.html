? Christianity: Religion of Sin ?

29 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.29.04 A white robed figure, no shoes. Floats. Halo balanced yellow tipped, starlike and striped, octopus light from behind, and face like morning sunshine. Flexes wings and feathers fly. As hair golden glowing and decends, more powerful than the most powerful or so he thinks. And burns and melts as a whisp of match flame; a fleeting glance of yellow liquid crashing; Icarus and torch like at the stake. And decends into pit holes. And is the dark prince. And wreaks havoc and pestilence. So why begin? ? Christianity: Religion of Sin ? The rain came pouring down more than the day before. God's anger turn into a fury as the thunder streaked through the heavens like sharp stomach cramps. Your taboo, clich? depressing weather. Akuma engaged in a dramatic soliloquy, defeating all odds in his head repetitively making sure there is no one ahead of him competitively in the near future. Looking to destroy the holy power that mentally incarcerates the sheep that follow the Lord blindly into slaughter. Melodramatic memories and thoughts plagued Akuma's mind as he gazed out his laboratory window down into the nothingness that was mankind. 'Why... why do these people walk around like nothing is wrong? Trapped in God's matrix, controlled freedom. Those idiots work for the machines they build. Suffering from a technological disease; it's quite disgusting.' With his hands folded behind his back, Akuma thought out loud as heaven's tears cried down the window pane before him. Vision succumbed to momentary blindness as lightening stuck. Akuma now staring up into the truth that many were too blind to see and confused for clouds shook his head almost in shame. 'How I envy you. Working your evil through religion. You are the reason some of the most evil people do the things they do. They kill over people for you. For religious beliefs. Believing you'll set them free from a world you created. They must internally incinerate when they die only to reveal their death was in vain.' Smirking almost, Akuma returned his attention back to the peons that carried on God's life. Liquid scarlet fuel seeped from Itagaki's flesh wounds in a repeating sound pattern as he hung dormant from hooks in the distance behind Akuma. His image reflecting off the window Akuma stood before. Akuma glanced at the reflection of his creation of the perfect human before returning back to his awaiting thoughts. 'Christianity is the root of all evil. Forsaking it's own people. They are the oppressors they try to free you from. They fear people like me. Because they know I will start the revolution of the mind and regenerate their souls letting them experience how it feels to live outside of false pretenses.' Another lightening bolt interrupted Akuma's thoughts before he continued. 'Going to a church and praying about how good of a person you are doesn't make fortification. That's only self glorification. Debating justice in the cipher doesn't mean you'll be emancipated. Christianity isn't reformatory, it's only purgatory. No longer will the way I break the faith be understood for simple blasphemy. Just like I freed Itagaki, the whole world will see things my way. And then Christ will fall from his o so all mighty haven and realize there is a new regime.' A moment passed as Akuma concluded his monologue, 'Itagaki, the first solder of the Army of Pharaohs will recruit two more engaged souls on evolution. Let the blood of the innocent fall from the skies and cleanse the minds of the people. Let them see they really belong to me.'
