Bad start to a day

24 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Bad start to a day Ronnie Long Global Wrestling Promo #461 Date: 7-24-03 Location: Long's apartment It’s a funny thing about starting your day. What you do, between the time you roll out of bed, and ten minutes later when you’re fully awake, can determine your mood for the entire day. If you step off the bed and onto a quarter, and you’re 25 cents richer going to breakfast… well, 25 cents isn’t a whole lot of money, but it’s one fourth of a 20 oz soda, so that’s always good. On the other hand, if you step out of bed, and the thing under your foot is the cap from a beer bottle, and it cuts your instep with its razor sharp edges… well, then, as you stagger into the bathroom, bleeding slightly and swearing under your breath, you get the feeling that this day is gonna be one of those where you just wish you could crawl back under the covers and hide from. Of course, there’s lots of more subtle things than stepping on beer bottle caps. That, at least, is one of the things that will never happen to Ronnie Long. Aside from a taste for bourbon when he first joined the GWF in late 2000, Ronnie’s never been a drinker of alcohol. And even if he was, he wouldn’t have been drinking beer in his room. The thing on that, see, is that he has a girlfriend. One that lives with him, and shares not only his bedroom, but his bed, with him. So what does it mean, when you wake up in the morning, and your girlfriend isn’t sleeping there, but you can hear faint sobs from out in the living room? With a sense of foreboding, Ronnie Long stands up. Lingering in front of the wall mounted air conditioner for a few minutes, he pulls on boxers, a pair of jean shorts and a black T-shirt. With a yawn that nearly splits his head open, he heads out into the living room. Deanna D’aimore is standing there in the living room, her arms around the shoulders of Jeff Andrews, her head on his chest. Her shoulders shake as she sobs. Jeff himself looks more than a little bit awkward as he pats her on the back with one arm, the other one hanging limp at his side, as if he doesn’t know quite what to do with it. Jeff Andrews doesn’t really have much to do with GW – he’s a friend of Ronnie’s from his other place of employment. None the less, he’s been sharing the apartment with Ronnie and Deanna for well over half a year. He and Ronnie have developed a firm friendship over that time… and really, that friendship is why Ronnie doesn’t on the spot attack him for the situation. Friendship doesn’t prevent him from feeling shock and horror, though. As though he were deluged with icy water, Ronnie felt his body seize up in shock. With an internal wrench, he kept his jaw from falling open. He stared, unblinking, for what seemed to him an eternity. Ronnie wasn’t the only person in the room who tensed upon his arrival. Jeff Andrews did, too. Staring over Deanna’s shoulder at the face of his best friend, his insides gave a jolt. Caught, his arms wrapped around his friend’s girl… not that this could be mistaken as anything other than his clumsy attempts to comfort her… but a touchy situation, to be sure. He swallowed, visibly. And this tipped Deanna off. Turning around, she gave Ronnie one, terrified glance. Then, crying harder, she pushed Jeff away from her with enough force that he nearly toppled off balance, and went running down the stairs before slamming the apartment door. The sound echoed through the apartment, leaving it almost as silent as a crypt. Only the faint sound of the air conditioner rattling in the back room could be heard. The silence itself seemed to grow louder, humming tunelessly in their ears. Finally, Long couldn’t take it anymore. “What the… what the hell was that all about?” For a good friend, Andrews was loathe to make eye contact. He walked into the kitchen, opened the refrigerator door, and pulled out a gallon jug of milk. Grabbing cereal off the top of the fridge – corn chex, to be exact – Andrews sat down at the small table. “She’s upset.” “Obviously.” Ronnie glowers as he walks out of the doorway and flops down on the sofa, turning the TV on. Quickly flipping through the channels, finding nothing worth watching, he gives up and turns it off. “So, what’s she upset about…?” Andrews stirs his cereal, seemingly fascinated by it. After a long while, he speaks. “Well, I guess it’s about your career.” “Jeff, my career is the best it’s ever been. I’m making everyone over in IWA fear me, and since my GW return, I’ve gone undefeated. I ran Vortex out of the GW my debut night…” “You haven’t had a real match since you came back, Ronnie. You’ve beaten Vortex, in a parking lot. You beat Mike McKendrick after Tony Stecchino clocked him with a gun. And you beat Angus The Bull and Mike McKendrick because McKendrick no-showed, and Asylum jumped Angus. For someone who’s gone undefeated since his debut, you haven’t really done very much, have you? Through luck, and through flukes, and through circumstance beyond your control, you’ve climbed your way up the ladder…” “Not by my decision!” Long sits straight up. He’s… well, pissed. Having his talent and integrity called into question by his friend… “It’s not my fault that these things happen! I said I’d go after Stecchino for messing up my match, but nooo, I gotta wait for the International Tournament! I said I’d go after Asylum, but nooo, I gotta wait for Master Of The Mat! Jesus Christ, Jeff, how am I supposed to do ANYTHING around the place with things the way they are?” “What you should do, Ronnie, is go and do something about it. If I were you, I’d drive straight to the GW offices, go see Christina McCoy, and tell her she’s gotta do something about people who can’t act right.” Ronnie relaxes, thinking. Finally, he makes a decision. “I’m not gonna do that, Jeff, I’m not the kind of person who likes fighting with politics. But I think I’ve got myself an idea what I can do about this… I’m goin to the GW arena, Jeff, see you after Evolution.” Ronnie was halfway to the arena when he realized Jeff Andrews had never told him why Deanna was upset in the first place.
