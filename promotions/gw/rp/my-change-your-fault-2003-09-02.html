My Change, Your Fault

2 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/2/03 The scene opens, inside of Ice's hotel room. The room is dark except for the dim green light radiating from his small cd player, which is sitting on the nightstand next to his bed. Ice is seen lying on top of all the covers, with his hands gently folded behind his head, staring up at the plain white ceiling above him. I removes his right hand from behind his head and rolls over onto his left side. He reaches over and hits a button on the cd player, and rolls back over, placing his right hand back behind his head. Breaking the silence the music crashes into the darkness and engulfs the room with it's sound. Ice closes his eyes, and the camera sits and watches. The monotany of the music filling the dark and silent room is broken as the hotel room door opens, and Star, dressed in a pair of low riding, hip hugger blue jeans, and a yellow top, walks through into the room. 'Are you ready?' Ice rolls over, and presses the stop button on the cd player. He sits up and nods at Star, in response to her question. Ice stands up from the bed, and follows Star as she exits the room, and begins to walk down the hotel hallway. Ice slowly lags behind her as she quickly is walking down the hallway. 'Roz Blayze, this week, on Evolution, I have to go through you, to get to what I really want. On Evolution, you and I will square off , man to man, one on one, to determine, the number one contender for the Cruiserweight Title. You look at this as your big opportunity, to prove Roz Blayze isn't just some has been, or never was. You look at this as your big opportunity to win back the belt you believe to be yours. However I, don't look at things the same way as you do Roz. I look at our match, as another chance to show Christina McCoy, that it doesn't matter who you put in front of me, I am going to hurt them. Whether it is in the back, in the parking lot, in their lockerroom, or in the ring. I look at this as another time, to show Christina McCoy, that I am the absolute best in this company. I look at this as an opportunity, to shut you up. Our match, may be a match for the number one contendership for the Cruiserweight Title, but what you and Jin, both don't seem to realize, is that belt, it is just a means to an end for me. I don't care if I win that belt or not. The main concern for me is to inflict as much pain, punishment, and agony, onto you, and Jin Akiyara, as possible. You have stepped into the fire, and now are going to get burned. No one asked for you to join in the fun, but at the same time, I am not complaining, because the more invovled, the more fun for me.' Ice sickly smiles as he continues to follow Star down a flight of stairs and into the lobby of the hotel. Star stops at the front desk, and briefly talks to the woman at the counter, before turning to her left, and walking down another hallway. Through the hallway, and down another flight goes Star, as Ice and the cameraman continue to follow her. 'After Evolution, when you are lying in a pool of your own blood, you won't be denying my words Roz. after Evolution, you won't be saying how you are a legend, how you are the greatest of all time, you won't be saying anything. After Evolution, when I'm through having my fun, putting you through a pain you have never experienced, I am going to make my way to the back, and I am going to see the expression on Miss McCoy's face, as she realizes, that there isn't anything left between me, and her little boy toy. Then, Jin, it will be your turn. Your turn to sit with your head in your hands, wondering just how you are going to get yourself out of this mess you have gotten into. It will be your turn, to run to Christina, begging not to face me. Begging for her to do something to stop the carnage that is inevitable. But you see, there will be no stopping it.' Everyone stops in front of a plain white door, that is down a small, dingy, basement looking hallway. Ice turns and looks at Star. Star opens the door and disappears behind it. Ice takes a step back and leans against the cool cement block wall. He gazes up at the ceiling and inhales deeply. Slowly he exhales, and begins to speak. 'You see, the man known as Ice, is not the man you all thought you once knew. All the things I have done in the past, they weren't just asks of random violence. I knew of everything I did, before I did it. I planned out every little detail, and thought of every possible senario, that would occur in result of my actions. The things you have seen from be before, are nothing compared to things you are going to see from me, after tonight. Because after tonight, the Ice you know, the Ice you have seen, will be no longer be the same man he was before. Roz Blayze, you have been chosen to be the sheep that is sent to slaughter, as I show Christina McCoy, Jin Akiyara, and anyone else who feels they want to enter the flames of my fire, that the everything you know, the comfort of this world you are used to, are no more. This Global Wrestling world, you have all grown to know and love, has been erased, extinguished. The things you are used to, the battles you have grown accustom to, no longer exsist. You all have open the door, to the gates of MY WORLD. You all have crossed over the threshold, and now there is no turning back and escaping the world you now inhabit. The things you will hear, the things you will see, and the things you will feel, some will find to be quite disturbing. And you have no one else to blame, but yourselves for all of it. I warned you all, time and time again, but no one listened, no one heeded my warning. Now there is no turning back. There is no escaping what fate has in store for you. So, look on in doubt, look on in question, look on in fear, because at Evolution, Christina McCoy, the things that happen to Roz Blayze are on your head, the blood that he sheds, is on your hands, and when I finished having my fun with him. You, and your little boy toy, Jin Akiyara, are next.' Star comes out from behind the door and taps Ice gently on the shoulder, and motions for him to enter the room behind the door. Ice, like Star moments earlier, disappears behind the white door. Star looks away from the camera, and then turns back towards it. 'A man, in his lifetime, attempts to stay constant. Until he is pushed too far, a man will try not to change, try to stay sane. But once that man is pushed beyond his limits, he changes. He changes his demeanor. He changes his style. He changes how he talks. He changes how he walks. He changes how he talks. He changes how he acts, and he changes how he looks. Christina McCoy, Jin Akiyara, have pushed, and pushed, and pushed. They pushed him too far. His words, no longer words of things to come. His actions now the actions of evil. His look, no longer the look he has held onto for so long. All because he has been pushed too long, and too far. I will no longer hold back the things you are about to hear. I will no longer hold back the actions you are about to see. For I have let Ice suffer too long. It is now time for you to experience, the things we have had to deal with, from you, for far too long.' The door opens, and Star spins around. From behind the door, wearing a blue jeans, and a white tank top, is Ice. His hands, on top of his head, slowly slide down the sides of his face, and it is quickly noticed, that his long blond locks, are gone. Replaced with a short, blond spiked look, Ice turns and sickly smiles at Star, and then sinisterly looks deeply into the camera. Ice steps forward and pushes the cameraman, around the open door and into the room. The door slams behind the cameraman. The cameraman frantically turns and tries to open the door, but to no avail. Slowly he turns back and begins to walk into the room. Around the only corner of the entire room, sits a single chair, with a man sitting in the chair. The cameraman walks around to see the man's eyes closed, and his shirt ripped open. Blood drips from the man's chest, down to a puddle in his lap. The camera, turns away from the man, and on the wall, in blood, and strands of hair, a message has been left. 'YOU DID THIS YOU MADE THIS YOU NOW LIVE WITH THIS' The scene fades to a commercial.
