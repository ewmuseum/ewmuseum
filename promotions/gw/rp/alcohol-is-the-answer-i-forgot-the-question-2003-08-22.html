Alcohol is the answer... I forgot the question

22 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/22 Here I lay. Shirtless. Inebriated. Incoherent. Sweating bullets on top of the covers in a pitch black room is how I’m reacquainted with the realm of consciousness. Well, not quite. I wouldn’t exactly call this consciousness... Random thoughts form inside my head and painfully ricochet off of each other causing a throbbing sensation similar to a bass drum being pounded. Only in this instance there is no drum, just my brain being repeatedly struck with a rubber mallet until any remaining hydration is squashed out. All of this compounded on top of the fact that I also feel as though there were a gigantic steel claw utilizing a shark’s jaws for a hand crushing my head does not help my situation in the least. Although the room is far too dark for me to make anything in it out, much less focus on anything, opening my eyes allows me to think just a minuscule amount more clearly. Unfortunately it also causes the room to gyrate. Definitely not a good thing... I call out for Zero in a semi-inaudible tone due to my current state of nausea. There is no response... I call out again, but this time louder. Not so much because I need him, but because I’m curious as to whether he’s around or not... and how tha hell I ended up in this bed. Come to think of it, he was drinking when I got here... he may be worse off. But the fact still remains, if he’s not shakin? this room neither am I. How much did I drink? Let’s see now... Four at the bar... Three more with those chicks... That’s seven. I remove a finger from my fist with each shot that comes to mind. Shit, then we played circle of death. Who knows how many I that was... I once again ball up a fist to re-begin my counting. Five body shots... now that was fun. Hmm, three or four Coronas with Sean when he showed up... and the cognac those two Italian guys with him slid me... Then there was that god awful absinthe shit Soma gave me... fuckin? weirdo, who drinks green liquor!” I still don’t know about that guy... Feeling extremely uncomfortable I slide myself sideways across the bed and hang my legs off of the side while making sure to plant my feet firmly on the ground. Much to my disappointment the room continues to spin Faster.. In an attempt to escape the centrifuge that has become this pitch black bedroom I tuck my head between my knees and place my hands over my head as though I were preparing for a crash landing. Seeing as how I’m terrified of flying I’ve become quite good at this... Another thought comes to mind... If a centrifuge is made to separate solids from liquids, what might this one be trying to separate me from? Consciousness maybe? If so, it’s working... Suddenly my torso jerks forward neck first, mouth slightly opening along the way with my tongue beginning to peek out over my lip as if only to take a look around. I feel like something in my stomach is attempting to get out but keeps finding itself lodged in my esophagus mere inches away from freedom. What could it be? My torso jerks forward once again like a stick shift car in the use of an untrained driver just before stalling out, this time bringing my shoulders along for the ride and creating a disgusting taste in my mouth. Whatever wanted out would have surely been successful on this attempt if not for me... I held it down this time. Believe it or not, it tasted worse going back down than coming up. Pellets of perspiration begin building up and congregating on the ridge of my nose and the middle of my forehead. A sheepish expression crosses my face, much like that of a deer caught in some trucker’s headlights as I realize what is about to happen. ...and this time there will be no stopping it. I burst into the air from my seated position as if fired from a gigantic circus style cannon and begin running full stride for the bathroom with my lips tightly pursed together in order to imprison any alcohol attempting to prematurely vacate my body. Even though it couldn’t be anymore than fifteen feet away, with me running at full four flat speed it seems to take forever to get to the bathroom. It’s almost as if I were moving in slow motion, or running in quicksand. Finally I make it to the door, bumping into it hard before backing up and throwing it open. Partially because I couldn’t slow down in time, mainly because I can’t exactly see it. Once inside my eyes widen to their full extent and become glued on the porcelain bowl of this particular bathroom’s toilet. Salvation. I drop to my knees as if bowing to some form of royalty and immediately gape my jaws open, expelling every drop of alcohol I consumed after stepping foot in John’s house tonight... Along with a few semi solid bits of jello... which also contained alcohol. Forgot about those... If John was asleep surely he’s awake now. I’m currently doubled over the toilet belting out incredible hulk style roars as I exhume the liquid remnants of my evening from the tombs of my stomach. An ab workout to say the least... When it finished over and done with I wash my mouth out and take a seat on the cold tile floor with my back pressed against the wall on the far side of the bathroom, away from the toilet. I tilt my head back, mouth gaped open as I hold my stomach and breath heavily. Feeling much better now, yet entirely exhausted, my mind drifts off to other things... Namely, my match on Evolution. Me versus BJP and Max Roswell for both of my titles. Sounds like Christina trying to screw me over for my titles. Of course, she’d never admit that. I bet Brick has somethin? to do with this... He probably whined to her about what happened last week and demanded that I be placed in this match. Why he wouldn’t face me is a mystery, I’d gladly throw everything I’ve got on the line against him. Just to take what he’s got... Come to think of it, I know exactly why he wouldn’t just ask for a match against me. He’s scared. I mean, it’s been proven that he can’t beat me. He’s been ducking me ever since our first meeting. He was expecting a push over and was met with an ass kicking. I don’t see how he finds time for matches as it is anyway. I’d assume having your head up Christina McCoy’s ass would be a full time job... Shit, she should worship me. Her dad would probably be dead right now if not for me. I cracked the safe with the antidote for Bubba in it. Some thanks I’m getting...
