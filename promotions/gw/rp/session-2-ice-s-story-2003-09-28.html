Session 2....Ice's story

28 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/28/03 'Please, for me, so we can get this over and done with. I don't like that you have to do this either baby, but the sooner you cooperate, the sooner this will be all over. So if you don't do it for anyone else, will you please do it for me? Ice looks at Star and shrugs his shoulders. 'Alright, but ONLY FOR YOU.' Ice and Star enter the building and walk down a long white hallway, which appears even whiter that what it normally should, as the bright white lights hanging from the ceiling shine down upon them. Ice stops, and smiles. Star not noticing Ice had stopped continues to walk down the hallway, before stopping and noticing Ice is not by her side. She hurrily looks around and see's Ice shaking his head and smiling at her. Her face slightly goes red, and she bashfully smiles back at Ice. Ice walks forward to her and wraps his large muscular arms around Star's petite frame. Ice gazes into Star's emerald green eyes for what seems like an eternity, but is actually only a matter of seconds, before leaning in and giving her a passionate kiss. As they kiss Ice moves his hands up and down Star's smooth, silky, skin. Her skin immediately reacts to his touch as small goosebumps noticeably rise. Ice pulls away from the kiss and they both let out a deep breath. Star gazes up at Ice in a mixture of awe and love as a smile sneaks across her beautiful face. Ice with his arms wrapped around Star's body, and hands locked behind her back leans back and stares. A calm, expression resides over Ice's face as the two continue to gaze into each other's eyes. Ice smirks at Star and blinks with both eyes, before letting her go. He reaches down and grabs her hand, and they continue to walk down the hallway. There is an extra hop in both of their steps as they enter the large black and white checkered room. The brunette behind the desk notices their entrance, and quickly heads to the door, and through it. Ice reaches up and scratches his nose, before turning to Star. 'I think it would be better if you left now. I don't want you to see anything that might happen. After what happened last time, I'm sure some sort of action will be taken, so that it doesn't happen again.' The brunette enters the room once again, and is followed by three large men in police uniforms, and six men in white shoes, white pants, and white shirts. In a single file line the nine men make their way over towards Ice and Star. One of the men in the all white clothing stops at the end of the desk and grabs a syringe, before stepping back in line with the rest of the group. Star's eyes widen as she sees the syringe in the man's hand. She hurrily looks over at Ice and her eyes begin to fill with tears. Ice looks over at the men, and then back at Star and reassurringly smiles at her. Slowly he raises his right hand and gently caresses the side of her face. The tears filling her eyes slowly begin to seep out and roll down her cheeks. He lovingly gazes at her before leaning in and giving her a kiss on the cheek. The brunette steps forward. 'We can do this the easy way, with you cooperating, or we can do this the hard way and cause a giant scene, and make a huge mess.' Ice angrily looks over at her, and then calmly looks to Star and nods. Now sobbing, Star turns and exits the room back down the hallway. Ice turns towards the brunette, the three men in police uniforms, and the men in the white uniforms and begins to walk in their direction. The men in the police uniforms read themselves, and the brunette begins to back away until she runs into the desk. Ice stops in front of her, and reaches into his back pocket. He pulls out a check and hands it to the brunette. 'This is what I owe.' She looks over the check and escorts him behind the desk, and into a long hallway, with numerous doors on both sides. They walk by three closed wooden doors before stopping infront of an opene doorway. The brunette steps out of the way, and Ice slowly enters the room. The inside of the room appears to be extremely expensive, and overdone. The floor is wooden with a small while circled rug in the middle of it. Just beyond the rug is a large brown desk, with a black leather chair, that is turned away from Ice. To Ice's left is a black velvet couch, that sits infront of a large bookcase, filled with trophies and awards. 'Mr. Ice, please be seated.' The voice echos from behind the black leather chair. Ice doesn't move, as he stands and waits for the voice to reveal a face. The chair spins around and Dr. Gruzielata, with white bandages wrapping around his forehead, and two badly swollen eyes, stands up, and points towards the couch. The doctor looks towards the doorway and nods, and instantly the three men in the police uniforms, and the six men in the all white uniforms rush into the room. 'Now Mr. Ice, what we are doing is for my protection, and your own well being, I hope that you know this.' Ice looks from side to side at the men surrounding him. Two of the men in the white uniforms walk forward. In their hands is an all white straight jacket. Ice smirks and shakes his head from side to side. Slowly he stands and snatches the straight jacket from the two men. The uniformed policemen take a step forward, but stop as Ice puts the straight jacket on himself. He slowly turns his back to the rest of the crowd in the room. Two men step forward and put the finishing touches on locking up the jacket so that Ice has no escape. They assist Ice in turning back around and facing the doctor. Ice sits down, and the doctor motions for the assistants and the policement to exit the room. After the crowd files out Dr. Gruzielata closes the door, walks back over to his chair, and takes a seat behind his desk. 'I am glad to see you are being alot more cooperative today. I apologize about delaying our session for a week, but due to your actions last time, I was unable to make it. I'm sure that you understand.' Ice cockily smirks. The doctor notices it, but ignores it and begins to shuffle through some papers that are lying on his desktop. He pulls out a piece of paper, and begins to read it. After looking the paper over he sets it back down and picks up his notepad and pen. 'Last time we met, you mentioned that you didn't care about anyone, with the exception of Star. Who is Star, and what is your relationship with her?' Ice sits back on the couch and looks up to the ceiling as he begins to chuckle in frustration to himself. 'What is so funny?' Ice still chuckling to himself shakes his head from side to side. Slowly he intakes a deep breath, and exhales it out just as slow. 'You don't get it DOC. I'm only hear, by force, not by choice. All I have to do is sit here for the next three weeks, and then I am done.' 'You see that is where you are wrong Mr. Ice.' The doctor pulls the piece of paper he was looking at off his desktop once again and holds it up so Ice can see it. 'What this little piece of paper says, is that you are here, as long as I see fit. If I do not feel that you are stable enough to go on without psychological therapy, then you will stay in therapy until I feel that you no longer need it. And you being uncooperative is not making me feel that you no longer need therapy.' Ice looks at the doctor in anger as Dr. Gruzielata stares back with a pleased smirk. The doctor sets the paper back onto his desk and leans back into his leather chair. 'Again, who is Star and what is your relationship with her?' 'You want to keep me here fine. I was nice enough this time to put the jacket on myself. But next time, the jacket is staying off. And what happened the first time we met, will occur everytime I see your shit-eating grin, and there isn't anything you or anybody else can do about it. So you keep wasting both of our time by bringing me back. And you keep wasting your breath by asking me your stupid little questions. Because I AM NOT ANSWERING.' 'Now now, no need for any threats. But let me reassure you that security will be upped each time you are scheduled for a session. And we will do whatever it is we deem necessary to make sure you do not cause any harm to anyone here again.' Ice smirks at the doctor. 'Oh is that right.' 'Mr. Ice, the question I asked was who is Star, and what is your relationship with her. If you do not wish to answer that she is a hooker you met on the street that is fine, just say so, and i will move onto the next question.' Ice eyes light up in rage. He shoots up from the couch in a standing position and glares at Dr. Gruzielata. Angrily he marches over towards the doctor's desk. 'A HOOKER I MET ON THE STREET! FOR BEING A GOD DAMNED DOCTOR YOU ARE ONE LOUSEY SON OF A BITCH AS A PERSON! IF YOU HAVE A PROBLEM WITH ME THAT'S FINE, BUT YOU KEEP YOUR MOUTH SHUT ABOUT HER! YOU HEAR ME?! As a matter of fact she isn't a hooker I met on the street. I was wrestling for a company known as EWA in Europe. The company was divided between it's president and vice president. The president wanted to keep his established talent at the top, and not give any of us younger guys a shot, but the vice president thought it was time to see what we could do. So at one of the shows, the younger guys on the roster banded together and took out the older guys. It was a message sent to show that we weren't going to be held down anymore. One of the guys owned a club, and we all went there to celebrate after the show. A few girls came to the club that night as well, and this short little blond, with these gorgeous green eyes was there in a lowcut, high riding, skin tight pink dress. I ended up taking her home that night, and she hasn't left my side since.' A smile crawls across Ice's angered face as he begins to back peddle back towards the couch, and finally taking a seat. Ice gazes in front of him, almost as if he is in shock, or just day dreaming. The doctor leans forward and is about to speak when Ice begins to speak again. 'I took her home that night, in a limo that was provided by the vice president. Paul was in the limo waiting, said we needed to talk. We talked about the big plans he had for me, and for the group. I didn't pay much attention to what he was saying, as my focus was more on this beautiful girl that was riding with me. We got back to the hotel, and she had to go to the bathroom, so she went in the one that was in the lobby, not even waiting to get back to the room.' Ice looks down at the floor and continues to smile. His gaze at the floor is almost a euphoric trance-like gaze, as if he was having flashbacks. 'So I walked to the front desk, and asked if the dining room was still open. The young man at the desk said no, but if i wanted something he would get it for me himself. I asked him if the lady I was with and myself could go into the room, and possibly get something to drink and eat. He immediately walked over and unlocked the dining room door, and said he would lock it back up once we were inside, and that he would be with us in a few minutes.' Ice looks up from the floor, and half chuckles to himself. 'We sat in that dining room, and had two plates of cheese fries, and talked the rest of the night. She told me she was in Europe on vacation, and was at the club because it was the only thing open that wasn't overly crowed with bumbling drunk Englishmen. I told her that I was glad that she came to the club, and was truly appreciative that she accepted my invitation to come back to my room. She smiled at me and said, 'Well don't get any ideas, this is all I was hoping for and planning on.' I reassurred her that this moment right now was better than anything else I could have imagined.' Ice's head shakes from side to side, then stops as he looks at Dr. Gruzielata. 'We went back to my room, and she asked if she could have something to sleep in. I told her to look in my bag and pick out something she thought would be comfortable. She went into the bathroom and came back out wearing a red and black flannel shirt I had. She spun around in the shirt, and asked me if I thought she looked good, and I must say that it was a very nice sight, and told her so. The red that filled her face after that was priceless. She climbed onto the bed, and I grabbed a pillow and headed for the floor, but was stopped when she grabbed my hand. She said 'We can share, I won't bite...too hard.' and then winked at me. We both chuckled as I hit the light. We talked a little while before falling asleep.' Both Ice and Dr. Gruzielata lean back in their respective seats. The doctor continues to scribble on his notepad for a moment. He looks up from it and is about to speak when he is cut off once again by Ice. 'The next morning, well more around afternoon, we headed down to the dining room and had breakfast. Everyone stared as we walked into the room. You see because she didn't bring any other clothes, she was wearing a pair of my gym shorts, and one of my t-shirts. Both were extremely oversized for her, and she was walking around holding up the shorts so that they wouldn't fall.' Ice begins to laugh to himself. Dr. Gruzielata smiles. 'What she didn't know is while she was still asleep I had gotten up early, went down to the desk, and asked one of the ladies at the counter to find some a nice casual outfit that matched the size of the dress she was wearing. So when we finished breakfast, we headed back up to the room, and there was a pair of khaki capris, and an American Eagle woman's tee laying on the bed, with a note attatched to it. She immediately thanked me and then changed into the clothes. I had some things to do for EWA and so we left the hotel, and she went back to where she was staying, but gave me her number. She was scheduled to go back to Minnesota the next day, but I wasn't going to let that happen. So that night I called her and asked if I could see her. We met and I told her to stay, with me. She started to cry, and didn't say anything at first, but then leaned forward, and we kissed. We went back to my room that night, and unpacked her stuff. We haven't been apart since.' Ice begins to shake his head and smirk to himself in amazement. The door to the room opens and the brunette accompanied by two men in all white uniforms enter the room. Ice stands and is removed from the straight jacket. His euphoric flashback gaze slowly fades away, and he turns and looks over at Dr. Gruzielata. 'Here is a list of the times and dates of our scheduled sessions.' The doctor hands Ice a piece of paper with a calender on it. Ice looks down at it, and then back up at the doctor. A confused look engulfs his face, as he stares at the doctor. Dr. Gruzielata stares back and nods. Ice still with the confused look on his face turns and exits the room, the hallway, and finally is back into the large black and white checkered room where Star is anxiously waiting for him. As soon as she see's Ice her eyes widen and a smile from ear to ear consumes her face. Ice stil with the confused look on his face walks over to Star. Her joyful expression quickly changes to worry as they walk back down the hallway and exit the building. They both enter Ice's black Expedition, and Star starts the vehicle. She stops, and places the vehicle back into to park and turns to Ice. 'Ice, honey. What's the matter? What happened in there?' Ice turns and looks at Star. 'I'm not really sure.' Star, confused, looks at Ice. Ice leans forward and gives her a deep passionate kiss. 'I just want you to know I LOVE YOU.' Tears immediately fill Star's eyes, and flood down her cheeks. Ice smiles at her, and she smiles back. 'I LOVE YOU TOO.' The scene fades.
