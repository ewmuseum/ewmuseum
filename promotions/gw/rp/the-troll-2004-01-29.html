The Troll

29 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/29/04 After being back at the apartment for not more than two hours, Johnie finds himself walking down the steps of the building. Taking the first of many into the deeper regions of the complex, he stares out the window as bright beams of light shine into his eyes once again. As it peaks over the tops of the nearby housing districts, the sun beams its last few rays of energy before it disappears for another day. The orange ball of radiance seems to glow wider than usual as the clouds help spread the color and help paint the remaining open spots of the sky as it fights against the pitch blackness of the horizon. But as Johnie looks out the window, he can’t help but notice the slender crack that twists itself up the middle while bending to the right like a river looking for a place to end. The vile stench of garbage and stale cigarettes haunt the hallway between the floors as the carpet holds scattered remnants of forgotten butts. Including some that are hidden beneath the small lumps next to the scattered holes in the floor. Johnie twists to the left a full one hundred-eighty degrees to switch from flight to flight on the stairway. Yet as he grabs a hold of the handrail, he levers himself a bit to take a first step and finds himself sailing towards the opposite wall. After taking a couple hurried steps extra down, he slumps against the other railing still with the smooth wooden mast in his hand. Looking back over his shoulder, he notices the metal spine coming out from the floor as the tip matches the hole of the rail. “What the fuck is this shit?” Johnie shouts as his eyes are still wide from the near tumble a few seconds prior. Stomping up each of the four steps to get back to the rail support, he shakes his head shallowly while his lips curl together in a clench. “Oh this is terrific. He places the bar over the spike and starts to wiggle it back into the hole until it goes about half way down. Clenching his teeth and his grasp, Johnie shakes it to go a little further until his face reddens. Rearing his right arm back, he slams it down as his forearm connects with the beam causing a sucking sound at the friction as the rail falls back into place. Taking a deep breath, Johnie takes a step back and exhales heavily. Rolling his fingers through the thick bangs that fell over his face, he runs his right hand down the side of his neck behind his ear. Tapping the base a couple times, he raises his head calmly. What a great fucking day. Johnie turns around and glides down the center of the steps toward the front door of the building and turns to go an extra flight down to the bottom floor. Trey had mentioned to him merely minutes ago that the landlord was at the far end of the hallway in room 3A. Passing by the first door, he can hear echoes of The Ramones blaring out their anti-pop anthems of the 1980’s. While the stench of burned macaroni and cheese eminates from the second. Strange how mac and cheese can be such an easy thing to make and yet some people can still find a way to make it into toxic waste. While he approaches the door of destiny, Johnie sees a soft glow from beneath the door along with some inaudible murmurs from within. Too soft to be company, although sometimes you can never tell. Raising his left knuckle up to the thin wood, he raps against it one heavily with two light ones following. Staring into the brass lettering before him, he waits for a response. Tick tock and nothing. Rolling his eyes, Johnie taps the door again but a little harder this time as he feels the door shake a little extra than before. Still nothing. He curls his upper lip into a hard grimace as his eye twinges a bit with it. Fatigue has already spent Johnie’s patience level to the max and making him wait does not bode too well for him. Curling his knuckle around, it turns into a fist as he angles it back to swing the bottom of it into the door. As it breaks through the air, it comes within centimeters of making contact as Johnie’s ears catch a soft tone from within. “Come on in...” Following the muffled moan, he turns his fist down to the knob and cups it. With a twist and a push, he was in. As the door parts its way open, Johnie is met with a view that made him scowl. A couch scattered over with empty potato chip bags with remnants of its contents on the cushions and on caught in the curled carpet nearby. Trying to take a first step in, the door is stopped by soft obstruction. Nudging it further doesn’t move it any further. He takes a look down at the door and sees multiple colors of fabric sticking out from beneath it. Clothes. Rearing back slightly, he rushes against it as he nudges it forward with some resistance. The door opens enough for Johnie to step inside and take a better look around. Glancing past the door, he sees the sink stacked twice over with scattered Tupperware and dark brown pots. Nearby is the kitchen table, instantly noticeable with its heavy lean to the left where another leg should be. Atop the leaning stand is a mass of scattered papers covered with a large model of a Star Wars brand spaceship near the center. The air is thin and stale with a slight trace of what seems to be similar to the smell of rotten onions, but only thicker. Letting out a soft choking cough, Johnie looks around for the origin of the greeting he received before entering. Raising an eyebrow, he turns his head towards the glow of the television left on by the unseen sentry. On the screen bounces the image of a young William Shatner in a bright yellow shirt. Black and white lines stand in between spaces of Shatner as it seems to be on pause. “He’s a fricking sci-fi freak?” he chuckles a bit while he shakes his head slightly at the action figures placed above the flat topped television. “What a dork...shit.” A loud splash and gurgle turns Johnie around as the door off to the left sways open letting a glow florescent light shine free. Yet with a quick flick, the light disappears as a bulky left leg steps out of the doorway. The other leg follows suit as both are covered in near skin tight blue sweatpants with oft white socks tucked underneath. But hanging over the waist of the pants is a stretched out yellow t-shirt obviously held down by some excess gut. With a plain smile, the bathroom fairy greets Johnie with a soft hello. “Sorry it took me so long, but I got a little held back as you can tell.” Ending with a nervous laugh, his mouth opened wide showing how it could take up half his face. Seems to be that eating fast food can stretch certain features. “And you are?” Johnie didn’t return the laugh, he just stood opposite of the monstrosity with a wrinkled brow. Delaying a little, he returns the question. “Johnie Ringo. I’m...uhh... moving in with Trey upstairs.” “Not if I have anything to say about it.” Chuckling a bit, the gelatinous wonder waddles over to his cluttered desk with his hands hanging limp above his belly. Flipping through the many small stacks of papers, he pulls a couple up for a quick glance before discharging them. “You see, what Trey has done is that he’s gotten himself into quite a problem. He’s done what he could with paying past overdue rent, but he has dug himself deeper with late charges.” Not being able to find what he’s looking for at the desk, the blob turns toward the kitchen area and starts grabbing at some papers there. “Those late charges got up to over two extra months rent and I’m sure left him unable to cover this month as well.” Picking up the model on top of the papers, he points it at Johnie and smiles slightly. “Are you a Star Wars fan?” Softly exhaling Johnie stares blankly back at him. “Uhhh...no. I never really got into that much.” “Oh you’re missing out.” Setting the ship down on the previous pages, he continues to flip through until he finally comes to the one he wants. Sliding it from under a medium stack, he wobbles it and holds it a foot away from his face as he continues. “As you see here, this is the lease. And it says plainly that the tenant cannot bring in any new roommates unless authorized by the owner or owners.” Pushing the glasses back up the bridge of his nose, he snorts while shrugging his shoulders in the air. “I don’t know what else I can say about it really. This little stunt was the last straw according to the owners and they want him out.” As the landlord lets out another nervous chortle, Johnie stares sternly at the bulbous pack of lard. Taking a couple breaths, he puts on a fake shit eating grin as he walks over toward the door. Reaching out, he grabs a hold of it and struggles to pull it away from the clothes beneath it. “You see...that’s were you’re wrong.” Finally pulling the door free, he gently latches it shut while slowly turning the lock. The click from the bolt startled the landlord as when Johnie turns around, he sees his beady eyes widen from behind his glasses. “I came here to talk this whole situation over with you to where we could get an understanding. With all this late charge bullshit.” Johnie slides his foot across the carpet to push the clothes out of his path. Taking a couple steps forward he realizes its not the fatass with the grin on his face. Its him. “You and I both know that its just an easy way to get a little extra cash off the unfortunate. Either for you or for your bosses. And they make enough money in the first place. So what I’m thinking is that you just want to score some pocket pay for yourself. What do you think?” The landlord watches as Johnie picks up the pieced together model on the table with a wide grasp. Fretfully he lets out a couple inaudible chuckles as his chins begin to jiggle. “I-I-I don’t know what you mean. Those...uhh...charges are stated in the lease and...uhhh...they’re....uhhh....documented.” Taking a good hold of the ship, Johnie mockingly swoops it in the air like a ten year old kid with a new toy. Smiling and grinning, having a ball. Until suddenly he stops and coldly turns his stare up at the landlord. “You know what, Sparky” I don’t believe you.” Pulling the ship back, he tosses it up in the air not far above the table. But just before it smashes against the papers and scattering everywhere, a pair of jiggly arms catches it and pulls it in. All the while, Johnie looks straight at the guy with a curled smirk. “Nice catch.” Standing in shock, he stands and stares down at his model. Holding as tightly as an infant holds its first dolly, but not enough to damage it. But before he knows it, its looking Johnie dead in the face as he looks up. “You scared?” Johnie says ominously. “You scared?” he repeats. Instead of getting an answer, all he gets in return is a sheepish angled glance back at him. But that quickly disappears as a loud snap of palm hitting echoes off his cheek, turning his head sideways. “You better be!” In a flash, Johnie grabs a hold of the landlord’s shoulder blades and pulls him down to meet a rising knee. As it connects between his upper jaw and bottom lip, Johnie launches him towards the couch where he runs into the top of the television. The shattering of glass combined with a quick zap resounds as the 19? TV falls flat onto the carpet. As he holds his head with both hands, the bloated buffoon staggers back until he finds himself flat on his back. After taking out his legs, Johnie walks over the beached whale as his arms are spread out over the floor. Slowly he puts his weight down on his bicep with a left foot and rocks it vigorously as he listens to the shrieks of pain. Following up, Johnie places his right foot over the other bicep while leaning forward as he leans his knee down on his throat. “Now...are we clear?” he mutters confidently. All he hears in response is a gurgled mess of vowels. Taking some weight off the windpipe, Johnie waits as the landlord struggles for air. “No late rent. No back fees. No eviction. Are we clear?” Thinking that he’s taking too long to recover, Johnie leans back on his gullet. After a short amount of gurgling, he nods in response. Smiling proudly, Johnie rises up off the fallen flatulent and allows him to grasp his throat with both hands as he gags heavily. Johnie steps away from him and slowly walks towards the door with a raised tone. “Now I shouldn’t have to mention anything about the police, do I” Because it’d be a shame for me to come back here and do something worse.” Unbolting the lock, Johnie turns to hear a stifled “Yes” as he opens the door. “That’s very good. Now if you’ll excuse me, I have a party to go to.” Johnie turns swiftly and heads out the door, catching it enough to close it on the way out. Back on the floor, the landlord coughs and retches to himself as he struggles to his knees. Yet as a pair of struggled tears go down his cheeks, his eyes glance down at the object in front of him. Scattered in bits, his former triumph. “My X-wing......”
