Blackmail for someone

24 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/24/04 (The scene begins inside the office of the Nomad Donny J McNasty at the Drunken Leperchaun in Altoona, PA. He just finished watching the tape that Hardcore Harv made backstage at last nights Evolution. The Nomad can see it's Train who drove off in the truck last night. Train made the mistake of taking off his ski mask before he got in the truck, thinking that no one would see him. But what a mistake Train made... McNasty heard what Christina McCoy has in store for him if she knows for sure it was him. You see McNasty isn't a tattle tale he's one who takes action into his own hands. He looked on the internet for Trains phone number and found it. The Nomad is planning on calling him, letting him know he has the evidence and that Trains career is in the hands of the Nomad Donny J McNasty.) **The Nomad Picks up the phone and dials Train's number** Train: Hello? Nomad: Yes can I speak with a man by the name of Train? Train: It depends on who the fuck this is... Nomad: No need for the temper, Train, this is The Nomad Donny J McNasty. And I have something I want to discuss with you. Train: What the fuck do you want to discuss with me? Look if your calling complaining that I kicked your ass the first time we met why bother? Nomad: No No, Train, lets just say that I can kill your short lived career here in Global Wrestling. Train: What the fuck are you smoking McNasty? If it's any good save some for me, I'll smoke after I beat your drunk ass again... Nomad: Train it's really simple, I have a tape of you leaving the scene of the crime last night. I have the evidence that I am sure Christina McCoy would love to see, and thus ending your career. Train: I wasn't even there last night McNasty, I was banned remember? Nomad: Train, you made big mistake when you took off your ski mask when you speeded off in the truck. Train: Even if you do have some sort of tape, I'm not fucking worried about McNasty. I'm not worried about a damn thing. Global Wrestling is nothing compared to where I came from in Chicago. I don't care if you call yourself the fucking king of Hardcore or not. In my view, your just another obstacle, your just a small ant hill in my way... There is nothing you can do that will make me worry McNasty. Nomad: That's too bad that you see it that way Train. Because I see it this way, Harv is going to be leaving here soon, to give the tape to Christina McCoy and you will be unemployed as soon as she sees the tape and there isn't a damn thing you can do about it! Train: What ever McNasty, there is nothing you, Harv or McCoy can do to stop me. Come Sunday, you're mine. (Train hangs up the phone) *The Nomad turns around to watch the tape over again* Nomad: Wow, to think he has a chance of stopping the inevitable. He has balls I'll give him that much. The sad part of everything is that I won't have a match at High Octane... Oh well I am sure they will find me a tougher opponent than Train.... (The scene fades to black)
