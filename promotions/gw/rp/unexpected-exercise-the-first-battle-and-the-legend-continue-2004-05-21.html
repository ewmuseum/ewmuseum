Unexpected Exercise...The First Battle...And the Legend Continues....

21 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-21-04 Pendragon was unsure what to make of this place after his trip into the halls of his dead fathers. Our hero was also unsure of his place in the land. John told him that he needed to veer south in order to gain a magical talisman of great power. With a last look to the forest where he would raise an army, he shrugs his massive shoulders and starts off at a jog towards the south, and the desert that would await him. The desert would be an obstacle tackled when he finally reached it? “I wish someone here had a horse that they would not mind lending to their liege lord’This footrace is growing tiresome?” You could never tell it in his posture nor in his pace. Unrelenting he rushes onward, unafraid of any danger that may be awaiting him on the path to his, and the Land’s, redemption. Soon though, shadows began to fall as dusk faded to darkness. The Saviour’s massive shoulders slump as he begins to look around for a decent place to take shelter. At last, he spots a massive boulder. He approaches slowly and walks around, finding, as if by providence, firewood, stacked neat and tidy, ready for burning. Further investigation reveals to him a small parcel. He sits on the ground, his back against the boulder, and unwraps the paper. Inside, he finds a couple of useful things’A small box containing flint and steel, and what appears to be a package of hotdogs. He also finds a piece of folded paper, which he unfolds and reads?. I thought you might be needin this, my lad. Things can be hard unless you have someone who might look after you from time to time’Just because it is dangerous for me to meddle with Akumancha, it doesn’t mean that I will forsake you? Merlin Pendragon smiles and refolds the paper. He unsheathes his sword and proceeds to shave some small slivers of kindling off of one of the pieces of wood that has been provided for him. He gathers them in a little pile and scrapes the flint with the steel. Soon, sparks are falling into the fuel, and a small flame begins to flicker. As it grows, Venneman adds some larger pieces and soon he has a nice sized fire going. He sits back and smiles, his heart warmed by the gesture of his old mentor. With a smirk, he opens the hotdogs and spits one with the end of his sword. He holds it over the fire, and soon the dog is cooking? Pendragon: Merlin’Thou could have at least left me something decent to eat? With a laugh, he shakes his head and pulls the hotdog from the sword and eats it in two bites. He spits another and holds it over the flames as the moon begins to show it’s face in the darkness. One by one, he cooks the hotdogs and eats them all. He lovingly wipes his blade clean of the grease, and re-sheathes the sword. He stares at the sky as the stars come out one by one. At last, he rises and stretches his massive limbs and yawns? Pendragon: One more thing before sleep takes me? He turns to the boulder, and climbs up. It is truly a massive boulder and it brings him that much closer to the sky. He draws the sword and drops down to his knees. Holding the blade in front of his forehead, he bows his head and begins his prayer? Pendragon: Hear my prayer, O fathers of the past. I find myself in a strange land’In thy land. Tonight, I sleep beneath an alien sky. Similar to mine own, and yet different. There is a different feel to this place. Although the darkness is deep, there is a feeling of purity and of home that mine senses have never before been exposed to. Mine senses are more intuned to the land and what is going on in these great out of doors. Thou hast foreseen this great malignancy that eats away at thy heritage, and though thee knows the outcome already, I am uncertain. Things such as this are never preordained. There is always a chance that the hero wilt fail; the strong, falter. Such is the nature of the human heart and conscience. Mine path is made by the ideals of honor and decency, but sometimes, even for the Warrior of the White, doing the correct thing is the more difficult decision. Sometimes I am weary, and sometimes I wish to hang up my ambition for glory and power. And yet, deep in mine heart where the souls of the past lie, I am not allowed to do so. Evil needs balancing, and I am the ballast that keeps order. A heavy burden and true, yet I walk the path that I take willingly. I only ask thee for guidance and knowledge’Guidance when my way falters, and knowledge when I am at a loss’In your names?.Glory and honor? Pendragon raises the sword, and almost on cue, a lightning bolt streaks down and strikes the tip, illuminating the whole area with a blinding light. As quick as it appeared, the light dissipates and Pendragon is left with a wondrous look etched upon his face? Pendragon: Truly mine prayers this night have struck a chord? With a final bow, he sheathes the sword and climbs from the boulder. He smoothes out a spot next to the boulder and lies down for a slumber. Soon, he breathes deeply as the fire begins to slowly die down to the embers. As the coals begin to glow red, the sounds of people creeping about can be heard’Shadows pass by in the darkness. Pendragon doesn’t seem to notice, and the shadows creep closer, and heavy breathing can be heard. At last, the light rasp of steel being drawn permeates the darkness, and still Pendragon lies still?. This will be easier than we thought?. With a flash, a large figure brings his sword down, striking for the sleeping king. Quicker than the eye, though, Pendragon has drawn Excalibur, gained his feet, and deflects the blow of the sword? Pendragon: And what have we here? Cowards who would attack a sleeping man in the middle of the night? The moonlight reveals three heavy looking figures, each brandishing a Saracen sword. The curved blades gleam deadly in the pale light? Figure 1: Let’s rush him’Akumancha said this threat needs to be gone? Pendragon smiles a sorrowful smile as he drops into a defensive position, the blade pointed at his enemies as he holds it with both hands over his head? Pendragon: My heart weeps for thy souls which will soon depart from thy bodies? With a grunt, the three figures rush the golden giant. Their blades are a blur in the darkness, but Pendragon’s is almost invisible with the speed by which it moves. Each blow that they aim is deflected, and here and there cuts begin to open up on the three. Blood drips from wounds to their arms’Their torsos’Their thighs. Finally they pull back, panting? Figure 1: How can one man be so fast? Figure 2: Enchantment’Akumancha warned of this? Figure 3: He is a devil? Pendragon smiles wryly at the three? Pendragon: A devil indeed I am not, however empowered though I may be. I gave the three of thee a chance to pass by, and you chose instead to attack. Thy maker awaits thee?. With a roar, he throws himself into them, his blade a blinding blur in the moonlight. The three swordsmen are unprepared for the ferociousness with which he attacks, and soon the claret truly begins to flow. One man falls, his guts a steaming mass on the ground as Excalibur opens his stomach’At last, a head flies into the air and it’s headless body falls without life into a heap on the ground. Blood covers the last two combatants, but Pendragon wears only the blood of his enemies. The heavy figure sags with exhaustion. He finally drops to his knees as Pendragon knocks his sword from his hand? Figure 1: Would you kill an unarmed man? Pendragon: Would you? He draws the sword back and swings, and off comes the man’s right hand. He screams and grabs at the stump with his left? Pendragon: Thou I shall let live. Go and tell Akumancha that I will not be quelled by knaves in the night. Go and tell him that I shall never be stopped, and soon?.Very soon, the fight will be coming to him’Within the walls that are mine by my birthright. He smiles darkly as he continues? Pendragon: Thy hand I have taken so that thee shall never again raise sword to mine countenance again? Figure1: *whimper* Pendragon: Am I not merciful? Glory and honor?..And now I wish thee to be gone from mine sight. Thy brethren at arms shall be food for the vultures’Carrion that will be left to rot and picked up by whatever is hungry enough to do so’Now go? The shot centers on Pendragon, wiping his blade as the scene fades?. TO BE CONTINUED
