The Long Road of Championism (Ch.3)

29 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-29-04 Prologue. Demons were something every person had, whether they came by singles or by groups, everybody had them, and at one point in life had or would face them. That’s what one man thought would never happen though, and he is now facing a demon so large that his beloved treasures of life are being sucked dry from his mind and being replaced with the sinful acts of his past. We learned this all when Jesse relived a bit of his past in his mind, the happenings on one given night where Jesse’s stepmother was beaten by his stepfather and the family covered it up by lying and not telling anyone. If that didn’t get to your conscious what would? Jesse being a man with a lot of dark secrets he definitely felt this with an impact. Bottled secrets brewed inside him and with each memory came more hate filling his heart. What will Jesse do to snap out of this trans and get into the flow of wanting to wrestle? Or will he not? Is it even possible for a man in such a sick state of mind to do such a thing, or does that call for a miracle? One way or another fears will be confronted, secrets will be revealed, and hope will be possible if all goes right. Story begins. Still rocking back and forth Jesse was planted firmly in the chair still gazing off into his own little world. The creaking of the chair had begun to slow down and Jesse’s trans was wearing off. He wasn’t out of it yet, but he was building a platform to step out of it, and that was better than nothing. Above the panel floorboards a scraping noise is heard, a door opening perhaps. Then footsteps confirmed that someone had entered the house. The boards creaked above Jesse’s head and as the person walked by dust had polluted the air from between the cracks of the floorboards. The sunlight that reached the cellar floor blinked off then on as the person above walked by. Moments later a trap door was opened and then a man climbed down inside to see Jesse sitting their stuck stiff as if a corpse. The man reached over and touches Jesse instantly awaking him from the trance. Turning his head Jesse stared at the figure and as it came from the dark and into the light Jesse’s best friend Ryan Bladez showed a face of foreboding and gratitude. Besides the hand he touched Jesse with Ryan left Jesse where he was, for even when somebody was out of shock the person could act differently or be light headed for a bit. Ryan sat down on the steps and stared at Jesse, who stared at the floor in front of him blinking his eyes every few seconds. ’How’d you find me?’ Jesse asked out of nowhere, as if he wasn’t sure where he was. ’We were suppose to go to the gym today to train for your match and you never showed, so after checking your girlfriends hotel, I came here… You know I know you best Jess, please tell me you’re okay?’ Ryan inquired as he leaned forward to get a better look of Jesse. He couldn’t answer right away though for the answer he searched for was far from that of what he had in mind. So he just nodded his head in a ‘yes’ fashion and kept staring at the floor. ’I know you’re lying but I don’t care. I just want to make sure you’re okay enough to wrestle… because if not, you’re not going into that ring with those other five guys,’ insisted Ryan as if he were Jesse’s mother. Yet it didn’t phase Jesse, he didn’t really care. He was just thinking hastily and with each thought he had a quick answer for. Ryan reached over and slapped Jesse in the side of the face, taking him completely out of his little state of confusion. ’I’m fine, really I am,’ responded Jesse looking over at Ryan and staring at him as he did at the floor, ’I’ve just been thinking… and I came to this conclusion that I’ll never be like my father.’ Ryan looked at him awkwardly, no doubt Jesse wouldn’t be like his father he thought and showed with his facial expressions, he was someone totally different. ’Why would you be thinking about your father? ’No… not that father, my stepfather,’ Jesse affirmed to clear up all lose ends for Ryan, ’about the past and what he did… I never want to be like him Ryan… If I ever am, I want you to ki-‘ Though before Jesse could finish Ryan hushed him, ’I’ll do nothing the sort brother, we’re blood, and I don’t kill family, even if I’m asked to in the mists of crisis, you’ll die peacefully and without me as your last image. However you’re not dying today Jess, so shut up about it.’ Ryan was a big guy and his voice was deep, so when he argued he put up a convincing stern voice that would make him sound more serious than he was. Grabbing Jesse he picked him up under the arm and helped him un the stairs. Walking through the kitchen, down the hallway, and finally into the living room Ryan seated Jesse and went to get him a drink. When he came back Jesse was cracking his neck. Ryan didn’t bother to ask, he just gave Jesse the water and let him guzzle it down. Sitting down opposite of Jesse, Ryan showed his massive body to be something that could barely fit into Jesse’s house. The chair slowly sank in as his large backside eased into it. ’You definitely need to get new furniture man,’ chuckled Ryan as he leaned back easily and waited for Jesse to speak. Eyes wondering again Jesse was seeing things, and then all of a sudden his eyes went completely white. The room froze as if crystallized and then a man came out of Jesse’s chest like a spirit, sitting on the armrest of where Ryan had his arm. It was the same man Jesse had spoken to the day of his first Global Title Match, his father, his real father. The man now had white hair receding down from his forehead all the way back over his head not showing any baldness but that which was in the front. His eyes the same navy blue as Jesse and the facial structure quite similar except for the age difference. This was of course without doubt Jesse Fristik Senior. ’Jesse… I am glad to be here to speak to you son… I know you’re wondering what is going on, for you’re probably livid about now, but don’t worry I’ll explain,’ Jesse Senior stated before going on to explain, ’Jesse, you’ve been seeing things that happened a long time ago, and it’s bothering you quite a bit, maybe more so than what you wish it to, but trust me, it will all mean something down the road. You’re a gifted person Jess, and I want you to use that to your advantage. Forget stressing over these little things, and get all your secrets out… Otherwise you’ll be like me.’ Jesse couldn’t talk, he wasn’t transfixed or anything, he was just stunned in awe as he saw a hologram type figure in front of him, not like a ghost but as if he were looking into the mirror of the future. ’Boy, you’ve got a long time to go before life is shortened for you, and so I want you to remember this when I leave. If you choose your paths and not let the people around you influence your choices then one day you’ll be told what all this stuff has to do with you. For now, I’m just going to leave you with knowing you’ll be happy in life… I’m always watching after you son,’ he exclaimed before vanishing into thin air. The room became normal within a snap of the fingers and Jesse stared over at Ryan who was sitting there waiting for some sort of reply about the furniture. ’Hey, you okay? Ryan interrogated in a casual voice but trying to find reason in Jesse. Jesse could only shake his head yes as he thought it all out. Inside his head much became clear, he realized everything he’d been doing was wrong, he hadn’t even prepared for the match as he usually did, and that he was more worried about what was going on in his head than what his opponents may do to him in the ring. Beyond all that, Jesse wasn’t focused because of all the stress he put upon himself. This wasn’t the Jesse Jamester who’d beaten Apocalypse, Chris Storm, and Patti Anderson to gain his championship, no it was someone totally different. Inside he could feel the difference, it was as if freedom had overtaken his soul and he didn’t care anymore, for what he had in his head now would only help him maintain his balance as a person. For once, Jesse felt… … Normal. As well ready to take on any challenge, whether it was the Mercenary Hessian, the American youth Josh, Iron Lung, Blake Wolfson, or Todd Dream… Jesse was confident in himself one hundred percent, as a true champion should be… … … …
