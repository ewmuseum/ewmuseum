Family Cookout

25 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/25/04 It’s such a nice feeling when family reunites after a long period of being away from each other. Donny and his brother Charles are doing just that, catching up on what they have been up to in the last year or so. What better way to do that than have a cookout with some friends and family at Harv’s house. Harv, Donny and Charles are all gathered around the grille talking over things as the steaks, and ribs are sizzling away on the hot charcoal grille. “You know living in the Big Apple you tend to forget what it’s like to have these cookouts and be outside and eat. It’s such a nice change of pace you know?” Charles said. “I can remember growing up my Dad always outside cooking on the grille, even in the dead of winter, there he was out on the grille cooking away. Just something about cooking over a flame like that, and not deep frying or sticking it in the microwave.” Harv said. “Charles wait till the fourth of July. Harv and I put on bigger and better pyrotechnic shows than anyone around. Thankfully I know all the police in the area. A lot of the stuff we have is actually homemade, Harv’s brother Jim makes it?” Donny said. Charles looks over at his brother with a look of concern, not so much for Donny’s safety but the idea in this day and age the ATF, FBI, and the CIA might be making a visit to Harv’s brother, Harv, or even Donny himself about these homemade pyrotechnics.. “See that worries me there, I mean, you’re talking about homemade explosives. Does Jim understand that a lot of Federal agencies might be watching him or should I dare say, that THEY ARE WATCHING him right now?” Charles asked. “Ah no worries there, Jim used to work for the ATF. It’s all good. They’re not going to bother him in the least bit?” Replies Harv. Charles does nothing but just shake his head in disbelief, he might be wondering what he got himself into when he agreed to become tag partners with his younger brother. Charles quickly changes the subject. “So what does Mom think about the whole, loan thing, me partnering up with you and everything else?” asks Charles “Well for starters she’s excited to have you home again. But you know how Mom is. She’s always up for company. It’s too bad she’s busy with some church thing, but you know Mom and Church, she’ll do anything for God as long as it makes sense. She wasn’t mad about the whole loan thing when she finally found out the whole ordeal, she’s confused just as much as I am on what in the hell is going on with one minute we’re going against White Rose and the next minute it’s The Syndicate. You know me I want to get to the bottom of this whole mess Monday night. If I don’t get an answer then, well I’ll be asking Miss. McCoy for answers.” “Donny that’s always been one of you shortfalls, you want answers and you want them now. If I teach you anything in the next couple of weeks it’s going to be patience. I know it’s something that doesn’t run in the family, but there is a chance you could learn it.” Charles said. Harv looks at Charles with a slight grin. “Good luck this is Donny were talking about.” Chuckles Harv. “Asshole” replies Donny “Now Donny, what could we expect out of The Syndicate, I want an idea about these two and what they’re about, almost like a economic forecast?” asks Charles? “With those two it’s simple, hand them a meatball, tell them Mario and Luigi finally saved Princess Toadstool, kick them in the stomach, down they fall and go for the pin. Ok so it’s not that easy, basically these two will do anything they can to cheat on the match. Don’t let them try and intimidate you with their whole mafia thing. They’re beatable and they’re not the toughest tag team in Global. I beaten them before Chuck, I beat them by myself. They have nothing?” “I hope you’re right” replies Charles. Harv takes the ribs and steaks off the grille “Alright guys, it’s time to eat, I don’t know about you but I am starving.” Said Harv. “Chuck we can talk about this later and on the flight on the way out to Lincoln.” Said Donny. “Good idea” said Charles. The trio sit down to eat, Wrestlecade is merely hours away, what will happen when The McNasty Brothers, have their in ring debut against The Syndicate. Tune into Wrestlecade only on PAY PER VIEW.
