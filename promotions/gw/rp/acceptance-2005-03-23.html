Acceptance

23 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03.23.05 'Acceptance' One day after arriving in Rome, Ken Partridge, Jr. awoke from a satisfying slumber next to Gunner Addison just as the sun was beginning to rise. The sight of her on that morning instantly brought a smile to the Superstar's face as her radiance was intensified only by the sunlight that snuck through the lush hotel curtains. As he sat, covered only slightly by the silk sheets that topped the feather bed, he couldn't help but get lost in her beauty. Her gorgeaous honey colored hair was sprawled about the bone colored pillows while her perfect body was outlined wonderously by the sheets that felt so seductive on their skins. He leaned in and kissed her gently on the lips, a kiss in which she returned in her sleep, bringing an even wider smile across Ken's face. He gazed at her face once more before rolling to the other side of the bed and allowing his feet to hit the floor. He reached down and grabbed a pair of black silk boxers, pulling them to his waist. He sat there, hands on his thighs, looking out of the large glass doors onto the streets of Italy. It was truly a magnificent country, even moreso than the last time he had been there. Perhaps, it was due to the fact that he was in an entirely different mindset now, as a year ago he was spending his days of preperation in an attempt to save his best-friend's life. His head had been cluttered by a million different things, none of them offering clarity and all of them causing distraction. Josh had overdosed, there was tension forming between himself and Ace, and he was attempting to hold together the still very young Elite. On top of it all, he was preparing to step foot in the Global Rumble and cement himself into the Main Event at WrestleCade. How things had changed in a year, and all because of one spectacular person. Ken turned and gave Gunner another look before standing and exiting into the bathroom, where he dressed in a pair of white and blue 'And 1' cross-trainers, a pair of white athletic pants, and a white sleeveless 'Superstar' t-shirt. Last year, he sprinted the eleven and a half miles from the hotel to the Coliseum every day up to the event, leading to him being one of the last two men standing in the ring after being one of the first to enter. On top of that, it gave him plenty of time to reflect and meditate. Unlike the previous year, though, he wasn't out to shatter any time record that he had set for himself. This year, his goal was simply getting there and back. He walked back into the bedroom, giving the love of his life another kiss before he exited. However, as he reached the door, her melodious voice stopped him in his tracks. 'Where are you going,' she asked groggily through a waking voice. 'I'm sorry,' he replied with a large smile on his face as he walked back over to the bed. 'I didn't mean to wake you.' 'Don't dodge the subject,' she retorted with a smile on her face. 'Running,' he answered honestly, now sitting beside her on the bed. 'I thought I'd go ahead and get it in for the day while you were still sleeping.' Gunner reached up, allowing the sheets to fall from her nude body as she wrapped her arms around Ken's neck, pulling him down with her, both laughing before coming eye-to-eye in a lovers' gaze. 'I'm not asleep now,' she said, breaking the silence with a sensual tone of voice. Ken moved his lips to her's as he wrapped his arms around her, engaging in one of the most true and emotional kisses that many could ever hope of sharing. He ran his hands through her hair, taking in a breath of her sweet smell as he did so. She wrapped her arms around his waist, grabbing the shirt he had just put on and pulling it over his head. In that instant, there seemed to be things more important in Ken's life than wrestling, and on thing in particular... her. He moved his mouth to her ear, fighting through the short breathes that were coming from both of their lungs. 'I love you so much.' She simply pulled away and looked into his eyes, her own moist with tears of joy yet to be cried. 'I love you, too,' she responded with a smile on her face. With those four words, Ken knew that he could accomplish all; that he could fight his way through the very pits of hell to take on Lucifer, the dark prince himself. Suddenly, the worries that troubled him seemed insignificent. He placed his hand on her cheek and met her kiss again with as much passion as can be transfered between two physical beings. 'Stay,' she said softly. 'Stay with me.' Through her soft and tender voice, it was as if he was looking through the entire world through new eyes. For once, training was not the most important thing to him, she was. The peace that he felt while seeing the smile on her face and joy in her eyes far surpassed the adrenaline he experienced in the ring. He could deny nothing, he was a wrestler, it was in his blood, and he always would be. However, he'd never experienced anything like this before. He'd never loved or been loved like this in the past. He could never deny that Gunner was the best thing that had ever happened to him. He could never deny that this woman was his body, heart, and soul and that he would give his last breath just so that she could live for a few seconds more. 'I'll never leave your side,' Ken admitted honestly. Gunner smiled before replying, 'I know.' Her mind began to race as the two of them lay there, holding one another. How had she become so lucky as to fall in love with this man wrapping his arms around her was all that ran through her mind. Apprantly, she had no idea that he was thinking the same in regards to her. She knew the relationship was, and always would be an unconventional one. The simple fact that they had woken up in Rome this morning, after leaving Honolulu before, was a testament to that. While stays in both Haiwaii and Italy may be a fantasy of most couples, few get to live it, and even fewer have to worry about the one they love having a match while there. Ken was a wrestler, she knew that, and would never attempt to keep him from that. She being one herself, she wasn't going to even ask for him to end his dream, as very few got the opportunity to do it at all, let alone on his level. She also knew that this brought about sacrifices and, while she may not like some of them, he would and had done the same for her. 'Go,' she said. 'What,' he asked, surprised and taken off guard. 'Go,' she repeated. 'Go and train. You've got a huge match coming up and you need to prepare for it.' 'I'd rather stay here with you,' he answered, pulling himself closer to her. 'I would, too,' she said, 'but you need to get ready for your match. Besides, we'll have this evening and all night to be together.' Ken simply looked at her with amazment and adoration in his eyes before getting up and pulling his shirt back over his head. 'Okay,' he responded, 'but we have a date tonight... doing whatever you want to do.' She smiled at him and pulled him back down for another kiss. Ken resisted the urge to fall beside her again and smiled. 'You're too good to me, Gun.' 'No, I'm not,' she responded. 'We're just perfect for each other.' Ken smiled and kissed her again before making his way out the room, exchanging another 'I love you' before grabbing his iPod and exiting, heading to the elevator. Once inside, he began to stretch, preparing himself for a very intense and physical work-out. The entire run would be twenty-three miles total, there and back, with who knew how many inclines and bumps along the way. There was one thing he was doing different this time, however, as his eyes held nothing but peace and his mind rested on noone but Gunner. Not Christian Zenith nor Ice nor Corvus... not on any of his opponents, but on is inspiration. As the elevator doors opened with a chime, he made his way to the large front doors of The Plaza and stepped out, plugging the ear buds into his ears and setting the MP3 player on shuffle. Ken looked down the steps of the resort as The Rolling Stones' 'Wild Horses' began to ring throughout his ears. He immediatly rushed into a sprint, brething in the brisk air and feeling the slight most of the oncoming rain against his skin. He could feel his legs burn as the msucles had begun to work rigorously. He loved this... all of this... and everything it entailed; the matches, the titles, the training... all of it. After all, he couldn't deny the fact... that he was a wrestler.
