California

6 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 6 (For Evo) The scene opens up to a bear grey street. No cars are moving whatsoever, Roswell is seen with Father Clark walking down the street. Roswell and Father Clark walks by a old gas station and see broken windows and the gas pumps laying on the ground. Roswell and Father Clark walk over to the gas pumps and one by one they put them back onto the stand. From the broken window a voice is heard. Roswell and Father Clark walk to the door and knock on it. They walk in to see who is in there. Voice: You dare touch my gas pumps! Roswell: We were just putting them onto the stands. Voice: Well I don’t want your filthy hands on the gas pumps. Roswell: Uhhhh, my hands are not filthy. Roswell and Father walk out of the station looking confused. Roswell then remembers something from his head. Roswell starts talking to Father Clark about who that man was. Roswell: that guy looked familiar. Father Clark: Who was it? Roswell: I don’t know really who that was but he looked familiar. Roswell and father start walking again down the street. They walk by a old cracker factory which has a sign on the fence saying. “I killed myself!”, Roswell and father ignore the sign and keep walking down the street. They also walk by a newspaper factory, clothing factory, and other stores that were taken down. Father: So you were saying you were going to move to California? Does your mom know about this. Roswell: No not yet, I am moving there because there is a brand new Training Centre that I am going to try out. Father: Is that kind of why you said you could never come back to Larry’s Training Centre? Roswell: Yes that is mostly why. Roswell and father who have been walking for about 5 blocks stop and sit on a old rusty bench. They start chatting about numerous things. At the same time they both stand up from the bench and start walking down the street again. Finally after a couple of minutes they reach Roswell’s big blue house. They walk up the metal stairs and open the door with a silver key. They walk into the house and sit down on a couch. Roswell immediately flies off the couch and run’s up the stairs into his bedroom. In his bedroom Roswell opens up a closet door and brings out a suitcase. Roswell set’s it on his bed and starts putting clothes into the suitcase. Father walks into the room and sit’s down on a brown chair. Father: When are you leaving? Roswell; My plane leaves in 1 hour, so I have to start packing and saying goodbyes. Father: I want to come! Roswell: What? Father: I want to come with you! Roswell: What about the church? Father: My backup will take care of it. Roswell: Are you sure you want to do this? Father: Yes I am sure. Roswell Ok, let’s go it will take a half an hour to get to the airport. Roswell grabs his suitcase and him and Father walk out of the door, Roswell stops at the phone and phones his mother. “I am moving to California, I will come and visit every week bye.” Roswell and Father walk out the door and Roswell locks it, they walk over to the blue convertible and hop into the seats. Roswell put’s a different key into the slot and starts the car, they drive out of the drive way and down the street. After a while they put on the radio and listen to some tunes. A half an hour later they arrive at the airport doors. Roswell gives the cars to the people and they drive it into a locked garage. Roswell and Father go into the doors and walk over to a counter. Roswell shows the lady a passport and she lets him through. Father who has planned for this already also brings out a passport and shows the lady, they walk over to the seats and wait till there flight is called. Roswell: This is going to be fun. Father: Oh ya, you said it. After 20 minutes Roswell and father’s flight get’s called and they go to the doors where you walk in. Roswell and Father show the other lady there passports and walk through the doors and walk down a long narrow hallway. Roswell and Father walk down some stairs and walk outside to the sunlight. They run to the stairs lifting them up to the plane. Roswell and Father walk over to the curtain and ask the lady’s. Roswell: We reserved for 1 seat. Lady: We need to see your passports. Roswell and Father show the lady there passport’s and then walk through the curtain. They walk to the front and sit down on nice comfy chairs, they put there luggage onto a little stretcher and lay back waiting for the plane to leave. The plane starts moving and they take off the ground, Roswell ask’s the lady to come to him and she does. Roswell: Could I please have a bag of peanuts. Lady: Yes you can have peanuts, just hold on. The lady walk’s away but then walks back with peanut’s in her hand. She hands them to Roswell and he starts eating them fast. Roswell brings out a pillow from his suitcase and put’s it behind his head and starts going to sleep. TO BE CONTINUED
