Making a Game Plan

13 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 13th I lay there, sleeping on my couch, thinking about Jesse Jamester. We were teamed for Evolution, and were against his arch enemy, and another man, that has been randomally put into this match. Me and Jesse have been trying to tag team for awhile here, but there has been some ups and downs, but we will always be the greatest tag team in the world. I know Jesse has been going through some tough problems, first of all, Jetsui killing his girlfriend, which put Jesse away in the jail cell for a little bit of time. Next of all of the stuff that has happened in his life over the past few months, I can see have been killing him inside. He has been telling me that he just wants to lay down and die and never come back to life. Just lay in his bed and wait for himself to die. but I wouldn't let that happen, so we went to the bar last night. We had a great time, with Jesse evantully meeting a girl named Amanda. Jesse was at home, right now, lying on his shredded couch, thinking of all of his memories. I got out of my bed, I was sweating like a pig. I had a white shirt on, with some very old pants. I don't really know why I wore that to bed, maybe because I was half drunk coming home last night I just went to sleep with my clothes still on me. I took the shirt off and walked over to my dresser that was in the corner of the room. I opened the top drawer and pulled out another shirt, except this one was black, and it was a tank top. It was so hot outside, you could wear pretty much nothing. But that would be sick, so I just put on very soft materials, to wear out there. I walked out of the room with my Nike shoes on. I needed to find Jesse, and ask him what our game plan was going to be in our first tag team match on Evolution. I walked down my creaky stairs, that I still didn't get fixed. They were going to snap if I walked on them once more, I could tell by the creak's and all when I walked down them. I walked out of the front door, and locked it. Jesse's house was a block away from mine. He was probably still sleeping, but it wouldn't pay to wake him up, so I'll just wait. I started walking slowly down the sidewalk of the street. There were a few other people walking in front of me, but most of them were old people with canes helping them to walk. There was also a man witha remote controlled wheelchair to help him go to where he would want to go. I walked past him, he gave me a small smile. I smiled back at him. By the looks of his eyes, he looked like a very nice person to be friends with. I came to a stop. I had to cross the street to get to Jesse's house, so I waited for all of the traffic to go by, and then I started walking across the street. I walked across, and was already really close to his house. It was a few houses away from where I was standing. I started walking a little faster, even though I wasn't in a big rush. I got to his house. It was the usual, it was kind of red with black, and the door was white. There was a gate at the front yard to get to the door. I walked up the little cement stairs, and opened the gate and walked up to the door. I rang the door bell, and a few seconds later, Jesse opened the door. He looked very tired in his eyes. 'Hello.' I said in a soft calm voice. 'Hey man. What's up?' he said. 'Nothing. I just came here to ask you what our game plan was for this upcoming Evolution. We are tagging again Jetsui and Freeze.'I said. 'Oh, that's right I know we had a match. It just kind of slipped out of my head.' he said. I walked into the house, as he shut the door behind me. Inside his house looked like a mansion. Big staircase, and everything you could dream for. I walked around, staring at all poof the cool stuff he had in his house. Jesse walked into the living room. I followed behind him. He sat down on one of the couches, as I sat down on the same one. We needed a game plan, because Evolution was just tomorrow, and we didn't know what we were going to do. Me and Jesse started talking about the game plan as the scene started to fade.
