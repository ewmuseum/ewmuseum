Teenage Recoil

9 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/09/05 'Let's do this! Leroooooooy Jeeenkins!' If Mindy knew how to use her cousin's microphone, she'd be screaming at the crazy man who had just run into the dark, dragon infested dungeon on a suicidal death wish. Not to say that she wasn't screaming at all, but at least if she could figure out what button was for her microphone, this Leroy person would at least know of her unbridled anger. World of Warcraft interested her ever since she started seeing her cousin play that past Christmas; it just hadn't been until around that time where she actually had enough to free time to play it. Besides, she needed a painless way to take out the frustrations of her life- both in and out of the ring problems alike. Before Mindy could throw her pixie stix at the flat panel monitor, however, her character- well, her cousin's character- had died. Maybe it wasn't such a good idea to start off playing around level sixty monsters with only five minutes of actual in-game experience. 'Well shazbot!' Mindy grabbed one of her orange flavored pixie stix, ripped open one end of it, and poured the sugary contents into her mouth. 'Mm, yummehness. Now to wash this down with some Lime Co-' To Mindy's sadness, she cut herself off upon finding her soda can empty. Mindy lifted it up and shook it; maybe there was at least one drop left? She turned the can over- directly over Michael's bedroom carpet. 'Blech... time to get more, I guess,' she mumbled, disregarding the droplets of dark liquid that fell to the pale blue carpet. Picking herself up out of her cousin's rolling chair, Mindy promptly began to dance over to the bedroom exit- singing the song from the Lime Coke commercial as she went. 'Mikey has to teach me that game some time,' she pouted to herself. After being benched by her own cousin last week in favor of that Liang fellow, Mindy was quite depressed; not to mention confused. How could he possibly choose some stranger to be his tag team partner, and not her? She refused to believe it was about her wrestling ability; one measly loss couldn't have really meant that much, could it? Even her boyfriend said he would be happy with how far she had gotten in Global so far, win or lose. Could Michael really be so discouraged over her loss at Wrestlecade? It hurt her head to think about it; not to mention how annoying it was trying to make sense of how Michael and that T'sao Liang even knew eachother, so it was probably for the best that she attempted to keep positive thoughts in her mind. It was also important for her to stay positive with her first- and hopefully not her last- Gold and Glory match coming up in a couple of days. Mindy had faced Adam Myers before, back when she was trying to remain the Television Champion and when he was trying to take that championship from her. She managed to escape defeat from him on two occasions, but now there was a major difference: it was now Mindy trying to take down the Platinum Champ, and Adam seemed to have improved his odds greatly since they last faced. Suffice to say, this wasn't setting itself up to be an easy match that one could mistake it for. Mindy would have to prove to herself that she was more than lucky on that string of wins- and to accomplish that, she was going to do her damndest to set herself up for another one of those winning streaks. 'First thing's first, though... Lime Coke Lime Coke Lime Coke Lime Coke.' Mindy placed the empty can into the bucket of plastic and glass recyclables by the refridgerator, before throwing the fridge door open and grabbing a full can. 'Mikey?' the nineteen year old called out as she nudged the door shut with one of her platform boots, 'Mikey, you awakey wakey?' Normally such a question would be pointless to ask at three in the afternoon, but that was leaving out one quite relevant rule: Michael wasn't much of a normal person in the first place. That gave Mindy little room for surprise when she found her older cousin sprawled out and face down on the living room couch. The remote control to the television across from him was clutched tightly in his left hand, which was draped over the couch and hovering just above the ground, and black and red mane sloppily covered most of his face from sight. Mindy gave off a slight smile at how peaceful Michael looked- especially given his inability to sleep as of late- but she couldn't help but sigh inwardly. 'You deserve not having to deal with any of this,' she confessed quietly. 'You've been nothing but nice to me, anyone who could take from you like this person obviously has no heart...' Mindy held out her soda can and popped it open. A soft fizz sounded and a small bit of foam leaked out of the top, and Mindy was quick to slurp the drops of liquid caffiene until there was no more of it looking to get on her and the floor. 'Damn soda with a mind of its own.' Mindy cursed the Lime Coke can in her hand, before her attention went back to her sleeping cousin. She needed to be serious, if at least only for him. 'I'll find out who did this, cuz, I swear it.' She would help her cousin get revenge. Everything was going to sort itself out, and everything would be just fine in the end. It all had to end okay, and there was no reason for it to turn out in any other manner than that. Feeling content with her promise, Mindy turned and made her way back to her bedroom, her soft padding footsteps rythmically shuffled off to quiet muffled creaks on the wooden floor through the pink carpeting in her own room. The young sugarholic plopped down on her queen-sized bed and placed her soda on the nightstand nearby. She would only think happy thoughts. She wouldn't think about Jaden and Gabriella being taken away. She wouldn't think about the loss to Jake Hix. She wouldn't think about her cousin's decision to not let her wrestle at the previous Evolution. 'I just wish I had someone around to keep me calm... it would make things so much easier.' Almost on cue, some kind of tapping noise made itself known outside of Mindy's bedroom window. 'Mew?' She sat up and turned around, sliding the window up and pushing the curtains out of the way. Mindy grinned, and waved out at the person she was staring back at: her boyfriend Markus, who had a handful of pebbles in his hand that he had been lobbing against the window screen. 'Why hello there,' he smiled, waving back to his girlfriend. Mindy giggled and shook her head. 'Hello, Markus... stones? Why would you throw stones at my window? You could climb on in if you wanted to.' 'I didn't want to risk it. Might strain something.' 'It's only a one story house!' 'But I strain easily.' Mindy proceeded to reach out of the window and bop Markus on his head, shielded from the sun- and more importantly, the bopness- by his ever-present samurai hat. 'Why don't you stay here anyway?' the teenager asked. 'You know you're welcome here and everything... so why won't ya stay with me and keep me company?' Markus chuckled, adjusting his hat a little bit as he replied. 'Well you know I don't feel comfortable around your cousin, and all... I mean you should know that he's got more to worry about, he shouldn't have to deal with me running around and making myself a guest in his house.' With a smirk, Markus dropped the small rocks he had been holding, and gently took his love's hand. 'After all, if you want me to keep you company, you and I should get our own place. Think about it- me and you, livin' it up in Nawleans.. could be fun.' He gave a playful, joking wink. Instead of Mindy answering her boyfriend's proposal, however, she tugged on his hand and pulled it to the window. 'Well we're not in New Orleans right now, hunny bunny, and I want you in this room with me, so climb on up here, lazy bones!' Both of them shared a laugh, and Markus gave in to his girlfriend's wishes. 'Just make sure I don't fall, I don't want to sprain an ankle or anything.' 'Oh geez, silly, just jump!' They were both giggling hysterically as Mindy assisted Markus in getting through the window, only to get louder when they both fell over onto the floor, Markus conveniently landing on top of the caffiene fiend. 'Well then,' Markus said, staring downward into Mindy's beautiful green eyes. They were a sight, indeed. Mindy looked back up at Markus, and slowly grew bold enough to reach up and slide the large hat off Markus' head, tossing it aside so she could revel in the cuteness of her lover's messy looks. Mindy had been begging him to cut that black hair of his, but still she couldn't deny that he looked sweet with his hair matted down- that sloppy, dorky kind of sweet. 'This is.. erm.. weird,' Mindy mentioned through a nervous smile, and immediately she flinched. The way that sounded wasn't the best way she could've put it; she was falling for that weird dude who had fallen on top of her- literally. He wasn't one of those creepy metrosexual guys who only gave a shit about their looks, Markus was one of those nice guys who let himself dictate every part of his own life; a rare find in a day and age where people find themselves more dependent on others than ever before. If only Markus understood that Mindy felt that way. As far as he was concerned, he felt convinced Mindy only agreed to be with him out of sympathy for being hit by a car. And not for nothing, two months ago when that incident had actually taken place, Mindy probably would have admitted to that. But now... The ringing of the phone on the dresser drawer across the room had caused Markus to grow even more nervous, and it showed. 'Sorry,' the mellow man in black spoke timidly, before he slowly pushed off the floor and got himself to his feet. Mindy lay there still on the ground, and she was silently wishing that Markus hadn't moved. However, to her dismay, there was no way she could possibly grow the courage to ask him to valiantly climb through her bedroom window one more time. Instead, it would be best if she answered the phone. 'I'll get it.' Mindy could hear Michael yell lazily from the living room, and there went Mindy's second plan of getting up from the floor. Markus had noticed his girlfriend and her refusal to stand up, and he managed to elicit a chuckle. 'Need help, sweetness?' Mindy blinked those bubbly green eyes and smiled meekly, reaching up and clenching her hand open and shut. 'Please,' she squeaked. 'Hm, should I tug him down, or should I save it for some other time?' Before Mindy could answer her thought, she felt herself being yanked up to her feet. 'Thanks,' she smiled gratefully, before she threw her arms around Markus in as much of a bear hug she could give the six foot six giant. Markus' eyes closed as he felt his girlfriend's warm embrace; he wished those kinds of moments came more often, especially considering he knew for a fact that they both needed them. However in the end, he wanted nothing more than to tell Mindy that thing's would be alright.. that he loved her, and that he would be able to pull her through everything that's been troubling her. When his eyes opened and he stared down as the sugarholic glomping him, though, it just didn't feel right. Was he really all she'd need for her to feel better? He surely didn't think so. Mindy pulled away from him, and by the exchange of looks they gave eachother, he somewhat expected an 'I love you' to escape those cherry red lips of hers, but he got something else- it gave him a smile none the less, but it just wasn't an able substitute for those three important words. 'Trench coat, leather boots, big black jeans, samurai hat... how can you possibly wear all this black clothing outside? It has to be at least a hundred degrees out there.' Markus blinked, and looked himself over. After a moment's silence and no reasonable answer popping up in his head, he shrugged. 'Sunlight pisses me off. Oh, and it's a hundred and nine degrees out by the way. I check up on the weather every morning before I head out, 'tis an important thing.' 'Like it matters,' Mindy replied, covering her mouth momentarily to stifle her laughter, 'you wear the same kind of clothes all the time anyway.' 'What can I say? I like wearing black. It matches my hair.' The sarcasm in Markus' voice made Mindy grin, as she stepped aside to grab her boyfriend's hat. Upon doing so, she grabbed Markus by the hand and turned him around so they were facing eachother when she stood up on her bed. Now that she was at least eye level with her love with the help of her mattress, she lifted up Markus' hat and placed it on his head. 'You know, I keep telling you to get a haircut.' Markus guffawed. 'You really think I'd ever take a suggestion like that seriously?' He added his mockery of her half-hearted request and swung his head around slightly, causing his shoulder-length black mane to whoosh just past Mindy's face. Mindy play-slapped Markus on the shoulder, giggling. 'Don't do that, dangit!' 'Or what?' Markus grinned. Another moment of silence. Mindy batted her eyes shyly before she slid into her boyfriend's arms. She didn't notice that her feet weren't on the bed anymore; instead, her body was held up by Markus' grasp alone. How he wished he never had to let her go. 'Cousin?' If there was ever a time Mindy wished Michael wasn't around, that was most definitely the time. Michael stepped in just in time to see the couple's embrace, and it was a split second after that when Mindy slid out of Markus' clutches and her feet found the floor. He didn't seem quite happy with what he'd walked in on- even if it could've been much worse- but Michael had more important things to take care of. Besides, as long as Markus wasn't hurting her again, it wasn't that big of a deal. 'Uh, that was the Liang dude on the phone. I need to talk to him in person about Jaden, and some other things, so I'm going to be out for a little while, alright?' Mindy nervously nodded her head. 'Sure sure, Mikey. Jus' be careful, okie dokie?' Michael nodded in return. 'Of course. Seeya around, Mindy.' Michael turned slightly, but not before him and Markus made slight eye contact. 'Markus...' The large man looked back at Michael, and waved slightly. 'Take care, Michael.' Without anymore words, Michael was long gone. After that, Markus' attention was soon diverted back to his girlfriend. They looked longingly into eachother's eyes, but that one perfect moment they could have shared had passed. 'Well... you wanna watch some Trigun?' Mindy offered. Markus shrugged. 'Sure. Just as long as you don't go drooling at Vash and Wolfwood in front of me again. That makes me a sad panda.'
