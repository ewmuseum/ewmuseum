An interview with the vampire...hunter

31 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/31/2005 Dowtown Houston ? Museum District. A light drizzle falls on the concrete wasteland’covering the attempted restoration of downtown in a shimmery coat of water that runs off into the alleys leading the eyes to the harsh reality of the failure. For once it’s a quiet day’no headaches, noone hounding me about my past. No fans wanting autographs or pictures and no stinking GW officials wanting anything out of me. I can concentrate on the task at hand’finding out more about Corvus. Passing through these winter streets alone gives me respite, a chance to catch my breath and sort things out. Mark Kullypso tried to ruin my coming out party’a failure just like any plans Salaok has. He’s tried to get me out the way for a very long time now, but it just never quite works out for him. The city streets seem to slow down as DeathSun walks through them, the traffic parts, the birds fly away and even the rain halts for a moment as he emerges from his Escalade and walks towards the library. On the egde of the frame, someone can be seen shadowing DeathSun, just out of his view’the silhouette would indicate that it is a woman’and a shapely one at that. DeathSun moves through the aisles of books, passing section after dusty section of neglected tomes with an occasional bookish person behind a counter asking for quiet. A few people notice his passage but they say nothing, either they do not know him or they are in awe and unable to speak. He smirks, always appreciative of the rush being famous offers’especially in his adopted hometown. Those that do not know him from his fighting appearances know him as the outspoken owner of one of the rowdiest metal clubs in the country, Club Syn. Craiford is the owner/operator/booker and sometimes even gets behind the bar of the club not to mention a special fighting match every once in a while. DeathSun spots the books he is looking for and moves towards them, a wide smile on his face. He reaches for a few as he catches something out of the corner of his eye that makes him pause. Leaving the books on the shelf, he turns to face the person who has been trailing him since he exited his SUV’Noi Tsunami, reporter for GW. DeathSun ? So’looking for a book or something? Noi ? Not really, I’m looking for you. I’m from GW, Noi Tsunami’reporter. DeathSun ? An extremely sexy one at that’why me? Noi ? You will be facing Corvus tomorrow on Evolution’I’m here to find out your thoughts on him, what you think of his’lifestyle. DeathSun ? His lifestyle as you call it, is why I am here. I wanted to know more about what I am facing so I decided to do some research. Anne Rice, Bram Stoker, that sort of thing. Noi ? What about Laurell K. Hamilton? DeathSun ? Nah, that shit is so fake’Anita Blake’pfft. She just ripped off Anne Rice and Stoker for the most part’the good parts anyway, not sure where she got the crap parts from, maybe Corvus helped her write them. I’d rather read transcripts of Ken Partridge’s life story than read that crap’but not by much mind you. Noi - *laughs* I’ve done my homework on you Craiford’you’re world famous, won titles all over the world, dominated federation after federation. Why come back to GW, where you starred a few years ago but disappeared before ever getting a shot at the world title? Why start something with Salaok again, who has already had Mark Kullypso attack you. He could send him again this week. DeathSun ? Let him send Markie, I shrugged off his best and still beat Mani. I can do it again and walk all over the dead man this week. Salaok better send some heavier weapons if he wants to stop me. Noi ? What about my question? DeathSun ? The question of why? What you said is true’you have done a modicum of research, I will give you that. But you didn’t dig deep enough I guess. You see, I have history here’with Salaok, with Silent Wind, with Bubba McCoy ad others. A history I’m not thrilled about, a history I wish to make amends for. You see, when I left before I withdrew and left Silent Wind without a partner. We were tag champions’dominant champions at that. Noi ? The Elementals right? DeathSun ? Correct’but as much as I miss that, I miss never getting a shot at Salaok even more. So here I am’back in one of the places that propelled me to stardom. Call it full circle, call it returning to my roots. Just don’t call it a comeback’I’ve been here for years, to quote one LL Cool J. Noi ? Nice hip hop reference, if a bit dated. DeathSun ? Heh, well its about all the hip hop I know. Back to the reason why’I’ve fought all over the world, but I really want a shot at Salaok’one last shot to show him who the better student is. We both learned from his father’but I learned more. And tomorrow night the road to Salaok continues. It started with Mani, continues with Corvus and will end with Mr. Davis himself? Noi ? Thanks for your time Mr. Craiford...hope to see you sometime soon for a follow-up. DeathSun ? I’m sure something could be arranged’call my agent some time. She walks away, her long legs making beautiful strides down the aisle. She looks over her shoulder and smiles as she vanishes behind a bookshelf. Craiford’s heart flutters a bit, a boyish grin forming on his lips. He shakes his head to regain his composure, grabbing the books he needs and heading towards the check out desk. The scene fades, reopening in a taxi cab as DeathSun heads to the airport for his flight to Evolution’daydreaming, he flashes back to the courtroom. Michael Craiford, age 14 sits next to his lawyer, awaiting the judge’s sentencing. The jury had just let the hammer fall, declaring him guilty of two counts of murder. The looks on all their faces were just like needles piercing his soul. He knew what he did was wrong’but it had to be done’that bastard killed his mom. Now he was taking the blame for both of the murders’great’just great. The jail doors slammed shut’right as the taxi came to a halt at the airport?
