Unexpected visitor (High Octane RP)

9 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/9/06 The last bit of clothing had been packed into his suitcase. Now all that remained was for Brick to close it, lock it and then head out the door to go to the airport. Brick was on his way to Detroit to face the World champion Jake Hix at High Octane. Theirs had been an intense rivalry. Ever since the end of Reload, Brick had been looking for payback. Each time, Hix had managed to duck him, but at High Octane, he would finally get a chance at payback. The two were facing off in a Dog Collar match, in which to win, one had to touch all the turnbuckles in the ring in succession. However, unlike many other wrestling promotions, Global's ring was an Octagon, which means Brick would have to touch twice as many turnbuckles. Not to mention, the Dog Collar match was said to be Jake Hix' specialty. But Brick had made his whole career beating the odds. But there was one other who had been on Brick's mind. An old nemesis from his past, from the past of Global Wrestling. An individual he had hoped never to have to deal with again. That individual: the Scarecrow Jonathan Davis. The eldest brother of the man who had plagued Brick's life for most of his career in Global: Salaok. Suddenly, as he exited the bedroom and headed into the living room area, he heard the intercom buzz. 'The taxi's here already?' Brick thought to himself as he walked towards the intercom. 'Who is it?' Brick asked through the intercom. 'It's Eve Davis, Brick,' replied the female voice on the end. Right away, a look of shock appeared on Brick's face. Eve Davis was the younger sister of Jonathan and Salaok Davis. The general consensus was that Eve was the 'good Davis'. Even when she was part of Salaok's plot to torment her then-husband Justin Marks, she could be considered the 'good Davis'. Instinctively, Brick pressed the 'door' button, allowing her passage into the building. Brick pondered to himself as to why Eve Davis would be visiting him and how she got his address. His train of thought was broken when he heard his door bell ring. 'Eve?' 'Yes.' Brick reluctantly opened the door. There was no mistaking her features: the long platinum blond hair, the porcelain skin, the large sky blue eyes, the willowy and delicate figure. She wore a black dress with a knee-length skirt and sleeves coming down to above her wrists that contrasted with her pale complexion. 'Hello, Brick. May I come in?' Brick stuck his head to see if anyone else was with her. Where Davises were concerned, even the 'good' ones, Brick learned to be wary. 'I'm alone.' 'I'm kinda in a hurry, Eve,' Brick replied, uneasily. 'I'm tryin' to get to the airport.' 'This won't take too long.' She then stepped past Brick and walked into the apartment. Eve walked towards Brick's couch and took a seat. Brick followed close behind her, taking a seat opposite her on the loveseat. 'I figured this has been a long time coming. I heard about what happened between you and Salaok.' It was a topic Brick had tried to avoid for some time. Rumors had been whispered back and forth about something big happening between the two men and that whatever happened had to have been big because no one had seen Salaok for months. But things were happening that would leave no other option except for the truth to come out. 'Listen, Eve, about your brother...I'm sorry about...no, I can't lie to you. I'm not sorry about what I did to Salaok at all. I'm only sorry I didn't do it a long time ago. It'd have saved me and the people I love a lot of damn grief. Especially after what he did to--' Brick stopped himself short. 'I know you love your brothers. Statistically speaking, I guess somebody has to.' Eve looked down at the floor, her hands clasped together. 'You don't have to apologize to me for anything concerning my brothers. If anything, I should apologize to you. I always feared for them going down the path they were going. I used to beg them not to go down the same path our father went. The hatred, the cruelty, I knew it would consume them. I saw what they did to the Welches, to Justin Marks, to Mark Gunthree, to you. I was always afraid that eventually they would be destroyed as well. And Salaok came so close. So, I don't really blame you for what happened to Salaok. It would have happened, anyway.' 'Jonathan's back,' Brick replied. 'I'm pretty sure he's the one who attacked Brandi Sights.' 'I know.' 'Then you also know that I plan to do whatever it takes if he decides to come after me.' 'Yes, I do, and as much as I love my brothers, I won't blame you.' For a minute or so, there was an awkward silence between the two. Brick merely looked at this delicate-looking woman, a Davis in name and genetics only. Was she capable of the evil her brothers were capable of? Perhaps, but one wouldn't know it to look at her. Suddenly, the intercom buzzed yet again. 'Yes?' 'Taxi.' 'I'll be right down.' Brick walked over to pick up his suitcase. 'Listen, Eve, that's my taxi. I gotta go.' 'I understand,' she replied, rising off the couch. 'I'll see you out.' * * * As Brick and Eve walked out the front door, Brick's cab was parked right out front. The cab driver climbed out of the driver's side and opened the trunk. 'Do you need a ride somewhere? Because I can--' 'No, no. I have my car. Thank you.' 'Right, of course. Listen, if things go down between me and your brother, I want you to know that--' 'Like I said, Brick, you don't have to apologize.' 'Right.' 'Hey, boss!' called the cab driver in a thick Middle Eastern accent. 'You ready to go?' 'Oh, yeah, yeah, sorry,' Brick replied before placing his suitcase in the back trunk of the cab before getting in the backseat. As the cab drove off for the airport, Brick looked back at Eve Davis one last time before looking forward. Looking forward to High Octane. Looking forward to facing Jake Hix. Looking forward to winning the World title once again. For a minute or so, Eve stood watching the cab drive away before deciding to walk towards her car. Suddenly, Eve's cell phone began to ring. 'Hello?...Oh, I wasn't expecting to hear from you...I'm--just running some errands...what? What makes you think...you've been spying on me? Please, you've both put him and his family through enough. It needs to end....And you could end up the same way! Don't you see that?! Please, I'm begging you, don't...Jonathan? Jonathan!' But there was nothing but silence on the other end. She then got into her car, put on her safety belt, started the engine... ...and began to weep.
