Pleased To Meet You

5 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06.05.04 The funeral was yesterday. Time had passed and finally, the brother’s Nakazura emerge from the shadows. We had paid our respects to Gohei Nakazura after the service and now, watch from far away via long-range binoculars. TUE had long ago purchased a home near the Nakazura estate. It was under a Japanese associates name but it was clear who it belongs to. At this point, Maverick Reno, Aiden and Tobias de Sarret set up shop here. The second story living area had been invaded by a cybernetic world; enough equipment had been installed to command a small army. Maverick stands on the balcony, calmly watching through a pair of binoculars. It really isn’t necessary as cameras had been placed around and inside the mansion; they knew if someone was there by a simple glance at one of the laptops. Of course, Maverick is still insisted on the occasional manual search of the perimeter via binocular. Mid-day, it happened. The brothers arrive in a black Mitsubishi, a Gallant or an Eclipse. From this distance, the model can’t be made out. It really doesn’t matter; Reno knows that it’s them. Maverick grabs his trench coat and motions for Tobias and Aiden to do their jobs. Tobias quickly rushes downstairs as does Aiden. Maverick grabs a suitcase and heads down after the two. Once there, Tobias continues to head down into the basement as Aiden rushes through a back sliding glass door; Reno follows him out. In the backyard, it looks as if a large tarp covers a square building, nearly a story and a half tall but much smaller in width and depth. Aiden grabs a rope and tugs once and then again until the tarp begins to give way. Tobias has made it outside and quickly puts a helmet on before climbing onto a black and red Kawasaki Ninja. Quickly, Tobias adjusts the helmet and kick starts the powerful engine. Within moments, dirt flies up into the air and he’s off. In the back lot, the tarp has been fully removed to reveal a crude wooden frame shaped like a box; inside of this, a sleek black helicopter depicting TUE’s coat of arms. Normally, Reno would never allow such an obvious depiction of the Echelon ? it’s too risky ? but in this situation, it’s just another scare tactic for the Nakazuras. With the removal of a few quick bolts, the walls of the structure fall to the side so that the helicopter can easily lift. Maverick is the first to board the helicopter, soon followed by Aiden who assumes the pilot’s position. The engine roars up and the blades begin slowly, whirling at their locked position. Quicker and quicker, the blades pick up their pace. The blades of grass bend in circles, layers upon layers of circles extending away from the helicopter. A nearby lotus tree bends, showing the strength of the wind created by the helicopter’s blades. Up, up, and away the helicopter lifts into the air, the wind calming quickly around the lift off site. The only sign that it was ever there are the indentions in the ground, and the roar of the blades as they whip through the air. “Tobias. This is Maverick. Do you copy?” Reno barks out, closing the panel on his side of the helicopter to reduce the noise. There’s a moment of static? Finally, the static clears and Tobias answers, the roar of the engine below him just as overbearing as the one near Maverick. “Yeah, I copy. I’m about a kilometer behind them; we just left the city so I’m trying to keep a low profile. The beacon should be on; just follow me.” With that, the chase is on. Maverick, seated in the helicopter soars through the air. Times passes? 10 minutes 20 minutes 30 minutes 40? The time is coming near. “Maverick. Tobias calling; can you hear me?” Tobias calls out, having stopped the engine inside of his sportbike. “Yes, I read you. We’re zeroing in on your location; I assume they’ve stopped?” Reno speaks, his voice collected and calm. “No, but the road they’re on leads to only one place and I can still see them. Mt. Kidake’s temple is right in front of them. I’ll keep an eye on them so keep the radio open.” With this, communication cuts off and we switch to see the black Mitsubishi speeding, moving quickly up the mountain. The helicopter drops in altitude but picks up in speed, the mountain upon with Mt. Kidake’s temple is located is in sight and they’re quickly heading towards it. Before long, the black Mitsubishi is in sight; the target is locked. Maverick leans forward, his eyes peering down upon his victims. He smiles a devilish smile; that twisted smile that he does oh so well. The brothers finally stop the vehicle and eventually, the doors open. Slowly they walk into the field, a lavish garden at their rear and a temple not far away. Ichiro speaks as does Jushin, the conversation quiet and solemn. The wind begins to pick up for a moment but then calms again as the helicopter, remaining out of sight, hovers around the mountain, taking a new position above a forest as it approaches. Ichiro opens the canister and kneels, pouring the ashes quickly. Suddenly, Reno and his helicopter lift above the trees and approach them. Both Nakazura’s react, Ichiro less so than Jushin. Jushin draws a gun from inside his coat as Ichiro continues to pour the ashes. Finally, he drops the canister and stands. Something is said but the loud winds created by the helicopter make it impossible for Reno to hear it. On a hill, not far away, Tobias kick-starts his motorcycle and is off, heading down the hill and towards the area. Jushin glances behind him and notices the cyclist on the horizon. They’re ready for anything. “I stand by you brother!” Jushin yells out as Ichiro aims and starts firing at the helicopter, shot after shot after shot. The helicopter slows its approach; a panel opens on the side and Reno swings out. A large bullet-proof and steel barrier is in front of him, a large automatic-rifle aimed through a slit in this barrier. Reno begins firing, bullets riddling the ground causing dirt and dust to fly up everywhere. Jushin and Ichiro each leap to the side. Ichiro yells out quickly, his voice still calm, “If you stand with me, brother, then run with me! This is a battle to which we are disadvantaged!” Both the Ichiro and Jushin start running, heading towards the car that they arrived in, straying and zig-zagging. They do not take an immediate route; that would be foolish. They cut through trees and bushes, avoiding the gun-fire from above. The brothers separate for a moment but then find one another again; Ichiro leaps to the side, dodging a line of gunfire. Quickly the brothers break free of the shrubbery and race towards their car. Jushin is the first to get there; he jumps and slides across the hood. He pulls the handle of the door and tosses his own gun to his brother, Ichiro. Ichiro drops to a knee and takes aim, firing quickly and precisely at the helicopter. The armor plating, however, makes it a fruitless endeavor. Reno takes aim and opens fire, blasting holes into the hood of the car. Ichiro glances to the side and quickly calls out, “GET OUT OF THERE!” Jushin throws the door open as bullets shatter the windshield and destroy the car’s interior. A bullet strikes the gas-tank, causing a spark and then a spectacular light show. Jushin and Ichiro are thrown in opposite directions, both suffering a few burns as the car erupts in flames. “If we split up, we’ll be harder to hit. I’ll page you with where to meet once I shake them!” Ichiro’s voice calls out to his brother, his mind for strategy working to their advantage. With this, he receives a nod; Jushin is off and leaps over a row of bushes. Ichiro runs in the opposite direction, remaining in the clear for a moment. The helicopter lowers suddenly, the wind almost strong enough to knock a man down. The chopper lifts again and moves after Ichiro, chasing him down and through the more lavish side of the mountain. Jushin keeps running, sweat beading up on his face. Faster and faster, Jushin runs. He glances up now and again, looking for the helicopter but it is nowhere in sight. From behind him, he can hear a lone engine but this is enough to keep him on his toes and running for safety. Finally, Jushin reaches the bottom of the mountain but his journey is not yet done. He turns right, running across a gravel area, his feet kicking up rocks with every brutal step. Jushin turns a hard corner and drops, his back pressed to the wall as tightly as possible, his chest heaving in? out? in’out. Silence. Silence is bliss when noise is your enemy. The whirl of the helicopter had long since ceased and finally, after cutting through a few areas and over a fence, the second engine stopped as well. Jushin closes his eyes for a moment and presses his head back against the wall, still attempting to catch his breathe. That must be how the lamb feels as the wolf approaches the fence. Silence is all too fleeting for Jushin Nakazura. The sound of a footstep is heard; at this moment, with head and heart pound, adrenaline pumping, and his mind racing? that footstep sounded like a bomb being set off. Jushin pushes himself up and forces a run, throwing dirt and rock into the air. Within seconds, Jushin leaps into the air and catches the top of a wire fence only to flip over it and drop to the other side, running before he even hits the ground. Jushin is breathing heavily again, his arms pumping at either side, his legs beginning to tense and cramp with soreness. Jushin now runs, heading directly towards the center of the city, the marketplace nearby. People would be used to protect him? but it wouldn’t work. It’s not good enough. Jushin stops in the middle of the busy area, his shirt drenched in sweat, his hair soaking wet. He drops his knees to the ground, his hands landing a foot in front of them. He’s panting and heaving, his legs so sore and tense that he’s not even sure if he can move them anymore. His shoes? if it’s possible to burn rubber while running, Jushin had just done it. “I” ? He spoke between pants, not to anyone, mind you, but to himself “I think I made it?” Jushin lowers to the ground, a chuckle finding its way to the surface. People spread and walk around him, paying Jushin no mind as he lay on the ground, breathing so hard yet chuckling, laughing with the success of his victory. It’s good to be free. There’s a price to freedom. Jushin has not yet paid that price. Jushin hears footsteps all around him, most staying a good distance away. One set walks right up to him and stops, demanding his attention. Jushin slowly steadies his arms and pushes up, his eyes trailing from boot, up the black dress pants, noting the black trench coat, all the way to the black mandarin collar and the black sunglasses. Reno wears a wide-brim hat, pulled low to shield his face. A Cheshire grin, however, finds enough light to be seen. “How are you today, Jushin?” Jushin forces himself up and grabs Reno by the collar, proving that even after his journey there is fight left in him. He pulls back and slams a fist home, turning Maverick’s head with its velocity. Maverick’s grin never goes away; he turns his head back and slams a stun gun underneath Jushin’s arm and right into his arm-pit, the shocks weapon concealed as Reno puts and arm around him, pretending to help his “friend.” Jushin grows weaker and drops to his knees? The lights go out.
