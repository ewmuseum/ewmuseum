Thinking in the Car

7 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-7-03 'Life isn't fair. Plain and simple. Shit happens that you can't help. All you can do is bear down and move on.' ----------------------- As I step out of the garage, the sun creeps up my body, hunting for my eyes from behind a cloud. My hand raises to my brow despite the overcast haze. I stand on the concrete step, looking over the parking lot to find my car, then I see it rolling toward me, my lovely wife behind the wheel. Cindy pulls up beside me and stops the car. I reach for the handle and pull to open the door. Locked. I tap on the window and Cindy hits the buttom with her finger, a slight grin surfacing on her face. I once again reach for the handle, but before my fingers touch it, she lets off the brake and the car rolls forward. I stand there looking into the window, glaring at her, before she finally lets me in. 'What was that about?' I ask as I sit in the passenger seat. 'What was what about? I didn't do anything.' Cindy looks at me with her huge deep brown eyes. I smile and let it slide. 'How's the car going?' I sigh, thinking about the project. My friend and I are working on a show car. Taking a 2003 Dodge Stratus Coupe R/T and customizing it. 'Well, our tail lights are on back order. Apparently people want Charger tailights. Until we get those we can't do anything else to the back end.' 'Sounds like fun.' 'More than you'd know. How's your mom doing?' I look over at her, while putting my hand on hers. Her eyes glance at me before shifting back to the road. The look on her face says it all. 'Uh oh. I'm sorry hun. Are you wanting to go to the hospital later to see her?' Cindy nods a little, slowing down to turn on to our street. 'Yeah, I guess. I hate seeing her like this. She's so scared, and even my dad's worried.' I lay my head back, trying to find the right words to say. It's hard, because my mother-in-law is laying in a hospital bed while doctors try to tell us the mild stroke she had was simple symptoms of a migraine. I reach out and hit 'cd' on the cd player, for lack of decent music on the radio. 'Diamonds and Guns' by the Transplants. 'Damn' escapes my lips because the bass is too high for the song. Distortion can kiss my ass. I hate it. Almost as much as I hate Roz Blayze. Almost. Almost. I almost regret not kicking his ass before when he was around here. Hey, I said almost. Last thing I want is to waste my time on a guy like this. 'Do you mind if I change the cd?' asks Cindy, snapping me out of my deep concentration. 'No, go ahead. I'm just thinking about tonight.' 'Oh. You defend your title, right? Or are you in another tag match?' 'Singles defense. Against a guy I hated before, and now he just makes me sick.' I say, as Cindy puts a cd in the slot in the dash. AFI's new cd, 'Sing the Sorrow' which is a kickass cd. I'm gonna take it with me to the arena, cuz I know it will get me pumped up to kick the shit out of Blayze. 'Derek, can you be a little more specific? There's a lot of guys in the federation you don't like.' 'No, there's a lot of guys I can't stand for very long, but on a short term basis they aren't bad. This guy I DO hate. Roz Blayze.' Cindy's jaw drops in shock. 'Roz Blayze? Did you say Roz Blayze? What in the blue haired hell did he do to get a shot at your title?' I try to keep my laughter to a minimum due to the validity of my next remark. 'I guess he signed a contract. Salaok seems to be on this 'hire anybody' kick. Personally it's pissing me off because a buy like Roz Blayze can come in, win a match against Brick, who has been here but never really made his mark--yes I'm the same way but I'm working on that--and get a title shot.' 'Roz Blayze? Oh dear Lord. Kick his ass twice for me, just because he makes me just as sick.' I close my eyes and go back to thinking. Roz Blayze needs to study up before he runs his mouth. I haven't been seen around since my scuffle with Jay in the back when Salaok threatened our jobs? Bullshit. I've been around. I beat Christopher Camero for the International Title. I also lost that title to JJ Dynasty. I regained that title in a match with my now-ally Jay Robson. Later that very night, TLM and I made our way to the ring as Salaok was in his match. We informed the 'Co-Prez' of the Cold Blood reunion. Derek Frost hasn't been seen since is squabble with TLM in the back. Ha. If this little bitch only knew. The past week alone has been hell. My wife's mother has a stroke, my brother's friend of many years died in a tragic car-train wreck, two of my best friends get a divorce. On the business end, rumor has it one of my greatest rivals of all time is returning. God, what if it is true? How will I deal with him this time? Will Cindy be safe? I'd hate to see her get hurt again. Last time was hell. Literally. Falling over 60 feet and crashing through the ring below. My knee still isn't healed from that hellacious 6 tier cell match Kullypso invisioned for us. That sick, sadistic asshole better not return to this federation. Only if he has a death wish. I'm at the highest point of my career to date, and I refuse to back down and let my arch enemy hold me back now. I once again snap out of my trance as Cindy speaks. 'Derek, we're home. Derek. Hello? Are you okay hun?' 'Oh, good. I was just thinking about work. Lets go inside, it's cold out here.' My wife and I get out of the car and walked toward the front door. I can't wait to get inside. I want to sit down in my oversized armchair. Damn I love that chair.
