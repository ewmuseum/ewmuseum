Let the Drinking begin! Part 2

28 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-28-04 Tuesday. ---- Darkhorse Saloon. “Come on Ely! Drink up! ? Logan says as he slams the mug of Beer in front of Ely. Ely snaps out of his daydream. With a deep sigh he grabs the mug and downs the beer, and slams the mug down on the table. “There Logan, Happy?” Ely says with a angry tone and he pops his cigar back in his mouth. “Yeah, What is up with you Braxton?” Logan says hoping up on a stool next to Ely. WHATS UP!” WHATS IP!” Man Logan where you been the past few months! I have my best friend lied up ion a hospital? And only because he was helping me try and find the man who I respect more then anyone.. A man who has been missing for months.. Christina’s father, Bubba McCoy. ON top of that Christina is acting all weird on me? Fuck I don’t know if I can keep on going man. And you? YOU! Want me to play games!!!” Ely screams causing some of the workers in the Darkhorse to quickly move away from the pair. Ely takes another sigh and lowers his head? “I am sorry Logan, It is not your fault. ? Ely says his head “Its okay Ely. I understand your frustration. The police have that tape. They are working on finding Bubba. I do to long for his return. And pray he is safe, but sitting in a corner brooding over it will solve nothing. You need to let the frustration out and have a bit of fun” Talon will be okay. He is a strong one. The Police will find Bubba, and every thing will be all right. And Christina. She is a Woman. Women are weird, and nothing or no one can ever change that my boy.” Logan said with a smile showing though the large beard on his face. The words of the old dwarf ring true in Ely’s ear. All his frustrations with the police, his friend, and Christina. Maybe this is what he needed a release, a chance to unwind and relax. “Your right Logan! Let6s start this thing up” I need to find some Drinkers!!” Ely responded more upbeat. Logan had a strange effect on Ely, He always knew how to bring things to light, make him happy when down. Ely held a lot of respect for the Man. “That’s the sprit! Also lets not forget. You got to get ready for that Rumble?” Logan said. Ely tilts his head looking at Logan weird. “Get ready for it! There ain’t no getting ready for such a thing.. It’s like being in a Bar fight. Guys are coming from all over. This one hits you this one saves you. No loyalty, Nothing. It’s chaos at its finest. No what I am going to do is Win this Drinking contest for Bubba. This is his bar.” Ely says a smile coming across his face. ? And the Rumble in Rome?” Logan asks. “Well that thing. I will just go in and whip some ass! If I win, I win. If I don’t, It is no biggie.” Ely says “Fair enough. Well I am going to Go and find some drinkers” Maybe Brick? Yeah he likes to Drink. Logan hops down and walks out the bar as Ely lights his cigar once more. Ely sits there thinking as another form steps into view? The beautiful Gunner Addison. Gunner stands there hands on her hips dressed in a par of riped jeans and a tight T shirt with the Jack Daniels logo on it. “Hi Ely.” She says “What you doing” “Gunner” Nice to see you. ME. I am just getting ready for a Drinking Tournament.” Ely says getting up and pulling a chair out for Gunner. “Drinking contest, Sweet, Want some help getting ready?” Gunner says. Her smile growing a little mischievous. Ely looks into Gunners doe eyes.. Here is a woman he just met.. A woman he only heard about though Bubba and Jesse. A small bit from Brick and Logan. A Woman who he liked very very much? “Yeah.” Ely says and takes his seet. “What will Christina say?” Gunner asks. “I have no clue” frankly, I am very confused about a lot of things?” Ely says sadness filling his voice. Gunner thinks for a bit. “Well nothing a little Drink wont hurt.” Gunner says. “True that, Bottoms up?” Ely asks raising a eyebrow. Gunner let out a little laugh and taped his mug with one of her own. “In more ways then one I Hope” Thursday? Global Arena: Drinking Contest. Day One. Ely sat across from his opponent. Donny McNasty. Nervous. Hell yeah. Donny is Irish. And the Irish are just as bad as Dwarfs when it comes to alcohol tolerance. GW Referee Jack Wolfe steps to the table set up in a pressroom turned into a place for this. “Heads or Tails?” He asks both men. “Heads!” Donny says before Ely can respond. “Damn!” Ely says. “Luck of the Irish!” Donny says. The ref tosses the quarter into the air and it lands on the table and spins? Tails. “DAMN!” Donny shouts. A smile spreads across the face of Elbracton and he lets out a small alugh. “Irish luck, Eh Donny?” Ely says with a chuckle. “Choose your Drink Ely.” Says Jack. What to choose? Not Genies. Not any draft beer... No, something more potent? Yeah. “Tequila!” Ely shouts. “Fuck me!” Donny says slapping his forehead. ------ Twelve shots later! ------ Ely and Donny both pull up the last shot. “Lucky number thirteen!” Ely says. And downs his and slams it on the table. “Your going down cowboy!” Donny says and tilts the shot up and slams into the table? Donny slumps to the floor as the Referee raises Ely’s hand. A loud cheer blast out from the crowd that came for the contest.. Ely stands up and quickly falls down next to Donny. Slowly he closes his eyes and darkness consumes him.
