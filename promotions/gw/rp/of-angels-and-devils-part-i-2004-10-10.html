Of Angels... and Devils: Part I

10 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/10/2004 Madness. I’d always wondered just how sane I really was, or rather, how I wasn’t. Exhaustion had caught up with me finally, here, in the most unlikely of places. I had been trying to understand my enemy. Pendragon. Seemed to me that this was as good a place as any to understand him, and his so called “God”. What sort of lunatic runs around in chain mail shirts, claiming to be a servant of a deity called Bob? Apparently, this sort. I was wondering now, if perhaps I had simply misjudged him. Perhaps he really was as misplaced as I was? I hadn’t dreamed in months, I hadn’t slept in just as long. I no longer required that maintenance, or so I had thought? ---- Darkness? familiar to some? terrifying to others. Cloying? embracing? velvety darkness. A point of light breaks the uniformity? a red glow? tiny, but bright enough to make the deepest dark retreat into dim charcoal shadows. The dark was so much more interesting; full of secrets, full of whispered threats. Time stretches out? the red glow blurred, hazy and indistinct? time dripping lazily from the bloated clocks, like rain in the desert. The air is thick and full, barely breathable, each breath is a choking gasp, having to be bitten and chewed, a fatty, grisly hunk of meat? the velvety darkness suffocating, syrupy like molasses. Limbs like lead, consciousness drifting in the ebb and flow of thought. A perfect state of balance between worlds? this plane so empty, yet so full? seeing nothing but feeling everything, all in magnification? the screams? the laughter? waking reality? the sea of dreams? so close to taste it? so far to push it away? And for a moment time seems to smile... stretching thin with a sarcastic leer... for a moment a glimmer of light is visible in the gloom... a pinprick, growing closer, begging to be seen and for a moment everything seems like it could be alright. White light? a contrast to the dull red? a dull red which doubles? then triples? growing larger, clearer? blood-red blurs? becoming the four letters on an exit sign? pinpoints of shifting white light the candles along the altar? stillness and silence? Perhaps not? perhaps simply the calm before the storm? The lights fade? blackness again? darkness? hello darkness my old friend. But now the darkness is full? a pregnant pause? an expectant hush? then laughter? faint at first? mocking? then louder? cruel? sarcastic? and the sound stretches out into infinity. Time slows again? a conspirator in this mockery, so it would seem. Then, a voice? not just a voice? no, rather? the voice. Eldest, elemental, familiar. From everywhere? and nowhere? surrounding? embracing? overwhelming? violating? echoing in its intensity? yet oddly quiet? the tone measured, matter of fact. “Greetings, my son. I have been awaiting your arrival for some time. Welcome to my world” come in? make yourself at home. Please, do wipe your feet? wouldn’t want to taint the purity of this ivory tower of ignorance. So, do you still think ignorance is bliss? Would you prefer the dark? Or is it time for the harsh light of truth? Truth? Did you say truth? Johnny? tell him what he’s won!!” The voice seemed to shift, moving from archaic cadence, to game show host bellowing. Confusion. Chaos. He recognized the locale, it was eerily familiar. Limbo. He was inside the great creeping nothing, yet something tangible had changed. He had no control. Zero. Zip. Zilch. He felt confined. A prisoner. Out of the gloom, an object appears, illuminated as though under a spotlight? an old television set. It snaps on of its own accord? the image on the screen a scene frozen in grisly, vivid tableau. A combat boot, resting beside a puddle of crimson, nearly scarlet blood, the contrast startling against the pristine white tile that fills the edge of the screen. A crushed, ripple cut carrot rests in the center of the pool. The image itself conveys no immediate threat? but at the same time? the feeling is harsh? like being mentally raped? pain? anguish? guilt? terror. As if in response to an unspoken question the voice continues? “Now. Tell me what you see, boy. Tell me if you have yet begun to grasp the truth behind these lime lies. Look. Look and let your mind free of the shackles! LOOK, DAMN YOU! See the truth unveiled.” He didn’t understand. The image sparked some sense of d’j? vu? familiar. A fragment of the past, one scene, among so many filled with violence. Indistinguishable from the rest of the painful tapestry of his memories, it was distant, yet strangely close. The television is larger, dwarfing the perception of self? enormous? the image so large the individual points can be seen? blurring the eyes? the message becomes clear? there? in the puddle? I am he? a revelation? I am he? and you are me? and we are all together. NO! Suddenly, the television explodes, vanishing? back to the darkness. Always the dark? always in the dark. Dark is good? night is safe? unknowing is better? truth hurts? love hurts? everything hurts? nothing hurts. Suicide is painless? change is freedom? death and change are one and the same? merely a transition. The screams of foreshadow echo in ringing ears. Feverish chills quiver through spines, like leaves tossed in a cold November wind, ripping across exposed skin, stinging with fright. A hand? a phantom steadying hand, gripping? and pulling? towards the surface? gripping? for a moment, the light is near? safety. But the moment passes, the snickering, malevolent hands of the clocks turn their mean turmoil, prolonging the moment, moving backwards? never forward? always backward? always reverse? dragging the liquid seconds into pregnant, bloated hours by sheer force of will. Warped, evil? the hands break suddenly, the axis tips? Atlas stumbles and the world comes crashing down? time marches on? light? the light? the cold, harsh truth the light brings. Blood red numbers scream? 3:36? harsh reality? life as we know it? the candlelight sheds its welcome glow? banishing the deepest shadows. The darkness which veils the secrets? hiding the truth from prying eyes. We hold these truths to be self evident- A gasp for breath? lungs full of thick air, tasting of decay? sandalwood? musky and musty? Wheezing, unable to breathe? groping, flailing, tangled in twisted, wet sheets? a burial shroud? groping for the light, twisted linens binding? and a fall? falling extended? time slows? then impact? and a muttered curse as the cobwebs of sleep flee. Light after all? welcome? but too bright. Dancing points of light, candles shifting in unseen gusts of air. He came back to himself on the cold stone floor of the old cathedral, shaken and dazed, his hand clutching an ancient Bible, fear turning his fingers into talons. ---- I didn’t understand it. A vision? Simply a dream? Who was that voice, why did I recognize it so well? Who was it? Somehow, I felt on the verge of some sort of awakening, as though my chrysalis was nearly complete. Perhaps I could spread my wings as a butterfly, leaving behind my life as a moth. ---- Silence crashed down around him, the voice of the night, the sound the darkness made. The void, the mysterious and ineffable. He pulled himself slowly to his feet, throwing the Bible away into the darkness. He listened to the sound of it slapping against the wall, and then wiped his hands on his pants, as though trying to remove a taint. Peaceful. Silence reigned supreme here in this wayside house of the Lord. Suddenly, it was shattered by the fluttering of wings as a bird takes wing from its roost in the rafters of the ancient gothic church. Stone angels smiled down from their perches, spaced evenly between world-renowned stained glass windows. Shadows, thrown by the flickering candlelight of the votives on the ledge at the back of the room, made the angels appear more like twisted gargoyles. The velvet curtains were drawn across the confessionals? not that anyone would be here to listen at this ungodly hour anyhow. Ruby red light bathed the floor, stretching between the pews, creeping across the rough hewn stone like the innocent blood that had been spilled to save the world from their sins? provided you buy into that crap. A smell of incense lingered in the air, barely concealing the smell of wood polish that pervades the cavernous space. He sat down gingerly at the back of the room, at the end of the last wooden pew, his head in his hands, his shoulders shaking slightly. Shaken, still. He remained like this, utterly silent for a long time? and then, suddenly he gasps, lowering his hands and wrapping them around his middle as though in pain. Tears coursed down his cheeks as he threw his head back and laughed heartily, breathing heavily. He leaned over again, trying to get the force of his mirth under control. Wheezing and coughing, he wiped his eyes with his wrist before digging out his cigarettes. He turned to regard the statue of the Virgin Mary and proffered the pack with a wry quirk of his eyebrow. “Whaddya say, Mare” Want one?” Mary continued to smile benignly, her hands clasped piously. Jackson shrugs, pulling one out and sticking it in his mouth. He lit it, and then turned to smile at the statue. “You don’t mind if I do, right” Thought so? so, Mary? what brings a nice girl like you to a place like this?” He took a long drag from his cigarette, a wide, and slightly loony grin on his face. As he exhaled, he chuckled again, almost unaware of the sound. Lazily, he blew smoke rings towards the high ceiling, reclining with his arms along the back of the hard wooden pew. After a long pause, he spoke again, still addressing the statue behind him. “Bless me, Mary” for I have sinned. What sort of insanity is this religion anyhow? Pray to some stupid chick? that’s idiotic. I tell you, Mary? I don’t get it. I don’t buy into this whole invisible, omnipotent, omnipresent being. Just stupid if you ask me. We make our own fate? kinda takes the fun outta living if I know everything is predestined and scripted by some jackass upstairs, don’t you agree, Mare? And if there really was a God? would he allow all this shit in the world to happen? If so? your God is one sick fuck, Mary? pardon my French. Is swearing in church a sin, I wonder? Not that it matters, smoking probably is too? alas, Mary, let me tell you something, confide in you. I cannot be saved; I’m already damned for eternity.” He flicked the cigarette to the floor and ground it out with his boot, stretching out his arms and arching his back, which cracks and pops loudly. He rolled his shoulders and sighed loudly. “I tell you though, Mare” sometimes I wish I had a life raft like religion to cling to. Some pathetic belief in the unseen that I could? could? I dunno. Something that I could blame my problems on. Oh, well? I’m evil. Must be the devil in me, huh Mary? Yep? just call me Lucifer? can’t you see the horns? The devil made me do it, so clich?, isn’t it, Mary? I take accountability for my actions. I made me do it. Does that make me the devil? Perhaps, perhaps it does.” He chuckled again, his face lighting up with an amused smile. “I suppose that proof is in the pudding, perhaps my somnambular visitor knows what I am. I don’t. I haven’t any idea, as much as it pains me to admit. Guess I’m about to find out if I can play with fire and not get burned” what’s your call, Mary? Can I master the darkness? Or will it consume me? Well?” Jackson lunged to his feet, his bantering tone suddenly more unpleasant, his brow furrowed in anger. He crossed quickly to the statue, towering over the cheap plaster. He glowered at her, his features darkening in anger, his breath causing the candles to flicker wildly. “Stupid bitch. Answer me, goddamn it! What use is there in praying, when I am the only one within the sound of my own voice” ANSWER ME, YOU HARLOT!” He raised a hand as though to strike the statue and suddenly she shook her head, her rouged lips silently mouthing the word “no”. With a cry of horror, Jackson stumbled back, tripping over his own feet, and falling heavily to the hard floor. The Virgin continued to shake her head, her face full of terror, and then she’s still, tears of blood dripping from her painted eyes. The blood flows over the ledge, creeping across the white floor, drawing closer to Jackson. He scrambled backwards, colliding with the back of the pew, hard. He barely stifled a scream by stuffing his fist in his mouth as he leaps to his feet, springing away from the advancing blood. He can smell it now, the copper scent sickening. He squeezed his eyes shut and shook violently, his body wracked with tremors. With a shuddering breath he opened his eyes, and found, to his surprise, the blood gone? if it was ever there at all. “All in my head” nothing there,” he muttered, his face still pale with fright. The fluttering of wings is suddenly overwhelming in the stillness as a thousand doves fly from their roosts, swooping and diving towards Jackson, deafening, but only to him? for they’re not really there? at least not in this realm. With a cry of horror, almost a shriek, Jackson steps back, peering at the floor, where a single white feather rests in a congealing puddle of blood. He bolted for the door, panting in his terror, the mocking laughter that reverberated in his wake slightly out of his range of hearing. The feather drifted in the wake of his abrupt departure, and then settled back to the floor, where it rests in the red reflection of the moonlight on stained glass. The single pigeon landed on the back of the pew and cooed in the deafening silence. The Virgin looks on placidly, same as she ever was? a knowing smile gracing her lips, her hands folded piously. And then? “she winked”
