Maybe they are right

18 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/18/04 As the picture faded in, the bright, white glare of the blazing mid - afternoon sun shined down over a dull and dusty gravel path. Everything in the distance waved and swivled thanks to the heat rising from the ground. A light breeze tickled the leaves of the surrounding trees, causing them to sway slighty from side to side with each small gust from the light breeze. Out of nowhere, in a pair of faded denim blue jeans, a pair of black Converse sneakers, and a white cut - off t -shirt, Ice walks into view. His long jet black hair, dangles around his head, resting gently on the tops of his shoulders. An audibly loud inhale is heard from Ice, as his chest heaves in, and then back to its normal state as he exhaled the breath, all while overseeing the view in front of him. Slowly his hands slid off his hips, as he firmly stuffs them into each front pocket. Ice's head tilts up, as he looks up at the bright blue sky above. The ends of his jet black hair begin to waver slightly as the light breeze catches them in its path. 'It's been such a long time..', Ice says, as his words seem to trail off with the breeze. 'It's been so long that I almost forgot what it felt like. Almost forgot just what it was like to be in that position.' Ice's words trail off with the breeze again, as he slowly lowers his head. His head continues to lower until he is looking down at his feet. Ice's left foot darts out and kicks the gravel, causing a large cloud of dust to explode. The explosion of dust leaves a small haze, which is quickly washed away as the cloud of dust floats through the air, with the current of the light breeze. Still looking straight down Ice begins to walk along the gravel path, kick up dust cloud, after dust cloud every three or four steps, just as a small child does, when playing in the dirt. 'I used to feed off of that feeling. I fought to climb out of that rut, and strived to prove I did not belong in that position. I scratched and clawed to get away from where I was so long ago, yet I stand here today, and find myself right back at the begining.' Ice's head slowly raises from the dirt and gravel he was fixated on before. The bright sun begins to dim as a large, cotton ball like, cloud floats in front of it, shadowing the earth from its rays. Ice continues to walk down the gravel path. His strides are long, and methodical. The sun begins to creep back out from behind the cloud, that it had been hidden behind. Ice's walking stops as the sun finally makes its way out from behind the cotton ball cloud it was behind, and glares its bright, and hot rays down. 'You know....', Ice says, with a depressing, and doubtful tone of voice. 'Maybe you are right. Maybe they are all right.' Ice pulls his hands out from within his jean pockets. Slowly his right hand raises, and combs itself through his long, jet black hair, pulling away the strands of hair out from in front of Ice's face. Ice turns around, slowly, and looks around at the trees, the blue sky, the bright, yellow sun above, and the dusty, dirt and gravel path which are all surrounding him. A wondering, expression resides on Ice's face, which is normally emtionless. Quickly Ice's tongue slips between his lips, and lightly he licks them as he continues to survey his surroundings. Ice's eyelids crawl over his eyes, as his eyes close shut, and his head drops. Ice begins to walk back towards the direction he was just walking from moments ago, with his eyes closed, and his head hung low. His strides remain long and methodical, as he walks along the dirt and gravel path. Ice takes three more steps before stopping. His head raises ever so slowly. Once again Ice raises his right hand, and combs it through his jet black hair, combing the hair out from in front of his face. 'I can't believe it took you.......I can't believe it took this long......how could I be so oblivious. I can't believe I didn't see it before. While everyone else saw it, and tried to tell me. I didn't hear them. I didn't want to hear them, I didn't want to believe their words to be true.' In almost a distraught, and pleading manner, Ice's words seep out into the open air. 'They are all right. Every word they speak is true. But how did I let it come to be? How did I not see it? Why couldn't I stop it?' Ice's words roll out in a defeated manner, as his head once again drops, also in the manner as if he was a defeated man. 'Things have changed so much, and so quickly, I didn't even have a chance to stop it before it happened. Hell, I didn't even know it was happening, until it had already occurred.', Ice says still in a defeated tone. 'Train this week.......Josh the next. What am I thinking?' Ice begins to continue his walk, as what was one a light breeze picks up, and becomes a stiff wind, blowing up the dirt from the gravel path, causing a HUGE cloud of dirt and dust. The cloud is so thick that Ice can no longer be seen walking as the picture fades out.
