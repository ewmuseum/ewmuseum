Out of Retirement. -Part 1-

1 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/01/2005 -August 25th, 2004- 'Good morning Mr Lei. You don't know me... but I know you! I know everything there is to know about you. From what you eat... to what you like to watch on TV. I know the exact amount of people you have on your hit list... and I know about your plans for Global Wrestling.' Choi Yen begins to rub the stubble under his chin. He's sitting in front of his lap-top listening to a voice recorded message. The room he is in is lit only by candle light. Chinese incense fills the room. The Person talking is an English man. He sounds quite old, late 30’s early 40’s. 'I understand you are going in to retirement... I'm afraid I cannot let you do that. You see, my brother has been blamed for one of your killings... you assassinated a Mob Boss by the name of Peschi... Mr Leo Peschi. My brother was also paid to kill him that night, you unfortunately were too quick... too good. By the time the Police had arrived you were gone and my Brother was captured by the police.' Choi Yen raises his eyebrow wondering what this guy could possibly want from him... maybe he wants Choi Yen dead for the arrest of his Brother... He pulls the top drawer open and reaches for his barrater and silencer. And listens on as he loads both weapons. 'Don't worry Mr Lei; he was released not so long ago. The police didn't even have a case because you left no trace what so ever. I'm leaving you this message because I have a couple of jobs lined up for you. This is an offer you Will not refuse Mr Lei. Let’s face it?. You don’t want your identity going public do you? I have the power to nail your ass to the wall! If you do not do as I ask? then your name will be headlining every newspaper world wide. Now I’m a reasonable man, there is something in this for you? The pay is excellent. All you have to do is meet me at the phone booth two blocks away from where you are right at 0300? Lawrence looks at his watch; the time reads 14:25? 35 minutes to spare. He places his two guns in their holsters and pushes himself away from the lap top. He steps up from his chair and walks out of his apartment to his destination. 15 minutes late Lawrence is sat at a table in the restaurant opposite the phone booth. He checks his watch for the time’which is now 14:52. He has counted 4 suspicious men; all white average sized, wearing the same clothes. Business men maybe? “Can I take your order sir?” Either Lawrence did not hear a young waitress speaking to him, or he ignored her. The young lady taps Lawrence on his shoulder. He slowly turns his head and responds? “No I’m fine thank you.” “Well sir” if you’re not eating, or drinking anything then I’m? I’m going to have to ask you to leave?” Lawrence sighs as he looks at the young lady. “I’ll take a coff” never mind?” Lawrence sees a white limo pull up next to the phone booth. He reaches in his pocket and pulls out a hand full of cash then places it in the waitress’s hand. “You don’t belong here” you are too pretty. Try modelling.” He says? the waitress smiles then nods her head. Lawrence walks out of the restaurant and across the road. He tries to peep inside the limo, but the windows are tinted. The door swings open. “Good afternoon Mr Lei”. Beautiful day isn’t it?” Lawrence holds on to the top of the limo door and peers inside. He can see a couple of champagne bottles, all in ice. There’s money all over the floor of the vehicle. “Please Mr Lei, come on in” I don’t bite.” Lawrence looks around before getting inside the limo. He helps himself to a glass of champagne. Before he tastes it, he sniffs the glass... 'I assure you Mr Lei, it is not poisoned? why would I poison “my- own glass?” The business man lets out a quick laugh. Lawrence drinks the rest of the champagne, and then places the glass back in the cup holder. “So what is it you want me to do” My time is limited? I have a contract signing next week? I’ll be gone for the whole week.” Exclaims Lawrence, hoping for a quick reply. He looks up at the ceiling of the limo, and then around? Sat at the other end of the limo are 3 middle aged females. “Ok” straight to the point then Mr Lei. Since you only have 4 days. I want you to take a trip to London. Go to 56 Florence Road, Hackney. There’s a young man who has something of mine that I want back! It will be hidden somewhere? it’s a gold box, filled with my wife’s diamonds. That’s all you need to know about that. Get that back and then? kill him!” “What’s in this for me?” Lawrence asks’he looks deep in to the eyes of his “new” boss. He nods his head, acknowledging his new assignment before speaking. “I will pay you “500,000 right now for the golden box and “1,000,000 when you get back from London. I don’t care how it is done” just make sure he is dead and nothing happens to the box. Plus? if you keep working for me, I will help you out in your other career? This Global Wrestling? I know of it. I will provide you with anything you need to help you kick off your career. I understand you are quite the fighter Mr Lei.” “Done!” Lawrence holds his hand out? “Call me Charles Gates.” The two men shake hands as the scene slowly fades.
