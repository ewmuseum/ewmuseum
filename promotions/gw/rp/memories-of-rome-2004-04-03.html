Memories of Rome

3 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 040304 Daylight shined its way through the drawn curtains of the large picture window in the bedroom that me and Vic shared at the Westin Excelsior hotel in Rome. I got up from where I lay, which was oddly enough on the couch, sprawled out in an awkward position with Victoria’s head in my lap. The air was light and brisk as I crossed the small gap and pulled the curtains as close together as they could be drawn. As I trotted back to the couch to rejoin Vic, I felt something heavy bang against my groin, which made me pause and look down. What the fuck? The Global Wrestling International Title hung loosely around my waist. I’m not sure what bothered me most. The fact that I had obviously missed the PPV and didn’t remember getting the belt. Or the fact that the International Title was the only thing I was wearing at the moment. ***** Flashback: the night before Clash of the Titans I walked around Rome with Victoria, her hand clutched in mine. I was dressed casually in a pair of jeans, a white un tucked Christian Dior dress shirt. At my back, hidden by the dress shirt, the glock from Reno waited patiently in its holster. Victoria had one a pair of black slacks that could have been painted on and a tight black shirt. Her black hair wafted softly in the cool breeze. Beautiful. We stopped before the Parthenon, staring at its stark whiteness being illuminated by the spotlights at night. Despite the ancientness of it, the building stood tall and strong. My free left hand made its way into my pocket and I rubbed a finger across the black felt box that lay tucked inside. “Tell me something,” I squeezed at Victoria’s hand. “Yes?” she purred. “Why do you still hang around with me?” “What do you mean, John?” “After everything that just happened with Vincent. Why do you stay?” I asked, turning to her, searching her eyes for an answer. “I stay because I love you, John. You should know that by now. I’m not just a fly by night whore after you for your money. That first night, after I kicked your ass?” “And I kicked yours as well, if I remember correctly,” I interrupted her, both of us laughing at the memory. “Regardless, after that first night, I kind of knew. I wasn’t going anywhere for a long time.” “Despite the danger?” “Yes, despite the danger. I’m a big girl. I can take care of myself. Those bastards just got at me from all sides. I went on for as long as I could before finally their numbers just got the best of me. I know what I put at line by loving you, John. I’m for you.” She wrapped her arms around my neck and pulled me down into an embrace. I held her tightly for several seconds before pulling away. “Why do you keep asking me these things, John?” “I just needed to know.” I reached into my pocket again and grasped the felt covered box. “I wanted to be sure.” Slowly, I sank down to one knee, opening the box. I held the box out for Victoria to see, showing off the diamond ring encased within, the gem sparkling in the artificial light surrounding the Parthenon. I opened my mouth to speak. “Well, now, ain’t this just sweet looking. Looky here, Carlos, someone here is about to make a proposition to this young lady.” I hate my luck. Thousands of miles away, I still manage to run into moronic rednecks. “S”. It appears we have come across two love birds.” I raise my voice loud enough for them both to hear. “Yes, two lovebirds in need of privacy, per favore.” I hear the click of a knife being unfolded. “Such rudeness, eh Carlos?” “Si, perhaps we teach the whelp a few manners.” I hang my head again. This is not fucking happening. Staying in my kneeling position, I reach to my back and pull out the glock. Turning on my knee, I raised the pistol up and fire off four shots, catching each man in both legs. They toppled onto the cobblestone road and screamed in agony. I returned the pistol to its holster, closing the box and rising up from my knee. “I’m sorry, Vic.” She pushed me back down onto my knees. “No. I think you wanted to ask me something.” She leaned down and kissed my forehead, whispering. “It’s okay. Ask.” I reopened the box for her to see. “Victoria?” “Yes, John?” Tears started to form in her eyes. “Will you marry me?” “Yes.” She leaned over and wrapped her arms around me head. The added weight and the positioning of our bodies caused us both to fall over in a heap. We rolled around, laughing and kissing as I slid the diamond engagement ring onto her awaiting finger. We stood up slowly and walked away, passing by the two fallen men. I looked down at them. “You know, any other day, I would have killed you two on the way out. However, I am in an extremely pleasant mood right now, and will let you live. Be thankful that she said “Yes,” gentlemen.” Victoria leaned over and whispered in my ear. “However, my beautiful fianc’e does have a few parting words for you.” Both men whimpered as Vic walked over to them. She stood there for a second before she started stomping the ever loving shit out of their legs, focusing on the areas where the bullets had entered. She finished with a final stomp to each man’s groin and grinding away with the heel of her shoe. Finished, she swayed her way back to my side and we started to walk away. “Buona sera, i gentiluomini,” I called out to them as we left the Parthenon. ***** The four of use were sitting around a round table in the restaurant located next to the Westin Excelsior. To my surprise, Deac and Rhaiza were actually dressed to the nines in the finest Italian clothing that money could buy. Not to say that me and Victoria looked all that shabby, Vic in her elegant, tight fitting red evening gown, and me in a pair of black slacks, a Christian Dior white dress shirt, and a black overcoat. I decided not to option out for the tie, but instead left the top button undone. On the table before us was an odd gathering of foods. Pastas mixed with veal and chicken. Penne Rustica. Chicken Marsalas. Fettuccini. Spaghetti. Lasagna. You name the Italian pasta, it was represented. And next to the gourmet foods? Hot wings. A shit load of them at that. I swear, I had never seen so many bones in all my life. I could just see all those poor wingless chickens, trying to walk around and flap their little wings, but they couldn’t. Why? Because Deac and Rhaiza had eaten their wings. I reached out to grab a fork to sample some more of the pasta, and my arm knocked over a bottle of wine. In the process of it falling, the bottle knocked into a second bottle of wine. The second knocked into a third. A domino effect in progress. The third into the fourth. And the fourth? Well it opted for suicide, dive-bombing to the hardwood floor and exploding in a shower of glass and red wine. We all watched this happen. And what did we do? We laughed our fucking asses off. A waiter arrived at our table, unsure of all of us, a tray in hand. “Ladies and gentlemen. Here is the finest wine of the house. It is a bold red wine that?” The waiter didn’t have a chance to finish is spiel about the wine as Deacon snatched the bottle off the tray and proceeded to chug the wine directly from the bottle. Two big gulps in, Deacon removed the bottle from his lips and started spewing the expensive wine from in his mouth and onto the waiter as if it were the most vile thing he had ever tasted. “Goddamn, this shit tastes like ass. What did you do” Shove the bottle up your ass and prance around like a goddamn sissy before bringing this swill out here. Jesus motherfucking Christ. You did, didn’t you? Oh my god, I’ve got Italian ass in my mouth. Quick, someone bring me a forty.” I couldn’t help but chuckle as the waiter stood there, wine, spit, and slobber dripping from his face and asked, “A forty?” It was like someone had pressed the paused button on Deac, as he stopped dancing about trying to get the taste of wine and ass out of his mouth. Slowly, he turned his head and stared at the poor poor waiter. “Yes, a forty,” Deacon slowly drew the words out. I had never seen him become so calm and civil in such a short period of time. It was like watching Samuel L. Jackson in character right before he explodes. The waiter shook his head, not knowing what a forty was. “You know’A forty. Forty ounce,” Deac’s voice started to rise. The waiter shook his head again. “Malt liquor, motherfucker. I’m talking about goddamn malt liquor. Please, for the love of all things holy in this god forsaken country, tell me that you at least have some fucking malt liquor in the back.” “Sir, I don’t think we have any of this malt liquor in the back,” the waiter calmly replied. This wasn’t gonna be pretty. Deac just kinda stood there, a dumb founded look on his face. He started to speak very low and very slowly. “You’don’t’have’any’malt’liquor” What kind of fucked up establishment are y’all running in this fucked up country? I mean goddamn. A brotha can’t get no malt liquor? Oh. I see how it. You just ain’t catering to the black man. What? Am I not good enough for y’all to represent a lil bit? I mean damn, here I am, on business, staying in your fine hotel, and you can’t keep a lil malt liquor in the back for a brotha to enjoy? That’s just some fucked up shit man. This is a fucked up hotel. Y’all just can’t keep it real, can you?” Deacon paused long enough. “I tell you what, if you can answer my riddle, I’ll drop this whole matter and we can just fucking forget about it. Aight?” The waiter slowly nodded, unsure. “What did the five fingers say to the face?” Deac asked slowly. The water shook his head side to side, not knowing the answer. In a deft move, D ripped open his shirt, exposing his chest, yelling “I’M RICK JAMES, BITCH!” before backhanding the waiter to the ground, taking the three quarters full wine glass that I was drinking from and hurling it across the restaurant, causing the glass to shatter and the wine to splash on several of the patrons, before turning and storming out of the restaurant. Rhaiza quickly stood up and chased after him, while me and Victoria still sat there. I picked up another almost full glass of wine and downed the rest of it in one huge swallow before standing up. Slowly, Victoria rose beside me, encircling her arm through mine. I reached into my pocket and removed some Lire and gave it to the waiter who still lay on the ground. “Lei dovr” perdonare il mio amico. ? una puntura,” I say to the waiter. “Nessune inquietudini. Tutte l'entriamo qui sono delle punture. Ma di che ? questo quaranta che ha parlato?” he replied. I picked up the bottle of expensive wine that the waiter had brought that Deac had left on the table and took a small pull from it. I spat the contents onto the ground. ?” una bevanda di alcool che assaggia pi? amando come che questo fa.” I strode off with Victoria in tow, leaving the waiter on the ground to ponder the mysteries of malt liquor. ***** In the club, strobe lights flashed, bodies gyrated and tempers flared. A circle of people had formed around Deac and our co-worker Josh. Words were being exchanged, but I couldn’t hear them over the throbbing music. Deacon mouthed something at Josh. Josh mouthed something back. Damn, if the room would quit spinning I might try to read their lips, but nooooo, this sonofa bitch keeps trying to go round and round. A downed yet another shot of Crown royal. I had had so many, I lost count. But I do remember the bartender telling me that between the four of us, we had gone through about six bottles. D went to raise his shotglass to his lips to drink again, but instead through the alcohol into Josh’s face, before bringing the glass crashing down on top of Josh’s head, who then hit the floor in a heap. A member of the club’s security rushed forward but I intercepted him, drawing out a small knife and poking him on the end of his nose with it. I shook my head no to him and he did not move. “Time to fled, D,” I shouted, hoping he would hear. Deac stood over Josh, who was slowly starting to get up. Josh touched the top of his head and pulled it back. It was covered in red blood. Deacon started laughing. And so did Josh. They laughed together before Josh hocked up something nasty and spat it into Deac’s face, who recoiled at the contact of the loogie. Josh leapt up at Deac and the tumbled to the floor, rolling around like two embraced lovers. I looked at the security guard and jerked my head over. He nodded. I lowered the knife and we both moved to the two fighting men. I grab D and he took Josh and we dragged them apart. Except I kept going and took Deac with me all the way out side, him struggling to get free the whole time. Security followed us out to make sure that we both left. “Ungrateful sons of bitches!” Deac shouted. “That’s it. EVERYONE FOLLOW ME!!! PARTY AT MY HOTEL!!!” We started stumbling our way back to the hotel, D grabbing people to come as we went. The hotel manager did not look pleased when we arrived. ***** FUCK JOSH! The room vibrated the phrase. FUCK JOSH! I honestly didn’t think that you could fit so many people in a single area. FUCK JOSH! I bumped by people as they thrashed about. Furniture was tossed, torn, tumbled, and destroyed. The room reeked of alcohol and spray paint fumes as Deac encouraged patrons to spray graffiti on the mosaic walls. My head throbbed. The room spun. Deac saw me and pushed another glass into my hand. I took the shot and downed it. The entertainment systems spewed forth lyrics from Twista. The words came out jumbled but the backbeat rocked. My knees started to turn to rubber as I made my way around the room, looking for Vic. She was no where to be found so I went to the room that the hotel had sectioned off for me and her. I wiggled the knob, but it was locked. I pulled the key out and after several attempts, got the key in the hole and the door unlocked. I went in and shut the door behind me, blocking out some of the noise. Laying on the bed was Victoria. Damn. Should’ve looked here first. I went to the bed and collapsed next to her. She smiled at me and put her head on my chest. I closed my eyes for one second. Darkness. ****** Present The rest comes out in blurry images that I can’t focus on. I can hear crowds cheering. I remember a great bladder pressure being relieved. I remember more cheering. But that’s about it. But, it still doesn’t tell me why I’m naked wearing the International Title around my waist. I looked back to Victoria who was still lying on the couch. She had woke up and was eyeing me and my title. “Care to go another ten rounds, champ?” Okay. I think that explains the whole naked with the title on thing. I gave her a nod and went back to the couch. “We should come to Rome more often,” she whispered as we lay back on the couch. “Yeah, but next time, with less to drink.”
