Something important

11 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/11/04 Brick had gotten a call from Ely Braxton. Ely had asked him to meet him at the Dark Horse Saloon. When Brick asked why, the only reply he got was 'I'll tell you when you get there.' It was a bit suspicious, but judging by the tone in Ely's voice, it was apparently important. When Brick got there, it was the usual energetic atmosphere of people dancing and laughing and riding the mechanical bull. 'Redneck Woman' by Gretchen Wilson was playing on the jukebox. 'Hey, Daisy, is Ely here?' 'No, haven't seen him. Why?' 'Because he asked me to meet him here. I figured he'd be here already.' 'Well, he might be at the bar or in the john. You can check.' 'Yeah, I think I will.' Brick walked right up to the bar. There, he saw Logan Creed sitting on a stool. 'Hey, Logan, what are you doing here?' 'What, I gotta check my schedule with you all of a sudden? I can't come into my best friend's establishment whenever I damn well please?!' 'I just asked you a simple question, midget!' 'Dwarf, you big ape! An' if you must know, Ely called me down here to meet 'im.' 'Let me guess, when you asked him why, all he'd say was 'I'll tell you when you get there', right?' 'How do you know?' 'He called and asked me to meet him here, too.' 'Wonder who else he called here.' Just then, the door opened and in stepped Jesse Briggs. 'Let me guess. Ely called you.' 'Uh huh.' 'And when you asked him why he wanted you to meet him here, he said--' 'He'd tell us when he got here.' Out of the men's room came El Serpenti, dressed in casual clothing, but still wearing his mask. 'Ely called you, too,. didn't he?' 'Yes, and obviously, all of you as well.' 'Man, do you ever take that mask off?' 'Not often, no.' 'Is there anybody Ely didn't call?' Just then, Bubba McCoy walked into the Saloon and right up to the bar. 'Hey fellas.' 'Hey, Bubba. Ely call you down here too?' 'No, he didn't. I just came to check on the place. He called alla you down here?' 'Yeah, but he didn't say why.' Just then, Dane Rennier walked up to the bar. 'Ely call you guys, too?' 'This is getting ridiculous. Where the hell is Ely, anyway?' Just then, the doors opened and in walked Ely. 'About damn time he got here.' He strode right up to the bar, where everyone sat or stood, and just stared at him. 'Ah, good, you're all here.' 'Yeah, we're all here. The question is why?' 'Yeah, Ely. What's so damn important that you gotta drag us all out here?' Ely smiled and then reached into his back pocket. He pulled out a small black box. 'This,' he said, opening the box to reveal a diamond ring. 'Oh, Ely!' said Jesse in jest. 'Yes! Yes, I'll marry you! I don't care if it's not legal yet!' 'It's for Gunner, you jackass!' 'You serious?' asked Brick. 'Damn serious,' Ely replied. 'I'm gonna take the plunge an' ask her to marry me.' 'That's great!' 'Congratulations, my friend!' 'Hey, Ely, let me holler at you for a minute.' Brick and Ely then walked off. 'What's up?' 'Marriage, already? I mean, you and Gunner have only been going out for, like, six months.' 'What's your point?' 'My point is marriage is a big step. Are you sure you don't wanna take some time to think about it?' 'No way. Darci is one o' the best things to ever happen to me. And I ain't gettin' no younger.' 'Yeah, well, you don't want to rush a decision like this. I got a cousin going through a divorce right now. You gotta be absolutely sure you're doing the right thing.' 'How long I gotta wait, Brick? Carpe diem. Seize the day.' 'If you really wanted to carpe diem, you'd have called Gunner down here and not all of us.' 'Look, I know what I'm doing, okay? I was hoping you'd be happy for me.' Brick sighed. 'You know what? Whatever you decide is alright with me. I'm just concerned.' 'Appreciate it.' 'So, have you decided on a best man?' 'Umm...umm...'
