Announcement at Sunday dinner

15 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/15/04 It was another Sunday night at Coretta Randall's house. Brick and his brother-in-law Kyle were setting the table. Brick's sister Monica, who was well into her second trimester of pregnancy, was resting on the couch. 'There's got to be something I can do.' 'Yeah, you can keep on that couch and rest.' 'Jamal!' Coretta called from the kitchen. 'I need some help with this food!' 'Be right there, Ma! Come on, Kyle.' Dinner on this particular Sunday evening was salmon, Spanish rice, candied yams, steamed broccoli, cornbread, potato salad. 'Father God, we come to you in the name of your son Jesus Christ with thanksgiving and praise. We thank you for this day and for this time together. We thank for our health and for our lives. We thank you for the food we are about to recieve and for the hands that prepared it. We ask that you bless this food to build the body, the mind and the soul. In Jesus' name, we pray.' 'In Jesus' name.' 'Amen.' 'Amen.' After blessing the food, they began to prepare their plates. 'I'm doing a guest spot on 'Hope and Faith'.' 'What?' 'Yeah.' 'People still watch that show?' asked Coretta. 'Ma, it's not that bad a show.' 'It's a stupid show.' 'I have to agree with Mom. It is pretty goofy.' 'Do I comment on the shows you watch?' 'All the time. Dr. Phil, Oprah...' 'So what character do you play?' asked Kyle. 'I play one of the next-door neighbors. I think Ted McGinley's character calls to complain about the noise I'm making. He insults me over the phone, so I come over to the door and there's almost a fight. I think my character has some scenes with Faith.' 'That's who Kelly Ripa plays, right? I know that's gotta make you happy.' 'What's that supposed to mean?' 'Oh, come on, Brick. Everybody knows you have a crush on Kelly Ripa. Have since she was on All My Children.' 'What? No, I don't.' 'Oh, please.' 'I don't. Sure, I find her attractive, but I don't have a crush on her. She's a married woman with three kids.' 'Oh, so you have problems with women who have children?' 'No, Monica, you are not gonna get me to go there. No way, no when, no how. It ain't happening. And I don't have a crush on Kelly Ripa.' 'OK, fine. Let's drop the subject.' 'Yes, let's.' Brick then began to take a sip from his glass. 'So, how are things with John Zero and Silent Wind?' Brick immediately slammed his glass on the table, some of his drink splashing. He then started to glare at his sister. 'OK, another subject.'
