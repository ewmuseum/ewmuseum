Histories #2

10 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09102004 Red moon rising. Red moon. Blood moon. Fitting for this place. The blackest night sky. The dull twinkle of the stars. The dark forest emitting its soft scurries and moans. In the middle of all of this’an open field. This is where I wait. I watch. Still. Stark still. No muscles moving. None twitching. Waiting. Patiently. My black cloak surrounding my body. Providing comfort? “Warmth” “and a few hiding places. A creak” ...A crack? “A crunch out of place. Motions in the shadows. A person” An animal? A shade? Shadows of the past coming back to haunt me in the present? It’s none of the above, but a mark. Though one might say that a mark is a person. You know what the difference is between a mark and a person. A mark is dead? “they just haven’t realized it yet. The man comes out of the shadows of the forest” Walking? Tripping? Stumbling? Swearing underneath his breath but still loud enough for me to hear him twenty feet or so away. That’s close enough. The large briefcase he was carrying thudded against his left leg as he came to a sudden stop. Why the fuck are we meeting all the way out here? I mean Christ oh mighty. This is the middle of fucking nowhere. You said you wanted to meet somewhere private. Yeah? I motioned around with my head. You see anyone around. He nodded in agreement. You have a point my friend, you have a point. Shall we start then? I gave him a curt nod. For services rendered, your party owes me a total of one point three mil. Yessiree, I got it right here. He set the briefcase down on the ground in front of him, popping the latches and opening the case. And Mr. Carletto would like to thank you, once again, for your help in this situation. A quick flash. The man’s hand dug down into the case and snapped back up, holding an object of some kind. Three light popping sound. Snaps of white light. A plinking sound of bullets striking metal. I smile underneath the mask. Why Donny. What on earth are you doing? What the fuck! I began to laugh as he adjusts his aim. More popping sounds. More snaps of light. More plinking. More laughter. On my part, at least. Donny stared at me, open mouthed. Why won’t you die, motherfucker! Now Donny, don’t you find it odd that Mr. Carletto would send you all the way out here, with such a large sum of money, to meet a trained killer, without any guards? That’s not true, I have men stationed by the car. They’re there waiting on me. Are they now? He dug something out of his pocket and raised it to his mouth. I need help. HELP ME! There was a slight garbling of noise before a replay came back. Sorry Donny-boy. We have our orders. He threw down the radio in disgust. I couldn’t help it. I laughed. He raised the gun again. BASTARD! Another pop. Another snap. One last plink. And then a new noise entered the foray. Click. Soon followed by more. Click. Click. Click. Click. Amateur. Who uses six-shooters anymore? I slowly raise my right hand out of the shroud of my cloak. Grasped in my fingers, a flat black H&K, the metal appearing blue-black against the cool night sky. Donny’s mouth opened to say something. But before he could, my finger squeezed the trigger, sending a bullet his way. Zipping through the air? “cascading the air around it, causing unseen ripples” “traveling the distance between us in less than a second” “striking flesh” “ripping muscle” “chipping bone, perhaps” “digging” “burying” “embedding” “splattering blood on clothes” “on the ground” “tunneling its way through his thigh” “exiting the back of his leg, leaving tendrils of cloth” “of flesh” “the words of Donny changes” “to screams” “yells” “pain” “inhuman thoughts” “no thoughts” “just pain” “nerve impulses running rampant through his body” “and then the thought. I AM GOING TO DIE. Even from this distance, I can see his eyes go glassy from the thought” “the screams fading from his lips” “turning into whimpers. Even before he speaks, I know what his next words are” Please don’t kill me. Damn, I hate being right sometimes. Please? Please? Oh you? I have a wife? “whiney” “and kids” “insignificant” I don’t want to die? “piece of” “not here” “motherfucking”. “not like this. shit. Please” I fire of several more rounds from the gun, circling his body, but never hitting him. Well, maybe showering him with a little dirt? “but a little dirt never hurt anyone. Donny, if you don't shut the fuck up right now, I will kill you and send your decapitated head to your recent widow so that she may look at your face and see it just as it was when you died. With that, the little son of a bitch did shut the fuck up. Look, Donny. You know what you did. And, little fuck up that you are, you got caught. And you, of all people Donny, should know that you don’t fuck with the bossman’s money. He began to whimper then. And then you got greedy. Thought that nobody would miss a hired hitman and his money. And that’s where you really got stupid. Do you really think, Donny, that there are secret meets like this where large sums of money are exchanged. No. It’s all computerized now, you ignorant piece of shit. Hell, if you had even thought to check the bills, you would’ve noticed that they are all fake. You would have walked away with one point three million dollars in funny money. With that, he began to cry. I decided that now would be the appropriate time to reveal to Donny how he was, in fact, going to die. Slowly, I stepped away from my position, which I had maintained during the whole ordeal. My cloak resisted at first, pulling against an unseen object. Then, it began to fall away, revealing a large, handless sword. To call it a sword was an understatement. It was more of a slab. Large. Unyielding. The tip was stuck deep in the ground. The base rose straight up, leveling off just below my shoulders. The width of the base, at least two and a half feet wide. The blade itself was two inches thick. In my left hand, I hefted its handle, sticking the long pole deep into the center of the blade, thumbing a button on its side to lock it in place. With two hands, I unearthed the massive blade, holding it high in the air. The blade is easily seven feet long, tapering down into a one-foot width before sharply diving into a point. Oh’my’Jesus’God” Anything beyond that came out as a babble? Incoherent words mixed with the smell of lost control? “bladder emptying” “sphincter giving way” “bowels becoming loose. Slowly, I closed in on my prey. The tip of the sword, of the slab, dipped low to the left” “the point coming around”. “gathering speed” “momentum” “my arms raising the blade behind me” “overhead” “descending” “faster” “striking” “the crown of his head” “bone crunched” “crumbled” “gave way to the might of the blade. I pushed harder, following through with the strike. The blade cut deeper” “sinking inward. Blood rained onto my clothes” “gushing upwards. Red blood. Red moon. Blood moon. Then nothing. The blade stopped, the earth on the other side of the body halting the downward motion. Which was fine. Donny was dead. Cleaved from the crown of his head to the center of his chest. Slowly, I pulled the blade back. Blood trickled out of Donny’s body, flowing freely like a little stream” “not a care in the world. The phone in my pants pocket vibrated. Flipping it open, I held the phone to my ear, saying nothing. You busy” I stared at the gore before me, watching with slight fascination as a few organs began to slide their way out of their now open vessel. Slightly. We need to meet. Face-to-face. I’ll need a little time. How much? I surveyed the scene again. I’ll call you. You do that. With the one hand, I closed the phone, sighing Donny, Donny, Donny? Maybe I will send your wife your face? “or what’s left of it. Or maybe your eyes” Or you hands? Or some little unidentifiable organ? Who knows? I certainly haven’t decided yet. Around me, the forest has gone quiet. The animal’s retreating from the evil that they have just witnessed. There is nothing?. “but silence.
