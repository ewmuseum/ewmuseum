Burning Highway

22 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07-22-2005 Cold water closed in over Dawn’s head, her skin instantly goose bumping as she hit the clear liquid in a shallow dive. Temperatures were reaching record highs in and around the Toronto area, and the McFarlane estates were to receive no reprieve from the blistering summer heat. Dawn herself had spent most of the summer indoors thus far, finding every small excuse not to venture out into the heat that worked such savage damage on her fair skin. There is only so much isolation a person can take though, and after nearly two weeks of being indoors Dawn had decided to brave the sun for some outdoor fun. One black bathing suit and nearly half a bottle of sunscreen later and she was in the estates large pool, testing the limits of her lungs to stay under the cold water for as long as possible. When her lungs burned the way her skin was going to Dawn placed both feet against the bottom of the pool and launched back towards the surface, breaking through to the air with a deep gasp. “I thought I was going to have to go in after you.” Time by the pool usually meant time alone, since Dawn disliked swimming after the sun had gone down, and no amount of sunscreen would protect Shadow from the suns rays. Since her last trip out of town however, Shadow had brought back someone else’a young girl she’d apparently picked up from the ruins of an old mansion of hers in the US. Alexis had been a shock for Dawn, perhaps almost as much as Dawn had been for her, and the two had butted heads often when she first arrived. Chief among their issues was the fact that Dawn was unwilling to allow Alexis to simply leave the estate, as Shadow had been very explicit in her instructions that the girl needed to remain where she could be watched. After a handful of aborted escape attempts the girl settled into a sullen pout, refusing to speak to Dawn or to even acknowledge that her hostess/jailer existed. This had lasted until Dawn refused to allow Shadow to take the girl out again, insisting that she be allowed to take Alexis shopping for some badly needed changes of clothing. Although it hadn’t been any kind of bonding experience, the ice between the two girls had melted some and Dawn found Alexis shadowing her throughout the mansion, usually settling down nearby to watch TV or plopping down on a bench to watch Dawn train. “Are you listening to me?” And of course coming out to bask by the pool. Dawn favoured Alex with a gaze normally reserved for small rodents and men like Mike Biddle, narrowing her emerald eyes as she did. “Yes I’m listening to you, I’m just thinking at the same time. Besides, what exactly did you want me say” That you should know better by now then to think I need to be rescued in my own pool?” Alex glared back over the top of her book, not giving any ground to the taller girl. “I was trying to make conversation; I can only be interested up here by myself for so long.” Dawn rolled her eyes in reply, kicking backward away from the edge of the pool and slipping back beneath the water, already feeling the skin on her shoulders beginning to tingle from the harsh sun. A strong kick propelled her to the bottom of the pool, where she closed her eyes against the harsh chlorine and floated for a moment, enjoying the feeling of being weightless. A nearby splash brought her eyes back open, scanning the water until she could see Alexis swimming toward her through the wake of her dive. If Dawn could have sighed underwater she would have’so much for a moment of peace. She curled her long legs under herself and shot towards the surface of the pool again, narrowly missing Alexis as the girl sped through the spot Dawn had occupied until just a second ago. Alex breached the water just a few seconds after Dawn did, beaming happily at her through sopping bangs. “Surprise!” Dawn rolled her eyes, resting one arm over the rim of the pool as she reaised a sardonic eyebrow. “Surprise” Did you get bored of your book already?” Alex made a face, swimming slowly in a circle to bleed off some of her excess energy. “it just wasn’t what I thought it was gonna be y’know” I can’t believe I paid money for that thing.” Dawn smirked, her familiar half smile sliding slowly across her lips. “Y’know when I first met you I would have never pegged you as the trashy romance novel type.” Alex flushed, glaring pointedly at her host. Dawn was certain she’d have folded her arms crossly if she could have done so and stayed afloat. “There’s nothing trashy about romance novels’well most of them anyway, this one is a new exercise in bad writing.” “What’s so bad about it” I don’t care for them as a rule, but what makes this one so much worse then all the others?” Alex made a face, pushing back to lean against the pool rim. “Well I kinda like romance novels with a touch of the supernatural you know” With vampires and stuff..” “Really?” Dawn asked, the surprise obvious in her voice. “I would have thought you wanted nothing to do with them.” Alex shrugged, having the grace to look a little sheepish. “Well I don’t mind the book kind of vampires’they’re all romantic and sexual and intense’not like the undead creatures we have walking around the modern nights.” Dawn nodded a little before turning her head away, staring off into the woods that took up the majority of the estates grounds. She could understand the venom Alex had toward the vampires she’d met, Dawn felt a great deal of it herself’but she’d be lying if she said she didn’t find them attractive from time to time, particularly one? ??kissing an angel just seems so’so’ridiculous you know?” Dawn started, looking back to her guest. “Sorry” I zoned out for a second.” “I SAID; This one just sort of took it to a silly level I think. Having the central premise being a devil kissing an angle seems a little’far fetched you know?” Dawn flushed suddenly, a rosy colour creeping up her neck and into her cheeks. “Uhm’yeah, I guess I can see that?” Her own mind was afire with the dreams she’d been having, of cold, red lips pressed against hers’of floating in a sea of raven coloured hair. She heaved herself out of the pool, heading over to where she’d left her towel, and began drying her hair to hide the blush that had taken up residence on her features. She heard Alex swim up to edge of the pool she’s just vacated. “You alright Dawn?” “Yeah I’m okay I ah’I’m just thinking about my Master of the Mat qualifier.” Whether or not Alex caught Dawn’s lie the taller woman couldn’t tell, but she seemed to take it in stride regardless. “You nervous?” Dawn paused, thinking it over for a moment before answering. “A little. I’ve never been as big on high flying as some people are, I prefer to stay grounded where I can work my opponent over with the stuff I learned in Japan and from my Father. But you’ve got to adapt to the times I guess, so we’ll see how I hold out with three of the top Cruiserweights in Global.” “Is that why you don’t ride your bike anymore?” Dawn turned, pulling the towel away from her face to stare at Alex in surprise. “Excuse me?” “I was poking around in the garage the other day and I saw the tarp off to the one side. I don’t know a whole lot about bikes or anything, but I know that one got a lot of love at some point’so I was just wondering why you don’t ride it anymore. Is it because you like to stay grounded” Or?” She paused for a moment, taking a deep breath before forging ahead. ??does it remind you of your father?” Dawn shrugged uncomfortably, going back to toweling her hair off. “It does, but not in the way you mean’I used to bike with Daddy all the time, but they’re all happy memories. I just’it hasn’t felt right for a long time. When I get on that bike’I fly’and right now I just feel like I’ve got my wings clipped.” “But why?” “I’I’d really rather not talk about it alright?” Dawn wrapped the towel around her waist and headed quickly inside, sliding the door shut behind her before Alex could ask any more questions. *********************************************************************************** Laying on her back in the estates master bedroom, Dawn found that the roof of her canopy bed didn’t hold any answers to the questions she was asking herself. The dark silk fabric simply hung as it was wont to do, leaving Dawn to count the creases and lines that ran through it the way most people would count dots on the ceiling. With an angry growl she swung herself up into a sitting position, staring balefully over at her walk-in closet. “Ugh, what’s the problem here?” She asked herself, already knowing the answer churning in the pit of her stomach. Sitting on the edge of the bed in only her underwear, Dawn let one hand drift to her unprotected neck, feeling the rough texture of the abrasion scars that had yet to fade. Memories of her attempt to end her own life pressed hard to the forefront, and it was only with great effort that she pushed them to the back of her mind again. “Daddy would be so upset?” She pressed her hands to her face, trying to find some relief from the pressure building in her head. Would her father really begrudge her the thing she loved because she’d had a weak moment? Was it just that she was afraid to go into the garage again, after nearly hanging to death there? Running her hand along her neck again, Dawn could feel every angry welt and weal in her pale skin, knowing without looking that each scar was still a faded red, rather then the pale white she wished it would be. She sighed, pushing herself away from the bed and walking slowly to the closet, pausing for a moment with her hands on the door knobs. “Okay?” She breathed, “Here we go.” Throwing the doors open she stepped inside, reaching to the right to throw the light on as she did every morning. Unlike every morning she headed to the back of the closet, pushing aside dress clothing she’d not worn for years until she could see the small chest at the bottom of the closet. Dawn sank to her knees, slowly, almost reverently reaching to open the chest and gaze upon the “forbidden” objects within. With practiced hands she removed her motorcycle helmet from the box, blowing the dust from the tinted visor and staring at her reflection for a long moment. Next came a pair of re-enforced gloves, done in the same black and red stripes that were painted along the helmet. A pair of tall boots were next, the silver clasps up each side glinting in the light despite the coating of dust, or perhaps to spite the coating of dust. She placed each of these things to her right, clearing herself space as she reached in to remove the final piece. The biking leathers had been custom made for her, each curve and contour hugging the corresponding curve of her body. Joints and vital spots had been protected with external pads, extra padding or a set of semi-rigid plastic plates designed to act as the last line of defense. The whole suit was the same black and red as the rest, the sole exception being the decal on the back’a rose made of steel, inlaid over the japanse kanji for “Family” done in white. Dawn stared at the entire ensemble for a long moment before rising to her feet again, dragging each part out to be tossed onto her bed. She slipped her bare legs into the leathers, tugging them up to her hips with an unhappy grunt. “Figures’I’ve gained weight again?” She grumbled as she fastened the belt there. Her arms went into the sleeves without effort, and she slid the zipper from her bare naval all the way to her throat. Plopping down onto the bed with a strained groan she stepped into each boot, curling her leg up to get at the clasps rather then bending over to get them. Finally she tugged each glove into place, flexing her hands and fingers to make sure they were snug in the wrist clasps. She turned to regard herself in the mirror, looking up and down the once extremely familiar leathers she wore. One deep breath followed another as she closed her eyes, trying to will the nervousness and shakiness she felt away’until she could open her eyes again. “Ready to fly?” ****************************************************************************************** The dirty orange tarp she pulled away from her beloved Ninja had done a poor job of keeping the bike clean, and a quick finger inspection revealed a thick layer of dust had built up on the outer skin. She cleaned it off as best she could, running her gloved hands over the chassis until it passed a cursory inspection. Gingerly she slid into the “saddle”, mounting the bike with one leg while keeping the other firmly on the ground. “Okay baby?” she whispered lovingly, “Show me what you got.” Dawn slid her helmet into place, making sure it was secure before reaching to turn her keys in the ignition and turn the throttle beneath her hand. Without hesitation the bike leapt to life beneath her, a throaty purr that rumbled out from the custom engine she’d had installed and reverberating the very floor of the garage. That sound said one thing to most??Power? but to Dawn it meant something very different??Freedom.” The door to the garage flew open to reveal a surprised Alexis, staring mouth agape, at the roaring monster Dawn was straddling. Dawn herself couldn’t keep back a grin at Alexis? shocked stare. “Hey kiddo!” She yelled, “It still works!” “No kidding! Are you taking it out?” Dawn nodded and Alex’s face lit up. “Can I come?” Dawn shook her head slowly, “Sorry Alex, this is something I need to do just me’although?” Dawn’s eyes flicked over her Corvette Coup for a moment before turning back to Alex, “You can follow in that though’if you can keep up.” She flashed a wink at the stunned Alex before hitting the door opener and maneuvering her bike into the front drive. Barely able to contain the sudden need for release she felt, Dawn eased the bike out the front gate, waiting until the Coup slipped out of the garage behind her before gunning the engine. The Ninja howled and roared beneath her, tires squealing against the highway asphalt before they found purchase and the custom bike shot off down the road, screaming with a triumph Dawn felt all the way down her spine. She checked the rearview mirror, the sinister shape of her midnight blue Coup gaining ground rapidly, even risking a glance to the side as Alexis pulled abreast to her. The other girl pulled down one eyelid and stuck out her tongue as the Corvette pulled into the lead, drawing a growl and a curse from Dawn. “Oh you little brat”.” Hammering the throttle home, Dawn fought to control the raging machine beneath her, remembering the advice she’d been given when it was finally completed: “A bike like this wants to get away from you Dawn, ? Doc Raven had said, “So you better make damn sure you can handle it before you try anything CLOSE to top speed.” She clamped her legs hard around the bike as she gritted her teeth, hunching her form low as the bike howled past the Corvette, barely catching a look at Alexis? eyes as big as dinner plates before she was shooting into the lead again. The corvette loomed large in her mirrors again, but a deft twist of the throttle kept the Ninja firmly in the lead, and a sense of accomplishment building in Dawn’s stomach. “I can do this.” She growled, tightening her grip on her bike. “I can fly again?” She would closed her eyes had she dared, the thoughts and feelings that had kept her tied down rushing to the forefront at her bidding, a cacophony of images and feelings that took several precious moments to sort themselves out. She found her mind drifting to Corvus, Eryc as he had asked to be called, and the late night rendezvous he had called her to just scant nights ago. Much had been said’but so much more had not been said, the rage and the hurt she felt over their relationship had remained in the pit of her stomach, clenching so tightly she couldn’t force it out, couldn’t make it clear and couldn’t find the words? The mechanical demon found them for her, another howl of pent up fury that shook the highway signs as they thundered past, joined to her wordless scream of frustration at her own spinelessness. Corvus was not alone in her thoughts; Soma, Shadow, Virgil and Emoticon each had their places, and each one drove her onward, forcing her to hunker tighter against her bike’s frame as if to coax more speed from it with her thoughts alone. Words unspoken crowded her mind, wreathing Shadow’s lovely face with things Dawn wanted to say, things she couldn’t bring herself to say. The same face darkened at the thought of what rode heavily on Dawn’s heart, those lovely features turning away from her in embarrassment at the thought of the truth’Turning to Soma’To Virgil. She screamed again, her throat raw and aching as unshed tears blurred her vision and burned her eyes. The Corvette’s darkened form became something more in her mirrors, the headlights burning into her mind with the force of everything she sought to escape and she tried to put more distance between them’willing her bike on with nothing more then the desire to escape’to get away. The Ninja’s wordless howl was broken by a throaty cough, instrument lights flashing a warning red as Dawn’s horrified gaze broke from the road. The engine seized with a scream of agony and the bike jerked violently to the right, the front tire seizing with the stricken engine with such force that the rear tire left the asphalt and Dawn was flung clear over the handle bars? There was no time for panic before she hit the road, last minute training taking over as she tucked her head in and tried to ball up, softening the blow as much as possible. Something gave where she hit the road and pain flashed up her side and down her leg, blossoming again in a dozen places as she bounced and rolled, striking the pavement with great force. Her helmet grated against the asphalt, leaving a long smear of red paint as it sheared clean away with an inch of the protective plastic. As suddenly as it had happened Dawn found herself rolling into the grass, the sudden right turn her bike had made pitching her into the nearby field instead of down the lonely stretch of road. Her body screamed and ached where she lay, but she forced herself onto all fours with shaking limbs, reaching up to open her visor and gulp air as fast as she could. Her vision swam and red droplets appeared on the grass beneath her’brakes screeched from a long way away and blinding light flooded her vision’she could swear a voice called her name’the ground reached up to catch her and everything else went dark.
