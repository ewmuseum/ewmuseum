TWC Hype

4 January 2006

Date: 1/4/2006 The building was run down with lawns growing too high and bushes out of control. As the camera came slowly up to the doors, where there had once been some sort of plaque was no a discolored portion of the brick wall different from all the rest. The front doors all had chains on them, connected with heavy steel padlocks. The camera slipped down to the end, where one of the chains had been cut, and the door hung slightly open. Stepping inside, the cobweb covered corners and dust filled rooms were abandoned, long since forgotten by a once rabid public. These were the empty halls of forgotten glories, long ago battles and warriors long left languishing for recognition. In the dust, there were two sets of footprints walking down and to the left. Trailing along the path, the viewer was taken into a huge room where some pictures and plaques still hung. The empty spaces had been sold off to the highest bidder, adding to collections for those most loyal to these superstars. Stopping before one of features, only the bottom corner remained. The inscriptions read? “Rules are the only Rules” The rest of the message was lost. In the hall, the echo of a voice could be heard. “If you have anything, bring it to the ring!” The camera panned around looking for the source of the voice. Instead, it found a picture of a powerful young man with a cleanly shaven head. He looked angry and determined as he stared out from years gone bye. “See the halo’ignore the horns?” The camera tried to find the source of the new voice, but to no avail. Everything began spinning, and the camera seemed to fall down as the picture spun faster and faster. A myriad of clips began playing. Pitt Hix slammed a barbed wire wrapped kendo stick over another man’s head. Small clumps of flesh and tissue were ripped away by the barbs. “Pitt Hix is trying to kill Gravedigger for betraying him!” HARDCORE Xavier Black jumped off the corner turnbuckle and landed on top of Prince Purple Tongue on the announcers? table. The force snapped the table in two. “That’s almost three hundred pounds flying around like a cruiserweight!” HELL Pitt Hix? face was covered in blood from two gashes across his head. He ran across and speared Versatu through a plate glass window taking them both to the floor. He began slamming bloody hands into the Reaver’s head. “Someone needs to stop this!” HAS Xavier Black had Xiem Iduo locked up in Embrace The Darkness, refusing to let go even as Xiem’s friend hit him with chairs and vicious kicks. “He’s refusing to release the hold! Xavier is trying to break him in half!” Pitt Hix was thrown off the entryway onto the concrete by Sensational Stephen Reed. As SSR celebrated, Pitt Hix began crawling, slipping on his own blood as he fought to get back up. “The hatred he has in his soul is scary! He isn’t finished walking the Path Of Destruction yet!” COME Black picked Curtis up around the waist and lifted him high overhead. Xavier charged forward and slammed Curtis over the top rope into the cage just behind it. Just as Curtis made contact the sky exploded with bolts of lightning. Two of the bolts nailed the metal cage structure surrounding the ring. The electricity raced through the metal structure and then into Curtis? body and following the connection into Xavier’s body. The two men jerked and spasmed as they were filled with more energy than any human being should receive. They were being cooked and shorted out by the surge of energy as they died a little in the brief instant with millions of people watching around the world. TO Pitt Hix was shown spearing competitor after competitor. Then he was shown delivering the Pitt Drop on concrete, through table and from atop a steel cage. Xavier Black was shown forcing people to tap out with Embrace The Darkness, dropping people on their head with The Descent and flying through the air as he delivered his own version of the Shooting Star Press.
