Date: 1/3/06 Black. Brick: Late June of 2003 was one of the happiest moments of my life. Fade in. Footage from the World title Pit match between Brick and Sage Sabbot at Whiplash 2003. Brick has Sage Sabbot in a sleeper hold until he's unconscious. Even afterwards, Brick refuses to release the hold. West: Here is your winner and the NEW GLOBAL WRESTLING WORLD CHAMPION, BRICK! The referee hands Brick the title. Brick looks at it and then cradles the belt close to his body with tears of joy. Brick: Four long, hard years of shed blood, bruised skin, broken bones and near-death experiences had finally paid off. Fade out. Fade in. Brick slowly gets up and is handed the title, Brick looks at it and staggers to the entrance of the Pit, holding the World title in the air. Brick: I was finally on top of the mountain where I belonged. I was living a dream. I achieved what no one believed I could. At times, even I didn't believe I could achieve it, but I did. I finally had a taste of World title gold. Fade to black. Brick: And then it was gone. Fade to a close-up of Brick's profile. Brick: And I want the feeling back again! Cut to a montage of Brick training intensely: shadow boxes, does push-ups, sit-ups, crunches, stretches, etc. Brick: I'm not just hungry for the World title. I'm starving. I worked and waited four years to win the World title the last time. I don't plan to wait another four.They think I've been here too long. They think I'm washed up, a has-been. But I have something a lot of others didn't: longevity. They may have victories over me, but in the end, I'm still here. And I ain't going nowhere. Fade to black.
