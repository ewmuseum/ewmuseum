Date: I Don't Really Know I’ve been hiding out in an abandoned warehouse for the past couple days. I don’t even know where I am. I don’t even know if I’m in America. And worst of all, I don’t know where Soma is. I want to go look for him, but I’m sure he’ll end up finding me. My stomach begins to growl from the pit of my empty stomach? I’m starting to get hungry. I hope he returns with something to eat. I haven’t eaten in days. I suddenly know what it feels like to live in a third world country. Where you have to scrape up scraps, just to survive. At times like these, cardboard makes you drool. I pull a button off my shirt sleeve and put it in my mouth. Sucking on something keeps the saliva flowing through your mouth. In my past life, if that makes any sense, I would never think about pulling a button off my shirt for survival. But now, I really don’t care. I have nothing to lose. And this damned suit that they buried me in is starting to become a pain in the ass. The pants are way to tight and very uncomfortable. They forgot to put socks on my feet, so my soggy and sweaty feet squeak with every step. I can’t wait to get back to...being wealthy. I’m going to buy a new pair of socks for every single day of the week. I’ll never have to wear a pair of socks twice. I miss those days. The days that I always had a full stomach. The days when I used to snap my fingers and have a 5-star meal in my hands. I’m so fucking hungry? Instead of sitting here, thinking about food’I stand to my feet and walk around. I try to keep my mind off being hungry. I walk around the warehouse. I’m on the second floor right now. You could tell that this place is extremely old. The floorboards creek within every step. And you can see particles of dust floating in the air where the sunlight beams in. I walk down the stairs, hoping I don’t fall through. I want to go out and look for some food, but Soma told me not to step foot outside this building. I walk around and look at the farthest corner of the room, where I’ve been defecating the past couple of days. Suddenly, I don’t feel too hungry anymore. The smell is retched. So I turn around and make my way to an open window. I take a deep breath and inhale a fresh breath of oxygen. I can smell the birds flying in the air and hear the sounds of the wind blow. The sky is getting dark’either its getting late or we are in for one hell of a thunderstorm. I wonder if this place can even hold out the rain. The wood is all warped and old. I so want to walk outside right now. But I don’t want to shrug off Soma’s command. He brought me back to life, I can at least be loyal? “or maybe a couple minutes couldn’t hurt. My mind is already made up. I quickly walk to an open window. I look outside for anybody. I don’t want somebody seeing me at all. It would be crazy to have somebody see me at a time like this. I’m supposed to be dead. And seeing a deceased person walk around would really freak somebody out. And it would probably blow my attempt at hiding out. The sight is all clear. I jump out the window and land hard on the ground. My feet squish in a pool of sweat inside my shoes. I dry-heave at the sound of it. I don’t think I’d even be able to vomit anything considering my stomach is so empty. I take another deep breath of the fresh air. Feels like spring, or maybe its fall. Hell, I don’t even know what time of the year it is. I wonder if I’ve been dead for a couple days, or a couple weeks, or even a couple of years. I’ll write that down in my “questions for Soma” notebook that I almost have. I sneak around the side of the building, Special Forces style. I step each step on the back of my heel first, so I don’t make a lot of noise. I rush a little faster this time and turn the corner and slam face first in the chest of Soma. I fall on my ass and he steps a couple steps and stands right over my body. I feel embarrassed, stupid, and feel as if I’ve betrayed him now? “What are you doing” I thought I told you to stay in the warehouse. Do you realize that if somebody sees you, you’re cover is blown. Word will get out that you are alive and shit will go down. You will probably be taken for tests your whole entire life. And that really would screw things up on accomplishing this mission.” “What mission are you talking about” All I know is that you woke me up from the dead. And you want me to come on this mission.” “You will find out in time. You are wise, but not wise enough to realize what is going on around you Sabbot. But don’t worry, time will show you my son.” “Your son” I’m over 40 years old, don’t call me your son.” “I’m over a thousand years old, I can call you whatever I’d like. You may have died once, but I have still lived more lives then you ever have and ever will Sabbot.” “he has a point. I shut my mouth. “I don’t ever want to see you leave this warehouse until I tell you to. And I don’t want you to ever disobey another order of mine, ever again. You got that?” “I was hungry, I haven’t eaten in days. I was just searching for something to eat.” “And you think I’ve been going to restaurants while you’ve been sitting here” You think I stopped at the pub for a drink?” Damn vampire has another point’again I shut my mouth. “If I ever catch you doing something stupid again, I’ll kill you’again.” You see, when somebody brings up a part of my life that I wish not to recall, it kind of pisses me off a little. I nip up and kick him in the stomach. The bastard catches my foot and spin kicks me in the stomach. I hit the ground hard and my head smashes on the ground. I lie on my back, not even moving. The fucker keeps getting the upper hand on me. You don’t understand how embarrassing this is? Instead of doing anything, I just tilt my head a little and look at him? “I’m sorry, I shouldn’t have said that. Even the wisest man can make mistakes.” He extends his hand down to me. I hesitate a second or two, but grab his hand and he hoists me up to my feet. He turns around and walks away from me. I don’t even ask where he is going. He walks at the end of the building and grabs about 5 or 6 plastic bags and brings them to me. I quickly open up the bags, they are full of homemade breads and jellies. Another bag has a quart of fresh milk in it. Another has a couple chicken breasts’I don’t even look in the other bags, I grab a chicken breast and take a big bite out of it. “Sorry, it’s a little cold. But it’s the best I could do.” Cold chicken doesn’t bother me at all. It takes like a medium rare filet mignon steak right now. I look up at Soma, he’s staring at me with a smile. Now I feel greedy? “You want some?” I hand him the bag of chicken and the bag with the milk in it. He brushes it off and laughs? “I’m not hungry.” He turns around, laughing and walks away. “ONE SCARY MOTHERFUCKER” I take another bite of the chicken while looking in the other bags. I take a swig of the milk, the freshest milk I’ve ever tasted. I put the bag with the bread and jellies aside. I look in the other bags. One has a pair of black jeans, a black Nevermore t-shirt’good choice. If he brought me a TUE shirt or a Soma shirt, I would have fucking killed him. I look in the last bag and it has a shoebox. I pull it out and open it up. A brand new pair of shoes? I search for another bag I might have put aside, no other bag? “WHERE THE HELL ARE MY SOCKS?” He turns around and smirks? “Beggars can’t be choosers.” He turns back around and walks away?
