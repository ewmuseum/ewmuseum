Responsibilities J. C. Diamond Global Wrestling Promo #1116 Date: 4/9/04 Location: J.C Diamond's House (The scene opens up in J.C’s apartment, he is lying on his bed his eyes lazily blinking he looks as he is about to fall asleep, he has silver tearaways and a black sleeveless t-shirt on. The room is now very well decorated, there are pictures of basketball players all over the room and a picture of a tropical island and a lighthouse on one side of the room. There is a TV and a large stereo system and you can see the room is now furnished as it is a real room, he has a nice oak dresser in the corner on the top is various colognes. The closet door is partially open and you see basketball jerseys hanging up on the rack, there must be about hundred of them up there. J.C is now sleeping what is he thinking well lets take you inside his head) [[Flashback not to long ago]] J.C has just won the match against Chris Steel and heads back to the locker room and he is met by Sebastian and Angel Angel: J, we just got a call they found Jermaine bloodied up in some alley, we gotta get there ASAP. J.C: You playing Sebastian: We ain’t lying man, our friend just picked him up and took him home. J.C: Fuck, Skip. ((J.C and Crew get to the nearest airport and fly off)) [[The scene opens up at Angel and Sebastian’s friends apartment]] (J.C , Angel and Sebastian rush up a flight of stairs and J.C pounds on a grey steel door in some drabby looking apartment building kind of like where we first saw J.C. An older guy opens the door and J.C rushes in, Jermaine is on a couch with a few ice packs over his face and shoulder, his lip is cut bad and you can easily see his nose in broken, he has a black eye and his shoulder is in a home made sling.)) Man: J, I called them as soon as I picked him up. J.C: Thanks a lot. J.C: Jermaine…Jermaine can you hear me bro Jermaine: Jayson? J.C: Yes, I’m here, tell me what happened. Jermaine: Skip….He wanted me to deal to kid’s and I remember Doc telling me NEVER to deal so I refused and he said some shit about you and then before I knew it I was out cold. J.C: That fucker….he’s gunna get it from me. Jermaine: He skipped town for a bit, I think he knew you were going to come. J.C: I got nothing but time.. I’m gunna take you back to my place. [[Fast Forward a couple of weeks later]] (The scene is now in J.C’s apartment again but it’s in a new room a spare which now belongs to J.Cs little half-brother Jermaine. J.C is sitting on a chair watching the tv and Jermaine is on the bed still kind of hurt) J.C: Jermaine, you know I am wrestling now right? Jermaine: I’ve been following it since you left, I know everything man. J.C: Really? Jermaine: Fasho, I was stupid to act tough that day, Doc woulda slapped me if he heard what I did, Im sorry J.C.. J.C: Don’t worry little bro, I was the same as a kid your age. Now we got it better, I want you to get a job somewhere and we will start living somewhat normal. But when Skip gets back I got a bunch of people going to inform me….Then we will go back. Next time don't follow people u don't really know and you won't get hurt. I told u not be on his side,ok. Jermaine: Ok J.C: School starts next month, I gotta ask the boss for a raise. Jermaine: Mr. McCoy? J.C: Yep, I asked for the week off but I think he will understand, he’s too happy for me but he’s controlling the cashflow. Jermaine: Haha for sure. J.C: Anyways bro get some sleep… [[Flash forward to Nick’s yesterday..]] (The scene is in J.C’s apartment again Jermaine is watching TV and J.C walks through the door.) J.C: Hey, man Diamond’s gym is even more ghetto than it was before, but I always get the best workout there. (J.C is wearing a pair of long nike sweatpants and a white tank-top.) Jermaine: Hey did you see this? J.C: What? Jermaine: The Evolution card changed you are booked after all! J.C: What? Against who? Jermaine: Brick and Rich Sherwin! J.C: What! A triple threat? Jermaine: Ye (J.C is getting dress in a Mets Jersey, he puts on the mets hat to go with the jersey and some blue jean-pants follow with some blue and orange air-forces. Jermaine is watching tv in the living room) Jermaine: Hey J, you ready to go for dinner? I’m hungry as hell. J.C: Yeh bro, I’m coming. (J.C looks into the camera) J.C: Remember Brick and Sherwin, you ain’t stepping in the ring with any damn body. You’re stepping in the ring with the REAL fucking TRUTH. (Nick grabs a coat and heads off with Tay) [[Flash Forward to Present]] ((J.C wakes up after this part of the dream he knows its reality but he doesn’t want to accept everything that is now going on in his life. His little brother moving into his house, a new responsibility, a grudge between someone who crossed the border of a lifelong feud, a match that will determine whether J.C believes that he is good enough to stay in GW and compete for a title or will he just keep fighting new comers on a weekly basis. He knows he can handle all the pressure its how he deals with it is whats going to make him show the world how special he is))
