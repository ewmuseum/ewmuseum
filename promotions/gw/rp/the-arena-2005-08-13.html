The Arena

13 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 13, 2005 “KICK HIS ASS!!!” “COME ON, FIGHT MOTHERFUCKER!!” “THIS IS LAS VEGAS BITCH, WE CAME TO SEE BLOOD!!” Vegas was definitely his kind of town. A hustler like the Brawler is the bane of places like New York, even though the society as a whole needs them more then they would ever admit. Here in Vegas, a man with the right type of ambition for the hustle could be a king, with no end to the riches. Drugs, gambling, whoring, fighting’there was money and power to be gained with all the middlemen cut out, namely the city officials and cops. Vegas was a wanting succubus of a woman, legs wide open and begging to be taken which ever way you chose to have her?.. And the Brawler always liked his women willing. It took all the money he could scrape together to get the flight to Vegas, a Global Wrestling tryout waiting for him upon arrival. However, a fault of his hustler nature presented itself upon arrival, that being never thinking enough steps ahead. The Back Alley Brawler had enough money to get their certainly, but what about money to live comfortably, especially if his tryout went well? Thank God for Vegas, opportunities for money just smacked you right in the face. SOCK~! Or in this case, gave him a solid right hand to his face. Enough talking to the right people and the Brawler went from roaming the Strip, to being surrounded by hundreds of rabid spectators thirsting for blood. The “arena” was situated in an abandoned warehouse on the eastside, home to illegal fights every month with big money to be had for not only the fighters, but those with a gift for betting as well. Tonight, the Brawler’s mission was money and to gain it would mean defeating Cristos, the hulking Mexican beast across from him with the Brawler’s blood on his tattooed knuckles. He smiled, revealing a silver encrusted “grill” over his teeth with “Diablo” on the top row, adding insult to the Brawler’s injury. “Come on bitch, make my night quick!” The fluorescent lights hung above them, putting the two competitors in the spotlight as Cristos shredded the air with another right hook, finding true as it smashed into the Brawler’s face. The Bronx Barbarian stumbled back slightly, a slight trail of blood coming from the edge of his mouth as tried to get his head back together. It had been a long time since the aged warrior had a real fight and despite being on the losing end, he was enjoying it thoroughly. The smell of sweat like old fried chicken. The blood-thirsty crowd waiting for the blow that would make or break the fight. The sick rush he’d experience from every blow he gave or received. This was living. This was not the 6’7, 329 pounder Brawler giving it his all however. Cristos had no idea just how much false confidence Joe was letting him build up as he laid more searing punches into the visage of the Back Alley Brawler. Joe had his own personal reasons after all as to why he accepted something as gritty as underground boxing after all. To put it simply, he needed to remember what it was like to feel any other kind of pain besides the one he harbored in his heart every aching day. Every other thought for Joe was about the family he left behind in search of claiming the brass ring in wrestling. Ever since the long flight to Las Vegas from his native Bronx, all he could do is worry for the welfare of his ex-wife and loving daughter, Karen and Carmen. No booze was strong enough for him to forget the pain and no cigarette smooth enough to ease his tension. Good old fashioned fighting would bring his cure, like it or not. Because the best way to get rid of pain, is to replace it with another. The howls of the spectators grew to a mighty roar as Cristos snatched the tree trunks-for-legs the Brawler possessed, putting him down to tarnished tile floor below him with a sickening thud. Brawler could feel the messy cocktail of sweat and his own blood smeared on his tank-top, Joe having just the kind of luck to land in a lingering puddle of his own bodily losses. Cristos played to the crowd, flexing his ripped arms for the crowd as the dragons tattooed on them pulsated with life. “I’M GONNA DESTROY THIS MOTHERFUCKER!!” YEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEAH!!! The masses had spoken and they gave their thumbs down to the Brawler. Like a good gladiator, Cristos was about to give them the river of crimson they had so desperately yearned for. The proud New Yorker asked himself if this was the time to fight back yet. His mind told dismissively rebutted with a harsh “no”. He laid as limp as the corpse he threatened to become with the beating he had absorbed, the overhead lighting now eclipsed by the bald head of Cristos. He locked eyes with Joe, the smile of a sadist on display for the Global Wrestling hopeful as a meaty fist raised itself in the air and rained down a thunderous blow on the Brawler’s bruised face. Cristos awaited the cry for help, mentally begging for it to escape the Brawler’s swollen lips, but it never came. Soon, another punch followed and landed just as furiously as the last. An ear clasped with his hand this time, no sound would come from the Brawler, only blood trickling from his mouth and down his neck. “SCREAM FOR ME YOU FUCKING BASTARD, SCREAM!!” The punches rained down in rapid succession with machine-like precision and speed. Cristos must have had some experience putting the hurt on people in the past, probably as hired muscle no doubt. Every punch was a step closer to litany. Clarity. Oneness with the Gods. Fucking Valhalla. At that moment, there was no crowd and no shitty warehouse. The Brawler was not some old “never-was” who wasted his prime achieving absolutely nothing. The fact that he had forsaken his only source of unyielding love was not even an issue anymore. He was bathed in the sick purity of a healing light, with Cristos as his guide. The beating must have been pretty severe, given that a hushed silence befell the crowd now, the Brawler very aware that he had given up much more blood for this selfish cause then he thought. No wonder he was so light-headed. As the punches rained down, there was no sound, but one. No it was not the sick cracks and thuds of fists meeting flesh and bone. Only?. “How could I eva fahget you, sweetness. You are mah everything’you ARE me. I’ll die befo” I fahget you’yah hea me! I LOVE YOU.” “I love you morer, daddy.” - - - - - - “I love you, Joe. Now say goodbye to Carmen, then get out. And never come back again.” This was a demon the Bronx Barbarian couldn’t topple with his hulking frame. And despite the beating he allowed himself to take in this random shit-hole, it only worked temporarily. He could not escape the gut-wrenching feeling that he may never see his everything, his daughter ever again. This quietly intense tone of his ex-wife forever lingered in his ear, kissing away a life of being a family-man and only being sore from playing with his child. It was enough to make a man want to kill. With that in mind, Cristos was now the equivalent of a condom. You see, a condom is only good temporarily. Good until it has served its purpose. No one keeps a used condom unless they’re a sicko or trying to get pregnant’and in most cases, the other male party has no idea and it makes for a great episode of Maury. Nevertheless, after it is used, you throw it away. Cristos had served his purpose. WHAM~! A meaty New York-style right hand rocketed from the ashes and barreled into Cristos? face. The cocky mauler was taken completely off guard by the blow, Cristos temporarily losing the ability to feel his own face as that same right hand bolted up from below. His head snapped back violently as it collided with his chin, two white entities known as “teeth” and the top piece of his jewel-encrusted grill made their escape from his mouth like a prison break. No mercy, no remorse, there had been none of the Brawler. He turned the tables on this fight as easily as he dogged it, his arms wrapping around a fleeting Cristos as he followed with a sudden roll. Cristos was now the one against the cold ground, doing his best to fight the predicament he was in with hardly any energy left. What would happen next definitely was not what the fans had come to expect. Of course they wanted to see beatings, blood, even a few broken bones. This however, turned into a sick massacre. This was a murderer playing with his victim instead of just finishing them off. This was Joe Balboa at his most simple. A war machine. “I AIN’T NO FUCKIN” LOSER!!!” SMASH~! “DIS AIN’T NO FUCKIN GAME, YA UNDASTAND ME?”!” SMASH~! “I’M DA BACK ALLEY BRAWLER, YO PUNK ASS AIN’T GOT SHIT BITCH!!” SMASH~! “YOU AIN’T TALKIN ALL DAT SHIT NOW, EH”! WHERE’S ALL DA MOUTH NOW, HUH ESSAY(ESSE)?!” SMASH~! Headbutts. Safe to say the Brawler was made. He never saw the need to use them unless he felt like being a “sanctimonious son of a bitch”. Keep in mind, The Brawler couldn’t spell “sanctimonious” if $1,000,0000 depended on it, but he just liked it?.the sound of it. It went with “son of a bitch” so well. Like ketchup on French fries or a good tomato sauce with angel hair pasta. One headbutt was enough to make Cristos a non-factor in the fight, 4 was just the Brawler’s way of expressing his anger the best way he could. A steady flow of Cristos? blood now joined the stains of blood the Brawler had just left behind. Punches soon followed, Cristos serving as the punching bag for all the anguish the Brawler had to deal with in the game called life. It would be an understatement to say he had a lot of it. There was no telling how long he had been beating on Cristos. Seconds, minutes, it all was the same really. The crowd even gave him the respect of scared shitless silence until finally, he got tired of altering Cristos? natural features. Joe fell to his ass, legs sprawled out as he caught his breath and stared down at the mess he left to be cleaned up. Blood all over his cargo pants and tank-top, the Brawler could only think about how unlucky of a bastard Cristos was. Cristos would understand what the Brawler did one day, if he ever had a family he loved. The savage intent of that “fight” would become completely clear to him. The Brawler stood up, already 6’7 but feeling like a titan as he towered over all the fans surrounding him as he prepared to make his way out. “Whoever’s doin the payout hea, give me my fuckin money. I want out or Cristos ova there gets a matchin” partna.” A suited man, Gucci shades adorned over his eyes stepped forward to the beast of a New Yorker, obviously the payout man for the fight. His hands trembled as he reached into the pocket of his fine pinstripe suit, presenting a wad of $100 dollar bills held neatly by a rubber band to the victor of this battle. The Brawler offered a bloody smirk to him, politely taking the money from his hand and placing it in his own pocket. As the Brawler prepared to walk away, all eyes on him as a bringer of destruction, the payout man could not help but ask. “What the hell happened to you man” That was just?.sick. What happened?” He looked directly at the payout man, decades worth of anger rooted deeply in the Brawler’s eyes. The Bronx Barbarian took slow, deliberate breaths as his fists began to clench in an offensive manner, making the payout man step back a few paces. He knew well enough however that if the Brawler chose, he would be his next victim without so much as a struggle. “Life happen’d to me muhfucka. Put it on me good.” And with that, the Brawler made his walk towards the warehouse door. As easily as he had dealt with the masses and Cristos, they would soon be forgotten without another word. Did he feel bad about Cristos? That would mean having to feel bad about every other person he had ever brought pain to in his 42 years on earth. Karen and Carmen had priority when it came to sorrow, no one else. Cristos was shit out of luck and if those headbutts didn’t promise him death, he would wish for it every waking day. Cristos would wake up every day and remember the Back Alley Brawler. He would ask to some higher power why that fate befell him and what he did to deserve it. He deserved it because he was born, plain and simple. Life doesn’t always make you suffer for a reason. Sometimes it does it just to be a say it with me now?. SANCTIMONIOUS SON OF A BITCH. Very good boys and girls. Shortly, the Brawler found himself on the Vegas streets, with its bright lights and fast cars pleasantly overwhelming him like always. Blood-spattered clothes on these streets were as normal as an Elvis suit or a skimpy dress, even more proof that this place would grow on him. As much as he worried and wondered about his forsaken family in the Bronx, he knew that the pain would be at a loss if he did not fulfill his dreams. There was no option for failure at Global. Whoever stood in his way would need to be destroyed, decisively and with flair. Joe Bal’excuse me, The Back Alley Brawler was a man driven. But even the most vicious of mere mortals needed simple things. “Fuck’I need some Jack in da worst fuckin” way.” So, it was off to the bar he would go. Would he do to his opposition what he did to Cristos? If it meant cementing a legend?.no beating was too severe. A loving man, yet a brutal destroyer. How could a man be both and find peace? The answer is? He doesn’t.
