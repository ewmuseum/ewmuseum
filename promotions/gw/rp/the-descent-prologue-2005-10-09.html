The Descent (Prologue)

9 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.09.05 Here we are again, at the bottom of the barrel. It's been such a long time since I've been here, but it almost feels like home. From New York, to New Mexico and back again, something always goes wrong. I guess this is no exception. Eh, the hell am I crying about? Things always have a tendency to go against everyone's plans, people die everyday, it shouldn't be anything new to me. But being single, being a father... being a single father... that's new. 'It's a very good neighborhood,' played the voice in Michael's mind, as he took a single moment to pull his pen from paper, 'and with the location of the house, you won't be too far from family. Not to mention, mister Mercer, the schools in this area are top notch. I'm sure Gabriella would fit in just fine.' Gabriella. The name of his daughter made his eyes close, even if it was only spoken in his own head. After his moment of silent tranquility, his eyes blinked open and Michael returned to writing. Don't bother asking me if I'll be alright after this. Of course I will. Spending time with my daughter and getting to be there as she grows up will be more than enough to pull me out of this. But for that to happen, I have to do something; that something has to happen now. We'll see eachother again, of course. Just don't worry about me. As Michael's pen was pulled away from the paper, he remembered the last words he told the realtor that day. 'I'm sold. I'll take the house.' The house itself was a beauty, and nothing short of that. It would definitely suffice as a place to call home until the bad things in Michael's life washed themselves clean from his memory, and that was all Michael cared about. That, and the well being of his child. One would think that Michael completely forgot about his GW Television Championship. However, with it comfortably placed upon his right shoulder, it was hard for Michael to ignore the pressure of the somewhat heavy leather strap. In fact, it was on his mind ever since he won it. How couldn't it be? He was a wrestler, and a wrestler's mindset was all about titles. But the way he was thinking about his Television Title was quite different. Usually, he would have cherished it, like Michael did any other title win. This time... it was a burden. 'Tomorrow night.. I'll get rid of this piece of junk,' Michael thought clearly to himself, 'and then, I can disappear. Then, things will be bet-' Michael's thoughts were put to an abrupt end, as Markus Riley stepped into the small room, dubbed the guest room for Michael and his daughter for the time being. Michael slowly turned, and in the dimly lit bedroom, the eyes of both men locked onto eachother. 'Everything okay, Michael?' asked the friendly giant. Michael formed a weak smile, and turned to the bed to their side. There, slept an angelic little girl, stirring only momentarily before she sank back into dreamland. Michael smiled. 'Can't complain.' Noting Michael's happiness, it caused the taller man to smile warmly in response. He tipped his samurai hat, and stepped back out into the doorway. 'Okay. Good night, Michael.' 'Good night.' Markus closed the door, and Michael was left to himself once again. He never was one to throw a match, but this title match certainly did call for it. The need to take care of his child, and be there for her, called for it. That was all that mattered. ---- 09.30.05 | The Bronx, New York | Night Mindy read the note left behind by her cousin the morning after Evolution. Shortly before the event was the last time she had seen older Michael, and she wouldn't have thought it to be the last if not for the piece of paper that greeted her and her boyfriend as they re-entered his apartment. 'Just don't worry about me,' were the last words scribbled neatly on the white lined paper. There are a number of reactions that certainly could have been garnered from those words, but the one Mindy chose shouldn't have been much of a surprise. 'Don't worry about you? How can I not worry?! Gods, you're my freakin' cousin! Of course I'm gonna worry!' Markus was immediately there to throw his arms around the Global World Champion as she whimpered in dismay, taking on the cuteness of a puppy dog on the process. Her eyes went bubbly as tears strained to roll down her face, to which Markus soothed her, rocking her back and forth slightly. 'Ssh..' he whispered, trying to calm his girlfriend down, 'Michael's going to be alright.' 'How can you be so sure?' Mindy cried in retort, 'Michael could be in danger from that demon whore wife of his, or something! How can you say Michael will be alright?' Mindy's voice cracked in sadness, and Markus could only support her as they walked to the living room couch. There, Markus sat the petite young lady down, and stood over her in concern. He knew how he would calm her down. 'You said it yourself,' Markus answered calmly. 'He's your cousin. Michael's strong, determined... he'd never let anything eat away at him for so long, and he would never let anyone beat him like that.' Mindy sniffled and rubbed at her eyes, seeming as though she was indeed settling down at least somewhat. 'It's Michael, for fuck's sake.' Mindy's lips parted, as though she wanted to speak, but the look in her eyes said that her voice was non-existent at that point. Slowly, Markus leaned over and kissed his girlfriend on the cheek. 'Get some sleep, hun,' said Markus, 'you'll feel better in the morning. Come.' Markus helped Mindy up to her feet and like a rag doll, he simply picked her up into his arms and carried her to their room. Once there, he tucked Mindy in. 'I promise you,' he stated once more, 'he'll be okay. He won't settle for anything less than okay.' Markus kissed Mindy on the lips. Mindy smiled, and whispered whatever she could. 'I love you.' That was all Mindy said, before she did as her boyfriend requested. She got herself a good night's sleep. 'Still... Michael is my family. I can't just read a note or listen to someone telling me he'll be alright. I need to hear it from him, just see him and make sure, because something tells me he won't be alright. He won't be alright, and it will all be my fault.. I made him wrestle again, just because I wanted to be a tag team with my hero, and I wrecked his life. He can't be okay...' But that didn't mean her sleep was plagued with harrowing thoughts. A week off would do some good. A week off, maybe a title defense to get her mind off of things... '...a wedding. A wedding would be so ideal right about now... anything good... otherwise, I think everything may get very, very bad. God speed, Michael.'
