Book him

8 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/8/03 Amid the cubicles and desks of the small police station, lurches Johnie Ringo. Groggily, he stands frustrated and handcuffed in the middle of it all. The smell of bad coffee and gun grease saturates the thin air along with the stench of old paperwork. He can’t help but clench his teeth and groan to himself on what’s happened. Not that there’s much else for him to do. Standing before the reception desk, Johnie hangs his head down staring at the metal cuffs around his wrists. His hands twisted and locked together as they are unable to move far as he tugs them apart testing the strength of the shackles. Might as well do something while he waits. Tilting his eyes forward he glances back at the clerk on the other side of the counter as he listens to the clacking of the keys he types in on his terminal. Hypnotized by the screen, his dark eyes glaze over going through the many lines of information he types in repeatedly. Eventually he blinks hard and shakes his head, fighting off the lethargy he lulled himself into. Leaning his arm on his desk, the clerk rubs his clubby fingers over his smooth face and scrolls through his shaggy blonde hair. “Johnathan Wilson Ringo,” he mutters with a light sigh. Johnie raises his head slowly gazing up in blank reply. “Aint that your name, boy?” The clerk turns his head trying to see if that drew his attention and frowns at the lacking response. “Frank.” That was the cue for the officer that brought Johnie in stands beside him as he bumps him with his shoulder. So his name’s Frank.. “Yeah,” Johnie mutters, glaring at the officer alongside him. “That’s my name.” Grinning slightly like a teacher that caught a student sleeping, the clerk turns back towards the computer screen and continues on his list. “Age: 19. Eyes: Green. Hair: Black. Height: 5 foot, 10 inches. Weight: 218 pounds. Current Residence: Taos, New Mexico?” Baffled by the address entry, the clerk turns back around towards Johnie and leans back in his rolling chair, crossing his arms. Just above his arms, his nametag gleams in the light showing the name, Officer Sherman. “New Mexico” What on earth are you doing out here?” Johnie lowers his eyes in contempt and shrugs his shoulders. Grumbling hard, he answers hoarsely. “I was driving through, minding my own business. Sounds like something that you should be doing.” The Sherman’s grin fades quickly and turns his eyes toward his partner. Leaning forward, he raises his pointer finger towards Johnie agitatedly. “You watch your mouth, boy, or you’re not gonna have much of a pleasant time here. You get me?” Raising his eyebrows, he glares back up towards him. “Why would I even want to get you?” Johnie scoffs. “What are you gonna do” Huh? Shove me? Beat me? Treat me bad?” He laughs lightly as he looks down at the round gut the clerk is sitting with. “The only thing you could beat me to is a pork buffet.” Turning back toward the computer, the Sherman continues to go down his list. “Next of kin.” Johnie trails off his laughter. “Valerie Ringo: Mother. Jeanna Ringo: Sister. Both of Taos. Hmmm...and we have a Jack Ringo for his father, but there’s no address here for him?” Like you’d be able to find him anyway. He tilts his head back to Johnie and asks sternly. “That’s weird. You know where your daddy is, son?” Johnie snorts a bit and shakes his head. “If I knew where he was, I wouldn’t have to worry about you dumbasses. That’s for sure.” Frank nudges Johnie forward a bit and breathes down his neck. “And why would that be, tough guy?” Johnie’s face turns beet red and slowly turns his head towards him. Clenching his teeth hard so that his jaw cracks, he answers angrily. “Because I’d be out of here by now. And the next time you do that to me, you’re not gonna be too happy.” Chuckling to himself, he puts his hands over Johnie’s shoulders. “Whatever you say, big shot. Let’s move on.” Pushing Johnie forward down the counter, to the laid out papers and forms. Alongside that is a little tin box. “Time to get your fingerprints.” “What’s next” Cake and ice cream?” Johnie pops off over his shoulder. “Funny, smart guy.” Frank leans Johnie over the desk and lifts Johnie’s hands up by the cuffs and plops them on the table. “Just don’t mess up my counter. An ear curdling squeak sounds from the Sherman’s chair as he gets up out of his chair. He groans and leans himself backward as he creaks and stretches as though he’s been sitting there for hours. Bending at his desk, he picks up his mug of coffee and starts to waddle over to the counter. “Alright,” he opens with as he flips the tin case of printing open “let’s get this started already. Fingering the form of finger blocks on there, Sherman twists it around and faces it towards Johnie. He also nudges the tin case to him as well. “Its simple, kid. Just like on TV, only a bit different. All you have to do is rub your fingers in the printing and roll your fingers in the blocks. Frank, show him.” “Gladly.” Officer Frank grabs a hold of Johnie’s wrist with his palms and grips the fingers with his thumb and fore finger, holding it straight out. Putting Johnie’s pointer finger into the ink, he rolls it around the bottom and lifts it out slowly. Nudging his hand over to the paper, Frank lowers the hand and flattens Johnie’s finger down and rolls it left to right putting down a print. “There. You think you can do that” Or do I have to hold your hand again?” Johnie turns and glares back at him with a slight grimace as he pulls his hand away from Frank. Shaking his head, he repeats the motion for his middle finger and turns it back towards Frank, still extended and flipping him off. “There! That good enough for you?” He turns his finger back towards Sherman and grins a bit. “How about you, teletubby?” Both of their faces drop into a scowl and Frank grabs a hold of Johnie’s hand lowering it and glares straight into his eyes. “Enough of the jokes, kid. Just get the damn prints done, or...” “Or what?” Johnie returns the stare as a certain coldness rolls over his eyes. His jaw becomes raw as though every muscle in his face is flexed. “Huh” Or what?!” Johnie twists his hands around the cuffs and grabs a hold of the officer’s wrists. Frank looks back, but the toughness he once displayed starts to fade for an instant. That’s long enough for him. Sherman can’t help but just watch in minor shock and mumble lightly to himself. “You’re nothing more than a pussy in a blue suit, Franky,” Johnie snarls. He shoves Frank back a couple feet and smirks slightly as he looks back at the fearful officer with spite. He turns back towards the counter and continues with the printing, finger by finger. He finishes it with quick ease and shoves the paper towards the clerk. The paper sails off of it and falls into Sherman’s chest enough so he could scramble and catch it quick. Johnie turns his head back towards Frank and gawks blankly. Grabbing a miniwipe off the counter, he cleans off his fingertips slowly. “Done.” Stepping forward with his right foot, Johnie goes towards Officer Frank and tosses the wipe over the desk. Looking him up and down, Johnie shakes his head sighs loudly. “You see, I don’t care about what jokes and shit you get out of bringing me in. Or about how you think you’re better than me. I could kick your ass all over the place right now and walk out the door. No problem!” Chuckling to himself, he steps back towards Sherman and leans on the counter. “But the deal is, I don’t want anymore of this bullshit. Not any of this escaped from custody crap and I get a worse sentence than what I’m already getting. So here’s what we’re gonna do. We’re gonna finish this booking crap, I get my phone call or whatever, and we get this shit over with for the night. We clear”!” Both officers stare blankly at Johnie and don’t reply. “I’ll take that as a “Yeah”. Now what’s next?” Sherman pipes up softly and answers. “Mugshots.” “Oh goody,” mocks Johnie and motions to Sherman to lead the way. “Let’s get this shit over with.” ********************************************************************* Officer Frank follows behind Johnie as he leads him towards the phone near steel bars of the prison cells. There are only a couple of cells there, so it must be a small county jail. Frank leaves Johnie by the phone and backs away a few feet for privacy. “Make it quick,” he mutters. Johnie shakes his head and picks up the receiver as he dialing out. Buttons clicking and beeping make a crude synonymous sound as the call starts to go through. The phone hums over hardwire lines to the person being called. A click echoes from the other end as a grumble is heard on the other end. “Hello?” The voice is rough, but there’s still some feminity to it. “Jeanna?” He doesn’t know why he’s calling her. But somebody has to be called. “Its...its Johnie.” “Johnie?” she questions with a tired exclamation. “What are you calling so late for” Its 4 in the morning.” “Jeanna, listen.” Johnie pauses and sighs roughly. “I got arrested.” “WHAT”!” That woke her up. “I got arrested and I’m somewhere in Texas. I have no idea where.” “What the” What are you doing in Texas?” she inquires with a more sobriety in her, but still a little grumble to her voice. “Nevermind that.” Johnie leans his forearm against the phone cubicle and shakes his head downward. “I just needed you to know that and to bail me the hell out of here.” “Bail you out” What did you do?” Johnie sighs deeply and twists himself around to lean his back this time. “I got a DUI.” Silence... His eyes open up from a slight droop as he raises his brow in surprise. “Jeanna?” Nothing... “You there” Jeanna?!” The silence is broken from a hard groan and a response just a rough. “Fuck you, Johnie. You’re on your own.” Click Johnie stares forward and his face turns dark red with his eyes. “What”!” He turns around and slams the receiver repeatedly against the phone and screams in rage. Beating that booth and phone apart, shattering the plastic case around it. Officer Frank runs over and tries to restain Johnie by pulling him backwards. He doesn’t fight...just convulses in anger. “DAMMIT!! URRRRRRGGGHHHHHH!!” The cop pulls him back to his feet somewhat and brings him towards the cellblock as he fights and contorts himself around. Leaning Johnie against the ground, he quickly tries to unlock one of the cells and swing it open. The scream and creak of the rusted door echoes through the hall. Frank picks Johnie back up and shoves him into the cell, locking the door behind him. As soon as Johnie gets into the cell, he starts pounding on the concrete walls and beating his head and knuckles against it. His hands grow red and bruised quickly, but he keeps beating against the wall. He quickly grows tired, and he falls against the wall and streaks down it slowly to the floor. Leaning against the lower wall and steel bars, Johnie tries to pull himself together. Wiping his jaw and forehead, he turns around and props against the steel bunk frame beside him and shakes his head. TO BE CONTINUED.....
