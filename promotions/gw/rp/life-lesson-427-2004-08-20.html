Date: 8.20.04 Life Lesson # 427 - Nothing in life is ever too important to be taken without humor. “Ah, such a day is today. The squirrels are chirping, the birds are hording acorns into their four story condominiums and I’m late, I’m late, it’s nearly 27 o’clock.” The rant comes from none other than TUE’s pretty boy, Tyler Pierce, the Echelon’s prodigal son and earliest retiree. Has this prodigal son fulfilled his prophecy and returned beneath the Echelon’s banner!” “I’m late, I’m late, If I don’t slow down, I might make that meeting.” Tyler walks along the street underneath a Bostonian sun, a flare in his walk as he struts his beautiful self down the sidewalk in Boston’s business district. Adorned in an alabaster white suite complete with black accents and a golden finish, he lifts his wrist to gaze down at his golden Rolex. “Indeed, indeed, I’m late I see. Time for me to fly the coop, as it were.” Tyler stretches a bit and glances to the side, his eyes following the curvaceous form of a beautiful woman, adorned in business clothing, her hair up atop her head, a pair of glasses resting atop her nose. A briefcase dangles from one hand, a hand purse in the other. Tyler stops and turns on his Armani wingtips, his eyes blatantly aimed at her barest of essentials. To Tyler, he’s not seeing the dressed-for-success business woman, oh no no! He’s seeing her blouse next to his bed and her bare flesh laid out for his enjoyment. Softly, Tyler begins to sing in a whiny, mocking tone, “Your body is a wonder-laAAnd.” He reaches up, tilting his glasses down a bit to continue his all-to-obvious stare before sucking air through his teeth, releasing it all at once and pushing them up again. “DAMN! I love this city.” Tyler continues walking again, strutting towards the medium sized sky-scraper at the corner of the block, his eyes lifting and gazing at the multitude of windows that ornament the building’s face. “Pierce Imports Unlimited, like money in the bank, baby!” Tyler adjust his tie and walks to the building and pushes the double glass doors open in one powerful thrust. From inside, it’s one hell of a vision. The doors fly open simultaneously, catching everyone’s attention. Light rushes in from outside, the Bostonian sun giving a near silhouetted view of Mr. Tyler Pierce. He stands with his arms outstretched and his head thrown back, his shimmering blonde locks reflecting the sun much like a prism. His alabaster suit glows at the edges in strict contrast with his black shirt and tie. He stands with his legs together, his body at a slight angle, a wide grin on his face. “No worries, ladies, I may be late” but we’ll make sure you’re not!” “Arrogant, as always, Tyler.” Tyler lowers his head, his Armani gold frame sunglasses slipping down his nose a bit so that his deep blue eyes can peer at the source of the voice. The voluptuous Naria Xiou is seated atop a receptions desk in the center of the room, her feet adorned in high heels and her legs wrapped in shear stockings that ride almost up the where her short skirt cuts off. The skirt rides up at her thigh and leads perfectly into the business suit top that finished the ensemble. Her hands are covered in black gloves and her neck clasped with a black collar. Her hair drapes in all directions from her head to her shoulders and back, transitioning from black to purple and back again. Her lips are purple, Naria’s lipstick color of choice. Tyler’s slightly shocked expression turns to one complete with a shit-eating grin. He slips his sunglasses off and steps away form the doors, Tyler strides with pure confidence towards Naria and stops directly in front of her, his hands on the desk right at her sides. Naria glances at each of his hands and then back at him. “What brings you here, Naria” Miss me?” Naria smirks and slowly lifts her leg, giving only Tyler a peek beneath that obscuring skirt before pressing the sole and heel of her shoes against his chest and pushing him back. “Not for pleasure.” Tyler makes an exaggerated face to show his pain and shakes his hands, “Ouch, I think I just got frost bitten!” Tyler grins and picks up a dossier from the receptionists desk and opens it up, his eyes skimming it quickly, “So what does bring you here to this, my humble abode?” Tyler speaks even as he reads, his charm still being applied rather healthily. Naria slips off the desk as Tyler begins to walk away and joins him at his side, speaking softly now that no one else can hear. “As I said before, not pleasure. There have been a few” complications on the Global front.” Tyler nods as they reach a stairwell and glances back at the small group of people in the main room of the building. “I’ve been watching. What happened to Maverick?” Tyler glances at Naria, his eyes showing his true curiosity. The two continue up the stairs, speaking softly out of earshot of anyone, including us. ---- “So that puts the Echelon in quite the predicament. John and Deac are covering for him and aren’t having as much time for the Global front and that just results in us looking bad.” Naria speaks softly, her hands now clasped behind her back as they arrive at the 18th floor of Pierce Imports Unlimited. What? You were expecting the 27th? “And so this is why you have come to Bean Town.” Tyler glances up at her, a shimmer of mischievousness in his grin. “No other reason” Not even a little love for a friend?” Naria laughs softly and shakes her head, “You never give up, do you?” Tyler smiles and shakes his head no. The two approach a large door and stop, Tyler’s hand on the handle. “How about a little lust?” Naria’s normally dry expression loosens yet again to show a smile as Tyler sighs again, “Can’t blame a guy for trying.” Tyler smiles and motions with the dossier, “We’ve got this guy from Korea in today” or rather, he’s coming in today and we’re getting a briefing about him. Care to sit in? After this, we can talk more in depth? or whatever in ? depth.” Tyler doesn’t allow a response as he pushes the door open instantly, revealing a small board room complete with files, a small projector and computer system, and stiffs in suits. All what you would expect in a board room in Boston, Massachusetts. “Gentlemen, Tyler Pierce has arrived. I may be late but” ? Tyler smiles even more, “Well, never mind. How are you gents doing today?” The room is complete with four men, one of them is Kyle Pierce, Tyler’s older brother and one of the five co-owners of Pierce Imports along with their other brother Scott, their sister Melissa and of course their mother, Deborah. It has been only a year since the death of the patriarch of the family, Samuel Pierce. The other three men are other high-level shareholders and an advisor from Korea. “This is Miss Xiou, she’s from our brother company Echelon Incorporated. She’ll be sitting in on this meeting if there are no objections.” Tyler gives enough time for a breath to be taken before continuing, “Good! Let’s get started.” One of the men stands up and begins to speak and does so rather well; it’s obvious that he’s been in this position numerous times, “Later today, the sole son of Mr. Takari Kenn will be arriving by plane to inspect the shipments that are to be shipped out to Mr. Kenn in Korea. It’s a random process that the Kenn’s have been doing for quite some time and up until now we’ve always been passed over. Even though this is normal procedure, do not take this lightly. Mr. Kenn is very serious when it comes to this practice and will very quickly jerk his account away from us.” The man picks up a folder and hands each person, excluding Naria who they were not expecting a photo of the young Korean man. Naria slides in next to Tyler and the two gaze at the photo. “The man’s name is Kantaki Kenn and ?” Tyler lifts his head and lifts a brow, “Kantaki Kenn” His father must’ve have been in a good mood that day?” “Yes” well. His name is Kantaki Kenn but he demands to be called by his full name, including his middle name, Che.” The advisor speaks and prepares to move on when Tyler interrupts him again. “Wait a damned minute. His name is Kantaki Che Kenn?” Tyler grins a bit, looking around to see if anyone else notices the humor in this situation. No one else in the room seems to be laughing or enjoying this half as much as Tyler. Naria smiles at the other men in the room who all seem to be quite perturbed. Kyle stand and walks behind his brother, a hand squeezing his shoulder as a signal for his silence. Tyler smirks and jerks his back, popping it quickly, “Ok ok, fine. Let’s move on” what do we need to know?” And hours passed like millennia. Days would have taken eons, and years would have been an unfathomable stretch for the human mind to ever comprehend. However, the meeting only took another half hour so the above was a waste of time. ? anyway. “So, Naria” what’s the point? I mean, what do you want me to do about all of this?” Tyler asks as the two walk slowly down a near-vacant hallway, his eyes shot towards the tile floors before him. Naria opens her briefcase and reaches inside to withdraw a clipboard with a few papers attached. “Tyler, we want you to renew your TUE Global contract and return to the ring.” Tyler stops dead in his tracks. Naria takes a few steps before turning and glancing back at Tyler, “and what if I say no?” Naria smiles a bit and walks forward, her arms slinking around his neck. “Do you remember last year in Japan. We spent a week before Showdown there” on the Echelon’s bill, just enjoying one another’s company?” Naria leans in, her nose gently brushing Tyler’s cheek. “Do you remember ? when you won your match” A three man tag match? you came to the back and you were tired and sore. Do you remember how I got your mind off of it, Tyler?” Tyler slips his arms around Naria’s waist before she pushes him back against the wall, her teeth finding the flesh under his jaw line and upon his neck. Tyler exhales and closes his eyes for a moment before Naria pulls back and straightens her top. “So are you in?” ?” you don’t give a guy much of a decision to make, do you?” Tyler smiles, his eyes opening again. Naria picks up her briefcase and starts walking towards the elevator at the end of the hallway. “Your first match is on Monday against Brick and Train. You’ll be tagging with Josh” and if you win, I’ll bring you your paycheck personally.” Naria smiles without looking back, a satisfied look on her face. Tyler stands in the center of the hallway, his hair slightly disheveled. He speaks up, calling after her, “You already set it up” What if I had said no!”” Tyler asks curiously as Naria reaches the elevator. She hits the button and the doors open for her to step inside. She turns and faces Tyler with a smile. “A moment ago, Tyler, “no” was not in your vocabulary.” The doors close with Naria behind them. Tyler grins and slips his hands into his pockets before he crinkles his brow. He pulls a small key from the jacket pocket with a tag marked “127.” Tyler shakes his head and turns, looking at one of his employees. “What” I was in charge the entire time?” The employee just smirks and turns away shaking her head. Tyler walks after her, holding his arms out slightly, “What!” I was!” And with this, the scene fades to nothing.
