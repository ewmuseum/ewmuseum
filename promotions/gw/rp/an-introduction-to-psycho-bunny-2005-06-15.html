An Introduction to Psycho Bunny

15 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
An Introduction to Psycho Bunny Psycho Bunny Global Wrestling Promo #2097 Date: 06/15/05 Location: Happy Town and the Arena Then… Happy Town is so Happy… That is what the sign reads as you come into this swanky little town in the middle of nowhere. Cartoon animals roam about acting like humans. In the inn, a rabbit is cleaning dishes. At the local farm, a marmot is pulling weeds. In the town orchard, a pika is picking fresh fruits. But all that is about to change. At the entrance to the town, a fierce, feral looking creature with a backwards hat on appears. He is wearing black sunglasses, a blue T-shirt that says “F*CK YOU ALL” on it, jeans, black shoes, has a large assault rifle in his right hand, and is smoking a cigarette. It’s a bunny that many in this little quaint town both know and fear. It is their brethren Psycho Bunny and he’s escaped from the metal box the government kept him in. After the government found this little happy place in the middle of nowhere during an environmental survey, they found one of the cartoon creatures and captured it. It was Psycho Bunny. He was just a little bunny back then. So happy, so fragile… And then, they took him away to their labs and experimented on him. They used experimental mind modification techniques on him and tortured him for days on end because he supposedly had no feelings… They were wrong. He had feelings. He had emotions. And he had a home that he knew and loved. But then they took him and used him like a lab rat. That was their mistake. They didn’t kill him. They let him live. When the experiments got too brutal and too dangerous, they decided to end them altogether and place him in a metal box deep within the Earth for the rest of his days. And then, something happened. His caretaker had a heart attack right in front of his cell and Psycho Bunny grabbed his keys. He then let himself out and grabbed the nearest weapon he could find. An Uzi 9mm… He then killed everyone he came across. Those bastard scientists fucked him up for life, so he decided to fuck them up himself. After he had killed everyone in the secret underground lab, he escaped through the hatch topside and ran through the forest. He then came upon a small town where he stole some clothes, stole some food, and then stole a shitload of guns. He then decided to go pay his old town a visit. Those bastards never came looking for him. Where we they when he was being tortured? He knew where they were. Picking their fruits and cultivating their crops. They were all one thing he wasn’t. Happy. So now it’s payback time. He cocks, locks, and loads his weapon. He takes a puff from his cigarette and smiles as he looks around Happy Town. “Fuckers,” he says to himself. “None of them cared about me when they took me away.” A demented, evil smile comes across his face. His rabbit teeth stick out like a sore thumb. “It’s time to fucking pay the piper.” He heads into town and that is where the scene fades out for the violence that happens on this day has a lasting effect on us all, for one of the world’s most callous, dangerous, and evil murderers begins his quest at vengeance. And this evil, cold blooded killer has only one name. A name that strikes fear in the hearts of even the bravest of men. A name that has a $2 billion bounty on his head. Look at the reward posters in your local town and I’m sure you’ll see one name at the top of them. Psycho Bunny. * * * Now… Psycho Bunny has not had his fill of blood and violence just yet. Oh no. his bloodlust is never ending... After destroying nearly everything and everyone on the planet, the authorities finally caught him and because of lax laws passed by the idiot president of the future, Psycho Bunny is now on a work release program working as a wrestler. Oh yes. Psycho Bunny, the world's most dangerous killer, is now a professional wrestler and working here in GW. Cops accompany him to the arena and take off his shackles and harnesses, almost as if they are unleashing a captive beast back into the wild. Now, he has a tough task ahead of him. A match against two people. “Fuck them,” he says to himself with a growl. “They are nothing against my bunny powers. I can rip them apart like carrots if I wanted to. Fuckers.” Bunny looks around. His eyes gleam as scared people run when they see him. “Fuckers,” he says to himself. “That’s all they are. I have no fear of anyone they have here in GW or in that AMW Root Beer company or whatever it is they’re fighting. (shaking his head) Children. That is all they are. Soon me and my friends will rule this federation with our violence and power.” The cop motions for him to move along. “Hey! You’re not in your (chuckles) *comic book* anymore, so move it Bunny before we have to MAKE you move your ass.” Psycho Bunny smiles. Foolish human. You never mess with a psychopath in a bunny costume who thinks he’s a psychotic comic book character. Psycho Bunny turns around and smiles at the cop. “Huh?” the cop says. Psycho Bunny then punches the cop repeatedly in the face and takes him down in a headlock only to beat the crap out him some more before the other cops come and take him to his locker room. Once they throw him in there, they lock the door. Psycho Bunny slams his fist against the door. “Fuckers! The show’s not until Sunday!” No response from his captors as Bunny looks around and then sits on a bench nearby. He starts looking for an escape route immediately. “These bastards can’t hold me in here forever,” he says as he looks over and sees some golf clubs and rope just sitting there, waiting to be used. A smile comes across his bunny face. “It’s almost too easy…” [Cut.]
