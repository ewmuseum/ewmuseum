The Sorrow of One Man...The Weight of the World

27 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-27-2004 Sometimes, only the presence of great sadness can make ones mind clear. Only in times of the greatest mental duress can one find true meaning to the things that he must do, and the things that he is able to choose to do?. Pendragon sat by the fire, shirtless in the moonlight. Staring into the flames, his eyes seem mesmerized by the flickering oranges, the vibrant yellows contained in the fire. Leaning against a log for support, he holds the black blade Lucien by the hilt with both hands. The fire crackles and spits on occasion, yet he pays the happenings no mind. His mind is occupied by other matters, as of now, even though it would appear that he only has eyes for the flames. A short journey inside the man’s head may divulge a little?. “Sadness. Hand in hand we’ve walked these long years, and she is a lonely mistress. Countless battles fought, in a hundred lands’Some have proven pointless, whilst others have gained me much glory and great honor. The size of the battle has never truly mattered’I’ve led armies against hordes of Fangspawn, and I’ve defeated men in single combat. I have been one with mine blade, and mine wrath has been the stuff of legends. Each man must find his niche, and it seems that to lead’To fight’To rule’These are all niches to which the puzzle piece known as Venneman Pendragon has fit. Mine only cause was mine ownself, and I never realized the sadness that I ran from; that I STILL run from.” Pendragon stands abruptly and stretches his strong frame. The sword moves with him, almost as if it were a part of him. He notices it not as he absently scabbards the blade at his hip and steps to a small pile of wood. He takes a handful of the faggots and places them in the fire, each in a point where it will catch flame and burn with the most efficiency. He smiles to himself. He is one for proper order, after all. An owl hoots in the darkness, and his hand drops quickly to his hip, dark blade instantly free and set to deal death to whatever ventures to close to his campsite. With a wry chuckle, he shakes his head and drops the blade back into the scabbard, careful to keep it loose. Who knows what beast or enemy lurks in these parts. Sighing, he sits back down, facing the fire. He grabs a stick lying nearby and idly begins to prod at the fire? “Sadness has been mine whole life. I knew mine true father but for a short time, and those memories come back hazy at best. Mine adopted father lies below the ground, a victim of fate. I have no family. The last of the great Pendragon line, and when I go, so to do I fear does the blood. Memories. Memories of many things. The defeat of sadness, the joy of victory. The love of a hundred different women. Sometimes memories such as these float to the surface of mine mind’Unbidden, I know not from where these dreams come from. “Tis madness I fear’Creeping in around the edges of mine being. They grow stronger since Bob made himself known to Lord Pendragon’Surely these are things I never bore witness to, yet it seems as if I were there?”..” ************************FLASH************************ Clad in steel chain mail, helmet under his arm, Pendragon looks out over the castle wall at the approaching enemy. Whether he admits so or not, it is him. Blonde hair streaming back from his brow as he uses a looking glass to peer into the distance. A small group of men with the look of veteran campaigners stand behind him, faces grim. One man holds a banner’A magnificent golden dragon. It almost seems to move as the wind ripples the material of which it is made. Pendragon shakes his head and hands the peering-scope to one of the men standing behind him. A shorter man with dark hair, he looks into the glass and shakes his head also? “What say ye, Lord Crelior?” Pendragon rumbles this in a voice resigned to the answer? “They shalt roll over this keep as floodwater over a footbridge. We shall not hold’And yet, with the Fangspawn at out backs, retreat is not an option either. I fear this is the last for Kingdom Cenethor.” The smaller man replies, and although death awaits them, the light of battle slowly dawns in their eyes. These are hardy King’s Men, and if death awaits, they shall go to it screaming defiance to the end? In a soft voice, Pendragon whispers?“Death smiles at us all’All a man can do is smile back?” The men nod and bow as they move to take charge of their units of foot soldiers and horse soldiers. Lord Crelior hangs back?“Crelior. Thee has the defense of the wall. If it comes to it, the women know what to do. There shall be no prisoners taken this day.” He places his right fist to his chest and nods his head. Crelior follows suit and adds? “It has been mine honor serving thee, King Pendragon.” Pendragon claps the man on the shoulder before jaunting off? “I shalt see thee in the next life’Good journey, mine friend”.” **********************FLASH************************** The campsite’The flames have died a bit lower, but healthy fire still burns as Pendragon sits lost in his thoughts? “How did it come to that” Cenethor was a strong Kingdom. Her people brave and hearty to the last. As I rode out at the head of the last army of Cenethor, the people cheered me, though they knew that death was just over the horizon’I remember the spear that took me, and the sword that cleaved mine skull as I lay prone?”.NO! It cannot be! I live yet’Where are these memories coming from? What does this mean? And yet, not all of the memories are bad ones. Some are the remembrances of an old King?.Some are the remembrances of great General Kings who led their armies to decided victories’I was there, on the shores of Loch Schiel’The great battle of Henry’s Gap?.” **********************FLASH************************** Mounted upon a white horse, Pendragon sits in battered armor. Nothing decorative about this suit. It has seen use and not always come out on the good side of the fight. “The Loch on one side, and the mountains on the other. This is the only way for Lord Ranigal to get his troops through to mine kingdom. The only way that he can get his men into our homes. We must hold the gap until First Lieutenant Jondril can get his men here from the west. We have the men to hold this gap’With his men, we can hold the gap and destroy the upstart Ranigal.” He reigns his horse around followed by his retainers. A different banner flies behind him on this day’A black flag holds the red image of a Lion. Red on Black’Sigil of the Kingdom of Narces. The scene abruptly changes, showing Pendragon, at the head of his army, doing battle with wild eyed invaders right in the Gap. Bodies are strewn here and there upon the field, and Pendragon’s mighty sword, Judgment, sends death in it’s wake as Pendragon swings the blade. From his rear comes the cry of... “JONDRIL!!! JONDRIL!!!” Pendragon smiles as the heavily armored cavalry swoops down from the hilltop. Pendragon’s army parts and the charge leads straight into the enemy. They scatter, and the men?. *********************FLASH************************** “They cheered for me that day’Cheered for the death that Judgment doled out’Judgment” What of mine blade Excalibur? Were they the same? They felt as the same to mine palm’So many memories. So many fights. Happiness found and lost, life lived and given for a cause. Yet now, I am embarked upon a journey never conceived by mine mind’The lonely Pendragon is to save the world?” He smiles ruefully and pokes absently at the fire before him. His eyes remain far away as he thinks, trying to figure out the route he must take’Bob has not been especially forthcoming with instructions? “God’s Hammer. Would it not be better that I were God’s Blade” Who uses a hammer? I am no blacksmith or carpenter. “Tis with a strong arm and a strong blade that I have always done mine work. Perhaps Bob works a bit differently?” (For those of you new to the Pendragon saga, Bob is God. Or, so Pendragon has been told’By none other than Bob! Bob intends to make Pendragon his weapon against evil’Enough with the background’If you wish to learn more, read the previous writes’Geesh! You think I got all day?) “He wishes me to use GW as a staging area. GW would seem a perfect place to start. The problem thus far is that GW has forced me to do battle with men who are just that”.Men. I need to face evil. Evil men are more than men; they are the shadow. “Tis the shadow with whom mine war must be fought. Anyone else who falls by mine hand is but a casualty of the war that has raged between the Shadow and the Light for thousands of years. I had nearly given up hope of honorable management by GW officials until the Maximum Carnage card was finally released’Men of poor character headed the card’Killers, a demon’A demon. This Jackson was surely off kilter, but a Reaver” I would have to ask Bob about it’Carnage was set up to be just that: Carnage. This was not supposed to be an arena where an agent of the White showed the world that good would always triumph?.This was to be an event where darkness gathered to snuff out the light. Alone, I can destroy all opponents put before me in the upcoming gauntlet. If a pair of the wrong ones enter the ring, it will be more difficult. Glory and honor would once more be mine for the taking. That is mine price, and the only one Bob has agreed upon.” Pendragon shivers and reaches behind himself. He finally finds what he is looking for and he pulls his cloak around his shoulders, wrapping his upper body in the garment. “And yet, I truly know not for what it is Bob has me fighting. This is a dieing world, beset on all sides with evil and villainy. I remember clean places, pure places, that fell along the wayside. A hundred deaths defending purity I have suffered. Each one was a noble end for a noble cause. Each loss was a tragedy that amplified the sadness that still holds to me like a shroud. And the memories become more clear each day. Memories that make me ask?” Leaping up, he shakes his fist at the sky, roaring defiance into the night?“Where were thee, Bob” Where were thee when Cenethor fell to the Shadow? Where were thee when the Ten Nations were destroyed by the demon reaver army led by Chanithor? What kept thee when?.” *********************FLASH*************************** Pendragon lies shackled, stripped to his smallclothes, on a platform in the middle of a Keep Square. Thousands of faces stare at him, and he looks back at them with no fear. Lashings from a whip cover his body, and blood drips slowly from the wounds. At the sound of trumpets, the people quiet and a large black haired man steps onto the platform, followed by a man with an axe and another with a barrel. The man with the black hair grins at Pendragon and his grin is met with a snarl as Pendragon strains against the chains that hold him bound? “My people! Long has this’Person’Desecrated this wonderful world of ours. Only in his defeat can you truly be free!” The crowd cheers, but interspersed throughout are the faces of those who weep for their fallen king. Kind and just, complacency was his only sin?“And now, to be rid of his line forever, I bring you the child, Benjamin, and the lady Sarah!” The crowd parts as a small boy and a beautiful woman are led up the platform. Tears well up in Pendragon’s eyes as he strains harder against the chains, each muscle defined beneath the skin, quivering with the exertion to free himself. The chains hold though, almost as if designed to hold just this man’The man with the black hair forces the crying Benjamin and the serene faced Sarah down, and straps their heads to the barrel. Faces towards Pendragon. He thrashes about, but it is to no avail. “SARAH!!!!!!!!!!! BENJAMIN!!!!!!!!!!!!” He roars in pain as the axe drops and the two heads fall free of their bodies’Sarah mouths the words “I love you” as?”. **************************FLASH********************** “SARAH AND BENJAMIN WERE TAKEN FROM ME!!!” He sags, and a tear rolls down his cheek?“Though I do not remember them fully, I shalt never forget them”.” In a blinding flash, Bob is there, a kindly look set upon his face. Pendragon staggers back at the suddenness, hand dropping to his blade, but it stops as he reaches it. “I know your pain, Venneman”.I am sorry?.Come, we must speak. There is much that you need to know?” Pendragon slumps, his head in his hands, as Bob walks near and places a comforting hand on his massive shoulder? “I was happy, then’A wife, a child’What more did I need” Was I not deserving? What have thee set me to save here? This world is but a shell of the one’s thee allowed to fall to darkness’Why are these people more deserving than I?”” ~TO BE CONTINUED~
