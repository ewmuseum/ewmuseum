News she didn't want...

26 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-26-05 One woman? One woman in a fight against several other men. Clash of the Titans. In a style that I have never faced before. I was to walk into a ring and try and toss out several other men. Some that are stronger, faster, and more experienced inside a style like this then I was. They say that in every person’s greatest fear he or she will find a place in their heart that will bring them great strength. A power that will make it to were they can over come almost everything. For me I must find it without my uncle. Without the person that has taught me everything inside a ring that I know. But in that adventure I will find the entire side of me. In Japan, they say that in all of us we have an entire animal. That our spirit is driven by a single tiger, bear, or something else. I’ve never questioned this as I was always driven by the power that he gave me. I was proud to be my father’s daughter and to carry the Smith name. But would that be all that I needed or did I need more? In this fight, would I be strong? Would I be scared for myself or would I be scared of failure? So many question that could hurt me but there was only one way to get the answers. That would be to fight first hand against them? ********** It was a bright sunny day outside. Not a single drop of rain or cloud in sight. The thing you’d see on a beach in Florida or California. There was a slight small breeze that came from the west. Blowing after the brownish leaves that had fallen from the local trees that stood every few feet down the street walkway. But the day wasn’t so good for everything, or for everyone. I hadn’t been gone for long. Only being up a couple hours but the hot and steamy coffee that I had resting in the cup holder in the car wasn’t helping much. As a matter of fact it wasn’t helping at all. Though what was helping me be awake was the fact that I was going into a game that made me think. Clash of the Titans. The match that I wasn’t trained for. I just couldn’t stop thinking about it. I thought of all the could happen in the match. I could lose badly and be tossed out right when I enter. I could mess up and cost my family name, my uncles name, and make him and me a failure. Or I could win and become something that I am not ready to be. So many different things could happen and the music wasn’t helping either. On the radio, as I raced down the highway towards Uncle Johnson’s training facility, was a man with a bitter strong voice. His name was Russ. Bright and happy on this Saturday morning. “Hello folks and welcome today’s news on the news.” His flash and cocky voice said. “Today we start off with the local wrestling promotion out of Dallas called Global Wrestling with their PPV called, Clash of the Titan’s.” The hand had a mind of it’s own, turning up the volume button before reaching for the coffee cup that I had. “It’s just one day away. I’m so excited about this because some say that the bar’s will be lifted once this hits. And for me I got so many men that can win this.” Pausing, the guy coughed a little before continuing. “One day away and my money is on Mike Biddle or Adam Myers. Both are red hot and on the move up the talent roster.” Did he say men? Only a man can win this? Again, another person to hold down a woman. I might not be the strongest dog in the fight but this world wasn’t made for a man only. Turning left at the light I saw the building. Listening a little longer before my stop to come, came. “But lets not forget the women. Women like Mindy Mercer, Aradia Xiomara, or the new girl Elia Smith might do well but come on, they have no chance at winning this. They might as well just stay out of the way.” SKRECKKKKKKK! The car came to a stop as I slammed on the break. Did he just say that I should stay out of the way? I swear, some people really know how to tear an already downed woman even more. I opened the door, not even at my needed stop fully and slammed it right behind me. Walking down the long windy street towards the facility, I began to think of everything my father once told me. ******* “You can’t wrestling,” My father said. “You aren’t good enough.” “Yes I am. I’m better then you think.” Slamming the door that was in between the two of us. I walked away from it, not caring but it wasn’t long till it was pushed open, making me jump around to see who it was. Standing there still was my father, looking even angrier then he was before. “Don’t you slam that door on me. I own this house and you will listen to me.” Screaming as I turned around. “I will not listen to you because I will do this and there is nothing you or anyone can do about it!” ******** God those were the times. All the fighting, all the screaming, finally it’s over with the death of him. But this new fight was harder then I could ever imagine. Passing by the people that were yup walking the streets, I turned the corner and grabbed the handle of the training facility. “Come on?” Those were the first words that screamed out as the door opened. I got that sudden sweaty and musty smell right up my nose, making be stop in my tracks. The heat from the massive amount of people in the workout room made me wipe the top of my forehead, rubbing the little make up that I had on my face. At the front desk of the gym was none other then my good friend, Massy Williams. “Elia!” She said, turning to me. “Hey Massy. Uncle Johnson here yet?” Walking up to the hard wood desk that she stood around from. “Yeah,” she said, pointing towards the ring. “he told me to tell you that he wants you first thing when you come in.” I began to walk to the back towards the ring, looking at him as he trained a few of the other men inside the ring. They looked the way I looked several years ago. Pacing by the machines and the men and woman that used them, I could hear his voice rattle inside that square circle. “You got to show aggression when you’re in here. You got to want it more then the other person or you’re gone.” His father like voice said. “So I should do whatever it takes?” One of the young and up and coming talents that he trained asked. Uncle Johnson shook his head, “No.” Walking up to him with his hand placed on his shoulder, “You should do what he least expects.” Uncle Johnson grabbed him by the head and shoulder and ran for the side of the ring. Tossing him right over the top rope and to the floor were I stood. The trainee held his head and body as Uncle Johnson looked right at him. “Ok boys, times up.” He said, calling me to get inside the ring. What did he want me in there for? I didn’t wait much longer as I was there to talk to him even if he needed me more. I walked up the set of stairs and into the ring. It seemed weird being in there after so many years. Uncle Johnson came over, padding me on the back before he gave me a hug. “I’m glad you came.” Uncle Johnson said, pulling away and looking at me with his sad bluish eyes. “What do you need from me Uncle Johnson, I’m fixing to lift some weights before flying out for the Clash.” Asking away, trying not to look to worried towards him. “Look, you know I’m not one to be on speeches so I’ll make this quick. I’m retiring from this business. I’m tired and I want all my teaches to ride on you.” He said, resting on the ropes behind him. WHAT? If I didn’t have the fact that I didn’t want to let him down on my mind, now I have more. I walked away from him, shaking my head from side to side, trying not to look back at him to fast. You got to understand something here, without him early in my career I was nothing. How am I to be the better without him? I turned around, looking at him with what seemed to be a small tear about to run down my face. “Why?” Asking, trying not to cry just yet. “Why quit now when I need you so much?” “You don’t need me. When you can figure that out you will be good.” He said, wiping away a tear that was about to come down his face. I felt so angry now. I wasn’t good or I’d be stronger not only inside but outside. The tear did fall from my eye, going all the way down the curve on my face to the corner of my mouth. “I can’t do this on my own. You told me you’d always be here for me. Don’t back down on your word now.” Feeling the water of tears running down my face now as I stood there in front of him. I didn’t notice how much tears he was crying. All I knew was that he was hurting me. But I didn’t feel any comfort from him. He walked up to me and gave me a small kiss on the forehead before walking away towards the other side of the ring. “Elia, you are my best student. You will make me proud no matter what you do.” He said, going through the ropes. “I must go now.” Turning away from him all I could feel now was deep sorrow. Time to me had stopped and it wasn’t going to start again. My time in the spotlight to me was over and empty. I wiped away the tears that still kept flowing down my face and to my mouth cheek. Clash of the Titans, it was my beginning and my end. I would go in alone and come out the same way. No matter the outcome, the night would not be the same without Uncle Johnson. Lowering my head, I just stood there, letting the tears flow down.
