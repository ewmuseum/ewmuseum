Date: 10/7/04 Brick and Aaron sat in the front row of the theater. On the stage behind two old-style microphones stood Damian, Marcus and Andre. 'OK, Keith, we're ready,' Aaron shouted. Keith ran out onto the stage and slid towards the edge of the stage, grabbing Andre's mike and starting to sing. Keith: Ohhhhhhhhh!! 'Cut, cut!' Brick shouted. 'What's wrong?' 'You slid too close to the edge of the stage. I don't want you sliding off into the audience, breaking your neck.' 'What are you talking about? I wasn't that close.' 'I saw you, Keith.
