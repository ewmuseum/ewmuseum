Consequences and repercussions: the expose.

7 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 7th, 2005. Consequences and repercussions: the expose. ---- 10:18 PM. August 7th. SoHo, NY. ---- The woman sitting across from me was beautiful, there was no other way of putting it. Gorgeous locks of black hair fell across her face, a blonde streak in the front falling across her face, her big green eyes were covered with a pair of black oakleys, as she tried her best to remain inconspicuous. She had even dressed down, to avoid attention, wearing a pair of black snap-up's, Nike Free trainers, and a sleeveless white shirt, devoid of any logos. Her attire was her disguise, an attempt to trick the world into thinking that she wasn't something special. Somehow, it just didn't work. Maybe it was the way the shirt wrapped around her breasts, showing their supple nature and perkiness. Perhaps it was the way the snap-up's squeezed around her perfect buttocks, showing the rest of the world exactly how a woman's rear anatomy should look. Then again, maybe it was just the fact that she was drop dead gorgeous. Try as she might to avoid the attention she still got it nonetheless, that tends to happen to beautiful people, which was a category she most definitely fell into. 'How close are they, Josh' Asked former super-model Casey Branner, who had walked away from modeling at the tender age of 26, a mere two years ago. By they, she did of course mean the police, who would love nothing more than to hear this conversation between Casey and I. 'I have no idea, Case, at this point they're just asking questions but I know they're going to dig deeper into this...' I said, my eyes turning down to the cup of plain, but highly sugared coffee in front of me. I glanced around nervously, hoping that no one was paying attention to us in the small cafe we occupied. For privacy purposes and the sake of making things look as innocent as possible we had decided to hold our meeting in the public eye, all the while maintaining calm and level heads. The public forum, wasn't so public after all; a little all night diner on a sidestreet. The place was named 'Chuck's', and threw out a unique feel too it. It seemed like the kind of place college kids would hit up at 3 AM for a late night meal and a quick break from their rigorous routine, either that or a cup of coffee to battle their raging hangover. The burger joint was fairly empty at this time of the night, occupied by only a waitress, a cook, two men in a booth on the other side of the restaurant, and a ragged looking elderly gentlemen seated at the bar, his head drooping, showing signs of exhaustion. In retrospect, another more crowded venue would have probably served as a better avenue of escape, it seemed that amidst this small congregation Casey and I would attract a lot of attention, due to my attire and the fact that she was the only woman worth looking at in the restaurant. No, she wasn't the only female there, but she was definitely the only one worth looking at when you took the two-hundred and fifty pound waitress named 'Olga' into consideration. As for my eye-catching attire? I too, had tried to dress down for the occasion and failed miserably. As it worked out, the least noticeable attire I had in my wardrobe was still enough to catch attention. Faded A&F jeans with a green Subseven t-shirt, the bands name plastered across the front and yellow arrows on the right shoulder. As can be expected, I wore my trademark Chuck Taylor's, black hi-tops for tonight, and topped the look off with a black baseball cap, flipped backwards, my multi-colored, black, blue and silver locks peeking out from beneath. 'How deep?', Casey asked, concern etched across her face. 'As deep as they can get.' I shrugged. 'Or as deep as we'll let them get.' 'Have we left any tracks, at this point?', she thoughtfully posed as she took a sip of coffee. 'Nothing, that I can see.' I said, shaking my head in doubt. 'The weapon?' She asked. 'Completely gone.' I smiled confidently. 'Gone? It had better be impossible to link the weapon Josh, it had Howie's blood on it, that would screw us over right there.' Her face showed signs of worry as she spoke. 'Hey, its alright....' I smiled, reaching across the table to take her soft hand into mine. 'I took care of it, okay?' She glanced at me through her shades, and somehow behind the tinted lenses I saw eyes full of bewilderment and concern as she spoke. 'How was it taken care of?' 'I burnt it.' My smile was full of pride as I spoke. 'So much for any blood or fingerprints linking me to the weapon.' 'Thank God, I was worried you just tossed it into a back alley somewhere.' Casey breathed a sigh of relief, even allowing herself to smile as she looked into my eyes. 'So Josh, tell me, why are you so cool about things? I'm freakin' out and you've already had two altercations with the cops, whats your secret?' She inquired, her hand playing with mine. I shrugged. 'It isn't really that big of a deal. When you've been suplexed on C4, driven through tables wrapped in barbwire, and had nutjobs trying to cash in on a hundred thousand dollar bounty some freak put on your head...that will generally translate into nerves of steel.' 'Are you ever nervous when dealing with the cops? Casey asked me, her fingers interlocking with mine. 'A little, but not so much for my own sake, for your sake.' I stated sincerely. 'Aww, that is so sweet.' She smiled, leaning across the table to give me a peck on the cheek. 'Casey, you aren't like the other girls I've dated.' I sighed softly as I stared at her beautiful face. 'The others have all...wanted something. They wanted to get into the Wrestling industry or be a star, something like that, and you? You don't, you've already met fame.' 'And ran away from it.' She said, her face maintaining a beautiful smile. 'I'm really getting into you Case...' I began, but stopped as I leaned in and our lips met in a gentle kiss. My eyes closed, as I brought one hand up to her face, pulling her in closer. My stunning partner in crime didn't resist in the least, choosing to reciprocate the kiss instead as I slid my tongue into her mouth, slipping my tongue around her mouth I felt a warm surge of joy flowing through my body, my heart beating faster with excitement. As I grew even further entranced by her sweet embrace I was suddenly shocked by the flash of a camera, which was only visible to me as a flash of light through my closed eyelids. Immediately opening my eyes I glanced around, as a second flash came, this time I pinpointed its source, directly outside of the window we were seated next too. 'Son of...' I began, my eyes wide open in shock. Casey gasped as her eyes opened and she spotted the photographer through the window. He was short and wiry, no taller than myself at 5'9, but weighing in at a thin 160 pounds, a camera dangling around his neck that looked like it weighed more than he did. He wore black jeans and a pair of cheap white tennis shoes, topped off by a plain gray t-shirt and a matching beanie. He looked like a college student, and he probably was. No doubt he worked as a photgrapher, freelance most lilkely, and looked to score several hundred bucks from his two quick snapshots. He snapped another one off as I stared at him in surprise, and then another. 'Josh...' Casey began, looking up at me, fear in her voice. I needed no further encouragement to take action, I was already on my way as I slid out of the booth and bolted for the door. The very second my feet had hit the floor running, so had his on the sidewalk outside. His shoes showing that they didn't have to be Nikes to be fast. I shoved the glass door open roughly and stepped out into the night air, a gentle breeze swirling about. Turning my eyes down the street in the direction which he had ran I spotted his bobbing figure at least twenty yards ahead of me. I hated running people down, but hey, I wasn't offered several track scholarships to numerous top schools for nothing and it was time to put that to good use. I leapt into action, my Chuck's slapping the pavement of the sidewalk furiously, my legs pumping as I raced down the sidewalk in an attempt to close the gap between the photographer and I. He was fast, but I knew that I was much faster, after all I wasn't known as the fastest man in Global for no reason. He glanced over his shoulder, foolish choice, that caused him to slow down ever so slightly giving me an even better opportunity to close the gap between us, turning it into an estimated fifteen yards. I could hear the gasps from his lungs, he didn't seem to be in the best shape, probably a smoker from the sound of things. My feet pounded the sidewalk as I watched the camera around his neck swing back and forth violently, reaching down with his right hand he grabbed it to contain it's motion, by doing so her had slowed down ever so slightly, giving me an even better opportunity to close the gap to an easy ten yards. I was suddenly reminded of my senior year in high school, where I, team captain and starting strong safety chased down an opposing wide reciever from twenty yards back and forced a fumble, giving our team a chance to pick it up and hold on for the win. This situation was almost similiar, I was going to force this kid to fumble his camera and of course, pick it up for my own team. He glanced back again, big mistake, I was now less than five yards behind him and closing quickly. He seemed to sense what was coming, as he let out a gasp of worried anticipation...then I hit him. I leapt into the air, in my minds eyes, everything paused for a moment, rolling off of an imaginary film reel in slow motion. I was in the air, flying...like superman... He turned, his face was full of fear... He screamed, his eyes widening, both hands on the camera to protect it... And suddenly we came back to real time as my body slammed violently into his from the side, in a tackle that would have made Vince Lombardi proud. As hard as I hit him didn't even compare to the impact of his body being driven into the concrete below. His figure crashed onto the pavement, a scream emitting from his lungs, accompanied by the sound of tearing clothes, the camera lense shattering and the sick crunch of breaking bones. His beanie had fallen away, and I grabbed a handful of stringy blonde hair, putting one knee into his spine I leaned down and lifted his head up. 'Give me the camera.' My voice wasn't playful, he knew I meant business. 'I'm sorry man, please...' He begged, tears in his eyes. 'Just give me the camera and no one gets hurt...any worse.' I sneered at him, his quivering mass weak beneath my body. 'Okay.' He sighed weakly, in obvious pain. 'Take it. Take it and leave me alone.' Snatching the camera away from him I shook my head. 'No.' Placing my face next to him I snarled violently, his body shaking in a combination of pain and fear. 'YOU leave me alone.' Standing to my feet I shoved his body over, so that he was on his back, looking down into his eyes I warned him sternly. 'And leave Casey alone too.' With the camera in my hands, I smiled, I had exactly what I wanted. Without so much as a second thought to the battered cameraman I used the strap to toss the broken camera over my shoulder and turned my back, walking away. It was safe to say he had learned his lesson. ...the hard way.
