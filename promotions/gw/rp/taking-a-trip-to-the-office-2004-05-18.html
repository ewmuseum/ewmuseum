Taking A Trip To The Office

18 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 18 The scene opened up to a building, I was walking down the lobby of the building with my newly won Cruiserweight Title draped over my shoulder. It was shining because at the very top of the building, there was a sun roof so the sun was blazing down onto the people that were beside me. I walked past a few people and saw that they were staring at the title that was hanging off of my shoulder. I took my hand and cleaned it a bit and kept walking. I saw two elevators that were at the very end of the wall, and started walking over to one. Why I wasn't at home, god only knows. I just felt I had to come here and tell her something. 'Hey, what you up to.' a voice said. I turned around quickly and saw my best friend standing there, Jesse Jamester. He was wearing some blue jeans with a Nike shirt on and a pair of white Nike shoes. he walked over to me with a smile, but he wasn't looking at me, he was looknig at the title I had just won over Deacon Holmes. 'Congratulations on your win over Deacon, that was some pretty good wrestling I saw.' he said. 'Where did you watch me?' I asked. 'I was right here in my VIP lounge watching it on my big screen teevision.' he replied. 'You have a VIP lounge here? I don't even have one and I'm the Cruiserweight champion. Well I guess I dont really need a VIP lounge, I have a house like 2 blocks away from here. Hey, I might come over to your room in like 15 minutes, I gotta tell someone somethnig.' I said. 'Ok.' He replied. He walked away from me and went straight to the stairs that led to the upper levels. I walked into the opposite direction and went straight for the elevator. I push the button to go up and the little button turned orange. I few seconds later the elevator opened and I walked in. There was a butler that pressed the button for you, and there was also another man in the very corner saying something to himself. I turned to the butler and said. 'Could you please press to goto level 7?' I asked. 'Certainaly.' he said. He pressed the number 7 button as I waited, we were on main, and I had already saw that this man had to get off at level 6. It got to 3, 4, 5, and then six as the man looked up at what he was doing. He smiled at the butler and walked out of the elevator. I waited as it changed to the level 7. I walked out of the elevator and smiled at the butler, he smiled back as the elevator door's closed. I was left standing in a empty hallway, there were lots of rooms on each side of the hallway. I saw at the very end of the hallway was a big brown door that had a gold name plate on it. I couldn't really reconize what the nameplate said, So I just started walking towards it. 'Man this place is huge.' I said to myself. I got to the door and looked at the nameplate. It was the lady I was supposed to talk to. I knocked on the door 2 times and after the second one the door opened and there was a very long table, and at the end of it sitting on a big black leather chair, was Christina McCoy! I looked at her as she smiled at me. 'Very pleased to meet you Mr. Roswell. Very impressive win over Deacon Holmes on Evolution. I was very pleased with the performance you put on against him.' he said to me in a very high voice. 'Thanks.' was the only answer I could say at this moment. 'Now, what was it you wanted to see me for again?' she asked. 'Oh yeah, I just wanted to say thank you to you, because after all, your the boos and you put me in this match, and it was a match I had to redeem myself, and I guess I did because if it wasn't for you, then this title wouldn't be on my shoulder right now.' I said. I looked at my title and smiled, this was the very first time I had won the Cruiserweight title, and I really think it was the best match I've had all of this year. The only other title I had was the Primestar title, but that didn't last too long, I lost it the Evolution I won it. Man, you wouldn't even feel how I felt after I lost that title, only one week after winning it. 'Well, your welcome. I put you in this match because I knew it was your time to shine, I knew that if i put you in this match, that you would give it all that you had gotten, and I think I have just seen that from you. Oh, and just in case you didn't know, your booked against Rich Sherwin for his newly won Television Championship, and that's next Evolution.' she said. I stared at her. I did not believe the words that had just came out of her mouth. I have just won the Cruiserweight title, and now she has put me in a match, for another title, and this one being the Television title, I could not believe it. I was so happy, if I won that match then I would be a double champion, and that's never happened to me before. 'Oh my god, thank you so much, I will not let you down in this match.' I said. 'Oh I know you won't. Actually Max, you have a pretty good chance at winning this.' she said to me. I smiled at her and walked out of the room. Could someones life be any more better than mine, well obviously not because as of right now, I am a champion, and I can do whatever I want, whenever I want. I walked down the hallway, but I had totally forgotten about Jamester. I was supposed to goto his VIP lounge and check out what he had in there.One problem, I didn't know what floor it was on or which door it was. I walked past an office with a lady sitting at her desk. I stopped and turned to her. 'Hello, could you please tell me where Jesse Jamester's VIP lounge is, and what floor it is on?' I asked her politely. 'Ah, yes. It's on floor 5 and its the very first room you see after coming out of the elevator.' she said back. 'Thank you.' I said. I started walknig again to the elevator. I pushed the button, but this time the one that went to the lower levels. It opened quicker than when I was going up. I walked into it, but I saw the same butler standing there by the panel on the wall that had all of the level buttons on it. 'Could you please take me to level 5?' I asked him. 'Sure thing.' he said. He pressed the button that had the 5 on it. This time there was no one in the elevator but me and the butler. It went down to level 5 and I walked out. I smiled at the butler yet again but this time, he looked at me like I was an ex-convict. I didn't look back. I walked to the very first room I saw, it had a special gold name plate on it that read, Jesse Jamester. I knocked on the door and Jesse opened it. I walked inside and sat down on the couch. Scene Fades
