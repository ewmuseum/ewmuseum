Running mad.

6 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 6th, 2004. I let out a soft sigh as I set my pen down. My eyes traveled across the paper and the words I had written down. Words of madness? Perhaps. Words of wisdom? Probably not, but they were soothing words nonetheless. Soothing to me at least, I couldn't think of another person that could possibly enjoy them but for me they just seemed to calm me. I scanned the white piece of paper, as my scribbles in blue ink were a bit scrunched together and messy yet readable and calming…at least to me. Poetry had always been there for me so it was no surprise that in one of the darkest hours of my life I would return too it. It had been my friend when all others had forsaken me, my comfort when nothing but pain lurked. Therefore it was no big surprise that I decided to wax poetic in this dark moment of my life. Looking down at the sheet of paper I couldn't help but give a half smile, sure the half-smile was my attempt at bravery after being crushed but it was all I could manage. To say that I was doing things half hearted would be an accurate description because with my heart broken into shards, half was probably all that remained. I stretched my legs out onto the glass coffee table in front of me as I sought for maximum comfort after all I had been through. Sitting here on my leather couch in my cozy apartment I was seeking a combination of three things. Number one, solitude. I just wanted to be alone. I wanted to be alone for numerous reasons. To think, to relax, to calm down, and to attempt to make peace with the internal demons that haunted my poor soul. Number two, comfort. Being comfortable was high on my list of priorities right now. Sitting here wearing only a pair of black gym shorts and a white Tanktop I had achieved physical comfort for the most part. My head was still throbbing from my recent encounter with the sidewalk and I bore a Band-Aid over the left side of my upper forehead. Luckily my shaggy hair covered it to the point where it was unnoticeable. Number three, to gather my wits about me. I had a lot of important things on my itinerary and I couldn't afford to throw those things away. My beloved Cruiserweight Championship was on the line and my spot in the International tournament as well. Those were both things that I simply couldn't afford to let slip through my fingers because of my personal difficulties. It was strange…the poetry I had written had a gripping effect on me. Perhaps it was due to the fact it portrayed everything I was feeling right now. Well…perhaps not everything but almost. Turning my eyes back to the paper I began to read the words I had scribbled into the notebook. There's a monster, screaming in my ear... It reminds me of my fear... It screams about my emotional pain... And how my life is slipping down the drain... It screams at me for my imperfection... It laughs at my misdirection... I don't know what this monster wants from me... I don't know what this monster is trying to see... It drags up issues from my past... Mocks me for being an iconoclast... It sneers at my convictions... Shatters my life's predictions... It's holding my heart in a vice... Jeering at me for being too nice... It shouts at me in an angry tone... It cuts and bruises me down to the bone... It's trying to force me to grow colder... This monster is locked onto my shoulder... It feeds me false hope and lies... It silences me with its disturbing cries... I pray for the day it dies... But until then it's the only thing...keeping me alive. My poetry was an introspective mess of personal feelings and a violent rush of confusion. I had to admit that even I was beginning to question my sanity. My poetry wouldn't really be understood or even accepted by anyone else. It was the type of writing that only the author understands. No one that read it would understand who the monster was. They wouldn't realize that the monster was a combination of things built into one roaring multi-headed demon. The monster bore many heads…the head of society, pressure, relationship problems, personal issues, and social difficulty. He only seemed to grow more heads and scream louder as the days wore on. Letting out a sigh of utter frustration I ripped the sheet of paper from my notebook. In one quick motion I crumpled it into a ball and hurled it away from me. My eyes didn't follow its landing as it inaudibly crashed onto my blue carpet. Pulling my legs off of the glass coffee table that sat before me I stood to my full height of five feet and ten inches. I blinked in an exhausted state; a state brought on by insomnia and the roaring of personal demons. Turning away from the couch I walked towards the bathroom. Grabbing the knob I gave it a tight wrench as the door opened. Stepping inside I flipped the light on and closed the door behind me. My eyes didn't focus on the plain white walled, undecorated bathroom but instead went straight to the mirror. The mirror was where I looked into the bloodshot eyes of a possessed young man. Gazing back into those bloodshot eyes I saw the bluish gray mixed with an unusual combination of red. This was brought on by my lack of sleep in the past forty-eight hours. I just couldn't sleep…no matter what I couldn't. I didn't understand why sleep eluded me but it ran away from me every time I chased it. Perhaps it was God's way of forcing me to confront my issues instead of running away from them again. Because even I had to admit…I was running mad. Reaching down to the sink I turned on the cold water and let it run over my hands. As it flowed across my palms I slapped my hands to my face wiping my face and eyes. I was so exhausted this seemed to be the only thing to do as I rubbed at my eyes before turning off the faucet. I hung my head slightly as I still gazed into the reflection of the haunted man before me. His shaggy black hair eclipsed the small bandage that he had merited when he decided to chase the woman of his dreams. This man truly was troubled…but this man was I. I wasn't sure if I could love myself but I knew that I certainly wasn't far off from hating myself. Exhaling heavily my head drooped in exhaustion as my eyes closed painfully. I was running…away from my fears but they were catching up to me. I was running…away from my commitments yet they entrapped me. I was simply running mad. But sometimes…it's ok to run mad, just as long…as you don't faint.
