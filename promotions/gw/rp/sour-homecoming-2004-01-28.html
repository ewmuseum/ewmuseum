Sour Homecoming

28 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/28/04 Wincing his eyes closed as the sun peaks through the heavy cloud cover, Johnie begins to stir and brushes his face against the headrest. He exhales loudly as he attempts to readjust himself to get comfortable enough to fall back asleep. Its been a long night and a long trip on top of that. For a second or two, he’s actually glad that he doesn’t have a license anymore. But after that split second, he’s reminded of the downsides to being revoked. The lack of freedom. The humility. The guilt of asking a friend for a ride. And worst of all, having to deal with community service. Johnie’s upper lip curls in a snarl as his eyelids lift slightly in a mock stare out the truck’s window. Seeing beyond the lack of vegetation and environment, he sees the lack of life for the elderly. Soon he’ll have to sit on some of them hand and foot for sixty hours. Restlessly, he pulls his head away from the polyester seat with a Velcro rip vaguely audible as his three day stubble didn’t want to let go. Twisting himself over, Johnie turns to face the driver’s side and leans over against the seat again. With his eyes open slightly, he glances over as the chueffer for the day has his attention on the road. “Tough time sleeping, Johnie?” Trey mutters as a cocky grin sneaks across him as he turns to look at his drowsy friend. “You’ve been out for a little while at least. Sometimes I can’t see how you sleep at all during the day.” Softly closing his eyes again, Johnie’s grimace turns into a light smile as he attempts to lull back into dreamland. “It’s a talent you see,” he mumbles with a heavy breath. Attempting to curl up for comfort, he rolls his legs against the lower dash for support. “You could get there with practice if you really wanted to.” Trey lets out a light laugh as he switches his attention back and forth from Johnie and the road. “Sure, whatever bro. Just make sure that you don’t teach me how to sleep like that. It looks like shit from here. How did you get so tired last night” There couldn’t have been all that much to do in that shit city.” A heavy grin comes across Johnie’s face, yet its covered up by most of the armrest beside him. It was an eventful night, but it’ll probably be one that Trey won’t know about. He may be his best friend, but some things are better left unsaid. “Nothing really. It was just a long few days being locked up and all. Plus that bullshit with the judge kinda made me a little restless. I’m just glad to be outta there.” “No shit. I can’t believe that pig fucked you over like that. You weren’t doing nothing and yet he halls you in for a DUI.” Gradually raising his inflection as he goes along, Trey begins to frown heavily as his lips begin to sink into his mouth. His face would probably turn a little red if it weren’t black already. “Fucking bullshit!” “Easy killer,” Johnie says as he lifts his head up to glance at him. Trey lets out a heavy sigh as he shakes his head slightly. “Its over and done with now. Yes, it was a fuckjob. We know this and we can’t change shit about it either. I wish we could though. I didn’t do jack, they know it too.” “They just wanted the fucking money for their shitty ass town!” interrupts Trey again as he vents further. “Probably.” Johnie raises his tone with a little more stern to maybe calm his friend down. “But its stupid to get all pissed about it now. Shit. I just wanna forget about it, if anything.” Shrugging back down into the armrest, Johnie forces his eyes closed as he tries to get some more rest. Even though he knows that rest will not come to him. He’s already riled up and awake. But the strange thing about it is that just a couple days ago, he was saying the same things as Trey. Venting with the same words and arguing the same points. He knew he got screwed over. Is he accepting it? Or is there something deeper? Trey shrugs to himself as he stares out at the road before him. “Sorry about that man. I just...” “I know,” he fires back gruffly as he struggles to speak through the phlegm gathering up in his throat. Hacking heavily twice, he gathers a chunk of it up and holds it in his mouth. Sitting up quickly, Johnie pushes the button allowing his window to scroll down halfway. He rears his neck back and shoves it forward as the wad of saliva shoots out of the vehicle. “Well that’s healthy.” Trey spouts cockily. “Helps keep me regular,” Johnie replies with a grin as he wipes the bottom of his chin for any leftovers in his black soul patch. “Whatever works for you.” Trey smiles back a little as he turns his head away from him again. But the smile goes away rather quickly as he continues. “Anyway, we should be back in town pretty soon.” “Kick ass. What do we have lined up for tonight then” I’d wager that there’ll be some sort of “Welcome Back, Johnie” festival back at the pad tonight.” He lets out a good laugh as he goes over the possibilities in his head on what he has to look forward to in a few short hours. Babes, beer, and blowjobs as it might go. Just the usual. But as Johnie turns his head back to Trey, he doesn’t seemed as jazzed. “Actually, something’s happened.” Trey stares down at the yellow lines as they whiz by, trying not to turn away. Johnie’s face turns to a frown rather quickly as he waits for Trey to finish. “Uhhh...while you were gone, the landlord got wind that you had moved in and he wasn’t too happy about it.” “What’s so wrong with me moving in?” Feeling the stress coming on, Johnie reaches into his pocket and readies a cigarette for lighting. “You said he’d be all down with it. What’s changed?” “He would have been down with it. Originally anyway.” Trey shifts back and forth between Johnie and the road. One hand on the wheel, one hand in movement as he tries to describe. “But the deal was that with you moving in, that is gonna change the lease around. And he doesn’t like that. He also didn’t like that we did it without talking with him about it first.” Johnie tilts his hand over the tip of the cigarette as his strikes his zippo. Sucking the air back through the cancer stick, the end lights up to mask the darkness of his hand. “What’s the fucking difference?” he blurts out as he exhales the first puff. He lets the window roll down a little bit to allow the smoke to change direction and float its way out of the truck. “I had a lack of options, you let me in for just temporary at least, and I’d be paying some of the rent while I was there.” “I told him that.” “And?” Trey bites down on his bottom lip as he turns he head opposite of Johnie for a second. Frustration’s getting to him. Or else it was already there. “And he didn’t care. It violates the lease and is grounds for eviction. Even if we did get clearance, rent was gonna be bumped up with another tenant.” Johnie takes a heavy drag and hits the dashboard in front of him, but as he hits it, the glove compartment pops open. An old quirk by the previous owner maybe. Either way, he turns his left hand down and slams it shut again. “That motherfucker...” Lifting his chin up, Trey looks forward with a heavy face. “And besides, he said he was gonna evict me anyway...” “What?” Choking himself back a little, he continues. “I’m a couple months behind in the rent.” Johnie’s face turns to a darker shade of red as his brow creases far enough to touch his nose. “What the fuck man” How could you get behind on rent? Shit...” Rolling his neck back, Johnie leans against his passenger seat and sighs. He raises his curled right hand to his forehead, scratching it a bit. Still with the Camel in the hand, he lets it roll down and take a drag. Trey turns his head back to driving and lets out a soft regret. “I’m sorry man.” Staring forward, Johnie doesn’t hear Trey’s apology. He doesn’t hear the wind whipping from the open window either. Not the truck. Not the soft beat coming from the radio. Just the thoughts inside his head. His brow bends slightly as his eyes grow dark and empty. Slowly, his mouth curves into a heavy pucker. Silence begins to get to Trey as he periodically looks at Johnie. Finally he grows wearily of the stillness and raises a question. “Johnie?” In response, Johnie raises his head solemnly. Letting another puff of smoke filter out, he stares forward and sees the city coming up ahead. Time is up. “Don’t worry about it, Trey. I’ll talk to the landlord tonight.” “You sure about that?” “I’ll handle him. You just get that party ready for me when I get back. It’ll be all cool by then.” Johnie gives Trey a light nod as he takes one last drag from his cigarette. Looking back at the city he sees the sun hitting against the large mountains jutting out from the ground, reflecting the light to all the city. Yet right before him rolls up the city limits sign greeting him as they pass by. “Taos: Where the Enchanted Circle Begins” Johnie lets out a soft chuckle as he throws his cigarette out the window and it rolls off to the edge of the road. Welcome back, Johnie...
