Creating The Crisis (Chapter 4: Vivica J. Valentine {Part 1})

7 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/07/05 Date: October 19, 2002 Time: 23:00 Central Time Location: Chicago, Illinois A chill wind passed over the twilight, a series of clouds temporarily covered the moon as if they were cars going by a toll both in the sky. Underneath the stars, the blackened streets of Chicago were covered with the oranges, yellows, and reds of dying leaves. All it took was one shrill wind to bring the leaves to their uneventful death... so ironic when you think about the nurturing it takes to bring a tree to bloom. A car passes by throwing all the dead leaves high into the air, where the wind catches them and they swirl around in a cyclone in the middle of the street. As the wind dies down the leaves fall back down to their eventually demise on the sidewalk in front of an old pawn shop. THUMP, THUMP THUMP, THUMP THUMP, THUMP Suddenly the leaves were crushed under the march of combat boots going down the sidewalk. The group of three known as 'The Cru' continued their usual match down the street... making sure no one was up to something seemed to be a full-time job these days. There was always some sleeve bag who thought he owned the place... it was tough to keep the peace when someone always wanted something they didn't have. The leader of the group stopped underneath a white street light, giving him an unusual angelic property. He had shaggy blonde hair and wore a black Adidas jacket over a white 'wife-beater' tank top. A pair of black jeans completed the look as he surveyed his surroundings... making sure no one was lurking in the shadows ready to pounce. 'Let's go, there's no one here.' He said to the girl and the large African-American man behind him. The girl wore a light pink zip up sweat jacket and a pair of blue jeans, her blonde hair over her brilliant blue eyes as she looked at the sidewalk as they moved along. Her hands stuffed deep into the pink pockets of her jacket. The style of life was starting to wear her down... sometimes she wanted so bad to take the first bus back to Highland Park, and leave everything behind. But she couldn't... she couldn't leave Jimmy hanging like that. After all she was homeless and alone until he took her in, the least she could do to repay him was keep him company. Plus she couldn't deny the fact she felt safe around Jimmy and Darius. 'Is everything okay Vivica?' James 'Jimmy' Carlisle said as he stopped and looked at the young teenage girl. She looked up at him and managed to muster a smile. 'Just a lot of thinking... it's nothing, just sometimes I miss...' Vivica was interrupted by a loud explosion. Suddenly Darius fall limply forward to the ground... 'What the fuck?!' Jimmy screamed as blood trickled out of Darius' body on the Chicago pavement. Vivica's eyes grew wide in horror as she stared down at Darius. He body tensed up, she couldn't move as her heart raced. 'RUN VIVICA!' Jimmy screamed at the top of his lungs before he pulled out a gun from his jacket and fired three shots into the air, trying to scare off any rival gang members or snipers who might've done this. But Vivica was in a state of panic, the only thing she could hear was her own heartbeat pumping loudly in her ears. BUM BUM, BUM BUM BUM BUM, BUM BUM BUM BUM, BUM BUM Suddenly the pumping of her racing heart was interrupted by another explosion in the darkness. She could see the bullet whizzing towards her in slow-motion as if she was stuck in the matrix. Her life started to flash before her eyes as the bullet grew near. Her eyes looked like a deer stuck in headlights before suddenly James Carlisle jumps in front of her, and the bullet strikes him in the heart. Jimmy lands next to Darius on the ground clinching his chest and coughing up blood... 'JIMMY!!!!!' Vivica shrieked in horror at the sight of James Carlisle’s eyes rolling back into his head. Once again in a panic tears started streaming down her face like a waterfall. '...leave...' was the last word Jimmy could manage to get out before his body grew limp on the ground. Suddenly Vivica took off in a mad sprint, tears raced down her face clouding her vision but it didn't matter. She'd never ran so fast before in her entire life. Another shot was fired and Vivica squealed in panic... BUM BUM, BUM BUM, BUM BUM, BUM BUM BUM BUM, BUM BUM, BUM BUM, BUM BUM BUM BUM, BUM BUM, BUM BUM, BUM BUM The bullet stuck the building behind Vivica and lodged itself into the brick and mortar. Shrapnel shot out at Valentine as she sprinted around the corner. She raced blindly into the darkness of the alley, through her blurry vision she could only see one thing... a fire escape leading up the side of a hotel. She wiped her eyes before taking a leap of faith into the air towards the wall of the hotel, and the thrusted off towards the fire escape. She barely caught the bar with her left hand hung there, trying to catch her thoughts. Hyperventilating and crying hysterically, it took her last burst of strength to climb the ladder and lay on the fire escape on her back, looking up at the stars wishing it would all go away... ---- OCTOBER 20, 2002 ANOTHER ASSASSINATION RATTLES CHICAGO RESIDENTS Early this morning, a local woman was horrified to find the body of 2 young men on her doorstop. Seemingly they were shot dead at approximately 11:00pm last night. A single shot to the head was the cause of death for both men, which appeared to be shot from a rooftop above. Darius Germaine Mack, 28, or Chicago and James Kennedy Carlisle, 25, of Chicago were the victims. Carlisle was is rumored to be a major player in the local underground scene. Everything points to the shootings being gang related, but the police have other options open as well. 'This homicide is still under investigation but it looks almost identical to the string of assassin-like murders that have occured over the last year.' Officer Jeremy Young said, 'Our sources have told us that the word on the street say that this is the work of Die Erntemaschine.' Die Erntemaschine has been a hot topic as of late. The name is German for 'The Reaper.' A fitting name as none of Erntemaschines's victims have escaped nor seen the assassin's face. The age, gender, and nationality of Die Erntemaschine are also unknown, leaving law enforcment grasping for leads. The tales of this ruthless assassin has shaken local Chicago residents. A recent poll of the area of the homicides has shown a 20% jump in vacany in local hotel establishments and a staggering 24% jump in residents leaving the area. This news obviously puts many local businesses in jeopardy of going under. Such statistics have obviously concerned Chicago Police, who have put Die Erntemaschine as #1 on their Most Wanted List. ASSASSINATION Continued on A5 'I can't read anymore.' The woman side aloud as she put the paper down and began to investigate her surroundings. It was the first time she'd been alone since she'd first ran to Chicago. It was unfamiliar, and to be honest with herself it was quite frightening. The clamoring around the busy diner helped her ease her nerves a little bit and made her feel a little more at home. Her cerulean blue eyes seemed a little bloodshot and heavy makeup hid the bags that had begun to collect under her eyes. She hadn't slept a wink since last night... the body of Jimmy Carlisle was still fresh in her mind. There had to have been something she could've done... 'Miss?' The sound of the woman's voice rattled her as she looked up at the lady, holding a plate of waffles, 'Is something wrong?' 'No...' She responded quietly to the waitress, but her actions spoke a different story, 'I'm just tired... that's all.' The waitress didn't ask any questions before laying the waffles down on the table and walking away. Her blonde hair reflected the sunlight which came through the glass window next to her. She knew a few men around the diner were gawking at her, but she has more important things on her mind while she stabbed her knife into the spongy waffles in front of her. As she picked the waffle piece up off the plate to stick in in her mouth, she felt a hand touch on her right shoulder. Her eyes grew wide as she forced down her food only partcially chewed. She had been shot at earlier... how in the world could she be so oblivious? The end was lamented into her mind as she waited to her what the mysterious stranger had to say. 'Are you Vivica Valentine?' A man with a deep voice asked of her, she was still too shaken to turn around, but replied anyway. '...that depends on whose asking?' She replied. Her eyes looked around the diner which had grown very uncomfortable and silent. Every pair of eyes in the place were focused on her and the man that stood behind her. 'Chicago Police Department.' The man replied, 'My name is Officer Roger Cantor. I have some questions to ask you Miss Valentine. Could you please step outside the diner with me?' 'Yeah... I suppose so.' She said under her breath as she stood up and her way towards the door. The Chicago Police Officer kept his hand on her shoulder while they navigated through the crowded diner. Vivica looked on as one of the waitresses turned to the other and started whispering comments back and forth between shooting dirty looks her way. After opening the door Vivica was blasted with a cold Chicago morning breeze. Her blonde hair blew over her face like a painting of a siren over the sea. Crossing her arms overtop her pink sweat jacket, the cool fall weather made her pull her pink hood up over her head. The officer followed her quietly around to the back of the mobile style diner, where they should have some privacy for their little talk. It was hard to look the police officer in the eyes. She couldn't help but feel somewhat responsible for what had happened, even though she never fired a single shot before in her life. She couldn't help but think that she should've saved Jimmy's life... she couldn't help but think he would've saved her. They came to a stop near a dumpster around the back fo the establishment. The back alleys of Chicago looked so different during the day. Vivica couldn't help but notice that Chicago seemed to turn into a very different place when the sun went down. The officer tried to maintain eye contact, but it was useless... she just couldn't seem to look him in the eye. 'I take it you know why I wanted to talk to you.' Officer Cantor said, looking at the beautiful young Vivica J. Valentine. She was only 18 but she seemed so much older. The time away from home really forced her to mature. 'Yeah I do... but I didn't do it. You know that right?' Vivica said, shooting a glance up at the officer who looked back at her. 'I know.' The officer replied, 'But I have 3 people who have placed you at the scene of the crime that night. All 3 people said you were almost killed. That gives me reason to believe that you were supposed to die last night Vivica. But you got away, and now you need to tell me everything you know about what happened.' 'I wish I could.' Vivica replied, 'But I really don't even know what happened. We were just walking and all of a sudden I heard shots. Next thing I know Jimmy and Darius were on the ground, Jimmy told me to run and I heard a shot. I just kept running... hoping I didn't die. I don't know who in the hell it was or what they wanted. I just knew that if I didn't run... I'd die too.' 'Did you see the murderer?' The officer asked her point blank. 'No, I just ran for my life. And then cried like a baby if you want to know the details. I havn't slept a wink and I've been walking around town for hours, just trying to clear my head. I don't know a damned thing...' Vivica explained before continuing on, 'You probably know more than I do.' 'I still need your help.' The officer explained, 'I need to ask you a question. Do you know anyone who works for Zenith Corporation Insurance Company? Or does business with Zenith Corporation Insurance company?' 'No...' Vivica paused after replying. Everything started to fall into place... the mysterious murders started happening around the time that the new kid Christian Zenith started working the underground scene. Every person assassinated was a major player. Zenith was a well known weapons supplier and could easily do all the killings. Suddenly a flicker erupted in Vivica's mind, she tried hard to contain the conclusion she'd just arrived with. 'Okay, well here is my number.' The officer said before handing her a card, 'If you need to talk to anyone or find anything out I need you to contact me immediately. This is important okay Vivica? You're the closest thing we have to getting this psycho out of the general public.' 'Yeah...' Vivica said, 'I'll keep this in a safe place.' 'Have a nice day Miss Valentine,' The officer stated with a smile, 'Oh... and get some sleep, you look like you need it.' Officer Cantor walked away silently after those words, leaving Vivica to ponder on her thoughts. She could've easily tipped him off on Christian... but the bastard was the one who killed Jimmy. She couldn't let him get off by going to jail, this was something she had to handle herself. If anyone could get inside Zenith's little circle and kill him like he killed her new family... it was her. A smile crossed Vivica's face for the first time all day long as she walked away from the diner. A few questions and a couple tough answers later... she'd make it to Christian Zenith in no time. Then she'd have to prove herself, but she'd been waiting all day to let out her frusteration. 'That bastard...' She said, a new spark returning to her eyes as she walked through the back alley towards the street. It was only a matter of time until she had her revenge... she just needed to play her cards right. ---- Date: October 21, 2002 Time: 1:42 Central Time City: Chicago, Illinois The stars glistened in the dark night above. A half moon shone down on the streets of Chicago, seemingly asleep from a busy day of work. A shadow struck the side of a large building... the glass door read Zenith Corporation. As the shadow moved by, it was apparent that this wasn't the only one. Behind them marched dozens of people, moving silently through the night of the slumbering city. Lurking in the shadows, crowding around a dumpster behind the building. They talked amoungst themselves as they waited in the darkness. In the crowd, a beautiful blonde tucked herself inside of a pink sweat jacket. She tried not to bring attention to herself, but found it increasingly difficult not to in the sea of all black clothing. Her cerulean eyes seemed to glow underneath the moonlight, while she fixed her attention on a dumpster in the corner behind Zenith Corporation... where the group seemed to be facing, waiting for their leader. Suddenly the small crowd began to cheer, and the cheers made her scowl towards the dumpster in disapproval. She watched silently as a man in a custom made black suit climbed atop his make-shift thrown. His eyes, icy and cold navigated across the crowd as he regain his composure. Adjusting his black tie before raising his arms into the air... silencing his followers. 'Welcome everyone,' Christian Zenith spoke as everyone gazed upon him. Even Vivica consumed with hatred couldn't be blind towards the charisma the man had. It was natural to be drawn to him... but she had other plans, 'I'd like to welcome you all to the rest of your life.' 'I wanted to start you all out tonight with a fairy tale I learned when I was a very young boy. Not too long ago... I was a church going man. I found the story of Jesus Christ to be a complex and holy individual, who killed himself for the people. He had walked next to man on earth as the son of God, and felt the temptation of sin. He himself did follow this temptation being the son of God... but he quickly learned that God should not blame the people for caving into this temptation. It was a strong force that everyone felt, it was a strong force that could not be ignored by a mere mortal.' Christian Zenith's cadet blue eyes looked around the crowd below him, 'So Jesus Christ gave himself so that the people of earth could be free of judgement. Not too long ago... I'd tried to read as often as possible about Jesus Christ. I looked up to Jesus Christ and wanted to model my life around a man that everyone could look up to... a martyr.' 'But soon I learned after several altercations...' Christian Zenith paused, 'The death of my parents, and the assassination attempt placed on me. That no matter how much I devoted myself to God... God always turned the other way. Where was God when I had a gun pointed to my head for just being successful? Where was God when my parents were buried because they just couldn't go on with their lives? I ask you all... where was God when you were born into poverty? Where was God when your girlfriend left you to screw some guy why you were out trying to make a living? Where was God when you had to sleep in the cold... just for being born while others got to sleep in their warm homes while they treated everyone else like trash?!' The crowd started to make some noise as Christian took a couple steps across the dumpster lid. Vivica looked up... completely pulled in by Christian's speech. 'That's what I ask everyone, where was God when you needed him? I was there for God when he needed me... and that wasn't good enough. It was then when I realized... if there was such a thing as God, who in the world was he to dictate my life and demand that I bow to him. I've worked too hard in my life to bow down to someone who doesn't even have the time to listen to my voice when I need him the most. If my voice is going to be dead to God... GOD IS DEAD TO ME!' Christian spoke and the crowd applauded. Vivica looked around the crowd, they really felt what Zenith was saying in front of them. It was almost as if they worshipped him... 'As people, we deserve better than to bow our heads like good little boys and girls and expect someone to make everything all better. We have to work together, and make changes and committ ourselves not to God... but to ourselves. I've come to this conclusion after years of asking questions... I'm tired of asked questions, it's time to make answers. Are we supposed to just turn our backs when we're being stepped on? NO. Are we supposed to be discriminated against and take things that are unjust just because we're told? NO. The time to change is now... there may not be a Jesus Christ... but there is a Christ... there is Christian Zenith!' The crowd cheers as Zenith rose his right arm into the air and the rest of the crowd followed, 'God may not have your back but I do... and so do your brothers and sisters who stand around you today. Together we can accomplish things that the bible isn't even able to comprehend! Together we can change the world, and I have the power and the money to make this dream come to a reality. So ladies and gentlemen, grab a friend on the streets and tell them the truth about this world. Standing around waiting for the return of Jesus is waiting too long... why wait for the future when Zenith is right here? You friends deserves better! My brothers and sisters, you are what makes this movement a reality. And the reality is, together we will make our own heaven. Together brothers and sisters... WE WILL RISE ABOVE!' The crowd erupts as Christian Zenith jumps down off the dumpster next to Jason LeMaster and Demitri Miasnikov to join his people down below. They all flock around him as he moves through the crowd with his two closest friends. As he approaches the middle, Vivica J. Valentine makes eye contact with the charismatic leader and begins to move in his direction. Zenith pauses in place as the beautiful girl grows closer. A fire deep inside burns as she moves closer to Jimmy's murderer. She grabs Christian's hand as the crowd around them becomes silent. Christian Zenith stares deep into Vivica's eyes and pulls his hand away from hers. Vivica looks confused as Zenith walks past her. How could a boy just ignore her like that? How rude... and disrespectful. A beautiful lady deserved better than that... why that ungreatful... 'Hey Zenith!' Vivica shouted through the crowd. Christian stopped in his tracks and his worshippers opened up a path between Vivica and Zenith. Slowly, The Midnight Messiah turned around and looked at his beautiful counterpart. 'I want to be a part of your heaven. With me by your side, we can make even more of a difference. I can help you.' Christian looked back at her with a look of disbelief. 'What do you have to offer that makes you an asset to us?' Jason LeMaster responded, 'You're just some valley girl who thinks it's cool to hang out on the streets cause you listen to rap music. So you can go to your school the next morning and tell them you're a thug. You talk like you're something around here when in reality no one even cares that you exist, and you think that you can have the privledge to talk to Christ...' 'Enough Jason, I know who she is.' Christian Zenith interrupted Jason, who looked back at him confused. 'You're the last member of The Kru aren't you? It's a shame what Die Erntemaschine did to James Carlisle, seems you're the only one who survived. If you want to be a part of this following, you have to prove you're something special... um...' 'Vivica.' She replied, 'My name is Vivica Julian Valentine.' 'Right, Vivica.' Christian said looking back at her, 'Let's find out if you're as big of an asset as you think you are. Let's see if Die Erntemaschine not killing you was luck or if it was destiny. Jason, let's see what Vivica is made of before she talks to me.' Jason walked towards Vivica J. Valentine and spit on the ground. LeMaster cracked his neck and looked over at the angel in front of him... 'What a joke...' Jason didn't even finish his sentance before Vivica slammed a swift kick into Jason's stomach and smashed her palm into his nose. Jason stumbled back holding his nose before Vivica punched him with a right hook, kneed him in the stomach, and smashed her elbow on the back of his head sending him to the ground. 'Any questions?' A smile crossed the face of Christian Zenith as Jason LeMaster started cursing into the concrete. Vivica cracked her fingers and looked over at Demitri Miasnikov, 'Want me to do that to the big guy too?' 'No, that's quite alright.' Christian Zenith said, 'Sure... I'll listen to what you have to say, but not tonight. I'm occupied for the rest of the night. Demitri, give Miss Valentine my card... I'll meet with her in my office tomorrow afternoon. If you want to make a difference you better show up Miss Valentine. I'll gaurentee you can't do that to me.' 'Thank you Christian.' She said taking the business card out of Demitri's hand and walking away from the crowd whose jaws remained on the ground from her rather impressive display. Vivica bit her lip and glared back and Christian Zenith who had turned his back on her, 'As for not doing that to you Zenith... we'll see about that.' TO BE CONTINUED...
