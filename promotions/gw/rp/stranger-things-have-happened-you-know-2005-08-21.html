Stranger things have happened you know

21 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-21-2005 “Stranger things have happened you know.” My voice was sort of different in this breeze morning. It felt less effortless seeing as how I finally got a good’s night rest. My head didn’t ach at all. Though after that hard lost to Danielle Northsky and The Back Alley Brawler, I could use some good old fashion hot water to soak in. Walking towards the bathroom which was down the hall and to the left, I could hear a few voices in my Uncle’s bedroom. One of them sounded like my trainer, Michael Johnson. The other, a voice I hadn’t heard at all and it didn’t sound good. Walking a little further down the damp hallway, I placed a little ear closer to the opening of the doorway to listen in a little better. “She’s just not right anymore. She doesn’t want to listen to anyone.” Says my trainer, Michael. “What are you saying?” Uncle Johnson replies. “I’m saying that if she keeps on doing what she’s doing and going crazy, she doesn’t need to be wrestling.” Michael responses. “How crazy is she?” Says the man that I never heard of. Creeping around the corner of the opening, I could see that he was wearing a long dark suit. Perfectly clean to the bottom of the pants. Holding a clip board with some writing already on it that I couldn’t see. “She talks to go knows who is there. Plus last week I tried to make her do something and she went off on me like some mad woman.” Responses Michael with a very concern type of voice. Mad woman? Talking to god knows who? It’s like they are trying to get me commended. Taking a small breath before pressing my hands through my head. What was going on? As I stood there, I felt a sudden urge to just run through that door and scream at them with all my rage that I had but I was better then that. Waiting for a few seconds, listening carefully a little more at the words that came out of my uncle’s mouth. “Well either way you look at it, if she doesn’t get in the game I’ll pull her out of everything very soon.” My uncle says. “WHAT!” Accidentally screaming out. My cover was blown. Leaning over a little bit to show my head to them all. I didn’t want to be seen in this conversation but it was to late. Stepping fully through to the concerned filled room, I could see the anger and disappointment that fell right out of my uncle’s eyes. “What are you guys trying to do here?” Asking in my very own shallow deep anger voice. “Elia’you got to realize something. You aren’t the same person.” Response Michael as he turns towards me. “How can you say that?” Almost screaming at him but doing it with my hands. I was the same person, I didn’t see anything different in me. “Ms. Smith. My name is Doctor Thomas and I’m from the Texas State University of Deep Depression. I’d like to see if I can?” Asking the other man before sticking his hand out for me to shake. “SHUT UP!” Screaming out at him like he was some punk kid back in school. I wasn’t about to go to some nut doctor for help. Looking over at my uncle who’s head was just dropping to the floor with both anger and disappointment for everything he had heard coming out of my mouth. But right now it wasn’t about him, it was about me. “You listen here. I’m fine the way I am. So what if I talk to some special friend. As long as I’m doing what I need to then that should be all that matters.” Pointing at them all with anger. “Now if you would excuse me, I got to go get ready for practice.” The words were done and over with. Turning around to walk through the door, not even waiting for a response from them, I could notice that he was standing there. Deep with hate and despair. His eyes, glowing with redness and a hell of a lot darker then the depths of a fire. I could feel my heart skip a beat, making me stop for a moment in my foot steps. “They want you to be their slave?” He says, standing there with his red eyes, locking them on me. “No their not?” Responding with a whisper that was as low as it could possibly be. Turning my head to get one more glance from the three men, wondering if I convinced my uncle to believe me, that I wasn’t crazy. Walking through the doorway, passing by the red eye man, I could feel apart of me almost drop out of my body. It was as if something in me, a part had just gone away. I didn’t feel like the same person. Maybe they were right. Maybe I was changing to the worse. Walking towards my room to get ready for practice for my match this week. “You shouldn’t trust them Elia.” The red eye monster says. “I don’t but they do know what’s best for me.” Responding with a soft voice, trying not to make any eye contact with him. “You should trust me. I am the only person that knows what’s best for you know. Not your uncle and not your trainer either.” He says, walking towards me and placing both hands on my shoulders. A blast of fear began to rage throughout my body. Raising up to my very head was the sort of fear that you get when you think you are going to die. I didn’t know what to do. Standing there, he began to rub my very shoulders, tormenting me in everyway. “I will make you see the light that I shine.” Says the red eye man. “And’what if I don’t’want to?” Saying in a shaky scared voice towards the wall in front of me. I could feel his breath getting closer and closer to my neck. Caressing my back so gently to in the process. “No matter what you do Elia, good is always overpowered by evil. I will make you see that.” Saying with a soft and warm voice. “Shut up!” Saying out loud once again but this time to something much stronger then my uncle and Michael. Turning around to see is eyes slowly changing from the fiery red that the always were to a very deep and angry blue that would even scare the biggest guy away. My heart began to stop inside my chest. My body just froze, as if I was in a freeze, with a temperature of under zero degree’s. I began to back up a little more, driving my back right into the wall that held up my pictures. “Elia?” Driving his eyes deeper into me. “Things are not what they seem. I might not be able to touch others but I can be felt by you.” “Yes?” Shaking my head. As I said that, my uncle came walking into the room. His mouth and eyes were dead with depression of me I could tell. He just stood there in the hallway of the door, looking right at me with his eyes and heart. Though for the few seconds that had pass by it felt like a life time. He then continued the walk on by, making me drop to my knee’s, covering both my eyes with my hands. Would it ever go away? Would it ever go’away? That was all I could say over and over in my head. “I’m not crazy!”
