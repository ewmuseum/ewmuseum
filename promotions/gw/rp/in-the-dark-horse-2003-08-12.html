In the Dark Horse

12 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-12-03 The mood in the Dark Horse saloon was at an all-time high. People were dancing, drinking, riding the mechanical bull, etc. Then the door swung open and in walked Brick and Ely Braxton. “Hey champ,” someone shouted. Brick turned and nodded his head. Truth be told, Brick was not exactly in the mood to party. He was only here at Christina McCoy’s insistence. Braxton was hanging around to keep Brick out of trouble. Brick had already gotten into a bit of a brouhaha with Ice, Deacon Holmes and the returning Mr. UK. It was Mr. UK who had really angered him. Granted, Deacon Holmes had been a thorn in his side for some time now and Ice had disrespected him by slapping him in the face. But Mr. UK had decided to sneak attack Brick with a steel chair. He then proceeded to insult Brick and question his worth to hold the World title. Brick came to the Saloon to unwind and let off some steam. “Feelin” any better?” asked Braxton as the two men. “No, not really.” “Well, that’s why we’re here.” “No, I’m here because Christina didn’t want me beatin” the shit out of anybody. You’re here to play babysitter.” “Confidant, Brick. Confidant. How many times do I have to say it?” “Fine, fine, confidant. Well, “confidant”, are we gonna order any drinks?” “Yes, we are.” Braxton then turned to the bartender. “Gimme my usual.” “OK, that’s one Texas mudslide. And how about you, champ?” “Nothin” alcoholic?” “Not my babysitter, huh?” “Look, you’re already pissed off. Now you bein” pissed off an? drunk would be even worse.” “How about a Venom Alexander?” asked the bartender. “Glass of milk” I don’t think so. I’ll just have some ginger ale.” “One Texas mudslide and one ginger ale coming right up.” Brick then spun around and began to sulk. “UK still got you steamed?” “Damn right he’s got me steamed! Who the hell is he to say that I’m bringing down the title” Just because he comes in for about a month or so, wins a whole bunch of fluke victories, one of those being a World title win and then he just bails out, he thinks he’s such hot shit! I’ve been busting my ass for this company since it got off the damn ground! I earned this damn belt! Every time I lost blood, I earned this belt! Every time I broke some bones, I earned this belt! Every time I cheated death, I earned this belt! And Mr. UK wants to come in out of nowhere and talk shit about me? Where does he get off?!” “Easy. See, this is why you don’t need to be drinkin” any alcohol when you’re in this type o? mood.” Just then, the bartender brought the two men their drinks. “One Texas mudslide and one ginger ale.” Brick and Braxton took their drinks and each took a sip. “And then that little bitch Ice wants to try me, too” Slappin? me in my face like I’m Tina Turner and then when I’m down from the chairshots UK gave me, he wants to kick me while I’m down, knowing if I was on my feet, I’d have been Ike and he’d have been Tina.” “Uh-huh.” “And you know where it all started” With Deacon Holmes. Ever since that fool came into my life, I’ve known nothing but misery. He just goes out of his way to piss me off.” “So, don’t let “im.” “Excuse me” You’re giving me tips on anger management? Weren’t you the cat that was ready to rip Sean Price’s head off a few months back?” “OK, so we both need to learn some anger management.” “Amen to that.”
