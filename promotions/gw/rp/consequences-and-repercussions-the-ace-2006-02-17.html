Consequences and repercussions - The ace.

17 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 17th, 2006. Consequences and repercussions - the ace ---- Knight Enterprises. Seattle, Washington. February 14th, 2006. 10:37 AM. 'Okay, this is what we have.' Began attorney at law Colton Cornell, decked out in a fancy black Italian suit, with matching shoes and shirt, the top button on his shirt undone and the absence of a tie noticeable but forgivable. 'Wrestling International News, also known as WIN have made several false accusations against you, Josh.' Colton turned his brown eyes to meet my bluish gray orbs before flipping his gaze back to my golden haired and silver tongued agent, Donovan Knight, as he continued. 'Due to the negative paternity test that came back, we've got the evidence on our side to slap WIN with a hefty class action lawsuit.' He turned his gaze back to me, making sure that I was still paying attention. Satisfied that he had my full concentration he surged on with his booming, professional voice. 'The charges against WIN? Those would of course be libel and defamation of character, and would most certainly carry a rather heavy price tag. I'm telling you, Josh...Donovan...we've got these guys.' Donovan grinned, hooking his thumbs into the hip pockets of his khakis as he leaned back in his chair, and swung his white K-Swiss sneakers up onto the oaken conference table our faction had huddled around. 'I like it, I love it, I want some more of it.' Donovan quipped, in a direct quote from a Country song. 'Well Josh, what do you think?' Asked Cornell as I stared down at the white toe caps of my black Chuck Taylors. 'I don't know...' I shrugged. 'What do you mean you don't know?' Mused Donovan as he playfully brought his right hand up to muss my dysfunctional black and white locks even further. 'There is big money in this, kid, big money.' 'You're right.' I agreed, looking first at Donovan and then at Colton. 'You're absolutely right, there is big money in this, but is that really what its all about...the money?' Colton and Donovan both stared at me, they blinked slowly, almost simultaneously before breaking into synergetic laughter. 'Of course its all about the money!' Donovan yelled joyously. 'Money makes the world go round, it turns black to white, day to night and wrong to right, its all about the Benjamin's, baby.' I shrugged yet again. 'I'm not convinced.' 'How can you not be convinced?', asked a stunned Cornell. 'That publication did something to you that is morally reprehensible, Josh! They attacked you without showing a single iota of journalistic integrity!' 'Josh, they went after your reputation as a role model, as a hero to the American Youth.' Donovan interjected, his smile dissolving as he folded his hands over the stomach of his blue Hurley t-shirt. 'In short, they tried to destroy you, because the only thing people love more than a hero, is to watch a hero fall.' 'I'm sorry', I disagreed, 'I'm still not convinced.' 'Josh, what do I have to say to convince you?' Asked Donovan as he dropped his feet back to the floor. 'Do I have to read you the part where they called you a neglectful father? Did you see the part where they called Espen a bastard lovechild? They went after you man, now lets turn it around and go after them!' Donovan emphasized his point by slamming his fist down on the table. 'We're talking millions, Josh, millions.' Cornell added. 'They called me a few names, big deal.' I smiled, shrugging it off. 'I get called worse things in the octagon than they called me. I walk into an arena with thousands of people screaming my name but somehow I always manage to hear that one guy in the eighteenth row calling me an asshole.' 'But Josh,' Donovan explained, 'this isn't a guy in a crowd of thousands calling you an asshole, this is a guy calling you an asshole in front of a crowd of thousands.' I raised my eyebrows in pure skepticism. 'I don't think those were the exact words written.' 'Tell me Josh, after everything that was said about you...why don't you want to go after these sons of bitches?' Colton Cornell asked, beginning to show signs of frustration. 'Whats it going to prove, Colt?' I asked. 'Is it going to prove that I'm not really the father? Is it going to prove that I'm not the bad guy they made me out to be? Is it going to prove that to the world?' 'Yes, thats exactly what its going to prove.' Colton answered. 'Then its pointless.' I countered. 'I don't care what I prove to the world, I only care about what I prove to myself and stepping into that court room in hopes of making some quick cash isn't going to prove a damn thing to Josh.' I retorted. 'Okay, you lost me...' Donovan said, shaking his head. With honesty in my eyes I turned to face Donovan. 'Its like this D...' I began. 'If I win a cool million or two, thats great, it'll do wonders for my bank account and maybe I'll save a little face. Then again, maybe not. My fans may turn on me when they see me in the middle of a nasty legal squabble. Either way, we're missing the big picture.' 'If millions of dollars isn't the big picture, then just what the hell is?' Cornell asked, a little bemused by me hesitance to jump on the big money bandwagon. 'A little boy without a father.' I answered. 'Josh, don't make this any harder than it already is.' Donovan pleaded. 'Espen is not your son, we all know that.' I nodded my agreeance. 'Yes, we know that I'm not the father, but we also know that his real father is dead, and there are a few questions that haven't been adequately answered.' 'Josh, the federales have closed the case.' Cornell stated with a scowl. 'It was an accident, accidents happen.' 'Maybe.' I said with a slow nod. 'Then again, maybe not.' 'What the hell are you talking about?' Cornell asked, his patience wearing thin. 'This April it will be a year since the death of Juan and Rosa, and maybe I've been so traumatized and so caught up with the way I've been involved...maybe I've been too distracted to notice, but...something doesn't feel right.' I said softly, my voice low and secretive. 'Don't be ridiculous.' Cornell sneered contemptuously. 'Its over Josh.' 'Yeah, he's right, whats this all about, Josh?' Donovan queried with a pair of arched eyebrows. 'No, its ridiculous to dismiss the idea of foul play. Think about it, another vehicle was involved, but not at the scene of the accident? Did you see what that Lexus looked like after the wreck? Juan and Rosa were dead on impact.' My voice grew in both volume and intensity as I suddenly felt as if my skepticism was vindicated. 'What the hell did they hit, a tank?' 'Or maybe a couple of drunk Mexican's who didn't feel like going to jail that night?' Cornell countered. I ignored his snide comment as I further explored my theory. 'I've been thinking this over for the last few days...' I paused in thought, my eyes subconsciously studying the outline on the face of my gray t-shier, sporting the namesake of the band 'no tagbacks'. 'I need to know what really happened to Juan and Rosa, I think I'm going to fly down to Juarez and see what I can find out for myself.' 'Josh, no, this is asinine and stupid.' Colton shot at me. 'Fuck you, Cornell!' I retaliated loudly. 'Piss ant lawyers like you are a dime a fucking dozen, and don't think I'll hesitate to fire your stupid ass!' 'Josh!' Donovan scolded. 'Relax, he's just trying to keep you out of getting into more trouble.' 'Donovan, it doesn't matter how much trouble I get into because this quest isn't for me.' I said, as I slid my chair back and leaned across the table. 'This is for Juan, this is for Rosa, this is for Espen...this is for justice man; justice.' 'You aren't going to justify anything or anyone by going to Mexico and wasting your time on a wild goose chase.' Cornell threatened. 'Did you hear me the last time when I said; FUCK YOU?' I snarled in response as I stood to my feet. 'Josh, please...' Donovan tried his best to reason with me. 'When are you leaving? Lets at least think this through.' Reaching into my ragged Guess jeans I extracted a plane ticket and a smile. 'I'm leaving for Juarez the second I walk out of here.' 'Josh...' Donovan stammered, seemingly rather surprised by this turn of events. 'We...we haven't even talked about this...' My smile spread even further across my face. 'Actually...we just did.' And with that I turned on my heel and walked across the room, I opened the door without bothering to look behind me and closed it without hearing another word. In a matter of hours I would be in Juarez. In a matter of hours I would be looking for the truth, in a matter of hours I would be looking for the only thing that mattered to me at this point; forgiveness. ---- And as quickly as all of that Josh was gone. He had walked through the door of Knight enterprises with a 'fuck you' and a plane ticket and was winging his way towards Juarez, Mexico. Towards the God awful truth... They say that the truth sets men free, but in some cases it also locks them up, this was one of those cases, and two of the culprits in question sat at the conference table, dumbfounded. 'Oh...fuck.' Donovan breathed the obscenity heavily. 'What the hell are we going to do?' 'Don't worry...' Cornell started but Donovan quickly cut him off. 'Don't worry?!' Donovan screamed. 'Don't fucking worry?!' 'Don't worry.' Cornell assured him. 'How can I not worry?' Donovan gasped. 'If he goes down there and finds the truth, I...I...we're fucked, Colt, we're fucked!' 'But who says he has to find the truth?' Cornell answered with a cold smile. 'Maybe you're right.' Donovan nodded. 'We've kept it hidden for almost a year...' 'As I said,' The lawyers slick tone was assuring. 'he'll never know.' 'Or...maybe not?' Donovan mused softly. 'I can't hinge my life on a maybe. I've got a multi-million dollar company, the hottest star in the industry and several up and comers on the list. I can't lose this...' Donovan stood to his feet and with quick nervous paces he found his way to the behemoth of a liquor cabinet that stood in the corner of the conference room of Knight Enterprises. His nervous hands and shaking fingers extracted a bottle of scotch and a glass. He snapped the lid off of the bottle and poured himself a glass, gulping the liquid down almost as soon as it had settled into the bottom of the glass. He sighed heavily and poured himself another shot. 'Come on, don't worry.' Cornell smiled. 'We'll make sure that he never gets too close to the truth.' 'How...' Donovan was desperate for an answer as he gulped down the scotch. 'How are you going to keep him away from finding out what really happened on April 25th?' Cornell smiled, he had an ace up his sleeve, he always did, this time the ace had a name. The name was Eduardo Ruiz. ----
