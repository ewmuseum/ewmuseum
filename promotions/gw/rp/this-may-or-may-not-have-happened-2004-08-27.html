Date: 8/27/04 DISCLAIMER: The following has parts that are specualative and may or may not have actually happened. Date: August 16th. Earlier that day, Deacon Holmes had gone into Bubba McCoy's office to complain about the booking for Whiplash. The week before on Evolution, Deacon had gotten into a ruckus backstage with some of the crew members. The reason: Brick had continued to claim that he had slept with Deacon's girlfriend Rhaiza. The altercation came as a result. After security had broken it up, Deacon told security to relay to Bubba that he wanted Brick at Whiplash. However, it was announced Later that evening, Brick and Donny McNasty had been hanging out backstage. The night before, Donny was a guest for Sunday dinner with Brick and his family. In addition to thanking him, he also hinted that he had something up his sleeve as retribution for the stunt Train pulled the week before with the garbage truck. Brick then wished Donny luck and excused himself. When Donny asked what the rush was, Brick replied with a sly smile, 'I'm, uh, expecting company.' Given the recent rumblings backstage, one could only assume who this 'company' could be. Immediately after shutting the door, Brick made his way to the shower, stripping off his clothes on the way. First, he turned on the hot water and then he turned on the cold, adjusting the temperature until it was just right. Then he jumped in. A few minutes later, just as Brick was coming out of the shower, he heard a knock at the door. He wrapped a towel around his waist and went to answer the door. There, standing on the other side, was Rhaiza, wearing a black trenchoat. In fact, it was the very same trenchcoat she wore the night she visited him in his hotel room. 'I was wondering when you'd get here.' Smiling, she then opened her trenchcoat and let it fall to the ground. She had absolutely nothing on underneath. She was almost completely naked except for some sandals on her feet. 'Glad I waited. Come on in.' Brick then ushered Rhaiza into his locker room, checking to see if anybody saw her. He then placed a 'Do Not Disturb' sign on the doorknob and slammed it shut. 'Well, shall we get started?' 'Wait, my coat,' said Rhaiza. 'Oh, right.' Brick then quickly opened the door, grabbed the trenchcoat and pulled it in before slamming the door shut. 'Heard Deacon threw a tantrum in Bubba's office today.' 'Yeah. He was pissed, but I let him know it was his fault this was happening, anyway.' 'You mean him and I not facing each other at Whiplash? Well, truth be told, I wouldn't have had a problem facing him in the ring, or whereever. Still, he couldn't have been half as pissed about this as he was about what happened last week.' 'So, did you just get out of the shower, or were you about to go in?' 'Just got out. Figured it'd loosen me up.' 'Well, we both might need a shower after we're done,' Rhaiza purred. 'OK, but this is gonna have to be quick. Donny and I have a match later on and we need to get ready.' 'Then, let's skip the foreplay.' And skip the foreplay, they did. Some time later, there was a knock at the door. Fortunately, Brick and Rhaiza were done and getting dressed, or at least as dressed as Rhaiza could. Lying on the floor was Brick's towel and a few open condom wrappers. Brick had already had on his pants, and gloves. 'Hold on, I'm coming!' Brick exclaimed as he headed to the door. Rhaiza couldn't help but giggle. 'You are so nasty,' Brick whispered back. As he opened the door only slightly, there was a stage hand standing on the other side of the door. 'Excuse me? Can you not read? What does the sign say?' 'Huh?' 'The sign on the doorknob! What does it say?' 'Um, 'Do Not Disturb'?' 'Congratulations. You can read. So can you tell me why you're knocking on my door, disturbing me?' 'Umm, Donny McNasty sent me. He said he needs you to meet with him so that you can get ready for your match tonight.' 'OK, tell him to meet me by the soda machine in, say, ten minutes.' 'OK.' Brick then noticed that the stage hand was trying to peer into his locker room. 'Something wrong?' 'Um, are you alone?' 'And that's your business why?' 'It's, um, just that you have the door partially closed. Plus, Mr. McNasty said that you might be busy.' 'Well, my locker room is a mess right now. I don't want anybody to see it.' 'Oh, okay.' The stage hand just stood there. 'Weren't you supposed to go do something?' 'Oh, right. Sorry.' The stage hand ran off to find Donny McNasty. 'Well, I gotta go. Sorry.' 'I should be going too. Wouldn't want Deacon to get suspicious. Um, don't you want to straighten up before you go?' 'Nah. I think I'll leave it like this. I'll deal with it later.' 'What about the shower?' 'Again, I'll deal with it later.' As they left, Rhaiza took off, holding her trenchcoat closed. Brick fingered the 'Do Not Disturb' sign he left on the doorknob and then locked the door before walking away.
