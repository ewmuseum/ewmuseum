A Conversation with The M.O.B. (Evo.#2)

11 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/11/04 THUMP, THUMP, THUMP....</CENTER Train figured he had pretty good timing. He was stepping out of the bathroom, the towel wrapped tightly around his waist just as the banging came at the metal front door of his building. He'd guessed right that he had just enough time to take a shower before the Papa John's guy got there with the two large 'Works' pizza's he ordered. His stomach rumbled just a bit as he quickly walked across the room and grabed the wad of cash of the inn table situated on the upstairs, apartment level of his warehouse flat. Train turned and made his way around the corner to the stairs and headed down. He'd been waiting for this, he knew this pizza would hit the spot just like the shower had. His muscles needed the hot water to loosen a little. No doubt he was still going to be sore from the workout he'd pounded through after getting himself accquainted with Jamester's tapes, but he wasn't worried about that. He was in tip top shape, but he pushed himself farther than he had in a long time, blasting his muscles with weight, attempting to force growth on them before his next match. He needed an edge. No doubt he'd feel it in the morning, but he wasn't worried about it. Mostly because he knew what it would be like. He knew the second he heard his music blasting through the arena...looking down on Jamester, prone and half broken square in the middle of the ring...with the ref raising his hand in the air, sending a signal to the world that he'd just punked out the PTC Global Champion...he already knew... There would be no pain. Not only would there be no pain, but there would be a rumble. Ground shaking. Earth shattering. The world would suddenly be jarred awake and everyone would have to turn their eyes and take a fucking note. There was a new star in the game. Yeah... Choo-Choo muthafuckas. They'd hear him coming to the top of the game and there'd be no more ducking and dodging. Ice, Zero, Holmes...especially that little twerp Holmes. Nobody would be able to run from him anymore. Of course, there was one small problem. Once he dropped Jamester and lined himself up for a title shot...who would watch his back? Holmes and Zero had TUE... Ice had The Elite. So who did Train have to make sure none of the champs buddies didn't come down and screw him? The M.O.B.? Pshhhh.... What a fucking joke. More than that...what a fucking waste of time. Yeah, all of 'em talked a good game, but when the rubber met the road Train was the only one of 'em headed anywhere. Ain't no point to being in a clique with a bunch of cats that can't tote their own weight. Especially that ignorant cocksucker Makonie. Everytime Train thought of how he'd gone out on a limb for that jag off.... They say opinions are like assholes, everybody has one. It must be true because it seems like Train had to take shit from every loud mouth asshole walking for working with Makonie. And what did he get for his trouble? A fucking anchor around his neck, weighing him down. He had the carry that jerk off every step of the way. It felt good when he dropped that bastard right on his big, bald head. Unfortunately Train knew he'd have to watch his back from here on out. He knew The Greenisos and Makonie would be looking for him. It was a pain in the ass. Not because he was scared. he knew he could take any or all of 'em out of the equation anytime he got ready. Thing about it was... It was the life. Doing what you want, when you want, why you want, because you want. There was nothing like it. Nothing that could beat it. Which is what pissed him off. If those bastards could have pulled whatever it was that was jamming the gear boxes in their asses...which from Train's veiw point was their heads, they could have been unstopable. If they had played the game the way it was meant to be played guys like The M.O.B. could have buried The Elite and The Upper Echelon. Guys like Train and the Greenisos... They knew what it was like to not play by the rules. And not those fucking wrestling rules. The real rules. Guys like that, all working together? He knew, if they operated the way they should, they could get the job done in and out of the ring. In and out of the arena. Train had lived in that world. He knew the Greenisos. He knew what they did for a living. When they first showed up in GW he'd wondered what the hell guys like that were doing in the wrestling biz. He wondered until he remembered that he was there too. Oh well. That's life. Train wasn't nearly as concerned about them as he was his dinner right now. THUMP, THUMP, THUMP 'Hold on a second!' Train yells, making his way down the steps to the bottom floor. Train hops down from the last step and turns, now heading towards the door. He thumbs through the cash in his hand, counting out the bill the girl on the phone game him, plus a tip. The metal bolts on the door pop loudly as Train opens them, releasing the door. The door itself could use a shot of WD-40, squealing loudly as he opens it... '$21.80, right? Here's....' Train stops. There is no point to continuing...since there is no one there. Train's spidey senses are in full tingle now, his eyes now shooting back and forth, surveying the street of the industrial area where his him is located. There's no one on the street. A few old, rusted cars, but, unless they're ducking there's no one in them. His hand instinctually begins to reach to the metal partitian between this door and the garage door he pulls the Navigator through. The frame of the door was about 6' thick, the metal attatched to the outer wall of the building. He he added another strip of metal a few weeks back, making a half hollow spot between the the inner and outer side of the frame. It was very efficent. Not only did the insulation in the upper part of the frame help keep the place warmer, the lower, still hollow part of the door frame held the 12 guage snuggly. Anybody who thought this shit was funny wasn't going to thinks so when that buckshot caught 'em. Just as Train's hand makes contact with the door of the secret compartment he freezes. 'What the hell?' Train looks down and spots something he knows isn't supposed to be there. A silver cell phone. Never letting his eyes leave the horizon as he scans, Train bends his knees, keeping his upper body straight and poised, and picks up the phone. He examines it for a moment before stepping back inside and slamming the door shut. He gets about 2 of his deadbolts locked back in place when the cell begins to vibrate in his hand. The sensation catches him off gaurd combining itself with the adrenaline now running through him. It startles him for just a moment, but he's back to himself a second later. The phone vibrates again. Train feels the irritation at this situation growing inside him and somehow he already knows who's on the other end of the line even before checking the phone's caller ID window. Yup. He knows the name that's displayed on the phone... Terrelle Greeniso. Train reaches over and pushes in on the metal frame of the garage door. There is a small click and the compartment pops open. The shotgun fits snugly in his hand. click-clack.... The shell slid effortlessly into the chamber. The exit of the shell would be anything but if someone was trying to use this as a distraction. Holding it with his right hand, Train lifted the barrel of the gun and let it rest casually on his shoulder. With his left he even more casually flipped the phone open. 'What?' Train asked gruffly. 'Now, now, now....' Terrelle said in a tone that was half playful and half menacing. 'Is that any way to greet an old friend?' 'Nope. And when I get an old friend on the phone I'll be sure not to answer it like that. Now what the fuck do you want?' 'That's what I love about you my friend...right down to business.' 'Yup.' 'Well the, let's get down to it. Train... A man like you and me we got a lot in common really. You came up rough, I came up rough. We've done what we felt like we had to do on the streets. That means we both know the rules. In the life you know there's one rule above all others. Once you're in? There is no out. So what the hell did you think you were doing on Evolution Train? Huh? You don't really think it's going to be that easy to just walk away do you?' 'It could be as easy as a walk in the park. Could be as hard as climbin' Everest. But either way... I'm out. Fuck the M.O.B. You need someone to carry yalls jock, get somebody else. I'm doing just like I did when I started out in the game... I'm carrying my own weight.' 'Haha... You little son of a bitch. Do you know what I could do to you? I could make your ass disappear with a snap of my fingers.' 'No you couldn't.' 'Oh, is that so?' 'Yeah, that's so. See we both know what you're in this game for. You're washing money my friend. You need it to avoid the tax evasion case the feds are probably building against you as we speak. You need a source of income that justifies the jets and limos. The $200 cuff links and $2000 suits. I go down and the cops are gonna know to come looking for you. I mean damn, we couldn't have broken our association in a more public setting. That's why I dropped Makonie for the world to see. Not to mention I was sick of that fuck costing me money by costing me matches. You go down for murder or conspiracy you won't see the outside till it's time for you to collect social security. ' 'If I put you down they'd never find the body.' 'That happens you might as well put your finger prints on the gun and leave a business card at the scene. Besides... you're forgetting something.' 'Forgetting something?' 'Yeah. You want me? You gotta catch me first. I pity the muthafucka you send after me. I'll cut his fucking balls off, shove 'em in his mouth and send his head back to you in the mail. Not to mention my connections ain't gone. I make one call and half your customers in the Queens and The Bronx boycott yo' ass. Fuck a dry spell. I'll turn your coke business into a fucking desert. Oh, and don't the Yakuza want you dead? If I remember it right...they owe me a favor. Do yourself a favor... I'm trying to leave that shit behind. Don't make me turn around. Don't forget who the fuck you're talking to.' 'You'd be wise to do the same you arrogant son of a bitch! You cut up one of my men? Whatever. I have 3 more waiting in the wings to take his place. Your friends want to buy from someone else? Hey, it's a free country. But when I put all of them in the morgue somebody will come up in their stead who'd be more than happy to do business with the largest supplier on the east coast. 'Why would they want that when the largest supplier in the mid and sourhwest can cut 'em a better deal?' 'hahaha... You're a funny guy Train. Quick on your toes. I could almost like that about you. And, while your threats are meaningless, you're right. I'm not gonna kill you. I have way too much on the line to risk bringing the cops or the feds into this over you. But you understand The M.O.B. can't just let this pass. An example has to be made.' 'haha... You know if you could put half as much effort and creativity into building The M.O.B. as you do into trying to bullshit me into being scared of you? You'd have a hell of an organization.' 'Oh..and I suppose you have the master plan do you?' 'Tell you what Terrelle. When you're tired of playing at this M.O.B. thing? When you're ready to kick down the doors in GW and take what the fuck you want no matter who don't like it? You give me a call. Until then? Stay out of my face.' 'Oh, but I will. Be in your face I mean. I will be in your face and I will stay there and I will make you suffer. Unless of course...you can give me a good reason not to.' 'Greeniso, get real. Now that I'm out you and Mikey ain't doing nothing, but carrying around dead weight. You're telling me to give you a reason? I don't need to give you a reason. If you're really in control of The M.O.B. like you say you are?! You need to make a fucking decision.' With that Train snaps the phone shut and drops it to the floor. The butt of the shot gun shatters it as it makes contact. Train starts to head back towards the stairs... THUMP, THUMP, THUMP Train whips around, the shut gun clutched in both hands. 'Who is it?!' 'Pizza' an adolescent voice responds. Train creeps up to the door. He undoes the locks and places the shotgun flat against the back of the door as he slowly opens it, peering outside. Though he keeps the gun ready, hidden behind the door he opens it to the young man, who can't be half out of high school. Train hands him the money with a $10 tip and the young hands him dinner in return. The door slams shut and the locks follow suit.
