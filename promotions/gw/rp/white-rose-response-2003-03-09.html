White Rose response

9 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-9-03 Ace stands in front of a brick wall, his hair dripping wet and a black towel around his shoulders. Ace: Look what we have here. A new group by the name of White Rose has decided to shoot their mouths off at would-be “families” as they call it. They seem to think they know what it means to be in a group of trustworthy individuals, bound by trust and honor. It’s amazing the crap that will come out of people’s mouths, without the slightest idea of what they’re saying. They think by spouting a bunch of definitions from a dictionary, that they have proven to have some sort of knowledge to back up their ignorant view of what a real team should be. They think that trust amongst brothers, faith in their ability and determination is what makes them a team. My boys’you haven’t a goddamn clue what you’re talking about. For 10 years of my life, I spent every waking and sleeping moment, eating, breathing, and living this business, with the firm belief that honor, respect, and above all trust, would lead me to the top of this business, the way it ought to be. I honestly thought that doing it the right way was the best way, and that all my hard work, pain and suffering, would eventually be worth the effort. I was WRONG. 100 percent WRONG. Because you guys have to understand that first, before anything else, this is a BUSINESS. This is NOT a sporting competition, which is based solely on working well with others, mental integrity, and athletic ability. This is?.a BUSINESS’FIRST and FOREMOST. It’s nice to have those dreams and fantasies of glory and fame while under the illusion that things like “trust” are still a priority, but let me tell you that in the end, it all means NOTHING. People that I thought were my friends, people I grew up with and showed me the ropes, shared every experience and every ounce of knowledge with me, turned around and PLUNGED the knife so far into my back it PIERCED my heart, and TORE through the other SIDE!!! Where was their fucking trust NOW?! WHERE WAS IT?!”! You know where it was? In all the money, attention, and infamy that they gained. They would go on to be HUGE, they would make themselves into household names. ALL by whoring out MY TRUST!!!! You may THINK that that cock head standing next to you is your brother, that they would within a heart beat step in front of a silver bullet to save you, but what you can’t see is the truth’that they’re only close to you, because it is the BEST place to take advantage of your gullibility! You see White Rose, or whatever fruity name you guys are going by, the difference between the Elite and other groups like yours, is NOT that we trust each other or even that we know each other like brothers. Frankly, I would just as soon snap KPJ’s neck as I would shake his hand. What we are is a group of people with a common purpose, a common view point, and a common level of skill, experience, and ability. We aren’t doing this to make friends. We’re doing this to leave a SCAR on the business, by proving to everybody that we ARE the best at what we do, NOT because we are best buds, but because we are just that fucking GOOD. For years I’ve been trying to make my mark on this business, and only now have I even scratched the surface enough to smell the blood beneath it. And you will be the next layer of epidermis that we will RIP and TEAR and GOUGE at like ravenous wolverines, until we taste the blood we SO thirst for. Because trust has NOTHING to do with this, fellas. What matters is whether the person behind you is going to do his job, whatever it takes, even if it means using you as a means to an end. I don’t trust KPJ, not for one SECOND. But I KNOW what he is capable of, I know his ability, and to be entirely honest he is quite possibly the BEST at what we do. And I would be a complete fucking IDIOT to have him against me. I am not his friend, I am not even his enemy. I am an ally, a soldier in his army, willing to do what it takes to help him make that scar in the business ALL that much bigger. If one of us has to screw the other eventually, SO BE IT. I walked into this federation expecting no less than to be screwed over, because I’m used to it. But even if that happens, you can rest assure that nobody will EVER forget the Elite, even if I have to end my career making you remember. You WILL remember your match against Ace Harding and Ken Partridge Jr.?and you will NEVER forget your defeat at the hands of the ELITE.
