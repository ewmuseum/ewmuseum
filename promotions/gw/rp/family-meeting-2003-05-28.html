Family meeting

28 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/28/03 The doorbell rings. Brick opens the door and sees Aaron and Felicia. 'Hey man,' Aaron says. 'We got here as soon as we got your message.' 'Glad you could make it, man. Come on in.' Aaron and Felicia walk into the living room where they see Brick's mother Coretta, his sister Monica and her husband Kyle all seated on the couch. 'How are you, Aaron?' says Coretta, as she gives him a hug and a kiss. 'I'm good, Mrs. Randall.' 'And Felicia, look at you. That baby looks like it should be here any day now.” “Just a few more weeks until he or she arrives.” “Well, I’m happy for you, Felicia.” “Kyle and I are thinking about having a baby,” says Monica, “but it’s all about timing.” “That’s what Aaron and I thought. But things just have a way of happening.” Just then, Brick walks up and everyone else takes a seat. “So, what’s up, man” You sounded serious on the phone.” “This is serious,” Brick responds. “You all got front-row seats to Dead Zone in the mail, right?” “Yeah. You didn’t send them, did you?” “No, I didn’t. But here’s who did.” Just then, Brick walks over to his answering machine and presses play. BEEP! 'Hello, Brick.' Right away, everyone except for Brick reacts to the sound of Salaok’s voice. “I hope your family got the complimentary front row tickets I sent them. I thought it was appropriate that they be up front to see your finest hour, or rather, see your destruction. But then again, being that they’re going to be so close to the action, one never knows what might happen. My prot’g’s do have a tendency for being, shall we say, spirited. I’d hate to think of your poor mother or your lovely sister getting hurt. Of course, Sage will do anything to prove himself to me. Just a little something for you to think about. I’ll see you at Dead Zone, Brick.” Brick presses stop. “Oh my God,” says Monica. “That’s?” “Yeah.” “I wish I was surprised,” says Aaron. “This is just the type of shit he’d pull.” “Yeah, putting just about everyone I care about right up front so either they can see the Nevermore beat me to a bloody pulp, or they can attack you any time they want. Which is why I called you all here today. This match coming up is maybe the most important match I’ll ever have and I’d like nothing else but for all of you here to share it with me, but after this?” “Look, Brick, you don’t have to say it. We understand.” “Listen to me, Jamal,” says Coretta. “I don’t want you worrying about us.” “I do worry, Mom. I promised Dad before he passed away that I’d protect you ad Monica whatever it takes. I meant it. I’ve already slipped once, with Salaok getting into Monica’s wedding. I don’t plan to slip up again.” “Come on, Brick,” says Monica. “It’s your big day. You should have your family with you.” “And you’re gonna be comfortable being there, knowing that Salaok might be there” That he might try something?” “I can deal with Salaok,” says Kyle. “No, you can’t, Kyle. I already told you, if anybody here needs to be dealing with Salaok and the Nevermore, it’s me.” “But you don’t have to do it alone, son.” “Yes, I do, mom. Yes I do.” “Now you listen to me, Jamal. We are family. What affects one of us affects all of us. Now I know you don’t want anything bad to happen to us, but this is important to you and we want to be there for you.” “I guess I could have Logan Creed beef up security around you. And I got a plan to deal with the Nevermore that night, anyway. Every time I’ve come close to the World title, they’ve found some way to screw me over. Well, not this time. At Dead Zone, Sabbot is mine and more importantly, the World title is mine.” Brick then embraces his family. He draws strength and love from them. And he’ll need all he can get. For he is about to go in to the fight of his life.
