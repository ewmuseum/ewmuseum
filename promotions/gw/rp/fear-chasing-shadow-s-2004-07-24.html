Fear: Chasing Shadow's.

24 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 24th, 2004. 'Chasing Shadows.' Have you ever had one of those dreams in which the line between fantasy and reality seemed to vanish into thin air and you were left hanging on for dear life? If so you'll realize how bad that is, but what about when you aren't dreaming? What about when you are living in reality yet can't distinguish the parallel between the two dimensions? You have to pinch yourself and repeatedly go over the scenario in which you were placed, yet still it seemed far too strange to be true. It's almost as if it never happened…yet you know it did because you witnessed it, you saw it with your own two eyes, heard it with your own two ears, soaked in it's odor with your nostrils and felt it's taste on your tongue. Yet somehow, someway you know that it couldn't have really happened…but it did! You argue with the mirror debating the topic with yourself all morning long as you shave, shower and brush your teeth. Yet still you come to no conclusion on this subject. It never happened…but it did happen…you just couldn't believe it. Such is my case, enter a dazed and confused young man, wondering exactly what happened to him in the early hours of Thursday morning no more than sixty minutes after midnight. A terrifying event that occurred on a desolate highway, something that left me bewildered and tugging at my ragged locks until they were even messier than when I woke up this morning. Yet…could this have really happened? Logic concludes that this is far too bizarre to be reality, yet reality itself has a strong point as it debates the subject and enters the evidence. What evidence? Three scratches on the back of my left hand, the calling card of this…shadow creature. A human, yes, but in what description? He was an elderly man, easily at the mark of seventy or above, yet he had such agility and grace for his age that he almost seemed to fall into another species. Suddenly the world of Edgar Allen Poe, Dean Koontz and Stephen King came alive as this could have easily been a character that sprang to life directly from the pages of their novels. He was so…intriguing. A wrinkled face, unmatched athleticism, great speed and strength, and pale blue eyes with a look of such inner torture in them that they will no doubt haunt my wretched soul until it reaches the grave. Who is this shadow man? Sighing aloud at my inaudible questions of a rhetorical nature I glanced down at the meal placed on the table before me. Such mouth watering steak as thick as could be sliced and cut from only the finest selection of beef, peppers danced along the edge of the beef adding to it's spicy flavor. The remnants of the steak were nothing more than a few scattered peppers and a T-bone that had been picked clean, almost as if it had been devoured by a flock of starving buzzards. The rest of the meal itself had also been consumed in a frenzy as I had quickly disposed of the less tasteful side dishes before turning my focus to the main event which was of course, the steak. A fine meal, indeed, yet not even such delicious food could take my mind off of the shadow man that seemed to have infiltrated my mind and danced through the corridors of my cerebral cortex as he invaded each and every thought. 'Fear', had been the word he cried out not once but twice as I gave pursuit his cracking and feeble voice had screamed it with the lungs of a man half his age, yet what could it have meant? Was I in fear? Of course not! Or at least not to anything I would admit. After all, what do I have to fear? I'm young, athletic and fully capable of handling myself. No fear may as well be my motto, right? After all, we have nothing to fear but fear itself, so they say. Wiping my mouth with the napkin placed before me I crumpled it into a ball and dropped it on my plate. Pushing myself away from the intricately decorated table I stood to my feet as I turned away from what had been left of a fine steak. 'Maybe this will help ease my mind.' I thought to myself as my feet, clad in Black Chuck Taylor's squeaked across the title floor and toward the duo of glass doors that served as the restaurants exit. My mind should be at peace now; I had a hot meal inside of me that had helped to warm me on this unseasonably mild July night. I should be feeling great, yet I'm not. Something is still out there and I know it…I can't stop thinking about it. My mind cant be put to rest as it tries to play Sherlock Holmes and discover what went wrong as fantasy and reality came to a head on collison. Shoving the door open I couldn't help but glance at the scratches on the back of my left hand and ponder the whereabouts of the perpetrator. Yet as I stepped out into a biting wind my mind was momentarily relieved of it's burden as I grimaced in the cold. The wind stung my eyes and whipped through the tattered jeans I wore which of course led up to a sleeveless white shirt bearing the words 'Causality of society' across it in black letters. Such a breeze of fierce cold…it must have been a mere twenty degrees out and this was July. What could possibly be going on? Suddenly the wind ceased and instead of the twenty degrees that I had just experienced the temperature seemed to magically rise to the mark of sixty-five that had been the same as when I entered the restaurant. Mother Nature has often been one to play tricks but never mind games…something was awry. My bluish gray eyes flashed across the street soaking up every movement that struck my orbs. The numerous people moved about at random, young people, old people, middle aged people, men, women; boys, girls…and a man dressed in a dark cloak. My eyes widened as I saw someone in a dark cloak step out of sight as he entered an alley. He was back! Should I pursue him once more? I must…I must. I am compelled to discover what he meant by the word 'fear'. Without a moments hesitation I sprang into action, taking off across the street on a mad blitz, navigating my way between honking cars and cursing pedestrians I reached the entrance to the alley just in time to see him step around a corner. 'Not this game again…' I audibly groaned, as I took off on a sprint the canvas soles of my Converse All Star's slapping down hard on the pavement beneath my swift feet. He once again had a significant lead on me in this foot race yet he hadn't seen me…or had he? The latter thought struck me as odd. I had never thought of that before. Was he luring me into a trap? Had he tried to lure me into a trap merely a few nights ago? And if so, why had it failed? What could he possibly want with me? The realization that he was baiting me entered my mind yet my pace did not stall as I proceeded on with reckless abandon and a million thoughts dancing through my mind. Reaching the spot at where the man had turned I quickly cut the corner, stumbling and almost tripping over my own feet as my pace slowed down before I came to an abrupt stop. At the end of the alley there was a brick wall...where had he gone? With an angry grunt I bit my lip, loathing these questions and the fact that I was unable to answer a single one. Turning my eyes I suddenly wished that along with the Superman tattoo on my arm I had the X-ray vision to go with it. On three sides of me there were brick walls littered with random doors, and behind me the alley continued on into the street. Yet I had seen him turn down here, which way could he have gone? His options were limited…a fire escape route and two unchained doors. The fire escape was too high for me and therefore would obviously be far too high for a man of his age no matter how great their agility. Turning my focus upon the doors I studied them diligently. Both were made of solid metal…graffiti had been spray painted over both doors. It was random graffiti, the usual barrage of obscene references and immature insults. Yet the other door seemed almost untouched save for a small blotch of paint…three silver crosses that decorated the brick. My gut instinct pointed directly to that door while screaming to enter and I was compelled to obey such a command, after all, I hadn't gotten this far by ignoring my instincts. Unless some magical force of great mystery was secretly leading me away to my untimely demise… Reaching for the knob of the door I noticed that it was slightly cracked open already, this was no doubt our door. Chasing the knob with my fingers my skin pressed against the cool steel of the knob…and the door opened by itself as could be witnessed in a half dozen horror movies. 'Now this is getting freaky...' I whispered to myself, my voice trailing off to oblivion. Stepping inside the dark building I couldn't see anything…yet my other senses were working overtime. My nose detected the faint smell of sulfur, my ears heard the striking of a match and suddenly the room was sent into illumination from the flame of said match! …At that instant the door slammed shut behind me. 'Who is in here?!' I demanded, taking a step back in defense as my hands curled into fists of anger. 'Fear not my son…' Came the straining voice of the old man I knew only as the shadow. The room was then sent into light as the match knifed through the dark and brought a candle to life, casting a flicker about the room. My eyes adjusted slowly and with a squint I gazed about the room. It was simple and obviously the resting-place of the Shadow. A cot neatly made with the blankets perfectly free of wrinkles, juxtaposed with the cot was a bookshelf with books of various natures. Everything from Edgar Allen Poe to books about the art of witchcraft adorned the library of this shadow man. Turning my eyes to the shadow man I saw him clearly for the first time. His hood was down and his pale blue eyes still bore their haunting gaze. His face seemed more wrinkled now than it had mere days ago. Lines of worry danced about his face creating a roadmap of stress, fear and anger that served as an autobiography of his life. 'What do you want?' I asked in reluctance, not wishing to speak to this man. 'To help you…' Came the answer as it rolled off of his chilling tongue. 'And in return?' I asked, with a strong sense of worry. 'To see you punish such minions of hell is payment enough for me.' He hissed in a nature that confused me. Perplexed by his words I remained silent. 'Sit.' He softly commanded as he pointed to a small stool that was directly across from the miniscule table he sat at. Glancing down at the table I saw things of rare and pagan nature that were brought to life by the candle, a book of spells, a crystal ball and an elixir of some strange concoction. Resting on the edge of the table was a dagger, sharp enough to shave with and at the end it bore a large cross. This was no doubt a place that I suddenly wished I hadn't stumbled into. Taking a seat across from him I kept my fists tightly clenched expecting the worst while silently begging God's forgiveness for having put myself into such a place, while beseeching his mercy and protection at the same time. 'You and I have something in common my son…' He said, the light bouncing off of his pale skin and reflecting his dirty nature. 'It obviously isn't our hygienic standards.' I thought as he placed both hands on the table and next to the crystal ball. 'We both have a common enemy and a common fear…Maverick Reno.' His words captured my full attention and suddenly I forgot where I was…who I was with and what type of place this was. 'I don't fear Reno.' I said in defiance, knowing that it was a lie as soon as it escaped my lips. 'You do. Yet no one knows that, do they? You fear Reno because you know what he is capable of. He could take everything away from you. Your job…your glory…your pride…or your life. How do I know, you must wonder. Because…I was yet another victim of Maverick Reno's evil nature.' The old man angrily hissed. This struck me as ironic. He was declaring Maverick Reno evil, yet he himself sat before me in a room full of occult books and objects. Perhaps Reno was even more evil than this shadow man that sat before me was, and suddenly I was plunged into even deeper fear. Fear, that I didn't even want to admit to myself. The old man returned to speaking, his voice consumed by anger. 'Once I was much like the poor fools that walk the streets. Happy, content and relaxing as the world just surged around me. Nothing could bother me; nothing could put a damper on my mood. I was happy and content…until the day that the love of my life was stolen away from me. She would never hurt a single soul…a loving wife, devoted mother and perfect grandmother; she seemed to be invincible. Her beauty continued on despite her years, her intelligence never dimmed and she always knew how to make me happy.' The hands of the shadow man began to shake slightly, his voice cracking with emotion. 'One day, it was all taken away…she was gone and in essence so was I, for a large part of me died with her. Yet was her blood spilled for a just cause? No. She died because she was in the wrong place at the wrong time. Her eyes were the only things that could incriminate Maverick Reno…so a bullet was placed between them.' The table rattled as the old man's shaking hands rapped against the wood. 'I could do nothing…I was helpless. With my heart and soul torn to a thousand pieces and my sanity dwindling I did the only thing I could think of…I became a recluse, an outcast and set to work diligently on lashing back at Maverick Reno for the pain he was cost me. Yet I am an old man, what can I do? I don't have an Army of bodyguards like Maverick Reno does…so I began to study the black arts. Magic became my friend; magic became my defense, my passion, my obsession…my new love.' His voice changed slightly, going from pure unbridled hatred and anger to a tone of depression. 'I am a weak old man in body and I cannot extract my revenge upon Maverick Reno…yet you can. Or can you? Can you put your fears away…can you hide the cowardice that burns inside of you when Maverick Reno confronts you?' His voice now became a plea, almost as if he were begging me to dispose of Maverick Reno! 'I know that you can…for I have seen it in your future.' He confidently stated. Sitting there in stunned silence I opened my mouth to speak but his voice silenced me. 'Go my son…there is work to be done for the war that will wage. I can help you no longer I can only tell you to face your fears, face Maverick Reno. And when you face him remember one thing…this isn't just for you, but for the families of all of his victims.' He closed his eyes, blocking the tears that were visibly welling therein. With his head held low he spoke softly… 'Justice must be served and you are the hero. You are the savior…' …and he blew out the candle.
