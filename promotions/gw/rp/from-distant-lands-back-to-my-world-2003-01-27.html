From Distant Lands, back to MY WORLD

27 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/27/03 'If you're so big and bad, prove it. Those were the words said to me. So proving it is all I have left to do. You see, I walked into this company without a video package. I walked into this company without the world praising me for my skills from distant lands. I walked into this company without any hype at all. I was given matches to open our weekly shows. Each week I guarenteed victory, and each week my guarentee was fulfilled. Still I was overlooked, ignored, and had to fight for what I got. So while they were ignoring me, pretending I wasn't climbing that ladder, I beat everyone in my path, and left a trail of blood just to make sure those who ignored me knew I was coming. It wasn't until I won the GW WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP, that I finally got noticed. And even then I was called a fluke champion. A champion that wouldn't be able to produce the things a champion should. But yet week in and week out I walked into the arena with that belt, and when the night was over I walked out with that same belt. There was not a single person in this damn company that could walk into that arena, down that aisle, into that ring, and hang with me. But then, one man went to far. One man did the only thing he could do. One man took my life away from me. Single handedly he killed my unborn child, and then only a few weeks later he killed the only person I ever loved.' The picture fades into Ice sitting alone on the edge of a building rooftop. His short blond hair is spiked in various places, and looks as though it had just recently been fixed up. A long black leather trench coat covers the rest of his body that is visible. The camera slowly inches its way towards him, as he sits facing away from the camera, gazing out over the dark nothingness that is before him. As the camera approaches, Ice does not turn to acknowlege it's presence. Rather he sits still, continuing to gaze out at the view in front of him. He lets out a deep, longing sigh, and begins to scratch the short five o'clock shadow that is begining to form on his pale face. 'After the deaths, I did not know what to think, what to do, where to go, or who to turn to. I was lost unto myself, searching for something the fill the void of what I had just lost. Four days after my love's death, I flew into Tokyo, Japan to face the man who murdered my unborn child, and killed the love of my life. There were no rules to abide by, except ONE. That ONE rule, was the match was to end, when and only when one of the two participants said the words.......I.......QUIT!' Ice's body shifts as he opens up the left side of his coat. From underneath his arm, he pulls out a small suitcase and begins to open it up. Once opening it, Ice pulls out a jet black laptop. The dim glow from the monitor screen lights Ice's face and shows a menacing look. Without looking at the camera, Ice's left hand darts out and snatches the camera by the lens. Violently he pulls it towards the monitor, so that it can see what is on the screen. The screen is still with a picture of Akodo Rokku and Ice. Ice presses a button on the keyboard and the picture begins to play. Rokku grabs Ice by the legs quickly and flips him over and leaps and drives his knee into the back of Ice’s head locking in a Lion Tamer. Ref Brad Jones: Ice! Ice. Do you submit! Do you give up. Ice: I? I? NOOO! STAR!!!!!! AHHHH!!! Rokku Leans even further back bending Ice’s neck at a extremely odd angle.. Ice scrams and tries to fight out of the Lion Tamer then falls limp. Bishop: NO!!!! Sights: Ref Brad Jones has Ice’s arm. One? Two? Three!!!! That’s it We have a new Champion! Ice presses another button on the keyboard and stops the video feed. Angrily he pushes the camera away, and then gently closes the laptop up, placing it back in the small suitcase. Ice sits straight up and smugly tilts his head back slightly, while looking out at the darkness once again. 'If a picture says a thousand words, how many words did we just see there? Let me go over the rules of that match once again. The match was to end, when and ONLY WHEN one of the two participants said the words.......I.......QUIT! Now, after the matches I've been in, the shots to the head I've taken, and the extreme sounds from pyro, music, and the crowds I might be a little hard of hearing. However... the camera shot... just like this one right here!!!' Ice again violentely snatches the camera, and rips it towards his face. Ice turns and gives an extreme close up of a pissed off expression which resides on his face while he begins to speak again. 'Like I said, just like this camera shot, the one of that video footage was right in my face! Clear as day, you would have been able to see me, or hear me say the words which were regulated as the ONLY WAY THE MATCH COULD BE ENDED! But you didn't hear those words come from my mouth. And you didn't see them form from my lips. Because I NEVER SAID THOSE TWO WORDS! So the way I see it, I never lost that match. I never lost MY TITLE.' A long stream of air exits from Ice as his angered expression soon morphs into a cold, unmoved look of focus. Ice turns head head from side to side, and loudly the 'crackle' of his neck popping is heard. Ice's expression doesn't change as he once again pushes the camera away, and turns to stare out into the night sky. He looks over the sky for a few moments, but the eerie silence makes the short moments seem like forever between his words. 'Do you understand the point I am attempting to make? Or do you still not 'get it'?' Ice again begins to scratch the short five o'clock shadow that is begining to form on his pale face, and turns to face the camera once again. While it appears he is 'waiting for an answer' to his rhetorical question. 'What I'm getting at, is like it or not, a picture doesn't lie. I NEVER LOST, therefore, I AM STILL THE MAN TO BEAT AROUND HERE. But the suits don't see me as being 'marketable'. They find me to be rather psychotic, and detrimental to the business itself, because of the violence, and lack of remorse I show with it. So they steal MY TITLE, and give it to someone who fits their standards. And then, after slapping me across the face, the decide to spit on that same face. They give you this video to build up your debut. Then build you up, because you carry that piece of shit pleather strap, with gold spray painted tin on it, calling it 'THE GLOBAL CHAMPIONSHIP', and call yourself 'THE GLOBAL CHAMPION'. Well in case you didn't hear, while venturing into the world of Final Fantasy, this is GLOBAL WRESTLING, and I AM THE TRUE GLOBAL WRESTLING CHAMPION! Not because I got some fancy ass video to build me up. Not because I am strickly a marketing ploy by the suits. No, I AM THE VERY BEST AT WHAT WE DO, because with me, unlike you Seymour........there's NO HYPE NEEDED.' A smug, almost arrogant look crawls across Ice's face. 'You know, it's been a long time........almost too long since I've been forced to inform someone just who they are going to facing. And just where they are facing them at. However, Seymour, you....you seem to be unknowing of that information. You seem to need to know. At Global Reload, your music will hit, you will walk through those curtains, and down that ramp. Then you will step into that ring, surrounded by thousands of fans. All of a sudden, just as you are preparing for battle as you say, the lights will go down, and my music will hit. My name will appear on the Globaltron, and I'll walk down that aisle, up those rings steps, and into the ring. We will be standing twenty-five feet across the ring from one another, and our eyes will lock. It will be at that moment, that the hairs on the back of your neck and on your arms stand up. Goosebumps ravage your body, and the realization of just what you have gotten yourself into will sink in. You will stare into these cold, merciless, blue eyes, and realize that I am not like any other man you have faced before. You will look deeper into my eyes, searching for a soul, but will find nothing of the sort. But as I stare back, I will see the fear as it overtakes you. And then Seymour, you will realizes this is not your FINAL FANTASY. This is not the distant lands you have fought in before. No Seymour.....at Global Reload, you will realize that when you step into that arena, walk down that aisle, and get into that ring, you are in MY WORLD!' Ice snarls at the camera, then leans in so that his cold, merciless, blue eyes are the only thing able to be seen. Suddenly the eyes shut, and the picture goes black.......
