A Meeting With Evil: My Boss

2 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/2/03 The Trickle Of Blood Flowing From Your Mask He is seen standing in front of the office of Salaok. He has his head down and is thinking very hard. The Trickle Of Blood That Wasn’t There Before My Hands Were Laid Upon You He nods his head and grabs the handle with courage and his Cruiserweight title across his shoulder. You Didn’t Think It Was Possible He twists the handle and opens the door. He takes a deep breath and walks inside to face the most powerful man in the GW. You Didn’t Think I Could Make You Bleed Each and every step towards the chair in front of the desk, the twinkle of respect glare in his eyes. That Is Why I Am The Soul Survivor He takes a seat in front of the chair. The leather swivel chair slowly turns around to face him. I Am Sage Sabbot “Hello, Sage.” He nods his head? “Good afternoon. What is next?” He smiles and chuckles. The same chuckle that many have heard here in the GW. The same evil chuckle that is known throughout the whole entire world. For he controls everything nefarious in this world. And that chuckle is not a good sign for the fate of the GW’s heroes. “Next we take care of the rest.” He nods his head and smirks? “What do you have in mind?” “This week at Evolution, we will get rid of Brick, Ely Braxton, and Jesse Briggs.” He shakes his head side to side in confusion? “Ely who’and Jesse what?” “Well what do you expect” We already took care of Silent Wind last week.” “Took care” We exterminated every hope and dream that he will ever have in his life.” He chuckles once again’that chuckle sends chills down your spine. “Exterminated him” That’s an underestimate. I put him in a match against Alexander this week because he needs to rest.” “Great, I get to sit back and watch Alexander bust open that huge gash Vyper and I put in his forehead.” They both share a laugh? “But anyways, we are going to take care of Briggs, Brick, and Braxton this week.” “Whatever you want boss. How are we going to “take care of them”.” “Handicap match.” He smirks? “Brick, Jesse Briggs, and Ely Braxton stepping into the ring against The Nevermore.” “That sounds like fun. I can’t wait to bust open these guys now. My hands have been cold lately and I am in desperate need to warm them up in the blood of Brick, Braxton, or Briggs.” “Oh, and it will warm my heart.” They both laugh? “You know Sage, you have really opened my eyes here lately. You walked out last week and you and Vyper both beat the living hell out of Silent Wind. You busted him open and you demolished him. I couldn’t have done better myself.” “Oh stop Salaok, your making me blush.” He chuckles? “You have reminded me lately why have are such a gem here in The Nevermore. You have captured the Cruiserweight title off of Jay Robson. Not many people have defeated him.” He points to the title on his shoulder. He just smiles and pats the title belt proudly. “You and Vyper beat the hell out of Silent Wind. Again, not many people could have done that.” “Thank you, thank you.” “And this week, this is the third week in a row. Sage, we need your help this week to take care of Brick. He has been a thorn in our side for many years now. He is cocky, arrogant, and just too damn happy.” “Don’t worry Salaok, after Evolution, his smile will be wiped off his face once and for all.” “That is what I like to hear. And Braxton, I want to make him wish he never came back to the GW.” “I can do that in a singles match. A handicap match” Hell, he won’t be wrestling another day in his life.” “And Jesse Briggs, he has been around for quite some time. He has experience and he has the talent. He is possibly our biggest threat.” “You want to hear about a threat” I’ll tell you a threat that will become a promise when the curtain flies back and I enter Evolution. I’m going to make the beating I gave Silent Wind make like child’s play. I’m going to break bones and draw blood. And the blood will flow and stain. You know Salaok, the hardest point won’t be to win the match, it will be to get the blood stains out of my clothing.” He nods and pauses for a moment? “Now Sage, you have been with us for quite some time now. We have taught you everything we know and it seems as if you have learned from it. You have become a heartless bastard over the last couple weeks, and I am very proud of you. Now Sage, I have one last question for you. Why are you in The Nevermore?” “I’ve said it once and I’ll say it again. I am here to eliminate the weak group in the world.” “What do you mean by “weak group?”” “In my opinion, there are two groups of people in the world. There are those who get whatever they please without working for it or lifting a finger. And there are those who bust their asses for what they want. Salaok, we are the group that bust their asses for what they want. And guys like Jesse Briggs, Ely Braxton, and Brick get things for free in life. You see, this angers me and disappoints me. It hasn’t gotten to the point of envy or jealousy, but it surely angers me. And that is why I want to eliminate these guys once and for all from the face of the planet. Salaok, Evolution is coming and I am counting the days, the hours, and the minutes until it is time. I am counting every second until I throw that curtain back and step out in front of the peons that surround us and take care of these posers once and for all. Then I will show them what real work is, because they will have to bust their asses to survive in this match. But they won’t survive. You know why” Because I am The Soul Survivor, and that is my job to survive. Why? Because it is what I am good at and it is what I am known for. I survived over the past couple weeks in a ladder match against Robson. I survived in a match against Silent Wind. Forget the so-called help I received, and remember who grabbed that title down from the heavens above. Remember who swung that steel chair at Silent Wind’s face last week. Because in the future, you will remember my name. And you will never forget the things I have done and the live I have changed. Why? Because I am Sage Sabbot.” He pauses for a moment. He lifts his hands and entangles them together and thinks? “Good answer Sage.” “And this little dream that I have will become reality Salaok.” “Calm down, calm down’I believe you. Take a deep breath and get ready for the ride of your lifetime, because at Evolution, we will drop Brick, Braxton, and Briggs off at the nearest hospital.” They both smirk and he chuckles? “I’m more than ready.” He nods his head and they both smile as the?
