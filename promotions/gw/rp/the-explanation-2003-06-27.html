The Explanation

27 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/27/03 7**Here it is, the moment of truth. No turning back now. Tonight I will show the Salamone family and most importantly myself that I have what it takes to be a mobster, that I have what it takes to be a hitman, and that I have what it takes to be Sean Price. The Appex, The Natural, The Mobster, all of these are nothing more than names used to describe me but now is my chance to show that they all have meaning. Because that’s what life is all about isn’t it? Just a big search for meaning? And once we have found it what do we do with it? Now is my chance to find out.** (As the scene fades in we see Paulie leaning up against his car at the Pier. Suddenly a blue volkswagon beatle comes driving up. Paulie reaches inside his coat pocket and pulls out a gun. He points it at the approaching car. The car suddenly stops, the driver side door opens up and out steps Sean Price.) “Whoa calm down Paulie its just me. It’s Sean.” “Sean” What the hell are you doing in that car?” replies Paulie with an obvious bit of hesitation and confusion in his voice. “Well I figured If I drove my car here and something were to possibly go wrong with tonight’s job that the cops could trace my car back to me. So instead I’m using a car that isn’t mine.” “Well a cop can trace that car back to the dealer you rented it from.” “I didn’t rent it from any dealer.” “A friend?” “Nope.” “Well then where the hell did you get it?” “On Broad and Market Street, I was walking out of the coffee shop earlier and I saw the car and I figured it might come in handy tonight so I walked upto the driver side door, acted like I was reaching for my keys but I was really picking the lock. After a few seconds I got in the car, hot wired it and here we are.” “Boosting cars’you boost cars?” questions a somewhat delighted Paulie. “Well its not my profession but it’s something I learned to do when I was younger. You never know when these things will come in handy.” “That’s true. Ok enough talking, we have a job to do. Where are the supplies?” “Right here.” (Sean reaches into the back seat of the Beatle and pulls out a black duffle bag. He then shuts the car door and walks over to Paulie and hands him the bag. Paulie sets it out on the hood of his car and then unzips the bag. Paulie reaches in and moves around some of the items to see exactly what it is Sean got. He then zips the bag back up and hands it back to Sean.) “Good job. Now do you have a piece on you right now?” “Yeah, I have a Colt 1911, I always carry one with me.” “Good. You ready?” “I was born ready.” “Good to hear. Cause now I have to explain to you exactly what the job is that we have to perform.” “I’m listening.” “Ok, for the past year the Don has been running protection for Leritz Jewelers.” “Why would they need protection” They are a well known business?” “I’m getting to that, hold on. Now the reason they need protection is because behind the scenes Leritz Jewelers deals with a lot more than rings and necklaces and such. They are also one of the biggest underground drug smugglers on the west coast. Specializing in Heroine. Now a few weeks ago Leritz decided they were no longer going to pay protection money to the Don. And the Don doesn’t like that. So now it is our job to go in there, get the money they owe us and give Leritz a warning that they will never forget. And make sure that they continue with their payments to the Don.” “Ok, but that doesn’t explain why we are here at the Pier instead of Leritz Jewelers.” “Because tonight is Saturday night, and every Saturday night Leritz get’s another shipment of Heroine in from wherever it is they get it. It’s much easier for us to cut off the shipment before it actually gets to the shop you see?” “Yeah Paulie I got ya.” “Good. Now in 10 minutes two trucks will pass by, the second of the two trucks will stop for a minute to talk to one of the pier guards to find out what the rotation is for the night. When that happens we have to quickly but quietly get on the back of that truck so that when it pulls into the dock we can get out and do our business.” “Ok Paulie, sounds good.” “Good. So now all we have to do is sit and wait.” (The scene fades out as Paulie and Sean hide behind a set of wooden crates waiting for the two trucks to pass by.)
