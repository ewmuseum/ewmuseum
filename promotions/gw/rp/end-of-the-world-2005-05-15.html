End of the World

15 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/15/05 'Ouch!' Mindy sniffled in pain as she placed the large ice pack on her head. After weeks and weeks of relentless assaults her body had gone through, it all finally caught up with her that night after Evolution. Nariko- or Dawn, whichever she preferred to be called- had done enough of a number on Mindy's head to have her seeing stars well after the match. Sure, she'd taken a heavy amount of bumps over her days in Global, but they hadn't truly caught up to her until that moment. She hoped that perhaps she could just shake off the pain like always; use the leftover adrenaline from her match to take her mind off the hurt, but it was no use. No matter how hard she tried, there were just things that needed healing beyond the mere bruises on her skin. 'Mikey...' 'Hey, kiddo,' a voice called from outside Mindy's locker room door, 'are you in there?' And as if he could hear his cousin's very thoughts, he stepped into the locker room slowly. 'Hm, not feeling well?' Mindy frowned and shook her head. 'My head is killin' me,' she pouted. There was, again, more to the pain than a mere headache, but she said nothing. Best not to get Michael worked up about things, she figured. He had Jaden on his mind as well, and she was so much more important to him than she was to Mindy, so surely Mindy didn't have to mention the whole thing once again. 'You want us to leave?' Michael asked. 'You're all ready to go, right?' Mindy quickly nodded her head, and with that movement, she began to feel slightly dizzy. 'Uh yeah.. yeah, Mikey, let's go home.' Mindy looked up and locked eyes with someone, but he wasn't her cousin. 'Nn...' Mindy rubbed at her forehead; her first instinct was that she was seeing double, as she could not make out the features on either of the two men in front of her, she was so groggy. 'Maybe you should take her to the hospital instead,' another voice stated, and it was then that Mindy realized that there were indeed two different people talking to her. She immediately shot to her feet in protest. 'No,' she snapped back, 'I'd feel much better resting in my own bed than on some cold examination table, or whatever those things are that they make patients rest on.' The unknown man sighed and turned towards Michael. He then continued to make his point, with complete disregard for the words Mindy had just spoke. 'It would be better for her to be in a hospital anyway, instead of leaving her alone in your house. Those men, they do know where you live, you said?' 'I won't be alone!' Mindy cried, oblivious to who the other figure was. He sure was attempting to shoot down Mindy's idea, but if he was with Michael, he couldn't have been as bad as he was letting on. Either way, all she wanted was to make sure that she wasn't stuck by herself in any place other than the safety of her home. 'My boyfriend would be...' Michael's concerned eyes went from the floor to Mindy once she trailed off. She was still somewhat confused on what to make of her relationship with Markus, although that was the least of everyone's worries. Noting this, she snapped out of her daze and continued. 'Markus would be there to watch me. Please, Mikey.. I'll be fine at home. I don't ever want to go to the hospital again...' And rightfully so; the last time Mindy was in a hospital, it wasn't the greatest experience in the world. Hell, none of her experiences in a hospital had ever been positive, nor did she know anyone who ever did have a good time in one. Who possibly could? Michael had no time to think about the decision. He had more important things to take care of; and the easiest way to deduct an answer was to think of the best place that Mindy would be safe. 'Just stay there overnight, okay? Stay at the hospital overnight and come home in the morning.' Mindy fell silent, not knowing what else to say. Of course, she could understand where Michael was coming from in making the decision that he did, but that didn't necessarily make it any easier to take. Defeated, her head was lowered in shame. Not the hospital again... just anywhere but the hospital... ~~~ 'How did I give in to letting Michael take me to the hospital?' Mindy shuddered in freezing stiffness as she curled up into a ball on the freezing hospital 'bed', in a futile attempt to stay warm. The air coniditioner nearby had left Mindy chilled to the bone, and the paper thin sheet over her body left no shelter from the cold. However, through the uncomfortable feeling in the air and the pounding headache that refused to go way, Mindy was going to try and get as much sleep as possible. Soon it would be morning and she would get to go home; it was just a matter of getting through the night. It wasn't as easy a task as one would think it to be. 'Okay. All I have to do is keep my mind off Mikey and Jaden and Gabi and everyone for just a few hours. Alright. Maybe counting would work... yeah, that always works. One, two, three, four, five, six, seven... geez, my head hasn't felt this bad before. Stupid Dawn. Stupid ninjas and their stupid sneakiness.' With a huff, Mindy switched onto her other side. 'Who's that guy I have to beat now? Jake Hix? Gods... that guy's like, really huge and stuff, and he really seems to like Vanilla Ice... and now I'm even colder!' She promptly rolled back onto her right side and brought her knees closer to her chest. '...This is really tougher than I thought it would be.' Then there was a knock at the door; things were only going to get tougher. 'Who is it?' Mindy called as she lazily flopped over to face the door, which slowly creaked open. 'Mindy?' a female voice questioned, before the face that it belonged to had peeked in through the small opening. Mindy recognized it, but only after squinting her eyes and studying the features for a long couple of seconds. God, she hated dizziness. 'Oh.. hi, Noi.' The GW reporter set foot inside of the room, softly closing the door behind her. 'Hm. So, what brings you here?' Noi pulled up an uncomfortable looking folding chair and sat down, shrugging her shoulders in indifference. 'Just wondering what was wrong. I don't remember you having to make any trips to the hospital before, so I was hoping I could find out what was wrong. I'm sorry if this is a bad time or anything, but you know how it is.. reporters are always looking to catch a fresh story before anyone else.' Mindy wished she could laugh at that remark, but all it did was make her annoyed. She didn't want to have to go through an interview session this late at night- or early in the morning, if you look at it that way- if she actually wanted her head close to exploding from pain, Mindy would have went back to Dawn McFarlane for another beating. With a low groan, Mindy managed to exhale in response. 'Well, what is it that ya wanna know?' Noi smiled. 'I was just hoping I could have some information on your condition.. like will you be ready for your title defense against Jake Hix at Wrestlecade?' Mindy winced; even in her weakened state, she anticipated that question from a mile away. 'I should be fine,' she nodded slightly, 'I only checked in because Mikey forced me to... but I'll be perfectly able to defend my lil' title at Wrestlecade, yeah.' 'Are you at all worried about Jake?' Noi prodded further. 'He's been on a roll ever since he came to GW.. some might be saying he's your toughest challenge yet.' Mindy was worried. She couldn't say otherwise and be truthful about it; what woman could? She was a petite five foot five and must have weighed a feather over a hundred pounds soaking wet. Jake Hix looked like a natural athlete; probably the star QB in high school, or something of that sort. It wouldn't have surprised Mindy if that was the case. Whether she was right about that or not, however, didn't change the fact that she would soon be in the same ring as a three hundred pound mountain of a man. And she was scared out of her mind at that thought. 'Oh, of course I am,' Mindy stammered, 'I mean.. I certainly have other things I got on mah mind, and there's tons of other issues I'm quite worried about to say the least... but yeh, having to go up against a guy who happens to be three times mah size and wants to make everybody his bitch, I would be foolish if I weren't at least the teensiest bit worried.' Mindy stopped momentarily, but amidst the silence she was reminded of something. 'Heh.. but then again, I get worried every time I wrestle, so it shouldn't be saying much to tell you that I'm worried now.' 'Really?' The normally aggressive GW reporter leaned back in her seat, legitimately surprised by Mindy's comment. 'But you've been working for Global for nearly six months and you've yet to be pinned in singles competition.' 'That's true, too.. but the only reason that's the case is because I worry.' The young champion had a point there, and it left Noi nodding her head in agreement. 'I'd be kidding myself if I said I had some real talent... the only reasons I've had my cute title for so long is 'cause of three lil' things. Heart, instinct... and just a smidgen of luck.' It couldn't get much more simple than that. Sure, Mindy had some wrestling talent, even if she refused to admit it. Still, however, she relied on her emotion to pull her through every match. Up to that point, it worked. Whether heart would work on a man two hundred pounds heavier than her, though, remained to be seen. Yet however, that wasn't important. Mindy's boyfriend was important. Mindy's family was important. Mindy's well being was important. Mindy's Television title... it was an award; one she looked at as though it were an insult. Did holding that title really mean enough to her to stay in the hospital for? 'I don't feel very lucky having to spend the night here, though.' 'I'm sorry,' Noi started. 'I can assume you wouldn't want anyone bothering you with questions like this while you're trying to rest, either, huh?' Mindy hesitated. Even though she would be quite justified in asking the woman to leave, all she could muster was a small shrug. 'No problem, you deserve your rest. I'll leave you be.' Noi stood up to leave, and Mindy sighed in relief. She was beginning to roll over to turn her back on the reporter, but something stopped her. 'Ugh, I'm sorry, can I just ask one more question?' Frowning, Mindy craned her head back in miss Tsunami's direction- as much as it hurt her head to do so. 'Ask away.' 'Well... I don't know how to ask this in a polite manner, so I'm just going to be blunt. Your involvement with the police.. does that have anything to do with the man who appeared with you and Michael at Evolution tonight?' Mindy blinked in confusion. Police? Man with her and Michael? She could barely remember any of that stuff, and she definitely didn't want to talk about it even if she did. 'I'm sorry.. I truly don't...' That was when she remembered the man from her locker room before. The unknown man sighed and turned towards Michael. He then continued to make his point, with complete disregard for the words Mindy had just spoke. 'It would be better for her to be in a hospital anyway, instead of leaving her alone in your house. Those men, they do know where you live, you said?' 'I won't be alone!' Mindy cried, oblivious to who the other figure was. 'That man...' she whispered to herself. Noi heard her, but didn't make out what was said. 'Excuse me?' Mindy's green eyes remained open wide for a second, before they blinked and the teenager snapped out of her momentary trance. 'N-nothing. I never seen that man before, and I never had any talks with the police. My cousin is the one you should be talking to about that, it's his problem.' 'Oh...' Noi trailed off slightly, right before getting back on track. 'But you have an idea why you and your cousin's names were in the paper about having to go to the police, right? I mean you must know, since you two are both quite...' The brazen reporter soon realized how prying she was being. Mindy noticed it, too; and she didn't seem very happy with it. 'Sorry.' Mindy stared at the woman who stood by her bed. If she weren't in such pain, Mindy would have stood up and punched Noi's lights out, but all she did instead was look down at the tiled floor. 'I'd feel better if I got some sleep,' she stated finally, 'I've had a very rough night.' 'I understand.' Noi gave a slight bow of her head in thanks. And then, she was out the door just like that. The black and pink haired vixen sighed and blew her long bangs away from her eyes. She glanced up at the LCD clock on the wall that faced her. 'Two thirty... Gods, I'm never going to get to sleep, am I?' Even as she debated her chances at slumber, Mindy closed her eyes and went back to her fetal position on the bed. Alas, the second Mindy went still and tried to give in to the fatigue, Mindy's thoughts overtook her. 'What if that man hurts me even more than Dawny did tonight? Ugh.. what if Jaden and Gabi are hurt, too? Please... however bad I'm hurt, please let Jaden and Gabi be okay. I'll go through whatever pain I have to, just let them be okay...' 'They're going to be fine.. they have to be. Michael's going to find them and they are gonna be just fine...' 'I wish I had someone I could listen to... I wish I had a real father...' 'AH!' Mindy's thoughts led to her dream, and the dream turned into one massive nightmare. Her eyes welled up with tears as she sat up in one swift motion. The memories had gone so deep that they couldn't even be considered memories anymore; men had become hideous creatures that wanted nothing more of little Mindy than to take her life. Helpless bystanders that remain silent in apathy; without a care for the woman in the middle. But how... '..how did I remember that?' She was so young, it was amazing that she caught so much as a glimpse of what she did of that nightmare. With her streak of luck outside of the ring, Mindy wouldn't have been surprised if she had woken up and forgotten everything. But yet to her amazement, she found herself only digging for more past memories. Those days, before Verona... 'Sister Caprica, why did God take away my mommy and daddy?' the young girl asked, on the verge of tears. The middle-aged nun who stood before her soon dropped to one knee and smiled faintly. 'What do you mean, Mindy? God did not do such a thing... you still remember them, don't you?' The tiny five year old nodded her head quickly. 'I sure do. I always will!' 'Then as long as you have those memories, you do not have to worry about God ever taking your parents away from you. I promise.' Mindy blinked, and her right eye shed a tear down her pale cheek. Memories were not enough for the little girl, and Sister Caprica gave Mindy a gentle hug in hopes that maybe it would calm the girl's spirits. 'They will always be with you, dear child. Never forget that.' However, somehow, Mindy had sadly forgotten. The bitter times in Verona, with a perpetually angry stepfather and uncaring stepmother took away every memory from her childhood; buried them in sorrow and defeat. The wishes of seeing her real mother and father again as their own Mindy were taken away by the suicidal temptation that came with every day she lived as the Rosalina that no one gave a damn about. Who her true parents were, Mindy would never even figure out. What had triggered the memory of that conversation in the first place? Was it the sufficient blows to the head she sustained in her match with Dawn? Was it her cousin's own family problems that caused her to reminisce about the family of her own? Or was it just God's way of compensation over the dreadfully awful few weeks that led up to her odd revelation that night? Mindy looked down at her bruised and battered hands, beaten so soft shades of purple made their mark all over. They didn't hurt her physically, though; it was the sight of them that dragged her into heart wrenching sobs. 'I don't want to be here!' the GW Television champion screamed, the hands that helped her win numerous matches had entangled themselves in her hair, threatening to pull strands out at the roots. For those last five or six months, Mindy was a competitor with a heart as big as any other in Global Wrestling, if not the biggest. It was a heart that belonged to the very same girl who cried out at that very moment, 'I want my mommy and daddy...'
