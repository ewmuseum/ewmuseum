The Silent Angel of War Pt.5

6 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 06, 2005 The Silent Angel of War Pt.5 A leap of fate. That’s all it takes. From mind to matter, you, the recipient has to do that simple action, and then you’ll make ripples into waves. Crippling the doubts like bear traps do to ankles, and in a period of a few moments, your life will change? drastically. This is what I fight for? what I live for? and what I’ll surely die for. A wounded man, scarred by the anger of another, lies beneath the shadows. His eyes slightly open, as they narrow to look at the detail of his chest wounds in the dark. Despite the worries of them, his face is emotionless. The brown goatee upon his chin speaks volumes of his history in this prison, for it hangs near half a foot past his chin, and is shaggy from the unkemptness it has been given. Ah? He gasps as the thought flood in. It’s almost like? the world is coming to an end. Yet, I don’t have any control over my life. And despite my deepest efforts, I’ll fall short no matter what. He sighs. Brushing his hair back from his face, his shaking hands tie the hair back in a ponytail. He rests his hands on his thighs and closes his eyes, to fade off into his own mind of self-punishment. This feeling reminds me so much of the past. How I lost everything, and I felt so empty? I feel exactly like that now. As if this room could cave in, and the first thing I’d do would be to thank god for doing me the favor? His head leans back against the wall, as his lips purse before he licks them with his parched tongue. His throat gulps for the spit to come back down as he stares up at the ceiling. A dark cloudy mist is what looks to be lurking near the top of the room, but to Jesse’s knowledge, not a nook or cranny of the room had the ability to shine light through. He was literally in a carved block of cement, left to rot, if not go insane first. Forgetting everything we’ve learned in the past, I realize now that its all worthless. Nothing can prepare you for this humiliation I’ve gone through. Or this suffering? and definitely not the, sickness. His eyes narrow again as his face cringes from the thought of the sickness in him, that eats away day by day. The virus in which he has no cure for, nor cares to find one, for it’s effect would only make him half mental-case he is currently. I can accept death. At least with death I know theirs a new beginning. Yet, this? I can’t see myself forgetting this anytime soon. This is like a nightmare that continues on forever, without any knowing of where it left off the night before. The pain it causes, almost bearable? if I were inhuman that is. Biting his lip, he breaks through the skin, letting droplets of blood trickle down onto his tongue. The sensation of liquid flowing through his dehydrated mouth is now fulfilled, even if it’s just a replenishing of his own body fluids. “Besides all that though. I know theirs something left for me. Otherwise, I wouldn’t have hung around for all this BS. So, god damn it, please don’t leave me die in this pit of shit. Please” His eyes shut again, as he sucks on his lip until the punctured skin finally heals, and he has nothing but sleep to do. *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** “You seem to be in good health?” A doctor opens up the buttoned up shirt Jesse wears, and feels his chest with the metal piece of the heart beat instrument they use in doctor offices. His face gives off some odd facial expressions before he nods his head and pulls the instrument off of Jesse’s chest and lets it hang from his neck. “You are all right on the insides anyway” Now why don’t you take off your shirt so I can see those scars I was told to be treating.” Lifting his shirt up, the doctor looks at him observing quietly. The chest of Jesse’s is clear, not any wounds or cuts visible on him. The doctor glances back at TBC and raises an eyebrow. “Is this suppose to be a joke?” TBC grins, “No, not at all doctor.” “Then do you care to explain?” begins the doctor in his quest for answers. Told to stand up, Jesse does. His back is examined, as again is his chest, and then his face where the cheek had supposedly been snapped at with the leather whip. “Tell me your kidding. This man has not one scratch on him” Your pulling my chain aren’t you?” the doctor says now half smiling, half laughing at the situation. Shaking his head “no” TBC does, and then turns to Jesse and nods for him to speak. Clearing his throat, a lump is seen sucked in by the Jackal as he sits back down and puts his hands on his thighs. “I was injured. I felt the wounds last night. They were real?” “But” theirs no proof!” the doctor claims, as he raises his voice. Shaking his head to the words does the Jackal, “Listen” the pain was too real to be fake! Either accept that I’m a freak, or? get over yourself and look harder!” Grinning, TBC walks up to the doctor and hands him a clipboard with information of all the tests and chemical in Jesse’s body. “Oh my god” he’s unstable! He could die! What the hell are you doing with him sir?” panics the doctor as he looks at the chart through his bottle cap glasses. TBC’s grin becomes larger as the doctor drops the chart on the table and sits down staring at the jackal in complete shock. “He” he’s inhuman.” The jackal looks up from the floor to the doctor and then to the grin of TBC, and realizes? he isn’t normal, anymore. *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** It’s not over yet. The world is yet to even realize the true potential behind the man that they know and see on a weekly basis. His eyes flood their worlds like sex, drugs, and alcohol do their nights. His determination and heart is a mere replication of their desire and dreams to be like him one day. His long dark auburn hair ignites them with malicious thoughts of the medieval times they read in books. Yet nothing, compares to what they haven’t seen. This is my sorrow filling up in me. This is my hate boiling through my finger tips. This is my love bursting through my veins? this is, surreal. His head tilts back as he leans in the chair against the cement wall. The jackal peers across the table at the man he despises more than the devil himself, TBC. The grin of earlier still remains, yet now more sinister. His mind twisted for sure, even through the facial gesture could that comment be made, yet deep down, Jesse knows theirs more of that sickness that has yet to spill. “What do you want?” as TBC right before his grin fades. “Want” I think the question is, what can I have?” counters the Jackal, making sure that there is no loop holes before he gets too deep into the conversation. He sits down and plants his hands on the desk as he stares into the eyes of the jackal. His hands come together, forming an odd circle as his fingers interlace. “I’m offering you something you want” more than life itself, you could say,” mutters TBC. Sighing as he leans forward and keeps his head raised as he looks at the ceiling instead of TBC’s eyes, “You know, you’re really good at lying to people. What makes you think I’m going to fall for something like that?” TBC clears his throat before reaching into his pocket. He pulls out a pile of pictures wrapped in a rubber band. One side has pictures, as the other side is black with a white boarder. Jesse reaches for them, but his fingers are smacked by TBC. “Now, in return for the pictures, I want you to do exactly what I want. If you play your cards right” you’ll get to see-? Like lightning striking twice, Jesse’s face goes blank but then shocked as his eyes widen, and his jaw drops, letting his tongue be seen just lying in his mouth motionless. “Speechless, I know. Well, you’re not going to want to be speechless tomorrow, so I’m going to make this agreement with you and you never have to say a word.” Nodding, Jesse clothes his mouth and stares at the pictures, just waiting to get to see them. “You do these last three side projects for me, and I let you two walk out of here scotch-free” You don’t, and I leave you to rest in the room next to her, where you’ll both rot and die, smelling one of the others rotting corpse. So? do what I say, and you walk out of here like this never happened. Shake on it.” Extending his hand, Jesse hesitates, but then seeing the stack of pictures he extends his hand and grasps TBC’s, shaking it quickly, and then reaching for the pictures. Just as his fingers grace the delicate top of the stack, TBC puts his hand down on Jesse’s. “Remember, revenge is a bitch Jesse. Don’t try to fuck me over, I’ve got eyes in the back of my head.” Nodding, Jesse doesn’t care. He waits as TBC’s hand moves and he grabs the pictures sitting down and spreading them out all over the table as he looks at them. TBC smiles and turns around leaving the room. *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** Sitting against the wall, his eyes glance at the floor as her memories splash into his mind like a tidal wave. Cramming all the emotions at once, his tears come out, but he has a look of disbelief on his face instead of a frown. In his left hand he grasps the picture of his wife when they had first met. Her smile makes him repeat something he once said to her? a poem, of memory, and of secret. “I Sit here Awake In a Quiet State Some think I can’t Hear Yet I just Choose when To listen I tend To distance From thee As a Notion not A plee Inside my Organs Turn black One more Thought of Death Then I’ll Be surely Possessed Those who Think its Easy To be Where I am Need a Dose of Reality There are No rules To stop What I’ll Do When I’m alone Theres No fate Strong enough To take Me out Of this Pure malevolence Lair I Call my? Mind? Jesse sits against the wall, his eyes close, the tears roll off his cheeks, and once again he rests in his world of paradise, or nightmare, whichever invades him first.
