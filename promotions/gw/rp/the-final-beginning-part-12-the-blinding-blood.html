The Final Beginning. Part 12 - The Blinding Blood

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: ... The sky was beginning to lighten even though the sun hadn’t risen over the distant mountains yet. Soon however the sun would appear and everything it touched would be golden. All apart from one man. A man who sits in the long grass with eyes of pure white staring off into the nothingness that had taken over his world in the last twenty four hours. His wife and unborn child had just been murdered at the hands of his younger half brother. A brother that was never wanted and never should have been allowed to walk amongst them. Never should have allowed him to dishonor the Harvester title in which he was awarded. The Harvester of Blood had taken the life of the woman who had held the group together. Mani had shot and killed Lisha Davis. The wife of Salaok. The mother of Darrell The Sister-in-law to Justin Marks and Eve Davis. The God-mother of Emmanuell Marks. The brother of Mephiston. Lisha had been everything to Salaok and with the news of her pregnancy had reached him, there was no joy more powerful in the world that what he was feeling. But now all of that was gone. The sun edges closer towards the horizon and more light is placed on the earth. Salaok’s hair, all over the place as if he hadn’t stopped ripping at it all night long, soon found his hand running backwards through it to get the black strands out of his face. A face that was cold once again. His rocking back and forth with his arms folded over his chest was a clear warning to anyone in the area not to come near him. Not that anyone was near. He was alone. Alone. All he had left now was his child. Not even enough to call a family of his own. Family existed no more for it had been family who had done this to him. His brother-in-law arranging everything. His brother firing the gun. His fumbling hands reached down into his jacket pocket revealing the one thing that had always been used to calm Salaok but no amount of nicotine would calm him now. The packet of cigarette shake as he tries to pull a single white stick from the cardboard packaging but his hands shake too much and can not get a grip. Growing frustrated quickly, he tips the packet up-side-down and empties the cigarettes onto the ground before him. Not caring which one he grabs, he doesn’t look at them as his hand moves across the grass and finds a cigarette. His hands shaking still, move up to his mouth and lets his lips grab a hold of the filter end of the cancer causing habit. His zippo was where it always was, tucked into the right-side pocket of his pants and he reaches in and pulls it out. Flipping the lid open he fumbles with it until finally getting a flame and bringing it to the cigarette. With the send of a rabbit stuck in the hunt by a pack of wild dogs, the flame takes a hold of the cigarette and Salaok sucks it down, needing it. No longer could he claim that he only wanted to smoke, he now knew more than ever that he needed it. She was gone. That thought running through his mind. But not just her, but his unborn child as well. The cold of the night was clearly taking effect as he had been there all night long since walking out of the arena for the last time. The doctor that he had gone to see once had told him that when he felt out of control, that he had to go and find a happy place. A place that meant something to him. A place that had memories and would allow him to go back to those memories and escape the real world if only for a few minute. This happy place was one that wasn’t letting him escape. This happy place is the same place that Salaok took Lisha to on the morning he would propose to her. It was standing right at the very spot where Salaok now sitting rocking back and forth, that he got down on bended knee and asked Lisha the question that would be answered with a smile and a nod. A question that would end up sealing her fate as it was only through being married to Salaok that she was shot. It was his fault that she was dead and this place was doing nothing to help him get over that fact. The sounds of footsteps. Growing nearer from behind him. He turns to look at who has found him but never should have. As the thick branch which had fallen from a tree a few feet away swings through the air with the full power of the man who holds it. The sound of the air parting as it flies towards him is all that can be heard until the sickening crack. The crack being from the branch breaking or Salaok’s jaw snapping? Like some sort of old fashion movie, Salaok’s all ready sitting body mimics that of a tree fallen in the woods as it starts off slow and spends up as it nears the ground. His head which had already snapped to the side with the force of the deathly blow slams into the ground and bounces as blood flies from his monstrous gash where the branch had come into contract with his skin. His eyes remain open but only for a few seconds as all he can see is the thick long grass and the cigarettes he had poured out only a minute ago. His hand lays a few inches away from his face and he tries to move it. Wanting to move his hand to his face to feel the damage or to push himself back to his feet but it wouldn’t move. Nothing would move for him. And now his eyes slowly begin to close. Like a curtain lowering of the final performance of a long running stage show, his eye lids seem to lower in slow motion until finally they black out the world that had finally turned it’s back on him. Wanting to simply lay down and die in order to be with Lisha again, he lets the darkness consume him. Lets the darkness take over and soon it isn’t long until he sees the light off in the distance that everybody says you see on your death bed. However this light wasn’t that one. Wasn’t the light he was hoping for. His eyes were opening and the light he could see was the sun peaking over the horizon. Not fully exposing itself, but still enough to make a difference and take some of the chill out of the air as each particle begins to warm.<P.His disappointment in being alive is soon replaced by a pain too instance to describe. It was almost like he had been shot through the hand and upon looking at his left hand, he could see it was far worse than that. He hand was nailed to a plank of wood with a huge wooden spike covered in blood. Clearly this was a symbol and he knew who it would be without even having to turn his head to look at the man casting the shadow over him. Justin Marks. And sure enough as he turned his head to look at his attacker, he sees Justin standing there with a grin on his face and the sun behind him, giving him an angelic glow. False but still a glow. Justin says nothing at first but simply looks down at Salaok as the Harvester of Fear begins to awaken fully and the pain sets in. But not wanting to give Justin the pleasure of knowing that he has won, Salaok shows no pain. It takes every piece of concentration and trying to remember everything his father had taught him in the School of Pain to show nothing but he does so. But just as he conquers that pain, Justin moves in to finish his masterpiece. Salaok’s right hand had been tied down into the perfect places and couldn’t be moved. Justin had clearly putt a lot of thought into this and seemed content with what he was finally doing. Kneeling down and placing his knee on Salaok’s right forearm just to make sure he doesn’t move, Justin looks at him before preaching to Salaok in a calm and disturbing manner. “You know brother, it never had to be like this. Your are the one who sent me on this biblical war and now it can finally end.” He holds the wooden spike in the center of Salaok’s right hand and raises the hammer high above his head. He holds it there for a brief second as he continues to talk at Salaok. “I changed your water into wine some time ago and you never got the hint. Now you’ve had your Last Supper and today you will be crucified.” The hammer comes down with force and speed connecting perfectly on the top of the wooden spike and this time Salaok can not hold off showing the pain. As the skin breaks and the small bones inside of the hand break one by one, Salaok’s back arches and a howl roars forth from his lungs. The sound of Salaok’s scream echoes through the empty hillsides and no one is around to hear him. There is no blood splatters just yet. There never is one the initial impact, but every hit after that brings blood like rain. Again the hammer is raised into the air and brought down, this time with move power and it again connects with the wooden spike. The blood falls like rain falling from the sky. Only today there was no clouds, this was his blood, which splashes onto his face. His nerves have been destroyed and his fingers begin to show it as they flinch, causing his hand to opening and close around the spike with speed but slowly begin to slow and then stop. But not before Justin raises the hammer again and brings it down. This time missing the spike and bringing it down straight on top of the fingers, snapping each one with the force and bone breaks through the skin. His hand was beginning to look move of something from a movie’s special effects unit but this was real. His entire body was now shaking but that wasn’t enough to stop Justin who would raise and drop the hammer down once more onto the wooden spike sending it further into the wooden plank he had been laid on. Blood by now was everywhere and his hand was no longer a living object, but a motionless object that propelled pain through Salaok’s body. Justin didn’t seem happy enough yet as he brings the hammer up above his head once more and this time grabbing a hold of it with both hands. Just before bringing the hammer down, he shifts his position and brings the hammer straight down onto Salaok’s exposed forearm shattering the bones and tearing a hole in the skin. Wanting so badly to escape the pain, Salaok turns his body but finds it only more painful as it rips at the nails holding him in place. Trapped. Finally the demise of Salaok at Justin’s hands was now. The moment in time that Justin had been waiting for, for so many years was finally at hand. But Justin isn’t happy yet and walks away from Salaok for a brief moment only to return with a smile on his face as he speaks. “I have a present for you dear brother-in-law. Do you want to know what it is?” Wanting to prove to Justin that nothing he did would render him dead, he must speak. With a voice strained with pain and a jaw broken, he begins to hiss out the words to the man he hates more than any other in the world. “You are too kind Justin. What did you bring me?” Mocking Justin and refusing to give in, Salaok breaks a smile on his blood drenched lips. Justin moves his hands from behind his back to reveal the final touch to his masterpiece. A ring a thorns which he holds out with both hand to Salaok like a gift.“I brought you your crown dear sir. May I place it upon your head?” Not waiting for a response, Justin walks forward and put the crown upon Salaok head and forces it down making sure as many of the thorns stick into his scalp as possible. He gives the crown a small twist once it is in place to ensure the thorns drag and cut open his skin causing more blood to spill from the already drained body of the Harvester of Fear. Justin takes a few steps back to look at Salaok on the ground helpless and completely under his control as the sun breaks free from the horizon and instantly the land lights up even more with the golden beams that now blind Salaok. Using a rope free over a branch of a tree tied to the top of the giant wooden crucifix, Justin begins to raise the structure into place. The pain increasing with every degree that the angle of the cross changes as more and more pressure is placed on the nailed down limbs. Justin begins to speak again as the cross nears it final resting place. “Hail Mary, full of grace, The Lord is with thee. Blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb, Jesus. Holy Mary, mother of God, Pray for us sinners, now and at the hour of our Death. Amen?” The look in Justin’s eye as the cross finally locks into place is one of satisfaction and yet one of knowledge of what he is doing. A calm knowing, a hunting knowing. Blood dripping from his hands and head, he hangs there, squinting through the blood and looking through the burning rays of light. A second figure. That makes sense as there was no way that Justin would have been able to do all of this on his own. There wasn’t enough time and more importantly, there was too much involved for Justin to have brought all of this to the mountaintop on his own. He would have needed help, but help from anyone but the man who Salaok is trying to make out. A figure that as his eyes adjust to the burning sensation of the sun and the stinging of the blood, gains more and more detail. Older and yet still the same. Those eyes?.. Those eyes, were his. As the man stands there looking at Salaok, he puts one hand on the shoulder of Justin Marks, and the other comes up to his face to rub his goatee. Those tattoos?.. Those tattoos were his. “PAIN” on the right knuckles. “LAIR” on the left knuckles. It wasn’t the fact that his blood was draining out of his hands and his forehead that made his face go white, but the mere shock at who this man was that was smiling at him. Salaok eyes open wide in horror and allow the blood to flood into them, stinging and causing more pain. Wanting more pain. Deserving more pain. The man that Johnathon had order to be killed. The man that Quartz had killed, or at least was meant to. The man speaks a in tone of voice that Salaok had come to know so well as his own. “Hello son.” That word. A word he never expected to be called again in this lifetime. Closing his eyes, a blood tear runs down his cheek and is collected in his long black goatee as he tilts his head back and lets out an all mighty scream of pain. His face screwing up in the force of the pain and his jaw now trembling with the power of the scream. The sound bellows from deep within him, from the depths of one’s soul. Silence. As quickly as the scream had filled the air, Salaok cuts it off and allows his eyes to open again. Slowly the corner of his mouth begins to curl upwards and his teeth begin to show in a sick and sadist smile. His normally off-white colored teeth are now pink as the blood and saliva mix together and they drip like a rabid dog’s. Lowering his head, he breathes out and looks straight at his father and brother-in-law. Letting out a deep sigh, which sends blood flying out of his mouth, he speaks his only words to his father. “Forgive me father.” It isn’t a question, but a statement. A quote! But there is no response from Chris Davis other than to turn his back on his son and begin to walk away. Justin begins to do the same but stops and turns back to Salaok and crosses his chest, mouthing the words “In the name of the Father, the son, and the Holy Ghost.” Justin turns his back on Salaok again and does not stay to watch Salaok die on the cross as a cloud begins to move in front of the sun and drown the land of sunlight with darkness once again. With nothing else to do, Salaok can only watch as his father and brother-in-law leave him there on the cross to die. His eyes begin to close as the blood now completely covers his face and his body begins to go cold. In the darkness of his own world now, Salaok remembers his past and yet one instant sits vivid but it is the words that mean more. When he visited Brian Welch in the mental home in which Salaok sent him too, Brian only spoke three words just as Salaok was leaving. Those words now making full sense as if Brian had known of this all along. “Justin will win.” And now he had.
