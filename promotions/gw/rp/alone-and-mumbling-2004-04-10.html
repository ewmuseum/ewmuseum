Alone and mumbling

10 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Alone and mumbling Derek Draven Global Wrestling Promo #1123 Date: 4/10/04 Location: unknown (Darkness.Complete and total darkness engulfs almost the entire room,with the exception of a old time interogation style light and small brown table,which shows Derek Draven.Sitting at the table his hands clasped in front of him.) 'What is the meaning of life' 'Asked the young man to the father' 'the Father not never cared..' 'the father that was to busy being drunk,to busy caring for himself to spend a little time with the people he was related to!' 'I learned a long long time ago you are given one friend,and many foes.The rest are just associates.' (The barley visible light given off by a lit ciggarette shines through the darkness.) 'Many will ask..none will listen' 'Thats what I always say' 'It's like the old saying if a tree falls in the woods but no one sees it,did it really fall?' 'You dont see or hear everything...' 'I believe sometimes I have the ability in me to listen to peoples souls..' 'I watch souls escape from their bodies as they do as I,and seek the reasoning and truth behind what is known and what is not known.' 'A simple day in public turns into a chore for me..' 'I read your thoughts..' 'Through facial expressions and body language..' 'Believe me...' 'all of you...' 'I read you like books' 'I'm a psychological profiler.' 'Thats what me so effective.' (As the light of the ciggarette gets further and further away it finally vanishes as a soft sigh comes through the room) 'The past is over...' 'How sad..' 'Some fans of GW expected a big Nevermore return...' 'To bad...' 'All you got was me...' 'And I personally don't give a flying fuck what your opinions of me are.' 'It's all to sad that the Nevermore had a proverbial cult following..I bet you were pissed when they disbanded...' 'Myself...' 'I was miles and miles away...' 'Not a care for what happend here in GW' 'In the middle of the desert,survival was key..not what the internet insiders had to say' 'When I decided my adventure was through,I came back to the United States.For a breif period I did some pit fighting on the streets of Los Angeles for some money....' 'Then I decided to come here...' 'Something was missing..' 'All around incomplete..' 'It was my GW legacy.' 'People always put me in the shadow of Saloak.' 'Never gave me credit..' 'I won title after title,nothing.' 'I was still second to Saloak.' 'While he was going around doing nothing,Derek Draven was taking control!' 'Fucking with your minds!' 'Putting people like Brick through hell,just to get a head start!' 'It's people like me in this business that get ahead!' 'Not family people...' 'selfish people...' 'The ruthless...' 'The heartless...' 'The unforgiving...' 'I'd do anything to get ahead!' 'And still will!' (The sound of breathing is the only thing heard for what seems like eternity.) 'I have a hit list...' 'Oh yes.' 'Takes no brain surgeon or rocket scientist to figure out it's contents.' 'The people I have history with have mainly faded away into obscurity...except a couple.A minor few,still wandering around like people without a clue.' 'I'll leave that to wandering imagination to figure who and what i'm talking about.' 'Evil is unpredictable...' 'Remember this....' 'Knowledge may save your career in this business..' 'But some posses to much of it..' 'Roz Blayze..' 'haha...' 'Oh,pathetic little soul you.' 'Have I ever told you your biggest mistake?' 'Hmmm?' 'Have I?' 'Well just in case here it is...' 'You followed me here...' 'A few years ago,we came in together..one broke free and rose to the top,the other...well do I have to say?' 'when it comes to me and you it's not respect...' 'Or titles' 'It's what you know' 'That may save your life...' 'Or kill you!' 'thats your choice...my friend' 'My only friend...' 'You,are my gift.' 'My game piece..My pawn if you will.' 'With you I can manipulate my way through the world of GW.You have no idea the power over you I have..' 'It reminds me of when we were little kids...' 'Like you said...' 'A fascination with death and the way life worked.' 'Thats been me...' 'But now,I dont seek death,just mental anguish and physical punishment of my foes...' 'Crossing the line here is life or death..' 'In my game of human chess,there is only one winner by destiny...' 'Me!' 'Remember that..' 'I play this game of life with one thing in my mind!' 'To win!' (Derek's face with the ultimatley evil expression stares at you.His eyes bloodshot.) (Static)
