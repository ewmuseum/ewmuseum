Exhaustion Vanquished

19 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03.19.05 It had been the first time in months that Ken had actually gotten a good night's rest. For so long, his insomnia had kept him from sleeping, which in turn led to exhaustion. Last night, however, he was back home, in his own bed and had Gunner laying with him. 'You know,' started Gunner, 'I was thinking we could just stay in and watch a movie tonight.' She was walking through the door with a small bag of candles and other trinkets, as well as a to go box from Sakura, their favorite sushi place. Ken was in the gym, adjacent to the den, on the crossramp, in the middle of his cardio exercise. He cut his workout short and stopped the machine before stepping off and greeting his love with a kiss, immediatly raiding the bag and box afterwards to see what she got. 'Sounds good to me,' he answered. 'I could use a night in.' Having already sat down the now open Sakura box, allowing the eel, yellow tail, and the rest to be seen resting next to their beds of ginger root and wasabi, Ken was now digging inside of Gun's bag. 'What'd you get,' he asked, as anxious as child whose mother had just come home from the market. 'Just a few small things,' she chimed, laughing, as she reached to get the bag back from Ken. 'A few candles, a photo frame... nothing big.' Ken pulled out one of the candles and took a deep whiff of it. 'Toffee,' he purred, 'my favorite.' He handed the bag back to Ms. Addison while walking away with the candle, sniffing it with a smile on his face the entire way. 'Where are you going,' she asked. 'I thought we were going to eat and watch a movie.' 'I've been working out, babe,' he projected from the bathroom. 'I figured I needed to clean up.' She smiled as she got down plates for their sushi, preparing each one somewhat equally. Ken's however did have more eel than her's, but she had made sure that he only got three California rolls to her nine. He didn't care either way, and she knew that. Eel was his favorite, but had she eaten it all, he would have just been happy that she was happy. That was one of the reasons that their relationship worked so well. The simple fact that both of them wanted nothing more than for the other to be happy. 'What do you want to watch,' Ken asked, still holding the toffee candle as he walked back into the dining room. 'Can we watch something romantic,' she asked. 'I don't know what you're in the mood for, but I really want to watch something romantic.' 'Okay,' he replied. 'Anything in particular?' 'I don't care, just something romantic.' Gunner grabbed the plates and took them into the bedroom as Ken began scanning the collection of DVDs in his home theatre. His eyes rested on The Princess Bride. It was his favorite book and one of his absolute favorite movies. He grabbed it before heading to the bedroom himself and placing it in the DVD player. It was 7:30 in the evening. The two had finished eating and had gotten to share a few intimate kisses while holding each other throughout the movie by 8:15. As Gunner looked over for another kiss, it was aparant that Ken had fallen fast asleep. Smiling, whe kissed him gently and fell into his grasp. She loved sleeping in his arms, and was happy that this time he was able to sleep as well.
