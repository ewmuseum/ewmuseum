She Will Be Loved

13 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02.13.05 Ken had always considered Valentine's Day a generic holiday created by chocolate factories, greeting card companies, and flower couriers. In his mind, everyday should be Valentine's Day, that is of course if Valentine's Day means showing those that you love that you care about them. However, when it came to Gunner, he was more than happy for any chance to spoil her. A smile wrapped around his face as the vocals to Elton John's 'Live Like Horses' began to fill the room. He adjusted the purple vest that rested beneath his Giorgio Armani high-collar tuxedo jacket and began to make a few final touches on his hair. As the song continues, he couldn't help his mind racing into memories of his and Gunner's past. 'What are you thinking about,' asked Gunner, breaking Ken's concentration. 'What,' responded a startled Ken. 'Oh, I didn't see you standing there.' Ken let the smile return to his face as he looked up to see Gunner at his bedroom door. She looked spectacularin what had to be the most beautiful purple Donna Karan dress that he'd ever seen. Of course, it was on the most beautiful person that he'd ever seen. It still blew his mind how extremly ravishing she was and then he began to wonder why she was with him. 'I love you,' he said without thinking. She smiled largly as she walked over to Ken. 'I love you, too, Sweetheart,' she responded. 'But don't change the subject.' She wrapped her arms around his neck and began to sway slowly from side to side, pulling him into a dance as the two continued talking. 'Us,' replyed Ken honestly. 'I was thinking about us. About how we met. About everything we've been through. About where we are now. About how amazing you are... how beautiful you are... and how much I love you.' She rested her head on his chest, her perfect hair brushing his chin as he continued. 'Gun,' he continued, 'you're the greatest Valentine's... the greatest gift I've ever received.' She stopped, causing him to do the same and leaving him to breathe in the wonderous smell of her hair. She looked up to him with perhaps the most entrancing eyes he'd ever witnessed and, without warning, intoxicated him. 'We fit,' she said. 'I never thought I could find that, but I have. You... you're everything to me.' The two stood, arms around one another and gazing into one anotehr's eyes. At that moment, they were infinite... lovers trapped in a spectrum of forever that could never be explained, or understood. At that moment, they were... immortal.
