'Demons', part 2

30 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 30th, 2003 'Wake up, my dear.' A voice? She knew that voice. Her eyes felt like fire, the lids like lead. She struggled to open them, but when she did, everything spun and blurred. 'Wh—where?' she stuttered. 'Oh, somewhere,' replied the voice. 'Somewhere we can have our own fun, away from that nasty son of mine.' She focused hard, striving to bring her vision into focus. The spinning slowed and things began to unblur. She saw yellow hair, green eyes, fangs? The back of her head throbbed, and she lurched forward, giving up her breakfast to the floor. The world swam around her again, and turned to black. 'Well damn,' said the voice, 'that's no fun at all.' *** She was in a house. Her house? No, it was his house? but it felt like hers. She was lounging on a plush couch, reading -- re-reading, she realized now -- Sun Tzu's 'Art of War' by the day's last rays of sunlight. She had always found the text fascinating, and gleaned some new insight every time she read it through. She had just gained a new insight into a passage on the use of fire in warfare, in fact. That was when she realized that she was lounging near a large bay window. And that the window was exploding in. Everything seemed to move so slowly. She could see the shards of glass flying towards her, and tried to leap away -- but it was like moving through mud. She floated over the back of the couch, and a chunk of glass bit into her biceps hungrily. As red hot pain lanced through her arm, everything sped up again, and she hit the ground with a thud. Grimacing, she checked the wound -- it was clear, the glass wasn't lodged in it. She watched as blood oozed out of the wound, pure and red. Everything around her became sharper, more real. Less like a dream, and more like a memory. She was Kakita Yoshiko. Born into the privileged Kakita family, living her life by her family's only standard: excellence, in all things.. Pushing the pain away, she rose up from behind the couch, and looked at the gray lumps on the floor. They rose and revealed themselves as gray-suited men. They would attack her? Here? That kind of insult simply was not allowed. With a fearsome kiai, Yoshiko leapt over the couch and rushed the two men. One of them pulled a gun from his coat -- but the Silken Crane was on him faster than he could draw, sending the gun flying from his hand with a spinning kick. She followed through with the motion, leaping from one foot to the other and driving her heel into the man's temple with a satisfying crack. Even as he fell she was moving again, bending back to duck a knife swing from the other man. She sprung back up immediately, driving a quick jab into his face. Blood spouted from his nose, and he jabbed angrily with his long knife, but Yoshiko danced backwards, up onto the couch. The man slashed his knife rapidly through the air, driving Yoshiko back over the couch. His attacks bit deeply into the cushions, leaving several long cuts to accentuate the holes from the glass earlier. Yoshiko scowled. 'You're paying for that,' she said. The man only laughed, and leapt up onto the couch, stabbing down at her. Yoshiko smiled, sidestepping and snatching the man's arm. She pulled down on it, forcing him to bend with his own momentum. In a showcase of flexibility, she bent forward as he did, and arched a leg up over her back, driving a kick into his injured nose. He stood dazed for a moment -- and that was all she needed. Yoshiko leapt up onto the back of the couch, and flipped over the man's back, grabbing him by the shoulders and driving her heels into the backs of his knees. His knees buckled and she pulled hard, landing kneeling on the floor and flipping him over her back to slam into the coffee table. The table shattered under the impact, but the man laid motionless on the floor. Yoshiko didn't move, just remained kneeling and tried to catch her breath. She wondered who these men were, why they were here? but her thought were shattered like glass by an eerily familiar sound. The sound of applause. Slowly, Yoshiko rose to her feet and looked towards the window. She noted that the very last rays of sunlight had disappeared behind the horizon. And here, framed by the broken window pane, was the man she knew would be there, but had rather hoped wouldn't. James Michaelson. Rokku's father. 'Well done Yoshiko-chan,' said Michaelson flashing her a fanged smile. 'You really are worthy of my son.' 'But,' he gestured to himself, as if he was showing himself off, 'wouldn't you rather be with the original article?' Yoshiko scoffed. 'First of all, I'm not 'with' Rokku--' 'Ah, but you want to be,' Michaelson interrupted, 'I could smell it on you the last time I saw you, the scent you gave off while we fought? but I could be wrong. Maybe that was for me, eh?' 'Second of all,' continued Yoshiko, with annoyance, 'I'm not into old men or corpses Michaelson-baijii.' Michaelson closed his eyes and shook his head, chuckling softly. 'Have it your way then, sweetness. Gentlemen?' Michaelson snapped his fingers and several more gray-suited men swarmed through the broken window. Yoshiko backed up, looking for an avenue of escape -- that's when the front door burst open, and even more thugs came through. Looking around, the Silken Crane counted at least a dozen. And that wasn't even counting any more that might be outside. Yoshiko gritted her teeth. She was outnumbered, outgunned, and had no conceivable route of escape. She should surrender. But a Kakita never surrenders. 'Alright then boys,' she said, shifting into a low Crane stance, 'who wants to bleed first?' En masse, the thugs charged?. And everything started to blur again. Yoshiko felt like she was watching herself from the outside as she fought, as she dropped one man here, one man there, and the fight sprawled over the whole of the first floor of the house. And she realized as she watched that Michaelson was watching too. Biding his time, waiting for a moment. She tried to shout, to warn herself, but she could make no sound -- at least not one that she could hear. And then she was back in her body, held fast by a pair of thugs grasping onto her arms. That was when Michaelson made his move. He charged, his fingers twisting into claws which he raked along the couch, and caught her by the throat. With unnatural strength and speed, he slammed her hard into the wall by the front door. The plaster gave with the impact, caving in -- and so did Yoshiko, coughing up blood onto Michaelson's face. The vampire's lips curled into a twisted predatory smile, and his tongue slid out between them licking the blood off of her face. 'Delicious,' he purred, as the whole world began to turn white before Yoshiko's eyes, 'I can't wait to try some more?' *** With a groan, Yoshiko regained consciousness. Her head was throbbing like a steady bass beat but didn't feel like it was going to explode any more. Hesitantly, she opened her eyes. It hurt, but nowhere near as much as before, and her focus didn't blur or swim. She looked around. The floor was concrete, the walls were as well, but she could see a large window or two -- was she in a warehouse? She moved to work the numbness out of her wrists, and found that she couldn't -- they were clapped in heavy chains, attaching her to the wall. As were, she found, her legs as well. 'Oh good,' said Michaelson, appearing from around one of the walls, 'you're awake! I was afraid I had broken you. That wouldn't have been any fun at all.' Yoshiko said nothing, simply glaring at the vampire defiantly. 'Don't feel like talking, do you? Well, that'll change soon enough. I've got so much in mind for you, my sweet Crane?' 'Rokku's coming, you know,' said Yoshiko plainly. 'Yes, I imagine he is,' said Michaelson. With a burst of sudden speed, he pinned Yoshiko against the wall. She shivered, pinned between his cold flesh and cold concrete. 'But I'm hoping he comes, little Crane. I'm going to destroy him in front of you, and then you'll be mine forever?' Michaelson raked a claw down Yoshiko's cheek, drawing blood. Slowly, he licked the wound. 'Just as delicious as before,' he muttered, before pushing Yoshiko's head to the side, and sinking his fangs into her neck. But even as she was being eaten by this monster, as consciousness slipped away from her again, Yoshiko smiled. Because she knew the Steel Lion would come. And this? this thing would never know what hit him.
