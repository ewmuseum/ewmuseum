Consequences and repercussions - stuck in a moment.

19 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 19th, 2006. Consequences and repercussions - stuck in a moment. ---- Knight Enterprises. Seattle, Washington. January 19th, 2005. 11:17 AM. 'So, is it done?' Asked the golden tongued Donovan Knight as he tilted back in his chair, a pair of black Vans resting on top of his desk, a black cordless phone pressed to his ear. 'All but the crying.' Retorted Colton Cornell, attorney at law. 'Good.' Donovan said, a smile stretching across his face. 'You've got to love the Mexican judicial system.' Colton chuckled over the phone. 'Oh, absolutely, how much did it set us back?' Donovan asked, chuckling along with Cornell. 'Thats the best part.' Cornell replied. 'I dropped a cool five K of good ol' American green in Judge Garcia's lap and it turns out that, gee...Josh isn't the father after all.' 'Only in Mexico, eh?' Donovan Knight laughed. 'Only.' Answered Cornell. 'When will you speak with Josh about the matter?' 'He's set to be here in about twenty minutes, make sure you call us then, I'll put you on the speaker phone and we'll break the good...errr...bad news to him.' Donovan replied. 'To steal a line from good ol' Hannibal...I love it when a plan comes together.' Laughed Cornell. 'Oh, of course, and when the plan works to perfection like this one did...' Donovan trailed off into a chuckle. 'So, we're all set and I'll be catching a redeye out of El Paso and back to Seattle tonight. You know, I'm expecting a pretty penny for all of the work I've put into this.' Cornell stated in a matter of fact manner. 'Don't worry. Remember our deal? For every dollar Josh puts in my pocket you get twenty-five percent of that for the next year. Trust me Colt, this kid is pure money, that kid would have killed his market value.' Donovan's eyes lit up as he spoke of the money. 'I want my worth, so you had better pull some sweet marketing deals with Josh.' Cornell warned. 'Hakuna matata, baby.' Donovan said, smiling into the phone. 'The kid is already in talks with Converse, GQ has him on speed dial, and get this...Jon Woo gave me a call this week asking about getting Josh a starring role in his new film.' 'So, moving on to more pressing matters.' Cornell began, the change in his tone showing a more serious manner.'Are we sure he's going to be able to handle this, there are a lot of rumors going around about his mental stability, or lack thereof.' 'Trust me, its golden.' Knight chuckled. 'He may go a little bit crazy, but that is exactly what I want, exactly.' 'Why?' Asked a puzzled Colton Cornell. 'Its like this.' Donovan Knight began with enthusiasm. 'Youth is a star and a big one, but what does he have in his life? He has no family, aside from the kid, most of his friends are involved in the Wrestling business and the industry is all that he's known since age eighteen.' 'Right...' Cornell agreed, following along. 'If he snaps, then guess where he turns? Straight back to the business. The more pissed off with life he is the more focused he is on tearing through competitor after competitor inside of that octagon. And when he wins big, I win big, and now that you're in for a cut...you win big.' Donovan sneered. 'You're right...damn.' Colton chuckled. Donovan's voice peaked with excitement. 'Of course. Remember the pissed off American Youth of old? He's back.' 'Lovely.' Cornell answered in his slick tone. Donovan pulled back the sleeve of his black dress shirt to take a peek at the silver rolex on his wrist. 11:23 AM. 'Hey, we need to get this show on the road...' Donovan stated, deep in thought. 'Yes, when do you need me to call?' Colton asked in an equally thoughtful state of mind. Donovan stared intently at his watch before answering. 'Twenty minutes and we're good.' 'Great, I'll talk to you then.' Colton replied, as he disconnected the call from his end. With a smirk Donovan Knight returned the phone to its base and pulled his feet down from the desk. Standing to his full height of six feet he stalked across the soft blue carpet of his sprawling office and directly to the tall mirror that stood on the other side. Glancing into it he admired the man standing before him. The smile, the spiked blonde hair and bright blue eyes all made him look like your best friend. It was only when you truly knew the man, his crisp manner, cutthroat tendencies and obsession with making another buck that you realized he had no friends. His only friend was the almighty dollar. ---- Knight Enterprises. Seattle, Washington. January 19th, 2005. 11:32 AM 'Right this way.' Directed the cute assistant that managed the front desk for Donovan, as she pointed me towards the door. I had been here enough times before to find it on my own but she took pride in her job and insisted on showing me the way. 'Thanks.' I said with a smile and nod as I stepped through the open door. 'Josher!' Donovan exclaimed with a bright smile and a mouth full of half-chewed blueberry bagel. 'Hey D.' I nodded in return. He dropped the bagel onto a napkin resting on his desk, he quickly brushed the crumbs from his gray tweed pants as he stood and extended his hand. I took it, gripped it, shook it firmly and released as I dropped into one of the black leather chairs in front of his desk. 'Hungry?' Asked Donovan, pointing to a box of bagels on his desk. 'No, I couldn't eat.' I said shaking my head, my multi colored black and white locks falling across my face as I did so. 'My stomach is in knots right now.' 'I bet, maybe a drink would settle your stomach?' Donovan asked nodding towards a wide liquor cabinet to his left. 'I'd love a Rum & Coke, hold the rum, please.' I smiled. He shook his head at the lame joke. 'I'll just get Jenn to bring you a coke.' Reaching across his desk he hit a button on his intercom. The voice of his lovely assistant came through the box. 'Yes, Mr. Knight?' 'Jenn, could you be a dear and bring Josh a coke from the vending machine?' Donovan asked kindly. 'Of course, I'm on my way.' She answered. 'Thanks Jenn.' Donovan replied. Removing his finger from the intercom button he glanced up at me with a wide grin. 'She's great, she really puts the ass in assistant.' I chuckled slightly as I watched him intently. 'So Josh, how are things?' He asked thoughtfully. 'Things are...okay.' I answered. 'Okay?' He asked, arching an eyebrow. 'I'm very unhappy with the loss to Biddle at Reload, it was a conspiracy, you and I both know I should be wearing the International title right now.' I snapped. 'Oh, absolutely.' He agreed. 'But you will. When High Octane rolls around we will have an all new International Champion - 'American Youth' Josh!'. I nodded. 'But we both know I should be wearing that belt right now.' We were interrupted as his assistant stepped through the office door and padded across the floor. With a smile she handed me a coke. 'Straight from the vending machine and still cold.' She said. 'Thank you.' I returned her smile. 'You're most welcome.' She turned to Donovan as she spoke. 'Was there anything else I can do for you Mr. Knight?' He shook his head. 'No, but thank you and please close the door on your way out.' Without another word she left the office, closing the door behind her as requested. I spun the lid off of the coke and took a long drink before twisting it back on. Sitting it on the floor next to the chair I leaned back and stretched my legs out, jamming my thumbs into the pockets of my light washed, torn and tattered jeans. 'So, have you heard from Cornell?' I asked. 'Not yet.' Donovan frowned. 'He'll call the second he gets out of the courthouse. We'll both know the verdict at the same time.' 'This waiting game is killing me.' I sighed. 'I know, but hey...thanks to the magic of a paternity test it'll just be a matter of time before you'll be able to experience the joy of being a father.' Donovan said with a broad grin. 'I can't wait.' I answered with a smile. 'There are so many things I want to show him, to teach him.' Donovan nodded. 'You'll have plenty of time to do that.' 'Maybe I wasn't there for his first steps or words, but I can't wait to show him how to play baseball, or teach him to ride a bike, take him to his first show at Global and show him what Daddy does for a living.' The smile evolved into a grin as it spread across my face. 'Josh, you're going to make one helluva father.' Donovan smiled. 'Thanks.' I said, my grin wide. With my elbows resting on the arms of the chair I folded my hands across my midsection, over my white t-shirt featuring the word: 'Showbread' in a white and orange vertical text, juxtaposed by a picture of a giraffe and an inset picture of a young female, the words 'inspire me to sing to you' listed below her face. 'I'm proud of you man.' Donovan said, his eyes showing deep sincerity. 'Converse wants you, Jon Woo wants you, GQ wants you...everyone wants you. You've got the world by the short and curlies. This is your life, this is your world.' 'None of that even compares to this kid Donovan. It all pales in comparison, this kid...he is my life...my world.' I stated in a sober tone, my smile slowly fading away as I fell into deep thought. Espen Padilla, the son I never knew I had until that fateful night in Mexico. I had inked my contract with Global Wrestling a little over a month before that moment, and little did I know how big of a star I would become. The greatest World champion of all time, a legend inside the octagon and a hall of famer, all before I even hit the age of 25. I was a lot of things, but the last thing I expected to be was a father. What came as even more of a surprise was the fact that I was the father of a child so close to me. His parents were from the famed Padilla Wrestling family of Mexico. His father, Jose Padilla was a star in both Mexico and Japan. Not only was Jose a star in the ring, he also created stars. The Padilla family ran a school of Lucha Libre which produced the finest aerial artists in all of the world. I was proud to have attended the Padilla family school and to have graduated as the top student was even more of an honor. Lucha Libre had always been about respect, about honor, about integrity. Until one night...it became about lust, deception and infidelity. As an immature nineteen year old I fell deeply into sin, stealing love from Rosa Padilla as we dove headfirst into sweet adultery. The feeling of skin on skin, the sweet taste of her sweat and the mere touch of her lips drove an insane desire through my body which was inexplicable. As we chased a fantasy, something happened. Something neither of us expected. The married Rosa Padilla became pregnant with my child, she only told one person the truth...and I was that person. In a moment of foolishness I spilled my guts to Hiroshi Ryusaki who made my actions public, broadcasting them to the world. The foolishness of my youth cost me. It cost me friends, it cost me a girlfriend that I truly loved. But most of all, it cost me integrity, and now I was ready to make everything right again. It would simply be a matter of time before the phone would ring and Colton Cornell would announce that the paternity test came back positive and I would have legal custody of my son. It was a moment more than beautiful. One that I fail to explain, one that I can't justify with words. It was an orgasm of emotions. And here I sit, my heart racing, almost as if its in a hurry to get somewhere. A pounding so violent it threatens to rip from my very chest as my bluish gray eyes stare intently at the phone on the wide oaken desk before me. My every hope longed for the phone to ring, to burst into life. And almost as if I had wished it to do so...it did. The phone rang brilliantly, the sound piercing the air. My heart pounding even faster than it already was, my breath ragged and hard to contain, my fingers trembling with excitement and a thousand damn butterflies in the pit of my stomach. I stared down at the toes of my Chuck Taylors, lost in the moment. A second ring and Donovan answered the phone... 'Knight enterprises, this is Donovan speaking.' He stated. My eyes came back up and locked on Donovan's. 'Colt, hey...let me put you on speaker.' Donovan quickly punched the speaker phone button on and returned the cordless to its base. 'Josh is here and anxiously awaiting the good news.' Donovan said, flashing me his winning smile. 'Okay, well the judge agreed to grant full custody of the child to Josh, pending a paternity test.' Cornell announced. 'Right, we already know that...' I cut in. 'Are we set? When do I get to pick him up?' 'It isn't that simple, Josh.' Cornell replied. 'I know, I know.' I frowned. 'Damn red tape. How long is everything going to take?' 'I don't think you understand.' Cornell said with a sigh. 'What do you mean..?' My eyebrows arched in confusion. 'Josh...' Cornell's voice was low and somber as it came through the speaker phone. 'This may come as a shock to you but, the paternity test came back negative...' I slid out of my chair...onto my knees...hot tears rolling down my face. 'No...it can't...' Cornell's voice was tight with emotion. 'I'm sorry Josh, but...you aren't the father.' ----
