The mysterious person was still sitting in front of the computer. The person stares at the screen through the gleaming lens of their eyeglasses. As this person just watched Kyo’s powerful reawakening via camera feed from the back room. A sigh of relief escapes the person. The mysterious person was relieved that Kyo’s awaken was a success. But the person was also relieved that the incubator’s alarm was shut off before Kyo blew it to hell. Courtesy to the mysterious person’s superb hacking skills. The person leans back in the chair. And the person continues to watch the camera feed on the screen. Still who was this mysterious person? And why is this person helping Kyo? Was it a trap? Was it this person sick idea of a game? Or could Kyo possible have a secret ally? The scene changes to the back room of the research lab. Kyo was still using the wall to keep on his feet. But soon sharp pains travel throughout his body. This pain made him drop down on the floor once more. He wonders what was responsible for the great agony that he was feeling. He examines both his arms. And to great shock, he finally saw the tubes that were sticking out of both arms. He checks himself with great panic. As he saw that the tubes were on both legs, on his chest, on his back, and on the right side of his head. Kyo yanks the tubes out one by one. He soon stops. And slowly closes his eyes. As he brace himself to pull out the three final tubes that were attached to him. Kyo already knew he was about to endure great pain. Since these were the tubes that were attached to his head. Breathes in? Breathes out... Kyo begins pulling on the tubes. His face scrunches up in pain. As he felt that the tubes were deeply lodged in his head. He stops again. Kyo tightens his grip on the tubes. Breathes in quickly? Breathes out quickly? Kyo begins pulling on the tubes harder. His eyes were tightly close. As he endured this great pain he was feeling. He pulls out the tubes with a painful yell. With the tubes out it was time to get out of this room. But the question was how Kyo was going to manage this feat. Remember that the door was reinforced with at least five sheets of steel. He couldn’t possibly break this mighty door with brute force. Even with his super strength he couldn’t knock this door down. Perhaps he could use his Shadow Fire power to break free. And he decides to? Kyo stands back on his feet with the use of the wall. He took a few steps back. As he prepares to fire a Shadow Fireball from his right hand. But nothing came out of his hand? Kyo looks at his extended arm curiously. He thought maybe his body has fully awakened yet. So he tries to focus harder on his power. Still nothing came out? Frustration kicks in. Kyo snatches back his arm. And he examines his right hand. “What the hell?” To his surprise he sees a cross shape scar on the palm of his hand. Just what exactly was this scar from? Was something implanted? Or was something removed? “What?”” He glares down at his trembling hand. “What the hell did they do to me?” He clutches his hand into a tight fist. “JUST WHAT THE HELL DID THEY DO TO ME!”” He strikes the door with a hard straight punch. Kyo repeatedly struck the door out of anger. I mean who wouldn’t be furious after finding out that somebody has poked you, cut you open, possibly played around with your insides, and then stitched you without you knowing about it. He stops after the tenth punch. Kyo still have his fist in the door that now has a deep dent in it. “Huh?” He sees a trail of his own blood dripping down from his closed fist. “My blood?” His blood wasn’t black anymore. “It’s red?” What happened? What was responsible for turning his black blood to red? Kyo stands in the room. As he starts to examine his bleeding fist. “Meanwhile” The scene changes to the dark room. The mysterious person still sits in front of the computer. As the person was still watching the camera feed. “It’s time for you to remember?” The mysterious person pushed a button on the keyboard. The scene switches back to the back room. Suddenly, the steel door slides open? Kyo looks at the open door with distrust on his face. The word trap instantly entered mind. But he decided to take his chances. He walks into the next room. “This is some type of lab.” He looks around the room. He was about to walk further in the room. But he suddenly stops. He saw his own reflection off of a nearby glass shelf. As he saw that he has full grown bread on his face. Not believing that it was real. He stroked it. As he realizes that it was real. “How long have I been here?” He continued to stare at himself. “But where is here?” Kyo looks around the lab again. His eyes soon stop on a stretcher. As the sight of the stretcher trigger a vision. This could be a clue to on how he got here. “Two months and six days ago” “These two men haven’t had oxygen more than fifteen minutes! We must hurry and get them to the hospital!” The scene opens in a long hallway. Two teams of paramedics were pushing two stretchers down the hallway with great haste. On one stretcher there laid a man who was recognized as a champion’to some people. As this man was none other then Syckle who was better known as the Dark Horse in the 4WW federation. On the other stretcher there laid another man who was recognized as a revolutionist’to some people. As this man was none other then Kyo Kushinaga who was better known as the Deathscythe Outlaw in the same federation. Both men were in grave condition after both were buried alive in their match. They were unconscious. They were covered in dirt. And they were breathing with the use of oxygen masks. The paramedics soon reach a fork in the hallway? The team with Syckle went right. While the team with Kyo gone left. One paramedic with Syckle stops. “Hey! That’s the wrong way!” He glares at the other team. “The ambulances are this way! Hey!” The other team ignores his words. As they continue to go the wrong way. Or was it the right way? The scene changes to an indoor parking area. The team wheels Kyo over to a suspicious looking black van. The van’s back doors open. A man in a white lab coat steps out. “How long has he been out?” He examines Kyo’s body. “Fifteen to twenty minutes.” “His regeneration will be kicking in any time now.” He pulls out a shot that contains some green liquid. “I must inject this into him before it has a chance to.” “What’s that?” “It’s a special kind sedative for his kind.” He squirts the sedative out with a gentle push. The man lowers the shot to the side of Kyo’s neck? Don’t know how it was possible. But Kyo suddenly opens his eyes before the needle could touch his neck. It was as if his mysterious sensed that he was danger in unconsciousness. The men were stunned. Kyo snatches the shot away from the man. As he stabs it right into the man’s eye. The man’s yells of agonizing pain echoing throughout the parking lot. As he have fallen to ground. As he favor his destroyed eye that was gushing out large amounts of blood. One of the paramedics goes to strike Kyo. But Kyo manages to strike the man in the chest with a stiff elbow. That paramedic fell to the ground. The other paramedic goes to grab Kyo. Once again Kyo slips out of their clutches. As he roll off the stretcher backwards. “I can’t believe you all thought it would be that easy to catch me.” He yanks off the oxygen mask. As he glare at them with one knee on the ground. “Did that bastard Yosho send you guys?” They responded by taking out semi-automatic guns out of their paramedic uniforms. Kyo grins. “I’ll take that as a yes.” They start shooting at Kyo! Kyo stays where he was. And hold out one hand. He forms a shield before him. As he watches the bullets bouncing off the shield. It would seem that Kyo has the advantage over these guys. But he was still exhausted from his previous match. And it was easy tell with his shield cracking with each shot. The men take notice of this. As they begin to move in closer to Kyo with their assault. How long could Kyo keep this up? Kyo’s arm was trembling. It was getting harder for to keep this routine up. “MASTER!!!” Entering the scene like a swift wind, his apprentice, Kairi jumps in front of Kyo with her hands out. As she create a stronger shield before Kyo’s. “Kairi!” He looks at her with surprise on his face. “Remember, I always got your covered, Master.” Kairi winks at him. Kyo soon notices that Kairi had both his large handgun holstered in the back of her pants. “I see.” He removes both guns with a grin on his face. “Ready?” Kairi nods. “Now!” He watched Kairi drop the shield. And saw her diving over to the right. As Kyo was diving in the opposite direction with guns blazing! Kyo struck two of the paramedics in the head for instant kills. The remaining three retreats for safety? Kyo and Kairi look around the parking area from where they stand. Kairi’s wolf ears suddenly sprout through her light violet colored hair. As she begin listening for the men. Kyo uses his senses to track them. Kairi soon disappears within a small whirlwind of pink cherry blossom petals. As Kyo stays where he was. He soon lifted one gun up to his face. And closes his eyes. Silence? Kyo spins around in a one hundred and eighty degree. As he drops down on one knee. And fires a single shot in the left that went underneath a parked car. A pain filled yell was heard. As a thud was heard. Kyo sees the fallen man lying on the ground. He fires another shot that penetrated through the man’s head. A man managed to sneak up to Kyo from the behind. He thought he would execute Kyo with a shot to the back of his head. Or so he thought? A gun was fired! The man stands motionless behind Kyo. His face was lit up with shock. Blood begins to come out from mouth. As blood was gushing out from the top of his head like a small geyser. Kyo’s gun was sticking up from underneath his arm. The man falls to the ground with thud. The final man was kneeling at the front of the black van. He was pacing himself to gun Kyo down with a surprise rush attack. However, someone else had other plans? Kairi was kneeling on the van’s hood with her short sword out. A thud was soon heard. Kyo looks over to the thud. And soon seen that a hand was hanging from the front end of car with a puddle of blood. He realized that Kairi took out the last man. “And that’s the end of that.” He twirls his guns around once. The black van suddenly explodes! “Kairi!” He watched her body fly through the flames. Kairi hits the ground hard. Was she dead? Kyo runs to Kairi to see. But he gets stop halfway by a powerful blast that came out of nowhere. The blast knocked him all the way back into the front windshield of a parked car. Any man would be out after taking a shot like that. But Kyo was still conscious’barely. As he rolls off the front of the car. He hits the ground hard. “Kairi?” He reaches out to the motionless Kairi. A person soon steps on Kyo’s wrist. Kyo goes to look up at the person. But the person quickly injects something into his neck. As Kyo starts to lose consciousness fast. “If you want to do anything?” Kyo catches a glimpse of the person. But he blacks out before he could say something to the person. “You got to do it yourself.” He turns Kyo on his back with a light kick. And soon took notice of Kyo’s necklace with the charm being his beloved’s wedding band. He grabs hold of it. As he yanks it off his neck. “My trophy for finally catching you.” Something in camouflage approaches the man. It wasn’t the common military camouflage. As it was the kind of camouflage that you would see in the Ghost in the Shell series. But what would have optic camouflage? This something turns off its? camouflage. As it was revealed that it was an eight foot mechanized robot suit that was being piloted by a man inside. “What do you want me to do with the Shadow Beast?” The person was still glaring down at Kyo. “We don’t need her.” He grins. “She’s probably dead as it is.” Kairi was still laying there motionless. “Grab the body and let’s go.” The person walks away. “We don’t need the police getting in our affairs.” The robot suit picks up Kyo. And followed the person. Who were these people? And what did they want with Kyo? “Present Day” Kyo was standing in the lab with a furious look on his face. It appears that vision jogged his memory. As he already knows who was responsible for this act. But he also found out that he has been here since the second of November. He soon glares at the clean surgical tools resting on a standing tray near the stretcher. As he knew that they must’ve used these tools to do God knows what to him. Kyo walks to a standing coat rack that had a lab coat along with a pair of lab pants. He puts those on. As these would have to suffice until he could find some better clothes to wear. He soon walked pass the tray. But the tools were gone after he walked by. He stands in front the lab’s entrance door. As he has a serious look on his face. “I remember?” The door bursts open! As Kyo exits the lab with the alarm ringing. Kyo knows who was responsible for all this. But who was it? When he does find this person what will he do? And also who was helping Kyo from the shadows?
