Date: September 16th, 2003 Drums. That's what it sounded like, Yoshiko thought. Like the beating of a distant drum, a rhythm full of pain and warnings. Ominous. How long had he been at it? She could only guess. Hours? Days? Maybe only mere minutes. Time had lost all meaning as she stared at the ceiling of her bedroom, unable to sleep, unable to really think, unable to do anything but listen to the steady, ceaseless pounding. Enough. Yoshiko tossed aside the bedcovers and abandoned the bed's confines, throwing on a kimono as she marched out of her room and towards the basement door. With every step, she could feel the vibrations, the ringing in her ears as agony and fury reverberated throughout the house. Quietly, she opened the basement door and crept down a few steps, crouching low and peering down into the basement. There stood Akodo Rokku. Yoshiko winced as the Steel Lion slammed a tape-wrapped fist into the large concrete block standing in the center of the floor, and winced again as he struck it with his other fist. With each blow, a resounding crack assaulted her eardrums, shaking her to the core. As the Silken Crane watched, Rokku struck the block again and again, fifty blows with his fists, then fifty with one leg, and fifty with the other. And with each strike, Yoshiko wondered how much longer Rokku could keep this up, how much longer he could punish his body, trying to harden his soul through calluses and scars. It had all started with the phone call? *** 'You're mine, Lion.' 'Think again, Crane. Take this!' The sound of steel on steel rung out, followed by a peal of laughter. 'Not even close, Lion! Like I said, you're mine!' 'Wait, don't -- NO!' With a whoop of joy, Yoshiko leapt up from the couch. Rokku's shoulders slumped in defeat, and he let the Playstation 2 controller fall into his lap. Meanwhile, Yoshiko did a victory dance in front of the big screen TV which clearly proclaimed, 'Winner: Sophitia'. Rokku sighed, and rubbed his jaw. 'So,' said the Steel Lion, 'um? best seven out of nine?' 'Oh, you are so on,' said Yoshiko, not bothering to pause in her celebratory dance, 'I've got all day to whip your sorry butt from one side of the map to the other, Lion. Hell, this time I might even do it with Heiha--' The phone's ring resounded sharply throughout the house, cutting Yoshiko off. 'Got it!' piped Yoshiko. Snatching the cordless phone off of an endtable, she pressed the talk button and raised it to her ear. 'Mosh moshi! Ahhh, Akodo-san! Hai, hai!' 'It's your mother,' said Yoshiko, handing Rokku the phone. 'Konbanwa okaa-sama, dochirahe?' Said Rokku into the phone. 'Soma-san? Hai—hai. Hai! Nani?! Hiretsukan!! Kesshite, okaa-sama. Sayonara.' Numbly, Rokku turned the phone off and set it down. 'I know that look,' said Yoshiko, sitting down next to him. 'What is it, Rokku? What's wrong?' 'Soma?' Rokku trailed off, a blank expression on his face. 'Leinad Soma was in Japan yesterday, Yoshiko. He visited my mother.' 'Kuso!' swore Yoshiko. 'Is she alright?' 'She's? she's fine.' Rokku stood and ran his fingers through his hair mechanically, like he wasn't even really there. 'Yoshiko? Soma had my father was with him. He told her that my father would be here? at Evolution. For my next match. Yoshiko? she said he was like him. Like Soma.' 'A vampire,' said Yoshiko, her voice quieted with sadness. For a moment, the room was silent. Then Rokku screamed, driving his fist into the wall. Bits of plaster fell away as Rokku extracted his bloody hand from the fresh hole. 'My father! He took MY father away from me, and turned him into a monster! That filthy son of a bitch! I'll kill him with my bare hands! I'll, I'll? DAMN HIM!' Rokku snatched his keys up off the coffee table, and made a beeline for the door. 'Rokku, wait!' shouted Yoshiko, but the Steel Lion was gone, the door slamming shut behind him. Yoshiko ran for the door herself, but stopped as she heard the scream of tires peeling. With a defeated sigh, she slumped against the door. *** Rokku had returned a few hours later with the concrete block he was pummeling now, and had been holed up in the basement ever since. Yoshiko admired his stamina, but didn't envy his emotional turmoil. To never know your father, only to find out a monster had taken him from you? she didn't know how he could stay sane, having had something like that done to him. Though, as she watched chips and dust fly off of the block as Rokku struck it harder and faster, she wondered how much sanity he had left. The kicks and punches came faster and faster, as if the Steel Lion were racing towards something, desperate to catch it.. or perhaps running away from something, and desperate to escape. A latticework of cracks decorated the block now, and large chunks began to fall away from it. With each blow, the block crumbled more and more, until Rokku unleashed a primal howl -- and as his bloody fist impacted with it, the block exploded in a shower of jagged bits and dust. Yoshiko watched in confusion as the Steel Lion stared at the broken bits of man-made stone, his chest heaving with every ragged breath -- and then dropped to his knees, howling in anguish. As sobs wracked Rokku's body, Yoshiko darted down the stairs and knelt in front of Rokku 'Hey!' shouted Yoshiko, grabbing Rokku by the shoulders and shaking him, 'Hey, Lion! HEY!' Finally Rokku looked up at her with red-rimmed eyes. 'Who are you?' asked Yoshiko forcefully. 'What?' replied Rokku dumbly. 'I said WHO the hell ARE YOU?' 'I? I'm?' Yoshiko's hand lashed out, striking Rokku across the face. 'WHO ARE YOU?!' Rokku's eyes turned steel-hard, and Yoshiko was sure she felt his stare bore into her soul. She almost smiled. 'I'm the Steel Lion,' said Rokku, his tone grave. 'What did you say?' 'I said I'm the Steel Lion!' 'Say it again!' Rokku sprung to his feet, and unleashed a ferocious roar. 'I'M THE GOD-DAMNED STEEL LION!' he screamed, for all the heavens to hear. 'You're damn right you are,' said Yoshiko, rising to her feet, 'and would the Steel Lion let a jackhole like Leinad Soma play him?' 'Hell no,' replied Rokku, 'and I'm not going to!' 'So what are you going to do about it?' Yoshiko asked. 'I'm going to get him in the ring,' said Rokku, 'and I'm going to destroy him! Soma has attacked me, my honor, and my family.. well not any-freaking-more!' Yoshiko grinned. 'That's the Steel Lion I know.' 'No,' said Rokku, 'this is more than the Steel Lion you know. Soma decided to make this fight personal? Well fine. I'll show him personal. Deadly personal.' As Rokku turned and marched up the stairs, a chill rode the length of Yoshiko's spine. Intensity. There was never a greater aphrodisiac in the world.
