An Eventful Night: Epilogue

12 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/12/05 ~Epilogue~ Things have changed, but there are some that won't know by just how much. Yes, Danielle Lynn Northsky had her first manifestation as to her true form and spirit. She showed that she's a being of pure spirit in the truest form.... a being of elemental fire and air as the old books call them. She showed that in her blood runs that of the angels themselves. But the main question here is... how? Dani's line is supposed to come from a legend of old to the Sioux American Indians of the Great Northern Plains. In that legend, a member of a race of cloud dwelling people, the servants of the Wakan Tanka itself, called the Maghpia Oyate (Cloud People). He came down to earth and took a Lakota Maiden as his bride. They inter-mated and she became pregnant with his son. One day while digging up plants in the cloud world where he had taken her, she fell back down to the earth. She died, but also gave birth that day... Her son, Fallen Star was found by the Lakota people and was raised by them. Fallen Star become a hero with mystical powers. He was a big, giant of a man, who could shoot a bow better then anyone. Wrestle a buffalo and tame the winds themselves. Fallen Star was trapped on the earth though, and he stayed with the Sioux Nations of peoples, the Lakota, Nakota and Dakota. His line is traced down through many tribal chiefs and holy men including Sitting Bull. But his line also goes through the line of a small family known by the surname Northsky. The morning star is the connotation of Venus when its still visible in the morning sky, or the great northern star. This is where they got their surname from, they were descended from Morning Star, the servant of Wakan Tanka and father of Fallen Star. However, there is a rule that comes to play with human genetics. All but proven. On the mystical plane though, it wasn't hard to prove that it had a force. The fate of the 7th generation from an anomaly. Danielle Northsky was 6 generations from Fallen Star. He was the hero. The Order of the Hammer had thought that it was her children that would be the great things. They miscalculated... in a big way. They had brought her in shortly after she was found by her grandfather, a long standing member of their ranks, to prepare her to raise children that might be of heroic stature. It was a good thing they did, that miscalculation almost made them miss that they were looking for not 7 generations from Fallen Star, but from his father. Dani Northsky was the one who would inherit the greatness. But that's where the flags now go up. Aine “Annie” Leanan, chief researcher for the Order knew what she had seen in the Nevada desert just about 12 hours ago, but she didn't believe it. Dani was from a Native mythos... but why did she show signs of being from another one all together? Annie didn't belive that they could be that strange. ~Outside of Dallas/Ft. Worth Texas~ ~Far in the Desert~ ~The Compound known as the Monastery~ Yeah, the Monastery. Also known as the 4 Winds Academy. The last of two training places for the warriors who come to the Order to serve them. The first one would be one of the other 4, each named for a single wind. West in Chicago, East in London, North in Northern Japan, and South on the island of Ryu Kyu (Okinawa). Annie herself was a graduate of the East Wind Academy, and was no stranger to the business of wrestling. However, she had not opted to come here to train as a warrior yet. Her forte was of a different kind. She was a book worm and lore master at heart. She now served the Mistress of Secrets in that aspect and after some disturbing reports, she had taken over the Northsky case. She was glad she had. The car she rode in came up the long driveway to the old church. A coupe of buildings lined the area, the centerpiece being an old church that was an exact recreation of the Roslyn Chapel in Scotland. Inside it, the Order co-ordinated most of it's business for the western hemisphere. This location also had one of the few houses that the Order maintained for the Keeper and Mistress of Secrets. As Annie heard, they were actually in town right now, helping to co-ordinate the Order's efforts in the struck Gulf Coast. A large meta-physical area, the Order's main duties of containing things that preyed on humans had to be done in the light of the recent disaster. This was disaster relief that no one would ever find out about... The car came up upon the statue on the courtyard, a good 3 stories tall. It parked next to it and a young male servant opened the door for her. Annie stepped out, wearing a sharp female cut business suit, with a scarf tie at the top. It was her usual black and red colors, keeping with her natural dark motif. She took a moment to look up at the statue and smiled at it. The statue was what set this church apart from those of the normal world, and one reason why it was over an hour trip outside of Dallas to get to this place. Standing there in the courtyard was a large man with wings outstretched from his back. In his arms, in a loving embrace, was a naked human female. Both were engaged in a kiss and other activities that hinted as to what they were intending. The engraving below the statue gave it's name... “Samyaza's Folly and Fall” Samyaza, one of the 200 angels who fell for the lust of women in the Enochian books of the Judeo-Chrstian Bible. According to legend, the stone this statue was carved from was actually mined and excavated from the very spot in the Israel desert that Samyaza had been imprisoned in before the great Antediluvian flood. ?Ye bastard, one of yer kind 'as made me job so much 'arder. Ye know tha'?? Annie turned away from it and walked up the large flight of stairs that headed to the main door. As soon as she entered, she was accosted by one of her apprentices. “Lady Leanan, good to see you made it. The Lore College is waiting for you on sub level 7 in conveyance room C.” ?Good t' 'ear tha'. Are they still flabbergasted?? “Yes, however you can't head down there just yet. To help out with your talks, your mistress has asked for you to undergo an Illithid Mind Probe. She wants your memories of last night presentable to the College so that there can be no issues with supposed fabrications.” Annie sighed outloud and stopped walking. “I know this is a troublin' discovery. I'll go a'ead and take care o' tha'. I migh' na' like it, bu' Th' Mistress o' Secrets is na' only me superior in th' Order, bu' is a princess o' the Seelie Courts. Please inform th' College as t' my delay.” ?I sure will, Lady Leanan. Oh, by the way. There are two sweeper teams working on the area where the fight on the street took place. We've also got Jaymes en route via helicopter. What do you want us to do with him?? ?What should I care?? “Uhm, well he was a merc hired under your funding...” Annie smiled at the young girl. “Yer riogh'. Even though th' bastard will never enjoy it, we meet ou' contracts an' deals. We keep our word. Pay 'is fee.” ?M'Lady?? “Jaymes was 'ired t' 'elp us figure ou' what Ms. Nortsky was. We now know, 'e kept 'is part o' the bargain.” “Yes, M'Lady.” At that the young apprentice turned on her heels and walked away. Annie walked past a couple of acolytes in their monkish robes and then entered into a not so well hidden elevator. ~A couple of hours later~ Annie's head was beginning to pound, and it wasn't fully the aftereffects of the Illithid that had probed her mind to extract the memories from the night before. Around her, sat at a table, were a collection of high end researchers from the Order. They were all arguing about the evidence that has been presented to them. In the middle of the table was a stone, a black mirror if you would, that shined with a brilliance. Hovering above it, as if from a movie projector in three dimensions as a recreation of Annie's memories, with Dani's wings in full extension, before the nexus shut down and they faded back into the ether again. ?Ye don'na bloody well get it, do ye? If she's na an angel, then wha' the 'ell is she?? A small mouse of a man looked up from a book he had brought along with him. ?Well, your theory is that these cloud people of the tribes, of which are shared throughout many of them, were actually angels? Judeo-Christian Angels that had come to live with them?? Annie had only answered this question 4 times already. “Yes. We know tha' the Angels in the Enochian books liked t' use stars as their names. We 'ave an angel who called 'imself 'Morning Star'. We know this is mentioned in the biblical books.” Another one spoke up. “Yes, to denote Lucifer.” Annie huffed under her breath. ?If yer goin' fer that passage in Daniel, it's known tha' does not denote Ibilis/Mastema or Sammael. Those are the three names o' the leader o' the Nephilim. Na' Lucifer. The fallen angels are na lead by 'The Light Bearer'. I'm na' even a scholar o' these myths an' I even know tha'.? The man spoke up again. “Well you also know that any angel-human mating would produce one of the giant hybrids: Gibborim, Anakim, or Raphilim. They don't have wings, they aren't a 'throw back' as some would call it.” Annie slammed her hand down. “Then wha' th' fok do you call tha'? She has wings, an' she's no gibborim.” ?How do you know this as a fact?? Annie looked at him with virtual daggers. “Maybe because she's the same height as ye an' me. She's only five foot, bloody six inches or so. She's no bloody giant.” A third man, with a soft voice spoke up. “However, reading about the legends of Fallen Star, did he not have the giant attributes. He was tall, was a strong warrior and that? There may be some truth to this. If one of the Noephilim, the ones that fell out of lust for women fell for a Lakota woman... he would have produced Fallen Star. The spot that doesn't make sense is Ms. Northsky getting the traits of the 5th great grandfather, and not Fallen Star as others we have seen.” “This is damned preposterous” The second person spoke out again, he was surely not going to support that a woman born on this earth could be, for all intents and purposes... an angel. Annie brought her hand up to the bridge of her nose and squeezed tight to try to fend off the headache. The men around the table started to argue amongst themselves for about 5 minutes straight. Annie tried to focus them out. They just didn't get it, they didn't have a pure myth here. They had a crossover! Finally Annie had had enough of their jabbering. ?ENOUGH!? All of them shut up and looked in her direction. “You guys are'na getting' anywhere on this. Ye've seen th' results o' me mind probe. Ye've seen th' evidence. Now don'na go by wha's in the bloody books. The Sioux legends weren't even written until the late nineteenth century. And we all know how much th' other books 'ave been edited by many, including out own allies over th' last two millenia. I want answers, an' I want them yesterday, think outside th' box...” They just stared at her, another couple of uncomfortable minutes... and then something broke the uneasy silence. ?Lady Leanan, if I may?? It was a heavy Texas accent that caught Annie's attention on that. One of the researchers who had stood up during the arguing, turned to face the newcomer to the meeting, and found his small frame looking right into the rather ample cleavage of a blonde haired woman. She was over six feet tall, but proportional as a pinup model from the World War II days. He adjusted his tie as she put a set of books down on the table. Annie adressed her. “Ms. Preest, ye 'old a position o' Honor on the Order, ye are one o' the Keepers right 'ands. Ye are always welcome.” Annie motioned with her hand to the woman, to sit down. Penelope “Penny” Preest, as she was named, didn't take a seat though. “No thank you for the seat. I shouldn't take too long for this all. I was one of them that intercepted the transmissions during your trip in. Including the scans of the feathers and the amulet designs.” The rude lore master spoke up. “Oh, yeah, like that would get you anything? C'mon, Ms. Preest, Penny, this isn't your field... let us do the research and investigation. You can go back to training people to bash each other's brains in.” Penny and Annie both gave the man a stern look. “You'll excuse me, but you have to realize a few things there. One... I'm not a dumb blonde. Just because I'm over six feet tall and can body slam you, doesn't mean I can't have a book side too. Two... I'm over six feet tall, and my brothers were almost seven and over seven feet tall. I'm a third generation Gibborim. I am a hybrid of a human an Angel if you have forgotten that. Third... You must remember that I am a personal friend of the Keeper himself. I have HIS ear.” ?So?? Annie almost wanted to throw something at the man. ?Well I just came from the mansion with the news. Remember, when you have the Keeper's ear, you also have the ears of that which is in side of him. The Wrath of God himself sends his regards... Suriel the Seraphim Arch Angel, resides in him... remember?? That reminder put everyone else in silence for over a minute. Annie then started to ask the woman what she came down to say. “Well, then. I will take it ye are brinin' word from a pretty good expert on Angelic beings.” Penny exhaled loudly, to let the others know who had the floor right now. ?Okay, well... let me start then. Suriel is very interested in this finding. Although in his disembodied and possessing form, he can't go see her until she's ready for that experience. He has confirmed that some of the others who have mated with human women don't know all that could happen. We do know that human genetic code seems to reset every seven generations. Doing what we see as a 'throwback'. But usually that is to reinforce recesive genes that both parents share. Not always the case, but usual there. However, he did confirm that the Noephilim, when they fell, and the Nephilim too, had no political boundaries. Some ended up in the new world, some in Europe, and some in the traditional biblical lands. To deny that they would have come over here, is just being stupid.? One of the men spoke out... ?So? Okay, he's basically said that an angel could have come over here and taken a female from one of the tribal peoples here. But which one?? Penny again shot a look of daggers for being interrupted. She quickly opened the book she had brought along, holding the page was a printout of the fax that showed the design of the amulet that Josh had made for Annie to bring along. “Well, he didn't know until I gave him this to look at. And then he directed me in the right place. It seems that it brings up something else too. Lady Leanan, I'll need to know more how young Corporal Brand came up with this... because it would lead to something else entierly.” Annie addressed her before the others could. ?Like what?? ?Well you said he had been given it, and told to give it to Ms. Northsky. Then when she was in danger from the Archon, it had acted to protect her. Yes?? “Yes.” “Well, this amulet is in the symbol of a very special angel... it's his actual icon.” ?What?? That word came as a collective from those around the table. ?Yes, an Enochian Angel... His name is spelled Kokabel in most texts, but that's usually a translation of the 'Kawk-a-bell' pronuciation. His name means 'Star of God'. He was the chief astrologer of heavane, but he fell with the Noephilm in lust. He taught humans astronomy and astrology as his sins.... He also has been noted to have over three-hundred thousand servants to help him keep the stars in order.? Annie asked the next question. ?But 'e fell riogh'?? Penny slid the book over towards Annie. ?Yes, and no. He's one of a few special ones. He was originally of the Order of Virtues, knowledge keepers. Suriel has confirmed that the darker wing tones that Dani has would show this type of angel to be the fathering entity. But, Kokabel, who's star was the brightest in the sky, or the North Star... The Morning Star to the Lakota... was one of a few of the ones that fell, who were allowed to come back. They were granted grace again, but remained on earth as the new caste of angels called the Grigori, or Watchers. So he's got his old job back to a point, but he's here, not in the sky anymore.? Penny smiled at Annie, feeling a victory for over showing Annie's researchers. Annie looked at the pictures in the book and compared them to the amulet design. ?Yer fokin' riogh'. So, basically, would The Wrath take it upon 'is word an' honor tha' we 'ave 'ad a throwback in a 'uman an' a Virtue 'as been born onto the earth from a woman's womb?? Penny nodded her affirmation as she spoke. “Yes. He says that other then a set of claws that would cause wounds that smell of pure elemental brimstone... a mixture of ethereal fire and air...” One of the researchers, who had also been there when they tested Josh's wounds on his back did a double take as she finished. “... She would mostly have powers of the mind. But being a direct decedent of Kokabel would be interesting, since his own abilities dealt with the stars in some way. That's all he gave me on that. The main thing here is, Kokabel hasn't been seen since the flood, at least we've not known it. But he appears to have come out of the wood work at least a little to deal with his grand daughter... he gave his amulet to protect her from the danger he knew she was in.” Annie just nodded her head as she read out of the old, dusty text in front o her. She didn't quite understand what she needed to do to help the young lady back in Las Vegas... but she'd figure it out. She did know that things were going to change a lot... things never were boring around the Order, that was for sure. ~End for now~
