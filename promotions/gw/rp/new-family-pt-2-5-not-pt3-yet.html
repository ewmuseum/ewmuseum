War. Love. Illegal street racing. Little kids being left at home because their parents are too fucking stupid to keep track of them. Bad guys who hijack submarines. Wrestling. Nothing is more glorified in Hollywood than organized crime. My newfound life. My wife's family heritage. My wife's until recently hidden family secret. My son's future. Possibly my future. 'You've been staring at the fire for about 10 minutes. It looked like you were going catatonic on me.' 'Oh, I'm sorry. I've just been thinking. Work stuff.' 'Ah. What cha thinkin about?' My wife sits down on my lap, with her legs over the arm of the chair. I look in to her eyes. 'Just what I'm going to do next. Corvus had a lot of nerve showing up when he did. Bubba should have been buried. God, it still pisses me off so much. How can one man take out my six men as well as a handful of Salaok's druids? The odds there are just staggering.' Did my wife just tell me to play mind games with the boss?' 'Yes, she did. Derek, I may be a woman...' 'A very pretty woman at that.' I interrupt, running my fingertips up and down her leg a little. 'I thought that was because she was a slut, and they were all getting laid from it. Hell, I know I wouldn't complain too much if I were in that situation.' She shoots me a sideways look. 'Oh, so instead you're stuck here with me and our son?' 'Yeah, but there's nowhere else I'd rather be stuck. Well, except maybe somewhere with you alone for a while.' I whisper in her ear before kissing her cheek. 'Hmm, well my parents did say they wanted to spend time with Damon tonight. Maybe we could get something for dinner and go from there.' I grin, and reply. 'So, you're saying you wanna go out for pizza then fuck?' 'Derek, you know I don't like pizza.'
