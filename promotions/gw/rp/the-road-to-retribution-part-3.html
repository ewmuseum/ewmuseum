The Road to Retribution: Part 3

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today The Road to Retribution Part 3: Blayze? Looks more like a spark. The scene opens inside the confinements of a small, dim room. Spread everywhere inside this room is various radio equipment, obviously used on a semi-often basis judging by the level of trash that was everywhere. Burger King wrappers, McDonalds packaging, Wendy's drinks... it was something of a sty, but perhaps that would be insulting the pigs. Sitting behind a large piece of the equipment are a couple of people, both of whom have microphones infront of their faces and headsets on their... well, heads. The character on the left is instantly recognizable as Rich Sherwin, the GW athlete who'd be going in to face Roz Blayze and Max Roswell this week on Evolution. The character on the right though, he didn't look all that familar and was dressed in a GW t-shirt. We couldn't see the lower half of this individuals body as he was carefully shoved beneath the overlapping panel of the radio equipment. 'HELLO! Welcome to KL Radio, I'm your host, Jim McGee and I'm joined by a special guest this morning. I said yesterday that we were going to have one of GW's up-and-comers beating his way onto the show and we have brought you just that. 'Captain Retirement' Rich Sherwin, it's a pleasure to have you on the show this morning.' Jim said in a loud and upbeat voice, this obviously being a show broadcast on a local basis at the very least. 'As always, it's a pleasure to be given the opportunity.' Rich replied, keeping the mood at a level that wasn't at all different from the host's. 'Paired with this athlete, we're going to have the same great news, gossip and rumours from the rich and famous. Now, first off, we're going to actually bring Rich onto the air and ask him a few questions about his GW career... and something that, many of us are still trying to come to terms with. That being, his real feelings over his elimination from the giant Rumble at 'Clash of the Titans' and Dylan Extreme.' the Host stated, his voice and mood still as chirpy as ever. Rich was impressed, he'd never seen many people who could fake a 'good' mood for this length of time, as short as it actually. On most occurances, he'd simply watch somebody try to be in a 'good' mood for a few seconds before they gave up. A few moments after the host's last words, he continued with his next piece, jumping straight into conversation with the GW athlete. 'Now Rich, the first thing I'm going to ask about is Evolution of this week. Roz Blayze, something of an up and comer like yourself, has been extremely outspoken about you and Max Roswell in the run to the actual three-way contest. Why is it that you've actually been quite quiet with regards to public understanding of your feelings?' The first question was going to be tough, but it was nothing that couldn't be handled by Rich. He looked up to the ceiling for a few moments, leaning back and letting out a sigh before answering, '... Roz Blayze is a unique individual. He'll stand about and talk about me as though I'm some kind of invisible challenge. He'd stand in front of the world and openly declare that I was nothing more than a sheet of paper floating in the wind. You see, I don't think my effort at 'Clash of the Titans' has actually registered in his mind. I think he still feels that I'm the guy who lost his debut and went on to win his second contest within twenty seconds of entering.' 'Twenty seconds? Now THAT is fast.' the host interrupted, sounding rather amazed at the fact that Sherwin had won his second GW match in a very impressive time. 'I mean, I can understand what Blayze did to a degree... and I can understand why he probably thinks I'm going to be a walkover. I'm a guy who managed to rank in a number four on our internal performance charts for the past week and he's a guy who only managed to scrape in a number seven. Honestly speaking McGee, how would you feel if a relatively new superstar joins your organization and charts higher than you within his first month?' Rich said, his voice still carrying a very amused tone. Compared to the last time we saw this man, his mood had improved by a percentage greater than any of us could care to imagine. He didn't look as though he'd lost any sleep over the desertion of his sister either. 'Well, putting myself in the position of Blazye, I guess I'd have something of a problem with you. I mean, it's quite common for people to develop levels of jealously against the 'new' guy because they appear to be better equipped... or even better skilled. In fact, it's almost like a situation that went by when I started out with this station. I just happened to be a better host than the old guy and he just couldn't handle it.' Jim stated, his voice sounding sympathetic, agreeing with everything that'd come from Sherwin. 'Exactly. It's good to know that we're on the same side when it comes to this matter. Blayze is a just a punk who can't do anything except moan, complain and undermine the competition... especially when it's proven to be better than him in contrast with the rest of the company.' Rich retorted, looking rather smug with himself, happy that he's been able to bring his point to the table and have it agreed upon by someone. 'Now, moving past this small issue of whether or not Blayze has been right to undermine you, how do you feel about Max Roswell? I mean, as far as my knowledge extends, the guy has been very quiet and hasn't said anything in public about either you or Blayze. Do you think this is his plan... or do you think the guy has been scared into keeping quiet?' Jim asked, actually sounding very interested. 'Max Roswell, he's a guy that I don't know too much about... although, to be more than honest with you Jim, I really haven't had all that much time to care about the quiet ones. Personally, I think he just isn't ready to compete with people and his silence is just a sentiment to the fact that he'll be getting into the ring with at least one person who is better than him in every way, shape and form.' Rich replied, sounding confident in both his tone and chosen lexis. Waiting a few moments for everything to settle, the superstar then continues, 'However, I looked at the record books of GW a day or so back and I found out that Roswell was actually zero and two against this Blayze fella. I was surprised myself at first, but my guess would be that Roswell is scared.' Jim nodded in agreement, making a brisk 'Hmmm' noise to inform the audience at home and wherever that he could see the point Rich was trying to make. 'It's interesting to hear an opinion like this and it's surprising that you have the evidence that you have. I mean, most people that come along and try to pass their opponent for a coward really don't have anything that would suggest any level of truth. In your case though, a zero-two record against an opponent can be something quite horrific. I should know about stuff like that too because in High School, I was actually kinda freaked by somebody who'd nailed me five times in a row at Chess.' 'It's a tough mental barrier to smash through,' Rich said enthusiastically before continuing, 'I really wouldn't blame Roswell if he chose to sit this one out and let Blazye try his luck against me alone. He could just tell the tens of people that follow his career that a very kind and gentle man called Rich Sherwin was going to avenge both of his defeats in one night.' McGee laughed in response, catching the blatant use of sarcasm in Sherwin's chosen words. 'Personally, I think it's a shame that Roswell hasn't made himself known in this week. The three of you seemed to put in pretty solid performances coming away from Clash of the Titans and I think everyone was rather interested to see who could start to establish their name in the company with a good win this week.' he stated, reverting from his humour to the more serious aspect of the situation. 'Well, if Blayze is able to show me that he really is a little more than the spark of a disused lighter then perhaps this match is going to be something worthwhile. If the guy can stop talking for a second and start to think about what kind of ability I have, what kind of moves I like to use, then perhaps you guys are going to be in for something of a great opener. Roswell really isn't going to be much of a challenge, that much I know for sure.' Sherwin said, giving what could very well be his final opinion of the bout in the lead up to it's occurance. 'Ok, well, before we head out for a short break and start to talk about the whole issue with Dylan Ford, is there anything else that you're wanting to say to the public about these two guys, Rich?' Jim asked, looking to get a few more words before he took the show off the air and went off for a much deserved break. 'I don't have much to say to either person to be honest. Evolution is going to be the first big show since the pay-per-view and I'm looking to walk away from the thing with nothing less than a win. Clash of the Titans showed me that I can get places when I put my mind to it. That one match, it told me that I had potential to be something here... and Evolution is going to be the start of that dream. Roswell and Blayze, you boys may have the same ideals and interests that I have however, THIS is going to be MY week.' With his final words, everything fades out into black, Jim coming in to say a final few words before the show went to it's five minute commercial period. Granted, Sherwin had made a lot of comments about the inability of Blayze, almost in the same way that Blayze had made about Sherwin. Undermining anyone was probably one of the most horrible mistakes to make and when two people were doing it, you can only imagine what kind of drama is going to come through when the contest actually begins. With a lack of word from Max Roswell, nobody even knows if the guy is going to turn up to Evo... let alone bother to say anything with regards to this rather important contest. Officially, this was going to be another opener on another GW event... but for the three guys, this was going to be the first step. : End :
