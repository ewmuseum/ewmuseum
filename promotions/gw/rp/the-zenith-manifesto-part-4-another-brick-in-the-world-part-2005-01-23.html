The Zenith Manifesto (Part 4: Another Brick In The World Part II)

23 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/23/05 The sounds of helicopters overhead greets the crowd below as they all peer into the sky hoping to get a glimpse of their new found liberators. Boom Boom Boom Boom Boom Boom Boom Boom The thunderous sounds of footsteps echoed in rhythm through the streets of Iraq. A symphony of screams and cheers lined the streets as the United State Liberation front marches single file down the dirt street. One kid was on his fathers shoulders holding an American flag high above his head. The people continued to cheer on their saviors, trying desperately to reach out and just say thank you, or just a hello at least. Tears of joy flowed down the cheeks of Iraqis and Americans alike, before suddenly their world comes to a halt. A deafening explosion bounces off the buildings and a cloud of smoke and fire shoots high into the air. A wave of shrapnel fires into the parade of humanity, as the ground shakes under its might. The tears of joy suddenly turned into the tears of agony and pain, the tears of sorrow and loss as a cloud of smoke and dust covers the gathering. The Sound of sirens cover up the screams of pain and suffering, just as the smoke and dust clouds cover up the visions of hate and terrorism at it's darkest. The smoke and dust clears to show a dark alley in the streets of Chicago, Illinois while the sirens fade into the background. Boom Boom Boom Boom Boom Boom Boom Boom The thunderous sounds of footsteps echoes in rhythm through the streets of Chicago, Illinois. A homeless man watching from behind a dumpster as the small army makes their way across town. A man wearing a black custom made suit leads the pack, his cold cadet blue eyes peering through the darkness to the light on the other end. Behind him, an army of men and woman alike... from white to black, from short to tall, from all different makes and models stand united, wearing a uniform of black. Tight black T-shirts, black cargo pants, and black shoes unite them together as one behind the lead of Christian Zenith. Zenith stops and puts his hand in the air, and the rhythmic marching comes to a halt behind him. His cool cadet blue eyes surveyed his surroundings, everything must be perfect. Christian Zenith blinked and leaned up against the wall impatiently. The dark streets of Chicago became the empty pearl colored walls of a school house. The faint sound of sirens became the faint lectures of teachers... the army of short and tall, became the army of short and shorter, the army of black became the army of white polo shirts, khakis or plaid skirts, and black one-colored shoes. When we grew up and went to school There were certain teachers who would Hurt the children any way they could Date: September 3, 1990 Time: 9:23 am Place: Alphonsus Academy & Center for the Arts Location: Chicago, Illinois 'Move it! I'm thirsty!' a little blonde haired girl shouts from near the end of the line. That very second Christian blinks and jumps back to life out of his daydream. He looks around at his surroundings before he steps forward into the line. The blonde haired rolls her eyes before she takes a step forward. A tall man looks on each child takes a step forward and takes a drink from the miniature sized drinking fountain. Mr. Harding, he was the teacher of this particular 1st grade class at Alphonsus Academy. He was around 6'3', 195 lbs. His hair was parted to the side and he wore a pair of thick rimmed tan colored glasses. Wearing his dress T-shirt and a black tie, and a bible in his pocket, along with black slacks and a pair of black shoes. He clapped his hands together as he looked down on the children... 'C'mon kids, we don't have all day, we have to get back to class!' he spoke as he peered down at 7 year old Christian Zenith. Christian approached the drinking fountain and took a drink. Taking slurps of water felt good to Christian, he'd been parched for quite some time. He knew that Mr. Harding wouldn't ever let anyone leave class to get water, as unfortunate as it was he had to get all he could now. Suddenly Zenith felt a tug on the back of his white polo shirt. Mr. Harding lifted him into the air by his shirt, and dropped him on his feet behind him at the end of the single-file line. Christian looked up at Mr. Harding... 'I said hurry up Christian, we can't sit here all day while you drink water. We have a lesson to finish.' Mr. Harding said. Christian glared back at him with his cold cadet blue eyes. Their eyes locked for the new few seconds while the last of the kids finished their drinks. As the blonde haired girl at the end got her drink Mr. Harding got in front of the line and began walking down the hallway. Movement started from the front, and one by one every child marched down the hallway behind him. Their heads were down and moping, but surely they didn't have much of a choice now, did they? By pouring their derision Upon anything we did And exposing every weakness However carefully hidden by the kids The door to the classroom swung open and the children scampered to their seats, which had little hand-drawn signs with their names on them. Mr. Harding held the door open as two straggling kids, Christian Zenith and the little blonde haired girl, slowly moped their way back to their seats. Mr. Harding closed the door firmly behind them and stood in front of the class. 'Sorry, but you guys might have to stay a little bit over now because two of your classmates decided that they were more important than the rest of you.' Mr. Harding said. 'But how are we going to catch the busses back home?!' One of the children shouted from the back. 'You might not, but don't worry, I'll call your parents to come pick you up. I'm sure the parents of the two children I'm speaking about will be thrilled to hear about why they have to come pick them up.' Mr. Harding spoke as he looked back and Christian Zenith, and the little blonde haired girl, who had a sign on her desk that said Katie Davis. The children sighed and whined as Mr. Harding pulled out some chalk and started writing math problems on the chalkboard behind him. Christian Zenith stood up and walked to the front of the classroom, startling Mr. Harding up front. 'Where do you think you're going?' Mr. Harding asked Zenith who looked back at him with frustration. 'Home, I'm not dealing with this.' Christian said as he walked towards the desk. 'You most certainly are not. Get away from that door and go sit in the corner Christian, I can't let you stall the progress of this class anymore!' Mr. Harding replied. 'Watch me.' Christian said as he walked over to the door and opened it. 'The office will be waiting for you young man!' Mr. Harding screamed as Christian slammed the door shut behind him. Zenith walked out into the empty hallway and began to walk towards the exit, when a taller girl caught his attention. He looked over and saw the girl, her brown hair went down just below her shoulders. She wore the typical uniform, white polo shirt and a plaid skirt... 'Christian?' she said confused as she saw her little brother walking down the hallway. Christian looked back up at Tera Zenith, frustrated from his day in class. 'Tera, I'm getting tired of these people pushing us around like we're their slaves. It's time we do something about this... I'm tired of being blamed for everything.' But in the town it was well known When they got home at night, their fat and Psychopathic wives would thrash them Within inches of their lives 'What happened?' Tera Zenith asked Christian, confused. 'My drink at the drinking fountain was 'too long.' Now Mr. Harding is going to hold us past the busses and call our parents to pick us up. He said he's going to tell mom and dad it's my fault. I didn't do anything besides drink water!' Zenith exclaimed. 'Yeah... he can't do that! Mrs. Buchanan sent me out into the hallway because I was flipping hair bands at classmates. I was actually trying to hit her. I'm tired of standing out here... I'm tired of being here!' Tera said 'No one should have to be here... all we're doing is sitting around and having things shoved down our throats and pushed around like we're sheep. I'm sick of it, we're all sick of it. I'm going to do something about it.' Tera Zenith said as she pulled the door open with authority. Christian Zenith watched on as his sister preached in front of the class and Mrs. Buchanan tried to shove her out the door. Her words were awe inspiring, as the kids jumped up and stood with her in defiance. Mrs. Buchanan shouted at the children to go back to their seats as Christian looked on with a stare of awe, his eyes wide and his mind open to knowledge. Suddenly Tera Zenith walked back out, along with her all her classmates. Tera smiled at Christian as she walked out... 'You know what to do.' She said with a smile as she and her classmates walked down the hallway. Christian turned around and sprinted back towards his class. He stopped and slid down the wax hallway in front of Mr. Harding's door. Christian grasped the handled and pulled the door open as hard as he could. The door slammed against the stone wall, making the hallway thunder under the door's might. The class looked on as Christian Zenith marched back into the classroom. Mr. Harding looked back at Christian with a stare of authority, Zenith shrugged it off as he marched into the class. 'I see you've changed your mind Mr. Zenith.' Mr. Harding said with a voice of success. 'You're right and I have. I'm sorry... I'm sorry that I left without all my friends.' Christian Zenith said, staring up at Mr. Harding. He looked back at his classmates as Mr. Harding looked down, shocked. 'Listen to me guys... I know I've just moved here and you all don't know me very well. But let me tell you something, we deserve better than this. We come in here everyday and do the most boring things imaginable. We write about red cows and do retarded math things, why? Because this guy up here tells us to? He's not our dad... our dad doesn't treat us the way this man treats us.' Zenith spoke as the class listened on, 'I'm tired of being treated like some kind of slave or animal. We may be small, but we too are people, and we don't have to take this kind of punishment. We are here to learn, not to be treated like slaves. He has NO RIGHT to come in here and take out his frustrations on us and it's time we do something about it!' 'That's it I've heard about enough!' Mr. Harding says as he pushes Christian Zenith into the wall, 'You get your little ass down to the office right now!' 'I'm not going anywhere!' Christian screams in pain as tears begin to flow down his eyes, 'There is only one of you, and sure we're small and you think you're so big pushing us around! But there are so many of us, you can't do a damn thing to stop us! Are you guys going to sit around and take this abuse from him?! Rise up and stop this tyranny, follow me in revolution! Just like George Washington did a long time ago!' Katie Davis got up from her seat and walked towards the door. Before she could get out Mr. Harding picked her up by the back of her shirt and lifted her into the air. Katie squirmed and tried to break her way loose with no success. Mr. Harding looked down at Christian, who pointed behind himself. We don't need no education We don't need no thought control No dark sarcasm in the classroom Teachers leave them kids alone Hey teacher leave them kids alone All in all it's just another brick in the wall All in all you're just another brick in the wall Mr. Harding looked up and saw Tera Zenith, and all her classmates behind her. Tera looked up at the bully as he held Katie Davis by the back of her shirt. 'Let's go!' Tera Zenith shouted into the classroom. Suddenly, like a stampede of cattle, the children rushed to the door knocking over Mr. Harding by sheer force. Katie Davis is dropped to the ground, she gets up and runs away with the other children. Christian Zenith smiles at his sister as the classroom is cleared and Mr. Harding lies on his stomach in the room. The kids rush down the hallways with screams of victory and revolution. Tera and Christian run down the hallway opening all the classroom doors . One by one all the classrooms clear, like protest march. The hallways are lined from wall to wall, packed with children on parade. Papers are being thrown high into the air and books being ripped apart under the feet of miniature protesters. Waves of children tore down the posters in the hallway in display of rebellion. The children ransacked the hallways, beating the drinking fountains and breaking the windows to the outside. They turned over the benches used for lunch and invaded the gymnasium, where some children then proceeded to play one HUGE game of basketball. What was one big in-school recess to the children became widespread panic to the heavily out numbered staff. While some teachers are out in the hallway trying to calm everyone down, others lock themselves in their classrooms and watch the chaotic mutiny from far far away. The office workers leave the front office and try to shove their way through the sea of children. The principle stands in the middle of the crowd and screams... 'WHAT IS GOING ON OUT HERE?!' Her response is a shower of boos and paper balls. Suddenly pencils start flying and she covers up her face in fear as she's being assaulted by a barrade of sharpened #2's. The mod suddenly picks her up off her feet and crowd surfs her to the nearest exit. A group of older children kick the door open and together the protesters throw her out of the school and onto the sidewalk outside. The mutiny cheers in success as Tera Zenith screams... 'WE'VE WON! THE SCHOOL IS OURS!' We don't need no education We don't need no thought control No dark sarcasm in the classroom Teachers leave them kids alone Hey teacher leave them kids alone All in all you're just another brick in the wall All in all you're just another brick in the wall Christian Zenith smiles and begins jumping up and down along with the rest of the kids before the school busses arrive, and the children file onto them one by one wanting to go home after the huge fiasco. The school busses take them to their houses where they have a normal day with their families. Until the 5 o'clock news of course, where all the children got their lectures from their parents. Tera and Christian in particular, who were said on the news to be the leaders of the Alphonsus Academy Mutiny. Christian and Tera were both grounded for a month from everything. Tera was quickly placed into Chicago public schools while Christian began home schooling with a professor from the military. In the end though, their mother and father couldn’t complain too much... they were getting along for once, and that meant more to them than any school performance could in the long run.
