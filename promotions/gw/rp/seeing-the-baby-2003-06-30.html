Seeing the baby

30 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/30/03 Brick has just arrived at the hospital. His friend Aaron called him in Texas with amazing news. Aaron’s wife Felicia was giving birth to their first child. The birth itself is two weeks ahead of schedule. Brick managed to book himself on the next flight back to New York, because he didn’t want to wait too long to see his new godson or goddaughter. A year ago, he was afraid he would never see this day, after the Harvesters kidnapped, tortured and nearly killed him. The time Aaron spent in physical therapy had put their plans of starting a family on hold. But, months later, Felicia discovered she was pregnant. “May I help you?” asked the security guard. “Yeah, a friend of mine gave birth last night.” “Labor and Delivery is on the fifth floor.” “OK, thanks.” Brick walks to the elevator and presses up. After getting off the elevator on the fifth floor, Brick then walks up to the front desk. “Excuse me, um, what room is in Felicia Foster?” “Are you a member of her family?” “I’m the godfather.” “The baby was just born last night.” “Hey Brick!” Brick turns to see Aaron standing in a doorway, gesturing for him to come to him. Brick automatically walks over to Aaron. “Hey man. I got here as fast as I could.” “You didn’t have to do that.” “Hey, I’m the godfather. Yes, I did.” “Yeah, but you got a lot on your plate as it is.” “Is that Jamal?” Felicia calls from in the room. “Yeah, it’s me, Felicia.” Brick walks into the room. Felicia lies in the bed, no make-up on her face, her hair slightly tousled, and yet she looks no less radiant for having given birth. In her arms she holds a small brown-skinned baby girl “Hey,” he says before kissing her on the cheek. “You look great.” “Jamal Randall, you are the worst liar on the planet.” “Yeah, that’s true,” replies Aaron, “but he’s not lying this time.” “She’s beautiful. So, have you named her?” “Ashley,” says Felicia. “Ashley Lillian Foster.” “Ashley Lillian Foster?” “What’s wrong with that?” “Her initials spell ALF. As in “Dial 10-10-220”. As in furry little puppet.” “Oh, don’t even start, Brick. You once told me you wanted to name your first son Prince.” “What’s wrong with Prince?” “Oh, nothing.” Aaron then goes “OOH-WAH!” in a high falsetto and starts doing an impression of the Purple One. Brick playfully shoves him. “Will you two stop?” “So, how’s the baby?” “She’s fine. We thought there would be something wrong, but the doctor said that even two weeks ahead is still full-term.” “I got a little goddaughter.” “So, what’s up with you?” “Oh, the best news I could get. I had a match with Derek Frost. If I won, I got Sage Sabbot at Whiplash in the Pit.” “So, how’d you do?” “I won.” “You don’t sound happy about it.” “It was a win by DQ. The damn Upper Echelon interfered in the match.” “What happened?” “That little bitch Holmes hit me with a steel chair. We both got screwed.” “What exactly is the Pit, anyway?” “It’s one of our most brutal matches. It’s in a round, open air iron cell, in the ground. The only way to win is by submission or by knockout. And it’s where I get Sabbot. He can’t run from me now.” “Then shouldn’t you be concentrating on that?” “Ah well, I guess the important thing is now I have another shot at the World title, and this time, I’m gonna win. Four years I’ve had to wait. I’m not waiting any longer.” “Well, good luck, man. We’re all gonna be rooting for you.” “Thanks. Well, I guess I should be going now. Congratulations, you two.” “Bye, Brick.” “Bye, Felicia.” “Bye.” “Bye, ALF.” “Stop calling her that!” As Brick leaves the room, he realizes taht a new chapter in his life has begun. And at Whiplash, he sees another chapter beginning.
