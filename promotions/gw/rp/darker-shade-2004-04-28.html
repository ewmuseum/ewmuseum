Date: 4-28-2004 *Ding!* The door to the elevator slowly opened, revealing inside, Kali-Ann (still no last name folks!). She’s dressed in her usual type day wear, baggy, white leather pants, and an extremely tight, and white shirt, sporting the logo of the long dead band Life of Agony. She casually moves to step from the elevator, but I promptly stopped as a man moves into sight before her? The sight of this man is enough to cause her to gasp as she looks up, past his Armani-covered, 6-foot frame, to the long, black hair and devilish face? Kali: “Niklaus?” Um? WHAT?! Ok dudes, for those of you who don’t recognize the name, think back to the intro of the girls? He’s the evil casino owner who liked to use Kali for sex and use Kali to keep Miranda working for him? Pulling down his shades, he stares down at Kali through those crystal blue eyes, smirking lightly and responding. Niklaus: “Kali-Ann” Hello dear?” Kali swallows, and lets off a nervous chuckle. Kali: “Hi” Um like, what are ya doing here?” Niklaus: “I came to see my favorite girl. And to see what success is bringing her in life. Nothing more, just to talk.” Kali licks over her lower lip and nods. She glances back into the elevator, and then to Niklaus again before speaking. Kali: “Miranda will be home soon though, you don’t wanna be here when she gets back.” Niklaus: “Oh” I’m sure she’ll be forgiving to our last meeting, if not, I’ll just hafta take the pain to show her I’m serious about making amends? You’re the one person who’s ever made me feel complete, Kali-Ann? I don’t want to lose your friendship? Can I please come up?” That was all it really took from the sly talking Niklaus to melt Kali? Kali smirks, and slowly backs into the elevator, followed by Niklaus. On a leather sofa at the far end, close to the window where Kali loves to peer out over the city, we now find the two sitting very closely? Niklaus: “I could give you a nicer home” A home even nicer than this one?” Kali draws in her lower lip. A soft smile of appreciation comes to her lips, but she knows she doesn’t wish to return to that life? She loves the feel of independence, the feel of being famous, over a span of more than just the Strip? Kali: “It’s so a wonderful offer, Nik” But we have so much going for us. We’ve even been invited to a totally bad tournament where if we win, we get like, a shot to become the tag team champions! That’s where the paycheck truly is? I..I just can’t up and leave it. Gone are the combat boots (shocker!).
