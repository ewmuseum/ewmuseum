More Of Memories

28 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/28/03 Pain Is Only A Pulse If You Just Stop Feeling It -Coheed and Cambria Pain Is Weakness Leaving The Body -United States Marine Corp. Pain Is Just A Little Something To Help You Remember That You Are Alive -Seth Buchanan Visions of the past flash through his mind? He stands in front of the tombstone, holding a red rose in his hand and tears in his eyes. He looks down and reads it? Won Lo Tse Ling October 11th, 1922 ? July 22nd, 1981 Forgotten By Nothing Remembered By All Remembering the memories’never hurt so bad. He holds Melissa in his arms. Bloodied and torn, nothing else can describe her after such a horrible accident. She speaks, with pain in each and every word. “I’ll be with you forever and ever.” “I know you will.” “In your heart.” Her body falls limp in his arms. Dead and deceased. He holds her tighter then ever for the first time, and for the last time. Love has a mind of its own. She spoke the greatest words alive through the pain. Speaking to him hurt her more then anything. Having her die in his arms hurt him more then anything. The pain, falling down his throat’choking back on each tear. Wishing it was all a nightmare. The memories that will never be forgotten. The memories that will last forever. He stands in front of Jessica’s grave. Realizing what could have happened. What he could have done. He cries’not because of her death. But because of the lost opportunity of leaving her and her leaving him. What would have happened if she just gave him that chance? Maybe she’d be alive. He sheds another tear. Maybe she’d be with him right now. Proud’that is what she’d be if she would have given him the chance. Sometimes I wish that you can forget the memories. Sometimes I wish they will never be remembered. Forgotten, dropped off the face of the Earth, but that would be too great. Some say that bad memories remain, so you can learn from them. But what happens when there is no lesson. I’ve been questioning that for years. Hopefully the good memories filter out in my mind. Maybe to even the sadness and sorrow that the bad memories have. The good memories, maybe God granted me something special. Maybe he felt guilty after everything he has done to me. A gift, each good memory, only wishing to have more and more for the future to come. Sitting in my Buick’driving to the Prom. An angel from heaven sits beside me. Her name is Melissa, beautiful at the least. Her smile made of pearl and her hair made of the finest material in the world. Her eyes, they sparkle like diamonds. Just the feeling, just the honor of her going to something so important with me. A wonderful memory. A smile’once hidden, now shown. He sits down talking to Lisha. Looking at the smiling son of Salaok. Uncle Sage, just has that special ring to it. A special feeling’never felt before. Just feels great to hear Lisha tell Darrel that I am apart of their family now. Salaok, my father’my mentor. A new found family. The Nevermore’not just business. I’ve finally found my place. I’ve finally found my home. Many say, “home is where the heart is.” And my heart is with The Nevermore. I’ve bled for The Nevermore and I’ve busted my ass for The Nevermore for a damn good reason. And I have no regrets in doing so. His heart lifts off the ground. He takes a deep breath of relief. It feels good to breath every now and then. Sighing’just something to help ease the pain. Remembering memories’only the good ones, offers more relief. He wrenches back on Brick’s arm. Nothing is heard through his mind and nothing is seen through the blood, his and Brick’s pouring down his face. A battle of the ages, his greatest match ever. His greatest accomplishment ever. Brick taps out’his arm raised towards the heavens above. A new GW World’s Champion. A new reason to live. A new reason to smile. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- He sits in a chair, one of those old fashioned ones. The old orange and brown chairs that was only beautiful to your grandmother. The same one with the yellow and brown flowers on it. So ugly, but so comfortable. As he sits, he ponders each thing in his mind. Thinking about each memory. Wishing he could relive them, just for excitement’just for another experience. A memory, one of the good ones, is about to leave. Destined for greatness, no matter where he goes. His father’Salaok leaving. SAY IT ISN’T SO Feels like a double jeopardy. Having two of his fathers, leaving the scene’leaving his life. They’ll live on forever in his mind and in his heart. The memories will never fade. The memories, they’ll live on and be passed down forever and ever. Each time spoken’creating a legacy. Now all that is left are pictures on the wall. The memories and the stories that are told. A photo album, a video cassette’none will feel the same. Not one will feel real. He, Salaok’he has molded me into what he wanted me to be. Coming into the Global Wrestling with Shynobi Kai, I was weak and pathetic. He molded me into an unstoppable machine. Like he told everybody he would. I didn’t make me who I am’something I regret. He has made me who I am. A student of the School Of Pain. A product of his talent. I am the son of Salaok without being related to him. Quartz, Salaok, Johnathon Davis, Jay Vyper’they all have a place in my life. They each took the place of my father, the place of my mother. The place of Melissa, the place of Margo, the place of Jessica. These men, these heroes’my new found family’they mean more to me then they’ll ever imagine. It hurts’the pain can’t be described right now. Pain is only a pulse if you just stop feeling it. What happens if you can’t stop feeling it? Pain is weakness leaving the body’I’d be the strongest man alive. Pain is something to help me remember that I’m alive’I’d live forever. It hurts to realize, another bad memory is about to happen. I can’t even say goodbye. Dreading the last time I’ll see him. More then a father’a friend, a mentor’somebody who is there for me in the ring and out of the ring. I Dread Goodbyes say it isn’t so
