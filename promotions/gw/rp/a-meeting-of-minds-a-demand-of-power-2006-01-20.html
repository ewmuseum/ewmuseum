A meeting of minds, a demand of power

20 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/20/06 The Roll’s pull up through the courtyard during the dead of night, its head lights casting an eerie light on the frozen grounds of the old castle. It was the fifth rolls to pull through in the past two days. Michael Cervantes leaned against the balcony, dressed in a three piece suit, his eyes piercing the darkness from the highest balcony in the castle. Many people had arrived to talk to Johnathan Davis, a man many believed dead or at least out of the picture. His sudden reappearance has brought many prominent members of his underground group “The Lair” out of there billion dollar holes to come and find out just what the Master of Fear had planned. Cervantes sighed and ran a hand through his growing hair. He had asked about his brother but John had dismissed it, telling him it was of no consequence any longer now that this grand scheme of his had been put into motion. Michael knew where he stood in this plot, he was muscle, he was the one who would do the work while John pulled the strings, ever since he met John he knew that the man loved to pull the strings. He could hear his chilling chuckle coming through the fire place, he had been talking to some woman for quite a while now, someone whom he said “Could help point the Lair in a common direction,” and by common he meant his. Turning towards the noise he heads back inside the castle, closing the large windows that lead to the balcony on which he’d been spending so much time since arriving in this home of the Davis family. The interior is beautiful, tapestries hang along every wall and oriental rugs are thrown across every floor. Candles lined the halls and hung in the rooms more for effect as there were lights placed in hidden locations to actually allow those traveling through the long corridors to see. Stopping just short of the central chamber Cervantes leans towards the door listening to the smooth voice of The Scarecrow as he continued to speak. “My brothers absence has reverted control of the Davis family castle back into my hands and thus this will be the base of operations for my intended goals.” Quietly stepping into the room, Cervantes does not arouse much attention blending into the background quietly. Johnathan Davis stood in front of the fire dressed in a silk three piece suit, his trademark hat and trench coat were hanging on a rack in the corner, he held a glass with brandy in his hand and a smug look on his face. His long hair was tinged with gray and the corner of his eyes had begun to wrinkle but his eyes still held the same fire. “Intended goals” You have brought us here to explain some grand scheme, if I remember correctly Mr. Davis you were removed from the inner circle years ago and replaced by your brother.” the remark came from a man who even in his seat he seemed tall. His skin was tan his hair almost white, he wore sunglasses and nursed a cigar as he spoke. “Removed” You cannot remove the base without destroying the whole building! As such you cannot remove me! Without me you would have never gained as much influence as you did. Who brought us money? Who brought us almost god like power? Who brought us influence in every major facet of business, art and politics?” He lets the words hang, fury barely held back inside the cold exterior. The man’s voice was majestic if hard. “I have seen you flounder with this new found strength, unable to direct it, unable to harvest it to a real goal. But I have returned to guide you, to guide the circle. My pet project has grown by leaps and bounds. That tottering fat fool is of great use and has a sound business mind, even if he does not know whom it is he works for.” He smiles, the smile the world has come to know. “It is generating money and influence not only over prospective business men but the common man. Through indirect channels I have built this company and now I would like to turn it into our strongest vehicle. I request?” he chuckles. “No, I demand the use of all Lair personnel and resources that I require to fully turn this new vehicle into the true juggernaut that it could become.” The woman whom Cervantes had seen arrive earlier lounges along the length of a wall on a futon, he curvaceous body hugged by a black dress that left little to the imagination. “And what of him?” she says in a breathy voice nodding slowly towards Michael. John glances over at Cervantes and raises his glass as if in a toast. “He is my weapon. A true killer in every sense of the word. Descended from good stock and well educated both in intellectual and martial areas, he is perfect for the task at hand.” Cervantes nods to the rest of the group who had begun to stare at him, some with disfavor as Cervantes was not okayed by the Lair to be brought into this form of confidence, but Johnathan Davis rarely cared about what the Lair thought and although he was technically without power he never ceased believing that he was truly its driving force. “But first?” he continues, “We must remove obvious opposition, although a man I have never considered a threat he has always been a nuisance and some day he may just get lucky enough to throw a wrench into our plans. We will make our hostilities known, we will draw him out and we will break him.” John voice holds an old hate with every word. “Michael here has been studying under me for some time and has had ample time to study past files regarding this little thorn, both him and I are confident in our ability to break his spirit?” then he adds, ??and his back.” The man with the white hair finally stands, at least a foot and a half taller then John, he walks over to John and stands over him for a moment before nodding his head. “You’ll get what you need, but we expect results, and soon. Don’t screw up this time John. As beneficial as you have been, you are still expendable.” he says, turning his back and walking out the door his green eyes locking with Cervantes for but a moment, an understanding of hardship falling between them. Cervantes smiles, a cruel mockery of Johnathan Davis? own as the man walks out followed by others, the last to remain are John, Cervantes and the woman on the couch. “John” she says, “We will be watching you, the coffers are open and the barracks are at your disposal, I see brilliance in this but I also see disaster. Show me brilliance John.” with that she stands, regal. Stepping with grace and knowing how to move her body in the ways that drive men crazy she stops by Cervantes and leans close. “My name is Victoria” I will be watching you as well.” Then there were two, the room was at the center of the castle, not large by any means but furnished well, a large fireplace dominated the scene, its coals now sputtering heat. A painting of a man who looked much like John but still different hangs over it. Sitting atop the bookcase in a glass dome is a mask, stitched from burlap and several other materials it’s a patchwork of terror, a crooked mouth and slots for eyes. For a moment it seems to hold the same blue as the blue in Johns own eyes. “Interesting piece of work huh?” he says gesturing towards it with his glass. “I keep it as a symbol of what you can accomplish with a bit of theatrics. In my youth people feared that mask” funny what it did to them when they saw it. Ask Bubba himself and he could tell you stories I’m sure.” he glances at it for a moment, as if he couldn’t or rather wouldn’t hold it’s gaze, but Michael stares. “Were leaving in the morning?” he asks. “Yes” comes the reply. “Do you think he’ll be surprised?” John only smiles, placing the glass on the mantle to be picked up by the servant later. He turns and walks out the door, leaving Cervantes alone? alone?
