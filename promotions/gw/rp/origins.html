An excerpt from the memoirs of Corvus: I don’t think there is one person in the history of mankind that can say they remember the day of their birth. I’m one of those exceptions ... kind of. Technically I have had two births: the one in which I was brought into this world by my mother and then there is the one where I was taken out of it and reborn into darkness. I remember it as if it were yesterday. All the pain, anguish, and torment I went through before, during, and after I had become this creature that I am now. It was then that I met the devil ... but she wasn’t in a blue dress. It was five and a half months after my daughter had passed and my wife had been rushed away by her family. I had begun a drinking binge which consisted of drinking massive amounts of ale before passing out in the streets. I had given up hope on anything and the only time I couldn’t feel any pain was when I was too drunk to function. On what was a rather warm evening in the year of 808, (after death or a.d.) I had gone to the tavern that night as usual to get my drink on and during the course of the evening I had been approached by a rather beautiful woman. She was as beautiful as could be. Fire red hair, an ample bosom, a body made for sin. In other words and I wouldn’t be wrong ... she was a woman of ill repute. However, she knew just what to say and of course with the alcohol I had already consumed it made decisions a little more care free. After a few more rounds she convinced me to “join her” in her room above the tavern I pulled myself off out of the chair I was sitting in and staggered my way towards the stairs. The floor boards creaked under my feet that were heavy from having too much to drink. We walked up the steps, where I stumbled just a bit. She lent a hand on getting me there being that I was not in the condition to climb stairs. We finally made it to the room; it wasn’t a much more than a bed, a chair and a table (much like today’s but less modern convenience). The woman then led me to the bed and pushed me down onto it. She began to make her move and I didn’t object; who wouldn’t in my position? Of course I had to be proper and stopped her for a mere second before asking her name. All she said was “Celine” and continued on with seducing me in a way that would put women today to shame. Celine wasn’t at all interested in my name nor the fact that I smelled of not only ale but body odor as I had not bathed in a while, but then again she already knew who I was it seemed ... I just didn’t have any clue to it. She didn’t just want my body either, it was what was inside of it that she had wanted but of course that never usually comes out in conversation that you want someone’s blood; it is kind of tacky if you ask me. The night progressed and without going into details other than intercourse was had ... several times I might add before the defining moment which changed everything I knew in a mere matter of moments ... forever. When you are having your neck kissed rarely do you ever think that the woman doing it is going to have mega sharp teeth and sink them into your neck. Much to my surprise that is what happened and I was helpless against the attack. My mortal strength was not even a challenge to the vampire strength she displayed. My life was slipping from me little by little; my heart slowing as the essence of what gave me life flowed through me and into her, giving her nourishment out of my misfortune. I was fading fast and she knew it before I did and that is when I was given the proposition ... As I was about to die she hit me with that question that you really don’t have much choice to answer unless you are a complete fool. “Aric, do you wish to die ... if not, I can give you things you could never imagine.” Promises, promises ... of course this lead to some things that I will get to in a moment but as much as I wanted to die, more of me didn’t want to. My belief in vampires changed as I was about to become one. I was raised Catholic and my drinking and whoring pretty much condemned me to hell; little did I know that what was about to happen to me would be the closest thing possible ... the next thing I knew was the lights went out in the house and there was nothing but darkness. When my eyes opened I could not see a thing; the only thing I could discern is that it was nighttime. If there was such a thing as claustrophobia back then I would have been panicking in overtime. I moved my hands the best I could and realized that I had been buried alive! I clawed my way out as best as I could and felt my hand poking through the dirt, feeling the air pass over it, which was neither cool nor warm in feeling. My body felt like I had been thrown in freezing water but it all dissipated as I pulled myself out of the grave. There was a twitch in my stomach as I staggered a few steps until I fell to my knees. The reality hit harder than a wooden chair to the back of my skull. I could no longer feel my pulse and my heart did not beat at all. That was the first sign that I had changed and the other signs became apparent. My first experience with the inner beast in me hit like a sword being run through my midsection. The hunger was building and the only way I knew how to cure it was to find some peasants before the light of day were upon me. I had no clue what I was capable of or the fact that the one who made me was supposed to teach me everything I needed to know ... she was nowhere to be found and this left me with a feeling that I might not make it very long in my new life after all. I was able to find peasants and beggars walking around and pulled them into alleys so I could not be seen and their screams could not be heard. I even for the fun of it pulled a few hussies with because of my detestation for the one who put this curse upon me. I had been very thirsty and when you are first embraced that is to be expected ... the killing ended with my fifth victim. I would have gone for a record but I became full and the sun was just about to peak out. Instincts told me that I needed to find shelter. Some nights I begin to wonder if I should have let her kill me completely ... then I think about how it brought me back to the one true love I ever had: Lia. v--v “Hello Aric ... ? Corvus froze as the voice resounded in his ears and a thousand years and change suddenly smacked him in the very fiber of his immortality. He couldn’t turn around; he didn’t want to because that would be admitting that what he heard was real, which was something he did not want to do. He closed his eyes; his voice filled with a haunted tone as he spoke in response. “It’s been a long time ... Celine.” Indeed it had been a long time since they last had contact with one another. In fact, it has been since that first night he became one of the creatures of the dark. She could sense the fact that he wasn’t excited to see or hear her voice. The sneer on his face that she couldn’t see was a good indication as well. “Please, call me Rhianna ... that is what I go by now, Rhianna de Vorace now.” “And I prefer to be called Corvus ... the name Aric died when my mortal body did. You should know a little bit about that since you played a major part in it.” The tension on Corvus? side was rather thick but the woman remained collected even if he was right about the statement he made. She did help bring him into the land of the undead, also abandoned him as well. He had a right to be just a little perturbed. “Ok ... that’s fine with me. I understand you are a little upset with what happened but that is why I am here. I am here to make things right between us and to offer you some advice.” Upon hearing those words he chuckled loudly in a mocking tone as there was nothing she could offer him besides her head on a platter that would give him any satisfaction. The entire existence he spent as a vampire had been one he paved on his own and no thanks to her. “There really is not much you can say or do to prevent me from separating your head from your body.” He said menacingly. “The time when I needed you the most is when you left me to fend on my own ... I learned what I needed to know from someone that could have killed me but instead took me under their wing.” Rhianna made a bold move and moved closer to him; she stood right behind him and put a soothing hand onto his shoulder which he shrugged off immediately, not wanting to have anything to do with her at all and he couldn’t be blamed at all. “You have no idea how much I regret that. I was inexperienced back then and there was not anything I could offer you that wouldn’t get you killed anyway. I knew you were strong and that you would be able to survive and here we are now.” She said fondly as if she had a hand in what he had become. “You’re one of the most powerful of us that still walks among the sheep; I’m very proud of you ...” “Inexperienced ... ha! You don’t expect me to believe that do you” I mean you went on to sire others and did the same thing to them. You didn’t expect me to not hear the same thing from James when I found him did you?” Rhianna crossed her arms as she was about to get defensive for his comments about one of her childer. She hated being told that she was a terrible vampire at least when it came to ushering in the ones she brought into the darkness. “James is a special case ... he was weak; I made a mistake with him. I didn’t expect him to live long after I embraced him. However, he was lucky because he ran into you. You have no idea of your importance in this world, do you” You spend too much time catering to the mortals in which you should be using to nourish yourself. I can’t say that I’m happy about that but you still do play a silent role in the vampire world as well.” Corvus finally did what he thought was impossible and turned around to face her. She hadn’t changed a bit except for her mode of dress was quite a bit different. She still looked like a prostitute ... “old habits die hard”, he thought. As far as playing a role was concerned even though there was this invisible war going on between vampires, he really didn’t give two shits about it since he managed to keep a good chunk of his humanity and you could liken it to him wanting to tune his guitar instead of taking out a bunch of punk vampires. “If we had kept up all these years I would feel comfortable telling you that I think you talk too much but I really can’t seeing as last time I saw you we weren’t doing much talking at all.” He chuckled as his comfort level increased and reached into his pocket where his lighter and cloves were. He pulled out the pack and one of the little black devils; slipped it in his mouth and lighted it. “There’s a reason why I came here and I don’t sense that reason ... so where is Lia” You said you had her and that is what brought me here. If she’s not here then I’m getting the hell out of here before I decide to take out a thousand plus years of frustration out on you.” She frowned hoping that he had changed and would try to work with her but he was being stubborn which she was not surprised about. She watched him over the years and his various travels. There was never a time when she didn’t know something about him. “Ari ... Corvus, I’m sorry. I never had her captive at all; I just thought it would be the best way to get you to meet me. I knew you wouldn’t if you knew it was me trying to contact you. I knew she had left Cleveland and it was the best way to get you here. There are just some things you should know ...” He listened while she gave him enough reasons to be pissed off. He flicked some ash onto the ground as he stared at her (it was more like a glare actually). His head shook as he rubbed his chin thinking that he should leave and not deal with this bull that she was trying to pull. “There’s nothing you could say to me that would put any of my trust into you. You had me followed by the worst ghoul ever and have him break into my house. I’m sure you know by now that I hacked him into pieces then incinerated him.” Those were some good times he couldn’t help admitting to himself. Grinding old Jack into bits really did wonders for him and renewed him spiritually you could say. “I wouldn’t hesitate to say that you fed a tip to the cops about the girl I fed on a few weeks ago just to see what would happen. Did it hurt you in some way that I hold contempt for street walkers” There’s a pretty good reason for it ... no reason for me to draw a diagram.” He could tell that he was getting to her because she was trying to hold her composure and not attack him. He probably inherited his temper from her most likely, it was a blood trait. She waited a few more moments to cool off before she tried once more to get him to hear her out. “I don’t know what you are even talking about ... but the one thing I do know is that you need to really keep tabs on your so-called wife. If you had any idea of what she was up to you just might save yourself some disappointment.” She rubbed the back of her neck because that was something she did when she was nervous about something. What would make him believe her? He was exhibiting hostile emotions towards her already. “I realize that you won’t believe anything I say but I’m going to just put out a warning anyway. Watch yourself around her ... just remember when she tosses you aside, I won’t say that I told you so but I will be here if you need me.” Corvus did everything he could to not ball his fist up and punch her directly in the jaw. He dropped the finished clove butt onto the ground and squished it with his boot. “Despite the fact that Lia and I have been touch and go the past several years we have common ground now. It’s nice to know that you’re looking out for me.” He starts to turn away but before he does he turns and looks at her once more. “By the way, I can smell your goons a mile away. If you want them to die tell them to come and play ... I’m just in that kind of mood.” “They aren’t here to attack you. They are here to protect me; I just want you to know that you should be careful ... I’ve heard the Assamite are still looking for you. Take care of yourself; I hope to see you soon.” Corvus did the thing he thought was right and stood his ground by turning and leaving the situation before it became ugly. He couldn’t deny the fact that he wanted to kill her and have the kind of revenge that most people never get a chance to have. He stops as he gets to the exit. A chuckle is heard from his rough voice when he takes a moment to think about the Assamite coming for him. There is only one more thing left for him to say. “If they want me, they know where to find me; as for you seeing me soon ... not if I sense you coming.” That was all that he said before he disappeared back into the darkness and the sound of his motorcycle starting. The bike took off and Rhinanna gazed at the exit where he had left. “Just wait and see my dear, you will be severely disappointed.”
