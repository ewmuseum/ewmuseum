The Return: Part 1

19 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19th October 2004 Absence... They say that absence makes the heart grow fonder, but is that? I have been away from the thing I love most for over a month. Over that month I have felt my body deteriorate, I have felt my mind give up hope, I have drifted away from reality, not communicating with anyone, letting myself become nothing but a shell. However over the month I have fought, I have battled, I have rebuilt my body, I have trained ten times as hard, all for one reason to return to the GW. I was ripped from the GW like a baby being ripped from its mother’s womb. It left me feeling cold and alone, like I had no chance to defend myself, it was like I had lost a limb. My world was shattered but now the pieces are back together and I am back to what I love... Like I said it has been over a month since I stepped in the GW. Many thought they would never see me again after the malicious and vicious attack from the Flying Vergomovz. When I was carted away in that ambulance many saw it as the end of the legacy of Adam Myers. Okay perhaps I hadn't made such a huge impact in the GW as I was only here for about 2 months. However I do believe that I did make some sort of impact, I mean I was the first GW superstar to win the Cruiserweight title in his second match, which is something at least. The Cruiserweight title is one of the many reasons I returned to the GW, when I was laying in that ambulance and in that hospital bed I knew that the belt would be stripped away from me and at that moment I knew I had to return. Now yet again I have to win the belt that was wrongly taken away from me. I have to face 2 new faces on the GW roster, who are thirsty for success but just because I have been here longer than them does not mean I do not thirst for the championship also. Anyway I had hoped to return to exact my revenge on the unexpecting Flying Vergomovz, however news got to me that they had run away from the GW with their tails between their legs. To be honest I was disappointed at the news but I am satisfied with the fact that I am back in the GW and have the chance to reclaim what is mine. However it has been quite the struggle getting here... 'Well your leg seems fully healed Mr. Myers.' The doctor was leaning over my legs with a grin on his face. He seemed pleased to break the news to me, but I knew my body, I knew my leg was fine, the only thing was I didn't care. 'Oh that is great news isn't it honey?' My wife, Rachel was holding my hand at the side of the bed with the same grin as the doctor. She had her other hand on her stomach where the bump of our baby was beginning to form, but yet again I didn't care. 'Thanks doctor for telling us the great news.' 'It is always nice to have some good news to give to patients. You take care, Mr. Myers hope we don't have to see you in here for a long time.' The doctor flashed me another grin and seemed to be waiting for me to say 'Oh thank you doctor you have been great' but I didn't because I didn't care. I simply twisted my head and looked at my wife, the one who was supposed to make me smile everyday but for the past month not one smile had crossed my mouth. I hated myself for it, I knew every night she got up and went to bathroom and cried for the rest of the night. I wanted to console her and say I was sorry but my own stupid pride stopped me. I love my wife, more than anything, yet there I was not able to tell her because my pride had been wounded. She looked at me with such pity in her eyes and such love, yet behind my mask, my face was like stone. The doctor left and I watched him walk down the hospital corridor all the way till he disappeared through the doors. Rachel rested her head on my arm and let out a sigh. 'Oh Adam, what is wrong with you? You have been like this for a month. Ever since that incident you have hardly said a word. You have hardly touched me, you don't seem to care anymore. I don't even know if you still love me, you are just so cold all the time.' My mind was screaming at me to tell her I love her and that she means everything to me, but there a lay staring up at the hospital light. I felt her look up at me, looking for a sign, looking for something, just to let her know I still loved her, yet I didn't move a muscle. She let out another sigh and weeped a little. 'It is like you are dead Adam and all that is left is this empty shell. You eat and sleep that is the only basis that tells me you are alive. This isn't like you Adam, usually you would see this as just another obstacle to get over and you would train harder, just so you could keep on top of your game but you haven't even looked at a gym. Peter is incredibly worried about you Adam, he is calling everyday. Will you just say something Adam, just so I know you are still with me.' I sat up and moved to the edge of the bed. Rachel wiped away the tears on her cheeks and looked up at me with hope in her eyes, hope I was about to destroy. 'It's time to go home.' Rachel stood up and huffed with frustration. 'Adam Jacob Myers!' She shouted at me, she always used my full name when she was angry with me. 'You are not the only one in this marriage. It may have escaped your attention but we are soon to become a family and I would like our son or daughter to have a father who actually interacted with them. But you don't care do you Adam?!?!' Again I made no reaction I simply stared straight-ahead not even looking at her. 'Oh what is the point! Fine, I will just go sign you out at reception.' She stormed off, muttering to herself, leaving me to my thoughts. Why was I being like this? Why was I tearing my marriage apart for no good reason? The truth is I didn't even know, there I sat shouting at myself in my head, to get my act together, to run after Rachel and make it all right. It was like I was caught in limbo with no escape. It had started when I was in that ambulance with Kitty Mac and El Serpenti over me, telling me that it would be all right, that I would be back in no time. But as the pain subsided I felt nothing just a dark cloud over me. My train of thought was cut short as I heard footsteps approaching. I jumped down from the bed, thinking it was Rachel but instead my best friend and lawyer Peter Willows appeared. His face was full of rage. 'What the fuck is the matter with you Adam?!?!' Peter screamed not caring who overheard him, he only used swear words in extreme circumstances. 'Just leave it Pete.' I said with no emotion sitting back on the bed. 'No I will not leave it Adam! I just pumped into Rachel crying her eyes out in that corridor. That is your wife Adam, crying her eyes out over you, blaming herself for your problem. Does that not make you upset? Does that not want to make you console her?' 'I said leave it Pete.' 'Again no, Adam! I will not let my best friend destroy himself and his marriage like this. Yes you had an incident, yes you were badly injured, and yes you were taken away from the GW. But the injuries have healed, you are better, if you started training you could be back in a couple of weeks, but you don't care do you?' 'I do care!' I said showing a bit of emotion for the first time. 'Well you certainly don't show it Adam! I have seen you fight with a broken arm, I have seen you dive 50 feet onto another fighter and get back up. Yet here you sit with no hope left. You always said to me Adam, there is always hope. How can you say that and be like you are now? This is not the Adam Myers I know. This is not the Adam Myers who would let the women he loves cry. I mean for god's sake Adam you are going to be a father in 6 months time and yet you don't care!' I hanged my head, I felt the dark cloud disperse. Peter's words were obviously showing me how much of an idiot I had been. 'Sort yourself out Adam before you lose everything! Call me when you are ready to talk.' With that Peter turned on his heels and walked away. I followed him with my eyes, Rachel just came through the doors as Peter reached him. Her eyes were red raw from crying she was mopping away the tears from her cheeks as Peter but his hands on her shoulder and muttered something to her. She nodded her head and he looked back at me with such disappointment in his eyes before walking through the doors. I nodded to myself, it was time to sort my life out. Rachel walked to the end of the bed and looked at me with one final look of hope. I looked back at her, tears were now running down my cheeks as it dawned on me what I had put my beautiful loving wife through. Rachel couldn't see the tears because of my mask and so still thought I was being unresponsive. 'Come on then, home.' She said as she turned to walk. 'Rachel!' I shouted at her the tears now making my voice wobble. 'What?' She said obviously not hearing the emotion in my voice. 'I do love you and I always will.' I said with all the passion in the world. Rachel smiled and tears began to stream down her face. She sighed with relief as she moved over to me and embraced me tightly and I returned the embrace. Tears were streaming down both our faces as we just stood there holding each other. It was like we had been away from each other for years and had only just been reunited. For the first time life was good again, my dreams had been dark as of late but now I saw an end. Now I was ready to return to the GW.
