? Retrospect For Death ?

22 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.22.04 I once thought I saw a monster. But it was in my head. Not of imagination. But of fear and dread. Sadness is what it spewed. That is what I took. And clinically I went by the book. I once thought I saw a monster. It was gray and green. In my head anything's possible. But nothing is what it seems. But it seems like this monster took hold of me. I once thought I saw a monster. I wished it would go away. Nothing could stop it. It wanted to stay. But all it took was a few pills for my monster to disappear. Now I really wonder one thing - Who won, me or the monster? ?| Retrospect For Death |? Darkness benighted a town with hidden evils. Dark clouds spread over the sky like a warm blanket, tucked in the city of insomnia as apathy was the all to common thought within those who lived there. White walls circumvent a slender room as Itagaki hangs suspended by hooks binding his flesh. Oxygen vacant, his body clinging to the hooks dormant, never wishing to be awaken. Crying blue blood, his soul tries to escape but soon on the other side of the bullet proof glass, Biohazard scientist stick their possessed hands through cleansed gloves and shot more chemicals into Itagaki's tegument. 'How is the patient doing?' Geoff Hoon verbalized his concerns. ?Is he almost ready? I need him ready for brain surgery at precisely 10 o' clock.' 'He is doing fine. His blood pressure has been lowered to the point where is no longer conscious and so far, his body hasn't been rejecting any of the medicine.' Highly concentrated, the Biohazard dressed scientist responded without taking his eyes away from the task at hand. 'Perfect.' And with that, Geoff Hoon scribbled more evil down into his clipboard while walking away. Glasses that hung low with grey hair of wisdom to match his white medical coat, Geoff Hoon was the brightest scientist in his day. But greed filled his heart. Caught trying to test on humans by modifying as he would say, their bodies, he was immediately shunned from the scientific society. As he walked off into the darkness that was his environment, the Biohazard feigns continued to fill Itagaki's soul with the devil. 'You think this one will actually work?' One of the Biohazard disguised scientist questioned the other. 'I'm not sure. If not it won't be the first. We'll just throw him in the psychiatric sanctuary with the rest of the loons.' Spoken as if it happened regularly, the other scientist replied casually. 'True. He's better off dead though. Memories of torture, soul destruction, and no past, I almost feel sorry for him.' Peculiarly, the scientist almost sounded like he had sympathy for the guy until: 'He's a looker, how much for him?' The trucker stereotype, a large man approached one of the scientist with hungry eyes. 'Fifty for five minutes. Any additional time will cost you ten extra per minute. Don't bruise him, Geoff Hoon would kill us if he knew we were doing this.' ---- Violated, misused, and abused, Itagaki Aisukurimu laid demented looking touched as he stayed buckled to black leather straps as if he were adhere to them; never to leave them. Hooked up to gadgets that calculated anything and everything, Itagaki's stupefied body found itself once again surrounded by white walls; tainted serenity. Their plugs and scalpels dissecting his brain and altering his anatomy, several scientist clad in white biohazard suits experimented and modified Itagaki. 'His brain waves are running abnormally doctor.' Valencia, the 'assistant nurse', attending the monitors lets Geoff aware. 'Lower the chemicals being injected and increase the Demerol.' Geoff replied with the solution. 'But doctor, he could go into cardiac arrest if we increase it anymore.' Concering Geoff of the side effects, Valenica countered. 'Then we'll do heart surgery and replace it. The heart doesn't concern me any. As long as the brain is functioning then that's all that matters.' Put through brain surgery and physical exams, Itagaki soon found himself thrown into white pillowed walls that made his cell in an asylum. Screams of abused souls haunted the asylum as they echoed down the halls trying to escape this place. Flickering lights, blood stained walls and floors; this was the ideal place for evil. The roaring thunder rumbled the heavens and shook the walls as lightening soared through the clouds like stomach cramps. Then the rain began to pour, pounding down like quickened drum beats. And there, crouched in a fetus position in the corner, stray-jacket holding him together, his hair screening his pale face, and blue sub-zero like mask covering the lower portion of his face, Itagaki laid there silently. His pain and misery lost, his identity forgotten. ---- And just like that, the sun peaked the next morning as if God was apologizing for the nefarious night. Itagaki woke to an eerie silence throughout the asylum. No screaming, no beating, no flesh ripping and blood splattering. It was abnormal for this place. Itagaki sat up as his white stringy hair flowed with his movements before steady footsteps approached his cell door. Keys rattling soon unlock the metal materialized door as SWAT team looking men bombard the room and grab Itagaki. Swiftly taking him out the room, another shot is injected into his neck resulting in him passing out as everything goes blank. -Later That Day: Arizona Dessert, 6:32pm- Itagaki's eyes slightly split open as his blurry vision struggled to focus. The intense heat reigned down over the dirt and cacti as lungs inhaled dry air. His mind slipping in and out, Itagaki soon realized he couldn't move his hands nor feet as they were tied once again. Held Jesus style bound to a cross, a group of SWAT team looking men stood in front of him as Geoff Hoon soon interjected himself through them and stood before Itagaki. Drowning in heat waves, Geoff Hoon took off his sun glasses and stared Itagaki in the eyes. 'Itagaki Freeze, welcome.' Complexity overwhelmed Itagaki's mind as of who Itagaki Freeze was. 'Oh, that's right, you probably don't know who that name belongs to. Well let me introduce you to your future Mr. Freeze.' Geoff paused to let Itagaki process. 'Before this, your life was filled with meaningless memories and no purpose. Now, I've given you life, a reason, a purpose for being. But ultimately it all depends on whether you accept or decline. Accept and you will live forever by my side. Decline and you will have proved you have always been dead.' Hearing these words, Itagaki understood them, believing them to be true. Like a child who has not yet lived by experience, Itagaki fell victim. 'I leave you now Itagaki. Whatever path you shall choose I will be back to collect my property.' Referring to Itagaki, Geoff continued, 'Hopefully, you do the logical thing.' And with that, Geoff turned and headed back to his awaiting parade of black cars. Climbing in, he soon took off along with the others down the cracked dirt crust of beaten Earth disappearing in the heat waves. As Itagaki watched with blurry eyes and a dying mentality, he nodded his head before falling into the darkness that mastered his mind.
