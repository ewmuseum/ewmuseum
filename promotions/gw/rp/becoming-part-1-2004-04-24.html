Becoming (part 1)

24 April 2004

Becoming (part 1) Derek Draven Global Wrestling Promo #1212 Date: 4/24/04 Location: Derek Dravens apartment The scene opens in a nice apartment looking structure.As we look at the room from the side we see nice red plush carpeting,leading into the living room where a big screen television and a black leather couch and lovseat compliment the room.As the camera pans to the left away from the living room,we see the kitchen.It's done in black and white marble with a fifties style bar and metal thick padded bar stools.Behind this bar is the refridgerator and stove.Further back into this area is a big window overlooking the streets below,the sounds of car horns and yelling heard vageuly. 'Derek all you have done for the past two days is sit in that chair and play guitar!! A womans voice is heard yelling from across the kitchen area.The camera moves closer to this voice and looks inside the black door.On the walls are psoters of Derek Draven and Jim Morrison and Jimi Hendrix and aliens and such.A Real psychedelic paradise.The room is dimly lighted by two orang lava lamps on both sides of the queen sized bed in the middle of the room. Derek Draven,sitting on a inflatable orange chair with a natural finish acoustic guitar and only his boxer shorts and white wifebeater on. Derek:'Chill out Jennifer!' The woman,now we know as Jennifer stares at Derek with a concerned look in her eyes.The look of love and sorrow.Derek fingers a chord and strums his guitar slowly making a mellow sound. Jennifer:'Ever since that woman called you from Mavericks place or whatever you have been upset!! Over what??!!! Derek so what if they kicked you out of The Upper Echelon,I mean you're still a tag team champion!!' Derek doesnt even bother looking up from his guitar. Jennifer:'Fine....pretend i'm not here Derek! Thats what you always do!!' Derek looks up slowly Derek:'Eh...you don't understand! Wouldn't understand!!' Jennifer:'And why's that??!! Jennifer looks at Derek impatiently. Derek:'It was my chance babe...' Jennifer:'Chance for what??' Derek:'To ditch the Nevermore gimmick! I just wanna be myself in front of the GW audience!' Jennifer sighs and puts her hands on her hips. Jennifer:'Well Derek the grass is always greener on the side until you get there!!' Derek looks up,exhausted. Jennifer:'Salaok made you..personal issues broke you! Now Maverick boots you out of TUE and you're going around all depressed..' Derek:'Yeah...you don't get it.' Jennifer looks like she is getting a bit frustrated. Jennifer:'Fine,Derek please explain to me what I don't get!! I want to know!! I can't stand to see you like this!' Derek puts the guitar on the ground and reaches for the bed and grabs his Global Wrestling Tag Team Title.He stares into the reflection before falling back into the chair and drapping it across his chest. Derek:'How much do you think this belt means to me?' Jennifer:'Alot?' Derek shakes his head Derek:'Nope,try again.' Jennifer:'Nothing...' Derek lighting up with excitement Derek:'Jackpot!!!' Derek stands up and puts the belt on the bed and sits back into the char carefully. Derek:'It means nothing cause I have to fight Maverick for complete possesion.Whats the point?' Jennifer:'Well...' Derek:'Well nothing,once a team alwyas a team I always say.' Derek picks his guitar back up and begins playing some soft chords. Derek:'But shit,If Maverick thinks he wants the belts i'll go with him for them.I want them in a small way...Just I have the displeasure of finding a new partner.' Jennifer:'Well...there's some guys out there just waiting to be picked up to the next level Derek.Just like you were!' Derek:'Yeah,but the next level brings on the next level of unhappiness and personal trauma with it.' Derek shakes his head slowly. Derek:'I know that one all to well,you know what I mean?' Jennifer sighs slowly Jennifer:'Just do it Derek! Get off your ass,get dressed,goto the gym and train and then come back home.' Derek:'I dont feel like going outside today.' Jennifer:'See how do you expect to beat Maverick if you can't even get off your lazy sorry ass and goto the gym?' Derek looks around the room.His eyes focus on the Global Wrestling posters of himself.One with his highest honor around his waist the International title.Jennifer notices his fixation on the poster. Jennifer:'Yeah,that used to be you.Once upon a time.When you actually cared about winning and losing.And being a chmapion mattered to you! Where is that Derek at now? Huh? Tell me damnit!!' Derek just looks around the room staring at the different posters.Jennifer gets upset and finally leaves the room.Derek still staring blankly at the poster of him. As we leave the scene Derek is putting his guitar down and stands up next to the bed. Derek:'Maybe I do care after all...' Derek begins to put on a pair of loose blue jeans and a red t-shirt.His eyes get a glimpse of the Global Wrestling Tag Title again and he smiles slightly. Derek:'Yes,Draven you are good!! Very Good!!' Derek walks into the kitchen,Jennifer is making toast on the counter.Derek grabs a slice of the buttered toast and begins to eat it quickly. Derek:'Honey,you did it!' Jennifer raises a eyebrow at Derek's words. Jennifer:'What the hell did I do now?' Derek shakes his head smiling. Derek:'Nothing,Except wake me up! I know what I have to do! I have to keep this titles and start fresh.If it means something as drastic as shaking Bricks hand in the middle of that ring I will do it! No more evil,no more sadisim!' Jennifer looks on curiously. Derek:'Jennifer this is it! I've been waiting for this moment to happen for a very long time now.I can finally be myself in Global Wrestling..and theres nothing better than that!' Jennifer begins to have tears sweel up in her eyes.The man she married has finally found himself.Found a reason to be the best there is in Global Wrestling.Jennifer throws herself into a embrace with Derek. Jennifer:'Thank you Derek! Just please,stay like this.Let nothing stop you..be what you can!' Derek slightly chuckles Derek:'Jennifer you sound like my mother now!' Jennifer cracks a small smile. Derek:'I have found a new reason to live Jennifer! I feel so invigorated. Derek reaches into the fridge and grabs a diet mountain dew of the top rack and closes the door.He turns and sees Jennifer smiling at him.He looks on confused Derek:'What?' Jennifer:'Why don't you go get ready...' Derek:'I dont know..I wanna relax. Derek heads toward the living room and sits on the black leather sofa. Jennifer:'No,you can't!' Derek:'Yes I can!' Jennifer:'Derek,be honest..are you feeling up to it? Derek:'Hell yeah! They wanna kick me out of TUE,Well I'll keep these damn Tag Titles and prove I don't need them! I didn't need the Nevermore! I damn Sure do no need the TUE!' Jennifer:'Well goto the gym or something! Kick your ass into overdrive!' Derek reaches for the remote and pauses. Derek:'Jennifer go grab my title belt please...' Jennifer steps backward into the kitchen slowly and heads toward the bedroom.When she comes back out she is holding the GW Tag Team Title belt. Jennifer:'Damn this thing is heavy!' Derek:'Yeah...' Derek grabs the belt from her and puts it on his tv,facing the furniture.He sits back down in the sofa and looks at the belt,mentally preparing for the challenges ahead.
