The hunt Begins#1

4 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/04/03 Silence. I sit in a dark corner of the Dark Horse Saloon watching this “Steel Dragon” make a mockery of him self on a mechanical bull. I sniff the air smelling his mixed blood course through his veins, pumping ever so hard and ever so fast as he tries to maintain his cool while holding on for dear life. The need to prove himself?..not an admirable trait to have unless its going to get him far in life. I sit there as time passes on with him on that bull. After a little over three minutes Rokku went flying off of “Blackie” as a man walks over and begins to talk to him. “Is this it?”” I say to myself. ?”..Is this going to be as hard as it gets? Does he realized he’s being stalked?” I shake my head at the thought of the Steel Lion. The man who is able to do anything, defeat all foes, be one of the mightiest men ever, and yet can’t even run when he is being hunted. To me all he ever will be is prey. A blood packet. A large amount of mass taking up space in my world. The two men continue to talk. I sit there getting bored at this not even trying. I see a busty blonde walk by me and with great speed I grab her and put my hand over her mouth. She screams but the sound is muffled by my hand. “You want to live right?” She nods as a single tear. I smile that vicious smile I do so well and lick the side of her face. She shudders at the touch. I smirk. “Then deliver this message to that man over there.” I whisper into her ear and let her go. I smirk at the thought of making her a meal but realizing she’s not my type. The blonde woman walked up, leaned on Rokku's shoulder, and whispered something in his ear. The Steel Lion's eyes widened, and he quickly said goodbye to Ely -- then he let the blonde lead him out the front door. I roar into laughter as I walk out of the shadows that hid me like a nice black blanket in the night. I look to Braxton who glares at me, I just smile and nod to him as I walk out the door. The night wind blowing softly over me. I sniff the air tracking down the smell of mixed blood of Rokku and head east. “Rokku! Do you realize what you are to me” You are nothing! Mere food for creatures like me! A very rare one you are. You call your self the “Steel Lion”. A lion has courage. A lion has pride. A lion has honor. You cower away in the depths of the night like a mouse being stalked by a cat. Alas Rokku, my dear Steel Lion but you have a ways to go before you can play this game with the likes of me!” I walk slowly thinking to myself. It was I who threw his mighty ancestors to the dogs. It was I who people ran in fear from. It is I who plagues mankind to these very nights. I have hunted the best the world has had to offer and now?.I face him?.. “Rokku! You will give me a challenge if I have to beat it out of you! You made the challenge”..I accepted now you run?..but I have things in store for you’oh yes.. I smile at the thought as I walk down the street I become one with the shadows and all things become silent.
