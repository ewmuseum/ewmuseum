Mother's Day

8 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/8/04 The place: Nassau Coliseum The date: May 8, 2004. GW is having a house show in the arena. 'Party Up' hits the PA and out comes Brick to the fans? applause. He makes his way down to the ring and climbs in Brick: Y’all probably noticed I’m a bit excited right now. See, tomorrow is Mother's Day. The crowd lets out a huge cheer. Brick: There's a joke that goes around backstage. They say in the back that any woman who raised me had to have been the toughest woman walking the Earth. I even said so, myself at Wrestlecade. Right now, I wanna acknowledge the woman who helped raise me, who helped shape me into the man I am today, my mother, Coretta Randall! 'Party Up' hits the PA and out comes Coretta Randall. She receives a huge standing ovation from the crowd as she makes her way down the ramp. She waves to the fans and slaps five with a few of them. She climbs up the steps onto the apron. Brick parts the ropes for his mother to climb into the ring. She walks into the center of the ring and waves to the fans. Brick: Ma, you've been one of the most important people in my life. You believed in me when no one else did. You pushed me whenever I wanted to give up. You put up with me a lot more than anybody else probably should. And right here and right now, I wanted to say thank you. The fans give a huge pop. Brick: Now, last year, I bought you a house, and I was thinking of a way to top that. So, I decided that I wanted to share you with all these people. Coretta takes the mic. Coretta: Jamal, you didn’t have to do all this, but I appreciate you doing it. I also want to take this time to thank all of you for supporting my son over the years. The fans applaud. Brick: Ma, I wanted to do something special, so, guys, hit it. Suddenly the music for “I’ll Always Love my Mama” by the Intruders hits the PA. Brick(sings): I'll always love my mama She's my favorite girl I'll always love my mama She brought me in this world Coretta then takes the microphone from Brick. Coretta: Please cut the music. Now, Jamal, a lot of times through your life, I have supported you through many things. But, know I say this with all the love in my heart. Baby, you can’t sing. The fans let out some laughter. Brick looks rather sheepish as he takes the mic back. Brick: Yeah, Ma, you're right. Fortunately, I know cats who can. Brick then turns to the entrance. Coretta turns afterwards. DMAK walks out onto the stage as the music for 'A Song for Mama' starts. Keith: You taught me everything And everything you've given me I always keep it inside You're the driving force in my life, yeah There isn't anything Or anyone that I can be And it just wouldn't feel right If I didn't have you by my side Damian: You were there for me to love and care for me When skies were grey Whenever I was down You were always there to comfort me And no one else can be what you have been to me You will always be You will always be the girl In my life for all times DMAK: Mama, mama, you know I love you Oh you know I love you Mama, mama, you're the queen of my heart Your love is like tears from the stars Mama, I just want you to know Lovin' you is like food to my soul Damian: Yes, it is (5x) Keith: You're always down for me Have always been around for me even when I was bad You showed me right from my wrong DMAK: Yes, you did Keith: And you took up for me When everyone was downin' me You always did understand You gave me strength to go on Andre: There was so many times Looking back when I was so afraid And then you come to me And say to me I can face anything And no one else can do What you have done for me You'll always be You will always be the girl in my life, whoa DMAK: Mama, mama, you know I love you (Andre: You know I love you, you know I love you) Oh you know I love you Mama, mama, you're the queen of my heart Your love is like tears from the stars (Andre: Your love is like tears from the stars) Mama, I just want you to know Lovin' you is like food to my soul Damian: Never gonna go a day without you Fills me up just thinking of you I'll never go a day without my DMAK: Mama, mama you know I love you Oh, you know I love you Mama, mama, you're the queen of my heart Your love is like tears from the stars Mama, I just want you to know Lovin' you is like food to my soul Damian: Lovin' you is like food to my soul A tearful Coretta turns to her son and embraces him in the middle of the ring. Brick then hugs her back to a huge ovation. Damian: You are the food to my soul.
