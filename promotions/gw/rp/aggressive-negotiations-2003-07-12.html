Aggressive Negotiations

12 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/12 The screech of low profile tires gripping sun scarred pavement can be heard as a jet black Porsche Carerra GT Turbo tail slides around a corner before roaring onto a highway sparsely littered with traffic. The sun beats down on the car's deep black paint job causing it's rays to reflect off the metallic flakes throughout the body giving it a shimmering wet look. With Holmes at the wheel the car is catapulted forward as he slams the gearshift down into second, riding on the broken line between two cars just centimeters away from rubbing door handles. After passing the two vehicles Holmes then spins the wheel, jerking the car to the left and crossing two lanes inside of a small space between the two cars he had just passed and an envoy of semi trucks ahead of him. John Zero sits in the passenger seat with a small silver cellular phone pressed to his ear as though all these things are commonplace. He soon removes it from his ear as Holmes instructs him to press play on the CD player while he throws the car into third gear. 'Excuse Me Again' by Jay-Z booms from the car's top of the line stereo system and a screen folds out from the CD player console displaying various random graphic designs that seem to pulsate with the music. 'So where are we headed.' asks Holmes while bobbing his head to the music. 'Raven's Crossing, just over the hill in Toluca Lake. We're to go pay a visit to a Mr. Johnnie Vincent.' replies John. Holmes' jaw drops slightly and he cocks an eye at John. 'Toluca Lake!” Isn't that like thirty minutes away!?' 'Yes' is the answer that slips through John's lips and finds it's way through the marsh of Jay-Z lyrics on top of Neptune beats, the roar of the car's engine, and the wind whipping all around the two men, to Holmes' ear. 'Shit.' is the only response Holmes can muster in a tone of obvious dismay. Turning to Holmes slightly John offers 'If my answers frighten you perhaps you should cease asking scary questions.' causing Holmes to roll his eyes. He continues 'Besides, you wanted to drive... 'Chuckling slightly Holmes informs him that they'll be there ten as he forces the car's pedal to the floor holding it firmly under his foot still weaving erratically in and out of traffic doing well over twice the speed limit. 'That's what I like to hear.' adds Zero. 'And don't think I don't see you bobbin yo' head over there Z!' exclaims Holmes as he finds an open patch of road and explodes on toward he and Zero's destination closing in on two hundred miles per hour. Nine minutes and thirty-seven seconds later the speeding car rolls to a stop in front of a rather ritzy looking hotel. A large gilded crest on the front of the building reads 'Raven's Crossing'. Upon Holmes' request John Zero opens up the glove compartment only to be met with an empty space. Under further instruction he reaches up and pulls down a panel in the roof of the glove box by a small silver ring. Down drops a custom holster that holds two shiny chrome fifty caliber desert eagles with roaring lion designs on the grips. A small dagger also sits in between the two hand cannons. “Yeah, those my bad muthafuckas.” claims Holmes as he motions for Zero to pass them over, the two men then hop out of the car. After latching the holster onto the back of his belt Holmes walks around the car and joins Zero on the sidewalk. He then opens the passenger side door and pulls the back of the seat forward exposing a hidden compartment from which he retrieves a white bullet proof vest which he holds up towards John. Zero replies by knocking on his chest to show that he came prepared and is already wearing one. Holmes removes his tanktop and puts on the vest under it before starting toward the door along with Zero. A revolving glass door with gold trimmings swings around twice allowing Zero and Holmes to enter the hotel’s swanky lobby complete w/ marble floors and a spinning chandelier. Both men nod toward the front desk attendant as they step inside the lobby elevator. As the doors close it finally strikes Deacon the has no clue why he and Zero are there. “So what’d this Vincent clown do to have Capo send us over here like this?” inquires Holmes. “He’s got a certain briefcase of Capo’s that he’d like returned to him post haste.” states John matter of factly while cocking his gun. “By any means necessary.” Still not satisfied, Holmes asks “So what’s in the suitcase?” “No one knows, and Capo made it very clear that neither of us was to open it.” replies John shrugging his shoulders. “So we’re just supposed to bust in this place for a suit case full god knows what!” And then on top of that we can’t even open it!” This shit is boof man, all I know is whatever’s in that suitcase better be real important.” shoots Holmes who’s curiosity is obviously eating him up now. “Let me put it this way, if Mr. Vincent were to take a trip to Singapore one of us would hiding in a bowl of rice right now waiting to put a gun to his head.” explains Zero. “Damn.” answers Deacon in amazement. The elevator’s doors slide open smoothly after reaching the thirteenth floor. Both men put on their game faces and step quietly out of the elevator and into the hallway only to see none other than Sean Price making his way down the hall with the door to the stairs still swinging behind him. He joins Holmes and Zero in front of the elevator and says “Didn’t think I’d let you two hog all the action now did ya?” as the three men make their way down the hall stopping at a door marked “1304'. Deacon presses an ear against the door before turning back to Sean and John to ask “Sounds like they’re watching TV. Anybody got a credit card?” Price hands him a credit card and Holmes sticks it inside the small crack between the door and the wall then slides it down to dislodge one of the locks. He then removes a paperclip from his back pocket and remolds it into another shape. Holmes places his left hand on the exposed end of the credit card while working the paperclip inside the door’s keyhole. After a moment a clicking noise is heard and a grin comes across Deacon’s face. “Still got it.” Holmes says obviously pleased with his work. “Ya’ll ready?” “Hell yeah!” exclaims Sean. “God hates a coward remember?” replies Zero. “Alright then, let’s make it funky.” adds Holmes while pull one of the guns out from behind him with his right hand, left still on the credit card. “I’ll do the honors.” announces Sean. All three men simultaneously draw a deep breath only to exhale it abruptly as Sean kicks the door open while clutching a 357. The door swings open and hits the wall to reveal two men in the front room of the suite, one standing not far from the door, the other on the couch, both adorned in cheap suits and gaudy jewelry, they were most likely Vincent’s henchmen. Upon the entrance of Zero, Price, and Holmes the man standing has no time to react before being pistol whipped by Sean Price causing him to fall into an Italian leather Barco lounger. The man on the couch makes a move for a gun but is stopped by a bullet from Deacon Holmes that hits him right in the ankle causing him to fall to the floor clutching his leg next to the couch. “Everybody be cool!” Holmes draws the second of his two guns and trains them both on the man on the couch, staring at him intensely. “Now look, I’m gonna count to three, and if you don’t wanna get hurt, when I count to three you’re gonna slide your gun over here and sit your ass down on the couch. But when you do it, you do it cool. Ready” One... Two...” The man slides the gun across the floor to Holmes? foot but then gets up a little too slow for Deacon’s taste. “Hurry up now, I haven’t got all day” “YOU SHOT ME!!!” screams the man who is obviously in excruciating pain. “Now I thought you were gonna be cool” Now when you yell at me, it makes me nervous. And when I get nervous, I get scared. And when muthafuckas get scared that’s when muthafuckas accidentally get shot!” explains Holmes, both guns pointed dead at the man. John Zero steps in front of the recliner the other man lays slumped in, a slow trickle of blood streaming down the side of his face as a result of Sean’s attack. “Where is Don Vincenzo?” questions Zero calmly yet firm. “I... I don’t know...” stammers the man, shaking with fear at hazing a gun pointed right at his head. Zero quickly moves the gun down and to the right, pulling the trigger when the barrel lands on the man’s shoulder causing him to scream in agony. “Don’t toy with me, now where is he?” questions Zero, this time in a more forceful tone. Once again the man stammers “But I... I really don’t know! Just please don’t hurt me anymore.” “Did he leave here with a briefcase?” Inquires Zero while cocking his gun once more. “Nuh, nuh, no... Ih, ih, it’s in the kitchen somewhere.” stutters the man, now on the verge of tears while clutching his wound. Zero motions to Sean with his free hand for him to go inspect the kitchen. The first place he looks is the refrigerator. Although he finds no suit case he finds something else that is right up his alley, ice cold coronas. Price grabs one out of the fridge and continues his search, emptying whole drawers onto the floor. Finally he pulls one out and in it sits a silver steel briefcase with two combination pads on it. “Found it.” he announces to his comrades. “Good, then let’s get the hell outta here!” exclaims Holmes, who is starting toward the door. Just then a third henchman runs out from the hallway with an Armalite AR-180 carbine gas-operated semi-automatic screaming at the top of his lungs while wildly spraying rounds into the living room area. John Zero flips the recliner over backwards causing it the man sitting in it to spill onto the floor. Sean Price stands no more than ten feet behind the man behind the kitchen counter finishing up his Corona with the briefcase sitting in front of him as one of the bullets nails Deacon Holmes in the chest, causing him to fall to the floor, seemingly dead. “This is gonna be good.” Sean whispers to himself as the man empties his last few rounds into the wall and one of his cohorts due to his wild aim and inability to control the hot weapon. He then drops the gun beside him while breathing heavily. Sean, who has the best seat in the house is now shaking head slowly from side to side as this henchman has no idea what he’s just gotten himself into. The man’s panting abruptly stops when he sees Deacon Holmes rise from what he thought was death brandishing two Desert Eagles and John Zero emerge from behind the overturned Barco lounger, raising the total number of guns pointed at him to three. Whistling comes from the kitchen area behind him and he slowly turns around with his hands raised in the air to find Sean Price standing with the briefcase in his hand and a pistol in the other, he begins to shudder at the thought of what’s to happen next. Suddenly, both Holmes and Zero empty one round each into the back of the man’s knees, dropping him to the floor in front of Price who steps from behind the counter. Sean walks right in from of the man and looks down at him sneering arrogantly before pistol whipping him, slamming the but of the gun into his left temple rendering him unconscious. “Pfft, what a joke.” says Sean as the three men make their way out the door, closing it tightly behind them. Sean, John, and Deacon all walk out of the building nonchalantly as though nothing had happened, the man at the front desk nods at them on their way out. After passing through the revolving glass door Price tosses Holmes the briefcase, who then pulls down the seats in his car and secures it in the compartment behind them. Sean then disappears inside of a black hummer with dark tinted windows and rides off leaving Deacon and Zero still in front of the building sitting in Holmes? Porsche. “Put on your rubber boots, cause we’re about to step in some serious shit.” says Zero. Holmes turns at looks at him confused. “What do you mean?” “We just took out two of Johnnie Vincent’s men, and left one to tell the tale, something I’m sure he’s less than likely to appreciate.” answers John, not bothered at all by the thought of having Vincent’s wrath cast upon him. “He can only tell the tale if he can still speak when Vincent comes back.” shoots Holmes, making Zero look at him out of the corner of his eye. “I left them a little present.” continues Deacon as he pulls out a small black remote from under his steering wheel. He then starts the car and whips a U turn out of his parking space smoking tires the whole way with “Speed” by Eightball and MJG pumping from the stereo system. The Porsche then peels off down the road, with a suite on the thirteenth floor exploding in the background...
