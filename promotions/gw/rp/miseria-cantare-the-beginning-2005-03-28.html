Miseria Cantare (The Beginning)

28 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/28/05 “People ask me all the time these days... “what’s it like being champion?”” The voice rang through the silence like a beacon in the fog. He pressed his hand against the cold window while he watched the drops flow down. The streets below turned into blackened rivers under the street lamps. He watched as a woman marched down the street below, pulling her hair out of her eyes so she could see through the small hurricane like conditions. The beautiful downpour outside almost drowned out the muffled screams of a man repressed. Cold and emotionless, Christian Zenith turned his back on the window and walked across the darkness like a tiger stalking it’s prey. He could feel the heartbeat of his prey, pounding from the night like a marching band in parade. The GW World Champion walked towards the corner, where heartbeat began to climb. Zenith looked down at his prey, whose eyes shot up at him as if a plea for mercy. His mouth bound by a white cloth, and shackled to a wooden chair by steel chains and padlocks. The muffled screams were of no use... they didn’t make it much further past Christian’s ears. “This championship does nothing more than save me breath in the long run. I no longer have to listen to the ignorant ramblings of those with lesser intelligence than myself, trying to analyze my weaknesses and why Ken Partridge Jr. and Salaok Davis are better than me. Salaok cannot be beaten I was told... before I pinned his shoulders down for a three count in a Glass Dungeon match. “Superman” Ken Partridge Jr..... The Franchise... the World Champion and hero of millions. That was... until I came along and took his world championship and his pride.” Christian reached down and violently ripped the cloth away from his mouth and the captive let out a scream in pain. Zenith threw the cloth down onto the prisoner’s lap, with small blotches of blood from where Zenith had forced the cloth to rip of the edges of his lips. The man licked the side of his lip with disgust, tasting his own blood. “Now... they want another shot at me, another chance for redemption and put their petty threats into fruition. I say come and try... because you know what’s pitiful” I’m not even trying yet.” The champion smiled as he looked at himself in the mirror, the GW World Heavyweight Championship hanging over his right shoulder in the darkness. He locked eyes with his own cadet blue eyes... “But this time is different... this time you’re facing an immortal. Doesn’t that scare you?” The man rebuttled as he violently pushed and pulled, trying to break free of his steel chain confines as he looked up at The Conscious Crisis. “Am I scared of vampires?” Christian Zenith chuckled a little bit at the remark as he threw on his long black suit jacket, “No... but they damn sure better be scared of me. Little do they know... death is the only sanctuary from my wrath.” ---- Love your hate, your faith lost You are now one of us Love your hate, your faith lost You are now one of us ---- WHAM! The slamming of the car door resonated through the drenched streets, bouncing from raindrop to raindrop through the cold shower. The raindrops bounced off the 2005 Chrysler Crossfire, flowing down it’s shiny black finish under the glow of the street lamp above. The raindrops reached their destination, in a puddle on the asphalt. A splash flew high into the air from the puddle as a black boot slammed into the ground before pulling out and walking away, as if nothing ever happened. The boots continued their course across the black asphalt river, created by the steady rain over the past couple of days. With a sudden halt, the boots came to a stop... and a pair of blue-green eyes surveyed the surrounding area. A puff a air released from her pink lips as he looked atop the building in front of her. Her usually stylish blonde hair laid against her back, soaked and dripping from mother nature’s show. Her hands closed her black Adidas jacket tighter as she made her way towards the building, trying to stay warm in the chilly downpour. The sound of a shriek startled her as she wrapped her hand around the door. Her blue-green eyes shot over to her left, looking for whatever could’ve caused such a horrific noise. The only thing to her left was what appeared to be an abandoned tennis court. Her hand slowly eased off the door as she backed away, getting a better glimpse of the tennis court through the sheet of water that continued to fall effortlessly from the heavens above. Her beautiful eyes peered through the rain, as her curiosity began to lead her towards the tennis court. Her boots clicked on the sidewalk in a series of splashes, as she crept towards the empty concrete tennis court. Her eyes narrowed through the storm while she looked hard into the rain. Nothing. She put her hand on the steel mesh fence surrounded the court, and opened the door leading into the inside. She closed the door behind her with a clang, looking across the net into the abyss. Suddenly another shriek startled her, making her spin around in reaction. Water flew from her rain-soaked hair as she spun around and saw nothing... “Games... my mind is playing tricks on me.” She said aloud in the confines of the tennis court, plunged into darkness. “It is a game... but I’m not just in your mind.” She heard clear as day over the downpour when suddenly all the lights surrounding the tennis court burst on, lighting it up as if it were game time. She looks around in confusion underneath the bright spotlights. “What’s a matter Vivica, you don’t believe in vampires?” A woman said from directly behind the confused Vivica J. Valentine. Vivica spun around just in time to get a slap across the face from a woman with long black hair. She grinned as Vivica held her face and stumbled backwards. Vivica looked back at the woman with disgust... “What in the hell do you want from me?” ---- Nothing from nowhere, I'm no one at all Radiate, recognize one silent call As we all form one dark flame... Incinerate ---- The woman looked back at Vivica J. Valentine with a smile before she began to speak... “I don’t want much... you see at the pay per view coming up, Christian Zenith has a match against a very close friend of mine. I’ve seen you in action dear Vivica, I know what you’re capable of... and I’m going to make damn sure you’re nowhere near the ring. I can’t afford to have Corvus be at a disadvantage because of a mortal like you.” She explained while she pulled a dagger out of her black pants... “Now I know why they call you Shadow,” Vivica J. Valentine responded with a sarcastic grin, “You like to hide and blindside people because you’re too much of a coward to take them on face to face. Why don’t you try me now... now that I can see your ass-kissing face.” “Cute...” Shadow responded before she hurled the dagger at Vivica, who did a cartwheel out of the way of the blade. Shadow watched the dagger fly through the mesh fence and stick into the wall of the building across the street. Vivica lunged at Shadow with a spinning heel kick, but Shadow quickly ducked under and countered with a sweep, which Vivica backflipped away from. Shadow made it up to her feet where Vivica did a series of karate straight punches and boxing hooks and jabs, which Shadow dodged with ease before burying her foot into the abdomen of Valentine. Vivica bent over in pain where slammed her forearm down on the back of Vivica’s head, sending her face first towards the concrete. Vivica got up to her hands and knees but Shadow quickly dug the back of her heel into Vivica’s back... “There is nothing you can possibly do to stop me Valentine. You should just admit defeat...” Shadow declared as she pulled another throwing dagger out of her pocket and looked at it with a smile before aiming it at the back of Vivica’s head. “Watch me.” Vivica said as she spun and knocked the leg out from under Shadow, sending her down onto the wet concrete ground. The dagger flew out of Shadow’s hand, straight up and into her leg. Shadow cringed as Vivica somersaulted backwards up to her feet, “You got a little something in your leg there.” Shadow snarled as she plunked the dagger out of her leg and hurled it towards Vivica’s head, watching as once again Vivica dodged the dagger with ease. Shadow sprung up to her fee and threw a punch that Valentine swatted away. Vivica countered with a kick, which Shadow caught before flipping Vivica backwards, who then landed on her feet. Valentine threw a punch which Shadow caught and then jerked Vivica forward before using Valentine’s own arm to wrap around her neck cutting off her air supply. “You know what girl... you’re not bad. It’s a shame that you’re in my way.” Shadow said as she attempted to strangle Vivica to unconsciousness. “Don’t tell me something I already know.” Vivica choked out before she hurled Shadow over her head in a snapmare like move, sending her crashing into the concrete. Valentine ran towards the mesh fence and jumped onto it before flipping backwards into a moonsault. Shadow somersaults out of the way, making Vivica J. Valentine land on her hands and knees. Suddenly Shadow stops and looks into the darkness. “Stop it!” A man’s voice rung through the darkness... ---- Nothing from nowhere, I'm no one at all Radiate, recognize one silent call As we all form one dark flame As we all form one dark flame As we all... ---- Click... Click... Click... Shadow and Vivica J. Valentine both stopped to see who was making their way towards them, as the approaching footsteps grew louder and louder. Shadow looked in shock as the face of a man she knew looked back at her, his face pale with fear and hands bound together behind his back in steel chain shackles. He quivered as a chrome desert eagle was shoved flush against his temple. His eyes pleaded with the black haired beauty his body trembling with the thought of death approaching. “Please.. stop it.” He said once again to Shadow, who looked back with disgust at the man holding him hostage. “Let him go Zenith!” Shadow demanded as Christian Zenith looked back at her with cold cadet blue eyes, piercing at her from the outside of the mesh cage. His long black suit jacket flapped in the storm, his face cold and merciless as he locked eyes with Shadow. Vivica got up off the ground and walked towards Christian with a smile, before Shadow suddenly grabbed her by the back of her shirt and stuck another dagger against her neck. “Now let him go...” Shadow demanded, holding Vivica J. Valentine hostage at knifepoint. Vivica’s looked up at the night sky, raindrops flowing down her face as she cold feel the cold wet blade just inches away form her neck. “Shadow... I believe you’ve met Rune before.” Zenith said with unstaggering confidence, “I see you’ve also met Vivica J. Valentine. I’m not the least bit worried on what you’re going to do to her... Vivica can handle herself. I’m not here to kill Rune... I’m here to talk.” “What in hell do you want?” Shadow snarled in response to Christian. “Why Corvus?” Christian asked of Shadow, who stood strong dripping Vivica tighter to her body, “Corvus left WMW, and look at everything you accomplished in his absence. PTC Extreme Champion... that’s nothing to scoff at you know. You’re an accomplished fighter... you stood toe to toe with Vivica there, I’m sure you learned she’s no slouch.” Christian kicked the back of Rune’s knee knocking him down to one knee on the wet asphalt. Zenith put the gun up against the back of his head with cruel intentions before continuing on... “Now... I think you’re fighting on the wrong side. We... we are hunters Shadow. We travel the lands in search of competition, we thrive on victory and power and glory. Corvus... well... Corvus is a scavenger. The likes of Corvus go around and feed on the weak. Corvus is a big fish in a small pond in the loser ranks of Global... but myself, I am the best in the business... the World Champion.” Zenith proclaimed as he went on, “I am also a billionaire. Everything in this skyscraper behind me belongs to me and me alone. My weapons company manufactures much more than guns and knives Shadow. We have a team of very skilled doctors that I’m sure can give you something you’ve always wanted.” “...what’s that?” Shadow responded with curiosity. “An injection that can counteract the effects of sunlight on your body. You’ll be able to march through the day like a mortal, and yet still be immortal. You will go where no other of your kind have gone before. Shadow... you will be the greatest of all time. Your kind will worship your legacy, you will be a GOD AMONG IMMORTALS! This can all be yours Shadow... all you have to do is join me... and that talented young lady you have a knife to.” Shadow contemplates the meaning of Zenith’s words as she looks into the terrified eyes of the pilot Rune... who looks up at Shadow pleading for his life. Suddenly Christian Zenith shoves Rune to the ground, banging his head hard against the moistened asphalt. Blood begins to trickle out of his forehead, but is quickly washed away by the pouring rain. Shadow stares at Christian Zenith, who looks back at her as he puts his gun back into his jacket. Shadow pulls the knife away from the neck of Vivica J. Valentine and Vivica drops down onto the concrete. Shadow runs towards the fence and leaps onto it beginning to climb over as Zenith opens the door and walks through. Vivica J. Valentine gets up on her feet and brushes herself off as Christian makes his way across the tennis court. Shadow leaps over the high fence and lands next to Rune on the ground on the outside. Shadow picks Rune up to his feet before she stares back as Christian Zenith in the rain. Shadow’s eyes glared back the GW World Champion, who stood next to Vivica J. Valentine... powerful and proud underneath the lights, before she takes Rune and walks away. ---- Love your hate, your faith lost You are now one of us Love your hate, your faith lost You are now one, one of us ---- Vivica J. Valentine peers into the darkness as Shadow and Rune disappear. Valentine looks over at Christian Zenith who adjusts his jacket in the middle of the downpour. “How did you manage to pull that off?” Vivica asked confused. “A magician never tells his secrets.” Zenith responds as he continues to look into the darkness, now nothing but a sheet of rain, steel, and concrete. “You’re just going to let her walk away?” Vivica questioned of Christian who then looked back at her with confidence. “Don’t worry... she’ll be one of us soon enough.” Musical Credit: “Miseria Cantare (The Beginning)? by AFI
