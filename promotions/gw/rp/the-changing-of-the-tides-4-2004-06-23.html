The changing of the tides #4

23 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/23/04 New York. The place I have called home for many a year. The place where men have been made and men have been broken. But i'm not a man at least not by humanities thoughts on man. Mankind is a creature that can feel love, remorse, hatred, all those emotions that for most of the many years I have lived on this earth. Oh the frailty that is mankind. I see myself becoming more like them everyday and that thought sickens me. At each match they cheer me and Sage on. Each match they applaud as we jump through hoops for their amusment. I have become a slave to a race inferior to myself. I a god among mortals. I who have looked into the shadows, seen what inhabits them and even those creatures of the dark fear my presence. Now I seem to be an attraction for their freakshow. The crowd loves me and I hate them. I've always hated them. They see me as the good guy because I have changed my ways....but my ways only deal with me and not them. My 'good' only concerns my spirit. Am I a good person? By all means no for I am not a person I am a monster. Its what one becomes after years of murder. After years of viscious acts. After destroying the soul with unspeakable atrocities the likes that which only God could condemn a creature. One can not change the stripes of a zebra or the nature of a snake. And so my nature hasnt changed. I am what I am. Silence. The sweet silence that embraced me like a newborn these so many cold nights kept me company as I stood outside the recording studio. It's raining out, no big suprise as I get out of the limo. Most people would make a dash for the place as not to get wet, but I after all these years have learned to take my time. I slowly walk into the place a little wet but nothing to worry about. The recording studio from what I could see had the state of the art equipment, it could make a person with the least amount of talent sound like a star. I walk down the long corridor and walk into a back room. A business office. At the door of this office a large man stands in front of it. He is nothing to the likes of I. I look him over, he stands about six feet seven inches, he has long brown hair, brushed back suprisingly, a goatee, and a black shirt and pants. The shirt having a large anarchy sign on the front of it. I stand there in my large white hat that covers half my face, white mandarin collared trench, matching suit and boots and i give a half smile. Not so much a smile of friendliness but a smile to let him know I could kill him quicker than he could kill me. He does not budge as I walk up to the door. I finally come eye to eye with the guy. He looks down at me and I look up just enough for him to realize who I am. His eyes grow quite larger and he steps aside as he opens the door. The office is nothing like I imagined. It looked mored like a small sanctum than an actual office. The man sitting at the sleak black desk has his chair turned away from the entrance. Having that 'finally we meet again' type of deal going. He never turns to look at me but begins to speak anyway. 'So what is it you want?' 'I need for you to call a rally. The war I have spoken of has finally come to pass. A Mr. Almeric St.Francis is sending a nice little fleet wherever I am...and so naturally he is probably sending a whole lot of them here.' 'Almeric huh? I met him once at the gathering in San Francisco. He is the prince of Texas am I correct?' 'Yes. Indeed he is.' 'This seems more like your problem not ours.' 'How dare you speak with such insolence! Turn and look at who I am before you brush me off like some young slut.' As the blood within me begins to boil the hiss in my voice becomes more clear. The man slowly turns around in the chair. Stunned at who he sees he stands and then kneels as he comes around the front of the desk. I look down at him. One of the most viscous men in the whole entire state kneeling to me for he now knows who I am and why he MUST help me if he wants to continue his unlife. 'Get up you fool. You have been one of the most loyal of the clan dedicated to me. I wish for you to rally up the clan....and any anarchs who may want to go head to head with the Camarilla. For they are coming. I have other business to attend to.' 'Yes sire.' I look at the man and give him a snide smile with a stern stare. 'Sire.....no no no....I'm not your sire. Your blood is tainted where mine is pure. Now get to work.' I walk out of the room the exact way I entered it...silent and cocky...as if gliding on air. I look at the big man who was standing at the door containing my minion. I gave him a stare for a minute, the man slowly began to silently slide down the wall and into the confines of the corner behind the door. I give a slight nod and walk back outside, the ran had stopped and Sage stands outside the car looking at me. I look back at him with the same look I gave the man in front of the door but i ease it a bit for Sage is now my friend and confidant. He is not human nor is he like me. He is something truly unique. He sees that strange look in my eye and cocks his head to one side. 'Is there something the matter Leinad?' 'No my friend. Nothing at all.' 'Thats what you think. I gotta call in the limo while you were in your meeting.' 'Really?' 'We are scheduled in a four on two match at the pay per view.' 'Ah.' 'Is that all you can say is 'ah'!' 'Who against?' 'A group calling themselves The Kindred'. Isn't that what your kind call themselves?' 'Indeed it is my friend....indeed it is....this match will be interesting to say the least.' 'Well you don't seemed worried. Four men against two that even makes me a little worried.' 'I have other matters to attend to first. When the time comes to worry about that I will. First I must finish what I have started in New York because then it shall be off to Vermont.' 'What?' 'Sage, I would like you to have a night on the town on me. Whatever you need charge it to my account. After that go to the Soma Estate not to far from here...I have much to do.' 'Fine man...whatever.' 'Thanks.' I had him my personal credit card and my pin number. Its not like I really have the use for money anymore except for spending on mindless trinkets that mortals play with and think them most dearest to their hearts. I know better. In all my years I have seen things destroyed, those things people adore. I watch as Sage and my limo pull off and I begin to walk slowly. I walk into the oblivion that is New York heading to my next destination. I walk slow but I move like a dolphin through water. I finally stop and look at what's ahead. St. Patrick's Cathedral. I walk with confidence up the stairs and with even more confidence through the large doors. The elaborate stained glass windows mock me as they show a 'savior' and his disciples. I look at each one and shake my head at the thought of mortal faith. Everlasting life after you die. Death. For no man knoweth the time nor the hour. Ressurection. This is what mortals believe in? With my very being I have proved that wrong. 'But it gives them hope sir. Mortals need hope and faith to exsist in this world. Without it they would parish like lambs to the slaughter.' Coming from out of the back of the cathedral a man dressed like a priest begins to walk towards me. I give him a half smile and nod with respect for out of all the world this man would know how it felt to be unique among our kind. He finally makes his way to me and embraces me. It is not natural for me to embrace anyone but only this man in over three thousand years. 'Father Lajos...long time my friend.' 'What brings you back to New York? Your work?' 'Something like that...I figured I'd stop by and see if you had any dreams lately.' 'How is working at GW going? I heard you have quite the match on your hands. Four men against you and your friend...it wont be an easy task.' 'That doesn't concern me at the moment.' 'I hear those four are really good and will be a hard task to take on.' 'Father I have come here to find out if you know anything of this upcoming war and all you can talk about is my match can you not help me in my situation?' 'Leinad....if you listen you will find all you need to in what I have said.' The man smiles to me, bows, and embraces me one last time before departing back into the reccesses of the cathedral. I stand there a moment quite perplexed and then walk out. As I walk back outside the rain has started again. I begin to walk to my last stop in New York. The last person I need to see....Lucan Veare, prince of this lovely state. I walk past a dark alleyway and stop for a moment. I look into the shadows and feel that in the shadows something looks back. I step closer to the shadows and hear a whisper. I smile at what I hear and nod to the creature of the shadow. I brush back all of my hair revealing my full face. One side beautiful as any victorian man set in modern times, while the other half reptilian and rather grotesque. 'You see creature of the shadow. I look into those shadows at the same as what you see before you. We are monsters both you and I but unlike you I know I have no reason to hide myself in shadow. For all know I am what I am.' The shadows stay quite as I fix my hair over the grotesque side of my face and put my hat back on. As I walk toward Veare&Associates, I hear a voice come from the shadows. 'I do not hide I merely take refuge for you will see soon enough that your monsters have come to get you.' I smirk as I walk into the large lawfirm and passed the secretary. He voice merely a mouse to me. I walk until I see a door that says Lucan Veare, attorney at law. I knock before walking in, the secretary right behind me apologizing to the man sitting behind the desk. He waves her away and offers me a chair. 'Leinad I know why you're here. I'm assuming you have come to take your rightful place as prince. We are at your command.' 'Lucan, I know you better than that.' I smile and so does he. The lights in the room go out and it is completely black. But I still since him. I smell his blood. I smell his essence I lunge to where I sense him and actually grab him. I claw my way through to his heart and there it beats like a mortals. I gorge myself on his heart turning my white outfit a nasy shade of red. I stand and pull out my cell. 'Have the limo meet me out in front of Veare&Associates.....asap.' I am what I am. A monster.
