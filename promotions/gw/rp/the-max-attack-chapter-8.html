The Max-Attack: Chapter 8

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today The scene opens up to the outside of the movie theatre where Roswell stands. Roswell starts walking down the sidewalk humming his theme music in his head. Roswell walks by a big billboard and sees a thunderous picture on it. The picture is Metallica with there guitars around there necks. It says that Metallica is playing in Chicago on July 18th. Roswell also sees a booth with a lady behind it. There is a big line behind the booth with a lot of people in line. Roswell quickly runs to the line hoping to get a ticket for the concert. Roswell lines up behind 20 or so people hoping it won’t get sold out. Roswell puts his hands on his head and starts rubbing his head. All of a sudden the line of people start leaving. Everybody leave but it is not sold out yet. Roswell walks up to the booth and asks for one ticket. Roswell takes out his wallet from his jean pocket and pays the lady for the ticket. Roswell walks away and puts the ticket in one of his pockets. Roswell walks by the police station and sees the man with the coat on behind some bars looking out into the sky. The man notices Roswell and gives him the finger and starts cursing also. Roswell just ignores the man and keeps on walking down the street. Roswell then hears a lot of yelling coming from the police station. The man that was looking at Roswell was now outside the police station carrying a knife in his hand. The knife is covered in dripping blood. The man charges at Roswell with the knife in the air. Roswell ducks and uses one of his kicks taking the man off his feet. The man quickly gets to his feet punching Roswell straight in the face. “What the hell do you want with me”!” yells Roswell. The man ignores what Roswell says and punches him again in the nose. The man picks up the blodd covered knife and raises it in the air pointing it at Roswell’s heart. The man brings the knife down but again Roswell moves and the knife hits the hard ground. Roswell hits a spinning kick into the mans nose and it starts bleeding. Roswell runs back to the police station and sees where the m an got the knife from. Roswell sees 5 police officers laying on the ground bleeding hard. The officers look to be dead but one isn’t. The one that wasn’t dead got up from the ground with a gun in his hand. Roswell goes inside the station and hides behind one of the desks. The man with the knife comes to the police station looking for Roswell. The man walks into the doorway of the station and the officer with the gun shot! The man with the knife fell to the ground with a thud. The knife fell to the ground and the man lay there bleeding from two places. The officer puts his gun away but Roswell keepd hiding behind the desk. All of a sudden the man on the ground quickly flipped up to his feet and picked up the knife. The police officer and Roswell don’t know this happened and just walks away. The man throws the knife at the officer and it goes right through the police officers back. The officer falls to the ground just laying there dead. Roswell quickly comes up behind ht e man and punches him in the back of the head. Roswell picks up a gun that was laying on the ground and shoots but it misses the man and hits a girl in the head that was in the police station hiding in a closet. Roswell drops the gun knowing he is better with no weapon but his feet and hands. The man gets up from the ground and throws the knife at Roswell and it barely misses his neck hitting a metal pole on the outside. The man takes off his trench coat and it reveals a blue suit that is made of 100% armour. The man takes off the mask he was using and it reveals another mask but it is blue with glass eye protectors. Roswell runs outside of the station and goes into a back alley. Roswell takes off his normal clothes he was wearing and it reveals a red suit. Roswell then pulls out a red and black mask from his pocket and puts it on covering his long messy hair. Roswell jumps and goes really high onto a building. The man walks out of the police station and starts looking for Roswell. Roswell jumps off the top of the police station onto the man with a cross body. Roswell strangles the man on the ground and starts asking him some questions. “What is your NAME!” yells Roswell. “I am THE BLUE BAT, and no one stops ME!” yells the man back at Roswell. “You’re The Blue Bat?” questions Roswell. “Yes, I am The Blue Bat!” yells the man again. “You tried to kill me in California!” yells Roswell. “Yes I did! And I’m not stopping until your DEAD!” yells the man back. The man otherwise known as The Blue Bat kicks Roswell in the back of the head. The Bat punches Roswell on the ground. The Bat starts punching him some more and starts asking questions of his own to Roswell. “What is your NAME!” questions The Bat. “Well, my real name is Max Roswell. But right now I act as The Max-Attack!” answers Roswell back. Max now kicks The Bat in the back making him fly front wards. Max flips up onto his feet and charges at The Bat with a spear. Max takes The Bat right through a window at the police station. The glass hits the ground as Max and the Bat get up at the same time. The Bat this time charges at Max taking him down the a kind of clothesline. The Bat reaches into Max’s pocket and brings out the Metallica ticket he bought before this had happened. The Bat rips up the ticket and drops the pieces into the sewer. Max starts getting angrier and punches as hard as he can right to the face of The Blue Bat. The Bat flies backwards through the already broken window. Max reaches and takes one of the shattered glass pieces and throws it at The Bat. It hits The Bat right in the mask making a big rip in it. Max tries taking the mask off of The Bat but he can’t get it off of his face. The Blue Bat gets up with Max not knowing and kicks Max in the back of the head. The Bat jumps off his feet and hits Max with a standing splash. The Bat gets up from the pain and picks Max up to his feet. The Bat tries taking Max’s mask off but again he can’t get it off of his face. Max gets out of The Blue Bat’s headlock and hits another spinning kick into The Bat’s throat. Max gets up and hits a superkick out of nowhere onto The Bat’s throat again. Max quickly jumps up really high and lands on the top of the police station. Max quickly jumps off with a modifed corkscrew moonsault onto The Blue Bat. Max hits The Bat really hard but Max doesn’t feel any pain. Max gets up and starts walking away from The Bat signally that he kind of won the fight. But from behind comes The Blue Bat with a wooden bat in his hand. The Bat starts hitting Max with the bat making him fall to the ground. The Blue Bay throws the bat at Max and jumps up into the air and starts flying around. The Blue Bat then brings out one of those little mysterious balls with wings on it and throws down to the ground. BOOM!
