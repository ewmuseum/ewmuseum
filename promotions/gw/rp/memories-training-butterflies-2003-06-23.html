Memories, Training, Butterflies.. Stan Stone Global Wrestling Promo #341 Date: 6/23/03 Location: Seattle Docks/2 hours before Evolution It had been nearly a week since Stan Stone had walked down the entrance ramp and introduced himself to the people of GW. Sure it had gone less then he had expected, Stan had never really wanted to be introduced by Salaok, he despised men who dressed in mod clothing and went about using underhanded tactics to get what they wanted. But Stan needed a way in and that was the quickest way what with Bubba out of commission. So he had taken it, and had taken a crack at one of the biggest stars, Brick, and quite frankly Stan was impressed, really he was shocked, the man was good, he absorbed punishment better then almost anyone he'd ever seen before. Yeah Stan had been a tough man in WCWF but this wasn't about that, he wasn't here to talk about what he did there or anywhere else, he was here to start a new legacy and one that he knew soon enough would bring him back to Brick's doorstep. **** The wind was cold, odd for the time of year but normal for the location. As the hero of our story jogs down the wooden docks of Seattle's bayside area, the water sends wave upon wave crashing along the barnacle infested posts and sending a spray of sea into the air casting its smell of brine and salt far and near. With every few steps Stan threw a punch alternating right and left, the sting in his eyes from the dripping sweat offered a bizzarre almost sadistic distraction from the boredom of the late night jog that he had grown accustomed to running since he had walked into the buisness of wrestling. He smelled the air and it took him back, the old days of playing on at the seaside market, of going out on a fishing boat with his cousins, the feel of the waves beneath your feet the smell of the sea and the thought that out there in the open it was just you, your ship and god and you couldn't ask for anything more... He came back to reality at the edge of the longest dock the edges rotted away from years of exposure to the cold black bay underneath. Stan watched as it lapped at his feet, like a thick void, it's tendrils reaching for the last bit of light that lay before it in this concrete world. His sweats cling to his body and Stan could see that the buisness rarely changed, just the people who were involved with the buisness. The paper in his pocket was the next card, the card he had begun training for long ago, being a fitness nut he had grown accustomed to training and really wasn't sure if he could handle not training. Perhaps it was a flaw but then again the flaw came with a very profitable bonus, ninety percent of all situations can be overcome and even benefited from if only one would prepare and preparation was something Stan prided himself on. Twelve hour workouts, impeccable diets and an almost constant watch via the media on his upcoming opponents helped give him the edge. But going over a mans training regime time and time again causes you to yawn and sleep and really is boring. Hell, just talking about the same guy non-stop brings your eyelids just a little lower. Looking down into the deep abyss below he knew that what stared back at him would be his past, the past he tried so strongly to forget but he knew like the scars the crossed his body that his past was burnt into his mind and would always remain, that is why he shudders when he looks into the water or the mirror and sees the long haired devil smiling back at him, a mask of crimson running down his face and a gleam in his eyes that seems otherworldly. It was twelve O'clock, midnight and Saturday morning, the bars were still open and selling drinks, the streets were still alive downtown, but down in the docks were the dregs made there rounds only an aging wrestler with his old weary wounds jogs along the length of the bay, his eye on the water and the man who jogged along side him. His fiance would be worried about him if he wasn't back soon, she knew of his past and how he was a recovering alcoholic, she could remember the nights he'd come home, piss drunk, a man feared for his temper reduced to a baby before the woman he loved as his emotions instead of staying bottled up poured forth from him like the cries of a newborn baby. She had pleaded with him to quit, but it would take the intervention of an old enemy to bring about his eventual death and rebirth. His heart skipped a beat every time he thought of Nathaniel Duke, the English wrestler who had shattered his face and stopped his heart.... As Stan thought of his face being reduced to splinters his hands ran along the scars where his face had been opened and the bones surgically repaired or reconstructed. ***** It was only two hours before Evolution went live, and only two hours before Stan would walk out in his first official match. His muscles were twitching and he felt nervous, everything in his body was roaring for him to win, for him to succeed and not let up but he knew that there were risks in that ring and risks that people rarely prepare for, Stan hoped he had prepared for the worst of them. He stepped into the ring with a man who was in a buisness less then respectable and he knew that men like that could be dangerous. But then again, he was confident, he felt strong, fast and ready to fight, his adrenaline was pumping and his whole body knew it was time to get in that ring. Leaning against his locker sipping at a bottle of water he looked down at his new wrestling shorts, nothing special, black with white letter on the buttocks, no nothing special at all. He still wore a knee brace from an injury he had sustained just before departing from his former stomping grounds. Would it effect him tonight? Possibly, was he prepared for that? Yes. It wouldn't be long before he could remove the brace and function at one-hundred percent of Stan Stone potential. He raises an eyebrow as other wrestlers come and go, some casting him glances, some glares.... yeah it would be a rough road that he had ahead of him and he knew that his uncle would be livid the when he got word of his jump. Maybe Bubba was having one hell of a laugh knowing that the blood of his own rival had entered into a contract with him. Contracts weren't important now though, only wrestling, only preparation and being ready for the coming fight. John Zero was impressive, Stan Stone had to admit that, he didn't know anything about the guy except for his height, weight and from watching a few matches he had been involved with, other then that he was really in the dark about his opponent, but luckily his opponent was in the same fix. He didn't want to lose, he planned on doing everything he can to win, he planned to beat John into the ground if it came to that and while the thoughts would seem empty and typical of a guy who just stepped onto the scene but it's the truth plain and simple. Tonight was the night that would help make him or brake him and being a man who had been broken before he didn't much like the thought of returning to that... no Stan Stone had worked too hard, too many long nights and long days in preparation for this entrance into a federation that would change his life and he wouldn't let all that training go to waste... He didn't hate John Zero, he didn't like John Zero, he didn't know John Zero but he would beat John Zero... he had a point to make and some respect to earn and nothing is as important to Stan Stone as respect...
