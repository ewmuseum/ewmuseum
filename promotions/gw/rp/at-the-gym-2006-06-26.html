At the Gym

26 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/26/06 The scene opens up inside of a small weight room. The door is opened, and all the walls are lined with mirrors. 'The Anthem' by Good Charolette is blaring from the speakers that are in the ceiling tiles. A small blond haired woman is the only person in the weight room. She has her black streaked blond hair up in a pony tail, and she is wearing short skin tight pink leather shorts, black knee pads, white boots, and a tight pink leather top. The small blond is working over a red punching bag that hangs from the ceiling with a series of kicks and punches. The sweat that rolls down her back, along with the grunts and screams shows just how much force and effort is being put into her training. The cameraman closes in on her, getting a close up of the dust that flies from the punching bag as it gets hammered by the lovely woman. She hits it with a left forearm, right elbow combo and takes a step back. Violently she thrusts forward hitting a hellashish superkick. The woman stops her workout and turns towards the cameraman. Sweat rolls down her face, and her breasts stick out with every deep breath from her exhausting work out. The blond pulls the loose strand of hair that are in front of her face with her blood stained taped hands. She looks at the camer for a second, with a disgusted look on her face then walks away from the camera, and takes a seat on one of the weight benches. A bottle of water sits on the floor along with a black gym bag. Two shirts lie on the bench next to the woman as well. She picks up the bottle of water and takes a long drink. After finishing she begins to speak. Woman:'Excuse me, could I have some privacy? I don't know who you are, I don't work for you, and I do not want to give an interview for any reason whatsoever. All I would like to do is finish my workout, and finish it in peace.' The woman then gets a smile on her face and stands up from the bench. She walks back over and begins to go back to her work out with the punching bag. The camera takes a few steps towards the bag and watches for a while. Then slowly the cameraman turns around. Standing behind him with his arms crossed in front of his chest is Ice. Ice's long blond hair rests on his shoulders, and his cold blue eyes pierce through the camera's lens. The cameraman startled by Ice's presence hurrily takes a few steps backwards and almost falls to the ground. Ice's bare chest bulges as he reaches forward with both arms to catch the cameraman before he falls. 'And just what do you think you were doing? Trying to snoop around through our things? We were polite enough to invite you to our work out facility and you go and attempt to do something like that? Where are your manners? However, I am in a good mood today, and will let it slide this one time.' Ice smiles and pats the cameraman on the back as Ice sits down on the bench the blond was just sitting on and leans back. He extends his arms and when he pulls them forward two one hundred pound dumbells are in his hands. Ice begins to do dumbell flies with the weights. Almost effortlessly he shoots through the sets. Ice sits up and then begins to do dumbell military presses with the same weights. Again he blows through the sets with almost no effort at all. Ice drops the weights and watches as the blond continues her assult on the defenseless punching bag. 'Ah that scene looks all too familiar. Reminds me of what I did to Angus the Bull on Overload, and Dynamite X a week earlier. Angus, you see boy, you need to watch your mouth, because that is what happens when you talk your sh!t. Now Whiplash is just around the corner, and yet again I have been placed in a match with a couple of nobodies! What in the hell is going on around this place? How many useless sons of bitches do I have to mame before someone worthwhile grows some balls and steps up to the plate. I left an open challenge for anyone willing to step up and take it. Still I have had no takers! Still I have to walk into another pay-per-view, and carry the load myself. Well, I hope you are ready Vortex. I hope you are ready Stan Stone. This is your big shot. This is your chance to make a name for yourself. This is your opportunity to become famous. And believe me boys, I will make you famous. I really do feel sorry for you two. Because you happen to be the wrong men, in the wrong place, at the wrong time. So anything that I do to you two, it's not on my hands. Heed my warning. Walk down the aisle, and enter the ring at YOUR OWN RISK. You have been warned. Should you not heed my warning, I will not be held responsible for what bodily injuries you may occur during this match.' Ice stands up and stretches a little before begining to speak once again. 'Vortex, Stan Stone, let me get one thing straight, this Sunday night you two will be the lambs that are sent to slaughter. Because with your bodys I will send a message to the entire GW. Most importantly I will be sending a message to Angus the Bull and Salomane Vation. Angus, you keep running your mouth, and you keep talking your smack, and I have let you go for this long. You want me to watch my back. No need to. Because frankly you don't have the balls to do anything. However you keep telling yourself that you will do something, and you keep running that mouth and you will force me to either make you put up or shut up. Now as for Salomane Vation, I still haven't forgotten what you did to me. The way you walked your narrow ass down that aisle and chokeslammed me after I had just finished a twenty minute match. Well if that is how you like to do things that's fine by me. But get one thing straight. You are still here in this company, because I allow you to be. You walk around with that sh!tty smirk on your face, and your little b!tch boy lacky Angus because I allow you to do so. So I make this suggestion to you. Keep living your life the way it is. Pretend to you don't hear me calling you out. Pretend I am not around, and continue to hide from me. Because if you don't the world I allow you to live in, the life I allow you to have will be crushed and taken away from you. Ice tilts his head from side to side cracking his neck. Then he proceeds to cracking his knuckles on both hands. 'This Sunday night, you want to see just who I am? Get ready. The end is near.' Ice walks past the cameraman and makes his way to the squat rack. Ice begins doing a squat workout. The small blond continues her punching bag routine. Slowly the cameraman backs away and takes a look at the two shirts lying on the bench. One t-shirt is black and has bold gold letters that read STAR, the other shirt is black and has bold silver letters that look like metal blocks that read ICE. The camera zooms in on the letters on both shirts as the scene fades to black.
