Evolution for 02/14/2005 - Hell in Vegas

13 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/13/2005 Pressure A huge build up Expectations And I lost? I could take solace in the fact that I lost to the man who would become the TV champion But that would be too easy I should have won, I should be that champion Corvus didn’t beat me I beat myself Hey, some people that recognize me’good to know I have some bit of popularity still despite the minor setback. Wonder if the town will still like me once Syn Vegas opens. *An expansion on the idea that became Club Syn in Houston, TX. A music bar/restaurant and den of expressions such as tattooing, piercing and whatever else that pisses off your parents.* [Michael Craiford, better known to GW fans as DeathSun, stands in the lobby of a Starbucks in Las Vegas, preparing to order and signing autographs for some fans.] Seeing them and then seeing Hessian makes me realize how far things have come. [A pained expressions clouds his face for a moment] A few years back, that’s who I was’without all the satanic bullshit. I mean come on, if there is a higher power I’m sure it has better shit to do than worry about what we believe and they would proabaly intervene real quick if they saw all damn things doen in their name. [A smirk as he signs the final request and goes to look at the menu.] I was a cold blooded self professed killer, aiming to take out Salaok, the man I hate more than any other on this planet. I was blamed with the death of his father, which of course I didn’t do, but I had no problem letter him think I did’gave me an edge on him’something not often done. [He places his order, a tall caramel macchiato and a piece of their exquisite lemon pound cake’just like mom used to make. A minute smile shows as he eats the cake, it evokes memories, some good and some bad.] I can’t help but wonder which side of me has the edge needed to compete in this new GW, so different from the one I used to know. I could become the killer again, no reason not to’no family, no loved ones, nothing for anyone to use against me. [Sitting back for a moment he reflects on his past, the thought of his parents conjuring images in his mind?] ///They were fighting’again, nothing unusual. I wish I was at school instead of here. At least at school I was king, could do whatever I want. Helps to be the biggest kid I guess. I swear I will get out of here as soon as possible’but what about mom, she can’t defend herself against dad. I’m all she has in the world right now. I’m sure she only stays here because she wants me to have a stable home. Stable’yea right. I saw mom looking at that photo album again, the one they won’t ever let me see. I found it once, but dad caught me and made me think twice about even going near their room for quite a while. I wonder what is in there that they will not let me see?/// Hmm’I forgot all about that photo album’I wonder what happened to it. [Scanning his memory, his face goes a bit blank as he tries to recall the time after the funeral before he was sent off to juvenile hall. Try as he may, nothing comes to mind?] Not sure’maybe my aunt came away with that book. I need to find her and see. Mom and her were so close, I’m sure she got quite of few things out of the house. But will she want to see me? She, like some others, still blames me a bit, unable to believe what happened in the house that fateful day. I will have to get in contact with her and find out’perhaps after Evolution and my battle with Hessian?.and after I stop by Syn Vegas to see how things are going. Will be great to have a base here in Vegas to work from, now that I have a competent manager in Houston. [He pulls out some research he was doing on his opponent, a 7 foot plus monster known for his extremely violent battles all over the world. Thumbing through some of it, he sighs, finishing off his drink.] This guy is huge, but I think he relies on image too much. Trying to psyched out his opposition with murderous words and satanic imagery rather than just simply beating them in the ring. I’m not above flash and mind games, just seems overdone and played out. Why rely on useless imagery and mumbo jumbo when you can just get things doen in the ring. That last bit of footage from Hessian was disturbing enough by itself. A scene of blatant murder’hopefully it was just some doctored up snuff film, another tactic used to scare his opponents’though its not as effective on me as he would hope. Maybe its not an act, maybe its just the way he is? [As if on cue, he turns to a particularly “evil” image of Hessian covered in “blood” for some magazine layout. Michael stifles a laugh, drawing some attention from the other patrons. He apologizes, gathers his research, disposes of his refuse and leaves the coffee shop.] [Getting on his Harley, he speeds off towards the construction site. Times lapses a bit and he pulls up at the site, the large sign is being mounted above the front door’Syn Vegas it reads. There’s a few men milling here and there, finishing final details of the club’s outside appearance. The forewoman walks over to Craiford, shaking his hand as she removes her helmet, spilling auburn curls to her midback. DeathSun looks her up and down and smirks, she blushes a tiny bit but composes herself, handing him the latest progress report. ] DeathSun - Almost time it seems right April? Should be able to open in the next few weeks, barring any permit screw ups April ? Yes sir. We shouldn’t have any snags whatsoever. Everything has been triple checked by the lawyers. DeathSun ? Good’I like to see that, thoroughness and all. April ? Well, I have to be, enough disadvantages already being a woman general contractor and all. DeathSun ? Well, finish this job on time and I’ll make sure you have more work than you ever dreamed of. [The woman smiled, donned her helmet and went back to work on the building that would soon become hopefully the hottest new night club in Las Vegas. Michael watched the work being done for a few moments as the sun began to set in the distance. Scene fades?]
