And Point (End of Game Series)

24 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/24/04 'We are what we repeatedly do. Excellence, then, is not an act but a habit.' -Aristotle The scene opens to a silent hallway, lit by dim overhead lights tucked to the sides, aside the marble pillars that support the cathedral ceiling. The rafters above, adorned with twines of brass and iron Celtic roses, and the floor below an obsidian mirror, waxed with the sweat of those unfit to even revere us in our presence. The common world is a cruel circle of twisted metal, tempered glass, and solidified concrete. We are surrounded by artistic structures of stone, brass, gold, and silver. We walk on a material that many would build statues; we build statues of materials that many would base entire economic structures. To be the elite; the perfection; you must have the veracity to back it. The desire to rule and be the supreme; the cream of the crop, you could say. Foot steps and then a frame; a man in a black Italian silk suit, designed by one of the great designers: Armani, Versace, Gianfranco Ferre. The average man's Ralph Lauren and Hilfiger. Fucking plebeians. Excellence is not a moment, or a method. It is simply what you are. And everything that we are, we represent, is excellent. 'Wars have never hurt anybody except for the people who die.' -Salvador Dali He continues down the hallway before another set of foot steps fall in unison with the first's. A second set and then a third. The Echelon. ech·e·lon ( P ) Pronunciation Key (sh-ln) n. 1. A formation of troops in which each unit is positioned successively to the left or right of the rear unit to form an oblique or steplike line. 2. A flight formation or arrangement of craft in this manner. 3. A similar formation of groups, units, or individuals. 4. A subdivision of a military or naval force: a command echelon. 5. A level of responsibility or authority in a hierarchy; a rank. John Zero's frame, adorned in a similar designer black suit along side of Deacon Holmes, in a more casual attire. The three men walk down the hallway, standing tall. A fourth follows and then a fifth. Naria Xiou and then Tyler Pierce, in a white alabaster suit, follow behind the three men. A double door opens near the end of the hallway and the three men step inside along with Tyler Pierce. Naria Xiou bows respectfully to the four, not receiving a response (as is common practice.) The doors close and the elevator descends. Elegance... Descent... Sweat & blood... Tears... The elevator itself is elegant; Mahogany walls, golden fixtures, an old-style construction to the door, complete with hand carved Celtic roses. The doors held elegance refined; perfection in every hand-crafted niche. The doors slowly and gently slide open, revealing quite the different atmosphere. The room shielded from the remainder of the luxury scraper bore a different tone. A tone of twisted concrete, rusted metal, and shattered glass. Marble nor gold nor silver or bronze have their place here; only sweat, blood, and tears. 'He was brought here last night, gentlemen. Apparently, he's been holding a bit of information out on us... it would seem that this fellow works for Iso Systems and has been preparing to sell some of our footage to the highest bidder... as well as our custom security system.' Maverick Reno smirks, his black hair tied back into a pony tail, a silver band holding it back. He removes his knee length silken dress coat and hangs it on a nearby hanger, the blue dress shirt now fully exposed as well as a black silk vest. He now removes the vest and places that on a nearby table before clasping his hands behind his back and approaching the young man. The young man that Tyler had been speaking of is Markus Spround. He's of about age 30 with dark brown hair although right now, it's matted and carries a red tint. Normally, he would wear a pair of glasses to help his pitiful vision but tonight, his glasses have been shattered; the cut marks from the glass obvious about his eyes. His clothes are ripped and he seems to be in a fair amount of pain. He's standing before a large wooden post, his hands chained together behind it; his feet also chained near the bottom. 'Mr. Spround. It would seem that you've found yourself quite the profitable business, eh? The selling of corporate information.' Spround's head slowly and painfully rises, his bloodshot eyes focusing on the man now speaking to him. 'Corporate information? I was much more concerned with the barbarism!' 'Brave words.' 'Bravery comes easy when you're waiting to die.' 'Aww, come now, Mr. Spround. Don't be so melodramatic.' Reno's tranquility is almost frightening, his serenity is truly the mark of a mad man. 'You see, we paid you a lot of money for this system and what do you do? You turn around and you screw us, nay, you fuck us. You try to sell our personal information to your highest bidder? Is that the case? That's what I've been told atleast.' Spround looks down, anger filling his face before filling his mouth; he spits, a mixture of saliva and blood hits the Armani wingtip of Maverick Reno. Reno slowly looks down, his eyes fixating on the disgusting mass on his shoe. 'You're a filthy bastard, Spround. Now you tell me something, boy.' Reno grabs Spround by the collar, jerking him forth and causing the chains to tighten. Spround is strained and grimaces due to this. 'Does anyone else have access to our system? I advise you to speak now, boy.' Spround is quiet, his eyes locking on the face of Reno. 'I have to admire your veracity, Markus. You have the nerve to invade our privacy, to videotape our meetings, to watch our every move. All the while knowing that we know every little... fucking... minute detail or your pathetic life.' The words are spoken slowly, allowing Markus to form his own ideas as to where this is going. 'I know that you're holding out on us, Markus. Want to know how?' Markus's eyes widen, his gaze lifting to Reno's sub artic eyes. 'Tyler, have Brendan step in here please.' With this, Tyler flips open a cell phone and speaks quietly. He closes it and replaces it into his pocket. Very little time passes before another door opens on the far side of the room. Markus's eyes shoot in that direction; Reno's remain locked on Markus. Brendan, a man in a jumpsuit, is carrying a large bag over his shoulder. He slams the bag quickly onto the table and we can see that the form inside the bag is squirming, muffled cries coming from within. Brendan quickly unzips the black body bag and reaches inside; he jerks upward, pulling a young woman up by the root of her hair, ripping most out. He face has been beaten and her lips bloody. Their clothes ripped and torn, sullied with dirt, oil and gasoline. 'Now you tell us, Markus. Does anyone else know?' Markus's eyes grow sad and pathetic, tears welling up in the corners. He clenches his eyes shut and looks down. The young lady screams. 'NO MARKUS! YOU CAN'T TELL THEM! WE CAN'T LET THEM GET AWAY WITH THIS!' 'Oh won't somebody shut the bitch up?' The voice of Deacon Holmes rings forth, his eyes shooting to Brendan. Brendan quickly brings the handle of a hammer across the back of her head, knocking her limp on top of the work station. He then shoves her body off, keeping hold of one hand that he places on the tables edge. He raises the hammer and violently brings it back down onto the woman's finger; the snap and breaking is heard. Markus screams out. 'NO! I... BUT YOU'LL KILL HIM TOO!' Reno looks over at Brendan and nods. Brendan picks the young lady up and tapes a small packet beneath her nose and she suddenly coughs and jerks her head about, the smelling salts taking instant effect. He lifts the young lady over his shoulder again, kicking and screaming. Markus watches intently. He drops her body into a small concrete pool inside of the basement; there's nothing else inside of the vat. He picks up a bucket of gasoline and pours it in, the vat slowly filling up. 'NO! YOU CAN'T DO THIS!' 'Then tell us the truth, Markus. Tell us... and she'll live.' 'H-how can I trust you?' Reno smiles and motions for Tyler to approach. He holds up a pair of plane tickets and a key. 'A pair of tickets to Barbados and a key to a cottage there. In exchange for your answer and silence. Deal?' Markus glances over and then back again. Brendan has put a mask on a gas mask and open the water purifying ventilation system. 'I... I love you Lauren. I... I'll talk.' Reno grabs Markus by the throat and bashes the back of his head against the wood. 'His name, Markus.' Markus's eyes shoot to Lauren who has been gagged and had weights tied to her arms, keeping her from escaping the pool. 'Sam... Sam Downs. Please... please let us go.' Reno smiles and releases his hold on Markus, allowing the man to breath fluidly again. Brendan glances over and Reno waves his hand in a forget it kind of motion. Brendan nods and walks towards the group. 'Sam Downs, Tyler. Get Naria on it.' 'What about them, Mr. Reno?' Brendan speaks, his words filled with curiosity. Reno glances at Markus and the girl now known as Lauren. 'Remove her gag so that he can hear her scream as she burns... then afterwards, ease his pain.' Reno takes a small hypodermic needle from a nearby table and puts it in the hand of Brendan. 'Put this into the side of his neck... he'll die a few hours later.' Markus's eyes are wide as Brendan walks to Lauren and removes her gag, her screaming instantly remaining... it turns to questions of why and how could you. The four men return to the elevator, Maverick Reno fully dressed again. Markus is now yelling at the men. 'YOU FUCKING ANIMALS! YOU'LL PAY! YOU WILL DEFINATELY PAY! I HOPE YOU FUCKING DIE!' The elevator doors, exquisite and beautiful, begin to close as a vwoosh is heard. The room now closing off lights up and screams of anguish can be heard. Markus turns away and clenches his eyes shut. The doors close and the noise is instantly gone. 'So can we eat now? I'm fuckin' starvin!.' The others in the elevator turn and look at Deacon and shake their heads. The scene slowly fades to black. 'The distance between insanity and genius is measured only by success.' -James Bond: Tomorrow Never Dies Insanity: Killing another and their family over a business deal gone bad? According to the above, that doesn't make us insane, simply shrewd and intelligent, right? The Echelon, a group of men who among their shared wealth rival any member of the Fortune 500. Then why are they not a member of the Fortune 500? Simple; a question of reputable business practices as well as a few businesses that aren't exactly reported to the I.R.S every year. Need an example? October 21st, 2003 - Approximately 3:00pm New York City, New York - The Echelon Tower The scene opens to the office of Maverick Reno; he's standing behind his desk, his eyes focused on the city of New York visible through the window. He stands before us in a black dress shirt and black dress pants, his hair tied into a tight ponytail as is the usual when not in the ring. A moment passes and we pan back, allowing us to see the marble and Mahogany walls accented in gold. Footsteps and a voice... 'Mr. Reno, Mr. Anthony Cicero Stecchino is here to see you.' 'Anthony, eh?' 'Yes sir. He handles the...' 'I know who he is. Show him in. Also, get security on standby as well as Zero, Holmes, and Pierce. I never know when to trust Mr. Stecchino.' It would seem that the two men, mortal enemies, meet on grounds that are not exactly neutral. Naria Xiou, the source of the voice, leaves the room quickly. A moment passes and Tony Stecchino walks in wearing a red suit with white accents. He walks towards the desk, his eyes on Reno. 'Mavy! How's it hangin' old pal?' 'Anthony, no games. You've been doing good business, yes?' Stecchino smiles and opens a box on Reno's desk, picking out a cigar and placing it between his lips. 'You could say that. Hell, you should know; you get a fair share of the profits.' Reno turns, nodding. Tony lights his cigar and puffs it a few times before sitting back in his chair and kicking his feet up on Reno's desk. 'So you've got me here because of the low profits lately, huh? I figured you would... well, you know how the import and export business is. Supply and demand, supply and demand! Always something is coming up; gotta pay the dues to the guys who work overseas, gotta pay my own crew, gotta pay for repairs on my ship. All sorts of shit, Mavy. What can ya do?' Reno nods and leans back. 'I understand, Tony. I do... or did. Until I saw your new house in the Bahamas.' Stecchino coughs, dropping the cigar into his lap. He quickly pats out the cigar by beating it with his hand. He stands up, brushing his now burnt suit off. 'Mav, man. You have to understand... it's business. I ... t..that house is just something I propagated from someone I had to take care of. It didn't come out of your profits at all, Mav. I promise, I swear this.' Reno nods and points at the seat. He stands and starts to pace back and forth behind his desk; his hands clasped in a tight ball at the low of his back, his eyes on the floor. 'How long have we known each other, Anthony?' 'Uh... I don't know, Mav. About seven years?' Reno nods, still pacing back and forth. 'How angry do I get when someone fucks me, Anthony?' 'Mav, I've not fu..' 'Drop it.' Reno smacks the cigar out of Stecchino's hand as he attempts to relight it, Stecchino's eyes shooting to Reno. Reno grabs Stecchino by the collar and jerks him up from his seat, standing him before him. 'Do you think I was foolish enough to give you free reign with those arms shipments? I've been watching you from day one. You know Jason? The guy who helps load the trucks? Yeah, my payroll. Richard... helps you with the numbers? Again, my payroll. Arnold... the guy who feeds your fucking fish? MY PAYROLL! Anthony, you've been fucking me from day one. A few hundred-thousand here. A few hundred-thousand there. Yeah, you made a nice addition to the team... you got the Yakazura Shipping Company in Japan under our wings; great job. Guess what? You're term on the counsiliu is over. You're finished. You've fucked me for the last time.' 'Mav, I...' 'Shut the fuck up. I spoke with Holmes and Zero. Price and Pierce are also in agreement. We're tired of it. I'm putting Pierce in charge of your divisions; I can trust him. I respect him. You're no better than the blood soon to be on the underside of my Armani's. Do you understand that, Anthony?' 'But Mav...' 'Shut the fuck up. Don't you listen when I speak to you, you dumb son of a bitch? Just shut the fuck up.' Reno's voice is slow and calm, never going above normal levels. Stecchino on the other hand is quite the contrary; he's panicking, trying to over speak Reno so as to be heard. It doesn't matter how loud you are, if you don't matter then it's over; in this case, Reno doesn't give one damn about what Stecchino has to say in his defense. The case is over; the death penalty has been called for. Reno walks towards his desk and presses a button on his intercom. 'Naria, have John sent in please... have him bring in the plastic sheets.' Stecchino whips around at this comment, his eyes growing wide with fright. He can't believe what he's hearing; after seven years, there will finally be a victor in this battle of gladiators and there is no way around it; Stecchino is the mouse and Reno is the leopard. The time has come. This is what happens when you cross the man known as Maverick Reno. 'So this is it? No debate? No discussion? No defense?' 'We already debated; you weren't invited.' 'YOU CAN'T FUCKIN' DO THIS! YOU'RE MAVERICK RENO! YOU CAN'T TAKE A LIFE FOR THE HELL OF IT!' Reno turns and cocks his head, giving Stecchino a long hard look. 'You know you're right, Anthony... what was I thinking.' Just then, as Stecchino is beginning to calm with a change in Reno's attitude, Zero walks in with a roll of plastic. Deacon is right behind him. The two men roll out the plastic all over the marble floor, Stecchino watching. Pierce stands in the door way, leaning against the frame. Stecchino jerks back, looking at Reno. 'What? I'm not going to kill you...' 'I am.' The voice comes from behind Tony; he spins and there she stands, his ex-wife, Melissa Phillips. She smiles and holds up a semi-automatic handgun, silencer attached and aims it right at the head of her former husband. Stecchino starts to walk backwards, panicking and trying to talk his way out of trouble. 'Look Mav, I'll get you the money. I CAN GET YOU THE FUCKIN' MONEY! Just call 'em off, Mav. Call them off.' 'How can I trust you?' 'I'll get you the money and then I'll disappear. YOU HAVE MY FUCKING WORD! JUST CALL THEM OFF!' Melissa pulls the trigger back, aiming carefully. CRACK! Stecchino hits the floor; Deacon Holmes smacks the Billy club into his hand and shakes his head. 'Muh-fucka wouldn't shut the fuck up. Can't even concentrate.' Melissa laughs and puts the gun away before walking out of the office. Holmes and Zero roll Stecchino's limp body up in the plastic, tie it together tightly, and then lift it up, one man at each end. 'Dump him. Oh and Pierce... you've been promoted.' The scene fades from here. 'The entrepreneur is essentially a visualizer and an actualizer... He can visualize something, and when he visualizes it he sees exactly how to make it happen.' -Robert L. Schwartz I guess that's what you could say about Anthony Stecchino; he visualized a fortune and made it a reality. Now to hold true to the clich? for his kind, an aging Mafioso, he sleeps in a plastic blanket with the fishes. Many think that The Echelon is nothing more than a mafia with class; nay. The Echelon is much more than simple, er, 'aggressive' business tactics. The Echelon is a combination of shrewd businessmen and women, a culmination of every talent in the known world, as well as a list of under-the-table establishments longer than my, well, let's just say it's long. And as for the 'every talent in the known world' part, there are even individuals who grace and have graced the halls of The Echelon who were not necessarily restricted to that statement. --- July 15th, 2003 - Approximately 11:00am. Soma Corp - Prague, Czech Republic The streets of Prague, Czech Republic, are desolate and quiet. A Soma Corp office is located here and inside, a penthouse that houses one of the worlds most elite business men; a man known not only for his business skill, but for his social behavior. What he is not known for is the second reason that The Echelon wants him. The scene cuts to an office, simple yet elegant. Curtains hide the cityscape from the office, keeping it completely sealed off from the outside world. Naria Xiou stands aside Maverick Reno as he sits, relaxed in a chair across from the unnatural; an abomination against God. 'The walking embodiment of sin.' -Aiden de Sarret Or atleast, that's what Mr. de Sarret referred to them as. He was sort of an expert, however. A night stalker; in Romanian - a Somnombulian, in German and French - Nosferatu, in English - A vampire. Reno is calm, however, Naria doesn't seem to be. She's on edge and nervous, her anxiety getting the best of her. Leinad Soma sits across from them, his hair covering one side of his face. He's dressed in an elegant suit without the coat, a golden watch chain hanging loosely about his waist. Reno is as usual, in an Armani black suit with red accents. A servant places a tray upon the table and nods to the two men and then Naria, who steps back from the servant and into the light. She's dressed in a simple black and gray business dress, her hair hanging loosely about, her neck covered by a very thick choker. She smiles and nods to the man and adjusts the briefcase in her hand. Soma lifts his glass, a thick green liquid inside, and swishes it about. Reno nods and lifts his sifter of brandy. 'Shall we drink to a new business deal... or simply to the drink?' Reno's question prompts a response from Soma, his answer still pending in regards to The Echelon. 'I have heard much of your Echelon, Monsieur Reno. On the contrary, I've heard very little of your involvement.' 'And that is as I would wish it, Mr. Soma. Would you like to see my proposal?' 'Cash? Money? Dental insurance? I have no need for such things; look around you! I am wealthy, I have everything I will ever need. Why should I accept your proposal?' Reno smiles and motions for Naria to place the briefcase on the desk. She does so and and then stands; with a PDA, she types a few words and the locks on the briefcase open remotely. Reno pushes the lid up revealing its contents: A single finger set in a foam cast; the finger looks to have been ripped from it's owner, a very exquisite silver ring attached still. Reno spins the case around and Soma chuckles. 'You do your research, Monsieur Reno. I must give you that. You have a deal... you have slain my enemy, saving my involvement, and you offer me great adventure with worthy companions. The Echelon is worthy...' The scene fades to black. I'm coming for you.
