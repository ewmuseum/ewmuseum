You'll always look up to me from the ground

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sunday A man is seen walking through Deacon Holmes? penthouse. After an inspection of the living room and den the man continues on and into a hallway. He stops at each door along the way peeking in to find nothing. At the end of the hall there is one door which unlike all the others is closed. Lights of all different colors can be seen spilling from the inside of the room and escaping through the space between the floor and the bottom of the door. A low roar can be heard as the man gets closer. After moving in more the noises sound more like people screaming. Now right in front of the door the man slowly reaches his hand out and grips the knob. He pauses for a moment and takes a deep breath before turning the knob and bursting in the door to find Deacon Holmes sitting at the edge of his bed eyes glued to the TV with all the lights off and curtains closed. Holmes looks more like a zombie than a GW superstar as he sits resting his elbows on his knees and his head in his hands with his eyes wide open and focused on the television. The darkened room is only lit by the TV which paints a small area in technicolor. On the screen there are highlights of Quartz in action including him tossing Holmes into the globaltron and chokeslamming him out of the ring. At the end of the tape there is a clip of Holmes with Quartz in a sleeper which he then turns into a DDT. He looks up to find CW standing in his doorway as he presses rewind on the VCR. Deacon Holmes: CW what are you doing here? CW: Checkin up on ya. I thought you might wanna get some training in today after we eat since you got that big match tomorrow. Deacon Holmes: Big match? You mean big opponent, this match means nothing. The only thing it will prove is that I can singlehandedly beat a slow, dim-witted, unkept, Nevermore foot soldier that is slightly more exciting than a box of rotten fruit and likes to roam the streets in his spare time. That clown’s got nothin on me, I mean look at me, I’m strong, I’m fast, I’ve got charisma. What’s he? CW: Much bigger and stronger than you. I’m sure they don’t call him a manbeast for nothin. Deacon Holmes: I know, they call him that cause he’s stupid. He may have a size advantage but he’s gonna look up to me when he’s on the ground. I’m like a machine CW, I’m built for this. You might as well call me Deacon 5000. On Monday there won’t be no lettin up so when he goes down there won’t be no gettin up and by the time he catches on I’ll have ended his career and walked out with tha whole floor so he has nothin to fall back on. CW: Well aren’t you worried about all the backing he has? Seeing as how you have none? Deacon Holmes: I don’t need any, they ain’t gotta like or love me but they gon respect me. Followin tha pack is how you wind up dead last. You know that. CW: That may be true, but not when your opponent rolls with a squad. Deacon Holmes: Of what? Cowards! He can bring them too, don’t really want it to be like that but I’ll tear them apart if I have ta. You don’t seem to understand yet that I’m in a classroom of my own, I’m too far gone for competition. Although I could use some but they ain’t makin me much. My strength, speed, and strong mind complete such a special blend. I am not like them at all and I cannot pretend. Besides, Quartz is so worried about Tragic Hero right now that he doesn’t see anything else, I’ll walk right through this clown and tap dance on his kidney on the way. CW: Alright, well what if one of your “friends” shows up? Deacon Holmes: Such as? CW: Leviathan. Deacon Holmes: I can only hope, I’ve still got some of the ass whoopin I gave Chaz Bryant saved up for him. If he knows what’s good for him he’ll stay his ass home in whatever cardboard box he’s squatting in now. But if not he can get it too, I owe him one. CW: You’re still on that guy? Deacon Holmes: Did you see what happened at Overload!” That nimrod interfered in my match right when I was gonna win. It’s cut and dry, he wants a piece of me and I want to beat him with frenzied glee and reckless abandon leaving him a crumpled heap in the corner reminiscent of superman’s dad on kryptonite. CW: Well what about Roz Blayze? He said he’d be there, and he’s one of your opponent for the Primestar. Deacon Holmes: Shit I should whip the spanish fly’s ass first! If he doesn’t think he needs to worry about me he’s got another thing comin. If GW had enough TV time I’d wrestle him and Quartz back to back on Monday. Damn I need my own show! Tuesday Night Holmes! Then I’d have enough time to show Roz first hand what he needs to be worrying about and why he shouldn’t even show up for our match at the pay per view. I hope he watches me good and hard tomorrow so then he can see the exact angle at which my foot will be entering his ass at Wrestlecade. I’ll finally be able to get back at Leviathan once and for all there, me versus him for the Primestar Title. CW: Huh? You know you’ve both got to win triple threat matches for that to happen right? Deacon Holmes: Yeah, but who are those people to me? Roz and Akio? Roz has lost what? 9 of his last 11 matches? I’m out of his league, Akio couldn’t even handle Venom Alexander, one of if not the softest champion I ever saw. CW: What about Leviathan’s bracket? Deacon Holmes: Are you serious? He’s gotta beat Chaz Bryant and some Andy Griffith Show reject that is now homeless. Chaz is nothin without his gun, I beat the brakes off that clown without even breakin a sweat. Unless he’s gotten soft-er, he should be waiting for me in the final round of the tournament. CW: And then what? He’s beaten you before... Deacon Holmes: And hasn’t seen me and walked away without a black eye since. He’s gonna get it at the pay-per-view until his blood, sweat, and bones ache...and then I’ll get serious. CW: Yeah, yeah, yeah, if you don’t hurry up and get dressed I’m gonna get serious. You were supposed to be at my house twenty minutes ago to eat. I was gonna start without you but your aunt insisted you be there since it’s Easter. Deacon Holmes: Oh, so that’s why you came here. What’s it like to be whipped? CW throws a punch at Holmes who ducks it and both men start laughing. CW walks out of the room as Holmes opens his closet to find something to wear.
