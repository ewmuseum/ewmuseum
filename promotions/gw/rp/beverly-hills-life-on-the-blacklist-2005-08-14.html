Beverly Hills: Life on the Blacklist

14 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 14, 2005 The fairy tale world of Mike Biddle had come to an abrupt end. Both his professional and personal lives were in shambles, shattered by a deft blow from the fickle hand of fate. Not only had he lost his Platinum Championship on his first defense but the new 'Mistress' of the Mat had also embarrassed him during one of his rare moments of anger. There was one person to blame for the downward spiral of both his professional and personal lives: Nicholas Rumson, that fat, curly haired son of a bitch had ruined everything with his threat of placing Mike on the blacklist. Rumson had been responsible for placing many a name upon the dreaded list and once one’s name was placed upon the list one’s social life was over. Mike blamed the distraction that Rumson’s threat had caused him for the embarrassment that he had suffered at Master of the Mat VI; after all, none of those horrible things would have happened under normal circumstances. His addition to the blacklist was recent and Mike hoped that the knowledge of his placement on the list would spread slowly; he’d hate to know that his last social outing was dinner at that bastard Rumson’s home. By an ironic twist of fate Mike’s favorite social event of the season was near, a small but exclusive get together at Charles’s home. The only reason Mike wanted to attend the event was because the women always outnumbered the men, the amount of women made it well worth putting up with Charles’s pathetic attempts at competing with him. Mike’s only hope of attending the event would be if the invitations were sent out before Charles heard of Mike’s predicament. Despite the fact that the party was five hours away and that the invitations always arrived three days prior to the event didn’t stop Mike from hoping that he would soon receive his invitation. He sat in a comfortable armchair located in the center of his library as he waited for his invitation to arrive. It was five o’clock, the time when the mail normally arrived so Mike knew that his wait wouldn’t be much longer. His butler appeared in the doorway that was behind Mike’s back; he cleared his throat in order to catch Mike’s attention. “The mail has arrived sir,” the butler said with his most formal voice. A smile instantly appeared on Mike’s thin lips as he turned to look at the butler, at long last his loophole invitation had arrived! “And what has arrived today?” Mike asked with an excited voice. “Some coupons from Pizza Hut sir,” the butler replied, effectively killing Mike's buzz. Mike’s smile suddenly vanished. “Is that all?” “I’m afraid so sir.” Mike slumped back in his chair as the butler quietly slipped away. The fact that he hadn’t received an invitation could only mean one thing, that it had been lost in the mail. With this revelation Mike reached over to the table beside his chair and grabbed his cell phone. Within in seconds he was connected with Charles. “What the hell do you want?” Charles asked with an annoyed tone. Normally Mike would cuss out anyone that addressed him with that tone of voice, but he was too desperate to notice. “I’m just calling to let you know that I’ll be there tonight Charles,” Mike said with an upbeat tone that did little to disguise his true feelings. The laughter from the other end of the phone was so loud that Mike had to hold the phone a few inches away from his ear in order to avoid going deaf. “You’re a funny guy Mike,” Charles said in between laughs, “that’s why it’s such a shame that you’re blacklisted.” Reality is a harsh mistress, and she really let Mike have it when he learned just how quickly news traveled. “How the hell do you know about that?” Mike asked with a quiet voice. “Rumson told everyone a few days ago. You really are one sick bastard Mike,” Charles replied. “DAMN IT!” Mike screamed as he threw the phone across the room, the object shattered into tiny unrecognizable pieces once it hit the wall. He began to angrily pace up and down the room, his hands folded behind his back, and his head down. There had to be a way out of this situation, if only there were could back up his lie of wrestling being fake...someone that Rumson would actually believe. Mike’s face lit up when he realized that just such a person existed. He exited the library and made his way to his room down the hall to use the home phone. He dialed the number and after a few rings a female voice answered. “Hillary!” Mike exclaimed, “Could you possibly do a small favor for me?” A sigh came from the other end of the line. “No I will not come over and pleasure you,” Hillary said, her tone sounded as if she was in a hurry to get somewhere. “No I need you to talk to Rumson for me,” Mike hastily replied. “Forget it Mike,” Hillary started, “I’m not going to waste my breath explaining whatever lame excuse you’ve come up with to get off the blacklist.” “Oh come on Hillary...can’t you just do it for old times sake?” Mike asked, he suddenly felt very vulnerable. “No,” Hillary said, “I have to go anyway...I have to start getting ready for Charles’s party tonight.” The line went dead and for the first time in his life Mike felt alone. He didn’t know what to do or say to make things return to the way he was accustomed to. He curled up into a ball on the floor and for the first time in his life Mike Biddle cried. ***** Mike cried alone in his room for hours, by the time he snapped out of his trance his eyes were red and his precious blond hair a tangled mess. He looked over at the digital clock on the wall and saw that the glow of the red letters said that it was ten o’clock, which meant that Charles’s party had started an hour ago. A sinking feeling came over Mike as he realized that he would never again get to be a part of the high society of Beverly Hills. “Stupid uptight bastard,” Mike breathed to himself as he got up from the floor. He walked up to the large mirror that rested against his wall and looked at himself, a sigh escaped his lips as he looked at his image on the glass. He reached into his robe pocket and produced the comb that he always carried with him no matter what and quickly fixed his hair. He then wiped away the remainder of his tears. He looked over at the chair next to the mirror and saw the clothes he had picked out for the party, a black polo shirt and a pair of khakis. A Lucy Ricardo look came over his face as he looked at the clothing. “I’m going to that party whether they like it or not,” he said to himself. ***** Charles’s Alpine Drive residence was both large and fashionable, something the ladies really loved. Tonight was a special night for the redheaded man; his house was full of women and a few personal friends. Beside him sat his latest conquest, Mike Biddle’s famed ex-girlfriend, Hillary Watson. An idiotic smile was plastered to his face as he looked around his crowded house. Tonight his happiness didn’t come from his party thought, it came from the fact that he had finally beat Mike. Unknown to Biddle he had actually convinced Rumson that wrestling wasn’t fake and that Biddle had in fact really done all those horrible things on television. With Biddle out of the picture Charles would now be the most popular ladies man in Beverly Hills. What Charles didn’t know was that right outside his home Mike Biddle was about to enter through the front door. What Mike didn’t know was that Charles had set up some security at the front just in case Mike tried to crash the party. Mike approached the door with a fake grin on his face, a grin that quickly faded when he saw that two large security guards stood in front of the door. He nervously approached the two men, fully aware of the cold glares he received from each one. He stopped at the doorstep and nervously looked up at the two large men. “Hey guys,” Mike said with a nervous laugh, “I’m here for the party.” “You’re late,” the man on the left said as he cracked his knuckles. “What’s you’re name?” The man on the right asked as he pulled out a clipboard. “Mike Biddle,” Mike said with a winning smile. “The boss warned us about you,” the man on the left said as he cracked his knuckles. “Warned you about me” Nonsense, Charles and I are close friends,” Mike said as he slowly backed away. “He said you’d say that,” the man on the right said as he pulled out some pepper spray. “What are you going to do with th-AAAAGGHHH!” Mike wailed as the guard sprayed the burning mist into his eyes. The two men then dragged Mike off to the side of the house and began to give him a beating he’d never forget. Meanwhile, everyone was having a grand old time inside without Mike there. Biddle’s life on the blacklist had officially started with a bang. -To Be Continued-
