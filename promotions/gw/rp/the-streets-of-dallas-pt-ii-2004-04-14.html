The Streets of Dallas: Pt. II

14 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 14, 2004 **Every era that the world has ever gone through sees a great man at the forefront. These men might not be the smartest, these men may not be the flamboyant, but something inside their hearts got them to the peak of the mountain. However, there is no such thing as an ever lasting time at the top of the mountain. Some men slide down the other side grace-fully, while others go down it a mess of blood and bodies. What makes a man get to the top in the first place? Some say it may very well be the same thing that ultimately causes their demise** - THE STREETS OF DOWNTOWN DALLAS - .:We last viewed Takahashi Jetsui exiting an alleyway that was the breeding grounds for maggets, and a home for those without a home. Around the corner, we find Takahashi calmy roaming down a street in what appears to bed the more 'redder light' of all the districts that Dallas has. Takahashi struts down the street with almost a superior swagger, as he passes by assortments of people, basking in the neon lights of strip clubs, and hanging out on the front stoops of various establishments such as chinese food restaraunts and run down housing. Suddenly, a baggy-pants man with a Texas Rangers jersey, baggy pants, and a large chain around his neck steps in front of Takahahi's path. The man adjusts his blue bandanna before proceeding to speak:. HOODLUM: Yo, G. I was chillin' here on my steps, when I noticed you freaky-lookin' ass walkin' down sa street. Ya mind tellin' me what wit' da' face-paint dawg? .:Takahashi looks around for a second, as if he was confused. Takahashi then stares the man before him up and down with a disgusted look on his face. Before speaking, Takahashu grabs onto the man's Cross hanging off the long chain dangling from the hoodlum's neck:. TAKAHAHI: Are you a religious man? HOODLUM (Confused look): What'd you say motha' fucka'? Yo, you need a nine busted up side yo head, you face-painted, goofy looking sack of shit? .:The hoodlum reaches for an object (assumed to be a weapon) underneath his jersey, tucked into the belt around his waist. Takahashi then puts his own hand out to stop the man:. TAKAHASHI: I am not walking these streets in search of violence, but if violence is what you want, then violence is what I can bring you. HOODLUM (Another confused look): Yo, I'm not gonna' do dis' shit because I can see your ass ain't from around here. But I'll tell ya what I can do fo' ya ma' fucka'. .:The street hoodlum reaches into is pocket and pulls out a small bottle that appears to be for medicine. The medical container seems to have a couple of white pills in it:. HOODLUM: You eva' heard of dis' shit? It's called opium dawg. It's da' real shit, and I'll give you dis' sample bottle with three pills in it fo' free. You feelin' dat? .:Takahashi takes the bottle from the hoodlum, and as soon as the exchange is maid, the hoodlum quickly retreats into the house that the two men are standing in front of. Takahashi looks at th bottle for a minute, then places it in an inside pocket of the trench coat. Takahashi then proceeds up the street very hastily, as though he had somewhere in particular to be. Takahashi sees a brightly glowing sign just up ahead that says:. BIG - D MOTEL VACANCY .:The half-working motel sign is attached to a very decrepid building. The building has paint peeling from it, as well as a couple pieces of board covering up holes in front of the building. Takahashi wastes little time before entering the motel's rusty, creeky doors. Once inside, Takahashi proceeds to the front desk:. .:The front lobby is very tiny, and is surrounded my nicotine-stained walls. There is a man at the front desk sitting within a fenced in area. The man behind the desk is very sloppy looking wearing a very dirty wife beater, and with coarse, curly hair sticking out from all areas of his body. We can see a slight glimmer reflecting off the man's reasy bald scalp, due to the barely-lasting florescent lighting brightening the lobby:. TAKAHASHI: Excuse me, the sign says VACANCY, this leads me to believe that there are rooms available. Am I correct in this assumption? .:The man at the front desk turns around with a very synical look:. MAN (Strong southern accent): Hey, who in the hell do you think you are coming into MY motel and asking ME questions boy? Huh? TAKAHASHI: Look, I need a room for a night, can I get a room or not? MAN: Why the fuck should I give you a room? You ain't even showed me any cash! .:Takahaashi reaches into his trench coat and pulls out a wad of American bills, which he places onto the counter of the front desk:. TAKAHASHI: Two hundred dollars for a room for one night. .:Through the wiring of the steel fencing surrounding the fat man on the other side, we see a very surprised look on the man's face, as we can clearly see on a billboard in the back that the usual rate for rooms is $35 a night:. MAN: Well that's a migty fine wad of cash you got there sir. I'm thinkin' we can fix sumthin' up for ya here tonight! .:The fat man reaches back into a cubby-hole amongst a series of cubby-holes on the wall, and pulls out a key with a big ed tag attaches. The fat man passes the key through the hold cut in the front wall of the fencing:. MAN: Well there ya' go! You have yerself a mighty fine night. And do you mind me asking what that there facial-painting is all about? TAKAHASHI (Walking away): Go fuck yourself. .:Takahashi walks out of the small lobby, and as he does the fat man picks up a fly swatter and brings in down on a side of the front desk:. WHIP! SMACK! .:Takahashi proceeds up a set of stairs that seems as though it is going to crumble with every step that Takahashi takes:. CREEK! CRACK! CREEK! .:Finally Takahashi reaches the top of the set of stairs. Amongst a set of doors that appear to be barely hanging on their hindges, Takahashi proceeds to room four. Takahashi places the key he received from the fat man at the counter. Once on the other side of the door, we see a disgusting room. The bathroom is a small whole in the wall on the other side of the bed, which appears to be on a wire structure. The bed is very stained and has only a skinny sheet, and a stained pillow. Takahashi removes his trench-coat, exposing a button-up shirt on the inside. Takahashi sits on the bed for a moment, before reaching into his trench coat, and retrieving the mysterious bottle he was given by the hoodlum earlier in the night. Takahashi stares at the bottle for a moment, before removing the cap and tipping out one of the pills. Takahashi stares at the pill for a moment, before putting it in his mouth:. TAKAHASHI: Ugh! Ugh! Ahh! .:Takahashi begins to grab his throat as he rolls onto the bedroom floor. Takahashi rolls around for a moment screaming in agony, before ceasing and attempting to pick himself up. Once Takahashi gets to his feet with the help of the bed, he stumbles and trips again. A very disoriented Takahashi gets up with the help of the bed again, and is able to make his way to the small bathroom. Once inside the bathroom, Takahashi peers at himself in the mirror:. **Colors. Colors. Feelings that are foreign to my body. I can't feel anything. What is happening? You! You are the one who made this happen! You bastard! You bastard!** .:Takahashi rips the mirror of the wall and hurls it into a wall on the other side of the motel room:. TAKAHASHI: Argh! SMASH! .:After the display of violence, the wide pupiled Japanese fighter proceeds to the pile of shattered glass shards. The silver and black face-paint is now dripping off Takahashi's face, as he appears to be experiencing some sort of cold sweats. Once Takahashi reaches the dangerous pile, he falls to his knees in the center of the pile. Takahashi kneels for a minute, before reaching his hand into the pile of shards, instantly drawing blood. Takahashi picks up a particularily large piece of mirror, and brings the piece to his arm. Takahashi begins violently rubbing the piece to and fro ancross his forearm, and as he does this, blood surges from the self-implied wound:. **I hate you. I hate what you have turned me into. I hate what you have made me become. I HATE what you make me do!** TAKAHASHI (ceasing the slicing): Argh! .:Takahashi lays back as his forearm continues to make a violent mess all over the carpet. As a pool develops, Takahashi stares into the stain-filled ceiling, as if he was in his own land, away from concious reality:.
