Just another test

10 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/10/03 'I told you not to get involved Sean. And what did you do? You got involved.' the words are like a knife pearcing the skin of Sean Price. (As the scene fades in we see il Capo sitting comfortably in his chair at the TUE headquarters. Along side Sean Price are stable mates Tyler Pierce and Deacon Holmes, all dressed in black suits the three men stand their proudly and at the same time fearful of il Capo.) 'Sean, do you think I ask too much of you? Because if that is the case than maybe you are not the right man for the job. Maybe you are not capable of performing the tasks that are expected of you as a member of my elite organization. Is that what you are telling me?' remarks a disgusted il Capo. 'No, that is not what I am saying at all.' replies a somewhat angered Sean Price. 'Well Sean, that may not be what your saying but your actions tell me otherwise. Have you ever heard the old saying 'Actions speak louder than words?' Tha t is not just some saying made up by a deaf person. Its the truth Sean. And based on your actions last night you make me think that it was a mistake to bring you in here.' 'Excuse me boss.' Interjects Tyler Pierce 'Go ahead Tyler what have you got to say.' says a now interested il Capo. 'Capo, brining in Sean was probably the smartest thing you did since the formation of TUE. There is no one in this business that I would rather have by my side to fight with me than Sean. Sure he may of done something stupid last night, but when your adrenaline is pumping through your veins and you see a man that you absolutely hate infront of you, your first reaction is to take him out. I can't say that I blame Sean for what he did.' replies a proud Tyler Pierce. 'Well Tyler, im glad that you are so convinced that Sean is the right man for the job and that he is the man you want to fight next to, because remember one thing. When the war starts, its you three that will be fighting....not me....not me! Now if you will excuse me gentlemen I have some other business to take care of. I will talk to you all very soon.' After finishing the sentence il Capo motions towards the office door. Deacon turns first and opens the door. The three men stand there for a moment as they allow the TUE lawyer Preston Banks to walk into the room before they exit. Tyler is the last man out of the room and in doing so shuts the door behind him. At the sound of the door shutting Sean lets out a gasp of air and then begins talking. 'Well that sure was an exhilerating time let me tell you.' remarks an angry but somewhat calmer Price. 'I didn't think it went all that bad personally.' replies Deacon who obviously has a sarcastic tone to what he says. 'Maybe not for you, but you did not get grilled for the last 10 minutes either. Hell all I did was spear the bastard and Capo flips out like i dropped the bomb on Hiroshimo. If that is his reaction to a simple little spear I am almost afraid to see what the man will do when i really flip my top and give someone like Sage the beat down of their lives.' 'Look Sean, what you did was not a big deal. Capo may of grilled you in there but if he was that angry you would not be standing with us right now. I can assure you of that. Capo trusts you and he knows that you are exactly what he needs in this group.' 'He sure does have a warped way of showing it Tyler.' 'Maybe so. But like I said, if what happened was that big of a deal than we would not be having this conversation with you right now.' 'Personally Sean I think Capo was just testing you, to see if you would keep your cool or flip out on him. You have to understand that as much as we may not agree with everything he asks us to do, he have no choice in the matter. We have to do it. That is what we get paid for.' ' I know Dec, and a test it may very well of been, but the fact remains is that I am not one for taking orders. This is still all relatively new to me. As long as Capo does not start making ridiculous requests of me than everything should be fine.' 'Don't worry about it Sean, this is just the begining of a long road of power and prestige. Sure there will be a few small road blocks on the way, but that is to be expected. No one can stop us Sean, you know it, Deacon knows it, I know it and Capo knows it. It is only a matter of time before he gives the word and we do what we were recruited to do.' 'Lets hope you are right Tyler because patience is certianly not one of my virtues.' With a slight laugh in his voice Tyler replies 'Don't we know it.' “Come on Sean, we have to get the gym and train. Now that people know of our existence it will be nothing but an uphill battle from here on out.” “Your right Dec, we do certainly have some hard work cut out for us.” (The three men continue down the hall until the reach the lobby where body guards are waiting for them. The men are escorted out the front door of the building and loaded into a black Escalade. The body guards pile into Escalades that sit infront and behind of the one containing Price, Pierce and Holmes. The scene fades to black as the three SUV’s begin pulling out of the driveway.)
