Brick confronts the VP (Evo RP)

29 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-29-04 Brick stormed down the hallway. In his right hand, he held a piece of paper. Something obviously burned in his mind and he intended to let someone know it. Finally, Brick came to the door he was looking for. The door marked 'Vice President, Global Wrestling'. Brick slammed the door open and barged in, shouting 'Aight, mother--!!' Before Brick could finish, he was met by a bright light shining behind the V.P. so that all one could see of him was a silhouette. 'Aah, Brick,' said the VP in a distorted voice. 'To what do I owe the honor of your visit?' 'Cut the bullshit! You know why I'm here!' 'Do I?' the VP said, amusedly. 'Yes, you do!' Brick then held up the sheet of paper in his right hand. 'I'm sorry, I can't read that from here.' 'You know damn well what this is! It's the card for this coming Evolution. But one match in particular has me pissed off, and I think you know which one.' 'And that would be?' the VP asked, facetiously. Brick then pointed to the bottom of the page, which was highlighted with marker. By Order of the VP Donny McNasty v. Brick As if Dark Rituals wasn't enough for these friends, Brick with a bare victory in the Graveyard Match against Namenlos-Duisternis and McNasty having partook in one of GW's most gruesome matches ever, now these two friends must face off in the octagon as per demand from Global's mysterious VP. Why is this match mandatory and why pit two friends against one another? To hell if this reporter knows. 'I don't understand why this is a problem, Brick. You and McNasty have already had a match against each other for the Platinum title.' 'That was different. It was a three-way dance with Pendragon. A lot o' the times, Donny and I didn't have to face each other.' 'Well, now you won't have Pendragon to get in the way. 'Lemme tell you somethin', right now. First of all, if you're tryin' to pull some 'divide and conquer' shit with me an' Donny, it ain't gonna work. The problem I have with this match is that neither of us is a hundred percent, especially not Donny after what that motherfucker Hessian did to him! So, neither of us could go full out, even if we wanted to!' 'It sounds to me like you're making excuses, Brick.' 'If anything, the match you should have booked is Brick vs. Hessian! I owe that freak an ass-whipping and then some!' 'Hessian already has a match on the 1st. I wonder how McNasty would feel if he knew you were trying to weasel out of this match.' 'I ain't tryin' to weasel outta shit!' 'You obviously weren't watching the Hell's Arnory match close enough, Brick. Your friend finished that match with a possibly broken right hand. And since you and McNasty are such good friends, then answer me this question: where were you when Hessian was beating your bosom buddy to within an inch of his life?' Brick clenched his fists. The only thing that kept him from punching the mysterious Vice President in his face was the fact that he couldn't see the VP's face. However, he was starting to care less and less, with each passing second. 'You better pray long and hard I never find out who the fuck you are,' growled Brick through gritted teeth. 'Because the day that happens, I am going to fuck...you...up.' 'Let me explain something to you, Brick,' said the VP, his voice now devoid of mirth. 'I am not Jason Ayers. I am not Eric Lansheer. Your little 'angry Black man' routine won't work on me. And I will not tolerate these threats. Now, this match is mandatory, as stated in that card. You and McNasty will compete in this match Monday night and there will be a winner, and if either of you gets the bright idea of throwing this match in any way, I will...' 'You'll do what? Fire us? Maybe you can fire us, ok? Maybe you do have some pull, but Bubba owns the damn company. He's one of the only three cats who can really decide whether I stay or go.' 'Really?' 'Really.' 'And exactly who would the other two 'cats' be?' 'You're lookin' at one of 'em right now, motherfucker! An' Donny's the other! You mention that you're not Ayers or Lansheer. Well, you know what? You have the same fucking death wish they did, apparently. You won't be hidin' behind this office an' that damn light forever.' Never taking his eyes off the silhouette of the Vice President, Brick began to back out of the office. 'Oh, and thank you ever so much for your time.' If there were still to be another person in the room, they probably wouldn't have been able to see the rage forming on the VP's face. But, visible or not, it was there.
