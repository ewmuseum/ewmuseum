Date: 8/14/2005 I can't remember when you weren't there, When I didn't care for anyone but you. I swear, we've been through everything there is, Can't imagine anything we've missed... Can't imagine anything the two of us can't do! February 11th, 1969; 1944 hours Lupe Juarez's apartment, Mexico City The song, 'Cumplea’os Feliz,' or Happy Birthday to You in Spanish, was sang heartily by everyone celebrating the birthday of both Lupe Juarez and her steady boyfriend, Mateo Ramirez. Even Mateo and Lupe themselves, the birthday-boy and birthday-girl, sang along to wish the same well-wishes on each other. Lupe's parents and Mateo's parents had become fast friends, watching their children's love for each other blossom. This was their 22nd birthday, and they still held hands and smiled at each other just like teenagers with their first crush... though their true feelings for each other was like soul mates. Mateo and Lupe had been inseparable for over a year now. Even on their first date, back in June of 1967, there was a noticeable glow on Lupe's face when Mateo brought her back home. Lupe's mother wiped a tear away from her cheek remembering that look... the same look she saw on her daughter's face right now. Mateo's mother had baked a beautiful angel food cake trisected by two layers of luscious raspberry icing and a thin but generous layer of whipped cream on top. Everybody attending the party giggled as they watched the birthday-girl and birthday-boy lovingly feed each other a bite of the cake from their plates. Encouraged by the light giggle, they did it again... and then again, until they'd each fed each other the whole of their pieces of the cake. Several flashbulbs went off as they fed each other the last bites of their cakes; their fathers, uncles, and best friends taking snapshots for posterity. The looks on both Mateo and Lupe's faces were priceless. Lupe's father was an amateur musician, although he played the mandolin better than quite a few professionals. He and a few friends in attendance at the party formed a small band that provided the night's musical entertainment. Mateo and Lupe were given the honors of the first dance, and flashbulbs went off again as they leaned in to share a tender kiss. They'd shared enough dances by now that they knew each other's styles by heart; each other's favorite steps and moves, and had blended them together in a beautiful pattern of motion and grace. The mood was quite infectious, and soon everybody was dancing together to the lively songs played by the band. Through the years, You've never let me down, You turned my life around! The sweetest days I've found, I've found with you... It was fairly late in the evening when birthday gifts were finally opened. Among the presents were an exquisite dress sewn by Lupe's aunt, who was a seamstress; a sterling silver wristwatch given to Mateo by Lupe's father; an adorable Chihuahua for Lupe given by Mateo's father, which he'd kept hidden at the next-door neighbor's apartment; and a polished brown bowling ball for Mateo, given by Lupe, which had a deep red heart over where the finger-holes were; a monogram of 'L&M' in stylish calligraphy along the top of the heart. Mateo dabbled a bit in bowling, and occasionally bowled with a few friends. Mateo had said his birthday present for Lupe was perhaps the best of all, and thus he wished to save it for last. Lupe had an impish smile on her face when it was finally time for him to give her the gift. But warm smiles and giddy heartbeats all turned bewildered and still as Lupe unwrapped a well-made, but still plain, china doll of a woman with long dark hair. Lupe managed to keep a gracious smile on her face as she showed the doll to everyone, but the rest of the guests were baffled, and not overly pleased. After all the affection Mateo had showered on her, after all the dinners he'd taken her to, after the other gifts he'd given to her-- this doll clearly paled by comparison. The only thing of note was a somewhat largish gold-colored bracelet around the doll's wrist. 'That looks less like a bracelet and more like a ring,' said Lupe's mother curiously. 'And what's that stone set into... it...?' Lupe slid the bracelet off the doll's wrist and saw that it was indeed a ring... a beautiful gold ring with a masterfully cut diamond set into it. The diamond sparkled under the ceiling light as the facets were turned. It looked very much like... Through the years, I've never been afraid, I've loved the life we've made! And I'm so glad I've stayed Right here with you... Through the years. Everybody gasped as one suddenly, and the hairs on the back of Lupe's neck stood up. She'd felt the sofa shift, and instinctively looked over. Mateo had risen up off of the sofa and taken one knee in front of her. He carefully reached over and clasped her left hand, in which she still held the ring. 'My dearest Lupe... how do you give a birthday gift to someone so beautiful, so warm and wonderful a person?' said Mateo, in the voice full of gallantry she remembered from when they met... the smile that just melted her heart. 'What can you give to a lady to whom you'd given everything else you were able? It was the most difficult question I could pose to myself... and I found the answer at Church two Sundays ago. I've given you of myself for so long... and yet there was one thing remaining. And so, on your birthday... OUR birthday... I give you my full heart. My full soul. I give you, me. Dearest Lupita-- will you marry me?' All the sniffles around the room as everyone wiped away tears of happiness, easily paled against the sobs of pure joy and delight that Lupe gushed freely, throwing her arms around her beloved. She was barely coherent enough to answer Mateo. 'Yes... oh, yes...! I will marry you!' Mateo carefully took the ring from her hand and slid it onto her finger; a perfect fit. Rising to his feet, he lifted her up to her own, and kissed her. A lingering kiss of the purest love she could imagine. A rousing cheer and round of applause followed, as Lupe's father walked up and embraced Mateo heartily. 'As your parents gain a daughter... I gain a son,' he said proudly. 'And a finer one, I could not ask.' 'The honor is mine... pap?,' said Mateo, and Lupe smiled. I can't remember what I used to do, Who I trusted, whom I listened to before... I swear you've taught me everything I know, Can't imagine needing someone so... But through the years it seems to me I need you more and more! August 10, 2005; 1056 hours GW Commissary; Las Vegas Noi Tsunami understood how the story brought tears of happiness to the masked Luchadores telling it to her... she had to wipe a few away, herself. Though as she watched Teresa sniffle quietly with Juan's arm around her; Juan wiping at his eyes with the back of his hand... she knew that there were tears of sorrow, as well. Much of the story around how Teresa's parents met, fell in love, and married, the tag-team champions told as a way of explaining the bitter animosity that Teresa and her sister still felt today for their mother... how their love for her turned sour and how Lupe had lost the family she should have held so dear. Noi only wished she were able to meet Lupe and ask her why she left Mateo for her second husband, just when it seemed Mateo would have needed her the most. In a previous interview, Noi had pointed out that if Lupe hadn't done this, and the family ended up in the squatter settlements, Juan and Teresa would never have met, and Melina would never have been born. But as Juan and Teresa had pointed out later in a cafeteria chat that Noi agreed not to publish, there was no way Lupe could have known that when she married her second husband and moved into his mansion. A man who bore no real love for her, and whose displays of affection were like wood. 'Those who do not remember the past are doomed to repeat it,' thought Noi to herself. 'But in this case, it seems that even those who DO remember the past, end up repeating it, too.' She'd noticed it very quickly; soon as Teresa arrived at the station, about five minutes after Juan. And due to this interview being in the GW Commissary's TV station for an Evolution pre-show-- the first time Herencia had done such an interview-- Noi knew... hoped... that the audience would notice as well. Teresa had kissed Juan hello when she arrived... and it was almost perfunctory. It looked wooden. The way they hugged... it looked wooden. The way they were holding hands now... looked wooden. It all looked like they were simply going through the motions for the sake of the audience... 'But is the audience that blind?' Noi thought to herself. A closing comment from Juan, about the story he and Teresa had been telling about Mateo and Lupe's engagement, brought Noi's attention back to the here and now. 'I can't say I ever came to know or feel the same about Lupe, as Teresa and her sister did,' he said. 'I never loved her in the same manner... nor came to detest her in the same manner. But she and Mateo were a match made in heaven, and I cannot fathom how or why she gave it up for a match... made in hell, it seems.' 'Even if she couldn't have known what would have become of her life with her second husband... and yours and your sister's lives, Teresa,' said Noi, 'she might have felt that it would have been better than what would have become of the three of you, had you found yourselves living in a cardboard shack on a patch of mud, begging for food and clean water.' 'She ASSUMED that,' growled Teresa, 'she didn't know. My sister and I had been able to obtain work after we moved out of our stepfather's mansion, using none of his money or his influence. We could have done the same if we had to walk from the squatter settlements. My parents had a relationship built on love and trust... they not only loved each other, but were IN LOVE with each other... and they carried it down through the years. Twenty, to be exact.' She clasped Juan's hand, and he squeezed it, for mutual support. 'The way Teresa and I have carried our union down through the years,' said Juan. 'Twelve and counting.' Through the years, Through all the good and bad, I knew how much we had! I've always been so glad To be with you, But Noi wasn't fooled. She saw Teresa's hand suddenly go slack in Juan's grasp; saw her arm go almost limp. And if she could see it... 'How many fans who will be watching them on television, will see it as well?' 'Maybe your mother did assume,' said Noi, trying to play devil's advocate in order to delve deeper into Teresa's mind. 'Maybe she made a judgment call on making sure you and your sister had a future. Parents don't always make the right decisions, Teresa... remember the tutor you hired for Melina, who never taught her to read?' Teresa blinked hard at the blow, and her teeth were bared at the reporter as she drew in a ragged breath. 'That was a mistake I took great pains to correct,' she hissed. 'And you have seen the progress we've made. My mother never tried to correct her mistake. She made her bed in a moral pigsty and has wallowed in it ever since.' 'Did you give her a chance?' Noi challenged. 'Are you so sure she never did anything while married to her second husband that helped Mateo in some manner?' Juan scoffed. 'All she did was pay respects at his funeral,' he said. 'Like Mateo would have cared then that she might have regretted his suicide. My sister-in-law gave Lupe what for... she put her mother in her place. That man was Lupe's husband only on a piece of paper. She was Mateo's wife in soul and spirit-- at least I hope and pray she still was when she said that prayer over his grave. To the day he put that gun underneath his jaw and pulled the trigger, Mateo's heart and soul belonged to Lupe. The way mine belong to Teresa.' Juan put an arm around his wife again, and she reached up to put her hand to his. Noi smiled at them and took a brief second to glance at the camera. 'Old-fashioned family values, from back in the days when coupled stayed with each other through thick and thin, for better or for worse,' she mused, looking at the lens, before turning back to Juan and Teresa as Juan gave a grunt of agreement. Suddenly Noi's eyes narrowed as she looked at the tag-team champions. 'But is staying together really all there is to it?' Through the years, It's better every day. You've kissed my tears away! As long as it's okay, I'll stay with you... Through the years! It was the clear glass pane separating the television booth from the rest of the station that had defeated the masked Luchadores. Noi had turned to face the camera, but her eyes glanced toward the pane just in time to see what was reflected in it. She clearly spotted Teresa, when she reached up to put her hand to Juan's, gently pull it down from around her shoulders. She saw Teresa dip her head just as Juan leaned in to kiss her, so that his kiss scored her temple. And she saw Juan take it in perfect stride without flinching, as though it was all perfectly normal to him. 'Enough is enough!' 'For better or for worse means more than being there for one another,' said Noi to Juan and Teresa. 'You've carried more than just love for Mateo down through the years... you've carried a lot of bitterness, and anger as well. You've sided so unilaterally with Mateo over Lupe that the thought of forgiveness isn't even in your hearts, let alone your minds... you tell a beautiful and heartwarming story of their courtship as a way of condemning Lupe for the split-up. Can't you see what it's doing to your own relationship as well?' Silence. The tag-team co-champions stared a hole right through Noi's body; brown eyes unblinking through the eyeholes in their starkly contrasting masks. Noi looked back at them unafraid. It was time for the gloves to come off here; this was no pencil-and-paper information gathering session for an article to be printed in a magazine or webzine. A picture was worth a thousand words; a person's eyes could deceive them, but a person's heart could not. Everyone who would be watching this television segment would see with their hearts as well as their eyes. 'Our relationship is quite healthy, thank you very much,' Juan finally broke the icy silence with icy words. 'Fight fire with fire, the saying goes, so why not fight ice with ice?' Noi absorbed the counterattack quickly and met with a simple raised eyebrow. Juan rolled his eyes. 'You think because I support Teresa's condemnation of her mother for leaving Mateo, that I don't love Teresa the way her parents loved each other? Isn't supporting my wife's feelings the very thing I'm supposed to do as her husband?' 'Are you just supporting your wife, though, Juan?' Noi challenged. 'Or have you come to feel the same way about it all, yourself?' 'If you're asking whether I think Lupe's leaving Mateo was WRONG, then you're damned right I do,' the Crimson Comet snapped. 'If you're asking whether I condone my sister-in-law smacking her mother across the face for showing up at Mateo's funeral on the arm of a man that Teresa, her sister, and I all agreed was more her pimp than her husband, then you'd better believe I do that as well. I have devoted myself to my wife's cause, but I have my convictions, Ms. Tsunami. My sister-in-law was the true stereotype of what American culture affectionately labels, 'Daddy's girl.' She'd been pushed too hard, watched her mother cross a line she wasn't going to let her cross.' Noi laid a finger vertically along her cheek; the fingertip near the top of her ear. 'Teresa told you all about that?' she asked. 'Your memory of the incident is near eidetic.' 'I was THERE!' Juan shouted in exasperation. He put a hand to his forehead and let out a slow sigh. 'Madre de Dios, do I have to prove my loyalty and love for my own WIFE to you? Teresa's vouched for it... isn't that enough?' 'Oh yes, Teresa's vouched for it, all right... over and over, right in front of me. That's exactly WHY I'm asking you to prove it.' Noi seemed to perk up at Juan's challenge. 'You suggested that, I didn't,' she said. 'So as long as you made the offer... why not put your money where your mouth is, Juan?' Juan's nostrils flared wide. 'What do you want from me, then?' Through the years, When everything went wrong, Together we were strong! I know that I belonged Right here with you... 'Teresa's your wife, so you should find no shame in having this requested of you,' said Noi. 'Kiss her... the way you did on your wedding day. Here, on camera.' Teresa drew a sharp inward breath that sounded like a hiss. 'So now we must do tricks for the audience like trained chimpanzees?' she demanded indignantly. 'Like we give a DAMN how much they might clamor for that kiss? This is GW, not Cirque de Soleil.' 'That's Cirque DU Soleil,' corrected Noi. 'And no, GW isn't that... but since wrestlers have to be adept in front of a microphone and behind a camera... think of it as soap opera. How many actors and actresses regularly kiss as husband and wife when only their characters are married, because the script calls for it? You have an edge... you really are married.' 'And THAT will prove to you that I love my wife?' Juan's voice cracked with a sarcasm that showed he wouldn't believe that if his and Melina's lives depended on it. 'Loving your wife is about more than telling her you love her,' Noi retorted. 'Why not show her... in front of all your fans? Why not show them, instead of telling them?' Slowly Juan's breathing started to slow. 'He knows he can't argue against that logic,' Noi knew. The Crimson Comet slowly turned to face Teresa; his eyes looking deep into hers. Do’a Teresa looked back at him and trembled. Her lips pressed tightly together, almost as if to keep his out. Very slowly, very gently, Juan reached out a hand. His fingers brushed Teresa's eyelids and eased them closed. Teresa didn't resist, and she didn't open her eyes again. 'Think about that night,' Juan said. 'See it in your mind.' Juan's hand lay along Teresa's right cheek, steadying her face. Their lips met and pressed together as both husband and wife held their breath. Their mouths relaxed slightly, though their cheeks didn't puff up or twitch. Juan's head eased ever so slightly to one side, then the other. Teresa's eyes opened as Juan slowly pulled back. Her body trembled again, and as she looked at Juan, a plump teardrop slid from each of her eyes, rolling onto the royal blue fabric of her mask. The tracks darkened the smooth material as the tears rolled off of it and onto the bottom parts of her cheeks... finally falling from her jaw line to tap silently on the floor. Through the years, I never had a doubt, We'd always work things out! I've learned what love's about... By loving you Through the years! 'Are you satisfied now...?' Teresa demanded of Noi, turning to glower at her. Noi put her hands up in front of her chest. 'I'm not your enemy, Teresa, whatever you think of me,' she said carefully and respectfully. 'I'm here to dig down deep to the heart of your whole back-story. I'm not here to fight you. You'll get all the fight you care for at Evolution, from Absolutely Merciless.' 'And so will they,' said Juan. 'Now that we've fulfilled the legacy we set out to fulfill, we're not about to let it go so quickly. These championship belts are the vindication we've sought to provide El Zorro Azul with, for many years. We honored his memory the night we won these. We will honor him with all challengers we fight. Anyone who wants these belts, you will have to do what Teresa and I did in order to get them... and that's TAKE them.' Teresa took her husband's hand as they displayed their championship belts. 'Whether Michael and Mindy Mercer, or Josh and Gray Brand, or Ken Partridge and Bill Moresi, or Shadow and Vivica Valentine... challenge Juan and I when you see fit, and we'll be there to meet you. There's always someone waiting in line to knock us off the top; but we're always standing with our feet firm ready to knock you back. These belts mean more than just a championship to us, and we won't let them go so easily. Absolutely Merciless gets their shot this Evolution... they'd best bring a good, stout, solid crowbar if they hope to pry these from our fingers.' 'And now when they join hands, you can see the conviction in the gesture,' Noi thought to herself. 'Providing mutual strength as a tag-team, that they're good at, I have to give them. If only they could provide each other what they're supposed to... away from the Octagon.' 'You heard it from the champions themselves... La Herencia de Herederos, set to defend for the first time at Evolution in Las Vegas,' said Noi, smiling at the camera. 'Thanks for tuning in, and be sure to stay tuned for an analysis of the tournament at Master of the Mat.' Through the years, You've never let me down, You've turned my life around! The sweetest days I've found I've found with you... Soon as they were out of the studio, Teresa grabbed her new cell phone and tapped the keypad to dial a number. 'Hi Maggie, it's me. I'll be a little late picking up Melina... are you sure it's no trouble? I'll pay you back for the pizza.' She paused and then chuckled. 'Then I'll take you and your kids to one myself, next time. Yes... thank you. I'll see you in about an hour.' As Noi watched, Juan approached Teresa, only to halt in his tracks as her head snapped over her shoulder to face him. The two masked Luchadores looked at each other for a few seconds before Juan turned on his heel and walked out. Teresa handed Noi a long look before following Juan out. Her lips barely moved as she delivered a parting comment. 'Dig too deep for crude oil... and you just might strike a gusher that comes right back at your face.'
