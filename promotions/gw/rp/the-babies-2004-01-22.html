The babies

22 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/22/04 The time: 7:35 p.m. It was a rare occasion when Brick and his family got together for dinner, except on Sundays. It was a week or so after Reload. Brick was still angry about the events of the evening. Not so much losing to Salaok and Frost, but what happened afterwards. 'So, who are you and Donny facing on Evolution?' Kyle asked as Monica passed him the bowl filled with collard greens. 'The Connection,' Brick replied. 'Josh Curtis and Zack Johnson.' 'Those gay guys?' 'I could care less about them being gay. The fact is these guys are rising in the ranks. Plus, they managed to beat Salaok and Frost in one of their first matches.' 'You're not still upset because you lost to them at Reload, are you?' 'No, that's not what really has me upset.' 'It's what happened afterwards.' 'After that, I called Aaron and Felicia to check. Fortunately, Ashley was fine, but they're not exactly happy that Salaok could get someone in their house.' 'Yeah, that stunt with the baby stroller was sick,' replied Monica. 'If he ever did something like that with our babies...' 'Hey, don't think like that. You don't need that kinda stress, right now. Let me worry about him.' 'What are you going to do?' 'I promised Dad when I was sixteen that I'd look out for this family, whatever it takes. And I do mean whatever.' 'Wait, are you suggesting...' 'Yes, I am.' 'Jamal, as an officer of the court, I feel compelled to tell you this could constitute conspiracy...' 'I don't give a fuck what it may constitute!!' roared Brick. 'Language.' 'Sorry, Ma, but that's the way I feel.' 'Still, cold-blooded murder? I wouldn't expect that from you.' 'If a mad dog is about to bite your child, you put it down. Salaok is a mad dog I should have put down years ago.' 'Jamal,' said Coretta, placing a hand on Brick's to calm him down, 'I can understand you wanting to protect the family, but I will not have you throw your life away over that piece of trash.' Suddenly, Monica gasped and then started moaning. 'Monica? Baby, what is it?' Kyle asked. 'I think this is it.' 'The twins? They're coming?' Monica responded with a quick nod and a whispered 'Uh-huh'. Right away, everyone except Monica rose out of their seats. Brick and Kyle slowly walked Monica over to the couch. 'Jamal, I need you to time the contractions. I'm gonna call Monica's doctor.' Kyle frantically dialed a number 'Hello, Dr. Sutton? It's Kyle Fontaigne. Monica's going into labor.' 'OK, honey, just breathe,' said Coretta, holding Monica's hand as she breathes. 'How far apart are the contractions?' 'Ten minutes,' Brick replied, looking at his watch. 'They're ten minutes apart. I should get her to the hospital as soon as possible? Right. We'll be there as soon as possible. OK.' Kyle then hung up the phone. 'We gotta get her to the hospital now!' 'I'll go start my car.' 'Brick, we can't take her in your Hummer.' 'Why not?' 'Do you really want to have to lift your pregnant sister up into that thing?' 'You're right. Gimme the keys to your Lexus.' Kyle tossed him the keys and Brick headed out the door. Kyle and Coretta helped Monica to her feet and slowly led her out the door. ---- The time: 7:34 p.m., the next evening. Monica Jeanine Randall-Fontaigne gave birth to a twin boy and girl, Randall James and Mary Alice. Both babies weighed in at 8 lbs. Brick, Coretta and others came to see the babies for the first time. 'They're so beautiful,' said Coretta. 'See there, Ashley?' asked Aaron. 'Those are gonna be your new playmates.' 'Come on, folks,' said Kyle, 'visiting hours are almost over.' 'Jamal?' 'Yeah, I'll be there in a minute.' Brick stood alone, leaning against the glass. 'Welcome to the family. You two won't understand this for a long time, but this is not a nice world that you came into. People can be mean and cruel and they can hurt you for no reason but that it makes them happy to see other people hurt. They won't care if you're a man or a woman. They won't care if you're a grown-up or a kid. As long as you or someone who loves you is in pain, they'll be happy. I do not intend to let that happen. I will do whatever it takes to keep you and everybody else safe. And if I fail, I will make whoever hurt you pay. That's a promise.' 'Sir,' said a nurse, 'visiting hours are over.' 'OK. Thank you.' Brick then began to walk away with even more determination in his heart than usual. If his conflict with Salaok wasn't personal before... It is now.
