The meeting at midnight

19 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/19/05 'There must be no majority decisions, but only responsible persons, and the word 'council' must be restored to its original meaning. Surely every man will have advisers by his side, but the decision will be made by one creature.” Soma stood looking out of the window as the moon shown down on his naked body. The light making shadows on the wall, creating monsters that were not truly there. He slowly began to bind his hair, pulling it out of his face letting his hardened features show that more clearly. The act had almost become habit as he did it before he went out each night. He called it “getting ready for battle”. Most would call it a crazy infatuation with one’s hair. The cloth he used to bind his hair with was black silk, bathed in the blood of those who have fought against him. He wore it as a symbol. A symbol that said “I am here for eternity.” He knew they were after him now, the vampyre council. The council he founded. His council took the name Rex Deus, The Dragon Sovereignty. The name was suppose to remind his race of times when they were reverenced and respected instead of fear and being hunted. Now in Global Wrestling he had found those who would be worthy of the title and help him gain the power he once had known. He was a king and he wanted respect. “You were always one for tradition Leinad.” The voice came from the shadows of the room. “Tradition is the only thing that I have. Many have forgotten all we stood for. I will make them remember once again.” “Even if that means you have to kill off your race?” “Since when did you care for others” You have always thought of yourself Lilith. What has changed that?” The woman appeared with a small grin on her face. She was as lovely as ever. The same dark hair and piercing violet eyes and her pale skin glistened in the moonlight. She was wearing a black dress and black boots. Nothing to fashionable but she seemed to have a sophistication about her that Soma had not seen in his race in many years. “Are you afraid I will kill you Lilith” You should have no fear, you aren’t really of my kind.” Soma watched as his words seemed to get under her skin. He smiled at her sorrow. It had been a millennia since he had seen her and the last time was not on good terms. “Those words hurt me Leinad. I may not be of your kind but I am mother to the oldest of your species.” “Not me.” “No”.we all know you are the first and I was the first to have a child with your blood. I’m here as a concerned mother. You are killing off the ancient line and trying to start again with a Cainite. Do you really think her blood even if it is mixed with yours will bring about the child of oblivion?” Soma smiled as he still looked at the moon. “I believe through her and those I have chosen will be those who will purge this world of its impurity. Lia is strong. She is viscous. She is the only vampire to ever best me in combat. That is a claim no other of her species can make. She is the new dark mother”.or she will be. Feeling threatened?” There was a brief pause. “She is not me. And she will never be me.” “No she is not. She is superior to you.” “The council comes for you”.and her. What will you do when they find you?” “I will do what I have always done. I will survive.” “And the Cainite?” “She will adapt and be a survivor as well.” Lilith walked over to Soma putting a hand on his chest, tracing the dragon tattoo with her finger. He grabbed her hand and pushed it away. “I ask you as a unique being like yourself’Don’t kill my children.” “I can not give you the answer you require.” She stood there a moment not knowing what to do. She had been rejected. And now her children would die at the hands of their father. “I will do what I must Leinad, if that means turning your own against you.” “And I will do what I must, if that means eating every heart of the council. I killed my own son in the sake of change. I know that I’ve become a monster. I have done what is necessary for change. And I will continue to do what I must. Leave now or become the product of change.” Lilith looked on Soma with hatred and loathing in her eyes. She would kill him but not that night. She would swear to herself that she would not let her children die. She left as swiftly as she came. Soma now knew that the council was truly after him. The war was beginning and the sides had been chosen.
