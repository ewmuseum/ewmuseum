The Plot Continues

14 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 14, 2005 The Los Angeles office of Richard Wood was a tall and dark building that towered high above the busy city streets. The building peaked high above the city’s infamous smog; if one were to stand at the top of the building the odds of actually seeing the ground were slim due to the amount of smog that blanketed the skyline. Next to the office building that served as Wood’s west coast headquarters was a parking garage. A long black limousine pulled into the garage and drove to the top level. The garage was one-half the size of the neighboring skyscraper. Once the limousine was parked in the spot closest to the door, the young and portly driver exited the long vehicle and rushed towards the backdoor. The driver pulled the door open with his pudgy fingers and out stepped his passenger. The passenger was a young man in his mid-twenties. He wore a pair of khakis and a green Hawaiian style button up shirt. His perfectly styled blonde hair was parted to the left and part of his hair fell over his left eye. There was an air of arrogance about this man but that was to be expected since he was none other than Beverly Hills socialite Mike Biddle. Those that were acquainted with recent events in Mike’s life would automatically notice that the brash young man was noticeably calmer and that his classic cockiness had been restored. Mike looked over at the driver and noticed that the fat man had his right hand extended in expectation of a tip. Mike looked at the driver with his dark blue eyes and let out a short chuckle as he turned to walk away. He paused when he heard the man stammer out, “E-excuse me s-sir.” Biddle turned and glared at the man. “What?” He asked with a great deal of irritation in his voice, if there was one thing Mike hated more than anything else it was making small talk with those that were beneath him. “A-all customers are to give the driver a monetary tip once they safely reach their destination,” the portly driver said with a bit of a stutter. Mike rolled his dark blue eyes with great annoyance. “You expect me to give you money,” Mike stated with a bitter tone. “W-well i-it is c-company policy sir,” the driver said with a nervous smile. “Why should I give you money” You’ll waste it all on Krispy Kreme and make yourself fatter than you already are!” Mike said as he pointed a finger at the other man’s gut that hung over his belt. The driver simply stared at Mike with his mouth agape, never before had a customer treated him with such disrespect. “That’s not true,” the driver defensively said. “Yes it is,” Mike replied, “you didn’t really think that I wouldn’t notice the donut crumbs all over your uniform did you?” The driver looked down at his uniform and frowned when he realized that Biddle was right. Mike smirked when he saw the look of defeat on the fat man’s face. Mike then said, “I’m doing you a favor by not giving you money. Now you wait here while I’m in my meeting.” Mike then exited the parking garage by way of the door that led to the mid-level lobby of the skyscraper. As the driver watched Biddle walk off his face became red with anger, the driver then mumbled a few things and got back in the vehicle with a slam of the door. “Wait for you my ass. Enjoy your walk home asshole,” the driver said as he drove off. If Mike knew that his only ride back to Beverly Hills had left he’d be furious. Fortunately for him he was already in the building when the driver ditched him for Krispy Kreme. When Mike entered the building the first room that he came across was a large lobby that was empty except for the large silver desk that stood between him and the beautiful brunette that served as one of Wood’s receptionists. Biddle leaned against the cold steel desk and flashed a winning smile at the beautiful woman that sat before him. “Can I help you sir?” She asked with a soft, sweet voice and a helpful smile. “Yes you can my dear,” Mike replied with a wink, “but first I need to speak with Dr. Wood.” The receptionist blushed slightly as she looked down at her hands. “Do you have an appointment sir?” She asked in a quiet voice. “Of course I do,” Mike said, “it should be under Mike Biddle.” At the mention of his name the receptionist’s face lit up. She looked up at him with wide eyes and pointed her finger at him and said, “You’re that guy that married “The Gold Digger” from the paper!” Mike buried his face in hands and wondered when that series of newspaper stories would stop haunting him. He then looked up and stared into the girl’s brownie colored eyes and said, “There was a ceremony but we didn’t actually get married,” he paused, “it’s a very long story.” “Oh,” the girl said with wide-eyed wonder. “Now about my appointment?” Mike said. “Oh,” she said as she snapped out of her short trance, “just take that elevator to the top floor.” She said as she pointed her right index finger at an elevator door on the opposite side of the hallway. Mike thanked her for her help before he made his way to the elevator. Once inside the elevator he inspected the buttons and pressed the one marked “Top Floor”. The elevator sprang to life and began it’s ascension to the top floor. Moments later the doors parted with a “ding” and Mike stepped into a room that looked as if it were carved out of solid oak. “How may I help you sir?” asked the middle-aged woman that sat behind a large oak desk. “I’m here to see Mr. Wood,” Mike said as he approached the desk. “Your name?” she asked with a flat monotone. “Biddle, Mike Biddle,” he replied. “Dr. Wood is taking a phone call at the moment; I’ll let him know that you are here Mr. Biddle. Please have a seat while you wait.” She pointed to the black leather sofa that rested against the wall near the elevator. The woman left the room and entered the large oak doors that led to Wood’s office. As Mike sat alone he went over the events that had led him not only to Wood's office but to the stipulations of his Wrestlecade match. Wood had personally contacted Mike with a business proposition, all Mike had to do was keep an eye on one Adam Myers and in exchange Wood would allow Mike’s company, Biddle Corp, to use some of the technology of Myers’s suit to further Biddle Corp's goal of creating a light weight protective suit for firefighters. Naturally, Mike agreed to the deal but quickly found himself harassed by Myers as to the whereabouts of his family, who had been kidnapped by Wood and his men. Today marked the first time that Mike would meet face-to-face with Wood. He had known of Wood for quite some time but the two traveled in different social circles and had never met. In fact, the two probably would never have met if Mike hadn’t agreed to Myers’s stipulations. Wood feared that if Biddle failed that he’d lose everything that he had worked for. A few minutes later the receptionist returned to the room. “Dr. Wood is ready to see you now sir,” she said with her monotone. Mike rose from his seat on the leather couch and crossed the room to the two large wooden doors that led to Wood’s office. He grasped one brass door handle in his right hand and opened the door; he stepped into the room and allowed the door to slam shut behind him. Wood’s office was a large room decorated much like the waiting room. The only difference was that there was a large window behind Wood’s desk that looked out over the smoggy skyline of Los Angeles. Wood sat in a black leather office chair behind a wooden desk that spanned the width of the room. Wood was older than Biddle by more than a few years; this was evident by his thinning hair. Wood wore a black business suit and a scowl. Mike responded to the scowl with a smirk. To Wood he said, “Hey Dick! I can call you Dick, right?” “No,” Wood replied with an angry tone. Mike sat down on one of the three chairs that rested in front of Wood’s desk. Mike looked over at Wood and said, “What is it that was so important you needed to see me in person Dick?” Mike asked with a grin on his face. Wood’s brow narrowed with anger and he began to wonder why he had even considered going into business with Biddle in the first place. “I called you here because I have a few things to discuss with you,” Wood stated. “What sort of things?” Mike asked as he crossed his right leg over his left. “I want to know why you would endanger the program by agreeing to the subject’s stipulations,” Wood said with an annoyed voice. Biddle let a small snicker escape his lips as he listened to Wood’s inquiry. “I’m not endangering your program Dick,” Mike replied, “I’m just making sure that Bubble Boy leaves me alone after I win the Platinum Title.” “What if you don’t win?” Wood allowed some of his frustration to escape when he asked the question. “Myers is more than capable of walking away the winner of that match, and he doesn’t have to beat you’he can beat that sideshow freak that somehow wandered into the situation.” The last comment caused Mike to laugh. “You mean that sentimental fool Corvus” He couldn’t beat a newborn baby if he actually tried! Come on Dick, even you have to realize that he’s not a factor in this. This match is between me and Bubble Boy’period.” Wood nodded his head; Biddle’s assessment of the match had managed to calm him down somewhat. “Fine,” Wood said as he reached into a drawer in his desk and produce a manila folder. He handed it to Biddle. “Study this,” Wood said, “in this folder is a list of the suit’s weaknesses. If you can find a way to exploit at least one of them then you’ll actually stand a chance in this contest.” Mike flipped through the folder. He wanted to point out how he had already beaten Myers’s senseless once before but didn’t want Dick’s head to explode. “Now go,” Wood said, “there is nothing further for us to discuss at this moment. However, I will be at your home at 6 o’clock to discuss the suit’s weaknesses with you.” Mike nodded his head and got up and walked towards the doors to exit the room. Just as Mike was about to step out of the room Wood said to him, “If you fail then I guarantee that your life will not be very pleasant Mr. Biddle.” Mike didn’t reply to Dick’s threat, he just rolled his eyes and made his way to the elevator. Once he was inside the elevator shaft he pressed the button that would take him back down to the mid-level lobby. When he entered the lobby he looked over at the silver desk and gave a suggestive wink to the receptionist, an action that caused her to blush once again. She tried to ask him for his phone number but he ignored her as he walked out the door and onto the top level of the parking garage. He looked around the lot realized that the driver had left, most likely for another round at Krispy Kreme. Mike swore under his breath and pulled out his cell phone and called for another limousine to take him home. He first told the driver’s supervisor how the fat man had ditched him for the donut shop and then he requested another vehicle come and pick him up. Once the ride had been arranged Mike went and leaned against the cool concrete wall and studied the file on Adam Myers’s as he waited. -The End-
