Finally, within his grasp

28 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/28/03 Finally he knew where she was. It had been a long time coming for him, but finally it was here. Not only did Salaok now own half of Global Wrestling, but he also knew where his beloved wife was. A glass cell of sorts is what Quartz had told him about, inside a house which Brian Welch had. A place where Salaok should have looked first but never did. Why? Because he never knew of Brian's estates, only of his home address which he had due to GW files. Files which would now become some of the most important tools of this life. Tools that would not only help him to destroy those who stood against him, but would help him lure those who weren't sure, over to his side of the story. A story etched in time and in history Everyone worldwide seemed to know the story of the four men who wanted nothing but to kill each other. Two sets of brothers who were doomed to end each others existence. Welch Vs Davis. Davis Vs Welch. These two families had started something that no one man would ever forget. But these two families would soon see what they were doing to others. It was no longer a battle between two families, but a battle between two warning sides. Families were not the only ones who took part in the battles. There were outsiders. But even the outsides played some part in the family side of things. Silent Wind and Quartz. Each had chosen their side in the war a long long time ago. Each man destined to destroy the other. Each man, the blood of the other. As Salaok moved through the halls of GW, the thoughts of this family feud which had known over-taken most of the attention in the area. A family feud which had nothing to do with the bigger one in which it was brought into. Quartz and his son had nothing to do with the Welch's and the Davis'. But they certainly play a part. Quartz had chosen to be a apart of The Lair a long time ago while Silent Wind's choice had come a little bit into things. Salaok had always thought that they had made a terrible mistake by not letting Jude know that Quartz was his father during his training at the School of Pain. And it was a mistake that he and Salaok still pay for to this very day. Quartz's son would have been a valuable asset to The Nevermore and during his time with them, he was. But as soon as he found out the truth, hell had broken loose and it caused them pain. Although Quartz was Silent Wind's father, Salaok was his teacher. Because of families ties to Quartz, Salaok had lost one of his prized students. It wasn't everyday that a student like Jude would come along. There had only been one before him and that had been Salaok's trusty friend in Draven. But now, Salaok found himself, driving to the place where he knew Silent Wind would be. But why was he going there? To find his wife of course. In this messed up war between two families, Wind had been caught up and he didn't even know what he was getting himself into. Didn't understand that no one messes with the wife of The Harvester of Fear. Would Wind die for this? Not likely. Would he have to pay? Of course, but then again, most people thoughts he had already paid due to the torment of Samantha. But Samantha was never meant to die. That was a mistake made by the angry and tortured soul of Salaok's half brother. Yet another one who had been caught up in this family war, but not in the same way. But Mani was not to be worried about anymore. He had disappeared after he had killed Samantha, thinking it enough pay back for what Jude had done to him. Enough of a pay back for Jude killing Mani's wife to me? Samantha clearly meant very little to Jude and the act by Mani wasn't needed, nor would it do the job for his revenge. But for now it would keep him settled. Salaok presses a button on his keys which are still in his pocket and the alarm system of his hearse turns off. He was now in the car park of the GW Offices and ready to leave. No bag in hand, not papers. This wasn't a business trip, that much was clear to any on looker watching the movements of the dark haired, demon face which now owned one half of the federation which had tormented him and played his life out on their screen. No, this trip would be taking Salaok to one place, and one place only. The house where Lisha was. To the place where his former student stands guard. To the place that Brian Welch had finally snapped and ran away from a life he could no longer control. To a place which served as a jail. A jail for his wife and the tomb for his ex-student. The car door opening and shutting as he moves into it. With a flick of his wrist, the engine roars to life as the sound of a reworked V8 engine sparks to life. The lights flash on and soon the car is moving straight out of the parked position in a great hurry. Wheels spinning and the rear end of the car sliding from side to side until finally there is traction and the sounds of screeching tyers ends. ---- The time of the trip seems to take forever as the black hearse speeds through the night air as it makes it's way towards it's special destination. But the questions would begin to arise. Would Lisha still be there? Had Quartz been seen by his son? Would Brian still be there? Was this nothing more than a trap set up by The Tragic Kingdom to finally get Salaok for all that has happened in the past? He tried nothing to think too hard about things, wanting to see things clearly in his mind. This would be a time of tests for him. Silent Wind was no mere rooky at this game. He was a professional and Salaok knew it. The games, the traps that Wind had done in the past were all known to Salaok and this was what kept creeping into his mind. The thought that Wind would be torturing Lisha in some way? The way that Johnathon tortured Talena? The way that Quartz had tortured Samantha? Would the son of Salaok's long time friend be so stupid to do such a thing against the most people group in the organized crime world? Would he risk his own life for a friend? That was something Salaok doubted greatly and knew no matter what, Wind would make sure that his own life was never at risk. He would always make it seem as though he had control of the situation. Or at least that is something that Salaok expected from his old student. It was a lesson from The School of Pain that Wind had excelled at. Would it now be time for this teacher to face a student which could stop him? Was Silent Wind that student who could push Salaok? In Salaok's mind, he wasn't. Not only was Wind still young, but he was a Harvester and bound by the laws of The Lair. Putting the car quickly into Neutral and turning off the car, Salaok lets the beast of a machine roll silently towards the house where she was being held. She, being the love of his life and mother of his child. There was lights inside the rather small house. It's brick finish had been painted white which made it stand out amongst all the other houses and was one thing that Salaok had been told to look out for by Quartz. Finally the car comes to a stop and it is placed into Park. From where he sat, he could see no movement, no sign of life. Was it the right house? The thought was quickly put out of the question as Salaok saw the number of the house which was explained to him earlier and was the reason for Roz's mistake which had left him in the middle of something that never should have happened. So what now? Would he simply walk up to the door and give it a knock? Would he creep through the gardens until he found the window that Quartz and Roz had? The car door opens and then closes behind him. Silent footstep, after silent footstep, he moves up to the front door, grabbing at his knife which is nestled closely to his underarm. Fuck the games, it was past that now. He walked straight up to the front door and.... DING DONG DING DONG Salaok pushes the door bell and waits. Waiting. A game in itself that Salaok was sick of. Waiting. BANG. The waiting was taking too long and Salaok's foot smashes into the door breaking the poorly attached lock from the door frame. The knife now securely in Salaok hand, although held upside down to conceal it in his fist. Ready to straight at the first thing that moved. The long nights without sleep had made Salaok that little bit more careless. But he knew it and was willing to take the punishment that came with it. Especially if the punishment resulted in Lisha coming back home with him. A good man he may not have been, a good husband he was.
