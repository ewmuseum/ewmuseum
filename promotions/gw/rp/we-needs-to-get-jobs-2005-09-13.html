'We Needs to get... Jobs'

13 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/13/05 The streets of Las Vegas are rough, and tough? Right? This is the town where the rich come to loose money and hope that they could win it all back. This is the town that people come to escape from the hustle and bustle of the far north east or other places. But for some people it becomes a trap. Worse then the sand traps of the many golf courses around, people get stuck there. They can't seem to get out... This is the story of two such people who want to make something of themselves again. Yes, again. As is the story of many of those who come to Las Vegas, they got here... but they've never seemed to be able to leave. These are men with little morals, nor convictions. They have very little in self restraint, or willpower. We find our anti-heroes as they're sitting in a pair of chairs on a sidewalk. One of these men is a large man, easily near the 7 foot tall mark, maybe an inch or two shorter then that. He's got a darker skin complexion them most, and his hair is a close crew cut. The beginnings of a goatee, also closely trimmed gives away a Hispanic background. His friend, a little shorter, has much darker skin and a mess of hair on top of his head that can only be called false-dreadlocks, or what used to be called corn rows. Both men are wearing pairs of shorts, and collared shirts with a tropical pattern on them. They're sitting in folding chairs, with a bucket in front of them. A small folding table sits between them, where a couple of cans of soda are placed. Both are holding signs advertising their availability to do odd jobs for assorted fees... mostly to get food. ?Damn man. You'd think that with the summer ending, more people would come around here!? The larger man starts things off. “Ey, mon... da t'ing is, we're not down on the strip. We keep getting kicked off the other properties. Hey, I'm getting hungry.” His friend, with a strong accent from the islands chimed in. ?Yeah, I hear ya there. How much of that 200 bucks that one guy gave us for our spy-toys, do we have left?? The dark skinned man started laughing. ?What's so funny?? “Dude...” He slapped his knee as he responded. ?You don't remember much of last night, do you?? “Uhm, not really. I know I woke up in the car, that was it.” “Damn boy. It was you, me and the ladys of da Satin Saddle! You were tipping the one girl like 10 bucks a pop on the lap dances as it was.” ?Damnit! I hope our check is in. How about we go check in on that?? “Check? It's been so long...” “It's been a month...” “Yeah...” The larger man gets up and walks a few doors down on the small strip mall next to there they were setup. It's a UPS Store. He walks in, and pulls out a key. Heading over to the P.O. Boxes that were setup, he opens on and there is a single envelope. He pulls it out and then yells and runs out of the door. ?YAH HOO!? He runs over to where his friend is still sitting. ?Looks like we gots some cash!? “What? Yah, mon. Dat's cool. We're eating real food tonight. Maybe a buffet...” The larger man rips the envelope open and pulls out two pieces of paper. ?What's this? Only $1000? What the hell?? He starts to read the other piece of paper. His friend looks to be day dreaming already. ?$1000, that's what? $600 each? We're set for a month!? The taller man stops reading to correct the other. ?$500, $500 each. Cinco Cientos!? The other still trails off. ?$500 dollars...? “Damnit, Billy says the sales aren't going too well when it comes to the clothing line. In fact the only thing selling still are those bootleg videos... this is all we got for the next two months.” ?Sales are down? Why?? “He says that we need to get some advertising going for it. Our recent time being out of work isn't helping at all. No one knows of our stuff. If people aren't seeing our pretty faces on TV anymore, it's dying.” The other man starts to look like he's pondering something. “Man, well we need to get this check cashed and come up with a plan to get some more dough coming in.” ?Mon, you know what else we need to do?? ?What, dude?? “We needs to get jobs. We needs to get back in the business. We needs... to get paid.” The larger man nods his head and walks up to a newspaper dispenser. He pulls it open, being a free Nifty Nickle dispenser and opens it up. ?You're right. But where could two people like us get a job at?? He starts to flip through the paper, coming to the wanted ads section. He sits down in his chair again and tosses aside part of it, The smaller want ads section starts getting folded and manipulated as he looks through it. Suddenly... he stops... ?Dude!? ?What? What?? ?You ever heard of Global? Fighting, wrestling and whatnot?? The black man thinks for a few seconds and then answers. “Yah, mon. I've seen them played on the different PrimeTime Central stations. Pretty good stuff.” The man smiles as he reads. “Now Hiring: Global Wrestling & Fighting, at the Global Hotel and Casino. Looking for entertainers and other staff for our weekly shows. Must be available to travel for monthly pay per views.” ?DUDE! Dat's the shit right dere, mon!? ?Okay, looks like we need to go get a hotel room for a few hours, each take a shower, and get all prettied up... time to go get a JOB!? Both jump up and pack up their stuff, then stuff it into the back of an old El Camino car. A few hours later, we find ourselves sitting in the plain white washed offices of the Global Tower's Human Resources Department. Our poor subjects are sitting on a pair of very blue chairs, plush, but still business like. They're are tables with magazines laid about, but nothing that you'd expect either of these guys to be reading. The shorter of the two men is trying to read one though, it's some sort of fitness magazine. The larger man is sitting with his hands folded in his lap, looking to be about to fall asleep in the chair. A large woman, very large, with a mole on her cheek walks out. ?Excuse me, Mr. Dermont, Mr. Lanza... are you two ready?? The shorter man answers, putting the magazine down. ?Yeah, when we get paid?? The woman sits down in a chair across from them, smiles and gets adjusted. ?Okay, both of you have some impressive applications here, but do you have any resumes?? ?Resume? What's a resume?? She scribbles something down on the papers attached to a clip board. “Okay.” The taller man starts to get fully awake and aware again. “Well, I'd like to offer you both some work here at Global Tower...” ?YES!? Both of them say it at the same time. “But your first assignment would be actually not here, but up at our show this coming week in Seattle, at the Key Arena.” Both look at each other, and then give their answer. ?SWEET! The home of Nirvana and Pearl Jam!? She scribbles some stuff down again. ?Well, if you all are ready to fill out the paper work needed, what you do is sign it all, then we'll make sure you have your tickets to get up there, and make sure you have your per-diem needed for everything. Can you leave tomorrow?? ?SURE!? The woman puts some of the papers down for them, and hands them each a pen... Looks like the first part of this story has come to a point where two lives could be changing... or will it be Global that will be changed with their arrival?
