More target practice

23 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/23/03 It's nighttime. A Lincoln Navigator drove out into an empty field. Brick is setting up some cardboard cutouts, one by one. There are five of them. Each one is a member of the Nevermore. Salaok. Quartz. Sage Sabbot. Mark Kullypso. All of them are lined up. Brick then went back to the back and pulled out an old khaki green duffel bag. Slinging it over his shoulder, he carried it over to where he set up the cut-outs. He then set it down and opened it up. One by one, he began to pull out some guns. A sawed-off shotgun. A Glock 9-mm. handgun. A Tec-9 automatic. An Uzi submachine gun. First, Brick picked up the shotgun and walked over to the Sabbot cutout. “First, we come to you, Sabbot. The Harvester Of Tears, the Soul Survivor, the Warlock. It's me and you at Dead Zone, little boy. Notice how I don't say 'just me and you.' Cause I know it won't just be me and you. It'll be me and you and Salaok and Quartz and Kullypso. You Nevermore bitches ain't slick. That's how you've gotten most of your victories and all your titles. With help from the Nevermore. You became Cruiserweight champion with help from the Nevermore. You remained Cruiserweight champion with help from the Nevermore. You became International Champion with help from the Nevermore. Well, don't count on winning the World title with help from the Nevermore. I'll be ready for them. I know you have it in your mind somewhere that you're going to get that title. Well, it all boils down to who wants it more. Now, some cats might say that I'm hungry for the title. I'm not hungry. I'm starving. And the most dangerous animal is one that's starving. At Dead Zone, you see just how dangerous I am.” Brick then pointed the Glock in 'Sabbot's' face and pulled the trigger, leaving a gaping hole. He then walked over to 'Mark Kullypso'. 'Still jerking off to pictures of Cindy Frost, huh, Kullypso?' Brick asked the cutout as he loaded another shell into the shotgun. 'You know, I get the feeling that you might try something in my match at Dead Zone like you did my match at Wrestlecade. In case you do, remember what happened at Wrestlecade.' With that, he fired a shot right into 'Kullypso's' chest. He then turned his attention to 'Quartz'. He went and grabbed the Tec-9 before walking over to the picture. “Now, we come to you, Quartz. The so-called original monster of GW. Salaok's #1 guy. His right hand man. His other wife. I've beaten you a number of times lately. I can and will do it again. Just like I did at Wrestlecade and on Evolution.” Brick opened fire and blasted 'Quartz' to pieces. Now Brick found himself face-to-face with the only Nevermore cutout still intact. Salaok, the leader of the Nevermore and now 50% owner of Global Wrestling. The man who had tried more than any other to make Brick’s life a living hell. Brick regarded the effigy with a cold stare. “I knew it wasn't gonna be over between us. Not by a long shot. I bet you thought it was real damn funny sending that video disc to my family, scaring my baby sister. You said you could do anything you wanted to in order to get to me. The question is: should you? You think you're the only sick motherfucker in this company? Oh no. Ask Christina McCoy, you'll see. Keep in mind, you have family, too. Your big brother, your little sister...your wife.” Brick paused for a bit and then backed away. 'I made my father a promise before he passed on,? Brick said as he aims the gun at 'Salaok'. 'I told him that I would protect my family, whatever it takes. And I do mean whatever.' Brick opened fire on 'Salaok' and shredded 'him' to pieces. He then heard the clicking that told him the clip was empty. Dropping the gun, he started to gather all the pieces from the destroyed cutouts into a pile. He then went back to the Navigator and pulled out a gasoline bottle. He opened the cap and doused the entire pile of shredded cardboard in gasoline. He then pulled out a book of matches, struck the match and threw the match onto the pile, causing it to burst into flame. “Damn, I feel better now,” Brick thought to himself, as the fire consumed the pile entirely, and the flame went higher.
