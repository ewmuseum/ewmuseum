Sticky Situation

8 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/8 Following their meeting with Il Capo at TUE headquarters Deacon Holmes and John Zero were given an assignment not unlike ones they’ve taken in the past. A simple infiltration of a laboratory operating under the guise of an abandoned warehouse and the extraction of an encrypted computer disc containing who knows what. But it’s contents are of no concern of theirs anyway. The job is simple, sneak in, grab the disc before it’s code is deciphered, and be back home by dinner time. Sounds simple enough. After being briefed both men suit up, Zero takes along a sleeve that goes from his wrist to his elbow and has an LCD screen on the inside of his forearm. After knocking the easy part out of the way, infiltrating the building, Zero and Holmes follow the screen on Zero’s arm which shows a map of the building’s floor plans. Aside from having to hide from the occasional guard so far everything has been smooth sailing, but all of that soon changes when they run into and unexpected hindrance. Both men stop in front of a large steel door with an elevated vent who’s cover is hanging by a single screw at it's bottom right corner, momentarily and re-check their map. “How tha hell are we gonna get through that door!” Any bright ideas Johnnyboy?” Questions Holmes. Zero then moves forward towards the vent while removing a medium sized screw driver from his pocket. He then begins the slow process of removing the screw from the vent, which is just barely in his reach as he stands on his toes. “Alright, once I get this cover off you'll be all set.” Informs Zero. “What!” What do you mean I'll be all set!” I'm not going in there...” Grunts Holmes while gritting his teeth in a failed attempt at keeping his voice down. Zero continues on removing the final screw from the vent cover as though nothing were said. “So, are you gonna need a boost?” Questions Zero in a tone of faux courtesy. “No! I'm not goin, we don't even know what's in there John.” Explains Holmes in an increasingly aggravated tone. John quietly sets the steel vent cover down, propping it against the wall. He then looks up at Holmes with a rather confused look on his face. “You sure you don't need a boost man” That vent is pretty high.” “I am absolutely, positively, metaphysically, one-hundred percent, without a shadow of a doubt in my mind sure that I don't need a boost because I'm not going in there John. Besides, it's dark in there man.” States Holmes rather matter of factly. “Oh, is that all” Here, take this.” Zero removes a small push button flashlight from his keychain and hands it to Holmes. “Now that that's taken care of, are you sure you're not gonna need a boost?” Asks Zero while trying desperately to keep a straight face as Holmes glares at him furiously. “Man why I always gotta do all tha grunt work” Deacon steal that disk, Yo D pick this lock for me, Hey Deac jump into the dark ass rat infested air conditioning vent. This is bullshit John, I need a promotion or somethin, this 'Capo's Angels' gig ain't workin out with you punkin' me like this all the time. I'm puttin' my foot down this time John, why can't you just go in the vent?” Whine's Holmes. “Who's gonna read the map and tell you where to go then if I'm in there too” Besides, someone's gotta supervise and make sure you don't screw up.” Replies Zero while fiddling with the device attached to his right forearm. “Fuck that man, I'm not goin. I finally figured out why I'm always the one that has to do shit like this.” Exclaims Holmes, now extremely agitated. “And why might that be?” Asks Zero calmly. “Because I'm black!” Answers Holmes immediately, sending John into a fit of laughter. “That's not it.” “Then why?” Asks an indignant Holmes “Because you're a thief.” Replies Zero flatly “Ex-thief! And I prefer con-man.” “Why?” “It sounds more professional.” “You're a weird kid Deac.” “I'm a grown ass man John.” Announces Deacon with an air of conceit as his chest slightly pokes out. “Whatever man, ya know what” Here, you can take this and I’ll go in since you’re scared.” Explains Zero while removing the device from his arm and dangling it in front of Holmes like a raw steak in front of a ravenous dog, Slowly, Deacon reaches out to grab it. “I mean, you do know how to use it, right?” Asks Zero with a smirk, causing Deacon’s outstretched hand to fall back to his side and his shoulders to slump in defeat. “No” is the only word that escapes Deacon’s lips as he walks toward the vent before quickly whirling back around toward Zero with a new found fire in his eyes. “Rock paper scissors!” He shouts, causing John to grimace at the possibility of them getting caught. “What?” “You heard me, rock paper, scissors, loser climbs through the vents.” “Are you serious?” “Dead.” Both men form a fist with one hand and bang it against the open palm of the other three times while whispering “One, two, three.” The fourth time their fists are raised each man calls out “Shoot!” and quickly throws it down once more but this time forming their symbol of choice along the way. Deacon lays his hand out flat as if he were resting it on a table while Zero’s fist remains clinched, save two fingers which are spread apart from each other. “Scissors cut paper, I win.” Remarks John triumphantly, much to Deacon’s dismay. “No, it’s a rock, it’s just a flat rock. Rock smashes scissors, I win.” Holmes retorts desperately. “Well then in that case these are laser tipped atomic scissors, so like I said, I win, and more importantly, you lost. So... are you gonna need a boost or what?” “No.” Answers Holmes somewhat solemnly, once again slumping his shoulders. John removes a small flat metal square from his pocket and adheres it to the inside of Deacon’s hood. “What’s that for?” Inquires Deacon, still somewhat bent out of shape about losing. “It’s a tracking device, it’ll allow me to see where you are on this screen. That way I can tell you where to go.” Responds John while activating both devices. “So you mean to tell me that while I’m stuck up in those musty ass vents you’re gonna be sitting around somewhere calling out left or right!”” “I prefer supervising.” “Why?” “It sounds more professional.” Both men chuckle quietly to themselves as they adjust their earpieces. Zero watches as Holmes backs away from the wall and then runs forward at full speed before leaping into the air and kicking off of the wall to gain the extra height he needs to catch on to the edge of the open shaft and pull himself inside. After seeing Holmes slide into the vent John cautiously makes his way back down the hall in search of safer terrain in which to play navigator. “There’s rats in here!” Blares through Zero’s earpiece as Holmes screams about his shocking discovery causing John to flinch. “Fuck this man, I’m comin” out!” Continues Holmes. “Just be calm, they’re as afraid of you as you are of them.” “The hell they are! Oww! I think one of those fuckers just bit me!” Is the response John receives back in his earpiece followed by three gunshots in rapid succession. “Deacon what tha hell are you doing!” Should I just got pull the fire alarm and hang a banner that says TUE is here!”” “Why would you go and do something like that?” “For the same reason a barely nineteen year old would fire a Desert Eagle inside of a ventilation shaft during an operation in which he’s supposed to be employing stealth! Now hurry up and turn left so we can get the hell out of here. Do you see it now?” Deacon Holmes watches from above as three well dressed men in security suits follow another man wearing a lab coat out of the room. He then silently manages to push the vent cover off and drop down to the ground below. “Where the hell is this thing supposed to be?” Asks Deacon as he hurriedly searches the room. “There should be a computer terminal in the upper left corner of the room, all logic points there.” “Got it!” Answers Deacon while tucking the computer disc inside his hoodie. Just then a high pitched ear splitting siren is heard and all lights in the building begin flashing red as the words “Intruder Alert” sound off again and again throughout each room. Although on opposite sides of the building both men’s reaction is the same. Holmes and Zero’s eyes widen as they realize the gravity of the situation. Then, they reach for their guns.
