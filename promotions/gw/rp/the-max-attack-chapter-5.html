The Max-Attack: Chapter 5

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today BOOM! Pieces fly everywhere and Roswell flies backwards through a desk. The desk breaks in half and everything falls onto Roswell making the pains even worse. A light from the ceiling falls and smashes over Roswell=s head making the top of his head start gushing out with blood. Blood pours from his head as the explosion stops. Roswell starts glaring at the little ball and starts struggling to get to his feet. A few minutes later Roswell is up but can only walk by holding onto desktops and walls. Roswell looks back at the man at the end of the room. All Roswell see is one arm and one leg with blood covering the whole back wall. Roswell starts limping the other way towards the man and stops at the leg. Roswell keeps walking some more and sees the head laying on a desktop with blood coming from the inside. On the outside of the building more police officers are seen rushing into the building trying to find anything that happened. Police Officers go into every floor checking to see if there are any people left in the building. One goes into the floor Roswell is in and runs towards Roswell. “Are you okay sir?” asks the police officer who is now sweating. “Yeah, but I can’t stand. I came onto this floor and saw a man at the end of the room. I ran to get him but found a mini bomb laying on the ground. And then it just exploded.” tells Roswell “Please come with me, sir and we can help you.” says the police officer back to Roswell. The police officer helps Roswell to his feet and helps him walk to the door and down the stairs. Roswell says that he can try to walk on his own. So the police officer lets Roswell go and he starts walking very slowly. Roswell walks out the front door of the building and walks under the police tape. Roswell walks over to the closest bench and sits down resting his legs. A police officer walks over to Roswell and sits on the bench with him. “Ok, Max Roswell. I’m going to be asking you a couple of questions. Why did you go into the building when I told NOT TO!” yells the police officer. “I’m really sorry. I wanted to help some people. I didn’t want to get hurt or anything.” tells Roswell quietly. “Sorry for yelling. It’s just I have a problem about yelling.” says the police officer. “I went into the building because I like saving people. I don’t want to be a big shot or anything. But I like saving people.” Says Roswell. “Well don’t do it again.” Says the police officer. Roswell gets up from the bench and starts walking towards his hotel still holding his head. There is still some blood dripping from Roswell’s head but he ignores it. Roswell gets to the hotel door and walks inside. Roswell walks over to the elevator and pushes the Up button. The door opens and Roswell walks in pushing the 5th floor button. Roswell gets to his room door and opens it with the key. Roswell walks inside and sits down on a chair by his desk. Roswell takes the little mysterious ball out of his pocket and lays it on the desk beside the others. Roswell walks into the bath and grabs a towel to put on his head so the blood wouldn’t make a mess on the floor. Roswell then remembers that there was another ball laying in the building. The one that exploded on him. Roswell then walks back out of his hotel room and down the stairs. Roswell rushes down the stairs and out of the hotel. Roswell walks to the back of the blown up building and climbs up the fire escape. Roswell looks through a window and sees the ball still laying under the desk. Roswell opens the window easily and runs to get the ball. Roswell picks it up and puts it into his pocket again. Roswell looks down the hall to see if any police officers are coming and then runs back to the window. Roswell climbs back down the fire escape and runs back to the hotel. Roswell goes up to his room and lays the extra ball beside the 4 other ones. Roswell then sits down on his chair and starts studying each of the little balls carefully. “I wonder how many of these that guy has?” asks Roswell to his self.
