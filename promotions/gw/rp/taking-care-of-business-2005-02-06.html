Taking Care Of Business

6 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/06/05 A strike of lightning ignites the sky in a stunning show of radiance, before fading to black. The rolling bass of thunder shakes the rain soaked earth below. The offset towers of glass and steel reach up towards the crying sky... rolling its tears down to the ocean of concrete below. The dank city is blanketed in green hue of darkness, with only scattered sources of light coming from the street lamps. In the alley, there were no street lamps... only the wastes of the city. Dumpsters lined the soaked alley, with paper trash laying smeared and helpless under the force of mother nature. A symphony of raindrops serenaded the puddles below, if you closed your eyes... you could swear mother nature was singing you a sonnet. A black shadow streaks across the dumpster, before an out of place splash interrupts the sonnet in mid-stanza. A new rhythm, the pounding of man takes over... held by the metronome of the heart. The tick of the metronome speeds out of control with fear before the man comes to a screeching halt in front of a large stone wall. The man stares up into the dark heavens, its tears drop down upon his distraught face. Tick, Tick Tick, Tick The hairs on the back of his neck stand up with instinct. His metronome speeds up at a racing pace... induced by panic. Adrenaline runs through his veins and hits him like heroin to an addict. As the tears of man and united into one with the tears from the heavens, the man spins around and almost falls on the slick pavement. Breaths become visible through the humidity and grow louder as time passes, he backs into the stone... staring through the sheets of water falling from above. A silhouette appears near the end of the alley way, a black form against a backdrop of darkened greens. He falls to his knees clutching at his heart... speeding out of control. A vice clamps around his throat, the symphony reaches it’s climax as the silhouette grows larger and larger. Shadow engulfs the man, crying like a baby in the fetal position. An ostrich with its head in the sand, what you cannot see is not there. Primal instincts take over as the warm breath of the silhouette can be felt and the thoughts of survival encompass every thought and action. Tick, Tick, Tick, Tick Tick, Tick, Tick, Tick “I didn’t mean it I swear! You have to believe me! You won’t get away with this... justice will prevail...” the man sobbed through staggered breaths. His body began to shake beyond control as he looked up towards the silhouetted man. Who remained expressionless, cold, and in-control. “When I was a child... the lines between good and bad were finely drawn. They were black and white, and justice always prevailed. The wicked were punished for their sins and were locked away in whatever vile personal hell awaited them. The new millennia unleashed a world of greys... there was no more black and white... no more good and evil. With this, how could one really tell right from wrong and good from evil” Maybe that’s because all the good is gone... and only the evil remains. When all the good is gone, the evil shall inherit the throne in which it will be declared there is no more justice my friend, now there is only destiny.” The silhouette spoke as he reached inside his jacket and pulled a silver Desert Eagle. “You’ll get caught... you’ll never get away with this! You’ll be arrested, and they’ll throw away the key!” the man screamed through the tears desperately trying to talk his way out of destiny. TICK, TICK, TICK, TICK TICK, TICK, TICK, TICK “How ignorant you are my prey... all the police in the world couldn’t raise the money to keep me in their prisons...” BANG! A man sits straight up in his bed like a ghost as the covers float to the wood-paneled floor like a feather. Sweat pours down his face like a waterfall, as he hyperventilates... trying desperately to pull air into his lungs. He takes deep breaths to try and regain control of himself before he takes a look at the clock... 3:26am Looking out the window he sees waterfall flowing down the side of the building. With a sign of relief, he pulls the covers back up off the floor and over his body. Closes his eyes... and thanks God it was just a dream... ---- Date: February 2, 2005 Time: 6:00pm Local Time City: just outside Dallas, Texas Location: Zenith Manor South The sun hangs over the 2.5 acre Zenith Manor South just outside of Dallas, Texas. Birds chirp in harmony from nearby trees as Vivica J. Valentine relaxes out on the back patio. The patio is connected to a waterfall style pool, with clear blue water that glistens in the sunlight. She wears a black baby-t with a white and blue killswitch engage logo, her blonde hair drapes over the back of the padded reclining patio chair. Her black stretch pants give the impression that she had just finished working out, with her legs propped up on the glass patio table. A pair of black designer sunglasses covered her brilliant blue-green eyes, which looked over the tranquil waterfall. A loud screeching sound broke the silence as Vivica sat up and looked towards the sliding glass French doors leading to the patio. Jason LeMaster came running out onto the patio with a piece of paper flailing in his hand... “What in the blue hell is wrong with you”!” Vivica says as she sits up looking at Jason LeMaster, in his backwards Baltimore Orioles fitter ballcap, tight black fitting T-shirt, and a black pair of jeans. He slams the paper down in front of Vivica, who takes off her sunglasses and puts them on the collar of her shirt. “Yeah... we have a problem.” Jason says as Vivica eyes over the picture and article... “You HAVE to be shittin me...” Vivica said as she reads over it and looks back up at Jason, “Is this real?” “Yeah... you need to get Christian.” Jason said with concern as Vivica jumps out of her seat. “Call the pilot... tell him we’re on our way.” Valentine said as she walked off the patio and into the massive Zenith Manor. Vivica swerved in and out of lifting machines, making her way through the elaborate personal gym Christian had made... complete with attached racquetball and basketball courts. The next room was the theater where Christian laid on the black leather couch watching the movie Resident Evil: Apocalypse. Vivica walks up behind the sofa where Zenith sits up and looks at her... “What’s wrong?” Christian says with a glare. Valentine hands Christian the paper, he looks it over carefully. Zenith’s cadet blue eyes shoot back and forth across the article, before taking a close look at the picture... “Dietrich and... Jake Sloan... great...” Christian said as he got up off the couch and hurled the paper across the room. “Isn’t Jake Sloan one of your engineering geniuses” What in the hell is he doing in this picture with Dietrich... and what the fuck is Dietrich doing out of jail, this makes no sense at all...” Vivica said before running her hand through her hair. “We’ve known Dietrich’s been out of jail... he came after me right before Dark Rituals. Now he’s somehow snaked his way into United Arms Corporation... trying to take my company back again.” Zenith as he walked over and got his jacket off the coat rack on the other side of the room, “What he’s doing with Sloan I have no clue... but you will.” Vivica J. Valentine stopped dead in her tracks, shortly before her jaw dropped down to the floor... “Whoa whoa whoa... this is YOUR company, I have nothing to do with this.” Vivica said backing up as Zenith headed out the door. Without turning around Zenith paused and said aloud... “But Jake Sloan is a dirty pervert... and you’re his type.” ---- Date: February 3, 2005 Time: 8:00pm Local Time City: Chicago, Illinois Location: Hyatt Regency Hotel Bar “Bartender! Another martini please!” a middle-aged man shouted from then end of the bar. He wore a red dress shirt with the collar unbuttoned and a pair of black slacks. Standing about 5’9? and weighing around 180 pounds, he wasn’t really a man of stature but he had a certain swagger and confidence about him... that only a business man could have. His light brown hair was slicked back and pulled into a pony tail, he caught his drink and took a sip with a smile. Suddenly be began to choke on his drink and the entire room grew into a hush... when Vivica J. Valentine stepped into the room. A short, strapless black dress and a pair of black stiletto heels. She had long blonde hair that went just down past her shoulders, and a smile that could kill a man. The man locked eyes with her from across the room, it was an instant connection. As he peered into her blue-green eyes, the sound of “Landslide” by Fleetwood Mac was drowned out by sounds of “Ode To Joy”. Walking across the room seemed to happen in slow motion, making her way past hoards of gawking men... before stopping behind the middle-aged man and his martini. “Is this seat taken?” She asked with a slight British accent. “Not at all!” The man said with a smile, “Please sit down!” “Thank you, what a gentleman...” Vivica says before she takes a seat on the stool, “since we’re going to be sitting together, I might as well get to know your name right?” “My name is Jake Sloan. And do you think I could have a name to go with this pretty face?” he says with a smile trying to flag down the bartender. “Jessica Harding.” she says with a big smile... “BARTENDER! Get this lady a...” “ICED TEA!” she shouts before giggling a little bit, “I’m sorry, I’ve had a bad experience with guys ordering drinks for me. Some guy slipped something into my drink one time and I’ve been paranoid ever since.” “It’s quite alright, but you don’t have to worry about that from me beautiful, I’d never do anything to hurt you.” Sloan said cheesily with a toothy grin. “Awww, how sweet...” Vivica says as she takes a sip of her Iced Tea. She looks down at his shirt and back up into his eyes, “you look like you’ve had a long day at work” maybe?” “Yeah... I just recently started a new job...” He said before sucking down on his martini. “A new job” Like what is it that you do? You look awfully important!” Vivica said with excitement, while deep down inside she felt like she was going to puke. “Well actually I’m an engineer! I’m one of the most respected arms engineers in the field today. Most of the new prototypes the military has been trying out was written down by me on paper... and then turned into a reality by the United States Military. I’m trying to get them to switch contracts from my old job to my new job... they seem like they may be interested, but it’s hard work. Not to mention something you have to do to get a quality guy like me.” Sloan said, with his arrogance flowing. “Wow, that doesn’t sound like something that your old boss would appreciate...” Vivica said, trying her best to crack a smile instead of cracking him upside the head. “Actually... to be completely honest with you, he doesn’t know yet. Well, I technically still work for him, but that’s only until I get the contract signed over. You see... my new boss actually founded my old bosses company, but was kicked out by his father. Now that little punk kid has taken over... and spends all his time pro wrestling or some kind of fake shit like that... a complete waste of time. I need someone who has some incentive... not some cocky ass hotshot kid who spends all his time putting on little fake ass fight shows for some rednecks in Texas, I’m sure a girl like you with some class understands where I’m coming from! But that’s enough about me honey, what do you do for a living?” Sloan says, looking down at Vivica’s chest while she responds... “Well... actually I’m a personal assistant, with a company called.... PTC.” She said... biting her tongue because she couldn’t think of anything else. “What’s PTC stand for” The P has to stand for Pretty... if everyone else is anything like you.” He told her, trying to sweet talk his way into her pants. “Umm... Prime Time Central... it’s a... talent agency.” She said with a smile... “So, how do you like your new boss?” “He’s alright... he’s a little old school if you know what I mean, but it works for him.” Jake said as he took another gulp of his martini, thinking about the perfect way to get her into his hotel room. This was just the kinda break he needed after a long day. “It’s not going to work for him... and it’s not going to work for you.” Vivica J. Valentine says ditching the British accent and speaking with her real voice. “...What is going on here?” Sloan says as he looks back at Vivica J. Valentine. “Fucking with Christian Zenith isn’t going to work for you... and don’t bother telling him you quit, we’re gonna punch your ticket ourselves.” Vivica said as she stood up. Suddenly the entire bar stood up from their seats. As Sloan looked around in complete shock, he noticed every single person in the bar had a tattoo on their right arm of a black “Z” intertwined with an upside down crucifix. Sloan jumps over the bar and runs through the kitchen area. The 40+ man mod attempts to chase after him but Vivica J. Valentine jumps up on top of the bar... “STOP!” She orders the mod as they stand still in their tracks. Their eyes stay fixated on Vivica J. Valentine as she hold her arm in the air. “It’s taken care of.” she says with a smile, “we had that front entrance...
