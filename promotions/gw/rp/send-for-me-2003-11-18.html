Date: 11-18-03 This day, Brick and DMAK were to record another track for the upcoming cover album. They had been making pretty good time recording the album, considering Brick’s hectic schedule. In this case, it was the Atlantic Starr song, “Send For Me”. Yet again, the group’s baritone, Keith, would be singing lead. The last two DMAK albums were heavy on tracks where either Damian or Andre, the group’s two tenors, sang lead. Marcus had yet to do a song where he’d be on lead, except for the group’s cover of “The Secret Garden”, in which he sang the part originally performed by the late Barry White. “OK, let’s go over that again, from the top,” Brick said over the intercom as he and Aaron watched from the control booth in the recording studio.
