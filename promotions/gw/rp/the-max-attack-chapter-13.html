The Max-Attack: Chapter 13

Date: Today The scene cuts back into the 15th floor of the Sears Tower. Roswell is seen getting off the bench still all ragged up. Roswell walks to the closest bathroom and goes inside. A couple minutes later he walks out with his suit not ripped up. The suit is perfectly good with no marks on it at all. Max runs forwards and jumps through one of the windows shattering it. Max flies through the air only a little dot to the people below him. Max starts slowing down and lands on the sidewalk right by his hotel. Max quickly runs through the sliding doors and through the lobby without the people seeing him. A kid who is walking down a hallway looks at Max and the costume he is wearing. “It’s not Halloween today sir!” yells the little kid. Max ignores the kid and walks into his room. Roswell walks straight into the bathroom and in 5 minutes walks back out with normal clothes on. Roswell opens his room door and walks into the hallway again. The little kid that yelled at him was standing right at his door step staring straight into the eyes of Max. “Hey little guy, what’s your name?” asks Roswell “Ben” replies the little kid. “How old are you?” “I’m 7 years old” “I have a cousin that is 7 years old. He loves wrestling” says Roswell. “I like wrestling too!” yells Ben. “Guess what. I’m a wrestler myself” says Roswell “You’re a wrestler”!” yells Ben. “Yup” “What’s your name in wrestling. I watch a fed every week called Global Wrestling” says Ben. “You watch Global Wrestling?” asks Roswell “Yeah, it’s the best federation in the world” “I’m in Global Wrestling” says Max. “Your in GW” As who?” asks Ben. “Well, my wrestling name is Max Roswell” says Roswell. “The Christian Beast!” yells Ben. “You know my nick name?” “You’re my favourite wrestler in that federation other than Brick and Jin Akiyara” says Ben. “Maybe sometime I could bring you backstage and you could meet Jin and Brick’says Roswell. “You mean it”!” “Yup” All of a sudden the hotel starts shaking. A big explosion happens and sides of the wall starts cracking. Some of the hotel room start breaking off and ben holds on tight to Roswell. “I don’t know where my mommy it” says Ben. “I’ll find her for you” says Roswell. Max picks Ben up and starts running down the hallway to the stairs. Roswell quickly runs down all the stairs with Ben in his hands and out of the lobby to the outside of the hotel. Roswell sees a lady yelling out the word Ben! Roswell runs over to her and asks if this is Ben. “Is this your child?” asks Roswell. “Yes, where did you find him?” asks the lady. “He was walking around the hallway in the 5th floor” tells Roswell. “Thank you so much. How can I give you something back?” asks the lady. “You don’t have to give anything to me. I’m your sons favourite wrestler.” “Your Max Roswell?” asks Ben’s mom. “Ya” Roswell looks into the clear sky and sees a little black dot coming straight for him. Roswell runs closer to see what it is. Roswell then sees a even smaller dot fall from the big dot. The little one hits the top of a building and blows the top of it off. Roswell jumps into the air and lands on the roof of the hotel. Max looks and sees that it is The Blue Bat!!! Max jumps into the air. SCENE FADES
