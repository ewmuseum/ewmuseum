:: Abilities and Ghosts Prologue: Time ::

22 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22/10/04 Winters stumbled into his apartment, throwing his bag full of wrestling gear on his couch, slamming the door behind him, whilst idly attempting to slip out of his denim jacket. Once off, he hung the jacket upon the wooden coat by the door and slouched into his distinctly comfortable armchair. He’d just come back from Global Wrestling’s Evolution show and it had taken a helluva of a toll upon his body. He’d been pitted against the Television and Platinum champion, Pendragon and it had been incredibly taxing to say the least. Eventually, he’d lost the match, but reportedly his performance booked him a place in a prime match on the next Global Wrestling pay per view’Dark Rituals. The match would be for the cruiserweight title and he would have to get past two of the federations best cruiserweights to get it, but the way he figured it, the odd favoured him greatly. On the one hand there was Joshua Curtis, he was a new guy who has worked hard over the past couple of weeks and has really made an impression on Winters. That was enough to make Winters question whether or not the match would be a landslide victory. And then there was Adam Myers. He was the last GW Cruiserweight champion, yet he would enter this match a contender just like himself and Curtis’confused? Myers had to abdicate the title a couple of weeks back for some reason. Winters wasn’t entirely sure why, but that did not matter. The important thing was that he was a former champion’he’d done this all before and more importantly he knew what it took to get it done. That was the most worrying thing of all. Winters shook the thoughts from his mind. There was no point in worrying about battle strategies to capture the cruiserweight gold this early into the contest’not when he had more important things on his mind. Thus far, with five plus weeks of television exposure behind him, he’d not encountered the guys in black suits that had shot at him - and were obviously hunting him - at all. He didn’t know quite what to make of that, and ultimately he did not care to examine the phenomenon any further. He was just glad that they still hadn’t found him and that was good enough for him. However, he did allow the prospect of the future creep into his conscious mind. If they were that determined to find him, then one day - given time - they would probably succeed in the task. He had to think about what he would do in such a situation. What edge did he have that could possible aid him when that fateful day arose? He thought about his time since waking up from his amnesia and the things that he’d done in that time. He thought every time he amazed himself and made himself wonder whether his action were normal’or abnormal. Take for instance the building jump he’d accomplished whilst escaping from the guys with guns. That was not normal for a human being. Until this moment he’d avoided even thinking about some of the abnormal things he’d done - for some reason it didn’t sit well with him. What if he wasn’t a normal human being? If he found that to be true’could he live with himself? The answer was: he had to. It was the only edge he had over the guys with guns. He’d made a decision. He would learn more about the capabilities and to hell with the consequences. Hell, if he did find out more about himself that would be a bonus. It would mean that he was drawing closer to normality’but then again you can’t get everything you ask for. That’s not what life’s about.
