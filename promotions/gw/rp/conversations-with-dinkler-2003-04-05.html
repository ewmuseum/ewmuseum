Conversations with Dinkler

5 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/5/03 GW lead Interviewer Steven Dinkler sits in his black leather chair while an attractive young lady delicately puts make-up on him. The other chair, directly opposite of him, is empty. Between the two chairs sits a black table with two bottles of Aquafina water. The attractive blonde lady finishes her work and walks off the set and Dinkler goes to work. Dinkler: GW fans welcome to another instalment of Conversations with Dinkler. I’m your host Steven Dinkler and tonight we have a special guest. He is none other than Mr. Redneck Rampage, the GW’s own, Jesse Briggs! No one walks onto the stage. Minutes creep by uneasily. Dinkler: Apparently Jesse hasn’t arrived yet. Dinkler appears a bit agitated at this development. Dinkler; Fans, can you believe what Salaok did to Brick on Evolution this week.... Suddenly the door to the studio bursts open, the camera scans to the right revealing Jesse Briggs. He stumbles through the door in a drunken stupor holding a mason jar of his home made brew. His clothes are ripped up and he has cuts all over himself from an obvious brawl he’s just had. Briggs staggers up onto the set and collapses into the seat opposite Dinkler, whom has a look of sheer terror etched across his face. Dinkler, trying to remain professional begins the interview. Dinkler: Welcome back to Global Wrestling Jesse! Jesse: BBBBBBBBBBBUUUUUUUUUUUUURRRRRRRRRPPPPPPPPP!!! Dinklers face turns purple with embarrassment at Jesse bodily function. Jesse, who seems to be almost unconscience drops his mason jar onto the table. Luckily it falls in a upright position and doesn’t spill out. Dinkler, still trying to do his job and act professional, attempts to start the interview again. Dinkler: It’s been well over six months since you’ve stepped into a GW ring, how do you feel about your absence? Jesse:.......the hell? Dinkler: Global Wrestling.... Jesse: Whar the hell am I? Dinkler: On the set of Conversations with Dinkler.... Jesse: What with Dinky? Dinkler: (Angrily but politely) Conversation with DinkLER! Jesse: A’ight, nighty night. Dinkler: Somebody....just get this drunken buffoon out of here... Jesse’s eyes open and anger flinches across his face! He leaps up and gets right into the face of Dinkler, who slouches into the chair seeking refuge. Jesse: LEMME TELL YA SOMETHING, DINKY BOY! i JUST MIGHT BE MANY O? THANG, BUT I AIN’T A APE! Dinkler: I never called you an ape.... Jesse grabs Dinkler by the shirt and tosses him off of the set. He stares directly into the camera and stumbles around for at least a good thirty seconds before he regains his balance. Jesse: AND STAY OUT DINKY! Now lemme see....where am I again? Global Wrasslin? Have I been here before? Nah I don’t think so....oh wait!!!! It’s Bubba’s joint....yeah now I remember. That son of a bitch Salaok put Bubba in the hospital just before I came back, damn you Salaok you eye o? newt eatin, nut o? toad slurpin sumbitch! I’ll make ya pay fer whatcha did ta my best friend, I surely will! “Course it’ll be after I sober up some, but I’ll do it! Now wait, I had somethin” perpared fer this special accasion. Where is it. Jesse starts looking through the pockets of his battered flannel shirt and torn jeans. Finally he pulls out a wrinkled up sheet of paper. He holds it up in front of his face trying to focus his eyes to read it, finally he begins. Jesse: I wrote a song specially fer old Sally! I like to call this “un Drinkin” Shine and Whoopin? Ass. He begins to sing out of tune. Jesse: Drinkin? Shine and Whoopin? Ass. Drinking shine and whoopin? ass. Them’s the two things I love the most, and do the best. Drinkin? shine and whoopin? ass. There’s this sumbitch... there’s this sumbitch.... Name o? Salaok. Dunno what he did to his parents. But they hate him. “Cause they named the poor bastard, Salaok. I’ma whoop his ass. I said, whoop his ass. It’s what I do, and I do it good. When I’m done, I’ma drink some shine! Good ol? Kentucky Mash! The good stuff! Jesse passes out and is carried off the stage by the GW crew.
