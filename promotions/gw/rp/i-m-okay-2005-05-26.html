I'm okay

26 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/26/05 I sit alone in the living room 'The Hand That Feeds' by Nine Inch Nails plays over the stereo. My head is still in immense pain from the events that went down on Marvelous Matters. The sickening thud that was heard when Shadow dented the chair over my head was sure to have killed a few brain cells. If that wasn't enough to do the job the DDT on the chair sure as hell was. Shadow will get what's coming to her as will Mike Biddle for kicking me while I was down. However, they are not the task at hand Leinad Soma was. I heard a knock on the door I get up slowly and as I stand I grab my head for a moment. I walk towards the door and peek out the window to see who it is. Thankfully its not Twisted Cowboy but this man could annoy me just as easily but he atleast has to try. I open the door and I'm greeted by a middle finger. 'Yeah, Nice to see you too Kevin' 'Whatever butt stabber!' Kevin practically shouted as he entered. 'Butt stabber? You're more random then me. Where you get this stuff I'll never know.' 'I'll never give away my secrets. Don't close that door you got more company.' He said with a smirk. 'Damn it, I knew I should have stayed in bed.' 'Maybe I'll join you' I can't believe who I see walking up the steps. Samantha Foxs a very sexy British woman. I haven't seen her in ages and worth the annoyance of the other company present until I see them walking behind her. Owen, Vyper and worse of all Chill. I get a hug from Samantha Foxs and let everyone else in. Chill for some reason is holding a basketball and has a big smile on his face. 'Okay, what's with the meaning of all this company.' I asked. Chill looks at me and says 'We're going to play basket.....' 'HOCKEY!' Owen interrupts. What the hell? You said we were going to play basketball in this hizzle!' An upset Chill proclaims. 'Do you really think we'd tell you the truth?' Owen asks. 'I hate you guys!' Chill shouts. 'Well I'm up for a game of hockey. Come on lets go close off the street' I said. Owen, Kevin, Samantha, and I head out to do just that. Scott Vyper starts to and stops beside Chill for a moment. 'I just here for two reasons Chill.' 'Oh yeah, what's that?' 'To hit on that piece of ass.' As he points to Samantha's ass as she walks out the door. Both Vyper and Chill are checking out her ass. As she walks out of eyes view Chill looks over at Vyper. 'And the other?' Chill asks. 'To watch you skate!' Vyper proclaims. Scott Vyper goes to join them. Chill mutters 'Skate?' Chill sighs and drops the basketball and joins them. 15 minutes later They managed to close off a side street. There all in hockey gear except Chill whose rebelling against this street hockey game. 'Chill what are you doing? We're starting the game.' Owen says. 'I don't have a pair of skates. Sorry man.' Chill remarks. 'That's all I have an extra pair' Owen replies. Chill mutters something under his breath. 'What was that?' 'Nothing Owen' Another 10 minutes later. Chill is doing is best to stay on his feet. He's grabbing whatever he stay on his feet. There about to pick teams Owen & I are captains. 'I'll take Samantha' Owen Says. She skates over next to Owen and says 'Can I be goalie?' 'Sure' Owen answers. 'I'll take Kevin' I said. Kevin skates over next to me and with two people left I realize what's going to happen. 'Lets see who do I want?' Owen teases. 'Oh you know damn well who you you're going to pick!' I yell. 'Maybe not seeing as how I own you every time we play this might make it challenging.' 'Kind of like how you always beat me at Sock em bop em robots?' I say with a smirk. 'I forgot how you cheated to win that.... I take Scott Vyper!' I turn towards Kevin and see he's already getting suited up to be goalie. Samantha is ready in her net and I see Chill holding himself up with his hockey stick being used as a crutch. Vyper yanks it out from under him and Chills flat on his face. Vyper skates away as we all laugh at poor Chill. 'Speaking of which Grest maybe you should put on a helmet.' Owen says with a bit of concern. 'I never fall.' I reply 'Yeah but after what happened at Wrestlecade.' 'I'll be okay.' I win the face off and I pass it back to Chill whose using the hockey stick as a crutch again. Luckily, it goes straight back to Kevin who passes it back to me. I try skating around Owen and he's all I have to deal with since Scott is attempting to hit on Samantha. Samantha isn't paying attention to him though and has here eyes dead set on me. She's a tomboy of sorts but a very very hot tomboy with big boobs. So you can see why most would want her. I try to go low on her but she knocks it away with her stick. Owen chases it down and has a lot of distance on me but thankfully Kevin stones him cold. There is no rebound because Scott is still flirting and Chill can't skate. I take it and skate by Chill. 'Want the puck Chill?' 'Fuck you homie! They told me basketball! Hockey is for rednecks!' Chill shouts. 'You're such a whiteboy.' Chill looks pissed but he's sort of stranted in that one spot. Owen and I skate around him and the only purpose this serves is to taunt him. Owen poke checks me and knocks the puck over to Vyper who isn't paying attention and it bounces off his skate. Samantha takes control of it passes to Owen who is on another break away and skates pass Chill again and puts it in the net this time. All of a sudden I see Chill skating towards me sort of and by the time I see him its too late and I'm cross checked. My head goes crashing into someone's car door. I'm knocked my ass holding my head in immense pain. Chill skates away as Vyper gives chase. Chill crashes and landes on his ass as Vyper crashes over him. The rest skate up and check on me. 'Are you okay?' Kevin asks I don't answer still holding my head. 'Grest are you okay?' Kevin asks again. 'I'm okay' I reply. I have to be okay. I can't afford to take any time off now. I have so much to prove to Global Wrestling. I will always have something to prove. I know no matter what I do I will always be the man under the radar. Even if I have the belt over my shoulder I will still be underestimated. And you know what? I don't mind that one bit. I've always been the man with the chip on my shoulder. I will be at Evolution..... I will be in the ring against Soma.
