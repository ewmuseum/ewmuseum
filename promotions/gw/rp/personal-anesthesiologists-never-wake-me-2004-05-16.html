?| Personal Anesthesiologists: Never Wake Me |?

16 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5.16.04 :| Heavy breezes cast on thick vapors propelling curtains in dance on sultry winter night. Electrified air sets emotions afire, felt heavy on the skin, with a foreboding of doom, yet without really knowing. As a storm brews on dark horizons, announcing its approach with a light show of electricity. A Low rolling symphony of percussion follows a crescendo portent of violence building. Exciting even at risk of possible destruction. Disaster or promise, merely a twist of fate, flashes of lightening cast flickering shadows; evil gives birth today. |: ?| Personal Anesthesiologists: Never Wake Me |? :| White walls circumvent a slender room as Itagaki hangs suspended by hooks binding his flesh. Oxygen vacant, his body clinging to the hooks dormant, never wishing to be awaken. Crying blue blood, his soul tries to escape but soon on the other side of the bullet proof glass, Biohazard scientist stick their possessed hands through cleansed gloves and shot more chemicals into Itagaki's tegument. |: 'How is the patient doing?' Geoff Hoon verbalized his concerns. ?Is he almost ready? I need him ready for brain surgery at precisely 10 o' clock.' 'He is doing fine. His blood pressure has been lowered to the point where is no longer conscious and so far, his body hasn't been rejecting any of the medicine.' Highly concentrated, the Biohazard dressed scientist responded without taking his eyes away from the task at hand. 'Perfect.' And with that, Geoff Hoon scribbled more evil down into his clipboard while walking away. :| Glasses that hung low with grey hair of wisdom to match his white medical coat, Geoff Hoon was the brightest scientist in his day. But greed filled his heart. Caught trying to test on humans by modifying as he would say, their bodies, he was immediately shunned from the scientific society. As he walked off into the darkness that was his environment, the Biohazard feigns continued to fill Itagaki's soul with the devil. |: 'You think this one will actually work?' One of the Biohazard disguised scientist questioned the other. 'I'm not sure. If not it won't be the first. We'll just throw him in the psychiatric sanctuary with the rest of the loons.' Spoken as if it happened regularly, the other scientist replied casually. 'True. He's better off dead though. Memories of torture, soul destruction, and no past, I almost feel sorry for him.' Peculiarly, the scientist almost sounded like he had sympathy for the guy until: 'He's a looker, how much for him?' The trucker stereotype personified by this human approached one of the scientist with hungry eyes. 'Fifty for five minutes. Any additional time will cost you ten extra per minute. Don't bruise him, Geoff Hoon would kill us if he knew we were doing this.' ---- :| Violated, misused, and abused, Itagaki San laid demented looking touched as he stayed buckled to black leather straps as if he were adhere to them; never to leave them. Hooked up to gadgets that calculated anything and everything, Itagaki's stupefied body found itself once again surrounded by white walls; tainted serenity. Their plugs and scalpels dissecting his brain and altering his anatomy, several scientist clad in white biohazard suits experimented and modified Itagaki. |: 'His brain waves are running abnormally doctor.' Valencia, the 'assistant nurse', attending the monitors lets Geoff aware. 'Lower the chemicals being injected and increase the Demerol.' Geoff replied with the solution. 'But doctor, he could go into cardiac arrest if we increase it anymore.' Concering Geoff of the side effects, Valenica countered. 'Then we'll do heart surgery and replace it. The heart doesn't concern me any. As long as the brain is functioning then that's all that matters.' :| Put through brain surgery and physical exams, Itagaki soon found himself thrown into white pillowed walls that made his cell in an asylum. Screams of abused souls haunted the asylum as they echoed down the halls trying to escape this place. Flickering lights, blood stained walls and floors; this was the ideal place for evil. The roaring thunder rumbled the heavens and shook the walls as lightening soared through the clouds like stomach cramps. Then the rain began to pour, pounding down like quickened drum beats. And there, crouched in a fetus position in the corner, stray-jacket holding him together, his hair screening his pale face, and blue sub-zero like mask covering the lower portion of his face, Itagaki laid there silently. His pain and misery lost, his identity forgotten. |: ----
