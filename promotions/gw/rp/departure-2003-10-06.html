Departure

6 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/6 The wailing engine of a Porsche Carerra GT Turbo can be heard screaming out in misery as the finely tuned piece of German machinery rockets past the two hundred mile an hour mark. It’s matching paint and tint jobs, both of them pitch black, make the car hard enough to see at night, even when it’s not on the verge of achieving flight trajectory. Racing down the interstate, the vehicle meshes seamlessly with the night sky and can only be seen when passing under elevated street lamps, at which point in time it briefly appears as a black flash being carried on chrome rims, dodging raindrops so fast that they spill onto the already moist road in a vertiginous heap. Inside the seemingly rocket propelled vehicle sits Deacon Holmes, who is bobbing his head on pace with the thumping bass of “Bezzle” by T.I. while displaying a veritable cornucopia of reckless driving techniques. Flying at over two hundred miles per hour, driving is no longer a fitting description of what on, is dangerous enough, much less on a wet road at night and randomly cutting people off while doing so. Beside Holmes sits John Zero, who is holding on for dear life while attempting to smoke a cigarette in order to calm his nerves, he is also bleeding rather profusely from a deep gash in his right forearm, his jacket acting as an impromptu sponge. Zero sits in quiet awe as to what’s going on as Holmes flips a switch, activating several black lights outside of eyesight that now illuminate the car’s interior in a bluish purple tint. Staring across at Deacon, John observes his partner as he continues bobbing his head while loudly reciting the song’s lyrics, all the while never missing a beat. “Burnin” up just like in Waco Texas, heatin? up yo necklace, scorchin? up yo afro, tha devil comin? at yo, ass and he hollow pointed for bitches who really want it.” Out of the corner of his eye Deacon spots the miniature river of blood pouring from Zero’s arm and immediately turns the music down. “What tha fuck John!”” exclaims Holmes, momentarily taking his eyes off of the road. In lieu of an actual audible response, Zero simply looks over with one of his patented blank stares. This only provides more fodder for Deacon’s rant. “Don’t look at me like that! You know exactly what I’m talkin” about! You couldn’t get an ace bandage or somethin for that shit on your way out!” You know, like before you got in my car!” Fuck man! That’s Corinthian leather you’re bleeding on!” Continues Holmes, drawing his attention away from the road for an extended period of time. Staring straight ahead, then at Holmes, suddenly John’s eyes open about as wide as they possibly can without popping out from their sockets and rolling down into his lap before falling onto the floor never to be found again. “Now what tha fuck is that look for!” Seriously, you need to do somethin? about that shit man it looks like someone dropped a fuckin? period in here!” Zero points forward, at the road beyond the windshield, which is quickly passing under the vehicle, before finally speaking. “Look at the road Deacon!” he exclaims, as Holmes continues his tirade. “Don’t you try and change the subject! This is some fucked up repugnant shit John! I mean seriously, who tha” hell does shit like that!” Just bleeding all over people’s personal property like that, you must not have any home training...” Continues Holmes, still not concentrating on the road ahead of him... “Deacon the road!” yells Zero, now obviously nervous. “You know what!” When we get to your house, I’m trackin mud ALLLLLLL in your living room... Let’s see how ya like that shit! And another thin...” blasts from Deacon’s mouth in surround sound before abruptly stopping as if the CD inside were scratched as John Zero quickly reaches across the car and snatches the steering wheel out of Deacon’s hand. With one hand on the wheel and the other on the shifter he then screams “Clutch!” as he slams the car down into fourth gear while forcefully whipping the steering wheel to the left, sending the car rocketing forward as it cuts across two lanes, just barely missing a semi truck by mere inches. “Holy shit!” Deacon yells as he regains control of the vehicle. Easing back into the butter soft leather of his seat Holmes inquires “Why tha” hell didn’t you tell me about that shit man? We could’ve died!” he continues. Cocking an eyebrow, John turns to Holmes with a cocky grin. “Oh I’m sorry, I thought I did. Does Deacon, look at the fucking road mean something different in ebonics?” “Fuck you man, next time you just better be careful about how you go grabbin” up on my baby like that. I really think you should apologize.” states Holmes indignantly while stroking the dash and whispering “It’s ok Lucille, Zero’s never gonna hurt you again” to the car while thrusting it into a center lane between two semi trucks as John stands by shaking his head in awe before muttering “You’re a weird kid Deac.” Apparently the near death experience narrowly avoided by the two men no more than a moment ago did nothing in the way of making Deacon’s driving less reckless as the car once again races past the two hundred mile per hour mark. Drops of rain beat down on the car, bead up, and hang on for dear life before being flung off by the windshield wipers like dejected club goers being forcefully “escorted” out the back door by a surly bouncer. Music once again blares inside the car, it’s pounding bass vibrating the innards of the two men, as well as a billboard off to the right and who knows how many feet up in the air as the blacklights inside the car flicker on and off to the beat as the car speeds across three lanes and races down the off ramp. The bass inside the car hits so hard that it’s hard to gather one’s breath and can even be felt in nearby cars on the off ramp. Deacon’s prized possession slowly rolls to a stop at the bottom of the ramp. Once there, Holmes anxiously waits for the light to turn green while bobbing his head to “Boogieman” by Big Gipp ft. Andre 3000 while singing along in an attempt to take his mind of the wait. “B-O-O-G-I-E M-A-N You know the one who make y'all suckas wanna run The one that make you wanna spend time with your son Nobody that I know no longer do this shit for fun I am the... B-O-O-G-I-E M-A-N You know the one who make a suga drop her drawers I dig her cause she's unashamed, she do me just because She wanna and I wanna that's on the niagra falls I am the... B-O-O-G-I-E M-A-N? Once the light changes a bright emerald green Deacon’s eyes light up like two four karat reflecting the blinding brightness of a police officer’s flashlight as he floors the gas while throwing the car into first gear. The car’s tires loosely grip the wet pavement causing the car to fish tail back and forth slightly before shooting off down the road with such speed that it slams Holmes and Zero into their seats and forces them back firmly against the soft leather, which is pulsating due to Deacon’s exorbitantly loud stereo system. After a series of turns down back roads, hairpin turns, and long, winding passageways, the black blur abruptly screeches to a halt at the center of what appears to be nothing more than a long high row of immaculately trimmed hedges. Still parallel to the apparent wall of foliage, the car’s engine once again roars, signaling a set of four screeching tires to form a cloud of thick white smoke before fish tailing then propelling the vehicle right into the center of the hedges at an alarming rate. Just when it seems as though the car is going to slam into the wall of green, it plummets down onto a steeply sloped hidden driveway that looks like it could be something out of an old episode of Knight Rider that was only show in Romania. Rocketing down the sloping tunnel created by paved floor and covered by foliage the car’s tires grip the ground below and squeal as the vehicle is flung around curve after curve. Eventually the tunnel flattens out and the sky can be clearly seen overhead as the walls of foliage have receded. As they further traverse the weaving driveway it begins to elevate once again up to the same level as the street beyond the walls. Once back up to it’s original height, where the paved strip of land leads can be easily distinguished as Zero Estate. The car slows as it approaches the sprawling estate and Holmes lowers the music, which ceases the shaking of the glass windows on Zero’s house. What was once nothing more than a black blur can now be discerned as a Porsche Carerra GT Turbo as the magnificent machine rolls to a stop behind a black Mistubishi Eclipse Spyder that is also parked in front of the stairs leading to the front door. Most likely having hearing the bass from Deacon’s car, John’s assistant Debra pokes her head out of the two large double doors leading into the house before swinging them both wide open to welcome Zero back home. The two men gingerly exit the car obviously exhausted by their prior operation. They walk in each splitting off to one side of Debra, who is standing in the center of the two doors wearing a pinstripe suit jacket, white blouse, and a matching pin striped skirt that just barely meets the middle of her thighs with heels that make her Holmes? height. As they pass, she smiles at Zero, who responds with a simple head nod as Holmes sneaks by without making eye contact but gives her ass a pinch in passing that causes her to tense up in shock before smiling to herself as she closes the door behind the two men. “Can I get you two anything?” she asks while securing the lock before leaving the door. She turns around to see Holmes passed out on the couch with his legs hanging over the arm rest and Zero nowhere to be found. Moments later he returns from one of the hallways leading into the back to the house with a small plastic box containing first aid equipment. John takes a seat atop a stool and places the box on the counter in front of him. “Looks like it’s time for me to play medic again.” says Debra with a smile as she approaches Zero. As she opens up the box and begins fishing out the needle to stitch up the gash in John’s forearm he removes a small black cell phone from a clip on his belt and flips it open with a flip of the wrist before pressing it up against his ear. Debra begins stitching the wound closed as Zero begins his phone conversation, not even flinching as the needle is forced through his flesh. Looking over at Holmes, who is passed out on the couch, John begins speaking into the phone. “Yeah... Soma this is John... Well, I was thinking about taking a little time off for R&R... yeah, looks like the weary are finally gonna get a little rest... What”... Yeah, that sounds great... See you in a few.” With that said John claps the phone shut and leans against the counter as Debra continues sewing him up. Moments later the shrubs and tree branches beyond the houses? windows begin to sway in a strong wind that seemingly came out of nowhere. Suddenly there is a knock at the door which wakes Holmes, he rolls off of the couch and lumbers over to the front door. After looking through the peep hole Deacon identifies the man on the other side as Leinad Soma and welcomes him in. “In order to speed our departure I’ve taken the liberty of having suitcases prepared with everything you two will need for our trip, including clothes.” Soma informs the two men while removing his coat and hanging it at the door. “Our trip” Where the hell are we going? ...and what kinda clothes are we talkin here? I’m not wearin? a suit, it’s bad enough I have to wear one around Capo... I’ll stay home damnit!” Inquires Holmes loudly, still shaking off his fatigue. “Well, John contacted me and informed me that you two were interested in a little R&R so I suggested we all head to Japan and pay your new house a little visit. And as far as clothes go don’t worry, I’ve got you covered, Sean John, Enyce, Mecca, Jordan tennis shoes, it’s all inside packed.” He informs Holmes, causing him to relax his posture and return to the couch. He then turns to John before telling him “And for you I’ve got a bagged stuffed full of Gap clothing.” before cracking a smile as Deacon erupts with laughter from his seat on the couch while Zero cocks a concerned eyebrow at the two men. “Kidding of course, but none the less, you’re all taken care of and as soon as you’re done receiving medical treatment we can be on our way.” “Road trip!” exclaims Holmes as he rushes into the back of the house, most likely to catch a shower while Soma waits for John to finish getting stitched up.
