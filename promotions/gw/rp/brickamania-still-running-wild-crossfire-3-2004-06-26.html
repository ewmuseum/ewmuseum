Brickamania still running wild (Crossfire #3)

26 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/26/04 (The arena is packed. People have filled it to capacity for this Global Wrestling live event. The fans are getting restless. The T-shirt guy has just gotten through shooting T-shirts into the crowd. It has been ten minutes between matches so far. Just then, a familiar tune hits the PA. The tune, to everyone's surprise, is 'Real American'. People rise to their feet and turn to the entrance awaiting some big surprise. All of a sudden, who should make his entrance but... Brick, dressed in full Hulk Hogan drag. He wears a skull cap with blond hair on the sides. He runs to each end of the stage, doing the famous 'hand to the ear' taunt that made Hogan famous. Around his head is a yellow headband. He also wears a sleeveless yellow t-shirt with the word 'Brickamania' written in red text. Around his waist is a yellow weight belt. He has on yellow trunks and yellow boots with red laces.) When it comes crashing down and it hurts inside You gotta take a stand, it don't help to hide (Brick struts down the ramp, pumping his fists. He stops once and does the hand to the ear taunt again.) If you hurt my friends, then you hurt my pride I gotta be a man- I can't let it slide I am a real American Fight for the rights of every man I am a real American Fight for what's right,- fight for your life! (Brick climbs into the ring and goes around, doing the taunt on each side of the Octagon. All eight sides. He then goes into a series of flexing poses. Brick then calls for a microphone, which he receives.) I feel strong about right and wrong And I don't take trouble for very long I got something deep inside of me Courage is the thing that keeps us free! I am a real American Fight for the rights of every man I am a real American Fight for what's right, fight for your life! Fans: HOGAN! HOGAN! HOGAN! HOGAN! Brick (in best Hulk Hogan voice): Well, ya know somethin', maniacs?! (The fans let out a huge cheer.) Brick: Seems there's this dude Umbrage, who's been goin' all around, flappin' his gums about what a bad dude he is! He wants to talk about how he's gonna Well, let me tell you somethin', brother! Brickamania lives, dude! Fans: HOGAN! HOGAN! HOGAN! HOGAN! Brick: Brickamania has been runnin' wild for years and there ain't been no stoppin' it, brother! It's all about the prayers, the training and the vitamins! Now, I'll admit, you've had some pretty good matches, Umbrage, but our match at Crossfire ain't gonna be one of 'em! Fans: HOGAN! HOGAN! HOGAN! HOGAN! Brick: Cause lemme explain to ya just what ya did! Ya started a fire and brother, that kinda fire And come Crossfire, with all my Brickamaniacs behind me, and these 24-inch pythons... Brick flexes his left bicep. Brick: the only question that's gonna need to be answered is: whatcha gonna do, brother? Brick then begins to rip his shirt off. Yes, the famous shirt ripping routine. Brick: WHATCHA YA GONNA DO...WHEN HULKAMANIA...RUNS WILD...ON...YOU ?!?!?!?!??!?!?!?!?!?! Brick throws the torn shirt down on the mat and begins to flex as 'Real American' hits the PA again. Just then, Brick woke up on the couch in his hotel room with a jerk. Brick: AAAHH! Aw, damn, not again! I thought I was over this! Just then, there's a knock at his door. Brick groggily rises from the couch and heads for the door. Brick: Who? Voice: Housekeeping. Brick opens the door to see a housekeeper. Housekeeper: Would you like some fresh towels? Brick: Hmm? Yeah, yeah. Brick takes the towels from the housekeeper. He rubs his eyes some as he takes them Housekeeper: Are you all right, sir? Brick: Yeah, I'm fine. Just had a bad dream. Housekeeper: Well, if you need anything... Brick: Thanks. Brick closes the door and starts humming 'Real American'. He then stops himself, mid-song. Brick: I'm really sick of that song.
