Date: 4-16-03 We see Brick, Aaron and his group DMAK coming out of a recording studio. They’ve just gotten through laying down some tracks for their sophomore album. They’re looking to surpass the success of the first album. Right now, they’re taking a break for lunch. Brick: OK, fellas, that was dynamite. Few more tracks and we’ll have this album done. Damien: Well? Go ahead and tell him. Brick: Go ahead and tell me what? Keith: The fellas and I had an idea for a song we wanted to add to the album. Aaron: Really? Andre: Yeah, it’s a cover. Brick: We already have one cover song on the album. “Two Occasions”, remember? I thought we established the one cover song per album rule. Keith: Well, the song we wanna do might change your mind. Aaron: What song is it? Keith: “Secret Garden”. Brick: Old joint Q did with Al B. Sure, James Ingram, El Debarge and Barry White? Marcus: Yeah. Aaron: Man, I used to dig that song. It was playing the night Felicia and I first? Brick: Yo, way too much information, man. Aaron: Oh, don’t hate. Brick: Guys, let’s just hear the song. Then we can decide.
