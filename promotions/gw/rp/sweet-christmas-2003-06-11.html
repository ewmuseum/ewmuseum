Sweet Christmas!

11 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/11/03 Brick sits at the computer, checking his e-mail. Since Dead Zone, Brick has been taking things easy, mostly on Christina McCoy’s orders. He’s also been doing some reflecting since losing to Sage Sabbot. He’s biding his time until he can return to settle the score with Sabbot and finally win the World title. He finds an e-mail from his friend Aaron. The subject reads “This might interest you.” Normally, Brick would delete e-mails with a subject title like this, as it may contain a virus, but since it was from Aaron, he decided to click on it. The message reads: “Hey, Brick! Remember when you were thinking about going out for the Luke Cage movie” Well? http://filmforce.ign.com/articles/422/422645p1.html http://filmforce.ign.com/articles/422/422854p1.html Thought you’d like to know. Aaron.” Brick clicks on the first link, which brings him to a website with an article that reads: Luke Cage: Uncaged! Yet another Marvel pic in development. June 05, 2003 - According to The Hollywood Reporter, Columbia Pictures has snagged the screen rights to the Marvel Comics superhero Luke Cage, Hero for Hire for development as a feature film. The studio behind Spider-Man and Ghost Rider has tapped scribe Ben Ramsey (The Big Hit) to pen the pic. Cage was previously in development at New Line with John Singleton attached to direct, and then later director Hype Williams and star LL Cool J. Cage will be produced by Marvel Studios' honcho Avi Arad and Original Films' Neal Moritz (2F2F). (I wonder if this means that Moritz's Furious leading man Vin Diesel isn't far behind to portray Cage, a.k.a. Power Man?) The pic follows Luke Cage, 'a former gang member who is framed for a crime. In prison, he volunteers for a medical experiment that goes awry, giving him super strength and bulletproof skin. Using his newfound powers, Cage escapes and becomes a hero for hire. Cage's archenemy is Willy Stryker, a former childhood friend from Harlem. The two committed petty crimes together, and both fell for the same woman, Reva Connors, which led to their eventual falling out. It was Stryker who framed Cage for a drug deal that sent him to prison.' Cage's comic book partner-in-crimefighting, Iron Fist, is set up at Artisan Entertainment as his own film project (currently stalled in development hell). -- Stax A while ago, Brick had been rumored to be in the running for the title role when it was still in what film insiders and Internet buffs refer to as “development hell.” There was even the chance that the movie would never get made. So, he concentrated on his wrestling and managing DMAK. But in the back of his mind, he still held on to the idea of playing Luke Cage. When he clicks on the second link, it leads him to: Vin Diesel: Hero for Hire? The latest grist from the rumor mill. June 06, 2003 - When The Hollywood Reporter broke the news yesterday that Columbia Pictures was going to team with Neal Moritz's Original Film to bring Marvel Comics' Luke Cage, Hero for Hire to the big-screen, I wondered whether Moritz's xXx and Fast and the Furious star Vin Diesel might not be in the running to portray the titular superhero. According to an update yesterday at 4Filmmakers, Diesel is indeed attached to star in Cage. Please note, however, that 4Filmmakers once reported that Diesel and Moritz were teaming for the comic book movie The Flash, which turned out to be false. Diesel has been rumored for a number of genre flicks he never ended up making, including Daredevil, Terminator 3, Hellboy and King Conan. Still, after all the studio-generated hype surrounding Diesel's 'arrival' in last summer's xXx, the conventional wisdom is that Diesel needs a sure-fire hit in order to justify that hype. His last two releases ? the much delayed Knockaround Guys and A Man Apart ? weren't especially successful. Even xXx was considered by some to have under-performed. Nowadays, comic book movies seem a sure thing so perhaps Vin really will portray Luke Cage. So what do you think of Vin as Luke Cage? - Stax “OH HELL NO!!” Brick shouts. Brick then clicks “reply” and starts to type away. “Vin fucking Diesel” What are they smoking? Have any of these cats actually ever read a comic book with Luke Cage in it? Diesel doesn’t even look like Cage! I’m not exactly Sidney or Denzel, but I could probably do a better job of playing Luke Cage than Diesel. He’s too beige! Either me, LL, or Ving Rhames, but not this cat.” Brick then clicks “Send”. He reads further into the site and finds the latest reader comment on the story. It reads: I certainly hope that Vin Diesel is not going to portray Luke Cage. There are very few African American heroes in contemporary mythology and to cast Vin Diesel just because he is ethnic would be unfortunate. Yes, the liberty of changing races for characters has been taken before as it was with the Kingpin, but then again when has it ever been clich? in Hollywood to villainize black men? In a world where movies like Hollywood Homicide continue to perpetuate black men as criminals and where high school age kids are inundated with hoods like 50 Cent glorifying negative lifestyles what everyone needs right now is a definitively black super-hero. (And no Spawn didn't count!) Not to mention Vin is not a great actor unless he is getting killed off (ex: Saving Private Ryan). On a side note do you know what the stylistic approach to this film will be? Cage has always been a bit of a blaxploitative character, which is fine by me. I wouldn't mind if they took the Shaft-like approach to Cage as long as they pulled it off properly. A modern approach would be fine as well. Regards, [name withheld] P.S. Oh and Stax, Steel sucked and Blade was OK, but hardly the black heroic figure I'm talking about. Power Man has gotta be a brotha. Maybe someone like Tyrese could pull it off. He doesn't look much like the character, but if they are considering Vin Diesel perhaps they would go for the new wave of 'hip' black actors. I'm sure after 2 Fast 2 Furious' undeserved success Tyrese will seem more viable. I also think Jon Singelton should reconsider directing it. This brings a smile to Brick’s face and causes an idea to formulate in his mind.
