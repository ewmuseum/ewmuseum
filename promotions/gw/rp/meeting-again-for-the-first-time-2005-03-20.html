Meeting Again For The First Time

20 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/20/05 'Is that all, senorina?' the well-mannered waiter asked, to which Mindy Mercer nodded her head politely. 'Yes, I'm not very hungry today. Thanks anyway, though.' The courteus smile the young woman gave was enough to make the waiter beam with joy. Mindy's chipper exterior made her out to be an overly optimistic woman. However, inside, happiness was ditched in favor of something compeltely different; a bile, ravenous sense of despair that wrenched the pit of her tiny stomach into numerous intangible knots. If only there was a way to stop herself from giving in to her father's orders... 'If only Mikey was around, he'd know what to do.' Mindy hadn't seen her cousin ever since Valentine's Day, which was practically a week ago. She was so distraught that her mind just wasn't on wrestling- and even when she had been requested to compete for the Cruiserweight title on the final show before High Octane, Mindy declined. She didn't know why, either, but she felt that half of her negativity came from fear of how her father would react if he saw her wrestling yet again. It was weird... she hated the man's guts with every fiber of her being, but even from across the globe, his mere voice still held power- through every single word. It irritated her to the point where she could barely eat. Upon ordering her wine and pasta, Mindy looked out into the street as the refreshing cold air gave a gentle breeze. It had been so long since she had gotten to eat at a fancy outdoor Italian restaurant. 'The last time I was here I was with dad,' she remembered. That thought, coupled with the wind that picked up ever so slightly, it gave her chills. Spending time with her father... how could she have been so crazy? As she lamented her past days in Italy, the waiter returned with Mindy's glass of red wine. 'Merlot,' he confirmed as he placed the glass down in front of the young woman. Amidst the sad memories that returned to haunt her, she looked up at the handsome young waiter and smiled. 'Grazie,' she answered, before the man bounded off once again. 'Why does he always have to take away from me when I try to get something good going?' That was one of the only questions she could bring herself to ask as she stared up into the clear blue skies that hung over her hometown of Verona. It had been a little over a year since she left, but it only felt like a day to her. How couldn't it, if her father continued to give her grief well after she had left home? Mindy had to make sure everything was settled if she wanted to continue wrestling, if she wanted to be able to live a normal life without the disapproval of her dad. She would plan those methods out, indeed. And she did so as she ate her tortellini and sipped her sweet Merlot wine. Even Mindy had to admit- Italy did have better food than what she ate in New York, not to mention New Mexico. Mindy loved to eat, even if her slender body didn't show it. 'I love this place,' she mumbled to herself in between little nibbles of pasta, 'so why must I feel so upset to be here?' As Mindy pondered that question, she was jolted to hear the empty chair across from her being pulled back. Mindy quickly glanced away from her meal, and sighed in relief that it wasn't her father. 'Er, hi,' Mindy started meekly. She had never seen the person before, and was so surprised that she forgot that he might not even know what she had just said. Mindy opened her mouth to speak in Italian, but the man replied before she could. 'Hey,' he answered. A mess of stringy black hair concealed his face; only one startling blue eye was left visible, along with the pair of lips that remained curled tight- almost as if he was just as curious as Mindy was as to what the person in front of them had to say. In the middle of the silence, Mindy took a brief moment to look the stranger over. Black clothes, silver finger armor, big leather boots... 'All he needs is a large hat and I'd mistake this guy for Markus...' 'So I saw you on television,' he continued. With the one eye that Mindy could see through his black mane, he either looked deprived of sleep or just doped out. Mindy prayed it wasn't the latter. 'I figured it was about time I meet you, cause like.. technically we're family and all, sorda, well.. yeah.' At that point, Mindy was truly freaked out. 'Family? Did I miss something?' She was ready to simply dismiss the young man as a drug addict, but he wasn't about to let her go that easily. Not that he seemed angry or violent, however- in fact, he let out a chuckle at Mindy's cluelessness. 'I see Michael never told you about me,' he nodded. 'I assume you lived with him, right? I mean.. considering you've never been at your parents' house or anything.' Mindy slowly slid her chair back as if to stand up, but she couldn't bring herself to. Something about him... could he really be telling the truth? 'How would you know that?' she stammered back, worried for the number of potential answers. Even though the one she got was plausible, she still didn't understand it at all. Maybe it was the jumbled bits of information hitting her too fast, or perhaps something else that simply slipped by. 'Because I kinda lived with them for about a year. Yeah, around a year, I think that's when I finished school. Don't live there now, though.' The man seemed a bit annoyed getting into such a conversation, but since it seemed that Mindy needed to know, he didn't bother holding it back. He didn't know whether to be further upset that she didn't recognize him, or that Michael told her nothing about him. 'Your uncle, Chris?' he spoke again, not giving the girl much, if any time to reply to the 'question'. 'He adopted me about, I'd say, four years ago... about five or six months before he passed away. I lived with his nephew Michael after that, wrestled a little bit, and then, well... then I came here.' The man shrugged as he inhaled loudly. Slowly but surely, Mindy allowed the story to sink in. However, something didn't sit right with her. 'So... how did my parents exactly let a stranger like you into their house?' she questioned. In her mind, there was just no way her father would have let this guy into his house- not without taking a number of drug tests and cleaning out the more than obvious black dye in his hair. The person in question looked down at himself, and smirked. 'Heh, well, I didn't always look like this either. But you know, your father was a real pain in the ass, so.. I just felt like it'd be best that I return the favor.' Mindy shook her head; she was still quite confused about what was going on. As if she didn't have enough problems regarding family, now she had to deal with a cousin that she didn't know she had. But then again... 'Michael didn't exactly know that I existed until I came to New York to meet him... maybe this guy's telling the truth after all. He sure does have my dad's personality down, anyway.' 'Heh, so he was a dick to you, too, huh?' Mindy asked. She was a bit more relaxed than earlier, that was for sure. 'Sadly, yes,' he smiled in response, as he pushed away some of the black hair that obscured his face. In result, an almost childlike face was revealed; one that almost made Mindy fall out of her chair. For someone as old as he was, he looked like he belonged back in high school. A harmless smile topped off the number of innocent features that almost went undetected by the punked out look he had gone with. 'What exactly could you have done to piss him off?' she inquired next, playing with one of her pink and black pigtails with one hand as the other clutched her wine glass. 'Eh, apparently I'm too dumb because I couldn't get perfect grades. That led to the alcohol, and uh, I don't think he was too happy when he met my ah...' Just as he attempted to finish his sentence, a red-haired man who looked about as old as him and was decked out in similar goth-like attire had grabbed an empty chair from a nearby table and set it down next to Mindy's newfound relative. He then took a seat and placed a hand on the other's shoulder. 'Am I missing an interesting conversation, or am I just in time to see the flirting begin?' Mindy watched in surprise as her cousin turned and ruffled the other man's red hair. 'Never,' he laughed, 'besides, cousin on cousin action isn't my cup of tea.' Mindy's cheeks almost took on the color of the wine she had sipped. The shocks just kept on coming. 'I was just about to mention you, though,' he continued, 'Mindy, this is my, um.. partner in crime, so to speak, eh heh.' The red haired man grinned and extended a hand, fingernails dabbed in black polish. 'He puts it so nicely, doesn't he?' he asked Mindy as he tried not to laugh. 'Dante's the name.' Mindy put down her Merlot and shook Dante's hand. 'Mindy,' she greeted, before she then drew back her hand and slid her chair closer to the table. By then, she had actually started to feel better. 'So,' Dante began, 'what were you guys talkin' about?' He leaned back in his seat, as if he looked ready to fall asleep at any moment as he waited for a reply, which he eventually got from his companion next to him. 'Oh, we were just talking about the wonderful soul of Mindy's father,' he grinned evilly, to which Dante leaned toward the table and began to laugh loudly- so loud that the few people eating at the surrounding tables had looked over in his direction. 'The only thing wonderful about him is that he's apparently devout to the church. Too bad he's such a prude about religion that he can't care about the things that are supposed to be close to him. A pity, it is.' Mindy quickly nodded her head in agreement. She felt embarassed to know that it was her father they were talking about, but she couldn't say that they were wrong in doing so, either. Her father was an arrogant asshole, who deserved every mean-spirited remark he recieved, and not once did Mindy ever think she would feel remorse for it. And to that day, she never did. 'Well, I've told you why he hates me,' her cousin began again, 'what made him drive you halfway across the world?' Mindy sighed at the question; such a long story. Lucky for her, however, she found an easyw ay to sum it up into one word. 'Everything.' The remark was simple enough to have the two men glance at eachother and start laughing once more. 'What?' Mindy giggled. 'It's true!' 'Hehe.. so what brings you back to Italy?' her nameless relative inquired next, to which Mindy's tone of voice grew sorrowful. 'Bleh, he found out where I had been staying,' she answered lowly, 'wants me back home.' The black haired boy frowned at his younger cousin and leaned forward, bringing one arm across the table to place a comforting hand on Mindy's shoulder. 'So you plan on going back home because of that?' Mindy couldn't bring herself to actually say yes, and instead she just nodded her head. 'Do you want to go home?' he asked next, to which Mindy quickly shook her head no in defense. 'I sort of understand I guess,' he continued, 'but you shouldn't have to worry about what he wants. You should be worrying more about yourself and what you want to do!' He did have a point, and it was enough of a point to get Mindy to nod her head again. 'I just get so worried,' she murmured, 'I'm just afraid that Mikey doesn't care.. and that if I don't go to my dad, then I'll have no one.' Both men frowned at Mindy's statement, and her cousin picked up on it. Turning to his acquaintance, he patted him on the head. 'Head back to the car,' he whispered, 'I'll be there in a minute, alright?' Dante nodded. 'I understand.' With that, he quickly kissed the black haired man on the cheek and scampered off to a jet black car in the distance. 'I know what you mean,' he said as he turned back to Mindy. 'I felt the exact same way after Chris died... shit, I felt that way when my actual parents died... and I know it's not a good way to think about Michael but hehe, he's always like that. It's nothing personal.' As he stood up from his chair, he looked down and smiled at his cousin, who gazed back up at him in wonder. 'Don't worry about your dad. Sooner or later, he's going to understand. I promise. So go back to Michael... he probably cares more than you know.' Slowly, a comforted smile formed on Mindy's young, pale face. 'Thank you... cuz.' The boy smiled and shrugged his shoulders. 'Anytime. We're family, after all.' He then turned to leave. However, before he was out of sight... 'Hey!' Mindy called out to him, which caused him to turn back around. 'Yes?' 'I never caught your name.' The boy smiled and scratched his head. 'I'm sure I'll get time to formally introduce myself to you sooner or later. You have other things to tend to.' Before Mindy could protest his comment, he was gone. He was right, however. She did have important things to tend to, like getting back to her cousin. The only problem, though, was whether or not her cousin would be waiting for her when she got back.
