Like Father Like Son

20 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20th January Death... It is a morbid subject isn't it? No one likes to talk about it because no one likes to admit to themselves that they have a deadline on this place we call earth. The biggest question of course is what is after death, what awaits us when we close our eyes for the last time? There are many religions and beliefs about what happens in the after life, for example according to Buddhism there is no after life in the sense that the person is reincarnated and comes back as another species. Christianity in all shapes and forms believes that when we pass on we arrive at a place called heaven where we experience paradise with God; however this is only if you have led a good life, if you haven't then you end up going to a place called hell where you will be tortured for all eternity. Personally I don't believe in anything after death, I believe that you die and that is it. Depressing I know but it doesn't worry me.... You see the reason I am talking about death and the after life is because I want to talk about life. Wait a minute why talk about death when you want to talk about life, I hear you ask? Well it is simple, you see many religions believe that your after life is judged by your living life. As I said before if you lead a good life then you will be rewarded in the after life but if you do not then you are doomed. All of us in the GW play with our mortality every time we go out to the ring and so it makes you think what if something goes wrong? It is too late for us to repent our sins as what we do is a sin in itself; we raise our fists against our fellow man. Even though we do it as our job we are, in the eyes of most religions, sinners. That is why I do not worry about death and the after life because in my hearts of hearts I do not believe there is anything waiting for me. What we do in our lives is what counts and the memories we leave behind is our after life. I have said this once before, all you need is for someone to remember you and in a way you will live forever. However there is a memory that I wish to be forgotten, eradicated if possible and that is my match at Reload. You are probably wondering why I want to forget about a match where I successfully defended my Cruiserweight Title. It is not the match itself that I wish to forget, as Curtis was a great adversary and a great competitor. It is the diabolic act that happened during the match that I wish to forget. For those who do not know what I am talking about let me refresh your memory? A new tag team called The 138 recently entered the world of Global Wrestling. They had a reasonable match at Reload but obviously it did not quench their thirst for action as during the middle of mine and Curtis? match they came charging down and attacked Curtis. Now why do I have a problem with this? Well I fight under one simple rule and that is no interference. Even if the interference is to attack my opponent I do not accept it. It undermines the whole point of a match, which is for competitors to fight until one of them comes out victorious. If someone interferes then it destroys the whole concept of the match. There is no honour in the victory if someone interferes, in a sense it makes the victory an empty victory. This is what The 138 turned my victory into at Reload. Now everyone will think that I won because of them and not because of a hard fought match between me and Curtis. Thankfully I get some retribution at the next Evolution when me and El Serpenti take on those disgraceful fighters. We will show them true honour and true victory? Click! Click! “Come on light you damn thing!” Click! Finally the lighter lit and I placed the wick of the candle into the flame. The candle burned with a warming glow as I placed it by my side in the sand. I was sitting alone in the Las Vegas? desert staring up at the night sky, letting my thoughts flow through my mind. Before you say anything, no I haven’t lost it and no I have not joined some sort of cult! I did this every year on the 19th January, the date of my father’s birthday. He would have been 60 years old this year. For those of you who do not know, I never knew my father he died of cancer weeks before I was born. I only got to know him through my mother’s stories and only ever saw pictures of him. I never got to visit his grave, even when I was given my bio-suit my mother would not allow it as it would be too upsetting for her. So instead every year on his birthday I would wander out into the wilderness and light a candle in his memory. I would spend the whole night there whilst the candle burned away, thinking of my father. I always thought of my dad as a strong man, not like he was muscular but he had a strong will. My mother often told me that he would not back down from an argument and hated anyone who was too scared to stand up to him. I often wondered where my determination came from and often thought that me and my dad would have got on famously. I don’t think he would have agreed with my job but that is only because he was some sort of businessman and he preferred intellectual conflict rather than physical. Even though I do not believe in God, I do believe that my father lives within me and that through his characteristics he is still alive within me. I fell back onto the towel I placed behind me and continued to stare at the stars. I glanced over at the candle as the flame flickered with the wind. I sighed and let myself drift away? “It’s a beautiful day isn’t it son?” “Sure is dad!” I said smiling at my dad who looked across at me from the other side of the bench. We were sat on a bench in the middle of this beautiful garden. The sun was shining and the flowers were blooming wonderful colours such as blues, yellows and reds. The air was so fresh and there wasn’t a cloud in the sky. You couldn’t help but smile at such a wonderful day. “What you smiling at?” My dad chuckled. “It is just such a wonderful day, don’t you think?” I asked looking at my dad’s aging face. The worry lines and wrinkles beginning to show his 60 years of life. He still had that youthful sparkle in his dark blue eyes but his face and grey hair told a different story. “It certainly is but I don’t think that is all you are smiling about.” My dad looked at me with those soul piercing eyes and I laughed. “All right, all right, it isn’t just the day. Life is just good at the moment dad. I mean I have my own family now who I love dearly and I am at the top of my game. Apart from that incident at Reload I have been on top form.” “That whole Reload thing still bothering you?” My dad asked as he put his arm around me reassuringly. “It just wasn’t right dad! Corvin and Ekx had no right to be there. Now everyone is going to think that I didn’t deserve the victory because Curtis was jumped and I took advantage of it, but I didn’t! I was down when those guys came barging in! Which again doesn’t look very good for me either does it?” “Come on now Adam,” My dad said tapping me on my back, “When has a Myers man ever cared what anyone else thinks” You are your own man remember? This is what you dreamed of doing. I mean you didn’t even listen to your mother, so if you don’t care what your own mother thinks why should you care about anyone elses opinion?” “It is because this is my job dad! I need to be looked upon as a professional not some underhanded snake like Corvin or Ekx. I didn’t even know anything about the interference but everyone will think that I did and so will lose whatever respect they had for me.” “I understand Adam. When I was climbing up the corporate ladder, I wanted to be seen as a hard working man, not someone who would cut corners just so he could go home early or delegate any work that was handed to him. I understand it is tough to keep the image you want of yourself but I am sure that you are underestimating the rest of the roster. They probably know you had nothing to do with what happened at Reload but if they do then you will put it straight at Evolution won’t you?” “Yeah, you’re right dad, you always are,” I said as I looked at my dad who was smiling a heart warming smile, I chuckled, “You know, I always thought you would have hated me for wrestling for a living.” “Well I will be honest with you, Adam, I still don’t like it,” He said looking at me with a stern look but it soon melted away as he smiled again, “But I wouldn’t be much of a father if I didn’t support my son now would I?” We both laughed and looked off into the distance watching the blue birds swoop and chirp in the sky. I turned to my dad who continued to watch the birds almost hypnotised by their movement. “I almost forgot,” I said snapping my father out of his trance, “It is someone’s birthday today isn’t it?” “Oh don’t start,” He said looking at me with a sarcastic yet firm look, “I don’t want you making a big deal.” “But you are the big six zero” Surely you want to celebrate?” I joked being just as sarcastic. “It is not a big deal, it is just another year.” “Okay then, I guess you don’t want this present I got for you?” “Present?” My dad’s eyes sparkled with a 5 year olds? delight; I knew that would get him excited. I leaned over to the side of the bench and picked up the present from the ground. Its golden wrapping sparkled in the sun as I handed it to my dad who quickly began to tear into it. I could see his smile grow wider and wider as each bit of paper flew over his shoulders. Eventually he reached the box and quickly opened it to reveal a beautifully crafted Gucci watch. My dad’s eyes welled up with tears as he looked at me. “Oh son that is fantastic, thank you.” He said as he hugged me tightly. “I always saw you as a Gucci man.” I laughed. “Only the best, huh?” My dad smiled as he placed it around his wrist. “It makes you think doesn’t it?” “Makes you think about what?” I asked. “How little time we have.” “How do you mean?” Suddenly the scenery around us changed from a gorgeous beautiful day to pitch black apart from a circle of light around me and my dad. My dad’s expression had changed his face was now sombre and serious. He grabbed me by my arms and looked me directly in my eyes. “You will take care of yourself and your family won’t you?” “What” What are?”” “Promise me Adam! There isn’t any time!” “But?” “Promise me!” My dad was now screaming at me. “Okay, okay I promise.” I replied feeling confused at the whole situation. My dad then looked at me with a tear rolling down his cheek, he said something but I couldn’t hear him but by the movement of his mouth it looked as if he said “I love you!” but all I could hear was a ringing noise. RING! My dad began to drift away, I reached out to grab him but he was too far. “DAD!” I screamed at the top of my voice. RING! “DAD!” RING! “DAD!” RING! I jerked awake and looked to my side to see my mobile phone ringing with the display saying “Pete”. I took a second before flipping my phone open and answering. “Pete” What is it?” I asked still feeling quite groggy. “Richard Wood.” “What about him?” “I know who he is!” “Who?” There was a silence as I heard Pete take a deep breath. “Pete?” I asked now worried at Pete’s silence. “We need to talk?”
