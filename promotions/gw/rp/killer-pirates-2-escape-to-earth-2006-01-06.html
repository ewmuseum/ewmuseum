Killer Pirates #2: Escape to Earth

6 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/06/06 Continued from where Part 1 left off? ESCAPE FROM CASTLE GAYSKULL [After the confrontation in the dungeon of Castle Gayskull, the Gaylord of the Universe and his friends, the Man with Purse, the Carpet licking Ballerina (CLB), and the Body Cavity Search Police Officer have escorted their new acquaintances the Circus of DOOM~! and the Horny Mathematician up to their base at the top of Castle Gayskull where they look out into the wondrous land of Eternia and see the minions of Heterosexuality and their minions of homosexuality. All of the stars are aligned for a war between all things gay and straight in Eternia. Gaylord looks out into his land and shakes his head.] Gaylord: Straightor is out to get me this time. Body Cavity Search Police Officer: But why? He isn’t like us. He has another orientation, so to speak. But why? Why now? Gaylord: ? [Gaylord ponders for a moment.] Gaylord: He must’ve sensed a tremblor in the pathways of sexuality, just like I have. [Darth von Doom and the Mathematician exchange wary looks.] Darth von Doom: Tremblor in the pathways of sexuality? [Gaylord nods his head.] Gaylord: Yes. [Darth von Doom tries to keep from laughing.] Darth von Doom: Everyone knows that the powers of sexuality are insignificant in comparison to the powers of the Force. Gaylord: How so? [Darth von Doom pulls out his crimson Sith lightsaber and ignites it.] Darth von Doom: Because I can kill you with this. [Gaylord gets turned on and turns around to hide his’um, turn on. Darth von Doom and the Mathematician slowly start backing out of the room. The minions of Homosexuality go over to check on Gaylord as the Circus of DOOM~! sneaks out.] CLB: That guy is crazy! Man with Purse: I think he’s cute. [The Police Officer nods his head.] Body Cavity Search Police Officer: Yeah. I’d love to search his cavities. [Once Gaylord is calmed down, they look around and see that the Circus has escaped from them.] Gaylord: Damn that Straightor! CLB: What? Gaylord: Don’t you see. He sent his newest minions over here to distract us. I knew it! Body Cavity Search Police Officer: One things for sure. We all have to worry about Darth von Doom now. [Gaylord nods his head.] Gaylord: True that. * * * THE PIRATES Meanwhile? [In the Castle of Straightor, the Killer Pirates are having a much easier time talking with Straightor and the Homophobic Priest now that they’ve found out about the Cosmic Rod. They are all sitting at the Table of Straightness and are talking about things in a casual manner. The only problem is Straightor wants to barter for it so he can take over Castle Gayskull and make everyone straight.] Straightor: But the universe needs for me to do this. Jack: But why, mate? Straightor: If not me, then who else? [Jack raises an eyebrow.] Jack: What are you talking about? [Straightor smiles.] Straightor: I’m talking about using that Cosmic Rod thing to create a portal into Castle Gayskull and take over the place. [Jack has a little smirk on his face.] Jack: What? Like a pirate plundering a lab to find a portal maker? [Straightor nods his head.] Straightor: Yes. Jack: Then I think you’re more of a pirate than we once thought, right clown? [The “Killer Klown from Outer Space” nods his head.] KKFOS: Yesiando. Straightor: Then you should give me the Cosmic Rod so I can save the universe from the Gaylord and all of his propaganda. [Straightor eyes the Cosmic Rod.] Straightor: Or, better yet, I can plunder it myself! [Straightor grabs the Cosmic Rod and holds it up.] Straightor: (cackles) Behold! The power of the Cosmic Rod is MINE! ALL MINE! [Just then, Jack swipes it from him.] Jack: I think not, mate. We need it to get home. [Straightor snatches it from Jack.] Straightor: But I need it to invade Castle Gayskull. [Straightor grabs the Cosmic Rod and he and Jack start fighting over it. The KKFOS gets up out of his chair and growls. He goes over, grabs the Cosmic Rod from the both of them, and then threatens to eat Straightor, who backs away as the Killer Pirates make their getaway out of his Castle. Straightor turns to the Homophobic Priest.] Straightor: Damn that Gaylord! Homophobic Priest: Those two’we should’ve known they weren’t playing on our team! [Straightor growls and looks out his window at Castle Gayskull.] Straightor: That Gaylord has ruled that castle for far too long. Now, it will be the Age of Straightor! Homophobic Priest: He’s going to hell. [Straightor cackles.] Straightor: And I am going to Heaven’also known as Castle Gayskull! [They both laugh evilly.] Straightor: Now we must find that blasted Cosmic Rod. (looks around) Now where’d those Pirates go? * * * ESCAPE FROM ETERNIA Meanwhile? [The Killer Pirates and the Circus of DOOM~! run for their lives as they reach the same spot in the direct middle of Eternia. They stare at each other for a moment.] Jack: Get us the hell out of here, mate! [Darth von Doom nods his head.] Darth von Doom: Indeed, that is why we are here. [The Mathematician pushes the big red button again and a portal opens. All of them jump in and end up in the Mathematician’s lab as the portal closes behind them. They all look each other as they stand up and brush themselves off.] Jack: Well, see you when we try and kill you next week. Mathematician: I look forward to it. [The Pirates leave the room. Suddenly, one of the Circus jumps out at them with a paper in his hand. It’s Lobster Man.] Lobster Man: Here. This came for you while you were gone. Damn courier left it here by mistake. [Jack is handed the paper and looks over it.] Jack: Hmmmm’what is it, mate? Lobster Man: (smiles) It’s the pay-per-view card for Reload. Have fun, guys. BWAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA! [And the Lobster has the last laugh as he leaves them alone to see the horror of a match that they’re in. The paper reads as follows.] Tag Team Championship Eight Corners Elimination Match The Enforcers vs Killer Pirates of the Outer Caribbean vs Mike Biddle & Back Ally Brawler vs The Texas Wrecking Crew vs The Hammer Brothers vs Prodigy Inc. vs Justice Inc. vs Project Mayhem The Long dormant and vacant Tag team Championship Division now shines bright. With Eight teams looking to become the Brand-new Tag team champions. Some are known and have fought in Global over the past here, Some are new to Global. All have one thing in common A chance to rise to the top of the division in one night [Jack and the KKFOS look at each other and shrug their shoulders.] Jack: Oh well. Could be worse. There could be nine teams in there. And besides, you’ll probably eat half the people in the match anyway, so we’re good to go. KKFOS: Rightiando. [They then leave as the Lobster Man overhears everything and goes to Darth von Doom with a plan’to sneak in as the ninth team and destroy the Pirates! What will happen at Reload? Tune in to the next volume in the Killer Pirates and find out!] Cut. End Killer Pirates #2
