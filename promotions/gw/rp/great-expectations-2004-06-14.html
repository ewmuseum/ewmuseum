Great Expectations

14 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: #3 Evo 6/14/04 This is the grand entrance he'd been waiting for. As the glass door of Global Wrestling Headquaters slid open it was if the the entire world opened wide to accept him. Train stepped through the open doors with his passport. Every light outside and inside glimmered on the surface of The Platinum Title as it hung steadfastly over Train's shoulder...as if it were designed for just that task. The lobby of the multi-storied building was eye catching...striking even. The visage of all who enter is reflected in the chromed GW symbol that sits behind the silver desk that sits directly across the lobby from the doors. The tiled, white marble floors had it's own glimmer as the muted sunlight poured through the bank of hight, glass windows that stood as the front wall of the building. The walls of the lobby read like a text book of Global History. Pictures of important moments in GW history lined the light tanned walls as a testament to great accomplishments as brass placards bearing the likenesses of superstars from the past imortalize their attempts at greatness. And GW had grown into a true industry now. The hustle and busle of the company...memos and briefs and paperwork going back and forth as people moved through the lobby to get from department to department filled the place with activity. And it all stopped...the whole place froze in time for just a moment as every eye turned towards the door. Train...the Platinum Champion entered the room. Train just strolled right through, exuding the confidence of a champion. He knew he had the place by the short hairs. Christina McCoy had pulled out just about every trick in the book to put him in check. But none of it worked. Not a barfight in a chicken wire cage with her 'dick in a glass case' Ely Braxton. Not threatening to supsend and fire him. Not McNasty trying to black mail him. Not Brick and Deacon Holmes running their mouths about how he couldn't beat them. Even with the threat of Maverick Reno and TUE looming on the horizon... Nothing. And now, even her ultimate end game had fallen by the wayside. Train had been pounding his way toward the top teir of the company for months. Now declared a champion...so it likely would have surprised many to know his contract was only hours from expiring. That was it...the final doomsday device to rid GW of the Train menace. The only thing she hadn't counted on is the one thing she'd probably been praying for the hardest... Bubba was back. The day after the return of the McCoy patriarch Train's lawyer recieved a UPS overnight package with a brand spanking new GW contract to be reviewed inside. It had been a few days now. Train could have headed down to headquaters just about any time to get the contract signed. Of course, they'd tried to make him sweat. So he returned the favor. But today it was time to get this petty shit out of the way so Train could get back down to his real business. Maverick Reno and TUE had a stangle hold on this company. Their arm was firmly wrapped around the throat of everyone that worked in the company. Train had never been one to deal well with people who thought they could intimidate him or give him orders. Before that had been Christina... Now... Now that Reno thought he'd come and stare Train down because his house boy Deacon couldn't get the job done at High Noon... Like Train would just back down? Now it was Reno. Train sauntered up to the front desk and the raven haired girl behind it. She qualified as a hell of welcome wagon. As Train approached she raised her dark brown eyes in his direction, flashing a bright smile that makes it a slightly better day than before. And the fact that her blouse is open, not enough to be unprofessional, but just enough to be enjoyable makes the day near perfect. 'Hi Train.' she says in a silky tone that pulls Train closer. 'Mr. McCoy is expecting you upstairs in conference room 3.' 'Cool, cool...' Train responds with a grin. 'But the name ain't Train.' 'Ummm... It's not?' she says, her face leaving no room to miss her confusion. 'Sorry about that sir. I just thought....' 'Oh, no need to apologize sweetheart, you didn't know.' 'So did you change your name?' 'Huh? Oh, no girl! Naw...see I was just saying my name ain't Train...to you. See for girls with eyes and a smile...and a body like yours? Naw, they don't call me Train.' She stops for a moment, a little dumbfounded. The cheeseyness of it all catches her off guard. Train knew it would...it always does. That's why it always works. 'hahaha... Ummm... Ok... And what am I supposed to call you? 'Well see... lil' punks and suckers...cats that're scared of me? They call me Train. And only my momma calls me Anthony. So...beautiful women? The call me Tony. So...you get to call me Tony.' 'haha.... Ok... Tony. Mr. McCoy is expecting you upstairs, conference room 3.' 'Aight, bet. Ms.....' 'You can call me Angie.' 'Aight... Angie. I appreciate the help. Matter of fact, I appreciate it so much... I think you should let me take you out to dinner tonight. Show you just how much a brotha appreciates you.' Her lightly tanned skin wasn't quite dark enough to hide the rosey red color that gathered quickly in her cheeks, though she did a good job of muting her giggling. 'Ummm... I'm afraid I'm busy tonight. Sorry Tony.' Train could only grin at her, knowing she was just making an excuse. But that was alright. He was the champ. He'd be spending a lot of time up in GW headquaters. He'd wear her down. 'Well, that's aight. I'll get at you some other time.' Train just brushes her hand with his as he moves past the desk into the hallway off to the left that holds the buildings bank of elevators. 'Of course....' begins Angie, her silken voice stopping Train in his tracks at the opening of the hallway. 'I get off for lunch in 30 minutes.' Train just grins, turning back to look her in the eyes. 'I'll be back in 20.' He turns back and disappears into the hallway. ===== ===== ===== Glossy, cherry wood finish of the large, oval, conference table glowed even brighter with the Platinum Title layed out at it's center. Train just leaned back in the one of the leather chairs that surrounded the table, his eyes staring out at the bright Texas day outside of the large windows. That sit across from him. His eyes are on the outside, but his mind is more on the bundle of papers that are laying right in front of him.... 'Official Global Wrestling Contract... Anthony 'Train' James.' Train reaches into his pocket and grabs his cellphone. A flip of the thumb and it's open. A push of two buttons and the phone dials on it's own. As soon as the sequence of high pitch tones are finished Train puts the phone to his ear, listening to the rings on the other end of the line. Finally there is a voice... 'Hello, this is Leonard...' says the man on the other end of the line. His voice puts him in the neighborhood of 40 or so and squarely in the city of New York. 'Yo, what's good Lenny?' inquires Train. 'Heeeeey, Train... What's going on man?' 'Nothing man. Chillin' up in GW headquaters, checkin' out this new contract. So you like this for real? Ain't no bullshit fine print or nothing? I don't too much trust these McCoys, you know what I mean?' 'Well, I understand Train. However, really, there's nothing to be worried about here. They made a fair offer on salary and they gave you pretty good points on merchandising and personal appearance fees. Not to mention title defense bonuses and pay-per-view cash out bonuses for main eventing or succesfully defending a title. Look, I know how much bad blood you've had with this Christina woman up there so believe me I was on the look out for any tricks. Didn't find a one.' Train let's go a heavy sigh followed by a moment of silence. It all seems too easy. After all the conflict, the apparent cease fire between himself and the GW front offices have flanked him and taken him by surprise. It seemed like the war might stretch on forever. Now all of the sudden there is some kind of non-aggression packed. It should have been the ultimate relief with all that Train had on his plate. But somehow it just seemed like the ultimate sneek attack. Sure...it could just be paranoia. Then again, it's not paranoia when they're really after you. 'Alright man...' Train relents, his voice filled to capacity with reluctance. 'If you say it's straight, it's straight.' 'Trust me Train.' Lenny says confidently. 'The way this thing is set up if you get the merchandise moving and hold on to that title of yours for a while and you could be set for a long time around this time next year. It's a generous deal. Be happy. You're about to be a rich man.' 'Bet. Good lookin' out.' 'No problem. You got anymore quesitons you knw where to find me, alright?' 'Right. Peace...' Train snaps the phone closed. His eyes slowly drift back down to the bundle of papers. 'Fuck it.' Train looks around the room, figuring there would be a pin in the midst of a conference room. Of course he was wrong. He rises from his seat preparing to continue his search outside when the door opens. Train freezes mid move...his eyes locking with that of a man he's never met before. Train knows he's at a disadvantage. While He had hours of tapes and interviews that could tell him what Train was like...Train had only conjecture and rumor. Of course the rooms looked like they might be true. Bubba McCoy steps into the room holding two shot glasses and a bottle of Jack Daniels. And he didn't really step as much as he sauntered in. He was dressed in a pair of crisply creased blue jeans and blue buttoned down shirt and a nice black blazer...but none of it covered up the cowboy in him as he stepped up to the far end of the conference table and kicks the chair back from the table. He thumps the glasses and bottle down onto the table and pops a squat as he'd likely say. The whole time Train can only look on...trying to figure out what to make of him. 'So are you just gonna go on half standing there or are you gonna have yourself a seat?' Bubba asked with an air of whimsy in his voice. Train considered for a moment, still trying to figure out if he was crazy or crazy like a fox. Train reached back and grabbed hold of the arms of the leather chair and pulled it back under his legs as he settled himself back into it. 'Damn...is it happy hour already?' Train inquired with mocking sarcasm dripping from his voice. 'Oh, it most certainly is happy hour for me son.' Bubba said as he cracked the label on the bottle of Jack that Train finally noticed was the green label variety, aged for at least 20 years. 'I'm here conducting business with one of the bright spots on my roster so I say this is kind of a special occasion.' 'Special is it?' 'Absolutely.' Bubba lifts the bottle of Jack and pours a generous helping into each glass. Christina's father seems an old hand at tending bar as he slams the bottle down on the table and slides one of the glasses to Train, covering half the length of the table without spilling a drop. Train simply opens his hand and lets the drink slide in like a runner to home base. Bubba twists the cap back onto the bottle and shifts in his chair, lifting his left leg up to cross and rest on his right. 'You know Train,' Bubba begins. 'I believe when two men talk business... I mean real business, not that polite and friendly, happy horseshit most people do... I mean business, where all your cards are on the table.... I believe that should do it over a drink. So... Let's you and me talk business.' 'Let's...' says Train lifting his glass and swirling the dark carmel colored liquid around for a moment. 'Let me just start by saying... No matter what you think? You and me? We're alot more alike than we are different.' 'Is that so?' 'You better believe it. See, you and me? We're from the same places. Now I know we're not from the same city but we are from the same place. See poor folks is poor folks the world over. I don't know exactly what it's like on the Southside of Chicago, but I damn sure know what it's like in just about every craphole town in Texas trying to make a living where there ain't none. Sometimes you just have to do what you have to do. Sometimes right and wrong just don't enter into it. Your hood was a concrete jungle. My hood was a mix of deserts and backwoods. In your hood if there were any animals it was the cops or the people around you. In mine it was probably some God damn bear or mountain lion. Either way the rules were simple. Kill or be killed. It's all a jungle out there right? 'Damn right.' 'It's easy to get dragged down and bogged down when you're in a place like that. Easy to just let it get to you...to just settle for making it. Or it is for other folks. Not for boys like you and me. Enough just ain't good enough is it? Of course it ain't. Bottom line is we'll do whatever the hell we have to get what we want and...after all the shit we have go through to get what we want...we'll be damned if we just let someone tell us what we can and can't do as they didn't have shit to do with us being where we are. See that's the thing my daughter can't understand. That's why you and her have such a hard time getting along. She doesnt' quite get it. Which is good in a way. Fact is I never wanted her to have to. When I was scraping and clawing to carve out my own nitch in this world it was to keep my blood from ever having to come up the way I came up. Otherwise what's the point right...if you can make it easier for those who come after you... Your family...' 'I suppose I can dig that... But what's your point?' 'Easy. I like you Train. I respect who you are. I respect where ya' come from. My daughter? She doesn't like you, but she doesnt' get you. All you're looking for is a chance to leave your mark. You don't want any special treatment...just to be left alone to do what you have to do. And anyone who stands in your way is gonna catch hell. I get you my friend. Hell, a few years back, I was you. Now, you wanna know my point? It's simple. I'm gonna give you want you want Train. I'm gonna give you the chance to accomplish everything you want. But, what's that old saying? To whom much is given...much is expected.' 'Is that right? Uhuh...and what is exactly you expect from me?' 'I expect you do back up all that shit you talk.' 'Excuse me?' 'Nothing to excuse cuz I know you heard me.' 'You testing my patience dogg.' 'I'm sure you are, but that doesnt' change anything my friend. You say you're the best. You talked a whole lotta smack about our World and International champion...about how they just had the gold and you had the Platinum. Well now I'm gonna give you the chance to prove all that shit you been talkin' son. And you're gonna start doing it this week.' 'Oh, for real? How's that?' 'Easy. Not so long ago we had a little infestation vermin in GW that called themselves The Nevermore. Maverick Reno? He was the solution. Now that the Nevermore is gone... Maverick and his TUE buddies are just another infestation...they've outlived their usefulness as far I'm concerned. And now that they've started taking the lives of men and women that work for me...that's unacceptable. See it's like this... I called in a cat to chase out the rat. Now I need a pitbull to get rid of the cat. ' 'So why not your prescious World champion or International. Why don't you let them handle it? Unless you think they can't beat Reno...' 'Oh, I know they can beat him. If they couldn't they wouldn't be where they are. But you see...I don't want to see Reno get beat. I want to see Reno get beat up. That's gonna require somebody with a bonafide mean streak. If TUE and Reno are going to beaten it's gonna be by somebody who's isn't afraid to get his hands dirty. And I think you and I both know...you're the man for that type of job. I mean come on Train. You and I both know you already want to.' Bubba reaches into his pocket and produces a silver pen. He slides it down the table as nimbly as he did the glass a moment ago. Train lifts hand and slams it back down, stopping the pen. Bubba and Train lock eyes for a moment, some kind of quiet agreement being struck. Finally Train picks up the pen and flips the bundle to the back page and, with a flick of his pen commits his name to the contract...and GW's future. Train slams the pin down and Bubba raises his glass. Train returns the gesture...both men tilt the glasses back and imbibe the 20 year old whiskey within. Train stands and grabs the Platinum Title, putting it back on that spot that was custom made for it on his shoulder. Train and Bubba lock eyes as Train passes by his chair headed for the door. Train's hand grasps the door knob, but freezes as Bubba speaks to him, still seated, facing the opposite way. 'I'm glad we understand eatchother, but there's just one more thing. As much as I know my daughter doesnt' understand you and she can be spoiled and a plain pain in the ass sometimes. But if you ever speak to or about her the way you have in the past? I promise ya'... I'll beat you within an inch of your life.' 'And I promise you...you ever threaten me again? You won't feel a thing.... Cuz you'll never see me coming.' The door opens and slams shut behind Bubba who simply sits, still relaxing in his easy going manner. 'Yep. I definitely like that kid.'
