Dealing the cards you're dealt with

9 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/09/04 'WHERE IS HE?!!!' Ely Braxton raged all around the backstage area. His girlfriend Gunner Addison was close behind him, trying to calm him down, but to no avail. 'WHERE IS THAT SUMBITCH!' 'Ely honey, calm down!' 'Don't tell me to calm down! Nobody talks to you the way that little bastard did an' gets away with it! When I get my hands on him--!' 'Ely, it's nothing! He's not worth it!' 'Gunner, I saw how upset you were! Don't tell me it's nothin'! It ain't nothin' to me!' 'Hey, hey!' called a voice from down the hall. Ely and Gunner turned to see Brick heading towards them from the opposite direction. 'I could hear you from the other end of the hall, man. What's going on?' 'Nothing, Brick.' 'It didn't sound like nothing, the way Ely was carrying on.' 'You're damn right it ain't nothin'! Brick, for the first time, I truly understand why you wanna kill Deacon Holmes every now and then.' Brick sighed annoyedly at the mention of Holmes' name. 'What did the boy do, this time?' 'I was at the soda machine, just getting a soda. All of a sudden, Deacon comes up to me and starts talking trash.' 'What exactly did he say?' 'Oh, he called me a slut, he made little insinuations about my relationships with men...' 'What the hell does it matter what exactly he said? The fact is he disrespected the woman I love and now I'm gonna snap the little bastard in two for it!' Just as Ely began to advance, Brick stepped in front of him. 'Whoa, man, take it easy.' 'Don't tell me to take it easy! You should be just as mad as me! And you probably hate Deacon the most outta anybody in this whole damn building, if not the world! So why the hell are you tryin' to stop me?' 'Because, as much as I would like to see you stomp his monkey ass into wine, I can't afford for anything to happen to Deacon and you know that, Ely.' 'Oh, right, I forgot.' 'Forgot what? And Brick, why are you of all people trying to keep Deacon from getting hurt?' For the longest time, Brick was silent. 'Brick?' 'Because he's my partner in the Master of the Mat tournament,' Brick mumbled. 'What?' 'I said, because he's my partner in the Master of the Mat tournament.' Gunner's jaw dropped the instant she heard what Brick said. 'Oh, Brick, you're kidding.' 'This ain't something I'd joke about, Gunner. Apparently, I did something to piss Bubba off because he's punishing me by teaming me up with Deacon.' 'You sure it was Bubba? Christina may have put you two together before Bubba fired her.' 'At any rate, I can't let anything happen to him. Normally, I'd be holding that fool down while Ely beat his ass, but not this time. This is Master of the Mat, and I'm not gonna let anything ruin my chances, even if it means Deacon and I have to work together. But, I'll tell you this much. Once that match is over, whether we win or lose, all bets are off.' 'Yeah, looks like you got dealt a bad hand.' 'Yeah, but I'm gonna play the hand anyway. There's always a chance I'll get a flush.' Just then, security guards ran by them. Ely managed to stop one of them. 'What's goin' on?' 'It's Jesse. He's been beat bad.' 'Where?' 'Christina's office.' 'Reno,' Ely snarled. 'Come on!' When they arrived on the scene, Jesse Briggs was being carried out of the office on a stretcher. His body was bruised and bloodied. Pieces of glass were strewn all over the floor. Bubba stood over Christina's desk, his head bowed and his fists on top of the desk, his back to the door. 'That son of a bitch!' Brick and Ely walked into the office, slowly approaching Bubba with caution. 'Bubba?' 'Get...Reno...NOW!' 'Come on, Brick.' Later... Brick and Ely were exhausted. Maverick Reno was being placed in a cell in the Arena's detention center. These two had the unenviable task of helping to keep him in line, along with Christina McCoy. At one point, Reno began to beat Ely with his handcuffs. Brick managed to restrain him while Ely stunned him with a stun gun. Even as they walked away, Reno managed to finish singing 'Jailhouse Rock'. 'Never thought I'd learn to hate that song,' said Ely. 'Me, either, and I'm not even an Elvis fan. So, how's your cheek?' 'You think a few slaps get me worried?' 'Yeah, well, I've been slapped by Christina before. I'm surprised you didn't slap her back.' 'Believe me, Brick, I wanted to.' 'I don't know about you, but I feel like heading for the Dark Horse.' 'Yeah, well, we might wanna stay behind for a bit, just in case Reno tries anything.' 'OK, but afterwards we head straight for the Dark Horse. I could use a drink.' 'I'd want a drink, too, if Deacon Holmes was my partner.' 'Well, like I said, after the match is done, all bets are off.' 'Just save some of him for me.'
