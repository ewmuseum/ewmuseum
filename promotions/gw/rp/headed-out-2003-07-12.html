Headed out

12 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/12/03 As Brick walks backstage from the arena, he can’t help but smile. For weeks now, he has had to deal with Deacon Holmes. The constant disrespect from Holmes was starting to irritate him. Tonight was the last straw, or at least should have been. Holmes had interrupted Brick’s official representation as Global Wrestling World Champion, a moment that was four years coming. But, interestingly enough, Brick had predicted it and had something ready. Brick told the young GW superstar that he had a present for him to make him feel better after the end of Whiplash, where before they had retired, Salaok, Quartz, and Derek Frost, along with TLM Jay Robson, had humiliated Holmes and the rest of the Upper Echelon. Salaok had forced Holmes to detonate a bomb placed underneath his apartment building at gunpoint. Then, Salaok pulled the trigger on the gun’only for it to be a squirt gun. Nevertheless, young Deacon soiled himself. Brick decided to buy a special gift for Deacon in case he tried anything on the night Brick would publicly celebrate winning the World title. The gift was a pack of diapers. He thinks back to the day he bought it. * * * Brick walked into the supermarket. He had gone to get some odds and ends for the house. He got some cereal, some milk and some juice. Just then, a mother and child passed him. In the shopping cart was a pack of diapers. A bright idea then began to take root in Brick’s mind. “Um, excuse me. Where are the diapers?” “Aisle three,” said the mother. Brick promptly made his way to aisle three. He saw a wide assortment of diaper brands. He then saw a large pack of Huggies. “Perfect.” He then grabbed the bag and put it into the cart. “Let’s see. Milk, juice, cereal, bread, and diapers. I’m all set.” Brick then headed for the checkout counter. He went to the 12 items or less lane and began to unload the cart onto the conveyor belt. “Ah, Soap Opera Digest,” he said, taking the magazine and placing it with the rest of his groceries. * * * After putting away the rest of the groceries, Brick took the pack of diapers and went to his closet, looking for a roll of gift wrapping. “Bingo!” Brick then grabbed the roll and headed quickly for the dining room table. He then went into the kitchen and looked in a drawer for some Scotch tape and some scissors. Just then, the phone rang. “Hello” Hey Aaron, what’s up? How’s ALF doing? Hey, don’t get mad at me. You and Felicia named her. Oh, she’s healthy. That’s good. Oh, nothing much, just got back from shopping. I’m getting ready for Evolution. Yep, my first Evolution as the World champion. Well, I got things to do and I imagine you do, too. I’ll talk to you later. Bye.” After hanging up the phone, Brick sat down with the diapers and the wrapping and began to go to work. After cutting off enough wrapping, he wrapped the diapers and then cut off some tape. * * * Brick walks out of his locker room and heads for the parking lot. He’s headed to the Dark Horse Saloon to celebrate his night off courtesy of Christina McCoy. He’s happy tonight for three reasons. One: he’s the World champion. Two: he’s got the night off. Three: he’s gotten one over on Deacon Holmes.
