She's The Blade: Prologue part two

23 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 23, 2005 Lisa's cunning plot to rope Mike into marriage had began to take shape and the mere thought of the impending success brought a smile to her angelic face. She sat in the living room of her Los Angeles penthouse clad in a nightgown and listened intently to her voice mail that brought tidings of the successful completion of the first part of her plan. Her sources informed her that Mike had been asking about her every chance that he got. She had planted the seed, now all she had to do was nurture it and wait for it to mature into something beautiful. During the time since their first “encounter” Lisa had also found out quite a bit of information about Mike. The most interesting tidbit she found was that he had much more money than she originally thought, which meant that she would have to change her plans a little bit even if it meant compromising her original purpose. However, she had to finish her first objective and set up a personal meeting with Mike. 'Charles,' Lisa called out as she rang a small bell. Scant seconds later a man that was on in years entered the room, he was well dressed and it obviously pained him greatly to walk. He was the Charles she had called for. 'Yes ma'am?' He inquired with a respectful tone. 'I need you to make a delivery for me,' she said as she reached into a nearby drawer and produced an envelope. 'Take this to 5944 Crescent Drive,' she continued, 'make sure that you hand deliver it to Mike Biddle.' 'Yes ma'am,' he replied as she handed him the envelope. She giggled softly as she watched Charles exit the room; the time had come for the seed to be nurtured. Later That Same Day... Mike Biddle’s personal gymnasium was a large well-lit room. Normally this room was where Mike performed his vigorous training routine but today it played host to Carlos Fraga’s physical therapy session. Mike sat off to the side and watched the session with great interest, even though the manliness of Carlos’s female therapist disturbed him greatly. Other than that small detail he was comfortable in khaki shorts and a blue Hawaiian style shirt. Mike couldn't help but laugh as he watched the portly Carlos attempt to re-learn how to walk. Every snicker drew a glare from the manly woman that was Carlos's physical trainer, however; no matter how intense her glare became it was no match for Mike's lack of proper etiquette. Every little slip up by Carlos caused Mike to laugh even harder. Carlos stood between two parallel bars; he gripped each bar with his pudgy fingers. The aim of this exercise was for him to walk from one end to the other using as much leg power as possible. One could easily gather from Mike's reaction to the situation that Carlos had not yet gotten the hang of this particular exercise. Carlos tried his best to not let Mike's laughter affect his performance; while he couldn't tell why Mike always mocked him he had become used to it and was able to ignore it from time to time. Once again Carlos took a step forward with caution and lost control, he fell flat on his rump with a loud thump. As Mike once again cracked up with laughter a disappointed look came over Carlos's face. All Carlos wanted to do was walk again and the fact that he couldn't saddened him deeply. 'Please Mr. Biddle,' pleaded the trainer, 'Don’t laugh at Carlos. Your negativity isn't helping him at all.' 'Oh boo hoo,' Mike replied with a chuckle, 'This is my house and I'll laugh at whatever I damn well please.' The trainer didn't take the bait; she heaved her manly shoulders with a defeated sigh and helped Carlos to his feet. 'That's enough for today,' she said as she helped Carlos to his wheelchair. 'Next time we won't have an audience, don't worry Carlos I'll have you walking in no time.' Carlos smiled at the thought of walking again. The trainer then turned to leave. 'We'll pick this up again tomorrow at noon,' she said as she exited the room and gave Mike another glare. Mike rose from his seat, careful not wrinkle his clothes. 'You’re coming with me to Evolution this week. I have a small job for you,' Mike said with a smirk. The comment brought another smile to Carlos's face. Even though his trips with Mike to shows always resulted in him sustaining some sort of bodily harm it was always fun to be backstage and see things that other people normally don't. “You want me to be with you at ringside for the Television Title match?” Carlos asked with a surprised voice. Mike rolled his eyes in annoyance. “No,” Mike said, “I need you to slow my opponent down before the match.” “You mean you want me to get beat up again” I can’t even walk so how exactly am I going to slow down a vampire?” Carlos asked with a worried tone. “Don’t worry about it,” Mike said, “I have it all planned out up here.” He finished as he pointed to his forehead. Without saying another word Mike left the room, Carlos was no longer amused him so there was no further reason for pudgy young man to be blessed with Mike's presence, besides he didn’t want to give the little bastard a chance to refuse to go with him to Evolution. As Mike was about to step into the main part of the house he was met by his housekeeper, a saucy Frenchwoman named Belle, she was a beautiful woman that did more for Mike than just clean the house. She “accidentally” brushed her large bosom against Mike’s arm to catch his attention as he passed by. 'Mr. Biddle,' she said in her heavily accented voice, 'there is a man here to see you.' 'What the hell does he want?' Mike asked in an annoyed tone. 'He says that he has a message from someone named Lisa Jameson,” was her reply Mike's curiosity was officially piqued by Belle’s response. He needed answers to why Lisa would even be interested in him, after all, he was twenty-six; much too young to even draw a glance from a woman of her 'tastes'. 'Where is he?' Mike asked the maid. 'He's sitting in the living room,' Belle said with a sigh as she returned to her house cleaning duties. Mike rushed to the living room to meet this man that was sent by the mysterious woman. Once he entered the living room he found an old but well dressed man seated upon the leather armchair closest to the window. When the man became aware of Mike's presence he rose from his seat and respectfully approached Mike. 'I have a message for you sir,' the man said as he reached into his jacket. The man produced a white envelope that had Mike's name written upon it in fancy handwriting. He handed Mike the envelope and politely excused himself. Mike tore the envelope open and took out the piece of paper contained within. He clung to the hope that this paper would answer any and all questions he had about the current situation. He unfolded the paper and quietly read the words to himself. Dear Mike Biddle, I have heard around town that you have some questions that require answering so I would very much like to meet you and answer and all questions you may have. I am personally inviting you the benefit dinner that will be held in honor of the Breast Cancer Awareness Association that I sponsor. Naturally a minimum donation of $1,000 is required upon entry and the event will be black-tie. The dinner will be held at the Beverly Hotel next Friday at 8pm, a limousine will be sent to your home at 7:30pm to take you there. I look forward to seeing you. Always, Lisa Jameson Mike folded the letter and put it back into the envelope with a sigh. He was frustrated by the fact that his questions would have to wait for answers while he was also confused as to what to do about what he thought was going on. He then smirked as he wondered if he could find a way to get Lisa and Mindy Mercer together for a threesome. He then placed the envelope on a nearby table and left to go buy a tuxedo for the event. -To Be Continued-
