The Slump

5 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 5th I sit down across from my friend Owen. I put my hand on the controller and press the two buttons repeatedly. Sock em Bop em robots what a dorky game eh? My robot gets blasted with I guess would be a right hook and its head shoots straight up. 'You lose again Freddy!' Owen exclaimed. 'This game is fixed! Its stupid as hell! What kind of moron would play this?!' I said annoyed 'Another game?' Owen asked with a smirk. 'Of course' I said without hesitation. I shoved my robots head down and started playing immediately. Yes this was kind of dorky but dorky is fun. Why else would I walk around The Global Arena with a super soaker? All of a sudden my robot's head shoots up again. 'Mother fucker!' 'I am the master of the sock em bop em robots!' 'Shut the hell up Owen' Owen smirks 'This is good times man... remember that time you had Justin Timberlake as your guest on The Fred Grest Show?' 'That was the lowest point in my career' Early Last Year You see a fake Justin Timberlake sitting on the sofa. 'So what are you doing after the show Freddy poo.' I turn my head quickly at the fake Justin Timberlake and raise my eyebrow Rock style. 'What the hell are you talking about?' 'I was thinking about going back to my place' Timberlake winks. Current Time I cringe at the thought. 'You know him and the rest of your boyhood idols are making a comeback right?' 'Shut up you loser' 'Sadly I made a habbit of that lately.' 'Don't worry one day you might beat me at Sock bop em robots!' 'You know damn well I'm not talking about that' 'I know but I figure I'd keep your mind off that.' 'I have one win out of four tries Owen. I'm not even average in Global Wrestling. I'll be nothing more then a side bit comedy act.' 'Well you'll be on TV at the very list.' 'Not good enough Owen. I have to a belt around my waist..... I have to be somebody.' 'This slump you've been in as really gotten to you lately.' 'I've been in a slump for the past year. Of course its gotten to me. I have very little to show for my career the past year.' 'Not true' 'What do you mean' 'You've gotten over with the fans for one. You have shown the potential to be so much more then a certain jerking comedy act.' 'Yeah.... I guess you're right but I don't want to be one of those guys making a living off my potential' 'So what do you say Freddy? Another game against The Master?' I go back to playing the game of Rock em sock em robots against the master. He beats me again with a right hook I shove the head back down and give Owen the finger. He snickers and we go back to pklaying. I guess he's right though I have shown the potential to be so much more. I guess I'll have to show that against Josh Curtis at Evolution by making him tap out and I'll do so right in the middle of the ring. The head pops up again and I shove it back down and as he keeps snickering I nail his robot square in the jaw and his head goes up. 'No fair! I wasn't ready!' Owen yelled. 'Who is to say what's fair?' 'Me! Damn it!' 'Well now that I've beaten you fair and square I'm going to go take a nap.' 'Oh that is so bullcrap!' 'Yeah so now get the hell out of my house!' 'Why? You know me!' 'That's the problem Owen I do know you.' Owen tilts his head slightly puzzled. 'What's that suppose to mean?' 'Over the tenture of our friendship which is pretty much since we were 5 what makes you think I'd trust you in my house alone?' 'Example' Grest puts his hand on his chin for a moment and thinks. 'Remember the time you and Scott Vyper shaved off my eyebrows and put shaving cream in my slippers?' Owen points at Grest 'Hey that was all his idea.' 'I don't doubt that in the least but you still took part.' 'So just lock your doors.' 'That would work but remember the time you and Chill change the shampoo with green food coloring. My hair was green for a week.' 'So? We thought you'd like it! Your hair is bright fucking red!' 'Yeah but my hair was brown at the time.' 'Just be glad we didn't use chlorine like Chill wanted too.' 'You have to be kidding me' I say surprised. 'Yeah I figured you'd be a lot more pissed with you're eye burning.' Owen nods and he says that. 'Very True' Suddenly the door swings open and a man in a cowboy hat busts in. Owen and I look at each other and without hesitation Owen says 'I told you to lock your doors' 'Yes you did Owen Yes you did' 'Hello Grest' said the cowboy in a slow voice 'Hello Twisted Cowboy' I responded in a slow voice. 'Shut up Owen this is between me and the yankee over here.' 'Man do you ever change your clothes.' 'Listen I am finally going to shut your mouth Freddy' 'You say that every time I feel like I'm Bugs Bunny and you're Yosamite Sam. It's just not funny anymore.' 'Kind of like your career.' 'Haha that was gold man like I haven't heard that enough the past few months.' 'Think there might be something to it?' 'No, but there is something to this!' I shout. His jaw meets my foot with the Freddy Kick hitting right where it's suppose to. The Twisted Cowboy is out cold. 'Why didn't you just do that in the 1st place?' asks Owen. 'It's always fun to hear what he has to say' I answer. 'To think he's been seeking revenge for the past year based on the fact that you made him look like an idiot.' 'Yeah.. its great' We drag him out on the front porch and lock the door. I wonder sometimes if there is something to what everyone says about me leaving the sport I love. However I always shrug it off no matter how bad the slump gets. Everything happens for a reason and maybe the reason is to be the biggest surprise in Global Wrestling. Who knows? I am not about to quit and will be in this industry as long as it takes. I will be considered one of if not the best in wrestling today. This slump as caused me to grow as a wrestler and as a person. I am no longer a one trick pony. I am no longer some small minded man who is just in it to be the best. While that is everyone's goal and including mine its not what I'm all about. Night in and night out I wow the fans and for now that is enough. However I will have THE title held over my head and be the guy. However, unlike every other guy I will not forget where I came from. I will beat this slump.
