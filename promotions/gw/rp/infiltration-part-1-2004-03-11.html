Infiltration (part 1)

11 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 11 The wonders of meditation are not something to be taken lightly. Many a time has it brought me to stunning realizations that I might never have found in the real world. There is just something about it, that makes it so relaxing and fulfilling. It is easier to look at the whole problem. And with the aid of the Lotus, I often find the answers I need right in front of my face. It is the loneliest place in the world. Locked in a deeply meditative state, your mind wandering the depths of your subconscious all alone. But, a satisfying feeling nonetheless. I sit alone on top of the roof, of our apartment building, I close my eyes, and I am taken to another world. A world where everything, even the most complex things, seem overwhelmingly simple. It is my release from the real world, it is my solution. Ichiro sat perched atop the roof of his apartment building. He sat cross legged his eyes open as he gazed over the Dallas twilight. The sun is beginning to fall from the sky, throwing the city into a beautiful array of lights, towers and sun. The purple and pink twilight seems to illuminate every action occurring in the city. Ichiro watched without care, he observed as the birds chirped and flapped their wings, soaring high above, above any height that a human could ever reach. Then he relaxed. He closed is eyes, and his muscles relaxed. He began to feel a lift from his body, his weight began to evaporate and he began to feel lifted. He watched as he was lifted but his body merely sat there, still with his eyes closed and still sitting atop the roof. That was when it began. It was not like his normal form of meditation, he was normally taken to the Shao Lin Temple when he meditated. Usually, he felt the soft caress of the warm mountain winds, smelled the scents of the beautiful wildflowers and saw the temple where he spent much of his youth. But today was different. Today was not like the others. Ichiro began to walk around, did his eyes deceive him? He walked slowly throughout the visionary realm. He knew it wasn’t real, but for some reason it was different today. It seemed so real, so life like. He walked through the jungle, the thick brush and foliage surrounded him. He smiled as he walked through it, his troubles disappeared as he walked. He was consumed in this world, consumed in the realness. He walked through the foliage and emerged on the other side. He now looked into the beautiful sight of a Japanese mountain water hole. He looked into the water hole, he saw a beautiful crane standing there, inside drinking and bathing itself. Then he looked around and saw the other side of the water hole, he saw a terrible sight. A monstrous lion, sat lurking in the bushes behind the waterhole. Ichiro tried to warn the crane but he couldn’t do it. It was strange however, the lion did not seem intent on killing the crane, nor bringing it any harm for that matter. It seemed as if the lion was protecting the crane, merely watching over it. That was when it clicked into his head, the symbolism in this vision. Rokku and Yoshiko, how could he have missed it at first? That was when she appeared, almost as if she had descended from the clouds. Ichiro stood dumbfounded, he stared at the diva who merely stood smiling. How could this be? No one real had ever been in this realm with him, whether they were real or imagined. Yoshiko giggled slightly as she saw the look on Ichiro’s face. “Don’t worry, there is no evidence that I was ever here. It’s like a dream, but I do have to speak to you, and with what happened at Evolution. This is probably the safest method of me contacting you,” she said. Ichiro still stared straightforward. “But, how are you here?” “I’m meditating, just like you silly. I just had to concentrate on you, and I found you here. And by the look of your little vision behind us, you had something similar on your mind?” “The Echelon setting us up,” he replied. Yoshiko merely nodded. “Well at least we know that it was a setup. But, now we need to prove it. For the sake of ourselves and for many others.” “The Legion, the fans?” started Ichiro. “The Lion,” Yoshiko sighed as she said the words. Ichiro only nodded as he heard the words escape from her mouth. He looked around this land, it was so beautiful, so full of serenity, so real, but he knew none of this was real. The only real thing here were the words being spoken. Even the image of Yoshiko, as beautiful and real as it was, was still not real. “Then what, exactly is your plan Crane?” asked Ichiro. “A little spy game, beating the Echelon at their own game. On their home field,” replied Yoshiko. “You’re not thinking what I’m thinking are you, because if you are” There is no way in hell that you will ever get close!” exclaimed Ichiro. “I know, that’s why I am not going,” answered Yoshiko. “Good?” started Ichiro. “You and Jushin are,” interrupted Yoshiko. “WHAT”! Echelon Tower, is like breaking into The Pentagon, but on a bad day, when all the guards have PMS, and will just kill you. It won’t happen. It’s security range covers the blocks within two city blocks, and that’s just cameras, not to mention the guards that patrol around outside the two blocks!” “And?” asked Yoshiko. “And the guards inside the building. AND the fact that every single guard will instantly recognize us.” “Don’t worry, you forget about my technological skills, not to mention my expertise with makeup,” smiled Yoshiko. “I don’t like it, I don’t like it at all.” “Come on, you want to clear this up don’t you” You two are the only ones with past knowledge of that place, not to mention the fact this is your specialty. I’m sure Jushin would take it, why won’t you?” asked Yoshiko. “Leave him out of this, I will need his help. But, he is not of your concern. We’ll do it, but I don’t like it in the slightest and the second I get a bad feeling I am leaving.” “I can not ask for anymore,” answered Yoshiko. “Meet me tomorrow at 7:00 pm. I will have everything you need, along with a plan drawn up. It will take precise timing, but I’m sure you can handle it. Oh, and tomorrow, make sure you buy some C4. Lots and lots of it, I assume you know how to use it?” Ichiro nodded, and smiled. The smile began to slowly disappear as the vision of Yoshiko faded from view. The Next Day The brothers walked into the small armory shop. It was not there first visit, and they were sure it would not be there last. But this time, it was on a more personal note then most of their visits. Jushin, now knew what was going on, he knew how much it meant to his brother to get this mess cleaned up, but this was quite possibly one of the craziest ideas he’d ever heard. Echelon Tower was solidly protected. Getting in there was going to be one of the most difficult tasks ever attempted. It would probably be easier to jump off a five story building and walk away, then it would be to walk in and out of Echelon Tower undetected. But, Yoshiko had a plan and if anyone could pull off this, it was her. They walked through the doors, once again they saw the eyes peer through the small peak slip in the wall. The eyes turned to the side and then the steel reinforced door opened up. The brothers walked inside, and a huge smile spread across their faces. This was where they belonged, this was where they felt comfortable. The room was simple, white walls, white ceiling. But all over the walls were guns, many, many guns. Bullets also lined the edges. To the right there was a small door that led to another section of the store. That was their destination. “What can I help you boys with tonight?” asked the gentleman inside. “Explosives and lots of them. We’re planning on throwing a BIG surprise party for some friends,” said Jushin and he smirked. Ichiro continued to look towards the small door, as the gentleman nodded his head. He opened the small door leading to the next room, and inside were piles and piles of the most explosive devices known to man. C4, Plastic Explosives, Napalm, as well as several detonation and timing devices. “Jushin?” started Ichiro. “Shhhh, I’m starting to fall in love,” answered Jushin. Later That Night The two brothers pulled their car into the parking spot. They stepped out quickly, and looked up at the Den. They quickly entered and saw Yoshiko was already waiting impatiently. “You’re late!” “You didn’t tell us what time to be here at,” answered Ichiro. Yoshiko narrowed her glance at the two brothers. They always seemed so professional. They were as usual, dressed in matching business suits, with offsetting color schemes. Ichiro as always in white, and Jushin as always in black. They stood, with excellent posture waiting for some form of instruction. “Well, it doesn’t matter now I suppose. Alright, I have a plan. Follow me,” said Yoshiko. She lead the two Nakazuras back into the small meeting room. It was quiet and concealed. She gave them everything they needed to know. Fifteen minutes later the brothers emerged from the room. Each of the two men, now had a black gym bag slung over their shoulder. Inside were their technological devices and tricks. They would need them severely for this to work out. They also had a brief itinerary drawn out inside the bag. The document was very precise and would require exact following for the mission to be a success. They needed to get information, but they did not have a large time window to extract it. This was going to be the hardest mission to date. The brothers left the Den, with a huge weight on their shoulders. Jushin worried about their well-being. He worried that the mission might be too dangerous. He worried about what would happen if TUE caught them. He wondered, just what kind of trouble this could cause them. But for Ichiro it was different. It was a personal responsibility. A quest to cleanse his name of any dirt. For Ichiro, he did not think nor wonder. He only knew. And he only knew one thing. Failure was not an option.
