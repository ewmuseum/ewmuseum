The Road to Retribution: Part I

undated

Date: Today The Road to Retribution Part 1: Dylan Who? Pissed off? Angry? Annoyed? Fuming? Enraged? All emotions directly linked with the whole concept of anger. All emotions currently floating around the mind of ME, the man who could have and should have left 'Clash of the Titans' as the one who'll challenge for GW's very own, World Championship. It'd been three whole days since that jammy bastard had snatched my dream from my hands... It'd been three whole days since that stupid prick had snatched the dreams of a revolution from the reality of people worldwide... To say that I was disappointed at the way that I was eliminated is an understatement and a half. Regardless of the number that I actually entered the contest at, I was THIS close to getting the one thing that I'd wanted. True, for almost every day prior to the event, I was insecure of myself and my ability. However, just because that's how I felt and how I said I felt, it didn't mean that was actually what was running in my blood, flowing through my veins, circulating around my body. Expectation is something that I don't think I've ever lived up to through my career... and expecting to win that thing, it wasn't a thought in my mind until we got down to the last five people. At that moment, immediately after nailing the Elite with a stunning pair of DDT's, I was filled with a passion and an emotion... a drive to win, a will to succeed. I believed that I was just four people away from getting what I wanted. ... It's just a shame that somebody had to come along and spoil that little party. It was even more annoying when I discovered that the guy in question didn't even have any kind of history with me. He just charged down to that ring and stirred the pot in the wrong direction. For that, I'm going to get my revenge... and it's going to be a sight to behold for all. You see, unlike the whole situation with Arik Idol... this one is far more important. Wednesday; the point of the week where emotion was both happy and sad. Sad that only three days had passed and happy because the weekend was only another two days ahead of this one. Following the 'COTT' pay-per-view, I'd decided to check myself into a local hotel and I'd been there since the end of the show. Fortunately, I'd had enough money to cover my own expenses for that little holiday and so GW didn't need to stump up anything in the format of accomodation. '... Who the Hell is that guy? I just can't put my finger on it... and I don't even know where to start looking. You were so close to winning that entire contest... just, so damn close.' I said out aloud, the tale opening in the confinement of the room. Tired and disinterested with many walks of life at the moment, I'd chosen to remain on the room's Kingsize bed, clad in nothing more than a pair of white boxers, the television glaring both image and static as I used the clicker to run through the available channels. A few moments passed before I could find the words, '... and yet, it took all of ten seconds for a nobody to catch you off-guard and a tag team to eliminate you. It was those precious seconds that meant the difference between success and failure.' Defeat, although expected, had not settled all too well. Could you honestly blame me for taking defeat in such a manner though? Here I was, Mr Unknown in GW and within three weeks of officially competing, I'd ranked in at fifth place. Granted though, the last four men saw a case of 'Team vs Team', which would have hardly looked quite as impressive had I been able to avoid defeat and eliminate one of those instead. Dejected and depressed, I threw myself back onto the bed and let the remote drop to the floor. '... This is going to weigh on your mind for many, many moons to come. Worst of all though, this whole matter is going to be in control of your career until you've extracted a measure of revenge.' I said to myself, sighing loudly at the end of the sentence. Another period of silence followed and I did nothing to prevent it's consumption of the room. The television continued to throw forward its marathon of 'Starsky and Hutch' repeats. I didn't care, I didn't even want to know... I just wanted to get my hands on Dylan Ford and erase his existence from my life. It wouldn't change the outcome of the match, but it'd sure as hell make me feel a whole lot better. Suddenly however, there was a very short and sharp knock at the door to my room. Disinterested, I answered with a half-hearted, 'It's unlocked... come on in.' They did so accordingly and stepped into my room, heels clattering on the floor with each passing step. 'Rich...' they said, the familiar voice of my darling sister hitting home. She walked over to the bed and propped herself on the edge, looking down on me with a sympathetic smile. 'Hey soldier...' she said, a hint of sorrow floundering in her voice, '... I saw what happened to you on television. I really thought you could have done it, or at least got down to the final two or something. It's just a shame to see your dream destroyed by somebody who wasn't even supposed to be involved.' I looked up to her sympathetic stare and forced a smile, responding, '... It was more than just a shame, you know? This entire situation, it wasn't just an unfortunate occurance or something that destroyed my dream. It was a humiliation that I don't ever want to have to experience again. It was a traumatic event that I must avenge with victory on Evolution... and then victory against Dylan on every possible front.' 'Rich...' she began, her voice becoming stern and mother-like, 'If you're going to nail the guy when he leasts expects it, that's only going to make YOU as bad as he is. You're in a business where you can confront people and beat them to a pulp inside a ring with rules. Why would you want to try and take it out of there? Why would you risk losing face by beating somebody in an illegal bout where nothing counts for anything?' I thought long and hard about what she said, the words flowing around my mind as though they were part of a record on repeat. She made sense... but at the same time, she was so logical that it was all just a farce. How could I honestly sit back and allow this man to continue living his life, fighting his career... KNOWING that he was the sole reason why I'm not going to be challenging for the GW World Title in the near future? 'Jo, I know you're only looking for me... and I can respect you for all of that. I can respect the fact that you're just looking out for your brother. I can respect the fact that you love me and you don't want to see me in pain... but this man, he did something that cannot be justified without the extreme use of force. Inside/Outside/Out-wide, it doesn't matter where it happens.' I said, sounding sad but determined to extract revenge and work my way back into the frame that I'd so violently been shoved from. With those words, I arose from the bed and throw myself off onto the floor, landing on my feet, of course. I walked over to the television set and pressed the button, cutting the power from it. I turned back and stared at my sister, a blank expression appearing across my face. 'What?' she asked, looking confused and sounding more worried as opposed to anything else. 'What?' I said, throwing in a brief chuckle for good measure. '... I'm going to get ready and we're going to go visit a few people. At the same time, you can help me get through my third triple threat contest within GW.' Flashing a smile, the scene ended and the mass thoughts of happiness, sadness, revenge and victory all flowed through my mind in random succession. Like a skitz trying to battle with their various personalities, I was battling with my own emotions. I didn't know what was going to happen next... but by the look of my face, it wasn't going to be too good for somebody. : To Be Continued :
