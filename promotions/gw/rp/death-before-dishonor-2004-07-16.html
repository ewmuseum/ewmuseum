'Death before dishonor.'

16 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 16th, 2004. Taekwondo Times. 'Death before dishonor.' Standing at five feet and ten inches and weighing in at a pound north of the two hundred mark, Josh isn't exactly what you would call a man of great physical size. And when you throw in the fact that he makes his living in a sport ruled by three hundred-pound behemoths his stature seems to shrink just a little more. He doesn't look like a lethal weapon…but the moment you step into the Global Wrestling Octagon, you realize that looks can be quite deceiving. The man known Worldwide as 'American Youth' Josh is the reigning Global Wrestling World Champion, an accolade that was achieved with relentless determination, hard work, perseverance and a never say die attitude. Josh didn't just wake up one day, snap his fingers and stand alone on top of the Wrestling world, but rather earned his spot through four years of training and Wrestling. Now I know what you're thinking…what the heck is a Professional Wrestler doing in a Martial arts magazine such as, 'Taekwondo times?' Perhaps it's because we've gathered to celebrate the fact that Josh's place of employment, Global Wrestling, is a mixed fighting and Wrestling federation or…perhaps it's because he's a second degree Blackbelt in Chungdokwon Taekwondo. Josh has often shown off his martial arts prowess in the ring juxtaposing it perfectly with his crisp technical maneuvers and his death defying leaps of faith about the ring. With talent such as this it's about time that the Martial arts world recognized one of it's own and shined the spotlight on a true Martial arts celebrity. The training of Josh began innocently enough in his native state of Nebraska where he first studied Martial arts as a teen. Quickly realizing his potential he ignored the track scholarships coming his way and focused on the two things he loved the most…Wrestling and Taekwondo. Josh was quick to realize that each seemed to compliment the other and as both art forms bounced off of one another, Josh handled them perfectly. Or at least well enough to become a multi-time regional champion and pull in a pair of second place trophies at the Junior Olympics as a second degree blackbelt, all the while pursuing his Wrestling career in Japan, Mexico and the Independent Wrestling circuit of America. Of course, this is the same man often known for his heartless behavior in the ring, but who is the real Josh? Imploring this, I made a trip to Dallas, Texas, the home of Global Wrestling where I arranged an interview with none other than the best in the Wrestling business. Oh, and as for the real Josh? He isn't all that you see on television…he's so much more. I was immediately impressed when he arrived at my hotel for our interview at exactly two PM, which, was of course our scheduled time. A celebrity, not arriving fashionably late? Unheard of! This was ludicrous behavior…but what was even more stunning is the fact that when he heard I was a fellow Martial artist he bowed and shook my hand. A celebrity who actually sticks to the fundamentals and principles of the art he studied? Astounding! Josh has often been accused of being brash, cocky, arrogant, egomaniacal and referred to as a prima donna. No such behavior here, in fact he couldn't have been more of a nice guy. Stepping into my quarters he declined a cup of coffee, citing his reason as 'being kind' to my wallet. A celebrity, actually showing regard for others? Ludicrous! At first I questioned his logic, watching, waiting for him to slip up, but the more I studied him I saw how straight up he is…how genuine and quite frankly…nice. You've seen the shows where he is yelling at his opponent and talking trash? In my motel room he was as soft-spoken as I had ever heard a man be. His voice was oftentimes a low mumble and much to my embarrassment I had to ask him to repeat himself on several occasions. A celebrity, that isn't loud and impolite? Shocking! The more I studied Josh, the more I saw about him to admire. And the more I saw to admire, the more I admired. Eventually, and much to my chagrin, I was forced to reluctantly admit to myself that not only was this man honorable…but also a role model for the youth of America, which is exactly why he chose the name 'American Youth'. 'As someone still young and full of youthful exuberance I feel as if I speak for all of America's youth that have been repressed. My message is simple…stand up and fight back.' American Youth said when I made the inquiry about his moniker. The inspirational message that has carried over into his name is really a quite simple one for such a complex (albeit admirable) man. But as for his clothes..? 'I am who I am. I dress the way I want, I talk the way I want and I act the way I want…which, is exactly what I'm trying to get across to the generation of fans that follow me. I'm trying to show them to never be afraid of who you are and to always do what you want.' Was the response American Youth gave to justify his fashion sense, or lack thereof. A man that is polite, open minded, nice, strong willed and honorable…what more could someone ask for in a role model? Well, how about a clean slate? Something that Josh (admittedly) doesn't have, but in his defense his perseverance has always helped him to overcome the tumultuous tides of his life. And by tumultuous tides we mean his addiction to painkillers coming off of a nearly career-ending knee injury. But who can forget about the time he overdosed on Rohypnol and ended up in a hospital in Rome, mere days before his first big career opportunity? When asked about this, his countenance fell and he couldn't even look me straight in the eyes as he spoke of this incident. Keeping his soft bluish gray eyes away from mine he stared into the shag carpet of the motel room, boring a hole through the floor as laser like beams spilled from his eyes. He chose his words carefully yet when speaking of this nearly life-ending tragedy he often stumbled over those very words and became tongue-tied. At one point he closed his eyes and his voice sunk to a low whisper, it was then and there that I realized Josh hasn't always been the man he is. But rather, he became the man we now know, due to that very incident that nearly cost him his precious life. Yet in retrospect I feel as if he is grateful to that nearly fatal episode, as it seems to have moved him in the right direction. He now lives day to day, ready to live and die in the same moment, seeking out the pleasure in the simple things. Yet, the pleasure he seeks is often thwarted by the demons that seem to haunt him 24/7. What demons, you may ask. And the answer would be…the demons that are too many to count. Josh has more personal demons than anyone I've ever met, yet he has also overcame them better than anyone I've known. Nearly dying in Rome? Most people would have taken the first cab to a therapist and pumped their system full of Prozac. But Josh isn't most people…instead he reacted by winning the Global Wrestling World title and cementing his place as the best Wrestler in Global Wrestling. His close friend, Ice, leaving Global Wrestling? Most people would respond with disappointment and slump into depression as they realized they had lost a close friend. But hey, is Josh most people? No. So his response wasn't that of the average Joe, but rather that of a Champion as he rose to the occasion and became the franchise player of Global Wrestling, taking over the spot previously anchored by the man known as Ice. Maverick Reno putting a bounty on his head? Most people would hire a half-dozen bodyguards and pay them well enough that Reno couldn't get to him. But as previously stated…Josh isn't most people. His reaction was rather one of defiance. Instead of running and hiding he readily accepted all challengers for the bounty with steely resolve and more guts than brains. But that's who Josh is…a man of honor, integrity and undying courage. Known for his sportsmanship in the ring, Josh has never met an obstacle he couldn't climb or kick over. So the question lies as to who is Josh? The answer isn't quite so simple, because he's a man of great intelligence. A friend to many and a role model to all. A man known for his talent inside of the ring and his intellect outside of it. A track star, a martial arts champion, a world class athlete, a great Wrestler and most recently…a role model. So out of my chat with American Youth, there was one thing I quickly learned; nice guys may finish last…but here is the exception. Russ Silvo. Taekwondo times.
