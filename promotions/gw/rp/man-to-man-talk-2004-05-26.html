Man-to-man talk

26 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/26/04 The mood in the Dark Horse saloon was at an all-time high. People were dancing, drinking, riding the mechanical bull, etc. Then the door swung open and in walked Brick. The crowd cheered ?HEEEY!!!? after he walked in. As Brick walked towards the bar, a waitress with strawberry blonde hair and blue eyes walked up to him. 'Hey, Brick, how you doin', darlin'? 'Hey, Daisy. I'm good, I'm good. Just here to unwind, maybe get something to eat.' 'You know, as much time as you and Ely spend here, you ought to fill out some change-of-address forms.' 'I heard he was here. How's he doing?' 'He's been sittin' at the bar for about a half hour now, downin' mudslides left an' right.' 'Man. Guess I better go talk to him.' Brick walked up to the bar where Ely was sitting hunched over. In his right hand was a glass, half filled with Texas mudslide. 'Whoa, Daisy said you were poundin' 'em back. She wasn't kiddin'.' 'Leave me the hell alone,' Ely muttered, not looking at Brick. 'This about what happened between you an' Christina?' 'How'd you know about that?' 'Everybody was talking about it. I heard she really lit into you.' 'Well, I don't wanna talk about it anymore.' 'Yeah, well, as many mudslides as Daisy told me you've been drinking, you definitely need to talk about it.' Ely was silent for a moment, then turned to Brick and then turned his head back to the bar. 'Go on an' sit.' Brick took the stool right next to Ely. 'What'll ya have?' asked Jack the bartender. 'A ginger ale and some coffee for Ely.' 'I don't need no damn coffee.' 'You do need to sober up.' 'I can sleep it off.' 'You might not wake up afterwards.' 'Fine, gimme the damn coffee.' 'Maybe I'll get you some food, too.' Jack then gestured a waitress over to him. 'So what exactly happened? I've been hearing different stuff from different people.' 'Shelly calls me up, says Chris wants to see me 'She's still upset about you an' Gunner? I thought she got past that.' 'I thought so, too, but she was just ragin'. Told me to refer to her as Miss McCoy from now on. Told me she an' I weren't friends anymore. She was just so damn cold.' 'Damn.' Brick then reached into his pocket and pulled out his cell phone, dialing a phone number. 'Yeah, Gunner? It's Brick. I'm at the Dark Horse. Ely's here, too, and he's kinda wasted. I think you should come down and get him.' 'Hey, I don't need Gunner comin' down here. I'll be fine.' 'So what did you say to her after she ripped on you?' 'Nothin', really. I just slammed the door on my way out.' 'That's it? You didn't let her have it?' 'Not my style.' 'Not your style, or not your style with Christina?' 'Yeah, well, from what I hear, Christina ain't the only one upset about me an' Gunner bein' together.' 'Where did you hear that?' 'A lot o' people talk about it.' 'OK, I'll be honest with you. Part of me was upset, but that's only because of how close me an' Gunner were. But you're my boy, so that cancels it out completely. I'm happy for you, both.' Just then, Jack returned. 'OK, that's a ginger ale, some black coffee and a T-bone steak.' 'Thanks, Jack.' 'Look, Brick...' 'No, you look, Ely. This is bullshit and you know it!' 'Well, I can't deal with her right now.' 'Don't tell me you're afraid.' Ely gave no response. 'You are afraid of her.' 'Don't start, Brick.' 'I don't believe this. Big, badass cowboy and ex-soldier is afraid of Christina McCoy.' 'Well, then again, I heard Christina was havin' her period.' 'No excuse, and if you don't want to let her know that, then maybe I should.' 'Wouldn't do that, Brick.' 'What, I'm supposed to be scared cause she's on her cycle? So what if she's crazy a few days outta every month? I'm crazy 24-7-265, ok? Besides, I grew up with a mother and a sister and me and my dad had to deal with their cycles all our lives. My mother's easier now, because she doesn't get periods anymore.' 'What about your sister?' 'She's my brother-in-law's problem, now.' 'Fine. Do whatever you want.' 'OK, I will. Hey, Jack, I'll take that ginger ale to go.' Brick rose out of his seat and left some cash on the counter. Jack picked it up as Brick took his leave. 'Where's Brick off to?' 'Gone to talk to Christina on my behalf.' 'Aw shit.'
