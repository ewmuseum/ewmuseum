Moving up in the world...

19 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/19/05 ~Bremerton National Airport~ ~West of Bremerton, Washington~ ~About an hour from Seattle~ Out in the boondocks is more like it, but it was the only 'private airport' that the Order could get for one of their three jets they had in their fleet. Two were reserved for the highest ranking officials in the Order, the other one was reserved for special cases. Annie thought that this was a special case. Back in Vegas they were moving all of their things to their new house that they'd be using for their stay there now. Finally out of the hotel after months of staying there. But with the happenings of last week, Annie didn't think that Dani driving to Seattle was a viable solution to getting there for her match at the Pay Per view. Not to mention everything else that needed to get done up there. Now that long week was behind them. Danielle did a great showing at the Pay Per View, beating the Cowboy Barbie (Elia Smith), once again. Yeah, the little bitch had actually taken to trying to cheat in the end, but destiny was not with her at this time. Elia Smith went 0-3 against Dani, and it looks like Dani is getting the reward for that this week. The two boys had gone there in case they were needed by the federation and as moral support. They ended up not being needed by the fed, but that allowed Josh to set up the other things that needed to be done. Dani stood on the tarmac, getting out of the half buss, half van that had brought them from Seattle. A man walked up and started to load their bags onto a cart to take it to the large Bombardier Global 5000 jet that sat on the runway. Not quite one of those jets that was over at Sea-Tac, but this was still a big plane. Why did they have to fly? Yes, seems kinda strange that a woman who's shown to quite possibly be a genetic throw back to an honest to god, Angel... would be afraid of flying. Someone who one day may be able to manifest her wings on demand, and use them... hates to leave anything more then a few stories off the ground. But to get back to Las Vegas, they'd have to go much higher then that, and very fast. Dani nodded a 'no' to the guy who offered to take her rucksack and over the shoulder bag from her. Those were hers. She watched as first Josh went up and hugged the small blonde haired woman who had come with them, but would be taking the Airporter back to Seattle to get back to her studies. That woman, Margarette, was Josh's mother. The second wife to the father that he shared with Gray. She had been given a week of leave from her studies for the Order and the University to spend some time with her son... scratch that, her sons, and Dani. Although it hadn't been a vacation so to speak. It was more like an interrogation when it came down to it for both her and Josh. But Dani had noticed some other things happening there too. Over the last few days it had been a full tour of the area for Dani, put onto their expense accounts. Pike Place Market, the Aquarium on the piers, the Woodland Park Zoo, and even the Sol Doc Hot Springs over on the Olympic Peninsula in the mountains. All of it with her two men, who were there to protect her, and the mother of the one of those two men she was in love with. A small French immigrant with green eyes like Josh's. She had taken to Native Studies while going to school in Seattle and getting into the French and Indian war... and falling for it to study the people she saw as very interesting. Now she was considered an expert in the field. But she was also a good person. It was a feeling that Dani had never had, having grown up in the Orphanage until she was a pre-teen, and then was an only child growing up in a small cabin with her grandfather. She'd never known the touch of Lynn Northsky, her own mother, nor Daniel, her father. Josh finished hugging his mother, and Dani stepped in, dropping her over shoulder back to embrace the smaller woman. She held on for just a moment, and then let her go. As she stepped away, she noticed the small woman look up at the big man who was left. Her relationship with Gray had always been a hard one. She had replaced Gray's mother after she had passed on. She wasn't native, but Gray's mother had been. But there had been a love there between his father and this woman. And now that his own father had passed on a few years ago, Dani had noticed that Gray had gone out of his way to not bring up bad things over this last week. Then again, he had been a bit shocked when Margarette had openly called him by his name that denoted his other side, the not so human side. She knew what he was. Dani hesitated, not wanting to step on Gray toes if he chose to deny her this small thing, but he didn't. Gray took the small woman in his arms. It wasn't like she was his mother, but maybe an aunt or something, but it was a start.. something both brothers needed right now. The trio then began to head for the plane as it sat there. It had been the one that had brought them up to Seattle also. Dani tossed her bags down into one of the davion style seats , and an attendant walked up with a small tray. On the tray was a bottle of air sickness pills and a glass of water. “Thank you, I see Annie still had the instructions laid out for you guys.” Dani grabbed the bottle and popped the top off of it. Taking a couple of the pills out, she placed them on her tongue and then downed the glass of water. The cute little brunette that was watching Josh as he got his stuff strapped in, answered her. ?Yes, Lady Leanan gave strict instructions dealing with your fear of flying. Once all of your luggage and other gear is strapped in, just take a seat and buckle your seat belt. We'll taxi out and take off. Once we're at our flight altitude, the sleeping room in the back will be available for you to get comfortable. “Thank you.” Dani moved to start strapping her stuff in, and then get herself into her seat. The worst part of flying was the take off. She sat in a seat next to Josh, and he took her hand as the plane began to move. Time to go home... at least to the one they'd be using. Time for things to change, up a bit, and move up in the world. ~Approach to McCarran Business Airport~ ~Las Vegas, Nevada~ “BUZZZZZ” Nothing... “BUZZZZZ” Dani's hand reached over and hit the button on the small panel on the wall. ?What?? She sounded half asleep, more more. “Sorry to wake you Ms. Northsky. We're coming up on our final approach for Las Vegas. We need you to awaken and take a seat soon.” She managed to push the button in again. “Alright... I'll work on it. Thanks” Dani's eyes opened, still looking a bit far away due to the medicine that allowed her to put up with this means of travel. She rubbed her eyes, to get the sleepiness out of them. She sat up and looked around at the small room with a bunk type bed that she had gotten into. “At least it didn't crash while I was sleeping.” She reached down and picked up the necklace that had been hanging around her neck since a few nights before the incident. She had found it in her purse, but the scent gave it away, Josh always used oils for his cologne, Drakar Noir to be exact, and the oil seemed to rub off on everything he touched. Even the necklace had a slight lingering smell of him. The night that she found it, something compelled her to put it on, something told her it was the safe thing to do. As she understands it, that necklace had saved her during her ordeal with Jaymes a few nights later. Josh claimed that an old woman in a casino had given it to him, claiming he had dropped it. But who gave it to him? Where did it come from? How did who ever had it, know that she would be needing it? Those questions kept her mind busy. She would really love some answers. She would really love answers from Jaymes; but she found out that he wouldn't be able to give such answers. During the fight with the others, Annie had used her deadliest power on him. The kiss of a Leanan, left Jaymes, a babbling baby, in the body of a man. All knowledge of what he knew gone. In a way Danielle hated Annie for that, but she also knew that it was the last resort and they had done it to protect her. Danielle stirred and lifted the window shade, high in the dark sky with only the stars kept them company. Her thoughts drifted to the discussion she had with her Grandfather, right after the match last week, on the phone. He was proud of his granddaughter, but sad that he himself didn't get to see the pure beauty of the manifest ion. As requested Danielle sent one feather from her wings to him, Josh had collected them in the aftermath of the display. She tried to collect in her head how everything had changed since that night. Dani's nightmares and pains had gone. Although sometimes she felt this itching sensation go down both sides of her back at the same time. Gray had taken to calling her Sirius, her given “Native” name. Josh just kept calling her his Angel. She rolled over giving up on the idea of ignoring the captain and just going back to sleep. She hated flying. She just was never comfortable with the idea. Now she had all new things to come up and face once they were back. First was a new house that the Order had paid for. The last message from Annie said that everything had been moved out of the hotel rooms and taken to the new house. She'd also informed them that the staff had been hired. Basically a gardener or service for such, and a couple of women through a service that would clean, do laundry and cook for them. Dani didn't know why this was needed, and protested with her, but Annie had told her to trust her. Danielle got up and made her way out to the front room of the plane. Since they were now so close, she felt herself getting a little excited about the new house she would e calling home. A home with the two men of her life, her self appointed big brother and lover. Strangely enough a man she had always though of him as her soul mate. It just took her a little time to realize her heart was right and her head was to far up her ass. As she made her way out Josh, was rolling up a large sheet of drawing paper. Always the artist, he was always drawing. Coming up with new tattoos from the size of it. She suspected that this one was the new on for his back. Gray was working on something on a computer, most likely his weekly report. Like clock work he was, although something different about him. Danielle sensed it the next morning when he and Kaylee came to pick them up. But couldn't exactly put her finger on what it was. Josh addressed her as he was packing the paper into a cardboard tube he kept it in. “About time you got up, was wondering how long you were going to laze around in there.” Danielle smiled at him before giving her retort. “you could of came in and let me know that you two were still wake. Maybe, I would of come out sooner. I am not a mind reader you know.” Josh just smiled back. “Neither am I. Just remember that.” Then from left field, Gray, not looking up from his laptop, responded. “Actually he wanted to go back there, but I wasn't invited. I told him that now wasn't the time to try to join the Mile High Club.” Dani turned beet red in her checks, and Josh tossed a bag of peanuts in his brother's direction. Josh moved to change the subject. “So you ready for your match this week? We finally got the full details of it while we were in flight from Annie.” Danielle flopped down in the seat next to Josh and started to put her seat belt on. ?Yea guess so. I know it is no Dq and all, what else is invovled with it?? Gray pulled up a piece of paper from the printer that was mounted on a stand next to him. “Basically it looks like once the bell rings, only the area around the ring is allowed. No DQ, No Count Out, nothing like that. Pinfall or submission anywhere. But if anyone goes over that guard rail, it's also an immediate loss. And the title can change hands on it. Only means it'll have of affecting you is going from ring to outside. You'll have to purposely call it short so you make sure you don't sail into the crowd. Other then that, shouldn't be too much of a difference for you.” Dani's head dropped a bit though, she was thinking of other things with the match. “But I am worried that Shadow might try something. I know she has a thing for Virgil, then again she has a thing for Dawn, too. Since that match at Master of the Mat, when Virgil won, he's not done crap. And last week, Shadow basically did his fight for him. I guess i am lucky he hasn't had any title matches since then that mattered, other wise I would just be facing another man on the roster. Instead of a Champion.” Josh and Gray just looked at her, more liked stared at her. She smiled and then changed the subject. “So you to ready to see the new house? I think it will be nice to be out of the hotels, finally. Although I will have to admit I am going to miss room service.” “Yeah especially with him in your room, he is a slob.” Danielle just laughed, at that she was happy Gray was taking everything so well, with Josh and her getting together. She wasn't sure how he was going to take it. Dani kept talking about the house. “From what I understand, Annie has already hired the staff. What for I don't know. I can take care of the house just fine. Hell, we usually order out anyway. And he may be a slob, but my room won't be like your old hotel room.” As she said that the plane bounced from the touch down. She shook with a sudden shock from it. Josh again grabbed her hand, just like at take off. The three were home at last, waiting for them was, basically, a new life. As they taxied to the the terminal, they started to get ready to depart from the plane, Josh finished sliding the rolled piece of paper into the canister holder that he had been keeping in, to protect it from being crushed. Dani looked out the window and saw that out on the tarmac was a long black Limo, standing to one side was two figures. One was unmistakably the tall, blonde haired figure of Kaylee, the handmaiden for the other figure... Annie Leanan. They waited the few minutes needed for the plane to come to a stop and as soon as the lights were on, started to get up and unstrap their belongings. Shortly thereafter the doors opened and they started to file out and down the gangplank. Dani could see that the other luggage was already coming out of the storage in the back of the plane, to then be put into the large trunk of the car that Annie had come to get them in. As they approached, Kaylee bowed her head to Dani and then moved to open the rear door to the car. Annie started things off though. “Well, nice t' see ye all made it in one piece.” She purposely looked at Dani with that comment. “I know it's getting' a bit late after seven hours o' flyin' fer yet. So let's go a'ead and get ye t' where ye need t' be. It's abou' an hour drive from 'ere, mostly due t' traffic.” Josh climbed into the back of the limo and then put his hand out to help Dani in. Gray climbed in right behind him, followed by Annie. Kaylee moved to help things with the loading of the trunk of the car. Dani noticed that Kaylee was wearing a traditional uniform for chauffeurs do in England and similar places. Annie opened up the bar and let them get settled in for the trip. With three men helping her, Kaylee got the trunk loaded up and then took her spot in the driver's seat of the car. The conversation wasn't much else as they traveled across the city. Eventually they came up on a large iron gate that opened for them, automatically. Dani noticed that a sign over the gate read as 'Queensridge'. ?Where are we?? Annie looked across the backseat area at her. “Almost 'ome. Almost 'ome.” ~Queensridge Neighborhood Community~ ~North West Las Vegas~ The limo pulled up in front of the house, and came to a stop. A van sat in the driveway, a collection of people who were movers apparently. Kaylee got out first and signaled for the men who were standing there to come over and help out. They attacked the trunk storage and started carrying bags inside, with Kaylee identifying them all and taking them to where they belonged in the building. Annie then stepped out of the car, followed by the other three. Josh again got out before Dani and helped her out. Annie looked at Josh in this, and Dani just rolled her eyes at her, as if to say “I can't get him to stop doing it, so I've just given up on the notion.” The front of the house showed that it was 2 stories, and big, huge as a matter of fact. Dani almost didn't move when Annie called them forward. “C'mon now. Ye all want t' be getting some rest eventually. Ye've all got t' get ready fer this week's matches t'. So let's get inside so I can show ye around really quick. This floor is the main ground floor only from th' front. In the back it's something else.” Dani was a bit shocked. ?Three stories?? Annie looked over her shoulder in her direction. “Yes. The bottom one is partially underground. Keeps it's temperature around 'ere better. The spa, gym, one kitchen an' dinin' room... the small one, an' family room are down there. As well as the access t' th' pool. This floor is th' main livin' room, great room, main dinin' room, an' the master bed room. Top floor is the other 4 bed rooms.” Dani was obviously in awe at the description already. But when they walked into the house, into the foyer that lead to the living room, she almost felt faint. Josh moved in and grabbed her when it looked like she was getting a bit weak in the knees at it all. Annie chuckled at her. The front room had an atrium, as well as a couple of couches and a fireplace for the colder winters here. The vaulted ceiling showed that this wasn't just a regular house like she had expected. Dani was speechless as Annie started to point things out to them. “This is th' main livin' room. Entertaining guest an' all tha'. Over there is th' main dinin' room, as well as the kitchen. An tha' door back there, is the door t' the master bedroom. It's abou' two o' the other bed rooms combined. Tha's the one tha' Dani's stuff 'as been moved t'. includin' wha' she 'ad in storage from the move.” Dani walked up to the door to her new room and opened it. She only got a chance to look around a bit. Off to one side was a balcony that looked to overlook the pool area in the backyard. The bed was a large on, huge, with a conopy on it. Everything looked like the Order had emptied out their antiques from storage and put them here. The wall features, with the built in flat screen TV and such showed the modernness of the house too. ?Upstairs, the largest room is Gray's. The next largest room is Joshua's. Although as I understand it, tha's goin' t' be more of a personal storage area fer 'im now. Other arrangements 'ave been made?? Dani again blushed at someone's comments of her relationship. But it was true. She had actually asked Josh to share her room with her. She felt a need for his company more then ever after that night in the desert just a week ago. ?So where did you have them put my stuff then?? Josh asked, trying to break the attention off of Dani. “I 'ad them put it in yer room. You may move anythin' else t' where ye need it. Ye an' yer brother also 'ad stuff in long term stroage. Tha' 'as also been moved 'ere. So yer drawing desk an' all yer supplies are in there t'. As well as yer drum set. Ye personally don'na 'ave much room t' walk around in. Bu' I figured ye'd figure ou' if ye wanted stuff in another place 'er na.” Josh just nodded as he answered. “Alright.” “Each bedroom 'as it's own full bath, there are 3 other ? baths with showers, an' 2 half baths on top o' tha'. I've 'ad signs put on yer rooms fer ye two. The other two rooms? One is fer Kris when 'e gets 'ere in a week fer 'is use when in town if 'e wants t'. Kinda a guest bed room. Th' fifth one i's goni' t' be used by th' two maids we 'ired. They'll alternate days. They're only supposed t' work until after dinner, bu' sometimes they may work late, so we provided a room they'll share when needed. They're na' 'ere now. Ye'll meet them t'morrow.” Dani suddenly blurted out. ?What is this house for?? Annie spun completely around to give her the answer. “It's for you to live in.” “But we can't afford this. We don't make enough for this house, and others will know this.” Annie laughed. ?Nah, even Global will know yer livin' 'ere. Bu' as far as they know, it's based on one o' yer contracts fer moddelin'. We've funnelled in th' details. Basically from it, yer makin' jus' enough t' get a sponsored 'ome 'ere. Na' buyin' it. Bu' sponsored. It's all legal details fer yet. Bu' it's yer 'ome fer now. We've fronted th' bill. There are a few more things fer some 'o ye tha'll be 'ere in th' next few days 'er so. Bu' tha's neither 'ere nor there. I'll be takin' tha' plane t'nigh' back t' Texas, so let's get yer quicky tour done.? Annie funneled them all to a spiral staircase that went to the floor below. This was on the opposite side of the house from Dani's bedroom, and it went down to part of the lower floor that was actually partially under ground. In this room was a full spa. Not just a jacuzzi, but a small water pool that could be set to either act as a large jacuzzi or to steam the room up for a sauna. Dani could only gasp as the enormity of the whole thing. They walked through that, into a gym with quite a few machines in it. In the back a small octagon ring had been setup, to allow them to get ready for their fighting in Global. It looked to be actually very close to the size needed. But in this room, it wasn't as tall as the real one. The falling distance would be a bit off, but that wasn't too bad. From there, they went into the next room, which was a large living or family room setup. A small kitchen with breakfast bar off to the side. It was the common room for just everyday get togethers. Looking out from there, was a pool that Dani could barely believe she was seeing. All of them walked through the doors to the outside. The flood lights in the back showed Dani what she thought she was seeing. The pool was large, with a waterfall design on one side of it, coming off the house. Across the way a small patio was setup with a built in barbecue that was mounted into the wall. Next to it a small bridge went over some of the water, to a small deck that separated a small shallow pool from the rest of it. Faux rock designs covered that small jutting out and then went up to a diving platform. The shallow part snaked over and rejoined the pool. Another small piece came off and made a small intimate hot tub on the outside near where they were walking out. Dani looked up and saw that they should be right below the balcony that came off of her room. Annie giggled at the three of them just looking around in awe. “Ye three 'ave som' getting' used t' with this all. I can assure ye, th' Order 'as ye taken care of. With th' events o' last week, this case 'as been taken t' th' very top.” ?What?? Gray asked that, just blurted it out. Annie grinned an evil grin in his direction. ?Yes. Seems tha' th' Wrath 'imself 'as put someone on th' case. Last weeks events, an' the evidence we've come up with shows us tha' we 'ave an Angel 'ere. Remember, we're in a war. Either o' th' other two sides would really like t' 'ave somethin' like this on their side. We'd prefer t' see 'er grow up an' see wha' she'll become.? That got Dani's attention. ?What evidence do you have?? Annie's grin went away. “None o' tha' 'as been cleared yet. Well I can tell ye some. I can tell ye tha' ye are wha' we thought.” ?How do you know that?? “One o' our top non-researchers made a breakthrough. She's a gibborim... 'alf angel an' 'alf human as it is. She found exactly wha' tha' little trinket ye wear is.” ?Really?? Dani's interest peaked. ?Yes, an angel of th' order of the Virtues, who by beliefs, fell fer the love o' a human woman. 'Is name means “Star of God” and 'e's attributed with being on of the few who fell due t' lust. Bu' unlike th' others, 'e was let back in. We're still looking up things, but that symbol right there could very well be the symbol of your 5th great grandfather. Tha' would explain why it protected ye. But it would also show tha' th' one you call Morning Star could very well still be on this Earth, as one o' the Watchers.? Dani's face sank at that. Too many things right there, and she didn't know the full details yet. ?Ye don'na need t' be worryin' abou' stuff like tha'. It appeared tha' 'e wanted ye safe when faced with the challenge o' Jaymes an' 'is attempts to get ou' o' 'is curse. Ye three worry abou' yer upcomin' matches... especially ye young lady. This is a shot at the Platinum Title. Don'na let anythin' else get in yer way. I'll be feedin' Gray more data as we get it. As it is, the computer in 'is room 'as a report o' wha' we 'ave so far. 'E can decide what 'e wants t' do with it.? Gray nodded his head at her. ?Now, Ye'll all excuse me. Bu' I'll be leavin' ye now. I'm needed a' th' main compound fer a further meetin' on this. Mr. Joshua Brand, ye've ga' a package in yer room t', I made arrangements fer it. As well as instructions fer somethin' else. Dani, yer car is in th' garage, takin' up one o' the 14 spots it can 'andle. Gray, yer truck tha' was in long term is in there t'. Josh, follow th' instructions. T'nigh' ye can order 'er eat ou'. Tomorrow yer two ladies will be comin' in an introducin' themselves. I'm sure Gray will start ye guys usin' tha' Gym an' all. Good night, I'll pop back in a week t' see 'ow yer doin'.? At that, Annie turned and walked away from them, leaving the three of them standing out on the patio near the hot tub. She was obviously rushed, and had to get to her meeting quickly. ~A few hours later~ The house was just too big, it truly was. Dani didn't know how to really take it at all. They had ordered out for Chinese and sushi from some place down the street from the community they lived in. Now it was time to relax and try to get some rest. But Dani found herself not able to really do that just yet. She had to write stuff down, and after getting comfortable in a dressed state, while Josh managed to move most of his clothing to the second dresser and chest or drawers in her room, she took to writing in her journal. Josh though, was apparently being slow on purpose when he saw her start to get the writing in. It was almost midnight when she finally put her book down, and capped up all of the inks. Then putting the journal onto her desk to dry she decided to take in the sky a bit, walking out onto her balcony. Dressed in only a pair of satin and lace underpants and a babydoll style t-shirt, she was glad that the design on the balcony gave some privacy for the sake of the neighbors. She didn't really care, but they might. She stood there, looking out into the valley beyond the pool in what was now her backyard. She didn't even realize that Josh had come into the room behind her. “Hmm, nice moon out tonight.” Josh should have gotten a slap for that one if he had been right there. He caught her leaning on the railing, half bending over. It's no secret that Dani prefers underwear that rids up a bit, or is just a flat out thong. ?Thanks, bye the way. Are you going to finish sometime tonight?? Her hair was completely laid down, and even if she turned her head, she'd not see him too well with it like this. Although that did mean that if she changed her position, she'd probably be able to cover the assets he had mentioned. “Yes, but I wanted to get one more thing. But I had to unpack about 5 boxes to find it.” She turned around, now leaning backwards on the railing. ?What's that?? Josh, wearing only a pair of shorts, suddenly walked through the thin gossamer curtains that would give some privacy on the room from the outside on the large bay doors that separated it from the outside. As he did, she noticed something in his arms, something big... she remembered a long time ago when they were living at the academy, learning how to wrestle. He'd had a room with a balcony at the 'barracks' as they called them. On that balcony, probably the only other time that Josh has lived on his own, he had a huge electronic telescope. Some of the first, semi-personal times with him had been on that balcony with some of the others from the class. She'd spent hours upon hours looking through that telescope. And now it stood there, on her balcony, part of this big, huge, house that the Order had setup for them. ''I'll set it up to look for the Morning Star in the morning. This telescope might be the only thing that's powerful enough to get through the morning haze and smog here. But I figured it would actually get some use from you.? He moved the tripod that mounted it into position and turned it on. The gears and that suddenly reset and moved to the last saved position. Dani couldn't resist, and she headed over and peered through the eyepiece. As she started looking though it Josh started to rub her on her back. ?So, what do you think of the house?? He leaned over and kissed her on the shoulder. ?Oh, give a girl a telescope to play with and you think you can start getting intimate?? He chuckled. “No, but I'm heading to bed. I know that you'll probably be up for a while with that. Just remember, I probably need to get new batteries for it. So it won't last long. While I'm out at the dealership tomorrow, I'll look into getting some more.” ?Dealership?? Josh started to walk back into the room. Dani walked closely behind him to catch up. ?Dealership? Explain?? “Yeah, Annie's reward for my services last week. Among other things, I'll be looking into getting a car for myself. That way, I'm not relying on you or my brother, now that his truck is here.” Josh pulled back one corner of the huge bed that sat one one end of the room, moving aside the gossamer canopy. Dani looked at the telescope, that showed it had been auto shutdown by the lack of activity, it's flashing led lights now off. Josh turned to look at her just in time to get hit in the face with her shirt as she took it off and threw it at him. He held it in his hands, and then looked in her direction. “Miss, I think you dropped this.” We leave off here, and let them be alone. ~The End~
