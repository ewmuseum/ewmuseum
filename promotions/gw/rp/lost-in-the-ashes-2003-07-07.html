Lost in the ashes

7 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/7/03 Bricks, charred personal belongings, and tattered articles of clothing litter the sidewalk in front of the Kingswood Manor high rise apartment complex. Thirteen floors above a tunnel of smoke, ash, and debris billows from a gaping hole in the building. Passers by crowd the opposite side of the street in order to avoid the falling debris and gain a safe spot to gawk. The hot sun beats down on them as they all huddle in front of the fruit stand across the street, hands pressed to their eyebrows to form a visor, staring into the hole awestruck while wondering what happened. No one knows, and ultimately no one cares. It’s just something to look at and a possible subject for future gossip. One by one they all lose entrance and move on making way for a new bunch of goose necked passers by who’ll take over their old stations and stare into the gaping darkness in the side of the building only to eventually walk away with no knowledge of what went on. A cloud consisting of the charred remains of the apartment conceals Deacon Holmes, who is crouched in the one of the two remaining corners of the apartment that is furthest from the door. He sits solemnly in the corner clutching a half empty bottle of Seagram’s in his bandaged left hand. Beside him is a portable CD player blaring “Until It Sleeps” by Metallica while Holmes sings along drunkenly off key in between sips looking into the bottle as if singing to it. Where do I take this pain of mine? I run but it stays right by my side. So tear me open, pour me out. There’s things inside that scream and shout. And the pain still shakes me. So hold me, until it sleeps... Since the actions that took place at Whiplash Holmes has been holed up in the scorched remains of what was once a rather nice apartment attempting to drink his problems away. By now he was well aware of the sound of a bottle when it’s hollow and you swallow it all wallow and drown in your sorrow, and tomorrow, he’ll probably do it again. While his house in Florida was still in tact, this apartment was more home than that house would ever be. It housed all of his prized possessions, everything that actually meant something to him. Not like the house, which was filled with expensive furniture and luxury cars. The Upper Echelon had made Deacon a rich man, he easily had enough money to refurbish the apartment and restore it to it’s previous state. But nothing could buy back what had been taken from him. No dollar amount can be placed on sentiment, and the only picture he ever had of his mother was now gone. Holmes begins to wail along with the song once again. So tear me open, but beware. There’s things inside without a care. And the dirt still stains me. So wash me, until I’m clean. Holmes polishes off the last quarter of the bottle then whips it across the room and out the hole where there once stood a wall. Through the ashes he sifts out what remains of his mother’s picture. A browned Polaroid with misshapen edges due to the flames. The image once held inside the square is now abstract and indecipherable. It’s trash now. Salaok had done this. He who laughs last laughs loudest, and Salaok’s was earsplitting. Worse still, was the fact that Holmes would never gain an opportunity to exact revenge on Salaok, at least not one foreseeable in the near future. Salaok had escaped, which meant that Holmes would just have to do the next best thing. Take it out on Salomane Vation, his next opponent. Holmes slowly rises to his feet and kicks the remaining half of his scorched couch out the hole only to watch it fall down to the sidewalk below and shatter against the pavement into a pile of wood splinters and velour cushions. Just then Holmes hears a low purr and a gentle yet firm rub against his leg. He stands there pondering what it could be for a moment before realizing what it is and removing a chrome M1911A1 forty five caliber automatic pistol from the waistband of his Iceberg jeans. Twirling the gun faster and faster around his index finger Holmes slowly raises the spinning pistol above his head. Once there he stops spinning it and quickly brings it down and across his body as though he were slamming a gavel down. The barrel of the gun lands squarely between the eyes of a black cat. For a moment Holmes contemplates emptying a round or two into the helpless animal before dropping the gun back to his side. He hated cats, ever since his grandmother bought one. “Fluffy” Holmes grumbles as he thinks back to his grandmother’s overstuffed cat. It looked like a bloated pig with cocaine white fur. All Holmes remembered of the cat is that it got treated better than he did and got to sleep in his bed while he slept on the cold living room floor every night. But this cat was different, it had jet black fur and something about it’s eyes made Holmes find him tolerable. Deacon’s eyes followed the cat as he walked circles around him meowing softly. “As though things weren’t going bad enough...” Holmes sighs to himself. He picks up the cat by the back of the neck and raises it to eye level with one hand while pointing the gun at it with the other. “You can stay, but I don’t want any trouble outta” you? Holmes says to the cat. “Okay?” He asks as if he really expects and answer from the feline before dropping it back to the ground and watching in alcohol induced amazement as it lands on it’s feet. “Hey, you’re like me! “Cept I can do a flip, watch out now” Holmes warns the cat as he tucks the gun back in his waistband and gathers his wits. The cat watches on indifferently as Holmes pulls off a back flip but muffs the landing and stumbles a few steps before getting his balance back. “Now if you’re gonna be hangin” round here you’re gonna need a name.” Holmes informs the cat as they leave the ledge and walk back across the apartment. “Hows about Skipper?” Holmes suggest as he sits back down in the corner. “Nah, you’ve got too much character for that, how about Elix?” Holmes questions on second though. “Elix it is!” Holmes exclaims as the cat sits there indifferently. Suddenly the sky begins to blacken and the wind picks up. Elix goes to the ledge and investigate, a storm is coming. The first few drops of rain fall and then it’s a torrential downpour, most of which gets blown into the flame gutted apartment. Elix retreats back to the corner where Holmes sits. Just then the apartment’s door swings open allowing light from the hallway to flood a portion of the room. In walks a snooty looking man in a pinstriped suit with gray temples. Holmes watches as he walks around the apartment as if he were a forensic scientist examining a crime scene. Finally he returns to the front room where Holmes is seated. Elix hisses and begins circling the man, who just looks down haughtily and returns his focus to Holmes. The man’s name was Worthington King, the owner of the building. A faint glimmer of a smile appears on his face as he asks “Do you have any idea how much this is going to cost you Holmes?” Holmes? eyes grow wide as he looks up from the corner he sits in. “Yeah, right around nothing. Ever heard of insurance, dick?” “Yes, of course Mr.Holmes, insurance, but isn’t that for people who have accidents that aren’t their fault” Last time I checked, you did all this.” retorts the arrogant landlord in a patronizing tone. “Ever had a gun to your head Mr. King?” questions Holmes before exploding from the corner. He then grabs a two fist fulls of the man’s collar and reverses their position by whirling around, forcefully slamming him into the wall. Deacon then removes the gun from his waist and presses it firmly against the man’s jaw. “This is what is feels like, kind of a rush huh?” A muffled plea for help escapes the man’s lips but is barely audible and doesn’t sound like much of anything with his speech temporarily hindered. “What was that?” Holmes asks as he turns an ear to the man and removes the gun from his jaw. “Please don’t hurt me!” replies the man, obviously frightened as a tear wells in his eye. He pulls out a large set of keys and holds them up to Holmes while continuing his plea “Please, take any one you want. Any apartment you want! Actually, ya know what, take two! You deserve it! Just please don’t kill me, take “em, their all yours! Just please don’t kill me man, I got a girlfriend, two kids, and a wife on the side man please!” the man begs, speaking faster and faster as he continues sobbing. The left side of Deacon’s mouth curls slightly as a grin crosses over his lips. He pulls the man closer to him and whispers “I wouldn’t rent from you again if the world were flooded with piss and you owned the only tree.” The man shudders at the thought of what’s to happen next. With a tight grip on his collar Holmes throws the man to the ground behind him spins the gun around his finger before setting it’s sights on Worthington King, who is now attempting to crawl out of the room. Holmes fires one round into the floor just inches from King, causing him to stop dead in his tracks as Holmes slowly approaches. He bends down in the man’s face and whispers to him “New deal, I stay for free from now on... and I get your newspaper.” “My newspaper!”” the man shouts in an indignant tone, which causes Holmes to once again raise the pistol to his face. “Yes, your newspaper, problem?” Holmes says dryly. “No, no, of course not” replies King nervously, “but you’ve got to work with me here, these apartments are worth millions, do you know how much money I’d be losing by letting you stay here for free?” Holmes scratches his head with the gun for a moment then abruptly stops as though he’d found what he’s looking for, “Probably about as much as you lose for letting the French chick down the hall say for free... I hear she’s quite the looker, wonder how she pays for such an expensive place with no job” fires back Holmes much to the chagrin of King. “I’m sure Mrs. King would like to know of such strange happenings going on in the building... I hear she’s quite the looker as well. Maybe I’ll try my hand with her after she leaves you.” continues Holmes. “No! Here, take the keys!” Yells King as he latches on to Holmes? leg as he starts toward the door. Holmes turns around and snatches the keys from his hand then kicks King’s hands from his shin. From out nowhere a slow repetitive clap is heard coming from behind the two men. Holmes spins around one eighty and points the gun directly at the doorway to find John Zero standing there clapping. Obviously unfrightened, Zero simply tells Holmes “Nicely done.” Deacon spins the gun backwards and drops it back into his waistband with a confused look on his face. “How’d you know I was here?” “I know a lot of things.” Zero replies “This just happens to be one of them.” Holmes? shoulders drop as he realizes he’ll never get a straight answer from Zero and moves on to his next question. “Alright, well then in that case how may I help you?” “I wanted to know if you’d be interested in assisting me with some aggressive negotiations for Capo.” responds Zero still standing in the doorway. “Aggressive negotiations?” asks Holmes, obviously puzzled. “Yes, negotiations involving a pistol.” answers Zero. Holmes replies “I’m in.” after selecting an apartment key from the ring and dropping the rest onto the floor. “I’ll drive.” says Zero as they start for the door. “No, I’ll drive.” retorts Holmes causing Zero to glare at him with a certain fiery look in his eye. “Fine.” replies Holmes in a disappointed tone, dropping his shoulders as the two men step into the hall leaving Worthington King still on the floor with Elix still hissing at him. Suddenly Holmes pops his head back inside the open door. After a quick look around the room “And clean this shit up, this place looks like a dump...” comes out of his mouth in a smug tone. He then points at the cat while cocking an eyebrow and says “Hold down the fort while I’m done.” That said Holmes closes the door and heads off.
