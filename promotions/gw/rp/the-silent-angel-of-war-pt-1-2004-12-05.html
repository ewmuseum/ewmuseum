The Silent Angel of War Pt.1

5 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-05-04 Silent Angel of War ”Why… am I… like this?” The swindling words sway with the wheat as his hands brush up against their golden tips. A harvest of wheat spread throughout what were cornfields is seen for miles. From his black loose fit jeans, up pass his white tucked in muscle shirt, to his now almost black hair, he stands taking in this sunset as he runs his hands through the wheat. ”I’m not psychotic, paranoid, or even demented, I’m just different… But why?” Walking through the fields he leaves a path of fallen wheat behind him, and marks his path in the field distinctively. A confused offspring of emotions distort his face as his eyes stare at the ground and he continues in a straight trail. ”Those papers were something I’d never seen before, something that made me question my life. Chemical research, bio tests, and experiments done to me. I just want to know so many things… But, don’t know who to ask.” A single part of white hair falls down between his forehead and curves past his eyebrow and the corner of his eye. His goatee now almost black as well, with small white hairs showing near the bottom of his chin where the goatee hangs down two or three inches. His eyes close, as his face tells a story of a man searching deep inside for answers. ”Why me? What in me was so special? Why couldn’t they pick someone else? Why not choose Akodo Rokku? Or maybe Josh? No, why not someone messed up like Salaok? Or Silent Wind? Those people have issues, but me… I’m just a washed up wrestler, with a history of drinking and giving up…” Opening his eyes he peers at a hole ahead of him. The ground dug up and scattered around it, as a shovel lies in the hole. ”What is it that makes me the one who always has to be fucked with?” Reaching in his pocket, his hand not in any hurry, before pulling out a small hand sized box. Bringing it to chest level, he opens it up with the flick of the wrist and stares at what lies within. A crystal clear white diamond on a silver ring with “Forever Our Love Will Last” is engraved around it. ”I can’t see myself being some type of protégé to what possibly is a project of mass destruction. But, if these people think they can get away with whatever they did to me, without even the smallest bit of redemption from my part…” Closing the box he kisses it as a tear falls from the corner of his eye onto the box, almost instantly after he kisses it. He puts it to his chest for a few seconds and stares up into the lightly lit sun, as the wind begins to blow and the wheat around him turns direction. A smile crosses his face faintly, before he tosses the box into the hole. ”Then they have another thing coming.” He folds his arms across his chest and stares down as the sun drowns the sky and makes it too bright to open your eyes and look a foot ahead of you. *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** The office doors swing open, followed by a quick flash of a man with a black trench coat on. Swinging two sawed off shot guns from the inside of the trench coat the man points them at two of the 10 men at the long wooden oak office table. “I want answers… and I want them now. Understood?” The 10 men immediately responded with nods, but didn’t speak a word. The mask on Jesse’s face gives him the undeniable protection of his identity not being revealed as he steps up on a chair and onto the wooden oak table and walks down the middle of it. ”Who the hell did experiments in an abandoned hospital in New Jersey last weekend.” Nobody answered, and this ticked Jesse off. Pointing the shotgun barrel straight at one of the pudgier men in the room, he locked and cocked the gun, ready to blow his head off. ”I guess your deaf, so I’ll speak a little louder. WHO IS IN CHARGE OF THE BIOCHEMICAL TESTS RAN IN NEW JERSEY?!?!” His finger pressures the trigger, clicking it back further and further as nobody raises their voice, before finally Jesse raises the gun from the man and shoots a hole in the wall. ”I know…” Says a man from the other side of the table. His face pale white, as his eyes rang a dark brown and he stumbled with his next few words. ”A project ... we’ve been funding… was their… an… we… just know who they are. Not what they were doing.” Jesse’s eyes leapt from the fat pudgy man to the man behind him, whom was much smaller, and he stood their looking as if to believe the man or not to. BANG! He didn’t believe him, and he paid for giving him bullshit. The man next to him sat frozen in fear, his eyes wider than a rhinoceroses nostrils. The blood splattered from the guns hot onto the man’s face, giving him not only the gun to be afraid of but another man’s blood all over him. ”If you keep lying, that will be the result… So somebody, speak up or I’m going to get trigger happy!” As he turned and said all this to the room, one man stood up and raised a card in the direction of Jesse. ”The address is on this card sir. Now please will you leave us?” The man showing no fear, only guts, which may be his downfall, as Jesse takes the card from the man and smiles. ”Thank you… Now have a good night.” With that, a gunshot echoes the walls of the room. Followed by a thud, and then another thud, and the doors open before Jesse exits the room and leaves it in silence. *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** A foot trail of blood, a slight breathe echoes in a silent room, as a splash is heard, and water smears the blood across the floor. Their in a tub sits the behemoth Jesse Jamester, a crimson mess as he washes the blood off his body and lets the water in the tub soak it up turning a light red. Taking his fingers he clears the blood that is speckled around his eyes in dots, and patiently wipes it away. No words, no utters of disgust, just simple motions of his hands that continue to wash away the blood. On his back a jab wound is seen, right below his collarbone. The wound oozes blood, but not vastly. Reaching over the shoulder and touching the wound Jesse closes his eyes and clenches his teeth as he sticks his pinky finger in it and then applies pressure with the other fingers around the wound. Blood builds up around the pinky and drains down his back for a minute or so, before slowly fading to a stop. Sitting back Jesse looks up at the ceiling and stares off into his own world. ”I’ve gone this far, I cannot stop now. I’ve stained these hands with blood that will never be able to be washed completely clean. But one thing is for sure, I’m not a killer, because I don’t exist. These men, they deserved this… They deserved the cruel fate in which I gave them. This they asked for, and I cannot deny what is asked for when I’ve been given so much. It’s hard to believe, that now, with these sick incidents happening, that I, one man, can get away with it just because my name is not valid as a serial killer. I know that as soon as the police get involved, they’ll be looking for some psycho path, not me. Even though, I’m dead… So, yeah, I’m safe… For now anyway.” Cupping a handful of the bath water, Jesse splashes it on his face and drinks some, before squirting it up and out like a fountain back into the tub. The muggy blood tainted water, he just spit, and yet no care of it. ”Theirs gotta be a reason, a notice, as to why… Why I was the one to be experimented on, and exactly what happened to me. I don’t feel different. Maybe a bit psychotic now and then, but definitely I don’t feel any different physically. Unless, they were trying to use me to make some type of disease or what not? What if I am a disease? This psychotic streak all a master plan from the person who did the experiments, and now he’s playing chess with his enemies? What if…” He shakes his head and turns closes his eyes again, this time, dozing off into a dream world, so far away, even the evil in him would dare not to go. *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** “This was what it was all for Jesse.” The voice made chills go down his back as he was held their by 3 men whom have chains that connect to his safe-jacket. Jesse’s eyes narrow, as his lips purse, and he spits on the mans shoes whom speaks the words. ”You’ll go to hell for this…” ”Oh, I know that, but I won’t be the only one on that trip.” Disgusted more now than ever before, Jesse turns his face from the man. The man’s face being scarred three times with what looks to be slash marks from a claw, and his hair receding back, balding in the forehead. Yet, a sharp dresser, as the man sports a black Italian made suit with white undershirt and a buckle with the letters “TBC” engraved on it. ”See Jesse, you cost me the world in which I loved… The wrestling world, and since your run in with cancer oh about a year ago, I’ve taken upon myself to use you, as a test subject. You may think I’ve cruel, but really it’s an eye for an eye. I’m doing what I would have been doing had I been able to wrestle, to you now…” A smirk comes across the mans face, as if he’s ready to laugh, but he holds it in as Jesse begins to speak. ”And what would that be exactly? Get your ass handed to you? Because that’s how I feel right now, like some slave in a suit waiting to be executed. If you want to fight TBC, then FIGHT ME LIKE A MAN!” Laughing breaks out from the man Jesse referred to as TBC. He turns and slaps Jesse with the back of his hand and then snarls at him as he leans closer and gets in his face. ”No you idiot! I’m not going to fight a perfect specimen! Do you think I’m insane!” ”Perfect specimen?” ”Yes… I’ve given you the power to become perfect, I’ve engineered anti-bodies that will increase your endurance, your speed, your strength, muscle growth, and brain activity. It was illegal and risky, but I felt it a necessary operation under the circumstances of well… you know what.” The man opening his jacket up along with his shirt, and revealing a stint around his chest, in which a steel plate protects the skin of his left pectoral muscle. Lifting the steel plate, you can see the man’s lung through transparent skin. ”You almost took it Jess, too bad eh… I’m betting you wished you had now huh?” A slight smile of greed and agreement comes over Jesse as he looks at the wound and then stares at TBC’s face and begins to chuckle insanely. ”Oh you think it’s funny? You think almost ending my life is funny? Putting me out of the work in which was my pure enjoyment of life is funny? Well how funny is this!” A television monitor behind TBC lights up as the lights go out and Jesse stops talking and sees his ex-wife Julie, on the screen. Her face instantly lights Jesse’s eyes up as she is being taped and doesn’t know it in a store. She leaves the store and heads across the street to the ATM. Getting out some money she then goes back to her car and leaves. The screen flickers from her getting in the car to where she is tied up to a chair and she has cuts all over her chest and face, small gruesome flesh torn off cuts, only made to inflict pain. Tears roll down Jesse’s cheeks as he now realizes his wife’s fate. She wasn’t killed in an accident at the grocery store, no… She was killed by TBC, as she was kidnapped, and tortured, until her body could no longer take it, and she gave up. Like the world is falling, and the tides of humanity are dying, Jesse crumbles to his knees, and though the men try to pull him back up, TBC only smiles as he lets the tape roll and enjoys Jesse’s inside pain developing and breaking him down like a virus. With the flick of the hand, TBC instructs them to take him away. Not fighting it, Jesse is dragged by the chains as he lies on the floor and is taken through a door. *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** A padded room, completely closed, no window, a metal door in which is also padded, is all that surrounds Jesse in this room meant to help those unable to cope with life, to let out their angers. Yet for Jesse, it’s a room of memories that stabbed him in every spot of his heart and mind until he’d eventually break down and give up. ”… I should of known it. There is no such thing as an accident in this world that would be so mean to such an innocent soul as Julie. ‘She was my everything, and she did no wrong, so why would God take her from me’ is all I asked after her death… Now I realize, god didn’t take her, he did… He took her from me, and is now using me in ways he must to get the satisfaction of guilt off his mind. I can’t stop him. I don’t know how he is doing this, but I feel stuck in this world in which he has forced upon me. It may be this padded room that makes me think this, but inside, I feel controlled in other ways, in a constraint to her, I feel I can’t hurt him just yet… Even though that’s all I want to do. I want to live, but not like this… I want to carry on my life like I did for those five years after her death. I’d overcome it, but now I’m to face it again. WHY!? Why must I feel this pain? Why must someone like me, someone who only wished to rid the world of another sorrow, be convicted of such ruthless content? Is it my greed? Is it my pride? Is it my quest for glory? What is it that makes me so unable to live a normal life… That’s all I want to know.” Tears drown his face as he falls into the corner and stares at the door just praying it’d open, but unfortunately it does not. ”In this corner I’ll lie, until death either takes me, or he comes back to torture me more with footage of how he killed her. The sick bastard… he will pay, somehow, someway, he will pay. Maybe not by my hands, or even by my word, but I can feel it, he’ll pay the price for this… He thinks I took his wrestling career, but in reality, I saved the world of wrestling the agony of his pitiful skills. He’s a two bit wrestler, and a third rate man. All of which he may have realized had he stepped back and looked at himself in the mirror, but he was too obsessed with taking over the company, and giving himself shots, that he forgot what wrestling was all about. The entertainment we deliver, the fans that come through the doors paying their last cents to see us give them the best show ever, no matter what consequences our bodies endure. He lost sight of what I did, and what I showed in that ring, and instead… he made wrestling a soap opera, a mere symbol of show business, in which degraded an athlete like myself. That… I wouldn’t allow, and he knew it. He couldn’t stop me though, and instead took a shot to the chest, and paid for trying to screw up my world. I may lose this war, but I know… that I’ve saved a legacy in which I want to carry on through my son Julius… And that’s already happening, so it doesn’t matter what happens to me now.” As the voice in his head ends it’s talking, the door creeks open and the three men from earlier walk in and pick Jesse up. No resistance, just cooperation is what Jesse gives them, as he gets up and lets them shackle him and guide him out. Seeing TBC on the outside of the room, he lowers his head and looks at the ground, as he cannot face this man in which killed his wife, without seeing her screams through his eyes. He closes his eyes and awaits what will be his fate, a slow, cruel, unknown fate, ending his life for sure, as the papers said. ”What is wrong with you? Afraid I’m going to do something to you?” Eyes still closed, he rises his head up and nods. ”Well your in luck… Because I don’t want to waste billions of dollars of research for my own personal satisfaction today. No, I want to mold you into my perfection project. So, if you will… (laughs), excuse me, I keep forgetting I call the shots. Anyway, you will be taken to be injected with my new syrup, in which I’ll test on the fellow members of the Global roster at Showdown in Tokyo.” Eyes shaking as they open, Jesse realizes what is going to happen, TBC isn’t going to kill him, he’s going to use Jesse as his overgrown pet, in which he’ll order around and teach new tricks to via the syrup he injects in him. ”Your going to experiment me on other wrestlers? But what if I become insane like before?” ”It’s a risk I’m willing to take Jess. All in the game of science, you win some, and you lose some… We’ll see which tonight.” The knobs in Jesse’s head turn a million miles per second as he thinks to what TBC is saying. If he is serious, he’s referring to the massacre of thousands of innocent people. And that, would mean the end of Global Wrestling as we know it… Epilogue All is what seems for sure Oceans of crimson tides Cities of battered bodies Air of deathly stench Gunshots echo in the air Screams are ensued Whistling through the empty barrel As a whimper escapes her lips Pierced in the forehead Blood dribbles down her cheek Eyes of a dawning dead Shine through the lifeless face SMASH! Skull compresses in Kicked to the side Ooze of the inside Pours to the outside Leaving from within A hollow minded emptiness An assassin’s balcony Perfect silence Drowning sun Purple hazing sky Fading… away.
