Killer Pirates#3: Narrow Escape

21 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/21/05 Killer Pirates#3: Narrow Escape ---- Page 1 PANEL 1 [Isle of Tortuga, Caribbean.] [Captain Jack Morgan and the 'Killer Klown from Outer Space' are peering around a corner.] 'I wonder where they went?' Jack says with a sense of urgency. PANEL 2 [Jack looks around once more.] 'Coast seems to be clear, mate,' he says. 'Let's get a move on.' The 'Killer Klown replies 'Rightiando.' PANEL 3 [Jack and the clown make their way out into the open.] 'This could be a trip to Davy Jones' locker for us, mate,' Jack says. PANEL 4 [Jack pulls out his fake pirate sword and turns around.] 'Maybe sooner than I thought,' Jack says with a scowl on his face. ---- Page 2 PANEL 1 [Jack takes swipes at three of Darth von Doom and the Horny Mathematician's zombie stormtroopers that have been sent there to kill them.] PANEL 2 [Jack swipes again with his sword and slashes two of them.] PANEL 3 [Jack leaps back as the zombies fall over in a heap.] PANEL 4 'Kickia assiando!' the 'Killer Klown' exclaims with a grin on his face. [Jack smiles back at him as he walks away from the zombie carcasses.] ---- Page 3 PANEL 1 'Begad!' Jack exclaims. 'That was funner than the time we got addled on rum and used all the doubloons from the swag on wenches!' PANEL 2 [The 'Killer Klown' nods his head in agreement.] [Jack looks around.] 'But we'd best get moving quickly,' he says. 'Darth von Doom is not one to let us stay out of his evil grip for long.' 'Whatiando deosa wesa makeina a dose nowsa?' the clown asks in his weird alien language. PANEL 3 [Jack shakes his head.] 'I don't know,' he replies. 'But whatever we do, we must do it in a way that avoids detection from Darth von Doom.' PANEL 4 [Jack continues on.] 'He is far too dangerous to let us go on living without him knowing about it,' Jack says. 'He probably can already sense that his zombie troops have failed and will come here himself to face us if he has to.' PANEL 5 [Jack looks intently at the clown.] 'That bilge-sucking cyborg will feel Davy Jones' grip if it's the last thing we do, right mate?' Jack says. PANEL 6 [The 'Killer Klown' nods his head.] PANEL 7 [Jack hears something in the distance.] Jack says, 'Now let's get the move on before they come to give us the black spot.' ---- Page 4 PANEL 1 [The Island of Misfit Wrestlers. Once the home of many misfit and unwanted wrestlers but now it is a dead zone. Well, save for the activity in the Jobber Temple.] PANEL 2 [Darth von Doom has one of his former trainees, HIV Joey, in a chokehold, raising him high above the ground.] 'Where is the pirate scum now?' Darth von Doom asks in an angry tone. PANEL 3 [Darth von Doom pulls Joey's bloodied face closer to his. Joey is already severely injured from the lighstaber wound von Doom gave him during the original invasion of the island.] In a muffled voice, Joey replies 'I wouldn't tell you a thing...traitor...' PANEL 4 [Darth von Doom howls with frustration as he throws Joey across the room as his master, the Horny Mathematician, comes stalking into the room.] 'I see your interrogation methods are improving to the new standards set by the Bush Administration,' the Mathematician says with a smile. PANEL 5 'Don't do anything foolish, Lord von Doom,' the Mathematician says. ---- Page 5 PANEL 1 'Those Pirates could be a danger to us,' Lord von Doom replies. PANEL 2 [The Mathematician looks him down.] 'That is foolishness,' the Mathematician says in a cold tone. 'I have the power of the origin of the universe. The tip of my penis is just the tip of the iceberg.' PANEL 3 'This is not about the Pirates,' the Mathematician replies. 'As you have foreseen, they survived the zombies on Tortuga. But now we have another problem. My Cosmic Rod prototype that they stole is now in the hands of the Gaylord of the Universe, who can come and go as he pleases.' PANEL 4 'Go out and destroy the Gaylord of the Universe,' the Mathematician says. 'Go find Straightor. He will be able to help you.' [Darth von Doom bows before the Mathematician.] 'Yes, my Master,' Lord von Doom says. PANEL 5 [Crab Man, another minion of the Circus of DOOM~!, comes walking over.] 'Lord von Doom, Mathematician, the Killer Pirates are apparently meeting with Adam and Matt on the Isle of Tortuga,' Crab Man reports to them. [Lord von Doom looks at him.] 'Then I must find them and destroy them,' Lord von Doom replies. ---- Page 6 PANEL 1 [The Leaky Penis, one of the most notorious hangouts of pirates in the Caribbean and the Galaxy. Part pub, pub whorehouse, it sets up a joyous time for all who enter its doors.] PANEL 2 'JACK! CLOWN' Matt exclaims. PANEL 3 [Jack and the clown walk over to where Adam and Matt are seated.] Jack replies 'It's good to see you too, mates.' [Jack looks around.] 'But there is much to discuss,' he says. PANEL 4 [Jack and the clown sit down at the table.] 'That swab Darth von Doom is out to kill us all now.' PANEL 5 [Adam and Matt gasp with shock.] 'James von Doom lives?' they both ask in unison. ---- Page 7 PANEL 1 [Jack grimly nods his head.] Jack replies, 'Yes, lads. Unfortunately, it is true.' PANEL 2 [Adam and Matt look shocked.] Adam replies 'But we...but we killed him.' [Matt nods his head.] Matt says 'Yeah. We chopped off three of his limbs and watched him burn. How could he still be alive?' PANEL 3 [Jack looks grimly at them.] 'He was kept alive by a cybernetic body suit created for him by the Horny Mathematician,' Jack says. 'He is now a walking death machine called Darth von Doom who kills people at a whim and carries around a crimson lightsaber. Two men - ONLY TWO - killed everything we worked so hard to achieve.' PANEL 4 'Kill von Doom and the Mathematician we must,' Jack says. PANEL 5 [Matt agrees.] 'James helped create the Island of Misfit Wrestlers only to be manipulated into destroying it himself,' Matt says. 'Kill them we must.' PANEL 6 [As Matt sips his mug of rum, Adam chimes in with his thoughts.] 'But if we go on a mission to kill them,' Adam says. 'Won't that lead us to the Comedy Side of the Force like what happened to James?' PANEL 7 'To return order to the world of fighting,' Adam continues. 'Why must we use the thing that destroyed it to save it?' ---- Page 8 PANEL 1 [Jack looks at them.] 'Because we have to,' Jack replies. 'That is the only way to bring balance back to the world of fighting. And, to top that off, we have to face Projekt Mayhem and those superheroes Justice Inc. in a tag match.' PANEL 2 'I always liked Justice Inc.' Adam replies. 'Me too,' Matt says in agreement. PANEL 3 'But they hate us, mates,' Jack replies. 'They can't stand everything that we stand for. And Projekt Mayhem are a bunch of tough customers. We have many problems to deal with, not to mention Darth von Doom and the evil Circus of DOOM~!' PANEL 4 'And thus, we must crush them like dust,' Jack replies. 'And then go on to face the Hammer Brothers and steal their plunder! The GLOBAL Tag Team Championship belts!' PANEL 5 [Just as suddenly as Jack makes his proclamation, they all sense a disturbance in the Force.] ---- Page 9 PANEL 1 [Just then, the door of the Leaky Penis swings open and Darth von Doom comes marching in with his lightsaber already ignited and ready for a deadly confrontation with ones he once called friends.] PANEL 2 [Adam and Matt pull out the prototype lightsabers they stole from the Mathematician's lab all those many months ago and ignite them. Jack pulls out his pirate sword as the 'Killer Klown' pulls out a unicycle and starts driving around in circles.] PANEL 3 [Darth von Doom, spewing with hatred for his former friends, marches in with a purpose. His latest cronies, Crab Man and Lobster Man, are by his side.] PANEL 4 [Matt and Adam look ready for a rematch.] 'He is one but we can take him,' Matt says. 'And besides, he's got crabs.' [Jack eyes Crab Man and Lobster Man.] 'Fiendish monsters of the deep,' Jack bellows. 'We shall destroy you and send you back to Davy Jones' locker where you came from.' ---- Page 10 PANEL 1 [Darth von Doom charges at Adam and Matt, striking at them.] 'You stupid fools!' he hollers out angrily. PANEL 2 'We are not stupid, James,' Adam says. 'We are simply Real Juggaloes.' PANEL 3 [Darth von Doom holds his lightsaber close. Bar patrons, wenches, and pirates alike scatter for their lives to avoid the fighting.] 'The first of you who bows down before me lives,' Darth von Doom says. 'The rest of you will die at my hands.' PANEL 4 [Matt charges at James from behind.] 'It is you who will die Lord von Doom!' Matt screams out. PANEL 5 [Darth von Doom knocks Matt out using an invisible Force blow.] ---- Page 11 PANEL 1 [Adam watches on in horror.] 'NO!' he screams out. PANEL 2 [Darth von Doom and Adam begin their duel.] PANEL 3 [Jack and Crab Man start fighting as Lobster Man chases the unicycle riding 'Killer Klown' around the bar.] PANEL 4 [Adam tries to push Darth von Doom back but it's not working.] PANEL 5 [The 'Killer Klown' honks the horn of his unicycle at Darth von Doom as Lobster Man continues to chase him around.] 'Moviatu waysa!' the clown exclaims. [Darth von Doom gives him a look.] 'I always hated him,' Darth von Doom grumbles. ---- Page 12 PANEL 1 [Darth von Doom knocks Adam down with a forearm shot as he continues to hold onto his lightsaber.] PANEL 2 [Adam is lying in an unconscious heap on the floor.] PANEL 3 [Lobster Man lunges for the 'Killer Klown' but nails Darth von Doom instead.] 'SMACK!' PANEL 4 [Darth von Doom shoots him a look.] PANEL 5 [Lobster Man looks back at him with mortal dread.] ---- Page 13 PANEL 1 [Lobster Man has a look of sheer terror on his face.] 'NO LORD VON DOOM!' he pleads. 'I DID NOT MEAN TO STRIKE YOU! IT WAS AN HONEST MISTAKE! I ADMIT GUILT BUT PLEASE DO NOT KILL ME!' [HA HA! Bye, bye Lobster Man!] PANEL 2 [Lobster Man grabs hold of his throat and gurgles.] 'GWARPH!' PANEL 3 [Lobster Man falls to a heap on the floor of the Leaky Penis.] PANEL 4 [Jack then lunges at Darth von Doom from behind.] ---- Page 14 PANEL 1 [Darth von Doom senses his attack just in time to lunge out of the way.] 'Foolish pirate,' he mutters. PANEL 2 [Only as Darth von Doom moves out of the way, Crab Man runs right into him!] 'BAP!' PANEL 3 [Darth von Doom looks at him.] PANEL 4 [Crab Man looks like he's about claw his face off with his giant crab claws.] PANEL 5 [Crab Man starts to gurgle.] 'GRRGHHHH!' PANEL 6 [Crab Man lies on the floor in a heap.] ---- Page 15 PANEL 1 [The Pirates look on with amusement.] 'That's what he gets for hiring seafood to do his dirty work, right mate?' Jack asks the clown. The clown replies 'Rightiando.' PANEL 2 [Darth von Doom then turns in the Pirates' direction.] PANEL 3 'You fools should've left when you had the chance,' Lord von Doom says coldly. 'I may have underestimated your powers, but you are still no match for the Power of the Comedy Side of the Force.' PANEL 4 [Jack laughs.] 'Yeah, you and what army, mate?' Jack mockingly says aloud. PANEL 5 [Darth von Doom snaps his fingers.] ---- Page 16 PANEL 1 [Suddenly a whole bunch of Celine Dion clones appear all over the bar.] 'In case you hadn't noticed by now,' Darth von Doom proclaims. 'I now have the power to create characters and objects at will thanks to my new bodysuit.' PANEL 2 'Too bad for you,' he continues. 'I was just starting to like you idiots.' PANEL 3 [Darth von Doom commands his army of Celine Dion clones to do their worst.] 'SING!' he yells at them. 'SING DAMMIT! SING UNTIL THEY CAN TAKE NO MORE!' PANEL 4 [And they do so with demented glee. They sing the most horrifying song that any pirate can hear. 'My Heart Will Go On' from the Titanic soundtrack!] ---- Page 17 PANEL 1 [Jack and the 'Killer Klown' cover their ears from the horrible, terrifying noise that they are stuck listening to.] PANEL 2 [Darth von Doom cackles with demented glee.] 'NO ONE CAN HANDLE CELINE DION!' Lord von Doom exclaims. 'SOON YOU WILL BE MY PRISONERS!' PANEL 3 [Jack covers his ears even harder with his hands.] 'Can't take much more of this...' he barely mumbles out in obvious pain. PANEL 4 [Jack then looks over and see a jukebox. This gives him an idea.] ---- Page 18 PANEL 1 [Jack then does the unthinkable. He uncovers his ears and grabs a mug from the table.] PANEL 2 [He looks over at the clown and the clown does the same, grabbing the other mug from off of the table.] PANEL 3 [Darth von Doom looks on, perplexed.] PANEL 4 [Jack readies his mug.] 'Aim for the jukebox buttons,' he yells to the clown. [The clown nods his head.] PANEL 5 [Darth von Doom looks over at the jukebox just as they throw the mugs.] 'Clever boy,' Lord von Doom whispers. PANEL 6 [The mugs hit the jukebox and shatter on impact.] PANEL 7 [Loud rock music starts to drown out the Celine Dion choir of DOOM~!] ---- Page 19 PANEL 1 [Darth von Doom growls with anger.] 'Now that really pisses me off,' Lord von Doom angrily growls. PANEL 2 [Jack and the 'Killer Klown' make a break for the door.] 'Go for the door...QUICK!' Jack yells out. 'Rightiando!' the clown replies. PANEL 3 [The clown pushes the Celine Dions out of the way and pushes Jack out the door.] PANEL 4 [Darth von Doom can't believe it. He uses his bodysuit to create a banana peel.] PANEL 5 [Darth von Doom throws the banana peel at the 'Killer Klown.'] ---- Page 20 PANEL 1 [The 'Killer Klown' slips on the banana peel as it hits the ground.] PANEL 2 [Jack looks back and sees the clown sliding toward him.] PANEL 3 [Jack's eyes widen.] 'BLIMEY!' Jack exclaims. PANEL 4 [Darth von Doom's plan backfires as the 'Killer Klown' slides right into Jack and out of the bar, closing the door behind them as he slides out.] ---- Page 21 PANEL 1 [Darth von Doom puts his hands on his hips.] 'Dammit,' Lord von Doom growls in frustration. PANEL 2 [The bar walls start shaking.] PANEL 3 [All of the Celine Dions fall to the ground dead.] PANEL 4 [Darth von Doom looks down at all the dead Celine Dion clones lying on the ground only to be interrupted by the presence of another Circus of DOOM~! member by the name of Dumpster Man.] 'What is it?' Lord von Doom asks before Dumpster Man can even get past just opening the door.' 'The Horny Mathematician requests your presence back at the Island of Misfit Wrestlers.' PANEL 5 [Darth von Doom looks at him, sensing something is wrong.] 'What is it?' Lord von Doom asks. ---- Page 22 PANEL 1 [Back at the Jobber Temple, the Cosmic Rod Prototype that the Killer Pirates stole the week before has opened up a portal and out through the portal have come the battling forces of Straightor, leader of the Heterosexual Side of Sexuality, and the Gaylord of the Universe, leader of the Homosexual Side of Sexuality. The Horny Mathematician is cowering under a table in the background, holding his ever precious papers on the theory of the origin of the universe.] 'Damn you Gaylord!' Straightor exclaims as they clash swords. 'You followed us through the portal!' PANEL 2 [The Homophobic Priest and Redneck Robby, Straightor's minions, are stuck battling the Gaylord's minions, who are the Man with Purse, the Carpet Licking Ballerina or CLB, and the ever popular Body Cavity Search Police Officer. The Priest strikes at them with his giant cross of DOOM~!] 'You're all going to Hell!' the Homophobic Priest exclaims. 'And you're going feel the love of the rainbows,' the Body Cavity Search Police Officer replies. PANEL 3 [Straightor looks around and then pulls a smoke bomb out from his belt.] 'Straight people...RUN AND HIDE!' he says. PANEL 4 [Straightor tosses it down and a cloud of smoke appears everywhere.] PANEL 5 [Nothing is seen except for smoke and the only thing heard is coughing.] PANEL 6 [The smoke clears and Straightor and his minions are gone. Gaylord looks around.] 'Come on!' Gaylord exclaims. 'Let's find them and show them the power of Gaity!' 'YEAH!' the Man with Purse exclaims. PANEL 7 [The minions of homosexuality run off in search of the minions of straightness. But the portal is still open and four more creatures come through. The Mathematician clutches at his papers as the minions of Bestiality are sick of watching the war. Now, they have come to win it and defeat the two warring parties after spending years watching them fight. Now, the war begins!] (/END/)
