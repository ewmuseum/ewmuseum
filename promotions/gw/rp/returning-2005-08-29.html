Returning

29 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8 29 05 It had been a while since Serpenti had seen the City of lights that is Las Vegas. Serpenti sighed as the city loomed below the approaching airplane. Serpenti sat in the private jet liner, Dressed in black dress pants, and a green and gold silk shirt, Of course a mask donned his head, this one solid black with a green and gold snake along each side of his face rising over his eyes to meet each other in the middle of his head. His trip to Mexico and then South America left him full of more questions about his parents then ever before. Serpenti sighed as he thought about his trip. His parents had defiantly been there and sent him a map of a temple in South America. Strangely the letter was dated ten years ago, five years after his parents vanished. But the postal worker said they had been paid and ordered not to deliver it till now. However Serpenti could not find the location of the temple. The strings of his life pulled him back to Vegas, as his granted leave time was getting shorter?. Plus he missed Teresa so much, another reason to return. Plus he promised her and Melina that he would indeed return. Serpenti looked down to the box in his hand wrapped in paper, with pink ribbon tying all together. A present he bought for Melina as promised when he left Las Vegas. Another sigh escapes from Serpenti. At least the trip was not a complete waist. He met a good friend, and now was bringing him to Vegas. He had a good visit with his uncle and ant and his many cousins in Mexico. “So not a total failure” Came a voice familiar to Serpenti. The voice coming from a Small white monkey Sitting next to Serpenti, This however was no normal monkey and was a spirit guild of Serpenti’s. Serpenti looked over the Blue and White clad man, with a blue mask and long shaggy white hair. The man lay passed out and sleeping. “No, I guess it was not a total loss after all.” El Lobo M’scara. Serpenti had found him in a small cantina in San Jose, Costa Rica. For some reason he was banished from Mexico. Something about an affair with a politicians daughter. Needless to say Lobo was intrigued when the two met. Wondering what a fellow Luchador was doing in his cantina. After hearing Serpenti’s story Lobo had decided to join with this Luchador and aid him in his trip to Brazil. The two became good friends and Lobo asked Serpenti if he could help him come to America. A few calls and Lobo was signed to Global as Serpenti’s Tag team partner. Los Cazadores; A pretty good name for the two as that is what they had become Hunters. ? I have good news Gaspar.” Said the Monkey. “Ohh yes?” Serpenti said unsure. “I have been giving the approval to be seen.” The Monkey said. “Well that’s is good, At least people will not think I am a insane man in a mask talking to a invisible monkey.” Serpenti said with a slight smile. “Nope” Just a Insane Man in a mask.” The Monkey said with a small laugh. “Wait. Will they here you talk” ? Serpenti asked as his eyes got a bit bigger. “No, All they will be able to hear is normal monkey sounds.” “Good, It would not be a good thing Amigo, if otherwise.” Serpenti said. “You know I have always wondered. Why send you in the form of a monkey, and not a snake?” Serpenti asked scratching his head. “Well simple really. For one, all do not like snakes; a snake that talks is liked by even less. SO they sent me to you in the guise of a monkey. It was that or a cat. And I did not want to be a cat again.” The Monkey said. “Ohh okay. Just wondered.” Serpenti said. The plane jolted as it touched down signaling the trip was finally over. Lobo woke up from his sleep with a roar and stretch and the two Luchadores stepped out of the plane and down the sitars. A car waited then along with a Man in a blue suit and black sunglasses. “Mr. Serpenti and Mr. M’scara. Please come with Me. Serpenti You have a match tonight. Miss Reno has sent me to bring you to the Arena.” The man said in a deep voice. “Now?” But I just got back?.. Very Well Amigo.. I guess it is best to jump back into the fire then sizzle in the pan.” Serpenti said with a smile. “Come Lobo Tonight you get to meet your fellow Global Fighters.” “Both climbed into the car as their luggage was loaded into the trunk of the car. Once all was in the Man stepped on the gas and speed off.” Once again the fast paced lifestyle of Global had consumed him once more. “Tonight I shall introduce you to my Amigos here in Vegas. I think you will like it here.” “Hum” A bit to bright for my liking? We shall have to see though.” The two conversed together as the driver turned onto the road heading for the Global Arena.
