The Keeper of Oblivion

28 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/28/05 Silence. Darkness. This is how it always began. This is how it will always begin. Although this time there were differences. There were complications. Still Leinad Soma had not recovered from the stake he received at Whiplash last year. He lay there in his pit and for the first time he was afraid. The fear of death began to haunt the creature of eons, but something loomed in the shadows like a vulture over rancid game. 'Come out. My body maybe impaired but my senses are not.' Still there was silence. Darkness. 'So it has come to this. Some childe has come to give me my end when I can not fight back. Well let us make this quick.' 'I may not be as old as you but I can assure you I'm no childe.'A voice said from the dark. 'Only a childe hides in the darkness waiting to kill injured prey.' 'Only a coward tries to talk his way out of something.' Soma smiled at the comment. 'So you have come for me. For the blood I’m guessing.' 'If you would wait to hear what others say, you may actually learn something.' Soma let out a laugh. It was not often he got banter back and forth with someone who would actually not back down. Still Soma did not get up, the pain too unbearable even for him “What has brought you here?” “I have come to help you.” “Help me” Do you realize who I am?” “I know exactly who you are and this is why I have come to offer my assistance for a price.” “A price” I’m listening.” “You and I are alike but your blood is different. You are a vampire who is not a child of Caine. I want to know how that is possible. I want to know how many others there are like you out there.” “You want knowledge. I want to leave this pit. It is time for me to get revenge. I can teach you to become a true vampire.” A figure walked out of the shadows and looked down on Soma and knelt down beside him, pulling a large vial out of a long trench coat?”.. ?” To be home again. Starting anew from a situation that didn’t kill me but only made me stronger. The new mission; To find new students and take them down the path of oblivion. In the ashes of Manifest Destiny, a darken phoenix will rise bringing pain and suffering to all who oppose. This was my vision. This was my destiny. I sat on the plane thinking how much has changed over the years. I look at my new contract and a list of the new roster. Many of these people will not know me. Many of these men and women will not fear me. They will speak of how they have done so many bad things. They will speak on how they are the franchise. They will even speak on how there is nobody out there to defeat them. There are those who will see me as the betrayer, having betrayed men like Maverick Reno and my cohorts of The Upper Echelon , Sage Sabbot, a Harvester and hall of famer. These thoughts make me smile because it matters not what the roster of GW thinks of me they all will know me. They will fear me. I look out the window of the plane overlooking the Dallas sky. I am hungry and I need to feed. “Are you comfortable sir?” The flight attendant smiled. I look at her and smile and nod. She is the first woman I have seen since my time in the pit. She is attractive for a mortal. Her hair long was long and black. Her eyes a light green that shown bright over her brown skin. Her body shapely, and had been well endowed in the blue flight attendant uniform. I smile at the though of making love to her. Or maybe it was the thought of wanting to feed. Either way my smile made her blush. “Your Leinad Soma right, the guy that owns Soma Corp and the wrestler right?” The flight attendant asked and once again I nodded. “Can I have your autograph?” “Sure. Can I have a date?” She blushes at the comment and smiles. Humanity is too trusting. “What is your name if I may ask?” “My name is Courtney. Courtney Jackson.” “Well Ms. Courtney Jackson at the end of this flight I shall take you out to dinner and then I’ll give you your autograph. How does that sound?” “Your in luck Mr. Soma, this is my last stop and I haven’t had dinner yet.” I smile once again. “Good. And please, call me Leiand.” The flight lasted for another fifteen or so minutes and I watched the flight attendant as all her movements now have become more flirtatious and sexual. For over millennia I have seduced women and I have realized mortal women have no challenges toward them. They like to be swept off their feet. I have money, looks, and a hunger that, to them, looks like flirting. So simple the ways of courting. Everyone got off the plane and I waited on Ms. Jackson, she soon got of the plane and walked with me to my limo. As the gentleman I open her door, and like a lamb to the slaughter, she innocently stepped in. She didn’t care that I have been called a monster. She didn’t care people said I was a vampyre. For all she knew it was just a gimmick I played on television. And that would be her fall. We went to the restaurant, a fine Italian place I had been to a couple of times. We sat under the stars on their outside deck. “The food here is to die for.” She smiled as she begin to eat. I just stared at her. Wanting to get this part out of the way as fast as possible. The small talk was as usual as was the flirting until she made a certain comment. “Leinad, do you know what drew me to you?” “The fact that I’m a celebrity?” “No, that wasn’t it. It is the fact they say you are a vampyre.” “Is that what they say” “They say your are the first. And that would make you the most powerful. Ever since I was a little girl I always wanted to be one of your kind. I guess it’s a fetish of mine.” “If that is the case let us leave here and you can get your autograph back at my place.” This woman began to intrigue me. It is rare that my kind runs into those who want to be us. Most of the humans like that are disturbed or would make a lousy vamp. I took her by the hand and we got back into the limo. As soon as we were in she became like an animal, straddling me and kissing me all over. She ripped open my shirt and it was apparent she didn’t want to wait until we got to the mansion. I had the driver pull into a dark ally as I begin to pull off her top revealing her sweet breast. I grasped her ass as she began to grind on top of me. I sat back starting to enjoy all she had started to do. As she kept grinding she pulled a knife from her boot and as a shock to me cut open her own hand. The sweet smell of blood catching my nostrils as it had been a while since I was able to hunt on my own. She took the hand with the newly opened wound and smeared it across my mouth not realizing what she was actually doing. “Feed on me. Turn me into one of your kind that I may live forever.” I had to give her credit. She thought this was a simple procedure. She thought it was a bite and then poof she was going to be a vampire, but no that is not how it would work. She wanted to understand what it was like to become a monster. She wanted to know what it was like to see into oblivion. She wanted to know what it was to be immortal. I began feeding from the wound she opened. She began to moan at the euphoric feeling. Most do not understand that the bite of a vampyre can be one of the most tantalizing feelings ever. As I continued my feeding she began to moan even louder while she hiked up her skirt and began to open my pants. She began to grind harder, her moaning turned into screaming as now I was inside her and also draining her life away. I pull away from her wound, blood covering my face, and a sadistic smile on my face. “Are you sure this is what you want?” “Yes.” “Are you sure you can handle living like this?” “Yes.” “Then so be it. I shall show you oblivion.” I begin to feed again feeling the warm liquid as it ran down my throat. As she continues to kiss me all over, she realizes her life starting to slip away. She tries to pull away but I grab hold of her so she can’t fight back. Her screams become screams of terror and fear. As I continue to feed I look up at her face, no longer a face of ecstasy but of pain and suffering. Her last facial expression was looking at me and realizing she would not have immortality but a violent death at the hands of the creature of oblivion. I smile at her before she finally dies. As soon as the screams were over, the window to the front of the limo rolled down. “Shall I dispose of the body” “No. We still have things to do this night. And we must handle them with extreme care. We must kill one of our own tonight this was a detour to strengthen myself, for who we go up against is strong.” “Who is it that we shall face?” “My wife. The bitch that left me to rot in that pit after my loss. It will be through her blood that I will become transformed.” “How do you know this?” “She carries my child, and through ancient prophecy I know what must be done.” I threw the body from the limo and started on the mission that must be accomplished that night. We drove to the Soma Estate in Dallas. There she would be waiting. Cassandra. My wife, the vampire that started me on my path to oblivion. She saw me as weak when I loss the match at whiplash and left me to die, never to see my child. But she would know that I would come for her if I had not perished. As we drove up to the doors of the mansion I looked at it with disgust. So many things had happened in the past year. Sage Sabbot had come and gone also taking my assistant Cherish with him. My so called death. My son having control of Soma Corp. All actions that would make me seem weaker than what I was. It was enough to make this night of macabre all worth it. I opened the doors and walked in. She changed everything. The look of the mansion was entirely different. Everything that said this was my house was gone. My pictures taken from the walls, my weaponry gone, even all of the things I had kept for thousands of years were now nowhere to be seen. I would find her. I would find Cassandra and like the flight attendant, I would drain her dry. I walked into the bedroom and there she was dressing as if preparing for a night on the town but also ripe with child. “You are positively glowing Cassandra. Pregnancy has just made you that much more beautiful.” “Leinad” You’re ..” “Alive. Yes my dear I’m quite alive.” “I see you have made changes to the mansion. All my things have pretty much disappeared. Did you even check to see if I was alive after my match?” “Does it really matter” Would you have checked on me if our roles were reversed?” “That is not what I asked you Cassandra. You know why I have come?” “Of course. It had to be this way.” “No things didn’t have to be this way Cassandra, you made them this way. You could have survived this. Now you will die, as will our child. “Leinad you were weak. You let that mortal conquer you and now so must I.” Her speed was unlike that of any other vampire I had faced and before I knew it I was up against a wall. She had rushed me and thrown me into one of the bedpost. I smiled at the attack and stood up brushing myself off. She tried to rush again but this time I was ready and grabbed her baring my fangs before head butting her and throwing her across the room. She stood and dived at the bed and pulling a stake from under her pillow. I broke the closest bedpost and awaited her attack. We stood there like the most viscous of snakes hissing and baring our fangs at one another till finally we both went for the attack and ending up grabbing one another and trying to press our stakes into each other. I got the upper hand and straddled her , pinning her arms where she couldn’t move them I drew my stake to finish her but before I could, she kicked me in the back moving me just enough to get her stake arm free. She swung at me but I had already moved. I could tell she had started to become tired for she had not yet fed tonight. I grinned at her misfortune as I lunged at her, her stake nearly catching my right leg as my stake caught her in the heart. She screamed and stopped moving. I slowly climbed on top of her, holding her face down to one side looking at her neck . “And with this I will claim the prophecy.” I bit into her neck and began to drain her dry. The blood of two immortals running into my veins but that wasn’t enough. I removed the stake and plunged my fist into her chest and pulling out her heart and feasted on it. “What are you doing! You are breaking the third tradition.! You will certainly die!” The creature of the shadow pulled me off the body but it was too late. I had consumed the heart and lunged back onto the body to finish draining the blood from the body. When I was done I stood laughing maniacally. Then something happened. I began to shake violently and fell to the floor. For a half an hour this happened then it just stopped. I stood and looked around. “Are you ok Soma?” “I’m better than ok.” “You broke the third tradition and lived. Your eyes., what has happened to them?” “It is because I’m not the childe of Caine. What do my eyes look like?” “They are black. You have no pupils your eyes are fully black.” “Interesting. I have become the keeper of oblivion.”
