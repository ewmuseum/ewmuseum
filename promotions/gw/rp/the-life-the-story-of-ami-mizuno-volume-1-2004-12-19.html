The Life: The story of Ami Mizuno (Volume 1)

19 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-19-04 Early Afternoon Well hello again friends. This is Joshua Curtis and I know that you haven’t forgotten about me or my stories for you but even if you have I got a new one for you now. This is called “The Life”, which is the story of the YOUNGEST member of a group that is called “The Connection” and the truth is she is one of the more impactful members of the group! Ami is also the younger sister of the “leader” of the group named Joshua Curtis and she is a very good friend of Zack Johnson, whom as we all know is Joshua’s boyfriend. It the early afternoon in New York City and its kind of a dreary day out as can be told by looking at the citizens and noting the umbrellas and slickers that the people are holding and wearing. When you look around in the city you notice that some people are wearing Eli Manning jerseys and some Pedro Martinez jerseys and a lot of people are wearing shirts that are of course from their hometown heroes in the wrestling business called the “Old School Connection”. Today was a day that a lot of people had been murmuring and talking to their friends about as there were rumors that Ami would be coming out from the shadows of her brother and stepping into the ring of Global Wrestling. The thing of it was is that there were only a select few that knew the truth of the matter and while one of those people wasn’t thrilled with the idea the whole group knew that they could protect her forever. “So Ami” Juri started as her and the blue-haired genius were walking through Broadway during the misting rain “I’ve heard from Joshua that you and he were politicking the owner of Global Wrestling, a Mr. Bubba McCoy to allow you to wrestle in the company. Is it true?” Juri asked and Ami, clad in a light blue sweater, Levi’s blue jeans and a pair of Nike’s blushed slightly before answering her sempai. “Well” Ami started “its true that I have talked to my brother about wrestling ever since I left the one fed I was in and was the Women’s champion there and its true that when I wrestled it was one of the few times that I was actually happy! When I lived with my mother, God bless her, I was constantly worried about my grades and never had a chance to explore the different facets of life. It didn’t matter if it was a relationship or just hanging out with friends but I just wanted to not be confined to the life that my mother led. I wanted to be able to be expressive and when I started back in England wrestling for my brother’s company I felt like I could work hard and be free to do as I needed to do.” Ami explained to Juri whom just smiled and held her hand as they walked all through the district until they got to the bus stop and a couple of the people there recognized Ami right away. “Miss Mizuno!” a young man cried out and when Ami turned around she saw a young man with brown hair and blue eyes and wearing a t-shirt that actually read “Ami Mizuno” on the front and on the back it read “The best wrestler you never saw!” and that was partially true because of the fact that when she wrestled in England the shows were never really televised in the United States so to see the matches you had to either be at the shows or somehow get the matches on VHS back then but when you saw them my God were they something special to see. “How may I help you young man?” Ami asked the young adolescence and the boy couldn’t help but blush at the comment “Well Ms. Mizuno?” the young man started before Ami cut him off “Please you may just call me Ami.” The blue-haired prodigy told the youngster as he continued “well Ami I was wondering if you could sign something for me. I’ve been a fan of yours since the IWF and it would be an honor to have your autograph.” The boy asked and Ami was more than willing to comply as she signed the young man’s shirt and even gave him a book that had her autograph on it! The young man smiled after receiving the items and said his thanks before walking away from the duo. This was all Ami ever wanted when it came to her life. All she wanted to do was make people happy and see the smiles on the faces of young men and even young ladies as they look to Ami as one of the classiest lady wrestlers in the history of the business and that is saying something considering the state of wrestling today with a lot of the women with the fake boobs and the sparse wardrobes but not Ami oh no! Ami is one of a rare breed whom likes to wear the conservative outfits and doesn’t usually swear in her interviews, so I guess you could kind of say that she is one of those wrestlers that when it comes to bucking trends she does it better than anyone huh? When the bus came Ami paid the fare for the two and boarded at which point they took their rightful seats about 2 seats behind the driver and boy did the people recognize them right away! “Hey aren’t you Ami Mizuno, the former World Champion of NoW?” one person asked that was sitting acrossed from them and Ami nodded before he continued “I watched you wrestle in that fed as well as in Japan and you are definitely one of the better wrestlers that is out there today and anyone that says there are better are kidding themselves!” the man told Ami and she blushed at the compliment. Why? Well ok Ami is a good wrestler and there are no two ways about that but to her she is fighting to get out of her brother’s shadow and while no one is making a comparison between her and her bother she has felt like she has had to push herself in her training much like when she had to study for school and her friends in Japan, whom you’ll meet at the Xmas party that the Connection is having in a couple of days think that she pushes herself TOO hard! Right like for Ami there IS such a thing! Meanwhile back on the bus Ami and Juri were listening to their RCA Lyra MP3 players when the bus had to stop suddenly and during the stop there was a decent number of people swarming around Juri and Ami, which she didn’t really mind because a lot of them asked about her past wrestling experience and what it was like to wrestle in England and Japan. She told the bulk of them that one of her plans was to compete at the next SiT with her brother and if she had the chance to wrestle against him in the free for all then that would be the ultimate honor to her. Want to know why? Because a lot of people believe that Ami is secondary to her brother and the fact is that they are equal with Ami having a little bit of an advantage as far as titles go. (Read she has held a World Title and Joshua hasn’t) The thing that is somewhat disparaging to Ami is the fact to a good portion of the wrestling public she will just be “Joshua’s sister” but to her and the rest of Connection family she is one of the young up and comers that if people don’t watch it will steal the spotlight and literally make a tsunami in the federation known as GW because she my be small (5 feet 8 inches) and she might be one of the lighter members of the group (175 pounds) but the fact remains that Mizuno Ami is not someone any member of the GW roster should take lightly and it doesn’t matter who you are she will make anyone that isn’t paying attention feel something cold! Anyone up for a “Deep Freeze?” See ya later folks! JLC
