The Journey: Part 23/From Joshua’s POV

12 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-12-05 Shattered Dreams/Fractured Memories I “The easier you think you have it the harder it is to live your life” That is a quote that a friend once told Joshua Curtis and its one that he will never and can never forget. It’s the early morning in the city of New York and although things appear to be normal in the “Big Apple” there is a tension that hasn’t been felt since the attack on 9/11 and its one of those types of tensions that makes people somewhat leery of leaving the house! What caused it you ask? That’s a good question and not one answered simply either. The reality is that two of New York’s beloved superstars are about to enter their most pressing of matches at one of the biggest shows of the year for the federation known as Global Wrestling. One of those wrestlers lives in Manhattan’s SoHo District and the other lives on what is called “The Outskirts of Times Square”. Why do they call it the “Outskirts?” It SOUNDS like something you call the run down section of a town or city but its far from that in this case! No, the “Outskirts” that are mentioned here are simply what the people that live at the location call it. They live there and they work there and most importantly, as can be argued by anyone, they are a family there! They are Joshua Curtis and the “Army of Old School” and they have lived there for a little while now on the “Outskirts” where a lot of the populace of New York City have come to pay them a visit over time and they have been well received for them living THEIR LIVES and no one else’s ,which they will tell you is rarity these days. It doesn’t come easily in these days of prejudice where people are very quick to judge you about your choice of lifestyle and your choice of friends ,but the people that live around the area in which the “Army” live don’t care about all of that because they have said that since they came around things were a lot more peaceful without people trying to poke fun at them and the fact remained that they were some of the least troublesome people in the city. So what brings us into the city today? Well Joshua Curtis, The leader of the “Army”, is scheduled for a match this week at the big WRESTLECADE PPV and its not for a title or for a title shot but rather for his pride and another man’s ego! A man by the name of Josh ,whom has been with the company known as “Global Wrestling” for 2 years now, had decided that for some reason he was “fed up” with the faction known as the “Army of Old School” and he wants after Curtis in the worst way. Has he resorted to attacking Curtis physically yet? No but to his credit he is waiting until he gets him in the ring. No he has resorted to the most underhanded of tactics to try and get at Joshua and make him so enraged that he’d make a mistake against the veteran but Curtis knows better than to fall for the mind games that a vet like the American Youth would pull on him ,but then again Curtis didn’t just fall of the turnip truck yesterday either! The fact is that Curtis has been around the block in other places and in almost every place that he has been in he has held a title or managed someone to a title of some kind. Be it the Television title of the ENWA/EHCW or the Women’s titles of the JWPW or NoW ,Joshua has been at the height of it all for his career and while the one title still eludes him that the man known as Josh might have held for the longest time he has not shied away from his goal of becoming the World Champion someday. In reality while he might have never been the World Champion when you look at how many years he’s been around how many years he’s been around he is about to the point where a certain WWE wrestler named Steve Williams a.k.a. “Stone Cold” Steve Austin finally won his first World Title years ago! For Curtis this match means nothing more than showing what he can do in the ring against the likes of the “Top Guns” of the fed and while he might not have beaten many of them he HAS beaten some of the best in his time in GW which has yet to reach one year yet! Nazo/ John Winters - Been there and done that and they were the first opponents for Curtis in a tag match for his Debut in October. His partner? The irreplaceable and unmistakable Ken Partridge Jr. How’d they do? About as good as you can do when you first come into a fed: He won! Ok that might not really sound all that impressive but consider that Joshua had just started in the fed and his first tag team partner was a major star in Partridge and THAT might be as big an accomplishment as one can find’until you get to later on in the period. El Serpenti - This was a match that Curtis took great honor in because he had heard that Serpenti was a legend and he did everything that he could to give the legend a good match and fact was that not only did he give him a good match but he also BEAT the former CW champion! When he got to Myers that was when he suffered his first taste of defeat but by the time that he got to the match he had amassed a pretty impressive 3-0 record and that in and of itself is a big deal considering the talent pool in the fed at the time. As Curtis was getting momentum it started to show in the fed where people took notice to the “New Kid on the Block” and they wanted him around. One particular faction had actually went so far as to recruit him for their team when the big SiT PPV came about and while a little apprehensive about the idea of being a really new person in the fed he took up the offer of the team and why wouldn’t he? The team was captained by his former tag team partner and best friend off camera in Ken Partridge Jr.! Curtis? streak of going through the legends continued when he PINNED a member of what WAS The Upper Echelon in Maverick Reno ad Reno hasn’t been seen in GW since! When Curtis finished in the top two of the final match at the PPV some thought that Curtis would claim gold a lot sooner that later and truthfully so did Curtis but alas it hasn’t happened but that hasn’t dampened the spirits of Curtis’no it just made him hungrier! When Curtis made a decision to leave GW, albeit for like an hour, he lost his tag team partner and boyfriend Zack Johnson and was replaced with his younger sister in Ami Mizuno. Now Joshua will admit that he had asked for the change but it was mainly to stop the gay bashings about himself and Zack and they HAD stopped for a while. When Curtis and Ami won the chance to the tag team championships recently and with Curtis? success in the Platinum Series one would have thought that they would have been continuing on in one of those runs but this is where the ego of the aforementioned “Legend” comes in. Josh was a friend of Curtis? when the SiT match was going to go down and they were to work together at the match. Everything worked out fine for the two and Curtis actually did his part and made the top two only to lose to Christian Zenith but that wasn’t so bad. Now its a different story? Joshua has since that night made friends with Ken Partridge Jr. and his girlfriend Gunner Addison. Typically this is no big deal BUT for some odd reason the American Youth believes that Curtis wants break up the couple and the fact is that he(Curtis) wants to do no such thing, in fact if you talk to Curtis he would tell you that the American Youth has gone off the deep end with his personal attacks on the young man formerly from London ,England and now from New York City and any further actions would cause him to retaliate against the egotist. So where do you think Joshua is today? The answer is that he is in the SoHo district yet again but this time NOT to go for a visit to Ken’no its to go to the Starbucks that is in the area and Joshua isn’t alone today? Remember Ian? He’s back along with Trowa , Quatre and Zack. Why are they there? Well its not like this is a gang war where if you violate someone’s territory you get shot at’no Joshua and his friends have as much right to be in the area as anyone else. The thing that makes this particular trip so agonizing for him is that Curtis knows damn well where his friend’s apartment is and he has a letter to get to him BUT he doesn’t want to walk his ass up the steps again?.not today, not tomorrow, not EVER AGAIN! “Master Joshua” Quatre started “What’s bugging you?” The young blonde asked and Joshua did his best to put on a happy face for his friend as he hates depressing the young Arabian. “Nothing really Quat. I’m just thinking about this match that is going to be coming up soon with one American Youth. I think that he has the wrong idea about Zack and myself and I’m concerned that the American Youth has done a lot of damage to my name in the process.” Joshua explained and a reassuring hand fell upon his shoulder and wouldn’t you know it happened to be Zack’s! “Its ok darling” Zack began “I know what its been like for you without me around and I know that that miserable son of a ***** has tried to make your world hell. I know that you don’t want to face the American Youth in this match and you’d rather work with him and his friends to get the goal that you both want to attain. I know I was the cause of your problems with the American Youth and if you’d like I’d would like to help you fix the problem either this week or next week.” Zack said and Joshua just smiled at his boyfriend before speaking. “I appreciate that Zack, you know I do and I’m glad that I got your support and believe me it WILL be needed as this feud or whatever it is that the American Youth wants to call it ,but honestly I don’t want to fight him. I just want to get through this match unscathed and then move on!” Joshua explained to Zack but someone at the table wasn’t buying that for a second! “Hold on a second Mr. Curtis” Trowa started “You mean to tell me that this man ,whom at one time was your friend, is going to be allowed by you to make the comments and do the things that he has done without any response from you”! Why? Do you really feel that if you do nothing that he’ll just go away and stop bugging you?” The brown haired young man from Mexico asked Joshua and Josh knew that Trowa meant business when he took that tone. “I concede to your point Mr. Barton but at the same time do you have any ideas on what I could do to make him think about what he’s doing?” Joshua asked but when he asked he wasn’t just asking him but everyone at the table and everyone was having a hard time coming up with an idea for a good spell until finally Ian broke the silence and decided it was time for him to make himself heard. “Joshua” Ian began “What if you went somewhat into the American Youth’s realm and showed everyone that part of you that doesn’t get shown much.” Ian explained to Joshua and Josh promptly gave him the “Huh?” expression before Ian continued “The American Youth has this idea about gays ,which is the wrong one I don’t mind telling you, that all gay men or women are sinners and that they are all flamers. What if you went on one of those religious channels after WRESTLECADE and used that to help tell your side of the story. You could read that letter on the air that you have right now on the air and hope to God(No pun intended) that Ken hears it.” Ian said and Joshua actually liked the idea’he liked it so much that he made a note to go home and write the address down to station when he found it and after he made that note the group decided that it would be best to leave for the day ,but not before Joshua had one more thing to say to everyone? “This week has been an emotional mess for me guys. This week I have had to deal with the fact that a person that I once called “friend” has come out and verbally attacked me and tried to really ruin my name has really hurt me. I don’t intend on winning this match but I do hope that I can either end this war or ,if he is so insistent on going to war with me then we will do so in the most unusual ways because once you fire a shot at me its ALL OVER!” Joshua said as he and his family got up from the table and paid for what they ate at which point the only thing left to say is this? Au Revoir darlings
