Yes or No?

21 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/21/2005 It was a warm day. Just like you would find on a morning Saturday. The clouds all rolled back to show the bright lemon like sun shining through. The wind wasn’t blowing very hard. Maybe at a little two miles pre hour. But that was fast enough for me. My hair rolled back in the running breeze. My breath picked up the pace from the hard pace that I was keeping. For some reason or another I woke up today ready for a run. Ready for my heart to pound out of my body before getting ready to go back to Dallas for another Global Wrestling Show. The area here in Chicago was crappy. It was so hard to even find a good cup of coffee in this place. God forbid my uncle ever come out here. I rolled around the rush hour morning traffic. Passing by the massive amount of people that kept walking in front of me. All them in their nice and fresh smelling gray suits and long blue dresses. It looked like they were into some political race or one another. What ever happen to the tight blue jeans and cowboy button up shirts? Did those days somehow fall off the sky? As the sweat raced down my face, a buzzing came from my pocket. It had to be the phone. I paused my run for a few seconds, grabbing on to a pole that was near by. “Hello?” Asking into the cell phone. “Hey?”, The other side said. “How was the run?” It was Karla. She sounded upright and excited about something. Though she always seemed that way. “Well was on it?” Trying to catch my breathe still. The sun was still uncovered from my head and I was starting to not be able to take it. Looking over to see a lemonade stand in the shade, I began a slow walk towards heaven as I knew it. “So you coming by to tell me everything?” She responded, screaming out like a little teenage fourteen year old. “Yes but dang girl. Let me get done here please.” Laughing as the words rolled off my tongue. Hanging up the phone and putting it back into the pocket it was in, I walked across the street, right through the empty now traffic that wasn’t there. The little shop was in the middle of the corner. Surrounded by a restaurant and a coffee house. I wasn’t into coffee. It wasn’t my kind of thing after I spilt it on me once and it burnt me badly. Grabbing the front door and opening it, a sudden rush of cold air ran through my body. Making the little hairs on my arms raise up like they were singing in a church. The place looked empty. Not many people there for a morning like this. They had a few pictures that interested me in the corner. A nice one that embraced the sea with a very beautiful wooden boat. “Hi can I help you?” A young and spunky looking male cashier asked me. “Yes?”, looking at the menu for a second. “Give me a large lemonade please.” “Is that is?” Replying with a smile. I gave a slight nod to him, pulling out a little bit of money that I had stuffed in my pocket. “Two-fifty right?” Asking as that was all I had. “It’s Three-fifty miss.” His sudden reply making me jump. I didn’t have it and my mouth was watering deeply. I felt embarrassed for the first time now. I was so confident being away from Dallas and now it was like I was screwing up all over again. As I searched through all my pockets, I heard a voice. A familiar voice that I knew from somewhere. “Here you go sir.” Turning around, he was a hunk. Tall and slim and the same aftershave that I smelt form him when I was taking shot after shot. He was smoother looking out of the dark club bar life. He was clean shaved and stood with a confident ego. “You didn’t have to do that you know.” Dropping my head for a moment. “Yeah but what can I do for a women who falls after taking five shots of tequila in under five minutes and falls and hits her head.” Laughing with the question. I raised my head before reaching back and taking the large drink. It felt good to have SOMETHING going down my throat. It felt cold and pleasant. Like fresh water from a rain forest. We both walked over to the table next to the window. Sitting down across from each other, before looking deep at one another’s eyes. “You know what’s funny?” Smiling as I sat the drink down. “I don’t even know your name.” Giggling with a bit of a jiggle, “It’s Mark.” I liked his giggle. It felt a little safe and warm to me. Making my heart almost skip a beat. I took a sip of the lemonade, making a small face as it slipped down my throat. “What are you doing here?” Asking with curiosity. “Oh out for a small walk. I come here once a week. Have been for the last two years.” His eyes danced in the sun’s glare as he responded. “You don’t seem like the walking type to me. More like the wake up late and drink lots of coffee type.” Grinning with him as the sun shot almost right on queue in my eyes. I hadn’t felt this good in a while. Karla would kill me if she knew I was talking to a guy like this when I didn’t even know that much about him, let alone his name after drinking myself on the floor. “Well I got a nice side to me. You should see more of it.” leaning forward, almost getting right in my path of the sun. See more of it? Was he serious? God yes I would but I just knew I couldn’t. If I did I would be chancing my dream to go down the drain. Almost making it die with a simple lust for love. That I couldn’t do. I promised myself that I wouldn’t screw this up. I took another drink and pushed myself up off the chair. Looking at him with a smile. “Where are you going?” He asked with a confused look on his face. “I got to go. If I don’t I might do something very bad.” Trying to walk away with my eyes in my head. “Like say yes to me asking you out?” WHAT? I shook my head inside at what was said. Me saying yes? Me saying no? Choices. God how I hated them. I turned back around, looking right into a face that looked so happy right now. How could I say no? How could I really tell this man that a dream was more important then him? I giggled a bit before replying. “Mark?”, trying to make it less obvious. “I’ll think about it.” Giving him one last smile before turning around, going passed a few people that were coming in. Ok so I lied. So what. I was going to think about it but man, how could I say no? It kept racing through my head with what could go wrong but the only problem is, what will go right?
