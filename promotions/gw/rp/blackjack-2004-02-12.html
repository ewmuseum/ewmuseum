BlackJack

12 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 12th,2004 Four cards are lain on a felt table, two down and one face up on each side. Hands can be seen, just hovering at the edge of the table, a deck of cards placed carefully there; the back of which is in elegant black rose design. We can see a 10 of clubs for the dealer and an 8 of Hearts for the player. Another hand creeps into the shot, lifting the lower card underneath the players side, allowing the player to see but no one else. A voice grumbles out the word, 'shit;' giving us an idea of what was just seen under the card. 'Hit me again.' The crack of a card is heard as the dealer, quickly and efficiently, slaps a third card on top of the eight. A 10 of Diamonds. 'Fuck. I'm bust with 22.' The cards are pushed to the side and the deal begins, one to the player, face down. One to the dealer, face down. One to the player, face up: a Queen of Hearts. One to the dealer, face up: Jack of Diamonds. Both men check their hands and you hear the player say confidently, 'Hit me.' The card is slapped down; A Jack of Spades. 'So why now?' The words are calmly spewed forth from the dealer. The player tosses his cards to the side, 'bust again?.' 'Seems that way. I hope this isn't a sign of things to come.' A snicker is heard from the dealers side of the table. The scene slowly fades away. February 21st, 2003 - Approximately 8:40 pm. Dallas, Texas - The Global Arena The scene opens up to the locker room area of the Global Arena. Random people, janitors and service crew for Global Wrestling make their way up and down the hallway outside the doors. A man in casual dress rushes down the hallway, an envelope in hand. He bursts through one of the locker room doors and the camera spins, allowing a full view of the room from behind the flustered man. The room is elegant but simply: tiled floors and panel walls, a mirror, and a sink. Everything you'd expect in a locker room. This room however, also held a black leather sofa and a plasma TV mounted to the wall before it with a direct feed from the building. Maverick Reno sits on the couch, tying on his right combat style boot. He glances up, his eyes curious as to the sudden interruption. The flustered man is panting as he walks slowly to the couch. Reno leans back and glances at him, looking him up and down. 'What is it, Jim?' There's no hostility towards the young man, even with the abruptness in his entrance. 'Mr. Salaok wants to see you, Mr. Reno.' Reno smirks and leans back against the sofa. 'I figured he would. Any idea what it's about?' The flustered man begins to catch his breath as he hands the envelope to Reno. The envelope reads in a crimson red handwriting, 'Reno.' 'How formal.' The tone in Reno's voice is less than amused. He opens the envelope and pulls a single index card from inside. Reno nods a few times and rips the index card in two before dropping it on the floor. 'Where is the fucking bastard?' The tone in Reno's voice catches the assistant off guard, causing him to jump. He's apparently been chewed out (or had the shit scared out of him?) by Salaok several times this evening. Jim gulps one good time before speaking the obvious words, 'in his office.' Reno nods and stands before grabbing his trench coat and heading out of the locker room. Voice: The descent. Reno walks through the halls of the Global Arena, his eyes tracing the doors and quickly skimming the name on each office. Finally he stops; 'President Salaok.' He certainly works fast... he's only been in charge a week. Within a moment, Reno has pushed the door open and begins through the secretary's office, heading straight for Salaok's door. A small woman stands and steps in front of Maverick Reno. 'Mr. Salaok is busy now, sir. Do you wish to acquire an appointment?' Reno just sort of looks at the secretary and steps around her and kicks the door open, sending it off of it's hinges. Salaok's office is neat and it's not exactly empty; accompanying Salaok in his office are fellow Harvesters and friends, if you will, Quartz and Kullypso. Reno points to the door. 'Out. This is between Salaok and I.' Salaok nods to the two men as they, slowly and begrudgingly make their exit from the office. It would be a fools errand to attempt anything with these two warriors right outside the door. 'Mr. Reno... so glad you could come on such short notice.' 'What is this about, Sal. I'm not much in a mood for a scam.' Salaok smiles in his own demented way and reclines back, his eyes scanning Reno much in the way a Tiger examines it's prey before the pounce and kill. 'Well, as the note said, Mr. Reno. I found a discrepancy in your contract.' 'What kind of discrepancy? My record is spotless.' 'Oh I know, I know! It took quite a bit of looking and a few loopholes to find something on you, Mav. After all, you were one of Bubba's favorites. You show up for all your matches... you take the federation by storm at the November pay per view. You win the World Title in just a short amount of time. Hell, you even put yourself as a thorn in my side. What about you is there not for Bubba to like, right?' Reno folds his arms behind his back, his eyes locked on his nemesis. 'Too bad however. He liked you so much that he just renewed your Enigma contract to incorporate a longer stay. You see, the problem with that is... you're not Enigma, now are you? You are the legendary Maverick Reno.' The spite in Salaok's voice is obvious. 'And Maverick Reno does not have a contract with Global Wrestling.' The words hang in the air, the ear canals of both Salaok and Maverick Reno; festering and eating away at the serenity that is so well known in Reno. The words were like a mold, settling in and decaying all sanity remaining. Killing off every bit of tact and morality in his mind. Anger had finally found a home inside the heart of Maverick Reno. 'You fucking son of a bitch.' The words were angry and full or vengeance. That's what now glimmered from the eyes of Reno, his serenity and valor long since vanished. His rage overfilling the once peaceful mind of the man, the myth... 'Oh no no no, Mr. Reno. You're the fucking son of a bitch. You thought that you could go out there, say whatever you wanted. Do whatever you wanted. You thought that the McCoy's would protect you. You were wrong, Mr. Reno. You were dead wrong.' The room fell silent although the tension had a loud buzz of it's own. Reno's head was full of thoughts and voices, images and memories, feelings and pain. Hatred highlighted everyone of them. The noise inside of Maverick's head, nearly unbearable, came to a screeching halt as it was replaced by the melodic tones of Salaok's baritone voice. 'You have tonight's show to say your good bye, Reno. After that, I don't want to see you in my company ever again. Now - leave. I'm done with you.' Serenity is slow and thoughtful, full of steps. Anger is rash and violent, full of moments where a thought is the last thing on your mind Outside of Salaok's office, Quartz and Kullypso are having a menial conversation. Suddenly the opaque glass where Salaok's name is printed shatters, a golden desk lamp hits the ground, clattering about the floor. 'What the fuck was that?' The ogre turns and bursts through the door only to find everything that was formally on the desk of Salaok, swiped to the floor. Glass scatters slightly from the broken picture frame lying on the floor. Salaok is standing next to his toppled chair, an elaborate ivory and platinum ornated single shot revolver aimed directly at the head of Maverick Reno. The eyes of Reno have changed, a new sense brewing about him. His aura has even changed. Quartz and Kullypso stop, looking on at the two men. 'No reporters, no staff. Have this cleaned up... no one is to know of this. Understand me?' The odd request came from Salaok himself towards his minions and secretary, who now stands behind the twin gargantuans. Reno's eyes focused past the revolver and straight to the throat of Salaok. An evil grew in his heart that day; a lust for vengeance. Does it remain? 'Now get out, Maverick. Before I have you thrown into the Harvesters rec room as a pre-show sacrifice.' The joke (or was it?) fell on deaf ears. Reno's mind had once again begun to fill with the noise that left him perplexed and numb to the words that floated around him. Salaok presses the barrel of the revolver to Reno's forehead and shoves him back. 'Out.' Before Reno can comply, Kullyspo and Quartz each grab an arm and suspend him, preventing him from getting away as they drag him out of the office, through the secretaries room, and then into the hallway. Quartz shoves Reno against the wall opposite Salaok's office and grabs him by the throat, gritting his teeth and glaring at him. 'Now you listen to what Sal had to say or you'll be just another chalk print somewhere. Understand!?' Reno's silence spoke for him. Quartz released the grip and Kullypso laughed slightly, knowing that his words were more than just a threat. The two returned to the office across the hall, the door closed and locked. Reno stood there, his eyes dead set on the door before him. Tyler Chambers comes rushing down the hallway, his eyes focused on Reno. 'Mr. Reno! Can I please get an interview!?' A camera man was right behind him. 'We just got word that you've been released from Global Wrestling, is this true?' Reno, without a second thought, sends a hard right into the camera, shattering the lens and shoving (with great velocity, might I add) the camera into the face of the camera man, knocking him to the ground and completely out. Chambers jumped back and dropped to his knees next to the camera man, checking on him. Before he could look up again, Reno was gone. February 21st, 2003 - Approximately 9:15 pm. Dallas, Texas - The Global Arena The office of Bubba McCoy had been vacant until today. Salaok's reign was still supreme but a member of the McCoy family had begun to set up shop in her father's office. Christina McCoy stood inside with brother, Donovan. They were packing a few things and unloading a few others, getting the room set up for Christina's stint as an official in the business. Although currently, Salaok had relinquished to her only paper pushing duties - nothing of authority. The door opened and Reno walked in, Stephen Perdue standing outside the door. Still the acting commissioner, there was only so much he could do. 'McCoy. I need you to review my contract and make sure that it's in fu..' Before Reno could continue, Christina answered his request with a simple response. 'Salaok.' Reno knew what she meant before she continued. 'He took away my father and in his absence, diminished his position to a mere paper pusher. Yeah, I work here. Yes, I'm co-president. But all I handle are fines.' Reno stood silent again, his mind filling with noise unparalelled. 'I'm sorry. There's nothing we can do... He is in full control.' The scene fades to black. A moment passes and a voice can be heard. 'That was the day it all came to an end. The victories, the titles, the awards. That was the day I lost. I don't lose. I am forever victorious... and I was going to see that Salaok paid for his crimes against Global Wrestling. Even if it meant that I had to sink to his level and then submerge again. Salaok paid... he paid dearly even after my influence had run it's course and now, Global Wrestling is once again in the hands of the McCoy family. There are no more obsticals. No more hurdles. Infact, the only thing remaining is to aim... and aim high.' The scene returns to the Blackjack game. The Dealer has a 10 of Diamonds showing. The player looks at his card and orders a hit. An Ace of Spades comes down hard. 'When playing to win and playing against the house, you have to bet big on that one hand and make sure that you're going to win. That's when you take the house. And if that fails... then you become the house. I am now the undefeatable.' Black Jack. The player flips over the hidden card to reveal a 10 of Spades. 21. He places his hand on the table to reveal a tatoo - The Ankh of Reno intertwined with a black rose. I'm Coming For You
