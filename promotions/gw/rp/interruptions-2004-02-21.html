Interruptions

21 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/21/04 'Five more............ FIVE............. FOUR.............. THREE........... TWO................ LAST ONE!' Ice places the bar back on the rack and sits up from his lying down positions on the bench. His is a bright red color, and sweat rolls down his face as he sits up and leans forward onto his knees. His breathing is deep and heavy. He sits there for a moment, heaving in and out with his head hung and swayed to the left, and his arms resting gently on the top of his knees. Ice stares off to the side, and his eyes glaze over, as if he as day-dreaming. GW trainer Donny Walsh walks over to Ice with a bottle of water in hand, and offers it to him. Ice's eyes close quickly reopen as his day dream is ended. He nods in acceptance of the water, and reaches up. 'I'm going to go grab some towels for the next exercise.' Again Ice nods in response to Donny Walsh. Ice watches as Walsh walks out of the weight room, and turns down the hallway. Slowly Ice unscrews the water bottle's lid, and tips the bottle back. The cool sensation of the water brushing over his lips, across his tongue, and down his throat encompasses Ice, as he drinks the bottle dry. He pulls the empty bottle away from his lips, screws the cap back on, and twirls the bottle around in his left hand. Again Ice's eyes glaze over, this time as he watches the empty water bottle twirl around in his hand. Over and under, over and under, over and under, the bottle rolls as Ice plays with it. 'Alright, I'm back. Let's get down to business.' Ice's daze is again broken by Donny Walsh. His twirling of the empty water bottle in his hand ends, and Ice turns towards Donny. Ice looks at Donny for a moment, then turns back, looking away from him. Ice tosses the empty water bottle from his left hand into his right hand, then tosses the bottle out of his right hand, off the wall, and into a trash can next to the wall. Donny begins to clap as Ice turns with an arrogant half smirk on his face. The two men walk over to the jet black squat rack, which sits across the room from the bench press, where they just were at. 'What do we have next Donny?' 'I'm glad you asked that. We're going to be doing pull up nexts. But I'm adding a little something to it. I'm going to tie these towels around the top, and you'll be gripping onto them, pulling yourself up. What this does is build the muscles in your hands, focusing on strengthening your grip. I figure with this cage match coming up, it wouldn't hurt to do something like this.' Ice doesn't say anything in response. He just stands and watches as Donny ties the towels onto the top bar of the squat rack. Donny steps away as he finishes his tying, and Ice immediately steps forward and begins to grip the towels. Once he gets a good grip Ice pulles himself up, and lowers himself back down. The perspiration from his straining begins to trickle down his body, over his large bulging muscles. As Ice continues through his reps, a voice other than Donny Walsh's is heard. 'And this ladies and gentlemen is the work out facility for our superstars here in GW. And what a surprise today. Former World Champion, and current International Champion Ice.' Ice drops down after finishing his last pull up and turns to see a man in a suit, with a small crowd of about fifteen people behind him. Ice looks over the situation, then turns and shoots Donny an angry glare. Donny meekly shrugs in reliance to Ice's glare, before approaching the man in the suit. 'What do we happen to have here Mr. Chambers?' 'Well, first let me introduce you. Everyone this is Mr. Donny Walsh. He is the head trainer here at Global Wrestling. He keeps our superstar's as healthy as possible, and like it appears he is doing so with Ice, helps the superstars with training. To answer your question Mr. Walsh, this is our bi - weekly arena tour.' Donny Walsh turns back to Ice. Ice turns away from him and the group and wipes the sweat off his face on one of the hanging towels. 'Ice, we apologize for interrupting, but this is a great opportunity to say hello to a few of the many fans of GW. If you aren't busy that is.' Donny again looks back at Ice, then back at Mr. Chambers. 'Mr. Chambers, Ice is in the middle of a very intense work out session. He is trying to prepare for his brutal cage match this week, and ......' Ice places his right hand firmly on Donny's shoulder, stopping him in mid sentence. Ice turns and looks over at Donny, as Donny nerveously looks up at Ice. Ice nods at Donny, before looking at Mr. Chambers and giving him an evil glare. Mr. Chambers swallows loudly, and takes a step back away from Ice. Mr. Chambers picks up the hint given by Ice's glare, and turns towards his tour group. 'It appears as though Ice is extremely busy in his training, and doesn't have time to sign any autographs. I apologize for this, and for interrupting his training. But there will be a chance for autographs with other superstars at the end of the tour. Now let us move along and continue.' A low groan from the group echoes down the hallway, as their flashbulbs go off, causing a sea of white. Ice and Donny squint at the excessive light, and turn away from it. The two begin to walk away from the door and get back to what they were doing before the interruption, until they are interrupted again. 'Excuse me.' Donny and Ice turn again, to see a woman, no more than 5' 6, with a slender, yet curvy body, in a pair of painted on black stretch pants, and a matching black halter top, with the word 'FREAK' in silver glitter across the chest. Her brown hair rests on her shoulders and hugs the sides of her pale white face. The paleness of her face makes her bright blue eyes stand out even more than what they would if she had some color to her skin. The woman meekly smiles and steps into the room. 'Excuse me. I don't know if you recognize me, but I met you the other day.' Ice raises his left eyebrow in a questioning manner to the brunette's comments. 'In the parking lot. Of the hotel you live in? My name is Andrea, you and I talked, you signed a couple autographs, and took some pictures with me. Then you had to leave, with Brandi Sights. Anyways, I just wanted to apologize for freaking out like I did that day. And I also wanted to thank you for being so kind. So...uh... if you aren't busy, could I buy you dinner this evening?' Andrea pale face turns a light shade of pink as she asks her question. Ice's questioning expression changes to a look of surprise at the girl's boldness. He looks over at Donny, and then back at Andrea before responding to her question. 'That is mighty nice of you....' 'Andrea' 'Right. And I would love to take you up on that offer but I already have plans. Speaking of which. Donny, I'll be back in a moment, I have to go make a phone call.' Andrea looks hurt from the rejection, as Ice hurrily walks past her. She almost instinctively follows him out of the weight room, and down the hallway. 'Excuse me again. Ice....I hope you don't think I'm some kind of freak, or whatever.' Ice looks down at her shirt and smirks. Andrea notices his eyes wandering to her chest, and looks down herself to see the front of her shirt. Embarassed she blushes and nerveously laughs.' 'Well.. haha... yea, the shirt doesn't help. But I was just wanting to repay you for your kindness. But if you have plans ... that's cool too, maybe some other time then??' She shoots him one of those puppy dog faces, hoping for his agreement to her proposal. 'Sur....' As Ice is answering the girl he is interrupted by a sexy female voice. 'Hey you. We still on for dinner tonight?' Ice turns to see the lovely Brandi Sights standing behind him with a seductive smile on her face. Ice partially smirks at her and nods in repliance to her question. 'Yea, I was just on my way to call and ask you the same thing. I've got to finish this workout, then shower. I'll meet you at six?' 'See you there.' Brandi waves as she walks away, and Ice along with Andrea watch as she goes. Andrea has an evil, angry expression on her face as she watches the beautiful red head walk away. Ice turns and notices it, but ignores it and walks past her, heading back to the weight room. FADE
