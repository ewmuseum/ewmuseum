A happy New Year? Not so much

10 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/10/06 A happy New Year? Not so much. January 10th Toledo, Ohio. Morning Fred Grest is in his bed room throwing clothes onto his bed. Literally. It looks like he is packing to leave for Manchester, England where Reloaded will be held. Finally getting back into action after the break. That’s what he is had to do over the break in December. A month that was not kind to Fred Grest. Aside from losing to Jorge Samuelson he lost the girl yet again which was quite common. This time it was over something that usually gets him the women. Wrestling the thing that has made him famous even though as of late he’s been nothing but a foot note. The clothes start piling up over the gym bag and suit case. Most of which seemed to have been bought straight out of a Hot Topic with some smartass remark written on it. He thought about the match he had coming up and weather or not he stood a chance in hell in it. He’s wrestling two former world champions in Jay Roberson and The Innovator of fucking greatness KPJ. Grest groans as he walks over to his bed and starts shoving clothes into the gym bag. Grest doesn’t seem to thrilled about his return to the ring. Its almost as if he’d rather be doing something else. Anything else. Grest’s head was nowhere in the game and he knew that. He zips up the bag quickly and a sleeve gets in the way. He unzips it and punches it down. Staying down he zips it up all the way and throws it in the corner. He plops down on his black king sized bed. Sprawled out on the bed wondering what 2006 will hold for him in and out of the ring. He doesn’t think much of 2006 because 2005 or 2004 didn’t hold much for him. Every goal he has set out to do in PTC he has failed. This is where the best wrestle and Grest has proved one thing that he is not the best. He once used to say You’ve messed with the rest, now mess with the best, because there isn’t anything like Fred Grest. He comes no where near that now that much is obvious. “I’m so pathetic.” he says to himself. He lifts himself out of the bed as he has done so many times before. He grabs his bag and throws it over his shoulder. Walking out of the room he heads down the steps with his head held high knowing one thing. That there is hope that he can come out on top in spite of the talent he faces to night. He promises himself one thing. He’ll won’t lie down for any of his three opponents and will give each one a fight. Regardless if they are a former every champ like TLM and KPJ seem to be. He opens the door and heads down the rickety old steps. The last step you can hear crack loudly. He turns around to look back at it and thankfully its still intact. “I have to get that fixed.” Grest shakes to his head and heads to the car. Opening the door to his black 2004 Escalade. He pulls away not mentally prepared for his match as usual. All he’s going on his the same thing that’s always going on. Hope. Hope that things will get better and that needs to change. He needs something more then hope and the best way to do that is too give it everything he has and win The Platinum Championship.
