The Big News (PPV # 4)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: today Through all of our lives, Lara has never let me down. When our mother died when I was just 6, lara became the woman I looked up to as my mother, even though she is only 4 years older than I was, she was there for me. When girl scouts had their mother daughter slumber party, she was there for me. My first date, she helped me through the uneasiness I had. When prom came she was there helping me getting ready, and looking my best for the biggest day of my life. And when I wanted to go to Europe, she paid my way. Because of her, I am the woman I am today. Because of her I met the love of my life. And now that he has caused me to break, Lara is once again there, by my side, to pick up the broken pieces, and put them back together. 'Aww. Honey, don't worry about him. He's ignorant, and doesn't deserve you if he's going to treat you like that. You know that, but I also know that it doesn't change how you feel. So let it all out sweetie. Don't worry, I'm here for you and we'll get you through this.' Her words comfortating as they are, still don't change the feelings flowing through my body. How did this happen? How did I go from having the life I've always dreamed of, to this hell I am living in now. These tears just don't seem to stop. My mind, is not in control, my heart has no use of functioning any longer. WHY? Why did this have to happen? Why did I have to feel this? Why now? As I sit in the living room of my sister's house, half the country away from the man I love, crying my eyes out over the trauma my life has just been through, these thoughts run through my mind. Driving me crazy everytime I say them in my head to myself. Driving me crazy that just two days ago everything was fine. But now I sit here, with my head on Lara's shoulder, letting out everything I've been feeling for the past two days. Thinking about the love I have for that man, and how it hurts to be here, and not with him. Again my eyes flood with tears, and I begin to sob hysterically at the thought of what I have just lost. I don't know what I have left in me to give anymore. I gave it all I had for us, only to have it all thrown away. Now, with this news, that I have been given, I still have one last thing to give. I want to go back, and see him again. I need to go back and tell him this news. But how will he react? I wonder if he will give me that cold emotionless reaction I have lived with for so long. Or will he be the man I fell in love with so sweet, and caring, full of love and emotion for me. 'Lara, I just don't know what to do. I want him in my life. I need him in my life, but I can't live with what he has become. I just can't do it. And now...' 'I know. I know. I know sweetie. It will all be ok. You just stay here with me and Stan for a few weeks, get yourself put back together, and then we will figure things out. I promise you everything is going to be just fine. Have I ever let you down?' Come to think of it, she never has. Everytime I've ever needed her, she has been there for me. Without question, with lecture, she has stood by my side, and walked down this path called life with me every step of the way. And once more I call on her to be my crutch, in this time of my life. 'Lara, you don't understand. This is more than just what happened with Brock and I.' I can tell by the questioning look on her face I have confused her with my last statement. I finish letting out my last few cries, and catching my breath, before I answer her quizzicle look. 'Sweetie, what are you talking about?' I give her an uneasy look before answering. 'Before what happened between Brock and myself occurred, while he was at his sessions the days before I had gone to the doctor, but didn't tell him. I hadn't been feeling well, and when I would wake up in the morning, I would get sick. So I went to the doctor and had him check a few things out.' Her caramel brown eyes widen, in a knowing anticipation of what I was going to tell her. The sound of the door opening steals her attention as her husband Stan walks in. She immediately gets up and greets him with a loving kiss, and leads him over towards the couch. I was really hoping to tell Lara alone, about this before telling anyone else. But now that I have began to tell, and knowing that she already has the thought spinning in her head, I can't just leave her waiting in anticipation. Plus, I'm going to go nuts if I don't tell anyone soon! 'Hun, please finish.' I take in a long deep breath, and exhale it quickly. As the breath leaves my body, my hands begin to shake, as the nerveousness of telling my sister the news sets in. 'Well, I went to the doctor, before I woke Brock up that morning. I was going to wake him up, make him breakfast in bed. Take him to his sessions, go home, and prepare a romantic lunch, so that we could go have a picnic, and then tell him there. Well, that all didn't go as planned as we all know. Which is why I'm here.' The tears begin to creep their way back, and down my face, as I relive that day in my mind one more time. Lara opens her arms wide, and embraces me. Stan gently rubs his hand down from the top of my head to the back of my neck, in attempt to show some compassion and comfort. But I pull away, and stop the crying before it really begins and prepare to give the news. 'So anyways, when I went to the doctor that morning, he told me, that the reason for me not feeling well, and getting sick, was because I'm pregnant.' As the words squeeze between my now grinning lips, Lara and Stan smile in joy, and both rush towards me, in congratulation, with a barrage of hugs and smiles. Even though I'm smiling back, happy of the news myself, and happy to have their support, the taste in my mouth about it is still bittersweet. Because, the father of this unborn child, does not know. The father of this unborn child, is not here with me celebrating our news. But more importantly, the father of this unborn child I am carrying, I feel, is not mentally able to handle this responsibility. The smile fades off my face, as I again begin to cry uncontrollably. 'Sniff'..........'He doesn't know. But he needs to know. But I don't think he can handle this. I don't believe he is ready to handle this.' The joy and happiness they had, subsides as well. A troubled look of concern now is etched on their faces, as the realization of my thoughts and worries sinks in their minds. Stan looks as though he is wanting to say something, but holds back, when Lara speaks up first. 'Well, maybe we should go to Dallas together, so that he can find out. And then we can be there for you and bring you back up here to Lansing afterwards.' I shoot her a reluctant, grateful smirk, but shake my head from side to side replying to her idea. I know where his mind is. I know that right now he is focused on MAXIMUM CARANGE. I know how much this Tuesday night means to him. And going down there now, telling him this news, is not what he needs. 'You know he has a big night this week. And as important as this news is, he doesn't need to hear it right now, at this time. But you are right, he does need to know.' The discussion is abruptly stopped as the telephone rings. Being that I am sitting right next to it, I turn and answer. 'Hello, Hedley's, this is Star speaking.' No reply or response is given. The low humming of what could be a vehicle engine, or a water heater, or a loud and noisy washing machine is the only sound that comes through the phone. 'Hello? Is anyone there?' Stan and Lara give me a puzzled look, and ask me who it is. I shrug my shoulders in response, and wait for something from the other end of line. There is a click followed by a dial tone, and I know that the other end has hung up their phone. I slowly put the phone back down on the receiver. 'Okay, so who was that?' 'No one, just a recording from a telemarketer.' They both nod, and Stan stands up and heads towards the kitchen. Lara watches him and then looks back over at me, and gives me a reassuring, 'it's going to be alright', smile before following her husband. I begin to smile, not because of Lara's reassurance, but because I know in my heart, that it was he that just called to see how I was.
