Visiting Father.

19 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 19 The scene opens up to a fairly new hotel room. Very familiar to when Roswell was first there. A figure opens the door and walks into the pitch-black room. The figure switches on the light and it is Roswell. He walks into the room and opens the curtain seeing kids going down the waterslide. He then walks over to the bathroom door and slowly walks inside it. Inisde the bathroom Roswell is seen taking his clothes off and walking into the shower. “I hope Father Clark will be alright” Roswell says in a deep voice. “But I also have to worry about my match with Tyler Pierce” he says again. 'Well I don't have to WORRY. He is a new guy, yet I was once a new guy so' Roswell says yet agan. “Oh shoot! I have to leave to the arena tomorrow. What about Father, he won’t get to come” Roswell whispers to him-self. Roswell then turns the shower off and walks out with a towel wrapped around his waist. He watches into the mirror as he combs his black hair in a ponytail. He then walks out of the room and grabs a suitcase off the ground, and opens it. Roswell reaches into the suitcase and grabs out his red and white sweater and black wind pants. Roswell walks over to the desk at his bed head and grabs his car keys off of it. He then goes over and shuts the curtain on the window covering the faces of the people outside. Roswell then walks over to the light switches and turns them off. Roswell then opens the door and locks the door on his way out. 'I hope Father is alright' Roswell says as he walks down the lobby hallway. Roswell walks over to the spinning doors in the lobby exits and walks through them. Outside Roswell is seen opening the door of a car and starting it up. The engine blows a puff of smoke out the back and leaves. Down the hard pavement the car is seen going about 80 miles an hour. Finally the car stops in the same parking lot as before that Roswell was in. Roswell opens the door of the car and runs out to the hospital doors. Inside there is white rusty stairs that Roswell runs up. Roswell is then seen going into a elevator, with the grey metal doors closing behind him. On the 5th floor the elevator doors open with Roswell walking out of them. Roswell then walks over to 2 white doors and pushes through them. He also just walks over to a curtain in one of the many rooms and staggers in. Inside the curtain Father Clark is seen with a white checkered hospital suit on. Roswell walks over to a chair that is sitting right by the window, and sits down in it. Outside the window rain starts pouring down onto the California streets. 'How's it going, Father' SCENE ENDS
