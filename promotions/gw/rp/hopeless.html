Date: Sunday Inside the guest house of Zero Estate on a particularly sunny summer afternoon Deacon Holmes sits on the Berber carpeted floor wearing nothing more than a pair of mesh gym shorts that stretch past his knees with the words “Poster Child” printed across the front and a pair of tight fitting white cotton socks that just barely reach his ankles. Behind him sits his girlfriend Rhaiza on the edge of the bed, legs parted the width of Deacon’s shoulders in order for him to fit in between them. With her hands working diligently and extremely dexterously she peers down at Deacon’s head as she braids his hair, which currently stands in a large puff of an afro somewhere around two feet in height. Out of the shallow hip pocket of the frayed cut off bottom jean shorts trying their best to cover up Rhaiza’s lower body stands a thin pink comb, which she breaks out sporadically to comb his soft curly hair straight in order for her to braid. Deacon’s arms lay draped across her near naked muscular thighs with several small rubber bands clinging to each finger which had been placed there by Rhaiza for later use. In his right hand he clutched the television remote with a kung fu grip. Continuing to work to tame his unruly mane, after a quick glance up at the TV across the room, which Deacon’s eyes had been glued on, Rhaiza begins to speak while returning her eyes to the forest of hair before her. “I was thinking the other day, we’ve been together for a while right?” she says, keeping one hand on a strand of hair while dipping the thumb and forefinger of the other into a small container of grease sitting nearby. “Yeah” replies Deacon dryly, obviously not paying attention. As a matter of fact, had he been asked if he’d be willing to leap into shark infested waters to save a drowning cat at that moment the same response would have most likely been garnered. And Deacon hates cats. Taking note of his tone, Rhaiza continues holding onto an long strand of hair while lifting herself up off of the bed slightly and bending forward almost to the point that she was doubled over in order to look Deacon in the eyes. “I’m serious.” she informs him in a firm voice yet congenial voice, her long slightly curly reddish brown hair hanging down toward the floor. This warranted a quick rolling of Deacon’s eyes as he realized she probably wanted to “talk”. Now Deacon has never been one to shy away from talking, as a matter of fact, running is mouth played a big role in making him the star he is today. But in the area of relationships talking was to be avoided at all costs, especially in situations where the discussion was labeled “serious”, as more often than not it resulted in either and argument or him doing something he didn’t want to do. Dreading what he figured was to come, Deacon quickly rattled off “Yeah yeah, feels like an eternity...” before returning his eyes to the plasma screen with a mischievous grin on his face as he knew there would be some sort of backlash for his comment. Rhaiza flash a quick smile before returning to her seated position. At almost the exact instant at which she made contact with the mattress Deacon felt a sharp acute pain coming from his scalp. He immediately yelled out in pain as the torture, which had only lasted a second, stopped. A satisfied smile on Rhaiza’s face shattered into a sea of laughter due to his reaction to his hair being pulled. The fact that he went out and got beat on by men who were more often than not twice his size with no complaint yet screamed when at her mercy was simply too funny for her to keep quiet. “That’s what you get!” she warned him before starting on the next braid with a playful nudge to the back of his head. After a brief pause in which she caught her breath, Rhaiza resumed her previous line of questioning. “Now that I have your attention...” she started, a grin coming over her full lips as she finished a braid and took another strand firmly in her grasp before continuing “I was thinking, we’ve been together for a while... so why haven’t you ever introduced me to your parents?” Trying to turn the tables, Deacon quickly shot back “Why haven’t you ever introduced me to yours?” in a slightly accusatory tone while raising an eyebrow, yet still not prying an eye away from the television. Rhaiza scoffed at Deacon’s weak attempt at turning the tables and quickly silenced his argument. “I’ve invited you a million times to come meet my parents, but you always say California is too far of a drive, and you refuse to get on a plane! I’ve told them all about you though... I bet yours don’t even know about us.” she replied, every word of it spoken truthfully. Allowing every word of it to enter one ear, rattle around a bit, then slide effortlessly out the other, Deacon merely shrugged before applying smugly with “They don’t.” Another sharp pain shot through Deacon’s head and he winced in agony as the skin of his scalp was stretched upward while holding onto his hair for dear life as Rhaiza gave it a quick, hard tug before responding “Why not!””, but to no response. Deacon remained silent for a few moments before the process was repeated, garnering the same result, which wasn’t the one she was going for. Deciding to give it a third try, Rhaiza yanked Deacon’s hair once more while simultaneously leaning down near his ear and reiterating her question. “Huh!”” she exclaimed while pulling his hair, causing his eyes to squint and tear up slightly. Having had much more than enough, Deacon sprang up from his seated position, ripping Rhaiza’s hands away from his head in the process, and quickly whirled around one hundred and eighty degrees in order to face her. During this lightning fast display of his explosive nature Deacon also managed to answer her question “Alright, damn!” he began as he tucked his legs under his body before springing from the ground. Then turning to look her dead in the eyes, those sparkling green, beautiful, now frightful eyes... he continued “Because I don’t have any.” Fright quickly turned to sorrow as Rhaiza’s stiff defensive posture melted and her eyes became large and watery. “I... I’m sorry, I... I didn’t know Deacon.” Slowly, she rose to her feet and stepped closer to Deacon to console him as he stood rigidly with his chest slowly expanding and contracting as he audibly exhaled from his nostrils with his hands balled into fists. He wouldn’t dare hit her, but he’d never discussed the issue of his past with anyone before, and didn’t enjoy doing it now. Slowly but surely, tearing up in the process, Rhaiza made her way over to Deacon and wrapped her arms around him, her hands slowly moving in a smooth, quiet, continuous motion while gliding over his back softly in an attempt to make him feel better. Deacon remained still as a statue as Rhaiza laid her head on his chest and pressed her body against his. Through his chest she could hear the sound of his rapidly thumping heart trying to beat it’s way would of his slowly heaving chest. “I... I really didn’t know. I’m so sorry.” she said apologetically, but to no response. With a look into his eyes which were now burning with rage long since locked away, she proceeded to ask a question she already knew the answer to “Are you mad?” Deacon broke her embrace and moved over to his dresser, snatching up a normal sized rubber band and removing the army of small ones from his fingers once there. With his back turned, Deacon proceeded to answer her question “Yes.” he answered over his shoulder before continuing “but not at you.” while turning to face her. A flat smile came over Rhaiza’s face, lasting only a moment before disappearing as she watched him pull his now completely straight hair back into a pony tail and secure it with a rubber band before moving toward the door. As he approached it, Rhaiza watched on, a few odd tears welling up and sliding down her face before she found the courage to ask him another question. “Where are you going” I... I thought you wanted me to braid your hair.” she inquired in a fluttering voice. “I do.” he replied in a cold fashion, prompting her to rush to the door, almost blocking it with her body as she looked into his eyes. “Then where are you going?” she asked, worried as to what he was about to go do. Deacon’s brows furrowed as a quick scowl fell over his face before he replied flatly “To train.” That said, he maneuvered past his girlfriend, and right out the door. Leaving her behind to wonder just how mad he really was. While that question remained to be decided as the sound of a Kawasaki Ninja ZX-12R speeding off filled the air, one thing was for certain.
