Happiness

31 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05.31.05 'I can't believe you're still asleep,' laughed Ken Partridge Jr as he and Gunner Addison stepped through the door of his penthouse home. They were both dressed casual chic in their sunglasses, Ken in a pair of strategically torn RLPL jeans, black Prada tuxedo loafers, an untucked white button-up by Versace with the sleve rolled up, and his red-tppied black hair mussily styled. Gunner in a pair of black Epidemic flare pants, black HT platform boots and a torn-tattered white short-sleeve with black Paris script scribbled all over it. They truly looked like they belonged in SoHo. 'What time is it,' asked a groggy Josh, who'd fallen asleep on Ken's sofa instead of the extra bedroom the night before. His hair was a black mess with blue streaking through it, so it muched the usual, and he was still wearing the ripped Versace jeans that he'd on the previous evening. 'About eleven forty-five,' responded Ken as he looked at his watch, tilting his coffee slightly in the process. Gunner smiled at a now sitting Josh on her way to the kitchen to put up a few of the things they'd just purchased at the market. 'Morning, Josh,' chuckled Addison as she handed him a cup of coffee from the nearby organic coffee shop SoHo Coffee Co. 'Morning, Mrs. Partridge,' chuckled back the Youth as he ran his hand through hair, the other graciously accepting the hot cup of joe. 'Hey,' began Gun laughing, 'when it happens, he's gonna' become Ken Addison, and don't you forget it.' Ken raised an eyebrow and let a smirk spread across his face as Gunner finsihed her way into the kitchen, tossing a wink Ken's way in the process. 'Yeah, yeah, yeah,' said Ken. 'Laugh it up.' 'What is this,' asked Josh with half a grimace on his face after tasting the contents of his cup. 'It's a decaf skinny caramel macchiato,' responded Ken, taking another drink of his. 'With sugar-free caramel of course.' 'From that organic joint,' fired Josh with another question. 'Of course,' smiled Ken. 'This stuff'll kill you, man,' joked Josh as he took another drink. 'Ha! Ha,' mocked KPJ with a smile still on his face. 'Get dressed man, I've got something I want to show the two of you.' 'Gunner,' yelled Josh, 'should I be scared?' 'I've got no idea, Hun,' replied Addison returning from the kitchen. 'He won't tell me what it is. apparantly it's one of his little surprises.' 'Surprises,' started Josh, 'uh huh. Like that one where I ended up somewhere in Canada naked on a building with some guy named Dunlevy.' Gunner gave Josh a perplexing look as Ken began to laugh, turning her attention to him with an 'explain this' stare. 'Joshy,' started Ken through his chuckles, 'I told you not to drink all of that Absenth.' 'You told me it was a non-alcoholic drink,' laughed Josh. 'Let me get this straight,' laughed Gun. 'You got loaded on Absenth that you thought was non-alcoholic and woke up naked on the top of a building with some other guy in Canada.' 'Yeah,' he responded. 'Don't ever get drunk on that stuff because it makes your butt hurt really bad the next day.' Gunner's eyes got huge as she shot off of the sofa, where she had been sitting next to Josh. KPJ and Josh were to busy losing themselves in laughter to realize how confused she truly was about the entire situation. 'Nothing happened, Gun,' stated Ken. 'The guy was some fan boy named Billy that we met up there. Josh was so hammered that he'd passed out, so we took him to the roof of a hotel and paid that Dunlevy kid like fifty bucks to stay with him and make sure he stayed okay.' 'Yeah,' started Josh, pulling a grey 'Silvertide' shirt over his head. 'Then I come back down the elevator in nothing but a pair of boxer shorts to find Ken and Brock laughing their tails off in the lobby with a pile of clothes in the chair between them.' 'Hey,' lauhed KPJ, 'you laughed about it, too.' 'When,' questioned Josh. 'Like, two or three hours later, but you thought it was funny.' 'Well,' smiled American Youth, 'it was a little funny.' 'Sweetheart, you should have seen him,' started Ken again. 'He gets off of the elevator in nothing but a pair of Snoopy boxers and a perplexed look on his face with his hands over his crotch.' 'Hey,' retalliated Josh, 'it was cold. Besides, you said I looked cute.' 'Absolutly precious, pumpkin,' laughed Ken. 'Okay,' said Gunner with a smile on her face. 'I gonna' let you two lovebirds finish this up while I change shoes.' Ken contiued laughing into the kitchen, where he finally sat down the bags he'd been carrying. In the living room, Josh was lacing up a pair of Chuck Taylors. Ken was shortly joined by Josh, who began helping put up the contents of the bags. 'So where are we going, Amigo,' asked Josh. 'Well,' responded Ken, 'later on you and I are hitting the gym at The Elements. We've got to get ready for this tournament.' 'You know what I meant,' smiled Josh. 'And you know what I meant when I said it's a surprise,' said KPJ as he retalliated with one of his million dollar smiles. The drive didn't take long. Ten minutes, tops. They were still in SoHo when Ken switched off the engine to his silver Mercedes CLK-GTR S.V. The three made their ways out and stared up at a beautiful two-story Greek-Mediterranian house, Ken with a very satisifed smile on his face. Gunner and Josh simply looked at each other and shrugged. 'Um,' began Josh, 'so this is the surprise?' 'Yep,' fired back Partridge in a single word response. 'I'm not so sure I understand, Sweetheart,' said Gunner as she placed her hand on Ken's chest.' Ken simply pointed at a sign in the front yard with 'SOLD' written across it in giant red letters. Josh and Gunner once again looked at each other, this time with smiles on their faces. As much as Ken loved the loft penthouse that he'd lived in for six years now, he'd always wanted to buy a house. He'd often told Josh that the only thing keeping him from doing so was that he didn't want to be in one by himself. He wanted it to have a family atmosphere when he finally purchased one. 'Dude,' said Josh with a smile on his face, 'I know you're trying to cheer me up, but you didn't have to buy me a house.' 'It's not for you, Sweet Cheeks,' laughed Ken. 'well, not entirely, anyway. It's for us, all of us. It's got six bedrooms, which is more than enough. One can be ours, one can be yours, and we make the others into guest rooms.' 'Ken,' started Josh, 'I--' 'You're moving in with us, Josh,' said Ken, cutting him off. 'Gunner and I'd already decided that you were going to move into the loft with us. I don't want you looking for another place. Just move in. I mean... we're a family.' The three had pbviously lost thought that a house had just been purchased as they began discussing more pressing matters. 'Josh, you're like a brother to Ken,' began Gunner, 'an you're one of my most near and dear friends. This is we want.' Josh, fighting back tears, responded with the only thing he could, which was his own way of accepting the offer. 'You bought a freakin' house?!' 'Oh,' began Gunner, 'shit. I can't believe we lost the subject. When exactly were you going to discuss this with me.' 'Look at it, Gun,' stated Ken. Gunner gave the home another look before a gigantic smile breached across her beautiful face and tear began to sweel in her eyes. 'Wait... is... is this--' 'It's the house you always make me drive past so you can look at it,' confirmed Ken. 'I- I've already decorated this house in my mind... I love this house.' 'That's why I've been in the process of buying it for the past few months,' smiled Ken. Gunner through her arms around him and pressed her lips against his before Ken held up the key in fornt of her. 'Why don't we go on in, huh,' said Ken with a smile on his face. Gunner grabbed the key gently and kissed Ken again before walking quickly to the front door an unlocking it. As she waved them in, Josh looked at Ken and gave him a hug, which Ken returned. 'Thanks, Buddy,' said Josh with the utmost emotion. 'You two... you really are my family.' Ken smiled at Josh... at his brother. 'Welcome home, Josh.'
