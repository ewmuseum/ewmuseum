In the beginning

29 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/29/03 It is said I have no feeling. It is said that what I am is a mythos....a tale....to some.....a legend. Since I have told the world of what I am mortals have began to look over their shoulders at night. They wonder if they might be the next meal. They wonder if they may come to the hands of creatures such as I. But what they dont know is how I came to be. Now it is time to share the legend of how it all began.....how it was I, the vampire that the world knows as Leinad Soma actually came to be, in the beginning....No that is not how I should start this story, but its the only way to start it. I sit down in my chair.....my throne in the deepest darkest part of my pit and remember how it first began. 'Do you remember when it all began Leinad? Do you remember why we were created?' Cassandra asked as she lay in my lap pressed close against my chest. 'We were created, you and I as the opposites of mankind. It was you and I who were the first creatures of humanity....it was we who brought forth sin into the world. It was we who propositioned Adam and Eve. It was we who brought wickedness, hatred, mysticism, and magic into the world. We..... Sameul and Lilith...... Koumuo Ade and his queen.. Since the dawn of time we have lost and found each other. Each time another incarnation... Each time reclaiming what we have lost....our love....and our hatred for humanity. We are what we are....murderers and deceivers. And we have left a unique trail throughout history and now we have finally started cleaning up after ourselves. We started with the redeemer....the hunter of hunters....Revelation the mighty met his end at my hand and now it is time to clean up everything.' 'I agree my love. It is time to find the Ba Xian, the eight immortals.....my eight children...each century they meet and discuss the goings on of the vampire nation of the east, as your great grandchildren do with the vampire nation of the west. It just so happens that their meeting happens tonight and I know where.' 'Then tell me where Cassandra and we will go finish unfinished business...' She whispers in my ear and I look to her and smile. She stands while taking my hand, leading me to the staircase. The trip we take was an interesting one....reminding me of what once was how all men had to travel to get somewhere. Seeing parts of countries in a small plane, adventuring to someplace secluded from most of the world and it takes some sort of animal or by foot to the final part of the destination. Tibet. One of the last place someone would look for another human being....but alas we are not looking for human beings we are looking for those of the vampire nation's oldest vampires....theBa Xian The eight immortals who stood against their mother for control of the Dong Jia, The Eastern Family, or better known as the vampire nation of the east. These eight children, Bao(Panther), Xie Ze(Scorpion), Se(Snake), Lang(Wolf), Pai(Tiger), Hok(Crane),Ri(Sun), and Qian(Heaven) once tried to kill their mother in attempts to make them less than what they were...human. In the true story their were ten children, the last two being Yue(Moon) and Xing(Star). The two sided with their mother and in acts of greatness died defending their mother into the harsh and grim coming of the dawn. These ungrateful seeds of Cassandra turned on her when all she did was offer them immortality.....the chance to be like those who truly deserve this world....those born as our kind. To see the world as gods instead of insects. Her children saw this as a curse.....and for that we must end their suffering. The temple in which they choose to conjugate is hardly suprising. The temple on the highest of peaks looking down on a small village. The monks their tending to the every need of the Ba Xian, to the monks the reincarnations of the very essence of the world. We enter the temple dressed in the clothing of those befitting of godhood. I, representing the light of the morning star, take on the name Quian(Light), and Cassandra being the mother of night took the name Shen(Dark). The monks bow at the sight of us seeing that we are gods it is only befitting they bow with respect. They stand in silence, none moving, none staring, all in awe. We walk into what seems to be the great Hall of prayers . Being the spiritual center of the lives of the monks in a monastery they guard it very tight. Warrior monks surrounding the Ba Xian on all sides. This place, often dark, is only lit by high windows letting in the light of the moon. The dim light shines down on the small circle of the children of my beloved as she walks quietly around the circle. She stands close enough to touch them and yet none of them move. None of them make any motion of attack. I stand there quietly watching the events play out like an old martial arts film. Seeing a myth from the days before the Christ, from Asia with all the beauty of a geisha but with all the destruction of the goddess Kali Ma. 'It has been too long mother. We have been expecting you for thousands of years and yet only now do you show your face once again.' I watch as the what seems to be young woman stands, her hair an almost too radiant white and her skin as pale as the snow. Her dress rather elegant for sitting on the floor was also disturbingly white. Her eyes, on the other hand red as the blood in which she feeds on. Instantly I realize she must be Qian. She looks to me and my eyes never falter in her presence. I give a small nod and a small smileas she turns and looks toward her mother. 'So it is true what many of our western cousins have said. You have formed an alliance with the Xian who has said he was the first...our dark father....' 'Foolish girl......Before any of you were even thought of.....Before any of you were created there was he and I. This is not merely an alliance. This is destiny once again bringing us together to put an end to what you and many others have brought bad name to. What you and many others have turned into a silly myth. You and those who are like you live in fear from what they are and were destined to be. My darling daughter....my lovely Qian, who hurt me so deeply when you killed your brother and sister when they stood by my side. This night you and your unruly siblings must be punished for your transgressions.' Another of the children stands, this one male. His complexion that of an olive color. His bald head glistening in the moonlight. He moved with a silent but deadly grace that could only befit the most skilled of assassins. His blood red eyes never leaving mine. We stand there staring at one another. Murderer to murderer. He gives me a nod and I, one to him. 'Mother it pains me to once again have to banish you with my siblings. I understand why you and that man do what you have done for centuries. I being a killer understand, but you must step down once again. Our time is not yet over and yours is not to begin again.' 'Prideful Se, this time is different. You shall be striken to the ground and made to eat the dust of the humans you protect.' 'I do not protect them mother. I am protecting the Xian way of life.' 'Se you should not have to explain yourself Se. We have defeated you once mother and, if need be, we shall again. Leave mother and never return for it would break my heart to have to kill you.' Another female stands wearing a tight black fitting dress and almost an exact replica of my Cassandra. The same jet black hair and the always present red eyes. This was Bao in her finest moment. She who looked as much like her mother had just threatened to kill her. I stand there in my silent state in awe of the beauty of the daughter for the only other female to captivate me like that was her mother. 'Bao, the child who looks most like me hates me more than the rest? What have I done to have you children turn against me? I gave you eternal life and I am repaid with the threat of murder.' 'Mother, it was you who came here to tell us you were going to punish us.' 'Xie Ze, I said punish, not murder, but since you brought it up, I suppose you all must die. I already know that what will be said from you Pai. You will say something to the lines of 'Mother your ways are not our ways.' and you Ri would say 'Why must we fight when we have defeated you before?'It is a shame but I know I am right.' 'It is a shame mother but still a true fact. We have defeated you before. Do I think we can if we must yet again? Indeed I do.' 'Mother, ye of little faith. This does not have to happen, but you are forcing us to make no other choice. Xie Ze, stands quietly, flaming red hair covering most of his tanned face, reminding me of my own face watching quietly... somber... cold... Pai and Ri both stand as twins a prone to do, at the same time. As they stand their, both in catholic priest robes, each glaring at their mother with a rather disgusted look. Cassandra stood there quietly look over her now all standing seeds. We all, standing in the silence of this ancient temple waiting for someone to take the first move. Cassandra looks at me with a tearful smile, the bloody tear running down her face. Qian looks at me and I back at her. 'Mother how do you still choose this creature over us. We could have loved you but your tireless hatred for those who could have served you well. Why must it always come to this senseless violence. We feed cause we have to and nothing more.' The only of her children who compared to me in size. The gray haired, red eyed Lang stands looking at his mother with pleading eyes. She walks over to him and runs her hand down his face. He takes her hand with what looks like remorse. Cassandra slowly takes her hand from his face and looks away from him. 'My son. You speak with such wisdom but you lack the drive to be what you should be. You should have the blood lust of a vampire instead of seeing yourself as a part of nature, an animal when you should be seen as a god.' ' Enough of this talk. What have you to say? You the foul creature that has forever corrupted our mother. You took her away from us and now you stand here planning our fate. What do you have to say!!' Qian screams at me, her eyes becoming more of a firery red. Her hatred for me shown very planly on her face. Hok stands in her plain white dress and tries to calm her sister down. Hok being only a child. An eternal porcelin doll, grabbed her big sister's hand looking at their mother. At this point I stay quiet for a moment as I have done all the way until now. She waits for me to speak as if my opinion matters in this situation. I walk closer to the circle and their mother, my eyes never falling from Qian's gaze. 'Well Qian let me first say you can call me Quian. Our names so very close and seeing that we are so different its ironic. You as well Se, for your name is my very being. You who are all distant children of mine....and yet unlike my own children you all rebel against your mother and for that you all will parish. I am sorry for this cause you all seem like you could be of aid in our destiny. But sadly I know what is to happen. But I do promise it will not be my hand or your mother's.' I give a slight nod and another smile. Qian turns to her mother in horror, then to her siblings, and then to the monks. As she turned to attack the monks pull stakes and swords from their robes. Some of the monks even grabbed torches. The monks of the temple begin to burn, impale and decapitate the Ba Xian. The screaming and cries from the children rang through the temple, even louder than the chants that were heard from many of the rooms in the temple. As each of the children fall, I look to Cassandra. Her eyes steadfast and never falling as each of her children fought valiantly but still falling to their deaths. Xie Ze.... Fought at least ten of the ever persistant monks as they each took their swords and plunged them into his chest. He killed each of the tainted monks except the last who took his head. Cassandra's eyes watch on. No feeling. No remorse. Hok.... A valiant effort by the least feared of the children. In an effort to save her sister Qian he took a stake to the back by using her own body as a shield to protect her older sibling. Cassandra sheds a bloody tear. For Lang was the most passive of all of her children. Pai and Ri.... Both met their end back to back as a group of the monks burned them alive. The screaming and the wailing of the two priest a sweet melody of anguish. The almost orgasmic smell of fear. I watch with a fanatical look of extreme pleasure. Cassandra a small smile on her face as those she considered the turncoats. The betrayers getting their just deserts. Lang... He fought off a great multitude of the monks. Each monk that seemed to get near the greatest of warriors among her children, came to horrible deaths. Blood covering over the almost invinsible Lang finally met his end while he was grabbing one of the fearful monks. He slipped and fell and the monks dismembered him once they got him to the ground. Cassandra watching the death of the peaceful warrior with a look if distaste. A warrior of his caliber should have died better. Se... The only one who got close enough to his mother to scar her. He slashed his mother cold face. His blow making his mother scream in pain. Her scream, so deafning it brought Se to the ground. I personally ripped his heart and bit into it and begin to feed. The sudden rush of his power into me. I look to Cassandra who stands once again, blood all over her face, she takes part of the heart and eats it. Bao.... The Xian looking most like her mother was pinned against a wall and burn alive. She did not fight back but looked at her mother and cursed her till she died. It was the first and only time when Cassandra weeped. Blood now covering her face. Blood shed for her daughter mixed with the blood from her now murdered son. Qian.... As she looked and saw there were no other siblings there to aid her she knealt down and looked at her mother. 'If I must go mother, I want you to know this. If I had to kill Yue and Xing all over again I would.' With a look of horror and one of hatred, Cassandra grabbed Qian by the head and bit into her draining her oldest daughter and then lighting the corpse on fire. By the hand of the mother, was the oldest of her children destroyed. I kiss Cassandra passionately as I wipe the tears from her face. Blood still covering both our faces I turn to the monks and I bow to them and those who are still living do the same. 'It's funny what a little money will do even to the holiest of men.' Cassandra smiles at me and kisses me on the cheek. She grabs my hand and we slowly begin to walk out of the temple. On the plane back I sit there quietly, Cassandra staring out of the window in silence. 'Are you sad for what had to happen?' 'No.' 'Is there something that is the matter?' 'No. I am thinking when our child grows the life we shall make for her.' 'What?' I look at her not knowing if she is serious ever hopeful that she is. I wait for her to say something but she just continues to look out her window thinking of the acts that transpired this night. The elimination of the Ba Xian but it also maybe the birth of something new. A child of both eastern and western blood. The world's first and only true full blood vampire. We sit on the plane in silence.
