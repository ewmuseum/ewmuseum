revelation: rewrite, rewrite, rewrite.

7 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: july 7th, 2003 Thinking about what to think about. I think I need to think about what I should be thinking. I have been in GW for three weeks, closing in on four, and I have more enemies then people I know. Let’s list those people that I have insulted or just made mad: Judgement (given), Tyler Pierce, Tyler Pierce’s friends and Tony Stecchino (not in the fed!). Let’s count the friends I’ve made: ... Well, I guess I could count Angus Bingham on this one, but I did punch him in the face. I think I’ve got too much to think about. What would work is if I had people think for me. Then, I could wrestle, have money, and not have to worry about threats from people who aren’t even in the federation. Maybe then I’d have people to back me up against “the only stable in GW” tee-you-eee. Maybe I wouldn’t be here alone, thinking about thinking to do thoughts I shouldn’t think about. The situations I go through are pretty perplexing, wouldn’t you say so? If I took a drink of tap water would the tongue twisters become clearer to you? I wonder if Sean Price gets what I’m getting at when I get through all the dust and clear my throat! Ahem. Ahem. “Trey?” What? “Trey.” Oh, yeah. Have to reply out loud. “Yes?” Ronny sits on the couch, looking at me strangely. His facial expression of confusion confuses me even. He has a brow raised and another one slanted down over his eyeball. One of his hands is on the top of the couch making his arm abridged, and the other across his hip, making his arm at an angle. Wow, he looks really weird like that, goofier than usual. I smile. “What are you doing, Trey?” I look at myself from head to toe. Hair, face, neck, shirt, pants, sandals. I think I’m okay. In my hand is the remote to the television (yeah, I just got a tee-vee!). I look at the pose, the position I’m in. Left hand on the couch, right hand leading the arm across the chair’s rest. Seems okay. Eyebrows in normal position, check. Heh, what is he talking about then? “What do you mean “what are you doing, Trey?” I think I’m not doing anything.” “What are you watching, then?” he asks. Oh. “Oh,” I flip the channel from the Golden Girls. Old habits don’t die fast, I guess. I tend to get caught up in my thoughts, thinking about things to think about, and lose track of reality. I like to get into my speedster and drive off into my imagination, where I don’t have to worry about the things I need to deal with. Ronny, the only person who I really talk to about this sort of stuff, analyses it as something everyone does, and that I shouldn’t worry about it. I’ve never been to a psychologist to talk about any of my thoughts, and that’s because I’ve never been interested. I know people who go there, they’re real messes though, and I don’t feel like I’m a mess. I feel good. I look good, I know that much. I am good when it comes to wrestling, that’s what they’ve always told me. “Going for a walk.” So I stand, move into the other room. Adapt to the situation outside by changing from pants to shorts, t-shirt to tank top. Evolve, you could say, to my surroundings. “It’s just like how the upper echelon is taking on an evolution; I seem to be doing the same thing everyday. I took the tie against Jeffery, and now I’ve come up with ways to improve my judgment on situations I find myself in. Maybe Sean Price has evolved since he lost that tag team title match to two people who have never tagged together before.” I take a bottle of water from my fridge, and step out the door. “Then again, it’s not Sean Price’s wrestling ability that I worry about when I deal with him Tuesday. What I fear having to deal with is the attitude that glows out from his pores every time he loses a match. His “partnah” Tyler Pierce after I picked up a clean pin over him one evening, had nothing to say but excuses. “It shouldn’t have happened,” “I was off my game,” and “I was tired from battling aliens all day long.” I see Sean Price in a heated situation, and he runs off the same little list of excuses as to why he lost.” Maybe I should light a cig for this one. And take a drag. “It just seems that this whole Evolution thing has gotten to him. If he isn’t able to go away with the win in this match, is the same passing of the blame going to happen to me that happened to true evil” Did I double team him, make it completely unfair from the beginning? The thing is, I have a feeling that Tuesday isn’t going to have anything to do with Evolution. It’s going to be about revelations. Realizations. Back to reality - Oh, there goes gravity, don’t forget to pump your wings Price.” Blowing the smoke out into the street, I feel I’ve evolved on this reflection. But something about it doesn’t seem just right. Sigh. Erase and start over. Rewrite, rewrite. I have to make these things perfect you know. I want to fit in with the crowd.
