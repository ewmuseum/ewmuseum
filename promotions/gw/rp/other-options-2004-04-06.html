Date: 4/6/04 It was a lazy afternoon. Brick often spent these afternoons training for matches or surfing the Internet. These days, he was usually surfing for info on the Luke Cage movie. This time he was checking his e-mail. One of the e-mails was from Aaron. The topic read 'GETTOSAKE 4.0.' Gettosake.com was one of Brick's favorite Web sites to visit. It was the official site for Gettosake Entertainment, a studio run by Jeremy and Robert Love. Among their most known creations was a crimefighter named Chocolate Thunder and a sort of pulp sci-fi character named Credence Walker. Another famous Gettosake creation was Venus Kincaid, a 30-year old government agent shrunken down to a 12-year-old version of herself. A live-action film was already in the works at Fox. http://www.gettosake.com He clicked on the link and a new window opened. It was to a new and improved site. There were new sub-sites for Chocolate Thunder, Credence Walker and other Gettosake creations. First, he clicked on the link for Credence Walker: In the annals of history, there are legends untold, extraordinary lives left undocumented, and stories long forgotten. Such is… THE LIFE AND TIMES OF CREDENCE WALKER An original graphic novel from Gettosake Publishing Story and Art by Jeremy and Robert Love Written by Travis G. Johnson RELEASE DATE: JULY 2004 Credence Walker, an adventurer from our world, is thrust into a strange alternate reality AFRICA, known as SHABAZZ, a land of strange creatures and exciting adventure. There he will struggle for survival and ultimately, enlightenment. Credence Walker is pure classic pulp adventure in the grand style of Flash Gordon, John Carter of Mars, Tarzan, and Doc Savage with the bloody fast-paced action of Robert E Howard's Conan the Barbarian rooted in African mythology. Robert Love says, 'It's rare that we get to see diverse lead characters in big epic sci-fi fantasy stories. Credence Walker is pure pulp adventure without the vague undercurrent of racism found in many of the stories from the 30's and 40's. This story has been with us for a long time, and we're excited to finally get it out there. Travis Johnson is an excellent writer and he knows the genre top to bottom. He was the perfect choice to supply the script and breathe life into these strange characters and worlds.' Travis Johnson says. 'I grew up on a steady diet of Al Williamson's Flash Gordon, HP Lovecraft's stories and various other old school science fiction/horror treats. Writing the adventures of a character like Credence Walker is a natural extension of all of that. I'm honored and excited to be a part of the project.' Jeremy Love says, 'CREDENCE WALKER will be a pocket-sized 128 page graphic novel that will only set you back 7 bucks. We always loved those old ACE Fantasy novels, and this is designed to fit on the shelf with those. This whole thing is one big experiment. If all goes well we'll release more Credence books. We hope to have this for sale at the San Diego Comic Convention in July.' Brick always believed in having other options. If he didn’t get the part of Luke Cage, he could always see about getting Credence Walker optioned as a feature film. Brick then went back to the home page and clicked on the link for the Chocolate Thunder sub-site. He read the following: KEVIN KING, a government agent presumed dead, returns to his home city to find it riddled with drugs and crime. Using the skills he learned as a government operative, he takes on the persona of a mysterious crime fighter. The people on the streets call him CHOCOLATE THUNDER. Kevin sets out to stop his former mentor, the drug kingpin known as KING SOLOMON, from enacting a deadly plot that will propel his empire to a global level. CHOCOLATE THUNDER is the ultimate 70's Blaxploitation film on paper. Mixing elements of superhero mythos and kung fu, Chocolate Thunder is an action packed yarn set against a backdrop of urban blight. Guns will blaze, fists will fly… The 140 page Graphic Novel will be released in OCTOBER 2004 from ONI PRESS. It will provide a totally revamped vision of Chocolate Thunder. Leave your premonitions behind and prepare for no-holds barred old school fun! JEREMY LOVE provides the story and pencils while his MIDNIGHT MOVER collaborator JEFF WASSON provides the inks. RELEASE DATE: OCTOBER 2004 Brick knew he’d have to lose a lot of weight if he was to play the lead role, but he didn’t rule out the idea of playing the main villain King Solomon. All in all, Brick liked to keep his options open.
