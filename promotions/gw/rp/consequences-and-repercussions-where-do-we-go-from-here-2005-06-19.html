Date: June 19th, 2005. Consequences and repercussions: 'Where do we go from here?' ---- 8:18 AM June 11th, 2005. Unknown location. The blue mailbox gleamed beneath the rising sun as the lone figure approached it. The figure belonged to a young male, physically fit and in his mid or early twenties, his face was hidden by black hair that fell down across his features. He wore a white mandarin suit and a black shirt. His black shoes clicked across the sidewalk as he reached the post office box, and pulled the hatch open. Holding a manilla envelope in his hands he glanced down at the mailing address. Wrestling International News. [detail withheld]. Satisfied, he drops it into the mailbox and turns to walk away, fading into oblivion. ---- 9:12 AM. June 19th, 2005. Seattle, WA. Sliding behind the wheel of her red 2002 Monte Carlo, Raki Saito jammed the keys into the ignition and with a quick wrist motion brought her car to life. Glancing up in the rear view mirror she gazed at herself, she was still beautiful despite the obvious sorrow etched across her face. Her big brown eyes were swollen and bloodshot from the tears she had shed, her black hair was a tangled mess, she didn't care enough to brush it out after her morning shower. Her attire was comfortable and casual, a pair of Capri jeans and white sandals with a white tank top stretched across her lovely figure. She may have looked more than fine on the exterior, but on the inside she was falling to pieces. Shifting her eyes away from the rear view mirror she turned them to the sunshade as she pulled it down. Tacked to the sunshade was a small photograph, a photograph that took a lot of strength to look at. Strength that she didn't have, yet she stole a glance never the less. There he was... Standing behind her, his arms thrown around her waist and his chin resting on her shoulder. His hair, as always, was a mess, his bluish gray eyes beaming with joy, his smile reflecting happiness into the camera. Her smile was just as wide as his, her heart radiating unparalleled exultation. Raki returned the sunshade to it's original state, a sad smile crossing her face as she glanced back up into the rear view mirror, before throwing the monte carlo in reverse and pulling out into the street. As much as she hated to admit it...she really did miss that boy. Pulling on a pair of shades to cover her tearful eyes she shifted into drive and sat off to begin her day, she turned the radio on as she hit the gas, the song that met her ears was familiar, far too familiar. '...would you die for the one you love? Hold me in your arms tonight.' 'I can be your hero baby... Well, I can kiss away the pain. I will stand by you forever. You can take my breath away.' Their song was playing on the radio, she reached down to turn it off but as Enrique Iglesias's voice broke through the speakers the photograph from the shade suddenly fell, fluttering gently into her lap. ---- 9:17 AM. June 19th, 2005. SoHo, NY. I kicked the covers off, my mouth opening wide with a big yawn as I swung my legs off of the bed, my bare feet landing on the floor. Today was the first day in a week that I hadn't awakened wondering exactly where I had ended up, but somehow Ken's penthouse was becoming more familiar by the day. Wearing only boxers I staggered forward towards the closet and pulled the door open, reaching inside I sleepily extracted a pair of worn Levi's and fresh shorts. I threw these items over my shoulder and reached back into the closet for a shirt, I grabbed a plain white t-shirt, thought better of it and returned it to the hanger before settling on a light gray 'Project Global' sleeveless shirt. Turning away from the closet I glanced over at the window, which had been invaded by the suns awakening rays. The alarm clock on the nightstand read 9:18, meaning that I was running roughly 20 minutes earlier than my normal sleep cycle. Next to the nightstand was a small collection of photographs, three in total. The first photograph...was of her. A beautiful woman, black hair flowing past her shoulders and down her back, big brown eyes flickering with bliss and a smile that echoed the former statement. She was stretched across the lawn, propped up on her elbows, her ankles crossed, seated next to her was the fool she had fell so hard for only to have her poor heart broken, I'm that fool. In the picture I looked so happy as I rested my head against hers, my bluish gray eyes shining with delight, my smile spreading abnormally across my face, one of my hands preoccupied with my mop of multi-colored hair, the other squeezing her body close to my own. Turning away from the photo I choked back the tears, swallowing a lump of emotion as I proceeded to find a shower. I couldn't look again...it hurt too much. ---- 9:44 AM. June 19th, 2005. 'This interview...is over.' Ken leaned across the table, his eyes flashing rare sparks of anger. 'Fine!' Branner shouted, slamming his fist against the table. 'Just walk out, walk out like you did on Rosa, walk out like you did on Espen!' The room suddenly changed colors...everything turned red. Images of brilliant crimson flashed before my eyes, my pupils dilating, an angry snarl coming across my lips as I faced Branner. My hands turned into claws, ready to rip Howie Branner's throatbox out and feed it to him, I was still trembling but now the shaking had became more intense, to the point of violence. A growl emitted from my throat as I lunged for Branner! Hands leading the way, zeroing in on Branner's throat I was prepared to literally kill the man with my bare hands but Ken served as an intervention stepping in front of me and throwing his arms up. 'Josh, stop!' Ken demanded, attempting to calm me down. 'This wasn't an interview Ken it was a set up, we both know it...' I seethed, trying to sidestep my partner. 'I know Josh, but attacking him isn't going to solve anything, he isn't worth it, just calm down, okay?' Ken pleaded with me, I was so infuriated that I hadn't even noticed how rationale the equally perturbed Ken had became in an effort to keep me from damaging my career. 'Ken...I just...' I didn't have a chance to finish my sentence as I made a desperate lunge attempting to escape from Ken's guard but my larger cohort caught me in his arms. I thrashed and struggled, Ken kept a firm grip on me, sliding behind me and dragging me out of the room, restraining me with both arms he nodded at an attendant to open the door and they complied willingly, Ken struggled through the door but finally managed to drag me out of the studio, kicking the door shut behind us. We fell to the ground, still thrashing and struggling, the larger KPJ landing on top. Leaning in he spoke to me in between weary gasps. 'Josh, stop, stop...calm down...' I began to calm down slightly at the gentle sound of Ken's voice, yet I still thrashed about in Ken's restraining grip, his strong arms didn't flinch as they kept me locked in place. 'Josh, we can either fight all day, or you can calm down...we'll go somewhere, just you and I and we'll talk about it, man to man, friend to friend, brother to brother. We can work this out...' Ken's voice was calm, which came as a surprise because I knew of the rage he had inside and believed that without a doubt he would have loved nothing more than smashing Branner's face in as well, but he also knew it wasn't the right way to handle this situation. 'Okay...', was the most I could manage to choke out, my body going limp in Ken's arms, my heart still pounding uncontrollably. 'Okay, just relax...' Ken said, as he slowly stood up, releasing his grip on me. I pushed myself to my knees and drug my shaking body to a vertical base with the aid of the walls of the hallway we stood in. 'Lets go home, okay Josh?' Ken asked, looking at me, a smile on his face, yet deep inside I knew he was as angry as I was. 'Okay...lets go home.', I nodded weakly. 'Just promise me one thing Josh...' Ken began, looking at me sincerely. 'Yeah?' I asked, still shaking. 'Make sure you don't eat any...shellfish, okay?' Ken asked, with a playful wink. 'Okay.' I promised with a smile. A smile... It felt good to smile.
