This one is for you, kid.

27 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 27th, 2005. ---- Colton Cornell. My first glimpse is of his back, and it doesn't impress me. His shoulders aren't broad, his frame is slender, hidden inside of a white track jacket, he's as thin as a rail, and in my eyes there is no way in hell he weighs north of the two hundred pound mark that he's billed at. His hair is a mop. Shaggy, wild and shooting in every direction as if he just rolled out of bed. Its dark, black to be more precise, with streaks of royal blue and sterling silver scattered throughout the tangled mess of hair on his head. A pair of small silver hoop earrings dangle from his ears, catching the reflection of a Washington Sun hanging high above Seattle, drifting to the west where the glorious golden rays will drop behind the mountains, to let night settle in on the birthplace of grunge. I'm still not impressed as I tap him on the shoulder and clear my throat as I announce my presence. 'Excuse me.' He turns, his movement quick, his muscles coiled as tight as a spring. As his eyes meet mine, I suddenly change my mind. There is something piercing, something cold, something hard in those eyes...I can't quite describe it. 'Yeah?' His tone is cordial but hardly warming as he faces me without a smile. I look over his clothes, taking in everything about him. Underneath his track jacket is a black t-shirt, a pink 'X' riding high on the left side, the words; 'Underoath' scrawled over the stomach in large white letters. His faded BKE jeans ride low on his hips, a gaping hole in the left knee, a smaller hole in the right ankle about two inches above his shoes; a pair of pink Chuck Taylors. His clothing is unique but not something you would expect a man of power to wear, I'm still skeptical. ...Until I look in those eyes again. Theres something in his eyes that changes everything about this moment. The loud buzz outside of the ritzy 'Seattle symphony' restaurant seems nonexistent as I find myself lost in those eyes. They aren't blue, they aren't gray...they seem to be a strange mixture of both. But it isn't the color that I find so striking, but rather something within the eyes themselves. Oft times its been said that the eyes are windows to the soul and if this is the case here, I pray to God that the windows make things look a lot bigger and a lot scarier than they are. Its a strange mixture of passion, of greatness, of...hell, I don't know, but his eyes are beginning to freak me out. I've changed my mind. Standing before me, pound for pound is the greatest Wrestler on the planet, 'American Youth' Josh, and quite possibly the most intimidating bastard I've ever came into contact with, in spite of the fact that my six foot one frame towers an easy four inches over him. 'Josh, I do presume?' I inquired with a smile as I extended my hand. 'Guilty as charged.' He answered. He took my hand and gripped it firmly, shook it twice, two hard jerks up and down before letting go. I looked at his chin, I didn't want to have to look back into those eyes again. 'I'm Colton Cornell, attorney at law. I do believe you were referred to me by our mutual friend Donovan Knight?' 'Donovan said you were the best.' He answered, I was a little surprised at this statement, based upon my knowledge of how wily and two-faced Donovan Knight was, the sports agent who had quickly gained the nickname of 'the parasite of Wrestling'. 'And you believed that bastard?' I said with a chuckle. Josh shrugged and smiled. 'Sometimes you have to trust your agent.' His smile grew wider and he winked at me on his next statement. ''Just...not very often.' The kid had his own reasons to distrust Donovan Knight, the sports agent who had hooked his wagon to the shooting star known as Josh and racked in countless millions of dollars by selling Josh's name and face, slapping it all over corporate America. In spite of his many downfalls, which would include; lying, cheating, conning and stealing, Donovan Knight was one hell of a businessman, which was the only reason Josh or I would ever be seen with such a scumbag. Then again, we were both counting on him for the big bucks, maybe we were scumbags too. 'Shall we go inside?' I asked, jerking my right thumb towards the double glass doors of the restaurant, my left hand occupied by a black attache case. 'After you.' He answered with a nod. I took the first step, swinging the door open and stepping through, he quickly caught the door and followed me in, trailing by just a few steps. It felt strange to have my back exposed to him, I hardly knew him, and didn't trust him at all. Anyone that had dealings with Knight Enterprises wasn't to be trusted; myself included, but I sure as hell wasn't going to tell the kid that, he could figure it out for himself. Donovan was right in his theory of business; theres a sucker born every minute, and it would just be a matter of moments before newborn baby Josh got welcomed into this world. ---- Josh. I stayed slightly behind the lawyer, I had already forgotten his first name and if I wasn't an Audioslave fan I wouldn't have remembered his last name either. He was very business like, which is corporate America slang for 'conceited asshole'. He wore a black suit, matching black shirt and Italian leather shoes, a silver tie and black attache case finished off his look. His black hair was high and tight, the buzzed look suggesting perhaps prior military experience, a class ring on his left hand from the Naval academy verified that thought. 'Do you have reservations, sir?' Asked a chirpy blonde, with a big smile and even bigger chest, protruding from a white shirt, tucked beneath a black dress. 'Yes, table for three.' Cornell answered. 'The name, please?' She asked, ignoring Cornell's eyes locked onto her cleavage. 'Cornell...or it might be under Knight.' Cornell said, his brown eyes flickering with lust. 'Donovan Knight?' Asked the waitress. 'Yes, that would be him.' Cornell nodded. 'Excellent. Mr. Knight has yet to arrive but I would be happy to seat the two of you, follow me please.' The waitress said, lifting a trio of menus into her arms and tucking them against her chest. 'Certainly.' Cornell said, his eyes following the waitresses every single move as she turned and led the way across the soft red carpet, her high heels leaving slight indentations in its surface. She led us across the large dining area filled with corporate smucks, wearing the standard business uniform of a suit and tie, on occasion there would be a radical, daring enough to go without a tie, one of the younger socialites was bold enough to toss a pair of black vintage adidas sneakers on with a ridiculously expensive suit. The waitress stopped in front of a table set for four, Cornell froze in his tracks and I followed suit. With a quick wave of her hand she indicated for us to have a seat. Cornell dropped into a seat and quickly planted the attache case in the seat next to him. I casually slid into the cushioned chair across from him, my hand brushing the white silken tablecloth as I settled into my chair. 'A waiter will be with you both momentarily.' The lovely blonde said with a smile as she turned away from our table. She walked away, her steps quick, her manner brisk as the eyes of Cornell followed her, undressing her in his mind. He licked his lips as she vanished from sight. 'So, Josh...' The lawyer started before I cut him off. 'Do you see my dad around here?' I asked, raising an eyebrow. 'No...I guess not...' The lawyer replied a puzzled look crossing his face. 'Thats my dad's name, my name is Josh. Cool?' I shot an icy stare in his direction. 'Cool.' He answered, diverting his eyes from mine. 'Cool.' I smiled broadly at him, showing him that things were 'cool' now. Much to his relief a waiter stepped up to the table, a pencil and notepad in his hands, ready for action. The waiter was dressed like every other stuck up, self-serving jackass in the restaurant. Same black suit, same white shirt, same red tie, same leather shoes and even the same damn cheesy ass slicked back hair. The only difference between him and the rest of the normies was the nametag dangling from the left side of his jacket, the bold black letters resting on a white background, proudly proclaimed the twenty-something year old to be known as: 'Chandler'. Even the name made him sound like a pompous ass. 'Can I start you gentlemen off with something to drink?' Chandler asked, opening his mouth and proving that not only did he look like a pompous ass, but he sounded like one as well, with his high-pitched whine. 'Yes, I'll have a Sam Adams.' Cornell said with a pleasant smile. 'Water, bottled water. Aquafina if you have it.' I nodded, not even bothering to look at the waiter. 'Of course, is there anything else I can get you, or are you ready to order yet?' He asked, trying to sound as polite as possible and failing miserably. 'No thanks, we're waiting for someone.' Cornell answered. 'I'll be right back with your drinks.' The waiter responded with a quick nod, turning on his heel as he walked away. As the waiter walked away Cornell turned his attention back to me. 'Donovan hasn't really talked to me about the details of exactly how you could use my assistance. I'm really wondering, what brings us here?' 'A kid.' I responded, before adding further information. 'My kid.' 'Custody issues?' Cornell asked. 'Exactly.' I nodded. Cornell opened his mouth again to speak but stopped, closed his mouth and stood to his feet, his eyes drifting away from the table. He opened his mouth again, a smile spreading over it as he spoke. 'Donovan!' I swiveled around, my eyes locked on a wily bastard in a mustard yellow suit with black shoes and a black shirt, missing a tie, the top two buttons undone, and black shades covering his eyes, spikes of blonde hair shooting up a clean inch and a half. 'Colt.' Donovan said with a nod as he approached the table. 'Josh!' Donovan had more enthusiasm in his voice as he clapped his left hand down on my shoulder and shook Cornell's hand with his right. Donovan and Cornell both sat down, Donovan to my left, Cornell still seated across from me. 'Took your sweet time getting here.' Cornell teased. 'You know me, fashionably late! Nothings says cool like being fashionably late everywhere!' Donovan chuckled at his own joke, he was the only one to do so. 'Are we ready to get down to business, D?' I asked, glancing over at Donovan. 'Business? Hell, we can't talk business without a drink, who do I have to sleep with to get some alcohol around this place?' Donovan grinned, his eyes sweeping the room. 'The waiter should be coming in a mome...' Cornell stopped mid-sentence as the waiter, Chandler, stepped up next to our table, sitting a bottle of water in front of me and a full bottle of Sam Adams next to Cornell, planting a glass next to it. 'Are we ready to order gentlemen?' Chandler asked, in a voice that sounded as if it had never escaped the puberty stage. 'Not quite.' Cornell answered before nodding towards Donovan. 'My friend would like a drink.' On cue, Donovan quickly spoke up. 'That Sam Adams looks absolutely spectacular, I'll have one of those, thank you.' 'Absolutely. One Sam Adams coming up.' Chandler answered as he whisked away to his duties. 'So, is there anything good to eat around this joint?' Donovan asked, raising his eyebrows over his shades. 'We haven't even had a chance to crack open the menu yet, but I...' Cornell opened his mouth and I shut it for him. 'I'm sorry, is this a date or a business meeting?' I snapped, glaring at Cornell. 'In a hurry as always my good friend.' Donovan scolded with a click of his tongue. 'Sorry if I'm rude but I didn't come here to compare suits with the other social sluts or to spend entirely too much for a meal, I came here to talk business, now lets talk some business, damn it.' I spat out with a scowl, twisting the top off of my bottle of water and gulping down a mouthful of the icy liquid. 'Okay, okay, business it is.' Donovan said with a chuckle, as Cornell shot a questioning look at him before popping open his attache case and extracting a notepad, a pen and tiny albeit ridiculously expensive PDA. 'Before Donovan decided to grace us with his presence we were actually about to hop into the custody battle.' Cornell stated, the confidence in his voice shining through. 'The custody battle isn't going to be a fun one.' Donovan said, his eyes lighting up as our waiter, Chandler, arrived with his drink. 'Are we ready to order yet gentlemen?' Chandler asked. 'Not yet.' Cornell answered shaking his head as he picked up the pen with his left hand. 'Take your time.' Chandler said with a phony smile as he turned and walked away from our table. 'So what makes this custody battle so difficult?' Cornell asked. 'Ex-wife?' I shook my head, glancing down at the table to hide the shame in my eyes. 'It was another man's wife.' 'But a DNA test would prove you're the father, am I correct?' Cornell posed as he began to write on the notepad in front of him. 'Yes.' I said, adding a slow nod for emphasis. 'Okay, then the only issue I can see is that the courts would be afraid to remove the child from a stable family environment.' Cornell said thoughtfully. 'No...' I shook my head. 'No?' Cornell echoed with a raised right eyebrow. 'The family is dead, they've been dead for several months now.' I mumbled softly, taking a sip of my water as I kept my head down. 'I see...' Cornell said slowly. 'Who has custody at this time?' 'The grandparents.' My voice low and weak. 'Have you been in contact with the child since the death of the parents?' Cornell asked, taking notes. 'I've tried but the grandfather has refused any type of communication.' I sighed, raising a hand to my face. 'Did you see the child often, before the death of the family?' Cornell inquired his pen moving quickly across the paper. 'Rarely.' I answered. 'I'm based out of Seattle, live with my best friend in New York, spend most of my time working in Vegas or around the globe, Juarez doesn't fit into my travel plans very often.' 'Excuse me...where?' Cornell asked, blinking. 'Juarez.' I replied. 'Juarez...as in...Juarez, Mexico?' Cornell asked in surprise. 'Yeah, why?' I asked the lawyer, surprised at his reaction. 'Wow, this situation just got a little stickier, Josh.' Cornell shook his head. 'What gives, bro?' Asked Donovan Knight, knocking back a hard chug from his Sam Adams. 'Look, everyone knows that the courts in Mexico are crooked.' Cornell said with a frown. 'Donovan said you were the best...thats why you're here.' I responded. 'Are the grandparents Mexican?' Cornell asked. 'Yes.' I answered quickly. 'The mother?' He asked, his pen flying furiously across the notepad in front of him. 'Yes, also Mexican.' I said with a nod. 'This just doesn't look good, Josh.' Cornell shook his head. 'The courts are going to be biased. You're a gringo that knocked up a married Mexican woman, and even worse, your an American citizen trying to gain custody of a Mexican citizen.' 'Not to make the bad look even worse, but you also have a very hectic travel schedule and a record of erratic behavior and suicide attempts.' Donovan interjected. 'He's right Josh.' Cornell said with an honest sigh. 'The odds are stacked against you.' 'To hell with the odds!' I snapped, slamming my fist down on the table. 'I've spent my entire life living against all odds. The odds said that a two hundred pound kid would never be World Champion...and the odds were wrong, because I became the greatest World Champion in Global history. The odds said I couldn't make it to the top and the odds were wrong.' 'I know Josh, but this is different.' Donovan said in a gentle tone. 'This time you don't control your fate...the court does.' 'Yeah?' I asked, suddenly feeling something rise within me. 'The court may have the final say but to hell with letting them decide, lets make up their minds for them. Cornell, Donovan said that you were the best, and I expect you to prove that. I want you to change their minds, I don't care how crooked the court is...we can play their game and beat them at it.' Cornell shrugged. 'I'll do what I can Josh, but this one is going to be tough.' 'Please...' I stared him straight in the eyes, he held my gaze for a moment before looking away. 'Donovan and I will do what we can.' Cornell nodded. 'Thank y...' I was cut off in midsentence by the ringing of my cell phone from its station inside the left side pocket of my track jacket. I pulled the phone out and flipped it open, recognizing the ring tone as that of Ken Partridge Jr.' 'Yeah?' I said into the phone. 'Josher, when does your plane leave?' Ken asked. 'Damn it...I need to get to the airport, thanks for reminding me. It should be leaving in about two hours.' I answered. 'Look, you cannot be late for this...its going to be a long flight to Tokyo.' Ken warned me. 'Don't worry, I'm leaving now.' I promised as I hit the 'end' button on my phone and terminated our conversation. 'Damn, do you really have to leave?' Donovan asked with a scowl, as he glanced at his watch. 'Yeah, I need to get to the airport, but look, you have my number, right?' I asked, looking straight at Donovan as I stood to my feet, taking my bottle of water in my left hand. 'Yes, of course, and I gave it to Colt as well.' Donovan replied. 'Good.' I nodded. 'Don't hesitate to call if you need anything...more answers, additional information, either of you.' 'I'll be in touch.' Cornell said with a smile. 'Good, now I really have to get on that plane if I want to make this show.' I said, forcing a fake smile as I walked away. 'Have a good trip.' Cornell said to my back. 'Bring me back a souvenir from my Japan!' Donovan called after me. That was the last thing I heard them say as I walked out the door. In a matter of hours I would be on my way to a showdown. ...a showdown in Tokyo. ---- Donovan Knight. 'So, what do you think?' I asked Colt with a grin. 'The kid is a star, you can tell that.' Cornell answered in his greasy little attorney way. 'He's my money-maker.' I said, my grin growing even further. 'Whats the deal, Donovan?' Cornell asked with a hint of suspicion in his eyes. 'I'm sorry, deal?' I asked, playing along. 'You've got an angle playing somewhere, I can tell.' Cornell said, his eyes narrowing as he peered at me. 'You wouldn't tell the kid I'm the best unless you wanted him to believe that, and hell, we both know that I'm not the best...at least not with custody cases.' I smiled, he had already caught onto my little game before I told him. 'I'm playing the kid.' 'Oh...?' Colt asked with an intrigued smile. 'The kid is my cash-cow, and his damn offspring needs to stay the hell where its at, which is off of the public radar. Right now, I'm raking in an absolutely insane amount of money off of Youth and its because of his mass market appeal. Throw a kid into the picture and he loses that appeal. Suddenly all of the teenie boppers go find another pretty face to fawn over, the kids don't want to be like him. They want an irresponsible role model, one that tells them to live hard, fast and the hell with everyone else.' A grin spread across my face. 'Right now, 'American Youth' Josh is promoting the image that lets me live the way I want to live. I want to keep it that way and I need your help to do so.' 'You want me to lose the custody battle?' Cornell asked, a look of shock on his face. My grin spread so far that I squinted beneath my shades. 'Exactly.' Colton paused, a blank look on his face. The blank look slowly turned into a grin equal to my own, he spoke behind the grin. 'I'm your man.' I raised my drink, and he raised his at the same moment. 'Heres to the kid.' We toasted with a quick clink of the bottles. Taking a long drink I couldn't resist another toast. 'And heres to the money he'll make us.' ----
