Tyler Chambers interviews Deacon Holmes

25 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/25/03 ***Scene fades in on an obviously aggravated Tyler Chambers standing out on the curb in front of a Holiday Inn Resort on a particularly sunny day. Dressed in traditional tourist garb which consists of a Hawaiian shirt, khaki shorts, and some leather sandals, he fans himself furiously with his notepad. The quiet of the afternoon is suddenly broken by a ground shaking boom followed closely by several others. Chambers turns to see a navy blue stretched Lincoln Navigator complete withe chrome trim, dark tinted windows, a custom grill reading “Deacon Break 2003”, and twenty four inch rims, pulling into the parking lot. “Still Ballin” can be heard throughout the air now as if it were thunder. As the monstrous vehicle makes it’s way into lot on a bee line for Chambers picking up speed the whole way. A nervous Chambers fans himself more furiously now than ever as the the Lincoln stops dead in it’s tracks no more than a foot away from him. He stands in half awe half fear for a number of seconds before the driver pops up from his leather seat and flashes a mouth full of gold before ushering Chambers to the left side of the vehicle. The driver then opens the door to reveal Deacon Holmes wearing a pair of black AND1 basketball shorts with some flip flops. He is surrounded by an entourage of beautiful women, most of whom are scantily clad. They look to be in the middle of an exhibition match as Holmes is being dogpiled but doesn’t seem to mind at all. “D” is heard from the driver. Holmes suddenly sits straight up showing a neck full of multicolored beads and causing more than a few of the girls to tumble to the floor*** Deacon Holmes: Oh, we’re here already. Get in. ***Chambers squeezes in between two of the girls to find a seat directly across from Holmes*** Chambers: You mind telling me what all that was about? Deacon Holmes: What what was all about? Chambers: Well first of all you’re late, and on top of that you damn near killed me. Deacon Holmes: Let’s be serious now Ty, who looks more like they’d have the answer to that question? Me or the guy you saw in the penguin suit? Yeah you know, the one that was sitting in the driver’s seat. But more importantly, how big of a rush do you think I could have possibly been in? ***Chambers looks around slowly sizing each girl up before returning his eyes to Holmes*** Chambers: Good point, but what was that little impromptu game of chicken all about!” Your driver almost killed me! Deacon Holmes: Oh CW? He wasn’t gonna hit you. He hasn’t hit anything since I told him I’d fire his ass if he hit one more thing after he hit this boy with a limp. Insurance is a real pain but you can’t beat good help. We made it here from Panama City in two hours. Tyler Chambers: Wasn’t girls gone wild up there for spring break? Deacon Holmes: Who cares, I was there! Tyler Chambers: Ok, moving on. What was the reason behind your run in during Evolution. Deacon Holmes: Oh that, well I was backstage watching the show and something didn’t seem right. The excitement wasn’t there ya know? So I figured I’d spice things up. The show just needed a little bit of D, a dab’ll do ya! I couldn’t have picked a better time either, that had to be one of the most boring matches I’ve ever seen. I hope those clowns appreciate what I did for them. Tyler Chambers: You DDTed Johnny Cruse! Deacon Holmes: Yes, it was the most exciting moment of the night and I allowed him to be involved in it. What a jerk though, I’m still waiting on a thank you letter. Some people just have no class ya know? Tyler Chambers: I guess...but a thank you letter? Deacon Holmes: Well some token of his appreciation. That DDT was one of the high points of his career. I mean think about it, he not only got to be in the same ring as me, he had the pleasure of receiving the best damn DDT around. Did you see the angle on that thing?! It was perfect! Tyler Chambers: Yeah it was! If only you could’ve seen the way his face smashed into the... wait what am I saying!” Nevermind, moving on, next week you’ve got a match with David West. Do you have any comments on that? Deacon Holmes: Yeah, why? That clown couldn’t even beat Faith Slayer. Wait, is it that clown or those clowns? Tyler Chambers: Well actually it’s... Deacon Holmes: Aw who cares, he’s quite the opposite of important. The only thing making him worth wasting my time on is what he might do after I publicly embarrass him. I mean if he lays on the ground in soiled rags and drinks himself in and out of hangovers because of Brick and Faith Slayer I can’t wait to see what he does after I beat a new personality into him, hopefully smart enough to tell him to never step into the ring with me. David West, what a joke! Even if he does get rid of those voices in his head in time for our match they’ll just be replaced by the sound of his head crashing into the mat! Tyler Chambers: Well you certainly sound confident. Deacon Holmes: Very! Ya’ll may not know who I am but come Monday you’ll all find out what I am, THE BEST! ***Suddenly everything in the vehicle shifts the the left. It would seem that the Navitgator is up on two wheels. After being put back down on all four wheels the vehicle jerks to and extremely rough stop sending everybody up towards the front. The side door then opens and CW is standing there holding it open. The entrance to “Club Harem” can be seen at the end of a crushed velvet carpet that begins at the door. The girls all file out of the Navigator one after another followed by Deacon Holmes has been visibly angered by CW’s driving technique*** Deacon Holmes: CW! What did I tell tou!” You hittin tha corners too hard again! Am I gonna have to replace you with uncle Leroy!” CW: ... Deacon Holmes: Now take him back to his hotel room. Tyler Chambers: But I wanted to... Deacon Holmes: You wanted to do what? Tag along? Don’t get it twisted, I was on semicongenial terms with you because you were interviewing me. We are not, I repeat, are not, friends, comrades, allies, or any other term that you may use to describe someone on your side, you ingrate. It’s not enough you get a free trip down here to interview me, but you gotta try and ride my coattails too!” Get a life. ***Holmes slams the door on Chambers and makes his way ladies in arm toward the door of the cheesy looking strip club***
