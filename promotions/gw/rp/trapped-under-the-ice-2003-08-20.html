Trapped Under The Ice

20 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/20/03 The scene opens, inside of Ice's hotel room. The room is dark except for the dim green light radiating from his small cd player, which is sitting on the nightstand next to his bed. Ice is seen lying on top of all the covers, with his hands gently folded behind his head, staring up at the plain white ceiling above him. I removes his right hand from behind his head and rolls over onto his left side. He reaches over and hits a button on the cd player, and rolls back over, placing his right hand back behind his head. Breaking the silence the music crashes into the darkness and engulfs the room with it's sound. Ice closes his eyes, and the camera sits, watches, and listens.
