Strategy

3 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-3-03 Business has been a bit slow at the Dark Horse Saloon since Bubba went into a coma, and morale amongst the staff has been at a low. Currently, Bubba's son Donovan McCoy is working there in his father’s absence. The ordeal has hit the entire family extremely hard. But Donovan took it upon himself to act as the head of the family. There is a large dance floor. Next to it is a big mechanical bull pit. Right now, enjoying the mechanical bull is one Ely Braxton. It’s one of the few pleasures he’s been able to enjoy in the past few days. The third floor of the Dark Horse Saloon is usually for people with reservations. Tonight, it’s serving as a war room. In it are three men, two of which are involved, along with Braxton, in the fight of their lives on the 7th of April: Brick and Jesse Briggs. “Hey, yo man, where the fuck is Ely” We need to get this meeting started!” “The hell do you think he is?” replies Briggs. “He’s down ridin” that damn mechanical bull.” “Well, tell him to bring his ass up here! We got planning to do!” “I thought you were gonna calm down.” The time is fast approaching. The time is coming when these three men (Brick, Briggs and Braxton) will face the Nevermore in a handicap match. Brick is on edge because he has the most to lose. Last Evolution, thanks to interference by the Nevermore, Brick lost the triple threat match for a shot at the International title, thus he was suspended as per Salaok’s stipulation. As a sadistic game, Salaok gave the three men a chance to redeem themselves and remain in Global Wrestling “You hold that beer bottle any tighter and you better show it an engagement ring!” says a voice. Brick looks around but he sees no one. “Down here!” Brick looks down to see Logan Creed. “You do that on purpose, don’t you?” “What?” Brick asks, looking innocent. “You know what!” Creed replies, as he climbs into the seat. “Listen, is Donovan cool with me being here?” “Any reason why he wouldn’t be?” “Well, there was that drama with Christina?” “Hey, if Bubba’s put it in the past, Donovan should be able to. Besides, he knows how much respect you have for Bubba.” “And Christina?” “She understands you were doin” what you thought you had to do. I think she’s forgiven ya.” “Yeah, well, I hope so. I don’t want any more drama than I’m dealing with now. So what’s the deal anyway with her an” Ely?” “Why don’t you ask me yourself?” Brick turns to see Briggs and Braxton. “Finally. Now we can get to the business at hand.” Brick pulls out a manila folder and places it on the table. In it are photos of the Nevermore. Brick reaches for the first photo, which is of Jay Vyper. “Now, first off, there’s Jay Vyper. Now he’s the quiet one, so far. I didn’t see him do much in his last match, so that either means that he ain’t shit or that he might be someone to watch out for.” “I think I can handle him,” says Briggs. “Sylo’s old partner, right?” “Yeah.” Brick then pulls out another photo and puts it on the table. This one is of the Cruiserweight champion Sage Sabbot. “Now, we come to Sabbot. Another little punk joining up with Salaok who thinks he’s gonna take me out. Apparently, Salaok never told him about Derek Draven. Draven thought he was gonna take me out too. Look where he is now.” “Yeah, I’m definitely lookin” forward to kickin? his ass after what he said about us,” says Braxton. “What’d he say about us?” “That we’re weak, that we get what we get for free, that we don’t work for what we get.” Brick rises out of his seat. “That motherfucker said what”! He wouldn’t even be cruiserweight champion if it was for the rest of the Nevermore! Who the fuck is Sage Sabbot to say I don’t work for what I get?! I have worked, sweated, ached and bled for this company, and this little bitch wants to run his mouth about me?! About us?! Who the fuck is he?!” “Easy, Brick,” says Creed. Brick calms down. “OK, so we deal with Vyper and Sabbot last.” “Why last?” “Because these are the cats we need to deal with, first and foremost,” Brick replies, placing a photo of True Evil. “Oh yeah, I’ve been lookin” forward to stompin? a hole in their sorry asses, especially Salaok’s!” says Braxton. “Yeah, me too!” “I don’t think there’s anybody here who doesn’t want to beat their asses. The problem is they’re probably gonna be the hardest to deal with. Now Salaok is a sick, evil genius. There ain’t a low he won’t sink to if it’ll get him what he wants. Fortunately, we have a bit of psychological advantage.” “You mean “cause we’re sane and he’s not?” “In Brick’s case, that’s questionable,” adds Briggs. “Ha ha, funny. No, I mean because Salaok is afraid of me. He wants me out of this company bad. He doesn’t want to have to deal with me. That’s why he screwed me into getting suspended. That’s why he’s trying to keep me and you guys out with this match. But we’re gonna show him different, ain’t we?” “Oh HELL YEAH!” Briggs, Braxton and Creed yell in unison. “Now Quartz might be the X-factor in all this. Jesse, we’ve both been in the ring with him. We know what he’s capable of. Just three of them with Salaok planning would have been enough, but all four of them, especially with Quartz?” Just then, they hear footsteps coming up the stairs. They turn to the stairs to see one of the last individuals they’d expect to see? The World champion Silent Wind. “Don’t worry about Father,” he says. “I will take care of him.”
