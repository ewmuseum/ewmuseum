Wrestle at Wrestlecade: Episode I

22 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22/04/04 Wrestle at Wrestlecade Episode I: A New Hope Que the Star Wars theme.... The Star Wars theme suddenly began to play, breaking through eons and lightyears of time and space... and all that other techno sci-fi bullshit. 'Deep... in the depth's of all that is good and radical, of all that sucks and has to be made unsucky... there lived a man. He had goals, he had objectives... and on Evolution of this past week, he defeated a finger-lickin' reject called J. C. Diamond. The battle was tough, the battle was fierce... and despite everything that had come to pass prior, this hero managed to walk away with the win. Now, days on from this tragic event... we look toward the masterpiece of masterpieces... we look toward the opportunity that could propel this man through time and space to riches that he could have only dreamt of. In a contest that will test the limitations and desires of six men, Rich Sherwin... the Captain of Retirement; will attempt to snatch a shot at the Cruiserweight Championship from all those who dare to oppose him. However... before that contest comes into play, deep down below on a small and pathetic planet called Earth, our Jedi hero trains with Wang Kenobi, a foreign exchange student drafted in from Korea to play Obi-Wan. Will the training of our talentless hero do him well... or will he simply lose. Furthermore, what will happen to J.C?! Nobody really knows...' Slowly but surely, the scene fades in to show Rich Sherwin clad in various dirty rags and robes. He looks the part of a homeless person but we know better than to think he's trying to be a hobo. Instead, we are obviously well aware of the fact that Rich is simply playing the part of a Jedi, desperate to carry his training through to the next level. Standing still in the middle of a desert, the wind brushing past his face, body and legs... he stands, completely silent and completely focused. 'You will... have to... learn the... ways of... skill, powah and control... if you are to stand chance... at victory!' exclaimed a Korean sounding voice, all of it coming from the near distance. Judging by the way that Wang Kenobi was the only Korean employed by Rich, it would most likely be safe to assume that this voice would indeed... belong to him. Broken in english and strung together worse than an amateur band, his words of encouragement do much to the morale of this superstar. 'Yes, I will win... I will battle through action movies, more ghetto's, fire fiends and something that's supposed to be amazing. I will use the power of Ultimate Muscle... and my Jedi training to demolish and destroy all those who wish to prevent me from being the number one contender to the Cruiserweight Championship.' Rich shouted aloud, throwing his fists into the air as the emotion became too much to contain. He looked around his immediate area for his trainer... but unfortunately, found no-one. The voice of the Korean master still lingered in his head and with night beginning to settle, Sherwin made his way away from his spot... looking for anywhere that could be used as a resting place. 'You walk with intensity... you walk with fury... you walk with a fire that is matched by none, a desire only challenged by your love for McDonalds' Double Cheeseburger and Fries. I know of your heart for this match and I know of your status against your opponents. Roz Blayze and Max Roswell have both fallen to you before... as has J. C. Diamond! Your other two opponents don't even know what they're walking in to!' the Korean voice screamed over the air, suggesting that he may be communicating with his student in a way that none of us could actually imagine. 'I walk the walk of a Chojin, a Jedi... I am one that should rise to the occassion, not run away and insult people whilst they're not looking. Besides, that kind of tactic is going to be a lot harder to do when there are another five people to contend with, each and every one of them wanting to win this opportunity also. Wang Kenobi, I'm going to have to walk in this manner if I even want to think about the potential title match following this.' Rich shouted in response, not caring if the Camel-Riders of the seven sands believed him to be one of the psycho's who wander the desert without food, water or mobile shelter. 'All that you must remember... is that I AM your fath...' 'Hey, I thought you said you weren't going to try and pull freaky Darth Vader shit on me. That was totally and completely out of... hey, I feel myself wanting to wake up for some reason...' :: BEEP BEEP BEEP BEEP BEEP BEEP BEEP!!!! :: 'Gah!!' Rich screamed, waking up into what we now know as the real world. The scene, as it was supposed to be outside his dream sequence, was a small but brightly coloured room. The white paint appeared to be more radiant than usual and everything else just appeared to fall into place for one reason or another. Sherwin rose from his bed, a cold sweat forming on his brow. This dream that he'd been having was obviously a lot more meaningful than any normal dream... because if it were normal, Rich would have looked down to see if any accidents had been caused. '... Rich!' screamed a voice from inside the house, sounding exactly like the Korean voice that we'd heard in his dream. There were a few moments of silence before the voice called his name again and then continued, '... I know that you're on something of a vacation now until the Pay-Per-View match but you should really wake up and start your training now.' 'What? Start my training now? You want me to train with you freaky-little Korean people from the time I wake up until... well, the next time I have to wake up?! Get out of my head, foo'!' Rich screamed, obviously still believing that he was still in some kind of twisted and parallel universe. 'What? You screaming toward me? Anyway, I made you breakfast and leave it on the side. I have to work for now but I expect training when I return, yes?' called the voice again, seemingly belonging to a Korean trainer of Rich... whoever that may actually come to be. Answering with a simple, yet secure 'Yes', Rich threw the bed-sheets off of himself and swing his legs off the bed. '... Why do all these people expect me to do nothing more than train and train and train? Don't they understand that I have to like... eat food and be amazingly cool. Miss Addison and the other female beauties aren't going to be able to drool over my sexy body if I'm not winning and fighting. Training might seem like a good idea... but if they don't see me, what's the point?' Rich said to himself, literally throwing himself away from the bed to get up and press forth with the day ahead. Walking out of his apt bedroom and into the very open hallway, Rich looked around to see if his friend and house-mate was still about. Calling out a few times, the future Champion Chojin seemed content with the fact that Wang wasn't around to cause a fuss about the food Rich decided to guzzle down with his breakfast. 'Hehehe...' Rich chuckled, a sly twist being added onto the end for added effect. He soon continued, '... with nobody around to check on what food I decide to eat, I think it's about time I treat myself without being thrown into the dog house immediately afterwards.' Walking down a flight of stairs, Sherwin passed through the living room and into the kitchen... the one place where everything laid. '... Hmm, I think I should eat foods relevent to each of the five competitors involved in this contest. So, for Roz Blayze I'm going to have something Mexican... like, a bag of Taco's! For Max Roswell... I'm going to have some Cola! For Jay Quickness, I'm going to have some KFC!! For Mike Storm, I'm going to have something... amazing, but I don't know what yet... and for J.C Diamond, I'm going to have...' Sherwin stops for a few moments before laughing out loud, '... Pfft, I really don't think I'm going to be having ANY of that. Fortunately, I'm not the kind fella who looks out for sausage meat.' Opening various cupboards and the sort, Rich looks through for any of the food items that he'd suggested. Scrambling through the empty boxes of oatmeal and shit, Sherwin is unable to find anything worthwhile. He keeps looking and looking for these choice items... but cannot find any! Oh how will the Jedi-Master be able to continue if he is unable to get something worth devouring? '... Ergh, I should have known that crafty Korean bastard was going to do something like this. Taking away all the rich foods and leaving me with nothing more than Cow and Rice. What kind of meal is this anyway?' Rich moaned, walking back into the living room where he sat down at a small wooden table and stabbed at the bowl of Cow and Rice with his fork. Slowly stabbing his fork into a piece of cow, Rich pulled it up to his face and shoved the meaty food into his mouth, chewing vigourously to get rid of it as quickly as possible. Like magic, Rich could feel the sensation of the cow meat as it tingled in his mouth... the sauces and the flavourings all combining to create one magical experience. This was more than just a selection of food from the background of each character... this was a NEW hope, this was a NEW goal to achieve. With Sherwin continuing to eat the food as the scene vanished under the curtain of the night, we could only wonder what crackpot events were going to happen next time~! :: END :: Next time...
