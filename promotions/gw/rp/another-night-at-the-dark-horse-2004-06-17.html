Another night at the Dark Horse

17 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/17/04 The mood in the Dark Horse saloon was at an all-time high. People were dancing, drinking, riding the mechanical bull, etc. Then the door swung open and in walked Brick. The crowd cheered ?HEEEY!!!? after he walked in. 'Hey there, darlin',' said Daisy the waitress. 'If you're lookin' for Ely, he's at the bar.' 'I figured as much. Thanks, Daisy.' Brick walked up to the bar where Ely was sitting hunched over. In his right hand was a glass, half filled with Texas mudslide. 'Hey, man.' 'Hey.' 'The usual, Brick?' 'Yeah, sure.' The bartender left, and Brick noticed Ely's rather grim mood. 'What's wrong?' 'Ah, it's Gunner.' 'What, you and Gunner have a fight?' 'Not really. She told me that she ran into Josh.' 'Yeah?' 'Anyway, she told me that she asked him about Ken Partridge.' 'What?' 'Said she was just curious.' 'Why?' 'Hey, you get curious about exes sometimes. I still ask Christina how she's doin'. She may not wanna tell me, but I still ask.' 'I've never really been curious about what my exes might be doing. If we broke up, I no longer feel entitled to know what's going on her life. That's just my policy.' 'I feel like there's somethin' she's not tellin' me.' 'Probably nothing to worry about.' 'That ain't the half of it. You know, I ran into Elizabeth, you know, the receptionist with the desk down at the end of the large great hall.' 'Oh, the brunette,' Brick said, with a slight smile on his face. 'Yeah. Anyway, she told me that he came around, lookin' for Gunner. She told him she was already gone for the day. She mentioned him lookin' real disappointed.' 'I can imagine.' 'I should have figured a long time ago that he wanted to get her back, since Wrestlecade. Anyway, Elizabeth told me that he just left a bouquet at the desk and just went out. She looked at the card and then photocopied it for me.' Ely then reached into his pocket and pulled out a folded piece of paper. 'This is what it said.' He handed the paper to Brick, who then unfolded it and began to read. 'Gun, I realize that I did a lot of things wrong, and I just want to make that up to you now. I love you more than words could ever say and I'm ready to start over new. We can take things as slow as you want, I just just need you back in my life again. Maybe this time you'll stay. Love, Ken' 'Man.' 'I've been hearin' for weeks now that Partridge might want her back. Now I'm afraid she might actually go back to him.' 'What? Man, get outta here!' 'No, I'm serious! I mean, look at him, he's rich, he's handsome...' 'He's an arrogant jackass. He probably wears more mascara that she does. Look, he may be rich and good-looking, but you're a solid cat, ok? I know Gunner wouldn't throw that away.' 'Think so?' 'I know so. Look, let's talk about something else.' 'OK. So, how about Donny an' Alexis?' 'Aw, man, don't get me started!'
