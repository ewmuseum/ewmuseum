Fed Case(Part II) Terrelle Green Global Wrestling Promo #767 Date: 1/17/04 Location: Houston,Texas Houston Police Department As the two officers bring me into the interrogation room I sit back in my seat lighting a cigar. First they send in Detective Morris Lead Detective Narcotics he comes in with a sheet of paper and pen. He scratches his half bald head as he slides the paper to me. 'What is this?' I asked. 'It is so that you understand your rights.' 'I dont understand my rights I needyou to read them to me that is your job.' He snatches the paper back. 'One you have the right to remain silent. Two you have the right to an attourney if you cant afford one, one shall be assigned to you. Three you have the right top trial.... 'Do you have to go through all of that shit?' 'No you can sign here.' He hands me the pen and document. 'Are you suggesting that i sign this piece of paper without my lawyer present?' 'Terrelle this papersimply suggests that I read you your rights and you understand them. There is no need to have a lawyer present for this situation.' 'There is always need for a lawyer when you are dealing with pigs!' He quickly jumps up grabbing me up by my collar while I am still handcuffed and throws me against the wall. 'Listen here you piece of shit you may get away with that shit with these other fucks at this station but, watch that tone or you will have more problems than that.' As he motions to slam me down my lawyer walks inside the room carrying a briefcase and slamming it on the table. 'Detective thats a lawsuit that you will be hearing from my office is that understood?' Cunningham says. 'Yes Sir. Says Detective Morris. 'Now if you would please uncuff my client and we can talk as soon as the others arrive.' 'What others?' Says Morris. 'Oh the arresting officers your supervisor and the Sherrif of course.' As he syas that Sheriff Helm comes walking in the room followed by Harris, Jerry Knowles and other officers that were at the scene. 'Okay down to buisiness.' says Cunningham. 'Today at approximately 7.34p.m your men detective Morris executed a raid in front of the Houston,Texas arena. Am I correct?' 'Yes Sir.' said Morris. 'Okay exactly what was the purpose of this raid?' 'We recieved a tip that there was a drug transaction in where Mr.Greeniso` was involved.' 'Please my clients name is green please reframe from calling him Greeniso`.' 'Well my men executed a raid at the at the fish plant in Dallas,Texas where Terrelle men where found hiding a controlled substance in fish.' 'Exactly how much.' 'Estimated in all fish where 200 kilos.' 'Did you get the lab results?' 'Yes Sheriff Helm has them.' 'Sheriff have you red the results?' 'No I planned to read it when everyone is here.' 'Okay would you now do the honors.' 'Yes the results came back negative it was salt.....' I laughed as everyone in the room headdropped to the ground and Harris left out slamming the door. I stood up as my lawyer made his final argument. 'Me and Terrelle's legal team if filing a Federal Suit for Harrassment,Battery,and a false seach warrant. Which I went over and it has no serial numbers on it so you will hear from my office.' I stood up brushing off my suit putting back on my sunglasses and walking out of the room a free man every officer in the building looking at me. I turn around looking as Sheriff Helm slams down the results. 'Well men we lost him again and it is estimated that he has a 22.5 Million Dollar lawsuitagainst the federal deaprtment.' Said Sheriff Helm. 'But sir it was cocaine I tasted it.' Said the officer. 'I know men but you have to realize that he is a Mobster they have ways of altering everything.' 'So he can get away with everything there is nothing he cant avoid.' 'Give him time he will come to us.' I look at the Sheriff as he closes the door and goes into his office. Then on my way out of the door I se Agent Harris standing outside smoking with a few other agents. I walk out and he looks as my Mercades pulls up and my driver stands openeing the door. 'No hard feelings okay pig. I mean besides we all loose our head on the real things in life our family.' 'I know Terrelle you got away this time but.........' 'Agent please spare me the TV promises that you will get me and you will bring me down and yoda yoda yoda. Because there is nothing to nail me on I walked tonight I will walk whenever you come to get me again. Oh and you might be fired seeing as you have costed your organization millions of dollars in lawsuits. Since that is in mine why dont you go on a vacation like to.......the grave and talk to your old man.' He runs toward me as his agents grab him. 'We wouldnt want another suit now would we detective?' He snatches away from the two men and walk into the building I get in the car and light a cigar. My lawyer gets in the car with me taking off his glasses. 'Luckily we have the Sheriff huh sir?' 'Yes or else I would have been going to jail for a long time George.' 'Naw sir these are the police they always make it possible for us to slip through.' 'You are right.' 'I am a lawyer I am always right sir.' 'Yes you were also right about Harris. Do you think he knows what is about to happen to him George.' 'I dont think.' 'Well he can wait Uncle Greeniso` needs to be taken care of and I know just the way.' 'What?' 'You know it is ashame how he overdosed of his medication at the age of 54 isnt it George.' 'Yes it is. Do you want me to set the funeral arrangements.' 'Yes cremate him.' 'But sir....' 'Do as I say.' I look as he places the call and he signals the hit. 'It is done sir.' 'Good now is my home finished?' 'Yes Sir.' 'Well good take me there so that I can wash myself off for my big day tomorrow.' 'Ah. Yes you have to go to the United Way fund raiser dont you sir?' 'No I have to do that tonight.' 'Oh Whats tomorrow.' 'Everything is on a need to know bases George and right now it is not the need.'
