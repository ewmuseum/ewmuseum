Party Crasher

9 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Evo #1 (8/9/04) It's a good thing he didnt' mind being the uninvited guest. Truth be told Train loved the thought of it. What could be better than somebody who you know spends a good portion of his time talking shit about you... plotting against you... just waiting for your one, brief moment of weakness so they can strike... All of that hate... And they still can't do shit to stop you when you want in. Hell that was better than sex...unless it's sex with that punk's bitch, but that was a whole nother issue. Train stood, leaning his weight against the the waist hight concrete wall that surrounded the roof of the building, staring out at the twinkling lights of the city. The moon was bright and full, hovering steadily over the city as it stretched as far as the eye could see from this building. It was only about 4 stories, no different from the majority of the other buildings in this industrial section of Dallas. Train wasn't sure how long this building had been empty and abandoned... it had probably been a few years from the look of the broken windows and faded out sign. It's not like it mattered though. The only thing he needed to know about the place was the steel fire escape was still sturdy, its metal skeleton creeping it's way up the side of the building with the short metal ladder connecting it to the roof where Train stood. It had taken a couple of weeks worth of scouting to figure out this was the best way to get in. It had been kind of a pain, but it had to be done. He couldn't just walk in through the front door. The first night he'd done enough looking to know security would have been all over him the second he'd shown his face. Technically this was all just wrestling. A little business that wouldn't be handled with anything more than a couple of punches and a suplex or two. But technicalities didn't apply to this. He knew this guys mindset. Knew how far he'd be willing to go to protect his status as a legitimate athlete pulling in cash. Not because he cared about it so much as much as it gave him a legit source of income. Train knew all of it. And not just because Train had the same mindset, though he did... It was more because he had the recordings and negatives to prove it. Both men were on the roster, but this shit went way beyond wrestling. Hell, when McNasty had tried to blackmail Train awhile back, all he had was enough proof to get Train's contract thrown out and Train was willing to end someone's life if need be. Well....the hell with contracts... Train had enough to send this cat on a not so lovely, paid vacation up state for 6 or 7 years. The hell with punches and suplexs... This shit had the potential to end with bullets and bodybags. This was beyond business...this was real. Train drew in a deep flow of the cool night air and turned his attention away from the city, leaning over the wall slightly, down to his Navigator parked in the alley between this building and the one next to it. Train digs into his pocket and retrieves his remote for the luxury trucks. Without having to look Train finds the security button and gives it 2 more pushes. The first push causes the hazard lights of the truck to flash once, meaning the alarm is off. The next push causes them to flash twice, proving to the owner the vehicle is protected. Train couldn't remember a single instance where he hadn't locked the doors on any vehicle he'd ever owne, but, if he was to have a first time, he was determined it wouldnt' be in this neighborhood. Finally he stepped back and turned 180 degrees from the direction he'd been facing...towards the feint pulse of house music that beat in the relatively quiet night. Casually Train, dressed black jeans and boots, his white t-shirt and blue plaid Polo button up shirt hidden underneath the black leather jacket he has buttoned over them, strolls across the darkened roof top, nearlly invisible to anyone who might have been watching. Train reached under the jacket and retrieved one of the few tools he'd brought along to ensure his safety. Train approached the wide ledge at the other side of the roof and ducked down a bit, hiding most of his wide frame behind it and raised the low light binoculars to his face. The world quickly turned from technicolor to green, brightly illuminating the once dark roof tops of the buildings ahead of him. Of course his only interest was in the roof two buildings over. His eyes focused on the building. It was basicly identical to the rest... metal ducting and pipes sticking out... the triangular protusion just big enough to hold the door that is the rooftop access from the inner stairwell. Train let his eyes carefully scan the area, looking for anything he should be concerned about. For a moment Train honestly thought the offer he'd come to make... the type of 'apprenticeship' he wanted to extend was the best thing that could ever happen to the buildings owner. Open roof top access and no guards despite know there were cats out there that wanted to put him in a pine box? It spelled amatuer all the way. Luckily he'd learned to look a little harder. Most would have stopped after the first few scans the of the scene, but Train's compulsion to be thurough helped him spot it... the feint glimmer of something moving on the far end of the rooftop. Train slowly turned the wheels along his eyepiece, adjusting the focus. After a moment he could see it, feint as it was, hidden in a particulary dark spot sitting ring next to one of the air conditioning ducts on the roof... He could see the video camera slowly swiveling back and forth. Train just continued to crouch, watching it's cycle. Figuring out it's timing. The camera's movment played out like a beat in Train's head, drowning out the music coming from his destination. Train quickly tucked the binoculars back in his jacket and moved to the far near front corner of the building. The power in his arms quickly pushes the rest of his mass up onto the wide ledge of the building. The beat plays on as Train steadies himself...waiting for his spot. Finally he has it. Train quickly picks up a head of steam, running parallel to the rooftop next to him... once he has it Train veers to his left, leaping over the darkness seperating the two...7... 8 feet.... Train takes flight and sails the distance and over thin wall, landing flat on his feet, before quickly doding to his left, hiding his wide frame behidn the triangular structure harboring the rooftop doorway of the building. The beat of the surveilance device drones on in Train's head, though the music from the next building cant' help, but compete. Train quickly sticks his head out and takes a quick inventory of the situation. The camera pivots away from him, just like he figured. Train quickly spins around the backside of his hiding spot and squats into a ducking position before charging over to the raised, wide ledged side fo the building, sitting down as soon as he touches the concrete border. It's time for the other tools of the trade. Train reaches for the holster behind his back and pops the snap attatched to the leather strap. There is an almost inaudible scuffle sound as the nickel plating on the Berreta slides free from it's housing. Like second nature Train quickly pushes the small switch on the side of the gun, releasing the clip, which slides out into his leather clad hand. Train checks the contents, making sure everything is in order. He runs his thumb over the bullet at the top of the clip. The rubber tip flexes ever so slightly under pressure. Train hadn't ever used the rubber tipped amunition before, but this situation called for more subtlety than he'd ever needed before. He had to let this guy know he means business, but he can't push the situation to the point that the only logical response he has to keep any kind of face is to put a bullet in Train's skull. Not that he was worried about said bullet. Fact is, if he wanted to he could end this all tonight. He knew they weren't expecting him. Music will be too loud for the witnesses to hear the shots. Even if it wasn't he knew he was covered as he grasped the metal tube and twisted the silencer onto the barrel. He let his finger slide over the slick, cool surface up to the the small gadget hooked onto the under side of the barrel and pushed in the tiny button. The red dot appeared on the ground beside Train where the barrel was pointing. Go time. Train popped his head up above the ledge, his eyes instantly locking onto the target. Train quickly ducked back down as the camera's field of vision passed over him. He counted to 7, figuring that was about the time he needed to be in the clear and raised himself up, his eyes just above the ledge. The beat played on in his head and his count was in sync. Train knew he had one shot to pull this off right. He had to make it count.... Train bolts to his feet. Pfttt, Pfttt The rounds were made of rubber but they seemed to hit like the real thing. The metal panel on the side of the camera warps inward with the first shot. The second pops it off, sending a short series of sparks into the air, accompanied with a small puff of smoke. Train put another shot in after that, the laser guiding the bullet in to the side of the lense, seperating it from the rest of the machine. Train looked at it for a moment, admiring the work ethic of these non lethal rounds he had his boys up north ship to him. He could learn to be a fan. That was enough time admiring. He needed to hurry as he didnt' know how much time he had. If security was worth a shit... he might have 45 seconds to a minute. If they were half assed, he might have a couple. But even if they were the latter he had to assume they were the prior. Train quickly hopped up onto the ledge and got a running start... he lunged putting one foot firmly on the edge of the building and pushed off with it. The power in his legs lifted him up carried him over the, pitch black, 4 story deep crevasse to the next roof. The second his feet touched he could feel the building pulse beneath his feet, the waves of sound rippling from the building to his body. He had arrived. Train tucks the gun into his belt at his hip and reaches into his back pocket as he approaches the roof doorway. He reaches out and grabs hold of the knob, checking for the obvious. The metal inside clatters as the lock slams into the other metal pieces inside the knob. It's nothing Train didn't expect. He produces a small leather bill fold his back pocket and unrolls it. The slinder, metal instruments bound tightly in tiny pockets inside the leather carrier glimmer in what little light there is up here. He scans the contents, looking for the proper instrument to get the job done. But, no sooner does Train's gloved hand pick the pick he needs to eliminate the lock he hears something comeing from inside the building. thump, Thump, THUMP Footsteps. Train tucks the picks in his pockets and sidesteps the door, quickly ducking behind the triangular structure. Train grips the Berretta and pulls it free. Just as he does the footsteps reach their peek, finally stopping, echoing just for a moment in the enclosed doorway. Train can hear the scrubbing of metal on metal as a key slides into a lock from the inside. Click The door squeaks loudly as it opens to the roof...releasing the a strong blast of the music... and allowing access to the inside. Train hears two sets of footsteps and two voices. 'This is some bullshit man,' insists one man, his voice thick and husky. 'This cameras a piece of shit homes! How many times we gonna have to fix this shit before J spends some real money on a new one?!' The man's voice trailed off a little as the first set of footsteps did the same, letting him know the complaing man was first out the door, obviously headed towards the camera. There were no more sparks and smoke, so the dark corner the camera was placed in worked to his advantage...but not for long. 'Just get this shit done man.' stated the next voice, just a tint of bass, helping Train spot his relative youth without even seeing him. The way his voice reverberated also let him know he was standing just inside the doorway. 'Fix that shit so we can get back inside dogg. Shit I left this fine bitch at the bar. I know that ho' wants me son so hurry the fuck up!' Train stepped out, sliding around to the other side of the doorway.... 'Why? You that ready to get another drink thrown in your face?' Train steps to the side, clearing his view of the structure... The man in the lead is heavyset with a bald head... He needs to learn to look behind him... Then again he will in the next few seconds... In a few seconds he'd a look at the camera. 'Whatever man, I....' Train knew this was going to be a short conversation, one way or the other. He decided it would be better if he just butted in. Train stepped forward switching the gun for his right to his left hand... he took a big step to his left and swung... The butt of a gun, butting into a conversation... it was almost poetry. The gun clattered as the force of the impact with the kid's forehead sent Train's hand back forward... The impact sent the kid back. He was a nimble little bastard though... he managed to turn and tuck into a semi ball before tumbling down the stairs. The other guy, Mr. heavyset had already started to cock his head, spotting the damage to the camera. The moment he'd spent trying to determine whether what he was see was really what he was seeing was a good distraction. The combination of the whelp of pain that left the kid's mouth less than a second before the loud succession of thumping sounds rang out just loud enough to snap him out of his daze of confusion. He turned to see what was going on. He didnt' hear anything but the music, but saw the flashes of light... Only the first two though. Train's finger carressed the trigger three times, the red dot guiding the rounds square into the man's chest. The third shot spun Mr. Heavyset and sent him down, his head bouncing off the thin, metal ducting as he went, leaving a noticable dent. Train spun on his heels the second the man hit the ground. He peered down the stairwell, the commotion, from the falling body at least, having expired. The kid had hit the back wall of the last flight of steps and was slumped over, not moving an inch Train held is gun out, pointing down the stairs, his finger still gently stroking the trigger as if it's one true love. He stood for about 20 seconds, occassionaly glancing behind him, confirming the other man was still out cold. Not movment...no sounds of approaching footsteps. He was in. ===== ===== ===== ===== ===== Ten Minutes Later... The dark was calming. Good for the nerves after all that excitement earlier. It was a nice room too. The owner had obviously spent a whole lot of money sound proofing it. Guy seemed to think he was Superman... Untouchable with his own fortress of solitude. Train hoped he wouldn't mind the intrusion into his space away. Well.... Maybe a little. The door, somewhere around 30 feet across the room opens, spilling light and sound into most of the darkness. But not in the little lounge area that had been set up on the back wall of the office a good distance off from the desk that sat facing the door. Train just sat calmly, his body entrenched comfortably in the black velvet couch, his feet propped up on the small, circular inn table that sat in front of it. The slight illumination allowed Train to really see where he was sitting. The couch was flanked by two easy chairs, upholstered in the same crushed linen as the couch. A quick glance above revealed the small, parcan lights hanging from the ceilings. Train could see the colored gels over the lights... Yeah... this was the real VIP section of the club. Train just grinned. He liked this kid more and more. Two shadows appeared in the shaft of light that shot through the door straight to the desk, spilling tiny bits of ambient light over everything else. The two figures, one tall muscular, the other small and undeniably feminine, quickly step into the room. The man strides in first, his arms flailing around in anger as his lips puff on a cigar. His obviously tailored white suit moved well with him even in the rage he seemed to be flying into. The woman strode in slow and calm behind him. She looked on with a sense of concern, but one tinged with amusement as if she thought he was making a mountain out of a molehill. Her determination not to be caught up in his drama was obvious and written over her face in the form a tiny grin. Train sat listening, his body still and unmoving. Anyone who was reeeeeally looking for him might have spotted him, but with the black clothes on the black couch, dark hair and dark skin he might as well have been The Predator with that cloaking device. It was obvious the man had other things on his mind. Train didn't know why. Sounded like the guy needed to stop and smell the roses. See everything he has going for him. Train checked the city records. The man owned this club outright. No mortgage and his liquor liscense was good through 2007. Hell, he might as well have been printing his own money. Not to mention the trafficing and prostitution he had going on throughout the city. The kid was on is way...of course the place he was headed might have been a Triple Max, ADX with 23 hour lockdown if he didn't learn to be a little more fucking careful. The shit was too blatant. Lucky for him the cops... FBI... DEA... they couldn't find their ass with both hands. Give 'em enough time they could get the evidence they needed, but it would take them awhile. Train knew cuz he'd been there and done all that. There were ways around all that. He could teach the kids some tricks of the trade if he was willing to learn. Yeah... the kid had time. He could afford to be a little sloppy, but he needed to cinch it up. Train would help him do that...whether he liked it or not. In the meantime... What's not to love? Nice suits. Nice cars. Good food. Boys to watch your back...no matter how inept. Not to mention the bad ass bitch who walked up in the room after him, the leopard halter top, hugging half her curves, the long black skirt with the long slit up the side accentuating the other half. Yeah, it was obvious. Screaming and yelling wasn't neccessary. 'El Mariachi' Juan Gonzalez had a lot to be thankful for. 'Son of a bitch!' Juan yelled. 'What the hell is going on around here?!' 'Baby, calm down.' Raspberry tossed out calmly, not putting too much into it knowing it would be run over and dismissed. 'What the hell do you mean calm down?!' Juan inquires in irritated fashion, spinning back around to stare her down. 'Do you know what's going out there? Huh?! I got those little bastard in the Latin Riders thinking I dont' know they're shaving off my coke and selling the leftovers for themselves. These God damn cops are asking for bigger payoffs. Those idiots Ramon and Joey still haven't fixed that damn camera yet. And besides aaaaaaaaaaaall that... There's a shot at the tag straps at the pay-per-view on the line and I have to trust that bastard Train! That PUNTO that has the balls to blackmail me!!!' 'Baby, stop!' she demands. Juan just turns and flips on a small lamp on the desk. He turns back to her and motions for her to shut the door, which she quickly does. She turns back to him quickly as he takes another adjitated puff of his cigar and meets him eye to eye. 'You're going to drive yourself crazy! What good is that going to do you?! God... Look, you already send Paco and Chocolate to take care of those little punks who ripped you off. You sent the pictures of those cops' wives and kids to them, right?' 'God damn right.' 'Good. So when they get it they'll know they better be happy with what they got and stop fucking around.' 'Yeah, yeah, yeah... To hell with the cops. What about that bastard Train?! Huh?! What about him? He can blow this whole damn thing out of the water... I still want to know how the hell he got those pictures...and the damn phone taps!' 'Well.... I don't know that one baby... But look, if he wanted to blow us out of the water he would have done it by now, right?' 'Hell if I know.... I don't know what the hell his game is. The son of a bitch wants something. I just don't know what it is.' 'Well, whatever it is, just give it to him and be done with it.' 'My ass.' 'Baby, think about it. He's the Platinum Champion... half the roster is either scared of him or just don't want to mess with him... If you guys could get this thing together think about the damage you could do.' 'Fuck....' Juan sighs, grabbing at his temple. 'This guys giving me a God damn headache.' Juan crosses from the front of the desk and moves around to the side closest to where Train just sits calmly with a smug grin on his face. It's obvious he's trying to get behind the desk, probably pull a bottle of asprin out from one place or another. But Raspberry, a gleam in her eye, intercepts him, pushing him up against the side of the desk. 'What you doin' woman?' Juan asked pointedly. 'Curing your headache you big baby....' Raspberry replies, pushing her womanly body against him, slowly reaching behind him...finally retrieving a small remote control. Without having to look she finds the button she needs... 'Splash Waterfalls' by Ludacris starts to grind itself sensuously through the rooms speakers. 'So shut the fuck up.' The latina tigress reaches up and grabs him, pulling him into a deep kiss. Finally she frees herself, pushing him back into the desk. His hands tracing her she winds her body to the beat, turning her back to him for a while, his face finally starting to register a smile. She turns back to him, he back to Train again. He can't help, but enjoy the show. Train slowly reaches into his jacket's inner pocket and retrieves the tightly packed, chocolate flavored Philly he rolled just for this occasion. Train slides the re-rolled cigar between his lips digs into his jacket pocket again as Raspberry slowly slides down her top, showing off her bare back to him and her more impressive assests to Juan. Train can't do anything but shake his head in a gesture of adoration for the bold young woman. He finally retrieves what he needed from his pocket to call this 'meeting' into order. Raspberry slowly crouches down in front of Juan as he giddily watches her sink. She slowly begins to tug at his belt... The dark, plus a woman crouched down at waist level would be enough to keep almost anyone's attention off the man sitting in the room under cover of near pitch black. That is until the dark goes away. Train's two fingers slide hard off the side of the Zippo's lid, the opposing force popping up and open. The simple flick of the thumb against the jagged roller sparks the flint, which chain reacts with the lighter fluid inside to set the wick of the lighter ablaze. With every distraction in the world in front of him not even Juan could ignore that. 'What the?!?!' Juan exclaims, his head shooting up to gather enough information to answer his own question. Raspberry turns her head and part of her torso around, instinctually using her arm to block the revelation of any of her sensitive areas. Train just takes a long pull of air through the cigar, turning the tip into a collection of glowing, red embers. Juan freezes for a moment, but Raspberry's high pitched scream as she dives to the side, struggling to cover herself from the unexpected audience member brings him back around quickly. Train snaps the lighter closed and drops it back into his pocket before shooting up to his feet, while Juan spins around to the back of the desk, tearing open the top drawer closest to him. Juan reaches into the drawer as his other hand slams down onto the same remote Raspberry had used a moment ago. The second he touches it as series or blue, purple and pink lights flood Train's former hiding spot with a dim, but revealing light. A second after that Juan's chrome plated Desert Eagle glows with the white light of the desk at it's rear and glowing in neon at it's tip. Train was forced to stare the gun down...but Juan had to stare himself, the long silver extension of Train's Berreta reaching out as if to grab him, the whole of it's deadly mechanics glowing blue, pink, and purple. Juan slowly reaches behind him and, this time, gently taps the remote, killing the music. The room is still... nearlly silent.... The two men strare at eachother, the instruments of their wrath locked onto to target while the woman trembles ever so slightly, trying to pull her clothing back into it's proper place...... Tension...... 'Motherfucker.......' Juan says, shaking his head, trying to come to grips with the gall involved in this encounter. 'You got some fucking balls on you homes... I'll give you that.' 'Shoot his ass Juan!' Raspberry yells. 'He ran his eyes all over me like it's ok... Kill that bastard!' 'Hold up baby girl,' Train interrupts, not taking his eyes off Juan as he addresses her. 'You was giving him all that good advice a second ago about how he should cut a deal with me. Don't go fuckin' that up now.' 'How the hell did you get in here?' Juan curiously inquires. 'Your boys on the roof let me in. Well... I shouldn't say they let me in. To be honest with you, when they finally wake up, they probably won't really remember much about it.' 'You're a real son of a bitch, you know that?' 'We all have our flaws.' Train retorts smugly. 'haha... Uhuh....' Juan chuckled. 'So... what the hell is this essai? Huh? What the hell do you want? You wanna turn me in to the cops? You got the proof to do it. You want to tell me I better be on point come Evo? Hell, there's a shot at gold on the line man. Dont' worry abour me holding up my end, you just hold up yours. Or what... you come to watch me and my girl, is that it?' 'Hey.... yall ain't got to stop on my account.' Train says with tiny grin. 'Fuck you punto!' Raspberry spits back, fighting back tears. 'Raspberry, chill.' Juan says plainly. 'So... Give me one good reason I shouldn't waste you right now.' 'I'll give you two...' Train responds. 'One... If I go?' he asks just before letting his finger off the trigger long enough to press the button on the gun's laser sight, causing a tiny red dot to materialize at the center of Juan's chest. 'You're coming with me. Two.... I didn't come to hurt you. I came to help.' 'You came to help me? Is that so?' 'That's right.' 'Why the hell would I believe you?' 'You said it yourself. If I wanted to fuck you, you'd be fucked by now. But here we are.' Train and Juan stand, still poised to destroy eachother, but the tension has shifted slightly...not so much filled with Malice as much as curiousity now. 'Why?' Juan asks after a few moments of silence. 'Why, what?' Train repsonds with a question of his own. 'Why is it you wanna help me?' 'Cause... I look at you and see myself a few years back. Scraping around... Just trying to come up in the world. I know how you can do it. Just looking to help a brotha out like I wish someone would've helped me.' 'He's lying Juan.' Raspberry says, jumping in. 'He broke in here and pulled a gun on you!' 'I told you to chill, girl!' 'Naw, naw....' Train says, cutting him off. 'She's right... That's not a good way to start a meeting.' Slow and steady, Train turnds his pistol up towards the cieling, pointing the broadside in Juans direction. He slowly sinks back into the couch, placing the pistol on the table in front of him. He calmly settles in and grabs hold of the blunt, pulling a hefty helping of the pungent smoke into his lungs. Juan's gun stays trained on him, waiting for an excuse to explode and wipe away the problem in front of it. But it stays silent.... 'Now, he ain't got no gun,' Raspberry quickly observes. 'Shoot him!' 'God damn, I said chill woman!!!' Juan yells. 'Go out and check on Ramon and Joey, make sure they're alright. Lock the door to the roof back. Close the door on your way out.' 'What?! Juan, he...' 'NOW!!!' His ferocity and volume, take her off guard, startling her, having Juan's intended effect or reminding her who's in charge here. A look of anger, mixed with utter mortification wipes over her as she quickly turns and exits, slamming the door behind her. Juan slowly lowers the gun and waks across the open space over to the office lounge. His eyes never leaving Train, Juan reaches over and pulls one of the plush chairs over until it sits directly across from the man casually enjoying a blunt. Juan sits down in the chair, observing that the barrel of Train's gun is pointing directly at him. Juan responds by slowly placing his gun parallel to the other, the barrel of the .50 caliber piston pointing at Train. Train just responds by taking in another plume of smoke before pulling it from his lips and leaning over with an outstretched arm, offering up the cigar to be shared by his new companion in the VIP lounge. Juan looks at him a second...finally taking the cigar and taking in his own smoke. Juan holds it in for a few moments, before releasing it as he speaks in a graveled voice... 'So....' Juan begins. 'What do you wanna talk about?' 'What I always want to talk.' Train says. 'Business.'
