Wrongly Accused Part 2

29 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-29-03 Harry and Johnny stood in front of the phone a police officer standing watch near by. ?How strange was that. I know I saw that guy in there but there cameras were off? Johnny be careful something is not right here I just have one of those feelings. ? Harry said as he looked over his shoulder. “Look Harry I need to get out of here. I think Valire is in real legitimate danger. I thought if I left the wrestling world. I could get away from this.” Johnny said as he listened to the phone on the other line say once more in a computerized voice. “Were sorry the costumer of this cell phone is out of the area, Please try your call again.” Johnny sighs with frustration and tries a different number. ?Damn it Jay where are you!? Johnny says more frustrated then before. He turns and looks at Harry. “Look do what you can get me the hell out of here.” Johnny says. Harry nods and walks off you can here him ask to speak to the chief of police. Finally a ring at the latest number Johnny dilled.. ?Allo! This be the one and only BJP! So speak to me Mate!? Comes the heavily accented voice of Ben J. Pailthorpe, barely audible over the loud music in the background. ?Ben! It is Me Johnny!? Johnny screams. ?Turn the music down!? ?Sure thing Mate?. Okay who is this again?? BJP asks. “It is me Ben.. Johnny.” Johnny says a little aggravated. ?Cruse!! Ohh my bloody god! You are alive!? BJP exclaims. “Barely Ben. Barely. I am in Jail.” Says Johnny ?Jail! What you doin in jail mate. ? Ben asks. “Look Ben. IT is a long story.” Says Johnny. “Ohh Good I like stories. Hold on let me get a beer.” Says Ben. ? NO! Ben!!! BEN! Focus man. I need to find Jay. This is important!? Screams Johnny “Right-O Mate. No reason to bloody shot in me blooming ear! Jay he is in the mountains. Near his home in Oklahomo.” Ben says “Damn> Look Ben I need you to come and pick me up. We have to go get Jay.” Johnny says. ?Right. Sure thing Mate I am there. ? Umm Where you at?? Ben asks. “Miami Florida.” “Ohh Looks like I am in for a trip! Be there for you as soon as I get from Dallas to Florida Mate.” Ben says and hangs up the phone. Johnny lets out a worried sigh. Hoping Ben stays focused long enough to get him out of jail. The officer takes Cruse back to the small room.. Johnny sits alone for a while. Soon Harry Malfoy walks into the room. “Johnny look. I got to talk to the judge. These guys are running me in circles here. I should have you out soon. But it is going to cost. You have a high bail amount.” Harry says. “What ever. Just get me out of here. Ben is coming with the cash.” Johnny says. ?Good. Now I got the chief to give you a single cell. You get some needed rest. I will be back as soon as I can. ? Harry says as he walks out the door. After a few moments two officers walk in and escort Johnny out. As they do the scene backs up and you see a television set. Inside is the empty room of the police station. ?Make sure this Malfoy character don’t make it to the court house. ? Says a voice. “And then take care of TLM , BJP, Cruse and Reed.” “What about Ace Cash sir.” Says another? “What about Cash. He is harmless.” Says the original voice.
