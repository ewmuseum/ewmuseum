Visiting Angus

12 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/12/03 The cameraman stands and then …makes his way back outside of the larger than average size house. As the camera leaves the house he notices a figure leaning against a tree, staring at the house with a slight smirk on his face. The cameraman begins to walk closer towards the figure leaning up against the tree just outside of the the larger than average sized house Angus the Bull was just seen in. The long blond hair that partially covers the figure's face, along with the long black trench coat, blue jeans, and plain black t-shirt, gives away the identity of the mystery man. The camera moves in just a bit closer , and the man now revealed as Ice. Ice smiles at the cameraman, and nods his head, motioning for him to come over even closer to Ice. The cameraman looks around, and then cautiously beings to walk over towards Ice. Ice raises his left arm, and looks down at his silver ROLEX impatiently. Noticing the look of annoyance on Ice's face, the cameraman picks up his walking pace, and quickly reaches Ice. Ice lowers his left arm, and looks over at the cameraman with slight distain. 'Not a bad place. Didn't think you could afford something like this though. Ah well, I'm not here to talk about houses, or your living quarters, or whatever else. However, I am here, watching you, and listening on to what you have to say.' Ice turns away from the camera for a moment as a few people stop and take pictures. Ice gives them a nice little smirk as their flashes go off, before turning his attention back to the cameraman. 'Now as I was saying, before I was interrupted. Angus, buddy, pal, you know me from a previous encounter, and you caught my eye then, just as you do now. But you see, this is a new place, a new time, and hopefully we can start off with a clean slate. Man to man, I have no beef with you personally. Hell, this visit is strictly business. Nothing else. We aren't friends. We aren't buddies. However I believe we do have something in common. Now as can plainly be seen you are not home. But you know how, and where to reach me. So Angus, when you do get home, give me a call, because I have something very very important to discuss with you. I think you will want to hear what I have to say, before next week.' 'Now because this is the very first cable televisised show the GW will be putting on, with me in it, I feel the need to make this something a little more special, than just Dynamite X versus ICE. And what better way, to show just what you've got, then by looking good on the first show, by guarenteeing that I WILL BEAT DYNAMITE X, with the single most devestating move in this business. THE HANDS DOWN! Now Dynamite, you are one talented superstar, don't get me wrong, but you lack that something that will take you into that next level. And when you find that something, then you will be that force to be reckoned with that you proclaim you are right now. But until then, BOY, don't run your mouth about people having egos, or not being as good as they say they are, or about how good you are.' Ice pauses and takes in a long, long breath, before exhaling it out, and begining to speak once again. His face continues to stay calm, and almost unemotional as he speaks. 'Speaking of egos? Tyler Pierce. What planet are you from? Nevermind that, no need to get into the planet URANUS, from which you hail. However, you have felt the need to put yourself on such a high pedastool, that I hope someone like me doesn't kick that stool out from underneath you, and laugh as you go crashing down. Because, you can keep living your lie. You can keep living in your fantasy. You can keep telling yourself that you are a legend. But eventually reality is going to catch up with you, and slap you across the face. And when you wake up, you will look into that mirror, deep into your soul, and realize you are just like everyone else. Nothing special, no one important, just another nobody, walking this Earth. You bitch and moan because you don't receive the match that you want. Well shut up and just show up and do what you get paid to do. Obviously someone thinks you deserve to be in the match you are in now, not the match you wanted to be in.' Ice shifts his weight from the tree he had been leaning on the entire time, and stands up straight. As he does so, he begins to straighten out his long black trench coat. Ice turns to walk away, but stops, and begins to speak with his back towards the camera. 'Look into these eyes, and you will see there is no fear, there is not a single drop of doubt. The cold blood that runs through this body, is the reason I have no equal . With that said, if there is anyone who thinks they have what it takes to shed this blood, step on up, all comers are welcomed. Because starting this Friday, The GW, will lose it's trademark colors, and the cold look of the ice blue that will soon take over, will rise up. Starting this Friday, BRICK by BRICK I will slowly begin to tear down the foundation this company has built itself upon. And I will continue this act until the GW officially becomes known as the house that ICE built.' Ice walks away. As the cameraman stands and watches him leave, the scene fades to black.
