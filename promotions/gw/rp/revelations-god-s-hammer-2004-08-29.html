Revelations...God's Hammer

29 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/29/2004 A bar. Nothing fancy about this place. The joint is fairly empty, only as couple of patrons sit here and there’They either nurse beers or they stare deep into their glasses. One man looks as though he doesn’t quite fit in. Pendragon sits alone in a corner booth, a full bottle and an empty bottle sit before him, along with a shot glass. With a glassy eyed look around the place, he pours himself a shot of Appleton’s Rum seven year blend. With a quick flick of his wrist, he knocks it back with barely a grimace. Leaning on the table for support, he motions for the bartender to come over. The bartender, a slight man with a nervous look to his bespectacled eyes, slowly makes his way to the massive, and massively drunk Pendragon? “Ummm”.What can I do for you?” At a glare from Pendragon he adds “My Lord King?” The whole time the man dry washes his hands and his mouth hitches in a nervous tick. Pendragon pushes the bottle at the fellow? “Aye?” With a slight slur to his speech, he is a bit hard to understand’Heck, mix archaic jargon with rum and see what YOU get?“This be a decent blend of Rum, but I need something a wee might stronger’Bring me thy most potent of liquors’I believe mine coin is still good here?” Swaying, he leans over and reaches into a pocket of his breeches. He pulls a gold coin of uneven mill out and sets it on the table. The bartender snatches it up quickly and smiles, before running to the back to return with a bottle of Everclear. Grain alcohol. Pendragon looks at the label, barely comprehending the One Hundred Ninety Proof writ upon it, and pours a tall shot. He raises the glass in a bit of a toast to the barkeep and knocks it down. His eyes widen a bit, the equivalent of jumping up and saying “Hot DAMN!” were he a normal man. With a nod, he motions the bartender away and pours himself another. After a couple more shots, he begins to look around at the other patrons in the bar. With a smile, he leans back, relaxed, in his lonesome booth. “Hey there”..Yeah?.I speak to thee?.Nothing better to do at two in the afternoon than ingest thy poison? Got no job?””” Pendragon directs his question at a man who sits across the bar. The man ignores him, and Pendragon smiles even larger. He pushes himself to his feet and stumbles across the bar to the unassuming fellow, Everclear in hand. As he reaches him, he claps him on the back and upends the bottle in a rather largish swig. ??Tis no matter. Who be I to speak so at thee? Look at me’What am I? King? Warrior? Bah?” He slides onto a barstool next to the fellow, who now looks like he is ready to bolt?“I was a warrior and a king. I guess I still am a king in name, yet, what kind of warrior have I proven myself to be” I lost mine sword, and slowly but surely, mine power has been ebbing. Soon, I might be as thee are. Insignificant in strength and in deed alike?” Pendragon takes another drink and frowns as the man finally looks up from his beer and sees just who is sitting next to him?“Say, buddy. Aintchoo that wrestler type feller from GW?” Pendragon doesn’t answer, but his sigh and the slumping of his shoulders shows his ascent. The man continues on, now that he realizes who Pendragon is?“I thought you were the real deal! I thought you were the Lord King and all. What have you done” You haven’t even won a belt yet! I used to watch that damned Evolution ever Monday. Least, till I lost my job when the plant closed. Damned Bush, I tell ya. Maybe you oughtta be Thy Lord President and keep the jobs from goin overseas. Course, with the skill you’ve shown in the ring, I don’t think I wantchoo runnin the show in Washington either’What happened to you?” Pendragon takes another drink and stares at the man with the blue eyes that have stricken many a man numb with fear. There is no insanity brewing there this moment though’Only a weary type of acceptance?“I know not the reason. At first, I was a man afire as I fought with glory and with honor, Excalibur at mine side. Things changed when foolish men took mine sword. Long has it been since I held the blade, and it seems that as the blade has left me, so too has mine skill and strength. It seems that the sword was greater than the man after all, and that the man drew his strength from the sword. Now, my victories are meaningless, and when they come tis by dumb luck rather than my superior combativeness”.CURSE THEE, AKUMA!!!” He yells the last before upending the bottle once more. With a sigh, he retreats to the safety of his booth once more, staggering across the bar under the contemptuous gaze of the former fan. Suddenly a thought strikes him, and he turns towards the barkeep? “Why is there no music in thy common room” No entertainment! No stories, no flutes, no bitterns, no harps?.Not even a juggler. Blast, these people need to be entertained’Mayhaps a singer will do!” He exclaims as he clears his throat. He looks as though he thinks for a second before launching into some lyrics in a rich baritone that would be nice were he on key, and were the words more clear.. “Home, no more home to me’Whither must I wander” Hunger my driver, I go where I must! Cold blows the winter winds, over hill and heather! Quick drives the rain, and mine roof is in, the dust?.” “WHY DONTCHA WANDER ON BACK TO YOUR BOOTH! Damned FOOL!!” Shouts the fellow at the bar. Pendragon looks around and sees all eyes staring at him. Some hold amusement, yet many hold pity. Pendragon looks mortified for about one second. The he smiles, takes another drink and heads back to the booth. He flops down and shakes his head, a chuckle escaping his lips. As he takes another drink, a huge thunderclap and a flash of light whitens the shot for a moment. As he blinks away the glare, he notices a man sitting with him at his booth. Clad in a white suit, the man’s look is that of a disappointed father of a two year old. An older man, he has his white hair parted simply, and he wears a close cropped white beard. He seems like a kindly sort, but one never knows’Pendragon rubs his eyes, but the man remains, looking at him. “And who might thee be, to have taken a seat with the Lord King?” Pendragon titles himself with a sound that is close to contempt, and the man in White shakes his head? “You really don’t know” You don’t know me, Venneman? We have had some times together, you and I. You provided some interesting entertainment?” Pendragon laughs?“Oh that” “Twas nothing’Just a bit of a traverling song?” He hiccups and smiles at the fellow, but the smile doesn’t really reach his eyes? “Not that?” The old man sighs. He passes a hand in front of Pendragon and the larger man convulses with the images he is sent? *********FLASH************* Pendragon, saving a small child, before being hit by a large truck?.Darkness?. *********FLASH************* Pendragon, chained in the depths of the nether world, smiling at the weakened links that bind him. With a shrug, the links pop? *********FLASH************* Pendragon, clad in white, pushing into what appears to be a white office, where the man in White sits? *********FLASH************* Countless lives flow by. In some, Pendragon is a king of the Land, and his reign is of peace. His father was not overthrown by Akumancha, and all was right’Now Pendragon lies on the ground, surrounded by Fangspawn, who close in slowly as his breathing becomes more shallow?..Here, Pendragon does battle with a dark version of himself, wielding a dark Excalibur’Now, Pendragon drinking himself into oblivion in a dive?. *********FLASH************* *********FLASH************* *********FLASH************* *********FLASH************* *********FLASH************* *********FLASH************* *********FLASH************* Pendragon struggles up from the countless visions of himself...One hundred times and more he watches himself die, and one hndred times he watches as he fails, triumphs, rules and lives happily. Blinding white burns his eyes, sears his soul and his brain...Finally, he puts his hands in front of his face?“No more, I beg of thee’No more?” The man smiles a kindly smile?“I had to let you know who you are dealing with”.” “So, then thee art God?” Pendragon looks at the man in awe, but the fellow shakes his head? “Some call me such, but I am really just the creator of you people. I created you so that you would create other things. Sometimes you really amuse me, other times, you are quite boring. You though’You are special. You are worth watching?” “Me” But I am a failure. Without mine sword, I am nothing’Never was’Creator?” He adds the last and bows his head, and the other man laughs?. “Please’No need to be so formal. Call me Bob. You are important yet if only you would see. You relied to heavily on your blade. It isn’t that gave you strength. I gave you that. It is inside, where only you can get it. You are singular, Venneman. You have the power to move mountains and to shape entire worlds, if only you have the right motivation. A king by birth and by right and deed. You are a throwback, and that is why it is you I call on now.” Pendragon looks puzzled?“What kind of god are you” Why would you need me?” Bob gives Pendragon a look that says “I realize this is hard to understand” as he explains. “I am not the god of your bible. I am the god of my world. I created it because I felt like it. If I wished, I could destroy it, but to what end” I do not destroy what I create. Would be a sacrilege. If I influence things to strongly here, I take something away from what I made. I would offset the balance between good and evil’Chaos would be the answering action?.” “Stop’If you created, why is there evil” Did you create that too?” Pendragon eyes him with wonder, and looks abashed that he asked the question. Bob laughs and shakes his head? “No, I did not create evil. Man did. And evil took on personification that now fights me for mine most wonderful creation. The devil as you would call him, was created by the blackness that crept into some men’s hearts. He touches the world, but he knows not the error of his ways. He throws the balance out of whack because he wishes to have domain over the world. I wish to protect what is mine, and that is where you come in. You are to be my hammer. I set you as my arm upon the earth?” “And what of GW?” “The perfect stage. Evil abounds there. Demons, vampires, hell spawn. You are there for a reason. You can show the world the power of the White. The power of good will be spread to all corners by your exploits in GW. You will root evil from all corners of the federation”.Destroy the bloodsuckers and the resurrected’The murderers. All shall fall by mine hammer.” Pendragon’s eyes grow wider with each word. “And what of mine blade?” “Still you question” Very well. Your first task is to recover the dark half of Excalibur.” With a flick of his wrist, a parchment appears on the table?“This map will show you the way. The sword is strong, and darkness is always the strongest when used upon those of it’s own kind. This blade glows black, as the night, and will cut away thine enemies like so many cobwebs hanging in your path. Get the sword, and take your first title. Those are your charges”.I know you will not fail. I set the hammer loose?”” Another flash and the man is gone, as if he never were. Pendragon looks around the bar and notices nothing strange about the patrons. It is almost as if they did not see?“Did thee see a man?” “Only you over there talkin to yerself, ye great lummox!” is the reply. Pendragon frowns and looks down at the table and sees the parchment. Writ on the back is this?. Venneman? Do you expect me to give myself away to everyone? I like my privacy! Now destroy the Mexican and get your blade’Stop yer dawdling! Bob
