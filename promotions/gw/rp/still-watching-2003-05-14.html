Still watching....

14 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 14, 2003 Sometimes a man's heart is where his home is. For Kullypso, there is no such thing as a home; unless someone calls the Shadows his home. The scene opens with Kullypso creeping through the darkness of the shadows barely seen by the trained human eye. Every once in a while, one can see the glimmer coming from the mail of Kullypso's jacket. Walking with an intention, Kullypso briskly walks down the dimly lit alleys of the city. Finally coming to a point where light must see his devillish grin once again. Walking the streets for a while, Kullypso finally notices a sign that looks very familiar to the Harvester of Pain, the street to what he used to call 'home.' Turning down the street, Kullypso goes back into his shadows once again not being seen by the traveler's of the night. After walking for what seemed forever to a human being and not a creature of the shadows, Kullypso finally comes up to two houses. One being a nice looking mansion, nice kept up-to-shape. A house that 'love' can be felt. The next being one that is very familiar to him. This one is of gothic architecture, stone placed all around the house with several gargoyles on top of the house seeming to protect from inthruders of the night. Mark walks up to the gothic house first. Peering through one of the windows, he can see two children one of decent height running after a smaller child. The older child catches the smaller one like a lion pouncing on his prey. Sounds of laughter can be heard coming from the younger child as the older child is on top of him tickling him. A slight smile comes across the face of Kullypso. A type of emotion that usually does not come across his face without some evil intention behind the motive. But this time is different, no evil intention is planned thought of. These two children remind him of happier times when Kullypso could be a father to them. Now he is just a mere image barely left pressed into his children's thoughts. Changing to a different window, Mark finally lays eyes upon his one time love, Eliza. ELiza was no stranger to the world of the shadows but after the children she did not want to live the life that her husband still leads. TO this day Kullypso still waits for his mistress Syn to come back into his arms and help rule the octagon once again. A noise can be heard from the two children from the other room, instead of laughter now turns to cries. Eliza jumps from the comfortable couch her and Mark once shared countless nights while he was injured gazing into the fire talking about memories that they once shared a long time ago. As Eliza leaves the room, Mark decides to leave the life he could never share again. Mark follows the treelines down through the yard and travels over to the next house. This house also has someone Kullypso once called a friend. Now a bitter rival, jealous of Kullypso's success and vows to thwart the evil he claims Kullypso possessess. As Kullypso slowly inches his way to the house. A car can be seen pulling into the driveway and pulling into a now opened garage. THe car is familiar, a Spyder, and now an evil grin comes across the face of the Harvester of Pain. One only with intentions of evil behind the grin. Kullypso passes by window just in time as the light in that room is turned on. Inside the room is none other than Derek Frost, Kullypso's former best friend now turned bitter rival, and Derek's beautiful wife and the reason for Frost's life, Cindy. As Frost and Cindy take a seat on their couch and turn on the television to watch, Kullypso's cell phone begins to vibrate on his right hip. Mark detachs the phone from the buckle and lays it against his ear. 'Yeah? Did you get the job done?' Kullypso quietly says while underneath the window of his rival and his wife. On the phone is a fellow Harvester that Kullypso trusts more than the normal person. To Kullypso, this Harvester seems as though he and Kullypso had similar lives at one time. A deep voice can be heard of the phone and with the response given to Kullypso another evil grin comes across his face. 'Good...He will never know who had done such a thing to someone he cares about. It just shows it doesn't pay to care for the person in my sights. Right Frost?...' With that last comment Kullypso backs slowly away from the window glaring at Cindy who is in the arms of Frost as like nothing could happen to her. Slowly disappearing into the shadows once again, Kullypso is heard saying something. 'I'm still watching...' End of the scene.
