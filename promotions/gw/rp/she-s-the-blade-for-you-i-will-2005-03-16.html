She's The Blade: For You I Will

16 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 16, 2005 He was completely abashed after the loss to Mindy. He couldn’t even believe what had transpired himself. Mindy Mercer of all people! He could have and should have made short work of the irksome wench. If only he hadn't promised Lisa that he would lose the contest in an effort to prove his fidelity to his new damsel. He made the promise after he slipped and asked Lisa to be his. He had been caught in the moment as they made love and declared his affection for her. 'Prove it then,' she had said. 'How can I prove it to you?' He had asked. 'Lose to that half-witted girl that you're trying to win a date with,' she had stated with a cold tone. 'But my title?' 'If you truly want to be with me you'll prove it by losing to her and putting an end to this silliness,' she had said in interruption. 'Fine,' he had said in dejection, 'for you I will.' After he had gave his oath there was nothing he could do about it, he hadn’t even wanted to promise such a thing but there was something about Lisa that made him powerless. The Television Championship that he had worked so hard to obtain would no longer be his; it would be in the hands of an ignoble woman that had no business to even be considered for contention of such an honor. Naturally he couldn’t go to William with his dilemma. William was already furious that Mike was wasting his time in the lowest ranks of Global and he’d never understand why Mike would agree to purposely lose a match. His only solace was that he had been entered into the Platinum Series and would now have the chance to lay claim to the dormant Platinum Championship and breath new life into the division. He thought of all this as he lay in his bed. He had come home right after High Octane had finished; a deviation from his usual routine of going out on the town after a show was over. He hadn’t felt like celebrating after the match, his ego was far too bruised from the events of High Octane. Thankfully no one would ever find out that he had intentionally lost a match or that he had allowed a woman to boss him around. ***** Lisa’s angelic face was highlighted by a smile as she walked up the front steps of her new lover’s home. He had fulfilled his first promise to her and she wanted to celebrate. She wore a long white fur coat that covered a small red negligee; her brown hair was flawless in its styling, her makeup perfect, and her nails freshly done. As she came to a stop at the door she reached into her small black leather purse and retrieved her compact. She opened the small mirror and fussed over her appearance one final time before she decided that she looked perfect and seductive. She put the compact away and straightened her fur coat before she pressed her finger to the doorbell. She tapped her right foot against the concrete step impatiently as she waited for someone to answer the door. A few moments passed and she became more restless. This time she reached over and pushed the doorbell button three times in a row followed by a light knock. A few seconds passed and she heard a muffled “I’m coming!” accompanied by footsteps. The door swung open and the person that stood on the other side caused Lisa to gasp with surprise. The woman that answered the door was hardly dressed; all she wore was a skimpy French maid outfit that left nothing to the imagination. At first Lisa thought that she had come to the wrong house. “Is this Mike Biddle’s home?” She asked. “Of course it is,” the maid replied with a thick accent, “I’ll go get him for you.” “Wait a minute,” Lisa said with an aggravated tone as she held out her finger, “let me get this straight’this is Mike’s house?” The ill-dressed Frenchwoman nodded her head. “Then what the hell are you doing here?” Lisa asked with a cross look. “I’m his maid,” the girl replied, “I clean things for him.” Lisa’s hands balled up into fists and a fire raged in her eyes. “What things?” She asked through clenched teeth. “Whatever he asks me too,” the maid replied, “do you want me to get him or not?” It was then that Lisa literally exploded. ***** Mike’s thoughts were interrupted by the sound of an argument, the sound emanated from downstairs. With a concerned look on his face Mike rose from his bed and covered his half nude body with a red silk robe before he rushed downstairs to see what the commotion was. What he found when he made it to the foyer was Lisa locked into a confrontation with his maid, Belle. Lisa immediately noticed Mike’s presence and turned her angry glare from Belle to him. “Who the hell is this whore?” she growled. Mike gulped. “She’s my maid,” he replied, “not a whore.” “Then why is she dressed like that?” Lisa asked with a finger of accusation pointed at Belle’s saucy French maid outfit. “It is my uniform,” Belle answered with a respectful tone. “You might as well be naked!” Lisa exclaimed with frustration. Belle took a moment to translate in her head what Lisa had said to her; unfortunately she misunderstood what was said. “Very well then,” Belle calmly said as she started to remove her clothes. Time slowed down as Belle unbuttoned the front of her dress and her bare breasts popped out from under the fabric. She began to pull the dress down and Mike could literally feel the heat of Lisa’s anger roast his skin as time dragged on. He decided that the only way to fix the situation was to take action. “NO!” Mike finally yelled as he rushed towards Belle and forced her to put her clothes back on. Belle didn’t struggle; she complied with the wishes of her employer. Mike gave a sigh of relief and wiped the sweat from his brow as he thought that he had the situation diffused. What he didn’t notice was that there were tears that streamed down Lisa? face. Lisa watched the scene in horror before she could finally muster a shriek. Mike’s shoulder’s drooped when he realized that this was far from over. “Belle,” he sighed, “you’re dismissed for the day.” Belle walked out the front door with a confused look on her face, her absence left Mike all alone with Lisa as she cried. The situation left Mike confused; normally when a beautiful woman began to cry he’d kick her out of his home but this time he was strangely compelled to clutch her in his arms and gently tell her that everything would be fine. “I knew this was a mistake,” Lisa said just loud enough for Mike to hear. “What?” Mike asked with a bit of anger. Lisa looked up at him and wiped the tears from her eyes. “I knew that it was a mistake to believe you,” she said with a frown. “Don’t give me that shit,” Mike began, “I lost that match like I told you I would.” “And then I come over here and I find out that you have a stripper for a maid! Not only that but you’re in your robe’what the hell were you two doing?” Lisa demanded to know. “First of all she’s not a stripper,” Mike said through clenched teeth, “secondly I was asleep while she was cleaning the house’we weren’t doing anything.” “Then what the hell was she doing taking off her clothes?” “I don’t know,” Mike said, “she probably misunderstood what you said. English isn’t her first language.” Suddenly a shocked look came over Lisa’s face. “Oh my God,” she exclaimed, “you are sleeping with her!” “What?” Mike exclaimed with a confused look on his face. “No! Where the hell are you getting that from?” “I can just tell,” she said, “the look in your eye while she was taking off her clothes’it’was the same way you looked at me when?” she trailed off. Mike clammed up inside and he made a mental note to wear sunglasses more often so that she couldn’t read his eyes. He placed his hands on her shoulders and looked her in the eye. “If it makes you feel any better I’ll fire her first thing tomorrow morning.” He said with a pained voice, he didn’t want to give up Belle but it was better than the alternative. “Really, you would do that?” Lisa said with a relieved smile. “For you I will,” Mike said. He began to wonder how many times he was going to say those words to her before it was all said and done. “I’m sorry that I accused you of all that stuff,” Lisa said as she nuzzled up to Mike. He held her tighter and kissed her forehead. “It’s okay,” he said, “it was nothing more than a small misunderstanding.” “And I know just how to make it up to you,” Lisa said as she wiggle free of his arms and shrugged her coat off to reveal her negligee. As they shared a passionate kiss Mike couldn’t help but wonder what else he’d have to give up being with this woman. He hated to make changes to his life but for some reason he gladly did such things for this woman. The power she held over him had begun to confuse him. ***** The next day, bright and early, Mike waited for Belle on the front steps. He hoped that she would show soon that way he could fire her and then head to Hawaii for Evolution. He wore a red Hawaiian shirt and khaki shorts, hardly the right attire to fire someone in but he didn’t feel like dressing up as Donald Trump for this. He wanted the experience to be quick and painless; he was partially attached to Belle especially after some long nights spent with her. As the sun began to peak over the horizon Belle finally walked up the front steps of the mansion. She wore the same skimpy maid outfit that she normally wore for work. When she saw that Mike was waiting for her a smile formed on her face, the last time he waited for her like this he had been drunk and she was given the day off to attend to his “needs”. “Took you long enough,” Mike said with a sigh as Belle approached him. “Do you need me to clean your special place?” Belle asked with a broad smile. “No,” Mike said as he looked at the ground. He couldn’t look her in the eyes. “I need to tell you something.” “What?” “Belle,” he said as he looked for the courage to continue, “you’re fired.” Belle’s smile suddenly melted to a confused look. “What do you mean?” He looked up at her and he felt sorry for her. “I mean that you can’t work here anymore,” he stammered, “I no longer require your services.” She began to cry. “Where am I supposed to go?” she wailed, “I have no other job.” Mike took a deep breath; he hadn’t expected it to go this way. He thought she’d simply leave and he wouldn’t have to worry about dealing with something like this. He watched her cry and while her tears didn’t affect him he realized that it would be quite bad form if one of the neighbors saw the scene. That was when an idea suddenly came to him. “I know where you can go Belle,” he gently said. She wiped the tears from her eyes and looked at him. “You mean I can stay?” “No,” he said, “but I know a place where you can go for a job.” He reached into his pocket and produced a small address book. He thumbed through the pages until he found the address of his best friend, James. He tore out the page and handed it to Belle. “Go to that address and just tell him that I sent you,” he said, “I know for a fact that he’ll take you in.” Belle looked at the paper and turned to leave. As she walked away from the only life she knew in the United States he looked over her shoulder at what she was leaving behind and whimpered softly as she continued on her way. Once she disappeared around the corner Mike sighed with relief. Now he could leave for Evolution and turn his attention to more important matters; like advancing in the Platinum Series.
