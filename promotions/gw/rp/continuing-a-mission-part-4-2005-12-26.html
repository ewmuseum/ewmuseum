Continuing a mission part 4

26 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-26-2005 In the light of the shadows there is always a small voice that comes about. A voice that only few hear. It comes from deep inside your soul, a place that only very few can venture. You must have passion? You must have heart? And most of all? You must have rage? For me, I’ve always had it all. Passion for my family, passion for my life, and passion for the sport that I’ve always wanted to be in. Heart. For the simple things that make me click. For the love that I lost. And for the father and mother that never wanted me to do this. And for rage. The one thing that I never hard till I met him. The inter being that came into my life and tore open the box that was hidden deep in me. Rage? Yeah I got rage? And you will find out how much. ------------------------------------------------ Thursday the 24th Thanksgiving Day? “Lock down?” The screams and cries of the thousands of men and woman inside their cells. Their arms, covering in locks and chains grasping on to the little life that they have left. Dreaming of what was and hoping that what is left of their little pathetic and miserable lives will come back to them. “I SAID LOCK DOWN!” The hot air was swallowing away. Pressing down and out the little cracks that had been impaled in the walls. But that was nothing to the silence that was coming over the inmates. The casual cry’s for help that was coming from the inter rage that they were putting out. The darkness that was rolling in through the windows came over what was left of the security officers that rome down the halls. “I want watch on this hallway in thirty minutes intervals.” The head officer for the night says. “Yes sir.” The very young and gullible kid replies. Standing there with his eyes locking on to the comic book that he had brought from his home. Captain America! Yes the superhero that everyone wants to be like. His eyes were becoming as wide open as the door down the hallway. Oh he would never know I was coming. My mysterious wonder that I was bringing was just like the fog that was covering the outside grass. Slow and methodical, my feet took step after step into the cold hallway of death. “Mommy?” One of the inmates screams out. “Hello Mrs. Dracula?” Another whispers out into the open air. The na’ve officer raises up from his reading to look at the screen that was sitting in front of him. Several hands grabbing for the outside of their drama sized cells. Grasping for freedom and for whatever they could get. Slowly getting up from his chair, the officer began to walk down the dark passageway. Surveying the hands that were coming along, watching from the dark hidden corner, out of sight from even the crawling rats that came by. “Anyone there?” Asking out with his query voice. “Help me?” Screams one of the inmates. “Get back in bed NOW!” Replying the officer. It was my chance. Slowly down the wall behind the security officer, keeping as low to the ground as possible. Faith was what I was going for. My time for viciousness was here. Reaching up behind him, grasping his head and pulling back. “WHAT THE?” Screams the officer. Holding on to his mouth, covering it with a rag that is soaked with sleeping chemicals. His breath was drowning just like his knees. Trying to fight off what he could not see. Tossing his arms around in the air, fading off into a deep sleep. Laying him down, letting his limp body collapse on to the fresh waxed floor. Turning towards inmates that were screaming. I wasn’t here for this man. No, he was just a victim in the way. No. I was here for him. The other being. The other side of this mystery. And the other side that knew were it was. Continuing down the hallway, towards a man who is the starting point to what I need. Step after step, crossing the white lines that stand before me, and going through the darkness to my destination. Coming up to the door, the only one without a hand to show for it, I could see the very name on it. The rage, the sudden sensation that was coming from it. Grabbing the door handle, twisting it to the right, and pulling the door wide open. “Hello Elia?” Huh? How did he know. Pausing at the door, looking through the pitch black that was in between us. He knew, he knew why I was here. “Johnny Crisp.” Replying to his creepy voice. “You return. So what do I owe the pleasure?” Responding as I close the door behind me. “You know why I’m here.” Walking towards him. “I need to know where it is.” “Where what is?” Replying as he lays still in his bed. Tall. About ten feet tall to be exact. Black to the eyes and about three hundred years old. The story in the family is this. It was used to drive the demon that I now carry. The very same demon that has taken over my body, mind, and everything else. In the deepest darkest days, it was the center of everything. It’s power was used to help him take over every little mind he could but there is a problem. He can only take over the body for a short time. He needs it to finish the transformation and fully take over the body for good. Looking down at Johnny, feeling the fear that he was trying to hold back from me. Feeling the sweat that was beginning to pour down from the top of his head like Niagara falls. His breath, beginning to pick up as he slowly sat up from the bed. “The cross of Vinaca. The one that your family took from me when my power was raging on. I want to know where it is and you are the only living person that knows.” Feeling the rage in my heart boiling up. My eyes began to turn. Turning from the dark blue that they were always, to a blasting red. “You’ll never find it. It’s long gone from anywhere you’ve been.” Looking deep towards me, feeling his body trying to resist me. “Never?”” Walking through the dark that had taken over the room. I could hear screams coming from the outside. Screams from the inmates that were still trying to get out. “Never say never Mr. Crisp because I can get my information from the man that hides inside you. What, you didn’t think I knew?” “He’s dead. He’s dead from your hands.” Pressing up against the wall behind him. Getting right up to his face, pressing against his face, taking over the center of the room. His breath, hot, almost scorching my own face. “Mr. Crisp, he isn’t the one dead. He’s the one that I am talking to right now?”, pausing for a moment. “On the other hand, it is you that is the dead one.” “Huh?” “Yes Mr. Crisp. I didn’t come to you for an answer, I came to see him and see him I will even if that means killing you.” Grabbing his chest with both hands. “ERIC!” The shocking sound of the voice from outside came in to my ear. Making me twist towards the entrance of the doorway. “Our time is up here.” Grasping the shirt of Mr. Crisp and pulling him towards me. “Now we must go.” Taking the rage from inside my body, boiling over into my arm and slapping him right across the face. His life went dead, drowning him to sleep so that I could take him over my shoulder. Lifting up off the bed and going for the doorway before seeing the officers standing right there. Sundering into the darkness that was there and going out the door I came before the officer began to turn around. Walking to the open door, his eyes began to peel open as wide as they could. “Sound the button?”, screaming to the others. “We’ve got a huge problem.” To Be Continued??At GTT5!
