After Max Carnage

18 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/18/03 Needless to say, it has been a wearying night for Brick. He has just been eliminated from the Global Eliminator match for the World title. It was his first World title match since he lost it to Deacon Holmes weeks before. He finds himself taking yet another loss. This time, he was the third participant to be eliminated from the match. “Can’t believe I let that bitch beat me!” he snarls to himself. It was Ice who eliminated Brick with a sharpshooter. Suffice it to say, Brick is disappointed for two reasons. One: he wanted to reclaim the glory he had worked four years to achieve. Two: he wanted to bring the World title back to Global Wrestling. One thing Brick definitely didn’t want was for the World title to remain in the hands of the Upper Echelon. TUE was running roughshod throughout Global Wrestling, and tonight they had grown by three new members: Ice and the Souls of the Black Lotus. Of course, there were two things that Brick could take comfort in: that he wasn’t the first to be eliminated and that Deacon Holmes is no longer champion. Suddenly, Brick can hear footsteps closely approaching him. Quickly, he spins around and cocks his fist. He sees a stage hand behind him. The stage hand flinches and screams “AAH!” “Man, don’t sneak up on me like that! I’m havin” a bad night!” “Dude, sorry. Just thought you might want to hear who won the match.” “Who?” “It was Ice.” “What”!” “He just pinned Akodo Rokku.” Brick then leans forward towards the stage hand. “Tell me you’re not serious!” “I-I’m serious! I wouldn’t joke about something like this, especially not with you!” This news makes Brick even less happy. Even if the belt isn’t being held by Deacon Holmes, it’s still in the hands of TUE. And this is not a good thing, as far as Brick is concerned. Suddenly, Ely Braxton comes racing past Brick and the stage hand, brushing against them. “Hey yo, Ely, wait up, man!” Brick rushes after the frantic Ely. “Ely, slow down!” “Can’t slow down, Brick! Gotta see Christina!” “Why” What’s up?” “Heard she was askin” for Samantha Vale. I know somethin’s up and I aim to see what!” This is nothing new to Brick. He has seen Ely like this before. The McCoys are very near and dear to his heart, particularly Christina. Recently, rumors have circulated about her relationship with Jin Akiyara. But, right now, that doesn’t matter to him. Brick and Ely enter the office to see Christina sitting in her chair, sobbing, her hands covering her face. “Darlin?”” “E-Ely?” “What happened?” Brick asks. “This,” she replies, pressing “Play”. After a few moments. Footage of a middle-aged man beaten and bruised is shown. The man looks into the camera and reveals himself to be Donovan McCoy. Donovan: You aren’t getting shit you bitches! Donovan reaches for the camera when a large black night stick comes from nowhere to the back of his head, sending him to a heap in the concrete floor. The scene flickers and then goes black; a voice can be heard although it has been digitally altered Voice: Your punishment has begun, McCoy’s. Immediately, Ely presses Stop and goes over to Christina, holding her in his arms. She rests her head against his shoulder and cries into it. “It’s alright, darlin”.” “You know who sent it?” Brick asks. “No.” The wheels in Brick’s head then begin to turn. “I think I do. Take care of her, Ely.” Brick then leaves the office and heads for his dressing room. “Fuckin” TUE,” he snarls.
