Fizzle and die? More like sizzle and fry old man!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sunday I know you got yo eyes on me I feel you watchin me but it ain’t hard to see that you can’t see me, ya try, but what you think you saw ain’t what you thought you saw, you better off not lookin at all... Fizzle and die? The two words used by none other than Benjamin J. Painintheass to describe my career. Now if those words hadn’t come from someone on the verge of senility they might just bother me, but in this case I find them amusing. A slight chuckle escapes my lips as I attempt to push my triple black Porsche GT to it’s limits on my way to Texas. Could he be serious? He actually thinks he has a chance. I can’t help but think of his entrance music though. Dinosaurs will die, now that’s an appropriate title for the old kodger, and at Wrestlecade he will share the same fate. Eradication at the hands of a superior species. Me. I can’t help but smile, something I don’t particularly enjoy doing, and shake my head as I consider how many actual dinosaurs BJP has actually lived to see. Probably even rode a few... The fake sound of progress, who was he kidding? He probably couldn’t hear much of anything without his hearing aid plugged in, and if he did the only thing he would hear is opportunity kicking my door down. If I’m seventy percent ego and only thirty percent everything else what must he be? One hundred percent dilapidation and exasperated dismay? The only shot he’s got at beating me is if I’m overcome by the stench of mothballs and preparation h that haunt him wherever he goes. At Wrestlecade I will crush his hopes of a triumphant comeback and prove to him that he is just an old man clamoring for a last taste of glory when I leave him face down and dentures shattered along with all the other fodder vying for a shot at Sage. Pailinthorpe has never seen anyone claim to be progress and live up to their words, but that’s just it, he’d never seen anything like me before... I find myself now laughing hysterically as I mash the pedal to the floor. He predicts that he will win the shot at Sabbot. What a joke, from what I hear he can’t even win on bingo night at his retirement home. His predictions are about as flawed as his face. All his memories of fighting for his money will return when he steps into the ring with me but so will those of bleeding, crying, and defeat. At Wrestlecade a dinosaur will die. Benjamin J. Pailinthorpe. I am nothing more than a black flash to those sharing the road with me as I am now doing a yard over the limit and still find myself nowhere near top speed. This ride has been a long and tiring one without CW driving, but at least I’m not in the air. I have finally crossed into Texas, only a few more hours now... Two more clowns enter my thoughts Roz Blayze and Steve Kincade. A family man and yet another washed up old man snatching at my heels in a failed attempt to regain the mediocrity they enjoyed in their youth. Both of these men face off against me in the first round of the Primestar tournament yet neither realizes the fate that awaits them. If Blayze’s wife is as smart as she is bloated and unattractive she’ll take out some extra insurance policies on him. Daddy may not be coming home to the shack after Wrestlecade. Most likely a slip and fall in the shower will shatter both old man Kincade’s hip and hopes of a Wrestlecade victory, such is the plight of our nations old people. But what do I care, I’m young. Roz Blayze and “The Inferno” Steve Kincade, two men that will fall in a flash, The Final Flash. I can see it now, Holmes kills Blayze and Kincade, film at eleven. As I approach the offramp I notice the new GW billboard featuring me. “Deacon Holmes, burnin” you ass like an STD since 1983.” I should’ve done a better pose... While waiting at an abnormally long red light the grin that has been plastered to my face for the entire final stretch of this trip begins to fade. Leviathan, the only man in GW that has managed to beat me. The anticipation of meeting him in the ring again grows stronger every day and at this point my blood is almost boiling. What happened in that match haunts me everyday and nothing short of pinning him in the middle of the ring 1, 2, 3 will stop it. No matter who I beat, no matter how many bones I break, no matter how much blood I spill, and no matter how many times I head Deacon Holmes wins what happened to me at the hands of Leviathan still plagues my thoughts. I’ve been chasing him long enough and now at Wrestlecade with the Primestar title on the line I will finally have a chance to pin him in the ring 1... 2... 3. We’ve both got triple threat matches ahead of us and I’ve got a cruiserweight mass genocide to perform but I can feel it in my bones that I will meet him in the Octagon once again, and this time, I’ll win. I’ll do whatever it takes, but I’ll win, and that’s all that matters... The sun is now barely peaking over the horizon as I pull into GW headquarters to sign all the legal nonsense that will allow me to permanently end the careers of so many so called stars in one night. Roz Blayze, Steve Kincade, Akio Hakura, Ace Cash, Gunner Addison, Mike Storm, Max Roswell, Ben J. Pailinthorpe had all better say their prayers. Hopefully I’ll get a chance to make Leviathan scream his out in agony...
