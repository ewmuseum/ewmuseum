Loose Ends Unraveling: Part II: Madness

31 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/31/04 ---- ??another taste of the evil I breed will level you completely? I tell you, that David Draiman is a fucking genius? and truer words have never been written? at least in my eyes. Evil. Spelled backwards, it was live? and that’s what I was doing? living it? reveling in the power that flowed through my veins? letting the control slip through my fingers, unnoticed then, as the blade played upon my darkest desires, making them reality. I knew what I wanted? and he knew just how to get it. Suffice to say, I was simply putty in the hands of a master sculptor? So, how had I allowed myself to become so involved in this masquerade? How had I lost sight of the end goal? How had I allowed myself to fall into this trap?” ---- Flashback? Saturday, October 23, 2004 0333 hours, local time In the dim light of the starry sky? under the poor illumination of the leering face of the full moon, we see a white picket fence, gleaming like the bleached bones of a forgotten era. Boards are broken, sticking up awkwardly like smashed teeth, the mud clinging in places like clots of long dried gore. Nailed to the fence, its paint peeling, is a worn sign. Something about this place being state property and that trespassing is a federal crime. But then, to be prosecuted, you’d have to be caught, right? Moving silently, like a cat across carpet, he crosses the muddy lawn, his boots, remarkably, leaving no impression behind in the soupy mud. He passes a worn stone, marking the name of this barren place. Ridge Home. There’s an air of disuse about this place, the buildings have seen better days. There’s a tingle in the air, almost as though a faint electric current pulses in the still night. Graffiti adorns the wooden structures; splatters of paint from paint ball guns? nothing recent? the feeling of abandonment is heavy. The wood is bleached, bare of paint in places. He crosses to the closest building, shivering slightly, not from the cold? but from a quickening feeling. This place is filled with power. The blade is warm against his back, the metal seeming to quiver, responding to the afterimages that few mortals registered. A momentary flash changes the scene to a negative? black to white? white to black, and the buildings seem to whorl and writhe with some unspeakable evil. A ghostly hand reaches out towards Gabriel’s face, caressing his cold cheek, riffling his veil of golden hair, and then the proper image snaps back, and the night is as it was? preternaturally silent. A broken door sways slightly although the night is still, hanging askew on only one hinge, and somehow, the image invokes a heavy feeling of wrongness. With a smile, he reached out with a gloved hand, grasping the icy metal handle. He recoils as though burned, looking down as his palm, yet finding nothing there, the leather unblemished. But the door continues to sway. A feeling of dread overcame him and he backed away, crossing to the next building, where a large broken window affords entry. He felt the prickle on his neck, the hairs that rose of their own accord, the electric feeling growing stronger. His heart began to race, his palms sweating inside their thin leather gloves. He slipped over the windowsill, displacing brittle glass. The silence envelopes him as he reaches into his coat, pulling out a large Maglite that does little to dispel the heavy shadows. Outside, dogs begin to bark, sounding distant... their voices warped? sounding utterly alien? and ghastly. This room is filled with a jumble of papers, spilling from warped and battered filing cabinets. Yellowed with age? open files and scattered medical documents. A faded black & white photo rests on the floor, spattered with bird shit. Some of the papers have names? dates on them. A patient file, torn and water-swelled, a scratch of handwriting. He peers at it in the gloom. A date: April 13, 1992. A scrap of readable handwriting? notes of some long forgotten head-doctor. ??patient displays a marked personality defect? antisocial and schizoidal tendencies?” He chuckles? that could aptly describe himself? hell, it was almost a prerequisite for anyone involved in the CID. Genius, madness? the two went hand in hand. He drops the file and it slides to the floor soundlessly, garbage among the refuse. Suddenly, a flutter of wings is heard, overwhelming, deafening. He raises his hands to shelter his face, but there’s nothing there. Odd. He dismisses the phenomena and continues though the room, out into the hallway, the wooden floor creaking under his weight. He plays the flashlight over the broken plaster. Words here and there, not really readable in this state of decay? and then something that is. “Suffer the little children to come unto me” pain and suffering in me?” Blood red whorls, and a whole handprint mar the off white of the wall. Dents and scratches, long scratches dig furrows in the heavy plaster, hastily painted over, but blatantly visible in the shadows cast by the powerful flashlight. Human fingers had made these marks? perhaps human heads bashing against the wall had made the large dents. What sort of suffering had taken place in these walls? He pulled off his glove, slowly, his movements jerky, as though orchestrated by someone else. He reaches out a finger to touch the long gouge, a feeling of hopelessness nearly overcoming him. The wall felt impossibly warm beneath his palm, suddenly sticky? and he saw the flash again, this time, ghostly hands dug at the walls, a relentless, agonizing wailing assaulting his ears. The scene shifted back with an audible snap, like an elastic band returning to its original position, and he straightened up, aware that he had been hunched over, breathing heavily. His ears rang in the silence, the echoes of the phantom screams still lingering in his head. He pulled his hand away from the wall, not noticing the fresh blood that covered his palm. But this was not the lesson? the blade urged him on, almost as though speaking to him. Down the hall to another room, this one filled with chicken-wire cages just big enough for a person to stand in. No larger. The wire sags and droops? a smell of dark evil fills this space. One could only guess at the purpose of the enclosures. Punishment? Here is not the place either. He reaches the end of the hall, rotted stairs ascending into dank gloom. Up? Apparently so. Unbidden, he walks swiftly up the stairs, listening to the voice of the blade. Up? watch the holes, watch the refuse. The stairs creak and tremble, but surprisingly support his weight. At the top of the stairs is a small landing, littered with feathers and more bird droppings. Nothing of consequence. Another room, this one empty, shattered window looming, deep indigo, absorbing the light. A barren table near the window. Ah, but not barren after all. Something white glimmers in the light. He crosses slowly, almost reverently. The broken, twisted body of a pigeon rests atop the table, crimson blood flowing, still warm, still impossibly fresh. Blood next to it. Random patterns, almost letters, maybe not after all. Perhaps a trick of the light... or trickery of the mind. Madness suffused these walls. Heavy and alien, these thoughts squirming in his fevered mind. But how, how had this- never mind that, boy. ...and what is to be learned here? “Ah, I believe I may have a glimmer of understanding. Life” life is pain? it is suffering. And why? why have I been drawn to this place? this place of madness? The walls are filled with evil? I can feel it on my skin, clammy and disgusting, filthy. A perversion of the pure, undiluted power I have felt? but, somehow, I find, that it’s not so bad. The darkness must feed? it grows stronger?. I am powerless to resist the pull. I have touched that center of power? I? must possess it all. I don’t want to be a pawn in this vendetta? in this revenge game that has become my own? but I have already accepted my role. I am to dole out revenge, just punishment? he must be made to pay. I am compelled to complete the task? oh, the promises? the blade has offered me so much? I WANT IT ALL!” Gabriel’s words were twisted, rising and falling as the madness crashed around him, dragging him under. He peers down at the twisted body of the bird. Broken, mangled, drained of life as the blood spreads in a pool. His lips curve into a smile, a private smile as he reaches out, touching the lifeless creature, feeling it’s warmth. He throws his head back and laughs, heartily, crazily. What good were morals in a depraved world? The darkness was madness? and he was the darkness. In the night there were no rules? things were not as they seemed. His hands wrapped around the body, feeling the last vestiges of life fading, as the creatures heart still pumped in the last throes of death. He raised the bird to his lips, teeth digging into the neck, blood spilling over his lips as it cooled. The scene shifted again, and another mouth? another set of teeth, wickedly pointed, bit into a human jugular. Blood sprayed up, the red strokes? and moans of ecstasy mingled with shrieks of anguish? Elsewhere in the house, a door slams shut, rattling the window frames. The space seems full, heavy with foreboding. The presence in his mind twisted, growing restless now. The feeling of death? both fresh and ancient loomed between these walls. The plaster and wood screamed with a thousand anguished voices. He could feel the power in this place, and it filled him with a sensation of joy he’d never felt. He was the master. The blade whispered in his head. The reaver had been awakened in this place teeming with souls that could never move on. Their very fibre was tied into the foundations, forever bound to this, their dark master. Yes, all of this could be his? this realm? this power? these gifts. All his. All this and more. Peace. Peace could be his. Forever. All he needed to do was accept the bargain. The contract would be sealed, as all should be... in blood. A flash, and lightning illuminates the room as the flashlight tumbles to the floor, rolling off the table. Blood on the walls, dripping, dark and heavy. And then, the walls are bare. A breeze stirs up, fluttering the wings of the bird that was still clutched in his hands. Sticky blood stained his lips, making them glimmer like rubies, his eyes glowing as well with a feral light. With a twisted leer, he lifted his arms, holding the mangled creature reverently between hands that tremble nervously. The air is thick with the coppery stench of blood... fresh blood that spills across the floor like torrents of water. Warm and welcome... The past floods back into this room, as the sticky warmth of the bird’s life coats his fingers, running down his hands as he twisted the body, wringing the life from its corpse. The sizzle of electricity? a tangle of electrodes. Tanks filled with water. All manner of “cures” for the insane and mentally defective. Torture? pain? and anguish. Key elements to this darkness? the darkness that rightly should be feared. As the room fills with shadows, and echoes with half-remembered wails, the scene fades away, lost in a confusion of heavy shadows as the moon vanished? plunging the night into chaos. The darkness whispered of secrets? of things that should never be revealed? of ancient rites? and of death and decay. Darkness? loneliness? ---- “I was alone” scared shitless? and alone. The things I had done? the thing I had become? so awesome then? was loathsome to me now? it’s funny, how things can shift so quickly? how your views can change in the blink of an eye. I might as well be dead? I longed for it? I prayed for it? with every passing moment, but the blight upon my soul was too great? I was evil incarnate now? and here I would remain? The wind howled outside, hollow and lonesome, but I could feel it just the same inside. The walls were cracked in places, giving a glimpse of the star-dappled night sky. Outside, it began to rain, the heavens opening up with a mighty clap of thunder. Water dripped through the sagging ceiling, running down the rusted iron bars, pooling on the floor, running towards me. They say, that everyone gets theirs in the end? they say that every action has an equal and opposite reaction, but do you think I listened to that shit? No. In hindsight, I could see my every mistake? a lot of good it did me now? The shit hit the fan? and kept going? taking everything down in its path. And just like that, I was fucked. I suppose, in retrospect, it served me right for failing to tie up all those loose ends. But the hammer went down faster than a dead hooker at a necrophiliac convention? and that’s how I ended up here? in Butt-fuck nowhere. Hallowe’en? day of the dead. Fitting end, perhaps. I allowed myself to fall into his trap, and now, I was? ---- “Screwed,” he breathed, his voice hoarse, echoing the pain he felt. “Time to pay for your sins, Jackson.” The voice snapped him from his fitful sleep, as effectively as dousing him with water. His eyes flew open, clouded with pain. Someone was on the other side of the bars, hidden by shadows. He sat up slowly, gasping for breath as the pain sizzled in his nerve endings, blood still seeping from his flayed back. He glared into the shadows, his face filled with fury, eyes blazing with wild blue fire. “Nice fucking accommodations, brother Gabriel. I was hoping for the Presidential Suite... but this will do nicely. Tell me, when does room service get here” I ordered that steak three hours ago. That’s it, they don’t get a fucking tip now.” The man in the shadows stepped forward, his cat’s eyes gleaming with insanity. His hair was wild, hanging in lank strands, and the sword was clutched in his hand, the rubies on the hilt sparkling in the gloom. Gabriel sneered and spat his words. “An eye for an eye, Jackson” or in your case? your life for my family. Your life, for this? for my immortality!” He withdrew the gun from behind his back, holding it in a steady hand, pointed directly at Jackson’s forehead. There it was. The moment of truth. Looking into that tiny black hole? that in an instant became a gaping void? was like looking into himself. There was nothing there. Emptiness and darkness, and nothing more. “Kill me.” Jackson’s voice was flat, filled with no emotion. Bang. A shot. Stench of spent gunpowder. Fire. Pain. Then blackness. Welcome darkness? MY darkness? all for the love of a woman? the wrong woman.
