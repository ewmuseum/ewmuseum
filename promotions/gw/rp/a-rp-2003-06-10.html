A RP Venom Alexander Global Wrestling Promo #282 Date: 6/10/2003 Location: Toronto After periods of rain, just outside of Toronto Canada, the weather is starting to look better as the week goes on. Venom is sitting inside of a local bar and watching VH1 classic, he looks up to the T.V and notice’s a rare video is being played called Vital Signs by Rush. “Sweet, this song kicks ass, hey bartender turn it up.” Venom asking nicely so he can hear the song. The bartender makes his way to the T.V., just before he would touch the dial, the T.V gives off a small spark and goes off. “Figures, just my luck, the damn T.V blows up, looks like I’m going have to get a new one.” As the bartender slams down the T.V to the floor and shatters everywhere. “Sorry folks, but it looks like we are going have to get out the good old juke box to listen to till I buy a new T.V for the place.” As the bartender explaining to about half past out drunks, who could care less about a T.V or even a jukebox. Venom sit quietly as the bartender is cleaning up his mess he made when he threw the T.V down to the floor. While, Venom was looking around the place something got his eye, some lady from his past, but he couldn’t remember who though, she had blonde hair down to her shoulders with green eyes. “That woman, I seen her somewhere before, maybe a dream?” Venom thinking very hard, to were he could have seen her before. “Don’t mind if I could ask who you are talking to.” Asked the bartender. “I’m just talking to myself.” Venom replied in a sigh style of voice. Venom pay the bartender for his drinks, and grab his leather coat, and leaves the bar. Venom makes his way out to his brand new Dodge Dakota SLT that he brought during his recovery from surgery. Just before Venom, opened his door, he happen to notice a business card, on it in big bold black letters. YOU BEEN ACCEPTED “What the hell, I’ve been accepted, in what.” As a confused Venom looks around to see if he could spot anyone, but no luck, he then looks down on the ground for foot prints, again no luck. So Venom, just take the card and throws it down on the card, but what he didn’t know, they was cop on a motor cycle that spotted him throw down the card. Venom only made a few yards when he notice behind the lights of the cop bike, and pulls over for the officer. “You know why I pulled you over sir?” Asked the cop, who was highly over weight, and breath that could kill, a skunk. “No, officer. Why?” Venom, holding on tight to the steering wheel with sweat rolling down his face. “For littering. That’s a huge fine here in Toronto.” The police officer makes his way back to the bike and sits down on it write out the ticket for Venom. The officer makes his way back to Venom and heads him his ticket, and a court date, to pay his fine, of 50 dollars for littering. “That was a close one.” Venom decide to head back to his house before he gets in any more trouble, could this mean bad luck for him, at the up-coming overload with it being on Friday the 13th Venom finals make his way back to his house, he makes his way inside and checks his answering machine. -You have no messages at this time- “Figures, I’m not important enough for people to call me, see how I’m doing since Chris’s death.” Venom sits down in his lazy boy chair, and pulls out a old TCW magazine of him and Chris (A.K.A Cobra) on the front posing with the tag team titles. “Those were the good old days, hard to believe he isn’t here, hard to believe we had a hard fought match a month ago. I’m going to dictate my match for him.” Venom puts down the magazine, and begins to cry. Remembering all the good times, they had. Venom, gets up and whips away the tears, and call’s it a night. Wondering what tomorrow will bring. End Of Story
