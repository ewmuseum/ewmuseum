23 Years Of Roses No More

22 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/22/04 Walking up a hill of grass that comes up to my knees, I kick my feet to rip out the grass from the roots. Perhaps this is the easiest way to make it to the top. Somebody should really cut this stuff. And that somebody should have been me if I took over father’s land how he wanted me to. I remember back to that day that Bubba McCoy heard about me and traveled to my house. He convinced me into traveling to America to be apart of a new sports entertainment called professional wrestling. At first, I didn’t want to be apart of it. But when McCoy wants a Chinese wrestler for his Global Wrestling organization, he can talk a glutton out of eating. He told me I made the right move. But when I looked into my father’s eyes, I seen disgust and heart felt hatred at me and the decision that I’ve made. McCoy told me that I’d make a life for myself instead of follow in my father’s footsteps. Since then, I feel as if I let him down. I strive for his acceptance and his forgiveness. I wanted to talk to him every night. And when I worked up enough guts to confront him about the situation, I received a phone call informing me that he died of cancer. Some things in life, just aren’t fair. But McCoy said that there is a new family awaiting me in Global Wrestling. Back then, people talked around town that I was going to be famous. That made my father even more angry. It was a slap to his face when somebody would congratulate him on a trophy that he did not want. People talked and talked and talked. When I came to America, they talked about little Sage Tse Ling. About how I couldn’t stand tough with the giants of Global Wrestling. McCoy told me to call myself Sage Sabbot. They said that beasts like Quartz and Brick would kill me with one punch. I shrugged off what they said and I defeated Jay Robson for the GW Cruiserweight Championship only a couple weeks of my debut. I made these people eat every word that they said. A couple weeks later, I stepped into a cage match against Jay Vyper, Salaok and Quartz himself. I ended up walking out the winner with a shot at the Platinum Championship. I ended up winning that. Then winning the International Championship. And then the Heavyweight Championship of the World. The point is, every time somebody opened their mouth about little Sage Tse Ling, I shut it. Perhaps I’m fueled off of what they have to say about me. I accept the fact that people go against the underdog. Who wouldn’t? You have wrestler A who is 7 feet tall and 350 pounds and you have me, who stands 6’3 and 215 pounds. Professional wrestlers can curl my body weight with one arm. People can talk, talk it up. Please. Because I enjoy the competition and dropping jaws after a match. People seen me in the ring and said I was as fast as a speeding bullet, as graceful as a dove, and as deadly as a hawk. They ended up realizing that I wasn’t the pushover little guy that everybody that I was. I am the soul survivor. I am the Harvester of Tears. I am everything you wish you were and everything you dream of being. I am me, Sage Sabbot. And I know that you can’t handle this. McCoy said I was one of the greatest ever. This week marks one of the greatest Pay Per Views of all time. It marks the Master of the Mat. The finals of the tournament that has been going on this entire month. Winning this tournament guarantees your name in the record books, right beside Sean Price and Jay Robson. There are several Heavyweight Champions every year, but there is only one master of the mat. And this year, it will be me. I’m the veteran of this year’s tournament. Well, besides Brick but he won’t make it far enough to see the semi-finals. He’s washed up after all. This is not talk or some rumor. It is the truth. McCoy says that Brick is a GW legend. Well that makes me a legend destroyer because I’ve beaten him every time I stepped in the ring with him. Most of these guys don’t even remember back then. They were too busy watching me win matches through the comfort of their 20? television set. They don’t know what competition is about until they have faced me. Tournaments are my thing. Why? Because I don’t need to rely on somebody to be there. I don’t need to carry somebody on my back. Well, except for the first round, which took me two times to get through because of this situation. The last tournament I was in, if you taped it, you can watch it over and over again, I won the GW International Championship from Tragic Hero in the semi-finals. In the next round, I won the GW Heavyweight Championship in the finals and I was crowned the Harvester of Tears by Salaok, Grendel, and other harvesters. And this tournament won’t be any different. I plan? “I will walk out of the Pay Per View with the title of Master of the Mat. McCoy says that it’s an honor that can’t be described. Dressed in success, like I would be for a press conference, or a meeting. It sure feels great to be apart of them once again. But when I left, my seat at those have been empty until recently. Since I have been gone from the Global Wrestling, guys like Josh, Maverick Reno, Ken Partridge, Jr. and others have taken my spot. I hope they haven’t attached themselves to it too much because I came back to take it. These men are your temporary heroes. Your champion of the week. Your false idols. They are just keeping my spot warm. And this is where the term Fallen Stars Forgotten came from. Josh and the rest have been your stars for the last couple months. And when I come back at Master of the Mat and face them, they will be dropkicked from their pedestals. They will be pushed off the proverbial ladder to success. They will fall. And when they fall, they will be forgotten. Who will be able to remember them once I am on top? The answer is plain and simple, not a soul. Things are about to change here in the Global Wrestling and I hope you are ready for it. McCoy says that a change is sometimes good. “its going to be great. People also say that being smart and wise comes with age and experience. I can tell you that those people are liars and should be killed for their nonsense. I have always been smart. I don’t know if it is because of my powers I have, or that they were passed down by father or mother, but I know that since a very young age, I have always been on top of the game. I seem to always have the upper hand on the competition. And that is exactly how I like it. I love to play with people’s minds. Their foolish and pathetic little minds. I love to feed them a dose of pain with a glass of agony to wash it down. They are my little puppets. Even back when I used to play around with Chris and Seth, they were my guinea pigs of torture. I used to make them do extremely idiotic things, because they didn’t know better. I told them that eating worms was healthy for you and what do you think happened” Chris picks up the biggest worm and swallows it whole. Seth gets jealous because Chris is running around making muscles with his shirt off. You think this is funny, but the truth is’all I do is up the standards. I tell you something greater and more important then eating worms. I lie to you. I tease you. I play with your mind. And you accept it. You believe me. You are fucking stupid. I am the kind, the master of the mind games. And next week, I will be the master of the mat because of it. This Pay Per View means more to me then it means to anybody else on the roster. Nobody knows what honor comes from this Pay Per View. They haven’t seen the blood and sweat that has stained the ring mat to earn this title. They take this for granted. But not I, I know what it takes to be the Master of the Mat. And I know that nobody else on the roster has the talent and the capability to win it besides me. I may not be able to raise the dead or begin a new life, but by God, I can control my own destiny. And I can be successful next week. McCoy wishes me good luck. “truth is, I won’t need it. I storm to the top of the hill, my legs growing weak and tired. I can see the tree that Chris, Seth and I used to build forts and tree houses on with the help of father, of course. Every step, the tree becomes more visible. I continue to walk until the base is hidden behind a marble stone that reads: Won Lo Tse Ling October 11th, 1922 ? July 22nd, 1981 It doesn’t take a rocket scientist to realize that today marks the 23rd year of my father’s death. Every year, I come up here to wish him luck with the stars above. To wish him thanks for giving me life. And to ask for acceptance and forgiveness for things that I’ve done. I consider my father one step below Jesus Christ himself. He was a great man, even though I never got to know him too well. He always wore the pants in the family. He made the rules and enforced them. If you didn’t abide by what father put out, you knew what was coming. He was a very respected man for his work and what he gave to his family’everything except the love that they needed. “Afternoon, father.” I cough in my hand, take a deep breath and hide the fact that I’m getting old and can’t take running up this hill any longer. “Another year has passed. I’m sure you already know this. When I was in heaven, I was upset that I didn’t see you. Perhaps you were just around the corner, but if I did, I would have told you many things that I’ve always wished I could. I’d tell you that I love you. That I care about you. And thank you for everything you’ve done for me.” I pause? “I never seemed to make you proud. But this year father, things are different. I know that I say this every year, but this time I’m serious. Father, every year, I bring you a dozen roses and place them on your grave. I light a candle and I stick it into the ground, letting it burn throughout the year. Representing the everlasting love that Mom and I have for you. Last couple years, you know that I’ve been apart of Global Wrestling and I’ve won many things. But this tournament this week father, I dedicate to you. I know it doesn’t mean much to you but it’s all I have. And this year, this victory is your dozen roses. And the flame from the candle is the burning desire and determination that I possess when I step into the ring. I hope it means something to you, that it makes you proud of me for once in your life. I hope that I’m not a let down after let down after let down in your eyes. But this week, I’ll make it up to you once and for all.” I stand to my feet and turn around, leaving 23 years of guilt behind me?
