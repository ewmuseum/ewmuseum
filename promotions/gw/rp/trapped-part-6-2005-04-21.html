Trapped (Part 6)

21 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21st April 2005 Insanity? What drives a person to the brink of their sanity? Is there a certain line that they cross that separates them from the “normal” people. A philosopher once said that there is no one who is truly sane and that each and every one of us in our own way can be seen by the world as crazy. It is a true statement as there is no one who can say they are normal because their version of normal could be seen as insanity by another person. When people see me walking down the street, one of the reactions is that people think I am some eccentric and that I am insane. However I get more of a reaction when I tell people what I do. People think that I am insane being a fighter, putting my body through such pain on a daily basis. They don’t seem to understand that this is my job, that this is what I have chosen to do, this is my life. Just because I did not want to sit behind a desk the rest of my life I am branded insane? To me, the people who live in regret and who hate their jobs are insane. Why waste life doing something you hate? Enjoy your life because we are only here for a short amount of time. But it is hard to enjoy life when you are locked up in a steel box? That is right ladies and gentlemen, you find me still stuck in the same steel box I have been in for 3 months! But finally I have a man who I can point all my anger at, not Richard Wood but Mike Biddle. Like me you found out at last Evolution that it is in fact Mike Biddle who is helping Dr. Wood keep an eye on me. Apparently Biddle is being paid a huge amount to make my life a misery. Okay I will admit that it isn’t actually Biddle who is making my life a misery that is all down to Dr. Wood but it is however Biddle who is aiding Wood in my misery. I really want to know how Biddle can do it. How can he stand back and see a man fall apart because for 3 months he hasn’t seen or heard from his family? How can he keep his mouth shut about the location of the family, when he knows exactly where they are? I talked of insanity before and I am close to crossing that line. I am sure Biddle couldn’t give a crap about my feelings or my sanity for that matter, all he cares about is the big fat cheque that is sent his way courtesy of Dr. Wood! This is all some damn game to Biddle, but this isn’t a game to me, this is my life, this is my family! Do you know what it is like to live everyday with just one thought going through your head? Everyday you see the same faces, all with the same uncaring look, all of them there just for the money. Now I see that face every time I arrive at the GW arena, the same uncaring eyes, the arrogant grin, forever his name will be cursed by me! Mike Biddle’Mike Biddle’MIKE BIDDLE! That name means just another fighter to the rest of the roster but to me it means the misery I have been through the passed 3 months, the anguish I have gone through, it is the name that keeps me from my family. Biddle will tell me where my family is and if I have to use force then so be it! Evolution will be the night. Now I know I have a match at Evolution with 2 up and coming GW superstars, Fred Grest and Jake Hix. Now both have impressed in matches so far and to be quite honest this match is the second thing on my mind but don’t worry I will be at my best and I wish both competitors a good match. As for Biddle, well let’s just wait for Evolution? I dipped my feet into the stream. As the cool water rushed passed my bare feet, I leaned back and placed my hands onto the wooden planks of the small walkway. The small wooden boat tapped against the beams of the walkway just to my side. I looked up and smiled at the peaceful surroundings of the woodland around me. The sun was beaming down warming my face. I heard footsteps behind me and looked around to see Rachel smiling broadly at me with Emma in her arms. She sat down next to me and dipped her feet into the stream and kissed me on the cheek. “It is so beautiful here.” Rachel sighed as she splashed her feet in the stream. I didn’t say a word I just looked down at Emma who was sleeping peacefully in her mother’s arms. A smile crossed my face. “What you smiling about?” Rachel said smiling back and playfully jabbing me in my side. “Just all of this.” I replied still smiling. “What do you mean?” I looked at my wife, her blues eyes looking deep in my soul? Every time I looked at Rachel, I always wondered what she was doing with me. Under my suit I will admit that I am a good looking guy but 90 per cent of the time I am locked up in my suit and when I first met Rachel I was wearing the suit. Before I met Rachel all the women I approached just laughed at me then walked off but Rachel didn’t. When we got married she told me that she saw through the suit and that underneath there was a good man with a good heart. Every day we tell each other how much we love each other and always promise that we will be with together forever. Without a doubt I love my wife but the woman I looked at now was not my Rachel? “Adam?” Rachel asked snapping me out of my thought. “Huh?” I replied still half in my thought. “What did you mean” When you said you were smiling about “all of this?”” “It’s not real.” I stated looking at Rachel who now looked at me in shock. “How is it not real?” “This is all in my imagination. In reality I am still locked up in that steel box in Wood’s laboratory.” “Why do you think this is not real” It could be that Wood’s laboratory is the thing you are imagining?” “Look at the facts. I am outside.” “So?” “I’m not wearing my suit; if this was real I would be dead by now. As for you and Emma I don’t even know where you are. My imagination is just trying to stop me from going insane by giving me this dream scenario.” Rachel placed her arm behind my back and rubbed it up and down reassuringly. Part of me wanted this to be real but it wasn’t, my life isn’t that fair. I put my arm behind my back and grabbed Rachel’s hand and pulled her in close so that she and Emma were pressed against me. “Oh I miss you so much.” I said feeling the tears well up in my eyes. “We miss you too Adam.” “It has been 3 months, Rachel, I don’t know where you are. I don’t even know if you’re safe or not. All I want is to just here your voice but Wood won’t even allow a call. I don’t know what to do anymore, I can’t see any escape.” “Sssh, Adam.” Rachel said softly as she squeezed me. “I don’t know what to do.” I mumbled as the tears began rolling down my cheeks. “Keep fighting.” Rachel stated as she released herself from our embrace and looked me straight in the eye. “What?” I questioned wiping the tears from my eyes. “If you give up Adam then you give up on me and Emma. If you give up then Wood and Biddle have won. You are stronger than this Adam.” “But anything I do, doesn’t make any difference. I always wind up back in at Wood’s laboratory.” “Then do something about it! You are the only who can change things Adam. You are the only one who can find us. Don’t let them win Adam, don’t give up, please, for me and Emma.” I looked at my wife, even though I knew this was simply my own mind talking to me, I felt the tears in her eyes. I felt her heartache because all of it was mine as well. “You are a good man Adam Myers and you will find us, I know you will,” Rachel said as she held out her hand. “Don’t leave me!” I said as I grabbed her hand. “Never,” She replied, “Remember I will always love you!” Suddenly in a bright flash of light, I jerked awake and took a look around. I was indeed back in my steel room. I sat up and sighed as two tears rolled down my face? One for my Emma? One for my Rachel.
