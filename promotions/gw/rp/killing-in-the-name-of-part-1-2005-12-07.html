Killing In The Name Of (Part 1)

7 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/7/05 --------------- Tony’s Condo Living Room Seattle, Washington 4:35 PM --------------- Tony sits at his coffee table, the television is on EPSN. Peter Gammons and company talk about the baseball hot stove as it is to be called now. “I hope the Mariners get Millwood and Morris --- This looks good, not World Championship, but hell Bubba gave me a chance at some other gold.” Stecchino: Gold I have yet to win. With a man I have faced a few times? Jay Robson. Tony laughs as he signs a small piece of paper in front of him. On the top a logo. The GW logo, under it, the Reload logo. A woman, blond hair, no older than twenty-three, her heels softly tap on the floor as she makes her way to the table. Her blond hair in a bun, her white blouse slightly undone. Tony smiles as she stops, he looks up at her. “She’s so beautiful.” She grabs the paper from the table. Woman: You want me to mail this? Stecchino: Please. She starts to walk away, her perfume wofts around where she stood, Tony smells it, he closes eyes to enjoy it. She turns as he opens his eyes. Woman: Have you thought of what I asked you last night. Stecchino: I can not say anything now, I want to, but I can not commit yet. She turns forward, hurt in her eyes. Woman: I understand, but you do love me right? Stecchino: Yes, yes I do. Woman: More than Melissa? Stecchino pauses for a moment, he gently bites his bottom lip. “I knew this question was coming. Can I answer honestly?” Stecchino: I do, Andrea, I really do. “I lied.” Andrea forces a smile, grabbing the door handle, she twists, it opens. She steps through. Andrea: Dinner, downtown, remember? Tony leans back into the couch. “Shit, I forgot.” Stecchino: I have a meeting and some other things to take care of, but I will see you tonight. Andrea: (unbelieving) See you tonight then. Stecchino: See you tonight. Andrea walks out the door. She softly, but firmly closes it behind her. Stecchino stands, his boxers show as his warm up pants sag a little bit. He looks down at his shirtless upper body. Stecchino: I need to get a tan. Tony grabs a towel from the arm of the couch. --------------- Bathroom 4:59 PM --------------- Belinda Carlisle’s “Heaven Is A Place Called Earth” plays in the background. Tony is in the shower singing along. --------------- Warehouse Industrial South 6:00 PM --------------- Tony stands in a black on black on black suit, leaning up against a black Toyota Camry. Stevie Nickels, in the same attire leans against the car right next to him. Their breath both visible on this cold Seattle night. Bundled up workers go past them, not even paying attention to them on their way to their cars to get home as soon as they can, hopefully they won’t spend more than an hour or so on I-5 tonight. Nickels: Do we have to do this? Stecchino: Yes. Nickels: Why? Stecchino: To move forward with the future, the past must be erased. Nickels: Why kill Melissa’s new husband? Stecchino: It is the way it has to be. Nickels: Then what of Melissa? Tony pushes himself off the car and begins to walk through the wet gravel that is what they call the yard. Nickels: What about Melissa? Stecchino: That bitch ain’t getting half of what I’m worth. Stevie grabs Tony by the shoulder. Tony spins, draws his gun from his back holster and holds it to Stevie’s forehead. Stecchino: Don’t be part of the past. Stevie nods. Nickels: She was my friend. Stecchino: She betrayed me twice Stevie. TWICE! Tony lowers his gun and puts it back in his holster. Nickels: Is that reason enough to kill someone? Stecchino: You have no idea who she is married to now do you? Nickels: No. Stecchino: You’ll see. Tony and Stevie walk through the yard toward the warehouse. --------------- Warehouse Office 6:07 PM --------------- A man, dark complection, golden hair, runs around with a file in one hand and a cell phone in the other. Tony and Stevie slowly walk through the warehouse toward the office. Man: I will be home when I can. Fucking shit Melissa, you worry too much. The man hangs up the phone. Tony and Stevie walk into the office. Nickels: Chenzo! Chenzo drops the file and the phone on the floor, he slams back against the wall. Chenzo: Hey guys. Stecchino: How ya doing little brother? Chenzo inches his way toward a file cabinet in the corner. Nickels: Don’t do this Tony. Stecchino: Enjoying my leftovers, enjoying Maverick Reno’s leftovers, huh? Chenzo: It’s not like that. Stecchino: What’s it like then. Chenzo: I love her. Tony pulls out his gun and points it Chenzo, taking a few steps forward. Stecchino: Quit moving. Chenzo: Please. Stecchino: This is unforgivable, I searched and searched and searched for her after Reno dumped her, I love --- loved her. You stole her from me, you lied to me. She married you once the marriage was annulled. You were fucking my wife. Nickels: He’s family. Stecchino: I had no problem killing my mother and father, this is no different. The past must be erased. Chenzo drops to his knees, tears well up in his eyes. Chenzo: Don’t please bro, please! I love you! I love -- Tony fires a shot, the bullet passes straight through the forehead of Chenzo and lodges into the wall. Brains and blood splatter against it. Chenzo Stecchino lays dead on the floor behind the desk. The rats will chew on him later. “He deserved it or did he deserve happiness” He wasn’t happy here.” Tony holsters his gun, turns taps Stevie on the shoulder and walks out of the office. Stevie crosses his heart and follows Tony.
