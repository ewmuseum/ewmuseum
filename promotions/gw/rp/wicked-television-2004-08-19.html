Wicked Television

19 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/19/04 Gonzalez moves around his office, a cigar in his left hand and smoke filling the air as he waves it back and forth. Two bald men, of greater age, sit in front of the desk and look on as Gonzalez moves around. 'So are you vato's understanding esse? We've got a situation on our hands homes.' The two men just nod. 'Theirs too much blood on my hands esse, you two need to take them out.' This causes the two men to stir a bit. 'Que pasa vatos? Scared of blood?' 'It's not that sir, it's just that we have families...' A picture at the end of the desk seems to catch the attention of Gonzalez, he stoops over it and examines it as he brings the cigar out of his mouth. He lifts the picture up and burns a hole through it. 'That was a nice picture of your family esse! You want that to happen to them?' The two men shake their head and get up looking scared. 'That's what I thought vatos! Make me proud! Take him out!' As the door shuts and the two men exit, Gonzalez takes a seat behind his desk. He props his head back on his hand and leans back putting his feet on his desk. A beep raises his attention and a small TV/PHONE raises out of his desk. 'Senor Gonzalez, theirs a man that want's to speak with you.' 'Let him through.' Still relaxing Gonzalez closes his eyes as the picture of the female secratary leaves the TV/PHONE and is replaced with the picture of Trey Reed. 'Just the man I wanted to talk too...' The voice of Trey Reed causes Gonzalez to open his eyes, he almost stumbles backwards and the cigar in his mouth falls out onto the floor. 'My main man! You look scared to see me!' 'You little piece of dirt! You cockroach!' 'Calm down there VATO! You see this?' Trey read holds up the black suitcase from Evolution. 'You thought it was going to be that easy didn't you VATO? You thought that you and your boys could just jump me and get the suitcase, well you thought wrong! You see here VATO, I own you! And I want that Television Title around your waste!' Gonzalez slams his fist on the table as Trey Reed continues to speak. 'Listen homes, you don't know what you're getting yourself into, people are dying, people are dying because you want to play games esse.' 'I'm not playing games Gonzalez, I want that title and I'm going to do what I got to do! If it means taking this to the right authorities and putting you behind bars, then I'll do it! I wan't an answer at Evolution!' The screen fades to black and Gonzalez is left there looking at the blank screen! 'You want an answer vato! You wan't this title homes! Well you're gonna get it esse! You're gonna get what you got coming! Carlos! Meto!' The two bald men that were earlier in the office are seen coming back into the office. 'I want it done today! I want you to take that cockroach out! He thinks he can scare me! He thinks he can get a shot at the title just like that! Kill em esse! Bring me that cockroaches head!' The two men grab a rifle apiece and they nod, both wal out of the room together. 'You want this title esse! It's gonna cost you your life! It's gonna cost you your head!' Out of anger Gonzalez throws everything off his desk, and slams down the TV/PHONE! 'Your head is mine esse! It's mine!'
