Sunlight slipped along her pale shoulders and caressed the gentle curve of her neck, slipping up through her hair to kiss the still blonde portions of her dyed black hair. It was unusual for the Steel Rose to sleep so late, even after a grueling six man tag match’but the early morning had held more then just aching muscles and a late bedtime for Dawn: It was early morning when Dawn stirred in her sleep, her mind playing all manner of tricks on her aching body. Cool lips started at the base of her ankle, gently moving their way up her bare calves towards her thigh, sharp teeth lightly scraping the skin. Hungry lips pressed to the hollow of her throat, then up her neck and along the bottom of her chin where they paused for but a moment before slipping up to press lovingly against her own lips. “I can’t breath.” Her mind noted suddenly, the fog of her dream starting to fade. Green eyes struggled to open and suddenly found themselves staring into emerald eyes so deep she thought they would go on forever. Lia broke the kiss reluctantly and Dawn gasped in surprise, realizing for the first time that she was fully awake, her vampiric stable mate pressed against her. “Lia?” She breathed, “What?” Lia placed a cool finger against Dawns mouth, leaning down so her lips brushed against the Steel Roses ear. “Spandex silly?” She whispered, before the tip of her tongue slipped against the curve of Dawn’s ear. She stirred again under the sheets, a contended smile spreading across her lips as she snuggled against Lia’s cool body’the smile turning to a pouty grumble as she found her arms wrapped around a body pillow instead of Lia. She slid onto her stomach, her left arm stretching out across the bed as she tried to brush her fingers against her lover’s skin’and found only air. Dawn forced her eyes open and found them watering in the bright sunlight as she tried to find Lia visually. Nothing. Instead Dawn was looking at the wavering image of her own arms, stretched to full length across the bed, with her fingers splayed out as though she were reaching for something she couldn’t possible touch. Lips curling into a delicate frown she sat slowly, running one hand up through the mess of her tousled hair. “Was it’just a dream?” Dawn wondered aloud, slipping to the edge of the bed and swinging her long legs down to the floor. The underwear and tank top she usually wore for sleeping were lying on the floor, the tank top not far from her feet and the underwear nearer to the balcony door. A slight pink tinge crept into Dawn’s cheeks as she looked around, reaching up to rub the back of her neck sheepishly. “I guess not’ow!” She hissed in surprised as a sharp pain spiked up under her fingers, prompting her to crane her neck sideways in an attempt to see what was causing her such pain. Unsurprisingly she failed to see the back of her own neck, but did manage to make out five small red marks just below her right shoulder. An inspection of her left shoulder showed a similar array of marks and an exploratory touch provoked the same kind of pain as the back of her neck. She slipped from the edge of the bed and padded across the room toward her mirror, feet sinking into the dark blue deep pile carpet and a cool breeze blowing through the balcony doors to slide across her naked skin. She turned her back to the mirror, craning her head to get a better look at her neck and shoulders. An impish grin slipped across her lips as she pushed her long hair aside, revealing three angry red lines up the back of her pale neck. “No, defiantly not a dream.” Like a tidal wave, a wash of dizziness spread over her body, her knees going a little weak and threatening to buckle out from under her. She grabbed the edge of her dresser for support, clinging weakly to the drawers as she tried to fight it off. “W-what the’the hell?” She stared at herself in the mirror for a moment, taking in the pale tinge of her lips and the even whiter colour of her skin. “Oh crap?” Dawn murmured, trying to remember what happened. One hand drifted to her right thigh, memories of sharp teeth coming to the fore. “Oh’right?” Dawn blushed at her own foolishness, having almost forgotten that her vampiress had likely fed? She clung weakly to the dressed until the wave of dizziness passed, gently testing her own weight before settling her feet firmly against the floor again. Using the wall for support she made her way to the bathroom, darting inside before turning the shower on. ************************************************************************************** It was almost an hour later when Dawn finally emerged from her room, leaning on the railing the entire way down the four flights of stairs to the ground floor. Wobbling on unsteady knees she managed to find her way to the kitchen, using the island in the middle of the floor for support as she raided the fridge for red meat. There was a delicate cough from behind her and Dawn spun quickly to locate the source, nearly losing her footing once more and scrabbling for support. From where was sitting at the kitchen table, Alex watched Dawn’s flailing with wide eyes. “Uh’are you okay Dawn?” “Yeah’yeah I’m fine, just feeling a little woozy is all?” Dawn managed to force out as she finally managed to attain something resembling upright. “Just not feeling very well, that’s all.” Alex shook her head with a sigh, pushing herself up from the table and moving across the tiled floor to where Dawn was struggling to stay standing. “Why don’t you sit down and let me get you something then” It’s the least I can do since you let me stay.” Alex didn’t phrase it so much as a question, rather she took the larger Dawn by her arms and guided her to a seat at the kitchen table. That Alex could so easily muscle her to the table when she didn’t want to go showed Dawn how weak she really was and so she allowed herself to be sat down. “Uh’thanks Alex.” “No problem.” Alex mumbled as she started rummaging through the fridge. “So, red meat red?” From her spot at the table Dawn stared at Alex’s back with wide eyes. “Uh yeah’how did you?” Alex flushed a little, using the door of the refrigerator to hide her sudden shyness. “Well you’re very uhm’loud?” A fiery red crept up into Dawn’s cheeks from her neck and her emerald eyes got as big as plates as she stared in shock and horror at her guest. “Loud?” She coughed, just barely above a squeak. “You could?” ??hear you clearly.” Alex cut in and finished the sentence for Dawn. “So when I saw you were all pale and dizzy, you probably got’well’bitten in the heat of things.” Dawn couldn’t decide if she wanted to crawl under the table and die or just cry. Things had been strained at best between herself and Alexis since she’d been “outed” on national TV, but for her guest to have heard what happened the night before was a little much. “I’m sorry Alex, I wasn’t thinking?” “It’s’it’s okay Dawn, don’t apologize to me. I’m a big girl, I can handle it.” “I know you are but?” Dawn couldn’t finish her sentence, couldn’t put into words what she was feeling. She was sorry to have made Alex uncomfortable, but not at all sorry that it had happened in the first place. ??for me last night was like a dream Alex. It’s something I’ve wanted for, god for so long I was worrying about my state of mind. It’s not just a physical thing Alex’I’m in love?” “And why am I telling HER this?” Dawn’s mind asked of itself, “She’s probably the LAST person who wants to hear about it.” Alex emerged from the fridge with a shy smile and a plate of sliced salami, taking it over to the bread box on the island. “I know what you mean D, I’ve felt like that before’I just don’t get how you can feel that way about a’vampire.” It was Dawn’s turn to smile, a slow grin that slipped across her pretty lips and settled firmly on her face. “I’m not in love with a vampire; I’m in love with Lia.” Alex opened her mouth to object and Dawn silenced her with a look. “Let me finish. Now eat this before she sees you all weak and feeble and feels bad.” A plate with three sandwiches slid across the table to rest in front of Dawn, each one piled high with salami and cheese. Dawn picked at one idly before picking it up to munch on, “Thanks Alex?” ******************************************************************************************* After ravenously consuming all three sandwiches Dawn managed to stagger back upstairs before collapsing back into bed and lapsing into a restless sleep, not even crawling under the sheets before her exhausted mind closed down on her. “Dawn sweetie” You need to wake up, even if only for a little bit okay?” She was floating in a haze of white light, light so bright that it obscured everything around it, leaving only a play of fuzzy and indistinct images. When I go you’re going to be all better, and you’ll stay that way forever.” “I don’t want you to go mummy’I don’t want to be better!” A small hand came into Dawn’s vision, grasping weakly at her mothers arm. Gently her mother freed her arm and took Dawn’s small hand between both of hers, pressing it up to her own cheek. “You have to get better Dawnie, I could never live with myself if I knew you were sick because I stayed.
