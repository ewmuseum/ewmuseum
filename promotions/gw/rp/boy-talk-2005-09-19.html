Boy Talk

19 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/19/05 ~Late at night ~ ~In the new House~ Josh just couldn't sleep. He laid there looking at the beautiful young lady laying next to him, her breathing calm, and relaxed. Her face snuggled into his chest, him laying on his back. The last week, she'd taken into laying like that when they slept. He didn't mind, but it appeared to help her feel safer. However, while her sleep had become easier for her, it didn't for him. He remembered things from that night that she couldn't remember. His mind still filled with the memory of her in so much pain, and him totally helpless to stop it. He wised that on no one, to ever be that helpless with the one they loved. He now understood to a point why Gray had left his wife, he didn't want to ever put her though that. Not to mention the fact that Gray didn't now if he'd ever change in front of his family and carry out his rage. The pain he must have had been going though before his first change. Gray was a strong man, but Josh knew now it took more then that to survive the rebirth. He wondered if Gray's had been as painful as Danielle's, or because; Gray wasn't immortal if his was spared a little of the pain. Questions he had always been afraid to ask his brother, maybe he would finally ask him or ask one like him. He knew here in Vegas there was one like him, he might look him up and have a heart to heart with him. Plus from what was understood he was the cousin of he Mistress of Secrets, and a high ranking member of the Order, not to mention one of the Order's Hunters. He might have more information or be able to get information that for some reason being kept from Danielle on what was going on. Anything to make her more comfortable in her new skin. That was the number one thing for Josh. They had flown into Vegas late, just past Dark. When they reached the new house all of them were in awe. Danielle a simple girl was in the biggest awe. Five bedrooms, one was occupied by Josh's stuff, as Annie explained she didn't want to call attention to what was personal between the two of them so didn't tell anyone that Josh would be sharing the room with Danielle. But that was the size of the house, made it so Josh's room would become his art studio. After moving some of his things to Dani's room, they had gone to bed. After getting her to sleep, he had already been up once. In his 'studio' he had placed his drawings up there for a bit. Some went on the wall, some were laying about his old desk that had made it's way to here. After about an hour of that work, he then climbed into bed, again. But to no avail, sleep avoided him like a a rabbit avoiding the fox. He slid out of the bed carefully, so not to disturb Danielle, the silk sheets just covering the lower part of her back as she laid slightly on her side, and slightly on her stomach; in the indentation that his body had formed as he had held her there. The light that was coming into the window of the bed room made her body glow. He reached down and brushed the hair out of her face, her eyelids rapidly moving let josh know she was deep into REM sleep, and was dreaming. And as it had been every night since she had finally manifested her angelic self in the desert, the dreams didn't appear to be the ones that hurt her. He sighed, there was something he wanted to get done for her... but first he decided that a late night snack was in order. He made his way quietly to the kitchen, not like it mattered in this big house anyways, it would take the intercom for anyone to hear anyone else. As Josh passed the wet bar he looked down at the two folders of paper work that had been handed him and Danielle, and sighed again. Nothing like the Order to ruin a perfectly good welcome home with paper work. Josh had no choice but to reveal himself in front of Annie, it was a question of Danielle's wellbeing. He found the coffee pot still had some coffee in it, from his earlier trips down. With a cup of coffee in hand he walks out into the pool area, the glow of the Strip can be seen, takes a seat next to the pool. The vision of her hovering with out stretched wings still hung in his mind, The vision of her in pain, the memory of her in pain. He had done the only thing he could, he had drawn some of it into himself. He knew that if he had pushed to hard he would of caused more damage then good but he had to, no matter what it was something that he had to do. He played with his cup for a few more minutes then went back tot the kitchen, getting himself a second cup he then quietly made his way to what now he was now calling his studio. Walking past his brother's door, at least hoping not to make to many sounds to wake him. With Gray's more attuned senses it seemed that even a light noise woke him up. He made his way into the room, he sat down at the drawing table reached down and grabbed the tube that he had taken off the airplane with him. He uncapped the container and then pulled the drawing out and unrolled it. He then used the clips on the table to hold it flat. He then grabbed a pencil from the cup on one of the shelves and started to work on the sketch again Between this, his music and his martial arts, it was all means to keep his mind calm, thinking and calm. He heard the door open again, his brother wasn't one for knocking on his door. Never had been. ?Couldn't sleep?? Gray asked him, stepping in while wearing only a pair of sweat pants. His short hair was tussled, but not as bad as it could have been if he had actually been sleeping. Josh replied without looking up from the drawing. ?What gave you that idea?? “You've been walking around a few times. Maybe because your up here in this room, instead of in bed. Then again if you lay off this stuff you might sleep better.” Gray picks up the cup of now coffee that josh had forgotten to drink as he got into his work. “Nah, it's weak stuff. But I didn't want to wake Dani up, she is still fighting to understand everything in her mind. For a change her mind is settled. So she is sleeping soundly.” Gray moved to come into the room more, and took a position behind his brother. Looking over his brother's shoulder a still rough pencil drawing of Danielle's manifestation; but with her wings folded slightly over her upper body covering the parts of her that were uncovered on that night. “So that is what she looked like? Breath taking... Sirius Star was shining brightly thats for sure.” Josh turned and looked at his brother, the look on his face was concern. ?Why didn't you tell me how painful your first change was?? “Becuase it's over. My change was painful, as my bones reshaped themselves all around my body and such. But it's over. But, it seems to me we both have been keeping secrets. I could ask you: Why didn't you tell me about you? I just finished reading Annie's report.” Josh turned to the table and set his pencil down, then turned back to his brother. “Your mind was to full as was, your duty was to Danielle, not to me. She needed you more, I am just as stubborn as you are. I just don't have the teeth and claws to back myself up. Besides, I'm surprised the were completely blind to it. Margarette has it also. Looks like we both got our bag of tricks from our mothers. Dad must have been a catalyst to reinforce the genetic code.” Gray smiled at that. He had tried to be cordial with Josh's mother over the last week. A small spot in Gray's heart thought that they might actually be able to get along. It was pretty unfair for Gray to hold a grudge against her for replacing his own mother after she had died. But there was the fact that even with being so normal, frail... human... Josh was still something special. An empath, he could sense people's emotions. ?Although, Joshua, your little secret explains a lot. I know Annie outlined it. But, as to why you got into it with Shadow: she was already pissed off when she entered the room. As to why you seem to always argue with me. Your attempts to show affection to Dani. You sense emotions, at least that is what Annie is guessing by the report she filed. She has also has placed you on the top of the list for testing, when you're ready. She thinks that the Order needs to give you time to be with Sirius instead of dragging you though weeks of testing.? Josh made a sour face. Yeah, after 8 years of dealing with the order after he found out who his mother's real bosses were, he knew about their testing better then anyone else did. ?How kind of her. So what do you think of this house, she picked out?? Gray's face was just as stoic as ever. ?Big. Face it our lives have changed. Mine was changed with my first change, you knew what to do with bringing me into the order. Sirius Star's life has been slowly changing over the last few years, She has grown strong. Her Grandfather has requested a visit with you and her, I think he wants to know your attentions towards his granddaughter. although he really cant complain to much at least you didn't try to force her into marriage. Josh just winced at the mentioning of that. To him that was still salt in a open wound. He almost lost Danielle to a man who was hired to help her, but instead tried to help himself. Josh just hoped that he would remember the pain of the beating he gave him, even if he couldn't remember anything else. Gray continued on while Josh was in throught. “However, you've got to remember the old man is very traditional... old school native. Some of the things you two have done, he might already consider you two in a union.” Josh missed that part, and kept thinking of other things he could have done to Jaymes that would have been much more violent then breaking his knee. “So what you going to do” ? Josh shook his head and looked at his brother ?Excuse me?? “What are you going to do? Obviously the relationship, your feelings, between you and Sirius have gotten stronger, by old laws you two would be married. You both would be on a way to a hand fasting and making your own tee pee together if you were Sioux, like her. Not that this is much different with you two sharing a room.” Josh struggled with the words to answer. “I... I... We're....” Then his brother hit him with the killing blow of informatin. ?So you going to place your great grandmother's promise ring, that Margarette gave you a few nights ago, on her hand?? Josh spun around with a look of surprise on his face, almost spilling the can full of pencils all over his desk. ?How did you know about that?? Gray just tapped his ears and smiled, And let out a loud chuckle. “You two can whisper and conspire all you like in the back of a restaurant. But I could hear you as clear as if you were talking to me. Some of my abilities make me a pretty good spy. But nothing like you and her to leave the Order into havoc and confusion.” Josh looked to feign shock. ?Me? How did I?? Gray's face moved to a large grin. ?Yeah, your tests didn't show anything. From so long ago, Annie pulled them up from when you were at the academy. Never once... amazing how well you hid it all this time. But you've always known?? “I guess you could say... I didn't see it as an ability until recently. As for hiding it, it's not hard. It only gets hard with intense emotions around you. Shadow was out for blood, yours. You're always angry, and let's just say that Dani's always been...” Gray caught his stopping there. ?Which leads me to a question, why would you ever be jealous of me, when it came to her?? Josh sighed at that one. ?I'm not trained. I don't read them all the same. You've always loved Dani. I was sensing that. ? Gray questioned him. ?I've always loved Dani?? “Yeah, you've always loved her like a little sister. I was confused with it. She's always returned that to you also. Between her and I, it's a bit more.” “So I heard...” Josh glared as his brother. “I'm going to need to start wearing head phones or something when I try to go to sleep.” Gray smiled at his brother. “Shut up. So what's up with Dani and the confusion? Sounded like Annie had a grip on things.” Gray pulled one of his brother's extra chairs over. “Sirius Star she is just a mystery to everyone, officially. She's got everyone confused, because if what the evidence is showing is true... she's what they're calling a crossover.” Josh snapped at his brother suddenly. ?You know, she does have a proper name. ? Gray snorted his answer back. ?Yes and I use it. ? Josh started to look irritated. “Her given name is Danielle Lynn Northsky.” Gray tried to remain calm. ?No, that is her birth name that was on her human birth certificate. Her GIVEN name is Sirius Star, that was given to her by her tribe and nation when she came back to them, her grandfather being one of the main sources for that name. And I think she has earned the right to use it, after what she had gone though. Rebirth is just as painful as a woman giving birth. Basically she did give birth that night, to herself.? “Yeah, so Annie told me... don't think I will try to ever put that on her, don't think I could ever handle seeing her go though that because of me.” Gray just smiled at his little brother. The tough street smart kid was getting a lesson even better then Gray could ever teach. Gray always thought hands on was the best way to learn. In this case it was the only way to learn. But they were intimate. That was for sure. Gray wondered though if she would be able to bear children. Angels had apparently proven to be capable of mating with humans, but with this being a throwback, could she carry on her line? Then again, supposedly everything changed when you became immortal. Gray's thoughts wondered a bit around though, he couldn't think of Dani that way, but... ?So you going to tell her? Or at least ask her to dinner?? Josh broke his concentration, and Gray was taken aback from his brother's question. ?Excuse me?? “You're a open book big brother. Your mind betrays you on your thoughts of her. I sensed it when you guys came to pick us up in the desert.” Gray actually went on the defensive. “You can just stay out of my mind, thank you. There is nothing to tell, noone to tell anything to. Noone to ask out for dinner.” Josh just smiles at him. “At least you know she can kick your butt if you get out of line. I would suggest NOT getting a house with a pool.” Gray just turned red a little. “You know i am really going to hate you doing this, one thing you need to learn real quick is privacy. But, I'm a male... I might also be more then human, but I'm allowed to fantasize about a beautiful woman. You have done enough of that yourself over the last year or so.” Josh played it off, on his now figuring it out. “At least I now know what I have been reading all this time. It wasn't Dawn or Danielle.. it was her.” Gray appeared to be trying to change the conversation. “You know I think you better get some sleep. We have a match this week so your training starts tomorrow... err, today.” ?A match? Cool. We finally getting out title shot, that I earned for us?? Gray chuckled again. “Nope, they're running a tourney. As I understand it, it's going to be for every tag match we have, and everyone else has for some time. This week it isn't much, just a dark match against the Enforcers. I've not even seen them wrestle since we came here with Dani. That's 3 months, so they're probably pretty rusty. We move through them, and keep going through this tourney and claim what we should have had at Whiplash.” Josh agreed with him. “Know what you mean. I wouldn't mind that myself. My first titles ever, were ganked from me. Not to mention anything about you, big brother.” Gray stopped for a second and tilted his head a little bit. “She is waking up. You might want to go let her know where you are at least. I think her mind still is trying adjust to everything. The betrayal of her faith in Jaymes, and the demons, and then her change. I am surprised she didn't crack. I know stronger people that didn't make it. Showed that you should have been there, little brother.” Josh nodded. “Makes her worthy of her true given name. Her Christian name, isn't a bad one. At least we now know where it came from, her mother and father. Tells us the Nuns knew more about her then they ever let on. The poor child has been though a lot in her life, she at least now has you.” Josh closed his eyes for a second, Gray was right Danielle's mind was starting to wake up, she was realizing he wasn't right there. Josh Got up and left his drawing on the table. He moved briskly, leaving his brother there. “Good night, little brother.” Gray called that out, he didn't need an answer. Gray looked over once again. At the drawing his brother had been working on. A memory of the artist of his first view of the the woman called Danielle Lynn Northsky. Still in the works, but still breathtakingly beautiful. His little brother had always been the true artist. Drawing, music and his movements. Gray suspected this was going to be Josh's new tattoo for his back. A Fitting design. Gray: Breathtaking, little Sirius Star, Just breathtaking. ~The End~
