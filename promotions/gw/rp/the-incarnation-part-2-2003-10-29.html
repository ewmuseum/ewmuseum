The Incarnation: Part 2

29 October 2003

Date: 10-29-03 The Incarnation: Part 2 It was there on the road that he came out of the dark. A man of great mass and the appeal of a tortured soul were he, and with each step, you could see that he was fighting a pain in every part of his body. From his first to his last step, he walked giving blood for the sacrifice he'd later realize. There on the porch he fell, and for a week, he was out cold. Who was he? Not a soul knew. The people who brought him in were unsure, and he himself was unsure. It was as if he had no identity. However as it began to fade away before, the man realized who he was. As well, what he was… a murderer. Story Continues: There the man sat on the bed, and with the words he just spoke the boy outside the door was in a moment of panic and confusion. Was what the man said was true? Was he really a murderer, or is he too in confusion. Unknowing the man has no idea that the boy hears his words and just sits there as the young child steps away quietly. Jesse looks down and sees that the clothes he had worn before are now folded and in a neat pile beside his bed. If he had the clothes taken off of him, why did the woman not know his name? Did she know who he was and fear him, or was it the fact that she thought it’d be better he told them? Jesse is in confusion, as he does not understand a lot of things. His eyes gaze around noticing the room is much cleaner now. New drapes, not as much dust on the dressers, and the bed sheets brand new, he can tell the woman had something to do with this, but… why? With each question his eyes started to turn from confusion to misguided anger. He grabs the sheets beside him and clenches them tight within his grasps, as if wanting to rip them from the bed. However, with a knock from the door he swerves from angry to calm in the matter of seconds and mutters, “Come in.” There appears the woman in front of him. Wearing now a long dress like t-shirt and looking quite ravishing. Her eyes twinkle from his eyes with the reflection of the flame from the candle. He looks at her and knows she is here to comfort him. Slowly picking up her dress she reveals her body and then comes to him in the nude. As she seduces him, he blows out the candle and the two are lost in the dark. *** *** *** Sunlight bursts through the white drapes and blinds Jesse as he tries to pry his eyes open with his hands. Rubbing he finally removes the sleep from his eyelashes and the corners of his eyes. His face is full of life as he rolls over. He sees the woman lying beside him and he kisses her on the cheek. Slowly getting out of bed he grabs a pair of pants and slips them on, then puts some socks on, and finally a shirt. Jesse gets his clothes and packs them in a bag, knowing he has to leave for, even though in love, a danger to anyone here. He kisses the woman once more and then slips his shoes on and makes his way out of the room and down the long hall way. Slowly creaking down the stairs Jesse makes his way through the kitchen and through the back door. A sigh however is heard just as he presses the door open. He turns around and the old man sits there on a chair drinking a bottle of Jim Beam. Jesse turns around abruptly and turns his head, flicking the hair from one shoulder to the other in a quick motion. His eyes peer at the old man in question, for he knows that Jesse is a murderer, and Jesse can feel it. Why else would the man be down stairs and stop this stranger he doesn’t even know from leaving his house so early in the morning? “It’s not a good idea to leave without breakfast,” states the old man as he glances at Jesse and shows him the plate of eggs, ham, and toast with a glass of orange juice on the other side of the table. Jesse ponders for a moment, unsure if he should, and then replies, “I really have to go sir, but-“ With that the old man says, “But no need to explain, I know,” cutting off Jesse, “I know and I want to help. So sit down and let’s talk.” Jesse just stands there looking confused for a moment, not sure to sit or go. However, as he looks at the man he realizes the man speaks with meaning, and so he sits down. “Well Jesse, I think I should introduce myself, my real self. My name is Kevin Mancini. I’m an under cover officer for the CIA. For twenty-two years I’ve been in the business, and since I’m getting to be a bit to old for the running down people and what not, they stuck me in the middle of nowhere for a mission. I’ve been in this dump for six months, and to be frank, when I saw your face I was so happy. For once some news came out here in the middle of nowhere, since those murders were happening an all. You really shook up the CIA and FBI. You’re wanted for murder, but no one knows it’s you, since the CIA wants to keep it wrapped up.” Jesse was mind boggled. What had he done that was so bad? All he can remember is flashes, and then finally with two words, he remembers what happened. “Rebecca Mains…” the man stated as he began a sentence. But Jesse only heard the name, and forgot everything else the man had said. With the fading memory slowly coming back, Jesse was floating into a daze of memories. Twitch, twitch, the hand had reacted then gone limp. There lies a woman dead with hr neck slit, her face bloody, and her eyes gouged out. A knife in her chest, a cut in her side pelvic area, and a skin wound on her abdomen, she was in horrible shape. Their lying in the middle of nowhere, a forest perhaps, yet Jesse could not understand it. Then below he saw his hands, covered in crimson blood, his clothes stained like a die, and her face just shook his mind with such pressure that he began to cry. Snapping back into reality, Jesse could see that Kevin was clapping in front of his face. Jesse’s focus came back to him, and he realized he was crying. His face damp from the tears that still flowed from his eye corners. His goatee making some tears become lost in a mess of forest like hairs, yet Jesse was actually crying, and he could not control it. “You saw something, huh?” asked Kevin as he peered into Jesse’s eyes. Jesse just nodded for he could not answer, and the words he wanted to say were still lost within the flashback he had just encountered. Jesse sitting there wondered, he wondered if the woman he saw was the person he murdered, and yet trying to deny it, he had to face it, he had killed her. “Listen Jesse, I’m not sure of what we can do to help you, but you can’t stay here. They said they’d be back, and I know they will. Probably sometime tomorrow, so it’s best if you leave here soon. Try and get on a train or something. I know your innocent, and if I can prove it by catching him, I’ll make sure your off the hook, okay?” claims Kevin. Jesse nods again, but then finally getting some words out, he blurts, “But why are you helping me?” “You’re not the kind of man I want to see behind bars, or even worse, killed for a crime you didn’t commit. If I’m right, you’re being set up, and I am sure it’s by an old nemesis of mine. So please Jesse, get, flee, and be gone. I can only help if you listen. So leave!” boasts Kevin finely and with that he grabs Jesse and stuffs a ticket in his pant pocket and pushes him out the door. However, right before he is to push him out, he says one last thing. “Take care of yourself Jesse, I have a son who loves you dearly, and I can not let him see a man he looks up to be locked away for something so bad. You’re a hero, keep that in mind my friend, good bye.” (To Be Continued…)
