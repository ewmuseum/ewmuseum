After the main event

15 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/15/03 Brick has just gotten through receiving a win that was all too easy. He had been preparing himself, both physically and mentally, for a huge match with the returning Chris Camero. He was certain that he’d get that match. Unfortunately, this wasn’t to be. Just as the bell had rung, Camero had dropped down to the mat and laid down for the count. Brick wasn’t sure what to make of it. Finally, someone yelled out from the crowd, “Just pin him” made it clear. He then pinned him with one hand for the 1-2-3. Brick should be happy. The victory meant that he would face Salaok one-on-one at Wrestlecade in a Hell in a Cell match. However, he was expecting Camero to at least put up a fight. He isn’t big on getting easy wins, and this was the easiest win he has ever gotten. “Brick!” Brick looks to see who the voice belongs to. It’s Brick’s friend and ally Ely Braxton. “Hey, Brick! Slow down!” “He cheated me, Ely!” “What are you talking about?” “That son of a bitch Camero! He cheated me!” “I can understand, but hey, you won the match.” “He gave me that match!” “Well, it’s better than going the distance only to have that bastard Salaok screw you.” Suddenly, Brick hears part of Camero’s rant against the fans. “For 2 long years, I worked to be the best, to give you a hero, to give this company a hint of respectability and this is how I get re-paid. So why should I entertain you” Why should Christopher Camero risk injury the night against Brick- a trashcan swinging homeless assclown?” “What did that motherfucker just call me?” Brick growls, starting to head back out to the arena, but Braxton holds him back. “Homeless assclown” I’ll show that little faggot a homeless assclown!” “Let it go, Brick. Camero ain’t worth it.” “So many times that son of a bitch has been asking me to beat his ass. When he first came here and screwed me out of the TV title, he was asking me to beat his ass. When he tricked Trista Romano a couple of years back at Showdown in Tokyo, he was asking me to beat his ass. All the times he used to talk shit about me backstage, he was asking me to kick his ass, and he just screwed me out of the chance tonight! And for what” So he could say “fuck you” to the fans?” “Yeah, well, don’t think about that, right now. Camero’s not important. The fact that you have Salaok at Wrestlecade, that’s important. Now, Salaok’s gonna try and pull every trick in the book to get you suspended and to get himself out of this match.” Braxton is right. Salaok has proven that he’ll go to any lengths to see Brick gone from Global Wrestling. Now, to hold on to the chance, he’ll have to go the distance, beat the odds. Brick has been waiting for the chance to get Salaok one-on-one for a while. Now that he has the chance, no one is going to take it away from him. “Besides, what Bishop said should annoy you more. He was actually hoping you’d lose and get kicked out of the company.” “Man, Bishop’s a little punk. Beating his ass wouldn’t be any fun.” “Sure o” that?” “I saw Overload” says Brick. “How’s Christina?” “She’s okay. Shigeru was yanking my damn chain, trying to get in my head.” “Runs in the damn family. Scarecrow, Salaok and him. Neither one of those bastards can face anybody man to man. I gotta have my friends and family watched 24-7 because o” them. Good job kicking Shigeru’s ass, by the way.” “Felt good. Nobody messes with the McCoys and gets away with it when I’m around.” “All the McCoys or one particular McCoy?” “That supposed to mean something?” “I think we both know what that means.” “Yeah, well, Bubba and Donovan are all in the hospital. I’d rather Christina didn’t join “em.” “Uh-huh.” “And if I do have feelings for her, that’s my business.” “Hey, I’m not saying a word.” “Good.” “Good. Of course, you do know her last boyfriend left me to die in an icy river.” “Drop it!”
