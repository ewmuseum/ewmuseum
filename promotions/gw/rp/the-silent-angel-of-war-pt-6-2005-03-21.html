The Silent Angel of War Pt.6

21 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 21, 2005 The Silent Angel of War Pt.6 The world opens up like a bleeding heart. People spread their problems to friends and family, expecting support. Yet in reality, it's just the infection of a plague that weakens the minds. Feeble people? they never expected it. His head leaning against the wall, eyes are closed as normal, and his face in a state of unrest as he ponders heavily. Yet, with all this the jackal taps his fingers in a rhythm of solemn. His head slowly arches forward, and the faint glaze of his eyes appears through the slit of his eyelids. This is why I don't care anymore? I'd rather die in here where I know where I lay, where I know the reason to my death. Than out their where the world lies about it all. They feed us loads of shit about this and that being the reason of our demise, when the truth would hurt a lot less. His lips purse as he spits through them onto the canvas in front of him. Moving his foot he smears the white liquid into the floor and cracks his neck. Eyes opened fully now, they glow like the moon in the dimmed room. When they tell you your dying of cancer? they should explain why you picked up a cigarette in the first place. The reason behind how it's a stress reliever, and the smoke just blows away all your problems with one puff. They should explain that the cigarette is like a key to the childhood problems, the ones that begin with yelling and end with juvenile detention homes. See? people fret so much over them not stopping that person from smoking, when in reality, the cigarette they puff on is their medicine to the past. It's a selfish thing to the doctors and the medical records to lay that blame on the loved ones? I know that better than anyone, because I too was dealt the same belief with Julie. His eyes begin to tear just slightly, one bead glistening in the corner of his eye as the opposite eye starts to water. Yet simultaneously the droplets roll down his cheeks, and drop from his face onto his shirt. Opening his palms he lays them on the floor next to him, looking down as he frees his memories through the tears. Julie didn't have cancer, but she smoked. She smoked twice a day, once on Saturday, and not at all on Sunday. She always said she hated doing it, but since she was twelve she couldn't stop picking them up. Later on after we got married, she told me about how her mother and her always fought over her father's death, and how she was always blamed for her mothers shitty life. She was eleven at the time. I said to myself I'd never let her feel bad for her life again, and held her close to me for four years? until it happened. Reaching up he wipes his tears from his face with his forearm, and then sniffs as his throat gulps and his whole body now echoes its ache through the small noises. She left me? dying, or so I thought. Now I know that’s not true. That TBC took her from me and kidnapped her holding her prisoner. I can't imagine how that feels? I mean, I've been here 5-6 weeks, but her? she's been stuck in here for over three years. That's an eternity to some. It's scary to think that had I never been trapped and taken away by him, I'd never had the opportunity to see her face again? It makes me sick to say it, but I have to thank the heartless bastard. So thanks? I hope you rot in hell. *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** Thud? Thud? Thud! The echoing of two heavy feet, linked by a chain, stop, and the clatter of the chain links are the only thing left ringing through the room. A chair slides from it's original position and screeches to an abrupt stop, before the chatters begin again, signifying the person has moved and sat down in the chair. Moving forward, the chair screeches as the clatters ring, and together the noises make a sound similar to nails on a chalkboard, if not more horrific. A slap of a hand on the cold steel table sends shivers up the spine, as it like all other things echo in the room. Then a slight clearing of the throat before a rough voice begins to speak. 'Well good morning Mr. Fristik, how are you?' Almost faint, like an angel, yet hoarse with a southern accent knocking on the back door. It slowly grows in volume as Jesse's eyes roll from their hazed glaze. Smacked upside the head, he shakes it off and comes back to reality staring at his surroundings with observing eyes. 'Where? where am I?' he barely mutters under his breathe. 'Save the small talk for later. I promised you something, and now? I'm going to deliver.' With that, the snap of a bolt is heard, and a door swings open. A large man comes through it, and behind him is a woman, skinny, almost deathly like. Her face frail, almost like porcelain. Her hair, black, yet straight and dull, showing no life. Her eyes bright and alive as they bulge out the slightest due to her thin face. The appearance is easiest to be described as the closest point to death while yet still being able to live normally. 'Julie? is that you?' The petite woman sits down at the chair she's instructed to, before staring at Jesse and giving a blank stare. 'Julie? it's me, it's Jesse. Your husband.' She blinks at him. Her face gives no sign of memory, or familiar knowledge towards the name or the person in front of her. She raises her eyebrow, slightly, as she concentrates. 'I? I was married?' Tears split at the corners of his eyes, running down his cheeks. Now more than ever, as they roll and roll until he can't control them anymore, and his eyes turn pink with a glisten blink of each eye. 'You bastard? what did you do to her? What did you do!' Going to stand up, he's shoved back down into his chair by the man who escorted Julie out. Balling his eyes out, he's too weak to fight back or even argue. Breaking down as he stares into her eyes and sees the love of his life, who he thought he'd never see again, yet now, she isn't even half the woman she was before, and her soul eaten away with the time she has spent in this pit of hell. '? Julie, I know you don't remember me? (clears throat) I can tell, this isn't going to effect you? but, I love you, and have always loved you. I could never love another woman like you. I'm so sorry I let you be taken away, and locked up. Had I known you were alive, I'd have looked for you. I know now though, I failed you as a friend, as a lover, and as? your husband.' Her eyes blink, still forgetful of who she's talking to, but to Jesse the words he say, he only prays they hit her in the mind, and stick with her. Bowing his head his tears wipe off on his arms, and he shakes his head, as he then looks up and stares at her once more, taking a final picture of who she is into mind, and then nodding to the guard to take her away. 'I love you?' His voice faint, like his hope. Choking on his final words as she is helped from her chair, he stares at the floor as his head hangs between his arms, and the tears swell so large they splash as they hit the floor. 'Our love will always be, so don't wait for me.' These words break Jesse's state of unstableness, and he stares up at her as she nods to him and then walks through the door. The jackal's eyes look lost, like he doesn't know what he just witnessed. Like a rare happening of seeing a second glimmer of hope, his eyes were lit up as the angel of a wife he remembered, for at least five seconds, was again herself. Staring off blankly does Jesse, just as she had done before. Now he, in the same trance ponders on every simple thought that comes through his mind, as if it were a perplexed equation of mathematics, that could be solved, yet? took that extra inch of thought to figure out. Yet, with all these things flooding in, his head is not quick enough to process and he just becomes a zombie like her, staring at the white walls, as he two is picked up and dragged off. She? she's alive. Thank you god. Thank you. *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** I can see hope now. Maybe not for everything, but for some things, it's clear. Crystal clear. I can’t keep living in the past, I can't keep worrying about these memories. I need to progress, warriors, they progress. They don't ponder they take action. Sure, they think things through, but they don't grieve over a loved one they lose. No, they use it as the fuel that boils their blood, that tickles their mental state enough to make them lose it, and then? they feed off it all, and becomes the warrior in which no man is capable of ever measuring up to be. I know deep down? I have the blood of a warrior in me. When I step into that ring, I have to quit grieving over her, and use the determination of what I am, and what's inside me, to fight past all this, and become the warrior of the octagon? His face mourns no more. No disgust, pity, sorrow, or grief lies on his shoulders to hold him down. Only the necessities to become what he believe he has always been destined to become, a warrior. Just as he goes to stand up and stretch, the door opens, and the light shines in, just merging with his body enough to show how white he truly is. Staring at the door with a hand over his eyes enough to shade the light, he notices who it is, TBC. 'Jesse? she has passed.' His face going blank, and then his eyes staring and narrowing as they do, he pauses before looking back up and nodding. 'She knew she was going to.' Nodding again, in reassurance to the fact and acceptance of her death, he looks back down at the floor. 'If it means anything to you? she had a smile on her face, as bright as the sunshine, just as she left us.' The jackal's face glimmers a small half smile, before letting it grow to the size of his face, and then flooding him with joy. 'Just like an angel.' The door slowly closes, and the room returns to darkness, but inside, the man who once was depressed, worried, and lost, has now found his way, and has found a reason to live. For her?
