The Max-Attack; Chapter 19

Date: Today The scene opens back up to the broken up brown bench. Roswell is sitting on it with a terrified look on his face and sweat dripping down his forehead. Roswell gets off the bench and starts walking down the sidewalk wiping the sweat off his face with a clothe he had in his pocket. “I think I know what this is about. It’s about me losing the Primstar title the first night I defended it. Now it’s coming back to haunt me.” Roswell walks down the sidewalk more as he is still talking to himself. “Well I’m not going to quit just because of that one title lost. I can do better. I know I can.” Roswell walks by a kid who is sitting on another broken up bench with headphones covering his ears. Max smiles and keeps on walking down the street now losing the vision of the flashback. Roswell walks past a book store and smiles again. Roswell turns a corner on the sidewalk and starts walking down the side of the sidewalk. Roswell walks by a confectionary as a window inside it explodes right onto Roswell’s head. Glass shatters everywhere as Roswell ducks and runs to a garbage can. Roswell then walks to the door of the confectionary and sees a blue armoured man threatening the Chinese man working at the confectionary. Roswell quickly opens the door and dives at The Blue Bat with all his strength taking The Blue Bat off his feet throw the counter of the confectionary. Roswell starts punching The Blue Bat viscously with rights and lefts. Roswell grabs the armour and throws it at the aisle taking everything off it. “Who are you” And what have you wanted with me since the beginning?!” “I’ll tell you over my dead body!” The Blue Bat kicks Max straight in the balls and flips onto his feet kicking Max in the face. The Blue Bat throws Max at a glass window and Roswell hits the sidewalk with a hard thud as blood starts flowing down Roswell forehead and arm. The Blue Bat jumps through the broken window and lands on Max’s chest taking all the air out of him. “I won’t stop until your in hell!” yells The Blue Bat. “I will never go to hell. Heaven the only place I’m going. Your going straight to hell!” says Max. Max punches The Blue Bat again and hits a uppercut to the jaw of The Blue Bat. Max takes The Blue Bat and starts punching him rapidly again making blood drip out of the blue armoured mask. Max flips The Blue Bat throw a different aisle and jumps to the one where he landed and starts uppercutting him. “As I said before. WHO ARE YOU” yells Max. “I’ll never tell you!” “Why not”! Is it someone who I know?!” “I have no fucking clue. Now shut the fuck up!” The Blue Bat monkeys flips Max into another aisle breaking all the stuff off of the wall. Suddenly the police walk into the confectionary and walk over to Max and The Blue Bat. But they aren’t there. The police officers look at the wall and see a huge hole in it. “They must have went through there” says one of the police officers. “Well obviously!” yells another police officer. “Just saying.” Outside The Blue Bat throws Max straight into a brick wall making it crack cause of the strength. Max runs back at The Blue Bat but he moves and runs into a metal pole. Max gets up and walks over to him. He tries a punch but it gets blocked and then he hits a kick connecting with The Blue Bat’s chest making a big hole in his armour. “For the last time. WHO THE HELL ARE YOU”!” yells Max as hard as he can. “WHY THE FUCK WOULD I TELL YOU!” yells The Blue Bat. Max takes his fist and lines his knuckles up. He punches at The Blue Bat as hard as he can and makes a hole hin the blue armoured mask. The Blue Bat falls to the ground with blood all over the sidewalk. Max walks over to him and starts to undo the blue mask. SCENE FADES
