Date: 5/25/04 I know what you're thinking Train, WHY? You want to know WHY. The question shouldn't be WHY, it should be WHEN! I told you a week ago that I wasn't a team player, I'm not a guy that works with someone or for someone Train and well kid, you were basically at the wrong place at the wrong time. But as I said, the question isn't WHY, it's WHEN! When will I get my hands on you, when will you let me break your jaw and when can I make you bleed! The scene opens up in a local bar in Dallas, J-Gonz has been having a bit too many drinks and is way past his limit. He stumbles back and forth on his stool in front of the bar as a short midget sits beside him drinking a glass of milk. 'So let me get his straight Ralph, you have a six inch penis? Brother that's bigger then your arm! It's bigger then your legs for goodness sakes.' 'Well like they say brother, big things come in small packages!' 'Well you know the obvious question, if you really got it show it.' Being too drunk to realize how loud he is being, J-Gonz has managed to grab the attention of other drinkers around the bar and they all jump in unison as they chant, 'SHOW IT! SHOW IT! SHOW IT!' Ralph stands on the bar and begins to unzip his pants and finally whips out what is clearly a six inch penis. Then just as the people gasp in shock J-Gonz pulls out a pocket knife and slices the penis. With a thump it drops to the ground and the people around him shout in horror as the penis drops and the blood oozes out. Ralph in horror and pain falls off the bar to the ground. 'You little piece of crap! You had a six inch chicken but now you got a headless dove! Pinche puto! What are you fools looking at eh? You got a problem with me? You got something you need to say? Chocolate, blow this place up!' A humungous man steps from behind his chair and pulls out a machine gun shooting first the bar and then the people attempting to run out of the bar. From behind Chocolate another man appears with a torch blazer and begins lighting up the oozing alcohol and the stench of burning alcohol, blood and wood mix in the air. J-Gonz in his unstained white suit walks out the door summong Chocolate and the other man. 'You boys did a good job. CSI won't even be able figure that one out, they'll see that wee-wee on the floor and a dead midget...AH!' Out of nowhere the midget that had his penis chopped off runs and dives at J-Gonz stabbing a knife into the side of J-Gonz face. Gonz grabs the midget and slings him against the wall. Ralph slithers down holding his side only to have J-Gonz jump in an start kicking his side and head. 'You stupid freak! Who do you think I am! Look at me! You sliced my cheeck puto! Get up! Get up! Get up bastard!' In anger J-Gonz pulls out a small pistol and blast three shots into the head of the midget. The holes pour out blood as J-Gonz smiles putting the pistol aside. 'Boss you need some help, you want us to take ya to the hospital?' 'They'll ask to many questions and we can't risk that, lets get out of here.' 'What you gonna do boss?' 'I'm gonna go home Choclate and you and Paco are going to bring me my box and then leave me alone. This aint nothing that a little coke can't take care of.' 'Boss you're bleeding bad, and your suit got stained!' 'Ah crap! My suit, that stupid midget! You know what bro, I'm gonna name that little homes Jonathan Drachen. Cause that's what Drachen is to me, he's a midget that want's to take me down but can't see me homes!' 'Ah boss, you need to see a doctor, don't worry about Drachen, boss...boss!' The loss of blood has cause J-Gonz to pass out leaving him in a pull of blood on the sidewalk. Choclate and Paco attempt to help him up as the scene fades away.
