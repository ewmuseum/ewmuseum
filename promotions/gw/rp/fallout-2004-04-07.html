Fallout

7 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04.07.04 In the ring, 'American Youth' Josh stands triumphant with his hands held in the air as 'The Red' by Chevelle erupts over the roaring crowd. As the camera zooms out to a wide shot showing Josh making his way to the turnbuckle and throwing his arms up in victory, 'Superstar' Ken Partridge Jr is getting to his feet, pulling on the ring apron with 'Clash of the Titans V' etched across it. He pulls himself into the ring and stands holding his ribs as he waits for Josh to descend from the ropes. As the American Youth does so and turns around, the music stops and the two meen stand staring at each other in the center of the ring. Not much is being said before you see Ken nodding his head. He reaches out and grabs Josh's right arm and holds it high into the atmosphere as 'The Red' picks back up. When the American Youth's hand is brought back down, the two men hug in the middle of the ring and you can almost read Ken's lips as he informs Josh of how proud he is of him. After the embrace, Ken once again shoots Josh's arm high into the air. Suddenly, the sound becomes a little distorted and the picture becomes a little blurry before it becomes obvious that we are watching Ken Partridge Jr's monitor in The Elite's personal room in the back of the Global Arena. As the camera continues to zoom back, Ken can be seen with a half smile on his face sitting on a white Italian leather sofa. Ken: Damn... I let it slip right through my fingers. Ken gives a slight laugh before shaking his head. Ken: Hell, he deserved it. He deserved it more than anyone else in the locker room. Just as Ken finishes, the door opens and Ace Harding steps into the room, immediatly bringing Ken to his feet. The two men stand motionless looking into one another's eyes before either speaks. Ken: What do you want, Harding? Ace smirks a bit before responding in a sarcastic manner. Ace: Harding? Oh, am I not on a first name basis with you anymore? Ken: Get on with it. Harding becomes much more serious this go round and the anger can almost be seen in his eyes. Ace: We've got a match Monday... me and you... against two low life up-and-comers. All I need to know is whether or not I'm gonna' have to carry your dead ass through the match. Because, if that's the case, don't even bother showing up. Ken: I've got a job to do, just like you, Harding. You know as well as anyone that I'll be doing my part in the match. Hell, I'll probably be doing yours as well. Ace and Ken become even more intense. Ace: Those are big words coming from someone's mouth that couldn't even win the Global Rumble. Ken: Well, at least I could get them out. I'm sure you would have had a bit of a tougher time considering I kicked your teeth down your throat to eliminate you. Ace: So it wasn't an accident! Ken: No, Ace, it WAS an accident... I just don't regret it, that's all. The look on your face when you finally regained consciousness, when you realized what had happened... it was priceless. In fact, Harding, I believe the only other time I've seen that look on your face was after I made you tap-out to The Epidemic in Power House and retaining my Grappling Gods Championship. Ace becomes infuriated with Ken's comments, but it's hard to tell through his demeanor. Ace: The only thing that kept me from grabbing that strap was politics. Ken: No, Ace, the only thing that kept you from becoming Grappling Gods Champion... or any champion, for that matter... was, is, and always will be... you! You can't step up, Harding... you simply can't do it. Even with help, you can't seem to reach that next rung of the ladder. I brought you here to Global Wrestling... I made you the enforcer of the greatest damn thing to EVER happen to professional wrestling, excluding myself, of course, in The Elite. I deliver you opportunity after opportunity after opportunity... and you can't seem to do anything but fumble the ball after I snap it to you. Well, Ace, you've got another opportunity at Evolution. An opportunity to prove that you belong where you are when we face off against Arik Idol and Josh Kosidlo. These two aren't on our level, Harding, but they have the potential to be. Don't take them lightly. You do your part and don't worry about me... I'll do mine. With that statement, Ken leaves Ace standing and heads to the door. He opens it and is on his way out before leaving Harding with one final tidbit. Ken: Don't forget whose team you're on... got it! Ken exits and lets the door slam behind him. In the room, Ace stands motionless, brething much the same as he was when he entered... almly and cooly. Suddenly, he picks up a vase sitting on the coffee table Ken had his feet propped up on and tosses it to the wall, meeting it's shatters with a loud roar.
