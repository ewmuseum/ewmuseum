Histories #1

9 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09092004 BeepBeep. The noise is a constant. A reassuring sound. The sound of hope. The sound of faith. The sound of my will. Me willing the noise to continue. Me willing the noise to speed up. Me willing the noise to go away and the person on the other end of the machinery to wake up. BeepBeep. But the noise continues. I am thankful for that much at least. BeepBeep. One such noise that I do not miss is that of the respirator. The constant hissing in and out, almost sounding like a person with asthma trying to breathe. Luckily, she was able to outgrow that very quickly. The doctors, the pricks that they are, god’s gift in white lab coats, assured me that this was a great sign and that she should snap out of it in a matter of hours? BeepBeep. “days” BeepBeep. “weeks” BeepBeep. That was four months ago. BeepBeep. And this is where I spend most of my time. BeepBeep. Next to her. BeepBeep. Next to my love. BeepBeep. Beyond the machinery. Beyond the heart monitor. Beyond the IVs. Beyond the catheter. Beyond the logistics of it all hides my fianc’e, lost in a world of endless dreams, hoping to find her way out into the land of reality. Hoping to find her way back to me. BeepBeep. I place my hand on her belly. BeepBeep. Hoping to find her way back to our unborn child. BeepBeep. Everyday I ask myself if she knew before the “accident.” And everyday I tell myself that she would’ve told me if she knew. At least, that’s what I like to think. BeepBeep. I say the word “accident” as if this “accidentally” occurred. I could use a word like “unexpected” since it “unexpectedly” occurred, but that wouldn’t be right either. Maybe something harsher would sound better. Something like “attack” or “assault” or “unsuccessful hit.” Any of the three would successfully describe this situation. BeepBeep. This’event? I like that word. It takes away from the ferocity of the situation and makes it sound calmer. Makes it sound more like an everyday occurrence. Anyways. This event occurred because of me. Because of my profession. To quote the most famous of all of the sailors, “I am what I am and that’s all that I am.” And what am I, pray tell? BeepBeep. I am a monster. BeepBeep. I live, eat, breathe, and sleep the destruction of the dreams and well-beings of other people. And I am good at what I do. I am not perfect. I am not the best of the best of the best. But’I am pretty damn close, if I do say so myself. The path that I have chosen is not the simple life. It is wrought full of danger. If I very well sucked at my job, I would not be sitting here, next to her. BeepBeep. In fact, proof of my imperfection is laying here next to me. She is here because of me. Because of my line of work. I placed her in harm’s way. She is here because someone felt they needed vengeance. She is here because someone tried to take away my life, my existence. Instead all they got was a few ribs, a sprained wrist, a mild concussion, and the sight in my left eye, which did eventually return to me. BeepBeep. I grasped her limp hand, squeezing it tightly, trying to will my soul, my life-force into her, to re-animate her body. To make her come alive again in my arms. BeepBeep. This attempt, like all of the others, fails. BeepBeep. The people that did this are gone. Scattered into the wind in a torrid of explosions, bullets, sirens, blood and death. Lots and lots of death. I made sure of that. Glocks loaded with mercury filled hollowpoints. Molded blocks of C4, the world’s greatest explosive. Carbon-bladed knives dark as the shadows themselves. Most never knew what hit them. Those that did know didn’t live long enough to tell the tale. BeepBeep. And now I am at another turning point in my life. My two friends are inaccessible to me at the moment. One gone for an extended vacation, the other suspended and stripped, wallowing in sorrow and booze. The path that I will take, the path that I must take, I cannot take alone. I will need someone to help me. BeepBeep. For support. BeepBeep. For backup. BeepBeep. I know just who to call. BeepBeep. But it will cost a shitload of money. That, and I don’t know how well he will be received. BeepBeep. Ahhh fuck it. BeepBeep. Fuck “em if they can’t take a joke. BeepBeep.
