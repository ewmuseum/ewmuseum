Shed 10 pounds Part 1...Call from an old friend Part 2

9 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/9/03 Words from last night's Evolution open the scene, as still pictures of Ice's reaction are shown along with those words. 'The Upper Echelon's Tyler Pierce has been placed on the inactive list until further notice. With unforeseen circumstances, it is beyond our control that this situation has arisen. With this development, The Upper Echelon and Global Wrestling have no choice but to hand the Cruiserweight Championship off to the current Contender to the title, Ice. We apologize for this incident and hope to have an official statement for you sometime in the near future.' The Global-Tron flickers up and Christina is sitting there. 'Christina: I am sorry to do this, But what just transpired is null and void. Titles are meant to be won not handed over. Ice's victory will remain over Tyler Pierce do to forfeit. Normally the title would change hands in this situation… However there is a two hundred and thirty five pound limit on this division. Ice by weigh in tonight is ten pounds over the weight limit.. So the GW Cruiserweight championship is not vacant! However… Master of the Mat Four. If Ice can shed the ten pounds He will be placed in the triple threat match making it a fatal four way for the Cruiserweight title. IN the match will also be the returning Davis Fury, Max Roswell and Deacon Holmes.. The match will be a TLC match. Thank you.' The scene fades in to Ice, in a plain grey tank top, and black NIKE shorts. Ice is standing in the middle of his gym overseeing all the equiptment, with a blank stare and expression on his face. The door opens, and Ice's gaze out into nowhere is broken as the beautiful blond Star walks in, wearing a pair of short white shorts, and a matching sports bra. Her black streaked blond hair is up in a high pony tail. The beautiful blond walks up to Ice with a huge smile, from ear to ear, and gives him a small peck on the cheek. Ice looks down at her, but doesn't respond. He simply turns his head away from her, and walks out of the door. Jaded and confused Star watches as the door closes behind Ice. The scene fades out. ________________________________________________________________________ The scene fades back in, outside of Ice's gym, where Ice is outside all alone sitting, and stretching. Ice is sitting on the ground with his legs out stretched in front of his body. Slowly he leans towards the left leg, and holds. He does the same to the right leg. Ice finishes his streching, and stands up. He walks over to a green bench and begins to hop over the bench. Up and down, back and forth, over and over Ice jumps over the small green bench. Ice does this jumping exercise until he is abruptly stopped by Star, who comes running out of the gym with telephone in hand. 'Ice! Ice! Phone!' Star runs up to Ice, and thrusts the phone into his chest. Again with an expressionless face Ice looks down at the phone thrusted into his chest and then up into Star's emerald green eyes. 'Hello..' Ice stands and listens as the person on the other end of the phone speaks, for what seems like an hour. During this time Ice tilts his head from side to side, like he is bothered and annoyed by the voice on the other end of the phone. While Ice is doing that Star is hopping around like a jack rabbit, trying to find out who it is Ice is speaking.....listening to. 'Yeah....sure....whatever....I know, you didn't need to call to tell me something I already knew about. Now if you don't mind, I've got some things I have to do. Don't call back. We'll see each other around, I'm sure of it.' After speaking Ice hangs up the phone, Star leaps into his face. 'Who was that? Who were you speaking to? Who was on the phone? Tell me PPPPPPLLLLLEAAAAAASSSSSEEEEEE!' Ice shakes his head at her childness, and a small cocky smirk crosses his face. 'Let's just say it was an old friend who just wanted to say hello.' Ice places his left arm over Star's small petite framed shoulders, and escourts her back into the gym. The scene fades to black.
