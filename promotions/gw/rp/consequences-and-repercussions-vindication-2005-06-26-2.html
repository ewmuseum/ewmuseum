Consequences and repercussions: Vindication.

26 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 26th, 2005. Consequences and repercussions: Vindication. ---- 10:33 PM. June 26th, 2005. SoHo, NY. Howie Branner, professional shock-jock of KT81 - 107.2, celebrity radio - often kept late nights and this lovely June evening appeared to be one of them. He sat in the studio, oblivious to the music playing through the radio station. The song was 'Deadbeat Holiday' a throwback from 5 years ago, courtesy of Green Day; Billie Joe Armstrong's vocals rang out as his fellow bandmates kicked in their individual talents. Howie's long brown hair fell across his face, a mullet and a mustache acted as evidence that he was guilty of living in the 1980's - the orange jumpsuit and hi-top Nike's he wore only served to further convict him of falling away from the times, if we could see his collection of cassette tapes at home we would all be forced to agree that he was in dire need of some technological upgrading. Technological issues aside, one must admire the work ethic of Howie Branner even if they don't care for the morality of his character, or the brash manner in which he represents himself and his work. Hardly a stranger to late nights, Howie was known for putting in an immense amount of effort into his material and priding himself on the fact that it was all original, never mind how disgusting and repulsive it may be. 'Howard Stern with balls', was how he often referred to himself. Unlike Stern, Branner was unafraid of tackling certain issues with guests, he never compromised his beliefs or backed down and the FCC hated him for it. He considered Stern a rival, granted Stern had more fame and financial resources than Branner did, but Branner made up for it with exceptional creativity and controversy. In reality Stern would always be more famous, yet their bank accounts would arguably juxtapose evenly if Branner wasn't involved in no less than four - five lawsuits a year. Branner laughed at getting sued, he was used to it by now, it had became a well known song and dance. Despite his numerous drawbacks, Branner did have an enviable imagination, a multi-million dollar imagination, even if he didn't dress the part. Raising his left hand Branner rubbed his temple wearily as he stared down at a script in front of him, releasing the pencil with his right hand Branner drummed his fingers against the table. His brown eyes followed the pencil as it rolled away, coming to a halt when it bumped into a pack of cigarettes on the table, as the orbs of Branner fell upon the cancer sticks he smiled. 'Best idea I've had all night.' Branner said with a smile, speaking to no one but himself. Pushing himself to a vertical base, Branner's right hand snaked out and snatched up the pack of Camels and the red lighter on top, walking out of the studio he passed the nearby control room, where the night DJ - a 20 year old college student by the name of Dean - glanced up at him. 'Gonna' step out for a smoke, D.' Branner announced as he passed by. 'Cool man.' Was Dean's answer, not even bothering to look up from the newly acquired Sony PSP in his hands, obviously enraptured by the moving pixels in front of him. Branner continued down the hall to a door at the end, a glowing red 'exit' sign hanging above it, attached to the plaster ceiling. Reaching the door, Howie shoved it open with his unoccupied left hand, he slipped through it, his 5'9, 150 pound wiry frame hardly filling the passage. Lazily, he allowed the door to close behind him as he stepped out into the darkness of a New York alley. The night was dark, the dim illumination from the singular streetlight in the alley didn't seem to change that fact in the least. A cool breeze was blowing from the West, scattering debris down the alley, Branner's ears picked up the sound of an empty aluminum can rattling down the alley, driven by the wind, another sound, papers rustling as the wind sent them on their destined course. Ignoring the sounds surrounding him Branner makes a sound of his own as he extracts a cig and flicks his lighter on, lighting the coffin nail and placing it in his lips as he drops the pack of cigarettes into the left front pocket of his jumpsuit, the lighter follows suit. Running a hand through his - 'business in the front, party in the back' - mullet, Howie Branner took a puff off of his cigarette inhaling deeply before blowing the smoke out into the New York air. As he smoked, Howie fell into thought, deep thought...he didn't notice the bluish gray eyes locked on him. ---- I sat crouched atop a second story building, staring down at infamous radio DJ, Howie Branner a man that had been partially responsible for telling the world of my affair with Rosa Padilla and the illegitimate child that we had conceived. Howie had damaged my reputation, thrown mud at my name and wounded my ego, all of the aforementioned was forgivable; besmirching the recently deceased Rosa Padilla was not. Somewhere I could almost imagine Rosa's beautiful face staring down from the heavens, her heart broken by the lines of action that I had taken, sorrowful and deeply burdened. It was an agonizing thought for me, one which I quickly turned away from as I returned my attention to Branner. He sat there in the empty alley, inhaling doses of poison generously as smoke wisped around his face, the smoke casting an eerie cloud about his visage. His eyes were downcast, pointed away from my direction, but it wouldn't matter, he would never see me anyway...at least not until it was too late. My attire for tonight was quite unusual, black pants, belonging to the dobak issued when I received my instructors certificate in Taekwondo, a sleeveless black skin-tight shirt, black Chuck Taylor's, and most unique of all...a mask. The mask was black, dual rings of silver circled the eyes, a pair of bluish gray eyes - generally soft and kind, yet cold and hard tonight - stared from behind the mask. Streaks of white traveled vertically down the face of my mask, the top was open, my shaggy black hair streaked with shades of sterling silver and vibrant blue spilling out messily. I had originally worn the mask in Mexico, a gift from the Padilla family upon the completion of my training. The mask hadn't been worn in 3 years, yet the history alone kept it near and dear to my heart and as I donned the mask tonight I felt a certain vindication for the justice I was prepared to serve. The final piece of the puzzle was a bo, clutched firmly in both hands. The bo had often been a featured attraction in weapons competition at Taekwondo tournaments, tonight, for the first time in my life I would find out exactly how good I really was with the wooden staff, but I wouldn't learn nearly as much as Howie Branner. ---- Exhaling a puff of cancerous smoke, Branner squinted, trying to read the label of a can as it blew by, clinking on the cement. He stared at it for a moment before giving up, and allowing his mind to drift back to what it had been on moments earlier. 'American Youth' Josh. Branner had his fun with Josh, screwed with his head, toyed with his broken heart but that wasn't enough, Branner wasn't going to let it go like that, he had more mind games to play. It had been a stroke of luck that Josh had been scheduled to appear on the show the same exact day the magazine had debuted on newsstands. Branner was thankful to the dirtsheets, he knew he could always count on WIN - Wrestling International News to provide him with all of the dirty little secrets, but they had delivered a gold mine in Josh's case. A detailed story of how Josh had impregnated his mentors wife, then skipped out on the kid, leaving Jose Padilla believing the child was his own. The story itself was great, but numerous photographs of Josh & Rosa holding hands and kissing, showed visual proof that American Youth wasn't quite the model American that he pretended to be, he had flaws too, countless flaws. Howie Branner smiled, his teeth were a tone of pale yellow, stained from far too many cigarettes and pots of coffee, it was an ugly smile for a man with an ugly personality; it fit. With pleasure at hand Branner thought deeply of how he could torture Josh, he had already emotionally ambushed him once on a national radio show and he knew a repeat of this wasn't possible, yet he had to do something. Branner thought deeply, racking his conniving little brain, looking for a road map to point him in the proper direction on his journey, this sick quest, to destroy the reputation of a man held so near and dear in the hearts of many. It had always been a sick obsession of his to tear the idols down in front of their adoring masses. As much as everyone loved heroes, Branner loved watching them fall. Perhaps it was insecurity, or the very fact that it made him feel important, worthwhile, that he inflicted such emotional trauma upon celebrities. He laughed, they were far from helpless, they deserved to rot like the filthy rich dogs that they are. The more celebrities that got arrested, divorced, or found themselves in bankruptcy; the more fun Branner had, and Branner intended to have a lot of fun with 'American Youth' Josh, the so-called posterboy of America's youth. 'What can I hit Josh with?' Branner mused to himself, flicking the remains of his dying cigarette out into the alley, watching as the glowing embers scattered across the concrete. Leaning back against the building he extracted another cancer stick in addition to his lighter, and he promptly brought the evil member to life, once again polluting both the atmosphere and his lungs with the demonic item. 'WMW...' Branner thought to himself. 'He has a huge match coming up against 5 of WMW's top stars, but where can I take that? The only one he has history with is Ice...' Howie Branner reflected back upon the long standing rivalry of Josh and Ice, it had began in 2002 and carried into 2005. Three violent years, blood had been shed in numerous arenas across the globe, each man stopping at nothing to destroy the other. Ice had even been responsible for bringing a halt to Josh's infamous 184 day World title reign in Global Wrestling, ending the longest streak in the history of Global, an impressive feat to say the least. Josh's amazing title reign had captured the hearts of fans across the World, turning him into a true staple of the Wrestling industry and gaining him recognition as one of the top talents in the game today. Maybe Branner could use Ice as a vital member of this psychological chess game. Ice would be more than happy to inflict a little pain and suffering upon his nemesis, regardless of whether the pain and suffering was physical or strictly emotional. He would have to think about it, but sooner or later, a genius plan would fall upon him...it always did. ---- As Branner brought another cigarette to life I could see an ugly snarl across his face, a hideous expression that appeared to be his version of a smile, a smile that I would love to rearrange with the bo clutched firmly in my hands, but first things first. Reaching down, I locked a gloved hand onto a video camera, feeling around gently before finding a wire in the back, as expected. With a violent jerk I ripped the yellow wire from its base, killing the cameras feed. I ushered a silent prayer to God in an attempt to remain undiscovered by whomever may be inside, their eyes no-doubt focused on the camera, I knew this would have to be quick. Glancing down at a fire escape below me I rolled off of the edge of the second story building and landed on my feet atop the fire escape, my Chuck's came down on the steel, making a slight 'clang', hardly noticeable, but apparently Howie Branner caught it. 'What in the...' Branner began, flicking his gaze skyward, his eyes sweeping past me as I hugged the shadows. Branner stared up at the fire escape - directly above him - for a moment before shaking his head, dismissing it as paranoia and returning to the deadly task of smoking his cigarette. The second Branner dropped his gaze I sprang into action, throwing my free left hand onto the guardrail and leaping off to the cement below, I landed perfectly before dropping to a knee and rolling twice to avoid being seen. A suspicious and surprised Branner turned quickly, adamant that he had heard something, his glare penetrated the darkness where I had been mere seconds ago, the only sound in the air was a gasp of panic from his lungs as he looked around warily. 'Over here.' I snarled, my voice a cold whisper. Branner spun around, his eyes wide as they met mine...filled with anger and hate. There I stood, face to face with the man who had exposed my sins to the world, torn me apart in front of the nation and made a mockery of my very being, a bo staff was clenched tightly in both hands, ready to be brought into action at any moment. 'Who...' He started, before staring me over, noticing my tattoos he made a quick match. ...what are you doing, Josh?' 'You know what's lacking in society today, Howie?' I asked, a cruel smile tugging at the corner of my lips, needless to say his smirk had faded by this point. 'Justice.' 'Justice? Now hold on a second, what I did was completely legal and if I remember correctly you were the one who put everyone in danger, they had to carry you out in a rage!' Branner protested, his ever-confident voicing showing a strain of fear in it's tone. 'It's a shame Howie, if you had apologized I was going to play nice.' I shot, raising the bo. 'Touch me, I'll sue!' He threatened weakly. 'You really think I care about another lawsuit? When I'm finished with you a lawsuit will hardly be compensation, threaten me all you want Howie, but I'm the man holding all the cards.' I said, twirling the bo in my hands. 'Don't even come near me with that thing...' Howie snapped, his eyes wide with terror, as they searched for the door, much to his chagrin, I separated him from the door. Without a single word being uttered I lashed out with the bo and caught Howie behind the left patella, dropping him to a single knee his face turning white with pain. 'Stop!' He cried out, naturally I ignored him, cracking him across the chest and stomach with the primitive weapon, his eyes rose up to meet mine...begging, pleading...I ignored them. Stretching the crude weapon forth I playfully jabbed Branner in the pit of his throat, causing him to cough violently as he staggered back onto his posterior, only his elbows keeping him semi-vertical. 'You can't do this to me!' He cried out in protest, tears beginning to roll down his face. 'Why not Howie?' I asked, poking him with the end of the staff. 'Because this is your job? Because this, in a metaphorical sense, is what you've done to countless people over the years? You don't like having the roles reversed, do you Howie? 'Please...' He begged, before receiving another stiff shot from the end of the staff, directly in the pit of his throat. 'I'll give you the same amount of mercy you've given me...' I sneered in controlling arrogance, raising my hand, commanding Branner to stand. He followed the command slowly, hesitantly, his face puzzled, obviously confused by my last statement. As he reached his feet, the statement was understood, my point was made... ...with a violent swing of the staff that resulted in a skull-crushing blow to Branner's left temple! Branner's eyes closed immediately as he took one step to the right before collapsing in an unconscious heap, his body unmoving as his head ricocheted off of the cement, blood spilling from the left side. Dropping to a knee, I spoke, a smile on my face. 'Consider justice served.'
