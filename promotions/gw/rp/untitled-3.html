“Ya know, sometimes I lay awake at night and think to myself...” Deacon starts, holding Rhaiza close as they cuddle in the silent room. He holds her close, pressing her body firmly against his, their hearts beating in synch... he once again begins to whisper in her ear. “You’re the sexiest, funniest, smartest, most talented and charismatic person I’ve ever known, and what a great loss it would be if anything were ever to happen to you... I guess what I’m trying to say is... You’re my world, and I don’t know what I’d do if I could never see you again.” Although obscured by the darkness, Rhaiza’s cheeks become rosy as she blushes due to Deacon’s sweet whisperings. Still remaining in his arms, Rhaiza turns to face her boyfriend and begins nuzzling his neck while purring softly before gazing into his eyes. Deacon’s eyes meet with hers somewhere in the darkness and they share a loving embrace as Holmes gently brushes her hair aside while leaning in to once again whisper softly into her ear. “And then I think to myself...” Deacon starts once more, this time pausing to look into her eyes. “Yes?” Rhaiza inquires in a sultry tone, grinding her silky smooth curvaceous body against Deacon’s as she gently bites his ear. She had obviously been turned on by the sweet nothings he’d been whispering into her ear, and he had no intention of stopping now. Holmes returns her sentiment by gently sucking her necking before kissing his way back up to her ear, causing her toes to curl in the process, and replying. “Damn I’ve gotta get that mirror off my ceiling.” Nothing... Everything stopped. No more grinding. No more kissing. No more sucking. In a matter of seconds Rhaiza had made the transformation from a red hot love making machine that was polished, primed, and ready for action... Into a cold... dead... fish. Falling away from Holmes and rolling onto her back, she lay there stiff as a steel frame, looking up into the ceiling in silence as Deacon cackled and hissed to himself uncontrollably like the cartoon dog Muttley just after his master Dick Dastardly had lost yet another race. Then all of a sudden she noticed something. In the darkness it was barely visible, but something was definitely there. It was about the same size and shape as the bed, and stood out from the rest of the ceiling. Then, sitting up halfway in order to get a closer look she realized exactly what it was. A mirror. With no hesitation, in a single fluid motion Rhaiza reached back behind herself and snatched the pillow he head had rested on only a moment earlier. Then with a devilish grin, she slammed the large overstuffed pillow down onto Deacon’s face. The blow only causes Holmes? laughter to become even more pronounced, and impairs him to the point that he can do little to guard himself against the second. Deacon’s laughter proves contagious as Rhaiza erupts with laughter herself while diving on top of Holmes. The two playfully wrestle and struggle against each other while rolling back and forth wallowing in the sheets. Then pinning her boyfriend against the mattress with a giddy shriek, Rhaiza gazes into Deacon’s eyes before spouting “I’m not Josh, I’ll kick your ass!” while giggling loudly. “Oh really?” Holmes snaps back. “Well then good thing for me I know your weakness!” With a lightning quick flick of his wrists Holmes frees himself from Rhaiza’s grasp and wastes no time in hastily turning the tables. Deacon immediately goes to work, rapidly twitching his fingers as he places his hands against Rhaiza’s ribs, he sends her into a fit of convulsive laughter. Her posture instantly melts, allowing Holmes to gain the upper hand as he continues to tickle her while rolling over and straddling her. Now on top, Deacon continues his attack while calling out in an announcer’s voice. “One! Two! Three! THAT’S IT! IT’S OVER! DEACON HOLMES HAS WON THIS MATCH!” he exclaims with all the vigor of an excited fan. Rolling off of his girlfriend, Holmes climbs to his feet atop the bed and begins striking random poses while still proclaiming his greatness. “Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of this match, “The Poster Child for Don’t Try This at Home”, DEACON HOLMES!” With that, Deacon leaps off of the bed and onto the carpeted floor while simulating the noise of a roaring crowd. Rhaiza shakes her head with a smile at the antics of her boyfriend as he trots around the room as though he’d just won the world title, stopping to gesture to her each time he passes the bed. After finishing his victory celebration, Holmes adjusts his boxers while sauntering over toward the refrigerator. Once there he slowly pry’s the door open, allowing the sound of the door’s rubber gasket being pulled away from the fridge’s frame to be heard. The light emanating from the inside of the refrigerator causes Deacon’s fully dilated eyes, which had adjusted to the dark, to quickly contract, causing him to squint. Though somewhat blinded, Deacon’s hand still manages to deftly maneuver through the refrigerator, stopping once it finally falls upon it’s target. A carton of orange juice. Holmes removes the carton from the fridge and proceeds to guzzle all but the very last drop of it’s contents before turning toward the bed where his girlfriend still lies. “You want any?” A simple shaking of her head provides Deacon with Rhaiza’s answer, which in response to he shrugs before drinking the final sip of juice from the carton. Then examining the label, Holmes finds something he likes. “Florida’s finest.” He reads aloud from the orange juice carton. A thin smile forms on his face before he looks up at Rhaiza with an inquisitive look. “Maybe that should be my next tattoo.” With a seductive lick of her lips, Rhaiza slowly crooks her index finger three times before completely derailing Deacon’s train of thought. “Why don’t you come over here and prove it first?” That was all the motivation Holmes needed, causing him to nonchalantly toss the now empty orange juice container over his shoulder and knock the refrigerator door shut with a well placed back kick before rushing over to the bed and diving on top of Rhaiza. While kissing her on the neck Deacon remains himself and still finds time to talk a little trash. “You done messed up now, I got my second wind... girl I’m bout to work you like a part time job!” Just then a faint rattling can be heard coming from the front of the room and Deacon looks up from under the covers like a mongoose standing on it’s hind legs peering over grass in search of a snake. Holmes listened attentively as Rhaiza lay rigid and motionless, her eyes growing wide as she hears the sound too. Something finally clicks inside Deacon’s head and he identifies the noise as the door knob. He immediately turns his attention to it, and due to his eyes having adjusted to the darkness, Holmes is able to notice that the knob is jiggling slightly as it slowly makes a counter clockwise rotation. An impetuous flame begins to burn in Deacon’s eyes as he stares a hole in the door, which is now slightly ajar. A single slim line of luminescence creates a bright revealing stripe that extends all the way from the small crack between the door and the wall, to the bed where Deacon and Rhaiza now lay. Having seen enough, Holmes bursts from the bed appearing as little more than a black flash and a pair of Sean John boxers as he races toward the now quickly opening door. Upon reaching it, Deacon slams into the door shoulder first, causing it to shake violently upon impact. The reflection of the force from each side of the door causes Deacon, and most likely the intruder on the other side of the door to stumble backward as a result of being forced onto their heels. Rhaiza watches the ordeal with a facial expression most closely resembling that of a deer trapped in the headlights of an oncoming truck. Showcasing the agility he’s renowned for, as well as a bit of the speed that constantly makes up for his lack of any formal wrestling knowledge by quickly regaining his balance and charging the door once more. This time rather than straight on he moved to the left, where the opening was and as the door swung open once more, in the blink of an eye Holmes had reached out and snatched his trespasser by the shirt collar and yanked him into the room. Whirling around with the man still in hand, Deacon forced him back first against the door, closing it forcefully on impact. The quick opening and closing of the door allowed the full luminescence of the sun’s rays to enter the room before being cut off all together created something of a brilliant flash of light, which caused her pupils to instantly contract, keeping her eye from being able to see in the dark any longer. Across the room, which was now completely black for her now, a struggle could be heard between Deacon and the unknown intruder. Fearing the worst, she throws the covers off of herself and slowly makes her way to a light switch, feeling her way around along the way. As she continues through the room, across the way random profanities can be hear being exclaimed by her boyfriend amidst several rumbles and knocks against the wall as each man battles it out against the other. With all hell seemingly breaking loose in front of the doorway and her level of panic critical, Rhaiza’s hand finally stumbles upon what it’d been frantically feeling along the wall for all the while. A light switch. With a fretful flick of the switch the entire room is suddenly illuminated. Rhaiza then quickly snatches one of Deacon’s twin custom desert eagles up off of the counter while whirling around to turn her focus to the front of the room where suddenly all had gone silent. She immediately took aim, and trained the pistol on where she’d heard the struggle going on. Then immediately burst into a fit of laughter. In front of the door stood Deacon Holmes with none other than John Zero in some sort of odd choke hold. Zero clutched a partially obscured object in his hand. Both men were squinting as a result of the dramatic change in lighting while breathing heavily due to their struggle. Through half cracked eyes, each man looks over to a wildly laughing Rhaiza with a loaded gun in her palm, then at each other. They then immediately relinquish their grips on each other as their eyes continue to adjust. Still catching their breath, each man regains his normal posture and looks at the other with a faint smile. All of a sudden Holmes catches a glimpse of what had been in John’s hand, a knife. Deacon quickly points a finger in Zero’s face with a concerned look in his eye. “You were gonna stab me!” he exclaims in an accusatory tone. Zero quickly tucks the knife away in it’s sheath and at something of a loss for words, John starts off “Well...”, his eyes scanning the room for something of a scape goat. Zero’s eyes eventually find their way over to Rhaiza, who then quickly goes to conceal the rather large gun behind her back and begins whistling while looking around innocently. Having found the necessary ammunition, Zero continues “She was gonna shoot me!”. A beaming ear to ear toothy grin took over Deacon’s face along with the look something like that of a proud father. Holmes walks over to Rhaiza, who’s still clutching the pistol and wraps his arms around her from behind in a loving embrace, giving her a kiss on the neck before whispering into her ear “That’s my girl.” In an instant, Deacon then returns his attention to John while peering over her shoulder. “That’s what you get for not knocking!” he says, quickly reverting back to his normal self. Zero shoots Holmes one of his trademark blank stares before re-adjusting his shirt to the manner in which it’s supposed to be worn and stretching out any wrinkles. He then looks around as though he’d never been inside the guest slash pool house that Deacon now inhabits before replying “Last time I checked this was my house...” in a coy tone. Holmes quickly cuts him off and interjects “Stop right there, cause if I hear you say anything to the effect of as long as I live under your roof I have to live by your rules I’m gonna be forced to kick your ass... dad? John simply rolls his eyes at the thought before replying. “No, actually I came to invite you two to have lunch with Victoria and I. I also...” Zero is once again cut off by Deacon, who seems taken aback by John’s invitation. He begins in a rather surprised voice “Lunch?” before yawning and scratching his back then continuing “Who the hell is eating at this hour?”. With the lights on, Deacon’s lax posture most likely brought on by fatigue can be seen, implying that he most likely hadn’t gotten much sleep. Zero shakes his head with something of a smirk before calmly walking over to one of the windows inside the room and yanking back the black curtain covering it to reveal that the sun was shining rather brightly and it was a beautiful day outside. “Early huh?” he says, moving to the next window and uncovering it at well. “So like I was saying before I was so rudely interrupted, I kinda need to talk to you D...” As Zero spoke these words, he glanced at Rhaiza, silently signaling that they needed to be alone. Taking the hint, Rhaiza slipped back into bed as Holmes walked over to Zero, who ushered him out the door and followed close behind him. The two men stepped out onto the pool deck and took seats at the nearby bar. “So what is it?” Deacon asks, his overwhelming curiosity coming through his voice. “Well, you know me and Victoria have gotten pretty serious...” Zero starts, causing Holmes to cock an eyebrow as he wonders just where this is going. “Yeah...” he interjects, trying to usher the conversation along. John complies, and continues on “Well, I proposed... she said yes.”. With that, Deacon’s eyes both grow wide with what would appear to be amazement but apparently there’s a little something else mixed in with it. “WHAT!”” he exclaims, hopping off of his stool, causing Zero to crack a bit of a smile as he assumes that the young man is excited for him. “What the hell did you do that for!” Who the hell buys a cow when they’ve got a fridge full of free milk and a goddamn stockpile of the shit in a deep freezer in the basement!”” Holmes continues, obviously somewhat upset at what he feels to be stupidity. John shakes his head slowly at the animated nature of Deacon’s response before standing up himself. “Glad to have your support Deac..” he says calmly, walking back toward the sliding glass door at the rear of the house, Holmes watching him the entire time waiting to be told he’s been had. Upon reaching the door, Zero turns around with an inquiry. “So will we be seeing you two for lunch?” he asks. The thought of free food caused Deacon’s expression to change dramatically, and he quickly replied “Yeah, but I’ve gotta knock out the three S’s first.” John’s face contorted into a look of confusion after Holmes? response before turning around once more after sliding the door. “The three S’s?” he asks, looking at the half naked nineteen year old standing on his pool deck. Deacon smiled at the fact that John had no clue what he meant for before enlightening him. “Yeah, you know... shit, shower, and shave.” he said, turning both his palms upward and shrugging as if to imply that Zero should’ve known. With that, John gave him a wide eyed nod before slipping inside the house. Holmes then walked back into the guest area to find Rhaiza waiting for him under the covers. A cocky smile forms on Deacon’s face as he decides to lay into his girlfriend now that Zero is gone. “What the hell took you so long to hit the lights” You were scared weren’t you... sissy.” He said mockingly, flashing a smile at her to let her know he’s joking before continuing “And that deer caught in the headlights shit ain’t cute!” and laughing at his own comment. Rhaiza smiles a bit herself before posing a question of her own. “So you mean to tell me you’re never scared?” she asks, cocking an eyebrow at Deacon. “Only when you miss your period.” Holmes retorts almost immediately, causing Rhaiza to quickly grab a pillow from the bed and launch it in his direction. Holmes catches the pillow in mid air while running toward the bed and playfully dives on top of his girlfriend, using it to cushion the blow. Rhaiza laughs as she wrestles him off of her, causing him to roll to the side before leaning on her elbow and looking up into the ceiling then into her boyfriend’s eyes. “So how long has that mirror been there?” she asks, staring him dead in the eye with a failed attempt at a serious expression. Deacon shrugs before replying “It’s been there since I moved in, you just never noticed because we always do it doggystyle!” with a huge smile. They both begin to laugh as Rhaiza gives him a playful slap on the cheek before diving on top of him. Holmes quickly overpowers her and rolls over while yelling “Come here girl! I’ve got my second wind now... I’m bout to flip you like a car wreck!”
