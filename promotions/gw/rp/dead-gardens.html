Part I “Your eyes, they were my paradise; Your smile made my sun rise.” Madrid, Espa’a His angelic blue eyes flashed across the ancient structures of Madrid, their depths stirring with want. His existence had almost always been a lonely one, so loneliness was not particularly what he was lamenting. What pricked at him the most, what drove the stake so deeply within him was that things didn’t have to be this way, and it was his own fault that they were. He gritted his teeth together, and dug his pointed, perfectly groomed fingernails into the crumbling wall, immediately disliking how the dirt collected under them. The wind whipped around the corner of the building across the street and wafted its fingers gently through his sable locks, but he was as still as the castle-stones. There was only one thing that would have moved him right now, and he had no doubt that that one thing was nowhere near him. Worlds away, even. He continued to look across the sloping landscape of Madrid, almost unchanged since so many years ago. He’s been here many times since he was changed, but the last time he can remember looking on this place with mortal eyes, he can remember being almost as emotionally desolate as he is right now. “Perhaps we are as unchanging as they say, then,” he scoffed to himself. Who was he kidding? There was no “we.” Just him. And it was his fault. He dropped his head to look at the ground disinterestedly, not even paying attention to the set of footsteps slowly approaching from the stairs. Probably just another stupid mortal tourist, coming here to stare gape-mouthed at the landscape view of the capital of his home country. His expression turned to a slight sneer. “And if it’s a slayer?” half of his mind goaded. “What do I care” If he has the mettle to attempt taking me down, then good? I could use some refreshment. I’m rather parched,” answered the other half. The footsteps slowly approached him, but he still chose to ignore this interloper’until she spoke. “Mi corazon acelera (my heart quickens),” she said, her voice sliding fluidly out of the darkness behind him and winding around him like a serpent. Instinct took over, and he spun around, his eyes catching on her, re-memorizing every feature. For a few moments, he could hardly be sure it was her, as he took in every detail’ebon hair cascaded from the crown of her head down her back in a thick, slightly rippling wave, and everything else was refreshingly the same, from the porcelain white of her skin to the way her emerald eyes stood out from her face as the obvious expression of her deep-seated passions. Her crimson lips curled beguilingly upward into a smirk as she saw that she’d left him without words for once’that, she always took as a good sign. She could practically feel his eyes raking over her lithe panther-like form, which was clad all in black, as usual. As his eyes snapped back from far-away contemplation, the beast growled through him again, suddenly obliterating walls that he had built up over the past few months, revitalizing feelings that he had been allowing to deaden in order to keep himself from going insane. A smile spread across his face, one of the rare genuine smiles that he didn’t often favor this world with. “You came?” The smirk faded from her face as that lush verdant intensity that he was so fond of boiled up inside her eyes. “I come where mi le’n (my lion) calls... you really expected that I’d pass up a call from you?” He paused, in silence for a few moments. In truth, he’d expected that she would pass it up. Yet, from the look she was giving him right now, he had to wonder why he’d been so insane to think that she would have been so angry with him that she wouldn’t show up when he asked her to. She folded her arms across her chest, arching an eyebrow at him, daring him to make the next move’and it was at that moment that he finally remembered that he had a lot to make up to her. The flash of movement in the beryl depths of his eyes sent chills racing up her spine as he finally stood from leaning against the wall and walked a slow circle around her. He stopped behind her, one arm wrapping around her waist. The other produced a rose, seemingly out of nowhere, practically dripping with dew in its half-bloomed state. She took in its every crevice and crease with her eyes as she accepted it, half-smiling’no doubt another example of his rather phenomenal talent for chimerstry. His fingernails gently scraped at her neck as he pushed her hair away from it and leaned down to press his lips firmly to her skin. She leaned her head to the side, a gesture of near-submission that seemed to frenzy him still further, a madness creeping into his eyes only seemed to crop up when the source of his addiction was near him. One of his fangs lightly grazed her neck, eliciting a growl from her’another familiar sound that served only to push buttons as his hand traced up the lush curve of her side that he had long ago committed to memory. She opened her eyes as he pulled away from her, leaving just the touch of his fingers laced through hers. He took a step closer, running the backs of his fingers fondly over her cheek as he looked down at her. “Venido con m”, cara mia.” (Come with me, cara mia) His voice, containing more than a hint of a growl in its lower tones, caressed her and bid her do as he asked. He could have probably moved her on his own, but after what had last happened between them, he wanted her to come to him willingly. The unnatural cold of her skin didn’t keep him out’slowly, the warmth of passion burned between both of them, setting alight two beings who thought they had long ago closed the doors to such encompassing emotions. “Donde usted conduce, seguir” (Wherever you lead, I will follow),” she said, the words barely coming out a whisper. .:: ::: ::. Disorientation engulfed his senses for a good five minutes before he realized where he was. “Alone, that’s where you are” the same place you have been ever since you let her go, like an idiot,” he reminded himself. As the seconds pass, a feeling of inescapable misery crashed into him like a tidal wave. Why was his brain playing tricks on him like this? He was supposed to be the one in control of these tormenting illusions; in fact, he was supposed to be wielding the illusions, but to torment others, not himself. What the hell was wrong with him? Sacrilege came to his lips rather naturally in situations like these; the only holiness he knew was what he felt for her, and that was something that tormented him every night that he spent searching for her. Now, that feeling evidently haunted his dreams too. His hands clenched involuntarily. They weren’t supposed to be empty, they were supposed to be resting on her back as he held her close to him while they slept through the waning hours of the day. They were begging for that destiny, but there was nothing that he could do to bring his quest to fruition except keep looking until he found her, however long that might be. It was hopeless though, wasn’t it? An entire globe came in between the two of them. He would never be able to find her so long as she didn’t want him to’and as long as Kindred tended to hold grudges, she could potentially be quite a while in changing her mind. What if they ran out of time? The soothsayers from his own clan that he passed by now and again on this endless journey were always babbling about Gehenna, saying that the end came sooner than anyone wanted to admit. What would he do if they were right? If the last memory he had of her was that letter she’d written him in response to him leaving? He was helpless’his hands were tied. The feeling started to weigh down on him heavier than it ever had before. “Get a hold of yourself. Every word of those doomsayers sets you back one step farther from her,” his head snapped at him. All the chastisement in the world couldn’t have changed this oppressive misery. He had been fine until the dream’he only wished he could conjure up something like that fantastical rose that would fix everything with one small gesture. In fact, as he thought about it, a rose materialized on the table nearest the bed, but he didn’t notice until a few moments later. His brow furrowed’he must have been idly manipulating his chimerstry. That had happened once or twice when he was deep in thought, though not often enough to make itself notable. As his oceanic eyes caught on it, the petals wilted and blackened, as though dying under his glance. A snarl crossed his face as he picked it up and flung it away from him. It faded into nothingness before it ever got near enough the wall to hit it. His head fell easily enough into the palms of his hands as he stared helplessly at the stone floor of his crypt. His eyes caught on nothing, but were instead far away, remembering a much happier time. ?” Mi mujer? esto me rompe?” (My woman... you're breaking me...) It was almost a question addressed at her, an impotent wondering whether or not it affected her so deeply as it did him. But she wouldn’t answer it. His eyes clouded with something like sorrow. “Siempre fuera de vista, nunca fuera de la mente” (Always out of sight, never out of mind)? he murmured, shaking his head. Was she ever going to feel him calling for her every night? Did she have any clue at all? .:: ::: ::. Part II “Alone in this garden of pain.” Undisclosed Location Her eyes opened at gunshot speed as she was fiercely launched out of sleep and into reality. She sat up quickly, running her fingers over her shoulders and down her arms as though dusting off phantom spiders. In truth, she was tracing a path’a path that he had traced in her dreams when they were pressed together, skin to skin in a sea of satin. Memories pressed forward, only at the moment, she hadn’t yet prepared herself to suppress them. She remembered the way he had touched her that first night in Napa Valley, the way he’d looked into her eyes with a surprising tenderness; despite his years, he still had some of the faults and foibles of humans? if they could even be seen as faults. No matter how her elders had told her differently, Lia could not honestly find fault in such emotions as Virgil, then known to her only as Gideon de Medici, had displayed that night. Perhaps that was her one weakness, that feeling that not all human emotions made you weak, that sometimes, they were necessary to keep you sane’even more so than that, the semi-superstitious belief that she had in love. Had she told anyone else, they would have laughed in her face. She had been a passionate soul as a mortal, both in love and in anger, and that hadn’t left her, even over a thousand years of dreary existence that all blurred into one another. In fact, perhaps the only remaining human faculty that she possessed’the ability to love. It was considered a weakness in Kindred society; if that be true, she supposed it was a weakness that she willingly gave herself, a handicap that she volunteered to have. How long had it been since she had seen Virgil? Near three months, she supposed. So why did it seem like a blank eternity, stretching from her last moment of happiness until now? She had been alive for so long that three months was like one single tear in an ocean. But this three months? for some reason, THIS particular set of months weighed down on her as though it was a great beast, grabbing her in its maw and shaking until she understood just what she’d given up by not trying to find him. Part III “I knew you never before I see you never more.” Hunting Grounds Gym Shadow’s eyes betrayed a great effort at focus at the moment’she was trying hard to suppress the feelings that were deviling her at the moment, and she was trying to suppress them by attempting not to accidentally rend her tag team partner in half by forgetting her own speed. From across the ring, the fairly petite blonde woman peered up at her over a pair of fists that were raised in a defensive stance. Her blue eyes pierced Shadow’at least she hadn’t agreed to team with a weakling. Shadow and Vivica Valentine had no love lost for one another; in fact, the last time they had actually spent any significant amount of time together, they had been beating the hell out of one another, much like they were doing right now. However, it made a great deal of difference to be doing in a training situation as opposed to one fraught with tension and anger. There wasn’t quite so much focus now from either woman towards ripping the others arms out of socket and the like’in fact, the fact that she’d seen that emotion in Vivica’s eyes made Shadow respect her a good deal. No matter what remarks Soma might have made, she had seen something quite worthy in Vivica. This was their first chance at making a mark. Choosers of the Slain; that’s what they had chosen to call themselves, and the idea made Shadow smirk slightly. “How do you eat with those things?” Vivica snapped at her irritatedly. Shadow’s attention snapped back to her tag team partner, who was obviously quite annoyed that Shadow had stopped paying attention for a few moments. Shadow smirked a little wider, displaying her fangs a little more clearly. “I don’t eat the slop you eat” and they certainly don’t get in the way for eating my kind of food,” she said, narrowing her green eyes wickedly in insinuation. “I’d like to see you try,” Vivica retorted. She threw a blow at the end of her statement, which Shadow blocked. Vivica, of course, blocked the return punch, considering she’d been quite prepared for it. She held a hand up. “Need a sip of water,” she said. Vivica backed away to her corner, walking over to her water bottle and taking a small sip from it. Shadow dropped her own defensive stands, putting her hands on her hips as she watched her partner with disturbingly detail-conscious eyes. Drinking for hydration was, after all, quite the curiosity to her now. Vivica shrugged, making a wry face’she was obviously quite annoyed at being made a specimen of study. “So why don’t you just drain your opponents” That is, if you can, what’s the point in wasting that much energy to wrestle them?” Shadow arched a brow. “Why do YOU wrestle?” The question sank in for a moment, but it obviously wasn’t going to get an answer, so Shadow continued. “Contractual obligations. Besides, there are enough people in this world who want to get their hands on a specimen of my kind that if I used my powers out in the open like that, I’d be an instant target. Better that as few people know the truth as possible.” In her mind, Vivica wondered why, exactly, Christian had to be one of those people. You could say that she wasn’t too fond of her associate? you’d be right. She set her bottle back down on the edge of the apron and cracked her knuckles as she paced a bit to cool down, her thoughts falling to rumination on the match and all its possibilities. If they won, this could possibly lead to some quite intriguing opportunities. She could only ignore this feeling of lonely isolation for so long. As she sank to her knees in the parking lot, helplessly crushed under the weight of so many thoughts, there were no words. There was only a sharp, animalistic howl that sounded like that of a wolf’s, only filled with more agony than words could properly describe. From the edge of the woods, Damek and Jirina’s yellow eyes closed as they tilted their heads back and howled along with Mistress. They were the only solace she had now. A cacophony of unbearable howling assailed Vivica’s ears as she drove along the winding road back to the main house, the night wind caressing her tied-back blonde hair gently. She narrowed her eyes. It was probably those two awful dogs that she had; dreadful looking beasts, really. She leaned over and turned up the music of the stereo until the noise was drowned out, but she couldn’t drown out the uneasy feeling that the oppressive noise planted like a weight on her chest. .:: ::: ::.
