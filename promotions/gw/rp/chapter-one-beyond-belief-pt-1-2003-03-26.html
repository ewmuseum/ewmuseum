Chapter One: Beyond Belief? (PT. 1)

26 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Chapter One: Beyond Belief? (PT. 1) Leviathan Global Wrestling Promo #27 Date: March 26th, 2003 Location: Blagoveshensk, Russia The Book Of Judgment Chapter One: Beyond Belief? Date: February 28th, 1980 Location: Blagoveshensk, Russia A female newscaster is sitting at her desk, with two minutes to spare until she is on the air. She briefly checks over her script while staff personal get ready for the on - air news. The camera man gets ready, positioning his camera on it's stand to the proper angle, for the right effect. The lights go on, and the female newscaster is joined by a male partner. 'Two minutes people, get ready!' says the producer of the news station. He walks over towards the news casters and rests his arm on the desk, looking at the two of them. They both look toward him, and he raises his left arm, holding onto a single sheet of paper. 'Do you know what this is Nikita?' He asks. 'Well...I know for sure it's a piece of paper, but knowing it's contents, no...' She answers with a tone of sarcasm. 'This is going to be the top story for tonight, and we will be showing full coverage for the next half hour.' He says. 'What is it about?' asks Vladimir (the male newscaster). 'Well, our station received two video clips from the police to air tonight, and....' the producer trails off for a second, and is interrupted by Nikita. 'And what? It's got to be pretty damn important for us to do half an hour on it.' Nikita says. 'Yeah, just tell us.' Victor agrees. 'Well, you guys are going to think this is a little bit weird, but....we are doing coverage on Judgment Day. You guys will better understand what I mean after you see the clips. Just read from the page, than just comment on the clips. The people must be aware of this.....' the producer says, seeming anxious as he hands over the paper, walking away. Nikita and Vladimir look at each other in confusion, wondering what the producer meant. Nikita shakes her head and grabs the paper off the desk. She looks over it, then looks up at Vladimir. 'This is bullshit. It is just a damn scam!' Nikita says with anger. 'What? What's going on?' Vladimir asks. 'The station has went to far this time. But the question is, who would actually act this out for them? Whoever it is, he must be pretty damn sick. Things like this really piss me off.' Nikita says to herself. 'WHAT IS IT!' Vladimir shouts at her, catching her attention. 'They have someone on here, claiming to be some demon named Leviathan, a worshiper of Satin, talking about Judgment Day and how it is near, and crap like that.' Nikita answers. 'Who says it's fake Nikita.....what if it's real? Listen, just go with it, do the story, and lets go from there. Alright?' Vladimir says, very seriously. '..........Alright....but I still don't believe it Vlad.' Nikita answers softly. 'FIFTEEN SECONDS PEOPLE, GET READY!' the producer shouts. The lights are brightened up a little bit as a staff member quickly grabs the box of doughnuts that is on the desk and rushes off the stage. 'FIVE...FOUR...THREE...' the producer shouts, than shows two with his fingers, than points to Nikita. 'Good evening, I am Nikita Zhukov and this my partner, Vladimir Khabarovsk, and this is the six o'clock news. Tonight's top story, is Judgment Day near, or is this just a sick joke, you decide for yourself.' Nikita says as the scene cuts to the video clip. The camera view is still, and a dark figure is seen standing in what appears to be a tunnel. The only light in the tunnel is given off from two small candles, one on either side of him, each held by a stand attached to the wall. The camera view zooms in to the figures upper body. He is wearing a black cloak, with the hood up, which casts a shadow upon his face so it is unseen. 'It was you who crushed the heads of Leviathan, who left him as food for the denizens of the desert. This was stated in Psalms 74:26, as God is praised for crushing the heads of Leviathan. According to Isaiah 27:1, on Judgment day, the lord will slay Leviathan: 'In that day the Lord will punish, With His great, cruel, mighty sword, Leviathan the Elusive Serpent -- Leviathan the Twisting Serpent; He will slay the Dragon of the sea.' Neither is true...for I...I am still upon this earth...not yet destroyed...yet never will be.' Leviathan says in his deep, hollow voice. 'The earth will be over thrown from it's human inhabitants, and the lord Satin will soon rule what is rightfully his. Judgment day is nearing, yet there is no preparation that can be made. You humans can not do anything about this. Pray your lord saves you now, for if he doesn't, your soul will become a small part of Satin himself.' Leviathan says, warning the mortal's. His presence vanishes into the darkness, as the news broadcast pauses, leaving complete darkness upon the screen and within the human mind. 'Is this all beyond belief? Or could it very well be true. It's your decision, but we have more when we come back!' Vladimir says. *****To Be Continued*****
