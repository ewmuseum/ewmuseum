The end

13 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/13/03 I turned and and gazed back down the long, endless hallway, and my eyes began to swell with tears. Just as I was about to begin a sob session of my own, a soft, warm hand slid across my shoulders. Slightly startled I turned to see, her smiling at me. ' Everything is going to be ok.' I smiled back at her, and nodded in response. ' Thank you.' She shot me a confused look, and cocked her head to the right. ' For what?' I smirked to myself, and shook my head. ' For everything.' We just looked at each other in a moment of silence, before that silence was broken. ' I..I should get going, it's getting late.' I nodded, agreeing with her statement, and nodded in a thankful manner at the same time. She turned and grabbed her things, and left the waiting lobby. I watched her as she left, then turned and walked back down the long corridor leading to Star's room. I looked straight ahead, not wanting to look at the images on my sides, in fear of the thoughts that would enter my head before I made it to Star's room. So I walked to the end of the hallway, ignoring all that was around me. Trying to keep my mind clear of all the bad things that were trying to break into my positive thoughts. I made my way just outside of her room and gazed through the clear glass window. I watched as her sister Lara sat there with Star and gently caressed her beautiful blond hair back. Her motions, her actions were done in such a loving manner. A loving manner of which I had never seen from anyone before. A loving manner that you would only expect to see from a parent to their child. I watched as she began to speak to her. Even though I could not hear the words, the mannerisms of her body spoke the words to me. Her actions of love, and caring spoke to me in through the silence. Her speaking and her loving mannerisms stopped as her eyes swelled up with tears as she broke down and cried. I watched her lean over her sister and sob uncontrollably onto her. My heart jumped into my throat, as I choked back my own tears, and made my way into the room. The opening of the door startled Lara. She quickly turned, and began to wipe away the tears, as she stoop up and attempted to greet me. ' Hey.' She smiled meekly as she finished her statement. With my heart still being stuck in my throat, I was unable to choke out a response, so I just nodded. Lara walked over and wrapped her arms around my and hugged me tightly. ' I.....I know that you cared about my sister. I know that you loved her. I know that she loved you, more than anything else in the world. And I just....' ' Please. You don't have to say anything. We both are here because we love Star. And want to see her get better. I'm not very good with kind of stuff, so I don't know what to say, or how to say it. But, I think the best thing is for us to put all our energy into doing what we can for her.' She looked over at Star, and stared at her for a while, before turning back towards me and nodding in compliance with what I had just said. I wrapped my arms around her and pulled her tightly against me. Reassuring her that everything would be okay. Lara rested her head on my chest, and began to cry. I kissed the top of her head gently as she pulled away from me, and tried desperately to regain her composure. ' I'm going to get a cup of coffee. Would you like anything?' ' If they have something to eat, that would be nice. I haven't eaten all day.' Lara half laughed at my request, and headed for the door. I grabbed her right arm as she reached the door and stopped her before she could leave. She stopped and turned, looking back at me. I reached into my back pocket and pulled out a twenty dollar bill. Lara smiled at me and took it. ' Thank you.' I shook my head and smiled back at her. ' Don't mention it sis.' Her eyes lit up at my words, before she turned and continued on her way out the door to get a cup of coffee. As she left, I turned my attention to my love, lying in this hosiptal bed, trying to stay alive. I slowly walked over to her bedside, and took a seat. I looked over the numerous tubes and wires that eminated from her body, and couldn't believe this was happening. I took her hand into mine, and closed my eyes tightly. I began to replay the moments of our life in my head. The first time we met. Our first kiss. The first time we said ' I LOVE YOU' to one another. The first time we made love. When she told me she was pregnant. All those moments, crazy, funny, happy, sad, stressful, and whatever else we went through began to play through my head like a highlight reel. Frame by frame I could see each moment. In my mind I was slowly reliving the greatest times of my life. I could feel the smile grow across my face as I saw Star as the woman I loved, the woman I remembered her to be, and not as this person that was lying in this hospital bed. My daydreaming was brought to a screeching halt, and I opened my eyes when I heard a faint voice. 'B..B...Br..Brock.' My attention darted to Star, who's eyes were begining to open, as she said my name. I feverishly leaned in so that she could see I was there with her, by her side. ' Star? Sweetie..you need something. A nurse, doctor?' I began to stand up to see if I could get someone's attention. I could see Lara in the hallway talking to one of the nurses, and heading this way. In my head I hoped that she would make it shortly so that she could have a moment with her sister, before the exhaustion and drugs knocked her out again. As I leaned Star tugged at my arm, and I quickly lowered back to her. ' What is it sweetie?' I could tell she was struggling with trying to speak. She looked so weak, and so tired. It tore me up inside to see her that way. ' Brock.........I want...........you to know.................I love you.' Tears filled my eyes, but I held them back. I smiled from ear to ear at Star, and she weakly smiled back at me. ' Star, I want you to know that I love you too.' She weakly smiled ear to ear at me, and then her eyes closed. The heart monitor beeped it long, unrelenting beat, and my heart sunk. Lara and the nurse walked in the room, and the nurse, quickly ran back out the door yelling for help. A hoard of nurses rushed in the door, and feverishly tried to revive Star. However after numerous attempts, lasting almost thirty minutes, they gave up, and she was gone. Tears filled my eyes again, but this time I could not fight them off, as the flood gates opened and they streamed down my cheeks. Lara cupped her hands over her mouth, and looked to be in complete shock. I didn't know what to do. I didn't know what to say. I didn't know what to think. And I didn't know where to go. My body stood frozen, in its place, as I stared at the cold body of the woman I loved. The woman just moments ago, who was alive, and speaking to me. The woman who just minutes ago told me she loved me..........for the very last time. Without conscience of what I was doing I walked towards the bed, and sat on it next to Star. I took her hand in my hands again, and lowered my head and began to cry uncontrollably.
