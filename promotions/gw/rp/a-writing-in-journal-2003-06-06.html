A writing in journal Venom Alexander Global Wrestling Promo #272 Date: 6/6/2003 Location: Venom's office Day 1 It’s been little over a month since I stepped into the square circle, and of all days Friday the 13th is when I get into the ring with Blitz and Mike Strom in a triple threat match. During my long recovery from my knee surgery, I manage to slim down to the cruiser weight division. So what have I been doing while my knee was getting better, hitting the gym. Pushing non-stop to get back into shape, and take on anyone that will step in my way, and hope that I will make my way up the primestar title hunt and of course cruiserweight title hunt as well. During the long break, I had my heart broken by my long girl friend, she wants a break, I was like shit, and I knew she was seeing someone else. But that was just fine with me, me Venom Alexander, doesn’t need some girl to hold me down. Taking all my hard earn money I make out on the road and spend it on some necklace. And buy here all kinds of ear-rings and here to find out they wasn’t even real gold like the sales clerk told me. I told her it looked like real gold. But back to the subject first I’m going to start off on Blitz, I really don’t know much about this guy, hell he might be my worst nightmare, he might beat the living hell out of me. Like that will ever happen. If he wants to make a name for himself, he has to beat me, which will be doubtful. My return is bigger than the Bulls winning 30 games this year, bigger than Jay Vyper making a return, which is if he ever does return. The guy what been kidnapped for crying out loud. Sure he is a good friend of mine; we go back in the good old TCW days. No to Mike Storm, and again I don’t know much bout this guy, and the forecast for the 13th isn’t calling for any kind of storm’s, so I’m sure they heard of Freddie, and Jason, well, welcome to Venom Alexander’s Nightmare on Friday the 13th. I’m going to clean my plate, since this is my return I’m undefended I’m 0-0. I’m going to forget the past. This is going to be a fresh new start for me. One thing when I walk down the ramp, I hope I don’t get a warm welcome, from the fans, which will make me sick. Sure, you see all these other stars coming back after a painful surgery. Return, the fans go nuts, oh my god, he is back, yippee… Am I proud of my decision, you probably thinking what the hell am I talking about, my decision turning my back on Cobra, I brought him in the GW and I took him out of it in style. Am I proud turning on my fans, yes I am, for two years, I carried them on my back, letting them chant my name Venom. Besides it isn’t the fans, that help people like me win matches, its how you kick the hell out of someone’s ass 365 days a year. Yours Truly, Venom Alexander Day 2 Journal, a new day, the sun out, girls outside of my house wearing thongs bikini’s while rollerblading. It can’t get any better than this, last night before I went to bed I looked at some of the memories I had when I first arrived at GW, and how much I accomplished and I haven’t been in the league for a year, a former platinum champion, a Iron Fist Award winner. And I’m still on top of my game. But now my look onto the future, its time for me to make a name for myself, instead of being a one hit wonder like Talking Back Sunday. I want people to remember the name Venom Alexander. For being the greatest platinum champion of all time. If it wasn’t for Cobra I probably still be the champion right now. Like, a wise man once said and I quote “To be man, you got to beat the man.” I will be that man, somewhere down the road. Yours Truly, Venom Alexander
