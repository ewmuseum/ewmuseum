Aftermath of Reload *Fusion RP*

18 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/18/06 Brandi Sights' first night as the new Global President was certainly an eventful one. Bubba McCoy had promoted her to the position soon after firing the entire Global Commitee at Reload, blaming them for the mass exodus of superstars that occurred at the end of 2005. Reload had just gone off the air. It was the end of the main event--a three-way elimination match for Global's World Heavyweight Championship between he, Jake Hix and then-titleholder Christian Zenith. Zenith had been eliminated early due to relentless battering from both Brick and Hix. Referee Jack Wolfe eliminated him when he determined he was unable to continue. The fight was then between Brick and Hix. It was a long, hard-fought battle, but, in the end, Jake Hix got the win and the title. It didn't end there. Hix' brothers, known collectively as the Texas Wrecking Crew, rushed down to the ring and began a brutal assault on the still-downed C.E.O. of Ruckus Inc., while Jake directed traffic. It all ended with them hitting their tag finisher the Texas Death Penalty onto a steel chair and the three brothers standing triumphant over Brick's fallen body. She realized just how right she was, as she was suddenly assaulted by crashing sounds and obscenities being shouted. She then saw a stage hand running up to her, ducking a crate flying at his head as he ran. 'Ms. Sights...' Before he could finish what he was trying to say, Brandi raised her hand to stop him. 'Let me guess. Brick's conscious.' 'Yep.' 'And extremely...' 'Yep.' 'Shit!' Brandi knew just how out of control Brick's outbursts of rage could get. She'd witnessed many of them firsthand since she was put in charge of Global. Calming him down was a chore, but one she'd gotten used to. When she arrived, Brick was storming around the backstage area, kicking empty crates and throwing chairs around, to the despair of various backstage crew members who were forced to duck and side-step the rapidly-approaching projectiles. 'You know, I could hear you halfway across the entire Arena,' Brandi stated, walking towards Brick. Brick continued to stomp around, Brandi trying to keep step with him. 'They're dead!' 'I'm sorry?' 'The Hix brothers! They're all dead! Next time them motherfuckers see me, they better just lay on the ground, aight? 'Cause they're dead!' 'OK, Brick, I need you to calm down.' 'They're lucky they pulled that shit while I was still down, 'cause if I was conscious an' on my feet, it'd have been a different motherfuckin' story then!!' 'Brick, damn it! Calm down! Now, look, I understand that you're pissed. What Hix and his brothers did to you was totally unnecessary...' 'Totally unnecessary,' Brick mocked. 'That's an interesting way of putting it.' 'But you've got to let me handle them. I'll think of something.' Brick finally stood still. 'Yeah, you think of something. I got some ideas of my own.' Brick then stormed away. Brick was on the warpath and Brandi knew full well what that meant.
