Driving

8 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 8 I was afraid. The cops were chasing us and I didn’t know what to do. Jesse seemed like he was having fun because I was going 180 down a highway, but if I didn’t stop then the cop would start ramming into my car, and I can’t get any repairs, cause that is a lot of money. I did not know what to do. I took my foot off of the gas pedal and started slowing down. The cop started slowing sown to. We both came to a stop as I waited for the ticket he was going to give me. The cop got out of his car and started walking to me. I rolled down the windows and looked straight into the face of the officer. “Do you know how fast you were going back there?” he asked me. “Yes actually I do, I was going 180 miles per hour.” I told the truth. “Well, your very smart because the other people I stop just say I don’t know when I ask them how fast they were going, just for that, I’m going to drop all of the charges, but don’t ever do that again.” he said. Whew, I did not know that was going to happen. Just because I told the truth he dropped all of the charges, I must be really good at this. It was the first time in my life that a officer had to stop me, and I guess I just got out of it. I stepped on the gas pedal and only went up to 140, I think that was the speed limit. “You must be good with cops.” Jesse said. “I guess so.” I said. “If I was like that with cops, I wouldn’t have been in jail. Oh well, I’m out of jail now so I don’t have to worry.” he said. I passed a few more people be fore thinking, I have to goto the bathroom. I saw convenience store in the middle of now where, so I turned into it. I parked the car at the side of the store and locked the doors. Me and Jesse got out of the car and walked to the door of the store. Jesse didn’t even ask why we stopped here, and I don’t really think he cared. “Why did we stop here?” he finally asked. I didn’t think he would ask the question. Oh well, I guess its nothing bad that we stopped, I just had to goto th bathroom. Is that such a crime or problem? “I had to goto the bathroom.” I said. “Oh.” he said back. We opened the door of the store as I walked to the back of it. I saw a sign that said Washrooms on it so I followed it. I saw a door that was the mens bathroom, I walked over to it and walked in. It smelled really bad, but that’s what every bathroom in a store smells like. I closed the door, and a few minutes later I walked out. I saw Jesse purchasing some candy, what? Just because we are like 30 and 20 years old doesn’t mean we cant have candy. I walked over to the counter and picked a Kit Kat bar up. I put it onto the counter and payed for it. Me and Jesse left the store, and walked over to my car. “Next stop, Evolution.” I said. “Yup.” that’s all he replied with. Me and Jesse got to the car and I unlocked the doors. We got into the car, as I backed out of the space. I drove up the little gravel road and back onto the highway, I waited for a car and then started going. We past a few more stores in the middle of now where, a few more cars and finally got into the open, there were no cars or anything. It was free road the whole way. I drove a bit faster as the scene started to fade.
