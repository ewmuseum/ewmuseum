Three day tour around the basement

undated

Date: Days 1 and 3 Day 1 I winced at the thought; Trey Reed was just not in his right mind. I can understand the fact that he was upset with his showing at Master of the Mat. I mean, going from nearly defeating the Master last year to not even making it out of the preliminaries this year could clearly bring a man down. Not that it was disappointing all on fronts, considering he ran through three people of the gauntlet, but was taken out as soon as the Platinum champion arrived. So uneasiness set in, and Trey decided that he would have to take a different route to return to the stature he was in by GW’s standards. The idea he came up with, however, was not as good of a plan as I would have hoped for. I sighed a sigh to let him know I wanted nothing to do with it, and he shrugged with his right hand to say “whatever Dennis” while dialing into his cell phone with his left. He had the thought that true inspiration came with rock music and drug experiments. I will admit, all he ever seemed to do last time he was around was party it up with Jay Robson, and take the federation by storm. But one fluke match wasn’t enough to start calling up friends looking for coke hookups, right? “Ronny” Yeah, it’s Trey. I’m back in Texas, man!” He was so cheery and convincing you’d think that they were the best of friends and not worst of enemies. This was Trey Reed, the ever-fronting who he really was and how he really felt to everyone he could see. “You can stop by tonight” Great, then here’s the address...” A crazy thought, he would just run around inviting whoever and whatever to my house, just because he didn’t happen to be able to afford his own. I went up the steps of the basement and left him to revel in himself, and his thought that he could actually improve himself by locking himself away in solitude for three days, and do lots of drugs. To me, it was just a sign of him falling back into bad habit, but sometimes people have to learn the hard way and I could let him learn that on his own. Upstairs, to block out the sound of his so-underground-it-makes-no-sense hip hop, I blast some classical beats. It isn’t soon before Ronny, his good old buddy (drug dealer) shows up with a demeanor on his face. Old friends who turned bitter last time around the block, joined together by the omnipotent PCP. If I could explain the reasoning behind all of this, I wouldn’t need to be up at four in the morning drinking coffee. Some things may never change. ---- Day 3 He awakes somewhere between six or seven pm screaming with sweat. The nightmares were brought upon him by the drugs he took the night before. His buddy Ronny has been here too, somewhere between alive and dead, across the basement floor. He woke up rambling on about some big dramatic scene, trying to make it seem as though it had actual life implications. One of those “I just came out of a coma, but it was so real! And you were there, and you!” type of speeches. It’s sad what mixing angel dust with gin and coke can do. “I’m telling you, it was like I was in a freaking Highlander movie or something. It’s like I could just sense that power that Dagoth Kinslayer has. Dude, I have the feeling he is going to come at me with that sword! And I don’t know how to stop him, I just don’t know how to do it by myself.” He sighed, “and that’s what the problem is.” A realization, finally, slipped from his mouth. He was willing to listen to me, finally. A grin came along my face, and I was ready to show Trey what he needed to find his golden key to success. So we start reality NOW.
