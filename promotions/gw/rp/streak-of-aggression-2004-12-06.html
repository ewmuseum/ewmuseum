Streak of Aggression

6 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/06/04 I'm sorry, John. I really didn't mean to do that much damage... I've just had so much on my mi... Don't apologize for the things you do on purpose. You're not sorry you've done this... you're happy. You're proud... it's one of your trophy moments. Please... please accept my apologies. Do you really think he can hear your lies in that state? Do you honestly believe he even wants to hear your pitiful excuse? I know what happened... and you know what happened. This wasn't a case of an accidental thrashing... this was intentional. NO! ... ... ... ... ... ... ... ... ... '... FUCK YOU!' I awoke, throwing the bed cover away from my body and off of the bed. I arose, sitting up, a cold sweat forming on my brow and running down my back. It'd been the second time in as many days, the same dream... the same methods... the same result. Nightmare? Message? Prediction? I didn't even know what to make of these horrible scenes. Zero was scheduled to face me on Evolution in a few days time... scheduled to face a 'me' that wasn't anything close to being normal in their current state. Nobody would care though... nobody would even think twice before allowing me to unleash everything on this poor individual. I had a degree of respect for Zero, an understanding and appreciation of everything he brought to the table. Skill. Looks. Charisma. I knew the guy was big enough to look after himself, but this contest wasn't going to be like anything he's probably experienced for a while. I'm not going into this contest with the sole intention of winning... the sole intention of just finishing the match and walking away. I need to extract some kind of frustration from myself, I need to remove a degree of this anger; a slice of this irritation. Sounds weird doesn't it? I want to rip somebodies head off but I don't want to do too much damage? Perhaps all of these dreams are just another way of telling me that I can't avoid the unavoidable? Perhaps it's a sign of what's likely to happen when I look to waste a little of that pain? '... This is getting stupid, Rich. These whole dreams and visualisations are getting pathetic. Get a grip... get a hold of yourself. This isn't some kind of destruction derby, you have to be around for the longer run. You have to make sure you extract revenge against Juan Gonzalez.' I said to myself, trying to thrust the words into my mind like some kind of fierce pep-talk. I could see the kind of future that existed for me if I broke through Zero in the way that I'd dreamt... I could see the level of disappointment that Ryan would be subjected too and the lack of revenge I would be able to extract. I looked ahead of myself for a few moments, still panting and beginning to shiver, too many thoughts and further thoughts beginning to cram into every nook and cranny of my mind. '... Perhaps I could do with a call to that freak who tried to unload themselves on me the other day. I can't say I'm going to be impressed looking into this method of dealing with a problem, but I don't want this whole ordeal to make me unreliable in any line of work. The last thing I want is to take things too far.' I got out of bed and walked over to the computer desk where my phone remained. I looked down to the cell and began to think for a few moments, weighing up the possibilities of going through with this thing. Shrugging my shoulders and sighing loudly, I picked the phone up and opened the flap, turning the device on before looking through the previous incoming calls of the phone. Surely enough, without much effort, the number of that guy was there. '... Well, here goes nothing.' I said, pressing the 'call' button before holding the phone to my ear, waiting patiently for somebody to answer on the other end. 'Hello, you've got through to the office of Pedro Gomez, may I ask who is calling?' came a young female voice. I was a little mystifed at the name at first, being that it was Mexican and everything. I thought about Gonzalez for all of a few moments, thinking that something could be up... but quickly threw away the assumption, not wanting anyone to think I was going to have an issue with every Mexican just because one of their people happened to almost kill me. 'Yeah, I'm Rich Sherwin and I'm calling because somebody in your office gave me a call yesterday about legal aid. I wasn't all that impressed at the time, having a few too many things hanging on my mind... but now that I've given the whole idea a little more thought, I'm thinking that maybe you folks could help me a little better than I could help myself.' I answered, trying to sound sincere and appreciate about the offer that had been thrown in my direction yesterday. I still didn't quite understand how they'd got my number in the first place, but the situation was a little too important for me to worry about such trivial matters. 'Oh, Mr Sherwin... Mr Gomez is looking forward to talking to you about the case in question. He was a little shocked and hurt by your reaction to his offer yesterday, but he understands that you're going through a lot at the moment. Would you like me to patch you through to his office or would you like me to leave a message on your behalf?' the young, female voice asked. I thought for a few moments, wondering whether it would be best if I ran back now or left a note and arranged some kind of meeting. Maybe I could go as far as to ask for some kind of meeting arrangement at Evolution of this week? Perhaps I could get something organised so I could meet with this guy before my match with John Zero? Thinking long and hard about the situation at hand, I soon found myself coming with an answer. '... You know what, I think I'm going to have to leave a message or something.' I began, before leaving what could possibly be one of the worst excuses in the history of excuses, '... because you see, I've got a lot of things to look into and do before Evolution. You know what I mean, don't you? Considering that I'm something of a superstar athlete and everything.' 'Yes, I understand what kind of preparation and training you guys have to do just to make sure you're at your very best for each and every one of those events.' she began, showing sympathy for my lie-ridden excuse. She hesistated for a few moments before continuing and finishing with a follow-up to her previous offer, 'What would you like the message to be, Mr Sherwin?' 'What would I like the message to be?', I asked myself aloud, trying to sound a little intelligent and a little concerned at the same time. I must have been there for a good couple of minutes, running through various different combinations of words that all carried the same actual meaning. 'Mr Sherwin, are you still there?' the young lady interrupted, forcing my train of thought to halt at the nearest station, the best thing I could come up with ready to be laid out to the listening audience. '... Yeah, I'm still here... and as for that message, I'd appreciate it if you could tell Mr Gomez that his presence would be greatly appreciated at GW's Evolution of this upcoming week. Just tell him to walk through the security station at the back of the arena and explain that he's here to see me about a few legal matters. I'm sure he'll be able to walk right through.' I informed the young lady, the sounds of her apparently scribbling down coming through the phone as clear as day. 'Ok, I'll do my best to make sure that Mr Gomez is able to see this message as soon as possible. Thanks for your call, Mr Sherwin.' the young lady responded before hanging up on her end of the line. In response, I did the same thing and closed the flap of the phone. Dropping it back onto the table, I turned and looked out of the bedroom window. '... Let's just hope this whole arrangement helps take the heat off my attitude before my bout with Zero on Evolution... I'd dread to think what would really happen if these dreams had any truth in them...' I said to myself, the worries and the concerns of those messages riding high in my mind as the scene faded out, deep into black. :: End of Part 2 ::
