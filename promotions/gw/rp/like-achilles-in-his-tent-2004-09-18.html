Like Achilles in his tent

18 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/18/04 Aside from Deacon Holmes, one man in Global Wrestling had proven to be a huge thorn in Brick's side in the past year. That man was Sage Sabbot. Brick's history with the Harvester of Tears was just as long and intense as his rivalry with Holmes, perhaps even more so. Recently, Sabbot left Global Wrestling. Brick sat on his couch, sulking. 'You've been sitting there for almost a half hour. What's wrong?' 'He's gone, Aaron,' Brick said. 'Excuse me?' 'Sage Sabbot. He's gone.' 'What do you mean he's gone? You mean he's dead?' 'No, but I'm starting to wish he was. He quit.' -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Date: May 2003 It was a month after Wrestlecade 3. Brick had defeated his longtime adversary Salaok. Now, Brick was free to pursue what had eluded him for so many years: the Global Wrestling World Heavyweight champion. When Chris Camero left the company, the title was vacated. So, a tournament was held to determine the new World champion. The final two contestants would face each other in the main event at Dead Zone. The two final contestants would turn out to be Brick and Sage Sabbot. At the time, Sabbot was a member of the Harvesters. They would face each other in a Hell in a Cell match, where one could only win by submission. In an attempt to stack the deck against Brick, Salaok mailed front-row tickets for Dead Zone to Brick’s family and friends. The logic was that Brick would be so worried about his loved ones being harmed, that he wouldn’t be able to concentrate on the match. Also, his loved ones would experience of seeing him possibly getting beaten to within an inch of his life. Brick managed to tip the scales in his favor a bit by barricading the door to the Harvesters? locker room. This, however, didn’t last very long, as the Harvesters managed to break free. Salaok had yet another idea to stack the deck against Brick. He had the Cell doused in gasoline and set on fire. Brick was already having problems breathing, considering how long the match had gone on and the fact that his nose was broken. But now the cell was on fire, and the fire was using up oxygen. At the end of the match, Brick suffered a humiliating defeat in his hometown of New York City, when he tapped out due to Sabbot dislocating his shoulder with a crossface. Afterwards, Brick popped his shoulder back into place by slamming it against a turnbuckle and showed sportsmanship by handing Sabbot the belt. He then attacked Sabbot and hit the Bricklayer on him on top of the belt. It was Brick’s message that this matter was far from over. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Date: June 2003 Brick was looking to get back into the World title picture and pay Sage Sabbot for his loss at Dead Zone. Unfortunately, Salaok had tried to place obstacle after obstacle in Brick’s path, but to no avail. Brick won a shot at the World title in a triple threat match facing Derek Frost and Quartz. Now, there was no keeping Brick from finally winning the title. His match with Sabbot was part of a double main event with Salaok vs. Derek Frost in a retirement match. This time, he and Sabbot would face each other in the most brutal match GW had to offer: The Pit. The Pit was fifteen feet deep, with concrete walls and a hard iron floor. Weapons dropped down at random. The only way out was through two large iron doors which would only lock and unlock at the command of a computer. The only way to win a match in the Pit is either by submission or knockout. Brick finally had payback and also finally achieved his dream. He was finally the World champion. After that match, Sabbot left the company. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Date: July 12, 2004 Master of the Mat, one of the company's biggest pay-per-vie events of the year, was fast approaching. Brick was competing in one of the qualifying round tag team matches. His opponents: Jesse Jamester and Sage Sabbot. His partner: another of Brick's rivals, Deacon Holmes. Brick's alliance with Holmes was a tenuous one, given their genral dislike for one another. In addition, Holmes had taken to verbally abusing a friend of Brick's, Gunner Addison. Brick had done everything in his power to ensure that everything went smoothly. Unfortunately for both Brick and Holmes, Gunner's boyfriend was 6'6' and 295 lbs. of short-tempered Texas cowboy named Ely Braxton. Right before their match, as Deacon made his way to the arena, Ely ambushed Deacon and beat him bloody, threatening to kill him if he ever disrespected Gunner again. Caught off guard by Ely's actions and outnumbered, Brick was easy pickings for Jamester and Sabbot. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Date: July 26, 2004 Brick had won the first lottery match in the beginning of the Master of the Mat card. He then went on to compete in the semi-finals against Sabbot, Venneman Pendragon and World champion Josh. Brick was still tired from the lottery match, but in the end, it took Pendragon, Josh and Sabbot combining their forces and triple teaming Brick to eliminate him from the match. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Date: August 9, 2004 For the first time in a year, Brick and Sabbot would face each other in a singles match. A botched frog splash from Brick resulted in Sabbot getting the pinfall. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Date: August 31st, 2004 Before Whiplash came, the International title was held by Leinad Soma. After an incident involving security, he was stripped. Bubba McCoy then booked a match to determine who would be the new International Champion. The competitors: Brick and Sabbot. The match went back and forth. Finally, just as it seemed Brick was going to finish the match as the new International champion...just as the ref's hand is coming down for the third time, Sabbot turned around and sprayed purple mist right into his eyes and all over his face. Brick broke the count and scrambled, trying to clear his eyes. When he finally did manage to clean the gunk from his eyes, Sabbot snuck up behind him and knocked him unconscious with the timekeeper's bell, enabling him to steal the victory and the title. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Now, Sabbot was gone and the issues between he and Brick were still unsettled. 'It's not fair, Aaron! Motherfucker cheats me out of the International title and now he wants to leave before I can get my rematch!!' 'So what, you're just gonna obsess about it? If he's gone, he's gone. I hate it when you get like this. You're like Achilles sulking in his tent.' 'And I hate it when you reference Homer. You know I don't like loose ends. Sabbot leaving the company before I can settle the score is a loose end.' 'I know. But hey look on the bright side. You can still get a chance at the International title. Plus, you're still a contender for the World title.' 'Yeah,' Brick grumbled, 'the number three contender.' 'Better than not being in the rankings at all.' 'Hmm...' Aaron then reached over to a comic book. 'Maybe this'll cheer you up.' It was issue 5 of a new Marvel Comics series called The Pulse. The premise of the series involved a new column at the Daily Bugle. 'Oh, I haven't read this yet. I wanted to see the fight between Luke Cage and the Green Goblin.' 'I flipped through a copy in the comic book store. It's...' 'No, don't tell me, don't tell me. I want to see it for myself.' Finally, Brick got to the last few pages which depicted the Luke Cage/Green Goblin fight. 'Ooh! Oo-OHHH!!' 'I guess this means your mood is improving now.' 'Yeah, it's getting there.
