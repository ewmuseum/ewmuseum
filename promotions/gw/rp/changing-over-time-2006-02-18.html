Changing Over Time

18 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/18/2006 Taxi He was in the back of the cab, wondering how it had gotten to this point. The year had ended so differently than he had ever expected, and yet here he was wiping the small droplets of blood off his hands as the taxi took him away from the hotel. “Where to bud?” Jake’s mind drifted back to the end of the year, when he thought the world would be better this year. To Japan The flight went like flights always went. Not enough room, too much sitting and poor food for the price he paid for a first class ticket. Of course, it would have been even worse had he been sitting in the back with all the other travelers in coach. Paying a little extra money for first class meant his not inconsiderable bulk didn’t get wedged into too small a seat, a benefit for him of course, but also for those he wasn’t forced to sit next to on the plane. The approach to the airport was smooth, and he spent the moments before landing stretching out his weary muscles. He had a lot of press obligations this trip, and he didn’t plan to waste any time while he was here. There were several obligations, and they all had important impacts on his career. 1. Promote Global Wrestling in 2006. They needed to keep their loyal international fan base interested in their shows, and a few well placed appearances and interviews in Japan would certainly cement that. 2. Promote the SAWF Encore Pay-Per-View. He and his family had at least three matches, and so keeping the fans involved would help the buy rate. In addition, any press would only help Global at the moment. Besides his appearances for his professional obligations, he also had to make arrangements for the personal struggle his family was facing. There were traitors at the gates, and he had to play the game very, very carefully to make it through to the new day from the darkness he was facing. The negotiations for the book had not gone well on any side. Pitt was unable to secure it through delicate negotiations with intermediaries, but then he learned the other side faced similar problems. The one stroke of luck belonged to them, they had an inside mole on the other team. Pitt was handling the contacts with McAllister, but no one else was being let in on the plans. There were’other reasons? The information must be wrong! He told himself for the hundredth time, but there were too many clues and signs to ignore. He shrugged them away for the moment, leaning back as the jet’s wheels hit the concrete, bounced a bit and then smoothed out. The ride to the gate would take a little while, but he didn’t mind. Jake pulled his carry-on from beneath his seat. There were three cell phones, which was unusual for North America, but simply made him part of the crowd in Asia. The phone flipped open to reveal the entire keyboard. He used it for text messages and email exclusively, and he had some interesting information coming through at the moment about the current situation. There were new clues and leads, but he would peruse the new information later. For the moment, they had made it to the gate quickly. The passengers deplaned in a very orderly, proficient manner. He went to collect his luggage, and then proceeded through customs unaccosted. There, beyond the security gates, stood several familiar faces holding a sign for him. Jake approached, luggage in tow as “Sensational” Stephen Reed and John “Hawk” Peters nodded at him. “How was the flight?” Hawk asked with a wicked little grin. “As good as a commercial flight can be.” Jake handed him the heavier suitcase. Stephen laughed, “Yeah, we can’t begin to tell you how sorry we were to use the private plane we chartered. But, to be fair, we did have to come earlier than you. I particularly enjoyed the food. Almost a real kitchen on that plane you know.” The next suitcase went to Stephen with a slight thud as it hit him in the chest, “You two are enjoying this far too much.” “Yes, we are.” Hawk laughed as they led him outside to the waiting limousine. They slipped inside quickly, and made sure the partition was up before talking. “Where are we at with the book?” Jake looked across the space at Stephen. His old friend was not looking well. Travel had not agreed with him as a paleness graced his features than betrayed the excellent tanning beds he normally utilized. There was also a continuation of the gauntness he had noticed before. Jake shrugged it off, Stephen was just not enjoying the food or had caught a virus. Stephen could see the recognition in Jake’s eyes, but he was thankful he wasn’t being pressed on what it could mean. “We have a new lead. Apparently, the book was once in the possession of a certain group of warriors as it were. You’re gonna love this, the place is a wrestling training school.” “Really?” The intersections of fate and coincidence were strange indeed. “A wrestling school?” “The Mifune School Of Wrestling Excellence to be exact. The lead trainer is named Toshiro Mifune.” Stephen coughed into a handerkerchief. He took a moment to recover, “Damn cold. Anyway, the school is somewhat remote. They’re up on Hokkaido. We can’t speak directly with Mifune, but you are a known entity in the industry he is a aprt of. He would receive you fi you made a personal visit.” “I still don’t know if we need to do that. We’re pursuing other options to recover the book.” Hawk counetred. Stephen shook his head, “Eggs in a basket. If we learn from Mifune where the book is, we don’t have to pursue these more dangerous courses in the negotiations. We could get the book and skip out before we have to get too deeply involved.” Jake thought about what they were facing. Sometimes, all the lines of fate and chance converged into a few critical junctures. They did not know what path they would be walking once this was all over. Everything was changing before them, and he doubted that everyone would make it past the encoutner to see the next day. “Everyone else is on the way?” “We’ll have the full team here.” Hawka ssured him. “We have sent them on some separate missions as we get ready. The authorities are not on to us, but we worry that our good friend Sid might deciede to drop an annonymous line on us.” “Run some interference through some of our friends. Make sure they believe this is simply a business dispute, and any interference will not be met kindly.” Jake wondered if Pitt might know any good contacts here for these kinds of situations. “Try and talk to Pitt. He was always a little more active on the other side of things. He might know a few people.” “I’ll call him.” Hawk made a note on his Blackberry. “For now, let’s get checked in at the hotel and then we need to make arrangements to get to Hokkaido. The sooner the better.” Jake just nodded as he watched the scenery fly by outside the window. He was back in Japan, and beneath the regret and pain he found a little smile to warm his soul. Some of his best memories had been here. If only there could be more good memories coming instead of the dread. Hokkaido The waters below the helicopter looked choppy and uneven. They could have chosen to take a boat trip to the island, which actually sounded leisurely and relaxing. However, Jake had insisted on taking a chopper trip because he was here on business, not vacation. Looking at the seas below, he was happy he had chosen a helicopter trip to reach the island. Besides, looking pale and sickly would not make a good impression on Master Mifune. Jake had known Master Toshiro Mifune for many years, through reputation alone. The man had been one of the wrestling legends in Japan before retiring. Once, just once, Jake had been scheduled in a match against Mifune before last minute booking changed it all. Opportunities lost should never be dwelt on is what the man beside him would have said. John “Hawk” Peters was pushing up closer now to sixty than to fifty, but he was in remarkably good health. The cane he carried was only really needed in cold weather, but otherwise even his knee was fine. Jake had worried if his friend and mentor would have been able to handle the trip, bit he seemed fine. In fact, he was doing more than Jake had expected in this whole ordeal. Without children of his own, he had long ago adopted them for himself. Now, he looked healthy, powerful and ready for anything. Not like SSR? “Sensational” Stephen Reed had not been himself for quite a while. He seemed down, always withdrawn from the world around him even as he led projects or performed duties. Jake wanted to make time to see what was happening, but Pitt had cautioned against it. “That’s just SSR’s way. He’s a loner, even when he’s at a party he’s always a little bit alone.” The helicopter touched down on the helipad. Jake knew the winds were already whipping about without the added roar of the chopper blades. He used the black band to tie his hair back into a ponytail before opening the door. He and Hawk walked across the asphalt to the waiting car. Smaller than he preferred, he didn’t mind a little crowding for the moment. The Mifune School of Wrestling Excellence was just outside Sapporo. Master Mifune had chosen the location well, keeping the students just far enough from the bustle of the city to hold their attention a little more while staying near enough to top-notch medical facilities to handle any unforeseen problems. Jake was reading through some email as he wondered what Toshiro would think of him. In Japan, Jake had been a gaijin who quickly conformed to the society. He had become a fashion plate like the young men going to the hottest clubs wearing trendy fashions, and even fit in with the business set in his custom made suits. When he had returned home, Jake had been quite a bit rougher in appearance and attitude. Would Mifune have preset notions and ideas about him? He had dressed sharply in the black suit with knit shirt to remain respectful of the man he was meeting. He found himself thinking back to his brother’s time in Japan. Pitt had been quite the opposite, setting himself constantly apart as a gaijin with his cowboy boots, blue jeans and American tee shirts. Of course, the fans had eaten it up. He was marked by how different he was in this world, set apart setting him up for his successes. Jake knew where some of his jealousy had come from when he looked back on these days. No matter, these things too had passed. Now he focused on what to say to the man who trained plenty of legends. He needed information, but he also needed to keep secluded plenty of other information about what they were really after. The car pulled in front of the school. They stepped out, and Jake felt a suddenly cold wind rush through the street, chilling him as he looked around the area. There was nothing too far out of place, but it did seem unusual. “What’s wrong?” Hawk asked. Jake kept looking around, “Nothing I guess, just a cold wind came rushing through when we got out of the car.” “Superstitious often?” “Lately?” Jake raised his eyebrows at his friend as his mouth widened in surprise. “A lot. Let’s go meet Master Mifune.” The climbed the steps to the front door. Green doors hung surrounded by carefully maintained and constructed white walls with molding and hand carved pieces. Master Mifune had used the inspiration of old temples in his design for his school. Jake opened the door for Hawk, and they walked into a small room with sliding door on each side of a large, black lacquered desk. A man in a sharply pressed suit was behind the desk. He spoke in English for their benefit, “Gentlemen, welcome to the Mifune School of Wrestling Excellence. How may I assist you?” Jake bowed slightly, “My name is Jake Hix. I have a meeting with Master Mifune.” The young man nodded excitedly, “Of course sir. We are honored at your presence. Please follow me.” Following right behind, they stepped through the doorway as he slid the frame aside. Inside, they saw plenty of training activities for the young students. They were being put through all manner of physical activities to maximize endurance, strength, speed and agility. The quality of training here was superb. As a trainer himself, he found the facilities enviable. The young man led them down the entire length of the large, open room to the back. A full size ring was set-up here with black ropes. Inside, several students were going through drills under the watchful eyes of Toshiro Mifune and his fellow instructors. Jake and Hawk waited, watching as he berated one of the students in Japanese for failing to properly execute the dropkick. The young man waited until Master Mifune had finished yelling before bowing respectfully and explaining his appointment had arrived. Toshiro yelled at the young man again before turning the class over to his assistant. Master Mifune approached, speaking English fluently for the sake of his guests. “Mr. Hix and Mr. Peters. An honor to meet you gentlemen.” “The honor is hours Master Mifune.” Jake said as he and Hawk bowed deeply. “If you would come with me to my office we can speak in private.” Master Mifune scowled at several of the students. Jake looked over his shoulder, noticing he and Hawk were getting interested looks from many of the students. They seemed distracted from training, something that was never allowed at Master Mifune School of Wrestling Excellence. Master Mifune smiled, “Some of my students are fans of your work Mister Big Dawg. They are not used to receiving visitors from foreign promotions.” “I see.” “If you will follow me.” Master Mifune led them down a short hallway into his office. The room was small, with a floor desk that Master Mifune came around, kneeling behind it. Jake came forward and kneeled down across from the wizened master, but Hawk stayed standing in the background with his cane. He would be the observer for this meeting, and using the cane as an excuse to create separation was simply a good opportunity. “Mr. Hix, I was honored by your request to come to my school, but I am confused. Why did you wish to see me?” Master Mifune folded his hands together in his lap. “We have no business together, and I no longer do any public appearances. I am, now, simply a humble teacher.” “I understand sir. I appreciate greatly you allowing me the time to come to your school. I also ran a training school, and your facilities are to be envied. I can see you do a tremendous job in training the next generation. I have come to Hokkaido on personal business.” Jake smiled politely. His eyes never wavered from the strong gaze of the man across the desk. Intrigued, Toshiro tried to think of any business they might have together. He never had the opportunity to work with Jake, but his reputation was strong within the community. Even now, he had a following of fans within the Japanese wrestling community. He was a man who could be respected, but how that related to the current situation he did not know. “Please, how can I assist you?” “I understand you were once in possession of a book I am seeking.” Jake began. Toshiro was surprised. Of all the items Jake might visit with him about, the last he expected was for a book to be the topic of discussion. “You see, I have a’personal need for this volume. The book is very old, wrapped in leather with writing in a very old form of Chinese actually. The book was rumored to have been taken from China during World War II.” Jake held his arms out briefly, “Not that you or your family would have taken it, but collectors collect what is available on the open market. I am sure you did not know the history of this volume.” Suspicions began increasing. Toshiro had an extensive collection of books, and as a rare book collector he knew certain volumes came through’undesirable channels. One of his last passions were his books and scrolls. Why does this gaijin ask about my collections? Master Mifune took a deep breath, holding it for an extremely long time before speaking. “What book is it you seek?” “I believe it was called the Book Of Fire or the Book Of Flames.” Jake remained calm, knowing there were many’controversies around the book. Master Mifune looked away from his guest to a mural on the wall, Such a book is’highly regarded by certain people. It contains many dark mysteries waiting to be unraveled.” “Yes, but it has something more than that.” Jake was disturbed by the lack of eye contact. “Fort the right person, it also has the key to great goodness. There are’there is information in the book to help with certain goals.” Toshiro returned his fierce gaze to Jake, “What goals are those?” The awkward silence between them now was only filled by the loud sounds coming from the dojo. Really, it was more the vibrations from the throwing and falling drills that penetrated his haven inside these art covered walls. Jake searched for the right words, “We don’t know each other Master Mifune, but I will speak plainly as though we were old acquaintances.” “A rare gift.” “The information in the book will help me close a dark chapter in my family’s history. Since you seem to know the contents of this book, then you must also know there are instructions for defeating dark adversaries.” Jake leaned forward just a bit, to help emphasize his urgency. “I must know how to remove one of these beasts, or else I leave my family in jeopardy.” There it was, plain and true while cloaked in a healthy sense of vagueness. Jake would have to rely on Toshiro to trust him. At the moment, Toshiro was trying to determine that very issue. Reputations covered business, but most people had several different faces they presented to the world. For all he knew, this man was evil behind the scenes, evil most foul. He had to make a judgment here as he looked the man up and down. He reached out, trying to sense the man’s ki as he sat before him. Impressed by what he sensed, master Mifune decided to trust this man. He was strong and true from what he could sense. “I had the book, but it is no longer in my possession.” Toshiro looked distraught, “When I refused to make payment to a certain organization they broke into my building. My artifacts were destroyed or stolen, and they vandalized my training facility. Certain, arrangements ensure this will never occur again, but the book you seek is among the items they took with them.” Jake was disappointed. Their one hope for an easy solution had just disappeared, but he might still be able to get his information through another contact. He bowed his head at Master Mifune, “I appreciate your honesty in this matter. We are in negotiations with the people who stole the book from you. I did not understand it had come to them through these means.” “Then you may get it through negotiation. All hope is not lost.” Master Mifune was watching him carefully. He sensed there was more to this situation not yet revealed through such’careful dialogue. “No, not all hope is lost.” Jake smiled briefly, like a flicker of flame before going out into ash. “However, we are not the only interested party in this particular item.” “I believe I understand.” He bowed his head. Jake Hix stood back up on his feet. He smoothed out the few wrinkles in his suit, “I appreciate your time Master Mifune.” Hawk stepped forward beside Jake, “We appreciate your frankness here today/? “Anytime.” Master Mifune stood and stepped out from behind his desk, “Allow me to escort you out.” Master Mifune led them out of his office. As they left, Jake heard someone call his name. “JAKE!”!” He turned, not expecting to see her here. Taxi “Bud, where to?” “Swing by the nearest clothing store. I ripped my pants, and need to buy a new pair. Then I need to go to Dulles. I have a flight to catch.” Jake explained.
