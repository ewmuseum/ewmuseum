Date: 6-28/29-03 Just under one week ago, I was happily set to go in to Evolution, beat Brick one on one, then continue on to the pay per view to face Sage Sabbot for the GW World Championship. Little did I know then that the day after that match I would be asking for my release from the very federation I had grown to call home. Today, I take a little break from the hectic world of injury, high impact action, backstabbing, blood, sweat, and tears that I call a day in the office. ---- 'Derek, when are you going to be here?' asks my wife over the phone. 'As soon as I can. I need to be with you right now. It seems I made more of an impact that I thought. Nobody really wants to see me leave.' 'Well, could you settle for seeing me and about 4 other people? I told them we would have them over for a barbecue and a swim, if they promised to leave by 2,' she answers. 'I guess. As long as I get to spend more time with you after they're gone. I miss you so much baby. It's been a long day. Salaok really wasn't that bad, after we talked a little. Guess respect runs deeper than gold can scratch.' 'That's great, hun.' I can hear the genuine happiness in her voice. 'So you got the release?' 'In the glovebox. After Whiplash goes off the air I will officially be a free agent.' Cindy squeals a little. 'Why are you so happy? We will both be on the unemployment list.' 'Derek, if you haven't noticed we have enough to live very comfortably for quite some time. I don't care if we are both sitting around the house all day, I just don't want you to be working in a place where you don't feel like you need to be anymore. Besides, I'll get to see you more often now,' she comments. 'I'm sorry. Guess I better find a different federation to work for. Last thing you need is to be stuck in a house with me more.' 'That is NOT fair and you know it! I love you, even if you do say stupid crap like that. How far from home are you now?' I look for a familiar landmark so I can judge the time. 'Oh, about 10 to 15 minutes, depending on traffic.' 'Good. I will see you then. I love you.' 'I love you too sweetie.' I hang up my phone and go back to driving. Ten minutes later I pull my gold convertible into the driveway, the side farthest from the house as it is the only open path, and then into the garage. Two other vehicles in the driveway. Blue Subaru Impreza WRX and a black 68 Dodge Charger. I shut the car off, hit the button to close the garage door, then get out and walk into the house. 'Honey, I'm home!' I yell out. 'Damn bro, what took you so long? I could have made that drive in 5 minutes in the Charger.' Not the response I expected, but I'll accept it anyway. 'Yeah, but then again your Charger won't keep you out of jail, will it?' I shoot back at my little brother, Damon. 'Where's David?' 'Probably over in Paris again. He claims he has work over there, but I personally think he found himself a nice french woman,' replies one half of the evil force simply called The Twins. 'Too good to come and visit his brothers,'adds Stefani, Damon's girlfriend. Right behind her enters my best friend outside of wrestling, Troy, and his girlfriend Leslie. 'Derek! I hear you got the release. Congrats, man. Ready to spend all your extra time working for me?' 'Troy, I would go back to working at the movie theaters like I was when I met you years and years ago before I worked for you.' Troy laughs a little and we share a slap of the hands before I give Leslie and Stef a hug at the same time. 'Ok, ladies. Where'd you put my wife?' 'Oh, she just ran upstairs for a minute. She said she'd be right back down.' 'Excuse me, I'm gonna go see her real quick. We'll be back in a minute or two.' 'Yeah cuz that's no more than it ever has taken you,' Troy snaps. I turn around to face him while walking backwards. 'This coming from the guy who went 5 years single.' Leslie puts her arms around Troy's waist as he is left standing there speechless. Damon and Stef laugh a little then the four heads out onto the back deck. Five minutes later Cindy and I walk back onto the deck, me wearing a pair of dark blue swim shorts, and my wife in an orange bikini top and a pair of jean shorts, that hug her hips to show her near-perfect figure. The deep tan of her skin matches the light brown streaks in her hair, which in turn matches her gorgeous brown eyes. Everytime I see those eyes, I see everything in her that I saw the day I asked her to marry me, and the same things I saw the day we joined our lives. But, what does this have to do with my brother and friend, as well as their respective girlfriends being at my house? Nothing. 'How's the water?' Cindy asks before unzipping the shorts and stepping out, exposing a little bit more of her slamming body. 'Great.' answers Stefani. 'A tit bit nipply tho, wouldn't you say Stef?' Troy, once again mouthing somebody else's girl. Stef glares at him, then spins around quickly to give the same glare to my brother Damon, who made the mistake of letting her hear him laughing. 'I haven't really had a chance to be in the pool after dark much. Too bad, this is the best time to get in. Water warmer than the air. It makes you not want to get out.' I smile a little, then put my hands on Cindy's hips as we stand on the edge of the pool. 'But the bad thing, people take too long getting in the pool,' I say while giving Cindy a slight push toward the water, then jumping in after her. As soon as she regains her sense of balance in the water, she turns around and leaps on me, trying to dunk me under the shimmering surface. Damn I miss these simple pleasures in life. Maybe I don't want to find a new federation to work for so soon.
