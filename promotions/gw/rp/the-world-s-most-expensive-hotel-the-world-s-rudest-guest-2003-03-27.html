The world's most expensive hotel, the world's rudest guest...

27 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/27 Inside the room of Christina McCoy atop a desk sits a fax machine along with a lap top and printer. From the closed bathroom door running water can be heard running, implying that someone is in the shower. Suddenly the fax machine buzzes to life and the following letter begins to roll out. The Westin Excelsior Via Vittoria Veneto 125 Rome, Italy Phone: [detail withheld] or [detail withheld] Fax: [detail withheld] Dear Ms. McCoy, When we here at the Westin Excelsior we first informed that your great company was going to be sending over one of it’s biggest stars and that no expense should be spared we were overjoyed. The opportunity to have a member of Global Wrestling’s prestigious roster excited every member of our staff, and even more so when we received a list of his accomplishments. The list of accolades seemed to go on forever. Not only the youngest wrestler in Global Wrestling history, but the youngest world champion as well? Then add to that list a pair of platinum title reigns, as well as a pair of primestar and cruiserweight title reigns alongside a very impressive reign as a tag champion. We were positively awestruck at the fact that the star we were rolling out the red carpet for had such an astounding record. Preparations for your roster member’s arrival began immediately. The reservation of our largest and most lavish suite was made, our specialty wait staff went through rigorous maintenance training, and arrangements were made for the as yet un-named star to arrive via a private jet complete with every modern luxury that could be expected. We also went so far as to reach a deal with a local car manufacturer to allow the superstar the use of their newest model sports car. That company Ms. McCoy? Ferrari. The car? A brand new 612 Scaglietti. Midnight blue with custom leather interior, an expansive MP3 collection pre-downloaded into the deck’s memory, voice activated global positioning system, a twelve speaker system, and an engine powerful enough to successfully simulate the sensation of being fired from a cannon. So with all of these preparations coming together quite magnificently the only thing left to do was lay out the red carpet and set up the velvet ropes. Then, in response to an inquisitive letter from myself in which the identity of the un-named Global Wrestling roster member was requested, I received a second letter from you. Upon opening it my eyes were immediatly drawn to three phrases in particular... Ruler of your universe... Rocker of your world... And reason you breathe. At which point mine promptly stopped... After being quickly resuscitated by our emergency medical staff the realization of what would soon be unleashed upon this prestigious hotel and it’s affiliates quickly began to set in... Deacon Holmes was coming to The Westin Excelsior. Had I known this important fact from the get go I would have never gone through with such a rigorous gauntlet of preparations, mainly because I would have never signed the contract guaranteeing him so many privileges. His entire stay here has been nothing short of an abomination. Problems began before he’d even boarded the very expensive jet that was very expensive jet that we’d arranged for him. Did I mention that it was VERY expensive? Well at any rate. He was late. And when he finally did show up with his girlfriend in tow he denied all claims that he was anything less than punctual, deciding instead to classify himself as “fashionably late”. Whatever he chose to refer to his blatant tardiness as, it cost our jet it’s lane and proceeded to further delay the jet’s departure. For which he repeatedly blamed us, although in actuality it was his fault. After over an hour wait, which we had to pay extra for, the jet finally gained clearance for takeoff from an unused lane. Once in the air, it was revealed that Mr. Holmes has a terrible fear of flying. He required every window in the jet to be closed in order to keep the clouds out of his sight and would fly into some sort of wild eyed terror anytime there was the slightest bit of turbulence. Finally, after a little “coercing” from his girlfriend and SEVERAL bottles of champagne, Deacon finally calmed down and at least attempted to enjoy the flight. Once inebriation set in and he was actually calm enough to pay attention to the plasma screen, Mr. Holmes decided that the in flight movie selection didn’t include a great enough volume of “black” movies, and refused to watch anything in which a black man was the first to die. Or die at all for that matter. In fact, at one point during the flight he went so far as to try and destroy the DVD “Glory” by casting it out the door. Luckily, we were able to stop him before he was able to open it. Thank god. My heart is still racing from that episode... After what seemed like an eternity, we finally landed in Rome. As mentioned earlier, the very instant Mr. Holmes stepped off of our jet his feet met not wet pavement, but soft red carpet. The velvet rope lined path from the plane’s side led directly to the door of a brand new midnight blue Ferrari 612 Scaglietti, which was held open by a waiting attendant. He and his girlfriend slid into the car with great enthusiasm. The smiles on their faces reflected the sun’s light. Finally, we’d done something right. After giving them a few minutes to acclimate themselves to the masterpiece’s surprisingly roomy interior and take note of all the special features. With a beaming smile I made my way over to the driver’s side of the car and placed the key directly into Deacon’s outstretched hand. They jingled slightly as I dropped them in his palm. The only sound I heard after that was a set of racing series tires screeching in protest as a powerful twelve cylinder gasoline powered beast roared to life. The coupe sped out of sight like a bat escaping the depths of hell, leaving behind only the resonating reverberation of the car’s impressive twelve speaker stereo system and a think cloud of dense white smoke. At that very instant, I realized what a mistake it had been to place a nineteen year old in control of five hundred and forty horses. The vehicle’s smooth lines raced away with a certified mad man at the wheel, and I had put him there. Worry and guilt soon subsided though as I realized what I’d finally done. After several hours of being cooped up in a jet with him while traveling faster than a speeding bullet seven miles above sea level, I had finally rid myself of him. At least for a little while... After several hours, the global positioning system I was hoping would somehow malfunction showed me that dreams do not always come true as the very same car I’d seen race away from the jet we’d arrived in appeared in front of the hotel and rolled to a stop in front of a member of our valet parking staff. Seeing this, I decided to meet them outside, and putting on my best fake smile, greeted them and attempted to make small talk about where their travels had taken them but was abruptly cut short by something that had caught my eye. Peering into the vehicle as the valet slid into the buttery soft leather driver’s seat, I noticed that the car’s glove compartment hung open with several traffic citations hanging out of it and even more adoring the floor. Upon later inspection, the car had also had four of it’s speakers blown. Silently fuming, I decided to let this particular incident slide as it had gotten him out of my hair for a number of hours. Once again masking my contempt, I decided to make small talk my asking if he’d enjoyed the ride. His reply... “It was alright.” Alright? We gave him a finely tuned piece of Italian machinery and all he had to say was “It was alright”. Now as outrageous as this was, what he followed that statment with somehow managed to boggle the mind even more. “It wasn’t really what I had in mind, think ya’ll could hook me up with something faster for tomorrow?” The fake smile I gave him was all I could do to keep from ripping his head off as I ushered him through the front door and into the lobby on our way up to their suite. The “Villa la Cupola”. By far the finest in our illustrious hotel, and one of the most expensive in the entire world. Although, the money is well spent... The suite has all things Roman and excessive, a cupola(or vaulted ceiling as you Americans call them), a Pompeii-style Jacuzzi pool, frescoes and stained glass windows just to name a few. Located on the fifth and sixth floor underneath the cupola of the hotel, the suite covers 6,099 square feet and has an additional 1,808 square feet of balconies and terraces. The cupola itself is entirely hand-frescoed, and the fifth floor features the master bedroom, a terraced study that was paneled in Italian walnut and a living room. The stained glass windows in the living room detail allegories of a mythological figure paired with a modern one, such as Atlas and Television, Hypnosis and Neurosis, Hermes and Marketing, and Hermaphrodite and Fashion. The downstairs also has a private kitchen, and the dining room features an antique Murano glass chandelier, a private wine cabinet stocked to the brim with the finest bottles of wine we could find, and an antique mosaic-tile-covered dining room. And that's just the beginning. What really makes this suite over the top is a private cinema with Dolby surround sound. Such luxury makes even the private elevator that leads up to the fitness area and Jacuzzi--complete with mosaic floors, vaulted ceilings and frescoes--seem ordinary. The painted horizons on the frescoes were designed to match perfectly with the real Roman one. Now if that's not living red carpeted velvet roped la dolce vita I don’t know what is. As though all of this wasn’t enough, I also went through the trouble of placing the hotel’s best chef on elusive duty to their orders as well as giving them unlimited access to one of the foremost, and not to mention most expensive, spas in all of Italy. Once I opened the door to their room the sound of two jaws dropping to the floor soon followed. After giving them adequate time to roll their tongues back into their mouths I gave them a quick tour of both floors covered by the room, as well as all the things it includes. At the conclusion of the tour I ushered them into the master bedroom to show them the reason we’d advised them to pack light and bring no clothes other than those on their backs. I guided them to a door inside of the luxurious master bedroom and urged them to look inside and see what they find. Behind said door they found an expansive walk in closet large enough to serve as an extra bedroom. Split in two, allowing one side for each of them, hanging from wall to wall were clothes ranging all the way from pool side apparel to evening and formal wear. We even included shoes and all necessary accessories. But these weren’t just any normal brands we had provided them with, these were pieces from the world renowned Italian designers Valentino Garavani and Laura Biagioth. In the Roman fashion world these two names tend to stand out. The eternally elegant Laura Biagiotti is Rome's queen of fashion. Luxurious knitwear, sumptuous silk separates and lots of white and cream are her trademarks. When Valentino set up his alta moda in 1959, his clientele included Jackie Kennedy, Sophia Loren and Audrey Hepburn. While his couture collections featuring superb evening gowns have always been inaccessible to all but the most wealthy of customers, his ready-to-wear lines for both men and women, introduced in the 1970s, have become staples of the fashionable set. Each article of clothing placed in their closet ranged in price anywhere from two thousand to ten thousand American dollars. Rummaging through such an impressive collection of clothing caused their eyes to practically bulge from their sockets, at which point I laid yet another surprise on them. A set of his and hers Movado Portico watches. Each with a platinum bracelet and case, a bezel set with twelve diamonds, and yet another marking the twelve “o clock hour. For some reason I thought that adorning the two of them in elegant clothing would cause some of that same elegance to be instilled in them. Was I ever wrong... I’d just insured that from now on they’d be impeccably dressed while they were unleashing hell upon the city. I’d simply created a pair of incredibly well dressed devils. At the arrival of his friend, a Mr. Johnathan Zero, who was accompanied by an Asian woman who’s name I believe to be Victoria, Deacon ordered one of the optional five additional rooms that can be attached to the Villa la Cupola suite be given to them. From that point on they became absolutely unbearable. They kept their private chef that I’d placed under his control up all hours of the night concocting everything from “hot wings” to the finest in Italian cuisine. At dinner the four of them would order SEVERAL bottles of our most expensive wine, which more often than not hovered somewhere around the two hundred dollar range. But they didn’t actually drink the wine, no more than two glasses would be poured from each one before Deacon would order it disposed of, informing a member of our wait staff that he was “ready for something new”. On one particularly infuriating occasion, he ordered the best wine in the house, and upon it’s arrival began to guzzle it directly from the bottle like some sort of barbarian. Then, claiming it “tasted like ass” he proceeded to spit the wine he’d drawn into his mouth directly in the face of the waiter and demand a “40oz.'. What, pray tell, is a 40oz.” Upon the inquisition as to what exactly such a thing might be he replied “Malt liquor!”, before standing in his seat, ripping off his three thousand dollar Valentino Garavani dress shirt, and exclaiming “I’m Rick James bitch!” while hurling a wine glass across the restaurant. Needless to say... this caused quite a scene. When we informed him we had none of this 'malt liquor' he insisted we 'wasnt' representin'... nor 'keepin? it real' before throwing some sort of tantrum and storming out of the restaurant. Fearing that the young Mr. Holmes may have become delirious somehow during his stay due to his actions earlier in the evening, I decided I would check in on him to see how he was feeling. What I found was all out bedlam. Apparently, after dinner he and his entourage decided to travel to a nearby club, which one was actually sober enough to drive is a mystery to me. At any rate, Deacon somehow managed to get kicked out, and almost arrested in the process, for inciting an altercation with lesser known Global Wrestling star “American Youth” Josh. Holmes allegedly threw a drink into the other roster member’s face, then used his head to smash the glass which once contained the drink as “American Youth” stumbled backward unable to see due to alcohol being in his eyes. After being forcefully escorted from the club by local authorities, it would seem that Deacon decided to invite those inside the club to attend a party inside his hotel room that he could as they dragged him out. I hear that he also invited those standing outside the club waiting to get in as well. The Dolby surround sound system attached to the private cinema the room enjoys was now being used to pump out loud and vulgar dance music. From what I could see the room’s wine cabinet had been picked bone dry as seemingly everyone in the body to body tightly packed human tuna can Deacon had turned his room into was grasping a bottle in their hand. Several empty ones were also found lying around in various locations. As I exited the room in disgust Deacon began to lead the mob in a chant of “Fuck Josh”. The following day Mr. Holmes approached me about having a “care” package sent to the hotel room of the same man. Apparently, this “American Youth” fellow had attempted suicide after an embarrassing loss to Holmes after constantly touting himself as the “better man”, and Deacon wanted to attempt to bury the hatchet. Or so he lead me to believe... Hours later he handed me a cardboard box containing a pair of his shoes produced by NIKE, the edition of Sports Illustrated on which his face adorns the cover, and a framed picture. The picture was of him pinning this Josh character in front of a roaring crowd amidst a hail of camera flashes. A personalized signature in the bottom right corner read as follows... To he who made it all possible, Josh... A stay at the Westin Excelsior in the Villa la Cupola - $16,000 a night For me? Free. A pair of Nike Shox Air Genocides - $115 For me? Free. A brand new Ferrari 612 Scaglietti - $1,000 a day For me? Free. Proving beyond the shadow of a doubt that you can’t beat me when I’m healthy? - PRICELESS! Sincerely, Deacon “The Better Man” Holmes. He also adorned his signature with a quick sketch drawing of a human hand with it’s middle finger extended. A letter was also written between each knuckle. “F” and “U” I’d seen the ongoing struggle between these two, and in all honesty, I couldn’t blame him. I actually got a chance to watch the match and saw first hand that everything Josh had been saying in the weeks prior failed to hold true. In the end he even had to resort to the use of an outside weapon, yet still failed to claim victory for himself. Indeed, Deacon Holmes is a truly phenomenal wrestler, but the man is also a walking manifestation of everything that is wrong with the world! His brash ways and all around rudeness has soured the atmosphere here at the Westin Excelsior. During the course of his stay here we lost several of our finest employees, the majority of whom were female, citing “sexual harassment” as a reason for their resignation, and more than a few guests demanded full refunds for their rooms. And let’s not even venture into the subject of Deacon’s room. Which was completely decimated come check out time. Furniture was broken, we found several holes in the walls, the carpets we stained, and several of the frescoes had been vandalized with spray paint! Also, the bed of the master bedroom was broken, and it’s sheets in deplorable condition. I have little doubt this is due to the fact that he and his girlfriend spent most of their time here going at it like nymphomaniacal rabbits... who’d just broken free from prison. The magnificent Ferrari 612 Scaglietti we’d loaned to him for his stay was returned with a glove compartment full of parking and traffic citations(which the hotel was forced to pay), several scratches to the paint job, and severely blown speakers. The majority of the clothes we’d provided them with for their stay were missing, and those they left behind were either ripped or had liquor stains on them. So despite the large sum of money given to us by Global Wrestling to cover Deacon’s stay with us, it is beyond the shadow of a doubt that we have certainly much more. Not only in the way of money, but personnel and patrons as well. I’m currently devoting most of my time to praying that we somehow manage to break even... But at any rate, in short... Deacon Holmes, nor any other member of the Global Wrestling roster or staff is EVER to be given a room at the Westin Excelsior from this point on. Sincerely, Giovanni Fortellini
