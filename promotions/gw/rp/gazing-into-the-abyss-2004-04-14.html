Gazing into the abyss

14 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/14/04 He who fights too long against dragons becomes a dragon himself; and if you gaze too long into the abyss, the abyss will gaze into you. ? Nietzche ---- “You know what?' Brick says to Train over the cellphone. 'I don’t need no damn morality lessons coming from you.” “Maybe not, but you damn sure need a reality check!' Train shoots back, before sighing. 'Look...I know I ain’t the voice of restraint or nothing like that, but...Look man. I went through a whole lot of shit to get out of the crap you’re asking me to get you into. More than that... you’re about to take something that’s in the arena and take it to the streets. Dogg.... there are some bridges that once you cross” You ain’t got to burn them. They burn themselves behind you. You’re about to escalate this shit to a level you won’t be able to bring it back down from. You do this... you use the dudes I got for you... There ain’t no going back. Now... I’m asking you one more time. You sure you wanna do this?” “Let me tell you something. I don’t give half a damn if Salaok puts his hands on me. Hurts me. Attacks ME. But see, now” He’s threatened my friend and my family. I didn’t take the shit here, he did. Now let me ask you... if somebody took it there with you.... What would you do?” There’s a long pause before anyone says another word. “Ain’t about me, dogg.” Train says somberly. “It’s on you now.” With that, the call ends, the shrill buzz of a dial tone taking over until Brick flips the phone closed. Suddenly, there’s another knock at the door. Brick quickly moves over and swings the door wide open. There, on his front stoop stand two men. One is heavy set with a thick bushy beard, a cold expression on his face as he stands like a dark shadow in his black suit. The other is tall and slender, his silvery suit shining in the bright sunshine as Brick sees his reflection in the man’s shades. “Yeah?” Brick asks, narrowing his eyes at the two rather unsavory looking strangers. “Yeah....” begins the man in the silvery suit. “I’m JJ. This is Street. A homeboy of ours said you needed a favor.” 'Ah, y'all are Train's boys. Let me just get something and we can go somewhere and talk.' Brick walks back in and walks to his desk. He opens a drawer and pulls out a manila folder before walking back out the door. 'OK, let's go.' ---- It's the middle of the day in Fort Greene Park. Brick, JJ and Street sit on a bench while people pass them by in both directions, walking their dogs, strolling with babies, jogging, etc. 'Aight, so what's this favor Brick reaches into the folder and pulls out an 8x10 black and white photo of Salaok. 'This is Salaok Davis. For the past five years, he's been a pain in the ass for me. He hurt a very good friend of mine to get to me. someone who was like my brother. He's threatened my family on a number. What I need from y'all is to help me let him know I ain't takin' his shit no more.' 'You want us to fuck him up for you?' asks Street. 'No, he'd be expecting something like that.' 'Then what?' asks JJ. Brick then pulls out another photo, this one of an attractive woman with fair skin and platinum blond hair. 'This is his sister, Eve Davis. Right now, she's the only person in his life who still cares about him and who he still cares about. He has brothers, but no one has seen or heard from them in a while. And he lost his wife and children a few weeks ago. 'Hol' up, man, hol' up. You want us to beat on some female?' 'Not exactly. I need you to bring her to me.' 'What do you mean, 'bring her to you'? Suddenly, Brick's cellphone begins to ring. 'Hold on just a minute. Hello?' 'Jamal...' 'Monica?' Brick straightens up. 'Yeah, I tried to call you at home, but you didn't answer. Where are you?' 'I'm out...running some errands.' Right about this time, Brick starts to feel his heart sink. Something about hearing his sister's voice makes him think about the plan he's about to implement. 'Oh, well...Mom's been trying to get in touch with you. You are gonna make it to dinner on Sunday 'Y-yeah, sure...' 'Are you okay?' 'Yeah, I'm fine. Listen, Monica, I'll talk to you later, okay? Bye.' Then, as he hangs up, it all hits him. What the fuck am I doing?, Brick thinks to himself. Train was right. This is going too far. Brick is realizing his worst nightmare might be coming true. He might be turning into the very thing, the very individual he hates the most. He might be turning into Salaok. And there is no way he is going to let that happen. He needs to back away from the abyss before the abyss gazes into him, or worse...before he falls in. 'So, are we gonna do this or what?' 'Sorry, fellas. There's been a change in plans.'
