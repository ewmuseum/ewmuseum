BLOG #3 (PPV#2)

27 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/27/04 According to what I have been told as a young child, everything happens for a reason, and if that's the case, me losing to Josh for the World Title was meant to happen for a reason. I look at it as, that maybe in this period of time I'm not supposed to be the World Champion, but to be honest with you, I tasted it, and I want more. So that brings me up to my next topic of disscussion, The Television Title... Now sadly, Rich Sherwin was hurt pretty bad in a car accident and won't be able to defend the title. Rich is a very good friend of mine, a lot of times we'll sit near eachother on the plane, my deepest wishes for a speedy recovery go out to my friend and colleague. I know when he comes back he'll be looking to get that gold around his waist one more time. Some people have been asking me where my brother Charles has been lately, well he's around, just right now he needs to time get situated with his family now in Altoona. It's nice to have all of them around now, family runs deeper than any rivers or oceans on Earth or anywhere else in the Universe for that matter. This Monday night on High Noon ought to be great. You know I love a challenge and well I have one by facing two up and coming superstars in GW. Jonathan Drachen and El Mariachi Jaun Gonzalez are deserving to be in this match, but that doesn't mean they deserve to win. I am going to win this match, in fact I am also going to dedicate this match to my good friend Captain Retirement Rich Sherwin. So Rich if you're reading this while recovering, let it be known this Match is for you... So on that note my friends it's time that I go, I am sleepy and I need my rest. Rest is important if you didn't know already. Keep it safe my friends, and tune into High Noon on Monday night only on Pay Per View...
