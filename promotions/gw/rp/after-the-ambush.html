After the ambush

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He’d been waiting weeks for it. It haunted his every waking thought. It consumed every fiber of his being. Ever since Dead Zone, Brick had been looking to even the score with the World champion Sage Sabbot. Tonight was to have been the night he settled the score. But it wasn’t meant to be. Earlier in the evening, Leviathan, newly inducted into the Nevermore, had been arrested and was being detained. Salaok had sent Sabbot to post bail, but neither of them returned in time. Time had passed until it was time for Brick to face Sabbot in the ring, but Sabbot still hadn't returned with Leviathan. It was also at that time that Salaok introduced Stan Stone. After Salaok leveled Brick with a shovel, Stone destroyed Brick with his finisher, the Point of Impact. Only problem was, Brick sat straight up and the brawl continued until security ran down to the ring to stop it. As the commotion was being dealt with, Christina McCoy named a match. Next week, Brick will go one-on-one against Derek Frost. If Brick wins he goes into the Main Event of the upcoming PPV. It’s a match Brick definitely intends to win. But at the moment, Brick’s mind is still on the ambush from earlier. “Brick!” Brick turns to see Ely Braxton and Christina McCoy coming towards him. “Hey, you okay, buddy?” “Yeah. Take more than those punks to put me down.” “Well, I hope so,” says Christina, “because I need to know if you’re gonna be ready for next week.” “If I ain’t ready now, I’ll be ready next week. I suppose Sabbot is still tryin” to bail Leviathan out.” “Far as anyone knows.” “Typical. I ain’t gotta say Salaok set this whole thing up. It goes without saying.” “Tell me about it,” says Braxton. “Sally knew you were gonna kick his lapdog’s sorry ass tonight.” “Yeah, he could have sent anybody else. He could have sent Kullypso or Quartz or even gone himself, but he sent Sabbot to protect him. He knew I couldn’t wait to rip his ass apart in that ring, so he sent him out to be safe. Then he set me up in the ring. Well, I’ve got something for his ass the next time I see him.” “Don’t worry about Salaok. Worry about next week. You’ve got Frost to deal with.” “That’s something I wanted to ask you, Christina. What the hell does Frost have to do with whether or not I get into the main event” I thought I was entitled to a rematch automatically. And if I pinned Sabbot tonight, our next match would have been for the title.” “Well?” Christina says, hesitantly. “Well what?” “Take it easy, Brick,” says Braxton. “Before Dead Zone, Derek had a special stipulation added. “What kind of stipulation?” Brick growls. “If he were to beat Kullypso, he would have the power to book himself and Mark Kullypso in any match he wants. Word has it that he’s going to book himself into the main event at Whiplash.” There’s an expression used backstage for whenever Brick loses his temper: “Mount Brickatoa is erupting.” “THAT MOTHERFUCKER!!!” This is just such an occasion. “Hey, calm down, Brick!” “Who the fuck approved this”!” “Well, Logan gave the okay before he got hurt.” “Yeah, well, it’s a good thing he’s in the hospital now, or I might have put him there myself!” “You still got a shot. Just beat Frost and you’re in the main event.” “Oh, I have no doubt I can beat Frost. It’s just I don’t want to have to deal with the bullshit.” “I know, Brick,” says Christina. “Just get ready for next week.” “Fine,” Brick murmurs.
