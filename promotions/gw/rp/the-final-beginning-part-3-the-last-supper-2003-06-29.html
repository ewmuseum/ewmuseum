The Final Beginning. Part 3 - The Last Supper

29 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29/6/03 A day had passed since Lisha had given him the news that not only would he be a father again, but that Justin Marks, a man who he didn't trust one inch was throwing them a dinner in order to celebrate the event. Normally this wouldn't be a problem except for the fact that it was Justin who was doing this for him and therefore the idea that a reason other than the one in which he knew of was behind all of this. Should Salaok just not attend and hope that Lisha would one day forgive him for it? The thought had crossed his mind more than once but each time he knew that it would more than likely just end in more trouble for him than what it would be worth. What on earth could Justin do in a room full of people? It's not like he could try and shoot Salaok or anything. Not that he would do such a thing anyway. The idea of shooting someone would be beyond Justin, not because of the fact that it was able to kill, but because it simply wasn't personal enough for him. The hatred that Salaok and Justin had left for each other over the years couldn't be measured and yet Eve had to choose him of all people to get married to. But the time was near and Salaok could hear some of the guests arriving down stairs. Yes it was in his own castle that this dinner was being through. Justin wasn't stupid, why do the work when Salaok's pays people to do it normally. The smell of the turkey was filling the air and had reached the second story of the castle where Salaok's room was. As if the smell of turkey wasn't enough, they had also made fresh bread earlier in the day and the smell was driving him crazy. Lisha had asked him not to eat anything throughout the afternoon in order to make sure that he ate everything at dinner. Not that he ever would have left a single thing on his plate normally, but Lisha seemed to have this idea that the amount of food for the night was going to be huge and she didn't want to be eating leftovers for the rest of the week. A few of the voices downstairs seemed to sound familiar but Salaok just passed it off and didn't pay any further attention to it. It was stupid of him to even think that he would hear Brian's voice in his own house. Standing in front of the wall sized mirror, Salaok looks at himself. He hadn't realized before but a small patch of his fridge was slowly turning white with the stress of running Global Incorporated. No one had said anything to him about it, but then again, why would they? Would you want to tell your boss who is known for making people disappear that he is going grey? No. With his hand, he brushes his long black hair back out of his face, revealing the white orbs that are his eyes. So many people had asked him where he gets the contact lens to cover the entire eye with white but they never seem to be able to understand that his eyes are naturally like that. A lot of people think it is some sort of problem caused through sickness, but in truth it is just how he was born. The doctors rarely see such a thing, but it is known to happen from time to time. And with his eyes being so white and standoutish, it had never done anything but good for the evil identity that Salaok had taken on over the years. As he lets his hair fall again, he realizes that he doesn't have his brother's rings on his hands which is a rarety these days. His brother hadn't been around lately and in fact seemed to have disappear off the face of the earth, or so Salaok thought. Walking over to the desk at his bedside, he opens the top draw and pulls out a black box and opens it. The inside is padded with red and black fabric and holds the ten rings which his older brother had given to him some years ago now. One by one the rings are placed on his fingers, turning his hands into metal weapons. Once the rings are one his hands, one punch with them on would knock out even Quartz, not that Salaok would ever even try because he even knew that if it didn't work and Quartz didn't fall, then Salaok would be in a world of trouble. Plus, he had no need to punch Quartz, right? Another voice rings out from downstairs and again it sounds like someone he knows but again, why would such a person be in his house. Surely Justin wouldn't go to such a large amount of trouble to ruin such an event as the announcement of a second Davis child, would he? What would Justin have to gain from inviting all of Salaok's enemies to his own house? Apart from the knowledge that with them there, it would be so easy to destroy Salaok. But then a voice from downstairs sounded out above the others and it was one that not only did he recognize, but he was hoping to see tonight. Johnathon was hear and by the sounds of the heavy foots steps coming up the stairs, he was coming to see Salaok straight away before doing anything else. The door of Salaok's room swings open and slams against the wall with the force of the push. There in the frame of the doorway stands his brother. The feared Johnathon Davis. The Scarecrow. The Harvester of Minds. He looked stronger than the last time he had seen him, and his blonde hair looked to have grown a fair amount as well. His face however still had that cold expression on it as usual with that evil one sided grin which he had become known for. His eyes icy blue, cold enough to see right through even the hardest of men. The words hissed with every movement of his mouth. 'Welch's, Marks? What is this brother?' Salaok reaction was one that everyone would have expected from him. Complete and utter surprise. Was Johnathon telling Salaok that both Brian and Vincent were in his castle? 'What? They're here?' 'More than that, they are sitting down at the table talking with Justin and looking like they are here for the meal.' Justin had done it too him again. It was all a game to Justin and Salaok knew this. So the one way in which to not allow Justin to win, would be to simply not let it effect him. Not let it get to him. The hatred building up inside of him seemed to grow and grow as Johnathon lead Salaok out of the room and towards the stair case. His heart beating fast with every step and his stomach beginning to tie itself in knots. This is exactly why he didn't trust Justin, and why he never should have allow this to go ahead. But nooooo, Lisha thought it would be a good way of getting the family down and having a conversation. But instead, Justin seems to have invited the wrong family to the dinner. The Welch's weren't a part of the family and it was eating Salaok alive watching them sit at the table where he would eat his meals. From the top of the staircase Johnathon and Salaok watched over the three men downstairs like eagles looking for prey. Their eyes never faultering even once they saw where Brian was sitting. Almost a year ago, Brian carved his name into the dark red wood table and it had never been repaired. But now Brian sits at the exact spot where his name is carved, almost proud of the fact that he has his own place at Salaok's dinner table. The feeling in the air was anything but friendly for all those involved and the only one who seemed to be enjoying this was Justin Marks. Both Brian and Vincent visually seemed uncomfortable in their surroundings but Justin seemed to be telling them something that should have calmed them down. What is what couldn't be heard from the watching point in which the two brothers has now taken up. Thuds begun to sound from behind the brothers but neither man moved an inch as both knew what that sound was. For most people, turning around and looking to see what the noise was would be normal, but Johnathon and Salaok had know Quartz almost their entire life and knew the heavy thuds which sound when ever he walks. Being well over seven feet tall would do that to a man. Standing directly behind Johnathon and Salaok, he places a hand on each of their shoulders and stares down at the table with them. 'I thought I heard them.' His voice, deep and rough sounds but gains no response from the two men it should have. However, it does gain reaction from those down stairs. Vincent looks up at Quartz, whether it was the low tone of his voice that he heard, or the size of his body off in the distance that caught his attention is unknown, but what is known is that Vincent hadn't come alone. Vincent motions for someone to come to him and then a small female figure walks up to him and pulls up a seat next to him. With her back turned to the viewers, they can't tell who it is, or at least that was the case until Vincent pointed to them and she turned around. Rose. She looks up and catches Quartz's eye and all seems to stop for the two for a brief second until both exchange a nod and then Rose goes back to talking to Vincent. Almost half an hour goes by of the uncomfortable situation before finally Lisha appears at the bottom of the stairs and then begins to glide up towards the three men. Salaok's eyes divert from the Welch's and Justin to her but the other two keep a straight stare. Her figure was one to be admired, and Salaok did just that every time that he saw her. Her long legs move seductively and continue the flow through her hips which was nothing less than hypnotizing. She smiles at him as he catches his eye and finally walks all the way up to him and places a hand on his check to calm him down. 'Sal, do not worry. They were searched for weapons before they were even allowed in the door. I'm not that stupid Sal. Lets go down, eat and that will be the end of it. There are three of you, and there are three of them. Plus you know that Sage, Mark, Grendel and Flare are all going to be coming. Those three would be fools to try and do something when they are out numbered so greatly.' Her voice of reason was something that Salaok just never had. Guess that is one of the things that made them a great duo. Salaok lets out a small sigh before Johnathon speaks up. 'She's right Sal. We could destroy them if they were to do anything anyway.' Johnathon didn't get a chance to finish what he was saying as Quartz had to come in as he couldn't wait any longer. 'Yeah, and I'm fucking hungry.' Whether or not Quartz had meant that to be funny is unknown, but it drew a smile to Salaok's face and with that the group of four all walked down the stairs together like the close nit group they had been over the many years. Without a word being spoken from either of the two groups, Salaok, Johnathon, Quartz and Lisha all sit down at the table. The silence is more than deadly as the glares are exchanged from one side of the tale to the other. Justin sitting at one end of the table and Salaok at the other. Their eyes never moving from each other. 'Hello brother-in-law.' The way that Justin said those few words was enough to drive Salaok crazy inside but he refuses to show it. Always using the fact that he is married to Salaok's sister as a card of attack against Salaok. Knowing full well that there was nothing that he hated more than having Justin in his family. Thankfully a knock on the door sounds as the metal door knock slams against the heavy wood, everyone but Salaok and Justin turn around to see who it will be. The guard for the night walks on up to the door and swings it open, blocking the view of those at the table. He nods to who ever is on the other side of the door and then steps aside letting them have a direct path into the castle. However, it isn't just one man, but four. Mark Kullypso, Grendel, Flare and Sage Sabbot all stroll into the room before stopping dead in the tracks in seeing the three men sitting at the far end of the table. The silence again fills the room as the four men take their seat. The table had been set for thirteen people but only twelve were there which seemed to keep capturing Salaok's attention. His glare would go from Justin to the empty seat time after time and every time Justin saw him look at the seat, he would smile to Salaok, as if to tell him that he knew who it would be and Salaok would have to find out for himself. Vincent and Rose talked most of the night leaving Brian and Justin to share small conversation with no depth to them at all. But the talks weren't why either man was there. They were there to create the tension that Justin wanted to create. Keeping Salaok on his toes and always guessing as to who that last seat would be filled by. Whoever was going to be sitting there, they were about to miss the first meal as the two cooks and the waiters bring the food out all at once and as Salaok goes to dig into the food to take his mind off things for a brief moment, Justin's voice rings out. 'What Salaok, you don't have anything to say thanks for? Are you that ungrateful?' Although not a single word was uttered back by Salaok, the look he gave Justin seemed to say everything that was needing to be said. A raise of the eyebrow, the crossing of his arms over his chest and the resting back in his seat seemed to tell Justin that if he was wanting to say grace, then he would have to do it himself. And Justin was just the one to do it. 'In the name of the father, the son and the holy ghost.' Justin's voice was clear and meaningful, but Salaok's trailed behind him, low in volume and deep in tone. 'Ateh, Malkuth, ve-Geburah, ve-Gebulah, le-OLAHM, Amen.' Justin pauses knowing that Salaok is only doing this to get at him. But then Justin continues to say grace. 'Dear Lord, we are gathered here tonight in this place paid by crimes and murder, to give you thanks for the life in which you have granted to our dear friends in Lisha and Salaok Davis. Their second child in now on the way and I ask you to keep this young one safe and sound. Thank you oh Lord for the food in which you have so provided for us tonight as we are all unworthy of this grand gesture. And more so father, thank you for allowing us all to be here tonight for this special occasion.' Again both Justin and Salaok do their own versions of the cross of the chest and without another word being spoken, everyone around the table begins to dig into the plates which sit in front of them.
