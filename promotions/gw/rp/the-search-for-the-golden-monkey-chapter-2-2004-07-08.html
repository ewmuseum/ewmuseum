The search for the golden monkey [Chapter 2]

8 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-8-04 El Serpenti stood in front of his wardrobe Towel warped around his waist. Shelf’s full of different types of masks. All with the same face design. Some with horns some with fins some with openings on the top for his hair others solid. El Serpenti grabbed a black one with no fins. Serpenti pulled the mask on over his head and pulled his hair though the hole in the top.. He then moved to area with different costumes. He pulled out a solid long sleeved black one with the same design on the face running down the sides with the sun in the middle of the chest. He tossed the suit on to the bed next to an assortment of other things, a hand pistol crossbow, several different bolts, a pack, a coil of cord, several throwing daggers, a utility knife, flashlight, and glow sticks. Different things one would need for what Serpenti was about to do. ---- Fade out ---- Fade in ---- Serpenti stands looking in the mirror fully dressed in his attire. Two pouches strapped to the sides of his legs. One on his arms two knife sheaths holding his throwing knifes on his forearms. A belt across his waist held a few other pouches, the glow sticks stuck out of pockets in the belt. Serpenti grabbed a long trench coat with a hood and put it on. He then grabbed his pack and placed it on, over the coat. From the pack stretched a length of cord attached to a crossbow bolt. Serpenti picked up his crossbow and placed the bolt in it and clipped it to his belt. Slowly he picked up one last thing. His sword. He pulled the sword out of the scabbard to a hiss the blade was a bright azure in color, the hilt was gold with a flame design going up the blade its self. The handle black leather with gold thread wrapped around. The pommel held a blue stone that sparkled. Serpenti twilled the blade a few times and then placed it back in its scabbard and clipped it to his belt as well hiding it under the trench coat. El Serpenti tonight was no longer the Luchador loved by his homeland. Tonight in his black attire he was once again La Serpiente De la Muerte. Famed Pirate. Not many knew the two were the same, although he often though the names would give that away, but so far it had not. Tonight how ever he would not be sailing on his ship that sat hidden in a cove on a small island in the Gulf of Mexico. Tonight he would be heading as he had many times in the past into the darkness of a tomb. Serpenti was more then a Pirate he was a treasure hunter. He was not a Pirate of old. Many mistook what a True Pirate was. Many believed they were those who pillaged and burned and at times committed acts of rape. No. A True Pirate was a free spirit un-chained by the laws of the land, with a lust for gold and adventure. Serpenti walks out of his room of his newly purchased mansion located outside of Dallas Texas. The halls and everyu thing is pretty bare as the movers have not finished relocation his home.. Serpenti takes a deep breath and heads down the stairs. “Out on another venture master” Came the sound of a male voice. Serpenti stops and looks over his shoulder. Spying the old man in his suit and bow tie. “Yes me Amigo, Bentley. I should be back for breakfast.” Serpenti says with confidence. “Very well Master. Breakfast shall be waiting for you.” Bentley says nodding as he turns back to hanging a large picture of a ship at sea.. Serpenti sighs again and heads down the spiral staircase leading into the carport. ---- The lights of the carport flicker on as Serpenti steps off the steps. Several cars a couple of trucks and a few motorcycles. Serpenti walks past each one. Stopping and thinking at each one. “Humm.. No” No? No? No? No? No? Ahh Yes!” Serpent says with satisfaction at his selection. “Hummer Two! This will be a good field test for it.” “A good selection Master.” The Indian known as Talon Eagleclaw says as he walks towards Serpenti. “It is completely stocked and loaded. The upgrades Braxton asked for have been implemented. “Good. Me Amigo Talon. ? Serpenti says looking at the cane in Talons right hand. “How is the leg?” Serpenti asks raising his brow. Talon takes a deep breath. “It is getting better. Time will heal it.” Talon says then changes the subject fast as he hands Serpenti a pair sun glasses with a mic attached. “Here put these on.” Talon says. Serpenti again raises his brow as he takes the head gear from Talon. Serpenti hold it up then places them on. ? I look like some sort of Avenger or something from those X men movies you like so much Talon.” Serpenti Says as he looks at his self in the window of the Hummer. “Hehe. Yeah. Well that’s partly where I got the idea. It is a slimmed down version of military gear. Now come on lets go. I’ll explain what it does on the way.” Talon says as he opens the drivers door. Serpenti tilts his head side ways. “We?” Serpenti asks confused. “Well yeah, We as in the two of us. The headgear is linked to a computer on board. You’re going to need some help. I been working on this since I heard you were after that amulet. Braxton told me. I have files on the amulet and the tomb it is located in.” Talon says as he climbs into the Hummer. Serpenti shakes his head and climbs into the passenger side of the Hummer. “Look Amigo, I normally go solo.” Serpenti says looking at Talon. Talon looks Serpenti. “Look, I am not trying to step on any toes. You hooked up with Braxton. We all know he is highly un-focused right now. I been needing something like this. Plus, what is any captain pirate with out his crew and first mate.” Talon says smiling big. “Si you are right Amigo. I am sorry. I do appreciate the help. Maybe this will be a better venture then I thought. ? Serpenti says tapping Talon on the shoulder. Talon turns the key powering up the Hummer and hits the lights and heads out of the car port Destination: Central Dallas Graveyard. To Be continued.
