A Casual Dress Day To Mexico

3 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/03/04 Sitting in silence on those very bored workdays, but this is what people do these days, office jobs, nine to five, everyday till they die. I don't have that luxury. Each day I awaken to what is eternal, myself. I pick out the best suit for the day, I have my personal chef make breakfast, my butlers iron and press my suit, I do my aerobics in my gym, come back in the house, eat my breakfast, and am in the limo drinking my favorite drink mixed with a little coffee. That is my morning. My late morning to noon, I am on the phone constantly buying up businesses like there is a clearance sell on the world market. The rest of the day I sit in my office in silence. But today, I feel different, I am not going to sit in all day, even if it is daylight out. I call up my secretary and tell her to call to get the limo ready i'm going out and lets take the usual procautions. She calls down as I put on my over coat, hat, and gloves, each specifically made for my 'condition'. I walk out of my office and tell my secretary i'm out for the day and that all calls should be transfered to my cell or email. She nods as I get on the elevator. On the way down there is an old man, i'm guessing an employeesince he is dressed in a suit, and holding a stack of files that say 'SOMA CORP' on them. I look at the man he looks at me, my presence makes him look down back at the stack of files. 'Hello.'I say to the man, he obviously does not think I am talking to him. 'You're an employee of mine right?' He looks at me in shock, eyes widened, his face losing all its color. I smile at him so he doesn't think i'm firing him. He gives a look of relief. 'Hello Mr. Soma, yes I am an employee of yours. I work in the mailroom. I am just bring the mail to people on the other floors.' 'Not to pry but why is a man of your age only doing this type of work? Why aren't you retired somewhere?' The man smiles at me and gives me a shrug. 'Well Mr. Soma I figure if keep working as long as I have the power to. I mean, what is a man if he doesn't make an honest living?' 'A monster.'I say to him calmly kinda looking away from his eyes. 'Exactly. Well Mr. Soma this is my stop, thank you for talking to an ordinary guy like me.' 'No, thank you for talking to a man like me, you aren't ordinary to me. Come talk to my secretary tomorrow about giving you a large raise.' 'Th....Thank you sir.' 'No problem.' I smile at my small act of kindness. But the man was right, a man who does not make an honest living is a monster. And thats what I am....a monster. I finally make it down to the first floor and out the main doors, the limo already waiting on me and attendants to hold umbrella's over me whenever i'm outside. I get into the limo, the attendants into a black Navigator behind the limo. My driver asks where we are headed and I tell him just to drive till I say stop. Since this was my private limo and nobody was allowed in it, I had it set up a certain way inside. Three small television screens cover the side closest to the driver, closest to me were a fridge, a computer keyboard, a remote control, a dvd player, my special stash of intoxicants, and xm radio installed throughout the car. On one of my television screens I have the movie Queen of the Damned playing, the second screen is on the New York stock exchange, and the third is connected to the internet. We drive for about an hour and a half I look out my window and I tell the driver to stop. We sit there, at the Mexican boarder, in all the years I've been alive I have never been to Mexico. I hear the vampires of Mexico are act a little differently, they follow some sort of different caste system than the other vampires in the world. Today I plan on seeing for myself. We get checked at the border and of course they find nothing and we head into the country. Most of the terrain like I expected it to be. Flat, or at least the part I was in . The land looked like it could probably be molded into a very industrious country if the right people financed it. We drive a little while longer before we get to some sort of club slash bar type of deals. The sun has finally went down and seems the night owls have come out to play. My attendants come out dressed in their armani suits, and my drivers stay by the vehicles just in case something were to go wrong. We walk into the place, and the place was larger than it looked on the outside, not shocking because this is how my kind works. People walk pass places like this unless they are brought, invited, or you have a special eye for these things. 'Boss this may not be a good idea. I know you already know this but I thought I would state the fact.' 'It is ok, I think we will be able to handle any situation that happens here.'I smile as I see some rather pretty females. We stand in the doorway and look around like this was some sort of old cowboy movie. We proceed to an empty table closest to the dance floor and watch some of these half drunken girls dance with these half drunken guys and I shake my head. I look around once again trying to see who looks like they literally own this place. I whisper to one of my attendants to go to the DJ booth and ask for an odd request. He walks over and whispers what I told him to tell, the DJ shrugs and after the current song was over, David Dramian singing 'Forsaken' comes on.. As the drunken people leave the dance floor, I close my eyes listening to the song as if I lived by it. I listen to each word, like it was my nurishment and as soon as I begin to relax my attendant to my left whispers into my ear. 'Boss, there is a lady here who wishes to speak to you. She says she is the owner of this place, says her name is Casandra Valesquez.' 'Does she seem genuine Ramone?' I say without looking back at the woman. 'I would say so boss.' 'Ok, I will meet her in her office, or at one of her tables in the back of this establishment.' 'The far left table in the back will be fine sir.' A voice like velvet speaks softly with a think spanish accent. The song ends and the drunken dancers get back on the floor while I stand and begin to walk over to the table in the far left of the establishment. Standing there is a woman, whose beautiful voice matched her body. She stands six feet tall, with long black hair, and hazel eyes. She has a petite nose and full red lips turned up in a half smile. Her skin is a dark tanned brown, and looks smooth to the touch. She wore a tight black halter top and skirt. The outfit just tight enough to show of her figure, which was not too slim or too large. I walk over to her as she extends her arm gracefully, hand facing down as waiting for me to kiss it. I take her hand and lightly kiss it and look up into her eyes. She offers me a seat, and I respectfully take it and she sits with me. 'You must be new to the parts sir.' 'A brief visit to somewhere I have never been before.' 'You must travel often then.' 'Yes....I do' 'Well I wanted to welcome you to Casa El Diablo, The house of the Devil.' 'I thank you for the welcome. Do you welcome all your new guest like this?' 'No, your the first' 'Well I should feel extra special then.' 'That you should.She says with her calm and sensual voice. 'Why did you choose to name this place 'The house of the devil?' She sits quiet for a second before opening her mouth to speak again. I look at her quietly awaiting her answer with my calm, somber expression and she looks at me the same way. 'For most of the people who come through here are marked by the devil. They are not human....like myself......and you...' I smile at her and I look down then back to her. Her comment 'marked by the devil' is this how my kind sees me, as a devil? 'So you think its a mark of satan? Do you hate being what you are?' 'I don't know what I think anymore, or what gave me this mark, but by no means do I hate it.' 'What let you know that you are like me?' 'Your presence, it would probably let anyone know....besides you're a talked about man, Mr. Soma.' If I could have blushed that would have been the time. I nod to her comment. She is very smart, cunning, she also seems to have a third sight. 'How old are you Cassandra?' 'I'm about three hundred, you?' I laugh at her question. I try and think, or at least I act like I am. 'I'm not sure...' 'That is rather interesting Mr. Soma.' 'Please, call me Leinad.' 'Ok, Leinad that is still rather interesting. How does one forget their age?' 'It's simple, being around way to long and stop counting.' 'I see. So what brings you to this place inparticular?' 'It is said the vampires in this area seem different than the ones in other parts of the world. Here, China, and Africa. Each place is a little different but all have a common ancestor, and he is unknown. I wanted to see what I could find out.' 'I wish I could help you Mr....Leinad.' 'You know nothing of this?' 'No information that I can give away for free...' 'Ah...I see.' 'I'm sorry Leinad but this country needs the money and anyway to get more income into it I will do.' 'Are you from here?' 'No I am originally from El Salvador.' 'I see...' I pull out my wallet and look at Cassandra. She looks a little bad about what she has asked but I am use to it. The world is ran by money and who better to know about this than I? 'How much is this place worth?' 'Excuse me?' 'I am asking how much this place is worth?' 'About ten grand. Why?' I call for one of my attendants as I put my wallet back into my pocket. When he gets over to me I tell him to go to the limo and get my checkbook. He nods and runs out. 'Well I'll buy it from you and make it larger and let you run it. The only things I ask of you is the following. The first is you keep me informed of the going on of the vampires in Mexico.' 'And the second thing?' 'The second thing is I want to take you out on a date.' My attendant comes back in and I write her a check for thiry thousand dollars and pass it to her. She looks at it quietly, and finally taking it. She looks up at me in shock, smiles, stands and kisses me on the cheek. Then she sits back down. 'I'll bring the Soma Corporation to Mexico and have a branch here bring jobs here and such, you will be my CEO here and the only person you will have to answer to is me.' 'I don't know what to say....' 'There is nothing to say but see you tomorrow night.' 'I'll see you tomorrow night.' 'Now how would be a good way to end this night?' 'Do you dance Leinad? 'A little.' She stands and she takes me by the hand and walks over to the DJ and ask him to play a salsa. As the music plays we do the salsa. She seems I know what i'm doing but then that expression fades away as she smiles at me and I smile back, as my hair still covering the very scarred face, almost animal like. Her never realizing the monster that is about to be unleashed upon the world. I smile on as she....this Cassandra is apart of my destiny. After the song I begin to leave, as I begin to get closer to the door, Cassandra walks over turns me around and hugs me and then kisses me lightly on the cheek. I smile as I walk out of the door my attendants following me. I get into the limo, heading back for my almost caslte-like house in Texas. I think of the night I just had and I compare it to the days that are going to follow after this day. A chain of events that will be remembered in history...my destiny, but there is something that makes me wonder do I have to do what I think needs to be done. Do I really NEED to do these things that I am going to do? Of course...its the only way. I been to recap the night and smile once again, I say a couple words before I sit in the silence of my limo. 'Happy birthday to me.'
