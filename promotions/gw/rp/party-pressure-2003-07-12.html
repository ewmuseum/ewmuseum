party pressure.

12 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: saturday, july 12th, 2003 So. I’ve been put into a pretty interesting predicament. On one hand, I am facing Jay Robson in round one of bracket two’s international title tournament. On the other hand, Jay Robson is teaming with me to face Tony Stecchino and Davis Fury to qualify for the Master of the Mat tournament. What we’re looking at here is on one hand ? Jay Robson is the guy I have to put an end to, lay him out. But then, on the other hand ? Jay Robson is the one person that I have to put my trust in and work with to qualify. On one hand Jay Robson and I have a storied past of being good friends from before Global Wrestling Federation even started. On the other hand, Jay Robson and myself have only spoken in increments over the past three years. “Come on, pick up.” Right now, I’m on my cell phone, like I’ve been all day long. At first, it was to find out what Jay Robson’s number was; now I’m on the phone trying to get a hold of him. I’ve gone through four cigarettes, two cups of coffee and at least six re-runs of the days of our lives. Jay Robson hasn’t been at home all day long, all afternoon long, all night long. As far as I can remember, Jay has always been a sort of party animal and skirt chaser. Jay and me have a lot in common, or so I suppose we used to. I was a womanizer for six years of my gimmick, which was enough to turn it into my real life. On camera, off, I loved the women and everything else that came with that. The alcohol flowed every weekend; the drugs were passed around daily. Kick flip the phone back into my pocket. So, my guess is that Jay Robson is at a party of some sort right now. He’s drunk as hell, or maybe high as heck. My guess is that he’s not being bothered with the situation that I am in right now. My imagination tells me that’s he’s not stuck in this dump in the middle of the states that they call Texas. Jay Robson is a rich man, he has money to go around, and he has a jet to fly him out to whichever five star hotel or mansion he feels like going to. I just need to get a hold of him, talk things out. I have to go over plans for Tuesday night, and then plans for the Tuesday after that. I have to work something out with Jay, figure out what we’re going to do about this dilemma. The cell starts to ring, half nelson pull into my ear. “Jay?” I ask, hoping. “No, it’s Ronny,” was the reply. “Hey, what’s going on?” “Are you a bit distracted today?” “Yeah, I’m unable to get a hold of someone. What are you doing?” “Well, it’s a Saturday night. I’m planning on heading out to a party, what say you join me?” I grinned at Ronny’s offer, after all this talk about the party animal TLM. “Okay, come on over. I’ll see you in half an hour or so.” Vertical suplex the phone onto my counter, and sit there waiting. Turn on the shower, let the water run at scorching hot. Wash up, get ready for the party, think about all the times in the past where you’ve been the life of it. Think everything is going to go perfect, think you’re going to have a great time. Brush the teeth, clean behind the ears, throw on the cologne. Dress up, not too casually or too dress, just the right side of things like usual. Look at self in the mirror, look at those eyes looking back at you. Smile, you’re ready. Door swings open, Ronny steps into the front room. “Ready to go?” “Yeah, let me just get one last thing.” I walk back to the sink and take a drink of the tap water just to build my character. “So where is this get-together taking place?” “This guy I met yesterday, he said he throws big bashes all the time.” This is the thing that’s been weird. So far in my stay in Texas, I’ve been unable to make any friends. Meanwhile, my good friend here happens to be communing with the best of them. It seems like the ice is too thick on the lake for me to be able to break it. Maybe I’m just avoiding those warning signs because I don’t want to have to worry about all of it. I’ve been isolated from the outside world for two months, the only conversations I’ve had have been hello’s, how may I take your order’s and watch it’s, aside from my occasional chat with Jeffery. We got to the festivities as the clock neared midnight. I feared the worse after about five minutes in Ronny’s 86 Festiva. He asked me “how possibly bad this party could be”. It is Texas, what if it was just a bunch of people at a barn swinging their pitchforks and doing some line dancing. It could possibly be the worst thing I’ve ever done in my life. But then again, I could have a fun time. Maybe meet some people. “You look stressed, Trey.” “Heh,” I am nodding my head to him. “Don’t worry, I have you covered,” he says as he reaches over and opens the dashboard. I look in and see a baggy. “Wow,” I say lifting it for examination. “Go ahead man, you need to loosen yourself up.” Four hundred and fifty two days. One year, two months, three weeks and four days. That’s how long it’s been since the last time I rolled a piece of paper up like I’m doing right now. However, that wasn’t the same type of situation that I’m in now. Before, it was easy, because it was the norm. What I had been used to day in and out. Then, the fans wrestling federation threw me out, and I had to quit so I could get into three sixty. Now, I’m in the middle of Texas, worried about parties going just right. I don’t have all the fame, all the money, and no paparazzi. So I start to tighten the edge of it. Do I have to worry about being thrown out of Global Wrestling, though? I haven’t seen any drug tests in the federation so far, but that doesn’t mean anything considering how little time it has been. Think about it though, all the corruption in GW right now, what do I really have to worry about? Tony Stecchino is an infamous mobster, who probably cartels drugs, women and weapons. Then there’s Deacon Holmes, who I’ve seen carry a gun on him everywhere he goes. I’ve heard stories about Silent Wind and Mani Shigeru, two who have murdered for a living. So what is a little bit of indulgence in drugs going to hurt? Ronny holds the Zippo in front of me as he hits a right turn, “Almost there.” He smiles as I let out a bellowing cough. I offer it to him, “No, this one’s yours.” Another hit. Muscles relaxing. Another, and another. Coughing, I pound my chest and tell my lungs to stop bleeding. My eyes start watering as I continue to choke on the smoke. “Just what is this stuff?”
