Talk is Cheap

27 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-27-05 Weeks turn into months and months turn into years. That’s what people say, at least. In reality, it’s all just one big clump of dead matter which every individual must trudge through. Well, that’s what she says. Perhaps her philosophy is a dead one, but in light of recent events, she had no other philosophy to adopt. For two weeks, she had mindlessly dawdled her time and now the time had come for her to take some initiative. In her mind, two weeks would give her father a sufficient amount of time to forget that his daughter was too stubborn to heed his advice. Or, perhaps she should have waited longer. That way, she wouldn’t have to listen to the dreadful pop music pouring from the headphones of the man standing next to her. What is that shit? she thinks as she brings her cigarette to her mouth. She stands with her weight equally distributed on both feet. One arm is hugging her torso while the other is left hanging, her cigarette pouring flame and ash down onto the asphalt. Taking another drag, she amuses herself by blowing the smoke in the direction of the cheerful man beside her. His spastic head bopping pauses to allow time for his gagging fit. Pleased with herself, Aradia turns back towards the road and impatiently waits for the bus to arrive. The sun beats down from above, bathing the area in blinding golden light. The Las Vegas sun is brutal in comparison to the Pittsburgh rays she is accustomed to. She pushes her sunglasses further up onto her face in order to see the road better. The shades offer minimal aid in allowing her to see more than several feet in front of her. Standing at what may be the most peculiar place for a bus stop, Aradia releases one last puff of her cigarette and then tosses it to the side of the road, not bothering to put it out. “Hey, that hurts the environment, you know?” She turns to look at the man beside her, who has stopped his music to scold her. Staring at him, she wears no emotion, even as she blows the smoke of her cigarette into his face. He gags again, glares, and then lets his music start once more. Once again pleased with herself, she pushes a few strands of her raven locks from her face. Her black duffel bag rests neatly on the ground, warming in the Nevada sun. Aradia glances down at it as she shifts her weight to one foot, listening as the leather of her boots squeak in the process. Inside her Cruiserweight belt sits comfortably, shielded from the sun. As is her skirt and baby-tee, which would have been more comfortable in this warm weather than the pair of black cargos and long sleeved black shirt she currently wears. She shrugs at the thought and then cracks her neck. Glancing down at her watch, she scrunches her face. The bus is more than twenty minutes late. As if her blood was not boiling enough from the heat, now she had to deal with an inconsiderate driver. Leave it to Greyhound. She brings her hands to her hips and looks around in disgust. For two weeks she waited for the right time to leave Las Vegas in order to start an investigation of her own and now that the time had arrived, she had to be set back by a late bus and the male version of Britney Spears dancing beside her. Quickly glancing over at the man, her stomach turns as she watches him bob his head and do a few freak show sidesteps. She holds back laughter, and turns back to the road, amusing herself with the million ways she could further humiliate the imbecile. Somewhere in the distance, she hears the familiar sound of a bus and a minor smile bestows her blackened lips. Quickly, she reaches down to grab the strap of her duffel bag and grumbles to herself as the new contestant for Miss Teen USA does the same. She looks him over again and judgments flow from her brain in waves of wit. Another coy smile places itself on her face as she steps forward, begrudgingly watching as the bus comes to a halt. She flashes a pass at the driver as she hoists her bag further onto her shoulder. Turning, she starts down the aisle and winces at the sight of the other passengers. Where’s America’s Most Wanted when you really need them? She notices a multitude of empty seats in the back of the bus and heads in that direction, disregarding the stares from the horny men. With a sigh, she tosses her bag onto one of the seats and then sits down beside it. She tries to make herself comfortable, but finds it difficult. These seats aren’t very adequate? She looks up and notices her friend from the bus stop setting his belongings into the seat in front of her. I can’t fucking escape, can I? Looking around again, she bites her lower lip, realizing just how disgusting many of the passengers are. A few of them are still staring at her and so she angrily bares her teeth in response. They all turn around, except for one small boy, who seems intrigued by her presence. Content with the boy’s indifferent stare, she leans further into the seat and thinks silently to herself. How did I get myself into this situation? She pushes her sunglasses up onto her forehead and rolls her golden brown eyes. The same way I always do? the thirst for revenge. Dad will surely find Johnson before I do? but if I can get my hands on him there’ll be hell to pay. That’s all I want? hell. She smiles and looks out the window, whispering silent goodbyes to Las Vegas. That bastard will pay for what he’s done. Dad and I will make sure of that. A look of puzzlement comes to her as she looks away from the window, the same question which has haunted her for weeks coming back to her like a recurring nightmare. But why does dad care so much? They must have threatened him with something? but what could they have possibly threatened him with? That’s the only reason he’d be involved. Another thought occurs to her. Or maybe he really does care for my welfare more than I’ll admit. If he loses me, he’ll lose the only person that can carry on our history. He knows his time is coming with his age, so it’s better him than me. She takes a break from her thought to glance out the window again, admiring the beauty of her new home. There is still some kind of ulterior motive... If raping me wasn’t incentive for dad to take the bastard out, then there’s something bigger involved. Thoughts of the usual incentives pass through her mind and she dodges them quickly. It takes more than a few idle threats for her father to risk his life and career on such an insidious task. Maybe they threatened mom? This was a new thoery which she had yet to consider. She considered it for a moment, unsure of how to digest it. It was an awkward thought? her parents had divorced shortly after her disappearance. Her mother could not handle her father’s life style and the disappointment of losing their only living child was devastating for her mother. Aradia knew this when she left. The thought had almost kept her from leaving, yet the resentment for her father’s cold indifference to her struggle and tragedy was too powerful a force to keep her in Pittsburgh. Her mother felt the same resentment, which was the driving factor in her parent’s separation. The news had traveled to Aradia some years later, and it floored her. She knew her parents as inseparable lovebirds? therefore her mother’s disappointment must have been too grand for even love to conquer. As though love could conquer anything at all. Aradia’s vision of love was a bit askew, but after the life she endured, so would anyone’s. The only person she had ever truly loved could be dead? another factor that contributed to her thirst for revenge. It must be mom Aradia thought, knowing this to be the truth. There is only one person on this earth that her father cared for more than himself, and that is her mother. He’s doing this to prove to her that he’s good at heart. She’ll undoubtedly fall for it. Not only is he taking back everything he’s ever said to me about my situation, but he’s saving her life as well. She chuckles in antipathy. How typical of him. It’s always about him and making himself look good. That’s it? I has to be mom. Her thoughts are disturbed by the princess in front of her, who is currently jumping and writhing in his seat, belting the lyrics to “Toxic.” Aradia’s eyes widen and she feels her lunch rising in her throat. Her eyes dart back and forth, scanning the bus and the other passenger’s contempt expressions. Frightened by the man’s antics, Aradia reaches forward and smacks the man on the back of his head. He spins quickly, just as she resumes her idle position, looking as if nothing had happened. He looks at her in shock and anger, while several passengers giggle. Wearing a boastfully innocent smile, she bats her darkened eyes several times before responding with a timid: “There was a fly.” The man nods his head and then turns around slowly. A few passengers who overheard the exchange chuckle to themselves and then turn as well. Rolling her eyes, Aradia slouches in her seat and then looks over to her duffel bag. She opens it and is blinded as the sun reflects from the gold belt which is nestled neatly on the top of her belongings. Gently pushing it out of the way, she retrieves her own set of headphones and then seals the bag once more. Placing the earphones on her head, she pushes play and smiles as The Dresden Dolls begin to serenade her with their ironic beauty. The ensuing trip would be a long one, but well worth the time. If her lead was correct, than she’d be one step closer to finding Johnson, without distraction this time. She had received word that his head lackeys were planning an intervention before an interview with Alexis Steel, and so promptly cancelled the interview, but not without requesting that Steel keep the cancellation a secret until their scheduled day. Somewhat baffled by Xiomara’s unusual request, she obliged and they promptly rescheduled for another day. This would set Johnson’s team back a few days and would grant her time to search for them. As far as they were concerned, she is sitting in her apartment and not on a bus headed for Virginia. Aradia had received word a few days before that Johnson was spotted right back in Virginia, not far from the college town where he had taken his aggressive flirtations one step too far. At the thought, Aradia cracked her knuckles, knowing retribution would be much too sweet. As if violating her wasn’t enough measure for her to seek vengeance, six years of trying to humiliate, attack, and even attempt murder was enough reason for Xiomara’s intentions. Many people would say that vengeance does not solve the problem and would only bring her down to his level, but those people have not endured almost ten years of pain and self-destruction at the hands of this monster. After nearly a decade of failed attempts, the odds are suddenly on her side and she relishes the thought. As she slowly drifts to sleep, a smug smile flashes across her black lips. For the next fourteen hours, she would dream of his pain and torment and a beautiful reunion with her only love. No dream could be more beautiful. Dream? dream while you can, because life is the real nightmare.
