The Long Dark Road

29 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-29-2006 The night was stormy. Little bits of rain coming down on the window shield of the truck. The car headlight, shining through the front window shield right into the eyes of David Crisp. It was dark, the time of the CD player was showing a little passed midnight. Driving down the road at about fifty miles an hour, David presses on the radio, turning it to the crisp sounds of Dallas's own, 106.1 KISS FM. '...And we are down off of Goodladamer and Oak at the Lizard Lounge and it's hot. We got two dollars well shots and one dollar beers tonight. Come see us...' The guy said on the radio. His voice, familiar as the man you would call, Billy The Kidd. David gave a smile as his cell phone went off. Reaching down into the coin tray to grab his brand new Nokia cell phone. 'Hello...?' David said. Turning to see Jamie laying there in the other seat. 'Yeah mother. We're about twenty or so minutes away from your house.' David said with a little grin. The rain began to come down even harder as the voice on the radio went off and another familiar beat, the soft riffs of a guitar came on. It was the very soft and almost worshiped band of Staind with their new song called Right Here. 'Yes mom.' David said. 'I'll make sure to have it all for you. Just get some rest till we get there.' David gave a soft sniff. 'Goodbye.' Responding. As he hung up the cell phone, looking up at what was in behind him into the rearview mirror, a set of flashing lights came on. Red and blue, the colors of what David liked to call them, 'Pigs.'. David gave an angry sigh before pressing up on his turn signal and slowly pulling over to the right. Turning the key off, Jamie slowly opened her soft brown eyes. Her hair was wild tonight. Her black straight hair, everywhere. As if a tornado of little proportions came through it. 'What's wrong?' Saying in a low voice. 'Pigs.' Giving her an angry look. 'What were you doing?' Sitting up straight in her seat. 'For once I wasn't doing anything.' He said responding with his hand on the window button. *TAP TAP* David began to roll down the window. Looking up at the cop, water dripping from his hat and coat that he had on. David gave a grin, a sh*t faced grin at that before questioning him. 'What did I do officer?' 'Well we saw you pass through that stop sign back there. Can you please give us your license and registration?' The officer asked. David reaches into his back pocket, pulling out a black Armani looking wallet. Opening it up, David pulled out his license from it and handed him them before Jamie handed the registration to the 1996 Dodge Pickup that they were riding in. 'Thank you sir. Well be right back.' He said as he walled away. Jamie, with her lusty brown eyes reaches behind the seat for her purse and pulls out her make up mirror. 'What are you doing?' David says. 'Trying to get us out of this.' Patting her nose with the blush. 'And how in the hell do you think your going to do that?' Confused with his words David asks. Jamie tosses her hair up a little bit, flipping her red lipstick up and across her lustful lips. Puckering them up and out, covering the red sex filled lips together with the lipstick. 'Just watch.' She said with a smile. As they waited, the cop returned, bending down to take a look inside the truck. As he did, he caught a eye full of Jamie, reveling a little bit of her skin, mainly the cleavage of her size C breast. Her breath got a little faster as he kept looking right at them. David, his once anger filled eyes were now even more confused at how she was doing them. 'So how much is the ticket going to be sir?' David said. 'Ticket?' The cop said. 'Yes...' 'OHHH...', he said as he looked over at David. 'Only a warning si..sir. Sorry, I'm out of breast...I mean breath.' David for once gave a laugh before responding. 'Ok, so are we free to go officer?' Raising up off the door panel, still looking at Jamie. 'Yes sir you are free to low...I mean go.' David then rolled up the window as quick as he did going down. Turning on the key, Jamie gave the officer a wave before David peeled off. Steering on to the road, David took a soft sigh, looking back into the mirror at the officer. 'That's right PIG. You can't touch! Who's the man now.' David said, slapping his chest. 'You mean who's the woman right?' Jamie said. 'Hey I could of done that.' David responded. 'Sure...', Jamie grin's. 'So who called?' 'My mother. She wants us to stop by. Only she would even though she knows I got a HUGE match against the legend Rich Sherwin.' David laughed out loud at his funny legend comment. The cars kept passing by. A few with their bright lights on which gave David a slight problem in seeing. 'Dammit. I swear if they keep on giving me the brights I'm going to turn around an run their ass off the road.' David said as he looked back into the rearview mirror. 'Well baby, wake me up when we get there ok. I'm going to go back to sleep.' Jamie said, letting the seat go back all the way. David looked over at her before turning the radio up just a little more. Coming out of the speakers came the soft sounds of James Blunt with his love song You're Beautiful. He gave her a wink before taking his hand threw her hair and continuing through the night.
