The Phone call(mini rp)

13 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/13/04 Silence. I sit in my office in silence as I dial a number on my phone. As the phone begins to ring I shake a little. This is the first time I will have talked to my son in two hundred years. Over the years his name changed to go along with mine if I ever need to contact him but I didn’t want him to depend on me so I never contacted him right out…I’d send him things every now and then but only enough that he could make an honorable living from…But this time was different…This time I shall give him the world if he wants it. The phone continues to ring and finally he answers. “Hello.” “Is this Maximilian Soma?” “Yeah. Who’s this?” “Max….its your dad.” “Dad?…wh…hi. Long time no talk.” “I know. How have you been?” “Ok I guess. What about you? The business going well I’m assuming?” “Yeah its good…listen son I need a big favor to ask you. Actually two favors.” “What is it dad?” “How would you like to become senior VP of Soma Corp?” “Um…that’s not where I saw my life headed dad. I kinda wanted to continue my work as an enforcer.” “You could still do that but you’d also be able to provide for yourself and start your own business under the Soma Corp name. But I also need someone to be my new bodyguard. And I figured whom better than my oldest son. It would be a way for us to bond and actually get to know each other in person.” “I’m flattered dad….and I do think it would be a good idea for us to get to know each other. And besides….” “What?” “I’m a big GW fan and I’d get to be on tv….so sure dad I’ll take the next plane out to your corporate building in Texas. Should I get a hotel room?” “Nah. Stay at the house. I’ll pick you up from the airport. In shall we say…seven hours?” “Dad, I’m not packed at all.” “That’s ok son. We can buy ya everything ya need here….Just pack things ya want to have with ya and let me take care of the rest.” “Your stubborn sometimes dad.” “And that’s where you get it from.” “Ha ha…very funny…I’ll see ya in a couple hours.” “Ok son. Goodbye.” “Bye dad.” I hang up the phone with a smile on my face. This new journey has brought forth things I never thought would happen. Beware to all those who have sinned greatly for their time is at hand. I sit in the office in silence.
