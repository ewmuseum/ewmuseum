No more lonely nights

21 March 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
No more lonely nights Jenny Callihan Global Wrestling Promo #3046 Date: 03/21/07 Location: Dallas: A restruant and her condo “Darling?” At a cozy family restaurant, Jake and Jenny are sitting at a table with the blonde being lost in thought. She had been this absent minded ever since Fusion, and she basically got her ass handed to her. It was one of those “Go Straight to Jail, No Past Go” ordeals where she provided no defense and no offense. Basically, she got manhandled like Kaye Heart did when Jenny entered BACW in June of 2003. However, Kaye was a little different from herself; Jenny tried to remind herself quietly. Kaye was the type of wrestler who wanted people to adore her, but didn’t want to put the effort into it. Kaye wanted to be the champion, but didn’t put the bite in her bark. Jenny swears if she hears “Fear my beautiful ways” one more time, she will take a sledgehammer and unbeautify Kaye in a mere second. Jenny was different from Kaye. Jenny always fought hard in every match she ever been into. She had the spotlight on her because of her astounding work ethics in the ring, and when she met Kaye for the first time in her first match, Kaye had “life threatening” injuries supposedly. It was Jenny’s belief that the only thing that was life threateningly injured was Kaye’s ego. When one looks at the match with Amanda, Jenny was a rag doll. Amanda owned her ass, and not in a way that Jenny prefers in regards to women. It makes her think about where she got off the exit to Suckitude. “Jenny?” Jake had mention once more with her looking up at him. This time, his wife finally looks up at him with her beautiful blue eyes. “You ok?” “Yea...sorry, just…got lost in thought.” She blushes upon admission. “Still thinking about last week?” “It’s hard not to. I mean, I know I should take one match at a time, but it’s just…” she tries to express herself but finding herself extremely losing the words to do so. “When did I become the world most pathetic wrestler?” “Jenny, stop it.” “What?” she replies back. Thankfully, she wasn’t speaking utterly loud, but the tension in her voice was still there. “You saw it.” “You had a bad night…” he begins to say until she cuts him off. “It was a horrid night. I just lost my game all over the place.” “Jenny…” “I let myself get powerbomb…I mean, Jesus Christ…” “Jennifer!” he speaks with a low stern voice. She immediately stops as she is rarely ever called Jennifer. It is by preference people refers to her as Jenny since she got very tired of Jennifer when he parents would scold her with that name daily. “Sometimes, things happen. You just lost focus in the ring for a brief moment, that’s all. Don’t go hating yourself and dwelling on it, because it will not help you at all. Ace pitchers even have a night where they get nothing but home run hit off them. Just relax you’re still my favorite wrestler in the world.” “That’s because you are married to me and know better then to say otherwise.” She teases him. “That may be partially true but in any case.” He nudges her with a little causing her to giggle. “Be a man, rub some dirt on it.” Jenny starts laughing at his comment. “Oh, so you want me to be a man now, do you? You aren’t trying to hint something?” she lashes back at him with a mischievous grin. “Hmm…no, surgery would make you no fun to Tango…or Salsa.” “I thought so.” A waiter comes up to their table. “So how is everything?” he asks with a smile. “Absolutely perfect.” Jenny says with complete sincerity. “Let me know if you need anything.” He said before making his way off to his next table. “So want to go to the movies after this?” Jake asks. “I’m buying.” “Jacob, I don’t see you too often. What I really like to do is go to Blockbuster, or whatever, go home, cuddle on the couch, and you know…” “Are you sure you don’t want to go to the bedroom for ‘you know’? You do have a roommate.” Jenny raises her eyebrow but maintains her grin. “Naughty naughty.” She says as she rubs her hands across his thigh. “But I like the way you think.” “Shall I include popcorn?” The look of shock flashes across her facial features. “Dare I ask what you are going to subject popcorn to.” “Oh I will think of something. Maybe a little buttery…” Jake bats his eyes causing his dear wife to nearly choke on the morsel of food she manages to get into her mouth. She slaps him lightly in retaliation. “You know better then to make me laugh while I am eating.” “I beg for your forgiveness, and bask in your glory!” “Oh you will be doing a whole lot of basking tonight.” She grins as she takes a few more bites. “And maybe some groveling too.” It was Jake’s turn to raise an eyebrow with a mischievous grin. “Dare I ask what will make a proud Irish man grovel?” “I visited Victoria Secrets.” She said with a straight face. “Draw your own conclusions.” She begins to eat her food and Jake seems quite motivated to eat his. Jenny only visits Victoria Secrets when she wants something sleepwear. Of course, they may not remain on throughout the night, but still it is a worthwhile investment to her. As they enter her condo in Dallas with a Blockbuster movie, Jake heads towards the kitchen to make the popcorn as Jenny ventures to her room and closes the door. They may be considered old fashion, but they don’t microwave their popcorn. They have a nice popper where they put the seeds in, and let it start stirring. As he waits for the kernels to pop, he removes two glasses from the cabinet, and checks to see what sort of wine she has place in earlier that evening. He chuckles when he pulls out one he could barely understand, but he had a very good clue why. Even though Jenny left the BACW, she still had some connections there. One of those connections was Adriana MacDonagh. However, don’t let the Irish surname fool you as she is also half French. Well, Canadian-French to be precise as she was born in Montérégie, Quebec, Canada, but French none the less. Adriana tends to import fine wine from France that normally doesn’t get exported as she has no qualms of flying over there, and doing aggressive negotiation in her native language. However, she was quite generous to her friends and family, and would occasionally ask Jenny if she would like some wine. Jenny spoke a little French, but nowhere near as conversational as Adriana. He pops the cork, and pours the wine into the respective glasses as the popper starts roaring with puffs. Soon, the bowl was filled and he collects the glasses to head to the bedroom. Luckily, the door was ajar a bit as he would have to struggle to get that door open with his hands full. He taps the door with his foot, and become exposed to the luscious red glow illuminating the room from several candles in red holders. Scented too, hmmm. The movie was already on their DVD player, and all other distracting lights were either out or dim. However, what might of cause a man to drop his jaw and items in hands in sheer awe was the lovely Jenny sitting at the head of the bed in nothing more then silk panties and lacy babydoll. Thankfully since wine does stain carpets, he keeps his composure while approaching her and hands her a glass of wine. He places his glass down and bowl down before he closes the door. It’s been awhile, so it’s time to make up for lost time. No one will disturb this time, no one.
