Reflections

25 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 25 As he lay there he looked peaceful. Almost as if a scene from a movie, or a great statue masterpiece. But, it all soon changed, his face first. His facial complexion went from a soft and gentle smile of calmness and tranquility, to a face that had seen the struggle and pain of many battles. His body was next, he went from his comfortable, relaxed self and his body tensed up severely. His body began to drip with sweat, a cold sweat. His shirt became very damp. No longer did he lie still and peacefully, instead he was now tossing and turning. A terrible dream must have disrupted his sleep, and brought him into a different state of mind. There was nothing he could do to stop it, short of waking up. But, there was no way to force that upon oneself. For now, he would have to watch helplessly. Ichiro stood there on the end of the road, his visions had told him something terrible would happen today. This scene was not making it any better. He and Jushin had not heard from Rokku in a couple of weeks, it had them more than a little worried. And this site, well, it sure as hell did not make anything better. Lone and behold across the street they saw a vision they never thought they would see. It was of course, The Lion’s Den. Across the street, the large architectural masterpiece of steel, cement, plaster, and brick. However, there was something wrong with it, it was eerily silent. Gone was the normal murmur and clatter of people moving around inside, gone was the hum of the electricity buzzing, gone was everything that made it alive. The place was dead. The lights were off. There was no one inside. There was no one around. What the hell had happened? It was like a ghost town, not a soul in sight, not a person to care. However, traumatizing as this vision was, it was about to take a turn for the worse. A final blow in the Legion, and the strong alliance formed within the walls that stood in front of them. Even if they would only stand for a few more seconds? Ichiro rolled over, the sweat making a large mark on the back of his grey sleep T-shirt. The blankets began to fall off of him, he laid still however. Not making any more movements, except for small shakes and the occasional roll over. He was silent, except for the slight bit of heavy breathing, and a low murmur as if he was talking to the dream. As the two Nakazura brothers stood there, they could not hold grip of what was happening. How in the hell did this happen? How could something so strong, so alive, just all of a sudden appear so, so dead?! Ichiro merely shook his head and began to turn away. It left a sour taste in his mouth to see this, it was indeed a sad day. Jushin stood watching still, apparently not letting the thought process. Not letting himself believe it. “I-I can see it, but I don’t believe it. I won’t believe it!” he said. “I do not want to believe it, but it appears as if fate has dealt us another cruel hand. Another turn for the worst. The Lion’s Den is abandoned, the Den is dead. The Legion, has failed,” came the reply. “But, it can’t be!” exclaimed Jushin. “Look for yourself Jushin, once again the team we have fought for has fallen. The Legion is no more.” “This place was a fortress. An impenetrable wall that stood for everything good, for everything we believed in, for everything that was right in GW. It was the fortress of the good, to combat the evils of The Upper Echelon,” said Jushin. “And look at it now, it is closed. Done. Jushin, we are on our own once again. The building is dead, it can merely stand as a memento, a memory of what once was,” As Ichiro finished speaking, there is a slight rumble in the streets. There is a large cloud of smoke coming out from the bottom of The Den. The Souls of the Black Lotus, look at each other and take a quick run from the building, as the earth vibrates. A large explosion goes off at the base of the building, sending a shockwave throughout the entire street. The brothers cover their eyes and faces as they watch on in horror, the large cloud of smoke, grows even larger. The bottom of the building begins to give out, as fire begins to burst through several of the windows. The sounds of the building collapsing are horrific. The glass shattering reminds one of a hail storm. The crumbling brick sounds like a stampede headed straight for you. The sound of the steel supports bending and breaking sends out an ear shattering screech and squeal. The concrete dust falling to the ground hisses like a thirty five meter long anaconda that is sneaking up on you. The only thing worse than the sounds of the Lion’s Den falling, is the vision of it. The building that once stood tall and proud and secure. The one place of refuge from the torture of the world’s corrupt. The home of The Legion, now laid in a heap of dust, broken brick, shattered glass, bent steel and an overall large mess. The two brothers turned to each other. The anger on their faces could not be hidden, they had a good idea who could do such a thing, but they had no way to prove it. And there was nothing they did to stop it. What the hell had they let happen?”” Ichiro’s eyes shot open and his body instantly shot up into a seated position. His breathing was extremely heavy and his body and face covered with the cold sweat from his nightmare. Slowly, he gained his thoughts, he looked around slowly and realized what had happened. “Just a dream, it was just a dream. But it was so real?” he thought to himself. He wiped the sweat from his forehead. He looked around the room, there was nothing here to comfort him. The past couple of weeks had been rough for the two Nakazura brothers. But, what the hell could they do. It was not the first time they had been dealt a swift blow, but no matter what they had been met with, they had always pulled through. And they had always done it together. Ichiro, stood up, he couldn’t sleep anymore. He looked at the small clock radio on his night stand. The red crystal lettering of the clock read 5:37 AM. Ichiro shook his head, he didn’t need any more sleep, he would survive. He quickly threw his clothes off and hopped into the shower. He emerged from the shower, no clean and dressed about fifteen minutes later. He wore his usual workout gear. A pair of white workout pants and a white muscle shirt, with a white baseball cap on. He smiled and sat down at his kitchen table, grabbing an apple out of the small fridge in his apartment. He sat down and bit into the apple. As he sat there absent mindedly eating the apple, the events of the past few weeks began to take over in his head. “What the hell are we supposed to do now” I wish there was a simple answer. There have been so many things that have gone wrong in the past few weeks, I just don’t know where I am supposed to go now. The world obviously has some sort of purpose for all of this, somewhere mixed in these curses is a blessing in disguise. The first part of the fallout was the disappearance of Yoshiko and Rokku. No one really knew what happened to the two of them, they seemed fine whenever they were around Ichiro. There had been no sign of them wanting to leave. The next downfall was the setup of Ichiro and Yoshiko. It happened slightly before the leader of The Legion and his girlfriend took their leave of absence. Now that he looked back, Ichiro wondered, could this have been what drove them away? We never really found out who set us up, although I have a damned good feeling that is was those TUE bastards. The TUE has been growing in strength. Both participants in the International Title finals are from TUE. Not to mention all of the out of ring momentum they have been gaining with their sneak attacks and dirty tactics.” As Ichiro reflected, something suddenly came to him. There was no blessing in disguise, for throughout this entire string of terrible things, there have also been many great things to happen for he and his brother. “There are some good things that have come about as of late however. Most importantly is the shot at the World Title this week. Ichiro did not know what he did to deserve such an opportunity, nor how it happened to be Jushin and himself in the same match, almost as if it was a handicapped match. But, he did not care. For it was not important, the World Title did not matter to him, and he was sure it did not matter to Jushin. But, the message that the Souls of the Black Lotus would send to both TUE and The Elite would be a strong one. Even though the Legion has died, we will fight on for the things we believe in. Also, one of the biggest opportunities for the brothers since their arrival in America, a chance at the PTC Tag Team Titles. The brothers would now have a chance not only to represent themselves, but to represent all of GW at the same time. They had a chance to carry two tag team titles at the same time, a feat not held by many.” Ichiro finished eating the apple, he had been thinking for quite a while. He threw the core into the small waste basket located under one of his shelves. He then stood and began to walk towards his door. It was still much too early to train. Jushin would not be up for at least another three hours and Ichiro had no desire to work out on his own. Instead, he planned on doing some meditation before his brother was awake and ready to train. He grabbed the small keychain off the hook as he walked out the door. He locked the deadbolt behind him, and made his way towards the elevators. He pocketed the keys and then as the elevator door opened with a ping he got inside and took his usual path to the roof. The doors opened once again on the top floor. He made his way off and began to walk towards the far edge of the rooftop area. He climbed on top of the ledge and assumed his normal position. He sat cross legged on the small ledge, the sun was just beginning to rise into the sky. A beautiful sight as its orange rays began to illuminate even the darkest depths of the large city. The sun gleamed off of the glass windows of the tall office buildings. It gleamed off the tops of antennas and lightning rods. It glared over the tops of the tallest skyscrapers. Ichiro closed his eyes, and his body began to relax. His muscles relaxed and he began to feel his mind slipping. He began to relax, he felt his soul beginning to leave his body. It continued to feel this way, until he seemingly was looking down at his body while floating above. It was an awkward felling to look down at oneself. To see himself sitting there cross legged, eyes closed, it was strange. To be able to see with his eyes closed. Ichiro, needed to relax, he needed to calm down and he needed to put things into perspective. And this was surely the best way to accomplish that. Memories of his singles career past through his head, and needless to say it was slightly less than impressive. As far as he could remember his only good showing in a singles match had come against his own brother. He remembered the brutal Sibling Rivalry match, but the match had done good. It brought the two brothers together, and now they were at the top of their games. Even though this was a singles match, it would be easy for the two of them to work together. They would need each other, now more then ever they needed to be able to depend on one another. They were brothers, they were friends, they were team mates. When one needed help, the other had to be there to give it. Would they fail?” No, they couldn’t. He wouldn’t allow it to happen. No matter what the cost, he was willing to pay it. No matter how painful, he was willing to endure it. He would not let his brother down, they would not fail.
