New Friends...

9 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/9/05 Things you don't always see are the little things that go on behind the curtains or behind closed doors. Except in the life of a celebrity, in this case, wrestlers, there are no closed doors there is no “personal” time. Your life is a book people say or see things they think is gospel and print it as such. At least not all believe in what they read. Otherwise the latest Gossip would have Danielle, recently moving into a huge house in Las Vegas, living with two boyfriends who are brothers, and looking to add a third. Yeah like she needed someone like him... Her hands were full with the latest addition to her life, the Hyacinth Macaw named Angel. Then you toss in her Grandfather showing up out of the blue, with the information that her fifth great grandfather, the Angel Kokabel, who the Sioux call Morning Star, could very well still be not only alive, but doing things behind the scenes. What a mind blower that would be for you to find out, now get into Dani's shoes. We won't even add into the fact that she's got 5 people in a match this week, all 5 want what will end up being... HER BELT. Her title, she's the platinum champion. The TV title is being merged into it, and the Cruiserweight title also. So what do you get now? A clusterfuck waiting to happen as 6 people vie for something that Dani fought so hard to get in the first place. Dawn McFarlane will want to claim it, because she's the cruiserweight champion who failed in her first bid for the Global World Title. Elia Smith will want it, so that she can first off be considered to finally get one up on Dani, and because she just finally crawled out from her own shadow and got the Television Title. Emoticon will want it, because he seems to be one of the most materialist guys in the federation. It's all about what he has. It's all about him. Especially if it's something he could use to try to convince some girl that she should be with him. Ken Patridge Jr wants it for the sole reason of making himself a grand slam champion. It's not about him being a champion and representing the federation. It's about him, him, him. His bid for the hall of fame, his bid for adding one more feather to his hat here. And then you have Mike Biddle, who is considered one of the greats, that never really happened. It would be a bid for him to once again go off and mouth off to everyone about how great he is. Dani? Dani wants it because she just won it. After coming to Global and fighting, scratching and crawling to this spot... she finally got something of an accolade. Loosing it now is not an option on her menu. But it appears that most think that this new and improved Platinum title will go onto the waist of one of the others. But if they think that Dani's going down without a fight, they've got another thing coming to them. Dani's riding high on a few things, and that's not something to look beyond. She won the Platinum Title a few weeks ago She moved into the new house that the Order supplied for them. She managed to actually pull her ass out of the fire in last week's match, with the help of a newcomer to her fold of friends, Blaise McClancy. As for the dealings with her grandfather when he showed up, things appeared to go well, or at least better then expected. At the dinner they all went out to, to spend some social time with him, her Cousin kept shooting daggers at Josh. The xenophobic man just couldn't get out of his head that Josh wasn't full blooded Native. Danielle could tell that the same sigma that cause the death of her mother and father, was being drawn towards the man she loved. Josh to his credit kept his mouth shut for once. His hot head was actually kept under check. Josh and her Grandfather wandered off right before dinner and Josh came back with a smile on his face and her grandfather the same. So as far as she was concerned that is all that mattered. Although Dani almost made one mistake that was quickly fixed up. When she had heard something about her partner in the ring last week needing a place to stay, she volunteered a few of the rooms in their house for him and his woman, Ukyo. Blaise turned her down, citing that Ms. Leanan was taking care of things and that with his two little girls, he didn't want to get in their way. Dani had offered based on how he had come in and stuck his neck out for her, and also upon realizing who it was... honor. Blaise McClancy was the cousin of the man who had run the wrestling school that Gray had taught at. The same one that she and Josh had taken lesson at. He was the head master of that academy. On top of that, he was related to the person who was their boss when it all came down to it. But Blaise had turned them down, and it wasn't but another day or so when they were informed that Lady Leanan had secured a home just down the street from them, not too far when you considered the size of the houses and their lots in this neighborhood area. Tonight was the house warming for them, as the movers had spent the last few days filling it with furniture and such. When the movers weren't doing it, Gray had gone over to help move things out of Blaise's Chevy Avalanche that needed some more muscle to do so. A raport was already forming with the man who was, according to Gray, taking over as the head person on her case. Blaise was a the ranking Order field officer, now, giving Lady Leanan a bit of a break to take care of her actual job. Dani stood looking at herself in the full length mirror in the large room she shared with Josh. She saw the Athletic but still shapely body that everyone else did, but didn't quite get it still. She leaned forward and looked at the mark on her forehead that remained from the wound that Virgil Ambrosi had done to her when she won the Platinum Title off of him. It looked like a blemish against her darker skin tone, but it was a small scar none the less. She stuck her tongue out at herself, showing displeasure and then turned to walk towards the sliding glass door to the balcony. As she walked by the cage that housed her newest friend, she mindlessly reached her hand up and placed it on the neck of the large blue feathered creature that lived in it. Angel was a pretty name for such an animal, and a big misnomer also. This bird caused Gray to avoid the room like the plague, itself, had taken up residency. Every time that he entered the room or the bird was in the same room at him, Angel would raise a loud fuss; sounding the alarm to let Danielle know there was a predator around. Danielle was sure the poor bird was confused. Angel could read Gray's other side, but was seeing a human too. Someone to snuggle up to, love and get to scratch his neck. She chuckled at little at the idea of the last time Gray came into the room: Angel buzzed his head a few times to scare him out of the room. Gray was more then happy to give the bird what he wanted. As she pet the bird , Angel's soulful eyes looked at her; in them a intelligence that would cause anyone who has been called a bird brain to say thank you. He snuggled down into her hand, lightly nipping her fingers if she stopped petting him to remind her he was there. She looked at the bird and realized that Josh had given her the best gift he had yet, other then himself. Even when he was off somewhere, she wasn't alone. Angel might out live her, then again who knows she might out live him. But she knew one thing and this hit her hard... Josh might not. She sighed and decided that she wouldn't dwell on such morbid thoughts, the now and here was more important then the future, that she wouldn't be able to do anything to stop. She turned and addressed her new friend. “You know what Angel, I really love you. You're going to be my best friend other then the man who shares my bed.” The bird cocked it's head at her and then in a clear voice, responded. “No Shit, Sherlock...” Danielle's eyes went wide with surprise. There was only one way he could have learned that. “Oh my, looks like Josh has already worked his magic on you... Just great.” Danielle shook her head and slid her hand under Angel's feet. “Step up, please.” The bird did as commanded, coming up onto her curled fingers. But as he did, Danielle moved to adjust herself and knocked over the cup of coffee sitting next to her on a small table. ?Damn it, Joshua!? Angel ruffed his feathers a little as she quickly placed the Bird on the perch on top of his cage, and then ran to get a towel. She returned with one of Josh's towels from the bathroom and started to soak up his morning drink that he'd not finished. As she patted the towel around the spill, begin to be glad the rug cleaners where supposed to be there in about an hour or so. It wasn't the first spill on the cream colored carpets. Danielle just tossed the towel over to the hamper of clothes, it was never really full, thanks to Jessie, and the other girl. Dani thought her name was Myra or something. All Dani knew about her was that she was about as bitchy as they cam. One thing could be said about all of this, Danielle was just a little out of her element. But she was still enjoying herself, although she couldn't bring herself to do one thing... Manifest her true form again. No matter how hard she tried it just didn't happen. She had begun to hope that it was a one time thing. That she would be “normal” for the rest of her life. But then the slight itch on both sides of her spine reminded her that she was far from it. Danielle turned to look at the time. “Crap, already eleven. I hope there is going to be enough time.” Danielle walked out the bedroom door and moved to the kitchen. At least one good thing, being on the reservation and growing up with her elderly grandfather in her teenage years, she had learned to actually cook. She could hunt too, particularly with a bow and arrow. She reached the kitchen, washed her hands, and then started to pull out knifes and a cutting board. As she did this, the other maid girl, Myra, came in and with a slightly annoying nasal voice combined with a slight Enligsh accent, interrupted her. “Miss Northsky, you have a guest. There is a Ms. Ukyo here. I couldn't pronounce her last name.” Danielle Nodded at her, as she kept her hands busy setting up. “Show her into the Kitchen please.” Myra nodded and hurried off, Danielle just rolled her eyes. And turned to the sink full of bloody water. A few moments later, Myra came in, leading the lavender blue haired woman behind her. Dani looked at the exotic beauty that Ukyo was... tall, busty, and Japanese. She should be doing more modeling then she did. Dani did know that they shared something in common. Both had been in FHM Magazine. Ukyo had won a little competition in her last federation to be the cover girl for FHM UK when that federation had headed over there and did a Pay Per View in London. Of course, Dani had worked a photo shoot a few months ago for the U.S. Version of FHM. Dani had gone a bit further in her shoot then Ukyo had, but all in the same, she was a woman who could be stunning if she actually tried. Although, Dani had already noticed that Ukyo was a bit more conservative in her clothing picks and that outside of it all. Jean, t-shirt, and boots or tennis shoes were the norm. Ukyo walked in and bowed to her slightly. ?Danielle, how you doing today?? Dani kept working, but turned her head over her shoulder to acknowledge the woman. ?Hey, glad you could pull yourself away from the busy moving in, for a quick visit. Blaise has the girl, huh?? “Yes, but I just got them to lay down and take a nap, so he'll have at least an hour or so of freedom.” Ukyo watched her pull a large slab of red meat out of the sink and place it on the cutting board. Ukyo gave her an inquisitive look. ?You wash your meat?? Danielle just chuckled lightly “No, no, it's just a salt bath. It'll take the gamey taste from the venison, for the stew that I am making for tonight.” Ukyo nodded at that. ?You don't mind stew do you?? Ukyo chuckled a bit. “Blaise and his father are Irish. Stew seems to be a favorite of theirs. Toss all the food into the pot and cook it. Easy for them to fix and such. I'm used to it. I've been living in the states for over a year now. Fortunately I have a man who is also respectful of my natural palette. We alternate nights for cooking, and we try many things.” Dani started to trim the fat from the meat, expertly, setting the fat aside for flavoring the soup base. “I've also got some nice wild rice cooking in the rice cooker. Handy little thing. Just have to get this meat browned then into the slow cooker for the rest of the day and it should be ready, I guess.” Dani started to cube the rest of the roast, cutting it into nice bite size pieces. She looked as Ukyo watched her. She knew Ukyo had a martial arts background, and was good with weapons. Both she and Blaise were members of the elite of the Order, they worked under Lord Malik's group, known as the Hunters. She could tell that Ukyo was impressed, but for Dani, it had been natural survival training for camping and that, which had taught her how to use a knife and, even skin and prepare a deer. Dani moved to break the tension that she appeared to have been building up. ?I've not had the opportunity yet, so... I wanted to say thanks to you and Blaise for putting your necks on the line with me. I know Shadow and Virgil would of rather it to have been just me in the ring last week.? Ukyo smiled at her. “Yeah I kinda got that just standing there. I know that... Baka Ama.. Valentine I think her name is, will not be too pleased with me from last week. But I couldn't let her ruin it. Although the rules of the Order show just what they really thought with that match.” ?What's that?? Dani wasn't to sure about that statement. “They weren't prepared. They weren't ready. They had to try to cheat to try to get the win. And they were all to happy to try it. They're about as honorable as thugs. They're fitting for each other.” Dani sighed, she knew that all to well from watching over the weeks. The Hunting Grounds showed no honor, and the ones that were trying to form some coherent resistance against them were just about as honorable. Emoticon in particular... you don't resort to their tactics when fighting them. It makes you no better then them. Before she started spouting off things, Dani moved to change the subject. ?So I take it Blaise is still busy moving in?? :No he is busy sound proofing one room still. I'm letting him play carpenter for a bit, as long as he doesn't wake the girls, he should be fine.? All of a sudden out of no where, Josh's head peaks around a corner. ?Sound proofing a room? Why on earth would he do that?? Josh looked like he was trying to catch Ukyo in some lewd design, like she'd admit that she and Blaise had wild and kinky stuff going on at night, but she caught Josh a bit off guard with the answer. “Yeah, sound proofing a room. How else would he wail one of his guitars at all hours of the day and night in a neighborhood like this, and not have the cops called on him...” Although not falling for the trap he had set for her, Josh suddenly got a gleam in his eyes. ?Guitar? Blaise is a guitarist? What type of music does he like?? Ukyo sighed at that one. “Ugh, he's into that rock scene. It's maybe the only thing that annoys me. Just look at the themes he's used through out his career. Faith No More, Geoff Tate, Tommy Lee, A Perfect Circle...” Josh suddenly turned to walk out “I'll be back later, okay? I'm going to go see if he needs any help. You know, be neighborly and all that. Can't let my brother be the only one to greet them and help them, just because he's got two-hundred pounds of muscle and I don't.” Danielle just shakes her head, and looks down at the pile of raw deer meat. Ukyo keeps Josh in the corner of her eye as long as she can, but then turns to Dani. ?Whats wrong?? Dani's half laughing when she answers her. “Nothing, but I'm thinking that Gray will be happy. I have a feeling Blaise will have a extra few things in that sound proofed room now.” Ukyo looks at Danielle funny. ?As in?? Dani points upwards with a knife still in her hand. ? The entire drum set that is in the room Josh uses as his own studio for his arts. He's got drawing tables and that up there too. But he's also a drummer. Mostly native, but he's not too bad on a big conventional set. I'm thinking that drumset will be moving if there is someone of similar tastes in music setting up a 'stuiod-like' environment.? Ukyo lets out a chuckle and just smiles. ?I take it that big brother doesn't like the loud noise?? Dani tries to cover stuff up about her older protector. “Lets just say his ears are sensitive to things like that.” Ukyo doesn't miss a beat though. “Yes, Blaise's are t'. But that's from his mother's side. But he's always played the guitar and has forced his changing senses to adapt to his music. But then again, I'd assume that Gray and Josh don't share tastes in music.” Dani moves her knives to the sink as she answers. “Bingo! Gray prefers it a bit slower. Not a lot, but a bit. And Josh likes to go as fast and as loud as he can.” Dani returns to the cutting board and then lifts it up, moving it over to the extra large crock pot that she had setup. She slowly moves the meat from the board into the pot, not missing a single cube. Because she had it on and running already, the first few pieces to fall in start to emit a sizzling noise. She moves the fat from it's place to a pot of water that's on the stove right next to it. ?So, do you mind if I ask you something?? Dani appeared to have caught Ukyo off guard with that. ?Yes, go ahead?? Dani swallowed hard, she didn't know how to ask these types of questions really. ?So, why don't you use the McClancy last name?? Ukyo looked at her blankly. “You know, man and woman, kids, married.” Ukyo suddenly laughs a bit. ?Oh, Blaise and I aren't married... yet. The girls are from his former fiance. They broke up before they could get married. Good thing to, she's a royal bitch. Blaise and I have lived with each other for about a year I'd say, only a month of so less then we've been seeing each other. Guess to a point, we're the same as you and... Josh?? Ukyo looked to take some pleasure with that shoot back. Dani blushed. “Yes. But we're just boyfriend and girlfriend. You and Blaise seem to have a bit more. Might be the length of time there. I'll not argue that.” Ukyo though, had more for that. “Well, technically I'm a bit more then that. For all intents and purposes, I'm so dependent on him for affection and love, that if we ever broke up it would drive me as insane as what Ms. Leanan did to that guy who attacked you.” Dani guessed that they had been briefed on the stuff that had happened so far with them, but this statement put her a bit off guard for it. ?What?? The buzzer went off for the rice cooker, and Ukyo moved to help her out and turn it off. “I've willingly given myself to what others would call a Dryad. His whole Irish family are in this same subspecies of their kind. Basically when they're attracted to someone, and such... if they have sex with them, willingly on both sides... it'll infect their non-Tautha partner. Well they call it an infection. But basically I can't live without him now and stay the way I am.” Dani looked confused by this. ?Did you know about this before you...?? Ukyo looks up at her, coyly. “Yes, I did. Blaise made sure I knew all of that.” Dani looks a bit confused. ?And you still did? Willingly? Why?? “Because I'm in love with him. I knew about that side of him, and I wanted to be with him. Compared to all of the other men I've known, he's been there to support me, my partner, and everything else. When people like my brother and that were telling me that I couldn't do it... he was supporting me in many ways. It just seemed, and still seems right.” Dani's eyes dropped. Suddenly Ukyo and Blaise reminded her so much of her relationship with Josh and why he had held out for so long to be with her. Most people would look at Dani's transformation and just not want to deal with it. But Josh wanted to be there. ?I take it you don't get much in the line of girl talk around here?? Dani looked up, shaken a bit by Ukyo's sudden voice as she had been thinking. ?Uhm, no. Not really. Just me, Josh, and Gray. Gray's always talking about other people, or getting ready for a match. He doesn't talk about himself at all. Josh is one that will talk your ear off, but if others are around, it's only certain things. When we're alone, it's not the same, but it's still not something to talk about 'girl stuff' with him. “I feel the same way around Blaise. I've been pent up a bit since leaving Seattle. His cousin-in-law and I were forming a bit of a team in gossiping about McClancys. But since leaving, I've not been able to do that other then on the phone.” Dani smiled at her. ?Well the boys are expecting us to do things a certain way tonight, but what you say tomorrow you and I get away from the boys for a bit?? “That sounds like fun. Would be a nice change.” As they finished what they were doing at this time, Gray suddenly walked into the kitchen, the smell of deer meat being braised drawing him like the old wolf that he was. “Hey, Gray Muzzle. Before you even ask, no, it's not ready. You can't have any of it raw.” He smiled a toothy grin at her. “Why not? It's the way I like it.” Ukyo looked at Gray with the look of someone eying a specimen. Gray returned it a bit. Dani walked over, drying her hands off with a towel and slapped him on the arm. “Don't be rude to our guests.” Gray grabbed his arm as he replied. “Hey, she's looking at me like that.” Ukyo apologized. “I'm sorry, Mr. Brand. But your features are very unique, even for one of your kind.” Gray's face went stoic. He had been the senior field man on this job until Annie had come in, and now she had assigned Blaise to the job. ?My kind?? Ukyo didn't flinch. “Yes, your kind. I know all about you Mr. Brand, even the tribe you belong to for your human and Garou sides. Most of the 'Get' that I've seen in the field have had more reddish tinges to their coat. Your gray coat is rather unique.” Gray didn't even answer. He wasn't prepared for that. Dani moved back into the kitchen to continue working. “Well, that may be. Gray's doing well on keeping things under control. The only incident we've had with him has been when Jaymes used his little spirit friend.” Gray continues to stare at Ukyo, she just returns it. Dani continues to talk. “My grandfather had a message for Gray last week though. Something about seeing a local Paiute man who might be able to help him continue his lessons to control that side of him....” Suddenly Dani looked to have an idea. “Wait, Ukyo... Blaise is Paiute? Born locally...” Ukyo turns her gaze from Gray. ?Yes. Why do you ask?? Dani addresses Gray. “Maybe he knows the man that Papa said for you to meet. Blaise could help you find him.” Gray nods. “Could be, but everyone else I've asked either won't tell me or doesn't know.” Ukyo gets inquisitive. ?Who are you looking for?? Gray turns to her. “A man, some say he's a crazy old man. Grandfather Screaming Eagle called him Billy Sly Fox. I've been asking around most of the Moapa Paiute, but no one seems to know how to find him or would care to help me. So I doubt if Blaise could help either. He's not exactly been living on the reservation for over a year.” Ukyo starts to chuckle. Dani gets to it before Gray does. ?What's so funny?? Ukyo is again almost laughing when she answers. “Oh, Blaise could tell you how to find that old Coot. You make the stew good enough, he'll find his way to our door to get some.” Now it was Gray's turn to ask her. ?What do you mean?? Ukyo tried to get a serious look on her face. ?Dearest, Gray-san... there is a reason why Blaise has the names that he does to his family. Blaise William Todd McClancy.? ?What's that have to do with it?? Ukyo can't hold it, and laughs some more. “His two middle names. William Todd. Todd is the Gaelic name meaning 'fox'. When Blaise was born, his mother wanted to name him after his grandfather. Conner wouldn't have it fully, due to his son's red hair he wanted him to have a fully Gaelic name. So they compromised.” Dani looked to get it first, Gray still looked a bit confused. Ukyo could see this and went to explain better. “William Todd... shorten it to common names. Billy Fox. Blaise can find you Billy Sly Fox because that's his grandfather.” It looked like the idea of that had hit Gray like a sledge hammer. He shook his head as he tried to comprehend that. “He'll be looking to get in touch with him, also.” ?Oh, is he a wise man too?? Ukyo chuckled some more. “No, he's a dirty old man who likes looking down the front of my shirt. Blaise needs to talk to him for the same reason you do.” Gray looked dumbfounded. ?What?? “Brand-san, you and Blaise have much to talk about, yourself. Maybe tomorrow while we girls take off, you and he could get some time to discuss things of such. There is a reason why a wise man would tell you to find Billy Sly Fox, and there is a reason why Blaise would be wanting to talk to his grandfather. There are traits they share. I'll talk to him for you, and see if things can be arranged.” Gray was still flabbergasted, and at a loss for words. Myra suddenly walked into the room. ?You'll all excuse me. But the carpet cleaners are here. Is this a bad time?? Dani looked around and noticed that everything was at a point where she could leave it to finish cooking. “No, it's perfect. Ukyo, would you mind if we headed to your place for a few hours and the carpet cleaner get done in here? We've got a couple of slobs living here who don't appreciate the carpets.” “Yeah, like my brother.” Gray took the shot, and then looked around to see if his brother was around. “No, let's go. We'll come back later to get everything else done.” There three then headed out the front door, to head to the new house down the road. As they do, Dani notices the Van marked 'South West Chem Dry' is sitting there. . Danielle nods at the man getting out of the driver's side, with a base ball cap pulled down over his eyes, his long dark hair pulled back into a pony tail that comes out the back of the hat. The name “Carlos” is on a patch, on the front of his shirt. Danielle stops and looks at him for a second. He acknowledges her. ?Yes ma'am?? “Nothing you just seem familiar to me for some reason.” He responds to her with a bit of surpise. “Ma'am, I have one of those faces. Or at least I have been told.” The man pushed the rim of his hat up and gives her a smile. His face, dark skinned just like hers, with eyes of a warm deep brown. He was handsome, with almost a model quality face, and body. Dani couldn't help but ask him the following: ?What Nation you from?? The man smiles at her. ?Nation, Ma'am?? Dani moved to cover her thoughts. She had been rude. “I'm sorry, but you look Native, like me.” He nods to her, but then moves to business. ?Is the lady of the house in?? “Well I would be, but we're heading over to a friends house to let you have access to the place.” The man blushes and looks towards the ground. “Sorry ma'am about that. The name on the Order is Ms. Leanan. But the lady over there called you Ms. Northsky.” “That's right.” He clears his throat. “Okay...” “Ms. Leanan is the actual owner of the house, but she doesn't live here. She took care of the arrangements. She's not in town right now as I understand it. You'll find that everything should be in order if you call it in.” The man excuses himself and moves to the front door, as Myra opens it, and shows him in. Danielle Just cant shake the feeling that she knows him from some place. The three head down the road, leaving Myra to watch him. As he enters though, Myra goes off towards the room set aside for her, and looks to be just about to ignore him. As she heads to the stairs she just points. “The main bedroom is through those doors. That's got some of the worst stains. The other bedrooms are upstairs. The only carpet on the sub level is in the gym. You shouldn't have to worry about that one.” She kept going as he just nodded and started writing things on his clip board around the living room. As soon as she was gone, though.. .he headed into the master bedroom. As soon as he entered he looked around to make sure there was no one else there. He noted the dream catchers and mandala shields upon the wall. All perfect tribal artifacts. Then something caught his eyes... A blue feathered friend of Dani's stood on his perch on top of his cage. ?Who are you?? The bird said, clearly but innocently as a bird mimicking something. The rug cleaner man walks over to the cage and smiles at the bird. “You, my fine feathered friend are just perfect.” The bird answers him. “No shit, Sherlock.” Carlos, as his name tag says, smiles wide with perfect white teeth. “Yes, you'll do just fine.” He pulls his hand up and then blows into it. Something that appears to be dust comes off of it and hits the bird, Angel, right in the face. The bird coughs once, and then sneezes as the man speaks. “You... my fine feathered friend have a job to do that I can't...” The bird cocks it's head to one side as he talks to it. “Keep an eye on things for me. You'll report ONLY to me. I want to know... everything that you can tell me.” The bird sneezes again and then flexes it's beak at him. Carlos then turns and walks out to start unloading the equipment he needs from the truck.
