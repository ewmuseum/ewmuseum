My Hangover

14 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
My Hangover Gabery Breed Global Wrestling Promo #763 Date: 1/14/04 Location: Places I can make you bleed. I can make you cry. Simple enough for a child to understand, but it takes while for people to get it implanted into their brains. Size never mattered. Look at me. I’m short and small, but I can get the job done. It’s not the size of the muscles, but the wits and the pride that comes along with it. I think of myself as a car. You got yourself the engine, which is the main component. Now to run that engine there has to be fuel…and I think I’ve found it. I’m not too positive. Then again who ever is positive? We all have our doubts about something. I think I had my doubts in Dallas on Monday. I’ve had my share of drinking at night, but I woke up with something that I thought a hangover could not compare to its pain. I was right. I’ve been in my share of fights too…but this made my head turn. I couldn’t even believe a bit of it. The pain that went to the head felt like a car hit me. The numbness in my body was baffling. I could of taken a kitchen knife and carved anything into my legs, but it wouldn’t of given me the faintest of a squeal. That match did something to me. One of my first starting off, but it did something. It intrigued me in someway. My pistons seem to be given life with a new motor fuel…and that’s wrestling. I’m just doing this for Antonio, and probably made him look very foolish by not knowing what to do when I knocked him out. I can apologize later. I’ve got most of the money I Need to bail him out of jail with, but I don’t think it’ll stop there. Wrestling is like alcohol. It gets into your blood, but this won’t leave. This is my hangover. ‘Did you really need to get your ass kicked?’ it sounds rude, but it’s actually asked nicely by Laramie, who sits on the side of Gabery’s bed and flips the pack of ice on top of his forehead. ‘Yeah,’ is his only response. Watching him talk without moving his lips is bearable, but understanding is the unbearable part. Lying in his bed, he looks like a cancer victim. So helpless, it makes your heart stain with guilt. Though remembering his past, the stains are removed by a solution known to man as hatred. ‘Can’t you get a real job?’ she asks sitting there, worried about injuries Gabery has encountered. He lets out a moan as he lies in his bed. On his back, he has a clear view at the ceiling. The white color of the walls and ceiling isn’t impressive. It is simple enough to make a man go angry just by staring at the blandness. The room obviously not his, but his kids, is starting to bring back memories. Memories of his child hood. ‘Jamie wants to go to the zoo, and I can’t take her,’ Laramie says. ‘It’s for the class fieldtrip, and she said I’d come but I got work.’ Gabery let’s out a moan, but is cut off from any words to be spoken. ‘So if you’re feeling better, go!’ she points out as she gets up. ‘I’ll leave some money for you on the table. Just don’t look like an idiot in front of her friends and teachers. Don’t try to get with one of her teachers. Is that understood?’ Gabery can just moan, but a smile rips his cheeks and is formed. William stares at the TV in front of him as he sits on his black love couch. In his hands is a slice of pizza, with a fork and knife on the plate the pizza rests on. Half of it eaten with the pieces sliced down the middle. His eyes reflect the TV screen, which is the very show Gabery appeared on in front of millions of people around the world. Taped the match, William stares as Gabery throws the blood from his nose at Kenji Lee. ‘Sick ass bastard,’ William says suddenly losing his appetite and setting his plate next to his feet, which are placed on the table in an orderly fashion. Picking up the cup from the plate toaster, he puts it up to his mouth and takes a sip of the alcoholic beverage within. He stands up and looks around his house. Behind him is a bar, where he takes a walk. Around his house is a pool table, nicely set up with the balls in the middle, and the cue ball lying next to them. Two pool sticks lie parallel from one another. ‘He’s exposing himself,’ William says as he takes the whole shot of the glass and lets it hit his counter, luckily not breaking. He lets the taste soak in, and he lets out a shiver. ‘Murder who exposes himself on TV,’ William says with a faint smile on his face. ‘Mental problems. Emotional problems. Everything is wrong with that boy.’ He picks up a bottle of Vodka, and shakes it up a bit. Unloosing the top, he fills half the glass with the foreign drink, and places it down onto the counter. He picks up the glass, and walks towards hit kitchen. An area that takes up a third of his floor. ‘I’ll have justice on him soon enough,’ he says as he opens up the door to his fridge and picks up the orange juice carton. He places it onto his counter, and unloosens the top. He pours it into his glass to the very top, and picks it up. ‘Cheers for you being behind bars.’ He takes the whole shot for him, and doesn’t leave a drop behind.
