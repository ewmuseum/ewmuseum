The Journey: Part 20/The Army's POV(Evo RP)

20 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-20-2005 Early Morning 6:45 A.M. Sounds typical doesn’t it? This is the standard time that Joshua Curtis would get up in his house and usually he gets up, gets his shower and goes to read the morning newspaper. Problem: He’s already done most of that?..4 HOURS AGO! Four Hours earlier? 2:45 A.M. Ever get so excited that you think that you can’t sleep? Joshua Curtis ,the esteemed leader of the faction known as “The Army of Old School” gets that way’a lot! Joshua had heard some news from a mutual friend of his in the legal department about a clause in a contract that stated something about a friend of his that lived with him that could help him get back into his place of business and that would be the wrestling ring. Having heard this Joshua went to bed REAL early so that he could get a jump on the day and be ready for any question that the media had for him concerning the rumor. Here was the thing: Joshua didn’t really know much about who this friend was that could get back into the ring or how they could but he did know that it was someone that was in the “Army” and for Joshua this was a HUGE morale boost because of the fact that he took a HUGE hit when it came to his ongoing mess with Christian Zenith and now Joshua has added his faction to his “hit list” but the problem was that he didn’t have many friends in the fed and this “Mystery VP” that everyone kept talking about in the backstage area didn’t help him much because of the fact that he didn’t really know whether this person be it man/woman or whoever was a friend or foe but it would appear that this person didn’t like Christian one bit and it was evident by the match that the person stuck him in. The match that this “mystery power” stuck the GW International Champion was a match where he(Christian Zenith) had to have his girlfriend(Vivicia Valentine) and some mystery partner face off against The Connection and KPJ! Now let’s be fair about this for Christian for a second. The man had the spaldings to get into a steel cage and face Curtis one on one for the International title, the second highest belt in GW’s title ranks and for that he should be commended greatly so for him to be stuck in a match by a mystery power and then not know his third partner?! Come on how unfair could one get! Like its any better for Curtis though. Ok consider that Joshua just had a hell of a match where he had suffered a gash that took about 10 stitches in his forehead and now he has to go into a match with a partner that he’s not sure who its going to be from his camp yet AND someone whom when Joshua went and offered his services to this “friend” he got basically told where he stood and that kind of burns Joshua as well. Remember that the whole reason that Joshua took this fight up was because of something that Christian did to Gunner months ago ,so to Curtis refusing his assistance at this point is almost the “Point of No Return!” As Joshua got his clothes he walked through the room that he shared with his Californian lover in Zack Johnson and he made sure not to make any noise until he got to the bathroom when he turned the water on with the door shut so that he didn’t wake his boyfriend of a good couple of years and Joshua can’t help but smile every time he sees the man from San Francisco lying on the pillow with a small trail of drool going down his chin enough to still look absolutely adorable and Joshua just couldn’t help but crack a smile ,which is something that he hasn’t done in a LOOONG time! When Joshua went to go get his shower the clock had turned to about 3:00 in the morning and he had just clicked the lock on the door and turned the water on when he started thinking about what he was told again and while ok part of him got “real excited” about the idea of whom it was going to be coming to the ring with him Friday a part of him also thought that maybe he was going to have to go in with Ami and that made him frown a little bit but not enough to kill his buzz. OK let’s face facts here: When Joshua and Zack were together they were dominant! They lost only like once in their time together and the truth of the matter is that there weren’t many if ANY teams that could touch them and probably still can’t! Ok so they were a little “odd” in that they were an openly gay tag team but that is what was so charming about them and what made the fans clamor to them that when GW was on the air you WANTED to see them and now honestly the team is nothing more of a novelty act that failed and that is the brutal yet honest truth! Can Ami compete in the ring? No doubt considering that she IS a former Women’s Champion but that is also against?.WOMEN! Zack is more capable of getting in the ring against people like Zenith ,Frost ,Salaok ,El Serpenti and others and that is why Joshua was hoping that the news he would hear at about 7:30 that morning would be the news that he so wanted to hear! Joshua completed his task of showering after about 45 minutes with an extra 15 for reflection so he could think about the times before when the were in the IWF and how much fun that was back then where no one criticized or questioned what the did or why they did it and that is what Joshua wanted again. Joshua wanted those days back but at the same time he wanted GW fans to get to see someone that they caught onto for their own reasons and not just cos he was Josh’s boyfriend either! Zack was special in that if you saw him at an appearance by himself(which happened more than you think believe it or not) you would see this guy that just loved being around people and the fact that he like men didn’t turn off the fans AT ALL! Something that made Zack such a figure within the faction was that at the appearances he did for them there were always little girls giving him roses and hugs and asking him about his family ,friends and his partner and he answered everything that he could without a moment’s hesitation because the thing of it was that Zack was a people person and he thanked Joshua for that A LOT! Some say that if Joshua didn’t enter his life Zack wouldn’t be able to be as open as he wanted to but because Joshua taught him that you didn’t have to give a damn in this business what people thought he did as he wanted and people grew to love him for it. After Joshua got out of the shower he got dried off and dressed and then proceeded to the kitchen to go eat some breakfast before going into the TV room and watching some good old fashion Anime and proceeded to do that for the next couple of hours. Back to a more present time? 7:15 A. M. Well it was about 15 minutes before Joshua was going to hear the news that would make Joshua either cringe or jump with delight and as he made his way through the house to the dungeon he noticed that a contingent from the OZ ,Gundam and IWF houses had filed into the workout room where a familiar sight was becoming right in front of him?. Zack Johnson, the 6 foot 2 inch , 225 pound man from San Francisco ,California was downstairs in the cold ,cement room on the mats showing some of the recruits in the Army what he could do?.on them! Apparently Duo went and shot his mouth off and said that because Zack hadn’t been in the ring in a couple of months he was “rusty”. Well Zack showed him “rusty” by suplexing him around the room and even showed him what the “Blackout” felt like! Now that was EASY compared to what he did to Wufei! The kid from China said that he could kick any pro wrestler’s ass around the ring because Pro Wrestling is fake and they don’t know real pain. Well Zack showed him some pain gave the kid a “Triple threat” back breaker where he picks up his opponent in a back breaker and has their back come down on his knee’OUCH! Chester tried to take the guy and got snap mared ,a leg drop and then he threw him into the practice ring and did Curtis? “Angel Splash” for good measure! “Whoa darling what’s going on here?” Joshua asked as he finally snapped out of his trance with watching his love eternal wrecking shop on the members of the Army. “Oh hey sweetie” Zack said as he hopped out of the ring in his black trunks and black boots with his brown hair tied back in a ponytail and his beautiful brown eyes shimmering with delight of the wonderful news that he apparently had just heard and that was the reason that he was down in the “Dungeon”. “Hey sunshine” Joshua replied before giving his boyfriend a quick kiss on the lips before coming out and asking the question that he wanted the answer to for a good couple of hours now. “So what’s the big news I’m hearing that you all got for me?” Joshua asked and Zack’s smile COULDN’T have been any BIGGER! “Well Hun” Zack began “I got off the phone with an attorney that I knew back in San Francisco and he sent me a document that has some good news in it for you.” Zack explained and Joshua had one of those “What the **** is he talking about?” looks on his face but when Treize handed him the letter from the attorney that Zack knew that look turned to one of pure joy! The letter went as follows: Dear Mr. Johnson, I read your complaint and I faxed it to a judge that handles these cases specifically. He had heard about your situation in which your partner had been released and then allowed back but without you as his tag team partner in the federation known as Global Wrestling. While the situation might be legal the judge has stated that at the coming show on the 15th you Zack Johnson will be permitted to climb into the ring for one match and this match will be “The Connection” and Mr. Ken Partridge Jr. against Christian Zenith, Vivica Valentine and their mystery partner and Ami is to be relegated to managerial duties for that night. I wish you well Mr. Johnson and tell your friend that I hope this much makes him happy as I am working on getting this to be a full-time situation within GW so that you two can be together in the ring again! Sincerely, Greg Winters Joshua could’ve cried ,wept, kissed the ground that he walked on ,but all that he did was hugged his boyfriend tightly and kissed him so passionately that it could have left them both winded but in reality everyone that was in the room was happy that that night would become something that no one and I mean NO ONE in GW would forget’EVER!
