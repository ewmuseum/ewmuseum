Hunter/Prey

5 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 5th, 2003 Leinad Soma grinned ferally. There really was nothing like a good hunt to get the old heart pumping -- or not pumping, as the case may be. He inhaled deeply, drinking in the scent of his prey. Such a lovely night for hunting cowards. Soma slipped noiselessly through the darkness as the minutes passed, staying out of sight -- it simply wouldn't do to have his prey see him before he desired it, and it wasn't like he needed to see him anyway. The so-called Steel Lion's smell was more than strong enough for him to follow based on scent alone. Still, this simple stalking was getting boring. It was time to end this hunt? Soma's muscles tensed in anticipation, as he prepared to unleash their unnatural power. That was when the car hit him. *** 'The man in the back said he'd kill me if I didn't tell you this,' whispered the girl, her voice quivering with fear. ''The hunt is on? have you ever been hunted by someone who will eat your heart?'' Rokku's eyes widened in surprise, and he caught a glimpse of a pale figure in one of the Saloon's shadowed back corners. Leinad Soma. Rokku felt his heart hardening with anger -- that bastard would threaten a girl's life just to get to him?! -- but he squashed it, refusing to let it show. 'Don't worry about him,' whispered Rokku in return, 'follow my lead, and I'll see that you get out of here safely.' The frightened blonde nodded. Rokku quickly said goodbye to Ely, and lead the young woman out the front door. Once they were outside, Rokku pointed off to the West. 'Go that way,' whispered Rokku, 'I'll go the other direction. If he wants me, he'll follow me, and you'll be home-free.' 'Oh,' he added, slipping a card into her pocket, 'and here's my number.' The blonde nodded again, and darted off like Hades was on her heels. Rokku immediately started walking briskly in the opposite direction. As he walked, he pulled out the anger he'd crushed earlier, and began to shape it into a tool. He tempered it with venomous thoughts, allowing his rapidly growing distaste for Soma to become the center of his world. He focused on it, drank and ate the hatred and spite -- and suddenly, he could feel Soma. Maybe twenty -- no, thirty meters back. Rokku just knew Soma was there. Stalking him, like he was some kind of animal. Disgust cooled the hot steel of Rokku's anger into a razor-edged blade -- and a plan sprung to life in his head. Quickly, he took a left -- then a few minutes later, another, and another. Soon, he was back in front of the Dark Horse Saloon again -- except this time, as he passed it, he ducked into the side alley where he'd parked his convertible. He slid into the driver's seat and slipped his keys into the ignition, bringing the car to life with a flick of the wrist. Sitting low in the seat, Rokku waited -- and when Soma passed in front of him, absorbed in his 'hunt', Rokku threw the car into drive and floored the gas. He slammed into Soma at forty-five miles per hour, sending the eastern European careening out into the street. Immediately, Rokku slammed on the brakes and reached for the baseball bat in his back seat. Finally letting his anger explode, Rokku leapt from the car and rushed Soma. The eastern European sociopath was getting back to his feet with a surprised look on his face -- until his face made an intimate acquaintance of a Louisville Slugger, and Soma was sent sprawling, landing flat on his back. 'That was for the GIRL!' Rokku spat, leveling the bat at the prone Soma, 'You want to threaten women to get what you want? Why don't you try threatening ME instead, you sick fuck?' Soma slowly sat up. Blood streamed down his face, and his clothes were torn from skidding across the pavement -- but otherwise, he seemed eerily unharmed. Soma looked up at Rokku as the Steel Lion stood over him, shaking with rage -- and he laughed. 'Well played!' said Soma, cackling more as he rose to his feet. 'You doubled back over your own trail, making following your scent useless -- and then the car, and the bat. You might just be a worthy challenge after all!' Rokku's glare turned icy cold. 'I don't know what the hell you're talking about, you sick bastard. But shut the hell up and FIGHT ME! Here, I'll even make it easier for you!' With that, Rokku stretched his arm out and let the bat clatter to the ground. But Soma only laughed again. Slowly, he walked towards Rokku. 'You have absolutely no idea what you're dealing with, child.' Rokku scowled, his muscles tightening in anticipation of a fight. Soma's lips split into an animalistic grin -- and Rokku was sure he saw fangs. Soma's tongue darted from his mouth, licking blood off his face. Soma stopped, his face inches away from Rokku's, and locked eyes with him. For an instant, Rokku's resolve faltered. Soma's grin widened. 'You're not ready for me yet,' Soma said plainly. 'If you fought me now you would surely perish -- and I when we fight in the eyes of the lord, I want you at your best.' Rokku froze solid, and for the first time in years, felt real fear. Soma just turned and walked away, vanishing into the shadows. Moments later, Rokku regained his senses. He realized immediately that he was surrounded by gawkers. Cursing his own weakness, the Steel Lion snatched up his bat, leapt back into his car, and sped off. As he raced towards home, one thought played over and over in his mind: What the hell am I dealing with now?
