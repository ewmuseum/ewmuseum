Insomnia

9 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Evo #2 (8/9/04) He could almost hear the seconds ticking off the digital clock. Train layed listlessly on the bed. His arms casually rested behind his head, beneath the pillow his dreadlocks sprawled wildly across as his legs crossed, just as casually, beneath his black down comforter. His eyes waundered aimlessy across the ceiling, tracing the contours of the bumpy, yet attractive spackle treatment he'd seen a million times. They only steal the occasional glance at the bright red display of his alarm clock sitting across the room atop his chest of drawers. He couldn't think of many reasons he should still be awake at... his eyes shoot over to the clock again... 2:17 a.m. True, there were a couple, but he had a hard time narrowing it down to the one most responsible for this terribly unusual bout of insomnia. What's more he wondered why any would way so much on him now. It was something way out of the ordinary for him. Even in the days where he felt compelled to sleep with two glocks under the same pillow he had beneath his head now, falling asleep was never a problem. The thought of someone breaking in the middle of the night to slit his throat never kept him away. Train had conditioned himself to be a light sleeper so if someone came a creepin' they'd leave a bleedin'. He was never scared because with the heaters right next him he knew he was ready. Heh........ Could that be it? Train had spent a lot of time in his relatively young life tracing over all the possibilities for how something that look to be relatively good could turn into a disaster. Some call that being a pesimist, but Train disagreed. If you can predict how the shit might go wrong or how somebody might try to fuck you, you could stop it from happening. What could be more optimistic than that in this world? It was like they always said at the end of G.I. Joe... 'Knowing is half the battle.' Thing is... That shit takes a lot of energy. Always thinking 10 steps ahead, running the angles everyone could be playing over in your head, making back up plans for your back up plans... Yeah, that shit was taxing. You need your sleep if you're gonna keep that shit up. Train thought for a moment, wondering if maybe his body's lack of response the warm covers and the dark, along with the peace and quiet was because he hadn't thought everything as much as usual. Maybe he'd missed an angle... skipped over a scenario that could cost him the big plans he had for GW in the next few weeks... Namely cementing himself as a true champion by putting the Platinum Title over his shoulder and he wrapped tag team gold around his waist. Naw... that wasn't it. Hell he had that shit locked down. The plan was layed out and solid in his line...he'd even found a way make J-Gonz line up with it. No doubt he didn't appreciate the shit, but that was ok. He just knew he had to give him a few weeks. Let him see how a real Don thinks and operates. Yeah, the kid was good, but a couple of hours sitting in a truck with a good camera and some creative wiring on the phone junction boxes outside his house and club had basicly handed Train the keys to his whole operation. It was too obvious. Even the fucking cops could catch on to the way he was running it and 99% of those bastards had the brain power of a turnip seed. But it was all good. Train would change all that for him. Whether he wanted to or not J-Gonz was gonna sit back, relax and let Train make over his whole operation. Shift it from out there and asking for trouble to underground and untouchable. Yeah... Gonz would fall in line when he saw how much money was going to be coming into his pockets. The kid was so damn worried that Train was going to hand the tapes and negatives over to the cops, but Train made him realize he wasn't holding that stuff over his head to bust him. He was holding it to make sure he stopped and listened when while he showed a kid who came up hard just like him, how to be somebody. He resented it now...but he'd fall in line. So what? What the hell was keeping him up? The plan was definitely on lock so what as there to worry about? That's when it hit him. The weight of the revelation fell on him like a ton of bricks, startling him just a bit... The plan was that Train was gonna show Gonz the ropes and make him a champion again. They were going to put those tag straps over there shoulders and every mouth in GW that flapped about what Train hasn't done and what he can't do would slam the hell closed for good. When he walked down those hallways in that arena every eye would turn to the only double champion in GW. Fuck Josh and his Master of the Mat shit.. he could have it... it was just some once a year, luck of the draw shit... The Platinum Title and the Tag Titles were year round, 24 damn 7. World Champ... International Champ... damn all that. He'd be twice the champion they were and he'd have the hardware to prove it. Only problem was... What was the back up? Train hadn't thought past that part. Hadn't even considered the possibility that he might fall short.... that he might lose. What then? What if one of those other chump teams...especially those little fucking circus monkeys the Vergomovz ran out of that ring with his shot? What would he do then? Would he have to put up with all the shit talking...with every chump who thought it was cool to run their mouths and disrespect him? All the talk had gotten back to him.... all the rumors about how he was ducking contenders and just trying to hold on to the belt... He'd heard it all. He wasnt' meant to, but it felted through... the whispering in the hallways... the snickering behind his back... How the fuck was he going to deal if he couldn't shut the muthafuckas up? Suddenly he stopped, a dam magicaly appearing, cutting off the flood of doubt. That was all bullshit. He had a back up plan. Hell it was the simplest he'd ever come up with... There was no back up because there would be no need for one. Jamester, Roswell... The circus monkeys... Fuck them. Fuck what they want. Fuck what they dream about. Fuck their career making moments. Fuck them period. If Train had to personally bust each and everyone one of those bastards open and watch them bleed to death in the middle of that fucking ring those cocksuckers would not take what belonged to him and those Tag Titles? They belonged to him. To hell with what everyone else had to say. This shit wasn't really about them. This shit was about him. He might need it to handle them...but he wanted it for himself. Yeah... it was all worked out... the plan was on lock... Sleep finally drifts in....
