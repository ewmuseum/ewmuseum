Freedom (DUI pt. 6)

9 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/9/04 Freedom. A simple phrase that not many can really define it in words. Only by feeling. Taken for granted by the majority, but respected by all. Being able to do what they want and act how they wish. Breathe the air a little fresher and be more grateful for what they have. To be open. To be free. Johnie pushes against the glass door, swinging it open to the left and steps out on the concrete in front of the Amarillo Courthouse a free man once again. A bright smile gleams across his face as he tramples down the many steps onto the pavement, letting out a loud shout of relief. Raising his arms up in the air, he looks up at the bright sky as the sun peaks out from behind the cloud cover as if to greet him as an old friend. Its been too long since he’d seen it. Lowering his arms and head down, Johnie looks down the sidewalk as a teenage girl looks at him crookedly after his weird behavior. Shrugging it off he can’t help but smile as he’s been freed from Amarillo Prison after being incarcerated nearly a week. “Finally, I can get the hell out of this hellhole.” Johnie exclaims as he sighs loudly, glancing back at the building itself. “I’m so sick of these morons keeping me from my life. Now I can go back to Taos and do something worth while. Speaking of...” Johnie reaches into his jeans and struggles to pull out his gray cell phone from his left pocket. Flipping it open, he presses the large red button in the upper left corner to power it on. Going through its usual loading sequences. Screen pans over and settles on a scrolling menu of options. Johnie clicks on messages and raises the phone to his right ear and turns back towards the street watching the cars cruise by as he listens on. “You have 13 messages,” coos the robotic female voice on the other end. Strange how they always seem to have a woman’s voice for automation. “First new message, received on January 3rd.” A brief pause comes over the receiver as it plays the gruff message of a slurred voice with a loud bass beat in the background. “Hey Johnie....wha-what’s going on man. I’m calling on Mike’s phone.” The blubbery tone is Trey. Apparently the message wasn’t passed on to him like it should have. “I’m calling to make sure you’re alright. I mean its been a couple days since you left for Tulsa and you’re not back yet. I just wanna make sure you’re all cool in the head and everything.” Trey pauses a quick second as he catches what he just said and stumbles to correct himself. “I mean, I don’t want you getting stuck out there because of some shit or something. Just call me when you can, yo. Later!” The hard click hits Johnie’s ear and makes him rub it a bit quickly. As he raises it to his ear again the automated voice announces the second message from the following day. Right away, he knew the hard sigh wasn’t a good one. “Johnathan! This is your mother. Your sister just told me about what kind of trouble you’ve gotten into now. I hope you’re proud of yourself.” Johnie could feel the anger welling up inside him again. Just when the day started off so well, cold reality seems to come back pretty easily. “Unfortunately, I won’t be able to save you this time. I just want you to be able to learn something from this and that you can grow up a little from it. Maybe by the time you hear this, you’ll...” Before she can finish her sentence, Johnie lowers the phone and hits the End Call and grunts loudly in the air. His left hand clenching the phone tightly as he can feel the plastic casing begin to give way a little. “Getting all caught up in life again, Mr. Ringo?” As he grimaces, he hears a soft voice from behind him spinning him around quickly. The person it originated from flinches backwards in fear and flings his briefcase in the air as he grabs his chest. Falling to the ground along with the contents of the case is Percy. “Ugh...what are you trying to do to me, Mr. Ringo”!” Johnie just turns as he changes from holding back anger to holding back laughter. He can’t hold it back long before he bursts out in laughter as he sees his lawyer flop flat on the concrete. “Real smooth there, Percy.” Johnie manages to cough out between breaths. “Real smooth.” Struggling off his back, Percy perches himself up and starts frantically to pick up his scattered papers. “I’m so glad that I can amuse you Mr. Ringo,” grumbles the flustered defense attorney. Picking the papers up with one hand and trying to brush the stringy blonde hair on of his eyes with the other, Percy gets up on one knee and sighs heavily. Johnie’s laughing begins to wane as he holds his right side and stands over Percy smiling. “I guess you’re good at one thing then. So what’s the big rush” You afraid to have me leave without saying bye?” As Johnie smugs, Percy looks up at him briefly and scoffs while grasping the last scattered pages on the ground. “Hardly.” He notices that the last paper is underneath Johnie’s right Nike and glances up at him, acknowledging that he won’t budge. Leaning forward, Percy starts tugging at the sheet. “I just wanted to make sure you weren’t leaving without the necessary infor--mation!” On the last pull, Johnie lifts his foot to turn away, unaware that the movement would slingshot Percy back a few feet in the struggle. Seeing him fall on his back again, he merely chuckles slightly. “You’re really liking the ground today, aren’t you?” “Not particularly.” Grumbling loudly, Percy places the remaining pages back in his damaged briefcase and rises to his feet. He brushes his cheap blue suit off lightly, trying to keep the dust from staining it too much. “Listen Mr. Ringo...” “Will you just call me Johnie already,” interrupts his client, raising his eyebrow in contempt. “You keep saying Mr. Ringo like I’m some old man or something.” “Fine...” Shrugging slightly, Percy sighs as he continues on. “As I was saying, I wanted to make sure that you were aware of your legal requirements of your case. Not only the DUI trial, but also the contempt of court that Judge Holt cited you with as well.” Johnie rolls his eyes as his thoughts go back to the day before in the courtroom as he blew up at the verdict the judge set down before him. “You were lucky enough to get off with just that. I don’t see where she got her leniency from, but you were stuck with just another night in jail and a $200 fine.” “So when can I just get my truck and go home?” Johnie sighs impatiently. Of course Percy was expecting this and backs off a couple steps nervously. “That’s just it, Johnie. Your license has been revoked for the next 3 months and you can’t get it out of the impound without a valid license.” Slowly turning his eyes to Percy’s direction, Johnie burns a stare to him that made the sweat on his brow run dry immediately. Taking a step forward with his right, Johnie turns his full attention to him. “You telling me...that I can’t drive myself home over this bullshit”!” Stuttering back at him, Percy chokes back the answer before eventually spitting it out. “Y-Y-Yes.” Raising his hand to his mouth, then grazing it through his dark matted hair, Johnie fights to control his composure once more. Instantly he pulls out a pack of Camel Filters and quickly has it in his mouth and lit. Taking a long, slow drag from it, Johnie sighs in partial relief. “Fine,” he grumbles as the last remnants of smoke permiates from his lips. “Let it be that way. If I can’t get my truck yet, then I can’t get my truck yet. I’ll find some way to get it home.” Surprised at the response, Percy lowers his shoulders and blinks repeatedly. He shakes himself out of it and continues on his lecture. “Also, the judge has decided what your seventy-two hours community service will primarily consist of. Her and I discussed it for a while since you are an out of state offender and it would be most convenient if you did your time in Taos. So we devised a place where you can go.” Johnie exhales another fog of tar and tobacco over his shoulder and turns his attention back to the busy street, contemplating what to do now. No truck, no cash. This could get interesting. “So what will they have me do” Watch over some kid playground or something?” Johnie laughs a bit at the thought of community service as a prime use of punishment. How the judicial system has to force people into doing this for the neighborhood. Sad how times have changed. “Paint some fences somewhere?” Percy continues on and allows a small grin to sneak across his chin. “Not exactly. It would be helping at the Oak Ridge Retirement Home.” He snickers at the selection as though its child’s play. Johnie doesn’t seem to agree. He turns back at him, tossing the cigarette aside into the street. Quickly the smile fades into a frown. “What”!” Merely inches away from his face, Johnie glares into Percy’s fearful visage and lets his anger roam free once more. “Babysitting some old geezer that’s about to kick off any day” Wiping ass. Hauling pills. Changing channels What the fuck is wrong with this judge bitch?” Trying to control his composure, Johnie pulls away from Percy and breathes heavily to himself. Cursing under his breath. “I...” Percy tries to come up with some sort of excuse for the reasoning behind it all, but there’s not much to it. Its punishment, pure and simple. “I tried to reason with her, but she insisted on this. She said it had something to do with teaching you common respect.” Johnie raises his head up quickly at that answer. Shaking it in doubt, he can’t help but scoff at it all. “Whatever. I’ll do my time and get it over with.” Turning back around, he walks over to and slaps his arm around the nervous lawyer. “Percy, I like you. In a sad sort of way, I do. With a little motivation, you’ll do whatever you have to for me. And I admire that. If I ever need any legal advice or just some bullshit, I’ll be sure to call on you.” Widely opening his eyes, Percy manages to smile timidly as he gets nudged back over by his client. “Now, you can go. I’ll handle my stuff from here. Send me a letter of all the shit that has gone down. Just make sure its in plain English so that I can understand it all.” Percy continues to walk on, clutching his briefcase tightly and nodding incessantly. Johnie stands and sighs while he pulls out his phone once more and pushes a single button for speed dial. Raising the mobile to his ear, it instantly rings a couple times before he hears a click. “Trey” Yeah, its Johnie.” Pausing in between responses, he keeps a cool voice over it all as he discusses business with his roommate back in Taos. Simple business of getting home. “I need you to fly out to Amarillo... No, no big deal. Its all taken care of. Just get out here. I’ll be around, I just have one thing to do before I go. Just have to pay a visit to a new judge friend of mine...” TO BE CONTINUED...
