Giving Answers, and the ultimate 'Whodunit'

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: day after yesterday (4-22) 'It's easier to run, replacing this pain with something more, it's so much easier to run, than face all this pain here all alone' - Linkin Park ----------------------- Here I am. Backstage at a house show the night before Evolution. In less than 24 hours I will defend my International Title against one of the greatest names in GW history. For the first time in singles action, I will face GW Hall of Fame legend, Tragic Hero. Vincent Welch. Anyway, back to tonight. Tonight I'm going to just sit around in this un-fucking-comfortable chair until Dinkler gets his lazy ass back here to ask me some questions. HA! Who am I kidding? Dinkler won't do anything. He'll just find some other reporter to come back here and do his job so he can take credit for it. I kick my feet up on the folding chair in front of me and lay back a little bit, listening to the stereo. 'Headstrong' by Trapt is playing, just barely audible over the shower water from the adjacent bathroom. Cindy decided to take a shower before the interview so she could look her best (which in my opinion was the sweatpants and tank top she had on before.) Just as I'm starting to get comfortable in that terrible chair, which pales in comparison to my tan arm chair back home in Michigan. Before the song's over, there is a knock at the door, and before I can say anything to answer it, the door bursts open. Alexis Steel stands there, holding the microphone in one hand and a clipboard in the other. She leans against the door frame, with one leg forward in a cheap attempt to raise her skirt a little. She looks at me and smiles. 'Hello Derek,' she says, moving the hand with the microphone up to push her hair back behind her ear, but only succeeds in smacking herself in the forehead with the square part of the mic. 'Hi Alexis, how are you doing tonight?' I reply, trying to hide my laughter, and failing miserably. 'What's so funny? Do I have broccoli in my teeth? Oh god, I hope I don't have anything in my teeth.' Alexis runs around the room, trying to find a mirror. I decide to have a little fun with this idiot blonde. I look her dead in the eyes and say, 'Hey, uhh.. Alexis? You've got some white stuff in your hair.' HOLY SHIT! This stupid bitch bought it! Not only that, but she incriminated herself! 'I do? Oh, damn, I thought I got all that out,' she answered, then covered her mouth before she said anything worse. After a few silent seconds, I said, 'Can we get on with this? Cindy's in the shower and I want to get some food as soon as she's out.' 'Sure. First of all, do you have any other words for Ben J. Pailthorpe?' 'BJP? Why would I have words for him? He's just a little British bastard trying to once again ride the tails of an all-American creation to stardom. First, the Young Bloods, now the Young Bloods again. Ben, face it man, the Young Bloods haven't been around for a while. Well, at least not the group. Young Blood has and always will flow through my veins, and from there right into the very being of the GW. You, however, abandoned me and the Bloods when we needed you most. You may have been a Young Blood since before me, but I never turned my back on the YB. You made a mistake doing that, and you will pay for that mistake.' Alexis looks vaguely shocked. Before she speaks, the water in the next room over shuts off. 'Wow. Derek Frost sending a plain message out to Ben Pailthorpe. Next question. Tragic Hero and Quartz. What do you think is going on?' 'I neither know nor care what those two are up to. Tragic Hero is in for a surprise if he thinks I'm going to go out there and not expect some freaky shit to happen. I'm ready for him and Quartz if I have to be.' 'Ok, basic answer but that works. How about Kullypso? Do you see him as a threat to you during this match?' Before I can answer this question, I hear a scream from the bathroom. I get up and dash through the door, throwing the door open. I look around the bathroom and see Cindy standing in a towel, pointing and crying. I rush over and wrap my arms around her, before looking in the direction she is pointing. The mirror on the wall. Steamed up from the heat. Words written on it by a finger. Eerie words. 'I'M WATCHING YOU'
