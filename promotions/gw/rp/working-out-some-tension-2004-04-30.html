Working out some tension

30 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/30/04 Brick intensely worked the heavy bag in the gym. A few feet away, Ely Braxton was pumping iron. The intensity of his blows increased with each that hit the bag. It seemed as if the bag would burst under much more pressure. Brick had been in a funk for days. Everyone around him could see it. Three days had passed since Wrestlecade. Three days had passed since his triple cage match with Deacon Holmes and Train had exploded into a huge brawl involving M.O.B., the Upper Echelon and Brick's friends and allies. The referee ruled the match a no-contest. Technically, this wasn't a loss so Brick's undefeated record at Wrestlecade remained intact, for all intents and purposes. However, Deacon Holmes was still Platinum champion. But that wasn't the only thing gnawing at Brick. There was something a lot deeper than the idea of losing to either Deacon or Holmes. 'You hit that bag any harder,' said Ely, 'you'll be wearin' it like a wristwatch.' Just then Brick stopped punching the bag and looked down at the floor. 'I'm tired, Ely.' 'You've been working out non-stop for about two hours now.' 'Not that kind of tired, man.” he replied, walking over to Ely. ? I'm tired of the bullshit this company is going through.' 'I know. Those punks at WMW...' 'I'm not talking about them, although they are a problem. I'm talking about the title situation in this company. Every damn title in the company is being held by cats who would sooner tear it all down. Reno has the tag titles, Deacon has the Platinum and Cruiserweight titles, Zero has the International championship...' 'I know, I know. I'd like nothin' better than to see those sumbitches in TUE get what's comin' to 'em.' 'I meant to ask. You still okay after what happened at Wrestlecade?' 'I ever get my hands on that asshole Reno...' 'I know. How’s Christina?' “She was a bit shaken up, but what happened afterwards put a smile on her face.” “You mean Rokku coming back?” “Yeah.” “TUE likes to say they’re the best. They couldn’t even take Rokku or Sabbot out.” Brick and Ely then walked over to the speedbag where Brick proceeded to work on it. 'Well, at least none of 'em are holding the World title.' 'Like that's supposed to be better. Josh doesn't care about this company any more than TUE does. Nobody in the Elite does!' 'Well, you probably won't have to worry about at least 1/4 o' the Elite.' 'What are you talking about?' 'Didn't you hear? Ice quit.' 'Get out!' 'I'm serious. He and Christina decided to terminate his contract. Guess he got frustrated with all that happened, what with Brandi an' TUE.' 'Bunch o? punk asses.' 'Tell me about it. If Jesse were healthy, we'd be in that Tag Team Invitational. I'd love to get that sumbitch Reno back.' 'Granted, I don't have any love for Derek Draven but still, I'm sick of Reno an' TUE actin' like they own the damn place. And Reno? He's the biggest punk ass out of all of 'em.' 'Even bigger punk ass than Deacon?' Brick hesitated slightly to answer that question. 'Well, maybe he's not the biggest. Wait, yes, he is.' 'Anyway, at least Donny an' his brother got a shot at the bastard.' 'Hope they get it too.' 'I just hope somebody gets to kick their asses. We need somebody to get at least one title away from TUE.' 'Something else about Ice leaving the company. Every appearance and match he had lined up gets replacements.' 'So?' 'So, one of those matches was a re-match with Josh for the World title on Evolution. Guess who the replacement is?' 'Serious?' 'Yep. You got the main event.' A smile then crept onto Brick's face. This was just what he needed. 'Actually, I hear Josh steeped up to the plate after Ice left.' 'Yeah? Well, let's hope he can step up to the plate Monday night.'
