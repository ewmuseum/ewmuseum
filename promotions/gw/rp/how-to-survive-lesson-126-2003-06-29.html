How To Survive - Lesson #126

29 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6.29.03 June 27th, 2003, Martha’s Vineyard, Massachusetts The screen is pure black with the previous words printed in white at the bottom. They fade quickly before the blackness begins to lose its opacity. Waves wash up onto the tan sand about the ocean front, the white ripples cascading up onto the islands edge and then slowly rippling back down. The scene pans back, showing a beautiful white colonial style home built just yards from the waters edge. A beautiful old style brick fence circles the house with the inside completely landscaped; a rod iron fence has been built at the entrance to the fence, a large “TXP” written in beautifully ornated letters in the center of the rod iron. Our view advances, passing between the rod iron bars and into the landscaped yard of this beautiful home. A black limousine is parked off to the left on a cement parking area; the custom Honda Civic, courtesy of The Upper Echelon, has been parked next to it. On the opposite side of the yard, another cement parking area has been created, also connecting with the main driveway. Two vehicles sit here as well, a custom metallic black Mitsubishi Eclipse Spyder convertible with metallic silver and blue flames on the sides, mirror tinted windows all around, 20 inch chrome rims with TXP in the center, euros on the back, and blue ground FX underneath. Beside the custom Eclipse Spyder, a custom black BMW has been parked. The scene continues to advance towards the door to the house. Suddenly it flies open and Scott Pierce steps from inside; he’s dressed casually in a pair of shorts and a green polo shirt. His dark brown hair is cropped to his head, giving him a very clean cut look. He walks with a limp however from the injuries he has sustained in the ring over the years. He glances back towards the door where two bodyguards step out behind him, both dressed in black suits with earpieces. They each make their way towards the limousine, making their way around Scott as they walk down the steps. A second passes and Tyler X. Pierce steps from inside. He glances at Scott and then the two security guards walking towards the limo. “Guys, take the bens. Scott and I were going to take the Spyder.” Tyler steps through the door fully and outside. He’s wearing a pair of black long shorts that hang just below his knees with a white stripe going up each side; on each strip the word “Outkast” has been written in block lettering. He wears a red t-shirt with a black tribal marking on the front and a silver dragon in the center. For once, he’s dressed down and like a guy in his early 20’s. He runs a hand back through his hair as he steps down off the porch and then starts towards the custom Spyder. He slips a pair of mirror tint shades on and glances back at Scott. “Coming bro?” Scott glances about and shakes his head, a smile on his face. “He said he could do it” I didn’t believe it.” Tyler glances back at his brother, a curious look on his face. “What was that, bro?” Bro? It doesn’t really seem like Tyler’s type to use the term but it would seem that it’s natural for both of them. Perhaps it’s simply a term that the Pierce brothers picked up through their childhood or just a forced on term to make them seem closer? Or maybe Tyler just can’t remember his own brother’s name and this is the best way he can think of to call him by name without actually doing so. No matter what the reason, he continues to call him by this term of endearment. Scott glances up at his brother and shakes his head, “Nothing Ty.” Ty, it would seem that Scott doesn’t use the same simple terms when regarding his brother. With this quick answer, Scott walks towards the car and his brother. Tyler reaches into his pocket and the car suddenly unlocks, starts and the blue lighting turns on. With a grin, Tyler glances at his brother and motions to the passenger side. “After you, bro.” Scott is quick to slip into the passenger side, shaking his head a bit as Tyler follows suit and climbs into the driver’s side. Within seconds, the windows and top lower to reveal the all black leather interior complete with TXP monogrammed on the seats in silver and on the center of the steering wheel. The sound of the engine as Tyler slams it into reverse and peels forth from the driveway and towards the now opened automatic rod Iron Gate tells of other enhancements that one can’t see with their naked eye. Tyler turns the corner very sharply, the tail end fishtailing towards the other end of the street. The bodyguards are quick to try and keep up with Tyler as he peels off down the road towards town. We can hear the gears of the BMW changing as it also peels from inside the gates of the Martha’s Vineyard estate. The gates slowly close behind the BMW, sealing the estate until their return. Tyler throws the Spyder into a higher gear, leaving marks on the pavement behind him. The BMW is hard pressed to keep up but does so for the moment. Within seconds, the two cars have met traffic and are quickly advancing around them, passing every vehicle on the road. The road changes slightly and Tyler cuts in front of a taxi and in back of a Porsche, nearly clipping the front end of the taxi. Quickly he merges into the third lane, barely making his turnoff. The BMW doesn’t have enough time to make it to the exit however and is caught going with the traffic. Our scene changes to inside the car with Tyler and Scott. Scott is gripping the “oh shit” handles, holding on for dear life. “What the hell are you doing, Tyler!”” the informalities have ceased as Scott panics, the speed at which Tyler has been going is something Scott is obviously not accustomed to. Tyler is laughing as he jerks the wheel and slams on the brakes, the not-so-stock sports car going into a parking lot at 80 or 90 miles per hour, having slowed down from the 124 the car was hitting on the highway. We change views again, getting a view from above as the Eclipse Spyder spins in a full 360 in the center of the parking lot before coming to a complete stop, rich black marks being left behind as reminders. “Tyler! You’re insane, boy!” Scott screams, one hand giving a death grip to the back of Tyler’s seat, the other gripping the oh shit handle on the door in the same fashion. “Relax, Scott. I’m just having a little fun. Besides, those guys have been following me around like my fuckin” shadows for the last month. I need a break from em, you know?” Tyler leans back in the seat, relaxing a bit. The car idles, the engine purring; the power beneath the hood extremely evident. Scott glances around, shaking his head. “You never fail to astonish me” I never know what you’re going to do. One minute you walk out of a business meeting in a suit that Dad couldn’t afford, talking with your private lawyer as you climb into a company limo? the next, you’re pulling a street race in Martha’s Vineyard by yourself? just to lose your bodyguards!! What’s the deal, Ty?” The words are spoken more rhetorical than complete but it’s obvious that Scott expects some type of answer or response. “I may be in The Upper Echelon, I may be a member of the most elite group of wrestlers ever to grace the rings of world, and I may be able to hold my own with Preston at my side in any meeting over any business deal” I may even be able to outfox Dad on some things. But when it all boils down to it, Scott, I’m just me? You can put me in a suit, put me in a ring, put me in a conference hall with TUE at my sides? but in the end, I go home at night and I want to be me. I’m twenty-four years old? I’ve given up the shit that used to give me trouble, but I still want to have a little fun. Life can’t be all about work, Scott. It can’t all be about the big deal you came to with Mr. Dollar in the conference room? it can’t all be about lawyers, suits, and briefcases. Life is life? and you’re meant to enjoy it. I plan on enjoying it, Scott.” Tyler never once looks up at his brother, but instead gazes off into the beautiful ocean nearby. Scott takes a minute, collecting his thoughts before offering a comment to the motivated and heart felt words that Tyler just laid before him; “But what about risk” What about goals and achievements? Having fun isn’t the only thing in the world, you know.” Tyler smiles and glances at his brother. “You think that’s the only thing on my mind” Scott, I’ve worked my ass off these last few months. I’ve worked harder in Global Wrestling than I have at any other point in my life. See, Scott? in a few days, I’m stepping into the ring with Sean as my partner? and we have to step up against Salomane Vation? Dynamite X? Angus The Bull? and Tragic Hero, Sparks and Salaok. I’ve been working my ass of to get ready for this. Sean and I have been training together every single day for the last two weeks. There’s nothing that can stand in our way, Scott. Not a single? solitary? thing. You talk about risk? hell, every time I get into that ring it’s a huge risk. Every time I jump from the top rope, it’s a risk. Every time I walk anywhere near a Global Arena, it’s a risk! I’m a member of TUE? I’m a wanted man. Nevermore would rather see me on a million machines in a hospital than dead! That’s how cold they are? my back is constantly being watched. If that’s not risk, Scott. I want you to tell me what risk is?” Scott glances towards the entrance to the parking lot. “That.” With this one simple word, the camera pans and shows a Mustang 5.0 pulling into the lot, the headlights are flashing blue on and off, on and off. Another temporary light is positioned on top of the car. The sounds of a voice being amplified through a megaphone can be heard. “Please remain where you are.” “Fuck!” Tyler glances at his brother and then towards the undercover mustang pulling to a stop right next to the Eclipse Spyder. Two cops get out, both dressed in jeans and bullet proof vests, their badges hanging from silver chains around their necks. One of the two is wearing a pair of aviator sunglasses and both appear to be in their early to mid twenties. The second one is laughing as the first approaches Tyler, a snobby grin on his face. “Damn that was a stupid move. Do you always slam the gas when a Mustang pulls up behind you?” Tyler’s first thought would be, “a Mustang pulled up behind me” Eeesh, must have a powerful engine in that thing.” But he knew much better than to actually say that. “I’m so sorry officer. I ? well, heh, I was trying to lose my bodyguards.” The first officer looks at the second and then back to Tyler. “Your bodyguards?” Yeah, I was born yesterday. Let’s see your license and registration.” Tyler complies and hands them the two documents before sitting back in the seat. The first officer takes the two items back to the car and slips inside, calling it in. “So” you were going one-hundred and twenty-six miles per hour in a sixty-five. Damn this thing has some power in it.” Tyler tries not to but grins none the less. “Yeah, it does doesn’t it” I’m really sorry about this guys? but I really was trying to lose a tail.” The officer just shakes his head and steps back, looking at the vehicle. Within moments, the first officer has returned. “Nice huh?” the first says to the second, only receiving a nod in return. “Wish my paycheck could afford one of these puppies.” Tyler glances up at him, the business man in him coming out. “Well, if that is a warning ticket” ? the officer quickly cuts Tyler off, his attempted bribing not even getting a chance of being offer. “Don’t even try it, Mr. Pierce.” Tyler chuckles and glances ahead again, the officer’s jest full attitudes lightening the situation. “Pierce” are you Tyler Pierce from Global Wrestling?” Tyler nods and glances up at him, “The one and only.” The officer nudges his partner. “This guy is awesome in the ring” that match between you and Roz Blayze? Very nice work. Very very nice? Im glad you put that slack-ass out of commission for awhile, I was tired of hearing about him. Oh, and congrats on winning the belt.” Tyler is somewhat shocked but nods his thanks and replies with the same intent, trying to be as friendly as possible. “Thanks?” Tyler says as the second continues to read over the information on Tyler’s registration. “I have to ask you though, Mr. Pierce. What happened with you and that new guy” Trey Reed?” the officer leans on the car, agitating Tyler in more than one way. He hides it well though, not showing his anger at his insult. “Reed wasn’t important enough to waste my energy to beat. I wasn’t worried about him.. I got too cocky in the ring. In all honesty, I just didn’t care about him. I’ve been training for this upcoming tag team match” for the gold you know, with Sean Price? and I didn’t want to risk anything going wrong before that match. I want to be at my best then and I plan to be.” Tyler speaks with pure honesty, his words obviously well rehearsed as he’s probably been asked this question quite a few times by quite a few reporters. “Now, I have a question of you.” The first officer looks up and toward Tyler and then towards the second before nodding. “Uh, ok sir. Go right ahead.” “You said you pulled up behind me when I was going that fast” why didn’t you pull me on the highway?” Tyler asks, quizzically. Scott sighs, leaning back in his seat. The first offer grins before glancing at his partner. “Well, to be quite honest with you. We wanted to see what this thing could do.” The second officer chuckles and glances down. “Here’s your warning, Mr. Pierce. Let’s try and keep the speed down.” The officer hands Tyler his registration and license and both walk calmly back to the Mustang parked a few feet from Tyler. Scott stares at Tyler, dumbfounded. Tyler smiles at him and starts the car, the engine growling and firing up instantly. “What just happened?” the shock in Scott’s voice very apparent. Tyler grins at him and puts a finger to his lips before pulling off and easing onto the road. “The eye is not so quick as reality, Scott. You say that I’m a business man one minute” and a adventurer, for lack of a better word, the next. You say that I take too many risks? that I can afford anything now? yet you don’t know what just happened?” Tyler’s words are calm, calculated. He’s thought through this? he’s been taking lessons. “No, Ty, I don’t! I saw you lose your license” and walk away like nothing happened!” Scott is excited, happy, and somewhat jealous all at the same time. Tyler smiles as they move down the road. “What you saw was my cooperating with the officers and the law. They liked my car” they know who I am?” Scott just shakes my head. “Ok, what didn’t I see, Ty” I know you’re dying for me to ask.” Scott shoots off. “The five-hundred-dollar bill folded underneath the registration card.” Tyler just grins and glances at his brother, taking his eyes off the road for just a second. “You didn’t see me buy two pigs in a five-hundred dollar blanket” he may have taught you how to wrestle, Scott. He taught me how to survive.” Tyler laughs as the scene slowly fades to black. Who is he? Who taught Tyler to survive? and who taught his eldest brother, Scott, how to wrestle? What is this all about? Could he be speaking of Il Capo? And if so? does that mean that Il Capo is more than a position? more than a shadow hiding behind an oak desk? More than a figure head? could he very well be the ace in the hand? The ace that TUE hasn’t played yet? Sean Price, Deacon Holmes and Tyler Pierce? Then came John Zero Who’s next? Who’s coming? Is he coming? And if so, who’s he coming for?
