Tha MOB dayz ! Jay Quickness Global Wrestling Promo #1141 Date: 4/13/04 Location: That was then. [i]Aggressive (Ag-gres-sive) adj. (1.) Characterized by or inclined to begin an attack or encroachment; hostile. (2.) Disposed to vigorous activity; energetic and forceful. The scene opens up in a Las Vegas, Nevada casino that is owned by 'The Million Dollar Man' Jay Quickness. In the casion you see slot machines being played and card dealers opening up new pack of cards and shuffling them. The casino look very busy with people alking around and playin games and losing there hard earned money, the way it should be. [b].::Scene Changes::.[/b] The scene changes and opens back up to the owners office. The office is nicely decorated, and the wall are pictures, stuffed animal heads and clocks. There is a golden name plate on the desk and it reads 'Jay Quickness'. The big black chair swings around and sitting in it is Jay Quickness. Jay Quickness is wearing black slacks, black shoes and a silk black shirt. Looking real sharp, and in his hands are a whole bunch of 100 doller bills. He threws the money on the desk and begins to laugh, out of no where the door swings open and in comes Frankie and Joey both holding a guy by his arms who is trying to get lose. Frankie and Joey are wearing there usual Black and white suit, while the guy who they have in there hands look straight like a bum. 'WHOA! What do we have here fellas?' Jay Q. asks them while he gets up from his chair and walks to the front of his desk. 'We caught this guy ova here trying to steal money from a ol' lady, but we caught him before he could do any harm to her.' Frankie explains to Jay Q. while Joey nods his head, agreeing with Frankie. 'Really, so you like to steal from people, especially ol' women who cant even defend themselfes' Jay Q. says while he sits infront of his desk. 'Uhm...Im sorry, I will never do it again. Just let me go. Please dont call the cops on me.' The guy beggs to Jay Quickness. 'Nah, homie. We aint gonna call the cops on you. You see we dont get down like that.' Jay Q. tells the guy with a smile on his face. 'Oh Yes. Thank yoo..........' ???? At that second that the guy was going to say thank you, Jay Quickness pulls out a 9mm pistol and pistol whips the man right across his forhead. The man falls out of Frankie and Joey's hands and hard to the floor. Frankie, Joey and Jay Quickness all look at each other and begin to laugh. Jay Quickness gets serious and stands over the bloody guy and begins to speak. 'THATS THA WAY WE GET DOWN, DONT YOU EVA COME INTO MY FUCKING CASINO OR ROB ANYONE IN YOUR LIFE AGAIN. OR I WILL FIND YOU AND KILL YOU.' Jay Q. screams out to the half consious at the top of his lungs. 'Now get this piece of shit out of my site.' Jay Q. tells the MOB while he sits back in his seat. Frankie and Joey grab the poor guy by his legs and drag him out of the office. Jay Quickness whips the blood of the handle of the gun with a hankerchief, and puts it away in his desk. and begins to count his money. [b].::10 Minutes Later::.[/b] Joey and Frankie come back into Jay's office with smiles on there faces. They both take a seat in front of Jay's desk and begin to speak. 'We did it, we got rid of him' Frankie tells Jay. 'GOOD! What did you do wit him?' Jay Quickness asks Frankie 'Let's just say, he will be slepping wit that fishies.' Joey speaks out with a serious tone and look on his face. 'Nice, and if anyone else down there act up. Handle them MOB style.' Jay Q. says with a smile on his face. 'I love this job, taking peoples hard earn money.' Jay says. They all begin to laugh, and Jay Quickness begins to speak again. 'Oh yeah one mo' thing before you guy's get out of my office. We have to find tha guy who shot me before the cops do. And handle him.' 'Yes Sir Boss.' Frankie and Joey says 'Now get your ass downstairs and check tha machines again.' Jay Q. tells them. Frankie and Joey both get up from there seat and walk out of the office. Jay Quickness begins to finish counting his money that is on his desk and laugh as the camera fades to black. -Scene ends.[/i]
