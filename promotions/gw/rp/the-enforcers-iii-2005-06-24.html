Mark Nason The Elements had been electric the entire evening with the pre-CrossFire gala that Ken Partridge Jr was throwing. “Ready, Steady, Go” was blaring throughout the New York City nightclub as, impressingly, Paul Oakenfold stood in the DJ’s booth himself mixing it. As Bill Moresi and Ace Harding looked up, they saw Ken, with Gunner right beside him, chatting with the man that was by far his favorite artist in the field of dance music. “I swear,” began Bill, “I Know they cast him as Superman, but the cat looks like James Bond.” “I can see it,” laughed Ace, “standing there dressed like a” well? a movie star and drinking his Gibson martini.” “That’s shaken,” chimed in Josh, fresh off of the dance floor, “not stirred.” The other two men erupted into laughter at Josh’s comment out of nowhere. However, the laughter was somewhat broken upon a first look upon Josh by Harding. “Josh, are you okay,” asked the submission specialist. “Sure,” replied American Youth with a hint of curiosity in his voice. “Well,” began Ace, “it looks like” well? hell son, it looks like Ken dressed you tonight.” Bill once again fell into laughter as Josh shot Ace a playfully angry look. “That’s because he did,” laughed Bill. “He called and told me he was gonna” get Josh dressed up tonight by hook or by crook.” “Well,” began a now laughing Harding, “which one did it take?” “I’ll have you know,” stated the former World’s Champion as he snapped the collar on his jacket, “that I decided to do this out of respect for Ken.” “Oh my God,” Bill began with astonishment. “You like it. Hey, Ace, Mikey likes it.” “It’s about damn time, man,” stated Ace. “Maybe we’ll see you more like this and less like Kurt Cobaine from here on in.” The three laughed before Josh hit one back. “Yeah, but you know the difference between you and me, Ace,” questioned Josh. Harding simply delivered a shrug with a smile on his face in response to Josh’s comment. “I make this” look good.” The three men erupted back into laughter, again before being stopped by the sudden silencing of the music. They looked up to reveal Ken standing right outside of the DJ’s booth and looking out over the crowd with a microphone in his hand. “Ladies and gentlemen,” KPJ began, “on behalf of myself and Gunner, welcome to The Elements.” The patrons exploded in regards to Ken’s opening. “You’ve all made it here on a special night,” he continued. “Not only is the greatest musical standard in the world of dance and techno, Mr. Paul Oakenfold here with us tonight?” Once again, the club goers cheered loudly as Oakenfold stood with a smile on his face. ??but, we also have even more special guests with us here tonight to help us celebrate our CrossFire victory over WMW pre-maturely, kind of like Mark Bishop.” The audience laughed at Ken’s joke as he continued with introductions. “We have, of course, my brothers-in-arms, the men that frequent this club more often than Donny J. McNasty on Jack Daniels Tuesdays, the rest of The Elite” Josh, Bill Moresi, and Ace Harding.” The crowd erupted into applause as they all pulled away from the trio, forming a circle around them. The three men smiled and waved at the response they were receiving while carrying on conversations through grinning teeth. “Every time,” mumbled Bill. “I know,” responded Josh. “I know.” “Just smile and wave,” instructed Harding, “he’ll move on in a second.” As if Ken instinctively knew what Ace was saying, he began the rest of introductions. “Also here with us this evening,” The Franchise continued, pointing at an upper-level entryway, “the voices of Evolution, Brandi Sights and Logan Creed.” The crowd once again went into applause as Brandi dressed in an absolutely beautiful blue sequin evening gown, and Logan, dressed in a pair of tuxedo pants and a white button-front shirt with a cigar in his mouth, made their ways over to Ken and Gunner. “Brandi,” stated Partridge, “you’re stunning as usual, and Logan, well, what can I say” I’m nuts over ya?.” Ken’s wisecrack came just as he stood behind the midget, causing another round of laughter to fill the club. Logan made a playful fist towards Ken as the two shared a laugh before shaking hands. “Finally,” finished KPJ, “our final two guests of the evening” one is one of the stars of the film Bewitched and the other is my co-star in my upcoming film, Superman Lives? Will Ferrell and Jake Gyllenhaal.” A loud and raucus applause filled the club as the two men walked out and towards KPJ. A chant of “Frank the Tank” filled the large building, causing Ferrell to stop and look out over the crowd. “Once it hits your lips,” shouted Will to the fans, “it tastes so good!” This encited a loud cheer from the clubbers in attendance as Ken finished up his announcement. “Once again,” he stated, “thank you all, and have a good time. Oh, drinks are on the house for the next fifteen minutes.” As one would suspect, a large stampede broke out heading to each of the different bars in the club, causing patrons to all but run over the likes of Josh, Bill, and Ace as they had began to make their way upstairs with Ken and the rest. “He eats it up, doesn’t he,” stated Ace in laughter as the started up the stairs. “What,” asked Josh, “the fame, The celebrity, The movie-star friends?” “Who would want any of that,” questioned a sarcastic Moresi. The three finally made their way up to the VIP area with Ken, Gunner, Oakenfold, Brandi, Logan, Gyllenhaal, and Ferrell and began to share in the champagne. Well, everyone minus Josh, who was drinking a Vault from a champagne flute. As the night wore on, Bill and Ace began to break away from the party and head down into The Elements Underground, Ken’s gym facility that lay in the basement of the club. They figured that as long as they were there, they may as well prepare for their match against Absolutely Merciless and La Herencia de Heredos at CrossFire. They may have lost to them last time, but that was with ring rust on either player. This time, they planned on making no mistakes. This time they planned on making no exceptions. This time, they planned on winning.
