Another night at the Dark Horse

22 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/22/04 There was a great deal of energy in the Dark Horse saloon. People were dancing to the sounds of “Hard to Handle” by the Black Crowes. The mechanical bull was being put through its paces. At the bar sat Brick. As he nursed a glass of ginger ale, much had been weighing on his mind. His rivalry with Deacon Holmes and Train had been escalating for months. On the recent edition of Evolution, Holmes interfered in a six-man tag match involving M.O.B., Brick, Jay Quickness and Status Quo. The feud started just before Wrestlecade and didn't end there. In fact, it was just starting. 'Hey, handsome,' a female voice called. Brick turned around to see who was calling him. It was Brandi Sights. 'Buy a girl a drink?' 'Brandi, what's up?' 'I could ask you the same. I've never seen you here alone. You're usually with Ely or Jesse. Where are they?' 'They're busy.' 'Busy with what?' 'Jesse's still getting over injuries. And Ely, well...' 'What'll you have, Brandi?' asked Jack the bartender. 'Oh, I'll have a margarita.' 'He's busy with Gunner.' 'Yeah.' 'And it bothers you.' 'No.' 'It doesn't?' 'No.' 'You're perfectly okay with it?' 'What, are you Torquemada now?' 'Well, you had your chance with Gunner and blew it.' 'I didn't blow shit. OK, we both considered it for a while, but in the end, we just agreed to remain friends.' 'Uh-huh,' Brandi muttered, rolling her eyes. 'What does 'uh-huh' mean?' ' 'Uh-huh' means I remember the time she gave you that good-luck kiss before your match.' In the spring of 2001, Brick had a short-lived feud with Jesse Briggs after Jesse had decided to go on his 'Redneck Rampage'. One victim of his rampage was Gunner Addison. The next week, Brick had intervened on Gunner's behalf when she and Jesse had an altercation backstage. Weeks later, Gunner visited Brick in his dressing room on an episode of Overload. Brick was getting ready for a three-way match with Jesse and Quartz. Before she left, she gave him a kiss for luck. 'What? That was nothing.' 'Nothing? I'm surprised she didn't jump your bones right then and there!' 'I know you're not talking about jumping somebody's bones.' 'Oh, fuck you!' 'You already did, remember?' Brandi quickly looked around to see who heard Brick. 'I thought we agreed never to talk about that in public!' she whispered to him. 'Don't get mad at me! You're the one who suggested it on Evolution!' 'When did I do that?' 'The second episode back in 2002. You talked about my stamina and my 'hard head'.' 'That was Mark.' 'Oh, you didn't reply 'Yes, he does'?' 'I didn't mean it like that.” “Yeah, that’s why “Let’s change the subject again. So, you Deacon and Train are going at it again at High Noon.” “Yeah, and this time I’m gonna win.” “Just do me a favor and kick Deacon’s ass.” “Oh, I plan to.” “No, I mean really kick his ass. I spent two months in a coma with broken ribs and internal damage because of him and his buddies. And they still haven’t paid for it” “I hear you. Don’t worry. Deacon’s gonna pay for that. Him and the rest of TUE.” “I mean, I know you also have Train to deal with.” “It’s cool. I got this. Mark my words. Deacon and Train are in for the ass-whippings of their lives.” “You know, it’s nice that we could talk like this.” “Yeah, because before we usually did more than just...” “Do not finish that sentence.”
