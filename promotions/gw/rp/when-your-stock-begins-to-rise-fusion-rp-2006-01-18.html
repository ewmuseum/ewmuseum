When Your Stock Begins to Rise *Fusion RP*

Date: 01-18-06 ?I don't care anymore if I let you down I believe that I need to be free I'm so used to my life with you around I don't know anymore the real me? It was mid-afternoon by the way the shadows cast on the wall of the bedroom; it was not the light that woke Eryc up, but instead it was the telephone. He rolled over reluctantly and opened his tired eyes to pick up the receiver. “Hello?” Eryc’s tired voice gave away the fact he was still in bed. “Are you still in bed?” The caller inquired. Eryc hesitated until he could no longer stall. “Uhh why?” Whenever he received a call from Jane, who is his agent/publicist, he knew it was going to be a busy day; it was just a matter of what she had lined up for him to do. “Make yourself decent because I’m on my way there.” He tried not to sigh loudly in her ear so that she would give him the “you’re the one who wanted to be a star” lecture she gives every time he complains about something she sets up for him. “Fine, give me about ten to fifteen then.” He didn’t give her a chance to respond before he ended the call. Eryc slowly rolled out of the insanely large bed and staggered into the bathroom to prepare a shower; his mirror let him know in a precise manner that his late night carousing was doing a number on him. He couldn’t keep up with the kind of schedule he was once used to. His hand reached in and turned on the water and waited for it to heat up to how he preferred it before shedding off his sleeping pants and stepping into the glass enclosure. The water was hot and perfect for his sore muscles that still hurt from his last fight. As the water ran down his back, thoughts about the night in Japan ran through his mind. The question Ash had asked him still lingered. Eryc, do you think that your present condition is permanent? It haunted him to think that there was a possibility that he’d become a vampire again. As much as his bones ached after a fight or how tired he was after a set during a concert, he did not want to go back to living the life of a monster. He knew though that by becoming mortal again things would be tougher all around. Those he called siblings (by blood not birth) would be watching his every move, stalking him and if he came close to blurting out secrets they would make pate out of him. The relationship with his brother felt strained to him the very second they came into eye contact in Tokyo. It would take some for the two of them to adjust to the settings one another presented if adjusting was possible. Another problem in his life was the fact that he was in love with two different women. There was Jane, whom he was paying money to make him look good in the public eye and to set up appearances and things of that nature. It just didn’t seem right to want to have a romantic encounter with someone you worked closely with. Then there was Krissy, or rather who he thought was named Krissy. He met her at a bar in Detroit while working some independent fights and they hit it off really well and the two were getting closer as weeks went on...until he found out something about her that forced his hand and he left town. He wanted both of them equally but leaned more towards Krissy because of how they bonded. For Eryc it was always easier being dead and not having feelings dragged in the mud or worry if someone feels the same way. BUZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZ! Eryc snapped out of the fog of his thoughts to hear the security buzzer going off. He quickly lathered himself up and washed off before jumping out of the shower in a hurry. Slapping a towel around his waist, Eryc walked out to the hallway to speak on the intercom. “Jane, I’ll buzz you up and leave the door unlocked so you can just come in. I haven’t finished getting dressed yet.” He pressed the button for the security door a few seconds to give his company ample time to get through; then unlocked his door so she could come in when she reached the top for you see Eryc lived in a penthouse suite that overlooked the west side of Central Park. Not only did he have a view of the park but also of the Statue of Liberty as well. It was beautiful at night and nice conversation piece to bring up with some of the snobby people he interacted with on a semi-daily basis all in part due to his somewhat celebrity status. Jane turned the doorknob and walked inside. This was not her first time at his place for business but she always expected him to have some new piece of art or musical instrument lying around. “You were out late last night, weren’t you?” She could tell by his reluctance to get up and by how slow he was moving getting ready. Jane heard him moving around in his room and had a faint thought about taking a peek. It was a well kept secret that the young Miss McNamara had a crush on her employer; she may have had other clients of high profile but none of them seemed as charming or mysterious as Eryc Morgan. “I would call it more along the lines of entertaining some friends.” Jane wondered what his definition of “friends” might be with the way he threw the comment out there so quickly. All she needed was to have a scandal on her hands to clean up. It was bad enough she had to try and find a way to spin some good PR his way after he was arrested on murder charges last year. “Friends you say” You’re not dating a porn star are you?” Jane said curiously. Eryc chuckled as he walked out of his bedroom with the towel he was previously wearing (in his hand). Jane had to try not to stare at his muscular arms that he showed off by wearing a black tank top. “No, I’m not dating a porn star...but I could if you want me to” It would give you something more to try and clean up for me.” “Please” she said with a reasoning tone of voice. “I don’t need any further mishaps like that one which landed you in jail last summer. You may think people will consider you a bad boy but it will leave less and less people wanting to invest their time and money into you.” Eryc shook his head as she leaned towards a lecture that he didn’t want or need. “Don’t worry sweetheart, it’s cool; I’m still being watched over that little fiasco.” Jane looked at him surprised like because she didn’t know that he was still being investigated by the police or whichever law enforcement agency that was. “I have some good news.” She had to change the topic and this was a good way to start. “There are a few events I have lined up for you in the next few weeks.” Eryc nodded before he started to make way to the kitchen for some orange juice. “Hold that thought, I need something to drink...do you want anything?” “No thanks, I’m fine at the moment.” He returned with his juice and stepped over to his couch and dropped down into it, folding his leg over the other to listen to what Jane had in store for him in the next little while. “Go ahead, I’m listening.” Jane opened her briefcase and pulled out a folder; then set it upon the coffee table for him to look at on his own time. “I’ve set up an appearance for you at the local children’s hospital. There have been several sick children there that requested through Make A Wish for a visit from you.” Eryc reached over and grabbed the folder to browse through it while she continued to give him details. “In the folder are names and information on some of the sick children who are only asking for a small bit of your time. I think it would benefit for you to do this and maybe just maybe the police will give up on hounding you.” He looked through the folder and saw photos of the sick kids and a little sheet with things they liked and what type of illness they had. It tugged at him so to know that he was someone they wanted to see when there were other celebrities out there who were so much better than he. “I’ll definitely do it, but I don’t think it’ll win me any brownie points with the Vegas P.D.” He paused to take a sip of juice before he continued. “This is New York, Jane; they could care less about what I do here because they figure the cops here would throw me in jail in a heartbeat.” He wasn’t quite done with his venting on the L.V.P.D. “I’m surprised if when they hear about this they wouldn’t want to have me arrested for breathing because it might kill one of these precious kids.” Eryc set the folder back down onto the table; he was curious to know what else was being placed on his social calendar. “You know how you were telling me that you wanted to do shows at smaller clubs, to get back to your roots in a way?” He remembered telling her that only a short time ago because he was fed up with playing huge crowds. He craved a little more of an intimate setting like he had at the beginning of his career. “I remember, why?” “Well...” Jane smiled widely, she was proud of herself for this project. “I was in contact with a Matthew Moore who is the manager of a club called The Elements here in New York and he told me the owners would really like to have Opaque Oblivion play there.” Eryc scratched his head a moment. The Elements...why did that sound familiar to him? It came to him quickly as he remembered where he had heard the name before. “I’ve heard of the place...it’s owned by someone who fights for Global as well, Ken Partridge Jr. is his name.” Jane was quick to add that he was only a partial owner and that it was jointly owned by Ken and his associate, CeCi Braden. Eryc had never really met Ken other than the one time the two had been put in a three-way fight between him, KPJ, and Christian Zenith for the International Title. Other than that they’d never really even spoken to one another despite having similar interests. “When is this supposed to be” I need plenty of time to call the other band members and make sure they are on board.” Of course he was interested but that didn’t mean Ash, Scott (the bass player), or Emily (the drummer), would be. They had all decided to take a break from playing together right before Eryc became human again. Everyone had their own projects going on or were about to have one start. “You have kept in contact with them, right?” Jane was concerned when he appeared to be unsure if anyone other than him would want to do it. The gig could mean exposure for not only Eryc but for the others involved as well. The Elements was one of the premier clubs in the city for those who didn’t know by now. “Yes and no.” He said hesitantly. “When we decided to take time away from the band we stopped talking and then I took my six month sabbatical.” That simply wouldn’t do. Jane wanted to make sure that this was a done deal. “Your job for this evening is to call everyone and see if they are interested and if they aren’t, then use that charm you seem to always have when the time is right.” Sure, now that he didn’t have the ability to talk his way out of paper bag he would need to figure out a way to get the job done. It was much easier when he had powers of suggestion that much was certain. “I’ll call them, don’t worry.” “Good. I want you to call me when you find out everyone’s answer; I don’t care what time of night it is since your old habit of being a night owl is resurfacing again.” The things he did for pretty women. If she wasn’t so beautiful he would have kicked her out of his apartment already and found another agent. “I know I said I’d work on that but last night was an exception...there was LAN party at friend of mine’s place.” He said shyly. “I like to try and keep my hand/eye coordination at the highest level.” Jane shrugged her shoulders amazed at even more things she was finding out about this man. “Men and their games...at least you don’t sit and watch television all day.” “Not likely to happen when I have you for an agent, I don’t have time for it.” Eryc shot a glance over to her playfully so that she knew he wasn’t being an asshole. “Keep it up...you’re lucky that you have me around.” “Don’t I know it!” He said under his breath and did his best to mask it. Jane watched him carefully and thought she might have caught what she said but it didn’t make much difference anyway, she’d never get him to crack and ask her out. Eryc was too hard of a shell to want any kind of romance in his life and he was a fighter for crying out loud. Who would want to see their boyfriend or girlfriend get beat on every week? She was definitely not on the waiting list for that. “Eryc, you know how I love to sit here with you but I have other things to do and other clients to take care of. Don’t forget to call me tonight, okay?” Jane stood up as did Eryc and he walked her to the door. He opened it for her and she wanted to say something more but he rushed her out with a simple “Talk to you later, Jane.” Both Eryc and Jane stood on one side of the door leaning against it. For them it was just never meant to be and for at least one of them it was torturous. Oh the frustrations of the opposite sex.
