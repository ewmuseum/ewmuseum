Sands and Surprises

22 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 22nd, 2003 At midnight, the Chihuanuan Desert is like hell frozen over. The extreme temperatures are braved only by the native flora and fauna, and those seeking military-grade survival training. No sane person would ever come out here without good reason, and they definitely wouldn't come without the proper equipment. No one had ever accused the Thunders of being sane. Akodo Rokku dropped to one knee and caught Kakita Yoshiko's axe kick at the crux of his crossed arms. He shoved hard as he stood, sending her off balance -- but Yoshiko went with the flow of movement into an impromptu backflip. Before she had a chance to recover though, Yoshiko found herself on the defensive, as Rokku rushed in with a flurry of punches. Ducking and weaving, Yoshiko avoided the blows by scant inches and dropped low, sweeping Rokku's legs out from under him. Rokku hit the sand hard, rolling up onto his shoulders just in time to avoid a vicious stomp from Yoshiko. He continued the roll, coming up in a crouch and springing into a spear, catching the Silken Crane in her midsection, and driving her several feet back. Yoshiko struggled to catch her breath as Rokku launched another flurry of punches, but the Steel Lion kept her on the defensive with his relentless assault. As Yoshiko blocked a snap jab, Rokku leapt into a backflip kick, catching her chin with the tip of his foot. Yoshiko staggered backwards, and swung her arms up as Rokku hurled himself into the Emperor's Right Hand. Steam rolled off the exposed skin of the two friends and sparring partners, mixing with their breath in the frigid night air. A chill wind whipped through the desert, biting into them bone-deep. And Rokku's fist stopped millimeters from Yoshiko's face, barely caught between her arms. For a moment, the two of them just stood there, their breath coming in ragged gasps, blood pounding in their ears, a self-made mist swirling around them. Their campfire, burning strong and bright, illuminated their smiles as they stepped back and bowed to each other. Yoshiko and Rokku began to stretch, loosening their muscles for another bout. It took more effort to keep the blood flowing and the body limber in the desert's cold temperatures -- which was exactly why Rokku had chosen to spar here and now. If he could move and fight at his full potency under these conditions, then fighting in a Global Wrestling ring would be much easier in comparison. It was an edge he'd need, fighting against Soma, the rest of TUE, and the Souls of the Black Lotus. So many enemies in such a short time? Rokku wondered if he had always been this unpopular. He didn't wonder for long though, as the sound of applause echoed through the camp. Appearing from the shadows as if he'd never been there was a man in a fine black suit. Long blonde hair cascaded down his shoulders, and his green eyes twinkled mischievously as he applauded. 'Wow,' said the stranger, 'that, that was just great. It's good to see my genes being put to good use.' Yoshiko stood stone still, her mind reeling. Rokku had told her what his mother had seen, but she hadn't really been able to believe it. But there was no doubting it now. The hair, the squared jaw, the smile! So many similarities, this had to be? 'Hello James,' said Rokku, his tone colder than the night air. The man smiled broadly, his fangs glistening in the moonlight. 'Please, son,' he said, 'call me Father.' Rokku scowled. 'I have no father,' said the Steel Lion, 'you're a corpse who hasn't realized he's dead yet -- and nothing more.' James feigned a hurt expression. 'Son, you wound me! That's no way to talk to your Father, when he's come to see you before you shuffle off the mortal coil!' 'Is that a threat?' Rokku shifted into a relaxed fighting stance, preparing to react at any moment. 'No, no,' said the vampire, 'it's just fact. Master Soma won't leave more than scraps of you, son. Probably not even a bite for me -- which is hardly fair, since I helped make you. Still, the Master's will is law.' 'You had nothing to do with making me,' growled Rokku, his hands squeezing into fists. ' I was made by my mother's love and my own hard work. Any connection I have to YOU is just an accident of birth.' James clucked his tongue. 'I try to be nice?' he sighed, shaking his head sadly. 'I just wanted to bond with you a bit before Soma killed you. You know, do the father-son thing? Maybe take in a ball game -- a night game of course -- go fishing, have 'the talk'. Though,' he looked over at Yoshiko, and she felt her skin crawl under his gaze, 'judging by the company you're keeping, I think it's a little late for that.' 'Stay away from her, monster!' spat Rokku, his muscles tensing. 'For your own good,' added Yoshiko, falling into a Mizu-Do stance. James chuckled. 'Well, isn't she just a firecracker! Reminds me of your mother, back when I met her. We've got the same taste in women, son -- just more proof that I'm as deep inside you as I can get -- deep as I ever was in your mother, if not further.' At that moment, something inside Akodo Rokku broke. With a primal scream, he hurled himself at the thing that was once his father. His fist collided with the vampire's jaw, and James went flying, bouncing and skidding along the sand. 'You don't EVER talk about her!' shouted Rokku. The Steel Lion leapt onto James' chest and started laying into him with his fists. Suddenly, Rokku found himself hurtling through the air and slamming into the sand, after a powerful shove from the vampire sent him flying. As Rokku got back to his feet, he heard his father laughing. 'I was really, really hoping you would do that,' said James. Slowly, the vampire rose to his feet. He peeled off his suit-coat, and cracked his neck. 'Soma said I could have a part of you if you attacked me first,' continued James, 'but I really don't see why I shouldn't take all of you. And maybe have a little fun with your lady friend when I'm done, eh son?' Yoshiko started towards James, but Rokku held an arm across her chest. 'No, Yoshiko,' said the Steel Lion, 'He. Belongs. To me.' 'Fine,' said Yoshiko, stepping back, 'but you'd better kick his ass.' Slowly, father and son circled each other, Rokku pacing like a caged animal, James moving with a cool, inhuman grace. For a moment, the only sounds were the beating of two hearts and the crackling of the fire. Then it began. They rushed each other, the sand crunching under their feet as the gap between them shrank. It was James who made the first attack, launching a spinning heel kick which his son barely ducked under. Rokku immediately counterattacked with a straight jab that barely missed his father's face. Cackling, James grabbed Rokku's extended arm, flipping him to the ground and driving his heel into his son's ribs. 'This can't be all you've got,' said James, as he slammed his heel into Rokku's side again, drawing a sickening cough from the Steel Lion. 'Really, if I knew you were going to be this weak, I would have killed you before you clawed your way out of your mother's womb!' With a sickening laugh, James thrust down with his heel again. Unfortunately for the vampire, Rokku's hand lashed out, catching the offending foot. With a roar, Rokku clasped both hands around his father's ankle, and jerked hard, slamming the vampire into the ground. Rolling onto his shoulders, Rokku kipped up, and wiped blood away from his lips. 'That better for you?' asked the Steel Lion. 'Oh yes,' said the vampire, as he rose again, 'much.' Immediately, the two of them launched at each other again, fists and feet flying. Yoshiko watched in awe, not just of the inhuman speed and prowess of the vampire, but of Rokku as well. She'd never seen him so singly focused on a purpose as he was right now, not in hundreds of matches in rings all over Japan. She knew, if he could hold on to whatever he had now, nothing would stop him -- not even a demon like Soma. Now, if he could just stick to the plan? Rokku and James rained blows on each other, hardly even bothering to block or dodge any more -- they were each intent on one thing only: Victory. Seeking to finish things, James lashed out with a wild roundhouse punch, clipping Rokku in the jaw. The Steel Lion was thrown off his feet by the impact, and landed hard, his head bouncing off the sand. 'You just don't get it, son!' growled James, the beast within him roaring for blood. 'You can never beat us! You're only human!' With a roar, James flung himself into the air, raising his fist to slam it through his son's skull. Suddenly, Rokku's eyes snapped back into sharp focus. As his father came down, Rokku rolled up onto his shoulders and into a back handspring -- and as he did so his fingers wrapped around something familiar hidden in the sand. James' fist slammed into the ground, his arm burying itself up to the elbow. Howling, he tore his arm from the earth, sending up a cloud of sand. It was through that cloud of sand that Rokku lunged, driving four feet of steel into his father's belly. A tiny croak escaped James' lips, and Rokku jerked the sword across the vampire's gut, then tore it free and spun, slashing open James' throat. Blood pouring freely from his wounds, James staggered back as Rokku advanced on him. 'You didn't think we were just out here for our health, did you 'dad'?' asked the Steel Lion. 'I've made a lot of enemies since I came to the States -- so 'Shiko thought it might be a good idea if we set a trap for them. The two of us, in the middle of nowhere, no witnesses, no chance of calling for help. I can't believe someone actually fell for it, it's so obvious. We hid all kinds of weapons and traps in the sand -- all I had to do was maneuver you towards one and take advantage of the surprise.' 'I was really expecting the Souls though,' said Yoshiko, 'I guess maybe they saw through it. They are professional killers and all.' 'I'm glad it was you, though,' said Rokku, kicking James in the face. 'Soma thought he could get to me through you, 'dad'. Well I'm not the lost little boy he thinks I am. I got over not having a dad real fast, because I had just about the best damned mother you could ever ask for. Why don't you go tell him that. And tell him that if he ever tries to manipulate me through my family again, I'll kill him. I guarantee you, I will find a way. Now get the hell out of here before I decide to practice by killing you, you son of a bitch.' His face a mix of pain, fear, and hatred, James backed into the shadows, vanishing as he'd appeared. 'Well,' said Yoshiko, 'that was fun. What now, Lion?' 'Now,' said Rokku, 'we're going to clean everything up and get out of here before my adrenaline rush wears off. I'm starting to feel kind of like I just went ten rounds with a gorilla on speed.' Laughing, Yoshiko began to dig up everything they'd buried, as Rokku poured sand on the fire. A few minutes later they had everything in their packs and were ready to go. 'Brrr,' said Yoshiko, shivering. 'Now that the excitement's over, I'm starting to really notice how cold it is here.' 'Well, we'd better warm up then,' said Rokku, smiling winningly at her. 'My thoughts exactly,' said Yoshiko, stepping closer to him. 'Alrighty then,' said Rokku, looking into Yoshiko's eyes. 'It's three miles to the car. Last one there buys gas!' 'What?!' shouted Yoshiko, but Rokku had already shot off, pushing his bruised and battered body as fast as he could. Sighing and shaking her head in exasperation, the Silken Crane shot off after him, and they both vanished into the night.
