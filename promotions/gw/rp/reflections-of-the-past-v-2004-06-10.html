Reflections of the past: V

10 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 10th, 2004. 'Mmmm, yes…that's it Angelina…' I said as famed Hollywood starlet, Angelina Jolie lovingly rode me, her nude body on top of mine. 'Call me Laura, baby!' She giggled as Little Miss Tomb Raider stared down at me with her beautiful eyes. Reaching up I locked my hands onto her perfect breasts and closed my eyes in great pleasure… …And then the phone rang. 'What the..?' I gasped as she suddenly disappeared and my eyes reopened. Looking around I realized that Angelina Jolie wasn't, nor had been in my apartment, and in fact I had been dreaming. 'Oh no, whoever woke me up from this dream is going to…' I ended my sentence abruptly before I could spit out the remainder of my idle threat. The phone rang for a second time as I angrily blinked sleep away from my bluish gray eyes and rubbed my sockets furiously with my fists. The phone rang for a third time as I kicked the covers off of my nude body grumbling all the way. Rolling over I fell out of bed and onto the gray carpet cursing loudly as I went over in the rustling sheets thrashing about wildly. 'Mother fucker!' I snarled angrily, now wide awake my body surging with rage. The phone rang for a fourth time and stretching forth my right arm I snatched the cordless phone from the nightstand next to my bed and pulled it down to my position on the floor. 'What? What? What the hell do you want?' I screamed into the receiver before the caller could even get in a word. 'Do you realize what time it is?' I spat out not actually knowing what time it was myself. 'Actually…it's eleven twenty three.' The voice replied. With an audible groan I responded, my anger dissipating. 'I'm sorry…you know I'm not a morning person.' I said in apology since I had immediately identified the voice on the other end of the phone as the man that had trained me in the art of Lucha Libre, the internationally famed, Jose Guverza. 'And you call this morning?' He asked laughingly. 'Hey it's morning for me!' I sleepily replied as the left side of my face rested on the soft carpet. 'Perhaps that's because you party too much?' He asked sincerely. 'I'm the World Champion…that's my job.' I said defensively. 'Oh sure, how do you go out partying? Do you hitch hike?' He pondered in jest throwing the fact that my car had been stolen right back in my face. 'Hey man, that hurts, you know how much I loved that car.' I groaned, already missing my treasured nineteen ninety-seven Chevy Camaro. Just talking about it brought forth recollections of the great memories that I had made in that car. I missed everything about it. The soft leather interior…the black stripe painted along its gray side…the Superman steering wheel cover I had lovingly adorned it with…even the cheap air freshener hanging from the rear view mirror, I missed it all. 'Can you believe those fags stole my car?' I queried with a soft sigh. 'It's Wrestling…they'll do anything to get under your skin, little buddy. It's all about that mental edge. In Wrestling…you have no friends when the bell rings.' He said with a laugh. 'I know…I know…' I sadly grumbled. 'You had better know, I taught you that.' He stated thoughtfully. 'In my first match nonetheless…' I said as my memory flashed back to days gone by. ---- July 16th, 2000. 'Nervous?' Asked my instructor Tommy Rock as I was spread across the floor in the splits position. 'Not really.' I lied as I slowly forced myself up from the split position. 'My first match I was a bundle of nerves.' Tommy admitted with a smile. 'Oh?' Was all I could ask as I pretended to be interested yet in reality wanted to be left alone. 'I had butterflies in my stomach…my hands were shaking, and top it off I got my ass stomped by someone that was a hundred pounds heavier.' He said with a chuckle. I didn't respond verbally, instead I offered only a simple nod, in hopes that he would take the hint and go away. Of course…he didn't. 'Yeah, and then after I got my ass kicked I was thrown out of the ring and some jerk-off in the front row threw his beer on me, damn it!' Tommy laughed as he recalled these memories of old. 'Tommy!' I snapped at him, before pausing to regain my composure. 'Just let me go out there and do my thing.' 'Alright…' He grumbled as he scowled beneath his shaggy mustache and goatee. 'You've trained me long enough, I know the ins and outs of the business, I know how to Wrestle, I know how to be a professional. And anything you didn't teach me…Jose did when he trained me in Lucha Libre.' I said, reassuring him of my ability inside the squared circle. 'Right…what am I thinking?' He said with a laugh. 'Go out there and get 'em kid!' He said as he playfully slapped me on the shoulder. I needed no further encouragement as I burst out of the locker room door and into the large hallway that led to the entrance. This arena was nothing fancy…nor was the show. In fact I wasn't even sure if there would be a paycheck waiting for me after it was over. So here I was…in a tiny Mexican town about to make my first appearance Wrestling, and to make things all the worse…I had to face one of the men responsible for training me, and that was none other than Jose Guverza. As my footsteps bounced energetically across the floor I approached the end of the hallway, and suddenly came to a halt. Before me stood the one and only Jose Guverza. Many times I had seen him Wrestle and demonstrate the art of Lucha Libre yet it was an entirely different thing to stand before him. He stood there as the perfect picture of a warrior. His black boots led up to black tights and a bright silver shirt. His mask covered his face with holes cut for the eyes and mouth; the mask itself was a work of art. Half silver and half black with a crescent moon of the contrasting color on each side. He looked impressive…this didn't appear to be the man I had observed in class as he taught me the art, but rather the man that was fearless in the ring. Suddenly my saliva vanished as my mouth became dry. My eyes widened and my breath came in shallow gasps, I could taste the vomit at the back of my throat as butterflies invaded my stomach. 'Josh…good luck out there.' Jose said flashing a dazzling smile from beneath his mask. 'Thank you sir…and to you as well.' I said in a weak tone as I licked my lips with my dry tongue. 'Stay on your guard.' He warned as he spun on his heel. The strains of his age-old theme music hit the cheap speakers and he stepped through the black curtain before another word could be uttered, as I stood there in confusion. I wanted to yell at him…to ask what he meant yet I had no chance, he had already walked through the curtain and I would follow him in a matter of mere seconds…and with that realization I suddenly began to second guess myself. Had I picked the right ring attire? Looking down at the pair of short, royal blue half-tights that I wore my eyes surpassed these and went to my black Wrestling boots. I wore kneepads of the same color and athletic tape on my wrists to match, on my left elbow I wore a solitary elbow pad and my Superman tattoo proudly gleamed from my right biceps. I sighed softly, as I took in a deep breath. I played with my braided hair, praying that I looked perfect to appear in front of a crowd. This was it… Had I chosen the right entrance music? In only mere seconds 'Youth gone wild' was going to hit the speakers. How would the crowd react to this? How would they react to me? How would they react to the actions inside the ring? 'Breathe…just breathe.' I told myself as I rotated my shoulders, nervously loosening them up. Could it be that my parents were right? Was I destined for a life of failure? Was Wrestling a waste of my time? Would I be leaving the industry in shame after failure? I didn't know…but I had to find out, and in mere seconds I would. Bowing my head I conversed briefly with the almighty…very briefly. 'Father…' I began. 'Help me out there…be with me…' Before I could continue on any further I felt a hand on my shoulder. 'Let's go kid, your on!' The stage hand yelled at me as he let go of my shoulder. I was frozen…I couldn't move. My feet felt as if they had just been sealed to the floor or my boots had been replaced by concrete. Move…move…I willed myself as I lifted my left leg and took a shaky step forward. As 'Youth gone wild' hit the P.A. a surge of energy rushed through my body and I through the curtain open…as the world froze. It seemed that no one was moving as the crowd, an attendance of no more than two hundred in capacity, suddenly became human statues. Every eye focused on me as the small crowd gave me a mixed reaction, as the world seemed to flow again. I should be paralyzed with fear…yet something inside fuels my fury as I blitz the ring; my feet make no noise as the rubber soles bounce off of the concrete floor beneath my boots. '…be with me…' I repeat once more in a whispered prayer as I slide beneath the bottom rope just as the ring announcer is calling out the name 'Kid Flash'. ---- 'I'm surprised you still remember that match.' Jose said with a tone in his voice that told me he was smiling on his end of the conversation. 'Oh, how could I forget it?' I asked, in more of a statement than a question. 'It was an event to remember.' He admitted thoughtfully. 'Master versus student…for twenty two minutes of all out insanity.' I said cherishing the sweet memory of my first match. 'I'll never forget the look on your face…I thought you were going to puke the first time you tried an Asia moonsault.' He laughed. 'Well…the fact that you moved and I landed across the guardrail may have had something to do with that.' I said feeling my ribs that had long ago healed from that. 'You know…I'm not sure which is crazier, the fact that you walked away with bruised ribs after your first career match…or the fact that there were Japanese scouts in attendance and they noticed you. Then to top it off they thought highly enough of you to take you to Japan.' Jose mused. 'And if it wasn't for Japan…I wouldn't be where I am today.' I admitted hesitantly. 'But that match was something special…' Jose said as his sentence trailed off thoughtfully. ---- The battle had raged on for what seemed like hours, it had been an all out war and my ribs were killing me, little did I know, what I thought was hours was a mere twenty two minutes and four seconds. I knew that this battle of master versus student was something special…and as I came off of the ropes those thoughts came to and end and were replaced by stars flying around my head as I became the recipient of a spinning heel kick. …That really does hurt. My body hit the canvas…and then the body of Jose Guverza was launched from the top rope onto mine with a 450 splash…my eyes closed. 'Are you okay, kid?' Jose asked playfully slapping my face. In the background I could hear his music playing…I had lost. 'What happened?' I muttered as I returned to the land of the conscious. 'You just showed the World of Wrestling their next hero…that's what happened.' He said as he looked down at me with a smile under his mask. Reaching down he pulled me to my feet and I staggered about before regaining my balance, my bluish gray eyes focused on his face as a strange sound hit my ears… Flash! Flash! Flash! The fans were chanting my name… Jose smiled broadly at me and shook my hand before raising it high into the air. My eyes gazed about the arena and one thought inhabited my mind… …if only my parents could see me now.
