Demon Days And Samurai Hats

22 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/22/05 'Mikey?' the teenage girl began, as her older cousin prepared to disappear into his bedroom. Michael slowly turned around to face her, to which she smiled weakly. 'She'll be okay. We'll always have tomorrow to look for her.' Michael would have normally been comforted by the girl's optimism, but it was too late. 'We'll always have tomorrow' had been Mindy's motto to her cousin Michael ever since the day he found out why his wife was missing in the first place. Almost two weeks later, they still had no direction of where to look. 'Yeah...' Michael sighed in defeat, before he quietly closed his bedroom door behind him. Mindy stared at the door in silence, as if maybe Michael would come out with a smile on his face and that everything was really alright. Too bad that would never happen. 'Good night!' Mindy yelled, in hope for an answer. She didn't get one. Well, at least she could try talking to Markus again. Being the natural sympathizer that she was, and since Jaden was gone, Michael gave in to letting Markus rest in the home until he got better. Markus hadn't been doing much; he could talk and everything, but he simply stayed in bed most of the day, only getting up occasionally for the bathroom or a snack. That led to Mindy's pleasant surprise that she got from the sight of Markus sitting up over the edge of his bed. He sure did seem to be feeling much better compared to the past couple of days. 'You're not asleep yet, eh?' Mindy questioned as she pulled up a seat in the form of a giant pink bean bag. 'You should be resting. Everything alright?' Markus nodded quickly, forcing a small smile onto his face. 'Yeh, child, everything's fine now.' Mindy flashed a smile herself. 'You look horrible.' Markus frowned, pushing his black hair away from his face. 'Really?' Mindy giggled, pointing to an object at Markus' side. 'Without your hat, I mean.' That samurai hat Markus carried with him quite possibly meant as much to him as having a smoke. He had it for a long time, and he usually never took it off- except to sleep and shower, anyway. Markus looked at it and chuckled, throwing it on over his head. 'Yeh true... I loves me nothin' more than my cool lil' hat.' Mindy's giggle grew louder. It was at that moment that she remembered exactly why she fell in love with Markus Riley. 'You're always cute when you talk like that.' Markus tilted his head in confusion. 'Talk like what?' 'Heehee, nevermind...' Mindy looked away from Markus for a brief moment- even though the man was in her house for practically two weeks, they didn't speak all that much before that very moment. Mindy hadn't even thought of bringing up the situation that caused the end of their relationship. There were so many things going on, and she had to defend her Television Title yet again the next night against Elia Smith, so she debated whether mentioning it would be the better thing to do or not. 'Maybe I should start with a non-offensive question. Yeah...' 'Where did you get that samurai hat, anyway?' Mindy inquired. 'That thing is damn sexy. I want one!' Markus laughed lightly, lowering his head so he looked down at the sky blue carpeted floor. 'Eh heh... where I got my samurai hat, eh?' Mindy nodded. 'Heh, it's weird, no one ever really asked me that before.' 'Why not?' Markus shrugged. 'I dunno... I guess it's just one of those things that people wonder about, but never really bother to ask cause they think that the answer they have in their heads is much better than hearing the truth, or something. Like if people tend to think that I just bought it at some flea market although they'd rather hear about an adventure or summin' like that. I suppose it's not really a big story about how I got it, but people who used to be friends of mine that saw it, well they thought it was freakin' hilarious.' Pause. Mindy was waiting for Markus to continue, but he did not. 'So?' she blinked. 'So... what?' 'What happened, silly?' Mindy reached over and playfully poked Markus in the ribs, to which he grabbed at them in slight pain. 'Oops!' Mindy squeaked quickly out of concern, to which Markus waved a hand in motion for her not to worry. 'I'm okay,' he insisted. 'But anyway.. what happened...' Markus took a deep breath, and let it out in a long, drawn out sigh. 'When I used to live in Louisianna, me and my Nawleans buddies saw this old dude walking down the street, and he had this big ass hat.' Markus pointed to his head. 'This big samurai hat you see on my head right now.' Mindy scratched her head. 'Ooh. Did he give it to you?' 'Nah,' Markus shook his head. 'I kicked him in the knee. Yeah.' Mindy blinked. '...You kicked an old man in the knee and took his hat?' Both Markus and Mindy stared at eachother, both not knowing what to say. Markus didn't even feel like arguing about it, since he had enough of a tough time thinking of an excuse for his friends as to why he did it. 'Well sheesh, I didn't say I was going to be telling you a fairy tale or a reimagining of The Karate Kid or something! That's all that happened!' Mindy sank into her bean bag chair, blowing at her strands of pink hair in front of her face. 'I don't even remember why I asked you that.' Markus grinned at the girl; it was the first time in months since he'd last been unafraid to show happiness in front of young Mindy. 'Well, it got us talking again. That's all that matters to me... it's the only reason I came down here, actually.' Well that sure surprised her. Mindy couldn't even think of a way to respond to that. 'I'm sorry about what I said that time on the phone,' he continued, 'you know, about that rich kid who was bugging you on television. I just get so frustrated...' Mindy frowned. 'Frustrated?' 'I didn't think you'd ever give me a chance to talk to you again, is all...' 'Oh...' Mindy stammered, not knowing what else to say. Markus hit it right on the head; if it wasn't for her cousin threatening to tear his head off, Mindy probably never would have given him the time of day ever again. How could she have been asked to do that after he put her in the hospital- for reacting to him cheating on her, no less? She couldn't have, and Markus had to have known that. Still... the fact that he remained persistent in his wishes to speak with her, Mindy couldn't simply disregard that. 'I fucked up big time, Mindy, I know.. shit, I knew I was in for somethin' when I brought that broad back to the house. I just...' Mindy narrowed her eyes in curiousity. 'Just what?' Markus hesitated in thought, seemingly trying to sort out his story- which he did before he sighed loudly. 'Too much to talk about, so I'll just some it up. That chick was friends with me from when I lived in New Orleans. When she saw me wrestling while you managed me, she managed to find me and we just hung out for a little bit. I was excited to see her, you know, 'cause of how long we knew eachother and all... and then she gave me this little speech. And that speech more or less said, 'I didn't know how much I truly loved you until you were gone'. At that point she was in my house, and it was just like... I knew I should have threw her out, but I couldn't. I really just couldn't do that.' Markus was mumbling to the floor by the time he finished speaking, but upon hearing no immediate reaction from Mindy, he swiftly attempted to fill the silence. 'I'm sorry. I'm sorry, but it's the truth.' Mindy bit her bottom lip. She had absolutely no idea what to say. Even if there was no excuse for him cheating on her- or at least attempting to- at least he told her the truth. And after the scare Michael gave him a couple weeks ago, Mindy seriously doubted that Markus had the guts to lie about such a thing. And even thought Markus hated bubbly women, Mindy just gave off that harmless feeling- like she could never hurt him no matter what. Maybe he didn't deserve someone as sweet as Mindy, but he was damn sure going to try and make things right- at least have one more chance; one he would use to make sure that he didn't mess up. However, once again, with everything that was going on, Mindy did not feel like she was ready to decide on something so serious just yet. 'Maybe... maybe it's best that I think about it for awhile. I don't want to jump into making a decision right now, I mean.. it's not you, but I gotta worry about Michael, too, and I gotta face Elia Smith tomorrow night, and I'm just really worried.' Markus nodded slowly, and Mindy could tell how upset he was from the lack of a definitive answer. Frowning, Mindy leaned forward and gave Markus a gentle hug. 'Don't worry hun,' she said warmly, 'at least right now, you'll have nothing less than my friendship.' Markus pulled away from Mindy slightly, just enough to look into her innocent green eyes. 'And it's that compassion alone that made you worth crashing my car into a telephone pole.' Mindy giggled and kissed Markus on the cheek, before she jokingly pushed him backwards onto the bed. 'Save the flattery for tomorrow, hunny bunny. You still need to get some rest.' Markus chuckled and lied back, watching Mindy head for the door. 'What? Not gonna sleep here with me?' he teased. 'I'll think about it tomorrow,' Mindy winked. 'Promise.' 'Heh... g'night, Mindy.' 'Sweet dreams.' Mindy quietly stepped out of the room and eased the door shut behind her, before she leaned back against it and sighed dreamily. 'That boy sure knows how to make a girl feel all happy on the inside.'
