Porcelain Hearts

7 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-7-05 The plane ride home for the Mercer cousins was not spent in sorrow, but relief. Clash of the Titans was over and done with, which meant that Michael and Mindy no longer had to spend their time in Italy for the moment. Ergo, Mindy no longer had to worry about the possibility that her father would find her again. She felt so much better after that confrontation with him the night before the Pay Per View, that the result of the Global Rumble hadn't upset her at all. She had one title already anyway, and one title was good enough for her. Hell, she couldn't even fathom how hectic her life could get with two titles. However, the only thing on her mind as far as her title was concerned, was... 'Geez, this thing is so freakin' heavy!' Michael glanced over at his little cousin, who seemed to be feeling in slightly lighter spirits from the night before, which was always a good thing. Sure, Mindy could get quite annoying with her cheery attitude, and sure she turned some heads with her hyperactive antics, but at the same time, it was better than having an anti-social, manic depressive brat for a cousin. And come to think of it, Mindy did have her cool moments... 'Flight attendant lady? Are you guys still serving food? 'Cause I'm like, really really hungry, I need fooooooood.' But at that moment, on that plane, right next to Michael's seat, was not one of those cool moments. Michael groaned and tried to get some kind of sleep- at least a catnap- but with one of the biggest mouths on the plane sitting next to him, so much as winking was next to impossible. At least when his cousin Justin was around, he could manage to rest. But now, it was like taking care of his daughter when she was a toddler all over again. 'God I wish I could have some peace and quiet,' Michael grumbled as he wriggled around to get comfortable in his seat. Mindy overheard his request in between her inquiries about food, and pouted in sadness. 'Would you want me to go find somewhere else to sit, Mikey?' she asked, bottom lip stuck out as if she were a mere child. Michael quickly shook his head and frowned. As much as he wanted her to shut up, he couldn't exactly be vocal about it; something actually made him give a damn if he hurt his cousin's feelings. 'Nah, I guess I'm just anxious to get home and see the family, is all.' 'Ooh,' Mindy smiled, suddenly feeling better once again. 'I'm sorry, Mikey, I'll try and tone down my voice a teensy weensy bit.' 'Okily dokily,' Michael answered, before he flopped over onto one side and tried once again to enter dreamland, body facing the window next to him. The clouds had made way for the night sky, leaving nothing in view but clear darkness. It was a beautiful sight, but the real sight Michael wanted to see was the sight of Jaden and Gabriella when he got home. It was early that next morning when they got back to the house. After landing at such a late hour, the duo simply decided to grab a hotel room and wait until morning before they finished their trip back home. Whether that was the reason why Jaden and Gabriella weren't home when they arrived, Michael did not know. 'Hey, sweethearts, I'm home.' Michael glanced around the empty living room as he dropped his duffel bag full of wrestling gear on the couch at his side. Mindy stood in the front doorway, still dragging her belongings into the house when Michael began to show a tinge of nervousness on his face. Never once was Jaden gone at this hour, and Gabi was supposed to be on spring break from school. 'Jaden?' Michael muttered slowly as he searched throughout the house. When Mindy took notice, Michael had just stepped out of her view and into he and his wife's bedroom; nothing doing there, either. 'Where the hell did they go?' Michael questioned himself, as Mindy began to piece together what Michael was doing. She placed her bags next to Michael's on the living room couch along with her title belt, and decided to do some searching of her own. 'Gabi?' she called, throwing open Gabriella's bedroom door. Strewn about toys and school books, but no Gabi. 'Jaden?' she exclaimed next, as he peeked over Michael's shoulder and into his bedroom- again, nothing. 'Sheesh, where could they be?' Shrugging, Mindy walked into the kitchen and looked inside of the refridgerator. 'Hellooooo?' As Mindy scampered about the house desperately in search of Michael's wife and daughter, Michael just stood in his bedroom doorway in disbelief. He knew what happened, but he just didn't want to admit it. 'She took her,' Michael grumbled to himself. 'She just.. went and took her...' 'Huh?' Mindy looked over at Michael down the hall, able to hear her cousin's hoarse whispers. 'Who took what?' 'Jaden..' Michael answered, not caring that Mindy had heard what he was saying, 'she took Gabi and left... didn't bother taking her things, just up and left...' Mindy stood there in the hallway, puzzled. She slowly brought one of her tiny hands to her chin and rubbed at it in thought. 'Why would she do that, Mikey?' Michael knew the answer all too well, however again, he simply refused to admit it. All he could say, was... 'Find them.... yeah, I'm going to go find them.' 'Uh oh...' Mindy immediately thought to herself. 'Uhh Mikey, are you sure that's a good idea? I mean how are you going to find them? They left their things behind and stuffs, so surely they would come back soon!' 'No,' Michael moaned in disapproval, 'Jaden's not like that, she won't let that shit stop her from leaving...' once again, Michael found himself on the recieving end of what could either be construed as another bad joke, or another horrid reality check. Too bad it was blatantly obvious which category the current situation fell under. 'Goddammit, I always have to fuck things up...' Michael grabbed at his hair, as if ready to pull every strand out by the roots. 'I'm such an idiot.. why the hell did I come back to this? Why can't I just be a normal person and live a normal life?' Mindy wanted to step forward and console her older cousin, but she was too afraid to. Maybe it was best she didn't get near him. 'Michael... just calm down.. we all have problems in our lives and we all make mistakes, but they're mistakes you can fix! Just relax.. sit down, have some sugar.. smile... and sing a happy song!' 'Maybe she's right. Maybe I should just sit back and try to worry about putting things in perspective before jumping to conclusions again. Maybe my wife and daughter will be back soon after all. Maybe I should just sit down, have some sugar, smile, and sing a happy song...' After Michael let those possible options sink into his mind, there really only was one alternative. 'I'll come back when I find Jaden and Gabi.' Without so much as unpacking his things, Michael clutched his car keys and marched off to the front door; not a damn thing was going to stop him from doing anything else, either. That was Michael, alright. 'Buh, but...' Mindy tried to think of something- anything- to say that would sway Michael's decision. Only one thing clicked in her mind, and she said it without thinking. 'I can go look for them for you!' Michael glared at his cousin, as if he didn't believe she had the audacity to make such an offer. 'That's not good enough.. it's my wife, and my child. I'm going to find them myself.' Mindy looked at Michael in wide-eyed despair. If only she could think of a way that Michael would let her help... but unfortunately, she couldn't. 'Michael!' the teenage girl called out to her cousin, but to no avail. Michael was beyond the point of reason once again, even if he sort of did have a right to be stubborn. 'Still... I don't want the same thing to happen like before when he disappeared like this...' 'Michael!' she screamed again, but all Mindy got in return was to hear the sound of the front door slamming violently. 'Michael...' the girl whispered. Mindy ambled over to the couch and crashed down onto it, knocking over her bags and Michael's in the process. 'Why won't you let me help you?' she questioned aloud to herself, snapping her eyes shut so tears could not overwhelm her. 'What happened in the past that you're not telling me?' Mindy's eyes blinked open feverishly, only to find herself staring into her own reflection; one that shined off of her prestigious Television Championship. 'This should have been what brought the family to one, not a group of different people who can't agree on anything... so why do I still feel so miserable and useless?' After thirty minutes of looking downward at herself and hear teary-eyed complexion, Mindy could no longer bear to look at how emotional she had gotten. She forced herself to her feet and made her way upstairs with the help of the wooden railing on her right. Mindy went right for the bathroom straight down the hall and leaned over the sink as if she were ready to puke at any moment. 'I can be so happy out in the ring with the fans.. when I get to wrestle, and actually be free. No worries about anything.. I can be myself, and no one can tell me that I can't. And then when faced with life outside of GW, I want to cringe because it always makes me sad. God, why the fuck am I still thinking this way? My dad's practically out of my life, why am I still sad?!' If only Mindy had someone to look to for guidance. And for once, she wished the someone she could go to wasn't named Michael. She had depended on him for advice, but she was completely lost without him. 'I'm so afraid to keep feeling this way by myself.. I don't want to do something I'm going to regret...' As soon as that thought popped into her head, Mindy tried as hard as she could to shake it off. 'No.. I'm not going to do anything stupid. I can't do that.. not now. I've got to start learning to be happy on my own. Yes, that's exactly it.' Slowly, Mindy took her hands from the sides of the sink and made her way back downstairs. The road to happiness was a long way away, but maybe a soda and some pocky would provide for a good start. After all, sugar always was Mindy's friend.
