Session 3... or is it?(PPV2)

1 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/1/03 'Ice, sweetie, you're going to be late for your session if you don't get up.' Lovingly Star caresses Ice's chest, as she tries to wake him from his deep sleep. A loud grunt bellows from Ice's mouth as he slowly begins to stir beneath the covers. His arms extend from each side of his body, and he yawns before blinking a few times, and finally opening his eyes. Slowly he pushes himself up a little and props his upper body up against the headboard of the bed. A smile crosses his face as Ice looks over at Star sitting next to him on the left edge of the bed. Bashfully she smiles back at Ice. She motions over towards the digital clock sitting on their wooden night stand. The clock reads 11:34, and Ice nods in response. 'I know, I know. I'm going to call and tell them I'm going to be a little late. Don't worry alright.' Star doesn't respond to Ice she just smiles and stands up from her seated position on the bed. As she stands she turns to walk away, but is suddenly stopped as Ice grabs her right hand and pulls her back down onto the bed, and rolls her on her back. She yelps, and begins to laugh as Ice attacks her with a barrage of small kisses to the sides of her neck, and tickles her on the sides of her stomache. 'Stop..haha.. it...haha. I've got..hahahahahaha, food cookING! HAHA..hun..hahaha' Ice ignores her request and continues to tickle her. Star's breaths become shorter and shorter, and her face quickly begins to give off a shade of red. Ice stops his tickling torture and roughly grabs both of her wrists. Star's laughing stops and her breathing slowly begins to take its normal routine. Suddenly she is thrusted up, as Ice pulls her towards him. He releases her wrists from his grip, and gently slides his hands down the sides of her body, and wrapping them around her waist. Star places her hands on the top of Ice's head, and slowly slides them down the back of it, and begins to softly caress the back of his head and neck. With love and lust radiating from both their eyes Star and Ice stare at each other, without speaking a single word. Star finally breaks the silence. 'You better get going, or call and tell them you are going to be late.' A frown crawls across Ice's faces as he releases Star and slowly climbs out of bed. Ice scratches the top of his head, and allows his legs to get their balance, by just standing next to the bed for a moment. As he stands there in his black and white checkered boxer shorts, Star reaches across the bed and playfully slaps his ass. 'Don't act all pissy with me mister.' Ice turns and winks at her before walking over to the telephone. Ice takes a seat on the edge of the wooden stand that the telephone sits upon, and begins to dial. 'Hello?' 'Yeah, this is Ice from GW, and I'm scheduled for a session today at 1, well I'm going to be a little late, but I will be there.' 'Be late, for your session? Mr. Ice, you don't have a session today. Infact you no longer are scheduled for any sessions.' A confused expression engulfs Ice's faces as he listens to the words coming through his off white telephone. 'What do you mean? I was told to..' 'Yes I know Mr. Ice, but a man today in a suit gave us some legal documents, that notified us that you were no longer obligated to attend sessions here any longer.' Ice still confused looks over at Star. Star looks back at Ice and mouths 'what'. 'Can you tell me..' A knock is heard at the door, and Ice hangs the phone up. He hurrily walks over and opens the door. On the other side of the door is a bell boy from the hotel, in his black dress pants, red coat, and little red hat. The bell boy smiles widely at Ice. Ice tilts his head to the right and gives the bell boy an angered look. The smile quickly leaves the bell boy's face, and he reaches into his pocket and pulls out a black envelope. Ice looks down at the envelope, and snatches it from the bell boy's hands. Ice slams the door in the bell boy's face, and leans against the door. 'Hun, what is it?' Ice doesn't respond to Star's questioning, as he stares down at the black envelope and flips it around in his hands. Star begins to walk over towards Ice. Ice stops flipping the envelope in his hands, and begins to open it. Star leans against Ice's arm, and looks over the contents of the envelope along with Ice. It reads. 'Your mindset is just the way I feel it should be I've been watching you, and will be in contact soon Your gratitude for my actions is already accepted and appreciated I will see you soon From one MONSTER to another' Star looks up at Ice and gives him a confused, and quizzicle look. Ice snears and drops the envelope to the floor. Star grabs Ice's right arm, and stops him from walking away. Ice turns back to her, winks, and gives her an evil smile. Fade.
