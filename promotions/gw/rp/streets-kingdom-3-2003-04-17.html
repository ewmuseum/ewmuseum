Streets Kingdom 3

17 April 2003

Streets Kingdom 3 Quartz Global Wrestling Promo #112 Date: 4/17/03 Location: Street With every step he takes, his large feet crash down onto the pavement which had long been forgotten. Long since had this area seen any sign of maintenance as those who inhabited the area were seen as simply not worth it. Not worth the light of day, which they barely seem to exist in anyway. It was night time that there people not only existed, but played a large part in the city's lifestyle. It was these people who would cling together, unlike those other kinds who would find it far more easier to simply disappear at night time and become the ever present solo survivors that they were during the day. At the end of the working day, you were meant to become a different person. You had the ability to do as you wanted, or at least that is how this world used to be. A world without The Lair was a world that people dreamed of. A world were fear didn't rule their lives. A world were the all mighty dollar wasn't corrupted by those who had gained power legitimately or otherwise. No these people who go home, only to continue their work and make sure that the deadlines were met at all costs. Making sure that everything they did was exactly as how it was told to them. Freedom was a thing of the past. Living your life as you choose no longer played a part in this world of hatred and deadlines. Or at least didn't exist to those who had given into it. There were some that hadn't given in and nor would they ever. And it was only now that Quartz was beginning to understand why. The reason the others had given into the pressure of a world controlled through fear, was because they had something in which they feared to lose. That didn't exist here on the street. Were there is nothing to lose, how can you fear? Splash. His foot sunk deep into a puddle, which had long since been filled by the rains and never truely fixed. Who would expect such a hole to exist in a road were everything had to be perfect? Only those who belonged to the road. His wet foot, squelching with every step but he paid no attention. His eyes were no locked firmly on the group of people who gathered around the barrell fire as they would every night. These were the people who were most valuable to The Lair. These were the people who would never confirm to the way of the rule makers. For in there world, they made the only rules. The rules of the street. 'Hey buddy, you got a lite?' The voice sounded rough and yet soft at the same time. No louded than a whisper and yet loud enough for the intended target to hear. Quartz turns to see where it had come from and yet there was no one around. There was nothing but shadows here and the odd piece of paper rolling down the street like a piece of tumble weed in some poorly made Western movie. 'Over here man.' Again the voice sounded out and a figure emerged from the darkness beside a building. His hair matted, his far unshaved, his eyes were looking old and yet the face still had youth to it. With his size, Quartz wasn't afraid of a single person and therefore, saw no harm in given the guy a light. As he turns to talk towards the men, his black leather trench coat flies out behind him and his hands come out of his pockets. With every step that he takes towards the man he can see that the man is scanning him. His eyes moving up and down as the giant sized figure of Quartz gets closer. As Quartz pulls up a foot away from the man, his hand goes back into his pocket to retrieve the lighter and as he does, the man's hand flies forth with a knife clasped tightly in his palm. Quartz's free hand moves quickly, grabbing the incoming arm and twists it over, causing the knife to drop to the ground. Quartz's hand comes out of his pocket, but no lighter is held in his hand as it now slams into his throat and grips tightly, lifting the man up into the air and slamming him against the wall. Holding him up there, Quartz's eyes bulge with the hatred, which had instantly consumed him. In the shadows of these buildings, no one would miss this man and no one would see Quartz do a thing. But was death really needed. Was the life of someone in Quartz's hands really what was called for on this brisk night? No, but then again, this was Quartz. Confusion. Quartz shakes his head as the words that Tragic had spoken fill his mind and Quartz echos those words.'I will never be apart of this world.' Shaking his head again, Quartz's eyes focus on the ground below him.'And yet I feel something drawing me in. This is your world Tragic? This is the world and the people in which you seem to have become a apart. The people that you say i would never be able to become apart of. The man's face, although full of pain seems to feel with confusion more with every word that Quartz's speaks. Who is he talking to? Was he meant to respond to what this giant was saying?'Vincent, what makes you so different to me? What makes me so different to these people? What do I have left to live for?' Quartz's grip tightens around the man's throat as Quartz closes his eyes and memories take over. Memories that had no right intruding on his thought patterns and yet had found a place in him which he hated more than anything else in the world. He sat there on the phone in his massive house. A house which crime had paid for and had paided well. Did his wife know anything of this? Of course she didn't. Or at least that is what he thought and that is what he told her. He was a business man dealing with debt collecting and company takeovers. It was good enough to keep her from asking questions but she was quickly finding out the real Quartz. But for now, she was at her mother's house and Quartz sat on the phone, but not to her. 'She's pregnant.' The worlds seemed colse and lifeless now. He loved her but a son was something that now struck something new in his heart and mind. His son couldn't grow up the way that he had. His son would never become what Quartz had become, that was something Quartz would make sure of. 'Yes, I guess you're right. We are pregnant. I can not let him grow up in this world and yet I do not want to lie to him.' The voice down the other end of the phone was the one which had always helped him in times of need. Quartz was a man who's feelings were something that had been a space filled with void for a long long time and somethings he had no idea about. This is where she came into things. She spoke words which were wise and well thought. 'Tell them? I can't. She would leave me.' This time the voice down the other end of the phone was clear, 'Tell her Quartz. If she loves you, she will understand.' Such a satement was forein to him. Love? Did they? A sigh escapes his lungs as he pauses to think for a second and listens to her. 'OK Rose. I think that would be best. I'll be around in a few.' The phone lowered down onto the receiver and Quartz sat back, pushing his hand through his long black hair. He shook his head again trying to get the thoughts of the past out of his mind and then he looked up. The man was no longer struggling. No longer breathing. No longer living. Which seemed strange, as he was one of the few in the city who still did live. Who still held onto his own thoughts and ways and now he was dead to everything. 'This is a strange world Vincent. A world were your past will always catch up to you even though you don't want it. You may think myself and the rest of my clan and power hungry fools, but we are the ones with the control of everything that counts. What do you have control over Vincent? Your life?' Quartz lets his grip on the man's neck loosen and he slides down the concrete wall and lands in a heap on the ground. His head smashes against the pavement at Quartz's feet and Quartz bobs down beside.'This man thought he had control. Thought that his fear free life would save him from the corrupt world that we all live. But there is a difference Vincent. You still fear. And you fear the one thing that I do. Quartz stands up again and begins to walk through the street again towards the group around the fire, who warm their hands by the only heating that they will ever know. 'Vincent, you fear that I am apart of this world. A world to which you also belong. I have nothing to live for anymore Vincent. I have no wife, and more importantly, I have no son.' His huge figure ploughs through the street and the close he comes to the group, the more the warmth of the fire takes hold. His hand pulls a cigarette from his packet and places it in his mouth as he gains and finally upon arrival, he leans down and lights his cigarette from off the fire. The men look at the large man and nod to acknowledge him. The nod is returned and every one goes back to their conversations as if nothing had changed.
