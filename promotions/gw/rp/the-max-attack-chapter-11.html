The Max-Attack: Chapter 11

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today Max stops and lands on a really high skyscraper. Some people in the skyscraper start looking at Max like he is a weirdo. Max jumps again and flies a long way until he gets back to his hotel he was staying at before. Max lands behind the building so no one would see him flying. Max quickly runs from the back alley to the front of the hotel. Max walks into the hotel and sees one of the workers at the hotel run up at him. “Mr. Roswell, you have not been here for 3 days and still have your stuff in the room you have. You have to takes you stuff out of the room or pay 25$ for not staying here when you wanted to” tells the worker. “Ok, sorry that I wasn’t here. I guess I’ll pay it” says Roswell back to the worker. Roswell takes his wallet out of his back pocket and gives the lady a 20 and 5$ dollar bill. Roswell walks past the worker straight into a open elevator. Roswell pushes the 5th floor button and waits patiently for the door to open. Roswell walks out of the small elevator and down a long hallway to one of the many doors in the hall. Roswell takes out his wallet again and holds his room card in front of him. Roswell opes his hotel room and walks inside to lots of smoke in the room. Roswell walks over to the window and looks out it. Roswell sees the swimming pool and a lady smoking right by his door. The swimming instructors walk to the lady and tell her to stop the smoking or she gets kicked out of the poolside area. The lady takes her smoke and leaves the area to the surprise of the instructors. Roswell closes the curtains and turns a small lamp on, that only shines half of the room. Roswell takes his clothes off in the bathroom and goes into the shower. 5 MINUTES LATER Roswell steps out of the bathroom with a towel around his waist. Roswell opens the dresser and takes out new clothes and puts those on. Roswell turns off the small lamp and leaves the room again with his wallet in his back pocket. The scene cuts to the outside of the hotel where Roswell is standing into the sunlight. Roswell looks around the windy city before he starts walking down the hard sidewalk. Roswell walks past the same billboard as before that has Metallica on it. Roswell then remembers that the concert is tonight and he rushes back to his hotel for the tickets so he doesn’t have to get them after. 3 MINUTES LATER “Oh my lord. I am sweating like a pig already” says Roswell. Roswell walks to the stadium where the concert is being held and see that there is a short line to get in. Roswell looks behind the stadium and sees that there is a party going on before the concert. Roswell walks into the big lawn area where the party is being held. Roswell then walks to the gate and gives the man his ticket and walks into the stadium. “Everybody is in that party. There is no one here in the stadium” says Roswell. Roswell goes to the very front row of the stadium and sees all the equipment is set up for the concert. Roswell hops up onto the stage and looks around at all the cool stuff. “HEY! Get off the STAGE!” a voice comes from ahead of Roswell. Roswell quickly jumps off the stage not knowing where the voice came from. Roswell then spots a security guard on the stage setting it up. “Sorry about that” tells Roswell “Don’t do it again, or your kicked out of the stadium.” says the security guard. Roswell walks away and to the cafeteria type place and walks up to buy a drink. Roswell takes the drink and starts slurping it. Roswell walks back to the inside of the stadiium and sees that there are a lot of people inside it now. Roswell goes to his spot and Metallica comes onto the stage for the first song. SCENE FADES
