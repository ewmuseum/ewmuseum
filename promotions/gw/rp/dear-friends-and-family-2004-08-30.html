Dear Friends and Family...

30 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/30/04 'Dear Friends and Family,' 'It has been awhile since I have last emailed you all, so I thought I would take time out of my busy schedule and say hello, and touch base with my biggest fans, and best of friends.' McNasty begins to type on his Dell Laptop in his Dallas Hotel Room just a few nights before Whiplash in what will surely be one of the biggest nights in his young career in GW. Train v.s. Donny, for the Platinum Championship in a ring of steel match. Only a KO or knock out will win it, and both men have nerves and skulls of steel. 'Life has been great of late, me and Alexis are doing well and I think I am falling in love with her. She really is the light of my life. Everyday is that much sweeter with her in my life. I can't wait till you guys get to meet her in person.' Even after Train dumped Donny into the garbage truck, Donny was still smelling roses because of the little flower in his life. If it wasn't for her, Donny might have killed Train for what he did. But there is just something about Alexis that makes him keep his head straight. Some girls have that effect on some men, Alexis and Donny are no exception. 'I am quite happy with my time here in Global Wrestling so far. Bubba McCoy is one of the best people I ever worked for. He's one hell of a guy and he doesn't take shit from anyone if you haven't noticed. He's been pretty good about giving me some time off of late, to heal some bumps and bruises. After whiplash though, I will be returning to full throttle and giving the fans more of what they want to see, and thats me kicking some ass.' 'Speaking of things related to ass, what did you guys think of what I did to Train last week on Evolution. I thought it was a clever idea to fill his Navigator up with Shit. I know he never seen it coming. He claims I talk shit, well I proved to him one thing for sure, and that is when I talk shit, I back it up! I wonder if ever got his Navigator cleaned? He smells like shit anyways so it wouldn't be any different.' Donny is still smiling from what he did to Train last week on Evolution. It has to be one of the most disgusting things to have ever happened on Global Wrestling programming. Who knows what will happen next if these two want to take things further with the personal attacks... 'Before I wrap this up, I encourage all of you to watch Whiplash, me and Train duke it out for the Platinum Title in a ring of steel match, where the only win is by knockout. Should be an interesting night, especially when I knock out that piece of shit Train. Just think about the party that we'll have at the Drunken Leperchaun when I win the Platinum Title. When I win the title, we just might have to rename the bar, How does the 'Drunken Platinum Leperchaun' sound? Sounds pretty damn good to me...' 'Well got to go my dear people. I love and miss you all, watch Whiplash ok? If you need to get a hold of me my cell number is [detail withheld] or call Harv at the bar toll free [detail withheld]' 'God Bless and Take Care' 'Sincerley' Donny J...
