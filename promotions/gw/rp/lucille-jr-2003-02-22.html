Lucille Jr.

22 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/22 The door of the stretched Lincoln Navigator cracks at the seam between it and the rest of the vehicle, allowing rays of light to slide through the small opening around the edge of the door like a blade of light. Slowly at first, then with blinding brilliance as the door is swung open to it’s full extent. This rapid surge of illumination inside the vehicle causes it’s passengers to wince and grit their teeth as they squint their eyes. After the initial discomfort of his eyes rapidly dilating in order to adjust to the influx of sunlight, Deacon Holmes springs from his seat and right out the door like a caged animal finally being set free. His eyes wide as those of a small child with a pocket full of quarters in a candy store, he quickly races away from the vehicle dragging his girlfriend, Rhaiza, behind him. The pitter patter of his quick feet repeatedly hitting the ground mixed with his overjoyed laughter fill the air as he flies across the pavement pulling Rhaiza behind him like a child does a wagon. They continue to advance toward a seemingly abandoned warehouse, but opt to take a narrow paved path extending to the right of the building which appears to be the only thing in the fenced in lot. Still having struggling to keep up rather than just being dragged along, Rhaiza releases Deacon’s hand and leaps onto his back, causing an outburst of laughter from both of them. Holmes continues down the pathway piggybacking Rhaiza, who enthusiastically slaps his ass while bucking, gripping his shoulder with her free hand, and yelling “Giddy up!” while giggling uncontrollably. The two gallop past the warehouse to find a small three bay garage attached to the back of it. The left most bay is occupied by the majority of a Viper, the rest of which is scattered around the back of the garage where several modifications appear to be in the process of being added. The one to the far right contains a Jaguar XJR-15 elevated on a set of hydraulic lifts, a winch holding an engine stands near it. The middle bay is bare, just as clean as can be, as though nothing had ever been in it. On the empty paved area in front of the garage stands a red, black, and white Kawasaki ZX-12R Ninja with shimmering silver accents outlining some of the designs on the bike. The most noticeable of which being a large black widow spider stretching across the side of the bike while sitting atop a web. In the background of the design red bolts of lightning scatter all around, reaching almost every end of the bike. Two men stand near it, one of whom holds a bucket filled with soapy water in one hand, and a large wet sponge in the other. The other man grasps a water hose with a gun attachment and proceeds to spray the bike with high pressure water, washing the thick white suds away and dragging them to the ground. Holmes slowly trots to a stop in sight of the machine, allowing Rhaiza to slide off his back and onto the ground. His jaw soon follows... “Lucille... junior?” he says to himself slowly, his mouth beginning to water. Taking notice of his presence, the man carrying the bucket signals for him to come over. Rhaiza cocks an eyebrow at Deacon Holmes, who is now salivating as he slowly lurches toward the magnificent super bike. “Oh, hey! You must be Deacon, I think you’ll be rather impressed with what we were able to do with the bike.” the man holding the hose says, looking up from the now sparkling clean motorcycle. Holmes begins to circle the bike, slowly taking note of each and every detail as the man begins to rattle them off. “We gave ya a performance engine management unit with optional real time mapping. Then we went ahead and added a custom thirteen hundred forty seven cc kit.” The man begins, causing Deacon to begin licking his lips like a famished lion lurking in the thatch just waiting to pounce on an antelope. Noticing the look on Holmes? face, the man continues. “Now that’s not just any old kit we gave ya there Mr. Holmes. Everything top of tha line, you’ve got a twelve-seventy cylinder block and pistons, a spacer plate, aluminum fan, a drilled basket, plates and steels, new springs, a billet hub, and some blockoff plates.” Deacon’s jaw drops just a little more with the mention of each performance upgrade, as he begins to run his hand along the smooth lines of the motorcycle. “Oh yeah, we also replaced the countershaft and rear sprockets, then we noticed the stock bearings weren’t exactly straight so we put in a new custom crank. Stroked, straightened, lightened, polished, ion nitrided, and balanced. Yep, this sumbitch is a bonafide monster now, knocked her up to a little over two hundred horsepower at the rear wheel and twenty six hundred cc’s. She one mean four stroke ya got there, she ran a seven flat in the quarter mile.” The man continues with a nod. At this point Deacon can no longer stand the anticipation and hops on the bike, then slaps the side of his gently, signaling for Rhaiza to join him. She quickly complies, as she’s obviously been excited by all the talk of power. Just as they’re prepared to ride off the man begins talking once again. “Now ya do understand what I mean when I say cc’s don’t ya boy?” Both Holmes and Rhaiza stare at the man, their expressions of excitement quickly fading to those of boredom and sadness as their ride is prematurely interrupted. “Now, tha cc stands for cubic-centimeters of displacement of the cylinder of the engine. The cylinder is nothing more than a large metal tube where the gas vapor enters and is ignited. The bigger the cylinder, the more vapor it can hold, and the more work that can be done. More work means higher speeds or the ability to climb steeper hills. So a twenty-six hundred cc engine, has a cylinder capable of holding the equivalent of twenty-six hundred cubes, each cube being one centimeter square.” The man says with yet another nod, before continuing on to say... “So in lamens terms... this sumbitch flies faster than a bat outta hell with gasoline drawz on! Only way you’re gettin” a ticket on this baby is if the cop’s ridin? a rocket! She does well over two hundred miles per hour, as a matter of fact, she’s MUCH FASTER than Lucille was Deac. But anyway, she’s ready to roll, just let me know if you have any problems outta her.” That said, the mechanic walks back into the garage, offering up a wave as Deacon immediately starts up the bike and guns the throttle, and thrusting the front tire into the air, speeds away taking the same path back to the front of the building. Once there they find John Zero sitting inside of his brand new, hand crafted, Aston Martin V12 Vanquish checking out all the bells and whistles. A faint grin forms on his face, which he quickly conceals once he notices he’s being watched. With the fading of Zero’s, one of mischief forms on the face of Deacon Holmes as he eases forward on the pavement, repeatedly revving the Ninja’s powerful engine repeatedly while staring down Zero inside his car. Deacon gives him a nod with a cocky smirk on his face before using his left leg as a pivot and slowly turning a circle with the bike’s rear tire rapidly spinning, leaving behind a line of black rubber on the pavement behind it. When the circle is complete a thick ring of dense white smoke slowly floats up from the ground before wafting away in the wind. The glare between Holmes and Zero intensifies and all Rhaiza can do is smile as she hangs on to Deacon, her head resting on his back. The corners of both Holmes and Zero’s mouths curl into cocky grins as they slit their eyes, each knowing full well what is about to transpire. Holmes whips his bike around to face the gate, it’s rear facing the front of John’s car, then begins to cause his tires to screech so loudly nearby birds quickly exit their trees, flocking away to unknown locations. A cloud of opaque white smoke begins to spew from the rear tire of Deacon’s motorcycle, partially obscuring him from Zero’s view as he rockets toward the gate. When the cloud dissipates John finds Holmes power sliding through the opening in the gate, stopping in the middle of the street still revving the engine. He looks back at Zero, who immediately cranks the car up, it’s powerful V12 engine rumbling to life before propelling the vehicle toward Holmes with it’s speed rapidly ascending. As the nose of the Aston Martin peeks through the gate Deacon flashes a million dollor smile before gunning the throttle. The bike’s front tire is thrust into the air like a whale breaching the surface of the ocean to catch a rare breath before once again being reintroduced to the ground and screeching off down the road leaving a trail of white hot smoking rubber in it’s wake. As the Ninja blazes down the road with the Vanquish powersliding out of the gate behind it one thing can be heard over the two roaring engines. “Catch me if you can!”
