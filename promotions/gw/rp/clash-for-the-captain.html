'Clash for the Captain'

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today I'd been with the company for all of two weeks... and already, I was being given the opportunity of a lifetime. I place emphasis on that 'opportunity of a lifetime' garb too. I mean, in all truth and honesty, it really isn't all that often that I find myself being given the chance to shoot up to number one in the rankings after just two weeks. Now, I know what everything is thinking when they see everything that I've ever had to say in my short time with GW. They know and understand that there is absolutely no way that a n00b can come out of the darkness and take something away from a number of people who have strived and cried to get into their current positions. I'm feeling the same groove at the moment too, being in a situation where all the odds are stacked against me. To a degree, it's really quite harsh because on the morale aspect of things, I'm expected to be nothing more than 'Cannon Fodder' for some lucky veteran. However, on the other side of things, this underestimation could prove to work in my favor. 'What?' I hear you cry. Having what could quite possibly be debated as the LOWEST expectation going into this contest, I'd say that staying in the ring for more than just fifteen seconds will amaze a lot of the people that'll be watching this event. If there truely is one thing that I have come to learn and understand in ALL of my time here, it's the fact that nobody gives a fuck about whether you have ten years of experience behind you or ten months. Things could work in my favor at the end of the day... but like everyone, I sincerely doubt it. This past week's victory had been an exciting affair and one that I'm not going to forget in a hurry. The contest, I'm proud to say, must have been a total of about one minute in length. Two people started to fight it out... then I came down, nailed a few moves on my unfortunate opponent and picked up a very easy win. I've no idea what Mr Idol would think about this stunning victory, although to be perfectly honest, at the moment, I really don't care. At this very moment in time, I'm only going to be allowed one focal point... ONE goal that must be attained if the opportunity if present. The people may have come to love me in time, but this requires all sense to be thrown out of the window. To a degree, it could be said that this kind of contest is going to be something that really works in my favour. I mean, let's imagine everyone being complete loserfied or something for the duration of the bout as I come across as being the 'No shit, risk-taking, ass-whooping bastard' that just happens to beat the piss out of each and every little gun that tries to snatch victory from the jaws of defeat. ... That'd be something special. Oh yeah, that would be something special. Scene: Rich Sherwin's very timely home... The picture begins to open in what would appear to be my own little home. For the first time since I'd started work with GW, I was allowed to come home and have a little fun, spend a little time doing the things that I could do when I was all unemployed and the sort. The decoration to my house wasn't fantastic, nor was it actually existent to be more than honest with you. Prior to leaving for GW, I'd taken everything from my house and left it with my sister about three hours away. Unfortunately, I didn't quite remember what I'd done with everything until I walked through the front door of my pad and dropped my travelling bag to the floor... 'Oh shit..' I said, my voice filled with disappointment. 'How the Hell did I forget that I'd taken everything to my sisters?!' I shouted to myself, angry at making such a pathetic mistake. Wearing my usual street attire, I walked into the shell of a home that I'd once lived in and hung my head low, a loud sigh expressed. '... of all the things that I had to forget, why did I have to forget that I'd left all the expensive stuff... as well as most of my own personal stuff at Jo's.' I said to myself, walking through the entrance hallway and into what was formerly known as my lounge. Aside from a stool and a cheap phone, the place looked like a white box with a few windows in the side. I laughed to myself at the level of blandness that existed and promised to change things on my next visit home. '... Ergh, what the Hell was I smoking when this colour scheme came around?' Despite the negative comment of my own colour selection, in defense, the place did look about three thousand times better with all of my furni in place. Looking around for a few moments, just to examine a few things within the household, the phone started to ring. I looked down to it in surprise, wondering who the Hell would have phoned me... but more so, who the Hell had actually acquired my phone number. Gingerly walking over, I stopped a few inches from the stool and picked up the phone reciever. 'Hello?' came my confused voice, echoing about the room itself. 'Mr Sherwin?' replied the voice, a very sullen and professional sound to it. I waited for a good few moments to respond, trying to establish what reason this gentleman could be phoning for. I stood and though about all the legal cases that I'd been involved in over time and any that were to be coming up in the near future. With all this silence on my end though, the individual across decided to try and catch my attention with another warm note. 'Dickhead? Are you there?' they called, a little irritation beginning to show in their voice now. I still didn't quite understand why I was being called but content with knowledge of there being no outstanding charges against me, I replied in a polite and formal tone, using language that would probably seem like common-speak to somebody with such a kind and gentle vocab. '... I'm sorry but the person you are trying to contact happens to think that you're a complete and utter moron. Better luck with your next attempt.' I responded, putting the phone down before the individual on the other end had a chance to respond to my aggro. I'd been in the door for little more than a few minutes and already I was wondering why I'd bothered to take this time off in the first place. '... Home for five minutes and I don't even know why I bothered going through with this. I guess I could phone Jo and ask if I could stay with her for a few days. I'm sure my little sister will be happy to see her older brother...' I said, my voice trailing off closer to the end of the sentence. I was quite confident that my sister would be happy to have her older brother come over for a few days but, I still wasn't sure if things were totally OK between the two of us. I didn't realize this at the time but she was one of the few people that really felt that I didn't need to get back into the ring. She thought that I had all the money that I needed to be happy and that I shouldn't risk myself anymore if I really didn't need to take such a risk. Nervously, I picked up the phone and began to dial in the number of my sisters house. Placing the reciever up to my right ear, I waited patiently for somebody to pick up the phone at the other end of the line. 'Hello?' came the sheepish female voice on the other end of the line. 'Who is it?' they followed up with, prompting me to respond and give a customary greeting. 'Hey sis, it's just your favourite older brother here!' I exclaimed, trying to sound as happy as possible so that my sister didn't ignore me or do the very worst, hang up. '... My favourite brother? Does that mean Jake has finally decided to call me from UCLA?' she responded, a sarcastic tone flowing through her high pitched voice. 'Unfortunately not, instead, it's that brother of yours that could be getting his opportunity at the most important championship that GW has to offer in the very immediate future.' I responded, sounding very professional and smooth about the whole situation. Like I said before, personally, I knew I didn't stand a hope in Hell of winning, but I didn't want to sound that way to my family. No, I'd rather my Sister had some hope that I'd come through the win as opposed to no hope. '... Oh Rich, how nice of you to finally contact me again. I swear, you only seem to contact me whenever you need something. In example... money, holding storage for your gear...' she said, seemingly wanting to throw everything back in my face. '... Ah, but this time I don't want money or any more holding storage!' I said enthusiastically, cutting her off rudely. I heard her sigh over on her end of the line and chose to say a few more words, '... anyway, I'm really hoping you could do me one more favor and hold me up in your home for the next few days. I just need a place to stay before I have to go out for this GW event... and at the same time, I figure we could try and sort things out?' My sister was a sucker for the whole idea of reconciliation, especially since she wasn't the confrontational or the violent type. Even in the absolute worst of situations, instead of just hating on somebody, she'd try and get them to sort the situation out; come to an agreement before both sides formally agreed and got on with their lives. 'Rich, you can stay here whenever. Despite not being in support of your decision to go back... I'm going to have to respect it.' she said, sounding dejected but happy to have the eldest sibling come over for some quality family time. 'You know you're the greatest... and you know I'm going to dedicate this 'Clash for the Captain' to you, don't you?' I said, sounding soft and sincere. She loved it when I'd go out of my way to dedicate things in her honor. For some reason, it just made her seem special... it just made her stand out from the rest of the crowd. 'Yeah, I know how you like to suck-up and get ahead of the others with your dedications to me.' she said with a chuckle following. I laughed a little too, knowing that we were finally back on cool terms. '... Feel free to stop by whenever you're ready. I don't have anything else planned for the day so I'll be here until I go to sleep tonight.' 'Awesome,' I responded before finishing the conversation, 'I'll be up at your as soon as I can. Loves ya, Sis.' She said the same farewell stuff that I did before hanging up the line at the other end. I popped the reciever back down onto the phone base and took a final look around my lounge. It was empty and ugly, as previously established, but now, it had some kind of relevence to my current situation. '... Let's just hope we can get this far at 'Clash for the Captain'. Let's just hope we only need one chance to get things right.' I said, taking one final look before leaving the room. The scene faded out to black, my hopes and dreams running silently beside me. Loss was going to be the most likely of outcomes... but hey, everyone loves a surprise winner. : End :
