Mall

24 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 24 The scene opened up to the inside of Joey’s bedroom. It was your normal bedroom with a bed in the middle of the room and a dress beside it. There was a doorway that led into his bathroom. He had a television on a stand on the other side of the room. There was a Playstation 2 set up to the television. Even though we were only 20 years old we can still have fun. Me and Joey were sitting on his bed playing WWE Smackdown 5: Here Comes the Pain. Me and him thought it was the best game in the world. “Haha, beat you again.” I said to him. He wasn’t very good at the game and I won against him every single time I played him. Well, I guess I’m the only one who plays him because no one every comes to his house because its to far out of town. I’m pretty much the only one who would drive all the way here to actually play with him. “I’m tired. Let’s watch television.” he said. “Ok then.” I said. He took both of out controllers and wrapped them up. He turned off the PS2 as normal television started playing. We switched through all of the channels and finally found a commercial of wrestling. But it wasn’t WWE, it was about GW. That was the fed I was in, and there was a PPV coming up and I was in the first match. It was a 6 way cruiserweight match. And the winner got a Cruiserweight title shot in May. “Hey, isn’t this the fed your in?” Joey asked me. “Yeah it is.” I said back to him. We watched it until it was over. We were so bored today, there was nothing to do but sit around or else go somewhere. We turned off the television and just sat there thinking of what to do. Finally, I thought of something. “Why don’t we goto the mall or something?” I asked him. “I guess so, there’s nothing else to do.” he said. We got off the bed and walked out of his room. We walked down the stairs and out of the house. There was kind of a wind but other than that it was a really nice day outside. We walked over to my car and got into it. I started it and backed up out of the driveway. I started driving down the gravel road. *15 Minutes Later* We got to the mall and parked the car like right beside the doors to the mall. We got out and locked the doors. We walked into the mall and looked around, we haven’t been in here for a long time. We started walking around, it was huge. There were a few escalators going up to the top floors, and then there were some stores you could go into. We walked into a SportChek just to see what would be in there. We started looking around as the scene started to fade to pitch darkness.
