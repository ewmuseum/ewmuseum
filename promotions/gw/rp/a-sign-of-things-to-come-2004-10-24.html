A Sign Of Things To Come

24 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/24/04 Date: October 21, 2004 Time: 23:24 Location: The Horseshoe Casino - Las Vegas, Nevada 'ALL IN!' A gentleman spoke over the commotion. Every man around him at the table shot glances at him and quickly through their cards to the center. Except for one... an older gentleman in his mid 60's. A smile crossed his face as he pushed his stack of chips near the center, tipping his blue ball cap to his competitor. The man who went all in seconds before lowered his black sunglasses and looked back at the man with his Cadet Blue eyes. 'Pretty gutsy, for a rookie.' The old man spoke from experience. Years of professional poker playing told him that the young man sitting across from him was very brash, yet very lucky. Chances are, There was no way he could beat his Ace-Jack Suited, Diamonds were the suit. The young man pulled his glasses back up over his eyes. 'Not gutsy old man... I just don't lose.' The younger man said with confidence. It was rumored the young man was a billionaire from somewhere in the Midwest, but none of them men around the table had never heard of him, so her certainly didn't get his money from poker. Easy pickings they all thought, lots of money and little sense. Those endless pockets could feed their families for a year. Everyone around the table thought this was the end of his rope, after all... no one had ever heard the name Christian Zenith before in their entire lives. Christian Zenith kept his hand firmly over top of his two cards as the man tried to read him when he flipped over his Ace-Jack of Diamonds. Zenith didn't flinch as he tossed his pair of Queens, hearts and clubs. The men around the table swooned over the matchup. The dealer quickly went to the flop, laying down the Jack of Clubs, the 8 of hearts, and the Ace of Hearts. The table looked over at Zenith, who sat unrattled at the old man's two pair. Then came the Turn, where the dealer looked over at Christian Zenith, then over at his much older competitor before he flipped over the Queen of Diamonds. The older man looked around the table in shock as Christian Zenith merely nodded his head in approval, seconds before the dealer flipped over The River. 'Queen of Spades!' The dealer shouted as the Old man got up out of his seat and threw his hat down onto the floor. The massive pile of chips was pushed towards Christian Zenith, as he relaxed in his chair. Every jaw at the table had been dropped, this kid had something... beginner's luck, the game rigged, something. Suddenly a playing card hit the table and skidded over in front of Christian Zenith. The Ace of Spades. Christian shoved his chair away from the table with great forced. All his chips tipped over and the people at the table screamed as poker chips littered the table and no one could tell what chips belonged to who anymore. Christian's chair tipped over, sending him somersaulting backwards. Christian popped up to his feet seconds before the poker table in front of him exploded, sending a handful of people flying away from the table, screaming in pain, some on fire... being burned alive. Paramedics swarmed the table with fire extinguishers, as Christian picked up his wooden chair and hurled it towards the balcony, where a man wearing a white suit jumped out of the way. Splinters flew everywhere as the chair shattered against the wall behind the man. The man stared down at Christian Zenith, with a creepy smile. He was an older man, his hair was grayed and slicked back, like a businessman. He had a red handkerchief in his white suit jacked pocket. A pair of gold rimmed glasses, reflected the lights from above, making Zenith not be able to see them. 'Dietrich...' Gentlemen... DESTROY! A group of shirtless men with camo styled cargo jeans and black ski-masks quickly rushed to the side of Nikolaus Dietrich, the man who once founded Zenith Corp. with Christian's father, Ulrich. Dietrich pointed his finger at the heir to the Zenith throne, and the small army of six pointed their AK-47's at Christian Zenith. 'There he is gentleman... you know what to do.' Dietrich said with satisfaction, while a smile crossed his face. Suddenly, gun shots rained down on the Casino, and the gamblers sprinted for their lives like a group of mice exposed to the light. Bullets ricocheted off the ground under Zenith's feet as he ran towards the door. The 6-man army scattered and rushed towards Zenith, who was quickly being lost in the crowd. Christian Zenith bolted out the exit, and ran aimlessly into the street. Jumping in front of a passing silver Mitsubishi Eclipse, Zenith grabbed his .357 Magnum and pointed it firmly at the driver. Screeching to a halt, the drive quickly jumped out of the car. Christian jumped into the driver's seat and floored the car, leaving behind a cloud of tire smoke. Zenith flips open his cell phone and uses his voice activated dialing '...Valentine.' He utters seconds before a ringing soon consumes his right ear. Suddenly someone picks up on the other end... 'Vivica here.' 'Vivica, where are you?' 'I'm at Circus Circus... kinda in the middle of something.' 'Leave now.' 'Are you insane?!' 'Vivica, it's an emergency, I need you to leave with Demitri and Jason... NOW.' 'Oh c'mon...' 'It's Dietrich, he's here. He must've stopped in Las Vegas while on his way to look for us in New Mexico, that's where he last knew about our whereabouts. I need you guys to meet me somewhere... I figured I can get rid of two birds with one stone.' 'What do you mean Christian?' 'Meet me at Palm Mortuaries & Cemeteries. The address 7600 S Eastern Avenue, it's about 8 miles out of Las Vegas... I'll keep Dietrich occupied.' 'What the hell are you talking about?' 'Goodbye Vivica, I'll see you there.' Vivica J. Valentine slams her cell phone shut and stands up... 'You owe me one Christian.' She says as she begins to walk out... that was before she was interrupted. 'DOWN IN FRONT!' Someone shouted at her as she made her way to the exit. Vivica shot a look at the man that would make ice melt... or most people spontaneously combust at least. The man sunk down in his seat, while Vivica continued her way towards the exit. The memories far beyond the reckoning Have begun to lurk in the distance Like visual objects dearly known The grace of devils hands As they walk with me like a medium When I choose and require a burn-out Resting in expanded malicious force Drained for murderous weapons Knowing where you stand In the magnitude of this thought Looking at the spirit of fire and flames Enduring on the throne of the black heart The howling of coyotes could be heard in the distance, carrying through the thin desert air. The full moon hung high over the Palm Cemetery, surrounded by a large brick wall and a classic wrought iron gate. You know what they say about cemetery gates... sure they keep people out, but it keeps others in. Other than that coyotes, the night was pretty quiet... until the crackling of gravel broke the silence. The headlights of a silver Eclipse shined off the wrought iron gate, exposing the sites within. The Mitsubishi pulled up aside the brick wall, and shut down suddenly. The coyotes could be heard once again as Christian Zenith got out of the driver's side and pulled his Magnum back out of his pocket. Out of the pocket on the other side of hid black suit jacket, Zenith pulls out some ammo, and loads it carefully into the Magnum. Christian carefully places the gun back into the inside of his jacket pocket, before pacing back and forth. Zenith looked up the gravel road as he dug his hand deep into his pocket, where he pulled out a poker chip. He looked at the poker chip, inspecting it closely in the moonlight. From the second Dietrich showed up at the casino, he'd had a feeling that it was something more than tracking his whereabouts to New Mexico. After looking at the poker chip closely it became obvious for him what purpose it served in the whole scheme of things. It was all so simple now, it's a game now. A game of cat and mouse, hunt or be hunted. The crackling of the gravel caught Christian's waiting ear, that was the only sign he needed. In one fluid motion, Zenith leapt to the top of the Eclipse and then jumped outstretched over the brick wall. Christian landed in a somersault on the other side, before reaching his feet and sprinting deep into the cemetery. Christian sprinted past gravestones and various other resting places, as he heard the car stop outside the brick wall. Following the gun shots of a band of AK-47's, an explosion lit up the sky outside the gate, where Dietrich eliminated any possibility of escape. His 6-man army pounded away at the wrought iron cemetery gate with the blunt ends of their AK-47's. It was only a matter of time until the gate swung open in defeat. The 6-man army charged, with lights on their guns to lead the way. One of the men lead the group, with a tracking device in his hand. The red blinking light on the screen grew closer and closer as they marched. The man stopped and held up his right arm, halting his group of mercenaries. The group turned in every direction, shining their lights on everything they could, Zenith was near... they could feel him. The leader of the pack looked down at his radar, the red blinking light was right ontop of them. He took a step forward, seeing the red dot would move. Hearing a crunch under his foot, the leader looked up into the sky, then back onto his radar. Much to his dismay, the red light was gone. He reluctantly lifted his shoe, and looked at the rimnense of the crushed poker chip on the gravel path. The circuitry was ruined, and the plastic chip base was shattered. The leader turned around to the group and pointed down to the shattered chip on the ground. The group began to sigh in disappointed, before suddenly a gun shot was heard. The leader limply fell onto the coarse gravel road, and blood began to stain the lightly colored pebbles. The 5 remaining members all brought their guns up, ready to fire. They all went back to back very carefully, slowly circling around while stepping over their fallen comrade. Stealthily, Christian Zenith lunged down from the tree directly above them landing in the middle of the circle of them. Christian broke the neck of the man on his immediate right and held him in front of his like a shield when the other 4 members turned and fired. Their party member's body was soon riddled with bullets, spraying his comrades with a shower of blood. Zenith hurled the body at two of the men before leaping into the air. The two men were struck hard by the body and fell to the ground, dropping their guns, which skidded across the gravel and off the path into the grass. The other 2 men with their guns fired at Christian Zenith in the air, who pulled out his .357 Magnum and shot them both in their right hands. The men dropped their guns in pain. Zenith landed and went to charge the men, but the other two grabbed him from behind and held him to be shot, not knowing the two men could no longer hold their guns. Christian elbowed the man behind him to his right, shattering his nose and driving bone into his brain. The other man behind him to his left went to attack, but Christian slammed his foot down on the side of his right leg, where the two bones in his leg connect. The man felt a crack after his knee was shattered into two different pieces. He fell to one knee, where he tried to punch Zenith. Christian instinctively grabbed his arm and slammed his other fist down onto the mans shoulder, breaking her collarbone in two. He then shoved man's head with his open palm, snapping his neck. The man in the ski-mask limply fell over to his right. 'Now, are you going to kill yourselves or do I have to do it for you?' Christian said, with an monotone sound to his voice. The men in ski-masks held their right hands in pain. Blood trickled down their left arms as they laughed at Christian's remark. The laughing didn't last very long when Christian struck the one on his left across the side of the head with a roundhouse kick. He flew hard into the ground, rolling across the dry grass. The other man quickly rushed towards Christian. Zenith threw out a punch, but the other man swatted it away and countered with a right hook. Zenith dodged the right hook and slammed his fist into his rib cage. The man in the ski-mask grabbed his chest and fell over as he felt his ribcage shatter. On his hands and knees, blood began to pour out of his mouth as his comrade got back up, shaking off the cobwebs. The man tried to kick Christian, who caught his leg under his left arm. Christian then choked the man with his right hand and forced him to the ground. The man began to hack and flail, no longer being able to breathe, before Christian grabbed tight and twisted, feeling his spine snap in his fist. Christian walked over to the last remaining man, looking up at him in fear, blood pouring out of the corners of his mouth. The man held up his hands, begging for his life with Zenith looked down at him. Christian's face was as cold as ice, before he spit upon the man in the ski-mask. Suddenly, a gun shot echoed through the desert air. The man's eyes grew wide as he looked up at Christian, and fell held first into the dirt. Behind him, Vivica J. Valentine looked up at him with a smile. 'Pussy.' She said jokingly, Christian just looked back at her when he pulled a knife out of his slacks pocket. Suddenly a huge explosion once again lit up the Nevada night sky. Christian looked at the fireball launched into the air, along with pieces of debris. Then he looked back at Vivica. 'Demitri and Jason wanted to play with Dietrich's car.' Valentine said, explaining the blast... 'Now, what in the hell did you need us for again?' One by one, Christian got gown on one knee, and gouged the blade of his knife into their backs and made a line. Lines became symbols, symbols became letters, and letters became words. By the time Demitri and Jason showed up... each body had a word distinctively carved on their backs in flesh and blood. The said 'Freeze, Brick, Jackson, Nazo, Jamester, Kinslayer.' Demitri smiled at the carved cadavers while Christian pulled a small disposable camera out of his pocket. He took ten pictures of the bodies, making sure to distinctly show the names on their backs. After he was finished, Zenith put the camera back in his pocket, where Demitri told him the bad news of the night. 'Dietrich got away on foot, but we blew up his car, he won't be driving that one anytime soon.' Demitri said with just a bit of a smile, Christian looked back down at the series of dead bodies. Little did anyone in GW expect, that those same 6 men... Freeze, Brick, Jackson, Nazo, Jamester, and Kinslayer would all get manila envelopes in their mailboxes the next morning. Bearing the same pictures Christian took that very night, the package was only labeled with on phrase... 'Good Luck.'
