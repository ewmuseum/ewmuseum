How it all began. (part 2)

Date: 29/02/05 1995A year had past since Salaok's initiation into The Lair and with each new season had brought something to to Salaok. A new way of thinking, a new way of seeing things, a new way of feeling things and a new way of being. However, there was soon a new thing to come to Salaok as he sat in the office waiting nervously for his orders of the day and today for some unknown reason didn't feel like the other days. Something was different. Everything was the same, but something within Salaok knew there was something different about today and as time went on, his beliefs were found to be true. The original Harvester of Sorrow had been selected to be Salaok's mentor throughout his first years with the Lair and served as a guidance tool more than anything as the young Salaok who was still just in his teens began to learn the ways of the underground societies in which he had now become. The Harvester of Sorrow looked at Salaok from across the table reading every moment and breath in which he took, knowing full well Salaok had an idea about what was to come but the order would still be a shock to the young man. For Salaok's brother had been able to handle his jobs with professionalism and now this would surely be the test as to whether or not Salaok would ever be able to stand up to the level in which Johnathon had set before him. 'Salaok, a year has passed since you joined us here in The Lair and you've proven a worthy subject during the past 12 months. However, it is now time to take the next step and earn you 'skull'.' Jeremy, which was the real name of The Harvester of Sorrows had stated this so coldly and with a lack of emotion that struck at Salaok despite the fact that Salaok knew this was coming sooner or later. Salaok takes a breath and then swallows before he nods to his minder before letting out a quiet single worded responce. 'OK.' The responce didn't fill Jeremy with great confidence in the young man's willingness to do such a thing but never-the-less, Jeremy knew Salaok would do anything for this group know and he would find a way to get his 'skull' if he was ordered to. The papers were slid across the table to the young man along with a small parcel which Salaok knew would have been holding the weapon in which he was to do the job with and a weapon he would keep for the rest of his life. Sitting in silence, Salaok reads over the papers with the details about the man in whom he must take out however it is when Salaok gets to the price of the hit that he can not help but let out 'My God!'. 'Yes young Salaok, it pays well, as do the rest of our hits. Should you prove yourselve calm enough to get this job done, others will come your way and money will continue to roll in. I know it's hard for you to imagine being a millionaire at your age Salaok, but it's about to happen if you can do this for me.' Planting the ideas of money past Salaok's wildest dreams into the young man's head was the only way that Jeremy could change the atmosphere in the room as until this moment, he had felt Salaok was somewhat unsure about the task at hand. However the look on his face now was no longer one that worried about killing another man, but one that was more interested in what he was to buy with such money. Money It would end up being the one thing that controlled Salaok's life for the next ten years. With with power comes money and with money comes power. The two would run hand in hand and Salaok was greedy beyond what The Lair would ever had imagined. But for now, the conversation with his minder would deal with the hows and whens of what would go down over the next week as Salaok prepaired to finally gets his 'skull'. Skull. It was the term that The Lair had used when refering to those who had done the job of an assassination for them before. Even those who had performed a job before they were in The Lair still had to earn there 'skull' the Lair way. Because despite the fact that they had most of the police paid off, even in the early days, they still needed to make sure there was nothing left that could bring them down. As it wasn't one person that would feel the effect of a bad job, but it would be the whole of The Lair that the problem would ripple through. The Italian families had seen this during the mid twentieth century as the police began to crack down on their fortress and it would only take one person to land in prison for it to effect the rest of the family as someone else always had to stand up to replace those who had gone. The same thing was apart of The Lair and the person that Salaok had been brought in to replace was the same person his brother had been. Their father. Salaok would leave the room that day a new man, even though the job was yet to be done. There was something about the fact that you knew you had to kill someone that changes your outlook on so many things like the way someone is looking at you. It would be two days later while Salaok was scoping the area of the hit with his brother Johnathon that this would become increasingly evident. The man's eyes just didn't seem to leave Salaok which frustrated the younger of the two Davis brothers. The man in question was nothing more than a homeless youth which later on in his life, Salaok would learn had a belonging in The Tragic Kingdom but that would happen some 7 years later. This young man in question simply watched as Salaok and Johnathon walked around the area, trying hard not to stand out and yet not looking like they are trying to fit itno the situation. His hair was matted and his clothes were dirty but to him it didn't matter because he was living his life in control of what he did and how he did things. To him, money wasn'tthe answer to his problems or his life question as he had been though things far different, far worse than some people's minds even dare to wander. His constant watching was growing on Salaok's nerves and Johnathon could see it clearly as he watched his little brother who stood half a foot taller than him fidget with his hair and clothing. It had always been the telling sign that something wasn't right with Salaok's mental state and it would gradually become more and more intense until something was done to stop that whcih was irritating him. Nothing seemed to stop irritation for Salaok more than beating the shit out of it these days and before Johnathon could stop him, Salaok had charged the young homeless male and slamming him against the wall with strength that Salaok was still only getting used to himself. Holding the youth off the ground against the wall, Salaok squeezes tighter and tighter around the neck forcing his thumb and forefinger deep into the skin as his eyes glare at the grimmacing face of his watcher. 'Why were you watching me?' Salaok's voice gruff and fueled with rage that had sprouted from no where so fast and intense. Johnathon watches his brother as he demands answers and shakes his head knowing full well what Salaok has gotten himself into and what this may end up causing down the road. Before Salaok can get an answer from the youth, a chunk of wood finds itself smashing into his lower back as another street stalker comes to the rescue of his friend and before Salaok can spin around to defend himself, three more have appeared and have Salaok circled. Not wanting to just give in to numbers, Salaok doesn't hold back as his large body gives him some sort of advantage, letting a right fist fly out and connect with the jaw of one of his attackers. The others jump the young Salaok and with the situation soon four on one, there isn't much he can do but there was a way out of the situation and it was walking straight towards him. Johnathon, although smaller than Salaok was far more vicious and within seconds of getting near the action has the sitaution under control as his hand rips one of the attacks back by the hair while his hand other presses a knife straight into the side of his neck. The hostage yells out to his friends as fear erupts over his body for a few seconds before the calm of not caring takes over but he had all ready caused his friends to stop beating down on Salaok. 'Now, either you gentlemen move away and forget this happened, or this knife will disappear into your little friend's neck, only to then reappear as I drag it from one side to the other cutting all major blood lines. Your choice boys, move away or watch him bleed to death knowing full well that it was your actions that caused his death.' Johnathon's voice as cold and in control as ever making the message of death even more chilling and real, causing the three other's to move away. If only that had been were it had ended. Salaok wasn't the kind to simply let something pass and still burning with the rage of having someone watching him plan his first 'skull' made him want one last hit. One last message to leave him alone. Reaching out from behind the one who first frustrated him, he grabs his jaw and goes to spin him around. The head spins around but the body doesn't as the sound of a loud crack fills the side allyway where they all stand. For a split second the youth stays upright but soon crumbles to the ground like a rag doll as crimson blood trickles out of his ear much to the shock of everyone. Much to the shock more than any of Salaok. His eyes wide as they look down on the youth laying in a heap on the stone ground and then up at his brother who just stared at Salaok in disbelief of his complete and utter stupidity. Johnathon knew that there was only one way out of this and lead the way for his younger brother has he pulled the knife across the throat of his hostage and then pulled out a gun firing single shots into the other two who had already began to run. Johnathon wasn't big in shooting someone in the back of the head but in this situation, there was no nother choice for they would have gotten away and that simple wasn't allowed to happen. The bodies of the two men running away fell as the bullets entered the back of their heads and quickly exploded through the front of their skulls sending bloody and bone matter into the air, showering down onto the ground. Hitting the ground and sliding across the harsh rock serface, the bodies came to a bodied stop and finally Johanthon let go of the man in his arm as the blood continued to spew forth out of the gaping hole in his neck. It would take a while for the man to die from blood loss and that simply wasn't what Johnathon was wanting to wait for, as he raised his foot into the air and then brought it down hard onto the back of the man's neck, snapping it at the top of his shoulders before drilling a bullet into his head. Death isn't what today was meant to have brought, and yet it had come with all guns blazing. Salaok couldn't believe how easy the death of four men had just occured, and what he struggled to accept was he was the one who started it all. Killed the youth in the same way he had watched Johnathon kill for the first time in 1987 in the school grounds. 'Are you just going to stand there waiting to be caught you idiot, or are you going to help?' Bites Johnathon as he begins to drag the bodies further into the allyway and behind a dumpster which instantly shakes Salaok out of his memories of that fatal day, causing him to grab a hold of the two remaining bodies and drag their lifeless masses over the harsh ground. Johnathon tosses the four bodies into a pile against the dirt covered wall and then begins to heap the garbage bags on top of them, giving at least a small bit of coverage until help arrives. And help wouldn't be too far off as it never is with The Lair and with a simple phone call, help was on it's way. As Johnathon hangs up the cell phone, he runs his right hand through his dirt blonde hair and sighs in disbelief of what his brother had done and the situation in which it has landed them in. 'You don't get it do you Salaok? You don't understand that times have changed and that there is no way we can be so reckless these days.' 'Johnathon, I didn't mean to kill him, I just wanted to turn him around to hit him one more time.' Salaok tries to explain that it was all just a bad mistake but Johnathon wants nothing of it as he knows of the situation in which this has put the both of them and the conflicts it will cause down the road. 'You've spent a year with me in The Lair now Salaok, and you know oft he deals we have with other smaller groups in the lands that we know will provide information and help should we need it. One of those groups is the street, The Tragic Kingdom if you wish to still call it that and now if word of this gets out, it's going to cause major problems with our information system and not to mention the added protection we have gotten from them over the years.' Salaok knew from the tone of Johanthon's voice that this would indeed be a mistake that would come back to haunt him in time and that no matter what Salaok said now, it would do nothing to ease Johnathon's mind of the actions in which have just taken place. As Salaok is about to try and explain again that he didn't mean for this time happen, didn't mean to kill the man it suddenly it home as to what had just happened. Salaok had killed. It may have been an accident and it may not have been his mark, but he had finally taken a life. Beeds of sweat begin to form on his forehead and his hands begin to shake which Johnathon instantly notices despite Salaok's attempts to control it and hide that fact. But Johanthon could see it in his eyes now that the magnitude of the events was hitting home and how Salaok took this feeling from here on in would deitrmine if Salaok was ever going to be of any use to The Lair as an assassin or if he would end up being just another man who couldn't stomach the life of causing death. 'Salaok, we came here today to do a job and if you don't go and do it now, you may have missed your only chance at working out how this is going to work.' Johnathon, trying to find out Salaok's reaction to what he has just done and the fact that he has to kill again later in the week and surprisingly the cold heartless voice that Johnathon had heard from Salaok a few times answered him back. 'Don't worry brother, I'm fine. In all truth, I'd rather stay here with the bodies and I'll come back here and if need be watch all night long until I have the info I need. In truth, I'd rather be doing the hit at night under the cover of darkness anyway.' Salaok's responce surprised even himself but it was exactly what he was thinking and although not what Johnathon was expecting to hear, it was an answer that he had hoped for. Whether it had been the realization that he had just ended another man's life and the knowledge he would have to do it again that had controlled him, or if it had been that way with Salaok all along didn't really matter now. For Johnathon knew that his younger brother who he had brought to The Lair was going to do him proud. And better yet, would make their father turn in his grave. It wouldn't be long until an old black van pulls up at the entrance of the ally and then begins to back down it which put's Salaok on edge however Johnathon places a hand on his shoulder which calms Salaok down straight away. The van comes to a stop but the engine remains on as the door then opens revealing the reason why there was nothing to worry about. The massive frame of Quartz steps out of the driver side of the van and from the passanger side steps the lovely Lisha, both of them clearly ready to get to work. Quartz walks over and begins to pick up the bodies with thehelp of Johnathon while Lisha is on her cell phone controlling a situation elsewhere by the sound of things. Her voice was cool and collected as she explained what was happening here and what she would be needing at the other end of the trip. 'Listen Jackson, going to need a boat with a shark tank and a crane for tonight.' She waits while the man known as Jackson replies and must have asked the question that he knew this was about. 'Yeah, Salaok got his first cap and Johnathon has cleaned up the witnesses down here. There is four in total so the normal sized cage should do the job. How does One O'Clock this morning sound?' Again she pauses while Jackson responds and as all of this is happening, the bodies are loaded into the van and Johnathon pulls out a high pressure hose from the back. Lisha has no choice but to block her free ear as she answers back to Jackson. 'OK, not a problem. We will store the bodies until then and get clearance from the coast guard and local cops. See you when we get there.' Lisha hangs up the cell phone and nods to Salaok and Johnathon as her way of saying hello. Everything was set for the night and as Johnathon finishes up washing down the ally, he loads the hose into the van and the doors are shut. The fast happens around Salaok seems almost like a dream in the smooth and calculated actions of the three that there was no room for wasting time nor any mistakes. This was the life in which Salaok was apart of now and soon his second murder would have taken place. However this time, there would be a large sum of money at the end of it to be collected. ---- Two days later The phone rings and the body in the bed stirs and mumbles that his sleep has been disturb but regardless answers the phone after clearing his voice. 'Jeremy here.' The Harvester of Sorrow yawns and sits up in bed and listens to the familiar voice down the other end of the line while in an apartment building two cities over, Salaok stands with his cell to his ear. 'It's been done.' Salaok stands in the room of his mark with his lifeless body being held by the hair with his neck twisted a full one hundred and eighty degree. The face of his mark is one of pain and shock while the face of Salaok is cool and emotionless with the only thought going through his mind right now was the collection of his money.
