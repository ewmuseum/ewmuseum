meeting up with roz Venom Alexander Global Wrestling Promo #501 Date: 7/31/03 Location: plane Days after Jennifer’s funeral Venom packs all of his belongings grabs his ticket to head to Dallas to meet up with Roz Blayze who got a earlier flight so Venom can have time alone. - 1 Hour Later On The Plane- “What a amazing few of the sky, not a cloud in sight.” Venom continues to look out of his window. Wonders what would happen if him and Roz would end up being the last two standing in the ring in the last chance battle royal. A few minutes go by, and Venom’s cell phone goes off, Venom reaches in his bag and it’s Alex calling from his home in Toronto. “Hey Venom, you in Dallas yet?” Asked Alex. “I’m probably about an hour or so from there, why you ask?” Alex tell Venom, that he needs a favor done while Venom is at the GW arena. “Who want me to do what?” Venom could not believe his ears what Alex wanted me to do. “You heard me Venom, I want you to get Brandi Sights number for me, she is hot.” Venom just paused for a few minutes knowing, the Alex has no chance in hell to get with her. “Alex, man I would, but I can’t. To be honest with you, look for someone else.” Alex, agrees on what Venom told him, so the two hang up on each other. Venom goes back to look out of the window, realizing how much he miss’s Cobra a friend of his for years along with his girlfriend who was soon to be wife Jennifer. Two loses in a short period of time how can Venom think about wrestling at this time. But he knows he hast to move on, get over the loses of his friends. - A hour later - Venom arrives at the Dallas airport and looks at his watch and notices that he is early, so he decided to walk around the airport visit book shops and other places. While visiting one book store he comes across a book called Ghost Rider by Neil Peart. “A man loses wife and daughter all in less than a year, so he hopes on his bike and rides across America to deal with his lose.” Venoms stands reading the back of the cover out loud. “May I help you with something?” Asked the sales clerk. “Not really, just wasting time, waiting for a friend to keep me up.” As Venom explain to the clerk. “Not to be nosy but who is picking you up?” Asked the sales clerk. Venom tells him Roz Blayze, the clerk looks at him, then turns around and grabs a GW magazine and notices that Venom is on the back cover of it. “Venom Alexander, I thought I seen you from somewhere, I’m a huge fan. I brought a ticket for the pay per view that is coming up. Are you going to be there?” Venom tells the clerk yes he is going to there, that he is going to be in the last chance battle royal. Venom give the clerk an autograph and live the bookstore. When Venom walked out of the bookstore, when the announcer came on asking for Venom Alexander that Roz Blayze was at the main entrance to pick him up. Venom then hurry’s his way to the entrance to meet up with Roz. The two final meet up and get in Roz ride and drive away. End of scene.
