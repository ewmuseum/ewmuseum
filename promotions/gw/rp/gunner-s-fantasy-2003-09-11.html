Date: 9-11-03 'Sweetie....sweetie.. calm down! I can't understand what you are saying, you have to calm down, and speak slower.' Star, in a pair of capri khakis and a plain white tank top, paces back and forth around the small off white living room, in her and Ice's apartment. She circles around the small black leather love seat, then around the long pea green reclining chair, and back to the black leather love seat where she takes a seat, and continues to listen to the person on the other end of the phone. Star crosses her left leg over the right, then uncrosses them and crosses the right leg over the left. Frantically she wiggles her right foot around in a circle, and begins to bite her finger nails as she still is listening to the person on the other end of the phone. 'Alright sweetie, I'll see if Ice has seen him or heard from him and get back to you. I'm sure nothing is the matter. The phone probably died, or he turned it off like he usually does when he goes to the gym. Now that he's a champion you have to realize he's going to be alot busier than he was before. Ok. I'll talk to you later. Buh-bye.' Star hangs up the phone, and sets it down on the love seat next to her. She slides her right index finger into her mouth, and gently nibbles on the end of it, with a confused look on her face. Her pondering is ended as the sound of the front door opening, and then slamming shut breaks her trance. Star anxiously jumps to her feet, and runs to the door. 'ICE.....Honey?! Is that you? I got something I need to ask of you.' Star reaches the door, and no one is there. Confused she turns in a circle and looks around. Out of nowhere a hand covered in a black glove clasps over Star's mouth. Another hand wraps itself around her waist, and a man in a long black leather jacket, wearing a black ski mask begins to pull her through the living room. Frantically Star kicks and squirms trying to escape from the man's grip, but cannot get loose. The man kicks open a door that is behind him, and tosses Star into the room, and onto the bed that is in the center of the room. She immediately sits up and tries to get away, but the man pounces on top of her and holds her in place. Firmly he places his forearm underneath her chin and securely on her throat. Star's eyes widen as the man reaches into his coat pocket and pulls out a small gag. He secures the gag, and pulls out a strand of rope. He ties one end of the rope around Star's left wrist, and then ties the other end to the bed post. Again the man pulls out a strand of rope, and ties it around Star's other wrist, and then ties that to the bed post. The man slides down Star's lean body, and pulls out another strand of rope. He proceeds to tie both legs to the bottom bed posts as he had done to her wrists at the top of the bed. 'Mrmrmrmrmrmrmr' Hysterically Star tries to wriggle out of the ropes that tie her to the bed, while trying to speak through the gag at the same time. The man chuckles out loud at his captives distress, and reaches inside of his coat. Slowly he pulls out a long, freshly shined, beaming silver knife. The man twirls it in his hand and begins to walk over towards Star. Her eyes widen to the size of softballs, and with a sense of emergency begins to kick and flail around as much as she can, doing everything and anything to escape. The man can be heard inhaling deeply as he leans forward and is inches from Star's body. Slowly he slides the blade under the right strap to her tank top, and cuts through the material. Almost in a mocking like manner the man takes his time and walks around to the other side of the bed. Just as he did on the other side, the man leans forward and cuts through the left strap to her tank top. He then proceeds to cut the material in half, straight down the center, revealing a sexy, black lace bra, covering her two bulging breasts. Tauntingly the man glides the cool blade over her smooth pale skin. Star's body trembles, and she slightly moans. 'Do you like that GUNNER? You do don't you? I can tell by the way you moan like the little slut you are. The way your body trembles in response to what I am doing to you. Only a slut like you would steal a man away from his friends. But that is alright my dear. You can have him to yourself. You can do all the nasty things sluts like you do to him. But, soon, he isn't going to be around. And just like I have this pretty little thing all to myself, I will have you all to myself. There will be NO ONE to save you. And the things you do to manipulate Ken, the things you do to manipulate all men, won't be able to save you from the things I plan on doing to you.' Just like what was done to the white tank top Star had on, the man slides the blade underneath the side of Star's pant leg, and begins to cut away the material. He reaches the top, and pulls away the khaki capris. A lacey pair of black panties, matching the bra, are revealed. 'Very nice GUNNER. What do you have hiding under there, that millions haven't seen before?' Star begins the squirm once again. 'What was that I couldn't hear you GUNNER! You said only a few hundred have see what's under there. Well my apologies, that is much better than the inaccurate figure I gave before. Gunner, you have decided to make your remarkable return at the wrong time. You decided to come back, alligning yourself with the wrong person. And you have decided to come back and try and reclaim your mark here via the wrong person.' The man slides the blade over Star's tight, firm stomach, inbetween her breasts, and begins to gently caress the side of her cheek with it. A sick smirk can be seen from the opening at the bottom of the black ski mask where his mouth is. 'Gunner, things here have changed. This place is no longer the place you knew when you left. The things that go on here are not for someone like you to be around. A pretty little thing like yourself could get hurt. And no one would ever want such a thing to happen now would we?' The sarcastic tone in the man's voice echos through the small bedroom. He slides teh blade back down Star's body, and cuts the fabric on the left side of her panties. He glides the blade over to the other side, and cuts the fabric there as well. The black lace still covers Star, as the man pulls the blade away from her body, and drops it to the floor. 'Gunner, I would be extremely careful this week. I would look over both shoulders every second you can. I would make sure that if Ken is not around you, that someone is, because being alone, is not something that is going to be very safe for you to do. Gunner, I wish to welcome you back, but in my own special way. You may not like my welcoming, but believe me honey, you will never EVER, forget who it came from.' The man reaches up and pulls off the black ski mask. Star's emerald green eyes squint in anger as Ice reveals himself as the man behind the mask. A sick sadistic smile crawls across his face as he looks down at Star, and then back towards the camera. Ice reaches down and removes the gag from Star's mouth. 'You son of a bitch! How could you do that too me!' Star's angered expression quickly fades as a seductive smile crosses her face. 'I mean you get me all excited, and not going to do anything about it?' Ice smiles into the camera and pushes the cameraman out of the room. The bedroom door slams shut. Star giggling is heard as the scene fades to commercial.
