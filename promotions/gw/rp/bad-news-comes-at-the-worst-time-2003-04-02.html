Bad news comes at the worst time

2 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-2-03 The Scene opens up in a small one-room place. The walls are all wood and there is not much of anything in it. A single bed lines one wall, covers neatly on the bed and pillows on it to make it look something like a couch. There is a shelf full of books just above the bed.. Next to the bed is a nightstand made from a large cable wire spool, containing an ashtray, A cig still smokes in the tray? a lamp, some loose change and a phone. A single sink; with a mirror just above it, and a shelf on the sidewall with soap and other hygiene products. A small fridge sits under the sink. There is a door at the far wall and a dresser next to it. On the dresser sits a TV a cowboy hat and some clothing items. Welcome to the home of one Ely Braxton. Ranch hand at The Golden Stag ranch just outside of Dallas. Shortly out of the door walks a large muscle of a man, baldheaded wearing only a towel around his waist.. He walks to the sink and begins to apply shaving cream.. Braxton looks at his self in the mirror, a scar just under his right eye, one on his left shoulder stand out, along with an assortment of tattoos. Including a large one on his back. He sprays the shaving cream in his hand and applies it to his face and pulls out the strait razor from its place in the self. Halfway though the shave the phone rings? With a sigh Braxton picks up the phone. “Yeah” Braxton says in his deep voice. “Calm down little one. What has happened?” Braxton sits down on the bed. His shave all forgotten, and a look of concern appears on his stony face. “I will be right there Christina. Don’t worry Bubba will be all right. Your dad is a tough one.” Braxton says to the person on the other line in a somewhat confident tone. After a while he hangs up the phone. Walks to the sink and splashes water on his face washing the shaving cream away. “Bubba what have you gotten your self into now” You better pull though you old basterd.” Braxton says as he grabs his cloths and drops the towel.. After a while Braxton comes out of the stables, where he lives on Bubba’s ranch. He heads for a old beat up blue and red truck climbs in and peels out heading down the dirt road.. What turn in his life would happen now. Bubba had to pull though, and Braxton would make sure who ever harmed the man would pay. More important to him is the fact that Christina McCoy was heart broken and for that whoever was responsible would pay and pay dearly.
