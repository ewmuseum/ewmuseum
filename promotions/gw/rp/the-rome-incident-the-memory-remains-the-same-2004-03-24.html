The Rome Incident - the memory remains the same

24 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03.24.04 [i]'Come on, Bro. Is this the fastest this thing can go?' Josh was egging Ken on while both were laughing. 'Trust me, man, you don't wanna' go past 85 or 90 on these streets,' uttered Ken. Paul Oakenfold's Bunnka album was playing in the car, pouncing out the beats of 'Zoo York' as the car sped up to 93 mph. 'I think you're just a pussy,' laughed Josh. 'You're telling me that you're scared to pop the Diablo up past 100 miles an hour.' 'Okay, you asked for it,' responded Ken, who was a former street racer. He stomped his foot down heavily on the accelerator, causing the car to easily surpass 110 mph. The New York City streets were a little damp and, oddly enough, weren't crowded by any means. 'Fast enough for you,' Ken questioned loudly to Josh above the music. 'So you've got acceleration and top speed in this thing, but my Viper could still out maneuver you.' Just as Josh finished, Ken pulled up the parking brake and cut the steering wheel 90 degrees to the right, bringing the car to a quick and sudden turn into a back alley as the tires squeeled with excitment. Ken soon hits a small hill and begins his ascent up a backway to the bridge full of twists and turns. 'Okay, so maybe you've got a little something on me in all catergories, but at least I'm younger than you,' Josh laughed enthusiastically. Around and aorund the cruves, Ken went. Slowing down at the beginning of them anly to speed back up in them. Fast,. slow, fast, fast, slow. It was pure adrenaline... pure excitement. The Oakenfold album only seemed to get louder as the two concentrated on the road and the pure rush going through their veins. Ken's hand quickly shifts gears with the double-clutch style while reaching for the parking brake when necessary. Josh notices the few times that both of Ken's hands end up on the steering wheel on the unnaturally difficult turns. Finally after 15 or 20 minutes of non-stop action, they hit the bridge. Ken lets his car calm down and turns the stereo up a smidge whil lowering the windows to enjoy the brisk autumn night. 'Old man my ass,' Ken shoots at Josh, who is unable to respond with anything but laughter. The laughter soon transforms into screaming. Screams coming from Josh's mouth in all different directions. Then... silence. A dark room filled with no noise... 'Where in the hell is he,' mumbled Ice in aggrevation. A bright flash of light fills Ken's mind and fades into himself beating on a door. 'Josh! Open the damn door, Josh!' Another flash of light, this time into Ken and Ice kicking in the door and finding Josh face down on the hotel room floor. There are movie-like pauses that usually see only in horror flicks as Ken and Ice make there way over to Josh in slow motion. Then, as Ken turns him over he looks into the face of not Josh... but himself.[/i] 'I think he's going to be just fine. He's stable. Should be up and at 'em before long he go home then.' Ken's trance was quickly interrupted by the doctor. He thanked the doc but was still transfixed to the window. Why had he seen himself in Josh's place. He knew he would gladly switch with him in an instant, but why is he seeing his face instead of Josh's. 'Are you okay, son?' 'I'll be fine, thanks. You say he can get out of here as soon as he comes around?' 'I don't see any reason why not. Just keep an eye on him, and it would be smart not to get him in any club-type atmosphere.' 'I don't see that being a problem, Doc,' responds Ken. 'Thanks again.'
