Desires of the One

22 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 22, 2004 Prologue: Chance… Destiny… Fate… Fortune… All of these are what make up a good night and persons chances in the world. Without these four things one will not believe in going for it all, nor will they believe in a reason for something unknown. Without these life would be way different and a man such as that of Jesse Jamester would lose the good fortune of having faith in him. Without these, desires of one man could be forgotten, and truths of the lies that are within could be sealed or faded away into a painful ache that causes death one day. Find out what those desires are of the one Jesse Jamester… Story Begins: The stars are a gazing haze as Jesse looks up into the sky and see them cross through the snowy atmosphere. There looking through the window of his hotel room he sees the outside as a world of freedom, and being inside is only a reason to stay healthy. Moving from the window to the couch Jesse flicks on the television, which has a cable connection due to the hotel being 5 stars. Jesse goes through the channels gazing for any news updates, however none appear. So he gets up and gets his coat on and decides to go out for a walk. Walking down the street his hair sways just slightly as he walks past groups and couples of people who are having a good night. Jesse observes each one with a close eye trying to pin point the expression they hold and possibly see if he knows anyone. However that is not to be, and Jesse just keeps walking until he reaches a bar called “Stateline”. He enters the front door and walks in to a near empty bar with the exception of the bartender, a few drunks on one end of the bar, and a few ladies playing pool. He sits down opposite of the drunks on the other side of the bar and waits for the bartender. Looking around the place is quite nice, wooden base around most of the area, a jukebox in one corner of the room and pictures of the sports teams that play for the area. The other side of the bar away from the bar layout where drinks are served is a few tables and an arcade of pinball games for those who are into those type of games. Then right behind Jesse about 4 feet away are two casino games for adults. Jesse orders a scotch and then goes to the machines and puts in twenty bucks for a set of 5 games. The machine blinks on and then the reels go spinning as he has to hit a button and match up the right combination of fruit to win, and each time the money gets bigger, but may then go down as quick as it rose. Jesse hits the first time and gets nothing, and then the second time and gets nothing again, but on his third roll he gets 3 watermelons for a total of $5. He bets the $5 and doubles it. Next spin he gets 3 black cherries, worth $3, so now he’s got $11. Thinking for a moment, Jesse has to wonder if he’ll bet it all or not. He looks around and then hits the double button and hits the button to spin it. He hits again but this time with 3 cherries! Three cherries is $20, and so Jesse gets $62! He smiles and hits finish, getting his receipt and going to the bar where his scotch is placed. ’Hey bartender, may I get my money for this?’ Jesse raises the receipt up with a hardy smile on his face as the bartender comes walking over and grunts a little at the knowing he has to give up money. ’Whoa! Sixty-Two Dollars! You’re good eh? Why didn’t you keep trying?’ ’Eh, I’m not one to mock sir, I don’t try and win it all but take what is given. Anyways it’s only luck, and that was real damn lucky of me to win right?’ ’Sure was. Hey, don’t I know you from somewhere?’ ’Uh… probably not, I’m just another ordinary guy.’ ’No… I’m sure I’ve seen your face before… You’re Jesse Jamester! Yeah you wrestle in Global Wrestling! Man you’re good!’ ’Well thanks, but I try to keep a low profile. I dislike being bothered everywhere I go… You know?’ ’Nah I don’t but I can understand how it is to have people on your back all the time. That’s what my wife’s like. Ah, I hate when she yells, but you have to love them once you marry them right?’ Jesse looking at him a little uncertain for a man who has to love is no better than one who marries to gain money, or one who cheats to win in any case. However he nods for he doesn’t feel like arguing and changes the subject. ’So are you coming to the show or watching it this weekend sir?’ ’Call me Ted, and no I won’t be going to it, but I may watch it. Are you wrestling? I haven’t watched for a few weeks, been a little dry on the money side so had to stop paying for cable TV.’ ’I’m facing a guy by the name of Ice. He’s a big man, a great athlete too. Has the potential to be the best if he didn’t think with his fists, and more with his brains, but not all men can be smart right?’ ’Definitely true. Ice, wasn’t he world champion a ways back. When I last watched it he was the champion, what happened?’ ’Ice and Akodo Rokku faced one another and in the end Ice lost to Rokku by a hair. Since then I’ve faced Rokku and came up short, and so decided to fight for the International Title. After winning it a few weeks back I lost it to Ice and so I’ve been pretty much frustrated with the outcome. This week we fight for the strap in a cage match, my all time favorite, and the only match I’ve NEVER lost in.’ ’So you’re the odds on favorite to win the match you say?’ ’Well, I’m bias, but I’d say I’m confident enough to win and if I were to bet on myself, which we’re not allowed, I’d definitely put everything I had down on me beating Ice. See just like that game, I’m going to take what comes my way and if I have to I’ll use as a tool to his own demise. Make sure to watch it Ted, because it will be one hell of a show!’ ’Sure will Mr. Jamester. Have a good night, oh and here’s one on the house.’ Ted hands him a bottle of liquor in a brown bag and pats him on the back as he leaves. Jesse smiles as he walks out the door and leaves the bar heading back up the street to where he is staying. Now with a wad of cash and someone who believes in him, his desire of winning has just risen to the peak and the smile on his face proves just that.
