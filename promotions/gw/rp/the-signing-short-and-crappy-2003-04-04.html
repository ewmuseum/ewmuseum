The signing (short and crappy)

4 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/4/03 Casinos were something that Salaok had managed to understand easily and was able to run them without thought. He was hoping that Gw would have been the same but it was proving otherwise. It wasn't the simple setting up and running under the laws. No, the fight business that he was now a co-owner of a large organisation was a busy one. Keeping people happy was something that Salaok was never good at. Keeping people silent on the other hand was what Salaok knew best. It had been a few days since he had had to put up with Roz in the back of his car. The mere thought of Roz was enough to make Salaok's head shake side to side. Why on earth did he have to get caught up in this war between The Nevermore and Silent Wind? All he was doing was getting in the way. Making life hard for Salaok which only made life for everyone else difficult. But he had a job to do. Well, he only had to do it if he wished the federation to stay alive and kicking. And with the amount of money that Salaok had poured into this place, he wasn't about to let it fall apart. No, he needed something. Something that the federation would draw money from. Something, or someone that was easily marketable. That would sell merchandice. Salaok knew the answer was something that GW used to have but due to Bubba McCoy's poor handling of a situation, had lost. And that soemthing had only just left his office with the contract already signed and dated. Salaok had done the one thing that would make him even more money. How would people react to the deal? More importantly, what would they do when they see it all take place. Cheer of Boo? Either way it didn't matter. No matter how this person was portrayed to the people, he was going to sell tickets. He was going to get asses into the arena night after night. Since taking over the controls of the federation, Global Wrestling had taken off again and everyone knew it. Bubba's falling sick couldn't have come at a better time for the business. It may have been wrong to say it outloud, but everyone was thinking it. Countless names had been signedto the federation within two weeks of Salaok taking the reigns. People who were eating up valuable places on the roster were quickly fired and shown the door. Everything that needed to be done was now being done. The pen which still sat in his hand was placed down on the contract which sat in the middle of his now clean dark wooden desk infront of him. It was only two weeks ago that this desk could barely have been seen due to the amount of paper work that was needing to be done. Soon he was to have a meeting with another man that Bubba McCoy had let go from Global Wrestling. Another man who deserved more than he got while the federation was under Bubba's control and Salaok was going to make sure it happened. Salaok was slowly becoming the saviour of the federation which he had claimed that he would destroy. Was it all just a ploy? Was Salaok setting GW up to new heights, just so that it's fall would be even greater? He pushes his chair away from the desk and it rolled a few feet before coming to a stop. Spinning around in his chair, he stands up and looks out the window. Standing there, looking out onto the people moving around below. Making sure that they stick to the dealines that their life have delt them. The black limo pulls up to the entrance way of the building and the man he had just signed to the federation walks up and gets in. His short blonde hair the only noticable feature of this man. But Salaok soon found his mind wondering. Wonding off to Lisha. To a place where he knew not. His wife was being held captive and there was nothing he could do apart from send out the troops to find her. He had searched high and low for his bride and nothing. Every house, every rock, every car was overturned in the search for her. And he had found her but only for a second. Quartz had found her and reported it to Salaok. But by that time, they were gone. Lisha had been moved by Silent Wind and there was no way of telling where. It had taken them long enough to find them the first time, but now to locate them again would be terribly slow and painful. He knew that there was only one time when Wind would have to leave Lisha unattended and that was when he was booked into a match. There was no way that he could be in two places at once and so now, a way of gettng his wife back would appear. But first, she had to be found. But not only found, but found and never let know that they had been found. The mistake last time feel from the loud mouth Roz. He had spoken loudly to Quartz and given away their presence. A mouth that never should have been there, cost Salaok his wife.
