Consequences and repercussions - the unquiet mind.

11 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 11th, 2006. Consequences and repercussions - the unquiet mind. ---- March 7th, 2006. 9:33 AM. Two men sat in the head office of Knight Enterprises based out of beautiful Seattle, Washington. One man, the owner and founder of Knight Enterprises; a very worried Donovan Knight and the other a cool and calm attorney at law, Colton Cornell. 'Josh hasn't checked in with us, I think he may have found something man...' Mused a fearful Donovan Knight as he tugged nervously at the tips of his spiked golden hair. 'Don't worry, Ruiz is a pro, he'll pull through one way or another.' A buzz cut Cornell assured him with an arrogant smile. 'What if Josh finds put?' Donovan asked in a near panic. 'Then what do we do...we're ruined.' 'Trust me.' Cornell's smile grew more devious by the second. 'Eduardo Ruiz is the man, he's not afraid of Josh or anyone else kicking up dirt. No one is going down, you've got nothing to worry about.' Knight didn't look convinced and was adamant about voicing his concern. 'What if...', he began only to be cut off by the ringing of Cornell's cell phone. 'Hold that thought.' Cornell said, raising his right index finger to signify a pause as he answered his phone with his left hand. 'Whats the good word?' Cornell asked. 'Josh's taken care of.' The voice on the other end said coldly. 'Oh?' Were there any complications?' Cornell asked without even the slightest breath of worry. 'No, he got close, damn close, but I took care of our problem.' Answered the voice of Eduardo Ruiz. 'Is he...', Cornell paused for a moment looking up at Donovan before finishing his question. '...still alive?' Eduardo's answer was short and far from sweet. 'I don't know.' 'Okay.' Cornell replied unalarmed. 'Look amigo, you owe me for this, you owe me big.' Ruiz threatened. 'Don't worry, I've got you covered.' The coldhearted lawyer smiled into the phone. 'But we'll talk later, alright?' 'Fine, we'll go over the details then.' Ruiz answered as he promptly hung up. 'Is Josh...okay?' Squawked a wide eyed Donovan Knight. 'Well...' Cornell paused, unsure of how to answer. '...I don't know.' 'You don't know?!', screamed a frantic Donovan. 'How the hell can you not know?' 'Look, that isn't the issue at hand, right now.' Cornell responded in a heated tone. 'Then what the hell is?', snapped the founder of Knight Enterprises. 'We're okay.' Cornell grinned. 'And right now, thats all that matters.' ---- March 6th, 2006. 11:51 PM. The first thing I felt was the cold, the bitter cold sting of night air as my bluish gray eyes slowly opened, to stare into the dead grass scratching against my cheeks and mouth. The rest of my face was in something warm and moist, unscratched by the grass, and it only took a simple sniff of the air to determine exactly what I was lying in. I was face-first in my own vomit, and I have no idea how I got there. Rolling over I felt the locks of my black and white streaked hair soak up even more vomit as I stared into a black sky. The stars shined brightly down upon me, illuminating my world in an eery luminescence. The night was cold, and the grass itched against the flesh of my back forcing me to sit up, it was then I realized I wasn't wearing a shirt, or shoes either for that matter. I wore a pair of faded BKE jeans, ripped and grass stained, showing evidence that I hadn't found my way to this locale on my own, someone had taken the trouble to drag my unconscious body here. The one questioned that loomed more ominously than the rest, was a very simple: 'where the hell am I?'. I looked around slowly, my eyes adjusting to the nocturnal world I had awoken too. I was surrounded by a sea of dim shapes, no higher than my waist and rounded off at the top. This wasn't looking good. 'Well, Toto, we're not in Fucking Kansas anymore.' I snarled to no one in particular, as I raised a hand to my throbbing head. 'No, you're not.' A voice answered, much to my surprise. 'Who are you..?' I asked, caught off guard. 'I can be your best friend...or your worst enemy, its all about who you want me to be.' The voice replied. 'Where are you?' Was my next question. 'I'm right here.' The voice answered. 'Where?' I asked, looking in all directions, my eyes refusing to focus. 'By your feet.' Came the baritone reply. I cast my eyes down in the direction of the voice and between my feet sat a large gray rat. 'You're a rat?' I asked, arching my eyebrows in confusion. 'The technical term is Neotoma cinerea.', chastised the rat. 'But you can call me Simon.', he said in a most distinct English accent. 'You...you...talk?' I mused, as I tugged thoughtfully at my shaggy vomit stained mop. 'Talk? Of course I talk!' The rat said, with a chuckle. 'I can do much more than talk. I sing, I dance, I graduated from Oxford with a degree in English literature and theatre arts, my dear fellow, I assure you I'm a rodent of many talents, would you like a demonstration of such?' 'No, thats alright...' I said, raising my hand in an effort to stop him. Simon paid me no heed as he sat up on his haunches and pulled a hat and cane out from behind his back. He tossed on the black bowler hat and put both of his front paws on the cane as he leaned forward, tilting his head back as he broke into song. 'Here's to those who love not too wisely, know not wisely, but too well To the girl who sighs with envy when she hears that wedding bell.' The rat had a beautiful voice, and it just so happened that I liked Sinatra. 'To the guy who'd throw a party if he knew someone to call Here's to the losers, bless them all.' The rat, or 'Simon' as he liked to be called, did a little dance as he continued on to the next verse. 'Hey, Tom, Dick and Harry, come in out of the rain. Those torches you carry must be drowned in champagne.' I had to give Simon credit, he could carry one hell of a tune, but after waking up God knows where in a pile of my own vomit without a damn clue of how I got there, I wasn't in the mood to be serenaded by a Neotoma cinerea, I wanted one thing; that was answers. 'Simon.' I said, cutting him off. 'What the hell is going on?' 'I beg your pardon?' He asked, looking slightly hurt that I had interrupted his version of 'here's to the losers'. 'Where am I, and exactly how the hell did I get here?' I questioned. 'Why you are in St. Peter's gates of pearl cemetery.' The rat answered. 'Perhaps you came to see your friends?' The rat raised his cane and pointed behind me, I turned slowly and saw two hulking tombstones dead on. Javier Castillo, read one. Rosa Castillo', read the other. 'Javier...Rosa..?' I blinked hard, and when I opened my eyes the tombstones were still there. I looked back at Simon and blinked hard again, he was still there as well when I opened my eyes. I closed them again and with hands balled into fists I rubbed my eyes furiously only to open them to the same result. 'This can't be real...' 'Yet it is.' Simon smiled in his unusual rat-like way as he spoke. 'Now tell me...how did I get here?' I asked, shaking my head in confusion. 'One of the federales dropped you off, he didn't seem to pleased with you, you must've been quite the bad body, eh?' Simon chuckled. 'Whats your vice? Drugs? Guns? Sleeping with his wife?' 'I don't know...' I shook my head, hoping to regain some memory of what had happened. 'This federale, what did he look like?' 'I can't say chap, I didn't get much of a look at him.' Simon answered with a scowl. 'No...but I did.' Answered another voice from behind me. 'Great, what now?' I thought to myself as I turned to face the voice. 'I saw everything.' Answered a chicken...wearing a suit of armor. I rubbed my eyes again, this was getting past the point of unbelievable and diving head first into absolutely ridiculous. 'You're a...chicken?' I asked, raising an eyebrow. 'A chicken?' The feathered fowl screamed back at me. 'A bloody chicken?!' 'Yes...a chicken.' I answered. 'I'm hardly just a chicken. I'm General Mortimor Abernathy, leader of the Fighting cocks of Kentucky!' The bird snapped in arrogance. 'I'm leader of the chicken revolution!' 'Sorry I didn't know you were so important.' I offered as I slowly blinked. 'Yes, I'll show that Colonel Sanders! He and the rest of those Kentucky Fried bastards! Death to Colonel Sanders, long live the chickens!' Have you seen what those bastards do to us? Because I have and its not a pretty sight! The bird squawked loudly and flapped his armored wings defiantly. 'Right...power to the poultry.' I nodded. 'Now...what did you see?' General Abernathy's voice was a low croak. 'It was awful, first they chop our heads off...' 'No, not the chickens, what did you see tonight?' I asked, my vision growing blurry as I began to see double. 'A man.' The chicken whispered in a low, suspenseful tone. 'Hispanic, maybe five ten, 170 pounds, well built and muscular, short black hair oh...and his badge said Ruiz on it.' 'Eduardo Ruiz.' I whispered weakly. 'That fucker killed them...' 'Killed them?' Simon asked. 'I think he may have killed you as well.' 'No.' I shook my head. 'I'm okay, just a little dizzy and I don't remember anything.' Placing my palms on the terra firma I felt the chill between my hands as I pushed myself to my feet. I didn't stay there long as I quickly lost my vertical base and came crashing back to the ground in a pile of vomit, presumably my own. 'I'm just dizz...' I stopped in midsentence, rolling over onto my stomach to vomit violently expelling the contents of my stomach. I groaned loudly, wiping my mouth with the back of my hand as I vomited again. 'You've been drugged.' A female voice said. 'I...' I tried to ask her how and why, but she wouldn't know the answers to that, then again I could hardly find the strength to pose the question. But she did have the answer as to what I had been poisoned with. 'Rohypnol.' She said in her soft voice. 'They call it the date rape drug.' 'I know what it is.' I answered between ragged breaths. I had overdosed on rohypnol in Rome almost two years ago in what may or may not have been a suicide attempt. I knew the effects better than anyone else. The drug rendered you unconscious and wiped out your memory for an extended period of time, provided enough was administered the drug could potentially be lethal. 'Truth be told...it doesn't really have much of a taste.' She said with a sigh, raising my eyes I identified the speaker, it was a green fly seated astride a pile of my vomit eating the contents. 'About time I find something that doesn't taste like chicken.' She said, glancing up at General Abernathy who flapped his wings in protest. 'Fuck you right back, I hope you run into a fly swatter!' Abernathy clucked. 'Please...' I raised my head. ...help me.' 'Help you?' Asked Abernathy, 'what are we supposed to do?' 'Nothing we can do, really.', answered the fly. 'Rohypnol affects the short term memory, consciousness and in extreme cases depending upon the amount in the human body it may lead to hallucinations and further physical complications.' 'This is a hallucination, isn't it?' I asked no one in particular. I puked again, my stomach jerking violently as the bitter liquid churned from my gut and through my esophagus to hit the ground and splash back up in my face. I raised my head to at my surroundings once more. Tombstones surrounded me, slowly and ominously the tombstones opened mouths I never knew existed. From those mouths poured the voices of little girls in a hauntingly beautiful sound. 'Ring around the rosies pocket full of posies ashes, ashes we all fall down.' I felt myself slipping off into unconsciousness...or death. Whichever would take me first would be my salvation. I closed my eyes and accepted my fate. ---- March 10th, 2006. 12:18 PM I saw white tiles as my weary eyelids pulled up over my bloodshot eyes. White tiles? Where was I? Furthermore, how did I get here? 'He's awake!', a voice said excitedly. Ken...the voice belong to Ken. Turning my head I stared weakly into the baby blue eyes of my longtime friend and partner; Ken Partridge Jr. 'Josher,', Ken smiled incredulously. 'We were so worried...' 'I'm...' I wanted to say I was okay but at this point I didn't even know. 'Where am I?' 'The hospital, you had a rather unfortunate incident in Mexico.', Gunner Addison, the beautiful fiance of Ken answered softly. 'What happened?', I asked timidly. 'We were hoping you could tell us that, Josh.' Ken answered. 'You were found in a graveyard with enough Rohypnol in your body to kill you. Somehow...you're alive. The doctors say that physically your fine, but you won't remember anything thats happened over the past few days, maybe even weeks.' 'How long have I been here?' I asked Ken. 'Three days.' Answered a voice from the opposite side of the bed, I turned my eyes to see the owner of the voice; Donovan Knight, at his side stood Colton Cornell. 'You're damn lucky to be alive, kid.' 'When can I go home?', I asked, turning my focus back to Ken. 'The doctors told us that after you regained consciousness they had a few more tests to run and then you were free to go.' Ken answered with a brilliant smile. 'So Josh...', Cornell began. 'Maybe you finally have some peace of mind?' 'No.' I shook my head. 'No?' Cornell challenged. 'They were murdered.', I answered in a feeble voice. 'Javier and Rosa?', Donovan asked in apparent surprise. 'Don't be ridiculous!', sneered Cornell. 'They were murdered...' I gasped weakly. '...by a man named Eduardo Ruiz.' 'Where would you ever come up with such a preposterous idea?' Donovan asked, tugging at his collar. 'I mean, what makes you think that? What proof do you have? Do you even remember going to Mexico?' 'No, I don't remember anything.' I admitted. 'Josh...', Ken asked sympathetically. 'How do you know?' I shrugged in confusion. 'I just...know.' Across the room I heard a clap as Cornell brought his hands together killing a fly. 'Damn pests.', he muttered to himself as he dropped a green fly into a trashcan. 'Those wretched insects serve no purpose whatsoever to humanity.' ----
