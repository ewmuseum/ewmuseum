Accelerator (Part1)

16 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-16-03 'When I see her eyes, look into my eyes, when I realize, that she can see inside my head, so I close my eyes, thinkin that I could hide, I disassociate so I don't have to lose my head' 4-16-2003 / 8:02 am The sound peiced my ears undaunting my everpresent prediciment, I happened to get myself into this time. The wrapping at my hotel room door was louder and louder, I knew who it was...they found me, I thought to myself as adrinaline shot through my veins and I arose from the satin sheets. They are still after me after all of of this, I have been on the run for two months, vanished, maybe by now people like Derek Frost and Johnny Cruse were starting to wonder about me and where the great self righteous super star was in this point in time. Who was I running from? Lets take a trip back in time... 1-28-2003 / 7:22 am Pitter Patter Pitter Patter I knew the time was quickly approaching, Cross Fire...A Global Wrestling extravaganza once again set in the Empire of Japan. A mystic country with a mystic past...to many thoughts circulate my head for me to even focus myself on the task at hand, Brian Welch handed me a defeat...but Brian Welch is a fluke, the punk got lucky...he is lucky those guys from OmniTech gave me a decent chunk of change to job me and Prices titles to him...but im not about to tell anyone that Pitter Patter Pitter Patter The rain was falling hard now, I thought to myself as I continued my gaze outside the window of my hotel room in Tokyo...The street below in just another dragging day like the rest of the world. I hate fucking Japan, these people suck. My bad mood was continuing to get worse as time slowly passed me by, and thoughts continued to swirl inside my head...things eating away at me...things that do not need to be discussed right now...fuck man, I should be thrilled that he called me, I haven't talked to him in a long while. His phone call merely complicated and confused me, but what needs to be done..has to be done Pitter Patter Pitter Patter 'I really wish he wouldn't have called me....' I said aloud...as I slowly shook my head back and forth, only to drop my head into my hands and run them through my golden blonde hair. The only result of this the dismantlement of my stable, but how will I approach them with this problem? Fuck...damn you Cruse I said to myself...me and him go back to UCWF, but I will not turn on my stable to reform another stable that was killed a long time ago...but respect runs deeper than an out of control ego, I know that first hand...but I knew from the beginning the MHC was nothing but a kamikaze plane destined for demise. By egos I mean Sean Price, I don't anyone knows right now...but Price only joined my stable for his own betterment...not the betterment of The Mile High Club Pitter Patter Pitter Patter The conversation I had with Johnny kept replaying inside my head, and I knew the words he told me were the truth, he wouldn't lie to me...I hope he wouldn't lie to me, but I have no other choice than to believe his words were pure. 'They are comming for you Jay...if they ask you to come with them...refuse...and run away as fast as you can' The sentence kept playing over and over in my head. I was not one to run away from anything, but if it is true what Johnny told me...then im going to run my ass off, I will not let then do to me what they did to Davis Knock...Knock My baby blue eyes opened up and I shot my head around, I already knew who it was...I grabbed my black eight ball YB t-shirt and pulled it over my head and slowly ventured toward the door Knock...Knock I slowly pulled the door open...there standing before me were two huge secret service lookin guys, black glasses, black suits and ties, and white ear pieces in the right ear... 'Yea...?' I said cautiously, as I looked over both of them with my baby blue eyes waiting for a reply from one of them 'Jay Robson...' The one closest to me said in a low uniformed voice 'Yea...' I once again said with a confused look on my face 'You need to come with us...Mr. Aldante wants to see you' The one closest to me said, once again in the same low uniformed voice as before. At that moment terror surged through my veins, Johnny Cruses voice was inside my head. terror quickly turned into adrenaline as one of them grabbed me by the left fore arm. I didn't think twice, I balled up my right fist and threw a punch as hard as I could and broke his black glasses, the guy stumbled back a fet feet wincing in pain as the other one made a move to grab me, but I side stepped him and pushed him through the doorway of my hotel room 'Run...Jay' Cruses voice kept repeating over and over inside my head as I reached the stairwell door and pushed it open. I was breathing heavily not because I was tired, but because I was afraid, I was afraid of what they were planning to do with me...The stairwell was quiet, no sound but that from my brown boots trampling downward, and then I heard the door from a few flights up burst open. I paused for a moment and looked upwards, I only seen two figures dressed in black, but that was all the confirmation I need to keep moving The first floor was quickly approaching, I slammed the metal door open only to be met with a man that looked the same as the ones that were chasing me. Instinctively I tackled him to the ground before he could get his bearing on me, I quickly shot back up and continued running knocking a small Japanese man over in the process, I stumbled a bit but regained my balance and darted through the lobby. I glanced around to now see three men dressed in black suits in pursuit...I pushed the entrance door to the lobby open Outside the rain was still falling hard and quickly saturated my hair and shoulders with precipitation as I looked let down the sidewalk to see nothing but different colored umbrellas being held up by short Japanese people...I looked to my right and spotted two more black suited men standing under a single black umbrella near a black Lincoln car. They spotted me and pointed in my direction, I turned back to my left and started in a dead sprint though the crowd of umbrellas...I bounced off someone which spun me around, as i turned I saw the two men now chasing after me, stampeding through the crowds of walking people What am I going to do now? I thought to myself as I continued to run, I was getting tired my legs were starting to hurt. It felt like someone was driving a hot knife in my side as I came up on an intersection and another crowd of Japanese people using umbrellas to shield themselves from the rain, I broke through the crowd and stopped on the edge of the curb as a bus flew by me. I wasn't about to run out and play in traffic so I turned around only to see my pursuers gaining ground on me fast Shit...im trapped, as that thought crossed my mind a black limousine with neon green lightning bolts down the side screeched to a stop in front of me, the tinted window slowly came down and a voice erupted from inside 'Jay...Get in...quick' The voice was familiar...I knew who it was, so I pulled the door open and hopped inside. The limo sped off just as the guys in black suits reached the intersection 'What the fuck Johnny?' I said once inside the limo, I turned around and looked out the back window to see a few black suited guys standing in the street looking at us as a few Japanese people crossed the street behind them. I turned back around and leaned back in the seat water ran down my cheeks as my wet shirt stuck to my body , I turned my baby blue eyes and focused them on a man in a white suit and sandy blonde hair, his eyes covered by a pair of silver glasses. He took one last sip of bourbon from a glass as looked over at me...this man is known world wide as Johnny Cruse, the creator of the Young Bloods, a faction that has been long dead in GW, at the hands of me and Ben Palithorpe 'Nice to see you to Jay...' He replied to me after my last vulgar comment, I pulled the wet shirt off my chest and dropped it on the floor of the limo and looked at Johnny once again 'Thanks man..you really saved my ass' I said to him as I wiped the water from my face and once again sat back in the seat 'Certainly not the first time, and probably not the last' Johnny said as I looked at him. This was the great thing about Johnny Cruse he is a smart man and always has the solution to any problem anyone may happen to have, he taught me every thing I needed to learn about becoming a great superstar in UCWF/GWF/GW, and he did it to, but I fucked up, it was my fault, it was the untimely death of his wife Valerie and my ego that forced this man out of the federation so long ago, I crushed the only other thing he loved besides his wife...and that was the stable known as the Young Bloods 'Im sorry Johnny...' I said in a sympathetic voice, as the thoughts of his wife’s death and the break up of the YB's, continued to haunt me as everything was now starting to cool off 'For what?' He replied in a confused voice as he poured another glass of bourbon 'For everything that I fucked up...for every arrogant and egotistical remark I may have made in the past...for fucking up the Young Bloods' I said reluctantly as I dropped my head down into my head and started to cry. Cruse reached over and began to pat my back 'Jay...its ok man, that was in the past, if I didn't want to help you I wouldn't have called you and warned you about Aldante's goons, I don't want them to do to you what they have done to countless others, your my brah, even if you did fuck up in the past, but that is where it needs to stay...in the past' I raised up and once again sunk back into the black leather seat and again looked at Johnny, who now had his glasses off and was looking at me with a serious face 'I don't know what OmniTech is trying to do in GW, but im going to find out..and I want you to help me' Cruse said in a serious voice as he looked over at me 'Really?...You want my help?' I said to Johnny with a confused look as I wiped a tear from under my eye 'Thats right...TLM and the Hot Stuff, running side by side once again under the Young Blood banner, just like old times' Johnny said, I could sense the excitement in his voice, but I was unsure, so I just nodded back to him not saying anything as I began to think about Dynasty, Cash, and Price and what they would think of this new alliance between me and Cruse, I created the Mile High Club, I recruited all three of the members of my stable. The pressure of the whole situation was starting to get to me, Aldante goons on my trail, Cruse wanting me to join him on some crusade to save Global Wrestling, and the consequences of my actions if I do decide to join my mentor in his fight against OmniTech 'Jay...' Johnny's voice snapped me out of my own little world I was falling into as glanced back over to him not knowing what to do, but I was going to have to make a choice, reform an old alliance or kill the MHC, but I couldn't let Johnny down again, and like I said before the MHC is a sinking ship only destined for the bottom of the ocean, and that is where I found my answer to this troubling question 'Ok' I said in a soft voice as I closed my eyes 'What?' Johnny said after a sip of buron and a confused voice 'Ok...I will do it' I said once again this time louder than before with butterflies now churning in my stomach for I know I have wrote the end to the stable I worked so hard to reform in the MHC 'Alright, sounds good' Johnny said in a normal voice as he nodded his head slightly as the ride to somewhere became silent Present OmniTech...the people who have made my life a living hell for the past months, something has to give, something has to change. I stood up in nothing but my white boxer shorts, I turned towards the in table next to the bed and picked up a pistol, Colt 45. to be exact, I quietly slid a bullet into the chamber and walked towards the door, gun at my side, I could hear my heart beating as the door knob came closer and closer, I reached down and grasp the cold brass and swung the door open....
