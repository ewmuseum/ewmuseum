Hurt

4 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 4th, 2003 I did eighty the entire way to the hospital, guilt and remorse crushing my foot into the pedal. As the city flew by me, I felt everything I'd thought was important -- the club, titles, victory -- peeling away. None of that mattered in the face of what had happened. None of that mattered in the face of what I, however unintentionally, had done. In minutes, I was screaming into the hospital parking lot. I slid into a spot and slammed on the parking brake, leaping out of the car and rushing into the hospital. Once inside, I took a moment to compose myself, and a brief flirtatious chat with the girl at the desk later, I was in the elevator, headed for Star's floor. What was I going to do when I saw her? What would I say? How do you apologize for something like this? Should you even bother? The questions rampaged through my head, filling me with an uncertainty I hadn't known in years. This was a situation I never could have dreamed I'd have to deal with -- but I had to figure out how to deal with it, and I had to do it fast. A ding, and the elevator doors slid open. Taking a deep breath, I stepped out into the hallway, and began to walk. The sound of my own footsteps rung in my ears, and it seemed as if I was moving agonizingly slow -- I was trying so hard to control myself, to avoid panicking, to strangle my emotions into check? Suddenly, I was there. Late as it was, her door was locked and her lights were out, but there was a window from the hallway, and the curtains weren't pulled shut. She lay there, unmoving and silent. Was she sleeping peacefully? Or was she having nightmares of the child she'd lost? With a sigh, I let my forehead rest against the glass and closed my eyes. 'I'm sorry,' I muttered, though no one was around to hear me. 'I'm so, so sorry, Star. Nobody deserves something like this, not even you and Ice? and it's my fault. I didn't want it to happen, I didn't mean to do it? but it's my fault. And I'm going to carry that around with me for the rest of my life. I know you can't hear me, and I don't know what it would mean to you, if anything? but I hope it's at least a small consolation. I? I guess I just had to say that. I know I can't say anything, do anything to make this right. You hate me I'm sure, and you probably always will. But--' 'You're damn right we do.' I lifted my head off the glass and opened my eyes -- only to see a pair of Icy blue ones reflected next to mine. Slowly, I turned around. 'Ice, I?' But words meant nothing now. He howled with fury, his fist colliding with my jaw, staggering me back. I simply stood there, mute, unresponsive. I deserved this. Ice grabbed my head, slamming it into the window, and a latticework of cracks appeared from the impact. Pain raced through my skull as he slammed it into the glass again, then threw me back into the opposite wall. Still howling, he kicked me in the stomach, then slammed fist after fist into my midsection. And all the while, I took it. I couldn't bring myself to lift a finger against him, monster of anger and hate though he was -- because I had taken the most precious thing he could ever have away. Ice grabbed me by the hair, and threw me to the ground, my head bouncing off the floor. 'You son of a bitch!' he screamed, throwing himself on top of me and wrapping his hands around my throat. 'I'll kill you! You killed my child, you bastard! YOU KILLED MY CHILD!!' Ice's grip only tightened, and as I struggled for breath, a thought shot through my angst and guilt: Regardless of what I'd done, I didn't want to die. Not here. Not now. Drawing on my reserves of strength, I smashed my fist into the side of his head. His grip didn't waver. Again and again, I struck his temples, but it seemed that nothing would shake him, as his eyes bored into mine with more fury and hatred and pain than I had ever seen before. Everything began to darken, the world swimming around me. Maybe I deserved this. Maybe?. Suddenly, I could breathe again. The darkness slipped away, and I felt myself being lifted off the ground. My eyes opened, and I saw Ice being dragged off of me, half a dozen orderlies barely enough to restrain him. Two other orderlies were helping me to my feet, I realized, and ushering me back towards the elevator. 'I'LL DESTROY YOU, LION,' screamed Ice, as the elevator doors closed, 'I'M GOING TO MAKE YOU HURT LIKE YOU'VE NEVER KNOWN!!' Then the doors were closed, and the sound fell away. For a moment, everything was silent. Finally, one of the orderlies spoke up. 'Hey man,' he said, 'we're gonna get a doctor to check you out. But I think you're gonna be okay.' I slumped against the wall of the elevator, letting my eyes fall closed. I could still feel his hands around my throat, feel all that hatred, that darkness, pouring out of him and mingling with my own guilt, and together, trying to crush me. 'No,' I said, 'I don't know if I'll be okay again for a long time?'
