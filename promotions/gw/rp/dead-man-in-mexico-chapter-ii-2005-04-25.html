Date: April 25th, 2005. Dead man in Mexico: Chapter II - Truth. ---- Juarez, Mexico. April 25th, 2005. 7:18 PM. The house was rather large and luxurious, a palace compared to the other homes of Juarez, yet simply an above average house in America. It was a place that I could live comfortably in, yet a billionaire like Donald Trump wouldn't even use as a guest bedroom. Aztecan rugs featuring beautifully woven images of gods and statues covered the hardwood floors, the walls bore large windows and random paintings, my personal favorite; a black canvas with a crescent moon and a dotting of stars in the background. I sat upon a crimson couch, by appearance I looked calm but one only had to look further to see the stress I was under, the first clue would be the vice-like grip that I had on the wooden arm of the sofa. The second clue would be my attire. While I did look relaxed as I lounged comfortably in ragged jeans, black Chuck's and a jade t-shirt featuring the words: 'I was the longest reigning World Champion in Global Wrestling history and all I got was this lousy t-shirt', genuine American Youth sarcasm at its finest. As routine as my attire was, there was one clue that pointed to my current state of mind and that was my face, or rather...what I wore upon my face. No, the shaggy locks falling into my features weren't a clue, no my hoop earrings provided no evidence but the key observation would be the black shades pulled over my bluish gray eyes. I hadn't removed them since I left Seattle. I also hadn't shaved since I had left Seattle and the growth on my face only made me look even more haggard and tired than I really was. Seated next to me on the couch was my strikingly beautiful girlfriend, her right hand clutching my left, her strength seemed to account for my weakness and somehow, almost as if she had willed me to continue on, I felt her strength in me. I glanced over at her and gave her a smile, it wasn't easy but I managed to do it nonetheless. What I called a smile could probably be more accurately deemed a weak attempt at pulling up the corners of my lips, but it was the best I could pull off at this point. Gazing upon her features my mind slipped away from the tragedy to dance down the halls of hope for a moment. Her big brown eyes bore sympathy in them and her black hair fell across her face and spilled onto her white long sleeved tee. She squeezed my hand as our eyes met, she couldn't see my eyes beneath the shades yet she seemed to be able to read my mind, or rather my heart as she saw the sorrow spilling forth. A voice broke the silence, snapping me back from hope and dropping me straight into tragedy. 'Last night, around 2 AM is when the officer stumbled across the crash scene. Apparently another driver was involved yet we have no record of whom.' The voice, thick with a Mexican accent, belonged to Juan Padilla who sat across from me in a chair of matching crimson. He wore a white business suit with a black shirt, black shoes and a missing tie. 'No idea?' I asked, punctuating my sentence with a heavy hearted sigh. 'No idea. When officer Ruiz arrived at the scene all he found was a destroyed Lexus in flames.' Juan's voice was low yet the message rang loud in my eardrums. 'So, we have no way of knowing what happened? We have no way of knowing who the other driver was?' I asked again, I already knew the answer was going to be 'no', yet I had to ask in hope that I would get some kind of answer that would pacify me. 'I've spoken with officer Ruiz, he stated both verbally and in his police report that when he arrived at the scene they were both dead, no signs of any other vehicles, no signs of foul play.' Juan's eyes were locked on the floorboards by his feet, he seemed to be taking this as hard as I was yet since Javier had left him in charge of the school we both knew that he would have to keep things together regardless of how hard it was. I sighed again as I spoke. 'I'm sorry to keep bringing this up, but Juan, it just doesn't sound logical. A man and his wife die in a flaming inferno, no other vehicle is found and the cops just turn the other cheek? Javier had a wife, a son.' 'Josh, I know how you feel, but remember this is Mexico. The police here are very corrupt, the government is corrupt, the almighty dollar is the iron fist that controls Mexico. I agree that it hardly sounds logical, yet what can we do my friend?' Juan raised his hands, palms up, signifying that he could do nothing. 'I don't know, but I know that I'm going to do something.' I said, shaking my head in disbelief. Juan shrugged, he knew my mind was made and after having spent time training with me he knew how stubborn I could be at times. 'I echo your sentiments my friend, but this is Mexico, you won't get far with politics and diplomacy.' 'What's the answer, Juan?' I asked, looking for a way to connect with the surrounding Mexican culture. 'I think you know that as well as I do, Josh. In Mexico there is only one answer...money.' Juan's face bore a look of sincerity as he spoke but he didn't have to convince me, he was right I already knew that the answer was money. 'So be it.' Came my adamant response. 'If money is what it takes...I just re-upped with Global Wrestling, snared a cute signing bonus in the process. Lanier fired me from Core and he had to pay out my contract to get rid of me, so at this point I'm more financially stable than I've ever been.' 'But Josh, we were talking about buying a house in Las Ve...' Raki interrupted, before I swiveled my head to shoot her a look that closed her mouth before the sentence had fully matured. 'I don't care who I have to pay, or how much I have to pay, one way or another I'm getting to the bottom of this.' My eyes were still locked on Raki as I said this, Juan rose from his chair, ready to exit the room. It was clear that he didn't wish to get trapped in the middle of what was looking like another nasty fight. 'I'm sure you're both tired, please, this is a very stressful situation, I'm going to leave the two of you for the night.' Juan said, speaking in a polite manner as he walked towards the open door on the other side of the room. I didn't say anything, a nod and grunt was the best that I could do as I watched Juan walk away, he pulled the door closed behind him and then the chaos began. 'Don't ever do that again.' Raki snapped, her dark skin turning red with anger. 'What?' I retorted, staring back at her through my shades. 'Cut me off like that.' Raki seethed. 'What's the deal Josh? Are we going to buy a house back in Vegas now that you've returned to Global or are you going to blow your future on a wild goose chase, bribing police officers and drunks to tickle your ears?' 'That's enough, Ra.' I answered, my voice firm and strong, it was the first time I had ever used a strict tone with her and by the look of shock that came over her face she was clearly surprised. The shock lasted momentarily before fading away into anger. 'Don't get an attitude with me, Joshua.' Raki spat out, jerking her hand away from mine. 'Joshua.' The name echoed in my ears, she knew how much I hated being called that and she was calling me that just to antagonize me. When I'm angry my skin always seems to stay the same shade, although oddly enough my ears turn a bright red color, at this point they were glowing. 'Ok, just what is your problem?' I snapped back, my anger increasing. 'All you've done for the past 2 weeks is rag on me non-stop, you need to drop the attitude, woman.' Inwardly, despite my anger, I couldn't help but chuckle at the term 'woman', I knew that she hated it as much as I hated being called Joshua, she responded accordingly. 'Better question? What is YOUR problem? Josh, I don't have a problem. You are the one neglecting his responsibilities. Look at what you're doing. You're talking about blowing our nest egg on this stupid conspiracy theory, in the meanwhile, what about your career?' Raki was becoming more perturbed by the second and dragging me down the same path. 'You have a big second round match against Silvio Fiore this week for CW2, back in Global the biggest event of the year is coming up and you may not even be on the show, what are you doing?' That did it, now I was incensed and my response told her just that. 'First off, I am better than Silvio Fiore on my worst day, he's a no-talent hack that I'm going to beat within an inch of his life. Secondly, Global Wrestling will always have a spot on it's show for Josh because I'm a freakin' legend and last but certainly not least: this is MY career, not yours. Lets not even mention the fact that I owe this to Javier, I have a debt to pay that you...you just wouldn't understand...' What came next was hardly predictable as she slapped me hard across the face; drawing blood as she cut open my top lip. I winced, almost lost my shades and raised my left hand to my mouth, wiping at the blood with the tips of my fingers. 'Don't you ever talk to me like that again Joshua, or you'll get worse than that.' Raki said with a hateful glare. 'I'm here to help you, to support you, you can either take it or leave it.' 'If you call this support...', I said, looking at the blood on my fingers. ...then I'll pass.' 'I hope this couch is more comfortable than the one back in Seattle Josh, because you just won yourself a free night on the couch.' Raki spat out as she jumped up from the couch and stomped away in anger, entering the nearby bedroom and slamming the door behind her. I licked at the blood on my lips before standing to my feet, walking across the room I pulled open a door that led out into the hall and walked down the dimly lit hall in search of a restroom. It had been a long time since I had been in the house but I seemed to recall where most things were. The house consisted of 5 bedrooms, Javier had always stayed in the master bedroom and his students had been stationed amidst the other four. The bathroom was somewhere in the middle of the hall, with a little difficulty I found it, turned the knob and stepped inside as I flipped on the light. It hadn't changed. A small sink, toilet, shower and a brown tile floor made up most of the bathroom. I pulled the door shut behind me as I stepped in front of the mirror hanging over the sink and stared at my reflection, talk about a sight for sore eyes. I removed my shades for a better look at my haggard, whiskered and now bleeding face. My eyes were bloodshot from the tears I had shed and I could only imagine how ugly the reflection of my heart would be if I could have looked into it. Heavy with the unmade confessions and the sorrow surrounding my friends death. As I looked into the mirror I saw something behind me, the shower curtain was moving, spinning around I jerked it open...nothing. Nothing but memories. ---- I was 18, a luchadore wannabe, just another white kid with high school level Spanish and high hopes that had traveled across the border in hopes of becoming a superstar. This was far before the tattoos, the gold, the glory, the girls, the money, the fame, the prestige. This was long before I became 'American Youth' Josh, in fact I was a wet behind the ears rookie with big dreams and a small chance at success. I thought I was cool with my long hair, yellow trunks, newly acquired mask and a grasp of what Lucha Libre was. I had only been training for eight months but it was long enough for people to take notice, Javier said I had potential and pointed to me as an example of how a Luchadore should train. It had been a busy day in the gym and Javier had taken his graduating class of Luchadores out to the local Cantina for a celebration before releasing them into the World of Wrestling to fend for themselves. I had been one of the graduates, yet I didn't drink so I passed on the offer and opted for a night alone in sweet reflection, or at least I had thought I was alone when I stepped into the bathroom. My heart was light, spirits high and dreams running wild as I stepped into the bathroom on that February night in 2000. As I stepped into the bathroom I had taken a few steps across the tile before I looked up into a pair of big brown eyes, filled with shock. My eyes widened as well, in front of me was a beautiful and completely naked woman. She was 27 and drop-dead gorgeous with her curly black hair, brown eyes, full lips and a body that was as good as anything I had ever seen at that point in my life and still tops most of what I've seen to this day. Ahhh!' I shouted in surprise. 'I'm sorry!' I exclaimed, throwing my arms up and turning around to walk out the door, before she could even react I had exited the bathroom and closed the door behind me. I quickly walked down the hall to my room and threw open the door, stepping inside. I wore only a pair of gym shorts on my half-clad body as I dropped onto my bed, burying my face in my hands in embarrassment. My face was red, I had just walked in on Javier's wife, Rosa, as she had stepped out of the shower. This would be an interesting story to tell, but one I thought I'd keep to myself for the sake of Rosa's honor and my own integrity. Nonetheless I couldn't get her body out of my mind, wow, she was smoking hot! 'Josh?', the voice asked, as a hand knocked lightly on my door. 'Yes, I'm sorry, Rosa.' I said, still embarrassed as I heard the door open. 'Don't be embarrassed Josh, we're both adults, you don't have anything to be ashamed of. It was an accident, accidents happen.' Rosa's voice was gentle, soothing, it relaxed me enough to sit up in bed, although my face was still buried in my hands. 'I'm so sorry Rosa, I really didn't mean to walk in on you, I swear I had no idea you were in there.' I shook my head as I said this, still refusing to look at her. She closed the door behind her and giggled lightly. 'It's okay Josh, I know that you didn't mean top do anything like that. Your a nice guy, a good kid. The shower in my bathroom was broken so I used that one and I guess I forgot to lock the door.' Her voice was relaxing yet I still couldn't bear to raise my eyes to meet hers. 'Josh, look at me.' She demanded. 'We haven't done anything wrong...yet.' Her sentence was ended by the sound of a lock being turned as she locked my door. Looking up at her I saw her standing in front of me, her body was soaking wet and naked. My jaw dropped, my eyes widened as I stood to my feet, I wasn't on my feet long as she pushed me back onto the bed. 'So innocent, have I ever mentioned that innocence is a turn on?' She fell on top of me as she spoke, putting her fingers in the waistband of my shorts. 'Rosa, we can't...what if Javier...' I began to speak but stopped when I discovered her tongue in my mouth. I could handle it no longer, grabbing her by the buttocks I rolled over on the bed... ---- I jerked the curtain closed as quickly as I had opened it, my hands were shaking, my entire body trembling as these memories haunted me. Spinning around to face the sink I turned the faucet on and dipped my hands beneath the water as I quickly splashed my face with the cool aqua. I splashed my face again and again trying to wash away the memories, trying to wash away my sins and return to innocence...it didn't work and I fell to my knees, the faucet still flowing. 'God, I'm sorry...' I cried out, the tears returning to my bloodshot eyes. 'I couldn't help myself. I knew I would regret it one day but I never knew it would be like this, I never knew things would turn out this way.' I banged a fist against the tile, my tears coming down even harder. 'Forgive me Father, I know I've sinned. Clear my heart, my mind and my soul, wash my conscience clean. I'm ready to be a man, I'm ready to accept my responsibilities, I'm ready to turn my life around. I made mistakes in my foolishness and I still make them today. Forgive me Father...forgive me...' My voice had degenerated into a hoarse whisper, my throat parched from emotional exhaustion. Kneeling there on the floor, head bowed, hands on the floor I realized what my responsibility was. His name was Espen Castillo, 4 years old and full of life. Everyone needed to know the truth about Espen, not today, not tomorrow, but someday...the world would learn the truth. ----
