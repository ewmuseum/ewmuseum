Blood is thicker than hatred

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'After all is said and done, above all else there will be one thing that I am remember for more than anything. I will be remembered for being a Davis.' - Salaok Davis ---- His eyes were cold. Cold and pure white like the snow covered land of Antartica. Much like the way most people remembered his father's eyes. Not that they would say it to Salaok's face as there was very few people alive, or dead that he loathed as much as his father. It was not the fact that Chris Davis had faked his own death and kept his life a secret from his family, nor was it the fact that he had joined himself with Justin Marks in attempt to try to bring down Salaok's reign of terror with The Lair. No The reason for Salaok's hatred for his father was the half brother that he had produced and inturn, caused the death of Lisha. Chris Davis hadn't been able to keep his hands off another woman while with Cheryl Davis and when the child was born, the fate of the future was to be sent then and there. One family. One blood. So many different levels of hatred. Mani Shigeru, the half brother of Salaok and bastard son of Chris Davis didn't understand what Salaok, Johnathon and Eve understood and nor would he ever. He hadn't lived the life that they had, hadn't been apart of the wars which had taken place over the years while he was growing up in another land. He would have heard the same stories and been preached to about the same ways of life that Chris had tried to install into the minds of his three legitimate childred and yet failed. 'Never follow that which is evil.' They were the words that Chris Davis would tell his children, and yet they would group up to learn things weren't always able to go that in in life. In fact, all three would see things from different point of views, and yet all end up on the same page reading the same final line. Johnathon was the first to level behind his father's teachings and it was his corruption, as his father called it that would then spread like wildfire throughout the family and into the blood of his siblings. But Mani had been kept away from these happening and therefore, all he ever knew was the teachings of his father and knowing his views against The Lair, Mani would be the one that would see the future end up in his hands. Had it all been planned to go like that from day one? Did each member of The Davis family have a purpose, each one more destructive than the last? But now, each member of the family stands alone in different parts of the world, not knowing what the other is doing, not wanting to know what had become of their once happy family. Those who had watched from outside of the family had seen it fall apart much like so many had watched as The World Trade Centre crumbled to the ground. It had not been a small event, not had it been one that was quiet and easily forgotten about by those who have seen it unfold. But with The Davis family, there had always been one sibling who had tried their best to stay out of the wars, out of the fury that her brother's had lived their lives through. Eve Davis. She had in Salaoks eyes made a grave mistake the day that she married Justin Marks but he had forgiven her for that as he knew that love was something you couldn't control. His love that he shared with Lisha had proved that to him despite what the two of them were told by Durian Orthane so many years ago now. But with Eve was the only remaining member of the next generation of Davis blood at this point in time in the form of little Emmanuell. The son of Justin Marks. And as he stood at the door of her house, he struggles to bring himself to push the door bell, knowing the rift that had been caused between them due to the hatred that Justin and Salaok shared for each other. He simply stares at the door, raising his hand to push the bell and then pulling it back down again, fearfull of his sisters reaction of seeing him. Salaok and Eve hadn't seen each other since the night in which Lisha had been killed by their half brother and the idea of sitting down and talking to her about it wasn't something high on Salaok's things to do list. In fact, it was the last thing in which Salaok was wanting to talk about these days as Orthane was trying to help him move on from the situation as it was destroying his life and mind more importantly. Inch by inch his hand rose up and as his finger extended, the front door swings open much to Salaok surprise as he steps back slightly and turns to look into the eyes of the woman standing before him. Before he can take another step backwards, Eve lunges forward and warps her arms around Salaok's massive chest never wanting to let go again. His chin rests's on the top of her head, his goatee blending into her white hair which as always smelt fresh and clean. With her face buried in his chest, Eve begins to speak, although Salaok had wished she never had as the silence was exactly what he was needing as the embrace showed no signs of letting up. 'I've been waiting Salaok. For a year and a half I've waited to see you, unable to bring myself to come to The Nevermore Mansion, and unable to pick up the phone. So I waited for you to come to me.' Her voice was muffled slightly as she pushed her head firming into Salaok's black shirt and Salaok simply nodded, understanding what was said, without it even needing to be said in the first place. The bond between Salaok and Eve had always been stronger than the one between Salaok and Johnathon due to the fact that while they were growing up, Johnathon hadn't been around much as Youth Detention Centers served to be Johnathon's home more so than the house the rest of his family lived in. With Johnathon not around, Salaok and Eve had been through most things together during the period in which they were growing up. The walks home from school together. The car rides to visit their grandparents. But no dates was ever so dominating as the ones where death were involved. Eve had been in the background, watching as Johnathon had killed the young boy in the school yard for Salaok. She had been still running up the steps when Salaok had found his father in what was thought to be his death bed. And now with age, she had been there at the table when Lisha was shot in front of their very eyes. Without saying another word, Eve looks up at Salaok and tilts her head towards the door motioning for him to go inside. With her hand holding his, almost pulling him inside the old, yet very modest house, Eve doesn't give Salaok a chance to back out despite the knowing that he was wanting to. It hadn't been hard for her to know that he was there as his car is easily spotted and it had been a long time since anyone had bothered to drive up their driveway and bother getting out. She had waited on the other side of the door as he continuously tried to convince himself to ring the door bell and each time his hand would near it, she would hold her breath in waiting to see her brother again. But her egerness had gotten the better of her and now the two of them stand in the middle of the foyar looking at each other like two old friends meeting again for the first time in years, unable to come to grips with the amount both had changed. Although the situation which had caused them to spend such a period of time away from each other had been a sad one, they both now shared a smile, happy to be in each other view once again. 'You've all I've got left Eve. Everything that I care for now stands with me in this room.' His voice so honest that although still smiling, a small tear begins to well up in her eye as she listens to his words. 'I don't want to lose you Eve. I'll never let anything happen to you as long as I live.' She knew that deep down, he honestly thought that was true. He wanted to believe that it was the case that he wouldn't let anything happen to her but the truth was, he had just gone well over a year without seeing her so how could he possibly protect her. She knew that as long as there was money and power in the world, she would always be second. 'Salaok, you say that and you want to believe that, but as long as you're alive, so is the legacy of The Lair. It has been through the Lair that this family has been torn apart over the years and as long as you live, you'll fight to keep it alive because it is you. I'm not asking you to give up on your beliefs in The Lair, but be realistic about who you are brother.' Eve pauses, just for a brief moment as her words have left Salaok standing a few feet away from her, confused at her approach. 'I know that should I be in any danager that you will be there to protect me, I don't doubt that for a second. Just remember that your Lair... well, it doesn't sit well with them that I married Justin. Nor does it sit at all with you.' Salaok tries to remain calm despite the fact she raises the topic of her husbane to the man in whom he had tried to kill. With his teeth grinding heavily, he begins to talk through them, not hiding his annoyance well at all. 'You were warned what would happen should you marry Justin and just as I told you, it caused a rift in this family. I still love you Eve, but your unity with Justin is one that strikes at my heart, much like he did when I saw our father standing next to him as he nailed me to the cross.' 'And what? You just expect me to listen to your warnings before of your position in The Lair? I am not apart of that world Salaok, remember that. But even my knowledge of the people in it would have had me listening to Durian when it was told that you would lose your first wife to a blood member. Only difference is that the warning Durian gave was from site, your's was from nothing more than spite and hatred.'Eve fires back, not willing to allow Salaok to gain a firey upper hand in this conversation that wasn't meant to have gotten to this stage. 'Enough with this childishness. We are both bigger than this and the mere fact that you share the same blood line as I, is why I am here. You are my sister, and should anything happen to you, I will kill those responcible with hesitation none.' A figure appears from a doorway behind Eve and catches Salaok's attention, but had that not, then the voice would have. 'You're not the only one who would take such actions dear Salaok.' The voice of Justin Marks was sickening to Salaok and the two men stood there, neither willing nor wanting to remove their gaze from the other in a distant power struggle to see who would back down first. The silence and tension was the same as always between Salaok and Justin, thick enough to cut with a knife. Or chainsaw. This however was his family now, and like it or not, this is what he would nor vow to protect.
