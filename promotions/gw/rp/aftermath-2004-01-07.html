Aftermath

7 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/7/04 The final piece in the puzzle had fallen into place. He had suspected the identity of his stalker for some time, since the first message he got right before Showdown in Tokyo. This past Evolution only proved his point. Of course, it was no reason to celebrate. It was Terminator after him, after all. He and Pitbull Pete Hardy had attacked Brick and Jesse Briggs during their street fight at the Badgers Gas Station across from the Global Arena. Because of the stalking, Brick’s heart wasn’t truly in the match, at least not until Briggs prodded him into it. As the match was starting to reach a climax, Terminator and Hardy ambushed the two, hanging Brick from a pole by his neck with a chain and beating him with a bat. Briggs attempted to rush the two, but was met with a baseball bat to the skull, knocking him out and busting him open. “They’re right here!” a voice called out. The voice belonged to Christina McCoy. Ely Braxton and a team of paramedics followed close behind. They ran to where Brick and Briggs lay unconscious or worse. Immediately, the paramedics went to work, attempting to resuscitate the two fallen wrestlers. “Come on, you guys. Come on.” This was one of the most brutal attacks Brick had ever suffered. There were severe bruises around his throat from where Terminator and Hardy hanged him. There were also bruises on the trunk of his body and perhaps some cracked ribs. Granted, it wasn’t the first time someone tried to hang him. Brick has suffered attacks from the Nevermore, which had some of the most sadistic individuals in the history of GW as its members. But some of his most extreme matches were with Terminator. The Alley Fight in Berlin at Whiplash “01. The Empty Arena Match at Master of the Mat II. The Three Stages of Hell match at Genesis in 2002. Images of these matches flooded through Brick’s unconscious mind. “One! Two! Three! Four! Five!” one paramedic counted loudly as he applied pressure to Brick’s lungs while another used mouth-to-mouth. Meanwhile, two more paramedics were checking on Jesse Briggs, seeing if they could rouse him up. One cleaned the wound on Jesse’s forehead “Come on,” Ely murmured as he watched the paramedics work on Brick and Jesse Then hope came. Brick began to cough “Oh, thank God,” Christina sighed. Brick started to sit up quickly. “Take it easy, Brick.” Brick groaned as he shifted himself. “Was it him?” “Yeah, it was, and he wasn’t alone.” “I knew it! I’m gonna kill that mother’augh!” “Take it easy, hoss. You’re still a little tender from that beating.” “I’ve taken worse. He’s taken worse and he’s gonna take worse before I’m done!” “Brick, take it easy! I know you’d like nothing better than to get your hands on Terminator--' “Around his throat actually. Then I’d just like to squeeze and squeeze--' “Look, Brick, you need to rest and you need to think. I’m pretty sure Jesse would like to even the score, too. So I have a little proposal.” On proposal, a smile crept on her face. Brick was familiar with this smile. It was the same smile she had on her face when she named him the special guest referee in a match where Deacon Holmes would be defending the two titles he held at the time. When she smiled that smile, Brick knew she was going to do something to make him extremely happy. It also meant that Michael Page could kiss his ass goodbye.
