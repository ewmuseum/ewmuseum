And Darkness Descends...Redemption

7 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-7-2004 The first snow of the year always makes The Land more beautiful. Here and there, game tracks can be seen dotting the surface of the fresh powder. The trees, bare of leaves before, have now gained new growth of white. They stand regally, although in this part of the Land, they are not very close together. Not a very good place to seek cover if you were the type to seek cover. The rider would have blended in with the snowy landscape were it not for a few small things that made him stick out’His snowy white cloak obscured most of him, yet his black boots protruded from below, followed by bits of his black pants. His horse, also a snowy color, would disappear if not for the saddle, the bridle, the rider?.Ok, so maybe he doesn’t blend in very well at all. Blend in, fit in; however you slice it, it matters not, because Pendragon doesn’t really do either’Ever. Xerxes crunches along, eyes fierce, but otherwise he seems alright with the setting. Pendragon, on the other hand, does not. As we get closer to the pair, man and horse, we see that Pendragon’s seem to look everywhere at once. His right hand rests upon the hilt of his blade. Always cautious, Pendragon is no different even in the serene landscape laid bare before him. “This is not a meeting I wish to have. I had hoped that mine own homeland would be clean of the evil that I have been set to eradicate, yet here I am’With a nice headache to boot. Crossing planes always gets me like that. I can remember the winters here from my early boyhood’What once was dark has been illumined for me by Bob. Bob’Now there is an enigma. He claims mine friendship, yet it appears I always end up doing what he wants. He makes it seem as though it is what I want as well, yet that is another point. Perhaps it is wrong of me to question him; after all, he is the creator. Yet sometimes, this all seems like it’s too much?” Xerxes had been plodding steadily onward, and in the distance, a shape seems to appear out of the white: white ground, white sky. All white. The dark shape growing ahead is unmistakable. A house. From this distance, it looks warm’Smoke rises in a thin stream from a chimney that we cannot make out yet, but we see the smoke. Pendragon slows as he nears the humble appearing abode. A house of wood, with wood piled high beneath the eaves of the porch, it is the picture of what we today would think of as country living. In what would normally be the yard, Pendragon dismounts’The snow crunches beneath his soft boots and he smiles? “There is yet beauty in this world’and yet, this meeting unnerves me.” He pauses as he straps the feed bag to Xerxes head, the horse’s delighted eyes showing their thankfulness. “The minions of the dark have grown ever more bold since I displayed a’weakness’A defeat at the hands of a man who might as well sign his soul over lock stock and barrel to the dark lord himself. Zenith. I went unprepared and was humbled’I feared that Bob may have stricken me with some pestilence, yet he was quite forgiving’Indeed a loving god?” **********************FLASH************************* Pendragon staggers to his dressing room after his defeat at the hands of Christian Zenith. His eyes burn with anger and what can only be described as chastenment. He has been bested, and he knows it. He likes not the feelings it awakens in him. With a sigh, he pushes open the door, and we see not a dressing room, but a vast grassy plain. Winter has not yet touched her cold hands to these lands. In the distance, the man in white draws near. Pendragon gives a start at the scene? “What is this alchemy?” His exclamation is almost a sigh of resignation, for he knows. This is Bob’s way of saying that the two of them need to hold some palaver. The door closes and Pendragon turns longingly, yet his gaze is met only with distance: the door is gone. With a sigh, he turns to face his Lord God Bob, and receive the full wrath of said lord’s vengeance. As Bob nears, clad in his usual white suit, Pendragon kneels, his head bowed before the deity. It pains him to do this, even to the Lord. Pendragon is not much of a kneeler to anyone. “I humbly ask thy forgiveness for mine failure’Mine transgression deserves punishment and I await it with an open heart?” Bob merely smiles and shakes his head at the huge man kneeling before him. He pulls him to his feet and runs a hand over his face, washing the bruises and the blood away. Pendragon looks down at him with awe in his eyes? “Such alchemy!” “Yet Bob merely smiles warmly up at the man. “I never expected you to be perfect’Yet even in defeat you are truly a king. Humility is a valuable lesson to be learned, and I do not believe that you will make the same mistake twice. Next time, you will be prepared and overconfidence will not be the bane of you.” Pendragon gasps?“But I thought thee would smite me down’Where is thy vengeance” I understand not!” Bob shakes his head once more and smiles warmly?“You merely made your own battle a little more difficult. Now that the dark ones have seen that you can be beaten, they will come at you from all sides’Yet if it is my forgiveness you need, you have it. You are forgiven for being human, even though you may not be entirely mortal?” Pendragon smiles and the exasperation fades from his eyes’Bob motions for him to follow, and the two men walk in the beautiful landscape, Bob speaking, Pendragon mostly listening? “Now. You must go to your land. There is a man there. He lives in the eastern province of Lagardia.” Pendragon’s eyes open wide as Bob touches him on the hand?“I know exactly where the man is’How can thee do such things?”” asks Pendragon in wonder. Bob merely smiles and shrugs? “Now’this man is a nice fellow, yet his allegiances lie elsewhere’You are not to dispatch of him. He was once a faithful servant in his own right. Now though, he is a kind of “Rogue Agent”. He knows the currents of the darkness well, and his’Friends’Allow him the same power that thee have naturally’He can step between the worlds. Seek him out. He has more instructions for you. Speak to him. Perhaps you can turn him back, and if not that, then maybe you might learn something.” Pendragon nods, taking in all the information. He knows though, that if the man should seem a threat that he will destroy him as any other devotee of the Shadow. Bob knows it to, and as if he is mind reading, he frowns? “Aye’He shalt live if he stays on the level. If not, and he comes at the Lord King, I shall smite him with furious vengeance. I am not so much of thy ally that I will not do what is needed to protect mineself.” Bob claps Pendragon on the back and laughs as the picture?. ************************FLASH************************** Goes back to the homely abode, Pendragon standing motionless in front of the horse, lost in his own thoughts. With a creak the door opens, and Pendragon draws steel before he even has time to think of it. With a start, he watches as a man comes from the house. An older man, his black skin is as fine oiled leather. White hair, still full, tops his head, and he stands a full foot shorter than the imposing Pendragon. His smile is warm though, and he almost seems to see right through Pendragon? “Come on in, young fella. Fire’s warm, and this cold can seep into your bones. Get on in here and make yourself warm’I’m Ford Johnson, and I’d be pleased to share your comp’ny. Ain’t ever day I get to sit down with the lord o the land!” Ford turns and walks back into the house, leaving the door open. Instantly taken in by the man’s manner, Pendragon wonders how he could ever think he would have to dispatch this man. A little voice tells him that looks can be deceiving, and the big voice that is Pendragon agrees. He hasn’t lived this long by underestimating people. With a wry grin, he sheathes the blade Lucien, gives Xerxes a pat on the head and a scratch on his ear. He then turns and walks briskly into the house. Ford wasn’t lying’It is pretty cold. Inside the house, it is almost as if Ford lives in a time capsule. An old pot belly stove sits in the center of the main room. Pendragon looks at it and glances around at the rest of the place. Small would be the ideal word for it. A table sits off to the side of the stove, set with two chairs. A pump juts up from a sink’must be a well under the house. The kitchen is adjoined by a living room. A roaring fire fills a huge fireplace, and Ford sits in front of it, pipe in hand, in a padded chair. He motions for Pendragon to sit also, in an identical chair next to him. Pendragon smiles and takes his leave. The chair creaks as it adjusts to his weight and Pendragon sighs? “Nice to be off’n your feet, ain’t it?” asks the old man amiably? “Nice to be out of the saddle’It is hard on ye backside?” Pendragon reaches into the cloak and pulls his own pipe from a pocket. He cocks his head at Ford who motions for him to go on to it. Pendragon smiles as Ford produces a tobacco pouch and hands it over. The tobacco is moist and Pendragon inhales it’s aroma as he packs it into his pipe with a thumb. He then produces a wooden sliver and leans to the fire, catching it alight. He pulls contentedly on the pipe as he holds the flame over the briar and settles back for a sit as it finally catches? Ford looks over at him, his black eyes twinkling?“So, laddie’Did Bob tell you about me?” Pendragon nods?“Aye, he did. Said you used to serve him, but now you have switched your allegiances?” Ford laughs out loud and slaps his knee?“Now he would go and say sumpin like dat now, wouldn’t he?” He laughs again before pulling himself under control?“Let’s just say that his rein began to chafe. I needed a change. There are other powers out there’and not merely those of darkness and light. There are grays’Indeed there are grays, sir?” Ford pulls from his pipe and blows a perfect smoke ring. He smiles as it floats a little ways before dissipating. “So what is that ya need, laddie?” Pendragon settles deeper into his chair. Being called laddie would normally grate upon him, yet from this kindly fellow, it seems alright?“I need knowledge. Thee knowest the currents that pull at the two sides’The two main sides. White versus the darkness. I fight for the white; I always have whether I knew so or not.” “Ayuh. I’ve heard of ya. There is talk of you on all sides, my boy. A very interesting being you are’Intriguing. If I heard right, things have gotten a bit tougher for ya too’Have they?” Pendragon grimaces?“Aye. A minion of theirs bested me in combat.” “Zenith. The man is not of the darkness, yet his gray is a darker shade than most. He is out for himself and himself only.” Ford takes another puff as Pendragon stares at him? “Thou does know many things indeed. He took me by surprise?” “It is his way. If he cannot defeat you head on, he will get you with your guard down’So what’s your problem?” Pendragon sighs and begins to speak. Seems the demons have been coming at him ever since he let himself be defeated’They do indeed pray on weakness? “It started after the loss’Seems as though the shadow was drawn to me?” *********************FLASH******************************* His words paint the pictures that we see. Pendragon walks down the street after the defeat, his wounds gone and his step spry despite having been bested not one hour before. A homeless man comes up to him, his change cup in a grimy hand. Pendragon sighs’It is his way to pass alms to the poor. He reaches in a pocket for a coin and barely has time to see the dagger appear from nowhere in the man’s other hand. With a gasp, he grabs the man’s wrist, snapping it like a twig. The fellow gives a shout, and the street quickly empties. Not a mess people wish to get embroiled in. The change cup hit’s the ground and the man tries to reach Pendragon’s face with his good hand, certain death waiting in the poisons leavings beneath his fingernails. Pendragon deftly grabs this hand as well, wrenching it up behind the beggar. “What is it thee would try to do” Stick a knife in me? Invade mine body with thy foul poisons?” he rages as the man merely turns his head to see him as well as he can?.The beggar laughs? “My Lord wishes you dead. When my lord asks, I obey. He rewards me.” Pendragon looks the man up and down, shaking his head??Some reward. A life of filth’And then no life. Thou hast forfeited thy right to live’May god have mercy?” With a quick motion, Pendragon snaps the man’s neck, wincing as the crack pops and echoes down the street. He looks around, and drops the body. As it hit’s the sidewalk, smoke begins to rise from the rapidly decaying corpse, until finally, there is no body. Just the outline of a figure. Even the clothes are gone. Burned in some unholy fire? “Good Christ!” **FLASH** The next day, Pendragon rides Xerxes through a grassy park. His hand rests easy on the hilt of Lucien’He is not going to be taken by surprise again. Autumn has struck here though, and leaves fall from the trees. A few people walk through the park, along with a few homeless people. The homeless eye him greedily from their benches and grass patches. Dirty and ragged, their eyes gleam as the man in gold rides by. He notices the stares, and stands at the ready. At the first ill move, steel will be drawn to combat whatever is thrown his way. As one, the homeless people walk towards him, cups held out, begging for change. As they get closer, he sees that they all have the same face’They are the man he killed the previous day. With a shout, he draws Lucien and deals death quickly to the interlopers. Stand and slashes, the ring of steel as it cuts bone. It is music and pain to Pendragon’s ears. This is not the man he wishes to be. He is a King, not a killer’However, he is also a man who does what needs to be done. Finally, the deed done, he looks at the carnage before him. With a gasp as the bodies turn to so much smoke, he rides quickly from the park, never looking back? **********************FLASH************************ Ford sits looking at him for a bit before he speaks. With a sigh and a headshake, he begins?“Boy, you are in it deep. Those are the no souls’They are assassins, sworn to the dark lord.” He spits as he utters this, and Pendragon smiles. Not a follower after all , it would appear?“To have them sicked upon you, you must have opened some eyes indeed. Yet, they would not have come for you had you not shown weakness.” Pendragon takes a puff and ponders this for a moment?“So how do I redeem myself” They do add a bit of unneeded stress to mine tasks?” “Defeat the man who beat you. You have a chance?” “But how can I get to him” Surely the powers that be at GW would not throw him to me so soon’would they?” With another laugh and a knee slap, Ford shakes his head with mirth?“Boy, you gotta get with the times! You need a cell phone or sumpin!” Pendragon looks puzzled for a moment?“Fono” I know not the word?” Ford roars with laughter and almost falls out of his chair? ??Tisnt mine fault that times move too quickly?” Ford pulls himself together and pats Pendragon’s arm?“You have a match with him already! Evo boy! A tourney for the ages! Of the Worthy, and you surely are one?” At the mention of tournament, Pendragon’s eyes light up?“Art thou sure” You are a bit out of the way here?” “I’m sure son. I get back now and again. Now listen up, and listen good. This man Zenith, he goan try to get under your skin. He goan try to rattle you. You don’t let “im. He goan say you crazy or you dis and you dat’It don’t mean nothin. He ain’t got the brains to see what you are’To see what’s before him’He can’t believe you for what you are. The boy, he don’t believe in nothin’Nothin but money and some twisted sense of vengeance that he has developed over the years?” “He thinks I am crazy?” “Ayuh. Sumpin about you bein an actor or what not. I see you Pendragon’I see you for what you are. A king of men’And of souls. Bob found a good one in you, Boy. Just don’t let him run you under?” “I do as I wish. Bob merely enlightened me to a few things’We have the same goals, he and I.” Ford smiles warmly and pats Pendragon’s arm gently?“I know you mean well, boy’But don’t you think you got Bob figgered out till you KNOW you got him figgered. He is tricky’May be that you and him aren’t exactly friends. May be that he thinks you are a tool. Don’t let him use you up is all I’m saying”.” Pendragon frowns and stares down into his pipe? “I go mine own way”.” But does he? Does he really? Would he have ever decided to be a one man crew against darkness on his own? Granted, it is more honorable to seek justice for mankind than for petty grievances for gold’He is the hero, yet he is still greedy. The TV Title would come home, and after that, who knew? The shadow might lay off of him yet’Just one things stood in his way?. “Zenith?” ~TO BE CONTINUED~
