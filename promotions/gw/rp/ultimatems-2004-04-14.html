Ultimatems

14 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04.14.04 The television screen cuts off, forming that thin white line before all goes black. Pure silence in floating in the hotel room air, settling on the suite's white leather sofas and pure pearl colored carpeting. Ken Partridge Jr, sitting on the left end of the center sofa and dressed casually in a yellow multi-striped tea-dyed shirt over a Riviera blue cotton fitted tee and a pair of flax linen drawstring pants topped off with some brown leather comfort thong sandals, places the remote control back onto the glasstop end table from whence it came. As he hand releases the slender black piece of plastic, it in turns wraps it's fingers around the stem of a red-wine glass made of fine crystal and half filled with a smokey Austrailian 'Yalumba Octavius' Shiraz. As he pulls the glass to his lips to indulge in the perfectly aged six-year-old wine, Ice, sitting on the adjacent sofa and dressed in a blue solid button up and a pair of khaki linen slacks, breaks the comfort of silence as he poses a question. 'You didn't have it planned the whole time, did you?' 'Have what planned,' asked Ken, finishing his sip and enjoying the lingering taste on his palate. 'Well,' responds Ice, 'this whole thing with Ace. I mean, you just heard him. He thinks you've had all this planned from this start just to screw him over in the end.' Ken's face shifts into a small smirk as he brings the lip of the glass under his nose and inhales ever so slightly, taking in the aromatic value of this fine wine. 'No, Ice, I have not,' retorts Ken. 'Let Ace believe what he likes, the bottom line is that he's unwilling to admit to himself that he'll never be more than a mid-card talent with an undercard lifestyle. Personally, I was happy in agreeing to help him tap into his potential... let him live a life he's only dreamed of and acheive notoriety and respect as a champion and as a main eventer in a sense of which he's never known. Unfortunantly, now, I've grown tired of carrying his dead ass on my back. I've to many things in front of me than to worry about the likes of one Ace Harding.' 'Like you're contender's match against Josh Kosidlo,' interupts Josh as he enters the room, attatching his silver cross pendant around his neck... the water from his recent shower dripping from his hair slightly onto his black Versace t-shirt, vandalized with holes and tears and untucked over a pair of ratty jeans. 'You know, little bro,' chimed Ken half sarcastically with a smile on his face, 'you could stand an upgrade in the wardrobe department. I would be more than happy to help you out in that arena.' Ken giggles a bit as he takes another sip of his Shiraz. Josh retorts with laughter of his own as he brings his hands down from behind his neck, letting the necklace hang nicely. He grabs a bottle of Fiji water from the refridgerator in the kitchen before finally making his way into the living area of the Sheraton penthouse and sitting on the opposite end of Ken. 'I'm quite happy with my clothing as it is, thank you,' laughs Josh. 'I do appreciate the offer though.' 'You know,' begins Ice, 'Josh brought up a good point. You do realize that you have a ranking match for the Platinum Title against Kosidlo at Evolution, don't you?' 'Of course I do,' retorts Ken as he finishes his wine. 'You've known me long enough to understand that nothing comes before this business to me. Not the fans... not relationships... and not some pouty has-been complaining about not being me. I've been studying this 'Ironman' kid, as he likes to call himself. What do you think I was doing before you two got back with lunch?' 'I'm just making sure, Ken,' responds Ice. 'I mean, you and I know that both of us can get ahead of ourselves at times.' 'True, but that's just because we can back it up... as can Josh.' Josh takes a long drink of his water before chiming in. 'Understandable, but we still need to realize that some ham-and-egger could step up and get a fluke at any time.' 'That's why we take every match seriously,' retorts Ice as he eyeballs the American Youth... the tension growing in the room. 'Then perhaps you should get in the gym, Brock,' shot Josh. 'I'm much more formidable than you seem to think.' An argument insues and Ken is caught in the crossfire. One best-friend caught in between his two best-friends is never a fun place to be. ------- Silence once again falls on the surroundings. Ken has sent both Josh and Ice on their seperate ways to train for their upcoming title match and he sits watching more videos of Josh Kosidlo... studying every move and maneuver. This kid still had some dampness behind his ears, but Ken was sure he would be able to have a good match with him if he wanted. However, his main goal was set, and that was to set Ace Harding straight on his ways... whether it be with The Elite... or against them.
