The Journey: Part 4/Joshua's POV

31 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-31-04 Late Night Well hello again my pretties. We have seen and experienced some highs and some exasperating lows within the past couple of days huh? We are part of one of the biggest PPVs for a federation called “Global Wrestling” and in that match we face a man whom calls himself the gimmick “The Prototype” and then a man whom was also lucky to beat a friend of Joshua’s in Ken Partridge Jr. Now on this particular NIGHT and yes I emphasize night we find Joshua Curtis with his boyfriend in Zack Johnson and his younger (half) sister in Mizuno Ami and they are at the home of Zack and Joshua which is where we begin our story with a little bit of a story within itself. It should be noted that after the PPV Joshua left the arena somewhat disgusted with some issues that will be kept confidential because of the sensitive nature of the material at this moment but when Joshua left the arena after his match he was pretty much upset and wouldn’t talk to many people outside of his “comfort zone” and that meant basically that he’d only talk to the members of his faction which is the “Connection”. Joshua is seen wearing a white shirt and tie, black dress pants, and black socks while Zack is wearing a black “Dark Rituals” T-Shirt, Arizona Blue Jeans and white hanes socks. Ami is wearing a White turtleneck, a pair of blue jeans and a pair of Converse sneakers and the setting is the kitchen table for a good dinner as Joshua had hoped with his sister and his lover. Tina would have come but unfortunately due to some “family matters” that Juri had to tend to she wouldn’t be able to make it because well’let’s just say “Because” ok? “Well guys its been a wild month for you. Joshua has been working within the ranks of the fed and oddly enough they haven’t booked Zack yet. What do you make of this little situation?” Ami asked and Zack decided it’d be in his best interest to answer for the team “Personally Ami I’m a little surprised. I’m flattered that Bubba has asked me to come into the fed with Joshua and right now as his manager I’m happy to see his success and be there for him when something happens that shouldn’t. I’m a little surprised that Joshua didn’t score a fall in the match that occurred but some might say that in the grand design of things him NOT winning the title could open up some doors for us in the future!” The slightly younger member of the Connection stated which caused Joshua to slightly raise an eyebrow at that comment. “Oh?” Joshua asked quizzically “And what doors might they be dear?” Joshua asked slightly puzzled by the comments that his young boyfriend made. “Well” Zack began “when you look at it if you had won the title you’d have had to defend it this week and the “so-called” prototype gets that “luxury” so you get to sit back and watch him get picked apart and that way if you get passed this match then you get a chance to rematch with the man that some friends I talked to say can’t beat you face to face...in fact some friends of mine say that you are more or less the “uncrowned” champion within the company!” Zack said to Joshua and the man whom some call the leader of the Connection got up from his seat and looked out of the window at the world that has condemned him somewhat for his choices. “This is the way it goes gang” Joshua said as he began his speech to his group assembled before him “we, the Connection are here in GW for a reason and that is that we are here to open the minds of the roster and of the Front Office here in GW. Right now we have nowhere to go and the fact that people are extending invites to us to join one of the biggest matches in the company is an honor but the question is “Are we ready?” are we ready to look at the millions of fans on PPV and enter into a battleground that we know nothing about? I don’t know yet what we are going to do if we get the invitation to enter into the match next month but I do know that for my money “The Connection” will be coming as our own entity in due time its just I don’t know when yet. I HOPE that the powers that be give us the opportunity Zack and hun’I KNOW that when SiT comes something big WILL happen and Zack’I can’t say anymore I’m sorry!” Joshua said as he left the room and two bewildered stable members whom can only sit in stunned shock as Joshua goes to his room and gently closes the door behind him?
