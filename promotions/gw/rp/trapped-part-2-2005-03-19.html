Trapped (Part 2)

19 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19th March 2005 Family? To me there is nothing more important than family. Everyone is someone’s son or daughter and everyone has a unique tale to tell about their family. I will admit that in my past I have turned my back on my family, the most prominent memory that sticks out is when I turned my back on my mother to pursue my career in wrestling. It was not my proudest moment and thankfully last year after nine years my mother and I were able to solve our difference. I thought I had strong family unit, I thought that finally my family was one thing in my life that I had sorted out and that nothing could take them away from me. How wrong I was? My family was snatched from me about a month ago now and I have not seen them since. Why, I hear you ask? Because of one man, Richard Wood, he is the man who has taken my life and twisted and moulded it to his own amusement. He is the only person who knows where my family is, he tells me that they are safe that he is looking after them but I won’t believe him until I see them. Wood keeps me locked up in some sterile steel box being monitored 24/7 by his goons and “scientists”. Every day I have to wake up to the sound of a “scientist’s” shrill voice screaming at me through the speakers. Every day I have to be put through idiotic tests just so Wood can test the limits of the suit. There is no escape from this nightmare; I have no choice but to go along with these procedures. Why did I let myself fall into this trap. All I want is my family back! Adding to my growing depression is the fact that at High Octane, I lost a belt I had come to consider my own. At High Octane I lost my Cruiserweight Championship. It was a hard fought match there is no doubt about that and with Aradia Xiomara coming out the winner and new Cruiserweight Champion, I can only be impressed by her skill in the ring, long may she reign. So what do I do now? Do I try and reclaim the Cruiserweight Championship for a third time? No, my time as Cruiserweight Champion is over, it is time to move on and there are two belts that are already in my sights. This coming Evolution I have the chance to claim a title that over the passed month has seen 3 different champions and I hope to make that 4. The belt I talk of is none other than the GW Television Championship. To win this title I have to beat yet another rising GW star, Mindy Mercer who after months of feuding back and forth with Mike Biddle finally claimed the title. There is no doubt Mercer is a tough competitor and after my battle with Aradia Xiomara, I have nothing but respect for the female fighters of the GW. Not only do I have the chance to beat Mercer for the TV title, I also have the chance to advance in the Platinum Series to claim the vacant GW Platinum Championship. So you see there is a lot riding on my match at Evolution and so failure is not an option. I need that TV title, I need to advance in the Platinum Series because as I learned, Wood does not look kindly on failure? “Get in there!” I was hurled across the cold steel floor as Charlie threw me into my steel box with a furious looking Richard Wood behind him. My body was still aching from my match at High Octane but Wood didn’t seem to care as he and Charlie menacingly walked towards me as I tried to prop myself up on my steel bed. “Pick him up Charlie!” Wood yelled pointing at me. Charlie grabbed me and smashed me against the wall propping me up with his huge muscular arms. I knew any struggle would be hopeless so I simply let Charlie from me around like a rag doll. As Charlie held me Wood came around the side still with the furious look on his face. “Look at me Adam!” I continued to stare directly into Charlie’s eyes trying to infuriate Wood more. He couldn’t do anything to me, he needed me and he knew it. “Look at me!” Wood screamed at the top of his voice. I stayed completely still. “Charlie!” Wood balled at the big lug holding me. Charlie knew exactly what Wood was getting at as he released one of his arms from my chest and pushed my head sideways so I was now staring directly at Wood. Wood moved closer so he was now directly in my face. “What the hell was that Adam”!”!” “What the hell was what?” I asked with my face being pushed further and further into the wall. “Don’t give me that Adam, you know exactly what I am talking about.” He was right, I knew he was talking about the High Octane match but I wasn’t going to give him the satisfaction. “Honestly Wood, I don’t know what you are talking about!” “You are on my last nerve Adam! I am talking about your match at High Octane. You assured me you would come out with the victory!” “I didn’t assure you anything Wood! In fact I don’t even remember talking to you about my plans for the match.” “You were beaten by a god damn women Adam. I thought you were some sort of perfectly tuned athlete!” “No one is perfect Wood! Besides I gave it my all at High Octane and Aradia is a great competitor and to be honest I wish her all the best with the Cruiserweight Championship!” “Do you not even care that you are no longer a champion?” My face was now becoming part of the wall as Charlie pushed harder and harder onto my face. Wood could see the difficulty I was having. “Charlie let him go, I do need him in one piece after all.” Wood said beginning to calm down. With that Charlie released me and I dropped to the floor in a heap. I was able to gain enough energy to push myself back so I was sitting up with my back to the wall. Wood knelt down on one knee and looked at me. “Now let me ask you again Adam, do you even care that you are no longer a champion?” “Of course I care!” I replied feeling my frustration build up again. “But I held that belt longer than anyone in GW history so I think it is time someone else had a go with it. So get off my back Wood!” I sighed and glared at Wood, even with my mask on he could tell that I was not happy but he didn’t care. “You listen to me Adam. I grew up on success, I built this business on success, so failure for me is not an option. To see one of my products fail, infuriates me.” I felt my rage boil as I dived at Wood but Charlie was on me quick before I could grab Wood. Charlie held me back as I waved my arms frantically trying to get Wood. “I am not one of your damn products Wood! I am a human being not some damn product!” “Oh I disagree Adam,” Wood replied with a cocky smirk on his face, “You are my product and I have a contract in my office that agrees.” “I hate you!” I screamed at Wood who still had that arrogant cocky smile on his face. “I don’t care Adam, because as much as you hate me it doesn’t change the fact that I own you!” I screamed in fury as I tried to grab him again but Charlie held me back. As I continued to scream and shout, a blond woman in a lab coat walked in holding a clip board she glanced at me and moved over to Wood with a fake smile on her face. “Mr. Wood the card for the next Evolution is in.” She said as she handed over the clipboard to Wood. “Thank you Emma.” Wood replied as Emma walked out. Wood looked at the clipboard and a smile crossed his face. “Well look like someone is going to Hawaii this week for Evolution. Also looks like someone has a chance to become Television Champion!” I didn’t reply as I simply looked at Wood with rage burning in my eyes. I was glad at the chance to become a champion again but at that moment in time all I wanted to do was rip Wood’s head from his body. “Now let’s not have another High Octane, Adam. As I said failure isn’t an option.” “Is Charlie going to accompany me again?” I asked not really caring. Wood looked down at the clipboard again. “No, I don’t think that is necessary.” “Why not?” Wood stepped towards me so we were now face to face. “Because Adam,” Wood said the cocky smirk back on his face, “Like I said, I have eyes everywhere.” Wood then tapped me on the side of my face and smiled as he began to walk out. I began screaming and shouting again as I tried to grab him but Charlie was too strong. I watched as Wood looked around at me and chuckled as he turned back around and walked out. As he did Charlie threw me across the room and my back smashed against the wall next to my “bed” before he followed suit and walked out. I felt tears well up in my eyes as I crawled onto my steel bed and two words filled my head? No escape!
