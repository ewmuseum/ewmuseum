Monica's wedding

15 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/15/03 The date is December 31, 2002. The time is 9:45 p.m. Brick's sister Monica is getting prepared for her New Year's wedding. She wears a beautiful white Vera Wang gown. Their mother Coretta wears a two piece outfit (jacket and dress). Monica: Let’s see. Something old, that’s the earrings. Something new, that’s the dress. Something borrowed, that’s the garter. And something blue, that’s? Coretta: Monica baby, calm down. Monica: Sorry, Mom, but I’m just so nervous. I can’t believe this night is finally here. By midnight, I’ll be Mrs. Kyle Fontaine. Coretta: Yes, God is good. My baby girl is finally getting married. Monica: I know. I’m so happy. Coretta: Now if only we could get your brother over the broom. Monica: You mean, without him kicking and screaming? The two women laugh. Suddenly, there is a knocking at the door. Coretta: That must be him now. Coretta goes to the door and opens it. In comes Brick, in a classic black tuxedo. Brick: Is everything cool in here? Coretta: Oh, Jamal. We were just talking about you. Brick raises an eyebrow. Brick: Mmm-hmm, I’ll bet. Talking about me getting married. Monica: Weren’t you and what’s-her-name close to that? That blonde who gave you that “good luck” kiss. Brick: Gunner, and no, we weren’t. Coretta: I’ll be right outside. Coretta goes outside. Brick: So, sis, you nervous? Monica: What do you think? Brick: Well, don’t worry. I got everything under control. Davis Fury managed to get some off-duty cops to watch the church and the reception hall. Salaok and his boys try to set foot either here or at the reception, they’ll be arrested. Monica: You sure he’ll even try something? Brick: I don’t wanna take that chance. Monica: But didn’t you say he was occupied with the Welch brothers? Brick: Yeah, but that doesn’t mean he wouldn’t try anything just for the hell of it. Especially after that threat he made to me at Dark Rituals. Plus, when he showed up at your engagement party. I already failed Aaron. I ain’t about to fail you or Mom. Monica: Look, you don’t have to protect me all the time. Sometimes, I can take care of myself. Brick looks his sister up and down. Brick: I still can’t believe you’re the same little girl I had to yell at to hang up the phone. Monica: The one you used to give noogies. Brick: I still can. Brick raises his hand as if to give her a noogy. Monica: Boy, you mess up my hair, you’re gonna enter 2003 with a bloody stump. Brick: Ha ha ha! Listen, Mom told me that you ran into Cedric. Monica(sighs): I asked her not to. Brick: Well, why didn’t you tell me? Monica: Because the last time you and Cedric saw each other, you told him you’d kill him if he ever came near me. Brick: And I meant it. Monica: Well, you know he didn’t try to hurt me. Brick: Yeah, Mom told me. He really apologize for beating you? Monica: Yeah, I couldn’t believe it either. But he really made a change in his life. Brick: Good. Monica: He also said that he hoped that Kyle gave me the love and respect that he didn’t. Brick: So do I. I’m really glad you’re marrying Kyle. Monica: You know, all my friends were taking bets on how long he’d last before you scared him off. Brick: And you lost the bet. Monica: What makes you think I bet against you? Brick: Cause I know you, Monica. Another knock at the door. Brick: I guess that’s our cue. Brick holds out his arm. Brick: Ready. Monica locks arms with Brick. Monica: Definitely. Brick and Monica head out of the room. Static.
