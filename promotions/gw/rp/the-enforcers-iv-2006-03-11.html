The Enforcers - IV

11 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03.11.06 'Where in the hell have you two been?' asked a somewhat disheveled Ken Partridge, Jr. 'I called you over three hours ago.' 'Calm down,' started Bill Moresi. 'We were at the gym getting ready for the Triple Threat match coming up.' 'Calm down?' whispered Ken. 'CALM DOWN?' he said, this time more ferociously. 'Josh was drugged and left for dead. Do you understand that? Somebody tried to kill him. Somebody tried to kill my best-friend and our team-mate and all you can say to me is calm down?' At this point, Bill was beginning to get somewhat aggrevated, though, he was trying his best to keep his temper cool. Afterall, they were in a hospital waiting room and Ken was distraught to say the least. Finally Gunner Addison broke a now crying Ken aside and Ace Harding did the same thing with Bill. 'What in the hell is wrong with him?' asked Bill with a slight of anger in his tone. He regretted saying it as soon it came out, as he knew what was wrong with Ken. It was on his mind, too, and he just wanted to know how Josh was, but the word vomit came out anyway. 'Bill,' began Ace, 'you know he's upset. Hell, we all are. Trust me, Ken will come to his senses. He just needs time to cool down. I mean, nearly the exact same thing happened two years ago and I was the one that caught hell for not being here. But, we're fine now.' 'You were kicked out of The Elite, Ace,' Moresi reminded. 'Then, you and Ken had a knock-down drag-out Submissions Match at WrestleCade that forced you to retire, remember?' Harding looked back at Ken, crying on Gunner's shoulder, and then back to Bill. With a smile on his face, he responded. 'That was a different Ken, Bill. He was struggling with a lot of his demons then. He's got them under control now. The only demons he deals with now are those of others.' Bill lowered his head a bit before looking around Ace's broad shoulders to spy a glance at KPJ, drying his tears and regaining his composure. 'You're right,' he began. 'We've both got our tempers. I guess that's why we've been friends for so long.' Before he could continue, Ken had made his way back. With a swollen face he threw his arms around Bill and embraced him in a hug. 'I'm sorry,' Ken stated before pulling away. 'This is just something I never thought I'd deal with again, you know?' Bill placed his large hand on Ken's shoulder. 'I know. I know this isn't easy. You've got to realize, though, we're there for you and we're there for Josh. The Elite, it's a brotherhood.' 'I know you two are,' Ken said with half of a smile on his face. 'I guess, with you guys off in Japan so long, I just got used to The Elite being Josh and myself again, along with gun, of course. Don't get me wrong, I'm beyond joyous that you two are back and that you're getting the opportunity to take the Tag Team straps off of those two blow hard cartoon characters that are holding on to them right now. It's just... I don't know... a lot of things.' 'Ken,' chimed in Ace, 'are you okay?' 'I'm fine,' mumbled KPJ. 'Are you sure?' Harding asked again. 'You look like you haven't slept in days and your growling stomach tells me you probably haven't really eaten in as long, either.' 'Thank you,' Gunner replied with her arms on Ken's shoulders for comfort. 'I've been telling him to get something to eat and go to a hotel for some rest since we got here. He's going to be in no shape to go head-to-head with Saint at the Pay Per View if he keeps this up.' 'I'll be fine,' Ken said again, this time a little irritated. 'Look,' he continued, 'Josh doesn't have any family. We're it... we're all he's got. There's no way that I'm leaving here without him. I didn't before, and I won't this time.' 'Ken,' Gunner continued in her effort, 'you have a match coming up. You all do.' 'We all had matches going into Clash of the Titans two years ago, too. Look at how that turned out. Ice retained his World Title against the Souls of the Black Lotus. Ace had a good showing, and Josh and I were the final two me in the Global Rumble. We did it then, we'll do it this time at High Octane.' 'Come on, Ken,' Bill smiled as he put his hand back on Ken's shoulder, pulling him away, 'let's go get something to drink. It'll do you some good, the walking. Besides, your water bottle's empty.' Ken nodded in compliance. He knew that they wouldn't be far from Josh... one floor at most. The two made their way out of the waiting leaving only Ace and Gunner. 'I'm sorry he blew up on you two,' said Gunner. 'He's just been so upset.' 'I know, Gun,' replied Harding. 'It's okay. We know that he's in a bad way right now. But, um... how's Josh? Is he okay?' 'Yeah,' she replied with a deep rooted sigh. 'You're never going to believe this, but it's rohypnol. It was an overdose on rohypnol... of all things.' 'Wait,' started Ace, 'that's the same as last time. Are we sure that he didn't try to--' 'No!' She cut him off before he could finish. 'He was drugged this time. We don't know who did it, but the police and the doctors both said that there's no way he could have gotten where they found him if he'd done it himself and they also said that there's no way he'd have been able to give himself that much and still survive. Apparantly, he was drugged slowly over a few hours.' 'Christ!' Ace stated. 'But, who?' 'That, Ace, is the question on everyone's minds.'
