An Inspirational Tune

16 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/16/2003 The scene fades in to an extreme close up of an averaged sized acoustic guitar. The picture widens, and Ice can be seen sitting on a black bar stool next to the guitar. He reaches over and removes the guitar from its stand, and places it on his lap, preparing to play the instrument. Ice strums a few chords, and fumbles around to adjust the guitar. Again Ice strums a few chords, this time however he seems pleased with the way the guitar sounds, and doesn't adjust it anymore. He shrugs his shoulders, and adjusts his sitting position on the bar stool before finally begining to play. The tune that he plays is slow and melodic, his eyes are closed tighty, and his body sways as the sound of the music engulfs him. Ice continues to play, until a loud screech abruptly halts the strumming of the guitar. Ice slides the guitar from his lap, and hops off of the black bar stool. The guitar drags behind Ice as he calmly walks out of the room he was in, and walks over to a plain white door, with a bronze doorknob. The screeching becomes louder as Ice reaches the door. With his left hand he reaches out and slowly turns the knob. The door swings open, and Yoshiko's head quickly spins around and her eyes widen in fear. She hurrily struggles with the chain that is wrapped around her wrists, and connected to a shower head, trying to escape. Ice smirks, and shakes his head from side to side, as if he is shaking it at Yoshiko, taunting her actions. 'Now what do we have going on here? You're not trying to leave me are you?' Ice calmly walks over to the beautiful young lady, and roughly grabs her face. He leans in so that they are nose to nose with one another and confidently smirks as Yoshiko trembles. Ice pushes Yoshiko's face away from his and turns towards the camera. 'Rokku, I want you to take a look at Yoshiko right now. You see the scabs on her face, on her arms? You remember seeing how they were inflicted. I do. You remember seeing the pain on her face? The desperation in her screams? Do you remember that feeling you got when you saw the most precious thing in your life being put through such trauma? Well no matter what you felt, it is nothing, NOTHING, compared to what I have been feeling, to what Star has been feeling, after YOU killed our unborn child!' Ice's face turns a mild pink color. He inhales deeply, and regains his composure. His pinkish skin tone, quickly fades back to the pale white color it usually is. Anger resides on Ice's face, as he turns back towards Yoshiko. 'So you want me to let you go? You want me to unbound you? Alright.' A surpised, yet uneasy look shoots across Yoshiko's face, as Ice reaches up and removes the chain from the shower head. He slowly lowers Yoshiko's bound hands until they are at her chest. Tightly Ice holds onto the chains, and he holds her arms outstretched. 'There, are you happy now? You are no longer attatched to that shower head. But now, I'm putting my faith in you, that you will be a good little girl, and not try anything stupid.' Ice nods for reassurance, and Yoshiko blinks in repliance. Ice turns and bends over to pick up his guitar, when Yoshiko lunges forward and strikes Ice in the back of the head. Ice falls forward, and out of reach of Yoshiko, who's feet are still bound. Ice shakes his head, and slowly gets to his feet. He snarls, as he pierces a hole through the woman's body with his angry, cold, blue eyes. 'Now, from the culture your ancestors have brought you up in, I would have assumed better manners towards keeping your word.' Ice grabs the guitar, and twirls it in his hands. Yoshiko, in an attempt to defend herself, begins to swing her bound hands violently. One swing catches Ice in the ribs, as he raises his arm to block her blow. Ice winces in slight discomfort from the blow, as his left arm clamps down and traps Yoshiko's only defense. Yoshiko struggles to get away from Ice's grip, but to no avail. A sick smile creeps across Ice's face. 'Rokku, I hope you are watching, because this is going to hurt you, alot more than it hurts me.' As Ice finishes his statement, he knees Yoshiko in the gut, sending her down to her knees, and gasping for breath. Quickly Ice raises the guitar above his head and slams it down across Yoshiko's back, shattering it to pieces. Ice bends down, and violently rips the shirt off of Yoshiko's body. Leaving only a pale colored bra to cover her. He then roughly pulls her back up to her feet, and once again binds the chains that bind her hands to the shower head. Ice tilts his head to the left, while he peers into the glossy, dazed eyes of his prey. 'Brick, don't think, that I don't have you in the back of my mind. Because I do. Now, I realize that you and Rokku are coming up with some sort of plan, to help him get his precious Yoshiko back, and to do something physically violent to me. But let me reassure the two of you, I always have an ace up my sleeve.' Ice turns towards the camera, and again sickly smiles into it. 'Brick, I noticed that you enjoy music. And I'm sure that while you and Rokku come up with your master plan for this week's Evolution, you two are listening to some wonderful music, as inspiration. Well, I too love to be inspired by the wonderful sounds of a great song, a great tune, or even a great voice. And when I picked up my guitar, I had planned to share a tune with the two of you, that was before I was rudly interupted. But none the less, I still plan on inspiring the two of you, with a tune of my own. And young Miss Yoshiko, is going to assist me in doing so.' Ice begins to chuckle to himself, as he turns and rips the dull grey duct tape from Yoshiko's lips. Ice tosses the tape to the side, and then reaches around the lovely young lady, and begins to turn one of the knobs in the shower. The water turns on and steam quickly fills the room. Yoshiko shreiks as the scalding hot water hits her unprotected skin. 'Hope you enjoy her performance.' Yoshiko continues to shriek, scream, and moan as the water hits her body. Ice's smug smirk is quickly engulfed by the steam as it clouds up the small room until the picture becomes white. The scene fades out, to Yoshiko's screaming.
