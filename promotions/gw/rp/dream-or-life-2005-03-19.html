Dream or life?

19 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-19-05 “Can I help you?” Two days. That was the call for when I was to leave for Hawaii. A place that I have never been to, let alone even been asked to go to. But it wasn’t to be a trip for the fun times that I wish I could have. No. It was for work that I needed to do. Another challenge was placed on my young career in the form of El Serpenti. “Hello?”” Now I can sit here and tell myself that I’m not scared. I can even say that everything that I have learned from my uncle has trained me to be brave in a situation like this but who would I be kidding? Only myself. But I must become the woman that my mother wanted me to be. I must find the strength that my father placed inside of me when I was young and show the world that I to can be a warrior. I must show them that I am more then just a country girl with boots and a short skirt. My time is now? My time is right now! “NEXT!” The loud voice rang in my ears as I stood in the line to the movies. It was a Friday night. Dry and noisy for this part of Texas. Though it was the first free time I had in the last two days before I fly to Hawaii. “Can I help you?” The lady said in the booth. “Yes, I need one ticket for The Ring Two.” Pulling out a few dollars that I had stuffed in my jean pocket. I tossed the money down on the try in front of me and awaited for the ticket. A small breeze passed through the open area . I turned to see two old couples that held their hands tightly. Like they were so in love that they didn’t want to be separated for even one second. I could remember that being my mother and father. Taking me to places for my birthday or for a little get together and never leaving without giving a kiss to one other. I always loved that. But now that the person that hated me doing this is gone, I’m lost without love in my life. I took the ticket from the woman and began to walk towards the doors in front of me. Taking ever step slowly, looking at the view of the place. It was noisy. Just like it was on Overload. The feel of the noise still rides in my ears. I can still hear the fans screaming at me to keep going as I rolled on the mat. The pain shooting up the spinal part of my back. Making me almost give up. But giving up wasn’t something I was taught. “Your movie is to the left, enjoy.” The lady said with a deep and bright smile before taking my ticket. I began walking the way she said. Turning to see the lines of people outside still waiting to get in. I brushed away the hair that I had in my eyes. As I did, I felt a hard shoulder slam right into me. I just came to a sudden lifting, almost falling down before two arms caught me, wrapping me around from the back. Oh my god, what just happen? I looked up and around to see something I hadn’t seen before. Beautiful blue eyes that caught me at first sight and a smile that almost made me fall head over heels. Tall like a sky scrapper and a body that you don’t see everyday. I lowered my head a little to stop me from showing the emotion I had deep in my body before dusting my shirt off. “Are you ok miss?” His soft voice asked me. “Yeah I think so.” responding to his question. I knew I was but I wasn’t in a different way but I wasn’t about to show it. Was he alone? That was the sudden question that I asked myself. Looking to the right and left for a sexy woman that I was sure he had. “Well I guess I’m going to go now. Thanks for catching me.” God what the heck was I saying? I asked myself as I turned to walk away. “Do I know you?” What did he just say? Did he know me? I wish he did. I turned back around, looking at him as the crowds of people passed by us in the hallway. There seemed to be two types of people I always seem to fall for. Hicks like me and weirdo’s that just want my body. I looked up at him with a smile before I replied. “I don’t think so,” Giving the answer that was needed to be said. “I’m sure I’d remember you if you did.” His eyes went down from wide to a small shy type of effect. He rubbed his chin, trying to figure out if he really did I guess. Whether he did or not, I knew I didn’t know him. I tossed my hair out of my face once again, trying to get my blue cold eyes looking right in his eyes as he would notice. But the more I looked at him I knew. I didn’t have time for someone special. I was a working girl now and my dream needed me full time. Looking down at my watch, “Crap!” “What?” The gentleman asked. “I’m almost late for my movie. I’m sorry but I have to go.” Trying to dust him off in a gentle way as I didn’t want to hurt him. “He looked sad. Almost a little depressed in a way that I wished he didn’t have to go. But the more I thought about it I was doing the right thing, or was I” Was it right for me to be alone without someone to love or was it right for him to be free of me as I wouldn’t be able to do both? Either way I knew what I had to do. I began walking backwards before turning around. “Wait!” Felling a hand wrap around my arm, I got a choking feeling deep in my gut. Like I was feeling that funny, “He likes me” feeling. I slowly turned around to see him still standing there. I didn’t know what to say. He wasn’t giving up for some reason it seemed to me. “I don’t know your name.” He said, still holding my arm. “Is there any way I can at least have that?” Inside I laughed. It was puppy love almost as I gave a gentle grin to the pearl floor under us. “Elia, Elia Smith.” Still standing there, wondering what to do. “I’m Eric. Eric Johnson.” His rough yet soft voice still said. Eric Johnson? He didn’t seem like the Eric kind. I pulled my hand away, this time getting to take a few steps backwards, heading for my movie. “So see you later I guess?” He said, still standing there in the crowded hallway. Deep inside I hoped he would but I knew it was for the best. I was a true dreamer and working girl and I knew he would only be second best to wrestling. I gave him a long last look with a smile before turning around and away from a possible life. God how he stuck in my mind with every smile and grin he took. Though the days of puppy love were over. Now the days of fighting and showing the world that I could live in it was here. I turned the corner, heading for the door before the I walked through, heading for my last time of my time before heading off to Hawaii the next morning.
