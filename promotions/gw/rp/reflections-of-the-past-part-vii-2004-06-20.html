Reflections of the past: Part VII

20 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 20th, 2004. 'Dear Diary…' I began as my pen wavered and I tapped it thoughtfully on the paper. 'What should I write about?' I mused aloud to myself in the emptiness of my broad apartment. Stretching out my body clad only in a pair of black boxers across my soft leather couch I leaned back and dropped my pin to the gray carpet stretched across the floor. It was a lazy Sunday night and the countdown was on until I ran into Ken Partridge Jr. I would collide with my best friend, my partner, and my brother in mere hours. Tick, tock, tick, tock said the internal clock in my mind. The countdown was on and the hours were fading fast. It really pained me to Wrestle Ken another time…but it must be done and I must beat him once more to retain my World title…my lovely, beautiful, magnificent World title. Glancing over at the gleaming gold it shined brightly on the trophy case which I had recently purchased to display my pride and joy. I was World Champion…one day I may lose it but no one could strip me of my legacy as a fighting Champion. So, I would fight and I would win…even if it meant defeating my best friend one more time. Suddenly…this brought back memories. My fingers danced across the paper of the roughed up black notebook in my grasp as I flipped back the pages, words flashing before my eyes scrawled across the white paper in messy blue ink. The dates passed quickly…until something caught my eye, August 21st, 2002. My bluish gray eyes soaked up the words etched messily onto the paper. ---- Dear diary, Today was all too interesting. It appears that I'm now working for an independent Wrestling company by the name of NeWave Wrestling, or NWW for short. It's not exactly the big leagues but the pay is decent and I'm getting some recognition, who knows…maybe one of these days some big Wrestling company will pick me up, but for now this is the best way to work toward that goal. NWW isn't exactly going to make me rich but it will pay the bills. If I keep up with my work in Japan and Mexico I can make a living as a Pro Wrestler without having to take up another job just to make ends meet. Of course, the scouts are in attendance…maybe in another year or so I'll have a contract that fattens my wallet but for now I'll just live my dream, after all most people can't claim they've been able to do that. Another thing most people can't do is claim that they've defeated and defied adversity in the way that I have. Everyone said I'm too small and they still say it. My first trainer said it…until I showed him that dynamite comes in small packages. The fans say it and the entire world of Wrestling keeps repeating it. The word on the street is that if I was six feet tall and two hundred and thirty pounds I'd be getting rich about right now. Sadly enough, they say there's no room for a skinny kid who throws his body around too much and is guaranteed a short and injury-ridden career. Let them say it…one day I'll be a success and then we'll see whose on top of the world. It won't be the fans, it wont be the president that stiffs me on my paychecks, it will be me…the man who has worked non stop to achieve his dream. But I have no right to bitch…aside from gaining experience and getting my name spread around I'm also making friends, and God knows how many of those I need, especially in the Wrestling industry where everyone is just looking to stab you in the back. Friends are hard to come by and finding a true friend is like digging a diamond out of a coal mine. But I think I've found one, and a damn good one. Someone, who I can trust, respect and look up too. I met him today and already I feel some sort of strange connection with him. It's odd…we just agreed on almost everything and got along well. The man that I speak of is Ken Partridge Jr. a man who knows the in's and out's of the business, a man who can mentor me…a man who can help me to reach my goal and finally climb that proverbial ladder to success. I see in him a friend and partner. We've already agreed to travel together on the road. Of course…he's generous and has a loaded checkbook so it's only fair that I accept him on his offer to share expenses. ---- Reading this in silence I laughed. Ken had impressed me with his knowledge of Wrestling and life in general when we first met. And reflecting upon that now…it was almost stunning to see what had changed and what had remained the same. With an amused smile I returned to my reading. ---- I'm confident that Ken can help me to take my career to greater heights…and I only pray that I can return the favor. And one of these days…I'll be a star. ---- I chuckled to myself my smile growing wider by the second as I examined my messy handwriting. Looking back down at the paper I saw where I had practiced an autograph… 'Kid Flash', Wrestling star of the future. Clutching my pen firmly I placed the tip of it to the paper and in one quick motion scribbled over these words. In their place I wrote… 'American Youth' Josh, Wrestling star of the past, the present and the future.
