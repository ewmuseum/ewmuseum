Wake Up (Part II)

23 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.23.05 10.18.05 | The Bronx, New York | 1:17 PM As Mindy lay in bed, trying to rest up from the further hell she had been put through last night, bad dreams continued to surface. Demonic faces of her family, wrought with anger. Silhouettes of hateful men, pointing and snickering; relishing the sight of Mindy and her distressed state. Her boyfriend lying on the ground, face a bloody mess. Why bother sleeping if she was still getting grief with her eyes closed? Mindy's mind wasn't going to stop bugging her about Bobby Brawler and what he had done to her boyfriend, but never the less the twenty year old wrestler allowed her muscles to ease into the soothing warm water. 'Jeeeesus,' she exhaled, finding at least a bit of relief from the water and soapy bubbles that covered her up to her neck in the bathtub. Amidst the distraction that the bubbly water was, however, there were still thoughts of redemption that Mindy had to take care of. Not often did she hold grudges; Mike Biddle had asked for it with his constant pestering her when she first joined Global, as did Dawn McFarlane when she decided to gang up on her with her Hunting Grounds buddies. They both got what they deserved in the end. And by God, Mindy was going to make damned sure that Bobby Brawler got what he deserved, as well: a vengeful, spiteful, and ultimately painful ass kicking. 'Sheesh, I gotta worry about HSW now, too, though,' Mindy sighed, blowing soap bubbles from her face. She leaned her head back against the tiled wall behind her, with hope that perhaps she could block out the negative energy that persisted in annoying her. It wasn't working. 'Dammit...' There were times in Mindy's life where she couldn't help but be happy. She was always happy no matter what the reason, and if someone she loved wasn't happy as well, she would make them happy. Now, all she could think about was sadness. Maybe she was turning emo? 'Oh dear gods, no!' But suddenly, there was a voice to calm her. 'No what?' The voice that caught her attention, came from none other than Mindy's lovable boyfriend; thusly, a feeling that a hot bubble bath couldn't possibly stir up, had risen the girl's spirit. 'Markus! How are you feeling, dearie?' The look on the tall man's face said it all; he wore a few stitches on his forehead, and a miserable scowl to go with it. His shoulders drooped, as though he were dying for a little sleep himself. 'Surviving,' he managed to mutter. As if he actually hadn't noticed it before, Markus' eyes began to scan the tub his girlfriend was laying in. Enticing, indeed, but the splitting headache he had was requesting him to save any 'feel good' urges for another time. 'I figured you would have been sleeping.' Mindy shook her head, bringing her wet hands through her hair and pushing it back. 'I couldn't.' 'Yeah,' Markus lazily answered back, 'I couldn't get any sleep last night, either.' 'I don't blame ya, hun. If I had to spend the night in a hospital, I doubt I'd be doing much sleep either.' Markus frowned. 'But you got to sleep here last night. What's the matter?' 'I didn't have you with me,' Mindy confessed with a blush. She blurted it out so matter-of-factly, however, that it caused Markus' frown to grow larger. 'I'm sorry. If there was any way I could've been back sooner, I-' 'No,' the young woman cut him off, 'it's perfectly fine. You're here now, that's all I care about.' Markus felt relieved by the warm compliment. After everything that had happened to them, he would never get tired of hearing Mindy say that she cared that he was around. To him, it practically felt as though it gave his life actual meaning; Hell, if it weren't for her, he'd still be a mindless drug junkie. Now, he was a strict drinker- and only then when he really, really needed a beer. Except there was something off about things... something just wasn't right. 'You're not thinking about going after Bobby Brawler, are you?' Markus asked. Mindy expected that eventually her boyfriend would ask such a question, but that didn't necessarily make it one that she wanted to answer. 'I... I don't want to think about that now,' she lied, 'you're here, that's what matters. I can think about getting him back some other time.' Markus wanted to make a word against it; anything to deter her from being put in any kind of danger, and yet... 'She's a wrestler, she's used to danger. And after the other day, how could I tell her not to get herself into trouble?' As concerned as he was, there was nothing he could say. It made him wish that sometimes he could just shut the fuck up and drink a cold one. Wouldn't that have made life easier for everyone involved? But Mindy didn't deserve a boyfriend who just wanted to take it easy. She deserved someone who genuinely cared. 'If you feel wrong about last night,' Markus began, breaking his sentence to cough ever so slightly before he continued, 'about Brawler and everything, that's fine. It's okay if you wanna get back at him, because I want to fuck him up now myself. At the same time, though... just be careful, alright?' Just be careful. Markus wanted to think of something more definitive, but that was the best he could come up with. Better than nothing, at least. And hey, by the look on Mindy's face, she seemed to be giving it actual contemplation. And then, her eyes narrowed. 'I can still kick his ass, though, right?' she asked. Markus' turn to think. 'Eh... as long as it doesn't draw attention to police.' 'Deal.' Smiling, Markus leaned forward and kissed his lover on the cheek. 'Good. So, how are the ribs there, champ?' Mindy slid one hand over her stomach, and sighed. 'They're gettin' there, hunny bunny. They'll have to if I wanna be ready for Friday, anyways.' A confused expression came across Markus' face. 'What's Friday?' 'The letter from HSW,' Mindy smiled, 'they want me to face Jonny Kae this Friday as this interpromotional deal for their debut on ESPN2. Whaddya think?' Whatever Markus thought, it couldn't have been bad, as he replied by pressing his lips against the sugarholic's. 'I love it.' 'I love you,' Mindy grinned. Another kiss later, and the two embraced- getting Markus' clothes wet with soap suds in the process. 'Well then. Perhaps a change of clothes is in order, and maybe then we can see if we can help eachother get some real sleep.' 'Sounds good to me.' If one were to ask Mindy how her Monday went, she'd have said that she'd never had a more peaceful rest than she had in ages. It was good to feel safe again. But at the same time.. she knew that eventually, she would have to wake up... and whether something bad or good was waiting for her, was unpredictable. 10.21.05 | Louisville, Kentucky | 6:58 PM Inside the arena where HSW Shocker would be making its ESPN2 debut, Mindy Mercer went relatively undetected by fans making their way to their seats. It was a surprise to her feeling so unknown, but then again, she was unknown when she shocked everyone by winning the World Title from Train back in July. 'Hm, someone sure has the crowd's attention tonight.' Up ahead of Mindy, she could spot people crowding around who could've only been a bigger star- or maybe even a bigger group of stars. As Mindy eased on by people to see who everyone was gawking at, it was indeed only one man- one she didn't believe she was seeing. Christian Zenith. '....Fuck.' If only she knew how much his presence would affect her showing at Dark Rituals in two days, she'd either be wishing that she never went to Shocker... or she'd be thanking her lucky stars for it.
