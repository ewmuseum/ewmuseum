Gift Horse....

23 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/23/05 The virtual geography that is Global changes yet again. For a long time it's been the place of the likes of the Hunting Grounds, and others like Ruckus, Inc or the groups around that don't seem to be really doing anything as of late. The others have been taking it upon themselves to go after each other, but how long can that really last? In the end one will be on top. This coming week in Fairbanks, Alaska, the leader of the Hunting Grounds will take on the best member of Ruckus, Inc in a match for the Global world title. Christian Zenith versus the defending champion, Train. Shadow of the Hunting Grounds will take on a man who's apparently sided in some form with those that don't like the HG in Emotion, and Dawn MacFarlane will fight for the contendership of the Platinum Title. And while not with HG, Shadow's paramour, Virgil Ambrosi, will attempt to regain the Platinum title who's holder just went through 5 people to retain it when it was merged with the Television and Cruiserweight Titles. All matches that could change the way Global moves into it's next season buildup, heading into it's December break for the holidays. Matches that could scar the face of the federation if the group bent on thumbing their nose at the federation and it's fans are allowed to get anywhere. But a new force has formed, one that bears a long standing tradition in this sport, one that holds the honor of the contest above everything else. One that follow simple rules. 1)Don't cheat unless your opponents have already tried to do so, or have a history of doing so. 2)Don't do anything that would harm your honor or that of the Brigade. 3)Use all of the weapons available to you, especially when it comes to inside the ring. Don't find others, use your opponents shortcomings to your advantage. 4)The most important: Until your opponents start to rely on others to help them, you will not allow others to get involved on your behalf. From opening bell to ending bell, the match should remain a contest between you and your opponents only. 5)All of the other rules are null and void if the rules of the contest allow them to be so. Always follow the rules of the match above all else. For that is the spirit behind the honor of the match, not whether or not you can win or loose. What do these rules mean? They mean that it doesn't matter if you win or loose, as long as you tried your best and you didn't loose any standing in doing so. There is no dishonor in loosing if your opponent couldn't beat you within the rules of the contest. But as soon as they decide that the fast path is the path to their victory... the kid gloves come off. Such things as taking the referee out, using illegal weapons, and outside interference are the paths to a victory that will get you, in the end, nothing. These are the rules of the wrestling faction known as the KnightMare Brigade a fully supported faction of the Order of the Hammer. A faction started almost 7 years ago by a man and woman, fraternal twins, known as Sean and Fey McClancy at the time. Both of them have gone on to become champions in many federations, as well as their brothers and allies in the Order and Brigade. Originally they were seperate, but years later when the leader of the Order, and Fey McClancy merged their families in matrimony, so did their wrestling factions and their outside organization. There are some that would say the Brigade won in that fight, for they remain in the wrestling world. But those in the know fully understand that the Order is there, just not as a wrestling faction. They are the support, the foundation to the Brigade's success and training partners. The Flaming Unicorn that is the symbol of the Brigade has trampled others in many federations over the years: The GIWF/UWF, PWW, RAW2K, TWF, 4WW, BDW, PTC's Extreme Division, and various tournaments around the wrestling world. But now there is a new McClancy, a man who's held many championships in his own right that has inherited the mantle of the leader of the Brigade. While other factions fall under the leadership of his Cousins, and their married partners... this one is new. This one is fresh. This one has something that the others don't. This man is the American-born cousin of the original McClancys who started the faction known as the KnightMare Brigade... Blaise William Todd McClancy, a Las Vegas Native. Born from a Paiute woman and his Irish immigrant father. He has collected some that have proven themselves in the Global world for the last few months. Gray and Joshua Brand, the tag team known as Unite... both of the Saquamish people in Washington state that gave the world the great diplomat, Chief Seattle or as he was known to the others that had taken his world... Noah Sealth. Both share a trait with Blaise that gives them a brotherhood that couldn't be forged in any other way. Joshua is a half Native, like Blaise. Gray holds a secret in his blood that is even worse if it ever got out, but it is one that Blaise also carries... a curse to the minds of some, a boon in the minds of others. And then there is the full blooded Lakota Sioux woman, Danielle Northsky. The current Global Champion, Virgil Ambrosi's opponent this week. A woman who has a secret to her personal life that is going to rock the world outside of the one that most know. Together with Blaise's girlfriend, the Japanese woman known as Ukyo Kushinaga, they're formed the latest in the history of the Brigade. But this faction isn't one built on just wrestling principles of going out there and 'kicking the other guys ass all over the place.' this one has something else to hold it together. The main competitors of this group are all representatives of the different Native American Nations. A collection of people within the faction that for the first time are only found from the area of the current national identity of the United States of America. But they're not from the states. Dani was born on Lakota Land, Josh and Gray were born in on the Port Madison Reservation, and Blaise was born in the Valley of Fire. They're from the countries that within the United States that most forget are there. They are representatives of the Native American Nations and that's how they've chosen to identify themselves. And now the time comes to start things on a roll. Virgil Ambrosi has his work cut out for him. He's not done to well against the woman known as Northsky before. But there are two who have got to be quaking in their boots right now: Elia Smith and Mindy Mercer. Two women... One is the now former Television Champion, and has gone zero for four against Dani Northsky. Elia Smith, or 'Cowboy Barbie' as Dani likes to call her... can she overcome her shortcomings and finally take one out against a real opponent? Or Mindy Mercer, the woman who beat Train, then beat Dawn MacFarlane, only to fall to Train a few weeks later; The former World Champion. Now reduced to opening the show a week later, against the former TV Champion and a newcomer. The prize of this match is the number two contendership for Platinum title held by Dani. Dawn McFarlane is also taking on Ken Patridge, Jr for the number one contendership later in the show. But why should Dani's match be considered for the number one contendership? She's facing someone who's not held a title in some time, and she's only the former Cruiserweight champion? While Blaise has to face a former Television and World Champion? Eh, there might be some things to take into consideration here afterwards. But everything must have a plan. First and foremost, Mindy Mercer and Elia Smith must learn that this isn't the Blaise McClancy in his last home we was always pulled aside by his cousins and asked to put people over for the sake of keeping the rumors and that down that he'd ever get special treatment. There is no one in Global who would be able to look at him and say that he's not earned what he will get. Former World Champion? Former Television Champion? Guess what ladies, that means you lost it some time ago and someone else is holding the glory that you once held. In the case of Mercer, it's the man she beat for it before he took a little break. In the case of Smith, it's the woman she will never, ever, beat. This is the path that Blaise wants to take though. Right... through... them. Others are only a speed bump in his path to the top. Fairbanks, Alaska Fairbanks International Airport The trip from Las Vegas to Fairbanks using Alaska Airlines had been pretty uneventful. This was one of the few events that forced Dani to fly though, and Blaise had to deal with her for the first time in this aspect. Danielle Northsky wasn't afraid of anything except her feet leaving the ground, of more then a few stories up. She'd launch herself all around the ring, twisting and turning, and she was fine when it came to taxing the airplane, but when it came to getting ready for take off... It was a good thing that both Josh and Gray knew about this, and the proper things had been put into place, such as the use of the pills she took when she absolutely had to fly. She sat down and then by the time the plane was getting ready to take off, she was fast asleep. Once she was out, Blaise couldn't help stir the ire of her boyfriend, Josh Brand, by making a few old A-Team jokes, but it was all in jest, and Josh eventually figured that out. Blaise had spent the flight on the phone though, a few hours was all it took, but he had to make sure things on the home front were ready and that the courts all knew about everything. They were flying up on Friday and going to be spending an extended weekend up there. Weather was already showing highs only in the thirties and a light dusting of snow was on the ground in Fairbanks. This was not something that Blaise needed to bring his daughters to, just for a 5 day jaunt. It was good that Global only traveled once a month or so, but this type of climate change meant that his 'hired nanny', Ms. Leanan's handmaiden, Kaylee was going to stay in their house and be 'Auntie Kay? for 5 days while Blaise and Ukyo went up to Alaska. However, stuff like this for the period of ninety days, or which there was about sixty left, had to be cleared through the courts. They were happy that Blaise had taken the job with Global. It would keep money coming in, and that he'd moved from his former open desert home on the reservation to the large house in the Queensridge community. Everything was in place, and Blaise was making sure to follow the letter of the law that the judge had put down for him. Upon arriving in Fairbanks, they were all going to be taking over a place called the 7 Gables. It looked like there were only a few hotels and that in this place that by any other state's standard would be considered a small town, but here it was as metro as you got other then Anchorage. Their little shithole of an arena only sat a little over six thousand people, less then the Global arena back home. But guess you can't discount Alaska for it's people either. Supposedly they had sold out and people would be flying or driving here to see this event. Some of the local bars were even going to show it on a pay per view basis, charging people to come and see it. Got to rake in that money somehow. Getting off the plane was reletivly simple, and it looked like fate was in their favor, as all of their luggage had made it. All were staying in the 7 Gables bed and breakfast, so it would be easier to take care of things, and keep away from the likes of the other wrestlers. They filled up a few of the local taxis, more like 4 x 4 Subaru wagons. Josh, Gray and Dani took one and allowed Ukyo and Blaise to take another. Ridding to the place they were staying though allowed some other decisions to be made. Ukyo, bundled up in a thick skiers jacket, and matching pants looked to be the most uncomfortable with the predicament of their location. “You know, maybe I should have just waited for you to come back down to Las Vegas. At least it's currently comfortable down there.” Blaise laughed at her. She'd never been this far north in her life, not even the northern reaches of Japan got this cold without traveling far up from sea level. Although Alaska wasn't exactly flat either. ?At least the rooms have fireplaces. We'll be able to keep warm. So, did you talk to Gray about the showing of solidarity?? Ukyo looked at him, a not so good look on her face, but one that showed she had and that Gray understood what had to be done. “He wasn't too happy, but he understands. I think when it comes down to it, he just doesn't know me or trust someone taking over Dani's case. He sees her as a little sister.” Blaise just hit the matter of fact statement on that one. ?Everything goes the way it currently is, and she will be. But that doesn't change the fact that we need to show up as a team and keep things going as a team. He understood right?? Ukyo just nods her head. “Yes. I'm now the valet for all of our entities: You, Dani and Unite. He's not a manager or valet, but a wrestler. But he does know, also, that this means that I'll be able to take over most of those duties like booking flights, rooms and that too. I'm not ready to get back into the ring as a wrestler after the fall. I could do it, but it's not on my list of things to do.” Blaise held her hand and squeezed it tight. Just a week before asking for his release from his former home, Ukyo had a final run in with the man she had disowned as her brother... and he had hit her so hard, she'd gone over the ropes and tumbled to the outside. Her neck and back took the brunt of the damage, and she was still healing. She was tougher then most, but she wasn't ready to do it if she didn't have to. Now she would take up the position as a valet, get healed and decide what it was that she truly wanted to do with her life. She had only started wrestling when trying to find out just who had killed her other brother, Haru, but that had all fallen apart with the apparent deception of her other brother, Kyo. Now she was at an impasse. The only thing that kept her even near the business that had cost her both of her brothers in some way, shape or form... was Blaise. “We talked about it. It's what you want to do right now. I'll support your decision and enforce it. I'm the lieutenant of this little squad. I know Gray's issues are two fold. He's seeing me as a threat on two levels. One for the squad. He was the sergeant in charge. Second for his pack. I'm a new alpha coming in and usurping him. Fortunately he's still got the human head in charge most of the time.” Ukyo didn't want to think about that. If both men decided to just let their animalistic sides go, that would be a fight that would rip this little group apart before it even started. But she knew Gray was smarter then that. He knew how the non-garou side of this equation worked. Blaise had been in the organization longer, had done more, even made it into sub-groups that Gray didn't. Gray had a desire to succeed and he'd do it. But he'd have to do it by playing by the rules. Josh was the one that didn't seem to want to follow the spirit of the rules, but more the letter of them... Just like Blaise at most times. They sat there holding hands, as the taxi pulled around a corner and then stopped in front of the 7 Gables Inn. It was one of the largest bed and breakfast type places that Blaise had ever seen. It was more then one building, with apartments, and full on suites. All of them were going to take up one of their separate 2 bedroom annex apartments. More like a separate building with two bedrooms and then a queen size sofa bed in the living room for Gray. The room had enough space for all of them, with a private fully stocked kitchen and entertainment centers in the living and bed rooms. Combine that with a large bathroom and a large covered jacuzzi it made for a nice place for the group to stay. The weather stung everyone's face when they got out of the taxis and started to unload all of their luggage and gear from them. The three men taking most of the brunt of the load to get them into the building as quickly as possible. Ukyo headed into the main building to get the keys and that, Dani following closely behind her. Everyone was bundled and tried to overload themselves so that they'd not have to do more than one trip. All three men looked like they had internal steam engines with the look of their breath frosting over into a fog each time they exhaled. The three hauled the various suitcases, and other assorted bags into the second building, through the green house entrance that housed a garden that shouldn't be in bloom at this time of year. As soon as they were in the living area, Ukyo and Dani showed up with their keys. As soon as Ukyo had the doors open, the men filed in, dumping their cargo onto the floor in the foyer to the apartment. Both girls quickly got out of their heavy jackets and started to help the men in their own way. As Ukyo make sure they all got their luggage dropped down in a way as to ensure no damage, Dani headed over to the fireplace and started to get a nice wood fire going in the living room. “Hey, I'm getting this one fired up. I'll get our room next and then your guys' room if you want me to.” Dani offered that knowing after the last two weeks of hanging out with Ukyo that she probably had the most experience in camping and thus starting a wood fire. “You do that. Let's get them thawed out and then we can get unpacked.” Ukyo answered her. Blaise hung up his heavy coat, and then grabbed a few of the bags of the design that he and Ukyo used and started heading for the room they would be using. Ukyo helped Josh loose the last of his bags that had been slung over his shoulder and then headed into the room with Blaise, grabbing what was left of their bags. As Ukyo was slowly closing the door to their bedroom, she heard Dani talking to the boys. “Alright, someone find my cell phone and see if we have an antenna. I need to call Kaylee and check in on Angel.” Kaylee was not only watching Blaise's twin daughters for this five day vacation, but Dani's own equivalent of a child in her new Macaw, Angel. Ukyo shut the door with a slight push, making sure it didn't make any noise in case some of the other patrons were trying to sleep. At this time of the year the Alaska nights were long, and there was no telling who would be awake or asleep. She walked over and tossed one of the bags on the four poster queen-sized bed and opened it to start to unpack. She watched Blaise out of the corner of his eye as he opened his laptop case, taking the computer out and setting it up on the table in the room. He broke out all of the gear, including the one that gave it a satellite uplink to the secure network that the Order used. “You know, you don't need to take the job everywhere.” Blaise turned and smiled at her as he powered it all up. “Kaylee will be using the computer at our place. It's cheaper and the phones won't have a good signal up here. So this way we can cheat and use the Order's resources. My babies are considered an Order case anyway. They kinda have a unique father.” Ukyo smiled at that one, he was too darned right at that. ?I'm surprised at this place. Why are there so few people living here?? Blaise gave the obvious answer. ?Maybe because it's too fucking cold?? He was a Vegas native, winter to him shouldn't be this cold. Wait... it was only autumn. “That may be, but wouldn't you think that would lead to more snuggling in the bed at night.” She smiled wide at him as she leaned on one of the posts on the corner of the bed. “Families up in the mountains in Japan tend to have ten or more children, because there is very little else to do.” He got up from his seat as his laptop was booting up and went over and grabbed her around her waist. She laughed and giggled a bit in an outburst. Blaise then got a good look out the window and then stopped. He walked over and looked out. “Strange.” Ukyo walked over to join him. Outside was a woman with long blonde hair poking out from her stocking cap. She stood there, looking up at their room. Her skin was a slight bronze cast, almost as if she, herself was mixed in blood like Blaise. ?Yeah, we'll need to draw the curtains if people are going to stare at us. You think she knows that some wrestling celebrities are staying here for something?? “I don't know.” Blaise answered her with a hint of question on his breath. Just as he looked down at the woman, she nodded to him and then dropped something onto the ground. She turned and walked away. ?What was that?? Ukyo looked on in disbelief. “Never look a gift horse in the mouth. I'll go take a look.” “Not without me you won't. We need to head out for some food as it is. We have a kitchen, but it's empty. I'll unpack when we get back.” Both turned away from the window at the same time and headed out to the main room. Dani was standing there, with her cell phone in hand, looking at it, pacing the room to try to get a good antenna. Blaise addressed her indirectly, actually addressing Gray who was inside their bedroom, getting the luggage situated. He was safer with it then his little brother. ?Gray, in our bedroom, my laptop is setup with a sat-link. Kaylee knows to have it on at home. When you're ready go ahead and call my home computer, it has the information for it. She'll get the page and she can report things. We won't get a cell signal up here. It's too much into B.F.E. for that. ? Blaise and Ukyo both grabbed their coats from the hangers next to the front door. Ukyo made sure she had her key for the room. ?We're going to head out to get some dinner for everyone and some supplies for the kitchen. We'll be right back, okay?? Ukyo opened the door as Gray walked out into the main room. ?Yeah. That's cool, what's up?? Blaise looked him square. “Hopefully nothing. But I had a message dropped off that I need to look into also. Go ahead and use the sat-link to get caught up on anything and let Kaylee know we're here and all. We'll be right back.” Gray nodded as they headed out. Quickly the two then left the room, Blaise in the lead. They hit the outside after going through the green house and both expressed their displeasure at the weather again. They made it across Birch street and to the place where the lady had been standing. On the ground in the snow was a business card showing a location: 'North Star United Christian Ministries'. On the back was written one word... “Come”. ?I think we can take that as a hint?? Blaise nodded, wishing for once he had free reign to use his other abilities to adjust to a new area. A nice layer of silver tipped red fur was much better then this synthetic material jacket schtick. Keeping everything as nonchalant as possible, they took each other's hand and started to walk down the sidewalk in the direction they had seen the girl walk away in. It had to be almost a mile or so down the road when they saw the small church with a group of pickup trucks and that parked in the lot. The sign gave the name off the card, including the name of the pastor. ?A pastor? So a baptist group? Why not use that term?? Blaise appeared a bit confused until he saw that most of the trucks in the lot bore the signs used to denote Native Alaskans and their vehicles for their driving privileges. They walked in and noticed that the place was in league with three other local churches based on the signs on the bulletin board: The Fairbanks Native Assembly of God and the Morning Star Baptist Church in the nearby town of Healy. “Interesting...” As soon as they entered into the main area, they saw that a small group of people were there, seated in the front. But a lone person, the lady from outside was seated near the back, looking at them. Both worked out of their coats, as the fireplaces were lit and giving warmth to the followers, all Native Americans, probably of the local Tanana Nation, an offshoot of the Yukon peoples. This lady though didn't look like she was of their kind, if she was, she was mixed. The blonde hair on her head gave that away immediately. “I must say, you look at little out of place.” The lady smiled and retorted. “So do you. But then again, it's not always that someone meets those of the blood of Tautha. It's been a long while since I've had the pleasure of their company.” Blaise nodded, as he looked the person over. She turned and acknowledged Ukyo. “And a member of the Kunitsu-Kami. That's an interesting mix for a couple. Although if I didn't see otherwise, I'd say you were a daughter of Amaterasu, herself.” Ukyo took the compliment for what it was worth, Amaterasu was the Japanese sun goddesss, and also a goddess of beauty. This woman knew more then she gave away in her first take. “Well, you seem to know a lot about us, you put us at a disadvantage.” Blaise was straight forward, as usual in his take on things. The letter of the rules he played by said that he could not manifest any of his powers or abilities in front of mortals. The woman had chosen a church that had worshipers in it. He could not shift his views to that of the Veil, the umbra at all here, they would see. The woman turned to the man who stood up on the pulpit nodded at her and then started to move the worshipers out of the room, taking them to the back where it appeared they were having a bit of something like a potluck celebration. A few minutes later, the pastor turned back to the woman and nodded to her as he closed the door to the back room. “Lieutenant McClancy, you may rest assured I know why I came here. It is a safe place. You're safe now to do whatever you feel you need to. But note, you are in my domain right now, so the proper respects should be followed.” Blaise didn't shift his views, he decided that he would play along with the game. ?Fine then, you know my name. How about I inquire as to whom I'm speaking?? She smiled at him an almost cruel smile. “They would call me Gilda in this world, but a long time ago they called me Gyhldeptis. It's a Haida name that indicates their goddess of the forest and trees. A long, long time ago, before the Great Deluge, I helped your father's people in Tir Na Nog.” Ukyo reached her hands into her pockets and started to look around as she moved into Hunter mode. She was just as much a trained soldier as Blaise was. Blaise took the comfortable spot and sat down next to the lady. “So, we have a name. But I find it hard to believe that you would be a Native goddess and have helped my people.” She smiled wide at him. “Why Mr. McClancy, you are as much a jigsaw puzzle as any of these people. You're heritage is rather mixed up and it has no one 'people' to speak of. Your father's people respect the forest and trees as the great creator made them. They've even at times become one with them. Your mother's people didn't have much for trees. Not many grow in the great basin area.” Blaise just looked at her, not answering. She continued. ?So, tell me... why are you here? And who is with you?? Blaise chuckled. ?Not quite up with today's world?? “The good Pastor showed me the flyers for the gladiatorial games that you will compete in on Monday. Is that why you're here? That's where I got your name from. He's associated somewhat with your Order. He bears a ring with the all seeing eye on it.” Blaise nodded at that, the baptists had no aversion to Freemasons taking their vows. But in this remote area it wouldn't act as a contact. ?Yes, I'm here for these fights. I'm in the opening match. Are you wanting to come see?? She looked confused... ?What of the other woman with you? Does she fight?? She opened up the local newspaper, which had a front page story about the wrestlers coming in for the event. Contained in it was a large graphic depicting the card for the event. ?Yes. She's the defending Platinum Champion. Why do you ask?? Blaise pointed to the spot on the graphic showing Dani's match. “Danielle Northsky... that name is familiar.” Ukyo walked over and put her hand on Blaise's shoulder. He knew the sequence of squeezes she then used, showing that the coast was fully clear. A signal to him. “She's full Lakota Sioux. Her native name is Sirius Star.” The woman looked wide eyed at him. ?She is special? This woman who fights men?? Blaise chuckled. “Actually this week she's facing a man who is not a man anymore. Don't know if its the fact that his heart doesn't beat anymore, if the fact that Shadow's got his balls for him. But he's no man. He's one of Cain's children.” She hesitated at that, Blaise had hit a cord with her that he didn't expect. She knew the legend, she had taken refuge in a church of a Christian faith. He touched her hand in passing and then it all flooded in on him... visions in his mind as to the true nature of this being. He saw her in her past glory, sailing above the forests of the new world on a pair of wings that looked as if they had been taken from a golden eagle. A name came to him. “Zuphlas...” She pulled her hand back from him, dropping the flyer for the card. Ukyo looked down at him. “The Angel... I stand corrected, Virtue that was put in charge of the Trees of the Earth.” She started to get up. ?No, I'm sorry. I didn't mean it. Why did you try to find us? Why are you interested in Dani?? She stopped. ?I mean no harm. But I sensed it as your plane landed. Something I've not felt in a long time. I've not used my given celestial name in centuries. Who is she? She's human, yes?? Ukyo answered as Blaise tried to come up with an answer. “Not exactly.” The woman... being... in front of them tried to grasp what she was feeling. ?What do you mean? Not exactly? She was born on this Earth, right? There hasn't been a new falling has there?? Blaise played the recovery here. ?She was born on Earth, there hasn't been a new falling that we know of. Why are you here?? She looked down at the ground. Blaise immediately knew the answer. Being in charge of the Earth's forests had led her to seeing and knowing the men of Earth too. ?You fell with the others? The Watchers?? She smiled. “Even Enoch was a sexist and forgot to mention that some of us were daughters of God who fell for the sons of man. She is not a gibborim, that is obvious.” “No, she's a bit small for that.” She looked to be loosening up after her secret had come out. “True, and a new batch of them would only be bad. The standing punishment for such is destruction of your world. That's not scheduled to happen again for quite some time. I'd hate to see someone speed it up. Why can I sense her though? It's as if one of my own kind came into town. Other then Rahab, I've not seen another of my kind in decades. If she's born of the earth.” Blaise arched his eyebrow at the mention of the Angel of the Sea mentioned, but knowing that the people up here relied heavily on sea travel for food, it made sense to him. “She's a special case. Maybe you could enlighten us. It appears that she's the daughter of one of your kind, only six generations removed.” ?Seventh Daughter of one of us?? She was catching on. “Yes, she's the daughter of a legend. To the Lakota there was a being who came down from the sky named Morning Star, who mated with a Lakota woman and produced a giant hero named Fallen Star.” She stopped him. ?Morning Star?? “Yes.” ?Kokabel? That's Kokabel's great granddaughter?? He nodded. ?A few generations removed, but yes. Essentially. She's manifested on us now twice. She's one of your kind, but born here. She's not showing any of the traits of the half-breeds. She's a throw back. Do you know of Kokabel?? “Yes, you could say that being a Virtue in charge of an aspect, we are brother and sister. But he ran afoul with one of the Nephilim and their war drew in many peoples. Eventually Kokabel fled and hid after binding his enemy. But the enemy had many agents, witches as you would call them, who serve him. They can summon some of the lesser demons and devils from this side of the earth.” Blaise cocked his head to the side a bit, inquiring as to whom she meant. ?Enemy? One of the Nephilim?? “Yes, those who fell before we did. Those who fell out of pride instead of lust. The ones whom served under Sammael before he fell and took the name of Mastema or Ibilis.” ?Who?? ?We do not mention such names as those that the Nephilim took after their fall. To do so calls upon them. Even bound he would know my location. If he were to ever escape, it would be not long before I would be fighting the seraphim of sorcery who tried to take this side of this planet and make it his own kingdom. I will give you the clues you need. But nothing more. How long will Kokabel's blood be here?? “We leave Tuesday night. We have five days here.” ?Hopefully that is long enough that she doesn't attract any others. You do know that those of my kind can find each other almost as if we were... what do they call it in the civilized areas? Lojack?? Blaise sighed. “No, we didn't know that. Our take on your kind is what we're learning from Dani, and the one that resides in a shared state within our leader. That one is no longer of a physical form, but is nothing but rendered spirit. His name is Suriel. On top of that I've had dealings with one named Zadikiel.” She gasped. ?Zadikiel, what came of him?? Blaise was in the middle of talking though, so he continued. “But other then that, our time with you kind is limited. Lately others have appeared to be coming out into the open. Zadikiel actually tried to play a game for some souls which I got involved in.” He looked up at her with a rather serious look. “The arch angel of destiny had one of his own.” At that, Blaise looked down away from the visage the woman put up. She was not prepared to know of the death of one of her kind, let alone one of the Thrones. ?What are your Order's plans for up here?? The new question took him a little off guard. Her voice had gone from concern in questioning, to now almost giving commands. “Nothing... there isn't exactly a lot of activity up here other then Yeti and some Wendigo.” ?Now that you know about me up here, what are you going to do?? “Nothing. You're information is useful, but nothing more then that. As long as you're not collecting souls up here to try to make a play for all the natives, and form your own kingdom. Then I'd have to come back up here. In the meantime, you're a surprise, but you have given me some information. No one else needs to know you're here.” She looked satisfied with that answer. ?I have a request for you then?? ?Yes?? “Don't tell her I'm here. But you can guess she's an anomaly to me. I'll be looking closely at this human born daughter of Kokabel. It would explain why he recently decicded to come back down for a bit.” ?Back down?? She started to walk away, slowly. “Back down. Out of the 200 of us who fell out of lust for what we saw here, he was the only one who was allowed to return. And that was after he had bound the evil one, and his taken down his co-patriot. Heaven was desperate, the stars had no path with both of the 'traffic controllers' for them down here having a nice hedonistic time. When it came down to it though, Kokabel was allowed to reascend back to the heavenly place. While the rest of us down here learned that with longevity comes boredom, with boredom comes trouble, and with trouble comes humans with torches and pitchforks... or 7 comets that wash this planet clean, killing all of our loved children. I'll not go through that again.” The woman walked towards the front of the church, towards the door that the other worshipers had been sanctioned away. “I'll be watching her out of intrigue. Nothing more. In the meantime, I give you passage into my domain up here without any hassles. But mind you Mr. McClancy, if I find your Order sneaking around my forests after you have left, I will make sure the Akhlut, itself, finds you and brings you back up to me for punishment. One wolf can't hide from another.” With that, she disappears into the other room. Blaise and Ukyo both head for the main door, back out into the cold. “Well, that was interesting.” Ukyo continued to look around as she commented. “Yeah, very. Something tells me that this information is probably the best we would get anywhere else.” ?What do you mean?? “Again, you never look a gift horse in the mouth. Let's get some dinner, head back, unpack and then get some rest. Other then that, I'll need to do some research. Just got to figure out how to do that without setting off any alarms.” “I'll leave that for you.” They headed out, noticing a small restaurant that was in between there and the Inn. A small bit of snow was starting to fall. They headed in it's direction, intent on using the phone there to call for what the others wanted from their menu.... Maybe coming to this wasteland of a state was a good thing after all. End...
