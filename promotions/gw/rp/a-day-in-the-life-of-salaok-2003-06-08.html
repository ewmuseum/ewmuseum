A day in the life of Salaok

8 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/8/03 Sitting at his desk, Samir Labanka rubs his chin while looking at the blank computer screen in front of him. That was the problem, it was blank and for the last half an hour, it had been that way. Samir was probably one of the youngest members of the Global Inc company at only eighteen but his position in the company was significant. It had been Samir's job to create GW specials for TV when it was require. His schooling alone was reason enough for him to have received this job as throughout high school, he had been top of his media and writing classes by a fair margin. But this day had been a hard one. A day of filming and now the pieces had to be put together but finally it was starting to come to him. One key after the other begins to press down and trigger the letters to appear on the screen with ease. All letters except for the letter 'L' which constantly had to be forced down as it didn't work correctly anymore. It forced him to slow down at him and concentrate while doing it but it was now nothing more than a pain as when the thoughts are finally flowing, the words appear with great speed on the screen. What a day I've just had. The filming of 'A day in the life of a GW superstar'. When the marketing department decided that this would be a great thing to do and they ask me do put one together as a trail run, I was over the moon. Nothing like this had ever really been done within the current Global Wrestling and this would be my chance at putting together something that was more of a look at the personal side behind these men and women who have lit up the stage and the TV screens for the past four years. So of course when I was first asked to put this together, I had names running through my mind and each one with valid reasons for having this special done on them. But one name stood out for me more than any other. A man who had been apart of the federation since it's very beginning, a man who had been to the top of GW and stayed there ever since, a man who has been instrumental in the revamping of the federation and become Co-owner. Of course I'm talking about Salaok Davis. A man who everyone sees as the villain of Global Wrestling, but no one has ever truly been able to see what this man does when he's not around the company. This led me to have to go and see Salaok about this and that visit to his office was one that will more than likely remain in my mind for the rest of my life. What I thought was going to be one hell of a daunting experience, turned out to be anything but. As I walked into his office and found him with his feet up on the desk and a cigarette hanging from his mouth, watching the big screen TV which he had installed when he first took the office. Peering at me with the stone white eyes which have often stuck me as extremely bazaar, he motioned me to sit and I began to explain the whole deal. I was completely sure if he was paying attention to me or the TV which was directly behind my head but he seemed to nod at the correct times and often gave input. But when I finally mentioned that I would be doing the special on him, the TV was instantly turned off and a smile came over his face. Not the smile that we are all used to seeing on the stage where you know there is some sort of evil intent building behind it, but this smile was one of real excitement and pleasure. That alone was something that instantly struck me but what was even lessed expected was his responce. 'Well Samir, I'm honor, really. Your going to need to be around me all day long to do it properly aren't you?' That comment was of course true and a nod was my only responce as I felt as if he was aboutt o tell me that he couldn't allow it. I mean we have all grown to know about the business of The Lair whether we want to or not. It was The Lair which had funded the federation during it's worst possible time where the company looked like it would have to be announced as insolvent. But then Salaok gave me a responce which was almost enough to knock me off my seat. 'Well then, if that's the case, let me know hen you are wanting to do it and I will have some of the spare rooms in the mansion set up for you and your screw. You can stay over the night before and the night after in order to make sure that you get everything you want to. I mean it wouldn't be a day in the life, if you only caught half the day now would it?' Confusion? You bet your ass it was. A man who most people had often either feared, or just didn't think was approachable had just offered to allow me to stay at his 'castle' for two nights in order to do the job correctly. And that is where I sit now. This room that I sit in with my computer is something that I have never seen before. No, I correct myself. I may have seen something like this in 'Interview with a Vampire' or something like that. The old romantic gothic castle wasn't haunting at all, but warm and extremely comfertable. I guess when you've managed your funds as well as Salaok has, you can afford stuff like this. But now I guess I should start from the very beginning of this day which for my crew and I started at 5:30am. Salaok's family wasn't like The Osbournes where they sleep until 11:00am. This family was up and about early and so we had to be up before then to catch everything. The night before had been one of a disturbed sleep as the excitement of doing this day was almost too much for me but clearly it wasn't going to effect Salaok or his family's line of thinking or behaviour as it wasn't until around 2:30 in the morning that Salaok finally came home. Where he had been I had no idea and I was in no place to ask. But I sat in the dinning room with the cook and my camera man. Yes, that's right, I said cook. Salaok had hired a cook, infact he normally has two I was tod, but the other was off as it was her mother's birthday and Salaok had given her the day off. Agin, this isn't something I expected from the co-owner of the company. The cook told me that the family would normally get up at around 6:00 and he was correct in saying that as slowly, one by one, the family and those who stayed in the house began to get themselves out of bed and wake themselves up. Lisha was the first to actually acknowledge me by offering me a cup of coffee which at that time of the morning, there was no way I would ever turn that down. I had never truely noticed just how good looking Lisha really is. Perhaps it was the morning sun making everything seem full of life but she slender, yet toned body had a gracefulness about it that was hard to remove your eyes from. And of course, her bright red hair which draped itself over her shoulders and cupped her breasts. Not that I was looking them on purpose of course. It was then that I felt a hand on my shoulder and his voice sounding near my ear in a interestin hello. 'Not bad hey man.' Salaok stood there with a smile on his face and then sat down beside me. His long black hair was pulled back in a pony tail letting me see for the first time his entire face. Without the black hair creating a dark frame for his face, he seemed a lot less intimidating which is probably one of the reasons in which he never wears it up like that at work. Wearing some comfertable old jeans and no shirt, he leans forward and props himself up on the table with his elbow. The night before clearing had taken it's toll on him but still I wasn't going to ask. Then it happened. A cyclone like no other. The cyclone Darrell. A ball of energy as Salaok's five year old son comes sliding down the staircase hand rail with great speed. He jumps off the rail just before hitting the ball on the end and within stumbling, he keeps his speed and starts running straight at Salaok. I could hear the sigh escape Salaok's lips as he knew full well what was going to happen. Darrell runs and jumps up onto Salaok's back, giving his father a huge hug, which even with the lack of sleep Salaok had received the past night, he manages to find the energy to spin Darrell around off his back and give his son a hug. A hug mind you that promptly turns into a bear hug as Salaok begins to swing Darrell all over the place much to Darrell's enjoyment. Breakfast turned out to be a great meal and we were half way through it when Quartz made his way down from his room wearing sweat pants and a singlet. He sat down on the other side of me as it was the only spare seat at the table but it didn't seem to bother him. A few wise cracks were made between those at the table and most of them were between Salaok and Quartz. So there I was, stuck in the middle of two rather large men, continually have a go at each other and laughing it off. I had never seen this side of these two men before. The show side of things, and the little we see of them around the company, they are portrayed as two men who just shouldn't be spoken to. It's almost like they are a world above everyone else in the company except for a few others. People like Jay Robson, Derek Frost, Mark Kullypso, Johnathon Davis, Brick and Vincent Welch. Those are the men that although are just human beings like everyone else. They are the men who make up GW. They are the history and the foundation in which the federation was built. They are like the good of Global Inc and they've earnt that right too. But Salaok and Quartz were now, in my eyes, starting to let me know what they were actually like in truth and I almost didn't want to have to do this documenty now as it may lessen people's ideas about them. But then again, I doubt it. They are still two of the great men to grace the GW stage and that is what gets them the respect from their peers, not the fact that they are people who should be feared. Now I can honestly say that I have never had a three course breakfast before, so when the fourth course came around, I was stuffed, to say the least. Didn't seem to bother Salaok or Quartz thought as they just kept on eating. Quartz even offered to help me clean my plate, which I think, was more for his benifit than mine, but I wasn't going to stop him. Food like that just shouldn't go to waste. But after breakfast, is where the life of Salaok began to reveal itself to me as a phone call was received and Salaok instantly took it in his home office away from the rest of the family and the others who were staying at the castle. What it was all about I would probably never know and that is fair enough as somethings just can't be touched on and asking Salaok about it would near be murder. However, it was when I was sitting down and talking to Salaok as we both had a cigarette that things began to hit home with me and made me feel even more comfertable around this large intimidating man who ultimately pays my cheques. The chat was nothing but it made the comfert levels so great that I finally began to ask questions that I had always wanted to know. And then it happened, the words spilt out of my mouth before I could catch then and the question was out in the air. 'We all hear about The Lair, but is all of that stuff we are lead to believe about you all, true?' I doubt I will ever get the look out of my mind that was in Salaok's eyes. They became wide, not out of anger, but out of shock. The question had surprised Salaok completely. Guess he never truely thought that I would have the balls to ask him such a thing, but it was his own fault. He'd made me feel so much at home that I had this feeling in my gut that I could ask him just about anything. His answer however, was even more shocking. 'It's worse. You people hear of some of the things, but you don't hear all of it. All of the hell in which we go through and all of the corpution which has now ruled this country for so many years.' His responce almost sounded like he regreted what he had become but from his surroundings, it sure didn't seem that way. But then he added a comment that I never saw coming. 'Trust me Samir, if you knew what I know, you couldn't live with yourself. It's a lifestyle that not man people can deal with, little own survive.' Was this Salaok opening up? Was he showing me the real truth behind him, or was he only giving me hint at which I would have to pry further into only for him to turn around and ask me to forget everything he has said throughout the day? We actually sat there infront of the television for an hour and talked about things and some of the stuff I found out I couldn't believe. This wasn't just a sports entertainer that I was talking to. This man was a master mind of business. He told me aboutt he state that the company was in when he and The Lair came to the rescue and then proceeded to tell me how he was going about fixing it. I honestly couldn't believe that in the short time he had been in office that he had put together so many plans and made them work without letting many people know. All just to make sure the company doesn't have to sell off it's assets. And assests was one thing that Salaok was building the company with again. No company is a good company unless there is a constant flow of money is what he told me and he is completely correct in saying so. But when he told me about the other arenas throughout the world that he had Global Inc buy out and then rent to upper market sports teams and making money from their seat sales and count use that I was suddenly surprised. He had taken a loan out and within two months, all nine of the new arenas had been paid off simply through the rental rates he was charging. But as if that wasn't enough to help fund Global Inc, he then told me about the buying out of federations who were becoming in direct competition of GW. At the PPV, we all saw something about Salaok now being the CEO of Defiant Wrestling and that was because he had bought it. But there were others. Many other federations and fighting federation which were now owned by Global Inc. My jaw simply continued to drop with every mention of the money making deals in which he had made for the federation. No wonder GW is in the best shape it has ever been in. I hadn't noticed until Quartz had walked into the room that sitting in the middle of the room on a table was a chess board that was half way through a game. Some of the pieces looked to have dust settled on them which gave me a fair idea as to how long the game had gone on for. But when the giant Quartz walked into the room, smile at Salaok before picking up one of the pieces I realised that the game was still being played. Quartz's move didn't take any of Salaok' pieces but did leave options. Options! The one thing that all smart men want. And as I was finding out, these two men were some of the smartest business men going around. When I thought of doing this special documentry on Salaok, it hadn't even come into my mind that it would also count Lisha and Quartz, not to mention any other member of The Lair or Nevermore who would just turn up as they normally would. Or at least that is what Salaok told me. I was disapointed either as Draven had turned up early in the day with some papers in his hand which he had given to Salaok which again had caused Salaok to go into his office again leaving Draven standing in the room with me. So much was the feeling that I wanted to ask Draven things but I knew that Draven wouldn't have known who I was. It was only because I had been given the chance to talk to both Salaok and Quartz over breakfast that they had become somewhat open with me. Draven looked like he was still in great shape as he stood there with a cigarette in his mouth but the smoke was soon put out as Darrell come running into the room. Darrell seemed over the moon to see Draven and gave him a huge hug which reminded me that Salaok did view The Lair as family and therefore, his son had grown up with these outside family members in his life. Darrell's youthful energy soon had Draven dragged off into the play room where the playstation was waiting. After a while, Salaok did come out of his room with a smile on his face and then excused himself for constantly having to leave the room. He then asked me to sit which I guessed would mean that he was going to ask me go as it would seem as he had something important to do and didn't need me tagging around after him. But I sat down and he sat in the chair opposite to me and lit up yet another cigarette. 'So Samir, ask away. I'm sure you've got questions that have been bugging you. I heard you up late typing away lst night so something must have been keeping you up.' Damn, the men was allowing me in closer. Every time I think something about this man, he proves me wrong without even knowing it. And so I asked. Asked all the questions that I had ever wondered about him. And what was the best part was the mere fact that he answered each one perfectly. He explained some of the greatest ever rivalries that had been in GW. And my personal favorite was when he spoke about the Retirement match between he and Justin Marks. I had never even known that GW had sanctioned this match, but that it was indeed a real fight between two men who hated each other completely and couldn't stand to be in the same business as the other. Neither wanted to quit and so they demanded a match to end all matches. 'To be honest, I was afraid I would lose that match. Dispite my hearted for my Brother-in-law, I do respect what he can do on a physical nature and that was running through my head for the entire week. I was a mess to say the least.' Salaok, worried about losing? Certainly not the man in whom we all know today. And then is struck me how much Salaok had changed over the years. Sure, for the first two years he was in GW, I only knew him through the TV but even over the last two, he has completely changed who he is and how people see him. That is something that not many people do well in this line of business. I mean on screen of course. Everyone knows that our line of entertainment are for young males and these people don't have a great attention span to say the least. I should know and you just have to read what I have here and see it is all over the place. The characters have to change and develop or else people get bored with them. And Salaok seems to change all the time. I actually asked him about that and his responce was one of maturity and a well trained professional. 'I do what I have to do to keep people interested. If people aren't interested, they don't watch. They don't watch and then people have to get cut back so the suits would look at the match ratings and if I wasn't doing my job, I would be out of it. I just guess that I have been blessed with the ability to do different things and yet keep the base of who I am intact.' This man was Global Wrestling and I was beginning to see it. I still remember him coming into the UCWF with Quartz by his side claiming that one day he would own the federation. We've seen a journey that has seen us all watch as Salaok under go the changes and ultimately owning GW. Both in the money sence, but also in the metaphoric sence. You see him walk down the halls and you know that everyone who sees him has respect for him. Even those who hate him still respect what he's done in the ring. We spoke a fair while and I was feeling really comfertable again which was something I should be more weary of and I began to ask some personal things which shocked even myself. But of course, he answered them all. 'Salaok, it's well known to the public that you own and run one of the most successful schools for fighting in the world with such students like Derek Draven, Sage Sabbot and Silent Wind all having trained there in the past. What do you think it is about the school that has produced such greatness?' There was a silence in the room as Salaok thought about it and gave the only answer that he could which was a shrug of the shoulders and the look of, 'I have no bloody clue' across his face which gave us both a small laugh. But then he answered truthfully. 'Well, in all honesty, I don't really know. We've just done the same things that my father and the other's thought us. Most of the students go onto bigger and better things like the three you so mentioned, but some don't. They just don't have what it takes to understand the mind set of some things. As long as you've got a strong mind and heart, the rest just seems to follow. Hell, now I find myself facing one of my students and fellow Harvester in Sage Sabbot this Evolution.' I had completely forgotten about and that instantly guilt began to pour over me as I realised that his time with me could better be spent in the gym or something and I think he senced that. 'I never work out before match day anymore. I once pulled a muscle the day before a show due to a workout and I just couldn't do the kinds of things I wanted to in the ring so I stopped that. Plus, this week with Sage is more in the mind for him than anything to do with me. But never-the-less, I will not hold back on him.' That was surprising. Being so close and all, I would have suspected that Salaok and Sage would have planned something out so they didn't have to fight but then I realised that Salaok would never allow it. This match if done right would be a ratings winner for sure and that is more important to Salaok and the company. As I was about to ask another question, Salaok got up and studied the chess board to see exactly what Quartz had done before butt hen sits back down before suddenly seeing the clock and then double checking with his watch. He looked me square in the eye and I wasn't sure what he was about to say so I waited. 'Samir, I got some stuff at the GW office I have to do, you mind if we head on over there? If you don't want to, it's fine, I can arrange to do it another time' Mind? Of course I didn't mind. I was here to catch Salaok in his normal life and the office of GW was a big part of that now. So soon we were off to the offices. But only after the cook had delivered to us a brunch which was the size of my normal dinner. I will never forget getting into Salaok's car. Or should I just say hearse? I will not beat around the bush, that is what he really drives and I can understand why when I see what he's done to it. This thing is more like a luxery vehicle than anything I had seen before. TV's on the back of the two front seats and one on the dash board and DVD player, a bar refrigerator, which mind you was fully stocked, a pile of the recent release DVDs, a sound system like you wouldn't believe. In fact I swear that my seat actually had at least three speakers in it. And there were other things which I didn't fully understand but we are talking one of the greatest all time autos that I have ever had the pleasure of riding in. That was until Salaok pulled over the car on the side of a empty road. Fear? There wasn't any need to although I've heard of stranger things happening to a lot nicer people. He asked me to get out of the car and he did the same. 'Hold your hand out' he said to me which only added to the confusion I was feeling. But my confusion was soon over as he placed the keys to the hearse in my hand and asked me to drive. The late night and early start had taken it out of him and he wasn't in the state to be driving which didn't bother me in the slightest. I got to drive the beast. It handled so smoothly and reacted to everything I did without a single jerky motion anywhere. I no longer doubt why Salaok drives that thing, that's for sure. The drive wasn't long enough for me, as we pulled up at the GW offices after roughly an hour of driving. As soon as we were there, Salaok seemed to change from the man who I had seen in his home, to the man I normally know as the unapproachable icon of Global Wrestling. His face instantly became cold as we stepped out of the car and I handed him the keys back. 'I'm going to be different while there are others around. You understand that right?' Of course I knew this was going to happen, although I wasn't expect Salaok to tell me about it. even he knew that he was a completely different person and it was somewhat like the person he is while at the offices is almost a character which he plays. Being a Sunday though, not a large amount of people are around and we reach his office without anyone stopping to talk to him. All of Salaok's ideas and plans for taking Global Wrestling itself to a new level. This plan wouldn't just effect the company but the federation which is something that Salaok had been wanting since taking over the offices. He knew that dispite being a fight fed, nothing really sold GW as being extremely different. People just saw GW as being somewhat more hardcore than the other wrestling federationsthat the viewers can choose from. His big thing that would make a huge different and going by the ideas and paper work that was in this file, he had been thinking about it for a long time and in my honest opinion, it would work. 'I can't take this Salaok.' What was I doing? Turning down a pay rise like that? Surely I was out of my mind and thankfully Salaok talked me into it. His reason for doing so was simple. 'I like you Samir. You're a good kid and in my opinion, you deserve to be able to have what ever you want in life. If money helps with that, then by all means, you can have it.' Was Salaok buying me out? Making sure that this documentry made him look the right way? Or was he for real? With everything that had happened so far today, I wouldn't think Salaok was hiding anything or wanting me to hide anything so my guess would be that he was for real. But then he offered me something that was more than money. Something that for almost all Global Inc employees was never even thought of. 'You want to go down to the ring?' As if the money wasn't good enough, but now Salaok had just asked if I, a media employee, wanted to go down to the sacred place. My responce must have amused Salaok as he smiled after I said. Down through the halls of the arena and then finally out onto the entrance ramp that as a 16 year old, I had watched the likes of Insain Cain, Bobby Brawler and Salaok walk out with there huge introductions. Wrestling was one thing, but what we do in the ring these days is so far beyond that and it increases the levels of danger. I mean shit, just look at what Sage and Jamal did last week at DeadZone. They were both lucky that they didn't take more serious injuries after falling face first from the top of the cell.' Salaok then looks around himself and then points to all of the seats. The sound that they make as you walk out onto that stage, or the reaction they give from a huge move is something that I think almost everyone in this company lives for. And if they don't, then they are working for the wrong man. This isn't a job for us anymore, it's a lifestyle.' I looked around the arena and at all of the empty seats as Salaok said this and I tried to imagine how it must feel but I couldn't. That many people yelling and screaming for you isn't something that you can just imagine. We sat there and spoke for what seemed ages and Salaok was perfectly happy to do so until he received a call on his cell phone which took his attention right away from me and the location we were in. But his eyes did turn to me during the phone call and there was that icy cold look that I had become used to until today had happened. After the call had ended, he sighed and looks at me. 'You know how you asked about The Lair and all that stuff we do? You've got a choice to make. You can either see exactly what happens, or you can just go back to my place and talk to Lisha about things. The choice is yours and I'm not going to push you in any direction.' What a question. You're camera man will have to stay behind though.' That was something I didn't have a problem with to an extent. Those he trusted. Samir finally stops typing and looks at the screen which is more than filled with the words of the day. A need for a cigarette which forced Samir to stop writing and reach over to the packet which was beside the screen. Flipping the lid of the packet open he sees that there are only four left which surprises him at it was his second packet for the day. Being around Salaok had increased the amount in which he smoked by about five times. Seaching for the ash tray that he had used just before sitting down to write, he moves over to the bedside table where he finds it. He stands there puffing away on the small stick filled with nicotine which had him hooked and looking out of the open window taking in the early Summer air. Despite the fact that it was nearly 4:00am, their was still a warm feeling about the air which blows ever so slightly through the window and into his world. A world that up until today was as normal as most others. Not anymore. I was starting to get a fair idea as to what I was aboutt o be involved in and it was starting to cause fear to bolt through my body but I also had to know. It wasn't for the reason of the documentry, but simply because I am afterall, an eighteen year old boy who wants to know everything. It was at that point that Salaok leant across from the drivers seat and opened the glove compartment and instantly I was froozen still. My eyes couldn't move from the contents which was either there to scare me, or there to protect me. Either way, it frooze me. 'You ever used one of these before?' I hadn't. I'm probably one of a very very few eighteen year olds who haven't used a gun before in the USA, but I hadn't and I told him so. He seemed to understand and told me three words which made it sound so cold and effortless, which is what I guess it was for Salaok these days. 'Point, pull, bang. When we get out of the car, that is if you come with me, I want you to take it as you may need it. But we will get to that point when we get there.' So that was it. Point, pull, bang. That easy. but we were now off. Salaok was driving, more erratic that before as he seemed to have woken up a fair bit more now. But also the idea that something was about to happen would make anyone more active. Although the feeling in the car was cold, Salaok did his best to give some light to the trip as we began to talk again. It was more stuff to do with GW which didn't surprise either of us but some interesting things came about. I asked him what his best and worst match was in GW and surprisingly, he quoted the same match for both situations. 'The best would have to be my match at Showdown in Tokyo. The orginal one. Between Quartz and myself in the Hell in a Cell. It wasn't a match for a show, it was a match for hatred. And that's what made it the worst match ever as well. We put on one hell of a match, but the feeling behind it made it hate every moment of it.' I tried to understand the answer as best as I could but I was too busy thinking about what lay waiting at the end of the trip. So I asked another question, and then another and another. 'What piece of advice would you give to the newer guys in GW if you had the chance?' I had asked him and it stopped him dead in his tracks. He sat driving for almost a minute and no responce had come and I was about to ask him something different when he opened his mouth. 'Learn what you can from whoever you can and know that nothing happens over night. I wasn't always as much of a powerful figure as I am today and I know full well that unless you work at it, you don't get anywhere. I was once a rookie too remember. Pay your dues and bide your time. Yeah, that's probably what I would tell them. Either that or just tell them to get the fuck out of my face. Depending on how I am feeling at the time.' With that I couldn't help but laugh and Salaok had done what he had wanted to. He was taking my mind off what would happen in ten minutes time. Idle chat followed and he even began telling me things without me having to ask. Even about the naming of his son Darrell. Apartently he has this friend overseas who despite never meeting in real life, he is that close to that they refer to each other as twin brothers. I stopped to think for a moment if friendships can really take place without actually meeting the person and I found myself unable to come up with an answer. But it didn't matter as we finally pulled up infront of a house that I didn't recognise, but one that Salaok sure did. His face went back to it's icy cold expression and he motioned to the the gun which I shakily picked up and shoved down the back of my jeans like Salaok showed me. This was it. I was going to be apart of something that I never should have been but couldn't help myself. 'Now Samir, you can either stay in the car, or come with me. The risk is the same no matter where you are but if you are with me, I can try and protect you as much as possible.' Well fuck me. I thought it would have been safer for me to stay in the car but Salaok was right this time, if I hadn't gone with Salaok into the house, I would be dead right now. That not something I can say on a normal day, but this day wasn't normal, because out of all the people I could have chosen. I chose Salaok. I chose correctly. With that we got out of the car and I couldn't stop thinking about the ganster movies I've seen where the car doors open and everyone gets out, perfectly in time and shut the doors together. Well that is what happened as I put on a face void of emotion, or at least tried to. With each step I came closer to the door which held something of importance behind it. Salaok knocked on the door. Yes, he actually knocked on the door and waited. I remember hearing footsteps come up to the door and stop. Salaok's eyes were focused directly on the peep hole and for a damn good reason too. Suddenly, Salaok pulled me down as a thunderous bang filled the air and the peep hole was shot out. Behind me I heard the sound of glass shattering as a bullet went straight through the drivers side window and out through the passangers. If I had stayed in the car, I would more than likely have been shot. Rubbing his eyes, Samir has had enough. The sky is beginning to lighten with the morning sun and he knows full well that in an hours time, people in the house would begin getting up and moving down to the dinner room for breakfast. Although an hours sleep may just make things even harder to get up in the morning, it was a risk he needed to take as his eyes were now just shutting on their own.
