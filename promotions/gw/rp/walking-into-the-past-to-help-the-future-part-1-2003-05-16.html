Walking into the past to help the future [Part 1]

16 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05-16-2003 The last few weeks have been not very promising. Though Braxton as he walked though the woods. Bubba now lay near death and he was still no closer to finding a cure for his friend, The father of his love. Ely stopped and leaned against a large tree taking a deep breath of the crisp air. Up in the mountains Ely was able to relax and be at peace. Silence is taken for granted, something that to certain people was as good as gold. Ely looked in the sky as the sounds of a hawk broke the silence? he could see the smoke clearly. “At last” Braxton said aloud. HE smoothed his hair under his cowboy hat and started hyis hike again. Braxton had left the hospital in Dallas Texas a hospital not only where Bubba layed.. but now where a young woman laid. Gunner Addison. Braxton barely knew her. But knew she was something special to Bubba. Christina called her as close to being a sister as one could be. She was a close friend to the McCoy’s much like his self. Much like the man who put her there once was. Jesse Briggs. Just thinking of Briggs made Braxton’s demeanor even worse. Jesse Briggs a man that Braxton had liked a lot in his meetings with him on Bubba’s Ranch. A man Bubba his self held in high regard, a man Bubba kept giving chances to. Why? Cause he believed in the man. How did Briggs pay that respect, that belief back? By stabbing his friends in the back when they stood up against the Nevermore. Event after event played out though Braxton’s mind. Logan had finally given Braxton a one on one match against Briggs, this time no Stupid goon named Eight Ball would be in the way. Braxton’s rage built until he shoved over a large bolder next to him with a loud roar of pent up rage and frustration. “Now what did rock ever do to you Cowboy!” Said a voice from behind a tree. “It got in my way.” Replied Braxton approaching the tree a slow smile coming to his hardened face. “Since when does one just push things out of his way like a common barbarian, Cowboy.” Said the voice this time from behind Braxton. “God I hate it when you do that shit Talon!” Said Braxton as he spun to come face to face with a Well built Indian man. “You know it creeps me out to all hell. But other then that. It is good to see you again old friend.” Said Braxton as he and the Indian grabbed hands and gave each other a hug. “Same to you Cowboy. It has been a long time. What brings you back to the mountains?” Asked The Indian man, Talon. “You can answer as you place rock back in its home” He said as he looked to large rock witch now was on its side. Under it you can see baby fox’s scared trying to crawl back further into their den. With a sigh Ely walked over crouched down and grabbed the large bolder. “It’s a good friend of mine Ravenclaw” Said Braxton in a strained voice as the veins in his arms bulged and pulsed as his mussels strained with the effort to lift the large rock. Finally the rock fell back into its place and settled. Braxton leaned against it and whipped his brow with his arm and replaced his hat. “Talon A good friend of mine, A man who helped me climb out of the bottle and become a man instead of a drunk, This friend is very ill. The doctors say he is poisoned but have no cure. You Old Friend are the only one I know who may be able to point me in the right direction.” Talon Ravenclaw stood there arms folded as he thought. “You saved my life once Cowboy. You also are a dear friend to me. This friend of yours I owe a great deal to for Bringing you back out of the darkness that had covered your heart. I will do what ever I can to aid you.” Stated Talon as he started walking silently down the trail. Braxton followed suit, just as silent. “This friend is not the only reason you’re here, There is another. The real reason you shoved rock out of way.” Talon said to Braxton Braxton took another deep breath. “Your right. There is a man. A man who betrayed my trust. The trust of My friend, and the trust of a young woman. Jesse Briggs.” Said Braxton with the anger returning to his voice. “Yes, I see. Then your friend must be Bubba McCoy.” Said Talon with a smile on his face as they approached a log cabin ahead. Braxton stood stunned and awestricken. He did not tell Talon Bubba’s name. “How” How did you know?” Said Braxton still stunned and amazied. Braxton knew Talon was a shaman and a bunch of other things inside being a Shaman he did not understand, but did not know how on earth he could have found out. Talon laughed and entered his cabin, Braxton followed suit.. Once in side Talon stood next to a big screen TV and laughed. “You think just cause I live in the woods and am now a Shaman, I have forsaken all technology” I am sorry Old Friend, But I do not live in the old West.” Talon said laughter still in his voice. Braxton could not help but to laugh. It felt good. He had not laughed in a long time. “Come Cowboy, lets eat and drink. You can catch me up on things that have happened, and as I said. I will do all I can to help you once more.” Said Talon as he headed back outside. Braxton stood there for a moment. At least not every thing in his past had a bad side. For the first time in a long time he felt that he would be close to finding a cure for McCoy.
