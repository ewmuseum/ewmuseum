Souls didn’t venture much into this area, preferring the lights of the city and the bustle of human activity. Those who remained were a different breed, the kind who kept to themselves and didn’t ask too many questions. The desolation was exactly what they were looking for to discover the secret of the book. The single story home was set atop a small, rolling hill. There was plenty of water and power along with satellite for television. The argument was still fresh in his mind, and the hurt look on her face just kept breaking his heart over and over again.
