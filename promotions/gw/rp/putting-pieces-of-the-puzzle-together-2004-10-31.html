Putting pieces of the puzzle together...

31 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/31/04 It's now a quiet end to the night at the McNasty house. Earlier in the day it was McNasty and all his friends at his house watching his beloved Steelers take on the undefeated New England Patriots, in what was billed as the game of the week, ended up being nothing short of a blow out. It's just Harv and Donny hanging oun in the game room in the basement of the McNasty house, the two friends are just sitting there enjoying some late minute drinks. 'Harv I'm still confused, all week Alexis didn't utter a word of what she did last week, you know the whole France is going to nuke us and all that shit... But then out of Nowhere she's drunk once again and she starts talking about the NFL as if she's coached a superbowl team before? I'm confused' A distraught and confused McNasty told Harv. He still doesn't know what to think after the drunken rant that Alexis did last week and then again this today while the Steelers were on. Any other time all she seems to think about is pure childish things of that nature... Harv who is confused himself and is sorta worried for his best friend interjects. 'You know Donny there could be a million reasons for it, I mean maybe she hit her head as a child or something like that. You just never know. Maybe you need to have a heart to heart talk with her about it all. Maybe there is something you need to know.' Donny agrees. 'Maybe your right... I love Alexis alot, I mean she's different than other girls I dated. She actually does care for me, she doesn't care about my money or shit like that. Now the problem is trying to approach her about it... You know how women can be when you approach them about something.' Harv just shakes his head having been through a few divorces and other custody battles of his own. 'Yeah, well that's why I have five kids who I never really get to see but yet I have to pay child support on. Although 3 of them don't look too much like me.' Donny chuckles a little knowing that it's probably true that most of those kids aren't really Harvs. It's just the sluts he's been with. 'If I do find out Alexis is smarter than she lets on, then I have to think about things, you know what else is she not telling me about. But then again, I do believe she's sincere when she says she loves me and when she talks about her feelings towards me.' Harv smiles in agreement. 'Donny let me tell you something, follow your heart. I've known you for a while we been through a lot of shit together as friends and I know what everyone else thinks of you, just follow your heart. You've followed it before and you never went wrong.' Donny takes his last chug of his Guinness and looks at Harv. 'Yeah, I guess you're right. I just have to follow my heart. Hopefully my heart will be right again, I would hate to lose her, she's a great girl and I love her very much... I just got to let my heart put together the pieces to this puzzle' Harv takes another swig of his drink, and gets up off his stool. 'Don't worry about it. Come on lets play a game of Madden 05. Lets rematch the 72 Steelers v.s. the 79 Steelers...' 'Sounds like fun to me' Donny gets off his stool, the two head over to the 64 inch large screen television and begin to set up for what's going to be a challenging game of Madden NFL Football on the PS2. Just so you know, Donny leads the all time series, 24-24.
