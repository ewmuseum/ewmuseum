I hate Derek Frost. No. Not becuse he has Wronged me. Just becuse he is the man that i have to face for Stardom. I have to beat Derek Frost to become International Champion. His face haveing a look at total Torment. Like this is something that is killing him. This very subject that he;s thinking of. He cant stand the fact that he is goign to face Derek Frost. One of the best in the Business for the International title. This is my chance. This is something that Roz has never done. The fact that he has never won two matchs. I had to over come Brick. Then i had to Over come Tragic Hero Trying to give the match to Brick. I beat Them. Both. I beat One half of the Tag team champions Pete Hardy. All of this has led me to were i stand today. I'm going to go to Dallas Texas. I'm going to go to the GW arena. I'm goign to lace up a pair of boots. I'm going to walk into the GW Fight Club. Beat Derek Frost and then. I'm going to be the International Champion. Frost. Blayze. What a Fitting battle. Fire VS. Ice. The two most constant subjects on the earth.
