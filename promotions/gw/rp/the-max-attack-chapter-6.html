The Max-Attack: Chapter 6

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today The scene opens up to the chair Roswell was sitting in with one of the mysterious balls in his hand. Roswell gently starts rubbing it looking for any other symbols or letters on it. Roswell notices that there is a picture of a bat on the ball. Beside the picture of the bat there are little wings hanging from the outside of the ball and it looks as if it is a full bat but with 3d wings. Roswell starts remembering that back in California he had seen that human like bat throwing balls at people. “I wonder if those people are the same?” Roswell asks himself. “I mean there is a bat on this ball, and then there was that man that dressed like a bat.” Roswell says again. Roswell then remembers that the human like bat said his name was The Blue Bat, and that no one could stop it. Not even me. And at the Whiplash PPV, I was thinking of that Blue Bat in my head through out the entire match, which I lost. Maybe me thinking of him made me lose to Deacon Holmes. Well, now I’m really going to have to worry, because I’m in the newly International Title tournament and I hope The Blue Bat won’t interfere in that. Cause if he did then there would be no chance of me winning it. Mind you there is a lot of good people in that tournament. Like, my first match is against Deacon Holmes? thug, John Zero. I hope to do my best in that match or else, there will be nothing left of me. I’ve seen John’s work in the ring and am really impressed. He is a good athlete in wrestling and probably any other sport. “I wonder if these little balls can still blow up even after they already did?” Roswell asks himself again. “I think I’m going to try, just to see if they will still hurt people or not.” Roswell says. Roswell picks up one of the little balls and walks out of the room putting a little doorstopper on the ground so the door doesn’t shut on him. Roswell goes to the closest end of the hallway and stops hoping it does not blow up again. Roswell throws the ball as far as he can down the hall. It hits the ground with a little thud, but nothing happened. Roswell starts running down the hall looking for where the fallen ball had went. Roswell stops right where he threw it, but it wasn’t there. Roswell then see a door opens at the point where Roswell is. Roswell looks inside the door and sees people all dressed up celebrating a wedding. Roswell sees the little ball underneath one of the tables. The groom and bride are sitting at the table where the ball is under. Roswell tries sneaking in but sees a butler walking out of the room. Roswell hides behind the door so the butler doesn’t see him. Roswell then tries sneaking in again but the bride see him and starts yelling that someone that was not invited is coming in here. Roswell hides behind the door again so the people wouldn’t see him when the bride yelled. Roswell then gets on the floor and crawls into the room so nobody would see him. Roswell goes underneath the table and grabs the ball. Roswell tries crawling back out of the room, but sees a man standing at the door. So Roswell just sits there under the table waiting for the man to leave. A couple of minutes later the man walks away to get something. Roswell quickly crawls out of the room and starts running down the hall back to his room. Roswell gets back into his room and puts the ball back on the desk and lays down on his bed tired from running up and down the hall. Roswell gets up from his bed and walks into his bathroom. Roswell puts his black hair under the sink and gets it all wet. Roswell then takes a comb from the counter and combs his black hair down. The red streaks are just a tiny bit visible after he soaked his hair. Roswell takes his mesh shirt off and looks at his good built body in the mirror. Roswell starts posing into the mirror saying how sexy his body looks. Roswell then walks over to the curtains and looks out the window staring at the city below. Roswell notices a man with the same trench coat on as the guy who through the first little ball at him. Roswell eyes the man until Roswell’s eyes start dozing off. Roswell closes the curtains and walks over to the light switch. Roswell turns the light off and then walks back to his bed. Roswell lays down on his bed and falls asleep instanly.
