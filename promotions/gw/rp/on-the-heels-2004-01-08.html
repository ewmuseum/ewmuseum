On The Heels

8 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
On The Heels Gabery Breed Global Wrestling Promo #754 Date: 1/8/04 Location: Places I made God cry. They were his tears, but rain to us. I stepped out and let one fall onto my tongue…it was that salty taste. I did what I was directed to do… now tears ran down my own eyes. I wonder who’d stop and stick their tongue out to take a lick of them…exactly no one. I had hurt the one I loved…and he gave me something I thought would never happen to me. He threw lighting at my feet…but I had no image to move. He was the ruler of me…and I was taking advantage of him. How could I? I felt sick to my bones. After that lighting bolt I looked straight up into the sky. The tears fell onto my head and slid down them like they were bouncing off of me. He didn’t want me to touch anything of his ever again. Not even his Earth. I felt like I was floating as I fell to the floor. Awaking wasn’t my idea…nor was it anyone else’s. I just found myself in my bed. How’d I get there…only God himself knows. Will he tell me? Obviously not… he lost my respect when I said I’d never harm another human being he brought into the world. It seemed as if he said to me to fix the world for him. Now I was his archrival, the Devil from below. He was the one that looked over my kids. The one’s I took to school every weekday. The ones that came from Laramie and me…yeah those. And yet I was the one that betrayed him. I’m no sobbing man. I am a man with a scar on his chest. It’s opened and now I’m bleeding. I feel mushy now. I feel like I need to sleep. Good night. Pushing the door open, Gabery walked into the bar. All eyes set upon him…nobody walked into this kind of bar this late. Every set of eyes were on him…the blue, green, brown, hazel, black, and even the eye patches. Mouths dropped as they looked at him. He was back. ‘God’s back!’ one of them yelled. The beer flew into the air, and surprisingly it hit the ground. Gabery flexed his stomach out and looked around. ‘Shut up guys,’ he said as he looked around his old habitat. ‘I’m not God…and never will be.’ ‘Boo!’ they came each corner of the bar as Gabery took a seat on the pool table. ‘We want God!’ Gabery pointed up onto the ceiling. ‘Is he going to fall from there?’ the voice of a super drunk said. ‘Damn straight,’ Gabery replied and hunched himself onto that pool table. He looked around at the dim lighting. ‘You guys make me sad with this place…looks like Cheers.’ ‘Who the hell you calling Cheers?’ came a shout from a corner. ‘You see a mailman and a fat guy?’ ‘In disguise,’ Gabery said and smiled to himself. ‘I’m here to think…so get on with your drinking.’ A middle finger rose into the air…but Gabery didn’t care. The bartender, Bert, came over slowly washing a glass in his hands and looked at Gabery with his good eye. ‘Hey Gabe,’ he simply said and continued to wash the glass with the cloth. ‘What’s up with you?’ ‘Just thinking…’ he said. ‘Kids don’t even have Godparents. I mean…what if something happens to me?’ ‘Laramie is always there…you know it,’ Bert replied to his question solemnly. ‘She’s been skull banged too much,’ his sigh is relentless. ‘I can’t trust anyone like her. All she does is spoil them with gifts…those damn game dudes.’ ‘That’s what happens when they grow’ he says slowly walking away from the aching one. ‘Yeah,’ Gabery says. ‘It should though.’ ’Who was the feller that came in and sat on the pool table?’ a man asked the bartender long after Gabery left. ‘Just an old friend,’ Bert replied as he stood there with his own mug as no one really around for service. ‘What’s his story?’ the man asks taking a sip from the stool he sits on and the counter he leans towards. ‘Story?’ Bert asks as he takes his own stool and sets it on the floors of the dirty bars itself. ‘Well…not much. Boy to man before you could even see it.’ ‘Hard life?’ the man asked as he reluctantly placed his beer onto the counter. ‘Hard?’ Bert asked himself. ‘No. Difficult? Yes.’ The man lets out an acknowledging nod. He knows what Bert speaks about. The man’s gaze runs around the old bar. It’s old first of all, and the best thing to say is that it won’t get any younger. If it is cleaned to a spark, after a couple of hours the dust will run. Misplaced chairs…balls missing from the pool table…light bulbs fused. It’s a regular bar in a regular dump. ‘Name is William,’ the man says looking at Bert. William, as we have seen him before, extends his hand and Bert, who returns his name as a reply, shakes it. ‘I see,’ William continues on. ‘Where can I find this feller?’ Bert looks up from his beer and asks if he has a liking towards Gabery, but not giving out his name. ‘No,’ William says. ‘Believe I might have seen him around and might like to get a few laughs.’ ‘Well it’s easy to find him,’ Bert says. ‘You just need to…look around.’
