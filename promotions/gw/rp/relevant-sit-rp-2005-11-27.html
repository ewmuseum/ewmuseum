Relevant (SiT RP)

27 November 2005

Date: 11/27/05 Fists pound the already battered heavy bag. Sweat runs down his face and body, stings his eyes. His muscles ache. But Brick is determined to keep pushing forward. He's been working out for hours, going through sparring partners with the same speed a person with a runny nose goes through Kleenexes. Showdown in Tokyo is fast approaching. As happens every year, four man teams battle in elimination tag matches. For the first time since he came to Global Wrestling, Brick is not the captain of one of those teams. Brick decides to move to the speed bag. Each blow to the bag comes faster than the one before it. Brick wants this victory at Showdown in Tokyo. In fact, Brick needs this victory. Suddenly, Brick's cell phone began to ring. He could see that it was Aaron calling him. 'Hey, A.' 'Brick, you busy?' 'Yeah, gettin' ready for Showdown.' 'How long have you been working out?' 'Since ten this morning.' 'Why don't we go get lunch somewhere? We can discuss the group.' 'Can't be thinkin' about that right now. I got more training to do.' 'Look, you've been working yourself like a slave all day. You need to just take a break.' ---- 'I'm thinking maybe I made a mistake,' Brick says, jabbing at his salad with his fork. Aaron sits across the table from him. 'What do you mean?' 'I mean coming back to Global. I'm thinking maybe I should have stayed away. I'm starting to feel like maybe I don't have a place in Global anymore.' 'Oh, come on.' 'I've lost two matches in as much as two weeks. I've gotten beaten down I couldn't beat Bobby Brawler at Dark Rituals. On top of that, now I'm not even a team captain this year. Ever since this company got off the ground, I've been a team captain at Showdown in Tokyo. Hell, I started Ruckus Inc. at Showdown in Tokyo.' 'Weren't you always telling me how much pressure being captain of an SiT team was? How you had to scramble to find the right team members? Maybe now you can relax a little.' 'Not really. Two of the cats on Team Ace are Mike Biddle and Christian Darke. Biddle, I just know I'm gonna have to keep in check. I swear, if he tries any o' that ego-trippin' bullshit o' his, I'ma have to bitch-slap his little sorry ass. And then there's this cat Darke. I don't know that much about him. I'm gonna have to keep a real close eye on him, if for no other reason than to see just what I might have to deal with if Team Ace makes it to the main event.' 'Even when you don't have the pressure of being captain, you find new ways to put pressure on yourself.' 'I gotta prove I still have a place in this company. I gotta prove I'm still relevant.' 'You know how these things go. Usually you fall in a slump and then you bounce back. Remember last year at Dark Rituals? You were in a slump for months then you beat all those guys in the Graveyard match. You never know. You might just win Showdown in Tokyo this year.' 'Maybe you're right,' Brick sighed. 'Still, it was ironic considering what card I pulled.' 'What card did you pull?' 'The Ace of Spades.' The two friends laughed heartily after that. After awhile, Aaron's tone turned serious. 'Listen, Brick, you never told me about what happened between you and--' 'No, I didn't and I'm not going to.' 'You don't even know who I was gonna say.' 'Yes, I do. I could tell by the tone in your voice.' 'Oh, please.' 'You know I don't wanna talk about that muthafucka.' 'Fine. But you know people other than me are gonna wanna know what went down between the two of you. People who won't care whether or not you don't wanna talk about him.' 'Yeah, but I ain't talkin' to one o' them now, am I? I'm talkin' to you, an' I'm tellin' you to drop the goddamn subject.' 'OK, fine.' Aaron sighed, 'Look, just keep your eye on the prize, okay? Never mind everything else.' 'Bet.'
