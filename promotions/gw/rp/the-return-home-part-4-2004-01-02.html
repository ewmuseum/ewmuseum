The Return Home (Part 4)

2 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-2-04 The brothers had a plan, now all they had to do was put it into action. It was a mere thirty-six hours until their plan would begin. But before they did, they had to take Yoshahiro’s advice. They were on their way to see their uncle Gohei. They did not know how he managed to survive the attack if he was there, nor did they know why he was not there when it happened. But, he was their uncle, one of their father’s dearest companions and one of the higher parts of their father’s Yakuza empire. It was him who was their father’s greatest street warrior, that is before the two younger Nakazuras showed up. He was the past generation of Yakuza street warrior. The blood and violence was disgusting, their use of brute force savage and dirty, but, it was effective. However, Gohei had faded out of need or use when the two young Nakazuras first showed up. There was no bitterness, Gohei saw it as his place to rise to the side of his brother and he became a key figure in the Nakazura dynasty. However, when Gohei left the street warriors, there was a small uproar from those of the older members who did not see fit to retire from the streets yet. They held closely to their baseball bats and blunt objects, but soon that would all change. The brothers with their elegant and professional shooting style showed the power of the new age. The key factor in the rise of the Nakazura empire and now seemingly their leaving, the fall of the empire. Their black Mitsubishi pulled into the long driveway of their uncle’s small estate. They were met by several security guards, most of whom knew the brothers by face. The shock on their faces was easy to read, but they had no time. They walked past the security booth and were immediately cleared for entry. Within minutes they found themselves lounging on the leather furniture in their uncle’s sitting room. They waited impatiently they did not have time to be wasting around here. Several minutes later their uncle finally came down the stairs. The smile on his face was broad and eager, right away the two brothers knew he was in. But, it was not time to leave yet. Gohei walked over to both of the brothers and embraced them. Soon after they all sat down on the leather furnishings and began to talk as one of the house workers brought out a bottle of champagne. Over the next hour the brothers explained the plan to their uncle. Over two bottles of champagne and two hours the brothers had their uncle’s full attention and interest in this mission. Finally, when it came to the moment of truth Ichiro decided he would be the one to finally ask the question. “So Gohei, your help is very much appreciated. But we cannot stay here to rebuild our father’s empire. We have other things to get back to in the United States, they have yet to see the Souls of the Black Lotus, at their full strength and unity. After this mission we will soon return to America to compete.” “Well my nephews,” the reply began. “I can see why you have come to me. I am the only one left of your father’s tight service and his passing has pained me greatly as I am sure it has you as well. I shall help you and I shall take your dream one step further. For I know, that many of my men that used to serve me are watching and waiting, waiting for the perfect time to resurface. After we clean up and you avenge your father’s death, we shall rebuild the Nakazura empire. Stronger and more unified then ever before, we shall create the next chapters in our family’s legacy.” “Excellent Uncle Gohei, we look forward to seeing you. But, now I am afraid we must part, for there is much to get ready for tomorrow. But before we leave, there is something we must give to you, somewhat of a gift for your participation in this mission,” said Jushin. “So if you would come out to the car with us we shall show you,” answered Jushin. The three men rose from their positions and began to walk out into the driveway. The evening was coming quickly. The sun was falling behind the horizon, painting a beautiful tint of orange and purple in the sky. The men walked to the trunk of the Souls? black Mitsubishi. Jushin clutched his remote car keys and popped the trunk open. With Gohei watching intently the brothers proceeded to hand over the customized leather gun holster, two pairs of the carbon impact pistols as well as several boxes filled with clips for guns. Gohei smiled and nodded to the brothers. “Thank you my nephews, I shall see you both tomorrow night.” Ichiro smiled and with a load lightened off of his shoulders made a joke. It was quite possibly the first time the three men had laughed in a long, long time. “Just make sure you dress nice Gohei, we don’t want you to not fit in.” After the laughter had ceased Jushin and Ichiro hopped into the car. With the sun fading fast they had to get back to their apartment complex quickly. They needed to make some arrangements, they also needed to get some sleep and something to eat. “So, you think we can do it?” asked Jushin. “For our family name, for our honor and for the vengeance of our father, I swear we will!” answered Ichiro emphatically. The rest of the ride home was quiet and uneventful. Within fifteen minutes, they were on their way towards the elevator. The time would soon be upon them, their time of vengeance, a time of change.
