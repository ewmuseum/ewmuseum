Making It Clear Why I'm Here

3 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/3/2004 The rain dropped soothingly over the muddy face of Juan Gonzalez, his torn brown jail suit was covered with blothches of mud, grass stains and ketchup from the burger he ate over 24 hours ago. 'El Mariachi' covered his face from the sun rays hoping that he could keep on sleeping. It was strange to see the sun out while the rain was coming down. Finaly Gonzalez rolled over giving into the suns call and he adjusted his eyes to the sun. 'Ay ya yi! Que esta pasando?' Juan rubs the back of his head making it evident that he has a head ache. It's been over a few days since Juan met with a road agent from Global Wrestling and he was beginning to give up hope. A part of Juan longed for a bed, a meal...even if it was in prsion! 'Oh no! Down with all that mess! The worse thing in this country is better than the best in Mexico!' 'El Mariachi' made his way down a hill unto an empty highway. From a distance he looked off and noticed an approaching car. The car was a 67 Convertible Mustang, a red one. The top was down and three young college girls were zooming down the road making their way home for Spring Break... 'Hey Sally, did you and Phil...do you know what?' 'What are you talking about Jess?' 'You know, that stuff...' Just as Jess is finishing her sentance, Blakely in the front seat swerves to miss a rough looking Mexican man that had stepped out onto the road. The brakes some how lock up and cause the red Mustang to fly into the ditch, Jess and Sally fly out onto the back and Jess lands on the road busting her head wide open. Sally rolls all the way down into the ditch, the water mixes with her blood. The driver Blakely gets thrown through the windshield head first and gets smashed up under the car. 'O, que paso?' An evil smirk comes over Juans face as he looks at all three dead girls. 'Stupid little frats! On your way to get involved in drugs, alcohol, and fornication! Now you're in hell...burning for your inequities! You should have confessed your sins and let El Padre save you!' 'El Mariachi' tears off his top and exposes his dark tanned upper body, it's obvious this guy comes from jail, with the tatoos, the long hair and the ripped body. Juan Gonzalez continues his walk down the empty highway and soon another car is zooming by, this time it's a long stretch limo. It's glimmering white. The limo zooms by and then slams on the brakes. It reverses and finally stops a little ahead of 'El Mariachi.' A big fat man, dressed in a black suit, with a cowboy hat pokes his head out of the sun roof. 'Ay compadre! Where you going?' 'Whats it to you?' 'Well you see, I'm looking for a beaner, about your height, your color and about your size...them guys over at Global Wrestling gave me a call and they told me to pick you up! I'm gonna be your new manager boy!' 'What?' 'Yeah boy, the names Godrrey James, but you can call me God. Ha-Huh-Ha...cough-cough! Ooh boy, I just tickle myself sometimes. Just call me 'Fat James,' thats what everyone else calls me. So hop in the car boy and lets go and sign your contract.' 'Look here James, I'm not getting into that car and selling my soul to no one! I'm here for one reason and one reason only, and that's to make a living. I don't care who I have to take out, or who I have to run over! I also don't need no manager!' 'Look boy, they said they was gonna put you in a mask, and you weren't gonna speak! So they gonna use me as a spokesman for you, come on get in the car. We'll take good care of you.' Juan's a bit reluctant to get in the limo, but finally submits realizing that the cushioned seat, the drinks and the food was too good to pass up. The door closed behind them and soon they were speeding away.
