Q & A with the Kyo Kushinaga

28 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
28 February 2006 I already know that I'm not a household name here in PTC. :: shrugs :: What the hell. This looks fun, so if you have questions, go ahead and asks them. I'll do my best to answer them. ---- 28 February 2006 How has your time in Global been so far compared with your career in 4WW? ---- 28 February 2006
How has your time in Global been so far compared with your career in 4WW?
Eventful and surprising... I became the Platinum Champion just after my first singles match when I defeated Global's Hall of Famer, Jay Robson. It took me a while before I won my first championship title in 4WW from a league veteran. But overall, it's great just like my career was in 4WW. Hope that answered your question. ---- 28 February 2006 What is it like to live in fear that soon you will have to face Kris Kanton one on one again? ---- 28 February 2006
What is it like to live in fear that soon you will have to face Kris Kanton one on one again?
You can't be serious? :: blinks :: Huh, you are? I shouldn't fear you. Matter of fact, there's no reason to fear you. What can you seriously do to me? Oh that's right, 'Do the Dew" on my ass. Pfft, yeah right... You're as sad as another episode of a that Flavor Flav reality show. And believe me that that's pretty sad. :: smirks :: I hope that answers your question. ---- 28 February 2006 Kyo, What were your reasons for leaving 4WW? Surely, you had enough reason to stay, did you not, with a group of people willing to defend you, even if grudgingly, and family ties. Why break with the past without first closing the book? Was a fresh start really worth abandoning your blood? Your Sister, Sekhet ---- 28 February 2006
Kyo, What were your reasons for leaving 4WW? Surely, you had enough reason to stay, did you not, with a group of people willing to defend you, even if grudgingly, and family ties. Why break with the past without first closing the book? Was a fresh start really worth abandoning your blood? Your Sister, Sekhet
Well, well, if isn't my sister, Sekhet and you came with three questions. What were my reasons for leaving 4WW? :: blinks :: Actually, I was sorta forced on leaving the league. Let's just say that I have been sleeping for a while like maybe 2 and half months. Hmm, Grave Revolution... If I remember correctly, your loving bastard of a boy toy over there didn't like me from the start. So, he wouldn't have avenged me. Hell, he wouldn't piss on me if I was on fire, that's how devoted he was. I mean he always thought that I had something sinister plan against you. Paranoid shouldn't been his nickname. Then there was Min who left the group because of some unseen circumstances. Quite the strange one, but she always did give me a chuckle at times. Wouldn't talk me for what happened even after I apologized. :: shrugs :: Oh well... Andi...well, he's a tricky one. Didn't really know where he's alliance was since sometimes he talked to opposition about business more than us. It was business for him, but to me it was a sign that this one was obsessive of power. And you sis, you didn't mourn for me neither did you even say nothing but the fall of me. Eh, it's not your fault. I'm asshole, so what's the point for someone to get even a mention. From what I can see, you guys didn't want me back, so I replied by not coming back. Closing the book? As far as I'm concern we did close the book on the KnightMare Brigade. Hell, there main forces moved on to GCW only leaving Patti and her darling bum shoulder husband. Though, I'm not responsible for it, it still happened as I predicted that a change will happen. Was it a worth it? In some ways I do miss our conversations, sister. You were more interesting than my adopted sister. But abandoning you? I'm did this for you're own good. Can't go in too much detail on why, but understand that I am keeping watch on you. Hope that answered your question, sis. ---- 1 March 2006 :: listens to crickets :: Hmm... :: smirks :: Damn, I really am not a household name here. Oh well, if you anyone of you have any questions about me I will be here to answer them. Heh, just figured it was time for PTC to get introduced to the Deathscythe Outlaw. ---- 1 March 2006 Kyo Kushinaga, eh? A name I hear mentioned a lot when I hear the name of my former home, Global Wrestling. So tell me, Kyo... how is Global treating you these days? I used to be a rookie with a fair amount of buzz there myself. What did you do to put a bee in everyone's proverbial bonnets so quickly? ~Shadow ---- 1 March 2006
Kyo Kushinaga, eh? A name I hear mentioned a lot when I hear the name of my former home, Global Wrestling. So tell me, Kyo... how is Global treating you these days? I used to be a rookie with a fair amount of buzz there myself. What did you do to put a bee in everyone's proverbial bonnets so quickly? ~Shadow
Hm, well, the great Shadow came to grace me with her presence. :: rests arms behind head :: Global has been treating quite well. No self-centered assholes haven't cross my path yet so I can say that everything is green. :: looks in dull manner :: Though, I wouldn't hold my breath on that lasting too long. To answer your second question I'm not doing anything differently than I did in 4WW. I'm kicking ass and taking name while enjoying a nice smoke. :: smirks :: ---- 3 March 2006 Kyo Kushinaga, damn...opening up some eyes around this place. You show up and start kicking ass like its going out of style, you put on one helluva show in a battle royal and then top it off by beating Jay Robson like he stole something. In a matter of weeks you've got a pretty piece of Platinum wrapped around your waist, so tell me Kyo, where do you go from here? -Josh. ---- 3 March 2006
Kyo Kushinaga, damn...opening up some eyes around this place. You show up and start kicking ass like its going out of style, you put on one helluva show in a battle royal and then top it off by beating Jay Robson like he stole something. In a matter of weeks you've got a pretty piece of Platinum wrapped around your waist, so tell me Kyo, where do you go from here? -Josh.
Well, well, the guy who had the longest reign as Global World Champion. You asked me a honest question which in return deserves an honest answer. I'm going on to the top which means that more causalities on The Battlefield. I have the Platinum Champion on one shoulder which means that there is room for more championship titles. :: smirks :: ---- 3 March 2006 Well, you have further title aspirations, do you? Then let me break it down for you, like this... According to Global rules if someone defeats a Champion in a non-title bout, much like I did with Jake Hix and you did with Mike Biddle, then you automatically become the number one contendor. Now, I noticed that you pulled out a W against Biddle, who I happen to have a date with at High Octane, the International title on the line. So, with you being the # 1 contendor and myself the soon to be International Champion... See you in a few weeks? -Josh. ---- 3 March 2006

Well, well, I've been automatically made the number one contender for the International Title without even knowing about it. That's a pretty cool rule there you guys have. And since you so kindly gave me an invitation to the dance, I kindly accept. Just don't disappoint...that's all I ask. :: smirks :: ---- 3 March 2006 If there is one thing "American Youth" Josh doesn't do its disappoint. But I have an awful tendency to get greedy, so...lets make a deal. I'll show you mine if you show me yours. Or to be straight to the point, how about when its time to dance one of us leaves a double champion? When I win the International title it'd be my honor to give you the first defense, but I've never been the Platinum Champion before, so hey...lets remedy that. You've got a shot with or without my stiuplation, but Kyo if you're the kind of man that I think you are, then I know you won't back down from a challenge. My friend; you've just been challenged. -Josh. ---- 3 March 2006

Or to be straight to the point, how about when its time to dance one of us leaves a double champion? Jee, I didn't see this coming... You're looking ahead which is something I rarely do since it's a sign of being overconfidence that leads to one defeat. However, you're right about one thing and that's I don't back down from challenges like my previous dance partner and his band of pussies does. So, I'll accept your challenge. But for right now let's concentrate on the present... When I take of my upcoming dance partners and when you take care of that transition champion then you and I can tango. Just remember that you were one who made the challenge, so I don't want to hear any bitchin' when you fall on The Battlefield. ---- 3 March 2006 Whoa, there sluggers. Let's not forget that after I defeat Aris Cagze Saint at High Octane, I'll be the number one contender for the Platinum Championship. Now, Kyo, I've got tons of respect for you, but you've got three other very capable men on your horizon in Jackson, Eryc, and, heh, TLM. As for you, Josher, you know that I carry nothing but love and the utmost respect for you, and we're looking forward to make The Elite stronger than it's ever been with you holding the International Championship, Bill and Ace carrying the Tag Team straps, and with myself as the Platinum Champion, but I don't feel as though you should be reminded that Global rule states that no one Global competitor can hold any two singles championships at the same time. Now, to my question for the one and only Deathscythe Outlaw: How do you feel about the level of competitiveness you're facing going forward into your Platinum Championshp defense at High Octane? You friendly neighborhood Superstar, Ken Partridge, Jr. Oh... and do you like the movie Sling Blade? ---- 3 March 2006
Whoa, there sluggers. Let's not forget that after I defeat Aris Cagze Saint at High Octane, I'll be the number one contender for the Platinum Championship. Now, Kyo, I've got tons of respect for you, but you've got three other very capable men on your horizon in Jackson, Eryc, and, heh, TLM. As for you, Josher, you know that I carry nothing but love and the utmost respect for you, and we're looking forward to make The Elite stronger than it's ever been with you holding the International Championship, Bill and Ace carrying the Tag Team straps, and with myself as the Platinum Champion, but I don't feel as though you should be reminded that Global rule states that no one Global competitor can hold any two singles championships at the same time. Now, to my question for the one and only Deathscythe Outlaw: How do you feel about the level of competitiveness you're facing going forward into your Platinum Championshp defense at High Octane? You friendly neighborhood Superstar, Ken Partridge, Jr. Oh... and do you like the movie Sling Blade?
I feel extremely calm about it. Yeah, it seems like anybody would feel pressure about competing against three fighters at the same time and also knowing that you don't have to be pin to lose the championship title. But I feel calm about that's because I know that I will come out of the fight with the Platinum Championship Title still in my possession. :: shrugs :: Call it overconfidence or bragging... But I have no doubt in my mind I'll make a impact much bigger than I have been already making after this fight is said and done. Also, I'm fully aware that I have to either face you and Saint. Just hope you two will be paying attention because you're going to miss the greatest fight of all time. Guess you should be taking notes too. You just might learn something. One more thing... I still earned a shot for the International Championship and come hell and high water I will use that shot rather or not that I'm still Platinum Champion or not. And who knows...maybe there's a loophole in that rule you stated, Ken. :: smirks :: P.S And bite me... :: glares ::
