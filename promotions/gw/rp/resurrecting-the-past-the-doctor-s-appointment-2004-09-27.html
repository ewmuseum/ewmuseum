The dry air picked up some of the pebbles, rolling them down the street before they were crushed by the boot of a passer by. The wind also carried the sound of country western music, from a bar down the street. Songs of drunken insanity and Texas pride could be heard any given night on the streets of Dallas. Christian didn't mind, continuing to crush pebbles beneath his black boots. He'd been in Texas a few times before, but never for a long period of time. Texas was pretty much everything he'd imagined it to be. A very hot, dry place filled with uneducated rednecks who work on ranches and factories. The Texan businessmen weren't much different he'd found out through experience, getting rich from oil doesn't take a whole lot of intelligence. The neon lights from signs shone on Christian's 3 piece tuxedo. He had a slight jingle as he walked, from the two gold chains on his jacking hitting his chest. Other people on the streets would stare, some would even poke fun. He wasn't exactly wearing a white T-shirt and blue jeans with a belt buckle that said TEXAS and a pair of old brown leather cowboy boots. Zenith ignored them as he studied the picture of Doc Bailey, that Jason LeMaster had gotten through a search on him. A search that got him to this little hole in the wall bar in the middle of the heart of Texas. Christian stopped in front of the Sundown Corral Tavern, with old shutter doors and all. 'Country Boy Can't Survive' blared out from inside the bar, as a drunkard choir sang along horribly out of tune. Zenith pushed the doors open John Wayne style as he made his way into the bar. The bar reeked of alcohol, and the sound of crushed peanut shells under his feet would've marked his arrival if the music wasn't so damned loud. Christian walked past the jukebox against the wall, and a few tables who paid little attention to him. Approaching the bar, he straightened his suit jacket. The music stopped, and the bartender... who was a large man with a big brown mustache and a mullet, approached his new customer, white cowboy hat on head. 'Hey boys! 'I want a shot of Vodka, straight... if you know what that is cowpoke.' Christian said solemnly. A few of the fat rednecks at the bar shot Zenith some nasty looks. The bartender pulled out a small glass and a bottle of Vodka. Pouring the Vodka ever so slowly, he never took his eyes of his newest customer. He walked over to the bar and put the Vodka in front of Christian. Just before he could take a drink, the bartender spit in Christian's vodka. The bar began to hoot and holler, one man even fell of his stool in drunken ecstasy. Zenith pounded the shot and slammed the glass down on the bar. The bartender walked back over, to collect his glass. Suddenly, Christian spit the Vodka back in the bartender's face. 'This vodka tastes like shit, or maybe it's just you.' Zenith spoke above the screaming drunkards. Unable to control themselves any longer, a group of 3 large Texas got up from their seats and cornered Christian around the bar. Their eyes burned holes through Christian's custom made black suit. 'You better leave you son of a bitch before we leave you bleeding on the curb.' One of them warned. A heavy set man, about the same height as Christian snarled. Unintimidated, Zenith responded by cracking his neck. 'I'd like to see you try.' Christian said, cold and emotionless back to the large drunk. The Texas roared as he threw the first punch. You could almost hear the air buzzing around his huge softball-like fist while it came thundering towards Christian. Zenith dodged the punch with ease. Unable to stop himself in time, the Texas drilled his friend on the other side of Christian, breaking his nose and making him fall to the ground. Zenith grabbed the Texas arm and threw a back elbow into his face, dislocating his jaw. The third Texan attacked out of instinct, but Christian was prepared and slammed his forearm down onto his shoulder, snapping his collarbone like a twig. The bar stood silent, other than the jukebox playing 'She Thinks My Tractor's Sexy' by Kenny Chesney. Christian slowly pulled a chrome .357 Magnum out of his jacket pocket and pointed it around the room. The bar didn't even respond, in shock as three of their biggest regulars lay on the ground rolling in pain. The man with the broken nose began to crawl to his feet, before Zenith pushed him over with his foot. 'Is there a doctor in the house?!' Zenith yelled, followed by a few small chuckles to his own delight. He began to walk out of the bar when he saw a man with an unbuttoned white dress shirt and a pair of beat up blue jeans. His dark brown hair was parted to one side, he had stubble on his face. Christian stood in the doorway and studied the man, who appeared to be a doctor, before he turned around and walked out of the bar. Christian exchanged his Magnum for a cell phone, walking down the sidewalk, accompanied by the clicking of his shoes on the concrete. Hitting a button and putting the phone up to his ear, Zenith waited for an answer. It only took one ring for the other end to pick up, and he made it short and sweet. 'He's in there... white dress shirt, blue jeans, hair parted to the side. He's cleaning up my mess, you can't miss him.'
