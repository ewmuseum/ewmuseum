The Curse Won This Round - but the War is Far From Over

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6.0 June 1st, 2003, a bar in New York City The scene fades from black, rain pouring down upon the streets of New York City. It’s 2 am in the morning and the lights of the famous NYC sky line are brilliant, even in this weather. The scene zooms from high above the city, descending towards a final destination: Rosie’s Bar located a few blocks from Times Square. A black BMW is parked on the side of the road; its windows are tinted out and the vehicle has been slightly lowered. The scene cross fades to show Tyler Pierce seated on a bar stool, staring down at a shot glass filled to the rim. The Curse Continues. Tyler stares into the glass, silent. Flashes of the Global Wrestling Pay per View flash through his mind? he’s in none of these flashes. What happened to Tyler Pierce? Why did the Prodigal not show up for his chance? The Cruiserweight Championship could have been all his? but he wasn’t there. He never even made it to Madison Square Gardens tonight. Instead, Tyler sat in an airport in Virginia? where his plane was rerouted after engine problems. Tyler Pierce lifts the shot glass and brings it to his lips, about to sip when his cell phone rings loudly. A drunken man shoots up beside of him, yelling at the top of his lungs; “WHERE’S THE FIRE!”” Tyler glances at him, near growling as he stands and walks towards the door; he’s dressed in a black leather jacket, black suit pants, and a blue dress shirt. His black tie has been removed and tucked, not so neatly, into his breast pocket. He takes a few steps forward and stands near the entrance to the bar, his eyes running over the text on the screen before he flips the phone open. “This is Tyler.” His words are not slurred, if he has had anything to drink, it’s not been much. The voice on the other end speaks; the voice a cold and harsh one. “Look, this is not my fault, Capo! It’s not my fault at all, I swear it.” The voice on the other end grows louder and Pierce glances at the rest of the room, and then focuses on the shot glass he left on the bar. Tyler clinches his eyes shut, a twitch obvious near the corner of his mouth. He speaks again? “My plane had engine trouble, Capo. We were in mid air, turbulence was horrible” believe it or not, a fuckin? bird flew into the jet’s engine! It wasn’t even fucking funny? stop laughing.” Tyler’s growing visibly upset as he paces back and forth near the entrance of the bar, his eyes searching the floor below him. “Can you get Preston in touch with someone at GW’s HQ and see what he can get done about this” That championship should have been mine? this shit’s not right, capo. You damned well know it?”” Tyler seems to have been cut off in mid sentence. He nods a few times and glances back towards the bar where the shot glass remains. Tyler speaks softly now, his eyes reverting to the floor. “The curse continues” I’m never going to escape it. I don’t know why I even try, anymore. What’s the point? Last night was all the proof I should need? Capo? I really don’t understand. What am I supposed to?.” Tyler stops, the mumbling can be heard from the other end of the phone as the man we know as Capo speaks. Tyler leans back against the doorframe, his eyes panning the room. Rain pours outside forming puddles in the streets, puddles that reflect light from the magnificent skyline of NYC. “You” what? You’ll make sure I get another shot at this? At Blayze?” Tyler’s words show his surprise, the look on his face shows the glee, and his mannerisms show the improvement in his mood. He takes a few steps forward as he speaks on the phone. He smiles a bit, glancing around the room. “I understand” nothing is set in stone. Right? I understand that. Thank you though.” The voice continues on the other end, Tyler nods a few times and then lifts his eye brows. “A four way” BJP, Frost, and Price? You’ve gotta be kidding me. That’s going to be? intense to say the least.” Pierce nods again and leans back against the wall, his arms folding as he listens. He begins to speak then stops, listening. “He’s there now, huh” I guess I could drop by? give me a few minutes to, er, clean up.” With a nod and a quick farewell, Tyler flips the phone shut and takes a step outside. He motions towards the BMW and the passenger side window rolls down. A man in a black suit leans out, an earpiece in his ear barely visible in the light. “Yes, Mr. Pierce?” “Toss me my duffle bag, will ya?” The simple suggestion draws the guard from the seat and around to the back of the vehicle. He opens the trunk and pulls a black duffle bag from inside and holds it up. We can clearly see the GW insignia on the side of it. He walks towards and hands it off, Pierce taking it in hand before walking into the bar. The guard walks back to the vehicle and inside, having closed the trunk a few moments before. Tyler walks back into the bar, his eyes set and focused on the bathroom just feet away. He shoves the door open and steps inside; within a second, he’s check the room and locked the door behind him. The scene fades to black. The scene fades in again to the main area of Rosie’s Bar. A young Vietnamese waitress walks about the room, serving the men and women inside. The men’s room door opens suddenly and out steps Tyler Pierce with a look not seen before this evening; a look of confidence and pride. Tyler Pierce walks through the tavern towards the bar. His hair has been pushed back, his composure straightened. He’s now dressed in a black suit, white dress shirt and black tie. He glances around the room quickly, his black Armani wingtips clicking on the floor as he walks through. He stops at the bar and withdraws a money clip, bills folded neatly in it. He places a 10 spot on the bar and nods to the bartender. He lifts the shot glass in the air and swishes the liquid around inside, his face distorting as we’re able to see through the whiskey. He smirks and glances at the man who screamed earlier when his cell phone went off. He’s now passed out on the bar and the bartender is making a phone call about his presence. “Yeah, Benjamin’s passed out again. Think you can be up here pretty soon” I wanna close up.” Tyler glances down at the man and taps him on the shoulder, attempting to stir him. He shrugs and places the shot glass back down on the bar and kneels down next to the man. Again? “Benjamin, huh?” Tyler shakes the man and pinches his nose, causing him to jerk straight up in his seat ? a tick he learned long ago. He shakes his head, Tyler pulling back quickly enough so that the man is unknowing as to what was going on. Tyler pulls a 100 dollar bill from inside his pocket and holds it in front of the man, grabbing his attention. “Look, do yourself a favor. Take care of this” get off of it. Clean yourself up. You do that, Ben? and I may be able to help you out further.” The man, whose alcohol blood level would be rather low at this point, blinks a few times not comprehending the valued message Tyler is attempting to push onto him. With a sigh, Tyler retrieves a pen and writes a quick bit of information on the back of it. He slips the card into the man’s pocket and pushes himself up. The man lays his head back down and Tyler sighs, wishing he could find a way to get through. A moment passes and Tyler lifts the shot glass into the air before him. The old man’s voice is finally heard again, slurred and unintelligible. “Thank ya son. But it’s too late for a man like me.” Tyler glances at him and then back at the glass before him. “It’s never too late. Call me when you wake up tomorrow. I’ll show you that.” With a quick gesture, Tyler leans over the bar and pours the liquid into a drain. He watches as it spirals, the liquid sinking into the drain slowly and swishing with the water already there. Tyler places another ten onto the bar and points at the glass. The tender is slightly confused but too busy to argue. Tyler picks up a handkerchief and quickly dries out the shot glass before wrapping it up and slipping it into his pocket. Tyler picks up his duffle bag and walks outside, his eyes shooting up the street and down the other side. The man in the passenger seat gets out again and opens the back door. Tyler slips inside, not letting him take his bag from him. He places it in the seat next to him and withdraws the shot glass? staring into the crystalline form of it before slipping it inside. “I may be cursed by fate” but never again by spirits.” The guard nods and closes the door before getting into the front seat. “Where to, Mr. Pierce?” the driver speaks, his voice carrying behind him. Tyler glances up as he places the duffle bag in the floorboard. He brushes his hand over the suitcase and then forward? “4077 Crabapple Cove Lane.” The drive glances back and nods. “Another meeting?” he says, inquiring as the engine fires up. Tyler nods and glances down, his mind on other things. “Il Capo wants to talk to me about tonight” plus, get a few strategies in mind for the four way. Frost and TLM are a tag team? I don’t need to be surrounded; a game plan is in order, wouldn’t you say boys?” The driver nods and presses the gas pedal down, the car moving from Rosie’s Bar. Tyler sits back in his seat, his eyes staying on the window as the city passes him by. I wonder of this curse This curse This curse that beats me Pummels me Slashes me Bashes me Knocks me This curse that torments me Agonizes me Assaults me Harasses me Plagues me Or do I plague myself? Optimistic verses pessimistic The glass is half empty The glass is half full My life is half over The city passed by as Pierce drove through the city of New York. They came slowly to a stop as a light caught them; Pierce’s eyes turned forward and could see nothing before him. Why does he have such luck on his side? Why do the fates fight against him? Fate The supposed force principle or power that predetermines events. But I am a power. Fate is a power. I choose my own path. Perhaps this curse Is nothing more than A waging battle between Two powers Two powers Fate and me Me and fate Tyler smiles to himself as the BMW slows to a stop at a large metal gate. The driver leans out and speaks into an intercom device; moments later, the gate rises slowly until the vehicle can pass underneath. It does so and comes to stop just feet beside another black BMW, another on the other side of that. Then one more and a limousine are parked nearby as well. A door opens and body guards, all dressed in black suits file out, one of which opens the door and Pierce steps out. He nods and walks before them all and into the door, the two from the vehicle walking with him. The scene pans back as the gate lowers, the small force standing before Tyler as he makes his way into the building located at 4077 Crabapple Cove in New York City. The time has come Fate has met its match I’m going to win Nothing No one CAN STOP ME!
