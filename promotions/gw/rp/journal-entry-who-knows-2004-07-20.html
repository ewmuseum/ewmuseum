Journal Entry #who knows

20 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/20/04 Silence. I sit in my bedroom in silence staring at the blank pages of a newly bought journal. To my side sits my newly won championship belt. I look at it and smile. I did not think winning it would bring me the pleasure that it has. I have finally shown those in this business that I am a fighter. A fighter who is worth the paycheck he is given. A fighter of championship proportions. I stare at the blank pages not knowing how to start this continuation of my life on paper. Each decade I write and over the centuries lose that which I have written and then years after find it once more. I begin to write using an old quill pen and ink set. As I begin to write a flood of old memories begin to wash over like water over a broken dam. The loss of true love. The joy of family. The agony of warfare. Silence. Journal Entry# who knows I figured it was time to start one of these tomes that shall be lost in history and found by someone who in the future will see how I lived during these times. Although I shall most likely still be around when it is found like all my other tomes. Life for the past couple centuries has been rather interesting but the thing that has made me begin this is how easy it is to become successful and put on a pedastool. I am a god amoung men in this day and age. Immortal. Strong. Seductive. Rich. Powerful. Am I confident? Yes and I always have been. I pause and look at what I have written and laugh at how the years have made me smug and overly confident. Forgetting the days when there was fear in my heart. Forgetting that there are still things older and darker than myself than could destroy me. At the thought of being vanquished by enemies of this time I smile and yet there are men out there who could do it, men of ambition, men of power, men like TUE. Am I scared of them? No. Do I realize my former allies are a threat. Yes. Mankind. Humans finally a threat to a being such as I. I lean back in the black leather chair remembering a time long before man and the disease they brought to the world. In the days where my kind ruled and protected what was and is and should have always been ours. I skip a few lines and begin again in the journal. 'In the beginning...in the silence...there was us....and nothing more...I was the first, and for the the longest time, the only. What I did was an unspeakable act. Do I regret it? Of course not. All I did was for the advancement of our kind. The start of our destiny....' This is what I always told the younger generations of vampires that took the initiative to find me. Did the searching. To find the one who started it all and ask him why. Why would I damn an entire race to live in the darkness to feed off the blood of mortals? What possessed me to think it was my duty to do this for all of those who, like myself, were born into our 'sickness'. If I would have known that this is what they would have turned into all those years ago, idiotic, cowardly, gothic posers of what they truly are. I would have destroyed the lot of them way back when. I look at those I call cousin in disgust. The ministry. The oldest vampires throughout the world who have made humanity think we are something to aspire to. Mythical creatures to make movies, books, television shows of. To be able to live without them having knowledge of us. Why? We were first. We were the guardians of this world, hell we even protected them,the so called highest of the food chain, while they slept. Some wish to go back to that life. Our life of servitude. To aid humanity before its destruction. To bring us together in peace. Like that will ever happen...For the most part we see them as cattle. They are our food. This is how I see my human adversaries, as great meals. Just like in nature when the lion hunts down its prey, I hunt down my enemies. I stalk them. I play their games. I corner them. And when the time is right, I kill them. This is how I, and my kind, live our lives. I am the beast that man has come to fear. I, who have stood the test of time why should we fear that which is inferior to us? Mankind, the disease of this world, my world..... I look over all of which I have read and I see what has made me become more feral as of late. The thing which has called out the monster in me. Almeric St. Francis... TUE... Each a meal like no other. Almeric a creature such as I. With power but, none like mine. He is the what all our kind wish to be like. A meal all the same....tender, maybe a little on the slimy side but the heart will be the best part. Eating the heart of an immortal is like no other feeling in the world. Feeding on that which gives life to an immortal, its driving force....like sex for the first time. Almeric St. Francis....his time shall come. Maverick Reno.... A creature who would fit in rather well into my society.....but needs penance for his sins. His flesh tasting like that of a freshly cooked steak, but alas human meat taste like pork. Unlike the rest of his kind though he sees something i'm not aware of and that scares me. He has a knowledge of my world....my world of darkness because I brought him into it. My time in TUE showed him much of how to survive as a mortal and have respect from other worldly creatures. He and the rest of TUE are the only people who have rivaled me in over two hundred years...I begin to write again I shall protect my world from all those who happen to be a threat to it. My dear journal...you once again hold all my knowledge as you did so many centuries ago...This time I shall keep you close to my heart. The phone rings on my desk tearing any concentration I had for writing. I pick up the phone rather ill at the hinderance. 'Hello.' 'Hello Leinad,its been a while since you have been staying at your mansion in Texas, why is that? 'Almeric, what do you want?' 'Only to say.....From us to you....' I hang up the phone and before I can get my sword ten of my kind rush the room and bind me by the only thing that can bind me. They cover my mouth and I pass out. They take me out of the room in silence.
