The Train's vs. The McCoys

21 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/21/04 ***The clattering of metal on metal chimes through Train's converted wharehouse home. The ring of the metal comes to a steady, just about in sync with 'Victory' by the B.I.G. and Diddy as it bumps through a set of unseen speakers. Train is no stranger to lifting iron, but today it's not at his waist, but in both hands as he steadily pumps 70 pound weights. The glossy black finish of Train's Lincoln Navigator serves as his gym mirror here on the lower level. Today, unlike most, a red F-250 pick up serves a second mirror. Both trucks sit just in front of the large garage door leading out to the street. It's obvious Train hasn't spared any expense in setting up his work out space. A good asortment of free weights and machines take up a good quater of the open space while the training ring takes up the other quater. Train hits his final rep and drops the weights to the mat under the bench with a loud thud. He reaches down and grabs a water bottle. The condensation on the plastic bottle speaks to it's coolness as Train lifts the pop up spout and squirts some into his mouth, then onto his face. The water mixes with the sweat as it runs down his muscular frame. It isn't much of a break between reps though. Train steps over to one of his combination machines. This one looks like a squat machine, 4, 25 pound plates on each side of the bar. Train places his thick towel over his shoulders and steps under the bar. Train twists the back, unlatching the hooks holding up the weight. With the 200 pounds sitting on his shoulders, Train doesn't work his legs, but dead lifts the weight up over his head. Train gets about 5 reps into his shoulder routine when there is a knock at the door. Train looks a little irritated as he finishes his rep. Exasperated, Train locks the back back into place and grabs a small remote. He pushes one of the buttons and the music goes silent. Train wipes the sweat from his brow as he stomps over to the small, steel door next to the garage door.*** Train- Who is it?! McCoy- It's President McCoy. ***An inquisitive look wipes across Train's face. He reaches out and unlocks the deadbolt. The door knob twist and GW President Christina McCoy steps into Train world.*** Train- Well damn, come on in. ***Christina boldly steps past him and walks deeper into the garage/gym.*** Christina- You can save the attitude. If you had shown up at my office like I asked you to on MONDAY I wouldn't be here now. ***Train follows slowly behind Christina. The step past the trucks into a small open area when they turn to face each other.*** Train- Aight, let's get something straight. I don't give a damn who you are or what you do. You standing in my house so you gonna have check the attitude at the door you need to hit the door. Simple as that. Christina- Oh no Mr. James, I'm afraid you've made this situation anything, but simple. Train- Maaaaaaan.... You ain't been here 10 seconds and you still about this close to steppin' over the line with me. Christina- Well, I'll tell you what Mr. James. Before you get too froggy, I'm letting you know I have a security detail right outside the door. Train- Oh, for real? Do you? Christina- For real. Train- Ok...so is the part where a brotha's supposed to be all intimidated and shit? Christina- You can be whatever you want. Fact is, you've been warned. Train- hahaha... Aight. You gonna storm all up in my house, talking this and that.. The only thing you ain't talking is what the bullshit is all about. Christina- What's this all about? Is that some kind of joke?! You know, I don't know who the hell you think you are, but it's time you and I got something straight. You see... I know, I was wrong just a second ago. I know exactly who you think you are. I've seen wanna be tough guys who think they're above the law come and go. They're a dime a dozen in the business. Now normally I'd just ignore the attitude. I would've probably even overlooked the fact that you thought you had the right to destroy a match that I arranged personally and used to advertise my show. Hell, I could half forget that you injured one of our brightest new superstars in Kid Wikkid in the process. But that crap you pulled last weekend? Oh no, I can't let that go. The city of Dallas has been good to Global. We have loyal fans and the mayor has been great, pushing through our zoning license even when the rest of the city council thought have something as silly as a wrestling arena was too pedestrian for the city. It took a hell of a lot of work my father to get past all the snobby attitudes to get this company not only started, but well established in the city. Now... In the course of one night you've damn near shot that all to hell! You attack the stars of the Dallas Cowboys?! This city's pride and joy! I've got the team owner and the mayor breathing down my neck, looking for answers! I've got reporters calling night and day along with the guys on Sportscenter making every snide comment they can come up with, dragging GW's name through the mud. Not only that, but I've got every other own in PTC talking snickering behind my back about how a woman shouldn't even be in charge of a wrestling company because there is no way she can keep them under control! Now... Add to all that, that when I call you in to discuss the situation, what do you do? You blow me off. No, don't you talk to me about stepping over the line, because you apparently have no idea where the line is! But that all changes today. Train- Ohhhhhh... So you gonna teach lil old me a little lesson now? Christina- Damn right. I don't care about who started the fight or why you thought it was ok to interfere in my match. I'm fining you $1,500 dollars. No questions asked, no excuses accepted. Train- *Pshhhh* ***Train steps over to the passenger side of the Navigator and opens the door. He doesn't hesitate, quickly dropping the glove compartment open and reaching, retrieving his bulging money clip. Train slides the platinum clip off the cash and tosses it into the passenger seat as he counts off 15 hundred dollar bills. Train grabs his clip and start to place it back on the cash, but pauses...*** Train- Shiiiiiit.... *counts off 3 more hundreds* here, you go, buy yourself something nice cuz that JC Penny pants suit you sportin' ain't working. ***Train holds out the wad of cash. Christina is not a happy camper, just staring at Train holding out the cash, amazed at his gall. Finally Christina decides to end this. She reaches out for the cash, but just as her finger tips touch the money the whole wad falls to the floor as Train drops it on the floor! Christina's mouth drops open as Train turns his back to her, heading back to his work bench.*** Christina- Pick that up. ***Train stops in his tracks, his back still turned.*** Train- Excuse me? Christina- I said I'm giving you one chance to turn around and pick that up. ***Train turns and looks at her for a moment. Suddenly he begins to look around the room.*** Christina- What are you doing? Train- I'm looking for whoever the fuck it is you think you're talking to. Cuz I know good and God damn well it ain't me. Christina- hahaha... I'll give it to you. You're a cocky one, that's for sure... Train- Hey... Christina- You think you're just as big and bad as they come, but I'm letting you know right now... Train- Hey.... Christina- I control your contract and I can put you on the shelf anytime I get ready. And another thi.... ***Suddenly Train closes the distance and he's right in Christina's face!*** Train- HEY!!! ***The sudden move rocks Christina back on her heels! She does well to catch herself before falling backwards! She scrambles backwards!*** Christina- Security! ***Train strafes to his left and hits a switch on the wall next to the stairs leading to the loft of the wharehouse. Suddenly there is more sound of metal clattering against metal as we here the bolts of the metal door Christina is heading for snap shut! On the outside we can here men calling Christina's name as they bang on the metal door, turnign knob, doing there best to make their way into Train's home. Christina tries to open the door from the inside, but to no avail. Train slowly walks back over to the pile of bills and stoops down. He shakes his head as he gathers the money back together. Christina tries not to look paniced as she props herself up with her back to the metal door leading to the street.*** Christina- Ok... Ok, let's end this before it goes too far. Train- Oh, it's a little late for all that. See, I have this saying I like to go buy. Don't play games when you don't know who you're playing with. Christina- Mr. James don't.... Train- Shut... *Stops, calms himself* Shut... Up. ***Christina looks like she's going to respond for a moment, but her eyes meet his disturbingly cold stare...his stare the seems to be looking through her rather than at her. She reluctantly closes her mouth.*** Train- Cut that Mr. James shit right now. You call me Train. You don't know me well enough to call me Mr. James. See I know, what you let come outta yo' mouth, but you don't know me like you seem to think. Fact is I have to go kinda easy on your right now cuz there really ain't no way you can know who I am cuz you don't know where I'm from. Christina- You're from Chicago Train. *Stands* Train- No, you don't get it. I didn't say you didn't know where I grew up and made my bones. I said you don't know where I'm from. *Slowly begins to approach* I grew up in Chicago, became the man I am there. But see I'm not from there. Or at least not just there. Where I'm from? It's actually all around you. Every day. All day. You just don't know the place cuz in yo little Brady Bunch, Partridge Family, Leave it to Beaver ass little suburbanite life... You and everybody like you does everything the can to stay away from where I'm from... from who the fuck I am. *Continues to close in* See... I am... I'm that place where you accidently get off the freeway too quick. You know... That spot where as soon as you look to see where you are you roll up the windows. Lock the car door. Hide yo purse under the seat. That spot where you'll just drive yo little ass around in circles trying to find your way back to the highway cuz you too damn scared to get out and ask for directions. You know the spot... You start checking the reception on your cell phone, hoping you have time to call the cops if some shit does jump off. That spot where you promise God you'll start going back to church if he just gets you out of it. See they got them places everywhere. And every time you have the nerve to talk shit to me? When you look me in the eyes? That's where you are. Why? *Standing right over her, looks dead in her eyes.* Cuz that's where I from. Cuz that's who the fuck I am. ***Train grabs Christina's hand and shoves the wad of bills into it. Suddenly he rears back and Christina's legs buckle in fear! Train throws what looks like a punch over her head! But suddenly the bolts of the door snap open as Train hits a small button just under the garage door controls! The door pops open and Christina would surely have toppled out onto the concrete if not for the two security guards that catch her as she falls out onto the sidewalk! As soon as the guards steady Christina they look like they're going to approach Train, but Christina latches onto them. She motions them back to the black limo parked behind them. One of the men opens the door and the whole group climbs in slamming the door behind them. The car's engine starts and the back window rolls down.*** Christina- I may not know where you're from, but I know where you're not going to be. You are officially suspended for one week! Don't even think about showing up at the arena. If you do I'll terminate your contract! I don't know you?! You don't know me either. I'll be seeing you. ***The window rolls up as the limo quickly pulls off down the street.*** Train- Not if I see you first. ***Train turns and heads back inside as the scene fades to black.***
