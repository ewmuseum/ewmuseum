A talk.

26 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 26 The scene opens up to a small ragged up curtain. Behind the curtain is Roswell sitting in a black chair. Right beside the chair is a big hospital bed with Father Clark laying on it. Roswell starts playing with his hands, waiting for the doctors to walk away from Father. The doctors who were beside Father walk away and Roswell starts speaking. “Did they find the bullet hole?” “No not yet” “Do you know where the guy shot you?” “Sorry but no” “Did I tell you I was in two matches at the GW’s PPV, Deadzone? “No actually you didn’t” “Well yeah, the first one is a Cruiserweight title battle royal. And the second one is a three-way tag match for the tag titles. I’m paired with Leviathan” “You mean the bad guy Leviathan?” “Yeah, but he said he was retiring. So I don’t know what is going to happen” “Well I hope you win one of the matches, it could be your first title reign” “I’m praying everyday for that to happen” “ Well now that I know I will pray to” “I hope your injury doesn’t affect the matches” “I should be ok in a couple of weeks” “All is I want to know is who would shoot somebody, in a hotel?” “I have no clue. Probably some city slicker.” “Well Father, it’s getting late you should get some rest. I’m going back to the hotel to get some sleep to. See you in the morning.” Roswell gets up from the chair and opens the metal knob on the door and walks out of the room. Outside the room Roswell is seen walking into yet another elevator. A couple seconds later the elevator doors open and Roswell walks out of them, he walks over to a glass door and pushes it open. Roswell then opens the door to the outside of the hospital and walks out to his car. Roswell opens the shiny door and hops into the car seat. A sound is heard and the car starts moving out of the parking lot down the street. “Man am I tired!” Roswell then stops the car right beside his hotel and opens the door and walks out. Inside the hotel is a crystal design on the floor, and brown walls with little dents sticking out. Roswell then walks over to a door with “EXIT” on it, and walks inside it. Inside the door Roswell is seen walking up some stairs, and walking into another door that says “Level 5.” Inside the number 5 door Roswell is seen taking out his wallet and putting the room key through the door handle. Inside the hotel Roswell switches on the light by the doorway and goes to sit on his bed. On the bed is the sports bag Roswell had when he walked into the room. Roswell then gets up from his bed and walks over to the TV, and takes the remote off of it. He then walks back over to the bed and lays down on it. He twitches his fingers and the TV turns on. He then turns it to the news channel and watches what the news has to say. Roswell then hears that there was some kind of accident in a nuclear power plant. Roswell just ignores the TV and turns it off. Roswell then walks over to the light switch and turns the light off. He walks over to his bed and pulls the cover over top of him. SCENE ENDS
