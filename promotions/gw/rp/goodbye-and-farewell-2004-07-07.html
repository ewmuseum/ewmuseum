Goodbye and Farewell

7 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/07/04 'And in the final days an army shall arise from the darkness and take their place in the world... Unknown to human and vampire alike, they will stand at the final reckoning...' 'And it will be they that hold the balance of power... Neither human nor vampire but both... Children of this world who will not bow down to go quietly!' Silence. I sit in the windowless room in silence. Even in the complete darkness I can make out whats in the room. I look around as I lay on my back on the smooth, cool, black, satin sheets. I look to my left and see the black onyx table with the laptop sitting on top of it. The crypt-like feel of the room is what is suitable for the monster that lives inside of me. I keep jolly around Sage, but its not who I am...a mere mask I put on to keep those around me happy. Buying things with the millions of dollars I have from over the years that shall never run out because of all the companies I own and all the stock I own. A monster who expanded his atrocities to the corporate world. Destroying small businesses...feeding on them....making me more powerful each day. Making me closer to what I am destined to do. Being the conqueror of all nations.....buying everything till I own everything. My manifest destiny....Its hard everyday trying to be something your not. An animal who is in a cage thats too tight. What is to happen when I realize i'll never get out of that cage, it will forever get smaller but I shall never get any bigger....... Forever I will fight this feeling and for that it keeps me alive...keeps me sane, it keeps me who I exactly need to be......And that is Leinad Soma, the man with the controlled inner monster. The past couple of matches have been very tough....more than I usually like to think about....more than I have had to really work at in a very long time...its rare I even mention matches when I'm talkin to you my dear mind. But this next match I have a chance...being who I have always, I had the option to do whatever I wished, whenever I wished. The years got tougher and the options less and less. I now have the chance to prove I am what I always knew I was, champion material. Everyone has that craving to show the world they are better than the rest, and each century I have shown my worth. People in time forget your contributitons..... and after a thousand or so more years they forget you really exsist. I mean, yes you have your notables but only because of their great acheivements. But of course you already know this my beautiful mind. Over the years I have helped men and women become kings and queens and now the only thing that is standing in my way for my destiny to take place is KPJ. Hmmm....Someone's knocking at my door. 'Are you awake?' 'I'm always awake, you know this Kassandra' Even in the darkness of my room I could tell it was her. My kind knows its own.' 'Its been a long time Leinad' 'Kassandra, we have not been formal for years now, why start now? I have bigger things to worry about in my life right now.' I sit up in my bed and turn on a lap on the night stand on the right side of my bed but nothing happened. I smirk. 'You would use that in here. Why?' 'Who says you have the right to look at me?' 'I can see you, you also know this.' I say quietly. 'Leinad, you were never one for games now were you?' 'No, I play games...i'm just on an advanced level you haven't got to yet.' She smiles as the darkness begins to fold away. Looking at Kassandra, I remember what attracted me to her all those years ago. Kassandra Langsuir, a mere youth compared to me, I was at least a thousand years old by the time she turned one hundred. She had shoulder length brown hair that curled around her shoulders, eyes as blue as a cloudless sky, her skin was tanned dark brown as if knowing the hardship of working out in the sun, she had an average bust and waist, but then going lower she had more bottom than top....alot more. I smile at the thought as I look at the outfit she wears, more business type, corporate executive, a black mandarin collar suit top, made for her exact shape with tight black pants made to be just a little tighter thatn they looked. She takes a seat at the foot of my bed and I look at her awkwardly and get out of bed sitting in the leather chair in front of my onyx desk and I slide it in front of her. 'What has brought you Kassandra? Its rare we cross paths.' 'I got wind of your exploits in New York. What is the matter with you? You've never been like this before. Your killing for unknown reasons...' 'Let's just say its getting harder to control the monster inside me.' 'You killed the top vampire of New York.....we all know what that means but what cause was there for it?' 'Someone has tipped off St. Francis about me living in Texas without his permission....' 'Two questions Leinad? One why would you want to live in Texas anyway? And two, do you mean Almeric St. Francis?' As I laugh a bit she stands and walks over to me and sits in my lap, spreading my legs apart to sit between them. I put my arms around her waist. 'I wrestle now and the place is in Texas......Global Wrestling, my gimmick is i'm a vampire.' 'What a way to be yourself without people knowing...or at least seeing your using your talents for other things....' 'And yes my dear I meant Almeric St. Francis.' I put my hands on the top of her thighs as she begins to brush up against me. 'I had a brief fling with Almeric...he doesn't like people being around when their not announced.' 'I've realized that.' 'I'm offering my assistance in this matter.' I pull her hair back as I begin to slowly mover my lips over her neck. I whisper in her ear as she reaches her hand around the back of my head, holding me close to her neck. 'And how can you help me Kassandra? You have been tainted. Like the others....you wish to hide what you are and you think that should pertain to me as well...You've come to end that blood hunt...but why would you put yourself in this posistion?' I feel tears on my face but not from myself. She weeps as I hold her in my arms. I sit there and let her cry for it is the only thing I know to do at the time. 'Leinad, I'm not the oldest of vampires by far but i'm old enough. I know the blood of another vampire can make you alot more powerful than you already are. I come because I want you to drain me dry. I'm tired Leinad. This life can only make me want to end it even faster. If anyone is to end it I would like it to be you.' 'Why?' 'Even though we have a hatred for each other, we also share a passion nobody else has shared. For me you have been that one thing I could count on over these hundreds of years....besides, Almeric is an asshole.' 'Are you sure about this Kassandra?' 'Yes.' 'Ok.' 'Goodbye Leinad.' 'Farewell Kassandra.' I hesitate the first time in my life before bitting into an individual. As i bite into her there is a small flinch. The kind of flinch that comes from a small pinch sensation. Out of all the pain in the world and sensations in the world that one sensation is always unsuspected. I begin to drain my precious Kassandra and viscious flashes from her memory started to poor into me like water that just broke a damn. Almeric.... Violence..... Destruction... A face.... A familiar face.... Silence... I drop the body as I realize that I have drained he dry and push it off me. I stand and go to my closest opening it and pulling a stake and a sword from it. I slowly walk back over to the body with blood all over my face and I stake the body and then slicing off the head. The body burns rather quickly as I hear another knock on the door. 'Who is it?' 'Its Sage.' I sweep the ashes under the bed and sit down in the chair and tell him to come in. I sit there as he enters in silence.
