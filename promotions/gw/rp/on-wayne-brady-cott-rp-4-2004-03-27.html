On Wayne Brady (COTT RP 4)

27 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/27/04 (The scene fades in to the set of the Wayne Brady show as Peter Michael Escovedo and the PME Five play the show back from commercial. The camera zooms in on Brady who is wearing a black suit with a blue shirt.) Brady: Welcome back. Our next guest is one of the toughest and most popular superstars in Global Wrestling. You can see him weekly on Global Wrestling Evolution. Let’s take a look. (Scene dissolves to a montage of Brick’s previous matches. Obviously, the bloodier parts are edited out for daytime TV. Scene dissolves back to Wayne Brady and the audience cheering.) Brady: Man, he’s tough! Please welcome Brick! (The audience applauses as Brick enters to “Brick House”, played by the band. He walks over to Brady who gives him a handshake before Brick walks over to sit down) Brady: Welcome to the show. It’s good to have you here. Brick: Good to be here. Brady: Man, you would not believe how long the line was of people who came to see you. (A large cheer comes from the audience.) Brick: Yeah, I know. I never get tired of it. Audience Members: RUCKUS INC! (Brick looks to where the shout came from and smiles.) Brady: What-what did they say? Brick: Ruckus Inc., that’s the name of the group I started in Global Wrestling. Brady: Man, you guys are some die-hard fans. Brick: Yeah, I love that. Brady: Now, you’ve held probably the most belts in your company. Brick: That’s right. I’m one of maybe three or four Grand Slam champions. That’s someone who’s held all the major titles. The only titles I’ve never held are the Women’s title? Brady: Yeah, obviously not. Brick: It’d look real awkward. Brady (in tough guy voice): Yeah, you heard me, man! I want the Women’s title! Brick: Then there’s the Cruiserweight title, which I’ll never win because I’m like 280. And also the Master of the Mat title, which is a yearly thing. Brady: You’re here to promote a pay-per-view. Brick: That’s right, I’m here to promote Clash of the Titans. It’s one of our biggest pay-per-views of the year. Ever year, we hold it at the Coliseum in Rome. Brady: Wait, THE Coliseum? Brick: Yeah, I know, it’s a really big deal. Brady: Yeah, that’s gotta please the Pope. (Brady then does an impression of the Pope) “Dominus omni’is that Brick”! Clothesline him, Brick!” (Brick and the audience laugh) Brady: So, what happens at Clash of the Titans? Brick: Well, at the end of the night, there’s what known as a Global Rumble. Everybody on the roster competes in a battle royal where they have to eliminate someone by sending the out of the ring over the top rope. The last person standing in the ring gets a shot at the World title at the next pay-per-view Wrestlecade. Brady: How many guys? Brick: Thirty-eight. Brady: Man, that’s? I get nervous about fighting one guy. And you’re looking to beat thirty seven. Brick: Yeah, I’ve already held the World title once. I plan to hold it again. Brady: Yeah, well, I think you can do it. Folks, Clash of the Titans airs on the 29th on pay-per-view. You can catch brick every Monday night on Evolution on HBO. Brick, it’s a pleasure having you here. Brick: Same here. (Brick and Brady shake hands.) Brady: Brick, ladies and gentlemen. Don’t go away. We’ll be right back. (The band plays the show to commercial)
