Date: 073003 I rolled over and looked at the clock. So much for work. Lily was still asleep, curled up in a little ball with her back to me. I slipped out from under the covers and made way to the bathroom. I stepped into the shower and let some hot water blast over my body. It was then that my conscience decided to come out and play. “Why didn’t you tell her?” I ignored it. “You should’ve told her.” I put my head under the hot jets of water. “Tell her what you are.” I closed my eyes, trying to shut it out. “Killer.” It kept getting louder. “Murderer.” I opened my eyes. I decided to tell her. She would understand. When I emerged from the bathroom, Lily was gone. The air was filled with the sounds and smells of fresh-brewing coffee. I pulled on my shirt and pants and went downstairs. There she was, sitting on the sofa, wearing a flowery silk robe and tiny glasses perched on the end of her nose. She was deep into a book. I walked up behind her. “Good morning.” She jumped. “You scared me.” Lily looked over her shoulder at me. “I thought you were never gonna get out of the shower. Coffee’s in the kitchen. I’ll be out in a second.” She jumped off the couch and started towards the stairs. She stopped next to me and kissed me on the lips. Pulling away, she smiled and walked up the stairs. I plopped down on the sofa. I needed to figure out how to tell Lily that I had killed Bryce. No way would be easy, I had at least figured that much out. As I waited for Lily to emerge from the bathroom, I decided to watch some TV. Flipping through the channels, I saw that there was nothing but the news on. I decided on some local channel that covered the college area. “This just in,” a young woman reported. “A major explosion rocked a home just outside of the city. Neighbors reported a loud noise and smoke coming from a nearby residence.” The story got my attention rather quickly. Or the police will wonder why I am here and they will take a closer look at me. Given the fact that I slightly resemble the sketch that they just flashed on the news, they would look more carefully at me. SHIT! I considered my options. I could tell Lily everything, from start to finish like I had planned. But, that might scare her away. I could just wait till the police come, try to make it through the investigation and the trial, if it went that far. Or, I could run. Run far away. Let the blame be placed on me and keep Lily out of it. Take the police far away from this place. I decided that I would run. Arriving at my apartment. Flurry of movements inside. Grabbing everything that was important. Clothing. Bankbook. Other odds and ends. The ride to the bank. The look of sorrow on the teller’s face when I say that I want to close out my account which changed to surprise when she sees the amount. Stuffing the cash into a spare duffel bag and leaving. The rest of the day was spent on the road, one eye on traffic and the other looking for cops. I figured that by now, the police had found Lily and had discovered the note. Luckily, I didn’t even know where I was going. He awoke with a start, a sharp intake of breath as his eyes snapped open, not knowing where he was, how long he had been there. The air was cool to his body, which was naked, except for a thin sheet draped over him. He turned his head this way and that, absorbing the surroundings, finally looking down to see a shock of brownish blonde hair covering his chest and the face of the woman it was attached to.
