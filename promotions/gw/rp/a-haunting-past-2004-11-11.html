A Haunting Past

11 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.11.04 'Who is it,' Ken shouted at the knocking on his office door. 'Delivery,' bellowed a voice from the other side, 'for a Mr. ... Ken Partridge, Jr.' Ken got out of his black leather massage chair with a slight moan and made his way to the door, opening it slightly to see the face of a young FTD delivery man that had his hair messily spiked and the tips died red... very similar to Ken's. He opened the door the rest of the way revealing himself, and noticed a gleam in the young man's eye. 'Wow,' proclaimed the delivery man, 'I can't believe it. I mean, I was hoping, but... wow. The guys and I had a bet going on if it really was but... wow. I'm you're biggest fan, man. I mean you're The Franchise... you're Superstar.' Ken laughed slightly under his breath, but could not hide the smile that had etched into his face. He began nodding as he answered the young man. 'Yes,' answered KPJ, 'that's me. I appreciate that a lot. I'm grateful for every fan I can get.' The FTD man stood slack-jawed and completly serious at Ken's comment. 'Dude, you're kidding, right,' he asked. 'I mean, you act like you hardly have any, but you have legions... I mean, like, LEGIONS of fans out there. There's even a name for us now.' Ken began to laugh again as his hand still rested on the doornob. 'A name,' he asked. 'Yeah, dude, a name. They've started calling us SuperFans. Like, no joke!' Ken laugh became heavier and more genuine. This truly made him happy. 'SuperFans, huh,' repeated Ken. 'I'll definitly have to remember that one. But, umm...' The surfer turned delivery man looked at Ken curiously as he trailed off before Ken began nodding towards the flowers that he held in his hand. 'I appreciate you're fan support and all, but, and no offense, but I really hope those aren't from you,' joked KPJ. 'That would be a little creepy.' The confused look remained on his face until things finally caught up with him. 'Huh,' said Haiwaii FTO in confusion. 'Oh, the flowers. No, man, they're from a Ms. Addison.' Ken's face began to beam. 'Wait,' he continued, 'like, Gunner Addison? Oh, dude, she's so hot.' Ken took the flowers and the signing sheet from the guy's hands and quickly put his signature on it. 'Thanks,' Ken said, 'I've really got some work to get done, though. Oh, and I'll let my girlfriend know that you think she's so hot.' Ken winked at the guy with the joke and flashed one of his million dollar smiles. 'Dude,' he shouted, 'you guys are a real life couple. Oh my God, man, you're like, so totally lucky.' Ken laughed again, as he had been since he opened the door. 'Thanks. I appreciate the conversation, man.' The FTD guy's face quickly went from a smile to another serious expression as he looked around and then pulled out a pen and a piece of paper. 'Umm... I was, like, wondering, man. Could I get, maybe, like an autograph... made out Chaz,' he asked wearily. Ken took the paper and pen and began to scribble on it. 'Of course,' he responded as he signed ~To Chaz ... My #1 SuperFan ... Evolve ... Superstar Ken Partridge Jr.~ 'There you go.' 'Thanks so much, man,' Chaz shouted as he began to make his way down the hallway to the stairs. 'You're even more Uber cool in person, man.' Ken simply laughed and waved goodbye as he closed the door. He looked down at the beautiful arrangement of flowers in his hand and the smile on his face became warmer. He immediatly saw Gunner's face and smelled her scent in his mind as he made his way back to his desk, on top of which he sat the bouqet after pulling out the card. He opened it and began to read it. 'I know I've already said it, but congratulations on going over Zero in Round 1. Here's to wishing good luck against VenPen in Round 2. I love you -Gun.' Ken's inside warmed with happiness, but it soon subsided to a feeling of cold and awkwardness. A flash barged into Ken's mind with all the subtleness of a steaming freight train. Visions from the accident entered his mind once more, for the first time in weeks. The rain was pouring hard onto the concrete as Ken's head smacked onto the pavement. A small groan exited out of his mouth as blood poured from it. He could feel the red fluid exiting from the crown of his head as his eyes, filled with tears and rain, looked to view the face of Silent Wind who was speaking to him... 'Our Father, Who art in heaven Hallowed be Thy name Thy kingdom come Thy will be done On earth, as it is in heaven. Give us this day or daily bread And forgive us our trespasses As we forgive those who have trespassed against us Lead not into temptation But deliver us from evil For Your's is the kingdom, the glory, and the light Forever and ever!' Silent Wind's words finsihed and the voice of another pierced the night sky. 'Rest in peace, young Superstar.' Ken looked to view a second face, one he hadn't remembered before. The rain beat down upon his dark hair, pulling across his forehead. Ken let out one final sigh as the final thing he saw came into focus as the face of... 'John Zero,' whispered Ken slightly.
