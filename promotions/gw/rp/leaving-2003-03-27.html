Leaving

27 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 032703 DING! DONG! Open up, Pablo. I cock my head to one side, listening. Pablo? Who the fuck was Pablo. I slid quietly out of my chair. Lisha lay curled in a ball still, in the corner of her glass house. She wouldn’t hear anything, the multiple layers of glass cutting out all sound. I crept silently towards the front door, listening to the person on the front porch. Damn it, Pablo, open up! I pulled the Glock from my the waistband of my pants. Removing a silencer from my pocket, I carefully screwed it onto the end of the gun. I could hear a mumbling through the door, what was being said, I did not know. All I knew though was that there was someone at the door that needed to get gone, one way or another. Pablo, wake your ass up! BANG! And then there came a gentle rapping, rapping on the outside door. I took aim, centering on where the knock came. I pulled back on the hammer, the gun clicking ever so slightly. Quote the raven, nevermore. The sound of two quick steps is heard and then, stillness. Nothing. I press my ear to the door. Nothing. Ever so gently, I take the doorknob in my hand, slowly turning it, pushing on it so the door wouldn’t squeak. Nothing on the porch. I ooze out of the doorway and outside, moving in the direction of the side rail, peering around the corner of the house. What are you doing here? I see two men, one of whom I do not recognize. One stands, while the other squats. The squatting man rises slightly, grabbing the other man by the throat and pulling him down. The moonlight reflects off of the squatting man’s face, allowing me to see who it was. Quartz. Father. I knew he was around here somewhere. Quartz and the other man speak in muted tones, and I can’t tell what they are saying. They speak for several moments, before half standing and coming back towards me. Quickly, I duck back into the house. I watch from a window as they come around the corner, heading to the street. So, to review. There is Lisha and myself. Quartz playing peeping tom. Roz, Roz makes this a party. Time to go. I removed the silencer from the Glock, removed the magazine and ejected the shell from the chamber, refitting the shell back into the magazine, stick it along with the silencer into my pocket and tucking the gun back into my waistband. Back into the room with the glass cage, I picked up a small syringe from the table. I thumbed the button on control panel, opening the door to the glass house, stepping inside. I leaned over the still asleep Lisha, her mass of red hair covering her face. She jerked up, not asleep but playing possum. Lisha pawed at my waist, jerking the pistol out and holding it on me. I stared down the barrel of the empty gun. Too bad she did not know it was empty. Give me one second to get focused. I could clearly see down the barrel now. Her hands did not shake or waver. I’m focused. Click. Click. Click. She continued pulling the trigger. She stopped, thumbed the safety and tried again. Click. Click. Click, It would help if it were loaded. She screamed a primal scream, swinging the gun at my head. I caught her hand and pushed up down. She hit the ground with a loud thud. I uncapped the syringe and poked it into her neck. Lisha, we’re going for a ride. Any requests? FADE
