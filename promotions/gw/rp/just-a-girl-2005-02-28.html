Just A Girl?

28 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/28/05 Exploit from interview in Wrestling Illustrated: 'Ever since I was a little girl I've wanted to do this. I never let anyone stop me from doing what I wanted to do, when I wanted to do it. Some people call it arrogant... or selfish. For me, it was just the way I did things... and I was never scared to defend myself. Why should I let people walk all over me? Because I'm a girl? That's a bunch of shit...' Date: March 12, 1992 Time: 1:26pm Central Time Location: Highland Park, Illinois Place: Highland Park Elementary Tick... Tick... Tick... The rhythmic clicking echoes around the room. Off-white colored walls and a coat rack are all there was to look at... besides the black and white clock mocking her on the wall. Instead, she decided to bury her face into her arms... staring at the shadowed wood grain of the plywood collapsible table. Her long blonde hair covered her arms, maybe the darkness would make the on-coming humiliation and guilt go away. It was too late for that though... the guilt had already set in. The door across the room squeaked open before two familiar faces walked into the room. She looked up at them... tears filling her vibrant blue eyes. 'Vivica... would you like to tell your mother what happened?' The larger woman with dark brown hair asked, with an affliction of authority in her voice. She didn't know what to say to her mother, who pulled up a chair adjacent from her daughter within arms length. Not like she could explain anyway... she had a lump in her throat the size of a dodgeball, breathing was complex enough. It was the honest to God end of the world... to a 9 year-old girl anyway. No matter how hard she tried... she couldn't look her mother in the eyes... 'What happened?' Her mother asked in a very loving tone. Vivica continued to look away, ashamed of herself in a way her mother couldn't possibly understand. It was an instinct, she couldn't help it... she knew it was wrong but at the time it seemed so right. Vivica thought something was wrong with her... that she was some kinda freak. 'I... I...' Vivica managed to choke a letter through her closed esophagus, before her principle interrupted her. 'Vivica was involved in another fight today. With a little boy named David. She hurt him pretty bad... gave him a black eye and busted open his lip. We had to send him home with his father.' The principle explained. Her mother's look quickly changed from one of concern to disappointment as Vivica buried her head into her folded arms on the table again. 'Why do you do that for Vivica?' Her mother questioned. 'I don't know... made me mad okay?!' She mumbled from inside her closed arms, face down into the desk. 'This is your third fight Vivica... I'm sorry but I'm going to have to suspend you. Hopefully you'll learn your lesson before you come back. Little girls aren't supposed to get in fights... I'd expect this out of a little boy but not a pretty girl like you.' The principle explained to the young girl. 'I apologized mommy... I'm sorry!' Vivica said into the table. 'Vivica, we've talked about this before. You're smarter than that... why do you do this all the time? Fighting isn't the answer, when you get older the boys are going to get bigger than you and you're going to have to stop fighting or you'll get hurt. You understand that right?' Her mother spoke, as Vivica continued to wallar in her self-pity. 'What is wrong with me?' She thought to herself as the tears started to clear up. As much as she was told not to... she didn't want to give up standing up for herself the only way she knew how, it was her natural reaction. 'Yes mommy.' She mumbled before she stood up with her mother. Her principle looked in disappointment as they headed past her towards the door. 'I'll see you on Thursday, hopefully with a new attitude.' The principle said as Vivica walked out the door with her head down. 'Yes Principle Hatcher.' Vivica said softly before she walked out the door with her mother, leaving school early. She didn't look at leaving school early as the punishment, but she knew for sure that she was grounded from anything and everything that she enjoyed doing. 'Why couldn't I be a little boy?' Vivica thought to herself, as she left the school behind her. ---- Take this pink ribbon off my eyes I'm exposed And it's no big surprise Don't you think I know Exactly where I stand This world is forcing me To hold your hand Cause I'm just a girl Little 'ol me Well don't let me out of your sight I'm just a girl All pretty and petite So don't let me have any rights Oh... Date: March 12, 2002 Time: 10:35pm Central Time Location: Chicago, Illinois Place: Downtown The full moon hangs high over the Chicago Skyline, as clouds fly over creating a blue hue on the city below. A man walks down a dark alleyway, between two 60+ story buildings... his reflection could be seen in the glass of one of the towers, which was nothing but glass all the way to the sky. A smile crosses his brown skin as he shuffles through $100 bills... over a grand in cash total. He stuffed the money back into his pocket when suddenly he stopped. Footsteps could be heard behind him, before suddenly they stopped. 'HEY!' A high pitched voice shouted from the end of the alleyway, the large man turned around. As he did so... he saw nothing but himself, in a black T-shirt with a blue jean ripped off vest and a pair of blue jeans. He steel-toes combat boots hit the concrete with a thud as he peered through the darkness. A silhouette of a fit girl appeared at the end, and the smile on his face grew wider. 'Hey to you too baby... do ya want somptin?' He said, licking his chops as he walked down the alley towards her. She started walking towards her with a gleam in his eyes. It was starting to look like his lucky day... first he gets 10 grand for selling crack to some local hoodlums, now he gets a sexy girl trying to throw herself at him. 'Yeah I want something... I want Marcus' money back.' The woman said as she stepped out of the shadows and into his sight. Her blonde hair drooped in front of her blue eyes. The man's eyes grew wide after seeing her face with excitement. He looked at her white tube top with a pink heart and some black tribal around it, then at her blue jean skirt and back at her tube top again... or rather, what was under her tube top. 'I'll give you some money alright... whatcha want baby? 30? 40? How about 10 1/2?' He spoke towards her chest with a big cheesy grin on his face. 'I can guarantee you my boobs don't want anything, they're fine without you.' She replied before rolling her eyes and grabbing his face and lifting it towards her eyes violently. 'I said... I want Marcus Horton's money back now. You know what money I'm talking about... the money you stole from him in exchange for giving him that shit you have him addicted to.' The large black man slapped her hand away and began to react. 'Who in the hell do you think you are?!' He threatened in response to the girl's blatant attack. 'The name is Vivica Julian Valentine... I'm the leader of a little group of people around here you might know named The Cru. If you don't want to cough up the money I'll take it from you... and trust me jackass, you don't want that to happen. I may be pretty but you know what they say about pretty girls... I can be a real bitch.' Vivica snapped back in response. 'That's REAL funny little girl, if you're boyfriend can't get off the shit that's his own damn problem. I don't take no shit from nobody... if you want some damn money you need to get on your knees and suck my di...' Suddenly grabbed him by the top of the head with her left hand and then his jaw with her right and spun them in opposite directions violent. With a crack the man scream in agony as he felt his jaw on the other side of his face. He suddenly lunged towards Vivica, who side stepped the big oaf and slammed her heeled boot down behind his right knee. He yelped again and fell to one knee. With a smile Vivica J. Valentine pulled out a handgun and pointed it at the back of the man's skull. 'Now what the HELL did I tell you?' Vivica said with authority. The man began to shake with fear and pain before he grabbed $200 and threw it on the ground. He began sniffling as Vivica kept the gun to his head as she reached down and picked up the money and slipped it into her skirt pocket. Afterwards she slowly began to move the pistol away from his skull... 'Don't mess around with us again got it? Next time I might not let you live...' Vivica said with authority, 'now run along before I change my mind.' The man got up to his feet slowly with a noticeable limp, he had his back to Vivica J. Valentine. He took one step forward and was suddenly struck hard with the blunt end of a handgun. He fell forward, unconscious from the blow... 'I changed my mind,' Vivica said with a smile, 'I don't trust you as far as I can throw you... and plus, it's a woman's prerogative to change her mind ya know.' ---- The moment that I step outside So many reasons For me to run and hide I can't do the little things I hold so dear Cause it's all those little things That I fear Cause I'm just a girl I'd rather not be Cause they won't let me drive late at night I'm just a girl Guess I'm some kind of freak Cause they all sit and stare with their eyes I'm just a girl Take a good look at me Just your typical prototype Oh... Shirley sits in front of Bubba McCoy's office, staring down at a piece of paper resting down on her desk. The abrupt ringing of the phone took Shirley off guard, breaking the tranquillity of room. Quickly she picked up the phone and placed it to her ear... 'Hello, Bubba McCoy's Office.' Shirley said with a nice tone as she listened to the other end. 'Oh hello Mindy!' She replied with a smile and continued to listen on. 'Oh... OH! I'm so sorry!' Shirley said before listening on intently. 'No it's okay... I'll inform Mr. McCoy immediately! Take care of yourself...' She said with a caring tone. 'Okay, bye.' Shirley said as she hung up the phone. She pushed back with her office chair so she could slip out from behind her desk freely. Just as she did so the sound of footsteps could be heard from down the corridor. Shirley looked down before stepping into Bubba's office, oddly enough... she didn't see anybody. Oh well, she had a message to relay to Mr. McCoy. It was rather urgent, she just hoped she could get him before he finalized the paperwork. She cracked the door open and peeked inside... 'Yes Shirley?' Bubba McCoy responded with a cigar hanging out of his mouth. He never once looked away from the paper he was examining as he waited for Shirley to continue. 'We have a small problem, it seems a personal problem will keep Mindy Mercer from being able to compete at Overload against Ami Mizuno. I believe it's booked as the main event.' Shirley stated as Bubba McCoy stopped what he was doing and took a deep breath from his cigar... 'Did she tell you when she can return?' Bubba questioned, still staring down at the paper on his desk. He removed the cigar from his lips and blew a cloud of smoke into the air. 'No she didn't... she said she'd call back to keep us updated, but until them I suppose we're out of a main event?' Shirley question of the GW Owner. 'I can do it.' A voice said from outside as the door opened slowly. Vivica J. Valentine walked into the room with a smile across her face. Shirley and Bubba McCoy both looked at her questionably. 'It's not like I haven’t done it before, I was in a match against Ken Partridge Jr. and Gunner Addison. If I can handle Ken Partridge Jr. I'm sure I can take care of Ami Mizuno if given the opportunity.' 'What do you think sir?' Shirley looked over at Bubba McCoy who looked into the dazzling blue eyes of the young Vivica J. Valentine. 'I don't think we have much of a choice but to do it...' Bubba McCoy said as he stared at Vivica and blew some more smoke into the air from his cigar, 'but if you go out there and do anything stupid... you'll never step foot back in that octagon again, got it?' 'I don't think you have to worry about that...' Vivica J. Valentine said with a smile 'I think Ami is the one who you'll have to worry about. I might make her look so bad she'll never want to step a foot back into octagon for the rest of her life.' With confidence Vivica J. Valentine walked out of the room and back down the corridor. Shirley watched as she walked out of site, with a new match to compete in. 'She better hope she's as good as she thinks she is... for her sake.' Bubba McCoy said before signing the paper and pushing it over to the side.
