Reflections of the past: Part IV.

30 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 30th, 2004. Standing in the center of an all too familiar Octagon that rested in the gym rented by K.J. Tachikara sweat dripped from my body courtesy of the last two hours I had spent training my new 'apprentice'. She was a quick study and even when I worked exceptionally stiff with her she didn't complain, she fully understood that this was all part of the business. I had shown her quite a bit in two hours yet these weren't moves she could actually apply to defeat her opponent, but rather moves that would allow her to escape defeat. It was once said that the best offense is a great defense and that holds true in Wrestling as well. Running a hand through my scattered locks dampened with sweat I hooked the thumb of my left hand in the waistband of my silver gym shorts as I caught my breath. Two hours in the ring isn't exactly an easy task even if you are an experienced Wrestler, but for someone such as Raki…it must have been excruciating. A one hundred and thirty pound female who stood no taller than five foot six her body was hardly built for being thrown around the ring yet she had taken my abuse with pleasure, her spirit was seemingly unbreakable. My bluish gray eyes drifted over to where she sat in the corner leaning against the turnbuckle making use of the middle pad as a pillow. A white towel hung around her neck and her black and red streaked hair was moist with sweat. Her nearly black eyes bore a look of pain in them, yet her jaw firmly clenched into a state of steely resolve juxtaposed the look of pain etched across her lovely face. 'Let's go.' I commanded gently clapping my hands together loudly to signal that her short rest period was over. Without so much as a whimper or a complaint she rose to her feet standing across from me ready for whatever punishment I chose to subject her too. Standing in the Octagon she looked stunning in a pair of black gym shorts with a black zip-up halter-top that complimented her athletic figure. Her black Nikes made no noise on the canvas as she stepped lightly approaching me from the front, awaiting my instructions. 'Now Raki, I've shown you all the escapes from the front so lets move on.' I said speaking in a tone that, while soft, commanded her attention. Taking a step behind her I firmly planted my black Air Max Trainers on the canvas and threw my arms around her waist. I was forced to admit that it felt good having my arms locked around her perspiring yet glorious figure. My strong arms encased her as I pulled her close her back pressed against my chest and stomach. My fingers pawed at the smooth skin that covered her abdomen. Snapping out of this moment of pleasure I continued on with my teaching. 'You know what this is, correct?' I asked, knowing full well that everyone who followed Wrestling could name something so simple as this. 'Yes, a rear waist-lock.' She responded. 'How would you escape the rear waist-lock?' I questioned her all the while clutching her tighter in my arms. 'A back switch should do It.' She told me not bothering to struggle in my grasp. 'And what if your opponent is too strong for a back switch?' I inquired awaiting her answer. Without a moments hesitation she quickly answered my inquiry. 'Maybe some elbows to the head or mid section.' She said thoughtfully. 'And if your opponent is too strong for such offense?' I interrogated, forcing her to think about this. 'A kick to the groin…' She began before I cut her off. '…Is illegal and wont work on a female or someone like Jay Robson.' I corrected her, as we both snickered at my little joke. I held her tighter in my arms comforted by her lean body pressing against mine. She felt good in my arms…a little too good considering she was a personal student of mine. Trying to focus more on the task at hand and less on the way her tight ass was pressing into my crotch I once again began instruction. 'Something I've always been fond of is the roll through. You simply drop down and roll over into a somersault on the canvas. It may not cause your opponent a lot of pain but it will make them dizzy and take them off of their feet giving you time to get things going your way.' I said keeping her squeezed tightly in my arms. 'It will throw them over…almost like a snapmare?' She questioned as she absorbed the knowledge I had just passed on to her. 'Basically, it's close to a snapmare, minus actually grabbing your opponent. I'll demonstrate. Do a back switch.' I ordered, raising my arms as she quickly stepped behind me. Locking her arms tightly around my waist I felt her perky breasts pressing into my back as she held me tight in an attempt to keep her arms from slipping off of my perspiring stomach. 'Now pay close attention…this should be relatively easy to learn. Just watch me and do as I do.' I commanded as she listened intently. 'I'm going to drop down…' I said whilst demonstrating by slowly dropping to one knee, the black Superman kneepad I wore cushioning my patella. '…And then I'm going to roll head over heels.' I explained as I leaned forward in an attempt to show her how my momentum would carry her over. 'Got It.' She confirmed as I stood back to my feet. 'Then let's try it full speed…hold on tight.' I instructed as I immediately felt her grip tighten around me. 'Ready?' I asked. 'Ready.' She said without a moments hesitation. In a split second I dropped down to my knee and quickly rolled head over heels onto my back as I felt her body go flying over mine where she collapsed on the canvas in front of me. Standing to my feet I grinned broadly at the look of surprise that danced across her beautiful face. 'Just like that.' I told her, while extending a hand and pulling her to her feet. 'My turn?' She asked with enthusiasm obviously a bit excited by this new trick that she had been previously oblivious too. 'If you think you know how to do It.' I told her with a slight warning in my voice. 'I can do it…I can do it blindfolded.' She said confidently. Stepping behind her I threw my arms around her waist and pulled her close, clutching her body tight in my muscular arms. 'Whenever your ready.' I told her. From the second the word ready split my lips she executed the maneuver without the slightest hesitation. Dropping to her knee quickly she rolled over sending my body flipping over hers as I crashed down on the canvas in front of her. 'Ready.' She said with a laugh as she shoved herself back to a vertical base standing over me. Looking up into her deep eyes I lost myself…it seemed to have been a long time since I found myself involved in something like this…a long time indeed. ---- July 11th, 2004. Bam! Was the sound made as the body of RJ Temple flew from the top rope and crashed down onto the canvas from the Superplex Tommy Rock had just administered. A slight grunt escaped the lips of RJ Temple as Tommy stood to his feet and helped Temple to a vertical base as well. 'That's enough for now, RJ.' Tommy stated as he proudly slapped his top student across the back. 'Yes sir.' RJ grunted as he painfully dropped to his knees before rolling under the bottom rope in obvious pain after being Tommy's suplex dummy for the past ten minutes. 'Hey…skinny kid.' Tommy said pointing at me with a long right index finger. 'Sir?' I questioned wondering if he was referring to me and assuming he was since he was pointing at me. 'What's your name?' Tommy demanded his goatee moving along with his mouth. 'Josh.' I emphatically stated. 'Whatever…well you haven't been in the ring yet, so get in here.' He barked at me. Without a moments hesitation I enthusiastically rolled into the ring a smile dancing across my features although the smile was a bit disproportional due to my still swollen lower lip courtesy of my father's right hand. 'You look amused…' Tommy bluntly stated as he eyed me over. I quickly wiped the smile from my face before staring down at my black gym shorts and silver Nike trainers, but alas my smile had faded far too late. 'Do I amuse you? Do you think I'm funny?' Tommy demanded his scowl nearly camouflaged by his thick black moustache. 'No sir.' I quickly stated shaking my head in an attempt to deny ever having made the mistake of showing any emotion inside Tommy's ring. 'Maybe this isn't fun and games kid. Is that what you were expecting?' He snarled at me his brown eyes shooting sparks of anger. 'No…no sir.' I stammered yet it was already rather apparent that I had gained his ire as he blasted me across the chest with a resounding chop that almost immediately raised a welt as it echoed through the small gym. 'So you want to be a Wrestler, kid?' He yelled as he lit me up with another chop followed by another. 'You want to play games in my ring, kid?' He spat as he drilled me with another chop that sent me staggering back. 'Do you think this is some kind of joke?' He growled as he grabbed me in a collar elbow tie up. Unsure of what to do I did nothing as he simply shoved me backward sending me flying through the air where I crash landed painfully on my spine as my head bounced off of the canvas while stars danced before my eyes. Before I could even cover up he was standing over me screaming. 'Get up! Get up!' He demanded and I followed his order as quickly as I could. Once on my feet he grabbed my hair and shoved me down from behind where I landed face first on the canvas breaking my fall with my elbows. I felt his arm snake through mine as he dropped across my back hooking in a half nelson. With my right arm tied up my left was free but I had no idea of what to do…this wasn't training this was abuse! Tommy Rock unmercifully grabbed a handful of my black hair and with the half nelson applied he shoved my face into the canvas where he rubbed it across the mat with a sneer on his face and disdain in his voice. 'Aren't you the tough guy?' He said mockingly. 'Are you as untalented as you are ignorant? Everyone knows that someone your size will never make something of their career!' He spat angrily in jest at my frame which at that time bore only one hundred and forty three pounds. 'Admit you'll never be anything and I'll let you up.' He said laughing cruelly at me as blood began to drip from my nose. 'No…' I mumbled in a barely audible tone as the blood ran down my face and into my mouth. 'What was that?' He screamed as he ground my face into the canvas. 'No.' I said whilst spitting out my own blood. 'What the hell did you just say to me?' He demanded as he pulled me to my feet with the half nelson still locked in. 'No!' I yelled defiantly as the rich iron taste of my own blood coated my tongue. 'You'll never be anything! He screamed at me. At this point my eyes were dancing around the ring where I thought I would see the faces of all the other students in grim shock…yet that was not the case. They were all laughing, laughing at me! Something inside seemed to boil as their laughs triggered my rage. I refused to be a victim of this stupid and immature rookie hazing. I had came to visit a legend in attempt to become a Professional Wrestler…there would be no massacre. This wasn't high school and just because I was the 'runt' of the litter they thought they could treat me like this…well they were wrong. 'You'll never be anything!' Tommy screamed in my ear. My eyes closed… A flash of red passed before them… Inhale… Exhale… My closed eyes saw nothing but crimson before my closed eyelids… I had been singled out…and I refused to be made an example of… I opened my eyes… Action! In one quick motion I dropped down to my right knee jerking Tommy Rock forward as I did so. The look of surprise was evident on the faces of those at ringside, but I was far from done. Rolling head over heels in an unorthodox manner I flipped my abusive trainer over and the second he landed I pounced on him! My fists were flying in blind rage and I was barely aware of the animal like scream that came from my voice as both of my fists cracked down on the face of Tommy Rock. The students at ringside quickly swarmed the squared circle tugging me off of my trainer as I spat angry obscenities at all that dared touch me. A total of six men all larger than I am restrained me, shoving me back into a corner of the ring, pinning me against the turnbuckle with their bodies and clutching my arms and legs. I closed my eyes… What had I done? My parents had disowned me for my decision to become a Professional Wrestler and less than a minute in the ring I had likely destroyed my career as well. Yet suddenly I heard a strange noise…clapping. My eyes reopened to this and I spied Tommy Rock now standing across the ring from me clapping slowly with a big grin stretched across his face. 'You've got spirit…and I like that.' He said as the students suddenly released me. I staggered forward unsure of what to do. But as Tommy approached me my mind raced…what must I do? The question was rhetorical as I felt a strong hand clamp down on my shoulder and the hand of Tommy Rock slap me on the back in pride. Squeezing my shoulder in an approving manner Tommy's voice now bore a hint of pride. 'Good job, kid…good job.' My ears then detected more clapping as the six students at ringside and an additional five outside the ring joined in the applause. I had stood up for myself to the legendary Tommy Rock…and it had all been just a test. 'Kid…I have the feeling you may go pretty far in this business after all.' Tommy said as the applause continued. A smile crossed my face as blood continued to drip from my nose…I had his respect and with that it was certain that I would make a name for myself in this industry yet. ---- 'Hey…are you okay?' Raki asked as she snapped me away from my journey down memory lane. Shaking my head the faraway look in my glazed eyes vanished as I sat upright. 'Yeah, I'm fine.' I nodded as she extended her hand and pulled me to a vertical base. ---- He was right…I had gone far. I was right…I had made a name for myself in this business. And after all I had accomplished there was no way I could be defeated now. Be it friend or foe…my opponents in the ring would fall one by one, for I am the World Champion…the best Global Wrestling has to offer, the man who finally, despite much adversity made a name for himself in the world of Wrestling.
