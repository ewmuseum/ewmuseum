Business as usual

8 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/8/04 'No! You listen to me! You yourself wrote up the God Damned contract! I don't want to hear any shit about what you had your security pieces of shit do, which went against that contractual obligation. You knew the rules. You broke them. So now you are going to pay me, what I rightfully deserve.' I really wasn't paying attention to Christina McCoy, across the table from me, trying to feed me her corportate bullshit. I knew what she was trying to do. She was just trying to cover her own ass, while kissing that bastard that runs the PTC's ass. I wasn't going to fall for her little game. She was the one who made up the contract, in agreement with my attending those fucking therapy sessions. I held up my end of the bargain, by going until I was medically cleared, albeit cleared after TUE paid off my doctor, but cleared none the less. Now this bitch fucked up, and was trying to backpeddle her way out of her own agreement. I couldn't help but laugh to myself about the whole situation. 'Look, I would love to make this all go away Christina. But you see, I can't do that. You and that piece of shit from the PTC were planning on filing a lawsuit against me for 'stealing' the PTC Global Title. Even though I won my match with the PTC Global Champion, according to the rules of the match. However, you steal MY TITLE, when I wasn't even beaten according to the rules of the match. And you expect nothing in retaliation from me? You are more ignorant that I conceived you to be to begin with.' Again Christina continued with her pleading at me to stop the 'childish games' I was playing with her and her company. Now normally a comment like that would irritate me beyond belief, and cause me to lash out. However, I knew I had her right where I wanted her. With those words, she shown her hand to me right then and there. She had shown me she was desperate to end this without any major damage. I knew that she had no other choice than to comply with every and anything I asked for. So why not go for it all, while I had the opportunity. 'Alright, you want this to end as quick as painless as possible. So I'll lay it on the table for you. As I said on Overload, I want my return match for MY TITLE at High Octane. I want the match to be a SIXTY MINUTE IRON MAN MATCH. I want my match against Jamester this week on Evolution, to not only be for my title shot, but I also want it to be for the INTERNATIONAL TITLE. Can you do that for me? If you can, we'll settle this shit right now....well, as soon as I see your name signed on the bottome of the proper papers, that assure all my requests will be granted.' The tone of her voice while talking it all over with her lawyers assurred me that I would be getting my way tonight. Isn't it funny, the little princess in all her glory is forced to submit to someone like myself, all because of her own stupidity, and over-aggressiveness. 'Ok, so you are sure the papers will be here tomarrow, to make everything official. Oh... one last thing. I am not to do another interview, with ANYONE other than Brandi Sights. Her face said it all with that request. She sighed, her face grew long, and she had finally given up. I was to have my way, and there was not a damned thing she could do about it. I had beaten her, just as I had beaten Seymour Almasy at Global Reload, and just as I was going to beat Jesse Jamester at Evolution. The feeling was more than I could physically handle. I could feel myself begin to shake, as the overwhelming feeling of the power I had took over my senses.' 'You get those papers to me SIGNED, and then we'll have a deal. Thank you for your cooperation in this very delicate matter.' I stood and shook all their hands. Their shitty looks couldn't erase the arrogant smirk I could feel etched on my face. I could see that Christina in particular was not at all amused with the smile upon my face, but I couldn't help myself from smiling even more just for her. This was just too good. But after these papers were signed, and the obligations were fulfilled, I knew Christina would do everything in her power to get me back for this. I HAD TO COME OUT ON TOP! ONE WAY OR ANOTHER! If I don't, I know I won't get another opportunity to do so. But I didn't have to think about that now. Right now I was in control. I had what I wanted, and that was enough for now. 'I'll see you on Monday.' I could have sworn Christina was going to jump over the table between us and attempt to attack me with the look she had on her face as I left the room. Now that I had accomplished goal number one, I had to begin to finally prepare for part two. My match at Evolution for the International Title. My match against Jesse Jamester. More importantly my last chance to get Rokku back in the ring, and get back what is RIGHTFULLY MINE! Yes, prepare to show the world my words are true. But how do you prepare yourself for a DEADMAN?
