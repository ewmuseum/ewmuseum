After Shock

14 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/14/04 Completely covered in sweat and drained of energy, Brick slowly walked back to his dressing room. He had just gotten through a hard match with the cruiserweight champion Adam Myers. He admittedly underestimated his opponent. This was something he definitely should have known better than to do, because he knew what it was like to have people underestimate him. All in all, Myers gave him one hell of a fight. Brick figured someone like him would be an asset at Showdown in Tokyo. Finally, he got to his locker room. 'Just a quick shower and I'm out,' Brick thought to himself. Just as Brick grabbed the door handle, he received the shock of his life. Literally. Thousands of volts surged through Brick's body. He spasmed and convulsed, roaring in agony. Trying to pull away from the door handle, his knees eventually gave way and he collapsed to the ground, his hand glowing red. A hooded man stepped out of Brick's locker room wearing a black robe covering his entire body, much like that of a druid. His face was covered by a black mask with blue highlights around the features of the emotionless face. It bore a slight resemblance to Silent Wind's mask. In his hand, he held a power lead with exposed wires. Laughing to himself, the hooded man touched the wires to the door handle again and let the sparks fly into the air as Brick looked up at him. 'We've had so many good times together, Brick,' said the masked man, 'but the games have gone on for too long. Way too long.' The voice sounded somewhat familiar, but the pain Brick was feeling blocked it out, somewhat. The pain then increased when the hooded man applied the wires to Brick's leg. This left him an easy target for what happened next. The hooded man pulled out a baseball bat and proceeded to strike Brick in the midsection and the temple, knocking him unconscious. 'Did you think that I had forgotten about you, Brick?' the hodded man muttered as he walked away. 'I know you hadn’t forgotten about me and what we put Aaron through.' If Brick were conscious enough to hear, he would have known right away who just attacked him. Fortunately, someone heard Brick cry out. Security and EMT's were on the scene almost immediately, tending to the fallen Street Soldier. One was working to revive him, using mouth-to-mask rescucitation. Another wrapped gauze around Brick's burnt hand and leg. 'Come on, Brick, come on!' A few puffs later, Brick began breathing again. 'Brick, do you remember what happened?' asked Jesse Briggs. 'Some maniac in a mask and robe set me up. He electrocuted me then he hit me with a baseball bat.' 'Any idea who it was?' 'I have a pretty good idea.' Jesse then picked up his walkie talkie. 'Situation in the parking lot. The Bogeyman strikes again.' 'What happened?' 'Somebody saw the same masked man with Gunner. Said they heard screams. Saw him attack Brick and Jesse went outside to the parking lot. Gunner sat flanked by Ken Partridge and Bill Moresi. 'What the hell are you doing here?' Partridge growled, rising from his seat. 'Ken, please don't,' Gunner said, holding Ken's arm. 'I'm here to see how a friend is doing,' Brick replied, his voice barely betraying emotion. 'Gunner, are you okay?' 'It was Salaok, Brick.' 'I knew it! He's gonna pay for this!' 'Very generous of you, Brick, seeing as how you pretty much wrote Gunner off after she got back together with me!' 'I didn't write her off!' Brick exclaimed, walking towards Partridge. 'I wasn't even trying to choose between her and Ely! It tore me up inside, but I had to see to the friend that was hurting the most first. And I ain't gonna apologize to anybody for it, not you or anyone.' 'How is Ely doing?' asked Gunner, concerned. 'I won't lie to you, Gunner,' Brick sighed. 'He took you leaving him pretty hard. But he's getting better.' 'You know I never wanted to hurt him, Brick.' 'I know.' 'Yeah, real nice of you, Brick,' Partridge muttered. 'What the fuck is your problem, Patridge? Why are you coming down on me? 'Maybe because it's your fault this whole thing happened in the first place!' 'Ken!' Gunner cried, turning to him. 'Oh, so it's my fault Salaok is a fucking lunatic, huh?!' Brick roared. 'Look at Gunner's head! Look at her hand! You know why Salaok attacked her tonight?! Because he was trying to get at you, that's why! He threatened one of her friends if he didn't get your file from Bubba's office!' 'In case you didn't notice, Gunner wasn't the only one attacked tonight! And just where the hell were you, since you say you love her so damn much?! And what's to say she's any safer hanging with you, since you seem to be pissing TUE off a great deal lately?!' 'Look, you son of a bitch--!' 'Hey, HEY!' Moresi roared, stepping between. 'This isn't helping! Pointing fingers at each other isn't gonna change a damn thing. Salaok did this. Not Brick, not you, Salaok. Anybody needs to catch grief for this shit, it's him.' Ken sighed. 'You're right, Bill. Hey, Brick, I'm sorry, man. I shouldn't have blown up at you like that. I'm just pissed off about what happened.' 'I don't blame you,' Brick replied. 'To be honest, if it was my lady, I'd probably act the same way. 'Also, I shouldn't have accused you of snubbing Gunner. I know you couldn't have had it easy.' 'No, I didn't.' Partridge then held out a hand. 'So, are we cool?' Brick then took his hand. 'Yeah, we're cool. Y'all take care. I gotta go.' Brick then turned to walk away. 'To do what?' Brick stopped and stood silent for a second. He then looked back. 'I gotta go make some phone calls.'
