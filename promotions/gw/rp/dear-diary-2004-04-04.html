Dear Diary

4 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/4/04 - A ROLLERCOASTER WEEK April 4th, 2004 - What a week it has been. Up and down, around and back, my emotions were taken for a rollercoaster ride, only felt once before. Not since before the Showdown in Tokyo did I ever feel the way I did going into a match. Not since before the Showdown in Tokyo was my concentration on my task at hand not one hundred percent focused. Showdown in Tokyo.....the night ended in such a different way, then how just one week ago, Clash of the Titans ended. Showdown in Tokyo, I lost the person I cared for most in the world, and then I lost the only material possession I gave two shits about. Then right before the Clash of the Titans, I almost lost my best friend. Was is suicide? Was is accidental? I don't really know. Quite frankly, I don't care either. The only thing that really matters, is that I didn't lose him. That night I ended up keeping my GW World Title, but even that took a back seat at the end of the night. At the end of the night....watched my two closests friends battle it out, mono e mono, man to man, one on one after going through the rest of the Global Wrestling roster, to become THE NUMBER ONE CONTENDER for MY GW World Championship, and for an automatic spot to face me at the biggest show of them all..........WRESTLECADE IV! Josh, the man I call my best friend, and Ken Partridge Jr., those two men are two of the most technically sound wrestlers I've ever seen in my entire life. They both deserve the chance, the opportunity, and the right to become world champion of ANY professional wrestling company around the world. But I knew only one would come out on top. Only one was going to last, and walk out of Rome with that shot, and to see those two friends, fight back and forth, tooth and nail trying to out best one another, it sent shivers down my spine. It was one of those moments in time, where while just sitting there watching, you knew that history was being made. Josh ended up walking out of Rome victorious, and I was first to congratulate him when he made his way to the back. But the celebration didn't last long. We soon found out that Josh's number one contendership status was going to be up for grabs in a match against Jesse Jamester on Evolution. Was is fair, probably not, but I'd be lying if a slight smirk of enjoyment didn't crawl across my face after hearing the news. What many don't know is Josh and I have known one another for quite some time now. When I first broke into the business, a small independent company called NeWave was my home. On my first night with the company I won my first piece of gold, the NeWave Xtreme Championship, by beating Josh. We instantly became rivals, with Josh taking the tag team titles from me the following week. NeWave closed their doors shortly after that, and I travelled to Europe, and joined the EWA. When I joined the company, they were trying to do a split, and form two companies, and without having a single match in the company I was drafted fourth for the new company Brutality. My first match with the company I defeated their world champion in a nontitle match. My glory there didn't last too long as Josh soon showed up in the company at my expense, and our rivalry started back up. All over Europe spilling each other's blood, opening up rehashed wounds, and pushing the envelope night after night. We made each other bleed, we bruised each other's bodies, we electricuted one another, and almost killed each other. The country of Europe, hell the WORLD had not seen such carnage, between two athletes such as Josh and myself. But the carnage didn't stop there. Due to some disagreements with management about my contract, and how I was being used, I left EWA, to join DWO, whic was now owned by the former vice-president of EWA, the man who drafted me fourth when EWA was doing its split, Paul Nicoll. I was to be the cornerstone of the company, I was to lead the company to the promised land, but once again, Josh showed up and spoiled everything. Once again, he set his targets for me, and our blood fued took off, almost like it hadn't missed a single beat. Things didn't last long though, DWO closed its doors a few months later. Josh was quickly picked up off the free agent market by Global Wrestling. It was less than twenty-four hours ago that the ink dried on his contract, when I received a call from Josh, saying he had gotten me a job interview, if I wanted it. So Josh, Christina McCoy, and myself met in Dallas, on a friday afternoon. We had lunch, and the contract was signed, sealed, and delivered that day. After lunch, Josh and I hung out, which was the first time we had ever done such a thing. And we discussed our past, and he made mention that he wanted to wipe the slate clean. Not because he'd forgiven me for our past, not because I had forgiven him, but because through all that we had put each other through, he had grown to respect me, and I felt the same way. Since then we've been at one another's side, training, travelling, side by side through it all. But now, the rivalry could start back up. Should Josh beat Jesse Jamester and keep his shot at me, and my title at WRESTLECADE IV, then one more time, Ice vs Josh, only this time its at the BIGGEST SHOW OF THEM ALL, for the BIGGEST TITLE IN THE INDUSTRY. Does Christina McCoy know just what she is getting her company into. Does she realize what opening the gates of this hell will bring? If she doesn't.......may God have mercy on this company.
