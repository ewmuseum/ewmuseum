No more Warnings...

30 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/30/03 **Here it is’the moment of truth. Now is my chance to prove my worth to the Salamone family and to myself.** (As the scene fades in we pick up right where we left off last time with Paulie and Sean hiding behind some wooden crates waiting for two trucks to arrive. Sure enough within a few seconds two trucks come by, one of them continues on its way while the second one stops. A man walks upto the driver side door, the driver of the truck and the man have a brief conversation, not too short but not too long. Just long enough so that Sean and Paulie can make their way into the truck.) “Ok Sean, here we go. Quickly but quietly.” “Right Paulie.” (With this, Sean and Paulie quickly creep upto the back of the truck without being seen. Paulie slowly lifts alittle a white sheet revealing the back of the truck to be filled with boxes. Sean climbs in first, he then holds the sheet allowing Paulie to climb in the back. After both men are in Sean releases the white sheet and then sets his black duffle bag down.) “Alright Sean here is what we are gonna do, as soon as the truck gets into the warehouse and stops we are both gonna jump out and point our guns at the first person we see. If you want to start shooting that’s fine too but I don’t recommend it.” “I think negotiating before killing people works best, but you’re the veteran at this not me.” “Ok Sean the truck is moving. Just sit tight and in a few seconds we will jump out.” “Sounds good.” (The truck starts up again, this time Price and Paulie are in the back awaiting for the truck to come to its final stop. Within a few seconds the truck comes to a complete stop inside the warehouse. Multiple voices can be heard from outside the truck, some of them getting louder and louder by the second. When it sounds like the men whose voices can be heard are within an arms length of the truck both Sean and Paulie quickly jump out of the truck with guns drawn.) “Ok everyone shut the fuck up and no one will get a bullet in their head. My buddy and I have some business to discuss with you all.” “You heard the man, no one talks’no one dies. You see this gun here, its pointed at this man’s head, one word, one flinch and he will get it. This is your one and only warning.” (Sean turns slightly and puts the gun against the back of an older man’s head. The man is obviously scared and is practically frozen in place.) “Now it seems that you ungrateful assholes have decided that you don’t need the protection of Don Salamone’s men any longer. Well if that’s true then why are we the ones with guns pointed at your heads?” “Seems like you assholes need the protection more than your willing to admit’or is it more than your willing to pay.” “That’s right Sean. The Don doesn’t like it when people suddenly stop paying him and he especially doesn’t like it when people don’t give him a reason why they stopped paying. And because of all of this, all of what you people have done, the Don has sent us over here to deliver a message.” (Sean walks over to one of the boxes that was taken off of the first truck. He then walks over to his duffle bag and pulls out a crow bar and alittle container of WD 40.) “Wait’what is going on?” Questions one of the men being held at gun point. “What did I tell you old man” Sean teach him a lesson.” (Without hesitation Sean points his gun at the man who just spoke and fires off a shot right into the man’s arm. The man grabs at his now blood soaked sleeve and then falls to the ground. Another man attempts to rush over to him but is stopped by the sound of Paulie’s gun being cocked and pointed at him.) “Good shot. Now the next time someone talks a bullet is going in their head you got me?” (Sean picks up his crowbar and takes it over to one of the unloaded boxes, he then uses the crowbar to open up the box. After doing so he removes alittle bit of confetti paper that is covering up what is really contained in the box. Sean then picks up a brick of Heroine and shows it to everyone in the warehouse. He then takes a small pocket knife and slices open the brick of Heroine and then dumps it out into the box, then he takes his can of WD 40 and starts spraying the lose Heroine.) “Here ya go Sean.” (Paulie reaches into his coat pocket and then tosses a lighter towards Sean. Sean catches it and then lights it, he waits a second and then throws the lighter into the box containing the now WD 40 soaked Heroine. Within a matter of seconds the box becomes engulfed in flames.) “Hey what are you doing” You can’t do that.” (Paulie turns around and fires one shot that goes straight through the man’s head and then gets stuck in a metal beam located 5 feet behind him. The man falls to the ground and a pool of blood starts forming around the man’s body.) “I warned him. I warned all of you. No more warnings.” “That’s right Paulie. We are done playing games. That box right there contains over half a million dollars of Heroine now useless because of what I have just done to it. You tell your boss that if he does not start making payments we will be back, and next time it won’t be one box that gets light on fire, and it wont be one man that gets killed. Every box in here will be in flames and every person in here will be dead. This is not a threat it’s a promise.” (Paulie walks over to a black briefcase and lifts it up ontop of the truck’s hood. He then fires two shots into the briefcase where the locks are located and then the top of the briefcase lifts up to reveals stacks of $100.00 dollar bills. Paulie shows it to Sean and both men begin smiling. Paulie then closes the briefcase and sets it next to him.) “Now I think it is time we leave you all to do what your being paid for.” (Paulie and Sean turn to walk out but in the midst of doing so both men turn around again and look at the crowd of shocked and fear filled people.) “One last thing.” (Sean walks over to the side of the warehouse and picks up a solid brick. He then walks over to one of the trucks, opens the driver side door to the truck and then gets in. He picks up the truck’s keys that are placed on the passenger seat and puts them in the ignition. One swift turn and the truck starts. Sean then gets out of the truck and places the brick on the accelerator pedal and then quickly shifts the car from park to drive. The truck immediately takes off, its speed accelerating every second. Within a few seconds the Truck crashes through the back wall of the warehouse and then takes a nose dive into the water that encompasses the pier. Sean again has a huge smile come across him as he turns to leave the warehouse.) “That was fun.” “Oh and another thing, you can thank your boss for that man’s life over there. If your boss hadn’t stopped paying us that man might still be alive. But I guess we will never know.” (Sean picks up his duffle bag and slings it over his shoulder, Paulie picks up the black briefcase and holds it up against his body. Both men walk out of the warehouse and towards their car as the scene fades to black.)
