Scrapbook of Sherwin: Episode #1

17 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17th June Scrapbook of Sherwin Episode I: Brawl For All [The scene opens inside what appears to be an old-fashioned biker bar. It was stereotypical in both it's nature and appearance. Leather-clad folk sat around tables and at the bar, doing whatever was in their business.. be it drinking, conversing or looking toward the 'new guy' who'd just walked in on their territory.] Sherwin: '... What are you looking at?' [His voice sounded cold, carrying what appeared to be a thirst for revenge. He made his way through the entrance of the bar and walked over to a table in the corner. Clad in a pair of black jeans, black trainers and a white vest, the rather buff GW athlete slumped down onto a seat, his eyes scanning across the various types of folk before they all resumed their daily business.] Sherwin: 'Barkeep... I want a drink, something that'll go down good for the long run.' Barman: '... Sure, Sure.' [Sherwin smirked as the Barman hopped to his civil duty of dishing out alcoholic beverages. He sat back on his chair, the general chatter amongst regulars now beginning to fill the air, killing off the unease that had arrived previously with the silence. All seemed peaceful... quite humane in fact, until a couple of rather sly looking characters made their way over to Sherwin's table... with a little more than just anticipated conversation on the cards. Looking sharp in their biker attire and fearsome in their appearance, they had a chill that would make a grown man cry. But Sherwin? He was no ordinary man... having survived one hell of car accident and a horrific jobbing to John Zero. No, he was strong enough to rebound even from the brink of looking like a complete and utter loser.] Random Biker: 'Word is... you're thinking of getting sex change because you look to that zero, John Zero.' Random Biker 2: 'Yeah... what he said... and the other word is that you're going to be running around using the name of Betty or something.' Random Biker 3: '... and also, I heard you like to take it in the pooper. Now that man, that's well and truely sick.' [Rich didn't look impressed at the sight of these burly characters, their presence doing little to stimulate his aggressive tendancies. Sherwin simply looked past the three morons and signalled to the barman, wanting him to bring the drink over to him.] Random Biker: 'Oh... so now the n00b happens to get served directly by the tender? Isn't that the biggest pisstake in the book of pisstakes, Billy?' Billy: 'Amen to that! I had to wait for close to thirteen months before I could get this kind of service around here. You ain't nothing but a little newbie who's come in for a few drinks. I bet you're not even going to be coming back to this place after tonight.' [The two bikers sounded menancing with their words, trying to make a point of the fact that Sherwin wasn't one of the regulars at this particular location. The one known as Billy took a step forward and looked down at Rich, his eyes piercing a proverbial hole through his being. Aggitated at the lack of response he was recieving from the former GW Champ, Billy took a step back and kicked the table away from our hero, laughing as the feeble wooden structure crashed to the floor.] Billy: '... Just incase you didn't get the message there, if you're wanting to have any of this fine quality service that is only reserved for the regular persuasion... you're going to have to do something that'll show us you're worthy of getting it. Nobody gets our service for free... not even you skanky multi-million dollar earning professional fools.' [Billy stepped back from Sherwin, his co-horts moving behind him, all three of them laughing at Rich's misfortune. It was only three paces before our Champion could no longer take the verbal abuse from these annoying people. Sherwin stepped up from his chair and looked toward all three of the bikers, them turning around in turn with Sherwin's vertical ascent.] Sherwin: 'I don't think you have the right to decide who gets service a, b or c. In fact, I don't think you have the right to determine anything of that sort. You see, to you I might just be some guy who fights on television for a living... but to me and about thirteen million people, I'm a guy who can mix it outside that television box just as well as I can mix it inside... or didn't you know, I survived the drop of a cliff and recovered in a little over one week...' [Sherwin's words stank of cocky sentiment, everything laced together as though it were some kind of river... flowing down toward this sea of biker individuals.] Sherwin: '.... and no, no, NO... don't think my excellence ends there, because if you did, you'd be beaten with a stick by big Deac's seventy-five member family... and that's just the kids from home. In fact, in truth, in whatever else says 'This is not fiction'... Rich Sherwin is also the master of a secret art, the art that formerly belonged to legends like MacGuyver... I can make official sized army combat knives out of a coat-hanger and tape.' [Sounding mighty impressive, Sherwin thought that his message was being sent across this tub of lard without delay. In any given moment, he thought these people were going to start screaming for their lives now that they knew exactly how dangerous Rich Sherwin was. After all, this was a man with a mission.. with a purpose... perhaps even something of a story on the cards?] Billy: 'Is that supposed to be everything? Were you trying to scare us with some kind of technical knowledge? I mean, I lost you after you started to rabbit on and on about how you were wanting to make a coat hanger out of an army knife or something.' [Billy and his cohorts looked confused, their attention turning to Sherwin for some kind of answer... some kind of guide... but NO, today was not the day that Rich was going to be giving out all these tips, all of these EXCLUSIVE secrets from the MacGuyver fan club... -=cheap plug=- MEMBERSHIP FOR A YEAR IS ONLY $5.95 IF YOU USE RICH SHERWIN AS A REFFERAL!! -=/cheap plug=-. Sherwin gave them a quick look, the hint of sarcasm lingering in the sparkle of his eyes. He took a step toward these three figures, looking at each and every one of their confused faces. He stopped for a few moments to examine the immediate surroundings, the three bikers still confused as to what he was actually doing.] Billy: '... What exactly are you doing now?' [Billy asked in a tone that was almost like that of a confused five year old, even the most simple of tasks running past his brain like a fat kid running the 100 to a twinkie. Sherwin chuckled to himself for a few moments before looking at each of these figures a final time.] Sherwin: 'I'm just looking to see where you'd land if I were hit each and every one of you on your asses.' [Rich replied with a grin, giving the three bikers almost no time to comprehend what was said before he nailed cohort one with a stiff kick to the mid-section and cohort two with a stiff right hook. Billy stood and watched in bewilderment as Sherwin shook off the blows with ease, composing himself in a position ready for attack. With his feet shoulder-width apart, one infront of the other... and his arms held up to shoulder level, fists poised at either side of his head, Billy saw no other option but to attack. Lashing forward without a hope or clue or doing anything, Sherwin easily side-stepped the oncoming attack and scored with a huge uppercut, sending the big man up... and down, through a weak, wooden table. Looking satisfied with his handy work, Sherwin walked over to the bar and dropped a few dollar bills onto it.] Sherwin: '... That should cover the cost of that cheap, little table.' [Sherwin said it as though he didn't care about the damage caused, walking off into the distance with himself confident that he'd paid enough to cover the damages. With his four way contest at Evolution creeping around the corner and his impending case against Juan Gonzalez, one can only begin to wonder if this method of ridding yourself of aggression is the idea of Sherwin's apparent legal representation... or whether this is his own last chance to control that damned temper of his. Regardless... NEVER GIVE UP!]
