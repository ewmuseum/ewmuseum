Sin Is In!

19 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01-19-05 The Global Wrestling logo appears on the screen for a few seconds before disappearing again. All that can be seen is a blank screen until the sound of a pumping heart can be heard slowly beating. Words in red lettering now come up and read: Just when you thought it was safe to get back in the ring... The beats start to get faster as now a monitor can be also heard along with it. At the top of the screen something that looks like blood begins running down the screen. Another message pops up for those who are viewing. 01-24-05 The beating heart gets to be uncontrollable and then in an instant it stops and the heart monitor goes off into what would be a code blue to those who are familiar with the hospital terminology. The blood continues to trickle until the entire screen is red. In silver metallic lettering one more message is displayed. GW will never be the same. The sound of a sinister laughter can be heard and the name “Corvus” appears on the screen before the promo ends and turns to snow and scratchy audio. This is the first thing that the wrestlers see of the man that has been talked about quite a bit lately. ---- Las Vegas: home to a melting pot of culture and also a place where people would bet the clothing off their backs just for the chance to become rich. On the strip where bright lights, people with big hopes, and many strippers try to make a living is where one man makes his way through the crowd. As the camera focuses upon him you see that he is a rather awesome specimen of brute force but yet almost seems like he might not be harmful. Looks are deceiving as they say and that would be very true of this man. He is a man who has garnered a name for himself in the arena of professional wrestling or “sports entertainment” as they are labeling it these days. It is just sport to this man as he does not care much about entertaining. He did at one time but it all seemed to wash away when he realized that he was in a business where you had to be the predator or you easily became the prey. He was a hunter, plain and simple. You could even say he was a shark with some of the ruthless measure he took when it came to business. This was the man who was going to bring something different to Global Wrestling. Sure it had its fill of “bad” guys and such but he was one of the all time greats when it came to destruction. He was Corvus, the scourge of the squared circle. There were so many other names that fit him well but they are too numerous to go over. Taking one look you know this man is trouble. From head to toe this 7 foot man has the sense about him that if looks truly could kill the world would have a population gap. Black sketchers adorn his feet as well as black leather pants, the same color silk shirt and a trenchcoat to match. His long black hair has crimson highlights in them that you can see with just the right lighting. Rounded sunglasses cover his eyes which if he had them off are a sky blue in hue. On his hands which normally you do not see when he is in the ring are covered with different rings that seem to have a distinct pattern to them and may even tell a tale about this behemoth that you would normally not be able to get out of him on your own. The camera continues on him until he seems to just disappear in the crowd. The footage continues rolling as you begin to hear his voice which seems a bit raspy and chilling when he speaks. ?Good evening...ladies and germs. You’ve already seen the Las Vegas strip and some of the creatures that inhabit it. Now it is time for me to give you a little synopsis of what I am about and why I decided to call Global Wrestling my home. Betrayal...everyone loves a story about betrayal and do I have one for you. It all started when my brothers (and you could say sister) in arms known as The Kindred decided to turn their backs on me. From that moment on my career seemed to go into a bit of a spiral. From there was the next worst moment of my life. The WMW had decided to sign a member from the GW roster. You all know him as Train. It was in secret meetings and things where the management decided they were going to screw me out of the Great Lakes Championship and to do this they were going to implement Train into it. The rest is history...the championship, my pride, pretty much shot to hell in a handbasket. There was nowhere else for me to go besides be a lackey and have a desk job where people would feed me bullcrap to say and that just was not me. I am a man who does what he pleases and I fulfill no destiny by my own even if that means taking down someone else’s.? The scene then changes from the strip to the very top of a building that has a great view of the skyline in which of the most elite and fanciest hotels in all of Las Vegas, the Luxor, can be seen. Corvus sits against one of the air ducts smoking a black cigarette (or clove for those who know what they are). He continues on with his story as he is getting towards the here and now of the present. ?After my now former employers raped me they decided that they weren’t going to give me any work even after I lobbied for a few different things. Then they decided that it was fine for me to try and get “revenge” on Train for his participation in the match in which I lost my belt. As all things are with that company, it was too good to be true. They decided that they wanted it to end in without a clear winner and that my associate Dawn McFarlane was going to betray me. The end result would be that Train and I would both get some payback on her for interfering. That never happened as she was suspended and hasn’t been heard from since...at least not by me anyway. I’m sure you are getting bored with this as much as it is boring me to tell you so I’m going to speed this up some and get closer to where we are here in one of the places that suits me better than anyone knows. After the match with Train they decide to not give me anymore work yet again. Despite trying to pitch ideas to them that fell on deaf ears I was able to sit home and terrorize no one other than myself and if you know me...that is unproductive. Then just a few days before my contract is up they give me a match with some punk they pulled off the street. I told them to stick it because by the time my match was to take place I would be not contracted. I was not about to take anymore of their crap.? Corvus stands up and drops the finished butt of the clove and smashes it into the ground. He walks over towards the edge of the building where he looks towards the hotel and stretches his arms out. ?I sat at home for a while until the phone rang...it was Bubba McCoy. He said that he had an offer for me and that he wanted me to come to GW. He liked my work and said he’d be willing to do what needed to be done to get me to wrestle for him. The money he offered was suitable but there was one other thing that I made sure that he knew needed to happen for me to be on the Global roster, which was to make sure that there would be a Crossfire 2 this year against WMW. Then and only then will I show them the error in their ways for screwing over their biggest star to have come along that wasn’t stale like left out milk. GW, your hopes of surviving the onslaught that I have in store for you is slim and nil. Just look at some of my matches for Weak Minded Wrestling and try and tell me different. I ran those bitches and just like that, the talent in GW will bow to ME!” The wind begins to rustle and the camera blurs a bit and the next thing you know Corvus is gone from sight. The scene fades to black but in an instant we come to what looks to be the inside of a hotel room. There is activity going on by the hot tub. Corvus has surrounded himself by two very stunning beauties: one having black hair and the other a red head. They are huddled close to him as they have glasses of wine sitting on the counter of the tub. The two women’s glasses are filled with white wine while Corvus? seems a bit darker in nature and not like most typical red wines. His arms are around them as he looks up towards the camera to begin a speech. ?While I have a little bit of time left before I begin my tempestuous career as a member of GW, I decided to enjoy some of the finer things the city of Las Vegas had to offer. They don’t call it Sin City for nothing...and the great part about that is that I fit right in. I fail to see though for my first match they decide to put me in with two guys that have about as much talent together as Fat Albert has in a diet program. I guess I’m just going to have to lobby for something better in my next venture. These guys are not going to be much just by their names alone. Conner Evenger: It sounds like this guy has been reading too many comic books. You shouldn’t have traded in your pocket protector for wrestling gear. I don’t care who you are aligned with but I don’t think there’s anything you can do from not catching a severe ass beating. Even your buddy The Lamb Molester won’t recognize you after I break your face. Chris Gothic: Another original name. I’ve probably ran into a half dozen guys when I scouted the independents that had that name or something similar. It seems as the years progress and wrestling is on the downturn people get less and less creative. That’s fine though...you’re not keeping yourself occupied with wrestling. You’d rather go off and play merry assassin to get into the “evil club”. You guys will learn...I nearly have stock in the word fear...shit I practically invented it. Your ineptitude shall be your downfall and just another notch in the belt for the Dark Prince. Evolution will be one that no one, fans or wrestlers alike will ever forget. January 24th, 2005 is going to be the beginning of the CORVUS era of Global Wrestling.” Corvus reaches over for his glass and finishes it in one fell swoop. The girls look at him in awe as if he were the only man on the planet. He seemed to have an effect on people. They were drawn to him and this was no exception. Could it be he had money? Maybe it was because of his physique. No one knows but it has also been rumored that he had some sort of devil worshipping powers or so was said by broadcasters of WMW. One of the girls asks him to tell them more about his “special abilities” in which he chuckles and has to address it quickly in front of the camera before they cut him off. He wouldn’t really care about what he says but in this instance it is different. ?Ladies now would not be a good time to discuss that. However, I will say that it has to do with this tremendous body that I’ve kept in fine tune in what you could say is...forever. There are so many that have tried to attempt the combination I have made so lethal in this business. I have the body of a god and the intelligence to match! Conner, Chris...I honestly look forward to facing you because it gives me the chance to use a combination of my techniques on the two of you. Though if you manage to still be breathing after this you may want to go see Bubba and ask him why the hell he fed you to the wolves. Whether it is the slow Descent Into Hell or the harsh reality of the Cold Embrace the two of you have a lot to think about between now and Evolution. I’m going to be thinking about something else I have planned until the time I walk through the curtains to see your jaws drop and the stupefaction on your faces because you’ve never faced anything like me. I’m not one of those muscular bone heads you see all over the place...I have a plan and a mission and I’ll see it out. The two of you are to be witnesses of it and later on you’ll be honored that you were a part of my list of victims in my Global reign of terror.? Corvus then looks to the two women with a smirk and then reaches and finds some darts that he had lined along the top of the hot tub. The camera swivels over to the wall where there are two pictures lined up one right next to the other of his opponents on Evolution. Corvus throws one dart precisely and hits Conner Evenger’s picture right in the jugular and does the same with that of Chris Gothic’s. Then he follows up with throwing at their eyes and finishing up with their foreheads. ?I stated before that a man controls his own destiny and I am fully going to start to implement that destiny for Global at Evolution. They picked a good name for their show...no one has evolved more than yours truly. I started as someone as plain as the day to becoming one of the most feared creatures on the face of the earth. I didn’t do that by standing idly by while others did their business. I don’t wait for things to happen, I take charge and make them happen. My parting gift of words for you boys since I know you are watching this is good luck. I hope you respect me for not sugar coating anything because that is just not who I am. When I became a wrestler for GW, they knew they signed one of the most controversial men to step into the ring. Evenger, Gothic...everyone has a devil; I just happen to be YOURS!” Corvus grins as he looks to the women again and they smile back to him as he turns to face towards the camera to give them the hint to hit the bricks. Before the promo goes off the air you can hear him saying: ?Ladies I’m sure glad I came to Las Vegas because Cleveland FREAKIN sucks!? A sinister laugh accompanies that and then the video footage dies.
