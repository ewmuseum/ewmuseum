The Max-Attack: Chapter 7

5 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 5 The scene opens up to Roswell’s little hotel room. On the bed lays Roswell still sleeping. Suddenly an alarm clock starts ringing and Roswell is up like that. Roswell gets up from the bed and starts stretching his muscles and he also cracks his back. Roswell walks over to the curtains and opens them. Sunlight blares Roswell in the face as Roswell peers down at the city of Chicago. Roswell looks straight down and still sees that man there with the big heavy coat on laying on the bench. The man is sleeping on the bench and some people start looking at him funny. “Does that guy live on that bench?” questions Roswell to his self. Roswell takes off the plain white shirt he was wearing and puts on his grey see through one that he wrestles in. He then also puts on his jeans he wrestles in that were specially designed for him with Roswell going down one side and lighting bolts going down the other. Roswell walks away from the window and walks into the bathroom. Roswell takes a bottle with water in it and sprays his hair till it’s really wet. Roswell then takes his black comb and combs down his long hair so he can see. Roswell takes out his toothbrush and wets that to. He then takes some of the extra hot toothpaste and puts that on the toothbrush. Roswell starts brushing his teeth for a couple of minutes and stops. Roswell wets the brush and puts it back in the bag. Roswell then takes a cup that was on the counter and fills it with water. Roswell starts drinking the water and swishes it around in his mouth and spits it out in the sink. Roswell walks out of the bathroom and leaves the room turning off the light right by the door. Roswell reaches into his pocket and sees if he brought a room key with him. Roswell brings out the key and puts it back into his pocket. Roswell walks over to the elevator and pushes the DOWN button. The door opens and Roswell walks into the elevator and pushes the M on the panel. The door opens again and Roswell walks out and to the door of the hotel. Roswell walks outside covering his eyes a little bit protecting from the sun. Roswell takes a package of gum out from his pocket and pops one into his mouth. Roswell walks over to a garbage can and puts the package into the garbage. Roswell still notices the man laying on the bench snoring loudly. Roswell looks at the guys face and sees that the man had lost a few teeth and his eye was bleeding. Roswell tries to wipe the blood but the man growls at Roswell. “What’s his problem. I was only trying to help the guy out.” Roswell says. Roswell also notices a gun hanging out from the guys pocket. The gun is a really shiny black and is pointing at the guys crotch. A police officer walks by and asks Roswell what he is looking at. Roswell says that there is a gun in that guys pocket that he just noticed. The police officer takes out handcuffs and wakes the man up. The police officer takes the gun and puts it into a bag. The police officer also handcuffs the guy and says he is under arrest for weapon in pocket. The police officer takes the man to his car at the end of the street and puts him inside it. The police officer drives away with the man and Roswell just stands there amazed and stunned. Roswell walks away from the bench but notices something else. The is a little ball laying on the bench in one of the cracks. Roswell picks it up and starts looking at it. “Hey, this looks like those other balls I have laying on the desk in my hotel room.” says Roswell. Roswell takes the ball and runs back into the hotel. Roswell quickly goes a the elevator and pushes the UP button. Roswell walks into the elevator and pushes the 5th floor button. The door opens and Roswell starts running down the hall to his room. Roswell takes out the room key and opens his door. Roswell goes and puts the ball beside the other ones and rushes back out of the room. Roswell starts running again but goes into the stairwell. Roswell runs down the stairs and goes into the lobby door. Roswell runs across the lobby and out of the hotel onto the sidewalk. Roswell starts walking down the street looking at all the hobos in the dumpsters and some living in boxes. Roswell takes out a toonie and throws it to one of the hobos. The hobo says go to hell to Roswell and runs down a back alley. Roswell then comes up to a movie theatre and sees that Bruce Alimighty is playing right now. Roswell walks into the theatre and asks for one ticket. Roswell also buys a bag of popcorn and a small drink. Roswell walks into the movie theatre and sits down in one of the chairs close to the front of the screen.
