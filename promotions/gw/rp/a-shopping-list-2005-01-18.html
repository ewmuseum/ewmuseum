A Shopping List

18 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Jan 18 2005 It was a long and endless drive from the prison to wherever Saloak had decided to take me. He told me many things on the route, everything he thought I needed to hear. I heard all of it, but only absorbed some of it. His father, Chris Davis had been brutally murdered, but I didn’t pay attention to which the killer was, nor did I care. He told me that for some reason a family fortune had been left in my name, hence making my bail. His father had left it to me, for reasons unknown and a small piece of paper. The paper itself explained to me everything that Saloak himself couldn’t. “Chris, If you are reading this, I am surely dead. Betrayed by my own blood with so much left to do in my life. I leave to you everything I own in hopes that you will be the voice of shining reason upon my son Saloak, for I fear what he would do with my power should he receive it. These powers shall be yours once you complete a small task for me, a list of names is all I need taken care of, and how you take care of them is up to you. -Chris Davis” The note was badly penned on a small piece of notepaper and sealed in an envelope with the wax seal of the lair holding it shut. Saloak practically read it off the reflection in my eyes and I saw a look of anger come across his face then suddenly disappear. He reached under his seat in the limo and slid a black briefcase over to my feet. With a simple nod of his head I opened the briefcase and nearly fainted at what I saw. Rows of green bills, wadded into ten thousand dollar stacks filled the bottom of the briefcase, and a silver pistol was taped to the top of the briefcases lid. It was a magnificent site, about a million dollars cash and a silver pistol with gold trim and the words “carpe noctum” engraved along side the barrel. Latin for “Seize the Night” It wasn’t just any pistol though; it was a .44 caliber desert eagle, a pistol with enough punch the blow a grown mans head off from 20 feet away. I didn’t care about the money I was just given, because shit, this gun itself is a perfect way to get a million dollars with little effort. The country scenery flew by as Saloak and I rode in silence, we were soon coming up to the Canada/US border and I gave Saloak a questionable look. He smiled as the black stretch limousine pulled up to customs and shot right through the gate, much to my surprise. The Border guards looked on as the limo peeled through the security stops and right into Washington State. They didn’t even chase us. “You work for the lair now, Young Gothic,” Saloak said quietly, as he exhaled a cloud of smoke from the cigarette in his mouth. He passed a cigarette to me and I lit it, taking a few drags. “And those who work for the lair, receive diplomatic immunity anywhere on the North American Continent. You could have shot the guards and nothing would have happened to you. You are a license to kill now Mr. Gothic” I looked at Saloak with disbelief and surprise, as I could imagine what this would mean for my personal gain, and myself “What do you mean by this, Saloak?” I asked him. “It means, that once you have been given the list, you have to worry about nothing as long as you complete the list. Many people on this continent have fought the Davis family and only those with the powers of the law have been able to put a dent in our reign. My father told me that you were the best hired gun on the planet, and for that he has left you in his will.” “A list?” I said quickly, I knew there was a reason for the gun and the money, and especially why this man of all people would bail me out of jail. “A list of what” Groceries, Christmas Presents?” I said with a slight mockery in my voice, something that Saloak didn’t seem very pleased with. “No, A shopping list of sorts” A list of names that the Lair wants you to complete, you finish this list for us and you will become a full member of the Nevermore, and will have more power at your disposal then you could ever thought to achieve on your own.” Saloak slowly stood up and sat beside Gothic in the limo, without stopping his sentence. “The people on this list are the people that have caused the lair the most problems in the past ten years, the names go from Lawyers, to Doctors, to retired Fighters. Each and everyone of them needs to be killed, and the law enforcement wont do anything if they catch you in the act. You are considered a god amongst men” Saloak undid the seatbelt of Chris and then reached across him and unlocked the door. “You have been given the power Chris, don’t abuse it.” Quickly Saloak opens the door of the limo and hurls Gothic out, he rolls across the pavement along side the freeway protecting his briefcase full of money more then his own head, the limo speeds off towards the South and Gothic is left Standing on the side of the highway. He glances down at his hand and somehow Saloak had written something on his hand, without Gothic noticing. The writing said “Daniel Meeks, Washington State Attorney” The first name on the list.
