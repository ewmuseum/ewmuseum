At The Docks

13 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
At The Docks Tony Stecchino Global Wrestling Promo #420 Date: 7/13/03 Location: Pier 41, Vancouver, British Columbia A cell phone is heard ringing. “Hello.” “It’s Nicky.” “And?” “It’s here.” “Good. Be there in a second.” Stecchino hangs up the phone. He reaches over with his right hand and turns on the light next to his bed. He slowly sits up, he is shirtless. The blankets, cover the bottom part of his body. He wipes the sleep from his eyes and puts the phone next to a picture frame. He slowly removes his hand and stares down at it. It’s a picture of him and Melissa a day after they were married, while on honeymoon in Hawaii. God damn how he misses her. He knows it all his fault she left him for Maverick Reno. Stecchino used her in the biggest screw job ever pulled in GW. It tortured him so much to the brink of madness. Well it took him over the brink of madness and into the depths of hell. Finally his life is starting to shape up again, things are all in a row and he is no longer psycho. Stecchino picks up the picture and holds up in front of his face. He sees his smiling face and Melissa’s. They were so happy together and he ruined it all. Nothing can be done now. All hope is lost in the relationship. She no longer speaks to him, but how he longs to speak with her. To hear her sweet voice over the phone, to see her beautiful eyes one more time, to hold her, to kiss her. Only in dreams can they be true. In reality, its pure fiction and nothing more. “Melissa…” Stecchino speaks. “Melissa, there is so much I wish I could do different. Using you to make people feel sorry for me. That was so wrong. So, so wrong of me to do. It drove the last barrier between us. Now there is a breach between us that a seven forty seven could not cross. Why did I do that? WHY!?!?!?” Showing that the man does have emotion, unlike the previous viewing of Stecchino a few months ago in GW, where he was a raging psychopath, throwing kittens into a fire in the mountains of Japan. Fearing that Maverick Reno stood behind him. Looking over his shoulder. Watching his every move. Feeling his every breath. Counting every thought and know what it would be. Beating him to the step before it was even conceived in the brain. “One day you will be mine again and we will be happy, like we used to be. You’ll see. Everyone will see. You and me. It’s meant to be.” Stecchino tosses the blankets aside. A forklift carrying a large crate comes in front of a ship. Byung Chi is the name. Nicky is close to the edge of the dock directing traffic as a crane pulls crates out of the belly of the ship and sets it down on the concrete of the dock. “HEY!!! HERE!!!” Nicky says pointing toward an empty space next to some guys standing with crow bars. Slowly one by one, girls are coming off the ship deck and down to the dock. They are all dirty, hungry and scared. They were literally ripped from their homes and brought to the ‘promise land’ Vancouver, British Columbia, where in thirty years of selling their bodies, they can become free citizens of Canada. But no man will touch the STD diseased body in ten. They will be replaced by a new batch. Some will die of drug overdoses, others will be murdered and their bodies dumped in a pig farm out in the middle of nowhere. No one knowing where they are, nor caring who there were or where they came from. True, it’s a sad realization, but its fact and not fiction. Those five hundred girls getting off the ship are cash pussy and nothing more. A black Lincoln Town car pulls up next to the warehouse. The back door opens and out steps Tony Stecchino. He says something fast to the driver and then closes the door. The engine of the car shuts off and the headlights go out. Stecchino spots Nicky and begins walking right for him. Nicky sees this and has some one take over his position as director of traffic. He begins walking toward Stecchino. They meet each other half way. They shake hands and exchange quick pleasantries. “We can have the ship unloaded by dawn, Tony.” “Excellent. We got a count of everything.” “Slight problem with that.” “Problem? Nicky, you assured me everything would be smooth.” “Everything has been smooth on my end. It’s the Chinamen sir. They did not complete their order.” Stecchino looks genuinely annoyed. He runs his hand through his hair and begins thinking. Thinking about to run a smooth crime operation, everything has to fall in place and be complete. Nothing half assed, nothing not completed. An order that is not complete, shall not be sufficient. He has men to pay, a business to run for God’s sake. With half the order, most of the money will go to his employees and he wont get jack shit. Good thing he signed the deal with the devil yesterday. “OK… We are going to have to make due for right now. I want a count on the pussy and I want a count on the opiates. Oh… I don’t care how old the girls are. I want them out on the street. There are busses coming soon, to take them to the apartment building I own. Make sure they get settled in and calmed down. I want them on the street by next Sunday, capice?” “OK…” “Fucking Chinese, always trying to screw me over. I gave them ten million for this and we had a verbal deal. Can you handle the rest of this?” “Sure can.” “That’s why I love you Nicky. I must go to Stevie’s.” “Nickels?” “Yep.” “Word on the street is he aint too happy with you.” “Well, word is wrong. Stevie is my best friend and has been for seventeen years. Since I was seven. We have a tight bond, that nothing can break. There is no reason for us to be on bad terms.” “I was just telling’ ya what I heard boss.” “Call me when everything is unloaded and inventory is taken.” “Will do.” “Later.” “Later.” They shake hands. Nicky heads back to directing traffic. The crane, the forklifts, the men carrying boxes and women. Some boxes of women. Stecchino heads back to his car. He opens the door and sits down. “Take me to Stevie’s.” “In Seattle?” The driver replied. “Yes. I need to talk to him. I need my right hand man back on my side.” “You’re the boss.” “Can you make it there?” “Yeah, I am good to go. Not that sleepy at all.” “Good.” The car pulls away as the bustling commotion of an illegal night at the docks continue. Stecchino thinking about Melissa again. He can never get her out of her head. She is a permanent fixture in his mind. Through every tragic thing in his life. Just thinking of her makes it all better. Everything bad is just magically whisked away. Too many mistakes. Everything was ruined. Now on to a more pressing matter. Why in the hell would his best friend hate him? Where did the heat come from? Why? How? Huh? So many questions and it’s 4:37 in the morning and there is no sign of daylight through the blinding rain. Isn’t summer? Got to love the northwest, well southwest Canada. It rains all the time. Two seasons. Rain and rainier. This is a joke.
