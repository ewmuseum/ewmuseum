Missing You

14 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08-14-2005 Dawn slipped one long leg into the steaming, soapy water of her bathtub and couldn’t suppress a sigh of quiet pleasure. The other leg followed and she gently, gingerly, lowered herself into the near scalding water, pale skin turning red almost immediately under the heat. Sliding into the molded “seat” at the head of the bathtub she sighed again, letting the water lap up around her throat and luxuriating in having a bathtub that could accommodate her lanky frame. When she had been given custody of her parent’s estate, renovations had been one of the first things on her mind. The only rooms that had remained untouched were her fathers study and the attic, although the latter had been cleaned of mold and re-insulated. When she’d had the master bathroom redone she’d disposed of the bathtub that stood on four legs and had a custom “bathtub” put in its place. Alex had remarked on first seeing it that it was more like having a pool, since the porcelain tub was designed to allow Dawn to stretch out to full length and still have the water up around her neck. Very slowly Dawn leaned back against the sculpted “seat” her back protesting with each tiny movement, finally ceasing its painful protests when it came to rest against the warmed porcelain. The aches and pains of Master of the Mat had come upon her in full force the day after, and she could feel each kick and moonsault, each spinebuster and suplex. The inside of her lip was healing slowly from its painful split, and she had to remind herself not to worry the itchy skin to the point where it began bleeding again. Letting her eyes slip slowly closed Dawn tilted her head back against the padded neck rest, trying in vain to bring her chaotic thoughts to rest in the same way her body was currently. Thoughts of Masters of the Mat bounced around inside her brain like it was a fleshy pinball machine, each thought sparking off a new series of worrying or wondering, thoughts of triumph and victory’and a very small voice that nudged the back of her mind, whispering in a cold voice?Was it really worth it? Dawn had never been a champion in her entire life, had never had the chance to truly follow in her father’s footsteps, to ensure his eldest daughter was just as successful a McFarlane as he was’and then suddenly it all changed. She’d scarcely believed it when her hand had been raised in victory over Elia, Josh and Fred’and the Global Cruiserweight title had been thrust into her hands. In fact she’d been so surprised it hadn’t even hit her at first, she was too focused on the prize, too focused on the end of Masters of the Mat’and too focused on Lia. She couldn’t deny the pride she felt in being a double champion, nor the ultimate sense of triumph she felt in having become the 2005 Mistress of the Mat, the first woman to ever hold that prestigious title. To be finally holding not one, but three belts at the same time’her heart swelled at the thought of her father watching her now, seeing what his little girl had achieved? “but at the center of it all was Lia, her face clouded by the sadness of what had happened. Dawn pressed the heels of palms against her eyes, trying to push the image of Lia’s prone form out of her mind’the sound and sensation when she’d delivered the Tsunami Bomb to the only woman in the world who meant anything to her. She could still feel Lia’s gentle hands on her shoulders, the cool touch of her skin around the straps of Dawn’s tank top. The press of her cold lips was burned into Dawn’s mind, the feeling she had longed for, the taste of the vampires delicate mouth was still foremost in her mind, the taste of something she’d wanted for what seemed like an eternity. She raised one dripping hand to her lips, memories of that day swirling through her mind like a tornado. “Lia’wait?” The words came unbidden to her lips, a stark reminder of how quickly the vampiress had fled the garden and the estate. Taking a deep breath Dawn slipped under the water, trying to force the confused jumble of thoughts out with the sheer heat. But even under the heat of the water she could still feel it, the cold press of Lia’s skin against hers, that feeling that for just one moment’for one second’everything was right in the world. Her lungs burned and she sat back up out of the water with a gasp, sucking in air as quickly as she could. A dark shape lunged up on the side of the tub and Dawn couldn’t hold back a strangled scream, scrabbling up the side of the tub towards the window and looking for something she could use as a weapon. A deep wuffle came from the dark shape and Dawn screeched to a halt, clumsily swiping her wet bangs out of her eyes to look at her “attacker”. The dark, shaggy form wuffled again and this time Dawn could clearly make out the form of one of Lia’s hell hounds, his paws up on the side of the tub, peering at her with those too intelligent eyes. Dawn slid back into the tub with an exasperated sigh, “Damek! Bad dog! You scared the life out of me!” Damek wuffled again, leaning over to nudge Dawn’s head with his huge nose, whining a little the back of her throat. “Oh sure,” She grumbled, “Laugh at me and then come looking for snuggles.” She reached up to absently scratch behind his massive ears, staring off into space over his shoulder. The “dog” whined again, butting his head against her shoulder and staring at her, clearly trying to get her attention. Dawn turned to look at him, fixing her eyes on his own. “What” I already put you out before I came up here, and I fed you a few minutes before that. What do you want?” Damek whined again, this time being joined by a similar sound from Jirina as she put her paws on the edge of the tub as well, staring at Dawn with those same eyes. Dawn looked back and forth between the two of them in confusion, “What” What do you two want from me? You can’t come in here if that’s what you want, this bath would be barely big enough for Lia and I?” Her voice trailed off and she sighed, reaching out to pat both hell hounds. “You miss your mistress, you miss Lia.” Both creatures perked a little at the name and Dawn sighed again. “I wish I knew. It’s been almost two weeks?” Lia’s absence was getting under Dawn’s skin more then she wanted to admit. Although she’d seen the sultry vampiress at Masters of the Mat they’d not had a chance to say more then two words to each other’and when the show was over Lia had disappeared again, this time not even leaving a note to explain her absence. Dawn thumped the back of her against the tub in frustration. “Dammit Lia’where are you?” Concern over Lia’s whereabouts and state of health had joined into the rest of Dawn’s confused jumble of thoughts, leaving her feeling worried and drained. The pain of her physical injuries had eased some in the hot water, but if anything her thoughts were even more chaotic then when she’d started. Shaking her head at her own frustration Dawn pushed herself up out of the tub, sudsy water cascading down her naked form as she stepped from the tub and toweled herself off, rubbing her already red skin a further shade darker as if she was toweling away the frustration of the day instead of the water of her bath. Wrapping the damp towel around herself she padded into her bedroom, Damek and Jirina following on either side of her and just a pace or two behind. Dawn paused by her dresser, eyes pulled by the gleam of gold in the dim light from the bathroom. Each of her three titles was laid out atop the dresser, the usual pile of combs and makeup having been forcibly relocated to a nearby chair. Her eyes flicker over each one with pride, reading over each individual nameplate as she passed??Dawn McFarlane?. Her gaze paused and wavered at the TV title, her expression becoming uncertain as she gazed at the title she wasn’t even sure she wanted to win. She could almost she Lia’s face reflected in the shining surface, and her own name was replaced in her mind??Shadow.” As quickly as the moment came it passed again, drawing a sigh of equal parts confusion and regret from Dawn. “I wish I knew what you thought of all this’I just want to talk?” Her face hardened again as the thoughts of her duties to that title intruded on her mind. “But whatever we work out’I’ll defend this title for you. Nobody is going to take it from me’and sure as hell not Leinad Fucking Soma.” She nodded resolutely, wishing Lia could be here to hear those words, and turned back to her clothes lying in a heap on the bed. As she reached for her shirt a low sound came from behind her, a deep rumble that began to gain in both force and volume. Dawn turned in surprise, her wide eyes going to the pair of Hell Hounds, each of whom was standing near the door to Dawn’s adjoining balcony. Their paired growling was enough to set Dawn’s hair standing straight on the back of her neck, robbing her of words for a moment. Fear was an unusual emotion for Dawn’but very suddenly she was painfully aware of how vulnerable she was right now. Nearly naked, alone and in the middle of no where’with a pair of potentially hostile monsters in her bedroom. “Damek?” Her voice was hoarse, little more then a raspy whisper. The hell hound turned his head towards her, the growling stopping for a moment, but his too intelligent eyes held a sense of urgency she’s never seen before’and one that took her only a moment to recognize. Dawn had always liked dogs, they were fierce and loyal, friendly and reliable’and they made wonderful guard animals. She dropped into a low crouch, slipping along the carpet until she was between the two rumbling canine’s, her breath sticking in her throat. With a hesitant hand she reached up to unlatch her balcony door, pushing it open just a crack. The hell hounds growling intensified, but Dawn’s eyes could pick out nothing in the darkened forest around her’wait? A shadow flitted along the edge of the lights on the outside of the house, darting out of the forest for only an instant, but unmistakable. Dawn’s breath caught as picked out another form mere moments later’they were ringing the house. She eased the door shut again, turning to look at Jirina. “Of all the times I wish you could talk’what are they” What do they want?” Jirina gazed back at her and the answer sprung unbidden to her mind, given words by the fear that suddenly blossomed alongside. “Lia’they’re here for Lia?” For a moment she was paralyzed, uncertain what she could possibly do against these’things. Lia could have defended herself, Vigil likely could have done just as well as his lover, even Alex would know what to do’her background afforded her a multitude of weapons against foes like this’for a few fleeting moments Dawn was gripped with her own utter helplessness, unable to accept that there was nothing she could do should they breach the house? “Breach the house?” Dawn mused to herself, a slow smile suddenly spreading across her pretty lips. “How could I be so stupid?” She practically flew to her bedside table, throwing open the drawer and tossing objects aside as she sought something thrown into it months before. With a cry of triumph she pulled put a small black remote, she held it up to the light, gazing with satisfaction at the two buttons within. Normal. Sunlight. She held her thumb over the button marked “Sunlight”, pausing for a moment before stabbing it down. On the outside of the estate the external lighting suddenly went dead, and for a moment she feared it wouldn’t work’but then the soft halogen glow was replaced by a brilliantly white light that pierced through the windows and doors of her bedroom. All along the rooftop a series of concealed lights sprang to life, ringing the McFarlane estate with a brilliant white circle of UV lighting. There was a howl from outside, quickly joined by several others, the sound slowly retreating into the distance. A triumphant grin settled onto Dawn’s face and she flipped the remote into the air, deftly snagging it once again. “Looks like it was worth the money afterall.” Scant weeks after her relationship with Corvus had ended, Dawn feared that Marcus would return to her life. The circle of abuse and humiliation she had endured at her hand had still be fresh in her mind, and she’d realized she had no way to stop him if he decided he wanted her again’until she remembered something Corvus has said. “UV lighting is just as good as Sunlight, “He’d remarked, “If that bastard comes at you while I’m not around, just give him a little UB flashlight.” Dawn had done him one better, paying extra to have a ring of outward facing UV lights installed on the roof of the estate. Inside the safety of her bedroom Dawn pursed her lips, looking with wary eyes at the door to her balcony. “Lia’where are you?”
