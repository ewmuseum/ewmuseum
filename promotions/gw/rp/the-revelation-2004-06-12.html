The Revelation.

12 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 12th, 2004. The blades of the helicopter whirred about as the force of the powerful engine filled the Dallas air with dust. Inside the chopper, dressed as if he had stepped from the cover of GQ Magazine, sat the Global Wrestling International Champion, 'Superstar' Ken Partridge Jr. While many things seemed to be racing through the mind of the established GW talent, only one seemed to come out in words. 'Josh, man this had better be good.' He sighed to himself in a slight mumble. On the outside he appeared to be calm, cool, and collected. He was dressed up to his usual standards, considering it was 5 o'clock in the morning, decked out in a pair of Versace jeans, a black silk button down shirt with short sleeves, and a hideously expensive pair of black leather Prada shoes. On the inside, however, was a tremendously different story. While on the outside, he looked only as the Superstar could look... stunning, handsome, charming, and well dressed... the reality was that on the inside, he was twisted into a series of emotional knots. Confused, perplexed, thoughtful and even a touch distressed, his mind raced to no end. Why had be been plagued with dreams of Gunner Addison and why couldn't he get her off of his mind? Moreover, why for Christ's sakes had Josh called him at 2 in the morning demanding that he fly to Dallas in such a rush? Josh's reasoning was neither here nor there, as all time seemed to stand still in Ken's ever-rambling mind. He had to face the facts that he was still, and always had been in love with Gunner... a fact that he had been hard-pressed to hide from himself in the past. Unfortunately, though, now there seemed to be more involved. Ken knew that the involvement of Addison in his life again would put her in harm's way of The Upper Echelon. Most notably, The Consiliu... Maverick Reno and John Zero. He knew that the two of them would stop at nothing to harm Ken physically and emotionally... although, he had no idea why... other than the fact that he had taken the International Championship off of Zero's waist and placed ever so elegantly on his. The pilot looked back to Ken, as they were finally about to touch down... 'Where about to land, sir,' he began. 'You should be cleared any minute now.' Ken nodded in accordance and looked back out of the window where he saw Josh waiting on the Helipad with a large smiled etched across his face. 'What the fuck is he so happy about?' Questioned KPJ. ---- Pacing back and forth on the Helipad the thick soles of my black combat boots made a loud noise as I walked about impatiently, as the black chopper closed in on my position. Standing here I felt an array of mixed emotions…Ken would no doubt be overjoyed to hear the reason I dragged him from a comfortable sleep to have him fly to Dallas. Sleep deprived…but overjoyed nonetheless. The helicopter descended slowly casting a large cloud of dust about and sending me into a fit of coughing yet as the dust began to clear I saw the face of Ken. Flashing him a rare smile my body tingled in anticipation as the chopper touched down gracefully on the pavement. Brushing the dust from my sleeveless 'Oxido' shirt and torn Dolce & Gabana jeans I cursed silently at the flying particles of hell as they tickled my allergies. ---- The passenger door to the elegant private helicopter opened as it touched down, allowing Ken to emerge from inside of the steel beast. His eyes immediately met Josh, whose smile still hadn't left his face, which was being covered and uncovered by his shaggy black hair whipping in the tornado of winds that the propellers had intrigued upon arrival. 'Honestly, Josh,' began Ken as the two approached one another, 'you must start thinking about a wardrobe consultant.' 'What's wrong with this?' Questioned Josh with a chuckle, as he knew what Ken's response would be. 'Well... it looks like Raven was having a fucking yard sale,' chimed Ken as he checked his Tag Hauer watch, still groggy from his lack of sleep, 'and you were the only one there.' The blades of the chopper were beginning to slow now as Ken looked back at the taxi that had brought the now car-less Josh to the airport. 'A cab,' said Ken in an unfavorable tone. 'Do you have any better suggestions?' Asked Josh. 'Two... actually,' responded Ken. 'First of all... get a new car, as I know you have the money to do so. And Second...' Ken hadn't finished his sentence as a stretch Hummer limousine pulled up to the chopper deck. 'I figured we'd need one, so I called on the chopper,' explained Ken. Josh simply laughed as the two made their ways to the limo stretched before them. Nodding to the chauffeur Josh stepped inside the limo with Ken in tow as they both comfortably dropped onto the seats and sunk within their cozy confines. 'Now this is the life my friend.' Said Ken as he flashed his million dollar smile. 'Ken…sometimes I think you'd break your tailbone if you ever had to suffer the indignity of sitting down without a foot deep cushion under your ass.' Josh said laughing. 'Oh, that's cute. Did you make that up or read it in a comic book?' Ken retaliated with a grin. 'Yeah, if it wasn't five in the morning I'd have a comeback for that and it would make you go in the corner and cry, kind of like you do when Old Yeller dies at the end of the movie.' Josh said trying to gain the upper hand in their juvenile insult contest. 'Sure, but with all menial banter set aside…why the fuck did you get me to fly to Dallas at 5 in the morning?' Ken demanded twisting his face into a scowl. 'Nice move, you know I had you beat.' Said Josh with a wink. 'But on a serious note…I've got quite an interesting story for you.' ---- It all began on Thursday night… Standing in the gym of the Global arena sweat dripped from my body as the soles of my Nikes beat down on the treadmill impacting the rubber that covered the innards as the speedometer clicked higher and higher. My breath was entering my body in shallow gasps and after what seemed like countless hours on the treadmill a stabbing pain shot through my side, almost as if a knife had ripped through my body. 'Enough…is…enough…' I said in between gasps as I raced forward, set on my unseen destination. Slowing my pace tremendously I came to a halt as I turned the machine down slowly before flipping it off. Such a lovely work out…sweat rolled off of my chiseled body as the perspiration glowed beneath the florescent lights that glared down from the ceiling of this basement workout facility. Dropping to my knees I grabbed both a towel and a drink that were resting next to the treadmill. Bringing the white cloth to my face I dried my face leaving only a clammy feeling. Opening my mouth I squeezed the blue sports bottle and shot a blast of PowerAde into my open mouth gulping it down quickly. Tired yet refreshed I stood, quite satisfied with my workout of two and one half-hours. Turning around I almost tripped over a woman…a beautiful woman. I staggered to the side rather surprised to see the lovely blonde in front of me. Dressed in a pair of black booty shorts with a matching halter-top and Nikes was none other than the lovely Gunner Addison. 'I'm sorry…I thought I was the only one here.' I decried in apology for nearly running her over. It's fine, I didn't mean to startle you.' She said with a laugh. 'It's just…I've been a bit more cautious as of late.' I explained as I brought the towel up and casually hung it about my neck. 'I'm sure you have to be, after all…you’re the World champion therefore you have a target on your back.' Gunner said as I admired her gorgeous figure. 'Right, well, it's not that…I can deal with the pressure of being World Champion, but with TUE attacking me from behind every week I tend to get a bit jumpy.' I stated as my mind began to cloud with thoughts of Maverick Reno and John Zero. 'Yes, well…get used too it, when your on top of the mountain everyone wants to be the one to knock you off…especially TUE, they can't stand someone stealing their spotlight.' Gunner warned as I tried to keep my eyes straight forward so as not to examine her cleavage which peeked ever so slightly from her halter top. 'I'm getting used too it, but this is the first World title I've held and it's an all new experience. I'm grateful to have friends like Ken to mentor me…' I ended my sentence abruptly as I realized that bringing up the name of Ken Partridge Jr. around his ex-girlfriend probably wasn't the best thing to do. She looked down at her shoes…apparently even his name made her rather uncomfortable and suddenly I was uncomfortable as well. With Gunner Addison standing before me and the man who was practically my brother her ex-lover I suddenly felt as awkward as I had when I touched my first female breast…which, contrary to popular belief was not the age of twenty-two. Looking back up and staring directly into my eyes Gunner broke the awkward silence. 'So…how is Ken?' She asked still seeming to have an air of discomfort about her. Pausing to find the right words I thought deeply trying to come back with a witty response that made him look like a Greek god in her eyes…and this is what I came up with. 'He's…good.' I said, suddenly feeling like an idiot for not being able to promote my best friend and boast about him to his ex girlfriend. 'In fact, he's doing very well if I say so myself.' I told her, finally saying something that sounder a little better. 'He's been on a hot streak since he returned. He grabbed the International title and I would even go as far as to say that he's in the prime of his career right now. I've never seen Ken performing so well.' I exclaimed with a broad, albeit, rare smile. 'Yes, I did manage to catch your match at the Pay Per View…you two were on top of your game.' She proclaimed with her teeth shining from behind her dazzling smile. 'Yeah, and best of all…you weren't around to scramble my brains with a chair this time.' I said with a grin, making mention of her interference in the Primestar title match between Ken and I back in the day. She broke into laughter as recollections of that came back to her. 'Yes, I had forgot about that actually.' She said still chuckling. 'Well…I haven't, that hurt!' I moaned, reaching up to feel the back of my head where she had cracked my cranium so long ago. Once again Gunner laughed as she spoke. 'You know Josh, I had forgot just how fun you guys were. In fact…tell Ken to give me a call sometime.' She said with a cute smile. My eyes widened and my heart skipped a beat, I couldn't believe my ears. No way…she didn't just say that? Did she? Did she really ? Yes…yes, she did. 'I'll…I'll pass on the message.' I stammered as I quickly began to back away from her. 'I've really got to run right now though…' I lied. 'Ok, well…don't be a stranger.' She said with a wink and a seductive smile. Without a response I exited the gym…I had to call Ken…he had to get to Dallas now. Right fucking now! ---- 'So,' replied Ken with an anxious look in his eyes and the smile of a five year old on Christmas morning on his face, 'give me the number. I'll call her right now.' 'Her number,' retaliated Josh as Ken was pulling out his cell phone. 'Shit.' 'You're fucking kidding me, Josh,' sighed Ken, lowering his face into his free hand. 'Look man, I was so excited that I forgot to get it,' began Josh, 'but it's not like we don't know where to find her.' Ken raises an eyebrow to Josh's comment waiting on him to finish. 'Look... let's focus on our match right now. Then... after... I say you surprise Gunner at headquarters and go from there.' Josh stated. Ken nodded his head with a smile still emanating on his face, agreeing to Josh's plan. But, how was he to focus on a match against Sage Sabbot and Leinad Soma after receiving the wonderful news that had just graced his ears. His inspiration was no longer just gracing him in his dreams, but before long may be back in his life as well. He tried with all his might not to get to excited, not to get to worked up as he didn't want to be let down to the point that he hit rock bottom again. Still, a certain sparkle had returned to his eye since receiving the words of his best friend... now he only prayed that it all wasn't a dream.
