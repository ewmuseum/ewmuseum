Inevitable Truth

24 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04.24.04 Water was splashing silently in the large black-tiled kidney shaped swimming pool, which 'Superstar' Ken Partridge Jr had been swimming laps in for some time now. His butterfly stroke was near flawless and he would only come up for air once per lap. Darkness had settled in and the end lamp was illuminating the pool nicely. Finally, Ken reached a stop, pulling himself mid-torso out of the water and resting his elbows on the solid ground. 'Man, I don't think I've ever seen anybody do that many laps at once before,' said Ken's younger brother, Daniel, enthusiatically. 'You did it so fast, too.' 'Well, speed's going to be one of my biggest attributes in this match,' huffed Ken, attempting to regain his breath, 'so I've gotta' make sure that I keep it up.' 'Yeah, but you've been going at cardiovascular and speed excersices all day, Ken,' responded Daniel as Ken began pulling himself up out of the pool. 'It wouldn't hurt you to rest some.' 'Daniel, I don't think you understand,' said Ken in a muffled, yet serious tone as he dried his hair and let the towel rest around his shoulders as he sat on one of the white sun chairs he had sitting around the pool. 'You see, Ace Harding, while he's not what he used to be, is one of, if not the most formidable opponents which I've ever stepped foot in that ring with. More than SnapDragon, more than Chris Carson... hell, more than Jay Smash. Harding is a true student of this business. He knows the insides and the outs... which puts him up there with me in that department, and while we're pretty neck-and-neck on the technical end, he's considerably stronger and more powerful than me. That leaves me with my speed... my suddeness.' Daniel sat down in the white chair adjacent to Ken's and tossed a bottle of 'Fiji' water over to Ken, who immediatly spus the top off and began drinking. 'But, Ken, you're better than this guy... that whould be enough, shouldn't it,' asked Daniel. 'How do you think I remain the best Daniel,' responded Ken. 'It's training like this that does that. Now come with me while I run the ropes.' 'Again?' 'Yes, again, and after that I'm doing the boxes before I run the ropes yet again,' said Ken in a mtter-of-factly tone in his voice. 'Boxes?' 'There are four boxes, each different hights. I'll begin with the smallest, which is probably a foot and a half off of the ground and end with the third, which is about three and a half feet high, doing seven jumps a set and seven sets a piece.' 'Then, why the fourth box,' asked Daniel. The two had arrived at the ropes by now and Ken was stretching as he finished explaining one of the most excrutiating and difficult physical exersices to his brother. 'The fourth box is four and a half feet high. I give it one jump... after my legs feel like jelly and they can't take anymore... one jump... to land on top of it with my feet. That's the ultimate for me.' 'Well, I suppose I can't argue with that,' said Daniel with a smile on his face. 'And if you did-' 'I would be over-ruled anyways because I'm the little brother,' interrupted Daniel. 'And don't you forget it,' chuckled Ken as he began running the ropes. The moon was up now and night had set in for the run. The stars watched on with Daniel as Ken continued his training for what may be the biggest match of his life. However, the doubt remained in Ken's mind. Would he be ready? Would he be able to defeat Ace Harding? Would he be prepared... to face his toughest battle?
