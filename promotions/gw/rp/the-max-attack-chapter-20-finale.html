The Max-Attack: Chapter 20 (FINALE!!!!!)

Date: Today The scene opens back up to the now red colored sidewalk with hundreds of people watching Max and The Blue Bat fight. The Blue Bat is still lying on the ground kind of getting his conscious back from the viscous punch given to him by Max. Max starts to undo the blue armored mask, but suddenly The Blue Bat opens his eyes and starts choking Max! “Did you actually think that you were going to take my mask off so you could reveal my face to the whole city of Chicago!” “Well no, actually I was going to take the mask off and kick your ass some more!” “Smart guy, eh?” “Well, yeah.” “I was being sarcastic dumbass.” The Blue Bat rises from the ground now making the choke even more powerful and with both feet jumps into the air and double kicks Max straight in the middle of his stomach. Max falls to the ground as The Blue Bat just leaves his mask wide open with only one part covering his face. Max pretends to be knocked out but starts looking up at the visible part of The Blue Bat’s face. On the visible part Max can see a little black scar on the cheek and something on his forehead. The Blue Bat looks down at Max who barely closes his eyes fast enough so The Blue Bat doesn’t see his eyes open. “So, Max. Have you had enough!” yells The Blue Bat. Max doesn’t answer as The Blue Bat brings Max to his feet. Suddenly from out of no where Max opens his eyes and him and The Blue Bat are staring into each other’s eyes. Suddenly The Blue Bat makes a weird face and starts holding his stomach as he falls to the ground. The scene shifts to Max who is holding a little knife in his hand with blood covering it. Max drops the knife and gets on his knees. Max holds out his hands and starts taking the remains of The Blue Bat’s mask off. The mask fully gets off and the face of The Blue Bat is shown. Max looks at the face and suddenly starts crying. “TYLER!” The hundreds of people start looking at Max like he is a stupid person. Some of the people start crying just because Max is. Suddenly, from the crowd of people a guy walks out of it. He is wearing a kind of black and white suit with a cross necklace around his neck. Max turns around and looks at the man. “Father Clark?” “Yes.” “It’s really you?” asks Max. “Yes Max, it’s me.” “But how did you get here?” “Oh, I can’t tell you everything.” “But, you were in the hospital.” Father ignores what Max said and walks over to the now dead body of The Blue Bat. “Max, what have you done”!” “I killed my best friend!” The hundreds of people look in shock as they start throwing all there garbage at Max. “Go away!” yells Father Clark. The hundreds of people start walking away and soon it is just Max, Father Clark, and the body of Tyler. Max gets off his knees and hugs the body of Tyler. “He’s dead! I killed him!” yells Max. “Oh, he’s not dead Max.” “WHAT!” Father Clark walks over to the body of Tyler and gets to his knees. Father Clark says something in German and then puts his cross necklace on the heart of Tyler. “So, who was this Tyler fellow?” asks Father Clark. “He was the only friend I had in school. When I was 5 to in college. When college ended we just stopped talking to each other. We still sent emails and all that, but never saw each other in person.” Says Max. Father suddenly says something else in German with the cross still on Tyler’s heart and in 1 second Tyler’s eyes suddenly open. “Where am I?” asks Tyler. Tyler looks down at the middle of his stomach and the blood hole is still there. Father takes his cross and puts it back around his neck and then says something in German again. Suddenly the hole fills back up and Tyler isn’t hurt at all. “Max, I’m really sorry about the last couple of weeks.” “But why did you come and attack me all these times?” asks Roswell. “It was my fault. There was this gang kind of group that has about 100 people in it. They captured me and made me The Blue Bat. They went to this place and got all of this armor and made me wear it. Then they sent me after you.” “Do you know what the group was called.” “Something like The Gang from H.E.L.L.” “What does the H.E.L.L mean?” “I think it means The Gang from Hell’s Evil Large Layer. I have no clue. They brainwashed me and now I’m a member.” Tyler shows Max his big finger and there is a weird looking ring on it. It has the letters H.E.L.L on it carved with gold around it. “So, where did they get the armor from?” asks Max. “They didn’t tell me but I was listening to them talking and they said they stole it from some store uptown here in Chicago.” Tyler looks down but doesn’t see the armor on him. There is only black NIKE shorts and a white tanktop. Tyler looks around to see how it could possibly even get off his body. “Max, did you know what happened to the armor?” Max looks at the body of Tyler and doesn’t see the armor on him. Max also looks to see where Father Clark had gone but Father wasn’t there. Max gets up and looks behind a dumpster but still doesn’t see him. “Um, do you know where Father went?” asks Max. “No, I didn’t see him leave.” “Well lets go back to my hotel room.” “Oh yeah, Max I’m really sorry for throwing those little balls at you. The gang stole those from somewhere to.” Max and Tyler get off the ground and start walking back to Max’s hotel room. They walk past a couple stores and malls and then they finally get to the hotel. They walks inside and get to Roswell’s room. It is not blown up and everything is back to normal. People walks up to Max and tell him that they had repaired his room back to normal. “Thank you so much for fixing my room.” Says Max. “Your very welcome.” The people start walking down the long hallway and Max and Tyler walk into the hotel room. They both sit down on the bed and rest from the fight they had. Max gets up and walks over to his dresser. He takes out his suitcase and brings out 2 beers from his suitcase. He throws Tyler one and they both open and drink them. “Tyler. I was thinking. Right after you told me about that gang, I’ve been thinking since we got here. What if you and me, could team up and go after that gang. But not just our self’s. It could be like a team of heroes. I would be The Max-Attack and you could be what?” “Well, first of all. I wouldn’t be The Blue Bat because I hate that name so much. I’d have to think of what my name could be.” “Hey, I have an idea!” “What is it?” “Your name could be Twister. The Max-Attack and Twister?” “Hey man. That’s an awesome name. I think I stick with it. It sounds cool with The Max-Attack and Twister.” “It really does.” “Cool. So from this day on it will be The Max-Attack and Twister fighting the Gang from H.E.L.L. And we start today.” “Tyler, what are you going to wear for you know. The super hero thing.” “I know. Find that armor and just paint it red, and black, cause those were my favorite colors from day one.” “Okay, let’s go find that armor.” Max and Tyler finish the beers and walks out the door of the room. They walk outside and back to where they were fighting. Max and Tyler stop right where they were talking, and start looking around for the armor. “Max. I remember taking the armor off and throwing it in that dumpster. Let’s go see if it’s still there.” Max and Tyler walk to the dumpster and sure enough fin the armor lying there with a whole bunch of junk covering it. Tyler grabs the armor and shakes all the stuff off it. Tyler slips the armor and suit on. Max looks at the back of the dumpster and finds some paint cans! And sure enough they were red and black. Max and Tyler grabs the brushes that were on them and start painting the suit. They throw the paint away as They both get into there suit. Max takes his clothes off to reveal his suit. “Tyler. Since your name is twister, I would say instead of flying you could twist around really fast. Why don’t you try?” asks Max. “Ok.” Tyler jumps into the air and turns around once really slow but all of a sudden starts spinning really fast like a twister. Tyler stops and goes back to the ground. “That kind of makes me dizzy.” Says Tyler. “Let’s start looking for the gang. Do you know where they are?” asks Max. “The main gang is in one of the warehouses here In Chicago. It is a red one with broken windows.” “I know where that one is.” Says Max. “Ok, let’s go!” A Max and Tyler jump into the air as Tyler starts spinning really fast and Max starts flying really fast. They leave the area and a couple seconds later they stop in front of a huge warehouse that is red and has broken windows. “Well, were here. We have to win.” Max and Tyler slowly open the door as it creaks a little bit. They walk inside and out of the sight now being a team, and fighting someone else. SCENE FADES
