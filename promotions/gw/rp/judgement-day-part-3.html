Judgement Day (part 3)

“Hello son.” Chris’s voice isn’t as surprised as Salaok had hoped it to be but never-the-less, now was the time. “Hello father.”It was the first time in a decade that he had said that word. Father? noun: God when considered as the first person in the Trinity (Example: 'Hear our prayers, Heavenly Father') noun: a male parent (also used as a term of address to your father) (Example: 'His father was born in Atlanta') noun: `Father' is a term of address for priests in some churches (especially the Roman Catholic Church or the Orthodox Catholic Church); `Padre' is frequently used in the military noun: a person who holds an important or distinguished position in some organization (Example: 'The tennis fathers ruled in her favor') noun: the head of an organized crime family Despite the use of the word, to Salaok, Chris Davis was none of these, and certainly not a “parent” in his eyes. For a parent is normally someone who helps to raise a child and is there for their child. No, instead Chris Davis wasn’t around for his children. Too busy taking care of business within The Lair. If anything, the word was nothing more than a spiteful way of reminding Chris that it was indeed his own flesh and blood that had come to finally take his life. A life which should have been taken ten years ago, but in this world, late is better than never. Wanting nothing more than to just leap at his father, knocking him to the ground, Salaok restrains himself, choosing to play games with Chris before any of the business has to take place. “Did you really think that by showing your face again that things wouldn’t have changed old man” You’ve been around in the last year, a year of my life which has seen my family be destroyed and I can’t help but think that if you had have been killed when you were meant to have been, that none of this would have happen.” Salaok voice although cold, still oozes with the spite in which he feels for the man who gave him life. His arms folded over his chest as he leans against the wall shows no fear, even though he knows that Chris always carries his gun in the back of his pants. It would have taken little to no effort at all for Chris to pull the gun and to have shot Salaok then and there but it didn’t matter anymore. His wife and unborn child had been killed a year ago. His son Darrell of five years had been murdered brutally just six months later. All of this was to be blamed on his father? Of course. Pushing himself off the wall with his back, Salaok stands upright but still there is no movement from Chris. Neither man had anything to fear anymore and it was clear by the simple fact that neither man was making the first move. However, as soon as Chris sees Salaok move even that small amount, he replies to the comments from his son. “Perhaps your words are true son, but you have no proof that I played a part in any of the deaths in which you have been through.” “I don’t need proof Chris, as I feel it in my heart. Had Quartz killed you when he was meant to, that bastard son of your never would have found out about our side of the family and in turn would have had no reason to have shot Lisha.” Salaok begins to walk towards his father a few small steps before stopping again as soon as Chris begins to answer back. “Mani would have found out about you regardless as if you so remember, he thought that I was still dead when he launched his attack on your family and the Harvesters. Infact, it was after your brother had proclaimed him as part of your family and made him a Harvester as well that things began to turn sour for you if I’m not mistaken. So while you can blame me all you want for Mani and the deaths in which he so caused, perhaps you are looking at the wrong family member that started all of this. You see Salaok, it was Johnathon that told Mani about the happenings and in turn began the downward spiral of your life. The people in whom you had placed so much trust in over your life are the ones to blame for what has happened this last year. And it all began with your close friend, Quartz, not knowing enough loyalty to The Lair, but instead paying loyalty to money instead that caused my life to be extended.” Chris? words are fast and hard, hitting straight at the weakened spot of Salaok’s head as he already had begun to doubt The Lair this morning. Was killing his father the thing that needed to happen, or was it another member of those who had been apart of his life for so long? A rye smile forms on the lips of the old of the two Davis men who stand face to face in a hotel room which would soon see the death of one of them. Which one it would be would be decided in the next few minutes. Confident that his words had gotten at Salaok that Chris begins to back away towards the bar, although never taking his eyes off his son. Not wanting to seem defeated, Salaok follows his father steps, one for one. Never letting the distance between the two of then grow as they moved towards the bar and as soon as Chris? back hits the bar, he stops which brings a grin to Salaok’s otherwise stone face. For Chris had backed himself into a position that wouldn’t allow him to access his gun with great ease and therefore was giving Salaok a second head start on the chance to pull his gun. And pulling his gun was exactly what he did. His arms had been folded over his chest for a reason as his gun holster was inside his jacket and his hand had been resting on it the entire time. While Chris stops at the bar, Salaok continues to move forward and pull his gun. Extending his arm, he was at the perfect distance to push the barrel up under his father’s chin and Salaok can do nothing but shake his head at the foolishness of his father. Visually shaken by what Salaok had just done, Chris? eyes open wide as Salaok presses his face right up into his. “You have grown foolish in your old age father and I can guarantee that the next move you make will be the exact one in which I want you to make. I may not have had anything to do with you for the past ten years, but I still know you father. Know you better than you think I do!” And the move in which Salaok refers to is the same one that Chris begins to make in reaching behind his back which allows Salaok to quickly latch onto that hand and pulls himself around the back of his father, yanking the arm up behind Chris? shoulder blades. Not wanting to cause any bruising, or at least none that would be looked at as evidence, Salaok holds the arm their while putting his gun away and grabbing Chris’s. As fast as he can, Salaok puts his father’s gun on the bar and grabs a hold of the silencer, quickly screwing it on with his one free hand while his father begins to talk again. “That’s actually rather amusing son. You are going to shot me, just like my favored son did you your wife and unborn child a year ago. That say goes, that those who live by the sword, die by the sword, and yet, I’ve never shot a man in my life.” “It’s not the weapon father that is important in that saying, it’s the act of violence.”Salaok responds without really caring as to what is being said. “I know Salaok, and that is why I can’t wait to see your death. How many have you killed” How many lives have you destroyed over the years? Don’t you understand that the death of Lisha, and your children are simply kamah for everything thing that you have done in life? Mani killed your wife because he thought that The Lair had something to do with my death, but now that it is you who kills me, how do you think that will make him feel?” “He will never know it was me Chris.” Salaok again answers back, although this time, there is more attention being paid to what is being said by his father. “You would like to think that wouldn’t you son” However, you know that he is always watching you, always haunting your every move. Take a quick look out the window at the building over the road. There is a single window facing this room and if you look closely, you’ll see your half brother watching you. I saw him enter that building as I was pulling up outside this one.” Despite not wanting to get caught up in his father’s games, Salaok looks over to the building and just as he had said, there was a figure in the window. Although covered in the darkness of the room, it was clear that the figure was watching everything that happened in the hotel room which caused Salaok to move even faster. With the silencer now attached the his father’s gun, Salaok presses it up under Chris? chin, holding the gun upside-down to make sure that the position of the gun was relative to the way someone would have been holding it when shooting themselves. “Son, you don’t have the balls to go through with this.” Chris? voice, still arrogant and trying to use it to effect Salaok but there is no spoken response from Salaok, only the squeezing of the trigger and the lowering of his body. Standing right behind Chris, if Salaok had pulled the trigger he would have shot himself in the face as well so he ducks slightly and moves to the side and that is the last piece of movement that Chris Davis would ever see. The sound of the gun going off under the silencer was nothing more than a soft burst of air as his head jolts back and the back of his head explodes. Fragments of skull and brain fly through the air and all over the bar behind them as Chris’s body goes limp. Letting go of his father’s arm, Salaok lets the corps fall to the ground into a pool of it’s own blood which causes Salaok to move quickly as to avoid standing in any of it. But now the work must begin. Killing his father was only one half of the job done, as the other was making this look like a suicide. Placing the gun in Chris’s hand to get the finger prints in the exact right position of the last hold of the gun was first, and then he picks it back up again, only to drop it. For there was no way that Chris would have been able to hold onto the gun after he had shot himself and therefore it would have been dropped. Next was the alcohol which Salaok prepared by pulling out a small hose from his pocket and leans down over Chris, again careful not to step in any of the blood. Slowly, inch by inch Salaok begins to feed the hose down into Chris? throat, which at first seems like a difficult task as the bullet had passed though the top of the throat on it’s way to the brain and the hose was getting stuck, or going the wrong way. After some slow and painful persistence, the hose finally began to feed down into the stomach which is as far as it was needed to go for now the drinking session could begin. Grabbing a hold of the bottle and taking off the lid, Salaok fits it over the hose and the brilliant malt scotch begins to drain down into the hose and into the now lifeless body of Chris Davis. With just over half of the bottle being drained into the corps, Salaok finally stops and places the bottle back onto the bench before slowly removing the hose and stuffing it back inside him pocket. Clearly his pants would need to be burnt later on that evening, as would the rest of his clothes due to the splattering of bloody from the gunshot. Turning his attention back to the bottle, he pours a small about into the glass and then swirls it around, because anyone who knew Chris Davis, knew that he was refined and never would have drunk out of the bottle, no matter what the situation was. The next thing was his exit and grabbing the keys from Chris’s jacket pocket. A task which was slightly harder than expected as the blood had begun to spread a lot over the floor and mainly that side of the body now. With great care, Salaok bends over the body, holding himself up with one hand on the bar, while with his free hand, reaches down into the pocket and finds the keys, much to his delight. And then, as if nothing had happened, Salaok walks over to the door and locks it, therefore making sure that no visitors were going to be walking in and finding Chris before he wanted him found. No, Chris’s body would need to be found in the morning when the cleaners came by to do their daily jobs only to find that the is room locked. Leaving the keys in the door, Salaok then casually, walks over to the window and leaves the same way in which he left. It was dark now and with Salaok always being dressed in black, it was hard to see him as he moved along the small ledge again to the fire escape but it was once his feet were on that fire escape that he looks up at the window from where he had seen his half brother just minutes before. The room was empty. Knowing that Mani Shigeru was the only man alive other than himself who knew he was in that room tonight would mean that things could now go wrong, terribly wrong. Not wanting to waste time on thought though, Salaok now had to get himself over to the other side of the country as fast as he could. The night was only just beginning and was going to prove to be a long one.
