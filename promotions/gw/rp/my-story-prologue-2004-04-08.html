My Story: Prologue

8 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04.08.04 -10 Man Championship Battle Royal- Featuring: 'Superstar' Ken Partridge Jr, Donny J. McNasty Ely Braxton, Max Roswell, Roz Blaze, 'Ironman' Josh Kosidlo, 'Captain Retirement' Rich Sherwin, Jay Quickness, 'El Mariachi' Juan Gonzalez, and Takahashi Jetsui Ken had a difficult time reading the banner that was up in front of the Global Arena promoting Evolution due to the sun that was beaming in his eyes. However, he was positive that the sign read his name underneath '10 Man Championship Battle Royal.' He was also sure that this was the first that he'd heard of it... and the match was only 2 days away. He made sure to double check, seeing once more that it was in fact his name written directly after 'Featuting.' He wasn't angry so much at the fact that he wouldn't be able to train for the match as much as he normally would... simpply because after looking over the list of names he knew that he really wouldn't need to. Besides, Battle Royales were one of his specialties. He was altogether angry with Christina McCoy, though, simply because of her disliking Josh and him so much that she wasn't even going to bother letting Ken know that not only did he have a match coming, but a title oppotunity at that. Ken stormed away from the sign and down the busy sidewalk where people seemed to just push out of his way as he made his way through them like the Red Sea. Of course, there were plenty of those in awe over the amount of celebrity that was withing touching distance. They looked on as Ken passed in his beige flare jeans by Giorgio Armani and his GW licensed black sleevless shirt with a Superman symbol beginning the word 'Superstar' scrawled across it in silver. The reflections of them bounced off of his Tag Hauer sunglasses just as they bounced off of his ego. He cared nothing for them anymore. They turned their backs on his, so he could sure as hell return the favor back to them. 'How did HE get here,' a small child questioned his parent as KPJ walked past. This question... this innocent ponderation of truth sent Ken mind into an overloaded factory of curiosity. How DID I get here? How long will I be able to stay here? His mind began shuffling through a kalidoscope of images of Ken's past. Ex-lovers, former friends, and deceased relatives all flashed before his eyes before it all stopped and all his mind's eye was seeing was the grandfather that Ken recently lost to cancer. 'Remember one thing,' advised Ken's grandfather, Lamaar, 'A man without fear... is a man without hope.' How true.
