Hatred

7 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-7-04 This post is meant to take place before Evolution on 2-7-04. There she stands, maybe seven feet way, dancing as though no one else is around. There is something about her? maybe the sweat dripping down her face or the red streaks in her hair that makes her so appealing. There she is, this dark queen of the dance floor, completely oblivious to the men who watch her. Is it a lack of interest or a surplus of vanity that causes this pulsing form to disregard her admirers? Her skirt moves in black waves around her feet as she spins in rhythm, her arms stretched above her head. Black armbands shield her pale flesh; a black and blue bodice covers her torso, perfectly accentuating her next to perfect form. The men stare, amazed by her figure, mesmerized by her face. She could care less, however, for none of these perverted men fills her need or desire. Then she arrives? the black goddess of the winter night. She steps through the iron cast gate and into the warehouse of flashing lights and deafening music. Her brown orbs look the place over; her blackened lips curl into a twisted smile. This is home. Her blackened eyes close for a moment as she breathes in the darkness and pain of this world. When they open once again, they fall upon the screaming, writhing crowd. Another smile places itself on her face and she moves forward just as the music picks up a new beat. Her long raven hair is twisted into a tight ponytail resting on top of her head. A black corset holds her toned physique while a long black skirt adds a touch of feminine elegance. Men stare, but she pays them no attention. Her mind is on other things and each of them possesses some feature that reminds her of? him. Slowly, her hand falls to her side and she feels at the waist of her skirt. She smiles in comfort as she wraps her hand around the handle of a dagger that is tucked inside her skirt. She finds herself lost in the crowd. Dancing to the next song, the woman runs a hand through her auburn hair, the red streaks mixing into each other in a frenzy of red and brown. With one swift movement, she wipes the sweat from her brow, still entranced by the music. Her hair falls in a mess of wavy strands around her face and then falls to her shoulders in waves of brown. Silently, she turns, ignoring the boys behind her. It’s the same deal every night: horny boys and poser chicks. Then her eyes open and she is thrust into an entirely new situation. Standing before her is an exotic beauty, an irresistible force of womanhood. The dancing stops and now she stands, mesmerized, just like her male counterparts. Who is this woman that has stolen her attention and wrapped it around her heavenly, toned body? Whoever she is, she has her attention and her mysterious stare has made her the most desirable person in the room. A man bumps into her and she turns to sneer. The man recoils, fear stabbing at his heart like a razor. Her brown eyes scan him over, finds some intrigue, and then looks away, deciding him unworthy. The air is heavy and the metallic smell of sweat hangs in the atmosphere; she does not know whether to find this exciting or repulsive. Her neck goes to one side, as the sound of dozens of bones cracking sounds through her head. As her head snaps back into place, she sees the girl, maybe seven feet away, dancing wildly to the beat of the night. She finds herself entranced by the girl’s unique way of dance. She reminds her of an African princess or perhaps a gypsy, floating and flowing around the dance floor, black cloth floating around her like angry black clouds. Then, as though she suddenly felt her stare, she stops and the girl allows her gaze to fall upon her. Their eyes met and she knew that this girl was hers. A crooked smile creeps across her lips as she quickly and quietly approaches the girl. The black beauty finds herself face to face with this queen of the dance floor, her brown orbs almost hypnotizing the innocent, na’ve child. She takes one finger and places it at the bottom of the girl’s bodice, and while still looking at the girl, she slowly brings it up the bodice and to her chin. The girl gulps silently, still intrigued yet oddly afraid. Once she finds herself following the woman out, the girl knows it is too late. She’s in for it big tonight. ~*~*~*~ And thus Aradia found herself at the side of the girl’s bed, somewhere deep in the city. Half naked and shivering, she looks around herself intensely. Memories flash before her eyes, a night full of passion and pleasure with the beautiful girl above her, sleeping silently. The way the girl looked into her eyes was beautiful, but now the thought strikes grief and hatred into Aradia’s heart. It had been months since she last found herself under the sheets with another woman, and while she enjoys it, something about it always brings pain to her heart. Once the night is over, she finds herself shrouded in darkness. Perhaps the result of her unknown happiness with women as opposed to men or maybe women were her true calling. Whatever the case, the pain she feels now is unbearable. How can such a beautiful creature lay above her, not knowing this pain, not understanding it? How can she be na’ve to the rest of the world? How can she get away with it? Still partially nude, Aradia rises and stands over the bed, looking at the girl, content in slumber. She tilts her head, studying the girl’s naked form, only partially covered by the white sheet. So content is this girl in her happiness and slumber, that she has no idea the pain that is surrounding her. She will probably awake in the morning and still not feel the pain. She can’t get away with it. Before she has a chance to think her actions through, Aradia pounces on the bed and grabs a handful of the girl’s perfect hair. Jolted awake, the girl’s eyes widen and she lets out a scream. Aradia covers the girl’s mouth with her fee hand and then continues to beat the girl’s face with the other. Muffled screams mix with the crimson liquid that begins to fall down the girl’s once beautiful face. They enter the room, silently and awkwardly. After dropping their coats to the floor, they face each other and fall into a passionate embrace followed by a long, exaggerated kiss. The dark beauty throws the girl to the bed and gets on top of her and begins to kiss her again, as she slowly undresses the girl. Her tongue begins to lick down the girl’s face and neck, as the girl turns to shut the lamp off. Aradia looks down at the girl, now covered in blood. The memory of their evening angers her more and she reaches at the waist of her skirt. Her hand falls to the warm shaft of her dagger and she slides it out swiftly and raises it over her head. She stares down at the girl again, seeing the rivers of blood that pour into the crimson basin around her head. The child’s red streaks are now indistinguishable from her own blood, wildly splattered in her hair. Her near-lifeless face looks back at Aradia, and for the first time in days, a stabbing feeling preys at Aradia’s conscious. Suddenly overtaken by pain and guilt, Aradia looks up at the dagger raised above her head. Shrieking in her own terror, Aradia drops the dagger and falls from the bed. The girl groans in a raspy, barely audible voice as she tries to watch Aradia. Taken back by her own senseless actions, Aradia quickly springs to her feet. She retrieves the dagger from the bed and slides it back into the waist of her skirt. The black beauty then rushes to the doorway, grabbing her corset and coat. In one swift motion she puts her coat on to cover her bare torso. Tears stream down her pale face as she heads for the door, completely taken over by guilt and shame. As she exits, she stops in the doorframe and looks back at the girl. An apology lingers on her lips, but never makes it past her tongue. A scowl replaces her apologetic look and she quickly turns and runs from the room, pain and darkness following her down the hall. The girl lets out a cough and one last moan. The room grows darker and becomes deadly silent.
