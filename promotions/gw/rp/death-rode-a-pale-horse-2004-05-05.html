Death Rode A Pale Horse...

5 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-5-04 The sound of horses fills the air. Soft hoof beats and slight whinnies mingled with the soft snuffling sounds that horses are prone to make. As the shot comes into focus, we are met with a view of an elaborate stable. Filled with horses, the place exudes an aura of peacefulness. Hay litters the floor, and a smallish man clad in an old fashioned leather jerkin rolls a wooden barrow filled with fresh hay first to one stall, then to another. He tosses some in to each horse; spotted roans, grays, appaloosas. All the while he hums a merry sounding tune to himself and occasionally mutters some words like “thee’s a good horse, thy’s a good lass’All the King’s Horses”..?and the like. At last he comes to the last horse who has been patiently waiting his turn at the straw from the barrow. Man: and how are ye, my lad? Horse:”. The horse, a huge and beautiful white stallion, stamps his front hooves and nods his head, urging the fellow to hurry the hell up?.Hey, horses can be impatient too. HALT!!! The man cringes as if he has been caught doing something he perhaps should not have been doing. The shot switches to the door and in walks our hero, Venneman Pendragon. He wears a billowing, open throated white shirt and black leather pants. His sword is sheathed and belted to his waist. He walks proudly, head up, eyes burning towards the small man and the very large horse who looks impatient as ever? Man: I..I’I’m sorry me lord?. Pendragon looks at the man and smiles. He lets out a small chuckle and the man calms considerably? Pendragon: Nothing to be sorry for, my good Joseph’I merely wish to feed Xerxes this morn’We have a bit to discuss? Joseph: Aye’No problem me lord?. Joseph backs away from Pendragon, bowing and touching the back of his hand to his forehead all the way out the doors of the stables. Pendragon turns to the horse, and the horse looks at the hay. Pendragon smiles and begins feeding the massive mount from his own hands? Pendragon: Aye Xerxes’the time of battle has come round once more. Xerxes munches contentedly on the hay, but his eyes are on the face of the Lord Pendragon? Pendragon: GW’fate has decreed my return to this place. And what is GW? Once a flagship federation, now the powers that be fight for each viewer they can muster. Perhaps that is why Pendragon has heeded the call once more. Merciful I have shown myself to be, and fate places me in GW’And what thanks is rewarded mine greatness? Trapped in an under card matchup against Mike Storm? Xerxes nudges Pendragon and gives him a “Hurry up with the grub” look that makes Venneman smile. His blue eyes still burn as he thinks out loud to what may be his only true friend? Pendragon: Settle down ye beast, ye?.Patience is a virtue best learned slowly. Too long though I have been patient. Fate has sat me at the edge of glory for too long this time. I have come to save this rabble that calls itself GW, and they lock me into an under card in an undershow against a half man??Tis ill tidings indeed that these events should bring home to me? ***********FLASH********* Pendragon stands center ring, throngs of fans screaming his name. In his right hand he holds the famed sword Excalibur high, in his left, the prestigious PCW Television Championship. A triumphant look is upon his face as the scene cuts back to the serenity of the barn?. ************FLASH********** Pendragon stands with his head lowered whilst Xerxes munches on some of the hay he has been given. Pendragon: Each place to which I find myself sent, I tear through like a storm’The trail of the defeated grows longer and longer by mine feet’And yet, the foolish never learn. I am always started with the weakest?.Those who would be most prone to be trampled by lesser men; these are expected to stand up to the might of the heirs of the Kings of old? Nonsense?. *********FLASH******** Pendragon stands center ring, a prone body draped over his shoulders. He seems hardly even to have broken a sweat in whatever constituted the match up to this point. The crowd roars its approval as he drops his opponent onto his face with the Last Crusade?. ********FLASH********* Pendragon: But the money does roll in, does it not? Xerxes nods, almost as if in understanding of the great man kneeled before him’Pendragon stands, stroking the muzzle of the beautiful horse?. Pendragon: Glory and honor’And time. All that men can do is use the time that is allotted them wisely. That is the truest measure of a man. Actions define the man’not the other way “round. And actions start here and now once more’In a federation of young brigands, punks, and half men, a MAN has been unleashed. He draws his sword and lays the blade flat upon his palm. He makes a quick slash and rubs the blood that flows from his hand along the gleaming blade. At the touch of the blood, the sword gleams brighter”. Pendragon: By blood oath I swear it; this place shall not stand against Pendragon. The ancient name of glory shall prevail against the murderers?.the criminals’the unseemly Knaves of the federation?. “They say that death rode a pale horse, and that he brought hell along with him’So too shall it be in GW’Pendragon was death on the pale horse, and hell would be his gift”..” ~FINE~
