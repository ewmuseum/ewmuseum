Looking back at past championships... (EVO#1)

19 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/19/04 The night before Evolution, and one day short of two weeks since the Iron Circle Match, Donny is finally at home by himself, as Alexis is in Dallas getting ready for tomorrows Evolution on HBO. Donny is already mentally and somewhat physically healed for him and Brick's shot at one of the hottest tag teams in GW, The Flying Vergomovs. Donny is taking this time to let it sink in that he's once again a champion. Donny looks at his library of tapes and see's the tape labeled, 1st Hardcore Championship Win... He turns and pops it into his VCR and hits play. It was his match against the man known as Junkyard Doug back in the SWF. It was January of 2002, and he had only been in the SWF for 1 month and he was already a champion. It was then that Donny began to rewrite history and become who he is today, 'The King of Hardcore'. His first reign didn't last that long, a man by the name of Reverend Johnny Love defeated him after holding the belt for only two months. The loss was devastating to Donny, his self esteem ran low, and his confidence had hit rock bottom. It was then that he began his close friendship and now business partner with Harv, who later was nicknamed Hardcore Harv, by Donny. Harv instilled into him the drive to regain that Hardcore Title belt and to show the world who he really was. It wasn't much longer that Donny regained his title, he went on an even longer title run, 6 months. Until he lost it to a man by the name of Jus. Jus and Donny fought in what was considered one of the greatest matches of all time in SWF. Donny was stripped of his title when the owners of SWF temporarily shut it down. They reopened and within a few months Donny reclaimed his Hardcore title and it became written in stone that The Nomad Donny J McNasty was the King of Hardcore. Even while holding the Hardcore Title, Donny became a Tag Team Champion as well, being of the only two title holders in the SWF's history. But as it happens most of the time, before Donny could move on to win any other titles in SWF, management decided to close it up and collect their money. Leaving Donny a still remaining champion of a company that will eventually be forgotten in the minds of most. Donny takes out the tape in the VCR and finds another tape and it's labeled, Whiplash 04. Donny pops in the tape of his Platinum Title match with Train. This was the third meeting between these two at a PPV, and Donny finally had the last word in their saga. Donny knocked Train out in the Iron Circle on such a rainy, soggy night in Dallas. When Donny first touched the Platinum title that was his, the tears flowed out of him like the rain that has fallen from the sky. Alexis ran over to him to bask in the moment with the man she loves so dearly. This is what Donny has been working so hard for since the day he entered GW back in Febuary. He's had title shots before and this was his time to finaly win one. As Donny watches this tape, as he see's himself tear up, he begins to well up in tears once again. He shuts the tape off though before the vicious attack of Train. What Donny realizes is that he is a marked man, and that his title defense will be harder than winning it. Donny knows he can do it, and he's determined to be the best Platinum Title holder of all time...
