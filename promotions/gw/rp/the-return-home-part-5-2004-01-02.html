The Return Home (Part 5)

2 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-2-04 Ichiro woke up as the sunlight beamed through his small bedroom window into his eyes. He stood and stretched, it was going to be a long day. But, one that he hoped might benefit his brother and himself. Their families name had been soiled and Ichiro would not stand for it. He had his visions planned out in his mind, first he would take care of his family reputation in Tokyo, then soon after it would be back to the United States, to regain the reputation of the Souls of the Black Lotus in Global Wrestling. But all that was in the future, all he needed to worry about was right now. Ichiro walked into the bathroom, he emerged several minutes later wearing only a bath towel. He quickly walked into his room and got dressed for the occasion. He wore his white business suit, it was traditional for him to wear the white, while Jushin would more than likely be wearing the black suit. Ichiro quickly got dressed and made sure he wore the specialized holster. He took a look at the small alarm clock on his dresser as he was dressing. The crystal, red numbers read 11:20 a.m. Excellent, he had to meet Jushin outside the door in ten minutes. He began to check all of the six pistols that were now lying on his bed. He checked the trigger, made sure the safety worked, he felt the grips, he inspected the guns until they could not be inspected any longer. He was satisfied that all of them worked and moved on to checking the clips of ammunition. Finally five minutes later he was satisfied with the guns and all of the ammunition. He tucked the clips into the specialized holding places on the belt part of the brown, leather holster. His normal ammunition into the right side and the carbonized ammunition on the left. Next he went about loading the six guns into the holster, he put his personal favorite customized pistols into the side holsters. They hung tightly, right above his belt line, hovering over his pockets. The first pair of the prototype guns that Yoshahiro had given them the day before went behind his back. They also were just above his belt line, but hung snugly enough so that the back of his business jacket would cover them. The third set of guns, went directly underneath of his arm pits, they hung downwards to avoid discomfort, but still it took some getting used to. He made sure everything was securely fastened before grabbing his white business suit jacket off of the bed and throwing it over his shoulders to conceal the guns. He glanced at the clock one more time, 11:33 a.m. He was late, but then again Jushin probably wouldn’t be ready for at least another ten minutes. He didn’t care though, he walked outside the room and checked the rest of his apartment. He grabbed his keys off of his countertop and walked outside, locking the door behind him. Surprisingly when he got outside Jushin was already waiting there impatiently. “Your late, I’ve been waiting for ten minutes.” “Welcome to my world,” answered Ichiro. Jushin flashed a smile and the two brothers began to walk down the hallway. They were soon in the underground parking garage and slightly less than an hour later they pulled into their uncle’s driveway once again. Their uncle smiled and led them to his garage. The brothers pulled the Mitsubishi inside and left it in park, they hopped out and walked over to their uncle’s black SUV. Soon after the three men were in motion. Jushin sat in the passenger window, with Ichiro in the back and Gohei driving the SUV. The weight of the mission seemed to linger in the air. The conversation was minimal and the silence staggering. The three men kept to themselves they all had rituals but none of them consisted of making conversation. They were getting focused. This surely was a mission among kamikaze status. The plan was not complicated or elegant, a basic scoot and shoot through Ishuzu’s Warehouse Complex. The plan did not allow for much error either, any simple slips or merely taking too long to clear out a room would lead to Ishuzu being warned and quite possibly her escape. This was a risk that the brothers were not willing to take, they wanted to crush Ishuzu and they were going to do it tonight. Through the darkness the headlights of the Black SUV cut like a knife through butter. Ichiro sat in the backseat. His eyes were closed and his body still, but by no means was he asleep. He was concentrating, his mind lost in a deep meditative state. His muscles relaxed he merely sat in nullity, his mind focused on nothing but the task at hand. He ran the thoughts through his head, how they could possibly accomplish this mission, the rest of the real world was but a shroud of darkness. Jushin sat and thought about the world as he knew it. He remembered those he loved, he remembered those he had lost, and he focused mainly on why they were doing this mission. Their name would not be soiled, after tonight no one will ever dare make an attempt on the name of Nakazura. After tonight, the Nakazura dynasty will begin a new chapter, in it’s legacy of eternity. Gohei was not at all prepared for the mission. He didn’t know what he had gotten himself into, but he did trust the Souls of the Black Lotus. There was a reason why they had replaced him upon their arrival, and there was a very good reason as to why they were two of the most feared faces in Tokyo. They were dealers of death, bringers of retribution, the sight of their face to those who had done wrong was a sure sign of the end. For they moved as shadows, invisible to those except those who had a reason to fear them. The black SUV pulled into the small parking complex and the sun was still high in the sky. It was time to wait and observe. Ichiro stepped out of the SUV which was hidden from plain view of the complexes security offices on the top floor. He watched as Jushin began to ascend the small, rickety, metal ladder at the side of the building, his binoculars in tow. He had to scout the place for snipers and would eventually have to commence the mission with his own sniper rifle from the same spot. Gohei got out and began to assemble the small pieces of plastic explosives that the three men would need to bypass some of the security features in the complex. It was all in order to protect Ishuzu, she would be watching over her operation closely now. The Souls had been quiet, but surely the recent events since they returned to Tokyo would have reached her ears by now. Her export of ammunition and artillery was running smoothly. She did not have a care in the world, until she caught wind that the Souls of the Black Lotus had returned to Tokyo. For they were the sons of the man she had slaughtered. She had eliminated her only competition in arms dealing but she had also brought upon her doom. Ichiro quickly and quietly began to shuffle around the smaller of the buildings. He scouted the area for any scouts or any signs of patrol. There did not appear to be any outside patrol, which was good. The only fire they would meet would come from inside the building. He returned an hour later after taking several tours of the outer premises. Jushin was assembling his sniper rifle in preparation for his return to the roof. Gohei was checking the explosives and beginning to package them so they could be carried. Ichiro smiled as the sun was beginning to fall from the sky. Many moons had passed since the rise of the red sun after their father’s death. Tomorrow a red sun would rise again, for death would surely be spilt this night. Ichiro looked at the three story warehouse complex that they would soon infiltrate. He looked through the windows in the top high security sector where Ishuzu’s office was located. He smiled as he glared into the window. “Soon thy retribution shall be delivered.”
