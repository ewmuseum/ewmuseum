Late Night with Conan O' Brien

28 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/28/04 (The scene fades in to the set of Late Night with Conan O'Brien as Max Weinberg and the Max Weinberg 7 play the show back from commercial. The camera zooms in on Conan.) Conan: Welcome back. Our next guest is one of the toughest and most popular superstars in Global Wrestling. You can see him every Monday night on Global Wrestling Evolution. This Memorial Day, he'll be a part of the main event at Global Wrestling's pay-per-view event High Noon. Please welcome Brick! (The audience applauses as Brick, dressed in a red and black warmup suit, makes his entrance. He walks over to Conan who gives him a handshake before Brick walks over to sit down.) Conan: Welcome to the show. Brick: Thanks for having me. Conan: First, tell me about the main event. Now, originally, it was supposed to be a different match in the main event, altogether, right? They just changed it. Brick: Yeah, originally it was supposed to be a six man tag team match between two of the factions in GW: the Upper Echelon and M.O.B. But then the fans spoke and Christina McCoy, the company's acting president, decided to move the triple threat match between me, Train and the current Platinum champion Deacon Holmes. Conan: That says a lot about the kind of fans you have. Brick: Yeah, they're wonderful. Conan: You know, my fans wanted NBC to move this show to an earlier time slot. Brick: Really? Conan: Yeah, they were both disappointed. (The audience laughs slightly. Conan gets a deadpan look on his face, and the audience laughs again.) Brick: Heh. Conan: And the Platinum title isn't even the top title in the company. Brick: No, it's the third highest title in the company, but the heat between the three of us was just that high. There's lots of rivalries in GW, but ours is definitely the hottest. We just plain don't like each other. That's a fact. Conan: I like that you say that so bluntly. Brick: I'm a very blunt individual. Conan: Now where did the name 'Brick' come from? Brick: It comes from my childhood. When I was born, I weighed, like, 9 lbs., 15 oz. Conan: Wait, you weighed 9 lbs 15 0z.? My God, and your mother's still alive?! Brick: Yeah. So, my uncles started calling me 'Brick'. Conan: OK, we have a clip of one of your matches. Can you set this up? Brick: Yeah, I think this is from the last pay-per-view, Wrestlecade 4. Conan: OK, let's take a look. (Fade to black. Fade in to footage from the triple cage match at Wrestlecade 4.) Brick looks at the two downed bodies and he climbs the first three rungs of the ladder and connects with an elbow smash onto Train! He pulls Train up and goes for an Overhead belly-to-belly suplex, but Trian counters the move and nails a chin breaker. Deacon rolls around and catches the attention of Train. He pulls Deacon Holmes up and rushes him into the side of the cage, it shakes violently. Train smiles as both men lay there. He starts climbing the cage without the assistance of the ladder. Brick is the first one to his feet, he starts climbing up the ladder heading to the cage. Deacon rolls over and looks up at both men that are now on top of the cage going at it...left...right...left...right...Brick blocks an attempted left hook from Train and nails him in the gut with a stiff kick...IMPLANT DDT! (Fade back to Conan and Brick.) Conan: Folks, Global Wrestling High Noon airs this Memorial Day on pay-per-view. Call your cable or satellite provider. Brick, ladies and gentlemen. (The audience applauds.) Conan: We're gonna take a break. When we come back, Velvet Revolver is gonna be here. We'll be right back. (As the band plays, the screen fades to black.)
