That night

23 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/23/04 It was late on a Tuesday evening. In fact, it was July 13th, one day after the July 12th edition of Evolution. Brick was in his hotel room, listening to the second disk of Prince's 'Sign 'O' the Times' album. The song that was currently playing was 'I Could Never Take the Place of Your Man'. He sat on the couch, wearing nothing but a pair of gray sweatpants, singing along. 'And I said 'Baby, don't waste your time! I know what's on your mind I may be qualified for a one-night stand But I could never take the place of your man.? Suddenly, he heard a knock at the door. The sound woke him up some, if not promptly. 'Who the hell is this?' he grumbled as he rose off the couch and headed to the door. Halfway to the door, there was another knock. 'Aight, aight!' Brick looked through the peephole and saw something he couldn't believe. 'What?' When he opened the door, who should he see standing there but Rhaiza, Deacon Holmes' girlfriend and number one fan? There she stood, wearing a black trench coat and some open-toed shoes. The way she clutched her coat, Brick wondered if that was all she was wearing. 'What are you doing here?' 'Can I come in?' she asked. Brick looked past her and down the hall, both ways. 'Where's your man? And I use that term, loosely.' 'Deacon? I left him at our hotel room. He's still sleeping off the beating your buddy Braxton gave him.' 'So why aren't you with him? I figured you'd be tending to him, hand and foot.' 'I needed some fresh air, among other things.' 'You expect me to believe you're here alone and Deacon's not here anywhere with you?' 'He's not.' 'Well, he must have put you up to coming here.' 'May I come in before somebody sees us?' To say the least, Brick was intrigued, but at the same time, he was wary. 'Yeah, sure, come in.' As she walked into his room, he never took his eyes off her. 'So, what brings you here?' 'What do you mean?' 'I mean, you're Deacon's lady. You have to admit, given the beef between him and me, I have a right to be suspicious 'I know this is gonna sound strange coming from me, but Deacon had that beating coming.' Brick raised an eyebrow at this. 'Really?' 'Really.' 'That's kinda odd to hear, coming from you. I figured you might have come here to bitch at me for Ely whuppin' his ass.' 'I was upset, but then I thought about it and sometimes Deacon opens his mouth before thinking.' 'Can't argue that.' 'Brick, I know you look at me and most of what you see is Deacon's devoted girlfriend. But that's not all there is to me. I do have a mind of my own. And I know I've been getting tired of this need he's gotten lately to mess with Gunner Addison.' 'You wouldn't be the only one. I know Ely's looking to kill him.' 'And, Brick,' she said, as she sauntered towards him, 'I've gotten tired of Deacon acting like a little boy.' She then leaned closer towards Brick. 'I need a man.' By that time, the track switched to 'Adore.' 'Whoa, hold up, hold up!' said Brick, walking away from Rhaiza. 'Deacon hates me more than any cat in the whole damn company, and now his girlfriend is trying to push up on me?' 'Well, to be honest,' she purred as she walked over to him, undoing the belt on her trenchcoat, 'part of me has always been just a little bit curious about you, and deep down, I think part of you has been curious about me.' Her coat opened to reveal that she only had on a black bra and black panties underneath. 'Well, I say we satisfy each other's curiosities. Besides, I know you're thinking of a way to get back at Deacon. How about I just save you the trouble of thinking?' Before Brick could protest, Rhaiza had her hand down his sweatpants. 'You...do raise an...interesting proposition.' 'I thought you'd see thing my way.' And then Rhaiza licked Brick's lips. Not even a second after, they began to kiss. And the rest is history. At least, according to Brick.
