Wikkid Dreams

20 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wikkid Dreams Kid Wikkid Global Wrestling Promo #774 Date: 1-20-04 Location: Corpus Christie, TX 'It's dark. The entire world seems to be off as if someone pulled the plug. The blackness is so thick that, strain as you might, you can't see past your nose. Without warning, you feel like you're falling. You're falling so fast that you must certainly feel the impact from the bottom of the abyss any second. That impact never comes though. You must be dead. That is the only explanation for this feeling. Suddenly, there is something in the back of your mind that piques your interest. A slight hum rises up in your ears. It seems to get louder, as if a bee was flying closer and closer to your head and threatening to fly into your ear. Then the voices come, many many voices whose words are still indistinguishable. Then there are one or two voices that begin to make sense. A question about your senses is posed, but you can't answer. It's not that you don't want to answer, but you can't. For some reason, your mouth doesn't want to move and the voices keep getting louder. The only thing you want is to be able to shout out and make them stop. Then come the other sounds. The beeping and the whooshing and in the distance, the crying of babes. It's so totally strange to you that the only thing you want is that sweet dark silence to envelope you. Then you feel it. The dull throbbing in your head that threatens to drum your brain clean out your ears. The constant feeling of knives jabbing into your neck. The pain echoing through each and every muscle you have that seems to make even the noise tolerable. Every moment seems to get worse. It's only then that you realize that you’re not dead. Dead men feel no pain. I have lived by those words for most of my life and now they are confirming my worst nightmares . . .. I lost.' The darkness is replaced by light and a man props himself on one elbow on a couch in what appears to be his apartment. There is a washcloth over his eyes and he moves his free hand to hold it in place. Another man sits a few feet away on another overstuffed couch. He is flipping through channels on the TV and finally rests on the repeat telecast of Evolution. The show is just beginning and the match of Kid Wikkid v. Terelle Green is just getting underway. The man with the cloth on his eyes flops back down on the couch and sighs. His friend shakes his head. 'Jesus H. Christ, Wikkid, you lost one freaking match. It's not the end of the world.' The prone man merely extends his middle finger to his buddy, who shuts of the TV. 'Jake, shut up. You know damn well that when I was doing the Brawls, I lost only one or two matches and those were only time limit decisions. That bastard Green beat the piss out of me.' 'You do realize that he was bigger than you all around, right Marc.' 'That's not the point, Jake. I have never hurt like this before. I've fought with guys that are bigger than me. I've never lost because of my size. If anything it made me fight harder. I don't know what happened; maybe I'm losing my edge. Maybe I should do something different with my life.' Jake shakes his head and stands up. He tosses the remote control at Wikkid, who is still on his back with the rag over his eyes. The remote falls perfectly to hit Wikkid in the junk, eliciting a grunt from the man. Jake walks toward the door of the apartment and unlocks it. As he begins to walk out the door he glances at his friend over his shoulder. 'Do me a favor and call me when you stop feeling sorry for yourself. It was just one match. It was a fluke. Things are different now that you are a member of the Global roster. You're not just gonna be fighting bouncers in a glorified bar fight. You are gonna be fighting seasoned men with something to prove. But you've got something they don't Marcus. There's something about you that is gonna put you to the top of the heap. Wikkid Dreams, brother.' With that, Jake makes his way out the door and closes it behind him. Wikkid continues to lie on the couch, listening to the commentary by Brandi Sights and Mark Bishop. At this point they are going nuts about 'The Don' making his appearance and how medics are tending to the unconscious rookie. Wikkid shuts off the TV blindly and rolls to face the back of the couch. 'Yeah, nothing more special than a kid who can take a beating. Wikkid Dreams, my ass.' The screen cuts to blackness.
