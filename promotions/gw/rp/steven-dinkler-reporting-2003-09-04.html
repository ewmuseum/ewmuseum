Steven Dinkler reporting.

4 September 2003

Date: 9/4 The silence of a quiet back road flanked on each side by nothing more than dirt, rocks, and the occasional patch of sun beaten grass, is quickly cut by the resounding roar of a ten cylinder five hundred and fifty horsepower engine propelling a black Porsche Carerra GT Turbo around a succession of winding turns at blazing speeds upwards of one hundred and twenty miles per hour. Small rocks along with various other road debris fly up into the air as the Porsche’s low profile racing tires grip the road and send loose gravel flying as the black blur hugs the curves. A large rooster tail of coarse sand forms behind the car’s rear tires as the vehicle pulls into a straight away after traversing the road’s razor sharp curves. The car’s windows which have been severely darkened by their tint, making them the same color as the car, obscure the magnificent machine’s driver, Deacon Holmes, from view. Although, at such speeds it would be hard to make out much of anything in the way of details about the car. To the odd motorist occupying the road nothing much more than a black flash and a hail of sand is seen as they are systematically caught up to, cut off, and then finally lost in a cloud of dust while Deacon Holmes speeds away. Over the smooth purr of the engine, loud, thumping, heart stopping, ground shaking bass can be heard along with the lyrics of “Warrant” by Bubba Sparxxx. Inside the car Deacon sings along while contemplating the next edition of Evolution in which he has a shot to become the Global Wrestling World Champion. ?It’s strictly for survival this time... Still don’t want what’s yours unless it’s rightfully mine. It really just ain’t no words, at least that I can define... For how I feel, lord please give some sight to tha blind. Cause I’m scramblin? down tha same road, travelin? in tha same clothes Feelin two-million days old, can’t let all this take hold... Cause see I always say myself as just a little more special than... These sappy, crappy, non-rappin?, whoever’s collectin? fans. I’m doin tha? best I can, I just need a better plan. I still ain’t at peace with flyin?, cause my plane has yet to land. I’m runnin? and runnin?, my stomach’s grumblin?, but I ain’t... Hungry or nothin?, I’m just disgusted because I can’t... Ever make what I think, translate into what I say... Unless I pour this vodka in that big cup and chug away. I wanna just run away, from what I’ve become today... A rebel with a warrant in his name that doubles twice every day.? Holmes downshifts coming out of yet another turn causing the car to lunge forward while quickly picking up speed. As the revolutions per minute grow and the acceleration slows, Deacon shifts once more, allowing the transmission to catch back up with the engine as he leans back into the butter soft leather seats with a satisfied smile on his face. He loves pushing this car to the limit, almost as much as he enjoys pushing himself to new limits. Since the beginning of his short career Deacon Holmes has been pushing the envelope and as well as himself, with over the top death defying maneuvers that dazzle the eye and boggle the mind, all the while filling seats in the process. Each week Deacon raised the bar for in ring insanity with his daring display of aerial prowess accented only by the innovative use of a chair at random intervals carried him to several victories but did not allow him the freedoms he enjoys today. Those came with fame. While sporting quite an impressive record for a rookie, Deacon Holmes still lived in relative anonymity. Until one day it happened. Global Wrestling took a slight interest in him and allowed Holmes to appear on it’s weekly review show; Overload, which is currently on hiatus, and made the mistake of allowing him to open his mouth on national television. At first it started out as just random small talk between he and she show’s host, Trista Romano, but that escalated into an impromptu interview that perked up a lot of ears... and propelled him to super stardom. When asked what he thought about being bestowed such titles as “The Next Brick” and “Brick Jr.” he quickly bashed the now current World Champion, sparking the flames to a now blazing rivalry between the two. He then continued on to admonish half the roster for their lack of talent and overall appeal, claiming himself to be “The New Face Of Global Wrestling.” Rightfully so it would seem, as he went on to become not only the youngest champion in GW history but the youngest double champion amidst much scrutiny from the rest of the roster. He even managed to peak Brick’s interest enough for him to come out as a guest commentator for one of his matches. Never one to back down, Deacon continued to taunt Brick all throughout the match while taking breaks to subdue his opponent in between vile hand gestures and foul remarks made toward the CEO of Ruckus Inc. In the end, Holmes won the match, but was then stuck inside the ring with an angry Brick. Even in the face of a much larger opponent Deacon did not break, he got right in his face and told him exactly what he thought. “There’s no way I’ll be as good as you someday, I’m already better!” That comment led to a small scuffle between the two, which ended with Holmes being left unconscious in the ring following a vicious Bricklayer. This would not be the end. In the following months Deacon Holmes lashed out with several attacks and cruel pranks all aimed at Brick. He vandalized his car, insulted his family, incited a brawl with him during an autograph signing, several more backstage, cost him more than a match or two, and even knocked him unconscious and had him sent to an alternative lifestyle club as the 'entertainment'. Deacon can hold a grudge to say the least. After back and forth attacks on each other in and out of the ring, Global Wrestling finally decided to pit the two stars against each other. In a “Hard 10' match, on in which the only way to win was to knock your opponent unconscious for over ten seconds, both men took the fight to each other. After a few close calls the fight spilled out of the ring yet continued... from one end of the ring to the next. At the climax, both men found themselves fighting atop the Globaltron with nowhere to go but down. Eventually Brick and Holmes fell not only off the Globaltron, but through the steel stage below it creating a rather large hole. Neither man emerged victorious, both laid unconscious on the ground underneath. Medical crews immediately swarmed onto the scene to treat whatever potential injuries might have occurred. Although unconscious for much of the time, as he and Brick were rolled out of the arena on stretchers I saw Deacon Holmes gain his consciousness for a brief moment. He smiled. It was the first time I’d ever seen him do such a thing. His eyes crept open ever so slightly, just long enough for him to glance over and see the World Champion incoherent on the stretcher next to him. After that they rolled into the back of his head as he faded out once again, but this time with an ear to ear grin. It appeared as if all the pain he’d endured was almost euphoric for him, and taking the champ to the limit was just the icing on the cake. Both men sustained minor injuries and were soon at each other’s throats once again. For the first time, Deacon was praised by the rest of the roster for his impressive performance on Evolution, but he was less than impressed with himself. The match was labeled a draw. No matter how impressive his showing in that main event was, he hadn’t won. As far as he’s concerned, he shouldn’t have even shown up that night. Sure he took the champion to his limits, and as satisfying as that was, he gained nothing. Taking the night off would have been just as productive. So he trained harder. In case you were wondering, my name is Steven Dinkler, and I currently occupy the passenger side of Deacon Holmes” vehicle. I work as a reporter for Global Wrestling and was fortunate enough to gain the opportunity to spend a day in the life of Deacon Holmes. This particular started at 5:40AM, with a hangover. After watching Deacon, who is underaged by the way, “Pray to the porcelain gods”, as he calls it for over thirty minutes, the possible next Global Wrestling World Champion proceeded to get dressed and headed to the gym, making two or three stops along the way to expel whatever remnants of the prior evening still remained. Once purged, we continued on at breakneck speeds of two hundred miles per hour while rapidly cutting in and out of lanes and even driving on the wrong side of the road only to cut back in mere inches away from oncoming traffic. The ride was interesting to say the least, and by interesting I mean horrifying. I’m talking worst nightmare, I could use a clean pair of pants, adrenaline rush inducing fright. He calls it a joy ride. Apparently he enjoys such things, throughout the entire trip an ear to ear grin reminiscent of the one he sported while being rolled out of the arena on a stretcher after his match with Brick remained on his face as his eyes twinkled with each moving violation. He even chuckled slightly when he almost ran head on into a semi while changing CD’s. Finally we reached the gym, and not a moment too soon might I add. As soon as I was able to pry my hands loose from the white knuckle grip I was keeping on whatever seemed stable inside the car I slowly rose to my feet on wobbly legs then made my way to the gym’s entrance just steps behind Deacon. Once inside that door he was all business and his intensity level kicked up several notches, to the same level at which he competes on television... if not higher. First up was cardio. Deacon hit the treadmill hard, running for three miles at near or full speed on an incline. After stepping off while sweating profusely he stated that he felt as if his legs were pumping battery acid. I handed him a soft blue towel with “D.H.” in silver letters at the bottom right corner. After wiping the perspiration from his face it was off to the weights for Deacon. There he spent a solid hour quickly pressing, pushing, and curling weights, causing long, telephone wire like veins to sprout up all over Holmes? body as he grunts to get the weight up. From there, Holmes takes his training session to the ring that occupies the center of the gym. In preparation for his street fight with Brick on the upcoming edition of Evolution he does a bit of sparring with one of the boxers also training on this particular day. Except he doesn’t wear any gear other than a pair of gloves. He also tells his sparring partner not to hold back, he wants him to come at him full force. Explaining that Brick isn’t gonna take it easy on him so he didn’t want any holding back, he wants to feel every punch. Surprisingly, Deacon feels very few. He moves quickly and erratically, causing many of the punches thrown his way to end up off target and allowing him the opportunity to slip in the occasional punch of his own. Watching Holmes exhaust his competition by causing him to waste several punches and steal the wind from out of him with well timed body blows was really quite a spectacle. The way his eyes lit up with each punch he dodged and counter punched really conveyed just how intense a competitor he really is. Holmes raised and open hand into the air signaling for me to throw him his towel which I did from a nearby table as he was hopping out of the ring. I patiently waited at the juice bar during the quick shower that followed his workout. Upon his emergence from the showers we were off to grab something to eat. Although I was somewhat reluctant to climb back into the car with him I somehow worked up the courage to get back in, this time making sure to wear my seat belt and checking it several time just to make sure it was connected properly. This trip was a much more law abiding one though as Holmes spent much of the ride on a minuscule black cellular phone talking to his stable mate and now friend, John Zero, discussing plans for Evolution. Apparently they’ve got a few surprises in store. We pull into “Kathy’s Wings and Subs” and park in the fire lane right in front of the entrance. When exiting the vehicle I asked Deacon if he knew where he was parked he replied “Yeah, close to the door.” as if the possible fines he could receive meant nothing. The Upper Echelon has made him a rich man, and he enjoys every minute of it. Deacon throws the door open, drawing everyone’s attention to our arrival. We step into the small shack like restaurant consisting of a counter at which orders are taken, a soda fountain, and a few tables strewn about in random locations. The restaurant’s patrons were an eclectic crowd to say the least. A motley crew of truckers, bikers, small time drug dealers, and your common neighborhood rif raf. This was hardly someplace you’d expect someone with as much money as Holmes to be eating. Apparently, restaurants in nicer areas don’t have good hot wings. Or so he says at least, but if those wings were what he considers good I believe this reporter has had his fair share. It was hours ago and my tastebuds still hurt, well, what’s left of them. The first three made my nose run a little, after five my face was bright red and I was sweating profusely, it would’ve been easier to believe I’d been the one working out. Holmes and I split thirty wings and a pitcher of grape soda before leaving Kathy’s. I’m actually surprised I’m still alive after such a feat, and even more surprised that I got out of there in one piece judging by some of the looks I was getting. While pulling out of the “Kathy’s” parking lot Holmes? cell phone rang once more. It was John Zero, apparently he and Holmes had some pressing matters to deal with at TUE headquarters and were supposed to meet with Il Capo at three “o clock. A quick glance from Holmes at his sixty thousand dollar watch reveals that time is quickly closing in on the three o” clock hour, which sends Holmes flying out of the parking lot screeching tires and also brings us back to where we were. Racing time, flying around corners at blazing speeds. He informs me that he’s gonna have to cut our outing short due to the fact that I am less than welcome at TUE headquarters and that he’ll be dropping me back off at the hotel along the way. We make it back to my hotel by the skin of our teeth as Holmes caused more than a few near accidents on the way here. His phone rings again as I climb out of the extremely low car and I hear a bit of his conversation. “Yeah... Yeah I’m on my way now, I’m dropping Dink off... Where are you?” Just then a black Mitsubishi Eclipse GT flies by causing my jacket to pull tight against my body due to the wind created in it’s passing. Holmes quickly hangs up the phone while exclaiming “Son of a bitch!” and speeds out of the hotel parking lot with his tires smoking, another loud screech is heard as he turns back onto the main road and rapidly accelerates out of view. No one can truthfully say where he’s headed in life or in his career, but one thing can be said... It’s gonna be fast. Steven Dinkler reporting.
