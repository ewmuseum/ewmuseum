Mo Money, Mo Problems

8 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mo Money, Mo Problems J. C. Diamond Global Wrestling Promo #1111 Date: 4/8/04 Location: Mean Streets Queens, NY (The scene opens up in J.C apartment which now is a little bit furnished with a couch and an flat-screen television sitting on the wall with a DVD player and Gamecube & X-Box & PS2 sitting on the bottom shelves. The camera pans to the bedroom where there is a king sized bed with pure black sheets and black and white pillows. There is some night tables and a desk with a Nice looking computer on top. The camera then goes to the kitchen where J.C is seen over a blender with an egg in hand. He cracks it over the edge of the blender and the egg falls into a blob of mixture that was already there. Devante Diamond, J.C's younger brother, blends it all up and then opens the top of the blender and gulps down everything inside. He shakes his head looking almost disgusted and then he looks up at the camera) J.C: What can I say. I lost. That is as simple as I can put it. I can’t blame it on anything but my lack of experience and just some stupid decisions by me. I let Creed out think me and get some well needed rest. Mad props to you, you deserved that ring, but that doesn’t mean your better than me, just remember that. I will take that loss with pride because hell I am new to this. Not new to the game but new to the spotlight, what man wouldn’t be nervous the very first few times he performs in front of millions and millions of people. You can ask any star, MJ, Deon Sanders, Ken Griffey Jr., they will all say the same thing. Well that is the past and I can only work off of it and look to the future. J.C: I think that some of my mistakes may have come from the lack of in gym time I was so caught up in this new life, the new money that I forgot about how important the training aspect of the game is. I can’t just live it up and expect to prevail. Hell I know I can beat anyone here, I just can’t put my life into focus yet, but I figured out how, there is only one way…Something I never wanted to do..I must go back to the streets. So many men have gone back to their roots, their upbringing to find themselves and I think it’s the only thing I can’t do. Live the life by day and come back to being a GW employee by night. Hell I know I can live it up all the same all I got is a few more perks. Ya know? J.C: I guess it’s time …… (J.C grabs a pair of keys from the kitchen counter and grabs a white fur rocawear jean jacket and slips on a white NBA headband and locks his door and walks down to the parking lot. There you see him hop into a white Mercedes Benz CLK 320 with the hood down and rolling on a couple of 22’ rims. He speeds off towards an unknown destination as the scene goes to commercials) (Promo for Vendetta 2 pops up on the screen and then it finishes and the scene cuts back into the insight the scenery has now changed dramatically you went from a well built up neighborhood with people walking dogs and kids playing in the parks to now a place that looks as it is about to fall apart, broken down cars on the street side, hardly any site of people except a group of people huddled around a can lit on fire and some occasional dealers pushing drugs to little kids. J.C turns sharply and comes up to a large cage.) Sebastian: Hell naw, look who tha fuck it is, J.C is back in town. Angel: You playin, you sure that’s him? Nigga what you been smoking. (J.C turns the ignition off and hops over the door and takes off his rocawear jacket and is wearing a Knicks Jersey with his name on the back and a pair of blue long jean pants.) J.C: Sebastian ain’t been smoking shit, I’m back in tha flesh. Angel: Where you been dawg, you got your own fucking clothing line now? That’s fucking wack, how did the poorest, sorriest piece of shit get up in a ride like THAT? J.C: Angel, just shut the fuck up. Sebastian: Like he said, where you been at. That shit wasn’t cool, one day you were here next day you were gone and people been frontin’ on us hell its only us two now we got no more back, how a nigga gunna leave his crew like that. J.C: Sabby, I told ya since I was a boy I wanted outta the life, but now I am out I need back in, hell I got evicted, what the fuck did you want me to do? Anel: You coulda stayed at my momma’s house. J.C: Man your momma’s cooking sucks and she wants to fucking rape me. Don’t be stupid. Sebastian: So like Angel wuz sayin, how did a nigga like you get up in a ride like that. J.C: You still got cable? Angel: Ye.. J.C: You ever heard of Global Wrestling. Angel: I seen some commercials sometimes, never sat down and watched the shit. J.C: Well you are looking at one of their newest signees, they know how to take care of a brotha. Angel: Don’t lie Jay, I know you always wanted to be a wrestler and shit, but how the fuck did you make it to the big time? J.C: Your asking me? I’m still shocked, they left me an offer that I couldn’t refuse, the owner said he picked up word of me from some indy and the rest is history. Sebastian: So how come you never came back to even visit. J.C: I got no excuse fo that, I was caught up in a lifestyle that wasn’t used to me.. I’m back now and that’s all that matters Angel: Man, you been away for a month, we don’t run shit anymore man, how we gunna keep all the shit up with you gone, Skip and his Boys took over around here, they are fucking selling all that shit to the kids. Everything is fucked up. J.C: You gotta be playin’, how did that wannabe-me get that high. Sebastian: Nobody knows man, he rolls with some big ass playaz. 7-Foot and shit J.C: Hah, you think I’m scared of that? ( J.C gets a serious look on his face ), Yo man wheres Jermaine? Sebastian: Man, your little bro is now rollin wit Skip. Skip is trying to get him to deal/ J.C: What the FUCK. He was rolling with us, how’d yall let him go wit Skip. Angel: It wasn’t our fault Jay, Skip had the buz already and he showed Maine the life, He left and we’ve only seen him up in Skips ride the last couple weeks. J.C: Well lets go get him back. That Skip will wish he never took any of the buznass round ‘ere. That mutha fucker was always a coward. Voice: Imma wat? (J.C, Angel and Sebastian turn around to see a smaller guy whos around 5’8 and is kinda big for his size with a bat and beside him is two huge 7’ foot guys. They are rolling in an Impala and J.C sees Jermaine his little half-brother in the back seat of the car) J.C: Skip, haven’t seen ya in a while…beat up any more kids lately? Skip: Don’t play yourself, I own these streets now, the truth has now passed. J.C: Don’t play MYSELF? You only “own” these streets cause I left, well I’m back bitch. Skip: Well I have no reason to fear no more, I got some back up these days (Skip points to the two big guys on either side of him) J.C: Those two bafoons? They probably can’t even count to 10. I’m not here to mess with you Skip all I want is my brother. J.C: Jermaine get over here. (Jermaine jumps out of the car and walks over beside Skip) J.C: What the fuck are you doing. I promised Doc I would take care of you, just get over here. Jermaine: Take care of ME? Where were you the last few months? J.C: Don’t start now Jermaine, I got evicted and I got hired by a company that pays good, trust me we are set now. Just come over here. Skip: Jermaine, remember this if you go back to him, you won’t live the same way, he don’t run things around here no more….I do. J.C: Haha, Skip I could be back on top in a few weeks if I wanted to, I live a better life now than you ever will. But if you want me to come back here and start back the way it was give me the word. I’m here for my brother not your business, but if you don’t give him to me then you will leave me no choice. Jermaine: Jay, I’m good now, Skip’s going to give me some work. J.C: Jermaine, his work is not the work you think he will give you trust me, I know. Skip: He don’t know shit Jermaine lets roll. J.C: Jermaine your not going anywhere…. Jermaine: Jay don’t tell me what to do, imma man now, I know whats good for me. J.C: Your fucking 17 years old you don’t know shit…..get here before I make ya. Skip: Haha, I think you fail to realize my two boys here, meet Backbone and Krusher. (J.C gives them the finger and goes and grabs Jermaine by the wrist) J.C: Come lets go. ( Backbone and Krusher jump J.C and start pounding him, Skip gets his bat in swinging position but Angel tackles him to the floor and Sebastian takes Jermaine and kicks one of the big guys on J.C. J.C gets up a bit and trips one of the guys and kicks him right in the face and the other guy stands up and they look at each other the guy charges and J.C sticks his leg out and it goes right into the big guys knee and it snaps backwards, in the mean time skip has just hit Angel with the bat in the ribs and Sebastian has Jermaine holding him back from the fight he goes and tries to help Angel but gets clocked by the bat. J.C sees this and goes after skip but Skip takes Jermaine and rolls off quickly in his ride) J.C: Fuck! Angel, Sebastian you guys alrite? Angel holding his ribs: Ye, I guess Sebastian is out cold, how the fuck are you gunna get Maine back? J.C: I don’t know man….I really don’t know…. (The scene fades away as J.C and Angel put Sebastian in the CLK 320 and drive off)
