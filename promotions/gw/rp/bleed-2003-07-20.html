bleed.

20 July 2003

Date: sunday - july 20th, 2003 Drip. Drip. Drip. Wood doesn’t mix with blood very well. Just like when you get a splinter, there’s a bruise that forms around where the fragment pierces your skin. Once the splinter is removed, the wound still seems to hurt. It’s not because of the fact that you had to use a needle or such to go in and pull it out, but rather because it becomes infected. The dirt and grime on the grains of wood spread into the blood. You poke at the wound, it hurts when you do it, but you can’t just “stop” doing it. Even time after the splinter’s been removed, you still have that acute pain underneath your skin. It takes awhile before that pain goes away. So just imagine a gigantic chunk of wood lodged in between the back of your ribcage and shoulder blade. I’m sitting at a hotel. To be more specific, I’m sitting on the edge of a king-sized bed in the heart of Los Angeles, California. It’s the first time I’ve been to California in three months, and the first time I’ve been out of Texas since the Whiplash ppv. Some kind of hell, I’d say. I’ve been awake for about roughly five minutes now. My head is aching as if that chunk of wood is still stuck in it. Still readjusting to the surroundings. The sun’s shining right onto my sockets, while the rest of the room seems to remain pretty dark. Looking over at the clock, it’s five in the PM. A haze of memory comes over me, last night I was up until today’s morning. I remember, I didn’t shut my eyes to sleep until the sun had already risen. 'Well well, look who's finally up.' I cock my head to the right, The Ladies Man walks into the door. The haze starts to fade, I begin remembering more and more about what had happened last night. That's why I'm here, Jay invited me to join him over the weekend in California, to celebrate. I didn't know what we were celebrating about, whether it was for what happened on Evolution, or just because Jay needed another person to help him juggle between the ladies. I look at him, and like always, Jay has a woman hanging from either arm, with a smile in the middle. 'How long was I asleep for?' I ask, finally rubbing the crust from the rims of my eyelids. 'Long enough,' he says and holds a hand out to help me up. 'I guess you're still not quite used to your old life style, Reed.' 'Heh,' I mutter as I grab his hand and hop up to my feet. 'Maybe you're right, but it has been a little while, so cut me some slack. I haven't been partying none stop like yourself, I've been in the ring - something you might want to try out every now and then.' 'What's that supposed to mean?' Jay looks at me as if he thought I meant it, but then smiles. 'Hah, I'm joking. I had to give you the match Reed, how else are you going to get back to your old status if you go losing every week?' 'True. So what's on the schedule for tonight then Mr. Robson?' He smiles and tosses the two women off his shoulders, and onto the bed. 'That's what I like to here Trey, and the good news is that the night is young!' He grabs the hotel key and waves off the ladies. My guess is that he'll leave them there and make use of them at a later time. Step out the door, go down the elevator. Reach the lobby, walk out of the five star. Jay pulls out a different set of keys, car keys. Walk through the lot, stop at a sleak black automobile, the top's down. An SL500 Roadster, I stop and observe the situation. How does TLM have the keys to a brand new Benz, I wonder. We came here without it. The money this guy throws around, it's insane. 'A lease,' he smiles as the locks pop up. 'Feel like driving?' I take a raincheck, and hop in the passenger side. Jay screeches the tires and heads out of the lot doing about fourty. Once on the road, he tests the limits. I crack my knuckles and neck as silence fills the car. Either he isn't in the mood to talk, or he's waiting for something before he says anything. I grab my backpack, and pull out the CD case. I go to toss some Jurassic 5 in there, but Jay knocks my hand away. No talking, no music. Just the awkward silence. Reach an allyway, stop. Turn in, brake, locks pop up. Jay turns and looks at me, 'Reed, I know you. I know you're not used to the GW-lifestyle. You haven't gone through the experiences of dealing with the likes of Stecchino or Silent Wind or any of those crazy murderers. It's time to learn.' I look at him like he's a psycho. 'You're joking, right?' But my question comes out half-heartedly. He reaches over above my lap and pulls open the dashboard. Freeze it. -Wad of cash. -Bottle of tequila. -A bag of unmentionables. -Two boxes of bullets. -Glock .357 -Colt .45 'What...the...' 'Trey,' he cuts me off. 'Keep it cool.' Keep it cool? 'Right.' 'Global Wrestling isn't the ordinary everyday federation. It's not clean like what you're used to. You've seen Stecchino come out to the ring with a gun, you know his connections with the mob. Now, I guess you can include Price and his goons in that mix as well.' 'So...what you're telling me.' Push hair back. 'Is that if you can't beat them, join them?' 'Well,' Jay says with a smile as he pulls out the .45, identifying it as his own and closing the glovebox. 'That's a start.' What I saw, what I'm seeing, is this for real? Is Jay Robson pulling back on the hammer of a semi-automatic pistol? I open the glovebox back up, and pull out the .357, it identifies as mine. If I was a gun expert, I would call it Glock's 32 model, a fifteen bullet capacity in the magazine. If I were a gun expert, I'd pull out the magazine and fill it with bullets. Instead, I fumble it in my hands, amazed at the fact that I could cease someone's life with this thing that stays snug in the palm of my hand. 'Are you ready?' he asks. 'Ready for what?' 'It's all about to begin.'
