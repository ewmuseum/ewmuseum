Festival De Campeones Part 3-Old vs. New

31 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Festival De Campeones Part 3-Old vs. New El Lunes Mascara Global Wrestling Promo #834 Date: 1-31-04 Location: Festival De Campeones, Tijuana, Mexico Meanwhile....In The Lucha Libre Area.... ~/~While Justin is involved in his offbeat antics, the brooding El Lunes Mascara is within the Luche Libre area of the festival, celebrating the stars and art that is the Mexican style of professional wrestling. He is within the exhibition area as the ring has been setup on the outside in the humid heat, a normal circumstance for most Latino's, let alone luchadores. No mat is placed outside, it's substitute being the mounds of red dirt and piney grass, not exactly the softest surface for any luchadore frequent to the outside to land on, but all this is unimportant in this land...for the art comes before the setting, a standard rule of anyone wishing to become a luchadore. El Lunes quietly watches, on still entrenched within his cloak as not to be recognized by the public, as a pair of young luchadores trade graceful maneuvers in the ring...the match flowing with great speed and ease for saying both of them are obvious rookies, El Lunes noticing the tell tale sign that both of them are yet to hit the ropes right as he watches them hit the ropes slightly wrong while being Irish whipped in.~/~ El Lunes-'The youth of lucha needs more work. They must learn to crawl before they walk...or in this case, hit the ropes before they tope' (Suicide Dive).' ~/~As EL Lunes watches on at the hard fought match the two rookies are putting on for a crowd of only one, the luchadore from before in a full black pinstriped dress suit and wearing a lucha mask with orange and red flame imprints decorated upon it, the man going by the name of Infierno, walks up beside El Lunes as the rustle of the grass beneath his Gucci shoe covered feet alerts El Lunes of his presence. El Lunes cares not to look to the side at Infierno to even acknowledge him as he says in a coarse and disdainful voice.~/~ El Lunes-'Why are you even here Infierno? Why do you even dare destroy the legacy of past 'Festival De Campeones' lucha champions by even setting foot here. I could still remember when you and I were just children...you always rushed, you were always so bitter....you always worried about the spotlight before the art.' ~/~Infierno fixes his mask and lets out a tiny laugh with his deep voice, folding his arms as he also takes part in watching the two rookies in action as he replies.~/~ Infierno-'Haha....your bitter words have no effect on me Lunes. You only say those things because you were too scared to truly 'embrace' what lucha stood for...giving your body to win the match. Did it kill you that maybe I was just a little bit stronger, a little bit faster...a little bit smarter? Ha, it sickens me how weak you still are even today. You take after your father so well Lunes...weak wrestling skill and all. Don't you see, lucha isn't what it used to be. Lucha has become more death-defying....more high-risk and THAT'S what the fans want. Why deprive them? Why if I did, I wouldn't be the rich man I am today amigo....but it sickens me how after Maria's death...' El Lunes-'Speak her angelic name from your dirty lips again and I swear to the highest power that I will rip your black heart out right here , 'amigo'.' ~/~The uncharacteristic and sudden bout of pure rage makes Infierno laugh once again as he fixes his blonde ponytail protruding from the back of his tightly tied mask, both men looking at the two rookies in the ring and remembering the days they would train in the hot sun, long before they even thought of matches where they would sell out Mexican venues respectively. Their eyes are yet to fix on each other as they keep their focus on the match, watching on as one of the rookies enwraps his legs within his opponent's an d leans back, wrenching his arms as well for a beautiful surfboard stretch.~/~ Infierno-'Lunes....that was always the difference between me and you...you were always content with the way things were 'meant to be' or 'what you were given'. Never ONCE did you attempt to grasp ahold of what you wanted and for that, you are incredibly weak. Fortunately, I will get to prove this soon enough...I heard of your entry in the tournament to take place at noon. Just think about it...' ~/~He holds his hands up in the sky as if to paint a vivid picture in El Lunes' mind, the dark luchadore yet to even crack a look of interest in Infierno's words as he continues.~/~ Infierno-'The BEST Mexico has to offer, all in one tourney and the winner simply known as 'The Greatest'. Let's not kid ourselves compadre....it WILL be me and you at the end of the tourney, and don't be a bigger fool then you already are...you WILL lose to me Lunes, your destiny was always to submit to me...it was all just a matter of when would be the time the hand of fate would choose for that loss to come...and you shall lose in the eyes of the fans you love so much and in front of your beloved moon...Maria nor 'love' could EVER change that fate and neither could your weak father Lunes.' ~/~Almost an omen of the conversation getting darker, the two men in the ring resort to simple brawling, trading meaty punches as they land on each other's faces respectively...both men looking tired and winded, but still going in the battle of dominance. This brawling shows a bit of symbolism, being in which that when the fists begin flying the dark words begin to flow from two of the most respected luchadores in Mexico. One fighting for the love of the sport and the tradition, the other a believer in the new way it should be fought and the fame that comes with it. El Lunes lifts his cloak back, finally staring a hole through Infierno with his deep blue eyes as he gets nose to nose with him, Infierno cracking a smile of utter cockiness while his rival keeps a straight face of hatred, saying calmly and articulately, so Infierno understands every word.~/~ El Lunes-'Infierno...your problem was always believing that the world revolved around you...your impatience and utter disrespect for the art five generations of my family has loved eats me from the inside. You are not worthy to wear the mask of a luchadore over your head...selling out the way lucha SHOULD be, and giving into this sellout style. You remind me of these 'spot artists' I gave to battle in Global Wrestling come their pay-per-view event. They will feel pain, the same pain that runs through my heart everyday while my Maria isn't here and they shall be broken until their bones are a fine powder, just as I will to you. The only difference between those two unknowing souls and you, is that they will have the dignity to be buried within a grave. You my friend, are not worth the dirt used to cover you. I hope to scatter you to the wind after I leave you laying in a heap of unresolveable pain...much like each man who comes before me will have to forever be scarred with a beating that was inevitable...one where no escape could be fathomed. My only regret about you and them...is that the pain I implant into your bodies can't last for a lifetime like mine! No remorse is planned and no help shall be given....the only thing there to comfort you and them, will be the screams of writhing pain you'll both make, all in the name of the moon, the woman I lost, and the little papito who was never given his fair chance at life. Infierno...enjoy your days as a 'complete' human being...for I am going to use the art of lucha the way it was meant to be used and you will regret the day you ever betrayed the style.' ~/~Wrapped up deeply into his speech, El Lunes puts his hand over the back of Infierno's neck and softly pulls him closer so he speaks within his ear. Infierno has lost his look of cockiness and has chosento revert to a look of seriousness as he now knows this matter goes far beyond 'just a match'.~/~' El Lunes-'The moon has beckoned my friend...it has awaited your downfall and today will be the day I can give it what it has awaited to see. Those in the tourney will at least be fortunate to have some chance of walking away with a slight chance of being able to live life without the aid of a fully automated wheelchair.....you on the other hand will suffer tortures unlike any you've ever experienced. I suggest you take this Festival a little more seriously mona mi' for next time we meet, it will not be to warn you of the thunder....it will be to strike you with the lightning. Enjoy your day friend...' ~/~At that instant, another sign of symbolism, one of the rookies pins his opponent, signifying the end of the match and the synchronizing end of the deep-seeded conversation between El Lunes and Infierno. Lunes turns away, putting his hood back over his head and walks off from the ring down the stone walkway leading back to the long road which takes you to the other areas as Infierno watches him take his long stride away, fixing his suit collar.~/~ Infierno-'To think Juan....we used to be friends. Hehe, I guess lucha can truly destroy relationships...but it will be MY hand that destroys you. I swear it.' -/With Justin in Lord knows what trouble as he runs about the Festival with his beautiful new friend....and El Lunes expressing his plans to once and all destroy his rival within the ring they both have made their names known off of...their is no telling the chaos that will happen at the Festival...for the answers to that as always will be....SHROUDED IN DARKNESS...-/-Fin
