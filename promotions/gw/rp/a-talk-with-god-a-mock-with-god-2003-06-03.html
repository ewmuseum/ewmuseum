A Talk With God; A Mock With God

3 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/3/03 The thoughts run through his mind? You’ll Never Make It You Are Weak You Are Small You Are Worthless You Suck You Can’t Wrestle On and on they flash through his memory. Relieved, making them all eat their own words. And jamming those words down their throat, making them choke on agony. Desperate, they search for words to ridicule him. None, words don’t bother him. Simply because he holds the most prized possession in the Global Wrestling. Simply because he has proven at Dead Zone why he is the best. Simply because he is the best and the GW World’s Title proves it. You’ll Never Amount To Anything You Are Just A Follower Of Salaok You Are Just In The Shadow Of The Nevermore He steps out of the shadow forever, now, now having something to show the world that he can survive. They have raised him well, The Nevermore, a family to him. Pleased? Underestimated pleasure at the least. He wears the GW Heavyweight Title around his waist proudly. It looks just as good on him as it did on Mani Shigeru. Or looks just as good as it did on Salaok. Or as it did on Silent Wind’s body. It shines brighter then ever, he’ll never let it go. He’ll never take it for granted. On his right shoulder lies the GW International Title, another achievement in Global Wrestling. Another achievement in life. He’ll never let it go. He’ll never take it for granted. His reflection shines bright into the title belt. He lifts his head into the skies above’searching for something. He wears a faded black pair of jeans and a black “Norma Jean” hooded sweatshirt. He wears black boots on his feet that stand on a hard rock on the cliff of the mountain. One more step and he falls to his death. He inches his way towards the edge’walking on a layer of fear to the average man. But he goes further, sending pebbles flying off to the Earth below. He looks up towards the skies and smirks’he kneels down and takes a seat on the edge of the cliff. Without fear, he hangs his feet off the edge. He un-straps his title belt and throws it over his left shoulder. He looks up in the sky again. The memories of what they said still plague his mind? You Need The Nevermore To Win You Should Go Back To China You Can’t Defend Yourself You Aren’t Good Enough To Be In GW You Aren’t Good Enough He looks up in the sky, talking to whatever may be up there? “It reminds me of a slap in the face.” He chuckles? “And this time it is not received, it has been given back to you. Faithful Lord” I laugh in your face. You pulled me down every time I rose up from the ground. Every time I stood up, you pushed me down. And now, now you can’t push me down. When I hold the GW Heavyweight title and the GW International title, I can only think.” He shakes his head side to side in disagreement with what he has just said? “I can only realize that I am the most powerful man in all of Global Wrestling. Finally Lord, I have accomplished something and not even the powerful God of Gods can overthrow me from my throne. Nobody, and I mean nobody can stop me. Send your best, because they won’t even match up to me. My whole life, I was always the weakest one. I was always the smallest. I was always the worst. I was always this, and always that. Now, now you can’t say anything bad about me simply because I am better then you. Lord, you can try to knock me off this ladder of success, but your attempts will be laughed at by me. You sent Tragic Hero, you sent Brick, you sent Roz Blayze, but they are weak compared to me. I beat the living hell out of Brick and made him cry for help. I made him tap out, going against all your rules. Now, oh Lord, I am more powerful then even you.” The wind blows hard, pushing his body forward off the cliff. He moves a little bit forward as the cliff breaks off and falls off the cliff. He rolls back quickly and escapes death. He looks up towards the heavens and laughs? “You can never, ever throw me down on the ground again. I am at the peak of my life, the pinnacle of my dreams. And you can’t take me down. Lord, it eats you up inside. To know that you can’t beat me this time, it kills you?” Rain slowly pours down on him, drip by drip’starting to soak his clothing. He grabs the title belts and put them under his hooded sweatshirt, protecting them from the rain. Lightning strikes only yards behind him. He doesn’t jump, or even flinch. He looks up in the heavens and laughs? “You killed the world by flooding it out centuries ago and only helping Noah. But you can’t flood me out of being the best. These title belts are awards for achieving this’and nobody. And I mean nobody can beat me.” A phone rings? He reaches in his hooded sweatshirt and takes it out and opens it up? “Hello?” “Yes, this is him.” “Thanks Salaok. I appreciate it.” “Brick did a good job too.” He chuckles? “Almost.” “Yeah, I made him tap out like a baby.” “I think I heard him cry for his Mommy when he was face first in the thumbtacks.” “So who do you have lined up for me this week” Another sucker like every other week? Another match with Roz Blayze or something?” He chuckles? “So you mean you got a bigger challenge this week?” “Really that big” Brick again?” “No” Umm, what about Sean Price?” “No” Then who?” He drops the phone. His smile turns into panic and his jaw drops. His eyes widen as he drops his phone down off the cliff? “I’m versing Salaok.” I’m versing my mentor. I’m versing my teacher. I’m versing the best.” His jaw drops as the screen fades to black>
