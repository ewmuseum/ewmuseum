Date: 9/11/05 1st Dual Assignment ? New Fighting Grounds, New Problems At the Asaka Ninja Clan’s stronghold in Okinawa, Japan, the older sister of Katsumi Asaka, Kagome sits on her knees before an open flame in her red and white priestess kimono in a dark room of the stronghold. She’s chanting a spell in an ancient tongue while she moves her fingers and hands in multiple positions with each chant she had said. Kagome opens her eyes sharply and says the last chant as she thrusts both hands at the wild flame. She watches grow even taller and wilder, but she gasps as she looks with terror from a disturbing vision within the flames. “This isn’t good.” Kagome softly said, stands up from the floor and stares at the flames seriously. “I must tell Father at once.” Outside a paper screen door, Kagome kneels beside the door. “Report, Kagome.” The strong male voice came from behind the paper screen door. Kagome takes a deep breath, clutching tightly on her kimono with both hands as she stares down at the wooden floor seriously with some concern on her young Japanese face. “Father, through the burning flames I saw that the Sword of Darkness has been revived.” “I already know.” Father replied. Kagome lifts her head and looks at the paper screen curiously. “You saw it in a vision.” Behind the paper screen door, the leader of the Asaka Ninja Clan and the father to Katsumi and Kagome Asaka, Hayato who wears his traditional black robes sits in the shadows of his room sipping sake in a sake cup. Hayato smirks. “You can say that.” Before him, he watches TV as it’s broadcasting a special news report in Japanese about the discovery of a mystic sword which was the Sword of Darkness that thought to be lost in Edo Years and it was going to be display in a museum in Los Angeles, California. Hayato looks at the paper screen door and sees his daughter’s silhouette on it. “You understand what will happen if one of rival clans get their hands on that sword.” “Hai.” Kagome replied. Hayato pours more sake in his cup. “We must retrieve this sword.” “Do you want me to go?” Kagome questioned. Hayato takes a sip of his sake and sighs softly. “No, you’re a Spirit Priestess not a ninja. And I believe I have the right ninja in mind for completing this task.” He smirks as he continues watching the news. At the mansion of Katsumi Asaka in Beverly Hills, California, the young English girl, Heather Burnside stands underneath a running showerhead with her eyes close, savoring the feel of the lukewarm water that runs down her pink flesh. She soon opens her eyes as she begins conditioning and rinsing her hair. While rinsing her hair, her facial expression changes from calmness to seriousness as she begins thinking back to a time not too long ago. “What!” Jade sits on the couch crying, as Heather sits next to her and tried to comfort her. The two are in Heather's mansion in Santa Monica, California. The same mansion that Heather and her ex girlfriend Jenny Anderson bought and lived in together. 'I can't believe she broke up with me.' Jade said, tears streaming down her face. Heather put her arm on Jade's back. 'Don't worry I'll get back at her for this Jade.' 'Thank you Heather.' Jade said, wiping away the tears. 'Thank you.' Heather drives over to Katsumi Asaka's giant mansion in Beverly Hills. She climbs out of her Chevy Camero IROC and walks to the door. After being escorted in and out of Kat’s mansion, Heather stares face to face with Jade's ex-girlfriend, Katsumi. 'I know you broke up with Jade.' Heather said. 'And she’s very upset. I challenge you to a match.' Heather’s thoughts of the past are broken by the sound of gentle knock on the bathroom’s door. She shuts off the water and steps out of the shower. Heather grabs hold of a towel and wraps it around her body as she opens the bathroom door and sees that one of Katsumi’s many young attractive maids stands outside the door. “Yes?” Heather queried softly. The maid bows her head before Heather. “I came here to tell you that Mistress won’t be accompanying you to breakfast this morning.” Heather walks pass the maid and enters her large bedroom. Heather searches in her closet for clothes. “I didn’t feel like breakfast this morning anyway. So, where is she this morning?” “Mistress is at the recording studio.” Maid answered. Heather removes her towel and changes to a workout attire. Maid stares at Heather curiously. “Will you be training today, Miss?” “Yes, Cookie.” Heather replied with a nod of the head. “Since I won’t be having any breakfast neither desert then I supposed I will train. We do have our first match this week after all.” “Don’t hurt yourself, Miss.” Cookie advised. Heather nods and leaves her room. At Crimson Maiden Enterprise, a twenty-one story building that Katsumi Asaka owns in Downtown Los Angeles, Kat stands inside a recording booth in grunge attire that’s black and red, as she finishes up a brand new song for her first American CD that’s entitle, Ninja Love America Style. “And that’s a rap.” A sound person said in the intercom. Kat removes the headphones and swings her short brown hair with red highlights away from her enchanting face. She calmly exits the booth and enters the studio where the Sound Person and Kat’s female Russian bodyguard/manager, Eve Gurlukovich are. Kat sighs softly and looks at the sound person. “Was it your idea to take out shorten my guitar solo in the middle of my song?” “Yes, I didn’t think it would appeal to you American listeners like it did with your Japanese listeners.” Sound Person explained. “Didn’t think it would appeal to my American listeners.” Kat repeated with a slow nod. “Ok.” Kat grabs the sound person by the back of his head with a blink of an eye and slams his face right in the expensive sound equipment. Sound person lets out a painful yell as he favors his broken and bloody nose. Eve watches with a smirk and arms cross over her chest. “I don’t pay your dumb ass to think, I pay your fucking ass to obey what I say!” Kat shouted. “If I want my guitar solo to be twenty-five minutes long on my song then leave it the fuck alone, understand?” Sound Person slowly nods and frighteningly stares at Kat. “Yes, Miss Asaka.” Kat pets the Sound Person on his head. “Good boy.” She glares at him. “Now clean up this fucking mess. And once you finish that put my five minute guitar solo back in.” “Yes, Miss Asaka.” Sound Person replied. Kat with her bodyguard leave the recording studio and walks down the halls of her enterprise. “Damn, it’s hard to find good help these days.” Kat remarked. While she and her bodyguard are walking, a young man with a female assistant who are in charge of designing a cover for Kat’s brand new CD approach them. “Miss Asaka, I got the designs you wanted to see.” Young designer said and handed Kat a folder that contain the designs. Kat stops in the middle of the hall and begins flipping through the folder. “Hate it. Hate it. Hate it.” The designer looks more disappointed with each comment that Kat made. “Hate it. Hate it.” Kat continued. “Oh God, this just sucks. I’m beginning to think that you drew these designs with your ass because this is purity shit.” “I’m sorry, Miss Asaka.” Young designer distantly said. Kat shoves the folder in the man’s chest and glares at him. “You’re fired.” “But Miss Asaka, I got a wife and three kids to support.” Young designer pleaded. “Well, your incompetent ass should’ve thought of that when you were drawing this work of total shit.” Kat coldly remarked. “Miss Asaka.” Designer’s assistant called. “What?” Kat coldly answered. Designer’s assistant hands Kat a sheet of paper that contains one design. “He forgot to show you this one.” Kat snatches it away from her and surveys it as a small smile appears on her lips. “It looks like you get to keep your job.” Designer bows repeatedly. “Thank you, Miss Asaka! Thank you!” Kat hands the designer the designer as she resumes walking down the hallway with her bodyguard. “Mistress, Global called not too long ago.” Eve said. “Oh, what did they want?” Kat questioned in a bore tone. “They just wanted to remind you that you and Heather are in action at Whiplash.” Eve explained. “Global needs to get a fucking life I mean that’s the fourth time they called telling me about that.” Kat angrily responded, she sighed with a look of boredom still on her face. “They’re already making a money costing mistake by booking us in a Dark Match, a match that’s not even going to be seen. My loyal followers should have the right to see their Queen perform and Heather too I guess. We should be booked in a match against their tag champions and then we will be filling in more seats than Dawn McFarlane, Mindy Mercer, or Shadow combine.” “I believe one of the tag team champions were in an accident.” Eve responded. “Please, I know he’s faking it because he heard that the Divine Darlings were coming and ran.” Kat remarked with a smirk. “Who am I facing again?” “Los Cazadores.” Eve replied. Kat enters the elevator with Eve. “A Mexican team, huh” Well, they’re be Mexi-can’t because they can’t defeat us since we’re just too divine. What a lousy match for us to start with.” The elevator door closes and descends down. While the elevator descends, Kat begins thinking what happened when Heather challenged her. “A match?” Kat sits on a lawn chair in a red bikini as she’s getting a nice tan from the sun. Heather stands before Kat, glaring down at her. They are in the back of the mansion where the large pool and the hot tub are. Kat lifts her head and stares at Heather through her red lens sunglasses. “You want to challenge me to a match” Little girl, what makes you think you can defeat someone like me?” “You broke Jade’s heart and I’m here to avenge her.” Heather replied. “I’m guessing you’re relative or friend of Jade’s and I’m guessing that Jade didn’t tell you what kind of woman you’re fooling around with.” Kat started, she leans her shades down and looks at her with her own brown eyes. “So, let me tell you what kind of woman you’re screwing with. I’m a down right bitch who doesn’t slap your ass, but punch your ass.” Heather stands in silence, but keeps her serious eyes on Kat. Kat moves her sunglasses back over her eyes and lays back down on the lawn chair. “So, come back in a couple of months. Better yet, come back in a couple of years and we’ll see if you’re ready to fight me then.” Kat sighs. “You’re in my sun, you stupid bitch.” Heather’s right hand trembles in anger, but she quickly clutches her hand in a tight fist, as she tosses Kat’s lawn chair over. However, Kat rolls off the chair before Heather did that. Heather charges and throws flurries of punches and kicks at Kat who easily slips through Heather’s attacks. Heather doesn’t let up on her assault, as she begins moving forward while she throwing punches and kicks at Kat. Kat’s back is against the wall. Heather goes in for a thrust kick to Kat’s face, but Kat drops down in a split second. “I felt the wind of that.” Kat remarked with an amusing smirk. Heather looks surprise. “What?” Kat strikes Heather in the mid-section with a crouching kick that’s pack with some power. Heather stumbles back on her feet. “This has been fun, but it’s time to finish it.” Kat said. Kat does a flip as she briefly rests her feet on the wall that’s behind her before she presses off and connects with a modify version of the Burning Slash Attack on Heather. Heather’s eyes are open wide as they’re losing color to them. Kat is in crouching position behind Heather, but she stands up with a smirk on her face. “And that’s that.” Kat commented. Kat makes a curious sound when Heather who is slowly losing consciousness rests her hand on Kat’s shoulder. Kat looks surprise. “What” You’re still conscious. That’s an impossible.” “I’ll get you back.” Heather replied in a very weak tone. Heather finally loses consciousness as she falls and makes a big splash in her pool. Kat stares down at the pool in silence as she watches Heather sink further down the pool. Another splash is made before Kat’s thoughts of the past are broken with the slight jerk of the elevator stopping. Kat and her bodyguard exit the elevator and disappear on the new floor. At Katsumi’s grand mansion in Beverly Hills, Heather is in Katsumi personal gym working on the Tony Little Gazelle Power Plus while listening fast pace music. While she exercises on the machine, she starts thinking back to what happened after her bout with Kat. Heather slowly wakes up in a large bedroom as she sits up in the bed, but she winces and favors her mid-section where Kat have struck with that monster move, the Burning Slash Attack. She looks around the room with a look of curiosity. “Where am I?” Heather asked herself. She notices that on one side of the room there are many tall stacks of boxes. Heather sharpens her eyes in seriousness when the bedroom door opens, but her facial expression changes from a serious one to a curious one when a maid enters her room. The maid approaches Heather with a tray that have a delicious breakfast meal on it. “Good morning, Miss Burnside.” Maid politely said. “Uh, good morning.” Heather hesitatingly responded. The maid rests tray on Heather’s lap and smiles contently at her. Heather stares at the maid curiously. “Excuse me, but where am I?” “You’re in your new room, Miss.” Maid answered, she approached the window and pulled the curtains letting the bright morning sunlight. “My new room?” Heather questioned. “What are you talking about?” “Mistress Katsumi has decided to allow you to move in with her at her mansion. The maid staff had the courtesy to bring all of your belongings from your mansion at Santa Monica here.” Maid explained. “What!”” Heather shouted. “Why!”” Heather winces and whimpers in pain as she favors her mid-section. Maid turns around and stares at Heather. “Mistress Katsumi said that you wouldn’t be able to move around a lot after being at the receiving end of her move. She figured that bringing your stuff here would make your stay here more comfortable.” “I don’t want to be here.” Heather said in disgust. “I don’t want to be in the same house with the woman who broke Jade’s heart.” “Mistress knew you would say something like that, so she told me to tell you that you have option to leave when your are all better.” Maid replied. “Now please eat your breakfast, it’ll make you feel better.” Heather glares down at her breakfast. “Not to worry, the food isn’t poisonous.” Maid commented. “It appears that Mistress is interested in you.” Heather makes a curious sound and stares at the maid. “What gives you that idea?” “Who do you think pulled you out of the water when you lost consciousness?” Maid responded. Heather looks surprise. Maid heads to the bedroom door. “Why does she interest in me?” Heather questioned. Maid stops in the open door with her back turn on Heather.?I’m not really too sure, but she told me that you had a lot of spunk and she liked that.” Maid replied. “I hope you have an enjoyable breakfast, Miss.” Heather sees the maid is about to leave her new bedroom. “Wait.” Heather stares at the maid. “What is your name?” Maid turns around and looks at Heather with a gentle smile. “My name is Cookie. I’m the Head Maid here. Now please eat, Miss.” Cookie leaves Heather’s new bedroom as she closes the door behind her. Heather sits in bed in confusion while she stares down at her delicious food. Three weeks later, Heather stands before the bed doing a couple of stretches by bending down and back as she feels her mid-section area is completely better now. While she stretches, she looks at TV. However, she stops midway in her stretching exercises when a promotion to Global Wrestling is shown. Heather watches the promotion diligently as an odd serious look with a smirk appears on her face. At Katsumi’s private dojo that’s behind her mansion, Kat is sparring with Eve in her red ninja attire. Kat glances at the dojo’s entrance and sees Heather standing there. “I see that you’re feeling better.” “I challenge you.” Heather said. Kat stops sparring with Eve and stares at Heather with both hands on her hips. “Are you kidding me” You still pissed about me breaking Jade’s heart. She should be the avenging her own broken heart not you. And besides you just got the ability to move again and now you want to fuck it all up by challenging me again. Haven’t your English ass learned your lesson the last time we faced each other? You can’t beat me.” “Do you accept my challenge or not?” Heather questioned impatiently. Kat sighs. “Very well.” “But there’s a catch this time.” Heather said. “I’m not challenging to avenge Jade.” Heather started. “I’m facing you because I want to. Now I win this fight, then you and I will form a tag team in Global Wrestling.” Kat scoffs. “I don’t do tag teams. I’m a solo star.” “Well, you’re just going to have to defeat me then, now won’t you?” Heather remarked with a smirk. Kat tilts her head and glares at Heather. “And what do I get if I win?” “I won’t tag with you and I will never bring up Jade again.” Heather responded. Kat lets out a small laugh. “I’ll take that bet.” “Good.” Heather replied. Heather’s thoughts of the past are broken when Cookie enters her Mistress? personal gym. “Mistress is home.” Cookie softly said. Heather nods. At the Great Hall of Kat’s mansion, Kat and her bodyguard enter through the front door. Kat stretches. “I had a long day of being around idiots. I just want to take a lap around the pool in the nude and call it a day.” “That sounds fun.” Heather remarked. Kat sees Heather walk out the door that leads to Kat’s personal gym. Kat walks up to Heather. “How was your day?” “It was good.” Heather replied. “What were you up to today?” Kat questioned. “Just training for our match and thinking about how we first met.” Heather said. Kat lets out a small laugh. “I was thinking the same thing for some reason.” “Well, don’t forget that our match is soon, partner.” Heather said. “Yeah, yeah, I know.” Kat responded in a tone of boredom. “Partner’I still can’t believe we’re a tag team.” Heather smirks. “Well, you lost my challenge, remember.” “The last time I remember your challenge ended in a draw, but since you started bitching and moaning about it I decided compromise. You stop talking about Jade, and then we will tag up for Global.” Kat explained. “Ok, it was a draw, but I didn’t bitch and moan about it.” Heather replied. “Yes, your English ass did.” Kat said under her breath. Heather glares at Kat. “No, I didn’t.” “Mistress.” Cookie called out. Kat, Heather, and Eve turn their attention to Cookie who approaches them with an envelope in her left hand. “What is it, Cookie?” Kat questioned. Cookie gives Kat the envelope. “This came for you this morning.” Kat examines the envelope and sees that the front is blank. She flips it over and looks surprise at the sight of the red cherry blossom stamp that is stamp of the Asaka Ninja Clan. Heather stares at Kat. “What’s wrong?” Kat closes her eyes and sighs with a disgust expression on her face. “Nothing.” Heather, Eve, and Cookie watch Kat leave them with envelope in hand, as the scene fades black. ==To Be Continued==
