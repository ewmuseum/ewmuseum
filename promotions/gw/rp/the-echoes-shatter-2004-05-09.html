The Echoes Shatter

9 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/09/04 Insight. I sit in this chair rocking back and forth… the fan blows the air that keeps me from overheating on this hot spring night. I’ve been a man known to do many things to other people. That list has grown to be paranoia in my mind. It’s become something I think of every time I close my eyes. That’s why when I’m in the ring, and I’m knocked down, I get right back up. Those images have become a fuel of hate that boils in me and keeps the motor in my head working. I recently lost my Global Title… The man who won it was Hessian of Rush Pro Wrestling. A man whose hideousness and grotesqueness makes him the ‘Goliath’ he is. Standing 7’2” and weighing over three hundred pounds, I knew going in I was going to have a challenge. When Hessian touched me though, evil became too much… I was disrupted within a realm of complete and utter hell. Fire’s burned the skin of people around me without even being hot. Pigs of a farm hung from trees and were slaughtered by the hands of a man with a chainsaw. Thousands of people melting away with screams of agony surging as their last breathes were taken away from them. I’d entered a mind that was so strongly built around this hate that my own body could not handle it. That’s when my body gave in, and my eyes closed, and people believed, that I, Jesse Jamester had been eliminated from the match. However that wasn’t the case. I awoken with that evil in my fingertips, and with all that hate I took it to the men in that match. But I couldn’t control the hate that I had in me, and so my decisions became based on rage rather than smarts. That is why I lost my Global Title… Not because a man named Hessian was more dominant, but because my mind gave into the evil he produced. Yet now, I sit here wondering, were those things I saw, those hallucinations, were they memories, or were they thoughts… I had been mislead in life so much, but now the picture was beginning to come clear. Hate… was inevitable… to be beaten. Story Begins. It was just another normal day. Jesse was driving down the country roads in his black dodge truck. The dirt roads were so great in Texas, because one it contained no people on them, and two you could go as fast as you wanted to without being caught. These two things Jesse loved most, for his prison life just ended a week ago and since then he’s been free. Free… a word that meant so very much to him, and yet was still just a word. The truck was a bullet, and Jesse was the person who pulled the trigger. Hitting bumps, flying over dirt roads, Jesse was free, and so he wanted to live that way. The road came to a stop sign, in the middle of nowhere, and Jesse sat their staring at the stop sign perplexed by it’s meaning. What did someone expect, a large traffic jam in the middle of the back roads like this one? Come on this was Texas, not New York. Jesse stepped on the gas and peeled out as he headed on down the road. Ah the fresh air, the beautiful sunset, and best of all… the empty roads. WHAM!!! Jesse hit something and he instantly hit the brakes. Whatever it was sent his truck for a spin and he barely kept control. He skidded to a stop as his trucks back tires went down into a ditch, and he was stuck. Hopping out the front seat his eyes gazed at his bumper as he approached it. The whole left side of it was covered in blood, and Jesse could only pray it wasn’t what he thought. Walking the distance the truck skid, about fifteen feet is what could be estimated from the skid marks on the dirt road, Jesse came to the object he hit. Indeed it was not what he thought it was, it was much worse. Their on the dirt road was a baby cow, one that had been born but not raised properly. From the tags on it’s ears, it was to be eaten at a young age, and most likely ran off because of it’s untamed naturist ways. Jesse picked the dead cattle up and carried it to his truck where he placed it in the bed. The cattle laid motionless, definitely dead in appearance, and all Jesse could think was how to get it somewhere where he could get rid of it. Getting back in the truck he suddenly realized his tires were in the ditch. Putting the truck in gear he hit the pedal and tried to power his way out, when that didn’t work, he backed up in the ditch and drove the length of the ditch until it leveled out. The dust blowing into the air as Jesse’s truck blew down the dirt road in ablaze. Home Again. The door flings open and Jesse stomps his way in with the dead cattle offspring over his shoulders. He lays it on the table and stares at it. The coat of hair it wears stained by blood that was caused due to Jesse’s driving. One hit is all it takes. Wham, they’re down and out. A finisher is much the same as hitting a cow with your truck, either way you know they’re not getting up anytime soon. Juvian was lucky at Wrestlecade. He was lucky he survived. Me being so damn tired from training for the Global Title match, then for the match with Juvian, I’d have no problem… I guess life makes challenges for a reason. Too bad this next one isn’t as hard on me as it is on Ramorez. Grabbing the knife he stands there eyeing the creature down before jabbing the belly of the cattle. It instantly awakens and the calf begins to move. His eyes widen as the calf flips around and falls off the table where it desperately runs in an attempt to get away. Legs stumbling as it runs it heads up the stairs, but Jesse doesn’t chase it. The prey will always run… It’s nature. When it does though, it doesn’t think fast enough to realize where it is going. Just as Juvian Ramorez will do come Evolution. He will not realize where he’s going when he’s in the ring. As so many others have they worry about the little things. I know I’m not the greatest wrestler by far, but when I see these actions repeat, I can’t help but notice. It’s logic. People will run when they are afraid, and in this case I’m a very scary guy. Looking around he grabs a washrag and cleans his hands and the table, but then realizes the floor has a trail of blood that the calf left when it ran. Grabbing his shotgun out of his closet he walks up the steps following the trail of blood. He turns through and goes down the hallway that leads to his bedroom. Step by step… I edge closer to ending the games. No longer is this going to be fun and games, oh no, I’m definitely a changed man. The Jackal has come back home… and he’s feasting on the first thing he sees, new blood. Jesse walks in and points the gun to the corner where he sees the calf, pulling the trigger the calf moves, allowing the bullets to dismantle the dresser and put a hole through it. The calf jumps on the bed and as Jesse sees the blood trickling from underneath it he knows he has to put it out of it’s misery before it suffers too much. With one hand he points the gun straight in the calf’s face, he closes his eyes and pulls the trigger. … … … The white covers of the bed look to be spray-painted, as does the rest of the room behind the calf. The walls are splattered with the blood, leaving the room look to be a murder scene. Grabbing the sheets he forms a bag with the dead calf in it, and heaves it over his shoulders walking down the steps. This… is what will become of Juvian after our match. I’ll have soiled his sweat, blood, and tears into the canvas before I pick him up and carry him to his disposal of Global Wrestling. Then after… will mark the beginning of hell’s new advocate, Jesse Jamester! Truth. Seeing those images that came to my mind when I touched Hessian made me think. What the hell was going on? I had just killed this young calf. Instead of leaving it on the side of the road as any normal person I took it home, tried to butcher it, and then blew it’s brains all over my bedroom walls. That room… it wasn’t the first time I had saw it. In my head when Hessian touched me, that same room with the blood, and the sheets covered in blood… was it me who I saw in those pictures? Could it really have been me? After all I had touched what was certainly the closest thing to Satan. I didn’t want to believe it, me, becoming like Hessian. A man with no heart, no soul, just the focus of getting done what was needed to be done, to survive. Sure it sounded like a great job description, hell I wouldn’t mind winning everything I focused my mind on, but to kill people, to see the world be shattered like a glass window so I could succeed? I doubt I could ever handle that. It wasn’t be, never would be, or so I thought. That wasn’t all I had seen… Those people who I had saw burning alive, those thousands of people… they were fans. They were the fans of Global Wrestling… Was this an omen? Was this a premonition? Could I really be seeing the future I asked myself? Shaking my head of the nonsense I tried to carry on the day, but it sat in the back of my mind dwelling up into something it shouldn’t have. It became too much so I left the house to clear my head. *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** ’oh father, please forgive me, for I have sinned.’ The confession booth was so dark you couldn’t see a thing, but the sound of the father clearing his throat gave me insurance that somebody was there, listening to me. ’What has my child done that would draw him to confession this late, may I ask?’ I didn’t want to say, for the words on my tongue were too bitter to be tasted at such a time like this. Yet I had to, for the insides of me were being eaten away over such stupidity as this. I cleared my throat and looked down to where my feet should have been, but were lost in the darkness. ’Father… I’ve become a monster. I touched Satan with my own two hands and he has scorched me with his hatred. I’m scarred father… how can you help me? The father did not answer right away. For to him this had to be a large task to manage. Who could have all the answers to life? Nobody, and it made common sense when the father kept opening his mouth to speak, but sighing in disapproval of his own words. He was as unsure as I was, but I knew he had something in him that would comfort me. ’Son… you must fight the devil. Fight the hatred that he has unleashed in you, for it’ll be the only way you can stop it from over coming you. I can’t tell you anymore than that my sons… but remember, fight the evil, and you’ll become stronger than it. Do not give up on yourself… do not.’ I sat their wondering even after the father had left the booth. Was I to fight Hessian? Or was I to fight all evil I saw? Juvian Ramorez, Takahashi Jetsui, Sage Sabbot, Maverick Reno, all of these people were the epitome of what made evil still exist in Global Wrestling. Did the father want me to fight them? Hours passed… the church was open twenty-four-seven, so I was okay, but in that time I sat their deeply thinking, and then I realized, it didn’t matter what evil I battled, because no matter who I faced that lurked with that ruthless streak in them, was a step in the right direction. I stepped out of the confessional booth, hands at my side and I walked up to the cross and bowed before doing the gesture of prayer and forgiveness. ’God, please forgive me, for Juvian Ramorez will soon be my potential sin. Have mercy on his soul… for evil too deserves forgiveness.’ With that Jesse stands up from his kneeling position and nods at the cross before walking gracefully from the church in quest of his next step of life, the fighting of this evil he pictures in his mind… and the evil that will consume him if he’s not successful. … … …
