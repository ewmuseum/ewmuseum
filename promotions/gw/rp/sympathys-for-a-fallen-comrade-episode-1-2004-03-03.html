Sympathys for a Fallen Comrade (Episode 1)

3 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3.3.04 'For the Angel of Death spread his wings on the blast, And breathed in the face of the foe as he pass'd; And the eyes of the sleepers wax'd deadly and chill, And their hearts but once heaved, and for ever grew still!' -George Gordon Byron 'PLEASE DON'T KILL ME!' The voice barely rose from a stocky man with brown hair stained crimson. He had been stripped naked, covered in lye, and strapped to a large wooden plank that was suspended by metal wires above a eight by eight glass cylinder. Inside of this cylinder, water had been deposited till the six foot mark. He's jerking around and squirming on the plan, his hands and feet securely fasted by razor wire; although holding him tightly, it's also cutting and ripping at his flesh with every moments passing. He jerks his feet up, the blood dripping from about his ankles and draining down his paling foot and then dropping, like a bomb, into the water. The water has been turning a tint or red slowly over the past few hours. The blood continues, a never ending stream of crimson life pouring from the veins of the sacrificial offering hanging above. The lye mixes in with the blood and then into the wounds caused by the razor wire, causing even more of a burning sensation. Footsteps are heard below, the raspy voice of this stocky man continues, pleading for mercy. The footsteps stop and a voice is heard from below. 'Why wasn't razor wire put around his throat?' Another voice comes in from behind the man in suspension. 'Didn't want him to die before you got here. We'll do that before we drop 'em.' Reno nods and calls up towards the near-lifeless body. 'So how's it hanging, Mr. Downs?' There is of course no response, the man who we can only assume as Sam Downs, has begun to lose consciousness. Reno claps his hands rapidly. 'Mr. Downs, it's not polite to ignore someone when they're talking to you!' A very weak voice can be heard, responding to Reno. 'I... please... don't.' 'Sorry? Can't quite hear what you're saying.' The man struggles and groans, trying his best to speak louder so that Reno might hear him. 'Please don't... don't kill me. I'll do anything.' Reno sighs and walks away, his eyes downcast. 'Ah, you're begging. Predictable...' Reno picks up a piece of paper from a nearby table, his eyes scanning it before he speaks again. 'So, answer the question. Did you and Markus tell anyone about us? And I advise you to answer and to tell the truth; it could mean the difference between life and death.' The man tenses again, the pain he's in purely excruciating. He tries to shift to aleviate the pain... then clenches his eyes. 'You killed him too...' Reno shakes his head no. 'No, he and his wife are living in The Barbados now. I made sure they were taken care of and the same can happen to you.' The look on Sam's face is a sign of hope; a sign of relief. 'No, Mr. Reno. I swear... we never sold your information or showed it to anyone else. Now please. I beg; let me go!' Reno nods a bit and smiles before placing the paper down and turning. He walks up a steel staircase, his eyes downcast. He gets to the top platform and turns, looking directly at Sam so that they may see one another. Reno nods and smiles. 'The truth shall set you free.' Reno picks up a large knife and slashes a set of ropes, one after the other. The plank shakes and then drops, plummeting into the cylinder below. Blood tints and taints the already impure water, coloring it an even darker shade of red. Sam Down jerks around inside, the lye and water massively burning his body. He thrashes about for just a moment... until a sudden calmness overcomes the water. Reno has already begun his descent to the floor of the room now and approaches the cylinder. The face of Downs is pressed to the side of the glass, both severely burned and stained in a watered down version of his own blood. Reno nods and glances to his side... 'Brendan, clean this up. I have to get to a meeting.' With this Reno leaves the room quickly and quietly, taking the beautifully ornate elevator out of the dungeon type area of the Echelon's headquarters. I am the angel of death. Blackness has tainted my heart and my soul, my mind scarred with years of betrayal and hatred. My actions full of scorn and violence. I am the black angel of death - the winged arch angel, delivering many to their graves. My life is no longer one full of just actions and servitude to the morals that I once held so high; vengeance and hatred guide my life now. Those who stand in my way will pay in such ways that even Satan's angels are sympathetic. I carry the sickle of death; my hands are black and crimson red, covered in the blood of my enemies and the ash of my soul. Saturday 28th, 2004 - Approximately 5:00pm New York City, New York - Office of Maverick Reno Downtown New York City. Buildings circle the area, creating a maze of perpetual Babylonian towers. We pan around slowly until we stop, focusing on one building. The building stands at twenty-seven stories. Not an architectural marvel in it's height alone, but when you consider that it's constructed of pure tinted glass, black marble, and steel, it becomes quite the spectacle. Archways complete with Latin markings are placed on all eight sides of the building. A single gargoyle, large and armored, is positioned at the height of each archway, guarding the building. The large blackened glass doors, framed in blackened silver slide to the sides with authorization from inside. This is only one of two entrances however and this is perhaps the easier of the two. The other option is via the below ground parking deck. The entrance to this is fairly easy to gain access to however getting past the two-inch thick sliding grate that closes the gate off from the world above is another. Even past this, a guard post and then another solid concrete wall with but one way through: A large pair of sliding doors that require identification card swipe, and corresponding approval from the sentry on duty. Once inside, half-hour checks are performed by the highly trained security and bodyguard force that is at the Echelon's command. Upon entering via the front sliding glass doors, the beauty of modern architecture truly comes into play. Elegance and security meet, both finding an apex within this very building. In the center, a large semi-circular desk is positioned. At the head of this desk, a man in a black suit who is obviously one of the Echelon's higher ranking security officers. To his side, a young woman handles the desk and all procedures there of with a staff of two other young men, all three dressed in a relaxed business style. Lining the room, black obelisks are in place, complete with both Latin and Japanese scriptures scrolling them ornately; they connect to the underside of the black marble ceiling that also works as the floor of the wrap-around interior balcony. The flood of the room is a simple black material, keeping the motif. In the center, the black seems to mix with and then become fully black and white marble, making a huge circular area in the center before the desk. Inside of this, a huge impression of the TUE Coat of Arms can be seen. Behind the desk, a large fountain, complete with miniature versions of the giant obelisks that hold up the balcony. Behind this, an elevator with doors of tempered black glass complete with Kanji scrolling up and down each side and an Echelon guard at each side. Completing the room, a pair of black marble and swept steel spiral staircases lift from the floor and ascend to the heavens of the cathedral like ceiling of the first floor. The stairs finally connect to the balcony level. The rounded balcony encircles the entire lower room, the floor of black marble with blackened glass coming up from the sides to a height of four feet and then topped by a steel bar, acting as a barrier for the balcony. Five guards are at all times positioned on the balcony. Perhaps the most beautiful artifact of this room is on this floor; a mammoth stained glass window complete with both he Echelon's coat of arms, Latin underneath, and kanji scrolling the sides, as well as the infamous ankh of Reno. Through the window, you can see the stone gargoyle positioned perfectly in the center atop it's arch, guarding the foremost part of the building. The ceiling of both of these rooms is covered in a large black cloth, adorned in many small phrases and scrolls in both Latin and Japanese. In the center, the coat of arms of The Echelon. Naria Xiou steps into view, dressed in a black business suit, a pair of high heels, a black choker, and a pair of reading glasses. Her hair is loose about her head, hanging down to her jaw line, streaks of purple from down from the center of her head to the tips. Aside her, a young man in a retail brown suit. He wears a GW-Press nametag and carries a notepad in one hand. In his jacket pocket, a tape recorder has been place. 'Congratulations, Mr. Chambers. You're the first person to be allowed access to the The Echelon Tower who has not had direct ties with the Echelon.' Tyler nods and glances about the large lobby, his eyes focusing on all of the minute and detailed designs of the room; a truly perfect modern mixture of business and the darker side of contemporary. He turns to Naria. 'So we're on our way to meet with Mr. Reno, yes? He did finally agree to an interview.' Naria simply nods as the two walk towards the desk. The man in the black suit smiles at her and extends his hand. She hands him her ID and then snatches the GW-Press ID off of Chambers coat. She hands both to him and he scans his quickly and swipes hers. He hands them back and both are on their way, heading for the elevator. Once one boards the elevator, accompanied by a single armed guard who remains inside, and under constant surveillance, they ascend past this beautifully intricate antechamber towards the levels above it, entering the true inner sanctum of The Echelon itself. On the first floor that our transparent yet highly secure transport to the Heavens travels past, houses a training center and headquarters for the security of the Echelon. On this floor, The Echelon Tower houses a state of the art security system that monitors the tower itself, the parking garage below it, the block and grounds that the building consumes, as well as all communications in and out of the building. With advanced motion and heat detection, even the ventilation shafts are protected from invasion; even if that invasion is from insects of any variety. But then the question is proposed... 'Why so many security guards with a system so advanced?' Naria was not one to make idle chat and answering his questions, she was not paid nor did she want to do. However, the answer is simple. ''Never trust a computer you can't throw out a window.' Steve Wozniak said that. Our computers, massed together, weigh approximately 1.12 tons. I don't know about John, Deac, or anyone else in the Echelon, but I can't quite pick that up. All semantics aside, a man can see, a man can know. Can they be fooled? Yes, they can be. Is it likely? It depends on the person. But when you cross advanced technological security with the in-depth verification of a well educated and trained security officer, it's hard to have a foul up. But they happen... that's why man invented the gun.' Chambers is silent and tries not to wear his concern for her lack of consideration for human life on his face; he fails. Nothing in life is fool proof. Hence why the Echelon is void of fools. The fourth level is past; a level covered in suites and accommodations for the many overnight security personnel. The fifth through the tenth, eleventh through the twentieth are covered in offices and business centers, complete with yes-men, no-men, and your occasional business tycoon. Above this, the many floors that make up the main offices and quarters of the Echelon. The twenty-first floor; a circular hallway with the elevator at one end, a large stone carving of the coat of arms at the other; nothing but suites line the sides of the beautiful building facing in every direction. The floor is of black marble, the doors of a heavy oak, and the walls, covered in tapestries. Golden door handles and accents mark the area, giving it that touch of elegance that it has earned. Another level; the twenty-second floor. As the elevator moves past this, the view is amazing. The walls come to approximately twenty feet away from the elevator, the floors covered in red walks complete with lines of text in many languages on each one. The walls appear to be solid marble with cherry finished oak edging; carved into the wood, Latin and Japanese scrolls and phrases. Along the semi-circular wall also hang portraits of every past member of the Echelon. Starting with Maverick Reno on the far left followed by Sean Price, Deacon Holmes, John Zero, Tyler Pierce, Leinad Soma, Tony Stecchino, Ichiro Nakazura, Jushin Nakazura, Jason Hughes, Ice, Naria Xiou, Gideon Fontaine, Neil Fracas, and Ed Gesumaria. A red sash hangs below every active member, and a golden light extends above each. The room is very dark however and certain lights have been extinguished. Those that have been extinguished can be guessed: Ichiro Nakazura, Jushin Nakazura, Tony Stecchino, and Ice. Large doors made of oak rest closed, thick and heavy. Carvings adorn the beautiful wood. Naria's voice breaks the silence. 'I've been told to explain a few of the things you are about to see. This is the Consiliu's Chambers; where our current members adjourn for conference. These walls are soundproof and inside, every bit of modern technology you can think of. They have satellite connections to databases all over the world including a continuous link with Soma Corp. I'm also to tell you that John Zero, Deacon Holmes, Tyler Pierce, Jason Hughes, and the rest of the Core TUE faction are in the chambers discussion upcoming business issues. Hughes since his time in the Global Arena has expanded TUE's branches into the Core federation. He's also taken up heading with one of our Asian subsidiaries.' Tyler shakes his head a bit and glances down, taking a few notes. Naria seems completely uninterested, her eyes still aimed directly ahead of them. The elevator continues for a moment to reveal the twenty-third floor. The floor itself appears normal enough, complete with marble pillars and floors. Doors line a dark hallway, lit only by dim structures tucked to the sides. 'These are the offices of the Echelon members; nothing of real interest here.' The elevator continues on it's ascent, Tyler thinking that it's taking forever. The twenty-fourth floor. On this floor, the walls are circular, the farthest point being the entrance to the large room a good thirty feet away from the elevator. The ends of the walls move closer towards the elevator's wall. The walls are white and simple, although an ornate tapestry is strung about on either side of the door, the Coat of arms of the Echelon visible on both. 'And this is the Echelon's personal gym; it's state of the art and houses every bit of equipment they will should ever need. Again, not much interest here. The next floor houses the office of the Echelon's founder as well as two security offices.' The elevator slows to a stop, finally. The tempered black glass doors slide open and Tyler Chambers steps out, his eyes glancing around him, taking in his surroundings. The walls are black marble with light fixtures every four feet giving off just enough light to keep it from being considered 'dark' in this area. The walls round at each side, columns built into the sides of the walls. The walls bend into a short hallway. Slowly Naria Xiou and Tyler Chambers make their way down the hallway; the crest of the Echelon has been carved into the wall between most of the columns. In one area, the crests are replaced by single oak doors. On each door there is a word marked 'security.' The two continue towards the large double doors at the end of the hallway. Naria Xiou motions for Tyler to stop; he does so, not quite ready to rebel in this modern day fortress. Two guards have stepped in behind him, obviously planned to assist in his escort while moving about the floors; two others had accompanied him on the first floor. Naria taps on the door and then steps in, a muffled voice heard from the other side. The door is cracked only enough for her to step through and she does so. There Tyler stands, uncomfortably waiting for his interview, assigned to him by Global Wrestling themselves. He stands in a figurative Lion's den. Fear of breathing arises, his eyes searching the room. Time passes... two minutes and then three. four minutes pass. five minutes. Finally, Naria reappears and opens one side of the door, allowing Tyler entrance into the room. 'Mr. Reno will see you now, Mr. chambers.' Tyler walks away from the guards and into the luxurious office that matches the motif of the remainder of the building. Columns circle the desk, six in all. A large glass window is in place behind Reno and a circular exposure in the center of the ceiling allows light in from a sky-light two floors up. Reno is seated at his desk, his eyes focusing on Chambers. 'Well take your seat. I'd like to get this over with as quickly as possible.' Tyler nods and walks over, taking a seat in a large plush leather chair. He places his tape recorder on Reno's desk and pulls out his notebook. He speaks. 'It's an honor to be here with you today, Mr. Reno. On behalf of the Globa...' 'Excuse me, are you going to blow smoke up my ass or interview me? You and I both know that I couldn't care less for your flattery or your gratitude. I couldn't care less for you for that matter.' '...er, right. My first question. Why have you returned to Global Wrestling after such a long hiatus?' 'The answer is simple; the timing. I've returned to reclaim what is mine, what Salaok begun to take from me last year. To show the world that I'm no longer a puppet.' 'You were never a puppet, Mr. Reno.' Reno's eyes shoot up, locking to Tyler’s. He sits back, extremely uncomfortable now. 'Why this change? Why form The Echelon after so many years of ... er.' 'Of what, Mr. Chambers? Say it.' 'Of...' 'I believe you're looking for 'of force-feeding justice to a world gone mad; battling and fighting for something long since passed.' Or how about this one; fighting for a belief that is no longer believed in. I saw everything I fought for get washed away; I saw the world going to shit and I was the proverbial toilet. I was taking the brunt of it and why, Mr. Chambers? Why? I'll tell you why. Because I was doing everything in my power to make sure that justice was served.' Tyler nods. 'But that's the right...' 'NO! Don't you tell me that, Chambers. You don't have a clue as to what I went through; the Hell's I've seen. I have scars on top of scars from the many battles I've fought, the many epic wars I've led. What did it get me? It got me a pink slip from Global Inc. It got me screwed... and not one person did a damned thing about it. Not one person I stood up for stood up after I had been removed and fought for me; not one person returned any of their gratitude. So you take your 'right' and your 'wrong' and you feed it to someone else; go sell crazy somewhere else, we're all stocked up here.' Tyler sits back in his seat, truly feeling intimidated by the passion in Reno's voice, the power behind his words and his beliefs. 'Call it dementia if you'd like, Tyler. I know that speculations are being made as to whether or not I have lost my edge. I can tell you this; I have not. The only thing that I have lost is patience, my moral virtue? Yes, you could say I've lost it. Although, I'd say it abandoned me along with everything else. I decided that my life's work, my pursuits had been all in vain and that it was time to do something; to create a force powerful enough to bring Global to it's knees, shaking and quivering behind its fabled heroes. I know these heroes... they think that they can save the world; that they are the unmovable force of justice standing before an army of winged arch angels. They are wrong, Mr. Chambers. They are wrong indeed. Hatred will consume them as it has me; they will feel the torment, the suffering that I have felt at the hands of those they stand against. They stand by their morals and their virtue, but when you fight with rules against a man or group who have none, then you are fighting a handicapped battle. You are sentencing yourself to execution.' Tyler is writing frantically, his mind reeling with the words that Maverick Reno has been giving, simply ranting them out as if he's needed this time or opportunity to let it all go. 'The only dementia present in me is that of ignorance but that has long since faded and it remains a part of my past. An ignorance towards the truth of the world, that no matter how much you do, how much you try, you will always be scorned by those you fight for. I stood in flames for so many, and so many times I was burnt that I would grit my teeth and bear it, letting the pain feed my thirst for justice. All the while, those I fought for fanned the flames! No, I am plagued with dementia - I have finally woken up to realism, to the cold hard truth that Hell is not a story created to scare the masses into leading a good life, NO! It is true and it is here today. We live Hell every single day of our lives.' Tyler continues writing. He notes a pause in Maverick rant and lifts his head, his eyes locking to Reno. He speaks up, asking his second question. 'And what of Scott Pierce? Does his loss account to hatred as well? Or simply to men like yourself who have seen the 'light?'' Silence fills the room, first normally and then awkwardly. Reno stares down and then lifts his eyes, glaring directly through Tyler Chambers. He leans in slowly, his mouth forming the first words of his sentence. 'What you do is what you get in return; Scott was not my fault! His wife's death was not my fault! I am not to blame for that and no one, NO ONE, will pin it on me! Do you understand me?' Tyler's nerve grew steadily before he spoke, his words falling into line like water droplets hitting a flower. Perfection... expectation... planning. Fucking yourself. 'But technically, it is attributed to both Johnny Dollar and by him, Tony Stecchino. Stecchino in his last days worked for you in the Echelon; isn't this similar to condoning his crimes?' Silence again, but this time, awkward to begin with. Tension grows steadily in the room, heat building about the walls and anger flooding the mind of Maverick Reno. His eyes grow colder and distant, his mind losing itself in the many voices and sounds of his life; images and memories flood his mind. December 2nd, 2002 It had been a long flight from London, Ontario, Canada. The airport in Los Angeles was hell and so was getting a taxi from LAX to Mount Sinai Hospital. That's where the future lie, waiting in a bed, hoping and praying for mercy in the form of death. Maverick Reno sits in the back of the taxi, his mind filtering over the news footage of the last few days. Flashes of headlines and news stories filtering and blurring together. Los Angeles family slaughtered Wife and children brutally murdered Martial Arts trainer severely wounded; he survives his wife and children after a horrendous murder. Cowardice hidden inside brutality: The act of a mad man. John 'Dollar' Russell questioned in connection with Pierce family murder. Could his connection to Stecchino family play a key role? Scott Pierce has survived preliminary surgeries. Police say he watched his family die, one by one! Cult connections? Friends embrace Pierce as he mourns for his family. Funeral for Alicia Pierce and her children, Brittany and Adam, to be held on Monday. Father, Scott Pierce, may not be able to leave the hospital by then. John Russell released after bail of 22 million is paid. Anthony Stecchino fronts 22 million for Russell! Stecchino family under investigation! John Russell is acquitted; fruit of the poisoned tree clause? Suddenly, we flash back to the present. Maverick Reno's eyes lift slowly, taking in all of Tyler that he can in one glance. 'What happened to Alicia was not my fault. I tried to defend him, and them, but I couldn't; I didn't fail! I simply had no idea.' 'But what of your hir...' 'Get the fuck out of my office, Chambers.' Tension is abundant. Reno is barely able to keep from standing, his posture extremely stressful, his eyes extremely focused yet distant and cold. Chambers stands and Naria Xiou walks into the office. Two guards are with her. Suddenly they both grab Chambers and drag him from the office. Naria slowly approaches Maverick as the doors close. She places her hand on his shoulder. He speaks, snapping violently. 'Get the fuck out. EVERYONE OUT!' Naria jumps, her eyes showing her confusion, her perplexion. She takes a step back and turns, making her way out of the office, composing herself before stepping into the hallway. Maverick Reno sits at his desk, fuming and thinking, mulling through the thoughts in his mind. His thoughts are cluttered and severe, all loud and stressed. Dementia perhaps is the term after all... Reno drops his head into his hands, the scene fading to black. 'This is the way the world ends This is the way the world ends This is the way the world ends Not with a bang but a whimper.' -T. S. Eliot, Hollow Men, The I'm Coming For you
