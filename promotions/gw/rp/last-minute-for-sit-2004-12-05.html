Last minute for SiT

5 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.5.04 The world is a smoking gun that is loaded. Soon it's going to blow you away. -- Pennywise ---------- Nobody saw it coming. Nobody, not even me. No, I don't mean the attack on Jesse Jamester, or the fact that I stood up to the Elite and they didn't do a damn thing. Not even the way Salaok and I showed the strength of our alliance when we painted Logan Creed blue and nobody could stop us. I'm simply talking about Johnny Cruse coming back. What the fuck is wrong with you, don't you realize a guy has a life outside of what's shown on GW television? Shit, if you only knew. Anyway, Cruse and I got the gym cleaned up. Finally, that place is in a condition so that it can be used to train. Bout damn time. Right now, I'm on my way to my friend Troy's house to work on my project car. Many people now, especially teens and 20somethings, are catching on to the 'custom compact' market. The majority of these 'tuners' drive the biggest piece of shit cars they can find, only to try to make them pretty. Little do most people realize, you can't polish a turd. Thats why I picked a car that was ahead of its time. A car that shoved the engine forward, created a large cabin complete with four doors, and a body line that is low and flowing at the front, rising to a steep rear. Top this all off with a wide stance, and you've got yourself the making ofa great car. I picked up a 95 Dodge Stratus for a few thousand dollars. Blown headgasket, clogged exhaust system, a thrown wheel bearing, and the brakes are squeakier than . For now, its engine is laying on the floor of Troy's garage, with the head and the block separated. Last night we were up until 2am trying to yank the intake manifold off. I, however, am in my Vette doing 80 down the highway tryin to get there before he starts so he cant get on to me for being late--again. Yesterday I showed up about an hour after I was supposed to. My reason was traffic. My REAL reason was my wife. You know how a woman can be when she wants something.....ok, well some of you do. Cindy hasn't been that anxious since Damon was conceived. Let me tell you, a year is WAY too long to wait for something that good. No, I'm not saying we never had sex in a year, we have. She just hasn't been as in the mood as she was this time. Wow. Simply wow. Anyway, back on track. I'm about 5 miles from Troy's house now, should be there in no time. First thing we're going to do when we get on the project is finish sanding all the oil out of the cylindars. Damn leaky headgaskets. Guess that goes with any mid 90s Mopar though. That and a weak transmission. Transmission = Achille's heel of any Chrysler product. Thank god for the DSM and their similarities to my project. I'm going to be packing a 420a tranny in this soon to be baddass little cloud. Got the new gearbox sitting in my trunk right now. We might be able to get there today, depending on whether or not the new camshaft and rocker rods are in. Oh, the sheer joys of mixing and matching aftermarket parts. There is nothing more exciting than having to custom create a part because nobody else in their right mind would make it. Suddenly, I'm forced to snap out of my dream of my creature in waiting. I hear tires squealing beside me, see brake lights shining in front of me, and instinctively my feet slam down simultaneously on the clutch and brake pedals, locking my wheels and drifting the ass end of my car around sideways. I try to correct the skid, but to no avail. The side of my 2004 ice blue Z06 slams into a BMW next to me, bouncing me nose first into the other lane. An old man in a Lincoln Continental swerves off the road to avoid me, sideswiping the same Beamer. Thank God, last thing I want is any Ford product on my car. Finally, everything comes to a halt, without anybody behind me colliding with my car. Can't say the same for the BMW I hit, as it is now laying on its side, leaned against the median. I get out of my car and sprint around it to the windshield of the black car. 'Are you hurt? Stay calm, don't move!' I yell to the driver of the BMW. He is frantically trying to get his drivers door open, but the weight of it lifting vertically is too much. After yelling a few more times, I convince him to stay still. I pull out my cell phone and dial 911. The operator says there are already officers en route to the scene, so I tell her to send some medical staff too. I think the old man in the Lincoln needs help, as his car slammed face first into the median, his windshield is shattered and his two driverside wheels are laying on the pavement. I run over to see if he's ok, and as soon as I get there, he hangs up his cell phone. He was fine, just a bit bruised from the airbag. Lucky for him, his $75,000 had decent airbags that just saved his life. I go back over and sit down in my car, and bury my face in my hands. I am too scared to look at the passenger side of my Vette. I've only had this car for 7 months, Cindy bought it for me for our anniversary. It was more than she should have got me, but meant a lot none the less. We've both enjoyed our Corvettes over the time we've spent together. When i was 18, she 17, I bought my first one. A 91 ZR1 Corvette. I paid Cindy's uncle, Johnny Cruse, $23,000 for it. I worked for years to earn that money, and drove a piece of shit Chevy Vega for the first two years I had my license to buy that car. Ever since my first year wrestling, I've bought a new Corvette, top of the line, each year. Unlike most people, I don't trade the old one in. My parents own a few hundred acres, and my dad and I split the cost of a huge garage. He uses it to repair cars for friends, as well as doing some small sales out of it. I use it mostly as storage for my toys. Currently, I've got a 67 Stingray, a 65 Mako Shark, my first and favorite, the 91 ZR1, and every model year Z06 2000-2004. Next to be added will be a 2005, the most shocking thing to hit the Corvette threads since the lack of a model for the 1987 year. But now, my newest beauty is sitting in the middle of the highway, undoubtedly with black paint streaks and bashed in side panels. Finally, a Cop car shows up. I stand up and go to walk to him, as a car drive by in one of the other lanes and honks. A bright orange Hummer H2. With a license plate that looks very familiar. It was the Hummer belonging to my former friend turned long time rival, Mark Kullypso.
