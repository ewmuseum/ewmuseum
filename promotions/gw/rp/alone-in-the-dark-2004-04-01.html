'Alone in the dark'

1 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'Alone in the dark' Derek Draven Global Wrestling Promo #1083 Date: 4/1/04 Location: Unknown 'Ask yourself a question...' 'Or maybe two..' 'Who do you think of when I say the words,Higher Power' (The shape,his shoulder length hair and his stubble of a beard some into view.A slight reminiscence of the last time we saw him,in his dreams walking through the desert.) 'It is not a religious thing going against the common views that make up your precious christianity,or Budhisim,or anything else.' (the shape sits in front of a small fireplace,a small half burnt log continues to glow the evervescent orange glow letting the smoke rise out of the chimney bringing on a peaceful feeling across the room.) 'Global Wrestling has me back...' 'Now i've become the first ever,to my knowledge wrestling televangilist for Deism,yes that is a world reknowned religion for you out there...the uneducated simple people.It represents my knowledge of God but my disbelief in him as the sacred higher power.' 'Now these people..' 'They leave me no other option!' (The shape stands and pokes at the fire with a metal rod) 'I have been gone for a long time..' 'I've seen alot of things' 'Things you dont even see in your worst nightmares' 'I went out seeking the meaning of life,and found nothing.' 'Poverty...' 'Suicide...' 'Adulterey...' 'Murder..' 'You name it I saw it' 'Somewhere' 'If you close your eyes and think of what goes on outside of your perfect little town,you see nothing.' 'that was me' 'Why I left' 'Not over drugs...' 'Not over money...' 'I was seeking revelation' 'Salvation' 'I was tracking the roots of Salaoks teachings' 'Seeking the inner core of religious anarchy' 'I found several things' 'Read many conclusions' 'Began myself,as a new man' 'Religion is like a screenplay' 'A epic' 'Someone dies in there beloved cause' 'And goes nowhere' 'Thats stupidity' 'Religion isnt defined in any book' 'It's in your soul' 'Just looking that deep into yourself,that really takes some work' 'I spent a fortune of money,and a year and a half of time.' 'Most of which was on foot through ancient ruins and sacred land' 'I found things..the source of ideas which sprang out and became the worlds most influential religions,when I completed my journey I found a desire to go back to what I knew best...' 'Global Wrestling' 'I dont where i'll go or what i'll do...' 'My Nevermore companions..all drifted away..' 'Reminds me of a sad but peaceful song' (The shape again pokes the fire,then uses it to light his ciggarette as he sits down slowly letting smoke creep out of his face) 'But another mystery now beffudles me...' 'Where will I go now?' 'what will I do?' 'I see Brick has taken a interest in me' 'And I'm fighting Terminator' 'Hmmmmm.....' 'Brick..buddy old friend' 'Your misery has always been my delicate delight' 'But I assure you,when I have conjured up what I will do to you next,you'll find out in the most severe and unpleasent way there possibly is!' (the shapes cigarette has burnt to his fingers as he stands and throws into the fire.He stretches his arms and begins to let his eyes wander around the room) 'I wonder what Aaron is doing these days Brick' 'Why don't you tell me?' 'I'm really interested?' (The shape sits back over into the orange glow area of the room as the fire crackles softly in the background) 'You see Brick,I dont have a real problem with you it's just that through you I can achieve other goals..' 'You are my stepping stone..' 'And I advise you to leave family and freinds out of our affairs this time...' 'Bring them nowhere near the Global Arena' 'Cause when we fight again,it will be bad Brick' (The shape spontaneously stands and begins to walk around the room) 'Real Bad!' 'The more I watch and listen the more my stomach turns in anticipation to get back into the ring!' 'I'm ready to feel that rush again...' 'Oh yes.that craving..that desire' 'To hold the power of someones very life...' 'in your on two hands....' 'How i've missed that feeling' (The shape stands again and walks toward the fire,methodically.He crosses him arms across his chest and stares at the fire.The whole is room is dark with the exception of this one corner.He stands there motionless,staring at the fire,His blue eyes reflecting the orange image.Every crackle heard ever so softly,as he reaches down and pokes one more time at the fire,and lights himself another ciggarette.) 'I wish I had the ability to turn back to time...' 'So much to do differently.' 'In my time gone,I was only reminded of my actual life by hand written letters from various distant memebers of my family concerning the death of my brother.' 'How interesting God does work...' 'How I would like to go back in time and change things I did..' 'Maybe another day..' 'Cause this days over..' (The camera falls back,looking at the feet of the shape as the small ciggarette falls slowly to the floor,still light) (fade to black)
