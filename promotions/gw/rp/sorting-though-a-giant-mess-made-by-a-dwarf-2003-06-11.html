Sorting though a Giant mess made by a dwarf.

11 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-11-03 It had been a trying few days for Christina. Logan good hearted and all really was not the best idea handle her fathers affairs. Christina had spent the last few days going over everything. Her fathers office was full of files and papers. She stopped working long enough to have Ely take her to the Arena for Evolution last night. Things went from good to bad. Salaok wanted GW under his iron fist. She was not going to let it happen. Christina leaned back in her chair and rubbed her temples.. She reached over and typed in on the PC on the desk and pulled up the GW roster. The names scrolled up with the current batch of champions on the top in red. The current World champion, Sage Sabbot. She respected Sages drive, but not his motives or ethics. He was a good champion none the less. Tag titles were held by True Evil and Salaok also held the International. All the top titles in the stable of the Harvesters. Deserving men for the amount of time Regardless of her personal feelings on them. There skills in the ring were unquestionable. Deacon Holmes held the Platinum, Salmon Vation the Primestar and Roz Blayze the Cruiser. Christina nodded to her self as she scrolled past the champions to the rest of the roster. “Thing is people take things to personal. This is a business, it must be treated that way. I do not want to be a main part of the show.” Christina said to her brother Donovan McCoy “Sis, It is bound to happen. What you have to learn is to separate things.” Donovan said looking above his glasses. Christina sighed and looked at the roster.. Several new names had been added to the list. Angus the Bull, She remembered Logan talking about Angus. Good signing by Logan. Former champions Venom Alexander and Achilles Abas were under his name.. More good signings if they can produce. She continues down the screen making mental note of each.. Jesse Briggs out for six months, Brick a busted shoulder. Braxton damaged to his leg. Chris Canada, Supposed to be a good signing.. Heard big things on him, let’s se if he lives up to his rep... Ace Cash, Derek Frost, TLM, Tragic Hero, BJP, All and all a good roster. With a good spred. How ever GW could use some new faces to get things going again. With her notes on the current roster finished. Hit a email button on the keyboard. She smiled as her reporter jack Wade had sent her a heads up on several Free Agents.. 360 Wrestling closed. Sad but it would free up talent. So she opens the link to there website and looks in the roster? “Holy Crap!” Christina Exclaims. Donovan climbs up out of his pile of papers.. “What sis?” he says as he rounds the desk. Christina points at the name? “No way.” Donovan says. “So that’s where Trey went to when FWF closed. I will get on it sis.” Donovan says as he grabs his cell phone. Trey would be one hell of a signing. Christina reopened the Email from Jack Wade. Seven new names popped her interest. Vortex, Billy Boltz, Rob “The Hit man” Davies, Davis Fury, Ronnie Long, Jake Crimson and Gabriel Fristik? ~Crimson and Fristik had a good look and could possibly be a good tag team. Vortex had a good look and he was a Streetfighter. Davies had a impressive resume. Fury and Long two former GW stars both former champs? Christina printed out the list of names and contacts and handed the list to her brother. Who was smiling on the cell.. “YES!” Donovan screamed as he put down the cell. ? Trey is now with GW. He is heading over here now to sign the contracts.” A smile came to Christina’s face. “Good Little brother. Now be as equally persuasive with that list.” Christina Said as she got up.. ? Ohh and lets get this place cleaned up? Tiffany!” Christina called out the door. A small blond practically bounced into the room. “Yes Miss. McCoy.” She asked. “Time to start straitening this place out. Go to the office supply store and buy me some new file cabinets and OHH Hell with it. I need a break. Don. Me and Tiff are going shopping. We will be back. Use the spare office for interviews..” Christina said as she grabbed her purse. “You got it sis.” Donovan said as the two walked out of the room. Christina was proud of her father. He was many things. But organized and professional he was not. He needed her and Donavan now more then ever. She would make sure GW lived on forever if she could help it. With new found confidence She headed out to buy her’s and Donovan’s new office.
