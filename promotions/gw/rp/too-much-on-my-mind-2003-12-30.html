Too much on my mind

30 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/30/03 “Roberta,” said Christina McCoy, “let me know when Brick gets here.” “Yes, Ms. McCoy.” Christina had a lot on her mind. She had just had to suspend the company webmaster for posting inaccurate information on the company’s official Web site. The possibility that one of her superstars was looking to walk loomed heavily. And a mysterious stalker was pursuing another of her superstars. “Ms. McCoy, Brick is here.” “Send him in.” In walked Brick. “You wanted to see me?” “I did. Sit down.” As Brick sat, there was a silence between the two. “I called you here because I wanted to see how you were doing, considering?” “Considering there’s some son of a bitch after me.” “And I can see that it’s taking its toll on you.” It truly had begun to take its toll on Brick. A full beard was starting to grow on his face. He looked like he hadn’t slept well for weeks. It wasn’t so much fear, as it was frustration. First the rumors started. Then there were the short and disturbing messages that had been sent to Brick the night of Showdown in Tokyo, including a package that contained a Brick action figure that was missing its head and covered in fake blood. “I’m telling you, Christina. This shit is really getting on my nerves. I gotta deal with this cat, one way or another.” “Do you really think it’s--?” “I don’t want to, but the signs are there. I mean, look where he sent that first message from.” “Yeah.” “It was shot from an alley in Berlin. In fact, it was the same alley I remember from Whiplash a few years back.” “Well, Brick, that doesn’t automatically mean that it’s him. I mean, that could have just been a red herring. Besides, I remember watching when you and he fought. He wasn’t exactly one for mind games.” “No, but he was a sick son of a bitch.” “Anyway, I thought you two already settled the score back in January of 2002.” “I would have thought so, too, but apparently not, because it might just be him coming after me. Now on top of all this, I gotta face Jesse in a street fight.” “Jesse requested the match.” “Why?” “I think he feels like he has something to prove, especially to you.” “What does he think he has to prove to me?” “That you can trust him. But mostly, I think he wants you to respect him.” “What the hell is that about” I do respect him.” “Apparently, he doesn’t think so.” Brick then sighed and rose to his feet and started to leave Christina’s office. “So, if it is who you think it is coming after you, how do you plan to deal with it?” Brick then stopped and looked back at Christina. “The same way I did last time: violently.”
