Questions and a Kidnapp.

11 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 11 The scene opens up to the police station downtown. Me and my mom were sitting in black chairs. We were sitting by this desk. A few minutes later a police man walked up to us and starts asking us a few questions similar to the ones he asked us at our house. Me and my mom got off our chairs and stood there as he asked. “First of all, sorry to see this happen to your house.” he said. “Its okay. This is probably the 5th time its happened in the last 5 months. Actually its happened for the last 5 months. Every month there is a break in.” I said. “Really. That’s fairly odd. Anyways, we the police man of Connecticut are happy to announce that we will pay you back with anything that is even broken a little bit.” he said. “Well thank you very much officer.” my mother said to him. “No problem lady. And next time, I suggest you lock your door.” he said. “Oh I’m sure we will.” I said. Me, my mother, and the officer started laughing. We stopped and the officer walked away. Me and my mother sat back down, my mother had a smile on her face. I loved it when she smiled. I looked at a doorway, and inside I saw a whole bunch of prison cells. I saw a officer walk by, I poked him in the shoulder. He turned around and saw me. “Hello officer, I was wondering if I could take a look at the prison cells and all that?” I asked him. “You sure can Mr. Roswell.” he said. The officer smiled at me and led me and my mother over to the prison cells. We walked inside and I saw a whole bunch of people sitting on a metal bed that hung out from the wall. They looked way to depressed, I could even tell my mother didn’t even wanna look at them they were so depressed. I looked at one of the cells and saw the same guy who was wearing the black suit who robbed me and my mother’s house. I looked at him straight in the eyes. I looked at his lips and saw that he lipped “Fuck You” to me. I looked at his face. Wait a second, I recognized that face, it was that man I saw sitting on the park bench with the black coat on. “Mom, you ready to go?” I asked her. “Yup.” she said. We walked out of the room. I looked at the man again, he had the meanest face someone could ever have. We walked out of the police station and out to the car we took to get here. My mother got into the passenger seat and I got into the driver’s seat. I started the car by no time we were already home. “Well, this day has gone by really fast.” my mother said to me. “You said it.” I said. We got out of the car and walked up our driveway and to our door. I took a key out of my pocket and shoved it into the lock of our door. I opened the door and put the key back into my pocket. I walked into the house and took off my jacket. I threw it and got it onto the couch. “So, what are you doing today, mom?” I asked her. “I don’t really know. I might just go up stairs and clean up the mess. Then I will go rest in my room.” she said. My mother walked upstairs and into the room where it was broken into and I walked into the living room. I turned on the television and saw a commercial for my fed, GW. I watched it. It has pictures of the world champion Ice. And all the other champions. And it actually showed me on it with my match with Chris Steel. I still can’t believe Roz Blayze had to interfere and piledriver me onto the chair. My neck is still sore from that stupid attack. Another commercial showed up. My eyes were glued to it. It showed Roz Blayze on the television. He was laughing. He stepped from the screen, and I saw my mother tied up to a chair with a sock in her mouth! “What the hell?” I yelled. Roz Blayze started to speak. “I told you Max. Don’t mess with me or I’ll screw with you even more. I guess your mother isn’t resting, well maybe with me.” he said. I turned off the television and ran upstairs. I walked into the room where my mother was going, but she wasn’t there! I looked all around but she was no where to be found. I started to panic. I ran to the phone and picked it up and dialed the police’s phone number. I told them that my mother was kidnapped. They said they would be right over. I hung up the phone and waited until the police came as the scene started to fade.
