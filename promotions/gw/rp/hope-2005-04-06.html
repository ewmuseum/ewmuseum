Hope

6 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/6/2005 You see a man laying down staring up at the ceiling with long bright red hair. Its obviously dyed since its not a natural red. Upon closer inspection its former WMW wrestler Fred Grest. It looks like hair color and promotions aren't the only thing Fred Grest changed you see a tattoo on his forearm that reads 'HOPE'. Hope... funny word for 'The Extreme One'. He has said and thought about that word so many times before. Hope... something that he had when heading into his last WMW Match against Syberus. How that match could have very well turned his career around. You could even say he put his eggs all in one basket. He left everything he had in the ring that night. He could have won the match but instead too while at the very top of the cage he decided to give the fans in the arena something they would never forget. The newest GW star hit a perfect frog splash on Syberus. When his 195 lbs body landed on the still Midwest Champion he thought everything had change. He was going to win the match and finally put his name up there with WMW Top Dogs. However it was not meant to be as Syberus ended up being able to crawl out of the door before he could. Despite his best effort to date he came up just a bit short. He hears knocking on the door. Grest seems a bit slow to get up to answer the door. Not much good has come from getting up as of late. Weather it was his ex girlfriend or answering the phone to hear that his father was in the hospital. He and his father had never gotten along seeing as he didn't follow in his father's footsteps. Fred Grest or Green at that point in time until he changed his name wanted to do his own thing. That thing being a pro wrestler and not a deacon like his father. Fred weather his last name was Grest or Green only wanted two things out of life. One being to be the best in pro wrestling which he has been far from but yet a goal he knew he could reach. The other was to raise a loving family another goal he knew he was from but there was no hurry at this point in time. There is Samantha but he had to cancel the 1st date do to his father's current condition. Life seems rather hectic as of late and why add to that? The knocking as turned into pounding now. He figures he better get up since it was either urgent or someone has come over to irritate him. Nowadays its a 50 50 chance that it could be either. Fred Grest slowly gets up and heads towards the door. The pounding keeps going and Grest is just ready to answer the damn thing and Freddy Kick and get it over with. He really should be in a more pleasant mood considering he's getting a fresh start in Global Wresting against Elia Smith but something just wasn't quite right. He opens the door and its Chill although he is tempted to Freddy Kick him he lets the loveable homie inside. 'Heard you left Wrestling Midwest' 'Yeah... I felt it was time for a change' 'So what's next for you?' 'Global Wrestling' Chill has a look of surprise on his face. 'Global Wrestling? Why? They kicked WMW's ass last year at Crossfire' I nod my head in agreement. 'Yes, they did' Chill has a confused look on his face. 'So why there? Too be honest you were average in WMW what makes you think you'll be better off in an even better promotion?' Fred Grest looks Chill straight in the eyes. 'To get better Chill. I mean you knew me before WMW I was winning matches and titles but I wasn't the best as I liked to call myself. When I joined WMW I got better much better yet it wasn't good enough. I believe going to Global I will have to push myself even harder to win.' 'That makes a tad bit of sense but how can you expect to be the best if you never claim to the top of a promotion' 'I will.... I just have to take it one step at a time' 'That's what you always say Freddy' 'Yeah but do you really expect to run away from my dream? I mean come on Chill you should know that I won't quit.' 'True but why Global Wrestling? You could have stayed where you're needed and to a certain extent wanted' 'You just asked me this to push myself even harder.' 'Well looks like there is no changing your mind but I just don't agree with the decision' 'I wasn't looking for your approval Chill. I wasn't looking for anyone's approval. I have never and never will.' 'Just throwing in my two cents.' With a stern look on Grest's face. 'Keep it' Chill shakes his head 'Peace out' Chill walks out the door and Grest closes and locks the door behind him. He sighs and goes back down laying on the sofa staring at the ceiling once again. He laid there and wondered if this was the right decision for him. Could I have very well jumped out of the fire pan and into the fire? With one decision I could have very well ruined my career. I was a mediocre 11 and 11 in Wrestling Midwest. What would I do in Global Wrestling? I could very well end up below .500. I can't believe I still doubt myself. If I don't stop this I will be torn apart in Global Wrestling. I have till Evolution to straighten my ass out or Elia Smith will be the one with her hand raised. Is this a new beginning or part of a continuing saga for me? I guess all I can do is hope for the best.
