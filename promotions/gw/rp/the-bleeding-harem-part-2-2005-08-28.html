The Bleeding Harem-Part 2

28 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 28, 2005 36?. 24?. 36. The kind of measurements no man could dare resist. A chocolate beauty of 6’1 and a sculpted 160 pounds, all in the right places mind you, was laid before the Brawler on a satin sheet bed. Any other time this would be a dream come true for the Bronx Barbarian. As much as he loved his wife, he realized vital opportunities when he noticed them. However, this was not the dream that you would think right off the bat. Far from it. In his dream, the beauty’s face wasn’t ripped to shreds the way a famished dog would tear through a juicy steak. Blood stained the purple sheets, uneven pieces of her flesh strewn about the bed as well. The expression of fear frozen on what used to be her exquisite face had said it all. Who or whatever did this to her, it had the murderous intent of a wild beast with the taste of blood on its taste buds. She was not meant to be an instrument of pleasure the way she had understood her job to be. She was prey. And there they stood, shoulder to shoulder, The Back Alley Brawler and the premiere “pimp” of the Las Vegas area, Luis Vega. Luis was a rotund man who still possessed handsome features, the innocence of his baby-face broken as he raised a Cuban cigar to his lips and inhaled the sweet taste. The Brawler and Vega were in silent agreement over what had happened to the siren that laid mangled in the hotel suite. What kind of person could have it in them to not only kill her, but mutilate her on top of that? A puff of angered smoke escaped Vega’s lungs as he looked away from the gruesome sight and out the crystal clear windows to his left. The city of Las Vegas was moving about with furious speed and blinding lights below him, a grand sight to behold in its own right as he fixed the collar on his fine Versace suit. “5 of them, 5 of my women like this. Allies. Hotels. Crackhouses. I get scared to pick up the phone because since I’ve been doing it lately, I keep hearing one of my females has been bumped off. Shit, it’d be bad enough if they got shot or stabbed”.but this?! Ripped to shreds? It’s like they let some dogs loose on em after they took her life. Mocha was a big earner for me too?.now it’s getting personal.” Vega was a rarity in the prostitution business. A pimp that actually gave a damn about the women he employed. He always made sure he carried the finest stock in sexual pleasure and made sure to tend to their needs, be it STD tests or just that little bit of help on a month’s rent. You take care of your stock, your business will thrive. To see one of the finest prostitutes under his employ, Mocha, now a piece of incomplete flesh?.it was enough to make a man kill. Enter the Back Alley Brawler. Vegas turned around so he was now eye to eye with the weathered monster of a man. The proud New Yorker could recognize the searing look in Vegas? eyes. It was the look of passion and care the Brawler had on display for his daughter, Carmen. It was the feeling of doing whatever it took, no matter the cost, for redemption. The Brawler’s eyes watched as Vega dipped into his inner suit pocket, unearthing a thick wad of crisp Benjamin Franklin’s. The saying was definitely true in this case. Money talks and bullshit walks. So with the sight of that kind of money, the Brawler finally let words escape his closed mouth. “Whatcha need me ta do” To be honest witcha, if not for da bills I gotta pay?” Brawler looked back to Mocha, a blood-spattered angel on the bed as he mentally tried to piece her back together. That image was much more pleasant for the Brawler to swallow, easily empathizing with Vega where it concerned his rage for her assailant. ?”.Shit, for a dime like dat. I’d kill da muhfucka fo free.” Vega shot daggers for eyes at the Brawler, the intensity growing thick without even a moment’s notice. “YOU DON’T KILL HIM.” The Brawler scoffed, leaning against the cherry oak foot-rail of the bed. He shot a smile oozing of sarcasm to his possible employer, his hands thrown up wildly. “So wha da fuck am I s’pposed ta do” Find da bastard and give him a lesson on propa ett?.etti?..-lost for words- HOW TO TREAT A FUCKIN? LADY!!” Vega took a long inhale of the Cuban, the air lingering with a brown sugar smell the longer he let it burn. The exquisite cigar managed to calm the nerves of the exotic businessman as the venom in his voice disappeared for a short moment. He drew his steps closer to the brick wall that was the Brawler, pulling open the chest pocket of his navy blue Dickies work uniform and filling it with the aforementioned wad of hundred dollar bills. A light pat on the pocket made the exchange of goods official as he deliberately made his wishes clear. “I don’t want you claiming that kill. I want to see the sick fuck squeal when I take the sharpest icepicks I got and stab him in his kneecaps until all the fluid drains out. Then any other joint I want until he’s a limp fuck who can’t fight back”.the way Mocha couldn’t fight him back. The fuck.” The Brawler was a man who walked his own road, but he knew better then to cross the intentions of an employer unless he was ready for constant hell in his life. He could tell this would be a situation that would keep Vega on wits end until it was solved his way. There would be no rest for the wicked until he had claimed the murderer of his women for his own. As strange as it might have sounded at that moment’those who stalked the streets had motives much more sane for their actions than anyone the Brawler could have met in wrestling. Wrestling, you fight over a leather strap with gold medallions on it. Two men will irk each other on TV for weeks and then blow it off with some dumbass gimmick match, then act like the whole “hatred” never happened a mere week later. As hard as it was, it was sane in the streets. You kill someone close to another, they find a way to kill you back. Need money? Rob a bank. The ends justified the means and damn if they didn’t make sense when you thought about it. Whoever did this to Mocha, they went into territory that would make your average “sick fuck” blush. Carving a working girl up like a Thanksgiving turkey was not the way to go, especially with how protective the prostitution ring was in Vegas. Whoever did this to her didn’t know the ground rules. It would be the Brawler’s duty to instruct them on what they were; as if “Don’t carve up your source for sex for the night,” needed to be explained to this newcomer no matter what planet they were from. “Give me a few days ta ask around, see wha’s poppin. I’ll get ya results. Lord knows these ladies go thru enough shit without muhfuckas cuttin” em up.” “I’m glad we understand each other.” “Not much to it mista Vega. We’re both guys dat don’t like to see da ladies fucked wit”.I know da feelin.” The Brawler took one last look at the corpse that was Mocha. The cops would make their official run through long after he and Vega had left, given the pimp owned the cops and all, they would come whenever it was convenient for him. Whatever did this to her would be a challenge and a half for the Brawler to stop in all honesty, but it was a challenge he had no problem trying to tackle’especially for a cool 100 grand. One thing was certain however, he had now become involved in something guaranteed to shorten his lifespan.
