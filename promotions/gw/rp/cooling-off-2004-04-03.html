Cooling off

3 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/3/04 There was a great deal of energy in the Dark Horse saloon. People were dancing to the sounds of “Hard to Handle” by the Black Crowes. The mechanical bull was being put through its paces. At the bar sat Brick. As he nursed a cold beer, much had been weighing on his mind. He had just angrily confronted acting GW president Christina McCoy about re-hiring Derek Draven. He knew that, sooner or later, he and Draven would collide and the result wouldn't be pretty. Draven had studied under two of the most depraved minds in the history of Global Wrestling: Salaok and Quartz. Brick’s history with Draven was a brutal and bloody history. It began in 2002. Brick had just lost the International title to Brian Simon at High Octane. He and Simon had been feuding since January. He had the chance to win the title back on the next Overload, which was the company's flagship show at the time. It was a Fatal Four Way elimination match between Brick, Simon, Draven and Ronnie Long. Two eliminations in, the match was down to Brick and Draven. After a hard-fought match, Draven won by giving Brick a Dravenizer right on top of the International title belt. After the match, Draven had proceeded to give Brick yet another Dravenizer on top of the belt. The next week, Brick had partnered with Gunner Addison to face Draven and Lisha in an intergender tag match. However, before they could get to the ring, they were ambushed from behind by Quartz. Draven and Lisha then tried to hang Brick from the rafters with a noose. This led to their match for the International title at Clash of the Titans. The attempted hanging had brought Brick to the brink of insanity. It had freed a sadistic streak in Brick that had only been seen on a few occasions. During the match, Brick dragged Draven to the top of the Globaltron. Situated on the stage some forty feet below was a dumpster filled with light bulbs. Brick then proceeded to throw Draven off of the Globaltron straight down into the dumpster, sending hin crashing into the glass bulbs. Even though the shards of glass cut into Draven’s skin, that didn’t stop Brick from dragging him back into the ring and getting the three count to win the title. Months later, Draven made his return to GW as part of the newly reformed Harvesters. His first act was to screw Brick out of the International title in the main event of the first edition of GW Evolution. The next thing the Harvesters did was to kidnap Brick’s longtime friend Aaron Foster and hold him for days, torturing him. This act, more than anything, enraged Brick. After Aaron had been dropped off in front of a hospital, Brick had gone into the second edition of Evolution where Brick faced Salaok for the Platinum title. Sometime during the match, Quartz appeared on the Globaltron, hovering over what looked like Aaron’s unconscious body. Salaok issued an ultimatum to Brick: forfeit the match or lose his best friend. Brick kept his word and relented, but Salaok didn’t keep his. Quartz plunged a large syringe into the body. Brick then beat Salaok and rushed out of the ring on the next plane back to New York. It had turned out that the body was not Aaron, but a cadaver. The real Aaron was missing. The next week, Aaron had resurfaced in Draven’s clutches during one of Brick’s matches on top of the Globaltron. Draven then proceeded to throw Aaron off the Globaltron into a stack of tables. Their feud finally culminated in August at High Noon when they faced off in a Dallas Street Fight which Brick won. It seemed to be the end of their feud. The doors had swung open and in walked Ely Braxton. “HEY!!!” shouted the patrons. Braxton then walked over to the bar and sat on a stool right next to Brick. “Figured I’d find you here.” “Texas mudslide, Ely?” asked the bartender. “Of course.” “What do you want, man?” Brick asked. “We need to talk.” “Look, if you’re here because of what went down in Christina’s office, save your damn breath, because I don’t feel like repeating myself.” “Christina was pretty upset.” “Yeah, well, your lady should have thought of that when she rehired Draven.” “First of all, she ain’t my lady.” “Oh, man, whatever.” “We’re just friends.” “Oh, so you and she are friends like you and me are friends?” “Exactly.” “You never put out an open challenge to a bar fight because cats were hittin” on me.” “I figured you’d beat the mess outta any guy that hit on you.” “How the hell do you figure that?” “Well, don’t you an” Donny McNasty have a tag match against Train an? Mark Makonie?” Train and Mark Makonie: two other names that made Brick’s blood boil. In Makonie’s case, it was because of his extremely racist views. In Train’s case, it had started with the fact that Train had joined forces with Makonie. When Brick called Train on this alliance in public, it resulted in a huge brawl. And now Brick was facing them with McNasty by his side. “You’re right. I gotta put Draven on the back burner right now. Besides, Train has me pissed enough as is.” “Yeah, you got enough people on your hit list as it is.” “The way cats piss me off, there may never be enough names.”
