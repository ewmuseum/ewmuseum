If you’re gonna get things done?

2 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/02/06 ? you may as well get them done yourself. A little less than two weeks ago, the reformed athlete, Rich Sherwin, took to the stage in a tag team contest with his fellow ex-PCW employee, Tim Walden. Together, the two went head-to-head with David Crisp and Kaden Calix. The contest seemed to be following a very nice route? until of course, a chair, intended for the hands of David Crisp went straight into the arms of one, Rich Sherwin. With all the might in his hands, Sherwin took the chair and introduced Crisp to a shot with so much intent that if it wasn’t in the ring, it’d almost certainly be the case for a court. This upcoming week though, the two that ended the contest have the opportunity to go at it again? this time though, they won’t have the benefit of a person on the apron. Well, put in better terms, they wouldn’t have the benefit of a “fresh” wrestler on the sidelines. ? more importantly though, how will everything play out? With Sherwin being the man to deliver that hellacious chair-shot, will there be any remnants of psychological trauma? Potentially unlikely? but also potentially likely at the very same time. Would Rich be able to secure the clean win? Or would the attack on Crisp merely serve as more of a motivational tool than anything else? We won’t know until game-time? however, “till then, we have the next exciting chapter of Rich Sherwin’s travels in Global. ~|~|~|~|~|~|~|~|~|~ ?” This match is going to be so boring. I’m not going to have any fun at all? I’m not going to be able to enjoy anything against this guy. I mean, did you see how things ended the other week? Did you see what happened at the end of the match? It’s not my problem if somebody wants to throw a chair to me. I just happened to use it! If anything, I should be congratulated for being so warm to receiving gifts.” “You can be about as warm as a hot-water bottle but if you’re going to go around hitting people with chairs that happen to get thrown to you” well, you’re not going to have a very good career, are you?” As the scene opened, we found ourselves sitting in the comfortable confinements of a road-side caf?. It was nothing particular and it was most certainly nothing fancy. The general d’cor and the organisation of equipment was very much like the stereotypical road-side caf??s that you tend to see in every movie under the sun. A little splash of white here, a little dab of some other colour there? and then of course, the odd coffee stain and sauce stain. The general mood of the establishment was mellow? with all of about three people sitting down at tables. That’s inclusive of our two speakers; who are sat at a table with the third party. Two men and a woman. One of whom being Rich Sherwin, the other male and female being unknown parties? at the moment. “Ah well, it just means that I’m going to have to be a little more on the careful side next time. If I don’t get caught out, I’m not going to cause a loss of any kind.” Sherwin said excitedly, looking forward to the potential prospect that could come with a second chair intervention. That is, of course, provided that a chair is thrown and that it’s thrown just as high as before. ?” You’re such a joker, Sherwin.” the other male replied, looking down at the table with a hand over his eyes. It seemed as though Rich was in high spirits, despite coming out of two matches without an actual win. “Awww, Rich isn’t a joker” he just enjoys causing a little damage when opportunity should knock.” the female responded, looking Sherwin in the eye with a smug grin on her face. The positioning of the two people appeared to portray that of the struggle between good and evil. One person provoking somebody to do the underhanded deed? and the other trying to talk them out of it. Sherwin however, wasn’t all that bothered about who was trying to press what point. This was all about taking the opportunity when opportunity comes? because David Crisp isn’t going to hesitate when the shoe is on the other foot. “Look, Sheryl” Damien? if the opportunity arises, then I’m going to take it. If it doesn’t come though, I’m not going to be the one looking for it. If anything, this match almost feels like something of a punishment? an opportunity for David Crisp to try and come out firing on all cylinders.” Rich explained, looking at the situation in a deeper shade of light. He then continued, ?” Ultimately, I’m going to have to win this contest. It’s not a matter of choice, it’s going to be a matter of fact. I have the desire to advance in this business? and I have an even greater desire to advance somewhat in this organisation. If I don’t defeat Crisp then I’m going to be left with two very kind messages.” “The first being that you’re only able to get one over on people when you use a weapon?” enquired Sheryl. ?” and the second being that when you get one over the illegal way, you’re unable to follow through in the legit sense.” confirmed Damien, the two people taking this all very seriously now. After all the fun and games that came through at the start of the conversation, things had seemingly taken a turn to a more serious element. Discussion was no longer about taking the opportunity against David Crisp, it was about being able to get through the match and exist with a reputation intact. “Yeah and yeah?” Rich began before continuing, ?” you’ve pretty much nailed both of those messages on the heads, guys. If I don’t come out with the victory against Crisp, this isn’t just going to be some kind of stigma attached to my name but it’s going to be some kind of horrible smell that follows me around from match to match. People are eventually going to get bored of somebody who can’t fight their way through a battle. They’re going to end up forgetting about that person and move onto the next guy who has a cool move? or a cool way of getting to the combat zone.” “You honestly believe that these people would just forget about an athlete like you” I mean, you’ve only just come back into things for starters. I’m sure a lot of people are surprised with the effort you made in the last match alone. Nobody expects you to return to fighting shape immediately? you’ve got to learn to give yourself a little more slack.” Damien insisted, believing that Rich needs the time to truly adjust on a physical scale before he is able to contemplate moving forth within Global. “Your career has spanned more than just a couple of years” and you’ve been here before. Just be a little patient with yourself. A loss against David Crisp isn’t going to spell total disaster.” Sheryl added, echoing the same sentiments that Damien had. It was a refreshing change from the usual love-hate atmosphere that had presented itself a short while earlier. “Guys” I appreciate the positive thinking on the worst case scenario but this isn’t something that I really want to be considering. I don’t want to be thinking about what things could be like if I’m unable to defeat David Crisp. My prior victories show me that I am capable of things? I’m capable of being something. You guys have both followed my career. You guys have seen the titles that I’ve picked up, the scalps that I’ve picked up. I have to win? there is no alternative.” Sherwin uttered, his cold stone of voice actually starting to reflect the dire strain of the situation. Sherwin knew that he needed to come out on top against Crisp. Any other result just wouldn’t be acceptable? and another loss by DQ? well, that’s something that isn’t even going to really be contemplated. Sheryl and Damien sat silent at the table for a few moments. What had started out as something of a light-hearted discussion had turned into something a little more serious? and then erupted into a must-win situation. How ever did things get so bad for Sherwin? How did things get to point where everything has to be taken in such a depressing manner? As the silence continued to rule the area, Rich knew what he had to do. Emerging from his seat, he looked down to both Damien and Sheryl and bid them farewell. Turning away, he walked off, urging the two to provide the final insight to this chapter. ?” Do you know what’s happened to him? I haven’t seen him in such a wild change of mood before.” “I don’t know what’s happened to him. I heard a rumour that something happened to him earlier in the year. Something about an attack that he carried out” but then couldn’t force the point when push came to shove. I don’t like to admit it but I think he’s actually nervous about this one.” “Heh, so much for doing things for himself, eh” He might end up having to get another partner just to be able to take on Crisp.” -- END --
