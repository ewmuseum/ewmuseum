What?

2 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/2/05 ~Dani's Room~ Danielle sat at her desk in her room, her journal open to a fresh page, she tapped her desk with her finger tips as she looked at the clock sitting next to her. They had been gone a while, she wondered how long it would take. She replaced the cap on the bottle of ink and closed her journal. She walked over to her dresser and pulled out what looked to be a string bikini, it was time for a swim and maybe some relaxation. Gray and Josh had taken off to the dealership to see what type of car he wanted. The fifteen car garage was going to be having three cars actually in it finally. It still sounded empty in there, especially when it echoed while Gray started working on his old hobbies. He was a woodworker by trade, making the old Saquamish style long boats and totem poles. He had said something about the front yard needing one of those totems out front to give the house a bit more character, and the others had agreed. She had the house to herself... except for the maid, that was doing her daily rounds. Jessie was her name. A sweet young lady that kept to herself. But Danielle noticed how she looked at Josh. Then again, Danielle couldn't blame her; his green eyes, long well kept black hair, firm body... Danielle shivered and sighed. Yeah she couldn't blame Jessie in the least bit. Danielle slid behind the changing screen that had been placed in her room, just in case anyone walked in, not that Danielle cared but others seemed to. As she stepped out from behind the screen she stopped and looked at her self in the mirror, turning just enough to see her back. She strained to see if there were any marks what so ever, but to no avail. The only sensation was the itch that seemed to mock her every once in a while, down both sides of her spine. But that wasn't the only thing that bothered her; her back was still a little stiff along with her shoulders. She thanked the gods that she was able to sit and soak as long as she wanted to in her bathtub or one of the hot tubs downstairs. All the while, Josh played doctor on her head, cleaning and checking the slight cut she received in her match against Ambrosi. Nothing like a head wound to cover someone in blood so quickly. She moved to head out to the pool, Stopping long enough to look on her bed. Her new belt, the Global Platinum Championship, laid there with her ring attire. Another title sat there too, the one from the now closed federation that she had gained and never lost, a fed that actually has it's roots with Global, the CCWA North American Title. She smiled at it then headed out for a few laps. A few good laps in the pool, and she would then just relax for the rest of the day. ~Catching UP~ She spent about an hour in the pool, swimming around to get a slight workout in, and then decided to climb out. She spent the time thinking about herself, her win over Ambrosi and the title, and other things, just to distance herself. The lounging chairs looked inviting in the early fall sunlight in Vegas, the nice warm air was cooled by the evaporating water from Danielle's skin. She put a towel down onto one of them and then reclined fully back to just escape for a bit. Her mind drifted off as her body relaxed. In the garage, shortly after that, Gray's large Chevy pickup pulled in. It ran silently as they pulled in as slow as possible, so as to make the least amount of noise. As soon as it parked, Josh jumped out of the passenger's seat and then rushed over to the door to the inside of the house. He opened it and poked his head in. ?Jessie? Jessie?? The young woman walked over from the dinning room, where she was dusting. ?Yes?? ?Where is Dani?? “She's out back, laying down by the pool.” ?Perfect!?? Josh ran back to the truck. “She's laying out by the pool. We're in luck. Help me get this out of the back and take it up to our room. Then you can start denying everything.” Gray climbed out of the truck, and walked to the back end. He popped the door open on it and then started to unstrap the box that was back there. A box that conspicuously had holes in it. Gray takes the large box, as Josh grabs another one that was laid down in the bed of the truck. Both then have Jessie hold the door open for them and disappear into the master bedroom that's on the ground floor from the front, second floor from the front. ~An Hour Later~ ~Poolside~ Dani lays still at pool side, flat on her back in her bathing suit as she had slumbered off while relaxing. Fortunately for her, the high desert sun wouldn't burn her skin, due to her natural darker skin tone. She was used to this. All was going just fine, when she was suddenly awoken by a soft voice and a light touch to her shoulder. ?What?? Dani shot up, a startled look on her face. The young woman who acted as their maid on these days was standing there wearing her jeans, t-shirt and apron. “Miss Northsky, you'll pardon the interruption, but you have visitors here.” Dani put her hand over her eyes to block the harsh sun, as they adjusted after having been closed for the length of time that they were. ?Who is it?? The woman answered her without any hesitation. “There are two of them. The older man, he introduced himself as Mr. Singing Eagle, ma'am. He is waiting for you in the living room with another man. I told him you may be a minute or two.” Danielle looked at the young girl with a surprised look on her face, her grandfather here? Now? Why? Danielle grabbed the sarong sitting there on the chair next to her. Wrapping it around her body like a sun dress, then tying it in the front at her bosom. She then took off almost at a run into the house. As she rounded the corner two men stood in her living room, one man older, leaning on a cane the the other younger, holding a package of some type in his hands. She recognized the younger man as one of her second cousins that she had stayed with for a few weeks before she left to start her wrestling career. She ran at the older man, who put his arm out to catch her. ?Papa!? She always called him that. Even though he was the father of her mother, he was the one who had saved her from the orphanage. Danielle hugged the older man, to her eyes he looked even older then before. His arms where not as strong as they once where, not compared to a year ago, let alone when she was fifteen. She gave him a concerned look. The old man just looked around the room, at the vaulted ceilings and that. “My Sirius Star, I see you have stepped up in the world, little one. Is this from your modeling and that? I guess a pretty lady can make it today by doing such as having her picture taken.” She watched as her cousin put his hands out and helped her grandfather walk a bit forward. “It's provided by many things. The key providers being the Order, themselves.” “I see. They are taking good care of you then. It is good that I support their cause then.” Danielle looked around and then looked at the ground. Her grandfather placed his leathery hands under her chin lifting it up eye to eye with him. “You have no reason to ever lower your head to anyone. You, my granddaughter, have earned the right to have others lower their eyes to you. For us to sit here as equal to you, Great Granddaughter of Morning Star; it is an honor.” Danielle looked at her grandfather, a tear rolled down her face. She stood wordlessly, as he continued on. “I hear that shyster, Jaymes, gave you the locket of your mother's. I had been wondering where it went to. You see, Jaymes knew them. I think he knew more then he let on... About them, what happened to them, and about you.” Danielle looked at her Grandfather with a confused look on her face. ?About me?? He brushed it off. “Never mind now, little one. I have come to talk to you about this young man of yours. I don't see him here right now. I've also come bearing a message and a gift for you.” Danielle smiled at her grandfather. “Joshua? You met his brother, Gray, when you first took me to the school. He was my teacher. His little brother has always been my friend.” “There are rumors that it has gone to more then friendship. Tradition says I must ask his intentions towards you. But the times say I must keep my nose out of my young granddaughter's personal life, also. But you are a special person. According to that, I must follow some of the traditions.” Danielle just smiled at her grandfather. As she did he reached forward to her necklace. Picking it up and running a leathery finger of it. ?Where did you get this?? He sounded worried and relieved at the same time, somehow. “I don't know. Someone gave it to Josh. But he doesn't know who she was, or where she came from. The woman even claimed that Josh had dropped it. But then suggested that it might look good on his pretty girlfriend. Even though at the time...” The old man laughed the laugh of a man whose had too many years of tobacco intake. “That is very interesting. That is the true mark of Morning Star. it looks like he wanted to protect you. Wondered what would pull him out of hiding.” Dani shook her head at that. ?Are you saying he is still alive? Morning Star?? “Alive. That all depends on your definition. But I am sure there is one that would be able to tell you more about their type. But you will have to wait to meet him I am sure when you are ready he will meet you when he thinks your ready. We've had our theories as to what he truly was. Some of course follow the spoken myths, but some of us had other facts.” Danielle got up and slapped her hands to her side in frustration. “Why is it everyone seems to know what is best for me. But no one wants to ask me what I think is best for me? I have Questions but no one gives answers.” Her grandfather stands to meet her eye to eye. ?You have become more then anyone expected. Your mother and father would be proud they gave their lives to protect yours. We now know Jaymes had a hand in that also. Lynn and Daniel trusted Jaymes. They knew what he was, and who he was at least to some point. It appears that when he turned on them, they gave up their lives to protect you. What they didn't know and what I didn't know is his long life was caused by our ancestor. After finding out about him, I went to the orphanage again, and asked for their records. This time, apparently the Order had called them first... they gave them up. Would you like to know who gave you to the orphanage?? Dani shook her head yes. “Lynn Northsky...” ?Mom?? “Yes, it appeared that they put you there, they knew what was happening. They then left there and headed for home, knowing that Jaymes had played his games. Up to the time you reappeared, everyone though you dead. No one knew you had lived. You were named by the Nuns not by us. They used the names on the sheet to give you the name. They named you after your parents. Your given name by us is Sirius Star Northsky.” At this point the man with her grandfather, her cousin, steps forward, handing him a bound package. “It is time this comes to you. Many moons ago, before the time of Sitting Bull and the great battles, Morning Star walked with our people, as did his son, Fallen Star. Morning Star was of the Waken Taken, and he left.... but he left us with a few things that your father's family was allowed to keep. When they passed on, I became the protector of the last remaining things of Morning Star to our people.” The old man opened the box as it sat in his nephew's hands, and then started to pull out something. ?Morning Star had a wingspan that surpassed those of the eagles. And when he left, he left us with a small collection of his feathers! He told our people that when he was no longer with us, no longer there to protect us, they would disappear. But they're still here. And now I have the feathers from your wings as well as his great granddaughter.? Grandfather Screaming Eagle pulls out a feather wand, done up in the manner that an owl's wing or a woman's wand, would be created. Although their all individual feathers, they look now, as if they were one wing. Dani can pick out her own feathers, with their dark, dusky shade of gray on them with white highlights and markings. But, the others are a pure white base, the markings, similar, are black and gray. The feathers are a negative of each other. ?I have bound your feathers with his. You are the new inheritor of his mantle, and his blood. This Fan is now yours. I would be honored for you to use it in your dances. Which is another reason I am here... I am here as a representative of our great family, and the people of the Rose Bud Reservation, to request the Great Grand daughter of the Morning Star to please come to dance for us, in our Celebration of your rebirth.? The man spoke like he was speaking to more then just his granddaughter; but a stranger, who was more then just a child. He spoke to her like she, herself, was a great Shaman or princess. The man that stood before her was a stranger to her, it wasn't the loving Grandfather, he was a man almost pleading with her to allow them to honor her. But for what? What had she done to receive such honor? She still didn't fully understand. Again more questions then answers. Her eyes welled with tears, as she looked down at the gift. Before she could speak two more voices broke the silence as well as the sound of her bedroom door slamming shut. ?I told you that thing doesn't like me. It just tried to take my hand off!? Gray spoke out, almost yelling in displeasure. “It isn't suppose to like you, it's suppose to like her....” Josh turned to see the three people standing in the living room. A loud sound came from the bedroom, almost as something was yelling or screaming. ?What are you two up to?? Dani put her hands on her hips as she stood there. Gray answered first, pointing at his brother. “It isn't us two, it was him. Remember, it is all his fault.” ?What is?? Josh quickly answered. “It is a surprise for later. You will see.” Gray turned to address the men standing in their living room, as Dani gave Josh the look that he was in trouble. “Grandfather Screaming Eagle, what a honor. Will you be staying long? I'll have Jessie prepare the guest room for you.” Gray reached forward to shake the man's hand. “Gray Brand, I remember you from the school. You are the sergeant of the Order assigned here also.” The old man narrowed his eyes towards Gray, as if he could sense something else. “I also know that I have to thank you for what you have done for my Granddaughter. With you at her side I know she is always safe.” “Thank you, I try my best. We're all learning all the time. I must say that she's a pleasure to have around too. Even if she is sometimes a bit of a handful.” The old man nodded to him. “Yes, she always has been. I have spoken with Ms. Leanan about you also about your issues.” Gray's hand dropped to his side, and his mouth opened in disbelief. “As I have told Dani, we've been doing some investigating up in the Black Hills, I've been in many talks with your current boss. Nice lady, even if she is a bit of a 'tart' as those with her accent would call her. She asked me about you, because I have had relations with the local Paiute also.” Gray nodded to him. “I give you a message, and advice then Mr. Gray Muzzle... You need to go look up a man that goes by the name: Billy Sly Fox. Most of the Paiute around here consider him crazy, but I think once you get to meet him you will know better then to think the same.” Gray nods again and shakes his hand. “I will do so. Words of such from people as wise as you, should always be followed.” As he speaks , Danielle looks over her shoulder at her room, giving Josh a what the hell look. Josh just smiles and moves to Danielle's side. He put his arm around her waist and just smiled at her. Josh then addressed her grandfather, as he looked down at the feather wand, recognizing half of the feathers bound in it as Dani's. “Sir, you honor us with your presence here.” Dani's grandfather continues to talk to Gray. “Also, thank you for the offer of your guest room, but my nephew and I will be staying tonight at the Suncoast. I used some frequent flier miles to get here, and I've got the whole vacation package through them. Room, meals, some gambling money. I might as well use it while I'm here.” The old man chuckled again, Gray added to it with a bit of his own. At that point the old man turned to look at Josh. ?Ah... yes... Young Mr. Brand. Joshua Bodaway Brand?? Josh nodded to him. ?I do believe me and you have some talking to do. Am I right?? Danielle almost looked horrified. Her eyes shifted to her grandfather then back to Gray, as if pleading with him to say something. Josh tightened his grip around her waist just enough to calm her. “Yes, sir. We do. I would be ready any time you are.” The old man smiled, almost toothless in his age and years of personal abuse. ?I'm sure you will be. How about tonight all of us go and have dinner at the Suncoast. We can enjoy things as a family, and maybe steal some time away from them to talk after we've had some good drinks to loosen our tongues?? Josh smiled at that. “Yes, sir. I would like that.” Just then, Dani's second cousin gave an audible huff out of from his nose. Dani's look of concern came back. His actions drew a comment from the old man though. ?Young Steven, you dishonor this meeting before it even starts?? The younger man looked startled. Josh picked it up though, the man didn't like him. “Grandfather... he's not...” At that point, Grandfather Screaming Eagle smacked his nephew across the top of his head. ?You will not dishonor them in their own house. You are going to question Mr. Brands bloodline, correct?? The younger man didn't answer. “I would advise against continuing that way. Mr. Brand's father comes from the line of Great Chief Seattle himself. And his mother, while not of native blood, was fully adopted as a member of the Saquamish Nation. She was one of them in spirit, if not in blood. Their father took her as his wife only after that....” The younger man lowered his head. Josh suddenly beamed in his direction as if to send him the same message as if he had just flipped him the bird. Gray gave a smirk to his brother as if to tell him that it looked like the old man had done his homework. “Now, we must go and get ourselves ready and make the arrangements for the dinner. You will meet us there at 7pm.” Dani answered as Josh was still glaring at her cousin. ?Yes, Papa. We'll be there. We'll need to start getting ready too. Semi formal? Casual?? “Hmm, I think it's a pretty nice restaurant, but this is what I am. They won't have a problem with me. But you had better be dressed somewhat nice. As with your two protectors.” “Okay, Papa.” “Steven...” The old man turned and started to walk towards the front door. Jessie moved in to open it for him as the man's nephew helped support him on the side without his cane. Dani immediately turned on Josh once they were out of the room. ?What the hell were you doing there?? “Hey, I don't want to put up a false front in front of the guy. He is the only person who by tradition could tell me to bug out and never see you again.... other then you.” Dani took defeat on that one. He was right. “Okay, I guess I'm heading to the shower to get ready. Now to see what you guys were doing in my room.” “Our room...” Josh tried to correct her, but she was already heading to the door. He tried to catch up and was coming up right behind her as she opened the door. She inhaled her shock as she did. Sitting over near the sliding glass doors that lead to the balcony outside was a large black iron cage. It was open on the side and the top for it's occupant to move about freely. On a perch on top of the cage was a large bird with feathers of a dark blue, with yellow highlights near the neck/jawline and around the eyes. Some of the blue goes into a mixture of blue and gray that would resemble a Russian blue cat's fur. The bird was about 2 feet tall from foot to head, with a long feathered tail that dropped down another few feet. It flapped it's wings and put out a loud yell, a squawk if you would. She covered her mouth as she walked over to it. “That's Angel...” He said it as the Mexican equivalent of Angel, pronounced as if the 'g' was an 'h' or 'an-hel'. With the range of Hycinth Macaws, it made sense. They were native to Central and South America. “I figured it was time we got a pet. And this one doesn't need a litter box, can talk to you... and he's got wings like you. He's a Hyacinth Macaw, very rare. They eat a special diet of nuts and that.” Dani walked up and went to touch the bird. However, it took the opportunity to climb up onto her hand, and then scooted up onto her arm. ?He's beautiful!? Dani didn't flinch at all. “Yeah, Gray doesn't think so. It's tried to bite him 4 times already. The last one took some skin off. Good thing he's a fast healer.” Dani started to pet it's head and the back of it's neck. “I wonder why. It would smell Gray and think that a wolf was after it. It would defend itself.” ?Yeah, guess it would think he was trying to eat it or something. So you like it?? She turned and looked over her shoulder at him. “Yeah, I like it. Now how did you come about the money for this? I've looked at large parrots before and they usually cost an arm and a leg.” “Yeah, well... guess you could say I pulled it out of the expense account. I added it into the cost of the car.” ?You got a car?? “Not yet. They're delivering it next week. Special Ordered with everything I could think of.” She gave him a stern look. “You're not supposed to abuse the opportunities they're giving you...” He looked like he was going to pout. Before he could answer, Gray walked in the partially open door, pushing his brother a bit out of the way. “We've got a problem...” Gray had papers in his hands off the fax machines and had his cell phone already out. ?What's up?? Gray shook his head in disbelief. “The match... the frigging' match.” ?What?? Josh asked him as he tried to look over his shoulder. Suddenly Josh ripped the papers out of his hands. ?What do they mean I can't tag with her?? Gray was fumbling with the phone, trying to use one hand to find a number on it. “Your contract. It states the only person you and I are allowed to tag with are each other.” Dani stayed where she was, seeing the bird ruffle it's feathers out with Gray's presence in the room. “Then you two fight them. I'm cool with it. Unite vs. Shadow and Ambrosi. I'll valet... I think I'd like to see you toss them around the ring like rag dolls anyway.” Gray didn't smile at her comment. “They already talked to Shadow about it. Apparently she's demanding YOU in the ring. Meaning that as of right now.... it's a handicap match unless we find you a new partner.” Dani's stomach felt like it had just headed to her feet. ?Handicap match unless I can find a new partner?? She yelled it, the bird jumped off her hand, back onto his cage and then headed back for the perch it had been on before. Dani turned around to address Gray directly. ?They can't do that? That's bullshit! How dare they!? She literally stomped her feet as she then moved across the room to rip the papers out of Josh's hands. She looked at them and then tossed them into the air. The papers began to fall as she stomped towards her bathroom. Gray looked at Josh and vice versa. “I think she's mad.” “No shit, sherlock. Thanks a lot. Ruin my night...” Gray hit his brother in the ribs. “Better me do it, before Grandpa tells you to keep your mitts off.” Josh didn't look too happy with that one. “Lighten up. I'm going to start spam calling Sights to figure this one out. She's not getting into that ring alone with that psychopath.” “Yeah, and Ambrosi is pissed too.” Gray chuckled with that, he fully remembered that Josh already had a run in with Ms. Lia Vlassy. “Let me make some calls. We'll get this fixed up.” Josh went to push his brother out the door. “Yeah, you fix it, I'll fix her. Go...” ~To Be Continued~
