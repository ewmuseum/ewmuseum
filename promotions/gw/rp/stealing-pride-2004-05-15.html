Stealing Pride

15 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/15/04 'His ass is mine.' That was the repeating theme that's been cycling constantly through Train's mind since Evolution. Ever since he'd lost to that chump Brick. Ever since his undefeated streak had come to and end at the hands of that fucking kiss ass. Ever since the punk ass little muthafucka Deacon Holmes had stuck his nose where it didn't belong. Everytime Train sat down to think about how his streak had come to an end at the hands of Brick he felt like puking his guts out. Now that sellout fuck thinks he has the upperhand in this. Now he thinks he's the better man. Knowing that bullshit was probably running through Brick's mind right now pissed him off to no end. Mostly cuz Brick didn't have shit to do with why he beat Train. Sure he was in the match. Yeah, he hit his little Bricklayer for the win. But it wasn't him who caused the shit. Naw.... that was all Deacon's doing. Train had the whole thing in hand. He had Brick on the mat and the next thing Brick was going to see was the muzzle flash when Train put a 'Hollowpoint' through his dome. That's how it should've gone down. But somebody stepped out of line. Everybody knew the punk ass Platinum Champion was gonna be at ringside. Train knew that jealous, spotlight sucking trick was gonna try to steal all the thunder. That's why Train had given his ass some advice. Long before that match jumped off Train made the shit clear. Deacon needed to do what he was sent out there to do. He was supposed to be a commentator. He was supposed to do what he does best...run his fucking mouth. He told him to stay in his place. Well.... The little muthafucka forgot his place. The shit between him and Deacon? It hadn't ever been personal like the shit between him and Brick. See Brick opened his mouth about Train is a way he shouldn't have and that made it personal. Getting on Deacon? That had all been about business. About getting some new jewelry... a new platinum chain so big it would fit around his waist. But now? Oh no... That 'strictly business' shit was all down the tubes. Not only was the shit personal... It was more personal between Deacon and Train that it had ever been between Train and Brick. See Brick didn't know when to keep his damn mouth shut...but at least he wasn't a thief. There wasn't no mistake about the shit. Deacon had stolen what had belonged to Train. The victory Train knew from the jump he could take from Brick anytime he wanted? Gone. His on unbeaten singles record? Gone. But more importantly than all that? His shot at the World Title? Up in fucking smoke. Anybody who didn't know that shot at the Kurt Cobain wanna be Josh belonged to Train was a damn fool. Donny McNasty..... Pshhhhh.... What kinda bullshit is that? How many times had Train left his punk ass looking up at the lights? And what had that drunk fuck done in the past few weeks besides ass fucking some drunk midgets? And now the muthafucka gets a title shot?! Naw... Train had been handling business. Dropping fools left and right while linging business for The M.O.B. The second Train had seen the card for Evolution he knew... For all the bullshit Brick was a former World Champion. If he had dropped Brick the way he was about to before Deacon decided to show his ass? That title shot would've been his. Train had been robbed. Now... You wanna steal from a cat from the streets? No problem. Do what you gotta do. Oh, but you wanna live to tell the tale? Now that's some different shit all together. It was times like this that Train struggled. It was times like this that Train had to remind himself he wasn't leading the street life no more. That it was wrong to kick in someone's front door and turn them into abstract are on the walls. Zero Estates wasn't far from here. He knew that little muthafucka was there. He knew that little muthafucka was fast, but then he also knew that Mr. 9mm slug was faster. He knew the ducktape would hold his scrawny ass while he listened to his bitch scream for help from the next room. Cocksucker. Brick? Train would get that loss back. He has a new shot at his punk ass this Evo. He'd handle that muthafucka then, no sweat. For now? Deacon was priority #1. And it was Deacon's lucky lifetime. Train wasn't living life like he used to. For the sake of his own sanity. So the nine and the shotgun would stay where they were for now. But there was no getting around the shit. Train had to hurt him. Deacon's ass was his and he was going to leave his boot print where the sun didn't shine. Now the question was how? How was Train going to hurt Deacon? How do you hurt an egomaniacal pissant who doesn't give enough of a shit about himself not to keep doing that suicidal bullshit he was known for, knowing full well the shit was taking months off his career everytime he does it? It was simple. You don't go after his body. You go after his pride. Yeah...there it was. Train knew how to deal with Deacon now. The little bastard didn't have much brains, but he has pride galore. And why not. The shoes...the magazine covers....the belts. Well. He couldn't do anything about the shoes or the Mags. Oh...but the belts? That was a different story. The Platinum strap? That was headed one place...over Train's shoulder. As for that piece of glorified tin they call the Cruiserweight Title? Fuck that piece of shit. Deacon thought it would be cool to rob someone. Let's see how he likes getting robbed.
