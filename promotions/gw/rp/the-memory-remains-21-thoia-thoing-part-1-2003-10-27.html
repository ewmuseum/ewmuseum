At the bottom of the staircase, I stop, straining my ears, listening for some clue as to where the intruder is. Faintly, I hear what sounds like a moan and rattling of glass in a nearby hallway. Reaching out, I uncover Deacon’s head and help him free the rest of his body from the mess.
