One Life to Live

1 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/1/04 It was 6 a.m. Monday morning. The place was ABC Television Center. The producers of 'One Life to Live' had called Brick to make a deal for he and DMAK to appear on an episode. In the episode they appear in, DMAK has a gig at the nightclub Capricorn. Since Brick is the group's manager, he would appear in the scenes with them, making deals with the club's owner R.J. Gannon, played by Timothy Stickney. 'Yeah, Brick and DMAK here for One Life to Live.' 'Yeah, you want to go to Studio ' 'OK, thanks a lot. C'mon, fellas.' As they walked towards the studio where 'One Life to Live' was being filmed, they each talked. 'My mother couldn't believe me when I told her we were gonna be on One Life to Live. She's got her VCR ready for when the show airs.' 'Yeah, my lady's got hers ready, too,' said Keith, the group baritone. 'We don't have anything to do with the other stories, do we?' asked Damien, one of the tenors in the group. 'No, of course not. We're just cameos.' 'And we just get a few lines with RJ and maybe Evangeline?' asked Andre, the group’s second tenor. 'Y'all do. I got a few more scenes than that.' 'What scenes do you have?' asked Marcus, the group's bass. 'I think they have one of the kids on the show as one of my biggest fans. I go to meet the kid. Either that, or somebody asks me for an autograph.' As they reached the studio, a production assistant greeted them. 'Hi. Brick and DMAK, right?' 'Yeah.' 'Welcome to the set. Now you've all got your lines down?' 'I do,' said Brick. 'Yeah, we do, too.' 'Well, there's gonna be somebody with a copy of the script, just in case. The director just likes these shoots to be precise.” 'I can understand that. Did the producers and director mention which songs they want for the club scenes?' 'Yeah, you guys just came out with a cover album, didn't you?' 'Yeah, we did.' 'Any two songs from that album.' 'Well, how about 'Kiss and Say Goodbye' and...' 'I'm thinking the second song should either be 'Gloria' or 'Send for Me'.' Brick hated making decisions like this. More often than not, Damian and Andre were heavily showcased when choosing singles for radio airplay. “Let me know which one you choose. I’ll tell the director.” As the PA started to leave, Brick called after him. “Wait, I’ve decided. They’ll do “Send for Me”.” “OK, cool. I’ll tell the director.” As the PA walked off, a deep voice called. “Hey, Brick, right?” Brick turned to see a dark-skinned man with long dreadlocks approach him. “Hi, I’m Timothy Stickney.” “I know,” said Brick as he shook the actor’s hand. “You play R.J. Nice to meet you. This is my group DMAK. This is Damian.' 'Hi.' 'Marcus.' 'Hey.' 'Andre.' 'Hi.' 'And Keith.' 'Hey.' “Welcome to the set. Let me show you around. I hear you're trying out for the lead role in a Luke Cage movie.' 'Yeah, how'd you know?' 'I collect a lot of comic books. Plus I read about it on the Internet. I think you'd be perfect for the part.' 'You think so?' 'Yeah.' Stickney led them to a set designed to resemble a chic-looking nightclub. 'And here is the set for Capricorn.' 'This is where all our scenes are,' asked Keith. 'Yeah, that and the scenes where Brick and I make the deal for you to appear.' Just then, a middle-aged man of medium build walked up to Brick. This was the director. 'Hey, Brick, DMAK. Good to see you.' 'Good to be here. We got our lines down and we're ready to go. Right, fellas?' 'Right.' 'I just thought you should know. There's been a slight change.' 'What do you mean” I'm not doing any scenes in Capricorn?' 'Oh, you're still doing scenes in Capricorn, but we added a scene with you and Ilene Kristen. She plays Roxy. Anyway, the idea of the scene is that she's something of a fan and she goes on about an old house show.' 'Is that all?' 'Yeah.' 'OK, cool.' 'OK, everyone take their places. We're gonna do a quick run-through and then we're gonna start taping.' Brick, DMAK and the actors all took their places and prepared to go through their scenes. And somewhere, Brick's friends and family and DMAK's friends and family all set their VCRs.
