Reminiscing...

3 November 2003

Reminiscing... JJ Jarone Global Wrestling Promo #688 Date: 11/3/03 Location: Basement Have you ever felt reality before? If you think about it, the life you live moves everyday as the world turns. But you never look back on all things you have done, and realized the terror you may have caused. Well reality hit me for the first time today. I am not an average man roming the streets like the rest, I am a superstar. As I sit here in my basement with one light on in the corner, and a beer to my right I reminisce on the past. Slowly working my way up to my fame, and turning point. I take a quick sip of my beer, my first sip of my beer in a long time. I remember JJ Jarone as the beer drinking bad ass who just never gave a fuck about anyone but number one. Now I am a young man who trains all day, and just never has time to even ponder about drinking, or raising hell. Why? Because this is my road to being a superstar, and on this road there is no room for screw ups. I sit here drinking by beer with my black hooded sweatshirt on, trying to be my old self for a good half hour before I move back to reality. As I sit there I close my eye's, lean back and begin to reminisce on the beginning. My eye's are shut, and picture begin to flash through my mind of my early childhood. I am no longer sitting on a stool in a darkened basement, with a beer on my lap. I am now twelve years old again, in middle school. I'm sitting at a desk wearing nothing but blue jeans with holes in them, and a gray and white sweatshirt, with a hood covering the crown of my head. I slowly lift my head peering at my eighth grade teach Mrs. Walmond. She use to be a real bitch, no one liked her. Her and her curly red hair, multiple freckles on her face, and a gut that could use some slim fast. The teacher approaches me and pauses in font of my desk. I note gets slipped on the table as she walks off and sits down in font of her desk. I pick up the note only to read in dark blue lettering. 'You have been assigned to the office, please leave now and collect your punishment without a scene.'. Right there I knew she only slipped that note so it could be private. Usually when the teacher would assign me to the office students would peer at me waiting to argue, but this time I decided to go. I knew there would be a lot of students and teachers in the hall to terrorize. I stood up from my desk and began to walk out the door, right before exiting the class room I peered at my teacher with a sickening grin on my face, and a quick wink of the eye. I slammed the class room door and walked down the hall with dark blue lockers to my left and right. Ahead of me was a kid a grade below me. James Atwater was his name, he was a little punk who liked to start trouble with the underclass man. I decided I just didn't like this kid enough to give him my respect. Yes, I was a punk, I liked serving kids there own treatment they hand out. As I approached the kid closer and closer I could see the fear in his eyes. Right before passing him I stick out my right arm shoving him into a locker door. 'What the hell was that for Jackson!?' That's what the kids called me back then, I guess my last, or first name just wasn't good enough for them. They had to pick my middle name, it was at some points embarrassing. Usually only my home boys would call me Jackson, so I immediately took offence. 'You got a problem the way I handle things around here punk? I have a right and a left saying you don't.' I replied. 'No, no problem Jarone. But next time you decide to take advantage, how about you wait till I get my boys together.' 'Yea that's if you can pry them away from there chess board. Tell the captain of the chess team he can come to.' 'You're going to be a nobody and you know it James Jackson Jarone. You don't even deserve to have that first name James, only greats have that name I tell ya. You are a piece of trailer trash, and always will be.' I can feel my face get heated. 'What you gonna do Jarone, hit me? You know damn well if you hit me it's off home for three weeks for ya. Yea, suprised I know huh, everyone knows you can't lay a finger on anyone or else suspension, or even the consequences of getting kicked out of school.' Now I knew what he was saying was right, but before I knew it he was on the ground with a scratch under his right eye and a bruise surrounding his eye. That's right I hit the punk, and if I had the chance to do it again, I would. You wondering where I ended up? You guessed it, the office. Same place I was heading in the first place. So there I sat on a wooden bench. Names were carved on the legs of the bench, and the white walls could mind shatter most. But I usually toughened it out. To tell you the truth I couldn't tell which was worst, sitting in the office for hours, or having to here my father bitch at me, and shouted obscene gestures. Well before long I was at home, getting ready and preparing for the lexture. 'What in the hell were you thinking James? What in the hell were you thinking, when you hit that kid. I'm not putting up with this anymore James, you are now kicked out of school, and on your own.' My father shouted. 'Fine, it's all good dad, I'd rather be on my home than be in this shit hole. Why would I stay here anyway? Everyone's always got a miserable attitude. But if that's the way you want it, fine. I'm a show you guys. I'm a show you guys I can be he seed that don't need much to succeed in life.' Well there goes that, I'm out no t he open road, with no where to go. No warm house to goto, no bed to rest my eye's in. I was on my own, doing what I gotta do. I knew what I wanted to do, I wanted to show my parents that I could make it without them. I'm gonna be someone big in my life, and I'll show them. I'm gonna move out of this shot hole of a town, and move in to the city. Once I cross them tracks there's no looking back. To Be Continued...
