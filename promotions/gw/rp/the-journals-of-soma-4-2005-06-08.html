The journals of Soma #4

8 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/08/05 A shriek of colors. A nearness that invites you to scrape it with your teeth. A silhouette lost in sparkles. And the feeling that your eyes can’t see it all, that outside the image there’s more passionate scene that we’re forbidden to see.- Luis Royo I am not a god. Nor am I mortal. I am part of nature. And nothing has survived as long as I. I have been hunted by more creatures then I would like to remember. But I fear nothing. And with each attack, I take in another memory. I take another life. I take another soul. These souls despise me. They try and destroy the little humanity I have. And I let them. Who needs humanity when they have power? Humanity is a weakness. One weakness that I will one day cure. Soma sat in his limo quietly thinking about all that had transpired on Evolution this week. It really surprised him that Mike Biddle accepted his offer so easily. But he didn’t blame him. All Soma did for him is make him stronger. Soma knew he would bring the viciousness out of Biddle. And even he would see into the oblivion. A small smile came to his face as he realized that Shadow was now his student and possibly later down the road even more. For the first time in a long time he was excited. He hadn’t had a student since teaching warriors how to be skilled assassins for the Lair and the Nevermore. But unlike his students there, Shadow was like him. Her teachings would be so much rougher than a mere mortal. He would teach her everything she was willing to learn. But he knew something clouded her mind. He now was able to feel what she felt, feeling the emotions she held back in the darkest part of her mind. He went seeking these emotions that she felt for others. He needed to make sure that those feelings would not become a nuisance for him later on. He sat there, eyes closed, reaching out to her mind. After about twenty minutes, he was in a trance, not moving, not breathing, not anything. He just sat there in the lavish limo and entered the mind of Shadow. When Soma opened his eyes he was standing in a thick fog. The fog seemed to be all around him and in every direction it seemed to only get thicker. Soma began to walk in the unknown place, looking for something that would give him a rough estimation on where he needed to begin. After walking in what Soma thought to be north, through the fog, he saw a young girl sitting in front of a crimson door. Soma looked at the door and saw that there was a tree that had been carved into. The tree held a family crest and the branches grew six generations. Her bright green eyes with a hint of gold looked him over, and Soma kept his eyes on the little girl. She wore a plain black dress and held a single black rose. Soma slowly walked over to the girl and squatted down in front of her even though he still towered over her. He smiled at the girl and she gave him an odd look tilting her head to one side. “You’re like the others?” “To a degree yes.” “Why are you here?” “I need to find out some things. Can you help me?” “No. I’m not allowed to talk to you. I don’t know you.” “But I bring you no harm little one.” “No. I’m too far from the castle as it is.” Soma smiles once again. “Let me take you back. Your family must be so worried.” “Well’ok.” The girl once again looked him over but this time smiled as well. As she stood up the door opened and a bright light came from inside. A flood of pictures took over his mind like a strobe light of memories. Childhood. Family. Pride. Honor. Death. The pictures swirled in his mind making the journey even more stressful than it was, but he took in the knowledge he needed. He saw the way she was raised. He understood her path was already chosen the day she was born, and now part of her re-education had taken place. The pain from her memories had almost hurt Soma, but he knew he must press on. He knew the journey had just begun and there were many stops on the way that maybe even stronger than that one. As soon as the though crossed his mind the sky in Shadow’s mind turned a dark crimson color and the fog a smoky black. All of a sudden the scene has changed to a place driven by war. Screaming. Hatred. Death. Soma walked around like an apparition, watching a battle that he knew he had been apart of. He watched his thin blooded cousins slaughter each other for power. He watched on until he saw another apparition like himself. He tried to make the ghost out but couldn’t from the distance. He moved in closer only to reveal that this apparition was another version of Shadow. This version much older but the difference was she appeared not to be able to see what was going on around her. She could not see her own family being destroyed. She could not see the younger version of himself fighting at her sire’s side and not being able to save him. She could not see her own brother fighting to keep his family safe. They were memories he had forgotten and now knew why Shadow was so important to him. He walked over to the older version of Shadow, the same bright green eyes with the gleam of gold looking into his as he approached. She was beautiful as always. Her pale skin showed bright through the dark smoke and blood red sky. She saw him and smiled as she moved the hair from her face. He smiled at her trying not to show the pain he was about to show. “Hello again my young one.” “You should not be here.” “Why not” Is there something you hide from me?” “There are things that I hide from myself.” Soon the fighting grew tenser around them and thick red droplets fell from the sky, the blood being spilled, now a storm sweeping the land around them. The ground began to shake and crack under their feet. “You must open yourself to your past Shadow or you will surely die.” “Maybe that’s what I want. Death. I failed to help those closest to me and they died. I should have died with them.” “You survived because there is more in store for you my young one. Your teachings have been left incomplete. Give me your hand and come with me. I shall give you a second chance. I’ll you the true nature of what you are. All I ask is for you to remember.” She looked down, a single tear falling down her porcelain-pale face. Before the ground could split between them she extended her hand and Soma grabbed it, pulling her toward him as they went through another door. The memories once again pour into his head like the pain of getting hit in the face with a baseball bat. When he looked up, he saw a dark and rainy city and that he and Shadow were standing on a skyscraper looking down. As Soma looked around he realized what they were there to do. He looked over at Shadow, now the cold killer that had attracted him to her in the first place. He smiled at her but her face showed nothing. She began to run the length of the ledge and began jumping from building to building. Soma followed closely. The man never knew they were hunting him. He wore simple clothes. Nothing looked of too much value. His glasses and balding red hair made him look even simpler. The rain poured on him, almost giving him the worst day possible. He did not know what lay ahead. Shadow and Soma followed the man for blocks, but finally the man turned onto a dark alley. Both vampires slowly started down the side of the skyscraper on all fours, more like creatures in the wild then man. The killing itself was done quickly but the actions were too fast for the natural eye. Soma and Shadow both lunging at the man from the building, circling him like lions on their prey. The man did not put up a fight, knowing why she had been sent. She ripped out his heart and fed on it. The act made the scene change completely, their surroundings no longer a rainy city but a warm night in on top of a hotel. Two figures stood at the edge overlooking the city. Soma did not recognize either of the figures. Shadow looked hard at the two figures but with no luck. “I can’t see them. I don’t know who they are.” Shadow said looking down once again in disgust with herself. “Are you content with not being able to see them?” “No.” “Then do something about it. I can only help you so much.” “What can I do?” “Bring to light what is in your darkness.” Shadow looked at Soma almost with distaste but realized the look would solve nothing. She closed her eyes, trying to concentrate on the two figures. After what seemed to take forever a grey light appeared over the two figures, which turned out to be two men. “Who are they?” “I only have a guess.” “Were they important to you?” “Yes.” “Do you know why?” “No. I can’t remember.” “Then remember. Go to them.” She slowly walked toward them making sure not to scare the two men, even though she was not sure if they could be scared. She stopped a few feet in front of them and none of them made a sound. “Amir” Riven?” The first man gave her a smile while the second looked down and nodded. Soma began to hear sobbing coming from Shadow as she embraced the two men from her past. As she took them in, memories began forming all around them of all the acts that happened in the forgotten part of Shadow’s subconscious. Soma watched the accounts fly around as if they were vultures flying around a carcass. As the two phantoms faded from in front of Shadow, the scene around them changed once again. The scene now was like it was in the beginning, fog everywhere and Soma and Shadow not knowing where to go. They stood there, looking in each direction in search for a sign, until finally they saw another phantom, this time in the shape of a child. The child walked up to them and gave a low hiss. “We have been waiting on you mother. Father wants you to come see him now.” “You tell Martel he can come see me. He has no power over me anymore.” “I wouldn’t want to make him angry mother. You, out of all people, know what he’s capable of.” The child with a devilish grin began taunting his mother. Reminding her of all she had to deal with her ex husband and her children. She looked to Soma who stood there with no sign of emotion on his face. “Are these demons holding you from becoming what is expected of you?” “I can’t just forget. I have to care. These experiences make up who I am.” “These experiences will be your downfall.” Soma turned his back on Shadow. She looked back at the child who now was accompanied by Martel. Soma looked at the man, bared his fangs, and gave a low growl. Martel did the same. Shadow stood between them not knowing what to do. “Do you want to get past these experiences Shadow?” “She will never forget us, we are apart of her. Forever.” “You are wrong specter, she can and will forget. Concentrate Shadow, and you will be able to do it.” “Try it Shadow, and you will perish. Remember it was I who made you who you are and nobody else.” The statement made by Martel made her glare at him. She closed her eyes, concentrating on ridding herself of the specter. A dark red light shined on her body, and appearing in her hand, a katana. She looked down at the magnificent weapon whose blade looked to be made of the darkest onyx while the hilt itself was made of black marble. Soma saw the weapon and smiled as he turned to look at the phantom. Shadow handed the weapon over to Soma. “I want to be rid of them Leinad. Slay them for me.” Soma let out a laughter that sounded more like the roar of some sort of animal as he rushed Martel slamming the sword into his chest, pulling it out and chopping of his head. The child began to scream as he tried to attack Soma. Soma looked down at the child and smiled as he tossed the sword back to Shadow “You have dishonored your mother’now pay the price.” The child’s screams were dimmed by the sword. They stood there in silence thinking everything was over but Soma looked around as he was still in Shadow’s subconscious. “What are you hiding from me Shadow?” “Nothing.” “That’s a lie. I would not still be here if that were true.” Soma sniffed the air and looked around. Two more doors appeared. Standing at one, Dawn Mcfarlane and at the other Virgil Ambrosi. “Leinad, what are you going to do to them?” “Nothing my dear. Nothing at all. Like you I have an interest in these two. Show me what you know of them.” “Will you harm them if I do?” “No, but if you don’t show me I will kill them.” She nodded and opened her mind to Soma. He smiled as he watched the memories fly around the two. Behind the hardness of the night there is beauty. Sensuality is half hidden in cold metal. An image burns itself into our retinas’and the drama begins.-Luis Royo Las Vegas, Nevada The trance Soma had been in left him disoriented as he came out of it. He smiled at the knowledge he had learned from the recesses of Shadow’s mind. He sat in the limo as it still headed toward its destination thinking who would be the next member of Rex Deus. Global Wrestling’s roster was now full of those who did not know the fear that he had once inflicted in the ring. Soma became a champion in what seemed to be overnight. He now had a chance at another championship belt. He would not let it pass him up. He would fight harder than usual. He would have to show those rookies he should not be taken lightly. With Shadow as his first student and the mortal, Mike Biddle aiding him to start Rex Deus, he knew there would be big things in his future. The limo came to a halt but Soma knew he was nowhere near where he should have been. He looked out the windows and saw nothing but desert. He immediately picked up his phone. “Driver, what is the meaning of this” Where are we?” “Where is she?” “Where is who?” “Where is Lia?” Soma let out a small sinister laugh. “You are the one who holds her. You are the one I saw in her mind. Virgil, how nice to meet you.” “Take me to her and no harm shall come to you.” Soma kicked the limo door open and slid out the back of the limo. The man who was driving did the same. They stood there facing each other in the hot, dark, wastelands of Nevada. The man looked at Soma as his features became a bit snakelike, his eyes turning a dark crimson. The man did not move and neither did Soma. “There has to be a way to solve this without having to kill you dear sir.” “Tell me where Lia is.” “How about I do more than that.” The man seem to let his posture relax a little showing he was listening. Soma’s snakelike form changed back to his regular face as he took a step toward the man. “What do you propose?” “Join me. Join Rex Deus. I know what you are and you have only seen a small portion of what I am. You can learn and you will be close to your precious Lia.” “Fine. Do not cheat me. You will find me to be a most powerful enemy.” “I have enough enemies right now. Its time I make friends.” “Then what do we do now?” “We go back into the city. There is much to discuss.” Behind desire is a beast.-Luis Royo
