Lost Loves: Part One, What She Didn't Know

27 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/27/2004 They say, whoever they actually are, that it is better to have loved and lost, than to have never loved at all. But, I wonder, did Adam think that about Eve, after she gave him the apple, and destroyed paradise? Women. Can’t live with them, can’t shoot them. Or rather, I could. I could kill just about anybody without batting an eye. Didn’t matter? the point was, I wanted to kill her. I wanted to hurt her. I wanted to make her pay, an eye for an eye? revenge. Or really, a life for my life. I mean, it was simple logic really. She pulled the trigger; she started this entire chain of events. But? something made me hesitate. Love. A double-edged sword, and it had carved me up inside. On one hand, I hated her with every fibre of my being. On the other hand, she still held the keys to my heart. I was torn. I just wanted to see her again. I brought her to me, with a few well placed words, and a situation I knew she couldn’t resist. Jewels. Priceless diamonds from Africa. A cache, here in Chicago, my stomping grounds? in the unlikely possession of the Russian mafia? held in none other than an underground fight club. It was B movie material at its best, but she took the bait. Women, some say they’re complex? but when it comes to her, I could read her like a book. She was, after all, my soulmate. I never believed in that mystical crap, I mean, someone being your other half? Your perfect compliment? It’s ridiculous. Yeah, at least until it happens to you. Have you ever looked at someone across a crowded room, and felt the entire universe fall away, leaving nothing but the two of you. It’s like being reborn, like a vital piece you hadn’t know was missing was suddenly filled in, completing the picture. That’s how it was for me. Someone once said, it’s better to have loved and lost, than to have never loved at all? sometimes I almost believed it. Sometimes, I almost felt like I was over her? and then something would bring it all back. A word, overheard? a smell, carried on the wind. I watched her, smelling the scent of her perfume on the wind, patchouli oil and sandalwood, a scent that still made my blood boil. She was atop the roof across the street, oblivious to my scrutiny, scouting the location. Her moves were mine, she was like looking into a mirror. I knew she was going to check her gun before she did? a Walther, same as the one I still carried. Good taste in weapons, German engineering at its best. I hated her then, more than ever, as I stood in the rain, allowing myself to get wet, letting the sky reflect my sorrow. Perhaps I would kill her after all. Jade, my Judas? but that wasn’t her name either. I called her that; because of her eyes? she’d used that name as an alias. She’d had many, but the only other one I knew was Kylie. Kylie Serenade. She hadn’t told me that. They had. The CID. Information, my primary weapon... second only to my P99. I know, you already know what happened when she entered that building. She blew them all away, my murderess? and then, she had to face me? Her version was slightly skewed, so bear with me while I tell the truth. ---- He stood there in the doorway, backlit by the harsh fluorescents in the gym cum morgue behind him. He didn’t say anything, nor did he need to as he recognized the look on her face. She knew him, even though she couldn’t see him clearly. He took a few breaths, his chest rising and falling, as she lay there. He could smell her fear, as much as he could smell her scent, mingling with the sickening bouquet of the candles. He wanted to shoot her, his hand tensed above the holster he wore low on his hips, like an old fashioned gunslinger. But, something stopped him, even then. He saw her move then, trying to hide it, looking for her guns, ever the clever fox. “Jackson?” One word, an entreaty. She sounded helpless, but he knew she was anything but. A snake? a cold-blooded killer. He squeezed his eyes shut briefly, still hesitating in the doorway, trying to banish the thoughts of sparing her. “Shut up,” he muttered as he stepped across the threshold? ---- He sat alone, naked from the waist up, the candlelight flickering off his skin, painting him with shifting light and shadow. His face was cradled in his hands, his shoulders shaking. Blood streaked his chest, looking almost like symbols carved in a language long forgotten. Somewhere in the distance, water dripped from the ceiling, eroding something, and he felt as though he was being washed away. Tears dripped down his arms, invisible in the half-light as he wept silently. The scars on his back stood out in stark relief, painted dark by the shifting shadows. Before him, was a wadded pile of linens, stained with dark blood, a gory testament to his actions. But there, amid the sheets, was something else. Something small, and pinkish. “She didn’t know,” he muttered, the words muffled by his hands. “Let her think it was rape, let her live, and remember only that. I couldn’t bring this curse upon her” this bastard?” He stood, and withdrew the lighter from his pocket. A silver Zippo. With absent motions, he pulled a crumpled pack of cigarettes from his back pocket, lighting one slowly, inhaling deeply. His face was streaked with crimson, wiped clean in precise trails from the tears that still fell unbidden from his eyes. He held the lighter aloft, and whispered something that made no sense. ??deserves to be loved, remember that? I did this out of love, my son?” He dropped the lighter, and the bundle of bloody rags burst into flames, searing him with the heat. He stood there, his expression unreadable, the cigarette smoke circling his head like a ghostly halo. He watched it burn? and die out, fading to embers? to ashes? and still he remained, the cigarette long burned away between his fingers. The tears dried? the night grew cold, and still he remained. ---- That was then? and this was now. Almost three months had passed, and I still wasn’t free of her. I know how it looks. Me, stalking her. I hadn’t come here because of her? you don’t have to believe me. I don’t care. I was a wrestler, in college? this isn’t new to me. Perhaps this is something I should have done a long time ago? making a choice for myself. Like she had. I saw her, kissing that freak in the mask. I wondered, had she seen him without it? Was he disfigured? I don’t trust people in masks. They clearly have something to hide. Like that asshole Silent Wind. Wind and Zero, I didn’t like either of them, although truth be told, there weren’t many people I did like. It seems, all I feel now is hatred. Hatred and loathing. I despised them all, even her. But especially him. El Serpenti. The Snake. They made a perfect pair. Both cold blooded? perhaps I should leave him to his fate. She would eventually turn on him, like the pitbull she was. Sometimes, I had trouble leaving well enough alone. Or maybe, I just wasn’t ready to give up what was? MINE.
