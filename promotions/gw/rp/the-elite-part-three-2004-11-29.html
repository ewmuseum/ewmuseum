The Elite (Part Three)

29 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.29.04 The smells of rosemary and thyme filled Ken's dining room as he sat the still steaming duck down onto the large cherry oak banquet table. He looked up to see everyone's champagne flutes filled and in the air, ready to toats. Everyone's, that is, except for Josh's. He was, instead, drinking from a bottle of Mountain Dew. Ken smiled at Josh and his choice to remain 'Straight X,' or a punk who chooses not to smoke, drink, or do drugs. One who chooses not to do anything that would harm his body, really, which is his temple. Well, anything but wrestling I suppose. It was beyond respectable and Ken wished that he had the will to do so himself. The way he figured, though, he'd beaten drugs and remained clean for eight years, the same with cigarettes. His alcoholism was beaten and he only drank in small quantities now. He'd made quite an accomplishment himself. 'A toast,' Ken's father bellowed as Gunner finished pouring the glasses for herself and Global's Superstar. 'Make a toast for us.' Ken smiled as the rest of the room chimed in as well, urging him to give some type of speech. However, to be honest, he wasn't even sure where to begin, or what to even say, really. It wasn't until his step-father, William Wright, looked at him with true parental love in his eyes and said, 'Whatever you say will be brilliant, son,' that Ken even had an inkling of how to vocalize things. It was the first time that he could recollect William ever reffering to him as 'son.' He raised his glass, causing everyone to cheer and follow suit. With the bubbling alcohol lifted high into the air, Ken began... 'There are many things in this life I am thankful for,' he began. 'I'm thankful for my job, which is my dream, and to Bubba McCoy for making my latest working environment a true home. I'm thankful to God for keeping me healthy long enough to persue this dream, eventhough he has put many difficult physical, mental, and emotional challenged in my way along the path. I'm thankful for the air which I breathe and the love which I share. I'm thankful for the relationships I've made. Therefore... a toast.' 'A toast,' repeated all those in presence. 'To love,' he spoke while gazing at his beautiful Gunner Addison. To Love 'To friendship,' he vocalized, peering out over the faces of Josh and Ceci Braden. 'Those that could be here,' he continued, looking to Bill Moresi and his dear friend Matthew Moore, 'and those that could not.' His thoughts raced through the past, ranging from the newest addition to his family of friends, Joshua Curtis, and stemming to those departed in the late Todd Ingram and Stephanie Self. A tear grazed his cheek. To Friendship 'To family,' he continued through a breaking voice as he gazed out over his parents, step-parents, his sister, and his half and step-brothers. Quickly, another tear formed in his eye as the visions of his recently departed grand-parents, Faye and Lamar Tumlin and Jo Partridge, smiled down at him from their beaming feast in the heavens. To Family 'And, finally,' he finished, 'to all of you. To family that are friends and friends that are family... Cheers!' Applause commenced as the glasses began to bottom up and hugs were given out. His mother gave him a proud look from across the table, mouthing the words 'well done' to her only son. 'To Ken,' resounded Josh. 'To Ken.' Josh was happy, for what seemed to be the first time in a long time. A very long time.
