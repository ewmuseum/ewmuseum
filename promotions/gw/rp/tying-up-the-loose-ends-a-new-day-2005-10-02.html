Tying up the loose ends... a new day.

2 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/2/05 In the mind of a man... Moving Day... Mourning Day... Today is the day that the world will mourn the loss of a man so many thought they could just step on, that they could just walk all over. Today that man officially dies a slow and painful death. But like a phoenix, he shall rise again, born again into a new mold. Today is the day that I go on a full bore rampage through everything that has ever done me wrong, or just shit all over my name and image. Today is my liberation from everyone else that sees me as a source for their ammusment. So far it's been busy, it's only 5 am in the morning and Ukyo and I are wide awake, waiting for the plane. In a few hours we'll be back in my home town of Las Vegas. As of this morning, the final arrangements were made to sell our condo here in Seattle. Soon, I'll be home... And if everything goes right, I'll have my daughters finally. 2 years ago, my fiance at the time, Rebecca, gave birth to two lovely twin girls, Kaylie and Kiley is what we named them. They bear my last night, but she's moved to try to keep me from them for some time. Always briinging up my tour schedule and everything else. What she doesn't know is that as of today, my lawyers say that we've got the silver bullet for her case. Going to shoot the damn thing down and take care of it.... I'm ready to move back home. I'm ready to get my life back on track. I'm ready to start anew. Only this time, it's take no prisoners with everything I do. It's time to take charge of things once again, just like I did when I was the highest paid wrestling celebrity on an entire satellite and cable network. The Outside World... A man stands, leaning onto a short, but still big, black limosine. He's wearing a pair of jeans, a black t-shirt for some rock band that can't be read due to the open collared shirt that he wears in a manner similar to a jacket over it. He's wearing a pair of very comfortable and broken in moccosin style boots on his feet, his pants tucked into them. His head bears the a hair that has a deep reddish color to it, yet his skin is as dark as a person who's been sitting in a tanning booth for some time. A pair of black, stylish sunglasses finishes off the look at the sun is just rising in the east to begin to impair the vision of those who look at it. Although with the gray clouds directly above, it might also be to hide the bags under the eyes of this person. He's got a few bags sitting at his feet, and a platic covered bundle, on a hanger, laying across the hood of the car. He's looking down at something in his hands, held at waist level. ?You okay?? A woman, almost as tall as him... oriental, Japanese to be exact, walks up next to him. It appears that she's got a bunch of bags of her own, and a similar set of more expensive clothing that needs to remain clean and is wrapped in plastic. She's wearing jeans and an Avenged Sevenfold shirt that looks to be a bit big for her. The really striking thing on her is her hair, dyed lavender blue. She wraps her arms to intertwine them with his and looks up at his face. “Yeah, I'm fine. Just want to get today done and overwith.” She looks down at what has his attention. A picture of two young girl, toddlers. Both with long curly hair, the same shade of red as his; his daughters. ?It'll all be fine once the court time is over. I have faith that today will end this fight, and you'll have won. Did you tell them yet what you intend to do?? She smiles a wide smile with perfect white teeth. “Nope. I want the shock factor with it. They're going to be on the plane anyway. I just want to know what this silver bullet they keep mentioning is. If it's not worth it, then I'll ask for an injunction today. We don't even have the house we're moving to setup yet. We could use a head start on that. Other then that, it's one of the hotels for about a week.” She nuzzled into his arm a bit as he put the picture back into his wallet, and then returned that to his right hip pocket. “That's okay.” She spoke, and he didn't. She knew many things were going through his mind. Things that really didn't need to be. Between a month ago when he asked his cousin for a release from the federation they were working in, till now it's all been pretty stressful. Ukyo had also asked to join him on that release. She was going to tough this out with him. His cousin had granted them their release and sent them on their way. Immediately he'd been in contact with the realtor to start the sale process on the condo, and now it was time to head back to Las Vegas, Nevada. He'd recently sold his small house far north of the city down there, so there wasn't much left. It was a true restart for him, and thus her. They stood there, silent as the plane, a small business model taxied up to where they were waiting. The small door opened and the stairs folded out into place. He wasn't supposed to have chartered his own flight, his cousin had offered to have one of her jets there to pick them up, but they were on a deadline and this was the quickest way. Two people walked out of the jet, two men wearing the uniforms of the charter company. He motioned for the bags that laid around them and then reached over and grabbed the set aside clothing. Ukyo grabbed hers and they both entered the plane. The workers took their baggage and made their way to the back of the plan to load it into the cargo hold. The duo hung the clothing up from the hooks provided and then took seats next to each other, at what was nothing more then a platform in front of them. This platform would become a meeting table for the other two passengers who now got up from their other seats and moved over to join the main passengers. A man and a woman, both wearing power suits that showed they were either millionaires or laywers. From the briefcases and folders of paper, the second option would be true. The man, a blonde who looked like he did spend too much time in a tanning salon extended his hand. “Blaise, I'm glad to see you could make it.” The red haired man, Blaise, took his hand and shook it. The man then moved his hand over to Ukyo and she took it for a moment. “This is my partner, Suzzane, on this case now, as she's the one who found the information that's going to end this during today's hearing.” The man sat down, across from Blaise and the woman sat across the table from Ukyo. Both checked their lap belts as they felt the plane start to move. “Alright, might as well get started. They've called for another hearing, I think I know why. But it's happening today. When we get there, we're going to have about an hour and a half to get to the courthouse. I see you two brought your clothes for this with you.” Blaise nodded. “Yeah, one monkey suit like yours, to a point. I'm not going to really take it to far. She's got a pretty nice dress she's going to change into. Still irks me.” Blaise wasn't one to hide what he truly was. Then again, these people had no idea what was actually working underneath all the exterior that showed on this man as it was. He was something else, beyond human. Unlike some though, he prefered to keep that a secret as much as he could. He was a mixture, a mutt... born of two pure blood lines mixed one time. His father was an immigrant from Tir Na Nog. Don't recognize that? Today we call it Ireland. But that's the name that a race of people called the Tautha De Danan, or the Seelie and Unseelie courts have for it. Reluctantly, Blaise is also known as Prince Turren MacNauda. But this is a name that you won't find him using in public. The other side of him, coming from his mother's people who are from the Great Basin area of the Mojave desert near Las Vegas... that's something completely different. The plane lifted off, heading towards the south from the Evergreen State to the Silver State. Blaise cut to the chase. “Alright, what is so important that we couldn't ask for a one or two day extension on this. We're going to be really tired when we get there as it is. I just signed the stuff to sell my condo last night. I'm not really up for something like this today.” “I assure you, Mr. McClancy, this is going ot win your case. Let me just show you. You're part of this case and we have all the court documents for this.” The man hands Blaise a folder that's held shut by an elastic band attached to it. He opens it and pulls out what looks to be... police reports. Arrest records, for the Clark County Sheriff's Department from the looks of it. “These are serious charges for someone who's trying to discredit a man such as yourself as a father, just because you were traveling a lot. Now all we need to do is get you two to counter the other punch they threw at the courts as of last week.” Blaise continued to look down at the reports in front of him, ignoring the lawyer. Ukyo continued in his stead. ?What is that?? The female lawyer decided to answer so as to not interrupt Blaise. “As of last week, Ms. Rebecca Fortune became Rebecca Morreti, husband of Francis Morreti. And not only have they filed for the full custody in this hearing, but for them to be allowed to change the surnames of the two girls.” Ukyo's face turned to a scowl. She'd had her run ins with both Becca and Francis before, right when she started dating Blaise about a year ago. But this was something new that would have to be overcome. The dangling carrot in the face of the court of a married couple, able to provide a 'stable home' to the girls, compared to the celebrity life that Blaise had lived for so long.... ?So how do we counter that punch?? Ukyo asked them as she watched Blaise stare, wordlessly, at the documents. “Well we could retaliate the same way. 15 minutes in one of the drive through chapels and you could become Mrs. McClancy.” That blow hit Ukyo hard. While she would never hide that was on her agenda for some day, on his too... it wasn't time yet. They had talked about it a lot recently after they had gotten their releases, but it wasn't time yet. “I don't think that's a plan. There are more things to it then that. Remember, I'm not a full U.S. Citezen. I'm here on a working visa. I'm a citezen of Japan.” She was right, and the lawyers knew it. While it does happen in Las Vegas, there is a lot of red tape for afterwards that is needed. Technically though, due to Ukyo's family position, when it happens, it'll be with them there, and a proper ceremony. Let's not forget Blaise's family and his position as a prince of his kind. You don't just jump into these things. The male laywer started to take over again. “Well we have this now. So the other thing really shouldn't matter.” Suddenly Blaise spoke out again, soft, but determined. ?How did you get this?? The male laywer's face went soft too. Everyone knew that Blaise loved Ukyo, but this was about the mother of his children. A woman who once held the same place that Ukyo did now. ?Well, there has beena big deal made about it recently in the area. Apparently there was a deputy clerk, who was in charge of reporting things to certain agencies, who was taking bribes. When he was found out, they found all of these cases had not been reported and they're trying to catch them up. This was in the last few months though, as you can tell. I think with Ms. Fortunes... errr. Ms Moretti's past, it's pretty damning in this case.? Blaise sighed out loud. “Yes, it is. It's sad when you truly think about it. It's really true, a leopard can't change it's spots. But, there will be no rushed wedding today. I've spoken to Ukyo's family but have not officially asked her father. I'll not violate their traditions or their wishes right now.” He was straight to the point, and they knew there would be no arguing the facts with him, or the wishes he put up right now. As it was, he was the one paying for their services as it was, wasn't for them to question him. ?Is there anything else I should know about this right this moment? And I'll remind you, that I want to know everything. I do have to ask how much the courts may know about this bribery case?? They stumbled for a moment, the lawyers weren't in control anymore. Months of Blaise traveling had left them in a bit of security for the case, but he was going to be there for this one, and he was going to be there if there were any more. “It's going to trial soon, well pretrial. Everything that we got, we got with my work on that case. It's been released by the courts as evidence in any case that needs it.” “Good, I'm going to catch a nap then.” Blaise turned to one of the workers on the small business plane. “Please ensure that Ms. Kushinaga and I are up and ready within an hour of landing in Las Vegas, please, so we can get our clothes changed before we hit the courthouse.” The young man nodded his head as he responded. “Yes, Sir. That would give you about 3 more hours of proper time. However, with the weather systems over the area right now, it could get a little bumpy too.” “Understood. Thank you.” Blaise unhooked his lap belt and then got up. The lawyers collected all of their paper work and started to mumble amongst themselves. Ukyo got up and started to follow Blaise. Blaise suddenly stopped and turned his head. “Yes, today I'm being an asshole or a bastard. Call me either one, just remember... I'm the one paying your bills right now. I'm sure if you want another case, it could be arranged, but it won't be with my cousin's companies anymore.” Both lawyers looked at him, shocked looks on their faces. How did he hear them? They'd never know. Blaise then took a seat on one of the extended couches, and laid down, his arm covering his eyes. ?Sir?? One of the workers came up to him. You could call them flight attendants, but they werne't the normal sense of the word. ?Yes?? “That seat will fold out to a small bed, might be more comfortable.” Blaise looked up at Ukyo and smiled at her before he climbed back up to his feet. The Attendants then both got on each side of the seat and folded it out like a convertible couch. Blaise sat down and then started to untie his knee high boots. Ukyo sat next to him. “You know, you need to stop doing that as much. You're slipping with it.” He gave a slight chuckle. “Yeah, I know. Just been feeling a little edgy. I'll need the rest before I get infront of this judge. Just do me a favor, don't punch anyone out this time. We both know all to well how you and Becca and Francis get along.” She gave him a smirk. “I promise nothing of the sort. At least in the hallways. While we're in the courtroom, I'll be a little angel.” “Yeah, I believe that.” Ukyo's face changed right in front of him to one of concern. “You know, with moving back to Vegas you might want to think about finding your grandpa and working some of that edginess out. There are things going on lately that aren't good without the proper training. As I understand it, there is only one other of your kind... well your mother's side of your heritage, around, and he's just as young as you are.” Blaise wasn't ready for that one. She was right, of course, she usually was. Unlike others, she wasn't afraid to speak her mind to him. It was one of the things he loved so much about her. Other then the fact that she could kick his ass if she really wanted to, and was a minx in the sack. “I'll think about it. That old coot... don't now how much he could help me though.” “Stop that... He's family.” “So you say.” It was a joke, one she knew about. A punch to his shoulder and some laughing was all that could be done there.... His boots were now off, and he moved to put his head on the pillows that were laying there, on his back. She told him to scoot over, which he did and took a spot right next to him. The spot she'd had for the last year. Right next to him. In the ring and out... ---- A bit later at the Clark County Courthouse, we find Ukyo, Blaise and the two lawyers standing in the lobby waiting rom. Had to take her time to get to her seat, right behind him, on the other side of the railing. As she was waiting for someone to make a path for her, the other group walked in. It's good to see you here, you and all of your... blue hair.” Ukyo resisted the urge to do a repeat performance from a year ago.
