A Night in Jail. PT 4 Roz Blayze Global Wrestling Promo #696 Date: 11-4-03 Location: Aden County Jail The overwelming Silence was just beggning to get to me..I felt like a child..Left alone in a small space that looked miles wide..It was like i was standing on clouds..But i would stand and walk..and find out that the walls were solid and i couldent move in all the directions that i wished i could...There was more then one time, in the dead of night..I would scream..and Scream..Time after time a garud would come and bang on my door.. 'Mr. Blayze, Quiten down.' I would stop..Just for a moment.Then i would slink up to the door..Very slowely..Looking at the slit in the door.. Then i would spring up...and look into this garuds eyes..What should i say to him...No Pig...I'll do what i want...or should i just say sorry and go about my business..Nither seemed like a think i would do...So i took the lesser rout 'Listen to me, I will be out of here before your next night shift...please sir...Go sit back down..' The officer wouldent bother me again..But another one would...This was the only conversation that i had during the ten days in the barren cell..There was nothing but padded walls...and a door..I was put in this confinement becuse of a man whom words got away from him...But that story is another time to tell... 'Now..I get out...Tommrow...and then i get to go to Glovbal Wrestling Evolution..and Partake in the Lions test..I will be tested by names such as Jin Akriya...Sean Price...and many other rookies that were placed into my situation...Not only to be a bother to me..But another body. JUst another sack of meaning less waster of flesh and bone that i will dispose of and win the Primestar title..Why? Becuse...I am the right man to do it.' I turned and grabed a hold of the side wall...I began to pull..Ripping the padding off..Space by space i did this untill there was a single line going the full length of the room...no worries...I'd be gone tommrow..Sweet Freedom...Sweet Nothing...Seeing my daugyter again..Sleeping in my own bed in my own house on my own land..God it almost seemed to be to good to be true...But it wasent...It was a gift that i was getting tommrow..and tommrow couldent come fast enough.. 'I long to get back in a wrestling ring..Or in my case.. an Octagon..It has been far to long since my lkast venture into a ring..I'm not very sure how long but far to long for my likeing.' I never liked staying inone spot for to long..I was a travling man...I liked to see new things and mett new people..But most people imet diddent care..They would sit around and bitch about how bad things were..They have never been to my home land..Where people work for pennies a day and support a family of five...No the Americans get what they want..How they want. When they want...You call this a Demoracy..I call this Country a charity.. 'Charity, Charity for the Lazy.. For the incompedent..for the useless...Most working class americans hate the system becuse it gives they tax money to the drug addict down the street or the drinking man who wont get out and work..Americans love charity..You give things away..But you cant give anything to your Bordeing counties..Like Mexico...' Mexico..My true home..I had to leave there..To much poverty..I was around it to much..I was bring my Sisters here to the states..Rosa..Rita and my Brother Hector..I had alaready staarted building on their three homes..Much more nice then they had ever had in mexico..I was going to let them have the homes rent free..It was on my land...I oculd do what I want..Rosa and Hector alaready had secure jobs waiting for them..hector was a Great singer and Roas was the best cook youve ever seen..but Rita..Rita was def and diddent understand much..I loved her so much..and wished to see her..I would..As soon as i got out of this place i was bringin them all back here..To a better local... 'A Better life.' and what a better life it wilkl be when i come strolling through the house. My two nephews want to become wrestlers..So i train them..Little Marko and Robert...I was going to send them to school..They wernt going to become wrestler..One was going to be a doctor and the other a famous movie star..They were going to be that. 'My life is a bed of roses..Yes..But there are still many hidden throns in a bed of roses..You find one..You pull it out and keep n going...I've found a large one..It's been just alittle harder to termnate..But soon it will be gone and i will be surrounded by my family..and the world would be right again...It would all be right once again..and no one would hinder anything' I would have the primestar title. I would have my family and i would be protected...that is all i would need in the world and there wouldent be a single hand that could take it from me. 'At one point in time i was the newest spectal..beatin gthe likes of Brick.BJp and winning the cruiserweight title..am i still that man who longes to be the one...That i used to be...I am a driven guy..I dont just stop when it's just enough..I keep on going becuse...I have to..There is no way possible for me to lose in my mind...I amgoing to take it far beyond the limit..Why? Becuse i have the will...I have the mind and i have the world behind me...I am destine for the primestar title..I am going to be that man and like i said./..NOTHING! is going to stop me...Not..any one' I could feel the utter silance breaking me down...Hunting me like an animal...Like a defensless animal..and was i going to be slain by this monster..I had hoped not...I had wondred not..But in the end there would be a desision..I still have time..I still had time in thsi cell...I would wait here untill my time came to get out of this place and i would walk the white walls to freedom...Ahh..Freedom...Do you have freedom..Do you know what freedom is...No..Of corse not...You have it..You take it for granted...But when you cant eat with out thirteen hundred other people watching you...YOu dont know what it is like not to have freedom...Freedom is what this land is based apoun..Freedom..and Charity..and still people are dyeing to protect your freedom and you care not...You dont...and never will..Like i said..This land is based on freedom...Freedom and charity..and never forget that Fin!
