A Demon's Demon

13 June 2004

Date: 06.13.04 “Scott?” June13th, 2004 ? Approximately 5:30 AM London, Ontario, Canada ? Reno Estate The curtains are closed with the French doors slightly open. A cool breeze blows in from the balcony, causing the heavy and dark silken curtains to rustle. In the dark room, their color is not fully visible. The room itself is beautiful; a double-king sized bed is positioned against the far wall, pillars rising each corner. A marble shelf juts out from a far wall, a line of candles, all at different heights, and all void of flame. Lying in the bed, Maverick Reno. Black satin sheets cover most of his frame, his upper body and head showing. He sleeps topless, his hair disheveled. A marble stand is positioned next to the bed, a single candle placed here. Aside this solo and slender pillar of wax, an alarm clock and a single, old-world phone. Reno turns and reaches to the end table, his hand clamoring into the unlit candle and sending it to a hard stop upon the hard wood floor. Reno rolls over again, his eyelids fluttering as his eyes race from corner to corner behind their slumbering shades. Again, Reno speaks out, his voice carrying from his bed to the door and down the moon-lit hallway. “Scott” calm down, Scott.” Reno rolls again, his mouth moving and forming other words of an alien dialect. “Alicia’s ? be” did this.” His words come in the form of sentences with words missing. He rolls again, clenching his eyes tightly as he grips his pillow. “Damnit, who d?” He utters, a death grip on his black-wrapped pillow. He turns again, forcefully hurling the pillow to the wall where it knocks a photo from the marble shelf and sends it down? plummeting hurling? screaming, if it could? to the cold hard floor. The crash wakes him, forcing him to sit upright in his bed, breathing heavily. His hair is plastered erratically to his face and forehead, his sweat holding it in place. He breathes heavily, one hard breath after another. Almost methodical. Reno tosses the cover off of him and glances hard at the alarm clock as if the digits were minute. With a sigh he stands and stretches, his eyes searching the room. He walks to the door without much direction, the path as familiar as the ring he works in. As familiar as winning? “Fucking nightmares”.” he mutters, his eyes still adjusting to the lack of lights. A whining creak is heard as he pushes the door open, allowing a little more light to shine into the room from the windows in the corridor. He sighs and grabs a robe quickly from atop his dresser and pulls it on; you’d expect a black satin robe with royal burgundy trim or perhaps a traditional Japanese kimono. No; Reno pulls on a trusty flannel robe, red, green and black in color. He sighs and starts his path down the hallway, barefoot and wearing nothing more than a pair of boxers underneath his robe. Reno pushes open a sliding door and steps onto the cold linoleum floor of his kitchen. With a flick of his wrist, fluorescent lights flicker to life, giving light to the room. Reno slams his eyelids to a close, the light nearly blinding him. He turns his head for a moment and opens his eyes slowly, allowing them to adjust. Slowly he trudges to the pantry; he opens the double door and glances at a rack of wines, champagnes, and assorted liquors. He reaches slowly then stops with a shake of his head and aims a little higher to grab a jar of instant coffee from the shelf. Reno turns and grabs a coffee cup marked, “NCW’s Brightest Star” from nearby. Reno places this onto the counter and gets a tea-spoon and opens the lid to the coffee. Reno sings softly as he spoons pile after pile into his cup, “Nothing says good morning like Folgers in” ? Reno stops and shakes his head, “Damn, you’re losing it. Singing fucking coffee jingles.” Reno grabs his coffee pitcher, already filled with hot water and slowly fills his cup, mixing it gently. Slowly, Reno paces through his home his first path leading through an arched doorway. The linoleum turns to lush carpet as Reno pads between rooms, his eyes moving to the side and peering through a large box-window. A sigh escapes Reno’s lips as he glances down, his coffee cup meeting them, steam bellowing from the ceramic mug. Reno walks slowly towards a door marked by a pair of ankhs, both made from black marble and molded into the hard wood door. Reno pushes the door open and allows the springs to close the door behind him. Steam bellows up, clouding Reno’s vision. Through the miniscule fog and with minimal light, Reno sees the room before him. The floor is black marble, a wrap around desk, and random collections strewn about the room. Adorning the walls, an assortment of weapons of all kinds. On one wall, a collection of leather-bound originals, and another complete with ancient tapestries. Reno places his coffee upon the desk and takes a seat, his hand idly flipping the switch to his monitor. The flat-screen monitor comes to life, an image appearing on the screen of the Echelon’s coat of arms. Reno grabs the mouse and jerks it quickly to reveal a login screen. Reno quickly types in his password, his eyes focusing to the glaring screen. “Six” it’s too early to be conscious.” Reno again sips his coffee, his eyes peering down into the steaming liquid. Reno quickly opens up his welcome screen, his eyes skimming down a list of news stories and other bits of information, composed by Naria throughout the night and morning. “There was a time when Stephen would call me every morning at six am on the dot to keep me updated on the company. It’s a shame that will never be again.” Reno sighs and scrolls the list again, his eyes narrowing. “Hmm. This doesn’t look very promising.” Reno reaches over and grabs his phone quickly, pressing a quick digit and holding the phone to the side of his head. “Naria” Yeah, It’s Maverick. Reno starts, his voice calm and collected. He reaches for his coffee and takes another sip, nodding as if he’s communicating, “Yes, it is early. But this is important?” again Reno pauses, a sigh overcoming him as he listens to the other end. “What’s with the FBI mark on my list this morning?” Reno speaks, his words serious. In New York, still at the Echelon Tower, Naria is seated at a wrap around desk, a look of exhaustion on her face. She’s dressed in a business suit, a coffee maker and a full pot of coffee sitting before her. She speaks softly, quickly scrolling a mouse to a certain point on her computer. “It seems that our contact in the bureau ran his morning check and found that your file was hit the other night. Not only that, but so was John’s. Whoever pulled the file up did a bit of digging too” ? Reno sighs and runs his fingers back through his hair, a look of annoyance on his face. “So you’re telling me that we have a federal agent with a personal vignette” This is not good business, Naria. Have our guy see if he can figure out which terminal pulled up the information. If he can’t do it, then he had best have all of that information gone and replaced with friendlier reports by noon. You know me, Naria, and so does he. I suggest you remind him of who he’s dealing with.” Reno says with a sense of hostility, his life only slightly threatened. What’s better to stop a threat than with another? Naria sighs and speaks again, “Well, Mav, it gets better. The reason I’m actually here and awake this early is because I got a call from one of my informants. It seems that the DA in Carolina has a bit of information on Frock and he’s been trying to piece it together.” Reno nods, sipping at his coffee, “So they know that Frock was running illegal arm shipments. Have they started investigating the new owner” ? “Yes. They’ve pretty much got him closed down as of late yesterday. They’re saying that an eye-witness saw one of their boats meeting with one of our foreign investors in International Waters.” Naria continues to scroll through her computers astounding amount of information. Reno places his cup down and stands, idly pacing the room, his mind racing at a million mile per second. “And the eye-witness went to the DA, they’re investigating ? damn, what’s his name?” “Sanders” Naria states matter-of-factly. Reno continues, substituting the name, “Sanders. Have all links to us cut. Have the arm shipments stopped for two full days and then restarted three-hundred miles south of our current location. I assume you can contact our investors and set that up?” Reno speaks, his tone without a doubt, serious. His ability to keep his business clean and free of suspicion? Damn near impeccable. “That’s not a problem, Mav. What about the DA” He may not want to let go of a piece of prime cut like this. Should I hire one of our usuals to fly in and take care of him? I assume you don’t want anyone on staff to take this one; too much risk in being seen.” Naria, herself, has quite the eye for detail. Reno leans back in his chair, his eyes skimming the list on his monitor, a few of the topics being flagged as the two speak. A grimace comes over Maverick’s face, “I don’t want him to die. Too much suspicion as to the timing; we might get an even heavier interest due to it being in his current case load.” Reno fingers the handle of his coffee cup, “Have Jason handle it if he’s not too busy, Naria. He’s local to the area and he could probably find a way to ? take care of him without taking care of him. If he’s too busy, let me know and I’ll figure out a plan B.” “Also.” Naria states, a look of annoyance on her face. “Also” What is this? Is everything falling apart, Naria? Do I not pay to have all of this taken care of? What the hell else is it?” Reno’s expression is calm yet stern, his demeanor obvious. Reno leans back, tapping at his keyboard without a purpose. “It has to be dealt with. What of Mr” well, this could be a problem. They’ve already put the eye-witness under witness protection.”Naria sighs and leans forward, resting her head in her hands. “That so” Let me get back to you on that one, Naria. It would seem that our southern operations are hitting the fan this morning. I’ll get in touch.” Time is lucid when you control the destinies of others. When you really think about fate, destiny, and life; you realize that it’s all a series of instances caused by you and the people around you. Nothing is purely at the whim of chance, just a randomized set of choices. The right mind can direct those people and their choices; the right mind can lower the chance of possibilities while enhancing others. The right mind? can see the future by knowing the choices that others around him will take. It’s great to have the right mind. Raleigh, North Carolina ? Governor’s Press Conference June 15th, 2004 ? Approximately 9:00 AM The scene opens up to a beautifully ornate mansion like building, located in the quaint capital of North Carolina, Raleigh. A dome rests atop the building, both American and North Carolina state flags flying at the roof. In front of the building, cars line the road allowing for reporters and politicians alike to enter the building, a security staff controlling the crowd. Senators and Representatives. Reporters and television crews. The Governor, Mike Easley is holding a press conference to speak on crime in North Carolina Schools. Primarily focusing on drug use, Easley plans to speak on the drop-out rate and weapon prevalence as well. A limousine pulls up slowly in front of the building, the crowd moving to get a closer look as the North Carolina Governor steps from inside; he lifts his hands, waving at the public who has arrived to support and/or denounce him. Applause rings forth, the sound of a thousand hands, hundreds of sets, slapping together over and over again to create a clap; an approval for the esteemed governor. A moment passes and another pair of men step out from inside the limousine. One is the Governor’s assistant, a young woman who is of little importance while the second is number one on our docket of people to know: District Attorney, Samuel Green. Green walks with the Governor, the two men walking slowly up the stairs and into the building that the conference will be held in. Why? Why are they here? It would seem that a financial supporter of Easley has requested that he speak on the school system’s issues regarding drug and weapon issues. Why is The DA in attendance? Why, who else would have all of the facts about drug and weapon use in North Carolina at hand? This financial supporter was very specific in his requests; he wants to know the statistics, what’s been going on and what can be done. What does the state plan to do and why hasn’t anything drastic been done? This supporter is an advocate of educational reinforcement of positive behavior; an advocate of psychological influences in the school room outside of the once a month visit from the school’s counselor. Easley, not wanting to upset one of his supporters, agreed to a conference where he would outlay his plans for school policy reform. Before the conference, however, this supporter has sponsored a small gathering for school officials, state officials, senators and other supporters of the cause. Easley walks into this room, receiving a rousing applause from his pseudo-captive audience. The district attorney wanders off to the side, mingling with a few acquaintances. Samuel Green. District Attorney since 1999, did his undergraduate work at Appalachian State University, double majoring in pre-law and criminal justice. From here, he moved east to the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill where he would continue in his pursuits, garner a masters and take the bar exam. He’d work as a lawyer for a period of time, primarily doing jobs for the state. Finally, he’d get pushed to District Attorney with an amazing conviction record. Known as a man who does his own investigating, and sometimes deemed, “The cop, judge, jury, and attorney,” Samuel Green is a highly respected member of the North Carolina Government. He would be sorely missed. Time had passed in the small recreation area, groups in suits mingling and discussing politics, religion, education, school reform, and the size of the trout that Jim caught last week. That was how it worked in these social groups, after all. Sam Green speaks in a corner with the superintendent and another lawyer, the three men exchanging laughs over triad of glasses. Governor Easley slowly walks over, placing his hands on two of the men’s shoulders. Beside the Governor walks the major supporter of this event; he stands at around six foot, wears an all black formal suit of Mandarin design, and has his long black hair tied back into a tight pony tail. He smiles, reaching forward to shake hands with each of the three men individually. “Gentlemen, let me introduce Mr. Reno. He’s the one who put this all together for us. Mr. Reno is the owner of The Reno Company; they give financial backing to a few of our favorite organizations, gentlemen.” The Governor smiles as a hand is placed on his shoulder by his lovely assistant. She smiles as he excuses himself. “So, Mr. Reno, you’re the big supporter who pulled all of this together, huh?” the unnamed lawyer speaks. Maverick smiles and nods as he’s handed a glass by a member of the wait staff. He returns to the three men a quick smile before speaking, “It would appear that way. I’ve always had a huge interest in education and with my company, I find myself able to assist those who need it most.” Reno smiles, his snide comment cutting away at the low-ranking school system. “Right. Well, your help is greatly appreciated, by us down here.” Reno smiles and slips his left hand into his open knee length dress coat, his right still holding his glass. “I do enjoy supporting the underdog.” Reno smiles again, his comments cutting away at the educational administrator. He sighs and looks away, as if seeing someone in the crowd. “Well, if you gentlemen will excuse me. It was a pleasure to meet you, Mr. Reno.”The superintendent walks away, shaking his head as he does so. “Mr. Green. Your work has been most amazing; the media seems to love your name.” Maverick smiles, his social prowess coming into play. Sam, a slightly obese man with graying hair, nods a bit at the comment, trying to take it in passing but on the inside, devouring the compliments. “I don’t do my job for the credit, Mr. Reno. May I call you Maverick?” Samuel spouts off. The unnamed lawyer quickly excuses himself and slips away, moving as far away from the pompous DA and the rude sponsor as he can. “Ah, but your accolades have even reached my ears in New York. I’ve heard them say that you’re aiming your sights at terrorism in your own state” Is that so?” Maverick’s smile grows even larger, “And you may not call me Maverick.” Samuel is taken back by Maverick’s honesty, his eyes shifting to the ground. Maverick grabs a glass from a waiter and nods, quickly offering his first to Mr. Green who takes it politely. “I” Well, yes. I think that if we weed out the holes in our own country first, then we’ll be safer.” “Wise man, Mr. Green. A chain is only as strong as its weakest link; so what would America’s weakest link be?” Maverick speaks calmly, watching Samuel closely. “Simple. Unpatriotic, domestic terrorists. And I don’t mean just terrorists. I mean drugies, organized crime, the works. All of it needs to go!” Samuel gets into it, quickly taking a sip of his drink, “But just between you and me, Mr. Reno.” Samuel leans in, his eyes looking around quickly to make sure that no one is in ear-shot of them. “I think it all started with the damned foreigners.” Maverick’s face loses its sense of composure. “I see. Well, since I intend to end this conversation here, I would like to propose a toast. To the health of my enemies enemy.” Reno smiles, holding his half-emptied glass up. Samuel holds his near-full glass up and smiles in return. They both throw back the remainder before Reno continues, “Kanpai. Now if you’ll excuse me, Mr. Green, I have to make a call to my accountant and have him stop payment on a check or two; wouldn’t want any of my Canadian money to fall into the wrong hands.” With this, Reno takes the near empty glass from Green and walks away, leaving a shocked expression on his face. Reno speaks softly, his lips barely moving, “That’s one bastard I won’t miss.” Reno continues walking, not even turning when the crowd gasps and rushes to Green as he collapses onto the floor, clutching at his chest. Softly, Reno sings to himself, his voice melodic, “Steve walks merrily down the street, his brim pulled way down low.” June15th, 2004 ? Approximately 6:30 PM Television Broadcast ? Raleigh, North Carolina A news reporter stands on the screen, a solemn look to her face. Professional courtesy. She speaks matter-of-factly, her voice telling the tail of the day. “Ladies and gentlemen, I’m standing in the lobby of the Raleigh Medical Arts Center where prominent North Carolina District Attorney, Samuel Green, spent the last seven hours of his life. Earlier today at a press conference for Governor Easley, DA Green was seen speaking with several others at the party including its sponsor. Moments later, he fell to the ground, suffering from a massive coronary heart attack. Doctors here are stating that Mr. Green’s death was of natural causes. A private service will be held for him in his home town of Asheboro sometime in the following week. This has been Trisha Takanawa reporting. Back to you, Mark.” June 15th, 2004 ? Approximately 11:00 AM Raleigh, North Carolina ? Governor’s Press Conference We have another view as Reno slips his left hand into his pocket, his eyes focusing on Samuel Green as he and the others speak with him. As the superintendent walks away, Reno smiles and turns slightly, watching him leave. In this same moment, he lifts his hand from his pocket and drops a pair of small pills into the glass. The conversation continues, Reno talking and moving the glass around with exaggerated gesticulations, the pills fully disintegrating and mixing with the liquid. Reno smiles, never taking a sip of the drink as the conversation continues with DA Green. The waiter passes again and Reno turns, taking a glass from his tray; Reno nods politely and continues turning, making a full revolution before handing Samuel the first glass. The two men drink. Time is lucid when you control the destinies of others. Life is nothing more than a wish while death is a breath away. Life is a dying ember while death inhales, its heart beating at its strongest potential. Samuel Green did not revel in fate nor did he have a chance to survive. His death warrant was signed and the sickle’s swing was set in motion. Only time would tell of his death although, it had already been written. Why did the two men cross paths? Because Reno chose for it to happen. The Governor would never have held the conference had Reno not pushed him, bribing him with financial backing. The DA would never have came to the conference had Reno not requested up to the minute data on drug and weapon use and methods of how to combat this, Samuel Green’s main focus. The superintendent didn’t choose to leave the group first; he was insulted and degraded, he was asked to leave in as silent a way as possible. The unnamed lawyer, the same. Samuel Green was lulled into a false sense of safety, surrounded by guards, speaking on good terms with a man who had praised him to some extent. No, Samuel Green wouldn’t have lived to see another day. Maverick Reno chose for him to die and die he did; he filled his glass with potassium chloride, a chemical naturally in the body in minute dosages; a chemical that no coroner would look for? not in a man as obese and unhealthy as Samuel Green. Reno smited Samuel Green on this death; he drew back with his sickle and he swung, clearing his head from atop his shoulders. There was no question that Samuel died from natural causes; a noted alcoholic, a man who was physically out of shape. Known for having health issues in the past as well as a insatiable craving for fried and fatty foods. Samuel not only was chosen to die; he had a hand in his own death. Samuel made it so that Reno committed the perfect crime; the prefect murder. After all, Samuel was the perfect victim. No real family to speak of, an easy way of pinning the death on natural causes, hundreds of people surrounding him. Not one able to pinpoint or blame Maverick. No one would even think to; why should they? Another funeral. Another wreathe. Another innocent slain by the sickle of the dark angel, taken from the grips of life and delivered to an unmerciful end at the hands of le diabolique himself; Samuel Green’s blood now joined countless others? upon the shining blade of Death’s sickle. June13th, 2004 ? Approximately 5:30 AM London, Ontario, Canada ? Reno Estate Maverick leans back in his chair, his eyes feasting upon the page before him. The phone is still pressed to his ear, a death in the works. A second? When you control destiny, you control time. To control the ending of a book is to partially control the author. To write the ending of the book and force the characters, players, or actors to bend to your whim so that this book might end when and how you say is to be the author. Maverick Reno is the author of lives. “So what about the eye-witness?” Naria asks, sighing as she stares at the screen. “I’ve already contacted a few of our guys and was able to find out which plain he’s going to be on when he leaves from the Piedmont Triad International. I think that’d be our best time to strike.” Naria states definitively. Reno smiles. “You’re catching on, Naria. Definitely catching on.” His grin tells all. More blood. More sin. More pain. Fewer lives. In this book, there are no happy endings, no hope for a positive climax. The antagonist wins because he garnered control of the book; the antagonist is the author. The author is the antagonist. The protagonist is dead by the time you read the epilogue, you just don’t know it yet. June 14th, 2004 ? Approximately 4:00 AM Flight 027 Leaving PTI for O’Hare The jet is traveling smoothly, passengers in their seats, flight crew taking care of their needs. Children sleep in mother’s arms, wives huddle close to their husbands as they sleep. It’s early and most have taken this chance to catch up on a little shut-eye. There’s no doctor on the plane, just a pair of US Marshals, armed with a pair of Magnum .45’s. Two men keeping their eyes on one passenger, an eye witness to an arms smuggling ring. A little man, of about forty-five, sits in the coach cabin with the marshals nearby. His scraggly black hair and unshaven appearance add to the image of a man on the lamb. A lamb before the wolf. He sits in his seat, his fingers drumming impatiently upon the arm-rest of his chair. A flight attendant walks up and down the aisles, giving drinks to those who want them. The little man lifts his hand, “Excuse me.” he speaks, catching her attention. The attendant never looks at him, her face down as she answers with a quick yes. “Can I get a glass of water?” The flight attendant nods and grabs a glass from atop the cart and hands it to him, pulling the cart away from the area and to the next set of seats. The man sits back in his seat and guzzles the drink, thirsty and tired. He’s been in hiding for days, having to give up his family and life for the greater good. He finishes the glass and sighs, a smile resting on his face. He’s finally safe; two armed guards watching over him, three miles from anyone wanting to do him harm. The flight attendant lifts her head and glances at the man. She smiles. Naria smiles and tips her hat at him, featuring the golden wings of a flight attendant. You can never escape the author. He’ll be a lifeless corpse before the plane touches the landing strip. You can never escape the author when you are his to define. You can never escape Death when you are on his list. If his sickle comes for you, I suggest you pray and make amends with whomever it is that you pray to. Prepare for your homecoming? I’m coming for you.
