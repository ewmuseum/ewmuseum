Father's Day

15 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/15/03 The date is June 15th, 2003. Father’s Day. Fathers celebrate this day with their children, going to restaurants, giving gifts, etc. But Jamal “Brick” Randall and his sister Monica are spending this Father’s Day in the cemetery, as they have for the past four years since their father died of a heart attack. Brick and Monica walk past a few until they finally come to a grave at the foot of a hill. The epitaph on the headstone reads: Joseph Randall 1944-1999 Husband, father, friend “Hey, Dad,” says Brick. “Hi, Daddy.” “Happy Father’s Day,” they say in unison. “Well, so far, things are good and bad. DMAK’s second album is due out later this year. Just got some more work to do on it. I’m planning to go for the part of Luke Cage again. Yeah, I know I’m not really an actor, but if anybody’s right for the part, it’s me.” “Oh, God! You’re not gonna go on about Vin Diesel again, are you?” “He ain’t right for the part, and you know it! Anyway, he might only be part of the competition. There’s a lot of cats that might try out for this part. LL, Ving Rhames, the cat from Spawn. I’m gonna have a fight, but then again, it ain’t like I’ve never fought for anything in my life.” Brick’s mood then changes. “Don’t know if you saw the last PPV, but I lost. Again. It’s partly my fault. I underestimated the cat I was facing. I thought he was weak, just because of the company he kept. Cat’s name is Sage Sabbot. He runs with the Nevermore. So far, most of the times he’s won titles, he’s won them with help from the rest of the Nevermore. But he beat me without their help. Even dislocated my shoulder. But I managed to send him a little message after the match. Bricklayer right on top of the title belt. Well, it ain’t over between us, and I’m gonna show him just that on Evolution. We’re not fighting for the title, but if I win, it might just put me back in the game. I plan to show Sabbot that he ain’t totally beat me yet, not by a long shot. He may have won a fight, but he ain’t won the war. I’m gonna get that title and there won’t be a damn thing he can do to stop it.” Brick then turns to Monica who steps towards the grave. He gets “Well, Dad, I guess it’s my turn. Well, things are going a little better for me than they are for Jamal. I’m still enjoying married life. I wish you could have met Kyle. I wish you could have walked me down the aisle. Not that I didn’t like Jamal doing it, but it just wasn’t the same. You’d really have liked him. Kyle’s definitely better for me than Cedric was. For once, he was someone Jamal didn’t try to scare.” Monica pauses for a moment. “Dad, I’m starting to worry about Jamal. I mean, he’s really taking the loss to Sabbot hard. Mom and I were there. After a while, he didn’t want to look at us. I think he felt he let us down. He didn’t say much that night, but I know. I know he also takes that promise he made to you seriously. He didn’t even want me and Mom at Dead Zone because he didn’t want to risk anything happening to us. He still blames himself for what happened to I don’t know what he might do. But just between you and me, I hope he kicks Sabbot’s ass.” Brick walks up to Monica and the grave. “You ready to go?” “Yeah.” “Well, Dad, we gotta go. We’ll see you next year.” As Brick and Monica walk from the grave, Brick says, “I heard what you said and I just want you to know that you don’t have to worry about me. I’m gonna be okay.” “You sure?” Brick is quiet for a while and then he says, “I’m gonna have to be.”
