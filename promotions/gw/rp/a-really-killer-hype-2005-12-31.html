A Really Killer Hype

31 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/31/05 [They are the most nefarious pirates on the high seas and the Alpha Quadrant of the Cygnus Galaxy. They are the Killer Pirates of the Outer Caribbean...and they're homeless. Their home, the Island of Misfit Wrestlers, was maliciously attacked by the Circus of DOOM~! and it's leader, Darth von Doom, killed most of the wrestlers and characters that he had created at the behest of the demented Horny Mathematician, whose hard salami grew three sizes that day. They are on a raft now, floating on the edge of space, time, and a toilet. Until they drift onto a magical realm of wonder and fun.] Jack: Blimey, clown. Where the hell are we? [The 'Killer Klown from Outer Space' (KKFOS) shrugs his shoulders.] KKFOS: Mesa knowsa wheresa we arese. [Jack looks around and shrugs his shoulders.] Jack: Wherever we are, we need to find us some rum matey! [The clown nods his head.] Jack: GARR~! Let's find us some God damn rum clownie! ARRRRR~! KKFOS: ARRRRRR~! [Jack and the clown then walk until they see a magical sign that lights their hearts with wonder and joy. points at the sign. The sign says as follows.] 'GLOBAL WRESTLING' [They smile. They've found a new home. But...they aren't alone. The Evil Darth von Doom and his minions, the Circus of DOOM~! have just landed here too in search of the Pirates. What will happen now that all of these idiots have invaded the Magical Land of Global? Who knows but one things for sure. It must be a full moon.]
