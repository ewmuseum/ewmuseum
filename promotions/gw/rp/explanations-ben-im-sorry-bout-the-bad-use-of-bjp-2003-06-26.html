Explanations (ben, im sorry bout the bad use of BJP)

26 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-26-03 Hold on to your promise, you can use it for a crutch. Stand by while all your dreams get trampled in the dust. Leave now before your slick machines begin to rust. ------------- Yesterday was a hard day for me. I had to tell my uncle that I am considering leaving the very federation he struggled to get me in and to do well in. Today will be just as hard, if not harder. I have to tell my fellow Young Bloods, save Johnny Cruse. In my own home. ---- 'Jay, Ben. I have something I have to tell the two of you,' I tell my two stablemates, Jay Robson and Ben Pailthorpe. 'Don't tell me Cindy's only a decoy and that you're really a hom......' 'No, Jay. I'm not coming out. Save that for certain other guys around here, cuz we all know some of them are questionable.' 'Oh, you mean that one bloke who hangs out with Dea...' 'Ben, no names. Last thing I need is guys getting pissed at me right now. First off, you have both seen the lineup for Whiplash?' Ben speaks up first. 'Yeah, I'm facing too bloody rookies, Mysterious and Ice. Winner is top contender for the Cruiserweight title.' 'Kick some ass out there Pyro. Get yourself some gold.' 'Who are you facing Derek?' Jay asks, almost as if he doesn't care. 'I'm in the International Title match.' 'International Championship? Isn't that.....' His eyebrows ruffle a little as his words fade off into nothing. I look at Jay with eyes that portray the emotion inside of me right now. Excitement. Intensity. Worry. Fear. Respect. Finally after a few seconds, I finish his sentence. 'Salaok.' 'No bloody way! I wanted that bastard in the ring for myself! Now I have to wait until you are done.' 'Ben, be careful how you say 'when I'm done' because that might be sooner than you think.' Both Ben and Jay look at me while trying to pick their jaws up. I just stare at them, blank faced and unphased. Jay regains composure first this time. 'What are you talking about bro? Are you leaving the team?' 'Well, yes and no. I just think the time has come for me to move on to different things.' Ben shoots me a look of almost pure hatred, then explains it. 'Damnit Frosty. I thought you said you were Young Blood forever. You can't leave us. We're all too dependant on each other.' 'No, Pyro. You two have proven that you don't need me.' Ben sits down in the chair nearest him. He runs a hand through his hair, then looks directly into my eyes. 'Frost. You, Jay, Johnny, Hans.. you're the only real family I've had for a while now. If not for you guys, I'd be stuck in a pub somewhere drowning in a bottle right now. How can you even THINK of doing this?!' I take a deep breath, stare at BJP, then sigh when I see the anguish building in his face. For everytime the Bloods have been there for me when I had a problem, Ben has had it ten times as bad. But even so, he is doing nothing to help me out right now, all he is doing is making it that much harder for me to do the only thing I can do. Finally, I can't take it anymore. 'Ben, shut up, ok? Just SHUT UP! If you think this is easy on me, you are very wrong. GW is really the only federation I've been at home in, and the Bloods are as much a part of my family as they are yours. I married into the group, remember? Just because I won't be in GW anymore doesn't mean that I won't be a Blood or that you and I will lose touch. But for God's sake, Ben, STOP TAKING IT OUT ON ME!' After my outburst, I take a small step back, feeling as if I might fall over. Then I turn around and storm into the game room next to the den we were in. I slam the door behind me and grab a pool cue off the wall. Pool is one thing that always calms me down, and in a time like this I need all the calming down I can get. I step to the side of the table where the triangle is already full and in place, then lift it up and go to the other end to break the rack. I pull the cue back, then slam it forward, sending the cue ball spinning toward the rack. The colored billiard balls scatter across the table at the same time the door bursts open. Jay walks in, rips the cue from my hand, then twists it around my arm, dragging me through the door in a weird modified armbar using the cue. He hauls me back into the den and throws me onto the couch. 'Damnit you two, what is wrong?! We have more on our hands right now than ever before and you two still don't seem to be getting along. Now whatever is wrong, GET OVER IT!' I look up at Jay and before I can open my mouth, he points his finger directly in my face. 'Derek, I know you have some reason for leaving us like you are, and I want to hear it. But first, you and Ben have to stop your God damn arguing!' Ben and I stare at each other. Finally, he speaks. 'Derek, I'm sorry. I know you've got things you have to do, and I'm not one to stand in your way. I just panicked because with the Nevermore and TUE after us, you leaving, Johnny more focused on getting Val back, it's basically me and Jay against the world.' Ben always has been quick to apologize. 'Pyro, I understand. It's just that this is a really hard thing for me to decide, but it's time. You guys will be find without me. I'll still be around until after the PPV, and between now and then I've got Salaok in my crosshairs. Jay, you've faced him in singles many times. Is there anything I need to know before stepping into the ring one on one with him?' Jay rubs his chin a little, then looks at me with a slight grin. 'Well, I found that it works better if, right before your match with Salaok, you find a hot little blonde, take her to your locker room and...' Ben laughs a little as I throw a pillow at Jay. 'No, seriously bro. You can do it. I've faced both of you in singles, and it was about even. He's stronger than you, as well as taller. Probably faster and smarter. But I don't think I've ever seen anybody with the ring energy you have when you get going. Just don't give up, and keep your eyes on him and everything else at all times.' 'Great,' I mutter, rolling my eyes. 'Looks like I'm gonna need more help than you're worth talking about. Lets go to the gym.' We all get up and walk down the stairs and out the door. Black.
