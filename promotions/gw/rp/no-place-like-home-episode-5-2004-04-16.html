No Place Like Home Episode #5

No Place Like Home Episode #5 J. C. Diamond Global Wrestling Promo #1166 Date: 4/16/04 Location: Martin Residence, Miami NO PLACE LIKE HOME- SMOKEY VISIONS [Scene: Inside of the Martin Residence. J.C is to the right of young Ynonick Martin‘s room. Inside of the bedroom lies, Ynonick, the youngest child- the only child of the Martins. Jayson stumbles into the door.] There comes a time in every young teenager’s life when he has to become a man. Yes a man- an adult above everything. The only way to achieve it, is to do something that will prove you are man worthy. As a youth, J.C had gained his respect- his glory as a young teenager on the streets of NY. He earned every penny of it. He did not struggle to find himself; it had come to him. But now, looking down at the young Martin as he sat still in his bedroom, the opportunity had come to him- well Jayson came to him with a solution. It was time to give Ynonick his man hood; it was about fucking time. Stepping into the room, a dazed and yet, sun burned face of J.C peaked around the door. The light was very bright in Ynonick’s room- making difficult for J.C to see. He struggled, but his eyes began to adjust. The room was rather clean, but, the fact still remained everything was in a tight little space. Across from the bed was a typical computer desk, something that could make a fifteen-year-old computer nerd very happy about, but Jayson, if he ever were to receive something like that- he would burn the fucking piece of shit. Hell, he never got anything for free. Jayson earned everything he got, even the shirt on his back. Look at him know, a GW wrestling star, not to mention the Cruiser Champion above everything. Proving the odds again, in the Main Event at GW, Jayson had won his match, showing that he deserves to be in the main spotlight. This day wasn’t about him; it was about one other person in the Martin Residence- it was about Ynonick’s man hood. Finally realizing that he had somehow found his bag of weed this early morning, Jayson knew that the only way to interfere with the lifestyle of the Martin’s was to break the trust of them. The cameras were close on J.C as he moved around the room, sneaking up behind Ynonick as he sat on the computer doing nothing. “What the hell is this? Porn!?” Jayson shrugs with a huge sarcastic smile. “Well, well, well...” he stopped his sentence there. At that point, Ynonick had turned around, a little confused as too what was going on in his bedroom. Jayson wasn’t allowed in there, well, only with permission. The thing about the Martin’s rules were, Jayson never followed them. He never gave a damn about the rules- yet alone following them. It had come to J.C’s attention that this family need some help, no, not physical or mental, but his help. J.C was a man of his word, and he wanted to do the family much better, give them some hope in life. Sure this family went to church each Sunday, but Jayson could give a shit about going to church, he would rather spend the Sunday sleeping in after a nice day of wrestling at Global. Sundays was Jayson’s usual day to hang out with the Gang- but now, since the destruction of the empire called “Hoods” there was nothing that he could do about it. Before long, Jayson had giving up his speech, clearing his throat he stared at the young teenage son. “Get ready,” J.C says sharply while shaking his head. “Excuse me?” asked Ynonick as he presses down on the EXIT button on the keyboard of his computer. “I said..” Clearing his throat for the second time, Jayson realized that you don’t smoke tobacco- TOBACCO SMOKES YOU. “Just get fucking ready Ynonick, I don’t need to stand here and give you a lecture on what I am trying to say!” At that instant Jayson had snapped, ordering Ynonick off his feet. “Jesus fucking Christ,” cursed Jayson again, for the millionth time. With a slight chuckle, Ynonick purposely turned his attention back on the computer screen, intending that he wasn’t going to listen to J.C. Beat down as a child, well not really that harsh- but thrown out of his home at the age of seventeen, well that didn’t happen, but the point was, Jayson made it on his own. Now a days, the youth cant even survive without their video games and television. What has this world came too? He had made it on his own, struggled through the streets of Queens, but he had accomplished his goal, and look were it had put him. At one point in NY, he had all the PUSH and POWER in the entire city. He was recognized. He was an idol to these people. But now, after the sudden down fall of his empire, he was no more than a common street civilian. Now, it was time to regain that power, not by drugs, and not by gangs, but by this show, NO PLACE LIKE HOME. It was time to change the way people look at reality television. It was time to change the way people look at television- period. “Are you fucking stupid?” wonders J.C, turning back to face Ynonick once more. “I don’t think I have to repeat myself twice. I am the man in this house right now, your mother and father are gone, and you know who is in charge....ME!” Ynonick begins to break a sweat under Jayson’s heavily glare. He hits the EXIT button one last time, before advancing towards the GW superstar. The large built J.C stares were he is, but then, Ynonick somehow ends chest to chest with J.C. He looks up at this large man- larger than he will ever be. The smell of fear was rolling off of Ynonick’s face, and J.C could smell it. He knew that this youth child wanted nothing to do with his power, and push. It was time to learn who the authority was around here. “If you want to try something,” exclaims Jayson, lowering his head to continue the glare on Ynonick. “I fucking dare you! I don’t think you have the balls to do anything Ynonick.” With all of the said, J.C turned around, his back facing the un-trusted Ynonick. He was in no fear of getting hurt. Jayson begins to shake his head rapidly. Suddenly, Jayson turns around, twisting his head in a heard of position. He takes a deep breath. “Yeah- that’s what I thought.” J.C stared down at the thin, blackened face of Ynonick Martin. He had no idea what he was getting himself into at this point of time. Usually when Jayson proceeded to talk smack to a child, they would listen to him. But Ynonick on the other hand was very ignorant on this very day, not listening to Jayson for one second. It was time to loosen this teenager up, and nothing better to do this with than the ole’ MARIJUANA. “Like I said before- get fucking ready!” yelled J.C, lowering his brow. Ynonick watches in silence as Jayson makes his way out of the bedroom. [Scene Fades] ----------------- [Scene: Inside of the Martin Residence. On the couch lies J.C- weed in hand. The cameras are in front of Jayson, capturing his every moment on tape. Young Ynonick emerges from the kitchen.] “Some people are fucking slow,” shrug J.C, also giving a sigh at that moment. It was a little confusing to J.C about this day. Usually it is the father that teaches his son about man hood, and how to become one. But looking back on the situation- looking back at Chris Martin, the father, he didn’t have a clue what the hell to do. Jayson was the man now, he knew how to turn a young child into a man, and that was his goal today, to turn Ynonick into a man. When Jayson was in his youth, he didn’t really have a father, well he did, but the poor old fucker was working half the time and didn’t spend much time with J.C. He had learned how to become a man by himself, growing up on the streets of Queens. He didn’t have just one family, he had two families. His second family happens to be the “Hoods”- well used to be the HOODS. The point of the matter was, if no one teaches Ynonick about becoming a man, he will end up to be another worthless business man that no one gives a shit about- Jayson sure doesn't. Could you ever picture J.C as a man with a suit- a tie, walking down the streets of Miami ready to hit his office job? FUCK NO! Jayson enjoys the HIGH life, and when he says “HIGH” he means it. He has always loved the lifestyle of the gang member ATTITUDE- it was just his way of life. Some people enjoy going to church. Some people enjoy investing their money in stocks, but Jayson, he enjoys smoking the grass and enjoying his life making people laugh and watch him wrestle and act. At this point in his life, Jayson knows he is not a man to be recon with, especially in Miami. He is a marked man- he knows that for a fact. But everything still remained; he was living the good life, and was still paying the price for it. “What are we doing Jayson?” asked Ynonick with a very confused look on his youth face. “You are becoming a man,” answers Jayson right back. “How do I become a man? I thought you have to be eighteen to become a man?” Ynonick insisted- brushing his thin blonde hair back. Jayson lowered his brow very enthusiastically. He shuffled his feet on the ground for a few seconds, trying not to laugh at this poor child. Realizing that Chris and Crystal were not the most influential people in Ynonick’s life, Jayson knew he had to do something before this child would kill himself- Jayson meant it. What did Ynonick have going for him? He was nothing in this society. He was a piece of common street trash. J.C grinned widely, glancing at Ynonick as he stood next to him trying to figure this situation out. There was no time for reasoning, it was time to get down to business. J.C took a soft-deep breath. “It doesn’t work that way Ynonick, you must under stand that,” he flared. “The only way to become a man Ynonick, is when you are ready- and I feel that this day is your time to shine in the light.” Jayson lied; he was only doing this for the hell of it- and to get a few laughs out of the show. This teenage child wasn’t ready. He was a complete dumb ass above everything else. He didn’t have a clue what Jayson was saying, and he could see in his dark blue eyes that Ynonick was thinking about the COMPUTER again. J.C smacked the thought out of his mind. “-So, how do I become a man?” asked the young teenager with a large smile upon his face. “You want to become a man in this world? You want to become much more than a youth..” chuckles Jayson. “I believe it is your time Ynonick.” Oh it was his time, and it was time to give this show some spice. Reaching deep into his pocket, feeling the pocket lint as it raped around his fingers, he pulled out the large bag of MARIJUANA. The smell incinerated the room, making it hard for Ynonick to breathe, on the other hand- Jayson loved the smell of GRASS in the morning. This is how Ynonick was going to become a man. He was going to smoke this whole bag, by himself, well maybe with a little help by J.C, but he was going to smoke it within two hours. Simple right? Wrong! This was accomplished only by one man- one man that was strong, powerful and over whelmed by the odds- this man was non other than J.C. He assumed that Ynonick was going to turn this down, but if he wanted to become a man, this is how he was going to do it. “Here,” demanded Pretty Boy J.C, handing both the weed and the pipe to Ynonick Martin. “Come on- we don't have all fucking day god damn it! Take the fucking pipe and the weed! Come on bitch.” Jayson leaned back in his seat, Ynonick snatching both things out of his hand. He nodded very proudly. “That’s how you become a man...” [The camera’s fade to black, Ynonick looking down at the weed as Jayson proceeds to give his advice to the young teenager.]
