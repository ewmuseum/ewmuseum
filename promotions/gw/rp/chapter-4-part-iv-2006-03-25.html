Date: 3-25-06 The scene opens behind Shinjuku Station where Widow was undergoing her transformation. Her pink skin tone turns to an ugly black as a couple of long legs started sprouting out from each side of her figure. She starts growing in size as her face started changing from beauty to monster. Before too long Widow’s transformation becomes complete as Kyo was facing a giant black widow spider. “That’s quite an improvement from the other look.” Kyo smirked. “Joke all you want, but you should know that you are facing the one who will defeat you.” Widow glared at him with her many crimson eyes. Kyo has his eyes closed with dull look on his face. “Like I never heard that one before.” “Did I mention that this is the same exact form I took when I devoured your best friend, Jun.” Widow smirked. “I know that told you that he was tasty, but I think he needed some soy sauce since that goes great with anything Japanese.” She started laughing with her eyes locked on him. “Let’s end this.” Kyo glared at her. ==|Part IV: The Things You Do For Love (cont.)|== Kyo blurs away instantly! Widow was moving her many eyes around searching for Kyo’s whereabouts both, visually and spiritually. “There you are!” She shifted all her eyes to right where she swung one of her many legs in that direction with great speed. Kyo soon reappears in that direction as he got nailed by hard by her attack and was thrown into the side of the train’s cart. “Damnit!” He slides off the dent and landed on one knee as he aimed the Spell Binder at Widow. Widow’s mouth split wide open and fired a large blast of webbing at Kyo. Kyo avoids it by leaping high in the air, but he quickly got knock back down on the ground with another strong blast of webbing. Kyo falls on a pair of train tracks and winced when his hand struck the third rail. He quickly moved his hand away from there and stared at the track as he realized that these pair of tracks has been activated. Kyo looks up and noticed that the train was heading right at him. Widow comes out of nowhere and jumped in front of Kyo as she was unaware of the train that approached them. “The itsy bitsy spider is going to get you now!” She glared down at him and brought her leg straight down at him. Kyo quickly catches Widow’s leg that was inches away from his throat. “I light the itsy bitsy spider up!” He pulled her leg down on the third rail! Widow screams out as she felt the electricity run throughout her large body! Kyo picks up the Spell Binder and quickly leaped high up in the air where he flipped over Widow’s head. Widow slightly looks at Kyo, but she finally noticed the train coming right for her. Widow looks with great fear. “KUSHINAGA!” The train plows Widow down! Kyo lands on top of the moving train as he almost lost his balance, but he quickly caught himself. “And the itsy bitsy spider becomes road kill.” He smirked and began resting on the train. “Ku’Ku’Kushinaga?” Kyo looks over to the Widow’s voice and saw that her upper torso was quickly crawling over to him. “Damn, don’t you know when to quit.” He closed his eyes and lowered his head as she sigh with frustration. “I’ll’I’ll take’I’ll take you to Lord Aion no matter what!” Widow thrust one of her front leg right at Kyo’s head. Kyo avoids her attack by swiftly tilting his head as he pulled out the Spell Binder and placed the barrel against the side of her head. “This is for you my friend.” His eyes flared violet as he pulled the trigger and watched Widow get blown away by the strong force of his God Shell. Widow covered in magic flames fall off the side of the train screaming, but her screaming soon ended when she exploded! Kyo stands up, but he soon looked with surprise when he saw that the train was heading for the station. “Shit?” [:: ----- ::] Chi flips up in the air and spin her sword around a couple of times before she swung it across releasing a millions of sharp energy needles on the remaining Ghost Men. The Ghost Men turn into black vapors when the needles struck them. Chi lands with perfection as she smirked and rested her sword over her shoulder. “That’s that.” A loud boom echoes throughout the station! “What the hell!”” Chi snapped her and saw that the train has crashed through the back wall. The train was heading right at her. Chi stays where she was as there wasn’t a trace of fear on her face while she watched the train. Train stops a few inches away from Chi’s feet where it stand in silence. The train’s side doors burst open! “Avenger?” Chi approached the train with caution. Kyo steps out of the train with his Spell Binder resting over his shoulder. “Well, it isn’t a Super Saiyan.” He approached her. Chi looks with slight surprise when she noticed that Kyo’s appearance has changed since the last time. Her look soon changes from surprise to curiosity when she saw Kyo reach out to her. “What are you?”” Kyo slips his hand within Chi’s cloak. Chi’s face lights up as she glared at him with anger and embarrassment. “What the hell are you doing!”” She has thrown a slap at him. Kyo blocks her slap. “I’m not in here to cup a feel.” He pulled out both, her cigarettes and lighter from her cloak. “I was only seeking these.” Kyo started smoking while looking at her with a grin. “Besides you’re not my type.” “Bastard.” Chi glared at him. “Well, I sense that the demonic aura has diminished which means that you destroyed the source.” Kyo sharpens his eyes in seriousness when sensed something approaching them from the air as spiritual fire suddenly appeared coil around both markings on his forearms. Chi raised a brow and looked with confusion. Kyo brings his hands together in cuff position as he swung his body around and thrust both hands out releasing a great blast of spiritual fire up at the certain which revealed to be Widow. 'Now it's diminished.' Chi looks with surprise as she didn’t even sense Widow’s presence. Widow falls straight down to the ground covered in flames as she looked with sorrow. “Lord Aion?” Tears managed to form in her eyes while she was on fire. She soon falls dead on the ground. Chi stares at Kyo with her eyes quivering in great shock. “So, this is the power of a Sinner.” “Damn bitch made me waste my last God Shell.” Kyo flicked his cigarette out his mouth in frustration as he started heading to the exit. “Hey wait up!” Chi followed him. [:: ----- ::] In an alley that was near Jun’s Herbal Store, a Ghost Man stand there with his eyes locked on the store as if he was waiting for someone to show up there. Someone taps on the Ghost Man’s shoulder as he turned and saw that Kyo was standing behind him. “Acquiring the target now.” He pulled out his large gun from within his suit. Kyo grabs hold of the gun and turned the gun on its? owner as he made the Ghost Man shoot himself in the chest multiple times until the Ghost Man turn into black vapor. “Don’t you think that was overkill.” Chi stood behind him. “It’s been a rough day.” Kyo dismantled the Ghost Man’s gun and threw it in the nearby garbage bin. [:: ----- ::] They enter where they saw that the store has been messed up all thanks to Widow. Chi covers her nose. “What is that smell?” Kyo stands behind the front counter and stared down with seriousness hiding the sorrow that he was feeling. “My friend.” Chi walks up to the counter and looked with surprise when seeing Jun’s corpse which appeared to have been melted before it was eaten through. “Shit.” She glared. “Why would someone do this?” “He discovered my secret.” Kyo pulled out the Book of the Damned from underneath the counter and opened it to the chapter that Jun was reading before his demise. “He discovered that I was a Sinner.” “I still don’t understand.” Chi glared at him. “How is it possible for you to be a Sinner.” “If you have stayed in the restaurant then I would have answered that question for you.” Kyo and Chi looked to the familiar voice and saw that Megumi was standing in the open doorway with a large metal suitcase in hand. Megumi takes notice of Kyo’s transformation. “I see that you have reawakened Sinner Ryuujin.” [:: ----- ::] Underneath the store in Jun’s secret weapon workshop, Kyo was assembling weapons that are going to much powerful than the originals while Megumi was explaining Kyo’s origins to Chi. “Kyo’s real name is Ryuujin Tenshi.” Megumi looked at Chi with seriousness. “Both his parents were angels.” Chi blinks. “I would’ve thought he gotten those powers from being a cross breed.” Megumi shakes her head. “Nope, he’s a full blooded angel and a full blooded demon.” She noticed that Chi has a confuse look on her face. “I know that doesn’t make sense, but he is.” “In other words I’m a freak.” Kyo finished assembling one gun and began work on another. “How did he become this way?” Kyo stops working on his gun. “You have to kill a few million people.” He looked at Chi with a smirk. “Then bathe in their blood to become what I am.” Chi blinks. “You’re kidding.” Megumi closes her eyes as she has a serious look on her face. “Unfortunately he is not.” “What!”” Chi looked with shock. “In the warring era of Feudal Japan, Ryuujin went on a killing spree. He killed over one million humans which made up Nobunaga Oda’s entire army.” Chi looks with confusion. “But why?” “Nobunaga was hunting down Shinto Priests and Priestess.” Megumi opened her eyes halfway and looked at Kyo. “His lover was a Shinto Priestess who was slain because of that foolish manhunt.” “So, this was an act vengeance over the death of one woman.” Chi blinked and looked with surprise. “What can I say.” Kyo smirked as he resumed working on his gun. “Love can make you do insane things.” “However, there’s an evil twist to this story.” Megumi crossed her arms. “It appears that this was all a set up to kill have Ryuujin kill Nobunaga.” “Why?” “The Genma Lord and the God of Evil, Amatsu Mikaboshi made an agreement. You see, the Genma Lord asked Amatsu if he would get him Nobunaga to lead his army.” Megumi stared down at the floor with seriousness. “Amatsu agreed to do this but for price. And that price was a million of his strong Genma Warriors since Amatsu was plotting on destroying the Spirit World with his prot’g”, Ryuujin by his side.” “Of course, I found out that Amatsu was responsible for my beloved’s death and I saw it upon myself to pay him back for my pain.” Kyo finished assembling his other gun as he started building another weapon. “Ryuujin killed Amatsu’s army and then killed Amatsu himself as he bathed in the blood of his victims.” Megumi has a serious look on her face. “In legend it’s said that if you kill one thousand demons and bathe in their blood then you will become half demon, but there’s more to this legend. If you kill over a million demons then you become a full demon.” “Ok, then why did his power happen to awaken now?” “The death of a close one can make anyone’s strength grow dramatically.” Megumi still looked down. “However, in Kyo’s case the power that Amatsu Mikaboshi granted him centuries ago was restraining his true power that is until The Organization had it removed.” Chi nods with a surprise look still on her face. Megumi looks at Chi with seriousness. “You have just witness the power of a Sinner which is something that Aion and Mother’s Children are. Will you join me in our fight to save this world?” Chi scoffs as she started heading to the staircase that lead back up to the surface. “Chi?” Megumi looked at her with confusion. Chi stops at the staircase. “You hired me to protect Avenger from being killed by the others like him.” She glanced over at Megumi. “You didn’t hire me to fight a war.” “But your destiny ?”” Chi glares at Megumi. “Shut up! My only destiny is to have Avenger fall before my sword!” Kyo scoffs with his back turn on Chi. “I’m getting tired of saying this, but you really do need to pick a number.” He placed a crimson crystal within something as he screwed something on top. Chi lets out a laugh before she turned away from him. “I will.” She smirked as she started walking upstairs before she blurred away. Megumi sighs. “I really wish she would come into terms of her destiny.” She turned and looked at Kyo. “How’ll about you, Ryuujin” Will you join me?” “In this fight for vengeance?” “Huh?” Megumi raised a brow. Kyo stops assembling the weapon as he turned around and looked at Megumi with seriousness. “You’re not only fighting to save mankind, but you’re fighting for vengeance.” Megumi has a distant look on her face. “But I?” “You’ve witnessed your sisters being viciously murdered by Aion and his group that has led you to seek vengeance.” Kyo light a cigarette and started smoking. “Vengeance is a foe that can be defeated by three ways. You can either let it go. You can let it kill you. Or you can kill it.” He stared at Megumi. “What will you do when you come face to face with this foe.” Megumi stands there speechless. Kyo turns back around and started assembling his weapons once more. Megumi sits the metal suitcase on a nearby table. “Athena and Morgan have made you this. They assure me that it can handle your new abilities.” She started heading up the stairs. Kyo was continuing to construct his weapons. “I think you were a bit harsh on her.” “I don’t want her to ?”” Kyo paused as looked at the gentle voice and saw that the ghost of his beloved, Rain has appeared behind him. He turns his attention back to his weapon. “I don’t want her to follow the same path that I have.” Kyo looked with seriousness. Rain looks at Kyo’s back with concern. “Rain?” Rain has a curious look on her face. “Did you know what I was?” “Yes, an angel from the Heavens that I have fallen in love with.” Kyo lets a small laugh. “Your angel has turned into a devil or maybe something worst.” “You believe that you have turned into something evil, but you haven’t changed as you’re still the kind man that I love.” “I believe I bleed all that out a long time ago.” Kyo smirked as he started constructing another weapon and installed a sapphire crystal before he screwed something on top of it. “Your kindness is still there. You just have a different way of showing it now.” Kyo sigh the cigarette smoke out of his lips. “I guess so.” “You know what needs to be done.” Rain looked at him with slight seriousness. “I also think you don’t need to know what will happen if Aion succeed with his plans.” “Hell on Earth times two.” “Be careful, Kage.” Kyo lowers his head as his hair fall over his saddened eyes. “I will?” He tightly held the silver wedding band close to his heart. Rain disappears as she returned back to Spirit World. [:: ----- ::] Megumi stands outside of Jun’s store as she was staring down at concrete ground in deep thought about what Kyo said to her. Her thoughts are broken when she saw Kyo holding a pack of cigarettes beside her as she shook her head. Kyo shrugs and placed the pack in his pocket as he continued smoking his already lit cigarette. “I see that the clothes that Athena and Morgan fit you.” Kyo nods. “It’s very comfortable.” He looked down at his shirt. “I see that they included the Punisher skull and I love the trench coat.” “The trench coat is bulletproof as well heat and cold resistant.” Megumi still stared down at the ground. “Your clothes have the same features except for being bulletproof so try not getting shot without your trench coat on.” She soon looked at him with seriousness. “Both gauntlets have been blessed with a light protection spell that can conceal out any darkness spells.” Megumi started thinking. “I believe that your new clothes can resist small energy attacks, but now real powerful ones.” “They outdid themselves.” Megumi looks at him. “I see that have returned back to your human form. That’s a good idea since your aura won’t be so easy to detect.” Kyo sighs releasing the smoke pass his lips. “I’m sorry for what I said before.” Megumi looks at him with curiousness. Kyo looks with seriousness. “Vengeance is not only a foe it can also be an endless and vicious cycle of painful events. I don’t it’s the kind of life that a Supreme Maiden should live.” He looked at her with a smirk. “So, let me be the one to handle Aion and his group while you continue doing what ever Supreme Maidens do without having ill-feelings.” Megumi looks with surprise. “You’ll help save mankind from Aion?” “Guess so.” Megumi turns and bow before him. “Thank you, Ryuujin.” Kyo turns away and started walking away from Megumi. “Be sure to thank to Athena and Morgan for me.” Megumi stands up and looks with slight curiousness when she noticed that Kyo has drawn a sun symbol on the back of his trench coat in white. It was the same symbol that’s on the back of Kyo Kusanagi’s jacket, but it has the Japanese characters for sin written within it. She stares at him for a moment longer before she blurred away where she was heading back to Eden Isle. Kyo has approached his bike as he got on and turned on the ignition. He was about to drive, but he gave his friend’s store one final glance. “Goodbye, my friend.” Kyo put on his shades and ride off. There are Ghost Men searching for something in the lobby. Aion was standing in front of the train that has crashed through the station as he his was staring down at Widow’s burnt and twisted corpse. Ghost Man approaches Aion. “Lord, we found something.” Aion stands there in silence. Ghost Man looks at Aion with curiousness. “Lord Aion?” “I was just wondering what would drive a woman to fight a fight that she couldn’t win.” Aion kept his light blue eyes on the corpse. Ghost Man stares at him. “For love.” Aion looked at the Ghost Man with a smile among his serious face. “A foolish emotion for a foolish woman.” He sighed. “Show me what you found.” “This way.” Ghost Man was heading to the large hole that the train made. Aion follows him as he stepped and crushed Widow’s skull. [:: ----- ::] Behind the station was a few more Ghost Men who were searching too, but they are now standing around something. Aion and the Ghost Man approach the crowd as the Ghost Men began moving out of Aion’s way. “I hope you found something useful in this war zone.” Aion looked with seriousness as he was cleaning his glasses. It was reveal that Dr. White was here too as she was kneeling before some of Widow’s webbing. “Oh yes, this is quite the discovery.” Dr. White picked up the webbing as she stand up and hold the webbing out. “Take a look at this.” Aion looks at the webbing and notice some skin tissue and blood. “So?” “Lord Aion since we can’t get Mr. Kushinaga to join us then may I propose an alternative plan of action.” “I’m listening.” Aion glared at Dr. White. ---- This ends the first volume of the Chronicles of the Deathscythe Outlaw. ----
