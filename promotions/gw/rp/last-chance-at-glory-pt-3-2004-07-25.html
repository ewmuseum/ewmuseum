Last Chance at Glory Pt.3

25 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 25, 2004 Prologue. An emotional train wreck is all that life has been for me. From the deepest speeches from my father, to the weeping cries I remember of my mother. The world has turned and twisted so many times in such a short time over these last few days. Aches and pains have grown from simple to complex in ways people could never comprehend. The walls of my heart are caving in, as the world around be begins to fade to dark, a pitch-black hallway to emptiness. It’s all I see now, inside of me, it’s all I can feel now. I can’t control the emotions, a stress roller coaster one ride, and then a weep fest the next. It’s a shame to know I could not control this. Beyond my limits this feeling is, a plague with no reason nor fixation. Lying inside me it infests the organs with a remedy of death, only to put me out of a misery I have wanted to be gone from long ago. Blessing it’ll be, when the base of my heart and mind collapse, and the last thing I see in my world is the floor in which I crumble upon. Let the world remember the name… Jesse Jamester. Forgotten Fortunes. ‘Spiraling down into the pitch black hole I find myself in, there is no way to stop this pain, no way to end this suffering, only patience and time will be the answer to those problems. It’s like my heart has jumped a life and decided where I will go, rather than me being able. There’s no end to the aches I feel, as much as I wish their was, I know deep down that the aches will grow until I can no longer fight them. Cricketing like old wooden steps in a horror movie house, I feel my veins giving me that same vibe. It’s the end of the beginning, and the beginning of the end all over again.’ Tapping his foot, the water begins to slowly drain from the puddle that the foot lies in. It’d rained all day, and everything around the park was completely drenched in the cloud’s tears. Yet that didn’t stop Jesse from sitting on the park bench staring at the lake as rain made the ground slush with each patter of feet across the sidewalk. Looking down, his face is reflected through the puddle of water that he protects from being disrupted by the rain as his body hovers over it. Eyes of mischievous content and soulless hope trickle down through what seems to be tears but is actually rain, that drips from the sides of Jesse’s eyes simultaneously. ’It’s a crippling feeling I can’t shake off. The agony of defeat does not even come close to this feeling of misery in me. Heartbroken I sit here, wondering whether if my life, or life in general, is worth living for. Then comes those pondering moments, when you wish death would strike you down at this very moment, and that their behind you the soul keeper of the dark world would stand to collect yours. I can’t imagine how I’ll ever live knowing my father has walked out on my life after already being gone for over twenty eight years of my life… I just can’t believe it.’ Steadily the rain falls, as Jesse stands up and walks down the paved sidewalk in the park. His mind nowhere near focused upon any single thought, but rather tossed in a million directions that are trying to find their way to an origin. Sojourning upon a bridge, he stares over into the water and looks at himself. A pale twenty eight year old kid lost in his ways while trying to maintain a decent life. To everyone else, they saw a 6’6” beast that could decimate their well being with a single punch or kick. The people outside of the world that Jesse knew, the wrestling world, people who he’d only seen at autograph sessions, they feared him. This he dwelled on, as people do so on things that worry them, and so normally it had taken Jesse a long time to realize what made him lose track of life, and lose track of what was important. ’It’s not the money, nor the fame, and not even the gold… It’s the love, the love to wrestle. I’ve gone from a kid who loved to wrestle to someone who has lost his passion when he became tangled with his own demons. I have to go back to my roots, and find what gave me that passion in the first place.’ Spitting over the edge Jesse’s spit disappeared in the rain as it all comes down around him. His eyes slowly close, giving his face a depressing look as he turns and stares up at the gray clustered cloudy sky. Not much can be seen but rain falling and murky clouds that seem to never move. ’It’s about time I get into what I have put aside for the longest time. The time to begin looking to the future and stop worrying about my past, and to let go of all the things that make me weak, so I can build strength in the belief I carry. It’s about time… I prove myself worthy of the name I made for myself long ago.’ Walking off the bridge, Jesse continues down the long path through the park gates and stops just outside them before raising his arms into the air and tilting his head back like a man who’s sins were washed away by Christ himself. A beam of happiness lights up on Jesse’s face as he raises his hand for a taxi and hops in. Swift Revelations. Tick… tock… tick… tock… tick… tock… Resonates throughout the apartment as Jesse sits at a table with six friends surrounding him. The first one is short, maybe 5’10” and has scraggily curly hair that is brown. A smirk of confidence shines over his face as he stares at his hand of cards. ’Heh… Corey thinks he has the upper hand, but oh he’s in for a big surprise.’ Next to Corey is the complete opposite of him, a 6’10” Muslim whom has short flat top hair. His face shows no expression only a blink or two before he folds. ’Omar, ah a man who won’t take a risk if his life depended on it. Was a great wrestler, sadly quit cause of his knee injury.’ After Omar came to guys who both had long hair and both looked like brothers besides they had completely different facial expressions. The one had no facial hair as the other had a black goatee that only appeared on his chin and went nowhere near the mouth. The one with no facial hair had multiple piercing, and a tattoo on his neck. ‘Ryan and Steve… Ah, two of the guys who made wrestling life for me so much fun back in NRW. Sadly both suck at cards, but rock on the instruments and have beautiful women in their lives.’ Next in line was a white Caucasian man with a scraggily beard and short black hair, but what mostly makes him interesting is his black glasses that hide his eyes. ’Nick Slater… A man of few words, yet many actions. Nick’s been depressed since his wife left him and took his son with her, and so has taken drinking up as a hobby… Pretty decent at cards too.’ Finally the last person, a man whose head shines with the light above, because he’s bald, and sits their completely bamboozled by the cards he has in his hand. A goatee and a neck that looks like a racket for all the chains it holds, he shows some signs of young age and boredom as he plays the card game. ’Ah… Chase Mathews. One of my best friends since high school, and finally we were reunited when I found him in a bar down in Texas. Does drugs, but eh, we all have once or twice in our life, so I realize it’s a stage he’ll get past.’ Everyone puts their cards down starting with Jesse. Jesse: Ace, King, Queen, Jack, Ten all in spades. Corey: 10, 2, Jack, 5, King mixed. Omar: Folded. Ryan: Queen, 10, 4, Jack, 2 mixed. Steve: 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 mixed. Nick: Ace, King, Queen, Jack, Joker all in hearts Chase: 7, 8, 9, 10, Jack mixed. Because jokers are wild Nick wins and Jesse looks down and smiles at his defeat. ’Eh, can’t win’em all… But I sure will give it my best shot. That includes MOTM. Sorrow had mislead him, Love had drained him, Cold blood culminated in his heart, Turning it the darkest of shadows, Yet peace somehow ceased, Even though he’d become the devils advocate, So when he turned his flaring eyes, Whipping his long brown locks over his shoulder, He burned a hole in your mind, As the man he would always be, Before smirking at you, Saying, “Hello there, how are you?”
