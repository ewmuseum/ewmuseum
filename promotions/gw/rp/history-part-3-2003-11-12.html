'History', part 3

12 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 12th, 2003 It was enormous. As Akodo Rokku sat on the hood of his beaten-up Hyundai, staring up at IWL Arena, a wide variety of thoughts ran through his mind -- but none more loudly than that. It really was enormous. A circular construct of concrete and steel, dominating the outskirts of Tokyo. It was imposing, to say the least -- some might say terrifying. Banishing all doubt and twisting his lips into a cocky smirk, Rokku hopped off of his car and shouldered his gym bag. He marched toward the front doors confidently. It wasn't so scary. When he looked at the building, he didn't see terror at all. He saw opportunity. *** The inside of the arena was, unsurprisingly, just as big as the outside. It didn't take Rokku long to become completely lost, wandering hallways that were by and large empty. Would it have killed Hari to be waiting at the entrance for him? Sighing, Rokku continued to wander through the arena, until he found himself pushing his way through a set of curtains. Immediately he winced at the bright light, shielding his eyes until they adjusted. When they did, he had to take a moment to absorb everything. Thousands upon thousands of seats swept out from where he stood, each one of them ready to house a customer -- no, a fan. He closed his eyes for a moment, and imagined what it would be like, to step out onto this stage, and walk down the ramp in front of thousands of people. They would be cheering. Cheering for him. Smiling broadly, Rokku began a slow walk down the ramp. As he neared the ring in the center of the arena, his attention was drawn to the people inside it. Rokku stepped over the security barrier and grabbed a ringside seat, watching with keen interest. Inside the ring, a large hulk of a man circled his opponent as trainers looked on. This was fairly normal, really -- but he wasn't what had caught Rokku's attention. His opponent was. Lithe and graceful, she moved with absolute precision, every step clearly calculated to counter his. Rokku's eyes never left her petit body, watching as the muscles of her flawless dancer's build tensing and relaxing in time, ready at any moment for her larger opponent to attack. And he did. A huge right cross came at the girl, but she hardly looked worried, easily sidestepping the punch. Before the man could react, she lashed out with both hands, gripping his arm at its sensitive joints, and applying an obviously painful amount of pressure. Using his own momentum against him, the girl took control of his motion, sending him into a corner. He lost his balance and stumbled, his face bouncing off of the top turnbuckle. Flowing from one move into the next with perfect timing, the girl drove a toe kick into the man's kidney, then swept his legs out from under him. The instant his head struck the mat, she drove a heel into his temple, then another into his ribs, until he started to get back on his feet. The girl stepped back, allowing him to rise, and smiled softly at him when he regained his footing. More cautiously this time, the man circled her, looking for an opening. Unfortunately for him, she wasn't willing to let him find one. As he circled behind her, the girl took a quick step back and drove a lightning quick bare foot into his stomach. The man stumbled back and she was on him, stepping onto his knee and pivoting to kick him in the head. As he fell towards the mat, the girl slipped by him, rapidly ascending the turnbuckles to perch at the top. And there she waited. Slowly, the man pulled himself back to his feet -- though it was obvious that he had no idea where he was any more, let alone what he was doing. He swayed on his feet, finally turning to face her. Rokku was awed by the brutal beauty of what he saw next. The girl launched herself from the top rope, looking more like she belonged in the sky than anything else he'd ever seen. As she flew through the air, she corkscrewed her body in a perfect -- though horizontal -- pirouette. Finally, her legs splayed, and then game together again with explosive force, a foot crashing into either side of her opponent's head. He collapsed, slamming into the mat like dead weight -- and she landed flawlessly, placing a hand on the fallen man's chest. One of the trainers at ringside counted out three, and there was a short burst of applause from those surrounding the ring. The girl gestured towards one of the trainers and he tossed her a water bottle. As some of the trainers checked on her fallen training partner, the girl brushed her short snow-white hair out of her eyes, and tipped her head back, taking a long drink. Rokku simple stared on mutely, absolutely fascinated by her every motion. She was just about the most amazing thing he'd ever seen. 'Just about the most amazing thing you've ever seen, isn't she?' 'GYAH!' screamed Rokku, nearly falling out of his seat. Next to him sat Yoritomo Hari, who couldn't help but laugh at the younger man's shock. 'Sorry,' said Hari, running a hand through his graying hair, 'I just didn't want to interrupt your enjoyment of the show.' 'You're going to give me a heart attack at the age of twenty,' grumbled Rokku, as he settled himself back in his seat. 'I doubt it son,' said Hari, smiling, 'you're much too hearty for that. So, you like what you see?' 'Like is an understatement,' said Rokku, his eyes locked on the beautiful young martial artist as she conversed with one of the trainers. 'Who is she?' 'Kakita Yoshiko,' said Hari, 'another one of our young prospects. And I have to say, I'm just as amazed by her as you are, son. She won her first martial arts championship at the age of seven. Not only that, but she has a master's degree in both law and business. I honestly have no idea what she's doing here with us.' 'Master's degrees?' Rokku raised an eyebrow. 'She doesn't look a day older than me.' 'She isn't,' said Hari, 'in fact, she's a year younger. She got almost all of her education at home apparently. Her father, it would seem, was nothing if not an overachiever. Of course, her genius level IQ helped her out, I'm sure.' Rokku whistled appreciatively. 'So she's beautiful, deadly, and brilliant? She sounds too perfect to be real.' 'Well,' said Hari, extending his hand to Rokku, 'if you want to get to know her better, there's really only one way. So let me ask you right now son: Are you in?' Rokku's lips split into an insufferably wide grin, one that would become more and more famous in the coming years. 'Oh yeah,' said Rokku, shaking Hari's hand firmly, 'I'm in. Hari-san, you've got yourself a superstar.' 'Superstar?' This time, it was Hari who rose an eyebrow curiously. 'Aren't you getting ahead of yourself there, son?' 'See me in the ring, and then tell me that,' replied Rokku. Hari only smiled, as Rokku rose from his seat and marched down to ringside, towards his destiny.
