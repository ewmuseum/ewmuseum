“The Journey” (Part 3/ Joshua’s POV)

14 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/14/04 Wednesday October 13th, 2004 5:45 A.M. How’s the first couple of words go to “Lamb Lies Down on Broadway” go?.oh yeah! Early morning Manhattan, Ocean winds blow on the land. Movie-Palace is now undone, The all-night watchmen have had their fun. Sleeping cheaply on the midnight show, It's the same old ending - time to go. Get out! It seems they cannot leave their dream. There's something moving in the sidewalk steam, And the lamb lies down...on Broadway. Ok so that was more than a couple of words but I think it will fit us properly right now because its early morning’in New York City and oh by the way its pitch freaking black in the house on the block near Times Square where Joshua and Zack live which means Joshua is sitting in his room contemplating the life that he lives and the lifestyle by which some people judge him and wrongly I might add. The thing that a lot of people know about Joshua is that he is a family man and the way he helps his sister tells them that ever since his mother, God rest her soul, passed away 4 years ago. Joshua has lived for what people to know as “The moment” and truth be told for his in the coming week his first true gut check in GW is about to come for the simple fact that the man he is about to face in El Serpenti for the right to go on to a Cruiserweight Title shot is staring him down and staring him down hard! 5:45 in the morning and ain’t it a bitch! Its raining its ass off and Joshua, being the trooper that he is still about to go do his morning jog and not give two craps if he catches Pneumonia! The fact that he is still going for his jog REGARDLESS for the fact that he doesn’t have his one “item of stability” with him has to tell you a little something about the fact that here is a young man and believe me 25 is still young enough to whoop some serious ass but here is a young man whom is alone right now and he is still going to do his routine because its very hard to phase him or get him to divert from the game plans that he sets for himself! But today?.today felt different for some reason. It felt like’I don’t know like Joshua had someone on his mind and it wasn’t Zack but then again he knew that Zack was doing his thing taking care of mother in the hospital as the headaches only got worse and not better back in San Francisco. Joshua was doing this jog and at the same time he was thinking of a young man that he had met only a day ago and couldn’t help but wonder if there is any way to help him WITHOUT pissing certain people off like a Josh or anyone else for that matter! The thing about KPJ for Joshua was that he knew that KPJ had zoned out, happens to him when he doesn’t take the medication that the doctor tells him he absolutely must take and not just because of the fact that he has Epilepsy but also because if he chooses not to take it there is a 6 foot tall, 210 pound demon that will make his life miserable that lives within him but nobody knows about the so-called “Dark Angel” and that’s how Joshua likes it. If you know about him the chances are you are going to exploit Joshua’s emotions get him to come out and reveal the worst side of himself that could ever possibly be seen! When Joshua was having the conversation with Ken a lot of things came out of it for Joshua and one of them was basically that these are two men walking on identical strings towards the exact same goal and they were going to have to open up to each other in the future to be able to see the other through to the title that neither man could accomplish: The World Heavyweight Title! Now I know its not typical for a wrestler to help another person to the same exact goal that he has in the company but out of respect for Ken, Joshua is willing to step aside and do his thing while Ken goes for the brass ring knowing damn well that his day IS coming its just not now! As Joshua is rounding the corner in his navy blue sweat suit the fans that are up at this hour are shouting encouragement towards Joshua as he goes for his jog from get this: New York City to the Brooklyn Bridge and back again! The reason for this “spiritual jog” is to quiet the negative energies that live in him because that is what “Dark Angel” feeds off of and to have him be released?. Lord almighty I perish to think what he would do to the people that Joshua cares about. See people firmly believe whether right or wrongly that Joshua is just the host for the parasite that lives in him and that “Dark Angel” can come out whenever, and I do mean whenever he really feels like it. The catch for “Dark Angel” is that Joshua emotionally speaking is getting “close” to Ken. By close I mean whether Ken realizes it or not yet by talking to Joshua in the Starbucks and actually having a conversation with him Joshua formed a “link” of sorts with this man because Ken “got it” and got it well enough that Joshua can safely say that he trusts him! Ok Ok I know what you are thinking and that’s this: But Joshua isn’t Ken with this “Gunner?” Am I right? That’s what is going through your delicate little head right? Well yes he is and Joshua recognizes it not to mention he has the upmost in respect for the former champion. The thing of it is about Ken that drives Joshua to him is that when it comes to people whom he feels he can trust outside of Zack that Ken is so far the only human being alive that he can talk to and trust. Maybe that’s not such a good thing but for right now it’s the only way that Joshua can survive in the fed known as Global Wrestling! After about another 90 minutes of jogging (which means its about 7:15 in the morning) Joshua gets back to the house absolutely DRENCHED in a mixture of rain and sweat and believe me this didn’t go over too well with a certain young lady that was waiting at the house for him! “[name withheld]” The young lady began shouting at Joshua as he came panting up to her as he finished his jog “do you have ANY idea what you are doing”! You are SOAKED! Let’s get you inside this instant!” The young lady finished before dragging Joshua inside, stripping him of his clothing and throwing him into a hot shower to warm up. When Joshua was good and warm the water was shut off and a towel thrown on him by the young lady, whom can now be positively identified as Juri Arisugawa, dried off Joshua and hugged him tightly! Why? Believe me when I tell you that no one knows and that includes Joshua but Juri has been called “The Mother of the OSC” even at her young age of 20! “Juri?” Joshua asked gently stroking the orange haired woman back calming any sadness in her body at the time. “Yes Joshua what is it?” Juri responded still with her head leaning on his shoulder and her eyes shut “What’s wrong Juri” Did something happen at home or is something else the manner?” Joshua asked and of course he knew he was going to get a straight answer from the woman whom is the girlfriend of the woman that he first managed. “Oh Joshua sometimes I wonder why I am the way that I am. Why is it that I like women more than men?” Juri asked Joshua even though she knew that her good and close friend wouldn’t have a direct answer for her. “Juri I think it might have had something to do with your encounters with the male gender. I remember that there were times that you like Touga to the point where you LOVED him but we both know that the end result was that he used your emotions to his advantage and played you like a fine-tuned fiddle. It happens and the fact that its wrong is really nothing new but what is that more and more people get away with abusing relationships to the point where they end up on “CHEATERS” more than you hear about the good things they do. In short the only person that can really truly define why you are the way you are is you!” Joshua told Juri and she actually seemed somewhat comforted by what Joshua said’odd I know but fact remains that whenever Joshua needs someone to talk to Juri is always there for him and vice versa. After the talk Juri went out of the shower and to the kitchen to make breakfast for the two and honestly this is almost like old times for Joshua. It reminded him of when he visited Ohtori Academy in Hou’ou-shi, Holland and he had dinner with the 1st officer of the Student Council in Juri Arisugawa? September 24th, 1993 I know what you are thinking Wasn’t Joshua wrestling by now? and the answer is “Yes but at the same time he had some time off and visited a school that he had received an invitation for a dinner to. The person whom signed the invite was named Juri Arisugawa and don’t think for a second that Joshua hadn’t heard of her because he had. Juri was a champion fencer and duelist in the Academy as well as the World! Is short if ya want your butt kicked go face her in a fencing competition! “Ohtori Academy” Yep this is the place!” Joshua said to himself as he walked through the gates of the school and to say that the student populace wasn’t shocked to see an outsider pay a visit would be kind of an understatement! They were quite shocked to see someone from the outside pay them a visit but at the same time they were happy because it meant someone got an invite! “Welcome to Ohtori Academy sir. My name is Emma Christie and it’s a pleasure to have a visitor at our esteemed institute.” The bouncy 13 year old said as she greeted Joshua at the entrance. “Good morning Ms. Christie?” Joshua started before being cut off for a moment by the student “Just Emma will do and if memory serves me you name is Joshua right” Sorry about the formalities but I was told to act as professionally as possible by members of the Student Council SHOULD you have come to the Academy.” Emma explained and Joshua raised an eyebrow by how the student was acting. “Emma would you be so kind as to take me to Ms. Arisugawa?” Joshua asked and the brunette did as Joshua asked and as she led him through the courtyard there were a lot of glances from the female and even some of the male population coming his way and I don’t know whether to tell you it was cos of his cool demeanor or the way he was dressed at that point in time, but the populace took one good look at Joshua and one thought ran through their heads at that time: God I wish I was him! A lot of people did and that is what made him the envy of the Academy even though the fact remained that he didn’t even go to that school! As the duo walked up to the dojo a young man whom was waiting by the entrance waved them through and it was there that Joshua was mesmerized by the orange-haired young lady that was standing about 45 feet away picking off opponents within a span of about 30 minutes! “Juri Arisugawa” Emma began “this is Joshua Curtis, the young man that I believe you sent an invite to about coming here?” Emma asked the 1st officer of the Student Council and Juri nodded before focusing her attention on Joshua. “Mr. Curtis it is indeed an honor to have you here at Ohtori for the day and while the rest of the council isn’t here allow me to formally welcome you to the Academy.” Juri finished and Joshua was kind of blushing from ear to ear because of how formal the woman was being! “Well its my pleasure Ms. Arisugawa. I was on some off time with my wrestling business so I figured that now would be as good a time as any to stop in and see the somewhat fabled Ohtori Academy because around my parts I heard stories of this place but people say it doesn’t exist.” Joshua explained to Juri and to say that Juri took a notice to Joshua’s cool demeanor and his outfit, which consisted of a white polo shirt, black dress pants with a matching belt and a matching pair of loafers by the way, it was well worth noting that Juri was taking a liking to the young man from, at the time London, England. Juri would end up taking Joshua to the locker room where the two ended up talking for a little while in private and Juri told him a little bit about herself with such fine details as her background, her family life and other details and everything seemed fine. When the subject of her past love life came up however she broke down and cried on Joshua’s shoulder for 20 solid minutes and Joshua knew that he had hit a nerve that he didn’t mean to hit. After Juri got done crying and composed herself the two talked for a little while about the incident she had with her past lover and then went as far as extending an invitation to join her at dinner, which he gladly accepted! That night at dinner Joshua was dressed to impress as was Juri as Joshua was in a black suit jacket, white shirt and black tie, another pair of black pants and the same matching loafers as earlier while Juri was had on a pair of diamond earrings, a ruby red dress that hugged her body like a glove and a pair of red high heels to finish the outfit. As the two walked out to the dining room there were a lot of noticeable glances coming their way and maybe for good reason because it was no secret that they were holding hands as they entered the room. “I think that we are the envy of the room.” Juri whispered to Joshua and Joshua couldn’t help but let out a little chuckle as his good friend was right. “Good evening Mr. Curtis. Juri has told us so much about you and honestly I can’t say that she has good taste in friends’oh listen to me I am being impolite aren’t I” My name is Touga Kiryuu and I am the head of the Student Council here at Ohtori. Please take your seat next to Miki so we can begin.” Touga told Joshua and as he did so Juri was right behind him so Joshua decided to seat Juri first and this did not get unnoticed by anyone as people were saying how much of a darling Joshua was being towards his new friend. As dinner progressed Joshua’s behavior was noted by all that were present and the fact that Joshua didn’t single any of the people at the table was the most noteworthy action of all of them and thus pretty much ended the evening for everyone there but one thing was for sure and that was that the bond between Juri and Joshua would never be broken! Back to the Present day? Juri had just finished making the breakfast for the two when the doorbell rang and on the other end was in fact Tina Davis in her usual getup and that always made Juri smile. “Hey hun how’s things here?” Tina asked as she walked into the room and took her jacket off and handed it to her long time lover. As she sat down at the table Joshua had just rounded the corner and by the look on his face was pleasantly surprised! “TINA! How ya been hun?” Joshua asked as he saw the “disciplinarian” of what used to be called the “Old School Connection”. “Same shit and different day darling and yourself?” Tina asked Joshua as he sat down at the table “Oh same old, same old. Zack is away and I made a new friend named Ken Partridge Jr. in this fed called Global Wrestling, which I think I needed sorely. The bookers at Global have put me in a contender’s match against this cat named El Serpenti and it will be a match to determine the number one contender to the Cruiserweight Title, which I think might be a positive step for the Connection as Zack is still awaiting his first match within the rings of Global.” Joshua explained to the two and Tina nodded as the food was placed at the table, which is where we leave you for tonight.
