Not The Girl She Used To Be

24 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.24.05 Not The Girl She Used To Be During large PPV's, the Hunting Grounds locker room often has visitors of many sizes and varying status. Tonight's visitor is a rarity however, perhaps the last person you would ever expect to see knocking on the HG door. To be honest...it seems as though Ken Partridge Junior isn’t entirely sure himself of exactly what he's doing here. With a resigned sigh he raises one hand and raps on the door. After a pause the door is flung open to reveal Dawn McFarlane, her hair still dripping from a shower. She glares, emerald eyes narrowing. Dawn: What do YOU want? KPJ: I came to see if you were alright. You took quite a spill last week, and I just wanted to make sure you were recovering alright. Dawn scowls, leaning up against the door frame and folding her arms under her breasts. Dawn: I'm fine, but you should have pinned me when you had the chance. KPJ shrugs. KPJ: That's not the way I operate. You were so hurt you couldn't even stand, I wasn't about to make that any worse. I figure you should know that about me by now. Dawn: I'm not going to give you another chance you know. Tonight you're going down, no excuses. Ken rolls his eyes taking a step back. KPJ: I've heard that before. We'll see what happens at the end of the night. He turns away as if to leave, but pauses for a moment. As Dawn turns to shut the door he speaks, startling her. KPJ: What happened to the girl I wrestled two years ago at Crossfire Dawn? He turns back to face her, locking his eyes on her suddenly wide ones. KPJ: When I first met you you were full of life, totally free. I watched you put your CAREER on the line for your fed mates goddamit...now you can't even find a kind word for someone who didn't take advantage of you when you were down. Dawn: I...I don't know what you're talking about Ken shakes his head, reaching forward and taking her chin between his thumb and forefinger. He raises her eyes to meet his again. KPJ: You're not as good at fooling me as you think you are. I know you're hurting...I don't know why, but I know that someone hurt you and you're still smarting over it. I'm not going to tell you it's all going to be alright or anything like that...but I will tell you that when you're looking for someone you can count on...you don't have to look any further then me. He releases her chin, turning away and heading off down the corridor. KPJ: Think about it. Dawn stares after him for a long moment, her green eyes wide as saucers. writingcredit dawnmcfarlane
