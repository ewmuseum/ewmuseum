Down Time

20 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 20th, 2003 [Apologies for the lateness -- I got the date of the card mixed up with the deadline. Ah well. I'll post this whether it ends up being counted or not.] 'Tell me, and be honest,' said Akodo Rokku. 'Were your toes always this cute?' Kakita Yoshiko propped herself up on her elbows, the white sheet that was draped over their bodies shifting with her movement. She looked down to the foot of the bed, where her eyes found Rokku's smiling face. 'Yep,' she said, 'as long as I've been able to remember.' 'Hrm,' said Rokku, furrowing his brow comically, 'I wonder why I never noticed before.' 'Probably because whenever you saw them, my foot was aimed at your head,' replied Yoshiko. Rokku looked up at her and rose a questioning eyebrow. She replied with a smile, her eyes twinkling. 'Well then,' said the Steel Lion, donning a mischievous grin, 'I'd better take this opportunity to examine them more closely.' 'Rokku, don't you dare even think about--' But it was too late. Rokku ran a finger up the arch of Yoshiko's delicate foot, and she squeaked. 'Aha,' he said, 'it looks like I've found the secret weak spot of the ever-collected Silken Crane!' 'Rokku,' said Yoshiko, trying to look stern and stifle a giggle at the same time, 'I'm serious. I'm really ticklish there, so don't--' Yoshiko cut her sentence short and let loose a peal of laughter as Rokku mercilessly tickled her feet. Rokku grinned widely as Yoshiko squirmed and squealed, trying to get her feet away from him, but unable to stop laughing long enough to do so. Rokku's assault seemed unstoppable -- but suddenly he paused. As Rokku looked away from Yoshiko's feet, his grin melted away into an expression of fear. From the other end of the bed, Yoshiko smiled and waved demurely, one of her hands wrapped around his ankle. 'Oh, you wouldn't,' said Rokku, trying to put on his resolve face. 'Oh,' replied Yoshiko, 'I really, really would.' The two of them struck at once, tickling each other with reckless abandon, and their laughter rang throughout the entire house. They battled each other this way, hands darting everywhere, trying to find weak points. They struggled and laughed, flipping and flailing all over the bed, neither one gaining the advantage over the other -- they obviously knew each other far too well. Finally, they came eye to eye. Rokku was tickling Yoshiko's sides as she attacked his underarms. He could feel her laughter on his face, see the glee in her sparkling eyes. Rokku couldn't resist, and suddenly his lips met hers. His fingers stopped dancing along her sides and slid down to her hips. Her hands slipped around to the back of his head, her fingers tangling in his hair. And they kissed. Deeply, passionately, fully, like it was all they needed to live. Finally, Yoshiko broke away. 'That's cheating,' said the Silken Crane, with a bit of a pout. 'Oh,' asked Rokku, with a grin and a twinkle in his eye, 'what're you gonna do about it?' Yoshiko's pout turned into a devious smirk, and one of her hands snaked under the covers. Rokku's eyes widened. 'Hey,' said the Steel Lion, 'now that is chea--' Yoshiko cut off Rokku's protest with another kiss. What the hell, he thought. If this was cheating, he really didn't want to follow the rules anyway. *** Hours later, blissfully sweaty and exhausted, they rested. 'Why didn't we do this before?' asked Yoshiko lifting her head off of Rokku's bare chest and looking into his eyes. Rokku raised an eyebrow. 'We've done this quite a lot over the last week, 'Shiko-chan.' Yoshiko swatted his chest. 'Not this,' she said, 'I mean, us. Why didn't we ever do the us thing before? We've known each other four, almost five years now. What took us so long?' 'Hrm.' Rokku furrowed his brow in thought. 'Lots of little things, I guess. I was so busy trying to fight my way to the top of the business--' 'And sleep with everything in a skirt on your way there,' Yoshiko interjected. '--and you,' Rokku continued, 'were so focused, so driven, and so distant, that for the longest time I thought you were just untouchable.' 'I guess I tried to be,' said Yoshiko. 'My father made me work so hard my whole life to be the best at everything, it didn't seem like other people could have a place in my world. For the longest time, I was afraid that if I took time to get to know someone, I'd get weak somehow. You, and the other Thunders -- you were some of the first friends I ever really had.' 'To be honest,' said Rokku, 'even if you hadn't been distant, it wouldn't have worked between us when we first met.' 'Why's that?' asked Yoshiko. 'I was just a kid back then,' said Rokku. 'For the last few years even, I still was. I wasn't ready for a real relationship -- and I honestly didn't want one. I was having far too much fun living from bed to bed.' 'Hmm,' said Yoshiko, snuggling closer to him. 'So what made you change your mind? What made you grow up?' Rokku smiled, and kissed her on the forehead. 'You did,' he said. Yoshiko looked up at him and smiled. 'So,' she said, sliding on top of him suddenly, 'I've got my second wind. You?' Rokku laughed. 'I think I could go a few more rounds, yeah.' 'That's my boy,' said Yoshiko, leaning down and kissing him again. That's when the doorbell rang. 'Aw, nuts,' muttered Rokku, as Yoshiko kissed his neck. 'They'll go away,' said Yoshiko, sliding further down the bed and kissing Rokku's chest. Again the doorbell rang, a long dreadful buzz shattering the comfy dream world of the bedroom, and dragging Rokku back into reality. With a sigh, he sat up. Yoshiko pouted, but rolled off of Rokku, letting him up. Again the doorbell rang, and Rokku climbed out of bed, slipping into a black silk kimono and tying the obi firmly around his waist and walking towards the bedroom door. 'Ahem,' said Yoshiko. Rokku stopped in his tracks and turned back to look at her. She pointed down, guiding his gaze. 'Might wanna reign that in,' she said with a smirk. 'Right,' said Rokku, snatching a matching pair of boxers from a drawer and pulling them on. 'Clothes,' Rokku said, as he stepped out of the bedroom and walked down the hall towards the living room, 'what a concept.' The doorbell rang again just as Rokku reached the door. He quickly undid the locks and swung the front door open. On the other side was a smiling gray-haired man in glasses and a dark gray suit. As the door opened, he immediately took his finger off the doorbell button, and extended his hand. 'Hi,' he said, 'I'm James Bowen, an attorney with the law firm of Jenkens and Gilchrist. Are you Akodo Rokku?' 'That I am,' said Rokku, shaking the man's hand firmly. 'What can I do for you, Mister Bowen?' 'Well,' said the lawyer, 'the firm sent me over here to talk to you. It's about your father?' Rokku blanched. 'Come in Mister Bowen,' said Rokku, stepping away from the door. 'Please, come in.'
