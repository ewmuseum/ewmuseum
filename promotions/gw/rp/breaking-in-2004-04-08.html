Breaking In

8 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 8 The scene opens up to the red doors opening of the school. Out walks me and my mom, ready to go home after a long day spending with those children. We walked to our car as I got into the drivers seat. My mom got into the passengers seat and put her seat belt on and so did I. I drove out of the school parking lot and down the street back to our house. I stopped at a few red lights and the finally pulled up to our driveway. It was almost 5:00 pm. We got out of the car and walked into the front door of our house. “Uh oh.” I said. ?What?? my mother asked. “We forgot to lock the door.” I told her. “That’s ok. No one would break into our house.” she said. But she was wrong. We walked into the house and heard a whole bunch of noises coming from the upstairs of our house. We heard a vase break, and then a whole bunch of books fall. We ran upstairs to see what was going on. We got into my moms room and saw a man with a full black body suit on. He had a brown bag and was taking all of our stuff. I ran at the guy and tackled him. “Go phone the cops, mom.” I said. “Ok.” she said. I held the man on the ground until my mom walked back into the room. She was holding the cordless phone. “They are coming.” she said. “Go get some rope so we can tie this guy up before the cops come.” I said. My mom ran out of the room and I heard her feet hit the stairs as she went downstairs. I held the guys arms and legs and waited patiently till my mom got back with the rope so we could tie this guy up. My mom ran back into the room with a big thing of rope. I took it and tied the guy up. I got off the guy and left him lying there, as we waited for the cops to come. A few minutes later I heard the door open to our house. The cops ran up stairs to see what was going on. ?What is going on?? they asked. “We came home and found this guy taking everything. I got some rope and tied him up though until you guys got here.” I said. “Lock him up boys.” the cop said to the other cops. “Thank you for coming.” my mother said. The cops took us outside and asked us a few questions as the scene started to fade to darkness.
