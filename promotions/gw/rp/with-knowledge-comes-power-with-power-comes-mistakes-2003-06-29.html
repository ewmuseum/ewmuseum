With knowledge comes power, with power comes mistakes

29 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/29.03 “I'm a one man power house succeeding within the scene Son of the sire livin' the love livin' the dream Wiper snapers get jealous it's true they don't know? (As the scene fades in we see Sean Price sitting in the living room of his Atlanta mansion. He is sitting in front of the mirror, looking directly into it as if he is expecting the mirror to talk to him. His eye are wide opened and filled with emotion, his heart is racing, the blood pumping through his veins like a freight train on the tracks. Then without warning Sean quickly gets up and storms out of the room. He makes a quick dissention down his victorian style staircase, and than out the front door. After doing so he stands at the top of his front steps and looks out at the view of the city of Atlanta. The view is amazing, an awesome sky line view that only the rich can afford to see each and every morning. With that Sean walks about halfway down the steps and than just sits down and looks out again, gazing as if there is a purpose to be met by just sitting and looking out.) “Each and every day we go to sleep smarter than the day before. Each and every day we learn something new. Some people thought that it was just an old clich”, however I have found there to be truth in the saying “you learn something new everyday.” Sean begins to crack a smile across his face and than lets out a little bit of laughter. “Just yesterday I learned that the term “franchise player” in professional basketball means that when you are designated with that tag, you get to approve or disapprove of all trades effecting your team. That is a pretty powerful undertaking if you ask me. But it was until yesterday something I never knew.” “A couple of weeks ago I learned that no matter what it is I do my brother Luke will never understand that what I did was simply part of business and not anything personal. And I guess I am ok with that. Do I have a choice in the matter?” “Not really.” “But you see, its not just learning something new that makes you smarter, its what you do with that knowledge. Some people apply it to every day life, while others do nothing with it.” “Then there are those who get jealous of the knowledge that I posses. People like Salaok, they get jealous and when they get jealous they get scared and with fear comes hostility. You see Salaok is afraid of what I know, afraid of what I am capable of, afraid of the TUE and consequently’afraid of me.” “And because of this fear Salaok finds no other way of dealing with these problems than by trying to push his power off on people. Maybe that worked against some people. But not with me, not with us.” (Sean gets up from his seat atop the steps, he slowly walks down them and towards his Suzuki motorcycle. He grabs a new white rag and starts whipping down the sides of the motorcycle in an attempt to keep it clean.) “You see Salaok is either unaware or ignorant to the fact that the TUE is not like any other force he has ever faced in his tenure here at the GW. In the past he had pathetic stables like the Young bloods to deal with. But we are no Young bloods. We are the TUE. A elite group all to our own.” “Just think about it, how many stables can claim that they have won their titles in their first three weeks of existence. How many stables can say that they had their own personal lawyer” None that I am aware of, we are not just a band of rejects and people with criminal records. We are the best in the business. Separate we are the best, together we are that much better. No one can stop us from what we will ultimately accomplish. Not the Nevermore and certainly not their leader Salaok.” “Now tomorrow night is when all of the TUE knowledge to this point will culminate in a pay per view in which TUE’s members sweep their matches. Deacon will walk out still Platinum Champion. John Zero will win his match, and Ty and I will become the GW’s tag team champions. A reign that will make all others look like a microsecond in the timeline of the GW.” “Even though Tyler and I won our first match as a tag team against former tag champs no less, there are still those out there who doubt us, doubt what we are capable of. Tomorrow night we will make the doubters believers. There is no doubt about it, we will be the tag team champs. And when that happens everyone out there who said it can’t be done can then admit that it was done. Tyler and I are the real deal.” “Tomorrow does not mark the end of TUE’s scheme as the GW powerhouse, no, it marks the beginning. Because from tomorrow night will stem events that will change the face of Global Wrestling forever, again you can doubt it, but in the end it is all of you out there that will be the ones to pay later.” (As the scene fades out we see Sean walk to the side of his house where he pulls out a remote from his pocket and presses one of the buttons. With that all 5 garage doors open up and he stands there looking at each car for a moment before walking into the garage holding his black hummer. He walks to the side of the Hummer, opens the door and then climbs in. He turns the car on and then starts blasting Linkin Park. He pulls out of the garage and goes about 200 yards before he turns down the music and begins speaking again.) “You can either cheer us or you can fear us, but no matter what, you won’t be us. And that, is what scares you all.” (Sean turns the music back up and starts to smile as he drives off to the airport to catch his plane to Whiplash.)
