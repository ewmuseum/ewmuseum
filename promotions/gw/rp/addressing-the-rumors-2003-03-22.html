Addressing the rumors

22 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Addressing the rumors Brick Global Wrestling Promo #12 Date: 3/22/03 Location: Brick's apartment Brick sits at his computer, searching through the Primetime Central website. He clicks on a link for a recap to the recent edition of Evolution. He scrolls down to the following. “The first match of the night was probably one of the greatest showings of the night as four men entered the ring, but only one man would end up standing as Brick, with chair in hand simply seemed to lose control and flatten every man and his horse. But that would not be the only thing that Brick did on the show as he turned up backstage after his match and attacked David West, who was in a match against GW's Faith Slayer. A woman. Brick ended up saving the day for the young woman and carried her off away from the cameras. What was going on between those two, no one is fully sure but the rumors are already starting to flow.” “Man, this is some bullshit,” Brick thinks to himself. Ever since that night, Brick had started to deal with the rumors about his connection with Faith Slayer. Naturally, they’re of the romantic kind. Brick had gotten sick of hearing them. Suddenly… RRIIIIINNNNG! RRIIIIINNNNG! “Good thing I got DSL.” Brick picks up the phone. “Yeah, hello?” “Brick?” the voice on the other side asked. “Who’s this?” “It’s Brandi Sights.” “Oh, hey, Brandi. What’s up?” “Oh, I just wanted to get a few comments from you on the whole Faith Slayer situation.” Brick sucks his teeth. “Aw, not this shit again.” “Well, people are curious as to why you came to her aid last week on Evolution. Plus, with all the rumors being circulated, I figured you might want to address.” Brick inhales deeply and sighs. “You know what, Brandi? I am gonna address the rumors. Every time I help one of the women associated with this company, it’s always assumed that I’m either hooked up with one of them, or I’m trying to hook up with one of them. They said the same thing when I saved Annea Dafoe from getting powerbombed by Terminator, and when I stepped in between Gunner Addison and Jesse Briggs.” “But Brick, didn’t you and Gunner have something going a while back?” “No, we didn’t. We were just close friends.” “Like you and Aaron?” “Yes, like me and Aaron.” “Oh, so Aaron’s kissed you for good luck before?” Brandi asks. “Every day before a pay-per-view.” “But, seriously…” “Look, Brandi, I’ma tell you why I helped Faith Slayer out. The same reason I helped out Annea and Gunner. I did it because it was the right thing to do at the time.” “Because it was the right thing to do.” “Is there an echo? Yes, because it was the right thing to do. Now do you have anything else to ask me? Because I’ve got enough on my mind with this bullshit match Salaok booked me in.” “No, I don’t, Brick. Sorry to waste your time. See you in Texas.” Brick hangs up the phone and resumes net surfing. “Maybe I should switch back to a dial-up connection.”
