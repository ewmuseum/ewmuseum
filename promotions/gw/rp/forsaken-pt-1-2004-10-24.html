Forsaken (Pt.1)

24 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-24-04 Forsaken (Pt.1) Prologue. Theirs a point in every man's life when the world goes dull, and the sound of pain is so loud it deafens his mind. The overall effect leaves the man crippled by his bed when he wakes up in his own vomit, realizing his pitiful life. Those are the moments a man must face, without choice or reason, he just must. Worst than what many believe to be a painful death, worst than the scum of life that we see walking on streets, and worst than the reasons our president gives for killing our troops. It's a day, we love to hate, because nothing goes as planned… Story. 'AHHHHHHHHHH!' Screams the man as a cross-face is locked on and his head and arm are cocked to their maximum flexibility. Holding his hand in a cocked position as if ready to tap, his fist clenches and his arm falls to the canvas, but instead of tapping, he just slams his fist down and brings his knees forward on the canvas. Turning his side slightly, he rolls out of the cross-face, and halfway through he stops, bridging his opponent for a two count. Slamming his fist on the ground he rolls over and plants his knee on the mat, pushing his other leg up and waiting for his opponent who does the same thing. The eyes of each man meets, a tense moment rises within them, clenched fists, beating hearts, they grapple, SLAM! Take down, flip over, half nelson, body scissors, locks in the arm, tap out! The men rise to their feet after the intense match up. The one on the right, a short man with long black hair tied back in a ponytail, light green eyes, and a long face. He looks at the other man, tall, brown hair, and as the man turns, he smiles. “Nice session Jess. I knew you had some of that old school training in ya, just needed to bring it out,” laughed the short man as he slipped through the ropes and stood on the apron. “I’m going to go get us something to eat. What’ ya want?” Looking around the ring as he began to bounce off the rope, he muttered, “Turkey sandwich is fine.” Dropping to his knee and performing an air-take down, he brushes the floor quickly and went from right to left seeking his best possible scenario for the escape shall he be caught by this imaginary wrestler he’s facing. It’s hard to predict one person’s moves, let alone eight. I know what this graveyard match means… It’s my chance at redeeming myself, but not only that, my chance at some notice. I’m sure everyone is thinking the same thing. ‘Oh if I win this I’ll be lined up for a title shot.’ Well I don’t want that, I just want to prove why I am who I am, and what makes me that. Rolling through a take down with his shoulder he grabs the rope spins and jumps leapfrogging up six feet in the air. A moment of silence as he perches the air looking like an angel of hell, and then landing on his feet in a crouching position ready to pounce on whomever comes near. I’ve fought in Japan, Europe, the United States, Canada, Mexico, and even Brazil… Yet nothing will prepare me for this graveyard match. It’s a group of selected elites, the skills of the world all combined in area… Having to go through this match, I’m going to need the endurance of a champion, the speed of a panther, and the instincts of all hunters. I must wait for the chance, and not try to seize myself in the glory bound victory… Or I too, will become another victim of a silent greedy defeat. A split second goes by before he spins kicks the air and seconds a ‘swoosh’ sound echoing off the walls. Grasping the ropes he presses his feet against the middle rope and lion saults backwards onto his feet. He smiles at the performance he has given, and drops to his knees. Falling onto his back, he looks up at the ceiling. It’s my time to get something I’ve waited to long for. A chance at what I’ve given up too many times… I think they know it’s coming, but they won’t know it for sure until I take it. The voice of the old man is heard from up stares, “Foods ready!” Rolling out of the ring, Jesse removes his elbow pads and wrist tape before walking up the steps into the kitchen to eat. What’s Eaten You Staring at the wall his eyes glaze over. Unnatural silver hints his lost ways through dreams. A goatee of forest like nature, and long winding hair that seems to never end. All of this shades of a man with no notion to fight, yet within contains a reason to why, he does. SNAP! The fingers portray the awkward yet simple motion. Their attempt of awakening the dream beast is fulfilled, and there, he stares at his awaked. “What?” calmly states Jesse as he opens his eyes steadily staring at the man in front of him. “Whats eaten you?” says the man, somewhat resembling Jesse, but with dark black hair, and green bitter eyes. A sigh releases his stresses and problems before a false insecurity answers his mind, “nothing, just bored.” “Jess, you’re a fucking professional wrestler, who has all he ever wanted. What’s wrong man? Come on, you know you can talk to me. We’re like brothers! “ mutters the man trying harder than normal to get an answer. Shaking his head again with the ‘I don’t know’ answer, Jesse pursues getting up and walks from the table to the fridge. “I’ve just been stuck on these dreams I’ve been having,” he begins, “It’s like I see another life within me. Like how your told you are reborn from people in the past, but your memory is wiped. Well, my dreams feel like memories… Strong memories.” The man sitting down stares at the table, at a heart with J & J in the middle is carved near the corner. Realizing what it is he looks up. “You miss her don’t you?” he shoots out without thinking. A silence overcomes the room. Too thick to cut, but thin enough to realize the depth of the question. No answer is given, only a predetermined action of Jesse picking up where he left off. “See… It’s this weird dream that I had like a week ago. All these lights, with green towels or something green moving in front of me. Then I saw a wire connecting a wall and me together. It was like I was stuck to a chain or something, because every time I pulled to get it off, I was pushed back down. They both look up from where their previously gazing and look one another in the eyes. “Sounds stupid huh?” says Jesse almost laughing at it himself. The man sitting down laughs, “Just a bit. Maybe it’s just a dream of you seeing ghosts or something, or maybe you remember a time you were knocked out on the mat and were looking up at the lights too long. Who knows?” They smile and laugh, but almost immediately Jesse shows a sign of fraud. He shakes it off quickly to no longer make it aware, and sits back down just staring at the man before leaning back and falling into his gaze again. Secrets Awoken “Hold him down!” Yells a voice as three men hold down the jackal on a table. His eyes are already closed, but his body is reacting as if he’s going to be slayed. “CONTROL HIM!” The man yells again, but the force of him is inhuman. Grabbing the man by his jugular he rips it out without hesitation, and leaves the man descending to his demise. A choke and then a splatter, was all that was heard. Blood drips from the mans fingers as he drops a chunk of skin. He grabs the next man who’s in his way and twists his next breaking it instantly. “DON’T HURT HIM! HE’S VALUABLE!” The voice fades as it runs the other way, but the men he yells it too do only what is natural, and that is fight to survive. Throwing whatever they can find, from metal syringes to pans, they may as well throw the sink, for their luck has just ran out. The man lifts them off their feet by their necks and tosses them through the windows into another room. Glass shatters like a bullet speeding through the barrel, a slight whistle before a burst. The men whom are thrown through both lie unconscious. The beasts eyes flicker and finally he comes to sense. Falling to his knees he glimpses at what is going on. “What… is… this?” The voice so known, so faint, yet strong in presents, the voice of Jesse Jamester. Looking up at his wrath, he witnesses first hand hell’s fury. His focus adjusts from one object to the next in a matter of seconds, before finally he notices his bloody hands. “GET HIM!” Just as those words are stated he turns to see the piercing of a dart in his chest. Grabbing it with his left hand, he pulls it out and then falls over lying on a bloody tile floor. “Get security up here now… We need to get him contained.” Are the last words spoken before a blackness is all that is seen from the behemoth in which is known as only, the Jackal.
