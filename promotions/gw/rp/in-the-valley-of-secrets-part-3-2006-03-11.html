In the Valley of Secrets (Part 3)

11 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 11th, 2006 December 26th, 2005 Ontario, Canada Holiday cheer was in the air even after Christmas was technically over; a fully decorated tree stood in the living room with ornaments of different shapes and colors. Some had names on them and others were simple themed designs. Silver, red, and green garland concentrated around the tree but that was not the focus at the moment. Eryc gazed out upon the frozen lake through the windows of the rustic log cabin he was spending the holidays in with Krissy. It was nothing like he imagined it would be the moment she asked him to come meet her parents and while it was awkward at first, they accepted one another and have managed to get along like he’s been dating their daughter for a long time. Her father Paul is a surgeon at the local hospital and has been for many years. (Somewhere around 20 or more would be a good deduction.) He came over from Europe to Canada and that is where he went to further his education and met Krissy’s Mom, Diana. Diana was studying to be a nurse and they just happened to meet one another through the school where they were taking classes; it was in the school library to be more precise. They started dating and fell in love and as their schooling ended, they were married and both took jobs at Mt. Sinai Hospital so they could see one another if they didn’t have the opportunity to do so at home. They were very gracious people and didn’t pester Eryc too much with questions about what sort of job he had or what kind of money he made. It could have already been because Krissy told them but he didn’t suspect it because they didn’t treat him strange like most would in the presence of famous people. Krissy snuck up and put her arms around his waist and leaned her head against his back, taking in the smell of his cologne which made her feel weak in the knees sometimes. It must have been because he had it made personally for his body chemistry and nobody else in the world would get the change to smell that way with regular on the shelf varieties. “What’s on your mind?” She exhaled lightly taking him in again and reached to lace her fingers between his. His eyes were still focused on what he saw outside the window: the lake...frozen and beautiful with a light cover of the snowfall from the previous night. Krissy had bought him a very nice pair of ice skates for Christmas along with a few movies he had mentioned he wanted for his collection but never bought for himself. She made him not get her anything special and just wanted a few cd’s and movies and he complied with her wishes. “I was thinking about putting on my skates and testing my form...I want to see if the old man still has it.” He followed the statement with a small laugh. “You never know when I might get an offer to try out for an NHL team.” Of course he was only kidding about that last part. Krissy quickly spun him around and looked him in the eyes. “You are not old!” She lightly scolded, “But if you want to...go ahead and go out there to see if you are still good at it.” Eryc smiled at the thought of not being “old” for once and it was a feeling he didn’t want to let go of for as long as he could help it and as many things about his old life went away more of the new ones that replaced them were comforting in ways he never expected. “Did you want to come with?” Of course he could have gone by himself which would have been a personal preference before he met her but he wanted to include her in what he could but not smother himself with being with her 24/7. “The ice looks to be just right.” She didn’t want to say no but she had already decided on going out for a bit, which she knew he’d understand since she wouldn’t be gone for too long. Krissy was one of those girls who understood that men needed some time to themselves and she figured that Eryc could enjoy himself without interference from her. “That’s alright baby, I’m going to go do a little shopping with Mom but you can go if you want.” Krissy’s words were encouraging for to him try to enjoying himself; it was the reason they went on this vacation in the first place. “When I get back we can make some hot cocoa because it is cold out there and you’ll want to warm up after coming inside.” Eryc wouldn’t press the issue. He spent plenty of time with her and he knew how she liked spending time with her Mom. It sort of made him jealous in a sense because he never really knew his parents very well in the way that most children do today. There was not much to do back in his time to really form bonds. “Krissy love, you go and have a good time with your Mother...I’m going outside to lace up.” He leaned down and kissed her full on the lips before they parted to go in their own ways. “I’ll see you when you get back.” ---- Eryc glided on the pool of frozen perfection as if he could have been in the Olympics but those types of things never seemed to be real for him; he always thought about all the opportunities he would have loved to have had if he would have earned back his mortality sooner. Could have played hockey before but it wouldn’t have been fair and Eryc always had a good sense of fair play. But what an amusing thought it was: a vampire playing professional sports. His feet moved in front of one another repeatedly as he began to show off a little more by making designs in the arctic crystal. He thought it was too sissy to make a figure eight or something like that but instead made a rough outline of a ring. The irony of the matter was that his thoughts had shifted towards Krissy and how much he cared about her. Eryc could drive that (fact) home as much as he needed to but unless he intended to do something about it he shouldn’t dwell on it nearly as much. It brought him to a crossroads that he never believed would be an actuality. Was he ready for marriage; that next step that would meld their bond closer together? Part of him was but there was always the underlying fear of one day becoming a monster again and doing something that he’d not be able to control not to mention the fact that he’d never be able to be with her in the afterlife and this was one girl he didn’t want to let go of. What he really needed was to wash himself of all of those troubles and maybe even go to a church and make a confession. A priest was sworn to his oath and Eryc doubted that even if the kindred started following him (which they might already have), they wouldn’t bother with it and besides would a man of god even believe him lest he was insane? Eryc exhaled and watched as the molecules from his breath floated upwards; he wasn’t paying attention much to anything as he was deep in thought about asking Krissy to be his wife. He had to...there was no other way he’d ever find out what true happiness was like unless he made a sacrifice. Just when things started to swing in his favor, his skate caught on a large branch or stick that had somehow been blown onto the lake from someplace. Eryc tried to maintain his balance but he lost control... His body smacked hard against the ice...so hard that he cracked it and plunged into the freezing temperatures of the lake. He knew how to swim but wasn’t able to grab hold of the edge of the hollow long enough to bring himself back out; the current started to sweep him further away from the The chill of the water began to creep into his body even with all the heavy layers he wore in preparation for the conditions outside. He knew that if he tried to struggle that it would only cut his oxygen supply faster and he would certainly drown. Eryc did what he could to try and kick at the ice so maybe he could make another opening in hopes that he’d at least be able to crawl out and have someone find him and do what was necessary to get him warmed up. A few dents were made but nothing big enough to stick his head through to catch a breath of air. It wouldn’t be long now before he was completely out of oxygen and the strain on his insides felt like repeated sledgehammer blows to the midsection. His thoughts raced around like stock cars at the Daytona 500 and everything about his life (recently) began to flash before his eyes...every single feeling and sensation went through him. The temperature of the water was much colder than the feel of his skin would be if he were still kindred. He never thought he’d meet his end in a not so similar but tragic way, like in the movie Titanic; the only difference was that he had nobody with him in his final moments...without anyone to say: I’ll never let go. The light from the entry point began to fade as he continued to drift him away slowly; his heartbeat began to slack and he knew this would be his last moments and sadly they wouldn’t be with Krissy. His eyes flickered and he began to fade... ---- The smell of kerosene and wood crackling flooded Eryc’s senses as he slowly came to. This was not the watery grave he remembered being last...instead he was in the old abandoned ruins of the church he remembered visiting his friend Deacon in (who was in a sleep like stasis called torpor). He looked around the darkened stone room but only saw his shadow and that of the sconces that were fixed to the wall. All that he could hear was his heart beat and that of his own thoughts. “What am I doing here,” he thought to himself as he took note that something was amiss. He was clothed in a style that fit the century of which he was born in: an under and over tunic and brown breeches. His feet were bare and they felt the cold smooth stone of the floor as he stood up to investigate. Eryc’s eyes had trouble adjusting to the dark (yet another terrible consequence to regaining mortality). He sharply turned around as he heard a noise behind him but saw nothing and dismissed as being a rat or even his imagination kicking into overdrive. He turns around again to grab one of the torches to help better illuminate his pathway and as he pulls it off of its bracket, he sees a shadowy figure in a cloak standing a mere twelve feet from him. “Deacon” he questioned the figure, “is that you?” The hooded phantom like entity slowly began to approach him and it would be safe to assume that he was nervous but could do nothing as he seemed to be frozen in his space; not able to move an inch while the being closed in. ?SHOW YOURSELF!? Eryc roared with a commanding voice and followed with, “I’m not in the mood for games; I want to know why I’m here.” A pale hand emerged from between the folds of the sleeves of the cloak to slowly move towards the hood. Eryc noticed the hand and some of the rings that were worn on the fingers and he then knew it was not Deacon Black at all but instead... “Why don’t you ask yourself this question,” the hood was removed to reveal the image he had seen in his latest nightmares...the one named Corvus, his former self. “I think there’s a reason why all these thoughts of me have surfaced.” He grinned with a hint of mischief. Eryc shook his head and refused to even look at the face or even into the eyes of evil at its purest form: the darkest part of his very own coil. “I must be in hell if this is what I’m being treated to,” he spoke of the reason that he was seeing all of this, “I am being forced to remember all of my atrocities of when I was...” Corvus folded his arms together knowing exactly where the conversation was leading to. He found it amusing how uncomfortable that the man separated from the beast such a pathetic excuse for flesh and bones he was. “A monster,” he interrupted, “was that what you were going to say?” He grazed a sharp tooth against his lip as he surveyed the expressions on Eryc’s face. “You seem so hasty to push away what’s kept you alive for this long; you would think there would be a smidge of gratitude.” What was there to be grateful for? Preying upon people and stealing blood from them and even going as far to taking their life for enjoyment? That was only the beginning of the heinous acts he committed in his life span. Dare he forget all the people he killed when he was a hired assassin...he was not in it for the money he could make but for the pure joy he received from murdering often times innocent people who shamed the wrong person or owed money they couldn’t repay. “I cannot pardon the things I’ve done,” he glared at his mirror image, “and if it is meant that I pay for those sins here in hell then so be it.” Eryc stood justified in his attempt for redemption. “Besides I never asked for any of it (becoming a vampire)...” “But you didn’t have the guts to turn it away either,” Corvus prided himself in reminding Eryc of that fact, “If you really knew the real truth...if you remembered what really happened you might not be so combative.” Eryc shook his head repeatedly wanting to deny himself the truth...because in actuality he had done this for many centuries even up until he recovered a file about his past but how accurate and complete was the information inside? “You created me to block out the pain as you watched everything you knew crumble,” the rogue in black commented, “I was there for you and guided you through and yet you stored away just enough humanity to break free and now look at you...” Corvus? face was left with only a scowl. “Pathetic and weak,” no punches were being pulled at this moment and that was how Corvus worked, “that little part of you that kept away from me was the one who always needed someone to hold your hand.” “Stop...” Eryc pleaded with the demon. “Why because I’m hitting so close to home?” Corvus rubbed his hands together nefariously. “You know I’m not wrong...I’ve never been wrong.” Eryc thought quickly and aimed the torch towards Corvus who lurched back as it was known that he would do because fire is a weakness to the damned. Fear the flame or it shall burn your life. “I was born the son of a king and I will not be belittled by you!” Eryc shouted without hesitation. “If anything you were the weak side because killing is easy...restraint is not.” Corvus kept his distance now because he knew the dangers of what the fire could do to him even if it was only in the corridors of Eryc’s muddled mind. It was true...Eryc was in a battle with himself while his body was fighting to stay alive. Would good triumph over evil and did it mean that if Eryc lost to Corvus that he’d return back to the land of the dead? Eryc had no idea of any of it but Corvus did and he would use it to his advantage. “Easy with that thing...” Corvus said in a cautious manner, “if you kill me then you may never find a way back to you so-called love.” He struck a pose of thought as he tried to recall her name. “Krissy is it?” Eryc almost let his temper get the best of him but that would have been too easy and would be playing right into his alter ego’s maniacal plans (whatever they might be). He calmed down and spoke with conviction. “You are not to talk about her...you might have been able to maim her in my nightmares but you’ll never touch her anywhere else!” Corvus mocked Eryc by placing his hand over his chest cavity; he knew what buttons to push and when. “Just another skirt to lead you around...how many has it been now?” He counted with his fingers until he reached four of them and held them out. “If you didn’t have them to tell you what to do in my absence you would be but a babe in the wilderness all alone.” Eryc knew just exactly who Corvus was referring too but he didn’t have to name them individually, the picture that was painted was clear enough. “What I have now is different then anything else...it’s a mutual understanding; a bond of love and trust.” “Tell that to someone who doesn’t know you.” He said with a harsh laugh behind it. “If this truly were a bond of love and trust then why do the two of you lie to one another back and forth and pretend like you are looking at everything through rose colored glasses?” Eryc was confused by Corvus? words. “What do you mean,” he questioned, “we don’t have any secrets and we wouldn’t lie to one another.” Corvus broke out into fake hysterical laughter; even he knew that was a bold faced lie and how Eryc could expect to believe it as well was anybody’s guess. “Boy you’ve been swimming in your own shit so long you’ve forgotten that it smells.” Corvus chided. “You don’t think that the two of you have ever lied to one another before” Genius...” Unfortunately the scoundrel was right, Eryc had kept things from her but they were not important to the relationship the two have so why bring it up? His eyes wandered towards his right arm and the middle eastern looking tattoo that was a symbol for something very deeply a part of his checkered past. “Ok, so I never told her that I used to drink blood so that I could live forever...is that imperative information?” Eryc shrugged his shoulders not thinking it was that big of a deal. “I’m different now and what she sees is who I am, who I want to be.” Corvus threw another verbal jab in his direction. “Does that include being someone who hides who he truly is regardless of what may or may not be relative?” Corvus? eyes flash a dark shade of crimson as a reminder. “A murderer in sheep’s clothing is still a murderer nonetheless.” Eryc started to see some of the victims he had claimed, one of them being the girl he met in Las Vegas (whom he brought back to his mansion) and bled her until there was nary any blood in her vessel. He shivered at the vision but it didn’t stop there...a memory of a little girl raged in his head while the sick feeling of enjoying the kill flowed through him. “I know what you’re doing,” he told Corvus, “and I want you to stop...it won’t change how I feel.” Corvus waved his hand in dismissal and the flood of images going through Eryc’s mind subsided only for the moment. “Do you know for sure that this mortality is permanent” What if all of a sudden you feel that oblivion in your stomach and the cold pale skin returns?” Eryc didn’t like where Corvus was taking this. “Would you still be able to resist not taking her and after you’ve ravished her, consume her mortal coil” Corvus had one more thing to say and this he wanted to make sure was clear. “Whether you refuse to believe it or not...the blood of Caine still flows in those veins,” he confided, “and one day that hunger will come back from wherever it’s been hiding and you won’t be able to control it.” Eryc’s dismissal of Corvus? claims only fueled the matter. There wasn’t a possible way that he could have become mortal in the first place if it hadn’t been for that night in New Orleans. It happened because Jake Hix’s brother Sid wanted him out of the way but the reason was never known. Why would Sid take it away and leave the possibility for him to become kindred again? There was not a logical explanation for this to begin with. “It will not happen and if it does I would do everything to make sure that no harm came to her.” Eryc solemnly said to his doppelganger. “I would forfeit my life before ending hers and that I promise.” Corvus mulled it over but made room to place some doubt in the tiny space in Eryc’s head. “Why are you so sure of this when you hardly know her” Are you certain that she is who she says she is?” “How can you say I hardly know her,” he asked, “and I have been with her for five months and we have spent nearly every moment of that time with one another.” Corvus tapped his fingers against his cheek contemplating Eryc’s words. Of course he was right, they did spent a lot of time together but there was plenty of time in between where misdirection could play a role in a bigger scheme of things. “Five months” You have certainly trampled over your standards as of late, Charles Alexandre; I’m saddened by this.” Corvus appeared shamed. “I always thought you knew what to do with a relationship but I was certainly wrong...five months is not nearly long enough to know all you need to know about someone.” Eryc shuddered at hearing the name he was called for reasons only he knew. He refuted the silver tongue of his facsimile and knew enough about Krissy to love her as she was without question and thought it was just another trick by Corvus to get into his head. “It’s enough for me,” he countered, “and if there was reasonable evidence to why I should not trust her then it would have presented itself by now.” Corvus cocked his head to the side taking small notes about Eryc’s mannerisms and demeanor; he was flustered but also had a curiosity in his eyes which gave the beast even more power to plant seeds in his mind. “I guess that would explain why you never question the reason she always leaves your hotel room long before you even wake up.” Corvus deducted this fact and had more to add. “Do not say it is because she attends classes at a University because this has occurred on weekends as well.” He wasn’t finished quite yet either. “All this time you’ve never seen her make calls or receive them on her cell phone,” this fact was one that Eryc could not deny nor any of the other ones placed in front of him, “and what about her apartment have you even seen it or has she even asked you to move in with her since you’ve become so close?” Eryc conceded slight defeat. “You have a point...but none of that really even matters at this point if I’m dead,” he shrugged, “it’s not like I can do a background check on her.” Corvus felt he toyed with him long enough and decided to let him in on what was going on. “Dead,” he chuckled, “you’re not dead but pretty close to it...so you have a choice.” Eryc was confused more than anything now what the whole point of their encounter was. If he wasn’t dead then why was he being tortured by the malevolent side of his soul? “I don’t understand,” was all he could say, “Why am I here then?” Corvus brought his hand up to replace the hood back over his face and one by one all the sconces began to go out leaving more and more shadow in the ruin they were in. Save the one Eryc held in his hand. “Because we needed to have this conversation,” Corvus informed him, “because one of us without the other would not last,” he added “and no matter how hard you fight me you cannot win...but sadly neither can I over you.” Corvus moved closer again but didn’t pose to be a threat this time or at least that is what Eryc felt inside him...he was no longer afraid. “Be mindful, Eryc, you always hate it when I’m right...” Corvus reaches out his clawed hand and starts to move it towards his spirit self’s chest, “you need to go now but I’ll see you again, I promise.” Eryc felt an electric shock going through him as the torch he held in his hand dropped to the ground and went out leaving him in complete darkness. ---- CLEAR! The doctor placed the defibrillator paddles upon Eryc’s chest and the result came in the form of his body lurching for a second time. They had already done this once before when he went into cardiac arrest from being in the frozen water for so long. His heart responded this time and gave a satisfactory readout for the attending Doctor who made sure the nurses gave him the right amount of medications to keep Eryc stable and as was commonplace, he would stay in ICU for as long as it took before they felt he could go to a normal room. When Eryc was brought in to the hospital by Krissy’s father, he had been presumed DOA but being a doctor himself, Paul wanted to make sure that everything that could be done for her daughter’s boyfriend was and if he was meant to survive then he would. Eryc stayed in a medicated sleep for about a day before he even showed signs of life. Krissy had rushed to the hospital after hearing from her father about the accident and stayed by his side the entire time after he was cleared for visitors; was admitted under another name to prevent media and other outlets from finding out about it and hounding for information. The doctors warned Krissy that things may be different when he woke up and that he might not remember much of anything (including her) as a result of possible injuries from the accident. CAT Scans were taken of his brain to make sure that he didn’t have any cranial damage from the lack of oxygen while he was under the water; they came back with positive results stating that there was no such damage and a full recovery was to be expected. Krissy stayed with him the entire time and only left his side to take rest room breaks or to get a cup of coffee when it felt like she might fall asleep. There was no way she was going to go home without knowing that he was awake and knew what had happened. Her hand was lightly grasping his when she could feel his fingers wiggle slightly and she looked up to see him struggling to try and open his eyes. She took her free hand and gently rubbed his forehead and spoke softly to him. “Relax baby, don’t strain yourself.” Eryc swallowed hard and was annoyed back the fact that his throat felt coarse and scratchy (from the tubes that were used to help him breathe for a short time and also ones that pumped some water from his lungs). “Where am I?” He squeezed Krissy’s hand lightly only because he did not have much strength. “What time is it?” She hesitated for a second because she didn’t know how he would react to being told that he almost died out on the lake... actually more like he was resuscitated. “You are in the hospital...it’s about four twenty-two in the afternoon; you have been sleeping since you were brought in,” she paused to gain her composure as her eyes welled up with tears, “and you fell and went through the ice while you were skating.” His eyes suddenly opened; he sat up and looked around to see the typical hospital room (bed, television, and equipment next to his bedside) and the heart monitor that was attached to him started going all funny. Eryc had a hard time distinguishing this from being real or a dream and waited for something strange to happen or for Corvus to re-appear again. Krissy was started by his actions and wondered if she should go get a nurse to give him something to calm down but after he glanced about for a moment or two he calmed down. “Where is Corvus,” he sharply asked, “I know he was here!” He received a blank stare from Krissy considering she didn’t have a clue what he was talking about; to her it was just rambling and that scared her a bit as well. If he actually knew what he was saying he would probably have smacked himself but the medication had an effect on his bearings of reality and until they wore off he might say something that could really put him in some trouble. “I don’t know who that is,” a bewildered look formed on Krissy’s face, “no one has been here other then me sweetie.” Eryc exhaled heavily as he could see that she seemed bothered by his words...he gauged the expressions on her face that made him seem like he was crazy in what he said which made sense to him because he knew if she had ever heard of Corvus that she might want to bring in a shrink. It’s not very often that you have reoccurring dreams or visions of conversations with yourself (both past and present versions). “I’m just out of it,” he explained in the best way he could, “don’t mind me.” Eryc took in the sight of Krissy and her infectious smile as she moved in and gave him a lingering kiss which made the road to recovery for him a lot less arduous; sure his body ached all over but he could overcome that with the passion that resonated within his lover. “Do you remember anything before now?” Krissy hesitated before asking her next question. “Do you know who I am?” He turned his head at an angle and thought maybe she was the one who needed to be in the bed instead of him but she was only asking him questions that were valid to see if he had lost any of his memory during his misfortunate event. “Some things are a little fuzzy,” he thought, “but I haven’t forgotten the woman I love...Kris, you are the reason I’m still here.” Eryc reached for her hand and took it into his, finding the strength to squeeze a little harder but not so it would hurt. Faith was not something that Eryc was surrounded by much in his life other than during times that he barely remembered that happened before he became a creature of the damned. His surviving what should have been his death gave him the much needed clarity that it was indeed time to move on with his life. “Kris...” he spoke softly with his voice trembling, “I know this might not be the best place but there is something I need to do.” His intentions were not clear to her as he tried to figure out the best way to conclude what he wanted to say. “I’ve given this a lot of thought and these recent events have brought me closer to realizing that I cannot continue life without doing something for myself as I’m always thinking of others ahead of me.” He exhaled heavily and his eyes started to become damp; Krissy immediately thought the worst of the situation and different scenarios darted around in her mind...the worst of which saw him letting her down easy and ending their relationship right then and there. “Eryc, if this is your way of telling me that you don’t...” She started to say but was cut off by him putting his finger to his lips and hushing her from continuing. “Let me finish,” he politely requested of her, “the words are not coming out like I want them to.” He laid his head back on the pillow and closed his eyes for a moment...concentrating on this moment and expelled a heavy breath and decided to stop beating around the bush. “This is not the proper way I would have wanted to do this but under the circumstances...I don’t have much choice.” He chewed on his lip nervously as if trying to stall for a few more moments but it wouldn’t change anything. “Kris, will you be my wife?” A burden felt like it was lifted from him as he spoke the words he felt were necessary for his long term happiness but Eryc seemed discouraged that Krissy stared at him in shock but it was all for good reason; he had taken her by surprise. “I don’t have a ring right this moment but if you say yes...” he paused to look at her again, “I would make sure one will be on your finger as soon as I get out of here.” Which he hoped would be sooner rather then later. Krissy had not expected this moment so soon but welcomed it openly as she tried very hard to hold back the tears that were starting to stream down her face. She buried her face into his midsection and gave him an answer without even thinking the proposal over or what it might mean for the future. “Yes, yes, yes...” she repeated in excitement, “I will marry you!” The hardest part was over for him as he reflected on the moment and laid his head back against his pillow and sighed blissfully. Eryc was happy (for once) and that was not a simple task by any means; (he knew) it wouldn’t be best to dwell on it or contemplate how long it would last because that would be the first sign of another impending failure and his relationship with Krissy was not something he wanted to see deteriorate.
