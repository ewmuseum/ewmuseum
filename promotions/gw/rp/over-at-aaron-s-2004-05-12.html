Date: 5/12/04 The intercom rang. Aaron walked over and pressed the 'Talk' button. 'Yes?' he asked before pressing the Listen button 'Yes, Jamal is here to see you,' said the voice of the security guard. 'OK, send him up.' Felicia walked into the living room. 'Is that Jamal?' 'Yeah, he and I are going over the cover designs for DMAK's new CD.' 'Oh, the one with all the cover songs.' 'Yeah. We finally got the record company to make it a 2-disk set. Good thing too, because there were a lot of songs to use.' 'Just try not to make too much noise. I just got Ashley to go to sleep.' 'We're gonna be looking at photos and logo designs. How loud could it get?' 'Aaron, this is Jamal we're talking about.' 'Good point.' Just then, the doorbell rang. Aaron ran up to the door and looked through the peephole to see who it was. 'Man, don't look through the peephole! You know it's me!' Aaron laughed as he opened the door. 'Come on in, man. We got a lot of things to go through. But we need to keep it down. Ashley’s sleeping.” “OK, cool.” Brick and Aaron went into the living room and sat on the couch. 'So, how are things with you?' 'Oh, let's see. Deacon Holmes is pissing me off. Train is pissing me off. Waiting to see whether or not I'll be playing Luke Cage is stressing me out, especially with the rumors going around that Tyrese might get the part. Aside from all that, my life is just peachy.' 'Well, things are going pretty okay for us. Felicia and I are considering renewing our vows for our anniversary.' 'Really? That's great.' 'Oh, you’ll never guess who I ran into.” “Who?” “Nathaniel Wallace.” “Nathaniel Wallace” Big sixth-grader I used to keep from picking on you Nathaniel Wallace?” “One of a lot of sixth-graders you used to keep from picking on me, yeah. You’ll never guess what he’s doing now.” “Running a protection racket?” “No. He’s a minister now.” “Get out!” “I’m serious. He has a small church in East New York. He invited me to come to service one Sunday. I think he’d like to see you there too.” “Well, if anybody needed Jesus, it was Nathaniel.” “Well, nobody’s perfect. We could all use some church.” 'You know, Ely and Gunner are dating.' 'Really? I kinda figured when I saw the Hall of Fame ceremony on Wrestlecade. But I thought he was hooked up with Christina McCoy.” “That’s what a lot of people thought.” “Funny. I always thought you’d be the one Gunner hooked up with.” “Look, Gunner and I agreed to be just friends. I’m cool with it. Besides, Ely’s a decent cat. I like this design right here.” “I don’t know. I’m not really feeling the font they used.” “The font?” “Yes, the font.” “Ooh, the font. Not like I don’t get guys asking me why I didn’t hook up with her. “Dude, she’s like the hottest babe you got in GW and you didn’t hit that?” Gets on my nerves sometimes.” “You mean you two really never...” “Nope.” “So, you’re cool with Ely and Gunner being a couple?” “Isn’t that what I said” Besides, I’m too busy for a relationship.” “Ely isn’t.” “Ely doesn’t have as much on his plate as I do.” “So, who was that cat who helped you out on Evolution?” “That was Jay Quickness. He’s been having some problems with MOB, so he came to help me out and even the score. I know one thing. Next time I see Roz Blayze, he better just lay on the ground, cause he’s a dead man.” “I figured having Deacon and Train on your hit list would be enough.” “I’ve always got room on my hit list and Blayze got himself a nice little spot on it last Monday. What do you think of this design?” “Not bad. How about this one?” “Pretty good, but not really eye-catching.” “So what do you know about this Jay Quickness?” “Not a lot. I’ve seen him in action. He’s pretty impressive. Plus, he’s got beef with MOB. I’ve got beef with Train. I figured he can’t be too bad.” “Is there anybody you don’t have beef?” “You and me are about to have beef if you don’t pick a design for the CD cover.” “OK, fine, how about this one?” Brick took the design from Aaron and studied it. “I think we’ve got a winner!”
