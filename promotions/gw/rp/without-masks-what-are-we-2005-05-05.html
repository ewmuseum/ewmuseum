Without masks, what are we?

5 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/05/2005 Another loss. Those were the first words filling Nariko’s head as she slowly closed to the door to Sada’s room, easing shut to avoid disturbing her resting partner. At one point in her life those words alone would have been enough to trigger a self-destructive streak a mile wide, leading down a dangerous road that left her back at square one again’taking a lot of time off from the ring. Self esteem had never been one of her strong points, and what little regard for herself she did have always took a hit when she felt her shoulders pinned to the mat, when she heard that thunderous three count that might as well be shaking the very foundation of the arena. A slight smile spread across her face, the only mask she was wearing tonight was the spidery pattern of shadows that slid across her face in waves, hiding all but her brilliant green eyes from any but the most determined observer. “Not this time?” she whispered to herself, the sound almost lost in the night air. It had been a trying series of weeks for certain. First settling into the USA again, never her first choice, and trying to once again find her feet in WMW’only to come up short once more. Out of fear and desperation she had fled to GW, following Luis? advice and seeking a fresh start in a new company, and this time bringing Sada with her’friends make all the difference, she had discovered. Eye opening victories on the tag team scene had restored a little of her shattered confidence, setting her up to truly feel the sting of the next three losses she was to suffer? But she felt only elation. The warm summer air blew in through the balcony doors, left intentionally open to allow the smells and sounds of the night to waft in and soothe her mind, to help quiet the fear she felt when contemplating her next step. The air warmed and chilled her skin at the same time, the warm summer breeze tempered by the cold bite that always came with night by the desert. Nariko strode confidently across the penthouse apartment she and Sada shared, the silk fabric her simple robe rippling in the night air, her feet making little sound on the deep pile carpet as she moved. Threading her fingers through the obi (belt) of her robe she unwound the simple knot, letting it fall to the floor, the winds probing fingers slipping inside to brush against her skin. A simple shrug of her shoulders and the robe followed the obi, piling slowly to the ground like the skin of a snake so recently shed. Clad only in the darkness that hid her face she slipped into the washroom, pausing for a moment to study her darkened reflection in the mirror. “It’s been such a long time?” she murmured, “Since I let this face see the light of day.” A thread of doubt crept into the back of her mind as she studied her own face, the alabaster skin contrasting sharply with the colour of her eyes, nearly glowing in the darkness. She shook her head sharply; trying to rid herself of the doubt and regret that clouded her eyes. “No. There can be no other perfect time. I have a golden opportunity before me’and I cannot simply let it slip by.” Turning away from her moment of vanity, Nariko slipped into the shower, turning the nozzles to as hot as she could stand, standing beneath the high pressure jets as steam rose from the bottom of the tub. Gritting her teeth against the pain she gripped the balance rail on the side of the shower, letting the hot water sluice away the dirt and grime from her back’and another, more powdery substance that gave grudgingly under the pressure. Liquefied concealer slipped down her hips and legs, running down the drain with the rest of the dirt and sweat, slowly painting a picture across the canvas of her bare back. As the concealer washed away it revealed jagged tribal markings, spreading like wings across her shoulder blades, slipping around her sides to disappear out of view, and the Japanese kanji (character or letter) for “Loyalty” tattooed squarely into her lower back. As the high pressure jets bombarded her reddening skin, it peeled away the most revealing of her tattoos, a ring of barbwire around her left bicep, obviously an older tattoo’with an obviously new addition. A silvery rose with blood dripping from petals and thorns twined with the wire, forming a perfect loop. “When you walk beneath the waterfall,” she said slowly, almost chantingly, “You can feel all that is old and unnecessary washed away, so that which is new may flourish.” “That’s a very pretty saying.” A sardonic voice interrupted, speaking from beyond the half closed bathroom door. “Is it Zen?” Nariko chuckled, swiping watery bangs from her face. “Something like that. You know most people knock before just wandering into another person’s apartment’and don’t I have to invite you in or something?” It was Shadow’s turn to chuckle, leaning as she was against the outside of the bathroom wall. “That’s superstition and old wives tales. I’d never be able to thrive in the modern nights if that were the case, it’s just not practical. And if I was meant to knock, then Christian would not have given me a key.” “You’re lucky you came in when you did, I was naked out there for a few minutes you know.” “At my age you’re not really perturbed by such things anymore. I’d be more worried about your tag partner catching you running around in the buff.” “It’s not anything she hasn’t seen before.” One of Shadow’s eyebrows rose in amusement, and she made a gentle tsking sound with her tongue. “You ladies these days’why didn’t I know anyone like you back when I started?” “Karma,” came the glib reply. “Do me a favour while you’re out there” Toss the Shoppers Drugmart bag in through the door please.” Shadow retrieved the aforementioned bag from the coffee table, taking a moment to sift through the contents, her eyebrow rising slightly again. “Hair dye remover eh” Decided it’s time to let it all hang out?” “You’ll notice I’m not talking in Japanese to you right now.” “Fair enough.” Shadow tossed the bag in through the door, sliding it as close to the tub as she could make it. “So’is this going to be for the whole world to see” Or is little show “For my eyes only?”” Nariko ran the dye-comb through her hair, trying to bleach out the black dye she’d used several months ago. After a few moments the first signs of success showed through, as streaks of strawberry-blonde began to peek through. “This is for everyone to see, for all of GW. Since my second loss I’ve been burning to even the score again, to prove to myself and everyone that I don’t need to hide behind a mask to be here.” “You’re second loss” To The Connection?” “No, I’ll explain that in just a moment, but what makes it relevant is that it restored the one thing I’ve been truly missing since my first loss over a year ago.” “One thing” What one thing is that.” “My love of the sport. Doing it for money, or doing it just to make a living stripped away the parts of it that were really important to me. I missed just getting into the ring and fighting for nothing more then the sheer sake of it.” “And now that you have it back?” “I’m going to prove that I’ve still got what it takes’starting at Evo. I suppose I really should thank RAD for that discovery, don’t you think”
