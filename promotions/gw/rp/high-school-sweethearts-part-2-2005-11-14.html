High School Sweethearts: Part 2

14 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/14 November 13th I leaning back in my chair as the plane takes off. Looking out the window as the man next to me thankfully is trying to get some sleep. I'm guessing he's a businessmen by the suit he is wearing. He looks at me with his head titled he just realizes who I am. 'Fred Grest?' He asks. 'Who?' I ask. 'Fred Grest... Global Wrestling superstar?' The man responds. 'I don't even watch wrestling' Hoping he leaves it at that. 'Oh, you just look like him.' 'Lucky him.' The man smirks 'Good thing your not.... He got his ass kicked last week.' 'Shit happens' I bite my lower lip not wanting to give away my cover. He nods and leaves me alone thankfully. Making sure though I pull out my lap top and put on my head phones hoping every one just leaves me alone. Not listening to a damn thing I get online and surf the web. Its not really like me to dodge the fans like this. Under ordinary circumstances I'd enter any conversation with a fan. However I just want to get to Las Vegas do my thing and get out. As always I have a lot weighing on my mind in and out of the ring. I think time out of the ring only made things worse doesn't matter though I still believe things are looking up for both. I look up and see a little kid staring at me wearing the new Radiant T-shirt. I really need to dye my hair maybe then I'll blend in better. Brown fits me for times like these. I look over and see the man sound asleep. I grab a pen and paper and sign my name. Handing it to the kid I put my finger over my mouth hoping he stays quiet and thankfully he does. I let out a sigh and go back to surfing on the web. I suddenly hear 'You got mail' and click the mail box icon. My computer freezes up for a few moments. I mutter ' Damn Machine' The mail appears clicking on it and its from Karen. Hey Freddy, After your match on Evolution you should stop by my store. Maybe you can find something to use for your next prank. lol Well hopefully I'll see you soon. ~ Karen It's been a few years since Karen has been in my life. Why the sudden interest in me? I don't think its because I am a part of Global Wrestling now. Hell, I've been on TV before but then again none as big as Global. Why am I doing this to myself. Shut up Grester you have something going for you don't screw it up by thinking. Its always been a dangerous past time for you. I lean back and rest my eyes awaiting to arrive in Sin City.
