Serial Killers

6 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/6/03 There Is No Happiness Without Tears No Life Without Death Beware I Am Going To Make You Cry -Lucian Stankiak Every Man Has His Passion Some Like Whist I Prefer Killing People -Rudolf Pleil It Wasn’t As Dark And Scary As It Sounds I Had A Lot Of Fun Killing Somebody Is A Funny Experience -Albert De Salvo I Didn’t Want To Hurt Them I Only Wanted To Kill Them -David Berkowitz One Side Of Me Says I’d Like To Talk To Her, Date Her The Other Side Of Me Says I Wonder How Her Head Would Look On A Stick -Edmund Kemper His voice spoken? To society, they are deranged. To the environment, they are insane. To the world, they deserve to be dead. The Women I Killed Were Filth Bastard Prostitutes Who Were Littering The Streets I Was Just Cleaning Up The Place A Bit -Peter Sutcliffe I’ve Always Had The Desire To Inflict Pain On Others And To Have Others Inflict Pain On Me I Always Seemed To Enjoy Everything That Hurt The Desire To Inflict Pain That Is All That Is Uppermost -Albert Fish Maybe they have just snapped. Or maybe they have seen society as being deranged. Or seeing the environment as insane. Or seeing the world, as deserving to be dead. You Feel The Last Bit Of Breath Leaving Their Body You’re Look Into Their Eyes A Person In That Situation Is God -Ted Bundy People Are Like Maggots Small, Blind, And Worthless -David Smith Ridding the world of the weak was their career. For goodness sake, or sake of goodness’their own minds controlled their hands. Believing in what they once believed in’their fuel. Each wound, each hole, each drop of blood was a sacrifice to something. Killing them, pleased them. Their own greed led to ending their lives. I Don’t Lose Sleep For What I Have Done Or Have Nightmares About It -Dennis Nilsen We Wanted To Do A Crime That The World Would Have To Stand Up And Take Notice -Susan Atkins Look Down On Me You Will See A Fool Look Up At Me You Will See Your Lord Look Straight At Me You Will See Yourself -Charles Manson But the world’s people love to rid the world of these serial killers. For the sake of good, in their minds, they kill them’hoping to bring back what can’t be brought back. They don’t care, their minds control their hands. Their fuel, believing in killing these killers. Each volt, each shot, and each hanging was a sacrifice to something. Killing these serial killers, pleased them. Their own greed, led to the murdering of the murderers. The Sixth Commandment Thou Shall Not Kill Fascinated Me I Always Knew That Some Day I Should Defy It -John Christie After My Head Has Been Chopped Off Will I Still Be Able To Hear At Least For A Moment The Sound Of My Own Blood Gushing From My Neck That Would Be The Pleasure To End All Pleasures -Peter Kurten Society From The Beginning Started To Make Me An Animal That’s Why I Started All That Killing -Albert De Salvo We, as the world’s people, love to kill. We love to end the lives of these murderers. Our fuel is the knowledge of knowing that ending their lives is wrong. So we take their lives and we kill them ourselves. We are the murderers. We are the serial killers. He is seen standing below the full moon, the sky’s masterpiece. His head, fallen towards the ground. He is weak and worthless’compared to those around him. The world’s love is just a talent show. The world’s care is just a popularity contest. He is weak and worthless’the world around him has laid him out to rest. But he kicks up for himself by himself. Leaving this world in awe over everything he has accomplished. The world is a killer and he is the victim. You can not kill, what you can not hunt. The hunter is now the hunted in his own mind. And he has just shot them between the eyes. Sitting down and watching them squirm in a pool of their own blood. Leaving them weak and helpless’he hasn’t changed. The world has changed. He lifts his head towards the moon. He licks his dry lips, his second wind in life and begins to speak? “The end of the world is about to be apart of us. Maybe not in the next week, or maybe not in the next generation. But one day, the world will end. Leaving us oblivious to the problems that we have yet to face. Each minute ticks away, each second’moving closer towards the end. Carpe Diem, seize the day.” He shakes his head side to side’deep thoughts running through his mind. Not knowing what to say next, each word’falling from the tip of his tongue as his mouth enlarges releasing these thoughtful and thoughtless words? “Seize the problems and correct them. Better your own lives, is what you love to do. Lift weights and get in shape. Work your whole entire life and make money. Do what you want, simply because everything you better yourself about will not follow you into the afterlife. But what will follow you is the people that are left behind. Better them’better the future. Make each breath feel better for those who aren’t leaving this greedy world.” He pauses as he looks up into the midnight’s sky. It has come early tonight, only being 10 o’clock. A miracle of miraculous ness. Isn’t it amazing? Isn’t it special? Isn’t it? “But nothing matters, simply because we are all greedy. We strive for what we can not have. And we weep because of it. We are men of men but we cry like children. Is that what you want to remember ourselves as” Famous authors, such as William Shakespeare, often wrote about loving what he can not have. We look up to him for his writing and poetry, but nothing more. I look down on him, for he is weak. He is greedy. But nobody realizes this. He was madly in love with a queen. He was madly in love with a goddess. Even though she sheds the same blood as him, she was his everything. He lusted over her and loved her. He wasted’wasted precious time doing so. There is no harm in hoping for change. But there is harm in hoping for the taste of her kiss. Shakespeare often wrote about her, while she loved another man. He has fallen to his lack of perception. He couldn’t see, he couldn’t realize that what was set before him was thousands upon thousands of miles away. He continued to love her even though she loved another man. In the end, after his death’I wish we could bring him back and ask him how it felt to waste his life in lust. We are greedy, my friends. Society in general, not pinpointing anybody’because everybody wants and doesn’t give. We are the serial killers. We want blood for revenge, when we have no sympathy. We killed Carl Panzram for killing people. We killed Dennis Nilsen for crimes that we have once done as well, the difference is, he admitted to these crimes when we are oblivious to them. When I speak my last words on Judgement Day’I’ll speak them softly when I’ll say: I’ve Survived In This World Of Greed I’ve Walked Around And Noticed The Weaknesses That Surround Us But I Don’t Do Anything Because Of Consequence I Haven’t Killed Not Because I Have No Will To Do So This Is What The World Wants The Opportunity The Chance To Strive To Greed Over The Ending Of Another Life I Won’t Give Them That Satisfaction But To My Satisfaction I Took The Time To Realize That Death Takes Many Forms Even While Alive -Sage Sabbot
