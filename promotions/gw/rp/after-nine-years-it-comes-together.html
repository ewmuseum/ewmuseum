After nine years it comes together

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'The next time I come to you Quartz will be the day that The Lair will change forever. Remember me and look after the Davis family as they hold many a key in our eternal struggle for power.' -The Harvester of The Lost (1996) ---- 'My apologies gentlemen for keeping you, I'm overseaing a take over bid for one of my cilents and it's taking a little longer than what was first expected.' Says Steve Rowe as he closes the door to his office behind him and moves further into the room only to see exactly who the two gentlemen where in which he was addressing. Salaok spun around in his chair and faces Rowe as he walked across the room towards his desk but says nothing, only watches. His eyes focused on the man who had been trusted with many of Salaok's assets and by the look on Mr. Rowe's face, something wasn't quite right. Fear seemed to engulf Rowe's face as the white orbs from Salaok's eyes pieced at his skin until he finally got to his desk and sat down. 'Mr. Davis, Mr..... um, Quartz. It has been some time since the both of you sat in my office together, so what do I owe this honor?' The fear in his voice so clear that Quartz couldn't help but chuckle to himself which caused Mr. Rowe to jump slightly and look straight at him which he wasn't wanting to do. With his eyes firmly set on Quartz, he watched as the giant of a man stood up and walked towards the window grinning the entire time. 'Mr. Rowe, it has been some time since we both came to you has you have just stated yourself and the last time we did was to give you the power to look after some of The Lair's assets while we were busy with other thing but now we come before you to take that burden off your shoulders as such.' Mr. Rowe continues to just sit in his seat, watching as Quartz looks down out the window while he speaks, knowing full well that what Quartz and Salaok want can no longer be given back. Suddenly Mr. Rowe's attention shifts for Quartz to Salaok who sits forward in his expensive leather arm chair and glares at his laywer with a grin stretching from ear to ear almost. 'I can not help but noticing Steve that you have but a small bead of sweat running down your forehead, is everything fine with you? Care for a cigar to calm the nerves?' Salaok's tone is mock if anything else as he slowly moves his chair closer, continuing to stare into the God-fearing eyes of Steve Rowe as he attempts to put an answer together. 'Everything is fine Mr. Davis, however a few small things have changed since you last can to me which going on memory was just after we settled the Will of your late wife Lisha. And no, I care not for a cigar.' His words are fearful and although he's quite clearly trying as hard as possible to remain composed, his voice gives away anything but. The booming low tones of Quartz's voice sound out again from the window as the sky opens up, letting the rain begin it's journey to the ground. 'You say things have changed Mr. Rowe, exactly what has changed since we last placed our trust in you? Bare in mind, you were left in control of wealth that some countries would invade to get a hold of but our faith in you saw you given strict orders to go through us first.' 'Neither of you could be reached sir.' Mr. Rowe answers back straight away as if he quite clearly knows he has to defend himself for the situation he is about to reveal is not one that the two men in his office will be excited to hear about. Growing tired of the back and forth filled with no information at all, Salaok finally begins to get serious and the tone in his voice turns from one of mock, to one of business and power. 'The situation in which we find ourselves now, is one where we now wish to take control back over our properties and business which have kept The Lair a powerful entity for the last few years. All hotel and restuarants are to be turned back over to us with all of the temporary management told informed that the old owners are back and new contracts will be drawn up. All casinos which are in The Lair's name are again to be returned to us and the Gaming Commission is to be notified and each member of the board there is to recieve a small bonus for their help over the years.' Mr. Rowe quickly had grabbed a pen and began to scribble on his note pad as Salaok spoke trying to take down all of the important information and knowing that it wouldn't be long until Salaok mentioned the part of the deal which was going to cause some pain. 'Yes sir, and how much of a bonus shall each of them receive?' Pausing for a brief moment, Salaok tries to think of what would be worthy of the Commissions help over the past few years that they have turned a blind eye towards their opperations and struggling to come up with a good sum of cash, he suggests the only thing that he can think of. 'Have them taken down to our car yard and they can have whatever they choose. They've stopped us from running into a lot of red tape over the years and it's about time they knew that we are indeed greatful for their hospitality. After they have been there, I want the car yard sold off as it's simply not generating enough cash flow for the position in which it is in. Have it listed on the market and then have The Hotel Nevermore buy it publically which should give a public idea that the hotel is doing well and generate some interest in the stock market.' Stopping for a moment, Mr. Rowe looks up at Salaok somewhat confused about that last order and why he would want the stock market involved in the happens of The Hotel Nevermore. 'You intend on listing The Hotel Nevermore sir?' 'No, not as yet. It'll take a good couple of years to get the interest in the building in which I want in order for it to get the prices in which I'm looking for. Now I'm going to need a building company contacted on behalf of the hotel as the car yard is to be destroyed and another hotel put in it's place. It is a prime location and we were foolish not to have done this earlier. What builders do we have on our lists?' Mr. Rowe stops writing and quickly rolls his chair across the back of the room towards a metal grey filing cabinet whic squeeks as he pulls open the botton draw and riffles trhough the folders. Grabbing one of the folders which looks like it would have taken up half of the draw on it's own, Mr. Rowe pulls it up and then rolls back over to the desk, opening it out straight away. Flipping trhough the tabs on the side of the file, he gets to where he needs and begins to rolls his finger down the page and just as he is about to spew forth the list of construction businesses that are on The Lair's payroll, Quartz interjects. 'Don't bother reading them out Mr. Rowe, Diasporo owns one of those contruction companies, we will use which ever one that is.' Straight away Mr. Rowe looks up at Quartz, almost having forgotten he was there which is hard to do when the man stands well over seven foot tall. Nodding to let Quartz know that he heard what was said, Rowe makes a note next to some more of his scribbles as he tries to keep up with the speed of the orders in which two of the three figure head of The Lair spew forth the imformation which is needed to take the company forward in their minds. 'Now about Global Inc, I will personally be going back to take control over The Lair's half of the business again. It was an area which has a huge chance to make us a lot of money and fund other projects and will be the base of a lot of business for us.' Salaok watches as Mr. Rowe's face begins to change from one of business, back to the face filled with fear when he first realized who was in his office. Instantly both Salaok and Quartz pick up on the change of face as a bolt of lightning cracks the sky, lighting up the room even more so for a brief second and the thunder which rolls in makes him jump. 'I take it by the look on your face Mr. Rowe that something is wrong with the last suggstion from Salaok?'Quartz's voice questioning the fearful Mr. Rowe while walking towards him and stopping at the edge of the desk. 'Well you see gentlemen, when neither of you could be reached, it had been deemed that Salaok was dead and the control of global Inc was sold off as McCoy had put in a bid for it which was in excess of two times what you had paid for it. It was deemed in the best interest for The Lair that I would sell it off and put that money towards other projects.'The words that seemed to have flowed so fearfully from Mr. Rowe's mouth brings Salaok to his feet and his hands pressed down on the desk with great force. With his knuckles going white under the pressure of his weight over then, he leans forward getting right into the face of Mr. Rowe. 'Dead? You presumed that I was dead and yet you had no certificate to say that was the case and yet you still went ahead and took control of my assets?' 'I did not want to do what I did Mr. Davis, however there was little to no choice in the matter as none of you could be reached. I merely went about my job trying to do what would be best for the businesses with no guidance from anyone. The last thing I wanted to have happen was for the companies to have no figures of head and have them sold off without my control.' Steve tries to explain but only serves to put his foot further into his mouth as Salaok bites back with spite and fury in his tone more than normal. 'Sold without YOUR control? Do you not think you've just done the same to me? I've kept you on the pay role for the past decade and this is how you repay me? You sell of my most valuable asset for only double of what I paided for it? You forget that when I bought into the business it was nearly dead and now it is one of the major sport entertainment companies going around. You under sold Global Inc to the last person who I wanted you to sell it to and you expect me to understand? Do I look dead to you son?' 'Salaok, you hadn't been heard or seen of for 6 months, what were we to think?' As his tollerance for his lawyer shinks with every breath he takes, Salaok leans further over the table, getting so lcose to Mr. Rowe's face that the heat from his skin could be felt. 'Do I look dead to you Mr. Rowe? Because I sure as hell do not feel dead, but with every second that I stay in this room, you become that little bit closer to feeling death yourself. What is stopping me from throwing you out that window right now?'Salaok asks as he grabs Mr. Rowe around the neck and pulls him to his feet before dragging him over the table. With fear running rampant with his body, Mr. Rowe looks over at Quartz, hoping for some sort of help which is clearly useless. 'Quartz, please, surely you must understand that what I was doing was for the good of The Lair and in no way was meant in any harm or wrong doing intended.' Quartz grins as Mr. Rowe sees the cold look over his face and knows that pleeding with him for help is useless. 'At what point in time did it ever seem like I would be of any help to you. Your deal with McCoy didn't just effect Salaok, but also effected myself. The money in which you've lost in that deal could have been the money I retired on and yet you felt that you had the right to sell off what is not rightfully yours?' 'Actually Mr. Quartz, with you two missing, the control of the estates is turned over to me as per my contract so i was simply doing what I thought would be best for the group of investors in which you represent.' Mr. Rowe ties to defend himself but soon finds himself standing on nothing but thin air as Salaok has him lifted with one hand placed around his neck while Quartz answers. 'Actualy Mr. Rowe, should you have read the fine print at the bottom of your contract when we first hired you, there has to be evidence of our deaths in order for that to take place and there was none. The sale with McCoy there was no a legal one. You've broken the law Mr. Rowe and being a lawyer, you of all people should know the laws. You can either make it so that deal with McCoy never took place, or you can pay us the lost money in which you have caused during your brief, self-appointed rule of The Lair's fortunes. Or Salaok can just throw you out the window and it can all be over and done with?' Panicing, Mr. Rowe clearly isn't sure what he can possibly do in order to make this situation right again but still manages to spit out an answer despite choking. 'My best sollution would be to hire another lawyer who will take the case stating that I sold something that I didn't have authorization to sell which should get you out of the deal once the money is returned to McCoy.' Suddenly Salaok's face of anger turns to a smile as he forces Mr. Rowe to look him in the eye as he speaks. 'So what you are saying Mr. Rowe is that I no longer need you?' 'I believe that is exactly what he is saying Salaok.' Responds Quartz who steps out of the way giving Salaok a clear view of the window which overlooks the road below. Walking towards the window with a grin on his face and tears beginning to stream down Mr. Rowe's, Salaok tightens his grip on the neck and then with a sudden burst of power, Salaok hurls his lawyer at the window. The window doesn't break at first and Mr. Rowe bounces back into the room with blood gushing from the back of his head but that isn't enough for Salaok as he picks up Mr. Rowe again and tosses him harder at te window, this time, it gives way. The sound of shattering glass and the screams of pain and fear dift down towards the road as Quartz and Salaok turn their back on the window. Knowing what has to be done now, Salaok and Quartz leave the room, but not before picking up the files which link them to Mr. Rowe. By the time the sirens had even begun to sound like they were close, Salaok and Quartz were already on there way away from the building knowing full well that at some point the police would come and ask them questions as always. However what most of the police didn't realise that their chief was actually on the payroll for The Lair and that no crime would ever be charged against them. It wasn't however until they pulled up in front of The Nevermore mansion that something didn't seem right as the front door was open and a dim glow from a single light was coming from inside. Had something finally caught up to those who said they could never be caught? Surely not but it certainly had placed a seed of doubt in their minds as the car skids toa halt and both men get out of the car in a rush. Both pulling guns and running up towards the front door, over the forty seven steps which Salaok still remembered Lisha walking up on their wedding day, the two men stop at the front door with their backs to the wall. Quartz motions that he will go in first and just as he is about to turn and burst his way through the door, a voice sounds from inside which seems familiar. 'It had been some time fellow Harvesters since we last spoke and I see that everything I predicted has come true.' Salaok and Quartz stop dead in their tracks as the voice in which had been apart of The Lair ever since they could remember seemed to be inside The Nevermore Mansion. The doors open through no contact from Salaok or Quartz and as they peer inside, they see that the voice they thought they had heard was right. Seeming surprised at the least, both, Quartz and Salaok stand at the doorway looking at the man who stands in the middle of the foyar with his entire body and head covered with a robe which was identical to the one they had last seen him in some ten years ago. 'Orthane, this is a surprise to say the least.' Salaok lets out as the large man slowly removes his hood to allow two of his fellow Harvesters to see that it truely is him and greats them. 'Salaok and Quartz, it has been many a year since we last spoke but I come to you today for a reason for sight has shown the way yet again.' With great respect, both men walk further into the room and listen to every word in which Orthane speaks. It was the very man who had foretold of the death's of Salaok's first wife and child and now he comes back with more to tell. 'While I am sorry that the fourtunes regarding your family came true young Salaok, I am here today to warn that the next death you cause will be one that hurts you as much as the family of the deceased. It is for this reason that I can no longer stand idly by and watch as you two work alone. It is time for me to come out of retirment and help you do what needs to be done.' Salaok looks up at the larger man with a smile on his face and a question on his mind. 'Rebuilding The Lair?' 'Yes Salaok, it is time to rebuild and take back what The Lair used to be. What we do from here on in I guarantee will not be the right thing, but it will be the done thing if we are to keep this legend that we have created for ourselves alive. And the first thing you must do Salaok is fix the wounds between you and Lisha's family as they too have shed many a tear over her death and wish for an alliance as such. It will not be easy and you will need my assistance in this, but it must be done.' 'Of course Harvester.' It had been sometime since Salaok had sounded so diciplined and it was music to the ears of not only Orthane, but to Quartz as well who was willing to simply stand back knowing that this day had been foretold to him many a year ago. A day that would create a new powerful Lair that was needed to take the underworld into a new direction. The time for change had begun and it all rested now in the hands and the mind of The Harvester of The Lost.
