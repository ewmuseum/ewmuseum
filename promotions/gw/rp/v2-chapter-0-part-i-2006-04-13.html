Date: 4-13-06 “There are three way to kill vengeance. You can let it go. You can let it kill you. Or you can find the source and kill it.” ? Kyo Kushinaga After last week you can say that I killed the source of my vengeance. “Jackson Namenlos-Duisternis” Fans are calling my fight with Jackson one of the bloodiest and goriest fights of all time. If you’re wondering why this fight was like this then let me recap for you. At High Octane, Jackson made the foolish mistake and laid his hands on me. He also cost me the Platinum Championship Title, but I don’t really care about that since it’s just a title and I can win it back anytime I want to. After the fight, I thought about what I could do to Jackson and many thoughts entered my mind which involved pain and suffering. However, I decided to settle this like a professional fight and challenged him to a fight that will make him think twice about ever screwing with me ever again. I challenged him in two out of three falls fight with no rules. Everything was legal. I did this for two reasons. One to prove once and for all that he can never beat me even if he had the advantage with the fight being No DQ. And the second reason was because I wanted his bloody defeat to be televised in front the millions and millions of Global fans. Jackson, next time pick on a prey that you can actually beat rather then one that can beat the holy shit out of you. It’s just a thought. Well, I’m back to being the Deathscythe Outlaw. So, goodbye Grave Keeper for now. With that victim buried there’s just one thing left to say. Who’s the next causality! [:: ----- ::] In a store of ancient magic and spells was the store’s owner who was concocting a strong potion in a steel cauldron behind the curtains of the backroom. He drops the final ingredient in the cauldron and begins chanting a spell in an unknown tongue as it begins boiling in result. The potion soon lets out a small explosion. The store owner watches smoke of a strange color rising from the cauldron. “It’s finally done.” He scoops up some of the hot potion with the use of a long steel spoon. “I hold the strongest potion in any magic art.” The store owner begins thinking about the potion and what it can do when it is used. The store owner’s thoughts are soon broken when hearing a gentle chirm coming from the front of his store. “I have a customer.” The store owner pushes the curtain aside and sees that it was one of his regulars. “If you’re here for more ammo for that ancient and forbidden relic of yours then you should know that you took the last three I had when you came here last time.” “I thought you were the kind of warlock who can produce the ammo I need.” The store owner stands behind the front counter and stares at the customer through the darkness within his hood. “I’m a Defensive Warlock, a warlock that can summon powerful barriers and healing spells in both, black magic and white magic. The kind of a warlock you seek is a Soul Warlock.” “You’re talking about a warlock that can summon and capture strong spirits from the Spirit World, so what.” “What exactly do you think you’re been firing with that weapon of yours?” The customer gasps surprisingly. “It was the Soul Warlocks who made the Spell Binders and the God Shells.” The store owner lets out a small laugh. “You really don’t know much about the weapon you have, do you?” The customer who reveals to be Kyo lights a cigarette as he takes a drag afterwards and breathes the cigarette smoke pass his lips. “Well, I do know that I now have to search for warlocks that haven’t been heard from since the end of the Medieval Ages.” “I know where one is?” “Oh?” “I can tell you, but it’s going to cost you.” The store owner rubs his fingers together with a nervous chuckle. “Oh ok, let’s see what I got.” Kyo goes in his trench coat and pulls out both his large handguns. “Will this do?” He smirks as he holds the gun in the store owner’s face. The store owner holds up his hands and begins laughing nervously. “I was only kidding.” He heads to the backroom. “I’ll get you the address to the Soul Warlock.” Kyo nods. “Yeah, you do that.” He holsters his guns back within his trench coat. “Oh.” The store owner was standing in the doorway with the curtain push aside. Kyo looks at the store owner curiously. “Could I interest you in purchasing one of the strongest potions in the entire realm?” Kyo stares at the store owner more curiously. ==|Part I: The Hunt Begins|== In the main lobby of NEST Headquarters there are a large group of suspicious people entering a room where an important gathering was about to take place. Two of Mother’s Children, Volt and Tsunami are watching from two floors above the lobby. Tsunami has a curious look on her face. “Who do you think these people are?” Volt keeps his serious eyes lock on the suspicious people. “If I would have to guess then I would have to say that these guys are your average lowlifes.” “You’re right.” Volt and Tsunami turn to the familiar voice and see The Iron Brothers are approaching them. “These guys are bounty hunters.” Smash glares down at the suspicious people disgustingly. “I must say that this is a weak bunch.” “Why are bounty hunters coming here?” Tsunami looks at the brothers curiously. “Lord Aion realizes that he made mistake when wanting to recruit that tight wearing joke, Kushinaga into our elite group of warriors.” Bash smirks. “This is his way of correcting that mistake.” Volt looks surprise. “You mean that Lord Aion hired bounty hunters to kill Kushinaga.” He soon looks curious. “Then what about us” Shouldn’t we be the ones to kill him?” Smash scoffs. “I say let the weaklings deal with the weaklings and the strong deal with the strong.” Tsunami has her head down with a sadden look on her face. “And I really wanted Kushinaga to join us.” “You just wanted him to join us, so you could make him your boyfriend.” Bash stares at Tsunami with smirk and arms cross. Tsunami blushes. “Well, I?” “It’s just like you women to be blinded with your feelings.” Smash looks disgust. “The only thing you women get is a broken heart. Or get a shameful death by a punk ass in Widow’s case.” Smash’s face soon turns to pure fear when feeling the sharp edge of a katana against his throat. Volt, Tsunami, and Bash look shockingly when seeing that their leader, Moon have Smash at her mercy, but they’re even in more shock when they didn’t sense her arrival. Moon has a dead serious look on her face. “I don’t want to ever hear you mock one of your fellow comrades especially one who can’t even defend herself.” She tightens her grip on the smooth handle of her katana. “Understand?” Smash begins sweating as he takes a hard swallow. “I’m sorry, Moon. It will never happen again.” “Good.” Moon moves her katana away from Smash’s throat and sheathes it on her hip. Smash sighs in relief as he favors his throat. Moon faces her fellow comrades with a little relax look on her face. “It is as you heard. Lord Aion has placed a bounty on Kushinaga’s head. Before you all even ask you all are not allowed to participate in this. This order comes straight from Lord Aion.” Smash and Bash look disappointingly. “Mind if you ask how much his bounty is worth.” Smash looks at Moon curiously. Moon sighs annoyingly. “If you must know he is worth sixteen million dollars.” Bash scoffs. “That’s way too high for one weakling.” Volt looks at Moon seriously. “I still don’t understand why Lord Aion doesn’t allow us to take Kushinaga down. I also don’t understand why he wants Kushinaga dead now.” “Consider yourselves off duty. Lord Aion wants us to rest up until we’re given our next assignment. And to answer your second question I don’t know why our Lord wants Kushinaga dead, but he does.” Smash smirks as he wraps an arm around his brother’s neck. “If you would excuse us, we’re off to the strip clubs.” He and his brother walk away from their other comrades while singing a song about women and breasts. Moon sighs and shakes her head in reaction to the brothers. “I’m going to resume my training.” She walks away in the opposite direction from Smash and Bash. “For the rest of you, I advise you to do the same.” Tsunami was about to walk away, but she stops when noticing Volt was standing there with his head lower in deep thought. “Volt?” She looks at him curiously. Volt snaps out of it and looks at Tsunami with the same curiosity that she was giving him. “Are you ok?” Tsunami looks at Volt with some concern. Volt sighs and nods slowly as he looks back down at the bounty hunters who are continuing to enter the room. “Yeah, I’m just thinking what I can do for my break.” “Ok, see you when I see you then.” Tsunami heads in the same direction that Moon was heading. Volt nods in reply to Tsunami while keeping his serious eyes lock on the bounty hunters. [:: ----- ::] It was late afternoon in a metropolis that was outside of California’s own San Diego where Kyo sits outside of the metropolis? entrance on his running motorcycle. Kyo looks at the entrance as he notices a large sign hanging with something written across in ancient Latin. “This place is called Sanctuary, huh.” Kyo enters and drives through the metropolis where he sees that the metropolis almost looks like Hong Kong. He notices that some of the natives are giving him stares and soon realizes why with the help of his special sight as he sees through the natives? disguises. Kyo stops at a red light. “It’s Sanctuary, all right.” He puts on his shades to nullify the effect of his special sight. “A safe haven for demons and angels alike.” The light turns green as Kyo drives off and continues his journey to the Soul Warlock’s place. [:: ----- ::] Kyo arrives at his destination as he has his motorcycle park outside of a building. Kyo looks down at a piece of paper that has the Soul Warlock’s address on it, but he looks at the building beside him then back at the piece of paper. “Yup, this is the place.” He turns off the ignition. Kyo enters the place and notices that he was in a large magic/gun store. There was no one minding the store. “Hello” Is anyone here?” “I’ll be with you in a minute.” Kyo turns to the woman’s voice and sees that she was carefully walking downstairs with a tall stack of books in her hands. She gasps surprisingly when missing a step as she and the books begins falling to the floor. The books hit the floor. “Are you ok?” Kyo quickly catches her before she hit the floor with the use of his great speed. Woman lifts her head as she moves a few long strands of her honey brown hair away from her face. “I’m?” She soon notices him and looks at him dreamingly through her glasses. “I’m ok?” “Glad to hear.” Kyo smiles at her. Woman blushes darkly as she looks away from with shyness, but she soon remembers the books and quickly heads down the stairs. “Oh man, Grandfather will be mad if these have gotten damage.” She was brushing the books off before stacking them up again. “I’ll help.” Kyo starts making a stack of his own after dusting the books off. Woman blinks and looks with some embarrassment as the blush reappears on her face. “You don’t have to do that, really.” “I don’t mind.” Woman stares at Kyo dreamingly. Kyo senses the woman staring at him and looks at her curiously. “What?” “It’s nothing.” Woman quickly looks around with a much darker blush across her face as she resumes brushing and stacking the books. Kyo and the woman pick up their stacks and sit them down on the store’s front counter. “Thanks for the help, uh?” “I’m Kyo.” “I’m Jessica. It’s nice to meet you.” Jessica smiles at him. Kyo smiles back. “Likewise.” “I almost forgot that you were a customer.” Jessica goes behind the front counter and looks at Kyo contently. “Is there something that you’re looking for today?” “I’m looking for a Soul Warlock.” Jessica’s content look soon turns to suspicion. “What do you want with this warlock?” “I was hoping that he could make me some more God Shells.” Kyo looks at Jessica seriously. “God Shells” How do you know about those?” Jessica gasps surprisingly and looks at Kyo in the same manner. “Unless you have a?” Kyo nods. Jessica looks with more surprise, but she regains her composure and looks at Kyo seriously once more. “Wait here.” She leaves the counter and walks upstairs. Kyo doesn’t wait long as Jessica soon returns downstairs. Jessica looks at Kyo seriously. “He’ll see you.” Kyo looks at the stairs cautiously before he walks upstairs. Jessica watches Kyo leave, but there was some admiration from before on her serious face. [:: ----- ::] Kyo enters an empty room that smells of essences. He walks further in the room, but soon stops when he notices magic crests has been painted onto all four sides of the room. Kyo sees that the crests were also on the ceiling and floor. “Spirit Summon Crests.” Kyo stares at the crests interestingly, but his interest turns to curiosity when he begins hearing a strange humming noise coming from within his trench coat. He goes in his trench coat and pulls out his Spell Binder to see that the engrave characters on the barrel are mysteriously glowing. “It is true.” Kyo snaps his head to the voice and sees an old man standing in the middle of the room with a wooden cane in hand. “You do possess one of our weapons.” “So, you’re a Soul Warlock.” Kyo stares at the old man seriously. Old man smirks. “And you’re a Sinner.” “How did y?”” “I can sense and see the demonic and angelic aura around you.” Old man looks at Kyo seriously. “But you’re not just a mere Sinner in fact you’re a Lord.” Kyo looks at the old man curiously. “What are you talking about” I’m no Lord.” Old man looks at Kyo disappointingly. “It appears that you have fully awakened yet.” Kyo looks at the old man more curiously. “But you didn’t come here to hear what you are.” Old man walks to a table nearby where he takes a seat at. “You here for God Shells.” He looks at Kyo curiously. “May I ask why someone of your strength and power needs to use a forbidden weapon?” Kyo lights a cigarette. “What kind I say.” He breathes the hot smoke pass his lips after taking a drag from his cigarette. “I want extra firepower to blow the holy hell out of any assholes who want to screw with me.” Kyo smirks. The old man stares at Kyo seriously. Kyo returns the stare to the old man. “I’ll make you some God Shells.” Kyo looks at the old man curiously. “Just like that?” He forms a disturbing smirk on his face. “You know I could use the God Shells to make havoc in this world.” “You won’t.” Old man smirks. “It’s not in your nature.” “You’ll be surprise what’s in my nature.” Kyo still smirks disturbingly. [:: ----- ::] Kyo walks downstairs as he sees Jessica placing one book at a time on a tall shelf at the far right side of the store. Jessica stops when noticing Kyo. “I’m guessing that my grandfather will be making you God Shells.” Kyo nods. “Grandfather must’ve seen you worthy of the shells.” Kyo looks at Jessica curiously. “Grandfather hasn’t made God Shell in years.” Kyo looks surprisingly. “You must be someone of very high importance to force him out of retirement.” Kyo takes drag from his cigarette and breathes out smoke through both nostrils. “I’m no one important.” He heads to the store’s entrance. “I’m just a typical outlaw who’s kicking ass and taking names while enjoying a good smoke.” Jessica looks at him curiously. “Later, Jess.” Kyo exits the store. “Uh, please come again.” Jessica stares admiringly. “Jessica.” Jessica turns to the voice and sees her grandfather standing on the stairs. “Grandfather?” “I want you to stay away from him.” Jessica blinks and looks at her grandfather curiously. “Why?” “I saw much misery and death in his soul.” Grandfather looks at his granddaughter seriously. “I don’t want you to get involved with him.” “But?” Grandfather walks back upstairs. “That’s order.” Jessica sighs and looks disappointingly as she resumes putting the books on the tall shelf. “But I sense something deeper than that in his soul.” She has a concern look on her face. Kyo gets on his motorcycles and rides off like a speed demon. [:: ----- ::] It was early evening as Kyo has decided to stick around a bit longer in Sanctuary to check out the scenery, but since it was getting late he figures why not catch a bite to eat here. He sits at a ramen cart waiting for the ramen chef to prepare the meal that he has ordered. “I don’t think I ever saw you here before.” The ramen chef stares at Kyo curiously. Kyo lights a cigarette. “I’m just visiting.” He takes a drag and breathes the hot smoke pass his lips. “I was just visiting too not too long ago that is until I met my soon to be wife.” “So, how long have you been here, old timer?” “I’ve been here for fifteen years.” Kyo whistles. “That’s a long time.” “Yeah, it has.” The ramen chef smiles contently. “But this a great place to live for our kind.” Kyo looks at the ramen chef curiously. “Our kind?” “Aren’t you an angel?” Kyo lets out a small laugh as he smirks amusingly. “I’m too dark to be angel.” “Regardless, you’ll fit in great if you decide to move here.” “Tell me something.” Kyo leans over and looks at the ramen chef curiously. “How can demons and angels live here together in harmony like this” I mean the last time I checked angels and demons weren’t the best of friends.” “It’s because of this city’s Guardian.” “Guardian?” Kyo raises a brow. The ramen chef points up a large temple on top of a far hill. “Yeah, the Guardian keeps watch of us here and makes sure that nothing happens that would cause any discord here. You can say that he keeps the peace here.” Kyo stares at the temple seriously. “Quite a role he got.” “Kyo Kushinaga!” The ramen chef and Kyo turn to the voice and see a man who looks like one of those gunslingers in those old western movies. “Are you Kyo Kushinaga?” The man looks seriously. Kyo leans back on the ramen cart with a laidback look on his face. “Who wants to know?” “I’m here to kill you.” “Go away.” Kyo looks away from the man boringly. “What!” The man falls in shock, but quickly stands and stares at Kyo furiously. “You’re not worth my time. I’ve defeated men much stronger than you.” Kyo shifts his eyes to the man and smirks amusingly. “Hell, I squashed cockroaches that can put up a better fight than you.” The man growls. “You bastard!” He goes for his weapon within his poncho, but he stops when Kyo swiftly appears before him. “Oh shit.” The man has great fear on his face. Kyo nods with a wide smirk on his face. “You got that right.” He drills a damaging thrust kick to the man’s chest throws him against the back lid of a large dumpster. The dumpster lid slams close. Kyo takes a drag from his cigarette and breathes the smoke pass his lips in a dull sigh. “Why can’t some take the hint that they can’t defeat me?” He walks back to the ramen cart. The ramen chef looks at Kyo amazingly. “You’re incredible, man. I didn’t even see you move from your seat.” “Child’s play.” Kyo takes a seat at the cart. “You should be a professional fighter with moves like that.” Kyo laughs a bit. “I never heard that one before.” He soon looks with alert when sensing a strong presence nearby. A loud riff from a guitar echoes out as a bolt of lightning comes out of nowhere and strikes the ramen cart with great wrath. Kyo reappears beside the flaming wreckage that use to the ramen cart with the ramen chef under his arm. “You’re as fast as I heard, Mr. Kushinaga.” Kyo glares at the man who reveals to one of Mother’s Children, Volt. “I feel that you’re going to offer me an artistic fight.” Volt smirks as he looks at Kyo seriously. ---- ...To Be Concluded... ----
