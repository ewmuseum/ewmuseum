Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/27/03 Brick walks into his personal gym, carrying a duffel bag. He’s finally getting in the ring again with Sage Sabbot for the World Title. This is a very important match for Brick. He has been a part of Global Wrestling for four years, for the bulk of his wrestling career. Of all the titles in the company, this is the one that has always eluded him. It eluded him at Dead Zone. It won’t elude him at Whiplash. He wants it just that badly. He walks over to the portable stereo in the room. He pops in the Eminem Show. He puts it on track 18, “Till I Collapse”. Like a lot of songs Brick works out to, he relates to it on a certain level. It reflects his approach to achieving his dream. When others told him he should give up, he persisted, nevertheless.
