'Consequences and repercussions - The bittersweet symphony of souls.'

8 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 8th - 9th, 2005. Consequences and repercussions - the bittersweet symphony of souls. ---- 11:23 PM. August 8th. Madison Square Garden. ---- 'I can not believe this!' Screamed an irate Josh in the Elite locker room, loading a right hand to smash into a metal locker his taped fist leaving a dent in the metal door as it popped open. 'What in the..' Josh's sentence trailed off as he took aim at a potted plant on the floor and cocked his leg, taking aim at the plant and swooping in like Adam Vinateri to blast home a game winning field goal. Adam's kicks were usually met with a lot of flashbulbs and fanfare, Josh's kick didn't have quite the same treatment, instead the pot cracked to pieces beneath his feet sending shards slamming into the wall and dirt flying across the white carpet. 'This is...' Josh began, pausing midsentence to drive another right hand into a locker, once more causing it to pop open, a gaping dent in the middle. '...a load of...' Josh stopped again, this time to charge at the leather couch in the Elite locker room and wrap his hands around it throwing it over with a brilliant display of strength while his cohorts watched on in awe. 'You know Bill, you're the biggest one of us here...' Ace Harding said, nodding at the furious Josh. 'That's cute Ace.' Bill Moresi replied, a smirk on his face. 'You haven't known Josh as long as I have, when he loses it, he really loses it...mentally.' 'Come on, a big ol' 300 pounder like you, scared of the runt?' Ace teased, tucking his hands into the pocket of his black suit pants, enjoying the show. 'Hey bro, the runt is psycho.' Moresi warned with eyebrows raised over his black shades. Harding glanced at Josh as he drove his fist into another locker, Ace smiled and then nodded his agreement. 'You're right, the runt is psycho.' 'Yeah, but I haven't seen him like this before.' Bill said with a frown covering his face. 'Ahhhhh!' Josh let out a strange animal-like scream of rage, storming over to the bar and throwing his foot high into the air to shatter the door of a cabinet into mere splinters. 'So, what's his problem, did Ken steal his Superman tights again?' Harding said, a bit concerned by Josh's violent display albeit highly amused at the same time. 'I have no idea.', Bill replied, straightening the red tie he wore against his white shirt. 'Sometimes Josh goes a little crazy, but tonight? He's really off of the deep end.' 'Where's Ken?' Harding asked, in hopes that the Superstar would be able to calm down their cohort. 'He had to do a quick after show bit. Interview, pictures, all that good media crap, you know...the usual.' Bill stated, wincing as Josh brought his fist down onto the bar viscously, with one quick sweep of his wrist Josh sent several bottles of liquor flying, they hit the floor shattering into pieces as their brilliant liquid seeped into the white carpet. 'Dude, we have to do something...he's spilling all the booze!' Ace protested, a grimace across his face. Bill chucked, deeming the idea preposterous. 'You want to do something? Go for it, you haven't seen Josh like this before. When he's ticked he has this superhuman, hulk-like strength, craziest thing I've seen.' 'But...it's...booze...' Harding gasped, his sad eyes watching the liquid stain the carpet. 'It's okay Ace.' Bill said with a playful slap on the back. 'Ken just won a title, he's in the mood to buy drinks.' Bill Moresi and Ace Harding both shared a smile as they returned to watching 'American Youth' Josh take out his frustrations on the furniture with violent kicks and punches. Josh's Chuck Taylor clad foot blasted a hole through a wicker chair and snapped the thin pieces of wood in half as it soared across the room to the other side. 'You know, I'm kind of glad he did that, wicker is so middle class.' Ace commented as he stroked his chin thoughtfully. The room flashed brilliantly as Josh round-kicked a lamp, shattering the glass as the bulb exploded in a flash of light, it hit the carpet accompanied by a tinkle as a wild-eyed Josh spun around, gasping for breath, his chest heaving. Josh dropped to his knees, sweaty locks of multi-colored black, silver and blue plastered to his face as he raised shaking fingers to his face, with Ace and Bill completely focused on Josh they almost missed the grand entrance of the new International champion as Gunner Addison thrust the door open and stepped through it, holding it open for none other than the 'Superstar' Ken Partridge Jr. to bound through. 'Gentlemen, I present your new Internati...' Gunner began in a joyful tone before her eyes fell upon Josh. 'Wh...Jo...' Ken shook his head, at a loss for words. His baby blue eyes peered around the room, wide with shock at the path of destruction that Josh had left behind. Josh, Di-Did...you do this?' A bewildered KPJ asked the seemingly insane American Youth. 'He did it all right, and Bill let him too.' Ace said with a nod. Bill shot an icy glare at Ace before answering. 'We couldn't stop him, he's...mental.' 'Yeah, you're the only one that can handle the kid, Ken.' Ace chipped in, a statement that all members of the Elite knew to be true. 'Josh...' Ken coaxed, dropping to his knees to look Josh in the eyes. Ken was still dressed in his Wrestling gear and holding his new International title, but he quickly forgot it all as he stared at Josh. 'Are you alright, buddy?' Josh didn't answer Ken verbally, choosing instead to close his eyes and lower his head, nodding fervently. 'It's okay Josh, I'm here now.' Ken said, forcing a smile across his handsome face. Josh didn't respond, only sinking down even further on his knees. 'Josh.' Ken began, drawing in even closer. 'What is wrong buddy, are you okay?' Ken placed a reassuring hand on his best friends shoulder, gently lowering the International title to the floor. 'Please be okay, I love you man.' Ken's words were whispered, audible onto to himself and Josh, it was obvious that Josh heard them as he slowly lifted his head to look Ken eye to eye. 'I don't know what's happening, Ken...' Josh gasped, shaking his head. 'What are you talking about Josh?' Ken's hand remained on Josh's shoulder and he squeezed it gently. 'To me.' Josh shook his head, his hair twisting about, falling into his pained eyes. 'I don't know...' It was apparent to everyone in the room that Josh was dazed, confused and angry, yet what wasn't obvious was the logic behind his sudden emotional meltdown. But if Ken knew Josh as well as he thought he did, he knew that Josh's logic was hardly comparable to that of anyone else. Josh lived life with a rather skewed logic, living by his own rules and no one else's. Ken was the only one that seemed to be able to tap into what he was thinking, the only one that could set him straight. In short Ken was Josh's only hope, minus the Princess Leia hologram and beeping robot. Ken was the one that had kept Josh from committing suicide, sat next to his bed after a nearly fatal overdose in Rome and even stood face to face with Josh when his little buddy had shoved a gun in his mug. Ken was Josh's saving grace and he knew he had to be here for him now because something was drastically wrong, Josh was fading quickly, Ken had to snap him back to reality before whatever was eating Josh away got the best of him. But hey, never fear, out of all of Ken's jobs...this was his best, maybe it was because of all the practice he had in Josh's prior breakdowns. 'Breathe.' Ken commanded Josh, before pointing to the overturned couch and snapping his fingers at Bill and Ace. The two big men moved quickly, showing respect for Ken and concern for Josh as they propped the couch back up, before retreating back into their corner. Leading Josh by the elbow Ken encouraged him to stand, drawing him to the couch and sitting down, Josh dropped into a seat beside him, still wide-eyed and confused. 'Josh...tell me, please, whats wrong?' Ken asked, his eyes pleading with Josh. 'I don't know Ken, I've lost it, I've lost something...' Josh shook his head, his voice barely a mumble. 'Did you see it?' 'See what Josh?' Ken squeezed his best friends bicep. 'What am I supposed to see?' 'Me.' Josh's voice was low, barely a mumble at this point, forcing Ken to lean in to make anything out. 'Blowing it, losing a step, throwing it all away.' 'Josh, what are you talking about?' Ken asked, his eyes soft, his voice compassionate. 'I'm talking about the rise and bitter fall of a legend.' Josh shook his head, his voice beginning to sound cold and bitter. 'I'm talking about going from a 184 day title reign to being nothing more than a fall guy that sells tickets on an old reputation and being one insane little bastard.' 'No, Josh...it isn't even close to that.' Ken said in consolation to the distraught Josh. 'You know it is Ken.' Josh balled his fists up in a manner of frustration. 'Virgil Ambrosi just won the Platinum title, you and I both know I've got more talent than that vampire wannabe.' 'I know Josh, and if you think everything is so wrong then there is only one thing to do; fix it.' Ken stared intently at Josh, watching his younger friend pause and thoughtfully scowl. 'Yeah.' Josh sighed, running his fingers through his sweaty hair, his tired face showing the strains from his hard fought match earlier in the night. 'But...how?' Ken patiently smiled. He wanted to tell Josh that he didn't have all of the answers to life's every single question but he knew Josh wouldn't accept that. His bedheaded little buddy was far too fragile and emotionally distraught, to deeply tormented at this point to be let down by someone he held in such high esteem. With this in mind Ken did what he's done so many times before, he exhaled, tossed a silent prayer to the heavens and said what he hoped was the right thing. 'You have to discover exactly whats wrong and fix it by going back to the source. In every machine, the breakdown always starts with one small glitch, Josh.' 'Yeah, and...a tree doesn't rot overnight.' Josh prophetically stated, his hands becoming steady again as he rested them in his lap. 'Now the key is pinpointing where you lost a step.' Ken advised, causing Josh to ponder momentarily. 'I think...' Josh began, before stopping to further contemplate the situation. 'Even if you have lost a step, don't feel bad, I know that you are dying to return to your old form, but Josh...when you lose a step, that is a step that many will never have and you know it.' Ken spoke with deep sincerity. 'Core.' Josh stated slowly, barely audible. 'Core?' Ken asked, showing signs of apparent confusion. 'Everything has changed since I came back from Core. I went to cW and achieved a mark of 7 and 2, snagging the tag team titles in the process, but since I left...it's all been so different.' Josh mused thoughtfully, his mind playing back to memories of Core Wrestling. 'That is a very logical concept, Josh.' Ken nodded his agreement. 'Core employs the traditional square four corner Wrestling ring that you've Wrestled for most of your career in, save for your two years in GW.' 'Yeah, it's logical.' Josh stated, his eyes roaming across the floor as he remained deep in thought. 'Of course, that has to be it!' Ken was emphatic, happy to finally have the issue resolved...or so he thought. 'It took a while for you to adjust to the eight post octagon when you signed with GW, you just have to get reacquainted with it.' 'Its most definitely logical Ken, but...that isn't it.' Josh said slowly, shaking his head. 'We both know that I can do things inside of that octagon that no other man can...it isn't octagon.' Ken blinked in surprise, not even bothering to ask Josh what he meant, choosing instead to shoot a quizzical stare in his direction. Glancing up, his eyes shot with pain, Josh spoke. 'There is so much more to it than that. A lot happened over those few months. I was at the top of my game, still am, but the reason I'm not winning isn't because I don't know what I'm doing out there, it isn't because of poor strategy or anyone being better, just...my head isn't in it.' 'Why do you think that is, Josh?' Ken stroked his chin thoughtfully pondering his friends issues. 'Everything that happened, the fallout from Core still haunts me, Ken...' The weak and painful tone of Josh's voice in addition to a mere glance into his pleading eyes showed Ken the emotional anguish his best friend was struggling with. 'You aren't talking about being kicked out...' Ken's voice trailed off, he knew exactly what Josh was talking about and it wasn't the bitter loss to Hiroshi Ryusaki that sent him on his way. 'No, I'm talking about Espen.' Josh groaned, he had tried to block every thought of the child from his mind and it was obvious that he had failed miserably in that quest. 'Espen, Rosa and Jose Padilla...being exposed in front of the world as a worthless father and a miserable excuse for a human, it's starting to catch up to me Ken.' Ken stopped for a moment to think, to wait for the right words to come out, reaching a hand over he rested it on Josh's shoulder as he spoke. 'Josh, how long have we known one another?' 'Wow, three...almost four years?' Josh replied. 'Quite a while, and over that time, would you say we've became close friends?' Ken asked, waiting for Josh to answer although he knew what the answer would be. 'Absolutely. You're like a brother to me Ken, you've been my only family for the past several years, I've had no one but you.' Josh said, his voice cracking with emotion, his bluish gray eyes beaming with gratitude. 'Exactly, and we've been through a lot together. We've made one another bleed and then we've bandaged one another up, we've talked all night on the road about life and love and all the things that matter most. We're family.' Ken's voice was earnest and sincere. 'Josh, you've always came to me for advice and I can only pray that I've led you in the right direction, but tonight, I'm not going to tell you what to do.' Josh blinked, unsure of what to say, he said nothing at all. 'I love you like a brother Josh, more than a brother.' Ken smiled. 'But this is a decision that you have to make for yourself, this choice is all yours, and as your best friend I'll support you regardless of the choice you make.' 'Ken, I don't know what...' Josh started, but Ken quickly cut him off. 'I'll tell you what to do, tonight we party, and you think about what you have to do, think about your responsibility.' Ken smiled, his eyes full of pride as he glanced at the International title. 'Come on Josh, lets go hit the showers and then go celebrate, okay? It wouldn't be the same without my favorite designated driver. Ken stood to his feet, anxious to leave and Josh followed him up to a vertical base. 'Okay.' Josh smiled, it was the first one in quite a while. ---- 2:18 AM. August 9th. The Elements. Get away! Get away! The sad transition from the one armed scissor! Get away! Get away! The sad transition from the one armed scissor!, the speakers blared out, as they played 'One armed scissor' by At the drive in, over the wide dance floor of Ken's personally owned and operated club, The Elements. It had been a while since my earlier meltdown, and I had managed to relax thanks to good friends, lame jokes and the drunken antics of Ace Harding after numerous shots of wild turkey. The following was the most amusing yet the hardest to watch at the same time when you take into equation the simple fact that Ace has a difficult time keeping his pants on in public when he is highly intoxicated. In addition, a shower and fresh change of clothes had served me well. My hair was no longer sweaty and I looked slightly more presentable in a pair of ragged D&G jeans, thick soled black Diesel sneakers and a dark gray 'Blindside' t-shirt. Ken had decided to go GQ on us with a fancy Versace suit, black, made of silk and matched with Prada boots, he stood nearby clutching a gray goose in one hand and bearing a smile across his face, the ever beautiful Gunner Addison at his side, sipping a martini. Tonight, on a rare occurrence, I was not alone. At my side was the dazzling Casey Branner, phasers set to stun, by the looks of things. Her blonde-brown streaked hair cascaded around her beautiful face, stopping at her shoulders where she wore a yellow t-shirt, an image of Mr. T on the front, the rest of her lovely figure was encased in a pair of checkered white and yellow capris, matched by white flip flops. One look at her face was all the evidence you needed to find her guilty of being one of the most attractive women to ever step foot on God's sweet earth. Such angelic beauty was rare. 'Mmmm, this is good, Josh dear, grab me another, please?' Casey asked with a smile, finishing off her favorite drink; 'the high school girlfriend'. 'Of course.' I said with a smile, rising from my seat in a corner booth and turning towards the bar. ---- 'Isn't he great?' Casey said in more of a statement than a question as she watched Josh walk away, heading towards the bar. 'Yeah, he has his moments, I guess.' Ken sneered in playful sarcasm. Gunner Addison slapped Ken playfully across the chest before answering Casey. 'Yes, he's as good of a guy as you can ask for, Casey, just...be careful with him, okay?' 'What do you mean?' Asked the lovely Casey Branner, tilting her head to the side as she glanced at Gunner. 'I mean, he's been through a lot, and...he's still going through a lot.' Gunner said in a soft voice. 'I'm his best friend but even I will tell you that Josh has issues. He needs a lot of things, especially people. Josher has had a rough life and once he became successful he wasn't sure of exactly how he should deal with it, he's done surprisingly well, but he still has a lot to work out.' Ken's tone was caring and truthful. 'I love the guy to death, but one thing I've noticed about him is that people don't love him as much as he loves them most of the time. It's so strange, Josh never imposes himself upon anyone, he lets them fall in love with him, the problem is...they don't always love him, and I don't understand it, neither does he.' Casey was quick to catch on. 'I think I kind of understand what you're saying, Josh suffers from the nice guy syndrome.' 'Exactly!' Ken agreed. 'He gets kicked around so much that sometimes he decides to play the bad guy, he becomes someone fake, he puts on this show that people want to see. He gives them what they want, even if it isn't what he wants. The truth is, outside of the octagon there isn't a man more gentle or caring that I've ever met.' 'Ken and Josh have been through everything together.' Gunner said, her mind taking a trip down memory lane. 'Josh's problem is the fact that he lets things get to him, he needs people, friends, lovers...whomever, he just needs someone.' Ken said, a smile coming across his face as he thought back to Josh's earlier antics. 'If he doesn't have whatever it is that he thinks he needs for inner peace, he tends to go a little crazy.' 'How crazy..?' Casey asked, looking a bit surprised. 'Most of the time his anger is directed at himself.' Gunner pointed out. 'He tends to hold himself accountable for everything, Josh thinks he should be able to change things. I don't think he's realized that he isn't really Superman and he can't save the world, because we all know that he would die trying.' Ken spoke up. 'Just promise me one thing, Casey.' 'Yeah?' Casey turned her eyes to meet Ken's, she could see the sincerity therein by a single glance. 'Promise me you'll be gentle with the kid?' Ken pleaded. 'Of course.' Casey nodded, then smiled. 'I promise.' ---- 'I'm back.' I exclaimed, stating the blatantly obvious as I slid into the booth next to Casey, placing her drink in front of her, which she eagerly accepted. 'Thank you sweetie.' Casey said with gratitude as she leaned in and kissed me on the cheek. 'You jerks were talking about me weren't you?' I shot in playful accusation. Ken's features were quickly animated into a faux display of being flabbergasted. 'Me? How dare you accuse me of such treachery, Josh?!' Ken shook his head vigorously. 'I may have overheard all of the vile things these two were saying about you, but I for one, never participated in the gossip!' I smiled. 'Evil little trolls, all of you.' 'You take that back, I'm innocent!' Ken retorted. Casey leaned into me as she spoke. 'Hmmm, I guess someone doesn't want any action tonight?' 'You win.' I said, with a jesting sigh of defeat. 'You lose, but hey, you should be used to that by now, right?' Ken quipped a grin spreading across his face. 'Oh you, oh...that was cruel.' I shook my head in disbelief but at the same time I couldn't help laughing at Ken's insensitive joke, presented so boldly. 'Ouch.' Casey laughed, snuggling close to me. 'That's okay Ken, just wait until Evolution rolls around.' I smiled in confidence. 'I'm going to rock the world on Evo, you think Josh is content to lose? Hardly, the slump is over.' 'Good.' Ken smiled, I should've known that his joke was a motivational attempt, it had worked. 'From now on Ken, things are going to change.' I rapped my knuckles on the table as I spoke. 'I'm not going to accept defeat, I'm the most athletically gifted competitor in the industry today and I think it's time that I prove it once and for all.' 'What have you got in mind, Josh?' An inquisitive Gunner Addison asked. 'A lot, Gun.' My tone was serious, my smile fading. 'Not just for the octagon but for life as well.' I glanced over at Casey, waiting for her to chip in. 'Josh and I discussed things, we both agree that he needs to have some role in Espen's life. Tuesday afternoon we're flying out to Juarez to speak with the Padilla family and Espen's legal guardians.' Ken's eyebrows raised in shock. 'Josh...that is a huge step.' 'I know, and I'll be honest with you Ken, I'm absolutely terrified.' My confession of fear was difficult but at the same time, quite necessary. 'Josh, I know you're scared but...I'm proud of you, that's a huge step.' Replied a beaming Ken Partridge Jr. 'Thanks.' Was all I could muster with a weak smile, as I took a drink of my virgin daquiri. 'You never cease to amaze me, kiddo.' Ken said, his eyes full of pride. I bowed my head in humility. Here I sat on this beautiful August night, surrounded by beautiful people, people that loved me, people that cared about me. The only family I felt I ever really had that truly cared. But as I sit here my mind is hardly on the current subject, or even those surrounding us. Amidst the music, the drinks, the dancing bodies, the laughs, the smiles, my mind was miles away. My mind had drifted away to a little boy named Espen. I've never seen him face to face, only in pictures and my minds eyes, the pictures are still burnt into my retinas. Four years old, cute, kicking a soccer ball around the backyard, a smile covering his face. Destiny had a way of messing with our minds and somehow, my heart felt peace in this image. Perhaps I would find happiness, contentment and peace yet, and in a way I had never expected. I never thought my song would sound like this, in this sad life; in this bittersweet symphony of souls. ----
