Long time no see...

3 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/03/04 (It's early morning in the Drunken Leperchaun, no one is there of course other than The Nomad Donny J McNasty. The Nomad is sitting there with a cup of coffee and a half eaten Krispy Kreme donut, he is reading the Altoona Mirror's sports section. Mainly he is reading the Baseball section, he's reading about how the Pirates are basically going to suck this year. Right now it would just add to what seems like the ever growing list of complaints by McNasty. He know's he can beat Train and it's tearing him up inside. McNasty isn't used to keeping his feelings deep inside. Right now he feels he has to. He knows what Train is trying to do, he's trying to get inside his head, right now it's somewhat working. Train is trying to turn Hardcore Harv against McNasty, it's not totally working, but it is shaking the confidence of McNasty. Hardcore Harv is now on a two weeks long vacation in the Bahama's. McNasty knows that ought to ease some tensions for right now.) (Just as McNasty is reading his newspaper, he hears someone putting a key into the lock of the front door to the bar. McNasty looks up wondering who in the hell it could be, as the door opens we see it's a slim and tall female figure entering the door. McNasty has a look of shock on his face as he knows who it is. It's his ex girlfriend Julia who he dated for a long time before getting into professional wrestling. When they dated McNasty was still working on the family potato farm. McNasty stands up, with a look of being speechless on his face.) (The girl we see Julia is 5'10' 158lbs, she has medium length light brown hair, and hazel eyes and with wire frame glasses. She is wearing a pair of blue jeans a red sweatshirt of McNasty's that he let her keep. She has a half smile on her face as she doesn't know what his reaction is going to be. Julia begins to walk over to McNasty) Julia: Well, Harv was right that I could find you here this early in the morning Donny.... I know your surprised to see me here.... (McNasty's face goes from speechless to a half a smile as well as he's not sure what to think.) McNasty: Surprised I think is an understatment right now.... How did you know who Harv was or anything else for that matter... I mean it's been a while since the last time we seen eachother. (Julia heads over to McNasty's table, but is not sitting down) McNasty: Please have a seat... Julia: Thank you... Look Donny, I know what you been up to ever since you joined the SWF over two years ago. One of my friends told me you were on there, and I watched you ever since. I have seen every match of yours since that time. When I heard that Global was coming to Pontiac in Michigan I had to find a way to see you again in person one on one. I am friends with some people that work for the Silverdome, and they contacted Harv and I guess Harv knew who I was. And he gave me a spare key to the bar here and he told me what your routine is. So here I am Donny and we need to talk. I think there are somethings we need to discuss. McNasty: Yeah, I think so to... Julia: Donny ever since we last seen eachother on that cold January day here in Altoona when you dropped me off at the train station, I have done nothing but think of you and how much I loved you. In fact, I still love you Donny. I miss you so much. I made a mistake by letting you go. If there is a chance I would like to work things out... McNasty: You know something Julia, I been wanting to tell you this. It wasn't that you hurt me, or I hurt you, we hurt eachother and here we are again after all this time, we're talking again. I won't lie to you Julia, I still love you as well. I am willing to work things out if you are. I will give you fair warning, life with me is different, I mean, I am on the road a lot, I train a lot and and I am here a lot. What you see on TV, what you see in this bar, this is my life. Can you handle that? Julia: Donny when it comes to love there is no sacrifice that's too big. I seen all this time who you were to those fans screaming your name, I seen [detail withheld], (Julia begins to tear up) Donny, you are the same guy who I knew, you still have that big heart of yours. No one in Global Wrestling knows that, but I seen how you are with your fans and the kids who scream Donny J, week in and week out. You are still the same guy who I first fell in love with so long ago. McNasty: (a rare moment that we see, the Nomad is tearing up as well) You're right, I am still the same guy, just older and more mature. I'm still in love with you Julia. You are still my world to me. I think about you everytime on those long flights I have to make. I still have your picture in my wallet. Everynight before I go to sleep, I lay there and wonder what dream I will have about you next. Everynight your in my dreams. Julia to prove my love to you, I will walk over to the juke box and I will play our song... (Julia has a look of happiness on her face as McNasty walks over to the Juke box pops in a quarter, taps a few numbers and begins to walk away from the machine. He walks over to Julia and asks her for this dance, she gets up out of here chair and they begin to embrace tightly as 'Open Arms' by Journey comes on: Lying beside you. Here in the dark Feeling your heart beat With mine Softly you whisper You're so sincere How could our love Be so blind We sailed on together We drifted apart And here you are by my side So now I come to you With open arms Nothing to hide Believe what I say So here I am With open arms Hoping you'll see What your love means to me Open arms Living without you Living alone This empty house seems so cold Wanting to hold you Wanting you near How much I wanted you home But now that you've come back Turned night into day I need you to stay So now I come to you With open arms Nothing to hide Believe what I say So here I am With open arms Hoping you'll see What your love means to me Open arms (As the two embrace and begin to kiss the scene fades to black)
