The Cell: Transformations

18 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-18-04 Prologue: Just recently Jesse met a kid who was doing cocaine. The kid reminded Jesse of some things Jesse never expected to think about. In a deep concentration he spoke about them, not caring whether they’d effect whatever happened or not, and not knowing he was being watched… Inside his cell he believed he was safe, but also to do as he wished without being watched on. That would be a completely wrong assumption however, as Jesse proved he was dangerous and what he’d done in the last couple of days proved it. So when Jesse started to speak about the things he believed, whether they were personal or just rambling, the camera and the bugs that were in his room had heard it, putting Jesse in a position he thought he’d never be… again. Story Continues: The lights shut out at night, but it wasn’t nighttime, and Jesse’s lights had gone pitch black in his cell. Staring around the room he noticed the hall lights had been the only things on, and though it provided light it was barely enough to use for guidance in the dark. Clunk, clunk, clunk in an echoed rhythm began to surround Jesse. Inside the cell he was petrified. His eyes burst open as they raked around the room and down the hallway to compute where the clatter came from. There at the end of the hallway as the darkness was being replaced by the light, six men walked in pairs as one man stood in the front guiding them all in the same direction. The six men were dressed as guards, and the other man was wearing a business suit, his tie a perfect maroon with diagonal stripes going up to his neck. Peering at the man, Jesse wondered what he was doing. A faint screeching noise began to appear as they walked nearer, and then that’s when Jesse noticed his cell door opening up. His eyes closed, for he knew this wasn’t a pleasant thing to be happening. The cell gate finally stopped as it was not completely opened, and Jesse sat there on his rugged cotton bed wondering what he was going to do. This wasn’t anything he’d plan. But now, it was not up to him. His choice of being in the cell wasn’t his choice, but it happened anyway also. Shaking his head ‘no’ Jesse sat upright and breathed in, he knew this would be his last time in the cell… but where he’d be going next he didn’t want to know. The business suited man walked in and flicked on the lights with a remote he had in his hand. The lights flickered on as he smirked evilly at Jesse, knowing some sort of twisted doom was ahead of him. The six guards stopped at the door, the first two came in and handcuffed Jesse’s wrists in front of him, then his ankles, and finally the last chain hooked the two together. Jesse looked up at the man, he’d never wanted to be in this position before, but now knew he’d be and had to say what was on his mind. ’You’re making a huge mistake…’ The business suited man chuckled under his breath, for those words were reciprocals he took no concern of. The man gaped at Jesse, he knew this would be the final time they’d see, and wanted to say his ‘good bye’ wishes. ’Mr. Fristik, this is your last moments of confession. You may confess now and serve little time in prison, or you can keep your secrets to yourself and let it be watered down in court. I advise you take the first option.’ ’I’m innocent and I’ll keep saying it until the day I die, if I must.’ Jesse spit on the man as he stood their awaiting a confession that he got none of. The man turned and looked down, the spit had landed right on his tie and before he went to wipe it off he back handed Jesse and muttered something under his breath. Signaling with his hand, the guards took Jesse down the hallway they came with him in the middle of the six guards and being held by two of them by the wrists. The doors opened and then shut as Jesse left the cell he finally had begun to like. The New World The sun blared down through the car window into his eyes, yet his head didn’t move one inch. Inside the police car he sat up right near the left side door. In the drivers seat was a police officer. The car was being lead by two police cars, and then two followed it from behind for maximum-security reasons. ’This… is what I’ve sat through in my dreams. This same time of insanity, where my mind just fades off into non-existence rambling on about what will happen. I knew this would happen, but the time I knew not. It’s so annoying to know you’re innocent yet be told you’re not. Whatever happened to you’re innocent until proven guilty? I thought that was the way of law. I don’t see the proof, yet I’m here and I’m cuffed. It’s a pathetic use of words to try and prove that the United States has the greatest protection service. I spit on this country, as I spit on the man who killed Nikki. The actions that man used will never go unforgotten in my mind, and he’ll relive the same pain when I meet him. Wherever there is life, I’ll search, and whoever believes they are immortal, will know that immortal is the key point of weakness I can find. I’m not a serious man, but when someone messes with my head, I take what I must to show them they were wrong in assuming my actions. Arik Idol believed that the Global Champion was a pathetic man who held a worthless title, and so when I cleared his mind of such beliefs, he knew he was barking up the wrong tree. This is wrestling Arik, and I won the Global Title as I would win the International or the Primestar titles of Global Wrestling, fair and square, mixed with a massive load of hard work and dedication. I’m global champion, whether it means the best, or if it’s just a name, it signifies I beat the VERY best.’ The sun kept shining through the window as the five police cars made their way down a dreadfully hot and dry highway in the middle of a dessert like area. An hour after the cars had left they arrive in front of a substantial concrete sized building that had barbwire fences surrounding it. Jesse’s eyes widen to the size of bottle caps, for this is… prison. The Beginning of a New Cell The cell gates open all the way. The sliding of the steel clatters on the floor, as the rust becomes dust at each touch of the steel edges and the concrete wall. The pattern of numerous feet stepping echoes down the hallway as Jesse is escorted into the prison. Step by step his feet move, the sun flickers through the windows on the wall next to him as he goes down the elongated hallway. His hair still even though he’s walking, he looks just like a criminal. The facial hair, the eyes, and the long hair that is foul because of no shower in a week, Jesse is the mere image of a Hollywood murderer. Making his way through the steel gate he is searched for any weapons and then escorted to a room where he is given an orange jumper suit with his pin number ‘0824880’. Moments after changing Jesse is sat down in a room where he’ll be told why he’s here and for what reason. A man walks into the room as Jesse sits in a steel chair. The room a small space that a lot of nothing is crammed into for instance a desk, a few chairs, and a few file cabinets. Besides that the room is white, with no interior design and only a large fan with the same number of light bulbs as it has sides. Jesse sits calmly awaiting his notes of why he is here as the man looks for such files. Finally finding them he drops them on the desk and sits down behind it staring at Jesse. ’Why am I here?’ inquires Jesse who’s been wondering the question since he saw the building. ’This is your new home. For the next 3 months you’ll be here until someone who isn’t family or work related bails you out. That means your boss, your cousin, none of the little wrestler buddies of yours, or even you’re daughters guardian can bail you from this prison. You’re here because of assault on an innocent man without the actions of provoking. Normally it’d be six months, but being that you’ve already been in a similar environment as this for a month, and you’re being accused of murder, the maximum time for us to hold you is three months. Any questions? ’No,’ mutters Jesse in disgust. ’Okay then I’ll tell you what your privileges are. As you were in the Mississippi penitentiary you had the privilege to go to Global Wrestling, your job, without being in too much of a hassle with your current state. For now on, you’ll be escorted to your match, and be followed around the arena. This is for the people around you during the events protection. If that is a problem then sorry but being that this is Texas and we favor the Global Wrestling show, we want to make an exception and this is it. Now you’re able to go to your cell. Be gone.’ Standing up Jesse is ushered to the hallway by two guards. The opening of another set of steel gates begins, and then Jesse disappears down another long hallway. At the end he turns and a large steel door opens up where inside he meets a fresh inauguration of his life. The guards walk him down the large hallway that is surrounded by cells. The steel below Jesse’s feet echoes as the inmates stare at him as he is taken up a set of spiraling stairs all the way to the fourth floor, which is the top. Staring down he notices that it’s not that high at all. As the steel bar door is opened to his cell he notices it’s not all that different than his last one. Besides the sink, toilet, and bunk bed that is there, it’s almost exactly the same. The guards shut the steel bar door and then leave, letting Jesse be acquainted with his new home. ’Hollow walls of solid brick, it’s nice to see you again. I’ve been so use to this that my mind calls it home… It was only six months ago that I’d saw this exact same room, but in another part of the country. I’m going to be here for a long time. I my as well get use to it right? But god as my witness, this will not be the place nor my time to die. Maybe… Just maybe, I can survive, if I prove to everyone come Evolution, that I mean business…’ With that Jesse doesn’t even bother to stay awake for the long drive, the excess speeches, and the head on his shoulders that is tired of being awake tell him it’s time to rest and get ready for his match with Arik Idol on Evolution.
