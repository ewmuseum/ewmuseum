Beverly Hills: A Threat

7 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 7, 2005 Mike Biddle’s personal gym had every exercise machine imaginable scattered across its spacious floor. Biddle had been in the gym all morning making use of the various machines, at the moment he was making use of the seated row machine. Sweat gushed down his face each time he pulled the weight back. On this particular morning he had spent more time in the gym than was his normal custom. After his most recent loss had been added to his losing record he realized that he needed to train more than normal if he wished to keep his Platinum Championship, it would be most embarrassing to him to lose another title on the first defense. As he finished his last set of repetitions he didn’t notice that his faithful butler stood patiently in the doorway. Once Mike had finished with the machine the butler approached and with his most over the top British accent said, “Sir, there is a Mr. Rumson here to see you. Would you like me to send him here?” Mike raised his eyebrow as he wondered why Rumson would come to see him at such an early hour. “Send him in,” Mike told the servant. Moments later Rumson wandered into the room through the door that the butler had used earlier. An unhappy look rested upon the chubby man’s face as he took a seat on the bench press next to Mike. “To what do I owe this honor?” Mike asked his guest. Rumson removed his glasses and nervously wiped off the steam that had formed on the lenses with his shirt. “Do you remember how we discussed your choice of profession at my home last week?” Rumson asked with a wavering nervousness present in his voice. “Of course I do,” Mike answered. He hoped that this visit wouldn’t turn into another one of the many lectures he had received over the years about his hobby. “Well,” Rumson began, “this past week I’ve been viewing tapes of the show that you’re on and well?” “Well what?” Mike asked with an annoyed tone. Rumson took a deep breath and continued, “I was more than appalled by your actions on this show. You sexually harassed a young woman, threatened women with violence, were an accomplice to the kidnapping of a man’s family, and that’s not even the half of it!” After Rumson had finished what he had to say he crossed his arms and gave Mike a stern look. Mike simply laughed in response. “It was all very entertaining wasn’t it?” He asked; his thin lips stretched into a smile. Despite Mike’s humor about the situation Rumson kept the stern look on his face as he replied. “No it wasn’t, it was repulsive and definitely not the sort of things that someone of your social position should be involved in.” Mike rolled his eyes and allowed a small chuckle to escape his lips. “You talk as if I was actually doing those things. How many times do I have to tell you that wrestling isn’t real?” Mike asked; he hoped that Rumson would fall for the excuse as he had the week prior. Rumson rose from the bench and looked up at Mike. “I did some research and I know for a fact that none of the things you did were for show. You really did injure that Myers fellow, and he truly is an invalid. All I have to say is that if you continue with this nonsense you will find yourself blacklisted!” When Rumson finished his face had become a rather stunning shade of red, which caused Rumson’s chubby face to look like a tomato. Mike suddenly got a sinking feeling in the pit of his stomach when he heard the word blacklisted. His mind raced frantically as he tried to think of a way to calm down the curly haired man before him. “And where did you read those kinds of lies?” Mike asked with a nervous gulp. “I started by contacting Adam Myers,” Rumson said with a cold voice. “He told me everything I needed to know.” “That wacky Myers,” Mike began, “he’s always in character’never knows when to quit.” Mike laughed nervously. Rumson began to pace back and forth with his hands held behind his back. “I had taken that into consideration but then I talked to Myer’s doctor and found out that his medical condition is not a lie and that you did in fact injure him during a televised fight. So stop lying to me, and give up this silly profession. What do you think your parents would say if they were alive to see you acting this way?” At the mention of his deceased parents, a subject that he always avoided, Mike became very angry. He looked Rumson square in the eye and spoke with a stern voice, “I will not give up my hobby just because you choose to believe everything you see and hear on television. Now kindly leave, I have a show to prepare for’I’ll see you down at the club once you’ve come to your senses.” “Very well then,” Rumson said, “I see that you refuse to stop this nonsense and I have no choice but to put you on the blacklist. I wouldn’t expect any invitations to anything if I were you.” Rumson then turned on his heel and stomped out of the gym. Left alone Mike began to feel very worried. The blacklist was no small threat coming from Rumson, he had blacklisted many a Beverly Hills socialite in his time and once one became blacklisted there was simply no coming back. Mike sat down on the floor and silently cursed the timing of Rumson’s threat, the last thing he needed was a big distraction such as this hanging over his head before his title defense. He was now very angry; if he was going to be blacklisted then he’d give Rumson a real reason to blacklist him. Rumson wanted to see the violent side of Mike Biddle, then that was exactly what he’d get live on pay-per-view! -To Be Continued-
