Far from over

14 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/14/04 Brick was having lunch with Aaron at their favorite soul food restaurant, discussing the upcoming match Brick had. Brick had roasted chicken with collard greens and yams. Aaron had smothered pork chops with the same sides. 'So, this guy Christian Zenith you're facing, what do you know about him?' 'I've only faced him once so far, in the Graveyard match at Dark Rituals, and even then we didn't really face each other. After losing to Myers, I need this victory. Plus, he just got through losing the TV title, so he's gonna be pissed. I'm thinking I can use that to my advantage.' 'But your mind isn't really on this match, is it?' 'I'm trying to keep my mind on this match, but this shit with Salaok...' 'Did you really think Salaok was just gonna let things go?' asked Aaron. 'Would you have?' 'I was just hoping this shit between us would be over.' 'It's never gonna be over until one of you kills the other, or until you kill each other.' ---- It was a war he thought was over. He should have known better. He should have known that it wouldn't be over until either one killed the other, or they killed each other. The war between Brick and Salaok was a painful and bloody one. It all began in January of 2001, when Salaok's brother Jonathan 'Scarecrow' Davis bludgeoned Brick from behind with a crowbar. For months, Brick had been looking to get even, which also brought him into conflict with Salaok. In November, Brick would win a shot at the World title, which Salaok held at the time. Unfortunately, it wasn't to be. Salaok would cheat Brick out of his title shot the night of the match. A week later, Salaok would cheat Brick yet again by leaving the company. In the summer of 2002, the feud between the two men became even more personal. Brick's childhood friend Aaron Davis was kidnapped and tortured at Salaok's behest. The intent was to scare Brick away, but it only fueled Brick's anger, inspiring him to wage his own one-man war against the Harvesters. He was determined to go through them, one by one. November 2002 was the first anniversary of the night Salaok screwed Brick out of his World title shot. It was also the night Brick's sister Monica and her then-fiance Kyle held their engagement party. Brick's mood was somewhat soured that night. It was made worse when he discovered Salaok and Quartz at the party. Then came the spring of 2003. Bubba McCoy had fallen prey to poisoning. He was in a coma for months. Later, it was revealed that Salaok had purchased half of Global Wrestling. He used this new position of authority to run roughshod over the comapny. But, as always, his favorite target was Brick. He placed a stipulation on all of Brick's matches: if Brick were to lose so much as one match, he would be indefinitely suspended, until such time as Salaok saw fit to reinstante him. In the meantime, Salaok did whatever he could to get Brick to lose a match. Finally, Brick had gotten tired of Salaok's games. He challenged Salaok to a one-on-one match at Wrestlecade 3. Their match would be one of the most brutal ever devised: Hell in A Cell. After finally getting his revenge, Brick set his sights on a goal which had eluded him for so many years: the Global Wrestling World Heavyweight championship. A tournament was held to crown a new World champion after Chris Camero vacated the title. The finals went down to two men: Brick and Sage Sabbot. But Salaok still had one scheme in play: he sent front-row tickets for Dead Zone 2003 to Brick's friends and family in order to distract Brick during the title match. Brick then countered by barricading the Harvesters' locker room with a forklift. Eventually, Salaok and the Harvesters escaped their confinement and headed down to the ring to stack the deck even higher. Brick eventually lost the match. The next month, despite Salaok's best efforts, Brick finally managed to get a rematch and finally won the World title. Meanwhile Salaok had his own drama to deal with. Suffering the loss of his wife and child, he decided to retire from Global Wrestling and the war between he and Brick seemed to have finally ended... Until now. ---- 'Wait, aren't you the cat who was talkin' about forgiving Salaok?' 'Yeah, well, like I said, I forgave him for me, not for him,' Aaron replied, prodding his collard greens with his fork. 'That doesn't mean he doesn't deserve to die. And trust me, there is absolutely no way in hell I'm gonna let him make me or my family his victims.' Aaron then discreetly opened his coat to reveal the butt of a handgun sticking out. 'Man, are you crazy?!' Brick whispered. 'Sshh!' 'Sshh my ass! What the hell are you doing with that?!' 'Look, just because I forgave Salaok doesn't mean I want the son of a bitch near me, my wife or my daughter. This is for protection.' 'And think about that daughter. What if she finds that and it goes off?' 'I'll keep it under lock and key.' 'You'd better. No, better yet, I'm calling those bodyguards back.' 'Listen, I know you're concerned, Brick, but I'll be fine. Just concentrate on your match.' Brick sighed. 'Fine,' he said, reluctantly, 'but I hope you know what you're doing.'
