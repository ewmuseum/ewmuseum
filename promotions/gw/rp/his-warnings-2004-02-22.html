His Warnings

22 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/22/04 Ice nods and steps into the large black SUV. The engine rumbles, and Ice backs out, and pulls away, as Brandi walks over towards her Sting Ray. The open road appears in front of Ice as his black Expedition rumbles down the highway, away from the pizza place he was just at, and headed back towards the motel he calls home. The stereo in the vehicle is off, and Ice's eyes are focused on the road in front of him. The humming of the engine fills the silence of the SUV while Ice continues his drive home. The consistancy is broken as Ice's phone suddenly rings. He slides his right hand off the steering wheel, and grabs his silver cell phone. Ice flips the phone open and gently places it next to his right ear. 'Ice, what do you want?...........Ken? No man, I'm just on my way home from dinner. ' Ice tilts his head to the right even more, and holds the phone up to his ear with his shoulder. He slides his hand off of the phone, and scratches the opposite side of his face. Once he finishes scratching, Ice slides his and back around the phone, and tilts his head back up to its normal sitting position. 'Alright, I'm going to still stop by my place, and change. I'll be over to talk about this business whenever I finish. Shouldn't be more than ten to fifteen minutes max.......alright? See ya then.' Ice pulls the phone from his ear, and flips it closed. He holds it in his hand and slowly begins to set it back into the charger that is placed in the middle console. Just as he sets the phone in the console, the phone rings. Ice's hand just centimeters away from it immediately darts back down and pulls it out of the charger, and again flips it open before placing it up against his ear. 'Ice, what do you want.' A weird look grows across Ice's face. 'Me? Who is this? What do you mean you want me? Brandi is that you? Why didn't you tell me this before I left?' An arrogant smirk replaces the weird look on Ice's face as he makes his comments. The smirk stays on his face as he pulls into his motel parking lot and listens to the person on the other end of the line. Slowly he pulls into a parking space and puts the Expedition into park. 'Hello? Are you still there? Hello?!?! I'm going to hang up now. ' Ice pulls the silver cell phone away from his ear, and flips it shut. He places it into the charger in the middle console of the Expedition, and opens his door. The interior light pops on, as Ice slides out of the Expedition. Ice takes a couple steps away from the vehicle, before suddenly spinning around and darting back into it. Ice emerges back out from the vehicle, and just as he does the driver's side door slams shut, missing his head by inches. Ice head snaps to see the woman he met earlier standing in front of him. Her mascara is large and bulky, as if she had been crying. Her hair is dripping wet, as she stands there shivering. 'What the hell was that for?' She doesn't answer him, she just stands there continuing to shiver. Ice takes a step towards her. As he does her mouth opens and a horrid shriek spews from within her. She runs towards him, arms flailing, while still screaming. 'YOU BASTARD! YOU FUCKING BASTARD! I SAW YOU WITH HER! I SAW YOU WITH THAT LITTLE SLUT! HOW COULD YOU DO THAT TO US! HOW COULD YOU DO THIS TO ME! I LOVE YOU! WE ARE MEANT TO BE TOGETHER! BUT NO! YOU WANT THAT FUCKING SLUT! AHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!' Ice blocks her attempt of hitting him by grabbing her arms, spinning her around, and wrapping his arms tightly around her. Andrea writhes and wiggles, squirms and thrashes her body around trying to escape Ice's clutches. Her screaming has subsided as she desperately tries to get away from Ice's grip. 'CALM DOWN. What the fuck are you talking about. Meant to be together? I've met you twice, and to be quite honest couldn't tell you what your name was if your life depended on it. I'm not quite sure what your problem is, but you really should see a professional or something............................ Now if you calm down, I'll let you go, and be on your way.' Andrea's thrashing around stops, and she begins to breathe deeply, in and out... slowly. Ice holds her in his clutches for a few moments before sliding his arms off of her, and letting her go. Andrea begins to walk away from Ice, but as she does she screams something to him. 'See a professional?!?! That didn't help you?! It didn't stop you! What good is it going to do me?! Can't you see? I'm just like you! That is why we should be together! That is why we are meant to be! ' Suddenly she spins and sadistically stares a hole through Ice 'You may not see it now. But I promise you will. One way or another we will be together.' Andrea suddenly spins again and dashes out of the parking lot. Ice watches as she crosses the highway, until she can be seen no longer. A confused expression engulfs his face as he stands there staring into the night sky. FADE OUT
