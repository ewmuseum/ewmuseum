The Pendragon Chronicles...An Invite!

22 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-22-2004 Having defeated the young Juri in one on one combat, Pendragon returned home to his castle. He sits alone in his throne room, resplendent in golden armor, and his face is that of a troubled man. He holds the Japanese style sword that he crafted himself in his hands, and appears to study the blade. A draft here and there ruffles brightly colored red banners that hang from the rafters high above. The throne room boasts suits of armor and various types of arcane weaponry upon its walls. Swords are crossed upon one side, while battle axes shine menacingly down on any who might enter the room. The door is closed as now, and a single man occupies the room with the King. A small man, clad in a brown robe with a black hood stands by the door, a quill pen at the ready, along with a scroll. He appears to be a nervous little man, and Pendragon pays him no mind. He continues to stare at the sword. When he speaks, the smaller man nearly jumps from his skin? “What is the truest measure of a man, Jonathan” Is a man judged by his mind? His body? His actions and heart? What is it that a man can leave behind to let the world know that he truly was a man?” Jonathan, the scribe, rights himself after his scare, but merely shakes his head. Pendragon smiles ruefully and shakes his head?. “Then that is precisely why thou wilt be unremembered, even by thy progeny. The measure of a man is the effect he has upon the world. How many lives can one man touch” How much glory can a man garner for himself and his line? Deeds’All these things make up the measure of a man. Remembered is he who is able to accomplish more than the common man. Remembered is he who is able to touch the masses’Remembered is he who is able to change the world. The echoes of great men are heard for eternity, and it is among the greats that I plan to be remembered.” “Sire’the question is not a fair one for one such as I, sire’I am but a lowly scribe. How can I be expected to be remembered?” Pendragon looks up, his eyes brimming with what can only be described as a type of barely reigned insanity. His blue eyes burn and he almost growls as he spit’s the next words at the poor scribe?“Were thee the type of man who wouldst be remembered, thee would care not for thy station. Thee would rise above thy life’s destiny and make thyself into something great?” As quickly as the outburst came, it subsides and Pendragon relaxes in his huge throne. He smiles kindly at the scribe and shakes his head?“Sometimes the fire that drives me is too much to bear, Jonathan. I meant nothing against thee’I realize that thee art a lowly man, destined not for greatness. Anyhow! What have we on the agenda today” Are yonder petitioners restless?” Jonathan nods and unrolls the scroll just a bit??Seems as though today, my Lord, we have twenty people who wish a word with ye?” “I assumed that thee have taken the three who art most worthy of mine time from the group” I haven’t all day to deal with people’There are things that I must?.MASTER’And these things are taking precedence over all else as now?” Jonathan brightens??Of course, sire! And the last is someone you will surely wish to see. Might provide for an interesting?.Diversion for ye?” “Alright, my friend’Send them in.” Jonathan opens the door and a woman in a business skirt strides in, looking confident in the presence of the king. Pendragon looks at her odd choice of attire and shakes his head with a “Wonders never cease” look in his eyes. She comes and stands before Pendragon, without showing proper reverence for his position. One doesn’t act as if she owns the world when in the presence of greatness? “What is it thee would have of me, lady?” Pendragon asks in a flat tone, obviously displeased by the wench’s lack of respect. He leans an one massive arm on the massive arm of his throne and rests his chin in his large hand. His other hand twirls the sword, lazily. The woman sees the look and the sword and a little sweat starts at her brow and begins to trickle down her neck. She speaks with the practiced tone of a swindler, and although the lady is quite beautiful, her eyes are cold towards the manly Pendragon. “My name is Sandra Locke, and I represent the National Women for Women organization. We provide services such as legal fees and advice to female couples who wish to be married or to have children. Without this guidance, we”..” She is cut off as Pendragon nearly falls off of his throne. He cannot believe what he is hearing!! “Female couples” What means that, woman! Speak plainly if ye would value thy hide!!” The woman takes a step back, as Pendragon leans forward upon the throne. His eyes blaze??We help lesbians! What would you think I was talking about!” “Lesbians” I know not the word’Art thee like actors? Some offshoot of thespians? For Pendragon has long been an avid patron to the arts. Nothing beats one of the classics on stage’Be it Homer, Shakespeare and the like?” Pendragon has relaxed a bit, and the woman rubs her chin? “Thespians, yes. We seek a grant from your generous philanthropist side. Our’Players’Are most talented, and put on the most amusing sideshow plays!” She smiles as she delivers the lie’Oh, the cunning of women? “Thou art quite the cunning linguist, Sandra.” The woman almost jumps as Pendragon delivers this inadvertent play on words. He doesn’t know how close he came to the truth. “Squires grant, Jonathan. She is deserving, and she speaks well. Go in peace, lass??Tis nice, although surprising, to find an educated wench.” The woman’s face reddens, but she is smart enough not to “anger the money”. She bows and leaves. Pendragon stops her with a shout?. “Might I have the respect of a “my Lord”, just once before thee goes, lady?” He smiles as he leans back into the throne. The woman turns and shudders a bit. Her! Subservient to a MAN! What is the world coming to. “I thank you?””..My?.Lord?.” She says this last and executes a sloppy curtsy before hurrying from the room. Pendragon beams after her. He does love to hear that. Lost in his revelry, he doesn’t notice as the next man comes in? “When will the other nations of the world finally begin to send some emissaries to mine kingdom” I tire of these same beggars, Jonathan. Why, I should be able to garner a bit of resp?.Oh. Number two. And what is it that brings thee to see the King, this morning, laddie?” The young man, dressed in a sport coat, slacks and a tie, appears to be quite nervous. He wears glasses, and he continually takes them off and wipes them as for some reason they are constantly steaming up. He smiles wanly as he offers the answer??I’m Brian Danville, senior editor for the magazine, Mat Masters. I was looking to do an interview with you!” Pendragon shakes his head and smiles?“Mat Masters’Must be because of yonder tournament that draws nigh in the world of Global Wrestling. Aye, the bloodsuckers come out of the woodwork for this one’Indeed. So thou cometh here to interview thy Lord King. And yet, thee has shown me neither the reverence of mine station nor of mine lineage?” Brian looks puzzled’Obviously he did not do his homework??Station? Lineage? What the hell are you talking about? You are a wrestler’Neat gimmick, but a wrestler. What do you think you are?” Pendragon roars, leaps from the throne and swings the blade. His arm is a blur as the blade streaks past the surprised face of the reporter. The glasses, the only thing touched, fall in two pieces to the floor. “How dare thee question thy Lord King! What is this nonsense thee speaks” Wrestling is mine glory’Mine diversion if thee will. It is what I do to substitute for the battle that I should be allowed. I am Venneman Pendragon, descended from the great Arthur Pendragon’Rightful owner of the sword Excalibur, and rightful king of all that I survey. What does thee know? Thou calls me a wrestler? I am a high lord! I am king!!!” Pendragon sheathes the sword and grabs the reporter. He swings him back and throws him from the throne room. A thud is heard in the anteroom, and Pendragon paces back and forth angrily because of the smaller man’s insolence?“What does he know” I am king’Always have been, and will be until life is wrested from mine body?” He turns quickly, startling Jonathan?“Thee said that there was one who would interest me’Get him now. This session is nearly over. I know not how much more insolence I can take.” Pendragon climbs back into the throne and sits in his most regal way, chin high, eyes bright, sword at his side. A man dressed in a way Pendragon would understand enters the room, carrying a red feather. Clad in a flowing colorful robe and a red cap, the man almost appears to be a kind of royalty himself. Pendragon’s eyes light up as he spies the feather. The man steps to the foot of the throne, bows, and holds the feather above his bowed head. Pendragon reaches down and grasps the feather, and the other man rises? “I am Achmed Alljandro. The Eastern baronies have requested thy presence at a tournament of the joust and of the sword.” Pendragon’s eyes blaze as the man speaks??As the Western Champion, you are accorded a place of honor and the high seeding of this tournament. Would thee send the feather back as confirmation of your intent to compete?” Pendragon smiles and hands the feather back?“Of course. Long have I waited to test mine mettle against warriors of other nations’This will be one mighty diversion to partake in before I am to be crowned Master of the Mat in mine wrestling federation. Glory garnered from two separate fields. The mark that separates those who wouldst live forever’Sir Alljandro’Take the feather and announce word of mine coming’The Lord King shall arrive tomorrow.” Alljandro takes back the feather and bows again, and bows repeatedly as he leaves the room. Pendragon rubs his hands together in his excitement for finally being able to engage in another competition that he loves. Unexpected, from the anteroom Ben Davis walks in. Clad in shorts and a t shirt, he walks over to his smiling friend? “Ahh BEN!!! Mine friend! Pack! We go to Spain! A joust and swordsmen contest has been announced!” “Great. Always wanted to go to one of those”.Say, this have anything to do with friggin dyke who was walkin around outside like she was on top of the world? Maybe she just ate the worlds best tasting carpet?” Pendragon looks puzzled as the scene fades to black’He is innocent’Or just friggin nuts!!! ~TO BE CONTINUED~
