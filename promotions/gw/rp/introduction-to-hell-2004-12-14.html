Introduction To Hell

14 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-14-04 ::Fade in:: * Here we stand in a small dank little apartment that looks to be in some sort of basement. Walls are made of a foot thick concrete, Mostly chipped away, nothing about them could possible argue that they've been there for less than 50 years. There is carpet layed down wich someone probably layed trying to make a home out of the place at one time, but after years of bad housekeeping has just been worn down to just a thin strip of fabric. First things you notice on the worn away walls are blood stains but none new, they look as if they've been there for a few years themselvs. One single lightbulb dangles from the cieling tryin to light the sad excuse for a room up but fails sadly only allowing you to see everything as if it was 30 minutes before sunset at all times. There is practiclly nothing in the apartment except a worn out old wooden round table, about 2 foot in diameter and a recliner wich has no padding left in it, just a wooden frame. On the chair sits a man, largly built, wearing some sort of black pants with no shirt and a worn out pair of boots. He sits on the chair slouched over nursing a wound on his face probably from a recent fight hes had. A stream of blood comes running down from above his right eyelid where hes holding a rag of some sort to try and stop the bleeding. Damian sits in the chair thinking about his current situation and what hes just done. He knows that there has got to be something more to life than what he is living. Due to bad raising, neglect from everyone in life, and no happiness during the key points in a persons life where they just need to have something positive, theres just not much to support that feeling anymore. But what has just happened is a story for another time. Quickly Damians thoughts shift from that nights events to what has caused him to become this way. His past life being the flash threw his head reliving every wrong road he's taken in life. Every sad event that has happened to him, or that he has caused to happen to someone else. But most of all his lack of abilitys to for once in his life to do the right thing. It goes all the way back to when he was a child. Hes blacked most of this out of his mind because of the pain it brings him to remember them. But due to recent events it seems as if he doesnt even have to think about it, it's just coming to him. He's 11 now, he stands in the middle of a sidewalk about 6 blocks from his apartment, a blank confused and scared face. He stands on the sidewalk looking a a mess of 4 cars smashed together wich now look as if there just 1 big piece of metal melted together. He sees his fathers face smashed up against the windshield of one of the cars but it wont move, just blood running down the cracks of what use to be a sheet of glass. As he begins to hear ambulences blaring down the street quickly this memory fades away. Damian now 17, is standing in another run down old apartment, wich hes grown acustomed to over the years, but this time is different He is clearly very angry. With his fists clinched tight blood dripping off his right knuckle, He stands over the body of a man that looks around the age of 35 unconcious on the ground bleeding from the mouth with a black right eye but not dead. Damian can't make any sounds out all he hears is the dull thuds of his heart beating a mile a minute. When he begins to feel a tugging on his shirt, it's his strung out mother drugs clearly overtaken her, (She looks a little beatin' herself but not by Damian this is most likely the reason he attacked the man laying on the floor.) jerking and hitting him. 'LOOK WHAT YOU'VE DONE!!! HES ALL I HAD' Damian hears this over and over racing threw his head as if a broken record got stuck in his head. Suddenly this thought is drowned out but only to flash to another stage in his life. Now around 21 years old. Hes stopped keeping track because life really has stopped meaning much to him anymore, it all just runs together as one day anymore, nothing changes but faces. He stands in a boxing ring in a extremly run down old shack of a bar, Gloves on his fists wearing nothing but a pair of shorts he stands in the corner waiting for someone to step in the ring. At one end of the building theres a counter stretching down the wall with 3 or 4 old drunks, who look like they havnt been sober in roughly 4-5 years sittin on stools drinking the cheapest drink money will buy them.in the corner is a door wich leads to a bathroom wich has been used for nothing more than cheap blowjobs from ugly hookers from the outside alley, or throwing up everything in your system wich is probably mostly alchohol anyway. A tall man about 6'3' steps in the ring with gloves straped to his fists starring at Damian with a confident 'tough guy' look on his face. 'Come on you big ass freak' yells the man to Damian waiting for him to get off of his stool. Theres roughly 15 men standing outside the ring cheering him on all completly drunk but hoping for some entertainment for the night. The man starts to raise his hands to the crowd tryin to get them to start cheering for him and all being drunk they of course do. Damian after a few seconds gets up of his worn out wooden stool with a emotionless stare on his face clearly not caring what happens, not even sure why hes there he approaches the man. The fighter gets ready and puts his fists up to fight and quickly goes for the first punch and smacks Damian right in the face. Damian falls backwards a few steps but then quickly recovers as the fighter goes for another swing but this time Damian dodges out of the way then retaliates with a stiff right hand into the kidneys of the tall boxer wannabe. The bald headed fighter falls back a few steps and crys in agony as Damian flys forward in a fit of anger and smashes his fist into his opponents face 3 or 4 times so hard and so angry that everyone in the building could feal the rage pouring out of Damian with every swing. The fighter now lays on the ground completly lifeless as one of his friends rushes over and pulls him out of the ring to try and revive him as Damian stands there starring at the ground at the pool of blood he just spilled out of the mans face. Suddenly the memorys leave Damian. Recalling just a few of the memorys of his meaningless life have done nothing but brought a single tear to his eye. Damian doesnt understand that because he hasnt felt an emotion in so long he almost forgot what it was like. It however seems utterly senceless to him anymore anyways. Damian stands up out of his raggedy old chair, the blood mostly stopped dripping by now, and makes his way to the door. Looks at a broken mirror hanging on his wall at his cut on his forehead, a sigh and a look of sadness almost comes across his face as if he wished it was worse. The words 'I guess its not over yet' roll off of his tounge to himself, then leaves and slams the door behind him.
