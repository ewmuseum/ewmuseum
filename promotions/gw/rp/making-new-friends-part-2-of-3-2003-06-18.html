Making New Friends Part 2 of 3

18 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/18/03 L **Years ago movies like Bronx Tale and Goodfellas came out, movies that glorified and praised the life that has been deemed illegal and morally wrong by the government, our parents and by god himself. But over the years I have come to love the mob life. Granted I have never lived it a day in my life but it has always been a wish of mine to be able to say that I, Sean Price am a mobster. Now almost a decade later since I first saw Goodfellas, I, Sean Price am sitting across the table from a mafia don. I feel the little boy who gets the red fire truck that he wants on Christmas. My dream is finally coming true.** (The scene fades in exactly where we left off before with Price sitting across the table from Don Salamone.) “I’m all ears.” “That’s good Sean. A man who can listen is a man who can get things done’can you get the job done Sean?” “Well that all depends on the job Don.” “Indeed it does my boy. You see Sean, I know what it is you do, I have seen you on the television, I have seen the way you handle yourself in the ring and outside of it. You’re the kind of person I like to associate myself with. You’re a goodfella Sean.” “I’m flattered.” “As you should be, it is not everyday that I give people the luxury of my presence. Not to mention compliments and such. Most business that I attend do is carried out by those underneath me. I usually sit back and watch, rarely am I ever seen or heard from by anyone outside of the family. That is just how the family works. I am sure that in your own family things are done a certain way.” “Actually I don’t associate with my family much. But I do understand what you mean Don.” “Well that is not good to hear. Why is that Sean?” “Well almost a year ago I turned my back on my brother to further my own career. At least that is what my brother would have you believe.” “So then Sean what is the truth behind the story. Because as you know there are always two sides to every story, the truth and the lie, what is the truth.” “The truth is that when I broke into the sport of wrestling and passed up my chance to be a professional football player my brother promised me great things. He broke his promises and as far as I was concerned when he broke his promise to me it ended any obligations I had to him. And so I did what I had to, to make sure that I succeeded. My brother was no longer my concern.” “I see where you are coming from Sean. I myself have always believed that obligations go only as far as a contract binds. When your brother broke his verbal contract to you, you were there obligated to do what you needed to do.” “Good I am glad that someone finally sees it for what it is.” “Now Sean, back to the topic at hand, the reason I have asked to see you is because I have a job for you.” “A job?” “Well actually a series of jobs to be more exact. You see Sean you are a tough man, tough physically and tough mentally. I like that. I need that. I need you Sean to become a muscle man for me.” “So pretty much you want me to pretty much brutally beat people to get them to keep their mouths shut or just for no reason at all?” “Yes, you will never be alone. There will always be someone like Paulie or Tommy with you. If you can do what I ask of you, and do it correctly then there might just be a place in the family for you.” “That sounds all well and good but what about pay?” “I see you waste no time in getting to the point Mr.Price, I like that. You’re a smart man. Yes I will indeed be paying you. Your pay varies on the job. The harder the job the more money I will give you.” “I like that. But I already have a job, two actually. One working for the Global Wrestling and another for T.U.E, it is more than just an organization in the GW, it is also a business.” “I know Sean. I know all about your jobs and what they entail. But when it comes to the GW you have one event a week. That leaves 6 other days for you to work for me.” “How about you give me a few days to think about it?” “Well Sean in a perfect world that would be fine. But this isn’t a perfect world. You have already seen too much, you already know too much. Consider yourself a lucky man that you have been approached to take on such a big thing Sean. Not everyone gets the chance that I am giving you right now.” “Your right Don. This is a huge opportunity for me to possibly live out my dream. I will help you.” “Wonderful. Now let me introduce you to some of the fellas, and most importantly some of the guys you will be working with.” “Sounds good.” (The scene fades out as Sean and Don head out of the office and down the stairs to the ground floor where there are atleast 15 other mobsters waiting, all armed, all with eyes on Sean.)
