The History of Max Roswell Part 4

4 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 4 “Hey Max. Get over here and do your laundry!” my mom yelled to me. I was sitting on the computer in the game room. For some reason my Mom wouldn’t let me have it in my room because she thought I would go on it all night and then affect my school work. I didn’t see why she thought it was a big deal. I was a straight A student. I passed all of my tests and projects. Except I felt really sorry for my friends Mark and Brady. They were only above average students. They pretty much get C’s on the report cards. I really am surprised that one of them hasn’t failed yet, although it really would suck to have one of them fail. “I??ll be there in a second, Mom.” I yelled back to her. She was always cleaning our house. I bet you if we went on a vacation that the second we get there she would start worrying about the dust that will collect on the cabinets and all that. I just tell her to come down and wait till we get home to do all of the cleaning. Not to brag or anything, but we were pretty rich. My girlfriend lived 45 minutes away from me so every now and then I goto visit her or she comes to my house. I got up from where I was sitting and walked over to my bedroom. It was a pure hell. Clothes were everywhere, bed all messed up, everything was just brutal. I walked over to my bed and looked underneath it. There was a rotten sandwich lying under older boxers. I just walked back to the door, and out of the room without saying anything. “Done!” I yelled to my Mom. “I’m coming to check in a couple of minutes, and if its not clean you know the consequences.” she yelled. I did know what the consequences were. I was grounded from Playstation 2 and the cimputer for a week. I hated that. To day was just not my day. Every other day I would clean my room and all that but today was different. I was going to see my girlfriend. “Mom, I’m going to see Jessica now.” I yelled. “Okay, but no funny business.” she yelled back. I’m sure all of you know what funny business is. My mom always said that to me when I went to see Jessica. We never really do anything bad other than kiss, but that’s what all girlfriend and boyfriends do. I grabbed my coat and the car keys and walked out the front door. I got into my brand new convertible. It was blue, which was my favourite colour when I was a kid. I haven’t shown Jessica it yet. I really did hope she would like it. It costed a lot more than our house. I put the keys in the ignition and drove out of the driveway. I could see my mom looking out the window waving at me and blowing kisses. 45 MINUTES LATER I finally arrived in the outskirts of Jessica’s city. But then something I saw came to my attention. It was a car. It looked like it had just been in an accident. Then I began to think about something. Something that had happened when I was a baby. I saw a car, almost the exact same one I just saw. It was just hit. I looked into the front seat of a car, there was a man, but he was dead. I looked in the back seat and saw a baby semi conscious. I opened my eyes. “I think I just had a flashback.” I said to myself. I started driving towards Jessica’s house. It was right across from McDonalds. I got to her house but saw a car coming out of her driveway. It had a man in it, he looked about 15, the same age as me. I started to wonder. What was a guy my age doing at my girlfriends house. I drove into Jessica’s driveway and stopped the car. I took the keys ad put them in my pocket as I walked up to the door and knocked 3 times. The door opened and I saw Jessica’s face. But she wasn’t looking at me, she was looking at my car. “Like it” I said to her. “I love it.” she said back to me “But I have one more question, who was that guy and what was he doing at your house?” I said to her as I walked into her front door. “That’s what I’ve been meaning to tell you. Well, I kind of met a guy, and I really like him. I was going to tell you but I knew you would get mad.” she said to me. I was broken. Jessica was the only thing in my life that mattered other than my family. I felt like crying. But I didn’t want to cry in front of her. I loved her and now she wants to dump me for some other guy. “So your dumping me?” I asked. “I’m so sorry. Can we still be friends?” she asked me. “No. No we cant be just friends. You were the only thing in my life that mattered to me other than my family. I was there for you when you were down or hurt. I was there whenever you felt cold. And now its all going down the drain.” I told her. I walked out of the door and back out to my car. “Please Max wait! I didn’t want to hurt you.” she yelled to me. “Yeah, well you did hurt me.” I got into my car and drove away. I was driving down the street, thinking about all the stuff we did together that’s now going to go down the drain. I thought to myself, we have had a great relationship and she just leaves me with no real explanation other than her dating another guy who seems to be “better than me.” I got back to myself and when I got into my front door I knew me mom would come up to me and ask what was wrong because of the face I had on. Yup, I was exactly right. My mom came rushing into the front door area and right in front of me. She sat down on the bench we had there and asked me a question. “What happened, honey?” she asked me. “Jessica broke up with me cause she was dating another guy.” I told her. “Oh honey. I’m so sorry.” She got off the wooden bench she was sitting on and gave me a big hug. I loved hugging my mom, not because she was my mom but because every time I did I got a special feeling in me. A feeling like I was really going to be ok and not be hurt for the rest of my life. And besides, I’m sure there is women out there that would like me. “Thanks mom. I can always count on you to warm me up when I’m feeling down.” I told her. I walked away and up the creaky stairs, but with a smile on my face. I walked into my room, through my coat onto the bed and walked back to the computer area, which was pretty much where I lived. I sat on the computer chair and saw my screen flashing, it said that I had 1 new message waiting in my inbox. I clicked on it and it was from Jessica. It said she was really sorry and hoped we could still be friends. I replied with. “Friends” I guess that could work.” I clicked the send button and watched as it sent on over to Jessica’s house. “Max, get over here!” I heard my mom yelling. “Just a second mom!” I yelled back. I was pretty sure it would have to do with the mess in my room. I walked to where my mom was and saw a shocked face on her. She was looking into my room, and then fell backwards. I dove and caught her right as she was going to smack against the ground. I picked her up and got her back to consciousness. “Sorry mom. I just didn’t feel like cleaning my room today.” I told her. “I-I cannot believe what I just saw.” she stuttered the words out to me. I walked over to my bedroom door and looked inside. I was even shocked. My room wasn’t messy at all. It was the cleanest room in the world. My bed sheets were nice on my bed, no clothes lying on my floor. It was like a miracle. “I’m so proud of you Max. This is the cleanest room I’ve seen from you. Your going to get an extra supper tonight.” she told me. “Thanks mom.” I said. Me and her both walked downstairs and into the kitchen ready to make supper.
