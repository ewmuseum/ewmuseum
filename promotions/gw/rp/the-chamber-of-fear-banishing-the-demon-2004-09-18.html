The Chamber of Fear...Banishing the Demon

18 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-18-2004 Remembering made Pendragon shiver’Remembering something so hideous as to have only been a nightmare by most standards of men? Pendragon walked back up the ramp to exit the Global Arena after successfully defending his newly won Television Title. As he reaches the back, however, the arena vanishes and he finds himself sprawled once more in the cave that he sought to gain a sword’His own hand forged sword in hand, his title is nowhere to be seen. His small torch gives little light as he hears chuckling in the dark?. “Thee were ever one to worry about the trivial’tsk?” Pendragon’s eyes burn as he peers into the darkness, yet he is able to discern nothing? “Glory be not trivial, knave! I fight for honor’Glory’Goodness. What art thou that thee must hide thy face in the darkness” A coward’No less. I have defeated the likes of thee before, and I shall do so again?” Pendragon takes a step forward, sword drawn, and disappears in a flash of white light, the other “thing” laughing as the shot changes abruptly? ***********FLASH************* Pendragon, battered and broken, lies upon a blood soaked ground. Around him lie the bodies of his soldiers. He knows they are his soldiers as the memory of the fight drift back to him. Five thousand men he led to protect his land, and he was met with an army of fifty thousand. A conglomeration of the half men, half beasts known as Fangspawn and men. An army with destroying him as the foremost thought upon it’s collective mind. His men fought bravely, but in the end, it was impossible to overcome the ten to one odds that they faced. The lack of the famed blade Excalibur hurt the cause also. The enemy general, white hair hanging below a burnished silver helmet, wielded the dark blade Lucien. Where that wretched blade fell, armor was split and flesh rent. Many a brave man died facing that blade and it infuriated Pendragon still. Looking about himself, though, he was in no shape to fight any longer. Left upon the field by the Fangspawn who were afraid to finish him off, he was to die alone. Giving up was never easy for the young king, however, and he still had work to do. Looking about himself, he starts to rise, ever so slowly, grasping his curved sword in one hand, almost using it as a cane?“I know how the feeble feel, now?” he mumbles to himself. In the distance, on a hill overlooking his lovely castle, sits a black stead. Mounted upon it’s back is the wielder of Lucien. Clad in black mail that mimics Pendragon’s gold, arrogance seems to pour from the man as he lacks even an honor guard?.Who would be willing to face him? Pendragon smiles as he begins to lurch forward, each step making one hundred gashes bleed fresh blood. Stumbling over carcasses that litter the field, he tries not to see faces. These were men who fought and died for him. They deserved better; they deserved vindication. If there was any justice in this unfair world, they would have it when he reached the horseman. Stumbling up the hill, he finally reaches the lone figure. The man turns to face the King, and his mouth, bare beneath the visor of his ornately worked black helmet, curls into a smile? “I feared you dead, brother! Would not have been an acceptable fate for one such as thee?” Pendragon frowns as he raises his sword and points it at the mounted man? “I am dead already. Mine mind merely knows it not. Only mine focused will keeps me standing’Thee must atone for thy transgressions?” He says this calmly, but sternly, and the man on horse merely laughs? “It does not have to be this way, Pendragon. Mine master could heal thee, give thee back what has been lost. All thee must do is kneel before him and all sins shall be forgiven.” The mouth takes on a mocking, assessing look that the palest blue eyes match behind the helmet. With a roar, Pendragon charges the horse, feints left to dodge a sword thrust and lops off the foreleg of the beast. With a squeal, the horse rolls, and it’s rider bounds off and to his feet in a fluid motion. Loathing fills his eyes and he quivers with rage as he stares at the wounded Pendragon. Pendragon smiles and bows? “Perhaps it is not over yet, Interloper”.” As he speaks, the man rushes him, dark blade raised high. Pendragon meets and deflects each blow, but can get none of his strokes to reach home either. The battle seems a stalemate. “Give over, fool! I can see the exhaustion in thy eyes! Say the oaths and be healed! Rule with me, brother! It does not have to end as thee press it to!” Pendragon’s sword falls for just a moment and he looks at the man across from him. The slight hesitation is all the fellow needed. With a cry, he lunges forward and buries the sword to it’s hilt in Pendragon’s gut. “I would never rule with thee’Mine master would favor thee always?”Pendragon sags, but a smile crosses his face? “Sometimes to win, thee must be willing to lose’Thee shalt rule nothing!” The other man’s eyes widen as the end of Pendragon’s hand wrought sword sticks from beneath one arm, Pendragon having rammed it through his chest from one armpit to the other’A scream, and then the blinding flash? ***********FLASH************ Back in the chamber, Pendragon holds his stomach with one hand. He raises his mail shirt to find a small gash there where he was run through in the other vision. He sighs with relief as he drops the mail shirt back to it’s position, and rises to his feet. He winces as the memories of his dead soldiers return to him. Just because it didn’t happen doesn’t mean that he will forget? “I will mark each of their faces’They loved me and I them?” Pendragon bows his head and murmurs “Please watch over mine boys, Bob’If I have ever pleased thee make it so?” He raises his head to the sound of laughter coming once more from the darkness?“If thee thinks this funny, perhaps thee would show thyself and face me like a man!” The reply comes, harsh and laughing?“Not yet, Crusader. There is another surprise in store for thee, I think. Come, brother. Come to me and see what you were meant to be.” The mocking tone infuriates Pendragon, and he rushes forward with a roar to be engulfed once more in the white? *********FLASH************ The blade cuts through the flesh of a slight man with a look of horror writ large upon his face. “It felt good to do this’It felt right. The joy of death, the thrill of the kill. All this would please the one master?” “No’that was not correct. Killing without cause is just that’It is wrong. There is no point to this. What master would be happy that someone died for no reason?” “The blade has a mind of it’s own’Darkened with hate, Lucien feels like it needs to kill. It needs to taste blood. This is the master’s blade, and it needs to be fulfilled.” Once more the blade lashes out and fells a person’This time a woman in a darkened alleyway. Her last look is one of forgiveness, rather than hate? “Yes’Servant of the other. Good to kill’So good to end life.” “No. That is not the way. Servant of the other?” “Aye’The other. He would take a hand in matters if he had the ability to do so’Instead, others serve him as though he is the master?” “If he is served, is he not a master?” “Not the kind of master we follow?” The blade lashes out again and again, each time, an innocent looking fellow’A placid lady or a screaming child tastes death and finds the flavor to be unpalatable. Hundreds of places, hundreds of people. The only constant is the death’The joy of the kill. “This’Cannot be the way. Where is it that I have been brought?” “Thee still sees not’Must I show him everything?” A hand reaches out and it seems we are looking through a set of eyes. A door appears and is pushed open. A mirror stands behind it, and the shape in it is blurred. A wave of the hand and the image sharpens. In the mirror, Venneman Pendragon stands, dark blade grasped in his right hand’It is him, and yet it is not. His hair is white, and his eyes are more pale. Hate fills them, mixed with the full on insanity that this man has embraced. “Dost thou seeist now, brother” The master will be most pleased?” Recognition dawns in the eyes, and they seem to deepen in color to the deep blue that Pendragon was born with? “NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!” “NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!” Both personas shriek as the hand raises the sword and drives it hilt deep into it’s chest. The eyes look towards the blade and Pendragon smiles as? *************FLASH************** The same chamber, but a bit different now. Illuminated, it appears to be some kind of temple. Torches line the walls, and Pendragon stands in the center of it all, his sword held erect in front of him. The white haired man, some sinister twisted version of himself, kneels on the floor, holding his chest. Blood drips from his fingers, and he looks up at Pendragon, hate in his eyes? ??Tis thy fault! We were meant to rule, and thee tries to throw it all away! FOOL!” He spits, but Pendragon remains calm. Strange’How often does a man really have to deal with himself? Even if this isn’t really “himself” per se? “Rule I shall, but not as thee would have me to do. Bob has set me a task that I intend to finish. Lord Pendragon is now the Hammer, and I will root evil where it dwells. And I believe that thee has something I have come for?” He points at the dark blade the white haired Pendragon clutches to his chest. He looks up at the golden haired Pendragon and smiles arrogance at the man as he rises to his own feet, both hands grasping the hilt of the sword. “Come and take it, Crusader. The murals on the walls of this place were not wrong. I will rule with the dark blade, and Bob shall be vanquished as mine master should have done long ago?” “And yet, thy master was cast from the halls’He is weak’Bob rules all that he surveys, and the Hammer shall fall upon thee with furious vengeance?” With a roar, the man and the dark blade launch at Pendragon, and he fights them with a savagery that equals ten wild beasts? “I have waited too long to be thwarted by the likes of thee!” Dark Pendragon shouts as he slashes his blade, seeking the golden man’s heart? “Thee speaks such as I do, yet thy true identity is hidden’Some kind of Hellspawn seeking to usurp mine own body!” His own blade is a blur, and soon the white haired man is driven back as cut after cut appears in his armor. Blood seeps through as he gives way more and more. Finally, he is backed against an alter. Behind the altar, upon the wall, is a picture of what is actually happening. The dark Pendragon kneels before the golden warrior’Behind them, larger than life, a white man in a white suit stands with his arms spread?“Bob?” The demonic Pendragon cringes from both the wall and the golden haired Pendragon staring down at him. Finally, the demon launches himself up, and Pendragon’s sword buries itself in the thing’s breast. The dark blade clatters to the ground and by some trick, the body impaled on the sword begins to fade to mist. Pendragon stares as the thing changes from his features to the twisted features of some ill gotten beast. Face slack as it disappears, it still appears to hold malice in it’s eyes’Eyes that are all too human in the twisted wreckage of it’s face. When it is gone, so to is the blade that had stuck in it’s breast. The sword now ended with a mere two inches of blade above the hilt. Pendragon shudders as he looks at it and the tosses it away’He stands staring at the depiction on the wall? And that was how we found him at the beginning. Remembering the madness’Remembering the nightmare. Finally, he shakes himself to action and walks to the dark blade. He bends to pick it up as the scene flashes white once more? *********FLASH************ No change, this time, except that Bob now stands beside Pendragon, holding the dark blade Lucien. He smiles kindly at Pendragon and pats the massive man’s shoulder? “I knew you would not fail me’You were always the strongest?” Pendragon peers at him, and a single tear slips from his eye?“Mine men. Dead. Mine mind nearly broken’And for what” A blade? There must have been more! Why didst thee send me naked!!!”” Thee could have told me what to expect in here, rather than sending me unprepared?” Pendragon stops before he oversteps himself, but his eyes burn? Bob shakes his head, not unkindly?“To tell you would have meant disaster. You would not have faced your fears, you would have become them as you tried to hack your way to the sword. That is not the way, and you needed to see that. You destroyed a strong demon, bound both to this place and to the blade. The powers of the blade have been cleansed of their taint, and you can now wield it safely’Though it is not so strong as Excalibur now, it is still the twin of that great blade, and it should help to focus what you already have inside.” Pendragon smiles a little as Bob hands him the blade. He hefts it, testing it’s weight???Tis been so long since a true sword recognized mine grip?” “You’ve done fine without it for so long’Time grows short. The staging ground?” Pendragon stares at him quizzically’Bob sighs and shakes his head? “GW” You know who the fools put up against you now? Jamester’Sherwin. Once more, two decent souls for the hammer to fall upon. If I didn’t know better, I would think that GW is ruled by the dark one himself. You have to get back quickly?” “Will a doorway not open this time?” Bob shakes his head?“No. That was due to the power of this place which even now ebbs as it is without it’s master. You must take horse to the port of Jenean, on the ocean”.It will be a bit of a trip by boat’If only you would go to the airport?” Pendragon stares?“Airport” For the sky carriages? I believe in them not?” He chuckles as if Bob were fooling with him. Bob throws up his hands and shakes his head with a look that seems to scream “STUBBORN BASTARD!!!” He snaps his fingers and Pendragon is outside the cave, mounted once more on Xerxes. His hand drops to his sword hilt and he smiles to find the already familiar sword belted there?. “To Jenean, Xerxes. My, but we do have work to do?” ~TO BE CONTINUED~
