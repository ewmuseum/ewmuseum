An Explanation To You, My Love

19 August 2004

Date: 08/19/04 Closing the door to my bedroom behind me, I look into Cherish’s eyes? “Perhaps Leinad and Cassandra have some business to tend to. Best to leave them alone.” I watch Cherish as she nods her head in agreement. If only she knew what business they were about to take care of? “So?” “Sooo?” We both give each other an awkward look and lower our heads, at a loss for words. Even with all the magic and powers I possess, I can never find the right words when confronted with an angel. “How about a movie” Shall we?” She turns around and I place my arm around her and we walk towards the big screen television? “Which one this time?” “How about Ghost?” I roll my eyes into the back of my head? “We always watch Ghost, can’t we watch something else for a change?” I look into her puppy dog eyes, filled with sadness? ? ? ? “Damn it, I’ll throw Ghost in then.” I throw my arms up in defeat? She smiles, she knows that is all she has to do. Give me those puppy dog eyes and I’m done for. Without searching through the entire movie collection in the cherry oak cabinet with pure gold handles, I automatically grab the Ghost DVD from the fourth shelf, third row’and the second to the left. I know exactly where it is, and it has nothing to do with my powers. That is how often we watch it. Don’t get me wrong, I enjoy it, a lot actually. It is one of my favorite movies of all time, but there is only so much Patrick Swayze a man can handle. I swear, if I hear Unchained Melody by the Righteous Brothers one more time? “Honey, can you turn it up?” I sigh, I’ve won matches against the toughest individuals in the entire world. My red leather sofa. “Come have a seat on my couch.” “our red leather sofa. I unhappily walk over to the couch and plop myself down on the seat next to Cherish. Nobody understands warlock humor. The movie starts to roll to the previews. This is something I’ve never understood. How the hell can you have a DVD of a movie and they still have the previews? That, that is just ridiculous? “Fast forward.” “Yes, your majesty.” She giggles, she is so lucky she is cute. Very fucking cute. “Can you get that spot on my back, I can’t reach it?” “Where?” If I could point to it, I’d scratch it. “In the middle, down my spine above my tailbone.” She reaches over, keeping her attention off the television screen and off of the instant fast forwarding action that I’m just so good at. She scratches my back with her long manicured nails. “Right there?” “Not’really. Up a little more.” I fast forward past the credits for other Grammy nominated films? “There?” “A little more?” And a little longer, its all I need to fast forward through these damn previews? ? ? Thank you.” “You’re welcome.” She turns her head towards the television screen, without even noticing that I’ve fast forwarded through the entire previews in a matter of seconds. Its great to be a warlock, especially for the small things like this. “Can you turn the light off?” “All you have to do is clap three times.” She lifts her hands over her hand and claps her hands, three times’and on the third clap, I close my eyes, picturing the blackness inside, then opening them. You see, I can’t ever be mad at her, she is just too cute and gorgeous. If you are a male right now, sitting at home, thinking that I’m “whipped”, it’s because you have never experienced a woman like Cherish before. Trust me, the last thing Sage Sabbot is, is whipped. Cherish chuckles, for some reason, its hilarious and no matter how many times she sees it, she always laughs. She places her hand on my thigh, circling her fingernails on my pant leg. She picks her hand up and places it on my stomach? “SHIT! MY STOMACH! She sits up in shock. “What is that?” “Nothing, nothing at all.” She lifts the bottom of my shirt and exposes the wound, covered with stitches, that I marked last week. “Oh my gosh, Sage, what is that from?” A million things run through my mind? “From’my match from Evolution.” “What happened?” “Well, you see, I was wrestling Partridge and well, he kicked me in the stomach pretty hard.” “This happened from a kick in the stomach?” “No, that was after I scratched it on the ring stairs in the corner of the ring.” “So, the stairs cut your stomach?” “Yeah, umm’yeah, that’s what happened.” I can’t go on lying to her my entire life? “No, that’s not what happened at all.” “Well’what the hell happened to your stomach, Sage?” I grab her by the shoulders, trying to calm her down? “You wouldn’t understand if I told you.” “I just want to know. If Partridge did this, I want to talk to him. If you got hurt from the stairs, I’ll talk to Bubba and see if he can replace them with new ones. I just want to know what happened.” And she is entitled to knowing. She is my everything’but, I can’t. “I have to. “I did it.” She looks at me, surprised. “You did it?” I nod my head? “But how?” “I took a piece of glass and I cut myself.” A tear runs from her cheek? “But why?” “Cherish, there are things about me that you will never understand. And some things, you don’t even know.” “Like what” Are you cheating on me or something?” Even a fool wouldn’t do that. “No. No, Cherish. I would never love anybody as much as I love you.” I lean over and wipe the tear from her cheek. “I’m so confused.” “And so am I. I’ve been going through a lot lately.” “Well, that’s why I’m here Sage. So we can talk things through. I’m always here for you.” “And you have been. And without you, I’d be nothing. Just that I’ve been going through some personal struggles as of late.” “How can things be personal when I’m trying to share my life with you.” I lower my head and shed a tear myself, for I feel as if I’m a failure. She leans over and wipes up the tear from my cheek? “I told you Sage. I love you, and I will be here for you. Forever and ever?” I nod my head, calming down? “Forever and ever.” We both pause for a few seconds and she kisses me on the cheek. “Right now, I’d like to know everything about you.” “Cherish, I am serious when I say this. Please Sage, please tell me.” I lower my head, I don’t want to ruin our relationship and tell her that I’m a monster inside, ready to come out? “I am more like Leinad then you think.” “And Master Soma is a great man.” “And he is a vampire.” “What?” “I’m telling you the truth. There are things about us, that you need to know. We have kept these things hidden from you, your entire life.” “You’re a vampire?” She leans back, not knowing what to expect. “I’m a sorcerer. A warlock. A magician who specializes in black magic so to speak.” “That’s bullshit Sage. I don’t belie?” I look over to the video and pause it with my mind?
