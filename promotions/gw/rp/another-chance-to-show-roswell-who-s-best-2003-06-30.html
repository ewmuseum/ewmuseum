Another Chance to Show Roswell who's Best

30 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/30 Here I am, running as hard and as fast as I can, yet not gaining any ground. Sweat squeezes out of my pores, beads on my body, then rolls down to the floor. A quick look over my shoulder reveals that no one is chasing me. I knew no one would be there, but it’s a habit. I’m not on the run from anyone, I’m at the gym, pushing myself to the limit. Well, rather being pushed to my limits. I’ve been running here forever. “Run.” He said. “Run until your muscles burn and your veins pump battery acid... then run some more” These were his orders. A glance at the treadmill console reveals that I’ve run five miles already. I sigh loudly as I refocus myself and run even harder, causing my hair to whip wildly behind me. “One more mile to go.” I say to myself in between gasping for breaths that don’t seem to be there. The only thing remotely comforting is that this will soon be over. Too bad the same can’t be said for my training regimen with CW, I haven’t slept in the past two days. “Plenty of time to sleep when you’re dead Deacon” I whisper to myself feeling that I’m drifting off. Everything blurs together, things move, some things aren’t even really there. And everything is so far away. With insomnia, nothing is real. Every thing is a copy of a copy of a copy. One thing however will be quite genuine at Whiplash, the pain Max Roswell will feel at my hands when he’s locked inside my Nightmare Circular. From what I hear it’s been haunting his dreams lately, and at Whiplash his nightmare will be made a reality. Soon we will both be stepping into an octagon made with barbwire ropes and C4 explosives. There will be steel chairs scattered in it’s corners and burning tables surrounding the ring. A Deacon Holmes Deathmatch, my own creation. Also quite possibly the most dangerous match I’ll ever have to compete in. Since signing this match it seems like everything else has had the volume turned down. I learned it from CW, letting that which does not matter truly slide. Right now the only thing that matters is keeping Lucille around my waist. A strange feeling begins to bubble up inside me. Nervousness. I’ve faced off against Max Roswell twice in the past. Our first meeting was at Wrestlecade, the first pay per view for both of us. It was a cruiserweight title contendership ladder match. Neither of us won the match, but he eliminated me. My head wasn’t in it. It was my second match of the night and I still had one more to go, facing my arch rival, Leviathan for the Primestar title. Although I didn’t put my all into it, Roswell had something over me until two weeks ago. At a recent Evolution I faced off against Roswell once again, but this time it was one on one, in a table match. Finally I had a chance to exact my revenge. I beat him unmercifully from pillar to post in a match that most likely stole the show on an Evolution that never aired. I finished him off with a top rope back drop onto a table on the outside of the ring to win the match. Even though I had to risk serious injury to do it, I got revenge. Some how I wasn’t satisfied. It was easier than I thought. Not only had I beaten him, I dominated him. It was too easy, the win wasn’t even worth celebrating. It was like his head just wasn’t in the match. Satisfied or not though, I had won the match. Now I’ve got a chance to beat him when he’s trying. When he’s giving it everything he’s got and still can’t seem to gain the upper hand. To crush his spirit. With Lucille on the line there’s no doubt in my mind that he’s going to come at me with everything he’s got, and that’s just what I want. So that when I leave him unconscious in the ring he’ll wake up knowing that his best wasn’t good enough. I wonder if he’ll cry when it happens. Finally I finish the last leg of my run. I feel relieved as CW stops the machine, but I know him stopping this phase of my work out would only be the doorway to the next. It’s always like this. First something common like running or hitting the bench. Then it ends with me doing upside down chest presses or wrestling a gauntlet. Can’t complain though, it’s gotten me this far...
