It's always somethin'...

12 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/12 That has got to be THE BEST sex I’ve ever had... The water was still but that boat was most definitely a-rockin?. I mean, we didn’t even make it to the bed, as soon as we got within? striking range we were all over each other. We ended up doin? it right there on the floor and still managed to shake the chandelier. I can’t help but replay it over and over in my head as we race back down the same back roads and side streets we’d traversed on the way to her houseboat. The wind whips fiercely around our collective form as I navigate one sharp turn after another while piloting her Ninja with her straddling it close behind me, her arms wrapped around my torso. Her head rests in between my shoulder blades as her hands curiously explore the crevices of my bare chest. The warmth of her body radiates through my veins as we speed down a series of long and nameless roads en route to my place. Soon enough we approach Zero Estate and head straight for the wall of foliage concealing the mansion that lies beyond the wall.Soon enough we approach Zero Estate and head straight for the wall of foliage concealing the sprawling property that lies behind the wall. Just when it seems as though we’re going to slam into the wall of green, the bike plummets down, engine wailing, onto a steeply sloped hidden driveway that looks like it could be something out of an old episode of Knight Rider that was only show in Romania. Rocketing down the sloping tunnel created by paved floor and covered by foliage, the Ninja’s tires grip the ground below and squeal as the 900cc bike is flung around curve after curve. Eventually the tunnel flattens out and the sky can be clearly seen overhead as the walls of foliage have receded. As we further traverse the weaving driveway it begins to elevate once again up to the same level as the street beyond the walls. Once back up to it’s original height, where the paved strip of land leads can be easily distinguished as Zero Estate. We begin to coast into an easy stop as we approach the sprawling estate and park near the front door before quickly dismounting and embracing each other. This is the first time I’ve been able to look into her eyes since we hopped on that motorcycle... they’re still just as captivating as ever. Holding her body tight against mine, I can feel the beating of her heart synchronizing in tune with my own. All the while I can only think of one thing. This isn’t very good... I can actually feel myself becoming weak in the knees. Suddenly I push her body away from mine as though it were causing me pain. Staring fiercely into her green eyes, each of my hands gripping one of her shoulders, with her staring back at me bewildered by my behavior I feel a cold chill run down my spine. She’s all I’ve every wanted but I’m terrified of her... “I’ve gotta go.” I mutter, still somewhat shaken by the fact I don’t know exactly what it is I’m feeling right now. Slowly, I begin to turn away and head for the door allowing my arms to drop to my side. As the gap between us grows larger I feel a delicate hand gripping my shoulder and attempting to turn me around, I go with it, and it slides down my arm caressing each detail along the way before grasping my hand. She leans in and plants the sweetest kiss on my lips, lightly sucking as she moves away. Then slowly, in a breathless voice she tells me the one thing I’ve been waiting to hear since we met at my party. “Rhaiza” She said I’d have to catch her to get it, and catch her I did. Now I’ve actually got a name to go with the face, and maybe even a little bit of feelings on the side. Could this be the one? I find myself momentarily lost in space while pondering the concept before muttering the name myself if for no other reason than to see how it sounds coming out of my mouth. “Rhaiza, I like that.” slips out of my mouth in a haze of rose colored thoughts of us being together. Well, until I realize what I’m doing. Apparently I look pretty goofy because she appears to be on the verge of laughter when I regain my senses. We exchange yet another brief kiss and assure each other that we’ll meet up again sometime before parting ways. As I make my way up the two or three stairs in front of the large double doors leading into Zero Estate I turn to get just one last look at her before heading inside and find her eyes looking back into mine. We both smile, and turn once again. Stepping into the house everything seems normal at first but then I hear a thud against the hardwood floor and what seems to be quite a ruckus, and not the good kind... Suddenly I get a sinking feeling that something bad is happening, it sounds like fighting, and I don’t think John’s winning. Every inkling of a warm and fluffy feeling brought on by an early morning bout of early morning horizontal full contact genital exams quickly falls to the wayside before being burned to ashes in the wake of my now burning rage. Moving closer into the room I hear a familiar noise. The same one people make when they get the wind knocked out of them, except this time I knew the voice too, it’s John’s. Springing into action I dash into the living room to find three men standing over John’s balled up body kicking, stomping and spitting on him. Blindsiding them I scream out “Aww hell naw!” as I dive at them headlong, grabbing whatever I can on my way down. The man closest to me receives a combination of my elbow and forearm being smashed right into his nose and mouth, my momentum carrying both of us forward and toppling the two other men. I land on top in a sort of dog pile then glance over to find Zero staring at me with a surprised look on his face, he looks so goofy I can’t help but smile. “Just in time to save your ass.” I spout off with a cocky grin while standing up. “Again, I might add” I continue while helping Z to his feet. Dusting himself off John replies “Yeah yeah, that’s one less you owe me.” before asking “The fuck have you been?” Shit, for a moment I’d almost forgot, but then those same rose colored thoughts flooded back into my head and a slight smile forms on my face. “Out.” I say with a wink, then “Who are these bitches?” looking down at the crumpled mess of tangled bodies on the floor groaning, one of whom is bleeding profusely from the nose and mouth. Before John can even answer, the sound of a gun being cocked fills the still air, causing both of us to turn around. Behind us stands some fucker with a pair of crooked glasses on holding a gun. Needless to say, the sinking feeling returns... “Haven’t I taught you anything Deacon” Always take out the leader.” John states dryly. I can’t believe he’s trying to pin this shit on me. “Fucker wasn’t there a minute ago.” I reply. “Yeah, and I’m catholic.” he shoots back sarcastically, and much more loudly than usual. I wonder what he’s up to... The man with the gun clears his throat in an attempt to draw our attention but to no avail. This shit is personal... “Well call the pope, cause that bitch was not here when I got here!” I yell back at Z. “Well he’s here now, what are you planning on doing about it?” He replies just as dry as ever while giving me one of his patented blank stares... I fuckin? hate it when he does that. Once again the man holding the gun clears his throat, and once again we continue to ignore him. Shit, if I’m gonna die I’m gonnna win my final argument! My eyes grow wide and my nostrils flare as I become even more annoyed “Well obviously nothing” I reply glaring at him. “Motherfucker’s got a gun. What about you” Any particularly bright ideas?” I continue in an aggravated tone, half wanting to expose him as just as stuck as I am while also half hoping he has a plan and has just been using this argument to buy time. He begins “I was thinking...” before being abruptly cut short by a gunshot fired into the ceiling. That got our attention... We both turn to find ourselves looking down the barrel of a gun. “Sorry to interrupt gentlemen, but Don Vincent did ask that we dispose of both of you,” he says straightening his glasses on his face. Me and John look at each other nervously as he continues “Now without further adieu...” Just as I see his finger begin to squeeze the trigger back two gunshots erupt, but neither is aimed at us. One fired from behind us hits it’s mark dead on and imbeds itself in his head after burrowing into it like a rabid badger while the other appears to have been fired into his back and wound up penetrating his chest. Awesome... Two shots fired simultaneously from two different directions, shit like this only happens in the movies. Both kill shots too, talk about your geometry. A look over my shoulder reveals the shooter behind us to have been Victoria, who tosses Zero his pistol... which he decides to fire at the man once more just for general principle before aiming it at the second shooter who now stands in the shadows behind the bullet riddled body on the floor. I’m still giddy off the awesome surgery that just took place here but apparently John isn’t, his pistol trained on the shadowy figure. “Drop it or I drop you!” he yells at the silhouette, which begins to move slowly into the light. Zero is obviously in no mood for games as he begins counting down. As the time before John lets off a round grows near, the silhouette begins to look more and more familiar. Very familiar... Just as John prepares to eject a shell from his pistol I realize just who it is... Quickly, I lash out, swatting his gun hand with my arm, completely throwing his aim off and causing him to shoot one of his vases. Not the first time my actions have caused one to break... Z and I stand nose to nose as he stares me down wondering what the hell has gotten into me before turning back to the shadow blanketed figure at the sound of a gun hitting the floor and sliding toward us. Just as I suspected. Rhaiza... She sheepishly steps out of the shadows and walks over toward us. Paying her no mind, John then focuses his aim on another target, the three men from earlier who are now beginning to climb to their feet. Zero takes out the first two like fish in a barrel with a couple of well placed head shots only to be met with a hollow clicking sound as he goes for the trifecta. With no hesitation John flies across the room and bashes the guy’s face in with a spinning back fist before hoisting him back to his feet and slingin? his punk ass straight through a set of double doors. Victoria rushes down the stairs chasing after him as it’s quite obvious John has gone nuts. But then again, getting your house broken into and your ass kicked tends to do that do a man... With my arms wrapped around her Rhaiza’s slim waist and two handfuls of ass, none of the stuff that just happened even matters anymore as she nuzzles against my neck. But we are soon stopped dead in our tracks like middle school kids caught makin? out in the bushes on a field trip by a chaperone as John and Victoria walk in, Zero obviously shaken. That doesn’t mean he’s gonna get any sympathy from me... “God damn, man. Sure, fine, don’t leave anyone for me to fuck up. Noooooo. Take all the fun for yourself!” I exclaim, grilling him from across the room. “You see!””, I continue, motioning to Rhaiza “See this shit!” John ain't got no kinda manners, selfish fucker just takes everything for himself without even so much as offering any to his guests... What if I wanted to fuck somebody up? I mean, damn!” further ripping into him, receiving only a blank stare in return. Feeling that I’ve sufficiently expressed my distaste towards John’s selfish behavior, I immediately move on to my next gripe after plopping down on the couch. Then, signaling for Rhaiza to join me I pose the most important question of them all... “We got any food?”
