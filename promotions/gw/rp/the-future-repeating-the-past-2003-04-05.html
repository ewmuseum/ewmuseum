The future repeating the past

5 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/5/03 It was an idea that should have come into play a lot earlier but with everything around him happening so fast, it had simply slipped his mind. The School of Pain. A worldly classroom which had seen Salaok train many of the great fighters and great men of today. It was the place where every single aspect of Pain was taught and everyone grew quickly. It was just what Global Wrestling was now needing. Salaok had rebuilt the federation now, but the future would have to be made stable. And how better to do it than with those in whom he trains himself. The doors of the school now stood in front of him in the early morning of this surprising sunny day. His shadow long as it stretches great lengths along the ground and up the base of the door. He moved towards the doors, disturbing the loose dirt under his foot and moving his hand out in front of him in order to reach for the door handles. Two large golden handles on two large cold metal doors showed not only the wealth of this place, but the cold hard attitude in which it had inhibited since Salaok took over from his deceased father. Wrapping his hand around the left door, he pulls hard on it, straining the muscles in his arms as he slowly moves the heavy metal door. Inch by slow creaking inch the door swings open, pushing the dirt which had formed in front of it due to the wind storm last night. This was the place where Salaok ruled. His word was law. His knowledge respected. He moved inside the building and pulled the door shut behind him, letting out a silence-breaking thud as it connected with the other door. It was still early in the morning and the students wouldn't be arriving for quite some time yet. The building was cold and silent. Silent except for the heavy thuds from Salaok's feet as they pound the tiles and move towards his Head Masters office. It seemed a strange title to have for Salaok. Head Master. Made him sound old and renowned. It was a title he never liked and had quickly abolished when he had taken over. The office door was black with red frame. The idea of it was to make the doorway seem dark and involve ideas of bad things behind it for the students. When in fact, it was symbolic of the black heart of the school which this office had become. On the door, at Salaok's eye level was the name plate. Salaok Davis Pain Master. Originally the plate had said Head Master, but Salaok had scratched that out a long time ago and changed it to suit him more. He wasn't his father and everyone knew that. His father had always warned them about staying away from evil and all things corrupt. The school of pain was once a tool to fight against The Lair. But when Salaok was lured to the regions of The Lair, and his father killed at the hands of Quartz, there was never anything that would save this place from it's hellish existance. Reaching into his lacket pocket and pulling out a set of keys, he begins to search through them. Often he had wondered why he had never changed the locks and made himself a master key as it would always save him much time and effort. Riffling through the many keys of the School was annoying but he was able to find it quick enough. After years of controlling this place, he should be used to it. Taking the suspected correct key, he inserts it into the lock, only to have it stop half way before not going any further. 'Shit. Fucking piece of shit.' Salaok was never one to be great with doing things slowly and waiting. Speed and time where two things which had always controlled him. Always played a major factor in the way in which his life was enacted out every day. And also why he was quick to loose control of his anger. Again he riffles through the keys before finding the one he wants and again inserting this one into the key hole. Thankfully this time the key goes all the way into the lock and as he turns it clockwise, a click is heard. Normally the sound of the door wouldn't have been heard, but in this still and quiet morning, it seemed to carry through the coldness of the air. Salaok's free hand grabbed the door knob and opens his office. Stale and heavy air consumes him as he had forgotten to leave the inside window open to move the air around. It didn't bother him as the stale seem was much like death itself and he had become used to that smell over his years. Reaching around the corner in the dark, he hits the light switch and life is restored to the fairly largish room. His old gothic wooden table the major feature of the room as it sits dead center with everything else revolving around it. The huge dark wood chair with the dark red leather padding sitting behind it as all ways. The huge dark bookshelf, which seems to stretch the entire length of the wall behind the table is stacked full of books. But not just any books. These are books that seem older than time itself. Their hard covers with leather gave them a real sense of not only value, but of complete class. Books of theories, ideas and lessons of those who had gone before Salaok in both The School of Pain and in The Lair. On the side wall sits a painting which seems the perfect blend of artwork and framing all in one. The picture itself is of a dark figure standing in the fore-ground, who's features can hardly be seen, standing in front of a world of fire and hell. From within the fires reach forth the hands of those whom had past into the flames and given their souls in hope for freedom. A freedom that they would never receive. The eyes of the dark figure seemed to glow and were nothing but white orbs staring blankly into the eyes of any man who looks at the painting. Following them around as if it were alive and watching all that took place inside the office. It's frame was stunning and detailed beyond belief. Each side of the frame was a picture in itself, telling it's own story from history. A history not know to normal man. A history learnt only by those select few in The Lair. A history and it's laws which are to be passed down with every new generation. Salaok walks further into the room as his footsteps now become hollow on the polished dark red wood floor. Everything in this room screamed out wealth and power which is exactly what Salaok was. Both wealthy and powerful. Walking in front of the painting, he stops and faces it before crossing his chest and letting the words which had been spoken for centuries before him, slip off his tongue. As Salaok touches his forehead his mouth moves and 'Ateh' comes forth. Slowly but surely his hand moves downward towards the bottom of his rib cage and touches his shirt. In doing so the second part of the passage is spoken. 'Malkuth.' His hand now moving upwards and towards his right shoulder, he makes contact and 've-Geburah' sounds off his rolling tongue, only to be replaced by the next part 've-Gedulah' as he touches his left shoulder. Finally clasping his hand into a fist and placing it in the middle of his chest, he speaks the words 'le-OLAHM AMEN', before bowing his head. Seeming content with what he had just performed, Salaok moves around to the back of his table and takes a seat in his chair. Resting his hands on the arms of the large chair, he runs his fingers along the carved patterns, which seem almost like a maze with the amount of twists and turns they seem to take. His eyes lock on the door which he had walked through only seconds before and focus on the object above it. As if looking down on everyone who walks through the door, a large head and upper torso of a cougar is moldered around the doorframe. It's front legs wrapped down and around the side of the door and it's head and upper body sticking out from the wall looking down. For most this beast would mean nothing, but for Salaok, it was a trophy. He wasn't a man who hunted animals, he was a man who hunted other men. The cougar was Mephiston's loyal pet. Or at least he was his loyal pet until Mephiston was no more. Mephiston was the last man to ever try and take Lisha away from Salaok and he had ended his life in great fashion. His castle and everything he stood for quickly fell to The Lair and was consumed. The castle would soon become one of The Lair's many and his men would find themselves fertilizing the soil which now pushed up the thorn bushes. He had taken the cougar as a trophy from Mephiston, but the question which lay in his mind was, what would he ake from Silent Wind after that battle had finally taken place. Reaching forth and grabbing the only photo which sits on his desk without even taking his eyes off the cogar. Slowly his eyes move to the framed photo in his hands. Lisha and Darrell. His beloved family. His index finger moves over the body of his wife, seemingly tracing the lines of her body as he utters the words with death in his voice.'I will have you back my dear Lisha. This Monday, you will return to me.' But even with the thought of Lisha coming home, all that was continuously entering his mind was the trophy. What would he take from the soon to be deceased Silent Wind? His eyes moved to the wall in front of him and he could almost see just how good that black and red mask that his friend's son wore would be on the wall. The corner of his mouth began to curl up as a smile crept onto his cold face. He would take Silent Wind's very identity from him.
