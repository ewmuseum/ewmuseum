Oh My God

14 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 14 The door busted down as the police charged into the room. There was about 5 of them. They were holding there guns with there fingers on the trigger. They searched the room, I was standing in the corner. I was kind of drowsing off into sleep. A police man walked up to me and caught me as I was about to fall to the ground in a deep sleep. The police man carried me over and layed me on the couch. I awoke within minutes. I looked around and saw the police. “Hello Mr. Roswell. What seems to be the matter this time?” a police guard asked me. “My- my mother has been kidnapped!” I told him. “When was the last time you saw her?” he asked me. “Well, when we walked into the door she said she would go upstairs and kind of clean up that room a bit. She went upstairs and I went into the living room. I was watching television when this really weird commercial came on. It was of one of my rivals in wrestling. He showed that he had my mother tied up. So I turned off the television and ran upstairs and she wasn’t here. I started to panic so I phoned you. I hope I didn’t bother you.” I said. “Don’t worry. The police never get bothered.” he said. “Oh.” I said. “So, who was this man that was on this so called “weird commercial?” he asked. “Well, as you know I’m a wrestler in a fed called Global Wrestling. I now have this rival, and his name is Roz Blayze. He has been screwing with me for the last while. I’m starting to think that he was the one who broke into our house today.” I told him. “Well, do you know where this guy is?” the police man asked me. “Well, on the commercial, he was in a big black room. The lights were out and there were a few candles on the counter of the room. My mother was sitting in a chair tied up by a fireplace.” I said. “Well, only one place sounds like that would be where it is. There is another apartment building a few blocks from here. The rooms are usually dark with candles and a fireplace. I can go check there if you want.” he said. “I wanna come with you. I know this man very well, and so I think I should come.” I said. “Well, if you insist.” he said. I got off the couch and followed the police man out of the room and downstairs. We walked out of the door and left the other police men to clean up the room and look for clues. We walked over to the police car that was parked in our driveway. I got into the passengers seat as the policce got into the drivers seat. He backed out of the driveway and started down the street. I was positive Roz would be hiding there. We got to the apartment building and got out of the car. “So your positive that he’s hiding here?” I asked the police man. “I really do hope so.” he said back to me. We walked into the spinning doors and into the apartment building. Inside was really weird, cause like on the commercial, everything was dark and there was a whole bunch of candles lighting up the room. We found our way to the counter in the dark. “Hello. We were wondering if you could check if a guy named Roz Blayze was staying at this apartment?” the police man asked. “Just a minute.” a lady said from behind the counter. “Ok.” I said. A few seconds later the lady’s was shown behind the counter with a smile on it. She opened her mouth to speak. “Good news, a man named Roz Blayze does stay at this apartment building. He is at the room 215 on the second floor.” she said. Me and the cop smiled at the lady and left. We walked over to the elevators and saw that it was on the main floor already. We pushed a little black button which now was orange. The door opened slowly as we walked into it. Inside the police man pushed the 2 button on the panel. The doors of the elevator shut. Inside the elevator had a lot of mirrors on each side. The was a silver railing you could rest on too. The elevator got to the 2nd floor as we walked out. The hallway was all dark with candles on the wall getting held up by stands. We got to the room 215 and knocked on the door. No one answered so we knocked again and still no one answered. “Enough of this!” the police man yelled. The police backed up and then ran at the door and kicked it right off the hinges. The door broke down and we walked inside the room. It looked exactly like the room on the commercial. The fireplace was lit and the candles were too. We looked around but saw nothing but a chair and an untied rope lying on the ground. We looked at a table that was broken, there was a white note attached to it. We walked over and grabbed the note. I opened it and start to read it aloud. “You think you could catch me that easy. I think not. If you want me you’ll just have to find me. You might want to keep this note if you wanna find me. Oh, and I have your mom.” “So he does have your mom.” the police man said to me. “But how do we find her. It said keep this note to find him.” I said. “I don’t know. But I’ll stay with you just in case.” the police man said. We walked out of the room and down the hallway. We got back to the elevator, and there was another white note stuck to the door of it. I ran and grabbed the note before the door shut. I walked back to the police officer and showed him the note. It read. “Haha. So you did manage to find the note. Well this is another clue. If you want to find me, then goto the Connecticut zoo. That’s all.” “Ok, so should we goto the zoo?” asked the police officer. “Sure.” I said. “Lets take the stars because this elevator is going to take forever to come back up.” the police officer said. We walked all the way to the other side of the hallway and saw a door with a big Exit sign at the very top of it. We walked through it and there was a small cramped room with no carpet. There was only metal stairs going up and down. We walked down 2 flights of stairs and walked into the main lobby. We walked out of the apartment building and onto the streets. “Aw, it feels good to actually see sunlight instead of stupid candles. Now my eyes hurt cause of those.” I said. “Mine too. Lets hurry up.” the police officer said. We ran down the sidewalk and turned into the back alley where we parked the police car. But it wasn’t there! We looked around and saw it no where. We walked over to the exact spot where it was parked and saw yet another white note attached to the ground. “What is up with these stupid notes?” I asked the police officer. “Who knows. Its probably from that stupid guy who kidnapped your mom.” he said. “Yeah.” I picked the note off the ground and opened it. I started to read it, but not aloud. My face turned from a smile to a very sad face. The police took it from my hands and almost started to cry when he read it. He dropped it as we were both very quiet. “Oh my god!” I said. “That’s right.” He said. The scene started to fade.
