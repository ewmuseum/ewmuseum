Gripping reality as your delusions fade away.

13 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 13th, 2005 It is now mere hours since Evolution has gone off the air, two men have paid a hefty price for their moral inclinations and will spend their night inside an Emergency Room while doctor peck away at their wounds. Two men have paid for the mental instability of one man and the callousness of others and this thought has finally occurred to the man who thought he had it all figured out. He had taken pride in his exploits, his time on top of the business when even the very mention of his name provoked fear and yet tonight had been different. Tonight he had let his emotions take control. Bobby Brawler got laid out by Emoticon. Never mind the final outcome, never mind the carnal image of two bloodied and broken men standing defenseless against three inhuman predators. Bobby Brawler did something stupid for once in his life, he reacted...and because he reacted Radiant got blasted in the face with a weapon that was never intended for him. Because Bobby lost control a man he had no clue even existed laid him out with an attack that, two years ago, he would have seen coming. Perhaps all that time alone in a sanitarium hadn't served its purpose, perhaps he had grown complacent with his mental abilities...no longer in constant competition with the very best the sport had to offer perhaps one muscle had been neglected after all. His mind. As Bobby drove out of Vegas the realization that no amount of physical training could have prepared him for the psychological effects of self-imposed solitary confinement for such an extended length of time. In the past his opponents and allies alike had questioned his sanity, but it had been clear after the fact that his actions were all too sane if not filled with human decency. He had done what had to be done to get the job done, taken what was available and accomplished his task. He had thought that, in the end, it was that the evil deeds had added up weighing on his conscience. Perhaps he had been wrong, perhaps what had sucked the joy from his work had been the simple boredom of it all. Seven years of pummeling men and women into the ground, no breaks, no vacations, just psyching one's opponents out before knocking them out can become repetitive. He had gained so many accolades for his work-rate and willingness to impose pain upon others it had become a joke. Inhuman monster. Extreme Legend. Human Atrocity. One Man Stable. He had at one time or another used the very folklore that had emerged around him to strike fear in the hearts of men and yet now many of the newest generation forgot the impact that he had made. Through blood, sweat and tears Bobby Brawler carved a place on the sport of professional wrestling that few had managed to come close to and yet a little over two years had succeeded in wiping clean the stain of fear that he had left. Cocky, arrogant fools had even feared him. The ones who always came out on top and assumed themselves invinceable. They were scared not by the chance of defeat, but by the things Bobby would do to them in and out of the ring. Winning had meant so little in his career, he had defeated so many men that giving up titles had seemed only the obvious choice when he had finished his business. It was in the ring where he felt greatest, performing small miracles in front of massive crowds and crowded bars alike. Was it the attention or the admiration that had driven him? Or was it simply the challenge of impressing the single most cynical and hard-to-please fans of any sport combined with that of doing what many deemed impossible. Bobby had taken the cult of extreme to places no one had before imagined possible, he had innovated matches and customs that were now mimicked by lesser men with little knowledge of their origins. All of this ran through the head of the Former Global World Champion, his body relaxing and his mindset clearing. What had he done tonight? Chalk it up to shaking the cobwebs off and old tool, Bobby had done things he was embarrassed of and yet the fans had no clue he had not been at the top of his game. On a bad night for Bobby Brawler the fans saw him come out on top and so assumed he had little else to offer. That pissed Bobby Brawler off slightly, knowing the full extent of his skill was no longer known or appreciated. He had spent countless hours sculpting his body into a virtual machine, his unnaturally thick skull complimented by the callouses he had formed himself, his relentless training and retraining of grapples and holds and the weeks of studying tapes, every tape he could find just to ensure that he would maintain a level just out of reach from those who stood at the 'top'. Nobody could touch Bobby Brawler for seven bloody years...his reign was now in question after returning to a company where he had made history. Realizing this he pulled his car to the side of the road and pulled his cell phone out the number to Global Headquarters...it was time he returned before the camera in a much more familiar presentation. Some fans would remember, others would learn.
