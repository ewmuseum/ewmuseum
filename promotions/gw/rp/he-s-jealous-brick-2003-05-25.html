He's jealous(Brick)

25 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/25 Bishop: Trifecta! This could be it folks! 1. Brick: Took him long enough, fifty seven photo ops later... The ref slides in to make the count. 1... 2... 3! DING, DING, DING Just then the lights suddenly go out. Sights: Oh no. 'Walk' by Pantera blasts over the PA system as the lights flash back on as Salaok, Quartz and Sage Sabbot run down the entrance way to the ring, slide in and start beating on Blayze and Holmes. Brandi: And we should have seen this coming! The Nevermore ganging up on Holmes and Blayze! Brick: Remember the bigger fish I was talking about? With that, Brick takes off his headset and slides into the ring with a steel chair STOP “Ya see that shit man!”” roars Deacon Holmes as he rises from his seat, muscles tensed, quickly and repeatedly tapping the frozen image from Evolution on the television screen. CW laughs hysterically at his obviously upset nephew who is now pacing back and forth in front of the television. He shakes his head as Deacon stops pacing briefly to start the tape again. “See what I mean!” See what I mean!” I was just about to get in that ass but no!” He whacks Sabbot and then Salaok and then gives Quartz a few whacks. The Nevermore begin to spill out of the ring. Brandi: What do you’oh wait! Bishop: What the hell! Brandi: Brick getting into the thick of things! Chair shots all around! The Nevermore backpedal up the ramp. Quartz screams 'You're a dead man, Brick!' Holmes stops the tape again still pacing rapidly no more than a foot away from the screen. “This is the shit I’m talkin bout unc, son of a bitch just had to cut into my spotlight! He’s jealous! That’s all it is, he’s just jealous! I don’t believe this shit man!” Holmes throws down the remote control causing it to shatter to pieces on contact. He stomps on it once for good measure on his way into the hall as he storms out of the room. “Take me under his wing!” That motherfucker wants to be me!” echoes Holmes from the hallway. “That boy’s gonna explode one day” mutters to himself as he cuts the end off his cigar and lights it before returning to his newspaper. “And another thing!” exclaims Holmes as he stomps back into the room causing CW to drop his paper in annoyance and throw his head back into the cushion of the recliner. “If that clown thinks he’s gonna make me respect him he’s got another thing comin! I’m the new face of Global Wrestling and he’s the only person there that doesn’t know it!” “He said you might have what it takes to be great D, what more do you want from the guy?” reasons CW. “Bullshit!” shoots back Deacon. “He just doesn’t want everyone to see how jealous of me he is! He saw how I handled the Nevermore last week and realized I’d done something he couldn’t. I beat them, by myself might I add!” “Roz Blayze helped you.” CW says. “The hell he did!” screams Holmes. “All he did was lay there in the corner while I did all the work! And watch him try and blame his loss to me on his son dying!” Holmes storms out of the room once more in disgust, slamming the door on his way out. “Kinda reminds me of me when I was his age... cept I was better lookin?” mumbles CW to himself before rising from his chair and leaving the room. He walks down the hall to find Holmes pacing back and forth in the living room. “You know, I’ve got just the thing for you.” says CW as he enters the room. “Unless you’ve got an unregistered gun and Brick tied up in the trunk of your car, you’ve got nothing for me” growls Deacon. “No, I’ve got something much better than that.” replies CW as he begins to pack a small duffle bag. “It’s time for a little training session, we need to work on those moves of yours.” Holmes sneers at CW. His lip begins to curl before he speaks. “How can we work on my moves” You didn’t train me.” “A great teacher inspires those he never taught” echoes from the hall CW has just walked into to grab his clipboard. “I don’t believe this shit” moans Holmes as he plops down on his sofa while waiting for CW to finish packing his “training equipment.”
