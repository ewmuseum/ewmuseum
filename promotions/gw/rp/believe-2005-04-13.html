Believe

13 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/13/05 Fred Grest is sitting in the corner of his bedroom his head leaned up against the wall. He stares up at the ceiling wondering if Chill was right. Hell, if his father was right to tell him to get out of wrestling. '45' by Shinedown plays as the man who just lossed to Elia Smith sits there. He's wearing black jeans, no socks, no shirt. He wasn't sure if he could be Elia Smith. He's not sure if he can beat Durian David Orthane this week. Funny how words can effect a man. He hasn't been the confident arrogant bastard in a long time. His attitude changed upon entering WMW and he doesn't want to go back. He didn't want to wear a mask of arrogance anymore. We all hide behind a mask don't we? We're all here pretending to be something we're not right? Just then a tall well built man walks in with blue hair. Its Scott Vyper wearing his favorite shirt from CWA that reads 'Bow to the man who signs your checks'. He owned the fed obviously. The man looks at me in disgust seeing a mere shell of the man that carried his CWA Extreme Championship. He shakes his head and turns off the radio. 'How did you get in here' Grest replied. 'You have one of those fake rock key holders things remember?' Vyper said slightly annoyed. Grest just wants to be left alone at the moment. 'Why are you here?' I said sharply and angered. 'Pretty much the same reason as home boy Chill' Grest finally makes eye contact with something other then the ceiling. He looks directly at Vyper's face and says 'You want to know why I left WMW? I don't understand where the two of you get off questioning me. Chill left after one simple dark match. You left several times! You even attempted coming here and was stuck in Global Underground as I recall.' Vyper tilts his head for a moment and straightens it out. 'Circumstances were different for us. You were at least somewhat liked backstage and reaching your prime not passing it.' Grest just blankly stares at Vyper and says 'I'll still be reaching my prime just in another fed.' 'What makes you think you'll amount to anything. I know I'm echoing the conversation between you and Chill last week but you were just average. I couldn't even make it to the roster and I'm a former world champion in multiple promotions.' The Extreme One sighs and mutters 'I'm a future world champion' Scott Vyper smirks 'With a career moves like this I doubt it. You keep moving to harder organizations. The worse you seem to do in a promotion instead of taking a step down you take a step up. You're logic is flawed on this one Grest. Freddy rolls his eyes and responds 'I've gotten better since my 1st match which by the way I won the WECW Hardcore Title. Shortly after it closed it doors and I met you and made me the wrestler I am today. I know how to cheat I have too and I know all the backstage workings thanks to you. If it wasn't for you not believing I could make it WMW I wouldn't have joined. I enjoyed my stay there because its where I pinned you. I will make it while you and Chill watch Evolution eating crow!' The blue haired freak looks at the man sitting in the corner and he can tell that Grest has doubt in his eyes. 'Is that why you're sitting in the corner ever so confidently?' Grest's head leans back against the wall 'Get Out' 'Yes, ever so confident I'm sure Durian David Orthane and the rest of Global Wrestling won't see right threw that.' 'Get Out' Grest said once again. 'Final words for ya Freddy... Never bite off more then you can chew' 'Thanks man don't need it' Everyone's favorite blue haired freak leaves and Grest sits and wonders yet again. He wonders how will this change my career? How has changing feds ever helped hiscareer? His young man's heart can't help but believe what nobody else will. Nobody knows what he believes... Nobody in Global Wrestling, Wrestling Midwest, or even the people around me believe he will succeed.He always made a living on proving people wrong maybe I got back to that. The last time He did that he beat Kenji Lee in a kindo stick match. That was about 9 months ago and with the ways seems went as of late it seems like eons ago. He has failed every other time since then. Justin Evers, Syberus, and Shadow all said he would fail each and every time against them and so far they have been right. It's time to show them and everyone else that Fred Grest can win. It's time to stop thinking and start doing.
