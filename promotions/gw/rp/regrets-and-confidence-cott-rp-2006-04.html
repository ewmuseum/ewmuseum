Regrets and Confidence (COTT RP)

April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April City Morgue New York City, New York The linoleum flooring had been ignored for years, allowing the continual trampling of filth from the outside to build up until it was part of the permanent flooring atop the tacky green walkways. The chairs and couches had seen better days as well, showing the effects of twenty plus years of holding grieving families. Now, as the overworked air conditioner blew the chilled air down onto his face he reflected back on all the years he had spent wasting the time they had together. The details were still sketchy, but from what his friend Andre had explained over the phone a car accident had caused all this pain and grief. Probably some God damned son of a bitch talking on his cell phone like an idiot. The bitter taste in his mouth wouldn’t leave. He had refused her every attempt to make their relationship anything beyond the casual, pushed back until ultimately she had accepted the arrangement. Then, when she had moved past him because he could not be there for her, he remembered the pain and anguish at his loss of her forever. He had been a coward, too afraid to trust anyone anymore, and now he was alone again. He had begun telling himself for a long time now that everything he had done was designed to protect her from his enemies. He didn’t want her targeted as a way for people to get to him, or used against him as leverage so he would have to make an impossibly difficult choice. By keeping her at arms length, he removed her from the scope of Sid and his followers rage. All of it was a lie. Jake had pushed Rebecca away because he was scared of being in a relationship with her for any extended period. Jake had come to realization that he was a selfish man, but even deeper down he was a scared man. Jake didn’t see how anyone could ever love him. He had done things in his life he wasn’t proud of, and then there were the things he felt other people couldn’t understand. He didn’t trust anyone except for his family and a few friends. I’m not sure I trust all of them completely? Not after what Veston did? All of his trepidation led him to this room, where he was forced to face the truth. He hadn’t been there to protect her, and so now she was gone. If he had been there he might have saved her, brought her to Texas where there were enough resources to protect her’then he could have been with her in every way. Instead, that night he simply ran away from her, afraid to see the anger in her eyes when she told him she had moved on, and how dare he be angry about it. He had been a coward, and now was no different than before. He kept staring at the gray metal door that would take him up the stairs to the street, and he wanted to run away from this. If he never saw her like this, then she wasn’t really gone. He could ignore the truth, run away from it for now. The doors to the freezer opened and a man in a white coat came out, “Mr.Hix?” Jake stood up, staring down at the shorter man. He was slim, standing on a frame barely five and a half feet tall. His voice carried the British accent from the schools his parents had sent him to in India. His receding hairline led down a huge forehead to the wire rimmed glasses. Jake offered his hand, “Yes.” They shook as he spoke, “I am Dr. Bakshi. I understand you want to view one of our’patients.” “Yes, I would.” Jake said meekly. Dr. Bakshi nodded, “I can understand that, but we have already made an identification of her thanks to her fianc”. How did you know the victim?” “We were’very close for a number of years.” “I understand there will be a service held in just a few days Mr. Hix.” Dr. Bakshi offered. “Perhaps it would be more appropriate for you to see her there.” “Dr. Bakshi, Becca and I had a relationship that not all of her family and friends approved of for her. She later left me for her fianc”, and I don’t want to cause them any more pain than they are in right now. I just?” Jake was lost, searching for the right words to explain the cacophony of emotions roiling inside of his chest. There was nothing that he could say to adequately express what he was feeling, but he had to make this doctor understand that he just needed to see her, to make himself face his own inadequacies and that she was really? “Doctor, I just need to see her to get some closure of my own. I don’t want to intrude on anyone or anything else, I just need to know. Can you understand?” Jake was searching his black eyes, looking for understanding of what he needed. Dr. Bakshi nodded, “I understand. Come with me.” Jake followed as the doctor swiped his ID badge across the reader, allowing the freezer doors to swing open. The mist spilled out from the bottom of the doorway as they entered. Inside, everything was cold thanks to the freezer, but it also seemed so indifferent with the white tile on the walls and the white linoleum on the floor carrying the perfume of strong bleach. He felt the chill run down his spine and out through his arms as they walked down half the hallway. Dr. Bakshi pushed the door open for him. There, in the middle of the room there was a body concealed beneath the white sheet. The fabric thick enough to prevent him from seeing anything, but he didn’t see anything else except the table now. He was locked in, fear wafting out of every pore in his skin. “I have to explain to you sir, this will not be what you would normally expect. Due to the circumstances of her death, we did perform an autopsy.” The doctor was used to people being effected by such news. Seeing a loved one cut up could cause almost as much distress as learning of the passing. However, the look this monster of a man shot his way was not concern over her physical body. “This is standard in cases of a suspicious nature.” “Why was this suspicious?” Jake demanded. Now his mind was racing, trying to connect facts where he had none. Dr. Bakshi realized he should not have brought this up, “Nothing, don’t worry about that right now.” “No, you brought it up.” Jake took a step forward. He wasn’t going to do anything to the doctor, but he realized his size could be an advantage. Towering over him and being this close, the doctor would want to ensure he didn’t cause him to become agitated. “Tell me what circumstances you’re talking about.” Dr, Bakshi took a step back. He wondered if any of the low paid security guards could handle this man if he became violent. “Originally, this was considered a hit and run, but the subsequent investigation found some disturbing evidence. The car that struck her never slowed down, and actually sped up to strike her. The police are investigating, and I know the detectives on the case. They won’t stop until they find out who did this to her. Such a tragedy, a lovely young woman and her child run down like this.” “Her child?” “Yes, she was five months pregnant.” Dr. Bakshi said sadly. He walked over to the table, “Do you still wish to see her?” Jake pushed all the sudden thoughts out of his head as he turned to face the table. He nodded, and the doctor pulled back the sheet to her collarbone, folding it down so Jake could look at her. Only the very tips of the Y incision were visible, and even though Jake knew it was her he found it difficult to recognize her. The color had been drained away as part of the autopsy, and her face, once so full of grace and charm, seemed slackened and empty. Jake stepped forward, looking down at the face he once’loved. He had been in love with her without ever admitting it to anyone, including most importantly himself. Self-deception had become a survival tool for him, and now when he realized how terrible a toll it had taken on him he felt his legs weaken. Jake grabbed hold of the stainless steel table. His mouth dropped open slightly, and he felt the rush of emotions overcoming him. He held on to the last tiny strands of himself, trying to maintain his composure. Dr. Bakshi saw the play of sorrow attack his face, and he knew Mr. Hix had indeed been close to her during their relationship. “I will give you a some time alone. When you are finished, you can exit through the same doors Mr. Hix, and once again my sympathies are with you.” He tried to thank the doctor, but all that came out of his mouth was a soft cry. He closed his lips together, trying to maintain. The echo of the footsteps from the doctors retreat were the last chain holding back the flood. When he was alone, the welling fluid in his eyes finally escaped down his cheeks. The tears fell down onto the table and his shirt. Uncontrolled, he felt the sobs escaping from his throat as he shuddered from the release more than he heard his own mournful cries. As his body convulsed, he refused to give in to the urge to fall down prostrate on the floor and rail against the heavens for what they had done. The grief all poured out, his eyes swelling as he continued bawling. He found his fist slamming into the side of the table, away from where she rested. He beat out all his grief and rage on the hard, cold surface of the table. Proclaiming the truths he had forbidden himself from recognizing until now. Time passed in a crawl for Jake, but even so he stayed in the cold of the room for more than fifteen minutes. He gazed at the face he had once loved for a long time before wiping away the trails of tears from his face and walking away. There was nothing left for him in that room? There was nothing left for his heart? There was nothing left for him in New York now? Jake took the long walk down the hallway through the doors to the outside. He stepped through, and saw a man standing there waiting on him with an older woman bearing a strong resemblance to him. He looked familiar somehow, and as he came up angrily Jake realized that he was the man he had seen kissing Becca at Gaby’s during the holiday party. Before Jake could say anything, the man slapped him hard across the face. The woman behind him came up quickly, grabbing his arm, “Michael, don’t!” “You don’t deserve to be here!” Michael yelled at Jake. The Big Dawg simply stared back, all the emotion had drained out of him in the room. He felt the heat on his face where the slap had left a mark, but he didn’t care. “She loved you, and all you did was treat her like a whore.” Michael carried on, Jake’s face changing from acceptance to anger with that comment. “She never let go of her feelings for you, even when she agreed to marry me I knew she still loved you in her way, and thanks to her baby she would always be connected to you!” Michael saw the twist, the play of shock and sorrow on this man’s face who he had come to hate for coming between them as he had. She had never told him, despite what he had been told this man never knew. Somehow, the anger inside of him just got worse. “You didn’t know” Of course not, because you would have just abandoned both of them like you always abandoned her! What kind of a man are you!”! You’re nothing! You’re a piece of shit! Get out of here and never come around here again!” Jake looked over at the young woman with him, “I’m sorry for your loss.” He stepped past Michael, ignoring everything he had said despite the well of rage and fury that had sprung up inside of him. He was almost to the door when he heard it. “Coward!” He stopped, and now the well was exploding inside of him. He came back to the man from behind and swung him around violently. Jake wrapped his massive hand around his throat as he slammed him into the wall, causing the sheetrock to cave in around him. Michael was struggling, but Jake was too much for him. “The truth is I was never good enough for her, and you loved her in a way I never could, but she still ended up settling for you and always loving me. She did what she needed to do for herself and our baby, and I don’t hold any ill will about it.” Jake was breathing heavily as he spoke. He closed down on the man’s throat once more, “I wasn’t strong enough to give into what she wanted from me, but if you keep pushing it I can arrange for you to have a plot right next to her.” Jake let go of Michael, and he slid down to the floor coughing and clutching his throat. Jake stared down as the woman came rushing to his side to help. “I loved Becca, I loved her as much as I could. I just couldn’t ever love her like she deserved to be loved, and I knew that. You could’you were stronger than me for that. But don’t let your love for her make you think I’ll take all your grief, we all have more than enough heartache to go around.” Turning away, Jake walked out. He was already making calls to his friends in New York, and he had no doubt they would confirm the fears weighing him down. A car hadn’t killed Becca, she had been murdered because his connection to her had been discovered. Lord Byron Hotel Rome, Italy The flight over was smooth and relaxing on the private jet, but he had not gotten any rest or sleep for his weary bones. Instead, he was constantly thinking about New York. She was gone, and he could do nothing to change that, but he could do something to avenge what had happened. There seemed to be forces working against him at the moment, and he didn’t like all the uncertainty. He had made arrangements with the Lord Byron Hotel for a limousine to pick him up at Fuimicino Airport, just like the previous year. Luckily, coming in on a private jet ensured a smooth and quick entry to the country through the customs checkpoint. There were no crowds here, no rush of the world all trying to get through at the same time. There was a chauffer wearing a nice suit waited on him, and Jake remembered him from the previous year. “Mr. Hix, nice to have you back again sir.” The chauffer said as he took Jake’s bags. Jake nodded, “Good to be back. Take me to the hotel.” They wound their ways through the rocky, rolling hills from the airport to the city. Carefully tucked away in the exclusive Parioli quarter of Rome, but just a few steps from Villa Borghese, the “Dolce Vita” of Via Veneto and a stroll in the park away from Piazza di Spagna, the exquisite Hotel Lord Byron was the destination for travelers who wanted to spend their time at the premier hotel of Rome. He enjoyed the privacy and lucury the hotel afforded him, and he could afford the very best for this particular trip. In fact, he had made arrangements for all his teammates if they chose to take advantage of the opportunity. The limousine pulled up in front of the hotel. The chauffer came around and opened the door for Jake. He stepped out into the warm midday sun. He looked around, enjoying the entryway with its pink granite pillars with black wrought iron gates opened to the inside. The same pink granite led up to the doorways, but the entire building was an extremely clean whitewash with black accents on the windows. Jake proceeded up the stairs and into the interior. The entire hotel had been decorated in the art-deco style from the thirties. Remembering his way around, he approached the desk. This time, there was an attractive woman behind the desk in a tight outfit accentuating her curves, “Good afternoon sir. Welcome to the Lord Byron Hotel. Are you checking in?” “Yes, there should be a number of reservations underneath Syndicate Rules: Extreme, with myself as the contact. My name is Jake Hix.” Jake looked around at the hotel, trying to find solace in all the comfort, but the more he looked about the more hollow the experience was for him. The man nodded, recognizing the name at once. “Yes sir, we have the penthouse for yourself and five additional suites for your friends. No one else has checked in yet, but we will leave them reserved per your instructions.” “Thank you.” After being shown to his room and taken care of by the staff, Jake found himself on his balcony, staring out at nothing once again. He showered, shave and put on fresh clothes. He was expecting a guest any moment. He had left instructions for them to be let into the room. There was a knock, and Jake waited for his guests to arrive. Chuck Reno walked in with a cameraman. Jake hated the weasely little reporter, but for now he would use him to get Brick’s attention. Jake stood up, looking quite formal in slacks and a button up shirt with the sleeves rolled halfway up his massive forearms. “Chuck.” “Jake, amazed you actually wanted me to come and speak with you.” Chuck said. He looked around the penthouse, “This is, wow’amazing.” “I like a little comfort on international trips. How about you get set up and we can get started?” Jake pressed him quickly. “Sure.” Penthouse Lord Byron Hotel Rome, Italy Chuck Reno was seated in the plush seat across from Jake Hix. The landscape and light of the city could be seen through the backdoor. The entire setting was gorgeous, and they were using it to show how luxurious he was living. Chuck Reno was staring right into the camera, “Ladies and gentlemen, this is Chuck Reno with a Global exclusive. Right before the Clash Of The Titans I am here with the man who will be trying to become a two time World Heavyweight Champion. Jake “The Big Dawg” Hix has been fighting back and forth with Brick for these many months. Jake, thank you for taking the time to speak with us here today.” “As always Chuck, the pleasure is all yours.” “Right.” Chuck ignored the comment. “Now, recently it was announced this would be a Hardcore Battle, no rules at all. How does this affect your strategy?” “Affect my strategy” Are you kidding me? That slut and her moronic pet played right into my plans.” Jake laughed as he thought about it. “What do you mean?” Jake picked up his small cup of espresso and took a sip, “All I had to do was take my anger out on some little street hoodlum. Some abandoned bastard of some whore mother who should have kept her legs together, and that attack made Brick all upset. So our illustrious Slut In Charge Brandi Sights decides, along with her board of jackasses, to make this a no rules match.” “So this is what you wanted?” “Look, Brick is an idiot. Last time he got lucky, but this time we’re in a foreign country where they let you take your life into your hands when you step into the octagon. He keeps talking about killing me according to the buzz backstage, but that broken down relic forgot something very important. I’m going to be able to do anything I want to him. What is he?” “Brick?” Chuck asked. “Right, what is he really?” “Well, he’s the champion with a long history of being a tough competitor.” “The man was born a bitch.” Jake smiled at Chuck. “Look, I already plan to take him apart and show him as a bitch in front of the world. All I am doing is reminding him that he doesn’t deserve to be in the same ring as me, and I’m sick and tired of having to share any kind of time with a little punk like him. He needs to be put down, and now all the plans have come together to let me be the man who ENDS Brick. I’m sick of playing these games.” “Okay, let’s talk about some other issues out there. Some would say you are lucky to be here because with Josh beating you in a non-title match he should have been the number one contender. How do you respond to that criticism?” Chuck didn’t mind pushing Jake, it made for better interviews. “I say he should go back South of the border and knock up some more whores. I mean, the guy likes to whine and talk shit, but I personally just don’t care about him. We’ll see how tough he is on the big stage, assuming he doesn’t take another powder. I mean the guy is a self-absorbed prick who isn’t consistent anymore. So fuck him.” “These are strong words. What about the rest of the roster?” “You’re just trying to get me to talk trash about people.” Chuck laughed a little, “Maybe, but why not?” “Okay, fire away.” Chuck thought of where to start, “How about Kris Kanton?” “I already made him my bitch. I mean, who talks more trash about a record of such incredible mediocrity than this guy” Hell Chuck, you’ve made everyone who watches this interview a little dumber for having brought him up.” Jake was laughing, enjoying the entire idea of Kanton as any kind of threat. “What about Kyo Kushinaga?” Chuck wondered what he would say here. “The Deathscythe Outlaw has been impressing lots of people in and out of the league. What do you think about him?” Jake slowly let a smile come over his face, “Now there’s a talented guy. I won’t talk trash about him. If it ends up that he and I face off I welcome it because he is a great competitor, he is someone, unlike Kanton, who has a proven track record in this industry and I want to see what he has. I’m keeping my eye on him to see what he does.” “High praise.” “Honest appraisal. You don’t run someone down who doesn’t deserve it.” Jake shrugged, “He could be a good guy or an ass, but in the octagon he’s talent.” “Lots of talk about Jackson Namenlos-Duisternis. What about Jackson?” “Not a new person for me, so no new thoughts. If he wants some it’s easy to beat a path to my door. He’s a little too crazy for my tastes. That’s the guy you expect to find nailing sheep in the pasture and wondering why people are offended.” Chuck tried to keep a straight face, “What about Eryc Morgan” There’s lots of talk about bad blood between the two of you stemming from the budding friendship you two seemed to have last year.” “I don’t even want to waste my breath.” “There’s lots of talk.” Jake leaned back, “Yeah, but so what” He’s the same in and out of the ring. A waste of flesh, a pile of shit that just stinks up the place.” “I have one more name I want to bring up. We both know this name, we both know this man and we’ve both known him for quite some time.” Chuck leaned forward, “The Killer himself, Dylan Knight has recently joined Global. You had quite a history with him back in another federation.” “Did I?” “I remember a long feud between the two of you.” Jake held up a hand, “No you don’t. What you should remember is me kicking his ass from one end of the world to the next. Dylan is chasing me like some fucked up form of the white whale in Moby Dick. He was never able to beat me, he was never able to get over on me and so he has had to face the truth that he was never as much of a man as I am.” “So you don’t worry about him coming for revenge after all those years of resentment?” “I worry about him like I would worry about a horse fly.” Jake madder a slapping motion in front of himself, “You slap them both away and keep going like it was never there.” “Confident as always.” Chuck decided they had covered enough, and keeping Jake too long only made him get more upset with him. “What do you predict for Clash Of The Titans?” “I predict that Mike Biddle will win the tournament, I predict Texas Wrecking Crew will win the tag team titles and finally, I predict I will leave Brick a bloody, broken bitch in the middle of the octagon to become a two time world champion.”
