What to do about TUE

18 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/18/03 Brick and Aaron sit at the table, having lunch and looking over possible cover designs for DMAK’s next album. “Another private war, huh?” Aaron asks Brick from the other side of the table. “Has to be done. TUE is starting to get too damn big for their britches. Somebody has to step to them.” “Yeah, well, why you?” “It was inevitable. Deacon Holmes is a member, and you know how I feel about that little bitch. Besides, Soma said himself that TUE was gonna go after everyone who crossed them and I fall under that category.” “So, now it’s how many?” “Seven in GW right now: Stecchino, Price, Holmes, Ice, Zero, Souls of the Black Lotus. Not to mention the bodyguards.” “What about this guy Il Capo?” “I don’t know much about him yet,” Brick says, perusing a photo. “What do you think of this one?” “It’s okay. Look, Brick, are you sure you want to do this?” “I’m sure I have to do this. And this time, I’m prepared. See that dude standing over there?” Brick asks, pointing to a man in a black suit standing in a corner. “Yeah,” Aaron replies, looking in the direction. “That’s one of your new bodyguards.” “What?” “I got some more to watch Felicia and Ashley. Plus, I’m putting some on my mom, Monica, Kyle and DMAK.” “You don’t think you’re jumping the gun a little?” “Ask yourself that when you look in the mirror and you see that brand Salaok left on your chest that one summer. Ask yourself when you feel pain from when Derek Draven threw you off the Globaltron. Ask Monica and Kyle that when they remember the VCD Salaok sent, showing he was at their wedding. I already slipped up before with the Nevermore. I’m not slipping up again. Not if I can help it.” “Can you even trust these guys” I mean, for we know, they might be working for the Echelon!” “Well, I have to do something.” “Look, Brick, don’t worry. We can just get the police to watch us.” “And how do you know the Echelon ain’t got cops on their payroll” For all we know, Il Capo probably givin? them orders as we speak.” “Look, Brick,” Aaron says, “we’re gonna be careful.” “That’s not enough. I could at least figure out the Nevermore to some extent. The Upper Echelon?” “Hey, Brick, who’s still in GW” You or the Nevermore?” “You’re right. I’ll just deal with TUE when I do.” “No.” “What do you mean “no?”” “I mean this picture. We cannot use this for the new CD.” “Why not?” “It just looks wrong.” “What do you mean, “looks wrong?”” It looks fine.” “No, it doesn’t.” “Yes, it does.” “No, it doesn’t.” “Yes, it does.” “No, it doesn’t.” “Yes, it does.” “No, it doesn’t.” “Aw man, you’re getting mayonnaise on the pictures!” “I am not!”
