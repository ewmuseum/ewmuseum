Global Arena Las Vegas 3:00pm local time Shadow didn’t often find herself in the Global Arena unless it was a bare moment before the show started. She was well aware that some of the talent made it their business to hang out there all the time, or, at the very least, to stay in the area? but she’d never done things by the book. Perhaps it was the strange looks she often got’what, did they expect her to hide when it was daylight now that she didn’t have to? In any case, she’d banked on getting a little peace and quiet at the arena, but that had been a terrible assumption. Crews bustled around doing their last minute preparations for last minute preparations. Evo, as it was referred to in fond colloquial dialect, didn’t go on until tomorrow night; the sense of urgency among the crew was somewhat odd to Shadow, who had soon found it impossible to concentrate anywhere in the open, and had ducked into the Hunting Grounds locker room at first available opportunity. She shifted on the leather couch somewhat uncomfortably. How people went from eating cows to making furniture out of them was completely beyond her. At that, she made a rather wry face. There was no sense alas-ing about the complete loss of functionality, because no one would have understood’especially since a good many of these people already thought she was a Halloween joke gone obsessive-compulsive. Even her medical manual wasn’t all that compelling today’a disappointment, because mortal anatomy could often keep her ensconced in the same place for hours, memorizing tendon placement, and saying the names of bones to herself, which only added to a growing feeling that she was out of place: who in the doctoring profession could pronounce Latin correctly, after all? That was a digression from the point, however interesting it was to amuse herself with thoughts of barging into a med-school class to teach them a thing or two. The door of the Hunting Grounds locker room closed, announcing another entrant. Shadow, still half-absorbed, quietly closed the manual and looked up just in time to catch the eyes of her tag team partner, Vivica Valentine. Vivica’s blue eyes widened, as she did a double take. “What the hell are you doing out during the day”!” Obviously, Vivica had not been aware of this new development in Shadow’s physiology. Shadow shrugged. “Reading.” Vivica made a rather perturbed face and leaned closer to look at the book. “What ya readi’wow. I’m not gonna ask.” The blonde’s eyes widened significantly as Shadow casually set her book on the table, and shrugged. “Research,” the vampire said simply, figuring that would alarm her even more. Vivica looked away from Shadow somewhat uncomfortably. Shadow smirked, satisfied that her work had been done’tag partner or no tag partner, what fun was it to be involved with humans if you couldn’t throw them off guard sometimes? “Umm” I won’t disturb you for very long, I just came by to pick up a couple of files for Chris, you know for MOTM?” Shadow nodded, which sent Vivica into the hallway that led twistingly to the other, interconnected rooms of the HG complex. For a few long moments, Shadow was left by herself, lost in thoughts of the weight of this week. Two matches’one against Emoticon, one of the only people she truly considered a friend in Global, and one the preliminary of the Master of the Mat tournament, with a partner she was entirely unfamiliar with, other than knowing that Virgil had managed to defeat him a few weeks ago. But more importantly than that, Leinad Soma was waiting for an answer. Leinad Soma wanted her to be a part of Rex Deus, and if she said no, she knew without a doubt that she’d better be prepared for a fight’and rightly so. By her own rules of honor, Soma would have given her the gift of his power, and she would have shunned it and turned away without returning him the boon she owed him. It was unthinkable because of her sense of honor, but in another sense, also dangerous. If she affronted Soma in that manner? she’d be putting everything and everyone she cared about in danger. Shadow’s crimson lips turned downwards, her eyes focused on the wall, but in reality, quite far away from its smooth beige gloss. Vivica returned fairly noiselessly’or at least she thought so. Shadow noted her presence, but simply chose not to comment upon it. At least until Vivica cleared her throat. “Shadow?” Shadow turned rather suddenly, and the intensity of her narrowed glance almost caused Vivica to flinch’but then again, Vivica had long ago ceased to be outright hostile to her tag partner. Lucky thing. But the woman was just strange, wasn’t she? No matter? Vivica stepped a little closer to the couch, despite being rather wary still of Shadow at the moment, research pursuits and all. “Yes, Viv?” Vivica’s eyebrows slanted downwards. “I’m worried about you.” Were it kosher at the moment, Shadow would have laughed at her’but something in the plucky blonde’s tone caused her to hold her mirth. “Worried” Why?” Vivica shook her head, raising a hand to gesture, as though apologetically. “Look, you might think this is stupid, but” I’m more attentive than a lot of people give me credit for. I saw that little scene with Soma last week, and to be honest, it has me pretty worried.” Shadow shook her head almost reflexively. “Soma” Come on? he’s nothing to worry about. He only wants?” “Hang on. Hear me out,” Vivica said, holding up a hand. Her tag team partner nodded, and Vivica continued, going so far as to walk around the other side of the couch and sit down on the other end of it’certainly not close enough to Shadow to touch her, but closer than she ever made a habit of being. A moment of silence passed between the two of them. “Shadow,” Vivica started haltingly, curling a leg up underneath her, “I don’t want to tell you who to trust. You’re certainly a big girl, and you can make up your own mind on shit like this, but I have something I need to say to you about it. I’m kind of glad that I found you before” well, before you made a mistake.” Shadow’s lips curled almost imperceptibly downwards. Vivica didn’t quite notice, but gut instinct told her that the vampiress wasn’t receiving this advice too kindly. “Shady, it’s not like I’m trying to tell you what to do. But just go with me here. You remember when Christian first met you?” Shadow nodded. “Right, well” I remember the way he saw you’something special. Somebody talented that he wanted on his side. He might have preyed on what you had a lot of at the time’anti-WMW feelings’but he preyed on it to get you into a better position to shine. Right?” “I” suppose?” Vivica shook her head. “No, there’s no supposing about it. Christian never pays attention to people who aren’t worth his time. More than that, he sure as hell never asks people to be part of one of his factions if they’re not worth his time.” “Right, but” what does that have to do with this?” Shadow asked, tilting her head slightly, her unblinking eyes never once leaving Vivica’s face. “Soma wants to change you!” Vivica blurted out rather suddenly, and with a bit more fire than Shadow would have expected, “Soma wants to take you and mold you into his image of the perfect soldier. Hunting Grounds knows that you already are that, just as you are, without any extra modifications.” Shadow sighed quietly. “Vivica, this is something that passed through my thoughts some weeks back. We all have to evolve in order to grow, and had I not felt so strongly about that maxim, I wouldn’t have drank Soma’s blood” which, if you must know, is the reason that I’m sitting here in this room right now, during the daylight.” Vivica paused for a moment, not entirely sure what she should think of that. In fact, she still wasn’t quite sure if she should think of her tag team partner as a vampire, or just an eccentric’a talented eccentric, but an eccentric nonetheless. Vivica’s hands dropped to her lap in defeat. “Look,” she said, sounding somewhat downcast, “I’m worried. Just like I said. Putting yourself willingly under the control of a guy like Soma is just asking for trouble. He doesn’t want you for you. He wants to mold you into some vision of perfection that can’t possibly exist.” Before Shadow could answer, Vivica stood up, gathering up the folders she had originally come to retrieve. “Just” think about it, okay?” Shadow smirked slightly. “Is this meant to be an indication of how much Hunting Grounds wants me around?” she asked, somewhat coyly. Vivica shook her head, and started towards the door. Shadow tilted her head, somewhat afraid that Vivica was just going to leave, and end the conversation on that note. Suddenly, she turned. “We shouldn’t have to tell you whether or not we want you around, and Christian is too proud to beg someone to stay when they don’t want to’nor should he have to lower himself to that level. You’re here by choice, and you can leave by choice if you want to. All that I’m asking is that when you make that choice” look at what’s really going on. Look at who has your best interests in mind? and look at who wants something that you may not be able to provide. What do you think will happen when he’s gotten what he wants out of you?” With no more than those words, her stable-mate left the room, and went on her way’back to Zenith with his info. Click. The door shut quietly, but the click echoed in Shadow’s head as though it had been a thousand times louder’a meteor crashing to earth, a boulder rolling off a cliff and sliding crazily into the canyon below. Shadow couldn’t deny it’she could write Vivica off as a little mortal she didn’t have to conscience all she wanted, but the petite, plucky blonde had left her with a lot more to think about than she had bargained for. .:: ::: ::. Las Vegas The Strip 11:00pm local time “Mmm” don’t you think that one’s nice?” Shadow’s black-taloned finger pointed towards a businessman, decked out in Vegas-style gaudy suit, belt buckle and cowboy hat. Virgil’s lip curled in disgust. “How gauche” He probably tastes like chewing tobacco.” Shadow smirked. “I don’t care what he tastes like” it’s more the pleasure of taking him down a peg or two by catching him off guard.” Virgil chuckled under his breath. “You and I hunt for very different reasons, then.” “I beg to differ, mon amour?” she responded, with a soft, fanged smile, “We hunt for hunger” we’re just a bit different in our modus operandi.” Both of them seemed to have eyes directed elsewhere as they strolled down the chaotic milieu that was The Strip. Despite the conflicted nature of her personal business at the moment, Shadow’s green eyes were relatively calm’perhaps focused in the throes of a hunt, or perhaps Virgil was the balm in Gilead for all of the turbulence surrounding her. They walked side by side, not touching, but occasionally casting glances at each other that said more than a word or touch otherwise might have. Shadow sighed quietly. “Virgil?” He smiled slightly’it was still relieving to hear her say his name, after not hearing it for what seemed like an eternity longer than his life had already been. “Si?” She pressed her crimson lips together for a moment, her eyes narrowing slightly as she focused on the question that was pressing her. “Do you think Soma’s just using me” Trying to make me into something I’m not?” Without a doubt, that question gave Virgil some pause. He removed a hand from his pocket and ran his fingers through his hair, shrugging slightly. “I” don’t know,” his deep-set blue gaze glanced off her for a moment, chilling and warming her at the same time, like it always did, “I wish I could tell you better than that, but I simply don’t know where the truth lies in that maze.” Shadow’s gaze scraped the pavement somewhat discontentedly. She sighed, and for the second time, Virgil was caught rather off-guard by it. “Is this decision bothering you more than you let on, cara mia?” he asked. “Do you want me in Rex Deus?” Shadow asked, her tone much sharper, much ruder than she meant for it to be’and much ruder than became a Fiend who had been taught better manners, by parents and sire alike. Virgil shrugged slightly. “To be honest, it does not make a difference one side or the other with me. My feelings on the matter are” not negotiable. I will still love you whether you are a part of Rex Deus or Hunting Grounds.” “Still?” He arched a brow at her. “You must have some sort of preference,” she said, her eyes catching on his, probing as deeply as she possibly could into what he was thinking’or trying to. He shrugged again, most aggravatingly for her. “No, not particularly. I suppose there is something to be said for sticking with our own kind. Especially one of our own kind who is” powerful,” he finished, smirking slightly. So that’s what this was about for him’suddenly, it all made sense to her. It had never been about allegiance; first it had been because Soma had a link to her, and then because Soma was supernaturally powerful. ?” of course, I could get that same amount of enjoyment out of Global by being a part of Hunting Grounds, or any other faction? so you see, it’s really neither here nor there for me,” Virgil said’evidently, he’d continued speaking, and Shadow had missed part of it, lost in her realization. She shook her head slightly. Virgil was right, of course. One faction or another, there were really only very minute differences in all of them anyway. But Soma had one thing that could make any preternatural being salivate in craving’power. Real, corporeal, magnificent power, and what an insidious gift it was, implying all sorts of obligations and responsibilities. Leinad Soma was an absolute genius, wasn’t he? Virgil cleared his throat. Shadow looked up at him rather suddenly; there was mirth in the oceanic depths of his eyes. He raised an eyebrow at her. “You know, maybe you can go out in the sun, but I cannot” and the longer we stand here?” Shadow rolled her eyes. “My meal awaits me, Shadow, this is hardly a laughing matter,” Virgil said in mock-pettishness, grinning. “Just like a man.” They slipped into an easeful silence again, walking down the street with a predatory gleam spiking into both of their eyes’after all, behind all the pretty pictures on the outside, inside, there was nothing less than a beast. Virgil, seemingly following his senses, slipped into an alley. One side was swarming with rats and darkness. Following natural instinct, Shadow slipped onto the darkest path of sidewalk she could find. Facing her were the imposing windows of a Catholic church. She was tempted to roll her eyes. What use did a city like Las Vegas have for God or his pretensions? The squeaking side door of the church interrupted her musings on it, and a sudden grin spread over her face. “Priest,” Virgil whispered quietly from somewhere up ahead of her, “I believe I shall go hunt elsewhere, I can’t stand the taste of communion wine in my vitae.” Shadow laughed under her breath, her eyes zeroing in on the man who was locking the side door of the church. Indeed, Virgil had started to move back out of the alley, uninterested in this sort of meal’but he stopped dead in his tracks upon seeing his partner in crime move forward, rather than move to leave the alley. Father Henry gasped in shock as a figure suddenly stood in front of him. An abnormally pale woman gazed back at him, half in shade, half in the palsied light of the moth-covered light that hung above the alley on the church side. The stained-glass windows gazed, glazed over behind him like dead eyes. A smile melted over the face of this ebon-haired beauty, and for a few moments, there was a stitch in time that seemed to pause it completely’as though he could see the past reflected in her eyes, the days of witch-burnings and Inquisitions. With a gasp, Father Henry took a step back. He couldn’t quite put a finger on what was wrong with this woman, but it was very disconcerting indeed. “Um” May I help you?” was his polite introduction. “Help me?” Shadow purred, “Yes, forgive me father” for I have sinned?” She trailed off into a grin, and stepped fully into the light, her fangs still hidden; the grin on her face was no less disturbing. Father Henry took another step back, spluttering like a child at its first swimming lesson. “I” I don’t hear confession outside the b-b-b?” Shadow tsk-tsked. “That’s a real shame. You’re going to turn away one of your children in her hour of need?” Father Henry took another step back’and finally, he met brick, brick that had long since cooled from the Las Vegas sunshine. He shivered, but it had not much to do with the cold of the building, and everything to do with the cold radiating off of the woman in front of him. “Do you know what it’s like, Father” To thirst for righteouness?” “Yes, yes,” he said, seizing on anything he possibly could, “We all do at some point in our lives, and it’s then that we turn to God.” “Do you know what it’s like to turn to God” and have him look away?” Shadow asked, her smile suddenly fading, “Do you know what it’s like to need him so desperately” and have him ignore your existence completely? To leave you to die?” “I” I?. I?” Father Henry did not have the words. Shadow took a step closer to him, her eyes narrowed. “Turn your head, Father, let me tell you a little secret,” she said, her voice full of ice. He turned his head’he found himself nearly powerless to resist. Shadow leaned in close, whispering in his ear with the breath of the tomb. “Your” God? lies?” With that, she was close enough to go in for the kill’her fangs unsheathed in a matter of seconds, and she plunged them into Father Henry’s neck. In surprise, he flung his hands out to the side, papers and books flying everywhere askew. All of this was no distraction to Shadow, who could think only to clap her hand over his mouth to quiet his howls while she took her fill. When she’d drunk enough to slake her thirst at least until tomorrow night, she pulled away, forcefully, her eyes wide, gasping for breath, blood running down one side of her lip. She wiped off the side of her mouth and leaned closer to the trembling Father Henry, running a tongue over his wound to close it up. She turned her head towards him, forcing him to make eye contact. “Now” You listen to me.” Father Henry nodded. “You say nothing of this to anyone” You remember? nothing.” Her forceful gaze seemed to yank the man out of his skull for a moment, before Shadow let go of his chin and pushed him in the direction of his scattered papers, disappearing into the relative darkness as quickly as she came. Of course, it was only seconds until she found herself pushed up against the wall herself; Virgil’s eyes pressed down into hers, practically wild with attraction. “Come back to my apartment with me?” She smirked, not at all attempting to push down the adrenaline that was starting to rise’her energy replenished with Father Henry’s blood, she could now focus on other things. “I thought you were hungry,” she whispered to him, pressing her lips teasingly closer to his. “I am,” he said, pressing her a little harder into the wall, “But it’s not blood that I crave?” Shadow, quite disarmed, could only smirk, and he took that as a yes. How could a girl have refused a man simply dropping at her feet with desperation, putting aside all other needs in need of her? In the darkness of the alleyway, it was almost impossible to see the shadow move away from the church window. .:: ::: ::. McFarlane Estate Unknown Date 9:50pm local time Smiling contently, Shadow stepped into the huge foyer of the McFarlane Estate. It was a place to be proud of, even by vampiric standards, which always tended to be rather archaic and lavish the vast majority of the time. Shadow turned to Virgil, who she’d brought along for the ride this week, opening her mouth to say something to him, but before she could get very far, there was a sudden ruckus. Alexis, her hair scooped up in a messy ponytail, and her forehead covered with sweat, suddenly darted out into the hallway. “SHADOW!” Shadow’s eyes widened, as she shook her head at the girl’s sudden outburst’she’d never seen Alexis act like this before. In fact, Alexis usually took every opportunity that she could to not talk to Shadow. “What”!” “Please, come into the den, it” it’s Dawn.” Suddenly, Shadow’s stomach hopped up into her throat, which chose that exact moment to go as dry as the Gobi. She didn’t ask questions, she just bolted down the hall, on instinct, with Virgil following closely behind. Before she could get there, however, she nearly crashed into the pale form of Dawn McFarlane, out in the hallway. Lacerations criss-crossed her back, and she was dressed in only a pair of black leather pants and a black bra. Her hair was a mess, and it seemed as though she was weakly trying to push herself up from the carpet, but with not much success. Shadow looked up at Alexis, her eyes glassing over somewhat. “What the hell happened?” Alexis gestured madly with her hands, plainly in quite a flustered frenzy at the moment. “I” she took her bike out? and I couldn’t keep up with? and then she had the accident, and she was barely conscious when I found her, but? I got her in the car and brought her back here.” “What is she doing up?” Shadow said, leaning down, and pressing an arm underneath Dawn’s shoulder to help her up. Alexis shook her head, almost tearfully, as though she expected to be punished. “I tried to keep her down! But she heard you at the door, and she insisted on getting up to answer it, and she just” she just passed out.” Dawn’s head rolled back insensibly and her smaller stable-mate gesture for Alexis to come help. Alexis slipped her arm underneath Dawn’s other arm, and the two of them, with Virgil watching on slightly dispassionately for the moment. Shadow, with singularity of mind only towards getting Dawn in a better position, laid the younger woman on the couch. At the moment, she was completely unconscious, but Shadow’s senses immediately picked up signs that she was still fine’probably passed out from pain and blood loss. In full damage control mode, she shooed Alexis away with a cloth. “Go to the kitchen, wet this, and bring a bowl of water along with it. I’m going to start cleaning up some of these lacerations.” Alexis sprinted off, which allowed Shadow a moment to look at the woman spread out on the couch before her’she was a real wreck at the moment. A frown stretched taut over Shadow’s features as she softly let her fingertips come to rest on Dawn’s cheek. Virgil cleared his throat. “Can I do anything?” Shadow jumped slightly, softly moving her hand upwards to check for fever in her patient, but she shook her head softly. “Unless you want to go find her bike and bring it back?” Shadow turned her head to look back at him, trying to suppress some of the sadness in her eyes with a slight smile, but even it came out twisted and drawn. Virgil tilted his head at her, his eyes catching on every feature of her face’but it was too much to make sense of right now, why she’d be so concerned about Dawn. He simply nodded. “I could use a walk anyway.” And that was the last she heard of his soft basso tone before being left in silence. Shadow surveyed the injuries for a moment, glancing briefly over each cut before coming to some old scars on Dawn’s wrist’a few weeks old, if she could put her finger on it. Her brow furrowed. “Damnit, Alexis” why are you taking so long?” Her question came out muttered, hardly with the force she’d tried to muster for it. While Alexis was gone, she did what she could’which was, first, to pull Dawn’s hand closer to her, and lick closed a long laceration on one of her fingers. Out of nowhere, a sudden feeling assailed her, and a sudden taste. All thought of Soma, Rex Deus, TV Title, Master of the Mat? all of it faded completely from her mind, and a flash of memory overtook her senses. Gritting her teeth against the pain Dawn slit open her wrist, running her thumbnail vertically along the sensitive skin there, waiting for a moment for the blood to begin to sluggishly slide from the wound. She pressed her forearm to Shadow’s still lips, hoping the vampires instincts would take over once she tasted blood. The vampiress stirred slightly, her lips moving against Dawn’s wrist, gently lapping at the blood dripping onto her ruby lips. She opened her mouth a little, instinct driving her torpid body, letting the thick liquid drip slowly onto her tongue and slide down the back of her throat. Dawn held her wrist in place for the count of thirty, before slowly withdrawing it. She cleaned the cut quickly with a cotton swab and rubbing alcohol, pressing fresh swabs to the wound and wrapping a strip of gauze around it, secured with a safety pin. She dabbed a small drop of blood from Shadow’s lips and then quickly left, hoping to be off that floor before Shadow even began to stir. A sudden heady dizziness rocked the entirety of Shadow’s body’no small wonder the taste of Dawn’s blood was familiar. Completely overcome by the knowledge that despite all the ire and discomfort stared between herself and her hostess in the past wee weeks, Shadow let her head hang. How dare she be frustrated at Dawn when Dawn had been the one to care for her when she most needed it? Alexis hadn’t yet come back from the kitchen’or presumably, she’d gone ahead to get the other supplies, the bandages and disinfectant and medical tape. To Shadow, it was somewhat of a blessing to be alone at the moment, as her eyes fixed on Dawn’s momentarily motionless form’she didn’t like for anyone to see her cry. But true tears or not, Dawn wasn’t awake to see her tears’and that brought about the question: when would there be enough lies to simply tear down the entire house of cards that she and Dawn had built up around their relationship with each other? When would enough be enough? .:: ::: ::.
