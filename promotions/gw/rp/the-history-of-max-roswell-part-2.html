The History of Max Roswell Part 2

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: I dunno. 15 Years Ago I was up early in the morning watching my favourite cartoon, The Incredible Hulk. Suddenly, my mom woke up and saw me sitting there just staring at the television. She smiled and layed back down. I got up and walked over to my mother’s room. “Mom, when can we go?” I said to her. “In a couple of minutes honey.” she said back to me. I ran back to the television hoping the show would still be on, but I missed the ending. It was on the credits with a black screen and white lettering. My mother walked into the room and walked into the bathroom. A couple minutes later she walked back out and her hair was fixed up all nice. “We’ll go in a minute.” she said. I walked over to our living room and jumped up onto a chair and opened the black curtains. I looked at the sky, it was a very light blue. I always liked looking at the sky, it amazed me. I also liked looking down at the streets with hundreds of people walking into stores, or even just walking down the sidewalk. My mother walked over to the window with a toothbrush in her hand. She walked back to the bathroom as I just stayed by the window glaring at all the sights and amusements. I jumped off the chair and did a kind of funny move I created. It was called The Flying Duck Jump. I always liked jumping off of things doing that move every time. “Max, we can go now.” my mother said. We were going to the amusement park. All the rides and attractions would be so much fun. I started jumping up and down like a walloping horse. I walked over to the bed and took off my pajamas. I took the blue NIKE shirt off the bed and the darker blue jeans. I put on my black shoes that were starting to tear on the front of them. I ran over to the front door and opened it. It was still the middle of July. I could’ve worn my blue striped shorts, but I didn’t want to. My mother walked to the door and walked outside. The sun was shining in her face like nothing else was in front of her. I walked behind carrying something that was inside one of my pockets. It was my toy skateboard. I loved playing with those. They were like my favourite toys in the world other than my plush Incredible Hulk toy. I always slept with that one. It was so soft. “Come in Max, we don’t want to be late.” said my mother. I opened the backdoor of our black car. I liked it. My mother hated it for some reason. She said she’s going sell it as fast as she can. It had a sunroof that I liked turning on from the back of the car. I put my seatbelt on and my mother backed up. .She started going down the street as I just looked out the window at some people that walk down our sidewalk every day. AT THE AMUSEMENT PARK We had just arrived at the park. There were rides covering the whole park. My mother parked in a spot right by the entrance of the park. I unbuckled my seat belt and opened the door as fast as I could. I started running to the entrance not waiting for my mother. A man stopped me at the entrance. He was dressed in a french fry outfit. He was scaring me so I just kicked him in the shin and ran into the park. My mother caught up to me and slapped me on the wrist. “Max, don’t you ever run away from me again.” she yelled at me. I started balling. I didn’t care that I was in front of thousands of people, I started crying. My mother automatically hugged me and kissed me on the cheek. She took my hand and we started walking around the park. Then something caught my attention. There was some kind of wrestling game. I tugged my mother’s hand and we started walking towards the game. “Max, I don’t think that game is very appropriate for you.” she said to me. “But I want to play it!” I yelled back at my mother. “Ok, only once though.” I walked over to the game and looked at the screen. It read, “WWF 1999.” I took a quarter from my pocket and put it into the game. I got to pick my wrestler. I stopped at a guy named Shawn Michaels. I played the game once and my mother started walking away with me. “But I want to play again.” I said to my mother. “I told you, you can only play it once.” she said back to me. “Ok, fine.” We played a few more games and by the time you knew it, we were sitting on a bench all tired. I had a Pepsi can in my hand. My mother was holding a balloon that I got for free at the little circus we went to. We got off the bench and started walking towards the exit for the end of our day. I was kind of glad the day was over cause I couldn’t have done anything else at the park. We got to the exit and a guy in a clown outfit had a stamp in his hand. “Would you like a stamp to come back to Wacky Fun World?” the clown said to my mother. “No thank you.” she said back to him. I took my mother’s hand and started walking down the parking lot to out car. We got to it and I hopped back into the back seat. I put my seatbelt on and my mother started driving out of the parking lot. “Will we ever go back there mom?” I asked. “Maybe, that’s a good place for birthday parties. Maybe we can come back on your birthday party.” she said. “That would be awesome.” My mother drove out of the parking lot and down the street back to my house as I just played with my toy skateboard. I would do tricks off the window and just go along the side of the leather seating. We got back to our house and walked into the door with another day passed by.
