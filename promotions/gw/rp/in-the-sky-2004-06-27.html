...in the sky...

27 June 2004

...in the sky... Jonathan Drachen Global Wrestling Promo #1349 Date: 6/27/04 Location: RP 2 for High Noon Jonathan looked into her eyes and she was still asleep, exhausted. He’d not known why she was so tired, but he enjoyed watching her sleep. He didn’t want to wake her and have to tell her that he was going to have to leave for Mexico fairly quickly here and leave this wonderful business behind for some awfully hard training that he knew would come from Pablo. He had to leave so that he could be ready for the title match on Sunday against his enormous opponents McNasty and El Mariachi. He had to gather every bit of strength and ability within him to meet and exceed the challenge of even stepping in the ring before those thousands upon thousands of fans in Mexico City. He figured he had a little while. But he wasn’t sure how little or big a while that was, still he did not want to stir her, and he knew she would not let him go easily. So a plan B had to be formed. He reached up and over the side of the couch upon which he lay and grabbed the cordless hand unit telephone. He turned it on and dialed a number that he memorized from use very quickly. It was the number of the airline that was going to take him to Mexico City this evening at 5. The beeping of the numbers, much to the chagrin of Jonathan, awoke her. She looked into his eyes and smiled and asked who he was calling. Plan B had failed. He turned the phone off and put it on the floor. He looked into her eyes and smiled. “How do you feel about Mexico?” ---- Six hours later they were on an airplane heading to Mexico City. Luckily he’d gotten some seats together. He felt surreal. He felt like he was floating at some height higher than the airplane as he stared into her hair as she rested her head on his shoulder. Why didn’t I just do this earlier? Then the answer hit him. ---- The flashback colors are dizzy and erratic as this is not something Jonathan wishes to remember. It was all tinged in red, him having been beaten severely with a blackjack. He’d later discover his skull had nearly been cracked with that instrument of evil. He watched as Deamont and the Solution crippled and destroyed his last remaining friend on Earth. He watched powerless, being held down and kicked by one of the men whose faces he wished he did not know. He still felt the boiling of the burns he’d received just about a week before when they’d blown the cage. He still felt the pain of the destruction he’d received in the ring to be vying for the RUSH World Championship that he’d fought so long and viciously to be able to attain. He’d been dominated and now he was being beaten within an inch of his life, and they probably intended to go an inch and a half further. He watched as that inch no longer mattered and his only friend was broken and laid broken on the mat, having tried to save him. More than just red wetness began to drip down his face. The tears washed a little of the blood away as he stared at what would put his only friend into a coma and permanent paralysis. But now he also heard the words that went along with it: He signed a release, we’re not responsible. He heard Kahlil’s mother crying as she stood next to the hospital bed. His hospital bed. He heard her praying that she would not lose the man who had saved her son, nor her son. He knew he was to blame, yet she would never blame him. He remembered signing the check that RUSH should have signed for Kahlil’s care. He remembered visiting him, and always avoiding his mother’s phone calls. He remembered all the messages he’d deleted that started with, “Jonathan, he wanted to save you like you saved him…”, “Jonathan, this is not your fault….”, “Jonathan do not make me lose two sons…” He thought of the guilt that it had caused him. ---- “Angel, what’s wrong? Please, tell me.” His eyes had gone wide and looked to be as filled with pain as his soul. He shook himself and tried to throw it all away, now that she could see it. He said nothing, but hugged her closely. A tear seeped into the fabric of the shoulder of her shirt. ---- He was awake and she was gone. A series of dreams had progressed through his consciousness and he continued to awake from them for what seemed like forever. He remembered meeting the evil Galuda in UEF in his big attempt at a comeback after his recovery from his psychological disorders. He remembered becoming part of Galuda’s forces of darkness and becoming that essence of evil’s grand apprentice. He remembered the horrible things he’d done while serving his then master. He remembered how completely he had been altered. Then he remembered one day that he had met an angel and that all of that changed. He remembered who that angel was. That angel was her. Then he remembered how he’d began to hallucinate after leaving Galuda’s side. He remembered his life being one long nightmare that led into another one and another one. He remembered losing her. He remembered losing his mind again and again. He remembered the hells that Galuda had sent his mind into and how he’d only escaped them by running to the Rocky Mountains where his powers were weak. In places of majestic beauty and wonder like that, very little evil could exist. He remembered the hallucinations he’d been through. He remembered the girl that looked just like her that he’d met or thought he’d met. He remembered leaving her behind. Wait a second… ---- He looked into her eyes in horror and disbelief and wonder. He did not know who she was. He’d never answered his phone because he didn’t remember her voice. Who was she? He looked into her eyes and tried to form words. She looked at him and sensed he was terrified. She was terrified as well. “Wh wh whu where did you last see me?” The question was so simple, yet she began to search her mind for the answer. She began to scour her neurons. She knew exactly where and she even knew the day. But she was caught off guard by his terror at the asking of the question. “That night, in Colorado, when you left me alone and ran away…” ---- So this wasn’t the angel? Where was she? What was she? Who had taken him out of Galuda’s clutches? Who had lured him away from darkness? What…had lured him away? ---- “Wait. Where did I meet you?” “You met me on the bus, and we talked and...” “How did you know where to find me?” She looked into his eyes, there was less terror, but there was still terror. Perhaps he was just tense. He had seemed a little disturbed at the time. How she wanted to be angry with him for running away. But she couldn’t. All she wanted was to see him happy. All she wanted was… “How did you know where to find me?” “Well, Jonathan, this is the modern world, you told me your name… That’s all I needed.” “And you just…found me….why didn’t you just be angry and go away and hate me for that? Why didn’t you just go off and find something great where you were headed? Weren’t you heading for the West Coast to follow your dreams or whatever? Why’d you come back to Dallas for me?” She didn’t answer, she just pulled his face to hers and kissed him with fervor and passion that she didn’t even think herself capable of before. Then she looked into his crystalline eyes as they softened…with what she hoped was love. “I found…something great…” She smiled at him and he realized. That his angel had gone nowhere.
