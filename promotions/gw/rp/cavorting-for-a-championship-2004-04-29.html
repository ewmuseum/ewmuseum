Cavorting for a Championship?

29 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29/04/04 Cavorting for the Championship? Part I: You're Dead... Everyone that was able to view the spectacle known as 'Wrestlecade' saw something that would probably never be seen again for quite a long period of time. With JC held above his head, Sherwin executed one of his ultimate moves, dropping a now former employee with a violent shockwave courtesy of his Kinniku-Buster. It was at that point in time that the pieces of a scattered jigsaw began to fall into place and victory was assured. A new wave of confidence, a new power and a new state of mind had propelled this returning athlete from his bit-part status into something more... something MUCH more. The people were confident that Sherwin was going to brush through the opposition...and THAT is what he did. Regardless, despite these bumbling issues of victory and defeat, Rich had rushed straight to the nearest phone-booth following the end of the show. Running down the countless corridors and hallways of the arena, he looked high and low for something he could use to make an out-going call. However, exactly who would Rich have to call at this moment in time? Wang Kenobi was still in the building and his sister hadn't spoken to him for almost a month now... having walked away from her days before 'Clash of the Titans' was set to occur all of one month ago. Eventually, Rich managed to find a phone that was situated just outside the arena. Braving a slight chill and a gentle breeze, the new #1 Contender to Deacon Holmes' Cruiserweight Championship wasted little to no-time popping a few dimes into the booth and pressing a variety of numbers on the key panel. He grabbed hold of the reciever and placed it close to his left ear, waiting eagerly for somebody to answer on the other end. 'Hello?' they said in a weak and frail voice, potentially meaning that they'd just been awoken by this call. Rich's face seemed to beam with joy and excitement at this point, the thrill of hearing this voice being much more than anything would have previously thought possible. 'Hey...' Rich responded, a brief sense of reply coming from his voice. His smile was still spread widely across his face, feelings of anticpiation and anxiety beginning to rule the roost at which he sat. '... I know we haven't spoken for a long while and stuff and I know that you probably don't want to talk to me following everything that happened last month... but, if you've been following what I've done for this previous month, you'll see that what I chose to do with myself... it was the right choice.' He waited for a few moments, hoping to hear the voice that first responded again. Judging by the time-frame that Rich spoke of, the person that was on the other end of that phone line was his sister. She'd allowed her brother to walk away to compete in 'Clash of the Titans' and hadn't spoken to him since... along with his whole family. They'd not wanted their child to go back into the ring and suffer any form of injury that could linger for the rest of his life. Rich understood the concern, but they lack the idea of his passion and exactly how much professional wrestling meant to him. '... Oh, it's you...' she began, the tone of her voice sounding colder than winds of the hate. 'I was starting to wonder when you'd finally call back and babble on about how well professional wrestling has been going for you since YOU chose to walk away from us.' The expression on Rich's face changed from a look of enjoyment and love, to one of sorrow and depression. He though that his family could have at least showed some form of happiness at his win... but alas, it looks as though there was nobody blood-related to share such a victory with. '... You left me there with no choice, Jo. You left me there without any choice in the world. You know that nobody would have stepped in your way if you wanted to do something... and yet, when my turn comes around to do the thing that I had always enjoyed, the fort comes up and I'm left with nothing except my career.' Silence came from the other end of the phone for a few moments, the expression on Rich's face only getting worse. He wanted to hear something, anything... but nothing would come. As the seconds ticked on, Rich dropped all hope and slammed the phone down onto the reciever, his tormented emotion getting the better of him. Turning away, Rich began to walk off... when, for almost no reason, the phone began to ring. Sherwin stopped and looked to it, a confused glare spread across his face. Gingerly walking to the phone, Rich sheepishly picked the reciever up and held it to his left ear, answering with a simple, 'Hello?' '... You should never have called this place.' started the response, '... When you decided to leave your sister in that hotel to pursue your dream of being a champion, you effectively cut yourself off from this family for good. Cavort around for as many Titles as you want child, the welfare of yours is nothing to do with us anymore. As far as your sister and I are concerned, Rich Sherwin is nothing more than a wonderful childhood memory. Rich Sherwin is nothing more than a twenty-one year old who died when he chose professional wrestling as a career.' The chill behind the words affected every element of Rich's mind, body and soul. Of all the things that could have happened to him, this was not something he needed... especially with his first real opportunity at winning a Championship belt was only a week away. Heartbroken and distraught, Rich put the phone down again, not able to muster the energy to fire back with a witty remark or a heartless insult. For his family to be in a situation where they consider him dead... was more than he would have anticipated. Turning his back on the phone once more, he began to pace away, his head held down... this Championship shot now seeming as small as the number of family members he had left. Drifting off into the curtain of night, the phone began to ring again... it's cry for attention stabbing at the otherwise silent night. Sherwin stopped and turned around, staring at the booth. '... You wanted me to go... and now, I've gone.' With his final words, the scene slowly began to fade deep into night sky. After finishing the show on a high, Rich had been cruelly brought down to earth with something of an all-time low. Rejected by his family and without friend in this organization, one can only wonder how far this troubled mind will waltz before it falls... :: End ::
