How Far?

11 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11th June 2004 I've been told that I'm something of a good man, despite my aggressive and pessimistic streak. I've been told that I'm somebody that people could look up to, even though there are folks who are far cleaner and have lesser troubles than I do. In Global Wrestling, I'm considered to be something of an icon for the kids and the young people a lot of the time. I see the look in their eyes when they watch me walking around... watch me beating the hell out of somebody. They don't appreciate the fact that I'm destroying anyone... they appreciate the fact that I have the power to carry out such a thing. They understand that I don't hate many people, they just know that I've gotta do what needs to be done... because at the end of the day, this is my job. This is my life. It was a Friday evening, boring and depressive as ever. I sat alone in my room at the computer, staring into the monitor with intent as I rambled away with various keypresses and such. For such a relaxed situation and mood, I was clad in nothing more than a pair of boxer shorts. The scars from the crash were visable on my arms, the pain of their occurance still running through my mind from time to time. ---- Dear Ryan, I'm sorry I wasn't around to see how Evolution went for you after I wandered about to try and find Juan Gonzalez, I wasn't quite sure how I'd come back around without anything new. I'm just writing this letter to say that I'd appreicate it if you kept away from things for a little while, at the very least, until this whole matter with Juan Gonzalez has been well and truely eliminated from my spectrum of worry. I know the guy did a lot of damage to you... he did quite a bit to me too, but not enough to keep me from being able to perform at a level that is close to 100%. You on the otherhand, I just don't see how you're going to be able to honestly get through this. Stay back for a bit, I'll calll you soon. ---- I moved the mouse about on my desk with a free-hand and clicked a button before pushing the dray carrying the keyboard and mouse in. I picked myself up from the chair and walked away, rubbing my face with both hands. The past couple of weeks hadn't been the best in my life... having been run off he road by some bastard and then made to compete with a tag team partner who may very well have died on that same night. Turning around again, I looked down onto the desk at my cell. It lay dormant, apparently doing little more than collecting a little more dust every day that it hadn't been used or moved. '... You're never going to ring, are you?' I said to the phone in a cold and heartless manner. It'd been something of a while since anyone had taken the good time to give me even the shortest of phone calls. Hell, not even the punky little kids from various places had bothered calling me with pranks... or other stupid little schemes they believe a guy like me is going to fall head over heels for. I shook my head from side to side, a brief sigh being released from my body as I prepared to turn and walk off into the distance. BUZZ BUZZ BUZZ I froze on the spot for a few momemts, turning around to see the cell buzzing around on the desk. Stepping toward it with caution, I picked it from the table and flipped open the flap, holding it up to my right ear shortly thereafter. 'Hello?' I answered in a casual tone, not wanting whoever was on the other end of the line to catch the wrong end of my current poor mood and lack of motivation. 'Are - You - Mr - Sherwin?' replied a voice, digitized and extremely false. I was reluctant to answer at first, believing that this was going to be nothing more than some annoying digital response service... but then I remember that nobody had called this number for a while; not even any of these previous calls. Intreiged but still somewhat reserved, I prepared for the answer. '... Yes, this is Sherwin.' I responded, sounding calm but keeping a little edgy in the event that this was going to be nothing more than a cheap rouse. 'I understand that you're in something of a spot of bother at the moment?' came the reply, the digital voice of before apparently vanishing as quickly as it had appeared. 'Who am I talking to now?' I fired back, refusing to answer their question unless my question had been answered to it's full extent first. 'How rude of me...' they began, before choosing to continue, 'I'm a lawyer of the sort and I deal with all sorts of crimes, specifically those that involve attempted murder or any degree of physical and mental harm. I understand that you've been involved in quite the accident recently... and yet, have neglected to press any charges.' they replied, not really answering my question in the way that I had preffered it to be, but answering none the less. 'What of the accident? Are you another one of those suits who thinks they're going to win me over with a few fancy words and some promises about how I'm going to get even with a certain Juan Gonzalez in court? Screw you, you know nothing about how to get even with somebody. You don't do it somewhere where regulation is in place... you do it where nobody know's it's going to happen.' I fired back in a hail of bullets and grenades. I flipped the lid of my phone shut immediately after that, almost hurt that anyone would have considered me for legal help with regard to this whole case with Juan Gonzalez. Regardless, I had a lot more on my mind at the moment... at least, a lot more than that lawyer probably had. After all, not only am I going to have to watch Gonzalez challenge for GW's Television Title... but later in the night, I'm going to have to face John Zero. It's not what I'd call the best kind of recovery but I don't think anything really bad is going to happen. At least, I'm hoping that nothing goes wrong. I threw the phone on my bed and walked away, the thought of how far I wanted to go with Gonzalez running rampant through my mind. In all truth and honesty, I was still very undecided about the whole issue of a case against Gonzalez. I wanted to get my own back inside the ring... John, consider it all a 'Wrong Place, Wrong Time' ordeal... :: End of Part #1 ::
