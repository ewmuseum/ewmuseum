I'm not one to say thanks Roz Blayze Global Wrestling Promo #211 Date: 5-18-03 Location: Hospital again. ((There i sit again. I feel more helpless now then i did three days ago. I was sitting in this same Blue Pleather chair. My son laying in a Hospital bed with tubes sticking from his nose to help him breath. One in his Arm so he did not lose water. My self hurting from the Crimson Mask. I couldent really feel my nose. I hadent really said any thing about it. I just sat there with my arms folded. Watchign the chest of my son go up and down. Up and Down. Just as mine. But Mine as of my own will. Then i couldent stand it. I had to watch him. I cant stand sitting here. I Cant handle it being My self here with My own breath to keep me company.)) 'Looks like i have quite a Bit to look over and talk about.' This was the first time i had been in front of the camera since Overload. Since then i had been here with my Boy. Going through the Hardships that i had to to be bhere with him.I knew that i had a match coming up. 'Salaok. You really did a bang up job booking the Next Evo. Holems Vs. Blayze. The very man that helped me fight all the Harvesters off. I'm not one to say Thanks and Right here right now. I'm not. I'm not going to tahnk deacon untill i can see him face to face. Simple as that. I'm just not goign to do it. Deacon i have to ask you why? You risked beaing beaten to no end. Just to help me. Then i hear in an Interview that you say that idont exist? Thats good Deac. If i dont exist. Thne when You and i face each other in the octagon. You wont have to worry with me. You wont have to becuse. I'm takeing that match.' Really i diddent. deacon helps me with Salaok,Sage Quartz and the other memebers of the Harvester. Then says that i dont matter. if i dont then i dont. But to this moment. I do. I havent been my self as of late becuse i havent been my self. I just havent becuse my life has gotten to much in the way. They say that you need some one to takje care of this shit. I had some one untill about a week ago. When my Damn wife left me. Then i noticed that she was my hinge. With out here i diddent have any thing. The only thing that i had now wee doctor bill's and a sick Son. A Burtn down house and one good car. AS i finshed up this thought a woman walked in. not the same cocky bitch that i met last time. This lady seemed to feel my pain. 'Are you the father?' I could feel in her voice that she cared for me. she must have felt my pain at some point in her life. as i looked at her she became more and more into focus. I started to feel her sorrow. 'Yes.' This was a blunt answer. What more could i say. Does he Look like me? I mean what the hell? 'Sir. It's been estimated that your son wont live to see His next birthday. When is it? They wouldent tell me.' A sudden pegging pain hit me in the chest. I fell flat on my back. As i laid there and looked at the celing chokeing back tears. I answerd her. 'Three Days.' Then i began to tear. I wasent crying. There were just tears comming down my face. I began to wipe them away with the hard back of my hand. 'Sir. are you Ok.' 'No. What is the possibilty that my son will live' I asked this asi was getting back to to my feet. The looks on herface was just as devestating as the woirds that had just comeout of her mouth. 'Sir there is a ninty three percent chance that he wont make it.' at this point in time I was wondring if there was really any thing to live for. I diddent think so. This woman here looking at my body. Dead as it could be. she diddent have any thing foir me but nsorrow. and i hate pity. I try and understand that there is nothing then me haveing a son who is going to die. I cant understand why God had to do this to me. Then there came a rapping on the door. 'Sir. There is someone here to see you.' I stood up and walked to the door. 'Thank you.' Inside came ablack hand that shook mine and i walked out with him. 'MY son is going to die. Could it get any worse?' Me and this man walked out. Nothing i could do now but bask in the ever dimming light of a useless world that keep me in the hell i was divulged in
