The Zenith Manifesto (Vol. 3): Another Brick In The Wall (Part I)

13 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/13/04 Date: March 23, 1990 Time: 8:00 Location: Rammstein, Germany Daddy's flown across the ocean Leaving just a memory 'Father!!!' A scream echoes through the massive hallways of the Zenith mansion compound in Rammstein, Germany. Young Christian, about 6 or 7 years old, runs through the hallway... wearing a blue-violet polo shirt and a pair of stonewash jeans. His shoes lit up as he ran down the hallway with the red lights attached to them. Christian looked down the stairway and screamed once again. 'Faaaatheeerrrr!!!' Christian screamed down the staircase. The screams alerted an older Tera Zenith... who looked to be about 9 or 10 at the time. She turned off her Madonna CD and opened the door to her room and looked at Christian running around hurriedly in search of his father. A sly smile came across Tera's face as she exited her room. She closed the door behind and watches as Christian scurried over to her. 'Tera! Where is father?!' Christian asked. Looking up at his older sister with his cadet blue eyes. She looked down, trying not to make contact with her younger brother. She spoke quietly, almost as if she was ashamed... 'He... left us Christian.' She said, giggling quietly under her breath as she looked down. Her hair covered her face, which was beginning to sprout a big smile. '...What do you mean?' Christian asked. 'I heard him and mother fighting. He was screaming about the children, you in particular. He said that you were a monster, and he couldn't stand living with you anymore. He packed his things and stormed out the door. Mother has been looking for him all day, but couldn't find him. I'm sure she's somewhere driving around the city crying right now.' Tera said to Christian, who just continued to look up at her. 'Liar!' Christian screamed at Tera before attempting to walk away. 'If you don't believe me, before now mother was in her room crying all day. Go look in her room if you'd like, her bed is messed up. You know she always makes us make our beds, and since she always does too she must've been in there crying. You see Christian, if you wouldn't have been born, mother would still be here and so would father. Because of you they left!' Tera screamed before she started to fake cry, something she had been practicing to get stuff from mother and father. 'I'm sorry... what did I do?' Christian asked, his eyes swelling up in sorrow. 'Get out of out lives Christian!' Tera shouted before throwing a potted plant at Christian, who jumped out of the way and watched it shatter on the wall. Christian looked up at his older sister. He clinched his fists and his whole body began to shake. Tera stopped fake crying and looked at her brother, overjoyed at what she had achieved. A snap shot in the family album Daddy what else did you leave for me Daddy what d'ya leave behind for me Tera walked over to Christian Zenith and shoved him onto the ground in anger. Christian slid across the waxed wood paneled floor and almost made it to the staircase going down to the first floor before he caught himself. He looked up at his sister Tera who towered over him... 'I HATE YOU! WHY DON'T YOU GET OUT OF MY LIFE!' Tera screamed as she picked up a piece of the shattered flower pot on the floor. Christian got up and ran into his room. He had just enough time to jump up and close the golden chain that locked his door. Tera Zenith banged on his door screaming as Christian looked desperately around his room for something to defend himself with. 'LET ME IN!' Tera screamed pounding wildly on Christian's door. It didn't take long for Christian Zenith to find his aluminum tee-ball bat underneath his bed. He held it like he was up at the plate as Tera ran and jumped into his door and fell onto the ground, trying to break it open. Christian figured it was only a matter of time until the lock on his door broke. 'YOU BETTER OPEN THIS DOOR, I'LL BREAK IT, I SWEAR! AND THEN MOTHER WILL HATE YOU EVEN MORE THAN SHE DOES NOW, CAUSE IT'LL BE YOUR FAULT!' Tera screamed from the outside before she kicked the door, with no success. Emotions of deep sadness and self-hatred began to rise up inside of Christian... maybe he deserved to be gone. He apparently did something to disgrace his father and his family. Maybe it was time to give himself up. Just then, Tera screamed and jumped into the door and the lock finally gave and was pulled out of the wall as the door busted open and slammed hard into his wall. Christian Zenith dropped the baseball bat as Tera Kylie Zenith looked at him. Her fists clinched tightly, breathing hard for the all the excessive beating on Christian's door. He knew there was no escape now, he had to face his sister. All in all it was all just a brick in the wall All in all it was all just bricks in the wall Tera started walking towards Christian when suddenly a door slammed somewhere else in the house. Tera's eyes grew wide and she turned around in fear. Christian looked confused as Tera sprinted back into the hallway and ran down the stairs. 'Tera! Christian! Where are you guys hiding at?' Sylvia Zenith, Christian and Tera's mother screamed across the enormous compound. Just as she said that Tera came sprinting down the stairs. In a panic she ran over to Sylvia, acting as if she was running for her life. 'Mother! Mother!' Tera screamed running over to her and hugging her, 'Thank God you're home mother! Christian has gone crazy... first he threw a flower pot at me and shattered it all over the place. I locked him in his room and he busted his door down, and tried to hit me with a baseball bat!' 'Now Tera, you better not be making up stories again. Plus it's your job as an older sister to make sure Christian doesn't get into anything, now go upstairs and clean up that mess.' Sylvia said. 'But I didn't do it!' Tera screamed. 'Now young lady!' Sylvia responded, followed by Tera pouting up the stairs with her head down. Christian made his way to the stairway, where Tera shoved him down for his involvement, although minor, in her troubles. Christian got up and ran down the stairs as Tera cleaned up the shattered flower pot. 'Mother!' Christian shouted as he ran down the stairs. Sylvia turned around and saw her son. She looked down at him... 'Yes Christian?' She responded. 'Where is father?' Christian questioned. 'Didn't your sister tell you? Oh well, dear, we're going to move to America. A nice big city named Chicago, your father is already there and getting a lot of money for it. We're going to go there to live him soon, in a couple or weeks or so. It's so nice there, you're going to love it.' Sylvia said with a smile, 'You and your sister will really have to start getting along though, because we can't take all your friends with you. You'll be the only other people there that you know, and you'll have to be friends or you'll be lonely. Plus she's your sister and she loves you, even if she don't act like it sometimes, she's still just getting used to you being around.' Christian smiled before running back up the stairs, his mother just watched him go and shook her head, hoping her children could behave and get along for the long trip to America. Little did she realize... exactly how good they would get along, for at least one point in time. Next Installment: 'The Zenith Manifesto (Vol. 4): Another Brick In The Wall (Part II)'
