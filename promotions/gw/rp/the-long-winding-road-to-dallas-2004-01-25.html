The Long Winding Road to Dallas El Lunes Mascara Global Wrestling Promo #790 Date: 1-25-2004 Location: The Roads Of Texas The day is anew, the sun has now arisen and is shining down on the world below.....a world with billions of souls leading their lives, all with their own pasts, presents, and uncertain futures. Just as the futures of the two I watch over are never quite certain. Justin may become the living legend that he thirsts and wants to be....and my poor child El Lunes Mascara, may find finally find peace in his soul. If I could promise these things to them, I would, but it is not my place to interfere with the futures of my two children. All I can be is an observer....a watcher of the drama they call 'life'. I can only hope that their drama's will both end with a happy ending.....'~The Moon ~/~The streets are filled with life and the world is in constant motion, the same holding true for El Lunes Mascara and his young apprentice Justin Styles, the young Justin behind the wheel of a 2002, silver Mitsubishi Eclipse. The metallic paint emits beautiful and radiant gleams, all due to the bright Texas sun as Justin cruises along a long stretch of road with bright green palm trees lined up, a row on each side. El Lunes sits within his seat motionless, turning his head with the hood down on his cloak as he looks out with his aqua blue eyes at this strange new world he has yet to see, looking fascinated like an infant at the sight of all these new things. Justin is laid back comfortably on his plush leather seat, handling the wheel with one hand as he fiddles with the radio dial until he hears the barraging, take no prisoners sound of hard rock overtake his car. The speakers blast 'Creatures' by 311 with no remorse as Justin rocks his head to the hard guitar interludes, the windows down so all can hear his music with the wind brushing through his untied black and blonde streaked hair. As they begin to race by the huge open plains of Texas, bevies of beautiful young Texas girls, as gorgeous as music and movies have depicted them, romp in the grass atop of regal horses. El Lunes continues studying everything he can take within eyesight as Justin rips up the open road, our brooding luchadore saying to him.~/~ El Lunes:'Texas is as beautiful as you have described young one. Es muy guapa (It is very beautiful).' ~/~Justin give his smooth smirk, lowering the volume on his music just enough to hear El Lunes and the lyrics at the same time before replying excitedly.~/~ Justin:'Was there ever any doubt? Texas is PARADISE Lunes. How you can't love it here, I'll never know. Too bad we won't be able to take in the sites too long...You having to lay down some beatings in GW and all. It really pisses me off when I gotta give up honey's and the Texas sun for some no-talent hack just begging for that luchadore a** whoopin'. Well, since my time can't be replaced....make sure their spines can't be replaced when you go to work on that punk. This is make or break maestro (master) and I know you won't let me or your fans down. It's proving time in that fed, and you WILL be the force from South of the Border to be reckoned with!!' ~/~With a sigh, El Lunes rests his arm on the door as the air also beats against his hair too, the Mexican legend fixing his white mask so it rests comfortably on his eyes and nose before saying to Justin.~/~ 'What did I tell you about being cocky papito? That has been the downfall of many opponents where I come from. You must never underestimate your opponent...' ~/~Justin replies with his usual wise-cracking and youthful tone, barely paying attention to the road just so he can make his snappy answer.~/~ Justin:'When your opponent sounds like a Sub-Zero ripoff, ya know, you kinda have reason to be cocky jefe. What is he gonna do, shoot an ice ball at you and 'FINISH YOU'!?' El Lunes:'No, no...of course not Justin. But you must realize some opponents hide their true threatening nature. You must remember, Ice had to have some kind of talent to get into the GW, especially with his reputation....I just have to show that I have more of it. You and I walk down the same road to our respective goals, let me show you the proper way to make your walk a long one.. The way MY road will bend depends on how well I do against him....so I have to make this match count papito. This has to be taken seriously, for it only takes three seconds to make or break a career in the world of wrestling young one. Besides, when I make a promise to my beloved, that being beating Ice to a rotten pulp until I am ready to present them before the moon to be judged.....I intend to keep it. I have yet to tell a lie to my sweet Maria and that trend will not begin at Evolution. Ice will come under the shroud of darkness, but the world he enters when he does will be like none he has yet to EVER experience. ~/~El Lunes comments were spoken with that same eloquent, yet intense voice he is known for, making smile as he turned the wheel slowly to turn into a sharp bend, El Lunes getting a nice view of the glistening ocean as the sun beads down upon the water, making an obvious beauty even more gorgeous. Swept up in the elegance of all he is seeing, El Lunes asks with the soft part of 'Creatures' still in the background.~/~ El Lunes:'Isn't all of this wondrous Justin...like a white rose...rare and one of a kind..' ~/~Justin gives his mentor a disarrayed stare as he finishes turning the bend, looking out at the large road signs for a certain destination while saying.~/~ Justin:'Seriously Lunes...with all due respect. How do you say this? Well...you sound like you wanna screw nature or somethin! I mean, when's the last time you've said 'DA** GIRL YOU GOT A PHAT A**!!' or 'LET ME GET A BISCUIT TO GO WITH ALL THAT JELLY!'? I swear man, your worrying me esse'.' ~/~Smiling slightly, El Lunes looks out to the sun with a lingering look. He doesn't even bothet to look to Justin as he replies to his protege' with an eloquent counter.~/~ El Lunes:'You will never understand what my true desire is little papito. You have not yet grown to that level of pure understanding and knowledge. But I am not one to cast stones so you shall not see me raise my arm.' ~/~Justin looks unimpressed by his answer, pulling off to an exit on the road and sighing with slight annoyance, the scene slowly fading to black as he exclaims for the whole Texas countryside to hear.~/~ Justin:'Great, I'm being trained by Ernest Hemmingway....just my luck. I don't know whether to wrestle, or turn in a book report.
