Two friends having a drink(High Octane RP)

23 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/23/04 The mood in the Dark Horse saloon was at an all-time high. People were dancing, drinking, riding the mechanical bull, etc. Then the door swung open and in walked Brick. The crowd cheered “HEEEY!!!” after he walked in. He looked around and saw his buddy Ely Braxton at the bar. “Hey, Ely!” Brick called, before heading over to the bar. He saw Braxton having his usual Texas mudslide. In this case, the glass was half-empty. “Bartender, lemme have an Angus Bingham.” “That’s one cranberry juice coming up.” Brick then sat on the stool right next to Braxton. “So, how are you feeling, man?” he asked. It was the day after Evolution. Braxton had a match with Mark Makonie, the current thorn in Bricks side. The match came as a result of an incident from the week before. After Brick had beaten John Zero for the Platinum title, Makonie ambushed Brick after making the lights in the arena go out. He then tried to spray paint the “N-word” on his back when Braxton and Jesse Briggs intervened. As Makonie made his getaway, he called Braxton and Briggs race traitors. That’s what prompted the match. “I’ve been better. I’m just glad I got a piece o” that sumbitch.” “Listen, man, I really appreciate you going to bat for me. That means a lot.” “Don’t mention it. Punks like Makonie make me wanna puke. Judging somebody because o” the color o? their skin. A person ain’t always the outside. It’s how what they do, how they live, how they act, and how they love. That’s what’s important.” “Amen to that.” “Too bad Makonie’s so damn twisted by his hatred that he can’t understand.” “Yeah, well, at High Octane, I plan to make him see that.” It was then that the bartender returned with Brick’s drink. “That’s one Angus Bingham special.” “Thanks.” As Brick took a sip from his cranberry juice, Braxton remarked, “Now this would piss Makonie off.” “What would?” “Us. Two men of different backgrounds and different colors, sitting together, having a drink.” “You start singing “Ebony and Ivory” and I’m gonna knock you on your ass.” “Brick, you know I can’t sing. Besides, I was leanin” more towards “Jungle Fever”!” he joked. “Man, shut up!” “Just like Martin Luther King said, huh?” “Yeah, when he dreamed that one day a person would not be judged by the color of their skin but by the content of their character.” “You’re a good man, Brick. That goes beyond physical.” The two then clinked glasses together and drank. “So, how’s your friend, the one you had looking for Bubba?” “Talon Eagleclaw” He’s in stable condition.” “Has he said who was behind Bubba disappearing?” “He was about to, when he passed out. Next thing, I hear tires screechin” outside. When I get outside, I see a black car pulling off, with the license plate missing. I tried to follow them, but somebody slashed the tires on my truck.” “How’s Christina handling this?” “Best as you can expect. Like she doesn’t have enough on her plate with this whole mess with Train.” “Yeah, beatin” up on those football players.” “Plus the son of a bitch tried to scare her. I ever get my hands on “im?” “Hope you do.” “Well, I might have to wait a while. Christina suspended him for a week.” “Never a dull moment, is there?” “Wouldn’t have it any other way.”
