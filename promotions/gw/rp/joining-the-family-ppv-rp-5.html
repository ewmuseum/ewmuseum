Date: today 'Hello, Hedley's, this is Star speaking.' I sit and listen to her beautiful voice, as I drive down the highway, in my search to see what is wanted of my by the monster. I do not reply to her words, merely sit and enjoy the moment of hearing her angelic voice once again. My dried lips begin to quiver as I part them slightly in attempt to udder something, before I am cut off and stopped before I can even get started. 'Hello? Is anyone there?' My heart jumps into the my throat, as I think of all the things I need... no all the things I want to say to her. To tell her how sorry I am for the pain I have caused her. To tell her I never meant for what happened to occur. To tell her how much I miss her, and need her back in my life. But my voice is absent. Running in fear from leaving it's save haven. Leaving me to just sit there, and listen to my love hang up on me. I hang up my cell phone as well, and toss it onto the passenger seat next to me. My hand slowly glides away from the seat, and over towards the radio. I turn up the volume, trying to get her out of my mind. Maybe finding a song to distract my thoughts will help my cause. Then again maybe not, but either way, I have to get her off of my mind, and focus on the task ahead. The task of seeing why the monster has called for my presence. What is his reasoning to end my sessions, ruin my life, and call me to his office? Does it have to do with this Tuesday night at Maximum Carnage? Or is what he wants from me totally unrelated? I guess those questions floating around in my head are my reason for this visit to see him. To see the monster face to face with this monster. 'I am a little bit of loneliness a little bit of disregard. Handful of complaints, but I can’t help the fact that everybody can see these scars.' I begin to sing along softly to Linkin Park's 'Faint', and my mind begins to settle. The soothingness of the loud rock meledy brings me to ease, as I pull into the parking lot, and look up at the large building that is the home of SOMA CORPORATION. The large corporate building appears to be nothing but glass, with a steel frame holding it together. And the dark tint, giving it a black glow gives a dark aura to the place. But none of that is of any concern, or bother to me. It actually is kind of refreshing to see a place that is similar to the darkness I call my home. I park the Expedition, and head into the lobby on the first floor of the building and approach a pale red head, sitting behind a large dark grey desk, with a black counter top. The woman doesn't notice me entering the room, and proceeds with her paperwork. Her red hair rests upon her shoulders and sways back and forth as she does, moving stacks of papers around her desk. She turns and takes a small gasp as she notices me, and it is then her bust, truly shows. Her blue eyes glow as she looks at me, and somewhat smiles, as she notices my eyes wonder down the front of her body and focus on her large chest. 'Mr. Ice. We have been expecting you, and are glad you could make it. However, I must regretfully infrom you that Mr. Soma is not here right now. But he did leave this for your.' She hands me a black suit, with a note attatched. The note reads. 'I believe these are your measurements. Welcome to the family.'
