A Love Story

22 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22nd August Love... We all have felt it, no matter how small it was. Since birth we have always shared a certain connection with our parents or caregivers, okay for most of us that connection is pretty much screwed up by the parents in later life but for the first couple of years we share a connection with our caregivers, a connection that can often be called love. No one can go through life without feeling love, if anyone has not then they are dead inside. A lot of you will probably say that love is for the weak but in my opinion love is what makes you strong. I mean I love wrestling and I love the thrill of the fight and that is what keeps me going, love is what keeps me fighting and the love for victory is what makes me give it my all. However this is not the true love I speak of. The type of love I speak of is that, which we share with that one person, that one person who when we see them our heart skips a beat, that one person who you cannot see your life without, that person who makes your life complete. I am a great believer in true love; I believe that you only get one chance at true love, sure you can have multiple partners in your lifetime but out of all of them there is only ever one true love. I hate the fact that nowadays marriages only seem to last a few weeks and then divorce rears it's ugly head. I am not a Christian, far from it in fact but I do believe in the sanctity of marriage and that once you have chose that one person that you are with them for life, no matter what. It is a very old prospective I know but although at times I hated my mother she did teach me that marriage is a wonderful thing and that it should be kept sacred. In my condition, you wouldn't expect me to be in love or for that matter be with someone, but the truth is I am in love and I am indeed with someone. This may sound bizarre and strange to you and raise many questions, questions which I hope I can soon answer but believe me this love I share is the real thing. The woman who has all my love and devotion, is my wife, Rachel Myers... I placed the phone down onto the receiver, after another extremely long conversation with an advertising agency wanting to use my 'image' to sell their product. As I placed the phone down the front door slid open and my wife's heavenly voice came soon after. 'Adam I'm home will you come give me a hand with this shopping?' I didn't reply as I already knew that saying 'No' would cause a 2 hour argument that I had no chance of winning. I quickly jabbed a sterilising injection into my arm and sprinted for the door. There standing at the door was my beautiful wife, with her red hair tied back and her sparkling blue eyes staring at me and her perfectly moulded face and a smile that would make your heart melt. I picked up the shopping and gave her a kiss, she smiled again. 'Was it busy at the shops?' I asked as I carried the bags through to the kitchen. 'It was surprisingly quiet actually,' Rachel said as she took off her coat and placed it on the hangers, 'Although I did bump into Peter.' 'Peter Willows?' I asked surprised. 'Yeah I know I was surprised too, apparently he had just spoken to you and now he was heading back home but he just stopped off to pick up some essentials.' 'Essentials?' I asked dubiously. Rachel laughed. 'Yeah 'essentials'. He was hiding something magazine shaped in his jacket as he spoke to me. I think the loneliness of travelling around is getting too much for him, I think he was looking for a release.' Both of us laughed, that was one of the many things I love about Rachel she has a great sense of humour. 'Anyway he asked me to ask you how preparations were going for your match on Monday. So sweetheart how are they going? Have you got over that dream that was bothering? You gave me a freight when you jumped up in bed. You looked like you had seen a ghost.' 'I had in a way,' I whispered to myself. 'What was that?' 'I said I have got over it. Training is going a lot better, I just don't want to screw up on my first big match in the GW I mean it is too important to my future in the GW. I know Kitty and Rich will have put in as much work as me, maybe even pushed themselves that little bit extra. I am going out there to win but I have to put on a good performance win or lose to show the GW I mean business. If I screw up on Monday then the GW may regret bringing me up for the GU and might send me hurtling back down there.' 'Oh sweetheart come here.' Rachel had her arms wide open, her eyes full of warmth and comfort. I moved over and grabbed her in my arms. We stood there cuddling. 'You are speaking about what ifs and maybes darling,' Rachel said reassuringly, 'All you need to know is that I love you with all my heart and that I will always be proud of you. Besides I have seen you in the ring and I have seen you train and I know you are going to be ready for this match, you know why?' 'Why?' I asked as I kissed her on the forehead. 'Because your Adam Myers! And you are one of a kind!' We chuckled and gave each other a kiss as we stood there simply holding each other... I truly love that woman and nothing would destroy me more than if she was taken away from me. I knew I loved that woman the moment I met her. It was just when I was getting started in Japan, I had gone through 2 years of intense training at the Dojo under a great man known as Bruce Uemura learning the art of Capoeira and also learning the discipline of using the art to it's full advantage. I had become a disciplined fighter and I was about to make my first tour of the Japanese circuit before returning to the Dojo to learn even more about the art of Capoeira. However before I started my first tour I was called into the Dojo by Bruce to make sure I was prepared... 'Welcome Adam,' Bruce said bowing as he greeted me. Bruce was a man of about 45 years old, although his eyes still sparkled like a child, his face told a different story. Indeed Father Time had not been kind to Bruce as his face was full of wrinkles and worry lines. He was also quite small in stature standing at about 5'1. However he was built like a brick house, he had spent years building up his body and learning all the great Martial Arts. You would sure not want to mess with Bruce Uemura. 'Hey Bruce, how is it going?' I said as I returned his bow. 'Good, Good! How do you feel about your first tour?' 'I am a bit nervous but I should be okay. I know how rough Japanese matches can be, especially the King Of The Deathmatch Tournaments they have out here, which I see you have kindly entered me into.' Bruce chuckled to himself. 'I have faith in you Adam, if you come out on top, your name will be remembered always in Japan.' 'I wouldn't hold up much hope, it is only my first tour.' 'Have faith in yourself Adam, I have told you time and time again that you are the finest student I have ever taught. You took to the art of Capoeira like a duck to water. It usually takes at least 4 years for someone to learn just the basics. I am not saying that you know it all, as that is why I want you to come back to the Dojo after this first tour, as there is more I have to teach you.' 'I understand Bruce and I will do you proud out there.' Bruce placed his arm on my shoulder and looked straight into my eyes. 'I am already proud of you Adam, never forget that.' 'I won't Bruce, I promise.' We both smiled and there was a brief second of silence. 'Okay so first of all I want to see how your kicking is coming along. So over to the kick bag and we will try your roundhouse kicks.' Bruce said breaking the silence. 'Sounds good.' I said clapping my hands to show I was ready. We moved over to the kick bag, which was a variation of the punch bag, but it was a lot slimmer and harder. Bruce grabbed the bag from behind and nodded to me to tell me to go. I swung my right leg and smacked the bag right in the centre. Bruce staggered back with the bag from the force. He smiled. 'Good, now again with the right.' I hit the bag with my hardest right roundhouse kick. Bruce smiled again and nodded for me to continue. As I continued out of the corner of my eye I saw the entrance door to the Dojo swing open and in stepped (although I didn't know her at the time) Rachel. I couldn't take my eyes off her as she glided across the Dojo towards the changing rooms. 'Adam! Adam! Stop missing the bag! Adam!' Bruce yelped. I snapped my head back round to look at Bruce who was obviously trying to avoid my flying feet by standing a few metres away. 'Sorry Bruce I was just...' 'Looking at that girl I know.' 'She was amazing, did you see her?' 'Yeah, she is quite beautiful, she comes in here quite regularly just to work out. She is a black belt in Kick Boxing you know.' 'Wow, what is her name?' 'Rachel Ashton...Now can we please concentrate on your kicks.' 'I have to meet her!' I exclaimed ignoring Bruce's order. 'But Adam...' It was too late, Bruce's last words had fallen on deaf ears, as I was already making my way towards the changing rooms. As I drew closer I realised I couldn't go barging in to the women's changing room but I had to meet Rachel. Thankfully as I approached the changing rooms, out stepped Rachel wearing her kickboxing gear and looking stunning might I add. She gave me a look and smiled and kept on walking. I chased after her. 'Excuse me, erm Rachel?' I said not knowing what else I could say. She turned around to face me looking concerned. 'How do you know my name?' She asked suspiciously. 'Oh, Bruce told me.' I said pointing over to where Bruce was looking furiously at me. 'He looks pretty mad.' 'Yeah he can get like that, if he is ignored.' We both laughed. 'I'm Adam Myers by the way.' I said as I held out my hand. 'Rachel Ashton.' She replied shaking my hand. And that was that, over the next 3 years we became incredibly close. I told her everything about me, telling her of my condition and how I had to wear the suit etc. I expected her to run a mile but she completely understood and said that she admired my outlook on life. She told me everything about her, how she worked for a media station in Japan as a Producer and how she missed England (she was from Manchester just a few miles from Lancaster). We found out that we were so similar and that we were kindred spirits, she often came to my matches and cheered me on, and then just as I was leaving Japan I asked her to marry me and well you know the answer. She gave her notice into her job and set up a job in America working for MTV, and the rest as they say is history. So as you see Rachel's love has made me stronger than ever and with her behind me, I feel invincible. I am ready for Evolution, I am ready to go in there and win, the question is will my opponents?
