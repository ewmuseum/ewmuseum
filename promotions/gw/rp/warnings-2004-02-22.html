Date: 2/22/04 The sounds of Justin Timberlake's 'Rock Your Body' ring throughout the large wooden oval room. As the music plays, Brandi Sights walks into the room. She is in a pair of tight hip hugger jeans, and a faded denim baby tee. Her viberant red hair is pulled back into a low ponytail. Brandi quickly walks about the room, and heads over towards the silver and blue stereo, which sits alone on a countertop, and turns it off. The room is now silent as Brandi exits it. She returns shortly, with a matching denim purse draped over her left shoulder, and aims for the door. As she grabs the doorknob, the sound of 50 Cent's 'In Da Club' softly plays. Brandi immediately stops and opens up her purse. She shuffles around inside the small purse, before pulling out a leopard printed cell phone. 'Hello? Who is this?' Brandi goes silent while the voice on the other end of the line speaks. Her face, eyebrows and nose squinted and crumpled, quickly smooths out as her eyes widen, and her face becomes stricken with fear. Her mouth opens as if she were wanting to speak, but nothing comes out. Brandi's bottom lip begins to tremble slightly, as she continues her silence while listening to the voice on the other end of the line. Brandi's right hand slowly rises towards her face. She inserts her right index finger just past her lips, and begins to nerveously nibble on her light blue fingernail. Her frightened expression quickly is replaced by a look of anger, and her finger is promptly jerked away from her mouth. 'You're ...you're a sick freak. I don't know who you are. I don't know who got you this number. But, you can go to hell!' The leopard printed cell phone is violently pulled away from Brandi's left ear, slammed shut, and thrown back into her small denim purse. She lets out a frusterated huff, and opens the door. Cautiously she peeks her head out of the doorway, and looks up and down the hallway before heading out of the room. Once in the hallway, Brandi hurrily marches towards the front exit, and outside into the large openness outside her hotel. Brandi again stops, and looks around before going on her way. She walks around to the backside of the building and towards the parking garage. Once she reaches the door, she shows her identification, to the security guard at the front of the garage, and heads in. 'Have a good evening Miss Sights.' 'Thanks Tony.' Brandi smiles at the plump, security guard, and walks down a row of fancy, European styled cars. About three cars down from the end of the row is where Brandi stops. She again reaches into her purse, and this time pulls out a set of keys. Confidently she walks over to a teal colored Sting Ray, and opens its door. As she opens the door and prepares to enter, she is stopped, as she sees a large white piece of paper, with black marker writing on it that reads.'STAY AWAY FROM HIM BITCH' Brandi darts her hand forward, and grabs the paper, crumpling it up instantly, and throwing it to the wayside. She rushes into her car, starts the engine, and speeds out of the garage. FADE OUT .................... ..................................... FADE IN Brandi drives a few miles, before reaching her destination, a large building with a giant neon yellow signt that says ' CHUCK E. CHEESE ' S'. Brandi pulls into the parking lot, and immediately sees Ice standing outside waiting for her. She quickly finds an open space and parks the car. Once out of her car, she casually walks over to the awaiting Ice. 'I can't believe I let you hoax me into coming to this place.' Ice shoots her a quizzicle look. 'What? This place below your standards Mzzzz Sights.' Brandi smiles, and playfully slaps Ice on the shoulder. 'I didn't ever expect someone like you would come to a place like this. I mean, it's a kid's place, full of family fun. That really doesn't match up with the Ice I've come to know through GW.' Ice arrogantly smirks at her. 'There's alot of things that I do outside of my job, that you wouldn't expect from a guy like me.' Brandi begins to say something, but is interrupted as Ice's cell phone begins to ring. He reaches into his left front pocket and pulls it out. 'Hello? This is him. No, I'm a little busy right now. I'm about to eat dinner. Chuck E. Cheese's. Why? Who is this? Hello?..........Hello?......HELL - OOO?' Ice shrugs and closes his shiny silver cell phone, and places back in his left front pocket. 'Who was that?' 'I dunno, doesn't matter. After you?' Ice opens the door for the beautiful red head, and she smiles and nods in appreciation. As Brandi walks by Ice and into the building, Ice's head tilts to the left. He quickly eyes her body up and down, before following her into the building as the door slowly closes shut. FADE OUT...................................... .........................................FADE IN Brandi, with an ear to ear smile on her face, pushes her way through the door and out of the building. Ice is half a step behind her. He doesn't quite have a smile on his face, but the expression is far from the usual pissed off look he normally wears. The two walk together to Ice's black Expedition. 'You know that was mighty nice of you to give all your tickets to that little boy. I'm pleasantly surprised.' 'The kid was in a wheelchair. He wasn't going to win any tickets himself, someone was eventually going to give him some anyways. I don't see the big deal.' 'Well, all I know is you keep stuff like that up, and you're going to lose that reputation you carry around the GW locker room.' 'Well, I won't lose it if no one else finds out about this now will I?' Brandi playfully smiles at Ice. 'I wonder who might say such a thing? But I wouldn't be surprised to hear that rumor floating around the locker room come Monday.' 'Oh you wouldn't dare.' Brandi's playfull smile grows even wider. 'Maaaaybe.' 'Well, then I guess that secret informer, who is spreading all the rumors will have to suffer my wrath.' 'OoooOooo. Big tough guy now aren't we.' A smirk creeps across Ice's face 'I'll show you tough guy' With those words he darts forward and grabs Brandi by the sides of her stomach. Her face turns a bright red color, almost matching her red hair, as she squirm and wriggles around, fighting off Ice's attack. 'Stop!........It.......tickles! AHAHAHA' 'Now what did I say about this never happening?' 'ALRIGHT! ALRIGHT! If you please stop, I won't say anything! I promise!' 'Now that's more like it.' Ice stops his monsterous tickle attack of Brandi Sights. The two stand there frozen, their eyes locked on one another's. Ice's stare is a blank stare, one that does not allow his thoughts or emotions to be seen. Brandi's however is the complete opposite. Her gaze into Ice's blue eyes, is one of lust, one of enjoyment, one of fondness. The two stand there in complete silence, staring at each other before Ice breaks the silence. 'I should probably get going. I've got a long day of training tomarrow.' 'Ye..yeah. I should probably get going as well. Thank you for the nice evening.' 'The pleasure was all mine.' Brandi again begins to smile. 'I'll see you on Monday then?' 'Alright. See you then. Bye.' Ice nods and steps into the large black SUV. The engine rumbles, and Ice backs out, and pulls away, as Brandi walks over towards her Sting Ray. Brandi reaches her car, and opens up her purse. She begins to fumble around searching for her keys, before finally pulling them out. As she places the key into the side of the door, a hand forcefully wraps itself around her mouth. A woman's voice whispers into her ear. 'Don't move, don't make a sound, and no one gets hurt. Understand?' Brandi's body begins to slightly tremle as she nods in compliance to the person behind her. 'You're shaking. That's good. You should be afraid. But you don't seem to recognize me. I called you earlier. You weren't very kind to me. As a matter of fact, you were one RUDE BITCH!. I asked you nicely on the phone to not go to dinner tonight. I even went to the trouble of breaking into the parking garage, and then into your car to leave you that little note telling you to stay away from the man I love. But again you didn't listen. So I decided to pay you a personal visit. To give you one last warning. STAY AWAY FROM MY MAN! If you don't, bad things will happen. And we wouldn't want a pretty little thing like you have something bad happen to her now would we?' The voice uses her free hand and begins playing with Brandi's red locks. 'Now, I'm going to let you go. Now I want you to close you eyes. Keep them closed. Do not move. Do not make a sound, until you count to one thousand. Do you understand?' Brandi again nods in compliance. 'Good. Now remember what I said Brandi. Stay away from my man.' The captor slowly removes her hand from Brandi's mouth. Brandi doesn't make a sound, and doesn't move for what seems like an enternity. Finally, she turns and looks over the parking lot. It's empty. Her body is still shaking as she places the keys in the lock, and unlocks the door. She quickly slides into her vehicle. The engine starts shortly after she enters, and she speeds away out of the parking lot. FADE OUT
