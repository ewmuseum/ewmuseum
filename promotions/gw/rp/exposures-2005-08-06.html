Exposures

6 August 2005

Date: 8-6-05 779, somewhere in modern-day Austria Virgil's eyes opened slowly, covered with the bleary haze of a long sleep, as though awakening at the end of an extended sickness. His limbs refused to move, despite his better effort to turn himself over, so that he wouldn't be staring into the dusty stone floor, which suddenly seemed a little more inviting, as his eyes clouded again, and slowly closed. When they opened again, he could hear voices next to him. What they were saying was incomprehensible, but he soon felt a pair of strong arms underneath his, pulling him up to his feet. For a moment, it seemed as though he could stand on his own power, but he lost his balance, stumbling into a plaster wall. His eyes finally cleared, and he could see the puzzled face of a town militia guard, looking him over. 'Are you well, milord? You appear to be ill...' Virgil looked down at his hands, which were still the same as he remembered them. Hardened from his years of combat lessons as a child, learning the proper way to wield a sword and polearm, in preparation for his life as the commander of the Sterescu family army. Under them was the finery of a noble, which he had been forced to take on instead of a suit of plate mail, when his brother strangely disappeared one night, leaving the manor under his command. He was still... alive? Why did that feel as though it should not be? 'Yes... I do not recall my journey to this place... but I am fine.' The guard nodded, still looking strangely upon him. 'You are deathly pale, milord... and your clothes are torn. Were you accosted by a thief?' Virgil looked down at his clothes, noticing the fine fabric covered with dust from the street, and torn in several places... rather embarrassing. 'I do not recall... but I shall find an inn for the night. Do not concern yourself with me, good defender. I should not wish to be the cause of dereliction from your duties.' The guard bowed, picking his halberd up from the wall it was leant upon, the sharp blade glinting in the moonlight. He quickly made his way back to the streets, dodging beggars in the alleyway. Virgil watched him curiously. He was no longer in Spain... that much was entirely obvious, from the German he had just spoken to the guard. He began to walk somewhat shakily from the alley, his eyes immediately drawn to the slightly unfamiliar construction of Austrian houses... dark wooden planks created a distinctive exoskeleton to the stone underneath... and the pointed steeple of a cathedral pressing its way obsequiously toward the heavens, asserting itself as the most important building in town. 'A cathedral...' he thought, forcing his legs to function properly and gracefully once again. 'Surely a priest can guide me to a tailor, and inform me exactly where I am.' The streets were empty, as it was some time quite late in the evening. The only men on the streets were the guards, beggars, and... himself. A Spanish noble in a foreign land, wandering the streets like a commoner. How ridiculous he must have seemed... He stumbled upon the marble steps of the cathedral's entrance, barely catching himself before his face impacted the cold stone. 'No... I mustn't humiliate myself before them, they will not believe I am who I say.' He forced himself to his feet again, straightening his back, and steadying himself against the door, before pulling open the mighty wooden portal, and stepping into the nave. He crossed himself. 'Ave Maria, gratia plena, Dominus tecum, benedicta tu in mulieribus, et benedictus fructus ventris tui, Jesus. Sancta Maria, Mater Dei, ora pro nobis peccatoribus, nunc et in hora mortis nostrae...' 'Amen.' Virgil turned his head slowly to see a younger acolyte who had come to see who had entered the church at such a strange time. However, when Virgil set eyes upon the young man, he dropped his Bible, obviously startled. Virgil blinked at him, rather confused. 'What is wrong?' The acolyte bowed his head several times in apology. 'I apologize... your appearance startled me.' Virgil turned his gaze toward the monstrance at the front of the large hall, slightly offended by the forwardness of this young disciple. 'Go and find a priest... I am not from this area, and I seek guidance.' 'Yes... I will return with him shortly.' Virgil took a couple of steps forward, still somewhat loathe to enter a church... but his betrayal had been by men, not God. He vowed not to dwell upon it in such a sacred house... but he would no longer have to, as the priest entered, terrified acolyte in tow. He brandished a crucifix at Virgil, shouting. 'Leave this place, foul demon! Your presence will not be tolerated in the house of God!' Virgil backed up in surprise and alarm, toward the door. He held up his hands, as he spied a pair of knights shuffling toward the entryway, alerted by the sudden shouting. 'No... you are mistaken, I only wish to know...' The priest continued to take steps toward him, gritting his teeth in grim determination. 'You will learn nothing, for you have turned your back on the teachings of God! Leave this place immediately, if you value your accursed life!' Virgil heard the sound of swords being drawn, and backed into the door, shoving it open with a sudden burst of strength, snapping the top hinge as though it were made of the thinnest strip of wood, and breaking into a full run down the marble steps, unable to pause to consider the very strange event that had just occurred. He stumbled to the street, and could swear he heard the clanking noise of plate boots behind him, and shouted German which he could not completely understand. Seconds later, as the cathedral fell farther behind him, he could hear the bells... ringing continuously. They were calling the men of the town to the square... he couldn't stop running. Fear raced through his heart, as he ducked into an alleyway, and the street suddenly gave way beneath him, dumping him with a crash of stone and wood into the wine cellar of a tavern... which seemed to be completely dark, save the tiny squares of moonlight leaking in through the small windows. He stepped cautiously to them, and stared at the somewhat distant square, where men were beginning to gather. Virgil could see many torches now, and various implements of the field being thrust into the night angrily. '...they are after me? What have I done to affront the church so?' A hand landed roughly upon his shoulder, and he was too paralyzed with sudden fear to turn. 'I should not have left you to your own devices. You have caused a riot with your naive ignorance. We must flee immediately.' - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - Present day, Bratislava, Slovakia 19.53 Virgil awoke with a start as he heard a door slam somewhere in the small hotel room in Bratislava, where he had been staying with his young prisoner, Alexis Key, while Lia had gone to visit her contact, Cyrus. Of course, she had already left, he silently complained almost immediately upon gaining full consciousness. And then he noticed the brightness of the room, and the sunbeam resting lazily on his chest. He scrambled out of bed hurriedly, spilling to the floor most disgracefully, bringing most of the contents of the nightstand on top of him as well. But then... he realized something odd, and slid his hand slowly toward the glowing patch of dusk orange... and into it. There was... no burn? No negative consequence? He turned his hand over quickly twice, before standing, his eyes wide in surprise. He took slow steps toward the large bay window, which was carefully covered by curtains, and tightened his hands around the two halves, before grimacing, and throwing them open, exposing himself to the dying sun... and feeling nothing but a slight warmth, and still nearly a blinding radiance. He stepped away from the window, blinking from the sudden shock to his eyes, but even that pain couldn't detract from his surprise and interest, nor from the large, rather sinister grin on his face. He stepped quickly to the bedroom door. Alexis had been watching broadcasts from the BBC's World Service for most of the day, or reading what few books she'd been able to borrow from Dawn's mansion before Shadow and Virgil had all of a sudden decided to run off to Europe... Shadow was somewhere in the Czech Republic, and now she was in Slovakia, a place she could barely find on a map. She sighed, as a British talking head began going over the same news from London once again, and her stomach rumbled in annoyance. Suddenly, Virgil threw open the door to the room, and she nearly fell off of the couch from the suddenness of his entrance, but instead, she couldn't quite make herself not be shocked and slightly mesmerized by his body, as he was completely naked at the moment. Wow... no wonder Shadow likes him so much. Virgil tilted his head slightly, staring at her curiously. 'Alexis?' 'Eh... huh?' Oops... I guess my training didn't quite prepare me for everything a vampire could throw at me. 'Are you aware of what time it is?' She looked at her watch, attempting to hide how red her face was at the moment. 'About eight, why?' Virgil held his arms out triumphantly, and Alexis found herself rather struck by his very detailed musculature, before shaking her head. 'I am quite awake... and I awoke under a sunbeam... so I threw open the curtains, and I still stand before you.' She smirked amusedly, finally free of his spell, though still somewhat red in the face. 'That's incredible... but you should probably put on some pants before you do it again.' Virgil would have blinked, but enjoyed not doing so around humans, especially Alexis, who he'd grown rather fond of picking at lately, though she was no longer nearly as loathsome as he'd originally thought. Instead, he smirked, as he was almost incapable of smiling. 'Bah. Social codes mean nothing to me. I am a predator among your kind... if they all act the same way you do when I am nude, then I believe I have set a personal evolutionary precedent.' Alexis gulped, flushing once again, and coughing. 'Um... sorry.' Virgil continued to smirk. 'Nonsense, child. I consider it a compliment. Now come, I will show you Bratislava while Shadow is meeting her contact.' He turned, and her eyes gravitated downward almost immediately. What part of him isn't toned? No! Stop that! Wait... I should probably tell him... 'Uh, Virgil.' She cleared her throat, tilting her head down. Virgil looked down as well, but was not entirely certain what he was supposed to be noticing... he was still slightly sleepy, in all honesty. 'You should probably still put on some pants first. I'd, uh, be more comfortable walking with you if you did.' He raised an eyebrow. 'Ah yes. It almost slipped my mind.' He stepped calmly out of the room, to rummage through his suitcase for some suitable clothes. Alexis shook her head, and turned back to the television, which was now her only saving grace from yet more awkwardness. - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - Bratislava Castle 21.15 Virgil walked slowly through the night exhibition at the large castle in the middle of Bratislava, a collection of 'old' instruments, held in glass cases, with small placards next to them, explaining their general failure. As much as Virgil enjoyed collecting random things, he found things such as this extremely interesting, though he watched practically every one of these artifacts rise in popularity and fall into obscurity. Alexis, however, was looking at everything curiously, attempting to reason out what made many of these strange horns seem like good or even marketable ideas... however, that eluded her for the most part. And, being puzzled, and not able to read Slovak, she found herself slightly bored. She looked around the large room, and up at Virgil, who was raptly looking at something called a 'sudrephone'. 'Heh... I can't read any of this.' Virgil's expression didn't change, but he glanced over at her. 'If you would like to leave, we can... I simply wanted to see the castle once again. I honestly only get a small sense of nostalgia from seeing these things.' Alexis blinked. Had she not seen exactly what vampires were capable of first-hand, she might have thought he was lying... but had since learned that it wasn't really something that needed to be asked. Virgil walked past her, apparently making the decision for himself, and she stumbled over her own feet trying to catch up to him, as he seemed to glide down the stairs to the courtyard. Suddenly, she felt an odd need to find out more about him... perhaps why he didn't seem so intent on killing her, or even interrogating her anymore. 'Virgil...' A gust of wind scaled the wall like a Magyar spy, and wrapped around both of them, blowing Virgil's jet black hair around chaotically, as some of Alexis' dyed red locks whipped him in the face. 'Si?' She looked down at the sidewalk. 'Why did you want me to come with you and Shadow to Slovakia?' Virgil's expression didn't change, but he considered the question for a moment. 'I suppose I did not want you to escape before I could find the location of your former organization...' 'Oh.' Virgil stared over the glowing collection of lights that composed the skyline of Bratislava... a pang of uneasiness shot through him, but he wasn't quite certain as to why. 'I was not done. I did not want you to be killed, but not for that reason entirely.' She blinked. 'So... why then?' He shrugged, trying to decipher this odd feeling of dread. 'I suppose I find myself somewhat obligated to protect you from your pursuers, as we are on the same side of the conflict.' Before she could say anything, he finally realized the feeling... Lia. Something was happening... 'Get to the hotel, Alexis. I have to leave for Zdice.' She looked up at him quickly. 'Huh? What's wrong?' His pace quickened, and he began to move away from her. 'Don't concern yourself with that. Just get back to the hotel. Shadow and I will return shortly... I doubt your Den will know you are here, but protect yourself until I can do so again.' And with that, he bolted from the courtyard, at an incredible pace, that Alexis could not even begin to match, even if she weren't standing completely still, now entirely confused for the evening. She'd never seen Virgil panicked before... but something about the mania with which he moved unsettled her. What the hell was going on? And why was he... protecting her?
