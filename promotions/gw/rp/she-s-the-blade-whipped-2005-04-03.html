She's The Blade: Whipped?

3 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 3, 2005 Both William and Lisa had been insufferable on the return trip from Rome. William with his constant “I told you so,” and Lisa with her constant nagging about what William had done had caused Mike to need a stiff drink or eight. He was able to accept the fact that he lost the rumble, sure it wasn’t what he wanted but he knew that it was simply a matter of time before Global Wrestling’s highest honor was his. Once his private jet had touched ground in Los Angeles Mike did his best to ditch William and make it known to Lisa that he needed some time to himself. After he had rid himself of his extra baggage his first act was to go to the bar at the country club he belonged to. The bar was where he sat. He still wore a suit except he had long ago taken off the tie. He had two days growth of beard on his face and his blond locks were slightly disheveled. In his hand he gripped a martini glass, within the glass was a dry vodka martini’shaken, not stirred. Mike was the only patron at the bar that afternoon; the room was completely silent except for when Mike requested that the bartender serve him another drink. “Is that you Mike?” a voice cut through the silence of the country club’s bar. Mike looked up to find the source of the voice and was pleasantly surprised when he found that it belonged to his good friend James Loewen. James was a tall, well dressed black man that Mike had known since childhood. James was also the man Mike had sent Belle too after her unfortunate firing. Mike acknowledged James’s presence with a nod of the head. James chuckled at the sight before him and took a seat next to Mike. “Does the Warden know you’re here?” James asked with a humorous tone. Mike rolled his eyes, he knew that it had been awhile since he had last been at the club but he didn’t appreciate the insinuation that Lisa ran his life. “I’m sure she knows where I’m at,” Mike replied. “It’s been too long my friend,” James said as he patted Mike on the shoulder. James ordered a drink and settled in next to Mike. James took a sip from his drink, whiskey on the rocks, and then turned to Mike. “Speaking of Lisa,” James began, “is she the reason why your maid turned up at my house?” A pained smile spread across Mike’s face. “Naturally,” he replied, “you know how women are.” James laughed, “The last time a woman told you to fire Belle you sent her packing,” James said, “why didn’t you do the same thing to Lisa?” Mike offered no reply; he just took a long sip of his martini. He was afraid to answer James’s question, for he knew that the answer would bring about many changes to his world’changes that he was not yet ready to accept. James began to laugh even further at Mike’s lack of response. “So,” James began with a smirk on his ebony face, “did it hurt?” “Did what hurt?” Mike asked, he was fully aware that he was about to be the butt of some sort joke. “When she ripped your testicles off and put them in a jar,” James said with a laugh. Mike looked about the room with a pained look on his face, James’s words had upset him but there was nothing he could do about it since his words were the truth’Mike Biddle was whipped. “I’m not whipped,” Mike said through clenched teeth as he stared down at the bar. “Sure you’re not,” James said as he smirked and shook his head. “Anyway,” James continued, “in case you’re wondering Belle is doing just fine.” “I was not wondering,” Mike said. “Sure you weren’t,” James replied. James took a look at the clock on the wall and realized that he was late for his afternoon tee time. “I have to go Mike,” James excused himself; “I’ll see you around bud.” Mike was pleased to have some peace and quiet again, after all, that was the reason he had come to the bar. Sadly his peace and quiet was short lived, he was approached by a young Hispanic woman that wore a revealing red t-shirt and tight blue jeans. Mike didn’t notice her until she tapped him on the shoulder while he was in mid drink. “Hey Mr. Biddle,” she said, “I don’t know if you remember me or not?” “What do you want Miss Martinez?” Mike interrupted, he remembered Alice from the benefit dinner he had met Lisa at. That day the young gossip reporter claimed that Lisa only wanted him for his money. Alice smiled at the fact that Mike remembered her. She sat beside him just as James had done moments before. “I know that you’re still with Lisa,” she began, “have you noticed that she controls you?” Mike rolled his dark blue eyes in annoyance yet again, the last thing he needed was for someone else to tell him he was whipped. “She doesn’t control me,” Mike stated, “I don’t see anything wrong with doing things that make her happy.” “You don’t understand Mr. Biddle,” Alice said with urgency, “Lisa Jameson is like a blade’and you’re just paper. She doesn’t love you; all she wants is your money.” Mike began to silently fume, not only had this woman accused him of being whipped but now she accused Lisa of only wanting his money. Mike turned and looked at the gossip reporter with a look of death. “I would appreciate it if you’d stop bad mouthing the woman I love,” he said coldly. Internally he was surprised by his words; he had used the dreaded “L-word” and actually meant it’a first for him. “Fine,” Alice said as she rose from her seat, “I guess I’ll just have to talk to you later when you’re not drinking, perhaps you’ll actually heed my warnings then.” She left in a huff. Mike was now left to peace and quiet once again, only this time his thoughts were bombarded by the four letter word he had spoken earlier. He loved Lisa very much, and it was a revelation that had greatly surprised him. Never before had he been in love with anyone other than himself. He ordered another drink and hoped that it would help him clear his head. ***** Lisa was glad to be back in her Los Angeles penthouse; in fact she was so glad to be home that the Beverly Hills Star’s scathing report about the incident in Rome didn’t bother her one bit. The gossip rag’s habit of making her front page news had started to annoy her even more, which is why she thought it best to let Mike have some time by himself. Perhaps a small break would allow that little insect Alice Martinez to discover a new person to bother. She sat on her balcony and observed the smoggy Los Angeles skyline. It had been a month since she had started her courtship of Mike Biddle and her plan had worked perfectly every step of the way. Now that she held complete control over him it was time for the next phase of her plan to take place. She needed for Mike to propose marriage to her and the sooner the better. She leaned back in her chair and evilly smiled to herself as the sounds of wedding bells rang in her ears.
