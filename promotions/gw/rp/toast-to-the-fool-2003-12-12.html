Toast to the Fool

12 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/12/03 This day, Brick and DMAK were to record another track for the upcoming cover album. They had been making pretty good time recording the album, considering Brick’s hectic schedule. In this case, it was the Dramatics song, “Toast to the Fool”. The song was about a man consoling a woman hurting over an unfaithful boyfriend. Yet again, the group’s baritone and second tenor, Keith and Andre, would take turns on singing lead. “OK, let’s go over that again, from the top,” Brick said over the intercom as he and Aaron watched from the control booth in the recording studio.
