Finding out about yourself

26 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-26-05 Wednesday May 25, 2005 7:06 AM *RING* The phone rang from the table, making a loud sound that made me jump from the deep sleep that I was in. I opened my tight brown watery eyes, looking right at the white telephone that was on the table. Reaching over to grab it, trying to grab the blanket as well. “Hello?”, I said, confused to hear the phone ring. “Maybe that is something I should be asking you.” The other side said, making me jump on the couch just a little bit. I wasn’t expecting to hear from them. I was still hoping that it was over. Sort of like a bad dream that I just wanted to end. I rubbed my hand through my hair, grabbing the phone with my other hand as I did. “So promising you are Miss Smith. I was just sitting here with your Uncle and got this idea.” His voice was fate and calm. Like a red rose in a cold winter day. “It just seems that maybe we are going about this the wrong way. Maybe you just don’t care about your uncle like I thought.” My heart dropped like a stack of papers sitting on a desk. I could feel my soul trying to go through the phone, trying to break this man’s pride, soul, and life. I cared more then he could ever imagine. I cared so much that not caring wasn’t an option. My breath speed began to change a little as I sat there in the cold empty house. “Are you trying to say that I want my uncle dead because if you are then you are dead wrong.” I said, my voice changing from a sweet innocent voice to a rough angry voice. “No, no, no Miss Smith, I would never say that. But your time is running out and very soon it will be over and done.” The hard crisp words that he spoke ran through me like a knife cutting a fruit. Though those were the last words as I sat that, the other side going dead in my ear. I hung up the phone, still not forgetting those words that rang from his mouth. I tossed the phone to the floor, almost breaking it with the impact that it made. ********************************** “Andrew Johnson,” A voice screamed out. She walked down the hallway towards his bedroom. He lived in the last room on the second floor. He loved that room for some strange reason. Aunt Tonya, a woman that always meant business and never played around, got to his door, grabbing the handle of the door and pressing it over. “You in here?” She yelled out, looking in the room. I was just coming back from working out, sweat still on my body as I went towards my old bedroom which was now a safe room for me. I looked up with my eyes to see her slam the door shut with a loud thud. “Has your Uncle gotten back from his trip yet?” She asked as she walked towards me. Shaking my head in a no answer I responded, “No.” Even though I knew were he was I just couldn’t tell her. Only Steven knew and I hated that he found out. I watched her pass by me with an angry snort, like she needed him or something. I just shook my head and entered my room. “How are you doing darling?” A sweet voice said. It was the one woman that I didn’t much care for but still loved. It was my mother. Her heart was always pure and even though we fight more then some boxers do she was always there for my dreams growing up. I crossed my eyes and gave a small giggle towards her as I tossed the towel on to the bed. “What do you want mother.” Asking a little angrily. “I’m not here to fight baby. I’m here as a mother who is concerned.” She said as she slapped the end of the bed next to her for me to sit down. “I’m fin,” responding to her as I turned around. “I just need to be alone right now with my thoughts.” Thoughts? The only thing I was thinking about was how to earn money to save my Uncle. I didn’t know how I was going to do it but I had to think of something. I grabbed the comb off the dresser as I turned away from her. “Come on Elia. You never talk to me like my daughter anymore. It’s screaming and cursing and all sorts of stuff. And now, you won’t even talk to me or your Uncle. What’s going on?” She said, blasting off the bed and towards me like a wrecking car coming off the street. “What do you want from me mother” I don’t need nor have the time for this right now. I need to get ready for something personal and far more important.” Looking at her through the eyes that knew far more then her. I just couldn’t tell her what I was going to do. It would break her heart if she knew where I was about to go. I walked over to the closet and opened the door of it wide open. I was just trying to pitch out the voice of my mother for a little bit. Hoping that she would get the hint to leave. “Ok Elia. You want it this way then you got it.” She said, bringing total silence to me. I lowered my head, not to where she could see it but inside, feeling all the emotions I had deep inside. I head her stomp out of the room, right as I grabbed the red dress that I had hidden in the back of the closet. “Guess it’s time to bring out this?” ***************************** “Guess this is the place.” I said as I shut the truck off in front of the building. The bright lights flashed on the window shield, making my eyes close and open several times. There was so much noise. It rattled my truck a little as the music played with the sounds of some dance fever music. I opened the door and grabbed my bag from the passenger side seat. Sliding out onto the concrete driveway or parking lot so to speak. Though my eyes couldn’t help but be fixed on one thing? “Topless and more.”
