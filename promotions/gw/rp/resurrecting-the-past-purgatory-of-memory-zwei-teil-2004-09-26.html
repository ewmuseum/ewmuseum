Resurrecting The Past: Purgatory of Memory (Zwei Teil)

26 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/26/04 She's got her eyes open wide She's got the dirt and spit of the world Her mouth on the metal The lips of a scared little girl A musty stench loomed through the air. The bare white walls, which had yellowed with age, could only be seen by the single swinging lamp hanging from the ceiling. Creaking in tempo, the lamp swung back in forth in an almost melodic fashion from the single wire in which it hung from. Underneath the light source sat an old wooden chair, fraying and falling apart from years of use. The chair reeked of mildew, and when sitting in it the light from above made it feel as if you were trapped inside a giant toaster. That was the effect they were going for after all. Footsteps echoed throughout the empty room, something certainly was lurking in the shadows, impatiently at that. Without warning a giant thud broke the tranquillity. The clanking of a doorknob smashing against the wall followed shortly thereafter. 'We got her, just like you asked,' a man said in a deep voice, completely concealed in darkness. He shoved what appeared to be a woman to the cold concrete floor. The dim light blurred her features, but from what could be seen the woman appeared to middle aged. As the direct light struck her face you could see a long scar going from her hair line down to the side of her mouth. Bound and gagged, muffled screams were lost in the dusky air as she rolled around on the floor and tried hopelessly to break free. A man stepped forth from the shadows, dust particles seemed to be ignited in the light as they swirled around him. He wore a custom fitted black 3 piece suit. The jacket was closed by twin gold chains, that shined bright when the lamp hit them just right. His black bangs just barely hung over his black sunglasses, a stern look crossed his face as he looked down at the woman. 'Tina... is it?' the suited man said with satisfaction. 'Put her up in the chair Demitri. We have some questions... and she has some answers. Or at least... she better hope she has some answers for us.' I've got an angel in the lobby He's waiting to put me in line I won't ask forgiveness My faith has gone dry A shadow casts across the face of Tina, struggling desperately to break free of her shackles. As almost seven feet of pure muscle hovers over her, watching her squirm with a look as cold as ice. His hair is short and spiky, sweat dripping down from his forehead and onto his black goatee. Demitri grabs Tina by the back of her white tank top, hoisting her high into the air. Tina clinches in pain when she's forced into the chair. Muffled screams are overwhelmed by the creaking old chair. Footsteps clank on the concrete floor, followed closely by the eyes of Tina. The suited man stalks his prey, walking circles around Tina. His demeanor was cold and emotionless, Tina had no doubt in her mind that her life was over... it was just a matter of time. 'Where are my manners?' he said with a cruel sarcasm, 'My name is Christian Zenith. Your name is Tina, no need to try and talk my dear, I know far too much about you already. You've already met my good friend Demitri I see. He's quite friendly, once you get to know him... but not much of a talker.' Christian Zenith cracked a smile, much to the surprise of Tina an attractive one. Sweat poured down Tina's face, glistening like tiny shooting stars spiraling down from the heavens. The heat was really starting to get to her, squinting under the burning white light. Zenith reached into his pocket, and continued to hover over Tina. 'I'd like you to meet another friend of mine,' Zenith said with a smile as he pulled an object out of his pocket. Tina's eyes grew large with terror once she saw the fiery light reflecting off the chrome. With a click, the cylinder of the Magnum .357 opened. Christian looked over at Demitri while he loaded a single hollowpoint bullet into his Magnum. Zenith smiled like a Cheshire Cat as he put his gun back into position, resuming eye contact with his helpless victim. 'It's name is Ares... shimmering and beautiful, yet violent and deadly. Named after the Greek God of War, the Roman's called him Mars. Tonight Ares thirsts for blood Tina, but... with your cooperation, he might spare you. Take off the gag Demitri, our dear Tina needs to speak.' She's got her Christian prescriptures And death has crawled in her ear Like elevator music of songs That she shouldn't hear Demitri took a dagger out of his left pocket. Jamming it behind the gag on the back of Tina's head, he ripped back with a single fluid motion and the gag skidded across the concrete floor into the darkness. A shriek pursued the releasing of the gag. Demitri winced, standing inches away from the source of the vile noise. Christian didn't budge as he looked down at Tina, whose screams quickly became whimpers of fear. Tears streamed down her face, her throat grew so tight she could no longer scream, but that didn't keep her from trying. 'Cute, real cute.' Zenith said as he clapped, 'We're in the middle of the desert Tina. You might've startled a few lizards, and got the attention of the vultures overhead. You might as well have sent them an invitation to dinner. I've had enough small talk, let's drop the formalities and get down to business.' Tina began to hyperventilate, her breaths gasping rhythmically as if to the beat of a drum. Her skin feels like it has been set aflame under the swinging bulb of unleashed heat. The only things Tina could hear over her own breathing was her heartbeat and the click of Christian's shoes as he paced the floor in front of her. Christian halted in front of poor Tina, who was slowly beginning to dehydrate. He plunged his hand back into the pocket in the inside of his black suit jacket, and pulled out a small glossy piece of paper. Zenith thrusted the paper in front of Tina's face. Tina squinted to try and see the paper through her own sweat and the burning light as Christian spoke... 'Can you tell me who the young woman in this picture is?' Christian with a tone of bleak seriousness. Tina recognized the woman in the picture immediately, and looked up before she spoke... 'I've never seen this woman before in my life.' Tina said, trying to lie her way out of her current situation. She then heard Demitri chuckling in the darkness, and she knew they had already caught her lie. 'Don't make this hard on us Tina, I told you we already know FAR too much about you. We know who you are, where you're from, and everyone you've spoken to. We know you know this young woman, I suggest you cooperate before we make you cooperate. Or you die... whichever you'd like. We do have more leads Tina... you're expendable to us.' Tina had heard people say things like that in the movies, and she knew most of the time they were lying to get information. But there was something about Christian's demeanor. When he said that he'd kill her, she knew he'd actually do it. Unfortunately for Tina, she didn't have much of a choice. As much as she cared, she had her own life to live after all... 'If you DARE do bad things to that lady like you did the last time, I'll hunt you down and do things to you that you'll WISH you'll be dead.' Tina screamed at the top of her lungs. Christian looked over at Demitri in confusion. The way Demitri shrugged his shoulders let Christian know, that either this lady was crazy, or she knew something they didn't. Christian quickly regained his composure and looked back at Tina, snarling back at him bound tightly to the chair. 'Do things to her? I think you're either going crazy or you have no idea who this girl really is. But that isn't much of a surprise... she hasn't been herself since she ran away from home almost 10 years ago. She has many names, Jade... Kylie Serenade... most people know her as Die Erntemaschine, but I know her as Tera Zenith. She's a deadly manipulative bitch, and she's my sister.' Christian Zenith said, cold and emotionless. 'Now where is she? I’m sure you’d much rather not play Russian Roulette, and I’m not really in the mood to make it look like you killed yourself. Trust me Tina... it’s very easily arranged.' Tina was confused, she didn't know whether to believe the seemingly innocent girl who she met bleeding on the street. Or, should she believe this stranger, whom even though he's been so cold and heartless, he had a sense of honesty about him... she was so deeply entangled in her own thoughts, she couldn't possibly answer him. 'I said where is she?' Christian spoke up as he spun the cylinder of his .357 Magnum. Tina began to shake at the sight of the gun. Christian poked the gun up against Tina's temple. Her lips began to quiver. Tina began to shake her head no when Christian cocked the gun and put it up against her temple once again. 'I can't tell you!' Tina screamed hysterically as tears of fear tumbled down her cheeks. 'Wrong answer.' Christian said as he pulled the trigger. The gun fired and Tina screamed in fear, but there was no bullet. Tina began to rock back and forth and mutter to herself as Christian moved the cylinder over one spot counter-clockwise. 'Congratulations... now there is only a 1 in 5 chance that you're going to die.' Tina gulped, her throat clinched tightly as to not let the spit reach the bottom. Christian Zenith cocked the gun before placing it back up to the temple of her head. 'Now... I'll ask again. Where is she Tina?' 'Please don't kill me.' Tina pleaded between sobs, trying to look up at Christian with the Magnum planted firmly against her skull. 'Well, Tina... that's completely up to you. Do you tell me where my sister is? Or do you die honorably and valiantly defending a cold, heartless assassin? Let me tell you something... she doesn't care if you die or not Tina. She got what she needed from you I'm sure, you served a purpose to her, then she disposed of you, just like everyone else. And if she had to choose between her safety and your life... she'd kill you herself.' And it spins around 1...2...3 And we all lay down 4...5...6 Some do it fast Some do it better in smaller amounts Christian's words hit home pretty hard to Tina, she had completely forgotten about the .357 Magnum against her temple. She looked down at the ground, throat grasped tight. It took most of her strength just to breathe, as she watched her tears fall onto her legs like a leaky faucet... 'Last time I saw her,' Tina spoke softly, 'she was with Doc Bailey, in Dallas, Texas. I was horrified when I first saw her. She was naked from the waist down, panties covered in blood. She was going to die if I didn't help her. I've kept some loose contact with him, he said she'd left his care. He’s not a formal physician... he lost his license a while back. He operates out of his house for cash... he doesn’t make a whole lot but it pay the bills you see. She should still be in Dallas... I haven’t been told otherwise.' Demitri nodded while Christian pondered the information in his head, everything seemed to check out all right... 'Who did this to her?' Christian asked Tina, who sighed heavily in response. 'I don't have a clue, she wouldn't tell me. As a matter of fact she didn't remember anything. Who she was, what she was doing there. It seemed like everything was a blur.' Tina mumbled. 'It was all an act Tina, she knew everything. She picks and chooses what information she gives to who. Someone else is after her it seems, they just caught her before I did. Now Tina, I need to know where I can find this Doc Bailey.' 'His business card is in my wallet in my back pocket, the address is no longer valid but the cell phone still works. If you untie me I could give it to you.' Tina asked, hoping for the chance to be freed. The ropes were starting to rub against her wrists and ankles, and her skin was starting to burn. 'Ah, nice try Tina... but we'll take care of that.' Christian said, while looking down and seeing Tina did in fact have a wallet in her back pocket. 'And another thing... that guy who hurt Tera, should feel lucky he found her first, cause by the time I'm done with her... there might not be anything left.' 'PLEASE DON'T HURT HER!!!' Tina screamed in panic as it looked like Christian and Demitri were walking away. Christian turned around and pulled the trigger. The blast from the gun lit the entire room in a blinding light. Tina suddenly fell out of her chair and onto the floor. Zenith, without even turning back, put the gun back in his suit jacket and followed Demitri out the door. Light overcame the two of them, leading them straight to the door atop the staircase. Demitri kicked the old decaying door open. There they stood in the middle of the desert, sun beaming down on their backs. Christian looked to his left where a beautiful blonde girl looked back at him. Squinting through the glare, she smiled a bit. 'I heard a gunshot... did you kill her?' She said with curiosity. 'No Vivica, I shot 6 inched behind her head, just to scare her really. She passed out in fear, apparently. We need to find Doc Bailey in Dallas, shouldn't be too hard, seems to be some hopeless addict who let his addiction run him out of business. She offered us a card, but it wasn't worth the risk.' 'Anyone snooping around?' Demitri questioned Vivica, who looked rather uncomfortable in her black leather pants complete with black tube top and a pair of sunglasses. 'No Demitri, we're in the middle of the fucking desert. Let's go Christian, it feels like I've been thrown in the depths of hell and I really have to use the restroom.' Vivica then proceeded to walk towards the black Chrysler Crossfire, pulled over on the side of the road. Zenith looked back at Demitri as she walked away. 'Apparently, she's pissed off?' Demitri queried. Christian shook his head and walked away, following Vivica. Demitri looked back at the raggedy old shack out in the middle of the desert. Wiping the sweat off of his forehead, he then began to head for the car. Another time, another place... Christian thought to himself as he got behind the driver’s seat. She couldn’t run forever, it was only a matter of time until her past got back to her, and if he had anything to do with it... it’ll be the last time she has a chance to run away.
