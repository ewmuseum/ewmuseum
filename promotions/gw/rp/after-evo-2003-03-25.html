After Evo

25 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/25/03 Brick slowly packed his belongings into his duffel bag before he left the locker room. He felt a little bit satisfied and with good reason. One, he had just won a taxing hardcore, falls count anywhere match with Humane, one that had been booked at the last minute by the new co-owner of Global Wrestling and one of Brick’s most depraved foes, Salaok. It wasn’t an easy victory, as Humane, for all his intellectual shortcomings, was virtually impervious to pain. Adding to the drama was the fact that had Brick lost, he would have been suspended until Salaok saw fit to reinstate him, which, knowing Salaok, would have been the day hell freezes over. This is now the mark of Cain on all his matches from here on out. Now more than ever, it’s about the one thing it’s always been about: survival. There was no getting around it. Salaok hated Brick. This was just fine because Brick hated him, as well. Last summer, Brick had to suffer through Salaok using his childhood friend Aaron as a pawn in a sick game of human chess. Salaok believed that Brick brought it on himself for involving himself in the Welch/Davis feud, something that probably wouldn’t have happened had Salaok’s older brother Jonathan Davis not tried to kill Brick on two occasions. Brick involved himself in the feud out of retaliation. The second reason Brick felt satisfied with himself was because after the match, he, along with Cold Blood, Silent Wind and new GW signee Lance Daniels, managed to get his hands on the Nevermore. The first one he managed to grab was Sage Sabbot, the newly crowned cruiserweight champion. Granted, it wasn’t Salaok who he really wanted to get his hands on, but it was a start. As Brick exited his locker room, a stage hand ran up to him. “Hey Brick!” Brick stopped and turned to the stage hand. “Yeah, what?” “Just thought you should know. Salaok got back to his office and saw the damage you and Humane did to it during your match. He’s pretty pissed off about it.” Brick merely stared at the stage hand incredulously. “Dog, let me holler at you for a minute,” he said, gesturing for him to come closer. The stage hand went closer. “Now, how long have you worked here?” “Um, a couple of years.” “So, you’ve been here long enough to know my history with Salaok?” “Yes.” “So, what makes you think that I’d give a flying motherfuck about Salaok being pissed that his office got fucked up in a match?” “Well, it’s just that?” “Oh, because I’m on his “list?” Look, lemme say this in plain English: I am not afraid of Salaok or any of the other bitches in the Nevermore. Not Salaok, not Quartz, not Vyper, not Sabbot, none of “em. And, besides, Salaok ain’t got nobody to blame for his office getting fucked up but himself.” “You mean because he made it “falls count anywhere?”” “Exactly. He didn’t say “falls count anywhere except my office”. He said “falls count anywhere”. And last time I checked, offices count as “anywhere”. So, Salaok ain’t got nobody to be pissed at but himself.” “Yeah, I just thought I’d give you a heads up, just in case.” “I appreciate it.” Brick slung his duffel bag over his shoulder and walked off. “But if you’re not afraid of the Nevermore, how come you’re leaving?” Brick turned around and replied, “I’m tired, man.” He then walked off to the exit.
