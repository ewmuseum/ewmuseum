Remember the past to control the future

23 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/23/04 Silence. Different than the silence i'm use to. This is a silence of being locked away....not knowing when or if you are going to get out. I smile as I think of early america in there days of buying slaves to do their work for them...and some of those same notions are still wrapped in our society today. I look around as much as I can, being chained to a chair and all....its some sort of warehouse. I see a door in the distance it slowly opening and three figures walk through....they stay in the darkness so I can't see them. The first voice speaks in a very masculine way, like that of an old general or drill instructor. 'Leinad Soma, do you know why you are here?'I smirk at his comment. I mean when does one ever know why he or she is where they are. 'I'm sorry to say I do not. 'Because you broke the law and for that you shall be punished for them. A dark voice answers more prominant than the man before him. He has a slight romanian accent which makes me smirk once again. An actual vampire from Romania. I mean I knew they were there but its comical non the less. 'Who's law did I break?' 'You broke the law of Koumou Ade, the royal one who will not die. It is said that he was the first of our kind and he was the one who realized for our race human blood keeps, and the blood of anything else keeps us immortal. After he and his two siblings destroyed and fed of the first kingdom of humanity they split there seperate ways, but not before being punished by the 'higher powers'. They cursed Ade to only be able to spread his race through the birth, and he could only have three children in his lifetime, and there mothers would die. The other two were cursed to be able to expand there race through biting humans such as ourselves and you. The law you broke was telling the world of what you are.' Its hard not to laugh at this point so I give a grin to the last voice I hear. That of a dark but sensual voice. 'Your story is flawed...'I say as i'm getting bored with this. I guess that struck a nerve with these three for they all stepped into the light so I could see them. The first was a man of large stature, his hair shoulder length, and as grey as a London morning, he wore a black and red mandarin collared robe that came down to his ankles. The man's face had asian features,black eyes, but his skin color was more a rust color than a tan. The next was a man dressed in an all white suit. He stood with a cane in one hand, and putting the pressure of his body on it. The man had black skin, his hair was done in dreadlocks which was partly grey. His eyes were an unusual color for someone of African decent, his eyes were blue. The last was the female, she was about five-ten, wearing a black business suit, firey red hair, and green eyes. I look them over another time feeling more confidant now that I see the people I may be up against. They stand in shock of my answer. 'Your story is flawed.....but who cares...since you three know who I am I think it is fair that you tell me who you are.'The lady, looking rather annoyed now, walks over to me and smacks me hard in the face. The pain tingles for a bit then is gone. I look at her smiling once again. The other two have joined her being closer to me. 'You would like to know who we are? Well, the man in the robe is Kuang-Shi, he is of the caste Asasabonsam, the female who just slapped you, that is Rebecca La Fayette, she is of the caste Upir, and I Mr. Soma am Almeric St. Francis of the caste Ramanga. Together we make up The Syndicate, kinda like a connection between all the vampires in this world who are alive. Each is given a number and has a file. We make sure the laws of the Dark Trinity. We are the eldest of vampires and voted to be on this tribunal of sorts. Mr Reno gives his reguards but he realizes it wouldn't be his place to watch the event thats going to happen to you this night.' 'So I'm breaking the laws of vampires that are all dead? Let me enlighten you children of something....' Before I can finish my sentence I recieve a severe punch to the face. I take the blow rocking the chair, and it finally falling side ways and the chair breaking, releasing me from my chains. I stand quickly and the two of them back up a couple of feet. Almeric still standing there with a smug look. I look at them, the face that was holding the smile through this whole ordeal now one of hate, anger, and wanting retribution. I begin talking to my self calling forth out of the shadows a large hydra like creature. I pet it softly. They begin to slowly head for the door but I shake my head 'Don't try it....I'm certain my precious here is faster. As I was trying to say before....let me enlighten you children of something....Asasabonsam was my brother, Upir, my brother as well....Killed by my own hand after there treachery of making more of creatures not like ourselves...giving you humans a chance to be as close to god-like as possible. You dare tell me that I am breaking a law, and a law that I did not proclaim.!!!' Still standing eye to eye stands Almeric giving me a small clap. His confidence never moving. 'And what of my caste, the caste of the kingships, the caste I know uphold. The caste makes me over all other vampires.'His smug look turning into a grin. I stand there silent for a moment. 'Yeah thats what I thought, you are just like us...human born, and turned sometine early in your life. You are not even as powerful as myself.' 'Almeric, you are too ambitious. Almeric if you knew your history you would know about your own caste. You there Rebecca tell him his history and then you and your asian friend over there can leave' The vampire nods. She walks over to Almeric and whispers into his ear 'Caste Ramanga, the kingship caste, Ramanga the middle child of Koumou Ade. Killed by his older brother for acts of evil.' She steps away and her and Kuang-Shi begin to walk out. Before he can strike I have moved out of his reach and he is caught by the creature it tearing him limb from limb. I look at the other two. 'Remember this day, I spared your immortal lives. Next time we meet it shall not happen that way.' The two move quickly out.After a couple of minutes, I walk out the door of the warehouse and look around.I'm not that far away from Dallas so I begin my walk back into the city in silence.
