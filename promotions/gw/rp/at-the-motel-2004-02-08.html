At the motel

8 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/8/04 How do you prepare yourself for a DEADMAN? How do you prepare yourself for a DEAD-MAN? That question ran wild through my mind over and over, as I drove myself away from the Global Wrestling corporate offices. I wasn't focusing on the road in front of me, the cars passing by me, or the music blaring from the radio. The only thing my mind could focus on was 'HOW DO YOU PREPARE YOURSELF FOR A DEAD-MAN?'. For the life of me I couldn't come up with a solution. And to be honest after a while it was starting to get on my nerves. So I did something I rarely ever do. I decided to ask for help. Because Kid knows everything, well at least thinks he does, so he should at least think he knows what to do in this situation. I picked up my cell, from the cup holder in the middle console of the Explorer. RING...............RING...............RING............. Pick up, pick up, pick up. Yo.......Yo..........Yo........Flashizzles it's me the one and only Hip Hop Hero, Mr. One Eleven himself. I ain't available now, leave one at the sound of the moan...................*female moan* 'Hey, Kid, its me. Just was giving you a call, I had something I wanted to discuss. You're obviously not available, so give me a call whenever you receive this messege. And I mean WHENEVER YOU RECEIVE THIS MESSEGE. You know the number.' That son of a bitch! The one time I actually need his ass, and he's busy. Guess there's nothing I can do about it now. Is what I thought as I pulled into the parking lot of the same old shitty MOTEL I've been living in since I signed with GW back in June. Alot has happened in that tiny living space I've called home. I guess as some things change, others still remain the same. That is the saying at least... <black>' ICE! ICE! I LOVE YOU! '</black> Ha. You don't see that one too often. A random fan, bum rushing MY VEHICLE, shouting things that don't have to do with me being sick, twisted, sadistic, or how I should be locked up and burn in hell. So this act kind of amused me. However after about five minutes of sitting in my car, trying to call Flash back again, with this hysterical woman still pounding on my window, I began to grow a little annoyed with her actions. I decided, the only way to get rid of this rabid fan was to actually exit my Explorer, sign an autograph or two, talk to her, maybe take a picture, and then head up to my room. Maybe that will get her to leave. <black>' ICE! OH MY GOD! It's really you! AHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!'</black> 'The one and the only. What's.....' <black>' My name is Andrea....Andrea Wilham. I'm like your biggest fan! I drove down here all the way from Toronto, just so I could get a chance like this! Just so I could continue to follow your career, and watch you acheive your dreams. Thank you soo much for just being you.'</black> 'You're welcome.' It was all I could spit out after what I had just heard. This was a little awkward for me. I don't usually get this type of treatment from the fans, so seeing someone this worked up, in a good way, over me was a bit unusual. But I took it in stride, chatted with the young woman for a bit, took a picture, signed her T-shirt, and even gave her a slight peck on the cheek, after she offered me five bucks to do it. And then a voice interrupted our little conversation. 'Did you forget about our little date today Mr. Ice?' SHIT! I had complete forgot about the lunch and interview I had scheduled with Brandi. But at least this gave me an excuse to get away from this psycho fan, who would not let me leave. 'Forget? I'm in the parking lot waiting for you am I not?' I turned and thanked the woman for her support, and then heading into a jet black, stretch limo with Brandi Sights. I looked out the window as the limo pulled away, and saw that the woman had not moved an inch. Her face contorted with anger, as she stared the limo down. It was actually pretty neat to see something like that from a fan. 'Ice... is Italian alright?'
