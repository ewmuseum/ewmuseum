Hey Baby Part I: Question Answered

4 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/04/2006 ---- Flashback-Wednesday, August 10, 2005 0414 hours, local time “somewhere dark” ---- She awoke with a start, her mouth so dry it seemed filled with cotton. She blinked, but saw nothing familiar as her vision swam into focus. She shivered, and that was when she felt the pain, accompanied by a strange feeling of emptiness. “Ah,” said a voice to her left, “it’s nice to see you awake.” The accent was strange, almost European. She turned her head slowly, trying not to succumb to the dizziness. Black spots swam in her field of vision as she stared at the stranger. He was wearing a heavy brown cloak with a rosary around his neck. The outfit would have been bizarrely comical, if not for the seriousness that rode in the monk’s eyes when he lowered his hood. The man stepped forward, nodding his head slightly as he spoke. “Brother Marcellus. I was praying for you. The Lord works in mysterious ways.” “Meaning?” She was surprised she could speak at all, and the word sounded distorted to her ears. He seemed to understand her question as he stepped closer, holding out a hand towards her. Her eyes drifted closed against her will, and then he slipped something between her lips. She tensed, and then realized as the coolness hit her tongue that it was just a chip of ice. It was refreshing, and instantly satisfying. After a moment of silence, Marcellus spoke again, his voice soft next to her ear. “I am, ma petite, referring to the present situation. You and I.” 'I...' she groped for words, confused and disoriented, 'I don't understand.' Marcellus shook his head, bringing a finger to his lips. 'Shhh. To speak more clearly will mark us both for death. Our captor has eyes and ears everywhere. When the time is right, ma petite, we shall make our move.' The mention of Gabriel made Kitty shudder and the look of pure terror in her eyes made Marcellus make a silent vow to himself. He would get her out of this place, even if it meant his death. Her and the child... but first her. He took a fresh cloth from the steaming bowl on the table, and used it to mop the last of the sickly sweat from her brow. He smoothed back her tangled, lank hair, and looked into her eyes. She was lovely, even with the bruises on her face. He could understand why Gabriel sought to possess this woman; Marcellus almost wanted her, just looking into those emerald eyes. She'd been through so much, and still maintained an air of innocence. She returned his stare, her eyes still glassy with pain. Thankfully, the salve he'd rubbed on her wounds had nearly eliminated the pain. His cheeks burned at the memory of looking upon those ghastly wounds. Gabriel was a monster. 'Marcellus?' Her voice was soft, pleading. 'What happened? Where's my baby?' Marcellus opened his mouth to reply, but it was Gabriel who answered as he breezed into the room with a carefree smile on his face. 'I'm sorry, whore... I'm afraid he didn't make it. Looks like your precious hubby is as lackluster at procreation as he is everything else.' 'Fuck you,' she spat at Gabriel, missing the mark completely. She snarled and hissed like a cat, glaring at him. 'You're a liar. Give me my son, you sick son of a bitch!' Marcellus placed a restraining hand on her shoulder, trying to hold her down but she shrugged him off, trying like hell to stand even though she was in no condition to do so. Gabriel shook his head slowly, looking sorrowful. 'Your words wound me. I speak with veracity. Look into my eyes, and you will know the truth.' She paused, staring at his face, past the smug smirk to the sadness in his strange amber eyes. Perhaps he really was telling the truth, or maybe he was lying. She didn't know him well enough to gauge his true intentions. He wasn't like Jackson in that respect, Brad may be able to remain expressionless and cold, but his eyes always gave him away. There was that faint flicker, just a slight shifting of the eyes that always betrayed him. She never let him know that, and now she wished she had. She wanted to believe him so bad when he said everything would be ok. She wanted to live without fear so when he'd held her close and kissed the top of her head, whispering that she was safe now, she believed him. Even after the dalliance with the pirate Serpenti, he’d forgiven her. Love conquers all. 'I,' she faltered, her voice breaking with emotion. 'Don't. Believe. You.' Every word was like a hard slap in the face, coming out from between gritted teeth. Tendons stood out on her neck as she tried to swing her legs off the table, bracing on her arms. Gabriel shrugged. 'Fine. Don't believe me. Would you like to see him?' She wanted to say no, the words formed in her mind, but instead she found herself saying the exact opposite. 'Yes, yes I do, you lying sack of shit.' Marcellus winced at her vulgarity, but forgave her at the same time. He expected he might have been in a similar frame of mind in this situation. Even the Lord's infinite patience had a breaking point. Gabriel left the room, whistling a merry tune, and Marcellus took the moment to turn to Kitty, whispering in her ear. 'My child, you do not want to see this, truly... you know not what you wish for.' 'I have to know, don't you understand?' Tears shone in her eyes. Marcellus nodded. 'Yes. I understand. But you needn't worry, where there once was two, there is now one. And the-' he broke off abruptly as he heard Gabriel's footsteps drawing nearer. He straightened up, and resumed his former pose at the head of the makeshift hospital bed. Gabriel appeared in the doorway, something small cradled to his chest. He was humming softly as he approached the bed, a slightly off kilter smile on his lips. His eyes were glassy, otherworldly strange as he thrust the bundle at Kitty, almost pushing the lifeless infant into her face. The body was already stiff, and starting to smell. Kitty gagged, going pale as she looked at the tiny body. Gabriel smirked, 'Well? You wanted to see... look! LOOK! Do you believe me now?' Tears rolled from her eyes, squeezing out from beneath the closed lids. She trembled, trying to hold in the sobs welling within her chest and almost succeeding. 'Please,' just the one word fell from her numb lips, with nothing more to follow. She wrapped her arms around her middle, not noticing the scabs and blood that bonded her torn shirt to her body. Marcellus stepped forward, his face dusky with anger. 'Gabriel!' He chastised, placing a hand on his arm, 'please, take that thing away. This is madness. She does not need this, not in her present condition.' Gabriel nodded, suddenly forgetting who was in charge. He looked down at the limp body in his arms, his voice distant when he replied. 'Yes, you are right, Brother. I should dispose of this trash...' ---- Flashback-Thursday, August 11, 2005 0333 hours, local time Jackson's condo, Chicago, Illinois ---- He sat in the same chair, soaked in his own miserable sweat, watching the television with glassy eyes. His thoughts played aloud, drowning out the idiot box. His fingers trailed over the scar on his chest, over the bullet hole that had nearly ended his life, wishing that it had. Some show was on, mindless crap reruns, Unsolved Mysteries or something, blaring at top volume, but it didn’t matter. Jackson was beyond caring. The blue light from the television shed little illumination on him where he sat in the corner, obscured by shadow, but the shadows and feeble light made him appear more haggard that usual. Dark circles cut slashes beneath his eyes and his cheeks were almost gaunt beneath the heavy black stubble. “I love you, Kitty,” he whispered, pulling out the blue steel Colt from the cushions of the chair. He sat in the dark, cradling the pistol in his hand, absently spinning the cylinder. This wasn’t new. The depression was the same. The numbness was the same. The pain, ever a constant. The anger, however, was new. Utterly self-directed. He wanted to kick the shit out of himself, perhaps fling himself off a building just to feel the pain, it was something. He sat there, staring down at the cold steel, thinking about all that had come and gone. What did he have? In this screwed up world, what did he have to keep him sane? Friends? Family? No. Nothing. The CID was dead, all save Gabriel, who had vanished like a thief in the night, without a trace. He grunted to himself, almost a chuckle, and shook his head. He’d been a fool, thinking Gabriel was nothing. He didn’t matter. Nothing but a lackey. Clever misdirection, nothing more than smoke and mirrors. He turned the gun over and over, watching the light play off the metal. This was the gun. The one that had ended his life. Perhaps he should have used it on her, and then himself, ending this pain. He’d known that Gabriel would strike back at him; he’d just never guessed how bad it would be. How much worse could it get? She was gone, the authorities had written her off as dead, and still had him in mind as a number one suspect. The bottle on the table was half empty, or perhaps half full. He wouldn't have chosen either of those options, instead he would have said it was not big enough. He'd been drinking since midnight, since the time it had kicked over to the next day. Something about today made him feel like drinking, at first he’d felt elated, almost like celebrating. This year was supposed to have been different. He wasn't supposed to be alone, drinking from the cup of bitterness. He should have been holding the woman of his dreams in his arms, laughing with his best friend, sharing drinks. Instead he was here alone, in this cavernous apartment. Silence poured out around him like a giant cauldron of emptiness had been upended over him. He ached inside, a raw wound, and his eyes burned constantly from lack of sleep and tears that he hadn't known he could still shed. He grabbed the bottle with his hand, tilting it to his lips. The bitter liquor spilled out of his mouth, dribbling down his chin unnoticed, staining his already filthy undershirt. He raised it once it was empty, swiping a hand across the back of his mouth, toasting the lamp on the table. “Here's to you, man. May all your dreams come true.” He let the bottle fall from his hands, feeling that telltale prickle behind his eyes. If he wasn't careful, the Tequila would do nothing more than make him weepy. “Here's to another wonderful fucking year. And here's to you Gabriel, wherever the fuck you are... may you die a horrible death, and rot in hell.” The last words were barely a whisper, laced with venom nonetheless. It was at this moment that the phone rang, vibrating against his thigh where it was clipped to his pants pocket. He cut off the sounds of “Fear of the Dark” by Iron Maiden and put the phone to his ear without checking the caller id. “Jackson.” “Excellent.” The voice on the other end was familiar, eerily so. The tone was smooth, almost silky, the diction flawless. The caller continued in that same tone after a slight pause. “I was hoping I would find you at home-? Jackson snarled into the phone, his expression bordering on manic, his anger was that intense. “Listen up fucktard, I don't find this amusing. Stop this shit and find someone else to play your sick little headgames with.” “Touch”, Mr. Jackson. That hurts. And here I was trying to congratulate you on a well deserved win. Good job,” The man almost sounded wounded, beneath all that pomposity. Jackson picked up the bottle, suddenly feeling sober. He unscrewed the lid, tilting the bottle, tasting the last bitter drop on his tongue. “Fuck you, buddy. You don't know me. Why the hell are you calling me” Are you some sort of lunatic who gets off on rubbing salt in people’s wounds?” “Perhaps I also have trouble sleeping?” The chuckle was filled with low menace. “You never answered my question.” “You hung up on me, asshole.” Jackson stood on shaky legs, still clutching the neck of the bottle. The silence stretched out while he stood there, blinking blearily, doing nothing but breathing into the phone. After a long span, almost five minutes, he realized he still had the phone pressed to his ear. “Hello... are you still there?” “Yes,” came the patient reply, “I'm still waiting.” “For what?” Jackson shuffled into the kitchen, rubbing the back of his neck. There should be more booze in here somewhere. “An answer, Mr. Jackson.” “To what” Your stupid question?” He could feel the anger rising, and this time he didn't even try to push it back. Old habits died hard, and even old dogs have trouble remembering their tricks. “You want to know if I love my wife” What kind of retard would ask that? Of course I love her you goddamn moron! Would I be looking for her if I didn't? I love her more than anything in this world... I love her more than life itself... without her life isn't worth living! You ask me if I love her? YES! Yes I do... you stupid bastard. FUCK YOU!!” “Thank you, Mr. Jackson. I'll be in touch...” The silence grew, and then was replaced with a hollow clunk. “TALK TO ME! What did you do to her” WHERE IS SHE?! Serpenti, is this you? I swear to God if you’re behind this shit, I’m going to hunt you down and break your fucking neck, you asshole? she’s mine, you hear me?! WHERE THE FUCK IS MY WIFE?!” Nothing but silence answered his desperate screams, a silence that was only broken by the honking of a car horn and the screech of tires. He threw his cell phone as hard as he could, not even caring when it smashed to bits against the wall. The Tequila bottle followed, and glass rained down around him, cutting his hands and knees to shreds as he sagged to the floor, sobbing brokenly with a voice that was barely audible from the force of his angry screams...
