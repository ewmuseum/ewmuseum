I've really gotta learn to lock that door...

7 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/7 Here I stand... In front of hundreds of thousands of screaming fans. Face to face... Toe to toe with my most dangerous of foes... Brick. The same Brick who’s shadow was cast over me when I first arrived in Global Wrestling. The same Brick who’s “wing” I was to be taken under. The same Brick who I was to be the “next”. How quickly that changed... I vehemently denied any supposed similarities brought up between he and I in interviews, and even went so far as to publicly insult him. From that point on I dedicated every match I had to showing that I had no interest in being the next Brick. Why be the same when you can be better? To prove this point I never backed down, and even went so far as to seek him out in the ring as well as backstage. There probably isn’t an inch of the Global Arena we haven’t fought through. In the ring... Backstage... Christina’s office... Everywhere. It didn’t matter. As long as I was getting my hands on him. So here we are again, in a rematch to what the first of our matches with the world title on the line. A Hard10 match. The only match I’ve ever had that ended in a tie. The two of us circle each other in the ring, Brick stepping and counter stepping as I gait back and forth showing off some boxing footwork while taunting him into charging at me. Soon enough we’re both within striking range and Brick’s face crumples into a scowl of rage as he throws the first punch. A huge right cross. I move laterally at the last moment, narrowly avoiding the blow which surely would have dislocated my jaw... And maybe even force a few teeth to find a new home on the floor. I can feel the wind from his tree trunk like arm as it rushes past, just inches away from my face. I slip in a quick left jab before he can fully recover. It causes his head to quickly snap back like a pez dispenser before slamming back down looking just as pissed off as ever. No effect. Brick fires back with a lunging left, which I quickly duck and slide behind. He turns around only to be greeted with a super kick. He manages to sidestep and amongst all the screaming from the crowd I can heard Bishop saying “No Contest misses!”. As I plant my foot back on the canvas I feel both of them being yanked right out from under me as Brick levels me with a big time clothesline that turns me inside out. I come slamming down on the mat at a weird sideways angle that causes my neck and shoulder to take the brunt of the impact. Before I can even begin to recover I feel by dreadlocks being pulled away from my head as if someone were trying to rip them out. Brick lifts me to my feet with a handful of my hair and continues to grasp it in one of his gigantic hands as he uses the other to knock the wind out of me with a gut punch. I attempt to go down to one knee clutching my mid-section when I feel myself being whipped toward the ropes. Regaining my balance I put the brakes on my trip and begin rushing back toward Brick who is charging right at me. He begins cocking his arm back to deliver yet another devastating clothesline. One that finds no mark... I quickly drop down, and sweeping my feet out in front of myself, nail him right in the knee cap with a basement dropkick. Wasting no time I spring to my feet and continue running, this time toward the ropes Brick bounced off of... Once I reach them I plant my left foot firmly on the second rope and use the bounce gained from it to place my right on the top rope. I then bring my left up to the top as well before performing a moonsault. Flying blind, I flip backwards, unable to see my opponent until I’m completely upside down. There he is, right where I left him... In the center of the ring Brick is just now beginning to climb back to his feet and has yet to turn around. As I descend I can feel myself getting closer to him, reacting quickly, I latch my right arm tightly around his neck and pull him down with me in a gigantic moonsault DDT. The impact shakes the ring and causes the entire audience to spring from their seats. The force of the both of our bodies colliding with the mat continues to reverberate through the ring, out onto the floor, and through the audience. Soon, the whole arena is shaking... Suddenly, the crowd’s shouts of “Deacon! Deacon!” turns into a single voice. That of John Zero... The screaming crowd and Zero’s voice toggle on and off as if they were taking turns. As my eyes begin to slit open so do the images of the Global Arena and John standing over me attempting to shake me into consciousness. Finally, John wins out... “Deac, wake up man!” “Deac! Get up man!” I hear the calls, yet pay them no mind, figuring he’ll soon grow bored and wander off. Silence. Ah, I knew it’d work. “Oh my god, we’re outta crown!” This immediately wakes me from my slumber, causing both my eyes to simultaneously fling themselves open without any doing of my own. I find myself laying upside down on my couch with John standing over me, a somewhat concerned look in his eye. After a bit of small talk he gets right down to business. “You seen Vic, D?” he asks. I think about what he just stormed into my room and woke me up from my dream for a moment before offering up a reply. “A booty call” You woke me up, while its still daylight out, cause you’re looking for a piece of ass? Fuck you, man, I’m going back to sleep!” I exclaim while rolling to my feet off of the couch. As I begin to stumble off to find something to drink I feel his hand on my shoulder pulling me back. “D, that’s not it. We went to go get the cars, and Vic wasn’t with us cause she went shopping. She hasn’t come back.” Still not seeing the point, I inform him that it was only a few days ago. His facial expression growing a little more worried he continues... “I know. Vic’s a tough chick, hard as nails. But?” Oh my god I’ve got to stop him or he’ll never let me get back to sleep... “Yeah yeah, I know I know.” I say with a nod, interrupting what was sure to turn into some sort of long winded heart felt expression about how much he cares about her. “Lemme get woke up, and we’ll find her.” Shit, I haven’t seen my girlfriend in three days either... But am I flipping out? Hell no. I never thought this would happen, but I’m beginning to think old Johnny boy might be whipped... As I stumble my way into a pair of flip flops and stretch a question of real importance pops into my head, one actually worth waking up for... “I wonder if we got any food.”
