?| Black Inevitability |?

5 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6.5.04 ---- ~? As far as I can see there is no light to follow No moon to let off a silvery glow ?~ ~? No sun to announce the beginning of tomorrow Life vanishes and death is what replaces ?~ :| Akuma took another glare at Itagaki who was now caressing the dead ladies drained of life hair. Still emotionless, he looked up into the sky wondering how long did she fall. Shaking his head, Akuma dipped the tip of his feather into an ink bottle and continued. |: Is Itagaki becoming a saint? Or has he always been one, drowning in the fiery lakes of hell? Caressing a dead woman's hair and kissing her... what does that mean? I will not loose control of my own creation. Is it that Itagaki finds death aesthetic? He indulges other's pain. It makes him feel human. The sweet taste of blood dripping from his fangs and sliding down his throat fulfilling his inner being and putting a face piece by piece on his blank image. ~? Vibrancy and joy are stolen And all that's left are empty spaces ?~ ~? His voice gives fear His eyes blood red ?~ God. That word is a cacophony. Imprisoning his so called 'children' with rules and boundaries instead of letting them explore grounds light can't reach. I live in a society where freeing people is wrong. Why? Itagaki lives a life other's can only dream. Pendragon thinks I'm influencing evil. I influence life. Why can't they see this? I still remember times when I was searching for shelter, entering immortal tombs. The darkness surrounding me almost in contentment. My twisted thoughts not letting me slumber. Clutching that damned silver cross not for belief but just thinking of better times with crimson tears running cold down my face. Boiling with hatred for the wretched bastard who took my humanity. Twirling thoughts dizzying my mind as i drift off to sleep. Maybe that's what's going on in Itagaki's head. I can only drown his old memories. Therfore his mind is trying to create memories that can float. He is trying to become someone. Of course I won't allow such foolishness to occur because if we were all someone, who's left to be the devil's spawn? Who's left to be no one? I am no one, no one's perfect. Itagaki will spread his black wings over society and intoxicate them with death. And I will be there when they begs for mercy. I will be there when Pendragon begs for my forgiveness. ~? He knows not love But lives in hate instead ?~ Itagaki is the bring of eternal damnation. For Lucifer himself told me. ~? What is he to others? He is their demise ?~
