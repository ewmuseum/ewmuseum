Dire Straits

30 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07.30.05 'I don't care,' screamed Ken at the top of his lungs. 'I can't take this anymore. It's getting ridiculous and I'm doing what I have to do to put an end to it.' The Global Icon was frantically packing a bag in the bedroom of his palacial SoHo estate. Gunner and Josh, who were both attempting to reason with him, both had their bags packed and were ready to go. However, due to Ken's beyond hectic schedule as of late, this was the first he'd seen of his home in two weeks. 'Look, Amigo,' began the American Youth, 'you've gotta remember what she put us through in the past... AND who she's with now.' 'Yeah,' chimed Gunner. 'Don't you think--' 'No,' interrupted KPJ. 'No, I don't. This isn't like last time. Some things are different... EVERYthing's different. She may have been corrupt in the past, but Josh and I were, as well. Don't forget that.' 'But,' began Addison. 'NO,' belted out the Superstar angrily. 'I'm going through with this. That's the bottom line. It's happening and it's happening at Evolution.' Ken closed the discussion and his bag at the same time, zipping the conversation shut. He stormed out of the room with his bag and downstairs, out the front door to his new Jaguar. Gunner and Josh just stood in the room, looking at their feet, before picking up their bags and following Ken to the car. 'You know,' said Josh mellowly to Gunner, 'he probably knows what he's doing. If he doesn't, we both know he's too stubborn to say so.' 'Yeah,' replied Gunner, 'but I don't want this to come back and bite him in the ass.' The two made their way to the car, where Ken was already in the driver seat with the car on and the CD player playing. As they entered the car, KPJ dropped it into reverse and backed out of the driveway and into the street. 'We're meeting Ace and Bill at the airport,' he began. 'Their flight's arriving twenty minutes before we board for Vegas.' And with those words, Ken pushed the shift into first, and began the course, letting only the beats of Paul Oakenfold to do any talking. ------------------------------------------------------------ 'He's planning on doing WHAT,' asked a concerned Ace Harding as Josh and Gunner let The Enforcers in on what Ken was planning. 'That's insane.' 'Yeah,' chimed Bill. 'Has he forgotten about all the hell that was gone through last time?' 'All we know,' began Gunner, 'is that he's tired of the way things are going in Global, and he's gone to desperate measures to take care of the problems.' 'I'd have to say I agree,' replied Josh. 'Not about his decision, but about the way things are being ran in GW right now. I mean, you can only get screwed so many times before you HAVE to do something about it.' 'I understand that, Josh,' resounded Harding, 'this is a dire strait that he doesn't need to turn to.' Before Josh could retort, Ken had made his way back from the ticket counter and was grasping Bill's hand in a shake before doing the same with Ace. 'How was the All Japan tour, guys,' he asked enthusiastically. 'I'll bank that it was insane.' Bill shot a look to Gunner, who returned it to him and Ace with an attitude of 'he'll tell you when he's ready.' The prompted Moresi to resume asthough he'd heard nothing at all. 'It was good, Brother,' the Bulldozer responded. 'The fans over there are absolutley insane.' 'We'll,' continued The Franchise, 'it's good to have you guys back state-side. I want to hear all about Tokyo, but first, we have some business to discuss. I spoke with one of the Security Guards, and he's arranged for us to go ahead and board the plane. We'll talk about in First Class.' The Elite, whole again, responded enthusiastically to relaxation with wine and lobster in First Class as the began to board the plane. Still, the though ran through all of their minds... could Ken really be doing the right thing.
