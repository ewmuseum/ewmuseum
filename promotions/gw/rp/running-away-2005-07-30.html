Running Away

30 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07-30-2005 “One foot’in front of the other?” Dawn was hard pressed to recognize the sound of her own voice, a dry croak that squeezed its way out of the back of her throat, rasping painfully against lips chapped and split from the heat of the Nevada summer. Sweat rolled in rivulets down the curves and contours of her muscular body, cutting small channels through the dirt and grime that had built up against her normally pale skin. Having always been of pale complexion, Dawn was more vulnerable then normal to the ravages of the summer sun, and nearly two days of walking along the Interstate 515 had left her porcelain skin red and raw, beginning to crack and split from the heat. Dawn had fled the Global arena in Nevada with no preparation, and the torn and dirty spandex and cloth of her wrestling gear offered little protection from the suns UV rays. But despite the pain in her feet and legs, despite the searing sensation across her face and shoulders, she forced herself to keep moving, doggedly placing one foot in front of the other. She had to escape, that was the one thing on her mind’she had to escape before’before? Before she had to face Lia. Dawn’s steps faltered and she went to one knee, her right hand burying itself into the highway gravel to catch herself from a complete fall. Bitter tears sprung to her eyes, leaking down the burned skin of her cheeks and spattering onto her dirt covered boots. Her shoulders heaved for a moment as sobs threatened to break the fragile grip she still had on her mind, shaking up from the pit of her stomach and escaping as a strangled gasp. She squeezed her eyes shut against the thought, as though she could close her mind against the thoughts that had plagued her for months? She lay on her back in a sea of dark silk, green eyes open wide at the Shadow that flitted in through her open door, gliding toward the foot of her bed as though floating along the ground. Bright eyes, the colour of the sea after a storm, gazed out at her from beneath a waterfall of raven black hair, promising things that Dawn’s mind could scarcely stop to comprehend. A pale hand reached out to gently brush her thigh, cool fingertips stroking along skin that felt to Dawn as though it was suddenly on fire. She gasped at the touch, the feeling of want springing up from the pit of her stomach hit her like a physical force. She sat up suddenly and reached for the Shadow, her hands finding pale shoulders as cool as the touch on her thigh. With sudden force she was pushed back onto the bed, the pale Shadow crawling up the length of her body until lips the colour of roses in full bloom hung mere inches from her own. “I?” Dawn stammered, her heart hammering in her chest. “I lo?” A pale finger pressed to her lips, bidding her to be silent, but Dawn shook her head slowly, gently pushing the finger aside. “I love you?” she whispered, longing to hear those same words repeated back to her. Instead the figure recoiled, pushing away from her and stepping off the edge of the bed to back away. Dawn sat up quickly, reaching out one hand to try and catch her Shadowy visitor. “No wait! Please!” Another shadow joined the first, with eyes as red as the horizon at dawn and with a smile that spoke of a predatory sharpness. “I told you that you would drive her to me?” came the throaty rumble, a sensual purr as he embraced the other Shadow. “DAMMIT!” Dawn punched the ground hard, pain spiking up through her knuckles where the bare skin scraped against gravel and sand. Her chest and shoulders heaved for several moments as she breathed deeply, trying to focus on the anger to block out the sudden pain in her hand. She had seen the encounter with Leinad Soma, and had been unable to tear her guilty eyes from the monitor as it had all been laid out before her’she was a burden. An unpleasant task forced onto a friend who would rather have been elsewhere’with other people. Dawn sat back on her heels, “AHHHHHHHHHHHHH!” she screamed aloud, tears leaking down her burned face. “Why me”! What the fuck did I ever do that was so wrong to deserve this?” Her answer came in the ear splitting form of air brakes, a sudden blast of sound as an eighteen wheeler raced past her and pulled onto the side of the road up ahead, hissing and squealing as it ground to a stop. The passenger side door opened and a middle-aged man in a fading ball cap stuck his head out, looking back towards her. “Uh hey?” he called out. “Are you okay miss?” Dawn swiped clumsily at her face, trying to regain her footing on unsteady legs. “yeah’yeah I’m fine.” “You’re looking pretty burnt, did yer ride break down?” “No I’m’I’m just walking?” He scratched his head for a moment, looking around at the arid landscape as if wondering whether she might be a little mad. “Oh’well uh’did you want a ride anywhere” I can’t go out of my way but I can drop you off or something.” With the alternative being a walk of unknown length through nearly desert territory? The decision wasn’t a hard one to make. Not trusting her dry throat she nodded, staggering forward until she could drag herself into the cab of the truck. The man beside her took one appraising look and handed her a still sealed bottle of water from behind his seat. Dawn fumbled in frustration with the cap for several minutes before finally managing to break the seal and poor the cold liquid down her throat. It burned and stung at her dry mouth, eliciting a coughing fit that drew another sidelong look from her sudden benefactor. “Are you alright” Do you need to see a doctor or anything?” Dawn shook her head violently, forcing a reply out between coughs. “No I’m fine’just’caught unprepared that’s all.” He nodded as he pulled the truck back onto the highway, guiding it with an ease that bespoke years of experience. “You certainly look it, I’ve never seen anyone walking along the side of the road in an outfit like that before.” Dawn just nodded, shifting uncomfortably in her seat to stare out the window. “Yeah I bet?” “My name’s Adrian.” “Dawn.” Dawn’s terse reply didn’t leave much room for conversation, but it appeared that Adrian wasn’t going to be so easily put off. “So what are you doing out here anyway” Trying to make it to Ely on foot? That’s an awful long way.” Dawn didn’t really want to talk, especially not to a random stranger’but the words came pouring out anyway. “Running away?” Adrian looked over his shoulder at her, his hands still expertly guiding the truck by feel alone. “Running away” You look a little old to be running away from your parents or something’you in some kind of trouble?” Dawn ran an idle finger down the inside of the window, trying to watch the print it left behind. “Something like that’I had a secret exposed that I didn’t want anyone to know’and now I don’t have anything left?” “Nothing left eh” So where are you running to if you don’t have anything left?” Dawn tucked her knees up to her chest, hugging them there with her free hand while she drew designs on the window with the other. “My sister. Maybe I can stay with her for a while?” Adrian nodded, turning back to the road. “Family can sometimes help you straighten stuff out for sure. So what kinda secret is so terrible that it makes you feel that you don’t have anything left but your sister.” “I’m in love?” Dawn whispered, almost too quietly to be heard. Adrian smiled and opened his mouth to reply, but Dawn cut him off before he could utter so much as a syllable. ??with another woman.” There was a long moment of silence between them as Adrian tried to digest what he’d heard, staring moodily out at the road. Dawn balled up further, trying to push herself into the corner of the cab as far as she could. She could feel the familiar tears of shame and confusion tugging at the corners of her eyes, but she stubbornly wiped at them before they could even get started. “Well?” Adrian started, sounding a little uncertain. “Maybe I’m just being stereotypically male here’but I’m not sure I really see the problem there. Have ya asked her if she’s interested in you?” “She’s with someone else’probably for eternity.” He chuckled, reaching out to pat her knee with a fatherly hand, ignoring the way she shied from him. “That’s an awful long time Dawn, I doubt they’ll be together for eternity.” Dawn turned her head back towards the window, refusing to let a stranger see the bitter tears trickle down her face. “Could very well be for eternity?” She whispered, ??just leaving me’isolated.” “So where is this sister you’re running to?” Dawn started from her reverie, stifling a twinge of annoyance that he’d interrupted a perfectly good bout of self pity. “Seattle.” “You were gonna walk clean across three states” Jesus woman, I don’t think it was that bad.” Turning her head to look, Dawn fixed him with angry eyes. “You have no idea.” Adrian shrugged, not taking his eyes from the road again. “Fair enough. I can take you as far as Salt Lake City, but you’ll need to find your own way from there.” ********************************************************************************************* Dawn awoke from a nightmare of fangs and shadows when the door she was leaning against opened suddenly, pitching her into a stocky frame with a startled yelp. “Whoa there lady, didn’t mean to startle you.” Dawn scrambled back up into the cab, staring with wide eyes at the man who had spoken, her fight or flight instinct warring for attention with the sound of her own heartbeat hammering in her ears. A large African American man smiled up at her’but not by much. He was easily six feet and half, with a build that would have made most wrestlers Dawn knew terribly jealous. He spread his massive arms wide, “I’m Clark. My good buddy Adrian said that you were looking for a lift into Seattle’and I just happen to be running that way tonight. Did you want to catch a lift?” Dawn stared at him in uncertainty for a long moment, chewing her lower lip in thought. Now that she wasn’t at the mercy of the elements, she found herself reluctant to climb into the cab of another stranger for a long ride. As if sensing her predicament Clark stepped back, gesturing to the truck behind him. “That’s my rig back there. You don’t have to come if you don’t want to, but by the look of things you’re not in much condition to walk on your own. Those boots aren’t going to hold up to much more rough treatment.” A quick glance proved him to be very correct, Dawn’s battered outfit of spandex and wrestling boots would offer her no protection against whatever perils the rest of the journey offered, and she would get to her sisters much sooner then if she walked. With an uncertain nod Dawn slid out of Adrian’s cab, wrapping her arms tightly around her torso. “Alright’thank you’thank you very much.” Clark sketched an elaborate bow, the grin remaining firmly in place. “No problem at all, I’m always happy to do a favour for a friend’and for a lovely lady at the same time. Now Adrian said your name was Dawn?” “That’s right?” “Alright Dawn, why don’t you climb on in and we’ll see about getting you to Seattle courtesy of the Cueball Express!” Clark rubbed his shaved head with a grin, gesturing towards the cab. ******************************************************************************** Behind the “Flying J” truckstop Adrian punched a familiar phone number into the payphone. He waited for a long moment before the line picked up, a warm baritone speaking from the other end. “Any news?” “Yes sir Mr. Ambrosi, I found the girl. I just handed her off to Clark and he’s taking her to see family in Seattle.” “Excellent. You did well Adrian, I’m glad I can still count on you. Payment will be arranged in the usual manner.” “Thank you Mr. Ambrosi, it’s a pleasure to work for you as always.” ********************************************************************************* It was late at night at Hunting Grounds Northern complex, and Christian Zenith was roused from a light dose when the phone beside him sprung to life. He rolled over languidly, snatching the phone from the cradle and pressing it to his ear. “You’d better have good news.” “I have very good news Mr. Zenith, regarding your missing associate.” Zenith sat up fully, not recognizing the warm baritone at the other end of the phone. “Who is this?” “That’s not as important as what I have to tell you Mr. Zenith, for the moment just call me a concerned third party.” Christian adjusted his sunglasses over his cadet blue eyes, his voice hardening. “What do you want?” “Just to tell you that Dawn McFarlane will be in Seattle within eight hours. She will be at the residence of one Katelyn McFarlene-Blok.” “Why are you-? The phone went dead and Christian made an annoyed noise in the back of his throat. He leaned across the couch to nudge Vivicia Valentine awake, drawing a sleepy noise from her. “Get your things together, we’re going to Seattle.” ********************************************************************************* The skies over Seattle were cloudy when Dawn finally stepped onto the lawn of her sisters house, looking with apprehension at the white picket fence and lawn gnomes scattered across a garden. The last time she had seen Katelyn was during her tenure with WMW, and like everything that she had gone through in that company it had not ended well. She swallowed deeply, raising her right hand to the knocker and rapping three times. After a moment a smaller version of Dawn herself opened the door, the family’s familiar strawberry blonde hair tied back in a small ponytail. Her jaw dropped at the sight of the woman on her doorstep, the stainless steel bowl she’d been cleaning following it to the floor. “Dawn”! Oh my god what happened to you?” Dawn flushed, staring at her ruined boots in shame. “Hey little sister?” “Who’s at the door babe?” An unfamiliar voice called from the kitchen, snapping Dawn’s head up with a suspicious glare. “Who’s that?” Katelyn sighed, folding her arms under her breasts and leaning against the doorframe in a manner very reminiscent of Dawn herself. “That’s my husband Dawn’we were married two months ago.” Dawn blinked twice, rocking back on her heels as though she’d been physically struck. “You’re’husband?” “It’s Dawn sweety.” She called back, before turning to face Dawn again. “Yeah my husband. You know the one I married even after you didn’t respond to the invitation.” Dawn floundered, trying to remember any time she’d seen an invitation cross her eyes. “I didn’t’I was’I’ve been?” “Busy.” Katelyn finished for her, glaring up at her big sister. “Just like Dad was always busy with wrestling, now it’s your turn.” It was Dawn’s turn to glare, growling from the back of her throat. “That’s not far K. He did his best?” “Sure for you maybe?” Katelyn sighed, holding her hands up. “Look I’m not having this conversation with you again Dawn, especially not after seeing the way you react to things you don’t like to hear.” “What’s that supposed to mean?” “You nearly killed that Emoticon guy Dawn, the police came here looking for you yesterday.” Thunder pealed and rain began to fall, small patterings at first, but slowly growing in size and volume. “The police?” “Yeah. You disappeared Dawn, you can’t just up and disappear like that. They should be here in a minute?” Panic seized around Dawn’s heart and she couldn’t breathe, for a long moment it felt as though the only sound was her own stilled heartbeat. “You’you called the police on me?” “You need help Dawn’you’re out of control?” Dawn gritted her teeth, the beginnings of sharpness showing at her incisors. “Help” I came HERE for help and you called the fucking cops!!” Katelyn didn’t back down, stepping right up to her older sibling. “You’re out of control! You’re acting like some kind of animal, just lashing out at everything!” Dawn growled, showing her sister back through the doorway. “Then you better get out of my face before I lash out at YOU!” Katelyn’s pretty face darkened into a scowl and she slammed the door shut in Dawn’s face, the sound of the bolt being thrown home slamming into Dawn’s ears. She staggered back from the door, out into the pouring rain, a look of disbelief on her face. “No’you’you were supposed to understand’supposed to care?” She clutched her hands to the sides of her head as rain soaked through what remained of her clothes. “You want a fucking animal”!” She howled, throwing her head back in anger. “I’ll show you and animal’I’ll show EVERYONE!” With a savage kick she snapped one of the lawn gnomes into the neighbors yard, slipping in the mud as she did so and collapsing to the wet lawn. She struggled to rise, slipping in the wet grass and mud until she lay in a panting heap, rage and anger leaking from her eyes in hot tears. “Dammit’you were supposed to bring me home?” The sound of rain pattering off plastic caught her attention, and she turned her head to see a pair of immaculate black high heeled boots standing right next to her. She looked up at Vivicia Valentine and the umbrella she was holding, one hand extended down. “Christian and I are here to help you Dawn?” The petite blonde said simply, offering her hand. ??we’ll take you back to Hunting Grounds’to your home.” Dawn looked up at her from the mud, and reached out, slowly placing a shaking hand in hers. ********************************************************************************************** Just under two days later, Christian Zenith’s private limousine pulled through the gates of the McFarlane estate, gently rolling to a stop by the front door. The sun had just gone down, and the ground floor windows were alight with a soft glow. Dawn stepped gingerly from the limousine, her burned skin aching and stinging with each movement. She paused briefly at the front door, reaching up with a tentative hand to knock. The quiet knock woke up one rather groggy vampire, who had insisted on sleeping on the couch in the hallway, stubbornly. She gasped out of sleep, struggling to her feet at the knock on the door, stumbling towards the front door on instinct rather than any conscious awareness. Virgil wouldn't be awake yet, she judged, looking at the light outside. She fumbled with the locks irritatedly for a few seconds before flinging the door open. Her jaw almost dropped in shock to see a very dust-covered and sunburnt Dawn standing at the door, looking like she could use a cold shower and a good night's sleep. Shadow did a double take, trying to get something out, and almost not believing her eyes. 'D... Dawn?!' Dawn herself stammered, all of the confidence she had built up over the last few days fleeing her in the face of the woman who's opinion mattered so much to her. 'I...uh...' She started to back away, the fear of Lia's dissapointment weighing heavily on her mind and even more heavily on her heart. Lia, surprisingly enough threw her arms around Dawn, and pulled her closer, a stream of sudden exclamations spewing out over her shoulder. 'Where the hell were you? Jesus Christ, I've been looking all week!' Dawn's arms went involuntarily around Lia's waist, drawing her close despite the painful sunburn. 'I...I...ran away...' She finished lamely. Lia pulled away, holding Dawn by the shoulders and looking into her eyes. Surprisingly enough, what seemed to be blood tears rimmed the vampire's eyes. 'I know you ran away Dawn, but why?! I've been... I've been a mess, and you... God, you could have at least called and said you were okay, that's all I cared about.' Dawn flushed, this close to Lia's face she couldn't help the blush that crept up her neck and into her cheeks. 'I was sure...that you'd seen what happened between Emoticon and I...I'm sorry, I never meant for you to worry.' Her brow furrowed. 'I did see it, Dawn... but I only had concern for you, I... I knew he upset you so much that you were going to do something like this, but I just got to you too late.' She reached away from Dawn with one hand, wiping away the small droplet of blood that threatened to spill down her cheek. Fighting back a sudden rush of fear Dawn reached out to gently wipe away the other with her thumb, still blushing furiously. 'I'm sorry. I never meant to make you cry...' Shadow shook her slightly. 'Nevermind that!' She paused for a moment, a bit of ire coming into her eyes as she slowly woke up. 'Don't ever do this to any of us again, Dawn. Aren't you satisfied that we all care about you now?' She paused again, well aware of the incredibly maternal tone that she had taken, and her face fell. 'I'm sorry...' Dawn looked away, the blush fading. 'It's alright...I know I was acting like a child. I was afraid you wouldn't...wouldn't want to see me again, once you knew.' Shadow closed her eyes momentarily. 'Look... I don't think you're a freakshow. You're allowed to have whatever feelings you like, Dawn, so long as... so long as we can coexist, instead of fighting all the time.' Shadow let out a deep sigh. She had just hedged around the issue slightly, but hopefully, this would be a fitting compromise. Dawn nodded slowly. 'I think we can do that.' Shadow breathed another sigh, this one of relief. She smiled, stepping aside to allow Dawn in- it was her place, after all. 'Come on... you look like you could use a meal and a hot bath.'
