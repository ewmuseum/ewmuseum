New year, old enemies (Reload RP 1)

9 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/9/05 A new year has dawned. With it come new beginnings, new possibilities. Brick is working hard for his first match of the new year. He's been working out for the past hour and a half, because he wants to start the new year off right. At Reload, Global Wrestling's first card of the new year, Brick and his Ruckus Inc. partner Donny McNasty have a chance to become no. 1 contenders to the World Tag Titles, facing off against the current titleholders GSA on the next Evolution. Their opponents: Salaok and Derek Frost. While the chance at tag team gold is enough incentive, for Brick, there's more. This match won't just be about gold. It will be about vengeance and fury. No other name in the history of Global Wrestling has made Brick's blood boil more than Salaok. Not Terminator. Not Train. Not even Deacon Holmes. Those rivalries were fueled more by ego. But Brick's rivalry ---- Late January 2001: Salaok's brother Jonathan 'Scarecrow' Davis bludgeoned Brick from behind with a crowbar. For months, Brick had been looking to get even, which also brought him into conflict with Salaok. November 2001: In a ten-man cage elimination match, Brick would win a shot at the World title, which Salaok held at the time. After two years, Brick was finally getting his shot. Unfortunately, the match would not happen. A week later, Salaok would cheat Brick yet again by leaving the company. July 2002: The feud between the two men became even more personal. Brick's childhood friend Aaron Davis was kidnapped and tortured at Salaok's behest. The intent was to scare Brick away, but it only fueled Brick's anger, inspiring him to wage his own one-man war against the Harvesters. He was determined to go through them, one by one, if necessary. November 2002: The first anniversary of the night Salaok screwed Brick out of his World title shot came. It was also the night Brick's sister Monica and her then-fiance Kyle held their engagement party. Brick's mood was somewhat soured that night. It was made worse when he discovered Salaok and Quartz at the party. April 2003: Bubba McCoy was in a coma for months, due to being poisoned. Later, Salaok revealed that he had purchased half of Global Wrestling. He more or less ran roughshod over the company. But, as always, his favorite target was Brick. On all his matches, Salaok placed a special stipulation: if Brick were to lose so much as one match, he would be indefinitely suspended, until such time as Salaok saw fit to reinstante him. In the meantime, Salaok did whatever he could to get Brick to lose a match. Finally, Brick had gotten tired of Salaok's games. He challenged Salaok to a Hell in A Cell match at Wrestlecade 3. The match almost never happened. Brick was held up at the auto rental dealer and Salaok was eager to declare himself winner by default. But Brick wouldn't be denied. So intent on getting payback, he drove from the airport all the way down to the cell. The minute, he got out of the car, he and Salaok started to go at it, no holds barred. Despite the interference of Quartz and Mark Kullypso, Brick pulled May 2003: After finally getting his revenge, Brick set his sights on a goal which had eluded him for so many years: the Global Wrestling World Heavyweight championship. A tournament was held to crown a new World champion after Chris Camero vacated the title. The finals went down to two men: Brick and Sage Sabbot. But Salaok still had one scheme in play: he sent front-row tickets for Dead Zone 2003 to Brick's friends and family in order to distract Brick during the title match. Brick then countered by barricading the Harvesters' locker room with a forklift. Eventually, Salaok and the Harvesters escaped their confinement and headed down to the ring to stack the deck even higher. Brick eventually lost the match. June 2003: Despite Salaok's best efforts, Brick finally managed to get a rematch and finally won the World title. Meanwhile Salaok had his own drama to deal with. Suffering the loss of his wife and child, he decided to retire from Global Wrestling and the war between he and Brick seemed to have finally ended... Late October 2004: Salaok returned to Global Wrestling at Dark Rituals. November 2004: Salaok renewed the feud, first by setting up a booby trap in Brick's locker room. The next week, he taunted Brick with a gun that Aaron had purchased to protect his family and bodyguards Brick had hired to watch the Fosters. ---- At Reload, the two rivals face off again. Will it be the end of their feud? Or the beginning of a new chapter?
