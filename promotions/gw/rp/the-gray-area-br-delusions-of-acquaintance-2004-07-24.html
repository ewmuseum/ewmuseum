Date: 07.24.04 “Sanity is but a stepping stone to death. For the insane live a thousand lifetimes before any of them end.” - Mikhail Makya July 24th, 2004 ? Approximately 11:00 AM San Francisco suburbs, California ? Outside a small villa style home The sun is bright and powerful in the sky above, heating the air and the ground equally. Caught between air and ground is a silver and black Bugatti Veyron, the engine purring. The windows are mirrored, preventing anyone from seeing inside the rare vehicle. The high-performance automobile has been pulled up to the curb directly in front of the home. After a moment, the passenger side window lowers slowly, revealing the sole occupant. Maverick Reno is seated inside the car, his hair hanging loosely about his shoulders and neck. He turns his head slowly, looking through the now opened window and towards the home, his eyes shielded by a pair of oval Armani shades. “What the hell am I going to say to him?”” Maverick mutters the question rhetorically, knowing that it won’t be answered. He sighs and glances down at himself, examining his uncharacteristic clothing: a black “Global” t-shirt and a pair of blue jeans. He sighs, his eyes closing behind the glasses as he lowers his head, “how can I even face him?” he continues, his mind flooding with the events that have passed since their last conversation? July 10th, 2003 ? Approximately 6:00 PM Los Angeles, California ? Pierce’s Capoeira & Tae Kwon Do Dojo The mats had been removed from the floor, leaving the hardwood that had not been seen since Scott opened his small martial arts school three years before. Years had passed and his school had become one of renowned success, attracting students from all over the Los Angeles area. Scott’s wrestling career where he was able to show off his innovative combination of Capoeira and Tae Kwon Do had won him hundreds of thousands of martial art fans and when he opened up a school to teach his style of fighting, he was rushed with eager wrestling and martial art fans who wished to become one of the great cruiser weight’s pupils. Tragedy had struck Scott Pierce nearly a year before and this had been the first time in that period that he has stepped foot inside of his business. After the brutal murder of his wife and the savage beating that he undertook, Scott was both physically and emotionally unable to work. The shop had become another drain on Scott’s finances and it would be closed now that he’s unable to work in such an athletic profession. Scott stands with a cane, wearing a pair of sweat pants and a LA SPCA t-shirt. He hangs his head, his blonde hair graying at the roots. His face is unshaven, presenting a 10 o’clock shadow for all to see. Scott lifts his head again as workers move throughout the gym, removing equipment and decoration. A large box has been placed near Scott filled with Championship trophies and plaques all won by either him or his “team.” He is truly a shattered man; he has lost his family who he loved more dearly than anything in his life but atop this, as if represented by a kick to an already downed man, he had lost his ability to continue doing what he loves, teaching his arts. The door to the gym opens and closes, a small buzzer sounding. Scott clenches his eyes but does not turn, his still scarred face shaming him. “We’re closed!” Scott roars, insinuating that whomever it is should leave. Footsteps echo upon the hardwood, infuriating Scott. He spins, roaring his demand even more, “WE’RE CLOSED!” Scott looks up, finding himself starting into the cold eyes of a man who could be considered his brother, Maverick Reno. Maverick is adorned in a black Mandarin suit, his hair pulled back tightly, his face clean-shaven. Maverick lifts a hand and places it upon Scott’s shoulder, attempting to calm and console him. “How are you, Scott?” Maverick asks, his tone gentle. Scott’s eyes narrow, his anger and disgust growing to a boiling point before calling out, “Get out, Maverick! You don’t want to fuck with me right now.” Scott turns away from Maverick, shrugging from his grasp. His cane taps the floor as he walks to the crate of commemorative items, his eyes falling upon it. “Do you know what today is, Maverick” Today is hell. Today is the first day that I’ve been allowed to leave the hospital in over a year? I’ve been looking forward to being out of a sanitized dome but now, all I want to do is die again! I thought this would be a great day? a happy day? but when I walked.” Scott stops and looks down before continuing, “I didn’t walk. They wheeled me out of that hospital. Then I stood up but I couldn’t walk, Maverick. I had to hobble to a waiting car. Who was in it waiting for me” No one! Not a damned person!” Scott slams his cane against a mirror, shattering it. “Then, I drive to where my home USED to be. What do I find” Mister and Misses Amos! That’s right, Maverick? they sold the plot of land that I used to own. They sold it to cover my hospital bills!” Scott hangs his head for a moment, still using the cane to support himself. “So I called the hospital, in denial, and demanded to know where my family was. They referred me to a psychologist who promptly told me that my family was dead! So I drove to see them anyway. You should see it, Maverick” three little funeral plots, all in a row. Brittany, Mark, and Alicia? all in a row, Mav, all marked with matching tombstones! It was so fucking adorable that I couldn’t cry. I just dropped to my knees and vomited? I vomited and vomited all over my dead wife’s? and my dead children’s graves!” Tears now flow down Scott’s face, his eyes clenched shut. “I just sat there, staring at those tombstones for hours. When I finally stood up, all I wanted to do was to drive here and visit the place that I built. My second passion. But when I walked in, everything was packed up. Cobwebs had formed over everything else and dust blanketed everything. I walked to the center of the room” and I put my cane down. Mav, do you remember that kick I used to do? The horizontal spin kick? The one that made me famous? I tried to do it, Mav. You know what happened?” Scott stops, his eyes on the dead-silent Maverick Reno as if he expects an answer. “I didn’t even get off the ground. In fact, I got even closer! I fell and” Maverick? I?” Scott glances up as one of the workers walks by, carrying a box. “All finished, Mr. Pierce. We’re moving it all to the storage building. Do you want me to get that last box for you?” Scott shakes his head, his eyes moving to the box filled with trophies and plaques that now sits in the center of a bare room. Scott begins a slow walk to the box, his eyes set on it. “Let me get that for you, Scott?” Maverick offers, walking towards the box. The two men meet at the box and Maverick leans down to pick it up but is quickly shoved back with the tip of the cane, Scott glaring at him. “Do you think that just because I’m laid up that I’m helpless! I’ll get the damned box, Maverick. I can carry the fucking box?” Scott roars again, his tone more than hateful. Scott puts his cane inside the box and then attempts to lift it but as soon as it leaves the ground, his leg gives way and Scott falls to the ground, the boxes contents scattering all over the floor. Scott clenches his eyes, trying his hardest not to allow tears to flow again. Maverick walks around and begins to pick up the items, placing them back inside the box. “I want you to move into my place in London, Scott.” Maverick speaks without looking up, his hands busy as they transport item after item to the box. “I’ve spoken with your mother and father and we feel that you need to be around people who love you. That?” Scott cuts Maverick off abruptly, his hand grabbing his cane, “You think I’m helpless, is that is, Mav!”” “No, Scott, we just think?” Maverick retorts, his eyes now lifting as he kneels before his fallen ally. Scott attempts to push himself up and does so at a very slow rate, using his cane to help him. “Fuck you.” Scott starts walking off, leaving the box where it lies. Maverick sighs and stands up, box in hand. Scott spins on his heel suddenly, glaring at him, “I expected the “pity Scott” routine from my parents and my family, Maverick. I never expected it from you? and I fucking hate you for it. You think that I’m a cripple now! You think that I can’t do anything on my own anymore, is that it?” Scott is so defensive, he doesn’t even see the truth? that Maverick is really trying to help him. Maverick shakes his head, speaking calmly still, “No, Scott. Not at all. I just don’t think that you’re living all the way out here in Los Angeles with no one is healthy. We want you around family or friends. If you’d prefer, I’m sure you could move back to ?” “Boston” Nantucket? That it, Mav? Yes, that’s what I want to do! Let’s run home to mommy and daddy so that all of their love and money can take care of me for the rest of my life? so that I can officially be a worthless, helpless, pathetic human being!” Scott pauses for a moment and then wobbles to Reno and attempts to jerk the box from his arms. “Give me the fucking?” “No. You’re not able, Scott! You’re not helpless, but that doesn’t mean you’re in good shape! You need help and you’re going to have to admit that to yourself sooner or later! The rest of us already know it.” A moment passes after Maverick finally explodes before a reaction takes place. Scott slams his bare fist against the side of Maverick’s face, causing him to stagger back and to the ground, the box on top of him. Scott tosses the cane down and motions to Maverick as if he wants to fight, “Get up! If I’m so fucking helpless” so in need of your help, why are you the one on the floor!”” Maverick moves the box from atop himself and pushes up. “This isn’t worth it. You want to be tough, Scott” Well congratulations. You’re a big man, Scott.” Maverick pushes past Scott and begins his route to the door, not looking back. Scott grows angrier by the second, not getting the reaction he wanted. Finally he starts after and slams both of his fists down atop Reno’s back, sending him staggering forward and to one knee. This time, Reno has taken enough; Maverick lifts up and spins at the same time, backhanding Scott. Scott falls to the ground and grabs the side of his face, his expression showing complete shock. “Get the fuck out of here, Maverick. You’ve done your damage” you proved your point. I’m helpless. Now help me out in the only way you can? get the fuck out of my life.” Maverick stares down at his friend, searching for words to describe how sorry he feels, how much he cares. “GET THE FUCK OUT!” Scott screams. Maverick turns and walks through the doors? July 24th, 2004 ? Approximately 11:10 AM San Francisco suburbs, California ? Outside a small villa style home Maverick leans back in the seat and glances again at the house. The grass is mowed and the yard has been kept nicely. A hunter green Jeep Wrangler sits in the driveway, marking the fact that Scott is at home. Maverick sighs and rolls up the window; inside the vehicle, he grabs a small black hat marked with the blue Ankh of Reno and pulls it onto his head. The drivers side door opens and Maverick stands from the car, his eyes searching the entire area. A black BMW passes him by, prompting his eyes to follow the vehicle carefully. The license plate reads “TUE27.” Maverick Reno is never alone. He turns slowly as he slips the keys into his jean pocket and starts towards the door to the home. No one is in sight, including any neighbors. Maverick stops at the door and looks around, attempting to see through a window nearby. No luck. Reno bows his head for a moment and lifts his hand, knocking at the door. Maverick’s anxiety is understandable; Scott and his last interaction had not been a milestone in understanding. After a moment, the door opens to reveal Scott Pierce. He’s adorned in a pair of red and black flannel pants and a Humana t-shirt. He’s still walking with a cane although, he’s no longer in pain. His face as healed drastically since the last time these two spoke. Scott glances down for a moment and then back up at his long-time friend. “Well, come on in?” Scott says as he turns away, walking into the home. Maverick follows him inside and closes the door behind him. “Can I get you anything, Mav?” “I came to talk, Scott.” Maverick responds, his eyes moving about Scott’s new home. He carefully walks around in the living room, “It’s been a long time.” Scott emerges again, nodding his head. “That it has, Maverick.” Scott motions to a soft sectional sofa before he takes a seat in a recliner. “Although, I’m curious as to why you’re showing up now” from what I’ve seen and heard, you’re leading quite the busy life.” “Busy, yes. Full, no. Scott?” Maverick speaks, removing his hat and placing it into his lap. It’s as if Maverick is coy and afraid of what his friend might say. Scott holds up his hand and bites his bottom lip before speaking, “I’m sorry for what happened in the gym. I” I’ve been meaning to call you for so long. I’ve just? there’s been so much that has changed, Maverick. I was afraid that I wouldn’t recognize you.” Maverick glances down, “Scott, you’re like a brother to me. You always have been. Last year, we both said some things and I think that we should just let the past remain just that.” “Well, you’re close. Everything you said was true.” Scott glances up, both men solemn and quiet. An awkward silence consumes the room. “I’ve been watching Global.” Scott glances down again, unsure of how this will go over with Maverick. Maverick glances up and nods before Scott speaks again, “you have a huge opportunity to be on top again, Mav. Don’t blow it?” “I don’t plan on it, Scott. In fact, that’s part of the reason I’m here. I wanted to talk to you about” some things that I’ve done.” Maverick starts, squeamish in his approach to the topic. Scott glances down for a moment before asking, “It’s not all show, is it” I’ve been suspecting that since Tyler started working with you. The family has been concerned about him? or atleast they were until he came back to help with the business in Boston. He was different after he joined, Maverick, and? not in the best of ways.” Scott watches for a reaction but receives nothing but a downward glance from Maverick. “And he’s not the only one.” Scott ventures to add, his eyes directed at Maverick, “I don’t know what to say about it, Maverick” and I don’t know what you want me to say.” “I did it” and a lot of it, I’m not proud of. But that doesn’t mean that if I could go back, I’d do it any other way. My accomplishments in the past year may have been aided by cruelty but they are still my accomplishments.” Maverick lifts his head, his jet black hair hanging about the frame of his face. Scott watches him for a moment before standing and moving to sit next to him, Scott’s hand finding Maverick’s shoulder. “We all do things that we’re not proud of, Mav”. The question is, what are you going to do to make up for it? What is in your heart?” Maverick glances to the side and at his friend, silence over coming them both. Finally, Reno’s words shatter the silence, “I fear the depths of my heart, Scott. I feel no remorse for my actions.” “Why do you do it then?” Scott asks, his curiosity lending courage. “Because” they?.” Maverick stutters before standing and walking towards a sliding glass door. “Can we change the subject, Scott?” “Sure?” Scott leans back against the sofa, his eyes reverting to the floor. Maverick paces for a moment before he speaks again, getting to the point, “Scott, I want you as my trainer, again.” Maverick glances behind him to his friend who lifts his head. “I need to be in perfect condition for Master of the Mat” and I need your help to get me there.” Scott exhales, his mind mulling over the request long and hard. Maverick looks back through the glass window, attempting not to stare at or pressure his friend. “So you’re offering me a job?” “I’m asking for our friendship back?” Maverick never looks back, still staring at the man-made waterfall in the backyard. “And I need your help. You’re the best at what you do, Scott.” “And” you want me to help you win Master of the Mat?” Scott persists, his question causing Maverick to turn and nod, giving his answer without speaking. “In Dallas?” “Ontario. London at first.” Maverick answers him this time, hoping for him to accept the offer. “I’ll pay all of the expenses and I can promise that it’ll be worth your time.” Scott stands, his leg shaking at first. He grabs onto his cane and walks to a cleared version of the room. “I’m not concerned with money.” “I know that, Scott.” Maverick offers a half-smile. Scott props his cane against the wall and motions at himself, “Mav”, do you remember that kick I used to do? The one that made me famous?” Maverick nods slowly, curious as to where his friend is going with this. “A lot of time has passed since I fell that day in my old gym. I’ve tried over and over again, nearly every day, to perform that kick.” Scott lowers slightly on his back leg before thrusting it forward and his front leg back, performing Capoeira style genga. Maverick takes a step back to watch. “If I can do it” then I’ll help you to train, Mav?.” Scott stares at Maverick for a moment before launching himself to the side, his body moving to a horizontal position about three feet from the ground. Scott bicycle kicks his legs, and comes down on the defensive, brings down his offensive and spins, delivering a spin kick before stopping and staggering to the side and grabbing the wall. Scott breathes heavily, his eyes down. “I was close, wasn’t I?” “More than I could do, that’s for sure.” Maverick offers a full smile this time. Scott looks at Maverick for a moment, his eyes searching him for some unseen factor. “So when do we leave?” The scene fades to black. July 25th, 2004 ? Approximately 3 am New York City, New York ? Echelon Tower The heavens pour rain from above, soaking the American metropolitan beyond saturation. The streets flood with biblical waters, cleansing the sin of man from the Earth’s terrain. The heavy raindrops beat down against the thick glass that separates the Echelon office from New York. Wind howls, forcing the clouds above to race as if their destination lies elsewhere along with some imperative goal. Importance is relative. To the citizens of New York City, their umbrellas will be important in the morning. To the clouds above, their destination is important. To Maverick Reno, domination is important. ---- Master of the Mat Five lurks around the corner, offering a chance for not one, two but three victories and two great awards. The first is, of course, the Master of the Mat tournament and championship. To say that total victory was achieved in this annual event would bring glorious reward. Maverick would garner a coveted GW prize that only four before him have held. In the previous years, those with endurance, perseverance, and an ability to demolish their opponents have always conquered. In the first Master of the Mat, in the days of when it was the Global Wrestling Federation, an upstart death-defying cruiser took the coveted award. Kamikaze was agile, fast and strong. His combination of speed and high impact strikes carried him from the first round to the winners circle at the end of the night. In the Main Event, he would walk away with the victory. In the second year, the Canadian star Chris Camero walked into the ring. It started off a bit turbulent for the cocky superstar but when the final bell rang in the main event, the man known as “The Canadian Guy” etched his name into the GWF record books. Camero’s mat wrestling skills were far above the common fighter in Global and he had a knack for forcing his opponent to fight on the ground where he was sure to obtain victory. Year three; Master of the Mat is officially a tradition. The “F” in GWF has been dropped and Global Wrestling reigns. The Young Bloods rule the streets and at the helm are Johnny Cruse and “The Ladies Man” Jay Robson. Robson’s combination of power wrestling and cruiser type “high spots” got him past his tougher opponents. His ability to distract his opponents got him past the rookies. In the end, ring psychology, speed and powerful strikes took TLM from roster filler to Global Legend. Year four. The days of the Nevermore were upon Global Wrestling. They had been here before but their power has spread and overtaken the staff. Salaok was a sure bet to take Master of the Mat in June. The Young Bloods are nearly extinct, Salaok having quelled their resistance. Bubba McCoy is no longer in the picture. Christina McCoy has taken her father’s daunting task of keeping Global Wrestling alive through the tyranny of the Nevermore. From the lowest rungs of Global Wrestling, a mighty force rears its head. The Upper Echelon is born and fights diligently, with Christina’s blessing, against the Nevermore. Power overcomes as these four, then five and so on unite against the powers that be. Master of the Mat IV. Sean Price hits the ring in round one, escaping with a near miss. Round two comes and another victory for Price. Sean’s amazing strength is unparalleled for a man of his speed. His ability to lock onto his opponents and deliver powerful, ground shaking maneuvers at lightning speeds scorches his opponents. Sean Price takes the championship. The Upper Echelon is finally in the record books. Master of the Mat V. Many consider this year to be the most competitive year ever in Global Wrestling. Many greats and legends have passed through the withered halls and a new force remains. Josh is in the running, the World Champion himself. Sage Sabbot, the dead man, stares at the ring in wait of his chance to show the world what he’s made of. Jesse Jamester, a man who dreams of standing atop the Global hierarchy and sees this as his chance. The caliber of the competitors this year is simply spectacular. But in the end, when the night comes to a close, only one man will be both Champion and Master. Only one man will hold ultimate victory. He’s coming for them both. ---- The echo of the rain nearly shakes the room as it continues its relentless assault upon the humble Earth. Questions swarm the mind as we watch Maverick in his endless pursuit of perfection. Why is Maverick so determined and vigilant about Master of the Mat?
