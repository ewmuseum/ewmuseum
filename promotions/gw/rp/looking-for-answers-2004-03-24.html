Looking for answers

24 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03.24.04 'No, I told you, Christina, I have no idea what the hell's going on. We busted in, Josh was face down on the floor with an empty bottle of Rohypnol next to h... Rohypnol... the 'Forget Me Pill'... Dammit, Christina, the 'Date Rape Drug.' ... Yeah.' Ken Partridge, Jr. is noticably on end as he goes back and forth on the phone with Christina McCoy. He stands in the hallway of a Roman hospital, one hand holding his silver cell phone to his ear, the other trading spaces between the wall and the back of his head, where he is sparatically grabbing his hair in anxiety. 'No... nothing. I'm still waiting on the doctor... a few minutes, I guess. He's in there right now with the nurse.' There's a long pause as Ken reaches to the back of his head again and pulls on some of his hair. He then rests the phone between his head and shoulder, lifts both arms in the air and stretches a little before scooping the phone back up and beginning to pace. 'Christina, have you heard from Ace? -- Well, neither myself nor Brock can get ahold of him. -- We've already left him messages. He still hasn't called us back.' It becomes quuite noticable, while Ken's on the phone every small movement catches his attention. Every sound rattles his senses. He wants to know what's going on with his best-friend... with his 'brother.' Tears begin to swell up in Ken's eyes as he begins to shout at Christina over her next comment. 'DAMMIT, CHRIS, I DON'T REALLY SEE IT AS A FUCKING PRIORITY AS TO WHETHER OR NOT WE'RE GOING TO BE ABLE TO PARTICIPATE IN THE RUMBLE AS OF RIGHT NOW!' A few heads turn with Ken's comments towards the owner's daughter. But, Ken honestly didn't care. This was one of the moments where nothing else matters but the situation at hand. Ken had already lost one of his best-friends and he was not prepared to lose another... not now... not ever. Death was something that Ken had come to accept for himself, but now for other people. Not for Stephanie... not for Todd... and not for Josh. Josh's hospital room door soon opens and is softly closed by a very lovely nurse. She turns and looks at Ken, who quickly finishes his call with Christina. 'Look, if you talk to Ace, tell him to call me immediatly. I have to go.' Before Christina even has a chance to respond, Ken flips the phone closed and looks at the nurse. 'The doctor will be finished in just a few minutes, you can go on in.' Ken takes a deep breath and eneters the room, while all at once, memories begin to flood his mind.
