The Joruney: Part 17/The Connection's POV

15 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-15-05 Early Afternoon/Late evening Sometimes we just need to get away? It’s a known fact that when you live in a house with people and you see them on a very consistent basis sometimes its best to go away for a little while. When you do sometimes you realize how appreciative you are of the things that you have and when you come back you area new person and you decide that things need to change in your life and you need people. Imagine leaving your home for a day when you think things are pretty bad and then realizing that you got some of the best friends in the world and even people that don’t know you respect you so it becomes a little bit easier for you to cope with things. Welcome to the world of Joshua Curtis and The Connection Is it necessarily easy in this world for Joshua Curtis? Hell no and the fact that there is a good percentage of people in what is referred to as “Straight America” that have cursed at him and called him names throughout the years that he has existed on this planet doesn’t make things any better for him but there are a few places that he could go if he really needed to get away from his usual routine at home? Now on this day its about 2:45-3:00 in the afternoon and Joshua is sitting at home wearing a “Dave & Buster’s” T-shirt, Arizona Blue Jeans and a pair of white Hanes socks laying across the big ass loveseat that they just bought from Raymour &Flannigan’s wondering what to do for the day. Ami was out with Michiru-san, Zack was out with some friends of his and Juri and Tina went trolling through Times Square so they won’t be coming back for a spell. What is a person to do? ?**** THIS!” Joshua yelled in frustration and went upstairs to go get changed for a trip out for the day. As Joshua went upstairs he looked at the time and 3:00 had just hit so he knew that he had time to do a lot of things but what is a guy to wear? Typically because Zack is home he helps pick Joshua’s outfits out for him and the two then would go on a trip to either in the city or just completely out of New York but since Zack wasn’t home Joshua either had to pick it himself OR get a completely outside opinion? Joshua chose the latter... *Ring Ring* The phone rang at the Winner Residence for a good 5 minutes before the young blonde there picked up on the other end and spoke into the receiver. “Hello. Winner Residence this is Quatre how may I help you today?” Quatre asked on his end and when he heard Joshua’s voice on the other end he beamed from ear to ear as it wasn’t often that he heard from the man whom was at one time Zechs? apprentice and Noin’s aide in class. “Good afternoon Quatre!” Joshua exclaimed as he heard his friend’s voice “How is my favorite Gundam Pilot doing?” Joshua asked and the two of them chuckled at the comment before Quatre regained his composure and responded to Curtis. “Just fine over here Joshua. I have a function to go to in a couple of hours with Trowa and its for the new organization that Zechs has formed to replace the Romefeller Foundation and Trowa and I got invited on behalf of Noin and Duo.” Quatre explained to Joshua and the “Man that wears Zechs” mask? couldn’t help but be happy for Quatre but one question always lingered in the back of his mind? “You ask Trowa out yet?” Joshua asked as he knew that Quatre had a thing for the silent circus perfomer. “Nah didn’t really have the time with working for Lucrezia and all. I really want to though to see if I can get him to open up and not be so damn anti-social around people. I remember when you used to be the opposite and Noin changed you quick! She made you into an obedient soldier that took orders and would fight for what you thought was the right thing to do and I respected you for that and still do.” Quatre told Joshua and that is when Joshua decided to get to the heart of the matter. “Ok enough serious talk” Josh began “Quatre the reason I called is..” Joshua was cut off by Quatre’s voice on the other end. “You want an opinion on what to wear and I’m the only Gundam Pilot with any kind of fashion sense right?” Quatre said as he playfully ribbed Curtis over the phone. “But of course Master Quatre you know that if I asked Duo he’d tell me to get that black outfit out that he gave me a while ago and unlike Tina whom wears black almost exclusively’it does nothing for me.” Joshua explained to Quatre whom thought long and hard about this one before answering. “Well I know about the scars on your left arm so we need something to cover that up and we need something to cover the scar about your left eye so?” And before Quatre could finish his thought Joshua had pretty much pegged it! ??The Outfit?”” Joshua asked and Quatre nodded on the other end which was when Joshua thanked his young friend for his help and hung the phone up. Now is it a secret that Joshua wears this outfit a lot? Kind of but really Josh wears other things its just that with where he intended to go he wanted to look presentable to his public. As Joshua went to his closet and pulled out the outfit that was tailor made for him by Lucrezia herself Joshua began the task of stripping before heading towards the shower and going through the act of cleansing oneself. Now let’s let it be known that Joshua doesn’t have a “perfect body” but then again really the only people that attain such goals is by steroids and other enhancers. Joshua stands about 5 feet 8 and weighs a slender 150 pounds ,which if you were related to him would probably make you think there was cause for concern but really there isn’t. Joshua will forever cite that Zechs was remarkably skinny and that the body that he had would make a lot of women(and some men) swoon over him like they were back in high school’hell Joshua once said while on the base with Lucrezia that if he wasn’t already dating Zack then Zechs would be the perfect person for him. Why? Well for starters Zechs was very much quiet, reserved emotionally and only really lost it when he was backed in a corner ,which was something that happened to him on an irregular basis on the base but when it did Joshua defended him as if they WERE dating! After about 20 minutes in the shower Joshua dried off and fixed up his platinum blonde hair just right so that the helmet wouldn’t completely mess it up. When Joshua got out of the shower it was on to reapplying the bandages to his arm from when the TV splintered and stabbed him there and the stitches above his left eye were slowly dissolving thanks to the procedure that they used to stitch him up back in Detroit. Afterwards Joshua left the confines of the shower to the eerie stillness that was left in the home that his stable’s reputation helped build and he didn’t like it. Zack was gone for the next couple of months and the rest of his group save Ami are on business affairs but Joshua didn’t let that bother him in the least. When Joshua got back into the room he shared with Zack he slowly picked up the pants that were to go on and the socks that went underneath them and his mind started to wander but he quickly dismissed it as he needed to do this NOW! Josh sat on the bed and put the socks up to just below his calf and rolled them down before putting on the white pants with the black belt that had a special one of a kind “OSC” insignia on the buckle as opposed to the letters that spelled “OZ” for them. After putting on the bottom half of the outfit Joshua decided to go ahead and put the boots on so that when he took care of the top half he could go without incident. Joshua slowly picked up the jacket with the black button collar and this time no matter how hard he fought against it the visions slowly returned to him of the day that he was presented with the outfit in front of all the soldiers on the base? “This is for me?” Joshua asked Lucrezia and the Baronet smiled sweetly at Joshua and he knew then that he couldn’t refuse it. He didn’t tear into the package as some thought that he would but rather with a delicate touch like a surgeon he carefully opened the package in front of everyone and pulled out the jacket that was identical to the one that Zechs wore and the rest of the outfit was very much identical to his! “Lucrezia?” Was all Joshua got out when he felt the lips of the young lady on his and it was a gentle kiss’you know the kind that makes your lips burn and but yet you have the knowledge that people care about you. Zack did that a couple of times and it always had the effect on Curtis that was to be expected. That night at a special ball that was being held by the military group Lucrezia had asked Joshua to come to it wearing the outfit that she made for him and he agreed to do it albeit at first reluctantly but Zechs promised him that if anyone wanted to ridicule him about it then they would deal with him personally and they didn’t want that! So Joshua went to the ball arm and arm with Lucrezia and a few of the guys shot admiring glances at the two and a lot of the soldiers were jealous of Curtis because of the fact that he “landed her” and while they played along with the ruse of them being together they both knew what was going on. When Joshua left the base he left some memories on that base and it tore him up in some ways because of the closeness that he and Lucrezia had and the fact that he left a very understanding man in Zech Marquis but somehow he knew that he would see him again some day. Back to the here and now? *Zechs where could you be? I need you know more than ever!* Joshua thought to himself but dismissed that notion because that was a sign of weakness to him and he wanted to remain as strong emotionally as he could for his Lieutenant and God only knows if Lucrezia were to ever visit the last person that she would want to see upset is the man that she has hoped would carry Zechs/Millardo’s name into the wrestling ring. “Lightning Count” wasn’t a name that Zechs took lightly when he worked for a friend of his and he would be damned if that name would be disrespected by anyone! As Joshua put the red shirt that came with the jacket on underneath and then proceeded to put the jacket on and button it up Josh remembered the one thing that was missing from the outfit and he remembered what it was like when he got that from Lucrezia “Josh Curtis you have a visitor.” The security guard told Joshua before Lucrezia came in a “OSC” T-shirt, Wrangler Blue Jeans and a pair of Keds with no socks and she had that smile that Josh would always remember and she had her hands behind her back as if she was hiding something and Curtis knew that something was up he just didn’t know what. “Josh remember when you came to our base and we presented you with the outfit that you are wearing right now?” Lucrezia asked and Joshua kind of trembled for a minute as he thought he was going to get lectured about wearing that for a ring attire in any fed and the reality was that she was happy that he did but she did have something that was apparently a missing piece to the outfit. “Joshua when you were on the base you told me that one of the things that you liked that Zechs wore was a helmet that you thought he hid behind but at the same time made him mysterious remember?” Lucrezia asked and Joshua nodded wondering where this was going exactly until she took her hands from behind her back and showed Joshua the same helmet that Zechs wore and now the outfit was complete for Joshua. “Noin I-I don’t know what to say!” Joshua somewhat stammered out but the look on his friends face told him that he didn’t need to say anything and Joshua left it alone. As the match time came Lucrezia was going to leave the locker room but Joshua thought it would be cool if she could escort him to the ring that night and she willingly agreed to do it for her friend. Joshua won that match and the beginning of the “Lightning Count” era began in the IWF and a lot of fans really didn’t know that that was Curtis behind the mask even though he took it off to wrestle all the time. It was like Joshua was a different person than the man that they were watching in the ring and the fans took to this “new look” Curtis a lot quicker than expected and even his wrestling style changed from the high flyer that they all knew to the submissionist that he tends to be now! Back to the present? Curtis has been out of the house for a good hour and riding around in his Viper when an idea comes to him while he is still near Times Square? *I wonder if that club is open now?* The club that Joshua is referring to is kind of on the boundaries of Times Square as you enter New York City. The club has been run by a friend of his for a couple of years and here is the interesting thing: Most clubs are catered to one gender/orientation but not this one and its run on the “no prejudice” rule for this long and has been widely accepted by all of society which always makes for good business. As Joshua walked in Nonpoint’s “In the air tonight” cover was playing over the speakers and a good sized crowd had showed up alredy which didn’t really surprise Joshua because it was one of the few “Under 18 allowed” clubs that he knew and he always helped out for about an hour if he could. “What’s up Brenda?” Joshua asked the woman with the psychedelic hair style and she smiled at Curtis before answering. “Not much boss” Brenda started and leaned on the counter showing her endowments a little which caused Joshua to clear his throat a little before she continued “I hear that you are starting to turn your career around in GW” I’m happy for you and the fact that you have found the way you want to go in that place cos some of us heard that you weren’t happy there.” Brenda explained and Joshua nodded before speaking again. “Its true that I was having problems but I came to realize that I need to make my own breaks and I think something good is going to happen in a couple of months for me I just feel it in my bones’say mind if I see what you are wearing” I got to pick up an outfit for someone and I want to see if I can get some ideas from you.” Joshua asked and Brenda obliged as she stepped from behind the counter is when Joshua took off his mask and saw that she had a tie-dyed shirt with the sleeves cut off, some acid washed jeans that showed off here slender legs rather well and a pair of black stilettos that looked to have about a 3-4 inch heel and after examining his friend Brenda went back behind the bar at which point Joshua excused himself and took a look at the populace in the club that was actually dancing to the song on the speakers which was actually “Disco Inferno” by The Trammps! While Joshua was walking through the crowd of people he noticed one person that was sitting at a table by herself and you wouldn’t believe who it was! “Lady Une”!” Joshua asked in surprise as he walked over to the table and made himself comfortable “To what do we owe the pleasure of you coming to this club at this time of day?” Joshua asked the lady that while they might not have been good friends he respected quite a bit in the military. “Huh” Oh hi Josh. I just came to see if I could get away from the people that have completely forgotten that today of all days was my birthday!” Une told Joshua and Joshua felt really bad for her because of this so he came up with a quick solution and when he heard the next song play and the fact that it was “Groovy Kind of Love” made for an interesting moment but Une knew him too well and agreed before he spoke which didn’t surprise him cos she was always like that. They danced in the center of the room and a lot of people just stared in awe that Joshua would dance with the woman that many felt he didn’t like but judging by the actions of what they were watching either Joshua changed or he just felt pity for the girl ,but only Joshua knew why he did it and that is all that matters to the two in the room. After the dance the majority of the room applauded the two and after taking their bows and Joshua did a quick check for any trouble he took his leave and bowed to Brenda before going back to his Viper and that dance with Lady Une made Joshua think of one woman that he felt he needed to go visit.. Gunner Addison! Josh got into his Viper and messed with his special system to find the location of the house that Ken lived at and after confirming it he put his helmet back on went to a place that he had visited one time before but he wasn’t alone at that time and while Curtis felt comfortable going anywhere this place was a little foreign to him and why? Well it’s the turf of his closest friend in GW and one of the few people that he confides in Ken Partridge Jr. and the fact that he was about to go see him and/or Gunner made him a little bit nervous because they haven’t had a real face to face encounter since?.2 weeks before SiT! Now to some this ain’t a big deal but for Joshua this meant that he was about to go and talk to someone face to face without any cameras or reporters around which made it that much more special and nerve-racking at the same time because lest we forget that Joshua had gone into their locker room a few weeks back and delivered a picture to Gunner that she wasn’t there to accept personally but Joshua had to basically walk into their locker room unannounced and uninvited and deliver it or he wasn’t going to be the same ever again! A little dramatic? Maybe but Josh knew that this was an important moment and after pulling up to the parking lot and shutting off the engine Joshua took one more breath before walking up the steps to the entrance to the apartment complex and looking at the directory of where all the rooms where. *1270B* Joshua thought to himself and now wished he DIDN’T wear the outfit that he had picked out but he knew that to not show how messed up he was to Gunner(or Ken for that matter) it was a necessary evil and but what caused it he will not discuss he already decided that as he went and looked for the elevator in the apartment complex and he was glad to find it cos let’s be honest when you climb that many steps yo wonder how the hell you are living afterwards’then again Joshua was always some what hard headed. By the time Joshua got his fist up to knock he heard a voice on the other end but he knew it wasn’t Ken’s? OH SHIT! As the door opened Joshua saw Gunner Addison, Ken’s friend and confidant for the first time since the Evolution before SiT face to face and Joshua’s first reaction was to start babling like a moron but another part of him decided to stick it out since he came this far! “Good Evening Ms. Addison. I’m not sure if you remember me but my name is Joshua Curtis a one time partner of a friend of yours named Ken Partridge Jr.” Joshua explained in as formal a tone as he could and the blonde-haired companion of KPJ motioned for Joshua to come in and as he did he didn’t take his helmet off’at least not yet. “Its been a while Joshua” Gunner said as Joshua took notice to the outfit that she was wearing and basically made a mental check that her and Ken were going out in a bit so this social call might have to be extremely brief unless an act of God happened but I digress? ??Yes its has Ms. Addison. I tend to think its been too long since I last spoke to you or Ken and believe me I don’t want to be a stranger to you guys but at the same time I know better than to pester a woman whom is quite capable of beating my ass or a man whom is one of the true legends of GW. I basically wanted to come and say hi and to tell you that if you needed me for anything and I mean ANYTHING I’ll be around as I think I might like this neighborhood y’all live in.” Joshua said and that got a slight laugh from the Memphis ,Tennessee native which was something that Joshua rarely got to hear or do himself so the fact that he heard it from Gunner was pretty damn special for him. “Hey Josh” Gunner started as she regained her composure from laughing “remember a couple of weeks ago when you and Ami came to our locker room to drop that picture off?” Gunner finished and Joshua nodded before she led him to their room and Joshua saw the picture with the note in it and at that point the soldier in Joshua wanted him to stand and explain himself while the coward wanted him to run like hell so Gunner couldn’t whoop his ass but honestly by the reaction that Gunner had to the note by reading it again he did a “choice C”: he hugged her! “Shh its ok Gunner. I’m sorry I had to write that note and quite honestly now I have my regrets for doing it but I just wanted to say “thank you” because without people like you and Ken in my life I don’t know what I would have done and I think its because of you guys that I’m still here and I am forever in your debt.” Joshua told his friend and Gunner looked him in the face and put her hands on his helmet to try and take it off but he wouldn’t let her’at least not yet. “Gunner” Josh began before I take off my mask I want you to know that what happened isn’t because of Ken but because of a blind rage that I was in after HO ok?” Joshua told Gunner and she nodded before taking off the mask and seeing the scar from the stitches about Joshua’s left eye and something told her the cuts on his arm would be worse! “Josh can you please take off your jacket for me” I want to see what happened to your arm because I heard from Brandi that it was pretty bad and I want to see it for myself.” Gunner told Joshua and Josh pretty much did as instructed and just by the bandage she saw she knew that it was pretty bad. “Fair enough you may put your jacket back on and come with me.” Gunner told Joshua and back they were in the living room but they went to the kitchen area where they sat and talked about life in general and how Joshua’s luck might have started to change after the incident at HO. It was during this time however that Joshua suffered a “freeze” where he just sat there and even though he heard everything Gunner said he just froze and didn’t respond which scared her half to death and he didn’t want that! “What the?” Joshua stammered as he woke from his trance holding his head and got an ice pack from the woman that he calls “Miss GW”. As Joshua held the ice pack Gunner placed a gentle hand on his shoulder and whatever worries or fears he seemed to have just left his body in an instant and any headache that he had was gone instantly! There was a reason that Ken and Gunner are a good couple and this one was one of the best anyone could come up with! About an hour or so later and Joshua had to take his leave as much as he didn’t want to but he had a sister to go home to and she was going to be worried sick if she came home to an empty home although now that you think about it when he was around Gunner he felt like he was back home but why?! As Joshua took his leave and went back to his car he saw that a Porsche had pulled up by his car and Josh didn’t need to guess ,for he knew who that car belonged to! “Kenny!” Joshua called out and the man that IS “The Superstar” or the “Franchise” of GW in Ken Partridge Jr. turned to see whom had called his name and here it was his friend that he hadn’t seen in a long time! “Hey Josh” Ken began “How’s life treating you?” he finished knowing the answer just by looking at Curtis’s face! “Any better and I’m dancing on your rooftop tonight”.naked!” Josh told Ken and the two close friends had a good laugh at the expense of no one. When Joshua left the apartment a part of him wanted to stick around SoHo but the reality of it was that sis was about to get real worried and Josh hated that with a passion! As Joshua pulled into the drvieway Ami was sitting on the doorstep to the home and Joshua felt bad for her waiting this long for him but he just needed some time away from that house at the moment. “Where have you been brother?” Ami asked somewhat sternly and Curtis knew that he had some explaining to do. “Well lets see here Ami” Joshua began “first I went to that club that you sometimes go to with Michiru and saw an old friend and then I went to Manhattan and the SoHo district to go visit someone.” Joshua explained to Ami and the genius already knew who so she didn’t press the issue. “Well let’s go inside brother. We got a lot of preparing to do for next week cos we get that “La Resistance” team again.” Ami told Joshua and Josh sighed almost instantly but held it in as they walked in and shut the door tight! See you later darlings
