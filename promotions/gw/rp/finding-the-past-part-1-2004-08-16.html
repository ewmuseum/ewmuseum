Finding The Past (Part 1)

16 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08.16.04 The sounds of the monitors awoke Ken from his slumber, only to send him into a state of distress. As his eyes panned around the room, the caught glimpses of computers and IVs and medical instruments of the sort. He looked down to find himself in a hospital bed and gown. And the screaming in his head began. The distress left him only to be replaced with panic. Ken had no idea why he was in the hospital or how he had gotten there. He had no idea even how long he'd been there. As he broke into a cold sweat, his mind raced through past events as best he could remember. He remembered losing the International Championship and then garnering the Television Title. He remembered when he finally got the opportunity to see and speak with Gunner again, causing a state of calmness as he took in the warm embiance of her smile. But he could not remember how he'd gotten there. A loud solid beep filled the room as he began ripping cords off of him and struggling to get out of bed, causing orderly and doctors to fill the room of the GW star. They struggled to get him back into bed as he screamed in protest. As one particularly large male nurse came towards him, Ken knocked him back and to the ground with a well placed elbow shot to the face. From looking at him, Ken tell that he'd broken the nurses nose. The man in the floor with blood on his white uniform was enough to cause the rest to release Ken and back-up. 'Why am I here,' Ken asked. 'A typical question for someone in your state,' bellowed a low raspy voice coming from an unseen source in the back. A short man that looked to be in his late fifties pushed his way through the crowd of staff members circling a poised and ready Ken. He gazed at KPJ over the top of a pair of circular glasses that where resting on the tip of his nose. 'You're quite a miracle story, you know,' continued the doctor. 'Most wouldn't have survived the accident you had.' 'Accident,' questioned Ken solemnly, 'what accident?' The doctor developed a curious look across his face. 'You... you don't remember,' he asked. 'Remember what,' yelled Ken. 'What in the hell is going on? What accident are you talking about?' 'Mr. Partridge, you were involved in a life-threatening car accident with and 18-wheeler. I'm sorry to tell you that your car is destroyed... and you should be as well,' replied the doctor in a serious tone. 'Wait a minute,' began Ken, 'you're telling me that I got ran over by a fucking semi?' 'In a nut-shell, yes,' resounded the man. 'From reports, you were sitting a traffic light when the truck came up behind you at around 50 miles an hour. It flipped your car several times before it made contact again, and when it did it ran over the passenger side of your car. Apparntly, the driver had fallen asleep at the wheel, and...' -Chaos- 'When in the hell was this,' scremed Ken violently as he began to stumble backwards onto the bed. 'Around three weeks ago or so, I would say. I was brought in after you were already here. You came out lucky, Mr. Partridge. You suffered a concussion, a neck spur, and a state of comatos... and memory loss apparantly.' 'And that makes me fucking lucky,' screamed Ken. The doctor approached a sitting Ken as he began his next words. 'Yes... you should be dead.'
