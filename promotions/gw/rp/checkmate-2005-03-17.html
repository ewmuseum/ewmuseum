Date: 3/17/2005 Waiting in the terminal once again. At least the terminal was private with real furniture, not the stock design that was so uncomfortable. Jake was enjoying lounging on the leather sofa as he waited for the private plane to be readied for takeoff. He had the journal out, reading the final pieces of the first act of what had become a tragedy. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . The preparations began immediately after the body was buried in the hay. Sean set-up his various tools and boxes on the wooden table. He wanted everything to be ready for when he had a plan. Bertrand and Xavier finished cleaning up the mess. Overall this is a heck of a place to meet, but stupid from a strategy standpoint.” Xavier explained. “The path over is fine, but the trails may leave footprints or something else if we’re not careful when we go down. Normally, the dirt hasn’t been touched in a long time.” Sean warned. The men moved across the ground to the paths leading down to the valley. The factory was old with rusting fixtures and concrete with foundation cracks all around. The windows were mostly intact, but a few were broken here and there. Bertrand pointed to the wall, “Graffiti?” “The local high school kids used to come out and tag the wall to show how cool they were. Right?” Xavier asked Bertrand as he approached the window.
