The crimson mask

21 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/21/03 The scene opens inside of a small weight room. The room is dimly lit, and looks dusty and dirty. Ice is seen lying on the floor doing medicine ball sit ups. Ice throws the large tan medicine ball up in the air sits up, goes back down and catches the ball. He continues to repeat that. As he continues his work out his breathing gets deeper, and he begins to grunt and yell. Just to the left of where Ice is doing his work out is Star. In skin tight navy blue tights, and a matching navy blue sports bra she is sitting and doing a chest work out. The athletic tape along the top of the machine says 'PEC DECK'. As Star extends her arms back her breasts almost break through the thin cloth that holds them. As she pushes the machine together she grunts and sweat pours from her forehead, down her face, and leaving a small puddle on the floor between her feet. Ice and Star finish their work outs almost simultaniously. They both walk over to one of the few benches in the small musky room. They sit down and take long drinks from their bottles of water. Both are breathing heavy. 'WHEW! That was great! What did you think of the workout my brother gave us?' 'Not to bad.' Ice puts the cap on his bottle of water and throws it away. Star stands up next to him and grabs her silver gym bag. Together they walk out of the small weight room. They head outside and enter a black room. Star begins to shiver. Ice looks towards the other side of the room to see a ring with three men in the ring. Ice takes in a long deep breath and exhales. His breath fogs up in the cold air. Ice motions for the cameraman to come over to the ring. Slowly the cameraman makes his way over to Ice. Ice slides into the ring, and charges the middle man. The two other men jump on Ice as he begins to maul their third partner. After the three men gaining upper hand Ice fights off his attackers, and hits a double clothesline. The third man, whom Ice started attacking, slowly raises to his feet, and drills Ice with a chair shot to the face. Ice staggers back from the shot, but does not leave his feet. Ice snaps his head and an expression of fear engulfs the man's face. Rapidly he shakes his head from left to right, and dives out of the ring. His two partners meekly slide out of the ring, and all three exit the room. Ice turns and watches them leave the black room, as his face is covered in a crimson mask. Ice looks over the camera, and then at Star sitting next to the ring and smiles. Star winks and smiles back at Ice. Ice then turns back and focuses his attention back towards the camera. Ice's eyes roll into the back of his head, as the blood continues to flow down his face. The scene fades to black.
