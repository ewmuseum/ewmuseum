Never Sleeping: Safe Hands

27 February 2007

Never Sleeping: Safe Hands Reginald Atkins Global Wrestling Promo #3016 Date: 2/27/07 Location: Dallas *Fusion RP* There isn’t any sort of statistical survey on the subject, but Nathan Boswell has always kept close track of the most painful thing that ever happened to him. For a very long time, the record holder was an accident when he was eight years old and he fell from a tree house his dad was in the process of reinforcing. He landed on his shoulder, broke his collarbone, and had to have it rebroken – without anesthetic – to set it properly. This was easily the front-runner for most of his life. Then he started professional wrestling. There were many winners after that. Broken bones, concussions, drops that made the tree house seem like falling off of the front porch – every experience hurt more than the last. But when he was pulled through a panel of glass at Global’s Pay-Per-View event, he was positive that the pain he endured would never be topped. Now, three hours after, he is proved wrong. Nathan Boswell lays on the medical cot on his stomach, his teeth clenched tightly on the end of a pillow. His eyes water and wince every few seconds as the doctor sighs. “I’m sorry, Mr. Boswell, I’m trying to be as gentle as I possibly can,” the doctor says sympathetically. But no matter how gentle the doctor tries to be, having the glass shards plucked out of Nathan’s body is the most painful thing that has ever happened to him, and it’s all because he wanted to get back in the business so badly. He had thought about it for a long time, going over every aspect of it. Where to join, who to join with, and whether to talk to his girlfriend about it. Judging from his current position, he had made the wrong decision on all three counts. As he winces while a considerably large piece of glass is pulled out, he remembers his conversation with Arianna when he told her he had finally decided to start wrestling again. “You’re not saying anything.” It frustrated him when she was silent in an argument. He wanted a rebuttal, he wanted something he could use…but she gave him nothing. “Come on, Arianna,” he tried again, grabbing her shoulders. Turning her around, his speech was halted by the sight of tears flowing out of her unfocused eyes. “What…why are you crying?” “Because I’m fighting with myself. You can go wrestle if you want,” she said, turning away again to hide her tears. “I could never live with myself if I forbid you from doing something you really love.” Sniffling, she felt around for the bed, then sat on it. “But I also won’t be able to live with myself if something terrible happens to you. I just have to finish fighting myself, and I’ll be all right.” “No,” he had said, once again grabbing her shoulder with his right hand and caressing her cheek with his left. “Don’t worry about me, honey. I’ll be fine. I’m not going to get hurt again. I promise.” And that’s why this hurts more than anything that has ever happened to him. Not only because the doctor is opening and re-opening cuts all over his body, not only because there is enough glass inside of him still to make a windshield, but also because he had made a promise he didn’t keep. More than anything else, disappointing the person who means most to him is what makes this hurt. And he fully intends to project that hurt on the person who caused this. ---- But while he’s planning this, that person is planning his next attack. Reginald Atkins never sleeps. It may be a phrase that is slightly cliché and definitely not completely true, but the message is that Reginald doesn’t let up when he has the advantage. He knows where to stick it, and what works once will work again. Which is why it’s a good thing that Nathan is still in the hospital. Reginald looks up to his German bodyguard and smiles, patting his chest. “Now, Lyell, I want to make sure that we are clear on what you’re going to do once you leave this house. Sing it back to me.” Lyell Jett rolls his eyes and looks up, exasperated at having to go through this again. Jett has been given the instructions, and he doesn’t feel like he should have to repeat them back as if he wasn’t a seven foot German machine of destruction (which is what he likes to refer to himself as). “Eins: Nehm den Zimmer heraus.” “One: Exit the room.” Reginald’s pat on the chest turns into a firm backhand. “Ha, ha, you berk. I said once you leave the house. Now, once you get there…” Lyell holds up a hand, stopping Reginald. “Ich weiss, ich weiss. Lass mich gehen, nicht? Ich enttäusche dich nicht.” “I know, I know. Let me go, okay? I won’t disappoint.” Reginald pauses for a moment, then nods. “All right, go on. Just be quick. And remember: don’t say a word. Don’t cough. Don’t laugh. Try not to breathe too loud either, because your breathing sounds German and you’ll give yourself away. Now go.” But Lyell, who had wanted to leave so eagerly seconds before, is now staring down at Reginald, confused. “What?” “Wie atmest du Deutsches?” “How do you breathe German?” “Oh just shut up and get out of here.” Watching his bodyguard go, Atkins sighs and sits down, his chin in his hands. Reginald Atkins never sleeps, and this is a remarkably bad thing for his enemies. ---- Passing the time has always been a thing of issue for Arianna Riley. It isn’t another way to point out her shortcoming of sight: it’s simply a sad and true statement. No television, no movies, although she can hear them it never seems to be enough for her. In addition, she doesn’t feel at liberty to move in her home as she used to, because now she’s scared of everything she can’t see. The only thing she trusts now is Nathan, and he responded by leaving again. She said she would never stop him from wrestling, and this is true. However, when she said that she would be able to overcome her worry for his safety, that was not true. Any match has a chance to be followed by a hospital visit, and any hospital visit has a chance to be fatal. And with nothing to do but pass the time, she waits, and she worries. Until she hears footsteps shuffling into the kitchen. There is no fear as she stands up, no alert. She knows it’s Nathan, she can sense it. As she makes her way into the kitchen, she can feel his presence, feel the space he takes up. “Are you okay?” she asks, but she doesn’t get a response. Instead, all she feels is a muscular arm surround her and pull her close to him. She feels safer as she buries her face in his chest, knowing that any argument they have is immediately resolved when she gets to hold him. “I’m sorry I was worried. I just want to make sure you’re okay.” She still gets no response, but it doesn’t matter, as she’s too wrapped up in their embrace to notice. He grabs her, forcefully, and brings her to the bed. She doesn’t resist until she feels the bristle of a mustache against her forehead. “Wha?” she starts, but is stopped by a hand covering her mouth as she is thrown onto the bed. Her shirt is ripped, her lip is cut, but still she fights, still she resists. She thrashes, kicks, punches, grabs, anything she can think of to stop the stranger who posed as her boyfriend. Finally she strikes gold as she hears a moan and feels her foot hit something hard. Rolling out of bed, she to the kitchen and bursts out of the open door, hoping her direction isn’t off. If she is running in the wrong direction by five degrees, she won’t reach her destination. As she has most of her life, she prays to whatever patron saint helps the disabled, and keeps running. “Woah, love, woah,” she hears, and feels two strong arms grab her. She recognizes the accent and, even though it makes her stomach turn still, she sighs in relief and accepts his embrace. “It’s okay, Arianna. You’re in safe hands now.” ---- As Reginald holds the blind girl in his arms, he looks over her shoulder at the pursuing Lyell Jett, a limp in his step and his shirt torn. Shaking his head at his bodyguard, he goes back to consoling Arianna. Getting out of this one required a lot of thinking. Cover-ups, stories, alibis… Reginald Atkins never sleeps.
