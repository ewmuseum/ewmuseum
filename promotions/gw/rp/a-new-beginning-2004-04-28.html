A New Beginning

28 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28APR04 All you can see is darkness. Everything in sight, if you call it sight, is black. The absence of color. You squint your eyes, hoping to see something. Praying that if you’re eyes would only adjust, you’d see something. You hope. You pray. Hope and pray for the best. Expect the worst. Slowly, your eyes begin to adjust. They adjust to this monotone setting. This setting of black and white. Its nothing spectacular. Its nothing special. You’re nothing special. You blink your eyes, you shake your head side to side. You shake the cobwebs out. Slowly, you can make out shadows. A small something to the left, sitting on the bottom of’whatever. To the right, you make out another shadow. One of a human, sitting in a rocking chair. Slower and slower, you’re eyes adjust to see more and more. You’re mind opens up to see what the world has to offer. You leave you’re mind blank’not knowing what to expect. You hope. You pray. Hope and pray for the best. Expect the worst. “You said he was gone.” GONE FOREVER “You said he was gone.” A boy’s voice. A little louder this time. A symbol of curiosity. A symbol of innocence. Do you remember when you were a child? So lost and lonely. He asks. He demands. The other shadow sits and listens. Not one noise is heard from it. A shadow. Do you remember following your shadow? Wondering why it kept following you. You ask. You demand. And it just sat there listening to you. It made you feel worthless. Shun your head. You’re shallow. You hope. You pray. Hope and pray for the best. Expect the worst. “I thought you said it couldn’t happen.” I LIED “I thought you said it was impossible.” He begs for an answer. He pleads. He asks. He demands. “Then why has he come back?” REVENGE “What does he want?” The shadow turns its head. Whoring to the boy’s questions. Hoping to stop his whining. My ears can’t take it anymore. It feels like my brain is bleeding. Innocence has left me ashamed. CAN’T YOU SEE WHAT YOU HAVE DONE “You said that?” The shadow puts its finger up to its mouth. I try to hush the fool. There is only so much you can take. YOU DON’T KNOW WHAT I’VE BEEN THROUGH The boy bows his head. Innocence feels guilty. So immature. Makes me want to vomit. “Christ turned bread into enough fish to feed a village. Christ parted the Red Sea. Christ gave the power of sight to a blind man.” The boy raises his head. You can not see his facial expression in the dark. Everything is black. The absence of color. “But you said?” He interrupts the child. So foolish. So immature. I put an end to his whining. “Christ walked on water. Christ quieted a restless storm.” “But you said?” I LIED The boys keeps whining. Damn his whining and damn him. Get it through your head, child. Stop your whining. Stop your crying. So foolish of you. So immature of you. “It can’t happen. It isn’t possible.” “Then how is it” How did it happen? How?” He pleads for knowledge. He begs for wisdom. He asks. He demands. Angered, I am, I don’t even know the answer. I’m so foolish. “Christ sacrificed himself for the sake of the world. Christ rose Lazarus from the dead. Christ died and rose from the grave declaring everlasting life.” “But. But?” “Godammit, if Christ can heal the sick’why can’t he?” He stutters. Fumbling around with his words. Saliva flies from his mouth. Even the wise make mistakes. You try to make him look stupid. You chuckle and laugh. GIVE ME A FUCKING BREAK “If Christ can walk on water’why can’t he?” The boy shakes his head side to side. He can’t process this much information into his head at one time. Stupid little boy. So innocent and foolish. “I don’t underst?” “Of course you don’t understand. I’ve lived much longer then you and I don’t even understand.” The boy takes a couple deep breaths. “If Christ sacrificed himself’why can’t he?” “He’s dead.” The old man jumps off the chair and grabs the boy by the chin and lifts it. Eye to eye, wise to immature. Smart to foolish. “If Christ can rise from the grave and declare everlasting life’why can’t he” Why can’t’Sage Sabbot? You listen to me and you listen well, boy.” He clinches the boys chin. The boy cries frantically. He whines. So foolish and so immature. “Things are about to change around here. He’s back. Godammit, Sage Sabbot is back.” He throws the boys face towards the ground and falls toward his back, holding his head. The boy quickly jumps to his feet. “What can we do?” Thinking he has a solution. Thinking he can solve the problem. So foolish. So immature. “I want you to hope.” HOPE “I want you to pray.” PRAY “I want you to hope and pray for the best. But expect the worst, boy. There is nothing we can do.” EXPECT THE WORST A loud screech fades everything in sight. All you see now are the veins inside your eyelids. You can see the blood between each layer of skin, covering your eye. You open them. Taking a deep breath, you hope and you pray that this was just a dream. But hope and you pray for the best. But you expect the worst. How shallow minded you are. You knew the answer all along. Prepare is what you should do. SAGE SABBOT IS BACK?
