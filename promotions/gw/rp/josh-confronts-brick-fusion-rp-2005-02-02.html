Josh confronts Brick (Fusion RP)

2 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/2/05 Brick was not off to a good start in 2006. First at Reload, the year's first pay-per-view event, he lost to Jake Hix in the main event. Right afterwards, Hix' brothers, the Texas Wrecking Crew, rushed down to the ring and started beating on him while he was still down. Next came the night of the first edition of Fusion. Brick had gone down to the ring, looking to call the Hix brothers out. This time, he would be ready for a fight. This time, he had an equalizer in a steel pipe. However, instead of coming out to face him, the new World Champion sent him a rather unkind message: flipping him the double bird on the Globaltron. This incensed Brick even further, causing him to burst into Brandi Sights' office, demanding a match either with Hix or the Wrecking Crew, even trying to get himself placed as Jesse Briggs' tag team partner. Before Brandi could refuse, Josh burst into the office as well, demanding a match with Mike Biddle in retaliation for what happened after their match at Reload. Just as an argument between the two men was about to start, Brandi nipped it in the bus, booking the two of them in a match so that they could blow off steam. Reluctantly, they accepted the match. The match went back and forth. At one point, Brick was about to hit the Bricklayer when he saw something or someone that caused him great distraction. Enough distraction, in fact, for Now Brick was headed back to his locker room to clean up, when... 'Brick!' Brick stopped and turned around to see Josh running towards him. He then resumed walking towards his room. 'I need to talk to you, man.' 'Congratulations on winning the match,' Brick replied, distantly. 'Yeah, that's kinda what I wanna talk to you about,' Josh replied, stepping in front of Brick. 'What happened out there, man?' 'What are you talking about?' 'Look, you saw something out in the audience.' 'Oh, that, um...some chick up in the cheap seats flashed me.' 'Dude, I saw your face. It did not look like the face of somebody who just got flashed. You looked like you saw a ghost.' 'Yeah, well, it was a pretty nasty lookin' chick, looked like Rosie O'Donnell.' 'Damnit, Brick, will you just be straight with me?!' 'Look, Josh, why do you give a damn what distracted me, huh? You won the match! You should be happy!' 'I won that match because you were distracted! It wasn't fair to you and it wasn't fair to me! At least before you got distracted, it was 50-50 that one of us would have beat the other!' 'Well, did you forget neither one of us wanted this match?! You wanted Biddle an' I wanted Hix!' 'That's not the point!' 'LOOK!!!' Brick roared before calming down some. 'Look, the match is over. Ain't nothin' we can do about it.' Just as Brick walked away... 'Was it Salaok?' Brick stopped dead in his tracks. 'It was, wasn't it?' 'Josh? Leave it alone.' 'Look, you think I haven't heard the rumors about something big going down between you two?' 'Like I told Brandi, Aaron an' a buncha other cats who kept pressin' me, drop it!' Brick kept walking on, leaving Josh alone. 'Damn,' Josh muttered.
