The Revenge of the Kinslayer(Introductory RP2)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Long ago... Location: Rome Time: 3:00pm He finally got to where all paths led him. He was in the most beautiful and impressive city in the world. Nothing could match the feeling of walking into Rome, the center of the world, and looking at the faces, looking at the architecture, just inhaling the air was a thrill. After so much time wandering around in the country and not having a home, this was a pleasant surprise. He started walking down the street; it was packed, full of life. He saw every kind of shop you could imagine, he saw carts with puppets, and he finally arrived at the first thing he wanted to see, the Coliseum. The people that passed him by thought he must have escaped, because he was big, strong and when you looked into his eyes, you felt fear. The most awful fear you could think of. Like when you feel something is dead, but it's alive. To the Romans he was a walking dead man, no feelings, no fears, and only hate as his ally. Manderb at his side only made it worse. A huge black warhorse accompanying a man who looked like he had fought in his fair share of wars. Dagoth walked towards the Coliseum and saw the gladiators behind a cell. He laughed audibly. He was staring the people he had wanted to be through his whole childhood. He daydreamed of being the best damn gladiator there was, and now their sight was laughable. They were wimps compared to Dagoth. He looked at his hands, his bloody hands after so much death had come from them, and he grinned once again. They were pathetic compared to him, they might have to fight for their lives every single day, but they hadn't almost single handedly destroyed a race. He still had nightmares even though he wouldn't accept it. He remembered the blood on his sword; the pain on his hip after a sword almost went through him and completely destroyed him. He has seen death face to face, and he laughed at it, with such madness that death had nothing to do but flee. He went past the gladiators and into a small alley; he walked through it until he found the house that he was looking for. The house of Cassius Cosades Meridius, member of the Roman senate and the man who conspired with Attila the Hun. Dagoth goes over to the door and knocks three times, waits a while and knocks two times more. This will make Cassius know that a Hun is there. Moments later the door opens, a sword is brought up and placed on Dagoth's neck. Dagoth doesn't even flinch, and another man walks in front of him, he is wearing a white toga with part purple in color. His black hair is not long, but not shirt either and it is down like a mushroom. He raises his arm and in his finger is a huge ring with a ruby. When he raised his hand, the sword immidiately went down. 'I must have known. The only Hun still walking the face of the earth.' Cassius grins widely. He motions for Dagoth to get inside. Dagoth agrees, but before, he ties Manderb to a piece of metal on the outside. 'No worries, it will be fast.' Dagoth's eyes were on fire now. Determination and hate filled him, but he must hold it for a short time. He goes inside and that same bodyguard asks for his weapons. Dagoth takes out his beautiful sword and gives it to the bodyguard, who had the build to be a gladiator too, probably was. He next gives him his bow and arrows, and a short sword that was sheathed on his right leg. The bodyguard put them all in a table far from where his master was sitting. Dagoth went over to Cassius and sat down. 'I should have known you would come to me,' Cassius started, his grin always wide. 'I'm surprised none of those lower in the gladiator food chain tried to get you to fight for them. I'm sure you would make a great gladiator. Might come close to my servant there. He is undefeated in fifty matches, and has 50 kills on his record,' Cassius said. 'Those gladiators are nothing compared to me Cosades, and that is not why I'm here,' Dagoth said almost rising from his chair. 'What are you here for then my friend?' Cassius asked. 'Revenge,' Dagoth told him standing up. 'Come on Dagoth, who do you want to kill now? You have spilled enough blood already, and if you kill me, how are you going to survive in Rome? You need someone to maintain you, you need someone that knows the city. This is not some Hun city where nothing happens. This is the center of the world; this is Rome! We rule the world.' Cassius got up and went towards Dagoth. He put a hand on his shoulder and tried to calm him down. Dagoth knew that he was right, he would never blend in, but the memories were more important than now. 'I will kill you Cosades, you are nothing but a traitor.' Dagoth went over to the gladiator and punched him in the face. The gladiator staggered back and drew his sword, but before he could use it, a kick to the stomach and a punch straight to the temple. Dagoth got the gladiators neck and snapped it like a twig. He now looked right at Cassius with the sword now in his hand. Cassius just smiled. 'How's the family?' *SNAP* Everything went blank. When Dagoth opened his eyes, and he could see what had happened, he sat down. He was having these blackouts when he got really angry, and he was hurting people, not just killing people, hurting them. When he snapped back to reality he saw what he had done. Cassius, or what was left of him, was hanging from a rope. All his limbs cut off and his tongue out through the gash on his throat. He had completely dismantled Cassius Cosades for mentioning his family. Cosades was going to die, but this was sick, and it sickened Dagoth. What would his wife and daughter think? Yes, everything now was about his late wife and daughter. His only family... well, he had considered Attila family, but he betrayed him. He had been having this nightmares every time he killed someone. Horrible nightmares, even for him. It was like a dagger being stuck into his stomach every single time, and he knew it was a message, he knew he had to stop killing. He just couldn't, he couldn't stop. His family was trying to tell him that they didn't need revenge, that they didn't need bloodshed, but deep down Dagoth knew that he needed it, it was like a thirst he could not quench. He knew that on that very night he would not sleep, not after that atrocity. He was going to try, but to no avail.............. He was walking through the beautiful fields; the smell of the flowers was invigorating. He walked towards the hut with smoke coming out of the roof. In his mind he could hear the voice of his daughter playing outside while his wife waited for him to bring home the animal that they would be eating that night. He carried a deer on his back; he never failed to bring his family food, never. He was so close to the house, but he could now see the hut closely, and it was not the fire inside, but the fire on the hut. He dropped the deer and ran towards his home. He went inside and grabbed his wife. When he grabbed her, her head fell off. Now tears where going down Dagoth's face. He fell down and just sat there while the flames consumed everything in sight. His eyes red from a mixture of utter sadness and the smoke. He went outside and left his wife inside, just then he saw the headless body of his daughter on the back of the hut. He crumbled. His legs just completely gave off. He sat there, his sword in one hand, and a fire burning in the background. He grabbed his sword and put it to his throat. He was going to end his life, what more could he do? Only one thing, get revenge. Avenge those he loved and destroy those who had done this to him. That meant he had to destroy Attila, the Huns, and his Roman collaborators.......................... He woke up with a start. He was drenched in sweat, and the bed of the late Cassius Cosades was completely soaked. Out of those three, two were done. Now he had to kill one more... the Caesar himself...
