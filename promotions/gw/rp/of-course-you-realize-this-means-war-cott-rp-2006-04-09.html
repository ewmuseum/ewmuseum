Of course you realize this means war (COTT RP)

9 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/9/06 Brick slung his World title belt over his shoulder as he turned to exit his locker room. His friend and former tag team partner Top Dawg was debuting his new talk show 'The Dawg House' that night and Brick was to be the first guest, having beaten Jake Hix for the World title at High Octane. Just as he grabbed the knob and turned it, he realized that the door wouldn't open. 'What the--?' Brick tried to open the door again, repeatedly turning the knob, pushing against the door. Finally, he ran into the door, trying to bust it down. All it got him was a sore shoulder. 'Ah, damn,' he groaned, rubbing his shoulder. 'Brick?' a voice called from the other side of the door. 'Yeah!' 'Brick, there's a forklift blocking your door! We're trying to move it but there's no key!' Brick paced around his locker room as he heard the grunts and the groans and the yells of staff working to move the forklift blocking his door. He knew the whole thing smelled of a set-up. 'Get another forklift!' 'Hey, it's starting to budge.' Brick called to the men on the outside, 'Let me know when I can try to open the door.' 'Okay!' Some more grunts and groans. 'Now, Brick!' Brick slammed into the door and it swung open under the force. 'Hey, Brick, you okay?' 'Yeah, I'm fine. How about you?' 'Each of us might have a hernia, but other than that...' Suddenly, a stagehand ran up to Brick. 'Brick! It's Jake Hix! He just went down to the ring and attacked Top Dawg.' 'What?' 'Yeah, and I saw his brothers headed for the ring!' Immediately, Brick looked around and picked up a steel chair, rushing towards the arena. He could only hope it wasn't too late. When he got down to the Octagon, The Hix brothers were putting the boots to his fallen friend. Just as he slid in, they cleared the ring. Brick was close enough to nearly hit Pitt with the steel chair. As he angrily watched the Hix brothers backpedal up the ramp with smirks on their faces, Brick knelt down beside the unconscious and beaten Top Dawg. Frantically, he called for the paramedics. ---- Brick sat in the hospital waiting room, staring ahead. He rocked back and forth in his seat, anxiously awaiting news on Top Dawg. Suddenly. 'Hey, aren't you Brick?! Brick looked up to see a lanky orderly. Man, I'd really appreciate it if I could get your autograph. My friends are never gonna believe 'Damn, man, do you mind?!' Brick roared, rising up from his seat to loom over the now-frightened fan. 'I'm waitin' to hear on a friend who got hurt! I don't need--' When he saw the look of fear on the young man's face, Brick then stopped and took a deep breath before continuing. 'Look, I'm sorry, man. I'm just on edge. I'll sign your autograph.' As he took the pen and paper, a nurse walked up to him. 'Sir, I'm afraid I'm going to have to ask you to keep your voice down, so as not to disturb the other patients.' 'I'm sorry. Uh, who do I make this out to?' 'To Shawn.' Brick quickly wrote 'To Shawn. From the CEO of Ruckus Inc, Brick.' And handed it to the orderly. 'Thanks.' Just as the orderly began to walk away with the autograph, Brick heard a familiar pair of high heels clicking. He'd heard them a number of times backstage at the Global Arena. He turned to see Brandi Sights coming towards him. 'I figured you'd still be here. I came as soon as Fusion ended. Any word on Top Dawg?' 'Nothing.' 'I'm sorry, Brick. I should have known something like this would happen.' 'We both should have. Hix is a dead man, Brandi.' 'Brick...' 'No, he just declared war. Well, if he wants a war, I'll give him one.' Just then, the doctor walked up to Brick and Brandi. 'Doctor, how's Dawg doing?' 'Are you the patient's next of kin?' 'For all intents and purposes, yeah.' 'What exactly does that mean?' 'His mother and sisters were killed in a gas explosion a few years back. He doesn't have any other relatives, at least as far as I know. Look, how is he?' 'He suffered a mild concussion, in addition to some cracked ribs, bruises, abrasions and possibly a ruptured spleen. We'll need to keep him overnight for observation.' 'Can I see him?' 'He's unconscious at the moment. In the morning would probably be the best time for a visit.' 'OK. Thank you, Doctor.' 'Hix is gonna pay for this,' Brick growled, his fists clenched. 'Him and his punk ass little brothers.' 'Brick--' 'Dawg is like my little brother, Brandi! Hix didn't just mess with one o' my friends, he messed with family! ' Brick then leaned a little closer towards Brandi. 'And bad things happen to people who mess with my family. Just ask Salaok.' Hearing that sent a cold shiver down Brandi's spine. She'd heard rumors of something big happening between Brick and Salaok. Whenever she brought up the subject, Brick was reluctant to discuss it. In fact, he was rather reticent. Of course, Brick could have just been talking about Aaron, but then again, she knew Brick was capable of anything. 'Brick, I've come to a decision. When you defend your title at Clash of the Titans, it's going to be in a hardcore match. That means you can do anything to him, short of killing him and it's all perfectly legal.' Brick then began to smile a bit. '*Short* of killing him, Brick.' Then a more deadpan expression appeared. 'I mean it, Brick. You've got to keep a cooler head. This is just what Hix wants. He wants you angry. He wants you sloppy. You can't give him that satisfaction or you might as well just hand him the title right now.' 'OK, you're right,' said Brick, 'but I meant what I said. Whether I keep the title at Clash of the Titans or Hix takes it back, this is war.' 'OK, here's some good news. The Elite beat SR:E. Morgan turned on Biddle and the Wrecking Crew.' 'Heh, serves those bastards right.' 'Knew that'd make you happy. Look, it's late. You should go back to your hotel room and get some rest.' 'Maybe you're right,' Brick sighed. Then he added with a smirk, 'Care to join me?' 'Don't press your luck,' Brandi replied with a smirk of her own and then walked away. 'It was worth a shot,' Brick shrugged.
