'Retirement looms for some, victory exists for the 'Captain'

Date: Today 'Retirement looms for some, victory exists for the 'Captain' It was a line that I wanted to hear myself say... once, twice, perhaps even three times if I felt cocky enough. It was a declaration of confidence, an exclamation of intent and a damning message to everyone who thought they were going to be in for an easy ride when I bumped into them at some point during this contest. I wanted to feel like I could take on the world... but my sense of reality prevented me from doing so. Meh, my entire career history was dragging me down in the same way also. I guess you could call this entire situation something of a double negative. It's just a shame that I was still standing around like a lemon, waiting for something positive to come along. I thought this opportunity was the positive that I need... although the only positive I feel about this contest see's me getting my ass handed to me. After discussing things with my sister, I decided that I was going to make one attempt at getting this whole fear of the elimination bout out of my mind. I figured that if I could erase this problem with a little hypnotherapy, then perhaps my chances of winning this thing on pay-per-view will increase ten-fold and the people will start to believe that 'Captain Retirement' is worth more than just a funny name a few dodgy promo's. Morning had come and passed on the Friday of the week and I'd been scheduled for an appointment at around 2:30pm that afternoon. My sister and I left the house at around 1:55pm to make sure we'd be there on time. Fortunately, we arrived a little earlier than anticipated... but that didn't really matter to be honest as far as I was concerned. Perhaps there would be the off chance that I could get seen earlier? Luck appeared to be on my side as I waltzed through the main entrance, into the reception area. For the first time in a while, I was recognized immediatly and sent through to acquire treatment of some sort. My sister took a place in the waiting area and waved as I followed a member of staff through and about a few thousand corridors. Eventually, after crossing what could have been half the planet, I came across a small, yet interesting office. The plaque said it all, 'Dr Steven Miles; Hypo-Dude' '... Please tell me this isn't the person that I'm supposed to be seeing. Of all the hypnotherapy specialists that this place could possibly have, why is that I'm the one that's stuck with the goofest title?' I said, not too loudly because I didn't want to cause any offense to the individual who was SUPPOSED to be the cure for both my nerves and my denial of my own abilities. Sucking up every little ounce of air, I pressed forth and walked through the office door, taking a step out of the bland, human world... and into the colourful world of hypnotherapy. 'WHY HELLO!! HOW ARE YOU?!' shouted a very excited man from across the otherside of the room, actually catching my by surprise. I know, that was kinda stupid when I take into consideration the fact that I should have expected something as stupid being thrown in my general direction. 'Hi...' I responded gingerly, trying not to sound too disappointed with the way that things had turned out. Unfortunately, for a lot of the world, I was one of those guys who seemed to take things at their face value. This guy looked like a freak, so of course... it was only fitting that I took him as a freak. 'WELL WELL WELL, AREN'T YOU SOMETHING OF AN AWESOME LITTLE WRESTLER!! MY SON TALKS ABOUT YOU SOOO MUCH!' he continued, speaking in his abnormally loud voice. I was tempted to say 'STFU' in it's entirity... but I'd rather save the foul language until after this nightmare had reached it's exciting [for you] conclusion. '... I'm very flattered to hear that your son happens to talk about me quite a bit. It's always nice to have fans that are quite content with the work I do in and outside the ring.' I said, sounding a little more content. It was all an act but I don't think this idiot would have known an act if it hit him right between the eyes. '... HMM, IT SAYS HERE ON MY CHART THAT LIKE, YOU'RE SUPPOSED TO BE HERE FOR SOME KIND OF TREATMENT. YOU KNOW, LIKE SOME KIND OF HYPNOSTUFF WHERE I PUT YOU TO SLEEP AND TELL YOU THE MOST STUPID OF THINGS. FOR EXAMPLE, YOU HAVE A COW ON YOUR HEAD!' he ranted, losing even more sense with each second. I tried not to put my head in my hands but I really couldn't help it. The guy looked, acted and even smelt like somebody who got off the wrong train. 'You know, I'm really not sure I was directed to the right office. Perhaps I'm supposed to be seeing another doctor in this facility?' I said, nervously smiling before getting out of my chair, wasting no time at all. The doctor glanced at me and stared, his expression changing from a look of psychotic humour to one of absolute seriousness. Looks like I struck something of a nerve X__x; '... Mr Sherwin, I know that for the first five minutes of this session I did nothing but act like a clown but you have to realize, that was just my way of trying to get the patient to loosen up and become more open to the idea of hypnotherapy.' he said, sitting in his large, leather chair in a very comfortable manner. I slumped back down in my own seat, hoping that this serious side to the doctor was actually the side that would be progressing with my treatment and the sort. 'Doc, I need to know straight off the bat... do you honestly think you're going to be able to help me get through these VERY annoying pre-bout nerves?' I asked, wanting to know the answer to my plight before I stepped up to the plate and allowed anything to happen. 'Mr Sherwin, you have to realize that this treatment isn't just about me making you believe in yourself... because if treatment was as good as that, then I wouldn't be here in this dump right now. Instead, you have to believe in yourself also. Think of me as the charger that plugs into the wall and you're the battery that needs to be recharged, that needs to regain meaning and direction.' he said, stopping for a few moments for me to absorb everything before continuing, '... In this situation, I'm going to the loyal fan that pushes you to the limit. I'm going to be the individual that pushes your morale and your efforts to levels that you never thought were possible. In return though, you're going to have to accept those and you're going to have to believe that YOU can make this become a reality. I know your situation and I can understand it.' Sitting in the chair for a few moments, the thought process of what was going to be done finally kicked in. I was going to have to decide here and now, as to whether this treatment was going to be something that I attempted to go ahead with. Did I want to walk into this match with semi-truths about my own skill and ability... or did I want to walk into that contest without the aid of this man, without the blocks my nervous nature. I didn't know what to do... I didn't know which path was truely going to aid my cause more. '... Should I accept the help and walk into this match with blocks on all my negative ports... or, should I decline the help and act like some kind of nervous wreck from the word 'Go'?' I asked myself aloud, the doctor looking at me intently, trying to decide when I'd come out of this thought train and give him an educated answer as to whether I felt I needed to move ahead with the treatment or not. 'Retirement will loom for some... victory will only exist for the 'Captain'...' I began to chant, repeating it over and over as though it were the 'Phrase of the day' or something. The doctor stared at me a little harder, a brief look of disbelief covering his face. I don't think he really understood what I was doing at the time. Well, either that or he just didn't like the idea of a chant being everything you need to hold the fort against all negative emotion. '... Mr Sherwin?' 'Yes, Doctor?' '... Do you think you're ready to go ahead with this then?' The room fell silent as the question was thrown at me for the last time. As I looked at the doctor and continued to try and weigh both the benefits and disadvantages of this move, the scene slowly faded out into the confinement of darkness. What had started out as some kind of whack fest had only slowed down into a mode which I could describe. Did I want to deny myself human emotion... or did I want to suffer the very worst that my heart and brain had to offer? : END :
