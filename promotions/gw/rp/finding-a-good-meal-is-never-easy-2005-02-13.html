The prick shot me too ...” Corvus rubbed his chest where the bullet had entered; it tore through him and went right out the back of him. He started to feel a little strange; maybe it was because of the bullet wound possibly. The Dark Prince made the rest of the call short and sweet. “Get here before sunrise, that’s an order! I’ll call you if I need anything more. I’m going to keep things quiet for a while again. You know where to find the money, talk to you again ...” He shut the phone and put it back into his pocket. It was getting closer to morning so his people would be here to take care of things shortly. Normally they didn’t interact much or even probably knew what the other looked like. The Leviathan made his way back through to the living room, contemplating taking a quick swim before he turned in for the night. For a moment he became disoriented and began to hear something. Why did you do this? He put his hand against his forehead; looked around to see if anyone was there. There could be, he was the only one there that was moving. The girl had died, he knew for sure. He would have been able to hear her heart beat. Pausing for a few more moments, he allowed himself to try and focus again but this time he heard the voice again; it had familiarity to it. Daddy ... how could you? Corvus looked around sharply but did not see anything. He thought he was losing his mind as he started to stumble towards the couch. He raised his head slowly, his eyes widening as he looked at the dead body of the girl he had taken. She had taken on the appearance of his long deceased daughter, Angela. It could not be, he thought over and over in his head trying to think of some logical reasoning. The eyes opened and stared daggers into his, freezing him where he stood. There were not many things that could stop the demon in his tracks but this held some power over him. A burning sensation surfaces in the area where he had been shot. There was nothing he could do to stop the voices. HOW COULD YOU LET ME DIE! Corvus did everything he could to pass by the body and get into his bedroom which was only several feet away from the main living room. He stepped through the threshold and closed the door behind him. His feet had become rubbery; he put his hands over his ears to stop the voices but they just wouldn’t go away. He managed to find the corner of the room and placed his back against it, sliding down to the floor where he pulled his legs inward and clung to himself. All through the night he rocked and whispered to himself, “This isn’t real, it is just your imagination. Mortal death is mortal death ... there is nothing after mortal ... death.”
