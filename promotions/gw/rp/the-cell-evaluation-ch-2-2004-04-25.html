The Cell: Evaluation (Ch.2)

25 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 25th, 2004 Prologue: When he entered the prison of Dallas Texas his wondering mind said one thing, ‘When the hell do I get out of here?’ Yet now, with the re-acquaintance of his old friend Richard Lorenzo, and the Elite wanting Jesse to follow their flock, that wondering thought soon was forgotten, and Jesse was now comfortable within the concrete walls. It’d been too long since he’d last been in prison to remember the difference but Jesse was sure it wasn’t this good. Being with the elite was like being the government, nobody could take you down without you having enough backups to kick the world’s ass ten times and back. He was on top of the world and the only thing that could take him down… was himself. How hard that would be, soon would shock Jesse in ways he couldn’t express but with fear. Story Continues. Their Jesse stood, surrounded by the members of the Elite. The leader Curt a.k.a. C-Wolf, then the guy Jesse knew well Richard Lorenzo or as he preferred, Rick. The big Africans name was Dante, the greasy rat like guys were known as Charles and Fred, and the final man, the next generation of the leader Curt, Markus his nephew. These six men knew the prison like the back of their hand, and if it weren’t for Lorenzo, Jesse would be in fights with these men rather than a card game. The seven sat around the top of the concrete steps as Jesse and Curt talked. It was unusual to see the leader talking to someone for he’d always been busy, but under the circumstances he’d left Markus in control of his duties as he talked to the new blood. ’I heard you wrestle Jesse, that true?’ muttered Curt who’s forty-eight year old face showed to be aging by the calendar. ’Yes… I’m a wrestler,’ Jesse responded. ’I need to have someone who can deliver packages for me… You up for it?’ Curt whispered, ’call it a favor for me taking you in. What’d Ya’ say?’ Jesse sat back for a moment. He knew whatever he got into would probably be a risk for him to do without getting caught. Yet at the same moment being in the elite proved to have many perks worth those risks. Thinking about it he nodded his head in a ‘yes’ manor to keep it hush between him and Curt. Smirking Curt patted Jesse on the shoulder. It was possibly the stupidest thing Jesse ever did but it was well worth it if he wanted to be a strong and respected name in the prison. So why not he thought, worst thing that could happen is he ends back up in prison, where he was already. The group continued on with their activities until the buzzard sounded again for them to return to their cells. Thoughts of the Masked One Sitting in his cell the lights began to go out one by one on his level. He knew it’d soon be dark and so climbed onto the bottom bunk of his bed where he laid to sleep. Yet the lights that were supposed to go out only dimmed and left a small glow of white light exist that let him see the room and all of it’s surroundings. This was his life for the next three months, no longer could he deny that. So with every day that may pass Jesse had to accept the truth and make his life easier within the indissoluble walls of this prison he would call home. Laying back his eyes would stray to stay awake, yet he couldn’t fall asleep even if his eyes shut. Something about the cell made him shift himself continuously on the cotton bunk bed. Thinking to himself he knew this wasn’t the true life of a prisoner. He’d taken a step into the heavens of gold digging prisoners yesterday when he joined the elite, letting him have access to everything he wanted. Yet this wasn’t the way it was. Prison was hard on a lot of people. Not only the inmates but also the family of the inmate, and being a child was the hardest part of it all. Jesse’s daughter Samantha barely saw Jesse as it was, and now him being in prison made it impossible to unless she was brought to Dallas to see Jesse via Julius, his cousin. Life in prison wasn’t laid back, you spent near 18 hours of the day inside a cell, unless you worked, or when you ate. In Jesse’s case he had money when he came in, but soon was broke. When inmates worked they made a good five to twenty cents a day if that. Sometimes they’d work for free if their work was considered unacceptable. This wasn’t unordinary either, for many prisons were like this. However Dallas’ wasn’t all the same as a standard prison. It allowed the inmates to have a break so they could be out when the guards got a break. The inmates made fifteen cents minimum and most of the supplies was cheap but still costly. Meals consisted of white bread, some sort of meat, and a cup of water. Showers were taken once a week on Thursday. Onto being a guard as a guard was one of the main reasons a prison kept balance. Being a guard was hard work, you couldn’t ever be friends with an inmate or your peer guards would pick on you and possibly drive you out of your job. So why did they do it? The money? Definitely not for being a guard paid squat and was most likely the least favorable job. With the lack of guards in prison’s in the early fifties it was questioned whether prisons should have them, yet the departure of a guard could leave a prison in chaos, so the government and states chose to keep them and pay. It wasn’t a great job but it kept you busy. For people who had nothing better to do than serve some time helping out their government or those who were police officers rejected from their force, the job as a guard would be their next step in life. To be frank, prison life sucked, and being a staff member in prison sucked even more. Relaxing on the thin cotton bed that had been so rough the night before, Jesse rolled over and closed his eyes before finally falling asleep. Corruption Begins After a few hours of working Jesse was about to turn in and go up to his cell when he ran into one of the Thugs. The man was a junkie and his face showed it clearly. His eyes bugged out, while he sported a blue bandana with the knot near his left ear. He wanted to talk to Jesse, for what reason, Jesse knew nothing about, but Jesse decided to speak to him. ’I saw you with the elite yesterday and was wondering how Dante was doing?’ asked the thug without introducing himself. ’He’s fine. Who are you?’ Jesse stated with a tone so that the man would answer respectfully. ’I’m his brother, Daniel. I just wanted to know since I barely get to talk to him. Being in different groups you know, it makes a big difference of who I can and cannot see, and same for him,’ retorted Daniel with no ignorance yet kind as if he wasn’t really anywhere near what you’d expect out of a thug. ’Anything else you want to know?’ Jesse questioned. ’Yeah… you want me to hook you up?’ whispered Daniel making sure nobody could hear him. Jesse didn’t answer quickly for he had to think. Knowing drugs were not the way to go he thought about it, money could grow quickly from drugs, and so if he was to get hooked with the man his ideas of being powerful within the prison would only be steps away. Nodding his head, Jesse signaled a ‘yes’ and Daniel handed him a small piece of paper with instructions on where to meet him. Smirking Daniel left to his cell as so did Jesse. The Meeting It was seven o’clock Thursday night, the same night Jesse would meet the thug, and the same night they took showers. Entering the shower room with a towel around his waist the abnormal sized Jesse Jamester walked past many guys as he carried his shampoo and conditioner in hand. Going back to the near end corner of the shower he began to bathe, and after about thirty minutes the showers were near empty when Daniel appeared. He had a bottle of shampoo and conditioner too, yet didn’t use them as he stepped up beside Jesse and used the shower next to him. Neither talked as they both showered but as Jesse finished he wrapped the towel around his waist and picked up the bottle of conditioner and shampoo in which Daniel brought. Jesse left the shower room and dressed as he went back to his cell. When he laid down he opened up the bottle of conditioner to find 5 needles crammed inside a plastic baggie. Then opening the bottle of shampoo he found heroin. It’d been a long time since Jesse had seen these drugs, and not long enough for him to forget what they’d done to him. Closing the lids he laid down, it was surging through his brain of what the drugs may do, and it’d been a long time since he’d tried them. Yet he didn’t want to do it, and tried to think of other things to fall asleep. Rolling over multiple times he finally couldn’t take it any more and grabbed the bottles. Taking a needle from one he loaded it up with some heroin and then put his arm down on his knee letting his elbow relax as he began to push the needle into his vein. But the pressure of injecting the heroin was to great for Jesse and he sat their bewildered at what he was doing.
