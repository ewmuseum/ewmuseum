The Journey: Part 12/The Connection’s POV

23 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-23-05 Early Morning Greetings lucky ones and welcome to the show that never ends(if it did you know that damn well you’d miss it anyways) and welcome to the return of one Zack Johnson! Ok ok so Zack hasn’t been “missing” but its actually been a while since we have seen Zack around and I know there are people that have been wondering where he has been right? Well Zack has been away for a little while visiting his mother in San Francisco, California and for all the fans out there wondering don’t worry Ms. Johnson is doing much better since she was released from the hospital before “Showdown in Tokyo”. So on this day we find Joshua Curtis and Zack Johnson out and about in New York City in Zack's 2003 Toyota Tundra which came equipped with a CD player among other things that Zack felt were essential so that he could mellow out before a match day and they got none bigger than that against the elite team of Ruckus Inc.! “So darling” Zack began as he was maneuvering the vehicle through traffic as skillfully as ever “what do you think about what the bookers are doing to us at Evolution this week?” Zack asked which caused Joshua to sigh and lean his head back in the seat before answering his boyfriend. “Well hun” Joshua began “I firmly believe that the absolute worst thing that could happen to us is that we end up losing and thus lose our undefeated streak but at the same time I think that if we win then the bookers of GW will have almost no choice but to put us in the title picture for the World Tag Team titles and that my friend would be a dream come true for me!” Joshua explained as he fiddled with the controls on the CD player to try and get it to a certain song. “Ah yes I seem to remember about you being a former tag team champion in a fed called OCW where you and Judge Reed held the belts but also don’t forget that we held the IWF tag team titles also even when some say that that was with some controversy.” Zack reminded Joshua and Josh sat pondering about what he was referring to and when you go and look at the history of the IWF it clearly shows that the two made the biggest splash of them all when they “came out” before the World on what was then Monday Night Malice on Sky Sports back in 1992? April 1st, 1992 The scary thing about this match was that it was no “April Fool’s” joke in that Joshua Curtis and Zack Johnson were going for the World Tag Team titles of the IWF in their first ever match together and to some that made things a little bit shakier in that when you looked at the track record of Curtis and Johnson they had been opponents against each other for a good 3 or 4 months in the territory so seeing them as partners didn’t seem all that likely. The thing about it was that Joshua’s mentor had been calling Joshua out for weeks on the show and when it came down to it Joshua wasn’t too keen on facing off against his mentor, but the time came and Joshua walked out from the back and looked down the ring at Chester Clarke and Brad Cummings as goaded him with the tag titles. Joshua to his credit kept his composure the night that he came out amidst some boos but more cheers than anything and when he stared down the ramp and looked at the champs of Cummings and Clarke and realized that it was time to shut his mentor up for good he was about to go down to that ring and hand them ass whoopings that were traditional for him to hand out to the wrestlers that believed that they were the ****. As Joshua began walking towards the ring a young man with long brown hair tied into a ponytail walked out to the cheers of the crowd and when he saw that Joshua was about to go face to face with the two tag champs by himself he had to stop him before he got hurt! “Joshua its not safe darling’at least not without a partner.” The young man told Joshua and Joshua to his credit walked back up the ramp but it was more to face off against the young man who just called him “darling”. “Two things” Joshua began “first I’m capable of walking down to that ring and putting this size 11 in the asses of those two because I’ve kicked both of their rears at one time or another in one on one matches and second if I EVER hear you call me “darling” again I’ll beat the hell out of you right here!” Joshua spat but when he saw that Zack was on the verge of a breakdown he actually apologized and told him to come on down to the ring with him, which Zack promptly accepted! Interestingly enough for those two when it came to the title match their chemistry wouldn’t be a question but at the same time Joshua and Zack were at times getting wrecked pretty good so a lot of people believed that they two wouldn’t be able to tag in and out much which would cause Joshua to lose his chance at becoming a champion of any kind and then it happened? When Zack reversed Cummings? neck breaker and turned it into a kind of suplex the fans stood up and were stomping their feet and clapping their hands just waiting for Zack to tag Joshua in so he could tear into the two and Zack with his last bit of energy tagged Joshua in as Cummings tagged Clarke in and it was on from there! The two battled back and forth in one of the exhibitions of skill and knowledge that not many NWA rivalries could match! Joshua was running on the adrenaline that he got when Zack tag him in and was had clotheslines for both of them! Zack got up and helped Joshua keep one of the opponents occupied while he worked like hell on the other one and when it came time Joshua used a shin breaker and then the figure four and you could hear the scream of the victim and the ref asked not once, not twice but three times if he wanted to give up and for a couple of minutes the victim, whom turned out to be Clarke wouldn’t give up but Joshua wrenched it harder until Chester was practically in tears and he finally tapped out! The place had gone ecstatic and Zack couldn’t believe that the teacher gave up to the student but the one thing that Joshua remembered learning from Zack was how to put max pressure on some submissions from watching his matches and Curtis had used that to amplify the power that he had in his figure four and when the crowd saw that Joshua made Chester tap and the belts were being handed to Curtis and Johnson a part of them were like “Cool we did it.” while another part of Curtis at least went “Oh no I just won the tag titles with a gay dude” but as they would find out that part of it was all a fascade! As Joshua was about to walk out of the ring Zack grabbed a mike from the ring announcer and decided to stop his partner for a minute so that he could ask an all important question? “Joshua” Zack started “I know what you said when we were standing on that ramp but at the same time I feel like if you get to know me you won’t hate me or my kind any longer so with that?” Zack stopped to extend his hand as he had a white rose in the same hand that the mike was in “would you give me the honor of going out with me?” he asked and the place exploded as it awaited Joshua’s decision. Joshua was somewhat stunned by the offer because he didn’t know what to do or what the fans would think if he said no so he did the one thing that he knew he could do? “First off get up!” Joshua ordered and Zack did as asked at which point the crowd didn’t know what to think as Joshua continued “Zack you and I were heated and much hated rivals because you have a lifestyle the I must admit at one time I didn’t agree with, BUT I didn’t forget the time that you helped me before tonight with the reunion of me and my mother on national TV and I felt like there was no way I could repay you for that. What I would like for you to do right now is put that rose down and come here towards me with your eyes closed.” Joshua commanded and Zack surprisingly did as instructed and when he got close enough he got his answer from Curtis’a soft kiss on the lips and a gentle hug which caused the biggest pop EVER in IWF history! That night when the two were leaving the ring with the belts hand and hand Brian Greene and William Clarke were standing and applauding the two as it was about time that Joshua accepted Zack for who he was and not having the homophobia that he did when he first met him. Joshua placed his arm around his newly discovered boyfriend’s waist and hoisted that belt high in the air cos he knew that he might not have the belt for too much longer so he cherished it for the time that he had it but he was happy to have a new friend and for that matter someone that gave two shits about him! Back to present day? “Don’t ya miss those days Zack?” Joshua asked as he snapped back out of his coma for a little while and Zack shrugged it off. “Nah its not all THAT important because when you win a title that can be taken away from you” Zack stopped before turning to Joshua and looking at his beautiful blue eyes “but your one true love is something that can never really be taken from you until its time to pass on to the next life and I promise you Joshua I don’t plan on doing that anytime soon my darling.” Zack said as he stopped the car and opened the door to his side but then walked around and opened up the door to Joshua’s side to let him out. “We’re here.” Zack stated and when Joshua realized where they were he was very happy to be sure! “How’d you know?” Joshua asked and Zack gave him “the look” which told Joshua all he needed to know. “Well shall we?” Joshua asked extending his arm and when Zack accepted the offer off they went into the one place that he never thought he would go? You want to know where? Joshua’s home away from home a.k.a. Makoto’s place! I’ll leave it at that? Au Revoir!
