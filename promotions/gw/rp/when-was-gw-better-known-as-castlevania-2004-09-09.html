When was GW better known as Castlevania?

9 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/09/04 The tale begins some vast time ago in the past where all women were whores and all men were slaves of their occupation. In a time where the gun wasn't yet invented and villagers had to fight each other with pitchforks and poisoned butter, there lived a very sinister and evil character... or should I say, a very evil and sinister breed of race. They looked like clowns of the future with their unholy pale faces... and unmaintainable women with their foot-long fingernails. They looked like supermodels with their looks... well, the ones that weren't dead or didn't have a direct blood-line connection to Maggie Thatcher. These things would control the night with their mind-reading and their immense power... not to mention, their apparent immortality. Of all the humans left on this planet, one man would learn to stand up to fight this breed of disgusting creature. One being would rise from the ashes of defeat and destroy every little blood-sucking freak in his way. This man... was SHERWIN~! The immortal, the impossible... and greater than Alexander the Great. Move over 'Unstoppable Eugene~!' and all you other little paperclip heroes, this was a time for real electricity to grace the world like never before... blah, blah, blah... yeah. Anyway, the scene was set and the battle was close to coming forth. Ten years into the Vampiric transformation of the planet, our alledged hero was finally brave enough to venture into one of these crypts all by himself. As the purifying light of the moon beat down upon our hero, we sat back in the confort of our 'fantasy' viewing chairs and watched as the hero ventured deep into this hole. '... Hello, did somebody order pizza?' he called, trying to confuse and prehaps bemuse the vampiric legions with his amazingly HUGE level of mental intelligence. There was nobody who could match his own mental-brain power... not even people like Sabbot and Josh, who were each apparently intelligent in their own rights. Oh no, this man was on his own playing field. 'Hey, are you dumbfucks death or something? I SAID I HAVE SOME PIZZA!' he called again, screaming down with his heroic voice... the pitch jumping up and down as the... shakes of alledged glory ran through every bone of his tiny little body. Sheepishly, our hero stepped into the crypt, actually making it past the front door of the creepy establishment. He stopped a mere five paces into his journey and turned around to look at the door. As if by magic, the only escape from this hellish place began to close. It clicked and it clanked, moving along the stone floor like some cheap movie prop before EVENTUALLY coming to a complete halt. The hero stood tall and proud, he hadn't urinated his pants at the thought of being unable to escape a brutal death. 'Now...' he began, his voice echoing off into every square-inch of darkness. At first, the hero thought the echo of his own voice was cool but after regaining his composure, the hero began to look through the kit that he'd brought with him to defeat this vampiric scum. 'Hmm, did I bring everything that I needed for this mission?' Sherwin asked himself, looking down at each of his sides to see that the vital element of equipment... was missing. His facial expression changing instantly to one of horror and damn-right cowardice, he put his head into his hands and screamed out aloud, the realisation of defeat before the battle had even begun. 'ACCCCK! Why the Hell did I leave my Vampire killing equipment back at the bicycle?!' he screamed out again, the echo of his voice coming back to smack him across the chops a couple of times. As the echo passed, silence began to rule supreme in the crypt once more. Sherwin was down a creak without a paddle... but considering he was a former GW Television Champion, the hero was not going to allow the simple fact that he was MOST likely to be killed... cloud his judgement. Not to mention, it was already established that going out through the front door at this point was virtually impossible anyway. Rich looked away from the door and stared deep into the vast darkness that lay ahead of him. Gingerly, Sherwin felt around the walls as he began to make his journey; trying desperately to feel out any kind of torch that could be lit with ease. As if by magic, Sherwin eventually found one of these devices, pulling it down from the wall and scraping it across the walls, causing the flammable liquid soaked top to erupt into a burst of flames. The hero nodded with joy as he now had the gift of light to lead him through this horrific domain. He turned left... then right to survey his current surroundings in full. To his right was the way that he had to walk... to get to the demonic chamber of this vile beast and to the left... was the entrance of this place. He'd managed to walk another five steps since looking for this new way forward. Another record broken by one hell of an amazing individual. Rich sucked in his gut and began to walk along the rubbished corridor, parts of broken stone laying senselessly on the floor. The cracks on the impending corridor suggested that this is where the majority of the combat was fought... well, the one-sided battles that would usually involve a hapless hero and the legions of the dead. To his own personal surprise, Rich experienced no problems walking through these passages. Perhaps it was 'Bank Holiday Zombie' or some other form of national holiday for these underlings? Or, perhaps the zombies had finally met their match? Perhaps Rich Sherwin was the hero that nobody dared to face? It would be quite convenient if this were the case though. Soma just might be the one without a hope in Hell of winning when he locks horns with the indestructible hero at this forthcoming weeks edition of Evolution. '... 99 bottles of blood on the wall. 99 bottles of blood. You take one down, pass it around. 98 bottles of blo...' our legendary hero began to sing, hoping to either entice the vampiric legions to come out and play or to pass the time. He had almost finished the first verse of the little song when something broken his concentration and caught his attention. Looking behind, Rich looked down at the floor to see fresh rubble spread around. Perhaps somebody had just come through? But how? How had somebody been able to pass when Sherwin was the only one walking this corridor? Rich shrugged his shoulders and turned around to see a zombie standing infront of him. A deathly figure stood there to greet him, skin hanging at the bones and various limbs broken into shapes... the kind of shapes you'd find in a childrens educational booklet. '... Brains.' the zombie said, it's glazed over eyes staring directly into the eyes of our hero. The zombie repeated the word again as Rich stood there speechless, unable to think of anything to say to counter this otherwise unique greeting. 'I... wants... your... brains.' the zombie continued to say, Rich looking rather confused at first but then completely understanding everything said. 'Oh, for a few moments there, I thought you were trying to say something in code... but now it's completely clear to me that you want my brains!' Rich exclaimed with a polite chuckle, attempting to make this situation a little better than it turned out to be. Unfortunately though, a situation like this was never favourable for a human. Many times before had people attempted to charm a zombie with their unlimited levels of wit and humour but as the record stood, nobody had been able to perform the actual task of charming a zombie. Sure, people had been able to perform these skills with the use of a Druid in Diablo 2 or Neverwinter Nights... but this was a little bit different than that element. Out of sheer hunger and frustration, the zombie dived toward our hero, hoping to come out with the ultimate prize of a fresh brain but as the matter of convenience went, Rich stepped back and accidently activated a switch... which opened up some kind of trap-door opening. Where was this amazing contraption, you ask? Why it just happened to be directly in front of this zombie. So, with the button pressed and the door open... the ghoul fell to his untimely redeath... or something, probably not death but close enough. Grinning as though he'd planned it all along, Sherwin jumped over the gap in the floor and continued on his quest. Deeper and deeper into the crypt he stepped, slinking in and out of the shadows with his faithful companion, 'George the Torch'. After being in this dusky prision for all of twenty minutes, the concern for ultimate escape was beginning to settle. Rich had been stuck in there for a fraction of the time that the Vampire had lived here... but already, it was feeling as though he'd been around for a lot longer than any night-walking s.o.b. EVARH~! 'Compose yourself, Sherwin. Compose yourself. This is just a little challenge to prove yourself ready to take on another vampire in the wrestling ring. Everyone thinks that this Soma is going to be the impossiblest task yet... but I'm going to show them otherwise. I'm going to show them and everyone else... that I'm more than just the perfect fighting machine. I'm going to show them that I'm... that I'm a VAMPIRE LAYER... I mean, SLAYER~! MWAHAHA!' he screamed with booming confidence. Where this confidence came from, we'll never know? Perhaps it was the thought that if a dweeb like Nathan Graves could kill Dracula in 'Castlevania: Circle of the Moon' or Buffy being able to kill vampires and then some in 'Buffy the Vampire Slayer'? Nobody but the hero knew at this moment in time. Nobody but him. After another ten minutes of walking in this hellish setting, Rich eventually came to the ultimate clearing. It looked like the command centre for some kind of vampire headquarters. There were all these stone machines everywhere, various slabs of stone acting as control pods and other sci-fi based equipment. Sherwin stepped into the excessively large room, moving quietly to the middle of it all. Upon reaching the said destination, Rich held his torch high into the air and looked around his immediate surroundings. The view was amazing... it was like nothing that nobody had seen before and for once, he was right. Nobody had been able to make it this far, especially with such ease. Not even those geeks playing Diablo II and Neverwinter Nights could claim to getting this far in such a short period of time. Oh no, they'd have to continually level up and spend hours slaving over beasts and demons just to get those few extra experience points. Sherwin continued to look around, eventually resting his eyes upon a substantially large coffin. He began to walk toward it, the door slowly beginning to open with every step. Then, without warning, the door was pushed completely open and the body of an aging vampire stood tall. '... Congratulations human, you have managed to come this far in your quest. Sadly, this is where you will meet your end. You are the first to come through the traps and trials that I have set before you... and you will now be the last.' the vampire barked, sounding croaky with his broken voice and dehydrated appearance. Rich stood ready to battle. 'I'm not afraid of you. I'm not afraid to die... I will win!' he screamed in return, apparently getting ready for the next phase of our battle. Everything went silent... nothing happened. Nobody stepped forward and nobody stepped back. It was like the calm before the storm, a dramatic moment stretched out THAT far to give this battle some kind of meaning... some kind of cause. It ended. The silence concluded and the battle was about to begin. Rich pulled out some kind of whip-like weapon from a backpocket and the vampire launched himself from his coffin. Everything was going to come through here! Everything was going to come to a climatic end and then... - PAUSE - 'Wow, this game is one hella good level of education for when it comes to fighting Soma. I can't believe that vampiric moron didn't try to destroy every copy of this game beforehand? I mean, if I were a vampire, I would have made sure that my opponents couldn't find anything out about actual vampire combat!' What was this? It was Sherwin's voice but wasn't he supposed to battling this vampire? A game control flew across the picture, hitting the floor hard and then an enlarged Sherwin walked into view. He looked happy with himself, happy that he'd been able to get this far without any cheats? Any cheats? And then it settled in. This had all been a game. - End -
