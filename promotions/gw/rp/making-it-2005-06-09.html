Making It

9 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 9th 2005 and 1999 June 9, 2005 Home I sit alone on my couch thinking about what I need to do to outlast the six other combatants on Evolution. Each one a worthy of the Global Wrestling Television Title. A Title that must be defended every week or else you lose it. Sounds like my kind of title! I need that title more then anyone could know. I think I hide it well on camera but if I know don’t pull off something huge soon I’ll explode. I seem to produce moments to remember but none have involved me getting my hand raised lately. I was a the hot rookie nobody ever heard of and I was tearing down the house and getting my hand raised. Now I’m the young lion?. the dark horse of the promotion. I get this is what the veterans call paying your dues. I don’t have time for that?. I have gotten a taste of success and I want to taste it again. The last two years have been average. What do I have to show for it? A few big matches that I lost? Lost! Syberus, Shadow, Dawn McFarlane, Elia Smith, Justin Evers, Leinad Soma and others whose names I care not to remember. I plop back on my couch with my head looking in the ceiling. I remember when I was full of confidence. When you could not tell me I wasn’t going to succeed. June 9, 1999 Cedar Point Owen and I were sitting down drinking are rather expensive lemonade for as much as it cost it should have been freshly squeezed. Back in the day when I had a lemonade stand I never charged this much. Huge difference between 10 cents and 5 bucks eh? Time was reaching close and we’ve been tired from riding roller coasters all day. Hell, even watching Owen get slapped by this hot chick was exhausting at least for me. All in all it was a great day for the both of us. Now we just have to find Owen’s Dad so we can get the fuck home. “So, what time is your Dad suppose to be here?” I asked “He said he’d wait for us by The Demon Drop at 7.” “Alright, man lets get moving.” “You’re the boss.” He said in a sarcastic tone. “I’m glad you finally started recognizing this. It’ll make both our lives a lot easier.” Owen just shook his head in amusement and replied “Right” We walked over to The Demon Drop where Owen’s Dad was waiting on us. I don’t know much about his Dad but what I can tell is that he’s pretty arrogant. I’ve never seen him much even though I’m with Owen most of the time. I think that’s by Owen’s choice. “You’re late.” He told us. “We’re not late we’re just early for tomorrow.” I said with a smirk. However it was not taken this way by Owen’s Dad who just totally ignores me. “Lets go” He says in a sharp quick tone. Owen and I look at each other and I just shrug. We follow behind him and get in his 1984 Mercury Grand Marquis. Owen and his father climbs into the front seat of the 14 year old rust white rust bucket. I get in back wanting to just lay my ass down but knowing I’ll probably get yelled at by this guy I just buckle up. I see him glance in the rear view mirror at me. I’m assuming he’s just making sure I’m buckled up. We get out of the parking lot with ease since we left 1 ? before close. “Did you two have fun?” “Yeah” Owen answers. “What about you Freddy?” Owen’s dad asks. “Yup” I reply exhaustedly. I lean my head against the door and rest my eyes. I just sit there and think about what one day could be. I aspire to one day become a pro athlete. My poison of choice is the one that gets laughed at the hardest?.. Pro Wrestler. I’ve been told everything someone following an unlikely dream can be told. I’ve been told to go to college and get a real job to I’m not big enough and most annoying off all its not real. It doesn’t matter to me what they say. I choose to follow my childhood dream. I will not listen to the people who are too scared to follow theirs and have nothing better to do then make me just like them. “What’s this I hear about you and Fred joining a wrestling school?” I snap out of my day dream but keep my eyes closed and listen to the conversation. “Yeah, he’s talking about becoming a tag team and making it together. I’m all for it. I love wrestling.” “You may love it but neither of you would make it. Owen, you need to focus on school and get a real job.” “Maybe”.” Owen said in a disappointed tone. “Maybe?” His father asks in a somewhat confused tone. “Maybe, you’re right”.” Owen reluctantly agrees. “I am” I couldn’t believe what I was hearing. My biggest supporter to follow my dream was giving up on his and replace it with a real job. A real job? Isn’t a real job nothing more then one you don’t want to have? I know I will always have a friend in Owen and he’ll have one in me. I don’t understand how people can try to bring you down from your dreams especially if those people are your own parents. I understand what he’s going through right now since I am in the same situation right now. However, I believe his are much tougher on him. I’m going to make a vow to myself right now though. I will not be just like them. I will not be just like the people that hold the dreamers back. I will not be just like you. I will live my dream and I will be the best at what I do. June 09, 2005 Home That was 6 Years ago and a lot has changed. I have gotten into the sport I have loved for as long as I remember but I seem so far away from being the man. I need to win this. I don’t care who stands in my way I must beat them. So Elia and I are the 1st two in? Seems fitting since we’re the two biggest underdogs in the match. Yet, she holds a win over me. Come match time none of this matters. It all comes down to who has that fire inside with just a little bit of luck. The fire inside has never been a problem for me?.. but the luck has. I will rise to the top of this business though. It may not be with my good friend Owen?. but it will happen. I get off the couch and walk over to the dresser and look at the picture of my 1st match. I have my hand raised holding the WECW Hardcore Title over my head. The short lived promotion was probably the highlight of my career?.. “I can do this”.”
