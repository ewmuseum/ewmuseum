The Memory Remains #17

15 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 91503 “I got it!” Holmes? whispered exclamation came through the earpiece, letting John Zero know that Deacon had found the disk. “Good, now grab it and get back here,” Zero whispered into his own mic. Zero relaxed in the dark corner of the room, knowing that as soon as Holmes made his way back through the vent, they could get the hell out of the warehouse/labrotory. Why is it that they always convert old abandoned warehouses into secret lairs? Did they read it in the HOW TO BE A VILLIAN HANDBOOK? Seconds later, the words INTRUDER ALERT began sounding off within the room, small red lights spaced at various intervals flashing on and off. Zero slumped down in his corner, pulling out a silenced Glock 40. “Fuck!” both men exclaimed simultaneously. “Deac, man, get the hell outta there,” Zero whispered, staying in his corner, turning his head side to side, checking the three doors that branched off from the room he was in. “I’m trying, I’m trying. I can’t reach the goddamn vent!” Holmes yelled. “Don’t tell me, off all the times, this is when you need the fucking boost.” “Yes, yes it is. Shit!” Zero could hear four gunshots over the earpiece. “Talk to me Deac!” “It’s cool, man. Those labcoats came back with guns. They ain’t goin” nowhere for a while.” “But you’re still stuck.” “Yeah.” “Is there a chair that you could use?” Silence came over the earpiece. “Why didn’t I think of that” “That’s why I’m the supervisor and you’re the grunt, now get your ass into gear.” “I’m movin”, I’m movin?.” “So am I.” “What?” “I’m gonna get the car.” “What about me?” “You’re a big boy now, Deac. I’ll still have you on mic.” “Alright, alright.” Zero checked the LCD map on his arm. The black screen showed a white outline of the blueprint of the building with a green line tracing their path. John Zero ran off in the direction of the exit, only to bump into a running guard. Both of their guns went clattering to the side. Zero and the guard eyed each other, both with their arms out and palms facing each other, a sign of peace. Zero cocked his head to one side. “If you want to go, go. If you want to stay, I’ll have to go through you.” The guard’s eyes narrowed slightly and his open palms clenched into fists. “I guess, I’ll just have to go through you, then,” Zero whispered. “Yo John!” Deacon’s voice called into John’s ear, causing him to flinch, giving the guard an opening, swinging a roundhouse right at Zero’s head. Zero sidestepped the swing, catching the guard’s arm, and bringing up his knee into the guard’s chest. “What is it, Deacon?” “Catch you at a bad time?” Zero brought down a closed fist into the back of the guard’s neck, knocking him to the ground. Then, reaching down, he grabbed the guard by the head, and jerking back and forth, snapped the bone. “No, just tidying up a bit. What can I do for you?” “I need a lil direction here.” Zero looked at the LCD screen on his arm and saw the glowing dot that way Holmes in the vent shaft. “Okay, you’re gonna wanna take a left., the next two rights, then another left.” “Aight.” Zero reached down and picked up his gun as well as the guard’s assault rifle. Continuing down the hall, John comes to a closed locked door. “Hey Deac?” “Yeah?” “Hurry your ass up, I need you to pick this lock.” “WHAT!”! See man, see what I mean? It’s cause I’m black, admit it. ADMIT IT!” John starts to laugh. “I got it man. Hurry up though.” Zero starts to look through his pockets, trying to find his lock pick set. “Shit. Ahhh fuck it.” Zero points the assault rifle at the doorknob and fires several rounds, obliterating the handle. Walking through the door, Zero sees several armed guards standing in a line, waiting for him. “Shit,” he says as he flings his body to the left, just as the men open fire at the point that he was just standing. Landing behind some wooden crates, Zero stands up and fires the assault rifle at them, taking out three of the four guards, the last guard ducking. The rifle clicks empty, and Zero drops down, tossing the weapon to the side. Several bullets slam into the wall behind him. Taking a knife from his boot, Zero waits for the shooting to stop. When the audible click of the guard’s weapon being reloaded could be heard, Zero leapt out, thowing the knife and catching the guard in the throat. Blood began to pour out of the guards wound, and he collapsed to the ground. Zero walked over to the guard and removed the knife from the throat, placing it back in the sheath. “Deac?” “Yeah?” “Where you at?” “Just reached the ground.” “Alright. With your back to the vent, go straight and follow the path of bodies. I’m one room away from the exit. I’ll meet you outside.” John Zero runs across the room, and swings open the door to the final room. After peeking inside, he sees the coast is clear. “FUCK JOHN, get the DAMN car! I think the fucking army is behind me.” Zero stands at the final doorway, the sun shining on his face. He takes a large ivory colored blocked with wires running in all directions from it and attaches it to the wall next to the door. “You wanna play soft, we’ll play soft. You wanna play hard” Let’s play hard.” John Zero runs across the desert like lot, going as fast as he can to get to his black Mitsubishi Eclipse Spyder. Reaching the car, he slides in, revving the engine up, which purrs to life. Popping the clutch, Zero speeds off in a cloud of dust. “Where you at John, I’m here and your not?” “Freeze!” John could hear the words over the ear piece. In the distance, he could see Holmes facing the building, with about ten or so guards standing near the building, guns aimed at Holmes. “Almost there D.” “Hurry the fuck up, John.” John shifts the car into fourth, aiming the nose of the vehicle at Holmes. About one hundred feet away, Zero turns the wheel, pulling the emergency brake. The car turned, ninety degrees, the nose of the vehicle now pointing at the building. Zero leaned over, stretching across both seats, and flung the passenger door open. “DOWN DOWN DOWN! Get down, D!” Holmes drops to the ground as the car slides to a stop, the door acting as a shield for Deacon as the guards open fire on Zero’s bullet-resistant car. Zero looks at Holmes and gives him a cocky smile. “Miss me?” “Yeah, sure, whatever. Man, what we gonna do about those assholes?” Zero holds up a small black box with a red button on it and smiles. Holmes recognizes the device and smiles as well. Zero pushes the button and the front of the building explodes in a burst of red flames, sending the guards to the ground, some getting back up because they are on fire, others knocked unconscious, while others lay under piles of rubble. Holmes climbs into the car and the two men take off. “You have the disk, Deac?” Holmes starts to search his pockets, and looks at Zero. “Shit, man, I think I dropped it.” Zero hits the steering wheel and starts to turn around. “Fuck.” “Gotcha.” Holmes laughs holding up the disk. Zero looks at the disk and back at Holmes. “Asshole. Hold on.” Zero pops the top to the car’s gearshifter and presses the red button, injecting nitrous into the car’s engine. The car flies forward in a burst of speed, throwing Zero and Holmes back into their seats. “MOTHERFUCKER! That’s how you beat me the to HQ! I knew it, I knew it, I knew it!” Holmes exclaims as Zero begins to laugh as the two men drive off in a whirlwind of dust.
