Memory Remains #13

13 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/13 John Zero hangs up the phone looking at book men. Holmes sits in a corner, both guns drawn, twirling them on his fingers, forwards and backwards, backwards and forwards, spinning them upside down and side-to-side, killing time. Soma lounges on the brown leather sofa that he had made himself comfortable on earlier, his long reddish hair hiding the left half of his face. Holmes cocks one of the guns and pointing it a Soma’s head, who turns, staring at a hole through Holmes? head. “Boom,” Holmes laughs. Soma hisses. “Okay,” Zero says aloud to gain their attention. “Il Capo has cleared you, Mr. Soma.” “Excellent. We shall waste no time and start preparing immediately.” “No, no, no,” Zero states. “Time we shall waste. Just because Capo cleared you, don’t mean didily shit to me. My boss said your okay. Fine. I don’t completely trust him. So, that means I don’t have to completely trust you, now does it?” “No, I suppose you don’t.” “I’m glad we see eye to eye on this.” “So, where does that leave us?” “It leaves us with me not trusting you and you putting your faith in us.” “Ah. And how would I go about gaining your trust?” “I thought you would never ask,” Zero whispered with a smile. The three men walked swiftly across the near empty parking lot to the metal warehouse, the moon illuminating the rusty outer walls, and the dim lit windows. Zero has a long black coat pulled over his body, which covered the dark clothes he wore underneath. Holmes walks to the right side of Zero, in back jeans and a black hoodie, the hood pulled up, covering Holmes? face. Soma walks on the left of Zero, still wearing his Mandarin style trench coat. “And what, pray tell, are we doing here?” Soma finally asks. “Well, you see, this is a holding of one Mr. John Vincent, former employer of Matthew Blake.” “Ah yes, the tasty morsel.” Holmes and Zero give Soma an odd look. Holmes clears his throat and Zero continues. “Anyways, we are going to enter this building and raid the office, see if we can find anything of use.” “Any particular reason why?” Soma asked. “Because Mr. Vincent hurt my feelings.” “He hurt your feelings?” “Yeah, he sent someone to kill me.” “Ah.” “And I am rather insulted that he sent Matthew to kill me.” “Was he your friend?” Holmes asked. “No, he was a damned fool. And practically an amateur.” Soma made a noise somewhere between a laugh and a hiss. “Good help is so hard to find.” They reached the doorway to the warehouse, Holmes pulling out a small lock pick set, going to work on the rusty lock. “Five seconds, Deac,” Zero whispered. “Five seconds, what the fuck man,” Holmes said loudly as he worked on the lock. “My best time. Five seconds.” “Five fucking seconds. Bullshit.” Holmes opened the door. How long was that then?” “With bitching and complaining?” “Yeah.” “I’ll be nice and say seven.” “Fuck you.” “No thanks, you’re not my type.” Holmes rears back as to strike Zero but doesn’t. “Gentlemen, if we can get back to the quest at hand,” Soma said, stepping between the two friends. “Okay, spread out. See what you can find.” The trio disperse, each heading in a separate direction, turning over boxes, checking dark corners. “Man, this is bullshit. Instead of searching through this maze of shit, we should be out busting a cap in someone’s ass.” “Patience, Deac,” Zero whispered. “Violence is not always the answer. Sometimes you have to search for a way to break your opponent. It’s a lot like chess. You must think ahead three moves in order to win.” Deacon mimics Zero, murmuring to himself, “Violence isn’t the answer, Deac’Bullshit” It may not be the answer but it’s hella fun.” The noise of a gunshot rips through the air, a loud growl following closely behind it. “Soma!” Holmes and Zero shout simultaneously. “Whoever it is, Soma, don’t kill them!” Zero yells at the top of his lungs as a body goes flying through the air, landing on a crate, shattering the wooden, and showering Zero with the splinters. “As you wish,” Soma whispers, stepping out of the darkness, his eye color changing slightly. Zero looks down at the limp body and then back up at Soma. “You wanted alive, Mr. Zero. That he is.” “Barely. Holmes, bring me that chair and rope.” The man moves his head from side to side, as if trying to clear the cobwebs from his mind. He jerks up, but not far due to the rope holding him to the chair, as his last thought before blackness returns to him. His eyes snap open and he scans the room, moving his head about, seeing John Zero sitting in a chair before him with Deacon Holmes and Leinad Soma standing behind him. “Hello, Robert.” “John, long time no see,” Robert says, no acknowledging Soma or Holmes existence. “You know, seeing you here today Robert, and seeing Matthew the other day, well, it makes me feel as if there is a reunion of sorts going on, and nobody’s invited me. And that hurts my feelings, Robert.” “Yeah, man, and you don’t wanna see him when his feelings get hurt,” Holmes puts in. Robert cuts Holmes a dirty look. “John, when did you start associated with niggers. I thought you knew better than that. WHACK! Soma cuffs Robert in the back of his head, knocking him forwards. WHACK! Holmes follows up with a fierce backhand, sending Robert backwards, tipping over in the chair. Reluctantly, Holmes corrects the chair, putting Robert back in front of Zero. A little blood trickles down the corner of Robert’s mouth. “Nobody, I repeat, nobody drops the n-bomb here,” Zero whispers. Holmes and Soma both lean in, adding, “Nobody.” “Fine, sure, whatever you say, John. You’re in charge. Always have been” Robert nods to emphasize the point. He smiles a bit. “By the way, John, how’s Lily.” This time, it was Zero’s turn to hit Robert, which he did, square in the face, shattering the bone behind Robert’s nose, causing blood to flow freely from Robert’s nostrils. “Never say her name,” Zero whispered coolly. “You don’t have the fucking right to say her goddamn name!” “Who’s name” Lily’s?” Robert says, a sarcastic look on his face. Zero tackled him, knocking both of them over with the chair that Robert was tied to. Zero began beating on Robert’s face with fierce lefts and rights, hammering away, Holmes and Soma pulling him off, surprised at the quickness and the anger behind the attack. Robert’s face was a bloody mess, though he could still see, slightly, and his voice was garbled. “What do you want?” he said slowly, measuring each word. “Why the reunion. Why all the old faces popping back up.” “It’s Vincent,” Robert finally said. “He’s merging the old crew into his crew. If he can’t get someone, they’re toast. They say he’s been gunning for you. He knew that he couldn’t get you to adapt, so he’s not even gonna try.” Zero circled around the chair. “Joy.” “So, what are you gonna do, Johnny-boy” You gonna save the day, like you did with Lily?” Zero pulled a long knife from a sheath located at his back and was behind Robert in an instant. Before Holmes and Soma could do anything, he sunk the blade into Robert’s throat, jerking it up and out just below the jawbone. Holmes? looked at Zero in shock and Soma stared at the body hungrily. Zero stared at the knife and threw it down in disgust, walking out of the warehouse, with Holmes running to catch up with him, leaving Soma and the bleeding corpse alone.
