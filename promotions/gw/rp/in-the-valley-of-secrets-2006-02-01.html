In the Valley of Secrets

1 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/01/06 December 6th, 2005 Opus One Detroit, MI The lunch crowd had cleared out pretty well as Eryc sat waiting for someone who was supposed to join him a half hour ago at the least. He looked at his watch and sighed about to give up and go back to his hotel when he smelled a familiar perfume and the voice that belonged to the person wearing it. “I’m sorry, Eryc, I was stuck in traffic after my class was over; I would have called but you know I never use my cell when I’m driving.” The girl leaned over and kissed him on the cheek. She would have gone for the lips but she wasn’t sure about his feelings on public displays of affection. He was a celebrity and you never know who might be hiding around a corner with a camera looking for a snapshot to sell to a tabloid. “You’re here and that’s all that counts.” He stood up and walked around and pulled her chair out for her to sit down; once she was down he slowly pushed the chair in for her. “Happy Anniversary, Krissy,” he said with a smile. “I picked up a little something for you.” Eryc reached into his coat (which hung on the back of his chair) and pulled out a medium sized box with the word Tiffany’s written on it. He slid it towards her slowly and then pulled his hands away to watch her as she observed the box. Krissy blushed as her fingers slowly brushed over the box as if it could be a dangerous animal. “It’s not going to bite you...go ahead and open it.” Eryc said in a persuasive tone. She bit her lip nervously as she pulled the box closer to her and like a band-aid she quickly opened the box to reveal a diamond swing necklace. Unknown to her, the necklace was worth close to ten thousand dollars. “Eryc, it’s beautiful...” He shook his head and stopped her from saying what he knew she would say. “No buts,” he stood up and walked to her side of the table and took it from her hands. “I want to see how it looks on you.” Krissy pushed her auburn hair out of the way as he brought the necklace around her neck and fastened it in place. Eryc swung back around and took a few steps away so he could get a good look at her with the necklace on. “Perfect” he mused as he moved closer to her and gave her a kiss on the lips. It lingered for a few seconds before he slowly pulled away and sat back down in his own seat. “I think maybe we should have a glass of wine, what do you think?” She thought for a second and nodded. “Yes, I’d like some.” Eryc motioned over for the waiter and when the waiter arrived ordered a bottle of vintage Chardonnay. The waiter walked away for a few moments and came back with an ice bucket containing the bottle and poured a glass for them both before he went away again. “Eryc, you really don’t need to do all this.” Krissy was appreciative of it but she had never been with a man who wanted to give her the world and possibly have the ability to do it. It wasn’t the expensive things he could offer to her or any luxury known to man but his affection for her and the way he treated her was what she looked for most. He had the heart and soul of a poet and that is what she loved about him most. “I know I don’t have to but I want to.” He replied and then added. “If I stopped spoiling you then you would probably be angry at me.” She took a sip of wine and looked him in the eyes trying to tell him that she didn’t need material things to be happy and she’d probably much prefer not having them. “None of that is important” she urged, “but spending time with you is.” “I know and I’ll tone it down...it’s just been a while since I could do something for anyone that I got a little carried away.” He knew she was right; it didn’t take diamond necklaces to make her happy. All she wanted was just the quiet evenings where the two of them were alone talking just about anything that came to mind. The four months they have been together have been some of the best times in his life in a long time and he didn’t want to ruin it with doing things she wouldn’t care for or treating her as he might have someone who he didn’t feel anything for. There was one thing that threatened to keep them from living happily ever after: lies. They both had something to hide and if either one of them told the other of them, the ramifications would be catastrophic. It would be easier for Eryc to keep his past from coming out because anyone who would hear the story would think it was ridiculous without being shown proof. Who would believe that vampires existed in the world? To most people, vampires are fictional manifestations of the mind that gave them a break from reality. A way for people to fantasize and be lost in a world that was unlike their own. If he even considered telling her this there was the obvious chance she’d think he was crazy and not want anything to do with him ever again or there was the danger of her believing and putting a potential mark on her by every vampire in the city. Krissy on the other hand had a much more dangerous secret to tell and if he’d find out the truth about who she was, it would be over without a doubt. Their thoughts betrayed them. They could look one another in the eyes and feel so much content and happiness but underlying was a mountain of pain and suffering all of which was the price of their deception. “I think we should probably order something or they might throw us out.” Krissy looked over Eryc’s shoulder and spotted the waiter talking to the hostess and looking in their direction. “I could buy this place and probably twenty others just like it and besides we’re paying customers. But you’re right...I don’t want to spend all day here.” He turned and looked behind him, raising his arm. “Waiter,” he exclaimed. “We’re ready to order now.” The waiter strode towards the table with the little notebook in which he wrote orders down upon with a scowl upon his face. - - - - After a nice lunch, Eryc and Krissy went back to his room at The Atheneum Hotel where he has been staying while he’s been in town for his independent bookings. There was much more to be done before the day (which was growing closer to early evening) was over and Eryc wanted to make sure that it turned out as close to perfect as he could possibly get it. “I know that I told you I would relax with buying you things but I made one other purchase before I knew that I would be easing up with spoiling you.” Krissy tilted her head slowly to the side. What more could he have possibly bought for her, she wondered? “I’m curious...what did you get me?” Her hand slowly brushed over the necklace he placed around her neck thinking that this was expensive enough to last her a lifetime of gifts. “If it’s expensive I’m going to make you take it back.” Eryc raised a finger so that she’d just wait until he showed her what it was that he bought. Her eyes followed him as he crouched on his hind legs and pulled out a large box from under the bed. His smile did not fade as he approached her with it and held it out for her to take. “If today is any indication of how things will be for us in the next four months and beyond,” he paused and tried to find the right words to describe the contents of the box without giving it away. “I just want to make sure that...” Krissy’s eyes started to grow a little wider as she started to undo the ribbon that was tied into a nice bow to make the box presentable. It wasn’t labeled with a brand store so it could be anything. However, with the size of the box...you just had to know it was clothing of some type. “It’s amazing Eryc; I love it!” Krissy was overjoyed with the gift he’d given her. Inside the box was an evening gown of crimson red color made out of silk crepe and chiffon. She held it up against her body to see that it would fit her perfectly but she had no clue how he knew what dress size she wore. “How did you know?” She inquired. “About my dress size...I mean?” Eryc shrugged for a second or so but her eyes had this look in them that made him want to tell her whatever she wanted to know. “I sort of cheated a little bit.” He laughed. “I just looked at your clothes in the middle of the night once and went from there.” Krissy didn’t know what to think about it but she loved the dress and that was what seemed to matter most. The one thing she was unsure about was why he bought it; what was it for? “Are we going somewhere tonight?” Eryc smiled and elusively answered, “Maybe?” The plans he made were not for a little while yet so he could toy with her some before he gave in and told her what was to happen later in the evening. “Eryc” she pleaded. “Don’t play with me! Tell me where we are going so I can fix myself up properly. I look like a mess right now.” He continued to play games with her. “You’ll have plenty of time to get dolled up before we have to go.” He winked at her before adding on to his words. “Besides, you look beautiful no matter what.” Krissy approached him and poked him in the chest, pushing him back some closer to the bed. “Listen here, Mister...” She poked at him again edging him even closer. “You’re going to tell me or I just might have to cut you off even if it is our anniversary.” She had him in a precarious position indeed; could he risk not telling her? It didn’t seem logical to potentially take away what could be the end of a magical night because of neglect in details. Eryc was at the bending point but he didn’t want to break. “You’re bluffing.” He grinned. “You would be cutting your nose off to spite your face.” Assurance was in his voice. He’ll crumble in a few seconds, she thought to herself. With playful force she pushed him down onto the bed and as he lay on his back she got on top of him and put her hands on his chest. Eryc placed his hands on her hips as their eyes met. “You don’t think I’m serious do you?” She pressed down on him just slightly. “Are you ever in for a surprise.” Krissy playfully tapped his nose with her finger and slid off of his lap and walked over towards the little table and chair that was customary in a hotel room and sat down. If he wanted anything out of her tonight then he’d have to give in. Damn, he muttered as he looked over at her in which she waved at him innocently. “Alright fine, I’ll tell you where we’re going.” He sighed in defeat. “I have tickets to tonight’s Red Wings game against the Devils...luxury box next to the owner’s box.” Krissy’s jaw dropped thinking she couldn’t have heard right. “Luxury box...are you kidding?” Just as Eryc stood up from the bed he was brought back down by Krissy, who pounced on him and peppered him with kisses of excitement. They had spent many nights in the hotel room watching Red Wings games on TV and telling one another that they should go together. She had instantly forgotten all about her threat to him as he continued to spoil her in ways she had never been spoiled before by any man. Krissy may not have liked it so much but if it continued to happen she might get used to it. “You’re the greatest man in the world, Eryc Morgan.” She whispered into his ear as she rested her head against his shoulder. “I don’t know how I could ever give anything back to you in return.” A look of contemplation came over Eryc as he thought about it for a moment but he already knew the answer all along. “I think you do enough by just being here,” He moved his hand to link it between hers. “I can’t express in words how much it means to me having you here.” Krissy cozied up to him a little more, nuzzling his neck lightly. “I’m sure you’ll show me later.” - - - - The Red Wings game had been a blast. Eryc and Krissy had come and gone in style in a rented limo he set up for their evening. They went in through the back way so that nobody might notice someone famous would be at the game. Eryc dressed to match the dress he had bought for Krissy by wearing a black Armani suit with a shirt that matched the color of her dress, along with a black tie. They looked like a million dollars and probably wore clothing that cost just as much. The Joe was going to be packed with fans and Eryc knew it. He was glad that he had made arrangements ahead of time for security to surround him and his girl so that they wouldn’t be mobbed with rabid people looking for autographs. They were there to enjoy a game of hockey. Security escorted the couple up to the luxury boxes, which had just about anything that you could imagine inside of it: a microwave, refrigerator, fully stocked bar...the works. You could think of it almost as a miniature hotel room just with seating and without a bed. The assistant who was assigned to the box fixed drinks for them so they could sit down and relax before the game would start; the sheer electricity of being in such a historic building made for an exciting time. Krissy, a rabid Red Wings fan would be cheering for the home team. Eryc, who lived in New York predominantly, was more of a Devils fan. He wouldn’t put his allegiance out in the open though...it wasn’t a good idea especially being in Detroit to root for the other team. The Devils were the first to score in the first period just a few seconds after the four minute mark. Eryc could tell that Krissy was not happy that the visiting team scored but she would have cause to rejoice in just a little while. Brendan Shannahan, a veteran on the ice, scored the tying goal close to the end of the first period to knot it up at one a piece. Eryc watched silently as the score was now even, which made it that much more important for one of the two teams to score. He had to do all he could to contain himself from showing where his loyalty was when the Devils Zach Parise (Center #9) scored a goal and went ahead of the Red Wings in the second period. The jam packed arena seemed to quiet down some as most do when their team is losing. It wasn’t long before they were back into it as Brendan Shannahan scored another goal to tie it up once more. Eryc could hardly hear his own thoughts as the arena rallied around their team. Hope began to dwindle for Eryc as the Red Wings went on to score two more goals before the end of the second period. His girlfriend was having the time of her life even if he was quietly suffering that made it all worth while. During the third period intermission, Eryc snuck out and found the pro shop and bought a few items to commemorate the occasion which included an authentic Brendan Shannhan sweater (they are not referred to as jerseys in Hockey). As soon as he brought it back to her she put it on immediately and moved closer to him, resting her head on his shoulder. The Red Wings scored one more goal in the remaining period to go on and win the game 5-2. It was an exciting game and Eryc didn’t feel like he walked away empty handed; after all...he still had the most beautiful of women by his side. As security once again helped escort Eryc and Krissy to the limousine he looked over at her and received a smile for everything he had done during the course of the evening. “Did you have a good time, babe?” Eryc asked as they walked. “It was the best; I’ll remember this day for a long time.” She said with a squeeze of her hand against his. They entered the limo again and were silent much of the ride back to his hotel; it wasn’t because they didn’t have anything to say to the other but were so worn out from the entire day that they needed a moment to collect themselves from all the excitement. They made it back into his room and he trudged towards the bed and crashed onto it. All he needed to do was take his clothes off and he could get in bed and go right to sleep if it wanted to but Krissy still had to take off her make-up and wash her face. “I really don’t want to but I need to get this make-up off,” she sighed, “will you be able to stay awake that long?” Eryc had his eyes closed but was still awake enough to answer her. “Yes, I’ll be awake for a little bit yet...I think all that pop at the game was a shock to my system.” He added a chuckle to his statement. He must have fallen asleep though because he was awoken to Krissy slowly undressing him to get him into bed so they could go to sleep. “You passed out on me, sleepy head.” She smiled. She was wearing the sweater he bought for her at the game and nothing else, which made sense though because it was big enough to wear alone. Eryc took over and finished undressing himself and pulled her close to him; she stood between his legs as he wrapped his arms around her waist. “Eryc,” Krissy said with a soft voice, “I love you.” His gray eyes met her hazel colored irises and knew that he couldn’t go back after this moment. The feelings he had for her were genuine but he just didn’t know if he was capable of love after all of the tragedies of his life. He couldn’t just ignore it and what would happen if he didn’t respond to her? She would probably not understand after they spent an entire wonderful day together and confessed how she truly felt if he didn’t reciprocate somehow. Why did he question it? Did she love him like she said? The experience was as alien as drinking blood for the first time but there was a natural way about it that relaxed his conscience. He moved his hands up her sides helping her out of the sweater and brought her into bed with him. As he lightly began to work over her neck with his lips, he whispered the words that would seal his fate. “I love you.”
