The Rome Incident - Perseverance of a warrior.

25 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 25th, 2004. 'The Rome Incident - Part III' Some days it feels like the entire weight of the world is resting squarely on your shoulders, pressing down on those thick muscles in your rotator cuff. That weight of monumental proportions causes you to sink down on the surface your standing upon. The ground beneath your feet becomes like quicksand and the more you fight the further you sink. Sometimes that weight stays on there a while and you fight it sinking lower and lower all the time. But eventually…you have to either pull yourself up or your head is going to go under. For me it was the latter that happened. I can't remember whether or not I intentionally overdosed and tried to commit suicide or if I just for some crazy reason overdosed accidentally. I'm not a drug user…I was given rohypnol for the sole purpose of curing my insomnia. It had definitely taken care of that. The only problem now was trying to discover what had happened. The 'forget-me' pill had definitely lived up to its name, but I was wishing I could remember everything. All I remember is that my fellow Elite cohorts and I were planning on taking a night to enjoy Rome. Maybe hit a few clubs…even though I'm antisocial I enjoy spending time with my friends even if it is amidst a bunch of out of control hormonal idiots. Ice had called me earlier that day maybe a few hours before we were set to leave. I had conversed with him and then called Ken. They had agreed to rendezvous at my hotel room since I didn't have the luxury of a rental car. But that's where the memory ends. From there I only recall what I've been told. Ken had tried calling me on his cell phone but to no avail. When they had knocked at my door there was no answer. Finally after kicking the door in they discovered my whereabouts…lying face down on the floor, half-naked and convulsing. Ice and KPJ had called 911 and Ken even rode with me in the ambulance as we sped away to the hospital in Rome. It was there I woke up and remembered only one thing…a dream…or at least I think it was a dream. That dream involved being suspended above eternity…and screams, countless, horrific, ear splitting screams of terror. I however can't remember anything. My memory ends at my earlier conversations with Ice and KPJ, and begins anew with the dream and waking up in the hospital. I've often heard that some memory of the events that occurred would slowly come back to you. Maybe not all of the events but a few pieces, and really those few minor details were all I needed. In fact I could do without most of the details…I just needed to know one thing. Had I or had I not attempted suicide? It was a scary thought and one that rattled me, the thought disturbed and bothered me beyond all human comprehension. I exhaled heavily as I gazed out of the window of our black Jaguar that sped down the Italian roads containing the quadripartite of men that compose the Elite. I was seated in the back seat with Ken lounging in the back as well. Ace Harding was behind the wheel and Ice sat next to him in the front. My loud exhale caught the attention of Ken as he glanced over at me. I leaned my head back using the comfortable leather headrest that matched the rest of the interior on this lovely machine. The locks of my shaggy black hair fell into my eyes yet I ignored them, closing my eyelids over their bluish gray orbs. As I sat there in silence I placed my hands in my lap resting them on the ripped Dolce & Gabana jeans I wore that led down to a pair of black Converse sneakers. My sleeveless black mesh shirt also had holes in it, almost as if it were a fisherman's net that had been torn and was due for retirement. As I sat there resting my bloodshot eyes and my scattered mind my thoughts begin to branch out, and for once it seemed that they were branching away from my near death experience…yet in a way they were still connected. I just found it strikingly odd that this unusual quartet had put aside their differences and stuck it to the management as we aligned to form a true super power of today's Wrestling world. My thoughts were on Ace Harding first of all…opening my eyes I glanced at Ace. He wasn't exactly a giant but he was far, far, from being a delicate man. Tipping the scales at two hundred and seventy pounds and with a body that would put many professional body builders to shame Ace Harding was an impressive talent. His back was towards me as he handled the wheel of this rented Jag. He had powerful shoulders and arms that matched. These were showcased by the white wifebeater he wore and the black workout pants, that continued on to reveal a pair of black Nike trainers. I had to admit before Ken Partridge Jr. introduced us I was a stranger to the work of Ace Harding. Once I saw his combination of power and technical Wrestling ability I was captivated by it. I was just happy to see him playing for the same team…even if I didn't completely know him. Ice on the other hand was quite a different story. I shifted my eyes to where he sat dressed in a plain white T-shirt, Armani exchange jeans and brown Doc Marten work boots. Ice was an interesting story. A legitimate tough guy as can be attested by the pain I inflicted onto him…and the pain he inflicted upon me in return. I've put him in the hospital before and he had returned the favor. I've spilt his blood time and time again whereas he has drained gallons from my blood supply. I've defeated him inside the ring…and he's defeated me. For such a long time I set out on a path to ruin his career and he walked the same beat. But now…we're friends as close as brothers. Inside the ring we know one another's strengths and weaknesses. We compliment one another by being able to perfectly execute that which the other cannot. But a very strong attribute that we share…is the fact that each of us has no limit. Glancing over at Ken I saw his baby blue eyes peering down into a tourist map, highlighting the hot spots of Rome. As he sat there dressed casually in a pair of Tom Ford jeans, Chippewa shoes and a red throwback 'Starsky & Hutch' T-shirt. Ken is another story in itself. As smart, talented, sophisticated and well cultured man as I've ever met. For everyone that degrades the Wrestling business and the men in this industry for being unintelligent, violent steroid freaks I wish I could introduce them to Ken. Much like myself he seeks out knowledge. His thirst for knowledge is insatiable and quite admirable. Whether he's driving a boot into your face and hitting you so hard with the sweet Ken Music that your nearly decapitated or whether he's explaining to you the works of Shakespeare everything he does is done with ease. Not only is it done with ease but also it's done with pride. And yes…Ken is another man that I've spilt the blood of and a man that's spilt mine in return. But when it comes down to it these four men aren't my enemies but rather my closest allies…my best friends…my only family. Whether they are brothers in bloodshed, ink or simply in the Wrestling business we have became a tightly knit family. Four men of high standing among the Wrestling community yet four men that have been ostracized by all members of the Global Wrestling audience simply for our career choices. Yet as friends…as partners…as brothers we would soon all wage war against one another. Ace, Ken and I had been placed in the Global Rumble with the winner receiving a shot at the World Champion. I had good reason to believe that Ice could shrug off the challenge of Souls of the Black Lotus, and I also strongly felt that one of the trio of Elite members placed in the Rumble would come out on top. Yet no matter what happens…no matter who eliminates whom…no matter whom wages war with Ice for his World title, no matter what we must remain a strong allegiance. I closed my eyes once again as I reflected on this thought. We had to remain a strong allegiance even if it was only for my sake. Because I had a strong feeling in my gut that something such as this may happen again one day…and if I didn't have the Elite… …who would be there to save me next time?
