Moving in and taking it easy.

14 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/14/05 ~This week~ ~The Mandalay Bay Hotel and Casino~ ~Executive Suite~ Things change... Like Dani actually loosing an honest match for the first time since she debuted last November. Even through 3 feds now. One dies, one should have died, and now Global Wrestling. Not just Wrestling though, but changing to just GLOBAL to make it more of a mixed fighting sports entertainment venture. This is nothing new to Dani. While GLOBAL has undertaken such things as building it's own small hotel and casino in North Las Vegas, just a few miles north of the Stratosphere hotel that marks the northern most end of the real 'Strip', it's got a real home. Something that Dani hasn't yet found. But, she can't find herself to stay at Global's residences, given that it would indicate exactly where she would be at. She knows some of of the wrestlers who don't want to take up residence in Nevada have chosen to stay there when they're in town, but Dani thinks ahead. It's more frugal when you just find a place to stay. She hates it when something else steps in, like her sponsors in the business actually setting her up in an Executive Suite in the Mandalay Bay Hotel and Casino at the southern end of the Strip. But again... things change. But, not the weather is Las Vegas. The ever so relentless heat searing her already darker skin and melting her shoes to the sidewalk in front of the tall, gold glass building that is also known as the Mandala Bay Hotel and Casino. The cauldrons that line the front of the building, ablaze, give off more heat to add to her discomfort. Daniele, dressed in her normal outdoor clothing of jeans and tank top, hurries to get herself inside. Heading past the front desk, ever being watched buy security as she moves to the elevators. Reaching inside her ruck sack for her key card, just as the guard stops her. The face on Dani pretty much tells the whole story as her hand comes up empty and void of the key. Grumbling to herself she turns and smiles at the guard Guard: Can I help you miss? These elevators are for Private Guests only. He looks at her with a sort of disdain like she isn't good enough to be there. Someone who doesn't even realize that Las Vegas is a haven for everyone. Then again, they're still pissed off about loosing those 20 acres in downtown to the Paiute Nation nearby. Tribal land, especially a burial ground, needs to be honored just as much as any other National Monument or place of history. They just see it as loosing 20 acres that some other casino could develop into a money making opportunity. Dani: Yeah, Guido, give me a minute that blasted Key card fell to the bottom again. Guard: You have a room here, or are you meeting someone? Dani stops and look at him with that comment. Dani: I know you didn't just suggest something? Guard: Excuse me ma'am but your key ? Dani just looks at the guy with disbelief,as her hand still moves around the bag. The guard mumbles something on the radio. And what seems almost instantly, the Concierge Manager shows up. The look on his face once he sees who it is would be funny but not after all of this. He smiles nervously at Danielle. Manger: Mis. Northsky, is there a problem? Dani: Yeah, I dropped my pass key for the room and cant find it in my bag. Go figure. The guard stands here with a somewhat defeated look on his face. As she places her bag on the Podium to help her rummage through it until her hand comes up with a thin credit card looking item. Dani turns and swipes the card for the private elevators. Dani: So how does one get tickets to one of the concerts this weekend? Manager: Which one are you interested in? The arrangements made by your sponsors included an expense account. I can make arrangements for any of the shows here for you. Dani: I was thinking Friday of seeing Pat Benatar, and on Saturday seeing Tears for Fears. Manager: Both of those shows are at our Beach Stage. Can I ask how many for each show? Dani: I'll be generous and say 4. Manger: I will have the will call setup and the receipts brought to your room. Dani: Thank you. You'll excuse me, but after walking around all day, I think it's time to catch some relaxing time on the beach in that big pool of yours. Manager: By all means, Ms. Northsky. Can I ask if you've seen our current show in the Mandalay Bay Theater, Mamma Mia. It's based on the songs of the 70's band called ABBA. Dani: Hmm, yes. I think we'll have to take that in too. ~Fast Forward~ We find Danielle in her room, dressed in what looks to be almost nothing. A white bikini that covers almost nothing. A bathing suit that looks very close to the one she's wearing in the soon to be released As a matter of fact. Gray would have a fit if he was to walk in on her. She actually sits at a desk with her now well known Journal open in front of her. The turkey feather pen in hand and bottle of ink sitting next to her hand as she writes in her own script inside the book. ~July 12th, 2005~ Great weather, yeah, too warm for my taste. They treat people different here: right clothes, right hair style, right money. I am either mistaken as housekeeping by some, or just someone there for a good time by others. Although none of the tourists who talk in other languages get that treatment. Then again, they walk around and just scream that they brought their money here to have fun and loose it. Although the manger has been pretty cool. He said he's met the Lady before when she was a wrestler, and remembers them well. This just seems to be the day of all days though. I've been busy all day running around Vegas to try to find a more permanent home before I wear out my welcome here. To top it all off, I have a match this week against a woman... second GW female for me to face. Of course the first one being Elia Smith when I was still with that other place. Hopefully, Ms. Valentine is better then the 'Cowboy Barbie' was. Well you know what some say you cant win them all. But hell I need a win badly. Starting to wonder about all of this. I missed out on the Crossfire matches because I was to new. That sucked big time! But I enjoyed watching it from the back. Our guys did good, they didn't win many matches but they still put their hearts and bodies on the line for the company. So all the more power to them. Then my debut last show against Emoticon. The stress of moving again and that obviously got to me. But there are no excuses for a student of the schools I went to. That night he was better then me. I've only lost one match in a way I can't call foul on since I moved up from the Indies, and that was against him. I'll take it. Lick my wounds... that night he was better, next time might not be that way. But now that is past, future is the match between the her and me this week. She wants to make a name for herself and I have a name. A loss is a loss a win is a win, but you know what a win is better. The sad thing is, I've trained for a while, but I'm still a rookie. She's only had a handful of matches on her own... one singles match. I'm a champion in my own right. The question will be, who's going to be even more ready for this? Who wants it more? It's my second match in Global,also. I have my goals to reach, and Ms. Valentine... you've not seen it as rough as I have. So it's time to break out of anything before a slump can even start. Dani puts down her pen and ink looking at her fingers. She has the tell tale signs of using the old fashioned way of writing. A black ink stain in her middle finger of her left hand. She reaches up and grabs some wet wipes from a box and starts to wash her hand off. As she does there is a knock on the door... Dani: Coming, Gray! She gets up and walks over to the door, opening it to reveal her friend and older 'cousin', Gray. He's dressed in a black silk collared shirt. with a long thin tie and black slacks. Standing behind him, and off a bit to the side is his little brother, Josh, in a matching outfit to his. Josh is also standing there, looking like a cod fish gasping for air. Josh: How did you know who is at the door, just by a knock? Dani: The sound, the knock and plus Gray always wears the same after shave. Which he bathes in. So the smell drifts. Gray has walked in past her already, and stands looking out the window. The setting sun bounces off the neighboring Luxor Hotel Pyramid, as the beam of light from the top of begins to come to life. Gray: You know they had to dim that light some. Because, it was messing with the planes when they first built it. And did you know that a waitress' costumes have more material then your bathing suit in ninety-nine point nine percent of all the casinos? Dani just giggle as she headed towards the back where her room way. Needless to say, josh watching her as she walks in. As the door begins to close, Josh walks over too take a gander at what his brother is looking at. Josh: Hey Dani, nice view you have here. Gray turns around and slaps Josh up side the head, not knowing what Josh was actually talking about. Josh OW! What was that for? Gray: like you don't know... Dani yelling from behind a semi-shut bedroom door..... Dani: You know, I am glad you two didn't mind coming tonight. Just thought it would be fun going out and seeing a little of Vegas. Before the show this week. I've also got tickets to a couple of the concerts at the Beach this weekend. Gray: Glad your taking the lady's Advice, and having a little fun. To much business isn't good for you. You have to learn to relax more. Dani: what do you think I did today. Spent three hours at the beach. And another hour with the spa getting a facial and some other work. I can see why the lady got the room for me here. Surprised her cousin didn't pull strings to get me to stay on the reservation up north. After all he is at least a half breed, who isn't embarrassed of his roots. Then again look at the lady herself and her husband. Talking about a interesting mix. Gray stands there, his hands at a 'parade rest' position in the small of his back, looking out the window and speaking without turning towards the room. He does look to be trying to keep his younger sibling in his peripheral vision here. Gray: Yes, I know, but we don't speak of those things. So you have any Idea of what your going to be facing this week? Dani chuckles at the comment. Dani: What I'll be facing ? Gray: You know: Grappler, kicker, high flying, garbage style? You've got to be prepared for anything. But usually you can find out most about them and concentrate a bit. Dani: Oh, ahhh... I think her file is there on the desk under my journal. Just don't close it yet, it isn't dry. Gray: You know; there is such a thing called INK PENS. You don't have to wait for them to dry and, they don't make half the mess. Dani: Duh... But if you look that isn't a normal journal either. Hand made paper, hand made leather jacket, so why not use hand made ink and pen. More fitting I think and more economical. Don't you think so too? Gray moves her journal, gently, and then picks up the file and starts to read it.. Gray: Looks to be pretty rounded. Rookie to a point, but you'll have to watch for those feet. Karate trained, gymnast. Nice mix. So she will be like a cat light on her feet, any throws she most likely will roll out of and come to her feet quickly. Dani: Oh yeah, it's Gymnast Barbie!!!! let me guess she did cheer leading also. Gray: Gymnastics, dance, cheer leading... typical arrangement. But most of that was in her school years. She did competition in gymnastics though. Dani: Well, it's not like I'm stuck on the mat, either. I actually prefer a little bit of aerial action all over. Much more fun and the crowd likes the spotty stuff anyway. Dani comes out dressed in a tight form fitting tea length dress, Rose pink in color, with matching shoes and purse. Her hair is pulled up in a French braid that would suggest the reason she took so long in getting ready. Josh misses his mouth with the bottle of water he is drinking, spilling it down the front of him. As he quickly recovers and tries to play it off. Dani: Nice to know I look that good, Josh. Towels are in the bathroom, that way. Josh looks dumbfounded with her direct statement. Then again, usually Dani doesn't bother 'putting on the nines' as it is. He moves towards the bathroom as Dani just smiles at him. At that a knock on the door.... Dani: Come on in Kris everyone is waiting for you. The lock clicks as Chris walks in dressed in a black silk pants suit. Dani: What's this? Am I the only one that doesn't think we are going to a funeral? The three of them laugh as Dani stands out of the crowd in her pink. Kris: Black is always good. But believe me, honey, you picked good on the pink. Especially with that hair. Kris gives a flourish in her direction, betraying his own feminine side. Wait, her agent doesn't hide the fact that he doesn't play well with women in a personal sense. Dani: Yeah, but now I look like something being paraded around, with three bodyguards. Gray: And your point is? Before Dani can answer, her room phone rings.... Josh picks it up... Josh: Yo!... Yes, this is Ms. Northsky's room... Yes, I know her... Thank you sir we will be right down. Dinner reservations ready for us also? Yes, 4 for dinner at Trattoria Del Lupo before we head to the show. Thank you. Dani: Since when are we going to dinner. Josh: Since I found out the plan of coming here, and was able to adjust for the second show and dinner at Wolfgang Puck's resturant, right before. Dani: You're getting sneaky Josh, been hanging around this old wolf here and you are learning bad habits. Gray moves in and offers Danielle his arm as they go to head out. But, quickly Kris pulls him back and nods towards Josh who is already opening the door for her. Josh: Shall we go? Dani grins widely as she walks through the door. ~End... for now~
