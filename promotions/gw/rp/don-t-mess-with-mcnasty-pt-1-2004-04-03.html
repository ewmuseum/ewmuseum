Don't mess with McNasty (Pt 1)

3 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04-03-04 It's a very busy night at the Drunken Leperchaun, it's dollar night. Which means all drinks, and all food is one dollar. It's quite the bargain especially for those guys who are cheap when it comes to their women. McNasty decided tonight was a good night to not work at the bar but enjoy a few drinks and try to relax, especially with what happened last Monday night in Rome and also what is about to happen this upcoming Monday night on Evolution. McNasty gets another chance to team up with Brick, to have another shot at Train and Mark Makonie. Things are going to heat up on Evolution. McNasty is at the bar right now chugging down a large glass of Guinness, a somewhat large man sits down next to McNasty and begins to talk to him... 'So you're the Nomad Donny J McNasty who I seen on Pay Per View last monday night.' McNasty looks at him with slight smile, thinking that this guy is a fan. 'Yeah that's me alright, you're name?' 'My name is Justin, I just started watching Global Wrestling not that long ago.' 'Oh yeah, you like what you see?' 'Yeah it's pretty interesting so far, there are some real athletes in your company' 'Well you have to be a good athlete to really take a beating like we do week in and week out' 'What do you think about that Train guy?' McNasty turns to him with a somewhat dirty look on his face as it almost seems like a loaded question. 'Well it depends how look at it. On one hand he's one hell of an Athlete, then on the other side of things, he's a real asshole. Personally I hate his fucking guts with a passion.' Justin looks at McNasty with a strange look of his own. 'I can see why you hate him, he seems to have your number since the first time you guys locked up in the ring.' 'Seems to is the key phrase there. I wouldn't exactly say that I can't beat him. It's not all about skill, it's about Oppurtunity.' 'Maybe he has more skill than you' McNasty has a angry look coming across his face, as if Justin is barking up the wrong tree, which isn't going to be good for this apparent fan of Train. 'You really haven't been watching long enough have you. He doesn't have talent, he's just been lucky.' 'What ever you say' McNasty is clenching his right fist if Justin says one more thing it's lights out for him. 'Justin look I'll only warn you once, if I were you keep your mouth shut.' 'What ever, you're a pussy, I seen you fight. You can't hurt me, you fucking spud eating drunk' McNasty gets off his stool. 'Stand up for a second' Justin gets up looking at McNasty with a cocky look on his face. Without thinking twice, McNasty punches Justin right in the jaw sending flying back onto a table and knocking everything off it. McNasty picks up the fallen man and proceeds to hip toss him literally out the front window. Glass has shattered and fallen everywhere inside and outside. McNasty looks at what he has done and smiles and addresses the whole bar. 'Hey everyone, your attention for a moment. This is my only real rule here in the Drunken Leperchaun, Don't mess with me Donny J McNasty, or you'll end up like that jackass. I know it's only 12:30 at night, but it's last call, I need to clean up this mess because of that asshole, but the good news is I'll just forget about your tabs. Have a good evening and drive home safely.' Justin the man outside is faintly heard saying 'I'll be calling the Police McNasty, I am going to sue your ass.' It's not going to do Justin any good calling the Police on McNasty, he's good friends with all the officers at the Altoona Police Department. Some people will never learn not to mess with McNasty.
