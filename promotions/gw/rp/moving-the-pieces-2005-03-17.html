17 March 2005

Date: 3/17/2005 Jake knew there more going on with his brother than he had originally believed. He hoped there was something to be gleamed. Maybe he was wrong? Jake slipped on a pair of blue jeans, some slides and changed into a tee shirt. He picked up his wallet and the room’s keycard as he headed to the door. The Big Dawg stepped out, letting the door slam behind him. One of the hotel perks was the all night “Sundry” shop as they called it. The shop offered everything the weary traveler could need like toothpaste, shampoo or any other small item. Luckily, they also had an all night convenience store that had snacks and drinks. He needed to relieve his mind. Maybe everything was hitting him too hard in light of everything else that had occurred. He realized he had been too wrapped up in his life before to see everything for what it had been. Jake was a selfish man, with no wife and no children in his life. After he left the spotlight he realized what had been missing from his life. The return to the Hix Family Ranch had assisted him in finding something again. Even Pitt had made the comment one day, “You’re always going to be arrogant, but lately you don’t seem like a cocky prick. That’s improvement.” Surprisingly, that summarized everything for him. He found some peace in his life in the solitude, and began growing as a man without the spotlight. Training the young wrestlers was where no one else in the family came close to his talent. He was happy. Then she came along. Caren was a good woman. She hadn’t taken any of the lines of crap he spewed and fallen for them, instead calling him on every little thing he tried to pull. Her obstinate nature had been what won him over more than anything else. They had begun dating. Everything was great until? The images of flames raced through his head. FIRE’PAIN’SCREAMING? He forced the thoughts from his mind. He couldn’t concentrate on those right now. He had other issues, and learning from the past was admirable. Reliving everything was not healthy, not for him. Jake walked into the room, nodding at the middle-aged man behind the counter. He went over to the cooler and pulled open the door. He grabbed a pair of Low Card Monster Energy Drinks in the baby blue and black cans. Energy through caffeine was the way he was going. There was something calling out to him. He had a hankering for something sweet, and so he checked out the candy aisle. The snickers looked good, so he picked one of those in addition to the drinks. Jake walked around to the counter, stretching to liven his muscles as he moved. The man behind the counter was giving him a look. Jake was used to the look he was receiving. He was a celebrity, but he had been out of the spotlight for a while. The man knew him and was trying to remember where he knew him from in his memory. “Aren’t you Jake Hix?” The man asked. Jake smiled, “I am. How are you doing?” “I’m good man! I’m good! You were one of my favorite wrestlers. Well, actually I liked hating you so to speak!” The man laughed. “Where have you been” After the SAWF you dropped off the face of the earth!” “I’ve been training wrestlers. Pay attention though, because I joined Global Wrestling now. You’ll be seeing a lot more of me soon enough.” Jake set the drinks and snack down. The man quickly rung him up. Jake paid by signing off to charge it to his room. “Hey, can I get an autograph?” He asked. “Sure, who do I make it out to?” Jake asked as he took a pen and piece of paper. “Darren.” “There you go.” Jake handed it over. To Darren, I’ll take you down, break you down and make you my bitch! Watch Global Wrestling! Jake “The Big Dawg” Hix Jake took his drinks and snack and returned to his hotel room. He sat back down and began the reading once again. . . . . . . . . . . . . Everything was on place. Xavier was hanging out in one of the apartments near the Pike House that had been rented out. The apartment was empty except for a table and four chairs. Xavier was sitting at the table working on his Dell laptop computer. Across town at the house Sean was getting ready to go out at the night. Sean was dressed up in black suit with a gorgeous green silk shirt. He wanted to fit in with the upper scale crowd who would be at the club. He still had a special harness on his back to conceal his weapon of choice. Carter was expecting to meet Sean that night so he could arrange to sale a large quantity of ecstasy for a rave Sean was supposed to be throwing. Carter was anxious to make the sale because it would help him clear out some of his inventory. He had already been spoken to about how much more Starkos was selling. Bertrand pocketed a nice wad of cash into the inside pocket of his light sport coat to use that evening as needed. He also used a handkerchief to pick up the white envelope he had carefully packed earlier. Xavier would keep in touch with both men through cellular phones they had purchased with cash then cloned. There were several instant messages already programmed in for standard updates and inquiries, but in the event of an emergency they would simply call Xavier. Bertrand made his way through the crowd to the kitchen where most of the liquor was always kept. Bertrand picked up a clear plastic cup and filled it with ice from the open chest. Bertrand poured some Captain Morgan Coconut Bay Rum into the cup and topped it with orange juice. Bertrand didn’t see any stirrers so he quickly mixed it with his finger. Xavier looked up and down the street as Bertrand did the same to ensure no one saw what happened. Xavier put the pistol away then grabbed Michael by the legs. Bertrand helped Xavier by grabbing the other leg. They dragged the corpse into the alley. Bertrand looked down at the dead body. He felt no shame over what had happened, and all his moral qualms seemed to disappear along with the life force flowing through Michael’s body. Bertrand reached into his pocket and pulled out the large baggie of white powder. Bertrand dumped the white powder out onto Michael’s head. The powder mixed with the blood and formed a red paste. Bertrand dropped the bag and the remainder of cocaine onto Michael’s chest. Xavier looked up the alley, “This alley will take us up to the street where I parked my car. I got a message from Sean and he’s taken out Carter.” “Already?” Bertrand was surprised by the speed. Xavier took one last look at the street, “Apparently Carter was no where near as careful as Michael here was.” Xavier and Bertrand walked up the alley quickly, but their stride and gait didn’t seemed panicked or strange. They took a right on the next street and went down two more blocks to Xavier’s Excursion. Xavier unlocked the doors with the remote and they got in. Xavier drove down the streets at the speed limit to ensure they wouldn’t be pulled over. They were on the way to the house to meet up with Sean. Xavier and Bertrand quickly moved up the stairs to the second floor. Sean was already there sitting down on one of the couches. “Hey guys, did everything go smoothly?” Sean seemed in a very strange calm. Xavier leaned against one of the support pillars, “Everything went smooth as silk. Bertrand here was calm and cool. Michael led us to another drug house where he deals out of, and on their way back I sang him a lullaby.” Sean looked up, “Where is the new drug house at?” “Right down the street from Pike House.” Bertrand spoke up. Sean thought about it, “Another drug house, huh?” “Yeah, we knew there were going to be a lot of these places spread throughout the city.” Xavier responded as he ran his hand over his head. Sean pointed at Xavier, “Well, we could make an extra stop on the way to the warehouse. Throw something special through the window, and another den of sin and debasement goes up in flames.” Xavier thought about it, “What would we throw in, a Malotov cocktail?” Bertrand thought about it, “You still have those radio detonators?” “Sure.” Sean replied. “See, there’s about six guys watching over the house. I figure if strap one of the detonators with some C-4 to it onto a filled gas can we’ll have ourselves a grown up version of the cocktail. The trashcan crashing through the window will bring the goons, and then we take them out in the initial explosion.” Bertrand was satisfied with the plan as he spoke it aloud. Sean was surprised by the reaction of his friend who had expressed doubts regarding the killing, “I like it Bertrand. I think we can accomplish that pretty easily. I have everything at the garage, and so all we have to do is rig it. Take five minutes tops.” Xavier was also in agreement with the entire plan, “What is the getaway vehicle?” “An old Bronco I picked up for cash from a not so reputable dealer. Engine runs well and it has four by four capabilities if we need it.” Sean gave the quick rundown. “Okay, who’s going into the warehouse?” Bertrand asked. Sean sighed, “I was thinking about that. Even with all our precautions if Xavier gets caught in the warehouse then he could lead back to Hargis, and that could lead to us. The possibility is remote, but it exists. So Bertrand and I need to drive in with you pulling clean-up.” “That’s paranoia Sean.” Xavier complained. “Even paranoids have enemies Xavier.” Bertrand quoted the saying. Sean concurred, “Exactly, so we need to make sure that nothing else happens. You can be right there to get us out as quickly as possible. We’re going to cause some chaos and mayhem here tonight gentlemen. Are you two ready for it?” Xavier snorted, “Are you kidding me?” Bertrand simply nodded solemnly. The brown Ford F-150 led the way down the street with the Ford Bronco right behind it. The Bronco was painted black to allow it to operate more effectively at night if needed. The two cars stopped in the middle of the street in front of the townhouse. Bertrand jumped out of the truck and walked around the front of the truck. He grabbed the red gas can out of the bed of the truck. Bertrand turned the receiver on as he walked up to the window at the front of the house. The goons had been hanging out in the back room during his earlier visit. Bertrand looked in the window to make sure no one was there. Bertrand lifted the gas can over his head. Bertrand took a step forward and threw the gas can at the window. The heavy gas can smashed through the window into the house. Bertrand was already running to the truck. He ran around and got into the passenger side of the vehicle. Sean peeled away as the truck screamed down the residential street. Xavier drove forward a little more slowly. He was looking in the home for signs that the men inside were moving around. He saw dark figures inside the home, and he flipped the switch on the remote as he drove off quickly. The fiery explosion sent shrapnel through the front rooms that ripped into the six men who came in to see what was happening. The explosion pushed through the glass windows still intact sending shards out across the street along with the front door, which was obliterated. The men were far down thee street when the neighbors came racing out into the night or began dialing 911. The distraction would actually help divert attention away from the next target they were about to hit. None of the men let their thoughts linger on the destruction the fire bomb had made of the home. The fire would rage through destroying product, home, and the bodies inside. The police would also find the dead body of Michael Starkos upon arriving at the scene to investigate. The plan for the warehouse was simple. One of the back bays had a ramp that allowed smaller vans and delivery vehicles to enter the facility. The entrance was closed off using a simple metal garage door. Sean and Bertrand switched from the truck to a converted Dodge Van at the garage on the outskirts of town. Extra armor had been added to the inside walls to protect them from possible fire from the men inside the warehouse, but there was also another reason for the extra metal inside the van. The metal would become shrapnel when the explosives were detonated inside the warehouse. Sean checked over the vehicle one final time as Bertrand changed into his assault gear. Black clothing was a requirement for stealth at night in case things went terribly wrong, and Kevlar body armor protected them from weapon fire to the chest and legs. Bertrand loaded the extra drums of fuel into the back of the van as Sean dressed, finally satisfied with the van. Xavier was also dressing in Kevlar body armor to be absolutely safe. Xavier and Sean both put their ski masks in their front vest pockets for use once they reached the warehouse. Bertrand looked back at them, “This is going to be a huge explosion. Didn’t you say there were chemicals in the building?” “Yes.” Sean tightened his straps. “They hold chemicals for shipment to and from some of the other factories. There aren’t many, but there are some.” “What will the explosion do to them?” Xavier asked as he realized where Bertrand was headed with his inquiry. Sean thought about the impacts. He knew there were volatile chemicals in the warehouse along with powerful acids. Sean dismissed their concern, “The chemicals will add to the fire, and there will be added danger to those inside. The acid will cause severe damage.” “Is it something we should be concerned about?” Xavier was worried that they may be starting a fire that would damage the entire area. “No, the fire wills till be contained.” Sean assured them, “The hours the innocent employees work are early. We will only be hurting the employees working under the illicit umbrella of the warehouse. The police will be on scene shortly after the explosion, and the final piece of the puzzle will be in place.” Bertrand spoke confidently, “Ashes to ashes, dust to dust. May the fire burn their hearts so that their souls may be cleaned of sin.” Xavier and Sean were surprised by the elegance of Bertrand’s words. They both gave him a respectful nod as the final adjustments to the body armor were made by all. Sean and Bertrand got into the van while Xavier slipped into the Bronco behind them. Sean pulled away in the van with the Bronco right behind them. The drive to the Industrial Sector took forty minutes following the speed limits posted on the various street. Xavier answered his phone when it rang, “Hello?” “Okay, we’ll bust through and then rush out as quickly as we can. I expect there to be return fire from those inside, but we should be able to handle it.” Sean said quickly. “Understood.” Xavier was calm, calmer than he expected he would have been. “I’ll be at the bottom of the ramp waiting on you to come out.” Sean hung up the phone as they turned onto the final street. Sean had the layout of the warehouse memorized. The entrance he would be busting through was on the South side of the warehouse. He would need to gun the engine to ensure they made it inside, but there shouldn’t be any crates or equipment for him to crash into. Sean would need to make sure the van made it to the center of the warehouse to guarantee the maximum effectiveness of the bomb. The danger would be in the escape back to the South entrance. Sean turned onto the street leading to the warehouse. He had driven by several times to ensure that the plan would work. Going through the rolling steel door was made easier by the fact that it was a straight shot from the end of the street to the ramp. Sean knew he needed a lot of speed to crash through the door, and the weight of the van would make it easier for him to accomplish this. Sean gunned the engine up to fifty miles per hour. He knew the ramp would rob him of some momentum when he let off the gas, but somewhere between forty and forty five miles per hour he would be able to breach the door thanks to the huge grill on the front of the van. “Get ready.” Sean said tersely to Bertrand as he put on his ski mask. Bertrand pulled straps of his racing harness tighter to help with the impact then put on his own ski mask. He made sure the safety was still engaged on the AK-47 he held in his hands. Sean had his weapons across his chest, having opted for am Ingram model sub machine gun rather than a larger fully automatic rifle. Xavier followed at a slower speed in case anything went wrong. The driving still presented one of the most dangerous aspects of the mission. If anything were to go wrong at this point the van would crash, and the resulting explosion would ruin the plans for the evening. Sean kept his hands on the wheel inn a classic ten and two positioning. Sean made the slight adjustment as he turned into he entrance for the warehouse avoiding the curb. He continued straightforward to the ramp. The high speed of travel made the van jump slightly as it proceeded up the ramp. The van scraped against the metal guardrails on the side of the ramp as it shot up to the steel door. The van slammed into the steel door with a loud screech as the metal grill on the van smashed through the steel door. The door was ripped out of the tracks it was set into. The door was carried inside along with the van into the warehouse. Sean put the vehicle in reverse and pulled back away from the steel door. Free of the door Sean turned right to head to the middle of the warehouse. Outside Xavier pulled into the parking area and turned so that the passenger side of the vehicle was facing the ramp. Xavier put his ski mask on then pulled out his pistol. He stepped out of the vehicle ready to meet any trouble that might occur during the retreat. The commotion inside the building had stopped productivity on the first sub level. The workers all looked up at the ceiling wondering what was happening. The main floor of the warehouse was actually scarcely populated on the overnight shift. Two men in the office watching television looked out to see what was going on. Both men were surprised to see a set of headlight driving down the middle of the warehouse at high speed. The van screeched to a stop in the middle of the warehouse as the two men grabbed pistols out of the desk drawer and ran outside. Sean and Bertrand were both out of the vehicle armed with their respective weapons. The two guards saw the weapons in the hands of the men and made the incorrect assumption that someone was trying to rip off the operation on the sub levels below. The two men shot wildly as they ran into the warehouse. The bullets flew over and around the van and the intruders. One of the men had a radio out and he shouted into it, “We’re being attacked! Someone’s trying to rip us off! We need help now!” Below a group of eight men grabbed weapons from a storage locker. The weapons included shotguns, M-16s and pistols. The loaded clips into the weapons and rushed out of the room to head up into the warehouse. Sean and Bertrand reacted more calmly to the situation than the guards did. Bertrand took careful aim with his rifle and squeezed off a controlled burst of rounds. The first bullet missed the guard by mere inches, but the next round slammed into the guard’s chest just below the sternum followed by one that went through the pectorals muscles on his chest into part of his heart. Sean didn’t take as careful an aim because he wanted the sound of automatic weapons fire to frighten anyone else they might not see. The Ingram had been converted to a full automatic, and the weapon cycled through half of the thirty round clip in a very short time period. The bullets ripped through the second guard’s body tearing gashing holes that blood flew out of. One bullet caught the guard in the neck, and the arterial spray that shot out seemed like a fine mist in the air. Sean emptied the rest of the clip in the general direction of the stairs and elevator to discourage anyone who might be coming up from below. He pulled the clip out and put it on an empty slot in his vest. He was not planning on leaving any evidence behind for the police to examine. Sean put in a new clip as he backed away from the van. “There’s no one! Let’s move!” Bertrand shouted at Sean. Sean agreed and they both turned and began sprinting down to the exit at the end of the warehouse. Sean and Bertrand each looked over their shoulders to see if anyone was following. Two of the guards from down below came running out of the stairway with shotguns in hand. They saw the two men running away at the end of the warehouse. “Let’s go get them!” The first man yelled. Both men began running down the warehouse after the fleeing figures. The guards were both in good shape, and they were in fast pursuit of those who had dared launch an attack against the Cartel. Sean and Bertrand ran out of the open entrance at full speed. Xavier pulled his pistol away when he saw it was his companions fleeing the scene. Xavier kept a watchful eye on the large entrance as Sean and Bertrand got into the Bronco. Xavier was about to join his friends in the vehicle, but he heard something from the entrance. Xavier laid across the warm hood of the Bronco and steadied his pistol as he took aim at the doorway. Two men came running out together each carrying a shotgun. They were moving to fast to adjust to the new circumstances they were now faced with. Xavier had careful aim on the first and put two rounds into his chest. Xavier adjusted his aim to the second man who was trying to bring his shotgun down to fire back. Before the man was able to get an aim, Xavier had squeezed off another three rounds that tore into the man’s abdomen. The guard fell to the concrete screaming in pain as he dropped his weapon. Xavier’s eyes were dark and angry as he fired two more rounds into the chest of each man. The first man was could not scream anymore as the bullets stopped his heart forever, and the second man’s screaming stopped as the next two rounds slammed into his chest and throat respectively. Only a gurgling cough escaped from the man. Xavier put his pistol into his holster and returned to the Bronco. Xavier put the car in drive and peeled away from the building at high speed. Xavier spoke forcefully, “Blow the van! Blow it now!” Bertrand pulled out the detonator from his pocket. He flipped off the hard plastic safety cover. Bertrand took a deep breath before his thumb pressed down on the red button when the Bronco was beginning to move down the street. The explosion that started in the van hit all the explosives and fuel Sean and Bertrand had loaded up. The explosives were directed down to rip through the floor and tear into the sub levels. The extra armor was turned into shrapnel that ripped through the two men that were looking around the van. The explosion was powerful enough to knock out support columns, blow out the doors of the building and tear into the sub level. What happened next was unexpected. The chemicals stored in the building were ignited and they exploded adding all sorts of colors to the fire already burning. The sub level had chemicals of its own to help process and refine the drugs being cooked. The chemicals on the second level were numerous and dangerous. The secondary explosion of the chemicals in the drug lab magnified the explosion. The fire rushing out of the building could be seen in the rearview mirror. Xavier and Sean were watching the in the mirror, but Bertrand 2was watching through the back window. The secondary explosion ripped through to the bottom level of the building and out through every opening it could find. The rush of fire and energy touched and shattered everything in its path as it looked for a path out of the confinement of the walls. The people inside were burned by the intense heat and flames. Bones were broken by the sheer force of the energy being released as the workers perished. Others found themselves shielded from the force, but the fire burned not only their skin. The people breathed in hoping to find breathable air. They only found fire that they sucked into their delicate lung tissue charring the inside of their bodies. The force finally escaped the building spreading outwards. The windows of the buildings surrounding the warehouse were shattered by outward force from inside the warehouse. The glass shrapnel spread all over the streets as the shock wave traveled out in all directions. The Bronco was pushed sideways as the force slammed into the back of the vehicle. Xavier kept control of the Sports Utility Vehicle as he increased speed to escape the explosion. Bertrand watched the fireball rushing into the night sky like the hand of a daemon reaching out for one last grasp at the world it was destroying. Bertrand felt a sense of satisfaction inside his soul. He knew the attacks of the evening,, especially this attack, would get the attention of the Cartel and further escalate the war between the factions. This is what they wanted. They all removed their ski masks and gear. The equipment went into a cardboard box in the backseat next to Bertrand. Xavier, Sean and Bertrand all drove in silence as they returned to the garage. Xavier pulled the Bronco into the parking lot of the garage. Bertrand jumped out of the vehicle and opened up the garage door for them. Xavier pulled inside the bay and waited for Bertrand to close the door. They all got out of the vehicle. Xavier clapped Sean on the shoulder, “That was one Hell of an explosion you gave us Sean.” “Amazing the destruction that came from it.” Bertrand spoke softly. Sean laughed, “Well, people should never keep volatile, flammable substances such as those around without the proper safety precautions.” “I’m going back to the house for a good night’s sleep before heading down to Laredo for the next part of the operation.” Xavier explained to them. “I will find out when the final game is scheduled to be played, and we will all go win that last match.” They all were ready now. Ready to follow the trail to the end to win the battle, fuel the war and seek the vengeance they all wanted.
