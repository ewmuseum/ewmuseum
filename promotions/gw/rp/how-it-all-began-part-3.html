How it all began. (part 3)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'The darkness which once consumed my life, now controls it. The Void which I once sought refuge in now has more of me than any could believe. Even the master struggles to understand how I have lived this far.' - Salaok Davis ---- 1995 It had been only a few short days since Salaok had accidently killed that young man in the allyway but the idea of it had been running rampant through him mind ever since. The power over someone's life was a power so strong that most men were unable to control it. Some would go insane. Some would struggle with the guilt. Some would enter into a state of denial. However Salaok seemed to embrace it more than anything else as he and those around him watched a momumental change take place within the young man. The power of that death would consume Salaok, but he would be able to keep it under control, or at least he did until the night where he had to do it all over again. However, this time it would be paid for and Salaok would become a very wealthy man for a task that seemed very little now. As Salaok stood behind the door of the man's apartment waiting for him to enter, he wanted nothing more than to light up a cigarette but he knew that his mark didn't smoke and would smell it as soon as he walked through the door. Salaok stood there. Silent. Waiting and knowing that in a few seconds those footsteps would stop and begin to unlock the door. A grin falls over his face as it begins to happen just as he had expected and the door slowly creaks open and he was in. The door had only just shut when Salaok reached out and grabbed the man's head, and twisting it around until the neck snapped like a tig begins stepped on. The cracking of the neck was followed soon by the heavy thud of the body dropping down onto the ground at Salaok's feet. It almost seemed to easy to Salaok. An easy One Million dollars and it took less than a second to complete. Although late in the night, Salaok makes the call to Jeremy to let him know the task had been done and picks up the body, although not completely. The man's head was facing the opposite direction to the body which amused Salaok slightly as thoughts of the movie 'The Exorcist' popped into his head and he kind of hoped that the green projectile vomet thing would happen just for laughs. A man had just died in his heads and Salaok was thinking about having a laugh? A massive change to the reaction he had at the hands of the young man's death a few days earlier. Was it possible for Salaok to have changed that much in so short a time? Was it change, or a realization that this was who he was all along? ---- 1999Four years had pasted before his first and second kills and many many things had changed. Not only with Salaok, but with the world as well as death and destruction seemed to be all over the place at this point in time. The Columbine shootings had unfolded only a few weeks ago and the world that is America was still in shock. Was anyone safe these days from the world that was controlled by darkness? Salaok had watched the news that fatal night in confusion about the world in which he lived in, much like so many people did. However, the only difference was that as he watched the news that night, he was on the phone taking orders for his next hit. And that hit would take place tonight. The room is dark and the only thing heard in the room other than Salaok's footsteps is the cries of the broken man who knew what was coming to him. 'Listen Salaok, it wasn't my fault, really!' The man knew Salaok, just as Salaok knew him. When business deals go wrong because someone's words isn't worth the amount of money in which has been placed on it, it tends to cause problems. In this case, the problem would be a loss of a lot of The Lair's money, and the result would be the death of Eric Wynveen. The sale of the business from Wynveen to The Lair was one that was meant to go without a hitch, however it didn't turn out that way as the other half owner decided to reject the money and want to keep the business. This just wasn't on. The business was the only one left in the strip of shops that The Lair needed in order to take a new delevopment into the next millenium and one man stood in the way of it. The solution at this point in time would seem somewhat easy as the business partner needed to disappear and this happened without a problem. In fact it had been Quartz who had taken care of the first half of this business deal which would see the poor man seeming kill himself. The means of the death gave no indication of foul play as he seeming hang himself from the balcony of his lake side house. No one had asked any questions about his death as everyone knew that there had been issues with the man's love life as the wife had found out he was having an affair. An affair which the Lair had set up. You see, when it comes to getting what they want, there is no level in which The Lair will not go to. Simply killing you isn't always the answer with this group of people but in the end that would normally be what it all came down to. But with his death the business was passed over to the wife who in turned sold it straight away silently to a group called Omni-tech. Omni-tech was a group that seemed detirmined to destroy The Lair's control over certain fields of crime and although weren't completely destroying the rule of the Lair, they were becoming somewhat of an annoyance. But this brings us back to Eric as The Lair had become furious with their failed attempt and saw that there was only one person in whom to take out their frustration on, being him. There would have been no point in harming the wife ofthe deceased man as she was already hurting enough from the death of her husband. No, this would be Eric's turn to learn what happens when you promise that which you can not give despite selling his half of the business to The Lair. 'Here is how it's going to happen Eric. You lay there and I kill you, hows that?' 'Salaok, please, I'll do anything.' Salaok chuckles to himself as he walks across the room into the light for Eric to see the tool in which he carried and the sight of it makes Eric's jaw drop and tears begin to uncontrollably drop from his eyes. 'Would you please just shut the fuck up Eric? You had your chance to come good with The Lair and you messed up. You promised that which you had no right to promise and now we own half of a fucking business with Omni-tech. The one group of people who will make sure we never get the fucking thing.' Salaok's tone is filled with nothing but spite as the idea of having to put up with more conflicts with Omni-tech was simply irritating. Stepping right up to Eric, who is strapped to a table which looks much like something from a sixteenth century dungeon. The entire room in fact looked very much like that which is the exact look Salaok was going for when he decided to turn his basement into a room of pain. It's not something that everyone does to their home, but Salaok wasn't like most other people as we all know so well. How many other people would buy a giant castle on the outskirts of a town just so he would have a place big enough to sleep almost the entire Lair? Salaok had indeed done well out of his assassinations but this one he was doing tonight had nothing to do with money, infact, the last few did have. He had enough money now to not worry about being paid to do a hit, as the money from the many businesses in which he was tied up in gave him more than enough to let him get by in his expensive way of life. No, this hit tonight on Eric was purely for the satisfaction of revenge and therefore the manner in which the death was about to come about was going to display that. In full light now, Salaok stands next to Eric and pulls the jagged knife up to Eric's nose and holds it there in front of his eyes for him to see clearly the object which would end up being the curtain down on his life. 'Where would you like the first cut Eric?' Eric can't answer as his mouth moves but only silence comes out as he chokes on his tears. But with Eric's mouth open, Salaok rams his fist into his mouth, breaking teeth as his hand enters and grabs Eric's tongue. The screams of pain due to the breaking of several teeth are muffled by Salaok's hand taking up much of the room in his mouth as he pulls Eric's tongue out and slowly runs the knife over it. Eric's eyes are wide as he watches as the knife gets closer and closer to actually making contact with his tongue and then it happens. Gripping the knife, Salaok plunges it into the soft red flesh of Eric's tongue and cuts it straight down the middle, starting from the back and pulling all the way to the front. As blood pours forth much like a water-feature in someone's garden with red food dye in it. The crimson wave flows down over his chin and begins to soak into his shirt as the two halves of his tongue flap around while he screams out to anyone who might possibly hear him. It is useless though as the only one's who would ever hear him are the ones in whom want him dead for in the rooms above them were more than half of The Lair waiting to see the result of the hit. Salaok stands there with knife in hand and blood all over him as he stops for a second to look into the painfilled eyes and think of what next to do. However it doesn't take long for another idea to pop into Salaok mind as he had done almost everything before at some point in time and slowly the knife is pulled up to the middle of Eric's eyebrows. As the blade begins to gid into the flesh, Salaok drags the knife backwards up to the top of Eric's head and then drags it down the back to the bottom of the skull. At first there is only a small trickle of blood which keeps Salaok happy, knowing that he doesn't want to finish that part just yet and so moves to the other end of the table where he is able to look up at Eric as his body shakes in pain and fear. Placing the knife down on the table for a moment, Salaok grabs the bare feet of Eric holding all ten of his toes in his hands before starting to laugh again. 'This little piggy went to the market, ad this little piggy stayed home.' Salaok begun as he mocked Wynveen. 'You're a sick fuck Salaok!'A voice came from the door of the basement which had been left open and people could hear what was going on. Those above knew exactly what Salaok was about to do and they waited in silence for the screams to follow. With all ten of Eric's toes in his hands, Salaok with a massive burst of speed and power bends all of the toes backwards snapping each and every one of them. Although his tongue extremely damaged and only just holding onto consciousness, Eric lets out a muffled scream bringing laughter to those listening above which serves to only add more insult to injury for the man who had promised that which he could never deliver. The stories of this night would most surely get out and thus increase the reputation, not only of Salaok, but of what happens when you cross The Lair. For only they were a great power in the underworld crime screen, there were still those who dared to stand against them but with every hit, there seemed to be less people willing to do just just. It had been almost a full year since the last hit was done on a member of The Lair which proved to them that people were beginning to understand that trying to defeat them was pointless. As that hit which had killed a member of The Lair ended up in the deaths of eight people, one of which was a close friend and contractor to The Lair but it needed to be done. Killing someone wasn't something that was taken lightly and was only done when it was absolutely needed which in turn asks the question, was tonight really needed? Of course it was. Needed for their reputation to be boosted and for Salaok to gain revenge for the lose of his money. The young Salaok had moved up the ranked in The Lair fast, almost as his brother Johnathon before him. With the reactions of those on the floor above him, Salaok's mind begins to tick over as to what next to do and it didn't take long for the knife to be picked up again and driven straight through Eric's foot. The blade enters through the bottom oft he foot and shoots through the top of it sending blood flying like a fountain but that is only to beginning of this one as he grabs the handle with both hands and begins to pull upwards. The knife drags and cuts through the flesh and bone with some ease as it rips up from the middle of his foot to his toes. Much like his tongue, his foot splits into two pieces and this time the screams are more defined and last a lot longer which draws more cheers and applause from above them. Wanting to do it to the other foot as well but deciding to do something different for now as he walks up to one of Eric's strapped down hands and grabs one of his fingers, smiling all the time. Running the very tip of the knife aroundt he base of the finger, Salaok cuts right around the bottom of it which although doesn't hurt at first, what happens next would do nothing more than shoot pain straight through the entire body of his victim. With his other hand, Salaok grabs the sliced finger and then pulls upwards with all his strength which not only breaks the finger, but rips the skin straight off. The finger remains as nothing more than bloodied flesh as the skin coating rests in Salaok's hand before he stuffs it into Eric's mouth for him to taste. 'Tastes nice doesn't it Eric? You see Eric, I could keep going with this all night long if I so wished, and you know what? I think I will! But that taste Eric, it's the taste of your own flesh and blood, the very taste that Corvus would have been tasting tonight should he not have already had the contract to ordered and paid for to kill your wife!'Salaok grins as he tells Eric of the future death of his wife which snaps Eric into reality as fast as lightning with his eyes shot open and screaming out into the dead air only to have his scream soaking into his cries. 'Out of all of the nice things I could have done for you Eric, I had your wife killed on the very night that you were simply so she wouldn't have to go through the pain of your death. Only difference is Eric, she doesn't know about your death as while Corvus is sucking the life out of her, she will face the pain of thinking that you will have to deal with her death for the rest of your life. A pain that no woman should have to go through. I'm actually beginning to wonder if she's dead already?' Salaok's mocking tone is doing nothing but torment to poor man. Eric tries to talk but the first time there is no control over his split tongue however the words are nothing but gargling and so with one all mighty spit, he empties his mouth of the pool of blood and gives it another shot and this time it comes out clear anough for Salaok to hear. 'You sick bastard. At least I will see her in heaven.' His words did nothing more than amuse Salaok who stands next to him playing with the knife before responding. 'Perhaps you will Eric, but then again, perhaps she will not make it to heaven as she hasn't been a faith person.' 'LIAR!'Eric's word is sharp and it surprises Salaok that the man still has the energy and daringness to raise his voice at him. Salaok responce is nothing more than a punch to the jaw, snapping it at the connection point to the skull and ending all further speach attempts. Salaok wasn't here for a discussion, he was here to hurt and mame and torture the pathetic little man that was Eric Wynveen. Moving around to the top of Eric's head again, he grabs a handful of his short dark hair from either side of his head before leaning down and whispering into his ear. 'You know what I'm going to do now Eric? Don't worry about answering because you'll never get it right. I'm going to pull on the sides of your head as hard as possible until the cut which I made earlier seperates and the skin is pulled from your scalp. Sounds like fun doesn't it?' As Eric shakes his head, almost pleading for this not to happen, Salaok rips at the sides of his head. It doesn't happen straight away but after a few seconds, the skin begins to seperate and pull away from the skull until until it folds down to his shoulders. Blood dripping all over the place and Eric nearly ready to pass out due to the pain, Salaok laughs like a mad man which draws a favorable responce from those listening at the doorway. The mad laugh of Salaok was something that had been noted by his peers in The Lair as something that when it happens, you know a knew extreme has been reached and will be more than worth a look. It didn't take long before those at the door had made their way down the stone stairwell which travels around in circles downwards for at least a drop of twenty feet. And they stop once they've reached the bottom of the stairs and look on with curiousity at what Salaok had done and the picture is worth a thousand words. The image of the foot was disturbing enough as the two halfs of it hang loosely and seem to swap with each flinch of pain, much like his tongue and then they see the man's head which Salaok stands behind. 'Nice huh fellas?' One of the men at the bottom of the stairwell was Salaok's former minder, in Jeremy who simply stood there with his arms folded over his chest and a grin on his face, trying his best to show Salaok that is was somewhat amused by what he had done. Suddenly the booming voice of Quartz sounds from above calling those who had gone downstairs to get back up to the floor above. Although only still fairly young, Quartz was higher in the ranks than most and was apart of the then little known Inner Circle of The Lair which controlled most of the happenings of the group. His word was final and everyone knew that but then his voice booms out again, this time aimed at one man alone. 'Salaok, you've got five minutes to finish up down there before I come down and do it myself.' His voice was one of pure power and strength which matched his figure completely because standing well over seven feet tall, Quartz was more of a monster than any other man. And although a good friend of Salaok's, he was also the one who was pushing him into that gray area between sane and insane which is where Quartz needed him to be in order for some of the jobs to be completeled. Hearing the tone in Quartz's voice, Salaok knew that he was serious ad so began to think as to how this would all end. Meanwhile Eric had also heard the voice of Quartz sound out and in turn knew he had now less than five minutes of his life left to live. Not that being strapped to a table waiting to die would be anyway of living your final minutes. The silence in the room is suddenly turned upside-down as Salaok cell phone rings and despite the situation in which he is in, he decides to answer it know full well who it is going to be. 'Corvus, I was just talking to Eric about you!' Eric hearing who is on the phone breaks down into tears again as Salaok responds to the voice on the other end. 'Done already? Guess I had better finish up here then. Payment will be waiting when you get here.' Salaok hangs up the cell and then returns his attention back to Eric who has tears streeming down his face knowing that his wife is gone. Touching the knife to his face as he thinks for a brief second, he wipes the blood of Eric on his skin and then begins to grin as the finishing touch enters his mind. 'I take it you know what I have to do now don't you Eric?' Regretfully, Eric nods and it's the last movement he willfully makes as Salaok turns the blade upside and grabs the blade in both hands readying himself for what he's about to do and the strength that he's going to need in order to do it. With great force, Salaok slams the knife down, straight into the middle of Eric's head, smashing straight through the rock hard skull. Blood spurts out everywhere as the knife digs deeper and deeper into the brain as Eric's body begins to convulse and his jaw opens and shuts rapidly with no sound being emitted. He pushes the knife down further until finally the handled slams against the skull. With a look of pure pleasure on his face, his eyes wide open and a grin stretching from ear to ear, Salaok stares straight ahead looking into the nothingness which he had learned to control. Staring face to face with his own Void. With one last violent movement, Salaok twists the knife around with both hands and brings an end to any and all movement from Eric. The job was done. But was Salaok ever meant to have received such pleaure from it? Such pleasure from the death of another human being? No. This state of mind that Quartz had seen Salaok enter had been happening more and more these days and it was bordering the line the shouldn't be cross. As Salaok walks away from the body, leaving the knife sticking out of Eric's head, he hads upstairs to see the others. Around and around the stone stairwell he travels, not fast and yet not slow. Mechanical more than anything. As he reaches the top of the stairs, people stop what they are doing and look at him. The eyes, the smile, the insanity. With his clothes and face covered in blood, Salaok walks into the room, grabs a glass of water from off the bench and sits himself down in front of the TV as if nothing had just happened. ---- Present day After offering a spot in The Nevermore, the smaller faction of The Lair to Corvus, Salaok was surprised to have his offer declined. But not only did his long time contractor decide to not join with Salaok, but turned against him making a mistake that would end up costing him not only future jobs through The Lair, but in turn would see him marked by The Lair itself. Although not the power it once used to be, The Lair still controls over fifty percent of the businesses which have kept assassins like Corvus, Jackson Diasporo, Silent Wind and John Zero rich enough to do what they want with their lives. It had been a long time since Salaok had been given a hit to perform, but he would sign this contract out to himself this time. Many things had changed since Corvus had first began to work for The Lair, the major thing was the fact that Salaok was now the man in charge with Johnathon and Quartz as his equals. Three men who had dominated the scene for so long would now be the ones who takes The Lair into the next generation. A generation which Salaok has already vowed will cause growth to the group, and pain to those who have chosen to stand against it. Salaok sits on the second bottom stair of the massive staircase which leads up to the second floor of the castle with the phone to his ear as it rings but knowing the man who owned the other end of the phone wouldn't be picking up as he was away on 'business'. The answering service kicks in and Salaok is more than happy to leave his old contractor a little message for the future. 'Corvus old friend, you made a mistake the other night and for that you will pay. When you meet me in my basement, it will not be a match, but a hit which will take place. I will be waiting.' Salaok puts the phone back on the reciever as his face breaks into a grin from ear to ear and his eyes open wide, staring off into the nothing which is his own.
