Purple - The Doctor is In (Sub Saga P.1)

30 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03.30.04 'An insincere and evil friend is more to be feared than a wild beast; a wild beast may wound your body, but an evil friend will wound your mind.' -Buddha March 20th, 2004 - Approximately 12:45 PM New York, New York - The Echelon Tower The scene opens to a room lit by purple and blue lights, giving everything a purple or dark blue tint. The black walls remain dark and deep however. The floors have been recently polished and the columns, recently re-sculpted. Amongst the beauty of their creation, ivy has been carved into the marble, creating solid marble ivy and vines encircling and tangling all the way up the beautiful structures. The large semi-circular desk that is commonly seen within these office walls has been placed nearby, a large window overlooking New York is behind it. Seated at the desk is Maverick Reno in a black silk shirt, dress pants and with his hair tied back. He has one elbow on the desk and his head held up by the same arm and the hand there of. His other hand is busy, drumming his fingers on the table. A blue light is visible on the phone, representing that it is both on and that speaker phone is on. A voice is heard through the speaker, demanding authority. It is the voice of old friend, Stephen Perdue - one of Maverick Reno's most trusted advisors, business partners, and supporter. Today, his support seems to run thin. 'Maverick, I just don't get it. Why did you have to do that? Why did you have to ruin three more lives?' The tone is not pleasant and it doesn't seem to be sitting well with Reno. 'Stephen, I am a man of little patience in recent times. It would be wise for you to remember that before someone breaks your door down.' 'Your threats don't scare me, Maverick. You know that I have enough information to bring down everything you've worked for!' 'Now who is threatening whom, Stephen? You call me because of a news report you hear and you 'think' that it might have been me. For all you know, it could be another of John's many enemies. He was not a well thought of man, Stephen. In his later years, he has done much for the area but for many decades before that, he was quite the barbarian both in the ring and in business and his personal life. Hell, it could have been Chenzo Stecchino getting him back for double crossing Anthony. Why do you call me, harassing me, threatening my life, my business... my innocence will speak for itself!' There's a pause now, Reno's temperament rising as he stands and clasps his hands behind his back, slowly pacing his desk. He demands for Stephen to respond... 'SPEAK, STEPHEN! Clarify yourself.' 'Maverick... you've changed so much. I don't even know you anymore. I... I want to know what has happened? What's come over you and why are you doing this?' Maverick stops and clenches his eyes shut for just a moment, he speaks again so that the microphone can pick him up. 'My concerns, Stephen. All of them. Now you tell me why you call here and accuse me of such atrocities!' 'I've contacted New York authorities, Maverick. I'm having your entire organization investigated; I'm sure that, as acting CEO of Reno Enterprises, I'll be a credible witness.' Reno stops walking, his eyes slowly turning towards the phone. His eyes glaze as thoughts pulse through his mind, streaming from one topic to the next with no pattern or method, no sense of stratification or even a glimmer of reason. Stephen is still talking via the speaker, his words mumbled and meshed in with the myriad of thoughts mingling about the tangled mind of Maverick Reno. A beat and another passes. It's Reno's heart, beating faster and louder, stronger and with more force than ever before. 'Maverick?' Reno does not hear his friends words, his eyes completely glazed as he loses himself in his own thoughts. 'Maverick!” Again, Reno does not respond. Suddenly, all thoughts die and nothing is heard; everything is muffled. Before him, a room covered in blood and bodies, pain and suffering forever etched onto the walls of this once well kept haven from the outside world. Seated in a corner, bleeding from almost every orifice, his legs broken and crippled, and his body so badly bruised that his skin tone could be either that of a Caucasian, or an alien of black and purple pigmentation. Scott Pierce is seated here, not thinking of his own pain but of the pain of his family who lie before him, dead and deceased, mangled and destroyed. His life comparable. Suddenly, the images change drastically and snap back to reality; Reno is standing in his office, his eyes wild with sweat running down his face and forehead. 'MAVERICK!' 'I hear you, Stephen. I'm going to ask you to hang up now before this gets any more heated.' There’s a pause before Perdue responds. 'It's too late, Maverick. You have to be stopped... I... I've never seen something this horrid. You're on their level at the lowest. You used to be a savior, an angel of morals, but now what are you? Your heart is black... I fear you, Maverick.' Reno closes his eyes slowly and speaks, his words forming carefully. 'True friends stab you in the front. Oscar Wilde said that knowing that he spoke the truth. You do what you have to do, Stephen. But keep in mind that it is not me that you risk, but someone much closer to you.' Reno presses the button and sighs, his eyes now glazing again. He leans over his desk and opens a drawer on the opposite side; from there, he pulls a list and places it on his desk, facing him. Names on the list are familiar but distant and only a few we recognize. The names are written in black but some have a red slash through them. Scott Pierce Kyle Pierce Tyler Pierce John Zero Deacon Holmes Leinad Soma Sean Price Luke Price Jason Hughes Ed Rexx Seth Buchanan Anthony Stecchino - A red circle and slash adorn this name. Chenzo Stecchino J. Pape Ichiro Nakazura - A red X is beside the name. Jushin Nakazura - Yet another red x Chris Koch Brock Gibbs Mina Akari [name withheld] Trinity Russell - This name is complete with a red slash John Russell - His name has been circled in red, an X has been marked through. Sean O'Riley Stephen Perdue - nothing rests beside or through his name. Naria Xiou Reno draws a red pen from the same drawer and with a very heavy sigh, marks an X next to Stephen Perdue's name. 'It's a shame when this type of treachery takes footing. You lose more this way than in any war.' Reno stretches a bit as he picks up the pad, his mind beginning to slowly wander again. He paces to the opposite side of his desk and replaces the notepad and pen before closing and locking the desk again. The phone rings again and Reno picks it up and quickly says his greetings, more of an answer however. 'Yes, Naria?' There’s a moment of incomplete silence as the sound of gibberish emit from the receiver. An eyebrow goes up and Reno speaks quickly and calmly. 'Dr. Purgason? Ryan Purgason?' Again, another pause as Naria speaks into her receiver. 'Yes, allow him up.' Reno hangs the phone up and leans back in his desk, popping his neck with such vivacity that it sends a violent quiver all the way down his spine and left arm. The other side, the pop and crack almost sickening. 'Ryan. The entrancing scent of rotten deeds fill the air, Ryan. I do hope that my nostrils deceive me.' A moment passes and a knock comes at Reno's double doors. Maverick smiles and stands, slowly approaching the chamber door. 'And there came a tapping, a rap-tap tapping, a tapping at my chamber door. Tis some visitor entreating entrance at my chamber door, tis this, and nothing more.' A single door opens slowly and Naria Xiou steps against it with her back, allowing a man of Reno's age into the room. He's dressed in black dress pants, a white shirt and a black tie. He holds his arms out and smiles. This man is as clean cut as they come. 'Eric!' 'Long time no see, Ryan.' The two men quickly shake hands followed by a brief embrace. A distance is put between them quickly before they continue their conversation. Reno shakes his head. 'Same ol' Ryan. How's life been treating you?' 'Not bad, not bad. I'd ask you but...' Ryan pauses in his sentence to look around, his eyes feasting on the beauty that is Reno's office, a pinnacle of modern architecture, 'I'd say the walls can speak in this situation.' Reno laughs a bit and glances around, he lifts a hand and lets his finger tips trail down one of the engraved columns, 'Life's been treating me well in this area, you could say.' Ryan glances away from the beautiful room and to Reno for a moment, curious of his last comment. 'This area? How about the others?' Reno glances at Ryan, trying not to seem suspicious as he speaks, 'Not too bad really. But let's not talk about me, Ryan. Let's talk about you.' Ryan's eyes shift slightly as he looks around. Naria is still standing in the doorway, completely silent as she observes. 'Perhaps we could grab a bite to eat? I've not had the opportunity yet.' There's a long pause before Reno speaks, his mind searching the situation for errors. 'Or... are you busy? Is this a bad time?' Reno snaps out of it and shakes his head. 'No no, of course not. Come... we have a small bistro on level three. We'll dine there.' With this, Naria turns and begins to lead the way down the curving hallway lined with doors. 'So tell me, what've you been doing since college?' Reno's voice is calm and collected. 'As little as possible. First I worked in a psychiatric ward in upstate for awhile before getting my own office and opening a little practice.' Reno nods, clasping his hands behind his back. 'Wish I had done something like that.' Ryan glances around and then back as they stop, Naria pressing the console and waiting for the elevator. 'Not sure I could say why you'd want to. I mean, look at this place. You live on the lap of luxury; hell, I think you may have found a new lap!' Reno laughs slightly as the elevator doors open. The three slowly step inside, the conversation continuing. 'It's not all mai-tai's and Yahtzee, Ryan.' The elevator is quick to reach its destination. The conversation continuing as the doors open and the three step out. 'Really? You live, from what I've heard, on an estate in Canada. You founded this company and own Reno Enterprises. You've got it all, Mav. What's not easy?' Reno is silent as they walk now, Naria leading the way. Finally she turns and opens a door and both men walk in slowly. The room is much different from the rest of the building that we've seen. First Maverick walks inside, quickly followed by his companion. The room is very atmospheric; a lattice hangs overhead with greenery mingling in and out of the criss-crossing wood. The walls are white but covered in the same lattice, lacking the greenery. Small cafe tables are scattered about the room. A swinging door is located against one wall. A bar comes out in the back of the room, creating another area for people to sit. Above the bar is a television which is currently off. Maverick points to the bar as he continues walking, still silent. Naria disappears, closing the door to the miniature bistro behind her. Ryan walks nonchalantly to the bar, his eyes wandering. 'You have everything in this building.' Ryan's tone is light but serious, trying to change the subject from what obviously upset Maverick just a few minutes earlier. Reno walks around the bar to where everything is stored. He speaks as he rinses his hands, his eyes not lifting from the sink. 'Everything but peace of mind, Ryan.' Again, silence fills the room. Reno quickly gets two glasses and then stops, his eyes falling on the two cylindrical objects. 'What's your poison?' 'I'll take a little Goose if you've got it?' Reno nods and kneels down. He reaches underneath the bar in a small crevice and retrieves a key to unlock the small cabinet. 'What's your poison, Maverick?' The words fall upon the ears of Reno with perplexion following. 'Probably a Chardonnay. But the drinks are my concern, yes?' Reno rises now, two bottles in hand. A 1863 Chardonnay and a new bottle of the expensive Grey Goose. 'No. What plagues your mind? You haven't been straight with me, Mav. Something's bothering you and you obviously need to talk about it.' Ryan is blunt and to the point, the words piercing Reno like an arrow poised to kill. Reno continues to pour the glasses. He places one before Ryan and then downs his own before pouring a second glass. He slowly walks around the bar, taking a seat aside Ryan. Ryan's voice is one filled with concern, his eyes focusing on Maverick. 'Are you going to tell me, Maverick? You know I'm not going to take no for an answer and you obviously need to talk about something.' 'I.' Reno stops as a man in black pants, a red shirt, and a tie walks to the bar. He steps behind it and places a pair of menus before the two men. Reno waives his off however and the man withdraws it. Ryan glances at the menu and then at Maverick. 'Do you need a little more time, Mr. Reno?' The waiter is polite and nearly perfect in his demeanor. 'Yes. Just a few more minutes.' Reno watches now as the man walks back through the swinging doors. His eyes return to Ryan. 'You're right about a few things. I won't deny that I have a plague upon my mind; one that's been haunting me for weeks now. But after so many years, you expect me to simply spill my thoughts into an easy to carry bag for you? So that you can analyze me and judge me?' Maverick has gone on the offensive, his eyes glazing and his mind pouring with thoughts of betrayal and hatred. Ryan shakes his head no, speaking in a caring tone. 'Maver... Eric. We've known each other for something near a decade now. I'm the one who first started calling you Maverick. We know each other well; hell, if it weren't for you, I probably wouldn't be who I am. I'm not trying to analyze you or judge you. I'm speaking as a friend and a confidant; I want to know what burdens rest upon your shoulders? Let me help you.' Reno slowly looks down, his eyes glazed still. He clenches them shut and tries to shake off the feelings of mistrust. They're not going anywhere however. With a fluid movement, he grabs his glass and takes a bit into his mouth, letting the flavor absorb before swallowing, the motion soothing him somewhat. He speaks softly, 'Let us enjoy out lunch and then, perhaps we'll speak.' Ryan nods and fidgets with the menu before letting it rest in his hand, his eyes still focused elsewhere. 'I want to help you...' The scene slowly fades as the waiter reappears from the swinging doors.
