The History of Max Roswell Part 3

22 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Feb 22 10 years ago... “Come back Max!” my friend Tony was yelling to me. I was running down the street to see how long it took me to get there and back. Before I new it I was running across the street into the open traffic. My friend started running after me to try and stop me. But I was already on the next block nearly hitting an old lady coming out of the local Subway. I finally stopped at a dumpster to take my breath. My friend barely made it without collapsing on the ground. He has asthma so he cant run very fast. “What were you doing?” He said to me. “I couldn’t stop, so I kept on going.” I said back. Me and my friend started walking back to my house. Me and Tony had been friends since I moved here a couple years ago. We weren’t just normal friends. We had a special bond together. We would go everywhere with each other. We have been to concerts, wrestling shows, everything you could think of. We both loved wrestling. My mom wasn’t really into it but she never really told me that but I could see it. But I didn’t care, I would always love it. Me and him walked into my front door and into the kitchen hoping my mom had something for us to eat. “Mom, can we have something to eat?” I yelled to my mother who was standing in the living room. “Sure, there are some chips in the cupboard and some Pepsi in the fridge.” she yelled back to me. I walked over to the cupboard and opened it. The doors squeaked when I opened them but stopped when I did. I grabbed a bag of Ketchup chips and tossed them to my friend. He caught them with one hand and opened stuffing his face with a handful of chips. I walked to the other cupboard and grabbed two glasses and put them on the blue counter top. I walked to the fridge and opened the door grabbing the big 2 litre bottle of Pepsi. I poured to the top of both glasses and walked them over to my friend. We both took a sip and grabbed a chip from the bag. “Whatcha wanna do?” I said to Tony. “Doesn’t matter. I heard there was backyard wrestling at Jon’s house today. His dad got all those big wrestlers to come and wrestler for us.” he said back to me. “How much is it to get in?” I said. “I think he said $2.00.” he replied back to me. I opened my jean pocket hoping to fid a Toonie I could use to get into Jon’s wrestling show. I pulled out a small silver and gold coin with a polar bear on it. “I’m all set.” “Darn. I didn’t find anything. Do you think I could borrow at Toonie and pay you back later Max” “Sure, I’ll go get one from my piggy bank that no one knows about except you because your are my best friend in the whole entire world.” I walked up the stairs and into my dirty room. Clothes were scattered all over even inside my bed as I searched the room for my small piggy bank with all my money in it. I finally found it inside my closet under all my toys and clothes. I picked it up and opened the bottom of it. Coins came out all over the floor. I spotted a toonie beside my black dress sock. I picked it up and left the piggy bank on the floor with all the other junk and ran out of my room. I ran down stairs and gave the toonie to my friend. “Thanks Max. Now you know why you’re my best friend.” “No problem buddy.” “Mom! Me and Tony are going to Jon’s house.” I yelled to my mother who was in the living room watching the television. “Be back at 6:30, honey. And no violence.” she yelled back to me. “Don’t worry. There never is.” I hated lying to my mom. But I knew if I would’ve told her she would say no, and I wouldn’t want to miss this. I never get to see wrestling shows. My mom always said no to when I ask her to watch a wrestling show that comes to our city. Me and Tony hopped off the stools we were sitting on and ran out the door hoping not to be late. When we got to his house we just walked into his backyard. There was a wrestling ring and chairs all over. There was a stand selling food and another stand where you had to pay. “Come on Tony, we don’t wanna be late.” I told him. Me and him walked to the stand and gave the lady our 2 dollars. We ran to the front row so we could see all the action and maybe get a few autographs. Then I started thinking. What was the fed who was going to wrestle today. “Hey Tony, what fed is here today?” I asked him. “I think its called Global Wrestling or something along the lines of that.” he told me. “Ladies and gentleman! Welcome to a special edition on GW’s finest wrestlers. Tonight, we hope all of you enjoy this show.” the announcer yelled to us through the speekers. “This is going to be so cool.” I told my friend Jordan who was sitting behind me. Suddenly I could see my mother walking down the street. She looked very angry. She turned into Jon’s yard and into the backyard. She saw me sitting there looking around. I felt bad, my mom was going to take me home. “Max Roswell! Get your sorry little butt over here this instant.” she yelled over all the people. I got up from my seat feeling so embarrassed. I went to my mother and she grabbed me by the arm and started walking the other way back to my house. She walked into the front door and into the living room. She pushed me onto the couch, as she sat down on a leather chair by our television. “What do you think your doing going to a wrestling show without telling me?” she yelled to me. “I, thought if I told you, you would have said no. You say no to everything I ask unless I lie. I never get to have any fun, and all my friends get to.” I told her. “I understand that part but the reason I took you home from there was that because, Grandpa Bill had a heart attack!” she told me. Tears started to fill up both me and my mothers eyes. I got off the couch and went to give my mom a hug. We both just sat down on the chair saying everything will be alright.
