The Past, Present, and Future

4 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: jan 4, 2006 Static “Reload is quickly approaching’im in the Platinum Title match’have I ever held the Platinum Title”...Hell yes’I’m Jay Robson damn it, the one and only, now I know when its time to have fun and party it up but I also know when to buckle down and get serious and this is one of those times. I know myself better than anyone else and I know that I am unpredictable and sometimes not one to count on in a clutch situation’but then again that was then’this is now, in this day and age in Global Wrestling nobody cares about the past anymore nobody cares about C4 Explosive Death Matches or Glass Cage Matches, nobody cares about Insane Cain or Bobby Brawler anymore’hell, I don’t think they remember who they are’those guys were the back bone of UCWF along with Salaok. A pair of eyes can become visible through the snow of static What was I? I was a snot nosed rookie that thrown in the pit with big dogs, back then either you ate or you got eaten. I didn’t know a thing about professional wrestling’oh I knew about the art of wrestling don’t get me wrong I was a two time state champion wrestler at my high school. Thinking I was the shit when I rolled into UCWF, but then I got my ass thrown around a few times’eventually I got the hang of it but not by myself’a man named Johnny Cruse saw my ability and my talent. The pair of eyes become more evident through the static and bring a baby blue tint to the back and white screen. Cruse quickly took me under his wing and taught me how to become a super star in pro wrestling. He brought in the name Young Blood to me and Derek Frost who was also a snot nosed rookie at the time, together with Johnny Cruse we blossomed into a duo to be reckoned with in the Universal Championship Wrestling Federation. A few months into my career I captured my very first title’The Cruiserweight Championship of the world and held that title for an unprecedented seven months and fourteen days. But UCWF didn’t last that long. A face begins to manifest around the eyes enveloping the static screen A rival federation known as Freedom Wrestling League merged with UCWF to become the fore father of GW known as GWF, so in all actuality I held the UCWF/GWF title for seven months and fourteen days a record that still stands to this day as the longest title reign for the Cruiserweight Title. But that’s not why im telling you all of this, I am telling you this because the Golden Era of GW is gone and will never come back. The face emerges through the static as Jay Robson I am the last of UCWF, I am one of the last of GWF, im no longer a snot nosed rookie’I am a seasoned veteran in this Federation, I am the Past, Present, and Future of Global Wresting.” The static fades to black as the screen pans out slowly to show TLM in his entirety. Golden blonde hair, baby blue eyes, accompanied with a physic only matched by a Greek God as he stands firm in the middle of the screen Global Wrestling presents’Reload live from Manchester, England January 11th 2006? Call your local cable company today for details.
