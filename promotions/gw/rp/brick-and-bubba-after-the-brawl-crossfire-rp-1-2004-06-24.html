Brick and Bubba after the brawl (Crossfire RP #1)

24 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/24/04 It was a rather hectic scene in the backstage area of the Global Arena. Tensions were high between the majority of members of Team GW, particularly between team captain Josh and the leader of the Upper Echelon Maverick Reno. The tensions between the Elite and TUE had been building for about a month, perhaps longer. But the most recent altercation hadn't been between the Elite and TUE. It had been the Elite and the Platinum champion Train. Train had overheard the World and International champions making rather unflattering comments about the O.G. This resulted in a huge argument, insults being thrown back and forth, followed by a shoving match, which had to be broken up by security. 'Nice call on Team GW, dad,' Christina said, with a smirk on her face. 'Bang up job.' As Bubba glared angrily at his daughter, she made her exit, still smugly grinning. And Bubba was left alone with his frustration. 'Bubba.' Bubba turned to see Brick walking towards him. He had just gotten through with a particularly grueling match for Train's Platinum title. 'Rough night?' 'Rough couple o' weeks.' 'Yeah, I can relate.' The two men then began to make their way down the hallway. 'Christina acting like a bitch probably don't help any.' 'Hey, watch it, Brick! That's still my little girl you're talkin' about!' 'I'm perfectly aware of that, Bubba. Don't mean she ain't acting like a bitch. You heard about how she chewed out Ely?' 'None o' the particulars, at first. Just heard a few bits an' pieces here an' there. Then I saw the episode o' Evolution it happened on. I figured, hey, Ely's a big boy. He can handle it.' 'You didn't see him down at the Dark Horse. Daisy told me he'd been there, downin' Texas mudslides like there was no tomorrow. He didn't want to have to face her, so I saved him the trouble.' 'Ah, damn,' muttered Bubba. 'Hey, when have you known me to back down from anybody in charge?' asked Brick. 'I never backed down from Ayers. I never backed down from Lansheer. I never backed down from Salaok, Hell, I never backed down from you. I damn sure wasn't gonna back down from Christina.' 'Let me guess. She wasn't too happy about it.' 'No, she wasn't and I didn't give a fuck.' 'Well, she and I already got into it, earlier tonight, when I tried to persuade her to take Reno off of Team GW, but she wouldn't budge. She knows puttin' Reno an' Josh on the same team is just askin' for trouble. But that daughter o' mine is determined to screw both the both of 'em over.' 'Well, I don't like the majority of the cats that are on Team GW. I don't like Josh, I don't like Partridge, I damn sure don't like Reno, an' you know I can't stand Train.' 'All too damn well.' 'Pendragon, I don't know all that well, but I could probably stomach him, with his weird ass. To be honest, I'm a bit insulted that I wasn't asked to join Team GW. That said, if you need me to help keep them cats in line, let me know.' 'That's okay. You just concentrate on your own damn match at Crossfire.' 'That's another thing I wanted to talk with you about,' said Brick as he stopped Bubba. 'I wanna know why my match with Umbrage is just a standard singles match. This little punk bitch wants to run his mouth, sayin' he's gonna hunt me down like a dog. He's supposed to be one of the most hardcore wrestlers WMW has, I'm one of the most hardcore cats this company has, if not the most hardcore, and we're facing each other in a bullshit standard match? I need something more hardcore, like a Street Fight or, better yet, a match in the Pit.' 'Just about everybody else is in a standard match.' 'And for that matter, who the fuck is Umbrage, really? See, this serves me right waiting for one o' them to start something. I should have been the first to make a move.' 'Well, Umbrage is one of their champions.' 'Yeah, the Heartland champion. What the hell does that mean, anyway? I've ripped cats better than him apart. All he's done is run his mouth. I've seen some o' his matches an' I ain't impressed. His First Blood match at their last PPV? Big damn deal.' 'Brick, maybe you should just stop pissin' an' moanin'.' 'I ain't pissin' an' moanin', man. All I want is a chance to make a bigger impact at Crossfire. I'm tired of Weak-ass Motherfuckin' Wannabes comin' up in here talkin' shit about us, and then talkin' shit about us on their own shows. It's time to put them in their place.' 'I agree with you. It is a chance to put them in their place. But this is a chance for you to make an impact. You beat one o' their champions, you can make that impact. You can send a message to everybody who watches Crossfire next week as to what Global Wrestling is made of. You can prove Hanson an' Corvus an' Matrix an' all the rest just how wrong they are.' 'I guess you're right about that. Umbrage is basically like a lamb to the slaughter.' 'Exactly. Well, I feel like a drink. How 'bout you?' 'Yeah, sure. I'm supposed to meet Ely at the Dark Horse, anyway.' 'Well, then, let's not keep 'im waitin'.' 'Let's not.' With that, the two men walked towards an exit. 'So, Brick, how's that actin' thing goin' for ya?'
