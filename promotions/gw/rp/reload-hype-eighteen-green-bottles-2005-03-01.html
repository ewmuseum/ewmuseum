Reload Hype: Eighteen Green Bottles

1 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/01/05 'Eighteen Green Bottles' 'Eighteen Green Bottles...' 'Hanging on the wall...' 'Eighteen Green Bottles...' 'Hanging on the wall...' 'And if one green bottle...' 'Should accidently fall...' 'There'll be seventeen green bottles...' '... Hanging on the wall.' [ Following the reciting of a verse from an age-old rhyme, the scene opens with a youngish man standing in front of a Global screen.] ' ... It's been a while since I stepped out on GW turf. It's been a long while...' [ The man pauses for all of a few moments, looking up and exhaling before looking level again. ] 'Some people seem to remember me... as the guy who didn't really amount to much. Some people seem to remember me... as the 'good guy', the kid who kept his nose clean.' [ He chuckles, reminiscing of the 'time' he had here before. ] 'I seem to remember the times where I would walk down to that fighting arena... and sing out some sing-song, sing-a-long crap. I remember the times when I would waltz down and make a god damn fool of myself.' [He chuckles again, obviously finding his past to be somewhat... humourous.] '... but that isn't going to be happening anymore...' [His facial expression changes, the humour wiped off like the essence of chalk off a blackboard.] 'I'm not here to dance around like a fucking fairy... I'm not here to sing along to crappy songs, try to play some kind of comical character. The jokes are gone, the fun is out. I'm here to go toe-to-toe with people. I'm here to drop bombs and make. you. tap.... well, that or die tryin' to tap.' [He looks smug, almost impressed at the way he's issued an element of threat] '... January 11, 2006... I introduce myself to eighteen lil' green bottles in the only way I know how. Pain first, gloat later.' [He turns away as the scene fades to black, the ominous warning issued to all those involved in the Elimination Gauntlet.]
