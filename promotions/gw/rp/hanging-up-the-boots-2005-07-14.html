Hanging Up The Boots

14 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/14/05 July 14h 4 AM It was hard too see anything in the dark room. The only light coming from the moon light out of the window and the computer monitor. I listen to “The Nobodies” by Marilyn Manson as I search around on the internet. Today I am dirty I want to be pretty Tomorrow, I know I'm just dirt Today I am dirty I want to be pretty Tomorrow, I know I'm just dirt We are the nobodies we wanna be somebodies when we're dead, they'll know just who we are We are the nobodies we wanna be somebodies when we're dead, they'll know just who we are I pulled up a popular wrestling website to read the dirt sheets. A bold bright blue link caught my eye at the top of the page. “WMW Star Syberus beats Fred Grest again” . I closed my eyes for a moment as everything I manage to put in the deepest darkest corner of my mind since Crossfire all came rushing out. I lean back on my gray computer chair as I open my eyes looking at the blue ceiling. I click the link and read the journalists thoughts on the match. WMW Star Syberus considered by many to be the next headliner in Wrestling Midwest formerly a PTC Member. We would say that after Crossfire he is the next in line for a Great Lakes Title Shot but lets face it guys, it was just Fred Grest. Yesterday I was dirty wanted to be pretty I know now that I'm forever dirt Yesterday I was dirty wanted to be pretty I know now that I'm forever dirt We are the nobodies we wanna be somebodies when we're dead, they'll know just who we are We are the nobodies we wanna be somebodies when we're dead, they'll know just who we are Some children died the other day we fed machines and then we prayed puked up and down in morbid faith you should have seen the ratings that day I pull the chair forward and spin it around. I stop and I get out of the chair and walk over to the sofa. And I crash land on it. I’m sprawled out on the black leather sofa thinking about what the future holds for me. Personally, I think the future is darker then this room. My goal was to beat Syberus at Crossfire and then move on to bigger and better things after that. If I can’t beat Syberus what makes me think I can beat men like Jason Ortiz and Michael Ryba. I close my eyes and fall asleep right there sprawled out on the sofa. The room is just like my career right now cold and dark. July 14th 7:00 AM Its only been 3 hours and I’m already up. I push myself up off the sofa and sit up. I try to keep my blood shot eyes open as I rub my rather stiff neck. I slowly get up and walk slowly up the steps to the rest room. It smells as if someone just took a crap on in here and I know it wasn’t me. Well, whoever it was I hope they atleast they wiped there ass by the smell of it. I go to the sink and turned on the cold water as I glance into the mirror. “What the fuck”!” In what seems to have been done with a black marker is the word “Cracker” on my forehead. Either Jason Ortiz has broken into my house or Chill manage to steal a spare key from me. I’m willing to bet the later. I splash some cold water on me to wake myself up even though with the total lack of sleep it wasn’t a very good idea to do that. I grab the bar of Zest soap and leather my hands up. I spread it across my face and wash it off. I dry it off hoping to atleast have the writing fade. It does but very little. “I’m going to be taking a shower anyway. No where is that asshole at?” I mutter to myself. Under normal circumstances I wouldn’t be pissed off at this but these aren’t normal circumstances. I’ve been on edge all week so I don’t appreciate this one bit. I’ve haven’t accepted phone calls from even my closest of friends. I stomp down the steps with a look that was less then pleasant. I come down the steps and as soon as I see Chill and Samantha sitting on the sofa. My eyes focus on the two. One being the reason The Offspring made “Pretty Fly” and the a very sexy British woman who I’ve started to develop a slight crush on but know I could never have. “You were in on this two Samantha”!” I say with enough anger in my voice to probably scare small children away. “It wasn’t either of us Freddy?” Samantha replies. “I only see two people here Sammers. I know it wasn’t you so I know its Chill!” I yell as I am about ready to attack him. “It was Vyper! He just left homie!” Chill says trying to out shout me. “Where the hell is he”!” “Dude, he left. What the hell is wrong with you” You do this crap all the time. Take what you dish out.” Chill responds annoyed. “I would but I’ve been in a foul mood all week and I’m just waiting for someone to give me a reason to let it all out.” “Good save it for the ring and you’ll come out with a win.” Samantha says, “That’s just the thing Samantha”. I don’t know if I want to show up at Evolution.” “What”!” Both say curiously. “I think I’m going to retire.” I say now a much less bass in my voice. My head lowered as I sigh. “Wow” You have so many years left.” Samantha says. “Sam, Imagine not being able to beat someone” someone you needed to beat and you blew what will probably be your last shot. A win you needed to finally get something going for you and then you blew it.” “Fred, I lost too Scott every fucking time and I never retired over it.” Chill obviously trying to get him to change his mind. “Yeah, but it looks like this is what I’ll be remembered for. So why disappoint anyone?” I ask. “Your fan base is growing Fred. You will be disappointing a lot of people if you do this.” Chill says. “I’m a comedy act” I can be replaced easily.” I say as I turn around and start to walk away. “I wouldn’t be so sure Freddy. You’re worth more then you think. You have a lot of potential.” Samantha says. I keep walking away as I start to walk up the steps . I look at my two friends on the sofa. “Potential” I’m a nobody. I’m going back to bed?.. try not to steal anything.” I can’t even smirk at my lame jokes now. I can’t believe how bad of shape I’m in because of this silly sport. A sport that isn’t even taken seriously because of people like me. I run around with a water gun and I chose to do this for a living? I could have chosen to do anything but I decided to follow my childhood dream something few actually do and even fewer achieve. I should have just followed in my father’s footsteps and became a Deacon. Hell, its not like I’m even getting laid anymore I should have just been a priest. Dreams, are for somebodies I’m a nobody. At Evolution I will make my retirement final. Sorry Global but this guy is hanging up the boots. I enter my room which is lighted by the morning sunshine as I crash onto my bed finally. I lay down on my head on a soft black pillow and put my arm on another. “Time to hang up the boots.” I mutter as I close my eyes for some much needed rest. Because after that conversation I sure as hell don’t want to stay up.
