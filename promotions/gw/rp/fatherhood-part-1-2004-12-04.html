Fatherhood: Part 1

4 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4th December 2004 Heroes... We all want to be one don't we? Well probably most of us have grown up by now and realised that the comic world is unfortunately nothing but a fantasy. However do you remember when you were a kid and you read those comics and you dreamt of one day gaining extraordinary powers and saving the world? Okay maybe that is just me, but I am sure you have all wanted to be a hero of some sort, whether it be Superman, Spiderman or even Wonder woman. I remember who I wanted to be when I was a kid, Jimmy 'Superfly' Snuka. Okay he didn't have super powers but to me at least, he was super human. To see what he did in that ring, taking risks and not caring. I suppose you can see why he is my hero, as I more or less live by the same philosophy as him, which leads me on to my next point. Not to give any of the wrestlers out there a bigger ego than they have already got but in away we are close to being heroes ourselves. We get to fly across the ring or stand up after multiple chair shots. Sure not all of us are admired or seen as actual 'heroes' but within ourselves we can see something special, something out of the ordinary, maybe even extraordinary. In a few days time one 'hero' will stand among the rest at Showdown In Tokyo and I plan on being that 'hero'... It has been a rocky few months for me, especially in this last part of the year. I know I continue to go on about it but it has been a time in my life when I have been able to take a good long look at myself and see what I truly want. You see all my life I have believed all I wanted was to be something other than a sick little boy hidden in his house, I wanted to be someone, I wanted to be Jimmy Snuka, I wanted to be a fighter. So since the age of 7 maybe even earlier I have had this one goal in my head, I never thought I would fall in love never mind get married. All these distractions I never contemplated because I was too single minded all the time, I had this one goal, I didn't need other goals crowding my mind. But I never realised how much I actually needed the distractions, how much I needed to be in love and how much I needed to be a father. Now all these things are happening whilst I have to concentrate on reaching the top of the GW. Do I wish I didn't have the distractions? Not at all, I love Rachel and I will love our baby. For now however I am back to my one track mind, winning at Showdown in Tokyo... Sunday November 21st My Birthday I stare blankly at the night sky with the stars twinkling down through my window. I am sitting in our conservatory as the clock ticks past 12 o'clock midnight telling me I am now officially 28. My mind fades in and out, something weighing heavy on my mind. I sigh as I feel two slender arms wrap around my neck. 'Happy birthday, old man.' Rachel whispers jokingly in my ear. I smile and kiss her arm. 'Thanks, old woman.' Rachel playful hits me on my chest as she moved around and sits on legs wrapping her arms around my neck. Her red hair whipped passed my nose and the smell of her shampoo filled my nostrils causing me to sigh and relax, her smell always made me feel safe and calm. For a moment we just sat there holding each other. She kissed me on the forehead and sighed. 'So how does it feel to be the big 28?' 'Just like it felt to be 27 a couple of minutes ago.' 'Very funny, you sarcastic so and so. So what is your birthday wish?' I placed my hand on her bump and looked up at her and smiled. 'To be a family.' I said as I kissed her. 'You old softy,' She said smiling back and putting her hand on mine over her bump, 'You won't have long to wait either, I am about ready to pop.' 'Pop? You've never used that before.' I laughed. 'I know,' She giggled, 'A man at work said it to me.' 'You going to miss it?' 'What?' 'Work.' 'Are you kidding me? 6 months maternity leave is going to bliss.' 'You realise you will be at the beckon and call of the baby when it gets here.' 'No different from work then.' We both laughed and again sat there simply looking at each other. 'Anyway,' Rachel said breaking the silence, 'We'll have you to bring home the money, as long as you don't get injured again.' I laughed nervously; Rachel quickly looked at me. 'What's wrong?' She asked. I looked up at her and was about to tell her but just sighed. 'It's nothing, nothing important.' 'Adam, darling what is wrong?' 'It's just...' I stopped mid-sentence and gritted my teeth as I whispered, 'I'm scared.' 'Pardon?' Rachel said obviously not hearing the last bit. 'I'm scared.' I said louder this time. 'What do you mean scared?' Rachel asked looking at me confused. 'Well you know this thing at Showdown in Tokyo?' 'Yeah, what about it?' 'Well every single one of them is going to be battling to come out the winner and it is going to be brutal. I just don't want to come out of Showdown in Tokyo on the injury list.' 'Darling I don't know where all this has come from but you realise you are talking complete and utter crap.' I looked at my wife amazed that she was the one talking tough. 'Let me ask you Adam, who are you?' 'Your husband?' I guessed. 'No you are Adam Myers, GW Cruiserweight Champion.' 'So?' 'So the matter that you are an actual champion in the GW and let's not forget what you went through to get that belt back, prove that you are a man who can not be messed with, who will go any distance to come out on top. You are one of the most talented fighters on that roster. I know you were injured but that was not in the ring was it? It was because you were sneak attacked. You know what the risks are but does Adam Myers care?' 'No.' 'Does Adam Myers live by the phrase 'Take The Risk, Forget Everything Else!'' 'Yes.' 'Is Adam Myers the GW Cruiserweight Champion?' 'Yes!' I shouted feeling the adrenaline rush through me. 'So who are you?' 'Adam Myers.' 'Who?' 'Adam Myers!' 'That's my man!' Rachel screamed as she kissed me. As we sat there a smile was beaming from my face, my wife, what a motivator. Tuesday 30th November... 'So you ready for Showdown in Tokyo?' 'You bet I am, I don't think I have ever trained so hard in my life.' 'Well you are going to have to train harder if we are going to get to the final. There is a lot of competition out there Myers and we got to prove our team is the best out of them all.' I looked at the man they call Brick as he stared at me with such intensity is what hard not to get motivated. We were running a long side each other on the treadmills in the gym. It was a great honour to be fighting alongside Brick, at least for the first part of the tournament. After our match in the Tournament of the Worthy, he approached me and asked me to fight in his team at Showdown in Tokyo, I accepted immediately. I knew it was the right team to choose as all the other teams began forming. Team Brick is the best chance I will have to get through to the final. I mean sure I would have to face them all in the final but that was for then and for now we were team mates. 'I've got to say Brick, I am amazed you asked me to be on your team.' 'Well it was for a simple reason Myers, I needed someone who wouldn't give up. You showed me that in our match at Evolution a few weeks ago. You were like a man obsessed, pity you were knocked out of the tournament afterwards.' 'Yeah well that Jackson is a monster of a man, twice I have faced him and both times he has got the better of me.' 'Well don't worry about it, at Showdown you will have Team Brick backing you up, we will teach that Jackson a lesson.' I didn't reply I just simply nodded, as I knew Brick was a man of his word. As I did my cellphone went off. I quickly slowed down the treadmill and stepped off. The display on the phone said 'Pete'. I quickly answered. 'Hey Pete.' 'No time to explain, Rachel, baby, now.' Pete said frantically on the other side of the line. 'What? Where?' I replied frantic also. 'At home, get here quick Adam! I don't think Rachel can hang on.' As soon as Pete said this, the phone disconnected. 'Pete? Hello? Pete!' Brick was now standing at my side, looking at me with concern. 'What's wrong?' He asked. 'Rachel's having the baby!' I said as I sprinted off. 'Good luck!' Brick shouted after me as I pushed through the gym doors and out onto the street. I was going to be a father... There was only one problem... I was 5 states over from my home and had no means of transport... SHIT!
