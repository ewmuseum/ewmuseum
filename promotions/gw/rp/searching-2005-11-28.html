Date: Week Of 11/28/2005 A Country Home On The Lake In Japan Minimalism was an art form unto itself. Inside the thin walls of his traditional house, the walls and floor were framed mainly is light birch with a couple of support beams in cherry oak that matched the few pieces of furniture. The table in the middle of the room, ancient looking with a few dings and scratches to lend humility to the room, was deep cherry oak surrounded by red sitting pillows. Here he was, in the middle of the room he had not been inside of in over a decade. He looked about the room, amazed at so little change from the man. This is where so many things began. I remember the table, but the pillows have changed. I had tea there, sake there and then I even signed my name to those papers. What was it that he needed from me all those years ago to do something like this? Was he testing me, or was it already done in his mind. He crossed the floor, avoiding the mats as he stayed on the wooden floor, shoes on his large feet. He stopped before the rice paper painting, admiring the images. The artwork was designed to establish the room in the visitors mind. Here, he saw the warrior with the naginata pinning down a snake-like dragon to the floor. A new painting? What is it you’re saying here Tanaka? How many of your guests run from the room fearing you? If they knew the truth, then perhaps more of them would have run away. Your mood and nature charms them, and yet all this time you have been the snake beneath surface moving the pieces around your dark little underworld. Why didn’t I see this before? The long trench coat came off, folded carefully and set down on the flat surface. He sat down on the edge of the windowsill, waiting for Tanaka to arrive. There were questions he had to get the answer to, and then there were all the questions he WANTED to know the answers to. As the sunset on the snow covered landscape, he looked out the small slit in the wooden blinds. Hunter watched for the movement of prey crunching the snow-covered ground’the time to bring the chase to a close was rapidly approaching. Later That Night The wind swept down the sides of the mountains and over the icy plains, carrying winter’s chill to him as he pulled the coat more tightly around his small frame. He could afford a home in the city, but he enjoyed his space in a country where space seemed to go against the very rules of such an exploding population in a confined space. He saw his home just another few hundred meters away. The building was not the original home of his ancestors, rather it was the seventh building. All of the others had been destroyed via various means from natural disasters, to wars and even to destruction by rivals for power. Just twelve years before, the home had been hit by a wave of fire started by a lightning storm in the field. The fire had down, lighting the wooden structure quickly and easily. Now, while he used wood inside he had utilized modern materials to create a more lasting structure. The roof was the classic, layered wooden tile on multiple levels to look like a palace or temple. The sides were framed in black wood lines on white canvases giving intricate box designs. He always felt an enormous sense of pride whenever he approached the home. Tonight was no different, and he was looking forward to a weekend of relaxing inside his home. Tanaka came to the front door, and used his keys to open the series of locks. He stepped inside, feeling the warmth of the interior wash over him. He removed the thick coat and set it on the hangar over the shoe rack. He added his black dress shoes to the others, and slipped into the beige slippers. He began walking across the floor to the bedroom when a voice cut through him. “Tanaka, you and I need to speak.” The voice was strong and powerful. He sensed someone sure of themselves and what they were doing. He paused, and looked into the corner windowsill where he enjoyed reading poetry in the dawn’s morning rays. “Who is it that intrudes upon my privacy?” “I should have known how you would react.” The man took a few steps out, the lower half of his body lit by the beams of pallid moonlight. Tanaka could see the man was large, someone powerful and thick with muscles. “You always try to remain calm, no matter the circumstances. I would have admired that in you before I knew who you really were. I might have emulated such serene calm. All before I knew you Tanaka, and your connections with certain organizations.” Tanaka looked down, seeing boots on the man’s feet, “Dishonoring me is one thing, but to dishonor my home by not removing your shoes” This is not proper, not in this country gaijin.” The man laughed. He rubbed his right palm in the light, revealing bruises on the hefty hands. “I insult the home because I am insulting the owner. Now is not the time to act brave. You should remember what can happen to men who try and act brave.” The man stepped out into the moonlight. Black hair hung down to his shoulders, vivid blue eyes with alert electricity seems to shoot out into him. The smirk and goatee were well known to him, and he called the man friend the last time they had sat for sake. Now Tanaka knew whom he was dealing with, and from the earlier words he knew his old friend Jake was no longer a sheep amongst the wolves’he was now one of the predators. Tanaka looked over to the drawer just a few feet away. If he was fast enough, he might be able to reach the drawer and draw the pistol before Jake would be on him. The odds were not on his side. “Going for your weapon would be foolish.” Jake crossed over to maneuver himself between Tanaka and the door. “I have already removed it, and any other possible weapons. If I meant to kill you, then you would never have heard my voice or seen my face.” “Why are you here Jake?” Tanaka moved to the cushion before the table. He sat down, looking up at the wide frame of Hix. “Last time we conducted business, I thought everything between us ended amiably.” Jake leaned against the dark wood support beam, “Of course, back then I didn’t know that you were working against me.” “I never worked against you, or anyone else for that matter.” Tanaka held his hand up, trying to settle the angry grimace of Jake. “I only worked for myself. I never worked against anyone.” Jake walked over to the opposite cushion. He sat down across from the man he really never had known, and leaned with both arms on the polished surface of the table. “So that’s what it comes down to, huh” You and me, but the only thing you care about is yourself. I have more than myself to worry about. I have to be concerned for my family, my friends and even the weight of the entire world.” “No one is required to care for the world itself except the gods.” “Well, there’s where we’re a little different. I believe in one God.” Jake interlaced his fingers, and rested his chin on his thumbs. “And I was selected by Him to handle this dark business. Then I find out that you have been acting, as a broker for what you thought was my brother. So, I need to know everything you know Tanaka. I have to know where the book is, and I have to know right now.” Tanaka laughed, “Why would I tell you anything” You may try to puff yourself up here before me, but we both know you are not a fighter. You do not do anything outside of the ring except drink and find young women.” “That was many years ago. Do not test my patience.” “Look, we are businessmen. We can come to mutually beneficial arrangement. I could use some assistance on a few matters, and then I would tell you what you need to know.” Tanaka showed his teeth as he smiled. “We have done business before, haven’t we Big Dawg?” Jake leaned over the table, and slapped Tanaka to the cold, polished floor with a backhand. He could taste the trickle of blood in his mouth from where his teeth had torn his lip apart. “I don’t think you understand.” Jake reached into his jacket pocket. He set the small plastic tube on the table. “I am not here to debate, to negotiate, or to pander to your ego. Tell me what I must know, and we can part without further harm. Play with me again, and you will not like what happens.” Tanaka sat back up. He pulled a handkerchief out to wipe his mouth. He stopped, noticing for the first time what was in the tube. There was a finger inside, with a large diamond ring with two fire rubies on each side. Tanaka spoke in a frightened whisper, “Where did you get that?” Jake leaned forward, “I removed it from the hand of your mistress when I asked her where you were hiding yourself.” Tanaka was not a violent man. Rather, he was a calm and reserved man, but this was too much. He stood up, yelling obscenities in English and Japanese as he did. Tanaka looked as though he might attack. “Sit.” Jake’s voice was normal, no emotion betrayed in his manner. Tanaka continued screaming, gesturing and trying to take control of the situation. Ignoring the demonstration, Jake grabbed the side of the table. He listed it off the floor and threw it aside. The table skirted along the wooden floor, slamming into the wall. Jake looked up at a stunned Tanaka. “Sit, or I will seat you.” Tanaka pointed his finger down at Jake, “Who do you think you are!”! You come to my country and threaten me? I will have them take you out and?” He never finished the statement. Jake grabbed the outstretched hand in his own massive mitt. He turned the hand over, and wrenched until Tanaka fell over onto the floor following the self-preservation built into his very being. Jake was on top of him, knee against his back and he slowly and purposefully broke the man’s wrist. “You see, I don’t have any limits anymore Tanaka. I’m interested in just getting laid and getting drunk anymore. There’s absolutely nothing I won’t do to get what I want. You want to talk to me about what’s right and wrong, let’s talk about the trips to Taiwan. You went last month, and there was someone following you. They blackmailed you for a nice payment, but after I had a discussion with them I ended up with the pictures.” Jake grabbed his first two fingers and snapped them. As he stood, he realized the screams didn’t bother him anymore. The music was simply the background to what had to be done. “Little boys and girls Tanaka, no older than six or seven. What darkness is inside of you that you would do that to children?” Jake walked over to the windowsill and raised the wooden blinds. “I lost my nieces and nephews recently Tanaka, in just the same way you violated those children. You know, in the darkest part of night, when I think my soul can’t bear the strain of what I do anymore, and the tears start coming I find myself warmed and comforted by thoughts that should rip me apart.” The moonlight along the rippling surface of the lake was beautiful. Like God’s canvas being painted before him, “I think that since they were murdered, right there in my own home, at least they didn’t have to live enduring the shame and damage to their souls. They went right up to Heaven, where there are no bad men like you to torture them. That’s one of the thoughts that comforts me.” Through screams and moans, with bloody bits banished out of his mouth by the painful expulsions, he began trying to talk his way out of the predicament in which he found himself. “Jake, I don’t know what you think you kno2w, but I wouldn’t hurt anyone!” He saw him there, kneeling on the floor as he held his wrist with his good hand. The red flush in his face from furious pain wracking his form. Tanaka was desperate, but pain could make the mind clouded, unable to act properly. Crossing the floor, Jake came right up to the man and slapped him down to the floor once again. Tanaka’s hand slipped on the wooden floor when he tried to get up. He ended up on his side on the ground. He saw Jake bent down next to him as his arms rested on each knee. Jake’s eyes seemed cold as he stared into them. “Old man, think about that. That’s the only thought that comforts me anymore. If that’s where I’ve been sent by everyone, why would you think that hurting you would bother me in the least” You think they enjoyed what you did to them? Are you so deluded you could just ignore the tears? The struggles? The painful cries’kind of like the sounds you’re making now in fact. Should I stop now? Did you?” Fear finally took true physical control of his body. He shook, like a leaf in a windstorm, looking at the deep desolate death before him in azure eyes. “What do you want?” “My brother was here. He came here to find a book, something he said was precious to him. I need to know where the book is, and what you told him. I have to get that book, and you’re going to tell me everything you did for my dear brother.” Jake kept watching him. “You’re going to tell me every dark secret in your black heart, and if you do that then I won’t mak4e you feel like you made those children feel. I can make your death into an epic. I’ll make them bring you back just so I can keep hurting you Tanaka.” The bottom lip trembled, and the tears began falling from his eyes. He was whimpering, like a beaten dog afraid of what was coming. He reached out with his good hand, “Don’t hurt me. Please, I’ll tell you what you want to know.” The hand was grabbed, and he squeezed down. The powerful hand, filled with more strength than any man should have had, began squeezing down like a vice. “I know you will, but the pain is necessary. Some penance is required, so I won’t kill you, but the pain is just a little taste of what you have coming to you.” Outside the home, the wind screamed across the landscape. Animals were bundled up in secret hideaways for the evening, escaping the worst cold of the night. The plants were already in their virtual hibernation, unseen processed in the unseen world. The landscape hid secrets no one knew. In the shallow graves, where flesh rotted away to bones, there were satisfied souls secluded in their earthly tombs. The screams carried over the landscape, giving just a small taste of justice to those Tanaka had buried in the soil of his home. Over an hour later, all the screaming had ended. Jake Hix was slipping his suit coat back on, staring out at the serene scene before him that was the opposite of the room he occupied. Jake grabbed the long trench coat next, and pulled it on over his torso. “Tanaka, I have something I need to tell you.” Jake turned, staring down at the man covered in blood, strange protrusions from where bones had pierced his skin into the outside air. He was beyond being able to speak now. He just stared? “I sent the photos, depositions and all other information to the authorities.” Jake turned around, locking his eyes onto Tanaka once again. “Of course, to ensure political connections don’t save you, I also sent them to several media outlets. They will all have them next week. I suggest, as a matter of self-protection you do what the Romans” once called the honorable thing. Your own people would do the same thing, with steel you don’t have enough honor to hold. Of course, you also don’t have the physical capability to hold it either. Your revolver is in the bedroom, on the bed with one bullet. Do what you will.” “What kind’of monster’are you?” “I’m not a monster.” Jake looked away. “There’s a saying about the right hand of the blessed, and the left hand of the damned. So I’m God’s left hand I think, the avenging arm of God. I’m the avenging angel, and what I do must be done.” Jake Hix moved over to the door. He left Tanaka alone. Crawling to the bedroom in his current condition would take several hours, but he would do it. Protecting the family name would be of paramount importance, and he would curse him before pulling the trigger. As the snowflakes fell onto his coat, he smiled. Living With Himself The sake was warm, just as he expected it to be in any proper restaurant. He sat at the end of the sushi bar, ignored by the customers as he consumed his wide selection of rolls. He was not in a conversational mood at the moment. He felt himself slipping away recently, changing from what he thought he was into something he didn’t recognize anymore. The trip to Japan was not necessary only for the Pay-Per-View, it was also necessary to find the book. Everything would hinge on what he did through the month of December. Now, he wasn’t sure where it would all end up. A young Japanese man in the standard uniform of the corporations, a finely pressed suit, sat down beside him. “Mr. Hix, it is an honor to meet you.” The man bowed, then handed his card to Jake with both hands. Jake took the card, “I’m not in the mood for autographs at the moment.” “Of course not, but I have been sent by mutual friends.” The man did not make eye contact out of deference to Hix. “I understand you are looking for an item in our possession.” Hix laid angry eyes on the man, “The book?” “Yes, there is a number on the back of the card to arrange a meeting.” The man bowed and left. Jake flipped the card over. He would make the call, and hopefully steal the book out from under Sid’s nose. The Meeting Steam came up from beneath the cement, escaping into the open through the vents. He stepped out into the alley; his visage alone prevented him from hiding here. He was a gaijin, and to add to the problem he was a celebrity. Anyone who saw him would know him. Subterfuge was not needed. Not for me? Jake walked down, pulling the trench coat around his body to ward off the cooling weather. While nowhere near as cold as home, the meeting was late enough to see temperatures in the forties. He could hear his cowboy boots click against the street, echoing throughout the alley. Jake slowed as he came to the back of the arcade where he had agreed to meet his contact. He looked around, but no one was there. He waited, not showing any sense of worry as he handled the games these men played easily. Nearly five minutes went by before the back door of the arcade swung out. For a change, he was surprised by what he saw. The man coming toward him had curly red hair atop his freckled face. He was another gaijin. Maybe meeting with me is considered an insult now. The man kept a respectable distance from Jake. His voice came out, frightened and small carrying a slight Irish accent. “I’m glad you agreed to meet me.” “You aren’t what I expected.” Jake looked him up and down. “Do you represent Sachio?” “No, not at all.” The man kept looking around nervously, “My name in Singun McAllister. I have arranged this meeting because I want to help you.” Jake looked down at the ground. Remarkably clean, he thought as he shook his head. “You understand, I don’t trust anyone.” “Nor should you sir. I know what happened to your family at the ranch.” “Do you?” Jake looked up at the man. He saw something familiar in his bright green eyes. “I have no idea what you’re speaking of.” “I sent the photos to Goff. I know about it because’because I was at the ranch that night.” Singun began shaking slightly, knowing he was at a critical juncture. “I work for your brother, the Dark messiah.” Jake put his hands in the pockets of his trench coat, “Now I know this is a trap.” There was a small sound, easily dismissible behind Singun. With the heightened paranoia of the moment, Singun turned to see what was happening. The swinging fist knocked him to the concrete. Another sound behind him signified the arrival of another man. The massive arms wrapped around his throat, choking away his life. Pitt Hix watched his brother Xavier choke the man, “I say we kill him now and be done with it.” “No.” Pitt looked ready to strike his brother, “He admitted he was there!” “And we’re on foreign soil, and easily recognizable.” Jake looked around, “We’re also alone. Pull him up Xavier. We’ll let him have a minute, or else we throw him naked into the river and let him take his chances. We can write that off to being drunk.” Xavier stood, pulling the red head to his feet. He shoved him forward, and Singun fell down in front of Jake. He looked up at the man . Singun now knew what he was. “I was being tested that night, and to them I passed. I’I helped. I did things?” “Jake.” Pitt angrily growled through clenched teeth. “No.” Jake stared down, “Go on.” “I passed, but then I realized what I was involved in. I got involved because I wanted power to go along with my money. I was seduced by what he offered.” Singun reached out, grabbing Jake’s slacks. “I looked for Hell to prove that Heaven was real. Most people will ask the question once in their lives if God loves us, why does he allow bad things to happen to us” I asked, and so I decided God had abandoned us all. So I sought the one who hadn’t abandoned us. I looked for evil.” “Free will works in each movement of our lives and the lives around us. Free will means we must endure the poor choices by other creations with no regard for the gifts they have been provided. God allows this happen because he loves us. Free will allows varied existence. Free will allows exploration of the world around us. God’s gift is a two edged sword, but the alternative is not living as he envisioned. We accept all aspects of the world: Good and Bad, Black and White, Happy and Sad. We must embrace all of them, for God has created everything for us. I found out God was real, but I felt I lost my soul to discover that.” Pitt spat on the ground, “Self-serving crap.” Xavier was clenching his fist. “I don’t want forgiveness, but I want to start atoning for what I have done.” Singun stood up, “Sid is about to buy the book. If he gets it, then it’s all over. I know what it means now, what it really means. Helping you is all I have left to give. I can give it all to you, and you just have to get the book.” A mole? He’s had people watching us from day one, but what if he wasn’t watching his own house? No one seems to know any details, but if he’s on the inside he could know it all Jake looked over at Xavier and Pitt, “If he knows, we can get everything we wanted.” “If he’s here to do the devil’s work?” Pitt asked. He had begun referring to Sid as the devil as of late. Xavier stepped forward, “Then we take pages from the devil’s handbook, and we find out what we need to know any way we can.” McAllister nodded, “I’ll tell you everything. I punish myself more than anyone ever can.” Pitt Hix stepped forward. He grabbed the man by the throat with both hands and lifted him into the air. Xavier and Jake were telling him to stop, but he ignored them. “You don’t know what pain is you fucking bastard. They might think you can help, but even if you do what do you think it means” You can try to save the world for the rest of your life, and you’ll move from pond scum to shit as far as I’m concerned. Nothing removes that darkness once it touches you, NOTHING!? Pitt threw him to the ground. He turned and walked away. Jake didn’t speak, his mind churning on what his brother had said. Nothing removes that darkness once it touches you. Jake helped Singun up, “You want redemption” You want forgiveness? Time to confess.” Singun began, detailing everything as Xavier struggled not to kill him for what he had done. Jake simply listened to the confession, knowing the soul needed to let it all out sometimes.
