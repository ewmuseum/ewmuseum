Date: 1-3-05 'So this message is to you Suits in the back. We can go about this the easy way or the hard way. You brought me here because you knew I was something special, that I stood out. I dont want you to do the spineless thing and try to protect your investment by having me come out last. You make me a star, or I will myself. Trust me, its in the best interest of you and your league to just be on my side. I rest assured you will make the right decision. You already made one great on by signing me, and you will make another at the PPV. It's the best option. . . .trust that.' Eddie Ross' promo plays on the big screen at Scores in New York City. A well dressed, dark haired man watches in astonishment, eyes wider than the Mississippi. 'You have got to be effing kidding me, Eddie!' Other patrons are startled by the loud yell. The man disregards them with the wave of a hand, as to tell them to mind their own business. He leaves some change on the bar, and heads for the door, as he begins to make a call on his cell. 'Eh, Eddie its me Tytus. Ya know your manager? The one your supposed to talk to before you go on national television, and spout your mouth off. Listen I made this deal for you, without me you would still be sitting at home watchin re-runs of Will and Grace. If we want this to go the way it should your going to have to start going through me on everything. . .you hear that? I said everything.' Tytus hangs up his phone and continues out of the club. Meanwhile . . . . . 'Will and Grace? What an ass.' Eddie Ross says with a laugh, about his esentric manager. As he sets his phone down on the bed. He sits down on a nearby reclyner holding what appears to be the days mail. Amongst the bills and junk mail is one black envelope with no return adress. Eddie adress is written in gold. The envelope brings him to look interested. He opens it to find a computer typed letter. ''So Mr. Big Shot is back in the game? Once again trying to make a name for himself. Have you forgotten the reason why youve been on the sidelines? You have resposibility Eddie. Responsibility that wont just go away while you toy around in wrestling. I have one message for you sir, take care of you know what or your career will be over before it even gets started.'' Eddie breathes deep as he brings his hand up to hold his forhead. A look of shock on his face. 'Damn. I got to much going to deal with this again.'
