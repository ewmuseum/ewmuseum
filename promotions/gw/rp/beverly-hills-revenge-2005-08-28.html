Beverly Hills: Revenge

28 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 28, 2005 Mike had found it deeply satisfying to mash Josh into a bloody pulp. Now the little bastard knew who the true number one contender to the Platinum Title was a fact that was supported by Mike’s chance to reclaim what truly belongs to him at the next Evolution. Mike only wished he could use the tactics he used against Josh against that redheaded bastard Charles. Over the past month Charles had caused Biddle more than just a little bit of grief. Mike didn’t know why Charles had forced Rumson to blacklist him, but he was sure that it had something to do with Charles’s inability to compete with him. How gratifying it would be to bash Charles about the head with a steel chair, sadly he couldn’t physically do this but he could do it in a figurative sense. The latest Beverly Hills gossip claimed that Charles planned to ask Hillary to marry him. Mike believed this rather juicy tidbit; Charles was the only person he knew that was dumb enough to marry that girl. Hillary’s relationship with Charles also made Mike slightly jealous, a fact that surprised him greatly and made him dislike Charles even more. Once the news of Mike’s removal from the blacklist had spread Mike had started to once again receive invitations to the social events that he desperately loved. He had received an invitation to a small private affair at the exclusive nightclub known as Hot Fuss. The invitation had come from Hillary, Mike figured that the party was so Charles could make a show by proposing to Hillary in public and he was sure that Hillary was unaware of this plan. Before he stepped out for the night Biddle made extra sure that he looked his best, he had spent an hour styling his precious blond locks, he wore his best faded jeans, and his most expensive red polo shirt. Mike arrived at Hot Fuss fashionably late. When he entered the small dimly lit dance floor/bar he saw that the usual people were there and that the club wasn’t as crowded as it normally was on a Friday night due to the private nature of the affair. Normally Mike would disappear into the crowd and end up going home with a woman that he didn’t know, but tonight Mike suppressed his primal urges for the time being. He scanned the nightclub and eventually found Hillary, she sat at the bar with Charles. Mike watched as the two of them talked, once Hillary noticed Mike’s presence she scooted closer to Charles an action that made Mike scowl. Mike decided now was the perfect time to make his move. He disappeared onto the crowded dance floor and took a little while to do some catching up with the people that he hadn’t seen during his stint on the blacklist. Not much later he was at the bar by Hillary, unnoticed by Charles. He laid his dark blue eyes on her bare shoulders and admired the small red dress she wore. The dress was the one she wore on television when she used to be his valet. She hadn’t yet noticed that he was behind her, she was engrossed by the conversation about jewelry she was having with Charles. Mike reached down with his right hand and swiveled Hillary’s stool so that she was facing him. She let out a surprised yelp at the sudden motion but quickly recovered when she saw the culprit. Her nymph-like features lit up when she saw him, in the background Charles’s mouth was agape. Mike put his arm around Hillary and looked into her eyes, “You know,” he said with a smirk, “I’ve actually missed you the last couple of days.” “Really?” Her heart melted, she suddenly felt as if she was in a dream. “Yup,” Mike replied. Charles suddenly came to and quickly objected to the scene before him. “Excuse me,” the redheaded man said, “but she is with me.” Mike looked up at his nemesis, “And now she’s with me.” Charles looked at Hillary for help but quickly noticed that she was in a trance. “Who invited you anyway?” Charles angrily asked, his face became a few shades brighter than his hair. “She did,” Mike said as he pointed a finger at Hillary. Charles became wide eyed again, it seemed as if his worst fears had come to life. He reached over and gave Hillary a shake, instantly bringing her back to reality. “Why in the hell did you invite him?” Charles asked. Hillary frowned, “Because he’s my friend.” “You still consider him your friend after all he’s put you through?” Charles asked with an exasperated voice. “Well,” Hillary began with a puzzled look, “yeah. I forgave Mike for that stuff a long time ago.” “Well isn’t that great,” Charles said as he rested his head in his hands. Mike smiled broadly as he watched Charles writhe with emotional pain. “It sounds like we need to talk Charles,” he said softly, “why don’t you follow me and we’ll talk in private.” “Yes,” Charles replied grimly, “lets.” Mike turned to Hillary, “We won’t be long.” “I’ll be waiting,” Hillary said as she secretly winked at Mike. Both men rose from their barstools at the same time and Charles followed Mike. They stopped in front of the men’s room and Mike opened the door. “Go ahead and step into my office,” Mike said with a sweeping gesture and a smile. Once they had both entered the restroom Mike made sure to lock the door behind them. When Mike turned to face Charles he had made a transformation from the nice, sweet man by the bar to a vicious brute that wanted nothing more than revenge. Charles shrank back in fear when he saw the evil look in Mike’s dark blue eyes. “I have question for you Charles,” Mike said as he backed the redheaded man into a corner, “why did you have Rumson put me on the blacklist?” “I-I don’t know what you’re talking about,” Charles answered nervously, his green eyes shifted from side to side. Mike grabbed Charles by the collar and lifted him up against the wall. “Don’t play stupid with me,” Mike said through gritted teeth, “I know damn well that you’re the one behind all the shit I’ve gone through lately. I just want to know why...now tell me.” Mike shook Charles violently in an attempt to shake the answer out of him. Mike didn’t notice it, but Charles’s pants suddenly became very wet. Charles took a deep breath and turned red from embarrassment when he realized that he had wet himself, “You were in the way.” The redheaded man answered. “In the way of what?” Mike inquired as he shook him again. Charles’s face contorted with fear. “I couldn’t be with Hillary if you were around. For some stupid reason she loves you despite all the times you hurt her. With you gone she was all mine. I’ve loved her for a long time you know, ever since the first time you brought her to a party about a year ago.” Mike’s dark blue eyes rolled in annoyance. “So you’re telling me that this is all because you wanted more of my sloppy seconds?” Charles nodded his head, he hoped that Mike would put him down soon. Mike let Charles drop down to the floor with a painful thud and for the first time he noticed that the redheaded man had wet his pants. “You might want to change your diaper pal,” Mike said with a laugh as he turned to leave. “Wait!” Charles exclaimed as Mike was near the door. Mike looked over his shoulder and to see what the bastard wanted. With a defeated look about him Charles spoke sadly, “Will you at least stay away from her” I do love her...” Mike laughed yet again. “She’s mine,” Mike replied, “so get over it.” Biddle exited the room and re-entered the loud world of Hot Fuss. He made his way back to the bar, where he found Hillary waiting. “Where’s Charles?” She asked. “He decided to go home,” Mike lied with a smile. “So it’s just you and me now.” Hillary’s face beamed brightly as she realized that her little plan had worked, she instantly forgot all about the redheaded man. Mike took her by the hand and helped her up. “What do you say we dance for a little bit and then get out of here,” Mike asked. “Okay,” Hillary answered; she looked as if she had just been swept off her feet. With that they left for the dance floor and danced the night away in more ways than one. ***** The next afternoon Mike awoke in his bedroom with a smile on his face, he had forgotten how fun Hillary could be in the bedroom. As he got up and stretched he felt relieved for the first time in months. A small chuckle escaped his thin lips as he thought of how easily he defeated Charles the night before. Mike had always found the fact that Charles thought that he could compete with him to be hilarious. Mike was a champion, a movie star, and damn good looking to boot. While on the other hand, Charles was nothing more than a pathetic imitation. Mike looked over and saw the sleeping form of Hillary beside him; he was surprised by her presence. He was sure that she had left the night before. He reached over and shook her awake. He felt sick to his stomach as he watched her roll over, her eyes and lips crusty from sleep. “Hey,” she said after she had woken up. She pulled him down for a passionate kiss and he wiped her face with the blanket before he complied. After the kiss he pulled back and sat up. “I want to talk to you,” she said. “About what?” Mike asked with an arched eyebrow. “About us,” she began, “you know how I feel about you. I want us to try being together.” She looked up at him with expectant eyes. Mike took a moment to think about this, he had mixed feelings about Hillary. On the one hand she had helped him regain his rightful place in society and stuck by him when no one else would. On the other hand she was nothing more to him than a nice evening in bed and annoyed him greatly with her clinginess. He thought about this and realized that the decision wasn’t a hard one at all. “There is no way in hell that I’m going to be with you,” Mike finally answered. He watched Hillary carefully and was able to see the exact moment that her heart shattered, it brought a smile to his face. Tears began to stream down her face once his words sunk in. “Why won’t you be with me” You were so nice to me last night?” “Strictly business dear,” Mike said with a chuckle. Hillary rose from the bed, her face red and wet from the tears. “What do you mean “strictly business”,” she asked with pained voice. “Well,” Mike explained with a big smile, “I had to get back at Charles somehow didn’t I” And taking away the girl he wants to marry was the best way.” Hillary couldn’t believe what she was hearing; she also couldn’t believe that she had allowed herself to fall for Mike’s tricks yet again. Charles had been right all along. She began to gather up her things and make her way towards the door. “Where are you going?” Mike asked as she put her hand on the doorknob. Hillary turned and looked at Mike with her tear soaked eyes. “I’m leaving,” she said with a somber voice, “and don’t you ever expect me to come back to you’you bastard.” She slammed the door on her way out. “Good,” Mike quietly said to himself as he drifted back to sleep. His problems were behind him for the time being and he could finally relax. -The End-
