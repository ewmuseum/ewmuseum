Creating The Crisis (Chapter 3: The Great Demise)

30 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/30/05 Long ago Just like the hearse you die to get in again We are so far from you ---- Date: December 27, 1998 Time: 1:33pm Central Time Location: Chicago, Illinois An eerie silence fell over the room. As he looked around the room, the rows and rows of people looked to be endless. Huddled together under the newly installed heating system, there were friends and family members as far as the eye could see. The deceptively cold December sunlight beamed through the pane-glass windows that surrounded the walls of the cathedral. The cold stone walls had housed many gatherings, but never one quite like this. Surrounded by religious figures and scriptures he approached the microphone with a nauseating feeling in the pit of his stomach. He looked back once again at the long black coffins behind him before closing his eyes and taking a deep breath. “Stay cool...” He said to himself under his breath as someone from the other side of the cathedral coughed, “They’re just boxes... nothing more. It’s better if you stay.” Every set of eyes in the house focused on him as he adjusted his black tie and the jacket on his black custom made suit. He cleared his throat, glancing over the audience with his cadet blue eyes. The beating of his heart pulsated through his ear drums as he tried to shake the thought of the twin coffins sitting behind him. Imagines of blood soaked carpet and screams for mercy engulfed his thoughts. He reached out towards the microphone, grasping it reluctantly before pulling it up to his lips. He tried to make a sound at first, but what seemed like a softball inside of his throat made it harder to vocalize than he ever imagined. Before he began to speak, he saw a tear run down the cheek of his Aunt Marie. 'We are all gathered here today to mourn the passing of Ulrich and Sylvia Zenith... my parents and your friends and family. Growing up I'd always wanted to be like my father... strong, powerful, and intelligent. My father was everything that any man could wish to be, but behind every great man is a great woman. My mother Slyvia was passionate, graceful, and nuturing... I couldn't have asked for two greater parents. As friends they were loyal and never afraid to stand up for what was right in life.' Christian paused, finding it harder and harder to speak as he went on, 'I think it's best to remember on this day all of what my parents have accomplished... and not the tragic way in which their lives were ended. Though the events in one way or another proved that they were truly in love with one another. No one will ever know exactly what happened that night but...' Suddenly Christian heard the screams of his mother in the back of his mind as a crimson ran across the floor. His breathing began to speed up... but he kept a cool exterior, concearling his troubles from the rest of the audience. 'But we must keep thougts of my parents in our hearts and in our minds. They were great people whose spirits will live on forever in the people in which they touched along the way. There are no words I can speak to you to make the pain go away. But all I can say is... for their sakes... I hope they are in a better place now.' Christian said the last line as the listening audience began to talk amoungst themselves about what Christian had just said. Dropping the microphone he walked off stage and half the audience clapped as he walked out the door to the outside. Once again the crowd began to stir, as the minister followed young Christian Zenith outside. Outside the minister saw Christian Zenith... sitting down outside the cathedral with his back against the cold stone wall. Snowflakes fell from the bright cloudy sky and you could see his quickened breathing exiting his body and dancing into the sky. Zenith's cadet blue eyes shot a look over at the minister who came over and sat down next to Christian... who was obviously upset. 'What's wrong?' The minister asked Christian who turned his head away and looked at the snow covered ground. 'They say God always has a plan... but where am I in his plans? It seems like no matter what I do, I'm just not good enough in the eyes of God. Keeps throwing me pointless obsticles to overcome. I do as he wishes and overcome, and what do I get in return? More obsticles. When do I get my reward? What is this master plan anyway?' Christian asks the minister who stares back at Zenith blankly. 'Well the reward is pretty obvious, you get to join Jesus and God in heaven.' The minister replies, 'Everyone has to go through tests you see, God can't just let anybody go to heaven. Joining the Lord's kingdom is a big deal.' 'Yeah...' Zenith says, 'Why can't God just accept me the way I am? Why does he have to keep messing up my life? Why can't he stop this sick joke and start messing with someone elses lives? I'm sick and tired of it. I just want it to end and I want to live my life without constantly being challenged and hurt. I keep reaching out to God but he doesn't answer. He keeps turning his back on me.' 'That isn't true...' 'How do you know?' Christian asks the minister who becomes very worried, 'Does God talk back to you? When you ask God to be kind to your friends and family why does he run them bankrupt and make them suffer through injuries and heartbreak. When a child is born without sight, what in hell did that baby ever do to God to play such a sick joke on him? You ever start to think that maybe God isn't running things anymore? Do you ever get the feeling God doesnt' answer our prayers because he just isn't there?' 'Hey now!' 'Stop being so jaded and wake up to the real world. If God ever did exist... he doesn't care anymore. Not about me anyways... I'm dead to God.' Christian Zenith stood up and began to walk away. The minister grew baffled and stood up and tried to follow the quick pace of Zenith walking through the parking lot. 'Christian! How dare you defy the Lord! You need to get back here and repent!' 'In God We Trust... what a load of shit.' Christian muttered to himself as he shut the car door and started the ignition. As he pulled away, he saw the minister trying to run him down in his rear view mirror. He's chosen his path in life in the eyes of the Lord... and there was no turning back now. ---- Burning on just like a match you strike to incinerate The lives of everyone you know And what's the worst you take (worst you take) from every heart you break (heart you break) And like the blade you stain (blade you stain) Well I've been holding on tonight What's the worst that I can say? Things are better if I stay So long and goodnight So long and goodnight ---- A Week Earlier... Click... Click... Click... The footsteps echoed through the darkness. His dress shoes clicked on the wood paneled floors, which lead only into darkness. The man knew that this one must be big though, he'd never recieved a call at 4am for some small time deal. Thoughts of collecting a huge paycheck filled his mind. Just another day, just another dollar didn't cut it in this world anymore. Everyone wanted more and more until the average man just didn't have anymore to give. Then they'd move onto the next one. But him... he was a specialist. A man with his calibur of skill was hard to find... and just average money wouldn't do him justice. His eyes began to adjust to the night, as he scanned around looking for a door. Click... Click... Click... 208? No... that wasn't it. He began to get anxious about what job he may be doing next. A hiest? or maybe a hit? These are the type of deals that go down in history on the streets. Anyone can kill a man with a lucky shot... not anyone can kill a man that you get a phone call at 4am for. 212... just the number he was looking far. He reached out into the darkness and turned the doorknob slowly... you never know who could be waiting on the otherside. He glanced into the room before taking a small step inside. In front of him he saw the silhouette of a rather large man standing in front of the window. His shadow cashed by the moonlight. The man slowly closed the door behind him, trying his best not to make a sound. 'Demitri... I'm glad you could make it.' A familiar voice spoke from the darkness, 'You know I wouldn't give you a phone call unless it was important. This is very hard for me to talk about... so I don't wish to speak about it for long. This will be very hard for you as well, but you must trust me when I tell you that this is for the best.' 'Of course Ulrich...' Demitri Miasnikov sat down on the bed, the moonlight highlighting his face which looks questionably back towards the shadow of a man, 'I understand completely. Sometimes business is hard... but business is business. Without business, surely both of us would be dead by now and wouldn't be here today.' 'I understand... and that is how I've gotten into this desperate decision. Over the past couple years... sales have started to go down from the manufacturing side of my company. The military wants more and more advanced weapons, and they want us to do things that I'm not entirely comfortable with. I'm not supposed to disclose this information, but talks of altering human genomes have come up. I came into this weapon to make guns and artilary... not freaks.' Ulrich paused before he continued, 'This is something I'm just unwilling to do. On the other hand... the insurance side of Zenith Corp. has skyrocketed. We are reaching numbers that we never before thought were possible. Expert business stratedgy and brilliant cutting-edge ideas has almost made Zenith Corporation unquestioned in their market. Of course you know, this is all the work of...' 'Christian.' Demitri stated... his late sister's ex-boyfriend and trusted friend. Demitri had been informed of Christian's breakout success in the company. Business magazines all over the world were talking about Chicago's new phenom. 'Right.' Ulrich paused before he continued, 'To be quite frank with you Christian's success has caught me incredibly by surprise. While going through school Christian showed flashes of brilliance but nothing like this. Enough to carry on his father's legacy of course... as he was destined to one day. With the pressure of the military and plummeting weapons sales... the pressure has been on me to make a move. Every business partner I have has been calling for my head... asking me to step down and hand the business over to Christian. Stating I'm washed up and need to get with the times. Saying I'm only hurting the company I CREATED. Do you know what that's like Demitri?' 'No sir.' Demitri answered. 'The nerve of some people to question my motives of my empire. Without me, they wouldn't even have a job. I've worked my entire life to build what I have and now they expect me to just move over and hand everything I've ever done to my son. I love Christian... I really do, but this can't go on any longer. I need Christian out of the picture so I can run my company my way again.' Ulrich told Demitri from across the room. Demitri could almost feel his jaw drop... 'You don't want me to...' 'Yes... there is no other way Demitri.' Ulrich cut him off..., 'Two million dollars Demitri as well as a job in my company for your services. I know Christian is a dear friend of yours... he is my son, I love him more than nearly everything in this world. But he needs to be stopped. His tragic demise would make news all over the world, the incoming pity for his remaining company and family would generate millions in revenue and the military will have no choice but to cave to my desire and extend our contract. But I can't do this without you.' 'I can't do that...' 'Five million Demitri. Two million up front and Three million once he's buried. This pains me as much as it does you Demitri... it isn't something I'm proud of. Look at it this way... finally you can stop living job to job and have enough money to support your poverished family. Your dear mother and father, who live in filth in a run down appartment building could live in a big house in the suburbs. You'll never have to work another job again. If anyone asks where the money came from I'll give you a nice high paying cushy job where you don't have to do a thing except for show up everyday. I'll forever be in your debt, but first... I need this favor from you. Will you do it Demitri?' Ulrich asked as Demitri looked down at the ground deep in thought. Christian Zenith had become one of his best friends, but his family needs him and the money. He just couldn't pass up an opportunity like this. He couldn't do that to his mother... '...I'll do it.' Demitri reluctantly said. 'Great.' Ulrich said, smirking in the moonlight. 'I'll give you everything you'll ever need to know first thing in the morning at the Zenith Corporation building downtown.' ---- Came a time When every star fall brought you to tears again We are the very hurt you sold And what's the worst you take (worst you take) from every heart you break (heart you break) And like the blade you stain (blade you stain) Well I've been holding on tonight What's the worst that I can say? As each snowflake swirled towards the earth, every one became unique in their own way. Snowflakes are much like people... although they are made of identical organic structures, each becomes their own before their demise. While some snowflakes fall destined to be destroyed... others are given comfortable lives living forever in the tallest mountains. Sometimes snowflakes have a weird way of cheating death... ending up in somebody's freezer until they grow old and past their time. But no single snowflake does this on it's own... but as part of a cohesive group which lives on together as one. As they began to fall on the white wonderland that is the streets of Chicago, a man in a black hood along with 3 others dressed the same way made their way down the street. The man leading the way watched carefully with his cadet blue eyes. For a few seconds the steam from his own breath blocked his vision, but he simply walked through it as he stopped under a nearby street light. He looked around in the shadows before he saw a red spark to flame... which was quickly extinguished. Christian Zenith motioned towards the dark alley, following where the flame had ignited. On the way across the street, Christian looked back at the men following him. Their faces turning red into the below zero windchill. 'He's over here behind that dumpster.' Christian said aloud as he stepped back onto the sidewalk. Behind him Jason LeMaster and Demitri Miasnikov followed him into the darkness. 'Why can't we do this inside?' Jason LeMaster commented before shaking a little under the cold conditions. 'Out here we're not trapped. Our options are more open than they would be inside.' Christian answered stopping in front of the dumpster as Demitri and Jason both pulled out their guns. Jason carried on him a Magnum and Demitri carried a trusted Desert Eagle. They walked ahead and made eye contact with the man behind the dumpster before jumping around the corner armed... only to see nothing but street. 'It's clear.' Jason LeMaster said before turning back around, looking at Christian and the man behind the dumpster. 'Men this is Dwayne Carson. He's a local dealer... he's here to speak with us about arms.' Christian said as he looked back at his men, 'I've spoken to Mr. Carson here in private and he's agreed to stop selling drugs and let us in on his territory if we supply him with standard handguns. Mr. Carson's gang has been involved in a lot of knife fights and drive-by's recently and have no way of defending themselves other than box cutters. By aligning with us, he's gained protection... and we've gained territory to further strengthen our ranks.' 'That is true.' Dwayne responded as Christian opened up his jacket to pull out a gun. Demitri's heart began to race as Christian reached deep into his jacket. Ulrich's words resonated in Demitri's mind... he knew what he had to do, and in order to do it he had to make sure Christian wasn't armed any longer. No matter who was around. 'Stand back guys!' Demitri stated as he grabbed Christian Zenith by the neck and pulled him back. Zenith dropped the hand gun to the ground in surprise as he began to lose his breath in Demitri's choke. Suddenly, Zenith had a Desert Eagle up to his temple. Jason LeMaster and Dwayne both looked in shock as Zenith's cadet blue eyes remained focused on the gun now located on the gravel alley. 'Drop your gun Jason!' 'What the fuck is your...' 'Drop your gun Jason!' Demitri stated before LeMaster placed his magnum on the ground. Dwayne's eyes grew wide looking at what was beginning to happen in front of him, 'Sorry Christian... I have to do this... my family needs me.' 'If you're going to shoot me Demitri, I'd rather you not waste your time apologizing and just fuckin shoot me.' Christian choked out from under Demitri's forearm. 'After everything I've done for you. I've helped support your family and even stayed your friend after your lunatic sister took everything I had in my bank account. What's this about? money? Your no different than your sister if you pull that trigger... an honorless thief.' 'Shut up.' Demitri said as Christian continued to choke off words. 'You can shut me up anytime you want to... but just remember, after I'm gone there will be others. I have your back and I've had your back for years now... you can't turn your back on me now. We've gone through too much... and we have much more to do.' Christian stated. Demitri hesitated keeping the gun pointed at Zenith's brain. 'Go ahead and shoot me if I'm just another price to you. You know you can trust me and I keep my promises... how do you know that this isn't just a trick to get you to do their dirty work? You don't... you need me Demitri.' Suddenly Demitri let go of Christian Zenith and he dropped down to the ground on his knees regaining his breath. Jason got down with Christian as Demitri looked down at them with a relieved look on his face. Jason helped Christian back up to his feet as Dwayne continues to look on with shock. Zenith's cadet blue eyes look deep into Demitri's. 'Who is behind this?' Christian asked Demitri who avoided the question by turning his back. Zenith turned him back around and looked into his eyes, 'You tell me right now who is behind this.' 'Your father...' Demitri muttered. 'What?!' 'I said your fucking dad!' Demitri screamed back, 'Five million dollars and a job. He's already given me two million, I get three million once you're under ground. He said he'd take care of my family and get us off the streets.' 'This is some kinda sick joke right?' Christian Zenith backed up against the wall in shock. He looked back at Demitri... whose facial expression told the whole story, 'Right?!' 'No... I wish is was.' Demitri replied. Zenith looked over at Dwayne and then to Jason. His cadet blue eyes narrowed as he became enfuriated. 'Get the hell out of my way!' Christian Zenith screamed as he pushed Demitri aside and ran out of the alley onto the street. Jason ran after him, but it was too late. Zenith was halfway down the street. No one was going to catch him now. Jason looked as Zenith disappeared into the darkness, before turning around and focusing back on the alleyway. 'I know where he's going.' Demitri replied, as Jason looked back at him questionably, 'If I was going to turn on you guys both of you would be dead right now and you know it.' Even though Jason still didn't fully trust Demitri... he didn't have much of a choice. ---- Can you hear me? Are you near me? Can we pretend to leave and then We'll meet again When both our cars collide? ---- BANG! A startled Ulrich Zenith fell out of his chair when his door echoed off the wall. Ulrich clawed to find scattered papers on the floor before pulling himself up to his feet. As soon as he reached a verticle base he was grabbed by the collar of his gray business suit and shoved against the wall. He looked back into the fuming cadet blue eyes of his son, wearing a black hooded nylon jacket over top a black long sleeve shirt and a pair of black jeans. Ulrich's heart raced as his son's breath could be felt against his neck. His eyes grew wide as he reached out for something... anything to use as a weapon. 'What's wrong dad?' Christian questioned of his father, 'Am I not supposed to be standing here right now?' 'Christian!' His dad lectured, 'What in the hell do you think you're doing?' 'Admit it!' Christian screamed at Ulrich, still holding him up... pinned against the wall, 'You had Demitri try to kill me!' Ulrich Zenith took a deep gulp and sweat began to pour down his forehead. His eyes shot back and forth across the room... trying as hard as he could not to look at his surprisingly alive son. Tears began to flow out of Zenith's intense cadet blue eyes, he began to shake all over as he watched his father turn bright red. 'Damn soviet bastard...' Ulrich said while looking away from Christian. That's all Christian needed to hear before he threw his father down onto the ground. Ulrich hit the floor hard and began to crawl towards the door before Christian kicked it shut with his foot. Ulrich backed up into the door, looking up at Christian. 'I'm sorry Christian... you have to believe me! Now you know I'd never do that to you. Obviously Demitri is trying to get you to hurt me. I've raised you and sent you to the finest specialist and the greatest teachers in the world. Why would I do all that for someone I don't love huh Christian? That doesn't make sense does it?' 'No.' Christian said before burying his foot into his father's stomach and watching him crumble over onto the ground in pain. Ulrich crawled across the floor away from the door trying to get away from his son, 'You never loved us Ulrich. You always sent Tera and I away to fancy schools and expensive specialists so that you wouldn't have to deal with pesky little kids. Tera got tired of your shit and ran away... and you didn't even lift a finger to look for her! She probably starved to death out in the cold and you don't even care! All you ever cared about was your money and your contracts! Your right... it doesn't make any sense, none of it ever did. But that doesn't make you any less guilty you deadbeat asshole!' Christian ripped open the rop drawer of his dad's desk and pulled out a chrome colt python. Ulrich looked up like a deer in headlights as Christian pointed it at his forehead. 'You had it all wrong dad...' Christian said before he spun the barrell, 'You were the one who was going to die tonight all along.' BANG! Just then the door was pushed open and Sylvia Zenith looked as Christian on his knees crying holding a silver colt python in his hand. As she moved in closer she saw Ulrich Zenith lying on the floor in front of him. A scream temporarily deafened Christian Zenith as suddenly Demitri and Jason ran into the room and closed the door behind them. Sylvia backed up away from Christian shaking with her eyes wide. 'Someone call the police. Get an ambulance! Help me! HELP ME!!!!' She began to scream in hysterics before she bumped into Jason and fell over. She looked over at Jason and screamed once again as Christian stood up and looked at his mother cowering on the ground. Christian slowly walked over to her... 'Mom...' Christian said, 'I don't want to hurt you mom. Mom calm down... it isn't your fault we can get over this okay? Dad was a bad bad man... and he tried to kill me and he doesn't deserve to be here any longer. But you never did anything wrong... you'll be fine mom I promise...' 'How could you!' Sylvia screamed in tears, 'You're not my son! You're a monster! A MONSTER!' 'How could you say that mom?' Christian said as his eyes narrowed, 'I've never done anything to you mom. Haven't I always been a good son? Done my chores and got good grades in school? I always said please and thank you.' Sylvia began to cry hysterically on the floor as Demitri, Jason, and Christian looked down at her. Her words began to not make any sense as she cried into the carpet. Zenith looked up at Demitri and Jason, before he closed his eyes reluctantly... 'I love you...' BANG! ---- What's the worst that I can say? Suds begin to fill the sink as Christian Zenith continues to scrub his hands. He looks down at his hands before picking up the worn down bar of soap again and scrubbing once again. He drops the bar of soap onto the side of the sink and turns off the faucet drying off his hands. As he rubs the towel over his hands... he begins to hear the screams of his mother once again. They never really went away... they've just become easier to ignore over the years. Suddenly the door opened and Christian threw the towel down onto the ground. 'Are you ready for the Battle Royal?' Vivica asked, sounding a little concerned. 'Yeah.' Christian Zenith said before picking a pair of sunglasses up off the small table by the door and putting them over his cadet blue eyes. 'Do you ever remember when you were a little girl Vivica and your parents used to always tell you that God had a plan?' 'Yeah... all the time.' Vivica said with a chuckled as Christian walked out of the bathroom past her. 'Why?' 'Well I have a plan.' Christian said with an eeirly uncharacteristic smirk, 'Zenith has a plan for everyone.'
