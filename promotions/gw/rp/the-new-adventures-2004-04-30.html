The New Adventures

30 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/30/04 Juan Gonzalez stood on a rock with his arms bent at his waist. He stared off into the distance with his long black hair dangling behind him. He smiled as the wind blew by making him appear as a super hero. And thus it was, that the great 'El Mariachi' was still undeafeted in Global Wrestling. Still a perfect two and zero. The master of the 'Les Miserable', noted for his outstanding body... FART 'Eww...did you fart Big Nasty Bastard?' Spencer K fades in wearing a haiwan style shirt, his hair pushed back with gel and baggy pair of brown cargo pants. 'That's why I'm a Nasty Bastard!' Big Nast Bastard picks his nose and flicks it to the ground. 'Yeah but you were doing the opening scene...and you farted!' El Mariachi looks a bit upset as he steps down the stone. 'You're rocking the camera man! Hulk! You're rocking the camera!' CRASH The camera lays on the ground showing only feet. The argument can still be heard in the background. 'Hulk what are you doing?' 'I got tired of holding it.' 'So you just dropped it? I mean just left it there, to die...to fade away forever.' 'Exactly!' 'Come on guys, I gotta get this done before going to Dallas to meet Jetsui.' 'Is he Chineese, because if he is he'll become good friends with Hideglo Mitsu, speaking of which where is Hidelgo?' Hulk lifts the camera, Hidelgo emmerges from behind a stone. 'I like cheese!' Big Nasty Bastard lets out a chuckle as El Mariachi takes a look at Spencer K. Hulk, Hidelgo and Nasty Bastard are all having a good laugh over Hidelgo's antics. El Mariachi runs up to the stone, does a short crip walk and dives off doing the famous worm in the process. He gets up, dustes off his clothes and looks over at Spencer K who has his mouth wide open. El Mariachi puts his finger over his lips. 'Shhh. And the funny thing Spencer K, is that nobody saw it and nobody will believe you.' 'You're right, that was great man...that was great.' The five men walk away from the stone headed to a large RV in what appears to be a camp ground. Hidelgo is the first to the RV and he heads in, behind him Nasty Bastard and finally Hulk who is still carrying the camera. 'You guys are great and it's been awesome these last two weeks out here in the wild. We've got to experiance a lot of great things,' El Mariachi leans on the RV. 'We saw the great fall of Nasty Bastard, we witnessed Hidelgo wrestle a mount lion to the death and we saw Hulk eat a chicken, whole!' Nasty Bastard exits the RV and comes up next to El Mariachi. 'Hey you remember when we were at that place with them guys, and then they made a big fuss about someone not paying for there food?' 'Yeah.' 'Well I was thinking, you know what I would have done? I would have lined them all up and started shooting them in the head until someone fessed up.' 'That's sick!' Spencer K looks appalled. 'So long as I'm not the one getting shot I'm fine with it, so I would have just shot them all in the head until they confessed...you know why?' 'Why?' 'Cause I'm a Nasty Bastard!' All three men laugh. Hideglo pokes his head out the window and calls Nasty Bastard to go play him in Sumo Volleyball. Bastard depards leaving Spencer K and El Mariachi alone once again. 'Well Mariachi, hows it feel to wrestle?' 'Well bro honestly, all I do is just git-down~! git-down~!' 'Ha Ha...git-down~!' 'That's why I'm two and zero! Yeah you know it! And after Monday night at Evo, I plan to be three and zero!' 'How you figure?' 'Well bro, what I'm first gonna do is make sure Jetsui knows I'm with him all the way, secondly I'm gonna ask your mom out on a date, then I'm gonna make love to you...' Once again Spencer K looks appalled,'What?' 'Dude! I'm talking about making love to you, the French way. Where they tell there friends thank you. What'd you think I was talking about? Pervert!' 'Oh...' 'Then after I finish that, I'll do a little jive, do a little a dance...get down tonight!' As if in syncronized fashion, Nasty Bastard, Hidelgo and Hulk stick there head out the window. 'Do a little dance! Make a little love...ooh!' Then again in syncronized fashion, all three men disappear. 'Random huh?' Spencer K scratches his head. 'Random was the time your dad got his finger stuck in his nose?' 'Huh?' 'Whoa dude? Did you hear that?' 'What you said about my dad?' 'Err...I didn't say it. Must've been Hidelgo. Lets get out of here bro. I gotta make my way to Dallas!' 'Where are we staying at while you're out with Jetsui talking strategy? Do we get to paint the town?' 'Only if you mean litterly, like actually go and grab red paint and go paint the town.' 'Oh yeah that's how I meant it!' 'You know it then bro!' The scene changes to the inside of the boss where Hulk is once again filming, with Big Nasty Bastard Talking... 'Thus it begins, the new story for El Mariachi. The new adventures with his new friend Jetsui. WHat will become of it? Will they come out on top? Can they be trusted...' FART 'Eww you did it again! Nasty Bastard!'
