Change in the Family

6 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1.6.05 Darkness. The cold rain falls from above, sticking to everything it touches. I turn the key back toward me, killing the engine of my 300C. I sit there for a few seconds, listening as the sleet plasters to the windshield. When I open the door, a rush of wind races down my collar, tingling my back, daring me back in my car. I ignore the cold and pull the umbrella out from behind the seat. Leaving it closed, I walk into the darkness. ---- By the time I reach the gravesite, my blue hair is matted down, my shoulders and upper back are soaked, and I am greeted by a warm hug from my wife. She looks sadly up at me, so I wrap my arms tighter around her. 'It will be okay,' I whisper, knowing that to be a lie. It will not be okay, because nothing can bring somebody back from the dead. I do my best to encourage my wife at this emotional time. 'Derek, how can it be okay? Grampa's dead. My dad is sitting by himself, undoubtedly crying. I don't know what to do.' I open the umbrella and hold it over our heads, moreso hers than mine. My eyes close and I fight back tears, thinking about my grandfather-in-law. The first time I met him. The first Christmas I spent with the family. High school graduation, when they had a barbecue for me. All three proms Cindy and I went to, when I could not smile long enough for her grandparents to take pictures of their only grandchild. Our engagement party. The wedding. The announcement that they were great-grandparents to be. And now I'm comforting my wife after a funeral. Her grandfather's funeral. MY grandfather's funeral, I'm family now. He made me family. Before the wave of sadness can overcome me, I think back on how Grampa Cruse was killed. Murder. He was murdered in cold blood. The police speculated it was a car jacking gone bad. They said he was pulling away from the ATM in his Mercedes, and a street thug came over and dragged him out of the car. Supposedly, the shooter was using a .25 caliber pistol, very compact, easy to hide. The cops say Grampa refused to give in to the demands, and was shot. Five times. Four in the chest. One in the back of the head. I clinch my eyes shut in rage and hold my wife tight. I start shaking from the extreme anger and sorrow building up inside me. 'Let's get to the car, you're freezing,' I hear whispered in my ear. Cindy looks up at me, full of a strange mix of love and longing. Cindy steps away from me and walks over to her father, Bobby. She puts a hand on his back and kisses him on the cheek. I can hear saying something, but I don't understand what. They stand up and start walking back toward me. When they get here, Cindy takes the umbrella from me, and Bobby puts his hands on my shoulders. 'Thank you, Derek, for everything. You're exactly the son I've always wanted,' he whispers before embracing me. ---- Back in the car, Cindy sitting beside me, with her father behind her, I crank they key over, letting the Hemi roar to life. Not much is said until we pull up in the driveway of my in-laws' house. Bobby leans forward, kisses Cindy on the cheek, rests his hand on my shoulder. 'Things will be changing soon. Looks like I'm the head of the family now. You two be careful on your way home, ok? The roads are terrible,' he whispers. ---- About 15 minutes later, on the other side of the city, I'm driving down I-75 about 2 miles from my exit. Cindy has fallen asleep in the pasenger seat, her left hand over the console in my right hand. From nowhere, a black Lincoln Continental comes up from behind me, maintains speed beside me. I look over at it, but can't see inside because of the dark windows. I speed up to get this car out of my way, but they keep up beside me. I slow down, but shortly after, I see their brake lights come on. I panic. I wake Cindy up, telling her to stay low in the seat. Right after I wake her, the back window shatters, sending glass in to the back seat. I glance over and see a gun out the window of the Lincoln. I swerve my car over, right in to the side of the other car in an attempt to run them off the road. It fails. Cindy picks up her cell phone and dials 911, while I'm trying to lose our pursuants. I slam on the brakes, and when the Lincoln is just ahead of me, back tire lined up with my front, i punch the gas and turn my steering wheel sharply to the right. The lincoln spins around in front of me, but apparently the driver has had the Fishtail Tactic applied to him before, as he corrects it and speeds off in the opposite direction. 'Are you ok?' I ask my wife while driving off. 'Yeah. I just want to get home to Damon.' ---- We got home safe. But I could not sleep that night. On top of Grampa Cruse's funeral, somebody shot out my car window, tried to run me off the road, and most of all, they endangered my wife. Somebody will pay.
