Continuing the mission

23 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/23/2005 Three people? All going for one thing? The glory? The pride? The prestige? And only one can win. October 24th, 2005. The night that Elia Smith will show the world that she is a force to wreck in with. Will she win or will she loose? Came see as she fights for her right for Glory in Dark Rituals. --------------------------------------------- October 23rd, 2005 7:00 PM “Ok and she did what to you again sir?” Peaceful. Sort of a dry feeling of air in the sky today. Even though it was cold it felt warm to the touch. The tree’s blew in the breezy winds that had come through. Though the winds weren’t the only thing getting blown through. The harsh words that were coming from my uncle to the man who was wearing the police badge. “She beat me with a bat. I’d like to file chargers towards here sir.” raising up off the seat he was sitting in. “Now you know it’ll take some time before we can pick her up so once we get the paperwork all filed out you can sign it and we can proceed.” replies the officer. “Thank you sir.” My uncle says as he shakes his hands. Gathering up his papers, the officer begins to towards the front door, walking by all the pictures and stuff in the house. Opening up the door, standing there was Mrs. Smith, standing there with groceries in her hand. “Hello?” Says the officer. “Hello. Can I ask why you are here?” Asks Mrs. Smith. “I’ll explain Jessica.” telling Mrs. Smith as he takes the food out of her hand. She came right on in to the house carrying what little bit of groceries that he didn’t take. Her face could tell a lot of questions she seem to want to ask. “Now what’s going on here Alex?” She responds. “Now just settle down but I did what was needed to be done.” Replying before placing the bags up on the counter in the kitchen. “What did you do ALEX?” Raising her voice just a little. You could cut the tension in the room with a samurai sword. Anger. Displeasure. All the emotions wrapping up into one huge one. “I called the cops to have her picked up.” Replying to her question. “YOU DID WHAT?” “Settle down now Jessica.” Raising his hands up in the air to calm her down. “If she is in jail then she won’t hurt anyone. The demon inside her won’t make her do anything that she doesn’t want to do. This is how we won the last time.” “Yes but not like this.” Walking away from the kitchen towards the living room. Just when they thought they were alone. Walking in through the backdoor I came. Wearing a black leather skirt with matching boots. A leather top on my chest to hold back what I knew every man wanted from me. Walking into the house, going straight for the corner of the dining room away from them just a few feet away from them as the voice continued. “How else do you think it should happen” I mean if we wait any longer then the demon will win just like it did with your grandfather and with your great grandfather.” Responding was Alex. Standing there, watching them both battle it out in the dark shadow of the dining room. “I’m not going to stand here and listen to this. I’d rather not know about it then to stand here and listen to it.” Walking away was Alex. Before she can get down the hallway, it’s almost as if she expects something of the ordinary, Reaching out for the body of Alex and pulling him right out of the very place he was standing. He was mine and he was going to pay for what he said and did. Trying to stop me? That’s wasn’t going to happen. “Alex” Alex?” Asking out for her brother who wasn’t anywhere in sight. Walking back into the living room, looking around to see nothing but the front door wide open. ------------------------------------------ October 24th, 2005 1:50 AM “Wake up sleepy head.” Standing over a dozy old man who’s life right now wasn’t even worth a penny. Watching him as he pulls both eyes open. “Where am I?” Asking with the sight of pain all over his face. Walking out of the shadows of the room were the two of us stood. Looking down at him as the shock of life from his very own came rushing out from him. Almost as if he was dying from the sight of me. With every step I took I knew it meant more to him then me. “You’re in a place were you were meant to me.” Grinning while licking my lips at him. “A place there you know very well.” It was true and his eyes were showing it. It was a piss smelling place. Dark all around the walls where he was laying in the chair, on his back looking up at the ceiling. “What are you talking about Elia?” Crying as he began looking around for me. “You don’t remember do you?” Walking up to him as his eyes cross over looking right at me. “What are you talking about. I don’t understand.” Responding with a feeling of worry in his voice. Oh it was tragic that he didn’t remember. It wasn’t long before when he was corrupt with his demon. His mind, his body, everything not in his control. It wasn’t that long ago that somehow another he was saved, having the demon taken away from him. Looking down at him before falling right into his face, getting so close to his lips with mine I was almost kidding him. “December thirteenth, nineteen-seventy four. That night you had everything going for you till you came upon this place. This is where it all started for you. Where you and him came together. And this is were?”, pausing for a moment as he gave him a sweet kiss on the cheek. ??you two will come together once again. “ELIA!” screaming, trying to get away from the chairs grip. “Your not right. I swear baby you need help. He can’t beat you if you get help.” “Help” What makes you think I need help? He’s given me more then anyone in my life. He’s taken control of my life and my destiny and made me more then a female but a queen.” Pulling away from him with my eyes still looking on to him. “Elia I swear I have no idea what you are talking about. I’ve never dealt with the demon before. But I know who has. Just please let me go baby.” Trying to pull away from the chair still. “You do?” Turning around. “Yes?”, Alex responds. Walking away from him, knowing his was a person that wasn’t on to tell a lie. Walking towards the darkness and standing there for a moment. “Who then?” Asking him with my back towards him. “Your Uncle Thomas. He was and still is I believe.” Crying with every word. “Still is” How?” Asking still with my back to him. “I don’t know how but he’s locked away in a hospital outside of Maine. Just let me go and?” Before he could finish, looking up to where I was standing before seeing my eyes looking out through the darkness. I had my next clue. I was going to finish what the demon had started several hundred years ago. I was going to finish everything. Dropping out of the light all together before I said my last goodbyes. “Elia?” Alex Asks. “Rest up tight because Alex, Uncle Johnson, your time isn’t up yet.” With that, walking backwards and out of sight of him or everyone else. The last think that was heard was the screams of Alex in my ear. The mission was continuing and there was nothing anyone could do about it now.
