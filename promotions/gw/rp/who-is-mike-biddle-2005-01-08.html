Who is Mike Biddle?

8 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 8, 2005 Mike Biddle had found a cure for his boredom in the form of a written contract with Global Wrestling. The contract itself took a lot of time to iron out and the negotiations demanded a lot of his free time which kept the young millionaire from attending the various social events that he had received invitations to. While partaking in his quest to cure his boredom Mike ended up neglecting a few personal “needs” and as a result it had been quite sometime since he had felt a woman’s touch. His particular personal need had led Mike to attend a social event being held just down the street from his Beverly Hills home. He arrived at the event alone but had plans to leave the party with some random attractive woman and if he was lucky two or more. He was dressed to thrill in his best Armani suit, a carefully selected cologne liberally applied to his person, and his short blond hair neatly trimmed with every strand perfectly combed in place. Mike stood in the center of the ballroom perusing the crowd for easy prey while sipping on a dry vodka martini. After a few moments he laid his dark blue eyes upon a brunette with a particularly large chest that was barely covered by a small red dress and a very skanky air about her. With a smirk Mike quickly finished the rest of his drink and handed the empty glass to a passing waiter before approaching his latest conquest. As he made his way towards the attractive woman he noticed that she was alone, instantly making her a done deal in Mike’s mind. He stood behind her and tapped her on the shoulder, she turned around and his ego didn’t allow him to notice that she rolled her eyes once she saw him. “Hi there,” Mike said in his most arrogant voice and most sincere smile, “my name is Mike Biddle, perhaps you’ve heard of me.” “That’s nice,” the brunette said with a sigh that caused her bosom to heave up and down...an event that did not go unnoticed by Mike. Mike decided that the time to act was now so he placed his arm around the brunette’s waist and leaned over to whisper in her ear. “How about the two of us get out of here and go back to my place?” He asked before he nibbled at her earlobe. The brunette didn’t say a word; she simply removed Mike’s arm, slapped his face, and stormed away from him. A hush came over those gathered near the incident as they turned to stare at Mike as he held his cheek. “What?” He asked as he waved off the onlookers. He stood there for a moment; fully aware of the attention he was receiving, and attempted to come up with a new game plan. A curious look came over his handsome face as he wondered how the brunette didn’t recognize his name, after all he was the most irresistible man on the planet and being turned down by a woman was completely foreign to him. “Maybe she had too much to drink,” he said under his breath. “She’ll definitely be kicking herself in the morning when she realizes what she gave up.” A smirk formed on Mike’s face as a new plan had entered his mind. He began to walk toward the small bar set up in the corner of the ballroom and his face lit up when he noticed that a sultry red head sat at the end of the bar. The look on her face made it appear as if she was daring the single men in attendance to take the chance to converse with her. Mike decided to take the dare and slid onto the barstool next to hers. The red head didn’t notice him at first but he soon caught her attention when he cleared his throat rather loudly. She turned her attention to him and her annoyance was instantly lost upon him. “Perhaps you’ve heard of me,” Mike began with a smile, “my name is Mike Biddle.” “Never heard of you,” the red head said before she walked off. Mike watched the red head walk away with a disgusted look on his face. Crestfallen, Mike decided it was time to leave the party and perhaps do some training or anything else to take his mind off of this disastrous night. “How the hell can this happen to me?” He silently asked himself. “I’m Mike Biddle damn it!” Once he exited the building and stepped out into the cool California night he began to feel calmer. He decided that it wasn’t him that was the problem, it was the women at the party. Mike’s thoughts were interrupted when he heard the sound of mucus ridden voice ask a question. “Who do you guys think is the greatest wrestler of all time?” The voice asked. Mike turned and found a collection of nerds gathered on the front porch of the mansion. Due to the subject they were discussing Mike decided to stay within ear shot, after all he could use the ego stroking at this particular moment in time. “I’d say that Dark Angel is the greatest wrestler of all time,” was the reply one of the nerds. The reply caused Mike to snicker a little bit, Darek DeAngelo was nothing more than a little fairy and thought of him being the “greatest of all time” was the most absurd statement Mike had ever heard in his life. “You’re wrong,” came another reply, “Chris Storm is the greatest of all time!” This particular answer left Mike confused, who the hell was Chris Storm? “That’s incorrect,” said the original mucus ridden voice, “Mike Biddle is in fact the greatest wrestler to ever live! Who else could pull off a Hot Cocoa Death Match?” The nerdy boy’s reply made Mike smile broadly. However, Mike’s smile was quickly wiped off his face when the other nerds asked, “Who is Mike Biddle?” An angry look came over Mike’s face as the discussion continued in the background. How could anyone not know who he is? This was a problem he was going to have to fix, some way...somehow. -To Be Continued-
