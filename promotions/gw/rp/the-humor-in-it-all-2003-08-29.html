The Humor In It All

29 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/29/03 In a pair of faded blue jean shorts, hanging half off his ass, with a pair of red and green plad boxers showing, a white tank top, with a gold necklace dangling from around his neck, topped off with a retro styled Baltimore Oriols baseball hat sitting sideways on his head Kid Flash stands alone in front of a wall mirror, impersonating a rapper with his hand cupped in front of his face as if he is holding a microphone. Flash bobs his head front to back, side to side, in rythem with whatever imaginary beat he has going in his head. While at the same time jerking his hand around as all rappers are known to do. The sound of Flash's hotel room door opening causes Flash to stop his 'rappin' and he turns to see who has interrupted him. Flash turns to see Tommy Rock with a smile on his face. Flash, in a semi-cool way nods at Tommy and continues to do his impersonation into the mirror. Tommy stands and watches Kid Flash continue his rappin, until Flash's hotel room door opens again. Flash again stops and along with Tommy Rock turn to see who it happens to be this time. Through the doorway, dressed in a long, silk lavender dress, with her black streaked blond hair tied up into a bun, walks Star. Immediately following her through the door way in a pair of blue jeans, and a plain white t-shirt is Ice. Star turns to Ice and immediately begins to chuckle as Ice shakes his head in disbeliefe. 'Yo yo yo. Wazzup Izzle?' Ice's head raises up and he angrily looks at Flash. 'Don't call me that.' Flash looks at Tommy and shrugs. 'My bad DAWG!' Flash bumps his chest with his fist. Ice turns from Flash and shakes his head at Star who's face is a bright shade of red, because of her attempt at not busting out in complete laughter. Flash turns his attention back to the mirror and continues his rappin. Ice walks over to Kid Flash and watches for a moment before Tommy Rock joins Ice at observing Kid Flash. 'What in the hell is he doing?' Tommy puts his hand on Ice's shoulder and smiles widely at him. 'He's practicing.' Ice looks at Tommy and raises his left eyebrow. 'Practicing what? How to give a blow job?' Flash suddenly stops and turns towards Ice. Kid Flash thug walks over to Ice and gets chest to chest, face to face with him. 'Why you gotta be hatin DAWG?! I'm da shiznit! And after Evolution I'll also be bling blinging as I win that Primestizzar Tizzle!' Ice's serious expression is broken as a smirk sneaks across his face. Again Ice lowers his head and shakes it from side to side in disbelief. 'Look, You do whatever it is you and Tommy think you gotta do Kid. I'm not even going to attempt to talk some reason into you about this. I just came over because at Evolution we both have some important business to attent to. You with your Primestar Title match and me with my match against that NEVER WAS Roz Blayze, and I just wanted to make sure that we were clear with everything and on the same page.' Kid Flash adjusts the gold necklace dangling from his neck, and opens his mouth to speak, but is immediately cut off before he can even get a single word out. 'Ok good, now that I know everything is clear, and before I have to listen to any more of your moronics, I'm going to go, I've go some other things I have to do. You continue....whatever....you are doing. I'll see you later.' Ice quickly walks away from Kid Flash and Tommy Rock, and grabs Star by the arm, and rushes her out of the room and down the hallway. Star wrestles away from Ice's death grip on hier right arm and slaps him on the chest. Her nose crinkles up as she looks at Ice. 'What the hell was that for?! I didn't even get to ask how Amber was, or get to call her or anything.!' Again Star slaps Ice on the chest. She goes to slap him a third time, but Ice grabs her arm and stops her before she can abuse him anymore. 'WHOA! Enough with the slapping already. I get enough of that in the ring, and in the bed, I don't need any right now.' Star smirks at his comment, and Ice smiles back at her and wraps his arms around her, before giving her a peck on the cheek. 'Look, the reason, I rushed us out of there is because if I heard the Kid speak anymore of his jibberish, I was going to have to do what I did to Jin last week on Evolution to him. I don't think any of us wanted me to do that to him.' Star busts out into an uncontrollable laughter. Ice shakes his head as he holds Star up from falling over. Star's laughter begins to subside as she wipes the tears away from her eyes. 'You have to admit, that was some pretty funny stuff.' 'No actually, I don't think seeing one of the best competitors I've ever seen and been in the ring with degrade himself in such a way is that amusing.' Star pulls away from Ice and shoots him a quizzicle look. Ice returns the quizzicle look. 'I never said anything about Flash. I was talking about what we did to Jin last week on Evolution.' An evilly delighted grin races across Ice's face. Star grins back with a grin just as evil, and turns to walk away from Ice, but Ice stops her, by grabbing her arm. 'What is going through that sadistically sick, yet sexy mind of yours?' Star turns and walks out of the hotel without replying to Ice's question. Ice stands and watches her leave the hotel, before methodically following the blond bombshell out of the large building. The scene fades to commerical.
