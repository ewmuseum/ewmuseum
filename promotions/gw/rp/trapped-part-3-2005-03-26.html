Trapped (Part 3)

26 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26th March 2005 Hope? When man has lost everything all he has left is hope. All he has left is the hope in himself, the hope that he has the strength to carry on and that he will not allow himself to be beaten. But the question is, how far can hope take a man? Can hope give a man such strength that he can fight until he has gained everything he has lost? Personally my answer to this question is leaning more towards the no. For nearly 2 months I have had nothing but my hope to hang onto and at this moment in time it is beginning to wear thin. The emotion of hope to me seems to be nothing but a distant memory. Without my family for me there is no hope? You have all seen my hope disappear over the past few weeks. Every defeat I have taken is a symbol of my hope fading away. A couple of months ago I was Cruiserweight Champion, I was happy with a wife and kid and I was making a fast rise through the GW. Then suddenly Richard Wood decides to poke his stupid little nose into my life and practically tear it apart. Now I spend my days in a steel box having sterilised food given to me through a slot in a door! All I have to look forward to is the endless experiments just so Wood can “understand” my bio suit better. The only freedom I get is when I attend the GW events. Yet whilst I am there I am never at a hundred percent because I always feel like I am being watched by someone and as you can guess that is pretty distracting. I am not saying that it is because of the feeling of being watched that I am losing my matches because obviously it is partly down to me also. But I ask you how can a man, who is locked up like an animal every day and every night for nearly 2 months, always be at a hundred percent? I have faced some tough opponents the past couple of months and I give them credit because they deserved the victory over me. But all I seem to face every time I go into a GW event is defeat. For a fighter who has the amount of pride in his work as I do, this recent losing streak is a hard pill for me to swallow. Of course Wood is not taking my losing streak any better than I am. He thinks I am losing because I am rebelling against him and that I am not trying hard enough. I tell him every time that it is hard to concentrate when you don’t know where your family is or if they are even safe. He always laughs at this and tells me that my family are perfectly safe, still expecting that excuse to satisfy my curiosity. It doesn’t of course and so I become even more infuriated and even more distracted. Now I have know that someone on the GW roster is actually watching me but I do not know who. If I am to stand any chance of winning the No.1 Contender spot for the World Title at Clash Of The Titans then I need to buckle down, I need to forget all distractions, I need to win that rumble? “Adam, are you awake?” I had my back to the two way mirror as I lay on my steel bed staring directly at the steel wall. The “scientists” behind the mirror had been trying to get my attention for the passed 5 minutes but I wasn’t ready to face them not yet. “Adam it is time to get up.” Came a different scientist’s voice over the speakers. I stayed still. “Do you think he could be ill?” One of the scientists asked his fellow colleagues obviously forgetting to switch the microphone off. “No his vitals are pretty stable.” Came a male scientist’s voice, “Try him again.” “Come on Adam, you’ve got a busy day ahead. You don’t want to keep Mr. Wood waiting.” “Maybe I do.” Came my sarcastic reply as I continued to stare at the wall. “He won’t be very happy.” One of the scientists behind the mirror replied, obviously expecting that to frighten me. “I don’t care.” I said still not moving. “But if Mr. Wood isn’t happy then who knows who he might take it out on.” I jumped up, knowing that they were referring to my family. It could have been an empty threat but I didn’t want to risk it, if my family were safe I didn’t want to jeopardise their position. “Ah glad you are co-operating today. Your bio-suit will be with you within a few seconds. And Charlie will be in, to accompany you to the lab in a few minutes where Mr. Wood is waiting.” “Thank you.” I said whilst I flipped them the finger. A side panel in the wall to my right slid open and out popped my bio suit. With a sigh and huff I got to my feet and slowly walked over towards it. I unhooked the suit and stepped into it. As soon as I had the suit activated and automatically closed air tight. I reached up and put on my mask just as Charlie entered through the door. He looked me up and down. “You ready” We go now?” He asked in his simple way. “Lead the way, Charlie.” I replied with another sigh. Even though I hate every moment I am in this place, I have become settled in my routine. My day always started at 6:00 am in the morning and always ended 11 pm at night. Charlie would always come and “accompany” me on my walk to the laboratory where god knows what new experiment awaited me. A new addition in the passed few days was the presence of Richard Wood in the laboratory. His presence always helped in the fighting experiments especially when they were testing the suits impact on different materials. I always envisioned Wood’s face as I threw a kick or punch at a piece of steel. But who knew what waited for me today? Charlie waited for me to exit my cell before following closely behind. He pointed down the corridor towards the laboratory and grunted. As I walked down the corridor I took in the familiar sights. We always pass a couple of windows that look into rooms where scientists are kitted up in plastic suits and goggles whilst they prod and poke at some aluminous green material. It always makes me smile because it tells me that these guys have no idea what they are doing. We then pass another window and to me this is the most glorious view, it is the view of the outside world where birds are flying and the sun always seems to be shining. I only get a quick glimpse before Charlie shoves me into the laboratory. “Ah Adam, glad you could join us.” Wood beamed as I stumbled into the lab with Charlie closely following behind. “I trust you slept well.” “What do you think?” Came my venomous reply. “Sounds like someone is a little cranky.” “Go to hell Wood.” “You first Adam, you first,” Wood stared at me still with that cheesy grin on his face, “Now lets get to work.” Wood clicked his fingers and an entourage of his followers in lab coats flocked towards him. “Put him in the chair,” Wood said as he smiled at me, “I think it is time we test the suits resistance to electricity.” There was a worried grumble from the followers but it soon faded away as they moved over to me and with the help of Charlie grabbed me and forced me onto the chair positioned in the corner of the lab. I didn’t put up any fight as I just stared at Richard Wood who still had that cheesy grin on his face. I could feel my blood boil as they restrained me with the straps and Wood made his way over to me. “He’s ready to go sir.” One of his followers said as the lot of them moved over to the control panel. “Excellent, wait for my word,” Wood said as he watched them gather around the control panel before snapping towards me. “So Adam, I have got to say I have been a little disappointed with you as of late.” “Why is that?” I asked not caring. “Well in the last couple of weeks, you have been on a bit of a losing streak. I mean you lost your Cruiserweight Championship, which I was willing to overlook but then you go and lose a match that was not only for the Television Championship but also a chance to gain some points in the Platinum Series. What has happened Adam?” “Hmm, I don’t know, perhaps it is the fact that my life has been turned upside down. I am being treated like a lab rat, every move I make is being watched and’oh yes let’s not forget the fact that my wife and child are being kept from me!” “They are all necessary precautions Adam.” “How is keeping my family from me a precaution?” “I don’t want any unnecessary distractions.” “Well at least tell me who you have got on the roster watching me?” I screamed feeling the familiar emotion of rage. “Do you think I am stupid Adam?” Wood chuckled. “Do I really have to answer that?” With all his might Wood reached back and smacked me across the face. My head smacked against the cold hard steel chair. I quickly snapped it back and stared at Wood, who looked at me now infuriated. “You listen to me Adam! You better give a good showing at Clash Of The Titans or I may not be able to vow for your family’s safety anymore,” Wood was now directly in my face, I could see the rage in his eyes. He turned to his followers, “Give him a hundred volts and keep going until I get back. I have got an associate to meet in my office.” Wood turned and walked out of the lab not looking back once. Yet again I had got under his skin and I couldn’t help but smile at my small victory. The smile soon faded as a hundred volts surged through my body and I screamed in pain until I blacked out but just before I did an image of my wife and daughter flashed through my head along with four words? Clash Of The Titans!
