Fate's Example

4 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/4/03 'For over a week, I have been silent. Watching. Waiting. Biding my time. You see, unlike many who jump head first into battle with everyone and everything in their path, I ...I have sat back, and observed. Watched this entire roster's every move, listened to every word. Which brings me to you Dynamite X. Dynamite X. Ready to explode at anytime, as we could clearly see from your assult on the ingorant. Dynamite X. Unstable, and dangerous if you get in his way. If you get in his way on Xtermination Day, you will get the Xterminator.' The camera fades in as Ice is shown sitting in a dimly lit room, all alone, at a wooden table. Ice is writing something onto a piece of paper with a quill pen. His strokes of the pen are long and methodical. Ice pauses for a moment, and a smirk briefly appears on his face, before disappearing just as fast as it appeared, as he continues his writing. 'Dynamite X. You look at things so narrow minded. You lack the vision to see the big picture. You open your mouth without knowing the facts. But you see, that is where it is my advantage. You see, BOY, I AM 6'4. I AM 284 pounds. And I AM NOT HERE TO LISTEN TO SOME LITTLE PUNK TALK ABOUT MAKING ME HIS WHOOPIN' BOY. The facts of the matter are this. Friday the THIRTEENTH is YOUR day of reckoning. Friday the THIRTEENTH is YOUR unlucky day. Because on Friday the THIRTEENTH, you leave the security of everything that you know, and everything you think you know. On Friday the THIRTEENTH, you enter a new place. You enter a new world. You enter MY WORLD!' Ice sets his feathered quill pen down on the tanned wooden table next to the piece of paper he was writing on, and looks up at the camera. His cold, icy, blue eyes pierce a hole through the camera lens. Slowly Ice lifts his left hand from the table, and combs it through his long shoulder length blond hair, pulling any strands out from in front of his face. He places his elbow on the table, and begins to gently rub his temples with his thumb and index finger. Ice slowly closes his eyes, and takes in a long deep breath. He holds the breath for a few moments before exhaling it out. Ice tilts his head back, keeping his eyes closed. His hand stops the message of his temples, and begins to scratch the underneath side of his chin. Ice's eyes open. 'You see, summertime may be upon us, but this summer, the temperature changes. Dynamite X. I gave you, and the rest of this god-for-saken company a glimpse of what is in store for you all at THE DEAD ZONE. This Friday, YOU get to experience fate's cruel hand of punishment up close and personal. The time has come for fate to give just a little more of a itself to the public. The time has come for another poor soul to be made an example of. Unfortunately for you, Dynamite X, fate has chosen you to be the example.' Ice's gaze lowers from the ceiling, and finally focuses back onto the paper he was writing on before. Slowly he picks up his feathered quill pen, and begins to write once more. The camera watches Ice as he writes, and writes, and writes. The scene fades to black.
