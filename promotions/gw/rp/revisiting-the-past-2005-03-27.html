Revisiting the Past

27 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/27/05 The humming sounds of water heaters fill the dark, musky room. A single lightbulb gives the only light into the room. Three large deep maroon colored water heaters are seen covered and caked in dust. The cement floor in which they stand upon is cracked in numerous spots, and is in desperate need of repair. The scene pans up to the ceiling to see rows of pipes and wires running from one end of the room to the other. The 'tink tink' of a pipe falling and hitting the floor echoes throughout the room. As the 'tink' of the falling pipe continues to ring throughout the room the lightbulb flickers, causing the room to grow even darker. 'There is nothing to be afraid of.....' The words pierce through the darkness of the room, and suddenly the flickering light halts, staying calm and steady once again. Softly footsteps can be heard slowly approaching with each step. Louder and louder the once soft steps become as they grow closer and closer. Suddenly the footsteps stop, and once again all is silent. '....There is nothing else left to live for....' Once again the room fills with silence as the piercing voice abruptly stops. The light flickers for a second time as a figure steps into the dim glow given off by the light. As the figure steps into the light it reveals itself to be none other than ICE. ICE, as usual, is dressed in his wrestling attire, which consists of his white and blue splattered airbrush painted pants with matching leather trench coat and boots. ICE's long, white streaked black hair flows down onto his shoulder tops where it lies gently. ICE continues to walk towards the light, and as he reaches it his head begins to tilt up, as his eyes lock onto the dim bulb hanging from the ceiling of the dark musky room. ICE's pupil's shrink, and the cold blue coloring that surronds them grows, almost completely overtaking his entire eyes 'What more do you want from me? What more do I have that you wish to take from me, that you haven't already? What more do I have to give? .................................................NOTHING!' ICE's angered voice echoes off the cement walls of the room as his head lowers in a shameful manner. Slowly, ICE's eyes lock onto the cement floor between his feet and then begin to close. His eyes fully shut, and once again there is an eerie silence in the dark musky room. This silence seems to last much longer than the silence that engulfed the room before. Slowly ICE's head raises, and his eyes open up once again. As his head slowly raises, and his eyes open up ICE lets out a long and exhausted sounding sigh. ICE's eyes for a second time fixate onto the lone lightbulb hanging from he ceiling . Just as they did before, ICE's pupil's shrink and the cold blue that surrounded them seem to have engulfed his eyes for a second time. ICE gazes up into the dim light given off by the single hanging lightbulf. The gaze in ICE's eyes is a lost, dreamful, and far away glazed over glossy - eyed gaze. ICE suddenly blinks and the gaze disappears. Slowly an angered expression begins to crawl across ICE's usually emotionless face. 'You took the one thing that meant anything to me. You took her without warning. You took her from my life, and left me here to rot. But now you are back. You have returned to torture me, slowly rubbing the salt in my forever opened wound. Mocking me with each sunrise that follows the sunsets I cherish each night. I am living in my own personal hell. Waking up each morning, and looking at myself in the mirror becomes a harder and harder thing to do with each day that passes. I know that you know this, and I know that it pleases you. So why do you continue to haunt me? Why do you continue this vengeful game? I have already lost to you....so what now is your reason? Tell me.......why?' As the word 'why' escapes out of ICE's mouth, and passes over his lips his words begin to trail off. His voice gives off the sound of defeat. Once again ICE's head lowers in a shame - filled manner, and his eyes fixate on the floor between and beneath his feet. Footsteps ring through the small, dark, and musky room. ICE's head instantly pops up, and his attention turns towards the direction of the footsteps that were just heard entering the room. Slowly ICE turns to face this unexpected visitor, and greet him the only way a man who has been interrupted having a meaningful conversation with thin air can. 'Who is there? I thought I made it perfectly clear that I wished to be ALONE! ALONE! That means no guests, no visitors, no company, and no surprise visitorsl' There is no response to ICE's mini - rant. His eyes narrow, and an annoyed expression begins to rise up and over the angered expression that was just etched on ICE's normally emotionless face. 'I figured my tone of voice would have made this clear. I ...WISH... TO....BE....ALONE! So you have one of two choices you can make. You can turn your narrow ass back around and leave me to myself, or you can tell me just who you are, before I do something you will regret.' ICE's tone of voice grows more annoyed, and angry with each word that slides over his lips and hit the air, but still there is no response to his demands. ICE's annoyed expression abruptly leaves as an expression of pure anger radiates from ICE's growing red face. Angrily ICE steps out of the light, and towards the direction of the intruder. ICE is gone momentarily when out of nowhere he comes flying back into the light, landing hard on the cracked cement floor. ICE's eyes widen in astonishment, as he quickly scoots himself backwards on the cracked cement floor. ICE's scooting quickly turns into a scramble to get to his feet as a red haired female steps into the dim lighting. 'Wh....wha....what are you doing here?' The red haired woman still does not respond to ICE. Instead the woman merely takes two steps towards ICE and angrily peers down at him as he continues to scramble and get up to his feet. 'I thought....' The red haired woman places her hand up, her palm facing ICE, and stops him mid - sentence. Slowly she extends her right hand towards ICE to help him up off the cold, cracked cement floor. ICE accepts the red haired woman's hand, and is quickly up off the floor and back into a standing position. 'You want to talk? Let's talk....just not here.' ICE nods at the woman's words as she hurrily spins around an walks back into the engulfing darkness. ICE pauses and watches her walk away before following her into the darkness, and fading away from the picture himself. 'So you mind telling me just where it is that we are going?' ICE's voice bellows through as the picture fades in to show ICE dressed in a plain black t - shirt, and a pair of matching black blue jeans. As the picture continues to fade in, and zoom out, ICE is shown sitting on a leather seat, with a beautiful red - head next to him. A small rectangular window behind them allows a small ray of sunlight to squeeze into what appears to be the back of a limo. ICE continues his annoyed, and questioning glare towards the red - headed woman sitting next to him while she seemingly is ignoring him, by not answering, and just staring straight ahead. 'Well?' Slowly the red - head turns her head, and attention towards ICE, and gives him a little smirk. 'Well what?' ICE's eyes widen in anger as he stares a hole directly through the young woman sitting next to him. Slowly he intakes a long and deep breath, holding it in for a few moments before finally releasing it in one long stream. 'As I asked before, do you mind telling me just where the hell we are going?' The woman smirks again before answering ICE. 'You'll see.' Quickly she winks after replying to ICE's question, and then turns her head and attention forward once again. ICE tilts his head to the right, and his neck makes a thundering crackle as his neck cracks upon tilting. Slowly ICE brings his head back from its tilted position and evilly glares forward. 'What's with the attitude? You don't like the free trip? What is this limo not good enough for you now that you are a big star? What is it?' The red - headed woman's now raised, and slightly anger toned voice breaks the momentary silence that was begining to take over. ICE quickly snaps his head in the woman's direction and smuggly looks her over, as her face begins to fade back into a pale white color from the redish color it had become from her outburst. ICE's smug expression towards the woman fades, and an arrogant and cocky smirk crawls across his face. 'What are you smiling at?' ICE does not respond, he just continues to smirk his arrogant smirk while the red - headed woman continues to grow more and more frusterated with him with each passing moment. 'Stop it! Fucking Stop! You know I have always hated that! You and your little facial expressions. You and your arrogant ass ways! I would have thought that after all these fucking years something about you would have changed! I guess I was wrong.' ICE's smirk quickly disappears upon hearing the woman's words, and his stare becomes distant as a far away glossy eyed look overcomes his face. Slowly ICE turns his attention away from the red - headed woman, and he begins to stare out the window. The humming of the engine of the vehicle fills the silence. 'Well?' The red - headed woman angrily asks ICE as he continues to stare out the window. She waits for a reply from ICE, but none is returned and her face becomes a bright red color, almost matching the firery red hair that flows from her head. Just as the woman opens her mouth, ICE's voice cuts her off before she can eek out another sound. 'Well what? What gives you the right to come out of nowhere, drag me to God knows where, and then brow beat me because I am returning the courtesy you have showed me by not answering a question that was asked?' The red head's anger slowly subsides, as a small smirk begins to creep across her face as she lightly chuckles to herself at ICE's response. All the while ICE's gaze has not wavered from where it was, staring out the side window, watching the world pass by as he and the red - headed woman ride to their unknown destination. There is a long and uneasy pause between the two before the vehicle stops. Slowly the red - headed woman reaches for the door handles, pulls the latch, opens the door, and slides out of the vehicle. ICE does not move at first, and waits until the red - head exits the vehicle before finally getting out himself. ICE steps out of the long black limo and looks around at his surroundings briefly before glancing back over at the red - headed woman, who is wearing an ear to ear grin on her face. 'SURPRISE!' The red - headed woman's ear to ear grin grows even wider as she flails her arms out wide, presenting, a small, beat up, broken down, building to ICE. ICE does not repsond to the 'surprise' in any fashion, no facial expression, no verbal expression, nothing.....just nothing. His face is solemn and stern, his eyes are focusing on the building, yet not really there all at the same time, and his body is as stiff as a board. He does not move an inch, he does not move a muscle, he does not make a sound, and his eyes do not leave the building. 'You okay?' The red - headed woman cautiously begins to approach ICE while asking if he is alright. Still ICE does not respond to anything, while he continues to gaze at the building in a trance like, euphoric state. Suddenly ICE takes a step with his left foot, and then follows that with his right. Slowly he begins to walk towards the building just a few feet in front of him. The red - headed woman quickly turns and follows ICE as he reaches the front door of the building. ICE reluctantly lifts his hand and places on the handle of the door. There is a momentary pause as ICE's hand grasps around the door handle, before ICE finally opens the door and enters the building. Hurrily the red - headed woman follows him. 'Hey...slow it down there, I'm having a hard time keeping up.' Showing signs of her fatigue the red - headed woman desperately requests, while huffing and puffing. ICE however, does not heed her request and continues on his purposeful march through the long, dark, and musky entrance hallway of the building. Step by step, left and right, ICE marches forward, until he reaches a pair of wooden doors. On the left wooden door is a poster of the Canadian flag with the word 'PRIDE' in the center of the red maple leaf, in a white bold print. On the right wooden door is another poster, this one however is a faded, yellow tinted, white poster, with ash grey writing that reads 'GARY'S GYMNASIUM: ENTER AT YOUR OWN RISK'. ICE tilts his head to the left, and looks at the poster on the right door. A small smirk grazes his face, along with a soft chuckle. 'You know, I haven't been here in years, but no matter how long I'm gone, or how far away I go from here......it's still home.' ICE's voice begins to trail off, and his head begins to lower. The red - headed woman begins to slowly approach ICE, but before she can reach him his head shoots, up and he violently pushes through the two wooden doors. The doors fly open, revealing a dungeon looking type of gym room, loaded with punching bags, rings, and various weight lifting stations. Everything in the room is covered in dust, and looks to be quite old, almost as if the place has not been entered in many, many years. 'It's been closed since my father passed away six years ago. Occassionaly we rent the place out....well the upstairs at least, for wedding receptions and things like that, but for the most part this is what it looks like on a regular basis.' ICE continues to look over the room, almost as if he is not paying attention to the red - headed woman's words as she speaks to him. A silence fills the room as the two stand side by side and look over the remains of the gym. 'So why did you find me, and why did you bring me here?' There is a slight pause before the red - headed woman responds. 'I'm not really sure.' ICE slowly turns his head and looks at the red - headed woman. 'You aren't sure, or you just don't want to tell me?' The red - headed woman bows her head in response to ICE's remarks. 'I..I've just seen you on TV, and the few times we've e-mailed one another, you... just don't seem like the same person you were before. I don't know. I watched my father take you in from the streets, and raise you like the son he never got to have. I watched you grow into a man, and become very successful. But you had a look then. You had this.....aura about you. Like you had a chip on your shoulder, and were going to crush anyone who got in your way with it. Now.....' The red - headed woman's voice trails off before she finishes what she was going to say. 'Now? Now what?' Still staring down at the floor between her feet the red - headed woman nerveously kicks the dust on the floor, reluctantly pausing before finishing what she was going to say, and answering ICE all at the same time. 'Now, you appear to have lost that. You fought so hard, and you made it, but now it seems as though you have nothing to push you. You seem to have lost that drive. So I brought you back here, to where it all began to .......' Before for she can finish what she is saying, the red - headed woman is abruptly cut off with the sound of a loud thud. Instantly her head shoots up and looks to see ICE picking up one of the weight benches and tossing it to the side. She continues to watch as he does this two more times before grabbing a long handled push broom. 'Well let's get started then Vicki.' A smile instantly appears on Vicki's face at ICE's words. Vicki begins to sweep, as ICE walks over to the ring, and begins to tighten the ring ropes as the picture begins to fade........... ..............The picture fades in showing ICE and Vicki sitting on the ring apron, their faces glistening with droplets of sweat, while both of their chests heave up and down in a long slow manner trying to catch their breathes. ICE does just the opposite, leaning back, propping himself up on the ring ropes. Slowly ICE titls his head back, until it hits the middle ring rope, and looks out in front of himself. As he looks out, a smile begins to grow across his face. Vicki lifts herself off of her thighs, and begins to look out in front of herself, just as ICE is, and also just like ICE, a smile begins to show from her face as well. 'Not too shabby. Not too shabby at all.' ICE begins to chuckle lightly to himself at Vicki's words, as he raises his hands and slowly clasps them behind his head as he continues to lean back against the ring ropes. The picture slowly turns away from Vicki and ICE, and begins to pan around the small, gymnasium, which is no longer a complete mess. The gymnasium, which when last seen looked like it had been abandoned for many years, now looks clean, vibrant, and brand spanking new. The floors and walls are no longer dust covered and dirty. The weight lifting equipment looks as if it was brand new, shining in the bright florescent lighting of the room. And the ring, the ring looks to be in tip top shape, almost as if it would be packed right up, and taken on the road to be used on the next 4WW show. The picture, now finished panning around the room, comes back to ICE and Vicki. 'See, when you have a goal, there is nothing...' Vicki is cut off before she can finish by ICE. 'I haven't seen this place look this good, even when your father had this place opened on a regular basis. I mean seriously Vic, look at this place shine.' ICE's voice begins to trail off slightly. I was just curious, I don't know....' ICE pushes himself off the ring apron, and hops down onto the cement floor. Vicki looks out as ICE begins to walk away from the ring.
