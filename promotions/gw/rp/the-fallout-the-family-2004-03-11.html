The Fallout - The Family

11 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/11/04 Fuck that hurts. The nerve endings below the torn skin on the side of Train's left arm lit up like a christmas tree when he accidently brushed it on the side of it EZ Chair as he sat down in front of the big screen. Normally Train might be working out in the downstairs gym he's set up in his converted wharehouse home. But not today. Not after that damn bar fight on Evolution. Fucking bitch. That ho' didn't say shit about no fucking barbwire...what kind of fucking cage is made of barbwire?! Whore.... That little McCoy bitch thought she was being funny. To bad for that ho' Train got the last laugh. Now Train sits in the upper area of his new home the curtains drawn, the light from the 51' flatscreen is about the only illumination in the room. It was the perfect environment for a brotha to recover from the bullshit he's been through over the last few weeks. The ice clinked against the glass as it floated in his special mixture of Cherry Coke and Crown Royal. Train took a big sip of his drink as he manuevered himself into a position that would keep his bandaged arm away from things that might cause another jolt of pain to shoot up his side... which is to say anything. Train sets his drink on the small, dark, cherry wood table next to his chair and reaches into the drawer on the front side. His large hands search inside the drawer for a few seconds before retrieving what he was looking for...a metal cigar holder. He pulls the cigar case out and snaps the drawer shut, the impact casing the ice cubes to clatter against again, making a jingling sound like a chime in a strong wind. With a flick of his thumb the case pops open and Train surveys a fine selection of blunts that's he's already rolled. Handy on a strict 'I ain't doing shit today' day such as this is. Finally Train chooses the chocolate flavored Philly Blunt and snaps the case closed. He pulls the Zippo from the pocket of his Adidas warm-up pants and uses it to ignite the cigar before slipping it right back into his pocket. The cherry of the blunt glows red hot as Train takes a deep pull of the intoxicating smoke. He looks like a cross between a lion and a dragon, his thick mane of dreadlocks rustle slightly as he exhales smoke...billowing as if a raging fire is roaring in the pit of his stomach. And perhaps that's the most accurate description of the man... Hungry... Angry. Train picks up his drink and takes another big sip. Train puts his glass back down and takes another toke as he gingerly settles into the EZ Chair, flipping his attention back to his new DVD '28 Days Later'. Could just be the weed talking, but this joker was tight. Rage as a disease, coursing through people's vein's. It seems like Train had felt like that half his life. Everyone and everything...didn't matter what it was. If it stood in his way it pissed him off to no end. He'd had a million people tell him all the respectable and responsible to confront problem... Yeah... Fuck all that bullshit. If it was in his way it was getting run the fuck over. Period. It's no wonder his boys nicknamed him Train. But it wasn't just the scenario of the movie that appealed him. The title seemed like a nice metaphor for his short stay in Global. In the movie it hadn't been long since the disease hit... '28 Days Later' it looked like the whole world had been left for dead. It hadn't been but about a month since Train had arrived and the number of bodies he's left laying was astounding. Braxton was just the latest. He let that bitch put him in the middle of a fight that ain't had shit to do with him and she wasnt' even giving up the ass. What a fucking trick. Real tough guy... right up until Train smacked his bitch ass up side the head with a bottle and tossed the asshole through the side of that cage. Yeah, he got in his little shots and all that, but bottom line? He's probably still digging out that piece boot Train broke off in his monkey ass. Train had to fight off the urge to laugh at McCoy, mostly because laughing caused that nasty spot on his arm he'd got going through that fucking cage himself to hurt. She thought she was so damn smart. She set up the fight in her bar... with her friend (or as Chris Rock calls a woman's male friend, 'Dick in a glass case') to fight for her. And though she probably doesn't think Train is smart enought to put it togehter yet... Akodo Rokku? Jesse Briggs? The Souls of the Black Lotus? Brick?! They just happened to be there? Bullshit. Those punks didn't just happen to be there. They'd been waiting on Train. Show up? More like a set up. The bitch tried to stack the odds and hedge her bets. If Braxton couldn't get the job done, Win, lose or draw, Train was still catching a beat down. Too bad for her he'd smelled that pile of shit from a mile away. And in the end? It had all come down to one thing... Superior back up. Not only did Train have a crew to watch his back... he backed up the shit he talked. Now? Christina and her chosen few are gonna be the first to find out you best to watch your step when you under M.O.B. Rule. Train takes another toke off the blunt and holds in the smoke for a moment before the phone starts to ring. Train exhales the plume in exasperation, irritated that his 'I ain't doing shit today' day. Train flips up the right armrest on his EZ Chair, revealing a hidden compartment. He reaches in and pulls out a silver cordless phone. The phone rings a few more times while Train takes another sip from his drink then checks the caller ID. Train looks even more irritated as he spys the name on the phone. Finally he flips it on. 'What?' Train asks, going against all written and unwritten rules of phone etiquette. 'What?! What do you mean, what?! What kind of way is that to answer the phone?' says the voice on the other end of the line, sounding as if is he's trying and doing a dead on immitation of Joe Peschi as Leo Getts in Lethal Weapon. 'Jerry, I'm busy. What do you want?' 'What do I want? Well, I'll tell you what I want Tony. I... Would really like to know... WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU DOING DOWN THERE?!' 'Hey yo, don't be yelling all up my ear son!' 'Don't yell?! What the hell else can I do, but yell Tony? I mean... Here I am, working my little tail off for you. I get you into Global and you're mowing guys down left and right, ok? Now... I got BET telling me they might want you to host a new sports magazine type show for them... Timberland boots is looking at you for a spokes person, a deal that comes with new Tims for life by the way... Both these places are high on you... talking probably half a million a piece to get you on board. Great, right? Really exciting... Reeeeeally nice, Right? Wrong! I call up today Timberland says they don't want their product being associated with any racist organizations. And BET?! haha...these guys are telling me that not only don't they want you as a host anymore, but they won't even let you be a guest on any of their shows cuz they...and I quote...don't want some punk brotha that's working with some Nazi. A Nazi Tony!!! What the crap are you doing working with some fucking Nazi?! I of course ask not only because I'm Jewish, but because I also just lost out on 12.5% of a million dollars! What the hell are you doing down there?!' 'Damn it... Fucking Makonie....' Train says under his breath. 'Shhhhit...' he exhales again as he grabs his drink and finishes off what's left in the glass. 'Tony? You still there?' 'I'm here' Train responds before taking another pull off of the cigar that's still streaming a steady sliver of smoke, wofting up and disapating. 'Well? What the hell are you doing down there in Dallas? I mean, what am I supposed to do now that you've got the whole world pissed at you?!' 'Hey! Didn't I just fucking tell you to stop yelling in my fucking ear?! DIDN'T I?!?!' 'Ok, ok, ok, ok, ok.... But seriously, you've painted me into a corner over here. What am I supposed to do now?' 'You wanna know what you should do Jerry? I'll tell you... Get yo monkey ass in gear! What the fuck you talkin', what should you do?! I been laying muthafuckas out since the second I stepped up in this bitch! Now you get on the muthafuckin phone with that petty bitch Christina McCoy and you start putting the muthafuckin' pressure on! I done ran through half these little small timers on the roster. Now it's time for the fuckin' big boys! I want some big names and title shots God damn it! No more fucking small time bullshit, you hear me Jerry?! You understand me?! Pshhhh.... Let me tell you something. I don't give two shits about BET, MTV, Timberland or Magoo! I didn't come here to be no fuckin' movie star. I came here to do what I do best. I came here to be the Train nigga. I came here to run muthafuckas over! You hear me Jerry?' 'I hear you Tony, I hear you.' 'You get that ho to start giving me what I earned...some high profile type shit...some title shots. And if she don't want to? You tell that little bitch the shit she thinks I did? That shit's gonna start looking like a weekend in the fucking Bahamas compared to what's gonna happen. You let her know I can't be stopped... I got the muthafuckin' MOB behind me. You tell her she can try to hold me down all she wants, but in the end she might as well spread them legs cuz she will be giving it up . We can make the shit pleasant or me and the MOB can run a Train on the ho'. It's up to her. Now... You want some money Jerry? Do yo' job son. Get me my title shot. Let me put one of them belts over my shoulder and see how the money starts rolling in. Now...you know what you need to do. Get the fuck on it.' Train snatches the phone from his ear, pressing the power button to turn it off. Train slams the phone down on the nearby table and takes another pull from the chocolate Philly. He flicks the ashes into a nearby ashtray and settles back into his chair. This is some bullshit. That's all Train could think. Here he was, not a week removed from one of the biggest victories of his life and nobody can do anything but hate and talk trash. He took a deep breath and exhaled a long sigh. He need somebody to talk to. A friendly voice. Train picks up the phone and enters a long sequence of numbers. It takes a moment as the circuits switch over and connect. Suddenly there is ringing on the other end of the line. It takes three rings, but someone finally picks up. The voice of a young woman gives her greeting... 'Hello?' 'Sup girl?' 'Nothing...who's this?' 'Girl...you don't recognize yo' big brother when you here him?! See, that's what's wrong with yall children today, no respect for your elders.' 'Tony! Boy... I oughta kick your behind!' 'For what?!' 'You been in Dallas all this time and this is the first time you've called, that's for what.' 'Yeah, I know... My bad. I just been busy as hell. But I'm here now... what's up with ya?' 'We're all fine here. You know us, we'll make it one way or another.' 'haha... That's the truth if I ever heard it.' 'So, how you doing down there?' says the woman as her whimsy turns to pointed concern. 'Just chillin, you know how I do.' he says in an extra light tone, trying to dismiss the discussion he already knows is coming. 'C'mon, stop playin' Tony. I saw your match on Monday. How are you doing?' 'I told you Joanne, I'm fine. I'm a little banged up, but it's part of the game, you know?' 'No, I don't know. Since when is barbwire part of game? Who's idea was that?' 'Shit, it wasn't mine. You can believe that.' 'It was that Christina chick wasn't it?' 'You know what, don't even worry about her ass. I'm taking care of all that.' 'Apparently not Tony. I know you was being tough on her, messing up her show and everything, but to making you wrestle in barbwire?! She knows you got a family and she's gonna do something like that?!' 'It's all over girl...chill.' 'Hell no! Tony, I know you. I know you wouldn't ever hit a woman so that bitch is lucky I ain't down there, ok?. I'd slap that bitch into the middle of next week.' 'Now see? Here you go, flying off the handle. You chill out. This is on me, this is what I do. I got this. Trust me, I got a plan on how to handle this shit.' There is a short pause, the young girl wondering out to broach a subject that she knows will be a sore spot. She hates to fight with her brother, but it's too important to ignore. He'd finally changed his life... She didn't want him going back to an old life he was too good for. 'Soooo... This MOB thing? Is that your plan?' 'Part of it.' 'I thought you went down there to change all that.' 'Jo Jo...chill, aight? Don't pay too much attention to the name and all that. We ain't doing jack illegal. It's just gonna look like it when we start stealing everybody elses gold.' 'So these, ummmm... Greeniso guys... They're not actually criminals? See cuz that ain't what they was saying on the news.' 'Jo-Jo... I'm legit, you know that.' 'I ain't asking about you, I'm talking about them. Are you sure you can trust them.' 'Look, we all got the same thing in mind. We gonna take down anybody in our way. That's all there is to it. Anything they do on their time is their business. Period.' 'Alright... But...' 'But What?' 'But what about what every body's saying about you. I mean they just completely dissed you on BET last night.' 'Dissed me?' 'Yeah! They did like 5 minutes on the BET news about how you had hurt the status of all black athletes and how...how you had set the cause of black athletes back 3 decades cuz you were working with that guy...the one who thinks he's Hitler's love child or something. I gotta be honest Tony... I love you bro, but I don't know how you can even stand that guy. I mean...' 'Jo-Jo... I know what you mean, aight. Look, I can't stand him. But I ain't got to stand him to work with him. Only think that muthafucka can do for me is help me get paid. As for B-E-T... Fuck BET! Black folk always talking that unity shit but the second another brotha gets something they ain't got the only thing they can do is tear 'em down. Fuck 'em if they don't like. I'm doing what I got to do for me and mine... Them and theirs can kiss my black ass. Now, I don't wanna talk about this shit no more.' 'Alright, alright don't get mad. I just wanted to make sure you knew what you were getting into.' 'Trust me, I got this. At the end of the next pay per view... I'ma have me a title over my shoulder. Bet on that. Now... where my little neice at?' He says changing the subject back to something that was in the vein of the reason he made the call...to talk about something pleasant. 'Being bad of course.' 'hahahaha... Don't be talking about my girl like that.' 'hahaha... I'm just kidding. She's too busy sleeping to be bad, but that won't last long. She's getting bigger every day. She misses uncle Tony.' 'Yeah, I know, I know... when I get this title I'll be able to take the time I want. I'll come back home and all of us will go to Great America.' 'She's only 3 Tony. She's not gonna be able to get on the rides.' 'They got kids rides.' 'You gonna be the one to take her on them, cuz I wanna go on the good stuff.' 'Shoot, it's me and my girl all day. Me and Angel don't need you to have fun. We doggs, we roll like that.' 'I know, I know... Partners in crime.' There is another pause in the conversation as Train tries to broache a new subject. A spot even more sore than his arm after going through barbwire. Joanne knows her brother...knows he wants to know about her...how she's doing, but doesn't have the strength to get over that damn James pride to ask... 'She's doing fine Tony.' Joanne says answering the question she knew he didn't know how to ask. 'Who's fine?' 'You know who. Momma's doing ok.' '*Ahem*... Good........ Good.' 'You should call her.' 'Joanne, I don't wanna do this right now....' 'She misses you.' 'Oh, for real?' 'Tony... She's not gonna be around forever. She loves you, she's just... She stubborn, you know that.' 'Better than anybody.' 'You've changed your life now Tony. You guys can patch things up.' 'Patch things up? Oh, so I'm supposed to go to her? I spent a year paying the bills and buying the food, but I'm supposed to go to her like I did something wrong?!' 'Tony, I know that, but...' 'But what?!' 'Tony, you were dealing dr....' 'Don't be talking about that shit on the phone!' Train quickly snaps, cutting her off. 'I'm sorry, ok?! But you knew Tony! After what happened to Leon... C'mon Tony, you know what that did to her.' 'Us...what that did to us. Hell, you was just a little kid when Leon.... Fuck Jo-Jo... I did what the fuck I had to do cuz Momma wasn't in no position to do it herself. I ain't proud of all of it, but I ain't apologizing for the shit. You don't remember it Jo-Jo, not like I do... Leon was hard on her...but when dad died...' 'I remember Tony. I do. I just hate that you two can't work this out. She's your mother, you're her son. Yall have got to get this thing together. I want my little girl to be able to get to know her Grandma and her Uncle at the same time without a whole buncha drama Tony.' 'Well I can't do shit about the drama Joanne. Every time I try she just ignores the shit. I ain't got no time to get all that spit back in my face. I got too much on my mind right now to go getting my head all filled up with that. I.. I can't do it right now. I wont do it. 'You two are like two peas in a pod. You only fight cuz you're so much alike.' 'Whatever man...' 'Tony, we're blood. If we don't look out for eachother who will?' 'Yeah... Look, let me know if you need something... and get them damn student aid papers in so you can finally take yo' behind back to school!' 'Alright, alright... You just be careful with those guys. I don't trust 'em Tony.' 'hahaha... Me neither. Trust me, I can sniff out any bullshit they got.' 'Alright. Take care. Love you.' 'Love you too. Tell Angel uncle Tony said hey and be good.' 'I will.' 'Bye.' 'Alright, bye.' The phone clicks off as the scene fades to black.
