Giving Thanks

24 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/24/2004 Twas the night before Thanksgiving and all are at the Bar, all the creatures were stirring and moving around. For all who were they had no work the next day and was a night of great joy for a couple days off. There is a tribute band there tonight and there name is Empire and they're a Queensryche tribute band. They just got done with their third set and finished with the song, 'Eyes of a Stranger'. Donny gets up on the stage and catches the attention of the crowd who is still gathered there... 'Ladies and Gents. I want to talk to you for a little bit and make some brief announcements. First though lets have another round of applause for Empire and they awesome show they put on tonight.' The crowd in the bar cheers with a thunderous ovation and the band still on stage tearing down takes a moment and thanks the crowd and all the members of the band shake hands with the King of Hardcore and co-owner of the Drunken Leprechaun. 'Alright first off as you all know Monday night is Showdown in Tokyo, and of course the Drunken Leprechaun will be open and there will be a party as usual. So be prepared for a great night when my self and the other members of Team Brick go into that ring and whoop some serious ass' Again the crowd reacts with another thunderous roar. 'And the rest of you also know that tomorrow is Thanksgiving and we will not be open as were going to be spending time with our families, and partaking in lots of turkey and watching some football.' The crowd gives a somewhat smaller cheer. 'I want to take this moment though and I want to tell you what I Donny J McNasty am thankfull for. First off I want to thank the Lord above for giving me the oppurtunity to expirience life, to live and to love one another.' There is another small applause but there is alot of whistles and claps. 'I'm also thankful for having the people in my life that I have. Everyone from my Mother to Harv and especially Alexis. Everyone in your life is a blessing, they are someone to behold and cherish. Every moment you spend with the ones you love is a precious period of time. Don't ever take those moments for granted, realize that you may never have that moment ever again.' The crowd is somewhat silent as they all seem to take in what Donny has said. 'Anyways my friends, it's almost time for last call. So everybody the last drink of the night is on me. Have fun and enjoy, thank you for coming here and Happy Thanksgiving' The crowd cheers with a large roar as they begin to order their free drinks. Donny steps off the stage to people thanking him and some of them asking for autographs. It will be an even happier Thanksgiving if Team Brick wins it all Monday night...
