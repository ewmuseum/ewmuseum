Soma Gonna Party Like Its 1439

2 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/2/04 Cut The Cake And Pour The Wine We’re Gonna Celebrate Like It’s 1439... “WHOOO!” Throwing the door open of our VIP room that Soma purchased for us, I hope it doesn’t come back and smack him in the head. “I knew we’d win this thing!” “Nobody better mess with us now. We just beat four of the best that WMW had to offer.” “I told you it was a chance to show everybody what we can do.” “And we showed them alright.” KNOCK, KNOCK, KNOCK “Come on in?” Pardon my rude behavior. Soma and I just defeated four of the best that WMW had at Crossfire tonight. We poured more sweat on that mat then we have in awhile. And to tell you the truth, it feels good. “damn good. The door swings open and Cherish steps inside, smiling and jumping up and down for us. “for me. “Congratulations Sage and Master Soma?” She runs up and gives me a kiss? “I never watched wrestling before, but you guys are the best I’ve ever seen. You guys are better then I thought.” I blush, I know that I’m amazing and all, but she made me feel like I’m a God. “We appreciate it.” “When are you guys going to do this again?” Soma and I look at each other? “We don’t know yet. Whatever Bubba decides to do with us, we do.” Soma nods in agreement? ??so what are we going to do now?” I say while holding Soma’s maid in my right arm? “Party.” ONE NIGHT LATER Short and sweet. Just one word, meant so much fun. I knew what was coming up, or did I? I knew that Soma had a ton of money, but this was ridiculous. Soma spent half of the day on the phone, renting out a resort in a Las Vegas palace. He called celebrities and friends, he told me to prepare for an amazing night. Soma told me to invite a few friends, so I called up some of the only friends I’ve ever had. I called up a couple of my buddies from a small little organization I met last year on the road, when I was with Salaok and Johnathon Davis. They are from Devious Wrestling, otherwise known as the DIWF. When I seen these guys, it felt as if I was looking into a mirror. They made up a group called The Heartless Bastards. These guys were the mirror image of The Nevermore. So of course, I got a long with them instantly. They dominated their federation for quite some time until Devious Wrestling went bankrupt. So right now, these guys are wrestling independently, surviving off of only one show a week and working normal jobs. I feel bad for them, but I’m sure things will work out. They have been following what Soma and I have been doing. They call me up every now and then to see how things are going. Some of you may remember a wrestler named Damien Nhyte in the Global Wrestling Federation, back when the GW was the GWF. He wrestled in the GW for about two months, went undefeated but was injured in a freak accident. He went through rehabilitation and started back with DIWF. He is waiting for his shoulder to recover before he tries to get back into GW. I hope for the best for him. Another wrestler, Marcus Stanton, was the DIWF Heavyweight Champion of the World. He was Damien’s tag team partner, together, they held the Tag Team Championship there for quite some time. You have Kurdt Wolf, brother of Davyd Wolf. Kurdt was also sidelined with an injury but is back on his feet. He will be wrestling in Japan in the next couple months. You have Brimstone, a giant at the least. He stands about 7 foot 2 inches tall and weighs over 300 pounds. He is a monster. And last, you have Greyson Blade. The founder of The Heartless Bastards and a legend in the DIWF. He is to DIWF what I am to Global Wrestling. These guys and Soma, are the only friends I have. “so they all decided to come for the celebration. I open the door of the palace and I am accompanied by two gorgeous women into a giant ballroom, where a giant chandelier hovers over stars from all around the world. I look around, spotting NBA star Vince Carter. I look around for Soma. I see him all the way at the other end of the ballroom, so I make my way towards him. I walk past Rob Schneider. He pats me on the back and congratulates me on the win. I nod my head and continue on my way. I women stops me and hands me a drink, I take it and push through the giant crowd. Cameron Diaz turns around from her group of friends and kisses me on the cheek. She tells me that I did an amazing job last night. I thank her for her kind words and move along. Another pat. Another handshake. Another kiss. “after dozens of handshakes and pats on the back, I finally make it to Soma, who is enjoying a drink of well’looks like a glass of blood, but its probably dark wine of some sort. I pat him on the shoulder and he turns his head” “Thank you, appreciate it.” I chuckle and pat him on the shoulder again. “Yeah, it was a big win. Great Pay Per View, indeed.” I pat him on the shoulder one more time. “he turns his head all the way around and notices that its me. “Oh, sorry about that. I didn’t know it was you.” “That’s fine. I’ve been doing that the whole entire night. I never thought I’d be getting annoyed of being famous.” “I hear ya. Come on up to the VIP room, I have some things I’d like to discuss with you.” I nod my head and I follow Soma into the crowd. Another pat. Another handshake. Another kiss. We eventually go through a door that is heavily guarded by a giant black man with a black suit on and black glasses. He spots Soma and moves aside, even opening the door. I feel like royalty. Now I know how Pendragon feels. We walk up a set of stairs into a carpeted room with a glass window, looking through it, you can almost have a bird’s eye view of everybody having enjoying themselves. “Take a seat.” Soma waves his hand to a leather couch against the wall. “so I sit. “Quite a party you’ve thrown.” “Thanks. Many people say that Sean “Puffy” Combs throws the best parties, but not even he can throw a party like mine. I’ve spent more money getting this party together then he has in his to his name.” “Wow, you serious?” “Of course. This isn’t all of it, in an hour, I’m having Metallica come on stage. Then after that, Dashboard Confessional, then the White Stripes.” “But I thought Jack White got thrown in jail or something.” “He did. I bailed him out for the concert before anybody knew he got arrested again.” “Maybe the poor guy should stop throwing punches.” “Yeah, pretty soon I’m going to ask him to join our little tag team we have going.” We both laugh? “good ol” Jack White. “Then we will have fireworks at the break of dawn. And in the morning, I have the Essence of Emerald cooking a fantastic breakfast for us.” “Awesome.” “BAM!” Soma screams in my face, scaring the shit outta me. I jump back? “Sorry about that.” I nod’still afraid. “But that’s not the reason I needed to talk to you. Both of us know that next month is the Master Of The Mat.” I nod my head. MOTM, the biggest chance for me to get back to the top again. “And Bubba just called me a couple hours ago and told me that me and you are both in it. So first off, I’d like to wish you luck on winning it.” I keep silent? “just like Soma taught me. “You and Angelus Sorrow, a new guy to the Global Wrestling will be facing J-Guanz and Partridge this week. If you win that match, you will proceed and fight Sorrow next week. From there, you are split into the tournament. And if things go as planned, I will do the same.” “I start to understand and get the picture of where this is going as he is speaking. “There is a chance that me and you will have to cross paths to get to the finals. To get to a shot at the World Heavyweight Championship against Josh at the next Pay Per View.” Soma walks around, back and forth, thinking this out. “Sabbot, if we have to fight each other. I don’t want you to take it easy on me. You are my best friend, you know this. I want to see the best fight out of you I’ve ever seen. I want you to make me sweat. I want you to make me hurt. And I want you to make me bleed. I want your best. Because I want the best match of my life. And when the match is over, it goes back to normal. We go back to living our lives how we are now.” I nod my head. “You know I want the Master of the Mat more then anything in the world. And I know that you want to make a name for yourself in GW as well. This is your chance Soma. I won’t take second best either. If we do face each other, I want the same competition. I know it’ll be like fighting my brother, like fighting myself. But if our paths cross, lets do it. For the fans’a match made for the history of Global Wrestling. A match of the year contender.” I stand to my feet and extend my hand out to Soma. He grabs it and pulls me in and pats me on the back. “But for now, lets go the hell out there and party.” I smile, releasing all the stress and emotion that has built up over the past couple minutes. We both walk out, together, and join everybody beneath the chandelier. I wave hello to Norah Jones. I wave hi to Dave Matthews. These are names of people that I’d always wished to meet. But now, they wish to meet me. I walk through the crowd. Another pat. Another handshake. Another kiss. “Congrats Tse Ling.” “with a pat on my shoulder. The general public, or any of these celebrities know my last name is Tse Ling. I quickly turn around and I’m face to face with Greyson Blade. “What’s going on man” Glad to hear you can make it.” “I wouldn’t miss it for the world.” Greyson is as proud as proud can be. He isn’t even dressed up at all, he is wearing a pair of faded blue jeans and a black Heartless Bastards shirt. “I’ll catch up with you later on, I have some business to take care of.” I nod my head and smile. He turns around and follows behind Carmen Electra? I look on the stage, Matchbox 20 is playing. Soma really did get every band in the world to make it here tonight. I guess when you have all the money and the power in the world, you can do anything you want. Matchbox 20 starts to play a cover of Earth Angel. I can tell what it is by the second chord. This is an all time classic. A circle opens in the middle of the ballroom and celebrity couples enter and start to slow dance. I stand there and watch? Pamela Anderson taps me on the shoulder and asks me to dance. I tell her that I’ll sit out on this one. “so does Alyssa Milano. And Jenny McCarthy. And Angelina Jolie. I tell them that my ankle hurts. I’d rather watch. “truth is, I would dance. But who I want to dance with is at the home, probably mopping the floor. Setting the table. Washing dishes. “missing me. I put my head down and sigh. I wish m’lady was here” “I never thought I’d say this, but I’m in love. “Would you like to dance?” This familiar voice asks from behind me. The voice of beauty and passion. The voice of my heart and everything amazing. I quickly turn around. It’s not the voice of Gilbert Godfrey. “or Fran Drescher. It’s the voice of my one and only. The forever smiling Cherish? “Of course I would. I grab her hand and walk out to the dance floor. By the time I get out there, Earth Angel has stopped and the next band is about to play. I have no clue who they are’just four black men dressed in suits standing in front of everybody. At least they look good, presentation is half the battle. Disappointed that it’s already over, until another slow song starts to play’a familiar tune. Cherish throws her arms around my neck and hums the tune” “Water Runs Dry, I can’t believe Soma got Boyz II Men to play at this thing.” I smile? “Soma is a miracle worker. Once again, he made things perfect for me. I look up at the VIP room and see Soma standing in front of the glass window, drinking his drink with his other hand in his pocket of his suit. He takes his hand out and gives me a thumbs up and winks. I smile back and turn my head and gaze into the eyes of the woman of my dreams” Another Pat. Another Handshake. And a kiss from the most amazing woman in the world.
