Visions in the Heat, Pt. 2

30 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/30/05 How long do we have to wait? It's pretty bad that we've had three contracts with 3 federations and they've never even bothered to try to book us. My brother and I have been waiting for so long. It'd be nice if we could get out of our spot in doing dark matches and house shows and not being on TV for more then bodyguards for a wrestler who's very capable on her own. Dani... Almost as much as a pain in the ass as this long wait has been. But I care for her more then anyone else here, even my own family. Gray and I have been waiting for so long. We were top dogs in the North West Indy Circuit. We were a team to face and to beat. But since Gray got assigned to watching her for his bosses. Out Bosses... Yeah, sometimes I forget just what I'm really doing here. Gray Brand, the man, the myth the legend. A man who in wrestling has passed up more options to go to the big time then anyone should have. The man who forced his childhood sweetheart to start to hate him. The man who has thrown everything away, and still tries to think he can comfort people. His kind aren't about conforting. He's a fighter, and a damn good one. Me? Josh? I'm just complicated. I'm the epitome of confused. My father is the same as Gray's father. Our mothers different after a tragic accident took Gray's mother from them. Buy my mother, an 'anglo' who had been 'adopted' by the tribe hit it off with the Old Man and they got hitched, even with him being 8 years his junior. That led to me coming about. But all was not a big happy family. Father died, Gray was an adult, and I wasn't. So I got to go to the big city and learn all about that. I went from farmer and salmon fisherman to coffee drinking, rock music listening, modern style wearing confusion. And you know what, I like it that way. I guess that's what Dani hates about me. She's been raised to be so traditional. This whole assignment of Gray's confuses me even more. Dani is a hottie, and she's knows it. She shows it. Boy, I out of anyone would know that. But she's so lost in the past, a past that may not even be her own 'physical past' that it's almost scary. I don't even know what it is, or what's fully going on. Then again, that gives me plausible deny ability. I may not have Gray's natural hiegtened senses from hanging out on the 'rez' for so long, as if that was the real reason, but damn, I've learned to be a good listening and watcher. People ignore me thinking that I'm some 'hot headed young punk' who's more concerned about my own stuff then anyone elses. I like it that way. Most don't know exactly what I know. Eventually they'll figure it out. But by the time they do, it'll be too late. I have plans that I have to follow. One of them is making sure that next week's plans happen as their laid out. One night of being special for one woman. I've got to make sure that it goes through before she figures things out... The sound of an opening door, with a knock as it opens. Josh steps into a small, regular hotel room in the Mandalay Bay Hotel and Casino, where their whole troop is staying while a more permanent home can be found in the city. The people they worked for wanted everything to go right, after two other feds have short changed all of them on their contracts, better to make sure that it's going to be a more permanent thing before dumping more money into it. The smell of burning sage, sweet grass, mixed with the smell of Old spice after shave, unmistakable as Gray Brand's room. Not a object out of place. On the desk was placed a Laptop computer next to it a set of items on a rabbit skin table cloth. In the middle was small puffs of smoke coming from a shell. Running water and some sloshing can be heard coming from the bathroom beyond the sleeping area. A hunched form can be spied working at the sink area. Josh takes a few steps in and his nose wrinkles at the barrage of scents. He calls out towards the bathroom, ?Hey, you're not ready yet? Why are we taking so long? Either your learning to slack off like me, and at the wrong time, or you've heard something from the rangers?? Gray walks around the corner from the bed room area, his look is one that is very serious. He's dressed in black shirt, with black jeans almost matching his brother. He's still buttoning up his shirt as he steps out. “None yet. But then again, The Valley of Fire is not a small area. It might take them a little to look around. Bad enough she's got a free pass to anywhere in there, and she's got a 4 Wheel Drive truck. She could literally be anywhere.” Josh throws his hands up and grabs his jacket. “Let's go big brother. I can't just sit around here, with the questions that are running through my mind on where she is. The cops in this town have a rep as it is, they'll just screw this one up,” Josh said as he headed for the door again. “Now just hold it, so she is a little late. She is allowed one slip on punctuality you know. And you're insulting Amerindian Rangers, not Metro in that statement,” Gray retorted. ? You know better then that. That Girl can tell time WITHOUT a watch. She is never late, not unless something is wrong,? Josh adds as he's about to leave the living room area. Gray grabs his little brother's jacket. Using the surprise to pull him off balance and in knocking him into a seat. Josh lands with the sound of air escaping his lungs from the landing, but manages, “You know, Old One I hate when you do that.” Gray looks at his brother questionably, “Old One? I'm only Eight Years older then you.” “Seven” “Not until Monday,” Gray adds with a smile. His brother's day of majority was coming up, as was Dani's. Two people who's birthday was but one day and a few hours apart, something that in their younger days at the school, Josh always reminded her of. “Okay, Old might not be a good word. Although your age is questionable with you other features,” Josh looked up at him with a serious look of his own. ?Features?? “What do you think? I am stupid or just never realized you disappeared so many times at the same time of the month? Come on I know better... Remember, I went thought that battery of tests to see just exactly what we shared when I got introduced to your bosses. I'm still worried that one day I'll start...” Josh Stops in the middle of his sentence to the sound of the Phone Ringing. Gray and Josh, both, rush for the phone, Gray just beating josh to the phone by part of a second in time. ?Hello,Gray Brand's room, how can I help you?? He hesitates for a few seconds, while listening. Shortly after though, he speaks again, the only side of the conversation that Josh can hear. “You found her” Josh jumps in, ?They found her? Is she okay? Gray ignores his brother and continues to talk on the phone, ?Okay, where is she going?? Again a wait as he gets the answer. Josh tries to say something, but Gray puts his hand up to his face as a motion to keep quiet. “Your taking her to the sun Rise Hospital? Okay, we're on our way.” Josh pays no heed to the hand though, ?Is she okay?? Gray hangs up the phone and finally fills his brother in, “They found her in the desert, passed out on one of her blankets. She's appearing to be coherent, and all that, but she was out here for hours. Her truck is being brought back here via the Tri-City Towing. I've got to tell the hotel management about that on the way out. I've also got to get her some clean clothes to change into after Life Flight drops her off at Sunrise Hospital, and they check her out.” ?Life FLIGHT?? Josh asks, in a loud manner. “Yes, Life Flight, helicopter. They evaced her out of the deep desert to a good clearing and then pulled the helicopter out to get her there quicker. They thought she had heat stroke at first. Looks like it was just heat exhaustion and some dehydration now.” ?Well? Get going old man!? Josh jumps to his feet and makes it past him this time. He walks up and opens the door to reveal a man standing there, about to knock. Josh immediately gives the bellhop an attitude with, ?What the hell do you want?? “Uhm... This just came for you or Ms. Northsky. But she isn't in her room, so I decided to try here.” Gray walks over, bumping his younger sibling out of the way. The man hands Gray an envelope, which he quickly opens and reads aload. Dani Or Gray, match Change Your now against Shadow. That's right the TV Champion, and yes, it's for the title. Girl, you must have impressed the hell out of them last week. Tell the boys their's isn't changed, ~Kris Gray could only add one comment to it, ?Our match?? Josh looks at him and shrugs his shoulders, ?Who knows, there is a young lady on her way to the hospital. I don't care if it's the world champion on the other side of the ring. There are more important things in life right now. Get out of my way, shit-bird!? Josh pushed his way passed the bellhop and headed down the hallway. “Let's go, old man I want to be there when she gets there. Or at least get some answers. And By the way big brother, thanks for your help today.” Gray closes his door and then double times it to catch up with the smaller and faster man. As he does he catches up not only with cadence and step, but also the conversation. ?I still don't understand why your going behind her back on this. Why not just tell her, to her face?? Josh ignore Gray comment and steps into the opening elevator door and hits the button for the parking garage. ?No answer?? ?It's going to be a surprise, okay?? ?Like the teddy bear was?? Josh's head snaps over to look at Gray as the doors close and the elevator starts to descend. ~Fast Forward~ ~Sunrise Medical Center~ ~Emergency Room~ We find Josh and Gray standing at the desk for the Nurse's station. Josh is impatiently taping his fingers on the desk, while Gray stands there almost stoned faced, showing no feelings except maybe annoyance at his brothers tapping. Gray reaches up and places his hand heavily on his brothers hand to quiet the fingers tips. “That isn't going to make them move faster. IF anything your going to annoying them into slowing down. It isn't smart to annoying the Nurses.” Josh looks at this brother, he cant believe his brother is so cold. Standing here with him waiting on word about a young lady that he loves, like a little sister, in a room some place behind the doors, and he didnt even seem little worried. Josh: I can't believe your not worried. Gray looks at him surprised. But says nothing. “What's the big deal? They move too slow.” At that, Gray does say something, “Hush, or I'll tell them that you have horrible hemorrhoids and you need to have them checked.” Josh's annoyance at that is vocalized by the sound of a half sigh, half growl that escapes him. He almost says something a bit more colorful, but one of the Nurses comes walking out with a clipboard. “Mr. Brand, you can go back and see her now. Room 13. If you follow me, I'll show you.” Gray answers her, giving his brother a look of 'I told you so' at the same time, “Alright, ma'am.” Both guys gollow the nurse down the hallway for a bit, and then turn to enter. As they step into the room, Danielle is laying on the bed, sleeping. The heart rate monitor beeping rhythmically showing a strong heart. The IV in her arm dripping a clear liquid in almost a steady stream. The nurse breaks the silence, ?The doctor will be in to let you know whats going on. She asked that you two be filled in, you're her brothers?? Gray answers before Josh says something to get them thrown out, “Close enough. Guess you could say her only local family with her.” The nurse just looks at Gray but says nothing. Josh moves to the side of the bed, not saying a word. He's startled as Dani starts to speak though, “You know I could smell you a mile away Joshua Bodaway Brand. And hopefully,Gray Brand. But I know something you don't know.” Danielle lets out a smile then using the bed set herself in a sitting position. Gray inquires, ?and what do we, NOT, know?? Danielle reaches for her ruck sack sitting next to the bed, and produces a small piece of paper handing over to Gray with josh reading over his shoulder, the second part being of the importance to these two. by the way, happy Birthday Josh. You and Gray finally have a match Absolutely Merciless v. The Brand Brothers ~Kris Josh comes in, “Nice birthday gift. I get to get all sweaty. Well as I said before with the other note, could be worse. Could be the world champion on the other side of the ring.” Gray turns around and slugs him in the arm. “Nitwit, Absolutely Merciless is the cousin team of Mindy Mercer and Michael Mercer.” ?So?? ?Mindy Mercer as of last week IS the World Champion!? ?Shit!? Dani giggles at the two arguing about this all. Gray tries to get one in on his brother though, “Figured you would be doing that anyways, though. This is Las Vegas, you know.” Gray gives his brother a wink. And then looks back a Danielle before hitting one back on her, “We know something you don't though.” Dani falls for the hook, takes the bait hook line and sinker. ?And that is?? “Card Subject to Change. You're not facing Biddle anymore. You're facing Shadow... for the TV Title.” “What” Dani yells that one out. Josh just smiles and nods his head in agreement. The smile is dropped when Danielle reaches for her chest and winces. ?You okay?? Gray asks her. “Yeah, it's been like this since the dream today.” ?Dream?? Both Josh and Gray ask her in unison. However, Gray quickly moves it to business upon hearing that word and realizing what's going on, “Josh why don't you go out and get us some coffee I need to talk to Danielle about something.” Josh looks at his brother then looks at Danielle. ? No I think I'll stay, I want to hear this dream.? Danielle looks at Gray “It's okay Gray. Well it started simple. But then it was like I was in the Sun Dance itself. It was pretty wild. I guess I was sleeping longer then I thought and thats how I ended up here. It was pretty weird. My chest has just been hurting since I woke up though.” “Well all dreams have a beginning and a ending, they have to run their course. I take it your telling me because you got the message,” Gray in all business mode now. This type of stuff annoyed Josh the most. Danielle shakes her head yes, smiles at Josh who looks like all blood has drained from his face. She quickly tries to comfort Josh, trying to think that his look was over the realization about their match this week, “Josh, you know things are cool about your match right? You will do great.” Josh quickly snaps out of his day dream and looks at Dani “Yeah of course, plus it's my birthday. So a nice win on that day would add to the perfect day.” Gray comes in from the side, ?And maybe afterwards, we can both head out to the Chicken Ranch in Pahrump and let one of those ladies make a man out of you the proper way, huh?? Josh doesn't really answer that with real words but just a look of disdain for that comment, before saying,? Now if you will excuse me I think I will go get that cup of coffee now.? Dani watches Josh leave the room, the remembrance of the great sorrow she felt in the dream fills her and her eyes well, she quickly looks down. Knowing she has to guard her heart from ever feeling this. Josh leaves the room heading for the door to the hospital. If there as one thing he knew, it was the location of all the Starbucks in town already. There was one in walking distance of the hospital as it was. As he walks though, he reaches down to his belt and pulls out his cell phone. He flips it open and starts hitting buttons. A voice can be heard not enough to know who it is but that it is defiantly a female. “Yes, this is Joshua Brand. I need to speak to your boss. There is something going on.” He continues walking ahead, wanting to get away from his brother for a short while, and get Dani some real help. ~End~
