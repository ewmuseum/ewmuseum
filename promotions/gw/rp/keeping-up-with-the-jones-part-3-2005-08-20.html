Keeping up with the Jones' Part 3

20 August 2005

Date: August 20th, 2005 Circling your head contemplating everything you ever said Now I know the truth I got a doubt A different motive in your eyes and now I’m out See you later I see your fantasy you want to make a reality paved in gold See inside, inside of our heads Well that’s all over we heard what you said Back off we’ll take you on Headstrong to take on anyone I know that you are wrong Headstrong we’re Headstrong Back off We’ll take you on Headstrong to take on anyone I know that you are wrong This is not where you belong I can’t take anything away I won’t give everything away It’s in your eyes and your disguise is not worth hiding anymore We’re in control you can’t tell us how to live our lives anymore Conclusions manifest your first impressions got to be your very best I see you’re full of shit and that’s alright That’s how you play I guess you get through every night Now that’s over I see your fantasy you want to make it a reality paved in gold See inside, inside of our heads Well that’s all over we heard what you said I know I know all about your motives inside and your decision to hide Fred Grest stands in front of a wrestling felicity with a piece of paper in hand.. Grest already in his ring gear a simple pair of black tights that stop right above his knees and black wrestling boots.. His long bright red hair pulled back into a pony tail. Fred Grest lifts up the piece of paper to read it one last time. Dear Fredster, Sorry buddy but it times we put an end to this tonight. Meet me at Midnight at the Fort Knocks Wrestling School. You don’t show up I know that you believe everything I told you. Otherwise I’ll enjoy beating it into you. You’re worst nightmare, Mike Jones He crumbles it up and tosses it to the ground as the wind carries it away. The Extreme One pushes the door open and the 1st thing he sese is Mike Jones. Jones stands at 6’3 and about 260 lbs. The man paces in the ring wearing short blue tights and blue wrestling boots. The muscular bald man stands there smirking at Grest “I’m kind glad to see you made it Grester.” Jones says. Grest tries to have a witty come back but words escape him. The Original Pranksta bolts to the ring and starts getting stomped on by Mike Jones. Each stomp in Jones? eyes proves why he is the better man. Grest makes his way back to his feet but taken down with a quick clothesline. Grest is stunned is head is not in this. “The dark match of wrestling won’t last much longer!” Jones screams as he picks up Grest by his head. He goes for a power bomb him on the outside of the ring to end it. Before Jones gets the chance Grest counters with a hurcanrunna. “That’s dark horse!” Grest screams after he executes the hurcanrunna. Grest is pumped both hands balled up in a fist as he moves thekm up and down awaiting Mike Jones to stand. He hits the Freddy Kick square on the chin that sends him crashing to the mat. Grest looks down at him and smiles. “Don’t forgot to lock up.” As Grest slides under the bottom rope and leaves the wrestling school. The door shuts behind him Grest takes a look around the dark, empty street and sighs. Feeling the cool breeze on him he realizes he should get back home before someone mistakes him for a male hooker. He heads to his black Ford Thunder Bird and opens the door. He hops in , shutting the door behind him and starts the car. The dark horse of wrestling pulls out of the parking lot and heads home. He pulls into his drive way but something doesn’t feel right. He hears a muffled scream from the alley. He slowly turns off the ignition to make out the screams. “Help!” He opens the door and speed walk to the back fence. “Help! Anyone!” Grest starts climbing the fence. He peeks at what’s happening and sees a young woman struggling to get away from a thug. A tall black man with a Michael Vick jersey, baggy jeans, and a blue bandanna. The Extreme One grabs onto the ledge of the garage and pulls himself onto it. He squats trying not to be seen moving closer to the victim’s attacker. He shows why he earned his original nickname as he leaps off without warning taking his attacker down. The victim is freed as she bolts away to get to safety. Grest gets off the attacker backing off to get to his feet. The rapist tries to get to his feet but Grest throws him into some trash cans. The attacker is dazed lying on his stomach as Grest sits on his back and forces the attacker’s hands behind his back. “Get off me! I didn’t do nothin!” The attacker yells at Grest but he doesn’t care. A man comes out from his house in his pajamas and a pair of hand cuffs. The older man opens his back gate and walks towards Grest. “I’m an off duty cop.” as the man hands Grest the hand cuffs. Grest is more then happy to cuff the asshole as the attacker plea his case. “Arresting me just cause I’m black! Excessive force! Police Brutality!” The attacker screams. Grest looks down at him and smirks. “I’m not a cop.” You can here police sirens close by as the cop looks at Grest. “You can go now. The police are on there way now. We can handle it from here. “Who the hell are you”!” The attacker screams. Fred Grest get off the man as the officer holds the attacker down. The police sirens are very close he looks down at the attacker with a smile. “You can call me The Grester.” “The Grester”! Some kind of super hero?! Let me go! I didn’t do nothin!” The man pleas. He heads over to his garage door and open it. He enters his garage amd turns around closing the door behind him. He lets out a heavy sigh after his long night. Grest puts his hands against the garage door and lowers his head thinking about what he’s done tonight. He got through the night with no major injuries. Surprisingly though considering he jumped off his garage. He’ll wake up tomorrow with some bruises at best. Grest wonders if he meant to do Great things but with nobody around to watch. He destroyed Elia Smith in a dark match. A tremendous competitor by anyone’s standards. He proved he could beat Mike Jones and stopped a heinous crime tonight. He didn’t keep up with the Mike Jones he surpassed him. Fred Grest knows that he’s moving up in his career even if it feels like he’s going nowhere. When he 1st started he was in the ring with nobodies. Instead of being a man among men he is a man among warriors. Grest is now out to prove he is headstrong to take on any of them.
