Re-Awakend

30 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-30-03 His eyes snapped open almost startling the black headed woman standing before the glass tube. The huge man looked around for a few moments. The tubes connecting to a mask on his face to pump air into his lungs followed.. “He is awake!” Says the woman in a cheerful voice. “Ohh Demitri! Welcome back to the living my husband.” She says placing her hand on the glass. A large hand reaches out and lightly touches the glass.. The woman turns and runs out of the room. “AARON!!!! HE awakens!!!” She screams. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ A man stands near a computer terminal. His hands working fast. The man is dressed in a black leather suit. His black hair spiked. He turns around to look at the tube and his blood red eyes shine. And he smiles. With a whoosh the tube of green liquid begins to bubble and the liquid begins to lower?. Slowly the liquid drains out of the tube and the large muscled man stands completely necked. Many small tubes attached to his arms and two large ones connect to a breathing mask. . He flexes his arms and twists his neck till a popping sound is heard. The red eyed man leaps down and slowly begins unhooking the breathing mask. “Glad to see you back Mephiston. Your sleep was long.” He says. As the tubes are removed a hiss of pure oxygen is heard and seen as the cold air comes into contact with the warmer air of the room. The room itself is not large. Computers and other equipment surround the tube in two levels, With one opening in front of it. Stairs leading up on both sides of the opening. The walls a dark gray colored of stone and mortar. Soon the mask is removed. “Thank you Aaron. Where is my wife?” The large behemoth known as Mephiston says. “She awaits you in your room. As soon as we finish here you can go to her.” Aaron says. Mephiston looks around and takes a deep breath. ? A lot of things have changed. You have added equipment.” Says Mephiston in a proud tone. “Ahh Yes. Well I to have changed. I have become an expert in biological research. The human DNA is quite intriguing. I have also learned Nano technology and a few other things.. “Says Aaron in a tone that borders on arrogance. “Interesting.” Says Mephiston. “My Research started that fateful day. All of it at the plea of my sister. I hope you don’t mind I did however take some liberties.” Mephiston looks down on the smaller man. “Liberties, what types of Liberties.” Mephiston questions. “Well it’s a long and drawn out list of things. Lets say I have enhanced what you already are. I had to do a lot of work to bring your rebirth about. Nano technology has helped. I hope you’re not angry.” “Not in the least. We shall have to go over these improvements later however. I have a feeling I shall need them. “As you wish, Brother.” Aaron says pleased. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Mephiston stands infront of his window in his room. The stars shine bright.. “A star is missing.” He says. “Yes its light was extinguished tonight. Before you woke up.” Says a female voice behind Mephiston. “Do you know when that star came to be?” He asks. “No my husband, I do not.” Says the female as she walks up and joins her husband hugging onto his side. “The day my sister was born. As you know we have always had a connection to each other Haven. Until now. My Sister. Lisha Starr is dead. Haven” I felt her pain, I felt her sorrow.” Mephiston says in a tone of voice that was devoid of emotion. “Ohh Demitri. I am soo Sorry.” Haven says. “They took her from me so long ago. She was only a baby. They killed my father and mother, and now my sister. The Davis family will reap a whirl wind. I vow this. They will pay. This time I will do it my self. No champions fighting for me. All I have now is you and Aaron.” Says Mephiston. His mussels tightening. “What needs to be done, My husband.” Says Haven looking into the giants eyes. “We prepare for war!” Says Mephiston. “I do not need my wife in this. I need my witch.” “Yes, Lord Mephiston.” Says Haven. “We are not alone. Aaron has reconstructed Golem.” “Very good. Your Brothers new knowledge will be the Lairs undoing.” Mephiston says Both stand there in silence, looking to where a star was, a star that died too soon.
