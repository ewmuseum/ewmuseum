The Beginning of a Journey...The Legend Continues....

15 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-15-03 *THUMP* *THUMP* *THUMP* Suddenly, a match light flickers into being and a candle soon follows, illuminating the room as only candles can do. We find ourselves n the bedchamber of Venneman Pendragon. Clad only in a pair of what would be called boxers by today’s standards, the massive man stumbles over to the oaken wardrobe. Mumbling, he looks in and hooks himself out a mail shirt of gold and pulls the metal shirt over his head? Pendragon: (mumbling) Good Christ! Who would come knocking at this time with me in mine short pants’impudent whelps’kings need their sleep too’why does this always happen to the Lord King? Everyone wants a piece of mine time? And he mumbles more of the sort, but you get the idea. He carries the candle and slips his feet into a pair of leather slippers as he walks past his sword, Excalibur, that rests upon a golden mantle. He opens his bedroom door and stops. He turns slowly towards the sword that seems to glow with an inner light. He nods and moves over, lifting it off of the mantle?. Pendragon: One never knows?. He says this almost as a premonition as he takes the sword and belts it to his waist. He is quite a sight, resplendent in his golden chain mail, glowing sword, slippers and underwear’But hey; it is the middle of the night! He passes through his door into a deep hallway. At regular intervals he lights torches that jut from the stone wall. As he lights one, he turns away and *****BAM***** Pendragon: GOOD CHRIST!! MY FOOT!! He pulls his foot up so he can grabs it and hops on one foot. He stares menacingly at the chair which was the instrument of his pain? Pendragon: Aye?.it seems that furniture goes whither it feels like? *****BAM***** Pendragon: DAMNIT!!! Hopping on one foot, he didn’t realize he was coming dangerously close to another torch until he banged his head upon it. Even kings can be klutzes at 3 in the morning?.Or whatever time it is. Rubbing his head and cursing softly, he limps slightly down the hall, candle still in hand. Gotta give him some credit; at least he didn’t drop it. Finally he comes to a great door at the end of the corridor flanked by two suits of silver armor. With a jerk, he throws the bolt on the door and pulls it open? Pendragon: By all mine ancestors’This had better be good! The door comes open and Pendragon stares, dumbfounded, at the man who stands there? Man: Good evening, my lord?””.. The man looks like a picture straight from a storybook. He sports a very long white beard, and he wears a deep blue robe. In his right hand he carries a wooden staff, and he has a fairly good grandfatherly look to him. He smiles a kindly smile? Pendragon: Merlin! It cannot be! Pendragon wipes his eyes in amazement, but Merlin still stands before him? Merlin: It has been a long time, my young lord? Pendragon: Didn’t you try to kill me last time we were met face to face? Pendragon’s hand drops to the hilt of his sword whilst Merlin smiles and takes on a “You hurt me” look and shrugs his shoulders? Merlin: I have taken many forms over the years’Or better yet, powers greater than I have taken my forms over the years. I stand before you now because you are at the center of a great upheaval? Pendragon stares at him quizzically, and Merlin smiles? Merlin: There will be plenty of time for explanations and formalities later, my young lord’For now though, you are needed! Pendragon: Wha? He cannot spit the word out as Merlin raises his staff to eye level. A bright light glows from its apex, and the screen suddenly goes dark?. Pendragon: Good Christ! *****FLASH***** A scene of pure beauty fills the screen. Hills and valleys, green and lush, stretch out for miles and miles. A golden sun sends its warmth to every blade. Here and there, a thatched hut guards a small field of barley, or wheat. Some have some corn, beans. The shot comes round and Pendragon lies, unconscious, in grass so green you can nearly feel it’Smell it. He begins to stir, and suddenly his eyes blink open. One hand drops to his sword and he sits up, taking in his surroundings?. “Where is this place in which I find mineself” The air smells’Clean. Non dirtied by the hustle and bustle of modern day society. And the grass?.softer than the finest goose down that has ever stuffed mine mattress. This land feels more real than anything I have ever felt, and yet, it cannot be real’Can it?” He rises and stretches his massive arms over his head. He looks out over the land and spies a small dirt road. With a deep breath, he begins walking towards it, through ankle high grass? “At the end of the road, there must lie a village of sorts. At least, I hope there is some semblance of civilization here’I need to get mine bearings?” He walks along, passing a hut here and there, but people do not stir forth. He feels the eyes upon him though, as he walks along, and his nerves heighten. Finally he stops and looks behind him and sees that he has attracted a small band of children. Giggling, the children point at the massive man. With a start, he looks down at himself? Pendragon: Good Christ! Still not clothed! Damn that Merlin! Evidently whatever portal Merlin took our hero through, it saw not fit to clothe him decently. He looks to the sky and slaps his forehead. At the slap, the children scatter to wherever they sprang from. Pendragon sighs and looks around. In the distance, he notices a scare crow in a field of corn. The scarecrow is wearing pants? “If only my luck will carry, those pants and boots will be mine size?” At a trot, he takes off towards the scarecrow. When he reaches it, it does appear that the scarecrow is on the same level size wise as he is. He smiles and pulls the pants and boots from the scarecrow. He pulls the pants on and smiles?. “Almost as if it were fated to be this way!” GET OUTTA MY CORN!! Pendragon comes out of his revelry with a start and looks towards a hut. An oldish looking woman runs slowly at him brandishing a broom. Pendragon puts his hands out towards her and backs up? Pendragon: Was just trying to cover mine indecency, old mother? With the term “old mother”, she stops and lowers the broom? Woman: It canna be! Can it be? Pendragon gives her a bewildered stare as a sense of recognition flares in her old eyes? Woman: The young prince has returned! Oh what a glorious day! And with the famed sword as well! Pendragon quickly pulls the boots over his feet and calves and stands before the woman. She stops her rant and approaches him? Woman: My lord?. She kneels in the grass before him, but Pendragon quickly pulls her to her feet. Pendragon: Rise old mother’I greatly wish to know the circumstances of this land, and I am sure that thee are well informed? Woman: Aye’been round these here parts for near eighty years now’I knows bout all there is to know bout the goings and comings of this here land?. Pendragon smiles warmly as he takes her hand in both of his? Pendragon: Then would thee invite a poor beggar into thy home to share a bit of palaver? The woman beams up at the mammoth man? Woman: of course, sire! Right this way! I will tell you all of the story’The land has lain under the shadow of Lord Akumancha’s Bane? “And who is Lord Akumancha” What bane has he unleashed upon this quaint and beautiful land? I smell a rat?.but surely Freeze’s grip cannot reach this far’Can it? Damned glad I am that I strapped Excalibur to mine side before I was ripped from mine home’And yet, tis said a hero goes where he is needed?” ******TO BE CONTINUED*******
