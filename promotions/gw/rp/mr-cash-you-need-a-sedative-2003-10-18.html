Mr. Cash, you need a sedative

18 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.18.03 Ace wakes suddenly, quickly sitting upright, causing his head to throb with pain and the room to spin. 'Oooh nooo.' Ace whines, seeing he's back in the hospital bed. 'Damn that little punk ass kid.' Ace surveys the room, noticing that it's dark and must be night time. Ace's eyes bulge as he remembers the nurse, and he searches around for any sign of her presense. Thankfully, she's nowhere to be seen. However, in the time between Ace being knocked out and waking up, he had a roomate in the bed a few feet away from him. 'Holy duck billed platepus, batman..it's a harpoon whale.' Ace mumbles, looking at the woman that occupies...and then some the bed beside him. A large black woman that has to be at least 400 pounds lays on the bed, sound asleep. Ace slowly inches out of the bed, ready to leave the hospital with or without permission. He glances over, and sees where his clothes lay on the table. 'Wait a minute...I was in my wrestling gear when I got hit.' Ace remembers. How did his clothes get there? On top of the pile is a note. Ace, I brought you some clothes from your house for when you get out. And bro, watch out for that nurse. She's seriously messed up! I hope you get out soon, then we can deal with the TUE. Peace, TLM Good old TLM, always looking out for Ace. Too bad he wasn't there when Ace got that baseball bat broken over his head. Ace feels the rather large bump on his head that the bat left. 'Damn homo repressed Hughes....ugh, that hurts.' Ace says quietly to himself. 'Maybe he found out I slept with his sister.' Ace shrugs as he slips on boxers under the gown and then sheds the gown. He puts on his jeans and a silk muscle shirt. Lastly, he puts on his K-Swiss boots. Laying next to his pile of clothes were what at first, Ace thought were blankets. But he quickly discovered that they were his 'roomates' clothes. There was also a note on top of that pile. Ace reaches to take the note, knocking down the clothes. Ace reaches down to pick up the note, but comes back with something far different. 'Ooooh my gaawwwddd!' Ace whispers, throwing a leopard thong far across the room. The thing could have fit around 4 of Ace's waists. 'That's just...wrong!' Ace sees the note and picks it up. Ahh hell naw! - TLM Ace supresses a smile at the thought of TLM seeing the woman in the room with Ace. The Ladies Man was sure to have nightmares for that one. Ace looks at the woman and a thought suddenly comes to his mind, and he inches over to the bedside where a clipboard lay on a chair with the womans name. Brooklyn Hughes Family Contact: Jason Hughes HAH! Jason Hughes' mom! Ace rolls on the floor laughing, regardless of the pain it causes his head, this was just too damn funny. After a good laugh, Ace inches towards the door, peaking his head out in search of anybody that may be walking about the hospital. He sees nobody. Ace slowly proceeds down the hallway, slightly crouched. All he wanted to do was get out of the hospital right now! 'Mr. Cash! You need a sedative.' A stern voice yells down the hallway. Ace freezes in his tracks and looks back to see the sillouette of his nurse standing with an epidermic needle in her hand like that of a knife for a serial killer in a horror movie. Reacting like only a true wrestler could... 'AHHH!' Ace screams in an unusually high pitched wail as he runs down the hallway away from the nurse. Ace is running for dear life, with the nurse running right behind him..even a little faster. 'What the hell are you, lady!? Are you Kenyan!? Wearing the Air Force One's!?' Ace screams over his shoulder as the unusually fast nurse chases after him. 'Leave me alone!....please!' Ace adds with a whimper. Ace sees his small window of opportunity as he takes a sharp turn, and hiding inside of a janitors closet. Ace crouches back into the darkness, and hears the nurse running past, mumbling to herself. 'God damn green haired freak.' She mumbles 'When I catch him, this needle is going in his ass!' Ace covers his face in fear as he hears her say that. But, as the footsteps slowly slowly fade away, he smiles and lets out a small smile and sigh of relief. The door suddenly opens! 'AHH!' Ace screams once again in that high pitched voice. However, it's not the nurse that opens the door, it's a midget with a mop. Because he's wearing headphones that are blaring, he didn't hear Ace's scream, and Ace is well concealed in the darkness. The midget grabs a bucket of soapy water and shuts the door again. Ace is taking quick, loud breaths trying to regain his composure from that fright. He tilts his head back with another sigh beforing cracking the door open. ' 'What the fu-' Ace says disbelievingly. The midget janitor is twirling around with the mop, singing the lyrics to I Do by 98 Degrees. 'Baby, I do...cherish you...for the rest of my life.' The janitor sings with an amazingly soothing voice. 'Hey, he's not too bad.' Ace says, snapping his fingers with the singing, swaying his head back and forth. He quickly jumps however when the singing suddenly changes. 'You d-d-d-d!!!! Don't have to think ink! ink! ink! chink! chink! twice.' The midget sings, his head jerking violently to the side as he stutters the lyrics. Oh great, a nurse with a needle, a midget with terrets and a 400 pound black mother of Ace's worst enemy. What a hospital this turned out to be! Ace slowly opens the door and turns the corner, walking down the hallway, avoiding the janitor. 'Exit, exit, exit..' Ace mumbles to himself searching the doors. He comes across lots of patient rooms before seeing the glowing exit sign at the end of the hallway. He breaks into a sprint towards the door, and almost makes it before a door bursts open right before the exit. The nurse jumps out with the needle pointing at Ace. She stares with an evil grin at Ace, pushing some of the sedative fluid out of the needle. 'Mr. Cash..' She speaks pleasantly with a smile. Her face then takes on a mix between anger and insanity. 'STAND STILL, MOTHERFUCKER!' Ace turns on his heel and runs as the nurse lunges at him with the needle. 'What the hell is going on!?' Ace screams, running with the nurse only steps behind.
