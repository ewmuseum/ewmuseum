My fathers day gift...Part 2 of 2

16 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/16/03 (A few hours have passed since Sean appeared in the home of his parents Matthew and Lisa, and from the start Sean was as expected looked on as being an outcast and not deserving to be there. From the second that he stepped into the family room his father voiced his displeasure and his brother voiced his disgust. Mean while Sean stood there and took the verbal abuse for the sake of his family and to try and live upto the promise that he made his mother. A promise that Sean has since realized will not be a very easy one go through with, but nonetheless he is trying. As the scene fades in we see Sean seated at the kitchen table, his brother directly across from him and at the head of the table is his father. Within second’s Sean’s mother walks into the living room with a big pot in her hand, she sets it down in the center of the table and than taking a seat directly across from her husband. She grabs her son Luke’s hand and then Sean’s hand. Luke extends his hand out to his father who accepts the invitation and takes a firm hold of Luke’s hand. Sean extends his hand out to his father, at first though his father pulls his hand back but after looking at his wife motioning him to grab his son’s hand he finally does. Now that the whole family is joined together, they all bow their heads down and begin to pray.) “Bless us O Lord for these gifts which we are about to receive and from our bounty through Christ our Lord amen.” (With the completion of this each member of the family lifts their heads for a moment, when Sean looks up he see’s his brother staring directly at him with a look of anger and disgust. Sean then looks over at his mother who has a huge smile on her face.) “That was nice, we have not ate dinner as a family in years. It was a nice change. I hope you appreciate this Matt because Sean came here for you.” Exclaims his wife “You keep reminding me of that, don’t you think I know that?” snaps back her husband “Maybe you don’t but you have an awful way of showing it. And you Luke, Sean is your brother he will always be your brother, you should be thankful that you have a brother who cares enough about you and this family to come here, knowing what he would have to put up with from you and your father.” “Some brother, leaves me beaten in the middle of a ring on national television. He made a promise to me, that we would be the tag team to beat in GW and he disowned that promise and me at the same time.” “You know Luke, if you want to talk about broken promises than I am not the only one at fault.” The words from Sean come as a surprise to Luke and their father. “What are you talking about Sean, I broke no promises.” “Oh is that so Luke?” “Yeah that’s so. When you first asked me to join the GW you said that we would win the tag titles in our first chance at them. You guaranteed it. And what happened our first match for the titles” Go ahead Luke, you can say it, we already know.” “We lost.” “That’s right, we as a tag team lost, but you lost the match, your cocky attitude and your excellent ability to overlook your opponents is what cost us the match. You were pinned not me. I have been in this fed for almost a year now and I have yet to be pinned in any match. With the exception of that match my record is flawless, 17-1. You broke your promise to me by losing that match. I don’t regret what I did, because when it comes down to it you were looking out for you, and you alone when you blew that match for us, I had a chance to better myself, to make something of my career and I took it.” “You took it at the expense of your brother, your own flesh and blood. I can’t forgive you for that.” “Oh get over yourself Luke, if I really wanted to end you I would of, but I didn’t, nor did I allow it to happen at the hands of Jason and Jay. You’re my brother and you always will be, until the day I die, I will fight by your side. But the fact remains that your not a pro wrestler. You don’t have what it takes. Sure you were all state in High School and great in college but you just didn’t have what it took to hack it with the pro’s in GW. That’s not my fault, though you seem to be very anxious to pin it off on me.” (Sean’s father attempts to intervene but their mother stops him, insisting that he allows their son’s to talk.) “How do you know what I could and could not do” You didn’t give me a chance Sean. If it wasn’t for me you wouldn’t even be in the GW. You wouldn’t be who you are today without me.” A look of surprise comes across Sean’s face before he stands up at the table, looks down at Luke and goes off on a rant. “Look Luke, your right, I would not be in the GW if it were not for you. And for that I am eternally great full. But I am who I am today because of what I have accomplished in the GW.” “There you go Luke, your brother said he was great full for you getting him involved in GW. He has not forgotten that he got his big break because of you. But it was him that made a career for himself with his accomplishments in the ring. Maybe you should congratulate him on that instead of being spiteful.” “You know what mom, I have come to realize that you can’t make everyone like you. Sad thing is, family should never be the one in question, but in this instance it is. I can’t force Luke to like me. I will continue to wrestle in GW despite him and in spite of him. Now if you will excuse me but I have to get going, I have a big match tomorrow and I need to get back to Texas. Dinner was great. Happy fathers day Dad.” (Sean walks over to his mother and gives her a hug. He then walks over to his father and pats him on the shoulder, the two make eye contact for a brief moment and than Sean walks over to the steps and takes his jacket which is hung over the railing, he slowly puts it on as he makes his way outside of the house. He turns back to see his parents standing at the front door watching him leave. One of the TUE body guards gets out of the limo and opens the back door for Sean. Sean gets in the limo followed by the body guard and within moments the limo pulls away from the driveway and towards its destination of Dallas, Texas.)
