She's The Blade: Playtime Is Over

28 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 28, 2005 Mike Biddle stood in front of the mirror that adorned the wall of his bathroom. He was freshly showered and stood before the mirror clad in only a pair of jean shorts. He smiled at himself as he stared into the mirror; he flexed his upper body muscles and chuckled to himself. “There’s no way I can lose that rumble,” he said to himself, “I’m too perfect. They should just call off the match and declare me the number one contender.” He ran a comb through his thick blond locks and smiled at himself even more. “I’m way too good looking to be stuck in the mid-card,” he said to himself. “Hurry up Mike,” called a feminine voice from behind the bathroom door. “We’re going to miss our flight.” “Just a second,” Mike called back, to himself he said, “You can’t rush perfection woman.” He put on a plain red t-shirt and looked at himself in the mirror one last time. He flexed his arm and kissed his muscular bicep. “Those turd snaps don’t stand a chance,” he said with a smirk before he exited the bathroom. Lisa Jameson noticed that her man had finally left the bathroom. “You’re worse than a woman,” she said with an irritated tone; she had waited nearly two hours for him to finish in there. “What the hell do you do in there anyway?” “I wonder why anyone in their right mind would think they stand a chance against me,” Mike said with a cocky smirk. Lisa rolled her eyes in annoyance. “You better not act like this the whole way there,” she said as she pulled her long brown hair back in a ponytail. “Fine,” Mike said, “when do we leave?” “Two hours ago,” Lisa said with a smile, “now hurry your cute ass up, the limo is outside waiting for us.” They left for the limo hand in hand; an innocent beginning that belied the horror that awaited the couple in Rome. ***** A week had passed since the young couple left Beverly Hills hand in hand. Since their arrival to Rome both had treated the trip as a vacation, not the business trip that it truly was. This angered Mike’s manager, William Bastafar, greatly. The hot-blooded Scotsman hadn’t yet made his thoughts known to Mike but he planned to force Mike to train later on. William could never quite figure out how Mike expected to be successful without the proper training. William waited in the lobby of Mike’s hotel. As he sat on a soft leather sofa he had to turn away a few autograph seekers that had confused him for Sean Connery. Hours had passed and he leaned on his black mahogany cane with the golden skull tip in annoyance. He removed his black bowler hat and wiped the sweat from his brow with a white handkerchief. He looked up at the clock on the wall and sighed in annoyance when he realized how long he had sat in the same spot. He was about to leave and wander about the city in search of Mike when Mike finally came through the entrance of the hotel with his arm wrapped around his newest tramp. With a quick glance William knew that Mike was intoxicated. With a scowl on his face William rose from his seat and walked over to the front desk where Mike stood. “Excuse me lad,” William said once he was in hearing distance, “what in the hell are you doing?” Mike turned around and if it wasn’t for the sunglasses that rested on his face William would see him roll his dark blue eyes in annoyance. “What is it William?” Mike asked in his most polite tone. Lisa simply looked on; she was confused since she hadn’t yet met William. “You should be training,” William began with a stern tone, “not carousing about the town like an idiotic high school boy.” “I don’t need to train William,” Mike said, “you know damn well that I already have the rumble won’I’m Mr. Main Event damn it!” “Is that why you’ve lost two matches in a row?” William asked, “and might I add that you’ve lost those matches to women that are half your size. How exactly do you plan to beat the entire Global Wrestling roster when you can’t even handle two tiny women?” Mike fumed for a moment but withheld his anger; he knew that he was drunk and that now was neither the time nor the place to allow this situation to get out of hand. “I don’t need to train William,” he said through clenched teeth, “now go enjoy Rome for a few days and I’ll see you at the arena the day of the show.” William glared at Mike with a look that would turn a normal mortal to stone before he turned and walked away. “If you won’t train willingly I’ll just have to force you,” William quietly muttered to himself. “Who was that?” Lisa asked after William had left. “Just my manager,” Mike said, “he takes his job too seriously I’m afraid. Anyway, where were we?” Lisa grabbed Mike by the hand and led him to the elevator that would take them to their room, where they would finish off their evening. **** The next evening Mike and Lisa dined at the black tie only restaurant down the road from their hotel. Long forgotten were William Bastafar and his mantra of “you should always train”. The two enjoyed their pasta dinner and wine so much that they did not notice a trio of ruffians approach their table. The other patrons did notice and a few of them quietly exited the establishment since they knew that this particular trio was nothing but trouble. The three boys were in their late teens and they were members of a local street gang. The three were similar in appearance; they wore the same black leather jackets and had their hair cut the same way. They stopped at the table which Mike and Lisa dined at. At first Mike paid no attention to them since he was too busy with Lisa to notice anyone else. “Are you Mike Biddle?” One of the boys asked after they had stood beside the table for a few moments. The tallest boy that was the obvious leader of the group became annoyed by the fact that Mike ignored him, so much so that he kicked the table over in a fit of rage. Now the trio had effectively received the attention that they had so desperately tried to obtain. Mike glared at the boys in anger and motioned for Lisa to go find a safe haven. Mike rose from his seat and stood face to face with the boy that had knocked over his table. “What the hell did you do that for colon cake?” Mike asked as he cracked his knuckles. “I asked you if you were Mike Biddle,” the boy said as he shoved Mike, “colon cake.” The trio laughed as they watched Mike stumble back in surprise from the shove. Mike immediately responded with a punch to the temple to the leader of the trio. The greasy headed youth unconsciously fell to the ground with a great thud. “Who’s next?” Mike asked as he gave the fallen boy a sharp kick to the ribs. The two boys that remained charged at Mike at full speed. Mike laughed as he clothes-lined the two to their feet. He then picked up the second largest boy by his jacket and smashed his head repeatedly into the wall until he heard the crack of his nose as it broke. Mike then turned and motioned for the smallest boy to join the scuffle. With wide eyes the boy shook his head no and ran away as fast as his legs would allow. Mike readjusted his tie with a smirk as he viewed the destruction he had just caused. Lisa was then by his side. “Are you okay?” she asked with great concern. “Naturally,” Mike replied as his smirk grew wider. Their conversation was interrupted by the sound of the owner of the restaurant as he yelled at them in Italian. The short pudgy man shooed the couple out of the establishment. As they left Mike briefly wondered why the trio of youths had attacked him in the first place. ***** As Mike and Lisa entered the hotel lobby they were once again met by William Bastafar. “You look as though you’ve been in a fight lad,” William remarked with a smile. “As matter of fact I was just attacked by three idiots,” Mike said in irritation as he gave William a suspicious look. “And I take it you beat them?” William asked. Lisa’s mouth suddenly fell agape at what she heard. “You mean you knew about that before it happened?” She asked with a voice that could cut diamonds. “Of course,” William replied, “I hired them.” Lisa then growled in William’s general direction. “I’ll be in the room waiting for you,” she said to Mike before she stomped off. Mike watched his lover stomp off with a sigh. He then turned to William, “What do you mean you hired them?” Mike asked the older man. “Well,” William said, “since you refused to train the only thing I could do was hire some ruffians to attack you. Surprise attacks are always the best kind of training for a rumble.” “I told you I didn’t need to train,” Mike said in anger. “If you don’t train I’ll simply hire someone else,” William said, “and I doubt that next time you’ll have such an easy time defeating them. Play time is over lad, you need to train if you expect to win.” “Fine,” Mike said in defeat, “I’ll train.” Would training help Mike in his bid to become the number one contender of the World Title? Only time can tell.
