Viva la raza

14 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/14/05 The Brick/McClancy match was weeks in the coming. It all began with a triple threat match he had with both McClancy and Virgil Ambrosi for a shot at the Platinum Title. At the climax of the match, Ambrosi pulled Brick into a spray of mist from Blaise's mouth, blinding him with it. Taking advantage, Ambrosi hit the Cocytus Drop for the win. While he was angry with Ambrosi, he was also somewhat angry with McClancy due to the fact that Brick doubted that he wouldn't have sprayed the mist in Brick's face anyway. Weeks later, Brandi Sights booked Brick and Train in a match with Unite for a chance at McClancy. After the match, McClancy confronted Brick about comments Brick made. He then gave Brick an opportunity to get payback by turning his back on him. As tempting as it was, Brick didn't take it. After claiming respect for Brick, McClancy agreed to a match right then and there, shaking hands with him. Then, when Brick attempted to pull his hand away, McClancy wouldn't let go and then came a spray of mist to Brick's eyes. McClancy then took to decimating the disoriented Brick. When Train attempted to come to Brick's aid, Unite attacked him. Needless to say, this ignited an inferno inside Brick. Brick was headed out the door to go to the gym, when, suddenly, his phone rang. 'Hello?' 'Brick.' 'Hey, Brian.' It was Brian Gibson, someone who used to work for Global and someone Brick still kept in contact with every now and then. 'Hey, Brick, did you hear about Eddie Guerrero?' 'No, what about Eddie Guerrero?' 'He's dead.' Immediately, Brick rose to his feet. 'What?' 'Yeah, it's on the front page of WWE.com. They found him in his hotel room this morning.' Brick felt a sort of knot in his stomach, like he'd just been suckerpunched. It was all so sudden. He hadn't really known Guerrero personally. They had come across one another a few times, worked various exhibition events, as people in their profession were wont to do. Still, it hit him hard just the same. And at that moment, his animosity with McClancy didn't matter. 'Did they say what the cause was?' 'Nobody knows. There were no signs of foul play. The current theory is heart failure.' How do I say goodbye to what we had? The good times that made us laugh Outweigh the bad. Brick respected Eddie as a man who faced the demons that plagued him and came out on top. He saw Eddie as something of a kindred spirit as far as endurance in the business. Both men had endured a great deal in their careers and in both their cases, it paid off. Brick never suffered the way Eddie had and wasn't going to pretend for one second that he knew what he was going through. He then thought about his own reputation as being someone who cheated death a number of times. He thought about how Eddie was only seven years older than he was. Then he thought to himself, It could be me someday.' I thought we'd get to see forever But forever's gone away It's so hard to say goodbye to yesterday. His mother often told him that tomorrow wasn't promised to us. It could have been a few days from now. It could have been the next day. It could have been that exact moment. I don't know where this road Is going to lead All I know is where we've been And what we've been through. If we get to see tomorrow I hope it's worth all the wait It's so hard to say goodbye to yesterday. Brick immediately logged on to WWE.com and read letters that fans had sent in. He had also searched various messageboards on the net. There was so much love felt by the fans. It seemed to just radiate from the computer screen, along with the light. And I'll take with me the memories To be my sunshine after the rain It's so hard to say goodbye to yesterday. And I'll take with me the memories To be my sunshine after the rain It's so hard to say goodbye to yesterday. 'Viva la raza, Eddie.' ---- Eddie Guerrero: 1967-2005 Viva la raza Lyrics credit: 'It's So Hard To Say Goodbye To Yesterday' by Boyz II Men.
