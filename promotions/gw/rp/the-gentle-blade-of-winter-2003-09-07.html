The Gentle Blade of Winter

7 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 7th, 2003 'When the way comes to an end, change -- and having changed, pass through.' -- The Tao of Shinsei In the recently-rented home of Akodo Rokku, one could find many things. The IWL Championship Belt. A collection of women's phone numbers. Photographs, chronicling the Steel Lion's entire career. Several bottles of fine sake. A closet full of expensive clothing. But tonight, more prevalent than anything else, you would find silence. Akodo Rokku knelt on the bare concrete floor of his basement, his katana laid across his lap. The blade had been a gift from Yoshiko, hand-crafted by an old-style swordsmith to specifications she had given him. When she had presented it to Rokku, he hadn't known how to react -- he wasn’t even sure he could accept such a gift. 'The katana is the soul of the warrior,' she had said, 'an ancient symbol of honor and strength. Qualities which I think you have in abundance, Rokku. Keep the blade with you always, to remind you of those things, and to help you find yourself when you are lost.' Rokku sighed. The way he had acted a few nights ago? he looked down at the sword. He only hoped Yoshiko had been right, that he was worthy of this gift -- and that it would guide him. He lit a stick of incense and placed it in a holder on the floor, then closed his eyes and inhaled deeply. He let the scent dance through his head, relaxing his thoughts. His hand closed around the hilt of the katana, and he slowly pulled it free of its saya. Rising to his feet, he began a Victory of the River kata, swinging the blade in slow, fluid strokes. Closing his eyes, he envisioned the weakness within himself. The weakness that he had let claim him that night. Melting together, those weaknesses took a form. Leinad Soma. Soma grinned at Rokku through bloodstained lips. Rokku immediately slipped into Striking Like Water, trying to unleash the crushing power of the waves on this demon through the kata. But the ghost of Soma in Rokku's mind simply laughed, dancing away from Rokku's heavy, clumsy strikes. Scowling, Rokku redoubled his efforts, but again and again Soma avoided him, leaving Rokku frustrated, sweating, and gasping for breath. Growling, Rokku launched into a different kata known as Fury Without End. Channeling his anger and frustration into his movements, Rokku launched himself into a rapid series of vicious strikes at Soma. Soma simply stood his ground, his twisted smile growing as he batted aside every swing. With sudden savagery, Soma knocked Rokku's blade from his hands, and slammed his fist into the Lion's stomach. Rokku gasped for air as Soma followed the fist with a knee, then drove a palm strike into Rokku's face, and kicked out his knee, sending him tumbling to the floor. 'You're nothing to me, Rokku,' said Soma, kicking Rokku in the ribs as he tried to stand, 'Not even a man -- just prey. I am that which darkness fears. A monster whispered of in legends for centuries. How can you possibly hope to defeat me?' Rokku felt cold fear grip him again. What could he do? How could a man, just a man, fight a monster like this? 'Gi.' Startled by the new voice, Rokku looked up and saw Yoshiko standing over him. Soma seemed ignorant of her presence as he grabbed the Steel Lion by his hair and began to lift him off the ground. 'Yu,' continued Yoshiko. 'Jin. Rei. Meyo. Makoto. Chugo. Justice, Courage, Compassion, Courtesy, Honor, and Sincerity. These are the seven tenets of Bushido: the way of the samurai. The way of the hero. With Bushido as his ally, a warrior will be at his strongest. When Bushido truly fills your heart and soul, you will be invincible -- and even the darkest of monsters will fall on your blade.' Soma slammed Rokku into the wall, locking his fingers around the Lion's throat. Rokku could feel Soma's breath on his face, and knew that his death was near. But he smiled. Soma's expression turned perplexed. 'Made your peace with death, have you?' he asked. 'No,' Rokku choked out, his smile growing, 'I've realized I'm going to win.' Soma laughed, but Rokku brought his hands up to Soma's -- and started to peel the vampire's fingers off his throat. 'Gi,' said Rokku, echoing Yoshiko, 'Yu. Jin. Rei. Meyo. Makoto. Chugo!' With the last word, Rokku pulled Soma's hands off his throat, and pushed him away. 'Those are the seven tenets of Bushido,' said Rokku, picking up his sword. 'I'd let myself forget them, but I remember now. That night I attacked you, I discarded them all, first letting blind anger guide my actions, then succumbing to crippling fear when I realized your true nature. No more.' Soma laughed again, a sick, hateful sound. 'You're still nothing more than human, boy. Another meal like any other. And it's long past time I proved that to you!' Soma charged, but Rokku slipped to the side, his katana biting deeply into Soma's flesh. As Soma howled in pain, Rokku began another kata -- The Gentle Blade of Winter. Serenity and control suffusing his being, Rokku batted aside Soma's attacks like the vampire had done to his earlier. Moving with absolute precision, Rokku slowly picked Soma apart, leaving a countless series of cuts and knicks in the vampire's flesh. 'This is impossible,' Said Soma, dropping to one knee in his weakness. 'You're just a man!' 'No,' said Rokku separating Soma's head from his shoulders with a single perfect strike, 'I'm a samurai.' Exhaling slowly, Rokku opened his eyes. The apparitions of his mind -- Soma, Yoshiko -- were gone. Glancing at his watch, he was surprised to see that it had been hours since he'd started his kata -- no wonder he felt so sore. He moved to sheathe the katana, but stopped. He took a moment to stretch, then assumed the first stance of the Gentle Blade of Winter kata again. Though his mind was now at ease, Rokku knew it would take a hero to defeat a monster like Soma. But if a hero was needed, then a hero he'd be.
