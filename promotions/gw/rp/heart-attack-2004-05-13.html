Heart Attack

13 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 13 (for Overload) The scene opens up to a white and blue ambulance racing down the street. Right behind the ambulance is a blue convertible going about 100km an hour. The ambulance comes to a stop in a old parking lot. The convertible also comes to a spot close to the ambulance. From the ambulance 2 workers rush out and open up the back of the ambulance. The two workers bring down a stretcher wich has Father Clark on it. From the car Roswell rushes from it and runs right behind the stretcher. The stretcher hits the bumby pavement and then slides into white sliding doors. Roswell runs into them and sees the stretcher go into a elevator, Roswell walks over to the elevator and pushes the button 15. Roswell walks out of the elevator and sees the stretcher just going into a room. Roswell runs to the room and walks into the doorway. Roswell walks over to Father who is laying on the bed kind of looking like he was knocked out. 'Are you okay, Father?' 'I'm pretty sure i'm fine' 'You survived a heart attack' 'Heart attack? I had nothing close to a heart attack' 'Well then what did you have?' 'Welll I didn't really have anything, I got shot!' 'What? Why would someone do that?' 'Well it is California' 'Did you see his face?' 'No not really, he was wearing a mask' Then a worker walks into the room and tells Roswell that he has to leave because they have to do some stuff to Father. Roswell then walks out the door and down the white hallway. Roswell then opens a door with a sign on it saying 'Waiting Room.' Roswell walks into the room and sits down in one of the chairs. A couple of minutes later a worker walks into the room and says that Roswell can come and see Father now. Roswell gets up from the chair and walks out of the room with the worker. Roswell walks into a curtain and sees Father all hooked up into this machine. 'So how are you feeling?' 'I'm feeling better' 'Father I have to go back to the hospital and change because i'm all sweaty. I'll be back in 20 minutes' 'Alright then, have a safe trip' 'I will' Roswell walks over to the blue door and walks out it. Roswell then walks down a hallway and walks down some stairs. Roswell then opens a door and walks outside. He walks over to his convertible and starts it up. Roswell then backs up and speeds out of the hospital parking lot. Roswell races down the street and then comes to a stop right beside 'Dairy Queen.' 'I could use a cone' Roswell then stops the car and hops out of it. He walks to the door and opens it, he walks inside and runs to the front racing to be first. Roswell rings a bell and waits for someone to come and help him. A worker walks up to Roswell with a little booklet in his hand. 'Welcome to Dairy Queen, what would you like today?' 'I'll just have a vanilla cone, please' 'Wait just one second' The worker walks away from Roswell and walks to the back of the store telling the other workers what he wants. A couple of secons later a worker walks back to Roswell with a cone in his hand, Roswell then pulls out his wallet and gives the worker the money and Roswell grabs the cone and leaves out the door. 'Ahhhh, brain freeze!' Roswell walks with his cone and hops back into his car and starts it. He then skids out of the spot and goes down the street. He then comes to a stop in the parking lot right beside his hotel. Roswell who is already done his ice cream stops the car and walks to the hotel doors. Roswell walks down the lobby and into a elevator. He pushes the button 5 and the elevator goes up. It comes to a stop and Roswell walks out and walks to his room. Roswell pulls his wallet out again and takes out the hotel key, he slides it through the door and opens the door. SCENE ENDS
