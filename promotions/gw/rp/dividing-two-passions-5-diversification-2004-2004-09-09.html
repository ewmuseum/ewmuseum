Dividing Two Passions #5: Diversification 2004

9 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 9th 2004 “Get up!” screams Constantino Zergolov. Zergolov is standing in the Vergomov apartment. He is wearing black shorts and a white 76ers jersey. His hair is scruffy and his beard un-trimmed. In his hand, he holds a glass of water. Slowly he slants it towards Igor Arefiev who is lying on the sofa. Gradually the water edges closer to pouring onto his tag team partner. Arefiev groans into the white sofa. Igor Arefiev is practically naked, except for a pair of navy boxers hiding his modesty. He is lying face down on the sofa and therefore does not see the glass and more importantly the water trickling towards him. Arefiev groans again, as though still feeling his tag team partner’s presence at his side. Again, Zergolov slants the glass even more. A small drop of water falls from the lip of the glass and splashes Arefiev’s back. Almost immediately, Arefiev is up and standing opposite his partner. “WHAT THE?”” questions Igor as he stares a hole in his tag team partner. Zergolov smirks in response. “What was that about” No need to soak me!” protests the circus performer as he rubs between his shoulder blades awkwardly. Constantino continues to smirk, and then turns towards the kitchen. “Why did you soak me?” complains Arefiev as Zergolov disappears into the kitchen. Arefiev grumbles beneath his breath as he rubs at his shoulder blades with a real look of stress across his face. Out of the kitchen walks Constantino seconds later minus the glass of water. “Right come on! We have training!” He smiles. Arefiev grumbles again. “Chop chop!” exclaims Zergolov as he claps his hands and shepherds Arefiev towards his bedroom, “Hurry up! We are leaving soon!” Despite protesting, Arefiev is hurried into the bedroom. The door slams shut. Zergolov smiles as he stands alone in the apartment by himself. “We won’t make the same mistake again. GSA” Step one?” The smile across his face grows more and more. “Lightning shall never strike twice.” Zergolov stands smiling out the window, overlooking Dallas. Igor’s bedroom door clicks open. Zergolov swings round to meet his tag partner. Arefiev steps out dressed in a navy Vergomovs tracksuit. “Aren’t you getting ready for training?” questions both men to each other at the same time. There is a weird icy silence. “WHAT?” question both surprised men. There is another silence. Igor looks at his partner’s attire with a surprised look. “What are you wearing?” questions Arefiev. Zergolov sighs. “We are training with Iversen today!” Arefiev’s looks puzzled. “Allen Iversen?”... The Basketball player?”” questions Constantino as though trying to jog his partner’s memory. Igor looks even more puzzled. “What?” questions Arefiev. “We have basketball training,” explains Zergolov, “remember” Obviously not! We are not going to let any chances of the tag team titles pass us by.” Arefiev still looks confused. He looks down at the ground and then back up at his tag team partner. “Is this?” begins Igor, “Is this part of your stupid new training schedule?” “Diversification!” nods Zergolov. Arefiev grumbles. “I didn’t actually think you were serious about that!” Zergolov smiles. “Oh yes! And I’m that confident about my idea, I’m going to release a Christmas video,” beams the overjoyed Russian. “You’re such a sell-out!” mumbles Igor. He turns walks across the room, and lifts a small bundle of clothes neatly folded on the side table. “What do you mean “sell-out?” This coming from the guy that convinced me to turn my back on the circus!” objects Constantino. Igor flaps away Zergolov’s comments with his hands as he disappears into his bedroom. The bedroom door clicks shut. Zergolov smiles in the empty door. “Lightning never strikes twice on me!” he exclaims, as he puffs a pillow. Igor’s bedroom door swings open and Arefiev steps into view. He is wearing grey shorts and a Puerto Rican basketball jersey. “Very funny,” smirks Igor. “What?” questions a surprised Zergolov. “This!” Igor grabs the basketball jersey and points it towards his tag team partner, while still wearing it. “What?” Zergolov smirks. “You somehow managed to organise a training session with the American co-captain, and guess what jersey I’m wearing” One of the teams that America lost too in the Olympics!” complains Arefiev as he looks at his jersey. Zergolov smirks in reply. His arms are folded as he rocks back and forth on the balls of his feet. Arefiev sighs. “Why do we have to do basketball training anyway?” whinges Arefiev. “Diversification?” states Zergolov confidently, “it’s all part of my plan so that we never lose again. We are the greatest circus-performing duo” ever? period. We will soon be the greatest wrestling duo ever? period. Just wait until diversification kicks in.” Igor stands in silence, still preoccupied with his attire. “Why Puerto Rico?” grumbles Arefiev. “Oh shut up!” slams Zergolov, “you’re such a baby!” “Am not!” “Are too!” “Am not!” “See what I am mean?” smiles Zergolov. Arefiev is stunned. He looks at his tag team partner, then turns on his heel. He faces the wall and cross his arms, as he huffs. “BABY!” whispers Zergolov loudly. “GO AWAY!” screeches Igor. Zergolov sighs and drops onto the sofa. There is silence in the apartment as Zergolov flicks aimlessly through a magazine. The silence extends for a number of seconds. “So,” begins Zergolov, “you finished huffing” So we can go?” Arefiev mumbles. “What?” questions Zergolov. Arefiev mumbles. Zergolov shrugs his shoulders. He passes through a few more pages. Suddenly, Constantino jumps to his feet and makes for the door. “Come on!” exclaims Zergolov. Arefiev looks up from his disgruntled pose. He turns away from his tag team partner and continues to huff. Zergolov disappears out the door. Igor looks up at the door and then around the apartment. He quickly rushes for the door. Zergolov steps out on the street and is soon followed by Igor Arefiev. “You decided to follow then?” utters Zergolov. Arefiev is silent. “Okay!” voices Zergolov. The pair are soon walking in deafening silence. The silence second-by-second gets longer. “Diversification” Really?” questions Igor. “What?” comes the sharp reply. “It doesn’t strike me as a brilliant name for a work-out program.” “What is that supposed to be mean?” Zergolov is now on the defensive and is biting his replies at Igor. “It doesn’t roll off the tongue” Tae-bo? That rolls off the tongue really well!” “I’m not calling my workout Tae-bo!” “Obviously not, because there is a workout called Tae-bo! But you need a catchy name!” Zergolov sighs. “Why” Whats wrong with “Diversification?”” Arefiev groans. “What’s right with it?” Zergolov turns and sticks his tongue out at his tag team partner. “Yeah. Really mature!” jokes Arefiev. Zergolov turns his head from his best friend sharply. He refuses to looks back. Arefiev sighs as the pair walk down the Dallas streets.
