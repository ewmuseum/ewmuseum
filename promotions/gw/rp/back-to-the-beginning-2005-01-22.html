Date: 01/22/05 It happened again, the third time this week’the nightmare returned. Only variation this time was I was my current age, not the defenseless 8 year old boy. But it made no difference, I just sat there and watched? [i]?Happy Birthday Michael’Happy Birthday to yo-? the levity of the ceremony is shattered by the sound of broken glass and muffled sobs. A drunken man stands over his wife, a broken bottle of cheap whiskey in his hand, the remains of it are scattered on the floor in a condemning pattern around her body. She struggles to rise to her knees but is mockingly kicked back to the floor. The stunned party guests make no moves, not sure of what to do or what to say. The man with the party hat, sitting at the head of the table in mid form to blow out the candles is not as shocked as the child he represents is. He is angry, wants to lash out and make it all go away. Maybe it would not escalate into the brutal scene awaiting the family 6 years from now. All he does is watch, however, watch and weep? Cold seat flies from his brow as he springs from his slumber, Micahel looks from left to right trying to gain his bearings as his eyes adjust to the darkness. “Another dream’that’s all, another fucking dream, the same damn one every single damn time.?[/i] Michael gets out of bed and goes to the mini fridge, retrieving a can of Guiness while grabbing a mug off the dresser. He pops the top and pours the dark liquid into its decanter. At the same time he twists off the top of a pill bottle and swallows a few. Turning back to the beer he, without hesitation, chugs it down, hoping to gain some respite from the pain in his head. Sleep rushes back upon him as he falls into bed. [i]?6 years later’dreaming again of course’once again I’m my current age despite the agedness of this played out scene. My father sleeps in his living room easy chair while my mother works to pick up the pieces from another of his episodes. Shattered glass and spilled whiskey stains the walls, dripping down the stove, the refridgerator and all the kitchen furniture. There is a small cut on Melissa’s brow, an errant piece of glass perhaps, maybe a blow to the head’its not readily known at this point. Michael walks in the back door and looks around’shaking his head.” Michael - “Again” What did he do this time mom?” Melissa - “Its okay Michael’your father is very upset’he lost his job today. I shouldn’t have made him talk about it.” Michael - “That’s bullshit Mom!” Melissa - “Do not talk like that to me’damnit, your father is waking up” An ominous, forboding message to be heard in his current state. Sebastian stirs form his drunken sleep’an angry scowl on his face. He pulls himself from the chair and walks over to get a drink from the fridge. He grabs a beer but his wife puts a hand on his wrist. Melissa - “Please, Sebastian’no more tonight’you’ve had enough?” The next few moments are always a blur, no matter how many times they are replayed. Sebastian’s face mangles emotionally into a scowl. He spins and backhands his wife, sending her sprawling and into the glass dining room table. Glass shatters and blood sprays showering the room in a hideous shade of red. Sebastian makes no move to do anything, only reacting with a familiar smirk, one that would be passed on to his son. Michael - “Nooooo! Mom!!!” In total reaction mode, Michael shoves his father into the stove and grabs a knife from the counter, driving it deep into his father. Sebastian gurgles, a bit of bloody spittle forming on his lips. He grabs at his son’s throat but cannot muster enough force to do much of anything. Michael’s face is without emotion as he forces the blade deeper into his father. The sound of sirens fill the air, as a neighbor must have called the cops’again? The dream fades as the police rush into the house to find young Michael standing in the middle of a horrowshow?[/i] The scene fades as DeathSun wakes up once again?
