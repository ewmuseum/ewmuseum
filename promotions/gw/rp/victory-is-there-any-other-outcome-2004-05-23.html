Victory. Is there any other outcome?

23 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/05/04 [Narrator] Rich Sherwin has always been one to show confidence when it can be displayed, especially when he's confident that something is going to go according to plan for once. Despite his reservations over what could happen against Max Roswell on Evolution, Sherwin is confident that a victory for him is going to be the only possible outcome. Having defeated the said individual twice already, neither myself nor Sherwin feel that there is any possibiliy of a third time being a stroke of bad luck. Given the current form of the recently crowned Champion, is there any possible way that something terrible could happen? Any possible concept that would deny Sherwin his apparent 'God given right' to get through at least his FIRST defense? The scene for today is, for once, not the home of Rich Sherwin. Unfortunately, since the last time we were able to watch the Champ in his own living environment, something bad happened and for the next couple of weeks it's going to be off limits. I can't say why or what the problem actually is, but needless to say... it's something that is preventing him from going home and us from spying on him there. Regardless, back to the scene at hand, we find ourselves situated in what appears to be a red Honda Civic vehicle. We're unable to see any of the finer points of this car but from the interior, it doesn't look all that bad. Remaining still, we see Ryan Moore in the drivers seat of the vehicle, Rich Sherwin taking the passengers seat for what could be the first time in a long while. The two athletes have their eyes and ears pinned on Evolution for two reasons... the first being the title match and the second being the contender match, to see exactly who could press forth and challenge for the strap next week. Needless to say, loss against Maxwell would nullify the reason to see who wins the contenders match. [/Narrator] '... Are you going to tell me that you're still overly confident of coming out of this match against Roswell with nothing less than a victory?' Ryan asked, a smile spread across his face. For some reason, he was finding it very hard to believe that Sherwin was buzzing with nothing but confidence at the moment. He'd seen Rich in hollow states of confidence before, a buzz of victory rushing around the place until the last moment. The other member of PCG turned to look at Rich, who did nothing more than look infront of himself, a relaxed and calm expression spread happily across his own face. 'Ryan, in the time since I picked up this Championship for the first time, there was something that I realised... something that I noticed about my matches here with Global Wrestling. Since my loss to Arik Idol in the beginning, I've constantly had to strive to better myself... constantly strive to come out of something on top and claim a prize. You remember my entry in that big Rumble for a shot at the World Title, don't you?' Sherwin said, now turning to look at Ryan. Moore nodded in response, allowing Sherwin to continue, '... Well, when I finished fifth, I had some idea of exactly how far up the ladder I had run in that very short span of time. I had this idea that if I finished fifth on my first ever run against the roster, every week should be taken in a lighter manner and every match should be fought to win... nothing more and nothing less. Sure, that loss to JC Diamond wasn't the greatest thing in the world, but he tested positive for some banned substance, so the loss didn't seem all that important, you know?' he finished, chuckling at the insignificance he was looking at the JC loss with. 'Rich, I know all about you and I know everything that you do to try and avoid the whole point of this stuff. Never before have you really had the confidence needed for some major Champion. I mean, you can talk the talk and you can walk the walk... but everything seems to come at a price for you. I mean, just look at the way that your knees turned to jello right before that huge bout at Wrestlecade? You looked like you thought you were going to be the first person eliminated... and then you used that ultimate move against JC Diamond, neglecting to use any more of those awesome techniques after the first elimination.' Ryan said, throwing his hands into the air in one of those, 'What happened?' motions. His points were all correct and true, Sherwin neglecting to use the most awesome array of attack for the rest of that Wrestlecade contest. Even in the contest to determine the first TV Champion, Sherwin didn't use any of the moves that could have made his victory come about that much quicker. Rich turned away and looked ahead of himself again for a few moments, seemingly gazing into the distance to see what help could be offered from the outside. He brushed his tongue across his teeth, looking up at the roof of the car before giving an answer of the sorts. 'Ryan, the way that JC Diamond was completely ruined after that Kinniku-Buster was enough to show me that I should only be using these moves when I REALLY need to use them. Did you see the way that his body was all twisted and bent out of shape afterwards? The impact that comes with that technique is enough to make somebody look like they were involved in a really bad car accident. These opponents may need everything to be thrown at them to take a victory but if I wanted to go about really hurting people, don't you think I would have just joined some illegal fighting ring or something?' he stated, looking to Ryan with a sympathetic look spread across his face. Moore could see that Sherwin wanted to win as much as he wanted to see Sherwin win, but he could also see that Rich wasn't prepared to go too far to take a victory. Beat the opponent down, he'd do in a heartbeat... but execute moves on a regular basis that could destory or kill a career... it wasn't a favourable outcome from decisions. '... With that said,' Ryan began, turning away from Sherwin and looking out of his side-window, 'Exactly how do you really expect to defeat Roswell now your Championship IS something that's on the line? As the stakes have been raised considerably and Roswell has the whole motivation of becoming a double Champion, do you really think that the same old moves and the same, usual form of combat is going to cut the mustard? It's been three months since you started working here and you've already faced Roswell twice. To say that he isn't going to be expecting a level of your offense is an idea best left behind. Rich, I may sound harsh... but despite my background and my career with the music industry, I know exactly how it's going to be going against somebody who knows almost everything in your basic arsenal. I mean, do you think I enjoyed any success in the music industry because I kept throwing the same, tired product to the people?' Rich shook his head from side to side in response, knowing that the same tired product was not the way to get a little life and continue to get the support of the fans. Ryan took a few moments to think about his words before continuing, '... Even though music doesn't have the physical attacks that are suffered in professional wrestling and professional fighting, the whole idea of changing yourself to mystify and confuse your opponent is going to be there. You don't want scouting to become something of the norm, do you? You don't want to fight people week in and out knowing that they're always going to be that bit closer to countering every single element of your attack.' Ryan's words were scathing and sent shockwaves through the mental barrier of Rich. The Champion knew that he was still one of the best and his confidence level was still at something of a high... but the thoughts and ideas that came with potentially being completely pwned by using the same level of offense, was something that he didn't want to think about. '... What about if I just change my level of basic offense from contest to contest? You know, like start to mix and match with a few of my styles a little more?' Sherwin suggested, hoping that he'd still be able to refrain from using some of his more devestating techniques... especially the one's that had been taught to him by the travelling Korean. '... Rich, what's the point of having this devestating array of moves if you're never going to use half of them? What's the point of being able to crush almost every opponent with the flick of a wrist if you're worrying about how much damage you're going to cause? You have the strength and you have the power to take out some of the strongest people around. People like you, you're not made to worry about how much damage you're going to do to somebody inside that fighting arena... they have medics and other people who can do that. The only thing you have to worry about is keeping hold of that title.' Ryan barked to his team-mate, leaving him confused as to what really happened in the eighteen months that the two were out of contact? 'In the time before now, you were so very different.' Rich began, turning to look at the almost angry expression that had captivated the face of his partner and friend. '... In the time before our recent reunion, you always seemed to be the one that was happy with anything and everything. You didn't have these ideas of crushing everyone for reason x, y or z... you were just happy with being you and despite the trouble that seemed to come our way, I was happy with the route we were taking. Now though, it feels as though you've been lying to me about where you've been for the last eighteen months or so. The unfound aggression and this need to watch people crushed by the strongest... this isn't the Ryan Moore that I left behind at the end of our days together in PCW. Now, you may think that I'm only going to get weaker and weaker using the same kind of plan against the opposition but as far as I'm concerned, until the time comes where somebody is able to completely tackle me move for move, I'm not going to have to worry about using the trio of tricks that I have saved up my sleeve. You need to understand that whilst I fight for victory... I don't fight to injure, not in this side of the field I don't.' Sherwin responded, his face losing it's grin and sparkling expression. It didn't look as though he'd dropped the level of confidence in his mind, but his mood had certainly changed from being bouncy to being rather defensive. Lacking the spark that he had when the conversation about Evolution started, Rich decided to remove himself from the car and get a little air. '... Look,' Sherwin started, opening his door and turning to Ryan, '... I can understand and appreciate the fact that you're wanting me to reach my absolute potential here in GW but bullying me into fighting my matches the way that you want them to be fought, it's never going to happen.' Undoing his seatbeat and getting out of the vehicle, Ryan had to act quickly and try to say something that would counter what his friend stated. '... but Rich, is this now your way of telling me that you're not going to come to the gym to finish your training for Evolution?' 'I'll be there... just a little later than I should be, of course. To be completely honest with you, your complete desire to win has kinda dampened travelling in the same car as you. I'll probably walk or catch a bus there... something that'll allow me to clear my mind and focus on the more important issue... defeating the corporate doggie, Max Roswell.' Rich responded, closing the door of the Honda before turning and walking away, his hands dipped into the pockets of his jeans. 'When will you truely appreciate the drive for what it's worth, friend? I changed because you needed somebody worthwhile in your corner... not some kind of joker from the lower scale of society.' Ryan said to himself, turning the key in the ignition of his car and preparing to drive off. Both Sherwin and Moore went their seperate ways as the scene finally began to fade to darkness. [Narrator] A difference of opinion is always going to lead to somebody walking one way and somebody else walking another. Rich and Ryan, desipte their friendship, still cannot see the differences that each individual has when it comes to fighting in the ring... or fighting in general. They each have their own ideals for battle... and both wish to have their own ideas shown when it comes to fight-time. Sherwin wants to defeat the opponent and sustain his own level of honor, not stooping to levels where he could damage people permanently with some of his strongest moves. Moore though, he wants to crush people... he wants to send messages that his friend is not somebody to be messed with. In his mind, if you have the power to do something, then you shouldn't have to think twice about doing it. Everything should be done if the option is there. With another period in the life of this hero coming to a close, we can only wonder what actually happened to Ryan Moore in his time away from Sherwin and professional wrestling. As far as the eye can see, he released quite a few tracks and such... but beneath the sugar-coated topping there is a truth that not even the front-office of Global Wrestling truely know about. [/Narrator]
