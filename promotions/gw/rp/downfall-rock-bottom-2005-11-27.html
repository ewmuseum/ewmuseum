Downfall: Rock Bottom

27 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/27/2005 Like a submarine surfacing through choppy waters, Air Canada’s flight 413 cut slowly up through the thick cloud cover, trailing streamers of wispy cloud like silk ribbons from it’s wings. The winter sun shone strong above the clouds, bright light gleaming like silver off the plane’s metal body. “It’s almost like another world up here?” Dawn whispered at the sight, one hand pressed to the glass of her window, her face hovering only inches from the window pane. The unhampered light from the sun reflected emerald green in her wide eyes, eyes that stared back at her from the window in wide eyed wonderment. From his place next to her, Toshiro Mifune chuckled, rubbing one hand through the black and grey hair that formed a dignified beard and mustache. “You would think she’d never flown before, the way she carries on neh?” From her place across from him, Alexis couldn’t help but giggle in agreement, having never seen the normally dour Dawn so transfixed by anything outside of Lia. “It’s like flying with a little girl.” She agreed. Dawn looked back over one shoulder and stuck her tongue out at Alex, pulling down the bottom of her eyelid with one finger as she did. “Hey cut me some slack, it’s been a long time since I flew anywhere. I normally stay as far away from planes as I can manage.” “Oh?” Mifune raised one eyebrow, “Have you developed a sudden fear of heights?” At that Dawn sat back in her chair, looking sidelong at her master with a wry expression. “Master you’ve seen me jump headlong off the top of a cage’do you think I’d be afraid of heights after all that?” Mifune smiled good naturedly, shrugging his broad shoulders. “Stranger things have happened in the sport. And jumping from fifteen feet in the air is vastly different from looking down at the very surface of the earth from above the clouds. To fly so high in the light’one would almost not wish to come down again.” Dawn nodded, leaning her head back against the comfortable seat and closing her aching eyes. “It’s not the flying I object to Master’it’s dealing with the airlines that I find intolerable.” She cracked one eye open again, smiling at Mifune impishly. “I also don’t normally get to fly first class on the odd occasion that I do fly.” At that her master scratched the back of his head in embarrassment, grinning sheepishly at his one time student and her guest. “When you are as old as I am, and have done so much the hard way, you learn to appreciate the chance to relax from time to time. Besides’it’s not often I get to leave Japan anymore.” “Thank?” Dawn’s voice caught in her throat and she swallowed hard, trying to force the sounds out. “Thank you for coming to see me Master’I’ve fallen far since we last spoke.” Mifune patted Dawn’s hand gently with his own, shaking his grizzled head slightly as he did so. “Only in your own mind Little Dragon, you have done much worthy of your training since you returned to America. You are the first and only Mistress of the Mat are you not” Would you shame that title by claiming it is worth nothing?” “N-no Master, but I failed to capture the World title when I had the chance’I missed my opportunity?” “Fah!” Mifune exclaimed suddenly, tossing up one hand as though exasperated. “And you think you are the only one to have ever failed a title shot hm” You think you are the only one who has had to pick themselves up and try again? I can personally recall no less then half a dozen lost opportunities myself and I am an old man and have likely forgotten more then a dozen more! Does that make me a failure hm? Do my years of wrestling mean nothing because I missed a few shots?” Dawn flushed at the rebuke, trying to stammer out a retort. “No Master, but I?” ??hold myself to a higher standard then I hold everyone else.” Alex finished for her, smiling shyly from her seat. “That’s not what I was going to say!” “No, but it’s the truth is it not?” Mifune asked, showing white teeth through his beard as he grinned at both women. “You have always held yourself to a standard that is higher then those around you Dawn’I saw that about you the first day you came to my academy.” “It’s not that?” Dawn ducked her head, frustration staining her words. ??I just expect better from myself then I keep doing. I know I can do better?” “Perhaps you are simply not learning fast enough.” Dawn’s head came up in surprise, “Master?” Mifune held up both hands before her, indicating his right. “In my right hand, I hold your triumph at Masters of the Mat 2005. In my left, I hold your loss at Whiplash to a strong champion’which occupies your mind more?” Dawn flushed again, her lips pressed into a small line. “Whiplash.” “And why is that” Are you seeing lessons you could learn? Remembering openings you should have taken, or moves you could have used?...Or are you simply remembering that you lost?” Dawn pressed her eyes closed, a slight sigh escaping her lips. “She’s almost a half a foot shorter then I am?” “Ha!” Mifune barked out a small laugh, grinning at her again. “As I recall Luis-Sensei is at least three inches shorter then you are Little Dragon, shall I tell him what you think of smaller wrestlers hm?” “No! I didn’t mean it like that I just’I feel like’I know I should have won, I had every advantage and I blew it?” She hung her head, rubbing her temples softly. “And you will blow it again, likely many times before you are my age’but you will learn!” Mifune’s voice snapped like a whip and Dawn’s head came up again, staring at him with wide eyes. “Your technique is not the problem here Dawn, your attitude toward yourself is. But if I could teach a skinny Canadian girl to be a Dragon?” Dawn flushed, mumbling something in embarrassment. ??then I can teach a Dragon to learn from her mistakes. Unless you think you are too good to learn from an old man like me.” “No!” Dawn flushed crimson at the thought. “Never master, never! You’re like’like a father to me?” Mifune squeezed Dawn’s upper arm firmly, nodding just as firmly as he did. “Good. Then once we land I will try to train some of the Dragon out of you again.” Dawn sniffed, feeling eyes start to burn. “I’m sorry Master’for everything?” “Hush.” Mifune squeezed her hand, his voice soft. “Don’t cry Dawn, Dragon’s don’t cry.” Alex reached over to pat Dawn’s leg awkwardly, smiling shyly. “It’ll all work out Dawn, once we get back to Japan we’ll fix everything up.” Dawn found her voice was gone, and all she could do was nod mutely, squeezing Alex’s hand in response. “You should get some rest Little Dragon,” Mifune said, releasing her other hand. “Nap a little before I teach you how to really sleep again’and before training begins in earnest.” Leaning her head back against the seat, Dawn let her aching eyes ease shut once again. The humming of the planes engine made a pleasant background sound, and she could faintly make out Mifune’s familiar scent of sandalwood and pine’As her exhausted mind began to drift toward sleep, she heard her master speak again. “So Miss Alexis, how long have you been staying with my student?” ********************************************************** Everything had been packed, the dogs had been hugged and notes had been left should either Virgil or Lia return’all that remained was to say goodbye, and that was the part that Dawn hated the most. She stood on her own doorstep, bags by her feet and looked mournfully up at Caitlin as she stood in the doorway. “C?” She started, her voice soft. “Hush sugar’there’s nothin” ya need to be sayin? ta me right now. You go with your master, and you come back when you’re better and see me again.” Caitlin smiled, fighting back her own discomfort at saying goodbye to Dawn for what felt like the hundredth time. “Thank you C, for everything’I know we havn’t talked much and?” Caitlin stepped up and wrapped her strong arms around Dawn, squeezing her tightly. “Ah told you, we don’t need ta talk about that right now. Once you’re better we can chat again okay?” Dawn nodded, holding Caitlin tightly. “Okay.” Caitlin leaned up to Dawn’s ear, whispering fiercely. “Just because we stopped sleepin” together, doesn’t mean Ah stopped lovin? you Dawnie.” She kissed Dawn on the cheek and took a step back, forcing a jaunty smile onto her face. “Now you go off with yer Master’Ah’ll take care o” yer woman’s dogs until you get back.” Dawn nodded firmly, not trusting her own voice, and then picked up her bags and loaded herself into the car. As Mifune pulled away from the Estate she looked back at Caitlin, who waved jauntily from the doorway with Lia’s Hellhounds standing on either side of her like a pair of enormous bookends. “Gods?” She whispered to herself as she waved back. “I hope I’m doing the right thing.” *********************************************************************** A hand on her shoulder startled Dawn awake, “Huh” Wha?” Alexis grinned at her, gesturing to the front of the plane. “They just turned on the seatbelt sign, we’re touching down in Japan very shortly.” Dawn blinked several times in surprise, looking around as she tried to clear the fog from her eyes. “We are” How long have I been asleep for?” “Not quite long enough.” Mifune remarked as he slid back into his seat. “Your mind is exhausted, even if your body is not. But you will have plenty of time to sleep once we get to the grounds, until then you’re going to have to bear with me just a little longer.” He grinned at her and fastened his own seatbelt. “How are we getting to the grounds Master” Taxi?” “Fah. I’m too old to rely on such things; I had them send a car.” He grinned, sharing a mischievous smile with Alexis. Dawn looked back and forth between them, bewilderment clear on her face. “What” What’s so funny?” “You’ll see Little Dragon, you’ll see.” They rode out the remainder of the short flight in silence. Alex and Mifune shared the occasional amused look, while Dawn nervously chewed the lower half of her lip. Although the promise of a surprise intrigued Dawn, she was more concerned with going back into the grounds after so long’Japan had never been a familiar place for her, and her height and hair colour made her stand out like a sore thumb’something she was painfully aware of under even the best circumstances. The plane touched down without incident and the trio filed off to pick up their luggage, each pleased to see that their luggage had safely made the flight with them. From there Mifune led them out the front of the airport, looking around until he located the vehicle he was seeking. A black SUV was parked off to one side of the lot, the brightly coloured Kanji that decorated the sides and windows translated to: Mifune School of Wrestling Excellence. As they approached the vehicle the drivers side door opened, releasing an extremely tall African American man from its metal insides. Dawn’s eyes widened and her pace quickened as he turned around, white teeth showing in a broad smile. “It’s been a long time Dawn!” He called in a rumbling bass. “Michi!” That was as close to a girlish shriek as Dawn McFarlane ever got, and she dropped her luggage to the ground and sprinted the rest of the distance to the SUV. Michi Noketsuna came around the front of the car and caught Dawn as she leapt into his arms, crushing her too him with a bearhug that reduced most mean to tears when applied in the ring. His 6’5?, 275 pound frame easily hefted the Steel Rose like she was weightless, crushing her against his massive chest. “SO you do remember me then.” He said with a smile. “Baka!(Stupid!)? She exclaimed, swatting him on one of his massive shoulders. “How could I forget” You teased me the whole time I was here!” Michi grinned as he set Dawn gently back on her feet, folding his arms across his massive chest. “Well somebody had to keep you on your toes you know.” Dawn smiled, squeezing his forearm happily. “I thought you moved back to America?” “Nah’after I graduated, Master Mifune gave me the opportunity to stay on here and be an instructor at the Academy’as well as continue my career here. How could I refuse?” “You couldn’t!” Mifune retorted as he arrived at the SUV, chuckling. “That’s why I made you the offer!” Michi chuckled, looking over Mifune’s shoulder and bowing low from the waste to Alex. “Konnichiwa Sakura (Cherry blossom), Daijobu?” Alex smiled shyly, looking a little confused. “I’m sorry, I don’t speak Japanese.” “Ah no worries. I was just asking after your plane trip,” He said with a wink, “Please let me take your bags.” Dawn turned to Mifune, smiling broadly. “This is quite the surprise Master.” “I had hoped it would be. Come now, we need to load up the SUV, then we have plenty of catching up to do when we return to the grounds.”
