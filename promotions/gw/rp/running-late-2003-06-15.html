Running Late

15 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/15 There I am, in the middle of the ring in front of millions of screaming fans all cheering my name. My opponent? Global Wrestling’s World Champion, Brick. We’ve been in here battling it out for the past thirty minutes to decide which of us will walk away with the title. It’s been a good match so far, I’ve been spectacular as usual and to his credit, Brick brought his “A” game as well. Slowly, we circle each other like two lions moving in for the kill, each just moments away from pouncing. We lock up and due to his strength advantage Brick gains the advantage and sends me flying toward the ropes. He goes for a big boot, his first mistake of this match. Seeing this I drop down and slide on my back right between his legs tripping him along the way and sending him face first into the mat. While holding onto his right leg I flip backwards and apply a half crab. He writhes in agony as I c attempt to compress his vertebrae. Soon enough he begins swatting at me with his arms, his last mistake. I catch his left arm then apply an armber and really wrench back as I yell “Game over old man”. Brick is now trapped within my Nightmare Circular in the middle of the ring with nowhere to go and no possible means of escape. I tighten my holds even more using every ounce of strength I’ve got. There is a repetitive thud on the mat, Brick is tapping. “I can’t take it anymore! I quit! I quit!” he exclaims as the ref signals for the bell. Although I’ve already won I continue to stretch Brick for a while longer before letting go. I relinquish my hold then get down in Brick’s contorted face and whisper to him “Looks like you’ve run out of lives.” before kicking him in the ribs repeatedly until he falls out of the ring. Now I would finally hear the sound I’ve awaited throughout the entire match. “The winner of this match via submission, and your new Global Wrestling World Champion, Deacon Holmes!” booms over the PA system causing the entire crowd to burst into applause. I go to grab the title and as soon as I touch it a long high pitched sound blares throughout the arena. Suddenly I’m awakened and find myself sprawled out in my bed, extremely disappointed. The only thing real about the dream was that piercing noise I heard, my alarm clock, which is still going off in earsplitting fashion. I reach my arm over to the night stand and clumsily feel around the top of it until if finally shuts off. Then I roll over to find that a rather attractive woman spent the night with me, although I don’t recall her name. Probably met her down at the Darkhorse Saloon since I’ve been in Texas most of the week. I roll over the other way only to be faced by the bright digital display of my alarm clock. It reads “2:05.” “2:05!” Fuck!” I exclaim as I slip out of bed and into my hangover. The entire room feels like it’s spinning on a pivot as I make my way to the bathroom while rubbing the sleep from my eyes. When I stand still I feel like I’m at the edge of a diving board on top of the Sears Tower. Walking across the hardwood floors feels more like walking on water but finally I make it. Hunched over in front of the toilet I devote the next fifteen minutes of my life to emptying my soul out into the toilet one stomach churning heave at a time. By the time I am finished my stomach is extremely sore and I feel as though I just had all of my internal organs removed. Slowly, I rise back to my feet from my previously semi prone position and flush the toilet. A look in the mirror reveals my haggard appearance. I rub my bloodshot aggravated eyes in an attempt to correct my blurred vision but to no avail. Still half asleep I begin brushing my teeth, hygiene is very important to me, I also want to get the taste of tequila off my tongue. After a vigorous scrub I run some hot water and wash my face. I’m officially awake, and suffering from a mind numbing headache. I feel like Quartz is following me around with a sledgehammer attempting to drive me into the ground like a railroad spike. My head feels like a piece of damp wood being split in two by a lumberjack’s axe but I press on. I’m already running late, I was supposed to meet Tyler and Sean down at the gym thirty minutes ago. I run back into my room and grab a pair of And1 basketball shorts off the floor to wear. I then grab a tanktop out of the oak drawer of my dresser and leave putting on my shoes as I walk out into the living room. Unconscious bodies are strewn about my furniture and floor in random fashion along with empty alcohol containers. “Looks like I’m not the only one who had a good time.” I say to myself grabbing my keys on the way to my garage. “Hmm, which car to take today” I wonder aloud while mulling over my options. Usually I’d bring out my custom Escalade but since I’m in a bind today I decide to take my new Porsche Carrera GT Turbo. With a simple press of a button on my keychain I start the car, drop the top, the driver side door pops open, and bass from the trunk begins to rattle the windows of all the other cars in my garage as well as my aching head. I hop in and immediately turn the music down as having “Hands In The Air” By 8-Ball isn’t exactly helping my situation. Another button press on my keychain opens my garage door to reveal that it’s a beautiful day outside. Everything looks perfect, the grass is bright green, the sun is shining, and there’s a slight breeze. Too bad I have to feel like absolute shit, and I’ll probably feel worse when I get back. Just as I’m about to pull out of the garage my phone rings. It’s Sean and Tyler wondering where I’m at. I assure them that I’ll be there soon and begin my trip. The gym is thirty minutes away, I’ll be there in ten... I smash the gas pedal with my foot and am almost pressed through my seat as the car roars out of the garage and down my driveway. My tires screech as I turn onto the main street shifting to second gear. By the time I make it to the highway I’m already going too fast to make out what the speed limit signs read, and I don’t plan on slowing down anytime soon... in or out of the ring.
