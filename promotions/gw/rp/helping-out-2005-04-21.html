Helping Out

21 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/21/05 It's a cold night and you see a man walking in a parking lot wearing a hooded gray jacket. The bright red hair makes his identity unmistakable as he passes parks car as a man approaches him. The man seems to be dressed in clothes rejected by Good Will. 'Can you spare some change?' I look at the man for a moment and as instant sympathy for him. The man before him has nothing and has to shallow whatever pride he once had to ask for some loose change.If the roles were change would someone the ego that a pro wrestler can carry be able to do the same? Doing what you need to survive on the street is much different then what you do survive in the ring. I am always willing to help someone out he reaches in his pocket and grabs his wallet. He pulls out a 20 and hands it to the man. 'Here you go sir' The bum is shocked at the generosity of the man in front of him. 'Thank You' He said with an enthusiastic tone as if this was his lottery. 'Anytime Sir' The bum continues to ask for money as I leave him. I head inside K-Mart I head to the back of store to the electronics department. Thankfully not many people are in the store so I don't have to worry about being stop every two seconds for autograph. Not that I mind really but I'm not in the mood. Besides who would want an autograph from an average wrestler like me? Even if I did win last week I am still merely average 1 and 1 is nothing to brag about. Now I am going up against someone undefeated on Evolution and a former cruiserweight champion in a 3 way dance. I want to be the hero but I'm simply not in a position to do that.. I'm one of the WMW spies backstage and it will probably be that way for a long time. If it weren't for Josh I'd be laid out by those Druids. I look around and see a kid trying to put a heavy table on a flatbed and the customer not lifting a finger to help him. I take a small detour from my shopping and walk up to the kid and grab the other end and set it on the flat bed. The kid looks at him and says 'Thanks man I needed the help' 'Its no problem' The kid leaves pulling the flatbed following the customers. I head back to the electronics department. I know the kid did not recognize me because even though I am ordinary there would have been at least an odd look on his face. I stroll through the cds looking for a certain one. I stop at the R's and scan through the cds. They don't have The Ramones stupid K-Mart. I walk out of the department slowly making my way back outside. I exit the store and looks around for the bum who is long gone. I decide instead of going home I'll take a little midnight stroll. It probably wasn't the safest or smartest idea I've had but those words never really did describe me. I cut threw the parking lot and head down the sidewalk. The cold dark surroundings have been a lot like my career lately. Seemingly no hope for success but there is a light at the end of the tunnel you're striving for. The moon is full tonight so maybe that means I have a right to be hopeful about my GW Career? Do I have the right to be confident after last week after all I am only one and one. I have some goals set for Global Wrestling and no exactly who I want to go after but I should keep my thoughts to myself until I am no longer the WMW spy. I lean up against a telephone pole and close my eyes for a moment. I wonder if there can be a better way. Did I make the correct move in jumping? WMW would never have me back and GW doesn't completely tust me. I need to do something to change popular opinion but what? If anything right now I'd be the guy letting GW in the WMW arena. Doesn't matter if I have to be the loner then so be it.. I don't need anyone's help to be a success. 'HELP!' I hear the shout and I begin to look around. 'HELP! SOMEONE!' I turn around and parking lot of a closing Good Will I see a woman struggling with a man. I dart across the street running as fast as I can. The woman attempts to mase him but its knocked out of her hand. 'HELP!' He starts to tare off her jacket but I grab him and spin him around. I connect with a hey maker with my left hand. He stumbles away from the woman as she grabs her mase connected to her key chain and quickly opens her car door. She hurries in and speed out of the parking lot. The man looks at me and looks ready to attack. He thinks for a moment but I go for a Freddy Kick and the man drops to the ground before I connect. He bolts away from me thinking better of the situation and maybe knowing what I do for a living. I give chase to the man not wanting him to walk away free for an attempted rape. He has quite a bit of ground on me but I won't give up. I'm catching up as I chase him down the black. I give it one last ditch effort and lunge at the man...... I fall just short and he dashes away from me. Yet again I blow the chance to be the hero. Chill, Scott Vyper, my rival Dante could they all be right? Am I stupid for going in over my head? Wrestling Midwest? Global Wrestling? GTT4? CW2? Should I just give up? Am I really just ordinary?
