Chapter One: Beyond Belief? (PT. 2)

28 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Chapter One: Beyond Belief? (PT. 2) Leviathan Global Wrestling Promo #38 Date: March 28th, 2003 Location: Blagoveshensk, Russia The Book Of Judgment Chapter One: Beyond Belief? Date: February 28th, 1980 Location: Blagoveshensk, Russia The news station cuts to a commercial break as the producer walks over to Nikita and Vladimir, with a smile across his face. 'Wasn't that great! Our rating are great so far, plus we have had eight phone calls to the station with in the past two minutes. This is exactly what our station needed!' the producer says with great joy. 'Is this all set up Victor, is this all a huge hoax to boost ratings, because if it is...' Vladimir is cut off by Victor. 'That's the beauty of it all, this wasn't set up at all, it is all real as far as we know. Plus, there is a second video to air when we come back on air! Finally, we can beat channel four!' Victor says, as he walks off the stage as the staff gets ready to go back on air. 'Have fun you guys! I'll be in my office if anyone needs me!' Victor says with a laugh. 'Welcome back! If you are just joining us, we have breaking news of what some may call Judgment Day! This next video is not for children or the squeamish to see, so if you fall under that category, change the channel now!' Nikita says. The camera view is quite odd, as it is high in the sky, from which seems to be shot on the rooftops. What is even more weird about it, is that the date in the bottom left hand corner of the screen reads February 28th, 1980. The view is shot downward, and cars are seen driving up and down the busy street as the snow flurries rapidly. The view slowly moves horizontal, and turns around, and the person who is filming is on the ledge of a building, at least six stories high, with about three stories above the person. The person who is filming, looks to his side, and beside him is the large cloak wearing figure from the previous video. 'Those who are none believers are in for a big surprise. Judgment Day is upon us!' Leviathan screams! He raises his arm and smashes the window in which he is standing beside. Leviathan punches the jagged glass pieces which remain in the frame out of his way. He pushes the purple drapes aside and steps through the frame into the dimly lit room, to find Victor, the producer inside. Victor runs for the door, and when he reaches it, grabs the handle and desperately turns it. But to no avail, the door doesn't open. Leviathan slowly walks towards Victor slowly as he desperately kicks the door. Leviathan reaches out, and grabs Victor by the shoulder, twists his body around and slams him back first into the door. He grabs Victor by his neck with one hand, and lifts him off the ground. He looks back at the camera, his face still hidden in the shadow of his hood. 'Beyond belief? I think not!' Leviathan screams. Leviathan squeezes his hand as Victor's facial expression becomes one of sheer horror as blood start to flow gently from his mouth. A crack is heard, and Leviathan drops Victors body to the ground. The prone body lay there as a blood puddle slowly develops on the tile floor. Leviathan again looks into the camera, and pulls his hood back, revealing a face of complete darkness. 'Believe it...or not' Leviathan says, as he grabs the camera, and crushes it between his palms, revealing a static scene on the television. Banging on the door to the office is heard, and twisting of the door handle. The door finally opens as Nikita and Vladimir enter the room with at least six other staff members. Victor's body is beside the doorway in a fetal position. Nikita starts to cry as Vladimir holds onto her and takes her out of the room. A staff member walks over to the window, pulls back the purple drapes, and looks out, with no sign of Leviathan around. He turns around to see the rest of the staff members staring at the wall to his left. He slowly looks over, to see 'Judgment Day - March 5th, 1980' written in Victors blood on the white wall. ***Too Be Continued***
