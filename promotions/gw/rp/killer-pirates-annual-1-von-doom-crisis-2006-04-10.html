Killer Pirates Annual #1: VON DOOM CRISIS~!

10 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/10/06 Killer Pirates Annual #1: VON DOOM CRISIS~! Last week... Darth von Doom and the Killer Pirates formed an Unholy Alliance after the Killer Klown's face was burned with acid and Darth von Doom killed the Horny Mathematician after revealing that he was the leader of the Ninja Army. The Circus of DOOM~! was all but destroyed, with Squideon being the lone known survivor. Sea Man and Swordman were captured by the Doppelgangers of Adam, Matt, James, and Cave Hulk while searching for our Earth's Cave Hulk and his arch-nemesis Chameleon. The Doppelgangers are out to remake their Earths and destroy ours after the Devourer of Worlds ate their universes and dimensions. Alien warriors are coming to Earth to destroy us. Morgan won in the first round of the Total J Cup Tournament and the Killer Pirates defeated Los Cazadores in a Walk the Plank Scaffold match on Fusion. Now, these are the continued stories of all things von Doom. These are the stories of our beloved anti-heroes... Morgan, the Dark Pirate Dark Harlequin, the Killer Klown Darth von Doom, the Dark Lord of the Sith These are the stories of the Unholy Alliance. - - - - - - - - - - - - The Battle for the Universe~! The Unholy Alliance has been formed. Darth von Doom and the Killer Pirates are now allied for a common cause. But that is not the only problem in this universe. Doppelgangers from other dimensions have arrived and are trying to destroy the Earth! But no, wait. It gets even better. Dozens of alien warriors have arrvied on Earth at the Hills of Satan to get revenge on the Devourer of Worlds and those who created him, after their planets were eaten by the creature. This is where we join Porno Yoda and his Coalition of the WIlling as they are running through the woods. The light is just coming through the trees at dawn. The dew drops on the leaves fly off as they run through the forest after being ambushed at the Hulk Family Reunion. The leaves fall down as the Coalition runs through the forest only to run right into the Hulk Family! 'Argh!' Porno Yoda yelps as he is knocked over by the gigantic Construction Hulk. Yoda brushes himself off and gets abck to his feet. He's a little bruised up but he's alright. It is hard when you run into someone who weighs a thousand pounds more than you when you're only 4 feet tall. 'Why, hello there friends,' Porno Yoda says with a smile. 'Are all of you okay? When the Doppelgangers attacked, we feared the worst.' 'NO. WESA ALL FINE,' Cave Hulk says with a scowl on his face. He's pissed and he has every right to be. That damn Gaylord of the Universe and Chameleon ruined his Family Reunion. The one time he was to have some peace in his miserable life was ruined because of some gay idiot and his arch-nemesis. Now, someone must pay and he hopes he finds someone other than his friends in the Coalition because he would hate to kill his friends over something stupid. 'Well, that's good to hear,' Porno Yoda replies. He then looks up and points to all the shooting stars coming down to Earth. 'Look at that,' he says with a glimmer of wonder in his eye. 'I wonder what all of those shooting stars are?' 'Suddenly, a loud rumbling is heard. The grounds shakes with such ferocity that the members of the Coalition think it's an earthquake. 'EARTHQUAKE!' Porno Yoda yells out as a bright flash of light comes toward them and then crashes into the ground with a supersonic force. They all cover their faces as dirt comes flying toward them and then, suddenly, everything stops. Once the dust settles, they all uncover their faces and look to see a giant spaceship sitting in front of them! They all then look around and see more and more spaceships landing in the general vicinity. Suddenly, the doors of the spaceship open up and something comes out from within. 'Uhhh...what in God's name is that?' the Exorcist asks with a sense of beffudlement. Out of the lights and mist, the alien appears. It is a giant shark-like barbarian creature. It snarls and exits his ship from a rampway. The beast them sees them and growls. 'You monsters did this!' he says with a tinge of evil in his voice. 'Now you all will face the United Coalition of Space Warriors and we will kill you all.' The Coalition and the Hulk Family look around and see they are surrounded by the warriors that came down from outer space. There is a being with a giant head, a Swamp Thing looking monster, an outer space warrior type, a red knight, a man that appears to be a conquistador, a space pirate, a mummy, a giant machine man, an evil warrior beast, a traveller with a bubble head, an evil demon, a barbarian warrior, a man with a machine suit on, an underwater superhero dressed like a Manta Ray, a flaming demon, a shark warrior, a giant android of doom, a giant cave troll from outer space, a cyclone creating supervillain, an ice giant, a black knight, a bird man, a cat man, a gorilla man, an evil cow from Hell, a fake superhero, and the orginal shark barbarian. They are armed and ready for battle. They aliens are about to attack when Darth von Doom shows up with the Killer Pirates (including the Dark Harlequin, who now is wearing all black and has an all black evil demonic looking clown mask with red rosy cheeks) as well as his new apprentice, Sith Bat. He's a psychotic bat man wanna-be that kills law abiding citizens and lets the criminals roam free in New York. Darth von Doom pulls out his lightsaber and ignites it. Jack pulls out his pirate sword and the clown pulls out a sledgehammer. Sith Bat has a two small knives in his hands. 'So, what's a little old meeting between friends without us?' Darth von Doom says with a sarcastic tone. 'While you idiots have been toiling away, I've been training the Pirates and the Sith Bat in my Sith Arts. Now you all will die like the already deap corpses you are.' 'You'll never kill us all von Doom!' Porno Yoda yells out. Darth von Doom chuckles. 'I didn't intend to,' he replies with a sense of glee. 'I intended for you all to kill each other but, like fools, the Hulk Family would have to join up with you and your brethren for this battle.' The shark barbarian points to Darth von Doom. 'You and your Mathematician caused all of this mess,' he yells out. Now you will die at our hands and then we will kill the Devourer of Worlds once we are finished with you.' Darth von Doom snickers at such a stupid remark. Who could kill him? He is Darth von Doom, the most unholy power in the universe! Not even his own Master could control him. Darth von Doom then senses something in the Force. A prescence he hasn't felt since... 'It's good to see you Adam and Matt,' he says as they try to sneak up behind him. 'I see you still have the lightsabers you stole from my former Master.' Adam and Matt pull out their lightsabers and ignite them. But they have someone with them. He's dressed like a Hades Corporation WARRIOR soldier. The three of them move over to where the Coalition is at. 'And I see you are as observant as ever, Darth,' Adam says with a snicker. Suddenly, they are all bum rushed by the James, Adam, and Matt Doppelgangers! James von Doom from Earth 2 tries to stop the battle before it begins. he comes from a peaceful Earth. Earth 2 had no wars and he does not this one to be his first. He goes over to Darth von Doom. 'Please...please don't do this,' he says. 'Please let us all get along and have a peace for all eternity like on my Earth.' Darth von Doom then slices him in two with his lightsaber. 'Objection noted,' he replies with a snicker. Some of the other warriors get a laugh over this but the Doppelgangers believe this to be an act of war! 'ATTACK!' James von Doom from Earth 4, the leader of the Doppelgangers, yells out and they all attack. Everyone is attacking everyone. It's complete chaos! Suddenly, more armies attack! The Furry Woodland Creatures have mobilized to protect their forest and are attacking everything that moves. The Running with Scissors Cowboy and the Illegal Alien go after Darth von Doom and the Killer Pirates for killing all of their friends on the Island of Misfit Wrestlers. The Queen of the Zombies and the Prince of Shrooms are mad that their lands are being trampled by spaceships and warriors and they attack as well. A Giant Spider attacks Porno Yoda but he kills it with his green lightsaber. Darth von Doom slices at the Queen of the Zombies, easily killing her. The Prince of Shrooms sees this and screams out 'NOOOOOOOOOO!' He then goes to attack Darth von Doom only to be shot by one of the alien warriors, killing him instantly. The Furry Woodland Creatures are getting dominated by everyone. Moose Man and Bear Man, the last surviving Circus of DOOM~! members not named Squideon go to attack the Killer Pirates but Morgan slices them up easily. A lot of the aliens are being killed too, mainly by Darth von Doom, Porno Yoda, and the Doppelgangers. Adam and Matt start fighting their Doppelgangers, easily defeating them until they have a showdown with Darth von Doom. Porno Yoda joins them. 'Ah, so it's three on one, eh?' Darth von Doom says with a hint of sarcasm. Sith Bat comes over and pulls out his lightsaber and ignites it. It's a Dual-blade lightsaber like Darth Maul had in Episode I. 'Now all of you law abiding assholes will die at my hands!' he growls out. Sith Bat attacks Adam and Matt as Darth von Doom faces off with Porno Yoda. Yoda jumps, dives, ducks, and dodges at Darth von Doom swings at him. Their mini-battle takes place with everyone else fighting around them. Horny the Bear, Smokey's Cousin, goes after the Evil Pigs but the Pig Man tackles him down and Evil Dead starts to eat him alive! As Sith Bat battles Adam and Matt, various aliens try to get involved so they temporarily ally and kill them so they can get back to fighting each other. Within minutes, everyone is dead save for a select few. The survivors are Darth von Doom, Porno Yoda, Adam, Matt, Sith Bat, the Killer Pirates, all of the Coalition and Hulk Family Alliance, the alien HEAD, the alien Sharkorian the Barbarian, the evil space demon Demonicus, the Great Android of Doom, the WARRIOR soldier guy, James von Doom from Earth 4, James von Doom, Matthew Litten, and Adam Wilson from After Earth, and Adam and Matt from Earth 3. Everyone else is deceased. The battle rages on. Sith Bat goes to attack Adam from behind but Matt cuts off his arm. 'GRRRRR!' Sith Bat snarls out only to be beheaded by Adam and Porno Yoda. Darth von Doom looks on with amusement. 'Impressive,' he says coldly. Adam and Matt from Earth 3, the Hell Earth, pull out machetes and try to decapitate Darth von Doom but he throws his lightsaber like a boomerang and cuts them in half. His lightsaber then returns to him and he inspects it. 'Just a little redder than before,' he says with an evil look on his face. Suddenly, a loud roar is heard. They all look up to the sky and see something that looks like a planet moving slowly through the sky. It's like a moving dot in the sky above them, but they don't know what it is. The aliens know what it is though and so do the Doppelgangers. 'The Devourer,' Sharkorian says aloud. They all look around at each other and decide to stop fighting. All that is, except for James for Earth 4. 'You did this!' he yells, pointing his finger at Darth von Doom. 'And now we're all going to die!' James-4 charges at Darth von Doom but the WARRIOR soldier shoots him dead. 'Well, now that we're done with this crap, can we kill the giant monster now?' the WARRIOR guy asks with a grin. They all look at him. 'Say, what's your name anyway, mate?' Morgan asks. The WARRIOR guy smiles. 'My name is Jake.' 'Jake, welcome to Hell,' Morgan says. 'Now how are we going to kill the giant monster?' Darth von Doom cackles and looks at Porno Yoda. 'The only way is to form an alliance.' Porno Yoda nods his head. 'Unfortunately, he is correct.' Darth von Doom cackles. 'I'm not that bad,' he says. 'But anyway, if we can get back to the Island of Misfit Wrestlers and get to my Death Star, then we can blow whatever it is up.' 'Good plan,' Adam-After Earth says. 'Get going. We can distract it from here.' 'But whose all going with us?' Morgan asks. Darth von Doom looks around and picks out who he wants to go with him. 'Okay, it's going to be me, Yoda, Adam, Matt, Jake, the Killer Pirates, and the Cave Hulk.' They all look at each other and nod their heads in agreement. 'I'm going too,' someone says in the background. Everyone looks and sees Chameleon, the arch-nemesis of the Cave Hulk who watched all of this unfold without doing a thing. Cave Hulk growls but Darth von Doom nods his head. 'Yeah, good idea,' he replies. They turn and march through the woods as everyone else prepares for the coming of the Devourer of Worlds! * * * An Even Unholier Alliance A few minutes later... Darth von Doom, Porno Yoda, the Killer Pirates, Adam, Matt, Jake, Cave Hulk, and Chameleon are walking through the forest toward the docks. The forest seems almost dead at the moment. And maybe it is. There was a war a couple of miles away just a few minutes beforehand. Now, everything is seemingly lifeless, like a surreal desert of the future. Then, something happens. There is movement. Darth von Doom pulls out his lightsaber. 'I sense a disturbance in the Force,' he says in an uncaring tone. Porno Yoda ignites his lightsaber. 'I sense it too.' Suddenly something burrows out from underneath the ground! Dust blows everywhere as everyone in the group takes a step back. Suddenly, some men come out from underground wearing googles. They look kind of like groundhogs. The resemblance is uncanny. The leader steps forward with his scepter made of a giant tree root. 'Behold humans!' he says in a bellowing voice. 'The Groundhog Men are on this plane and we are taking over this realm for our people! We are the real rulers of this universe, not you land dwellers! Hardy har har! Your end is now! Your time has ceased! Now it is the time of the Groundhog Men!' 'NO!' Another voice appears out of the deadness of the forest. Everyone looks in the direction of the voice and see a whole bunch of people wearing all white suits and have seemingly blank expressions on their faces. Their eyes seem hollow and empty, almost as if they had been cleansed of all emotions. Darth von Doom, as usual, seem unimpressed with what he has seen. 'People of the world,' the leader says in a mild yet thunderous voice. 'We are the Converters. We are here to convert you all to all religion so that you can all feel the prescence of 'Our Lord God' and be his servants. He is the Light and he is the Way. He is God. Join us or burn in the fiery fires of Hell!' Darth von Doom laughs and throws his lightsaber at the man, slicing him in half! The Groundhog Men quickly go back into their burrows and hide underground upon seeing that. They might seem ferocious, but they are cowards and know when not to fight. Twigs and sticks against lightsabers is not a very good fight to battle in. However, the Converters are more determined than ever. They look at their decapitated leader and then turn to the party. 'Satanic fiend!' one of them yells at Darth von Doom. 'You will feel the wrath of our God soon enough.' Darth von Doom's voicebox fills up with laughter. 'What are you? Mormons?' The Converters don't respond. They simply back up and walk away, whispering about this not being over and how they will have their revenge on them. Everyone looks at each other and shrugs their shoulders as Darth von Doom and Porno Yoda unignite their lightsabers. Cave Hulk says, 'Silly humans. Stupidity is for kids and college students.' 'Daylights wasting,' Morgan says as he looks around. 'We have to get moving to the docks quicker.' The group makes their way through the forest until they reach Darth von Doom's Sith ship. They all look at each other. 'Beats taking the boat and we'll get there quicker,' Darth von Doom says. They all nod their heads and enter the ship as the rampway is closed behind them. The ship then flies to the Island of Misfit Wrestlers as they reach Castle von Doom. Darth von Doom then leads them to the Death Star. They are all shocked and appalled by what they see. Darth von Doom motions toward it. 'This is how we will destroy the Devourer.' * * * Swordman and Sea Man Escape 'Argh...' Swordman, who is in full knight get-up, wakes up with a throbbing headache. He holds his head in pain and looks around. Sea Man is lying right next to him on the ground. Sea Man is unconcious just the same as he was. Swordman looks around and sees that he is in a darkened, windowless room. The walls are made of concrete. He looks over and interestingly sees that the door is open to their cell. He seizes the opportunity and starts shaking Sea Man. 'Wake up! Wake up I say!' he yells in his friend's ear. Sea Man quickly gets up and looks a bit bewildered as he looks around. 'Where...where are we?' he asks with a frightened tone of voice. 'I don't exactly know,' Swordman replies. 'But the door to our cell is open and we'd best get out of here while we can. Right?' Sea Man smiles. 'Right.' Swordman and Sea Man exit the cell door and look around. A giant club is sitting by the door next to an empty steel chair. Beside the club they see Swordman's sword and Sea Man's scimitar weapon. They look around at the concrete walls. Nothing adorns them except for rusty pipes and moldy patches. Water drips from a pipe, creating a pool in the middle of the hallway as they sneak past it and see the front door, grabbing their weapons off the floor next to the chair. 'Come on!' Swordman yells as they run past the bathroom. From inside the bathroom, a toilet flushes. They stop and take a peek. They listen as someone washes their hands inside the bathroom. They hold their weapons tight as the door opens and Cave HUlk-47 sees them standing there. His face has an expression of shock and dismay as they push him back into the bathroom and lock the door behind him. Swordman then breaks the handle so he cannot escape and they run for the door. Swordman and Sea Man run out of the place where they were being held and find themselves in the middle of a dark, scary forest. 'We have to find the others,' Swordman says. 'Right,' Sea Man replies and they run into the forest as Cave Hulk-47 breaks through the bathroom door without much trouble. He then looks over and sees his club. He grabs it and then runs for the door. He goes outside and doesn't see them anywhere, so he runs into the woods after them. * * * Blowing Up Baby! The Death Star is now in the air as the Devourer of Worlds reaches the planet Earth. The sky is dark and motionless except for the fiend sans a few asteroids floating around the Kaiper Belt. The Death Star hovers into position and waits as the being comes closer. 'I can't wait to get a look at this thing,' Darth von Doom says with glee. The being comes closer until they can finally see it. It's so horrifying, words cannot describe it. It's giant, he's ferocious, and it's deadly. It's...it's...it's... 'EEK~! IT'S A GIANT BABY!' Matt screams out. Cave Hulk starts laughing. 'Goo-goo, gaa-gaa,' he says with a chuckle. 'Only you think this is funny, dumbass,' Chameleon says with a sneer. Cave Hulk grasps his club with his hand tightly as he tries to restrain himself from killing Chameleon. 'YOU BASTARD! I KILL YOU! I EAT YOUR CHILDREN! YOUR WIFE IS MINE NOW! YOU BASHED IN THE HEAD LIKE A SEAL!' Cave Hulk exclaims with anger. 'Will you idiots calm down?' Darth von Doom says with impatience. 'We have to destroy this fiend before we destroy each other. Now, I need someone to target the trajectory right for the brain of this creature.' 'We're on it,' Adam says as he and Matt go over to the controls and start analyzing where to shoot. Chameleon spits at Cave Hulk. 'You are ugly!' he yells. 'You have no class! You have no style! You have no underwear! You are a Neanderthal! A beast! You are the epitomy of what it is to be called ugly! I despise you, wretched fiend. And for that, you must die.' Chameleon pulls out a scimitar as his true plan is revealed. Kill the Cave Hulk and then hide from everyone else before they kill him for killing their friend and ally. 'YOU FUCKING JACKASS!' Cave Hulk exclaims. 'I KILL YOU NOW LIKE THE HE-BITCH MAN SLAVE YOU ARE!' Cave Hulk swings his club wildly at Chameleon. Chameleon jumps from console to console with Cave Hulk chasing after him. Darth von Doom points at them. 'Somebody put a leash or a bell on them or something,' he says. Adam and Matt are laughing hysterically over this. Jake starts chasing after the two of them as Porno Yoda joins Darth von Doom at the controls. 'Maybe this wasn't such a good idea,' he says to Darth von Doom. Darth von Doom looks at Cave Hulk trying to smash Chameleon. 'Yeah, maybe you're right,' he replies. 'WE GOT IT! WE GOT IT!' Matt yells out. Darth von Doom chuckles with glee. 'Time to die, baby boy.' The Giant Baby of DEATH~! charges at the ship, trying to eat it as Darth von Doom pushes the big red button. 'Heh heh heh...' he says with glee. 'Time to show everyone that the universe was created with the Big Boom!' The Death Star shoots and fires at the Baby, destroying it in shot. Pieces of the baby fly everywhere and the shockwave barely rattles the ship. Everyone stops what they are doing and starts congratulating one another, even Cave Hulk and Chameleon. Everyone on the ground does the same. The war is over...at least, for now. * * * Battle is Over A synopsis from later that day... Upon returning to the Hulk Family Reunion, our heroes make a peace pact never to fight again. Immediatly, Darth von Doom and Porno Yoda begin arguing over which side Jake will join. Upon asking him, Jake shakes his head and decides to go back to Las Vegas with Adam and Matt so he can hang out with his roomate Squideon. This intrigues both Darth von Doom and Porno Yoda for some reason. The Hulk Family finally has their reunion in peace, but not before Gaylord ruins it again by hitting on Gay Hulk and almost starts an intergalactic war by doing the same to Sharkorian the Barbarian. On a related but lighter note, Sea Man and Swordman returned to rejoin everyone but with Cave Hulk from Earth 47 on their tails. Upon seeing the Hulk Family on this Earth, they saw him as one of their own and is now a full-fledged member of the Hulk Family. Chameleon and the Hulk Family made a peace pact that they will not attack one another unless provoked due to the current situation at hand. Gaylord was sent back to Eternia using the Cosmic Rod by the Killer Pirates and has said that he will return to Earth. All of the alien warriors says their good-byes to the heroes who saved the day by fighting off the Devourer of Worlds and take off in their spaceships for their homeplanets. The warriors, however, vowed to return if any of our heroes tried to destroy the universe again. James, Adam, and Matt from After Earth or the Earth where humans colonized space and moved to other planets, made the same vow and are now said to be trying to return to their dimension of origin. Porno Yoda, Adam, and Matt were overheard talking about the distinct possibility that Darth von Doom is not the real James von Doom but merely another doppelganger who replaced the real James von Doom. Jake, for some reason, seemed to be real intrigued by this. Jake has amnesia and has no idea about his past and that name rings a bell. Upon hearing that James von Doom is the owner of the Hades Corporation, he decides to do some investigations of his own at the Hades Corporation Headquarters in Santa Maria, California. Darth von Doom told the Killer Pirates that once the Clash of Titans is over, they will head to the secret Hades Corporation Facility known only as Black Mesa and try to steal some top secret documents. What is on these documents is some mystery, but it seems really important to him. Upon it's destruction, the Giant Baby of DEATH~! became a new life form. No one knows what it has become, but it has vowed to destroy the Earth the first chance it gets. * * * A New War Begins In the Horny Mathematician's destroyed lab, papers are flying about and most of his machines and gadgets are destroyed. He is now deceased, but lightning comes through the window. It strikes a machine. The machine turns on. But this isn't any ordinary machine. This is the Horny Mathematician's Creation Machine. Next to it is a portfolio of all of James von Doom's demented creations from when he was in elementary, middle, and high school, long before he joined the ranks of wrestling's elite. The Portfolio swings open and the Creation Machine starts sucking at it. Like a vaccuum cleaner, it sucks in all of the papers. A bright light appears from behind it. And then, the drawings start coming to life... Judging by the picture of the crow with the stitched up scarecrow face, mangled wings, stitched up body, and gnarled, sharp teeth that is shown going into the machine, I think we're all pretty much screwed. (/THE END/)
