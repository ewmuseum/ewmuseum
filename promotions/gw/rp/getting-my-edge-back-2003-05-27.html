Getting my edge back

27 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/27 Here I am, laying flat on my back, eyes tightly clinched shut, with a euphoric grin on my face. But instead of being in the arms of sleep, an embrace I dearly miss, I now find myself wrapped inside those of Hank, the local butcher and resident fat ass, whom I’ve just been powerslammed by. John stands about six feet tall, and weighs about three hundred and fifty pounds, a slim jim shy of three seventy give or take a pound. Another point of interest would be that he is constantly squinting and then opening his eyes wide as though he’s takin a reality test that’s being administered like an eye exam and he can’t seem to make out the letters. Shit, poor bastard is so deranged that the letters probably look like dancing girls to him. Yet another point of interest would be his uncommonly potent body odor. Today he smells of roadkill stuffed with garlic cloves and then possibly sauteed in random filth possibly containing sulfur. My nose hairs burn as a result of his stench as I snap out of my constant daydream of late to find his massive frame smashing mine into the canvas. I attempt to wiggle my way out from under him but to no avail. While still crushing my body under the weight of his he whispers in my ear “Pressure. Pressure’s on.” before lifting me up and completing the Oklahoma Stampede by picking me back up and running across the ring like a tornado of raw sewage only to slam me into the mat once again. My eyes clinch shut and I grit my teeth as I collide with the mat only to find myself being crushed again. Now for most this would be a less than desirable situation. Trapped inside of a cage under the instruction of your past his prime, seventy five cent cigar smoking, cheap wine swilling, loud Italian shirt wearing uncle who claims it will help you “get yo” edge back.” All the while combatting some crazy redneck that lives his life under the impression that he’s the real slim shady. I can’t help but crack a smile when he attempts to use all of his three hundred and fifty pounds to wrestle me into submission. Not that the idea of a three hundred and fifty pound sloppy fat cat attempting to use some sort of skilled maneuver on me isn’t just pure genius by itself, because it is. If only it didn’t hurt so much worse... He goes for a sharpshooter, but is denied and instead locks in a boston crab. He rocks back and forth as though he were riding one of those toy horses small children ride for twenty five cents in front of grocery stores. On the mat, two pounds of pressure away from being snapped in two, and I’m smiling. How grateful I am he passed up that sixth porkchop at dunch, a meal he made up to fill the space between lunch and dinner, in order to “keep his figure.” Everytime I’ve closed my eyes for the past week I see the same thing. It’s as though it were plastered on the inside of my eyelids and constantly touched up in order not to fade due to my constant blinking. Same thing every time. I see myself laying exhausted on the field of battle, victorious at Dead Zone. The bell rings, and the winner is announced by none other than David West himself. “The winner of the match, and your new Primestar and Platinum Champion, Deacon Holmes!” The crowd erupts in praise as I rise to my feet and raise both titles high overhead. I then hang one over each shoulder and look down at my fallen foes. The washed up “Ladies Man” Jay Robson, and my only rival since signing a GW contract, Leviathan, my devil. I wax philosophical for a moment as I place the sole of my shoes across his throat, crushing his larynx as I squat down to turn this into a more personal moment. It being a special occasion and all. So I get down into his face, and I whisper to him “Ya know, my grandmother used say every man’s got his devil. And you can’t rest “til ya” find him... but if it’s any consolation to ya?, you have put a smile on my face.” choking him half to death with my shoes all the while. I then rise back to a standing positing and remove my foot from his throat only to kick him square in the face with it, jarring his teeth loose in the process. A single finger salute is my parting gesture to both men as I climb out of the ring a double champion and head to the back in front of the hundreds of thousands of people in attendance. Soon after my name would be cemented in the record books, not only as Global Wrestling’s youngest superstar, but Global Wrestling’s youngest double champion. More importantly, I’d have gained the respect I deserve. Comments about me would no longer be trailed by a suffix of “but he still hasn’t proven himself” while comments on how good I could be would simply cease to exist. I’d finally get the credit I deserve. I understand people’s opinions, I just don’t really care about them... Suddenly I’m snatched from my dream once again and slammed back into the soiled canvas of reality. After minimal success with his stint as a submission wrestler he finally decides to stick with the girl he brought to the dance and goes for a powerbomb. I regain my composure to find myself high over his head in a more than slightly compromising position. Now there is a time for daring, and a time for caution, and a wise man knows which is called for. Obviously, old Hank had forgotten who he was dealing with because this maneuver was definitely on the daring side. As he prepares to drive me into the mat I lock on and inverted figure four headlock and knock his arms away, causing him to be driven face first into the canvas with all of my weight further smashing his face. I whisper to him “It’s been swell, but the swelling’s gone down.” as I reach forward to hook his leg and pull it back into a half crab, still choking him. Hank pleads with me in a muffled voice to relinquish my hold as his limbs flail wildly. Apparently he was trying to buy more sand for his hour glass, I wasn’t selling any. He kicks and screams in a desperate attempt to get out before finally giving up. The entire shabbily constructed ring, which was probably assembled in someone’s backyard, shakes as Hank pounds his massive hands, complete with sausage fingers, down on the mat. I almost feel bad for him, he wants to tap out but can’t... there’s no ref. This is what he gets for dealing with my uncle. One of his favorite training exercises, or tortures as it were, for me is making me run a gauntlet. CW books me in these strings bizarre matches against even odder opponents. He puts adds in the Orlando Sentinel on a regular basis asking for competitors. Takes all kinds, kick boxers, wrestlers, deranged butchers with severe eating disorders... doesn’t really matter, so long as they’ve got a killer instinct or some sort of ridiculous size advantage over me, not unlike many of my Global Wrestling opponents. Hank took the bait and now he’s paying for it. He is now the unfortunate victim of The Magnum Divider. His breathing has now slowed dramatically from the frenzied pace it was at just moments ago as he panicked to get out of the hold. I look out into the crowd of thirty or so to find CW scribble on his pad, probably more weird shit for me to do as part of my training. You know how you make decisions that lead to other decisions but you don’t know why you made those decisions in the first place? Well making my uncle my trainer is one of those. I wonder exactly how it happened as I roll Hank over. K.O.ed. He was unconscious, I could tell by the cold accusing stare in his eyes. I roll out of the ring and walk through the cage’s door exhausted. Reminds me of the past two years when I did this on a regular basis but with actual competition. CW comes to me clipboard in hand and informs me that I did “Alright” in between cigar puffs. “Alright!” I was a ninja! My kung-fu was strong! I shoot back in a joking tone. He then slaps me in the back of the head and puts his arm around my shoulder while escorting me out of the building. I haven’t slept in the past thirty six hours, and doing so in the next few seems to be out of the question, CW’s got more training in store for me.
