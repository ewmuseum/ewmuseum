The truth comes out

22 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/22/03 As Christina McCoy sits at her desk, she holds up a videotape and stares at. All of a sudden the door bursts open and in storms Brick. He walks right up to Christina’s desk and rests his fists on top of it. “Brick. Just the person I?” “I want Ice!” he roars. “Excuse me?” “I said I want Ice! He’s the motherfucker who sent me to that damn freak show last week! I want his ass on Evolution!” “Brick, given the fact that Ice tried to assault me last week, giving him to you is something I’d like nothing better to do, but I’ve already promised him to Jin Akiyara.” “What”!” “Besides, he’s not responsible for what happened to you last week.” “But they found his “It was stolen. Besides, given what we know about Ice, does something like this really seem like his style?” Brick thinks about it. He has seen Ice in action. Every thing he has seen has shown Ice to be a vicious and intense competitor, but not one for playing cruel pranks. “In fact, Brick, I’d like to show you just who’s responsible for what happened to you.” As she places the tape in the VCR, the puzzle starts to fall into place for Brick. And then the screen begins to flicker. The video shows Brick walking into Christina’s office, save no one is there. The room is empty, He looks at the door again and Christina’s nametag falls of and reveals the room as the former office of Steven Perdue. Brick: What in the hell?!”! Brick turns around and Out of nowhere Brick gets blind-sided with a super kick that nails him in the face and knocks him onto the linoleum floor unconscious. Someone can be heard snapping their fingers and four men in black suits quickly surround Brick, each grabbing a limb. Right away, Brick recognizes them as TUE bodyguards. They pick him up and hurriedly pull him back into the room, bumping his head against the wall accidentally along the way. Laughter can be heard as Deacon Holmes and John Zero step into view with the black duffle bag with Ice’s logo on it, containing who knows what. “I should have known,” Brick snarls. Deacon Holmes: Hurry up; we got a match in a few minutes! Holmes steps into the room himself and looks around to both sides of the hall before shutting the door. After a few minutes the dressing room door slowly opens once more and Deacon Holmes sticks his head out in order to survey the hall before coming out. Realizing the coast is clear Holmes walks out into the hall and signals for his guards to come out too. Out step the four black suit clad men, carrying the unconscious Brick wearing a hot pink dress with purple flowers, blonde wig and hideous make up job. Deacon Holmes: Hold up a second. Holmes reaches into Ice's duffle bag, which he probably stole, and pulls out a silver flask. He has his guards tip Brick's head back enabling to pour the contents of the flask into his mouth. Deacon then pockets the flask, which probably also belongs to Ice, and tosses the bag back into the room and closes the door. He then hands his bodyguards a business card. Deacon Holmes: Go prop him up outside and call him a cab, tell them to take him to the address on this card. I called them up earlier and said I'd be sending over some live entertainment. “Stop the damn tape!” Brick roars. The tape stops and all that remains is a blank screen. Brick rises to his feet in anger. “I’ll kill that little motherfucker!” “Brick, wait a minute. Now as much as I’d like to hand Holmes to you on a silver platter, I already have plans for him and you.” “What do you mean you already have plans?” “Because of that stunt he pulled with you and after what he and the rest of TUE did to Ely and the others, Deacon is going to be defending both his titles on the next Evolution. You, on the other hand, will be facing TLM in a non-title match. However, I do have an idea that might make you a very happy man.” “Yeah?” “Oh, yes,” Christina replies, with a smile on her face.
