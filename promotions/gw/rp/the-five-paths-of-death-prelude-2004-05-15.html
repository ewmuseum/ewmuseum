The Five Paths of Death: Prelude

15 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 15th, 2004 March 30th, 2004 He had no way of knowing how long he'd been unconscious, but when his eyelids scraped over his parched orbs, all his world was flame. Moments tumbled back into his mind. A spark. A roar. A girl. God, was she okay? Was she even alive? Rokku tried to stand, and failed. He couldn't move his legs. Why couldn't he move his legs? Scowling, Rokku wiped blood, soot, and sweat away from his eyes, then twisted his body and craned his neck. Of course. The cieling had collapsed. Tons of stone and metal pinned his lower body to the floor. And soon, that would collapse as well -- and then he would die. Gritting his teeth, Rokku slapped his hands down hard on the searing floor and pulled with all his strength. Pain ripped through his body like a thousand jagged blades, and he wasn't even sure if his legs were whole any more, but still he strained. 'It's no use. You are going to die.' Rokku collapsed on his face but immediately looked up. A handful of feet away sat his sensei, seemingly immune to the smoke and the fire, an eye of serenity in the blazing storm. 'I have trained you well, but you are not strong enough,' said Seikua, as calmly as if he they were discussing the morning news, 'you will struggle, you will burn, and eventually you will die.' 'I... will.. not... give... UP!' declared Rokku, struggling all the harder for his sensei's condemning statement. 'I did not expect you to,' said Seikua, 'but your struggles are for naught. Death is a certainty.' The simple honesty of his sensei's words struck Rokku, and he knew that they were true. Still, he fought, pushing away the pain and the hopelessness. 'Then I'll die fighting,' said the Steel Lion, 'If I must die, I will die with honor. I won't give the Echelon the pleasure of my surrender.' 'Again, as I expected,' said Seikua, rising from his seat and crouching before his student. 'You would choose your death, as befits the Steel Lion,' continued Seikua, 'you could choose to struggle and die here and now, a single death for a single man. But I can offer you a different death, a death five times over -- but a death in which life may be found.' 'Tell me one thing, sensei,' said Rokku, 'Yoshiko... is she alive?' 'Hm...' muttered the old monk, staring off into space, '... it would appear she does. YOu threw her clear of the collapsing cieling, and though her injuries are severe, she yet lives.' 'Then I choose the five deaths,' said Rokku, 'if that's the path I have to follow to be at her side again, I will walk it no matter the pain I face.' Seikua smirked, and held out his hand. Stretching with all his strength, Rokku clasped the old monk's hand, and the world went from red to black.
