The Quest to find the Key of Solo Success (MOTM#1)

20 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 20th - July 21st 2004 Vergomovs? Apartment Tuesday 20th July 2004 8:27 am “Igor! Hold on!” A flustered Constantino Zergolov sprints down the street outside the pair’s London apartment. Zergolov is wearing khaki shorts, white trainers and a navy t-shirt. His hair is scruffy and he is unshaven. He continues to run down the street shouting. “Where are you going” Igor!” Igor Arefiev is fifteen feet in front of his partner and turns around. Igor is dressed in green ? length trousers and a white t-shirt. He is wearing a black “Von Dutch” baseball cap and white trainers. On his back, the young English born Pole is carrying a large green and black rucksack. He scratches his head as his tag team partner approaches. “Where are you going?” enquiries a slightly out of breath Zergolov. “On a quest?” is the short sharp reply. “On a quest?” Constantino seems puzzled. “I’m going on a quest for the key to solo success. If I can find the key to solo success, then I am guaranteed to be the greatest superstar I can.” Constantino still seems puzzled. “You seriously can’t be on about the key that they talked about in Warsaw?” Constantino’s face now shows a look of confusion. Igor nods and turns on his heel, and walks away from his tag team partner. Constantino is silent for a number of seconds. “You realise that that is only a circus myth!” A story they create for us to strive to be the best we can be?!” Igor turns around and faces Constantino as he walks backwards. “Behind every myth, there is some truth!” shouts Arefiev as he walks off. Constantino stands puzzled as Igor walks away and turns the corner out of sight. “He is only going to come back, even more worried than he already is,” mumbles Zergolov, “oh well,” he says with a smile. Out of his pocket, Constantino takes a cell phone. He presses at a couple of buttons and then holds it up to his ear. He walks silently towards the door of his apartment. “Sophie” Hi! It’s Con! Yeah? I’m good? And you? Listen, my flatmate is away for a couple of days, so I was wondering if you want to come round for dinner tonight. Great! About half seven! Okay, I’ll see you soon!” Zergolov hangs up and goes into his building with a smile across his face. -------------------- Heathrow Airport 9:58 am “One ticket to Warsaw, please.” Igor Arefiev is standing at the check-in of Virgin Airways in Heathrow Airport. Around him, numerous passengers are rushing with their suitcases and other luggage towards desks and through check-in. The woman behind the counter smiles as Igor slides his passport across the counter. She takes the passport and looks at it. She then types at the keyboard in front of her. “Now, Mr. Are-fie?” “Arefiev.” “Mr. Arefiev. The earliest flight I can put you on is at four thirty this afternoon.” “What time is it now?” asks Igor Arefiev as he looks around the terminal, obviously not in possession of a watch. “It’s ten o’clock, sir.” Igor nods. “That’ll be okay.” The attendant smiles. She types at the keyboard a number of times, and then a printing noise is heard. She removes a piece of paper from below the computer desk and puts it with his passport. “Here you are sir. Check in will open in four hours time at desks twenty-five to thirty-two.” “Thanks.” Igor takes his ticket and passport and turns away from the Virgin desk. He steps out into the open of the large terminal clearing. He looks around, and then unpeels the rucksack from his shoulders. It collapses onto the clean ceramic floor. Igor unzips a side pocket, and stuffs the ticket and passport in. He struggles to get it closed and then reaches to the opposite side of the bag. He removes a small blue book. He looks at it and smiles. Igor flicks it open and begins to run his finger through each page. It is a small phone book. Igor turns all the way to the “w” page. At the top of the page in large, blue scribbled letters, it reads “WARSAW.” Igor smiles as he reads down through the list of names. He finger passes along the line of each name. Luke? Ivan? Gregory? Steffen? He stops on one name. However it is not the name of a person, but an institution. He taps at the name for a number of seconds, and then looks around his surroundings. His eyes focus in on a telephone booth in the far corner of the airport. Igor stands to his feet and pulls his luggage onto one shoulder. He sets off towards the phone booth still holding the phone book in his right hand. He looks down at the name. Warsaw Circus Academy? In his eyes, he seems to look at the name with a little conflict in his mind. He reaches the phone booth and realises it is occupied. Igor dumps his bag onto the ground and sits onto the bag, his eyes transfixed on the booklet. Warsaw Circus Academy? Should he call it? Why shouldn’t he call it? “Hey buddy it’s free.” Igor is taken out of his thought by the voice of the previous occupier of the phone booth. Igor looks up and nods. “Thanks.” He mumbles as he stands to his feet. Igor reaches deep into his pocket, and produces a fistful of coins. He picks through the handful of change, and lifts out a number of fifty pence pieces which he places on top of the phone, for handiness sake. Igor looks at the book again, which is now resting alongside the coins on top of the phone. Igor lifts the receiver and drops a couple of coins into the phone. He looks at the book and presses a couple of numbers. He hangs up. The coins drop to the returned change drawer. Igor sighs and runs his hand through his hair in frustration. He turns back to the phone and lifts the receiver. He drops a couple of coins in and bens to dial a phone number. Arefiev hangs up. Igor thumps the side of the booth with his fist and once again, looks back at the phone. “Right” I’ll ring them,” exclaims Igor, seemingly trying to reassure himself. He lifts the phone a third time, and takes a couple of coins from the returned change drawer. He drops the coins into the phone. He punches at the buttons on the phone as he reads off the phone book. Igor Arefiev is silent and motionless as he listens to the dialling tone of the phone. “Hey” Is that the Warsaw Circus Academy? It is! Great! Listen, could I speak to Mister Salem? Yes, I’ll hold?” Igor looks out into the terminal as he waits on the phone line. He drops another couple of coins into the phone and continues to wait. “Hello” Hello? Is that Mister Salem? Hi! It’s Igor Arefiev? I’m ringing to let you know, I’m coming to visit. Yeah? Oh good, you remember me! Yeah, I should arrive late this evening, so I’ll see you in the morning. Great! Bye? bye!” Igor hangs up and breathes a huge sigh of relief. -------------------- Warsaw Circus Academy 9:48 pm A blue taxi pulls up outside the grand gates of a large grey building on the outskirts of Warsaw. The building is surrounded by a seven foot stone wall. The grand gates are caste black iron. The taxi door opens and Igor Arefiev steps out. He hands the driver some money, and then turns to face the building that he has come to visit. The Warsaw Circus Academy is draped with a mysterious cloud due to the sunset that is eclipsed behind the large building. Igor Arefiev smiles. He steps towards a small panel on the wall and presses a white button. After an initial bleep, a voice is heard. “Hello?” “Hi. I’m Igor Arefiev. I informed Mr. Salem I would be visiting.” There is a short pause. Igor shivers a little as the wind blows past. “Very well. Come on in,” exclaims the voice from beyond the panel. After a couple of seconds, a large creaking is heard and the gates begin to open. Igor slides between the gates as they are partially open and walks down the gravel lane that leads to the main building of the Warsaw Circus Academy. As he walks, he is looking up at the windows of the building and smiling, clearly recalling days previous spent here. As Igor approaches the main doors of the school, the doors swing open, and an elderly man steps out helped by a brown walking stick. “Igor!” calls the man with a large smile on his face. “Mr. Salem! How are you?” Mr. Salem smiles, as he presses at his grey suit. “Please come in, Igor.” The elderly gentleman motions for Igor to enter the academy. Igor obliges and steps into a large foyer. The floor is marble. In front of Igor is a large Y staircase. A red carpet runs down the steps and to the door. The walls are covered in large portraits. Igor looks around the large room with a big smile on his face. “Is it how you remember?” asks the gentleman as he follows Igor. “Exactly,” nods the Flying Vergomov. “Good.” Mr. Salem smiles and brushes past Igor. “Igor” Lights are out? So we have set up your old room? And we can discuss your visit in the morning.” Igor nods. “Good night.” “Good night.” Igor starts up the stairs as the elderly gentleman disappears through a door to the side of the stairs. -------------------- Wednesday 21st July 2004 8:32 am “BOYS!” A large bellowing voice echoes throughout the breakfast hall of the Warsaw Circus Academy. The one hundred-or-so boys that are sitting in the hall are immediately brought to a halt. A silence descends over the room. A large, burly woman with black hair steps forward and surveys the crowd. “Boys,” she begins with a more relaxed yet still as frightening voice, “today we welcome a former graduate of this establishment back. So I would ask that you give him a warm welcome. He is a member of The Flying Vergomovs circus squad. Igor Arefiev!” The pupils break into applause. A couple of seconds later, Igor Arefiev steps into the room wearing a navy “Flying Vergomov” tracksuit. He waves at the pupils and then takes a seat at a table opposite Mr. Salem. Quickly, the room returns to its usual conversation. Igor settles himself at the table and is quickly presented with a bowl of cereal and a glass of orange juice. “I see the strict diet is still ongoing.” Mr. Salem nods and smiles. “Mr. Salem,” Igor begins, “I wanted to discuss my reason for coming here.” “By that comment I take it you are not going to be taking advantage of your graduate status and taking a class with our seniors?” Igor shakes his head. “Not this time. This time, I am on a quest?” “Really?” enquiries Mr. Salem, “A quest for what?” Igor swallows a mouthful of cereal. “The key to solo success.” An eerie silence engulfs the staff table. All the staff members look at Igor. “Surely you realise that the key is only a myth, a legend created for the boys” exclaims a bald man at the far end of the table. The rest of the faculty nod agreeing with his comment. “Not necessarily.” Mr. Salem’s two words almost immediately cut short the telling groans of the staff at the table. “You know of the key to solo success?” Igor perks up, and leans in, waiting to be informed of more. Mr. Salem nods. “Only the truly worthy can be in possession of the key. I will tell you more after breakfast. Alone.”
