Thinking

11 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 11 I was sitting there, Jesse had left to goto his house. I sat there alone, no one to talk to or anything. Now it was going to be silent. The road ahead of me was dead, no one was driving on it but me, I could probably go as fast as I wanted to, but I don’t want to take the risk of getting caught by the cops, yet again. The road was in pretty bad shape compared to the other roads I’ve driven on, this one was all torn up, the cement was starting to rot away, and I could see it was going to crack pretty soon if people kept driving on it. I hit a bump on the road, that sent my two front wheels off of the ground. My head hit the top of the car, I was certain there was a bump there. I drove over a mountain, but had just found out that it wasn’t free road all the way there, there were a few more cars ahead of me, but it didn’t look like the people inside knew where they were going. I don’t really think I did either. I had lost the big battle royal against the other competition. Rich Sherwin was the one who took home the victory, but he still doesn’t know what title he had won. Maybe it was the Primestar, or the Television title. Who knew, but whichever one it was, it wasn’t going to be around my waist. But, I had one thing on my mind that was going to make me happy, next Evolution, I had a match against Deacon Holmes, for the Cruiserweight championship. If some of you remember, Deacon was my enemy a few months ago in GW. I passed the people that were driving really slowly ahead of me, I look out of my window and saw a man with a long beard holding the steering wheel, but he had one hand holding a beer bottle, I guess this is the road where people come to drink and drive, but not me, cause I haven’t really been a fan of alcohol since I became 18. It just made a really bad after taste inside of my mouth. I passed another car, but this time inside it was a girl, but she looked very young, it only looked like she was 18 at maximum. I passed all of the cars, and got back onto free road, I didn’t even know where this road went to. I looked out of my window, but saw nothing but old brown weeds coming out of the ground, no grass at all, there was maybe one little tree. There was a green sign coming ahead so I was going to look at it to see where this road led too, but the green sign was just a advertisement for Dairy Queen. Boy, thinking of Dairy Queen makes me really hungry I wonder if there was a store like there was a bit back. “How was I going to beat him?” That’s all I could ask myself. How could I beat Deacon Holmes, he’s an ex World Champion. He must be really good, what am I thinking, he’s awesome. He’s beat me every single time we’ve been in the same ring as each other. He’s always had a way to beat me, but this time its going to change, this time I’m going to walk out and be able to say, “I Beat Deacon Holmes” and I’ll be the one with the Cruiserweight title around my waist, not his. I just have to pump up, do my exercises, and maybe goto the gym a bit, but I know in my heart, I will be able to beat him, but only if I try. Look at him, he’s got like 3 titles currently, like can he not give someone else a nice long run with one of those, instead, he has to keep them, just so he can brag about being a triple champion, or how ever many titles he has currently in the fed. “I can beat him, but only if I try.” That’s another thing I gotta listen too. I’m not just going to not try and let him kick my ass, I’m going to fight back and kick his ass, and take his precious Cruiserweight title from his waist, and onto mine. And this time, it’s Max’s time to shine, not Deacon’s.
