A dark union #1

31 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/31/03 Silence. I sit in the shadows in silence. I watch as the man called Holmes and Zero’s secretary, Debra begin to have sex. I think to myself “When did love get so free? How is it in these times we don’t cherish women more and have sex out of love instead of what it has become?” I laugh at the thought knowing I myself never in all the years I was on this world had sex out of love. I nod in approval of what the young man has done and is currently doing. I watch a little longer and think “Well if she gives it up that easily……I’ll wait my turn. This should be interesting.” They continue to consummate their “love” for each other as I sit in solemn silence. A door opens. Holmes throws Debra off of him. I look from my shadow to see who the man is. I do not recognize his face, but he seems to know Holmes. They talk a bit and then leave the room. From where I am I can barely make out what they are saying so I look over at Debra. She stands and fixes her skirt and fixes her blouse. A door closes. I realize both men have left, seeing how nobody came back into the room that Debra is standing. I sniff the air slightly. The sweet smell of blood, perfume, and sex mingle in the air. My eyes move from Debra to the dead body. Hunger. Bloodlust taking over the senses. I try and stop my feelings, my instincts but it has been too long since I have fed. Nearly two months since my last won honorable fight. I have had to feed on blood like a common vampire. It sustains me but it does not empower the likes of me. I look at the dead body once again then back to Debra who is picking things up from the floor. I take a step. She swings around as if she heard it, looking in my direction. I dare not make a move. Debra becomes hesitant but keeps cleaning up the room. The light switch. I look over to my right and there the light switch is. Now my instincts have taken over. I turn off the lights throwing the room into absolute darkness. Debra screams and runs toward the dead man’s body and takes his gun. She slowly backs up. Who’s there?</blue> She says while backing herself into the farthest wall. The woman has smarts. Zero has trained her well.…by human standards. I dive out of my hiding place and land on the dead man. I grab him with one arm and jump back into my shadowy hiding place and turn back on the lights. Debra looks around frightened but still pointing that gun in front of her. I look down at the guy. “This will have to do”. I open my mouth and sink my teeth into his chest. Snap goes the chest cavity as I feed from the body, spitting out bullets when I got to them.. The sounds of dismemberment come from that corner. I sling body parts from the shadows as I tear his body limb from limb. I finally make my way to the heart. I look down at the non-beating muscle and pull it from his chest. I look at it up close and smile and devour it. Debra stands awe struck with fear at the sight and begins shooting the few bullets left in the gun into the shadows. Two of the bullets catch me in the chest. I wince at the pain of the bullets, finish the heart and walk out of the shadows. She tries to shoot again but is out of bullets now. I look at her with my face covered in blood, my bluish gray eyes glowing an even darker blue, my fangs showing from my mouth as I start to speak. She looks at me. I have long thick shoulder length dark red hair that covers the left side of my face at all times.. And there is a tattoo of a cross that starts from the top of my face all the way down, with the centerline going through my eye. I wear a long black mandarin collared trench coat and a pair of black leather pants. On my chest, which can only be seen a little because of the coat being on, is a Chinese styled black dragon with red eyes. Under my coat the whole upper half is covered in tattoos from different lands all the way up to his neck. I wear knee high black leather steel-toed boots that have metal on the soles. Silence. She makes a break for the door and I head her off at the pass, being almost ten times faster than the normal human. I wipe my face clean on my trench coat sleeve. I slightly make a motion for her to sit down. She looks at the dragon on my chest and walks over to the table which she just had sex on a little before. I’d prefer to stand. Wh…wh…what are you? Who are you? How did you get in here? I look her over. A beautiful specimen indeed. She stands about 5’6, roughly about 145 pounds. She had long brown hair and blue eyes. She had curves in all the right places. And she was blessed by the almighty to have a nice sized chest and ass. I smile to myself thinking what a meal she would make but contain my composure. In my deep snake like voice I begin to answer her questions. My name is Leinad Soma, president of The Soma Corporation. I’ve heard of you…you are a very wealthy man. You were in Fortune 500 as the fourth wealthiest man in the world. What are you? Your not human. All answers will come in time. I was looking for the man who lives here…A Mr. John Zero? She sits down on the edge of the table and I walk over in front of her. She smiles a little but the look of fear and awe still in her eye. He’s not in at the moment. You can stay and wait for him if you like. If you don’t mind the company. I mean there are things to keep one busy till he gets back. I raise an eyebrow and sniff the air once again and look at her. I think I shall just sit here and wait on him. That would be the easiest thing. Are you sure? I mean…we could make it an interesting time. She takes off her blouse. Smiling at me she moves closer to me. I stop her and I shake my head no. I sniff the air once more and I sit down on the sofa. If I were not who I was. And you had not just had sex with that other guy right before me. And if I did not smell syphilis coming from you, then maybe I’d go a round with you. Believe me I usually don’t turn down women as beautiful as you but even creatures such as I can get what you have…I hope ya told Mr. Holmes… In shock she sits there, mouth opened. I smirk as we both sit there in silence waiting on Mr. John Zero.
