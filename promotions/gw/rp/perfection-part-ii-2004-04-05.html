Perfection (Part II)

5 April 2004

Date: 4.5.04 *I suggest reading Part I to fully understand* Perfection (Part II) 4.5.04 Evolution My head is spinning as I sit up in my bed. I throw the sheets that cover me aside and drop my feet off the side of the bed and to the floor. I feel like I've been hit by a truck. The drinking competition on Evolution really took a toll on me considering I'd never had anything alcoholic until that point. McNasty is a machine, I'm sure of it. Anybody that can drink that much alcohol and not puke simply isn't human. I don't even know how I made it to the finals, but I really wish I hadn't. I groggily stand to my feet, my knees shaking slightly for a moment under the weight. My head throbs uncontrollably, and I rub my face with my hands in a feeble attempt to stop the pain. I run my fingers through my hair, feeling the effects of my first hangover. The phone rings, which causes the pain in my head to spike even more. I grunt as I clumsily reach for the phone, but I drop the receiver. Too lazy to pick it up, I let it dangle by it's cord as I stumble my way out of the room. I hear a muffled voice from the phone, but I just don't care. As I walk out my bedroom door, Jameson is walking up the stairs with a slightly fogged over glass. Some people would find it scary that Jameson knew exactly when I moved, but I've grown accustomed to it. 'For your head, sir,' Jameson says as he gestured to the glass in his hand. His British accent Jameson had a certain smile on his face that I hadn't seen in a while. It was as if he was trying as hard as he could to not laugh at me. He had never seen me hung over, and I think he took a certain joy in it. I take the glass from Jameson's hand and eye it weerily. I'm not too keen on drinking fluids after the drinking tournament. Jameson, as if able to read my mind, eases my worries. 'It will help, sir,' He says as locks his hand behind his lower back. I reluctantly take a sip of the fluid. I feel much more secure as I taste the fluid, and I realize it's not alcholic in the least. I don't understand how people could ever become addicted to alcohol. Why in the hell would anybody want to feel like this every morning? A vibration from my cell phone startles me. I reach into my pocket and retrieve it, watching the green light flash, indicating that somebody is calling. (940 5616) Julie Wing I stare at the number until the phone finally stops vibrating. It seemed like an eternity. I see her face before my mind violentally slams me into a flashback. -------------- ~Flashback~ *shakes fingers* Doodly do Doodly do Doodly do I open the door the my house and enter. I've just gotten back from yet another tour of Japan, and I'm in a very good mood. There was always something about Japan that made me happy to wrestle there. I drop my luggage bag that I had on my shoulder to the floor, and I call out my girlfriend's name. 'Julie?! I'm home!' I yell up the stairs in the general direction of our bedroom. There's no response. 'That's weird,' I mutter to myself as I remove my sunglasses. I check my watch. She should be home by now. I start to walk up the stairs, still calling her name. 'Jame-' I begin to call for Jameson, but realize it's his day off. I begin to worry more and more as I scale up the stairs. It's totally unlike Julie to not be home immediately after work, especially if she knows I'm coming home that day. I come near the top of the stairs when I see her. My heart drops to the pit of my stomach. I sprint to her half naked body laying on the floor in a bloody mess. There's a pool of blood behind her head. I check for a pulse, and I let out a gasp of happiness when I feel one. I quickly pick up the phone and dial 911. The flashback slowly starts to fade away as I hear myself yelling for an ambulance. ------- *Doodly do Doodly do Doodly do* 'Mr. Idol?' Jameson's voice brings me back from my nightmare of a flashback. It takes me a few moments to regain my composure. I shake my head lightly as if the action would distinguish the flashback from my head. 'Are you quite alright, Mr. Idol,' Jameson asks as his eyes narrow. 'You look frightfully ill. Would you like a cup of tea?' 'Uh,' I stutter a few times before I can finish my sentence. 'No, no, I'm alright.' I shake my head quicker, still trying to forget the sight of her bloody head. I have to get my mind off of it! 'Jameson, did you get me a tape of my last match?' I ask. 'Of course, sir. It's in the video room.' Jameson replies. Nothing is going to stop me from getting ready for Evolution. Nothing. Especially some memory of a long lost love. I had more pressing matters at hand. 'I'm going to go down and watch it.' I say to him. I begin to leave, but he speaks again. 'Oh, Mr. Idol? A Ms. Christina McCoy called while you were sleeping. She says a Mr. Jesse Jamester has issued a challenge to you.' I roll my eyes before replying. 'Jesse who?'
