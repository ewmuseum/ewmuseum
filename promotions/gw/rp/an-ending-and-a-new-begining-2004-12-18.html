An Ending and a New Begining

18 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 18, 2004 Everything was perfect in Hillary Watson’s life at that exact moment in time. The nymph-like blonde was about to make her wildest dream reality; she was about to marry the man that made her feel complete and it was impossible for her to hide her smile. She was so wrapped up in her feelings of joy that she didn’t notice the concerned look on her father’s face as he entered into the room she had used to change into her wedding dress. Hillary’s father cast a nervous look at his feet after entering the room; he seemed as he had no idea how to say what he was about to say to his baby girl. “Hillary,” he said after clearing his throat. “I was just in the room that Mike was using to change and-? he abruptly stopped speaking once Hillary made eye contact with him. “And what Daddy?” Hillary asked with an oblivious grin on her face. The short tubby man that stood before Hillary took a moment to answer. “He wasn’t there,” he paused a moment to pull an envelope from his pants pocket, “all I found in the room was this.” Hillary took the envelope from her father’s hand and looked at it with a goofy smile upon her nymph-like face. She thought it was sweet that Mike was writing her love notes just before their wedding. “I’ll go ahead and leave you alone dear, I’ll be right outside if you need to talk to anyone,” he said with a somber tone before leaving the room. Hillary gently opened the envelope, careful not to rip the paper as she wanted to keep the envelope and it’s contents as a keepsake. She unfolded the paper contained within and began to read the letter. Hillary, By the time you receive this letter it should be obvious to everyone, excluding you, that I am no longer present at the church and have no intention of marrying you. This has all been a sadomasochistic game designed to rid me of you once and for all. I hope that after this you finally get the hint: I DON’T WANT YOU! While I do admit that you are one of the best lays I’ve ever had, you’re annoying and stupid--a combination that makes it impossible for me to love you. So this is good riddance for forever... Mike Biddle Tears began to stream down Hillary’s nymph-like features as she allowed the letter to fall to the ground. At that exact moment she felt as if she had died on the inside; she knew she would never be the same again. ***** A few months had passed since Mike Biddle rid himself of Hillary Watson once and for all. The brash young man decided that the best way to appreciate his newfound freedom was to take a vacation from his profession: professional wrestling. The timing of his decision was impeccable since his 4WW contract had expired, leaving him free to do as he wished. At first he enjoyed his time off, attending various social events held near his Beverly Hills home, spending time with women of questionable character, and of course pissing away his money on booze. However, he eventually became bored and began to long to once again to be under the bright lights pissing off multitudes of rednecks with his mere presence. He sat before the massive screen of the television in his living room. He restlessly ran his fingers through his wavy blond locks as he pondered his fate. He knew he could easily file for free agency within Primetime Central, there was quite the market for former PTC Extreme Champions, thus finding a cure to his boredom. The only question left on his mind was what to do after signing with another organization. Would he enter a new organization amidst fanfare and quickly rise to the top, or would he be forced to work his way up from the bottom? He sighed as he decided that it mattered not where he was, he knew he couldn’t be on the bottom for too long...the cream always rises to the top. Mike’s thoughts were broken up by the sensation of his cell phone vibrating in his pocket. He reached into his pocket and produced the cell phone. Mike was slightly surprised when he saw that the number on the screen belonged to his manager, William Bastafar. Perhaps William was calling him with news of an unsolicited contract offer, a thought that caused a smile to form on Mike’s handsome face. “What is it William?” Mike asked as soon as he answered the phone. “I have news that is somewhat good lad,” William replied in his light Scottish accent. “What do you mean by somewhat?” Mike asked, his curiosity piqued. William paused to clear his throat. “My idiotic grandson has finally awoken from the coma caused by that blow to the head he took from Fnord. He appears to be healthy and within a few weeks he could be ready to resume his duties as your lackey,” William stated with an uncharacteristic happiness in his voice. “I need your permission to call the Primetime Central offices and declare your free agency. With Carlos back it will be just like old times lad!” Mike heaved a sigh of relief, his dilemma had been solved and it would only be a matter of days before he was once again the ring wreaking havoc. “Go ahead and get it done William,” Mike said before ending the call. Mike stood up and finally allowed his trademarked cocky grin to form upon his face. The time had come for him to once again reclaim his place within the world of professional wrestling and he couldn’t wait to start the journey to the top.
