Know Thy Enemy

14 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: #1 Evo 6/14/04 'Who this?' 'Guess who nigga.' 'Yo! What's up Dogg? About time you hollared at a brotha! Mr. Platinum Champ. My nigga.... We proud of you up up here baby, for real.' 'Appreciate that love man.' 'Bet. Hey yo, so when you coming home so we can do the shit up right?! Shit, we gone party like a muthafucka when you get back homie!' 'I'll be home in a couple of days. But it ain't gonna be to party. I need to talk to our boy in black man.' 'The boy in black? What you need to hollar at him for dogg?' 'I need him to do some research for me. I got a name I need him to look up.' 'Dogg...what's the deal man? What you into that you need talk to the boy in black? I thought you was out the game and...' 'Dogg, is this 20 questions or what? I need to hollar... You gonna set it up or what?' 'Hey yo, chill, chill man...damn... I'm just askin' cuz... Fuck it dogg, I'm yo' boy, you know this. Give me till tommorow. I'll set up a place and time so you can holla.' 'Good lookin' out man.' 'Bet.... But yo, on the real? Why you need this man, seriously? I thought you was out the game?' 'You know how this shit goes man... You can change the board, you can change the players...but the game goes on.' 'I feel you. Aight... so what's this name you need looked at?' 'Reno. Maverick Reno.' ===== ===== ===== The wind bayed like a wolf at the moon as it whipped through the concrete emptiness of the parking garage. The Chicago air was crisp and unusually cold for this time of year. The wind had plenty of open space to play in. Other than Train's rented Cadillac Escalade that sat parked in one the spaces at the dead center of the parking deck the place was basicly empty. And, aside from the occassional piece of paper being thrown about by the wind, was still at this time of night. The almost sickly green glow of the flourecent lights was fairly dim...not interfering with the bright, twinkling lights of the city as they stood in stark contrast to the darkness of night. Back in the day Train remembered his boys saying this place felt like a tomb. Cold. Isolated. Silent. That was one way to think about it. But it hadn't ever been his way. It seemed more like The Fortress of Solitude. Back in the day, when he needed a spot to stop and think...to just chill and be alone with his thoughts this was one of his favorite places. It was the perfect spot to hide out for a second. The garage was 10 stories and he'd always wind up on the 9th... Below the top level where helicopters could fly over and spy you with infrared or lowlight shit... Too high up for anyone taking pictures to get a good perspective as none of the surrounding structures, mostly four or five story office buildings, were nearlly as tall, offering no windows to leer out of. Train felt a wave of dejavu wash over him. He stood at the concrete wall that doubled as the railing for this open air structure observing the place that would always be his real home. Though it seemed to him there may be a few more than he remembered, the lights of the city shined about the same as they did the first time he stepped foot in the place. When he needed to recharge...to rethink...to remember what he was fighting for... why he'd acquired all that blood on his hands. It was easy to remember when he stood up here, staring out. The city. The world. It was his for the taking if he wanted it. The hell with being on the bottom rungs. He wanted it. It'd had been time to raise up back then. He'd come up now. Funny it all seemed so long ago. Hell, 'The Day' his mind drifted back to had only ended about six or seven months back. Seemed like half a lifetime for some reason. He could only assume that it was a result of how far he'd come in those few months. From Thug to champion. Yeah, right... Who was he kidding. He was still that Thug...he just happened to be a champion at the same time. The attitude was the same. Take what you want. Fuck whoever don't like it. They step up beat them down. They get up again fuck a hospital...send them to the morgue. But the game did have to be switched up a little. Now? You can't be sending people to the morgue with a pack of cameras stalking after you. Now he had to settle for the hospital as the ultimate destination for opposition. Of course that didn't mean he couldn't steer them towards the ICU. Oh, and he didn't tote a heater anymore. Not on a regular basis at least. But tonight wasn't regular...the weight of the nickel plated 9mm barely registered as it sat snuggly in the holster behind his back. It's not that Train thought the shit would go bad. There wasn't any solid reason it should or would. This was a straight info deal after all. Information was less dangerous to pass than bricks or guns...but that wasn't a guarantee. The height and seclusion of the parking garage wasn't just a prime location to be by himself...sometimes it was a place to meet away from prying eyes. A makeshift meeting hall that had seen it's fair share of high tension get togethers...no more than an inadvertant twitch away from becoming that tomb people had described it as. By this time it had probably been close to a year since the last time Train had seen the man he came here to meet. In the past he'd always been trustworthy. In the past he had always been worth the money Train paid for the information he'd pass on. He'd always been on the up and up...as far as crooks go. But that was in the past. Just like life had changed for Train life could've changed for him. Who knows what was going through his head...what he might have in mind. Especially since Train knew he'd be thinking the exact same thing about him. What did Train know? Maybe the boy in black had hooked up with one of Train's old enemies. He may have traded in the street game for the wrestling game, but some would still pay a pretty penny to know the next big event Train headlined was a closed casket funeral. What did the boy in black know? Maybe Train had partied a little too hard when he won his little title. Got busted for DUI with a gun or coke in the car. That's serious jail time, even for a celebrity. No doubt Train could hammer out a deal for no time by selling him down the river. Naw... The past never counts. Only the potential future. Train reached back and unsnapped the strap on the holster. The gun was free...and ready. Train quickly slid his hands back into the pockets of his wool Armani overcoat. It protected well against the unusual cool of the evening, not to mention went well with the black Armani slacks and black Armani silk shirt, topped of with the platinum cuff links. The famous Chicago wind continued to blow, playing with the fringes of the overcoat and Train's hair as it dangled in a tight ponytail. Had anyone from GW been there to see him they'd have likely said he was dressed out of character. They'd have been wrong. Half the relief Train felt setting off for the greener pastures of GW was finally getting to shed his designer armor. For a long time this was his uniform to exude authority...and malice. When he put it on people looked and understood. Understood that his ability to drop hundreds...maybe even thousands on something as simple as a shirt, jacket and pair of pants meant he had his come up. Meant he hadn't let anything or anyone stand in his way. Meant he was in charge. If they feared him he stayed safe. In GW Train didn't need to dress the part of the badass. He just did what he did best... He walked it. People who mouthed off got dropped where they stood. It was easier there. In GW he just needed to be the man. Here...he needed to be the boss. The armor was a burden...but it still fit like a glove. His eyes still firmly focused on the city as it stretched out in front of him, Train reached into his inner coat pocket and retrieved his 'Black n' Mild' cigar and a box of matches. He casually slid the plastic tip of the cigar between his lips and and struck a match against one of a dozen square, concrete pylons lining the walls of the parking garage, holding up the tons of steel and concrete. Train had to act quickly, sheilding the flame from the wind that threatened to extinguish it before finally raising it to the tip of the cigar. A few puffs of air and the cherry of the cigar roared to red hot life. The rich aroma of the pipe tobacco cigar lingers. A trail of smoke rings drift off into the air, only managing to go a foot or two before being caught up and ripped apart by the wind. Train took another puff before glancing at his Fossil watch. 11:28 p.m. The meeting was set for 11:30 and he'd never known the boy in black to be late. That worked fine for Train. He was anxious to get this thing underway. A couple of weeks back standing in the middle of this deserted place ready to have a secret meeting would have been uncomfortable. A couple of weeks ago he was still lugging around that burdensome notion that he wanted to change himself... to change what he was. But the second he slid that Platinum Title belt over his shoulder it all came into perspective. He finally realized the truth about his 'conversion' from asphalt to cotton canvas. That was a bunch of horse shit. He'd come too far and worked too hard to let anyone take what he'd accomplished and he'd do exactly what he'd done on the asphalt to keep what he earned on the canvas.... Whatever it takes. He didn't want to change himself. Just the byproducts of his profession. He didn't want to be the nice guy, just the guy who didn't have to worry about wire taps and search warrants. He didn't worry about returning to an old life...only about keeping the new one. The life of a champion. And now Train needed more information. He didnt' know anything more about Maverick Reno than he needed to. Even during The M.O.B.'s war with TUE Train had never had much cause to worry about Reno. So, before this match was booked, Train didn't know or care to know much more than what the rest of the world did. He knew he was an arrogant prick. The type of cat who seems to think that he has the God given right to talk down to anyone and exercises the right freely. Every movement he made... Every inflection in his voice... It all had one purpose... To let you know he thinks he's better than you. Mr. dark and brooding... Mr. deep and complicated. Bullshit. He was about as deep as the shallow end of the pool and as hard to see through as a pane of glass. There was nothing complicated about what was going down. Deacon Holmes couldn't get it done and High Noon and Mr. Reno, unable to live with the shame of one of the almighty TUE members losing, couldn't let it stand. God only knows how many favors he had to call in to get this match between Train and himself booked. Train had seen his type before. Now that the match was set...this was the part where Train was supposed to be scared. Shake in his boots... cower in fear. Fuck that. There was no doubt Reno was a true to life carnivore. Surly... Aggressive... A tough son of a bitch. Oh, but there would be no cowering. There would be no shaking in fear. It was time for Train to show just what he was made of...not all tough talk and attitude... Reno was a stepping stone and Train would step up. Train took another puff of his cigar and his mind continued to race over the challenge in front of him. It would be just that... A challenge. Train had an idea of what was probably running through Reno's head as Mr. Mysterious analyzed the upcoming conflict. If Train thought right he only had one question for the man.... 'Who's coming for who Mr. Reno?' Train asked himself quietly, giving a little chuckle as a retort to his own question. Another pull of air through the cigar and Train, not exhaling, simply allows the smoke to slowly drift from his mouth and nose...as if he was a beast and the smoke was the simple result of the fire that burned in his belly. His eyes glanced back down at his watch. 11:30 p.m. The boy in black should arrive momentarily. As if on cue a squeal, faint at first, the distinctive sound of rubber tires making tight turns on glossy, concrete surfaces, such as these parking decks were made of, echos through the whole structure. As the squeal grows louder so does the sound of the car's engine as it continues it's steady climb. It only takes a minute for the jet black Crown Victoria, it's darkened windows obscuring the driver, to finally emerge from the ramp on the far side of the parking floor and pull into the center of the structure, pulling to a stop right next to the Escalade. Train calmly turns, pulling another plume of smoke from the cigar as the car pulls to a stop. The headlights glare into his eyes for a second before the engine of the sedan switches off, the headlights immediately following suit. Train casually strolls in the direction of the new arrival, the smoke he blows being whipped around wildly in the everpresent wind. Train stops a few feet from the car. Finally the driver's side door of the Crown Vic pops open and a man emerges. The boy in black had arrived. He didn't look too much different from how Train remembered him. A white man...somewhere around 40, and in the neighborhood of six feet tall... The specks of gray hair were a little more prominent now. Tonight he had on his beige overcoat to protect against the cool air along with a matching beige/earthtown suit. As he emerged from the car Train tried to remember who'd coined the nickname 'Boy in Black'. If he was correct it was him. It really never had anything to do with the way the man actually dressed. It was just that when you thought about guys like this they had a certain uniform look. Cops had their uniforms, that's why they were the boys in blue. When you think of federal agents you think of your typical black suit. Even if they were on the take. 'Well, well, well...' began the new arrival, slamming the door of his ride behind him. 'Long time no see Train.' 'You ain't seen me?' Train responded. 'You need to turn on yo' tv man. I'm on there every Monday night.' 'Yeah, yeah... That's what I heard.' The boy in black responded. He just stared for a moment. Train knew he was trying to size up the situation. Trying to predict the future. 'haha... I got to tell you Train. I was surprised when I heard you were back in town. I heard you were a big time champion now. Figured you left this place behind for good. I just hope you're not back up to your old tricks.' Train just lowered his head, chuckling to himself for a moment. He draws from the cigar again and raises his eyes back to the man across from him. 'Don't play yourself with all that liberty and justice for all, servant of the people bullshit. You wanna check for the wire go ahead.' The man eyed Train silently, the suspicion bleeding from every pore. The boy in black slowly approached. Train simply held out his arms. The man paused for a moment, trying to comprehend Train's intent. Finally he begins the pat down. It only takes a moment for the man to search for listening devices. He finds none. But, he does find something tucked silently behind Train's back. The man's hand runs over the surface of the 9mm. He tries to pull it from the holster, but the second he does, Train's arm comes up and under his, riding up into the pit of his arm, locking it in place. The boy in black grimaces a little. 'There ain't no wire.' Train said. 'No need to push your luck.' Train released the pressure. The man wrenched his arm back, leaving the gun snug and secure, still in it's cradle. 'Same old Train,' The boy in black said, shaking out his arm to stem the pain. 'More or less.' Train replied. 'You don't wanna check for wires?' 'Why? I ain't doing nothing illegal. All I'm doing is asking you for a little information. How you got hold of it is on you.' For a few moments the echoing howl of the wind is the only sound they hear as they continue to size eachother up. It takes a moment, but both relax a little, finally concluding that this deal is as on the up and up as a situation like this gets. 'You know, I'm still having a hard time understanding why you and I are meeting Train. If this whole wrestling thing is legit why do you need me?' 'Know thy enemy.' 'Pardon?' 'If I was facing the typical cat in the ring I wouldn't need you. But I ain't facing the typical cat.' 'haha... Yeah, you can say that again.' 'I need you to tell me what you know uz I need to know how far I'm going to have to go to keep what belongs to me. So... you talk like you know something. What you got for me?' 'Depends on what you got for me.' Train slides the cigar between his lips again, freeing up his smoking hand. He squints his eyes as he puffs the cigar again, the smoke drifting back into his face as he takes the newly freed hand and goes into his other inner jacket pocket. A second later the hand returns, clutching a white envelope, half stuffed to the brim. Train tosses the envelope to the boy in black who picks it out of the air like Jerry Rice looking for a touchdown pass. In one smooth movement the boy in black catches and opens the envelope...slowly thumbing through the cash inside. 'You need to tell them F.B.I. cats to give you a raise or something man.' Train says mockingly. 'Talking with you is starting to get expensive.' 'Oh, I wouldn't dream of it,' he responds still thumbing through the addition to his wealth. 'Getting to do my civic duty is reward enough.' 'Of course it is. The money is all there. Give me what I paid for.' 'Alright, alright...calm down. I've got it right here...' he says, reaching into his pocket and pulling out a cd case...the name 'Reno' printed boldly in black across the front. 'But before I give this to you... I gotta ask. Where the fuck do you do this wrestling thing man? Redhook? Hell's Kitchen? Compton? Beriut maybe? I mean Jesus man...hahaha... This fucking guy, Reno... If I saw him coming down the street I'd cross to the other side just to avoid eye contact. I was gonna tell you what I knew until I saw all the crap I dug up. I decided to put it on disk cuz I don't have all night to go through it with you.' 'Then hit the highlights for me.' 'Highlights?' The boy in black asks, handing Train the cd case which he proceeds to tuck into his pocket. 'Ok, let's go through the highlights. I put this Reno guys name in the central system and got a ping from about every other agency around.' 'What do you mean?' 'F.B.I... D.E.A... A.T.F... L.A.P.D... N.Y.P.D... and police department databases in 12 other major cities in the US. Hell man I even got hits from Fish and Game and the C.I.A. which I don't have the authority to even look at. And then there are these guys he's got working for him. They're all part of his little corporation... The Upper...upperrrrrr...hand? Lip? Ahhhh...' 'Echelon.' 'Right. The Upper Echelon. All of these guys are bad news. Especially this John Zero character he has working for him.' 'What's so bad about Zero?' 'Technically? Nothing. I can't find much of a record on him. But see the record isn't the problem. It's the rep...or possible rep I should say.' 'Are you gonna just babble or are you gonna tell me what the fuck you're talking about?' 'There was only one John Zero that I was able to come up with anything about and he's basicly just a rumor. Word was there was an assassin that went by the name John Zero a while back. Nobody knows exactly who he is or what he looks like but the guy has dozens of confirmed kills by the Bureau's count. I mean this guy might as well be Keyser Soze's cousin or something. This guy only dropped off the scene after he whacked three mob bosses within the span of a few days. Normally I would assume he was retired cuz if you're smart you'll only take a job like that if you get enough to disappear, change your identity and never worry about money again. But that's normally. But this Reno guy...if someone could pull a guy like that out of retirement it would be him. 'You know you're a grown man and you make your own decisions, but whatever you're doing down there you might wanna rethink your strategy. I mean Jesus... I heard a while back You got involved with Terrelle Greeniso, while he was working there... who by the way, is a fucking mob boss in case you didn't know. One of the biggest in the country. He's got half the families on the east coast paying him tribute and the other half running scared. Now you're getting into it with this Reno character? hahaha... I don't get it man. How the helld did you try leaving the life and wind up as deep in it as you were before you left?' 'Just lucky I guess.' 'Yeah, I was just about to tell you how lucky you were. Look I got to head out. But a little free advice. You want out of the life? You need to disconnect with guys like this Reno character asap.' 'I'm working on it.' 'Right.' Train's informant heads back to his car and slides into the drivers seat with little effort. 'Watch your ass.' he said before closing the door and cutting the engine back on. Again Train stood in the glaring headlights of the car before it finally pulled around him and down the exit ramp. Train stands silent and stoic for a moment. Taking it all in. He raises his cigar back to his lips and spins on his heels. Heading back wall of the garage. Back to the lights. He stared at them again. They were like beacons, reminding him of the proper path. No fear. No mercy. No games. He'd suspected it from the begining, but it was basicly confirmed now. He'd have to pull out all the stops. This was going to be a war.
