The Writing is on the Wall

16 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/16/03 The scene opens up to a blurry, picture. The cameram zooms out a little bit, just enough to make out the picture. The picture if of the nose, mouth, and jaw features of a man. Small smears, of what appears to be blue, and red paint, appear on the man's face. Along with a few strands of blond hair. As the camera begins to zoom out again, the man, begins to speak. And the camera, stops before it even gets a chance to zoom out. 'Many of you don't know where is came from. A few of you do. But this time around, this sticks, and sticks for good. Many of you want to know what my agenda is here. Who or what am I after. Why have I graced this company with my presence. Men like Tyler Pierce, call themselves legends. Men like Blitz, say I have an ego. While men like Jake Simmons, know what I can do, and know what's best for them, and stay quiet. Before this time is over, however, All those types of men will hear my words, and my words will stick in their minds from now until their death. I am a man, who uses his words as another weapon. I am a man, who will tell you how it is. I am a man, who can talk my talk, and still back it up. I am a man, who when is speaking, is heard by everyone, whether they admit it, or not.' 'Now you Angus the Bull. What a catchy name. Could it possibly be, that the bull at the end of your name, actually stands for everything that comes out of your mouth? You however, use your words intelligently, and just don't run your mouth, to hear yourself talk. You seem sincere in all of your words. Such a pity, that you have to eat them, and learn just who is running things around here. If you run your mouth here, if you step into that ring, if you do anything in this company, eventually, you will answer to me. Angus, it will be a pleasure to step into my ring with you, and it will be a pleasure to see if you truly do have the skills everyone says you have. Because if you don't it will be a huge disappointment when I run ramped over you. However, if you should happen to possess the talent, you are said to have, then maybe, I might get the work out that I am looking for, before I finish you off.' 'Speaking of finishing something. Chris Canada. The only man in this match, to be silent the entire time of his run here. Maybe he's busy finishing off his jail sentence. Maybe he's finishing that extra bowl of spagehtti. Maybe he's even finishing his obitchuary, because he knows his time is running out. Whatever the case maybe, I have no idea, and quite frankly, I couldn't give two sh!ts about it either. The fact of the matter is this. Whether it is Chris Canada, or Angus the Bull, no one is going to stop me from what I have planned. No one, is going to get in my way, of my destiny. However, should any of you think about it, or actually get in my way, I will give you a little dose, of what is in store, for this entier federation.' The cameran pans up Ice's face, continuing with it's zoomed in close up. The camera stops, and locks in on the cold icy blue eyes, which Ice possesses. 'This is just the begining. Soon it will all be revealed. Look into these eyes, and see the focus. See the intesity. See the cold. Because in time, it will come for you. And when it does, there is no escaping its wrath. You want to know what my agenda is? You want to know what I'm talking about? You don't want to know bad enough.' The camera zooms out as Ice stands up from a small stool and leaves the room he was in. The room is all white, with splatters of blue and red paint on the white floor. The camera pans around the empty room, but stops as it sees paint on one of the walls. The scene fades as the camera zooms in and focuses on the writing on the wall. ' THE WRITING IS ON THE WALL. NO ONE CAN ESCAPE IT. IT IS YOUR FATE. ICE.'
