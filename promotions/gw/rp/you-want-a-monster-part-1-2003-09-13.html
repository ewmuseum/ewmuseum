You Want a Monster Part 1

13 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/13/03 The scene opened outside of the GW Arena, where many of the GW stars such as, Ken Partridge Jr., Gunner Addison, Jin Akiyara, Roz Blayze, John Zero, and even Christina McCoy herself, are leaving the arena, after doing the weekly, promotional interview every GW superstar is required to do. After the superstars wever finished with their portion of the interview Christina McCoy gave a small speach. After finishing her speech Christina thanked everyone for coming, and lead her group of stars off stage. As Christina and the group of stars were leaving, the crowd chanted ' JIN..JIN...JIN...JIN..' Jin waved and nodded in appreciation to the crowd before making his way into a long black limo. Slowly the crowd begins to disperse in a million directions. Like an army, the sea of people make their way back to their vehicles, and eventually to their homes. Technicians hang around and remove all the microphones, pull down the monitors, remove the speakers, and all other equipment that was used. Where a large mass of humanity once was, is now an empty nothing. The technical crew pack up their things, and begin to pack up their trucks and vans. The cameraman finishes taping some extra footage, for a soon to be released GW special behind the scences DVD documentary, and turns to unplug his camera, and head home. With the tape still rolling, the cameraman walks back, following his cord, and winding it up as he goes. Finally he reaches outlet, and reaches down to unplug his extention cord. Just as he wraps his hand around the plug, a hand firmly grips his wrist. The cameraman jumps from the unexpected grip. The camera frantically pans up to see Ice coldly staring at the camera. Methodically Ice shakes his head side to side, and slowly releases his grip from the cameraman's wrist. Cautiously the cameraman removes his hand from the plug. The picture begins to bobble a little, as the cameraman begins to tremble. Ice turns and looks over the now empty area where his Jin Akiyara was recently. A calm expression engulfs Ice's face. The camera looks over Ice standing in his black sneakers, blue jeans, and jet black overcoat. Ice raises his right hand and scratches the underneath side of his chin. The scratching slows, and Ice's gaze does not deture. 'Where the masses once stood, praising their god, is now an empty abyss of of nothingness.' Ice's right hand slowly slides down his chin, down his neck, and across his chest ending back on his side. His chest heaves out as he takes in a long deep breath. Ice holds the long deep breath, tilts his head back, and stares up at the ceiling above. Loudly Ice exhales his long deep breath, and continues to gaze up at the ceiling. Ice slowly begins to lower his head, and turn it directly at the camera. 'Now though, those mindless sheep may follow you around like you are their shepperd. And they may scream your name like you are someone important. And they may even follow your every moves like disciples to their god. Get one thing straight, you are nothing. NOTHING! You have held that title for quite some time now, but please inform me, who have you defended it against? You see, you are either in a nontitle contest of some sort, or I have had my way with you, left you laying in a pool of your own blood, and you are unable to compete. Ice turns away from the camera and watches as a janitor starts to clean up the garbage and debree left by the mass of people that were there eariler. Ice watches the janitor sweep up for a while. Ice turns back towards the camera and smirks. 'Christina McCoy, again, thinks she is going to use me for her own purposes. She thinks that I am just going to sit back, and let her lead me around like she leads you around by your jock. Well Christina, you are completely wrong. Kid Flash and Myself are going to walk into our match and I'm going to have my way with Gunner, Ken Partridge, and Jin Akiyara. Kid, he is going to take care of Akodo Rokku, and the Souls. Then, when I'm finished having my fun, I'm going to go back to my locker room, sit back, relax for just a bit, and then I'm going to come back out, and put on one more show. Not because you say I have to. Not because those ingrateful fans want to see it. But because I enjoy the fact that I get to walk out, and enforce more punishment on another helpless soul.' Ice turns his head to the left, and cracks his neck. He turns it back to the right and does the same. 'Chirstina, you want your ratings. You want a MONSTER!' Angrily Ice places his hands on top of his head, and scrapes them down his face. Frusterated Ice exhales a deep breath. 'I live in the most intense environment imaginable. You, this company, know nothing of the places I experience on a daily basis. You know nothing of intense environments. But you will....SOON. Soon, all your nightmares, all those horror stories you heard, they are going to become the environment you live in. They are going to be the daily experiences you have. As the heat that we experience now succumbs to the cool and the cold, the world you know right now, the world you experience on a daily basis, will slowly fade away into the darkness. The things you know, that you are used to, that comfort you, will soon fade away, and then YOU WILL experience the most intense environment imaginable. Remember this world is a cold, dark place. This world, that you claim, is not what you think it is. And soon you will realize the truth, and the truth shall set you free.' Ice removes his jet black coat to reveal a white t-shirt with blue block letters across the chest that reads....'WELCOME TO MY WORLD'. Ice tosses his coat on the camera, and the scene goes to black.
