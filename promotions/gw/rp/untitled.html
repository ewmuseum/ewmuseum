It was Friday night at the Dark Horse. That meant Karaoke night. Brick often avoided Karaoke night like the plague. Truth be told, his wasn't exactly the best singing voice. It was the reason he only managed singers. But this was one time he wanted to be here for his friend Ely Braxton. When he arrived, one of the regulars was on stage performing his rendition of 'A Little Less Conversation' by Elvis Presley. Come on baby I'm tired of talking Grab your coat and let's start walking Come on, come on Come on, come on Come on, come on Don't procrastinate, don't articulate Girl it's getting late, gettin' upset waitin' around A little less conversation, a little more action please All this aggravation ain't satisfactioning me A little more bite and a little less bark A little less fight and a little more spark Close your mouth and open up your heart and baby satisfy me Satisfy me baby 'Now this is a shock,' a voice said. Brick turned to see Daisy headed towards him. 'You usually skip Karaoke night.' 'Hey, Daisy. Yeah, I do, but I figured I'd show up for Ely. He get here yet?' 'He's about to go on after this guy.' 'Let's hear it for Dwayne,' said the emcee as Dwayne left the stage. 'Coming to the stage next, Ely Braxton.' Ely walked up onto the stage and got behind the microphone. I fall to pieces Each time I see you again I fall to pieces How can I be just your friend You want me to act like we've never kissed You want me to forget Pretend we've never met And I've tried, and I've tried, but I haven't yet You walk by and I fall to pieces Even if Ely hadn't been going through his heartache with Gunner, a Patsy Cline song was definitely a tearjerker. I fall to pieces Each time someone speaks your name I fall to pieces Time only adds to the flame You want me to find someone else to love Someone who'll love me too The way you used to do. But each time I go out with someone new You walk by and I fall to pieces You walk by and I fall to pieces There was hardly a dry eye in the room. Even Brick started to tear up some. Ely got a rousing applause from the regulars as he stepped off the stage and headed back to the bar. 'Didn't think you'd show up here tonight.' 'Heard you'd be down here, so I came to see how you were doing. That is one depressing song.' 'Fits what I'm goin' through. Good way to let it out. So why don't you go up on stage an' try your luck?' 'Come on, Ely,' said Brick. 'You know I can't sing.' 'I can't sing, either. I got up there. Karaoke's Japanese for 'tone deaf'. Bet you didn't know that.' 'Just the same, I don't feel like singing.' 'OK, normally I wouldn't say this to you, but I'm gonna say it anyway. Brick? You're a pussy.' This comment caused the Rottweiler to rise out of his seat. 'The fuck--did you just call me a pussy?!' 'Yes, I did.' Brick then turned to Jack the bartender and asked, 'Did he just call me a pussy?!' Jack nodded his head and went, 'Mm-hmm.' 'I'm a pussy, huh?' 'Yes, you are.' 'Jack, where do I sign up?' 'Right by the stage,' the bartender replied, nodding his head towards the stage. Brick immediately took his cue to head towards the stage. 'Pussy? I'm a pussy?' Brick muttered under his breath. 'I'll show you who's a pussy.' 'Well, folks, this is a surprise,' said the emcee. 'A first timer here at Dark Horse karaoke night. Show your love for Brick, everyone.' The entire saloon applauded as Brick walked onto the stage and took the mic.
