Don Terrelle Greeniso Now Godfather Greeniso. Terrelle Green Global Wrestling Promo #854 Date: 1/7/04 Location: Cuban Island(Greensio Providence) he Greeniso Providence the small island of three Terrelle got from Padrino is Terrelle's center of his operation. Deep into the tropical trees on a hill you can see Terrelle's Mansion designed like a Greek Palace surrounded by trees and below it lie little houses and dirt roads. Around the bend is a huge factory with houses aroun it also boats leaving in and out nonstop as men carry out boxes marked fish. Inside the house sits Terrelle in his theater room where he sits down alone eating spagetti and drinking red wine. He looks at the Movie theater screen in his house watching The Godfather Series. His guards positioned at all sides by the door and the room only with one chair. 'Don Greeniso sir.' says The Guard. 'Yes.' says Terrelle. 'There is someone who wishes to see you.' 'Who is it?' 'They say it is urgent it is the Bolivian Secretary of Defense Sosa Mavtia.' 'Let him in.' Terrelle hands his food to the columbian servant and the other women servant whips his mouth and hands as Sosa walks into the door. He walks straight over to Terrelle and stands looking at him. 'What is it good news I hope.' says Terrelle. 'Don Greeniso I send my most humble apologies for not coming to your welcomehome party back in your hometown. But I was busy with things back hom and I have been training my men year around.' says Sosa. 'What do you want Sosa?' 'Don Greeniso I need to ask a favor of you our country is suffering over this Japanese corperation rising. We have not the funds and the military power to hold them from doing so. If you would greatly tell them to please leave us be there will be no trade problems nor will they be any other interactions when they pick up loads. They can use Bolivia for there running ground as long as this corperate takeover doesnt effect my people.' (Terrelle nods.) 'For this favor you ask of me it will require a lot on my part my friend. I dont know what to do you have to give me a favor to get a favor my friend.' 'Don Greeniso I will give you two hundred thousand american dollars.' 'I dont want your money I want something more than that I need protection can you provide that for me?' 'Yes I can send one hundred of my best men over here.' 'I was thinking more along the line of one hundred fifty of you finest.' 'Done.' 'Your wish will be granted.' 'Thank you Don Greeniso I will not waste anymore of your time.' 'Thank You.' Sosa kisses Terrelle's ring and walks out of the room and Terrelle waves his hand to his men and they come and stand behind him. 'Yes sir Don Greeniso.' asks The Guard. 'You know the leader of the messenger for the Triads.' aks Terrelle. 'Yeah boss.' 'Tell him that thing I told him to do.' 'Yeah.' 'Dont mind it.' 'Sure thing Don Greeniso.' 'Oh and also please tell him that tomorrow is the day that I will pay to have there Founding Fathers released.' 'They have fathers?' 'Not there actual father the fathers of the old triad gang they have made a deal and I promised them that if it was reasonable then they would get them back.' 'What was the deal?' '....Dont ask me another question now leave me!' The guards walk out of the room as they walk out Terrelle's cousin Micheal arrives in the room. He sits in a pull out chair next to Terrelle. Terrelle looks at him and kepps watching the movie. 'Cousin.' says Micheal. 'Yes.' says Terrelle. 'The chinese General has yet agreed to the five hundred million dollars to release the four fathers.' 'Of course they have.' 'But if these men are so dangerous cousin whjy would they just let you buy their freedom?' 'Listen I have always told you there are two things that can control the world. Money cause money can take to the top and give you power beyond your dreams. Then there is attitude with your attitude if you are soft then you get rolled all over. But if you can learn to be strong stand your ground then there is no way that any man can touch you.' 'Yes but why are we doing this for the Triads they dont like us and we hate them. I mean we are having them get power over us by doing this.' 'Again Micheal it isnt about what happened between us that was buisiness. They had a problem with me and I had to resolve it they know that day I could have killed each and every last one of them in that room. But you know what counts....I didnt they did what I would have done they had a problem and they confronted a problem. I mean they paid me a heavy fee but it is about respect I am going to give them what they want so that we can be united.' 'Tell me what are you getting out of this?' 'Listen I am getting more than any man has ever tried I am joining together everything. With the Triads on my side I can control a real deadly family. The four fathers wanted this when my father was around but he was to afraid of the possibilities. With me in the lead we have no limits we can be unstoppable. I want you to stand next to me as a brother Micheal you are wise and I need you to help me. When I am gone to this GW thing I want you to take over. I mean not permenent just for this little day I want you to take the money to the general I want you to get the Fathers and bring them back here.' 'Why here?' 'Because this is where they will stay they can not go back to China or they will be captutred again. I want them here cause here they can control the Triads and I can control all of them. I want more than power over our family and these streets. This is no longer just The Greeniso Familia I have decided that it will be called The Greeniso Triad Dynasty. I have accomplished one of my goals and once I get this done I will move on to the next faze.' 'What?' 'I dunno yet Micheal everything is a vision I see it in my dreams when I sleep. I want to have more power than the president of the United States. I know it will never be as that but I have to have a goal. I plan on opening a stock market and you will run that for me all I ask of you is to never betray me Micheal.' 'I will always honor you Godfather these men honor you and the people honor you. I will never betray you I put my life on it cousin.' Terrelle leans over and grabs his face and kisses his cheek and smiles. 'You dont know the potential you have Micheal you are no longer just Micheal from now on you are Made I have Made you just now. Now you will be giving the name Don Micheal make people respect you. Never let no one deprive you of what you stand for.' 'But you are The Don.' 'No I am The Godfather I have recieved word that Grandfather passed the other day.' 'What?' 'He didnt get killed he laid in the bed and died tomorrow I will go and this will be the most sacred event the world has ever seen. I will be dubbed The New Godafather you will be The Don.' 'So that means that The Greeniso Manison in Ruben is yours huh?' 'Yes Micheal.' 'I am 20 years old and I am a Don.' 'Yes you are a Don.' 'Have you seen Joe Giovanni does he know?' 'He will be there tomorrow to pay his respect to Grandfather and I will be passed The Ring. Now Micheal your cousin wants to be left alone and I want you to go to America to my Casino and have fun. This is a celebration for you and you will take my seat as Don over the Greeniso Family you control them make wise choices.' 'Godfather you give up the family?' 'Never but with this rank I am no longer to be involved with killings and things of the sort. I mean I liked doing it but thats why you are the Don of the family all things will be handled by you. I will advise you and you will listen and tomorrow I will give you your blessin in front of the family. I know this is a responsibility but you are the only one pure enough to take it. I am starting to realize that when I was younger I wanted the power to much and I did everything I could to get it. With the Fathers of The Triads returning I have to take my place at the top and no longer can I be The Don. I dont just control The Greeniso Family anymore I will take a seat next to The Fathers and Become one of The Founding Five Fathers of The Greensio Triad Dynasty. You must understand that this is for the best and The Family will be better now that you are under command.' 'What about this Island?' 'This island has been decided along with the others to be given to each of the fathers as a gift. I must return to my country and take over the place of Grandfather. 'You still gonna wrestle arent you?' 'Yes.' 'How you have a huge burden now?' 'Micheal I am still the same man I will make it to GW and pound on a few people. The thing is now I just have to make a extra trip. So you handle the Fathers tomorrow and send them to there islands and I will get ready for the GW.' 'Godfather I came to tell you GW is under investigation.' 'WHAT?' 'The FBI was searching the GW after that show.' 'How do you know and I didnt?' 'I watch TV.' 'Yes you are right what did they say?' 'They just talked to Christina McCoy.' 'Well on Monday I will pay her a visit.' 'Well I must be going I have to get this stuff together.' Terrelle stands face to face to Micheal he smiles and gives him a hug. 'Micheal be careful its ruff out there dont let no one push you around. Let no one get in your way of buisiness if they do you know what to do.' 'Make them an offer they cant refuse.' Terrelle smiles and Micheal walks out and Terrelle puts on his jacket and siganls his guards who come in the room. They walk out to the helipad together as Terrelle steps inside a jet and his cousin inside Terrelle's old helicopter. Terrelle gets in followed by ten guards and jumps on the jet. 'Where are we going?' says The Pilot. 'Ruben.' says The Head Guard. 'Please buckle up Godfather.' says The Pilot. The men strap down Terrelle as Terrelle looks out the window at his cousin. He then pulls down the shade as the helicopter takes off towards the US. 'They have arranged the transfer?' asks Terrelle. 'Yes the General will meet Micheal in California and the triads will meet him at the shore. He will be well protected by the triads and our men.' says The Head Guard. 'Have they gotten set for the Funeral?' 'Yes.' 'Good.'
