Search For Your Star (RP #1 For April 17th)

8 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/8/03 Stuck on the end of this ball and chain And I'm on my way back down again Stood on the edge, tied to the noose Sick to the stomach -Coldplay He raises his head towards the midnight sky. Dozens upon dozens of stars surround the night sky. Oh, such a beautiful masterpiece created up above. Americans have taken this for granted. But not him, he has cherished it since the first time he laid eyes on his star. And he has strived to become what he wants to be from the very beginning. When he was young, he would look up late at night and dream about his future. He would look up at that same star each and every nighttime and it would push him to accomplish this dream. That dream has become a goal for him. And that goal has become reality due to his hard work. For he has made something of himself, he has made more than many people have made of themselves in the Global Wrestling. He has already captured the GW Platinum Title and he is the current GW Cruiserweight Champion of the World after he defeated Jay Robson in a Ladder match. He has already accomplished so much already. But he doesn’t stop living to the best of his ability. It is his natural instinct. He looks around in the night sky. He feasts his eyes upon one particular star and he smiles. A childhood smile, the same smile an American would have on Christmas morning. Or the same smile that a grown man would have when they witness the birth of their child. The star has a wonderful and marvelous glow. A glow that has lasted throughout his whole entire life. Oh, such a beautiful star, and this star fuels his passion to give every drop of sweat he has inside of him. This is his star’and nobody else’s. “There you are my star. This week will be an easy victory. You have pushed me through these hard times over the past couple weeks. I defeated Jay Robson for the Cruiserweight Title. The Nevermore beat Silent Wind down to his knees, begging for forgiveness as his blood stained the ring mat. And tonight when The Nevermore defeated Brick, Jesse Briggs, and Ely Braxton. I have beaten the living hell out of 3 legends in the Global Wrestling in Jay Robson, Silent Wind, and Jesse Briggs. Hopefully star, hopefully I have opened some eyes other than Salaok. And hopefully I have sent the GW superstars a message during these past couple weeks. I hope a small wind of fear blows over them when my name is spoken.” He chuckles as a small gust of wind blows past him? “But instead of getting the opportunity I so deserve, I am granted a match against Roz Blayze. Granted” More like a chore. It won’t be long to dispose of this waste of talent. That is why Salaok put me in a match with him. Maybe when I’m done with Blayze, he will realize that he is pathetic and never step into the squared circle again and give us all a favor.” He pauses once again and looks up at the star’speaking each word to the night sky? “But I am going to take this match like I take every match. I am not going to give Blayze the chance to capitalize on anything at all. Simply because I do not make mistakes. Blayze, you are a stepping stone in my career. You aren’t getting me anywhere at all, I am just going to prove myself at Evolution. I am going to prove to each audience member to each wrestler in the locker room why I am who I say I am.” He takes a deep breath of the cool refreshing air? “Then maybe I will get an opportunity at something bigger. Such as the GW International Title or the GW Heavyweight Championship of the World. Blayze, you are the #1 Contender for the International title and you couldn’t get the job done, that is why I am here. To beat you, so I can get my shot to become something. Blayze, I told you that you were stepping stone.” He pauses. “Roz, what did you want to be when you grew up” Did you want to be a doctor? Or did you want to be a pilot? Did you want to be a firefighter or the starting Quarterback on a Championship Football team? Whatever you wanted to be, your plans didn’t help because you are nothing at all. Blayze, do me a favor, please. Look up into the night sky.” He browses the stars in the sky. “Then find your star, Blayze. I have already found mine.” He continues to look around. He lifts his finger in the air and points at a particular star and moves his finger up and down. The star begins to glow and shine brighter then dimmer, brighter then dimmer. “That one, Blayze. That shall be your star.” He puts his hand down to his side. “Now concentrate on your special star in the sky. Now study it for a couple of seconds.” He puts his finger and his thumb around his chin and pauses for a couple seconds and thinks about the star’studying every inch. “Now cherish that star up in the sky, Blazye. Because you will never be one.” He chuckles’the same sadistic chuckle we are used to hearing from him. This only means something horrible for the future of Roz Blayze. The camera picks up a bit of static and the camera cannot control it. It fades out to blackness and leaves reading? The Nevermore
