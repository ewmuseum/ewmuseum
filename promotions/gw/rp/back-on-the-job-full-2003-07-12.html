Back on the Job-Full

12 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Back on the Job-Full Tony Stecchino Global Wrestling Promo #413 Date: 7/12/03 Location: Vancouver, BC “It’s been such a long time coming. Months in the making. But sometimes good things come to those who wait and then again, those who do wait, get left in the dust. It’s simple as that. You really cant go wrong with killing either.” Tony Stecchino, the infamous wrestling mobster is sitting in the front seat of a black Lincoln Town Car. He slides a pair of black gloves on to his hands, he checks each finger, to make sure it is fit snugly into the pockets of the glove. They are Italian leather. “I have left for so long, I no longer really have anyone to do the hard jobs for me. No more goombas to bound at my every demand, leap at my every word. Thanks to George W. Bush, America has gone to shit and every wise guy from San Francisco to New York City has had to resort to shoveling fish for a living. I am gonna break that real soon. More or less right now.” Stecchino opens up the door to the car and steps out… He is in a black suit, with a long over coat. He steps across the drive pavement, to a glass door. He opens the door and walks in. The hallway is completely silent. Stecchino walks down the long hallway, a faint light is seen, just in the distance. Soon he reaches the light, which opens into a large room, with a security desk and black security guard asleep at the job. Something you would see right out of a movie. “Wake up” Security guard groggily, “what?” “I don’t pay you to sleep son!” “Mr. Stecchino. I was, I was…” “You were nothing! You could be fired if you do it again.” “I need this job.” “I need dedicated persons to help me run this business. Everything has to be smooth, like oil, like clockwork, no one can slack off in any position. Each position is as important as the next. Got it?” “Got it sir,” he replied. “OK. Good. Has anyone come in tonight at all?” “No sir, you are the first in seven hours sir.” “You sure?” “Yes sir.” “One hundred percent?” “I have probably been asleep for ten minutes or so. Conan got over and I was, just woken up by you sir.” “OK, I trust you. It’s all our lives are in your hands. Some one gets by, I could be dead in five minutes.” “Sir, I wont let anything happen to you.” “Good.” Stecchino looks at the security guard and then walks to the left of the desk and down into another dark hallway. Soon two golden doors open up and Stecchino steps in. The room is completely black. “God, I don’t trust this thing.” WHOOOOOSH!!! “Thank god, I am still alive!” The lights slowly come on and Stecchino is sitting behind a large, dark stained oak desk. There is a large window behind him, with the lit up skyline of downtown Vancouver, British Columbia. To each side of the windows are large ficas plants, freshly watered. On the desk are some papers, an IN and OUT basket and a computer. Stecchino reaches down into one of the drawers and pulls out a cell phone. “That’s where I left this thing.” Stecchino shakes his head with a smile and tucks the phone into is right inner jacket pocket. Stecchino kicks back and looks upon the ceiling and places his feet up on the desk. He begins to speak aloud. “I love this place. Brand new office, in some brand new digs. America is such a waste of time, George Bush is running that damn place right into the ground. I am surprised no one else has bombed it. He is so bad. No work. For anyone! CANADA! The new land of opportunity. Their dollar almost as much as America’s… So close its not even funny.” The phone rings. Stecchino reaches over and presses the speaker phone button. “Yes.” “We have a situation sir.” “Roger, what is it?” “Sir, it’s him sir.” “You mean, him, him?” “Yes sir. It’s him. He wants to talk to you about Evolution.” “He taking the swooshie thing?” “He wanted to take the stairs. Work on his wording or something.” “OK, thanks Roger.” “Shall I shoot him in the back sir?” “Nah, let him up here. He has an appointment.” “OK.” Stecchino presses the button again and the phone hangs up. He leans back again. “Evolution is such a step forward for me. I get to be in the International Title tournament and beat that punk Salomane. I don’t like that guy. I really don’t. But The Upper Echelon. Do I or don’t I?” There’s a knock on the door. “That was fast……. COME IN.” The door opens and a man steps in holding a briefcase. Face unseen. Stecchino has a bit of an uncomfortable look on his face. “All business right, no violence?” “Don’t worry Anthony, all business.” “Good, cuz you know what I can do to you.” “And I as well.” “Hmmm. Point taken. Take a seat.” The man walks over to the desk and sets the briefcase down on it. He opens it, revealing stacks upon stacks upon stacks of hundred dollar bills. “I love the smell of money don’t you?” “I’m nothing like you Anthony.” “I know you are nothing like me. That’s why this proposition seems weird to me.” “It’s not that fitting for me either, but for the good of the group. The best of the best.” “Sit, lets talk.” The man sits down. His black leather overcoat flows out of the chair. Stecchino looks up at him and speaks again. “So how’s Melissa?”
