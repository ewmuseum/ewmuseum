The Memory Remains #31

27 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22704 I rolled over in bed and threw my arm around Victoria. My arm only hit mattress though. My eyes creaked open. Beside me was an unoccupied space in the bed. Odd. I slowly unraveled myself from the covers and blankets and rolled out of bed. The tile was cold on my bare feet. I quickly snatched up a robe and put on my black fuzzy slippers. What? Can a man like me not own a pair of fuzzy slippers? Fucking floor gets cold in the morning. Quietly padding my way out of the bedroom, I began to look over the house. The upper floor was empty. As well as the lower living room, kitchen, office, and sitting rooms. Where the hell could she be. Outside, the cool air met my warm body. I shivered slightly. Hurriedly, I went across the lawn and let myself into Deac’s pool house. The place was practically brand new and was already in a state of disrepair. From the previous night’s partying, D was passed out on his black leather sofa, laying on it the opposite way that you would sit, his head pointed to the floor, mouth agape, a glass in one hand and a empty bottle of crown in the other. A steady snore escaped from his lips. Rhaiza was nowhere in site. I nudged Deac with my hand. Nothing. I nudged him a little bit harder. Still nothing. I thought about it for a minute. “Deac, wake up man,” I said, shaking his body. “Deac! Get up man!” I thought about it for a second. “Oh my god, we’re outta Crown!” His snoring jerked, and he sat bolt upright. “Wha” Out of crown? I got more, ladies, I swear!” He looked around and saw that I was the only one in the room. I couldn’t help but laugh. “Man, that shit ain’t funny! Make a brotha think that we outta Crown.” He held up the empty Crown Royal bottle. “Shit! We are outta Crown. Of all the motherfucking shit!” He tossed the bottle down on the ground, looking back at me. “So, what’s so important that it couldn’t wait until another coupla hours” Shit man, its still daylight! You have any fucking clue how much beauty rest this brotha needs?” “A lot,” I said, half-snickering. “Man, fuck you and the goddamn horse you rode in on. So what in the blue fuck do you want?” “You seen Vic, D?” “A booty call” You woke me up, while its still daylight out, cause you’re looking for a piece of ass? Fuck you, man, I’m going back to sleep!” Deac stood up from the couch and started to walk away. I grabbed him by the shoulder. “D, that’s not it. We went to go get the cars, and Vic wasn’t with us cause she went shopping. She hasn’t come back.” Deac stopped in his tracks and looked at me. “That was three days ago, John.” “I know. Vic’s a tough chick, hard as nails. But?” Deac nodded. “Yeah yeah, I know I know. Lemme get woke up, and we’ll find her.” I nodded my thanks to Deac and went back to the house. I renewed my search again. First stop was the front yard, this go round. Sitting on the ground at the welcome mat was a small brown package. Picking it up, I felt around the edges. It felt like a videotape. Tearing the edge off carefully, I slowly opened the package. Inside sat a tape, just like I thought. Pulling it out, I took it over to the TV and slid it into the VCR. On the screen, I saw an image of Victoria, returning to a limo, her arms full of packages. A group of men surrounded her. My fists tightened. Vic played them up, acting scarred and frightened, before going into action. My girl. Fists flew; kicks were lashed out without a thought of mercy. She was kicking ass and taking names until one of the brutes blindsided her with a blackjack. She crumpled down to the ground. My girl. I picked up the coffee table and hurled it threw the pane glass sliding door. It landed right at Deacon’s feet. He looked at the table, at the door, and finally to me. “You know, you’re right. I never did like that table in that room. It looks much better out here,” he stepped through the broken door. “What the fucks the matter with you?” In silence, I rewound the tape and played it for him. “Ah shit! Any clue who those fuckers are?” “I’ll give you three guesses, but I’m sure you’ll only need one.” Both of us spoke that the same time. “Vincent.” I turned sharply on my feet and headed towards my office. Deac must’ve seen the look in my eye, because he followed after me, close on my heels. “What the fuck you thinking, John?” I stopped, turned and stared into Deacon’s eyes. I let them boil over with rage. The world started to turn red. I kept them like that and held Deac with them for several seconds that felt like minutes. “What do you think, D?” “I think you’re gonna go fuck “em all up bigtime.” I gave a tight nod. “That’s what they want me to do. Who am I to let them down.” I turned again and started off for the office again. Once there, I went behind my desk and tagged a hidden button. The wall to the right jutted out slightly. Going to the wall, I pulled out, revealing a large, glass covered gun cabinet. Each gun was set inside a carved out section of foam. I opened a large metal door that was also revealed. Reaching in, I pulled out an all black assault uniform. Deacon walked in seconds later and stared. “I’m going after her D. I don’t expect you to come with me?” I didn’t get a chance to complete the sentence. Deac bounded across the small space and shut me up with a fierce backhand. If it had been anyone else, they already would be dead. “Will you just shut the fuck up” You don’t expect me to fucking come? Fuck you, Z. Fuck you. We’ve been partners a helluva long time, and you’re gonna lay this shit down on me? Oh hell fucking no. We’re partners, Z. Partners. I’m coming with you on this fucked-up little excursion. You’re my boy, John. And I sure as hell ain’t gonna sit here while they play pin the bullet on the dumb white boy’s ass. We’re gonna go get them motherfuckers. We’re gonna get them good.” I nodded at him and tossed the assault uniform at him. “We’re gonna do this my way though, D. After this afternoon, John Vincent will no longer be a threat to us or anyone.” I pulled another black assault uniform from the closet and put it on, tightening all the appropriate straps. Over the uniform, I snapped a special shoulder holster in place. The holster allowed me to carry four guns, two under each arm. From the gun cabinet, I pulled out four empty glocks. From a storage cabinet, I pulled out several magazines and started filling them with bullets. Not the normal slugs. No, not this time. Special little party favors. Hollow points. And within each hollow point? Mercury. That made every bullet wound a showstopper. The slightest scratch became a deathnail. The magazine’s full, I slapped one into each glock, chambering a round and putting them in place. “So, we gotta plan or anything for this lil trip, John?” “Sure do.” “Wanna share with the help?” “Yeah. Hand me that block of C4, would ya?”
