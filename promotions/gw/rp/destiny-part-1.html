Date: today 'Things are going to change. Your life is not what it used to be. You are something more now. You aren't on the same level as the rest of them. You are part of the elite. Being apart of the elite, has many responsibilities. Sometimes favors we ask of you...' He continues to ramble on about something, but Ice is not paying attention to the words piercing through the darkness of the room. Ice sits on the black leather couch in the middle of the room, gazing out in front of him, into the nothingness of the darkness. His eyes are glazed over, as if he has been hyptnotized. The voice emerges from the darkness. Soma walks over to Ice, and clamps his hand on Ice's right shoulder. Ice's trance is broken, as he turns and looks up at Soma standing above him. A disturbing expression glares from Soma's face, as he stares down at Ice. 'I suggest you get whatever thoughts you have running around in your head out, and out soon, because what I have to you, you need to know. Not because I like to hear myself speak, but because it is for your own well being.' Ice cockily snickers at Soma's comments. He brushes the cold hand from his shoulder, and stands up away from the couch. Ice walks around the end of the couch, and stands face to face with Soma. Ice is still wearing the cocky grin, from his earlier snickering as he stares into the empty eyes of the man standing before him. 'I'm glad to see that I have your FULL ATTENTION. Now back to what I was sa...' 'No. I'm done listening to you and your constant rambling. This isn't and wasn't part of the understanding, as I saw it. I don't sit, I don't listen, and I don't take orders from ANYONE. Not you, not the McCoys, not anyone. I haven't before, and I am not going to start now. So you can continue to talk all you want, but I'm going to go home. WHY? Because I CAN.' Ice turns and begins to walk away, but is stopped as Soma reaches out and grabs Ice's left arm. Ice's head tilts back, and he inhales loudly. Just as loudly as he inhaled, his breath leaves his body. Slowly Ice turns back towards Soma, to see the monster grinning in delight. Ice yanks his arm away from Soma's grip, and turns again to walk away from him. This time Ice is not stopped physically by Soma, but as Ice's hand turns the doorknob, Soma's voice bellows throughout the room, and Ice is stopped by his words. 'You are learning quickly. Soon you will be who you are destined to be. You can't stop it, so why do you keep running?' Ice opens the door to the room, and walks out into the dark hallway. 'DING' An elevator door opens and Ice enters. Almost instantly Ice is taken from the floor he was on, to the first floor. He walks through the lobby, and heads into the parking lot and into his black Expedition. Ice starts the vehicle, and the engine revs as Ice stares through the windshield, up at Soma's figure standing at the window. The vehicle pulls out of the parking lot, and drives away. FADE OUT.......... FADE IN.......... Sitting alone in his hotel room, Ice sits, on the green couch that sticks out from the side of the room into the middle of the room. Hanging over the back of the couch is Ice's GW World Championship Belt. Ice is sitting back against the couch, with his head down, as if he were staring at something. He lets out a deep sigh, and stands up from the couch. In his right hand is a silver cell phone. Ice pushes in the numbers, and walks across the room, to a small table, with a picture of Star. He places the phone to his ear, and picks up the picture with his free hand. 'C'mon...C'mon....pick up.' Ice gazes at the picture, in a loving manner. After a few moments he gently places the picture back in its place on the small table, and pulls the phone away from his ear. Ice leans back, and taps the phone against his chin. Again his eyes glaze over, as he drifts away into a trance like state. The tapping of the phone against his chin slows to a tap....tap.....tap.... like water dripping from a faucet. When suddenly the silence, along with Ice's trance, is broken by a knock at his door. Ice lets out and angered sigh, as he pushes himself away from the wall, and towards the door. Again, the knock is hear, and becomes louder as Ice slowly, unwantingly, makes his way over to the door. While walking Ice mumbles to himself. 'I thought I told them I wanted to be bother by no one. Stupid mother...' He turns the knob and opens to door. A look of astonishment flows from his face. His eyes widen, and his face turns pale, even more pale that it was before, almost as if he has seen a ghost. 'Hey....'
