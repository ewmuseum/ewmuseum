The Arrival of an Old Friend (Part 2 of 2)

6 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03.06.05 The rain had begun before they had made it out of the City and looked as if it would be following them throughout the rest of their trip to Detroit. The drops were hitting the windows of the black limosine with a torrential force and were causing the passing lights to sparkle even moreso than usual. As he peered through the portal to the outside world, Ken couldn't help but reflect on the blessings he had been dealt in life as he watched the heavens open up on the Earth. His mind raced to Gunner, who was laying asleep beside him, and how lucky he was to have her returned to his life and how the love which they had shared had brought him through so much more than he could have ever made it through on his own. His focus then turned to his two best-friends, both fast-asleep as well, in Bill Moresi and Josh. He wondered if either of them truly knew how much they meant to him. They, too, had pulled him out of situations that would have been impossible to make it out of alone. However, while The Upper Echelon was definitly a 'situation' worth meantioning, those battles in reference were of a much more personal aspect. The losses of family and friends, his bouts with his own demons as well as those of others, and making it through bouts with drug use and alcoholism were just a few worth naming, and, whereas most would have left long ago, Bill and Josh had remained there, by his side, throughout it all. There once was a side of KPJ that no Global Wrestling fan ever witnessed. A side that was on a one-way collision course towards destruction. A side that had relied on the companionship of booze and narcotics. A side that was, basically, lost and lonely and scared. For a brief history, after Lily, better known throughout wrestling circles as Shanghai Lily, decided to leave him some many years ago, Ken went on a binge the likes of which many celebrities often fall into. The loss of his, at that time, fiancee coupled with the miscarriage of his unborn child and the pain of finding the one you love in the arms of another had driven the World Champion to the point of no longer caring for himself, much less for those around him. He began to take comfort in the fact that anything he wanted was at his beckon call, be it China White or Venom or even your run-of-the-mill prescription pain pills. Luckily for Ken, this is where Bill and Josh came into play. They were there to catch him when he landed from his freefall. Them, Gunner, of course, and Ceci and Todd... and Ice. Brock was always there for Ken as well... always. It could honestly have been described as Ken being Eddie Guerrero and Bill, Brock, and Josh playing the roles of Dean Malenko, Chris Benoit, and Perry Saturn. So, when Brock's decided to turn his back on them, the world seemed to be falling down again on the Superstar. When had Brock's heart indeed turned to ice? When did Ken's good friend pass on and allow Ice to prevail? Moreso, as Brock had helped save Ken's life, would Ice be the one to destroy it? Ken didn't want to think about it, as right now, many things were precious to KPJ in his life. There was Gunner, his friends and family, and, without saying, his Title. he knew that Ice would do anything in his power to take a piece of that away, including helping Christian Zenith toss him some fifty-feet from the top of the cell to the cold unforgiving cement that made up the arena floor. 'You look distraught,' interrupting Josh, breaking the silence and startling Ken a bit. 'You okay?' 'Yeah, responded the Champion, turning his attention from the window to his friend. 'Just thinking.' 'About what,' questioned the ever curious Josh. Ken paused for a few seconds before answering and looked at the floor of the dimly lit limo. 'Everything,' he responded. It was as if Josh was a mind reader, as by that one word he was positive that his friend wasn't talking about his Tag Title opportunity or his World Title defense at High Octane. 'Penny for you thoughts, then,' responded the American Youth. 'I was just, you know,' began KPJ, 'thinking about where I've come from and where I've made it to. Thinking about those who've been by my side throughout it all... people like you... like Gunner. People like Bill, Ceci, and Todd.' The subject of Todd propted silence from both men. Todd had been Ken's best-friend for some twenty-years from the time they met at six-years-old in their first grade class. He had passed away, thanks in part to the AIDS virus, a little more than a year ago. Gunner had attended the funeral with ken and she and Josh both stood next to him during one of the most difficult moments f his life. 'I miss him,' said Ken, breaking the steady silence that had muffled the car. 'He was such a remarkable person, and he had no idea just how wonderful he was.' 'I know,' comforted Josh to his now crying friend. 'It can still get difficult from time-to-time, but don't think about how much Todd meant to you, but about how much you meant to him.' 'I know,' continued Ken through tears, 'but I just wish he could see me now, you know. I wish that he could see where I've made it and that he didn't stand at my side for all of those years for nothing.' He does,' said Josh with half of a smile on his face. 'He's smiling down on you, letting you know everything's going to be okay. He's smiling down on you, letting you realize how proud he is of you and everything you've accomplished, just liek Gunner, and Bill, and Ceci, and... well... and me.' The two sit and look at each other for a minute before Josh breaks the somewhat uncomfortable silence once more. 'Santa's roud of you, too,' said Josh witha grin as he pointed to the plastic four-foot St. Nick in the limo with them. The two began to laugh as Kend ried his eyes. 'Sure,' responded KPJ, 'but he never calls.' 'Well,' laughed Youth, 'he's a busy man. I don't suppose you could keep up with all the naughty and nice boys and grils all year long. I mean, its sure to be a tiring job.' The two shared a moment in laughter before Ken looked back up at Josh. 'Thanks,' he said whith his whole heart. 'You should know better than to thank me for that, by now,' responded Josh. 'You're my bro, Amigo.' 'I know,' responded the Champ, 'but I'm still grateful for you.' Josh simply nodded and smiled. 'Get some rest man, you're gonna be drained before we even get there.' 'Yeah,' yawned KPJ, 'that may be a good idea.' With those words, the two gave each other a nod, meaning godnight between them, and began to drift to sleep. Ken's dreams carried on his thought, however. A cemetary comes into view with a dark figure walking through it. His long black trenchcoat is floating behind him as he moves through and around trees. The moonlight inks it's way in through the foilage, lighting portions of his face from time to time. There's a light fog on the ground, rising slowly. He finally stops in front of a burial plot completley emersed in the light of the moon. Fireflies can be seen buzzing in and out of the moonbeam and the tombstone can be made out perfectly, though the name can not. The man steps into the light, revealing himself to be 'Superstar' Ken Partridge, Jr. He kneels down on one knee and drops a single rose on the grave. He stays there, motionless... soundless, for a few minutes, then without moving or breaking his eyes from the epitaph scrwaled accross the headstone, he begins to speak. 'It's amazing how precious one life can be. How, when it's taken from you, you realize all of the things you should have said, or done, but never did. It's absolutly astonishing to me that, day by day, we let these things slip through our fingers without paying them any thought. It's truly amazing... isn't it? People usually think that they know m, or my type. As if I fall into some sort of category. They see me at nightclubs and hot spots and the assumptions are all the same... that I'm some arrogant, care-free, partier. Well, let me clear some things up... I enjoy living the life that I live... the nightclubs, the fancy restauraunts, the box seats at my White Sox and Penguins games, the floor seats at my Spurs games. I enjoy going out with my friends, hopping from one club to the next, going from party to party, until the sun comes up. I enjoy the highlife... and who wouldn't, but it never... never clouded my view of what was important. This... this is important. No matter how many clubs I went to... no matter how many games I had the best seats for or how many drinks people bought for me... I never forgot the fact that I could see the universe in my friends' eyes. That's what's important. You see I knew what I had, and it was taken from me. TAKEN FROM ME! Do you know what that's like?' He turns to look at another man in the cemetary. A dark and shadowy figure at first, he makes his way into the light to be made out as Ken as well. 'Because I do! I was the most priveledged person in the world! I got to know him, and gaze into his wonderous eyes, and tell him everything he meant to me... FOR WHAT? For him to be taken from me! Do you know what that's like? Do you? Let me explain what happens to you at time's like these... you see, your insides, they become hollow and stop caring about the well-being of yourself... much less that of others! Your blood runs cold and any warmth that's in your heart is abolished! Then, eventually, the life begins to return to your body. If you're lucky, you begin to feel warmth... and compassion... and love again. You turn into your old self. I got lucky... WE got lucky. We were bessed with Gunner... with Josh and with Bill... with Ceci and a million adoring fans. But we must be wary, as there's always something... or someone that triggers this feeling to come back. We must remain strong. Death must NOT be in vain.' The first Ken then turns and disappears into the fog, moving out of sight, before the second kneels to the tombstone and reads the name aloud. 'Justin Todd Ingram.' Ken shot up in a flash covered with a cold sweat. Sleep did not look to be a luxery that he would experience on this night.
