Before Wrestlecade, End of the Road

26 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/26/04 Note: The following is not meant to count towards the match, it is simply meant to provide closure towards my character's storyline. Ace dropped the barbells to the floor with a loud, metallic thud as sweat poured from his body in droves, giving him the impression he’d just swum the entire Atlantic Ocean, as his entire body was drenched. He breathed in and out, feeling his legs give in to the weight of his body, and after regaining his breath he dragged himself to a bench and lay on top of it with his arm over his head. Ace: Will it ever be enough? Why do I even bother? Am I ever going to be good enough? Ace sat up and continued to breath in and out, then closed his eyes. Ace: If I don’t win’and I get the feeling there’s a good chance I won’t’what then? Suddenly footsteps echoed throughout the room. Ace stood up with a cautious expression on his face, when a short, red-haired, beautiful woman wearing a brown jacket and blue jeans walked towards him from the darkness of the back of the room. Ace frowned at the sight of her. She stood in front of him with a sad look on her face. He looked up at her with a menacing glare, which caused her to turn her head shyly away from him, unable to even make eye contact, her face becoming sadder. Ace: Phoenix. She turned her head towards him. Phoenix: Ace. For awhile neither of them said anything, although they did begin to make eye contact, Ace’s face still angry and Phoenix’s seeming to grow sadder. Ace: What do you want? Phoenix: I just came to wish you luck. Ace: For what? Phoenix sighed. Phoenix: Your match. Ace: Great. Bye. Phoenix: Ace? Ace: Why do you even bother, Mel? I mean, is there something wrong inside your head, some wire that’s in the wrong place, some screw that needs tightening, that makes you so goddamned stubborn? I shoved you in a public place, in front of everybody else, and cursed and frightened you so badly you were put into tears. And you STILL come back to wish ME good luck?! What in the hell is WRONG with you?! Are you STUPID or something?!”! A tear seeps from Phoenix’s eye. Phoenix: Maybe I am. Ace: Then go be a fucking moron somewhere else. Phoenix looked down at the floor as another tear dropped to the floor, then turned around and began to walk away, when Ace suddenly stood up. Ace: You said you came to wish me luck. Why? What would incite you to tell me that, now, after all this time we’ve been apart? Phoenix: Because this could be your last match. Ace’s eyes widened and his eyebrows raised. He stepped forward and grabbed Phoenix from the arm and spun her around to face him. Ace: What the hell are you trying to say? HUH?! That I’m not good enough to beat Partridge? Is that what you’re saying? IS IT?!”! You have the fucking NERVE to even suggest that around me? Do you?! Phoenix: Please’let go of me, you’re hurting me? Ace released her arm and Phoenix wiped the tears from her eyes and sniffed. Phoenix: Ace’you, you left Lance and me, because you wanted to become somebody. You said we were holding you down, and that you would never realize your potential with us as extra weight on your back. You, you hurt me, and said that I meant nothing to you anymore, because you finally understood that I was just a burden removing you from your target. Ace: What are you saying? Phoenix: Ace’have you been successful since then? How many belts have you won? Are you any happier than you were back then? Are you still angry? Has anything changed? Ace looked dumbfounded, then sat down again on the bench with his head down and his elbows on his thighs, his hands running through his hair. Ace: No. Phoenix stepped closer to him. Phoenix: You sacrificed me to better yourself. Was it worth it? All this time you spend working out, focusing on your target. Was it worth it? Sweat dripped from Ace’s face. Or was it a tear? Ace: Get out. Now. Phoenix: Will it be worth it when you face KPJ? Ace: GET OUT!! Ace rose to his feet when behind Phoenix emerged Chaos from the shadows, holding a large steel pipe in his hand. Ace: You? Chaos: Sit down, and shut up, Ace. And listen to what she has to say. Ace frowned but complied, sitting down on the bench again. Phoenix: Ace, Chaos had a vision of your match, and he knows what’s going to happen. Ace’s face changes, as if he knows exactly what she is talking about. Ace: I’m going to lose. Silence is the only answer he gets from them. He sighed and put his head down. Ace: All this time I thought I was doing myself a favor, thought that I would finally become somebody. Finally reach my potential, climb that ladder and take this business for everything it’s worth. Turns out not a goddamned thing has changed. I’m still a nobody, a nothing, a never-was, and I’m still less of a wrestler than Chaos. I still mean nothing to nobody. Phoenix: That’s not true, Ace. Ace: Is it? I mean, I finally go out on my own, and I accomplish nothing. I lose all the time even though I KNOW I can wrestle, and I never live up to ANYTHING I say. Maybe I was never meant to be a wrestler. Maybe I need to think about what the hell I’m doing with my fucking LIFE. Phoenix: Maybe you’re right. Maybe you aren’t meant to beat KPJ, and maybe you were never meant to even become a wrestler. Ace: ?””. Phoenix: But Ace?.don’t you understand, that it doesn’t matter? Ace looks at her. Ace: It doesn’t matter? It doesn’t MATTER?!”! Don’t you get it? DON’T YOU?!”! This business has been my fucking LIFE! I eat, breathe, sleep, and SHIT this business! I’ve depended on it to provide me a living, and what little recognition, respect, or fear I have, I got from toiling in its shit! I may be a nothing and a nobody in it, but this is all I know, this is all I was ever good at! Maybe’maybe, now that I know that there’s no point anymore, now that it’s all going to end’maybe? Phoenix: No. Don’t you dare. Ace begins to tear up. Ace: You don’t understand’this is all I am. Ace Harding is nothing without this sport, this business. If I lose that, if I lose it’that is all I have. I will become nothing. Don’t you understand what that’s like? To lose your entire point of living? It’s like being dead’but unable to DIE? Phoenix: Ace’don’t YOU understand, that you’ve had everything you’ve ever needed since you started? Ace: What? Phoenix: Ace’I know that what you’ve done to me was out of madness, out of losing a fight to the pressure that this business puts on us to gain recognition. Society itself is to blame for that. You felt that in order to find out who you were, you had to destroy everything in front of you, including me. But I forgive you for that. Chaos did the same thing. But what he realized, and what I’ve know all along, is that NONE of that matters. What we have’the three of us’is something that is ours, and ONLY ours. Phoenix held Ace’s face with both hands and looked into his eyes. Phoenix: You envied KPJ because he was exactly who you wanted to be. But it’s not because KPJ is famous, respected, and an experienced athlete, or because of his record or reputation. It’s because he had love. For another person. And he destroyed that love without even realizing how important it was to him. Now he’s surrounded by people who are only with him because of the financial and career advantages, people he has been at terms with before you even came here. They aren’t friends, Ace. Not really. They are businessmen. You are not a businessman, Ace. I know that. I knew that when you started. You used to care about things, about people, and about what was right and worth doing in this world we have called home. But now it’s time for you to understand that this world, this home you’ve made for yourself, is not what you need. It’s not even what you want. The world you need, and want, is with us, Ace. You have to realize that. Ace remained silent, every word that passed Phoenix’s lips branding itself into Ace’s mind and heart. Ace: I could never forgive myself’never’for what I’ve done to both of you. I can’t go back, not now, not ever. Phoenix: Yes you can. We will always be here for you, Ace. And I know you will always be there for us. We’ve gone through too much in the past for you to even doubt that anymore. Ace: Maybe?.maybe you’re right. Phoenix: You say that you will always be a nobody, Ace. But for us, you are everything. You are the world. You are more than any title belt, any record, award, or any moment of glory that the world of wrestling could ever bring to you. You don’t need this business to determine who you are. Just because it is dark and dank and wrong, doesn’t mean you have to be. Because who are you is who we love, and we will always fight for that Andrew Harding, for that Ace, that believed the right thing to do was the only thing that mattered. Everything else eventually deteriorates Ace, but what we have will never die. Ace continued to remain silent, until Phoenix removed her soft hands from his face and stood up. Phoenix: We’ll be waiting after your match Ace. No matter what the outcome is. And we’ll be ready to help you, like we’ve always done before, and rebuild what we once had together. Goodbye. We love you. Phoenix turned and walked away as Chaos gave a cocky smirk and pointed at him with the steel pipe playfully. Chaos: Don’t get your ass kicked too bad, pally. You won’t be able to enjoy the beer afterwards otherwise! A smile crossed Ace’s face for the first time in years, and he laughed at Chaos’s comment as Chaos disappeared into the darkness of the room. Ace sat there in silence for maybe over an hour, thinking.
