The Max-Attack: Chapter 15

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today The flames get even bigger as Max tries to rise from the ground. Roswell walks around trying to get the flames out but they just keep burning his clothes. Max walks over a little bump in the ground and jumps off the top of the building. Max glides through the air getting all the flames out of his clothes. Roswell lands on top of his hotel, and his feet go right through the roof making rocks fall on to the people inside the hotel. Max quickly jumps off the top of the hotel and goes through a different door at the back of the hotel so no one would see his clothes “Ow, I think some of the fire got on my skin” Max walks into his hotel room turning the light switch on while he’s at it. Roswell takes his burnt suit off and throws it in a large garbage bag hanging off his dresser. Roswell walks to a wooden table by the door and looks inside his suitcase trying to find some clothes to wear. Max pulls out a white muscle shirt and a pear of Nike wind pants. “These are my last pear of clothes I brought on this trip. I better not burn them or all that” says Roswell. Suddenly there is a knock on the door of Roswell’s hotel room. Roswell walks over and looks through the eyehole but doesn’t see any body. Roswell opens the door and sees Ben standing there picking his nose. “Hey Ben, what are you doing here?” says Roswell. “I can’t find my mommy again” whispers Ben. Roswell walks out of his hotel room and picks Ben up again and starts walking down the hall. Max sees Ben’s mom look in doors and in the elevators. “Lose Ben again?” asks Roswell. “Yes. He ran off again” Ben jumps out from behind Roswell and scares his mom. Ben jumps into his mommy’s arms as Ben’s mom thanks Roswell for the second time. “Ben, are you just running away from your mom just so you can see me?” asks Roswell. “Maybe” whispers Ben. Ben starts laughing and banging the walls. His mom picks him up so he won’t bang the walls and starts walking down the hallway with Roswell. “So, you’re a professional wrestler?” asks Ben’s mom. “Yeah. I spend most of my time wrestling at GW” “That stand for what again. I have a short memory.” “Global Wrestling. Actually there is a show called Evolution and this week my dream might come true.” Says Max. “What’s that?” “To become the Primstar or Platnium champion. I have a triple threat match” “I see.” “Hey, I could see if I could get you and Ben front row seats to Evolution.” “Ben would love that. But I’m not really into wrestling.” “Come on. It would be fun” “Well I guess I could see one show with sweet Ben.” “I could also take you two backstage and Ben would get to see the whole roster.” “That would be AWESOME!” yells Ben. “Well I got to go. See you later” says Max. Roswell pats Ben’s had and starts walking back towards his hotel room. Roswell stops at his door and hears a weird sound coming from the room across from him. Roswell ignores it and walks inside his room putting his room key on the counter. Suddenly a big blast is heard and Roswell’s room explodes. Max flies through a chair unconscious. Ben and his mom look back and see that Max’s room exploded. “MAX!” yells Ben. Ben starts running towards the room trying to see what had happened. His mom starts runnbing after him. Ben runs into Roswell’s room because the door blew up and sees him lying on the ground bleeding from the mouth. Ben’s mom rushes in and try’s to get Roswell up but can’t.
