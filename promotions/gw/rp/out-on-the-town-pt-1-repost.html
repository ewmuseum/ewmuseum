The new place was kind of suffocating. Dallas was a big city and all, but any place can seems small when you don't know anyone. Train had been chilling in his new pad, a converted industrial building. The metal and brick all reminded him of the south side of his hometown. Kinda gritty and rundown. Of course that was just an appearance thing. Train wasn't about to go back to living in some crap hole. He'd had enough of that when he was younger and he hadn't risked his life so he could go back to that. After a couple of days of cleaning the place had finally started coming together. The Plasma screen didn't take too long to mount on the wall. He'd have to make a run to Walmart soon to get some more cable for the surround sound system. More than that he'd finally got the space he set up as the living room in the large studio space. The black leather couches looked as good on the white shag carpet as it did back at the spot in Chi-Town. It looked good upstairs, despite all the boxes, and the downstairs would be tight once it was all hooked up. The weight equipment should be arriving in a day or two and the practice ring was downstairs just waiting to be set up. Yeah, it was going to be a great place, no hassles cuz he bought it outright. It was a big investment though. He'd drained a whole lot of his savings from the years of 'business' he'd handled up in the windy city. He had enough cash for a few weeks, but it's a good thing he had his first match coming up next week. Sure it was only some gimp tag team match with a bunch of wanna be badasses, but it's money. All that aside though, tv's getting boring. It's time for some fresh air. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ ***The street lights gleam off the glossy, jet black finish of Train's Lincoln Navigator as it casually cruise the strip in downtown Dallas. 'Roll With Me' by 8 Ball, MJG and Diddy flows out the windows as Train let the cool night air flows in. He was looking for something to do. The truck seem to slide down the street aimlessly for a while. Finally, a sign finally caught his eye. The cross hairs around the 'S' in Sharpshooters Sports Bar & Grille was slowly flashing red. It drew the eye, but not nearlly as much as the small sign underneath it... 'See your favorite Global Wrestling matches here every night'. The Navigator slides gracefully into the parking lot of the bar and into a parking spot near the door. Train shuts it down and hops out of the truck. Train, sporting his beige Timberlands, Roc-a-Wear jeans, leather jacket and the diamonds gleaming in his Platinum Rolex and in the 'SS' medallion hanging from his platinum chain, dreadlocks swinging free, steps through the front door. The place place is warmly lit, sports memorobelia and televisions line the walls. It looks like every tv is tuned to a different... Everything from Soccer to rugby. The place seems to be bustling, the crowd into good conversations or good games. Train scans the room, taking in the whole scene, but is only looking for one thing. Finally he spots the 3 televisions in the far corner of the bar, right next to the three pool tables. On the screen he spots some of GW's greatest matches going full tilt. Train walks down the steps to the sunken floor and strides across the rooms. There are some heads turning as the crosses the room. He can't decide whether it's the diamonds or just being one of the few black faces in the room, but he doesn't think about it long enough to give a crap. His mind is focused on the GW dedicated screens. Nothing like scouting opponents and having a drink. Now all he needs is...*** Women's Voice- Hi, my name is Arielle. Can I get you something? ***Train turns, greeted with a lovely sight. A gorgeous latina, long, layered, brown hair and a body that even the half drab bar uniform can't disguise. Train has to catch himself from the surprise, trying to think of a comeback, but they all seem inadequate.*** Train- Well... I'd say you could get me your number, but since you don't look like you wanna here that, I won't say that. Arielle- Well... I'd say no, but, since you don't look like you wanna hear that, I won't say that either. Train- Uh-oh, do I have a smart alec on my hands? Arielle- Not Alec, Arielle. But my dad always said if I had been a boy he wanted to name me Alec. Train- hahaha... Aight, I see how this is gonna be. How bout this? What's good around here? Arielle- If I didn't think it was only going to encourage you, I'd say me, but we wouldn't want that. But the cheese fries are pretty good. Anything to drink? Train- Tell you what Miss thang. You might wanna go get me a Michelob before you hang around too long and get a brotha encouraged. Arielle(grining)- Michelob and cheese fries, coming up. ***It's a testament to Arielle's beauty that Train didnt even notice the man coming up behind her. Fact is he'd normally be hard to miss. About 6'4', probably around 270, the overgrown cowboy, cowboy hat and all stands out in the crowd. Arielle tries to back away from Train and winds up back into the stranger. His two friends, equally as cowboyesque, are right behind him.*** Cowboy- Hey baby, did you miss me? Arielle- Yeah, with the .45, but I'm saving up for a shotgun. Excuse me. ***She sidesteps him, but he steps right into her path.*** Cowboy- Ahhh, c'mon now baby! Don't be like that. ***She rushes around him, but his large hand wraps around he petite arm.*** Cowboy- Come on, I know I can be a jerk sometimes. But you know the best part of fighting is making up. Arielle- You've already 'made up' with half the women in this town. Go make up with one of them. Now let go! Train- I know yall backwoods type don't read and write too good, but I know you can here. Step off the lady son. Cowboy- What? Who the fuck are you? Train- Keep fuckin' around... I'll show. Arielle- Travis, cut it out! Travis- Now can't you see the boys are trying to have a conversation? Train- The shit's fixin' to be short and sweet too. ***Travis holds tight to Arielle's arm, but his two buddies move around to his sides and this confrontations goes from one on one to three on one! Train just casually peels off his jacket and tosses it onto a nearby chair. But, before anymore hostilities can be exchanged...*** Voice- Travis! ***The whole group, as well as some of the patrons turn to the bar. Behind it is a middle aged man, with short, Captain Picard style hair. He's definitely older, but the muscular build under the 'Sharpshooters' t-shirt doesnt' show it.*** Man- I told you the last time that the next time you came in causing trouble I was calling the cops. You don't wanna go outta here in cuffs you and your buddies better calm the hell down. And you let Arielle go. Now! Travis- Alright, alright! Calm down before you blow a gasket old man. I was just kidding around. ***Travis lets go of Arielle's arm. She storms off towards the bar, the man behind the bar takes her aside as she arrives, checking to see if she's ok.*** Travis(to Train)- Oh and ahhhhh, no hard feelings, alright? You want 'em? You can feel free to have my sloppy seconds. ***Travis and his buddies just laugh as Train stares, his eyes cold and unwaivering. Travis brings his hand down onto Train's shoulder for a pat once. He tries it a second time, but gets it smacked away...hard. Travis and his buddies bow up.*** Travis- What are you, stupid? Do you know who the hell I am? Train- Naw... But I know what hell is gonna happen if you put yo' hand on me again. ***There is another, quieter, but equally as tense stand off. Travis and his buddies, all over 6 foot stare down at the smaller Train.*** Travis- hahaha... Is that so shorty? Man, I'll tell ya' you boys from the hood get a little costume jewelry on ya' and you get real bold, huh? Train- Son, when you from the hood, you don't do costume. If you gonna get jacked over something you want that shit to be real. Hell, you could take this watch, buy yo momma that double wide she's been wanting and still have enough left over to buy the rest of the trailer park. Travis- You know what? You're really starting to push your luck. *Pause* You willing to put your money where your mouth is? Train- What you got in mind? Travis- You play pool? Train- I played a game or three. Travis- You think your such a bad ass? Put your watch on the line. Train- My watch? Man, you talkin' all that... How much you gonna put up? Travis- I got $500 that says I can whup your ass up and down that table. Train- Bitch, you couldn't buy five stones outta this watch for that. But I'll tell you what though... ***Train reaches into his pocket and produces a money clip, nearlly bursting at it's metal seams. He easily counts out $500 in 20's and 100's.*** Train- If all you got the nuts for is $500 we can do that. ***Travis looks over to his boys, who simply smirk, egging their boy on, feeding that already monstorous ego.*** Travis- Ok hot shot. You think you're bad? You wanna do it big? I got ten grand that says I you can't take me. ***Train just smirks at Travis and his boys.*** Train- Let's do this. To be Continued......
