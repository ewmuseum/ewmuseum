The party

Date: 2/1/04 Once there I notice someone rather familiar standing at the counter and he pulls up a chair next to him. Beside me sits Deacon Holmes, who is surprisingly not doing rather well with the ladies. I however am. Whether or not I care though is a completely different story all together... I sit at the bar hunched over a menagerie of shot glasses, each of which has been discarded in a different manner. Some overturned onto their sides, some standing right side up, some upside down, all of them empty. I had no clue I could drink like that... apparently neither did Deacon Holmes. What’s even more surprising is the fact that I'm flanked on each side by a very attractive member of the opposite sex, both of which are bikini clad and posses what Deacon would like to call very impressive “chest talent”. The two girls? combined four hands roam all over my rather ripped upper body, caressing my bulging muscles as they glide over them. Not that I seem to even notice. I'm not even cracking a smile, or looking at them for that matter. It’s like they’re not even there, or maybe it’s me who’s absent. I mean, it’s hard to shrug off two girls at the same time...whatever... the only thing on my mind right now is Seymore Almasy and our match at Global Reload. Deacon watches patiently as the bartender pours me yet another shot, which I down immediately, before he gives me a nudge on the arm and I nod as I turn to him. “What up man?” Deacon says, I simply reply with a simple nod and return to blankly staring at the bartender, whom notices my signals for another shot two as the two girls, molesting me with their hands, are handed their drinks in hollowed coconut shells. After pouring me yet another shot from a large, and now half empty, handle bottle of Canadian Mist, the bartender makes his way across the counter and stands in front of Deacon. I listen as he asks Deacon, what he wants, but unattentively Deacon mumbles 'huh' in response. Shortly there after a woman begins to flirt with Deacon, and like the manwhore he is, he chases the tail, leaving me with these two sluts as they continue to grope my chest. Why couldn't they get the hint.... I couldn't believe it. I've been ignoring them for over a half an hour, yet the two bimbos didn't pick up that I was not interested in them and their slutty ways. I came here not because I wanted to, not because I needed too, not because I had to, but rather because I was paid too by TUE. John Zero had asked me to show up and I turned him down, but then he whipped out a check, which was for a hardy amount of money, just to show up at a party. I could not refuse the amount of money he was offering. So I drove my ass down here, and decided to avoid as many people as possible, by sitting at the bar, and drinking the night away. But then these two whores had to come and ruin my night, by bothering me. Worse then bothering me, they were too stupid to pick up my hints that I was not interested through my ignoring of them. But noooo... they are too ingornant to see that.. So now I am stuck here, with these two beautiful sluts groping me, while I am here, getting paid, trying to focus on my match at Global Reload with Seymour Almasy.' Why won't they leave me alone.... Deacon ended up walking away, and chasing that piece of ass he was after, before I made up my mind on whether or not to respond to him. Ah well, guess that really doesn't matter. I don't really like his smart ass anyways. Now if I could only get theses bitches off of me, I'd be set to go home, and get a good night's rest before the big showdown. 'Excuse me. Ladies, would you mind going somewhere else to find a cheap screw? I'm not really interested, and would like to go home.' Taken back my my comments, and basically offended, the two fine ladies with 'chest talent' hurrily walk away. I watch as they go back into the crowd, trying to find another wealthy victim. I can't help but shake my head at them as they try to work their 'magic' on another poor soul. However it is time I move on, and out of this place. Back to the sanctity and solitude of my appartment, where I can sit alone, and prepare for my upcoming battle. It's times like these when Star was at her best. Helping me prepare. Easing my tension, yet keeping me focused at the same time. I miss her so much, I don't even know where to begin. Ah well, another emotion, for another time. Quickly I head out the door, and hop into my large, black, Ford Expedition, and speed away from that hell hole. I didn't like being there to begin with, and was there too long by my standards quite frankly. The drive home, doesn't seem to take as long as the painful drive to Zero's place. In no time at all, I am back in my hotel room, kicking off my shoes, and preparing for a long night of sleep. Just before I turn out the light, I turn and look at a picture of Star and myself at Kings Island, which rests on the nightstand in the room. Her memory fills my head as I shut off the light, and begin to drift into dreamland. I know our times together will be relived in my dreams. But the morning will come too soon, and ruin them all.
