The Search for the Golden Monkey Pt.3

15 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-15-04 The windshield wipers barley keep the rain off the window as the roar of the hummer engine roars drowned out here and there by the rumble of thunder. “Why does it have to rain on this night?” Serpenti says breaking the silence. “Don’t ask me! The weather channel said it was to be a clear night.” Talon Eagleclaw Talon hits a few buttons near his seat and the darkness turns into green daylight leaving the driving a lot easier. Along the top of the now green tinted window numbers telling the time and date as well at the direction they are heading appear in black. Along the bottom are miles per hour, speed, and other normal gauges any vehicle would have, but towards the passenger side a list of ammunition rounds are listed. “Night vision! Got to love it! Well Night vision with a few enhancements by yours truly.” Talon says with a large smile coming across his face. Obviously very happy of his work, however it falls on death ears. “This is a bad omen Talon. Heading into such a place with rain from no where.” Serpenti says in a brooding tone? I don’t like this Amigo? Serpenti says looking over at Talon. “Don’t worry about it. Once inside the rain will not be a factor.” Talon says reassuringly. ? Ohh Yes I happened to pack a few extra equipment in the back. Just incase the legends are not just myths. Stuff to fight the undead. Serpenti nods in agreement. Not laughing at such a statement. In his travels he has seen many things that most would laugh off and call a man crazy for saying he saw them. He knew such things were real. The Undead. Such a large group of different types. Ghost, Skeletons, Zombies, Mummies, and of course the big daddy of them all, the vampire. Such things did not need to be taken as fairy tales or stories to scare little kids at night. No they had to be taken serious. After what seamed a good hour the hummer came to a stop. Serpenti looked up and saw that they had stopped in front of the cemetery gates. Talon pressed a few more buttons and the night vision switched to an area map. “Okay, This is a lay out of the cemetery. Talon points to a red square with his finger drawing the map closer. “and this is the crypt that you have to enter. Now I did some scans it is linked to some underground catacombs that are underneath this graveyard. According to talks with the spits of my ancestors this area was once a sacred Indian burial ground. The Talisman that you’re seeking is called the Talisman of the night. Legend has it a mighty warrior called Burning Sky was given this talisman by a great shaman, a gift from the spirits of his ancestors; He wore this amulet in battle leaving him pretty much impervious to most weapons. But came with a price the price was upon death he would serve the spirits of the underworld for eternity. . According to the same legend he was defeated and berried with this talisman to ward off his curse.” Talon finishes taking a long breath, but still happy about this report to Serpenti. “Okay well where is the bad part?” Serpenti asks. “Ohh Yes. Well some say that the talisman is cursed to bring him back to life if any one disturbs his rest. “Talon says with less excitement then he had about his first report. “Ohh great. But I need it to get that piece of the map. If it takes sending this Burning Sky to his final rest so be it.” Serpenti says as he takes a deep breath and then he climbs to the back gathering up his extra gear. “Serpenti when you get inside turn on the head set. I can guide you though a satellite reading of the underground caverns in your area.” Talon says as he clicks a few buttons. “I will. Thanks again Talon. Well I am off.” Serpenti says as he reaches for the back door. Serpenti opens the door letting the rain rush down on him completely soaking him. However his suit serves as a wetsuit in the water. Serpenti quickly scales the fencing of the graveyard and drops down. “Five hundred yards in front of you Serpenti” Sounds the voice of Talon in Serpenti’s ear. Serpenti nods and clicks on the night vision setting of his headset. And he quickly heads towards the crypt. After a few moments he is at the door encountering no trouble. As he reaches for the door his instincts flare like mad inside his head, Serpenti leans against the wall his guns drawn. “Talon. I don’t like this! Is there any other way in amigo” ? Serpenti says into the mic. Talon quickly hits a few buttons changing the view on his screen. Slowly another square flashed red. “Serpenti. I found another entrance, it is smaller and older, but I think it is safer. Looks like part of the wall opened up.” Talons replays pressing some more buttons drawing the images closer. “About two hundred yards to your left.” Talon finishes again pleased with his skills. “Thank you Amigo!” Serpenti exclaims and dashes away from the crypt entrance. Serpenti slides loosing his footing in the mud beginning to build up. Serpenti slides and slams into a tombstone hard knocking it over. Slowly he starts to get up. Nothing all is still clear. Slowly he heads to the smaller crypt ahead of him. CRACK!!!! Serpenti looks back as a large deformed creature pulls part of a tree limb off and turns with his glowing red eyes locking on the small Serpenti. The Zombie creature slowly heads for Serpenti letting a low growl out of its fanged mouth. “HOLY SHIT!” Serpenti yells as he switches guns, as the creature gets ever closer. Quickly Serpenti aims and fires the silenced gun ripping a hole into the creature’s chest and head. Slowly the creature’s glowing red eyes fade out and he falls to his knees. Serpenti lets out a sound of relief and turns and slowly opens the crypts door and walks in shutting the door after him. Serpenti pulls out a glow rod, and quickly the rod illuminates the room. Cobwebs and dust fill the old crypt four old mettle flowerpots sit on each corner of a raised cement coffin. Silently Serpenti says a prayer and kisses the cross around his neck before stepping further in. “I am in Talon!” Serpenti excitedly whispers. “Good. Now the wall to the right should be the weakened wall. A few good blows should open it up.” Talons voice says inside Serpenti’s head. Serpenti nods and looks around and walks over to one of the pots, pulling it up from its spot and heading to the indicated wall. Slowly he hits the wall hoping to make as little sound as possible. After about three slow but hard whacks the wall falls away to emptiness. Serpenti places the pot back in its spot and grabs his glow rod sticking it though the hole. Below is a large crack in the ground. After a ways the light from the glow rod ends. Looking around Serpenti sees a narrow ledge on the other side of the wall leading into a larger cave. Slowly he steps out and heads to the cave. Barely inching along the ledge.. Suddenly he stops as the ledge is gone from a large section close to the cave. Serpenti readies his self and leaps out grabbing the cave mouth with his hands and he pulls his self-in. Slowly he gathers his breath and moves on thought the small cave ? tunnel. After a few moments he comes to the end of the tunnel that is high above a large room. Serpenti pulls out a quarter and tosses it at the door. As he does fire shoots up from the floor lasting a few moments. “Good thing I did not take the door” Serpenti says relieved “Why is that?” Talons voice sounds though Serpenti’s earpiece. “Booby trapped with a inferno! Would have been one crispy pirate here if I did walk in.” Serpenti says laughing a little at his personal joke. ? Okay where now?” Serpenti asks. “There should be a stairway under the main coffin. You’re going to have to find out how to open it.” Talon says though the earpiece. “Of course I do!” Serpenti says shaking his head getting a small laugh from Talon on the other end. Serpenti fired his crossbow into the ceiling and test the cord. Slowly he lowers his self to the coffin and looks around before getting off. In front of the crypt is a design that looks like a skull indented into the stone work., After a few moments of looking around Serpenti notices that the torches have the same designs on the bottoms of the shafts.. Slowly Serpenti drops down and walks over to the torches. Closer inspection reveals three small holes next to each one save the one in the center. Serpenti reaches out then stops and looks on the other side of the room. He walks over and pulls that torch from its holder and walks away? “Clever, but not clever enough.” Serpenti says with a slight smile and a gleem in his eye. Serpenti walks over and places the torch into the indention in the coffin? There is a loud rumbling and all of a sudden the ground gives way from underneath Serpenti.. Serpenti drops into the darkness below. “Serpenti.. Serpenti!!! What’s going on!! SERPENTI!!!” Talons voice rings out from the earpiece that no lays on the ground next to the hole. To be continued!
