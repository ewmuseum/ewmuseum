A Cold Dish

9 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01.09.05 Mistakes are often made by those who aren't versed well enough in a particular craft. Criminals often screw up their initial jobs. Pro football players often fumble the ball during their first game, and writers often block up during their first novel. All due to inexperience and nervousness. However, sometimes, those that make the mistakes make them because they want to, whether they realize it or not. Serial killers make them because they're tired of running and are ready to be caught. Hall of Famers make them because they want some type of publicity and place in the public eye again, even if it's bad press, and editors make them because they want the open opportunity to lose the confines of their job and have the freedom to work on their 'great American novel.' But, there are instances where the mistake made is inexplainable. Times when the person making it neither wants to nor is lacking mentally capability. Just a common life error that happens to have knocked at a bad time and place. An unavoidable instance in which there was a psychological block that was breached by the ignorance that once dwelled there. These are usually the most fatal of all mistakes. It's these, the gravest of them all, that often lead to years of bewilderment and regret. 'Las Vegas... the city of lights,' bellowed the captains voice over the airliner's P.A. system. somewhat startling Bill from his slight somber. 'Our time of touchdown in Las Vegas, Nevada, will be approximatly 6:57 pm and the temperature is currently a mild sixty-six degrees. A quick sidenote, Global Wrestling's first show of 2005, Reload, will be taking place at the Global Arena. The show is sold out, but word has it on good authority that a few seats will be opening up before the doors open tomorrow.' The 'fasten seat-belts' sign illuminated prompting the passengers to do so for arrival. As Moresi secured his safety belt, he glanced over to see an astute Ken Partridge Jr sitting next to a sleeping Gunner Addisson. KPJ, dressed a pair of Perry Ellis jeans and a white cotton button-up by Ralph Lauren's Purple Label, was sketching feverishly in one of his many sketchbooks. His charcoal pancil flew majestically across the page, breathing life where there was none. Bill turned his attention to Josh, sitting next to him and fastening his seat-belt. 'Has Ken been doing that since we took off,' asked the Bulldozer in a whisper. 'Yep,' replied Josh, 'as soon as he get it out of over-head, the entire time you were asleep, and while everyone was eating their steak and lobster. He hasn't stopped. I wouldn't be surprised if the entire pad isn't filled up by now.' 'Christ,' replied the mountain of a man in response with a touch of laughter in his tone. 'Do you think he's nervous about the match?' 'It keeps me focused,' said Ken surprisingly, causing both Josh and Bill to jump. 'The match doesn't have me worried. Nor do Jackson or Zenith. All that's plaguing my mind right now is exactly how long I'm going to toy with good Christian until I put him away... and exactly how I'm going to do it.' Bill leaned in over Josh to talk to Ken, whose eyes still had not broken away from his charcoal and paper. 'Champ, forget that guy,' Moresi attempted to reassure. 'Don't let him get in the way of you and that title. Focus on him after you get that strap.' 'Zenith made a mistake, Bill,' began Ken as Moresi leaned back in his first-class seat. 'A fatal one. One he didn't mean or have any intentions of going as wrong as it did.' 'Ken, I'm pissed about what he did to you two as well,' said Josh, 'but, deal with that after, like Bill said.' Ken remained calm throughout the entire situation, which worried Bill and Josh, as they knew that was when he was at his deadliest. 'His mistake wasn't bonding Gunner in chains and dropping her on the tracks,' began Ken, still not lifting his eyes from his sketch, 'it was crossing me and my life. Josh, you know better than anyone that you don't want a match with me to get personal... and that's what he's done.' Ken was correct. Josh and Bill both knew this. They both also knew to drop the subject at hand immediatly, as there was no way of swaying Ken in another direction. They say that revenge is a dish best served cold. They're wrong. Revenge is like blood, best when it's still warm... still coursing through your veins. Too many self-righteous 'heroes' have fallen to the way-saide because they allowed their anger to dissapate before they laid out their plans for redemption. Redemption can't be put off Moreover, it is especially best to lay out your plans immediatly when there seems to be more than one target. Many people make the mistake of labeling each subject with a matter of importance. I hate this person more than person so I have to save them for last. If a subject is worthy of your revenge, there's no labels to put on it. Spite is equal among those. 'What was it,' asked Bill. 'What was what,' replied Ken without even a hint of concern in his voice. 'On the plane,' followed up Moresi as Ken locked the door to his hotel room and began heading to the elevator with his friend. 'What were you drawing?' The doors slid open and both men stepped in with Bill pressing the Ground Floor button as Ken reached into the back pocket of his freshly donned Pop-Star black and gray patchwork plaid bindage pants and pulled out a folded sheet of paper. He handed it to Bill without saying a word. Bill unfolded it as Ken adjusted his black rhinestone studded 'Rockstar' sleeveless. His eyes widened a bit as they gazed over a picture perfect rendition of Christian Zenith. 'This is perfect,' said Moresi. 'Every line, every shadow... even the eyes. But... why?' 'He's my prey, Bill... I study him. I have him locked in my mind,' Ken laughed a bit as they were about to reach their destination. 'Truth be known... I don't want him in there. If I put him on paper... well, it's one way to get him out. In another way... it gives me yet another opportunity to study him.' The doors opened with ding to reveal Josh and Gunner with blinking lights flashing behind them and music playing in the background. The casino was huge, one of Ken's favorites. 'It's about time,' Gunner said jokingly. 'Let's go lose some money.' Ken looked over at his friend, Bill, 'We're not worrying about any of that right now, though. Tonight we gamble, we play, we have a good time. We leave those cares in the ring for tomorrow.' Ken patted Moresi on the back, 'come on, buddy... let's go.' Don't cling to revenge for too long... it can consume your life. Redemption... redemption is a better dish anyway.
