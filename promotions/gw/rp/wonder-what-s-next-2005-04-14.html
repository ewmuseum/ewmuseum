Wonder What's Next

14 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-14-05 Mindy sat in the empty Mercer household as darkness quickly approached. It had been troublesome to Mindy's poor heart to sit there by herself and not worrying about her older cousin. What if he lost it while he was searching for his wife? What if he needed her help? If only he would let her help him once and awhile, perhaps then she wouldn't feel so useless. Even though the Television Title she had was telling her otherwise, she didn't feel like the great athlete that some claimed her to be. 'Champions are supposed to be confident and strong and passionate, and I only have one of those three things! I feel like such a joke sometimes...' Mindy frowned as she surfed the channels for something to watch. One would figure that having digital cable would lead to at least one thing appealing enough to watch, but of course anime hardly ever played on any of those channels, which left Mindy- as she put it- a sad panda. After endless debating of what show to watch, Mindy finally settled on Judge Judy. Not that she would pay much attention to the show anyway, but it would suffice as good background noise to help Mindy's chain of thought. 'How did I even win this freakin' thing in the first place? Oh, yeah.. I beat that creepy stalker boy, that's right. And like.. here I am. Hmph, and I thought I'd feel better without that annoying idiot in my life.. now I realize at least having to worry about him took my mind off of other things! ...Oh dear God, I'm not that depressed, am I?' 'Baloney!' screamed the woman on the television, to which Mindy snapped out of her thinking and glared right at the TV. 'Shut up, Judy!' Mindy then picked up the remote control and threw it at the monitor- and the monitor won. 'Uh oh....' Mindy stood up slowly and looked down at the ground; at the fragments of the shattered remote control that lie strewn about on the floor. 'Eh,' Mindy shrugged, 'Mikey's too worried about Jaden to think about some dumb remote.' Of course, it was true; if Jaden was gone, Michael would not rest until he had her again, and Mindy knew that from how much he would talk about her every day. In fact, the more she thought about it, the more she realized that Michael would have probably remained retired and content with staying with his wife and daughter, if it were not for her insistance that Michael go back to wrestling. 'Gods... no wonder Mikey's been so upset with me.. I've been so selfish without even realizing it!' Mindy plopped back down onto the couch, and looked over at her title with her arms folded across her chest. The glance she gave her belt was one of utter distaste. 'What are you lookin' at?' The more she tried to calm herself down, the more she was simply upsetting herself. She shouldn't have had to calm herself down, and she didn't deserve feeling so miserable. 'Do I really have to feel like this?' she asked herself in semi-defeat, 'Really.. who did I piss off in my past life that had me earn this one?' Mindy growled with every passing second that she thought about the matter- it was just so frustrating to her that she could not find a way to look on the bright side of things. 'Hmph, maybe it's just God's poor excuse for a joke.. or maybe he thinks I'm strong enough to fight through all of this. Sheesh, that would make him as stupid as those people who truly think I'm a talented wrestler... I don't have as much talent as I have mere luck... it's a shame.' 'Urgh, but I don't need wrestling talent.. I need to help Mikey!' Mindy screamed and slammed her right hand into the white porcelain vase on the coffee table- and as it fell over, shattering into pieces amongst the remote control that also lay broken on the living room carpet. However, there was something else, something that caught Mindy's eye when she saw it: a small white piece of paper, folded in one perfect crease. Mindy tilted her head in confusion before she squatted down to pick it up off the floor. The black, neatly written note that was left behind had generated only one reaction of the girl. 'Oh... my... God!' Before Mindy could so much as drop the note from her hand, an arm reached around from behind her and grabbed onto her tightly, hand clasped over her mouth. 'Shh,' a male voiced mocked, 'hush now, little girl.' Mindy struggled desperately, green eyes widened in a dumbfounded shock. The piece of paper Mindy had in her hand soon fluttered to the floor, as she clenched a pair of fists and swung wildly upwards, eventually catching her assailant in what could only have been the man's jaw. Mindy felt the arm relinquish her and she whirled around to stare down the attacker- and she might as well have kept her back to him. His whole body was shrouded in a black cloak, hood draped conveniently over his face which left his features consumed by shadow. The added darkness that night time provided did not help matters, and Mindy had a hard enough time so much as keeping focus on the man. Not even the fact that he was nearly a foot taller than her could help her keep a track of him any better. 'Where's Michael?' the man groaned, asking the abrupt question while he verbally showed no care for taking a punch in the mouth from the black and pink haired vixen. In fact, his shoulders slouched in a manner that could insinuate that he was instead rather amused by the woman's fighting spirit. Therefore, he showed no surprise when his question was not answered. 'No,' she cried back, 'you tell me who the fuck you are first!' Mindy pointed a finger and shook it wildly at him, to which the silhouette let out a sigh. 'I asked you first,' the man pried. 'I would appreciate it if you answered me.' Mindy stared in disbelief as she slowly circled around, trying to get the man to backpedal in the direction of the front door. 'I'm not answering any of your questions,' Mindy said bluntly, 'I don't know you and I don't know what the hell you want, but I know I don't have time to deal with you! Now get out!' 'Oh...' the man trailed off jokingly- it was obvious that he knew much more than what he was willing to let on, but he would soon make things interesting. 'Me telling you where your pal Jaden is... would that be enough to assist you in assisting me? After all, you want to help your Mikey... if you know where Jaden is, you can go on and tell him- if only you can find him before I do. And I have no time to sit and debate the decision with you, so if you cannot make up your mind, I will not hesitate finding him myself. And if that means I have to kill you to do it... I will.' Mindy blinked- too big of a decision to make in such a small amount of time. 'How do I know you're going to tell me the truth?' The figure did not voice a response; he simply lifted a hand, and pointed it at his wrist. Time was running out on Mindy's decision. If she knew where Jaden was, she could help her cousin out. But in return, the strange man before her would have an idea where to find him- and after that weirdo mentioned his dedication towards killing her without second guessing it, she had to fear for Michael's safety. Did she really want to put him in danger? 'But at the same time... would Michael want me to sacrifice myself for him?' 'Ten seconds.' 'Oh for fuck's sake, why did this have to come now? Of all the freakin' times, why now?!' 'Five seconds.' 'Come on, Mindy-' 'Four seconds.' 'Come on, damn you, think-' 'Three.' 'Dammit, what do I say?!' 'Two.' 'Oh screw this...' Before her attacker could continue the countdown, Mindy dropped down and grabbed a shard of the broken porcelain vase at her feet. The man had just as big a disadvantage of sight as Mindy did, and did not know what Mindy was planning until he felt the sharp stab in his stomach. 'Ugh,' the man gurgled, as a phlegmy cough derived from the back of his throat, and he spat a red substance to the floor as his hands groped frantically to pull out the object stuck inside of him. Before he could do so, however, Mindy was on him. 'You tell me where my friend and her kid is, or I'm telling you, if I have to kill you to find out, I will! 'Cause I am having a really bad day right now, and I don't need my friends and family mutilated or killed, alright?! Besides, I'm no ordinary chick.. I am the Global Wrestling Television Champion, and I am the last woman on Earth you want to fuck with!' Mindy grabbed the porcelain shard and twisted hard. The man gasped and clutched Mindy's hands as hard as he could. However, even as his hands threatened to rip hers clean off her wrists, she refused to give in to his surprising strength. 'You really think... I would tell you... a damn thing...' the man sputtered through choked breaths, '...eh heh.. you stupid little wench...' The man lifted a left hand and with it, he gripped Mindy's slender neck and squeezed tightly. Mindy gasped for air, but she still did not let go of her grip on her weapon- not until she finally could not take anymore and shoved the man backwards with her right foot. The silhouette staggered backwards into the front door, and he seemed to know it; in a heartbeat he was gone, and Mindy was left a mix of emotions. Maybe she wasn't the strongest person in the world, or the most confident; but she would have to step up and become the champion figure she made herself appear to be in GW if she was going to truly help her cousin.
