Out with the old...A new Direction #1

23 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/23/04 Once again my journey finds me here. Once again do I follow the steps into the hierarchy monastery. For it was they that sent me on my journey to rediscover who I am.....and now they must see what I've learned. The monastery looking the same way as I left it. Monks everywhere cleaning...meditating...trying to find themselves. I shake my head as I past them disgusted with what I see. By my side Cassandra looks around, a small smile on her face. I get to the monastery doors before I am stopped by one of the monks. I look down on him with a look of hinderence. He looks back at me fearfully before speaking 'Nobody is allowed to go in there. It has been closed for sometime now from visitors.' He looks at me then at Cassandra. 'Women are not allowed in the temple at all.' 'This is what I am trying to tell you....we are expected....both of us. I suggest you should get out of the way.' 'I am sorry sir but I must insist you not go in there and I will do whatever means to keep you out. Things have changed since your last visit we all know what you are and have been trained accordingly. We will fight to the death to keep you out.' I smirk at the monk's statement and he doesn't move. Many of the other monks begin to stand and walk over. Some of them stand ready with large bamboo poles, some with stakes, and lastly some with swords. I look around and smile as I have the feeling that much blood is about to be shed. Cassandra looks around as ready as I am. 'I thought he would get wind of what I was to become. I knew he would train you in the ways of the hunter, but this many it may actually be a challenge. This is why Cassandra was not the only person I brought with me to this place. As you can see.....' The monk looks to the entrance from where we came and sees a figure in the shadow with what seems like a bag in his hand. Behind him a very large man standing with what looks like a large sword. 'Tao, Max glad to see you both could make it on such short notice....I also believe Sage is with you, am I correct?' Out of the shadows as if he created them steps out Sage. He stands there calmly but ready. I smile as I see them there and I look back at the now fearful monk. 'Now we can do this my way and you let us in.....or much blood will be spilt and I have a quick snack before I enter the inner sanctum.' The monk looks up at me and without hesitation begins swinging at me I dodge his fist as the fight began. Cassandra standing by my side seeming to easily kill the men coming toward her. Max fighting his way up to where Cassandra and I are swinging that large sword and monks falling as he slew them. Tao pulls out his katana and begins fighting the mass of monks coming toward him. His skill made it look as if it were a dance....the grace of oblivion. Standing in the back Sage stands there he begins to mutter words and quickly men started falling seeming to burn from the inside out. We continue the fighting, monks falling everywhere.....fighting for a cause they can not win....humanity....not very bright when it comes to certain things now are they.....I smile as the blood begins to cover the floor. Suddenly the doors to the inner sanctum open. We all stop and turn around, standing there is one man of almost mythical size. The man is wearing a black priest outfit. His eyes midnight blue as they peer into mine. I smile and nod to the man as I snap one of the monk's necks.He holds up a hand and the fighting ceases. 'Enough of this meaningless bloodshed. It is I who you have come to see....it is I who you wish to try and subdue. And it is I who will vanquish you.' 'Ah, Revelation...or should I call you Lujai now? Which are you these days? It is a sad day this day, for this day a hunter of your caliber shall die.....you should have killed me when you had the chance.' Cassandra leaps for the man but he slowly just raises his hand and she stops. With a slight hand gesture he sends her back to where Sage is standing knocking them both down. Max begins to charge the man, Revelation sides steps him and clotheslines him. 'You send witches and warlocks to do your bidding now? Call off your dogs or I shall smite them.' Tao calmly walks over to me handing me his now bloodied katana, bowing respectfully to Revelation before walking to where Max is helping him up and moving him to where Cassandra and Sage are slowly getting up. 'Choose your weapon hunter.....' He quickly pulls a battle axe from under his robes and taking no time swinging it at me. I block with the sword backing away ever so quickly. I swing at him and he blocks. I push the blade closer to his face trying to get the upper hand but he kicks me in the stomach and with the same hand gesture sends me into part of one of the columns. The power of this hunter is remarkable, the column begins to break as I slam into it. By the time I look up, he is there and he swings once again this time at my face. I barely pick up the sword just in time to block his blow. I use a leg sweep to knock him of balance and he falls hard dropping the axe. I stand up slowly kicking the axe from his reach. I lunge at him, fangs bared and ready to strike, he counters and monkey flips me. As I land, the sword runs into my stomach. I lay there as Revelation stands and walks toward me. I try and reach for the axe but its inches from out of my reach. He pulls the sword from my stomach and has a small smile on his face. I look up at him still reaching for the axe... 'And now....demon....minister of death, you shall be slain by my hand, the hand of the righteous. The striking hammer of God.' He raises the sword and sticks it into my throat. I finally grab the axe, stand up slowly pushing the sword all the way through my neck and swinging the axe right into his stomach. We both fall back to the ground. I laying there.....nearly decapitated...and him dying quickly. I pull the sword from my neck and crawl over to him. He lays there trying to stand I grab his face and turn it toward me. 'Farewell hunter. You will make an excellent meal.' His eyes widen as I begin to tear into his flesh. He begins to scream but I cover his mouth. I pull out his heart and begin to eat..slowly my throat begins to heal.....leaving a scar. I look to the others and Cassandra and Sage rush to help me up. 'Max.....Tao....burn this place to the ground....' Cassandra and Sage lift me up as Tao and Max do as I asked. The flames begin to get larger and larger. I smile.... Was all this nessessary? Yes...I smile at the flames and begin to walk off 'This is the end of a chapter and the beginning of oblivion....'
