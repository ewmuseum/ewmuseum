ReLoad Hype

4 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/04/06 [The screen is black with an old school dos command line at the top of the screen. Typing sounds are heard as the screen shows the command “C:PROJEKTMAYHEMRUN”. The screen then begins to break into fuzz for a few moments before the voice of Zach de la Rocha of Rage Against the Machine fame is heard.] It has to start someplace ... it has to start sometime. What better place then here ... what better time then now? [It is followed by the opening gongs from the song “Hell’s Bells” by AC/DC. Black and white images are shown of Eryc Morgan posing with his arms in an X across his chest from different angles. The images fade and are followed by words on the screen.] The road to hell is paved with good intentions ... [Images of Ash Morgan are shown in the same way they were of Eryc (in black and white). He has his mouth open as if he’s screaming and points his fingers like guns towards his mouth. The opening of P.O.D.?s “Lights Out” begins to play as clips of past matches involving the Morgan men (with Eryc as Corvus) begin. Commentary is thrown in between them. The first is a clip of Ash getting onto Eryc’s shoulders in the corner of the ring then diving onto the opponent and following the move with a pin while the referee counts to three. The next one is from Crossfire of this last year when Corvus is outside the ring with Fury and is bashing himself with a chair to get fired up. Blood is pouring down his face as he gives his opponent a crazed expression.] Mat Maniac: These two are like the Energizer Bunny and the Incredible Hulk wrapped up into one sick package. Just when you think you have them beat, they’ll just come out of nowhere and steal a victory. It’s lights out. Game Over. If you wanna you can check my stats. It’s lights out. Game Over. Make way “cause the kings is back. [A clip of the two men celebrating in a ring with tag team belts is shown before going into a montage of several of their team finishing move: Sins of the Blood, which is a move in which Ash drapes their opponent across his knee facing upward. Eryc goes to the second or third turnbuckle and flies off and delivers a punishing elbow drop across the neck and flips the victim onto their stomach before one of the team members goes for the pin.] One word describes this team: devastating. [The clips that are being shown now are of respective matches with both Ash and Eryc where they are getting beaten down but come back to fend off their attackers. Ash is then shown hitting The Curse (the setup move to the Dust to Dust finisher) followed by Eryc nailing the Welcome to Reality, Mother F**ker. It switches over to both men showing their respective submission holds: Eryc with the Cold Embrace and Ash with the Ethereal Release.] It’s lights out. Game Over. Make way “cause the kings is back. [Projekt Mayhem is then shown standing together (in black and white like before) each holding up signs across their chest. Ash’s sign reads: 2006: The Year of ... Eryc’s reads: MAYHEM! Both men have devious smiles on their faces and matching looks in their eyes.] IT’S LIGHTS OUT! [The segment ends as static comes onto the screen once more.]
