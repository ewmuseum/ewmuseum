Red - The Dr. Is Out (Sub Saga P. III)

5 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05.05.04 March 24th, 2004 - Approximately 8:00 PM New York, New York - The Echelon Tower 'Dear Sigmund, It has been a trying week in my practice. In regards to my usual patients, nothing particularly unusual occurred. Richard Fisher admitted that he has a fetish for the cold, early Monday morning. I had noticed his affinity for frigid temperatures in many of our conversations but I never thought it to be a fetish of any kind. The details of the day I believe I'll skip as, just incase I'm reading through this a year from now, I don't become physically ill. It led to quite the interesting story of genesis, however, regarding his first serious relationship partner and a freezer-pop. I dare not disclose more although, I really don't believe it necessary. I'm not quite sure what he had hoped to accomplish by sharing this information aside from perhaps a clearer conscious. I dare not think of where our next discussion may lead. A sexual fantasy with Frosty, perhaps? I really should not make jokes about my patients, it's truly unprofessional of me. It is quite on the humorous side and after the week I've had, any form of hilarity is quite welcome. Then of course, the text book Miss Larson. Misses Larson, as you know, has been seeing me for the last four years. Every week, she comes to me with a new story of her noisy neighbors. I tell her that simply communicating her concerns with them, as well as perhaps, a stern warning or contacting their landlords might make for a few changes in their behavior. She of course persists in complaining and pouring out her bottomless pit of grievances on my aching shoulders. I continue to tell her the same as above and she continues to explain to me that it would have no bearing, even if she took the time out of her busy schedule to do it. Ah yes, Misses Larson's busy schedule. 8 AM, feed the cat. 10 AM, clean the carpet next to the litter box where the cat has chosen to leave it's business. 1 PM, pass out while watching her 'afternoon dramas'. 2 PM, repeat until 8 PM when she retires for bed. Once again, Thomas visited me. Thomas is one patient who I will not soon, nor ever willingly, forget. He's all of seven and, according to his mother, came to her one day and requested to see a psychologist because of the amazing amount of stress in his daily life. I'm not sure if I should take this as parental prodding, a generational influence, ancestral knowledge, or as a case of a child prodigy. In my meeting with Thomas, we discussed his classroom dilemma. It would appear that he is leaning towards the latter of my list with the conversation we had on Tuesday. Thomas is having problems with attention during the history aspect of his school day. At first, be it modern day thinking or simply symptom explanations, I thought of attention deficit. But I realized that a child like Thomas is having no problems anywhere else in his education nor is he suffering any from attention issues at home. Perhaps, I thought to myself, his lack of attention during the history part is simply due to the speed at which the other students are learning in comparison to his own speed. I thought of having an IQ test administered but I'd rather not push the young prodigy too early; I don't want to burn him out or force him into a higher grade where he may not be readily accepted. Another explanation for his requesting to visit me has risen, however. It would appear that his eldest brother, Zach at seventeen, is going through a rather extreme rebellion stage that has resulted in many arguments at home as well as physical shows of aggression. Our meeting ended before we could truly delve into Thomas' opinions, views, and ideas about these events but I plan to start off on these topics next Tuesday. He requested to see me again because, and I quote, he 'has some things to get off of [his] chest.' End quote. It was sometime very late Tuesday night or early Wednesday morning when the phone rang, waking Amber and myself up. I was weary but still answered, as it was my office line. I answered and reluctantly said my hellos expecting it to be one of my usual patients going through an unusual situation. I was, however, quite taken aback when I heard the voice of a women I had previously only spoken with or heard of one or two times. Miss Naria Xiou requested my visiting the office of her employer, Maverick Reno, the next day around noon for a lunch meeting. I was unsure if I was truly able or willing to take such an appointment but I did agree to do so. The Echelon Tower in New York? I knew Maverick from college as he was a year behind me in the Psychology department. He and I had grown to be good friends over the semesters in which we shared classes, seminars, and concepts. When I graduated, he had already transferred to another college to complete his final year of doctorate in New York. We've kept in touch of the years but we rarely ventured any further than the occasional letter or 'how are you' complimentary telephone call on one another's birthdays and sometimes, not even that. To say that I was 'shocked' was quite the understatement. To venture that I was in a state of surprise at the request of his secretary would also be an understatement. It would seem that this meeting should be my idea and it should be a facade none the less! She wished for me to 'review' my old friend. To be honest, I had no idea what was in store. Early Wednesday morning, I had an appointment with Sydney Preston. I've not mentioned him much as he's never really been a notable type; sad especially since I'm seeing him due to his incredible disbelief in his own self-worth. Heh, times like these make me wonder about my own credibility. None the less, today he surprised me quite a bit (and by the end of the day, surprises would be quite common) when he said that he was running for city council. When I first heard of this news, I thought it was a fabrication or that his issues had been self-induced. Soon after I realized that he actually meant that he's running for council on the Sims. It was so difficult not to laugh but somehow, I managed. At around noon of the same day, I packed my laptop and went out for, what I told my secretary, was a long lunch. I had planned to drive from my office on Manhattan Island to New York and to the Echelon Tower. It took nearly an hour with early afternoon traffic but when I finally got there, I was met with quite the surprise. Two guards met me at the drive and led me to a small parking lot, enclosed and well watched by the guards. There were no entrances from inside this parking lot however. I was then escorted by two rather large men to the front doors of the building. From there, I spoke with the secretary in the lobby and within moments, I was met by Naria Xiou. She led me to an elevator and to one of the higher levels of the tower. It was a short walk down an amazing hallway before we came to a pair of double doors. Inside would be the most beautiful office I've ever seen in my life. Maverick was there; he stood and greeted me as an old friend should. From there, Miss Xiou left us and the two of us left his office and went into what he referred to as 'the Inner Sanctum.' Once there, we were served by the Echelon Tower's staff. Finally, we were alone to talk of old times and new problems. If only I had known... Maverick has changed. When I met him in college, he was a moral driven studious young man who truly wanted to help those who needed it. From what I had heard of The Reno Company, I thought that I'd find him as the same devoted and caring young man. I was wrong! Maverick has serious issues ranging from high anxiety to paranoia as well as symptoms of disassociative disorders. At moments, he seemed calm and collected. His mind however seemed to jump from one temperament to the next; a second later, he was a man void of rationality. He seemed as if he would go to any lengths to cease what he wants or needs! His passion seemed to drop from that of a stable person to someone manic and depressed at the same time! His composure was amazing even in times when violence was brewing so strongly, I felt that my life was truly in danger. In the end, Maverick finally snapped. He pulled a gun on me and ordered me from the premises. The reasoning behind his snap, I believe, was a feeling of betrayal. He discovered that Naria had called me through a slip of my traitorous tongue. I fear for her but know that my suspicions are worth little without evidence. Maverick is not a stable man and needs help from some or any source. I know that the incident revolving around Scott Pierce is what triggered this change in Maverick; I do not know however how to revert it. His obsession with Scott and his own disintegration of morals astounds me. I can not logically see how the two would link unless through some type of dissociation or post traumatic stress reaction; perhaps it's merely a projection to protect himself from the same type of incidents? Maybe he fears that if he no longer develops such relationships, he can not be harmed in such a way. I honestly don't know. I'm just glad that I'm not involved in finding out. I fear for anyone around him. I wish I could help him but I can't. Not without endangering my family and my self. That's something I simply cannot allow to happen. Thanks for listening, Sigmund. You're always there when I need you. Sincerely, RLP.' The preceding had simply been read aloud. Naria Xiou sits across from Maverick, her hand hanging low as her indiscretions were discovered. She fears for her life yet shows little fear, simply anxiety. Maverick Reno stands and holds the notebook into the air before him, reading a specific line again. 'Miss Naria Xiou requested my visiting the office of her employer, Maverick Reno, the next day around noon for a lunch meeting.' Maverick glances to the side at Naria, his eyes narrowing. He slams the book down suddenly, causing the items on his desk to jump up. Naria jumps suddenly, half expecting the noise to accompanied by a verbal assault. There was nothing. Silence and nothing more. Naria sits in her helpless state, her eyes focused without any sign of fear upon the desk from which thousands of deeds are launched. Maverick paces about the desk and then her, standing with his back to her as she sits in the chair. Another moment passes. 'Naria, you betrayed me. If there is but one thing in this life that I cannot tolerate, it's betrayal.' Maverick turns on his heel, his long black hair moving and shifting with his sudden turn, now draping over his shoulder. His eyes are clear and wise as they focus upon the back of Naria's head. 'What you have done merits death, Naria. What you have done merits shame and humiliation.' Reno again begins to pace, this time approaching his side of the desk. 'So what are my options, Naria? I don't want to kill you. You've been a valued assistant to our crew... and for the most part, no one has tried to kill you. You've also done very little warranting any type of punishment. Until now that is.' Naria slowly lifts her head, gazing at Maverick. Maverick returns a cold, deathly stare. His words form carefully, the tongue of the god's flowing forth, 'Lapsus linguae, errare humanum est.' Reno places his hand on his desk and pulls a scimitar shaped dragger from the center, his eyes examining the brilliant finish. He sighs, his reflection visible within the blade. With a quick flick of the wrist, Reno pushes a small ornate bowl towards Naria's side of the desk. 'But even so, I cannot allow weakness.' Maverick flips the blade quickly and offers it to Naria respectfully. She nods and takes it by its handle, holding it in her hand as she places her other atop the bowl. A flip of the wrist. She tightens her palm, gripping the bowl's edge. Red. Ripping flesh. Trembling. The bowl begins to fill with crimson punishment. Naria's eyes close, sweat pouring from her forehead as she slowly lifts her right hand away from the handle, allowing the scimitar shaped dagger remain piercing her hand, penetrating it completely. A tear forms at the top of her cheek and slowly streams down, touching her lip and following the angle of her face to the side and then down again. Reno's hand moves over the dagger and grips it suddenly, twisting and jerking it from Naria's hand. She lets out a sudden yelp of pure, unadulterated agony. Her hand is pulsating, each throb allowing for a flood of vitae to flow forth. Reno places the dagger upon a cloth and presses a button upon his intercom. A moment passes before a pair of men enter the room with bags. They quickly disinfect Naria's hand and wrap it, prepare it, and bandage her. Naria stands slowly and nods to Maverick. 'Thank you for your compassion, Maverick.' She turns, holding her hand against her abdomen in an attempt to mask the pain. Naria makes her way from inside Maverick's office, the two doctors standing behind. Maverick looks to both and nods, meaning that they can leave. 'But sir, she needs some type of pain medication.' Reno shakes his head no immediately and leaves it at this. Reno motions again and both men walk from the room, leaving him at his desk. There's a moment of silence as Reno sits back, methodically cleaning the dagger. He stares at it, gazing into his own eyes in its reflection. Finally, his hand drifts over and he presses another key. 'Tobias. I need you to do a quick job for me.' Reno is calm and collected again although he still stares into the dagger's blade, the blood stains tinting his own reflection with a symbolic twist. 'What about my arm?' Tobias responds, bringing to memory the incidents that occurred on Evolution just a few weeks ago. Incidents that would lead to Derek Draven and his indiscretions. Incidents that would lead to advancements in Japan and the pilfering of businesses formerly operated by the Yakuza and especially, the Nakazura family. Tobias had recovered from the incident although, his left arm remains broken. Reno nods a moment, his eyes never leaving the blade. He seems distant, his eyes beginning to glaze over with a fog uncommon. 'I'm going with you. It won't be a tough situation, I can guarantee that.' Reno lifts his hand again and wipes the remainder of crimson life from the blade before placing it on his desk and standing. 'I would get your brother... but he's running an errand for me in Buffalo. We should meet him after this.' Maverick presses the button again and stands, sheathing the scimitar dagger in it's case attached to his belt. He grabs a black Italian silk suit jacket and throws it own before adjusting his collar. The screen fades to black. 'Every threat is spoken in hope that it will forever remain idle. When threats fail to meet their purpose and the ultimatum is ignored, then a threat becomes no longer idle. A threat ceases then to be a threat; it becomes an action. With each action, there is an equal and opposite reaction. For each act of defiance, another life shall pass with the blade of my 9mm sickle. Human life liquefies into a crimson fluid, spilled beneath and around the vessel that once contained it. For every drop of crimson life that hits the floor as a result of my infernal melee results in another ounce of humanity draining from the bowels of my soul.' Two figures stand before a large door. The night air is calm and cool, summer taking effect in New England. A fist rises and knocks upon the door and a dog barks from inside, almost instantly. The knock continues. Words are heard being called out from inside. 'Just a moment!' A lock is heard as it is opened from the opposite side. A man appears as the door opens, his eyes focusing on the two visitors. ''Tis some visiter entreating entrance at my chamber door ? Some late visiter entreating entrance at my chamber door; ? This it is, and nothing more.'' -The Raven - Edgar Allen Poe Within a moment of the door opening, Tobias kicks the door as hard as he can, sending it into the face of the man opening it. A thud is heard and the door shakes, moving slowly back and forth. Ryan Purgason lays on the floor, his nose busted wide open and a whelp on his forehead. Tobias walks in, drawing a silenced pistol quickly and aiming it down at Ryan. Maverick Reno walks in and slams the door, drawing a pistol of his own. He walks through the house quickly and down a hallway; he turns a corner and screams are heard. Several silenced shots are heard, each accompanied by a quick flash of light. Tobias stands in the living room, waiting patiently. Reno walks back in, pulling a young girl behind him. The girl is crying and screaming, begging for mercy from a pair of merciless men. Ryan slowly begins to regain consciousness and upon realizing what's going on, he lunges for and wraps his arms around his daughter. Reno stands behind her as she slowly quiets with his words, attempting to comfort his daughter. 'Even in your last moments, you play the role of a caregiver. You betrayed me, Ryan. You wrote of our encounter and now, all exposed must be eliminated.' Without so much as a flinch, Reno lifts his pistol and fires into the back of the little girl. The crying stops although, Ryan holds her even tighter, clenching her and beginning to sob silently. Another shot and both bodies drop to the floor, completely limp. The two men leave the house moments later, never to grace the front steps again. 'And the children are silent, forever more.' I'm Coming For You
