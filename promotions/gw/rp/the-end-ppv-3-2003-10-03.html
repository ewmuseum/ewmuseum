The End. (PPV#3)

3 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/3/03 'God damnit Brock! I thought we talked this over! I thought we made the decision already! I can't ...I won't live like this anymore! That character, that thing, that monster you have become is not the man I fell in love with. I've stood by your side through it all, and I've watched as you've changed! I've watched as you've thrown away the person you were. You were doing so well in your sessions. You finally told me how you felt after you held it back for so long. And now you want to throw away all the progress! FOR WHAT?!' So cold that you cannot cope With a frozen heart I guess we blow apart Tears fill up her emerald green eyes, and fall down her pale cheeks as her words echo throughout the room. Desperately pleading with the man who holds her heart in his hands, she looks at me to see no response, no emotion, no reaction to her words. She bows her head in defeat, and begins to sob uncontrollably. Her sobbing, a mixture of anger and heartbreak. Anger at me for throwing away our life, and our love. Anger at herself for loving me still, and expressing it to me, only to receive nothing in return. Heartbreak, that the man she once loved is no longer the man that stands in front of her. She intakes a long deep breath and angrily glares at the shell of the man she once knew that stands before her. Don’t say because you can’t Say what we should have been Don’t show what I resent Don’t know cause I forget 'I love you with all of my heart, but I am not going to go through this again. I don't deserve it! I can't take this again! I thought we meant something more to the both of us, but I guess that I was wrong.' So cruel to be so blind Darkness was on my side Now that you’ve come and gone I know where I belong Her sobbing turns into a hysterical crying, as the tears flow from her eyes and down her face like water flowing down a river. She looks at him one last time before bending down and picking up her bags. She slings her white NIKE gym bag over her left shoulder, and picks up her dull grey suitcase with her right hand. Quickly she turns and walks through the door, out of our room, and out of his life. I watch her leave, wanting to go after her and tell her how I feel, how I want her to stay, how I need her in my life, but my body stands frozen in the middle of the now empty room, in shock of what I have just done. My mind begins to wander as I stand staring out of the open doorway, where my love, my life just walked through and left me here standing. Words and thoughts begin to pop in and out of my head, while flashbacks of our life play in my head. 'Maybe they are right. Maybe I am crazy. How could I just let the only thing that has ever meant anything to me just walk out of my life, and not even put up a struggle to stop it. Maybe she is right and I have changed, and am no longer the man she met and fell in love with.' The room begins to become blurry, and I begin to stumble backwards, but catch myself on the edge of the couch that sits in the middle of this room. This couch, where we spent so many nights. Me holding her in my arms, caressing her face, caressing her arms, caressing her back, caressing her legs, caressing every inch of her body. Feeling each goosebump rise beneath my touch. Listening to her steady breathing, and feeling her heart beat in sync with mine. Those times while holding her in my arms, feeling so full. Full of joy, full of love, full of life. Feeling as though we were one. 'Damn this couch! Damn this room! Damn those thoughts! Damn those feelings! And DAMN HER TOO! I DON'T NEED ANY OF IT!' Violently I spin and grab a hold of the couch. The heavy couch feels like a feather, with all this aggression inside of me. The glass shatters and spills to the floor, with a few of the pieces catching my exposed skin as I watch the couch filled with our memories fall to the ground out side the window, and break into five large pieces. The regret I was feeling slowly subsides as emptiness engulfs it and takes its place. The little black envelope that was the cause of all of this stares at me, almost in a taunting manner. I snatch it off the floor and place it into my pocket and walk out of the room. I turn and look through the doorway into the empty room where my life resided, only to see a beat up, broken down, empty room that looks nothing like it was before. I let out one last emotion, as a deep sigh escapes my body. My life has changed once again. This monster has taken it away....why? Light is leaving as I watch you go Light is leaving inside of my soul
