Fighting Spirit

1 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/01/06 “Why do you even bother trying” It’s not like you’re going to be able to make the transition.” “Look at you, you’re pathetic!” ?” Professional Wrestling kills your instincts? and YOU can never get them back.” “A company is willing to carry your sorry, pathetic rear-end” I don’t even know why I bother? you’re going to get humiliated.” ?” humiliated.” ?” ? humiliated.” “NO!” ?” Dear, what on earth is wrong?” Lightning struck, illuminating the room of one, Rich Sherwin. Sitting upright with his fists clenched into tight balls, he looked down at himself, a fierce look of grit and determination etched into his stone-like being. ?” It’s another one of those dreams again, isn’t it?” came the soft touch of a feminine voice. She was now sitting up as well, her left arm hanging around the neck of Sherwin, he right hand stroking his chest in a caressing manner. Rich was pissed? but with such a gentle touch, it would be impossible for him to remain that way for long. “Yeah, another one of those dreams?” he said, his voice trailing off into nothing. Rich knew that these were always the worst of his nightmare selection. After all, a fighter without his spirit is just as good as a fighter without his tools. “Isn’t it about time you told me about these in full” You’re going to be making your long awaited return soon? and I can’t help but think you’re going to let yourself down without even getting half a chance to really showcase your talent.” she said, her voice sounding a little more irritated but calm all the same. Rich turned to his right and kissed her softly? as if to bribe her away from the point she was trying to make. It lasted for all of a few moments? before she pulled away, realising the predicament she was leaving herself open to. “No, no, no” you’re not going to bribe your way out of this one with showings of love and affection. Rich, this has got to stop? all the closed doors, all of the apparent inabilities to tell me anything. Most of your career is covered in magazines? journals? fan-sites. Most of your life is covered by various inserts? and ghost-written books. Isn’t it about time you told me something? that nobody else knows?” ?” but why? Why is it so important that I express myself with words? Why is it so important? I just don’t understand it? the need to know?” Rich commented, his facial expression relaxing? the fire within him beginning to simmer down into something that was much more manageable. “Don’t misunderstand me” it’s not that I have something to hide, I just don’t see the use in tell everyone everything. Does a magician inform the audience or his assistant of every trick?” ?” but? but?” she managed to stutter out in response, ?” this isn’t some kind of magician act here. I’m not just some cheap girl brought in to keep the act going. As far as I’ve been, you haven’t even allowed me to come to one of your bouts. You never let me come to your professional wrestling contests? and you haven’t allowed me to come to any of your MMA-style exhibitions.” “Ok” so you’re not just the sidekick to the magician but you’re not going to get anything out of me. I told you before, I don’t talk about everything. You know some things about me that everyone else doesn’t know? they just don’t have anything to do with my own career. They just don’t have anything to do with my own movements.” he explained, trying to find some kind of ground that would set her aside. She neglected to respond to what Rich had just told her. Instead, she turned from him and dropped down onto the bed? feigning tiredness. Rich threw the bed covers from his body and stepped out of the bed. ?” I’m going.” Again, she didn’t respond? she didn’t even turn to look as Rich threw on the nearest pair of sweats. Expressing the same kind of negative behaviour, Rich turned away and left. The girl only turned to look in his direction after the door had slammed shut. ?” Oh Rich, why won’t you tell me anything?” + + + + + + + + + + + Outside in the streets, the rain lashed again the floor? the intermittent bursts of lighting providing a little extra light to the pitch-black surroundings. Rich jogged along the pavement, his hood pulled over his head. He stopped every now and then, throwing a few straight punches into the air before moving onward. He seemed calm? at least, for the time-being? “When are you going to get it stuck in your head” You were good? but this isn’t the region that you specifically trained for. Professional wrestling? just stick to what you were doing best.” “Hey” look at this, Rich. Even the newspapers are finding your desire to be a fighter a joke. Did you know that hardly any wrestlers have made it into this game? Did you know that almost all the professional wrestlers get their asses handed to them?” ?” Oh I know? I know?” Rich said to himself as he continued to jog, the memories of his prior coaches and the opinions of various critics always running rampant throughout his mind. True, Sherwin had been a professional wrestler for almost all of his adult-life to date? but still, he believed that he could cross over into the world of mixed martial arts. Much like many athletes had been able to make the switch from MMA to professional wrestling? he felt in his heart that he could do something much like that. “Well, if you know it” why are you bothering it with? Are you that desperate to get noticed as somebody who takes ass whooping for a few bucks? Are you that desperate to get some kind of reputation? even if it is the lowest of the low?” “You didn’t get it then” and you don’t get it now.” Sherwin said to himself, reliving the worst moments of pressure during his time until this point. Eventually, after running enough, Sherwin made it to his local gym? a small, run-down establishment that meant more to his career than anything else on the face of the earth. Previous championships? Being voted as the best of whatever? Securing multi-million dollar contracts with companies? None of that meant anything when compared to this? the “holy grail” to Rich Sherwin’s career. Stopping outside the discoloured doors, Sherwin looked up at the large sign, identifying the establishment. ?” What did I tell you about using that place for muscle work? We have all the equipment that you’ll need here? and you won’t even have to pay for it!” “Heh, it was never about the fee” always about having somebody to urge you along the way. Somebody to pick you up when you were about to fall? somebody to break out that fighting spirit within you.” Sherwin told himself, looking down from the sign and entering the building. It hadn’t been too long since Sherwin last visited for a workout here? maybe something in the region of two or three days. As Rich began to walk through, the sounds of everyone working out were heard loud and clear. It was a refreshing sound? one that made Rich want to drop everything and start training again now. “Come on, you can make one more!” “Joe” you know you’re going to win? so prove it to me!” “Heh, do you honestly think you’re going to upstage me on the dead lift” I don’t think so, buddy.” As Rich made his way through the doors and into the full view of everyone, the room went silent. It was as if somebody of mass importance had come through the doors and into full view. A president, a king? or something of that accord. Rich commanded the respect of his peers? but never before did they come to a complete halt. “Yo?” he said, as they all turned to greet him. There was an even greater silence? and it remained one of awe. How did these people feel about the return to active competition? How did these people feel about Rich’s own decision to fight again. Rich waited patiently for an answer but unfortunately, there wasn’t one. ?” Well, isn’t anyone going to say anything? I didn’t think I’d done anything to you guys?” he began, looking around at all of the regulars. They struggled to respond but eventually, one of them spoke up. “Rich” we just can’t believe that you’re this close to getting back into the mix. Most of us just can’t believe that you would turn your back on something as steady as professional wrestling. We don’t understand the desire to throw something stable in favour of something that wasn’t stable. MMA is going to be based on your win record? if you lose, you may as well quit.” they said? as Rich tried to find them in the masses of people. “You don’t understand my desire to quit something that was steady” How can you not understand my desire to quit something that offered little to no career advancement? How could you not understand my decision to look for something that was going to be a lot more challenging? For years, I have dreamed of fighting somewhere that was real. For years, I have looked up to the sky? and hoped? just hoped that I would be able to take on board some fighting skills. It is my dream to fight with the best? and it is the competition that I live to see.” Rich answered, sounding strong and firm with his response. He truly wanted to be able to test himself against some of the biggest and the best names in the world of fighting. Global would be the starting point for this desire? it would be the first step of his long, long journey. “Why do you think you’re going to succeed though” Why do you think you’re going to achieve in this new ground? I’ve been telling you from the start? that you just don’t have that killer instinct anymore. You lost it when you started to run around and pretend that you were some kind of dancer. You lost it all when you decided to compete in an organisation that didn’t care about real competition?” came a voice from the back. Rich recognised it and his facial expression changed from the rather mellow look to something that was a little less than. He was frustrated and annoyed because it belonged to the same guy that had been training him all this time. The same guy who took his money and said that he would prepare him for life within an MMA group within twelve months. Obviously, Sherwin thought that he had done enough to prepare him? but this guy didn’t share the same kind of positive opinion. ?” Well, well, well? look who it isn’t. Oliver, I’m sorry if you don’t think that I’m ready to hack it? but I know that my grit and determination is going to say otherwise.” Sherwin retorted, waiting for his old trainer to come through and greet him at the front of the building. “You were always deluding yourself” right from the beginning. In a little less than one week, we’re going to see what kind of man you have become? what kind of fighter you have become.” Oliver shouted back, eventually making his way to the front of the crowd. When he finally made it, we were introduced to a man that looked very much like Sherwin. The only major differences between them were the natural colour of their hair and eyes. Aside from that, it was almost like looking into a mirror. “It’s just like you to be the jealous one, isn’t it Oliver” Always unhappy because you’ve had to stand back and watch as your sibling did all of the work and won all of the titles. You know what though? I’m not here to argue with you anymore? I’m not here to try and prove a point about completing your course? because even when I did, you didn’t want to encourage me anyway. It was almost as though doing this was bad for me? that doing this was counter-productive. Oh, I’ll show you exactly how counter-productive it is in four days time.” Sherwin said back, his voice slightly picking up in tempo to suggest annoyance. Oliver looked a little taken back? but not by the words of Rich. Instead, he was surprised at the absolute lack of fury behind his voice. ?” Lacking passion, I see? Lacking the very element of anger. You?” Oliver tried to exclaim. He was abruptly cut off? “Do you really think that I’m lacking in passion” Do you think that I’m lacking in aggression? This isn’t a lack of anything? it’s an ability to control those emotions that make us go out of our way to do things that are truly stupid. You know, for a long time? I used to bow down to everything that you would explain to me, that you would tell me. All of the time? you’re not good enough or you’re not going to be ready. I suppose that any normal man would have stopped? would have just quit. I was very close to it?” “So why didn’t you quit” Why did you end up coming back into the mix? Didn’t you have all the money that you needed? Didn’t you have everything that you could possibly think of what? What is the possible need to have come back? and to a straight-up fighting organisation of all places.” “My fighting spirit?” Oliver looked taken back by Sherwin’s three words. They weren’t just said in any common tone of voice, they were almost barked? all of his drive being put behind this message. There was a long silence in the building, Oliver not knowing how to respond to his former students comments? with Rich just waiting for something else to be said. Eventually though, Sherwin took the helm of the conversation, ?” I have a need to satisfy my own fighting spirit? my own drive, my own desire to be the very best at anything that I choose to compete in. I’m not just making the transition because I need something new? I’m making the transition because I need to become THE best. I start at the bottom of the ladder and crash my way to the very top.” Rich looks up at the ceiling, imagining it to be the top of the Global ladder. The biggest and the best await him there? but how long will it be until he joins them? One year? Two years? ?” You may look at me now and laugh. You may look at me and wonder how I’m going to be able to compete against eighteen other men in that Gauntlet match. For a few moments, I actually wondered about it myself. Your morale crushing words made me think? maybe I shouldn’t be doing this? Maybe I should just hide under the rock that I’ve been under for the last year. I was so close to just throwing in the towel? giving it all up.” Sherwin explains, looking down at Oliver again, his own facial expression changing to one that was a little more calm, a little more serene. “I soon realised that I couldn’t listen to you anymore” I couldn’t listen you anymore. I was determined to do my thing? and when you see Global next, you’ll see that thing happen. You’ll see?” Sherwin looked a little disappointed at having to explain his whole case to the one man who spent most of his training time looking down on him. Surely, he would have had a little more to say? but then, Rich hadn’t exactly been vocal about anything until the close of the training program. Turning away from both Oliver and everyone else, Rich didn’t feel the need to be in this building for a second longer. Oliver tried to say something as Rich exited? but couldn’t. When he finally did leave however, he managed to say a few words? ?” Rich, sometimes you need a little more than drive and determination to be a fighter. Sometimes, that fighting spirit just isn’t gonna carry you to the finish line.” ~~[ END ]~~
