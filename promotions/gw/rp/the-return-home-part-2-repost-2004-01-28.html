The Return Home (Part 2) Repost

Date: 28-01-04 The tears had not stopped flowing ever since the two had seen the gruesome sight. Their father, mutilated, beaten, murdered. They were deeply sickened by the thought, but almost immediately after the two knew exactly what was on the other’s mind. Vengeance and retribution for those who had soiled the Nakazura name. If there was one thing that their father had always taught them it was to be proud. He encouraged honor and strength as the tools of which to obtain pride. Ichiro sat with his eyes closed, his jaw clenched, his hands balled into tight fists and his head buried into his chest. The tears had lessened now, and as his sadness disappeared his fury and rage grew. He wanted nothing more than to have the head of the bastards that did this to his father. To kill the men who had taken away the best boss he had ever had. He could still hear his father’s words in his head as he became consumed by the solemn silence of fury. “If another man ever challenges your pride Ichiro, you stand up and you fight. You show him why you are proud, and no matter what. Neither you nor your brother should ever let the Nakazura name be dirtied, for to do so would be a failure to me and my pride of you two, my sons.” With that load weighing heavily on his heart, Ichiro stood up and put his pistols back into their holsters. Ichiro stood and walked past his smaller brother, patting him on the shoulder as he went by. Jushin knew what Ichiro was about to do and although he didn’t like it, he had to go with him. There was no way around it, there was going to be a war and likely the Souls of the Black Lotus were going to be standing alone in the front lines. - - - - The car pulled up into the drive way of the small dojo. Of course the Tae Chi Dojo was merely a front for the real operations that went on in this place. Both Ichiro and Jushin hated it here, the mere idea of such a place was disgusting and pitiful. The Dojo was the front for an underground information service. Spies in a way, some of whom are contracted in order to find out information on people, places, groups any number of things. There were also random informants who often gave out any information in their knowledge bank for a small price. Jushin pulled into the near empty parking lot, it was late and so the place was probably getting ready to close. Jushin threw the car into Park and looked at his brother who was undoing his seatbelt and reaching into his back pocket. He pulled out a small wad of colored bills and looked over at Jushin. “75 yen, you?” “50.” “That’ll do. Wait here!” Jushin disliked being told what to do, but in this instance he could deal with it. He despised those who worked and gained money from an operation such as this one, one that often led to death and betrayal. He handed the small wad of colored bills to his brother and Ichiro quickly stepped out of the car. As he watched his brother enter into the building he made a mental note, to eventually blow this place up. Jushin smirked as he dreamed off such things as his brother disappeared from sight? Inside the building Ichiro walked through the small corridors. He searched through every hallway looking for the large training room and office that would lead him to the information he sought out for. He finally arrived into the small waiting room, and noticed the office on the far side of the building. Quickly and quietly he walked through the near empty Training Room and made his way to the office. Within a minute he was speaking with the secretary. Her raven black hair was cut just above her shoulders and the light glistened off of it as she talked to him. Ichiro was mesmorized for a second, so much so that when he finally snapped out of the daze the secretary was giving him a rather impatient look. He looked down at her confused at first, but then remembered what it was he was looking for. “Sir” Are you alright?”!” “Yes ma’am, I am fine. How are you on this fine evening?” “I am fine sir, what can I do for you” Are you here for lessons?” “I hardly need instruction from these lowlife, poor excuses for martial artists. I seek information, information which I believe your people can give to me.” The secretary stared at him blankly for a second. Not quite sure how to react, she shifted her body slightly, seeming a little more seductive. She pushed her arms together and in turn pressed out her rather large chest. Ichiro got lost for a second before regaining his composure. “Well then, can you help me” Or should I take my money and business elsewhere?” “Well, Mr”..” “You don’t need to know my name.” “Well then, I believe we may have the services you require.” The woman pushed a small button and almost instantaneously two men emerged from a small door behind her. The men smiled and escorted Ichiro into the smaller room. In this new room it very much resembled a sort of interrogation room. The walls were dark and covered in dirt and plaster, it was definitely a place where information was extracted. And by the small pools of blood in corners it looked as though those giving the information were not always willing. Not one minute later a man walked in wearing a business suit, and a large smile. The man however was not very big by any standards. He stood about 5’7? and couldn’t have weighed more than 200 pounds. Ichiro stared at the man blankly, a look of pure hatred in his eyes. The man smiled and was seated in a small metal chair directly across the table from Ichiro. “I have seen that look in the eyes of so many who have come before you. Hatred, hatred of me for what I am and what it is that I do for a living. So do not think that you are the first. For you are not, nor will you be the last. But the fact remains, there is nothing, and I mean nothing you can do about it!” Ichiro’s fury grew stronger and the man could tell. He smiled and looked at Ichiro. “What is it that you wish?” “I need information on the death of Kyusin Nakazura.” “Well, well, well. This has been one that I thought would have been coming for a lot longer. But I guess with his two sons out of the country there was no one who really cared.” The look of pain and fury grew on Ichiro’s face. He reached down to his belt buckle. They had confiscated his two pistols, but he still had the small switchblade. He bowed his head slightly, and said a short prayer of forgiveness. He would kill this man, it was inevitable. The man began to speak again and Ichiro began to scout the room. He saw the small mirror on the wall and knew that on the other side of that mirror were men staring right back at him with guns readied. He wondered how he was going to get away with it. Ichiro pulled the small switchblade into his hand and concealed it from view. He began to slowly tap it on the leg of the metal table. “You want to know what happened to him. When his sons did not return when they said they would he slowly became careless. He feared nothing with his sons in town, but he kept the same attitude when they were gone and that was not the right way to do things. Many things began to change, the other bosses rallied their troops. There was going to be a war, and it looked like Ishuzu Ishizun was the one who would start the war. She sent a group of her strongest street warriors”.” As he listened Ichiro kept tapping away at the leg of the metal table. That’s when it hit him, the table was bullet proof. He had it figured out. He became focused again on the mans words, but reached into his pocket and began fingering through the bills in his pocket. He grabbed the wad and placed them silently on the table as he kept listening. “The warriors broke through, the Nakazura regime was completely unprepared. They were overwhelmed. It was a bloodbath, and the story has it that they left Mr. Nakazura alive long enough to watch every single one of his servants die. Then they slowly began to cut his throat. Slowly so he would die painfully, watching and laughing as the warm blood trickled through the wounds they inflicted”.” Ichiro had heard enough he kicked the chair back out of the way. He toppled the table as protection and grabbed the informant by the neck, whipping him to the ground quickly. He held him down and put the knife to his throat. “Bet you thought your story was very funny, allow me to introduce myself, my name is Ichiro Nakazura.” The look on the informants face went pale, he knew he was in for it as soon as he heard the words. The most dangerous killer in Tokyo had returned, and he was about to get his first kill since being home. Ichiro smiled at the look of fear he had caused, but it was not enough. He pressed the small metal blade against the bare flesh of the man’s throat. He could feel the man wriggle as the blade began to slowly press it’s way through the flesh. Small trickles of blood began to emerge and in a quick flash, Ichiro swiped the blade hard across the mans neck and got off of him. He watched in pleasure as the man grabbed for his neck, trying to stop the bleeding, as the huge cut on his throat instantly began to bleed out all over the place. The man finally stopped struggling and Ichiro changed his focus to the door. He had to get out, before the guys with the guns got in. He bolted for the door, and saw seems of fire as he ran out. He made it through the door, and into a small corridor. He looked around and saw the small holding room, where his pistols were being kept. He kicked the door open and grabbed the two pistols quickly. He didn’t have any spare ammo on him, so he would have to shout sparingly. That was never really a problem for him, more one for Jushin. He smiled and began to slowly creep out the door. He peaked his head into the corridor and immediately received fire. He pulled his head back in and began to look at the reflection in the window on the door. He saw three men standing, fully automatic weapons over their shoulders.
