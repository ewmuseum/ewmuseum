The Saga Ends? (Or Just The Beginning...)

5 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/05/06 Footsteps echoe through the darkened hallway as a silhouette marches to a familiar beat. Dress from head to toe in black, he becomes one with the darkness. With a deep breath he shoves aside the curtain and steps into the light. A large crowd awaits him and erupts as he faces them triumphantly and raises his right fist into the air where he disappears into a shower of silver sparks that rain from the sky. HIS REIGN OF TYRANNY... Duel black banners unravel from above and hang on each side of the Globaltron. Each shows an emblem... a black Z outlined in white, entertwined with an upside down silver cross. Slowly he takes off a pair of black Oakley's which conceal his cold, manipulative cadet blue eyes. A sadistic smile crosses his lips. He adjusts a jet black dress coat, before cracking his neck for action. IS LINED WITH GOLD The reflection of the Global Wrestling Heavyweight Championship rests inside his unforgiving blue eyes. Christian Zenith stands in the middle of the ring, holding his World Championship up in the air high over his head for the entire world to see with Vivica J. Valentine by his side. He looks down at a blooded Train with Mindy Mercer sitting helplessly in the background when a referee hands him the GW Championship. Standing in the darkness with the belt over his shoulder, he points back towards you. Relishing in the jeers of the crowd, Zenith holds the title in the air as Ken Partridge Jr. lays broken on the floor below the Hell In A Handbasket he just experienced. TWO TIME GLOBAL WRESTLING HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION Joshua Curtis crashes hard into the canvas of the octagon in The Freefall, followed closely by taunting from The Conscious Crisis before going for a pinfall. A Global Official counts for a three before Christian rises to his feet, forcing Jean-Claude Dafoe to reward him the Global International Championship. A shower of silver sparks rain from the heavens as he has his first raised high into the air with the International Championship around his waist. FORMER GLOBAL WRESTLING INTERNATIONAL CHAMPION Christian Zenith looks in frusteration after his debut loss to Venneman Pendragon in the Maximum Carnage World Cup Match. He hits Pendragon with the Freefall and then pins him for a convincing three count and his hand is raised high into the air. At Mideval Times, Zenith embarasses Venneman Pendragon by dragging the name of BOB through the mud, preaching his false conceptions to the crowd at hand. Gasping in awe, the crowd watches Christian dive off the Globaltron and land on top of Pendragon below, crashing through a series of tables shielding the concrete. Zenith rubs the GW Television Championship over his shoulder on his way to the ring. FORMER GLOBAL WRESTLING TELEVISION CHAMPION Images of Brick, Venneman Pendragon, Donny J. McNasty, and Adam Myers flash across the screen. Christian Zenith is tagged in by John Winters and lays out Adam Myers with a nasty clothesline. Team Jackson is declared the winners as Zenith lowers his arm before glaring around at his now ex-teammates and soon to be rivals. A battered Jesse Jamester looks in shock as Christian Zenith marches slowly down to the ring. Suddenly Jamester finds himself on his back with The Freefall. Josh Curtis suffers the same fate shortly after, and once again Zenith's hand is raised in dominance. 2004 SHOWDOWN IN TOKYO WINNER The crowd stands to their feet to boo Christian Zenith, who has Shadow, Dawn McFarlane, and Tessa Windsor by his side with their right fists high in the air for all of the crowd to see. Corvus stares down Zenith in the ring, but suddenly Shadow rushes in with the GW World Championship and smacks Corvus across the head with it, knocking him down to the mat. Zenith, Shadow, and McFarlane incite a riot against members of The Elite in the middle of the ring. Zenith taunts Jake Hix from outside the ring during his Super Series Match against Dawn McFarlane. The Hunting Grounds stand triumphant in the middle of the ring with their fists in the air, saluting the angered fans. LEADER OF ONE OF THE MOST TERRIFYING STABLES IN THE HISTORY OF GLOBAL WRESTLING Global Wrestling representative Jesse Jamester stands in the middle of the ring and looks in disbelief as PTC Wild Card representative and fedmate Christian Zenith walks out from the backstage area. Christian Zenith looks down at Jesse Jamester emotionless after defeating him and moving onto the Seven of the Best Finals. Karina Wolfenden looks around the arena before The Midnight Messiah kicks her in the stomach, lifts her up and powerbombs her to the outside of the ring. The crowd watches in horror as she bounces off an announce table and lands into the audiences chairs on the outside of the ring. Syckle looks up from outside the ring as Zenith glares at him after eliminating him. Danielle Northsky gets finished off with The Freefall before Zenith collects a victory and moves on in GTT5. 2005 PRIMETIME CENTRAL WRESTLER OF THE YEAR NOMINEE Christian Zenith throws a tied up Gunner Addison onto railroad tracks. She looks up at him with disgust, but is only met with a cold glare. Zenith and Global Hall of Famer Jean-Claude Dafoe stand nose to nose in the middle of the ring at WrestleCade. The crowd erupts into a chant of 'HOLY SHIT!' when Train is superplexed by Christian Zenith from the top rope through an announce table outside the ring. Ken Partridge Jr is thrown 30 feet down to the crowd below, losing his World Championship to The Midnight Messiah. ALL OF THIS... + Won #1 Contendorship to the World Championship in only 2 months + Nominated for Global Wrestling Rookie of the Year after only a month in the company + Held The World Championship more times in 2005 than any other Global Superstar + Never pinned or made to submit for the World Championship + Held the International Championship for a quarter of a year + Victories over more than 5 Global Wrestling Hall of Fame members + Was the favorite in all of his GTT5 matchups + Ended former GW World Champion Mindy Mercer's career + Injured Train twice in the same month, the second of which he hasn't returned from + Has never been eliminated in the first round of a tournment in his career + Multiple time #1 on Global Wrestling Top Ten AND THAT WAS JUST A LITTLE OVER ONE YEAR Christian Zenith stands tall and alone under a spotlight surrounded by darkness. The lone star amoung the black mediocrity, patting the Global World Championsh that rests on his right shoulder. The image of a true champion. 2006 Christian Zenith's arm is raised in victory repeatedly over various times, arenas, and matches. One by one, they all fell to the wrath of The Conscious Crisis. MANCHESTER, ENGLAND The Flag of the United Kingdom flies high over Buckingham Palace. A packed arena erupts as pyrotechnics erupts ushering in the new year. RELOAD 2006 The same silhouette from the beginning is seen once again, walking not towards the light... but fading into the darkness. DOES THE SAGA END HERE? A pair of cadet blue eyes flash across the screen. OR IS IT JUST THE BEGINNING... 'The Conscious Crisis' Christian Zenith
