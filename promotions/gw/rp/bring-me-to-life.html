Bring Me To Life

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/29/30 Bright Golden Arches, fill the view as the scene opens up. The camera zooms out, to see a McDonald's restaurant, glass windows, with a brick base surround the entire building. The camera pans to the right to see an empty parking lot. Then pans to the right to see a single man, sitting all alone, staring down at a tray full of food. The cameraman approaches the lone customer, and realizes just who the man is, from the lond blond hair. The cameraman takes a seat directly across from Ice. Ice doesn't even acknowledge his presence, and continues to stare down, at a box, which he is twirling around in a circle with his right index finger. The camera pans down from Ice's face to get a look at what he is doing and notices a BIG MAC box. 'Hunger. What drives most men. Hunger. The ultimate feeling, that takes you above and beyond what you are humanly able to do. Your hunger, to prove that you are a somebody, that is respectable. Your hunger, to hold that title belt you place in such high prestige, that is respectable. But your hunger, to be something you are not...your will to make unintelligent comments, that just gets you in trouble. You see when I step through those ropes, and into that ring, I expect, getting poked in the eye, punched in the face, kicked in the nuts, and everything and anything in between.' Ice looks up from his BIG MAC box, and stares intensly into the camera's lens. Ice turns his head to the left, away from the camera, and gazes out of the window. A car circles the parking lot, and heads into the drive thru. Ice continues to gaze out of the window. ' THE DEAD ZONE. The BIGGEST VENUE in this business's history. Kind of fitting, that it all begins at the place from which everything we live for began twenty years ago. Yet so many of you, worry too much about your personal lives. So many of you don't focus enough, and don't concern yourselves enough with the task at hand. My personal life, what I concern myself with, and what I focus on is one in the same. THIS BUSINESS IS MY LIFE. My family, my friends, are all those thousands of people who fill our arenas. They are what drive me. They are what make me who I am. And this Saturday night, when I walk into Madison Square Garden, they will let you know just who it is, that I am! Ice turns his focus back to the camera lens. He slowly raises his left hand, and pulls any strands of his long blond hair, out from in front of his face. His cold, icy, blue eyes pierce through the camera lens. ' For far too long now I have been dormant. Sitting back, watching, waiting. Waiting for that moment in time, when everything lined up just right. Well the allignments are right, and it's is time to wake from my hibernation. It is time to walk back into the world that created me, and the world I have come to take back over. When the pyrotechnics go off, and the lights dim. The music will flow through the arena like blood through my veins. The people I live for, my family, my friends, the crowd will stand on their feet, and Madison Square Garden will shake and tremble at the noise they bring. Eleven other men will be standing in anticipation in the middle of the ring. But as I walk through those curtains, the cold will hit their unsuspecting bodies. The hairs on their arms, and the back of their necks will stand on end, and it will be at that point, they will realize just what fate has instore for them. It will be at that specific moment in time, that all the watching, all the waiting, all the broken bones, and all the sacrifice will be worth it. It will be then, that the GW finally gets to see what it is I have been talking about. It will be then that the GW gets to experience the cold, the pain, and the anguish, as one by one the eleven other men in that Battle Royal are put on ICE! Ice slams his fist down, crushing the BIG MAC box. The camera zooms in on the crushed box, and the scene fades.
