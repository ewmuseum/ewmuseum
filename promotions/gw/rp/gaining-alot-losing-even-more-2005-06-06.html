Gaining alot...Losing even more

6 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-6-05 “Get the side door.” “What side door?” “That side door you jackass.” The sounds of foot steps came into play as the wind blew through the parking lot. Like a powerful horse race going on in a bright sunny day, the men raced around, splashing through the water that was filled in every hole on the concrete. “Don’t go in until I give the call.” A voice said over the intercom radio. He stood tall. Dark hair and no smile to cover his bright red lips. A cop. One that was always through when you need him and right now I needed him the most. I stood next to a cop car. The wind blowing every lengthy hair that had been growing through my skull. My dreams becoming reality, like the gods above rained down through my head to pull them out. I didn’t want anything happening to my uncle but I knew this was the only option to do. Kevin, the strongest one right now, placed his hand on my bitter shaky shoulder as the cold rushed up the leg of my tight pants. “Sergeant?” “Yes?” “The coast is clear from this side.” “Ok, on my mark people.” The screams seem to becoming from both sides. Driving the heart of my ear drum in a twist to try and keep up. Nothing seem to make sense until the entire area stopped, paused so to speak, for one single little line that was said. “GO!” The men raced through the darkness, driving their entire bodies through glass, doors, and wood that was placed up on the house. My heart raced ninety to nothing, almost pumping through out my chest. My mind wanted to be in there, to see, to feel, to think for what was going on but I knew I was needed out here. I turned around, covering my head deep into the soul of Kevin, forgetting all the was around me for a split second but it was no use. “GET DOWN!” Screamed a man that my ears slowly picked up. “AWWW!” “I SAID GET DOWN!” And as quick as it began, it had ended. Several shots, blood everywhere, and my heart wanting to be somewhere else. ************* It had been hours after the battle was over. The coffee was bad but it was the only thing other then the sound of my Uncle’s voice that would of kept be awake. Kevin, still standing and as nervous as me stood right there beside me, his hand on my shoulder still. “I can’t stand it anymore Kevin.” Wiping away the single tear that came rolling down my face. “Where is he?” “He will be here. I just know it.” He said with a pause in his voice. The cold summer night blew in still, blowing the hair that stood on Kevin’s arms. I could feel a change in the night. As if something was coming out of the house finally. As if a part of me was finally brought back. I lifted my head up to see a figure walking out. Blood stained shirt with a single rip in it on the right sleeve. He was limping severely on his left leg, limping like a person with a broken ankle would be. His life seemed to be gone from his soul, drained from the blood veins that dwelled through out his body. Passing by the other men and women that ran in an out of the prison that he was held in. “UNCLE JOHNSON!” “Elia!” Screamed Kevin. “Uncle Johnson, is it you?” Running at him like a mother who had just found her son or daughter. My heart skipped a beat. Nothing in this world could ever seem close to the way I felt right now. I knew I was finally complete. Finally a whole woman. My chest raged, not of sexual love but of wonderful joy that begged to be let out. My hands wrapped around his shoulders, pressing hard to his back as he returned the favor. “God how I missed you Uncle.” I said, feeling the tears pour down like rain. “I’ve missed you to.” Uncle Johnson said back, pouring out his love for me and the world. “I swear I’ll never let you down again.” I responded. “Never again.” I pulled away from him, smiling as big as I could before pulling him back once again. **************** “I’m glad you did it.” Kevin was sitting on the front porch of the house. Looking up at the stars that poured out from the middle of nowhere. Lighting up the pitch dark sky that settled above us. My breath hovered out of my mouth as I took a seat next to Kevin, next to the calm state that he seemed to be in. His wasn’t saying much. Maybe he was thinking. Maybe he was dreaming. Whatever it was, he wasn’t talking. “Are you ok?” I asked. Nothingness. A deep and sorrow silence still settled over us. My heart seemed to come to a complete pause as I tried once again. “I’m sorry Kevin for not trusting in you but you have to understand?” “I have to understand?” He said with a bitter voice. “I have to understand nothing. You took a matter that could of cost a life under your own ways and didn’t tell anyone. How could you?” “I was told not to.” My eyes felt as if they were watering up as I sat there. I placed both my hands on his forehead, trying to displace myself from the world that I was sitting in right now. Hoping that Kevin would somehow understand and that I wouldn’t feel smaller then I already did. “He was your Uncle. The man that took you under when you and your family split away. And this is how you repay him” You’re not the person I thought you were.” Kevin said. Basically saying what his heart felt. “Kevin?” Raising up from the ash setting on my hands as I looked over to see Kevin walking away. My heart just couldn’t recover right now. What was a girl that prayed for so much and getting it all, to lose everything once again. I could feel the water of tears coming from deep inside my self, from deep in my eyes, as it began to pour down the sides of my face. I looked up at the moon before lowering my head once again.
