Devil's Night Aftermath

6 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: week of 11/6 DISCLAIMER: The following contains spoilers to the events depicted in King of the Death Match Series II: Devil's Night, in addition to some offensive language. ---- Brick has had a rough few days. It all started at Dark Rituals when he faced Bobby Brawler in a Graveyard Match. The match was booked when GW On-site Director Brandi Sights needed someone to face Brawler, who had been attacking various Global Wrestling superstars since his return. Brandi needed someone who could go toe-to-toe with him. The someone she chose was Brick. It was one of the most brutal matches of the evening, almost turning deadly at points. Brawler was ready to end the whole thing (and Brick's life) with a sledgehammer when out of nowhere Mindy Mercer made the save, causing Brawler to drop the sledgehammer and concentrate on her. Brick took the opportunity to grab the sledgehammer, only to miss Bralwer's head and destroy the camera. The match ended in a no-contest with no clear winner. As far as Brick was concerned, the matter was far from over. ---- The date: October 30th The place: Detroit, Michigan. The event: King of the Death Match Series II: Devil's Night Brawler stood in the ring talking about the KOTDM Series when suddenly the lights went out. When they came back, who should be standing behind him ready to attack but Brick? Unfortunately, Brawler was ready for him, nailing him square in the forehead with a C4 loaded chair. Before Brick could recover, security swarmed on top of him, handcuffing him. Ever the opportunist and sadist, Brawler assaulted him while he was handcuffed. Before the guards could carry Brick off, the lights went out yet again. When the lights came back on, it was Mindy Mercer this time, anxiously toting a kendo stick. The instant Brawler turned around... WHACK!!! Mindy busted Brawler wide open. But Brick still hadn't had enough. His eagerness to settle the score with Brawler gave him the strength to break his handcuffs and slide back into the ring, delivering a clothesline that knocked Brawler out of the ring over the barbwire ropes. A dozen more security guards surrounded Brawler and both Brick and Mindy tried to get through only to be arrested by real police officers. Brick was forced to remain in a squad car. It still wasn't over. Later in the show, Brick had escaped from police custody by destroying the squad car from the inside. He then managed to hit the spear on Brawler and nail GW's World champion Christian Zenith with a Bricklayer. Brick had gotten a measure of payback against Brawler and had managed to send a message to the new World champion. The price: a few pints of blood and an outstanding arrest warrant in the Motor City. ---- 'No, I don't see the problem, Lieutenant,' Brandi said over the phone as she sat at her desk. 'Brick committed no crime that night. If anything, he was more of a victim than Brawler was. Or perhaps you'd like me to cry 'police corruption' to the press...I didn't think so. Have a nice day.' After she hung up the phone, the smile on her face turned into a frown as she looked at Brick, who sat across her desk from her. 'You know, if you weren't the draw you are and the Committee didn't want you out of this jam, I'd probably have let the Detroit police lock you up and throw away the key. What the hell were you thinking, attacking Brawler like that?' 'He and I had unfinished business from Dark Rituals. You know how I hate unfinished business. By the way, I still owe Blaise McClancy an ass-whipping.' A few weeks earlier, Brick was in a triple threat match with both McClancy and Ambrosi for a shot at the Platinum Title. At the climax of the match, Ambrosi pulled Brick into a spray of mist from Blaise's mouth, blinding him with it. Taking advantage, Ambrosi hit the Cocytus Drop for the win. 'I thought you were going after Virgil Ambrosi for that. I mean, he was the one who pulled you into McClancy's line of fire.' 'Yeah, but how do I know McClancy wouldn't have done that shit anyway? You didn't feel that mist in your eyes the way I did. It stung like hell.' Brandi merely rolled her eyes. 'Is there anyone in this company you're not looking to pick a fight with these days? Attacking Brawler is one thing, but that Bricklayer you gave to Christian Zenith? He's hard enough to keep in check as it is.' 'So? I ain't afraid o' Zenith. And I ain't afraid o' his little hoes, either.' 'I don't know what I'm gonna do to keep you calm.' 'Well, the way you used to calm me down ain't exactly appropriate these days,' Brick remarked with a smirk. Very little would have made Brandi Sights blush. Brick referencing their past 'friends with benefits' relationship was one of them. There was already a rumor circulating about an old sex tape featuring the two of them. 'And speaking of McClancy, I want him this coming Evo.' 'I'm afraid I can't do that.' Brick was not happy to hear that. 'Fuck you mean you can't do that?' 'He's already booked in a match with Mike Biddle. However, I can put you in a match with him if you and Train beat Unite.' 'Those two faggots who hang out with Danielle Northsky?' 'I'd appreciate it if you didn't use that word. A faggot is a bunch of sticks.' 'Fine, those two bunches of sticks who hang out with Danielle Northsky. How's that?' 'Not funny, Brick.' 'If it means getting closer to McClancy, fine. Hell, I'll take him, Brawler, Zenith an' all their bitches if I have to.' 'You seem to have a lot of restlessness since Salaok was kicked out.' Right away, Brick rose out of his seat and leaned over Brandi's desk, getting in her face. 'Why did you just say his name?' 'Well, Brick, the only time you were this ready to kill someone was when he attacked your loved ones. If he wasn't banned from competing in Global...' 'Even if he wasn't banned, he wouldn't be coming back.' 'You say that like it's a certainty.' 'I think it is.' Brandi then started to think. 'Brick, did you do something to...? 'I don't wanna talk about this anymore.' Right away, Brick stormed out of Brandi's office, leaving Brandi bewildered.
