Bad Dream, Black Knight

16 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Evo #1 (8/16/04) It was obvious that the late night programing on Cinemax was not given enough credit for it's contribution to the problems facing society. Train's jaw swung around in circles, opening and closing as another piece of the overstuffed submarine sandwhich disappeared into his mouth. Beads of sweat slid down the surface of the half drunk 2 liter of Cherry Coke while it sat idly on the night stand next to the bed. Train relaxed himself semi-reclined, propped up against his oak headboard, the two pillows propped up behind him provinding cushion. He was feeling particularly good right now. The roach laying in the ash tray that was partialy hidden behind the 2 liter had once been a whole cigar about an hour ago. However, Train had put an end to that, causing it and its contents to go up and smoke. He'd also begun putting an end to the sudden urge for a snack afterwards, throwing together a hearty snack and twisting open the soda. It wasn't long after that Train started pondering the ills of society through the view of late night cable. The programing definitely had something to say. The two women wrestled visciously, tugging eachother's hair and throwing eachother about. Occassionally one of their breast would threaten to slip out the side of their white t-shirts that had been converted to sleeveless by what looked like some inexact cutting tool. Their tight, cut off shorts, legs trimmed up in an arch on the sides making them about as close to underwear as denim can be threaten to expose similar intimate revelations. Yes, it was obvious 'Caged Heat 6' was a very insightful piece of cinema that offered serious commentary ont he state of society. For instance, the whole issue of Gay Marriage... the film stated plainly...who cares? Over the past 35 minutes Train had watched several women become intimate in there cells, the shower, the warden's office and it all said one thing to him on the issue. As long as the gays getting married are both hot women, it's all good. Also the violence inside American prisons, that apparently only resulted in in very small trickles of blood on the lip or the side of the mouth, was apalling. Of course as he saw one of those make shift, white cotton tank tops discentigrates, torn to shreds revealing what it was once hiding it didn't seem quite so horrible any more. Train sat up a little, eagerly anticipating the next piece of ripped clothing when the digitized version of 'Brush Your Shoulders Off' by Jay-Z breaks his concentration. He sets his sandwhich down with an irritated groan before reaching over to the nightstand on the other side of the bed, retrieving his silver cell phone. Setting himself back up straight Train grudgingly flips the phone open and put it to his ear. 'Hel...' Train begins before being quickly cut off and drowned out by the slightly shrill, demanding voice... 'Are you watching the tapes?!' demanded the voice. 'Jerry....' Train said, pausing to emphasize the obvious displeasure in his voice. 'What the hell are you calling me up yellin' into my God damn ear? You know what time it is?' 'Let me tell you something Tony... Ok? Just because you sleep? Doesn't mean the business does. Ok?!' 'Jerry... You blowing my high dogg. Stop babbling and tell me what the hell you want.' 'What do I want? What do I want?!' 'Did I stutter?' 'I'm your agent Tony! What I want is for you to make money so I can make some too. And le me tell you something Tony... You're gonna make the most money when you're the champ! Ok? See I had those Giovanni Rims guys ringing my phone off the hook a couple of days ago begging for the GW Platinum Champion to come on with the Platinum Belt and being their new print add spokesman. I call them back today and now they're fidgeting away because if they're not sure if you're still gonna be the Platinum Champion after you go up against this Pendragon guy. Now I tried to tell 'em this no name didn't stand a chance against you, but there were a little scared that some tall, blonde, white guy that talks like he came out a bad Robin Hood movie wouldnt' really help them pick up costumers in XXL or The Source. BET still wants you in there to read for the host of Mad Sports and How I'm Livin'... I'm telling you Tony... That Platinum? It's absolute gold. You want to make some real money, you got to hold on to it.' 'Oh, for real? Damn man, I'm glad you told me that... I was thinking about melting the shit down and turning it into a new chain.' 'Don't mess with me.' 'hahahahaha.... Either that or welding it to the hood of the Nav and using it as a hood ornament! hahahaha....' 'Oh, that's....tha...tha... that's cute. Real cute Tony..... Tony....' 'What?' 'Do you know how much money I paid... Out of pocket no less, to have that editor put together those tapes of Pendragon for you?! I mean we've got every move, and every telegraph the guy's ever sent there, forward, reverse and in slow motion... C'mon Tony... Do this thing for me man. Study the tape. Study this tape so you know what's coming and take this guy out quickly! I'm telling you man... No, I'm promising you... You keep that belt over your shoulder, go into the ppv and smack this McNasty punk around for like the 20th time in a row and promising people are gonna be throwing cash your way. So watch the tapes.' 'I'll watch 'em Jerry.' 'Uhuh..... Watch the tapes!' 'I said I'd watch 'em damn it! Now you yell in my ear one more muthafuckin' time you're gonna get the same Hollowpoint I'ma give to Pendragon!' 'Alright, alright.... Now there's only one other thing I need to tell you.' 'What?' 'WATCH THE TAPES!!!' Click. Train yanks the phone away from his ear half way through the tirade, a scowl etched on his face. But even pulled away, the message came through loud and clear. Train snapped the phone shut with a grunt, having ruled it too much of a pain in the ass to call back and let Jerry have it. Besides that Train knew Jerry was looking out for his best intererst... His own anyway. And, fact is, Jerry's best interest is Train's best interest because he gets a piece of it. Little leech. Jerry was just lucky Train needed an agent, otherwise he'd have stomped his ass a long time ago. Train shakes his head at the conversation and turns his attention back to this evenings cinema only to find the closing credits scrolling up the screen. Train lets out another frustrated grunt. Finally, unlike he's done in several hours, he lifts his eyes to the cardboard package laying at the top of the 32' screen. The Fed/ex guy had dropped it off way earlier today, he hadn't bothered to open it since he spotted Jerry's name on the 'From:' section of the label. Normally it's some crap Jerry wants him to agree to endorse that Train would rather eat glass than do, but now he knew what it really was. 'Fuck it.' Train relents. He slides off the edge of the bed and walks over, retrieving the box from the television. He quickly rips th packaging open and dumps out the contents, two VHS tapes sliding out into his hand. Train tosses the box in the nearby trash can and takes a tape in each hand. The one in the right labeled 'Pendragon Matches' and the one in the left 'Pendragon Moves'. Train scans over them for a moment, trying to decide which one will suck the least to watch and finally settles on the matches. Maybe he can get a bead on this guy. He'd been wondering all week about what this guys deal was. Is this just some stupid 'gimmick' to get the fans to give two shits about him or is this muthafucka really nuts? While the first was stupid, it was pretty harmless. Just another curtain jerker looking for a way to sell a few more t-shirts and posters to supplement his retirement before he finally winds up having to give it up when he gets some vertebrae compressed or his neck twisted the wrong way. The other? That... that's not good. Genuinely crazy bastards can be dangerous. There's no telling what the bastards might do at any given moment. Train slid the tape into the vcr and trudges back to the bed, falling back into his spot. Train grabs the tiny, universal remote that had been sitting next to him earlier. Train finds the proper combination of buttons and the vcr whirs to life. The screen come sto life with images of his opponent's past exploits... scrub matches in scrub promotions... the crowds didn't look to number much over 2... 300 people. Train watched Veneman Pendragon. The way he moved...his style of offense... He was big. Lot of power. Had the height to use the power with a lot of leverage. Slow... methodical... Yeah... Crazy or not he's a threat. However, there was no doubt his ass was crazy. Going around in that chain mail.... Damn pants that look like the shit Hammer wore in the 80's.............. Talkin' like some....*Yawn*....reject from Romeo and Juliet......................... Muthafucka needs a reality check.................................... Need to....*Yawn*....................... Kick his ass on.......... general................ principle...................................................... *SNORE* ===== ===== ===== ===== ===== WOOOOOOPSH!!!! It had been dark for a moment, but the searing pain that shot up his back, lit up his life. Maybe it was some kind of genetic memory inherited from his forefathers, but somehow, Train knew that crack that hit him about the same time as the pain came from some kind of whip. As Train stumbled forward in the dark, he finally realized that there were others in the dark space with him...crammed in there with him to be more accurate. However, they quickly cleared out as Train charged forward, his instinct to put as much distance between whoever it was that hit him and where he was. Train bulled his way through the crowd and burst out through a heavy burlap curtain into the light.... Suddenly Train freezes. His eyes open wide, not just dialating to the light, opening as wide as possible to make sure what he sees is real. Train is a little young to have seen a small English village in the 17 or 1800's... but if that's not where the hell he is, then he didn't know what was going on. Actually even if he was, he didn't know what was going on! 'Oh, hhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhell naw!!!' Train exclaimed with is jaw hanging agape. This shit wasn't right... Train's massive frame stumbled out of the holding pen, the uneven wooden planks rattling with each heavy steps. Train finally pulls himself to a halt in the center stage. He turns out and let's his eyes scan the scene. It's obvious to Train that this is the townsquare and this stage sits dead in the center. What was obviously the front of the stage was easily identified by the mass of humanity, staring back at him as he looked out in that direction. They stared. He stared back. There two quick conlcusions Train drew... One, they obviously didn't know what the hell to make of him. Most of them were obviously poor, covering themselves with ancient looking rags that Goodwill wouldn't even try to sell on a bad day. Train, decked out his his black and white Adidas warm up pants and ribbed, white cotton tank top, complete with a small, unexplained mustard stain at the center of the chest, obviously looked as alien to them as they did to him. Number 2, this had to be England cuz he'd never seen a larger collection of pasty, snaggled tooth white folks in his entire life. 'How in the fuck?' Train said himself. He raised his hand up to his head, attempting to scrach his temple to scratch as is customary in a confusing situation,l but stops halfway. He finds it difficult to manuever well with his wrists bound by rope. 'What the fuck?!' Suddenly the stage began to rattle and shake again and Train turned to watch dozens of more men trudge out onto the stage. They were bound like him, but he still stuck out like a sore thumb with these men that looked to be as much from this time as the people in the audience. Finally the burlap curtian parts again and outsteps the man who obviously figured he was in charge. His clothes, unlike everyone else's clothes, his were spotless, including his gay ass looking tights and his ruffly coat that sparkled with gold threading. Train might have thought to laugh at him, but the sword that hung off his hip made him think twice. However he definitely still considered whipping the punk's ass. That shot of pain from earlier had obviously come from him since he was the one wrapping up a long, black whip as he strolled arrogantly out onto the stage after the rest of the crowd. The Train finally spotted what gave the man the impression that he could walk around like his shit didn't stink. Yeah...that gold star attatched to his chest had probably done that trick. Train shoved his way through the crowd, freeing himself from the masses and making his prescence clear to the head chump in charge. 'Hey yo,' Train begun, stepping past the crowd defiantly. 'What's this bullshit man? You can just hold me here son. Yall muthafuckas didn't even read me my rights. I want my fucking lawyer!' 'Quiet Naive!!!' demanded the officer in a haughty, pompous tone Train could only assume was the only one he had. 'Ye are but a lowly theif and debtor in her majesty's kindom! Such a criminal as the shalt not be extended the right to demand any sorth of thing, nor speak to me in such a manner. Cry though ye may, ye shalt not recieve thy rights until thine debt hath been repaid in thrice years indentured servitude.' .......... 'What?' Train said, insisting on clarification. 'We ain't letting yo ass go till you pay back the money you owe!' 'Money?! I don't owe you know damn money!' 'Oh, but of course you don't owe me.' said the officer. 'I'm am, but a mere public servant. But... what of these people?' he asks, pointing out the crowd. 'Shit, what about 'em?' Train responds snidely. 'They hath put their faith in the... trusting the that thou might deliver unto them entertainment for their hard earned money. They call the champion yet the langquish without show nor attempt to live out thy title. You have stolen from them! Failed to live up to thy post.' 'Failed to live up to.... Man, fuck all that! I been whuppin' ass since day one up in this bitch!' 'Perhaps that is the way the started off... However thou came in like a lion and hath gone out like bitch.' 'What the fuck did you just say?! Fool, you done lost yo' damn mind... Who the hell do you think you are?!' 'Hold your tounge sir! You speak to the Sherriff of Knottingham! Rosgoth P. Coltrane!!!' 'Whoa, whoa, whoa,' Train insists. 'Hold up.... Don't I know you from somewhere?' 'That, sir depends, on your viewing habits of 80's television.' 'What?' 'Be ye not concerned with it. Be more concerned with thy fate...of what the King shall do to the when he arrives to pass judgement on the for thy lack of championship deservedness! Me thinks thou shalt most likely meet thy end in the arena for the entertainment of his new bride, Lady Pendragon.' 'Man, fuck Lady Pendragon! I want my...' Train suddenly stops with no choice in the matter. As soon as Coltrane's back hand hits his jaw, Train stumbles back as step, but quickly recovers his balance. He rights himself and charges forward, but is caught and held from behind even as Coltrane leaps back and half draws his sword, only pausing as he sees Train restrained. Train struggles against the wide arms that hold him, but, with his hands tied there is little he can do. 'Get yo' hands off me!' Train yells to his unseen assailant. 'Nay! Praise the hand that holds you as it saves your life!' Coltrane smirks as he shoves the sword back into the sheath, walking away towards the far side of the stage. Train tries to follow, but the grip that binds him is like iron. 'Hold on son. You gotta wait till the time is right.' 'Aight....' mumbles Train, finally starting to calm himself. 'Aight!' He insists again, pulling himself free from the relenting grip. Train spins to eye whoever it is that's had the nerve to put his hands on him. 'Relax boy. I ain't trying to stop you from runnin'. Just help you live longe enough to get your chance!' says the man who had held him away from the sherriff. Train expected a stranger, but got more...much more in a very strange package... Train figured he was alone in this, but he turned to see yet another black man. And what's more Train's face was forced to turn up into a bright, yet puzzled grin, realizing that he knew this man. His clothes looked pretty old, but they were more modern than anything anyone else was wearing, even if it was just as threadbare. It was James Evans... Husband of Florida and father to Micheal, Thelma and, of course, J.J. 'Oh shit!' Train yelled in excitement, distracted momentarily from the disrespect and dismissal of his worth that haunts him. 'How the hell did you get here Mr. Evans? I thought you died in Alaska?' 'My name ain't James boy!' yelled actor John Amos. 'It's Kunta... Kunta Kinte!' 'Oh damn, my bad dogg! I got that shit mixed up.' 'Eh... No problem. Damned if that don't happen alot. Tell you what.... I like you kid, so you can just call me Toby for short.' 'Toby? I thought you hated that shit?' 'I do, but nobody ever remembers Kunta. And now the office of Homeland Security thinks the shit sounds Muslim instead of African so the keep profiling me, trying to give me cavity searches in the airport and shit.' 'Damn.... Yeah, I can see your problem there.... But dogg, if you gonna change your name you should change it to something more diginified man.' 'Like what?' 'Ummm.... I don't know. How 'bout....... Tobias! Now that's a nice name.' 'Tobias.... Damn.... I like the sound of that. Better than that Toby shit!' 'Damn straight. But anyway... Seriously, what the hell are you doing here?' 'Hell if I know, it's your dream.' 'Oh.... right....' 'All I know is you got to pick your spots. You let them fools talk about how you supposed to do this and that as the champ. I say if you the champ you do what the hell you want. Don't do what they expect, do what they don't. If you do that, you won't get caught and wind up with one of these...' says Kinte as he stomps the wooden peg that has taken the place of his foot. 'Oh shit.' Train says, remembering how that viscious wound was inflicted. 'That was fucked up how they did you with the foot man.' 'That's alright.' says Kinte. 'I'll be taking my one good foot and puttin' it up somebody's ass the first chance I get!' 'I hear that man.' 'You boys need to learn some better things to use your feet for.' sasy a new, but familiar voice. Train and Kunta turn to spy out the voices owner and Train has to crack yet another puzzled grin. The man's dress is out of place...mostly as his toga looks kind of like a dress and his sandal look very worn. But still the man grins at both men with a friendly smile. 'Gregory Hines?' asks Train, wondeirng if it was possible to have two famous strangers flanking him. 'Not Gregory man.... Josephus!' 'Josephus?' both men reply. 'Yeah... And I'm telling yall... ain't nothing wrong with givign the audience a little show. I ain't saying you gotta do everything just based on what they want... but when they dig you more you get more.' 'Oh, for real?' Train asks with just a bit more than a passing air of sarcasm. 'Damn right. Trust me. I been through all this being sold off for the arena stuff before man. It was back in Rome, but it's all the same! All I got to do is fine me a little sand, do the little African Sand Sham dance and I get off the hook. Happens everytime.' 'Nigga, there ain't no sand in Enland!' yells Kunta. 'What the hell you talking about? It's a damn island!' 'Well this ain't the damn beach fool.' 'You know what? That don't even matter! I don't really need no sand. It's all about the show baby. And let me tell you something peg leg. You best find away to keep these folks out here entertained before these english bastards take you to the arena and feed you to the Irish.' 'What hell you talking about, feed us to the Irish?' ask Train, his face reading puzzlement yet again. 'C'mon man....' Josephus says with exassperation, as if the answer were perfectly obvious. 'You know all the Irish wanna be Blackified... Just look at that McNasty fool! And besides that... All they got is potatos! Hell, us?! We got fried chicken, collard greens, black eyed peas, macaroni and cheese, sweet potatoes, candy yams, chittlins, corn bread... We got too much soul not to taste better than a potato.' 'In a twisted ass way that makes sense.' Train relented. 'Hell naw it don't!' yells Kunta. 'You and damn fool...' he further insists, shoving Josephus. Josephus is quick to shove back. 'Whoa, whoa,' Train yells stepping in between two two sides. 'Ain't no need to fight about this shit. I hear both of you. It's all good. Besides that,' Train starts staring off into the distance... 'I think we got bigger problems now.' Joesephus and Kunta both turn just in time to see the crowd begin to part. Two lines of white horses, their riders done up in bring red uniforms with gold trim, trot through the masses. They make their way up to the front of the stage as a gleaming red 4-horse drawn carriage follows them in. The carriage gleams with the obvious hint of royalty written it all over. The carriage is a bright, candy apple red with the bars and steps on it gleaming with gold as well as the ornate crest on the doors. The two line or horses break off in different directions, traveling parralel to the front of the stage. It's obvious to Train that that's crowd control, the horses and riders, decked out in their nifty uniforms topped off with the swords hanging boldly at their sides. The new guest to the party is obviously looking to get up close and personal. The sherriff quickly motions for two of his men to bring in and place a set of stairs at the front of the stage as the carriage veers off to the left, running parralel to the stage. Finally the ornate vehicle pulls to a halt and a bevy of no-name servants scurry over and make all the neccessary perperations to recieve royalty. Rolling out the carpet, putting down the carriage stairs and, finally, opening the door. It takes a moment, but finally a figure emerges. the man, his clothing plush an velvetine, his strong jaw and features making him look regal, steps from the carriage, his long cape trailing behind him in the hands of yet even more no-name servants. As he walks the sunlight gleams from every inch and jewel of the crown atop his head. The sherriff quicly ushers the crowd on stage back to make room for the new arrival. 'Thy Lord, King.' says Coltrane, bowing as he bears the man honor. The king dips his head in response to the sherriff. He lets his eyes slowly and methodically scan the scene and the people...his eyes finally locking onto to something of interest. The kings face breaks with a small grin and he slowly walks over to stand toe to toe with Train. 'So....' begins the Lord, King. 'Thou art the supposed champion. The man that represents the best in all the world of competition. So valuable...ye art worth thy weight in platinum?' 'God damn right.' Train responds without a moments hesitation. 'An impudent whelp as well, aye? I shall take great pride in watching the die as I feed the to the Irish in the arena.' 'Whatever man. Bottom line? Yall can talk all the shit you want.... I'm the champ. Ain't shit you can do to change that.' 'Really?' asks the Lord, King. 'Then perhaps thou might explain how it is that this has come into my possession?' The Lord, King pushes his arm forward, parting the cape that covers so much of his torso. He flings it open with one powerful swipe. He reveals the crown is not the only thing that proves his status... The GW Platinum Championship wrapped around his waist emphasizes it even more. Train just stares at him and the belt he's somehow managed to steal for a long, silent moment. 'You got 5 seconds to give me by belt back.' Train states as coldy as he does plainly. 'Aye? And what will the do if I shant relent my prize?' 'One....' 'Do ye trully dare to threaten thy Lord, King?!' 'Two....' 'Guards!' No sooner does the command hit the air than the horsemen that lead in the carriage dismount and storm the stage. The King quickly steps back as the men form a semi-circle in front of him. 'Three....' Suddenly the line shifts just a bit. The Sherriff of Knottingham does his duty, stepping into the center of the semi circle. 'Protect the Lord, King!' he yells, reminding everyone of their duty as well. 'Four....' 'Draw thy swords!!!' yells the sherriff, pulling his own blade in a flash of light of the chrome like finish. Suddenly Train lunges forward, raising his arms high... with all his might he pulls his wrists apart as far as they will come and swings his arms down... The sherriff's outstretched blade, meant to take Train's life, grants him his freedom as Train swings, bring the ropes down across the sharp edge of the rapier, cutting him free! 'Five muthafucka!' The initial shock is all Train needs. He side steps the point of the sword and lunges forward nailing Rosgoth P. Coltrane in the face with a reverse elbow... The sherriff nearly crumbles to the ground there...would have if Train hadn't needed him for another task. Before Coltrane can hit the ground Train grabs hold of his collar and swings the man around, sending the sherriff barreling forward off the side of the stage...taking the whole row of swordsmen that were standing in his path with him. Train turns back. lunging for the kick, but quickly drops to a knee, narrowly dodging the swipe of a sword meant for his head. Train pops back up and launches himself backwards creating as much distance between him and the flailing blades as possible. 'Off with his head!' roars the King, Lord, still standing behind his personal guard. Train stands his ground, quickly using every synapse he has to reason a way through the defense to his prized possession, but the thick voice of one of his compatriots calls to him. 'They expect you to come at them straight on!' yells Kunta. 'Do the unexpected!!!' Train swivels his head to face his advisor. He turns back quickly, taking only a second to review the situation and calculate the odds an his head... It was definitely no good. Train points his Finger to the Lord, King... 'I'll be back for what's mine.' Suddenly Train turns and gets a running start. It only takes a few steps and he is airborn. He sails through the air, all eyes turning towards him as he flies... Finally Train lands on the upper deck of the Lord, King's carriage! Train quickly settles into the seat and grabs the reigns of the 4 horses, assuming control of the archaic vehicle. One of the footsoldiers tries to leap up to grab Train, but only catches the bottom of Train's boot in the mouth. As if he was an old hand at it Train whips the reigns charges up the horses. The carriage takes off like a shot, streaking red as it accelerates down the medieval road leaving a cloud of dust behind him. Train whips the reigns again, the carriage picks up even more speed... he manages to get a quick glance at at the wooden sign that points down the road he's traveling down... 'To Sherwood Forrest'.... Train travels for a few minutes, trying to calculate his next move... trying to muster a plan to retrieve the object he fought so long to get. It's hard for him to hear himself thing with the loud ringing of the hoof beats as they pound on the ground beneath the carriage. It takes a moment, but he finally notices the hoof beats growing louder.. a moment after that it occurs to him, as the horses are travelign with him, it can't possibly be his horses making that extra noise. Train turns as the carriage continues to fly down the road. Behind him he can see a plume of dust flying up far behind the spot where the dust he's kicked up had already resettled. They were after him. Fucking cops.... Train knows, even with the 4 horses in front of him, hauling the carriage behind means it's only a matter of time before those single horses and riders finally catch up to him. 'Aight muthafuckas....' Train begins, pumping himself up, for the next move he knows he needs to make. 'You wanna play? Aight... Let's play.' Train looks around at the heavy brush and trees that flies by in fast forward... His eyes scan the embankment and the heavy brush that lines it... 'Ride or die!!!' Suddenly Train takes flight again... The only thing Train hears from there is a series of pop and snaps he can only hope isn't his any bones... No sharp pains so it must be the underbrush he's falling through as his body tumbles down the embankment... It seems like hours before he finally comes to a stop. He can only lie there, staring up and the flecks of sunlight that shine down through the thick, green canopy of the forest overhead. His chest heaved, doing whatever it had to in order to draw in air. In the distance he hears his the hoof beats dying out. Train manages to turn himself over, laying on his stomach for a moment... In the distance as one set of hoof beats dies out another grows louder... Train pushes himself as close to the ground as he can possibly manage, allowing the posse on his trail to come....and finally go, the sound of their hard ride dying out as well. Train let his head hit the ground again, now confident enough to stop holding his breath and trying to catch it. No telling how long he had been lying there... However long it had been he could only think of one plan to recover his title from the unworthy King... If this was Sherwood then there was only one person he could get to help him. But how long would it take to find him? Train used every ounce of strength he had left to climb to his feet. He rose, wondering if he was poor enough to get someone to help him rob the rich Lord, King... Hopefully it wouldn't take to long to find someone to help. Train turned to get his bearings... to find out where exactly he'd landed. The light that shined down to the forrest floor glimmered off the point of the arrow... It was no more than 2 or 3 inches from his face...that made it very easy to stare down the shaft into the eyes of the person who was drawing the bow. Damn... That didn't take long.... ===== ===== ===== ===== ===== To Be Continued......
