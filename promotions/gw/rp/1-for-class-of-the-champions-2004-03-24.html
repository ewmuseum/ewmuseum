(# 1 for Class of the Champions)

24 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 24 I ran straight into that man that was standing on the side of the street. I was so excited I didn’t even see the man in front of me when I hit him. I knocked him right off his feet, and he hit the ground at a hard pace. I saw his body crumple up when he hit the ground. I reached out my hand to him and he grabbed it. I brought him back onto his feet and dusted his jacket off from the dust that had gathered on it. “Sorry about that sir.” I said to him. “Its okay, just next time watch where you are going.” He said back to me. He started walking away from me as did I. I started for my house, excited to tell my mom about my wrestling career. She never really did like wrestling at all, but when I joined it she had to like it or else she would think I wouldn’t like her anymore. But now if seemed like she started to enjoy it, other than the injury’s some people suffer, like I did. A few years ago when I was first wrestling, I broke my collar bone and went out of action for a few months until the doctors said it was okay for me to wrestle again. I turned the street and came face to face with my house. This house I had been living in since a few months ago. I ran up the creaky old stairs and opened the door. “Hey mom. I’ve got to tell you something really important.” I yelled into the house. “I’m in the living room honey.” she yelled back to me. I ran past the kitchen and straight into the living room. I couldn’t wait to tell my mom the biggest news I have ever told her before. I stopped and sat on the couch just to catch my breath from all that running I did to get to my house. “Guess what?” I said to my mother who was sitting on our chair watching her shows. “What is it?” she asked me. “I’ve just been put into the Global Rumble at the next Global Wrestling PPV. And the winner gets a shot at the Global Wrestling World Title. That title means everything to me, even though I have never won it. That’s the title I’m going after and I will not stop until I get it.” I told her. “That’s great honey. I’m so proud of you.” she said back to me. “And the owner of Global Wrestling told me what number I was going to be in the Rumble.” I said. “What number is it?” she asked me. “Number 11. So that gives me a even chance of winning. I’m going to do my best on winning that. I’m going to do everything possible in that match to win it. Even if I have to sacrifice my body in every shape possible, I’m going to win that match.” I said. “Well. I really do want you to win that and go on to your dream match. But please, Don’t hurt yourself that bad because then you might not be able to compete in your dream match.” She said to me. “Don’t worry mom. I wont come home with any serious injuries.” I told her. She laughed and smiled at me. “Well what are you going to do Max?” she asked me. “I think I might just walk around and go practice at the gym for my big match.” I told her. I walked from the living room to my bedroom that was upstairs. I walked into my room and it was the exact same as when I left it. My bed was neatly made, none of my clothes were on my floor, and my computer was still shut off. I walked to the corner of the room and grabbed my gym bag off the ground. Inside it was my wrestling outfit with some tape so I could tape up my wrists. I put the gym bag over my shoulder and walked out of the room and out of the house. I figured it was nice out so I started walking instead of taking my car. It was a red convertible. **15 Minutes Later** I walked into the front doors of the local gym. I forget what it was called. I walked right past a lady and into a room. Inside the room there was about 5 people using all the machines. I walked over to a brown bench and changed into my wrestling gear. I taped up my wrists and pulled out my black gloves. I slipped them on and strapped them into the velcro. I walked over to the treadmill but from behind me a man ran up to me with something in his hand. “Is that you Max?” he said to me. “Who are you?” I asked him. “I’m not sure if you remember me, but I used to own a wrestling centre that you came to a lot and even won the World Title but then had to leave.” he said to me. “Larry?” I asked in surprise. “Yeah. I didn’t really know if you would recognize me but yes, its Larry.” he said back to me. “Larry. How’s it been hanging with you” How come your not at your centre anymore?” I asked him. “Well. It kind of closed down. Too much taxes.” he said back to me. “Oh.” I said. I walked away from Larry and to the treadmill. I got on it and put it to the highest it could goto and started running on it as the scene faded.
