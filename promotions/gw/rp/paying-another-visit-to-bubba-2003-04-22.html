Paying another visit to Bubba

22 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/22/03 In through the front entrance of the hospital comes Brick. He’s still a bit banged up from the 4-on-3 assault he, Ely Braxton, and Logan Creed suffered at the hands of the Nevermore. It would have been an even fight, except for the shocking betrayal by Jesse Briggs. It resulted in Logan getting chokeslammed into an open steel chair and then flung into the audience by Quartz. Meanwhile, Brick and Braxton received double chairshots for their troubles. Today, however, he’s here to visit his employer Bubba McCoy. Bubba is still in the coma that he’s been in for about a month now. In his absence, Creed is serving as The doctors had finally deduced that Bubba had been poisoned. And Brick had a pretty good suspicion as to who was behind it. Many other shared his suspicions. Brick walks up to the front desk of the hospital. “May I help you?” asks the receptionist. “I’m here to see Bubba McCoy.” “I’m sorry. Only family members are allowed at this time.” “Damn.” Brick starts to walk out of the hospital when he hears a female voice call him. “Brick?” Brick turns to see Christina McCoy coming towards him. With her is Ely Braxton. Like Brick, Braxton is still a bit banged up from the events of the recent Evolution. Brick feels a bit uneasy around Christina. A few years ago, when Brick was feuding with her then-boyfriend Dane Rennier, he and the rest of Ruckus Inc grabbed her and handcuffed her to a shower head, naked. They then left a trail of clothes for Rennier and his partner Maverick Reed to find. This was a bit of psychological warfare on Brick’s part. Truth be told, if one were to make a list of Brick’s proudest moments, that one would be nowhere on the list. “Hey, Brick!” calls Braxton. “Hey.” “You here to see Dad?” “Yeah, they told me only family members could see him now.” “Well, we just got through seeing him.” “How is he?” “Still no change. For a while, he started to pull out, but then fell back under. The doctors are doing the best they can, but without an antidote, he won’t heal.” “Are the police coming up with anything?” “Nothing. No fingerprints, no eyewitnesses.” “But they’ve still got their eyes on Salaok, right?” “Yeah. But right now, they don’t have anything concrete.” “How are you holding up?” “Can’t believe Jesse walked out on us like that, especially after all that Bubba’s done for him.” “I know Bubba’s kept me from beating Briggs” ass on a number of occasions.” “Yeah, well, we’ll deal with Jesse later. You need to get ready for Wrestlecade.” “I’ve been ready since November 2001. I’ve been ready since last summer. I’ve been ready since last month. I’m ready now.” “I know you’ve been looking forward to it for a while,” says Braxton. “I wish it was me in that cell with Salaok. I’d be more than happy to rip his ass apart for what he did to Bubba. I’m glad one of us is.” Brick turns to Christina. “Listen, Christina. What I had done to you at Insurrection, I’m sorry. As much as I talk about Salaok, I’m not that much different from him.” “Look, Brick,” says Christina. “I’ll be honest. That was one of the worst nights in my life. But you and Dane both did what you thought you had to do to win. You were left for dead, so I guess you felt justified. You, at least, have some kind of remorse. Salaok is a coldhearted bastard. Just promise me that you’ll rip that son of a bitch apart at Wrestlecade.” “It’s a promise,” Brick replies, with a smile on his face.
