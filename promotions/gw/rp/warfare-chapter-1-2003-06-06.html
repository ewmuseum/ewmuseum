Warfare (Chapter 1)

6 June 2003

Warfare (Chapter 1) Jesse Caine Global Wrestling Promo #398 Date: 06-06-03 Location: Chicago, IL Warfare (Chapter 1) “Ready?” stated a voice to the left. The area was dark, not much could be seen. An old abandoned warehouse was in view as what seemed to be a few men had been peeking into. “Yeah I’m ready,” answered the other man. He just stood there still surrounded in the dark. “On my count, we’ll go in, Mike you take the left, Smith you take the right and I’ll go straight for the kill. Got it?” he questioned them wondering if this would go down right. “All right. On 3 then?” answered Smith with a gulp as though this were his first. “Yes…” replied the still unknown man. “Let’s do it,” followed Mike now with part of his face uncovered. Mike a black Caucasian n with a black goat on his chin and short black cornrows, and brown eyes runs across the opening to the left as Smith crosses him and goes right. Smith a white Caucasian male with no hair and a full beard runs across the cement and dives onto the front hood of a car shooting, as does Mike. Then finally the final mysterious man comes out. Long shaggy hair, light green piercing eyes, and a long beard that goes down past his chin and nearly touches his chest. He runs up the middle pulling to 6 round pistols out of his vest and shooting like crazy. The group within struggle to get out but none does, and after all the smoke has cleared only two men are left, stuck in the middle. Mike, Smith, and the unknown man just stand there looking at the two. The circle around forming a triangle then Mike speaks to the unknown man. “Jesse,” starts Mike as he address the long shaggy haired man, “should I just go in, or do you want to? Since these two are your friends…” As he says that Jesse smirks and starts walking in, drawing his guns away and staring at the two. He closes in slowly as he eyes the two. The first one is a white Asian male and the other an Asian female. Both are unarmed and have they’re hands up, but Jesse knows that these two Asians are very tricky. Over 4 years he has been chasing them and finally this is his chance to bust them. “Tisk tisk tisk… Do you fools never learn? I come in here being a civilized person, and yet you try and run… I thought you two were smarter than that. Now look around you. All your friends are dead, meaning now I have paper work to do. Ya’ll know that that means don’t yaw? It means I have to take you Asian fools in. Damn yanks ruining this great warehouse,” mutters Jesse in a southern accent. “You dumb American,” starts the Asian man, “You think I care about your pitiful grunt work, we’re not those who ponder on others misfortunes, we have our own to worry about. Now if you are going to arrest me, do so, for I am sick at looking at your foolish face.” Jesse looks around, he knows the Asian man doesn’t care for Jesse or his friends Mike and Smith, for he’d rather just have them dead than be arrested, so why is he asking to be arrested. Jesse pulls out his gun but instead of holding the handle he grabs the barrel and turns to Mike as though he has something to say. Then whack! Blood shoots from the side of the Asian’s face as Jesse reared back and spun around slamming the handle into the left side of his jaw. The Asian man falls to the cement holding his jaw and smirks as though it was an old gesture the two had done before. “Is that all you’ve got you Caine? Eh? Do you really think I am that weak… The Black Dragons never will fall! We are a group, and we are trained to kill, deal, and kill. You’re pathetic. Absolutely pathetic I say. Ha ha ha!” Jesse Caine just stares at him then kicks him square in the face with his cowboy steel toed boots busting his nose and spreading the blood around now on the ground. He kneels down and pulls out a pair of hand cuffs then puts one knee on the back of the Asian man and cuffs his hands together though does not stop there, for he pulls out another pair and cuffs his feet. “That should take care of you…” smirks Jesse and stands up. He turns around but is sadly mistaken for the bitch he runs into kicks him square in the jaw and knocks him flat on his ass. The Asian woman grabs Jesse’s pistol and then puts Jesse in a half nelson with on of her hands, the other with the gun pointed to the back of his head. She slowly turns as Mike and Smith now look to be in a hole. Both have they’re guns up and Jesse is having a hard time seeing. “Boss what should we do? Shoot or, drop our guns?” asks Mike whom is the rookie here, and needs to know. “…Gulp… drop the weapons,” states Jesse as he swallows his breath, the gun barrel crammed into the back of his head. He doesn’t care though for it’s happened before. “Now give me thee keys and I’ll let you go easy,” says the Asian woman.” “You know I remember when we were in this position before Shasi. It was a lot better though, cause you enjoyed it. What happened to those days eh?” chuckles Caine with a smile as he reaches into his pocket and grabs two sets of keys. He throws them over to where the Asian man lies cuffed and then is released. Shasi walks over to the Asian male still with the pistol pointed at Caine and steps over him. She grabs the keys and starts to un-cuff the hands, then lets the Asian man un-cuff his feet himself. “Come on let’s get out of here Mukasi,” states Shasi as she then shifts her gun and shoots Mike in the hand for he was reaching towards his weapon. Caine dives and pulls out his other revolver shooting, and just missing Shasi nailing a car window and shattering it. The two Asians jump into the Ferrari and with Mukasi driving Shasi shoots and they zoom out of the warehouse as bullets speed behind. “God damn it!” yells Jesse looking at Mike and Smith, “4 god damn years chasing those two, the same two Asians who had got be arrested before. Now I have to find a lead and get them again… This just blows. Do neither of you understand the words put down your guns? I had a fucking pistol pointed at the back of my cranium. Your lucky I didn’t shoot you instead of her. Mike, never ever reach for your gun in a situation like that. It makes everything go wrong.” Mike looks at his hand and though bleeding clenches a fist and wants to hit Jesse however doesn’t, for he knows Jesse will hit him back. The three walk to the car slowly and hop in, not just a regular police car, a 4-door dodge pickup with leather seating and a horsepower engine. It’s new and one of the best brands out there. The truck drives away into the night with all 3 men heading to the emergency room. Next Day (11:00 AM) Jesse comes strolling into the front door dressed the same as the night before. He though now looking tired and as if got no sleep whatsoever. He walks to his desk and sees Mike there along with Smith who both dressed properly and ready to go. Mike though has a bandage around his right hand and swallows some painkillers before guzzling down some water to clear his throat of the taste. “We’ve got a new lead,” says Smith as Jesse sits down, “A man named Carl Walsh. He owns a Chinese restaurant down town, and if we’re lucky he’ll be leaving when we get there.” “Does anyone say good morning anymore? Gee… I walk in and you two immediately want to do work. Take a break sit down. We need to plan this out. If he knows were onto him he won’t appear today, or tomorrow, probably not for another 3 days. This will be our time to find any way to plan out a quick and easy route into the building hitting him when he does come in. Got me? So we wait till Wednesday and hit him hard. By the way, how’s your hand Michael?” boasts Caine in a tired mood. “Oh not too bad… So we wait till Wednesday and then hit him? That’s a bit long really,” replies Mike holding his hand. “Long enough for you to heal that wound. So lets sit back, dig some info up on this guy, so that we can bust him and bring Carl Walsh in come Wednesday. Got it boys?” checking around to see if there paying attention states Jesse. Both shake they’re heads yes and go back to work as Jesse sits back in his chair and stairs out the window. A hot sunny day in Chicago city, however not too hot, just good enough to go out and stroll around.
