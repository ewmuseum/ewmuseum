One night...one team...one goal

26 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/26/03 (Everyone goes through life waiting for their opportunity to shine. Some sit back, hope and wait for it to come to them, while others go out and earn their shot. A couple months back Sean Price was given a shot, a once in a life time offer was laid out in front of him, he did not earn it, but the opportunity was there nonetheless. That opportunity was in the form of a Global Wrestling contract which Sean signed and to which Sean can attribute his fame too.) “Who would of thought, my entire fame and fortune can be attributed to me signing my name to a piece of paper.” Exclaims Sean (Since the singing of that contract Sean has wrestled himself into the spotlight, earning multiple title shots, and winning all of them but one. That one loose is the only blemish to mark Sean’s amazing wrestling career. However, since that loose Sean has gone 3 for 3 in title matches, winning the tag team titles, than the platinum title and finally the international title. This coming Sunday, Sean will get a chance to capture the tag team titles for a second time. This time Sean will be going into the match with the best man he has ever been teamed up with, that man is stable mate and friend Tyler Pierce. However despite tagging with one of the best wrestlers in the fed and the current cruiserweight champion does not make this tag team title match any easier. It is still going to be one of the toughest matches Sean has ever participated in. You see in this match its not just a matter of pinning one guy and that’s it. This is a 3 team battle, TUE vs Dynamite X and Angus the Bull, two highly talented up and comers as well as the team of Salomone Vation another rookie and ofcourse Tragic Hero, someone who is definitely used to holding the tag team titles. As a member of The Welch Brothers, TH held the titles on numerous occasions. But things have changed, The Welch Brothers are no longer a tag team and True Evil are no longer the tag team to beat, that honor is bestowed upon the tag team representing The Upper Echelon. As the scene fades in we see Sean Price working hard at the gym training for one of the biggest matches of his career. He is currently over at the weight bench lifting weights with his partner Tyler Pierce behind him being the spotter.) “9??hhhhh??10?”?hhhhhh? Price breaths in heavy as he along with the assistance of Pierce rest the weight bar in its proper resting place. “Wow Sean, I must say I am rather impressed with the amount of weight you are lifting, for a guy your size you life a lot more than anyone would expect.” “Its all those years of football Tyler. When you play football all day, everyday for your entire life you are forced to lift a lot of weights. Especially when you are gonna be out on the field tackling 350 pound fat asses who do nothing but eat fatty foods and play football.” “Whatever makes them happy.” Remarks Sean “I guess, all I know is that a week from today we will be the new Global Wrestling Tag Team Champions, on top of the business.” Reluctantly replies Tyler “That’s right, after we win the tag titles we can add them to the list of titles won by the TUE.” “You know Sean, those tag team titles should still be yours, if I remember correctly you never lost the titles.” “That’s true, I didn’t lose the titles, my sad excuse for a partner Jay Robson lost the titles in a match that I was not part of. And do you know why I was not part of the match?” “No idea Sean.” “Because Bubba knew that those titles would never leave my waist as long as I was in a match to defend them so he did the only thing he could short of stripping me of the titles and that was putting Robson in a match against Tragic Hero.” “How can Robson lose a match against TH” Hero is an old washed up has been.” Laughing Tyler “Well if TH is a washed up has been than Robson is just plain out horrible. I don’t know how he lost to Hero, and honestly I don’t really care. Those titles should be mine but there is no sense in dwelling over the past. Not when we have a bright future ahead of us.” “That’s right, because this Sunday marks the beginning of the end for the tag team division. After we win the titles Salaok might as well just lock the titles away in a safe because there is no one that is going to beat us for them. Not True Evil, not Cold Blood’no one!!” “That’s right. When it is all said and done, when people look in the history books for the best tag team in GW history they will see that True Evil won the titles 8 times. But if you think about it, with each time they won the titles it means they had to lose them first. But we are different, we won’t win the tag team titles 8 times. We will win them once and hold them for eternity. That is the mark of a great team.” “That’s right, we will be the best thing GW has ever seen, the most dominate team to ever grace the halls of professional wrestling. But you know Sean, every great tag team needs a name. A banner to wrestle under, something people can hear and think, oh yeah that was Tyler Pierce and Sean Price. They were great.” “This is true Tyler, however we will be in every record book for everything possible, our names will be remembered simply because of the greatness we show in the ring. We don’t need a team name to be remembered by. Cold Blood is a team name, and look at them, three years from now no one will remember that they even existed. But us”.we are different, our names will be spoken longer after you and I have both retired. It’s a promise. (The scene fades out as Sean and Tyler continue working out for their big match on Sunday.)
