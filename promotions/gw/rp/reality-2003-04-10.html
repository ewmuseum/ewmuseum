Reality

10 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/10/03 You Make The Acid From My Vomit Rise To My Throat He is seen? Your Worthlessness Makes The Hairs On My Arm Stand Up After such a glorious victory for The Nevermore. They have defeated Jesse Briggs, Ely Braxton, and Brick in the Main Event. After capturing the Cruiserweight title. And after demolishing the Heavyweight Champion, Silent Wind? The Sight Of You Makes Me Cringe In Anger But him, Roz Blayze, he did not succeed? The Sound Of Your Voice Urges Me To Clinch My Fists Even Tighter He lost against Derek Frost. He ruined his chance in becoming the GW International Champion. He lost to somebody who doesn’t deserve to be the GW International Champion in the first place? You Make The Sickening Thud Of Heartache Push Itself Down My Throat For you are the most pathetic human being I have ever seen? And It Is Hard To Swallow I pinned Jesse Briggs? Reality Is Also Hard To Swallow Pat me on the back because I am better than you ever will be? And At Evolution At Evolution, you will witness my power and strength first hand? I Will Help You Swallow That Reality Because I Am Better Than You REALITY You have been warned. The drips of water falling from the ceiling are heard throughout the basement. Pieces of the broken stone wall surround the floor. A cold mist is carried by the wind and blows. It is dark, except the light down the hallway. Slowly making our way towards the light, mice are heard scrambling at our feet. You get hit by a drip or two of cold water, which sends a chill down your spine. The closer and closer you get towards the light, an evil laugh becomes louder and louder. We stand feet apart from the doorway. The laugh is louder than ever now, it echoes off the walls around us. The light suddenly turns off and the only thing heard is silence. No laughter and no light. The only thing that surrounds us is complete darkness. You turn towards your left’nothing in sight. The only things heard now are the squeaks of the mice around the floor and the drips of the water dropping from above our heads. You turn towards your right’nothing can be seen through the darkness. And again, nothing is heard. All of a sudden, a whispered voice echoes off the walls? “You house yourself in false success’but even a sparrow builds a better nest.” The evil laughter starts to pick up once again, echoing off the walls along with the nefarious message. The laughter stops and the voice speaks once again. You look towards your left to find the voice? “You look toward the left’but nothing in sight. Nothing in sight once again to your right.” The evil laughter picks up once again. It suddenly stops and the voice speaks? “You can’t find me, because I lie in the fears of the ones who have experienced. The ones that have experienced the pain that I can give you. But I question that pain. Is it true” Or is it a plea for mercy? Whatever it is, it puts a smile upon my face. Oh, for watching one scream in agony’for nothing can compare. That my friends, that is true happiness.” A light turns on above and he is seen sitting in a steel folded chair against the stone wall to the right. He wears torn black jeans, a Nevermore shirt, and black combat boots. He has not shaved and his 5 o’clock shadow sticks out just as far as his pure white goatee. He lifts his head, looking at you with his ice blue eyes. He smirks’and the only thing you can think of is, what is going through his head? He gives the term, sadistic, a whole new meaning. For in his eyes, they contain the future of many things. Including the future of the GW. He looks directly at you? “Welcome. Welcome to a place where chimes are ringing. Welcome to a place where souls are singing. Welcome to a place of possessed sickness and a place that I reign. Welcome to my world, Roz Blayze. Welcome to the Global Wrestling.” He smiles and a small chuckle comes from deep in his throat. “Blayze, you weep over such pathetic things. You shed tears due to what people say about you. People mention the fact that you aren’t as good as anybody here in the Global Wrestling. They whisper statements that you are pathetic. And they simply tell you that you cannot last in GW. Well, Blayze, I’m sorry to mention this to you but’you need to step up to the plate and face reality. You have to realize a couple things before you do anything. You have to realize that you are what everybody says you are. And you have to realize that every week when you walk out at Evolution, that you are putting your health and life at risk. And I stress that you realize that fact Blayze, because on April the 17th, you will not be stepping in the ring with Derek Frost. You will not be stepping in the ring against Brick. You will be stepping face to face, one on one, against The Soul Survivor of GW and the GW Cruiserweight Championship title. A title that many wish they have. A title that many wish they can ever touch, but my friend, nobody can touch this title’why” Because it belongs to me. And to touch it, you have to pin my shoulders to the mat for the 1’2’3. And let me tell you rookie, that task is harder to accomplish than you think. Jay couldn’t do it, neither could Silent Wind, or Jesse Briggs. I hope you are starting to realize that you aren’t even at my level. But rookie, I will not show any mercy, for Salaok has taught me to take every match seriously, even though if I am facing a joke like you. And when I leave the ring’I leave with no regrets. Did you happen to see what happened to Silent Wind when he defied my power? I left him lying in the ring with a crimson mask of blood covering his face. And Blayze, I left with no regrets. I hope you seen what I did to the Heavyweight Champion of the whole world’because I am going to make the beating I gave Silent Wind look like child’s play. Blayze, this isn’t some dream’this is your worst nightmare. And at Evolution on April 17th, your worst nightmare will become reality.” Blayze for some odd reason, you think that defeating me is going to be a difficult accomplishment not because of my raw talent, but because I have “great champions” backing me up. Well Blayze, I don’t need The Nevermore to defeat you. I don’t need even one Harvester to stand in my corner to make you shed a tear of sorrow. Do you know who I am? I am Sage Sabbot. I am the GW Cruiserweight Champion for a reason. And at Evolution, I will show you why I am the Cruiserweight Champion without the help of The Nevermore. And when I’m done, I will make you swallow your words and help you swallow reality. The reality of realizing that I am better than you will ever be here in Global Wrestling or anywhere else in that matter. Blayze, I have a feeling that you are not concentrating on what I can do to people. I may only be 6’3 and 215 pounds’but I am a wolf in sheep’s clothing.” He runs his hands through his tightly pulled back white hair and cracks his neck to the left, then to the right. “Blayze, you say you want a title shot against me” Well here is your chance’earn it. Show me why you deserve a title shot and I’ll take it into deep consideration. But don’t get your hopes up, because you won’t have one bit of offence going up against me. Blayze, you said that we have no history, that we have never faced each other before. Well, that is correct. “but when Evolution is over’the only thing that will be history is your damn career. Now write that into the books, because you will never come back.” He stands to his feet and brushes his ripped jeans off and walks away from the camera. He mumbles off? “You respect Dynasty because he kicked your ass” Well, we’ll see how much you respect me after I demolish your whole entire career.” His laughter is heard once again as the?
