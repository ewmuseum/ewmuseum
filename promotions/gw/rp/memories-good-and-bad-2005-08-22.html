Memories, good and bad.

22 August 2005

Date: 8/22/05 ~Global Hotel, Arena and Casino~ ~Business Office Wing~ ~Photography Studio~ Dani stands, wearing a robe to cover her in the cold room. The air conditioner is cranked to the highest settings, and there are fans all over to keep the equipment cool, and that means the people get to have a shock from the still high nineties and low hundred degree temperatures outside. “Okay, Ms. Northsky” you're here for the shoot for the inside cover of the program for Whiplash in a few weeks, right?” An older guy, with a couple of cameras hanging around his neck says as he's looking down at a clipboard loaded with papers on it.” Dani acknowledges him, “Yes, at least as far as I've been told there. I was told to be here, and be in my wrestling gear.” “Hmm, you're the one who's done modeling work for magazines and that too, right?” He adds. “Yes, FHM so far. A couple of posters too, and a few others are showing interest, but I usually let my agent, Kris take care of that. This one is for GLOBAL though.” “Right-o, well let's go ahead and get you started.” He says as he motions to the area set aside. An area with a tarp tossed behind it baring the Global Whiplash Logo, with the flash reflectors, guards and spotlights already setup. Dani walks into the area and with a deep inhale, drops the robe and tosses it onto a chair off set. She's standing there in her to the ring gear, the breastplate and overskirt in place. “Nice, nice. Okay, you've done this before. Which means I don't need to explain to much. I was told to shoot many roles of each girl. Mr. McCoy likes to have extras around so ensure that if they need a promo shot of some kind, they won't need to look for you. Basically, let's just say he's been burned before needing a photo and the talent in question was in a cast from ankle to hip at the time,” The man explains as he sets up the camera on the tripod in front of it all. “No problems,” She says as she takes a pose, one showing a bit of attitude, “Then again, he might just want a bunch of pictures around 'cause he's a perv, right?” The photographer chuckles as he starts to look at the focus on the camera, “Not my business to know if he is or not.” The large flash bulb, more of an entire wall of them goes off as he presses the shutter button. Dani starts to listen for him to direct her, just like she did with the squad of photographers a few months ago when she did the large shoot for the magazine. As she listens to just him, and a few others start to talk about shop and direct her, she lets her thoughts wonder a bit, to last week, and her meeting with Lady Leanan, Annie as she's informed Dani to call her. Daydreaming... Her mind drifts off to the caf? that was turned into a Gothic night club, as she sat across the table from the woman who just put her in awe. Dani starts, “You, know I would like to know how you knew I would end up here” Annie just takes a sip from the bottle of Alcoholic Cider she had brought over, and looked across the table her her. She looked not unlike a cat watching it's potential prey, ready to pounce on it, “All in good time me dear, all in good time. Ye know ye managed to piss Gray off rather well. First time in a while tha' 'e's lost 'is temper like tha'. The damage 'e caused in the gym could 'ave been yer body. You play a dangerous game little girl.” Danielle looked like a chastised child, looking down at her bottle of cider in her hand. She usually didn't drink the heavier stuff, but tonight it tasted good. Although she was getting tired of everyone calling her a 'little girl', she is twenty-one after all, “Well maybe he should of just let me go, it would of never gone that far.” Danielle knew how lucky she was that Kaylee showed up when she did. She knew the damage that Gray could of caused her. She knew his strength was great, when he was mad. He could have broken every bone in her face without even trying. She had never seen someone so small have such complete control over him when he was like that. But just in case she didn't know it already Annie confirmed it for her. “Ye know, you were lucky, I decided I wanted t' look in on things personally. 'E would o' killed ya if Kaylee 'adn't arrived in time,” she said with her strong accent rolling off her tongue. Dani just nodded her head and commented, “I'll be surprised if Gray Speaks to me again, after that one. I don't think anyone has ever slapped him before.” Annie reached across the table and pulled Dani's chin up, “Gray Muzzle sees ye as th' sister 'e never 'ad. 'E'll jus' need t' cool off a bit. There is also 'is brother t' worry abou'. Why don't you start and tell me about these dreams that have Josh, and Gray, so worried about you.” Danielle stared at Annie, letting the woman feel she had a bit more control after touching her. She was sure Gray had already told her what they were about by now, if not him then Josh had. “They are not much, just dreams, that are almost like memories. But... not my memories... someone else's someone that lived long ago.” Annie appeared to have been paying a lot of attention, and only added in,” I'm listening.” Danielle told Annie all about her dreams even to the point of pulling out her journal, from the bag she had with her, and allowing Annie to read it personally. Every aspect of her dreams written down, right after she awoke, pages and pages of them. “I tough there were only two dreams tha' ye'd had?” Dani replied, “Two major ones that have caused problems one way or another. Such as the physical pain. The minor dreams have been while I was asleep, so I didn't think much of them to tell Gray. I just wrote them down just in case so I wouldn't forget them. Figured they were just caused by the others, memories of something that hurt me.” Annie thumbed through the diary as she read the dreams, more then three weeks worth almost every night. Adding in some of her own commentary, “Ye didn'na think o' tellin' Gray abou' these” Why na? 'E migh' 'ave been able t' keep Josh's back safe,” she said with a slight chuckle appearing to be trying to lighten the mood a bit. “Do you think my dream have something to do with the marks on Josh's back” I didn't mean to hurt him,” Danielle looks down at her hands, her finely sculpted finger nails: short, clean, natural and pretty. But, those where not the things that had done the marks on Josh's back and she knew it. SNAP BACK! Dani came back out of her day dream as the photographer shouted orders at her. “Hey, hey. Can we get some without the skirt and chest piece on. Just what you wear in the ring?” “Uhm... uhm... ? Dani tried to come to her senses quickly and take into consideration what he said, “Oh, yeah, sure. Sorry.” She untied the breastplate from around her neck and let it fall into her hands. Reaching behind her, she pulled the tie on the part at her back. The beads fell into her other hand and she folded them in a nice way before setting them down into the same chair her robe had ended up in. The skirt was a bit easier to take off, as it's not much more then a tied wrap around the hips. “Alright, let's get a couple of rolls like this,” The photographer said as she walked back over to the studio setup. A couple of other people stood around now, controlling the lights and the fans to ensure her hair didn't get whipped around too much. “Okay, show some attitude, you're a wrestler right?” She took a pose with her arms going under her breasts, and a stern look on her face. “That's it!” The flashes started again, and Dani went into autopilot again, drifting back to Back to the daydreams... Danielle and Annie have a few empty bottles in front of them, as they're talking. Annie seems to be handling it quite well, while Dani is talking more then she usually would from the looks of it. She suddenly blurts out, “Annie, do you know what is wrong with me?” Annie looked at Danielle with care and concern she knew the answer but also knew that Danielle wouldn't like what she had for one, “I would'na say there is somethin' wrong with ye. We knew tha' somethin' would be comin' up with ye. We've followed yer family blood line fer some time. Sayin' there is somethin' wrong would imply yer damaged in some way. This is wha' yer intended t' be.” Danielle didn't like that answer one bit, and she leaned onto her elbows, which were up onto the table, “You know what I mean. What am I” I've seen things before that aren't right. I've known about Gray for a while. Sometimes I think I see shadows where there aren't any. What am I?” Annie sighed and then looked at her with a face of resolution. She knew she'd have to give some sort of answer. Annie's always been one to just be straight up and in front of people, even when she was a wrestling valet. She answered her with a stern and to the point voice, “A' this point no, there isn'na anythin' wrong with ye. It is all a natural course tha' things are 'appenin'. We are just na' sure what course yer takin'. We though' one thing but somethings are na' matching up.” Dani interrupted her, “What do you mean you don't know?” Annie continued, “Let me finish, please... We are still working on the whole subject, as hard as it is to grasp: we still need time to work it ou'. Time we 'ave plenty of, we jus' need lots o' patience t'. Yer dreams are just yer mind awaking to the mystical side o' yer 'eritage.” “What?” Dani asked, leaning in more, but looking annoyed. “Let me explain it in a way ye migh' get. In the movie, “Thunder'eart, with Val Kilmer... where th' FBI agent starts t' see th' run an' th' ghost dance. Ye've been exposed t' many sides o' the veil: Gray, meself, Kaylee. And many more a' school, it is natural fer your mind t' try an' focus, t' adjust on one subject,” Annie looked to be trying to end it. “But you're not telling me what I am, I mean I could have hurt Josh with... whatever it was that I did to him!” Dani exclaimed, getting more perturbed. Annie's response didn't help much, “I don'na think 'e minded t' much, things like that 'appen.” Dani gave her a look that could kill on that, while Annie gave her a coy smile. It was almost like she knew exactly what went down that night. But only if she knew how Danielle was still feeling about the man. His smell intoxicating, his touch made her body shiver with a fever she couldn't fight, his voice made her.... well just made her mind go onto another subject. Then she couldn't help but remember how much like animal claw marks the scratches looked like. And, on top of that they were not healing right and Danielle knew that was something on her part but what and why she just couldn't figure out. Annie decided a bit more was needed to explain where they were actually at, “Damnit girl, t' be honest, fully honest, we pegged the wrong generation from the looks o' it. We were lookin' a' ye, 6 generations from Fallen Star. A 'ero t' yer people. Yer 6 generations from 'im, it should'na be ye doin' it.” “Then why am I?” Was all Dani could muster to answer that. “Because, we messed up. Yer 6 generations from the 'ero... Fallen Star. Bu' yer bloody well 7 generations from 'is father, the Wakan Takan known as Mornin' Star. We looked t' much int' the books an' legends an' missed the one true thing there, the starter o' the line was the one tha' came from the sky, an' took a Sioux wife as 'is mate. We were na' ready fer ye t' start this. We're still recoverin'. Usually when this 'appens we 'ave some time, bu' yer acceleratin'. So again, t' be honest, we're flyin' by the seat o' our knickers.” Dani gave a defeated look at that one and took a big drink from her bottle, one hand holding her head up. It wasn't the answer she had been hoping for. SNAPBACK! Dani's back in the studio, the photographer has stopped taking shots of her in her ring gear, turning her, bending her in ways that aren't natural to shove the tits or the ass out for the camera. Anything to enhance the picture to sell more she thought. She was still out to lunch on everyone's attitude when ti came to the human body. Video Games and Movies... show the brains being blown out, but show some skin and people enjoying themselves and the decedents of the puritans and pilgrims scream that it's all 'foul' and that it needs to be hidden. But, when the others turn their backs, they buy them up like hotcakes and then hide them under their beds or on their computer, where others won't know that they have those sinful vices. Dani collected her stuff and then looked around the room, “Where do I sign the paper work for it?” “Oh, nothing to sign. This ones on Global, remember. It's all taken care of,” one of the assistants said. She walked out of the room, and then headed to the dressing rooms. Quickly she changes out of her wrestling gear and into some normal clothing. Sliding on her new pair of Oakley Thump Sunglasses, with the built in mp3 player. Heading out to the main office area, she on down to the casino. She meanders on through, as she slips back into her self examination of the night she had the heart to heart with Annie. Daydreaming... Annie takes a drink, it's a little bit later in the night, they've been talking about things of such, more to get to know each other, then anything else. Dani's thoughts thought had drifted to Josh, and that night when Annie had hinted that she knew all to well what had happened between them. Suddenly Annie breaks out, “Yeah, I know 'ow ye feel, been there meself once.” Danielle looked at Annie with surprise she hadn't said anything out loud had she? She asks, “Excuse me” What are you talking about?” Annie just continues, not missing a beat, “Joshua, I know 'ow you feel abou' 'im. Been there at least once in me life.” Danielle's face turned to one of slight shock, she didn't hear herself say anything out loud about the man. Unless, she was reading Danielle's mind, there was no way for her to know what Danielle was thinking. She shook her head and brought herself back to sanity, “What about Josh” There is nothing there.” Annie's eyes narrowed in on her, “Bullocks!” Dani knew she was going to loose this one, so she conceded, “I can't be with him, not now, business first. I can't put my career in jeopardy for a fling. I'm sure you could understand that.” “At least ye 'ave a chance t' be with the one ye love. I 'ave no chance in tha'. Ye should take th' opportunity as it presents itself,” Annie's eyes were still narrow, and her features sharp. It was almost as if she was scolding her. “Excuse me?” Dani couldn't come up with any other retort. Annie let out a loud sigh and looked down at the bottle in her hand. Her eyes then looked off into the distance, now soft, like she was pulling a memory from out of the sky. She then spoke very soft, to match, “Me dear, Prince Bres, I will never be able to be with 'im.” Danielle actually found herself feeling sorry for the woman in front of her. She knew that if she had to be with out Josh, for any reason but her own, she would be torn apart. She loved Josh, there was no denying that. She loved him heart and soul, but it was something that would have to be put on the side burner for a while. But... Gray was right going back to being just friends wouldn't work. Danielle put her hand out, and touched Annie's, “Why?” “Bres, 'ad been betrothed t' me when we were but wee ones. We were to wed Bu' due t' business, bu, me Formorian Prince.. 'is heart fell fer another. One na' o' our kind. Now they are husband an' wife, an' I'm still jus' a researcher. I took t' long t' come back t' 'im.” Danielle looked like she was trying to draw from a old memory, on who Annie was speaking about. She had heard the name in school, but couldn't put a name to a face. “So, ye see” I lost 'im due t' me givin' up, and therefore, can na' be with him. Bu', ye? Yer reasons are selfish ones. Jus' like I was back then. I'd 'ate t' see someone like ye make the same mistake tha' I did. Maybe ye need t' get over yerself. and enjoy th' time ye 'ave with 'im. I speak from experience, 'E won'na wait ferever. 'E knows ye love him, t'.” Those words pulled Dani back a bit as she fired right back, “Get over myself, you have no clue do you” Your lucky, being not human. You don't have to worry about losing what makes you a woman. I heard of the Keeper's first wife. She lost that in the ring. I don't think I could ever face that. Plus I don't want a child, not yet. I love my career, it is my life. I can't have something getting in the way and ruining it.” Annie raises her hand and slaps Danielle across the face. Danielle's cheek turned bright red almost instantly. She went to stand up but Annie grabbed her arm and held it forceably in place, her eyes turning jet black. Dani watched as Annie's skin went paler, and what appeared to be a collection of piercings in her ears and at least one on her lip appeared, and then slid back into hiding, on the other side of the veil that kept her looking normal to everyone else. Annie was almost furious now, “Let me tell ye something, lit'le girl! Jus' because my genetic code isn'na exactly like yours, doesn'na mean I don' 'ave th' same needs an' wants tha' ye do. EVERY species 'as th' need t' continue, every woman ou' there: Gibborim, Faery, Seelie, UnSeelie, Vampire , Garou or anythin' else like tha', seeks companionship. Our kind, an' yer's, can feel something tha' animals can'na, an' that's love. Why would ye deny tha' which raises ye above tha' which ends up as food on your plate?” Dani had no answer to that. Annie didn't really give her time to come up with one, “Yer one confused lit'le girl. What ye want an' 'ave isn't always wha' ye need. Let me be one o' th' first t' tell ye, since I'm on a first name basis with the Keeper an' 'is new wife. 'E never blamed 'is first wife, 'e loved 'er. It wasn't until 'er death, did 'e find ou' she 'ad used magic t' steal him from 'is soul mate. But there 'ave been many kept from theirs. Most o' them na' by their own doings. Annie took a breath and then tried to force her accent away to get the point home, “YOU, me little friend, use a purely selfish reason to keep yerself from yours.” Danielle's face was hot from the slap, she sat in shock, she couldn't believe what just happen. She couldn't believe that the woman sitting across from her just slapped her. Danielle's already busy mind was still trying to figure out what to do with the subject of Joshua Brand. It wasn't like she didn't love him. She did, just didn't know what to do about it. He was to her... everything. she had loved him since she laid eyes on him so many years ago. But then again, she was young and just chocked it up to puppy love of the bad boy in class. But now as a lady her feelings hadn't changed. And from what she could tell after basically living with him over the last 7 months, she was more so in love now then she had ever been Back to reality...
