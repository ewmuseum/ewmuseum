You Learn Well, Son

3 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/03/04 Silence. I sit looking outside my tinted windows of my Texas branch offices in silence. My journal opened on my desk awaiting my thoughts, awaiting for me to tell the tale of my days in this century so far. Awaiting my account of how things were. I tap my pen on the table as I look at the blank pages then out the windows to the storm that is raging outside my building. I look to my desk and to the left of my journal is a wrestling magazine with myself on the cover. My face half cover by my hair as always and then I see the caption,LEINAD SOMA, the tamed animal. I look at the magazine cover in disgust as I push it through a shredder. I sit there in silence listening to the rain as I begin to write. How is there silence when listening to a storm? Silence can be in anything. A hurricane has a silence, people can be made silent, the wind has silence. As I said before, all things have silence. I just saw a magazine cover that had myself on the cover, it called me a tamed animal..never in my life have I ever been tamed. To be tamed you must abide by the rules of a superior at all times. I have no superior and I do not abide by anyones rules. They called me an animal and thats what I truly am. Actually I am more of a monster than anything else. I am everything people fear and yet I have been beaten by motals. Men like Maverick Reno, Ken Patridge Junior, Josh, these are men who have beaten me. I, the eternal monster have been bested by mere humans. This hurts one's pride. I have fought far stronger creatures than them and have walked away without a scratch but these men I have not beaten or I must always show that I am and should be the most dominant force, a creature of fear and loathing. Global Wrestling has become a haven for me, I consider it my territory no other vampire, makes his or her way through alive. I am slowly becoming more of a myth than a man....people not fearing me but thinking of me as a joke. It is a shame that they don't realize where I am heading and where I shall be. My destiny will soon come to light and they shall all fear me once again. At the moment I am the calm before the storm. The quiet before the war. The uncalm silence before death..... My door opens as my son walks in. He looks into my office taking note of everything in the room. He sees the same kind of setup as the offices in New York. The room is rather large, the floors and walls are mand of black marble, the ceiling is painted as if in a Italian cathedral, depicting the fall of Lucifer. My desk centered in such a way it is the center of the room but was connected to the wall in a moon shaped fashion. The wall behind my desk is a large tinted window that I can watch the sun go down from my office. He is carrying a worried look on his face. He sits down in front of my desk. We sit there in an uncomfortable silence for about ten minutes before he begins to speak. His words calm and cold but triggered something in me that could not be reversed. 'Father, Ice made an appearance on Master of the Mat after you left.' 'Is that so....' 'Yes father. I know you knew him a while ago and you two were called monsters, you had respect for him and vice versa. I was wondering if you were going to be up to your old ways.' I look at him and I smile a little. I see in his eyes he doesn't want me to change. He doesn't want to see that side of his father...its symbolic of many tales where the son hates the father of his wrong doings or the father hates the son for not being like himself. The smile fades away as I open my mouth to speak and yet could not form words. I finally strain out an answer or more of a question. 'Son, if I do change, will you deny your father and your promise to me? Will you turn your back on me as you did so many centuries ago?' He lowers his head taking his time before he speaks, for he knows something that needs to be said must take time and preperation. After a few minutes he looks up at me, I saw a few tears being wiped away as he looks up at me. 'I will follow you into the depths of hell father. I am your son so I must abide his father.' 'That is good Max. As for your question am I going to become a monster? I'm not sure, but you have seen the travisties I have commited in the past weeks and you have said nothing till now. If I am to become what I use to be what makes you know you won't end up like where my destiny is taking me.?' 'If it does then it was part of my destiny as well father.' 'You are learning fast my son. That is good.' 'What of my sister?' 'What of her? What is she doing?' 'I couldn't tell you. She must do things of her own accord... as did you.' I spin in my leather chair to look out my tinted window to see the sun setting behind me. I stare at the one thing I never get a chance to see, a thing that has been out of reach since my creation. I look back to my son and smile. 'Son, sometimes even destiny can't help you in matters....' He doesn't say anything as he stands, I hear him walk out the room and close the door. I whirl around again to pick up my journal and back to watch the darkend sunset. .....You know sometimes Destiny can't even get you things. Every day I awake as if I were ever human and come to work sometimes, always in tinted vehicles and offices so not to be killed by the one thing seems to have always been the one thing I have never been able to control.....the sun....The sun has always been the one thing I couldn't change, bend its will to aid me, or anything like that.....its the one thing that actually has tamed me....but even in times of feeling beaten are nye, something arises, a loophole if you will, my loophole is Ice, he has returned as if it was destined. One human that equaled me in my monstrocities and gave me my rightful respect. Shall I become a monster of his caliber once again? It's tempting, always very tempting to become that which I know i'm destined to become. Even tamed animals go berserk now and then. I think my time is up.....It's time to go berserk. Keep the children in at night for the nightmare which was and is Leinad Soma has broken free yet again. I stand and drop my journal on my desk as the sun sets and my office begins to darken in silence.
