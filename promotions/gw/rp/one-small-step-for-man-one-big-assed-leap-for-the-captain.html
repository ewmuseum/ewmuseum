One Small Step for Man, One Big Assed Leap for the 'Captain'.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today It'd been all of a few hours since the last time anything had happened to me and now, something else was going to make itself known to my otherwise quiet life. Arriving back home had originally been something of a bad move... after I realized that I didn't actually have any of my own gear there. Yeah, I know how stupid it was... but when you're finally able to go home for the first time in however many weeks, you seem to forget the whole idea of belongings and look more to the fact that opportunity to sleep in your OWN bed as opposed to one of a motel is there. Regardless, that aside, after a good few hours of a cab journey, I found myself arriving on the grounds of the property that belonged to my darling of a younger sister. She was a cute little thing that'd always had plenty of boys chasing after her during high school and things like that. As always, a lot of the time I was there to scare of the guy or whatever... but eventually, just like my father had to step back and allow the fruits of relationship to blossom, I was forced to watch from the sidelines as my kid sister grew into a 'teen' and began to date a whole host of male scum. [I say scum, not because they were scum... I didn't even know them, but they just looked like morons.] Anyway, I'd arrived at her home, which looked a lot bigger than it did the last time I was there. Perhaps she'd had some extension work done to it? I didn't know and I wouldn't know until I'd got into the house and sat down for all of a few seconds. I stood outside the front gate, admiring the rose bushes either side for a few seconds before inhaling deeply and pressing forward, walking into what was effectively, the domain of my sibling. '... She seemed cool with you on the phone, Champ. She seemed pretty Ok with everything too... so let's just hope that wasn't superficial. Remember the days of old and don't get into a position where she can kick you...' I said, reminding myself to be aware at all times and remember the pain that I suffered as I gatecrashed her first date. Walking up the stone path, I arrived at a giant white door. The door panel had a beautiful stained glass window that deplicted a wonderful summer morning. I admired it silently after pressing the doorbell a couple of times to show my host that I'd finally arrived. 'She always did have good taste...' I said, again out aloud for almost no reason whatsoever. Sometimes, I guess I feel like I'm a little insane too but at the end of the day, I just like to have a little commentary about certain things in my life... even if this is stuff that comes from myself. After waiting for a few minutes, I heard footsteps pound down a few steps before stopping on the other side of the door. I hesitated for a few moments, wondering whether I should bother ringing the bell again or just knocking until my knuckles bled. A few moments of silence followed before the door swung wide open, a blinding light [well, no, not blinding... but you LOVE the suspense. I mean, without it... I'd only be 99% of the man that I am today] filled my view. I didn't know what to think and I didn't know what to do. Truthfully, if I didn't know that it was my sister, then I probably wouldn't have known what to do. She greeted me with the widest smile that I could have ever imagined and thus, chose to reply with a smile equally as large. Of course, I failed miserably... but, in my defense, I hadn't had many things shoved into my mouth over the course of time. BUURN, I know... but she knows what she does and loves it too. 'Oh my God, like Hey, Rich! It's been so long since I've seen you in the flesh... and by God, you're looking like you did when you first started on in that professional stuff.' she commented, lunging forward to grab me in a huge bearhug. Heh, she looked the smallest girl in the world but damn, she had the strength of a few hundred terminators or something. It was freakin' scary! 'Hey Jo, it's been a long time since I saw your pretty face, isn't it? To think, you're looking even better than you did when I saw you last.' I chimed in, returning the hug with one that was just as gripping... at least, by my own physical standards. It was times like these when I became something of an emotional wreck, holding onto my family like it were everything I could ever have rolled into one package. We let go of one another after a few moments and looked into each others eyes, tears beginning to well up somewhat [more-so in mine though... hey, I told you, these times are emotional for me]. 'Let me bring your bags inside, Ok?' Jo suggested, reaching down onto the floor to pick up my sports bag. She took a few paces back before welcoming me inside, an offer that I happily accepted. Stepping into the house, I closed the door behind me and stared around in amazement. Previously, I'd always thought that Jo would have gone for something that was a little more futuristic and a little more 'modern', but to my absolute surprise, she'd chosen to go for something that seemed, a little retro... in more ways than one. I could picture the house as being a property that belonged to some kind of hunter... heh, not my little sister. 'It's amazing, isn't it?' she asked, looking on as I gazed around at everything that was in the entrance hall alone. Pictures of old school happenings, a few stuffed animals and then a few more paintings. '... You have no idea how amazed I am at the collection that you have amassed. Personally, I never took you to be somebody who was interested in all this stuff. When we were living with Mom and Dad, I always thought that this was something that you'd never take away from the house with you, ya know?' I responded, being careful with every word that I spoke. Meh, I really didn't want to imply anything horrible and be on bad terms with the lady only a few minutes after walking into her home. 'Well, a lot of the family didn't expect this to be something that I brought along with me either. In fact, the last tiem Mom came over to see things, she said this would have been one of the things that she expected you to take to your place.' Jo commented, looking around at her possessions for a few more moments before beginning to trudge up what sounded to be a staircase.. my bags in tow. 'I don't quite know or understand what it was... but, I just didn't think this kind of atmosphere really suited somebody like me. I mean, you know how little I'm at home anyway, right?' I stated, turning around to follow my sister up the staircase. I glanced ahead to see her nod in agreement with my statement. She knew that I would be very lucky to generally use my own home on a scale of nine days a year... and without a wife or girlfriend to stay behind and make sure everything was OK, she knew that I would never have kept items like these there. It'd just be the most counter-productive of things EVER. As she proceeded to walk the stairs, my sister did something that I would have never expected her to do. She actually started to converse with me about my career in professional wrestling... and more importantly, the match that was going to be coming up at the next GW PPV. 'So Rich, I see that you're already being given an opportunity to prove yourself at GW's next pay-per-view.' she said, sounding somewhat enthusiastic about the whole deal. I thought she was just being polite, just being nice to keep my morale up and keep the peace between the two of us. '... Yeah, I'm getting a lot further in a space of time that was quicker than I could have ever imagined.' I said, before continuing, 'This contest is going to be something really big though... so I wouldn't hold my breath when it comes to winning the actual thing. I mean, you know how it has been with contests like this, don't you? More times than not, it's often a case of one mistake being the difference between walking away as the winner and coming home as just a losing statistic.' I sounded surprising dejected, something that I told myself that I wasn't going to be when it came to discussing this point with anyone other than myself. I thought about trying to be all jumpy but my mind wasn't having any of it, neither was my morality for that matter. I wanted to lie and make everything seem as though it was going to be one huge victory... but, if my sister really was going to watch this event, I didn't want her to hear about an easy victory and then watch me get my ass handed to me by x, y or z. 'You don't sound all that happy about this contest Rich...' she said, her voice trailing off as we reached the second floor of the house. She walked ahead into a room, her voice becoming almost non-existent so I chased after her to catch the next part of what she said, '... I mean, you're usually very happy when you're giving chances to prove yourself to everyone. It's a shame that you're feeling this way going into a contest because we both know how counterproductive it's going to be in the long run.' I tried to look positive about everything but I just couldn't bring myself to be all that positive. I mean, here I was, going into battle with some of the biggest names that GW has.'Jo, I think it's really nice that you're trying to keep me positive about this thing... but despite the way that I look and such, it has been a while since I last really got into the ring and I don't know if I have the strength, stamina or skill to make something of a splash in this contest. Granted, I'm sure that lasting a few bodies would be a great result for me... but, you know how I am about these don't you? If I dont' win, I don't see the point in being happy about anything.' I said, sounding dejected and unhappy with things. Inside, I knew that I should just appreciate the opportunity that I'd been given... but how do you appreciate the most likely outcome of ownage? 'If you don't try to win then you're not going to come close to winning, it's as simple as that. As far as you should be concerned, even the smallest chance is a chance of winning this thing and if that opportunity knocks, you should take it... because you do NOT know when the next one is going to arise. You don't know when you could get a chance to fight for the number one contendership spot again, do you? For all we know, this could be the ONLY chance you get.' Jo stated, her voice firm and impressive. I knew that she was right about this because after all, it's not as though I'd done acre's and acre's of work since arriving at GW. I'd had two matches and both of those were over before the fans could understand what was going on. '... I know what you're saying and I appreciate it, I really do, but... this isn't just one small step for me, this isn't just the next stage, you know? This is like, one HUGE leap from the bottom of the ladder to the top, should the impossible happen and I win.' I began before taking a deep breath and continuing, '... You know, I really don't know what I'd do if I could talk about this stuff with you.' Jo dropped my stuff onto the floor and rushed over to embrace me, giving me a warm hug. I remained motionless, unable to move or return the show of emotion. The more I thought about this great opportunity, the more I seemed to ask myself if I was good enough to last past a couple of the guys... the more I seemed to ask myself, 'Is it possible for me to even have a chance of winning this?' As silence fills the room that I'd been previously in, the scene fades into black. With just a few days left until the ultimate showdown, I must do something about my nerves and my ill feeling. : END :
