Castles Made Of Sand

14 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08.14.05 08.12.05 Mindy Mercer was enjoying yet another good night's rest next to her boyfriend Markus on her large, comfortable bed. There were things that she was able to let go throughout her week away from wrestling, which was a welcome change from having to perform and think about little else. It saddened her that she couldn't defend her title at Master of the Mat, but she couldn't complain about the rest she was given; Mindy would never have admitted it to anyone else, but she needed the time off, and for more reasons than those that were immediately noticeable. Aside from the time that she needed to heal the physical bruises and beatings she had recieved every week in Global, Mindy wished to spend more quality time with her loved ones. Sure, Mindy spent time with her cousin and Markus every other day practically, but that wasn't really enough when in between that she had to worry about getting her pretty face kicked in. Away from the glamour, the glory, the money, the title... all Mindy had to worry about was her and her family, and she liked that. It was great to feel like she was a normal person again, unaffected by things outside of her own life. At the same time, however, Mindy knew that her ability to go without worrying about Global would not last, and those thoughts were only proven right when she learned of her and her cousin's Tag Team Championship match on the fifteenth. Not that she was dead set on winning another title; if anything, Mindy would have been determined to win for the sake of not making her cousin look like a fool. Absolutely Merciless and La Herencia De Herederos had squared off twice before, but under two very different circumstances. Michael Mercer had two different partners for each of those confrontations; the first one being T'sao Liang, and the second being Michael Ryba- neither man was able to help Michael lock down a win. Or maybe it was the other way around- maybe Michael didn't help either of them? 'He's going to be counting on you anyway, girl...' Mindy's inner voice gestured, 'the world won't be right for him with a win, but it would help him in the right direction.' Mindy's eyes blinked open, as she slowly sat herself up in bed. 'I have to win.' Mindy rolled over to glance at the sleeping giant next to her. She didn't wake him, thankfully. 'Brr...' Mindy felt herself shiver as she hugged herself with the blankets that covered her body; the air conditioner above and just behind the bed was on full blast, more than likely Markus' doing. With a lunge over the edge of the bed, Mindy managed to lower the power of the cold air blasting over her and her boyfriend. 'Much better...' Before long, Mindy was right back to trying to sleep, but it was no use. She was up, and there wasn't really much of anything she could do about that. Frowning, Mindy eased out of bed, careful enough so that she hadn't caused Markus to lose any sleep, either. Her feet found her pink bunny slippers that rested just next to the bed, before she ambled out towards the kitchen down the hall. In times like this, Mindy usually had a nice tall glass of chocolate milk to calm her nerves, and this moment was no exception. Mindy poured herself some of her favorite drink and took a small sip of it. 'Yum.. maaan... why do I have to be World Champion?' Sup, irony? Mindy was most definitely glad she was the world champion. Who wouldn't have been? She accomplished something some hardened ring veterans could only dream to do, and all at a tender twenty twenteen(?) years of age. Sure, Mindy was thankful for her title reign, but was she truly ready for it? Not just the responsibility that came with it, but the press, and all the talk? Mindy knew about the numerous words spoken about her; that she wasn't a worthy champion, that she specifically asked for time off from Master of the Mat, that she slept her way to the top... and those accusations couldn't have been farther from the truth. 'I'm just so afraid...' Mindy shivered at the thought of the allegations. There was nothing she could do to rebut those charges against her, except keep winning, and she couldn't possibly do that forever, could she? All streaks come to an end... she found that out herself the hard way when she lost to Jake Hix at Wrestlecade for her Television Title. 'I can't help it if I lose, though,' Mindy thought aloud, sniffling as she took another sip of her chocolate milk. Mindy wished silently that there was an easier way to prove her worth to the company that made her a star... that made her feel like a somebody... ...and those thoughts were interrupted by a soft whimper in the night air. 'Mew?' The soft sound barely whizzed past Mindy's ears, but she heard it none the less, and it was as prominent as the horn of a semi truck approaching at a hundred miles an hour; safe to say, it was enough for Mindy to go seek out the source of the noise. Mindy returned to her bedroom and waggled her tiny frame over to Markus quietly, little slippers pitter-pattering along the carpeted floor as she stood just feet away from her loved one; not a peep erupted from his lips. A confused Mindy placed her free hand on her hip as the other lifted the cup of milk to her mouth to drink. 'Hm...' ---- Running through the forest he ran, he was running through the forest. The drone-like sound of Michael's heavy breathing and heart thumping were the only sounds that penetrated his senses, causing his Converse clad feet to trek rythmically through the tall grass in search of his enemy. The only thing that entered Michael's mind were those two sounds, and the thought of bloody murder, which as Michael would have said, stuck out in his brain like a sore clit. The night sky made it all the tougher to find the man he was searching for, but at the same time, it wasn't as though he hadn't searched for something in the night before, because he had... ...except on those prior occasions, there wasn't so much on the line. Running through the forest he ran, he was running through the forest. Brush brush, thump thump went the sounds, backed up by the rustling of the leaves in the mile-high trees, when suddenly- THUD! Man crashed into man, and the six foot four man named Michael Mercer won out on the battle. The silhouette he had been searching for crashed into the muddy ground with a dull smack, and Michael immediately lay into him with a football kick right in the bread basket. The man screamed obscenities in Italian as he rolled over in the dirt, desperately moving for the one thing he thought could save him: the stream. Michael noticed it, with no more help than the sound of the calm waters that accompanied the loud, heavy breathing of his prey. Michael charged forward, staying on his enemy like he would an opponent in the wrestling ring, but something in the back of his mind... the sound of the small waves in the water provided by the wind had put Michael's soul in another place, one where it wished it could be. 'On the beach, lying in a hammock next to my wife... with a beer in my hand, and my daughter within eyesight as she makes a sand castle that stretches high into the clouds... yeah, that's where I wanna be...' And with the sickening feeling of bone being shattered under the weight of his fist, Michael was back in the dark trenches, fighting to make sure his name was not tainted by a nameless man's cry to the police. He needed to take care of things, if he wanted to make that thought a living reality. The silhouette cried, shuffling back on his hands and feet for the water behind him. Michael felt in complete control, approaching and reaching down to grab a handful of the figure's hair, when... ..his knees gave out on him, buckling from the sharp pain between his legs where the man had abruptly kicked him. Michael's air shot out of his lungs in one big huff, and he could only watch with widened black eyes as his enemy got ankle deep into the water. There was silence. And right when Michael thought that silence would very well be the end of his life- BLAM! The figure screamed as a stream of red liquid spewed from the open bullet hole in his back, before his body created a meek splash in the water. Behind Michael, in the darkness, was T'sao Liang. 'Finish it.' Michael slowly stood up. The man turned to his side, pleading for his life. 'Aiutarme! (Help me!)' he screamed. 'Aiutarme!' Before long, Michael was on top of him. 'Sorry...' Michael whispered in his head. 'I can't help you.' The cries did not die upon the first punch, but Michael did not let up. He let his anger carry him through his cruel deed, throwing harsh, fury-filled rights and lefts into the man's temple as he laid there helplessly, unable to protect himself from the savage beating until his body stiffened, and a brief spasm later, he was dead. Michael looked at his hands as he presented them in front of his eyes, palms up. Blood lined his fingers, seeped into his nails, and fully coated his bare knuckles. What had he just done? 'Bravo, Michelle,' the Chinese accent lingered in the darkness, a sinister edge to it making it more than a simple gesture of congratulations. 'Bravo... Lei ha ucciso il suo primo essere umano ?. (You have killed your first human being.)' With those spoken words, Michael stood back up, and turned around. Beyond the lining of trees, he longed to see Jaden and Gabriella waiting for him. Instead, there were two much older figures that breached the dark. 'That is not my beautiful child.... that is not my beautiful wife....' Michael tried to reason out where his cherished wife and daughter were, but all that he got in return was a soft, soothing voice in his mind. One that sounded remarkably like Jaden. 'We know... but you simply can't follow us everywhere, Michael.' 'Aren't you happy, Michael?' questioned the Chinese man. 'Aren't you just the happiest man on the planet?' 'NO-' ---- Michael woke up, and he did so with the sound of his cousin gasping over him, momentarily frightened by the scream Michael made in his shaky slumber. 'You startled me!' the comforting voice of Mindy Mercer whispered, kneeling down beside the bed as Michael sat up. 'I startled you...' he mumbled in confusion. 'I...' Michael's gaze quickly turned from Mindy to his own hands. The blood still felt present on him. He knew it was only his conscience, but his heart made him feel more than just mental disturbance. He was killing himself physically, as well. 'I startled... I..' Mindy slowly rose to her feet and leaned forward, hugging her cousin tightly. Something was incredibly, incredibly wrong. 'Mikey... Mikey talk to me.. what's wrong?' Michael remained silent for a moment, before he instinctively threw his arms around Mindy. For the first time ever, he cried freely. 'They're... they're all dead.'
