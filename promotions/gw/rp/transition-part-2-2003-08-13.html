Transition, part 2

13 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 13, 2003 And better is exactly what it got -- though not necessarily in the way I'd expected. It wasn't until later that night, as I was leaving my favorite restaurant with Satomi on my arm that the day got truly interesting. 'Mmm,' Satomi purred, leaning her head on my shoulder, 'dinner was wonderful, Rokku-san. I didn't know such a fine Italian restaurant could be found on this side of Tokyo.' 'Well,' I replied, 'I found this place during my work in the city's underground fighting circuit -- a few years before my IWL days. Boy, the stories I could tell you about those days...' She perked up. 'I'd love to hear them, Rokku-san.' I looked down at her and smirked. 'Well, Satomi-chan, if you'd like to come back to my place, I could share my stories with you -- perhaps over champagne?' Satomi giggled, and pressed closer to me. 'That sounds like a wonderful idea, Rokku-chan.' 'Yeah, it does. Shame you're gonna be too busted up to enjoy it, prettyboy.' Brandishing a knife menacingly, a man wearing a black ski mask stepped out of a shadowed alley. I looked at him, and laughed. 'Please. You're not even going to lay a hand on me, pal.' 'Rokku-chan,' said Satomi, her voice filling with fear, 'I don't think it's a good idea to taunt the man with the large knife?' I laughed again, and slowly took off my black silk shirt, folding it so that the golden Lion mon on the back faced up. 'Don't worry, Satomi-chan. And please, hold this for me. I'd hate for this punk's blood to get on it -- these custom shirts are expensive, y'know.' Satomi took the shirt, looking frightened and bewildered. I saw the thug's eyes widen in anger, and knew that I had him. 'We'll see who bleeds!' With that, the thug charged, throwing a series of quick slashes and stabs. Bobbing and weaving, I easily stayed a step ahead of him. As he made a straight jab with the blade, I ducked under his arm, grabbed it at the wrist and elbow, and twisted hard. With a scream, he let the knife clatter to the ground. 'Now,' I said, as the thug flailed, unable to reach me while held him in the tight armbar, 'watch, Satomi-chan. From this position, I have complete control over his body -- where he goes, and what he does. He should probably apologize before I do something really painful, like pop his shoulder out of the socket, or snap his forearm like a brittle twig. Don't you think?' Satomi simply nodded in mute awe. 'To hell with you!' shouted the thug, as he tried to claw at my face. 'Now, see,' I said, and the man screamed as I wrenched his shoulder out of joint, 'that was not the polite apology I wanted. Hopefully, next time you try to accost someone you have absolutely NO chance of beating in a fight, you'll have some manners.' Spinning him around, I slammed my forehead into his nose, and when he dropped to his knees, caught his chin with a kick, leaving him a bloody, agonized wreck on the sidewalk. 'Come on, Satomi-chan,' I said, smiling and offering her my arm again, 'lets go -- that poor guy might decide he wants to go another round, after slamming his shoulder into a wall a until it pops back into the socket.' 'H-Hai..' she said, wrapping her arms around mine, and leaned into me again. I could feel the heat of her flushed skin against my arm, as we walked, could practically hear her heart thumping in her chest. We walked in silence for a few minutes, allowing me to enjoy the feeling of Satomi pressed into my side. 'So,' I said, as we neared my convertible, 'you've never seen a real fight like that so close-up before, have you Satomi-chan.' 'N-no,' she stuttered the sudden break in the silence startling her, 'that was? you were amazing, Rokku-san. You were never even worried that you'd be hurt, and you beat him so quickly and effortlessly?' 'That's why I'm the Steel Lion,' I said, grinning. Satomi laughed, then stood up on her tiptoes and kissed me softly. 'It would appear we've reached your car, Rokku-chan,' she said, tapping her hand on the hood, 'lets get moving, I'd love to hear those stories you talked about.' 'Yes ma'am,' I replied, opening the passenger side door for Satomi, and bowed gracefully. She smiled, and took her seat, buckling in. Whistling happily to myself, I walked around to the driver's side of the car, opened the door -- and froze in place as I looked over Satomi's head. 'On second thought, Satomi-chan, ' I said, pressing the button that raised the car's top, 'I don't think we can leave just yet. I have more business to take care of.' 'What, do you mean you --' Satomi stopped mid-sentence as she looked in the same direction I was. There, on the far side of the mostly-empty lot, was a man with a bloody ski mask and a large knife, nursing a wounded shoulder -- and four more men who looked a lot like him, with chains, pipes, and other weapons. I fished my keys out of my pocket, and tossed them to Satomi. 'Stay down, Satomi-chan, and if things turn bad, peel out of here -- I don't want you getting hurt.' Satomi nodded, and locked the doors after I closed mine. The thugs slowly walked towards me, and I circled around the car -- not only to get closer to them, but to put myself between them and it. Surveying them, I let a predatory smile creep across my features. I really shouldn't have worried Satomi like that, but I couldn't help myself -- because this was going to be SO much fun. 'Get that son of a bitch!' screamed the man with the injured shoulder, and five of them rushed me. Roaring like the animal that is my namesake, I leapt into the fray. Ducking as one of the thugs swung a pipe at me, I fell back onto my hands and sent him sprawling with a leg sweep. Never letting myself stop moving, I sprung back to my feet, just as another thug swung his chain at me, I caught the chain in mid-swing, letting it wrap around my arm, and yanked hard, pulling the thug towards me -- and straight into a stiff knee to his stomach. Before he had time to react, I slammed my elbow into the back of his head, and he was out. As he fell, the bloody thug with the knife lunged at me. Twirling my newly acquired chain, I caught the blade between the links, and sent the knife flying from his grip. He paused in astonishment, and that was all the delay I needed. He dropped like a sack of coal after the chain connected with his face. The three remaining thugs spread out to flank me, and I swung the chain in wide arcs, trying to keep them at bay. The first thug brave enough to move on me was one of the two armed with pipes -- and I rewarded his valor with a kick to the knee. As he screamed, I wrapped my chain around his arm and swung him into the path of his comrade's pipe-swing -- and as he crumpled to the ground, I stepped on his back and sprung off, catching the other pipe-wielding thug with a shining wizard. I was back on my feet before the unconscious thug hit the ground, and stared down my final attacker. The thug scowled, and wielded a broken bottle at me menacingly -- then threw the bottle down on the ground and ran like hell. I sighed, and lashed out with the chain, wrapping it his ankle. With a help, he fell flat on his face, and I was on him in an instant. 'Alrighty pal,' I said, lifting him up by his jacket, 'you punks have disrupted my pleasant evening with a lovely young lady -- so now, you're going to tell me why, or I'm going to show you everything I ever learned about inflicting pain during my street fighting days. Now, you're obviously not muggers -- after I kicked the crap out of your friend the first time, real muggers would have just moved on and looked for easier prey. So talk, before I decide I don't WANT you to any more!' 'Genbu!' shouted the terrified thug, 'Yoritomo Genbu! He, he hired us, said he wanted you taught a lesson!' 'There, you go,' I said, setting the man down gently, 'was that so hard?' He let out a sigh of relief, and I slammed my fist into his face. With a whimper, he fell down. Dusting off my hands, I walked back to my Vehement and knocked on the door. Satomi popped her head up from the back seat, and a wide smile spread across her lovely features. She quickly opened the locks, and I swung the door open, then pressed the button to retract the top. 'Rokku-chan,' she said, her expression turning worried, 'are you hurt?' I grinned, then reached into the car and lifted Satomi by the waist. She squealed as I swung her through the air, then sat down in the driver's seat and placed her on my lap. 'I'm just fine, Satomi-chan. Great, in fact. Now, if you'll start the car, we can get out of here. I think there were some stories you wanted to hear.' Satomi slid the key into the ignition, and the engine roared to life. 'Who wants to hear stories,' she said, looking at me with eyes full of heat and desire, 'when you've just lived one?' 'Well,' I said, smiling at her, 'I'm sure we'll come up with something to do.' 'Yes,' she replied, licking her lips, 'something.' Genbu could wait for tomorrow, or later. As I peeled out of that parking lot, leaving five hired goons behind me, he was the farthest thing from my mind.
