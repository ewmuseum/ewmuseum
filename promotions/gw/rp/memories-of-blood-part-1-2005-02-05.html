Memories of Blood Part 1

5 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/05/05 She threw it all away My angel died that day No one came No one cared - Cold And I remember falling victim again and I feel the pain wont let me forget And I cannot let this get in my head And I... - Dry Kill Logic * * * 1st, March, 2005 Las Vegas, NV Gotham Manor Corvus woke up quickly, he shot up and looked around but everything in his bedroom was in place. He was waking up from another one of the bad daymares he had been having consistently over the past few weeks. They had alternated back and forth, some about his daughter and others about how he became to be one of the most vicious killers of all time. The sheets were flung off him quickly as he got up and walked into the living room. This was possible because he had all the glass replaced in his home after he purchased it, making him be able to walk around only in his home during daylight hours if he chose to. With remote in hand, he turned on his television just so there would be a little noise in the house. He picked up his phone and dialed a number that was longer than a local call; when the voice on the other end picked up there was only one thing he needed to say before he hung up. “Get the plane ready ...” That having been said, he walked back to his room and through the bathroom to the walk-in closet. He grabbed a bag and then pulled a few things off of the hangers they were on and put them into the bag. It was only going to be a few days trip because he knew he needed to be back. In less than three days he had to make it to Detroit for High Octane and the Basement Match that awaited him and his former “dirty deeds” employer, Salaok Davis. Corvus had everything ready that he needed, he walked back into his room and crawled back into bed and set an alarm for when the sun went down so he could be up and ready to go after taking a quick shower along with a change of his clothes. The hours passed quickly; then the music from his alarm came on which informed him it was time to get moving. After having such a rough day it was tough for him to get properly motivated. He moved to the shower and got cleaned up and then proceeded to change. When he was finished, he brought his bags into the front room and hit the speaker button on his phone and dialed up Deacon Black to have him do a favor for him while he was away. He answered the phone shortly after the second ring. “Deacon Black ...” He paused for a moment knowing it was coming from Corvus? phone. “Hello my friend, what can I do for you?” His voice was friendly as always even if the man that was calling him didn’t always act right towards him. “Hey ... I have a request for you to think about. It’s not much and I would appreciate your help on this.” Deacon paused once more before continue and giving a response, “What do you need?” It was usually the only time that Corvus ever called him was for a favor. “I need you to come get my bike and bring it back here. I’m leaving town for three or four days; I should be back by Monday at the latest. I have to take care of some business before I go to Detroit for my match.” Deacon listened as Corvus explained what his plans were and what he needed him for. It didn’t seem like too much of an imposition to pick up the bike for him. The question that burned in his mind was where he was going and for what reason. As an advisor to the big bad, he usually knew what was going on before Corvus did it ... except this time. “Does this have anything to do with what happened a few weeks ago” I know what time of year it is and being someone who knows you ... it’s pretty easy to figure out. With you having a tough opponent this weekend, I would not advise you to do this.” Corvus makes a face of mockery towards the comments Deacon has made, since he can’t see him doing it that makes it even more humorous for him. He checks to make sure everything is right with his bag before he finishes his conversation with Deacon. “I’ve already got the arrangements made ... I need to go home; that is what I’m going to do for a day or so before moving on. I’ll tell you all about how boring it was when I get back. As for Saloak Davis ... he’s a good competitor and I won’t take him for granted, trust me I have it all figured out. Thanks for the help, Deke, I’ll see you when I get back. Ciao! He felt there was no need to detain himself any longer than he had to. He picked up his bag and started to walk towards the coat rack by the nook when his phone began to ring again. A few expletives came out of his mouth as he thought it might be Deacon calling him back to hassle him for hastily hanging up on him. Corvus makes his way over to the phone and hits the intercom button again. “What is it this time?” A silken laugh comes through the phone, slightly distorted as most voices are over cell phones, whether preternaturally enhanced or not. 'What kind of greeting is that? I don't have to waste my long distance minutes calling you, you know.' A grin played upon his face as he listened to the voice of the caller, which belonged to his wife, Lia. More often than not now he’d been calling her by her real name instead of her assumed name. For some reason he was no longer comfortable with that. “What do I owe the pleasure of this call” I haven’t spoken with you since our last rendezvous so I figured that you had written me off, but I haven’t received any documents stating you turned in the papers I signed.” There was a slight pause. 'You're right. I suppose that I got to thinking about officiality anyway... it's quite silly of us to pretend like we're bound by what papers we do or don't sign. I'll keep them around in case we decide to shove a stake up each other's asses, but I suppose we're having one of our rare periods right now, where we can get along.' She chuckled slightly. 'I called to see how things were going. I've got the rare week off from WMW trying as hard as they can to steal my credibility, so I thought it was a good time to call.. and let you know that I've been keeping up with you. You're quite poised to take your world by storm, aren't you?' There was almost an assumed gentleness in her voice, which was odd for a woman who was usually so detached. “So you have been watching, I figured you would be ... I’ve been trying to keep up with everything you have been up to as well but I know that we both realize that my fondness for your current employer is less than zero. Most people I’ve come across have been saying they were sorry that I lost my title to Biddle but honestly you can’t make an omelet without breaking any eggs as the mortals say. In losing that title, I was free to move up within the system and by taking on some of the top of the crop in one match and coming out victorious ... I say I came out with the better end of the deal. Besides ... I was a little too confident with Biddle and he pulled the wool over my eyes, congrats to him.” The chuckle that followed his statement was indeed genuine. He could have been upset that he lost to Biddle: someone he easily has beaten up before and could at any given time ... it was just that he saw other opportunities along the horizon and used that to his advantage later on. As she spoke again, a smile could almost be heard in her voice, though what sort of disturbing picture that would pain was left up to the imaginations of those in hearing. 'I'm certainly going to be amused when you claw your way to the top, and WMW realizes what a mistake they've made. Of course, it would be awfully nice to be able to make them realize TWO mistakes... but that's another matter... I called for another reason too.' She paused, not entirely sure how to bring it up, as she knew how difficult this matter was for him- both of them, really, though she supposed not following any sort of path resembling humanity had helped her get over the past. “That’s one of the greatest things I’ve heard in a while ... but it seems for once my vengeance against WMW is getting put on hold. Until I hear from anyone about whether or not my request for a match with Fury has been granted ... I’m going to concern myself with only one thing; that is blowing up the Global Wrestling ranks. But your tone of voice and the way you said that you called for another reason leads me to believe you have more serious matters to discuss which are not ... ring related.” She paused again. 'I do.' Her voice lost the playful tone. 'Listen... your ring life isn't the only thing I've been keeping up with. I'm concerned about this... this end you've been pursuing outside it.' He had no idea what she meant, but he could have figured it out sooner or later if he hadn’t decided to make her stop beating around the bush. He wasn’t a mind reader ... well he was but usually the people can only be read if they are in a shorter distance than he and Lia had between them. “Are you talking about the time I saved that mortal girl from being mugged and raped” I would have thought you would be proud of me ... I brought her home and ate her like vampires are supposed to ... the only downside to it was that she was hopped up on some drugs and I didn’t have a very good time afterwards.” She chewed on her lip for a moment. He was already defensive, so how would he take it if she pressed the issue? 'That's not exactly what I mean... I'm talking about this thing with Ange.' This wasn't going to go over well. She braced for impact. “Ok ... who snitched on me” Was it Deacon or was it my brother because they are the only two who knew about me going to that store and buying that book ... which was absolutely useless and a waste of money. Bringing pets back from the dead wasn’t something I was interested in.” His voice wasn’t as irritated as one would think. The matter was addressed calmly but he knew that sooner rather than later she would really get on him about this because he was doing something that was not in the limits of what they were, which of course he knew but there was always that willingness to try for the specific reason of his daughter. She sighed. 'Where I got my information doesn't matter, okay?' She paused. 'What are you trying to do, anyway? You know even if you did find a book that was worth it, it wouldn't work out like you want it to.' His thoughts quickly moved to, “How dare she say something like this?” How exactly did she know if what he was trying to accomplish would work or not? It wasn’t exactly like she had tried to do anything of the sort herself. “I was trying to figure out a way to be able to cross the threshold of the higher power when my time is no longer on this earth. We may be damned creatures but there has to be a way to figure out how to reverse it. Most of us aren’t even given a choice whether we choose to be monsters or not ... it just happens. Our little girl has spent an eternity already without us, why take it fully away from her when we no longer walk as corpses” We abandoned her ... she didn’t abandon us.” 'It wasn't our fault or choice that we lived on either, now was it? We're Damned... nobody has ever figured out a way to circumvent it, and you're going to drive yourself crazy looking for the chance.' She sighed. Corvus stood there in his living room as he paced during the conversation. At this exact moment he stopped and began to laugh as if he had already cracked. It was not a secret that he’d been placed in a mental institution before. That was an experience he’d never forget even if he had wanted to. “Drive myself crazy ... that’s a good one. Are you sending the men in white coats to come and get me now, Lia” When should I be expecting them? Hmmm ... perhaps they’ll be too late; I was actually just walking out the door when you called.” She growled, the Fiend in her taking over without her even really thinking about it. 'I didn't mean it like that, and you damn well know it. I didn't have anything to do with that. I can see that my point is lost on you.' Corvus looked at his watch and realized that if he wanted to make good time he would have to end the conversation as soon as possible. He didn’t feel like getting scolded for what he was attempting, even if that was not her intention ... it was what he felt was happening. “Lia, I have a long weekend a head of me and I should really be going. I’ll call you when I return from Detroit; you can scold me more on this issue then. I know we don’t see eye to eye on this but I think it is something at least worth trying ... we owe her at least that much. I need to go ... I ... well you know how I feel, I don’t need to say it all the time.” She shook her head, a slightly sad look coming into her eyes. 'I know... Take care of yourself.' He ended the call just like that and didn’t care if it ringed again or not, he was not going to answer it. His bag was ready and so was he and nothing would keep him from leaving any longer. He grabbed it on his way out of the garage and secured it on his bike, which was a 1946 jet black Indian Chief motorcycle. It was specially made just for him; there was no other like it in the world. It took him about twenty minutes or so to get to the air field where his private plane was waiting for him. He made sure his bike was secure before he checked in and mad his way out to his plane. He brought his bag in with him and tossed it onto one of the empty seats. There was a chilled bottle of bloodwyne waiting for him which he was grateful for because he had grown thirsty very quickly. Since it was only him, he popped it open and drank straight from it ; and when he was finished with his first drink nearly half the bottle was gone. The pilot came over the speakers and notified him that they would be taking off momentarily for his destination. Corvus looked out the window that he was seated close to and leaned back on the rather nice luxurious seat and closed his eyes thinking maybe now that he was closer to going home that he would be able to rest a little easier. * * * Lyon, France 2nd, March, 2005 Undisclosed Cemetery The flight didn’t feel like it took that long but the only thing that detained Corvus was the sunlight that came through the window. He laid himself down onto the floor of the plane, covering himself with a blanket. He was informed by the pilot that he didn’t have much longer until the sun set once again. There was really only one thing Corvus could do for the remaining few hours of daylight; that was to sleep. He closed his eyes and felt himself becoming weightless as he went into the first stages of sleep. In his sleep, he had drifted back in time to when he first became associated with a pack of vampires that were fierce, calculative, killers. They had been watching him for quite a long time before they decided to make their move and give him a choice ... well it wasn’t as much of a choice as it was a forceful brainwashing move. It was sometime between the 1800’s and 1850’s that he was in the Middle East. He had decided to begin traveling to other parts of the world; this was one of his stops before he would find a home in the America’s. The sect that had been watching him were a bit peeved that he had been dwindling their supply of fresh meals but realized that he could be the ultimate and most unlikely ally in their quests, after all he was technically royalty and one of the sons of a very powerful King. Corvus had no idea that when he was surrounded by these vicious warriors that they would want him to be a part of their group. What little he knew about them told him that they were not very kind to Europeans, which he strongly represented. They trapped him in an alley and brought him to a place where they would bring some of their victims to torture. They certainly did a number on him until he decided to cooperate. They depraved him of the blood he needed to keep his strength and abilities up, only giving him what he needed to live. If he wouldn’t have agreed to their wants, he would have ended up dying from lack of food. The smell of the basement seemed real to him as he opened his eyes, realizing he was on the plane and that it was dark outside. He pulled off the blanket he had over him and rubbed his wrists, which felt sore as if he was really shackled up like in the memory he had of being in the Middle East. Corvus moved from the plane when all the cobwebs were gone. He knew that he had a rough time ahead of him ... after all it was the anniversary of his daughters death, the twelve thousandth one to be exact. Every year at this time, he would make the journey home so that he could honor her by bringing her flowers. The car pulled up to the cemetery, which of course there was no one there other than the grounds keepers which had known he was coming, he usually gets in touch with them before he pays a visit so they don’t call the police for him trespassing. He walked a ways and finally found the crypt in which he had bought for his family, except the only member in it was his daughter. Corvus used his strength to pull the stone open so he could gain entrance. This was much different from the original burial plot he had for her in Gaul. He thought it would be fitting to buy a crypt for her and had her moved from Gaul back to his home of Lyon. In the pitch black of the crypt, he used his lighter to light some pyres that he put in there for when he came to visit. He approached the tomb which had an engraved angel on it and a cross was mounted just below it. He walked around the entire shrine to make sure it had been kept up as he had asked for over the years and paid money for. Corvus laid the flowers on top of the tomb; followed by placing his hand atop it and ran along it until he reached the cross. The cross had burned his skin, the sound of sizzling and the accompaniment of smoke took place until he removed his now severely burned hand. He looked at it for a moment but the pain didn’t even seem to faze him. It was a rather harmless punishment he thought for trying to be close to his deceased child. Moving away from the crypt, he sat in a corner and began to speak. “I’ve come to visit you every year for a millennium on the anniversary of your leaving us despite the fact that where you are now shuns the creature I have become. It pains me to realize that even if you’ve been trying to reach out to me after all this time that it hasn’t changed the fact that your mother and I abandoned you for a life of eternal damnation, as did your younger brother. It was our choice to continue on, not wanting to give up the things we had going for us ... and a part of me wants to say that I am sorry, but I know that I cannot nor would it matter much. I came here to say that it was time for me to let go of you, to finally stop haunting myself with your memory. Whether or not it was truly you in the dreams and visions I have had, I will never know. What I do know is that there are the boundaries that the world of the dead as placed between us. My place has been chosen, I am a monster and nothing can change that. You always have and always will be an angel and maybe if there is a way for the two worlds to cross someday, you’ll guide me to do what is right. I’ll make sure that this monument continues to stay properly kept, but I know I can no longer come here. I must be going now; I need to prepare for a battle with an old friend ... goodbye sweet angel.” As Corvus walked back to the car that he had been driven in, he couldn’t at all think about what he was leaving behind that had been such a crutch for him over the years. What he needed to do was be the strong person he knew was there; that even his own wife knew was there. For as much as he wanted to believe that the humanity that still resided in him, he knew that the beast that shared the space in his being was far greater to stave off. It was one of the reasons why he had chosen to do business for Salaok Davis, murdering those that Sal wanted dead for a nominal fee. It was because he wanted to; because he enjoyed it so much. As Corvus reached the car and put his hand on the handle, he looked back at the mausoleum. He whispered something to himself before getting inside. “Even people who are beautiful on the outside tend to have the most ugliness within them.”
