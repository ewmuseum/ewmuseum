Beverly Hills Bordello

22 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 22, 2005 Upon making his Global Wrestling debut Mike Biddle had ran into a little problem. Not only was he having trouble with the ladies outside of his work environment but now his co-workers were causing his “condition” to worsen. The biggest culprit was Mindy Mercer, the young beauty that attempted to use her feminine wiles to seduce Mike in an effort to throw him off before the “Newcomer’s Bash” at Reload. Mike had managed to spot the evil plot as it unfolded and went along with it until the final moments of the match. However, Mike was attracted to Mindy and wished to do inappropriate things to her behind a closed door. There was also Ami Mizuno, who had appeased Mike with her pleasant jiggling as she celebrated her half of the win but had quickly turned him off once he learned what sort of company she kept. Mike was also confused as to why he had to face Mizuno for the chance to take the Television Title from Ken Partridge Junior, after all, she was obviously not anywhere near the talent of Mike and she had no business even being allowed in any title division. A friend had informed Mike that there was a temporary solution to his problem that would help him keep his mind off his needs and on the important business at hand: The Global Wrestling Television title. As it turned out there was a bordello not to far from Mike’s Beverly Hills home that contained working women that were of a higher quality than the ones in Las Vegas. The bordello in Beverly Hills was also much more discreet due to the social class of the clientele and Mike would be able to keep his visits a secret from those pesky Internet wrestling sites. Mike decided it was best to get it over and done with before his match against Mizuno on Evolution. He made his way to the bordello, which was conveniently located down the street from his home. He had to cut through a few neighbors lawns and enter the bordello through the back way just to make sure that he hadn't been followed. Once he stepped inside the back room he removed his dark sunglasses and looked around for the check in desk. Before he could complete his search an attractive redhead clad in a pink negligee entered the room. “Hello Mr. Biddle,” she said in a soft voice. “My name is Nicole; I’m the proprietor of this business.” Mike didn’t reply to Nicole, he simply smirked. “You’re a bit early,” Nicole continued, “but that’s fine, there is a girl available in the room down the hall and to the left. You can pay afterwards.” Nicole smiled at Mike and then left the room. Mike sighed and went down the hall to find the room he had been instructed to go to. Deep down he felt dirty, he never thought that he’d actually ever have to pay for sex and the fact that he was about to was almost sickening. Once he came to the door of the room he simply waved off the thoughts for this was something he needed, besides it would only be this one time anyway. Mike opened the door and found a beautiful brunette lying on a comfortable looking bed. No words were needed, they simply smiled at each other and Mike closed the door behind him. He sat down on the bed and leaned over to kiss the attractive woman he was paying to be with. Just as Mike began to get lost in the moment he heard the loud noise of warning shots being fired at the front of the house and women screaming as a loud voice boomed “THIS IS A RAID!” The brunette quickly got up and ran out the door without a single word to Mike. An angry look came over Mike’s face as he realized that he was about to be arrested for something he didn’t even get to do. “Damn it!” Mike said to himself. “I need to get out of here...” Mike knew that there was no way his career would survive if he were arrested at the bordello so he frantically searched about the room for something to cover his face with. He finally found what he was looking for in the form of a black leather bondage mask that was hidden underneath the bed. He quickly put the mask on and ran out the door. Upon exiting the room the first thing Mike saw was a scene of utter chaos as Beverly Hills finest chased prostitutes and customers around the house. “There’s another one!” An uniformed officer shouted once he spotted Mike. Mike wasted little time as he ran in the other direction. Only two police officers bothered to follow him as he ran down the hall. Mike looked behind him and realized that it wouldn’t be long before he was caught if he stayed at the bordello so he began to desperately look around for a way to escape. His salvation came in the form of a big window; Mike closed his eyes and jumped through the window. Glass shattered all about him and Mike quickly prayed that it was a first story window. Luckily it was, Mike didn’t stop to recover from his stunt and ran towards his home as fast as he could.” The two police officers stood at the broken window and watched Mike as he made his escape. “Should we go after him?” The officer asked his partner. “No,” his partner replied as he lit up a cigarette, “just let him go.” It didn’t take Mike long to reach his home. He entered his home through one of the back doors and quickly removed the bondage mask. He stood and panted heavily and silently cursed himself for wasting time with a hot bordello. It was obvious that the only way to solve his problem was to beat Ami Mizuno senseless so that he could get a shot at the Television Title and a chance to regain his fame. -The End-
