Vengeance?

17 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Vengeance? Tony Stecchino Global Wrestling Promo #654 Date: 10/17/03 Location: NYC The Restaurant A single candle is shown glowing brightly in the middle of a table. There is a bustling going on around the outside of the flame. To the eyes, no one can see. To the left of the table a beautiful woman in dark slacks and a dark red shirt, showing some cleavage, but still leaving enough to the imagination. She has shoulder length blond hair, evergreen eyes and smile that would kill you. Her name? Andrea. To the right of the table sits a man that is well known all the way around the world and then some. He has his newly died black hair slicked back, his ice blue eyes stare at the wonderment from across the table. A finely cut Armani suit. Black with dark blue pinstripes. Very classy indeed. His name? Anthony Stecchino. “Enjoying dinner my dear? “Yes very much so, it’s so wonderful here.” “It is. It’s a favorite of mine. And you are the first girl that I have every brought here.” “Well aren’t I special.” “Indeed,” smiles at her. “So tell me, what are you doing currently?” “Well I am an English major at Western Washington University and I am currently back east here checking out grad schools and stuff like that. What about you?” “Well I am a business man. My assets seem to quadruple each day and the money is more than I can ever spend in a life time. So it is all working out for the best right now.” “How old are you?” “Twenty-four. And yourself?” “Twenty-one.” They smile at each other for a second. Andrea takes a bite of her salad, Anthony a bite of his steak. As Anthony reaches for her hand two men in black suits on black ties approach the table. “Sir we have a situation.” “Can it not wait?” “It needs your addressing currently sir.” “Anthony?” Anthony holds up his hand to silence Andrea as he looks at his bodyguards. “It’s a matter of life and death Mr. Stecchino.” “Who’s?” “Yours.” Anthony starts to nod as Andrea looks on. A worried expression pasted all over her face. Though they have known each other for an extraordinarily short amount of time, Andrea is starting to feel things for Anthony, not love, just an extreme case of liking and even caring for him. “We need to leave now sir.” She sighs as he stands. And looks at him with worry filling her eyes. He grabs her and kisses her on the cheek. He walks away his two guards in flank. She watches him walk out the front door. She feels something in her hand. He handed her something. She opens up her hand. Its two one hundred dollar bills and his card with his phone number on it. She smiles and gets a feeling that everything will be OK. On The Way Stecchino is in his limo, body guards to both sides of him. Everything is deathly quiet. A pin could drop and be heard a mile away. Sorry to be cliché, but the tension could easily be cut with a knife. But it doesn’t feel so right. Stecchino starts to think to himself. The wheels constantly turning in his head. Wondering how is going to deal with the next problem, the next business deal. The next date? In a time like this he should not even have romance on his mind. Sure, the woman is beautiful and ever so smart. Her eyes light up his life. He has never felt this way initially about a woman. Sure Melissa Phillips he was in love with and put her above everything in his life, including his career. Then he went insane and it was ruined. Now she is in the arms of another man. God damn Yankees! “We there yet?” “Not yet Mr. Stecchino, soon.” “OK.” Why can’t we just get there now? I got way too much to do tonight. I want to get back to the date. Get back to her. Be happy and get a great night’s sleep. But how can I do this when there is a matter of life and death. All my business assets and my life could be lost in fire of stray bullet. The Office The limousine pulls up outside the 32 story Stecchino Investment Inc. building just outside of Manhattan. Outside waiting are three body guards and Stevie Nickels. Nickels is in a suit. White on white with a black tie. He looms larger than most of the body guards and he smiles as soon as Stecchino arrives. Stecchino steps out of the limo looking at his building and then walks over and embraces Nickels. Stecchino is soon flanked by his three body guards. Another car pulls up seconds later and five more exits. “Boy Tony, you come well prepared don’t ya?” “I get a little nervous, thins are going too good right now to mess up.” “How so?” “I will explain later.” Stecchino starts walking inside. Soon he is followed in by Nickels and the eleven body guards. They reach the lobby. Absolute silence. Stecchino looks to his left and then again to his right. There appears to be no one here. They head straight to the elevators. Stecchino and six body guards get in the service elevator, Nickels and the five get into the main elevator. Stecchino and Nickels stand behind a maze of flesh and bullet proof jackets. The doors close. As the elevators rise, a Japanese man with seven of his closest friends stands in Stecchino’s office. Waiting to strike vengeance upon him. The man? The brother of the deceased owner of Yakuza Shipping. His heart heavy and his mind a mess. The perfect ingredients of a man ready to kill. His friends, guns in hand, ready to destroy the man, who took over the company. The brother, wanting what is rightfully his. He was to kill his brother and become powerful. Stecchino beat him to it. Now he must die. The elevators reach the 32nd floor and the doors open. The body guards have their guns drawn ready to fire upon anyone who dares show their face with a gun in hand. They cautiously exit the elevator, guns still drawn. Stecchino and his men walk briskly and sternly toward his office. Nickels sees this and has his men pick up the pace. Stecchino opens the door and gun goes right to his forehead. No shots fired. Nickels and his men walk into the room and see this; they draw their guns toward the opposition. Stecchino has a giant smile upon his face. And begins to laugh. “Why do you laugh? You ignorant American!” The laugh and smile dies from Stecchino’s face. “See, you lousy American, this is not a funny situation. You are going to die.” “Hey boys, kill his men!” In a burst of gun fire, the seven Japanese men fall to their backs, bloody holes in their chests and heads. The brother of Mr. Yakuza scared and knowing he will die soon, pulls his trigger. CLICK. His worried expression grows and the fear consumes him. CLICK. CLICK. CLICK. CLICK. CLICK. Stecchino annoyed takes the gun. Releases the safety and then points the gun right at the man’s head. He fires a single shot, spraying brains and blood to the back of his office. The body of the Japanese man falls in a heap in the floor. “Next time make sure the safety is off and I am not American. I am Italian and a gentleman.”
