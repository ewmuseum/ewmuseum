GET IT?

20 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/20/03 You have no pride You severly lack manhood Your boss is a slut Your sister is a whore What are you going to do about it.... The sound of Ice's voice spills through the dark night as he sits alone. His blond hair is pulled back into a low ponytail, and rest gently on his shoulders. His shoulders which are covered by a thin blue sweatshirt, that looks like it is at least ten years old, and has been through hell and back with all the rips and stains on it. Ice leans forward and gently begins to rub his chin. His gaze is straight ahead and doesn't waver as the wind begins to blow, stirring the leaves in the trees, breaking the calming silence. The cameraman begins to stir at the eerie silence, and lightly clears his throat, in attempt to get Ice's attention. Ice's attention, however, is still unwavering. Impatiently the cameraman clears his throat once again, hoping to get Ice's attention. Again, however, Ice doesn't acknowledge the cameraman, and just gazes out into the dark nothingness that is in front of them. The cameraman scoots forward on the bench he has been sitting on, and attempts to stand up. Just as he begins to stand, Ice's left arm darts out, and knocks the cameraman back onto the bench. Ice slowly turns his head in the direction of the cameraman, and gives him and evil glare before slowly turning his attention to where it was before. 'You do this not for yourself. You do this so your family can remain economically independent. You fight so that people can enjoy the show. That's great, that's wonderful, that's just dandy.' Ice turns to the camera and sneers. His upper lip curls in disgust as he evilly glares into the camera's lens. Again the wind blows, rustling the leaves in the distance, and breaking the eerie silence. Ice's angry sneer fades away, and slowly Ice's head turns away from the camera, and his attention once again focuses on the empty, dark, nothingness that is in front of him. Ice leans back onto the bench, and takes in a long, deep breath. He holds the breath in for what seems like an eternity before slowly, allowing it to release between his lips. Ice's straight ahead gaze moves slightly up, and to the left, as he stares at the white glowing moon. The moonlight pierces through the black night sky and shines on the ground below it, like a spotlight to a stage. 'For being such an intelligent man Jin, you just don't seem to get it. You just don't understand. It's like what I do is too deep for you and your kind to comprehend. You see Jin, while you don't do this for yourself. While you do this to give your family economic independence. While you fight to please those people. I don't. You know why I don't? NO YOU DON'T HAVE ANY IDEA! THIS COMPANY, THIS BUSINESS, THESE VENUES I COMPETE AT, THE COMPETITORS I COMPETE AGAINST, THIS SUITCASE I CARRY MY LIFE IN, THE HOTEL ROOMS I SLEEP IN EVERYNIGHT, THE BLOOD, THE SWEAT, AND THE TEARS I SHED ARE ALL I HAVE! AND I WILL BE DAMNED IF I LET A FLASH IN THE PAN, GOOD FOR NOTHING, LITTLE PUNK, WHO HASN'T EARNED A DAMN THING HE HAS RECEIVED, TAKE WHAT I DESERVE!' Ice's breaths are heavy now, as he leans forward and runs his hands over his face, trying to calm himself down. His beat red face begins to lose its color and return to the shade of pale white it was before his outburst. His breaths quickly return back to their normal rate, as Ice once again leans back and gazes into the night. Once again calm, almost as if the outburst never occurred, Ice gazes into the empty nothingness of the night. Ice tilts his head back, and begins to chuckle to himself. The chuckling suddenly stops as Ice violently snaps his head in the direction of the camera, and angrily stares into its lens. Ice methodically leans forward, and the view of his cold, remorseless, icy blue eyes fills the screen. His words echo through their evil stare. 'Jin, this isn't about liking someone, or disliking someone. This isn't about a shitty pleather strap, with gold spray painted tin on it. This is about you, speaking before thinking. You speaking before totally understanding what your words will lead to. You jumping in and stealing the spotlight which is far more deserved by someone else other than your punk ass. Jin, three on two, Evolution, you and your bullshitters against myself and Kid Flash. Three on two, Evolution, it is going to be the begining of your learning. Jin you need to understand something, and you need to understand it very quickly. When you speak of this world, and who it revolves around, you need to realize, that this world we live in, this world we compete in, this world we call home, is MY WORLD! But you don't seem to get it. So at Evolution, I WILL MAKE YOU GET IT.' The sound of a car stops Ice's speaking, as he quickly stands up and looks around to see who is causing the sound, and where the sound is coming from. Out from the empty nothingness, Ice was staring at earlier, comes two headlights. Methodically the headlights approach, before suddenly stopping. Star walks into the light and smiles at Ice. Ice nods in response to her smile, as Star quickly turns and walks past the light, and into the darkness once again. Ice begins to walk towards the lights, but stops and turns back to the camera. 'Jin, before I go, I want you to think about something. You have entered a world you know nothing about. There is nowhere for you to run, there is nowhere for you to hide. I am keeper of the key, and guardian of fate. Through me, you live, through me you die. ANYTIME........ANYWHERE.' Ice turns and walks into the light. A car door is heard closing. The car backs up, turns around, and drives into the abyss of the night. The scene fades.
