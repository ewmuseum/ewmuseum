More Shopping and Thinking.

25 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 25 The scene opens up to the doors that lead into the mall. Me and Joey were ready to go back home. We pretty much wasted our whole wallets today on jackets and shoes and all that other stuff. Me and him walked out of the doors and started pushing the carts to where we had parked the car. We got there and put all the stuff into the trunk of the car. “I’ll go take the carts back.” Joey said. “Ok. I’ll start the car and make some more room for the things.” I said. Joey took mine and his carts and started pushing them back into the mall. He got to the doors and waited for hem to open. He walked inside and by 5 seconds he was already walking out of it. He got to the car and got inside. I was buckled up and already waiting for him to. He buckled his seat belt and I backed up out of the parking space. “So, now what do we do?” he said to me. “I don’t know. I think I wanna go home to just sit down and think about everything that has happened for the last 2 days. I’m also going to take a nap because I’m stiff and am really tired.” I said to him. “Ok, That’s what I’m gong to do minus the thinking of what happened for the lat 2 days.” he said. I started driving down the street as we kept chatting. I stopped at a few red lights. We drove by a few cars, it looked like they just had been in a car accident. The cars were both smashed and the gas tank looked like it was going to blow up any time now. *15 Minutes Later* I pulled up to Joey’s house so we could get out of the car and go inside his house. He got out and walked up his little sidewalk. We opened the door to his house and walked inside. I backed up out of the driveway and started down the gravel road. I few rocks came up and hit my car and I got it all dirty. *Another 15 Minutes Later* I had just pulled up to my own driveway. I got out of my car and locked the doors of it. I walked up the little sidewalk that led to my house. I took out my key and unlocked the door. I put the key back into my pocket and walked into my house. I took off my shoes and threw my jacket onto the couch. “What to do, what to do.” I said to myself. I walked up the stairs. My feet thumped every time I touched a new step. I got to the top and walked into my bedroom. It was clean, I remember my mother cleaning it for me. I walked over to my bed and sat down onto it and started thinking about my mother. “I miss her already.” I said to myself again. I got off the bed and went over to my dresser. I opened the first drawer and saw some pictures of my mother and me. There was also one of my father, but it was with my mother and him, not me. I missed my father and mother. They were both dead, and now they would have to wait for me. I started crying. A few tears dropped onto the floor as the scene started to fade to darkness.
