Interview (High Octane RP)

27 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/27/04 Fade in. We see Steven Dinkler, sitting in a black leather loveseat. Dinkler: Welcome, everybody, to Global Wrestling News Byte. High Octane is fast approaching. The main event: for the first time in Global Wrestling history, both the World and International titles will be decided in an Ironman match. “The Steel Lion” Akodo Rokku will take on Ice in the long-awaited rematch from Showdown in Tokyo. But perhaps one of the most anticipated and controversial matches on the card, without a doubt, will be the Platinum title match, where the reigning champion Brick will take on perhaps the most controversial superstar in the history of the company, Mark Makonie! Since Makonie’s arrival, these two men have been at each other’s throats, mostly due to Makonie’s racist views. Dissolve to the 1-26-04 edition of Evolution. We see Brick and Makonie trading blows. Brick then starts to gain the upper hand. The footage ends with Brick throwing Makonie out the second floor window. Dinkler: It began as early as the Evolution before Global Reload. Cut back to Dinkler. Dinkler: Since then, theirs has been one of the most emotionally-charged rivalries in the company’s almost five year history, and these two men will finally collide at High Octane. Joining me today is the current Platinum champion, Brick. Sitting across from him on a leather couch is Brick. On his lap is the Platinum title belt. Dinkler: As we’ve said before, this match at High Octane will be about more than the Platinum title. This is about beliefs, about ideas. From day one, you and Makonie have been at odds and a lot of people say that you started it. Brick: That depends on how you look at it. In terms of throwing the first punch, I started it. But it was Makonie who fired the first shot. Anybody who knows me will tell you that I don’t start anything with anybody who doesn’t start something with me. Makonie started this. He got in my face. He got under my skin. The minute he walked in the company, spouting that garbage, he started it. And whether or not I start something, I always set out to finish it. Cut to Dinkler. Dinkler: At Global Reload, after your match in the Pit with Terminator Michael Page, Makonie gave you a message on the Globaltron. Cut to Global Reload, the end of the Pit match. Brick stands in the middle of the ring looking around waiting for the perfectionist himself to arrive. But the jumbo tron flickers off real quick as it doesn’t show any more of the images. The arena is dark as the ramp way has fire going down the sides, one of the trademarks of Mark Makonie’s entrance. Then Mark Makonie’s face shows up on the Globaltron. Makonie: So Brick you probably thought that I was going to come down to the ring didn’t you? Brick stands in the middle of the ring, his eyes focusing right on the jumbo tron. Makonie: But I just thought that I would wait until you rest up because I know that you just had a tiresome match. I want to be fair, I want to make sure I get you when your fresh, I want it to be just perfect. (Makonie pauses as he takes a drink from the glass that he is holding in his right hand. It looks as if there is alcohol in this glass as well, like a mixed drink.) Makonie: That’s right Brick, I am perfect unlike you. Just look at your skin color you coon. That sets you off to be imperfect in the start. You should just hop on your little boat and head back over to Africa where you belong. Next thing is that why do you have so much hatred towards me Brick? I’m the new guy that comes into GW and you throw me out of a window the first Evolution that I’m at. You gave me something that I will never forget and that is this. Makonie takes the bandage off of his head to reveal the deep cut from the window of the car glass. Makonie: This is what I get from you Brick for no reason. Well there has to be an explanation out there doesn’t there? So Brick I’m waiting. Brick stands in the middle of the ring without saying a word and just focusing on the Tron. Makonie: Well I have one for you Brick. I have been thinking all day and drinking that is. After all of this I have devised a simple explanation. Say that I’m the new guy coming into this fed and you’re the veteran looking for the old spotlight that you used to have when you were at the pinnacle of your career. Now you’re afraid that someone as fit and as perfect as me will take over your spotlight so you might as well destroy the chance that they have at any stardom in GW. Brick you don’t rule this fed anymore, it has gone on to bigger and better things and one of those was recruiting me here. Makonie takes another drink from his glass as he starts to smile into the camera. Makonie: So Brick I remember what you did to be on Evolution. Just wait Brick we will tango sooner or later and for now I just hope that you don’t do anything that I don’t approve of. So in other words you have to be PERFECT! The Globaltron shuts off and leaves Brick in the middle of the ring stunned from what he has just heard. Cut to Brick. Dinkler: What do you say to the idea that you attacked him because you were just putting the new guy in his place? Brick: Now that’s some bullshit, right there. He may be new to the company, but there is nothing new about peckerwoods like Makonie. There’s nothing new about the way cats like him think. It’s old. Not only is it old, it’s tired. That kind of sick hatred, there’s nothing new about it. Hatred like that killed Emmet Till. Hatred like that killed Medgar Evers. Hatred like that killed four little girls in an Alabama church fire. Hatred like that killed Michael Stewart, James Byrd, Jr., Amadou Diallo, the names go on and on. That’s why I singled him out because of that hatred. Dinkler: To be fair, there have been times when you’ve made a big deal out of being the first person of color in this company to achieve certain goals. Brick: Yes, I have, because it was something worth talking about. I’m proud of the achievements I’ve made in the years I’ve been in this company. I’m proud of being the company’s first Black United States champion. I’m proud of being one of its first Black Tag Team champions. I’m proud of being its first Black International champion. I’m proud of being its first Black World champion. I am proud of my heritage and my ancestry. That said, that pride doesn’t come and never has come at the expense of another’s heritage or culture. I don’t subscribe to that kind of bigotry. At least, I try not to. Dinkler: This is certainly going to be one of the most talked about matches of the evening. A lot of people are rooting for you. Word is you even got a call from Rev. Al Sharpton. Brick: I got calls from a lot of Black leaders. Rev. Sharpton, Rev. Jesse Jackson, Minister Louis Farrakhan, Kweisi Mfume, Cornel Wilde, the list goes on. All of them wished me well in my match. They pretty much said that I was a symbol. That winning the match and beating Makonie would send a message. It would send a message that all the racists in the world are wrong. It’s a message I have no problem sending, and it’s a message I intend to send loud and clear at High Octane. Fade to black.
