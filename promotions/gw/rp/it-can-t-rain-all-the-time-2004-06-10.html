It Can't Rain All The Time

10 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06.10.04 The rain seemed to have been constant for months now, even though it had only been a few days. It beat violently against the window that 'Superstar' Ken Partridge Jr affectionally knew as his wall. The sound of it crackled through out the entire apartment, rhymically banging out a cadence that was sweet enough to calm the nerves of the current GW International Champion. The Elite member was sprawled out on his white Italian leather sofa with his arms behind his head staring up at the vaulted ceiling of his penthouse, all the while strumming his fingers about his chest to the same tune that the storm was singing. He was exubrient that his day of relaxation had been just that, as the next would begin his rigerous twenty hour work-out regime that had become the talk of a few gyms around the area. With his thoughts elsewhere instead of on Soma and Sabbot, his opposing team for Evolution, Ken began to drift into a wonderland where neither perception nor reality existed to the untrained mind. 'Never let me go,' Gunner repeated, soaking wet in the doorway of Ken's bedroom. 'Don't worry,' Ken responded, 'I never will again.' The two stood there, holding one another for what seemed like ages as their bodies pressed against one another. Ken took in the sweet smell of her hair and a smile formed across his handsome face. He pulled her closer to him, his strong arms around her beautiful figure, and she had never felt so safe in her entire life. 'Protect me,' she said to Ken's surprise. 'From what,' he pondered from her as he pulled away, looking deep into her eyes, which were suddenly further away than before. His room was gone and so was the embrace. The two now stood in a huge parking deck and Ken was looking onto her curiously before he began his run for her. However, to his dismay, no matter how hard he pushed or how fast he moved, he could never get any closer. 'Protect me,' she whispered again, still looking at Ken. His run became more violent as began to tread more intensly than he had in his entire life, only to make no progress. 'Protect me.' A large black SUV soon came into view, speeding through the deck, and Ken recognized it immediatly... it was the same vehicle that had put Akodo Rokku in critical status. 'Protect me.' As the car sped on, so did Ken. No progress was made, unfortunantly though, as Ken was forced to watch as Gunner was pinned between the SUV and the wall behind her. 'Protect m---' Ken stopped in shock, letting all his senses dissapate as he watched Maverick Reno and John Zero exit the vehicle. His shock soon turned into rage as he began running again, but this time, he was finally moving. Closer and close he approached with each second before he made it to the two men. He pulled back his fist with all of the anger and madness this world had ever seen and-- 'What the hell,' Ken whispered to himself as the phone on the table next to him rang. He realized that it was all just a bad dream, releasing a, 'Thank God,' before answering his cordless. 'Damn, man. I never thought that you'd come to the phone,' chuckled the voice of GW World Champion Josh. 'I'm sorry,' Ken retorted, 'I was asleep. You woke me.' 'You to, eh' responded Josh. 'That happened to me this morning. Anyways, I'll tell you all about it, but I need you to fly down here now. We need to discuss some things and get ready for our match against Leinad and Sage.' 'I can't,' said Ken as he set up on the sofa yawning, 'My routine begins tomorrow. i can't skip it.' 'You can this week,' pitched an excited Josh. 'Trust me.' 'Fine,' Ken gave in. 'I'll be at the airport in half an hour and hop the chopper. But, Josh...' 'Yeah.' 'This had better be good,' finsihed KPJ before hanging up the phone and standing up, refelecting upon the nightmarish scenerio that he had just bore witness to. 'What the fuck is going on with me?'
