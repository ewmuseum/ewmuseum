Red eye's once again

13 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-13-2005 “Once again.” Once again I had lost. Not to someone hard but to someone that I had already beaten before. Fred Grest. A super soaker boy. Beating me for the first time in my long quest of trying to come back. The day was just hard on me. The stress, the headache, the hangover from last night. It was all not worth it. The sun was shining brightly in the morning sky. Raining down on me and my exposed head of mine. The heat was becoming even more then I could bare, drowning me with every inch of power light heat that came out of the sun. Not even a cloud in the sky to pause the heat from hitting me. No tree’s, nothing to help. Running like I always due, traveling along the Whiterock lake path that was put in several years ago, trying to release all the pressure I had built up deep inside. Passing by the little river that had run off the lake and towards the middle of the little island like place I was running passed. All I could do was think about how I was never going to turn this heartache around for me. The losing was the hard part and till I got a win, nothing was going to be the same for me. Racing up the hill, a heard a small voice come from the set of tree’s that had came by over me. “Hello?” Stopping for a minute to take a look. Nothing. It was as empty as the can of soda after a little child gets a hold of the can. My eyes, wiggling through the tree’s in front of me. Walking a little more down the trail before the voice came again. “Don’t trust anyone?” “Hello again?” Saying out loud towards the tree’s once again. “Don’t trust anyone?” The voice says again, trying to scare me or something. Though I wasn’t easily scared. Depressed was one thing, scared is a totally different thing. Walking towards one of the tree’s a little closer, I saw nothing still. What was going on? Where was this voice? Stepping away from the tree, a hand came right on my back. Leaping up off the set of feet that I have, twisting around to see the same figure standing there in front of me. Red eyes, long black hair, strange looking to anyone’s eyes. “WHAT THE HELL!” Screaming at the top of my lungs. Taking a step back from the person, thing, whatever, tossing my hair away from my face. “Don’t trust anyone anymore.” He says, whispering it from his dirty lips. “No one?” “What?” Asking the man back, saying the first two things out of my mouth. “I’ve been right here watching you. I know what you are thinking of doing.” Says the red eye guy who seemingly is right there now at every pass I go to. Turning away from him, trying to not listen to him anymore as I began to walk down the trail once more. He might not be right in front of me but I could still feel him. His heart beat, his breath, those red eyes, everything right on my fingertips. I didn’t get far, stopping just a few feet from my other spot, looking right up at him once again. “Who are you?” Saying towards him, trying to not show what VERY little fear I had for him. “I’m the part of you that you have tried to get rid of all your life.” Waving his human hands around in the air like he was some office manager at a huge company. “Part of me?” Confused a little. “You can’t be apart of me. You are to real.” He was as real as anything. His skin, his eyes, his breath, his blood, everything that a man or woman would have, he had it. “I’m only as real as you let me be.” Grabbing me on the wrist as tightly as he could. “See what I mean.” “OUCH!” Pulling away before looking down at the red mark that was left behind. “You bastard?” Covering my mouth, hoping no one was around to hear the rudeness of my mouth but to be frank, that is the least of my worries right now. Walking away from him, stepping backwards towards whatever that was behind me. I couldn’t see and I wasn’t about to take my eyes off this freak. “You see Elia, you need me. You need my strength, you need my words, you need my emotion in you. Without me you will be the bottom of the food chain in everything you do.” Stepping along with me as the two of us were tangling to wherever. “I need you?” Slamming into a tree finally as it made me come to a sudden stop. “I don’t need you?” My words stopping in the middle as his hand smashes right into the tree, causing a section of the wood to come off the tree. Blood began to go down the hand of him, running like the river I had saw earlier. My god, he was real or was my eyes deceiving me? I began to breath harder, closing my eyes and counting to whatever number I had to, to try and end this from my eyes. “I won’t go away Elia. You and me are meant to be together. You are stronger with me and you know it.” His words, rattling in my ear as I still was counting, making sure he was gone. Opening my eyes, searching around the hardly lit area, I saw nothing. He was gone or was he? Walking out a little ways, I roughly heard a noise from behind me. Turning around swiftly, seeing the form of a squirrel sitting there looking at me. My eyes fell with ease, knowing it wasn’t him. As I began to walk back, happy that it was over, that the fear could be put away once and for all, I hard that voice once again. “I will not go away Elia’I WILL NOT !” Screaming towards my path once again. Beginning to run the opposite way, Passing by everything and anything in my way before reaching wide open space.
