Preparation (COTT RP 1)

27 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/27/04 Clash of the Titans was fast approaching. The entire roster would compete in an over the top rope GLOBAL RUMBLE to win a shot at the GW WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP at WRESTLECADE! The order of entry was announced on the previous Evolution. Brick was in his personal gym, doing some one-handed push-ups. Underneath him was a sheet containing the order of participants in the Rumble. He read it as he went up and down. This was going to be a very intense Clash of the Titans, what with the new talent, especially the new talent he had developed new animosities with. Also, if he won the Rumble this year, there would be the possibility of him going to Wrestlecade to face the World champion. If it was Ice, it would be his chance to finally beat the Monster and redeem himself after losing to Ice on the last Evolution. Fortunately, Brick didn’t have an extra match to compete in this year, but then again, this was true for many of the other participants in the Rumble. “Number 4, Aaron,” he said over the speakerphone. “And you’re not worried?” “What’s the point of worrying, man” It won’t help me win the Rumble. All I can do now is get ready. Because I got a lot of competition to deal with, and the cat that comes right before me is the biggest cat in the company.” “You mean that guy whatshisname” Sergei Straz-something?” “Just call him Surge. I do.” “How big is this cat again?” “Eight foot two, five hundred seventy-eight pounds.” “Damn!” “I know. He might be one of the hardest cats to get out of the ring. It’ll probably take more than one of us to just get one of his legs up. How does a cat grow to eight feet?” “Well, Goliath was close to nine feet. I think it’s called acromegaly.” “Acro-what?” “It’s a condition where people grow larger than normal.” “Oh, acroMEGaly,” Brick replied, sarcastically. “What about the other cats in the match, like Train and Mark Makonie?” “I know I definitely owe Train for that punk ass sneak attack on last week’s Evo.” “Well, you did kind of start it, calling him out the week before.” “Yeah, well, when I see brothers teaming up with Nazis, I think they need to be called on it.” “I agree with you, but out in the ring” And speaking of Makonie?” “He’s number 35.” “35 out of?” “38.” “Damn.” “I know. And he won’t hesitate to jump me when I’m not at my peak. Remember when I beat John Zero for the Platinum title” Has the lights go out and then jumps me when I’m not looking. Then he?” Brick then heard a baby crying. “Is that Ashley?” “Yeah, she just woke up. I think she’s hungry.” “Listen, you’re gonna have your hands full. Let me get out of your way. I’ll talk to you later.” Brick then hung up the phone, resuming his workout. He then started to look more closely at the paper with the names of the participants in the Global Rumble. It was then that he noticed something odd: There was no number 38. This puzzled Brick. He hated wild cards like this. Number 38 could be just about anybody. And that meant Brick had to prepare even more.
