Date: today Ice leans back, and taps the phone against his chin. Again his eyes glaze over, as he drifts away into a trance like state. The tapping of the phone against his chin slows to a tap....tap.....tap.... like water dripping from a faucet. When suddenly the silence, along with Ice's trance, is broken by a knock at his door. Ice lets out and angered sigh, as he pushes himself away from the wall, and towards the door. Again, the knock is hear, and becomes louder as Ice slowly, unwantingly, makes his way over to the door. While walking Ice mumbles to himself. 'I thought I told them I wanted to be bother by no one. Stupid mother...' He turns the knob and opens to door. A look of astonishment flows from his face. His eyes widen, and his face turns pale, even more pale that it was before, almost as if he has seen a ghost. 'Hey....' A look of bewilderment consumes Ice's face, as he stares in disbelief at the sight in front of him. Star simply smiles at him and walks into the room. Ice slightly back peddles as she walks towards him. Star stops and gasps slightly, as Ice leans in towards her, reaches around her, and pushes the door shut. Ice leans back, turns, and begins to walk away from the blond beauty. 'You want something to drink?' 'No, that's quite alright, thanks.' Star shakes her head and smirks as Ice walks into the kitchen, leaving her alone in the room. She walks around the end of the couch, and takes a seat, awaiting Ice's return. Ice walks back into the room with a bottle of water in his left hand. He takes a seat on the couch next to Star, and opens the bottle. Star begins to speak, as Ice tips the bottle back, and takes a long drink. 'I...uh...first wanted to give you my congratulations on your accomplishment last week.' She meekly smiles, in avoidence of getting to her point. Ice notices, and smirks as he pulls the bottle of water away from his mouth. He takes his time screwing the cap back on, and setting the bottle of water down on the floor next to his feet. Again Ice smirks at Star, and she nerveously smiles back at him. 'You looked really good.....' 'Alright, cut the crap. You're not fooling anyone, we both know you are a horrible liar, so why don't you stop now, save your breath, and just get to the point.' Star bashfully looks away from Ice, and down towards the floor. Nerveously she figets with her hair, pulling it back behind her ears. As her fingers circle behind her ears, her right hand stops and begins to gently kneed her right earlobe. Simultaniously she begins to bite her bottom lip. With his left hand Ice grabs her hand and pulls it away from her ear. While at the same time, using his right hand, he gently lifts her chin, so that they are looking into each other's eyes. Ice's eyebrows raise, in a questioning manner, but are not met with any verbal response. 'What? Something is up, and obviously you came here to tell me something. So let me have it. What did you come here to tell me?' His harsh, sarcastic tone, causes Star's eyes to swell with tears. The tears slowly sneek out and run down her silky smooth skin. With his thumb, Ice brushes away her tears, and reassuringly smiles at her. Her sobbing becomes more drastic, but Star intakes a long deep breath and fights away the tears. 'Brock.....I don't want you to think, that I'm doing this to get 'us' back, or to mess with you in any way. But, I'm..' 'You're what?' Star grabs both of Ice's hands, and her emerald green eyes lock onto his cold, icy blue eyes. 'No, not I'm....' Ice shoots her a puzzled expression. Star smiles at him, leans forward, and whispers into his ear. 'We're pregnant.' Ice snaps his head towards Star, and looks at her in shock. In and unsure manner she half smiles at him. Ice raises his right hand, and runs his fingers through his hair. He slowly rises from the couch, and walks away from it and Star, heading towards the lone window in the room. With his hands behind and gently resting on the crown of his head, Ice gazes out of the window. Star, stands from the couch, and cautiously creeps over towards Ice. 'Sweetie??' Ice suddenly spins, and stares directly at her. She freezes in her spot, and her eyes widen in fear as the cold, icy blue eyes from the man standing in front of her pierce a hole through her. She takes a small gulp, and begins to slightly tremble. Ice's stern expression fades as his emotionless eyes, twinkle, and a smile creeps across his face. He stretches out his arms, and wraps them fimrly around Star's petite frame, lifting her into the air. The two twirl around, and Ice lovingly kisses her neck, her shoulders, her cheeks, her forehead, her nose, and finally her lips. 'Sweetie? Are you sure you're okay with it?' Ice sets her back down on her feet, and tilts his head to the right. He stares at her for a moment withouth saying, while gently caressing the left side of her face. Star closes her eyes, and leans her head back, enjoying the feel of his touch. 'I'm more than okay with it.' Ice widely smiles at her, as he wraps his arm across her shoulders. They walk over to the door, and Ice opens it for Star. Star pauses and looks up at him. 'Where are we going?' Ice just smiles and winks, and he walks her out of the room.
