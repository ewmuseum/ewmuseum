Back In The Land....Civil War...

7 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-7-2004 Dawn broke over the land, the light rushing from beyond the horizon to defeat the darkness that enveloped the earth for the night. Just the slightest bit of fog lingers still in this early morning shot, and as light once more vanquishes it’s enemy, we are met with a stunning visage of rolling green hills and stands of strong timber. This land looks old, and untouched by the brutal hands of man. The view moves down and the soft nickering of horses can be heard, and when finally the animals come into view, it seems as if they are straight out of a medieval fantasy. Each horse wears a crest upon it’s back as a saddle blanket. The manes are styled in numerous fashions, some braided, some short’Hell, some are even entwined with ribbons. Next to a truly magnificent white stallion, Venneman Pendragon stands, staring at the newly risen sun. A slight breeze blows his blonde hair back from his strong brow, and his very visage is a projection of strength that is hardly found in the world of men these days. Clad in black breeches and soft leather boots, his flowing white shirt belies the power that is indeed in this man. He shakes his head and turns from the sun to stroke the horse’s neck? “Xerxes, mine old friend. “Tis been some time sense the two of us, thee and I, have journeyed into battle together.” The horse whickers softly and nuzzles Pendragon’s hand. Pendragon smiles and continues showing the affection. Hell, warhorses need to know they are appreciated too! “Aye’Thee’s a good horse’Today will be a bit different than what thou art used to. I know not why we art here, but it seems that there is trouble in this Land. Darkness lies in the open, and is not easily defeated. Waking here, with thee, and with mine sword, I knew that trouble brewed. This land is mine by birthright. I feel an attachment even when in mine other waking world?” **********FLASH**************** An elaborate living room, furnished with padded wooden chairs and couches. Banners hang from the wall, proudly displaying the Dragon’s Crest that is the sigul of Venneman Pendragon. The man himself sits upon the couch, lounging with one leg thrown over the arm. In his powerful hands he holds what appears to be a Katana sword. He absently sharpens this with a whet stone, and the sound of rock upon steel echoes through the cavernous room. With a smooth motion, he places the stone in his pocket, rises to his feet and dances through a couple of forms with the sword. Dancing is all that it can be called. Deadly, but beautiful. His movements are full of the sinewy grace of a wolf. The sword is a blur in his hands, and his concentration is unwavering. Three steps and a turn has the sword held above his head. Two retreating steps and a parrying thrust, and the sword points low in front of him. Each beginning and ending has a name, yet we shan’t bore you with the definitions here. As he dances, a shadow falls over the room. The change doesn’t disturb the twirling swordplay, and the blade almost seems to hum as it is taken from one form directly into another. Changing nothing, Pendragon launches an attack in the direction of the shadow, and in a flash of light he is thrown back to the couch. The blade glows, and as Pendragon shakes his head, he drops the blade and rubs his hand. Even kings get burned. Finally, the owner of the shadow comes into view. In a wizard’s cloak, an old man smiles amiably at Pendragon. Pendragon just stares and mouths the word “Merlin.” The wizard smiles kindly and smiles before striding into the room and taking a seat opposite the young king? “It has been awhile, my lad! Nice to see you still practice”.Although, I fear that in this dying world there is no need to live by the forms of the sword. It is an excellent way to keep the mind clean though? he frowns as Pendragon nods at the mention of the dying world. “There are other places than here, Venneman. Places where a man of your might can actually make a difference. Save what you can, my son”.” Pendragon frowns and shakes his head?. “I go where I am needed. Thee knows this. How can I be needed anywhere as badly as this bespoiled and dying world needs me” I am the light to the darkness here’And mine sword play might come in handy’Weapons match against Juri as I am sure thee knows?” At the mention of his profession, Merlin chuckles and smiles wryly at the blonde giant? “So’You are still a wrassler” Still locking horns with those who would be beneath you even were you not a king?” He says this with a tone of amusement, but his eyes share not the feeling. Pendragon smiles? “Some of the most vile men of the world have congregated upon the ranks of Global Wrestling. Their wickedness holds sway with acts such as murder and robbery. Why, murderous assassins are allowed a free hand in this place! Some of these men have the money and power to affect the world on a larger scale. And it is a good thing that the Lord King should be around to be a counterbalance to the evil that manifests itself”..” He is cut off by a dismissive wave from Merlin. “Boy, you don’t know evil. Evil is what destroyed your land when you were a boy. Evil is what took your father from you’Your real father. Evil is what waits for you in your true kingdom. Don’t you care what has happened since last you left?” Merlin eyes him quizzically and Pendragon’s eyes avert the gaze?. “I think about mine people every day. Yet, I have felt not the draw from them that would tell me that they art in dire straights. If something is amiss, I will be drawn to them?” He grows silent as Merlin casts a weary eye upon him? “And all would be well, my son, if that were true now. You see, Akumancha has vanished, and the land has been torn by civil war. So many seek to gain the throne’To gain YOUR throne. Some power has held you from taking part, and it is this power that I fear. I cannot even get a read upon the wielder, and I have been trying for weeks. A wizard is loose in your land, and only you can even hope to defeat his forces. There are but two factions vying for power. This nameless wizard, and the lords who still hold to you, their rightful king. The High Lord Benjamin holds power for them, and he is but seeking to steward your throne until you have the sense enough to seize it for your own. You would like this Benjamin. He is strong of heart and mind, much like you.” Pendragon’s face lifts in recognition. He, in fact, does have few friends? “Does this Benjamin have a penchant for sparring with words” Is he one to work the enemy into a frenzy without even leading men onto the battlefield?” Pendragon leans forward from his seat and Merlin smiles? “Yes, he does have a’Talent for stirring the pot. Why?” Pendragon smiles?.“Simply reminds me of someone I know well. If they are the same soul on two levels, this cannot be a mere circumstance. What must I do?” “Go to sleep. The spell I weave upon you will have you in the land when you wake. Xerxes is already there, waiting. Now, where is your sword” Not that thing you were playing with when I got here?.Your Sword!”” He looks around, unable to believe that Pendragon would have it very far from his hand. Pendragon’s face turns red as he looks at his feet? “Twas stolen. I am working on getting it back, but these people are difficult to find, and even more difficult to deal with?” Merlin looks aghast? “Was it a nation took it” To lead their army?” Pendragon shakes his head? “Nay. Twas stolen by a wrestler from GW.” Merlin cannot help but laugh. A mere wrestler taking the famed Excalibur from it’s true wielder. And you not getting it back?” “I told thee’these men are not “just” what they appear to be. Fates may hang in the balance here?” Merlin waves him off?“It matters not. The land needs you. The true power of the blade comes from your heart. The sword is just a tool with which to harness that. It is also a symbol of the rightful king. You must prove yourself without it I fear. There is no time to try and retrieve it. Now you must sleep. The men who support you have been informed that help is on the way. I only pray that it is enough, and that it is not too late. Sleep”.” Merlin waves a hand over Pendragon as the larger man lays a hand on his sword. Instantly, he falls asleep and begins to grow dim. Soon the couch is visible behind him, and then only a vague outline of the man is seen. Soon, the cushion rises back to its fluffiness as the weight that was on it has disappeared. “Go my boy’Greatness awaits you if only you will take it!!” **************FLASH*************** Back in the field, Pendragon shakes himself out of his revelry as the sun has climbed a bit higher on the horizon. His right hand rests upon the hilt of the sword that is belted to his side. His sword. Forged by his own hand, to conform to every nuance of the forms that he commands it. “Be ready mine friends?” he speaks to both sword and horse, for in battle, they are a part of him. And he needs to trust himself. He means to go to war, and he will be the main weapon for his cause. With a last pat to the horses head, he turns and nearly runs straight into a messenger. The messenger, no more than fourteen, steps back and puts his right fist over his heart in a salute. Pendragon nods as the boy hands him a bit of parchment? “What is thy name, lad?” The boy looks startled. He wasn’t expecting to actually speak with the King! “Willem sir. Willem Longbow.” Pendragon nods absently as he reads the message. With a smile, he pulls a quill from inside his shirt and writes on the opposite side of the parchment. When he is finished, he folds the paper and hands it back to the boy. He frowns at the quill as he puts it back into his shirt. “Damnable thing. What would I not give for a fountain pen!” The boy looks at him?“A what?” Pendragon shakes his head with an amused look on his face?“Never thee mind, son. Just wishful thinking on mine own part. Take that back to Lord Benjamin. The army is going to have to move?” The boy scampers off, as it seems only boys are really able to do. Pendragon smiles and strides after him, battle lust already beginning to shine in his eyes. **********FLASH*********** A trumpet blares, and armor rattles as men scurry here and there in the campsite’Well, what was the site. Tents have already been stowed, and the roaring Dragon banner is unfurled between two mounted horsemen. Pendragon sits his horse with an ease of long hours in the saddle, now dressed in his golden armor. A slightly smaller man sits his horse next to Xerxes, and the two are engaged in a bit of a palaver. ??hungry.” The smaller man cuts off as Pendragon smiles?. “Food shall come, Benjamin. It is not in short supply. The common folk of these parts will happily support their army. We are merely moving on without breakfast. The men can eat in the saddle. I have done so on many an occasion.” Pendragon shakes his head at the triviality? “As you say, my liege.” A slight bow of the head is the respect he shows. The High Lord Benjamin is second in command here, but not by much. The men know him, and have ridden with him. Pendragon is new, but he seems to sweat command. It is in his blood, and the men acquiesce to it. “Broadbane’s forces have converged on the small town of King’s Point. The Point is a town that is loyal to your crown. Otherwise, it is nothing strategic. Poor placing, no port’The high ground is probably occupied by now. It is only a ploy to draw us into the open. See what we got cooking on our side. Broadbane is no fool. He will not commit to battle with you until he has gauged your strength.” Pendragon looks thoughtfully at the ground before raising his eyes to meet Benjamin’s?“The fact that King’s Point is a loyal town would be enough to send mine army to help. The added fact of Broadbane hiding until I show myself drives me even harder?” “What” You want him to see you? See our numbers and our strengths?” Pendragon smiles?“This Broadbane wastes men. I shall not. I’ll not take our whole force out to fight. Just some cavalry. Enough to free the town. Perhaps he will even misjudge us, although I know that he will not show himself. Just the way he fights. He’ll not be seen until his champions are bested. We have to dismantle the body to get to the head, and that is exactly how we are going to fight this one out. Worry not, mine friend! Thou shalt see”.All will be well.” Benjamin tries to smile, but it comes across as a scowl and Pendragon bellows laughter and slaps the smaller man on the back?“My’Such a resemblance to mine friend from the other side”.” ~TO BE CONTINUED~
