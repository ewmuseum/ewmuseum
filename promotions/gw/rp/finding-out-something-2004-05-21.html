Finding Out Something

21 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 21 The scene opened up to the inside of Jesse Jamester's VIP lougne, in the GW headquaters. I has just come out of the office of Christina McCoy. But, there was only one problem, my Cruiserweight title, was stolen. And if your wondering who did it, it was Train. AT the very beginning of Evolution, and stole the title from Deacon Holmes, (when Deacon was the champion) and when it was mine and Deacon's match, I won it. But Train still has the title. And now it was my duty to get the title back from Train. I sat there on the couch, inside the lounge. Jesse was on the other couch watching the big screen television he had in the very corner of the room. We were watching some other fed's show, I think it was called NHW or something along the lines of that. Finally, Jesse took his eyes off of the television and looked straight at me. 'That stupid Train had to take your title.' he said to me. 'I know, and now I have to get it back.' I said back to him. He looked back at the television, as did I. We watching for a few minutes, and suddenly a knock at the door came. Jesse got off of the couch and walked over to the door. He opened it and standing there was a lady in a buisness suit. She had some kind of white card in her hands. I got off the couch to stand up. I walked over to the doorway to see what the lady wanted. 'I have a message for Mr. Roswell.' she said. 'I'm right here.' I said softly. 'Well, apparently there has been something going on at you house. One of your neighbors phoned us, and told us that they saw some people in the front of your house, and then they walked towards it. That's when the neighbor looked away and called us.' she said. 'I'll go home right now and see what's happening.' I said. I started walking out of the door when I saw Jesse turn off the television and walk towards me. I guess he was coming too. Me and him walked out of the room and down the hallway to the stairs instead of the elevator. We got to the EXIT door and pushed it open. We started walking down the stairs till we got to the lobby. I didn't drive my car here because my house is right down the street, so me and Jesse would have to walk there. Me and Jesse got to the lobby and walked through it. I pushed the door that lead outside, and we were on the sidewalk. 'I didn't drive my car here, so were going to have to walk. But its only down the street there.' I said. 'Its cool, I haven't had my workout today so this can be it.' he said. 'Ok.' that's all I replied with. We started walking down the sidewalk, the one that lead to my house. We passed a few old people, and a few young people and got to some traffic lights. There was a little button to push so the olights knew when to goto red so we could walk across the street. It turned red and we ran across the street where there was two lines of white paint that meant for the pedestrian that walked across. 'So, what have you been up to lately?' I asked Jesse. 'I've been mostly sitting in my lounge, watching television and eating stuff. There really isn't anything to do in this part of town.' he said. 'I know. I don't really know why I moved here. Maybe it was because this is where I grew up and all.' I said. We were almost at my house. We were about a block away, on te different street. We crossed itand walked a little bit and finally we got to the driveway of my house. I looked at it, and the front door was broken down. The garage door was wide open with stuff all over the place. Me and Jesse ran to the carnage and looked inside the house. Everything was lying n the floor smashed into little pieces, glass shattered everywhere. 'What happened?' I asked Jesse. 'I don't know, but from what I see, someone has trashed your house and left everything on the floor all broken.' he said. I ran inside the room, and saw a group of people in the kitchen with some knives, cuttinng everythnig in sight. They had black all on, with a black ski mask on. They had bags with the money sign on it, they were taking all of my stuff out of my house. I ran to them as they looked at me and screamed. 'Get out now!' one of them yelled. Me and Jesse ran up to some guy and kicked them in the stomach, one of them flew into the window, and broke it. The other guys ran out us with knives in there hands, they were at us, but me and Jesse double teamed them to the ground. One more guy came with a big baseball bat in his hands. He took a swing at me, but I ducked and it hit another one of the men. I jumped onto the counter and then came down with a big dropkick onto the man. 'This should get them out.' I said. 'You said it. There runnnig like little girls, lok out the window.' he said. I looked out the broken window and saw that all of the men were running down the street screaming like little girls. One of them tried to run across the street but a car came down fast and hit him. The man flew by the car and hit a mailbox that was coming out of the ground. I loooed back, and saw that there were two more men, laying in the living room, laying on the couch watching television. They must've not heard all the racket in the other room. Me and Jesse tip toed over to them, and kicked them in the stomachs. 'What the hell?' yelled one of them. 'Surprise, surprise.' me and Jesse yelled at the same time. Me and Jesse picked one of the guys up and through him into the wall. He busted right through it as paint and all that fell onto him. He had landed in the kitcehn. cause he broke through the wall. The other one got off the couch and ran at me. He hit me really hard as I flew into the other room. He turned around and got super kicked by Jesse. I got off the ground and walked back over and gave Jesse a high five. 'Good job with that kick.' I said. 'I needed to test it out. Haven't used it in awhile.' he said. Me and Jesse walked around the house to see if there were any other guys that were in my house. There wasn't, we had kicked all of there asses. Me and Jesse smiled at each other, as I walked over to the phone that was laying on the floor. I was going to call the police, and tell them what these guy did to my house. It rang for a few times and finally a young man on the other end answer. 'Hello?' the man said. 'This is Max Roswell. I had just come home a few seconds ago, and had seen that my house had been broken into. The people that broke into it were still here taking all of my stuff. Could you send some people over here to investigate.' I said. 'Sure thing, they'll be there in a few minutes.' the man said. Me and the man hung up. Even though, me and Jesse kicked those guys asses, and kind of trashed the house a bit, those guy were going to be put in jail for breaking and entering. Lucky to be them. Me and Jesse waited on the couch for the police officers to arrive here. A few minutes later, some guys walked through the door. They were wearig police suits, so they were here. They took one look at my house, and I bet they could've have shit there pants. 'They messed this up pretty good I see.' one of the officers said. 'Yeah. If your wondering where the guys are, there are two laying in the kitchen, one guy laying outside, he got hit by a car, and there are some more outside, one got thrown through the window in the kitchen, there was alot of them. I can't even remember how many there was.' I said. 'Ok, Mark, you go arrest all of the ones outside, and Dan, you go arrest al of the ones that are inside the house, I'll go all over and find out some stuff.' the one man said. 'Ok.' all of the officers replied at once. Me and Jesse walked around, now that the police were doing all of the work, we didn't have anything to do. Me and Jesse walked out of the door and outside, it was a very nice day out with the sun blazing down at us. We walked down the driveway and onto the actualy sidewalk. We saw the one officer carrying the man that got hit by a car. He had also handcuffed the other ones that tried to run away. 'So, what should we do?' I asked Jesse. 'I dont really know. Let's just take a walk around the block or something.' he said. Me and him started walking down the sidewalk. We walked past the officer that had all of the guys handcuffed to each other, all in one big pile. We smiled at the officer and kept walking. We turned the block a few times and were already getting closer to my house again. We walked past the officer again, but this time he was sitting on the neighbors lawn resting from all the work. We kept walking some more as the scene started to fade.
