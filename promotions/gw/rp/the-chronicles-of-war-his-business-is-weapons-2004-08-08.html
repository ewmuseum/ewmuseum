The Chronicles of War...His Business is Weapons!

8 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-8-2004 Merlin, omnipotent type being that he is, sits in a white room. Lounging on a white chair, his blue robe is quite the contrast to his surroundings. His staff leans across his knee and a smile plays about his lips. He nods as the shot comes into focus? “It is no accident that I am the chronicler of Pendragon’s tale. After all, it was I who seemingly forced him into action for this particular endeavor. I realize, many people of your world do not quite understand the Land’How does Pendragon get there” Why does he go? Why do some people over there seem familiar? I guess I should answer some of your questions, you of the unenlightened. The Land is the true home of Pendragon. It is known by many names’The Confederates, the Association’easiest if we just use the Land though. He was born there, son of the last good king. As such, even though I had him raised in your world, he will always feel a pull and even a longing for the Land. It is where he truly belongs. If you fail to see, the man is a bit out of his element with the rest of your time. To answer the how, it is simple. Magic?” Merlin smiles into the camera, a look of slight astonishment on his face?. “I realize some of you may scoff, but never underestimate the power of magic. Magic is in all things, though some things are more magical than others. The Pendragon name was always full of magic, even if they were not extreme wielders of the stuff. Venneman is no different. The power of the white uses him as it will. He is a type of good energy conduit. Where things are blackest, that is where he will go. I must admit, I do play a bit of a part in the scheme of things. I have the power to send him to the land and retrieve him to here should I feel the need to do so. This particular story is because I felt he needed to be there’I will get to that in a moment though. I will answer you one more question before we get on with this tale of high adventure. The question” Why do some people seem as though they are familiar? Easiest question of the bunch. Some people exist on two places, or three or more. These people are multifaced. Same soul, different lives’Linked through the soul. What one experiences will almost mirror in another world, in all worlds where he exists. Pendragon, however, is singular in nature.” Merlin nods with the simplicity of his explanations. He looks around and waves his hand slowly before the camera?. “I will now take you to the heart of a civil war. The upstart, Broadbane, commands his forces to usurp the throne that Pendragon should rightly sit”.” **************FLASH****************** Overlooking a smallish village, two horses sit, their riders sitting tall in the saddle. Pendragon and the Lord Benjamin. Pendragon uses a telescope, fashioned of leather and glass to look at the village below. He shakes his head? “Perhaps fifty of the enemy hold the town. There are bodies piled in the village square’Men, women, children. Despicable?” All through the speech, Benjamin’s gauntleted hands have tightened upon the reigns of his mount. “A small company is all we need. Perhaps twenty horse. Sweep down, go in fast, kill the enemy. It would free the town, and then we still shan’t show Broadbane our full power. Afterwards, we can move some infantry in to guard the town’Perhaps teach the survivors how to defend themselves.” Benjamin nods. For all his talk of protecting the integrity of the army, he doesn’t like to see innocents slaughtered either. With a jerk, the two men reign their animals and disappear down the other side of the hill?. ***************FLASH***************** Merlin sits, munching on a bit of popcorn from a bowl. He smiles as the shot refocuses?.“Hello again’Just me. Not meaning to interrupt, but I wanted to share a bit more background with you fine folks. Why would Pendragon only take twenty men to take on fifty” With a sword, none can touch the man. His speed and strength are unequalled. One on one, he is nearly unstoppable. Put him on a horse, and he is invincible. He is protecting the majority of his men with the belief that the men he takes will fight as five men apiece. Pendragon fights as twenty. Notice how Benjamin offered no resistance to such a paltry number of horse? He knows. Oh bother’I guess I need to throw a plug in for Venneman’s passion’GW. Weapons. Sword? Mace? Anything that would have a medieval twist, Pendragon has mastered. Juri speaks of domination, yet mine Lord has never made mention of that to me. In fact, he is appalled that GW has forced him into a weapons match with a woman. He is lenient, and though he will take the win, he will spare her serious injury’Poor lass’Anyhow, enough of that garbage. Let’s get back to the show!!” ************FLASH**************** Pendragon sits his horse at the head of an armored column of twenty. With the look of seasoned fighters, the men stare at their leader, awaiting his word of command. Pendragon paces Xerxes back and forth, looking proudly at the men who have flocked to his banner’The banner of the true king of The Land. In a bellowing voice, tinged with emotion, he speaks? “Never have I led a more deserving bunch of men! Into the mouth of the enemy we ride, to wrest innocents from being gobbled up by evil’We strike hard, we strike fast. When we clear the village, we bring down the reinforcements. Broadbane plays chess with his men, sacrificing here and there. I will sacrifice none! I plan to bring all of thee back with me, and if thou hast fallen, I shalt drag thy lifeless body home, so that proper honor may be done by thee. None stay behind”.” Pendragon bows his head and places his fist upon his heart. The men do the same, taking in a moment of silence. Finally, Pendragon claps on his golden helmet and turns from the men. “Yonder road leads into the heart of town. There are sure to be archers, so shield thyselves well, mine friends’Follow me to glory”..NOW!!!” The column heads off at a gallop, weapons drawn, heading straight for the town. Pendragon leads the charge, and as they round a bend in the road, the arrows start to come in. They think into shields, but none find flesh. Pendragon is a golden blur with his sword as they reach the gates and crash through. Two heads roll’The unlucky ones left to guard the gate. The men spit at the corpses, but follow their leader into the village. They meet the main group of the enemy on the village green, forty five hardy pikemen? “Surrender. To die this way is a useless death’Broadbane knows not what he does with thee”.” Pendragon says in solemn tones?“Why waste thy lives for the service of a man like that?” The pikemen only stare, his words having no effect. One of them launches his spear and takes down a cavalryman near the back of the column. With a roar, Pendragon is off his horse and into the midst of the pikemen. His blade whirls as men fall dead at his feet. The rest of the column has dismounted and they wade into battle, furious at the way the truce was broken. Bodies fall here and there until the only men standing are Pendragon and his men. Benjamin stands with the King, who wipes the blood from his blade on the corpse of one of the enemy soldiers. “My Lord’This was far too easy! Who did Broadbane expect to stop with that lot?” Benjamin asks in a disturbed tone’Pendragon looks thoughtfully at the horizon and shakes his head. His eyes perk up as if he has heard something? “Damnit’Thou art the bearer of ill tidings I fear, Benjamin. Remount!!! Something this way cometh! I know the sound of it not!!!!” As the men scramble to get back onto their horses, the sound grows clearer’It is the sound of a thousand marching boots. Heavy they sound, and not to be trifled with. With a shout, Pendragon races to escape the village with his men. What they find is not a pretty sight. A wall of’Men? No’they are not men. Their bodies are huge, and each looks as if he is half beast, half man. Some have horns, others hooves. They all share the same hungry look though, and Xerxes whinnies nervously? “What are they?” Pendragon asks the question of Benjamin, who sits open mouthed, staring at the beasts?. “Fangspawn. But, they cannot be real! There have been none since the last great war! Two hundred years ago at least! Half man, half beast. They are cunning and cruel. They kill to kill?” Benjamin shudders as the eyes of the beasts stare? “Broadbane has grown strong indeed’We need a hole in their line. We are not fated to end like this, in this place”.” With that, he draws his sword and races into the mass of Fangspawn. Surprised by his ferocity, the Fangspawn are caught off guard. Twenty have fallen as the column has almost neared it’s escape before they respond and fall upon the terribly outnumbered men. Pendragon is unhorsed, as is Benjamin, and soon they are the only two visible amongst the ruin of bodies, both human and fangspawn. Pendragon wields his sword and a mace that he has taken from one of the beasts. Heads roll and bones snap, but none can get a shot on Pendragon. Benjamin bleeds from a dozen wounds, and only keeps his feet because he leans back to back with Pendragon. With a scream, two Fangspawn are trampled beneath the hooves of Xerxes, and Pendragon grabs the wounded Lord Benjamin and soon they are flying on the back of Xerxes. Fleetest of horses, the Fangspawn have no chance to catch up?. **********FLASH************ Benjamin lies on a sheet of white, his wounds bandaged. Pendragon sits beside him, still in his armor. His eyes burn with rage?“Those things!!! They are an affront to good! They are pure evil’I underestimated this Broadbane, and I have been proud. Mine men’Eighteen fell and were presumably eaten by those monsters’I broke mine word. The bastards made me break mine word’They shalt rue the day?”..” *********FLASH************* “Hello again!” Merlin smiles as the shot is once more on him. He stands this time, and casts a kindly look? “That is all the time I have for you today. This story is far from done, however. The chronicler will return, and with me, more tales’Perhaps even some of glory rather than defeat. Pendragon may have broken his word, but I believe his vengeance will be something that songs may be written of”..” FADE ~TO BE CONTINUED~
