The invitation

14 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/14/04 'Hello, Donny?' 'Brick?' 'Yeah. I called to see how you were doing after Monday night.' 'I'm gonna be fine. It'll take more than that punk ass Train and whoever the hell was helping him to keep me down.' 'Donny, you were almost crushed in a garbage truck.' 'And your point is?' Earlier that Monday night, Brick and Donny had been having a conversation about Train, a constant rival of both men. Both voiced rather negative opinions of him, but Donny, especially. Unbeknownst to them, the Platinum champion heard every word they said. Later that evening, Train ambushed Donny and threw him into a garbage truck, driven by a mysterious accomplice. 'You gonna be okay for our match, man? I mean, we got Manifest Destiny and the Elite to deal with. Plus, this is our chance at the Tag Titles. Not to mention, we get the chance to show up Train and Juan Gonzales.' 'Oh, you know I'm gonna be ready.' 'Well, listen, I wanted to know if you wanted to come have Sunday dinner with my family, sometime.' 'Sunday dinner?' 'Yeah. It'd be good to have a friend from work meet my family.' 'You want me to meet the family? Oh, this--this is all so...so sudden!' 'Man, yes or no?' 'Yeah, sure, I'd love to.' 'One thing, though, Donny. No alcohol.' 'What?' 'I mean it, man. My mom does not allow alcohol in her house. No liquor, no beer.' 'I can manage, Brick,' said Donny. 'I'm not always drinking.' 'OK. I wasn't trying to insult you. I was just making sure you understood.' 'I understand. Say, can Alexis come?' Brick went silent for a bit. Donny had been dating former Evolution commentator Alexis Steele for months now. Truth be told, Alexis would never be mistaken for a member of Mensa. 'Brick? Brick, you still there?' 'Huh? Oh, yeah, sure she can come.' 'You took your sweet damn time answering me.' 'Huh?' Just then, Brick heard a beep. 'Hey, hold on, Donny. I got another call.' Brick then clicked over. 'Hello?' 'Jamal?' 'Hey, Ma, how are you?' 'I'm fine. I called to see how you were doing. I also wanted to talk with you about Monday night, and the stunt you pulled.' Brick sighed. He knew this was coming. His mother had often voiced her disapproval over the actions he had been taking as of late, concerning Deacon Holmes and Rhaiza. This past Monday, Brick had raised the drama level by playing an audio tape that depicted what sounded like Brick and a woman who sounded very similar to Rhaiza engaging in sexual activity. 'Listen, Ma, I already know what you're gonna say.' 'Jamal James.' 'Sorry, Ma, but I already know...' 'As much as I hate repeating myself, playing this game is only going to cause pain for everyone involved, yourself included.' 'Ma, I know what I'm doing.' 'I don't think you do. I don't think you know the full repercussions your actions might bring.' 'Whatever they are, I'll deal with them. Look, Ma, I hope you don't mind, but I just invited someone from work to have Sunday dinner with the family.' 'Oh, don't change the subject or anything. I gather it wasn't Train or Deacon Holmes.' 'Hell no!' 'Jamal!' 'Sorry. It's one of my partners, Donny McNasty. In fact, he's on the other line, right now.' 'The drunk?' 'He's not a drunk, Ma. And I trust him around y'all more than I trusted Train.' 'Train was on good behavior that night.' 'Ma, he was hitting on Monica almost the whole night. Right in front o' me an' Kyle. At least I know Donny won't pull the same crap.' 'And how can you be so sure?' 'Well, for one thing, he's already got a lady. In fact, he wanted to know if he could bring her along.' 'I suppose...' 'Thanks, Ma. I'll tell him.' 'Jamal, please, be careful.' 'I will, Ma.' 'OK, God be with you.' 'You, too.' Brick then clicked back to Donny. 'Yeah, Donny, that was my mother. I asked her about Sunday dinner. She said it was ok for you and Alexis to come over. Oh, and tell Alexis she might want to wear something a bit more...modest.' 'I hear you. OK, I'll talk to you later.' 'Okay.' Then Brick hung up and picked up his copy of the Luke Cage screenplay. Just then, the phone rang. 'Hello. Oh. Wasn't expecting to hear from you.'
