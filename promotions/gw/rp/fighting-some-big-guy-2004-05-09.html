Fighting some big guy.

9 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 9 The scene opens up to a rusty old wrestling ring. The steel steps all a black crap color, the ring posts a dusty brown. Inside the ring is Roswell and Phil (who is the owner of the training centre) who are waiting for the bell to be rung. Roswell doesn’t really wait and locks with Phil, Phil throws Roswell at the ropes and hit’s a butt bump. Phil brings Roswell up and hit’s another butt bump taking Roswell off his feet again. Roswell gets to his feet only to be met with a sleeper hold drop from Phil. Phil set’s Roswell up on the ground and hits a leg drop. Phil walks to the corner not watching Roswell and taunts him, Roswell who is laying on the ground uses his 211 body to flip up to his feet and knocks Phil with a flying elbow to the head. 1?”. 2?”.. 3’No! Roswell picks Phil up and hit’s a running knee strike to his abs taking him down. Roswell starts climbing the top ropes quickly and jumps off with a flying body press. Roswell lands on Phil and starts hitting lefts and rights. Roswell gets off Phil and picks him up again but Phil smacks Roswell with a hard low-blow! Roswell falls to the ground. Phil starts climbing the top rope and jumps off with all 356 pounds onto Roswell with a body press. 1?” 2?” 3?.NO! Phil gets angry and lifts Roswell up again and hits a huge powerbomb but it is reveresed into a huricarana by Roswell! Roswell quickly gets up and hits Phil with a big boot, and then a leg drop. Roswell lifts Phil up and kicks him in the gut and hits a huge sit-out powerbomb known as The System Crash! 1?”.. 2?”.. 3?”. DING DING DING Roswell gets up and starts cheering for himself. Roswell slides out of the ring and back through the black dusty curtain as Phil stays in the ring embarrassed from Roswell. Roswell is then seen taking his back and walking out of the building. On the street Roswell asks for a taxi and gets one. Roswell opens the taxi door and hops in. The taxi speeds down the street to Roswell’s hotel. Roswell pays the taxi driver and gets out of the car. Roswell notices that there is a line of police cars and ambulances. Roswell looks confused and walks through the hotel doors. Roswell sees there is a stretcher going into the elevator. Roswell walks over to the counter and asks for a lady. “What’s happening here?” asks Roswell. “I have no clue, you better go check it out” says the lady at the counter. Roswell rushes to the elevator and hits the button, after a few seconds Roswell walks into the elevator and pushes floor 5. Roswell then walks out and sees that the hotel room beside his is open. “That is Father Clark’s room” says Roswell to his self. Roswell walks over to Father Clark’s room and goes in. Roswell sees that Father is laying on the stretcher unconcisous. Roswell walks over to the ambulance guy. “What happened, what is going on!” yells Roswell. “He had a heart attack” tells the ambulance guy. Roswell starts crying and helps them get the stretcher into the elevator and walks out of the building all sad. Roswell helps them get the stretcher onto the ambulance and walks back inside the hotel. Scene Ends.
