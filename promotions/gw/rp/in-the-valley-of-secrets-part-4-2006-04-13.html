In the Valley of Secrets (part 4)

13 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 13th, 2006 Doctors and hospitals were foreign to Eryc; he had never been in one prior to the day before yesterday. He could see why so many people hated them because of all the poking and prodding these places do to a person. He decided that he wasn’t going to stay there another minute longer than he had to. All of his vitals looked good and he wasn’t showing any signs of head trauma; he then badgered the Doctor who was overseeing his case to allow him to be released...his influence as a celebrity had some benefits to it every now and then. The only thing Eryc had to do was sign an autograph besides his release form and he was free to go. “Hon, I think we should go back to Michigan and you can move your things in with me...if we’re going to go the next step we might as well right?” Her voice was a little hesitant but he didn’t really catch on. It was bold step to ask him to stay with her at her apartment so that she could watch over him but it didn’t sound like too bad of an idea. Besides, if he needed professional care he could hire someone that was qualified to take care of his health issues or go to a hospital in the states (though he would probably receive better care in Canada he figured). “I still have some time left on my lease for my penthouse in New York,” he quipped, “but we can go there for a getaway if we feel like it, right?” Krissy nodded in agreement even if in the back of her mind thought he was making a mistake by leaving the hospital so soon or that she might have been rushing things a little too quickly. The doctor chimed in with more instructions for Eryc now that his release was official. “I would advise you,” the doctor stated “to not fly in any planes for a little while because with sustaining head injuries such as the ones you have...it wouldn’t be a good idea.” Eryc looked at him and sighed...there went his way back home now; he would have to think of an alternate way of getting back to Michigan to Krissy’s apartment. Of course this meant that he probably should ask the all important question that now surfaced in his mind. “Will I be able to work?” He said as he looked the doctor square in the eyes. It wasn’t that he needed to work to make money because he didn’t have to lift a finger the rest of his life if he didn’t want to but he was humble and enjoyed either being up on that stage pouring his heart out to a small number of people (or millions) through music or getting the crap kicked out of him in the octagon for Global. “Well...” he said trying to look and sound optimistic, “I wouldn’t advise you to put on a concert for a while until you are fully healed up which might take some time.” The doctor stroked his chin a little bit. “I think you might want to refrain from any fights as well...if you sustain a nasty blow to the head it could possibly make things worse for you long term.” Eryc shook his head to show he understood but it didn’t mean he liked the prognosis. If only he were still a vampire right? There wouldn’t have been a need for this conversation nor would he have been in this predicament in the first place. “I’ll leave the two of you to finish getting your things together, Eryc.” The Doc started to make his way towards the door and turned to look back. “I’ll have a prescription for some pain meds waiting for you when you leave, just see my nurse.” Eryc looked at Krissy and then back at the doctor. “Pain medication for what?” His inquiry had a tinge of curiosity to it. The doctor had never met a paid fighter who knew so little about prescription pain medication. Not many athletes (in this day and age) were without a huge supply of the little white miracles these days (and no wonder so many people were addicted to them and dying as a result). “It’s just for the potential headaches you might have, Mr. Morgan.” He pulled his pen off the clipboard it was attached to and spoke again as he started writing. “I’m surprised that you are not in a great deal more pain to be honest with you.” Eryc shrugged and smiled as he replied. “I guess I’ve just built up a high tolerance for pain...goes with the territory.” Even his own words sickened him as he pandered to the doctor. “I wish you well, Mr. Morgan” he stated in a matter of fact way, “but I do hope once you get back to the states that you see your normal physician so your condition can be monitored.” Eryc nodded sagely. “I’ll do that,” he paused for a moment before continuing, “thank you doctor for all you’ve done.” The doctor bowed his head and then stepped out of the room and with him left a silence between Eryc and his new fianc’e. Krissy went to the closet to bring out the clothes she brought for him so he could put them on as she sensed his urgency to leave. “You heard everything he said right?” She commented, breaking the silence. “He told you to relax and I’m going to see that is what you do too.” Not even twenty four hours of being engaged and he suddenly felt like he had a mother or at least the concept of what a mother in these modern times might be; surely if it were in the times he grew up in there wouldn’t be as much concern but at the same time he could have been dead much easier too. “That leaves us stuck between a rock and a hard place then.” He uttered with a sour expression. Krissy helped him with his socks even though she figured he’d tell her that he didn’t need assistance, he seemed to struggle a little bit which had already been taken into consideration by her. “How do you figure?” She pondered out loud. “Considering that we flew to get here in the first place,” he broke his sentence to pull on his shirt, “we’ll have a hard time trying to get back home when I can’t fly.” She chuckled as she grabbed a hold of his little toe and wiggled it trying to lighten up his mood. “That’s easy dear. We’ll just rent a car and drive back.” She made it sound so simple. “I can do all of the driving and that way you won’t have to because I think that driving would not be a good idea for you.” Eryc wanted to object but it seemed like he didn’t have much choice in the matter. He felt fine but that could be because he was taking it easy and not doing anything too strenuous and driving a vehicle might be a little more than he could handle at the moment anyway. “You shouldn’t do all that driving yourself...” he grumbled as he sat up to pull his pants on, “it is a long trip from here back to Detroit.” Krissy didn’t seem to think so nor did she agree with him about it. “I do it all the time,” she waved a dismissive hand, “and I usually can make good time so we could be back before we know it.” Krissy moved closer and lightly kissed him. “You can sleep the entire way if you want, how does that sound?” The idea itself sounded fine to him because he did feel pretty tired still and stretching out would work well for him. Maybe sleeping through the trip would be best for him to try and erase the horrible events of the last few days. “I don’t care...let’s just get out of here.” He didn’t mean to sound short but the faster he was out on the road and closer to someplace resembling home...the better. “All of our things are packed up then, right?” Krissy had pulled out a bag from the closet with his things in them like his sunglasses and had put his wallet in his coat so that it wouldn’t get lost as easily. She held (the bag) up to show him that she had everything that belonged to him. “I’m going to go find the nurse and get that prescription while you are getting your shoes and coat on.” She could see that as he put his shoes on he was a little unsteady but there was a fix for that problem. “I brought a cane for you to help you walk; it belonged to my Grandfather.” Eryc hoped that it wasn’t one of those really clich? old people cane’s, he was pretty vain sometimes and didn’t want to look like a cripple to anyone because it could potentially threaten any projects he might be sent to do. “Just lean it against the bed,” he motioned his hand towards the end of the bed, “I’ll find you when I finish getting ready.” Krissy did as he asked and leaned it against the bed. The cane was made out of a very tough metal (possibly steel) and had a fashionable lion’s head handle that looked pretty fierce, which was not as bad as he anticipated it being. Her Grandfather might have been a pretty cool guy but it sounded like he’d never get the chance to meet him. She left the room and he slowly put his shoes on and threw on his leather jacket that he brought with him as an extra in case his other jacket became dirty or if they decided to go somewhere that would require him to dress more than casually. “The doc wasn’t kidding,” he rubbed his forehead which felt like a hammer was slamming against it “my head feels like it’s being hit with a wrecking ball.” He walked out of the room and used the cane to aid him in finding Krissy who was talking to the nurse who was giving her a few helpful ideas on how to best take care of him on her own. As he found her, he told her that he was heading towards the lobby and that he’d meet her down there. Krissy followed not too far behind and told him to wait while she went to get the car and bring it around. The plan was for them to go get their things at her folks cabin and say the proper “see you soon” before shuffling off back to the states. Her parents would most likely drive them to the rental car place to make things easier for them. It was a good idea that Krissy chose to handle the task of driving the entire way back because with the way he was feeling at the moment, Eryc would be lucky if he made it back home in one piece. His stupidity for not staying in the hospital longer might be his downfall but he didn’t want to rely on people he couldn’t trust. (Who trusts hospitals these days?) The last thing on his mind was getting back to work even though he knew that he’d have to get in touch with his manager/agent sooner or later before she dropped him as a client...which could be good (because he secretly had a thing for her) or bad (because she could potentially land him great projects). Could things possibly get any worse? ---- The only time during the drive back that Eryc was awake was when Krissy woke him up to get a small bite to eat at one of the gas stations just past the Canadian border. He had taken some of the pills the doctor gave him and it knocked him out mere moments after they were on the open road. He barely was able to get out of the “04 dark blue Ford Freestar they had rented from Hertz to get through the door of Krissy’s apartment building. She left their things in the car to get him inside before going back out to get their stuff. A neighbor friend of hers helped her with the luggage after seeing how sluggish Eryc was upon getting inside the apartment and going straight to bed. He was back in slumber land as soon as his head hit the pillow. Eryc’s eyes opened and slowly adjusted to the light and he looked over to the digital clock on the night stand next to him and read that the time was somewhere near 2 in the morning. He looked over beside him and Krissy was sound asleep. His stomach rumbled slightly as he slipped out of bed without disturbing his lady and went to the kitchen to find something small to eat because his appetite was still very limited. Crackers and tea would be good enough to work with. He felt better after all the resting he’s done but still wasn’t anywhere near one hundred percent; his head still felt like a car in a compactor at the junkyard but it was lessening as time went on. Eryc found some Saltine crackers in the cupboard and took them along with the cup of hot water he boiled for the tea into the living room with him and set them down on the coffee table before he went off in search of his bag...it had been far too long since he checked his phone(s) or his mail. His personal phone didn’t have any messages on it because hardly anyone even had that number but his work phone had at least thirty missed calls and at least that many voice messages. He accessed his voice mail to listen to some of the messages...the first of which was from his brother from just last week. Eryc... it’s been six months since you disappeared from Las Vegas; where have you been” I’ve heard little bits and pieces about you doing some indy wrestling in Detroit or somewhere around there but other than that you’ve completely fallen off the radar. What’s going on with you man? There’s been talk that you aren’t one of us anymore and if that’s true...I don’t know what to say but if something is going on then we should talk about it because we’re brothers...well whenever you feel like giving me a call you know where to find me. Stop being an asshole...later bro. An asshole? Eryc thought he was doing Ash a favor by not returning his calls just for the purpose of knowing how he’d be treated when it was found out that he was among the living again. He knew how kindred felt about mortals and the way they viewed them. They would not be brothers, at least not now...they would be strangers. The next call was from his agent/manager extraordinaire, Jane; she was worried about him not returning her calls in the last week or so as well with some potential hot projects being in the works specifically for him. Eryc, where the heck are you? I’ve been trying to reach you forever...what good is a cell phone if you don’t answer the darn thing? Listen, I’ve been getting lots of incoming things that people are wanting you for and if I can’t get a confirmation on your interest then they might fall apart and let me tell you...some of these are not a good idea to turn down. I’ve got this guy from this club...The Elements I think it is. He’s called once looking to book your band for a show and once I get more info on that I’ll call you back but you seriously need to get a hold of me ASAP! By the way...have I heard right that you’ve got a girlfriend now? When did this happen? Talk soon...ciao! The prospect of him playing a club anytime soon was pretty much out the window because he didn’t know how the loudness of the music would affect his head and with not having any contact with Ash that’s half the band right there so unless he found someone to replace him he would be out of luck. Eryc had heard the name of the club before but could not remember exactly where right away but after concentrating a little more he remember it had something to do with Global or one of the guys in it that had ties to the club. Ken Partridge Jr. that was who had an affiliation with the club...in fact he was co-owner of it with some other person in which in turn owned Nameless Records, a record label he’d kept tabs on for a while just out of curiosity. He saved the message for later use and went on through the next few which were just Jane trying to grab his attention and for him to call her which was nothing new. At the end of his voice mails he received a call which was rather surprising to him...it was from Global, whom he had parted ways with at about the same time he went AWOL from his hectic life to deal with the situations caused by his reanimation. Eryc, this is Brandi Sights from Global...I’ve been reviewing your file here with Bubba McCoy and if you would be interested we would like to meet with you regarding a possible return to our promotion. We know that you have been upset with how your career has gone so far but we have faith in your talents and would like to see a partnership between you and our company once again. If you are interested, give us a call and we’ll set up a meeting and see what can’t be done to change your mind about working for us. Eryc saved the message but did not have any intentions of calling them back. He remembered why he left and it was enough for him to stick to his guns and continue to work independent ventures or stop completely. His body could use a break he figured...he’d been beaten and battered enough both physically and mentally in the 6 months he spent working there and it would take a miracle for him to change his mind. He retrieved his laptop out of his bag just to see what kind of news and rumors were being published about him and or news concerning Global in case he did decide to take a crack at whatever they might offer. To his surprise the first thing he saw on the site was the notice that a handful of Global’s top stars were making an exit. Emoticon, Virgil Ambrosi, Dawn McFarlane, Vivica Valentine, Shadow, and Christian Zenith all gave their notices of their departure which to Eryc smelled fishy but it wasn’t something he was all that shocked over; it made his interests in returning a little higher now. He was never able to prove it but during his time there he always felt that he was being overlooked by the championship committee and other people who had come in after him were getting opportunities that he was putting in hard work for and not receiving. Some of those leaving could have possibly had something to do with that but like the JFK assassination...there are things nobody will ever know the real truth of no matter how hard people might dig for an answer. After reading the news, he suddenly wasn’t interested in potential gossip that might be spreading around the web...he was thinking about taking care of some unfinished business. Jake Hix was the focus of this particular piece of business as it related to their trip to New Orleans and his “changes”. Maybe Hix knew something more than he was telling or could find out what exactly it was that his brothers goons did to him and if there was a possibility it would reverse itself. It was a bit too much for him to think about this late at night...and he should probably get back to bed and get more much needed rest. His body probably needed it after the accident and overdoing anything might not be the best idea. Eryc shut down the laptop and closed the case and would put everything away when he woke up again (including the empty cup that was used for tea). His feet shuffled towards the bedroom and he used his hand to steady himself but knocked something over that was on the lamp table...a closer look would reveal to him that it was Krissy’s purse. A few of the contents had dropped out onto the floor which he picked up and returned to their rightful place but one of the items caught his attention: it appeared to be a wallet of some kind. It was never his intention to look through her things without asking her but this item had his curiosity just because it didn’t look like a normal women’s wallet that they would normally have in their purses. Casually Eryc opened the wallet and the expression on his face would give anyone the impression that he was disemboweled and somewhere deep inside he could hear the devious laughter of Corvus echoing through his head. He was too weak to pull his things together at the moment because he wanted to leave but he’d have to wait it out and this new discovery made him feel ill again. He put the wallet back into the purse and returned it where he found it before knocking it over and then stumbled back to the couch where he passes out. ---- Morning had arrived and the kink in Eryc’s neck from sleeping on the couch was the driving force in waking him out of his slumber. He was still pretty weak and even though he discovered a secret his fianc’e was hiding from him he could not will himself to stay awake to confront her. He rubbed his eyes and looked around; his eyes and ears telling him that the apartment was empty and that he was alone. The note that was fixed to his laptop gave him his answer. Eryc, I went to the university real quick to make sure that my classes were registered right and to see what kind of books I’ll need. I’ll be back soon so we can spend some time together if you’re up by then. Love you, Krissy “Yeah right,” he muttered to himself, “I bet you are.” His trust in anything she said right now was lacking because of his findings a few hours earlier and as soon as she returned they would have a discussion on what he found and why she was lying to him about who she really is. Eryc was mad but he couldn’t just lose it right there and start breaking things...he needed an outlet. That outlet looked to be in the form of Global in which he pulled out his phone and dialed the number Brandi Sights left for him in case he was interested in working for them again. Brandi’s receptionist picked up the call. “This is Eryc Morgan, I’m returning a call that Brandi made to me recently.” He was cool, calm, and collected and ready for just about anything to take place during the conversation. Would he let his past discrepancies with Global get in the way of the future? He didn’t know really since it all depended on Miss Sights. “I’ve been expecting your call, Mr. Morgan.” Brandi spoke as she picked up the call. “Bubba (McCoy) and I were not sure that you would get back to us.” Eryc waited a few seconds before he responded; the first thing on his mind wouldn’t have won him any awards so he thought he should go the safe route. “I was a little under the weather but all is well now.” He didn’t want to reveal that he had almost died as it might make them change their mind on giving him another shot if that is what they were interested in doing. “What can I do for you, Miss Sights?” There was a pause on the other end for a moment as it seemed the sounds of papers were being looked over came through to his end. “As I had mentioned in my previous messages to you, Bubba McCoy and I are interested in giving you another chance with Global if you were interested?” He didn’t want to sound too eager and jump at the chance to rejoin them to prove his worth once and for all to the Global crowd and to those who seemed to not think he could be anything special. “I’ve given it some thought since I received your voice mails” Eryc broke his thought for a second but quickly came back to it. “I’m interested to see what Global has to offer me.” Brandi was pleasantly surprised that the line of communication was going as smoothly as they were. The last time he was involved with them it was a very bitter exit, however, time has a way of mending things that are broken. “Global is going to be in Japan for our Showdown in Tokyo show in a few days” she informed him. “Do you think you would be able to meet there to discuss business with Bubba and myself?” They sure seemed to want him pretty badly but the overlaying thought that he could not fly with his head still a bit screwed up from his accident could be a factor in screwing things up. On the other hand he didn’t have anything to lose because after Krissy returned he wouldn’t be staying there after they spoke so if he was going to take a risk he might as well. “Sure, I can manage it,” He said, “just tell me where you want to meet and I will be there.” Brandi’s tone seemed to be joyous as she listened to him agree to meet with them in Japan to discuss contract negotiations. They were looking to rebuild Global after the defection of several key players at the moment and Eryc was a good example of someone with commitment and heart. “You can meet us at the arena and there will be security clearance for you and also some tickets for the show if you choose to stay after we’ve finished talking.” Eryc had a feeling of satisfaction from the talk with Brandi. It seemed that amidst the problems he was having in his personal life that his professional life was about to take a different course...a better one. “I’ll be there and I look forward to talking with you.” He added, “Give my regards to Mr. McCoy when you see him next and that I will speak with him (and you) soon.” Eryc ended the call after he exchanged pleasant goodbyes with Miss Sights. He put his phone back into his bag and started to put his things together so that he could leave just as soon as he had his conversation with Krissy. Much to his surprise he didn’t have to wait long as he heard the key turn in the door and it opened to the sight of Krissy walking inside with her purse hung over her shoulder and a smile on her face because he was awake. “You must have been too weak to get back to bed last night,” She said as she put her purse down on the table that he had seen it on last night, “are you feeling any better today?” He removed the power cord he was using to charge his laptop and also pulled the plug from the wall as he turned to give her his answer. “A little bit...” he said with hesitance, “I really need to go though.” Krissy looked at him confusedly. “You’re leaving?” was the only thing she could get out of her mouth at the moment. Eryc continued putting his things away as he waited for her to ask the right questions that were the reasons for him leaving so abruptly. “I have a flight to Tokyo to catch.” He grabbed a different shirt and pants from his bag and began changing. “A previous employer of mine wants to talk about my returning to work for them so I’m meeting them where they are going to be. None of it was making sense to her because here is the man she plans to marry not giving her any idea why he is leaving and being short with his answers and it scared her. “Eryc, talk to me...why are you leaving?” She remembered the doctor’s orders as he had earlier. “You can’t get on a plane! The doctor told you that you shouldn’t fly for at least a month.” He shrugged and kept going until she finally approached him and went up close to him, looking him in the eye. “Will you talk to me for Christ sake?” Her eyes pleaded with him. “Why are you acting like this...what happened?” Eryc turned his head away for a second because he knew that he would hate every moment of what was going to be said. “You’re lying to me is what happened.” His words were spoken in a disheartened manner as he threw on a knit hat and grabbed his jacket. Krissy stood flabbergasted not having the ability to speak for a moment and as Eryc started to walk past her she grabbed his arm to hold him in place so she could find out what was going on. “What did I lie to you about Eryc?” She raised an eyebrow. “I’ve been honest with you from day one.” Eryc shook his head as the image of what he found the night before pokes around in his mind and it took everything within him not to go completely apeshit on her but he at least had respect for her and what they had up until this point. “You told me that you were a college student,” he fought back his anger as he blurted out, “that doesn’t figure into you being a damn federal agent does it?” Krissy’s face turned whiter than a sheet as she could see his facial expressions. The hurt and confusion in his eyes needed some kind of reassurance that this was a bad dream except there wasn’t any. “The college girl thing was bullshit the whole time wasn’t it?” He tried to rationalize things more but it didn’t help. “It was a cover...but why?” Tears began to flow down Krissy’s cheeks as she moved over to the couch with Eryc’s eyes following her as she sat down to come clean about what was going on. “I’m sorry I lied to you Eryc but if I had told you in the beginning it would have compromised my job.” “What was your job, Elizabeth?” He proved to her that he had seen her badge by calling her by her real first name. The whole thing made her uncomfortable because she knew she was losing the best thing she had ever had in her life but that was the consequence of her actions. “My assignment was to watch you and keep an eye on you because my bosses had been given information by someone in Nevada about you potentially being a serial killer.” She used her sleeve to wipe away some of the tears. “I was told that you had taken your wrestler persona too far and was actually murdering people and making it appear as if a vampire did it...” Eryc exhaled deeply not believing what he was hearing and that the past five and a half months were based on lies. The woman he loved was nothing more than a fake. “I think by now you know that I’m not a murderer if you’ve been following me all this time.” He shook his head. “I think that the Las Vegas Police Department and Detective Van Buren bought into way too many horror movies because I didn’t have anything to do with whatever they said I did.” Krissy knew that he was a kind man and that he couldn’t have done what they were accusing of but she had to do her job and even that was going down the drain by blowing her cover (something she knew would eventually happen). “I was just doing my job...I never expected...” Eryc cut her off before she could say anything more because all it would do was make things more difficult. “You never expected that I’d find out is that what you were going to say?” His voice started rising higher with every word he spoke. “So what would have happened when you were pulled from your assignment” Just pack up and say “I’m sorry Eryc but we can’t be together anymore?” Is that it?” Even more streams flowed down her cheeks but she covered her face to keep it away from sight but he didn’t have to see to know what she was doing because he could hear the sniffles as she fought to speak. “No, that’s not what I meant at all.” Her arm was now smeared with black from her mascara. “I meant that I didn’t expect to fall in love with you and I knew that it would hurt you if you found out.” She sighed and tried to look up at him but she couldn’t. “I’m sorry...I didn’t want this to happen.” Eryc started to bite his tongue but let it go because he had to say what he was thinking as it was his only defense from the breaking that his heart was doing. “You were just afraid that if you blew your cover that I might try and kill you or something...but I wouldn’t do something like that because that is not me.” He grabbed his bags and began to move them towards the door. “I can’t believe that you took me to meet some fake people pretending to be your family and I almost died...that’s the lowest anyone can go.” Krissy was now the one putting up her defenses. “How dare you! I took you home with me to meet my real parents because my feelings were real all of it was real...” She stood up and wanted to move to him but couldn’t. “If you hate me I understand but I never mislead you with how I felt; leave if you want but even when you walk out that door I will still love you.” Eryc opened the door and turned to look at her and lowered his sunglasses to show her that his eyes were welling up from the hurt he was feeling. The best thing he had in a long time had just crushed his spirits. “You should have thought about that before you based a relationship on lies. I’m sorry but I have to go.” Eryc grabbed his things and left the apartment to meet the cab that he had called shortly before she came in. He needed to get as far away from there as he could and as fast as he could before he had an emotional breakdown. Krissy leaned against the door and looked at the ring on her finger that he had specially had designed and sent to them overnight and slid down and began to bawl her eyes out knowing that not only did she ruin a perfect relationship but put her job in jeopardy. “This is not the end. It is not even the beginning of the end. But, it is, perhaps, the end of the beginning.” - Winston Churchill As one chapter closes another one opens.
