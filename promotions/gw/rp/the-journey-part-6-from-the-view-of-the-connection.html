The Journey: Part 6/From the view of “The Connection”

Date: 11-06=04 A little past lunchtime Have you become used to this story yet darling? I hope so because it isn’t going to be ending anytime in the foreseeable future and for you the reader that should be a good thing right? Today was kind of a weird day for the “Old School Connection” in that Zack had gone out with Ami and Juri on a grocery shopping trip and Tina was off to the gym to keep her figure that Juri has found just so damn attractive as of late! So what could that leave Joshua to do? Well for starters Joshua had just finished cleaning up the table from the lunch that he had shared with the family that IS the “Old School Connection” and as he did the dishes he got a phone call from some lady friends of his named Lisa Matthews and Patti Lewis. Now Patti and Lisa had known Joshua for a good couple of years and its NO secret that Patti was madly in love with Joshua at one time and when she found out that Joshua stopped having an interest in the female gender that didn’t stop her from caring about him at all’matter of fact it strengthened their friendship because whenever someone went after Joshua about his preference Patti would be the first person to tell a person about themselves and consequently that person would leave Joshua alone for good! Lisa was the one of the two young ladies that knew about Joshua’s preference and when it came to dealing with the heartbreak she took it probably the hardest but mainly because she never thought that it would ever happen that Joshua would date men because for the longest time Joshua was against the idea of dating his own gender until he met a man whom you met in an earlier installment that went by the name of Brian. So Lisa and Patti had called Joshua to see if he could come on a little day trip with them and Joshua didn’t see anything wrong with it’after all he didn’t have any plans for the day since Zack was out with the girls and Tina was away so the time away from the house was going to do him some good! When Joshua met up with Lisa and Patti at Rockerfeller Center they were dressed to take Joshua out to a nice restaurant and Joshua made sure to either match or surpass them and when you come out in a tuxedo to go on a date with two of your favorite ladies that’s gotta say something for the kind of people that he hangs out with doesn’t it? While Joshua was out entertaining the his lady friends Zack was starting to feel the effects of a migraine headache that had been plaguing him since last night when the group was watching a movie in the big family area that they use to watch movies or just hang out together and when Zack passed out in Joshua’s lap he didn’t have the heart to tell anyone that he was sick because he didn’t want anyone to worry about it? Problem is people DO worry and people DO care when someone is feeling sick? Joshua had carried his lover up the stairs to the bedroom that they shared and while I will admit that it was no easy feat for Joshua to do such a thing it was worth it for one of the people that he cares about. As they entered the bedroom Zack awakened to find that he had been placed into his nightclothes and Joshua was sitting next to the bed that he was now tucked into. “Josh” Wha?” Was all the young man could get out when he felt Joshua’s fingers press onto his lips before Joshua began to speak softly to him “Shh sweetie you passed out when we were watching a movie with the girls tonight. Why didn’t you tell me you weren’t feeling well?” Joshua asked in a somewhat motherly tone that caught Zack slightly off guard but soon regained his composure enough to answer the question. “Oh honey I’m sorry. I just didn’t want to worry you or anyone else for that matter. I don’t have a history of these things but if one happens it hurts BAD!” Zack explained as he felt Joshua raise his head up to stick a second pillow underneath him to elevate him. “Well I hope you feel better hun because you don’t want to be sick when you get in the ring this Monday when we team up for the first time ever in GW do you?” Joshua asked and Zack gently shook his head “No” before he felt Joshua clasp his right hand with Zack left and kissed him softly on the lips before whispering in his ear “Good night dear.” and with that Joshua had left the room while Zack slowly drifted into a dreamlike slumber and Joshua could do nothing but smile at that point in time! Now back to the present day? Zack was at a grocery store with Ami and Juri and Zack was pushing the cart up and down the isles for the ladies and admittedly he still felt like shit but when the errands have to be ran Zack is always more than willing to go and help get them done and that’s not to say that Joshua wouldn’t do it but half the time he’s never up or he is busy with cleaning to get the outside errands done so Zack does it and he gets to spend time with Ami and Juri so it all works out in the long run right? As the trio was going through the list like wildfire Juri looked over like every 5 minutes at Zack to see if he was ok and Juri knew that he hadn’t recovered fully from the headache the night before but also they knew that Zack wouldn’t allow himself to be sent home in a taxi back to the residence cos again that just wasn’t his style and that is something that he has been openly criticized for numerous times as he knew he should risk his health like this! After a couple of hours Zack’s condition started improving slightly but Ami thought it would be best to go back home to get him situated so when Josh came home he could tend to his ailing lover and believe me Josh won’t be too terribly upset about it because he has almost become like a counselor to Zack at times! Later that night? Well the trip was over with the young ladies Joshua was on his way back home when his cell phone went off and of course it was Ami on the other end of the line. “Hello” Hey sis what’s up darling?” Joshua asked with a slight ring in his voice as he is always happy to talk to his younger sibling on the phone. “Oh nothing much. Zack went with us to do the errands today and unfortunately for us the headache that he is suffering from isn’t getting much better’matter of fact he almost threw up a couple of times with the headache just getting worse and worse.” Ami explained and as quickly as Joshua had a smile on his face it quickly turned to concern as he could fear the worst case scenario at this moment in time! “Ami I’ll be home shortly but keep Zack in bed if you can. DON’T let him get out of bed for any reason and I don’t care if its to take a dump understand”!” Joshua practically hollered and that got a good laugh out of the rest of the bus that he was on at that time. “Yes Joshua I understand completely and don’t you worry Juri and I will take care of him until you get back here.” Ami told Joshua as they both closed their ends of the conversation at the same time! After about another 25 minutes on the bus Joshua was back at the residence and when he got into the house he kissed his sister on the cheek and gave both Tina and Juri a kiss and a hug as well but understandably so his full attention was on Zack to see if he was ok! When Joshua got to their room at the top of the stairs he looked for Zack in the bed and he couldn’t find him. It turns out that darling Zack had rolled out of bed and didn’t even know it! Joshua bent down and picked up his lover’s body as carefully as possible and when he got him in the bed Josh made sure that he was tucked in so tight that he couldn’t move! After a quick 20 minute shower Josh got dressed into his pajamas and went back into the room with Zack’s sleeping frame still in the bed and as Joshua slowly caressed Zack’s face a smile couldn’t help but come across Josh’s face as he knew that he loved Zack and that was why he was always gonna worry like hell and why he just needed to be near Zack at this moment because if something happened to him what would either man do without the other? What COULD either man do without the other? Let’s pray that that day never comes and neither man ever has to find out! Au Revoir dearest ;)
