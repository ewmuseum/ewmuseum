Garh!

18 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18/1/06 Now. Justice Inc Headquarters. New York City. “I can’t believe it!” Warrior declared, angrily pushing the door of the Justice Inc living quarters open. “I just can’t believe it!” “I know,” Blind Rage responded, following Warrior further into their living quarters. He threw the keys of their bright orange Volkswagen people carrier on the heavily littered coffee table at the centre of the room and then slumped down, disheartened, onto their worn and torn sofa. “Damned pirates...again!” Warrior exclaimed, closing the door and then leaning against it. “We just can’t go anywhere without them...” Blind Rage muttered, more to himself than to anyone else. “Their the bane of our existence.” Then, quite mysteriously, the armchair at the other end of the room, slowly swivels around to reveal some sort of moron sat there grinning at them both. He is wearing a hat with a skull and crossbones on it, a diamond-studded eye patch and something green and fluffy that appeared to be glued to his shoulder. He eyes them both up silently, with a demented expression plastered across his face. Our two heroes simply glare at him, seemingly unphased and then glare at each other quizzically. “Who the hell let a Goth in here?” Warrior questioned. “A Goth?” Blind Rage responded, his face contorted in confusion so much, you could even see it through his mask. “What in the hell is a Goth?” “You know,” Warrior began. “Those guys who dress up all in black for attention. Kinda look like Vampires, but they walk around during the day. You know, we see them hanging around by the park all the time.” Blind Rage nodded, understanding. “Ah, yeah, I know what you’re talking about! But,” he paused to point at the guy sat in their armchair, “he’s not one of them!” Warrior rolled his eyes around in his head. “Of course he is! He’s so Goth he shits bats!” “Garh!” The man pitched in. “I be not one of...um...I’m not...huh...” Immediately, Blind Rage rose to his feet and pointed at the direction of the strange man in the chair. “He’s a pirate!” Warrior glared at Blind Rage and then rose, with equal quickness, with horror spread across his face like a liberally buttered bagel. “You’re right!” “...or at least he’s trying to be.” Blind Rage added. “Yeah,” the “pirate” began. “I’m not too good with the voice thing.” “He admits it!” Warrior declared. “Get him!” With that Warrior flings himself at the “pirate” sat in their armchair, sending it, and the both of them, toppling backward onto the hardwood flooring. Blind Rage simply stood there, glaring at the carnage before him, before making his way over to them and grabbing the “pirate” by the scruff of the neck, Blind Rage hauled him up into the air. The pirate struggled for a second, desperately thrusting out his arms and legs. Then, when he realised that resistance is futile, he stopped and simply glared at Rage. “Finished now?” Blind Rage questioned, the corner of his mouth curled up into a half smirk. “Just about...” the “pirate” admitted. “I wasn’t looking for a fight...honest!” Warrior grunted under his breath, delicately picking himself up from the floor. “Then why did you go all Blowfield on us?” Both Blind Rage and the “pirate” glared at Warrior confused. “What in the hell are you talkin” about?” Blind Rage asked him. Warrior sighed. “You should watch more movies, dude, seriously.” “I watch plenty of movies actually,” Blind Rage replied, defensively. “I mean,” Warrior began, “that aren’t firmly fixed within the superhero genre.” “Well, I...um...huh...don’t have much time these days...” Rage muttered and mumbled into response. “Blowfield was the guy from James Bond, the guy who swivelled around in his chair and stoked his cat...” Warrior explained. “Oh,” Rage said, “yeah I know what you mean now. Wait, wasn’t that Dr Evil...or something?” Rage replied. Warrior ran his hand over his face in sheer frustration. “Do you not understand the term “parody?”” “Anyway...” Blind Rage began, turning his attention back to the “pirate”. “If you aren’t here to fight, Cap’n Jack, then why are you here?” “I have a threatening message from the pirate community,” The pirate informed. Blind Rage and Warrior sighed in unison then replied, “Go on.” “We are outraged at your slanderous slurs about the pirate community on the Global television programme and we require you cease at once!” The pirate demanded. Blind Rage glared at him, then at Warrior, desperately attempting to hold back a laugh. “You aren’t in any position to make demands.” “And what happens when we don’t heed your words?” Warrior questioned. “There will be dire consequences!” The pirate declared. “Is that right?” Blind Rage asked, before hurling the pirate across the room into the opposing wall. The pirate bounced off the shabby plaster work and slumped to the floor in agony. “And yet, I don’t particularly care.” “Garh!” The pirate spluttered, scrambling to his feet and heading over to the door. “The pirate community will get you for this!” Warrior hurled a nearby shoe, catching the pirate square on the forehead, sending him sprawling backward through the open door and down the stairs. Blind Rage shook his head, staring intently at the armchair. “We’re so gonna have to wash this damned armchair! That filthy damn pirate has got his germs all over it! “I hate fuckin” pirates!” Warrior muttered.
