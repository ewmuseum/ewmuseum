The Return Home (Part 7)

8 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 8 The brothers moved through the doorway, being careful to step over the dead bodies that littered the front of the complex. They had not caused much of a ruckus and they had managed to sneak into the complex unnoticed. Their uncle Gohei followed them in right after. The brothers began to make their way forwards, their custom pistols extended in their capable hands. They moved slowly and quietly, not wanting to lose their element of surprise. The brothers continued onwards, checking for any open doorways or any signs of life. They found none and continued their trek down the long corridor. They could see the uneasiness on their uncle’s face, but they knew he had never been in this sort of situation before. He would not be in danger, they made sure to stay in front of him and planned on making sure everything was taken care of before he was put in a predicament, should things get sticky. They made their way to a small fork in the corridor. There were many possibilities they could go left, right or stay straight. “Well, what are you feeling?” whispered Jushin towards Ichiro. The uneasiness in Gohei’s stomach grew. He felt as if he did not belong here. “Guys, I don’t like this.” “Shhhh, be quiet or you’re going to have us noticed,” whispered Ichiro. Jushin’s ears perked as he heard the sound of footsteps creeping towards them. “Oh no, I think it’s too late.” The brothers and their uncle backed up, it was time for them to get ready, the fight would be upon them soon, there was nothing they could do. They waited and listened. “Hey, is that you Yoshi” You guys aren’t done for another half an hour, get back out there!” the guard began to yell. Jushin smiled, this would be easy prey, he was an unsuspecting guard. Ichiro nodded to Jushin, who immediately began to crawl towards the other side of the corridor. He pressed his back tightly against the wall, and peered out the side. His head was barely in view of the guard, then the pistol came flying around the corner, before he could even get his hand on the trigger, he felt the bullets pulsate into him. “What the fuck is going on out there”!” they heard the yells of confusion of many men. “Oh shit!” said Jushin. Ichiro and Gohei stood and quickly took a set of firing positions directed by Ichiro. Gohei facing down the left hallway, with his body covered in majority by the wall, Jushin held his position against the wall facing down the right hallway. Ichiro stood in the middle of the hallway, with very little protection, he faced straight down the corridor, should any guards come from there. Ichiro held his pistols tightly, keeping a steady aim down the hallway. That’s when the gunshots began to go off, the shots echoed through the entire complex. It would have alerted every guard in the entire complex of their presence. Jushin was firing off rapidly as a stream of men came out, and nearly all of them fell before the others could take cover. Jushin hid quickly behind the wall, and reloaded the clips quickly, before peering around the corner once again waiting for the guards to reemerge. On the other side Gohei was faring similarly well. The guards were not anticipating such a strong foe and many were overwhelmed. Ichiro sat and watched as no guards came from his direction, he was actually disappointed. Suddenly it seemed as if disaster sprung. From both of his partners Ichiro heard the words that he did not want to hear. From both corners of his eyes he saw Jushin and Gohei pull themselves back and both turn to him and yell in unison. “I’m out!” Ichiro frowned as he heard the advancing footsteps of the guards, completely oblivious to Ichiro’s presence, he decided that it was time to get into the action. He stepped forward with both of his guns pointed at opposite ends of the hallway. The shots rang out as the guards were taken off guard and all of them began to fall. As they fell into hiding for cover, Jushin and Gohei were once again ready, and stood up joining Ichiro in the middle of the corridor. Ichiro holstered his two custom pistols and pulled out a different pair of pistols. The three men stood united, each pressing their backs against one another. They waited for the remaining guards to muster their last ditch attempts at an attack. Soon it came, they met it head on. The blood spill was incredible, the death count unbelievable. Almost all of the guards had been finished off by the circle of death. When they were sure that all the guards had been killed they went through the bodies and checked all of them. However, they met a sad surprise, when one of the guards still had a slight bit of life. He snuck his gun to his waist and took aim, he fired a burst. Poorly aimed and inaccurate, the five shots managed to make their mark only once. Gohei screamed in pain and blood splattered from his bicep as the bullet entered. Quickly, the Souls of the Black Lotus turned back to the guard and he was pumped full of lead, driving the remaining life from his body. The Souls turned back to their uncle Gohei. He shook his head. He was gripping his arm tightly, trying to stop the bloodshed. “I’ll be fine, you guys just get up to the next floor and take care of that bitch Ishuzu!” he yelled. “We have to make sure you’re alright first!” Ichiro began to rip his own jacket off, as Jushin slowly assisted Gohei out of his business jacket. Ichiro began to rip the sleeve of his jacket off. He had easily just ripped off a thousand dollar jacket. But they did not care at this point. They quickly tied the bandage over the wound and made sure it was secure. Then they made sure their uncle would be alright on his own. They made sure he had his pistols ready should any other surprises occur. The two brothers ran out of sight leaving their uncle hoping for their safety in this next task. Gohei sat and watched as his nephews disappeared up the stairs. “Good bye Ishuzu you bitch. You took my brother’s life, because we were separated. There is no way you shall overcome the love of two brothers, for it is their strongest bond. One that not even you can break. You have manipulated, lied, cheated, stole and killed to build your empire, your kingdom in Tokyo. But now Ishuzu thy kingdom come, and thy empire fall.”
