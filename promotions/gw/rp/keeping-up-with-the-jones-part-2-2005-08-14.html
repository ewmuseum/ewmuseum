Keeping up with The Jones' Part 2

14 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sunday, August 14, 2005 Fred Grest always wondered if he did have what it takes. After a match against a competitor like Elia Smith you’d think he’d be on top of the world. Beating the person that destroyed him in his debut. However, it was simply a dark match so it was seen by no one and therefore met nothing. The people in attendance who cheered for me will remember how he dominated but that would be all. The general public eyes just viewed him as a comedy act which he doesn’t mind at all if it wasn’t just as a comedy act. The Extreme One was sitting alone on his black leather sofa. He was wearing a Marilyn Manson T-shirt and black jeans. After the Mike Jones incident and a dark match Grest doesn’t plan on getting up from his spot. He held the cordless phone and started to dial. He put the phone up to his ear as it rang. “Hello” he said as he was waiting for a response. He got his response. “What’s up Ashley?” A smirk came across his face . “Is your refrigerator running?” He hangs up the phone and mutters the word “Bitch”. We know why now that he is called The Original Pranksta. The long bright red haired Water Gun Bandit slowly gets off the sofa and heads towards the door. He turns the knob and opens it. He steps outside on a somewhat muggy day but not as bad as the past few days at least. He looks around the neighborhood he’s called up for the past few years and thanks the Lord above he’s gotten out of the area he used to be in. It was a down right hell hole especially his home. He knows he would not be in Global Wrestling had he stayed. If he stayed he would have never of wrestled a match. He wonders if following is dream was a wise decision. Although, at least he had the freedom to go the wrong way. The way is childhood dream has felt lately. It was just that?.. the wrong way. The door opened and Fred Grest turned his head up to see who was about to enter. He rarely kept anything locked. He wasn’t sure why he did this. He just did. The man entering was none other then Scott Vyper. He looks over at Grest. Vyper raises an eyebrow and asks “You won shouldn’t we have celebrated by now?” Fred Grest tilts his head and says “It was a dark match.” The blue haired freak folds his arms and replies. “A win is a win.” Fred Grest shrugs his shoulders “I guess you’re right. I could use an excuse to get out of the house.” “Lets go then man” Vyper responds. Fred Grest gets up slowly and cracks his back. He walks to the door at pretty much the same speed of an old man. Vyper is growing impatient and says in a fast tone. “Come on!” The Dark of Wrestling starts walking at a normal place. The two men head out the door and Grest closes and reaches into his pocket to pull out a key. He locks the door since he is leaving the house. Fred and Scott walk down the wooden steps. They join Chill and Samantha Foxs who are sitting inside Chill’s pimp mobile. Grest takes one look at the fuzzy dice and the wheel cover that just won’t stop spinning and shakes his head. “I think we should take my car.” he tells everyone. Scott Vyper immediately changes directions and heads towards my black Ford Thunderbird instead of Chill’s blue Ashton Martin which he has ruin. Samantha Foxs opens the car door and quickly gets out. She closes the door and follows Vyper’s lead and heads towards my car. Chill looks over at us in surprise “But this is an Ashton Martin” it puts his car to shame.” he points over to me after says that. Samantha Foxs turns around and looks at Chill and says “You put hydraulics on the car. I should kick your ass here and now for ruining that ride. “Hater” Chill mutters as he opens his door and gets outside. He sees Grest pulling out as he closes his door. He locks his expensive yet ruined car. He looks a bit worried about leaving the car out. Grest pulls up next to Chill and Samantha yells out “Get in white boy!” Chill shakes his head in disagreement. Vyper grabs him by the color and pulls him into the car through the window. You can hear Chill grunt trying to get away but he’s caught by surprise. Chill’s head bangs on Vyper’s knee. Chill grabs his head and says “Son of a bitch” in pain. Vyper bends over grabbing his knee as he squint his eyes in obvious pain as well. Fred Grest looks in his rear view mirror at them and shakes his head. “Buckle up you bastards.” Vyper pushes Chill into a sitting position and Chill’s head crashing into the window. “Argh!” The Blue One buckles up and looks over at Chill. “Buckle up you fucker.” Chill glares at Vyper as he buckles up. Grest sees this in the review mirror. “Hey, we don’t need Scott beating you again. You’re like 0 and 70 against him.” Chill’s eyes narrow at Grest. “Kinda like you and Syberus. Huh?” Grest raises an eyebrow as he keeps his eyes on the road ahead of him. “Touch”.” Grest pauses for a moment. “However, we both know Vyper is better then Sybie and by being able to beat him proves I’m better then Syberus regardless of my record.” Chill replies with a stern look on his face. “You beat him one time.” Grest smirks. “One more time then you.” Vyper looks curiously at both men. “You talk to me as if I’m a legend.” “Meanwhile I’m over here getting no attention.” Samantha replies as she does a hair toss. “Valley Girl” Chill mutters. “What was that Chill?” Samantha asks. “Where are we going to celebrate the big dark match win for yo yo over here?” Chill quickly says to change the subject. “This was Sam’s idea ask her.” Vyper replies. “I figure I’d leave that up to you guys.” Samantha says smirking. “Well, It looks like Grest has something in mind he’s been driving this whole time.” Vyper points out. “I have a stop to make.” Grest says narrowed eyed. Grest pulls into a parking lot of a motel. He parks the car and takes out the keys and puts them in his pocket. He opens the door to get out takes a look at his friends. “Stay here, I’ll be back in a few minutes.” His friends look puzzled and Samantha asks “Where are you going?” Grest has changed his attitude and looks very serious now. “To see a man I debuted with once.” Fred Grest turns around quickly and walks away. Samantha scratches her head more puzzled then anyone. “With that serious of a tone I doubt its that moron Torra. You guys have a clue of what’s going on here?” “Well if its from my short lived promotion it’s probably Adidax which would make sense if he was anywhere near here.” Chill says. Vyper looks deep in thought. “It could be from CWA the promotion I owned. I booked that show if I remember correctly it was against a man named Ice and”.. I seen him the other day!” A breif pause as Vyper quickly opens his door to get out. He speed walks to stop Grest but as he reaches the front doors he’s tackled by Samantha Foxs. Vyper grunts as he hit’s the ground and Samantha yells “You’re not stopping him! If its this important to meet with this Mike Jones let him do it.” Vyper struggles beneath the surprisingly strong Samantha Foxs to is a wrestler herself. “You don’t understand Sam” those two will kill each other.” Vyper says. While that is happening Fred Grest is in the middle of the lobby engaged in a stare down with Mike Jones. Mike Jones has a few inches on Fred Grest and plenty of pounds to go with that. “Congratulations on your win Freddy. It’s a shame nobody was able to see it.” Mike Jones smirks. “Plenty of people will see me beat Emoticon” Grest snaps. “Yeah, I believe he said he wanted to kill you. It’s a pity he’ll beat me too it.” Jones responds. “You weren’t able to kill me 3 years ago when I 1st started and you sure as hell can’t now.” Grest has his eyes locked on Mike Jones. Grest is ready for anything that could happen. “3 Years ago you were able to win matches. Now chicks are beating you.” Jones says mockingly. “You fucking egotistical asshole.” Grest mutters. The Extreme One is about ready to attack as Chill, Scott Vyper, and Samantha Foxs, grab him and pull him away from a fight that would have happened. “Come on man its not worth it” Chill says. “Well look at what we have here. It’s one big CWA reunion plus a hot blonde.” Jones responds. Chill stares him down as Samantha and Scott pull him away. Chill slowly walks out never taking his eyes off Mike Jones. Samantha and Scott are now struggling to get Grest in the back seat as he wants a shot at Mike Jones. Chill quickly opens back seat as Samantha pounces on him to get him in the back seat. Chill and Vyper get in the front seat. Chill is on the driver’s side when he realizes he has no keys. “Shit! Sam get the keys off Fred.” She quickly digs in Grest’s pocket who is struggling but doesn’t want to hurt Samantha. She grabs the key and tosses them to Chill. He starts the car and high tails it out of the parking lot. Grest is left to wonder if he could have taken him down or is Mike Jones right. Is Mike Jones in a much higher league then Grest? Something he feels he will never reach. With so much to worry about can he even beat Emoticon? Is making prank calls, elaborate jokes, and shooting a squirt gun all he can do? He won’t believe that for a second. He will achieve is goal of being the best no matter what the cost.
