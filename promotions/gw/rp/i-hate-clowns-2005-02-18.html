I HATE CLOWNS!

18 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-18-05 SHHHHTUNK!!! The sound as a dagger sticks deep into the wall that a large life sized picture of a clown adorns. The knife now sticks out of his forehead. A round of applause goes up, as do some cheers. “Nice shot.” Says a stout looking guy with a large beard. “Thanks Amigo” Says El Serpenti. Serpenti stands in a large room filled with wild bunch of patrons; Bikers, and other rowdy types. To El Serpenti this is a modern day Pirates tavern. “I hate clowns.” Serpenti says as he downs another drink. “Got to fight one tomorrow.” Serpenti says downing more drink. ?You got a problem with clowns?? Comes a voice behind Serpenti. Serpenti turns and to his amazement there is a table full of clowns. Circus Clowns and Rodeo Clowns there is even a damn Mime with them. ?Holy crap on a stick!? Serpenti exclaims seeing the table of clowns. ?Ya din answer my frin here pal! You got a thin against clowns!? Saw a very short clown in a bright orange suit and blue and green clown paint on. ?What is there a damn convention in this town!? Serpenti shouts and staggers forward. “Yeah, Boys I think he has a problem with clowns. Lets give him a problem he wont forget.” Says the diminutive clown. With that a pie flies though the air and Serpenti ducks and the pie his rather large biker in the back of the head. ?Who in the hell!?? The man says as he stands up and wipes off his tattooed baldhead. The clowns hold up sign with arrows that are pointing to Serpenti. Serpenti shakes his head no, but for some reason has a pie in his had. The small Dwarf clown laughs as he sneaks away from Serpenti. ?Ohh Your going down you masked freak!? The large man says and charges. Serpenti tries to move out of the way? but in his inebriated state he stumbles and catches a massive right to the side of the head sending him up and crashing into the crowd of clowns. The clowns then start kicking and stomping on Serpenti. ?BAR FIGHT!!!!? A voice shouts out and the bar turns into a scene of chaos as Serpenti crawls away. Serpenti staggers up as the clowns give chase Serpenti ducks out the door and starts to stagger away but the Clowns burst out of the bar. ?Come on are you guys not supposed to make people laugh!? Serpenti shouts ?We are off duty!? Comes a collective response as the Clowns chase after Serpenti. Suddenly a bike races down the road and spins to a stop right next to Serpenti. ?Hop on!? Says the rider with a female’s voice. Serpenti hops on and the bike peels out and takes off leaving the clowns choking on the exhaust. ?We will get you!!!? Says the tiny clown. ------------------------------------------------------------- After speeding though the streets of Dallas Texas the mysterious rider pulls in front of a large house. Turns off the bike and climbs off. “I can not tank you enough sinorita.” Serpenti says The Rider pulls her helmet off to let their long red hair fall out. She looks at El Serpenti with her bright green eyes. “The name is Jasmine. Looks like you owe me for saving you back there.” Jasmine says Serpenti tilts his head side ways. “I shall do my best to pay that debt up.” Serpenti says. The red haired beauty walks the steps to the door and looks back. “We will just have to see if you can pay me back indeed.” Jasmine says motioning for Serpenti to follow. Serpenti only nods and follows her into the house.
