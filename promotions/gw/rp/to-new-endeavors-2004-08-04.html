To new Endeavors

4 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/04/04 'The limo went to pick her two hours ago and they should be on their way back by now sir.' 'Good. Make sure she arrives at the resturant on time. If she doesn't...it will be someone's head' 'Yes sir.' I hang up the phone and put on my suitcoat as I get ready for this date. My attendants making sure that every hair is in place and I look the best I can look. I make it to the resturant before she does so I take my special procedures. I tell the waiter to take her to the desk far left and closest to the window. He nods as I slip him five hundred. I caution him that if he does not do as I have asked he will pay dearly for it. He stands and waits. I walk into the kitchen and talk to the head chef. I tell him that if he can fix a certain dish he could fix I would buy him his own resturant. He nods. I smile. This is going very well. She walks in and my heart, a thing that lay dormant for hundreds of years, skipped a beat. She was wearing a black dress with roses pictured as if they were falling, just showing enough of legs to make wish you could see more.. I send one of my attendants to bring in a special kind of wine. He brings it to her and she nods as...... .....I sit there watching her in silence, behind the door of the kitchen in the resturant. She adjusts slightly in her chair . I sat there minutes looking at her before I was being a hinderance to the kitchen staff. I calmly walk out of the kitchen and right up behind her with no problem. I look down at her and smile, she realizes me and stands. 'Merry night Leinad.' 'Merry night Cassandra. Please will you have a seat?'She smiles as she sits back down and I with her. She looks around the lavish resturant and smiles, I smile too. I raise my already filled glass. 'A toast.' 'To what?' 'To this endeavor, and to many more.' She raises her glass while not looking me in the eye but smiling. 'Then I too have a toast.' 'Really, to what?' 'Meeting one such as you.' I can not try and keep my composure any longer as when she says that, I give a develish grin. I sit back in my chair and drink some of the wine. She does the same. I stare into her eyes and she stares into mine. 'So, I did a back up on you earlier today, you have a pretty extensive past in some ackward places. Some things deal with murder, some bodies were found dry others incenerated. You're a black widow, trained in many martial arts styles. And from the looks of it, it has kept you wealthy for ages. How long did you think you would hold me?' 'Till I thought I could kill you, but there is something special about you Leinad?' Her calmness kind of struck me with suprise and I smile. She crosses her legs as if i'm boring her, but steady enough to fight if need to. I applaud quietly. 'This has been a hell of an application for a job Cassandra. But why did we have to go through all of this. I understand ya thought i was just some drifter you were gonna kill but you could have been upfront from the beginning. You're hired though.' Her defenses weaken some as she looks at me perplexed with those gorgeous eyes. Her dish arrives, as well does mine. My dish consisting of a rarely cooked T-bone, her's a small sushi plate with mint chocolate chip ice cream for dessert. A combination I find utterly disgusting. She looks at the plate and looking even more confused back at me. 'What are you doing Mr. Soma? Why did you make this dish.' 'I also found out that was your favorite dish to order.' 'Why are you doing all of this...this messing with my head...' I lose the smile I have had through most of this and my face becomes feirce, my eyes staring deep into her soul. She begins to hold her head as if hearing many things at one time. I look at her and her gaze fixed on my eyes, for she can not look away. No other customers in the resturant vacant excluding the kitchen staff, all else is quiet. I lean in and she forcefully does as well. 'Tell me of the vampires around Mexico and some of the areas.' 'We have lived in the areas of Latin America all our lives, all of us were female, all of us black windows, and with some hint of third sight.' 'There are no male vampires?' 'There are, they tend to be the native people's, ya know the Indians, there are many of them running around, suprised you haven't seen them in the U.S. they are everywhere I hear.' I loosen my gaze a bit as she still looks at me. 'What did you do with that money I gave you?' 'I bought a house and some land out in Nevada. Nice place for a female vampire to make a living.' I shake my head as I let the gaze go and she blinks a little bit. 'It's a shame you squander money as you do, being what you are and all?' 'Not all of it went to the house and land Leinad. As I said some of us have hint of third sight type of abilities.' 'Well what did you with the rest?' 'Human slavery black market, its really big in small countries.' 'Interesting...there is no point in being involved with all that house and land, it'll be a vacation place for you.' She starts to eat her food but stops abruptly looking back up at me 'What are you talking about?' 'Remember the job you got....' 'What job?' 'My personal assistant.' I stand from the table as she looks shocked yet cooled about it. She looks at the food, to me, back at the food, and back to me. She gives a slow shake of the head. She finally stood up facing the way i'm leaving. Before she can say anything I cut her off, and as I stop walking, I turn around 'I expect you to be moved out of your house in a couple of hours, one of my limos will come to pick you up, with a navigator with a couple of attendants to help with packing and such.....Oh and I expect you to be home before morning.....you have a big day ahead of you tomorrow and you need sleep...' 'Where does the personal assistant of Leinad Soma sleep?' 'In Leinad Soma's bed of course. I shall see you soon.' 'And if I don't show up?' 'You will....I have a hunch.' I walk out to my limo and get in the limo in silence.
