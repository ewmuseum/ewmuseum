Death Calls

30 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 30th, 2004 - TOWNSFORD MIDDLE SCHOOL: DALLAS, TEXAS - .:The camera follows a man, who appears to have a bucket of red paint in the hand the camera is watching, as the drips of dried up paint crumble with each step the man takes. The surrounding area is fairly dark, until the figure steps through a gate, into a sea of yellow light. Once the figure is on the other side of the fencing, the camera reveals that the figure is none other that Takahashi Jetui. Jetsui puts down the can of paint, and retrieves a cigarette from his inside trench coat pocket. Jetsui lights the cigarette with a zippo that glistens in the yellow street lights. Takahashi looks up at the building in front of him, and on the front there is lettering that reads: TOWNSFORD MIDDLE SCHOOL And under the lettering is a set of four doors. Takahashi picks the paint can back up and proceeds towards the front of the school, directly in front of the set of doors. Takahashi whips out a switchblade knife, and pops off the lid on the can of paint. With the pop of the lid, red paint splatters onto the cement below. Takahashi drops the switchblade on the ground beside the paint can and pulls out a dirty rag from his pocket. Takahashi then dips the rag into the can of red paint, when the rag is removed, Takahashi begins to paint the doors:. TAKAHASHI: I am not a forgetful man. Jamester, you have escaped your date with destiny this time, but your fate does not slip me this easily. .:Takahashi dips the red, slushy rag back into the paint can, and brings it back out with a string of paint dripping down as the rag is lifted:. TAKAHASHI: Jamester, when I came to Global Wrestling with the thought of pain in my mind, I gunned for you. Why did I make such a hasty line for your head? It is not because I envy your success, oh no. It was not because I wanted your relationship with Darling Nikki, dear my. I gunned for Jesse Jamester because I wanted to unleash the Jackal. (Continues to paint) I wanted to prove to myself, and to everyone back home in Japan that I could turn a man into the exact opposite of what he is. I wanted to bring out the hateful, scornful side of a good man. And when I came to America, I let my eye be caught by YOU. .:Takahashi once again dips the rag into the bucket of paint, a puddle of the red liquid now forming around the can. We cannot yet see what Takahashi is painting, but he continues to do so as he continues to speak:. TAKAHASHI: The courageous, valiant knight in shining armour of the wrestling world. This is how you caught my attention, Jesse. I had to pick you, Jesse. You see? Who else was I to pick? Everyone else in the wrestling world was already showing their inner evil. But you Jesse, you seemed to be on top of it all, on top of the WORLD. So I made my challenge even GREATER. I took the happiest, most go-lucky son of a bitch in the wrestling, and fucked up the love of his life. That is what I did Jesse. .:Takahashi dips the rag one more time, but this time instead of painting the set of doors, he swishes the rag in sloppy circles on the pavement in front of the doors. Takahashi then takes a long puff of his cigarette, and throws it into the mess he just made on the ground, igniting the painted cement, lighting up the message on the doors. However, Takahashi steps in front of the doors, and again begins to speak, the background illuminated by the flames:. TAKAHASHI: And now, you can run away from your problems all you want Jesse, but they are only going to stalk, and haunt you more and more with every waking moment. I am waiting for you Jesse, and so is your precious Nikki. The other side calls your name JACKAL, one of the pair has already had their blood mixed into the river, now my friend, it is YOUR turn. .:Takahashi places his hands into his pockets, and leaves the rag in the paint can in front of the blazing fire. The fire flickers on the message on the doors, and in dripping red paint, the message reads: DEATH AWAITS JAMESTER - 2004 >>>>>><<<<<<<<
