The Rebirth of the Kinslayer(Introductory RP3)

undated

Date: Long ago... Time: 3:25am Date: April 29, 2004 Location: A small apartment in Chicago. He had been turning and turning all night long. His long black hair full of sweat, as was the bed. He woke up with a start. What a weird dream he was having, he felt like all the energy had been completely drained from his body. It had been a hard night in the wrestling ring, but it wasn't like him to get this tired after a night's work. It probably was him fragging the pains of weeks past. It was time to go to the doctor, he couldn't let such a little thing like that strained arm muscle make him get sidelined now. He was now gaining the respect of peers, he had been working all his life to achieve this one goal of becoming the ultimate fan favorite. The top face in the industry, but things hadn't gone as he had expected. He had started out pretty good, but a broken leg had sidelined him for some time, and the owners and trainers didn't think he was in the same shape as before. Dorian got up from his bed after looking at the ceiling for a while. He decided to take another bath to see if it would relax his muscles. He went to the bathroom, and closed the door. Soon after the shower could be heard, what he didn't know is that an ancient pact would take over his body and his soul if he wasn't strong enough. Time: 3:25pm Location: The Great City of Rome In front of the coward he would go. In front of the man that the people of Rome call a leader... the Caesar. Dagoth had been waiting a long time to murder this man who had made his life a living hell until Atilla came... every single time he thought of Atilla he felt so sick. The man...no, the animal who murdered his family and left him for dead a little over six months ago. The thing that bothered Dagoth so much was that he could not get revenge on that animal, so he took it against every damn Hun that existed, he practically destroyed the race with the help of the Visigoths, Ostrogoths and the barbarians from Germania. It had felt so good, but at the same time so bad, to look back and think of what his wife would have told him, what his daughter would have said... the name would never be forgotten. Dagoth the Kinslayer. On the outside he didn't feel good about what he had done, but deep inside there was a small voice telling him that what he did was right, and that same voice was telling him to go over to the Caesar and destroy him in front of his people. He needed this to have his revenge complete, after this murder, he would die and be back his his family, that is what he wanted. He had condemed himself to the Underworld, to suffer Pluto's wrath. He had to do something to redeem himself in the eyes of the gods. What? He didn't know, but he was sure something would come to him. He was walking towards where the Caesar was going to be. Yes, the Colisseum, today it was going to host a tournament in honor of the Caesar, and Dagoth was ready to ruin the party and kill him in front of everyone. That was his dream, no, that was his destiny. He had decided this long ago, even before the treason commited by Attila, even before his family was dead and Cassius Cosades was rotting in his house. What better way to prove himself a warrior than to destroy the leader of the greatest empires of all times? Nothing, absolutely nothing. ------------------------------------ Dorian had been in the shower all this time, he kept having this weird dream. He saw some place he had never been before, but it didn't look like it was the present, it looked more like the past, but what would make him dream of this? 'I'm going crazy.' He got out of the shower with a towel wrapped around his waist, he picked up some boxer shorts and put them on. He put the towel in the hamper and went over to the little kitchen, he open the refrigerator and took a Coke can. Opened it and drank some. He had to stop using medication, it was starting to make him feel loopy and kind of crazy. Right now he just felt weird, like something was about to happen, but he had absolutely no idea what it was. The night got crazier and crazier as it passed, but this didn't start today or even last week, some time ago, he just started loving horses... he used to HATE horses! He had a horrible experience one a long tima ago and it wasn't like him to go towards one, start talking to it and combing it's hair... 'This is too weird... I have to see a shrink.' That wasn't all though, one day he had a horrible nightmare in which he saw the decapitated body of a woman and a child, and he felt like he was there, like somehow that was his family, but when he woke up, apart from the horrible sense of loss, his mother was alright. he didn't understand one bit of it. After a while of walking around the apartment trying to figure out what was wrong with him, he decided to check the internet for some information, he found nothing, except something about another life or something, but he didn't look that way. He didn't believe in those things, he wasn't superstitious or anything like that. Another thing he remembered was his sudden love for swords. He had been in Spain last summer in a vacation with his girlfriend, and he just decided to buy one. It was the most beautiful sword he had ever bought. In fact, it was the only one he had ever held in his hand, the hilt had a horse with a sword in it's mouth and a ruby the size of a quarter incrusted there. The man who had sold it, for only 150 US dollars, had said something about the greatest warrior planet Earth had ever seen. He didn't care who it had belonged to, he just knew that somehow in some strange way, he was supposed to hold it. He got up from the computer and decided to get some sleep. It had been about an hour after he had been awaken by that strange dream. No more, he had decided to take no more from those stupid dreams, he needed sleep and that was what he was going to do. As he was about to lay down, a sudden feeling of accomplishment and happiness, he felt as if he had done something that filled a huge void in his life, could it be that he decided to go to sleep? 'I definately have to get someone to check me out.' As he threw himself on the bed, he felt a sharp pain in his ribs. It was excrutiating, like someone had just struck him with a sword. He tried to stand up, but he just couldn't move, he felt a sudden fear, what is happening to me? He felt dizzier as the moments passed, he felt his eyes closing, his blue eyes hid upwards and all you could see was white, he was in a trance. His body started convulsing, at first it was slow and short, but then it started to escalade, higher, higher, until he was almost jumping up and down, white foam coming out of his mouth, it stopped suddenly. His eyes rolled back down, but there was something strange about them. They were no longer blue, they were dark brown, almost black. A sudden grin came accross his face. Dorian had dissapeared, and instead we had this monster. He got up from the bed and he felt the sword drawing him. He went to the closet and took it out. 'Exactly how I left you.' He then started to marvel at a new world, a very different one from the one he had come from. He was gaping at the TV, he turned it on and stood back drawing his sword. He stared at it, and told himself. 'This is what the witch told me about before giving me that potion. Beware of technology, this must be it.' He turned it back off he had so many things to see and experience, he went over to the bed, and suddenly felt weak. He fell on the bed, exhausted, he slept the whole night, dreaming of what his future would be, but at the same time feeling guilty of making this deal with the devil, he had reincarnated in the future and betrayed his family who were waiting for him in the afterlife. Tomorrow will be a interesting day. Six hours later he woke up with the worst headache he had had in years. He felt strange. He felt different. He felt like he was another person. Who am I!?
