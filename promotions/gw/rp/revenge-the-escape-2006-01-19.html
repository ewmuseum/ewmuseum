Revenge: The Escape

19 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 19, 2006 The shimmer of the belt underneath the glow of the sun shining through the window pleased him immensely. It had taken everything he had to obtain this prize, his body still ached from the rigors of the match but he ignored the pain for he was too excited. He had finally done it, and those bastards from the committee were no longer there to stop him’nothing stood in his way now. As beautiful as the International Title was there was a far greater prized that had caught his eye, however, the World Championship would have to wait. For the time being he would enjoy his most recent accomplishment and bask in its glow every chance he got. For the first time in nearly a year Mike Biddle was truly happy, 2006 had started with a bang. At last he could finally reclaim his former glory after nearly killing his career with his 2005 performance. 2006 was sure to be the greatest year of his career; after all, what could possibly go wrong? ***** Laundry duty was an awful chore that was beneath a man of his social position, but considering his current situation Charles Magee had little choice but to comply with the wishes of the prison guards. His duties consisted of sorting the dirty laundry so it could be put in the trucks to be washed at the Laundromat in town. The prison had stopped washing the laundry on site after a murder that included the use of the washing machine, since that date both washers and dryers have been deemed unsafe for inmates. This fact was what Charles planned to exploit for his escape from this dark armpit of civilization. He was left mostly unsupervised while he sorted through the dirty clothes, his only watchmen was a surveillance camera that could be avoided if one kept out of its line of sight. He had told Duke, Hawk, and Jimbo to meet him in the room as soon as they could for today he planned to leave. Charles hummed to himself as he placed the dirty clothes in different hampers. The hampers were large enough to hide at least one person if the need were to arise. At precisely 4:56 p.m., just as he had instructed, the three rugged men entered the room. They kept to the corner just beneath the camera. Noticing their presence but not indicating so, as to not arouse the suspicion of the guard watching via the camera, Charles began to push half filled hampers in the direction of his partners in crime. Each man climbed into a hamper and covered himself with the dirty clothes. Once all three men were safely hidden, Charles pushed the final cart underneath the camera and signaled to the guard watching on the other end that he was done and going back to his cell. Once the hamper was out of the camera’s range Charles hid himself within. A few moments later the guards in charge of the laundry came into the room and began to push the carts away. “They sure are heavier than usual,” one guard commented with a strained voice. “It looks like that bastard over packed “em,” the other guard replied. And that was all that was said about hampers as the two men loaded them onto their truck. With the carts loaded in the back of U-Haul sized truck the guards hopped into the cab and started to drive off the yard. The truck was stopped briefly at the gate; the escapees in the back knew this and all four of them held their breath out of fear of being caught. “Going into to town to get the laundry done huh?” The gate guard asked the driver of the truck. “Yup, you need to check the back at all?” “Nah,” the gate guard said, “just go ahead go.” He said as he raised the gate for the truck. As the truck started moving again the escapees breathed a sigh of relief, it wouldn’t be much longer until the truck stopped at the Laundromat and then they would be able to make their move. After a long wait the truck final stopped and they heard the engine turned off. Charles knew that now the guards would take a quick dinner break and leave the truck unattended, information he had learned by overhearing the conversation of the two guards. Once he was sure it was safe Charles emerged from his hamper. “You can come out now gentlemen,” Charles said with a smile. Once the three other men had emerged he continued. “Be sure to take off your prison shirt, that way we’re all just wearing white t-shirts and khakis. Once we exit this van I will rent us a car and we will head to Las Vegas.” “How the hell you gonna rent a car hoss?” Duke asked. Charles smiled. “I stole one of Biddle’s credit cards once at a party while he was drunk, to this day he still doesn’t know that it’s missing. So he will be financing our operation.” This fact made the three other men laugh heartily. Charles went to the end of the trailer and pulled the door open. The four men stepped out into the freedom of the outside world, unnoticed by anyone. Meanwhile back at Nicola Maximum Security Prison, the guards noticed that four prisoners were missing. ***** Biddle lay sprawled out on the couch in front of the television mindlessly flipping through channels as he muttered something about there never being anything good on. It seemed that the only thing on TV in Las Vegas was infomercials about all the damned casinos. Biddle was about to give up and just go downstairs and waste a few thousand dollars when he suddenly came upon CNN. “Nicola Prison reports that four inmates are missing today,” the young woman said. Biddle leaned in closely; he knew that Nicola was where Charles had been sent to after he had been sentenced. “Among the missing inmates is Charles Magee, the infamous Beverly Hills millionaire that recently murdered his former lover?” Biddle clicked the TV off. “Shit,” Biddle said with wide eyes of fear. He dropped the remote onto the floor and quickly rose from the couch. He rushed around his suite, checking every window and every door that led to the outside to make sure that it was locked. Once he was sure everything was locked he then went about pulling down all the blinds over the windows. Biddle now stood in front of the window that looked over the Vegas strip; he carefully slid one of the blinds up and peered into the outside world as if he were searching for snipers. Suddenly Biddle heard the knob on the front door turn, luckily it was locked. Once the person on the other side realized the door had been locked he or she began to bang loudly on the door, so loudly that it caused Biddle to let out a small scream. “You alright in there boss man?” The burly voice of the Back Alley Brawler called from behind the door. But what if Charles had captured his bodyguard and was using him as a way to get to him? Biddle shuddered at the thought; he had come to Las Vegas to get away from these sorts of situations. The banging at the door continued. “If you don’t answer da door I’ll break it down,” the Brawler said. “Are you a-alone?” Biddle nervously stammered. The Brawler paused behind the door; he wasn’t expecting a question like that. “Well’yeah.” Biddle finally worked up the courage to walk up to the door and peer out through the peephole. What he saw was his bodyguard standing alone in the hallway. Biddle quickly opened the door and ushered the bodyguard in and then quickly slammed and locked the door behind him. “What’s wrong boss man?” The Brawler asked with quizzical look. He had never seen Biddle look so worried. “H-he’s c-coming to k-k-kill me,” Biddle stammered, his dark blue eyes wide with fear. “Who is?” The Brawler asked as he cracked his knuckles. “Charles is,” Biddle said as he fell onto the couch. “Dat da guy dat killed ya girlfriend?” “Yes,” Biddle replied “How do ya know that he’s comin” for ya?” “He just escaped prison,” Biddle said, “I saw it on the news. I want you to increase security around my room.” “Sure thing boss man,” the Brawler replied. “I’ll do that right now.” The Brawler left the room, leaving Biddle alone and afraid. He had never suspected that it would come to this, that Charles was really crazy enough to break out of prison and try to murder him. As Biddle lay on the couch he suddenly wished he had been nicer to Charles.
