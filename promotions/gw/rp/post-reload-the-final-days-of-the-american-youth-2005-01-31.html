Post Reload (The Final Days of the American Youth)

31 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01.31.05 Ken's arms shot into the air as 'Superstar' blared in the background. The crowd in attendance was screaming so feverishly that the lyrics and licks of Saliva's ode to KPJ were barely audible. The referee walked to The Franchise, who was on his knees, and handed him that which he had faught so valiantly for, the Global Wrestling Heavyweight Championship of the World. Ken looked down at the piece of gold that he'd been chasing for nearly two years and tears began to stream down his face. 'Ladies and gentlemen,' began Brandi Sights, 'we have just witnessed a man realizing his dream.' 'I almost feel like we're intruding, Brandi,' rang the voice of Logan Creed. 'Ken Partridge, Jr. has just done what many men can mearly dream of acheiving.' The emotional KPJ springs to his feet, holding the title high for all to see before rolling out of the octagon and heading to the Splash Zone. His eyes immediatly spied the joyful face of his mother, which he embraced warmly with his voice softly ringing in her ear. 'Thank you,' he said. 'Thank you for being there, always being there for me. Thank you for being my biggest fan. Thank you for supporting you. Without you, this would not have happened. This belt belongs to you, too.' His mom returned the warm hug with her face wet from tears of joy for her beloved son. 'I love you,' she said in return. 'I'm so proud of you.' 'For those wondering,' began Sights, 'that Momma Superstar, right there... Ken's mother.' 'I had the opportunity to meet her before the show tonight, Brandi,' retalliated Logan. 'She's one helluva woman, and she loves her son.' He gave her a kiss on the cheek before turning his attention to the rest of his family at ringside, including his sister, neice, nephew, step-father, father, step-mother, and his two step-brothers among many more. He hugged each of them before heading into the fans. 'He taking the belt to the people that brought him here,' yelled Creed. 'Those fans have stuck with Ken through it all, and I can guarantee you he has not forgotten that.' 'Absolutly not,' chimed Brandi. 'He loves these fans just as much as they love him The arena was literally shaking as the fans rumbled Las Vegas to the point where you could no longer hear Ken's music and the announcers voices were barely audible. Ken's arm shot into the air as a slew of fans held him high on their shoulders, each chanting the familiar... 'FRAN-CHISE!' 'FRAN-CHISE!' 'Folks, we're out of time,' finished Logan. 'Thanks for joining us.' 'We are now going to take you to the final pre-taped award ceremony of the evening,' announced Brandi in the few seconds they had left. 'We'll see you at Evolution.' The cameras went off the air, but the celebration was just as jubilent as before. Jackson and Zenith had both made their way out of the ring and to the back as Bill Moresi, Gunner Addison, Josh, and Joshua Curtis all ran from the backstage area to the octagon with bottles of champagne in their hands. The fans finally let Ken's feet touch the ground, and he went to get his family, urging them to follow him into the ring. Joshua Curtis was the first of his roster-mates to shake his hand, but Ken pulled him in for a tearful hug. Moresi immediatly threw his arms around KPJ as the two shared a meaningful embrace. Gunner was next, as she hugged Ken, sharing the same tears of joy as him. 'I love you so much,' he said. 'You are the one that got me here. You've stood by me when you shouldn't have and held me up when you couldn't have. You are my breath, my tears, my life, and my heart. You saved me.' 'No,' she said through tears of her own. 'We saved each other. I love you, too, and I am so... so proud of you.' The two shared a passionate kiss as Kayla and Trever, Ken's neice and nephew gave out the 'ooohs' that ring true to children at that age. Ken and Gunner released their embraced and wiped the tears from each other's eyes before KPJ came face-to-face with Josh. The two looked at each other for a short time before sharing a powerful embrace. What those in attendance didn't know that Ken did, was that this would be the last time that Josh ever stepped foot in a Global ring. 'Don't you dare loose contact with me, got it,' said a crying Ken. 'You stay in touch. You're my best friend, Josh. You're my best fucking friend in the world and I wouldn't trade you for anything. This World Title, it wouldn't mean anything without you here to witness it. You're my running-mate, my partner, my friend, and my brother. I love you... I fucking love you. Things are not gonna' be the same. Now, you go to Core and you own that shit like we've owned this one.' The two release their hold as Kayla and Trever run up to Ken, each one throwing their arms around his legs. He reached down and hugged them both before putting each one up on his shoulders. Before he knew it, he was on a set of shoulders as well, as Bill and Josh had both picked him. His neice and nephew held the title above his head for him as Gunner and Ken's mom, Connie, hugged. As did Angie, his sister, and Joshua Curtis. After parading them around the ring, the three were finally let back down, and KPJ let Kayla and Trever run back to their mom as The Elite and Joshua Curtis began to shower the new World Champion with champagne. At that moment... Ken felt infinite.
