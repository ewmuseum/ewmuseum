She's out of control

2 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-2-2005 “She’s out of control” Yes she was. Yes she is. Elia Smith, or so the youngster seems to the un-train eye, was out of control. Her clothing, the way she spoke, everything about her made you notice that she wasn’t herself. And to her family, she was an outcast that was sent away years ago to only return. It was tragic as she had high hopes by the family. Tormenting their dreams of her one day making it big, doing something important in the world but those dreams, those wishes, they were ashes now. “You know what we have to do right?” Uncle Johnson says. “I’m not doing that. She isn’t showing what he did.” Mrs. Smith says, the mother of Elia. She face was made up to be perfect but all it was showing was the tragic sadness that of a crying mother. Crying her heart out for the girl she was raising to be a goddess. “HOW CAN YOU SAY THAT!” Uncle Johnson screaming like a mad man. “SHE’S SHOWING WAS HER FATHER DID.” “You don’t know that. You weren’t around when he was the victim of that demon.” Mrs. Smith responds, walking towards Uncle Johnson who was lowering his head. You see, long ago, in a place yet forgotten, my father was a deep and depressing man. Losing his job so that he couldn’t provide for the people he cared for. He lost everything that he was fighting for, his mother, his arm, and most importantly, his pride. Everything was going wrong till the deepest of evil, the fire that burns deep in everyone’s soul came to see him. Giving him back everything that he was loosing in his little world. But the price was to much for him to bare and the demon, the red eye monster, to over. “I was the one that quit my dream to take care of him. Remember, your not the only one that suffered through his pain and suffering.” Uncle Johnson says, walking away from her before placing his hand on the far window in the room. The mind. That was the first thing to go from my father from what I was told. His memories of who was his friends and who was his foes began to change. Disappearing from the family to deep dark places that no rat infest creature would even live in. Next was his work rate. He would slowly become unwanted at work or even anywhere else. Blasting the world that he knew and changing it from the boot to the skull. He wasn’t hurting himself’he was hurting the people around him, but for what? “So you’re telling me the bat to your body wasn’t a sign of something up” Come on James, you got to be blind if you don’t see that as one.” Mrs. Smith spoke with passion as one tear began to come down her face. “She’s just stressed. Besides, if we jump with this you do know what happen to Alex when we brought the problem to him right?” James, my Uncle says. Alex was my father. He got that name from his father, Alexis J. Lexington. My mother told me that when they went after my father, trying to save what little life in his soul that he had, Alex went ballistic. Torching the house, the very house that we had moved in several years ago. He began to talk in a form of words that were not human. They weren’t even anything that was documented. Attacking people left and right before finally being caught and sent to an institute. “He was insane James. You knew that. That is why he is STILL in the Terrell State Mental Institution.” Mrs. Smith responds with a bitter response. “Oh come on Eliza?”, pausing for a moment as he walks toward Mrs. Smith. “What do you suggest we do for your daughter?” “Yes mother?”, walking into the middle of a conversation that wasn’t meant for my ears. “Elia.” Both James and Eliza says as they look at me. Oh I felt good. My body, tight black leather swinging around every little inch. Caressing me like a man who was making love to a woman would feel like. Overflowing my inter self like a waterfall in a forest. My lips, passionately filling with red lipstick as I gave a soft and gentle licking of them with my tongue before responding. “So mother. You’re the person that took away my father huh?” Walking with a rhythm, foot over foot towards the evil that was trying to force me’or the evil inside me away. “No Elia. You don’t understand at all.” Watching her back up till she ran right into the white wall behind her. “So one’anyone’COME HELP!” Uncle James Johnson screams out, trying to get anyone in sight to come to his pathetic aid but it wasn’t going to happen. Turning towards him, like an act of god, his mouth began to press together and come to a close. Sort of like the life of Hitler, his breath was soon taken from him. “James, or should I still call you Uncle huh” Why do you still defy me? Why do you still try and destroy me what I once was what you were desiring for?” Licking my lips as my hand began to explorer his body starting with his chest. Oh he felt so good. As odd as that sounds he really did. But not in the way you would think. He felt like smooth ice cream going down your legs when it melts. Like shampoo when it comes running down your back when you are washing it off. Like a soft kiss from your lovers when the lights are all off and it’s only you and him in bed. Passing around him, I took a visit to his ear, quietly whispering in it. “James, do you fear me?” Biting his ear gently. “No’I don’t’fear you.” Shaking as James responds. “Well you will now?”, taking my right arm and smashing it right into the lower back of him. James went straight down to both of his knees. Like a man begging for his life, James falls all the way to his hands and knees, looking down to the floor before Eliza came running to his aid. “JAMES!” Screaming out only a few feet away. “Get away you hoer.” Saying that to my mother only made her draw back and stop midway towards my Uncle. “You and everyone else just don’t understand.” “Elia?”, crying like a little girl who just lost her puppy. “You need help. If we get you help now then we might be able to stop it.” Help huh. Is that what she thinks? I don’t need help, I feel for the first time in my life like I am in control of it. I’m not living for my Uncle James. I’m not living for my mother Eliza. For the first time I am being shown that there is more then doing what others believe I should. There is more to life then being a good little girl. It only took a man that no one else could see to show me this, and now they want to take him away. “Shut up you sorry excuse for a mother. You call yourself strong yet you lay there crying. How can I listen to someone as weak as you when I have someone who has made me strong. How?” Walking away from my Uncle who was still flat on the ground holding the back of his neck. “Baby’I love you?”, watching as she extends her hand towards me. “You love me” You love me yet you are trying to take away what I have found. Is that what you said to my father before you locked him up?” Looking down at her from a clear stand point. Seeing the tears that she had pouring out like a waterfall. “You are weak and right now, anyone weak that is in my way will be permanently moved aside’forever.” Grabbing my mother with both of my hands, pulling her straight to her two feet before slamming her against the wall. Her breath began to pick up, blowing out more and more hot air with her crying. “You think I’m insane. You think I’m crazy. Well listen here mother, you don’t know crazy. Forever now you will no longer be in my life. You tried to kill it, well you have failed.” Slamming my hand against the wall that she was leaning on. “From this day forth, your little girl is no more.” Walking away from her, letting her stand there with her eyes shut so tight that you would need a crowbar to get them open. Walking through the door towards a world that I knew was different. Walking to a world that wasn’t ready for me. Elia Smith, the girl that everyone knew, the girl that my family knew, was dead. This time, the true Elia Smith was here. She was here to stay.
