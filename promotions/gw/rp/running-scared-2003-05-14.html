Running Scared

14 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/14 So here I am, backstage, cornered with my back to the wall blocked in by the moster known as Quartz. Sure I’d faced him before but it was never a pretty sight. The first of our meetings included my body being hoisted into the air and then flung toward the Globaltron. The second ended with my hand being raised as the winner of the match, only problem was, I was unconscious. Now I’m much too cocky to call myself fearing this man, but never running into him again was most definitely one of the higher requests on my wish list. Up until now I was making good on having this wish come true, but how quickly things change when you burn the car of the co-owner of GW and good friend of the beast now facing me. After evading them for the better part of the evening, the Nevermore had finally caught up with me. Sure Sage got his cheapshots in, but was blindsided by the chance at advancing towards being World Champion. Idiot, he caught the devil, he should have kept him... After that bogus attack I rolled out of the ring before anybody noticed and slipped out through the crowd. Leading me to where I am now, stuck between a Quartz and a hard place. The only thing even slightly positive about this situation was that the aforementioned hard place wasn’t Leviathan coming to extract revenge for the loss of his car as well, but I supposed he figured the hell I’d be catching from the Nevermore would be more than enough, especially after that attack... I thought I had done the unthinkable, attacked the Nevermore and gotten away with it. I could just hear CW in his easy chair now shouting “They can’t catch him! That’s ma” boy!” Lot of good that would do me now... What I wouldn’t give right now for a steel chair or some sort of firearm. I’m not all that sure either would do much good in the way of stopping him but at least it would even things up a bit. Possibly buying me enough time to get the hell outta here instead of what I’m doing now. Slowly backing away even further into the corner with Quartz blocking my only possible means of escape. As he approached he cracked his knuckles as a sadistic smile crossed his lips as if to show just how much he was going to enjoy this. From the way he looked you’d think I’d run over his dog or stomped on his last begonia. Apparently I’d been a constant source of annoyance for him as he informed me I’d been a thorn in his side for quite some time. The only thing keeping him from squashing me in the past were the more pressing matters that actually concerned the Nevermore. How I wished one of those would pop up now... But then again, I was one. Derek Frost, Brick, Ely Braxton, any one of the Youngbloods, what I wouldn’t give for any one of those guys to just go strolling by, diverting his attention. You know that feeling you used to get as a child when you’d get sent to the principal’s office? But the principal was busy so you’d have to wait your turn outside moving one chair closer as each person ahead of you was called for punishment. Waiting and listening as each received a punishment more outlandish than the next, then watching as the walked past you in tears. Well that’s how I felt, and I was in the closest chair, it was finally my turn... Quartz finished cracking his knuckles, making a thunderous pop with each one, reared back his humongous fist and swung for the fences. With his fist on a collision course with my face I did the only thing a man in my situation could... I ducked. His fist struck with such concussive force that it penetrated the drywall that makes up much of the backstage hallways. My eyes widened as he stared down at me fist still implanted in the wall. He ripped his hand from the hole leaving an even larger one that made upon the initial entry, carrying out small bits of debris and a white chalky dust in the process. At that point I knew exactly what this was about to turn into... a foot race. I regained my composure and shot right past him and out of that corner, I could feel his hands whip through my dreadlocks as he tried to grab me while I passed. Now I run a four flat in the forty yard dash but I don’t think I’ve ever run like this in my life. I’m talkin? call Guinness, there are animals that don’t run this fast! As I bend corners in the labyrinth that is backstage I glance over my shoulder. As I glance over my shoulder I can see Quartz no more than a few steps behind me. How could he move so fast? This guy is an animal! I can hear him running with me step for step no more than a few body lengths away as we run down a straightaway causing backstage staff to press up against the wall and many of them to drop the various items they carry. I don’t see what those items may be as my eyes are focused on the end of the hall and the dead end that waits there allowing only a left or right turn. Faster and faster I approach the fork in the hall thinking of the important decision that must be made once I get there, left or right? Left, right, left, right, my thoughts came on beat with my footsteps. At the end of the hall I decide to go left. As I bend the corner I look over my shoulder once again to see Quartz still hot on my trail but now just inches off my heels. I barely make it around the corner without crashing to the ground, Quartz doesn’t have the same luck and slides into the wall attempting to slow himself down. I make it to a door marked “stairs” and fling it open. With Quartz now back up to full speed I shoot him a bird out of sheer reflex and high tail it into the stair well slamming the door behind me. Seconds later I hear that same door bang against the door behind it and a quick check confirms that Quartz has still not given up. Running of the stairs arms pumping the whole way, the only thought in my head is how am I gonna shake this guy. My legs feel like they are pumping battery acid. After who knows how many flights of stairs I am met by one final door that reads “rooftop” which I push open and pass through in full stride and keep running until I reach the edge. Staring down onto the busy street below I can’t help but think how much I hate Texas. I also realize something else, that I’m stuck, there is nowhere else for me to run. It’s now or never and tonight it’s all or nothin. The thought of jumping off the building enters my mind but only stays briefly as it exits just as fast as it came. Then it dawns on me that Quartz can’t be too far behind. I run back to the door and my heart almost jumps out of my throat as I open it up to find Quartz running up the final flight of steps. Just as he makes his way up the final few I slam the door in his face locking it immediately. Now the search begins. I search the rooftop nervously scouring the ground for any sort of solid debris that may prolong Quartz’s stay on the other side of the door, as it is only a matter of time before he bangs it down. He began as soon as the door closed and has already knocked several very noticeable dents into it. Suddenly the wind picks up heavily and I look up to find a helicopter hovering over the roof and it begins to lower. As the helicopter sets down I cover my eyes in order to protect them from the debris kicked up by the helicopter’s rotor blades. Once everything settles the door swings open yet no one gets out. I attempt to look inside but the interior is encased in shadows. The noises behind the door to the stairwell stop... Out of nowhere Quartz bursts through the door and resumes his chase. Right then and there my choice gets made for me and I run right for the helicopter. I dive inside of the open door and one of the men inside closes it behind me. I cannot see exactly who he is but at least I don’t have to run anymore. The two of us exchange a handshake as we lift off. He hands me an envelope...
