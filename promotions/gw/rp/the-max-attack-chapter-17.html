The Max-Attack: Chapter 17

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today The scene opens to the early streets of Chicago and a white car racing down the freeway in a Sunfire vehicle. It stops at a red light but keeps going when it turns green. The car stops in a huge parking lot with two people getting out of the car running to the entrance of the hospital. The scene now cuts to the inside of Roswell’s hospital room. Ben goes and sits on a red chair and Katherine stands beside the hospital bed. “Max, can you hear me?” asks Katherine. “What?” “Can you hear me?” asks Katherine again. “Ya, I think so” Katherine grabs the little ball out of her pocket and shows it in front of Roswell and asks a question. “Do you know what this is?” “Yes and no” “What does that mean?” “Ok, I have this little enemy and he throws these little balls onto the ground and it explodes.” “Wait a minute. If it explodes when it hits the ground then that’s what blew up your room” “Hey. You could be onto something. But I can’t leave the hospital till tomorrow” “Well, me and Ben will go back to the hotel and look around for more stuff. Ok?” Katherine gives Roswell a tight hug and her and Ben leave the rom once more down the long hallway. The scene shifts to the highway with the same white Sunfire speeding down the highway. It stops at the same hotel Roswell is seen but the scene goes back to the hospital. Roswell is still there resting on the bed, but there is something blue on the chair beside him. Roswell reaches over to it and looks to see what it was. It is the GW Primstar title belt with a white piece of paper hooked in the gold part of the belt. Dear Mr. Roswell, We are happy to inform you that you won the Primstar title belt at last weeks Evolution. You might not remember it because of your concussion. Congratulations and I hope you keep it long. From: Christina McCoy. “Oh right, I won that title. I totally forgot about that. Wouldn’t I remember bringing it back here?” “Actually we had to bring you back here” comes a voice from the doorway of the room. The doctor walks into the room with a slight smile across his face. “After your match you had got another concussion and the ambulance was there to bring you back here and in the parking lot Ms. McCoy gave me the belt and a note in the gold of it.” says the doctor. “Thanks I guess” “Well cause of the other concussion will I have to stay in longer than tomorrow?” asks Roswell. “Actually the tests came in today and your free to go whenever you want in the next 24 hours.” “You mean I could leave right now?” “By all means, yes” Roswell rises from the hospital bed and starts stretching holding the Primstar title with him in the air. He gets off the bed and walks into the little bathroom with his clothes he will change into. A couple seconds later Roswell walks out of the bathroom with his white shirt and black Nike shorts. He also has the Primstar title around his waist with the gold and blue shining in the day light. “Well thanks for all the help doctor.” says Roswell. “Hey, its my job. See ya Max” Roswell walks out of the room with all the nurses staring at the shiny title belt. He walks through the huge parking lot and gets in his red convertible parked in a spot by the opening of the parking lot. He races out of the parking lot and in no time is at his hotel with his car parked in the very back of it. Roswell puts a black cover over his shiny car and walks away to the front this time with his belt in his left hand. “Uh-oh! Ben and Katherine didn’t come to Evolution and they probably didn’t know when it was and now there going to be mad at me!” yells Roswell. All the people on the sidewalk start staring at Roswell but they get back to what there doing a couple seconds later. Roswell walks into the hotel hiding the belt behind his back still noticeable to all the people. He walks up the stairway and in no time is at the door of his room. Roswell notices Katherine and Ben looking on the ground for other things. They see Roswell and both look suprised. “What are you doing here. Your supposed to be in the hospital” says Katherine. “The doctor said I could go in the next 24 hours and I came to see you guys.” A high voice comes from Ben. “What’s that behind your back Max?” asks Ben. Ben walks around and sees the belt and looks back as if he is going to cry. “What is it Max?” asks Katherine. Roswell brings the title to the front of his face and Katherine looks in shock. Katherine grabs the title away from Max and looks around it. “I thought we were coming to see the title match?” asks Ben. “Well, I went to the show alone. I totally forgot about you two and went without you guys.” Ben starts to cry rapidly as Katherine holds onto Ben. “You made Ben cry! Get out of my sight” scowls Katherine. Katherine and Ben charge past Roswell as Max starts looking at them. Ben starts crying more as Katherine takes a kleenex out of her pocket giving it to Ben to wipe the tears away.
