Huh? A match? How'd that happen?

25 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/25/05 Two men can have their fortunes change on a minute. One moment you're nothing but a servant, the next you're the one being served. Especially in this business. One moment you're serving people hot dogs in the arena, and then next you're cleaning the showers in the bottom locker rooms of the arena. But what happens after that? Where else can you go? The key thing here is, that some people can actually manage to reclaim that which they had lost a bit ago. This is the story of two such men. What had they done years ago? Does it really matter? To them it does. Former World Tag Team Champions in two different federations. Hall of Fame candidates or winners. But then the bottom fell out of their little play area, and things went down hill from there. But sometimes, all you need is a chance, a chance to try again... to reclaim that which was past glory and to secure your place in the future to repeat that said glory. But right now? Right now they're nothing... nothing but cleaning men in the Global Arena. “I can't frickin' believe this, dude. We outsold everyone of those little bastards at Whiplash.” Two men sitting on a bench, doing something with their feet. They're both wearing overalls, t-shirts and rubber gloves. To get people caught up, one man is really tall, just a hair under 7 feet tall. He looks and sounds to be of Hispanic origin. He keeps his hair cut short, to as to maintain a pretty clean cut appearance. His face shows no trace of facial hair either. The other gentleman is one of dark skin. His accent puts him very much from the Islands of the Carribean. His longer hair is done up in faux dreadlocks, similar to large cornrows. He's a little bit shorter, going a bit over 6 and a half feet in height. Both men are lean, with a muscle definition on them that belays their usual life style. ?We both had over 2 grand in commissions, at 20% when the night was over. I think I refilled my cart three times!? The larger man is talking as he continues the operation with what he's doing to his feet, rubber overshoes on. “Yah, mon. We kicked ass in da salesman department.” His partner says something as he's putting a large sponge on the bottom of the overshoe he's wearing and then taking a roll of duct tape to it, pulling it out quickly to make as much noise as possible with it. “Well, you heard it all. The other salesman thought we were taking their commissions. So now we're reduced to mopping the jobber locker rooms down here in the basement for the mid week crap shows.” Both continue to strap the sponges to their feet. The smaller man stops after the second sponge is on his foot and then he starts to mess with a portable radio player, ensuring the CD is in the player. His partner finishes and then looks across at him. ?Alright, dude. You think this'll really work, huh?? The smaller black man smiles with a white smile that would make Martha Stewart jealous. ?Ya, mon. Basically we'll use this to break up da dirt, then it's just hose it all down to da drains, mon. Will cut our work time in half, and we'll be havin' fun while we do it!? “Alright, dude. Sounds good.” The smaller man hits play on the cd player and then both grab their mops. The smaller man kicks over a bucket of soapy water so that it runs over the floor. The cd player starts with a slow guitar sound and then a low, soft voice can be heard. “La lie lie lie lie lie lie” It goes for a few more seconds and then the music picks up. Both men start to... dance... to the music, using their mops too in the spilt water. Both men start to sing along with the music. ?Hey man! Look at me rockin' out, I'm on the radiooooooo, Hey man! Look at me rockin' out, I'm on the videooooooo, Hey man! Look at me rockin' out, I'm on the radiooooooo, Hey man! Look at me rockin' out, I'm on the videooooooo, With Danny and Lisa,? The music goes slower, almost with an old world feel to it, but still very heavy sounding with the guitars and drums. The cd's voices and the voices of the two men here go soft. ?They take me away from, The strangest place of... Sweet Danny and Lisa, They take me away from...AHHH.? Again they pick up speed and tempo in singing and... cleaning... ?Hey man! Look at me rockin' out, I'm on the radiooooooo, Hey man! Look at me rockin' out, I'm on the videooooooo, With Danny and Lisa,? And again, they're dancing around the old locker room, threw a water filled floor. Sponges on their feet and mops in their hands as they sing along with “Radio-Video” by System of a Down from a CD. The music goes soft again. ?They take me away from, The strangest place of... Sweet Danny and Lisa, They take me away from...AHHH.? The main singer of the band and the taller of these two... idiots... continue on for the next verse, almost whispering, except for one word in the verses. ?Hey man look at me rockin' out, I'm on the RADIO!!!!!!! Hey man look at me rockin' out, I'm on the VIDEO!!!!!!! With Danny and Lisa.? The music cuts to an almost Island music tone and tempo. Both men really start to groove a bit more, especially the smaller of the two men. The larger man starts to use his mop as a regular dancing partner. ?They take me away from, The strangest place of.... Sweet Danny and LisaAaAaAaAa.? Both continue through the guitar solo on the song, which lasts a bit when suddenly it breaks into a vocal sound reminicent of the old Jewish plays, like Fiddler on the Roof. As they start that, the smaller man starts to do something similar to a Russian dance, crouched down and kicking his sponge covered feet out. “LALALALALALALALALALALALALALALALALA” And then... “NANANANANANAANA AHHHHHHH.” The smaller man gets too close to a wall that looks to be just piled up boxes of bathroom toilet tissue refills up on pallets. He falls into them, slipping on the water on the floor and falls into them. His partner is taken down by a cascade of boxes as they fall! The CD player skips heartily and then falls off it's bench placement, stopping the song in it's tracks. After the boxes stop moving a single sound comes from the pile of them near the wall. ?Oh, mon... I'm fine... it be all iry!? The other man comes to his feet with some grunts, belaying the feel of it all, and the worn and tired muscles of a man who's been into wrestling for a few years... who was not limbered up first. The taller man moves in to check on his life long friend and he finds him covered in rolls of toilet paper from at least one of the boxes that busted open upon landing from the top of the heap. ?You sure you're okay, dude?? “Ya, mon.” A big hand goes out to help the other up, and as they both are now at a standing position, they take a look around at the destruction, and they look highly pissed at what has now been caused by them until they actually get a look at the concrete wall behind, what has been hidden by the boxes all along. The location of the boxes shows that it made a small 'room' or 'cubby hole' that could be hidden from regular view. Lining the concrete wall are... pictures. Pictures of all of the women that preside in Global Wrestling: Mindy Mercer, Dawn McFarlane, Shadow, Tessa Windsor, Dani Northsky, Elia Smith, Vivica Valentine, and a few others even from it's past. On the floor are copies of Global Wrestling Magazines from the last few months and a couple other magazines that have had interviews and especially pictures of the other women of Global. “Dude, this is a shine to that, which is feminine and in Global.” The smaller man picks up a magazine, a copy of FHM from a few months ago that had Dani Northsky as the cover girl and main centerfold. “No, dude, dis is just sick. I'd bet this magazine has sticky pages in it. I'm not opening it to find out.” “You have that issue in your travel bag as it is.” “No shit. But still, this is a hidey hole for some man who has something for all of these chicks.” The larger man goes to investigate some of the other pictures. “Some of these are posters, magazine cutouts or actual photographs. Like this one, it's from Dawn McFarlane a few weeks ago when she lost to Emoticon and had to wear pink the following week. She's a little hottie, but damn, if she found out about these, someone would be gutted and his head hung out on a pike.” ?No kidding, mon. But still, what is this place?? Both think about it and then suddenly it hits them. This is a hiding place used by someone who wants to... 'by alone' with themselves and these pictures on the wall. ?Ohh... gross man! You fell in it too!? ?NOOO!!? The smaller of the two men falls to his knees and then starts to roll around in the water that still lines the floor to try to wash himself off. “Okay, I can't work like this anymore. I betcha this is where that one freak with the mask comes down to when the shows over. Betcha he used a camera to get some of those shots and that. Let alone the fact that he's always trying to hook up with these women and can't seem to.” ?You mean da one that's always giving out the roses and that, with the fake french accent?? “Yeah, that one. Tickle Me Elmo or something like that.” “Ya, mon. It's got to be his. And I know a fake French accent when I hear one.” “Yeah. Let's get up to HR and report this. I'm not cleaning down here any more. I'm not paid to clean this type of stuff. I could go to work at the Silver Saddle and clean the booths if I wanted to do that. And I'd probably make more.” “Ya, mon, let's get out of here.” With that, the two men start to walk out of the locker room, the sponges still on their feet. As they leave, the sounds of the water 'squishing' out of the sponges continues up the hallway. ++Human Resources Offices++ ++Global Towers++ We find out two men standing, still in their cleaning gear, as they're talking to the large lady with the hairy mole on her face that originally hired them a few weeks ago. “No, you don't understand. That place is gross. They'll need guys down there looking for biological stuff. We're not paid enough to do that.” The woman just keeps nodding to them, as they give their official complaint as to what they found in the basement area. In the office next to the Human Resources room, separated by a large wall with many glass windows, the head Global Reporter, Noi Tsunami is talking to one of her subordinates in the Media Relations department for the Hotel and Casino. Noi looks through the window and almost drops the folder with various papers for her job that she's carrying. ?I don't believe it. What the hell are they doing here?? The woman she's talking to, turns and then smiles as she fills Ms. Tsunami in. ?Those two? Hired a few weeks ago. They're up here every couple of days to complain about something. They keep getting traded from one department to another in the arena and hotel maintenance or cleaning. Last week they were housekeepers in the hotel, but they got kicked out when they walked in on a newlywed couple in the act of consummating their marriage and instead of apologizing, they started to give the guy pointers.? Noi amost starts to laugh at that. “They don't change.” ?What? You know them?? “Oh, yeah. Not personally, but those two are almost legendary in the wrestling business.” ?They're wrestlers?? “Yeah, at least they used to be. Do me a favor, get Mr. Creed on the phone for me. He's the head of Talent Relations for the wrestlers. I think I just found him something.” “I'll have him call your cell phone.” “Thanks.” Noi walks out of her office, and into the regular human resources office for the hotel/casino. “I don't believe it” Noi starts with a slightly sultry voice, immediately drawing the attention of the two men. The smaller man instantly starts to look her up and down. “Ey, you don't believe what. Dat someone who looks dis good would be...” “I don't believe that one of the best tag teams in the world has been reduced to cleaning floors...” She hit them hard, they had no answer. ?Miguel Lanza, and Jamie Francois Dermont... Tropical Storm. What are you doing here? And why didn't you apply with Creed's office?? Both looked at each other then back at the woman. ?Who's Creed?? ?Uhm, Logan Creed. One of our announcers and also one of our top recruiters. He's the one you guys should have gone to. Let me guess, you thought the human resources office took care of the wrestlers too?? “Uh... yeah.” One answers that, but the other cuts him off. “Actually we were just looking for work. Any work. Not to many feds want a team that has done only small spots for a while. We've had mostly 'bodyguard' gigs for the last few years. So getting back into the swing of things isn't going to be easy.” Noi actually listens to something that comes out of the mouth of the person she's now identified as Miguel Lanza that sounds... intelligent. “True. But you guys are former hall of famers and world champions as it is.” The smaller man, Jaime Dermont, fills her in on that. “Yeah, mon. But the way we lost out last world titles isn't exactly something to be glad about. I mean, we were running between the ring and da bathroom to relieve the other as that guy and his ex-lax laden brownies hit us hard. We'll always be remembered as the team that lost them due to having.. da...” She cuts him off. ?Well you know, Global is having a slight issue with our tag team division. We're running a round robin tourney for the tag team titles, and we're constantly on the look out for new teams. There is always a chance to recover and get something going there. Both men just stare at her, finally realizing what she's talking about. She's talking about... A return to the ring! ?You're kidding right?? Noi shakes her head at him. ?No, you guys were great. You think you can keep up with young teams like Unite or the Darlings?? Jaime smiles wide. “You be t'inking dat da darlings could keep up wit us? I saw them wrestle up in Seattle. Hmmm... Hotties...” He looks to be moving off into his own little space in his head as Miguel picks up where he left off. “You kidding? We'd be ready. We're not that old ourselves. Just because we've got 8 years in the business doesn't mean that we're ready to slow down any time soon.” Noi smiles. ?Who do we need to talk to again?? ?I'm going to get you in touch with Logan. But I happen to know that he's looking for a team to face one of our teams in this weeks coming show. A dark match, against the Enforcers. It's part of the tourney also. Would be a nice warm up match for you. They didn't fare too well against Unite last week. You two think you'd be up for it?? Both answer her. ?HELLS YEAH!? At that, Noi's cell phone rings and she pulls it off of her belt clip. She flips it open and answers it. “Logan! Hey, you won't believe what I just found sitting in our house keeping department down here...” Both men smile as she starts to talk to the man she said could get them a job at doing what they love to do. They're back... Hide your wives and daughters... And by god, be prepared for them, if you can. Miguel Alberto Alexandro Lanza, the man from Roosevelt Roads, Puerto Rico... “Bad-Ju-Ju” Jaime Francois Dermont from Montego Bay, Jamaica... Tropical Storm is returning to the wrestling ring this week.
