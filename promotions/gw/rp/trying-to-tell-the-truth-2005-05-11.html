Trying to tell the truth

11 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-11-05 May 10th, 2005 2:57 PM in the morning. It was over and done. The relationship. The tournament. The world that I had remembered was over. Now all that remained was a bold look at reality at it’s worst. And that scared me to death. But the future wasn’t even here for me yet. I was still thinking of the past and wondering where it seemingly all went wrong. Was it something that I did that I just didn’t see in front of me? Was the picture of the dream that I had painted not big enough for me to stand in? But this was a different and new morning. I was ready to tackle the world with my pride, my eyes, and my heart. I was just waking up from a hard dream. Pressed to see the bright lights of the world that I had hated to live in. Though the world didn’t seem like it liked me either. My eyes took a while to adjust, feeling dizzy and rusty through the open room. Trying to push up off the sunk in bed that I had laid on at my mothers house. It was my old room when I actually lived here once. Flowered blankets with a few satin sheets that laid on top of me. Turning my feet from underneath the covers, as I sat them right on the crisp cold wooden floor that was underneath me. It brought a cold chill up my back and to my head, making my eyes easier to see through out the room. It was empty. Empty of noise and the outside world so it seemed. “What time is it?” I said as I looked over to the table that was next to me. The first thing that came to my mind was my uncle. I had let him down so much and wither he was alive or not was beginning to go through my head. I raised up out of the bed, trying to stretch the bones in my body out till they popped. I felt a cold draft go through my legs that made my head drop down to look. Gasping at the sight of my sudden naked body. Reaching over to the chair that was next to the bed, I grabbed the red robe, knowing what robe it was from. I tried to block the image of Mark out of my head. Knowing that I did love him but he was gone. Yes it was cold but I had other things to think about right now that had to be thought about. The crying for him would come but in a different time. I began to walk towards the door that was in front of me but was pressed back at the last second. “TIME TO WAKE UP?” The voice screamed out. The voice of my mother. Loud, annoying, and just plain tiring to hear. My eyes opened wide from her face as I stood there in my way. “God what time is it?” I said, rubbing my eyes from side to side. “Two past three darling. It’s time for you to get a shower and get ready to go get a real job.” Her annoying ways said as she turned around and walked down of the room. Did she just say a real job? My breathing got harder, driving my head down for a second before I began the long walk out of the room. Looking down the hallway towards the living room, the noise draining in my ears as I turned towards the bathroom door right next door of the bedroom. __________________________________________________________________ 30 Minutes Later? “About time.” From the heat of a shower to the heat of the family. What was worst? I often asked myself that but the same answer always came. The family was the more heat. I walking in, my eyes seeing three of the worst family members that I could ever see right now. One being my grandmother, the woman that I use to think was the princess of the world. Another being my other uncle that I just couldn’t stand. If he didn’t have a beer in his hand then he wasn’t ever happy. And last but not unheard of was my cousin who was fresh out of jail. He was caught stealing a car a couple years back, the tenth of a big steal for him. The rough past made him the deadliest person in the family. I hated him and sometimes wished he’d just stay in jail forever. “Well if it isn’t big Elia Smith?” My cousin said with his sharp and unwelcome tongue. “You look like you’ve done well.” “Well it’s called hard work I guess.” Giving him a grin as I tried to walk away. I wasn’t even two feet from where I stood at before when I heard the phone ring from the kitchen. “Can you get that Elia?” My mother asked. I walked through the shutter door that she had forever. Paint chipped on it and the white paint had dulled from the smoke infested house. The phone was just a few feet into the room when I grabbed it with my right hand, “Hello?” The other end was noisy and loud. I could barely hear anything that resembled a voice on the phone till one small rough voice came through the ear piece, “Hello Miss Elia.” Gasping loudly through my bitter lungs I knew what and who it was. “How did you get this number?” “I don’t think the question of how I got this number is the question you should be asking yourself. I think the question you should be asking is if your dear old Uncle is ok.” He said, giving a small and evil laugh at the end. My heart raced and with every word that I said, I just knew, deep down inside, that my family was going to walk in and find out. Find out that I was a failure to the very name that I held. “Please?”, begging him with the small tear that rolled down my eye. “Please don’t hurt him.” “Oh I’m not going to hurt him. No?”, pausing on the second sentence that came out of his mouth. “No, but if you don’t do like I told you to then he will for surely die.” As the tear came down my face, the door came flying open. It was the very cousin that I hated so much. He gave a giant laugh towards me, the laugh that he prides himself for giving. But the laugh faded faster then it came up. “You ok Elia?” I gave him a nod, telling him that everything was alright but they always tell me that my eyes tell a lie every time. “So just a fair warning Elia, you better win at Wrestlecade or it will be the end of your prideful Uncle.” His voice stayed in my ear even after I heard the phone hang up. I just stood there, trying to keep the tears, the hurt, and the lost soul that I felt I was losing inside. I slowly hung up the phone, looking over at my cousin who brought the anger right to the top. “Ok. I might not be the most romantic or caring person in the world but I know when someone is hurting.” He said, walking up to me and grabbing me with his huge arms. “Tell me who it is and I’ll hurt them forever.” “Jim, you can’t.” Pressing away and trying to walk out of the room and away from everyone that I cared about but Jim wasn’t about to let me do that. Grabbing me by the arm, Jim pulled me right back against him and looked down at me. “I can’t what” I can’t care for you or I can’t do what I would like to do to maybe be more of a loving person. You tell me.” Jim never got angry around me but when he did I knew, like a light that was turned on deep in my mind, I knew I had to spill the beans. I felt the tears coming once again but I held them in as good as I could before spilling it. “I screwed up big time and now Uncle Johnson is going to die for it.” I couldn’t bare to look up and see his face and I knew for a fact he was going to be angry. I felt his heart beat a little faster but a small laugh came from his heart. A laugh? Why was he laughing? I pulled away a little bit to see his eyes and that was when it hit me. This was not right. How could he be laughing when I was in pain. How could he be laughing when Uncle Johnson was kidnapped. “STOP LAUGHING!” Screaming out with a hard slap across his face. “You make me laugh with your joke. Uncle Johnson is out of town on vacation for a fishing trip in Maine.” He said as he turned around, grabbing a can of beer that was on the top of the shelf. “You need to go to acting school because I almost believed you.” Fishing trip? No way. He was there, I know it. I saw him come out of that door and look at me right in the eyes. Jim walked out of the room and closed the door behind him, leaving me to stand in the room all alone. My thoughts of what to do were going through me. The phone rang again, making me jump a little before another voice shot through the small door from the living room. “Get that Elia?”, the voice of my mother said. “That might be the kidnappers.” She said as a huge amount of screams and laughs came from the same area. My eyes filled with frustration and anger, knowing the truth but not being able to get help with it. Though there was only one thing left for me to do. That was finish the job once and for all. I grabbed the phone up and placed it to my ear, listing to the voice on the other end as they talked away.
