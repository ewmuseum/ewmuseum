A Possible Solution

9 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 9, 2004 This was the biggest problem Mike Biddle had ever faced in his young life. He was used to attractive women falling out of their clothes at the mere mention of his name, never before had he been turned down and he had no idea how to handle rejection. However, he decided that all the women at the party last night were lesbians, how else could they have resisted him so easily? Mike sat in the library of his Beverly Hills home clad in his red silk pajamas with his initials monogrammed on the front pocket. His bare feet were propped up on the desk and despite the fact that he had just barely woke up his blond hair was combed perfectly. In his lap sat his little black book, he leafed through the pages trying to find the magic number that would help him satisfy his needs before he had to report to work. He found the perfect candidate once he came across the phone number for a woman named Angela. There were four stars next to her name meaning she was definitely the sort of woman Mike was looking for at this particular juncture. He picked up the phone on the desk and dialed the number. “Hello?” Said a feminine voice after a few rings. “Is Angela there?” Mike asked. “This is her,” the feminine voice replied, “what do you want Mike?” Mike smiled; it was the first time in over twenty-four hours that a woman had recognized him. “What do you say tonight the two of us go out on the town?” He asked with a hopeful voice. “I can’t,” Angela said, “I have to wash my hair tonight.” Mike’s dark blue eyes became wide with anger once he heard Angela’s reply. “Your hair barely goes down to your shoulders,” Mike said, “how the hell will it take you all night to wash it?” “It just will,” she replied. “Bullshit! Tell me the real reason,” Mike said through clenched teeth. “Well,” Angela said, “aren’t you married to that psycho girl now?” She asked, making a reference to Hillary Watson. Mike rolled his eyes, an action that would have annoyed Angela to no end if the two were speaking to each other in person. “No I’m not married to her,” Mike said, “everybody knows that I didn’t marry her and I know that’s not your excuse! Tell me the real reason!” “Fine,” Angela sighed, “it’s just not fashionable to be seen with you anymore Mike. You’re practically a nobody now.” “What the hell do you mean I’m a nobody now?” Mike demanded. “You haven’t been on TV for months now. Do you really expect me to go out with someone whose fifteen minutes are up?” Angela asked in an accusing way. “Damn it!” Mike said before he hung up the phone in disgust. He rose from his chair and began to pace around the library. How could she say those sort of things to him? Surely he wasn’t some one hit wonder that would never amount to anything more than some trivia question on Jeopardy. He needed to find a way to fix this problem and make himself fashionable once again. As Mike began to think of a plan to regain his fame the phone rang. He hoped it was Angela calling back to say that she’d come to her senses and would make it up to him the proper way. “Hello?” Mike said into the phone. “Lad,” said the familiar Scottish accent of William Bastafar, “I have wonderful news for you!” “What is it William?” Mike asked; careful not to sound too excited. “You’re scheduled in the opening match of Global Wrestling’s next pay-per-view. You’ll be fighting seven others and when you’re one of the last two left standing you’ll be given the opportunity to have a contender match for the Television Title!” William said as he tried his hardest to make this sound great. He had really been trying to get Mike into the World Title match but Global Wrestling’s ownership group wouldn’t listen to reason. “They expect me to lower myself and compete for a Television Title?” Mike asked with a slightly disgusted tone. “Just win the bloody thing and once you get your name out there again I’m sure they’ll give a shot at a higher title lad,” William said, his patience sounded as though it was wearing thin. Mike paused a moment and allowed William’s words to sink in. Even though the belt was obviously beneath him the Television title would definitely help him get at least some of his fame back, at least enough of it so he could fix his little problem. “Fine,” Mike said, “tell them I’ll even be there early!” Mike hung up the phone and quickly exited the room so he could go get packed. It would only be a matter of time before he regained his fame now. -The End For Now-
