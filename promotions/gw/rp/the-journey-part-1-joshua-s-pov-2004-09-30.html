'The Journey' (Part 1/Joshua's POV)

30 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-30-04 Well its time once again for us to embark on a little trip ladies and gentlemen. On this trip we aren’t going to be stopping at any particular point. This time the rules have change for the story because this time we are going to tell the same part twice: Once with Joshua and the other time with Zack. In each part Joshua and Zack will be basically separated from one another unless the scenario dictates and if they are having a phone conversation in a segment of the story then only the part in which the person for whom the part revolves around will be heard. Now that we have a little bit of an understanding of how this story goes shall we get started? Its about 3:30 in the afternoon in New York City and the city’s citizens were walking around Times Square and observing the building of the Liberty Tower which was being erected in place of the fallen World Trade Center. The people were also going to different stores and buying things such as clothes, music, food and other things which made for some interesting transactions sometimes because if you aren’t from New York City let me tell you that the people there sometimes don’t speak English so making deals sometimes require some ingenuity and cunning. “WHAT THE ---- DO YOU MEAN THAT I OWE $8.20”! I ONLY BOUGHT A HOT DOG AND A SODA!” Some unlucky patron yelled at the vendor whom happened to be wearing a white turban and pretended to be speaking broken English’it didn’t work. “I WANT MY MONEY OR YOUR ASS IS GOING TO THE BBB DO YOU HEAR ME”!” The patron continued screaming until one passerby paid the money to the vendor and stuffed the hot dog in the customer’s mouth! Of course this happened about 5 minutes from where are subject of this section happened to be standing and Joshua just shook his head in disbelief and not because of what the patron did but because he knew the vendor personally and was kind of disheartened by what he had done! “Pedro” Joshua began as he walked up to the hot dog stand and looked at the burly 30something vendor in the eyes before continuing “you didn’t just scam that young lady out her money now did you?” Joshua asked dressed in a white “GW” T-shirt, Arizona Blue Jeans and black “laceless” Timberlands and of course the vendor KNEW he had to be honest with Joshua. “I’m sorry Mr. Curtis but you know I have to feed my family so I have to make money with the only food I knew how to sell at the age of 13!” Pedro told Joshua and the “Connection” member just shook his head again in disbelief, as he knew that Pedro was single and had no kids to support. “Pedro I’ve known you for a good number of years and I know that I taught you better than to lie to me or another customer but don’t worry I won’t tell anyone about this just give me a large Coke and I should be good.” Joshua told Pedro and he did as asked by the younger “Connection” member and when he handed Joshua his beverage he told him that it was on the house and Joshua walked off towards his bus stop because well he didn’t take his car for the day! “Mr. Curtis wait up!” A young kid wearing an “OSC” T-shirt and jean shorts said as he ran up to Joshua with a pen and paper in his hand. “What’s up son?” Joshua asked as the kid stopped in front of him to catch his breath and Joshua could tell the kid ran really fast to catch him before he got too far away so he was more than willing to do whatever the kid asked before he boarded his bus. “Could’you”.sign?.this’for me?” The kid asked in between breaths and Joshua took the pen and paper from the boy and signed his name to it before handing it back to the young boy and Joshua could tell he had made a young man’s day which is all he wanted to do since he escaped his contract with 4WW. After Joshua handed the pen and paper back to the boy the bus came and Joshua boarded it with his correct change and took his seat right behind the bus driver. “Sir are you Joshua Curtis?” The bus driver asked and Joshua smiled sweetly and said “Yes I am darling and I was just signed by Global Wrestling a couple of days ago as was my darling Zack Johnson.” which got a good portion of the bus cheering Joshua because of the fact that now one of the more elite feds in the world had gotten the talent of the “Connection” but at the same time it worried the bus drive a little bit as his expression changed with the news that he just heard. “Joshua you DO know about GW right” This isn’t the fake shit like WWE and WCW but its more like the Japanese style with people getting cut up, blown up, spit on’you get the idea right?” The bus driver asked and Joshua nodded before speaking “Oh honey you don’t have to worry about me because I’ve been there and done that. I have wrestled in Japan, England and even Mexico in a mask so I know all about the different styles and cultures that I am dealing with but this time I have Zack going with me.” Joshua told the bus driver and one of the passengers tapped Joshua on the shoulder to ask Joshua a question so Joshua obliged and turned around to face his questioner. “Yes darling what is it?” Joshua asked the man sitting behind him and while there was a brief blush on the man’s face he regained his composure long enough to ask Joshua his question “I’m sorry to disturb you sir but you mentioned Zack and while we all know that Zack is your boyfriend he isn’t exactly here tonight. Where is that nice man that helps you baby-sit?” The passenger asked Joshua and Josh was nice enough to answer that question for a fan “Zachary is away on a personal matter. His mother is sick in the hospital in San Francisco and he needs to go see her so I told him to go take care of business and he has assured me that he will be back in time for Evolution this week.” Joshua told the passenger and in time too as he was getting to his stop in a matter of seconds. “This is your stop Josh. Take care and maybe I’ll see you at your match next week.” The bus driver told Joshua and the “Connection” member smiled and waved at the rest of the bus as he got to the driveway of his home in New York City, which as Joshua just told you is empty because of Zack’s personal matter at home in San Francisco. When Joshua got in the house he took the bag that he had from the Virgin Megastore and placed it in front of the big high definition TV that Zack bought for the house and you’d swear it was an impulse buy because the fact was that Joshua like the TV that they had but at the same time Joshua was happy for when they watch the Bears and 49ers games so that they could see more of the field! * Oh dear Oh dear Oh dear * Joshua thought as the realization of the fact that Joshua had the house all to his self was just setting in and truthfully he didn’t like it because of the fact that that means that Zack wasn’t there. Joshua hadn’t been alone in a house since Zack moved in with him and to say that this was eerie is an understatement but at the same time he got to watch some movies that admitted Zack hated and there were movies that Joshua didn’t watch so they had that compromise in place. As Joshua is going through some of the music that he bought the phone rings and of course since Joshua is the only one there he HAS to answer it as a rule that they have. ??Connection? residence this is Joshua Curtis speaking how may I help you?” Joshua said to the individual on the phone as he did every time he answered the phone and the voice on the other end was a rather pleasant shock, for it was his former client Juri Arisugawa! “Hey Joshua how are you doing on this fine day?” Juri asked her former employer to start the conversation and Joshua of course knew how to answer this “Oh you know just trying to keep from going nuts for the couple of days that Zack is going to be gone.” Joshua told Juri and this worried her greatly as was indicated by the tone that she used in the response. “Josh honey is something wrong with you and Zack?” Juri asked in an almost praying tone that made Joshua concerned for his female friend. “No we are going strong Juri but Zack’s mother is in the hospital and she isn’t feeling well so Zack went to go see her for a couple of days which is understandable you know?” Joshua asked Juri and she nodded on the other end of the phone before speaking again “So I hear you are going to a place called “Global Wrestling”. Is Zack going to come with you to the fed or are you going along this time?” Juri asked and Joshua couldn’t help but laugh a little on the other end as Juri had to KNOW the answer before she asked the question! “Zack is coming to Evolution with me and we had to rename our team for the benefit of the fed because of the fact that a team once did a “Old versus New” before and didn’t go over so well so I agreed to change the name a little bit so as to please a lot of the fed beforehand’oh and I kind of made a new friend in GW already before I set foot in Global Arena! His name is Ken Partridge Jr.” Joshua told Juri and she smiled as she knew that that outgoing personality of his was going to get him places in feds if he found the right one to go to. “That’s great Joshua’listen I gotta go sweetie. Tina is making a glazed ham with sweet potatoes and hash browns and I don’t want to keep her waiting so au revoir!” Juri said as she hung up the phone the same time as Joshua and all this time of talking about his love eternal made him decide to call Zack before he settled down to relax even though hearing his voice was relaxing enough! Joshua dialed Zack’s cell phone and he heard the infamous “Two Worlds” song playing as his phone ran and he was greeted by Zack’s voice on the other end! “Hey baby’yeah we got promoted to GW and I found out that I got a match at this coming show but who its against I don’t know”.yeah I told Juri about your mother and she told me to tell you that she sends her love’right I know to sleep on your side of the bed when you aren’t here silly’mm hmm I’ll talk to you in a couple of days hun’Love ya!” Joshua said as he hung up his phone and then decided to cook some hot dogs for himself as he is the only one there and after a couple of minutes the food was ready and Joshua went to watch Sakura Diaries which he made it through about the first 4 episodes of the “Complete Collection” before he zonked out from exhaustion and not even he knows what the exhaustion was from. 4 hours later which is about approximately 8:45 and Joshua decided to go to bed early as tomorrow would be a long day as he had to go do “Cold Pizza” for ESPN2 tomorrow and that was going to be fun especially considering he was going to get to get interviewed by Skip Bayless and Woody Page! Want to know how that went? Find out when we return to Joshua’s section in 2 days!
