:| The Left Hand Of God |:

28 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5.28.04 ---- :| Sitting low in a corner, blood dripping from my wrists. Ill stay here forever, suffer through all this. Pain running through my veins, blood leaking to the floor. I will never be the same, I can't take life anymore. I see a shadow come over me, as I crouch down again. You look down and see, all the pain I'm in. I look at you and cry, as you bend down to me. You wipe the tears from my eyes, and stare down at me. A tear rolls from your eye, as you lift me from the floor. You press your lips to mine, as we cry some more. Im dying here with you, as you cry on my face. All that I can do, is smile in my tears place. The tears and blood are smudged, on my smiling face. Because I finally feel loved, we died in the same place. I died a tragic death, with a smile on my face. |: ?| The Left Hand Of God |? :| A lit red candle in hand, the anti-Christ priest stood before the little girl again. Looking down upon her with blessing in mind, the priest poured anointing oil over her head and anointed her with corrupt sanctification. |: 'Yes! Bath in the fiery lakes of hell and cleanse your sins! Let death consume you and open your mind to logic. Let Lucifer simplize the melodramatic context in the Christian bible and reveal it for the fraud it is!' Passion bursting with each word, the preacher enunciated to the eccentric general sitting before him. :| Une Toile D'Araignee lied still on the floor. Her eyes rolled back, possessed. A gong drowned the crowd in a massive sound wave that soon died in the vortex of passed sounds, as more red candles were lit around a tub repleted with crimson ancestry. An eerie, scarey feeling manipulated the priest's face to strike a chord inside of Itagaki. And as if the priest's eyes sent a trescending black spirit seeking the throat of Itagaki's soul with thorny hands, the priest fetched Itagaki's mind. |: 'The Inhumane Puppet. Akin to Dr. Frankenstein's creation, you are a tool of the devils'. Your pain so ripe, your ignorance so bliss.' Peaching on, he held Itagaki's attention in custody, 'Like a sinful child, your evil so innocent. A tortured saint: Held in imprisonment by God's Commandments; tucked warm under an angel's wing.' :| Spoken unholy scriptures echoing through Itagaki's blank mind and taunting his deadened spirit, these words filling Itagaki will dreams he'll never want to see. |: 'Shallow thought, skimming alone the cortex. Numbness crawls over dull eyes. There is sadness so brittle it crushes bones. Dreams do not grow in bitter wasteland. How much soul is left in a shadow? Maybe prayers are lost within honeycombs. Despair is wrung out of tears. To lie would be kindness. Truth slices thin ribbons of muscle from bone. Hollow question of Why? Secrets are a flush hand held in death. The emotional disembowelment every day.' Reading from the bible of Hell, the priest re-directed back to Itagaki. 'Apathy... that is your life... isn't it? You see blindly and breathe the sweet air of the matrix but suffocate in reality. I envy you.' :| As if his icy eyes hit a mental glacier, they sunk as Itagaki lowered his head allowing his bleach white hair to screen his face. As the sun suffered from depression in the sky bringing forth the inevitable night, rays of golden crisp light illuminated Itagaki's intangible dark aura; a beautiful disaster. Another gong struck hearing as only candles gave support in the benighted room of Lucifer's shadow. Summoned, Itagaki stood and traveled down the valley of the shadow of death up to the crimson tub. |: 'The Bringer of Eternal Damnation, welcome to Judgement Day. Today you die; today you're re-born.' And without anything further, the priest bowed Itagaki's head in the blood that cleansed his sins and devoured his soul. Then the shadow of Akuma crawled up the back of Itagaki before the final words he will ever truly understand left an everlasting message in his erased memory, 'Pendragon must die.'
