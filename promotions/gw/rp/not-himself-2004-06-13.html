Not Himself...

13 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/13/04 About 24 hours away from Evolution, Donny J McNasty sits in the bar by himself, it's a slow night at the Hyatt Regency Hotel Bar. Could be because it's a Sunday night, what ever the case it's just Donny there by himself with female bartender. Donny is slowly sipping on his Guinness and Jack on the rocks. The music in the background is that of Frank Sinatra,'My Way'. A somewhat of a sad look is on the face of Donny, for the most part he's a happy guy, but he does have his down days and this would be one of them. The Bartender walks up to him and asks if he needs anything else. Donny just looks at her and shake his head now with a slight smile on his face, the first time he's smiled in a few hours. He looks at the name tag of the girl and it reads, Alexis, strange irony, that's also the same name of the girl he has a crush on. The bartender named Alexis looks at him with a look of concern and begins to talk to him. 'Are you alright sir, is something wrong?' the young lady asked. 'I'm alright I guess, a little nervous, a little sad, a little anxious, just a mix of emotions. Donny responded to young ladies simple question. He takes another sip of his Guinness and puts it down. And begins to ask the young bartender a question. 'Let me ask you something? Have you ever been at a point in your life where, you know the right answer to a question but when it comes time to do that right answer or practice it you just can't?' The young girl looks at him and the rolls her eyes up into her head to think about the question. She resonds by saying. 'You know, I'm not really sure that's a tough question. If you want explain what you mean exactly?' Donny looks at her and shakes his head in agreement, takes a sip of his Jack Daniels on ice, and begins to explain to her what he means. 'Well it's like this, of late, I been kinda depressed, I am usually in control of my emotions but of late, they been all over the place. You know what I mean?' The young girl looks at him with sympathy and nods yes to Donny's question. Donny takes another sip of Guinness and continues on. '[detail withheld] I almost don't know how to get rid of it.' The young girl looks at Donny with more sympathy, and begins to talk. 'You know I have that feeling too sometimes, my Dad was in a car accident last year and was killed in it. He went to run an errand for my Mom, I was going to do it, but he said he wanted to, since it was a nice day outside. And then about 30 minutes later, that's when we got the call. The young lady begins to tear up as she continues with her story. 'The police called us and told us that he was hit head on by a lady on her cell phone who obviously wasn't paying attention. They said he was killed instantly, so he really didn't feel it. He may not have felt pain, but his family sure did, especially for a long time. I still carry this feeling of guilt about it. It should have been me that was killed in the accident, not my Dad. We didn't get a chance to say goodbye to him, or I love you.' Donny who has a tear in his eye, takes another sip of Guinness and begins to console her the best that he can. 'You know, Alexis, That is an all too common feeling. I always wondered if there was something I could have done, something I could have said, but the answer has always been no. I found the one answer that fits best, but I still haven't been able to accept yet. That answer being, that the time you spent together is truly the best thing you could have ever done for that person. That in heaven they know you still love them and that they can still hear you' The young girl Alexis looks at Donny with a half smile. 'You know that makes a lot of sense and it's kinda made me feel better' Donny smiles back. 'You know the only thing that truly heal the broken heart is time. It takes time to get your life back to normal, or close enough to normal. You're Dad, [detail withheld] aren't with us in the physical sense, but in our hearts and they still can have contact with you. You just got to keep an open heart and ear' Alexis looks at him with an even bigger smile. Donny begins to stand up and gets out his wallet, to pay for the check. The young Alexis shakes her head no and tells Donny,'It's on the house Mr. McNasty.' Donny smiles in shock that she did know his name after all. 'So you know who I am after all?' The young Alexis responds with sly smile. 'Mr. McNasty I know more than I let on sometimes.' 'Well I'll tell you what I'm gonna do, if you want, tomorrow night I can give you some tickets to Evolution, and I'll give you my phone number if you ever want to talk about things' Donny said to the girl which seemed to bring a large smile across her face. Alexis replied 'Mr.McNasty that would be awesome thank you very much.' Donny writes down his information on a napkin and hands it to the young girl. 'Well it's no problem consider yourself a friend of mine.' 'Thank you, good luck with your match tomorrow night and Alexis Steele' the young girl replies to Donny. Donny and the young Alexis have a friendly hug. Donny leaves the bar with a smile on his face knowing he at least temporarily has gotten things off his chest and cleared his mind for the night ahead of him tomorrow. Maybe then Donny will start to be more himself...
