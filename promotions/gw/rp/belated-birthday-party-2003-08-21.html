Belated Birthday Party

21 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/21 A lone black Porsche Carrera GT Turbo glides up the long tree lined driveway leading to the sprawling Zero Estate. The Porsche’s engine purrs gently as it’s tires hug the twists and turns of the path with it’s headlight illuminating the road ahead. Once in front of the house the car rolls to a stop just inches away from the back bumper of a black Mitsubishi Eclipse. The headlights dim slightly before turning off completely just as the driver side door of the car opens allowing the bass heavy music that accompanies Xzibit’s lyrics on “Hit U Where It Hurts” to blare outside the vehicle. ?When shit happens stomp and smash it, on and crackin?, who want action? Pistol packin?, fuck relaxin?, hit you where it hurts. Make you feel it, can’t conceal it, if it bleeds then I can kill it. Come and get it, custom fitted, hit you where it hurts.? A white patent leather Jordan XI hits the ground next to the car and out steps a rather surly looking Deacon Holmes wearing a pair of navy blue gym shorts which just barely grasp his hips, allowing his boxers to show, and a white tanktop. One of his hands is bandaged with a light cast, most likely due to the conclusion of last week’s Evolution, the other clutches a small cell phone. The wind picks up a little causing Holmes? dreads to hang in the air as he makes his way to the front door after shutting the door and adjusting his navy blue hat that reads “The Final Flash” in white letters. Deacon jogs up the short set of stairs in front of the large double doors of Zero’s house, holstering his cell phone halfway up. Once at the door Holmes takes a deep breath and shakes his head from side to side as a result of the royal bitching out he recently received over the phone from Christina McCoy, whom he by the way, hung up on. Deacon knocks at the door to gain entry but to no avail, he hears muffled voices coming from inside the house that cause him to press his ear to the door. After a moment of silence the door flies open causing Holmes to stumble forward and only catch his balance by grabbing onto the shirt of the man who opened it; John Zero. After regaining his balance Holmes looks around to notice that the house is completely dark, and silent, it would appear that his previous notion of people being in the house was incorrect. Just then Zero flips a light switch, illuminating the entire house, and revealing the throngs of people all hiding behind various items of furniture who all stand simultaneously and scream “SURPRISE.” Holmes looks around nervously for a moment before turning to Zero after noticing a banner that reads “Happy 19th Birthday Deacon!” “It’s not my birthday...” states Deacon matter of factly. “I know, it was the seventh, but did you have a party?” implies Zero, taking Holmes under his arm as they walk into the main room of the house with “This Is My Party” by Fabolous blasting throughout the house, each bass hit shaking the doors and windows. “Well I...” stammers Deacon while still absorbing the situation. “Exactly, well now you’re having one. This is your party.” explains Zero in between sips of a scotch on the rocks. “This is my party, stop by if you want to, ya’ll could stay home but why would you want to” This is my party so get fly if you want to, get high if you want to, cause I know you want to. Put your hands up as high as you want to, and if it feels good scream ay, ay, ay, yo!? “Shit, can’t argue with that. Where are the drinks?” inquires Holmes, who’s spirits seem to be on the rise. Zero points ahead to the opposite end of the house at two open doors leading out to the pool area that a large group of women currently occupy. “Damn, since this is my party are those mine too!”” asks Holmes excitedly while ogling the girls outside. “Sure, just as long as I don’t get to “em first.” says Zero walking away. “But it’s my party!” exclaims Deacon Zero half turns around and replies “But it’s still my house.” That said, he turns back around and continues where he was going. Zero takes a seat on one of the couches off in a cornes with a small table in front of him. As soon as he sits down three bathing suit clad women join him and begin filling shot glasses with the contents of the bottle of Jack Daniels sitting on the table. Zero continues drinking nonchalantly as the women rub him down and giggle to each other, although he doesn’t smile, he’s obviously content. Deacon shrugs his shoulders and continues through the house taking note of the current female to male ratio, which at the moment has the women seriously outnumbering the men; a definite good thing. Huge speakers sit in each corner of the room pumping out bass and causing it to circulate through the house. In addition to the bar out by the pool, another rests near the sofa that John Zero and company currently occupy. Lights of various color, shape, and size spin around the inside of the house causing it to look something like an epileptic nightmare. Scantily clad women of every nationality inhabit each inch of the house, some wearing bathing suits, some wearing birthday suits, all of them drinking. “Things are definitely looking up” mumbles Holmes to himself. He then takes off his tanktop and tosses it on the ground before stepping out onto the pool deck. Immediately all eyes are on him due to his albeit small but extremely chiseled frame and myriad of tattoos. Two girls approach him, each grabbing an arm, and walk him over to the bar. Four Hours Later The pool area is now empty save the colony of red plastic cups and various forms of debris littering the deck. John Zero strolls out onto the patio under the full moon scotch on the rocks in hand only to find Deacon Holmes strewn about the floor like much of the patio furniture. With a sigh he sets his drink down on top of a table and pulls Holmes up off the ground before hanging him over his shoulder. Zero walks back into the house after mumbling “Looks like someone had a good time.”
