The Void...Bob's Foundations?

6 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-6-2004 As he crouched in the shadows, what he was about to do finally came home to him. Sure, he had killed before. Numerous times the blades that he wielded tasted the blood of another man, yet there was always a reason. Always some wrong that the enemy had done upon him or his people. Aye, many a fool met death on the blade of Venneman Pendragon, yet, in his book, all had proved themselves deserving of the privilege. Some were killed to protect a kingdom’Others were cut down because they sought him in battle, wishing to test their own mettle against this blademaster. He could remember times when he had to resort to tactics such as this; hiding in the shadows, awaiting the best time to strike an enemy down. Times when he had been driven by force from his kingdom by a strong army. This time, however, was different. This time he had been set to destroy another being by someone else. His targets were chosen now not because of the wrongs they had done him, but rather the wrongs they had done in Bob’s eyes. Who was Pendragon to argue? After all, Bob was God! Any apprehension that Pendragon felt would soon fade away. The killing void he sought, and in the Void, things did not touch him the way they would touch a normal man. This was a place where emotion seemed distant. Wrapped in himself, the Void allowed him to be cold and observant. Pain touched him not, and different feelings such as anxiety and fear merely passed by. Time stretched out here, and he truly had the mindset to think. The Void was good for that, amongst other things? “Bob’Mine friend” Mine commander? Sometimes it is hard to tell which. He came to me in a time of mine weakness and brought me back to strength, but sometimes I wonder at his one dimensional motives. Why, the last time I saw him?.” ************************FLASH******************************** A nearly empty street. A few cars drive by now and again, yet few people walk. Of those who do, two men really seem to stick out. Bob, in his snow white suit, with his kindly face and white beard, strides along purposefully, while Pendragon, clad in a white shirt and black breeches, sword belted on beneath a black cloak, appears thoughtful. They walk together, and People move out of their way; the few people actually about. With seemingly no real destination, the men just seem to be walking? “Venneman’As my emissary on this planet, there are a few things that you need to be made aware of. Certain rules that you need to stick to lest I have to destroy you utterly.” Bob says the last with a straight face, and Pendragon appears a bit taken aback? “Destroy me” I asked not to be enlightened by thee, and I asked not to be thy tool. Now thee prattle on about rules that must be followed so as not to be destroyed? And when last we spoke, I believe that thee revealed to me, amongst the little thee would actually reveal, that I am not thy creation. That thou doubts that thee can destroy me at all, even should the mood take thee.” Bob looks at Pendragon in irritation, and Pendragon spreads his hands?“That is not to say I shan’t listen to reason. I am merely saying that if obeying thy rules will cost me, that thy rules shall be broken. Fear not, my man!” He claps Bob on the back and smiles?“I am thy weapon. Yet I am a weapon who thinks upon his own?” Bob frowns as he mulls this over. He knew from the start that Pendragon would be difficult to control. This was one of the reasons he was chosen. He could improvise and think on his feet. “You are right. I try to live in the past when even the enemy followed certain rules. Perhaps to defeat his emissaries, I need someone who isn’t afraid to get a little dirty now and again. Anyhow, the rules, or guidelines you might say, are simple. Each man has sin. The greater the sin, the greater my knowledge of the fact of said sin. Some deserve death for their transgressions. These are those whose souls have been to long with the shadow. Some, however, are not dark’Some have merely been led from the light recently. It is these that you must use care with. It is these souls that I know not?” Pendragon gives him a sidelong look and shakes his head? “I thought that God was supposed to know everything. An omnipotent being and all?” Bob grimaces as Pendragon eyes him, yet he does offer up an answer? “As I’ve told you before, there is no way I can see everything all the time. I am no Santa Claus. I am no children’s tale. I am pulled to great atrocity, and am able to track the transgressor of such. Your job is to use what I tell you. I am merely informing you that on the tasks I set for you that you might run into souls that need saving, rather than severing. I know you are skilled at the latter, but how good at the former shall you be” Sometimes a soft had is needed to teach people the error of their ways?” Pendragon chuckles and shakes his head?“Like the time when thee flooded the world and left but a remnant of those that thee had created” Aye, the Bible is full of stories where thee used a soft hand?” Bob shakes his head and laughs as well. He gives Pendragon a wry look?“Perhaps you should not believe everything that you read, Venneman. Though I am the god of the Bible, things in the book are twisted from how they should have been put down. I am the god of all divine writings, and none of them have gotten me right. You must remember that these books were written by men. These books were not written by my hand. Common sense would dictate that people question.” Pendragon gasps and looks at Bob questioningly?“But does the bible not contain the rules by which many a society was built” Why would thee allow it to hold such sway?” “It is hard to make people disbelieve something that they place their entire faith in. And, after all, the Bible has some very good things also. The Ten Commandments and all’I never liked Moses, although he didn’t lie. The things he did he DID do on his own. I only listened to the man, I never spoke. But embellishment you see” There are many instances such as that in the Bible and the other works that were written to give people an idea of how to live. Many of it is common sense. Don’t kill, don’t steal and the like. People just needed it held over their heads because generally speaking, if man is left to himself he will almost always do the wrong thing. He cannot help it. Wrong is easier and man is lazy. But we are getting away from the topic at hand?” Bob reaches into his inside breast pocket and produces a parchment of sorts. He hands it to Pendragon who tucks it beneath his own cloak? “Wise that you would not go about reading that thing here. On it is ten of the most vicious emissaries of the Dark One who walk the earth. Their names and their transgressions are writ upon that. A picture accompanies each name?” Pendragon gives him a sidelong glance and Bob smiles?“Heck, even I use SOME technology. You should try it.” Pendragon just shakes his head and Bob smiles?“These are the souls who must be cleaved. There is no saving them, no place in heaven for them. As they are, they wreak havoc an influence events in ways that they must be stopped from doing. They cut life spans short and kill innocents. They should not have this power?” Pendragon nods but the thoughtful look never leaves his eyes. Bob prattles on as the scene?”””” **********************************FLASH****************************** Back in the shadows and the calmness of the Void, Pendragon runs the scene over and over in his mind. To kill without remorse, even one who deserved a foul death, was not easy. Each man ever run through upon his blade was remembered by Pendragon, and each face sometimes screamed at him in his dreams. No, it wasn’t easy to forget when you took all a man had and left him nothing. This time, he waited upon a raper and killer of women. The kind of man that one should feel joy at killing. The trouble was that Venneman had a tough time taking it on the word of someone else that this man deserved death. He had never seen him commit these crimes, and though he would surely feel them as he killed him, it was tough to get started. Things seemed so easy in his other life’That life where one on one combat was the mantra. GW. He was finally making a name there. Defeating some of the company stalwarts would do that for you. He was a champion, and had a chance to be a champion twice over?.If only he could best Mcnasty and Brick. He had beaten Brick before, but this time his mind was not wholly in it. This time, the weight of death would be upon him, a third opponent that showed less mercy than the first two could dole out’And yet, a smile crept across his face as he thought of the fans, of the battle, of the pain. It was what he loved to do’Not even Bob could take that from him?”.. ~TO BE CONTINUED~
