Memories Past,The way to oblivion

19 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/19/04 You can't fathom what I am. You don't realize the pain or the suffering that has gone on in my life. And you dont know how I wish it to stop suffering. My addiction.....my curse....The trouble with knowing what is to become of you is you have to live with the consequences. Most days I smile at what I am. I take great joy in the pain and the anguish of others....as it is done to my will and for my continued existence. In this world, many from the realm of the dead all have one thing in common.....they each died by my hand....they knew death before there time because of me. I have been for so many years the angel of death...an angel of mercy to those creatures....those monkeys that think they are survivors in this kill or be killed life. None can know....my pain...and what i'd do to release it....I sit there, in my pit in silence, the chair resembling an ancient egyptian throne, I rub my hands down the chair feeling its past.....remembering where I have been....where I started....where I am.....and where I am destined to go. I sit in the darkness of my pit meditating. Each day of my life has been a stuggle...each day a fight to keep what I had...and an even bigger fight to get what I wanted. I hear the words in which I have heard all my life run around in my head.....monstrosity....evil incarnate....the way to eternal damnation...I smile at those kind words for they are what keep me killing....keep me destroying....fuel me to know what is ahead....What if this is not to be? What if this life created.....what I am is not to be as it is....not to death....not to be evil, but to show the way to eternity? Show a way of light to those who have lived in darkness way to long? Thinking of another possible way that my life could have went....where it could go...what have I become?....what have I always been? Why have I been this way? What could have changed?.....I made myself like this when I could have chosen another way. A way so much different than the way my life has been. I am the reason my soul is damned and me alone......It is only through death and despair shall they ever know what I must go through everynight....everynight dying a little more....every night letting the monster inside me take control....everynight losing a little more of me...they treat me as an animal and I play as their little lapdog....showing my tricks so that they applaud....going into matches with men and trying to destroy my opponent so I am claimed the victor....the romans did this as well and their society fell....now...the whole world plays these games.... I snap out of my thoughts realizing i'm sitting still on my throne in my pit. I stand wearing only a pair of black silk pajama bottoms. I make my way up the stairs to my mansion.....the night has come and tonight I have an event sponsored by Soma Corp. I have my attendants dress me in the finest suit I own. A black suit made of egyptian silk, mandarin collared and dress boots. This suit especially tailored for me. I look in the mirror and see what I look like and smile....the monster behind the suit. By the time I am ready so is Cassandra, Sage, Cherish and Maximillian. Cassandra adorned in a long black silk dress to match my own suit, Sage wearing an armani suit I had tailored for him, Cherish, a red dress and Maximillian wearing a royal blue silk mandarin collared suit. We make it to the large gala, people greet our limos with pictures, the media loving to see who Cassandra is, Sage signing autographs while standing there with Cherish. Movie stars everywhere eating the food and having conversations. We walk into the gala hall which was being held at Soma Corp, Texas branch and sit down at the main table. One of the men stands and gathers everyones attention. 'Today we are here to celebrate a great man. This man has given millions to charities and foundation and today this man has been nominated for the international brotherhood award, for being of service to all of you and many throught out the world. I give you Mr. Leinad Soma.' I look at all them as they stand and cheer for me. Cassandra kisses me on the cheeck as I stand. I give a slight nod and everyone cheers harder. Finally after a few minutes the crowd dies down once again. 'Today is a great moment for me, but I don't wish to talk about that today. Today I wish to talk about some very major things happening with Soma Corp. and myself. After today, I shall only be President of The Soma Corporation. Today I had the business over to my son Max. I know he is up to the challenge and you will get great things from him.' The audience applauds once again as Max stands and takes a bow. He sits and they quiet down once more eager to see what else I might say. 'Also dealing with business, two other businesses have opened under the Soma Corp name. Soma Corp, South America division, and something I like to call Vtv. Soma Corp, South America division will bring many jobs to the people of that region and also boost the economy. Second, Vtv will be a broadcasting networks for vampires. All vampire news shall be on this channel, whether they like it or not....I already have many who have jobs with this channel and each are reliable, and respectable vampires in the vampire world.' The room stays quiet as not knowing what to say. People break out into an uproar of panic, dismay, and fear over one channel dedicated to my race......I smile... 'I know there are camera people here and I'm saying this on national television. If you think you can stop me then come do it. You know who I am talking to out there. As for you mortals you have no choice as into accepting it. As for your little award....you can I think today's insult would be to....shove it.' Everyone erupts, people screaming at me the media trying to ask questions, people leaving, pure chaos. Through all the chaos I for once show my fangs and hiss over the microphone that stands in front of me, my eyes flaring red, everyone stops, finally realizing what I am. As some of them make a rush for the door security stops them. 'As you can see....i'm not done yet. I want the world to look at my face, and I want you people here to realize this is no gimmick. This is not something I do for your entertainment when you see me wrestle. This is what I am. A monster has helped more people than you yourselves have. A monster has given you jobs, helped the poor, fed the hungry, what have you done? Humanity is a joke and you all make me sick. Tonight will be the start of something major as I well know for I have just urinated on mortals as a whole, at least the ones who have not shown there worth. I am tired living like I owe you all something...hince why control of Soma Corp is now under my son. But soon he too will realize.....you all are just food....Oh....but while I have this time I would like to talk about one other thing.....Josh....I'm coming for you...I do realize after all I have said this seems rather trivial but he too needs to realize I am a danger and a threat unlike any other. But my visciousness will wait till after our match where I must be his partner. He must take the word of a monster, but hey many of you have done it for years.' I stand and walk down from the table and out of the building with Cassandra at my side, Sage and Cherish not too far behind me. People dare not look me in the eye as I leave and I smile...Now they realize the creature I truly am. They realize evil can be at the heart of anything but, they don't care as long as they get what they want. True evil is having a blind eye to what is really going on and not doing anything about it. We get into the limo, leaving Max at the gala to clean up the mess I just made. We all sit in the limo in silence, Sage looks toward me 'I know Sage......it was my intent.....' He nods with an expression not really different from my own. Could it be finally he realizes? Cassandra smiling at us both. 'I have seen things in your future Leinad.' 'Tell me what you have seen Cassandra.' 'Together we shall move mountains. But our time will be short lived. Who is to say how short only the powers that be. I shall show you the way to greatness but that path will be a bloody one. A hardship that many would falter and die trying. I shall show you the way to oblivion and through me you will bring oblivion to the hearts of all. Your destiny is to become oblivion. The monster who of the inifinite nothing. You have slowly started to make this transistion already, all you need now is to fully except what it is that I have brought to you. The way to oblivion. Let nothing stop you from killing. Let nothing stop you from destroying. Let nothing stop you from crushing all those who stand in the way of your glory....your destiny...' For the first time in my long life I find my one true love and it came in the form of Cassandra. Her words filling me with inspiration. I look at her as tears begin to fall from my face, natural tears with no hint of blood in them. For the first time someone sees who I am and can live to tell about it. This woman who calls herself Cassandra has certain powers greater than my own. I feel her power in me making me more powerful than I have ever been. Sage and Cherish look on in silence as they see my transformation....remembering memories dating back to a time long forgotton.....to the beginning. We finally get back to the mansion and I head straight for the pit. Sage begins to follow me and I stop him. He nods as him and Cherish make their way to their room. I sit in the darkness of the pit in quiet thinking of my action and thinking of the past. I hear light footsteps coming down the steps and I already know its Cassandra. I sit there in my chair as she walks up and sits in my lap. She takes a hand running it down the side of my face as I look down. She takes my face and lifts it so that it looks her directly in the eyes and for the first time I feel fear.... 'You are older than you told me aren't you Cassandra?' 'Yes.' 'I feel as if I have met you before....have I?' 'Yes.' 'Where and when?' 'You met me in the beginning....I was another person....not human...not technically vampire but immortal none the less. I helped you see things clearly....as I have done now. You now see your destiny as you saw it those many many years ago. Time passed and you forgot but I was always there watching over you.....manipulating your life....bringing you to this point...I have always loved you Leinad and for that reason I have always protected you. You were the first of your kind and I am the only of mine but we share the same weaknesses or at least some of them. You stayed in the shadows for many years watching your race become what they are today....weak....decadent...hiding behind mortality...while you...you never hid the fact of what you were, to a degree. You always told everyone you were a vampire, thats what drew me to you. Your pride in being different....your pride in knowing you could have rivaled 'higher' beings. You always knew you were the incarnation of oblivion but didnt accept it...but now you and the whole world will know...and that makes me love you still. Do you know who I am now?' 'Yes I do Cassandra. You went by a different name then.....you went by the name....' She puts a finger to my lips before I can say the name and smiles as she still is looking me in the eye. My mind remembering certain situations that could have put an end to my exsistance but I was victorious....as if a force protected me. I put my arms around her and my head on her shoulder. She begins to stroke my hair like a mother to her infant child. 'I want to marry you Cassandra....soon...' 'I know...and it will happen.' 'How far will we take this?' 'The question Leinad is not how far but how hard are you willing to fight to become the way to oblivion.' 'I am willing to fight with all my blood sweat and tears.' 'There will be obstacles you know this.' 'I do....but I am a monster....the beast...the oblivion...I hold the keys to the great abyss and its time has come to be opened. It's time for a darkness to cover the land. It is time.' 'My darling Leinad, you finally understand. You are the way and none can stop you and those who try shall be destroyed.' I kiss her lightly and we sit there.....in the darkness....in the pit....in silence....
