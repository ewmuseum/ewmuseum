Signposts Chapter 4: Insurrection?

14 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14/10/04 Winters finally found the building that the GPS bleeped as his ultimate destination, after three hours of searching the city to accurately pin point the exact location of the co-ordinates entered by his “guide” back on the outskirts of Kansas. Not wanting to draw too much attention to himself, when he was sure it was the right building, he simply drove past it, just in case there were people outside watching and waiting for him to arrive. A self-indulgent, almost paranoid conclusion, he did concede, but he had no idea what he was about to face and so a precaution or two would not harm his cause any. The building itself was nothing spectacular. It was a smallish apartment building, that looked like it had only three floors to it, probably around fifteen or so apartments in total. The building looked old and blended well with the area that it was in. Upon driving around the area, Winters had already come to the conclusion that it wasn’t safest place to be out alone at night...but where is safe these days? Winters parked about three blocks away from the building and managed to find more detailed information on where he should be from the GPS. It took him a while to play with the thing, but he found the appropriate buttons easily enough. Apparently, he was also quite adept at electronic systems. He learned more and more about himself everyday. The plan was to get into the building unexpected, then surprise the people who undoubtedly already be inside. He suspected that the car had a tracking device of some kind, so that they would no when to be there and they would also know that he was in the area, but the people already waiting inside for him wouldn’t know exactly when he would come. Winters assumed that they would have someone staking out the front of the building for that job and he had just bypassed that early warning security, so that the people inside will not be suspecting him when he crashed their little party. That would give him the edge he would need to better them’he hoped. The exact location that GPS had, was apartment 12 on the third floor. Winters cut through the back alley’s of the various buildings, finally coming across the building he past earlier. He saw nothing but clotheslines and windows, no sign of any dodgy-looking characters. He quickly made his way to the alley adjoined to the apartment building and found the fire escape ladders. He climbed upon the top of a bright red dumpster and jumped up to the bottom rail of the fire escape. From there he used his impressive upper body strength to hoist himself upward, a bar at a time, until he reached the first floor platform. He continued up the fire escape until he came to the very last ladder, which led directly to the roof. He quickly scaled the ladder and found himself atop of the building roof. Up setting foot on the concrete he instantly knew that he wasn’t alone. He couldn’t define the feeling in so many words, he just knew. He made a mental note of this discovery. Just another ability he needed to explore once he found a little time for himself. Winters ducked low, and ducked behind a rather sizeable air duct funnel, out of sight from any potential enemies based on the roof. Whilst doing so, he had a quick glance around and saw nobody, however, there was a large section of the rood that was cut from my peripheral vision. Having no reason to distrust his instincts, he took cover anyhow. Upon calculating his plan of action, he came to the conclusion that there were extremely reasonable odds that whoever was involved in this thing would’ve posted somebody to watch the back of the building and there were even better odds that somebody had been placed on the roof. Winters heard the faint crackle of the a handheld radio and became immediately alert. He glanced around the corner of the ventilation pipe and saw a man in brown leather jacket pick up the radio and begin to speak into it. “No, sir. No sign of the target yet. I’ll let you know when I do!” The man said. Winters waited until he was sure that the communications channel was dead, before he broke cover and silently aimed himself at the man. The man saw him far too late and was unable to escape Winters tackle. “Oh shit!” The man blurted as both men fell to the floor in a heap, whereupon they immediately began to struggle. Eventually, Winters managed to gain the upper hand, and found himself on top of his opponent. He hit the guy with a couple of right hands, perfectly aimed at the his jaw. The man grew limp and Winters dismounted. He took the radio from the man’s pocket and stood on it, smashing it into pieces. He then headed toward the door of the roof, where - upon reaching it - he found that it was easy to open. Winters slipped inside and quietly shut the door behind him. After a couple of minutes searching, he found the appropriate apartment and kicked the door down. Upon doing so he quickly noticed the three people in the room, before launching himself into another attack. The one nearest to him, on his right, saw Winters coming and responded with a good left hook, which inevitably missed the intended target, and left an opportunity for Winters to strike and strike he did. A perfectly elbow to the nose, a knee to the mid-section and a double axe-handle to the back of the neck and the man was down. Before Winters could get his bearings back, the second man was on him, and actually caught him with a snide punch, which sent him reeling, only managing to stay upright by hitting the wall. Winters managed to compose himself, to see the second punch arrive, but this time was quick enough to dodge it. The man’s hand impacted with force into the wall, and Winters followed it up with an impressive roundhouse kick, which caught the man in the chest and sent him backwards, over the couch and onto the marble floor. Winters turned to the third person in the room and saw the beautiful brunette, who previously claimed to be his “guide” back in the diner on the outskirts of Kansas. Now, she stood there in front of him, with an odd-looking gun pointed at him. “Sorry.” She simply said, before pulling the trigger. The dart hit Winters in the shoulder and there was nothing for him to do, but look at it confused and when he grew incredibly drowsy, he realised that this wasn’t a murder attempt, perhaps she was someone from his past? Everything fell into the cold embrace of darkness.
