Wanted Dead or...

25 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/25/04 After ten hours and thirty-one minutes, my time alone was done, as I pulled into the small Econolodge that I had booked to be my living quarters during my stay here in Lincoln. Sure the cracked windows, and chipping paint on the outside of the building made it look like a dump, and that it hadn't seen any repairs in a good ten years, but that's what I needed right now. The run down Econolodge was hand picked because of how its appearence was. The run down Econolodge was hand picked because of the fact that it wasn't fancy, it wasn't extravegant, it was a little hole in the wall shit hole that called itself a hotel. I knew Josh and Ken would be attatched at the hip this entire trip. They always are. And I knew Ken would not stay anywhere but the best. This 'Elite' thing has really gone to his head. It consumes his entire being, and even though Josh is not wrapped up in it like Ken is, I know Josh doesn't mind it. He wants to be be 'Mr. Anticonformity' but he has no problem conforming to what Ken believes in and lives for. That is why I am here. It was a run down, cracked windows, chipped paint, hole in the wall shit joint that I began in. It was a run down, cracked windows, chipped paint, hole in the wall shit joint that I first beat Josh in. And it will be a rund down, cracked windows, chipped paint, hole in the wall shit joint that I stayed in when I beat Josh this time. As I stared into the rearview mirror, I could see that spark light behind the darkness in my blue eyes. The small flame I had such a long time ago had been rekindled. Soon it would grow, and engulf Memorial stadium. Soon it would grow out of control and engulf Josh............Soon. My eyes were glued in the glare I gave back in the rearview mirror, but slowly I pulled them away, and opened the door of my Expedition, slowly slithering my way out of the vehicle. Grudgingly I made my way into the small office looking, 'lobby'. There was a large bearded man sitting behind the desk, who had just taken a large handful of potato chips and stuffed them into his mouth. I could tell because of the small crushed particles of the food still hanging from the whiskers protruding from around his mouth. Hurrily the fat man chopped down on his chips, and swallowed as I made my way to the lone counter in the room. I could tell that he didn't chew his food enough by the way he struggled with his swallowing, and the way his eyes watered just before he greeted me. 'Hello sir.', he said with a slight scratch to his voice. I could only assume it came from the choking down of the large handful of chips he just had. Maybe one was still caught. Either way his voice still sounded a bit scratchy as I nodded in reply to his greeting, and signed the small piece of paper he pushed in front of me. I looked over the paper to see it was just a waiver that if anything happened, blah blah blah, they weren't to be held responsible. I didn't really read, and signed the paper. 'Room fourteen. Would you like a wake up call in the morning?', The man asked as he handed me the small silver key, on a large diamond shaped key chain. Again I only shook my head in response to the large man behind the counter. He goofily smiled at me as I slowly turned and headed back to the Expedition to retrieve my things before heading to my room and calling it a night. 'Have a g'd evening sir. Hope you enjoy your stay.', He said as I walked out the door, and back into the tiny parking lot. The sun was begining to warm the earth, as the morning continued to fade towards the afternoon. I thought about the ten hour drive that I just made, and I thought about the preparation I had made mentally as I drove throughout the night. As I pulled my lone, small, black gym bag out of the backseat, and grabbed my cell phone, I thought of how Josh was in his expensive, and cozy hotel. He was living up his dream week. Fancy helicopter ride. Tons of media salavating over his every move, and every word. He was being treated like a king upon his homecoming. From rags to riches, the story he has always dreamed of. The only thing that was missing, was the storybook ending. My mind raced, and my body filled with anger as I thought to myself of all the glitz and galore he was being consumed by. The anger that began to rise throughout my body was not anger derived of jealousy for all the attention he was getting however. No, the anger I was begining to feel grow throughout my body was one of disappointment and shame. 'Mr. Anticonformity' was selling himself out to the public who outcasted him. He was basking in their bandwagon attention. He was swimming in their sea of lies. Those things shamed me. But worse than that, he was concerned about them. He was worried about what would happen if he failed THEM. He was busy thinking about what would happen if he won in front of THEM. This disappointed me, because Josh had lost himself through these actions. Josh had lost his focus. Josh was not thinking about the match, and what he needed to do. Josh was not thinking about me and what I was going to do to him, or what he needed to do to me to come out of Wrestlecade the victor. No, Josh had forgotten what got him in the position he is in, and was concerning himself with that the people thought. The people who boo'ed him every night, and every arena he showed his face in. The people the threw things at him, and spewed out vile names in his presence. The same people that told him he would never amount to ANYTHING! But now he has forgotten all of that, and was looking to please them through his actions. Slowly I slung the small black gym bag over my right shoulder and headed towards my room. The anger inside began to slowly subside as I methodically strided to my room. My eyes lit up again, as my conscience found a way to ease the dilema it was in. As I reached my room door, it was was then I realized that the actions being made were not the actions of the man I knew, and the man I called my friend. My best friend would not succumb to those slime. My best friend would not hunger for the admiration of those hypocrits who pay to treat us like shit night in and night out. These actions were the actions of a stranger. At Wrestlecade I knew what I had to do. At Wrestlecade I would tear this stranger apart, and bring my best friend back to the living. At Wrestlecade I would show this new and unimproved Josh the error of his ways. I pushed the key into the lock and turned the doorknob. The door flung open, and I walked into the room. A lone bed was the only furniature in the room. It was just the way I remembered things, I thought to myself as I dropped the black gym back, that was slung over my right shoulder, to the floor, and headed towards the bed. I needed sleep. Even though I was not really that tired, my body did not need to fatigue itself after the ten hour drive I had just made, so I closed all the curtains, and crawled into bed. To slumber I would succumb. And soon after that Josh's dreams. A smirk grew across my face as I closed my eyes.
