Who's the bitch now?

15 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 15th, 2004. Standing in the center of an Octagon I bore a sneer across my face as I stared back at the probing eyes that surrounded me. Outside the ring there were easily ten to fifteen students of mixed age, sex, size and ethnicity. They watched me diligently as I stood inside the ring wearing only a pair of black Adidas gym shorts, black Nike trainers with knee and elbow pads of the matching color. My messy hair was plastered to my brow from the sweat of performing a strenuous workout in the ring and my breathing was beginning to grow rather heavy as the oxygen seemed to seep into my lungs at an ever slowing pace. Today has been a long day. It began at Eight A.M. when K.J. Tachikara, the head trainer of Global Wrestling gave me a call and requested my presence as a guest instructor in his non-sanctioned privately owned personal Wrestling academy. Loyal to K.J. and in his debt I had agreed to train his students…and here I was. Beginning at Six P.M. class generally ran a mere two hours yet tonight it had been extended per my request as I did my absolute best to push the students to and far beyond their limits. In what had now became three hours and twelve minutes I had ran the students through numerous drills until several had to exit the ring area due to exhaustion. The remaining group seemed to run on adrenaline and sheer will power alone. Looking out into the huddled crowd at ringside I snapped my fingers and K.J. quickly threw me a towel and a sports bottle filled with the Mountain Dew 'AMP' energy drink. Wiping the sweat from my face I tossed the towel into a nearby corner before blasting a drink of the refreshing liquid into my mouth. With my thirst quenched I exhaled loudly before my retinas returned to their occupation of scanning these students. I was the World Champion of Global Wrestling and they were mere wannabe's. I was the wolf…they were the sheep, and it was time to separate the weaklings and stragglers from the flock. With my eyes locked on a tall redheaded kid I pointed my finger at him. 'You…get in the ring, now.' I said in a no nonsense voice which promptly sent him rolling under the bottom rope as he enthusiastically responded with; 'Yes sir!' Glaring at the big boned, wide-bodied redheaded kid I eyed him over. He looked strikingly close to Donny J. McNasty, the challenger to my beloved World title. Red hair, broad body and a head of unheard of proportions he only served to remind me of Donny, and for this young mans sake…that wasn't a good thing. 'What's your name?' I snapped at him as he stood there sweating from the group workout I had put everyone through. 'Dustin Koth.' He said breathing heavily yet keeping a smile on his face. Sizing him up I took a step toward him as we each stood in the center of the ring. My penetrating bluish gray eyes met the soft green eyes of Dustin and I glared angrily into them a bit angered by his helpless similarity to members of the McNasty family. 'So, Dustin, you want to be a professional Wrestler?' I asked, shoving him in the shoulder with my right hand. Despite the fact that he outweighed me by a good seventy pounds he staggered backwards. His answer was hesitant as his face changed from a gasp of surprise to a stare of anger. 'Yes sir, I do.' He responded still trying to be respectful despite my obvious dislike for him. 'Then what would you do if you were in the ring with the World Champion…just like you are now?' I shot at him while shoving him in the other shoulder, this time with my left hand once again sending him a step back. 'I would beat him, sir.' He said, his upper lip beginning to curl into an angry grimace. 'How? How are you going to beat… me? ' I asked him as this time I brought up both hands and sent him down on his posterior courtesy of a hard shove with both hands to his chest. As soon as he hit the canvas he seemed to bounce back up a look of anger flashing in his innocent eyes. 'How?' I snapped at him using this poor sap as an outlet for an anger. 'How?!' I yelled at him as we now stood face to face. His eyes met mine and he angrily hissed his words at me. 'Like…this!' His words predicted his actions and he threw a big punch at me which I saw coming a mile away. Able to duck his telegraphed punch he went spinning in a full three-sixty and I took advantage of this, as his chin became acquainted with the Nike symbol on the bottom of my trainer's. I blasted the kid with a jaw shattering superkick that would no doubt send him to the dentist. As soon as I had made contact his knees buckled and his heavy body slammed down on the canvas loudly as he lay before me, spread-eagled on the canvas. 'Get this fag out of my ring.' I said without even a second thought of my actions as K.J. Tachikara worriedly pulled his unconscious trainee from the ring. Turning to the remaining students at ringside I sneered arrogantly at the shocked expressions etched on their displeased faces. Pacing back and forth in the ring I addressed my audience. 'Your friend Dustin just learned that it doesn't pay to screw with the wrong guy and each and every one of you has hopefully learned a lesson from his stupidity. This boys and girls is what Wrestling is all about…welcome to the business.' I still had their full attention as a member of two of this class used Dustin's well being as an excuse to escape from ringside and avoid my impractical methods of training. Two large men vanished from ringside and the rest of the group seemed to now be terrified as they realized their future in the Wrestling industry was clutched in the iron fist of a madman. Amused by their fear I decided to pursue further fun and games as I looked over the fearful students. 'Now…who want's to volunteer to be next?' I asked while thoughtfully stoking my chin as I looked out at my scared congregation of future stars. 'How about…you?' I asked as I pointed to an almost quivering man of size similar to mine. He shook his head fervently but before I could compel him to enter into the ring a voice broke my concentration. 'I can take you.' Said the voice as my eyes widened in shock. The voice hung in the air as I finally spotted the owner of the voice. A female… The feminine voice seemed to echo in my ears as I stared down at the lovely brunette that had spoken up. With her brown hair pulled back into a ponytail she smiled innocently at me with a sparkle in her big brown eyes. I stood there in a state of shock unsure of what to do as she rolled under the bottom rope and stood before me. I sized her up as I glanced over her tight blue booty shorts, white tank top and matching white Adidas sneakers. 'I said…I'll take you.' She repeated as she stood before me. Looking her over I was stunned by her outright defiance as she grinned cheerily at me. Eyeing her over I suddenly broke out into laughter and shook my head slowly. 'Sorry babe, but I don't hit ladies.' I said with a playful smile. 'Why not? Are you afraid of a girl showing you up?' She asked with her broad grin only seeming to further grind on my nerves threatening to rip me away from the last thread of sanity I clung tightly too. 'I'm not going to hit a little girl.' I sneered at her as I leaned in close to her face and spat those words angrily at her. 'Your not going to hit me because you know you can't beat Me.' She insisted as I heard the group at ringside begin to murmur amongst themselves. 'I'm the Global Wrestling World Champion, I can beat anyone…especially you.' I said trying to keep my temper in check as I restrained from hitting her. 'How would you beat me then?' She shot as I heard the group at ringside snicker as this bodacious young feminine disrespected me. 'I am the World Champion!' I screamed at her as I glared angrily at the traitorous crowd at ringside. 'How?' She once again asked, raising my ire as she shoved me hard in the shoulder with her right hand sending me a step back. 'I am the one training you, I can beat you, I am the Global Wrestling World Champion! Do you hear me? The freakin' World Champion!' I screamed at everyone at ringside and the young lady in front of me as well. 'How?' She repeated as she shoved me in the shoulder, this time with her left hand sending me another step back. 'I am…' I began before she interrupted me with another hard shove that send me staggering back. 'How?!' She snapped at me, still not raising her voice as the smile danced across her lovely face. 'Perhaps you would like a demonstration?' I angrily snarled at her. 'Only if you think you can handle it…champ.' She retorted arrogantly as her training partners broke into a loud chorus of cheers on the outside in support of her actions. Without answering I reached down for her wrist yet she grabbed my arm first and quickly twisted me into a hammerlock. Using quick thinking and actions even quicker I dropped to my knees and whipped her over with a sharp snapmare yet somehow she rolled out of it and onto her feet before I could even stand. My eyes widened in shock as I spotted her…and it was her foot that I spotted mere seconds before it slammed into my face, as I tasted the leather of her trainer's. My head snapped back as I collapsed back onto the canvas. I quickly rolled over but it was too late as her boot slammed down right in the back of my neck and a split second later her sharp kneecap was driven into my spine as she landed across my back. Straddling my back she hooked in a half nelson and twisted on a hammerlock as well. In one quick motion she rubbed my face across the canvas drawing blood from my nose as I gasped in pain. The group at ringside broke into laughter as she released me and stood to her feet. Groaning I was stretched face down across the canvas unmoving. Reaching down she gently patted me on top of the head as I heard her beautiful laughter accompany the rest of the onlookers. As her laughter ceased she snickered as she whispered one last phrase. 'Sorry about your luck, I hope you do better next time…champ.' With my pride dashed into a thousand pieces I groaned as painful realization hit me…I, the World Champion, had just became the bitch of a mere female Wrestling student.
