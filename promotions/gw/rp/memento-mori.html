Date: unknown .:: ::: ::. I am decidedly not a babysitter. In fact, when it comes time for me to have any sort of mothering instincts, the entire spectrum of my emotions seems to just take a dirt nap. Does that make me so much of a “bad person?” Aside from not really being a person at all, I don’t think it does. Some people just aren’t cut out for the job. That begs the question, then, of why on earth I agreed with Zenith that I should be the one to come to the McFarlane estate to make sure Dawn was alright. During the course of our conversation, he said that he’d noticed her acting strange lately’and in my experience, when a man notices something’s wrong with a woman, it’s about time to take action. Of course, I knew something was wrong with Dawn, but enhanced sense and an ability to pick up vibes from people, not to mention, read auras, will do that for you. Despite the fact that I’ve not felt concern for a mortal in quite a long time, I can’t quite seem to shake the concern that I feel for Dawn. Ridiculous, really, considering that under the terms that our relationship began, I would have much rather sliced Dawn’s face open than talk to her, much less live at her estate, take care of her, or otherwise serve as any sort of friendly emissary. Hey, I’ll admit it: I spent most of my time in WMW jealous of Dawn. Mostly because Dawn was everybody’s favorite woman, and I was their favorite target. Dawn got the “look how far she’s come” and I got the “self-righteous joke of a Femi-Nazi.” Yeah, I got used to hearing it, and I STILL hear it all the time, but that doesn’t mean that I’m not prone to fits of rage about it. No matter my efforts to purge my emotions, it would seem that rage still remains’rage and sorrow seem to linger a bit longer than most others, at least for my kind. Sorrow’that’s the one I like to pretend that I don’t have. The only thing that I can say about it is that it starts to get old after awhile, this existence. First, you watch everyone who’s close to you wither away into death. It was a bit different for me. I was from a Tzimisce Revenant line in any case, so it was nothing out of the ordinary to have six generations living in one house. Our kind aged so slowly that lifetimes were stretched out to the point where old women saw their great-great-great-grandchildren getting married. Or, in the case of my family, great-great-great-grandmothers saw their spawn taking blood oaths to Florian Dragane’ti, and continuing the proud tradition of the Vlaszy line of honorably serving our Tzimisce domitors. I broke the mold, you might say. I took the blood oath to Lord Florian when I was in my early-to-mid twenties’pretty old for the Dark Ages, but not old for a Vlaszy revenant at all, though I did take the oath much later than the rest of my siblings did. That is, of course, because I had quite an interesting existence before then. I was betrothed to a local lord’s son, which was quite the exciting prospect, until I saw him forcefully having his way with a servant girl, Chessa? you might say he was a little peeved at my intent to expose him to his father, who was, bless his soul, a good man and a pious man, for what that’s worth. Of course, the problem was, Jaroslav chose to blackmail me by trying the same thing on me; his intent was to get me pregnant so my family would be shamed, but I didn’t really take too well to that idea. In fact, I took to it so badly that I didn’t realize he was dead until I’d already dragged him out into the snow and made it look like a wolf attack. That’s why I got sent to France to marry Martel. An illegitimate son of Charlemagne who was looking to take over Daddy’s kingdom when he died’history will show you how that worked out. Then again, I suppose everyone thought Aric was dead, so even if his parentage had come to light, it wouldn’t have mattered a damn. I look at that now as unfortunate circumstances. I fell in love because I was terrified and alone in a strange new country. I fell in love because my husband was the only one who didn’t treat me like I was some strange beast from hell’s lip intruding on their insular little society. What a mistake that was, falling in love with the most selfish creature on the planet’s face, as he’s proven numerous times since then. My parents eventually decided that crime or no crime, they had made a mistake, and I had to come home from France to take the blood oath to Lord Florian. My family would have been thrown into dishonor had I not, and one thing the Vlaszy, the king-makers, did not suffer was dishonor. That is why they called me home after Angela died. The biggest problem with that, of course, is that Jaroslav’s brothers came home from the Crusades, found me home? and instantly wanted blood. They would have gotten it too, had it not been for my beneficiary interfering on my behalf. I was set to burn at the stake, but Vaclav Ionescu leapt to my rescue before I saw the flames’and instead of being a Vlaszy? I became a Tzimisce. He took me back to Prague, where I lived at Vysehrad with him, learning his arts of Vicissitude and koldunic sorcery. I was happy enough, because even in life, I had an appetite for learning. Then came the Anarch Revolt. Any Kindred old enough to remember living through that will remember how the Anarch Revolt laid everyone’s plans to waste. My family was slain to a man protecting Lord Florian’or so I thought. Turns out I have a twin brother still alive, at least according to the research of Cyrus, the Kiasyd I met while visiting my home town of Zdice not too long ago. I haven’t seen him, and unfortunately, I don’t remember him. This personal fugue I seem to have had, the one that made me end up in Cleveland in the first place, is most inconvenient at times. But in any case, things like these are all you have when you’re my kind. Things like memories and keepsakes from the past become your truth. I’ve got chests of things stored away in Seattle, which is where my home base was before I ended up in WMW, all things that remind me of how life used to be. They remind me of who I was, and I have my days where I think that who I was is all I have left. That is, of course, before I realize that it’s all a matter of perception. Who I was yesterday isn’t as consequential as who I am now. Kindred existence is a process of devolution. You lose who you were before, and in my case, you strive to become something better. There might be those of us, some of my present Global company included, who would say that the devolution only makes you into an animal who cannot control its baser instincts long enough to have coherent thought. But I ask you, is it better to devolve into something that harbors absolutely no trace of who you were? Or is it better to stagnate, grasping at your humanity with hands that you desperately pretend don’t end in talons, just to make yourself feel “moral?” Morality is overrated, especially as far as it concerns life and death, which are largely just a veil, a distinction we put up to perpetrate the illusion that human life on this earth means something. The problem is that it doesn’t. And that’s what I come back to’I’m sitting in the McFarlane estate, looking out across the lush gardens whose color I can see in the dark, but only because of my preternatural gifts. If I wanted to, I could be the queen of a thousand blood cults dedicated to my name. But instead, here I stand, an employee of Global Wrestling, a member of a faction full of humans who hate me because of what I am, pacing this grand house like a nursemaid because one of them has hurt herself and is far too foolish to admit to doing so. What am I doing? I’ve been asking myself this ever since I got here, ever since I had that rather ill-fated encounter with Dawn, and Dawn’s stubbornness, which on some days seems like a beast entirely separate from the woman herself. I did my best to keep some sort of humor during that little spat, however dry, but my own aggravation coming to the fore was beginning to pose a danger to the both of us. I sincerely hope that she will not press my patience again. I’d love to say that I’m only doing this for the strength of the Hunting Grounds, but I can’t necessarily confirm that such an assertion is the truth. In fact, there’s been this nagging feeling from my end that there is in fact quite a strong draw between myself and Dawn, but I can’t put a finger on exactly why I would feel that way about her’why should I care, in other words? A lot of people used to wax poetic, in my presence, about how Dawn McFarlane just had something special about her. If I have to admit that they’re right, I’m going to be really pissed off. The worst part of that is, of course, that I’ll be forcing myself to be pissed off. Because it’s impossible to be pissed off at someone as great as Dawn McFarlane, isn’t it? I’m not going to think about it. I’ve got to go see if she’s awake anyway, and see if I can get her to take some medication. If I don’t, and she loses her match, Christian will be pissed at me. I guess it’s some people’s lot to be the one whose work isn’t quite so important. That’s alright, though’for every one of them who doesn’t believe that my world exists, there’s another moment for me to be the center player in my own passion play. .:: ::: ::. “My lover's charms are in a box beneath my bed, And piece by piece, I'll cherish them until the end.” The letter rested in her hands uncomfortably. Or, perhaps, it was more bittersweet than that. She enjoyed holding it. The folds had long ago since started to wear the ink, even as thick as this paper was, but then again, Shadow had opened and closed the letter more times than she could possibly recall, reading every last word he had written to her. She had pretty much memorized the contents of the papers by now, but there were times when the wound was a little wider open. Those were the times when she sat alone in her room and allowed the memories to disturb her calm. She had gotten to a point lately where, no matter how much Soma occupied the front of her mind, there was always a pair of haunting, ocean-blue eyes watching over her, even in her interactions with Soma. You might have even said that part of her mind was splitting apart from the rest of her, forcing her to push in the direction of a more direct connection with Soma in order to make her forget him“the one being who had managed to put such an indelible mark on her soul that even when she had promised to hate him in her own response to the letter she held, she couldn’t do it. The letter had started to feel good in her hands again’the comfort of the paper being something that he had touched had started to weigh on her. It still held the scent of him, or so she could easily imagine’the way his hair smelled when it had just been cleaned, along with the very sanguine smell that seeped into all of her sensory memories, as she assumed it did for most vampires. But it was something special’she could have recognized the scent of Virgil’s blood over the scent of anyone else’s. She’d tried to convince herself that that was only imagination, but was it really” Or was there something that mystical about her connection to him that she could pick even the slightest hint of him out of every other vampire’s tainted, bloody scent that she had ever smelled? She looked down, the tiniest hint of blood tears rimming her eyes. Her scrutiny fell on the words of the letter again, the parts that she liked the best, even if reading them over and over again was only torture when she was in a mood like this. “The time I have spent with you since finding you in Cleveland running from The Den has been incomparable to the many years I remember since the Embrace. I am not an easy man to force into the shackles of love, and yet you have done it, and seemingly in no time at all. I don’t speak openly of this to you, but I am sure you’ve noticed the way I look at you, the way I can actually enjoy my time with you. That is why my thoughts on the matter have come to burden me ever more as the time has passed. In the first place, I knew exactly what I was doing when I allowed myself to get so close to you in Napa Valley. In retrospect, perhaps I shouldn’t have done it, but you seduced me. You did not even do it by any active process, save being who you are. I couldn’t help myself, so I went against first of all, that age-old vampiric law that we do not fall in love with others, because we are only supposed to love ourselves. Self-love, after all, equals self-preservation, and among beings as old as we, that typically comes to be the only concern in one’s nightly machinations. ? “What I am saying, Lia, is that you hurt me. I am not too proud of a being to admit what I feel. I felt such a deep connection between us’I know that I betrayed it by manipulating your thoughts when you were still ruled by delusions back in Cleveland, and I know that you still refuse to allow me as close to you as I was because you still feel that betrayal. I would beg for clemency, but I am starting to think, with all of these reasons weighing down upon my head that I should not beg for you, I should just let you go, and exist according to your caprices. It was, after all, caprice that I believe threw you into my arms in the first place, so allow me to thank you for that. I have enjoyed nothing more sublime in the many lingering centuries of my existence. I must not stand in the way of your happiness. I cannot allow myself to do that and still retain the small shred of morality that I do’even if it is only morality in regards to the sanctity of love. You must do what makes you happy. I have started to realize, with the inevitable gloomy instinct that most Ravnos seem to have, that what makes you happy no longer includes me. I assure you of my love’nothing has changed regarding that. Even through my bitterness at letting you go, I still feel the touch you have made upon my being? There it was again, the biting hurt that always came with the end of the letter. A tear dropped onto her cheek, but she was careful to wipe away the droplet of blood before it sullied the letter. She would never understand why he’d done it to her’why he’d left. Nor would she ever be able to reconcile herself with it, or so it seemed. Every day, it dug still deeper. She had tried as hard as she could to force Virgil out of her mind, but he just wouldn’t go. And when he wouldn’t go, she had tried to hate him. As though begging for punishment, she looked down and read the last piece of the letter, skipping through to the part that she knew would hurt the most. ? I only ask that you love him as you seemed to love me’fully. Don’t think of me, because if you can have a blissful existence in these dark nights, it is fitting that you have one. Consider me, ever yours, Virgil DeLeo Ambrosi Like she couldn’t help but care about Dawn, and like Dawn, she couldn’t help but still feel exactly the same way she had before about Virgil. More than anything else, it irritated her. It irritated her because she was supposed to be above emotion. It wasn’t supposed to matter what other people did. It wasn’t supposed to make a difference whether or not Virgil had left her, because she’d promised him that she would hate him. Memories of the dream she’d had about him a few weeks ago pushed into her head’she didn’t know the entire premise, but she’d gone to Madrid, to meet him there after he had written her a letter, almost desperately requesting her company, so they could talk about what had happened. More than ever, she hated her mind for what it did to her. There might have been many who assumed Shadow was fearless, but they were wrong’what Shadow feared, more than anything else, was her own head, and what being caught in its grip would do to her. It was her own head that had erased her memories of Dante, her brother, the Sabbat pack that she was supposed to be captaining. It was her own head that had allowed her to fall in love with Virgil, and then refused to allow her to hate him when she most needed to put up that defense. And it was her own head that kept forcing her to dream about him when she most wanted to forget the way he made her feel. A demon herself, battling demons more fierce than she felt she would ever be. But that was a secret that no one needed to know’that was a chink in the armor that she refused to reveal. Perhaps that was why she’d put herself into the situation she had with Soma. She wanted another potent connection to make her forget about Virgil leaving. Seeing Rune the week of Wrestlecade had threatened to break that strength’surely, Rune knew where Virgil was; after all, he worked for him. But Shadow hadn’t asked. Shadow hadn’t even brought him up, not on the way to San Francisco, and not on the way back. She’d been proud of herself for that. It was her own display of weakness that had prompted her to do what she did with Soma’s blood. She hadn’t necessarily wanted to commit herself to someone who she didn’t entirely trust, but what vampire ever really did trust another of its kind? She would have been dead long ago if she’d based her life on trust. She was a part of Soma now, whether she liked it or not. And now, she intended to do what he had requested of her, if only to satisfy her own curiosity about what he could teach her. The only problem with being a part of Soma was how much of Virgil she carried with her, and how hard she was trying to deny it. She only hoped that Soma wouldn’t be able to see it’but that wasn’t because she was embarrassed about it. It was because she was afraid that if Soma saw these scars upon her heart, he’d try to take them from her. That, she would not abide’she would fight tooth and nail to keep the great burden in her heart that was Virgil’s absence. With a heavy head, Shadow stood up, slipping the letter back into its rightful place’in a box which held some of her most important keepsakes’the Vlaszy sigil ring that Cyrus had given her, her copy of the divorce papers that finally freed her from her past? and this one letter that she held in her hand, the only thing from the box that ever held any sort of interest for her. She was reluctant to put it back, considering how much it meant to her, but she had agreed to meet Dawn in the library a few minutes from now to discuss something or other with her’honestly, Shadow didn’t even remember what this discussion was supposed to be about, because there was only one set of memories in her mind right now. Whether these memories were scars or not, they were the only memories of Virgil that she had, aside from empty words, and an aching heart. When scars were all that remained, even scars became meaningful, beautiful, each scar holding shut a wound that would never fully heal without the source of the wound being present to pull them away. Perhaps it was unfortunate that these were scars that Shadow would never be able to heal. But considering how much she feared what was underneath them, perhaps it was just best for her to have the scars. “Between these walls and darkened halls, I've done my time If I should die before I wake, then you'll know why.” .:: ::: ::. -letter quoted from WMW RP #26??Goodbyes? -song credit??My Lover’s Box? by Garbage .:: ::: ::.
