Serch for the Golden Monkey: Graveyard final chapter

30 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-30-04 Slowly Serpenti follows the thin golden chain, which is emulated, on the tapestry causing it to look like part of the design. Finding no connections to hidden wires Serpenti slowly pulls the talisman off the tapestry. Serpenti lifts the Talisman up and then places it into a pouch on hi hip. As Serpenti turns around he is greeted by the visage of a large Indian warrior. His face paint looking as if freshly painted. The mans muscles ripple with power. ?Who are you to dare steal what is mine?? The warrior says in a booming voice. ---- Serpenti stands there? Trying to think of a way out of this one. His eyes lighten up and his face softens under his mask. “I not be knowing what your about sir. I am not stealing anything.” Serpenti says with a cunning smile. “Now lies. You are either very brave or a fool.” The large warrior says griping his hand ax at his side. “Truly neither Sir. You see a lot of things have changed and come and gone in your sleep. I have been sent to acquire this talisman for a noble cause. I am but a messenger and delivery boy par say. I was told you would be awaken, I have just been sent to find you and awaken you.” Serpenti says proudly holding up the talisman. The Large warrior hesitates, and for the first time looks un-certain. Slowly he scratches his head trying to figure out the words Serpenti has just spit out. Slowly he tilts his head sideways and looks at Serpenti. Serpenti just stands there smiling. ?So you have been sent to find ? me?? The warrior asks still unsure. “More or less Yes. I have. A certain chieftain needs you to aid him. Look! We could do what you had thought of doping and fight. You would loose and I would not achieve my goal and thus I would loose as well. So would you kindly allow me to take this talisman and you to the Jamaican Chieftain” Serpenti says placing his hands on his hips and stomping his foot in protest. The warrior walks around looking at the things from his life and a fire rises in his eyes. “So I am needed to aid a noble chief. I shall allow you to take the talisman to this chieftain and he can summon me. Good luck in your journey back to your tribal lands masked one.” The warrior says and then dissipates into nothing ness. Serpenti smiles and tosses the talisman into a mettle box. And then places the small box into a pouch. ?Stupido. Who said the dead were all knowing. HA!? Serpenti shouts. He starts to leave then stops again looking at the pile of treasure. “Well the talisman woke him up. But nothing was said of that gold.” Serpenti pulls out a two larger back and walks over to the bags and piles the coins and other golden items into the bags. And then lifts them onto his shoulders and walks back down the way he came. Serpenti looks up and sees Talon Eagleclaw waiting expectantly at the top of the hole, with a rope in hand. ?So you do live!? Talon shouts down ?I thought I told you not to follow me! How dare you fail to follow the order of your Capitan Amigo!? Shouts Serpenti back trying to contain his smile. Both stand in silence then break out in laughter as Talon drops down the rope. ---- Serpenti and Eagleclaw sit in Serpenti’s large living room, The place is filled with wooden furniture and plants of all kinds making it seam as if he is in the jungle then a lush mansion. Serpenti sits in a white gauze type outfit with the shit unbuttoned.. And brown sandals on his feet. He now sports a white mask with his black hair coming out of the top. Talon sits across in a wicker chair; dressed in a pair of kaki corduroy pants and a long sleeved loose fitting tan cotton shirt and sandals as well... Serpenti sits there feet propped up on a large wooden coffee table. Serpenti holds the box open looking at the Talisman. A smile on his face. ?You mean the spirit fell for that line?? Talons asks laughing a little. “Si Amigo! I thought I was a dead little Serpenti on a stick Amigo, but he fell for it all. Talk about luck Amigo.” Serpenti says laughing as well. Serpenti clicks the box shut as his butler walks in. “Sir. You have a fax from a Mr. Bubba McCoy of Global Entertainment Incorporated.” The Butler says handing Serpenti a piece of paper. Serpenti tosses Talon the box with the Talisman in it, Talon catches the box and quickly sets the box on the table as Serpenti reads over the paper after a bit of reading a smile comes across his face. ?Well. It looks like King Wile is going to have to wait. I have a match in New York! For the Global Cruiserweight championship! ? Serpenti says excitedly. Serpenti hops up and heads down the hall The Butler and Talon looking at each other. ?Against a bunch of other people Amigo! I have to practice and get ready! Hector get Talon something to eat and Drink! No one disterb me or you will feel the wrath of El Serpenti de Morte!? Serpenti says as he vanishes around a corner. The butler shakes his head and walks away leaving Talon and the Talisman alone in the living room in eerie silence.
