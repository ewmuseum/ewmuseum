La Merde

24 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/24 At the end of a long, sloping, hidden driveway stands a large steel gate that reads 'La Merde' in three foot tall gilded cursive script letters. The gate slowly opens in order to grant the entrance of a jet black Mitsubishi Eclipse Spyder convertible. The vehicle's engine purrs as it glides down the shrub enclosed driveway that passes through a rather impressive garden filled with vibrantly colored foliage en route to La Merde. 'Heated Heavy' booms from every speaker in the car which contains John Zero and partially contains Deacon Holmes, who is hanging out the window screaming his own rendition of the song's lyrics at the top of his lungs while shaking his head back and forth and waving his arms wildly in the air.?stackin my artillary shop to get the enemy and fuck up the cops this shit'll kill 'em on the spot throw 'em in the lake right over the rocks and get the fuck away and don't get caught ya betta hurry nigga, G-O for what ya N-O so hoe come on, nigga wanna see if it's real, we got promos stank 'em and tag 'em wit' the forty fo' magnum get up inside 'em hit the spine, paralyze 'em listen to the pistol when they whistle spittin many missiles, splittin niggas to the gristle hit 'em in the middle of the fo'head is the motherfuckin hoe dead, oh yeah reloaded, M-11 9 millimeters up in the front pump, post up when we jump out and run they in the trunk, I told you right in front my seat, i keep heat you flamin up, the gat is right next to my feet never know what I’ma pull out and shoot when I reach and y'all ain't leavin the scene without bleedin!” Bellows Holmes as loud as possible and rapid fire in his slight southern drawl while balancing on an unknown axis that just barely keeps him from falling out of the car. Deacon's antics cause a slight grin to form in the corner of Zero's mouth as he shakes his head back and forth slowly while looking at him. Zero isn't the only one taking notice though. Many of the upscale restaurant's patrons in the parking lot stand motionless, frozen in their tracks, jaws dropped at what is quickly approaching in a black Mitsubishi Eclipse. Zero lowers the volume of the car’s stereo system and informs Holmes that “The leather seats are inside the car.” With the music now off, Deacon slides back into the car just as it begins rolling to a stop. “I’m gonna park the car, go in and reserve a table for us...preferably in the back.” explains Zero. The passenger side door pops open and out steps Deacon Holmes wearing a white pair of low top Jordan XVII’s, a pair of Iceberg jeans that are just barely clinging to his waist, and a tanktop. He then turns around and leans back into the car to retrieve a hat that he throws on and wears rather low causing it’s bill to partially cover his eyes. Holmes also grabs a plan white t-shirt which he drapes around his neck and across his shoulder. Zero’s Eclipse speeds off into the parking lot as Holmes makes his way toward the large building, who’s doors and windows are made of black glass that only allows those on the inside to see through it. Deacon makes it to the building’s front door, which is flanked on both sides by large statues of horses. He pulls the door open to reveal a gigantic dining area complete with spinning chandeliers, a small music pit, and a fountain in the middle of the floor. Everything in the place from wall to wall was first class, Holmes had never seen anything like it in his life. Holmes stands wide eyed and awestruck at the restaurant’s lavish decor. This environment was very new to him, as he had only gotten a taste of it while working with Tyler Pierce before his absence. Deacon begins drifting slowly into the building looking in all directions, absorbing every aspect of the room while contemplating how much it must cost. Suddenly he is snapped out of his trance by a white gloved palm against his chest and a french accent exclaiming the words “Sir, sir!” stopping him dead in his tracks. Holmes looks slightly to his right to find a portly man standing no more than 5'8' with a salt and pepper gray pompadour impeding his entry. The man is decked out in a black tuxedo and stands behind a small podium. “Reservation sir?” asks the man in an arrogant tone while sneering at Holmes? attire. “Naw, but I see ya’ll got a few tables open so we’ll just take that one right there.” answers Holmes while pointing to an uninhabited area. “That, sir, is reserved for valued patrons of La Merde, not just common rif raf that strolls in off the streets. Besides, you’re not even wearing a jacket! ...or a proper shirt for that matter.” replies the man in an increasingly haughty tone, one which is less than appreciated by Holmes Deacon quietly listens to the string of rude comments aimed at him nodding his head slowly. “You finished?” questions Holmes. “Yes... for the moment.” shoots back the man superciliously. With lightning quickness Holmes snatches the small man up by his tuxedo’s lapels and leans into his face staring at him as though he’s about to get his heart ripped out. The man stumbles rapid fire over several phrases in French, none of which are understood by Holmes. “Good.” is the only word that comes out of Deacon’s mouth before swinging him over to the side, slamming him up against the wall with his feet dangling in the air. “Now I want a table and you don’t want your smug little ass kicked! Sounds like a deal to me, what do you think?” Just then a blade of light cuts through the dim foyer as the dark glass door slowly cracks open allowing John Zero to slide in unnoticed. He moves quietly into the foyer calmly surveying the situation before clearing his throat loudly. “I believe he asked for a table.” he states matter of factly. “Mr. Zero!” exclaims the man. “In the flesh.” replies Zero “Yet me and my associate here are still without a table.” he continues. “Yes! Right away Mr. Zero!” answers the man while still staring down nervously at Deacon Holmes who then drops him back down to the floor before grumbling “Now that’s more like it.” The man fixes his coat and straightens his tie before looking to Zero and asking “Your usual booth Mr. Zero?” “Of course.” answers Zero while as he falls in line behind Holmes who is following the man to their seat. “And I want a complimentary shrimp scampi too bitch!” adds Holmes while giving the Frenchman a push on the back of the shoulder causing him to stumble forward and bump into a table. The three men make their way to a lone booth tucked away in a secluded corner of the restaurant. From this vantage point the entire dining area can be seen, yet it is less than visible from most of “La Merde??s patrons. Zero and Holmes slide into their seats and begin browsing the menu. After a few moments Holmes tosses his menu aside and leans back into the padded booth. “This some bullshit man, next time I’m pickin” where we eat.” complains Deacon. “Problem?” questions Zero looking up from his menu briefly. “Hell yeah!” quickly replies Holmes. “And that would be?” asks Zero plainly. “I don’t know what the hell half this stuff is... I eat food man, this shit looks more like it’s for decoration than eating!” explains Holmes. “So what would you prefer?” asks Zero still scanning his menu. “Shit, anything.” says Holmes halfway under his breath, pouting slightly. “Really” Is “anything” good? Never heard of that before... you’re gonna have to be a little more specific.” “Fried chicken, hot wings, somethin I’ve heard of before.” replies Deacon. “So order it.” “It’s not on the menu.” “Watch and learn.” informs Zero That said, John lays his menu down and snaps his fingers, alerting the nearest tuxedo clad waiter that he’s ready to order. He walks over to the table wearing a name tag that reads “Pierre” “Ready to order Mr. Zero?” questions the young waiter. “That I am, I’ll have the bacon wrapped fillet mignons, and a scotch on the rocks.” “And for you sir?” asks the waiter, turning to Holmes. “I want...” starts Holmes only to be cut off by Zero. “We’re gonna need a little more time, go ahead and check on one of your other tables.” orders Zero nonchalantly. Pierre walks away to check in on a nearby table as Zero slumps down in his seat and holds his steak knife up in front of his face. Staring into the reflection he informs Holmes “Looks like we’ve got company.” “Fuck man, are you serious?” “Dead.” answers Zero while surveying possible means of a surreptitious escape. “Ain’t this a bitch, I was just about to order.” whines Holmes. “Well, you know what they say. No rest for the weary.” “Yeah, no food either.” shoots back Holmes. “We’ve got more important things to worry about, remember Johnnie Vincent?” “Yeah, the guy we got that briefcase off for Capo.” replies Holmes. “Well he remembers us too, and it looks like he’s sent a little welcome wagon over for us.” informs Zero “Every time I go somewhere with you there’s trouble, this is some real TV shit man. We could have our own show, call it The Misadventures of John Zero and Deacon Holmes. Denzel would play me...” “I’m not much for TV.” quips John, still monitoring the front door. “But you are aware of the fact that there is an invention called television, and that on this invention they show shows right?” inquires Holmes. “Yes.” “Well this could be one of those shows.” explains Deacon, clutching his gun under the table. “Alright, here’s the plan...” starts Zero, who is quickly cut off by Holmes. “Get tha fuck outta here?” suggest Holmes. “Precisely.” “Sounds good to me.” Both men look at each other and nod before simultaneously standing up and running into the restaurant’s kitchen. Using every ounce of his speed, Deacon Holmes makes it out the back door first with John Zero close behind. Once outside the two men continue running, making their way toward Zero’s car, a black Mitsubishi Eclipse Spyder.
