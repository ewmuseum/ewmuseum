Sword Fighting Roz Blayze Global Wrestling Promo #42 Date: 3/28/03 Location: That House The door was broken when i got there. I had been looking for Salaok all night. When i had got to the road i turned my lights off. I stopped a few houses down and opened the door. I walked back to trunk and put the key in the slot. With a swift movement the trunk lept open. Inside lay my Rapiar. The Slender blade was wrapped in a cloth. I put a heavy hand on the cloth. The solid gold hand garud was in my hands and i began walking. I carried the sword at my side. I knew that i was most likly going to have to fight Salaok. I knew i could never beat him with my two hands. His Rage was to much. Mine only building. As i walked i took on my lather jacket. Getting to the house i beag to walk much slower. I made not a sound as i walked up the steps. I stood there for a moment. Looking at the man who had his back turned to me. His head hung low. I walked to were i could almost touch him with my Sword. Roz: What are you doing here. ((His body turned and his left arm had slung his own sword up in the air then it began to come down. With a swift movement i threw my right hand up and the sword blocked the quick biteing blade)) Roz: The door was broken when i got there. I had been looking for Salaok all night. When i had got to the road i turned my lights off. I stopped a few houses down and opened the door. I walked back to trunk and put the key in the slot. With a swift movement the trunk lept open. Inside lay my Rapier. The Slender blade was wrapped in a cloth. I put a heavy hand on the cloth. The solid gold hand garud was in my hands and i began walking. I carried the sword at my side. I knew that i was most likly going to have to fight Salaok. I knew i could never beat him with my two hands. His Rage was to much. Mine only building. As i walked i took on my leather jacket. Getting to the house i begun to walk much slower. I made not a sound as i walked up the steps. I stood there for a moment. Looking at the man who had his back turned to me. His head hung low. I walked to were i could almost touch him with my Sword. Roz: What are you doing here. ((His body turned and his left arm had slung his own sword up in the air then it began to come down. With a swift movement i threw my right hand up and the sword blocked the quick biting blade)) Roz: Tell me. Salaok: I need to tell you nothing. ((With a twirl he turned and the blade targeted my side. I dropped the sword to my left hand and held it downward. Again the sword did not touch me)) Roz: Looking for Lisha. Salaok: Yes. ((I knew for a moment that i had walked into something that i could not handle. The sensation went away because i had to move the swift hand and blade of salaok was coming for my head. Ducking the sword i threw my own to the throat of salaok)) Roz: I saw her two days ago. She was Laying in a ball on the ground. Like an Animal. Salaok: Hurt. Roz: No. Tired. I could see her face for a moment. Not asleep. Blank. She looked as if there was something in her head. a plan for escape. Something. I came for you tonight. I saw Wind. Yes. Saw Quartz as well. Not together. In the same place. Neither one being able to see the other. I also Saw Lisha. I couldn't help her though. I saw the gun and all i had was the rapier that is pressed against your neck at this very moment. Salaok: Where were they? ((With these words Salaok backed away and his balde bit into my right arm. I dropped to a knee. Recomposing my self I stood. If i had not ducked i wouldn't have made it to my match on Evo. With three more quick parrys Salaok and myself were on the porch. Both fighting like madmen. CLANG CLANG CLANG ((The swords were vashing together like lions. The Looks on my and salaok's face told the full story of what was going on. Nither of us had any hate for the other. There were things happening in salaoks Life that he had to vent. I being the only man there. I was the vent at that moment. Thank god i knew something about a sowrd. I would have died. with a trust he had me pinned against the rail of the porch that only days ago i had jumped off of and got myself into the world of trouble. would it all end at this moment. No i couldn't let it. I stepped to the right and began to fight like a Tiger. With two quick thrust i had Salaoks back agnist a wall. The fight had no left him yet.)) Roz: This is Pointless. ((Salaok seemed not to care what i thought he just had Lisha and Silent Wind on his mind. Again he came at me. God my arm hurt. It felt like he had Cut it off. Never had i felt so much pain. My arm was drenched with blood. Apparently the cut was deeper then i had ever thought but i couldent stop. I had to keep fighting. I had a Son to go home to. a Life. I could not hold anything for the cut agnist salok though. I knew that if any one ever took my wife from me I would kill them with the hands that i bear this rapier in. Salaok Darted the tip of his sword at me. I blocked and took a step back. a Leathel step. Falling down off the porch onto my back. I couldn't have moved any faster then i did but it was not fast enough. Salaoks blade was agnist my neck. He had not spared me as i spared him. His Blade Bit. Not to far in. Enough to drawn a trickle of blood)) Salaok: Do you value your life. Roz: Not as much as i Value the life of your wifes. Listen to me and well shall go and find her. Salaok: Throw away your sword. Roz: Never. Salaok: fair enough. If i let you up will you stop this battle that you can never win? Roz: No. ((I swept his feet from under him and both we were both back on our feet and clashing like Lions again. Locked in a deadly battle that could claim the life of either of us. I was felling dizzy from loss of blood. This cursed wound. Both of them burned more then anything i had ever felt. I had inflicted not a wound of salaok. Nor would i. I knew he was much better then me in this battle. Plus he had nothing to lose. Everything to gain i on the other hand have both. I have the loss of my life and i can gain a faithful powerful friend. Salaok was now ten paces away from me. eight. seven. six. God i hope he doesn't kill me. five. The blade. I blocked and we became tangled. I looked into his eyes and he looked into mine)) Roz: I forfeit to you. Salaok: Thank you. Were is my wife. Roz: Please we will talk of this more when my two wounds are cleaned and then we shall go. Salaok: Then let us go and do this. ((I knew that Salaok was not going to kill me. Then Blackness. I remember hitting the ground. That is all. When i woke up i was sitting in a place that i had not remembered. Later i learned that it was salaoks Office at the GW HQ. I told him wearily were i had seen Lisha. In Silent Winds vehicle. Going west. To a destination i had not known. Wen i saw her in the passenger seat bound and gagged. Not moving. I did not know why. Her face was pale and her eyes were shut. at first i suspected death but i thought to my self. Not even Silent Wind is Capable of killing a woman. No. No man could be. Groggily i made my way with the Head of the Nevermore. with the harvester of fear. I followed him as fast as i could. my arm was taped and bandaged. I was grateful for that. The pain was great but the need to go with this man was greater. when me got down to the lot i noticed my car was not there. Salaok had driven his.)) Salaok: I wills end some one to get it in the morning. Get in ((I got in and laying in the back seat was my rapier next to the sword of salaok. It made me feel better knowing that i had it there. We drove off. The night engulfed us. The Darkness that i had come to know. That i had come to breath and Live. Yes the New Sensation was now the New Dark Sensation. We drove for what seemed like Age after age. Sleep was beginning to naw at the very being of me. I could not fight it for much longer. I had to sleep. The life giving sleep. I laid agnist the window in the car. Going to sleep. I heard Salaok say)) Salaok: Sleep well. You have stumbled into something that will take your very being. I hope you are ready. I hope that you are ready. ((The blackness took me again. I slept. I slept like child. The last thought was of my child. Tucked into his bed. My dreams were filled with him and my lovely wife Laura. Would i ever see her again. Only time could tell. Only Time could tell.)) Fin
