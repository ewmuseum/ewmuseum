I might have to kill that boy

12 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/12/03 Brick and Aaron are out for a walk in the park with Aaron’s new daughter Ashley, giving Felicia some time to relax. Brick has in his hand a medium-sized shopping bag. As they walk, they discuss the incident that happened on “I still think it was kind of childish,” says Aaron. “Seriously, man, what were trying to accomplish?” “I was trying to teach Deacon Holmes a little lesson. He’s a disrespectful, stuck-up little punk. Someone needed to put the boy in his place. And considering he came and tried to mess up a special occasion for me, he needs to be thankful I let him off so easy.” “But still, you gotta know he’s gonna be coming after you. I mean, I saw how pissed off he was when you embarrassed him.” “Look, it’s like I told him. Deacon thinks he’s a man, but he’s really just a boy and he got treated like one. Just like how I’ll treat him if he steps out of line again. Only next time, Christina McCoy might not be there to stop us.” “Be serious, man.” “I am serious. Only reason I ain’t dealt with his ass the way I should have is because I had bigger fish to fry. Well, Deacon’s time in the skillet is coming.” “Yeah, but he’s got the rest of TUE with him, doesn’t he” Price, Pierce and Zero. Not to mention all those bodyguards. And it’s not like you still have Ruckus Inc. with you.” “Look, I took on the whole Nevermore without Ruckus Inc. Salaok, Quartz, Sage Sabbot, Derek Draven, Mark Kullypso, Scarecrow, I took “em all on. I didn’t have Ruckus Inc. then, and I didn’t need them. And I don’t need them now.” “But, remember what happened the last time you tried to take Holmes on alone?” “Yeah, I remember. That’s only because Holmes is a little punk. He had his bodyguards with him. Yeah, I got stunned and kicked in the ribs, but at least I wasn’t bleeding.” Brick and Deacon had an altercation before Brick’s match with Derek Frost the Evolution before Whiplash. Punches were passed back and forth until Deacon’s bodyguards hit him with stun sticks, knocking him out. Deacon then took the opportunity to literally kick Brick while he was down. He walked away, but with a bloody nose. “Oh, by the way, I got this for Ashley.” Brick then reaches into the shopping bag and pulls out what looks like a plushie of Brick. He holds it over Ashley as she reaches for it. “Oh, man,” says Aaron. “Where did you get this?” “A fan made one and sent it to me. I liked it so much, I got the address and mailed her back, asking if she could make me another one.” ??Her?” It was a female fan?” “Yeah.” “And she just sent you a doll?” Usually, whenever Brick received fan mail from female fans, they sent things like pictures of themselves and phone numbers. Some even sent underwear. “Yeah. But she did spray the package with perfume.” “I wondered what that smell was.” Just then, Brick’s cell phone began to ring. “Hello” Oh, hey, Franklin. I got a package from UPS today? Yeah, I’ll be by in a little while to pick it up. OK, good bye.” “The super in your building?” “Yeah, he picks up my mail and stuff while I’m gone. He says I got a package today from UPS. I better see what it is. I’ll catch you later. Enjoy the rest of your day. Bye, ALF.” “Stop calling her that!” Brick gets in his Navigator and drives off. * * * Brick arrives at his apartment building and heads into the lobby. He walks to the superintendent’s apartment and rings the doorbell. “Franklin?” The door opens and out steps Franklin, the building super. Franklin is in his early 40’s with a stocky build. “Hey, how you doing?” “I’m good. How are you?” “I’m okay. I guess you want your package.” “Yeah.” Franklin goes back into his apartment and brings out a large package wrapped in brown paper, which he hands to Brick. Brick squeezes the package and then starts to tear away the packaging. Finally, all the packaging is torn away to reveal’a pack of Depends undergarments. Brick lets out an annoyed sigh. “Must be some mistake,” says Franklin. “No, it’s not a mistake. I know who sent this.” Just then, someone runs into the lobby and right up to Brick. “Hey, big man, there’s some dudes messing with your car!” “What”!” Brick runs out into the street. His Navigator is in flames. Someone in black run away from the scene before. “Hey! HEY!!” Brick chases after them as they drive off in a black limousine. “HOLMES!!!” Brick roars. Brick then storms back to his building as people get away from the fire. Franklin then stops him. “Take it easy.” “It was Holmes! It had to be!” “You don’t know that.” “Oh, no” Who else could it be?” “Well, at any rate, the fire department and the police are on their way.” “Good, because the police are gonna have to take me into custody.” “Why?” “Because I might have to kill somebody. And that somebody is Deacon Holmes.”
