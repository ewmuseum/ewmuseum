Getting ready

12 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/12/03 Business is still a bit slow at the Dark Horse Saloon with Bubba in a coma, and morale amongst the staff is low. Not to mention the fact that the Saloon has had to renovate, due to the damage caused by the fire set by the Nevermore. Donovan McCoy is back running the Saloon in his father’s absence. He’s still getting over being severely beaten and left to die in the fire. The ordeal has hit the entire family extremely hard. But Donovan took it upon himself to act as the head of the family. There is a large dance floor. Next to it is a big mechanical bull pit. Right now, enjoying the mechanical bull is one Ely Braxton. It’s one of the few pleasures he’s been able to enjoy in the past month or so. Brick sits in the reserved area on the third floor. He’s downed a few bottles of beer since he got there. Mostly, he’s spent his time contemplating the match he has coming up on the next edition of Evolution, where he faces both Sage Sabbot and Tragic Hero in a Triple Threat match. “So, Brick, you ready for your match?” asks Donovan. “Yeah, I’m ready. I’m always ready to beat the shit outta somebody in the Nevermore.” “But you gotta be careful goin” into this match. How you do in this match may reflect how you do in the remainder of the tournament.” “I thought Bubba tried to keep you out of the business.” “Hey, I still grew up around it. Anyway, you need to watch yourself, especially around a Harvester.” “You mean Sabbot” Look, Donovan, let me just say this straight from the jump. Normally, I’d say something tough or witty about my opponent, but in this case, I’m just gonna be real. Sage Sabbot is a fuckin? bitch! That’s not just opinion, it’s fact! He can talk all the shit he wants to. Yeah, he beat TLM for the Cruiserweight title--because the Nevermore helped him do it! He beat Roz Blayze to keep the Cruiserweight title--because the Nevermore helped him do it! He’s still in this World tournament--because the Nevermore helped him do it! Which only proves my point, which I cannot emphasize enough: he’s a fuckin? bitch! Him and the rest of the Nevermore! Quartz is a bitch! Mark Kullypso is a bitch! And the biggest bitch of all is the one who calls the shots!” “OK, fine. What about Tragic Hero?” “What about him?” “Well, you two did team up just recently, plus you two hate Salaok more than anybody else in GW.” “So” He and I both know it’s just business. I may be facing him in the finals. I may not. But he knows what this tournament means to me. Every cat in the back knows what this tournament means to me, and they know I’ll go through whoever the hell I have to so that I can win the World title. Because I’ve earned it. And truth be told, I was gonna keep my eye on Hero anyway.” “Because of the thing with Quartz?” “I don’t know what the drama is all about, but either way, Tragic Hero better know I got my eye on him. And Sabbot better know the same. In fact, the whole damn Nevermore better know the same. And in case they forget?” Brick then picks up a remote control and presses “PLAY”. The closing moments of the Hell in a Cell match from Wrestlecade roll on the screen. Quartz and Mark Kullypso attack Brick from behind, just before he can lift Salaok into the air, and proceed to pound away!! They then whip him off the ropes, going for a Double Clothesline, but Brick ducks the shot, bounces off the opposite ropes and connects with a Double Clothesline of his own!! He then catches Quartz with a kick to the mid-section, lifts him into the air and nails him with a vicious Powerbomb into a steel chair! Kully comes from behind, looking to hit Brick with a German Suplex, but a few elbow strikes later and Brick broke free, hitting another kick to the mid-section followed by a suplex that sends Kully crashing over the top rope and hard onto the concrete floor!!! Salaok slowly gets to his feet and puts on some brass knuckles that Kully slid to him upon entering the ring, and gets ready to nail Brick. He waits as Brick climbed back to his feet, before swinging at his face!! However, Brick ducks the shot, and nails Salaok with a kick to the mid-section, before whipping him into the ropes and lifting him into the air upon his return to connect with the Bricklayer over some overturned steel steps! Salaok is now down for the count and bleeding with a massive cut over his left eye. Brick slowly turns Salaok and makes the cover. “And the same can happen to Sabbot and Hero.” “Well,” says Donovan, “good luck in your match.” “Hey, speaking of matches, ain’t Ely got a match with Deacon Holmes?” “Yeah, he’s just unwinding before then.” “How are things between him and Christina?” “Why do you feel that’s your business” Do you like when people ask you about Gunner?” “Good point.”
