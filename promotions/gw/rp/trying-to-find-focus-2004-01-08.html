Trying to find focus

8 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-8-04 Ping Ping, Ping Ping Ping, Ping The sound of a hammer hitting steel rings out though the small room, a room illuminated by an orange glow of a roaring fire. The heat emitting from the fire causing one to feel like they were standing next to small volcano. A small but muscular man stands next to a large anvil bringing a large hammer up and dropping it down. Causing sparks to fly from the contact. Ping Ping, Ping The man is only clothed in a pair of jeans and boots, his long black hair matted down with sweet and sticking to his back in places. The man turns revealing the familiar masked face of El Serpenti. He pulls up a glowing rod of steel and sticks it back in the fire. After stabbing the mettle into the roaring fire Serpenti walks to a large barrel and pulls up a small bucket and lowers it into the barrel. Pulling the bucket up Serpenti dumps the ice-cold water over his mask and head drinking some as it entered his dry mouth. Serpenti walks back to the fire and pulls the mettle out once more. The mettle bright red and white in spots from the hot fire. A quick twirl to test the balance, and Serpenti looks along the blade. Shaking his head he places the mettle back on the anvils and lifts the hammer up, and slams it back down, once again sending sparks flying from the contact. Ping Ping, Ping Ping Ping, Ping Serpenti pulls the mettle up showing the formation of a sword blade. Looking the blade over carefully he smiles satisfied. Serpenti walks over and drops the hot blade into the water barrel. Causing the water to hiss and steam to rise. Serpenti walks over and sits down on a bench resting after working on the sword blade for several hours. Sweat causing his body to shine in the firelight. Serpenti sat in silence for a moment thinking to his self over the things that have gone on the last few months. How some how he failed to get Excalibur for Pendragon, the trap and explosion that almost took his life, not only his but Pendragon’s and Kitty’s as well. About his match coming up with the assassin known as Mani Shigeru. The touch of ice cold snapped Serpenti out of his thoughts back to the waking world. “Ahh! Cold!” Serpenti exclaimed. Turning his head to see the face of Kitty MacIntyre, a face that brought Serpenti peace and tranquility. “You have been out here for hours dude. Only so much of being waited on by your butler I can stand. So I figured I would come out here and keep you company.” Kitty said with a slight smile. “Ohh coruse mi amore.” Serpenti said. Kitty snuggled close behind Serpenti, wrapping her arms around him. Serpenti gimmicked as her cold arms stole his heat for them self’s. ? So? Are you ready for your match?” Kitty asked “I do not know Kitty. This Shigeru, I have head stories about him. They say he is ruthless and will not stop till your broken. Of all the opponents I have faced so far. Shigeru is going to be my biggest challenge to date. I watched the tapes in the Hall of Fame about him. He is fast and unbelievably strong.” Serpenti said with enthusiasm. “So are you intimidated?” Kitty asked. Serpenti pulled from her embrace, and spun around. “Intimidated, no. Concerned si. But more then anything I am anticipating this encounter. This is what I came to this place for, a challenge, a real challenge. This is not about winning a match; it is about surviving the match. This is my chance to prove my self.” Serpent said tapping his chest with his fist. “Good that’s the man I have come to know.” Kitty said with a smile. “Now what is it your out here doing?” Serpenti smiled. ? I am making a sword. Takes my mind of things, allows me to focus.” Serpenti explained. “Wish to see” Kitty nodded yes and stood up and followed Serpenti as he pulled the, now cooled off bade out of the water. A nice slight curve the blade had. “All that is left is making the hilt, and sharpening and polishing.” Serpenti said. “There you are!” Rang the voice of Talon Eagleclaw. Kitty frowned as her plans of a nice evening alone with Serpenti was now? ruined. Talon walked into the room carrying some papers in his hand. “Serpenti I have found a lead to the location of the sword!” Talon exclaimed. Serpenti tilted his head slightly, and motioned for Talon to continue. “Well I got word that Itagaki Freeze and Akuma had been spotted recently in a club in Los Vegas, Said they met with two other men. One of them being Leinad Soma.” Talon said with a satisfied tone. ? Freeze, He is one of the two who took Excalibur. What on earth are they talking with the vampire for?” Serpenti said. “Should we get ready to leave for Vegas now?” Talon asked. “Si we must go there anyways for Reload. Might as well go now. ? Serpenti said. “Wait” Vampire?” Kitty asked. The two men stopped talking and looked at Kitty who stood there in disbelief. “We will explain on the way Kitty. We must get there fast. I made a promise to get the sword for Pendragon, and I am not the type of pirate that goes back on a sworn word.” Serpenti said grabbing Kitty’s hand and following Talon out of the room. Hitting a switch on the way out causing a lid to fall of the fire to seal it off.
