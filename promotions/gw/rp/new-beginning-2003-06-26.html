New Beginning Judgement Global Wrestling Promo #347 Date: 06.26.03 Location: This just keeps getting worse and worse. There is no telling what is going to happen next. We've been standing in this living room yelling back and forth at each other for the last three hours. I have some how controlled my usual very short temper somehow. There's one thing I can't stand and that is yelling and being hit in the face. If those to mix, all hell breaks loose. Only the yelling has happened so far, so long, that I don't even know what the girl is babbling about. 'You know I should have never even came back here today, your an asshole. Your not even listening to me, you probably just thinking about the next time you get in that damn ring!' That is Lyndsi, my fiancé, she was great when we first met. We met through a friend about two years ago. When we met I was out of wrestling with back problems for a year and a half, rehab is a bitch. I never even told her about the whole wrestling deal. Well about 5 months ago I got back into the business and it was the first she heard about it. She was OK with it at first and was willing to give it a try. Well, she gave it a try and five months later...here we are. 'What do you mean you shouldn't have came here today? You haven't seen me in over a two weeks.' I said calmly to Lyndsi's angered face. 'Are you not listening to me? This is what this whole thing is about! You being gone all the time.' Lyndsi says yelling loudly in my face. I take a few steps back because she's pretty angered right now. I mean there is no telling what she will do when she's mad. I don't try and make her mad she just kind of fuels up and lets it go out of no where. Like today, she just showed up...kinda scared me. 'Lyndsi, baby, I had to go find somewhere to work. Finding the right place to sign a contract with isn't the easiest task in the world. It will get easier, I won't be gone that much now that I have signed a contract.' I told her reassuring her. Oh no, look at the face. She's about to let me have it. I'm scared now something is about to go down. She looks like a big whitehead when you go in the mornings to look in the mirror and you just wanna pop it, well she's the one and she's about to explode. 'Screw your contract and screw your wrestling career! Get a real job like a real man, not this crap. Your gone all the time and I never see you. I don't want something like this, when we started this relationship I didn't know it would end up like this with you being gone all the damn time. I don't want you wrestling anymore, you can take your pick me or wrestling.' Oh no, its the infamous ultimatum. Now I had a choice; I had to pick her or my wrestling career. My wrestling career was all I have ever known, since I was 15 I was into wrestling, the day I turned 18 I knew a wrestler is what I wanted to become. Then I came along Lyndsi three years later. I love her, I really do but I love wrestling. 'Lyndsi, don't make me chose you know I love you and I love wrestling. We can work this out, come on sit down lets talk about this with all the yelling.' I said grabbing her arm and sitting down the couch, but she refused. Oh god, I'm out of everything. Only God knows what is about to happen now. No one can predict it, but something was about to happen whether it be good or bad I was ready. I'm not sure what it is, but I am sure I can handle it. I can handle 400 lb men I am sure I can handle the words of this...women. 'Judgement, I'm not doing this anymore I told you to chose and your not chosing. I gave you a chance to be with me if that's what you chose, but obviously you are choosing your wrestling career over me. Hell, who could have thought I could change your 5 month dream for our two years being together.' Lyndsi says turning towards the door. What? I thought she knew. I thought she knew this was all I knew. Hell, I didn't even finish high school. What the hell did she think I have been doing all my life? I guess she never asked, so who was I to tell. I mean its not my proudest accomplishment, but hey things happen. I had to tell her or she may be gone for good. 'My 5 month dream,' she stopped in her tracks, but not yet turned around, 'I have been doing this since I was 18 years old. I am 23 now that is 6 years, and 4 months from now it will be 7 years. You expect me to throw it all away for 2 and a half years and I know nothing else. I dropped out of school for this, because I loved it. I don't know anything else.' Its all a blur now, I don't know what to do or say. It's all up to her now and what she wants to do. I can't make her do nothing its her decision, but I'm not stopping wrestling, so if she wants me to chose she knows the answer. Hell, maybe I can make a deal with her. 'Ok, look you want me to quit. I'll make you a deal. Let me see if I still have it in this debut match. If I can win then I get to keep going, if I lose then I'll quit wrestling for you.' She turned and looked at me for the first time in like five minutes. Maybe I was making sense to her finally. 'Ok, I'll give you till after your first match.' She turned around and walked straight out the door. She didn't hug me, kiss me, or even talk to me. That as the last thing she said. I guess It'll all be up to how I perform. Well Trey Reed is up for one hell of a match.
