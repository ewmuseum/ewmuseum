Bitterness the Star

13 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/13/05 'Hey, Mikey?' 'What is it, cousin?' 'Are you proud of me?' Michael sighed at the question he was presented as they got into their rental car to head back to the airport. The question was pretty stupid; if Michael got away with his cousin losing without going insane, he must have been alright with something. Either that, or he was on drugs, but the latter Michael left to people like Jose Canseco. Oh well, if it would make Mindy happy... 'What kind of question is that?' he stated finally, sliding behind the wheel of the car. With a flick of the wrist, he turned the key in the ignition, and the relatively weak engine sounded off. 'Of course I'm proud of you. You didn't get pinned and considering you were in the ring with three big muhfuckas, you handled yourself better than I thought you would.' Mindy glowed ecstatically. It was one of the first times that Michael truly complimented her, and the first time he complimented her about her wrestling. 'I just wonder how he'll respond to seeing me win next time!' The sheer thought made Mindy smile. And at that, Michael smiled, too. 'Hey, Mikey?' the girl started again. 'Yeh?' Michael asked in reply, backing the car out of its parking space. His muscles tightened, readying himself for yet another question about wrestling. He did decide to manage Mindy out of his own free will, but why did wrestling have to take over his life? The answer to that question, he did not know. However, he was relieved when he heard the next of his cousin's endless stream of questions. 'I'm hungry. Do you think we could get something to eat before we fly back?' Michael shrugged as he turned on the radio, and out blasted some Coheed And Cambria song. 'Don't see why not.' 'Thanks,' Mindy answered breathlessly, 'airplane food makes me feel anorexic.' As he kept a cautious eye on the road ahead, Michael turned his head slightly towards his cousin. 'I feel anorexic looking at you and knowing my cousin is as skinny as a toothpick.' That smile of Mindy's soon faded into a mocking pout, and Michael saw it coming. He sighed and let out a soft chuckle. 'I kid, I kid. Come on, let's get some Chinese food. I need some sushi.' 'Er, Mikey?' Mindy prodded. 'What?' 'Sushi is what you'd find in a Japanese restaurant silly.' 'Yeah,' Michael nodded slowly, uneasily, 'that's what I said.' '...' '...' --- 'Hey Mikey?' Mindy squeaked again, now in front of a plate of chow mein. Michael tried not to listen to the voice, attempting to enjoy his General Tso's chicken in piece. However... 'Hey Mikey? Hey Mikey? Hey Mikey? Hey Mikey?' Michael's fists smacked the table hard. 'I thought you were hungry.' 'Well I am!' the girl shouted, causing the few people eating in the restaurant on that late February night to turn and stare. Mindy could sense the eyes on her, and lowered her voice as she continued. 'I just want to ask you something, and not be ignored.' 'Hey, you told me you want to eat, not chatty chat chat,' Michael snapped back. 'And quite frantically, with how close that match was, I think I need to fill my stomach as well.' 'What's that supposed to mean?' Mindy inquired, almost afraid for the answer she'd get. 'You kiddin' me? That match was intense. Like holding in a big fart at a wedding type intense.' 'Ew! And you want me to eat when you talk like that?!' 'What am I talkin' like?!' Michael grumbled through gritted teeth. 'I dunno,' Mindy growled back, 'but it's disgusting!' Michael sighed. He wasn't going to win the argument no matter what, and it was painfully obvious. 'Whatever. Anyway, what were you gonna ask me, now that we're on the subject and now that my food's starting to get cold?' 'Ugh,' Mindy groaned in fatigue, 'I was just gonna ask ya if you used to keep a diary.' 'What kind of girly ass question is that?! You made me leave my chicken untouched for a question like that?!' More eyes focused on the cousins. A Chinese man approached them and began to speak in very marbled English. Michael concentrated as hard as he could, but he still couldn't make out a word of what was said. 'Can't hear you,' he spoke towards the man, but quickly became panicked when his words obviously weren't understood. 'No... no comprende, amigo! Tu madre es un fuego?' That generated a clueless look on the Chinese man's face, before he took a step back. The words he spoke then were more than clear enough for Michael to hear, thanks to the added volume the man gave to his voice. 'My mother is not on fire! And that's Spanish, you dumb shit!' With that, the man promptly turned at the heel and walked off, leaving Michael to register what had just happened as he blinked slowly. 'Uh... what was I saying?' 'You were talking about my question,' Mindy informed, 'and how it apparently kept you from eating. Although that wouldn't have been the case if you just answered the question.' 'Oh for fuck's sake,' Michael grimaced, remembering the true topic at hand. 'No, I don't keep a diary. I used to keep a journal, but that didn't last for very long.' Now that Michael had truthfully answered the simple question, Mindy could lower her voice knowing the answer she looked for. 'Why's that?' she pressed on. Michael sighed in disgust of all the questions, as he couldn't take being separated from his food any longer. His chopsticks clutched a decent chunk of chicken, and he placed the food to his lips. 'Because I realized something about.. a week into writing in that thing...' Michael trailed off, popping the spicy food into his mouth. He chewed quickly, quickly enough to finish his sentence soon after. 'I didn't want to be writing about my life as much as I was actually living my life.' 'Oh?' Mindy seemed intrigued, gathering a ball of rice and some pork in her chopsticks and beginning to eat. 'Why would you look at it that way?' Michael moaned. 'You're not going to leave me alone until I answer this, are you?' 'Nope!' Mindy grinned. 'Great,' Michael thought to himself bitterly, 'just fucking great.' 'It got to the point where I felt that I didn't want me or anyone else to be able to look back on the things that went on in my life.' 'Was your life that bad, Mikey?' the girl persisted. 'I lived in The Bronx, Mindy,' Michael shot back coldly- the thought of reminiscing such a past angering him all the more, 'my life sure as hell wasn't to be one that people wished to have.' 'Eh...' Mindy trailed off softly. Michael caught the slight noise she made. 'What is it now?' 'I wouldn't go as far to say that people didn't wish they had your life. Some people could have had it worse.' 'Yeah?' Michael tilted his head- now he was beginning to get intrigued. 'Like who?' Mindy shrugged as she ate more of her food. 'Anyone.' Michael almost spat out the piece of chicken he bit into. 'Really? What did I do with my life that would make someone want to wish they were living my life?' 'You were a famous wrestler, Mikey,' Mindy noted, 'you were famous and you were really good at what you did. A lot of people appreciated what you did for the business, including me. It's why I left Italy to come to you even though I personally never met you...' she rambled off again. Michael could have sworn he heard her whisper something else, but her voice was too low to pinpoint what it was she had said. 'What was that?' Michael prodded. In turn, it was Mindy's time to be agitated. She finished the sentence again, that time louder so Michael could hear- but low enough so that no one else could. 'Taking a chance and running to you would have been better than staying with my father...' Michael frowned. Michael remembered Mindy once saying something that referred to her hatred for the man who helped bring her into this world, but the reason why was never brought up. As far as Michael knew, he thought his uncle to be a well-mannered man. Then again, he hadn't talked to him all that much, either. 'I could have dealt with you later if I truly hated you,' the girl continued, her head lowered- as if ashamed to be talking about her own father. 'But him... he always would have had one up on me. I would never have been able to speak as freely to him as I do to you, and for that... coming to you would have been better than staying there.' 'One up on you...' Michael trailed off, confused by the comment. He thought about it for a second, and quickly the answer came to him. 'He was your father. No matter what you said to him, he was your father. He always had the final say, didn't he?' Slowly, abashedly, Mindy nodded her head. It was almost as if she derived physical pain from talking about him. 'That's exactly it,' Mindy confirmed. 'How'd you guess?' Michael smiled weakly. 'I had a father once, too.' Mindy looked up from the table she sat across Michael from- her eyes were watery once again. She wasn't going to start a scene for the small amount of people in the restaurant, but she did do something else. Standing up, she nervously moved to her cousin and gave him a hug. 'Thanks so much for taking me in. You didn't even know me, and you took me in. I'll always be grateful for that, and I owe you for it.' Michael placed a gentle hand on his cousin's shoulder, setting her back slightly. 'You owe me nothin'. Paint the picture however you want, you're family. Heh.. with my cousin Justin over in college and all.. you're the only true family I had around.. before I married Jaden at least. Mindy smiled in relief at that comment. 'What happened to your parents, Michael?' '...Something.' Mindy scratched her head. 'Something what?' Michael blinked. 'Bad.' 'Jesus Christ.... what was it?' 'Can we... not talk about this here? I don't care if you have to ask later. I just, don't want to be talking about this while I eat.' Mindy understood, or at least she seemed to. Slowly, she backpedaled into her seat. 'Sorry.' 'S'okay,' Michael shook his head, 'you didn't know. Now you do, though. Let's just keep it off-topic until I deem it necessary to talk about it. That's all I ask for.' 'Fair enough,' Mindy nodded, 'I don't wanna pry it out of you.' Michael soon nodded in return. 'Are you okay?' she asked. Michael kept nodding. 'Alright.' The rest of the dinner went by quietly. No laughs, no arguments, but sheer silence. Then... 'I think I need a boyfriend.' Michael looked up from his near empty plate of food. 'Why's that?' Mindy gave the quick and obvious answer. 'It's gonna be Valentine's Day soon!' 'True... just don't go out with that Biddle moron.' 'Ugh, never.' More silence. It took Michael awhile to ask the next question. 'What about Markus?' Mindy shrugged. 'What about him? I hate him, too.' 'Yeah...' Michael trailed off. 'I'll have to do something about that.' He glanced at the piece of chicken left on his plate, and swiftly ate it before he even thought of leaving. 'But that will come eventually. Now let's catch our flight. I think a good night's sleep would do us both a world of good.'
