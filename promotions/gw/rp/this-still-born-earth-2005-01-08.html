This Still Born Earth

8 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/8/05 'Is she alright? I just need to see her, is she alright?' -December 1, 2004 Michael fought to shove past the doctors standing in his way, but the numbers were far from being in his favor. His height allowed him glimpses to the person he was trying to see, but those glimpses alone were not enough. He needed to see his cousin up close- he needed to be sure that she was okay. 'I know you're upset sir, but we need you to wait outside and let us work,' was one of the random responses thrown at Michael from the numerous doctors and nurses. However, they were words that were not going to deter Michael from getting to Mindy. 'I don't care,' he sneered, 'you better tell me what happened to my cousin, or I'm not going anywhere.'' 'Look, mister Mercer,' another doctor chimed in, 'we don't want any more problems to-' 'If I shove my foot up your ass, would that be a problem?' Either Michael made a point to the doctors to let him by or they simply got tired of keeping a persistent two hundred and twenty pound man out of the room, because they all backed off at once and began to concentrate on what was their main priority. That gave Michael all the time he could ask for to watch what they were doing. All the time to watch his cousin breathing steadily in a stream of unconsciousness on a cold hospital bed. It was a sight Michael was all too familiar with. 'Is she...' Michael stammered, suddenly at a loss of words from the sight he was seeing, '..is she gonna be okay?' One of the nurses attempted to brush past Michael and out of the room, but Michael stepped in front of her. 'I dunno if you heard me or not, but is she going to be okay?' The woman looked up at the man who glared down at her, and it was then that she truly noticed how tall he was- much taller than he seemed on television when he wrestled, at least six foot three. Tall enough to make her choke on any words that she wanted to form. 'I.. well she's...' Michael shook his head, a lopsided smirk forming on his face. 'I'm sorry,' he began, 'am I intimidating you? Here.' He leaned forward, enough so that he was eye level with the woman. If anything, it made her even more frightened. 'Now, kindly tell me what happened to my cousin?' 'She... she's..' somehow the woman eventually managed to get the sentence out- even though she didn't exactly know how with all the pressure she was given with Michael's eyes practically staring through her. Those near-black eyes... 'She's sustained a concussion, the level of which we have yet to determine. A- and she has a gash along her right arm...' 'Yeh?' Michael answered, his gaze unwavering, 'and where did this gash come from?' 'It.. it seems like she was.. she was cut with a shard of glass. W- we found fragments of glass in her arm when we took her in, so...' 'No...' Michael cut her off, 'I mean who did this to her?' The woman gave Michael a dreary look, and that was all Michael needed. She knew the answer to his question, but was too afraid to say. She'd need a little reinforcement. 'Tell me,' Michael stated sternly; in a voice that forced the nurse to do nothing else other than comply. 'She.. she was mumbling the name 'Markus' before she fell unconscious... we think he might have something to do with...' The woman trailed off- it was obvious to her that she had said enough. 'Shit...' Michael frowned, a frown that panicked the woman further. 'Puh- please don't overreact.' Michael's smirk suddenly returned. 'Overreact...' Out of nowhere, Michael slammed his fists against the wall next to him. 'Let me see you try not to overreact if a family member of yours was in the position Mindy is in. How will you react then?!' Heads whirled in his direction as Michael took his right hand and bashed it as hard as he could into a food tray that rested on the dresser to his left, putting a large dent in it. He then spun around and stared into the mirror that was put up on the wall on the right side. 'How can you expect me to calm down when something like this happens?' Slowly, both of his hands reached up and gripped the sides of the mirror. He leaned forward, his hands being used for support, the look of disdain etched on his face. It sickened him to look into the mirror and see himself so helpless, but there was nothing he could do but pray for his cousin, and praying was something he stopped doing a long time ago. 'Hmph... I'm not spending any more of my life on prayer. Not after Chris.' 'Calm down, Michael,' one of the glaring doctors demanded as he stood over the bed of the wild man's cousin. 'You're not helping matters. Just calm down...' '...' Michael's eyes snapped shut, but only for a second. When they opened again, all they saw was hatred. 'Calm down?' Suddenly, the shatter of glass caught the attention of everyone in the room, except for Mindy who lay still in her bed. Anyone who hadn't paid attention to Michael's meanderings before were surely aware of his presence at that moment. Before he knew it, Michael stood before a broken mirror. Droplets of blood trickled from his forehead and down the sides of his face. Pieces of glass fell to the floor like raindrops all around the elder Mercer's feet. No look of pain crossed his face- if he felt any pain, it was the pain that another family member was left in the hospital. And this time, someone he knew... someone he thought of as a friend.. could very well have been responsible. 'Someone get him out of here!' One of the white-clad men grabbed Michael by the shoulder, but it wasn't his force that helped Michael out of the room- Michael left all on his own. The face that looked into his downtrodden eyes was more than enough to lead him out into the hallway. 'Michael...' --- 'No, Michael, absolutely not! What the hell are you thinking?!' 'I'm thinking... I'm thinking I'm ready to damn near kill somebody.' Michael and his wife Jaden both sat in the waiting room of Westchester Medical Facility, located in Westchester, New York. Mindy had been nearby, presumably getting ready for a show with Markus, which would explain her being away from New Mexico. The moment Michael heard about the incident was when he flew down, and Jaden wasn't too far behind. Jaden normally would have stayed home with Gabriella, but she knew how badly Michael got affected when someone close to him was hurt. Hell, he gave up what he cherished most in the world just so she wouldn't leave him- something which spoke more than enough about what boundaries he'd go to for his family. Who knew what he was going to do with Mindy as hurt as she was? No one knew... no one but Jaden, after he had confessed his plans to her. And just one look into her husband's eyes... to look at the blood on his face and realize that he just cut himself from putting his head through a mirror... she knew he would be crazy enough to fulfill said plans. 'You're not killing anyone, Michael,' she began calmly, hoping rationality would win out over recklessness, 'you're going to come home and look after your daughter. You can't be hurting yourself over these things, understand?' 'I can't?' 'Stubborn bastard...' Jaden thought with a sneer. She never was exactly the light tempered one, either. 'No, Michael, you can't. You have someone to look after, remember? You have to start setting an example for your daughter, and I don't want her to grow up coping with any of her problems by putting her head through a fucking window.' 'Well, hopefully she won't endure the problems I've had to endure.' Slap! 'For fuck's sake, Michael! Can you just think straight for one second? Your cousin is going to be fine.. you were put through a plane crash, remember?' Michael nodded. 'Still have the battle scars.' 'I know you do. And I know you're cousin will bounce back from whatever she's suffering from. She's related to you, after all. It's like you're all indestructable...' 'Except my uncle. Right?' There went Jaden's big mouth. She lowered her head, and sighed deeply- it was the closest to an apology Michael was going to get, and after how long he had known her, that hunched over look was better than nothing at all. 'I just wanted to make it clear that Mindy will be fine, Michael. She'll come out of it, just like you always have.' 'No she won't,' Michael answered coldly, causing Jaden to throw her head back and laugh out of spite. 'And why not? What is she dealing with? She's not dead, is she?' Michael shook his head. 'Nope.' 'Then what is it?' 'A broken heart.' Jaden stopped dead in her tracks, eyes narrowing. That was pretty out of the blue. 'What are you talking about, Michael?' 'Markus,' Michael answered, 'she was his manager. Apparently he's the reason why she's in the hospital, too.' Jaden hesitated for a second. 'So? She was his manager, that's-' 'Oh give me a break,' Michael interrupted, the distinct sound of repulsion in his voice, 'you think he was just a client to her? After all the praise of him she had for us both over the months she'd kept in touch with us?' Come to think of it... he did have a point. 'She can cope..' Jaden answered finally, with a shaky sense of uncertainty in her voice. 'No, you see, I don't want her to have to fuckin' cope,' Michael replied quickly; for the tinge of timidness in Jaden's sentence, there was as much definitive prowess to Michael's retort. 'I want her to be alright.' He placed a hand over his face, massaging his sliced forehead with his fingers. 'Michael...' Jaden leaned forward and wiped away one of the still wet trails of blood from Michael's cheek, before resting her head against one of his shoulders. The touch triggered an embrace between the two, one that may not have been a death vice, but it proved to show a stronger bond than the ones most couples share. And as hurt as Michael was, he didn't want to let the moment go- if only for the fact that the times he shared with Jaden that were as pure as that one were few and far between lately. For that second, the silence the two of them kept was as valuable as gold. But then, then there was... 'I'm sorry, Michael. Please, I'm begging you... don't wrestle again.' Slowly, Michael pulled away from Jaden's grasp and looked into her eyes. 'Why not?' 'I don't want us to get hurt again.' Michael's face was soon buried in his hands once more. Why was it so hard to let go? 'No, this time it's different... need to do something about this. Need to make that bastard pay...' There was no way he was going to let the situation slide. Even if he didn't relenquish his anger through wrestling, it was all going to get vented out on someone. He couldn't tell Jaden, though. He was instilled with such emotion, it was hard for him to so much as mumble a functional sentence. He opened his mouth and attempted to speak, but then... 'Mister Mercer?' Michael turned around to find one of the doctors standing just next to him. 'And what do you want?' 'Your cousin is awake,' the nameless doctor gulped, 'she asked if she could talk to you. Seeing as to how you reacted having to wait out here, we thought it best you talk to her.' 'How I reacted? What does that mean?' Quickly, the man stepped back and shook his head. 'Uh, nothing. Anyway, she seems to feel well enough to talk, so if you would like...' Michael sighed and stood up. 'Have you been taking too many of the blue pills? Of course I would like to see her.' --- 'Ugh, mm.. Mikey?' Michael pulled up a steel chair, unfolded it, and sat down inverted on it with his arms crossed over the back. 'Yeh, cuz, it's me.' Mindy sat up, struggling to get a good look at her cousin sitting across from her. Alas, she was still too weak to do much. Her right arm was bulked with all the bandages on it, and it sure didn't help that her head felt incredibly heavy. 'Don't sit up,' Michael continued, 'just lie back and talk to me. Tell me what happened.' 'Tell him... tell him what happened...' 'I.. you were right,' Mindy pouted, 'I shouldn't have went to Markus. He...' 'He what? What'd Markus do, cuz?' Mindy thought about what she had planned to say; thought about how Michael would respond to it. What if he didn't even believe her? 'He...' Mindy sputtered again. Michael frowned and leaned forward, looking at Mindy directly. 'C'mon, you can tell me.' He forced his lips to curve into a friendly smile- enough for Mindy to trust that she wouldn't get hurt any further from what she was going to say. 'Well...' she stopped just for a brief second to sort out her story, but something struck her a bit odd. '...Hey, Mikey, are you bleeding?' That was when Michael remembered the cut on his forehead, which sprouted a small chuckle out of spite. It was hilarious how he could just shrug off stuff like that. 'Oh, uh, I'm fine.' He reached to his side and grabbed a tissue from the stand next to Mindy's bed and quickly attempted to wipe the dried blood away, continuing to speak all the same. 'Go on, tell me what happened.' Mindy nodded slowly and let out a soft yawn before she started her explanation. --- Thinking of the way that you used to touch me My memories lost in your gaze Oblivion sweeping this Earth, you must flee Escape from this dust covered maze But this horror felt inside of your soul Is screaming to consume me This horror felt inside my soul Is pleasing to consume me 'Markus? I'm home!' Mindy practically leaped into the front door of Markus' house. After he had won his wrestling match that night, he wanted to go out drinking- something Mindy wasn't allowed to do for another two years. That wasn't to say that she didn't drink, but it was usually best that she didn't for how the alcohol affected her hyperactiveness, more or less. So she simply decided that she would meet Markus home, when he chose to return, anyway. Until then, however, Mindy had no qualms with watching some DVDs from her Anime collection on Markus' brand new Sony DVD player. Besides, it was one in the morning- how much longer could he be out? 'Ah, dear Saiyuki,' she crooned as she picked up one of her DVDs covers, talking to it and clutching it as if it were a newborn baby, 'how I've longed to watch you yet again.' Eagerly popping the cover open, she took the CD and placed it in the Sony and began to watch. 'Whee, nothing like anime to calm the nerves. Bleh, Mikey, woulda never let me use his DVD player. Always afraid I'd break it... what would give him that idea?' It was far different from how Markus would react. Yes... they were both different, for better and for worse. 'He's so sweet.. he treats me like a grown up, and he's never afraid that I'm going to get hurt. He trusts me... just like I trust him.' Her thoughts of Markus always resulted in praise. How couldn't she like him? He was nice, caring, and oddly attractive for reasons she couldn't exactly pinpoint. Maybe it was the sight of him wearing that large samurai hat of his with a cigarette perpetually dangling from his lips. Oh, how she couldn't wait for him to return home; then she could express her undying love to him.. or something of that sort. However, there was one problem with that. '...Hm, he's taking awhile, ain't he?' No matter. Eventually he'd come back. He couldn't be out forever, right? Well, she waited. And waited. And waited. And finally... 'Ugh. I'm going to fall asleep before he gets back. Meh, but I can't do that! I can't go to sleep without him around!' Quickly, she thought of something she could do to keep herself awake. Anything. 'Bah.. maybe I'll just go for a walk.' And that was exactly what she did. Mindy had gone on a nice, fourty-five minute walk amidst the cold New York weather. It also gave her the perfect opportunity to set things straight and clear hear head about her older cousin's worries. Why did you like the way I looked before? Why did you care about me before this? Why, tragic that you never looked inside of me Why, I think that this whole fucking world owes it all to me 'I hope I can get stronger.. I want to be just as awesome as Mikey.' She spoke those words aloud to herself, confident with no one was around to hear her. At least no one except crazies and homeless people... but then again, who cares what they think? When Mindy returned home, she fumbled her set of keys out of her pockets and opened the lock. However, the door still did not open. 'Eh? I didn't use the top lock... hunny bunny must be home. But he doesn't use that lock either...' Mindy was quick to shrug it off. Markus was probably too drunk to notice he did both the locks. He was so sweet when he was drunk, though. She searched for the key that opened the lock; which took awhile due to her lack of using it, but once she found it she was surprised to find the door open by itself. And there, there stood Markus Riley on the other side. 'Markus!' Mindy practically pounced into the large man's arms, to which he gently hugged her back before practically prying her off of him. 'Hey,' he answered out of fatigue, hat still on his head, black hair in front of his plastered face, 'uh, what were you doing out? Thought you were upstairs sleeping...' 'Nuh uh..' Mindy shook her head, 'I can't sleep without you, of course!' She poked the black-clad man in the side, to which Markus recoiled quickly. 'Um...' Mindy tilted her head slightly. 'Wha's wrong, hun?' The moment the question left Mindy's lips, she could hear a voice from inside the house. Another female voice. 'Markus? Who's at the door?' Mindy's eyes widened. All the color in her green orbs seemed to fade- what was going on? 'Erm Markus, who is that?' Mindy questioned slowly- almost as if she didn't want to know. 'No one,' Markus quipped back in defense. 'No one at all.' '...' Mindy poked her head past Markus and into the living room, and there she was- a young woman with the somewhat same stature as Mindy. Same hair color, same frame.. maybe a couple inches taller than her, but that's where the difference ended. 'Markus,' the woman started again, 'who is that at the door?' 'You...' Mindy muttered in bitterness. Remembering the way that you used to hold me I was lost in your evil ways And now the evil will bloom and the tyrants now rule And they'll rule for all of our day But this horror felt inside of your soul Is screaming to consume me This horror felt inside my soul Is pleasing to consume me 'I...' Markus started. He wanted to comfort Mindy- he never exactly meant to hurt her from the get go, and he felt just as bad about the situation. 'I.. I'm sorry.' It was a shame that Mindy no longer wanted to hear any of it. 'Look I'll get rid of her if you want,' Markus continued, but his words fell on deaf ears; Mindy was headed right for the mysterious woman. 'Who the hell do you think you are?' Mindy yelled. It was the first time Markus saw his manager get truly angry. Perhaps he should step in.. 'Mindy, back off.' He moved to get in between the two ladies, but it was no use. Before the woman could even get up to defend herself, Mindy grabbed her by her shirt and forced her to her feet. 'Piss off!' Mindy yelled back at Markus, before she wound up and slapped the taste out of the mouth of Markus' would-be one night stand; a smack that reverberated throughout the otherwise quiet house. It was blatantly obvious that she was not going to stop until she saw blood. Markus knew it, too, and ran over to them. 'Stop!' he yelled, staring as Mindy took the flailing woman and shoved her across the living room into the entertainment system which almost toppled over from the impact. The woman cried out in pain, but Mindy persisted. She approached the downed woman and raised a fist, but before any more damage could be done, Markus grabbed Mindy from behind and pulled her back. 'Cut it the fuck out, I'm too wasted for this shit, okay?' He turned her to face him, and they both looked into eachother's eyes. Mindy stared at him with shock; she almost wanted to cry at the vice grip he had on her shoulders. And she was just as stunned with the look of anger Markus gave her, before he promptly pushed her back into the bookcase behind her. She smashed into it hard, and the back of her head smacked cruelly into the glass vase atop of it from the impact. The vase fell and shattered to the ground, and with it collapsed the equally frail nineteen year old, who landed all over the broken shards. Why did you like the way I looked before? Why did you care about me before this? Why, tragic that you never looked inside of me Why, I think that this whole fucking world owes it all to me Markus stood there, with a look of near disbelief in his brown eyes. He was surprised he did that- either that, or he was afraid of what the reprecussions would be for doing that to the cousin of his best friend. He took two slow steps back, before he gained the mentality to look over to the other woman getting to her feet, and sighed. 'Are you alright?' --- 'That's all I remember,' Mindy coughed, 'I'm sorry, Mikey...' 'Sorry?' Michael questioned. 'You have no reason to be sorry, Mindy. Markus is the one who's going to be sorry.' 'No... not like that. I mean.. I'm sorry for being a bother.' That remark caused Michael to stand up, put a hand softly on Mindy's forehead, and smile. 'You're not being a bother.' Michael and Mindy shared a moment of silence, and it was all they needed to show eachother that maybe they were closer than one would expect them to be with how they annoyed eachother sometimes. Michael didn't say another word, though- he simply turned at the heel, and left the room. 'No,' Michael mumbled to himself as he left the side of his cousin, 'in fact, Mindy.. I think you've helped me open up my eyes.' Lyrics: 'This Still Born Earth', Cynder
