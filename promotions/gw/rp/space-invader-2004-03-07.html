Space Invader

7 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/7/04 Bon Jovi regails the radio audience with his classic neo cowboy track 'Wanted Dead or Alive'. This is an almost daily routine. Every week day at 4:45 pm KWBY am radio heralds the near arival and the near exit of one of it's most popular show. The 'Straight Shooter's Sports Review' is ready to hit the air, the show almost at it's end coming back from it's final commercial break. The station staff is poised a prepared, the whole broadcast no more than routine. Two men sit behind the glowing lights and host of sliders on the computerized board in the broadcast booth. Just looking at them it's easy to see their roles on the show. One man seems to radiate wisdom. Perhaps it's the light interspercement of gray in his predominately dirty blonde beard. His hair, slightly thining in the front adheres to the same gray, blond pattern as his beard. It's obvious he takes pride in his appearance. His khakis are pressed and the beige blazer he wears over his Polo shirt is neat and straight. It's definitely more effort than needs to be put forth for someone who works on the radio, but it apparently doesn't bother him. He sits now scribbling some last minute notes to himself on the yellow notepad that sits on the desk space beneath his microphone. Him? He's the good cop. The young man sitting next to him isn't his complete opposite, but they're definitely different. The young man, his long black hair pulled back into a slicked back pony tail seems well aware he's on the radio. His 'Dallas Cowboys' t-shirt and Adidas warm-up pants were both wrinkled, no compunctions about impressing anyone. His notes consisted of a few 3x4 index cards which he wasn't paying much attention to. His muscular frame was slumped over the desktop, propped up on his forearms, apparently making sure not to strain himself in any way. He's the bad cop. 'Alright folks welcome back,' says the good cop as he looks up from his note writing to switch on the mic. Of course it's Steve Ricker with you here on 'Straight Shooter's Sports Show'. And as always,' he tries to add before being cut off. 'And, as always, I'm your actual host Jake Spinicoli.' the slicker, younger man says in an accent that is unmistakeably New Yorker. 'Uhuh...' Steve utters mockingly. 'Anyway, my co-host and I are back on the air with you. And Jake, I just want to go back to what we were discussing before the break. That of course being this issue with the Cowboys line up.' 'Issue? Issue?! What?! Why are you saying that like this is some kind of stupid salary cap crap or something? This is waaaaaaaay more than an issue Steve! I mean good c'mon.' 'Ok, first of all stop blowing this out of proportion.' Steve retorts. 'Ahhhh, you gotta be kidding me!' Jake snaps back. 'Folks... folks, if you're just joining us let's recap here. Now the Dallas Cowboys announced earlier today that they were going to have to invest money in bring in some new additions to their defensive line up.' 'Yeah, yeah, yeah... Now tell 'em why.' 'I was just getting to that. That's why they call it a recap. Anyway... This is strange seeing that they just invested money in the last off season bringing a fresh line up that they wanted to grow into their roles in the future. Problem is three of these guys are going to be sidelined going into next preseason and even into some of the regular season because of injury. And there in lies the issue here because...' 'Oh, for craps sake!' Jake blurts out. 'Why do you keep sugar coating this crap?! The Dallas Cowboys, one of the most storied teams in NFL history are going to be missing two defensive linesmen and a middle linebacker because some schmuck from that gathering of degenerates they call Global Wrestling attacked them!' 'Well now, that's a debatable point of view which is why we've been arguing about this the whole show.' 'No, you've been arguing I've been winning.' 'Again, a debatable point of view.' 'What's to debate Steve? Look, we got a middle linebacker who's lost like 10 pounds of muscle cuz he can't eat solid foods on account of his jaw being wired shut. We got two linemen who've both had their knees scoped in the last week and will probably be going into surgery about the same time. Probably going to be back from rehab the same time too, pretty much the middle of the season. Now all of this because of this moron...this ahhh.... Train guy! This city and the team are shelling out millions of dollars to these highly trained athletes and this... this... wrestler goes and attacks them!' 'You keep say that Jake, but the fact is all the facts are telling a diffrent story. I've talked to the cops. I've gotten a ton of email and phone calls from folks who say they were there the night of the fight or knew someone who was...you know how that he said she said thing goes, but... It all points to the same thing. This is a case of self defense. These NFL guys thought they were big and bad and were dumb enough to pick a fight with a professional fighter.' 'Steve, what the hell?' 'C'mon, language Jake...' 'Screw that. I can not believe you're defending this guy.' 'Who's defending? I'm just saying... Look, the cops didn't arrest him, just about every witness says the same thing... It's kind of obvious this Train guy was defending himself. And being a fighter he does it better than most.' 'Oh whatever Steve. Look you always tell me I don't do enough research, but fact is, after this guy has jepardized the season for the Cowboys I did some checking up on him. This guy the Train? He's no fighter. He's nothing but a common criminal, ok?! He's nothing but a thug! The guy is from Chicago and he's got a wrap sheet longer than my arm. I can't even like count how many times this guy has been arrested.' 'So what, every NFL player is an angel now?' 'I ain't saying they're angels, but they're better than this guy!' 'Well for your information I've done my own research and you're right. Train...definitely been haulded down town a few times.' 'Thank you' 'However, I think it's important to point out that, as many times as he's been arrested... He's never been convicted.' 'So the Chicago PD is inept. That don't mean anything. Hell, I'm hoping now that maybe the government will step in on this guy since the cops can't do crap.' 'The government? What are they going to do Jake?' 'Get this jerk off the streets under the Patriot Act!' 'hahahaha... What are you talking about? This guy's not a terrorist!' 'Hey, let me tell you something Steve. Ok? The Cowboys? They're like mom, baseball and apple pie. They are a part of Americana. This Train schmuck, by screwing up the Cowboy's season, might as well be a terrorist! I say they just call him a person of interest and lock him away like they did those other nit wits. Forget lawyers and courts, just get rid of him.' 'Ok, you're gone waaaaay over the deep end with that. I'm not going to argue with you on why the Patriot Act is a load of garbage cuz that's a whole nother show, but my point is the same. First off, these guys the Cowboys lost weren't really all that important. Second off, it seems to me they asked for a butt kicking and they got one.' 'I knew you were a commie!' 'hahahahaha... Yeah, that's me.' 'haha... Look, my point is still the same too. The mayor and the city council need to shut this Global crap down. They suck away attention from actual professional teams like the Cowboys and the Mavericks. Two, this guy Train is a lowlife. All three of those guys were pushing 300 pounds and you expect me to believe this guy just took them all out? Give me a break! If those three wanted to take this guy out he would've been out. Any idiot should be able to see that the only way Train could have taken them out is if he hit them with a cheap shot...probably from behind since I here that's the way these penitentiary types like it!' 'Give me a break Jake, it's a family show here.' 'Well if this is a family show, let's send a family message. This Global Wrestling crap should be kicked out of this town and this guy Train is a coward. Period.' 'Well as far as family goes, my two boys love this stuff. I'm not that big a fan, but I catch it every now and then. As far as I can tell it's good fun, very community oriented. I've met the founder of the company, Bubba McCoy, a time or two and the always said that he wanted the company to be a part of the community and I really think it is that. As much as the Cowboys or the Mavericks. I just think these other athletes on these more main stream teams need to be aware of who the Global guys are and be smarter than to start trouble with them. Hell, athletes on both side need to learn to play well with others frankly.' 'Whatever Steve. You've always got to be Mr. Diplomatic instead of just calling it like it is.' Jake says in exasperation. 'Hey, I'm just doing what the title says...shooting it straight. Alright folks, we want to hear from you about this. I know the phone lines have been lit up with Cowboy fans and GW fans trying to say their peace on this thing. Alright... We've got our first caller and, ummmm... Our producer, Annie is trying to tell me something here... and... Oh... Oh, boy folks I think this show might just get interesting. Apparently we have the focus of today's debate on the phone with us. Ladies and Gentlemen apparently we've picked up a new listener in Global Wrestling's Train on the phone with us.' 'Son of a... This guy... this guys got like big ones made of brass to be calling up here.' Jake says adamantly. 'He shouldn't even be showing his face in this town, let alone calling the show.' 'Oh, for real?' says the mocking and defiant voice coming through the monitors. For a moment both men freeze. The voice may not be well known to the show's regular audience, but it rings clear to any GW fans who are tuned in. The man they call Train is on the line. Steve is the first to jump in. 'Train, you're the man of the hour here. I mean literally we've been talking about your brawl with some Dallas Cowboy players for the last hour.' 'Yeah, yeah... I caught the tail end of that lil' discussion. I was looking for a decent station to listen to when I hear yo' boy running his damn mouth about me.' 'Running my mouth is what I get paid for pally boy.' Jake immediately spits back. 'Hey ahhhh... Look here Jack...' 'Jake buddy.' 'Jake, Joe, Jack, Jimmy, Johnny... I don't give a (bleep) what you name is.' 'Whoa, whoa, whoa! Train, look I want to get your take on this, but you gonna have to take it down a notch with the language. We're on the radio man.' 'Aight, aight... Hey, but look... Jake. Let's clear this...stuff right quick. I ain't yo' buddy, yo' pal or yo' friends so you best to take the bass out your voice son. Before your mouth writes a check yo...butt can't cash.' Steve decides to step in before this conversation gets out of hand. 'Ok boys, let's break it up. Train what everyone wants to know is what happened in that bar that night? Why did you attack those three men?' 'What Steve means is why did you use whatever cheap shot it is you used to take them out?' jake interjects. 'Cheap? Hell, you said it yourself... Two of them fools is gonna be out till half way through next season and the other one is taking his meals through a straw. That don't sound cheap to me...sound pretty damn expensive actually.' 'See,' says Jake. 'This guys got no class.' 'Jake, let the man talk.' says Steve stepping in. 'Yo, it's like this man. I'm a grown ass man, ok? I told them chumps up front that they didn't want to play with me. We was engaging in a little competition. They lost and they refused to stand up like men. The triend to put their hands on me and that was that. I handled mine. Simple as that.' 'You know, you're so full of it. If you're so up front why don't you just admit that you hit these guys while they weren't expecting it?!' 'Let me tell you something chump. If I tell yo not to put your hands on me and you put your hands on me anyway? Dogg, if you ain't expecting me to hit yo' punk (bleep) then you ain't too bright.' 'A real tough guy. We're not talking about your little wrestling gig here man. This is all about highly paid, highly athletes that the league and fans are investing millions...you understand that? Millions of dollars in and you take a cheap shot and screw it all up. You seriously need to learn some respect.' 'hahaha.... You know... I keep hearing that same thing. How I need to learn to respect people. Hell, maybe you'll get your wish there this week son. All the odds are stacked against a brotha, know what I mean? Bar fight...in a cage...in the bar of the (bleep) who booked that match. Hell, looks like ain't no way a brotha can win.' 'Good,' Jake responds. 'Like I said you need to learn a lesson.' 'Hey, let me ask you something dogg... Who the (bleep) are you to tell me what I need to learn? I mean it ain't like you can teach it to me you little trick.' 'Hey, look here pal. You think cuz you spend all your time rolling around on the mat with sweaty guys that makes you a tough guy?! I've got a black belt in Tae Kwon Do and Ninjitsu, ok? You might be able to cheap shot a few guys and take them out, but I garuntee it's a completely different thing to do it to someone who's looking you in the eye.' 'It's funny you should say that dogg. You know you got yo little black belts in the bad boy attitude... Not to mention you way out in radio land in your home base. I mean you got all the advantages in here... It's just like this match I got this week. But see this conversation is gonna go just like that match. You got all the advantages, but you ain't got what it takes to take me down.' 'Oh really?' 'Yeah, really.' 'Holy... I swear to God, if you were here right now....' 'Ok, this is getting out of control guys.' The good cops says, trying to step in between them. 'You're damn right it's out of control!' Jake yells. 'This guys friggin' ego is out of line. And I'm telling your right now Mr. Train... If you were here right now, I'd do every Cowboy fan in America a favor and kick your ass.' 'Well, I'll tell you what. Since you such a big man with everything going your way. Why dont' I come over there and let you teach me a lesson? Would you like that?' 'Whoa, whoa, whoa...' Steve says, trying to turn this situation down a notch. 'Shut up Steve!' Jake bristles. 'Look pal, I wouldnt' want you to have to waste the money you got from mugging old ladies today on bus fare to get down here. And I'm not waiting on you so...' 'So nothing (bleep)! Hell all you got to do is step outside the front door and you can do whatever you think you need to do to me!' 'What the hell are you talking about?' 'Look out that window there and find out.' There is a pause there, uncomfortable to both hosts. The rustle of the microphones clatters loudly on the live air as both men scoot over to see the window on the left side of the booth. The window gives a clear view of the street aside from it's dark tint meant to obscure the sight of those outside on the street. Even through the darkened window the glossy, black paint on the Lincoln Navigator directly across the street gleams. But it's what's standing in front of the luxury SUV that's more interesting to the hosts of the show. A young black man, dressed in his baggy jeans and Timberlands, sporting wife beater that exposes his massive upper body stands, his legs spread widely and his free hand in his pockets. His other hands seems to be buired beneath his thick mane of dreadlocks...holding what must be a cell phone to his ear. As he speaks both men finally put a face with the name and the bravado. They can see his lips move as his voice ring out loud and clear as if he was in the room with them... 'No need for the bus when you got the Nav son.' Train says smugly. 'What the (bleep) are you doing? You can't be here (bleep)hole!' Jame blurts out, unable to control his reaction. Unable to adjust to the fact that his would be foe was actually within striking distance. 'Can't be here? What happened dogg? You was Billy Bad(bleep) a second ago son. Where you at now (bleep)?! You got it all going on for you chump. You thought you was all that a second ago when you was in your space and I couldn't touch you. Now all of the sudden, here I am... All up in yo' area... Invading your space. And what you got, huh? Not a damn thing. You can stack the deck, you still ain't got what it takes to take me down.' 'H...h...hey... Whatever pal. I'm not going to get arrested for assualt just because you deserve a beating. And if you try to come in here...' 'Oh, don't even worry about that son. See if I came up in there that could be considered trespassing. If I came in there and broke a piece of my shoe off in yo' monkey (bleep) they could call that agravated assualt. Those are criminal acts. Me? I ain't no criminal. All I'm doing is make myself available to you if you got something you'd like to come out from behind the mic and say to my face.' 'Ok, I'll say something...get outta here before I call the cops! And don't come back.' Jake says. 'Oh, don't pee in your panties dogg, I'm leaving and I ain't coming back. See if I stay too long or if I came back, that could be considred Menacing. It's only a misdemeanor, but I don't feel like paying the fine. Besides....' Train opens his car door and hops back in his truck. 'I don't need to come back. See cuz if you open yo' mouth about me again with disrespect?' The engine of the truck turns over and immediately 'Who Shot Ya'' by The Notrious B.I.G. starts to bump, carying through the phone and even the window to the street. 'I won't need to come back here to find you. Punk (bleeeeeeep).' With that the truck pulls off as Jake stands looking around at all the staff who are all now snickering at him as the scene fades to black.
