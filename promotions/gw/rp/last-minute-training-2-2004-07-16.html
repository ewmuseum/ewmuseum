'Last Minute' Training #2

16 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 16th 2004 “Grr!” screams Igor Arefiev into the mirror as he flexes his muscles. “What are you doing?” questions Constantino Zergolov as he gazes puzzled at his tag team partner. “I’m getting my pose right for my Global Wrestling pictures,” explains Igor as he strikes a different pose into the mirror. The two men are sitting in the changing rooms of the Bristol High School, wearing their wrestling attire. Arefiev is wearing a red flame covered mask and red spandex tights as he continues to pose and “Grr!” into the mirror. Zergolov is wearing a blue mask covered in yellow and white lines and blue spandex tights. He is sitting on a wooden bench. The door to the changing room opens and Coach Mark Jackson sticks his head around the corner. “Hey guys!” Constantino waves his two fingers, as Igor stumbles around to face the door. “The wrestling teams are waiting in the sports hall, if you want to follow me.” “Sure,” exclaims Constantino, as he springs to his feet. Igor grabs the handle and pulls open the door. The Flying Vergomovs step out of the changing room and follow the teacher down the hall. The coach turns a corner and steps into a sports hall. “Guys” Let me introduce? Global Wrestling Superstars? The Flying Vergomovs!” Arefiev and Zergolov walk into the sports hall. The group of teenagers gathered around blue crash mats begin to applaud. Arefiev waves in appreciation. “Con!” whispers Igor. “Yeah?” comes Constantino’s response. “Where’s the ring?” “I don’t know.” Constantino shrugs. The pair walk quietly into the hall. “Maybe it’s a different hall”!” suggests Constantino. Giggles are heard around the group as the two small men step towards the group. “He has socks down his tights! Look” The one in blue!” whispers a blonde kid, standing to the left of Constantino. Constantino turns his head and stares directly at the blonde kid. The kid stops laughing and swallows hard. His face turns pale. The rest of the group giggle even louder. “Con?” whispers Igor, “the socks are sticking out of your tights!” “Shit!” exclaims Zergolov as he looks down. The giggles turn to laughs as Constantino turns away from the group and pulls the socks from his tights. Constantino returns red faced to the group and joins the circle with Arefiev. The boys that stand beside the Flying Vergomovs are five or six inches taller than the professional circus performers turned wrestlers. “Igor?” whispers Zergolov. “Yeah”!” “They’re just a tad tall!” “I know” But so will every superstar we have to face! Get used to it!” “Sssh!” suddenly stops the chit chat between the best friends. Both men turn their attention to the direction of the noise and are met by a not too pleased Coach Jackson. “Gentlemen”! Are you ready?” asks Mr. Jackson. Constantino nods. “Okay then. Let’s begin. Today we are just going to do one-on-one matches. Mark” You begin with Luke.” Mark and Luke nod respectively as their names are called. The group separate and move towards some benches. Two teenagers remain on the crash mats. Luke, the taller kid, has blonde hair and is wearing a blue wrestling outfit. Mark has long, brown hair and is wearing a maroon wrestling outfit. The Flying Vergomovs follow the crowd and sit on the end of the bench. Coach Jackson blows a whistle. Mark and Luke circle each other a number of times. They try to rush each other but then drop back and continue to circle each other. “Are they dancing or something?” whispers Igor. “Beats me!” groans Constantino. Eventually, they lock up. The two teenagers push each other back and forth and then release their hold. Mark steps back and then charges at Luke. Luke sees the run coming and wraps his arms around him. Luke tosses Mark over his shoulder in a belly-to-belly suplex. Mark crashes onto the mat, and rolls outside the circle. “Ouch! That looked painful!” shudders Zergolov. “Why did we decide this again?” “It was your idea!” argues Constantino. “At least we know how to fall,” smiles Igor. Mr. Jackson blows his whistle. “Luke wins!” “We won’t have to. I was watching old tapes of Ely Braxton. He may be big. But he’s clumsy. We just have to wriggle out of his arms that’s if he can catch us.” Igor smiles. “We can run him all night long, until he falls over.” Some of the group applauds. Luke smiles, and then helps Mark. “Unless we knock him over.” “We will knock him over!” “It is ours!” The pair start manic laughter. It grows louder and louder. The teenagers sitting beside the Vergomovs turn and look at them. The pair stop laughing and embarrassedly smile back. “Simon?” bellows the coach. A tall black haired kid stands up, and walks towards the mat. He is wearing a blue wrestling suit. “And” Justin.” Another tall kid stands up. He has red hair and wears green wrestling headgear and suit. He steps onto the mat. “Five bucks on the red haired kid!” says Igor. “You’re on!” Igor and Constantino shake hands. Coach Jackson blows on his whistle. Simon and Justin touch hands and begin to sidestep to their left and their right. “Why can’t they just go at it?” groans Constantino. Justin runs at Simon. Simon tries to catch Justin in a move similar to that pulled off by Luke. Justin dodges it and spears Simon around the waist. Both men fall to the ground, but Simon’s heads falls outside the circle, followed by his upper body. The whistle blows. “Justin wins!” “Oh yeah!” cheers Igor. “Pay up loser!” Constantino sighs. “You can pay me later!” smiles Igor. “No, no. I’ll pay you now. I’ll only forget.” “How?” asks a puzzled Igor. Constantino reaches down his tights and pulls out five dollars. “Here you go,” exclaims Constantino as he hands over his money. Igor looks disgusted. “Ugh! No more bets!” Mr. Jackson blows his whistle. “Arefiev” You can go next!” Igor turns and looks over at the teacher. Coach Jackson smiles. “Come on buddy!” Constantino smiles and pats his partner on the back. “You can do it!” Igor swallows hard and stands to his feet. He walks over to the mat. “Conrad. You can take him on!” A large boy stands up. He has a shaved head, and is very muscular. As he walks over to the mat and towers over Igor. The whistle blows. Igor steps back, and circles around Conrad. Conrad just smiles and watches the smaller man. Conrad bounds towards Igor. Arefiev drops to his knees and crawls through the large teenager’s legs. He stands up and jump onto Conrad’s shoulders. “Go on Igor!” cheers Zergolov. Conrad grabs Igor’s head and legs, and then drops the small Polish man onto the mat with a Death Valley driver. Igor bounces outside the circle. The wrestling team jump to their feet and cheer Conrad. He plays to the crowd and then helps his defeated opponent to his feet. Mr. Jackson blows his whistle. Quietly, Igor walks over and sits down. “What happened?” enquiries Constantino. “He got lucky,” is the snappy reply. The whistle is blown again. “Matthew” You’re going to take on Zergolov.” Matthew stands up and walks to the mat. He is wearing a blue outfit and of the same build as Conrad. “I suppose I can’t do any worse than you,” jokes Zergolov as he gets up and walks over to the mat. The whistle blows. Matthew looks to circle his opponent, but Constantino is having none of it. He runs at Matthew and baseball slides at his right knee. Matthew buckles over onto his knee as Zergolov makes connection. He then jumps up and catches Matthew in the jaw with a roundhouse kick. Blood splatters across the mat, as Matthew crashes down onto the ground. The crowd groan, as Zergolov jumps up and down celebrating. The whistle blows. An excited Constantino runs over to Igor. “What did you think?” “Very impressive,” groans Igor as he rubs at his neck. “Where did you learn that?” “I decided to a bit of kickboxing in preparation for the whole wrestling thing. I guessed that the wrestlers would be bigger than us so I needed a way to bring them down a peg.” “And you didn’t think of asking me to come along.” Constantino shakes his head. “Nope. This way, we will have different styles. People we face, we not know what to expect against us.” Igor sighs. “I still would have liked a bit of kickboxing help.” “Oh well,” smiles Zergolov, “don’t worry. On Monday I’ll bring Ely and El Serpenti down to our size and you can finish them. We are quicker than them. We are fitter than them. We have more tricks than them. This is our night to shine. Just you wait and see.” Igor smiles. Zergolov smiles. In the background, the wrestling team continue to train. “Coach!” yells Igor. “Yeah!” Coach Jackson turns around. “We are going down to the gym!” Mr. Jackson nods. The Flying Vergomovs stands up and leave the hall.
