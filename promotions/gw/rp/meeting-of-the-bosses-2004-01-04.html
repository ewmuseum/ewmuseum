Meeting Of The Bosses!!! Terrelle Green Global Wrestling Promo #842 Date: 1/4/04 Location: The Bronx,New York Terrelle green after his encounters with the police and all the attention reflecting on opposite gangs in America and across the world. The fathers of the gangs called a meeting with Terrelle to meet on neutral ground. Terrelle agreeing to this meeting was told to come to The Bronx in New York to a sealed warehouse. The men walk in the warehouse each men followed by two bodyguards and they stand waiting for the arrival of Terrelle Green who is late. All of the sudden Terrelle comes through the door with a all white italian suit with white allagator shoes to match. The head of the meeting The Boss as he is called stands inviting the other men to sit down. 'Hello gentlemen. says The Boss. Today we meet today and I know everyone knows everyone but we have to be reintroduced. Starting from the right each men will say there name and who they represent.' 'I am Le Cheng of The Yakuza.' 'I am Hashen Kein of The Triads.' 'I am Valdimir of Russian Mob.' 'I am Troy of Kingston,Jamaica.' 'I am Pablo of Cuba.' 'I am Sashine of the Cartel.' '.......I am The Godfather Terrelle Greeniso' of The Mafia.' 'And I am the spokesman The Boss The overseer of peace.' The gentlemen all look at Terrelle and Terrelle sits back in his chair. they all begin talking and whispering to there men and Terrelle and his men sit back motionless with no worry in there eyes. 'Gentlemen....Gentlemen please quiet you have a problem with Don Greeniso I am told. So tell him what is your problem.' says The Boss. 'He is reckless doesnt care about nothing but himself.' says Le Cheng. 'He is greedy he wants verything to himself he doesnt share with us his plans nor profits.' says Hashen Kein. Terrelle pulls out a cigar and begins lighting it smiling as the men continue there complaints. 'He has no honor he is a disrespectful man and he doesnt have any means of listening to authoriity.' says Valdmir. 'He wasnt to control everything and he cant he has to let law and order serve its purpose.' says Troy. 'He has destroyed and stole land from Cuba and killed my brother Padrino and this must stop.' says Pablo. 'This...This disgrace has taken over 200 million dollars worth of my stuff and used it for his own persoanl use.' says Sashine. 'Don Greeniso' what do you have to says about what your brother have said about you?' asks The Boss. Terrelle puffs his cigar and laughs at the men who look at him in anger. The men look back at Terrelle glaring in his face as he begins to speak. '....I am not gonna stand back and get run over by any of you men who think you are just so dangerous. I sat back while you rolled over on my father and damn near caused the family to stop. You know what I told you all that you were the cause of many fights and wars in the streets. You control your shit I will control whatever I feel.' says Terrelle. 'Terrelle these men have families they depend on the money coming from the US and if you stop that they will take a lost also.' says The Boss. 'Thats right but we can all make money if you all would cooperate with my words and offers.' says Terrelle. 'And what would be your offer Don Greeniso'?' asks Valdmir 'The Russian gun dealings I want in.' says Terrelle. He looks at Sashine. 'The cartel drug market and trade of huge loads of cocaine I want in.' says Terrelle. He looks at Le Cheng 'The Yakuza hits, Car Importings and also heronie dealings. I want in.' says Terrelle. He looks at Hashen Kein. 'The triads corperations and conterfeit money scams. I want in.' says Terrelle. He looks at Pablo. 'The lands filled with yayo and trucking company fronts. I want in.' says Terrelle. He looks at Troy. 'The Jamaican marajuana shipping. I want in.' says Terrelle. Then men all look at each other. 'What do we get out of you?' says Valdmir. '.......You love your lives dont you?' asks Terrelle.' All the men jump up with there guards and rush toward Terrelle who still remians sitting. All the sudden the doors fly open and atleast a hundred men come in with guns and Terrelle throws the cigar in The Boss's face. He stands in fron of all his men who are holding guns to all the world gang leaders. 'I expect to see some progress by in the morning or there will be a war in the streets of Jamaica,Japan,China,Russia,America,Cuba,and Columbia. I will not tolerate late payments and i will not tolerate any kind of attitude. Either what i say will be abided by or all of you will have no means of supporting your family. It may be a long war but believe me I know I have the man power to do what I want. I know you see what is going on now so I suppose you all have your first payment?' The men one by one signal there men to go to there cars each bringing back two suitcases full of money. Terrelle's men take the suitcases and walk to his Bullet Proof Hummer and sit it in until everyone of the men gives him a down payment. 'Boss where are your payments?' asks Terrelle. 'What do you mean where is my payemnt?' asks The Boss. 'Is there a fuckin echo in here you heard what I said and since you are the peace coordinator you owe double. So signal your men to bring me my fuckin money or you will really be the fuckin peace coordinator only they will coordinate your pieces.' 'Terrelle you will pay.' 'No you will now give me my money I aint got all day I got a whole lot of new buisinesses to run.' says Terrelle. He signals his men who bring four suitcases from the car and the rest of the men pull off as Terrelle gets in his car and rides off leaving The Boss looking at him. 'Hey how much money was in those suitcases?' asks Terrelle. 'All together Don Greeniso' there was two hundred and seventy five million sir.' says Greg. 'I only wanted two hundred thousand a piece well atleast I know what to get every month.' 'Do you think they will pay up?' asks Greg. 'I hope they dont cause thats where all the fun starts.'
