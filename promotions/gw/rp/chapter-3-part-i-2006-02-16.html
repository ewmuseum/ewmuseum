Chapter 3 - Part I

16 February 2006

Date: 2-16-06 God creates man, man creates monster, monster goes on rampage and kills man, and then an outlaw of the Deathscythe variety comes along and beats the holy hell out of the monster! February 10th, 2006 was the night that my dance partner had the rude awakening of discovering that he wasn’t invincible. He also discovered it was a fatal mistake to underestimate me. Underestimating an outlaw is almost like signing your own death warrant. Have to understand that an outlaw doesn’t take shit from nobody and doesn’t lie down for no one including fighters who think being big is bad. Perhaps, he’ll remember that before he decides to tango with me again? “Jackson Namenlos-Duisternis” Jackson had a plan. His plan was to make me feel exact the same kind pain that he gave Michael Cervantes one week ago. I guess he still hasn’t forgotten the fact that I made him taste that steel chair at Reload. Hey, that’s what he gets for butting in. Not my fault he got eliminated. Well, it is, but still he had no right to butt in. Anyways, like the asshole I am, I didn’t cooperate with his plan as I went with my own plan to kick his ass from one side to the octagon to another. I’ll admit that I was a bit skeptic that he managed to kick out after he literally felt the wrath of the outlaw. I know that put a stupid little grin on his face. He figured with my finisher broken that it was all over for me. The finisher may’ve been broken, but that didn’t mean I didn’t have a trick or two up my sleeve. When I think about it, it’s kinda ironic on what move I decided to use to knock his ass out. Jackson is known as The Darkness and I used the Heavenly Lights. Guess it can be said that I shed some light of truth to him. And that certain truth is no matter how tall you are or how big you are, once you step foot on the Battlefield with me you’ll become just another victim or should I say another causality of battle? With the battle against Global’s so-called boogie monster said and done, it is time to move on to the next one. So, there’s only one thing left to ask. Who’s the next causality? [:: ----- ::] ==|Part I: NEST|== The scene opens in the backroom of Jun’s Herbal Store where Jun was looking through a magnifying glass at the cerulean colored markings that was on Kyo’s right forearm. Jun looks with surprise after he moved the magnifying glass from his face. “There’s no doubt about it. This is in fact the Wing of Neos.” ---- The Wing of Neos is sacred markings that give the wielder divine powers that can rival that of God’s. ---- Jun soon looks at the crimson colored markings on Kyo’s left forearm. “And this one is the Flames of Hades.” ---- The Flames of Hades is also sacred markings that give the wielder that can rival that of Lucifer’s. ---- Kyo lights a cigarette with his silver lighters, as he stared at Jun with a mix of puzzlement and seriousness on his face. “I know what these markings are. I just want to know why the hell these markings are on my arms.” “You tell me.” Jun looked with deep thought. “Perhaps, Mother and Megumi were both right about you. You are an angel and yet you are also a demon.” Kyo takes a long drag from his cigarette before he breathed smoke pass his lips. “I can’t be both. It’s either one or the other.” He looked at Jun. “You even know that angels can’t have demon blood and the same goes for demons.” “Don’t forget about Lucifer.” Kyo looks at Jun with puzzlement. “Remember that he was once an angel before he cast out of Heaven.” Kyo closes his eyes with a relax look on his face. “That’s true. But you also have to remember that he turned into a full demon, so there’s no trace of his angel self after his transformation.” “Maybe the swords within your arms could be the reason for the markings.” Jun looked at Kyo. “The markings could be preventing your arms from being ripped apart due to the awesome power that the swords possess.” “I doubt that.” Kyo put on his gauntlets one at a time. “I’m not sensing anything threatening about these swords.” He stared down at his hands with tranquility on his face. “It’s more like I sense that these swords have finally returned to me.” Jun stares at Kyo with puzzlement. He soon closes his eyes with a sigh. “It appears that I’m going have to pull an all-nighter on this mystery.” Jun approached a bookshelf and pull out a couple of books. “I’ll call you when I have discovered something about both markings.” “I owe you one, Jun.” Jun sits the books down on the nearby table. “Don’t worry about it I mean that’s what friends are for, right?” He smirked. Kyo nods. “Well, I better get going. I have a tricked out motorcycle to pick up back in the states.” He put on his trench coat along with his orange lens sunglasses and a black colored hat. Jun looks at Kyo with puzzlement. “What’s with the disguise?” Kyo blinks with a slight smirk on his face. “Uh, the last time I remember I was a wanted man around these parks since the police still think that I’m responsible for the death of my own mother.” “You’re not anymore.” Kyo looks with puzzlement on his face. “What?” Jun nods. “Yeah, a week ago it was reported the manhunt for you has been cancelled. I know this is good news, but?” He looked at Kyo. Kyo looks with seriousness behind his sunglasses. “But it’s too damn peculiar.” He had an idea who was responsible for this, but he didn’t know why this certain person would do this. It was evening time when Kyo left his friend’s store. He starts walking away, but he soon stopped walking from suddenly sensing that he was being watched from nearby. He looks around the area and saw that there was no one except for the natives here. Kyo sharpens his eyes in seriousness, as he gave the area one last glance before resumed walking. Kyo’s senses were right, as one of Mother’s Ghost Men soon came out of hiding in a back alley that was across the street from Jun’s Herbal Store. He began communicating with someone on his earpiece. However, he wasn’t following the primary target that was Kyo instead he stayed where he was with his cold eyes locked on the store. What was this about? [:: ----- ::] It was night at central Okinawa. Kyo decided to make a detour at a local bar, as he wanted to get a couple of rounds of Okinawa’s sake before he left for the states. He was sitting alone at the bar counter with a sake cup in his hand, as he staring at his own reflection in his drink. “This is to surviving another day to kick more asses.” Kyo drunk the sake in one gulp and sighed out in response to the slight burning of the sake he felt in his throat. He begins pouring himself another round of sake. An attractive woman walks up to Kyo. “Hello stranger.” Kyo looks at the woman with a smirk. “Hey.” “What is a good looking man like you doing sitting here alone?” She leaned back on the bar counter with seductive eyes on him. “I’m just enjoying time alone.” “That doesn’t sound too fun. How’ll bout I give you some company perhaps somewhere a bit more private?” She smiled. Kyo closes his eyes with a soft chuckle. “I know I must be an idiot for saying this, but I think I’ll have to pass tonight, darling. Pretty much feel like being alone.” He smiled at her. “Sorry.” The woman closes her eyes and shrugged her shoulder. “It’s your lost.” She left him in search for another male companion. Kyo sighs softly to himself, as he pulled out the silver wedding band from within his shirt and stared down at it with great sorrow of the thought of his beloved. His thoughts are suddenly broken at the sound of some familiar music. Kyo looks and saw that the music was coming from an old arcade machine. Kyo looks with shock. “It can’t be?” He slipped the band in his shirt before he got up from his seat and approached the machine. Kyo sees that the game was the 98? edition of the King of Fighters series. “I didn’t think there was no more of these.” He was still in shock, but he soon regained his composure and decided to play a quick round of his favorite game of all time. ---- There’s no surprise that he has a soft spot for this game after all this was the very game that gave him the name Kyo. When he awoken from his crystallized sleep, he didn’t have any idea who was and where he came from. It was a common case of amnesia. When the Kushinaga family took him in, they didn’t know what to call him. Outlaw didn’t mind being called Nameless until his memories would return to him. However, one day, his sister, Ukyo and his brother, Haru who would be later known as Josh Starwind was playing this same exact game on a home console. Nameless watched them play the game and that’s when he caught Haru’s game character that was Kyo Kusanagi did his victory pose with the flame on his finger before it blew out. Nameless mimicked it only for fun, but he soon discovered that he can will the power of a sacred fire and that would be getting a name all thanks to that character. And from that day forward he would be known as Kyo Kushinaga? Sure, his mother called him Kyosuke since she thought it was kind name for him, but he prefers being called just Kyo since it’s a strong name. That’s the Outlaw for you? ---- Kyo was playing the game with great focus, as he was already on the boss stage. Of course, he was using the team that had his favorite character, Kyo Kusanagi on it. “Excuse me, sir.” A waitress stood behind Kyo with a tray that stood a single glass of white wine on it. Kyo stares at the screen with burning seriousness in his eyes. “I’ll be off this machine in a minute. Right after I beat the living shit out of this guy.” He moved his body around slightly, as if he was one with Kusanagi, hoping that would give him the edge to win the fight. “I’m not here to play the game, sir.” “Well, I’m kinda busy to talk with you, darling.” Kyo didn’t bother looking at the waitress. “But this man in white asked me to give you this.” Kyo suddenly stops playing game which made his character, Kusanagi get knocked with a brutal attack from the boss. He faces the continue screen, as his raven colored hair hid the great rage that were in both eyes. It was obvious that he knew who the waitress was referring to. The waitress stares at Kyo with puzzlement. “Are you all ?”” “You did say a man in white, right?” “Uh, yes?” She pointed to the left side of the bar. He glances over there and saw that there was in fact a man in white sitting at a table. “Aion?” Kyo glared. However, he soon sees that he didn’t come alone, as he was accompanied by Mother and the large man who was known as Aion’s loyal protector, Zel. Aion signals for Kyo to come join him at his table with a smile on his face. Kyo still glares at him, as he wanted to draw his guns and blow that smiling bastard away, but he remembered that he was surrounded by innocent civilians who could get hit in the crossfire. “Sir?” Kyo snaps his head at the waitress. The waitress gasps and looked at him with nervousness. “Uh, the man over there wanted me to give you this.” Kyo takes notice of the single tall glass of white wine. He looks back at Aion and saw that he continued to signal him to his table. Kyo takes a deep breath and regained his cool. “I’ll be sure to tell him my thanks personally.” Kyo began making his way over to Aion’s table. The waitress watches him left, but soon saw that his drink was still on her tray. “Sir, you forgot your ?”” She was startled at the sight of the tall wine glass suddenly shatter apart. The waitress looked with puzzlement. It was obvious that Kyo shattered the glass with his some sort of a telekinesis in result of the anger he was feeling while he approached Aion. Kyo arrives to his table with great seriousness on his face. “Ah, Mr. Kushinaga, it is good to see you again.” Aion looked at him with content. “Please sit.” “I prefer standing.” Zel takes a single step towards Kyo in attempt to make Kyo do as his Lord’s wishes with force. Kyo notices Zel and got ready to do what he had to do. However, Aion stops Zel in his track with a raise of a hand, as he didn’t want a scene to be made. Zel nods at his Lord’s request and moved back to his side, as he glared at Kyo with great coldness. Kyo returns the glare right back at Zel, but he turned his serious brown colored eyes back to Aion. Aion looks at Kyo with slight concern. “I did want you to be comfort while we talked, but if you want to stand then that’s ok too.” “I see you’re back to kill more of the innocent.” “There’s no such thing as being innocent.” Aion took a sip of his white wine. “These filthy beings are all guilty for committing all sorts of sins.” Aion looked at Kyo with a grin. “You should know that.” Kyo scoffs. “No one is perfect.” Aion grins. “We are.” He wrapped his arm around the back of Mother’s neck, as he felt her rest her head on his chest. “Guess I have you to thank for canceling the manhunt.” “No thanks required.” Kyo sharpens his eyes in a more serious way. “I wasn’t thanking you.” Zel gives Kyo a nasty look in response to the rudeness that he was showing his Lord. “Well, we just didn’t want the police to have you.” Aion stared at him with more of a content look on his face. “As you can see that we’re not just perfect but we’re quite powerful too.” He turned and looked deeply into Mother’s eyes while he gently stroked her lips with a single finger. “Especially Mother here, she was the one who used her power to convince the Tokyo police chief to stop their manhunt for you.” Kyo lights a cigarette with his silver lighter, as he took a deep drag before he breathed the smoke out from the corner of his mouth. “Was that after she wiped her mouth off or before she got off his lap?” He glared at them. Aion sighs. “Why must you be like that, Mr. Kushinaga?” Kyo closes his eyes with a tranquil look on his face. “Well, because you’re an asshole and that bitch of yours is totally screwed up because she gets aroused by the sign of blood and death. So, you can pretty much say that both you make me sick.” He smirked. Zel moves away from and approached Kyo. Kyo quickly shifts his cold eyes over to Zel and saw that he was stopped by Aion once more. “You step up to me one time and I’ll knock your ass through that damn wall over there.” Zel growls in response, as he moved back to his Lord’s side. Mother smiles with arousal while she stared at Kyo. “Didn’t I tell that he’s rage is just intoxicating, Lord Aion.” Aion nods. Kyo turns his cold eyes back on Aion. “Now what the hell do you want?” Aion smirks. “I just wanted to see you again since Mother told me that you missed me. And I also wanted to congratulate you for winning your recent fight. For a minute there I thought he had you, but you once again showed me why I want you to join our New Order.” Kyo starts laughing. Mother and Zel look with puzzlement. Aion looks with puzzlement too. “What’s so funny, Mr. Kushinaga?” “You guys are.” He stopped laughing and stared at all three of them with a tranquil look on his face. “I was trying to figure out who guys remind me of and then it hit me. You guys remind me of NEST.” Mother looks with more puzzlement. “NEST?” Aion takes another sip of his wine. “It's an organization that went by that name and their goal was world domination with the use of their superior beings.” He stared at Kyo. “I'm aware of the King of Fighters series. But this is no video game, Mr. Kushinaga.” “Some video games do hold some merit of the truth.” Kyo stared back at Aion. “After all, don’t you want to rule this world with you all being the superior beings?” Aion shakes his head. “We want to create a new world for us superior beings. We don’t want to live with these disgusting creatures neither do we want their praises. We just want them to be extinct.” He holds his glass over one eye and looked at Kyo through it. “Join us, Mr. Kushinaga.” Mother still leaning on Aion with her seductive eyes on Kyo. “Yes, join us in our cause.” “I said it once and I’ll say it again.” Kyo closed his eyes and took breath, as he opened his eyes halfway, glaring at them with a smirk on his face. “Hell no.” Aion sighs to himself, as he stared down at the wine with disappointment while he moved it around in his glass. He stops moving the wine around and looked at his own reflection in the wine. “NEST, huh” I like that name. Nest is the birth place for a newborn bird, as our NEST will be the birth place of a New Order that will soon take this world by storm.” Aion looked at Mother with a gentle smile. “What do you think?” Mother nods with a smile of her own. “I like the name too.” Aion smirks. “Then it’s decided. We’re not known as The Organization anymore. We’re now known simply as NEST.” “I think this call for a celebrating.” Mother sat up and placed her hands together with a grin on her violet colored lips. “And I believe I have the perfect way to kick off this celebration.” She leaned over to Aion and began whispering in his ear. Kyo raises a brow with a serious look on his face. Aion laughs and looked at Mother with a smirk. “Oh, that’s a splendid idea, my dear.” Mother closes her sapphire colored eyes with a pleasing grin on her face. “I thought you would like that.” Kyo stares at them with suspicion. “What are you sickos planning on doing this time?” Aion and Mother response with plotting grins. Kyo slams his hands hard on the table with bitter rage in his eyes. “What the hell are you planning, you son of a bitch!”” His eyes flared up in a crimson colored aura. Everyone in the bar stop what they were doing and looked over in their direction with great puzzlement on their faces. “Temper, temper, Mr. Kushinaga.” Aion grinned. “But I believe that your temper will be put to ease when you witness Mother’s surprise.” Kyo glares at Aion with slight puzzlement in his eyes. On the bar’s plasma screen TV there was a news broadcast on, as it was talking about a new highly advance bullet train that was about to debut in a few minutes in central Tokyo. It was said that civilians and celebrities will be getting a test ride of the new prototype. ---- The markings on the Deathscythe Outlaw’s arms have been identified. But why have these sacred markings appeared on his arms? Are they meant for his own protection or for his own destruction? Or perhaps it is something else? And what does Mother have planned?
