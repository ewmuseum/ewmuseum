This Is My Dream 1/2

29 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/29/04 You'll never find another way to be You'll never change the way to live your life -August, 2004 'I win! Iwinwinwinwinwin!' Mindy Mercer exclaimed in triumph as she finished off her cousin Michael in yet another rousing game of Smackdown Versus Raw. The older Michael could only sit on the pristine white couch in stunned silence as Mindy stood up and did what was slowly becoming her trademark victory dance- The Cryp Walk. 'I wish I never taught her this game,' Michael thought coldly to himself, 'and teaching her that dance... good God, that was pretty fucking stupid. I wish I had that one back.' In the middle of the commotion, a little girl- no older than four- wandered into the living room with a yawn. 'Aunt Mindy,' the girl said in a tired, squeaky voice, 'are you still beating daddy in Smackdown?' 'Uh huh!' Mindy said in cheerful reply, 'Eleven times in a-' 'You should be asleep, darling,' Michael cut his cousin off, speaking to Gabriella with his drooping brown eyes staring at the floor. His right hand massaged his forehead, as if he felt a massive migraine attack coming on. Michael was actually glad that his daughter was awake- anything would be a good excuse to get off the subject of losing at wrestling... even if it was only a video game. 'It's...' he trailed off for just a second to read the LCD clock that shone brightly in the otherwise dark room, 'going on midnight, sweetheart.' 'Aw,' Mindy pouted, 'don't take your frustration out on your daughter, cuz.' The hyperactive nineteen year old sat down next to her cousin, gently patting him on the shoulder. 'She just wants to be here to console you after me owning you! Who's the leet one now, huh?' Michael could only muster a facial expression- one that exceeded words. -__________- Mindy frowned and glanced at Gabriella, who looked nothing short of confused in her drunken-like fatigue. 'Maybe it's best you go to bed after all,' whispered Mindy to her niece, 'I don't think we should worsen Mikey's headache.' 'A little late for that decision,' Michael retorted with a sneer, 'God, who said you could call me Mikey anyway?' Mindy attempted to hold back a giggle in between turning off the Playstation on the shelf under the television. Her cousin was always a sore loser- always was, always would be. Maybe that was the reason why he retired after SAWF- it was best to go out on top than to go out after making things worse. 'Still... he's only twenty-four. He shouldn't be down on his luck, stuck at home like this.' 'Michael,' she started, 'can I talk to you for a sec?' But no one listened. Michael had already ambled off into the darkness of the New Mexico home, leading his daughter to her bedroom to tuck her in. Oh well, tomorrow was another day. 'No,' Mindy mumbled softly to herself, 'I have to talk to him now.' Michael Mercer was the greatest wrestler she had ever known, and she could honestly tell herself that he didn't need to be her cousin for her to make such a judgment. How could he simply retire so young? 'Because he's selfless, that's why.' It was the truth, or as close as one could be to it. Michael left wrestling for one reason- and that reason just staggered out of the room. 'Gabi... Jaden...' Michael's family had obviously meant the world to him, and never did he hide that fact from anyone. He loved his wife and daughter so much that he gave up his life for them, and they loved him to death for it. 'But still... does that make it right for him to give up what he loves to do? Mikey's practically a freakin' wrestling legend, but he won't compete into his thirties.' As much as Mindy had admired her cousin for the sacrifices he made for his family, she would never be able to understand it. 'So much going for you, just to give it up...' Mindy had always debated whether it was worse that her cousin retired from wrestling willingly, or that SAWF folded one month into his first and only World title reign of his career. He worked so hard, Mindy didn't know how Michael kept his cool with only being on top of the world for thirty days- especially knowing how emotional he got about those kinds of things. 'I guess it's better to burn out than to fade away after all.' Mindy never believed in that, but her cousin apparently did- as hard as it was for her to accept that. 'No...' He was always the stronger one; could he really just give up like that? 'No. I have to get him to go back.' ----- 'Huh? Who? ...The fack?' And at one in the morning, Michael Mercer awoke to find his little cousin poking him relentlessly in the ribs. 'What the hell are you doing?' he groaned, turning on his side to try and wriggle from his cousin's annoyance. 'I want to sleep, not get accosted by a little girl at two in the morning. I went through that phase when I started dating Jaden.' 'I just need to talk to you for a second,' Mindy pleaded helplessly. Michael returned the favor by delivering silence at first- it took a whole minute for him to turn back to face his cousin. Upon doing so, he muttered not an answer, but another question. 'Do you want that finger of yours ripped off and stuck up your ass?' Mindy gasped. 'Wha'? But... what'd I do?' Given the right amount of time, Mindy would have had a stream of tears flowing down her face. Michael had knowledge of such a thing happening, and decided to act fast. Michael sat up and stared downward. When Mindy followed Michael's line of sight, she found the problem- her finger still pesteringly prodding Michael's stomach. 'Oops,' Mindy said with an embarassed grin as her hand recoiled, 'sorry about that.' Michael sighed in disgust. His night of peaceful sleep was completely shot, and more than likely to talk about wrestling. Wrestling, God he hated that word with a passion. He hated it ever since the end of June. 'Why do you need to bother me about this now, Mindy?' Michael leaned back, resting on his elbows in wait for an answer- one Mindy stumbled with. 'Well,' she stammered, 'because.. it's...' 'It's about wrestling,' Michael cut her off, 'isn't it?' Mindy choked on her own words. Michael had that look on his face; even though it was too dark to see much of anything in the room, Mindy could tell that one of Michael's foreboding scowls were present. 'Isn't it?' Michael asked again, this time sounding more urgent than before. Mindy took a deep breath, but still could not form any answer. She felt like a child being put on the spot like that- '-but then again, I am a child... sorda.' Mindy was so deep into thought, that Michael grew impatient; impatient enough to clamber out of bed. 'Let's just take this matter outside. No need to wake the missus over this.' Michael was always quick to brush such chance of a wrestling conversation away. He feared the temptation of returning to the ring with every time someone begged him to come out of retirement. Yet this time, he was going to make sure that it didn't happen- he had to. 'I thought I've told you my decision about this,' Michael yawned in exhaustion, 'why must you continue to annoy me?' 'I just worry about you, Michael,' Mindy pleaded. 'I mean you look like that Ryan Reynolds boy from the new Blade trailer with that scruffy beard of yours.' 'Yeah,' Michael mumbled back, rubbing said beard almost out of mockery, 'and normally I'd take that as a compliment, but we're not living in Georgia now. Are we?' Mindy let the remark swirl around in her head for a moment, but swiftly brushed it off from the confusion it brought her. 'But I'm serious. It's not like you to just quit like that, you know?' 'Quit?' Michael inquired. 'Who says I quit? I never quit. And I sure as hell ain't gonna quit on them.' 'You won't be quitting on them by pursuing what you love to do, Mikey. You can't quit that way.' 'No,' Michael snapped back, pointing at his closed bedroom door. 'That's where you got it all mixed up. What I love is my wife and daughter, and I'm not going to jeopardize them due to wrestling. Not again.' 'You wouldn't be jeopardizing a thing, Mikey. Why give up your career? There are wrestlers out there who would kill to have your talent, and you're just going to throw it away now?' Michael gulped, and looked into Mindy's pale green eyes. She couldn't tell through the dark, but she was sure he was glaring daggers at her at that point. 'Because I don't need that shit in my life now, that's why.' Mindy frowned in defeat as her cousin went back over to his bedroom door. 'I'm going to sleep. You become a wrestler, and you'll see how hard it is.' And without another word of protest, Michael returned to his room and closed the door behind him. 'Wrestling's hard, eh?' Mindy whispered to herself. She had one of those grins on her face- one of those mischevious, childlike grins. 'I'll show him. Hmph, how hard can it be?'
