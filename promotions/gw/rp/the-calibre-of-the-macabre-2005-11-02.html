The Calibre Of The Macabre

2 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-02-05 And so it had begun. I had returned to the ring in active competition for the first time in around over five months and, as I half expected, came out on a loss. Cautious about my recent injury perhaps I was too sceptical about my own abilities. No matter, that was only one match. I had lost matches before; I would certainly lose matches again. In that respect I always felt a sense of sorrow for those who thrived on competition, those who lived for the “Big W” and trained endlessly to achieve success. I learned a long time ago unless I deeply wanted to inflict pain and humiliation I would save my “A-Game” for those who deserved to have it dealt unto them. Of course that wouldn’t stop me professing my greatness, which purposely served to remind those of my past achievements as well as rile my opponents up on a psychological front. Just because I didn’t feel the need to waste any excess energy on them didn’t mean I loved to watch them squirm. Thinking back to my encounter with Corvus, I wondered if my next opponent would serve more of a purpose than simply fodder, given the research I had done on his character, and his relation to someone like Corvus? My leg tingled slightly as I sat down to pack my bag. Contracting and relaxing the muscle soothed it, and as I looked up I caught sight of my face in a crookedly hung mirror adjacent of me. Examining my face I smiled at the contrast within my own visage. The weathered, ageing skin of a road-worn man slowly fading as life passed by complimented by the impenetrable, smooth and cold steel of my half-faceplate. It would remain long after me, the only thing the maggots couldn’t chew through. It would pass on through the ages and never change, while every day some minute insignificant changes happened within me that would lead me into the grave when my time had passed. My reputation as a Dark Gimmick was different to that of any other’in that I was perhaps the only human Dark Gimmick to grace the sport. In my “bloodline” as I called it, Clyde was an immortal, burnt, paedophilic, sadistic hellion. Nerezza was a vampire with a dark sense of humour with a Chupacabra for a pet. The supernatural was always synonymous with being a Dark Gimmick, and yet I was the enigma. I could do everything they could do, kill as they killed and held the reputation they all held’only I was mortal. The only difference’well let’s just say when Vampir Nosferatu “birthed” me he passed on something special to me, which in itself made me unique, as I was only human’at the time. Now? I possessed a power that would make any Dark Gimmick quiver with fear. Vampir was the first to learn of it, ironically. Azrael Cain, “The Reverend” Jon Hull and Clyde all followed. Perhaps because I was directly evolved from them however, both Clyde and Vampir survive to this day. Funnily enough neither has tried to gain some measure of retribution’and I wouldn’t advise them to. Grinning into the mirror I turned my attention back to my things, and packing them into my bag got up and took my leave. I spoke to no one and made a quick exit. I had arranged to meet my investor the day after the show in the early hours of the morning to show him exactly what he was putting his money into. An injury was uncommon for me, but Bruce Shanahan a.k.a. Violence Jack successfully broke my nose during one of our matches, using a move specifically designed to injure’and on top of a steel chair no less. We were on the same level, sharing a mutual respect. It was only fitting that he be involved in something of this magnitude. It would take a few hours, if that, to get back home to Detroit for me to then meet with Violence Jack and tour the property, 3 storeys and a basement to do with as I wished. I could literally turn it into my own personal Hell, provided Bruce obtained licensing and so forth, take care of the theory while I handled the practical. ? The shadows seemed to chase me as I walked from my home to the building. The distance itself was too close to take a car, but too far to walk in its own respect, rather nestled comfortably between the two. It had rained on the way home, and the sidewalk glistened in the bright moonlight, my silhouette shimmering against the concrete while the stainless steel of my half-faceplate shone in the lunar light. A long, black flowing cape ran down my back and as I felt a couple of drops of rain tap me on the face I wrapped it around my body and continued. Surely enough the silence was dashed as a spitting hail of rain fell from the heavens, and with a hefty sigh I quickened my pace. Glancing up after a short while I realized I had arrived, and took in the breathtaking view of a dejecting shell of a business. The old sandstone office building stood ominously above me; semidetached to a bank on its right, and separated from the Delhi on its left by a wide alleyway that led to the rear of all three buildings, allowing access to the delivery bays. I disappeared from the amber-lit street into the still of the shadows and proceeded down the alley, knocking into a trashcan that had in fact already been knocked over, leaving litter strewn across the ground. At the bottom of the alleyway I stopped and observed the thick mesh wire gates that prevented anyone getting directly into the bays. I chuckled and peered over the tops of them, standing at around head height with the structures. Seeing the padlock I reached for my pocket and produced a ring of keys. As I searched for the one that would allow entry into the bay the feeling of another presence in the area made me freeze. My suspicions were confirmed when a finger tapped me on my shoulder, and expecting Bruce to be standing behind me with a sly grin I turned around? ?Don’t move a muscle motherfucker or I’ll blow your fucking brains out!? “And found myself staring down the twin barrels of a sawn-off shotgun, behind the trigger of which was a significantly smaller figure than myself, clad in typical street-wear that I could only identify as Adidas brand from the logos. By his side, poised with a baseball bat in hand, an almost identical thug sporting Reebok clothes and a black bandana covering the bottom half of his face. Peeking over it on his cheek I noticed a slight disfiguration, possibly the top of a scar running down his face. I couldn’t be too sure, and with the business end of a shotgun poking me in the face I couldn’t really care. “What do you want?” I asked calmly, quickly flicking the keys back into my pocket before raising my hands. Taking a step forward as I did so ensured their eyes were on my face and not the object sliding into my pocket. Perhaps not the best idea as the sawn-off shotgun was pushed into the steel of my half-faceplate, making a clunking sound on contact. “What the fuck do we want” Empty your pockets you dumb motherfucker! You’re being robbed!” the gun-wielding thug replied. I felt my eyelid twitch as adrenalin began to pump through my body. The rain began to pour faster and heavier as the thugs forced me further back into the alley, until my back was against the wall. Again the shotgun hit me in the face as I was told to empty my pockets once more. Here I stood, the belligerent giant that I was, a victim of petty crime. I was just another target for them, whether they knew me as Hessian or just another freak ripe for the picking. For the first time in a long time I felt powerless, and ironically that resulted in being shot by a barbed wire-wrapped shotgun at the hands of Clyde in my second Weapon of Choice match in RPW. Was fate toying with me tonight? Judging by the look in their eyes any more questions would result in my messy demise. Rather than suffer the humiliation, or not, of having my brains scraped from the brick wall behind me I reached into my pockets and once again produced the keys for the building? “These are all I have. They open every door in that building there.” I said with a heavy heart, nodding towards my future pride and joy. “What”! No cash? Motherfucker I oughta-?” “Yo man, what if there’s a vault in there?” The bat-wielding thug interrupted his colleague, provoking a moment of thought between the two, a moment I was thankful for as the shotgun was lowered to the criminal’s side. In my mind I had already planned how I would break his nose with an open-handed strike, grab the gun and drive it into his partner’s stomach, pulling the trigger and watching flesh and bone splatter over the two large dumpsters behind him. My moment of triumph was squashed however as if from out of nowhere a trashcan rose behind the two and crashed down over the Adidas-clad thug, who instinctively pulled the trigger on his sawn-off shotgun and screamed as it blew off half of his foot, leaving a grimy dark coagulating splat mark in the ground. Falling to the ground, he fired once more and clipped his own knee, screaming even louder as blood oozed over the congealed patella bone and pooled around his leg. Frozen on the spot, his friend quickly snapped out of his shocked gaze and turned to me with the baseball bat raised ready to strike. Before he could swing however the shadows seemed to grasp at him, yanking the bat from his grip and throwing it down the alley. Turning to his assailant the Reebok-clad thug reached into his pocket and revealed a switchblade, flicking it open and raising his hand to wildly stab the saviour in the darkness. Taking my chances I lunged at him, clutching his wrist with one hand and pulling it down while thrusting my other hand up directly into his elbow joint. I listened to the orgiastic sound of sinew snapping and bone crunching as he fell to the ground and hugged his broken arm. His whimpering spoke volumes more than the screaming of his partner. The fear oozed from every sharp breath, the wide look of horror in his eyes at his own fate and his quivering bottom lip made me smile. He knew fear well, while his friend simply screamed ignorantly at his wounds, expecting them to heal automatically. It was clear he had been goaded into partaking in the robbery and for his own poor judgement he would suffer. I kicked the shotgun away and rubbed my temples in an attempt to sooth the approaching headache. As I looked back up I wasn’t really surprised to see a figure clad in a similar black robe to mine, marked with strange symbols. His face was unforgettable, being that it too was covered in strange tattoo markings. “I’m liking this place already, Von Kelsig.” He said with a steady voice. “And what fitting punishment should these morons be dealt?” ??Your timing, as ever, is impeccable Shanahan.” I sighed, shooting a sarcastic grin his way. “A stint in solitary confinement wouldn’t do any harm, would it?” I said, nodding to the two large dumpsters. Flipping the lids back, I turned to Violence Jack as he dragged the two robbers to the foot of the dumpsters, dropping them to the dirt. Endorphins had relaxed their state, reducing them both to snivelling in tears in the foetal position. Grabbing one each, we tossed them into a respective dumpster and slammed the lids shut in time to hear the petty thieves crying out again. “That should do it.” Violence Jack proclaimed. I scoffed in retort, pulling both dumpsters out a couple of feet from the wall and gripping them from underneath I proceeded to flip them back, wincing as they slammed into the ground, the lids jamming against the wall behind them and the muffled screams beyond the steel getting louder once more. “Actually, that will do.” I chuckled and turned to the main reason we were both there. “Now’care for a tour of the Slaughter House?” I asked, to which Bruce nodded his head. With that we sloshed through the puddles of rainwater, past the gate and on to the innards of the stone monster that would soon harbour all manner of Hell, as the moon slowly started on its descent into morning, bringing with it the ascent of the warming, blood red sun.
