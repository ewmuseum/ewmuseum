Late Night Ramblings of the Drunk and Beautiful

24 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/24/04 It's after closing at the Drunken Leperchaun, Donny and some of his friends remain just having a fun time and being themselves. A visibly and stumbling drunk Alexis is over a the Jukebox feeing the machiene quarters and making a selection. She selects one of her favorite bands, No Doubt, and on comes, 'Just a Girl' it's the first track on their greatest hits CD. Donny, Harv and Tim all just look at eachother with a smile knowing what's about to happen next, and that's Alexis thinking she's Gwen Steffani. Truthfully speaking though, Donny doesn't mind, he thinks Alexis makes a sexy impression of lead singing diva of No Doubt. It's one of those small things that Donny loves so much about her that no one else really knows about. It's an interesting point of view to be dating a celebrity, only you get to see what their really like when the cameras are done rolling. Donny takes another large chug of his infamous Guinness, and begins to talk about something totally off the wall, and one of his favorite things in the whole world, STAR WARS... 'You know what would be cool, to own a Death Star, have it somewhere out there in the far reaches of space or even somewhere just past mars.' Harv and Tim both chuckle at the notion. But Donny just keeps going on with it. 'I'm serious guys, think about we could just blow up what ever the hell we wanted like the Empire did in A New Hope and Return of the Jedi.' 'Donny, Donny' Tim interrupts,'But didn't the rebel alliance end up blowing up both Death Stars?' Donny responds, 'That's not the point Tim. The point is we can just blow up shit for no reason' Harv laughs with authority, 'That sounds like fun to me. Since we're talking about Star Wars, do you think deep down inside Luke was kind of dissapointed that Leia was his sister?' Donny is almost disgusted by the notion. 'Harv that's just sick in a way.” Harv, realizing what he said shakes his head in disgust. “You know what I think is weird” The original Batman and Robin TV Series.” Tim interjects. “Why is that?” asks Harv. “Well don’t you think it was a little odd that no one said anything to them about wearing their underware over their tights” elaborates Tim. “You know I’ve wondered that before. You know what I think, there is a special reason why Robin was called the boy wonder” I think he was giving Batman a little knob action on the side.” Donny mentions. Harv shakes his head once again in disgust. “That’s just sick, but I think it’s a valid point.” Alexis over by the jukebox stops dancing and joins the three guys in their conversation. She’s definitely drunk and loose as can be? She begins a new topic of discussion. “I think we need to nip nuclear proliferation in the butt. I mean come on, there are just some rouge nations out there who don’t need nuclear weapons?” Donny, Harv and Tim look at Alexis with absolute shock on their faces, they never heard Alexis speak like this before, could it possibly be because she’s drunk, and there is something we never knew about here or could it be she’s repeating something she heard on Fox News Network? Alexis continues on her rant. “Think about this, France has nukes and well they were in association with Saddam Hussein in Iraq, since the 1970’s Jaque Chirac and Hussein have been buddy buddy. So therefore I think France is a sponsor of terrorism and they should be dealt with. Screw the United Nations, they have no clue as to what in the hell goes on, their in on it too” It’s all because they hate the United States of America, it doesn’t matter who our president is.” Donny still in shock tries to respond, seeing what she’ll come up with next? “So what happens if we don’t eventually deal with France?” Alexis looks at Donny as if it was a stupid question. “Well the French Government is basically a terrorist regime. They have nukes and eventually they’ll get pissed off at us and launch a Inter-Continental Ballistic Missile and destroy one of our major cities most likely New York. Then what will happen is that the US of course would strike back and Paris would no longer exist and the world’s leading export of rudeness would be destroyed.” The three men are still in shock as to what Alexis has said. She makes perfect sense but she’s never said anything like this before. Just earlier in the evening she was talking about the Carebears and how she used to own all of them at one point and how she liked them better than Cabbage Patch Dolls. Something is definitely odd here. Could it possibly be the Alcohol. Donny is set to ask her another question but as he’s about to ask, Alexis begins to turn pale? “Honey, I don’t feel so good” and then she passes out into Donny’s arms. “Well boys it looks like as if it’s time for me to go home”, Donny puts Alexis over his shoulders. Harv shakes his head and tells Donny “Take her home and if when she wakes up she begins to talk like that, call a doctor” Don’t worry about here, I’ll get everything taken care of. Donny replies, “Ok, well give me a call Harv tomorrow, Tim give my regards to the wife and kids. Give me a call this week”. Tim and Harv both shake their heads in agreement and both say goodnight at the same time? Donny walks out of the Drunken Leprechaun with his lady in hand and many questions in his head. Is Alexis really who he thinks she is? Is Alexis really smart and just hiding it? The world is full of questions left unanswered?
