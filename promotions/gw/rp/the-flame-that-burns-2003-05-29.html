The Flame that Burns

29 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/29/03 A dim candle flickers in the middle of a small cement room. A figure walks by and leaves a shadow that engulfs the entire room. The cameraman stands in fear. Slowly the dark figure makes his way towards him. The cameraman begins to back peddle and runs into the wall, stopping him dead in his tracks. The figure has long blond hair, is wearing black jeans, black tennis shoes, a long black trench coat, and a sky blue t-shirt with white letters on it. The long trench coat covers most of the letters, but the word ' ICE' can be made out from the letters that are visible. The figure licks his fingers, turns, and smushes out the flame that was atop the candle. All of a sudden a light flashes on and the entire room can be seen. The figure slowly raises his right and pulls the blond hair from out in front of his face. His blue eyes almost pierce through the camera lens. He continues to stare into the camera lens for what seems like an eternity. Slowly the figure rolls his head around his shoulders in a circular motion. He stops and suddenly jerks his head from side to side. His neck cracks loudly and the figure lets out a small grunt, and a grimmice appears on his face. The grimmice leaves his face just as soon as it appears, and a calm look begins to reside on his face. A small little smirk then appears, and the figure begins to speak. 'The days are slowing disappearing. The time is quickly fading away. So all the talking, all the waring, all of it stops, and the real fun begins. The honor and privledge it will be to open up my first pay-per-veiw with you eleven other stars is unbelievably high. With that said, when THE DEAD ZONE comes around, that music hits, the lights go down, and the bell rings, all the honor, and all the privledge will be gone. It will be you and me. No more compliments, no more wishing you the best, no more MR. NICE GUY. I am going to walk down that aisle, step into that ring, and I am going to do what it is I do best. Thousands of fans are going to be screaming, and that adreniline is going to be pumping. I've been there, and done it before. Can you guys handle that? Will you be able to handle the pressure of opening the biggest show in this company's history? Are you ready for that commitment? Do you think that you really have what it takes? An evil, sinister smirk grows across Ice's face. A noise is heard in the background, but Ice's focus doesn't leave the camera lens, almost as if he didn't even hear the noise. Ice's smirk fades, as he takes in a long deep breath, and exhales it slowly. ' This coming Saturday night, the rules are going to change. This coming Saturday night, I'm going to show everyone that I have no equal. Because boys, for me, same game, same name, different place. You can throw eleven other men at me. You can put a stupid little belt on the line. But none of it will make any difference. Get ready, because it is coming. Get ready, because it is almost time. You can't stop fate, and you can't stop me. Soon the cold will hit, the hairs on the back of your neck will stand on end, and the fear will settle in. It will be at that moment in time, you will realize your fate. And there is no escaping it. The lights go out, and the flame atop the candle lights up. Ice walks away from the cameraman. He opens a wooden door and exits the cement room. The door closes and all the lights go off. Scene fades to black.
