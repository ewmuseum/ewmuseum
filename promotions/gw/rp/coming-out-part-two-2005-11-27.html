Coming Out (Part Two)

27 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.27.05 'Hold on a sec,' said the twenty-eight year old Ken Partridge Jr, checking his watch simultaneously. 'Shit,' he continued as he snagged his black and silver Motorola slide phone from his right pants pocket. 'Give me one second, Kelli.' The woman nodding at Ken in compliance was Kelli Alberdeane, accomplished publisher and screenwriter. The well built Raven haired writer pressed the pause button on her MP3 recorder and stretched her arms a bit, allowing her chest to press farther out in her black pants suit with pink pinstripes. 'Josher,' Ken exclaimed into his cell phone, 'when does your plane leave?' Kelli could only assume that the man whom she was prepping for a book deal was speaking with his best-friend and Global partner, Josh. She was correct in her assumptions. 'Look, you cannot be late for this,' Ken warned, 'its going to be a long flight to Tokyo.' A long flight here, she thought as she looked onto the city from their seats outside of a small sushi bar in the Ame-ya Yoko-cho Market. The fine Swiss timepieces and French designer luggage of dubious authenticity, cosmetics, jewelry, fresh fruit, and fish could all be seen in trade as rushes of people scurried off of the Subway and out of the JR Ueno Station. After, sliding his phone back into his pocket, Ken lifted another piece of unagi with his chopsticks and placed it into his mouth. 'What is that, again?' Ms. Alberdeane asked. 'Eel,' Ken responded after swallowing, 'freshwater eel.' 'Ah,' she retorted with a half disgusted look on her face, 'appetizing.' Ken simply laughed as he took a sip of his green tea, careful not to spill any on his navy merino wool Burberry of London long sleeve crew neck sweater with black and white checked shoulder patches or his black flat front tapered leg trousers by the same label. As he looked down to check, he couldn't help but admire his newest pair of Prada MILANO's from the Winter 2005 collection as the sun shined on them. 'I suppose it's an aquired taste,' he retorted as he looked back up and used his chopsticks to pick up his last piece of sushi. 'I just can't imagine putting any kind of raw meat in my mouth,' she chuckled as Ken swallowed his eel and shrugged with a smile on his face. 'So,' he interrupted as he took another sip of tea, 'where were we?' 'You may not be able to graduate,' Kelli itterated as she picked up her hot towel and took it towards her face. 'Whoa,' Ken quickly stopped her with a look of urgency in his face. 'Your oshibori is for your hands only. it's considered very rude to use it on your face.' 'My oxy-what?' she asked, perplexed. 'Oshibori... your hot hand towel,' he answered as he nodded at the white fabric resting between her fingers. 'Oh,' she said, setting it back on the table gingerly. 'How do you know so much about this type of thing?' 'I used to wrestle here,' he said bluntly. 'I spent a lot of time in Japan and in the Japanese culture.' A look of excitement fell over Alberdeane's face. 'Tell me about it.' 'In due time,' Ken smiled, 'but until then, we were discussing the chain of events that led me to coming out to my step-father.' 'Ah, yes,' said a suddenly more professional Kelli as she pressed a button with a red circle on it on her MP3 recorder, 'please... continue.' ---- The seventeen year old Ken slowly opened the door of JoAnn Holcombe's classroom thirty-seven minutes late. He made his way to her desk after quietly closing the door behind him and handed her a small square sheet of paper. She looked over it and then back to him with a smile and silently mouthed the words 'go ahead and take a seat, hun.' Ken nodded back to her and turned around, pulling the strap of his bag back over his right shoulder as it hung free in the center of his back. He quickly sat in his usual desk, directly in front of Megan. She smiled as his spikey blonde hair blocked her view of the trigonometry work that was outlined on the large white dry-erase board at the front of the classroom, slowly leaning up and blowing lightly onto the back of his neck. Ken let out a short twitch and put his hand to the very spot her breath had carressed before turning around and delivering a wink and a million dollar smile to her. He didn't know it, but that smile had won her heart many times over. Ken gave a glance to the board only to realize it was mostly homework. He pulled out a notebook and began scratching down the pages and problems when a sheet of paper folded many times over landed on his trig book. He opened it to reveal the letter that lie within. What was everything with Principal Martin about and what took so long? Ken took his electronic pencil and began jotting a response underneath it. The Superintendant and Vice-Principal Barnes got involved. I'll tell you about it in the quad after class. He reched behind himself, with the letter in his right hand, and grasped Megan's hand, holding it for a second before releasing the letter into her grasp. He heard the letter being unfolded behind him, followed by a somewhat disappointed sigh. ---- 'Megan Mehaffey,' Kelli interrupted, 'isn't she a model now?' 'She was until a year or so ago,' Ken answered slightly annoyed. 'Oh,' Ms. Alberdeane continued with a smile on her face. 'So, how serious were the two of you after-' 'Mr. Alberdeane would you like me to continue this portion of my book or not?' Ken questioned, cutting her off quickly and coldly. 'Yes,' she said staring at the table. 'I apologize. Please, continue.' 'Thank you.' ---- 'They said what?' Megan shouted in the quad during their free period, causing a few heads to turn their way. The weather was warming a bit, but the snow remained on the ground. Ken and Megan were perched on one of the large square cement structures underneath the concourse. 'They said I was making a list and told them that I was going to start 'picking mother fuckers off,' essentialy insuating that I was gonna come in with a hand cannon and take it upon myself to blow everyone away,' Ken answered. 'Those little scumbags,' Megan continued. 'I'll rip their eyes out.' 'No need,' Ken laughed lightly, 'though, I do appreciate it.' 'I can't believe that the Superintendant of Schools even got involved.' 'That's what took so long,' Ken said. 'We had to wait on her to drive here from Columbiana on such short notice.' 'Well,' Megan began with a worried look on her face, ' did they believe you?' 'Of course they did,' he responded as he shined an apple on his shirt before biting into it. 'Over Jim Bob Roper and Cameron Keller, you're damn right the believed me. But, there's still going to be an investigation. They said it's mandatory.' Megan reached up and threw her arms around Ken's neck, embracing him in a hug. 'I guess this postpones you coming out to William, huh?' 'Actually,' Ken began as he held her tight against him, 'now I don't have any other choice.' Megan pulled herself away from he boyfriend and lookd at him with a concerned look in her eyes. 'What do you mean?' 'Well,' he began, 'as part of the investigation, they have to come to the house, look through my belongings, and ask mom and William particular questions. I'm sure that one of those questions will be something along the lines of 'has anyone ever attempted to sabotage or threaten your son due to his sexuality,' or something along those lines, and to be honest, I'd rather he hear it from me than somebody else.' 'Unbelievable,' Megan exclaimed. 'Un-freakin-believable.' 'Hey, Babe, it's okay. I've already called mom and told her what's going on. They're not coming until tomorrow, so I'll tell him tonight. Everything's gonna' be fine.' Megan gave Ken a playful pout. 'How can you be sure?' 'Well,' Ken smiled, 'because I have you.' He finished his reply with a wink as a smile spread across Megan's beautiful face before she gave him a playful hit on the arm. 'Good answer, Cassanova.' Ken checked his watch, before grabbing his bag and slinging back over his right shoulder. 'Look, I've gotta' go talk to your dad. He told me to be in his office at eleven-thirty. It's got something to do with everything that's going on. I'll see you after school, okay,' he said quickly. 'Okay,' she said before grabbing him again and giving him a quick kiss on the lips. 'Oh, don't let me forget I have something I need to tell you later.' 'Will do,' he said as he began his gallop towards the entrance, opening the door and disappearing from sight. 'I love you,' Megan whispered to herself, 'Kenneth Edward Partridge, Jr.'
