Date: Unknown I kneel at the alter Soma set up for me. On both knees I bow. The lights turned off. Candles lit around me. My head is bowed. My eyes are closed. And I being to say the first prayer I’ve ever prayed. “in my whole entire life. “Lord, oh Lord. I lift your name on high. Thankfulness overwhelms my mind and soul. While mercy begs from my heart. The guilt burdens me. Lord, oh Lord. Forgive me for what I’ve done. And bless the ground that I’ll walk upon. Lay posies along the path. Help me glorify and love you. I apologize from the bottom of my heart. Please wipe the slate clean. And wash me in the blood of the lamb. The tides have turned Lord. For I am a sheep now. And I am loyal to my Shepard. Lord, watch over me. “and forgive me for what I am going to do. I open my eyes and look around me. For the first time ever, the candles burn a blue flame instead of the fire red. A sign from God. A tear falls from my eye and onto the ground. I close my eyes and finish the prayer? “finish my prayer. I repent to you, oh Lord. And I ask for your acceptance. Please forgive me? “Amen. I squint my eyes and push out a few tears, then quickly open them. I look around the room, surrounded in blue light from the flames. I’ve never seen a sight like this before. I stand to my feet and slowly walk past Brylou, who is asleep on the floor beside my bed. I pull down the sheet and the hand woven comforter from my king sized bed and lie inside. I blanket myself in shame from what I’ve ever done. A mistake can be corrected. “what I have done couldn’t be. Except from God. I shed more tears? For the first time in my life, I feel complete. I have the love of God in my heart. The missing circle that I’ve been looking for is now filled. “I’ll never let you down. It is late. Almost two in the morning. I know this without looking at my watch Soma gave me. I close my eyes, squinting out the tears. Humming a sweet tune, I fall into a deep sleep? NEXT MORNING I throw the blankets off of me and yawn. It is 11 o’clock. I know this without looking at my watch Soma gave me. I look down at Brylou, who seems to have woken up approximately the same time I have. He yawns as well and walks around the room. I turn around and get out of the bed. I pull the covers up to the pillow that is stained in my tears? “my tears of blood. Somehow, someway’the tears that I’ve cried last night were bloody. This doesn’t make any sense. I walk towards the alter, wondering if it really happened. The blue flame from the candles have died out. I wonder in my head if I was seeing things. I look at the front of the alter, a picture of Jesus nailed to the cross. I look at Jesus” face, wrapped in a crown of thorns. Blood, pouring down his face and an expression of pain. I look at him, then look towards my feet. “blood. I’ve cried tears when I prayed last night. But they’ve landed and formed a shape of” “a dragon. A sign from God. Suddenly, I remember Soma saying something about a dragon. Being, “the year of the dragon.” “what does this mean”
