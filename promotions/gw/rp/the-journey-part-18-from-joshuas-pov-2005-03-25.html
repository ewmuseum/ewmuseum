The Journey: Part 18/From Joshua’s POV

25 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-25-2005 Death and Resurrection Early Morning Sometimes in life you need to take a good look at your career in the mirror? In January of 1990 what started as a trial run in a rebel federation in the country of London, England began one of the most improbable stories for the wrestling world to digest: A young 13 year old wrestler by the name of Joshua Curtis entered the wrestling scene and became a name to a lot of people. Combining old school substance with some of the at the time “new school” acrobatics that were being utilized by others Joshua made a name for himself as a wrestler whom wouldn’t so much sneak up on you as he would become someone that you either feared or respected just because of the sheer fact that he was unpredictable and for a time didn’t give a damn what he had to do to become a champion in the then rebel promotion called the “IWF”. Joshua became one of the regulars in late 1990/early 1991 when President Daniels officially signed the young hometown superstar to a contract and at the time 18,000 Pounds sterling was a big deal then to him to the point that he would do almost anything necessary to win his matches. His big break came in March of 1992 when President Daniels ordered for Curtis to compete against the IWF International Champion Tina Davis and at the time the International Title was THE championship because the fed wanted to be unique and this was the way that they felt that they had to do it. On that night everything changed? Joshua walked into the NEC Arena on that March night and walked into the confines of a 15 foot high steel cage to face off against Tina because according to Daniels Davis had become unfitting of a champion that he wanted to see carry his title and it was up to Joshua to stop her any way that he could. Joshua entered the cage staring down the champion and knowing that he was only acting under orders from the Owner/President she thought that she could talk some sense into Joshua and get him to snap out of the spell that Daniels allegedly put him under’it didn’t work! Curtis went after the champion with a fury and a vengeance that no one has ever been able to match and Tina was actually fighting for her life as the man that she had begun to trust as a friend had begun the savage destruction of her being. Tina fought with all her heart and almost did the unthinkable and got Curtis to tap to her submission maneuver ,but Curtis? will to become the champion and the fact that he didn’t want to face Daniels without the title proved too much and he broke the hold with such power that it was thought that he(Curtis) wasn’t human! It was like the person that they were seeing in the ring against Tina was a robot or something! Curtis got back on the offensive and it would only be a matter of time before he would finish off the champion but how did he want to “execute” her? How sick could he be if the orders from the Owner were to basically eliminate the champion? Tina was about to find out! Joshua drug the champion by the hair as they climbed to top of the fenced prison and Joshua smiled the higher that he got for he knew for what he had planned for Davis she was NOT going to get up IF she could move at all! Curtis with all of his might hoisted up the Canadian Blonde and power bomber her from a sitting position off of the cell and the THUD that was heard caused an eerie silence among the faithful and when Curtis won the match no one could say or do anything other than wonder what the hell just happened in the ring that caused Curtis to snap! That was when Curtis died but now its different? Some say that the man that they know now really isn’t Joshua Curtis and that this man really is a decoy that Curtis sent to fool them all. Some believed that except when at this past Evolution the man with the mask pulled out the chain that bore the symbol of his best friend in Ken Partridge Jr. and people realize that now this man is here to stay! Joshua Curtis had arrived in Rome, Italy a few days ago alone. He wanted to make sure that he had a clear head for his mission so he and Ami took different flights on different days to the land of the Pope and Josh also knows that the official resurrection of his group HAS to begin now ,for now people are going to be expecting to see big things from Joshua and his faction after he and Ami won the match against Herendos and now that they established that they will not be pushed around anymore they need more people, more followers or dare we say more soldiers because with as big an operation as they are about to undertake they would require the assistance of A LOT of people! Its early morning in Rome, Italy and Joshua had just woken up from his slumber of about 3 hours in a suite that some would argue is befitting of one of his best friends and they’d be right. One thing that makes this room stand out is that on the walls are pictures depicting the period that Julius Caesar ruled and when the Christians would be executed by being fed to the lions it always made Joshua remember when he “executed” for a time the champion of IWF years back in Tina Davis. The thing that Joshua remembers most about that night was that it was the first “International” title he held but he never held a World Title. That belt didn’t enter into the IWF until after Curtis took over the Owner role and promptly fired Daniels from his position which might have pissed him off but made the fed a lot safer to be sure! When Curtis became Owner he also formed a group called “The Alliance” which was a group of 6 wrestlers sworn to uphold the fed’s ideals and was looked upon as a “heel” faction. Why no one could figure then and probably can’t figure now but it was more despised than the “Corporation” of WWE past. So now here we are in 2005 and Joshua has been struggling ,clawing ,scratching his way back to the top and he’s on the doorstep to becoming possibly the next big thing in Global Wrestling. He has really been on a downslope at late with his latest win coming in the tag match with Ami but today he just needed to be either alone or with some friends of his outside of Ami and Zack. Today he needed to get his “army” ready! Now I know what you are thinking *?Army?” What kind of “Army” is he talking about?* am I right? The Army that I am talking about is the “Army of Old School”, a new and improved version of the OSC and with a roster about as impressive if not more so than any wrestling promotion out there today! Want to know more about that roster? Well a couple of the members are in Rome as we speak! As Joshua got out of bed and got his grey sweatsuit on with his white Allen Iverson’s he went out of the room and jogged down the fire escape through the back of the hotel and out into the streets of Rome. He went on this jog for about 5 minutes before he saw someone that he knew! “Lucrezia!” Joshua yelled out as he saw the former Baronet at a jewelry shop not 10 minutes from the hotel. “Hey Josh long time no see.” Lucrezia commented before continuing “How are things going with you?” she asked and Joshua had to think about how to answer that because he knew how she got if he answered the wrong way. “Well I’m trying to get my soldiers for the “Army of Old School” faction that I’m starting up in the wrestling world. I just need some recruits and I’m trying to find the people that I trust to come to war with me against the oppressing fed.” Joshua explained to Lucrezia whom smiled as Joshua explained his objective to her. “Well you know Joshua I’ve been training to enter the ranks of pro wrestling myself and the fact remains that I’m open for recruitment if you need a reliable solder. I also know that another dear friend of yours that we both know has been having a hankering to come with me to work with you.” Lucrezia told Joshua and the leader of the former “Connection” smiled as he kind of had an idea who she was talking about. “Think he’ll do it?” Joshua asked and when he saw the smile on her face he knew that they were looking at the beginning of something HUGE! “All right we’ll give him a call as soon as I get done my business with my jog. I’ll pick you up and we will go visit this person and pitch the idea to him.” Joshua said but Noin had an even better idea. “You go get ready after your jog and I’ll come with him to your room”.1170C right?” Lucrezia asked and Joshua nodded before going his own way for a little while. About an hour or so later and Joshua was back at his hotel room getting showered up and ready for this meeting with this friend of his and he hoped that he would be willing to help out with the cause that he was looking to champion. *Knock Knock* Joshua had just finished buttoning the top button on his shirt when the knock at the door came and when Joshua walked up to said door he saw Lucrezia and she had a couple of people with her. The first person that she had with her was Quatre Winner whom you met this past Evo. Quatre is somewhat of the type that he don’t like to fight but if push came to shove he’d do it and while he might not like it he’d get used to it in a big hurry for Joshua because of the respect that he had for him. Next was Treize Khushrenada? Now its no secret that Joshua and Treize didn’t see eye to eye when Curtis was on St. Edwards and they clashed a little bit at times but it was only because they both felt so passionately about the different means of attack and when it came down to it Treize may have had his own manners in attaining his goals but he was respected like hell by Joshua THAT is for sure! And lastly? Well I think you may or may not know him but if you don’t allow me to introduce you to Zechs Marquis. The “Lightning Count” is one of the few people that when Joshua sees him he has to stand and salute to show the proper respect and that in and of itself means a lot to the group because if he shows them respect then they will do the same for him when he needs them to do something. What’s this? Someone else came also? Apparently when Treize came to the meeting he told another young lady about the meeting and her name was Lady Une. Now Une and Joshua have a pretty nice history in that they are fairly good friends so when she heard that Joshua was recruiting for an “Army” she had to take him up on the offer just because of the sheer fact that Joshua has stood up to her before he left and she was proud of him for that. With the gang all assembled Joshua stood at the foot of his bed while everyone else was sitting on it and he began one of his most impassioned speeches yet! “Its been too long guys” Joshua began “too ******* long! I missed having each and every one of you around and I’ll tell you now that what you have been asked to come and see me about today is a little bit different than working on a military base. Yes it’s a little bit more trivial than killing someone or taking over a territory but this battle is coming because I’m sick and tired of people in this land called GW not respecting me or the people that I feel helped me convey the values of “Old School”. We get pushed and pushed and pushed because we are considered “too weak” or “too unusual” and people don’t respect that. I want to change that and I figure that I am going to need the help of those that I trust. Now Ami is in and I want to know if I have the support of each of you in this room right now.” Joshua asked and in unison he got a resounding “YES!” “Joshua” Zechs started “You have earned the respect of everyone in this room and you know where I stand as far as our friendship goes. You are very well respected around the base and when we are in the arenas with you we have agreed to do as you ask. Noin is one in particular that I know that you are looking forward to seeing get in the ring at some point in time and we both know that she can handle her self in battles. My question comes to you in the form of when do we start?” The good Lieutenant asked Joshua and Josh informed them that at the “Clash of the Titans” the “Army of Old School” was going to make its first appearance which caused the room to actually cheer especially for Lucrezia because this was going to be her first showing in public ever! “Are you expecting the same reaction to the “Army” as you used to get for the “Connection?”” Lucrezia asked and Joshua thought long and hard on how to answer that question. “I expect the fans to react how they choose to when we come down to the ring Sunday Night and I step through the ropes to attempt to get my title shot at Wrestlecade. I know that one of my trusted friends has withdrawn his name so that he can focus on getting his belt back from the man that just took it from him but to be honest I don’t know how smart THAT could’ve been! Ken as you guys know well basically got injured a couple of weeks ago and from my understanding he suffered a concussion and now he’s gotta beat three wrestlers in a row before he loses one time. I don’t like his odds quite frankly.” Joshua told the group before him and that is when Treize and Lady Une decided to make their presence known at the meeting. “Mr. Curtis” Lady Une started “if Ken’s health is so important to you then why don’t you go and get him to not compete in the match” Its obvious your concern for your friend and that is most admirable. I remember the concern you expressed when Noin got injured in a battle and you wouldn’t let anyone near her because you thought they wanted to injure her further and therefore you protected her like a little brother would his big sister. You were praised like no other person on the base and in some case was going to be given a place among the soldiers regardless of your homosexuality.” Lady Une rationalized to Joshua and while he took everything into account that the Count had said Joshua had to be the one to basically explain to her how things were. “Lady Une I have much respect for you but you have to understand that Ken is a big boy and he don’t need me to tell him what to do and what not to do. I am worried that he might be risking serious injury yes and I would rather he stay away from a match where there are 3 other people in the match that could cause serious injury to him but I can’t do much in that respect.” Joshua resigned sorrowfully and Lady Une hated seeing one of her favorite soldiers like this ,so Lady Une did what she felt she could do: She embraced him like the older sister that he looked up to her as and that made him feel so happy! “Joshua people do tend to make decisions that aren’t always the best and we know that right now you are essentially powerless to stop Ken from committing the suicide that might await him in the ring at COTT but remember that you also have a mission to fulfill. You have to get in there and move one step closer to the dream that you have had for years and then maybe end the suffering that has been with you for months by moving on to Zenith and ending that conflict once and for all!” Lady Une told Joshua and he nodded before speaking again. “I know Lady Une and everyone I’m just glad that you are here for me now and wherever I go I know that my Army will be with me for the support when I need it and Quatre in about 3-4 weeks you and I got some business to attend to elsewhere. You ready hun?” Joshua asked and the Young Arabian nodded before everyone in the room stood at the foot of the bed and the 6 individuals put their hands in and one final thing could be heard from the room before they left for the day to have a night out on the town? “TRADITION PREVAILS, ANARCHY FAILS!”
