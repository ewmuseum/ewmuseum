Checking Up(Part 2)

11 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-11-03 Monica Randall-Fontaine paces around her living room. She's waiting for her older brother Brick. 'Come on, Jamal. Where are you?' Monica is understandably upset. She watched Brick's handicap match against the Nevermore. Naturally, she was concerned over her brother's well-being, but one thing about the match, in particular, upset her. It was what Salaok said to Brick as he lay on the ground: 'By the way Brick, I enjoyed your sister's wedding. But the cake was a little dry, I thought.' Those words have permeated her mind. Monica is filled with dread over the idea that this depraved individual who hated her brother so much was at what was the happiest night of her life and no one knew it. She hears the door bell ring, and she immediately rushes to the door. 'Jamal?' 'Yeah.' She unlocks the door and then opens it, immediately rushing into her brother's arms. 'Hey, it's ok.' 'No. No, it's not, Jamal. He was at the wedding. He was at the wedding!' 'We don't know that. He could have just been bullshitting.' 'What if he wasn't? And you went after him for saying it.' 'The son of a bitch was taunting me about people I care about. Shit like that, I don't let slide.' 'I'm worried.' 'It's OK.' 'No, you don't understand. Kyle was watching the match with me, and he heard what Salaok said, too. He's actually talking about getting a gun.' 'He what?!' 'Yes. I'm afraid he might do something crazy.' Just then, the front door opens and in comes Monica's husband Kyle. 'Kyle?' she says, heading to the door. She runs up to him and hugs him. Brick walks out into the hallway after her. 'Hey, baby. Jamal, I almost didn't see you.' 'What the hell is this I hear about you wanting to get a gun?' Brick asks. 'Jamal!' 'What did you tell me when we ran into each other that day?' asks Kyle. 'I'm looking out for Monica's best interests.' 'Yeah, well, that was me not wanting to see my sister get disrespected, not about having her life in danger!' 'Her life *is* in danger! You heard what that bastard said on Evolution! What's to say he won't hurt me or Monica, just to get at you? You know Salaok better than any of us.' 'Which is why you need to let me deal with him.' 'I'll deal with it. It's my family.' 'Hey, she was my family long before she was yours. You know, the way you're talking, I'd almost think you were planning to go after Salaok yourself. Please tell me I'm wrong!' 'What if I was thinking that?' 'I'd ask you if you were out of your damn mind! 'No, I'm not. I'm gonna protect Monica, by any means necessary.' 'You wanna protect my sister? Stay out of it. You have no idea what kind of people these are.' “After seeing that footage of what they did to Aaron, I have a pretty damn good idea.” “Yeah, that’s why I have to be the one to deal with it. It’s my friends and family they’re dealing. That makes it my fight. That reminds me. I gotta go check up on Mom. I’ll call you later.” Brick kisses Monica and then shakes hands with Kyle, before he exits the front door. As he walks to his Navigator, he pulls out his cell phone and dials a number. “Hello, Mom” Called to see how you were. Yeah, I’m leaving Kyle and Monica’s now. I came to see how they were, after that Evolution. I’m on my way over there. No, really, I want to see how you are. You’re sure, now? OK, fine, I’ll go home.” Brick hangs up his phone and then gets into his Navigator. “First I get Camero, then Salaok. And I’ll fuck up anybody who thinks of getting in my way.” This the last thing Brick says before he drives off.
