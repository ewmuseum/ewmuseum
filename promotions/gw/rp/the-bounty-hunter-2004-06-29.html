The bounty hunter.

29 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 29th, 2004. The sounds of CKY's 'Escape from Hellview' gave energy to the entire group of punks that had crowded into Dallas's infamous club known as the 'Lost World'. The lost world was indeed busy as the black lights cast their glow upon the masses dancing in the only place where they could escape the social stigma attached to being a punk. Peer pressure seemed to melt away in this club as you were accepted for who you are. You weren't scorned or looked down upon for having enough metal in your face to pick up radio signals. And in fact my clothes were even considered stylish here, because not everywhere will you find people nodding approvingly as you walk past in a pair of jean shorts that hang about your knees in torn strips. As well as a black sleeveless shirt with 'Viva la Bam' scrawled across the front and scuffed Chippewa ankle boots. My accessories of choice were a black jetta cuff on each wrist, a silver cross hanging about my neck and a ring in my left eyebrow. The black light that shone down upon the dancing horde of punks seemed to cast a strange glow on my Superman tattoo, it brought out the colors and an illumination shone from my arm drawing in a flock of females…not that I need a tattoo for that. My bluish gray eyes widened as I first noticed the girl dancing before me. She had pale white skin, her black hair was streaked red and a nose ring was in her left nostril. She was dressed rather provocatively in a red flannel miniskirt and gogo boots her tight fitting white button up shirt, which was tied at the belly left little to the imagination. Standing at five foot six or around that area she was a mere four inches shorter than I am, yet her perfectly built little body gyrated before me in a manner that seemed to set her on a pedestal of greatness. She was truly beautiful…and apparently quite interested in me or at least that was the vibe I got when she began dancing mere inches from my body with her hands in the area around my crotch. 'I know you.' She stated in a voice which could barely be heard over the music. 'Do you?' I asked with an eyebrow raised. 'You're Josh…everyone knows you.' She said in a matter of fact way as she moved her body even closer to mine. 'Hold that thought…' I said as the ringing of my cell-phone interrupted me and I quickly whipped the black Verizon phone from my pocket…the caller ID showed one simple name, Raki. 'Hey Ra, what's up?' I asked my apprentice nonchalantly. 'Get out of the club now!' She quickly snapped at me. 'Wait…what's going on?' I asked in a perplexed manner my face taking on a quizzical expression. 'Someone is trying to collect on the bounty. Two of them…a woman and a man. As I was leaving the club I heard two people discussing it. The plan was that the woman would distract you while the man takes you out.' She said trying to stay calm although I could tell this news had rattled her. 'You saw them?' I asked. 'Yes, I saw them! Now get out of there before they find you!' She yelled at me over the phone in a state of near panic. 'Now calm down…describe them and maybe I can duck out of here unnoticed.' I said speaking in a soft tone that was barely audible over the music. 'A tall man in a black trench coat with long stringy silver and black hair, the woman was a brunette, a little taller than I am…she was wearing a school girl outfit. Have you seen them?' She asked almost gasping for breath in worry. Had I seen them was the operative question…and as I looked in front of me there stood the woman that Raki had described. She bore a lovely smile that appeared to be innocent as could be on the exterior. She was a brunette, taller than Raki by an inch and wearing a schoolgirl outfit…this wasn't good. I had to resist the urge to look over my shoulder for her partner in crime who was no doubt sneaking up behind me as I spoke to Raki. 'Ok then…I'll make sure that everything goes just fine…' I said, my focus now back on the woman who was pressing against me only now I was studying her mind…not her body. 'Josh…be careful.' She said in a near whisper as she choked on emotion. 'Be careful…right.' Pressing the talk button on the phone I ended our conversation and returned the phone to the pocket of my jeans. 'Trouble?' Asked the lovely young lady in front of me who I had now lost all interest in. Ignoring her seductive gyration and grinding I spoke to her flatly. 'No trouble, just business.' I lied, flashing a rare smile at her as I did so. I had to get away without alarming her that I knew of her intentions and after she had heard my end of the conversation with Raki that wouldn't be easy. 'That was…my agent, he informed me that I have a commercial to shoot, so if you'll excuse me I need to make a call.' I once again lied in a desperate effort to escape. Before she could say a word I spun on my heel and took two quick steps forward…that's when I saw him. Standing at about six feet two inches and easily weighing two hundred and twenty pounds was a man with stringy silver and black hair. He wore a black trench coat and looked just as Raki had described him. He saw me turn just as I laid eyes on him…this wasn't good. He stood merely six feet away from me and I glanced at him from only the corner of my eye as I saw him reach his right hand slowly into his trench coat. Great…a maniac gunman trying to collect the bounty that Maverick Reno put on my head and he chooses to do it in a crowded club. So I'm going to get gunned down by some low class fag who probably has no other way of making money aside from selling crack? I don't think so. If I go down I'm not going down that easy. Stepping behind a group of bodies I put them between the gunman and I, he may be crazy but surely he isn't stupid enough to take everyone out to get to me, or at least…I hope not. My steps were quick as I brushed through the bodies toward the sign that read 'Exit'. My heels clicked against the floor and glancing back over my shoulder in a calm fashion I noticed the gunman, giving chase. Apparently he had chosen a better route than I had and was catching up to me quickly. I had only one chance to escape…cause a distraction. Looking over at the muscular security guard next to the exit a light bulb seemed to go off in my head. Reaching forward I put my right hand directly in between the shoulder blades of a big boned man who was dancing calmly and before he even knew what happened I shoved him hard and sent him crashing into the security guard. The guard dropped like Deacon Holmes after two shots of Vodka yet he was quick to react as he lashed out with punches at his attacker. This distraction was all I needed as it caused a group to form between myself and the gunman, using this to my advantage I stepped out of the exit door and onto the sidewalk outside the club into the Dallas air. The sky was as black as Maverick Reno's heart, illuminated only by the streetlights and neon signs that flashed throughout the city. With the gunman still in the club I knew that I had grabbed at least a ten to fifteen second lead and as a former track star that was all I needed to get away…yet even I couldn't outrun a gun. The soles of my Chippewa boots slammed into the sidewalk with authority as I took off on a mad blitz rushing down the sidewalk unarmed and a bit worried about exposing my back to the gunman when he got out of the club. At least I was one speedy little bastard…I must have been about fifty yards away from the club on a remotely deserted sidewalk when I heard the report of a Nine-millimeter pistol as it went off behind me. The bullet zinged down the sidewalk punctuated by screams as the few pedestrians dropped to the concrete holding their heads in fear as they assumed the fetal position. They weren't the target I was…I couldn't just drop to the sidewalk and cower I had to get away from the gunman. Glancing back over my shoulder I saw that he was giving chase as he also ran, I knew that I had the superior speed yet as I had previously realized…a bullet could still run the fastest man down. He fired again only this time the bullet came much closer, ricocheting off of the sidewalk in front of me and bouncing off into oblivion as chunks of concrete spat up from the sidewalk and bit into my bare shins. These slivers of concrete drew blood from my leg as they imbedded themselves into my skin. 'I just had to pick that club didn't I?' I grumbled to myself as my hair whipped around trailing me as I tried to make my escape…and that's when I saw an alley, perfect. Turning into the alley I paused…I had a ten second lead now I just needed a place to hide. Dumpster? No, because then I would be trapped. Boxes? The homeless used these as living quarters and I didn't see much of a point in hiding in a box that he was sure to check. Trash cans? I could hide in them or behind them but if I hid in them I was trapped…behind them I was too easy of a target. 'Where can I hide?' I groaned to myself before pausing to think…I knew he would be coming around that corner any second and into the alley and I had no more than five seconds to make up my mind… 5. 'Maybe I could keep running…' I said wistfully before realizing that as long as the alley was my five second lead wouldn't take me too far away from his barking gun. 4. 'I could always fight him…' I pondered as I reflected upon my background as a Martial artist and my years of experience as a professional Wrestler. 3. Or could I fight him? I was no doubt one of the greatest Wrestler's in existence, my accolades far and wide had gained me that honor and I was also the Global Wrestling World Champion…a honor held by few. Then again…fists versus gun isn't exactly a fair bout. 2. So here I was standing in the middle of an alley about to be brutalized by some psycho…he didn't want to kill me, just to maim me and collect his bounty from maverick Reno. Nonetheless…that was enough motivation for my instinct to kick in. 1. In one quick motion I ran toward a nearby brick wall of a building and leaped into the air, I kicked off of the side of the building and in a desperate effort I clawed through the air until my right hand caught the side of a fire escape. Quickly bringing my left hand up I grabbed the bar and raised my knees to my chest. 0. That's when he burst into the alley. His eyes scanning the area around me, seeking everything in the darkness. My heart pounded inside my chest, threatening to burst from my body as he raised his gun and probed at the alley with his eyes. 'Come out come out wherever you are…' He said in a taunting tone using the old movie clich? to tease me with. His muzzle barked three times as he put a hole in a set of trash cans…one shot through each can, suddenly I was glad that I had made the decision not to hide in or behind those trash cans. His lips curled up into an angry snarl as he waved his pistol about frantically. 'Where are you?!' He demanded as he stomped over to the dumpster and threw the lid open staring inside. With an angry mumble he slammed the lids back down and turned his attention to the boxes that littered the alley. Only two of them were big enough to house a human body…his muzzle spat out a shell into each, apparently he didn't believe in taking precautions to only maim me. 'These games just aren't any fun…' He whined, turning on his heel and now standing five feet below me as he nervously tugged on a lock of his stringy hair. 'I couldn't agree more.' I silently told myself, as my heart pounded even faster than before. At this point I was worried that my heartbeat would give me away it was beating so fast and hard. Lowering his pistol he sighed in frustration…he had lost me and he knew it. I had made my escape and there was no way he could find me I was gone for good, passing third base and heading straight for home, a free man. Even if he did have half of a fully functioning brain he couldn't find me… …But the cell phone ringing in my pocket changed all of that. 'What the…' He began as he looked upward and prepared to raise his firearm. 'No!' Was all I could muster as my cell phone had began the chase anew. Dropping from my perch I came down quickly thanks to gravity's pull lashing out with a round kick I sent the pistol from his hands before landing on my feet. His eyes turned to the traveling gun as did mine, watching the firearm slide across the cement. I looked at him…he looked at me…and we both made a dive for the gun! I was the first to touch it as my thumb and index finger grabbed at it yet it only danced further away. He scrambled for it, crawling quickly past me and grabbing it. Using a quick reaction I punched his elbow sending a spasm of pain through his arm and the gun sliding even further across the cement. Standing to my feet I dashed toward the gun and grabbed it and as I clawed at it desperately…he jerked my ankle and sent me down hard to the concrete. The gun slid from my grasp and we both watched as it skidded across the cement before disappearing into the sewer drain. Now the odds were even…or so I thought until he stood and pulled a knife out of his boot. The dim alley bore home to little light but the light that did exist glanced off of the eight-inch knife blade as he spun it in his hand a grin across his face. He swung downwards, stabbing at me as I was sprawled stomach down on the concrete, and he missed as I rolled away desperately, the knife clanked off of the concrete and I made it to my feet without a second to spare. On my feet I knew that I had a fighting chance, he however failed to realize that as he came charging at me with his knife leading the way. Sidestepping him I gave him a hard shove into the brick wall where his body slammed into the unmoving stone with an impact, knocking the breath from him. He gasped for Oxygen before spinning around blood now decorating his teeth. With a flash of anger in his eyes he slashed at me and I moved to the right…he slashed to the right and I moved to the left. He stabbed to the left and as I moved to the right he made one quick slash to that area and caught me across the right biceps directly above my Superman tattoo. The knife bit into my skin blood gushing instantly from the wound. I grimaced in pain before reaching down with my right arm and catching his wrist. Turning him into a hammerlock he dropped the knife and I shoved him hard back into the brick wall, this time launching him headfirst. His skull hit the wall and he staggered back stumbling all the way. One quick reverse wheel kick to the head a second later and he was lying on the concrete his eyes opened wide in a combination of shock, pain and horror. Scooping up his knife I quick brought the tip of the razor sharp blade to his Adam's apple. He gulped in fear and this sunk the tip of the blade into his skin causing a tiny incision as blood trickled from his throat. 'Do you want to collect the bounty?' I asked calmly staring down into his frightened eyes. 'Yes, my girlfriend was supposed to distract you, and I was going to cut your Achilles tendon…that was it, I swear. The gun was only in case the job went awry, and then I would have to make it a little more bloody…' He gasped in fear. 'You were going to cut my Achilles tendon?' I asked, not in the mood to screw around. 'Yes…so I could collect the bounty from Reno.' He said tears of fear beginning to well up in his pale green eyes. 'You do realize that if you sliced my Achilles tendon, that would be the end of my Wrestling career don't you?' I snarled at him, the anger beginning to boil from within as I pressed down just a bit harder with the point of the knife against his throat. 'Yes…' He gasped earnestly, he must be honest…after all you wont find many people lying when their about to have their Adam's apple pierced. Looking down at him I removed the knife from its position and clutched it firmly in my right hand. 'Too bad for you you'll have to take on a little easier job, your not man enough to do Reno's dirty work…your nothing but an infection on the rectum of society.' I spat angrily as he stumbled to his feet. 'Your not going to hurt me are you?' He asked in fear. 'Should I?' I answered his question with one of my own and a playful smile. 'Just let me go man, I promise…I'll never do anything like that…I'll get my life straight…I'll get a real job…just…please man!' He begged in fear. 'Go.' I said with a jovial smile that appeared all too friendly. Holding up my left arm I motioned behind me. 'Just go.' 'Thank you…' He gasped relieved to be free of my torture. Walking quickly and painfully he walked past me and as he did so…I spun around and dropped to one knee, in one quick flash the knife sliced his Achilles tendon and he screamed loudly in sheer agony as he dropped onto the concrete and began rolling around in pain. Clutching at his sliced tendon his eyes filled with tears. 'And that's what you wanted to do to me… I sneered at him and stepped over him as I exited the alley. Glancing down at the knife I decided to hang onto it until I got home…it was only logical that there would be more nuts out there waiting to cash in on the bounty. Reaching into my pocket I pulled out my cell phone to check for my missed call. It was Raki again. Quickly dialing her number from my cell phone's memory it rang…once…twice…and then she answered. 'Hey…' She began slowly. 'Hey.' I responded in a voice slightly perturbed. 'Are you okay?' She asked with a strong sense of worry in her voice. 'Just barely.' I stated rather grimly. 'What happened?' She asked softly a bit relieved yet still worried. 'I'll explain later.' I told her still a bit pumped up from the adrenaline rush that came from fighting for my life. 'Maybe you should stay at my place tonight…your apartment can't be safe.' She warned. Slowly a genuine smile spread across my face as I spoke. 'I was thinking the same thing…' 'Okay…then I'll see you soon.' She said softly. 'I'm on my way.' I agreed as I hung up the phone and returned it to my pocket. The beautiful voice of Raki was gone and in its place were the wails of the wannabe bounty hunter. No doubt there would be more like him…looking for money and desperate for power. I had made it through today's bounty hunter…now could I survive tomorrows?
