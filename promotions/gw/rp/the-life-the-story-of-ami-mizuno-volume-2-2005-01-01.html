The Life: The story of Ami Mizuno (Volume 2)

1 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-01-2005 Early Afternnon Well folks its time once again to peer into the life of the woman whom has gone by many a nickname in her young career: Ami Mizuno Its a day pretty much like any other in New York City except for the fact that people were still cleaning up from the New Year’s Eve bash that was held by ABC and thankfully not a lot of incidents were reported by the police if any around where The Connection live because well the neighbors that live around the residnece are usually pretty reserved in their partying and if they do any its within the confines of their home and The Connection aren’t big party people themselves either! But there is a reason that they aren’t partying much or living it up. It seems that for only the second time ever a stable taht Joshua had constructed has two members competing at a PPV and Joshua is loving the challenge as he plays the role of wrestler and ,in this case, manager! Its early afternoon and in the home of the Connection its actually studying time for some of the members...I know I know one would think that wrestlers don’t need to study but the thing for these guys is that they take the occupation of Pro Wrestler seriously but I don’t think you’ll find one who takes it as seriously as Ami Mizuno. Yep it’ll be a little over a week for Ami till her debut in GW and already the house is jumping but not young Ami...no she rather sit in the study and read any and all notes that she might have on opponents or just use the area for meditation, which is fine because of the fact that if anyone needs a clear head when they get in the ring then she would deserve it most. Behind the locked door we find Ami in a knit wool sweater that her girlfriend, the incomparable Michiru Kaiou made for her ,navy blue sweat pants and white Hanes socks and she is sitting behind the desk trying to find any kind of notes on the rookies that are in the “Newcomers Bash” at the upcoming event but to no avail and that kind of frustrated the yong prodigy as she can usually find info on any and all wrestlers that are in the feds that she is in but this time its coming up empty. *Damn I hate this. I’m usually able to come into a match of any kind prepared but it seems this time I’ll be going in cold so...* Ami would have been able to finish her thought if not for a slight knock at the door and the sound of a certain auqamarine-haired goddess at the other end of the door. “Darling can I come in?” Came the voice at the other end of the door and Ami’s frown immediately turned upside down as she looked up from her notebook and hollered out “One minute!” before getting up and unlocking the door so that the “Miss Do-it-all” award winner of the Connection, Michiru Kaiou, could come in to see her girlfriend. Ok now let me be the first to tell the truth here: When Joshua found out that Ami was a lesbian and for that matter was dating a “slightly older” woman he wasn’t all THAT thrilled about the idea of Ami dating but when he found out that it was Michiru for some reason his worries melted away and gave them both his blessing for eternal happiness. Now back to the story... Ami opened the door to the study and in came one of the most beautiful women that you’ll ever meet in your life! Michiru came into the study wearing her jacket from Mugen Gauken, the academy in Japan that she used to attend with now mutal accquaintance Ten’ou Haruka as well as a white blouse, with matching mini-skirt and a pair of aquamarine colored knee high socks and was also carrying in a tray that Ami immediately took from her and placed on the table that they had bought for that room specifically. The table ,which was an old style...ornamental type of table I gues syou could say was mainly for if someone wanted to have tead or what not in the room and its usually only done if the two are related or in this case going together. When Ami sat the tray down, which contained a kettle of a specially brewed green tea that only Michiru knows how to make and its simply wonderful, a tray full of leftover Christmas Cookies (Josh was supposed to take them to Ven Pen’s place for the GW party but he “forgot”), and a bag full of pastels Michiru had taken her place ot the one side of the table while Ami sat across from her on the other side. “So how’s studying coming along?” Michiru asked as she poured the cups of tea for herself and Ami knowing all too well the answer that was about to come “Not so good dear but what can be expected when you are one of the new people walking into a company and you know no one outside of the people in your own group?” Ami asked as she took her cup and carefully sipped the contents being carel as not to spill a drop on her or the table. “I know how that is actually. You may or may not know this but I was actually asked to wrestle in Australia once and I accepted the invitation.” Michiru told the young Ami and she almost did a spittake on Michiru when se heard this news “You saerious?” Ami asked quizzically and Michiru smiled sweetly and nodded at her before Ami asked her second question “How’d ya do?” Ami asked and that is when Michiru finished munching on a cookie before speaking again. “Surprisingly well. Your brother taught me some stuff before I had gone over to make sure that I didn’t get owned and during the Xmas break we went over and I had my first match which actually went to a time limit draw if you can believe it!” Michiru exclaimed and Ami actually didn’t seem that surprised by the result. “You pick up quickly on things Michiru and that is what I love about you....hell that is what attracted me to you in the first place!” Ami told Michiru and now it was Michiru’s turn to hold her tea down as if she swa actually surprised! “How long was the attraction for ‘Prodigy’?” Michiru asked calling Ami by her first ever nickname in wrestling. “Oh since about the time I met you at the aracde in Japan and I had wished that I could have you. When I found out about Haruka breaking up with you over what she called ‘personal reasons’ I was more pissed off than anything because she didn’t know whom she was leaving and yes it hurt me to see her leve you because I know that she meant a lot to you.” Ami pointed out and she could see the pain in Michiru’s face so she tried to backtrack what she said “Look Michi-kun I’m sorry if I yelled but I just thought...” was all Ami could get out before Michiru whirled around from her end of the table and pinned her lover to the ground in a passionate embrace. Ami would have tried to wrap her arms around Michiru but Michiru pinned Ami’s arms to the ground and ran her fingers through Ami’s short blue hair while the kiss lingered on a little bit longer with their tongues meeting. When they finally broke the kiss they were both on their backs panting gasping for air and the only thing you heard anyone say at that point in time was when Ami muttered “WOW!” A little while later and the girls actually pulled out the sofabed and after they showered and changed into their nightclothes for the evening that is where they stayed for the rest of the night and no one....NO ONE said a word! Au Revoir!
