Date: 7-29-03 Stop’collaborate and listen Ice is back with my brand new invention, something grabs a hold of me tightly flow like a hawk daily and nightly’will it ever stop?...Yo, I don’t know, turn off the lights and I’ll glow Master of The Mat Four is quickly approaching’im the only Master of the Mat champion in the tournament this year, but did that matter to me? No not really I could really give a fuck about that..im Jay Robson damn it, the one and only, now I know when its time to have fun and party it up but I also know when to buckle down and get serious and this is one of those times. I know myself better than anyone else and I know that I am unpredictable and sometimes not one to count on in a clutch situation’but then again that was then’this is now, im this day and age in Global Wrestling nobody care about the past anymore nobody cares about C4 Explosive Death Matches or Glass Cage Matches, nobody cares about Insane Cain or Bobby Brawler anymore’hell, I don’t think they remember who they are’those guys were the back bone of UCWF along with Salaok. What was I? I was a snot nosed rookie that thrown in the pit with big dogs, back then either you ate or you got eaten. I didn’t know a thing about professional wrestling’oh I knew about the art of wrestling don’t get me wrong I was a two time state champion wrestler at my high school. I thought I was the shit when I rolled into UCWF, but I got my ass thrown around a few times but eventually I got the hang of it but not by myself’a man named Johnny Cruse saw my ability and my talent. Cruse quickly took me under his wing and taught me how to become a super star in pro wrestling. He brought in the name Youngblood to me and Derek Frost who was also a snot nosed rookie at the time, together with Johnny Cruse we blossomed into a duo to be reckoned with in the Universal Championship Wrestling Federation. A few months into my career I captured my very first title’The Cruiserweight Championship of the world and held that title for an unprecedented seven months and fourteen days. But UCWF didn’t last that long. A rival federation known as Freedom Wrestling League merged with UCWF to become the fore father of GW known as GWF, so in all actuality I held the UCWF/GWF title for seven months and fourteen days a record that still stands to this day as the longest title reign for the Cruiser Title. But that’s not why im telling you all of this, I am telling you this because the Golden Era of GW is gone and will never come back. I have been here for four years and counting I am the last of UCWF, I am one of the last of GWF, im no longer a snot nosed rookie’I am a seasoned veteran in this Federation, I am the Past, Present, and Future of Global Wresting. The cold steel was now warm between my clutched fists as I lifted the bar over my head, veins pulsated out of my arms as I heard the clank of the weight on either and of the bar, rapid thought swirled through my head as I tried to focus on the task at hand. As I lowered the bar the weight expanded my chest on the black padded bench as I stared into a light bulb that was hanging from the ceiling with my baby blue eyes. I blocked everything out of my mind except me, the bar, and the light. Up, down, up, down repeated repetitions as sweat began to seep from my pours, I couldn’t help but think about Master of The Mat, Trey Reed, Sean Price and his stable. Trey Reed beat me fair and square, Trey Reed is a good wrestler and I respect the hell out of him but this is competition, and my goal here is to once again be the best, to once again hold that World Title around my waist. My breathing slowed and synchronized with the bar which I was lifting off my chest, just one more I said to myself as I tried not to arch my back, my muscles spasmed and shook as I inched the bar to its resting place. The sound of steel on steel as I dropped the bar into the grooves and sat up, with my mind cleared everything seemed somber, the smell of sweat and a hot weight room filled my nostrils. I sat relaxed on the end of the bench and looked myself into massive mirror which encircled the walls. Sweat ran though my golden blonde hair, down my face and dripped from my chin leaving a small puddle on the blue rubber floor in between my feet. Veins ran from my shoulders to my wrists as I sat there, Scar Tissue played elegantly from the speakers I had set up in the corner of the room. I breathed in?. And out slowly, as my chest arose and fell, my gaze into the mirror became somewhat hypnotic as I sat there’still thinking’about everything, about what I should have done, about what I could have don’t and about what im going to do, about what I should do, and about what I can’t do. Sean Price says im a washed up’Sean Price says my glory days in GW are over’Sean Price is a punk’Sean Price is a never will be’Sean Price will never be what I am’Sean Price is a nobody’The Upper Echelon is a joke’comprised of two jobbers and two never will be’s. Adrenaline rushed though my body, from my head to my toes I could feel it, I missed that feeling, I flexed my pectoral muscles and let out a long breath as I laid back down under the bar, I let my arms hang limp at my sides. Thoughts were flowing uncontrollably through my mind, thoughts that needed to be locked out for the time being. Bulls on Parade the song of the YB blared through the speakers. I closed my eyes? Johnny Cruse, Shawn, The Soup, BJP, T, Hanz Kruger, Derek Frost, TLM, all Youngblood, all legends in their respective federations. Under the black of my eye lids I could see a dot floating around in my head from the light I had been staring at replaced by visions of the YB coming down the ramp to the Theme of Rage Against the Machine, the old YB is dead but it will never die. I placed my hands on the bar and lifted the weight now heavier than before’my eyes still closed I lowered the bar faster than before Faster? Faster? My breathing once again in synch with my repetitions, one after another I bounced the bar off my bare wet chest, the pain becoming more and more, my arms hurt, my mind was focused soon I will be ready for the Tournament?
