Chapter One: Beyond Belief? ( PT. 5)

Chapter One: Beyond Belief? ( PT. 5) Leviathan Global Wrestling Promo #65 Date: April 4th, 2003 Location: Blagoveshensk, Russia ﻿The Book Of Judgment Chapter One: Beyond Belief? (Part 5) Date: February 28th, 1980 Location: Blagoveshensk, Russia “Leviathan...your not done yet...finish them all off.” It says. Leviathan snaps around staring at the creature, with his fiery red eyes. He raises his hands and wipes them together, smearing the blood together. “Let’s go.” His deep voice booms. Leviathan slowly walks through the bathroom facility with his little creature follower, stepping over the prone bodies of the Russian fatalities. Leviathan suddenly stops, and looks back towards the little creature. “Xaphon, I will go deal with Vladimir and his followers. You, you find a way into the barricaded room under disguise of mortal form, and mess with their minds.....that’s all your good for anyway’s.” Leviathan orders. “Understood.....but let me have some time with them...” Xaphon says. Xaphon turns around and walks back deeper into the bathroom facility and turns into the very same stall he came from earlier. Leviathan turns back, and keeps on his quest for the final four, so to speak. He walks through the recording studio, and stops. Eight bodies are strewn through out the small room. Some still moving slowly as their mortal capsule’s lives slowly drift away, while others are completely demolished, some so bad one would not be able to identify that it was once a human body. Leviathan pulls his hood up over his gruesome appearance, and lowers his head. Oddly, his height and size begins to diminish into a small figure. He pulls down his hood and takes off his robe, dropping it to the ground to reveal a male figure. The figure of Boris. There is blood on the hands of “Boris” and cuts on his arms and face. He slowly walks towards Victors office and enters. He catches the men off-guard, surprising them. Once they notice it is “Boris”, they are quick to approach him, but a look of worry is administered upon their facial expressions as they notice his condition. Boris shuts the door behind them and walks towards the purple drapes. He looks out the window as the other look at his back. “What happened?” Vladimir asks. “They were killed...all of them. I am the only survivor. He caught us all off-guard and eliminated us individually. We didn’t have the opportunity to out number him.” Boris tells. “Oh my god.....well how did he do it...weren’t you all together?” A staff member asks. “He picked us out one by one...no one say him coming. His tricks are unbelievable.....” Boris trails off. “Well he won’t get the five of us, all we need to do is stay together” Vladimir proclaims to his clan. A huge thud outside the office is heard, which puts the group on alert. Vladimir walks over towards the doorway and peers his head out. He looks left...nothing. Right....nothing. Vladimir turns around to with a smile. “He ain’t here.........” Vladimir trails off. Vladimir is now speechless as he peers into the now red eyes of Boris, as he stands over the three fallen men. Each had been killed with in a matter of seconds, with no moans or grunts. Blood is all over the walls and floor, which makes one believe that the murders were completely brutal. Boris stands, staring at Vladimir and starts breathing heavily. Slowly his height is regained, as is his monstrous figure. As this transformation takes place, he steps backwards into the nights darkness and all that is seen is the glow of the bloody red eyes. After a few moments of pausing, Vladimir runs out of the room and makes a sprint for the staff breakfast room. Leviathan walks out of the room at a quicker pace. He walks through the doorway, and picks up his cloak that he had dropped earlier. He tosses his right arm into it’s sleeve, followed by the left one. He adjusts the cloak around his shoulders and keeps walking. He finds the narrow hallway, to find Vladimir dashing for the door. Vladimir looks back as he reaches the door, and oddly enough, Leviathan is gone. He looks around the hall, any possible grooves that his large figure would not fit properly to hide, but he is gone. He turns around to knock on the door....and you guessed it...Leviathan is standing face to face with the last of the sixteen. Leviathan steps forth, and Vladimir steps backwards, finally until he is pinned against the wall. “I am here.....and this one is for Victor.” Leviathan says. He grabs Vladimir, and tosses him into the steel door creating a huge thud. Vladimir is slow to his feet, but quick to the ground as Leviathan comes down with a hard punch to his skull. Vladimir lay there, as blood starts to flow freely from his skull, yet he still moves. Vladimir slowly gets to his feet, and staggers back and forth, as Leviathan stands, and examines his prey. When he has completely got his footing, he stares at Leviathan as Leviathan oddly enough smiles towards the man. “At least you actually had the guts to come back for more....” Leviathan says to the bloody Vladimir. With that being said, he gives a stiff palm strike the Vladimir’s facial region, tosses his body into the air and hard to the ground. Leviathan steps over Vladimir, and evaluates his injured victim, and no more movements come from Vladimir. Leviathan turns his head slowly, and faces the door. He steps towards it and stares at it, and can here the people and Xaphon inside talking.
