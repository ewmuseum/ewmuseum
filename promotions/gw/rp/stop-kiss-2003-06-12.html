Stop Kiss @$$

12 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/12/03 Inside a dark hotel Ice sits alone at a light brown desk, with a blank sheet of notebook paper, and a yellow number 2 pencil in his hand. Lightly yet consistantly Ice taps the pencil on the desk as he stares out the opened window that is in front of him. The dark night sky sends a beeming light from the moon, directly into the room, which is the only dim light that allows anything to be seen. Ice continues his staring out the window, and his tapping of the pencil on the desk. The constant beat of the pencil connecting with the desk stops, and the room goes completely silent. 'You know, Dynamite, I will thank you for all of the compliments, and the good natured jesture of your words. However, are you some kind of freaking retard or something? What do you not get about me? You are six feet tall. Two hundered nothing pounds. I am SIX FOOT THREE, TWO-HUNDRED EIGHTY FOUR POUNDS! What in the hell are you talking about you being bigger, and that whole cheesey generic the bigger they are crap! What do you not understand that this match, is more than just any other match. This match is the most important match to me. You see, you play the whole jekyl and hide bit, where one minute you are a bad ass, and the next you're a respectable guy. Fine, play your games, but I DON'T PLAY GAMES! Tommarrow night, you step into that ring, and you enter MY WORLD. No more fun and games. No more talking. Just me and you eye to eye. Face to face. Man to man. Bring your little clique if you want. Keep them in back if you like. It won't make a difference either way. Because whether you like it or not. You can talk all the bullsh!t you want, but face the facts, YOU DON'T HAVE WHAT IT TAKES TO HANG WITH ME! I have said it once, and I will say it again I HAVE NO EQUAL! So tommarrow night, get ready. Because when the lights dim and the music seeps throught the speakers, it will hit you. The cold and the fear will engulf your body. The chills will run up and down your spine. The hairs on the back of your neck, and on your arms will stand on end. It will be at that very moment, you will realize that your time is up, and fate is about to serve you a COLD DOSE OF PUNISHMENT! You may think you can stop it, but you can't. It will happen. And when it does, what are you going to do? NOTHING.' 'Speaking of NOTHING, that brings me back to you once again Tyler Pierce. The man who feels that he has been cheated, that what is rightfully his has been taken away from him. Are you really that arrogant, or that igorant to believe those words that come out of your mouth? For your own personal sake, and for the sake of your partner I seriously hope not. But where do you get such arrogance? Who are you, and what have you done?.....Hold on, excuse me, my cell phone is ringing.' Ice reaches into his left front pocket, from his black pair of workout pants. He pulls out a small silver cell phone, opens it and places it next to his ear. Ice turns and faces the camera. 'Hello? Tyler it's for you, it's NOTHING, he says he knows you.' A smirk grows across Ice's face as he closes the cell phone and puts it back into this pocket. Ice tilts his head slightly to the right. 'You see Dynamite may think, but he may not say that I'm arrogant. But you know what, if I am arrogant, it's because I have earned the right to be. Soon this company will find that out. However, I consider myself confident, and sure of my abilities. And there isn't anyone who can doubt that.' 'As for you Tyler, I can't wait, see what you have to say. I can't wait to see what you are going to do. Because I will be watching your every move. You won't know where I'm at, and you won't know when I will show my face, but you will feel my presence, as the cold hits your body, and the fear and uncertainty enter your mind and soul.' Ice turns his attention to the piece of notebook paper that is still blank, and still sitting on the light brown desk in front of him. Ice vigorously begins to write, and write. Ice takes in a long deep breath and exhales. He stands up from the desk, and leaves the room. The scene fades with the camera zooming in and looking at what Ice has written. ' i see the truth upon that pedistal's frowning down upon me like a trophy valued by masses of minds are closed to a variety and change in a life so bland and boring uncontent living through others learning from nothing experienced independantly responsibilty pointing failure cause the reflection in the mirror is what you fear to see like a trophy you pose like you're better than me like i care what you say... i've seen both sides of the fence because changing is evolving a learning lesson through experience more knowledge than your common man's 9 to 5 blindness uncontent with his well off being i'm just fine...no '
