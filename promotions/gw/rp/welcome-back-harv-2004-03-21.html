Welcome Back Harv...

21 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-21-04 (It's been a few weeks since we last seen Hardcore Harv, he was sent on a vacation by McNasty to get a way for awhile and to help stay out of harms ways. While he was gone McNasty won the last two matches he was in, made an offer to train and was also attacked by Train. McNasty is glad to see Harv back, it means less work for him in the bar. It's a Sunday night in the bar, the one night in the week that The Drunken Leperchaun is closed.) McNasty: Well Harv how was your deserved time off? Harv: It was nice to be away, I never realised how beautiful the bahama's was. I seen your last couple of matches since I went on vacation. You did a nice job... McNasty: Yeah I feel like I am headed towards the zone that I once was in. Harv: That's always a good thing. So what's your match tomorrow night? McNasty: Street Fight, Me and Duzza v.s. Train and Makonie... Harv: Wait a minute, you mean to tell me, Train and Makonie are now teaming up? I thought Makonie was some racist asshole? McNasty: Well they teamed up with the Syndicate, and now they're known as the MOB. Harv: WOW, what people will do for money you know... McNasty: No shit, It caught me and a few others off guard as well. Harv: So what do you have up your sleeve for this week? McNasty: Well it's something you MIGHT remember well, lets just say it's going to be a painful night for home boy and the skin head... Harv: Ah, I think I know what your talking about now... McNasty: Yeah exactly.... Harv: Well I need to get unpacked, come help me.... McNasty: Sure... (The two leave the bar, what remains now is what in the world McNasty has up his sleeve for Monday night on Evolution only on HBO...)
