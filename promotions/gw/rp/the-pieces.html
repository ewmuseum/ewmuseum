Date: Unknown Home of Mike Harris Tuesday Afternoon Mike ran from his car across the block and down the alley. He made his way through an empty lot and moved to his front door, but he stopped when he saw it already open. He didn’t know what to do. He could run away or go inside and get his emergency cash. I can’t live without some money? Mike walked up slowly, listening carefully for anything happening on the other side. He didn’t hear anything. He pushed the door open slowly, and slipped inside to see what was happening. The entire place was in a shambles, with everything tossed about or broken. He knew the hiding place should be secure, as the portable safe would attract most people’s attention. He moved down the hallway to the bathroom. He closed the door behind him and pulled the wire towel rack off the back of the door. He unscrewed the top of each tube, and slung the whole device sending the tightly rolled cash out onto the floor. All told, he had a few thousand dollars here in the rolled bundles. The rest of his cash was in a nice little mutual fund, but he wasn’t going to be able to get to that at the moment. He simply needed to lie low for a little while until it was safe to come up and see who was causing all of this. He heard the sound of the front door being thrown open, nailing the wall stiffly. Mike stuffed the money into his pockets and looked around. He had nowhere to go’except? Throwing open the shower curtain he jumped into the shower and pulled it closed behind him. He knelt down, trying to stay out of the sight of anyone who might check. Footsteps and the sounds of more things breaking came through the closed door, but he simply remained there squatting. The bathroom door came open. “Jamal said he had it” Are you fucking sure bitch?” “Yeah I’m motherfucking sure! Jamal didn’t tell him he had that shit, but he has it. It’s not here, but he has to come back here. Mike don’t know shit about shit!” “Fine, I’m taking a piss and then we can keep sitting on our asses waiting.” “Shut-up!” The man sounded like an animal, and judging by the shadow outline on the shower curtain he certainly was one. The man unzipped his pants and began pissing. The sound of him pissing seemed to go on for hours and hours as Mike could feel the cold trickle of sweat moving down his back. Finally, the man shook the last few drops out, and he pulled his pants back up. The man walked back out, and Mike was finally able to take a breath. Unfortunately, even as he began to calm he figured out that he was unable to escape because these men were still sitting in his living room. Mike took the rolled wads of hundreds and began unfurling them. He put them all together, then folded them over. He stuffed the money into his pocket, realizing he had been stuffing more than he thought into his hiding place. Dealing with gangsters and drug dealers had been a stupid decision. He needed the easy money, the quick hit they provided at the time to his crumbling little empire. Unfortunately, the infusion hadn’t helped. He lost the three rental properties to foreclosure, and his partner in the bar forced him out with a five hundred dollar check and a not so veiled threat. Of course, the dealers didn’t let him leave. They had one of the things they had been looking for all along. They had their accountant. So he had slowly taken all the money and invested it. No more partners, no more property and now he was as far away from that small retirement fund as he could be. He heard more crashing, they were tearing some of his possessions into smaller pieces. He could only imagine the few treasured items he had being destroyed even further. There were a series of pops, long and sustained, followed by the sound of tearing and breaking. Screams’thuds’and the curses he didn’t need to hear at the moment. He knew the sound, how could he not know that sound? Someone had just fired off an automatic rifle into the town home. He could only stay there in the shower, trying not to whimper as he wondered what in the Hell was happening. He still wanted to change his underwear and pants from where he had pissed himself earlier. Now with the shots, he was in need of some toilet paper too. Bar At Side Of Road Tuesday Evening Mike knew this man, at least the name and the reputation. There had been some shadowy words and dealings around the family, and even the local gangs knew they should never harass, rob or do anything to a property owned by the Hix family. “P-P-Pitt?” “That’s right.” Pitt smiled, his pearly whites seemed so far out of place in these surroundings. “If you look over near the door, you’ll see that’s my brother Xavier Black. We didn’t want you running out on us here.” “What are you talking about?” Pitt laughed. He poured himself another shot of tequila and threw it back. “We were watching Jamal, and he finally led us to a few mistakes. You see, he’s an old friend of an old friend. He’s been working on helping our old friend with a transportation and storage problem. Jamal owed him, financially and for the kindness he showed him over the years. Now we just need to know where he is.” “What are you talking about?” Mike felt himself becoming light headed. “Jamal has been helping someone we are looking for. He made certain arrangements through you, and he had you hold onto a few things for him. We need the location and we need the item.” Pitt poured each of them a drink. “I don’t know what you’re talking about.” “You do, you just don’t realize it.” Mike picked up the shot glass, and greedily took the tequila. “And if I give you what you want, then you’ll kill me.” “No.” Pitt refilled Mike’s shot glass. “I don’t kill people. Not unless they absolutely force me to do it. Now, let’s talk about some Japanese imports he may have asked for.” Montgomery Town Homes Tuesday Afternoon The noise was just getting too annoying to deal with. He heard the crashing again, and Benjamin had heard enough. He no longer wanted to have to put up with the constant assault when he was supposed to be asleep. He worked nights, and Mike knew he wasn’t supposed to make too much noise. Benjamin had been in the Army, and he didn’t suffer fools like Mike gladly. A lazy, fat man with no ambition at all. Benjamin was building toward his future everyday, and he often had rows with his neighbor. He slipped on a pair of jeans. Another loud crash, and Benjamin was out the door. He came around his bushes and walked over the dry, yellow grass. “Mike, you son of a bitch. I’m not putting up with this kind of shit today!” Benjamin was walking to the door. He had ignored the two men across the way in his half-asleep state of anger. The sounds of feet hitting the grass caused him to turn around, and he saw the rifles being raised. Benjamin was about to jump out of the way, but it was too late as the two men opened fire. The bullets tore through his body, and then through the windows, wall and door. The two men kept moving forward, firing again as they saw movement behind a window. They had heard about the troubles Jamal had been going through, and they were moving in on his operations. Taking out the accountant simply meant Jamal would be frozen out of his money and accounts. They kicked the door in, and saw two men lying on the floor. They quickly checked the room and the kitchen. No one was there, and a quick spray through the bathroom door came before it was kicked in. No one was inside. They cleared the bedrooms and closets. Mike wasn’t around, and so they left. The police would be on scene soon enough. They wouldn’t understand everything that was happening, but they also wouldn’t find the gunmen. Inside the shower, Mike heard the men leave as light from the bullet holes in the shower curtain came in just over his head. Mike stepped out of the shower, and stripped his pants and underwear off. He wiped away his own waste, and then ran to the bedroom. A fresh pair of shorts and jeans went over his pale legs. Mike opened his rear window, and fell out of it when he tried to escape. He had to get away’somewhere’anywhere. Quickly. Bar At Side Of Road Tuesday Evening Mike took the shot glass in his hand. He was thinking about it, “The only weird thing he’s had me do lately is use one of our corporate accounts to get a credit card and make hotel arrangements in Virginia.” “You see, that’s exactly the kind of thing we would be looking for.” Pitt smiled as he took a shot directly from the bottle. “Now, has he arranged for any storage” He has something from Japan we have to get.” “He was talking about taking a hot Japanese car from a friend, and that seemed strange.” Pitt motioned his brother over, “Why?” “The car was a Ford Mustang.” “Well, that’s a ride I’d like to see.” Pitt pulled out a hundred and laid it on the counter. “Let’s take a ride.” Xavier bumped Mike from behind for emphasis. He walked out with them, wondering if he would ever come back from this trip. Greenley’s U-Store Wednesday Morning Pitt Hix pulled the metal rolling door up. He turned on the light switch just inside, and then stepped in with the 1965 Mustang. He pulled the garage door shut behind him. Behind Mike, Xavier Black pulled out the notepad and black pen. He handed it to Mike from behind, “Start writing.” “Writing what?” “Everything about where Jamal’s friend is staying in Virginia. I want every piece of information you can remember.” Xavier’s voice was cold. Mike felt the cold sweat again. Where Pitt had been nothing but jovial, Xavier was hostile and cold. He seemed to hate Mike on general principle. There was something else going on with the longhaired man with the cold green eyes. “Can’t this wait until after I get a night’s sleep” I mean, I might need to go through some of the records I kept.” “Pitt said you’d be safe, didn’t he?” Xavier leaned back. “Uh’yeah.” Xavier pulled his lower lip into his mouth, biting down on it along with biting back the rising bile. He spoke in a slow, almost seductive voice that brought Mike’s full attention back to him. “You see, Pitt let’s people off to generate good will.” Xavier laughed, “Good fucking will. Some of the things I remember doing’you couldn’t even begin to understand. So when I see someone like you I start feeling those old urges. I remember men like you, the ones who are always at arms length even when they’re the grease making the whole drug machine run.” “There was once this guy in San Antonio. He worked for the Mexicans, and he kept trying to stay away from the darkness. He would clean the money, make arrangements and the whole time he’s just sitting there with two cars and three kids in a nice neighborhood. You’d never know looking at him, but this is one of the men responsible for all the violence. I like to think of him, and of course of you, as the enablers.” “Look, you don’t know me.” Mike protested. “I know you. I’ve known you for longer than you could imagine Mike.” Xavier leaned forward again, “I know all about you. You think you were trapped in this entire situation. You would do something else, but then something tragic might occur. You just want to stay around long enough to get far enough ahead so you can run away forever. Do you know what the problem with that is Mike?” The man was too scared to speak. Xavier slammed a forearm into the back of his chair, shaking him awake. “No, what?” “You’re a coward.” “You think I’m a coward?” Xavier put his head just behind Mike’s ear, “No, I don’t think you’re a coward. I know you are. You let the fear grip you so tightly the idea of standing up to those men was never allowed to penetrate your thoughts. You just wanted everything to go away, but you were too yellow to do anything about it.” “So, let me put this simply. I don’t care what my brother has told or promised you. I know what men like you allow to escalate through your inaction. So if you don’t write down what I want, then I’m going to introduce you to everything you’ve been keeping at arms length.” The cold, sharp edge pressed into the side of his neck. Mike knew exactly what was coming, and so he began writing as quickly as he could. Once finished, he passed the notepad back to Xavier. “See, that wasn’t so hard now was it?” Xavier leaned back, removing the knife blade. The door came rolling open, and Pitt was standing there with a small wooden box in his hands and a smile on his face.
