Date: 4-13-06 In the main square of Sanctuary was Kyo who was staring at Mother’s Children own Volt seriously. Volt returns the same serious stare to Kyo. Kyo drops the ramen chef on the concrete ground. “Get far away from here, old timer.” He keeps his eyes lock on Volt. “You don’t have to tell me twice.” The ramen chef runs away from both men. Volt glances at the fleeing ramen chef before he looks back at Kyo. “It’s just as they say.” Kyo looks at Volt curiously. Volt smirks. “You care more for others than yourself.” “I just didn’t want someone’s death on my conscience.” Kyo stares at Volt seriously. “You say.” Kyo takes off his shades and sees through Volt’s human disguise with the use of his special sight. “You’re Sinner.” He stares at him seriously with a smirk on his face. “You’re one of Mother’s Children here to capture me, right?” “No.” Kyo looks at him curiously. “I’m here to kill you.” Volt stares at Kyo with death in his eyes. ==|Part II: Fire versus Lightning|== “Here to kill me, huh.” Kyo takes a long drag from his cigarette and breathes the hot smoke in a sigh. “I guess that prick is really pissed off with me for not joining him in his foolish crusade.” “That is no way to talk about our lord and savoir.” “Can’t you see that your so-called lord is out of his damn mind?” Kyo still stares at him seriously. “He wants to kill all the humans and for what so you, Sinners can be dominate species.” “We’re just cleansing the world of all the filth.” “What about the angels and demons?” “They’ll be welcome to join us in our Utopia.” Kyo scoffs. “That’s only if they accept him as their lord and savoir.” “You don’t share Lord Aion’s vision and that’s why he wants you dead.” “Sorry, but I don’t feel like dying today.” “Well, you’re not leaving here until you kill me.” Volt stares at Kyo with a dead serious look on his face. Kyo takes a long drag from his cigarette and breathes the hot smoke through both nostrils. “It’s like that, huh?” Volt nods. “Fine, it’s your funeral.” Kyo takes the cigarette out of his mouth and soon drops it. The cigarette was slowly falling to the concrete ground. Kyo and Volt are staring at one another seriously. The cigarette hits the concrete ground with a bounce as both men blurs away at the same time. Kyo and Volt quickly reappears in the center of the main square with roaring explosion as both their fists were against one another. “It seems you have some zip too.” Kyo looks at Volt with a thrilling smirk on his face. Volt returns the same smirk to Kyo. “Well, they don’t call me the Lightning Emperor for nothing!” He goes for a rising uppercut with his other hand. Kyo avoids the uppercut with a quick back flip. He lands on his feet and throws a straight to Volt’s face. Volt blocks the punch with a quick firm grab of Kyo’s wrist and goes for a side throw. Kyo counters with a cartwheel as he knocks Volt’s hand away from him and strikes Volt across his face with a standing spin kick from out of nowhere that makes him stumble over. Kyo lunges at Volt to continue the assault on him. Volt quickly catches himself and drills Kyo in the face with a hard punch that throws him back some distances. Kyo stops from going any further with slamming his hand on the concrete ground as he stares at him with seriously, but he soon looks curious when feeling a weak current of electricity where Volt hit him at. “What the hell?” Volt begins laughing. Kyo looks at Volt curiously. “I see you just hand of one of my attacks.” Volt reveals both his fists are sparking violently of electricity. “These are my Lightning Gloves and if you get hit with one of these then you’ll get shock with five hundred volts of electricity.” Kyo spits some blood out and stands up. “Am I supposed to be impressed?” He looks at Volt boringly. Volt looks at Kyo furiously. “Well, I’ll like to see you try and counter this.” Kyo doesn’t waste time and reveals both his fists are cover in flames. Volt looks at him surprisingly, but he was even more surprise that Kyo copy from him to make an attack of his own. Kyo stares at his fiery fists, but he turns his attention back on Volt with a smirk on his face. “I guess these are my Fire Gauntlets and if you get hit with one of these then you’ll burn much worse than some poor bastard in the depths of Hell.” Volt regains his composure and looks at Kyo seriously. “Then let’s try it!” He lunges straight at Kyo. Kyo lunges straight at Volt. They meet in the center of the main square where they begin fighting each with swiftness and power, but they are only using their fists this time as roaring explosions occur with each time their fists collide. Volt ducks down where he dives in and goes for a lunging punch. Kyo avoids the punch with a quick sidestep and goes for a punch of his own, but he stops and yells out in agonizing pain when feeling a strong current of electricity running through his entire body. Volt smirks. “I know you thought you dodged my punch, but my fist grazed your side which got you a nasty shock.” “I can’t move.” Kyo winces while glaring at Volt. Volt laughs a bit. “That tends to happen when you get hit with one of my Lightning Gloves. You can say that it leaves a stunning after effect.” Kyo suddenly breaks the effect of Volt’s Lightning Gloves and lunges straight at Volt. Volt looks at Kyo surprisingly, but he quickly regains his composure and stops Kyo dead in his tracks with a powerful double palm thrust to the chest that throws him right through the front window of a store. Volt grabs hold of guitar and riffs it three times releasing three bolts of lightning striking the store with great wrath. Volt sighs in relief. “That was close.” He stares at the burning store seriously. “I can’t believe that he broke free. Bash and Smash are wrong about Kushinaga. He was strong perhaps even stronger than we knew.” Volt rests his guitar on his back. “Well, he can go to the great beyond with smile on face since I gave him a stylish death.” “I’m not dead yet.” Volt looks surprisingly when seeing Kyo walking out of the flames in his Sinner transformation. Kyo checks his attire and sees they didn’t have either tear or rip on them. “Megumi was right about these clothes being tough.” He stares at Volt with a thrilling smirk on his face. “Ready for Round 2, Lightning Emperor?” “Give me a sec.” Volt looks disturbingly. “I have to get ready after all this is the final round.” Kyo raises a brow with a curious look on his face. Volt pulls out his guitar and holds in the air as large storm clouds begins forming above them. Kyo looks even more curious, but there was some interest on his face. A strong bolt of lightning shoots down from the sky and strikes Volt with great fury. Kyo looks in great shock. Volt was still standing with his guitar in the air, but the bolt of lightning has caused him to transform into his Sinner form as he was now pure energy. “Now you can see why I’m called the Lightning Emperor.” Kyo looks at Volt interestingly. “If I were to guess your power has risen dramatically.” He pops his knuckles with a thrilling smirk on his face. “This is going to be fun.” “It’s time to which one of our powers is stronger, fire or lightning.” “Let’s skip the hyping and go straight to the kill with this thing.” Volt shakes his head as he waves his index finger side to side afterwards with disappointment on his face. “Don’t you know that theatrics make the fights all the more beautiful?” He looks surprisingly when seeing Kyo charging right at him. Volt quickly brings his guitar down and riffs it four times as four bolts of lightning shoots out. Kyo maneuvers through the four bolts of lightning with great swiftness and continues to charge at Volt. “Nice dodge.” Volt holds his guitar up straight before him and stares at Kyo seriously. “Let’s see you do that again!” He riffs it five times as five bolts of lightning shoots out horizontally in result to the guitar’s position. “Shit!” Kyo stops dead in his tracks and avoids each bolt of lightning with corkscrew flips. Volt smiles with an impress look on his face. “Hey, that was really smooth.” His look turns to shock when seeing Kyo blur away. “Huh” Where did he go?” Volt looks around frantically. “Nenshou”!” Volt turns to Kyo’s voice and sees him coming straight down at him from the air with his arm pull back. “Nakkurubo-ru”!” Kyo goes for a thrusting punch as his fist explodes in an inferno of fire, but he looks shock when seeing he has hit a strong energy shield. “This isn’t just an instrument of destruction.” Kyo soon sees the energy shield was form by Volt’s guitar. “It’s also an instrument of protection.” Volt holds the guitar out before him. “With you up close like this I can attack!” He riffs it once with all his might as a vicious bolt of lightning shoots out. Kyo quickly blocks the bolt of lightning, but it still throws back some distance with great force. He catches his bearings in mid-air as he flips down on the ground. Volt lowers his guitar as the energy shield disappears. “You can’t get near me while I still have my baby.” “Don’t be too sure about that.” Kyo notices the side of his hand was bleeding from a small cut in result to blocking Volt’s attack. “Matter of fact I bet I can get through your shield with my next attack.” He glares at Volt with a smirk on his face. “Is that so?” Volt looks at Kyo curiously as he has some interest in his eyes. “Then give it a try.” He tightens his grip on the guitar. “I dare you.” Volt smirks, but he soon looks curious when seeing Kyo standing with both eyes close. Kyo closes his hand very tightly as more of his blood begins seeping out from the open wound. “What is this?” Volt tilts his head as he looks at Kyo more curiously. “You think you can defeat me with some meditation technique.” A single drop of blood drops down from Kyo’s close fist and splatters on impact on the concrete ground. Kyo opens his eyes sharply and begins charging at Volt. “I won’t let you get close enough to me!” Volt riffs his guitar four times as four bolts of lightning shoots out. Kyo avoids the bolts of lightning just barely as some of them has grazed him, but he continues charging at Volt. Volt does a hard riff on his guitar as a vicious bolt of lightning striking Kyo with great wrath. Volt has a please look on his face, but it soon disappears and looks with surprise when seeing Kyo leaps up high in the air through the flames and smoke. Kyo dives straight down at Volt with his arm pull back. Volt holds his guitar straight up and riffs three times as three bolts of lightning shoots out horizontally. Kyo blurs right through them as he was still diving right at Volt. Volt quickly does a hard riff while holding the guitar straight up and shoots out a vicious bolt of lightning horizontally. Kyo blurs through the bolt with ease, but he looks surprise when seeing a vertical bolt of lightning heading right at him. Volt smirks as he watches the bolt strike Kyo with great wrath. “I got you this time.” His smirk disappears and looks surprise when seeing Kyo diving out of the flames and smoke. “What!” “Nenshou Chi Nakkurubo-ru”!” Kyo’s goes for a thrusting punch as his fist explodes in a roaring inferno of fire, but he once again sees he has hit Volt’s strong energy shield. “I told you that you can’t get near me!” Volt holds his guitar out before him as he stares at him seriously through the shield, but he soon looks surprise when he begin seeing the shield cracking. The energy shield shatters as the strings on Volt’s guitar snaps apart. “My guitar!” Volt looks in shock, but he looks in sheer pain when Kyo fully connects with the Nenshou Chi Nakkurubo-ru attack across his face as he stumbles over. Kyo dives across Volt, but he stops himself with slamming his hand on the concrete ground and quickly spins himself around with the use of the same hand as he lunges right at Volt. He doesn’t waste any time and strikes Volt with a brutal punching combination. Volt grunts as his body was jerking violently and spitting blood out with each brutal punch. “And the finish!” Kyo drills Volt under his chin with a jumping knee and quickly follows through with a somersault kick with his other leg as a streak of lightning appears in the sky in effect. Volt hits the concrete ground hard. Volt’s guitar was about to hit the ground too, but it stop momentarily with a quick swing of Kyo’s hand. The guitar soon hits the ground as it was reveal that it has been slice in two by something sharp and extremely hot. Volt looks at his destroy guitar with great heartbreak. “No, not my guitar!” His heartbreak turns to fear when seeing he was facing the end of Kyo’s energy sword. “Amateur night is over.” Kyo glares at down at the fallen Volt. Volt pants heavily as his body was glowing dimly in result of his Sinner’s power running low. “You’ve proven that fire is more superior to lightning.” His fear disappears as he stares at Kyo with some hope in his flaring eyes. “I accept defeat, but as the defeated I have a request to make.” Kyo stares at Volt curiously. “I want you to give me a stylish death.” Volt smirks. “There you go again with your theatrics.” “Please?” Kyo sighs annoyingly. “Fine.” “Thank you.” Volt closes his eyes with a content smile on his face. Kyo’s violet eyes flare crimson as he thrust the sword down with great force. Volt feels pain as he opens his eyes cautiously and sees Kyo’s blade was a few inches away from his head. Volt looks up at Kyo curiously. “What?” “I don’t kill people on an empty stomach.” Kyo deactivates his energy sword with relaxing his mind. “Transforming into a Sinner surely makes you hungry.” He slips the energy sword’s hilt back within the sleeve of his trench coat. Volt sits up a bit. “That’ll happen the few first times and?” He blinks and glares at Kyo. “Hey, what am I saying. We’re enemies! We’re supposed to kill each other not chit chatting!” “Yeah, an enemy that knows that prick, Aion is going to get everyone of you killed for foolish cause.” Kyo lights a cigarette and takes a drag from it before breathing the smoke out pass his lips in a gentle sigh. Volt glares at Kyo. “If you don’t kill me now then I’m going to go after you until I kill you.” “Good, I’m always looking for a good workout.” Kyo starts walking away from Volt with a smirk on his face. Volt watches Kyo leave seriously, but his look soon turns to an awkward one. “It’s not over yet.” The man from earlier slowly lifts up the dumpster lid and glares at Kyo with a short double barrel shotgun in hand. “I’m going to kill him while he’s extinguished.” He aims the shotgun at Kyo. Volt sharpens his eyes as he thrust his hand and shoots out an orb of lightning. Kyo stops and watches the orb of lightning pass his head and strike the assassin in the dumpster with great wrath. “You know, I could’ve handled the bastard myself.” Kyo shifts his eyes over to Volt. Volt still pants heavily. “Just paying you back for sparing my life. I don’t want you say that I owe you one.” Kyo smirks. “I want to ask you question before I go.” Kyo looks at Volt curiously. Volt holds up both pieces of his guitar and stares at him with tears rolling down from his eyes. “Did you have to destroy my instrument, man?” “It’s a basic rule of combat. Take away your enemies” weapons and they should fall at your feet in defeat.” Kyo forms a disturbing smirk on his face. “Be glad that you’re not a rapist. If you were then I would have taken all of your weapons if you know what I mean.” He slips a hand in his trench coat. “You’re not one, are you?” Volt blinks and looks at Kyo nervously as he waves his hands side to side frantically. “Oh no! I don’t use force when it comes to the ladies.” Kyo smirks as he removes his hand from his trench coat. “Well, I guess I’ll see you around then.” “I think you should know?” Volt looks cautiously. Kyo looks at Volt. “That Lord Aion has placed a large bounty on your head.” Kyo looks seriously. “Why are you telling me this?” Volt doesn’t respond instead he blurs away. “Hey wait!” Kyo stands in the middle of the main square with a confuse look on his face as the storm clouds above him are disappearing. While Kyo was thinking about what Volt just told him, a mysterious figure stands on a roof of a tall building staring down at Kyo seriously.
