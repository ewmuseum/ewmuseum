Aressted Again

Aressted Again Leviathan Global Wrestling Promo #210 Date: May 16th, 2003 Location: Washington, D.C ﻿Global Wrestling Chronicles Chapter Six: Arrested Again Date: May 16th, 2003 - 11:52 P.M Location: Washington, D.C The rain falls gently from the sky as darkness covers Washington. The surroundings a beautifully set, everything is perfect. The grass is that special green, and the perfect length. The buildings have no siding, pure brick with no chunks missing or dirt visible. A black Hearst, very poor condition is seen driving slowly down the street, Xaphon driving with Leviathan siting in the passenger seat, looking at the buildings. The vehicle is running poorly due to the damage caused by Deacon Holmes. The Hearst stops and Leviathan pulls out a folded piece of paper from his pocket, He unfolds it with his large bear like hands, and reads the name and address. Dr. Nicholas Cecil - Professional Help, 159 Rue Trudeau BLVD. is the name and address written. Leviathan looks up and reads the neon glowing sign on the building which reads the same. He crumples the piece of paper and drops it onto the floor of the vehicle. He sits up, turning around reaching into the back of the Hearst, which has many different stains and equipment in the back, and pulls out a crowbar. “This is the place Xaphon. I’ll need to get that data, it can’t get out into the public!” Leviathan says. “Alright, lets go get it then.” Xaphon replies. With that said, Leviathan and Xaphon both exit from the Hearst into what now is a complete storm. With in seconds, Leviathans hair is soaked and dangling in front of his eyes, dripping profusely. Xaphon does the zipper to his Nike sweat jacket up as his shirt begins to soak. They both approach the building, looking down the side walk to see if anyone is coming or watching the two of them. Leviathan stands right in front of the door, and runs his hand along the framing. He shakes his head and looks back towards Xaphon. “It’s not going to open, the door is securely locked. You know what this means right....your going through that window up there.” Leviathan says to Xaphon, pointing above him. Xaphon climbs up Leviathans back, getting a sturdy stance on his shoulders. He reaches up and is able to grab the concrete framing, pulling himself up, getting his knees up to be steadily positioned. He is able to pull the window open and get in, when he does so, he sticks his head outside. “I’ll get the file, you what here.” Xaphon says to Leviathan. Leviathan steps out onto the sidewalk, and leans against the brick wall, waiting for Xaphon to get the file. Five Minutes Later... “I got it, I put it in this briefcase. CATCH” Xaphon shouts. He tosses the briefcase down to Leviathan, and he catches it. He cracks open the two bolts, and pulls open the lid to reveal a folder with the name Xavier Gabriel on it. He smiles, and shuts the lid, picking the case up off the cold wet ground. “Good work Xaphon, lets go.” Leviathan says as he approaches his Hearst. Leviathan opens the passenger door, and tosses the briefcase into the vehicle. He looks back to Xaphon, but he is still proped on the window ledge. “Come on, lets get out of here!” Leviathan shouts. “I can’t get down, it’s too high up, help me!” Xaphon shouts back. “Fine you pussy.” Leviathan says, walking over under the window ledge. “Jump, I’ll catch you.” Leviathan shouts. Xaphon sighs and looks down. The drop is only about twelve feet, but his small body couldn’t handle the fall. He sighs once more, and rolls off the ledge for Leviathan to catch him. Leviathan steps back and watches his body crash into the concrete. Leviathan bursts out into laughter as Xaphon lays there moaning in pain. Leviathan is laughing so hard that he falls to the ground and has tears coming from his eyes. After a brief period of enjoying the moment, he gets up and looks at him. “Common, we got to get going now. I have to prepare for Salomon Vation this Monday.” Leviathan says, in all seriousness. Leviathan turns around only to see two police cruiser parked on the road, and four officers with their guns pointed towards him. He sighs and puts his head to the ground. “Damnit...........not again..............” Leviathan roars.
