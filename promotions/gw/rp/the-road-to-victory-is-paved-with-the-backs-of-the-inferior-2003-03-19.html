The road to victory is paved with the backs of the inferior...

19 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/19 With his next meeting with “American Youth” Josh looming over the horizon, Deacon Holmes prepares for what may quite possibly be the most painful test he’ll ever endure... If, he can endure... He’d kissed his girlfriend goodbye... He’d told his tag team partner and fellow TUE member John Zero so long... And now, under a black and starless sky, he was heading home... With only a backpack filled with a few of only his most essential possessions strapped to his back Deacon flies across state after state with a look of determination in his eyes. The wailing engine of a Kawasaki ZX-12R Ninja can be heard screaming out in misery as the finely tuned piece of Japanese machinery rockets past the two hundred mile an hour mark. It’s custom paint job appears as nothing more than a flash as it’s intensively polished chrome pipes blaze white hot while pushing the bike even further toward the verge of flight trajectory. Racing down the interstate, the vehicle meshes seamlessly with the night sky and can only be seen when passing under elevated street lamps, at which point in time it briefly appears as a black, red, and white blur being carried on chrome rims, and propelled by chrome pipes, dodging the raindrops of a light trickle so fast that they spill onto the already moist road in a vertiginous heap. Atop the seemingly rocket driven motorcycle sits Deacon Holmes, his shoulder length dreadlocks blowing about in the wind and dancing like a basket full of snakes, each one apparently dancing to the tune of a different charmer’s flute. As the thinly matted strands of hair waft languidly in the passing air beads of water resting on them are blown away, left behind, never to be stumbled upon or revisited. Unlike painful memories of the past... Those same very memories which are fueling this trip... The sky began to pulsate once more, just as it had no more than an hour prior, as thick gray clouds with rigid edges roll together. Their form most closely resembles a pile of dusty razors completely covered with years of buildup yet still sharp enough to split hairs. A low, reverberating rumble fills the night air, gentle rocking everything beneath the sky, from the air itself to the very pavement under Deacon’s wheels. Feeling another storm coming on, Holmes is left with nothing more to do than crank back even harder on the throttle and continue on his journey. Cold and shivering... A bolt of lightning pierces the sky with all the subtlety of a Molotov cocktail, breaking free from the clouds and striking down upon the ground with unbridled fury. And apparently breaking all hell loose along with it... The dark, dusty gray clouds which obscure the moon from the sky begin to roll. Slowly... Meticulously... They continue to wrap themselves around each other, rolling around like pigs in filth all the while swelling until the point that they appear full up like a land fill. A single drop of moisture finds it’s way from within? the clouds to the forehead of Deacon Holmes, who then looks up to find several thousand on their way down close behind it. Let the torrential downpour begin... A wall of water, formed by millions of drops of rain all falling simultaneously, that stretches as far as the eye can see comes crashing down, shattering against the body of Deacon Holmes and splattering against the already damp roads. Globules of water persistently beat down on Deacon’s body like a case of extreme Chinese water torture gone wrong. Small puddles form in uneven areas of the road as a combination of the now rapidly blowing wind and pouring rain rip leaves from their branches, snatching them up into the air where they are left to flutter to the ground. Deacon’s loose fitting mesh basketball shorts which once flapped freely in the wind now cling tightly to his muscular thighs, now allowing the definition of each muscle to clearly be seen. His dreadlocks now hang long and heavy, stretching past his shoulders due to the weight added by the water. The sky blue sleeveless shirt he’d initially left the house in is now a deep navy and hangs off of his body as he hunkers down on the bike. The only thing even relatively dry on Deacon’s body are his shoes. A pair of NIKE Shox Air Genocides, Holmes? own personal shoe developed by NIKE as part of his endorsement deal. Pellets of water splash against the shoes, bead, and roll off onto the pavement below, saving his feet from exposure to the elements. This is without a doubt the only comfort Deacon has been able to come across on his trip so far. Lightning continues to stretch across the sky, punctuated by the tumultuous rumble of thunder. They spider throughout the air as though the sky were a shattering pane of glass, the lightning serving as illuminated cracks, the rain, falling shards... Descending a mountain standing in the path between Texas and home, Deacon leans in and out of curve after curve along the steel rail lined road. A two lane road with nothing but an intimidating rock face to the left and a rail running along the right side of it to prevent people traveling down it from falling to their deaths. The fact that he is no longer cruising down a long straightaway and now maneuvering into and out of a series of long looping curves with a torrential downpour beating down on his body like a mallet on a steel drum does nothing in the way of encouraging Holmes to reduce his speed. Over the rail a stunning view of the valley can be seen, although Deacon has yet to take note of it. His eyes, burning with determination, have yet to even leave the road. The slow, deep breaths taken by Holmes cause his nostrils to routinely flare, pushing water away from his tightly pursed lips. Briskly turning into a near one hundred and eighty degree turn, Deacon leans in so hard that the bike’s begs begin to scrape against the ground, kicking up sparks in their wake. After exiting the turn, in the distance Holmes spots something that actually manages to bring a slight grin to his face. Further down the stretch, after a few extended straightaways ,with a couple of turns interspersed, lies a left hand sweeper. In the middle of said sweeper lies what is now the object of Deacon’s attention, a fifteen foot wide dip. With his grin widening, Holmes flies into the turn executing a controlled power slide before rocketing toward the shallow drop. Once there he barrels down into the short ditch and upon his exit manages to catch some nice air. For a moment Deacon wears a smile that threatens to envelope his entire face before finding himself once again in mid air This time without his bike... The wet roads did little in the way of facilitating his turn and caused his bike’s tires to lose grip, slinging him from it’s seat as it goes skidding across the wet road like a figure skater on ice. Holmes slams into the ground shoulder first with a sickening thud before rolling several feet. His momentum further carries him across the ground in such a forceful manner that the friction created by his one hundred and eighty four pound body grinding against the road leaves Deacon with several small nicks and cuts on his arms as well as his back. A few rocks manage to embed themselves in his hands as well. After slamming into the guard rail that runs alongside the road Holmes comes to an abrupt stop. The raging roar of his Ninja’s engine muzzled and silenced, his body bruised and battered, his conscious ness... Nowhere to be found. Deacon lies strewn about the road like yesterday’s rubbish in the dead of night just waiting to be collected. His body a crumpled mess in now tattered clothes rests in darkness with the only audible sound being that of the raindrops pounding against his limp body. Holmes? limbs lay swung over each other at odd angles as though he were a dummy that had fallen from the back of a truck. As the water washes over his mangled body the blood from his myriad of nicks and cuts begins to slide away from their wounds, allowing gravity to take hold as they make their way down toward the ground before being carried away with the rest of the rain. The same rain that was slowly filling Deacon’s slightly agape mouth... This goes on for several minutes until a light can be seen slowly making it’s way around the corner Holmes had just wiped out on. As the light continues to advance the humming of an engine can be heard. Soon the humble turns into a low growl as the car grows even closer, showing no signs of slowing as Deacon’s body is rather well concealed in the darkness. Suddenly his lifeless body begins to move with new found vigor as his chest begins to swell and contact rapidly with his shoulders furiously jerking along with his head. The car growing closer by the second, Holmes? eyes finally manage to unroll themselves from the inner recesses of the back of his head and slowly work themselves into focus. And what they find is anything but a sight for sore ones... Deacon is welcomed back to the land of the living unable to breathe, and clutches his throat as he rolls to his side in order to cough up the water, as well as the last few things he ate before departing from Zero Estate. Now breathing rather heavily Holmes looks up to find that the ride isn’t quite over yet as the car’s horn bellows out into the air, it’s lights trained on him like a pair of infrared beams. Deacon’s eyes quickly dilate in order to absorb the incoming light, which manages to temporarily blind him before he realizes just what is happening. The car’s horn once again sounds and Holmes quickly springs to his feet, clutching his ribs and wincing in pain once he finds himself atop them. Then still somewhat disoriented Deacon looks down into the pair of headlights he is now trapped in much like a deer just moments away from death. With little time to waste, the driver of the vehicle attempts to swerve out of the way but due to the narrow two lanes of the wet road there isn’t much maneuvering to be done as the driver struggles to regain control of his vehicle on the slick surface of the road. Then at the last second, when it would seem that a head on collision were imminent, Deacon manages to gather his wits about him. Fueled by panic, the driver slams on the car’s brakes, causing it to go into a slide directly toward Holmes, who immediately reacts in a somewhat feral nature by leaping into the air and landing on the hood of the car. On his toes, knees bent, eyes fierce, teeth clinched, the tips of all ten fingers planted firmly on the hood of the car, which slowly slides to a stop. Deacon stares in through the windshield panting heavily for a moment at the fear stricken? driver. Soon, both men’s adrenaline subsides and Holmes slides off of the hood and the emaciated expression on the driver’s face fades away. As the man speeds away he can be heard exclaiming “What tha hell are ya, crazy!”” to which Deacon simply replies with a nonchalant middle finger. “Asshole” he mutters to himself while slowly limping over to his downed bike. Looking down at his newest prized possession Holmes shakes his head before hoisting it onto it’s wheels. “What are we ever gonna do with you Lucille Jr.”” he mumbles to himself, halfway wincing as holding onto the bikes handlebars further drive the rocks into his hands. Taking note of this, Deacon manages to fish out the culprits and remove them with relative ease. Holmes then takes off his already ripped shirt and tears it into two strands which he proceeds to wrap around his palms before once again starting up his bike and tearing off into the night. As he flies through the darkness of the night he once again finds himself encountering the same car he’d had a brush with earlier. Holmes pulls up alongside the vehicle on the wrong side of the road and knocks on the driver’s window. Once he looks over he is greeted with a middle finger before being left in Deacon’s dust, his bike kicking up a huge rooster tail of water in it’s wake. With the rain still beating down hard as ever, Deacon races down the road as nothing more than a blur, his eyes tightened and focused on finding one thing... A sign... “Now Entering Orlando City Limits”
