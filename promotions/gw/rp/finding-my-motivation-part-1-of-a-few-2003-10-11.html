Finding my Motivation (Part 1 of a few)

11 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/11/03` Months in the making’possibly the greatest thing GW has ever seen, day by day the plan unfolds more and more. The picture is now beginning to take shape, the dominance of the TUE over the GW is becoming more and more with each passing day. With the return of Bubba McCoy to the GW it seemed as though maybe some of the power in GW would shift from TUE back to its owner, but as we all saw on Maximum Carnage that would not be the case as the TUE would emerge yet again as the dominate force, and not only that but some of the TUE’s original members returned to help lead TUE into its total domination of the GW. (As the scene fades in we see a well dressed Sean Price walking the campus of Princeton University, a school he attended for four years and left not just as another graduate, but as a Valedictorian, an All American and a name to be echoed through the halls of this magnificent university for years to come. As he nears the corridor leading towards the library two gentlemen both wearing football jackets walk out. One of the men holds the door open for Sean as he proceeds through. Sean continues on his way through the library for a moment until he is stopped by the two football players he had passed only moments ago.) “Can I help you gentlemen?” “Your Sean Price right” Number 21 for Princeton like 2 years go. The first All American ever to come out of an Ivy League school.” “Well I had a few more accomplishments than that but I guess that will do for now. Yes I am he.” “Wow, I just wanted to shake your hand, you’re the reason I came to Princeton, to be a cornerback as good as you were.” “No one can ever be as great as I was. That’s just a fact so don’t even bother trying you will only lose in the end. Now if you will excuse me, I have more important matters to attend to.” (Sean turns and walks in the other direction as the two men stand there in total disbelief at how Sean just treated them. Price continues to walk away with nothing short of a smile across his face. He walks up to the main desk in the library and waits a few seconds until a librarian approaches him. She slowly pushes her glasses up the bridge of her nose and then looks towards Sean.) “What can I do for you sir?” “I am looking for the head football coach. I was told he could be found here.” “Why yes that is correct however if you need to see him you will have to make an appointment. He is a very busy man.” “I am well aware of how busy he is, I played for him for 4 years, now I would appreciate it if you could just point me in the direction of his office so I can speak with him.” “Look sir I already told you that if you need to ?” (The lady is quickly interrupted by a now irate Sean Price. Sean picks up a book off the counter and heaves it about 50 yards towards a group of students. He then takes a pencil and snaps it in half and throws it at the lady.) “Look lady, I don’t have all day to stand here and argue with you, I am a very important man and I have very important business to attend to. So either point me in the direction of the coaches office or I go to the Dean of the University and make him show me to the coaches office. You obviously have no idea who I am, and you know what, that’s ok with me, because from the looks of you, I don’t want to know you either. But for the sake of this conversation and my time I will tell you who I am. My name is Sean Price. I am a former football player here, maybe you never took the time to glance over into that trophy case over there and see my name on about half a dozen trophies.” “Your Sean Price’why didn’t you just say so?” “Because I shouldn’t have to you imbecile. Now the coaches office’where is it?” “Go down the corridor, when you get to the end go down the steps and then make a left and his office is the third one on your right.” “Wonderful, now was that so hard?” ?”” “Don’t answer that, I am afraid of what you might answer with.” (Sean turns away from the lady and then proceeds to follow her directions, when he gets to the office that the lady describes as the coaches he looks on the door to see a name plate that reads “Janitor’s Closet”. Sean turns around to head back upstairs when he suddenly hears a man yelling at someone or something. Sean then walks down the hallway a little more, following the yell as it gets louder and louder with each step. Finally when Sean reaches the end of the hallway he pears inside a door that is half open, as he does so he sees his former football coach screaming at someone on the telephone. The coach them slams the phone down onto the desk and then grabs the phone and the holder for the phone and rips it out of the wall and then proceeds to launch it out the window. Right after doing this Sean knocks on the door for a second and the coach immediately looks up and yells out.) “Who the hell is it” And what the hell do you want?” (A few seconds pass and then the coach looks up at Sean and realizes who it is. And then walks towards Sean to embrace him.) “Well I’ll be, the All American himself. I almost didn’t recognize you with that black hair and the suit. What the hell happened to you” You look like an accountant, not an athlete?” “Why don’t I sit down and I can tell you all about it” You have some time?” “For you ofcourse.” (Sean goes to sit down but the coach makes a fist with his left hand, rears back and clobbers Sean in the jaw knocking him backwards into the chair.) “What the hell was that for?” “For giving up on football, you could have been one of the greats. Now look at you’wearing a suit and tie. You dumbass.” “Ok old man, I’ll give you that one, but next time your head is going through that wall over there.” “Try me you little baby.” (Sean sits back in the chair across from the coach and cracks a smile and then begins explaining things to the coach as the scene slowly fades out.)
