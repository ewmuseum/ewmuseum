Contagion Effect

27 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05.27.04 <bold>Contagion Effect</bold> The Blue Monkey Lounge was filling up quickly and Ken knew that it was, in part, because of his performance on Evolution. When he was hot, his nightclub always started jumping even more than usual, and his International Championship victory had definitly set him ablaze. The influx of customers was near amazing and Ken couldn't help but feel a little zealous, as his club hadn't been filled to capacity in some time. 'Did you bring the Title with you,' question Steve, Ken's partner and resident piano man. He was dressed to the nines as usual. A black sports jacket by Taylor West and slacks to match over a black Banana Republic button up singed by a white silk tie fit him nicely. 'It's in the office,' responded Ken, dressed in his usual public threads, consisting of one his full Versace three-piece suits, completly black with a white button-up. 'Why?' 'Well, I'm sure people are going to want to see it,' tackled KPJ's friend a little reluctantly. 'To be honest, Steve, I don't really give a damn about what they want. If they want to touch that title so bad, they can meet me in the ring and take it from me,' shot Ken before he took his Grey Goose Greyhound to his lips. Steve couldn't really respond with anything to Ken's previous comments. Therefore, he simply sat there, sipping on his Belhaven's of Scotland and going over his playlist for the night with a slight smirk on his face. 'What do you think, Ken, more Billy Joel or Dino tonight,' asked Steve after a few minutes. Ken finished his Greyhound before responding. 'You know you can't put that choice in front of me, Steve. I like them both too much,' he shot as he nodded to one of his servers to get him another drink. 'I guess I'll be playing an equal amount of both, then.' 'Works for me. Just make sure you get in Swingin on a Star and New York State of Mind,' instructed an anxious Partridge. The club was really beginning to get full now as Ken looked on from the back room, where he and Steve were in conversation. He finally gave the high sign to one of the bouncers to pull back the velvet rope and allow the patrons access to the entire club. 'Showtime,' muttered Steve as he began to pound out Your Song, by Elton John. The piano was getting noticibly more difficult to hear as people filled in the room. Ken simply sat in the back and watched the ants at work. Half of them sang along while the other half poked fun at them. 'Nothing more than a bunch of sheep,' Ken thought to himself. 'They're all so damn predictable. A herd of two-faced imbeciles.' Ken's attention was quickly taken from him as a familiar face stood in front of him. KPJ stood in shock looking at him, as this had been the first time that this friend had ever been to his club, whereas it normally wasn't his style. 'Tell me those aren't ANOTHER pair of new Pradas,' Josh said, staring down at Ken's black leather shoes. Ken still stood in a bit of shock as he looked at the man whom he would be attempting to take a World Championship from on Sunday. The same man that he basically told only a few days prior that he would stop at nothing to beat him. The man that was like a brother to him. 'Wh-what are you doing here, Josh,' question KPJ over a stuttering tongue. 'You've never been here before. Remember, it isn't your gig. So, what are you doing here now?' 'You got in your peace on Monday,' Josh began seriously. I just needed to know if everything you said was true, or if you said a thing or two in excitement that you now regret.' Ken's shock quickly left him as he realized that Josh was at the peak performance level of his career, and that this one match would be absolutly nothing like those in the past. 'Josh,' began the Superstar, ' you know better than anyone that I don't say anything that I don't mean. I want to know who's the best... and yes... I want the World Heavyweight Championship that you so proudly wear in arenas around the globe. This match, brother... it's going to be unlike any that we've ever had. You may have never beaten me, Josh... but I've never beaten Josh. Trust me, Kid Flash was a completly different person than the man that stands in front of me on this day. Josh, I'm going into this match with absolutly no animosity... just a point to prove and an opportunity to take. I live with the relief that no matter what happens in Mexico City, the World Champion after High Noon will be Elite.' Josh continued listening to Ken's words before briefly cutting in himself. 'Well,' he said dryly, 'Looks like we're each on our own until then.' 'Perhaps it's best that way,' resounded Ken. 'But know this... rivalries die down, Josh. World Titles come and go... but brotherhood... that lasts forever.'
