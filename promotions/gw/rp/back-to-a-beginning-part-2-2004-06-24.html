Back to a Beginning (Part 2)

24 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 24, 2004 [Mackenzie’s black Durango pulls into a small, nearly abandoned parking lot across the street from an abandoned subway station. Shutting down her auto she pops the tailgate and exits. Grabbing her black duffle and hoisting it over her shoulder, she makes her way across the street. Taking a look around she starts down the somewhat broken concrete steps that lead underground to the subway. Reaching the bottom she looks around and makes her way away from the entrance, heading down away from the deserted subway tracks. Following the faint sounds and her memory she finally makes her way to two large garage looking doors nestled within the concrete. Taking hold of the bottom lip of one door she pushes it up easily, enough to slide under and then lets it drop back down.] “Home sweet home.” [Breathing in a deep gulp of air and letting it out slowly, Mackenzie turns away from the door to look at the hell she had come to love so many years ago. This large room, walled in by concrete, was more of a home for her than that rat-infested apartment could have ever been. In the center of the room is a wrestling type ring, with three sets of black ropes lining the sides. There are fighters all around the room. Some in the ring, some around the ring. Away from the center ring in the room there is an area of work out equipment, where some more guys are loitering. There are all kinds of activity going on, so much that Mac is hardly noticed as she makes her way through the room.] ?Mackenzie?? The familiar voice came from a few feet away, to Mac’s back. [Whirling around to the direction of the voice instantly pasted an ear-to-ear grin onto her face. In an instant she has already ditched her bag to the floor and she’s sprinted across the few feet to a man with the same ear-to-ear grin. Mac’s spring concludes with her jumping into the man’s arms for one huge, tight hug.] ?Evan!? Mac’s excitement can’t be denied as she greets her oldest friend with laughter. [With one final mutual squeeze Mac hopes down from Evan’s arms, still all smiles and laughs.] Mac: ?It’s been like forever!? Evan: “Forever? You were just up here a couple months ago.” Mac: ?A couple months too long. Totally. I missed you. How much do you love me?? Mac smiles at him sweetly. Evan: “More than my fully restored cherry red 1971 Chevy Monte Carlo.” Mac: ?And how much do you love your Monte Carlo?? Evan: “More than my children.” Mac: “You don’t have any children.” Evan: ?If I did. Do we have to go through this very time?? [Kenz giggles, playfully punching him in the shoulder.] Mac: “Of course.” Evan: ?What are you driving these days? Still got that 2000 Cavalier?? Mac: “Yup, yup. I’ve got it in the garage. Now I’m driving a 2003 Dodge Durango.” Evan: “You always did want one of those,” he nods in approval. Mac: ?Damn skippy. How’s the newest prot’g?”? Evan: “Lucas. He’s promising. He’s got the same spirit as a certain girl I once trained. A lot of potential to be great like she turned out to be.” Mac: “Yeah? I should meet this girl. You sound quite taken with her.” Evan: “Oh, I am. I love her more than my cherry red 1971 Chevy Monte Carlo.” [With a laugh they both smile at each other. After some more small talk Mac and Evan head back to the ring where Evan’s trainee is impatiently waiting inside the ring, leaning leisurely against the top rope. He smiles at Mackenzie.] Lucas: “Just a shot in the dark but you must be Mackenzie. Evan raves about you.” Mac: “I would be her. Nice to meet you,” Mac reaches her hand out and shakes his. Lucas: “She’s cuter than you let on Ev.” [Evan shrugs, as Mac glares at him. After they release hands Lucas holds the top and middle rope open for Mackenzie.] Lucas: “Let’s see if all the ravings are justified.” [Mac looks over to Evan, raising an eyebrow as he smiles and shrugs. Then she looks back at Lucas and steps up onto the apron, placing her hands on the top rope smirking at Lucas. Leaping from the apron, she jumps over the top rope and onto the other side of it, into the ring in one graceful motion.] Mac: “Justify away.” [Lucas glares at Mac, with a thirst in his eye to prove himself. Mac just shrugs, laid back and ready, waiting for him to make a first move. Just as suspected, she doesn’t have to wait long before Lucas is standing inches from her face. In the next instant his fist is flying through the air, plunging towards her face. With a swift side step Mac avoids the contact, with Lucas following her step and taking another swing. A step back and Mac evades full contact. They go on like this for a many swings and misses, dancing around the ring.] Mac: “Are you fighting or dancing here kid? “Cuz, really, the last guy I dated that danced like you..well he couldn’t keep up with me either.” [Mac smirks, jumping back as his last punch comes at her with a fury.] Mac: “There we go. I struck a nerve.” [Lucas stops abruptly, standing a foot or two away from Mac. He drops his arms to his sides in a challenging fashion.] Lucas: “Fine. Hit me then.” Mac: ?Hit me. Hit me. What’s with all the anger”? [In a single movement Lucas lunges forward, sweeping his leg under Mac, taking her feet off the ground and landing her flat on her back. Before Lucas is back on her feet Mac is already doing a nip up back to her feet.] Mac: “No follow up? Tsk, tsk.” Evan: “She’s right Lucas. We talked about that.” [Mac rushes on Lucas, forcing him into a collar and elbow tie-up. From the tie-up Mac twists her body around his, twisting his arm behind his back. Lucas, not familiar with this wrestling style, tries to counter but just gives Mac the opportunity to push him away, into the ropes, and have him come back for a clothesline to the throat. With nearly the same quickness as Mac before Lucas pulls off a nip up and grabs Mac by the arm, throwing her into the ropes and mimicking her clothesline, only with more force. Back on her feet Mac throwing herself at the ropes and coming back with a dropkick square into his chest.] Evan: “Spirit.” [Scrambling to his feet, Lucas goes at Mac with quick body jabs, getting in a couple before she throws her fist into his jaw, stunning him.] Mac: “Now, now. There will be none of that wishy-washy girly jabbing going on. If you’re going to hit me, I want it hard.” [Letting go of his jaw and raising an eyebrow, Lucas lunges at Mac, landing his fist straight to her jaw, forcing her to stumble back a step.] Mac: “Nice.” [In return Mac throws her body into a roundhouse spin and kicks Lucas in the upper body reign, stunning him enough to land another blow into his face. With Lucas staggering Mac grabs a hold of his body and drops him on his face with a face buster. Hoping to her feet Mac leaps over the top rope again, this time out of the time, landing on the floor. Mac skips over to Evan’s side with a never-ending beam.] Mac: “I like him, E. He’s..spunky. Like a male version of me when I was young and dumb.” [Groaning, Lucas slowly pulls himself up to a seated position. Mac smiles at him sweetly.] Mac: ?Justified enough?? [Pulling himself up, holding his head with one hand, Lucas nods slightly. Evan begins to climb into the ring, leaving Mac sitting on the apron. After a second she grabs her duffle and makes her way over to the work out equipment area. Setting the black bag down on the ground, she unzips and reaches down into it, grabbing her cell phone. She then takes a seat on the nearest bench press, and dials up David.] ?Dave. Yeah, I’m here. How’s it going?? [Through the phone there is indistinct conversation, as, in the background, there is a loud blaring music playing.] ?What’s..are you having a party!”? [Even at the thought Mac is enraged at her friend, having told him no parties.] ?DAVID MICHAEL ANDERSON! I left that house with a kitten and when I get back there he BETTER still be in ONE PIECE!!? [Furiously she hangs up the cell phone and throws it back into her duffle bag. Suddenly, looking around, she notices all the other guys around watching her.] ?WHAT!” Don’t any of you have cats?!? [All around there are a bunch of random head nods and shakes as Mac lets out a loud sigh and stomps back to the ring. The guys, shrugging off the confusion, have gone back to their pre-interruption activities.] “Mac, babe. Stop scaring everyone,” Evan calls to her from the ring, smirking. [Jumping back up onto the apron of the ring and leaning over the ropes she playfully smacks Evan on the cheek.] Mac: ?Don’t you dare smirk at me Evan Andrew Ogden!? Evan: “Okay. Okay. Just, don’t go all Carrie on everyone.” Mac: “No, no. I’ll save my Carrieness for those at GW.” Evan: “I look forward to it. Of course I will be front row when you debut.” Mac: “You always are E. That’s why I love you so. Don’t forget to bring Lucas with you. Maybe I can justify myself a little more for him.” [Looking past Evan she grins at the still recovering Lucas. Lucas is less amused as he slides out of the ring and heads over a work out. Kenz giggles slightly, and looks back to Evan.] Mac: “Midol? Crunch it up and put it in his PowerAde.” [Evan can’t resist laughing.] Evan: “I remember when someone else I know could have used that.” Mac: ?I was young and stupid.” Evan: “And now you have grown. You have done well for yourself grasshopper,” his voice transforms to a bad imitation karate movie. [Mac bows to him as he bows back to her.] Mac: “Thank you sensei,” she smiles before letting out a sigh. “I better get home. Who knows what kind of wanna-be-a-rock-star party David is throwing in my house. I will call you later.? Evan: ?And the fact that he actually plays bass in a band, doesn’t that make him kind of rock-starish”? Mac: “Not in my house with my cat.” [Over the ropes they embrace for a minute. Mac jumps back down, going over to grab her duffle she beams once again at Lucas. She gives him a little cutesy wave goodbye, throwing a wink at him too as she throws her duffle over her bag and saunters away. Mac exits the room through the large “garage” doors. Mac makes her way back down the concrete lined subway station, her duffle over her shoulder, and her hands preoccupied with pulling her curly brown hair back into a bun. As she makes her way back to the day lit stairs she is passed by another fighter, heading to train. She thinks nothing of him at first, until her brain clicks and she spins around.] Mac: ?Hey! Do I know you?? [The other guy stops and turns, walking back a little ways towards her and into the light.] Mac: ?Webber. You remember me, right?? Web: “Geller. Didn’t expect to you around these parts EVER again. At least, not after the tournament. When you lost so horribly.” Mac: “I seem to remember having a little help from you and your bitch, Web. You must remember, when you guys jumped me before my match. From behind to boot.” [Almost at the same time they both take a step towards each other. Mac’s eyes narrow in on her attacker’s face, as a grin dances over his face.] Web: “You had no busy in a man’s tournament Geller.” Mac: ?And did you come to that judgment before or after I beat you, pinned you, and knocked you out of the tourney?? [Web’s grin turns into a scowl as he gets up in Mac’s face.] Web: “Before,” he cocks his eyebrow. Mac: “You know Web. I was angry with you guys for a long while. But hey, the Christian thing to do would be to forgive. Turn the other cheek. And being the good Christian I am, well I can’t be angry forever.” [With a shrug, Mac turns to walk away, leaving Web stunned.] Mac: ?Oh, and Web?? [Whirling back around Mac buries her boot into Web’s abdomen, forcing the wind from his body, and causing him to double over. Grabbing hold of his head she shoves him back up straight and throws her fist into one side of his face, followed by all her strength. She follows up by doing the same to the other side of his face.] Mac: “Forgive, never forget.” [A low groan comes from Web as Mac pushes him to the ground. She then smiles innocently as she makes her way to the steps, up them, and out to her Durango.] * * * [Pulling back into the driveway of her North Carolina home, Mac hops out of the truck, grabbing her duffle, and heading into the house. Pushing open the front door she is almost knocked over by a flash of blasting hard rock music. Walking into the living room, David is jamming out on his guitar, completely unaware. On the couch, Axel is sitting, with big black sunglasses, bigger than his face, balanced on his small nose. Closing the door behind her, Mac drops her duffle, and surveys the scene.] Mac: ?David!? She shakes her head.
