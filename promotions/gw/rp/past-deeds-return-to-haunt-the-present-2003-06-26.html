Past deeds return to haunt the present

26 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Jun 26, 2003 Ely opens his eyes, and shuts out the bright light just as quickly. He slowly rolls over looks over at the clock on his small table by his bed. 5 : 00 AM Ely takes a deep breath and tosses the rest of the covers, reveling his almost naked body save for a pair of red and gray brief boxes. Ely slowly sits up and staggers over to the small sink and mirror in his small room and looks at his self. Black eye, a cut on his head. Not much else. “Not bad after last nights fight” Rings out a females voice. Ely quickly turns to see a young woman sitting in a chair. Dressed in a pair of thigh high black leather boots with silver buckles on the sides. Red leather pant with the sides tied together with red leather straps and a black and silver muscle shirt over her chest. Her hair black as night and her eyes as bright as emeralds.. The woman lets a smile come across her deep red cooler lips. “How the hell did you get in here”! How did you find me?” Ely shouts. “Really now. Is that a way to greet a old friend” Besides if you have to ask that Niklas Bauer guy you fought last night really slammed that chair into your head hard.” The woman says sounding quite amused. “As for finding you. That was simple. You name has been plastered on flyers all over Vegas. Ely takes a deep sigh his aggravation plainly showing. Ely begins to put on his clothes. “Glad to see you have kept in shape Ely.” She says and begins to stand up. The woman slowly and seductively walks over to Ely and places her hand on his bare chest. He lust filled eyes locking with Ely’s Ely shoves her off and turns his back on her. “Fine! Your loss Ely!” She screams and tosses a pillow at his back. “Look Jade. A lot of things have changed since I saw you last time. And as I recall The last time I saw you, you had stolen the stuff WE were assigned to locate.” Ely says as he squints his eyes winching slightly at the pain in his right eye. “Ahh the good old days.” Jade says with a small giggle. Ely spins around causing Jade to flip over the bed and land in a crouching position. “Look! You deserted our unit. You left us high and dry and collected the reward for your self!” Ely says his rage getting the better of him. “Look Ely! I am sorry things went like they did. But for information. I never got a reward! The basterd took the disk and I was lucky to escape with my life! So if you knew the truth you would have been happy that I saved your life, and the life of the other guys!” Jade screams.. Ely notices Jades arm slip behind her back. So he makes his self calm down. He knew first hand how deadly she was with daggers. “What” Xion Dirge lied to us?” Ely says sounding very confused. “Yeah. Hard to believe huh!” Jade says a tear running down her cheek. “I disappeared.. I disappeared because I knew you would not believe me. Hell None in that unit trusted me. So I left. I left the only man I have ever loved.” Jade says as she sits down on the corner of the bed. Ely stands there and the silence builds as he watches this women cry. ~ Could she be telling him the truth. It’s possible that Dirge did lie about her.~ Ely things to his self. *** A white flash happens and Ely remembers all the good times he had with Jade.. Holding her in his arms, Both laughing after just barely surviving a mission. *** Ely shakes his head retuning to the present. “I am sorry Jade. I never knew. All those years wasted. I am sorry you though I would not trust you. I.. I Loved you to.” Ely says as he places a hand on her shoulder. “Awww. You do still care” Jade says her smile returning and a small giggle follows. Jade wipes the tears from her eyes and looks deep into Ely’s eyes and her smile quivers. “But not any more?” Jade asks. “It has been four years Jade. Things change. I have changed. I.. I don’t know what to say.” Ely says pleadingly. Jade nods and heads to the door. “Good seeing you again Ely. I guess I thought wrong. But I am happy you know the truth about me.” Jade says as she reaches for the door. Ely steps in front of the door blocking her. “Why now Jade. Why here of all places” I am sure with me fighting for GW you have managed to see my name elsewhere.” Ely asks. Jade stops walking away towards the window and smiles. She looks at Ely over her shoulder with a evil looking grin. “You will find out Cowboy We shall be seeing each other soon.” Jade says “Damnit!! I have not the time for your games woman!” Ely shouts. “That hurt Ely. Hurt a lot. Good bye Ely.” Jade says and leaps out the window. “HOLY SHIT!!” Ely shouts and runs to the window. Ely looks out and there is nothing.. “God I hate that shit.” Ely says as he turns from the window. Ely goes and lays down on the bed and flips on the TV. After a few flips he comes to ESPN? “In other news. Primetime Centrals tournament is moving along. This sixty four man inter-federation tournament now goes into round two.. Here are the up to date round two brackets for this tournament.” Says the commentator who Ely has never seen before? Ely sits up with an anxious look on his face.. He knows that he won his match? If Jade Won her match then Ely would have to face her. The brackets come up on the screen. And in deed Jade’s name is there. With a smile Ely turns the TV off, as he does the Telephone rings. Ely reaches over and grabs the phone. “Hello?” Ely says. “Hey Ely. I wanted to say congrats on the tournament win. ? Rings out a females voice. “Thanks Christina.” Ely chuckles. “I am facing Jade next round.” Ely Says with a stern look on his face as he turns and looks out the window. “Well that’s what I am calling about. I just got a fax. Jade is out.” “What” How? When? Ely asks. “Well Jade sustained a injury in DWO and had to drop out. So some guy by the name of Tyrent is taking her place.” Christina says. “Ely.. Hello. Ely you there!” Ely puts the phone back to his ear. “Yeah I am here. Look Christina. Send all the info you have to me over night it if you have to. I got to go.” Ely says hanging the phone up.. Ely gets out of bed and finishes dressing. Once dressed he splashes some water on his face and looks his self in the mirror. “Well this changes everything, Ely.” Ely says to his self. Ely puts on his cowboy hat and heads out the door.
