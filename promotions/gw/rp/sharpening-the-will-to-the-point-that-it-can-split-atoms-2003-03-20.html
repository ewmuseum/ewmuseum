Sharpening the will to the point that it can split atoms...

20 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/20 The sun lit up as though it were bleeding. He’d finally done it... He’d beaten Brick in what was by far their most grueling meeting... He’d unified the primestar and platinum titles... He was now officially cemented in Global Wrestling history at the tender young age of 19 as the last primestar champion... All of these things would definitely be cause for celebration by even the most dedicated of dedicated’s standards, but for Deacon it wasn’t enough... No parties, no celebration, no fanfare... Just training. He’d traveled over eleven hundred miles... He’d braved the elements... He’d risked his life... And now, cut, bloodied, bruised, wet and shivering cold, the whole point to this journey stands no more than a downshift and some screeching tires away... In the distance, standing alongside the road sits a plain green and white sign atop a pole. Although it’s much too far away to be read by the human eye Deacon already knows what it says. “Now Entering Orlando City Limits” Deacon’s eight hour voyage from Dallas to Orlando in which he maintained speeds much less than lawful was now complete. This gave Holmes, who’s long trip was beginning to make him quite downtrodden, a resurgence of energy. His eyes lit up as he wrenched back on the throttle, throwing himself backwards as the Ninja’s front tire stabs at the air above while the rear screams out in protest. Deacon flies through traffic making erratic lane changes and paying little to no attention to the implemented measures of traffic regulation. Stop lights meant nothing... Stop signs were a joke... And Brakes are for sissies... He raced through busy intersections like a modern day version of rocket propelled frogger. He weaved in between cars coming from all directions rapidly passing and replying to sounding horns with a stiff middle finger. Coming up behind two cars driving side by side Holmes opts to slide in between them driving on the line separating the two lanes before leaving them in his dust. The rainy weather he’d encountered all the while on his journey quickly subsided, giving way to the beaming Florida sun which shined down on his face and warmed his battered body. Soon his hair, no longer waited down by the water it’d absorbed, once again danced in the passing wind and the wet strips of fabric that were wrapped around his palms were warm to the touch. A grin formed in the corner of his mouth as he angled his ZX-12R off of the main road and into a subdivision. But not your average subdivision, there were three or even two door garages, nor luxury cars to house in those garages. There were no joggers, no white picket fences, no children laughing, or birds singing. Just a long narrow oak tree lined road of dilapidated houses built behind rickety old waist high chain link metal fences, loose dogs roaming the cracked sidewalks scouring the ground for their next meal. Deacon was now home. Not “home”, but home. He wasn’t in the house he had built when he got his girls Global Wrestling paycheck, he wasn’t in the lavish pool house of Zero Estate. He was at 825 Oak Lane Ct., home. The house he’d grown up in for the first sixteen years of his life as a child who’s mother had passed away giving birth to him and who’s father had skipped town upon news of his conception. In this house lived Hattie May Jackson, the devil himself disguised as a large black woman in a floral dress with a severe drinking problem and a propensity for “The Price Is Right”, and also, the woman who raised Deacon. He’d bought the house shortly after finding stardom as a member of the Global Wrestling roster. As a matter of fact, this house was his first purchase, but he’d never dared venture back to it until now. As he eases down the uneven shadow covered road the rickety old wooden screen door of every house he passes slowly creaks open. The inhabitants of the HUD homes poke their heads out the door before stepping all the way out onto the porch. Their eyes follow him... They know who he is... Yet he has no clue who any of them are... He grew up confined behind the chain links of Hattie May’s or “Grandma??s house, never allowed out to play with the other children, or leave for any reason other than to go to school. He had to be readily available for any sort of cruel or unusual punishment she got drunk and felt like doling out on any given day without any particular rhyme or reason. Well, without any good ones at least... As Holmes continues down the road every door follows suit and opens just as the one before it did, all except that of the house at the very end of the court. Deacon slowly rolls to a stop in front of this house, quite possibly the worst one on the block, and dismounts his Ninja. He stands for a moment just beyond the gate, apprehension obviously running rampant in his head as he takes in the hideousness of the structure. An old wooden sitting atop a layer of cinder blocks and held together solely by a few rusty nails and some rapidly chipping paint. Finally gathering his wits about him, with a deep breath Holmes wraps his hand around the top bar of the old fence and gingerly pulls it open, slightly scraping it against the uneven concrete sidewalk as it swings. He then sets off across the dirt moat of a lawn that surrounds the house, to which modest would be a severe understatement. Treading slowly across the dirt, Holmes remembers many a time when his “Grandma” would get drunk and pass out in front of the television with the door locked, trapping him outside in the blazing heat of the beaming sun. Allowing the memory to pass just as quickly as it had arrived, Deacon makes his way to the short two step set of stairs leading up to the door and with a somewhat wobbly hand reaches out toward the doorknob. As his hand grows closer it’s barely noticeable tremor turns into a violent shudder. He continues to force it, his hand seemingly fighting against him, growing stronger the closer it gets. First inch by inch, then centimeter by centimeter until it’s finally hovering over the knob. Pushing himself further, Holmes begins to close his hand around the knob. Suddenly he abruptly breaks the motion and quickly draws his hand back as if touching the door had in some way wounded him. It had... But not physically, there was no shock, the knob wasn’t hot, it was the memories that got to him. He was beginning to wonder why he’d even bothered to stop here in the first place. There was simply too much pain involved with entering that house again. Sure, Holmes had been involved in some of the most blood soaked battles Global Wrestling had ever seen. But this was different... This was pain on a whole “nother level, this was pain that could not be retaliated against with a backstage prank or an in ring attack. This pain would not fade, this pain seemingly would be with him for the rest of his life. Holmes had finally gained the strength to escape the torture he was suffering at the his of his “Grandma” and never looked back. This would make the first time he’d returned to this house in over three years. “Grandma” had since died, but for some reason he was still unable to bring himself to go into that house. With a deep sigh Deacon turns his back on the door and begins to drop his foot down onto the first step when all of a sudden the smell of cheap cigar smoke fills the air and he hears a familiar voice beckon to him. “You gon” stay out there and get blacker or you gon? come in?” Holmes cocks an eyebrow out of curiosity and slowly turns around, brushing his dreadlocks out of his face as he does so. What he finds once he turns around is anything but what he expected to encounter upon his return to the house. In the door way puffing on a seventy five cent gas station cigar stands Deacon’s uncle, and only known family member, his uncle, CW. CW was an ex-professional wrestler who’d spent most of his career touring in Japan, due to the fact that he wasn’t in the country for a span of several years he had no knowledge of Deacon’s existence for several years. When in town he’d visited him once as a child but Deacon was too young to remember. The two never saw each other again until the month of April in two thousand three when CW saw a commercial for the latest edition of Global Wrestling Evolution, at which point in time he immediately set out to meet with him. In an unusual turn of events the two actually got along quite well, as their larger than life mouths proved beyond the shadow of a doubt that they were indeed family. And although CW is Deacon’s uncle, the two behave more like brothers. CW, twenty years Deacon’s elder, the wiser, much larger of the two, and Deacon, the young, brash half of the duo. Their dueling mouths have led to several impromptu “sparring sessions” in a variety of public locations, most of which Holmes ends up unconscious at the conclusion of, but at the end of the day CW is the only person Holmes has ever really listened to. He trained Deacon for the majority of his short career until his sudden meteoric rise to fame caused him to relocate to Dallas for love of his local star power. At first Holmes would return to the rather large house he had constructed in an affluent neighborhood after every show, but then he began finding himself hitting up after parties and meeting new people. Soon he’d go months in between visits to his house so he decided to leave CW in charge of it. This is the first time the two have seen each other in several months as Holmes has been wrapped up in his own fame, amongst several other things, including T.U.E. affairs. Deacon stands on the steps watching his uncle puff on a cigar as though nothing is wrong with this picture and everything is perfectly normal. “How’d you know I’d come here?” Holmes inquires, a puzzled look on his face. CW puffs on his cigar a little more, performing a french inhale then blowing it out once more in the form of several rings before answering. “You know that big screen TV you bought when you got that house?” CW asks, his heavy tongued southern drawl slowly rolling from his lips like sap down the bark of a tree. “Yeah...” Deacon answers, now a little puzzled than when he’d posed the question. “It still works.” CW shoots back, his tone growing more serious. “I saw what happened to you, that American Youth boy beat you like a dick in jail.” He continues after another puff from his cigar. Deacon’s shoulder slump somewhat at the mention of his loss to Josh for the cruiserweight title even though he has since moved on to bigger and better things, unifying the primestar and platinum titles, in the process cementing himself in Global Wrestling history. Holmes remains quiet as CW begins again. “You’re losin” your edge, first he beat you for your title, then a week later he beats you on the humbug. Straight up on tha? house ass whoopin?, you lucky I wasn’t there, else you woulda got two of “em.” “I tightened his ass right up though...” Deacon rationalizes. “Yeah?” CW asks, slitting his eyes at Deacon while exhaling. “Then why’s he still got your title?” he continues. Holmes? shoulders once again drop in defeat before he prepares to respond. “Yeah, but now I got tha...” He begins before being abruptly stopped by CW blowing smoke into his face. “All those flashing lights and shoe deals have made you soft. I don’t care what title you have, you still got disrespected by that whiteboy, and you think barely pullin” a win outta? your ass against Brick makes up for that? You’ve lost your edge, you know it, and I know it...” CW puffs a little more on his cigar staring Deacon dead in the eye. “But that’s alright though D, cause I’m gonna get it back for ya”... no nephew of mine is gonna? be gettin? disrespected like a fuckin? trick on TV by some punkass skater kid.” This brings a slight smile to Deacon’s face. “So you know why I’m here then?” he asks, halfway already knowing the answer. CW pulls the cigar from his mouth and gives Deacon a look that asks the question “are you stupid.” without any words necessary. “Do chitlins stink?” he shoots back before raising the cigar back to his lips. CW then turns into the house and tells Holmes “Come on, I got tha” weights set up and everything... and where’s that pretty little thang I seen you runnin? around with on TV?” “Hell naw, fuck that! I ain’t goin” in there!” Holmes exclaims, taking another step away from the door. “I hate this place!” he continues. “Good, that’s what I was goin” for, that hate is what’s gonna? give you your edge back.” “You’ve got to be fuckin” kidding me man, have you suddenly forgotten what happened to me in there!” I want nothing to do with this place, the only reason I came back here was to see if it was still standing... and then burn it.” CW continues to smoke while looking much less than entertained as Deacon goes on. “Don’t pull his shit on me man, this place has witnessed some colossal kickings of my adolescent ass, I can’t go back in there... I want nothing to do with this place!” Holmes, now full of conviction steps right into CW’s face. “This is bullshit man, you know exactly what happened to me in there and now you’re trying to force me to go back!” You think seventeen year olds without parents, friends, or places to go, just run away from home for no reason!” I opted to live in the street rather than stay in this place, what the hell makes you think I’m just dyin? to go back?” CW just rolls his eyes and raises his free hand into a talking motion to show his lack of interest. Deacon’s shakes his head in despair before offering up a last ditch effort to avoid going inside. “I mean damn man, can’t we just do this at my house” Or the gym?” Holmes asks, obviously upset. This finally gains some sort of response from CW, who quickly flies into action, lunging at Deacon then takes full advantage by their dramatic size difference by hoisting him into the air and pressing him against the wall. “That right there is exactly your problem! You want everything to be easy, you want sterile gyms with new equipment and half naked girls runnin” “round tellin” ya how big yo? muscles have gotten! You want a big cushy house with big screen TV’s and Olympic size swimming pools!” CW exclaims, his piercing stare burning a hole into Deacon. He pauses momentarily before continuing in a lower tone. “Well you know what” That’s not where you came from, you came from this HUD house, and a set of rusty weights. No fancy cars, no big gyms full of “roided up freaks, just lots of sweat and hard work... torture. You’ve forgotten where you came from boy...” CW explains before releasing Deacon, who’s face has turned into a scowl. “Yeah I know where I came from...” Holmes starts, then looks down at the collection of scars on his tattooed body before turning his attention to the door from within which many of them came. “I’m just not tryin” to go back...” CW puffs on his cigar once more before replying. “Don’t you go getting all eHOtional on me Deac, I know you’ve had a rough life, and I’m sorry I wasn’t able to make it any better for you. But what this all comes down to is one simple question...” Both men train their eyes on each other and lock into an intense stare before CW continues. “Do you wanna go home, or do you wanna win?” Looking around as if he expects someone to be beside him agreeing with him not to go in, Deacon’s shoulders drop again as he realizes he doesn’t really have a choice. Holmes makes his way back up the steps and walks past CW, who is holding the door open, into the house. “So like I was sayin”... where’s that pretty little thang I seen you runnin? around with lately?” CW asks as Deacon passes. Still wandering into the house, remembering all the beatings that took place in it, Deacon replies “I left her at home.” “Boy I would love to...” CW’s comment is cut short by a piercing stare from Holmes, who quickly whirls around to show his distaste. Knowing he just got busted, CW gives Deacon a sheepish look before following him into the ramshackle house amongst a street of bedraggled tenements. The deplorable condition of the house showed no signs of anyone having occupied it in quite some time. The two walked through the house, the smell of the cheap smoke from CW’s cigar mixing with and exacerbating the already pungent aroma of the stagnant air. A bead of sweat beginning to form on his brow, Holmes begins to speak. “Damn, it’s hotter than the devil’s draws in here man, cut the AC on.” CW withdraws the cigar from his mouth and gives Deacon a disgusted look. Miraculously, he’s not sweating at all... “Boy, I never thought I’d see the day... You really are soft now huh” They must be treatin? you real good over there in Texas...” he says in a somewhat disappointed tone. The two exchange dirty looks before CW begins talking again. “You here to grill at me or you here to work” Now go on “head and start hittin” them weights, we got work to do.” He says as they come to a stop in front of the living room, which now has a weight bench situated in the middle of the floor and a stack of weights on each side of the bar. CW goes over and stacks a total of two hundred and fifteen pounds on the bar and takes his place standing behind it. Holmes takes a deep breath while wiping the sweat from his brow before walking over and laying down on it. Let the torture begin...
