No Emotion

28 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/28/05 There were certain things in the universe that Mindy Mercer just simply did not understand. Why was the sky blue? Why was the Earth round? Why did she have a soft spot for someone who had her hospitalized? Was it because of Markus' underlying charm? Or was it the cool looking samurai hat? Or maybe it was the drugs? No matter what the reasoning behind it, Mindy found herself falling for the long-haired, red-eyed boy in black. Mindy sat and pondered the situation as the darkness of her room gave her the comfort and reassurance of privacy. Mindy had been so upset those past couple of weeks, and it was understandable that she felt tingly at the thought of having someone to love again. Of course, she did feel guilty about it; she knew how terrible it was not to have a companion around, and it was the primary feeling her cousin Michael now had to endure. Sure, he'd gone through it before and sure things had worked out then, but that was before mysterious people came and threatened their lives. Now, was a reuniting between Michael and his wife really possible? 'Gods, I need to stop thinking about this...' Mindy groaned. Maybe a few lines in her diary would help her gather her thoughts. To the little pink diary I've sadly neglected, Sorry about not writing in ya lately. Things have been so hectic, I've barely had any time to sit down and actually write, much less have any kind of time to myself. Thankfully, things appear to be looking up slightly; I think me and Markus are back to having a friendship, which is good. Still, I think my cousin's beginning to lose it. I can't blame him for it, since I was pretty upset when I was all by myself these past couple of months. Wrestling is a great way to get my mind off things, but when I go home at night I just get that feeling that I can't just get home and go right to sleep... I felt like I wasn't living my life as fully as I wanted to. And I think now, Michael is beginning to feel the same way. I guess Jaden means more to him than wrestling, after all. I always knew he did, but you don't really bring those things up to Michael often unless you want your face punched in. I really wish I could comprehend the pain he's going through, because it's surely much more than anything I've ever had to take, but that goes without saying I suppose. I feel so useless sitting here while he's trying to sort himself out in his room right now, probably a wreck as it is... I want to cry so badly, knowing that my best friend hurts. I have to be strong, though... I don't want to mope around and in turn make Mikey feel even worse; if that's even possible, anyway. Oh well... tomorrow is another day. I can only hope things at least slightly improve; they can't possibly get any worse... Mindy xoxo 'Mikey?' The little girl knocked on her cousin's bedroom door, accentuating the start of a sunny morning in Truth or Consequence. It was another morning that saw Michael confine himself to his room- the same routine he had set up for the last three days. When Michael first found out the truth to Jaden's disappearance, Michael was out of the house non-stop. On some occasions, he didn't even return home at night. He had ended up spending over three hundred dollars of gas money in a span of one week; not that he seemed to mind, however. Michael was too wealthy as it was, and it hadn't mattered where his money went at that point. He did wish, though, that for every dollar he plugged out for the means of finding his wife, that some kind of clue to her whereabouts would turn up eventually. Michael did not have such luck. That led up to his refusal to try anymore- he didn't have the stomach to keep going on at the rate he was trying to set for himself. Even though it did cause him physical pain in his heart to think about 'giving up', he was doing even more damage to his body with the constant searching day in and day out. And was Michael willing to expose his weakness to anyone else? 'Not a chance in Hell.' So when Mindy knocked on his door that morning, he made extra sure that any consideration taken into opening the door was pushed away, deep into the back of his mind. 'Come on, Mikey,' the voice on the other side of the door continued, 'I know you're awake.. I made you breakfast and everything.' That was true. While Mindy had knocked on the door with one hand, the other juggled a tray that nicely balanced a full plate of french toast and bacon as well as a tall glass of peach Snapple; Michael's favorite non-alcoholic beverage. Still, Michael did not budge. 'Just leave it at the door,' he grumbled finally. Mindy pouted in defeat, giving a slight stomp to the ground beneath her. 'Are you sure? Can't I just come in and talk to-' 'Put it at the door, and leave me the fuck alone.' The abrupt and mean-spirited nature of the remark Michael had made left Mindy speechless. She almost lost her footing for a second, but quickly managed to regain her balance. 'Michael...' her voice stammered softly, but she couldn't find herself able to speak any further. After attempting to think of something to say, she simply let out a sigh and left the food tray down on the floor just outside of Michael's bedroom door. 'Mindy?' The girl swirled around in place to see Markus standing down the hallway, looking at her. 'What's goin' on, sweetness?' Mindy looked at the food at her feet, and shrugged her shoulders. 'Not much. Unfortunately, not much.' Markus frowned- he felt bad seeing Mindy sulking about the way she was, but he didn't want to show it. Instead, he tipped his hat forward, shielding his face from the sun that poured into the home through the numerous open windows. 'Michael's still not coming out of his room, is he?' Mindy shook her head. 'He go to the police?' Mindy's head went from making a 'no' motion to 'yes'. 'They have no leads, either.' 'Hm,' Markus toyed with a couple of strands of his hair in idle amusement. 'Perhaps you and I can go do a little searching of our own? I'm feeling up to it..' he lifted up his black tank top, revealing his taped up ribs underneath. 'Good as new. Almost, anyway...' Mindy probably would have smiled at Markus' offer, but she just wasn't up for smiling at the time. If her cousin didn't take the idea already, she'd be locked in her room as well. 'If you want, sure. I don't know if we'll be much help but.. it couldn't hurt to try.' 'Aye,' Markus replied, lifting his hat in an unnoticeable fashion; just enough to show a faint smile. 'Just as long as we take your car or Michael's... 'cause you know.. how mine's all raped like jail and such.' Again, Mindy normally would have laughed, but she shrugged the witty comment off and walked up to Markus quickly. 'Yeah,' she said, pulling out a set of keys from the pocket of her crimson colored hot pants. 'We'll take my lammy.' 'Lammy...?' Markus whispered to himself. It wasn't long after that to which he'd remembered what the 'lammy' was. And when he and Mindy stepped outside, his suspicions were confirmed. 'I always wanted to ride in that pink lamborghini of yours.' 'Well, now's your chance.' Mindy threw the driver door open and prepared to jump in, when something caught her eye. Why she bothered to notice it was a mystery to her; just one of those bad feelings. A sleek black limo was parked just across the street, with tinted windows that kept anything and everything inside of it out of view. 'Then again,' Mindy stated slowly, not taking her eyes off the shady looking vehicle as she slammed her own car's door as quick as she opened it. 'Going for a ride might have to wait until later.' The chauffeur in the limo sat nervously- and rightfully so- with a nine millimeter pistol pressed to his head. A gloved hand gripped the weapon, and that hand belonged to a figure dressed in all black; how he wasn't hot in that large cloak was a mystery, but that wasn't the first thought running through the minds of either of the two. 'Drive,' the figure ordered with a chilling voice, 'drive and hit the girl, or I will kill you.' The cloaked figure turned his head, and in the back of the limo sat a bound and gagged Jaden, her daughter in the same situation just next to her. Gabriella was crying incessantly into the black cloth that gagged her. 'And after I kill him, you two will be next.'
