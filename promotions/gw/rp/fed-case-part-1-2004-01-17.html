Fed Case (Part 1) Terrelle Green Global Wrestling Promo #766 Date: 1/17/04 Location: Houston,Texas Houston Rockets Game As my limosine pulls up I look on as over 10,000 people watch to see who is in the limo. They move frantically towards my door as my Boodyguards hoop out of the SUV in front of me shoving them back. I laugh as they fall off of their feet into the dirt. 'Why are those people not moving?' I thought. 'Normally at the sighting of a limo everyone would be moving towards not standing away.' I look on as these so called men slowly put there hand to their wrist and began talking. I realized that this was the time they have came to get me and they are planning it now. I looked around but, as I looked it became evident to me that I was boxed in. They were quickly closing in on me sitting back in the seat I flamed up a cuban cigar smiling. 'NOW!' He said. They rushed toward the vehicle as helicopters came from overtop the building and agents jumped from out of cars pointing their guns at me. As I turned my head looking at the shock on people's face I laughed as they called for me to get out. 'TERRELLE GREEN PLEASE COME OUT OF THE VEHICLE WITH YOUR HANDS ABOVE YOUR HEAD. TELL YOUR MEN TO STAND DOWN AND NO ONE WILL BE HARMED ANY SUDDEN MOVEMENTS FROM YOU OR ANYONE WILL BE SHOT DEAD. DO YOU UNDERSTAND?' I motioned the driver to open my door I heard the clicking of guns as he opened the door and I got out. I stood up as the smoke arose from my mouth and I smiled at the multitude that watched as agents stormed me throwing me to the ground. 'I guess you fucks finally got me huh?' I said. 'Well since you got me I guess I need my bond so I can get out of here I gotsta wrestle Monday.' 'Tell it to the judge you will be seeing him immediately to get your sentence.' The agent said. 'Sentence oh you have a warrant right?' I smiled. 'Save it Terrelle we got you this time and aint no getting away sneaky tricks nothing. Today you go to jail for murder, Kingpin, Drug Conspiracy, and possesing a firearm.' 'Thats all fine but I asked you a question do you have a Warrant for my arrest? Or are you deaf to the fact of what I just said?' 'We dont need one your guard had a blunt of weed on him with no insurance which gave us the right to search this and any other vehicle connected to you.' 'Oh so those reasons listed above has nothing to do with what you stopped me for so take me down town so it can be droppped and you fucks can wait some more.' He picked me up throwing me in the car with two cops slamming the door and finishe searching my vehicle. Sitting in the back seat I listen to the policeman's conversation. 'What do you think he will do?' 'I dont know what do you think?' 'I think he might but there is a slim to 1 chance he wont.' 'How would you know anyway you aint nobody but, you not him.' 'Well smart ass ask him then.' 'Alright I will.' 'Ask who what?' I asked. 'Hey umm Terrelle?' 'Dont call me that its Sir to you pig.' 'I mean sir. Could you guarantee us a position in your dynasty?' 'Now why would I do something like that?' 'Because we can give you information to anything you want to know about.' The other man shaking his head while the officer continues to try to bribe his way into a piece of my puzzle. 'Yes. But you will tell me something.' 'What' The two men say. 'Were is the Agent Harris's home located?' Suddenly there is a knock at the window and Agent Harris instructs them to let it down. 'Well..Well..Well if it isnt the Don Greeniso` the second, finally got you huh? The big bad man from up north The Don ,The Godfather, The Head Boss, Mister Mobster. I am honored to be in your presence. You know how long I have awaited such glamour? Three years since you killed my partner back in 2000 I have hunted and hunted but thanks to one of your own I got ya.' I began to think as to how he knew where I was gonna be nobody knows my moves no one. Then it came to me I told one man what I was going to do and one man only. Uncle Fracino a slight smirk went across my face and it sent Harris into amazement. I knew he knew that I was planning something and he was right. 'Well Mr.Greeniso` are you ready to go to jail?' 'Look you pig you embarrassed me once and you think I gonna let you do it again? Do not call me Greeniso` cause that name is not mine. You call my name exposing me to these people like we are Familia but, we arent just becuase your mother was italian your father was caucassion which makes you the same. I remember your father a good man running numbers for my father back in the days where we all used to be Familia. You changed Agent you are nothing like your old man God rest his soul.' Agent Harris reaching through the back window grabbing my neck shaking me I smile at him as he is enraged. As he released me he looked at the two men. 'Take him to booking.' 'Nice meeting you again Agent.' I said smiling. Agent Harris with a look of hatred as the squad car pulled off and I looked him in his eyes as we rode past. Rolling down the empty street the two officers loked at me knowing what information I required and I nodded as we went to the station.
