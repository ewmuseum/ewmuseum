Date: Evo #2 (8/16/04) So much for Merry Men.... Train didn't know much about the situation he was in. How'd he wind up in England? How'd that Son of a Bitch They Lord, King got his hands on Train's Platinum Title? Did Kunta Kinte manage to run out of there without losing another foot? Did Josephus managed to tap his way out of being fed to the Irish? Who knows? If someone does, it's not Train. However... he knows one thing for sure... The deep blue eyes sitting just about the shaft of the arrow pointed at his face.. The veluptuous breasts that heave beneath a plain white visably, despite the owner's attempt to appear cool and calm... The wide, sexy hips barely covered in the denim short shorts that travel down into a pair of toned and tanned legs... the long, raven hair that cascades freely over the slim, but muscular shoulders... This ain't no merry man. Just a pissed off woman. 'Who goes there?' The woman asks in a fervent, brassy tone. 'Whoever you won't shoot in the head with an arrow.' Train replies, curling his mouth up into a grin, while slowly raising his his hands to signal he's no threat working every ounce of charm he's ever possessed. 'There be not many, have they a silver tounge or no.' 'You sure?' Train asks with his still present grin, blatantly allowing his eyes to do laps, running up and down her curvy frame. 'You can do more than talk with a silver tounge.' 'Ye art a bold one,' she replies in a grumble, her tone letting Train know she is hardly amused. 'One stroke and I can end thy arrogance.' 'No you can't.' Train responds flatly. 'Is that so? How come ye to such a boisterous conclusion?' 'That's easy,' says Train feigning a head turn... suddenly Train swings his torso along with his arm, bring his hand hard against the inner forearm of the one extended, holding the wooden frame of the bow. The blow does what he thought it would, knocking her arms to the side, throwing her off balance and pushing him to the side of the arrows tip. It also has an unexpected effect, Train feeling the shaft of the arrow itself snaps in half with the force of the blow. The woman stumbles away, dropping the bow, freeing her hands to find the trunk of a nearby tree, using it to regain her balance. 'You'll never get the shot off.' Train steps back, knowing it's not over. With a rebel yell the woman charges him... She comes with a looping right hand, but Train ducks as he lunges to the side, leaving his leg out.... his extended leg has it's intended effect throwing her off balance as she steps through with her blow, tripping right over it. The hostile stranger does all she can to stay on her feet, stumbling forward. The trees appear to be her good friends today as she finds another one to grab onto, steadying herself. She torques her body a bit, her next move obviously to turn for a back hand shot to Train but she never gets the chance. Before either hand can come off the tree Train is on her, his wide hands grabbing hold of both wrists and locking them against the wood while Train uses his all of his weight to his advantage, pushing his frame into her behind, pinning her chest first against the tree as he holds her arms extended above her head. She does her best to wriggle away, but his massive body is too much r to free herself from. 'Damn ye!' she screams, struggling with all her might. 'Release thy grip or pay the consequence!' Train just presses in even harder, leaving her no room to move or struggle, so close he can almost feel her heartbeat. 'C'mon now. You know I ain't just gonna just let you go so you can come after me again. I rolled down the hill, not off the turnip truck baby girl. Now I'm gonna keep you here until you cool out...unelss we can come up with a better reason for being stuck together like this. 'I warn ye Moor... You shall not live another day if lest ye release they grip.' 'Uhuh... So what you gonna do baby girl? I suppose you gonna knock me out, right?' Train found the quip rather amusing himself. No sooner did it come out of his mouth did the world reduce itself to a series of flashes. The first was the searing pain at the base of his skull... then the ground, which seemed the hurt more than the last time he fell to it despite the lack of speed the second time around... Footsteps... alot of them... It was obvious one of them belonged to whoever he'd been too busy pushing up on what's her name to hear... Train was no expert on Elizabethan English, but the phrases he heard had all the earmarks of cursing which lead him to believe it was directed at him. Next came the series of silohettes.... They moved quickly...Train clutched at his head and rolled to his back... Train had the distinct feeling he was floating, but he was a little too low to the ground to be in the air... someone must've been holding him up on his knees, because his head was swimming too much to balance himself... That's when he heard the scrubbing of metal on some hard surface... He didn't use them often, but he knew the sound of a knife coming out of a sheath when he heard it. Suddenly she was there, standing over him... The only one he'd seen... she had a handful of dreadlocks, using it as a handle to tilt his head back at a very uncomfortable angle. As the world moved like it was stuck in molasses it was hard to make out what she was saying to him as she held the cold metal against his throat... Something about violation and honor... It didn't matter... dying at the hands of some bitch... That was always a risk of the trade on the streets...that's why Train always made it a point to use them and lose them... He'd stayed clear of any bullshit with a chick until now...this was a hell of a way to go... He felt the blade press closer to his neck... Suddenly there was another voice.. It was louder... spoke with even more authority... It was hard for Train to see her, his eyes unable to focus, but she suddenly stepped into his view...emerging from the shadows of trees in front of him... The woman still had the knife at his throat, but it didn't last long... Knife girl tossed Train's head down in disgust..... Black........ ===== ===== ===== ===== ===== Train opened his eyes. The world was finally moving in real time again. Train stared up at the gray, mason ceiling for a moment, letting his eyes follow the wooden slats of the rafters. He turned onto his side, looking around at place. The fire burning gently in the fireplace across the room and lent the only light, besides the shaft off moonlight that poored int through the narrow window not far past the foot of the bed where Train had been laying. Train kicked of the non-descript, brown animal skin that had served as his cover for Lord only knows how long. Train turn and swings his legs over the wooden frame of the bed and steps down onto the cold, brick Floor... there was concrete floor. It suddenly occurted to him that he shouldn't have felt anything from the floor...or noticed that the room was rather drafty... Train looked down finally realizing he wasnt' wearing a stitch of clothing. Train looked around the room in confusion... it was empty save for the bed, but there were no signs of what were his clothes. All in all this had been a damn strange day so he had a hard time being surprised. 'Ain't this some shit?' Train thought out loud. 'I should say so.' responded a voice not his own. It was young and feminine, ringing with an accent that hardly sounded English. Train whips around to face the direction from where it emerged. The little light that exists in the room doesn't extended the darkened corner. 'You'd be smart to step out where I can see,' says Train. 'Last thing you want me to do is to come get you.' 'Ye needn't worry,' says the voice again. Slowly the owner stepped out of the shadows into the light of the window and fire... She was tall and slender, her pinkish silken robe flowing effortlessly with her. He long, red hair blows in the slight breeze coming in through the window as her green eyes glimmer in Train's direction. Train, for his partner can only stare for a moment, making no attempt to cover himself. 'I have not gone through the trouble of bringing the here only to harm the.' 'You sicked those she-bitches on me?' 'Yes.... My apologies for such rough treatment such as that, but I was left with little choice. I need thy help in order to secure my future in this kingdom as well as the future of my true people in this land.' 'Who the hell are you?' 'I supposed it were best that I started from the begining. I... am Lady Pendragon. Sole heir to the throne of Ireland. However...my father, seeking peace with England agreed to have me be yet wed with England's king. There is no love in my heart for him, yet I agreed to be wed for the good of my land. But, upon the night of my first arrival, I heard my dear husband's plan to assassinate my father and take the Kingdom of Ireland for himself, extendign English rule. I sall not allow such an evil to befall either my father nor my land. That is why I summoned you hear... My father has always proven to be wise, sending a secret army to ensure my safety in the Kingdom of England... He called them my 'Caged Heat' army. When I heard the tale of the Moor who had escaped from the clutches from my husband I knew I was in need of they courage.' 'What you need from me?' 'I need two things. First of which is the death of my coniving snake of a husband. However... I can make no claim to the thrown after his death without an heir to present. I have to this day, not consumated my marriage, begging my husband for time. He grows more and more impatient and I feel that tonight will be the time he shall finally force me to the act that I loathe to take with him. So... When he comes, I need you to, by whatever means, send him to meet his maker. But first...' Lady Pendragon slowly allows her her fingers to slide down her robe, undoing all that binds it closed. The she gives it a gentle push and the robe slides off her shoulders to the ground, revealing her slender body, it's milky complexion seeming to glow in the moonlight. 'I need the to use thy...ample manhood to make me a woman... and a mother.' Train just allows his eyes to scan over her body for a moment. 'And what do I get out of it?' 'Besides the more obvious pleasures... My husband carries they prized possession with him, where ever he travels. When he arrives at this very chamber you will have your chance to retrieve it.' 'As long as you know as I ain't paying no child support.' Slowly and seductively she moves across the room, stopping right in front of Train. 'As I told ye, ye needn't worry. I need not any financial aid on your part. I only need the to be my baby's daddy.' Her hands slide lightly across his massive chest as she looks into his eyes. Train grins... ===== ===== ===== ===== ===== Thump, thump, thump... The door to Lady Pendgragon's Chamber opens abruptly after the thumping. The Lord King burns in, finding Lady Pendragon laying naked atop the bed of rustled and torn sheets. 'Finally...thou art ready to recieve thy husband in a manner that is fitting.' he says, looking at Lady Pendragon, only observing her smile. He never even hears the door behind him swing back towards the frame. Never sees Train emerge from the shadows. However he feels it as Train tears of his cape, spinning him half around. He has no time to react as Train tears the Platinum Title from his waist, throwing it to the ground. He doesn't even have a moment to protest as Train grabs hold of his foo-foo clothing and runs him across the room... It's a long drop out of the rooms window. His yells echo against the outer walls of the castle for less than 5 seconds. The thud at the end of that time tells the tale in ugly detail. Lady Pendragon slides off the bed, pulling the animal fur up to cover herself as Train scoops up the Platinum Title and slides it over his shoulder. 'All done.' he says to her. She strolls over and kisses him deeply on the lips. 'I feel within myself a life growing already, but, ou have done your duty. It is time for you to return to your own time.' 'You already sure you're pregnant.' 'Aye...a woman knows these things. I am so confident that thy trouser snake hat inject it's venom so powerfully I shall name him for that moment. I shall name our son... Venneman!' 'I like it...just dont' be asking me to come pick him up after school or nothing. Peace.' Train turns and steps through the dark doorway.... ===== ===== ===== ===== ===== BEEP, BEEP, BEEP, BEEP, BEEP!!!! Train shoots out of bed, nearlly tumbling to the floor of his room. He manages to catch himself and stumble over to the alarm clock on top of the chest of drawers. He slams the snooze button visciously, shutting off the shrill alarm. Train shakes his head, trying to bring himself around... His eyes, take a moment to dialate and he looks over to the Platinum Title as it sits next to the clock. The tv sits empty with a blue screen. He plods over and stoops down to the vcr, pushing the play button. It takes a moment, but the tv finally springs to life again, the video of Pendragon replaying itself after automaticly rewinding sometime during the night. Train watches for a moment...finally he presses the pause button and looks at the image of the man so many believe will end his reign as champion. Train just chuckles to himself... 'Who's yo' daddy muthafucka?'
