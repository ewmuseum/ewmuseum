2 Goliaths, 1 David

11 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11th February 2005 Discovery? Mankind is constantly on the search for something new, for something better. We hear many stories of people discovering new and better ways to do something but the next day we hear another story of how someone has improved on the original idea. This continues on and on and so the path of discovery continues. It is mankind’s curiosity that keeps the flow of discovery going. But the discovery I am talking about is not the discovery of inventions, I am talking about the discovery of self. People are always trying to discover themselves so they can define themselves. I always thought I knew myself, I always thought there was nothing left for me to discover but recent events have made me wonder. Perhaps I don’t know myself as well as I thought? I have been in Global Wrestling now for six months, six months where I have continuously battled some of the best in the business. I have claimed the Cruiserweight Title twice and still hold onto it. Some would say that I am at the top of my game, that I am a fast rising star in the ranks of the GW and a couple months ago these people would have been right. Six months I have been in the GW and yet when I look in the mirror I ask myself who I am. I maybe the GW Cruiserweight Champion but it is not a title that defines me. It is a title that at the moment people seem to be forgetting about, there is even talk of discarding it without any thought of me, the champion. Now don’t get me wrong I am not angry at the GW, I am angry at myself. It is my fault that the Cruiserweight Championship is being overlooked and since the Cruiserweight belt is being overlooked it means that I to am being discarded. I have noticed it recently, since I rid the GW of the tag team The 138, I have found myself at a loss. Even though I have been in the GW a while now I have not truly cemented my place in the roster. I need to do something big, something that will make me stand out from the rest and at the next Evolution I have just that chance? At the coming Evolution I have a chance to gain another title and become a double champion. When Evolution comes I could be the new GW Television Champion, not only that but I also advance in the tournament to claim the vacant GW Platinum Championship. Why will this cement me in the GW roster? Because to advance in this tournament I have to beat two Goliaths, who go by the names of Brick and Corvus. Now Brick I have faced and teamed with before, he is a man who I respect but a man who is in the way of what could be the defining moment of my career. Corvus is a man, or whatever he is, whom I have never faced but have seen in action and have heard stories about. He is this unstoppable monster that everyone believes is going to tear through this tournament. Corvus is indeed a vicious fighter but so am I. He probably sees me as a bug he can simply squash and forget about. Well let me just say this, when all three of us are in that ring just remember the tale of David and Goliath? The engine roared as I raced down the freeway towards the airport. It was 3 a.m in the morning and the freeway was practically empty, only a few lorries cluttered up the left lane. Night time still filled the sky; the stars twinkled off the black chrome of my Ferrari F50. As I slipped the gear into 5th and the engine roared again, I smiled? This would probably be the last time I ever drove this car because now that I had a family it wasn’t very practical. I smiled because I remembered Rachel’s first reaction to the car; she said to me “Aren’t you a bit young for a mid-life crisis?” Rachel had been so supportive of me recently and now that I was flying off to Australia it killed me to leave her and the baby behind. My train of thought was broken as my car phone began to ring. I glanced at the display which was flashing the name “Pete.” My heart stopped as Pete never usually calls me at this time unless it is emergency, nervously I pressed the receive button. “Pete, what is it?” I asked anxiously. Pete’s voice came over the car speakers. “Hey Adam, just checking your on your way to the airport.” “Yeah Pete, about 10 minutes away,” I sighed sensing there was something more, “But that isn’t why you called is it?” “What?” Pete asked trying to act innocent. “You don’t call someone at 3 a.m in the morning just to ask if they are on their way to the airport.” “Okay, okay, I just want to know that you are going to be okay in Australia.” “How do you mean?” “Well you are facing 2 huge guys Adam, who both want that title as much as you. You are a good fighter but I don’t know how your body will cope with the punishment it is about to take.” My grip tightened on the steering wheel as my teeth gritted in anger. The fact that Pete, my best friend thought that I couldn’t do this infuriated me. I took a deep breath before replying. “Listen Pete, I have gone six months without anything to get my teeth into. This Platinum Series is my chance to stand up and be noticed. My body can take all the punishment the GW wants to throw at me. This is my chance to shine Pete! So don’t tell me what I am capable of because I am quite aware!” I leaned towards the phone to end the call. “Sorry,” Came Pete’s reply from over the speakers, “It is just this whole Richard Wood thing, it has got me concerned about everything. I mean he could turn up at any second and ruin everything for you.” “Well he has had 2 years to do that Pete but nothing has happened has it?” I said feeling my anger subside. “No.” “And anyway I went to see him?” “What”!”!” Tuesday 8th February Since Bubba McCoy had given me last week’s Evolution off, I had decided to visit the offices of Wood’s company, B.T.I. My curiosity had got the better of me and I needed to know if Wood knew of me or not. I couldn’t march in and ask him out right as he could have me carted away into his labs. I needed to be subtle and I needed a story. I quickly decided that I was just going to thank him for his generosity last year and give him a token of my thanks. Thanks to the business card, Wood had given me on the flight last year, I was able to find the offices without any hassle. So there I stood outside this huge skyscraper, the sun glistened of the tower of glass. I gripped the bottle of wine I had in my right hand tightly. My heart bounded against my chest, the last time I was this nervous was when I was stood outside that wrestling academy when I was 18. The circle of people gathering around me in a busy New York street snapped me out of my trance. I looked around at the faces all staring and judging me. “What” Have you never seen a man in a bio-suit before?” I yelled and shrugged them off as I stepped through the revolving doors of the B.T.I office tower. Again I was in awe of the magnificence of the reception area. The floor was polished white marble that covered the whole of the reception area. The waiting area to my right was filled comfortable leather chairs with a beautiful glass table positioned in the middle. I walked towards the blonde women sat behind a huge circular oak table with the initials B.T.I placed on the front. She was staring at me just like the people outside; I decided to ignore it as I stood in front of her. She quickly snapped out of her stare and placed on her fake smile. “Yes’erm’sir, how may I help you?” “Yes I am here to see Richard Wood?” “What is your name sir?” “Adam Myers.” “Do you have an appointment?” “Erm, no I don’t, I was just hoping perhaps I could see him for a couple of minutes.” I said with the friendliest tone I could. The receptionist looked down at what I guessed was the appointment book. She looked up and made a sucking noise through her teeth. “I’m afraid Mr.Wood is fully booked today. I will call him and see if he can squeeze you in, if you would care to take a seat.” She said as she pointed towards the waiting area. “Thank you.” I said quietly as she picked up the phone. As I walked towards the waiting area I could feel the security family follow me all the way. As I sat down I looked around me and it seemed the whole area had stopped as people with files were looking at me. However they were not looking at me like people were before they were looking at the suit, I held my hands out in a way to ask what they wanted. They snapped out of it and continued what they were doing. I felt uncomfortable like something wasn’t right about this whole situation. My heart began to bound again, had I made the wrong decision? The receptionist walked over still with her fake smile. I stood up as she looked straight into my eyes. “I’m sorry Mr. Myers but Mr Wood doesn’t seem to be picking up his phone. He must be in a meeting of some sort.” “Oh okay,” I replied feeling quite relieved, “maybe another time, thank you.” The receptionist nodded her head and moved back to her desk as I made my way to the entrance. I took another look around me and looked up at the security camera which was staring directly back at me. The uneasy feeling filled my body as I walked out. Even as I made my way down the street I couldn’t help shake the feeling that? I was being watched!
