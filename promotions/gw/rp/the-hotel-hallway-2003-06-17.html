The Hotel Hallway

17 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/17/03 The scene fades in and the cameraman is in the process of disconnecting the camera wires, and getting ready to pack up and go home. The cameraman sets the cameraman on the floor, and two large black shoes fill the camera's view. Cameraman: 'OH MY!' The camera is then jerked upwards, and quickly the view pans up from the large black shoes, up a pair of long black legs, which are covered by a pair of faded blue jeans, up further panning over a plain white t-shirt, and finally stopping at the face. The long, shoulder length blond hair, along the with cold, icy, blue eyes reveals the feet were those of ICE. Ice stands there with his arms folded behind his back, staring straight ahead at the camera. 'Tsk...Tsk...Tsk. We've known each other for a while now, and I would have assumed that you'd put up more of an effort than this. I guess my assumption of some things never changing was correct. Because it seems that the level of your intelligence has not changed one single bit. However, it seems that your courage has changed Angus. Has it really been that long, that you have forgotten just who it is that you are. Has it been that long for you Angus, that you have lost that want and courage to say something in defense of yourself? Or were you just counting on Chris Canada not saying anything and being a little punk, like he is right now, then hoping that I would follow in suit. Well Angus don't think for one single, solitary second that I don't have you, and our little match in the front of my mind. You see, that match, it is the only thing I have been training for. The only thing that I have been preparing for.' A slight smirk crawls across Ice's face. 'But whatever you want to do is fine with me. You want to put yourself in a hole, not say a word, come off like a little punk, and play hide and go seek. Well you can hide all you want now, but eventually you will come out of hiding, and you will be found. Whether you want to be or not, you will be forced out of hiding, and forced to come down to the ring, and get exactly what it is that is coming to you. What about Christopher Canada, who cares about Christopher Canada?! The cowardous this mand is showing, he doesn't even deserve to use MY GREAT COUNTRY'S name. Hell he doesn't even deserve to be in the same match as me! Just like all the other so called superstars who came from the same hell hole that I did who have done absolutely nothing with themselves since entering the GW! What happened to the great Kid Flash? What happened to Blitz? Where the hell is Angus the Bull? Exactly, none of them have done a damn thing! But who has? ME! I stood victorious last week! Not Blitz, not Angus, not Kid Flash! ME! And who will stand victorious again this week? ME! Week after week, the name on the other side of the card will change, but the results will stay the same. Week after week, you can throw the biggest and brightest names you got, and they will all go down.' Ice pulls his right hand from behind his back, and slowly pulls any strands of his long, shoulder length, blond hair out from in front of his face as he lets out a long deep sigh, while he regains his composure. 'I know what you are thinking, after the comments I just made. Well let me inform you of a little something. I DON'T CARE WHAT ANY OF YOU THINK! Why? Because the fact of the matter is this. When the lights go down, and that music hits. You walk down that aisle, and step into that ring, you are in MY WORLD. No matter what city you are in that night, no matter what the type of match is, in MY WORLD I HAVE NO EQUAL!' Ice turns around from the camera, and walks down the long hallway. The camera watches until he turns the corner, then cuts to commercial.
