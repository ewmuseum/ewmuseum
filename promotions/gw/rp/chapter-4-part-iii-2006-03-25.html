Chapter 4 - Part III

Date: 3-25-06 The scene opens on the rooftop of a tall skyscraper was Chi who was looking down at the large metropolis that was Tokyo as she began to think about what Megumi told her the other night. ---- Chi waved the waiter away from her as she looked Megumi with seriousness. “Now start from the beginning.” “Have you ever the saying that if you keep knocking on the devil’s door someone is bound to answer.” Megumi looked at Chi. “Yeah, what about it?” Megumi sharpened her eyes in seriousness. “He has opened that door.” Chi raised a brow and stared at Megumi with great confusion. “You see, Mr. Kushinaga is a?” ---- Chi’s thoughts are suddenly broken when she detected Kyo’s power in the distant as she blurred away and headed in the direction from where his power was coming from. ==|Part III: The Things You Do For Love (cont.)|== Kyo has arrived at Shinjuku Station on his motorcycle where he stared at the station through his orange colored shades, but he sharpened his eyes in seriousness when he felt and saw a demonic aura around the station. “That’s a nasty looking aura.” Kyo shifts his serious eyes over to Chi’s voice as he saw that she was sitting on his motorcycle with her legs crossed and her eyes locked on the station. “What the hell are you doing here?” He turned his attention back to the station. “I was going to ask you the same thing.” Chi hopped off his motorcycle. “I don’t know what brought you here, but I do know that you’re not stupid enough to just go walking into what is obviously a trap.” She glared at him. “If I didn’t know any better it sounds that you’re concern for me.” Kyo smirked as he pulled out his Spell Binder and a God Shell, but he saw that this was his last shell. “Shit.” He loaded the God Shell within the gun with a look of frustration on his face. Chi turns away and scoffed with a dark blush across her cheeks. “I just don’t want anyone to kill you except for me.” Kyo sighs softly. “Didn’t I tell you to take a number?” He holstered the gun on the back of his pant and headed to the station with great seriousness in his eyes. “Hey wait for me!” Chi quickly followed him. [:: ----- ::] They entered the station where they're standing in the middle of the lobby and saw that the people were acting normally. “Look at them.” Chi looked with seriousness. “They’re unaware that this place is radiating with enough demonic aura that can light up Tokyo.” “I can handle this by myself.” Chi looks at Kyo. “Well, the last time I remember I found your sorry ass in a weakened condition not one but two times in the past two weeks.” She smirked. “If that was you handling the situation then I dare not see what happens when you’re not handling the situation.” “Funny.” Kyo nodded slowly as he glared at Chi. A loud dong sounds! Kyo and Chi look over in the direction of the sound and saw that it came from the indoor clock that hangs above the station’s front entrance. Kyo raises a brow. “I don’t remember that being there.” Chi suddenly sharpens her eyes in alertness. “I think the trap is coming into play.” Kyo stares at Chi with a slight curious look on his face, but his expression changed to seriousness when he saw what Chi was staring which was that everyone in the station was standing motionless. Clock dongs again! The people savage up any weapons they found in their clothes and their surroundings as they all was glaring at both of them with glowing emerald eyes. Clock dongs again! Most of the people lunge at them for a ground attack while the others leap high in the air for an aerial attack. Kyo and Chi maneuver through both their attacks as they glare at the people with seriousness. The people lunge at them once more and started attacking them. “What the hell is going on here!”” Chi was blocking and fighting off the people. Kyo grabs a man’s fist and pulled him in where he delivered a swift chop to the back of the man’s neck. “These people are being controlled that’s what the hell is going on!” He continued knocking the people out with swift chops to the backs of their necks. Kyo and Chi defeated the people, but they looked with surprise when they saw the people were getting up as if nothing happened to them. Chi glares at the people. “Enough of this shit.” She pulled out her sword from within her cloak and swung her sword at the nearest person, but she felt her sword stop short. “What the!”” Chi looked in slight shock when she saw that Kyo has grabbed hold of the blade. “What the hell are you doing!”” She glared at Kyo. “These people don’t need to die because they fell under someone’s spell.” Kyo kept his tight grip on the blade and watched the people surround them as his blood trailed down the middle of Chi’s sword. “Well, we can continue on knocking these people out, but they’ll just stand right back that is if we can keep them down permanently.” Chi smirked as she sharpened her eyes in seriousness. “Now let go of my sword.” The people slowly approach them. “Death isn’t always the answer, you know.” Kyo glared at her for a moment before he looked back at the people. “We need to find the source of this spell if we do then we can snap these people out of it.” He snapped his head over at the station’s clock as he remembered that everyone was acting normal before the clock dong. “That’s it.” Chi looks at Kyo with a raised brow. Kyo makes a couple of ninja hand signs with his free hand before he stroke two fingers across the blade smearing his blood. “I need to burrow this.” He snatched Chi’s sword out of her hand and leaped high in the air. “Avenger, what are you doing!”” Chi saw him soaring toward the clock. “Blood Slash!” Kyo swung the sword releasing an energy attack at the clock. The clock explodes! Something large falls through the flames and clock’s debris and hit the floor with a boom. Everyone stop as they are standing motionless again. The something turns out to be a giant spider that was covered in fire as it was on its? back with its? many legs kicking in the air. “What the hell is that?” Chi looked with shock. Kyo approaches the spider with the sword resting on his shoulder. “This nasty looking thing is the one responsible for controlling these people.” He saw the spider was looking up at him with its? many eyes, but he ended that with killing the spider with a fatal stab to the head. “I hate spiders.” All the people soon collapse. Kyo pulls the sword of the spider’s skull as he watched it mysteriously caught fire and burn up not leaving a trace of its? existence. He tosses Chi back her sword as he stared down at the spider’s death spot in deep thought. Chi starts cleaning the sword after she caught it as she approached Kyo. “The demonic aura still remains.” “This was just a mere appetizer.” Kyo sharpened his eyes in much seriousness. “The real fight is about to begin.” “Oh, how right you are, Mr. Kushinaga.” Kyo and Chi look around their surroundings as they’re trying to sense where that female voice came from. Webbing comes of nowhere and wrapped itself around Kyo’s waist as he gets pull back with great force. Kyo crashes right through the station’s back wall! Chi snaps her head and looked with great shock. “Avenger!” She started running after him, but she stopped when a large number Ghost Men suddenly appeared before her. “Not again.” Chi saw them lunge at them with their hands in their coats as they are preparing to draw their large handguns. “Come on then!” She held her sword in defense as her eyes was glowing crimson. [:: ----- ::] Kyo was outside of Shinjuku Station where the trains are parked. He lay motionless on a pair of train tracks for a moment before he stirred showing signs of consciousness. “I’m really getting tired of being thrown through walls.” Kyo slowly stood on the tracks as he winced when he felt a burning feeling coming from his waist. He looks down and saw that the webbing was smoking as he yanked the webbing off his waist to reveal that it has burned some of his shirt off. “My webbing can eat through anything especially the flesh of a strong man.” Kyo snaps his head to the voice and saw that a woman in a sultry black dress standing on top of a train cart. “And you are?” He glared at her. “I’m Widow from the elite group, Mother’s Children.” Widow glared back at him. “So, you’re from that group I’m been hearing about. I thought you were all away killing Shinto Priests and Priestess.” “Not anymore.” Widow walked up to the edge of the cart as she kept her serious and yet somewhat passionate eyes on him. “Lord Aion has given us new orders to capture you alive.” Kyo sighs in a frustrating manner. “When will he learn that I’m not going to join him?” He looked up at Widow. “Widow, wasn’t it” Before I kick your ass I want to know something.” “And what’s that?” “I already know that you put Jun under your spell.” Widow looks with slight surprise, but she soon smirked. “Lord Aion did say you were a quick one.” “It wasn’t hard to tell that something was wrong with Jun since he didn’t call me Kyosuke and I didn’t hear any reassurance in his voice.” Kyo glared at her. “Now I want to know is he dead or alive.” “Let’s just say that your friend was delicious.” Widow licked her lips as she smirked. Kyo lowers his head as his raven hair fall over his eyes hiding the furiousness that they hold. “Scratch that.” He closed his bloody hand into a fist. “I’m not going to kick your ass I’m going to kill your ass.” “Interesting.” Widow didn’t look threatened. “I wonder how you will be able to do that without any power because I’m sensing nothing from you.” She crossed her arms. “So, it’s pretty much pointless to fight me. Just be a good boy and come back with me.” “You don’t know me too well, do you?” Kyo has finished making the last of ninja sign with his unclenched hand. “If you had done your research then you would know that I’m nowhere near from being a good boy.” He stroked two fingers across the knuckles of his bloody fist. “Blood Bomb!” Kyo thrust out that hand releasing a fast energy orb right at Widow. Widow has a look of surprise as she avoided by leaping up in the air. The entire cart explodes! “Where did that come from!”” Widow looked with greater surprise when she saw Kyo was heading straight for her. Kyo connects with a powerful backhand slap across Widow’s face. She falls straight down and crashed right through the roof of a cart! Kyo grabs hold of a nearby pole where he swung around it halfway before he launched himself right after Widow. He looks with surprise when he saw webbing heading right at him as he tried avoiding it, but it caught him around his ankles. Kyo feels a hard tuck on the webbing as that brought him down on the ground hard. The webbing begins burning him, as he started pulling it off him. “I don’t think so!” Widow leaped through the hole that she made as she opened her mouth wide where shot out more webbing. She continues this until he was completely wrapped in her deadly webbing. “Don’t know how you were able to do that attack, but it won’t happen again.” Widow stared at the cocoon with some concern. “I hope he doesn’t melt away before I get him back to Lord Aion. I better hurry and get him there.” She approached the cocoon. The cocoon starts swelling up in the middle. Widow stops and stared at the cocoon with great confusion. Cocoon begins forming cracks from the swollen up part. Golden light shines through the cracks. “What’s this!”” Widow took a step back with a frightened look on her face. The cocoon cracks wide open as it exploded in a blinding golden light! Widow gasps as she shielded her eyes from the light. [:: ----- ::] The Ghost Men was shooting at Chi. Chi was slipping through the bullets for a while before she lunged at a couple of Ghost Men and struck them all at once with a powerful slash attack where they disperse into black vapor. The remaining Ghost Men continuing shooting at Chi. Chi lunges over to pillar as she was standing behind it for cover. Chi goes for an all attack on the Ghost Men, but she stops when she sensed Kyo’s power rising dramatically. “He’s transforming again.” She sharpened her eyes in seriousness. “And I feel that his power isn’t going to stop rising.” Chi looked at the large hole that Kyo went through and saw the bright light shine in. “I want to see this power for myself. I want to see if he really is what you said he is to be.” The Ghost Men ignores the light as they continued shooting at the pillar. “But first I have to get through these bastards.” Chi glared as she held her sword offensively. She takes a deep breath before she moved away from the pillar and lunged right at the shooting Ghost Men, but she leaped her in the air with her sword held up high. She was heading straight down as she screaming in loud battle cry with her eyes a glowing darker crimson than before. [:: ----- ::] The light disappears. Widow lowers her hands from her eyes, but her eyes started quivering in fear from seeing and sensing Kyo’s great power. Kyo was standing among the webbing within a raging inferno of energy that was radiating volts of electricity as his head was lowered and his clothes halfway burnt off his body. He clenches both hands into tight fists and sharply lifted his head back releasing a mighty battle cry which caused the inferno to become more violent as four wings sprout out from his back. Widow nearly falls over from Kyo’s sudden outburst. “Dr. White was right.” Her eye was still quivering in fear. “His power is equal to Lord Aion’s.” Kyo lowers his head which made the four wings disperse into many feathers. He locks his violet glowing eyes on Widow. “I don’t care how strong he is.” Widow looked determine, but some fear still remained on her face. “I’m still going to retrieve him and bring him back to Lord Aion by any means.” Kyo takes a step forward, but he made a curious sound when he noticed something in the corner of his eye. He turns and saw his own reflection in the window on a nearby train cart. Kyo approaches the cart and saw that his appearance has changed. His long raven hair has turned into a spiky red as his eyes soon stopped glowing where he saw that his eyes has changed from brown to violet. Widow watches him carefully. “What has happened to me?” Kyo stared at his reflection with great confusion, but his confusion disappeared and was replaced by great pain. “Not again!” He favored his head as the headache has returned which once again made the markings on his forearms glow and triggering the same visions that he saw back on Wave Cruiser. Widow looks at Kyo with confusion. “Is he having trouble controlling our power?” Kyo soon comes back upon the vision that consist of the figure standing among the demon and human corpses, but this time he was able to see the figure’s face. The figure reveals to be none other then Kyo himself in his transformation. He and both the mystic swords that he was holding in each hand were covered in the blood of the fallen victims. The vision comes to an abrupt end. Kyo was staring at his reflection once more. “I remember?” His eyes were quivering in great shock. “I remember who I am...” He took a closer look of himself. “What I am?” Widow sharpens her eyes in readiness as they glow emerald. “Now is my chance to attack him.” Her fingernails grow long and sharp as she leaped high in the air for an aerial attack. Kyo senses that Widow was approaching as he shifted his eyes over to her and thrust his hand out releasing a strong pulse of his own energy at her. Widow gasps as she was thrown back with great force and crashed into rafters! Kyo glares up at the rafters, but he soon sharpened his eyes in more seriousness when he saw Widow’s webbing was heading toward him. He catches the webbing with one hand ignoring the burning feeling as he gave it a hard jerk which pulled Widow out of the rafters. She slams into the ground hard! “If this is the only attack you have then this fight is going to end very quickly for you.” Kyo glared at her as he released the webbing. Widow staggers back up to her feet as she spits what remain of her webbing out. She wipes the blood off her lip while looking at him with much hatred. “You’re going to be coming back with me even if I have to bring you back half melted away!” Widow lunged right at him and started swiping at him with her long fingernails. Kyo slips through a couple of her swipes before he grabbed hold of Widow’s hand by the wrist. She glares at him and swipe at him with her free hand. He soon grabs hold of that hand where he pulled her over and knocked her back down on the ground with a hard headbutt. Widow favors her head and felt blood was on her forehead as she realized that she has been busted open. “What’s wrong with you! I’m a woman!” She glared at him. Kyo scoffs. “You’re a bitch.” He sharpened his eyes in seriousness as he popped his knuckles. “A bitch who is about to die for killing my best friend.” Kyo walked up to her with a smirk on his face. “You know, half the fighters at Global have more skill than you and they’re human.” “Don’t you dare compare me to them!” Widow shot up on her feet and started swiping at him once more. Kyo blocks her swipes with one arm. “This is boring.” He saw Widow go for a backhand swipe, but he raised a brow and looked with curiousness when she pulled her arm short. Widow halfway spins her body as she leaned her face up to his and planted a deep kiss on his lips. She has a content look on her face, but the look slowly turned to shock as it was revealed that Kyo has placed his hand over his lips before her lips made contact. “I’m going to take a wild guess here and say that your lips are coated with a deadly poison.” Kyo stared at her with a smirk. “You’re really pathetic as a fighter and really not my type!” He quickly nailed her with the somersault kick. Widow flies back and crashed into a control panel! One of the parked trains begins activating as the tracks do too in result to the damaged control panel. Kyo pulls out his Spell Binder and aimed it at Widow with death on his face. “This fight is over.” Widow begins laughing as she was on all fours with her head lowered. “You have one more attack, don’t you?” Widow snaps her head and looked at him with the skin on her face was moving around from as she continued laughing. “That’s a yes.” Kyo sighed with frustration as he rested the Spell Binder on his shoulder. ---- The Deathscythe Outlaw has finally reawakened his forgotten power and regained his lost memory about what he is. Widow is transformation but into what and will it be enough to defeat Kyo’s awesome new power? She is fighting for love? And he is fighting to avenge his friend’s death?
