The screenplay

21 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/21/04 The date: August 10, 2004 Brick had been sitting in his home for hours. For 5 minutes of those hours, he spoke with his best friend Aaron over the phone. 'It's really supposed to be coming in?' 'Yeah. Saul told me it'd be getting delivered today. I just have to wait till the deliveryman gets here. I'll call you as soon as I get it.' BZZZZ! went the intercom. 'Yeah, that's probably him. I'll let you know.' Brick then walked over 'Yes?' 'Fed Ex.' Brick quickly buzzed the deliveryman in. 'Hey, how are you doing?' 'Oh, my God, it's you!' 'Hey.' 'My buddies are never gonna believe I delivered a package to Brick's apartment. I swear to freakin' God, dude, you freakin' rock!' 'I swear, if you ask me if you can suck my cock...' 'Huh? Oh, like that part of 'My Band'.' Right then, the deliveryman started singing, 'I'm the lead singer of my band, I get all the girl's to take off their underpants...' 'Duke.' 'I'm the lead singer of my band, my salsa makes all the pretty girl's wanna dance My salsa, ' 'Duke.' 'Look out for my next single, it's called My Salsa...' 'Duke!' 'Sorry. Uh, here's the package. I just need you to sign here.' Brick quickly signed his name to the board. 'Thanks a lot.' 'Have a nice day.' 'Yeah, you too.' Brick shook his head after closing the door. After walking out of the vestibule into his living room, he slowly opened the package and pulled out what looked like a screenplay. On the cover was: Luke Cage: Hero for Hire By Ben Ramsey Draft Date: 11/01/03 'Hey Aaron, that was the Fed Ex guy. The screenplay's here.' A half hour later, Aaron arrived at Brick's. Brick held the screenplay out to Aaron. 'Draft date's kinda old. Almost a year.' 'Yeah, well, that's the most Saul could get me. He's trying to see if there's a new draft.' 'Remind me again who Ben Ramsey is.' 'He wrote a couple of movies. The Big Hit, Love and a Bullet.' 'Wait a minute. He wrote The Big Hit?' 'Yeah. Why?' 'Why? That movie was garbage, that's why! You cannot tell me that movie wasn't garbage!' 'Well, you got a point,' Brick sighed. 'Big Hit was terrible. So, he had one bad movie.' 'Yeah, and John Singleton's had quite a few, himself.' 'Aaron...' 'No, I mean it. There's that comment he made about imagining 50 Cent with superpowers. If 2 Fast 2 Furious was any indication of where his filmmaking is going, maybe you ought to think twice about being in this movie.' 'Well, I'm not gonna do that. Besides, there could be some changes before casting starts. And I know I'm gonna have to work for this. But, hey, I never got anything by not working for it.' Aaron simply shrugged his shoulders. He knew better than try and dissuade Brick when his mind was set on something. 'You know, I had a thought. The Luke Cage movie is being made by Sony Pictures, right?' 'Yeah.' 'Well, Sony also made the Spiderman movies.' 'What's your point?' 'Well, they'll never be able to do an Avengers movie because all the characters' movie rights are tied up at different movie studios. That's also why they couldn't use Morbius in Blade 2. But it's possible that they could do a Spiderman/Luke Cage movie.' 'What?' 'Well, they could. And it's not like Spider Man and Cage have never met. They could do it kinda like Amazing Spiderman 123?' '123?' 'Yeah, it was right after the issue where the Green Goblin died.' 'Oh, yeah, that's right. Still can't believe they brought him back. How did they explain him surviving?' 'The serum that gave him his strength also gave him a healing factor.' 'Oh, yeah. I think I remember issue 123. J. Jonah Jameson hired Cage to bring Spiderman in, dead or alive, because he thought Spiderman killed Norman Osborn.' 'And Cage got pissed off because Spidey said he was like a cheap thug and a mercenary.' 'Yeah, and then they fought on the Empire State University campus, before they finally talked it out.' 'But the best part was the image of Jameson with $5000 stuffed in his mouth.' 'You sure it was all 5000?' 'Pretty sure.' 'Now, that would be a movie.' 'Yeah, it'd be great, maybe if Toby Macguire wasn't playing Spiderman.' 'Toby's a decent Spiderman.' 'He mumbles.' 'So do you.' 'Not every damn word.' 'Still, a Spiderman/Luke Cage movie would rock.' Brick smiled. 'Yeah, it would.'
