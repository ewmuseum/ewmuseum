Date: 6-9-05 Malika Jadieu’s diary November 5th, 2004 I have grown quite lovesick. It is unbearable. Never could I allow myself enough time to fall into a trance so as my soul could rest. Fortunately, the man that I gave my heart to, returns within days. He has sent me many letters of his days of training. I am so happy for him. He still has yet to speak to his parents. I know diary, it is not my place, but I believe he needs to speak to his family. Making them an outcast would only cause many regrets in his future and I do not want that. Especially if I am in the boundary of his future. This lovely melancholy that has rendered my soul weak has grown. I do not know what actions I can take to appease the fire that burns. My friend Helen tells me that I should just be open to my love. But how can I... when I am still a child deep inside my heart and soul. I wish I never have to grow older. I just want to remain a child with the puppy love that T’sao and I used to share. But now it seems... it has grown to be something more and I fear it. Many times I ask myself what love really is, and nothing comes to mind. Is love so hard to understand? I have spoken to a neighbor of mine today about love. She is very fond of such an emotion. The most revealing and shocking words she spoke to me was that of her and her husband (for over 40 years), have just realized the full emotional revelry of what love really is. “It is a life long journey.” She tells me as I took my leave. Lifelong journey.... the knowledge frightens me. What if this “love” that I feel now is nothing more than infatuation, or worse... lust. I do not know if I can deal with such dishonor to myself and to T’sao. Though I guess if it is a journey, I wouldn’t want go through that directionless road without him. Oh diary, his words in his last letter still echo and pierce my heart with desire for him. ? Tell me you love me once more and I shall embrace you, guard you from this world and forever love you. Once that is accomplish, tell me you love me one last time and I shall take you to our castle in the sky, away from this fool’s paradise where my love for you shall be everlasting.” Everlasting.... My struggling heart draws to him like honey. I hope one day I can find it in my heart and tell him what my heart desires. How understanding he is... and yet I know it pains him that through the years of friendship and so much more, I have not opened to him as much as he to me. I pray that God will help me and guide me to that fateful day. ***** June 8th, 2005 There was the feeling of a storm brewing as the sun descended halfway down the horizon. His eyes glittered with hope as he watched the sunset from his window. “Darkly erotic... can it be that this world desires for solitude in the masses of emotion that fills it like leeches?” Satisfied from the sight’s grandeur, he turns away walking towards the door when his cell phone rang. Taking it in his hand, he pushes the call button and raises the phone to his ear. “Your late,” cold yet calm, a woman’s commanding voice echoed from the other line. “It is about to rain my darling assassin, the information that I have for you may be lost if you do not hurry yourself.” This woman who got a hold of him earlier in the week had told him that she has information on the disappearance of his family, and future bride. But he knows nothing of her, nor has he asked. Silent. He felt his knife underneath his coat. His protection just in case things were to get messy. Appeased by the revelation that everything was in order, he replied, “I am on my way right now, wait for me.” A snicker echoed from the woman’s line. “Good... hurry Liang, my patience does wear thin.” With those lasting words, the phone immediately dies. “This eminent turn of events grow cold in my heart. This woman for reasons unknown to me seems to be quite in a disposition in herself, shunning out the truth of this calling...” He muttered to himself. His hopes were low for such a meeting as this. Any concealed secrets of the events that has plagued his life by a woman whom only has exposed her voice... as any other human being, the doubts in his head grew to be that of a disease. Getting his keys, he casually made his way out of the bedroom and through his house to the front door. He did not want to glance at the pictures on the walls that he passed, he needed to move forward. Hopefully finally someone can give him some answers to the disappearances and the questions. Only time will actually tell. ***** (Fifteen minutes later) The rain fell like a faucet. It took longer than he thought to actually get to his destination; of course it was someplace where meetings weren’t actually, casual. The long alley way was filled with garbage cans and other trash but halfway down a car was parked. A black lincoln it looked like from afar with a woman standing before it. “How delightful.” He uttered as he came to a complete stop and then turning off his engine. “A woman standing in the rain... of course she’d expect me to leave the dryness of my car and get drenched with her. Hmmph...” Opening the door, he quickly escaped and shut it, not wanting to get any part of the interior that wet. She turned to him as he approach her. “Well well, I thought someone of your expertise would have some punctuality.” She was definitely an american. Her accent not only gave it away, but so did her way of approaching him. Her hips swayed as if she was walking down the aisle or towards the stage ready to perform. “Are you wasting my time with smart remarks, or do you actually have information for me?” Both individuals stood as the rain fell faster with lightning as there only source of light for the quick darkness that overshadowed them both. “Very well... have you ever heard of the many unknown accounts of mass disappearances” Tribes in south america disappearing without a trace. Most of these mass disappearance in the region happened during the time of fixing dinner. Or you may be familiar about the chinese regiment that disappeared without a trace? I’m sure that one you have heard.” This was nonsense. Nothing but another psychotic human being. He reached under his coat and thought of his reply. “Yes i’ve heard the myth of the regiment in china during world war 2 disappearing. But what does any of this have to do with the disappearance of my family and a girl who I hold in high regard?” Her glance cocked low, catching him reaching under his coat. She smiled. “It has everything to do with it... do you believe in vampires and those lot?” He hesitated. He had no idea where this conversation was going, and why he should allow it to continue. He grasp tightly unto his knife. “Where is this joke going girl?” The rain suddenly stopped. Thundred roared taking his attention to the sky. It sounded as if heaven itself was splitting in two by its seismic breach. The time he spent staring was enough for the woman made a dash for him. Bringin his attention back to her, a glimpse of her was enough for he swiftly took the knife out of its sheath and threw it to her. The knife pierced her in the chest. The force of the collision took her back and into the front of her car. “Little girl...” His steps echoed from the sound of the puddle as he made his way towards the body of the girl. Blood escaped the wound quickly. He grasp unto his knife and pulled it out of her chest, allowing the rain to clean off the blood that dripped down the tip. “Now, there must be something in your pocket my dear... a note, something.” He returned the knife in its sheath. Grabbing her by her wet shirt, he brings her up on top of the car. His hands searched through her pockets first. The right pocket of her pants was empty, but the left held a small envelope. He took the envelope and placed it in his own pocket. He began to search again just in case. “I do apologize, I tire quickly when it comes to those who talk in circles.” Satisfied, he released her. Her body slid down the car’s hood and into a puddle of water on the hard ground. Quickly making his return back to the dry interior of his car and closing the door, he pulled out the envelope that he had placed in his pocket. Some spots were damp, but nothing more. He put the key in the ignition and started the car. “Let’s see what this is.” Ripping it open, he pulled out a letter from which he flapped open. “T’sao Liang. How it is nice to meet you. You wish to find answers for the questions that fill your head, well here’s your chance. I reside in London, England. You can find me in any phone book there. We have much to discuss, stranger. Your friend, V.V.” The joke inside him for all this grew thin indeed, but as he lowered the letter for him to get a last view on the woman’s body, he was shock of what he saw. She had disappeared. The car did as well. How was it that he did not hear the car backing up from all the puddles of water on the alley? Confuse overgrew as if it was a tumor. Complete pain retained. “London, England... hmmph. These ignorant drones try my patience.” He threw the letter on the passenger’s seat and drove away. ***** O, alluring child Whose florid lips so sweetly parted How the shepherd in my desire to guide the sheep in you From the mist and through the ashes she appeared overlooking the tranquil scene with glee. The plain field looked as if it was painted divinely as did many of the flowers that bloomed. It seemed as though this landscape was harboured from a dream, yet it felt all to real for him. He stood, longingly in the distance glancing at her from afar. Perhaps it was the natural feeling of reality that drove him into the realiation that this was the real world for he called her name. “Malika! Malika!” He shouted, shuddering from the wind that fluttered. Sadness overcame his heart and he at once preceeded to her direction, but it seemed the closer he believe he was getting, the further away she seemed to be; a neverending attempt to be close to his jeweled pearl in the ocean of gold. He ceased, for he knew failure was at hand. Falling to his knees, he writhed in agony as the figure of the beautiful seraphim had just noticed him from afar. -My future brightens with you- My future brightens with you My dreams are filled of you Dwell with me in this fairytale And i'll love you Till the end of time But she seemed not to care for she only passed through, smelling the flowers in bloom. “Oh foul thing is fate!” He uttered. His chinese accent was quite clear. The wind began to embroider his words through a road, paving there way towards her but stopped and silence once again grabbed the serene atmosphere. Sulking fancied him; though within a flash, she appeared before him. His jeweled pearl, whose eyes peered down at him. Now the past emotion had dried and repose quickly arrived. Still I breath As your voice melts my heart Making me yearn more for thee There were many who would take advantage over such a situation; where there dream woman now stood forth before them with a look of innocence in her eyes. Those youthful vigor and half formed thoughts for another mind was thinkins instead of the one that counted. Rising to his feet, he wondered when he would be forced to wake from this dream and bare witness to his solitude. But no alarm clock came, no chirping of birds arouse the end of slumber. This had to be real. -My future brightens with you- My future brightens with you My dreams are filled of you Dwell with me in this fairytale And i'll love you Till the end of time His soul almost escaped his mortal form as she took hold of his hand, and without words preceeded to lead him through the field and into the whispering forest nearby. Naturally of course, he did not strive for escape; he was enthralled. They stopped before a house in the woods. Rundown, yet it itched a memory in his mind. She released her grip as he moved forward trying to attain what memory had happened within this house. Breathless I stay for your love... 'Tell me you love me once more And I shall embrace you, Guard you from this world And forever love you' Nothing came. He wanted to ask her why she had brought him to such a place, but as he turned to face her, she had disappeared. The whispering winds blew over the trees where the silence gave him shivers. The enchanted shrills of the wind echoed and lo! A blood curdling scream rushed forth and pierced his heart. It came from the house. Time passes Passion dies, Still my love shall bloom for thee Everlasting Never dying, You shall always have me to adore you He dashed for the house, kicking the door down and followed to where the screams resound. There in a room at the otherside of the house laid a woman struggling, screaming and moaning. That's when he had noticed that it was her, his jeweled pearl. 'Malika!' He screamed but she did not hear. A shadow had entered the room. Her night gown slowly began to drag down her petite body as the shadow drew closer, closer, closer to the bed. He could not think of what to do but run to her side. The best days of this life are to come... My flower Breeder of desire, My halo Against solitude He tried to reach for her but his hand just slipped by her form like the wind. Tears ran down his cheeks as the pain inside her was becoming more and more unbearable. O sweet star! Whose tears freeze time How I need you with me During these joyous days Falling to his knees as the shadow now hang above the bed. It was at this time that everything stopped. Her struggles fell like whispers upon a ball. 'Tell me you love me one last time And I shall take you to our castle in the sky From this fool's paradise Where my love for you shall be everlasting' And lo! Life suddenly rushed before her where she wakes. She sat up screaming once again, then disappearing.... -My future brightens with you- My future brightens with you My dreams are filled of you Dwell with me in this fairytale And i'll love you Till the end of time... ***** He quickly sat up on his bed, screaming from the nightmare. Sweat had rolled down his face and the shivers that filled the fear within the dream followed into this reality. He took deep breaths and slowly calmed himself down. 'A shadow... the talk of mass disappearances. I believe i've just gone insane...' Truth be told, he wasn't far from insanity. Missing dead bodies just added fuel to the circle of confusement that filled his mind. And with it, he realized before slumber once again came that, it was only the beginning.
