Seeing Bubba

9 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/09/03 In through the front entrance of the hospital comes Brick. Like he has for the past month or so, he’s come to pay a visit to the still-comatose President of Global Wrestling, Bubba McCoy. Brick has had a very eventful week, or so, to say the least. It began at Wrestlecade, when Brick finally received vengeance against Salaok. It then spilled over into the following Evolution, where, as part of a tournament to crown a new World champion, he was to face his former ally Jesse Briggs. Brick went into the match with two objectives: the first and most important, to get ever closer to the World title What was to have been a match between him and Jesse Briggs turned into a two on one assault perpetrated by Quartz and Sage Sabbot. The assault was thwarted by the arrival of Tragic Hero. It then turned into a stalemate when Tragic Hero hesitated to attack Quartz. Then the idea sprang into Salaok’s twisted mind that Tragic Hero and Brick would face Quartz and Sage Sabbot in a tag team match. Twisted, because these men are all facing one another in the tournament. There is also the mysterious connection between Tragic Hero and Quartz. “Oh, hello. Are you here to visit Mr. McCoy?” asks a nurse at the front desk. “Yeah. Am I too late for visiting hours?” “Oh, no, you’re right on time.” “Has anyone else been here to visit?” “Just his son and daughter and Mr. Braxton.” “OK, so, other than Braxton, I’m the only person outside the family to come visit him today?” “Yes.” “Okay.” “You know where his room is?” “Yeah, I do.” Brick walks down towards the elevator. An orderly notices him. “Oh dude!!” “What’s up, man?” “Dude, I cannot believe this! You’re really here! Dude, you really made Wrestlecade for me. That entrance you made in the Navigator was sweet! And I especially liked how you kicked Salaok’s ass, dude! That rocked!” “Yeah, I was happy about it, too.” “So, here to see Bubba?” “Yeah, of course.” “Oh, okay. Can I get an autograph” The guys are never gonna believe me unless I got proof I saw you.” “Yeah, sure.” Brick pulls out a pen and paper. “So, who do I make it out to?” “Todd, longtime employee of Ruckus Inc.” “Longtime employee of Ruckus Inc. That’s good.” “Say, dude, can I ask a personal question?” “About what?” “About Gunner Addison.” “Look, man. Gunner Addison and I are just friends. We might have been more at one point, but it never happened.” “Aw c’mon!” “No, seriously.” “She was the hottest chick you guys had there, and you didn’t hit that?” “What, are you Howard Stern, now” No, I didn’t.” “Sorry, dude, but inquiring minds wanna know.” “Well, there’s your answer. She and I are and were just friends. There you go.” “Oh dude, thanks,” the orderly gushes as he exits the elevator. Brick goes the other way down the hallway until he reaches the room he wants. There he sees the same sight he’s seen for a month or so: Bubba McCoy, one of the toughest men he’s ever known, unconscious and hooked up to a respirator, clinging on to life. 'Hey, Bubba. Just came to see how you were doing. Thought I’d tell you about Wrestlecade. Wrestlecade went good for me. Damn good. Oh, you would have liked it. I beat Salaok’s ass like you wouldn’t believe. His ass, Kullypso’s ass and Quartz? ass. Knocking me down is one thing. Keeping me down is a damn different matter, entirely. You’d think Salaok and his boys would have learned that by now, but no. Salaok still wants to push me. Well, that’s fine. But he ain’t gonna like how I push back. He thinks he’s got the market on being a sick son of a bitch? Oh, no. He keeps it up, and he’ll see otherwise. Apparently, he forgot what happened to his boy Draven. If I have to remind him, I will. Though, that didn’t stop them from trying to get me on Evolution. Especially after Briggs bailed. Probably knew I’d be coming after him for what he did to Gunner. I went to see her, by the way. She’s doing good. She’s tough. ' Brick pauses for a moment. “Anyway, Salaok booked me in a tag match with Tragic Hero against Quartz and Sage Sabbot. I don’t think Salaok gets it yet. His boys are like bowling pins and I’m a bowling ball. He sets them up, I knock them down. Overload won’t be any different. I think he’s starting to slip. I really do. I know he’s scared of me. Now it’s more obvious. Wrestlecade made it that much clearer, I think. I’m not afraid of him and that scares him. Although, I don’t really know about Welch. This business between him and Quartz has me worried. I don’t know if I can trust him. I mean, me, Ely and Logan trusted Briggs and look what happened there. Although, Welch is the only cat in the fed who hates Salaok as much as I do. Probably even more, so we have that in common. I hope the whole “enemy of my enemy” thing holds. But I may just have to face him in the tournament anyway, so? Anyway, I got a match to prepare for, so I’m gonna get outta your way. Oh, and I plan to beat Briggs? ass, too, just so you’d like to know.” Brick then takes his leave to prepare for his match.
