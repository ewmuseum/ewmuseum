The Rise And Fall Of Superman

6 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/06/05 .:STATIC:. 'Are you tired of girls on the beach willing to bare it al...' .:STATIC:. 'Tonight on the show we have Jude Law everybo...' .:STATIC:. 'Another 6 dead tonight in Iraq after a...' .:STATIC:. Another dark intermission is brought to life by an ignition of light. With the light brought a brilliant display of color, which danced off the light colored walls. The lights shifted and changed intensity as if pulsating and having it's own life. Perhaps that's why the entity known as the television has single handedly changed the life and culture of the world. The picture danced in the cadet blue eyes of Christian Zenith, who sat at the end of a hotel bedroom somewhere in Detroit, Michigan. In disappointment, he lifts his arm once again and hits down the button, hoping for better luck this time... .:STATIC:. 'Yes, we're all looking forward to the International Championship match... but now we go onto the MAIN EVENT. A match between The Elite's Ken Partridge Jr. and relatively new up and coming star, Christian Zenith.' A balding, stubby looking man said sitting in what appeared to be a lawn chair. Christian kept the station on the local public access channel, with intrigue... 'Don't forget Dave, it's a HELL IN A HANDBASKET match. For those of you at home who might not be familiar with the concept, a Hell In A Handbasket is a 20-foot steel cell structure mounted 30 feet above the ring. The only way to win the match I believe, is to get your opponent out of the cell and knock them 30 feet to the floor below.' A younger man said while looking over at the balding man, who is apparently known as Dave. 'That's right Sean, and if that wasn't enough add in a Weapon of Choice stipulation! This is a combination that will definitely see career shortening, if not career ending results. Now down to the physics of the match, who do you think has the advantage?' Dave asks of his nerdish counterpart. 'Christian Zenith sets at 6'5' 262 pounds... which gives him a distinct height and weight advantage over the smaller champion. Although Zenith has a strength advantage, Partridge has a speed advantage as well as more experience in big matches. Big match experience is usually the most underrated asset in sports, and with this Partridge should come out the victor in a heated battle.' Sean states confidently. 'I can't disagree with you there! Ken Partridge Jr. is on FIRE! Let's just say he didn't get casted to play Superman for nothing...' Suddenly glass shatters and behind it a spark ignites blinding Zenith temporarily, who covers his eyes in response. Christian takes his hands away from his eyes slowly to reveal the mere shell of the television that once sat before him. Smoke rose slowly from the smoldering cube of plastic and shattered glass, as a radiant display of sparks still shot periodically out of the plastic. Christian Zenith looked over his left shoulder, where Vivica J. Valentine sat up on the bed wearing a red tube top and a pair of red panties ready to go to sleep. In her hand was a dagger that she was aiming at the television with a smile. Suddenly she flung the dagger towards the TV, where it stuck into the plastic on the side... before it slowly broke into two pieces and fell over. Vivica smiled and started singing... 'Tell my whyyyy yeah, Superman's Dead...' Vivica started to sing before she started laughing. Her attempts to get a smile out of Christian always seemed to fall flat on their face, but at least she had a good time trying. 'Everyone is talking about this match like it's some kind of big deal, like falling 50 feet will be the worst thing that's ever happened to me. Compared to the rest of my life, anything Ken Partridge Jr. can possibly do to me is child's play.' Christian says to Vivica who listening intently, while thinking about going over and picking up her two daggers from the entertainment center. 'It's hotshot bad guy Christian Zenith against 'The Franchise', he's their 'Superman' if you beat their hero you'll ruin the days and the lives of Partridgemania. Children will cry and grown men all over the world will fall to their knees and weep like abused mothers. That's what happened in the comics anyway...' Vivica J. Valentine said before sliding into a laying position on the bed, with her hands behind her head. 'Maybe that's what the problem is with all these ignorant people who turn GW into their personal lives. They need to get their heads out of their televisions, comics, and asses to see the truth. There are much bigger things in the world than Ken Partridge Jr. losing the World Title. Even Superman has his kryptonite... and just like every other man I'll find his weakness. I'll bring him to his knees and make The Man Of Steel look like Lois Lane. It's unfortunate to the world that Kenny is going to lose, the only unknowns in the match is how bad I'm going to make him cry.' Christian said as he got up and walked into the bathroom to take his shower before bed. Tomorrow night was the big night... and he wanted to looks his best when his traumatized image is imbedded into the geek population of the world. ---- This place is a fuckin bomb For a few seconds this place was Armageddon IT WAS A FIREFIGHT! ---- Vivica J. Valentine laid alone in the darkness in her tube top and red panties, staring up at the ceiling. All this talk of blubbering like children made her think back to the last time she cried... it almost 3 years ago, but she could remember it like it was yesterday. It was her introduction into 'The Real World' after all... it was the day she learned that there was no Superman. Date: October 19, 2002 Time: 23:00 Central Time Location: Chicago, Illinois A chill wind passed over the twilight, a series of clouds temporarily covered the moon as if they were cars going by a toll both in the sky. Underneath the stars, the blackened streets of Chicago were covered with the oranges, yellows, and reds of dying leaves. All it took was one shrill wind to bring the leaves to their uneventful death... so ironic when you think about the nurturing it takes to bring a tree to bloom. A car passes by throwing all the dead leaves high into the air, where the wind catches them and they swirl around in a cyclone in the middle of the street. As the wind dies down the leaves fall back down to their eventually demise on the sidewalk in front of an old pawn shop. THUMP, THUMP THUMP, THUMP THUMP, THUMP Suddenly the leaves were crushed under the march of combat boots going down the sidewalk. The group of three known as 'The Cru' continued their usual match down the street... making sure no one was up to something seemed to be a full-time job these days. There was always some sleeve bag who thought he owned the place... it was tough to keep the peace when someone always wanted something they didn't have. The leader of the group stopped underneath a white street light, giving him an unusual angelic property. He had shaggy blonde hair and wore a black Adidas jacket over a white 'wife-beater' tank top. A pair of black jeans completed the look as he surveyed his surroundings... making sure no one was lurking in the shadows ready to pounce. 'Let's go, there's no one here.' He said to the girl and the large African-American man behind him. The girl wore a light pink zip up sweat jacket and a pair of blue jeans, her blonde hair over her brilliant blue eyes as she looked at the sidewalk as they moved along. Her hands stuffed deep into the pink pockets of her jacket. The style of life was starting to wear her down... sometimes she wanted so bad to take the first bus back to Highland Park, and leave everything behind. But she couldn't... she couldn't leave Jimmy hanging like that. After all she was homeless and alone until he took her in, the least she could do to repay him was keep him company. Plus she couldn't deny the fact she felt safe around Jimmy and Darius. 'Is everything okay Vivica?' James 'Jimmy' Carlisle said as he stopped and looked at the young teenage girl. She looked up at him and managed to muster a smile. 'Just a lot of thinking... it's nothing, just sometimes I miss...' Vivica was interrupted by a loud explosion. Suddenly Darius fall limply forward to the ground... 'What the fuck?!' Jimmy screamed as blood trickled out of Darius' body on the Chicago pavement. Vivica's eyes grew wide in horror as she stared down at Darius. He body tensed up, she couldn't move as her heart raced. 'RUN VIVICA!' Jimmy screamed at the top of his lungs before he pulled out a gun from his jacket and fired three shots into the air, trying to scare off any rival gang members or snipers who might've done this. But Vivica was in a state of panic, the only thing she could hear was her own heartbeat pumping loudly in her ears. BUM BUM, BUM BUM BUM BUM, BUM BUM BUM BUM, BUM BUM Suddenly the pumping of her racing heart was interrupted by another explosion in the darkness. She could see the bullet whizzing towards her in slow-motion as if she was stuck in the matrix. Her life started to flash before her eyes as the bullet grew near. Her eyes looked like a deer stuck in headlights before suddenly James Carlisle jumps in front of her, and the bullet strikes him in the heart. Jimmy lands next to Darius on the ground clinching his chest and coughing up blood... 'JIMMY!!!!!' Vivica shrieked in horror at the sight of James Carlisle’s eyes rolling back into his head. Once again in a panic tears started streaming down her face like a waterfall. '...leave...' was the last word Jimmy could manage to get out before his body grew limp on the ground. Suddenly Vivica took off in a mad sprint, tears raced down her face clouding her vision but it didn't matter. She'd never ran so fast before in her entire life. Another shot was fired and Vivica squealed in panic... BUM BUM, BUM BUM, BUM BUM, BUM BUM BUM BUM, BUM BUM, BUM BUM, BUM BUM BUM BUM, BUM BUM, BUM BUM, BUM BUM The bullet stuck the building behind Vivica and lodged itself into the brick and mortar. Shrapnel shot out at Valentine as she sprinted around the corner. She raced blindly into the darkness of the alley, through her blurry vision she could only see one thing... a fire escape leading up the side of a hotel. She wiped her eyes before taking a leap of faith into the air towards the wall of the hotel, and the thrusted off towards the fire escape. She barely caught the bar with her left hand hung there, trying to catch her thoughts. Hyperventilating and crying hysterically, it took her last burst of strength to climb the ladder and lay on the fire escape on her back, looking up at the stars wishing it would all go away... BUM BUM, BUM BUM BUM BUM, BUM BUM BUM BUM, BUM BUM Her heart still raced thinking about it as she was on her back staring at the ceiling back in her hotel room. That was when she realized the frailty of human life... that just like the leaves in the fall, could all be taken away with one shrill swoop. She sat up and looked around the room, realizing Christian was in the bathroom. She was tough but it was times like this where she still felt more safe with Christian, Demitri, and Jason. Vivica got up off the bed and walked over to the bathroom door. She put her ear up to the door to try and hear what Christian was doing, but heard nothing coming from inside. A bit worried, Vivica knocked lightly on the door. The door creaked open, and startled Vivica... ---- You are the fucking disease Another day, another memory But I have fucking failed I turned another lie into the work of a saint So where is the cure? Hundreds of souls With the look of horror on their face Now i rise from my knees I will not live in misery You won't take me You won't take me Now it's me Now it's me ---- A startled Vivica J. Valentine peers into the bathroom, where Christian Zenith stands with his back to the mirror. He looks over his right shoulder into the mirror at his gunshot scar. If Ken Partridge Jr. thinks losing Ice is tough, he should try getting shot by him once... Date: January 1, 2004 Time: 22:37 Central Time Location: Chicago, Illinois Click Click Click Click Click Click The sound of footsteps on marble resonate through the dark corridors. Clicking grows closer and closer as a man steps forth into the light breaking through the door. Half a man is revealed in the bright light, wearing a 3 piece custom black suit. He reached forward and pushed open the glass door, labeled with a gold Z emblem inside a circle over the words ZENITH CORPORATION. His cadet blue eyes seemed to glow under the super bright porch light on the front of the building, as he pulled a shiny ring full of keys out of his right jacket pocket. He pulled back on the door and jammed the key into the socket and turning it, locking the door in place before sliding down the metal sheath and padlocking it to the ground. Christian Zenith turned around in what appeared to be a Winter Wonderland on New Year's Day. The snowflakes danced around the 20 year old billionaire as he waltzed down the street towards his black Chrysler Crossfire. Christian looked uncharacteristically angelic as he walked through the series of street lamps underneath the flakes of white raining down from the heavens. He brushed the snow out of his hair, letting out a sigh which floated away as a steam into the twilight above. Everything looked so peaceful and perfect through the crosshairs of a sniper rifle, focused on the back of Christian's head waiting for him to stop moving in order to get a nice clean kill. The crosshairs moved with him as he plunged his key into the door of his black car. When he opened the car door a zip echoed from high above the streets of Chicago. The bullet swerved gracefully through the falling snow moving towards Christian Zenith at an unfathomable velocity. At the last second Zenith plunged into his pocket to pull out his ringing cell phone, and the bullet lodged itself into his right shoulder blade. With a grunt Christian Zenith fell to the ground, and his cell phone went skidding across the street. A moan escaped the lips of Christian Zenith as he reached out for his cell phone, laying in the cold Chicago streets. The blood began to seep through his dress shirt and his vest, his right arm was paralyzed. Christian screamed out in pain as he grew closer and closer to the phone, his life surely depended on it. With a last burst of strength Christian used his left arm to push himself up to his knees, where he crawled like a toddler towards the phone. Zenith lunged forward and picked up the lifeline before speaking two words... 'Vivica... help...' and collapsed forward grasping his right shoulder in pain. Click Click Click Click Click Click The sound of high heels on pavement grew closer and closer to Christian while the adrenaline pumped through the veins of his dying body. Christian's sight grew blurry as he stared into the wheels of his car unable to move due to the blood loss. 'Why won't you just die?' A familiar woman's voice said from behind Christian. As she grew closer, she saw the bloody train through the white snow leading halfway across the street, where her victim limply laid... helpless to the world. As she approached, she wondered about the face of her adversary... it wasn't often she got to see the last moments of her target, she quite enjoyed their agony. She approached the suited man and kicked him in the kidney, looking down at the large amounts of blood coming out from the back of his right shoulder flowing into the snow like a red river. She turned the body over with her foot, and her eyes grew wide in surprise. 'NO!' She shrieked through the darkness with the murder weapon clipped firmly to her back. The cadet blue eyes of Christian Zenith stared up at his older sister Tera while she looked, frozen in horror of what she'd done. 'Tera...' Christian Zenith managed to squeeze out his lips, '...you haven’t seen the last of me... how could you...' with those words the eyes of Tera Zenith, also known as Die Erntemaschine narrowed and she bit her bottom lip surveying the darkness. The sound of a vehicle approached and Tera raced into the darkness, hoping to leave her past and everything behind her in the dying body of her little brother. The car turned the corner and the headlights shone on the limp body of Christian Zenith lying in the middle of the street. The car came to a sudden halt as Vivica J. Valentine and Jason LeMaster can sprinting to their fallen friend's side. They lifted Christian to an upright position and Jason took off his shirt to cover his blood-soaked shoulder. With his last minute of consciousness, Zenith looked up at Vivica J. Valentine and said: 'Tera will die for this...' ---- All that's left is a bitter taste Of a life that was once so promising Rather cut at the wrist Than laugh about your mistakes Sickness fills the air Another life that you wish you could fake Your eyes will cut Through me But it's a risk That i must fucking take I must take So where is the cure? Hundreds of souls With the look of horror on their face Now i rise from my knees I will not live in misery ---- Christian rubs his right shoulder and rolls it around before locking eyes back with Vivica J. Valentine still peering through the door... 'Something wrong?' Vivica asked of Christian who stood shirtless in the middle of the sterile white hotel bathroom. A small chuckle escaped the lips of Christian Zenith as he turned around and faced Vivica. 'Just thinking if Ken Partridge Jr. thinks losing a friend is the end of the world, he should take a look at the world crumbling around him. Ice left him because he got tired of covering his ass, Josh jumped ship to core becasue he got tired of covering his ass... it's only a matter of time till he faces the facts that he's a failure. First his friends, then his world title, then he's going to be drinking through a tube in the hospital... we'll see where his friends are then.' Christian said coldly, 'The world isn't a nice friendly place where you can trust people and they'll sit loyally by your side forever. If you hold personal vendettas you best end them fast... because if you spend too much time hating Lex Luthor, Doomsday is going to kill your ass.' With a smile Vivica J. Valentine closes the bathroom door and goes to lay back down on the bed. Christian Zenith looks into the mirror with a cold stare, before a creepy uncharacteristic smile crosses his face... 'I got you right where I want you Kenny... you could NEVER destroy me. Welcome to your doomsday...
