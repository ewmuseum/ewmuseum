Yet another target practice session

10 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/10/04 It was a common practice for Brick. Whenever he had a big match coming up or somebody in Global Wrestling had made the unfortunate mistake of pissing him off, he would drive out to an empty field with an Army duffel bag full of firearms and cardboard cut-outs of the superstar or superstars he was facing or had angered him. The last time he was out in the field was when he was feuding with the Harvesters. Up until recent events, only two men had angered Brick to this point: Deacon Holmes and Michael “Terminator” Page. In recent weeks, one individual has done very little and angered Brick greatly. That individual is Mark Makonie. Brick has had issues with Makonie’s definition of “perfection”, a definition that could be espoused by men like Hitler and Tom Metzger. An off-handed racist comment he heard uttered by Makonie caused a brawl on the 1-26-04 edition of Evolution which resulted in Brick tossing Makonie out a second-floor window onto a car. The next shot fired came after Brick’s match with Terminator at Global Reload. From a bar, he broadcast a message to Brick, which was peppered with racist insults. Needless to say, Brick wasn’t too happy. Since there weren’t yet any cardboard mock-ups of Makonie given his relative newness, Brick had to substitute mock-ups of Hitler, Metzger, David Duke, Byron De La Beckwith (the man who assassinated Medgar Evers) and other well-known racists. He had already emptied out a revolver, a 9-mm Glock and a shotgun. In the distance, he could hear another car pull up and see the glow of headlights. He turned to look behind him and saw a pair of headlights. “Brick?” a voice called out. “Aaron?” Brick replied. Aaron walked right up to Brick’s left side. “Makonie really pissed you off, didn’t he?” “To say the least.” “Man, I feel you. I remember hearing every racist word that came out of that bastard’s mouth that night Global Reload was on. Wow, you really went all out.” “Yeah, I figured, if I could shoot something that reminded me of Makonie, I’d feel better.” “Frankly, I wouldn’t blame you if you actually shot Makonie.” A smile then crept across Brick’s face. “The thought is tempting.” “In fact, let me get one of your guns.” “You sure Felicia would approve?” “Approve of me letting off some steam” Yes, she would.” Aaron then reached into Brick’s duffel bag and pulled out a revolver. He then took aim at the mock-up of Beckwith. “Whoa, hold up a minute,” said Brick, as he rushed over to the Beckwith mock-up. He then lifts it up and turns it around with the back facing Aaron. “There, do him like he did Medgar.” Aaron then fired a shot and hit the mockup right in the back. “There you go!” Brick shouted. “So, I saw Evolution. I still can’t believe that the cops had to come in.” “I don’t think I had police coming in when I was feuding with Terminator.” Brick then took a shot at a mock-up of Strom Thurmond. “You and Makonie hate each other that much.” “Makonie hates Black people that much.” “So Christina just bailed you out” She didn’t bail Makonie out?” “Exactly.” “Well, not like I feel sorry for him or anything?” “I’m telling you, Aaron. This cat Makonie?” Then words weren’t necessary. All that brick had to say, he said by spraying the remaining mock-ups with gunfire. Aaron took cover and covered his ears. He had only seen Brick this angry once. That was when he was dealing with the Nevermore. After the firing was done and the smoke cleared, Brick stood, heaving, his gun empty. “Damn, man,” said Aaron, “don’t hold back. Tell me how you really feel.”
