Teaming Up

18 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/18/04 It's a sunny Sunday afternoon in Dallas, Texas as the masked Juan Gonzalez stands outside with his promoter, Goddfrey James. They both are outside what appears to be a lowrider shop. 'I'm telling you Pedro, giving you this lowrider is going to make you a star here in Global Wrestling!' 'El Mariachi' only shakes his head. Just then Goddfrey answers his cell phone. After a few minutes of 'uh-huhing' and several 'yes sirs' he turns to Juan. 'I have some good news and some bad news Pedro...the good news is that Global Wrestling has cleared up some stuff and you're now allowed to talk from now on.' 'Orarle! Yeah, that's what I'm talking about bro! So what's the bad news?' 'You're teaming up with Takahashi Jetsui, to take on Mike Page and Elly Braxton at Overload and it seems that Global Wrestling is keeping you off the pay-per view too.' 'Atleast I can talk now! I was getting tired of having to look like the stupid Mexican, I'll leave that job to Juvian Ramirez.' 'So the match doesn't bother you?' 'Oh bro, I hate having a tag partner, but as long as Jetsui stays out of my way, nothing will matter. I have one thing on my agenda now that I can speak...' Juan Gonzalez grabs Goddfrey James by the belt buckle. 'Whoa, what are you doing Pedro...ho...hold on!' 'El Mariachi' takes Goddfrey and shoves him down and then into a car window. 'Consider yourself fired James! I'm glad that them idiots over at Global Wrestling finally are letting me speak. It was getting to become a pain having that cracka speak for me.' Juan reaches out behind the car seat and pulls out a baseball bat, one of the attendants sees him smashing the windows of several lowriders in the lot. 'Whoa! What are you doing?' 'Getting rid of that stupid Mexican stereotype! I'm here to show all of the crackas in Global Wrestling what a TRUE MEXICAN is!' Gonzalez drops the bat and looks right at the camera. 'Take a good look Mike Page and Elly Braxton, this wasn't a smash mouth cut, but you get the point. 'El Mariachi' is a new man, and I'm a lone man! I don't need Jetsui to beat you, and I sure aint counting on him to beat you. This is my time to shine. I'm gonna make a name for myself, at your expense!' 'El Mariachi' picks up the bat and continues going to the low riders again as the camera fades out.
