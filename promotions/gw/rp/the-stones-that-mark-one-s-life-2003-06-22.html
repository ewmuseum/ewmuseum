The stones that mark one's life

22 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22/6/03 The name of those fallen before him now rest on the stones which stand upright from the ground as an eternal reminded that a man's spirit, will be as hard as rock forever. Like a rock, a man's spirit may break at times, but will still remain as strong as before, just now divided into smaller fragments. Each one with it's own wants and needs. It's own ways of dealing with things. Looking at the stone in front of him with his father's name on it, he lowers his head as a sign of respect before stretching his arms outwards in the sign of The Crucifix which had always been the symbol of The School of Pain in the early days. A school that was never meant to become what it has become in these days, but what something was meant to be in one's mind, will never be. There are always those who will stand in the way of that dream. Always those who will try and change the dream that they have no right in trying to do so. Each man's dream is something that he and only he can possess. Salaok's dreams? That is a question that no one really needs to know about. Still in his twenties but with the life of a Forty year old business man who had spent years in the industry in which they had so chosen. He lowers himself in the early morning sun to one knee and keeps his eyes down at the ground in front of him. It was a mark of respect, or was it something more? Could it possibly be the guilt which one would have in knowing that the only reason you became the man you are today is because your father had been murdered? It was nothing like that. Sure that had been the original feeling once Johnathon had pointed out to Salaok why he had ordered Quartz to bring death to the Davis door that night. Lungs expanding as the cool air is sucked into his lungs and then in a loud sigh, his lungs push the air out and his shoulders drop. The cloud of mist which flows from his mouth doesn't stay in the air for long as the warm air from his body quickly becomes stone cold. Slowly and almost cold of emotion, his head lifts to make eye contact with the headstone. His father's name curved around the photo of the deceased man. And what a man he was. Or at least in the eyes of Salaok he was te man of men. Strong and well educated with the knowledge that the brain would always be stronger than the muscle that you develop. As a child Salaok could never understand that. Never understand that his father was trying to teach him a life lesson that would one day control Salaok's life. No matter how big, how strong you are, it would be he who was thinking a head of time that would walk away with the victory. Salaok's right hand forms a ball and he kisses his knuckles where the word 'PAIN' is tattooed. his eyes close in thought for a mere second but a second long enough for the memories of the past to fill his mind and none more focused than that of walking into his father's room and seeing him laying there with his eyes closed. He was asleep but the young Salaok just had to tell his father about the movie that his mother had taken him and Eve to that night. He shook his father, gently at first but the longer that his father didn't wake, the rougher the shaking became. And once the shaking stopped, the screams of internal pain began to disturb the night air with panic and pain. He opens his eyes and looks directly at the photo on the headstone again before taking his fist and pushing it against it softly. The man who had helped to make Salaok the man he is today, had to die so that the transformation could be completely. But it didn't mean that Salaok didn't feel it. Did it? His face remains like the stone in front of him, cold and dry. Not a single tear even ten years since the day it happened. Ten years in a long time and the feelings numb as time goes on, but the memory remain. 'Father.' The trees rustle with the slight breeze giving life to the grounds which until this word had been silent all morning long. 'I've changed since I last came to see you sir. I'm more of a business man now. He can't help but shake his head at the realization that there was nothing that his father hated more than business men with their reports and the profit and loss statements. It was a life that his father just didn't care for. As long as he was teaching his students, he was happy no matter what the banks were saying. 'I own half of Global Incorporated. Not that you would careless really. It wasn't your thing the business life was it? And you know what sir? I couldn't give a fuck what you think. What you thought. What others think.' His voice changes so quickly without warning and harsh words begin to fly with a tone which is as cold as ice. His eye orbed eyes stare blankly now at the headstone as he stands from his kneeling position. 'I'm my own fucking man now father and I have been for a while. That's why you had to die and I know that now. People expect me to be sorry about your death but why should I? Look at what I have gained from your execution? I have money, power and control over so much.' A voice from above calls down to him but it's not the voice of his father, but more of the voice of his father's business partner in The School of Pain.'But do you have love?' The voice surprises Salaok causing him to spin around on the back of his right heel and look up at the tree. There he sits with his legs hanging limp from a branch high up in the tree. Pit, as he was known simply hadn't changed at all. Looking more like some sort of cross bread of monkey and man due to his beard and hairy arms, he sits there just looking at Salaok and shaking his head before speaking again. 'Well, do you have love?' 'Although it's none of your damn business, yes, I do have love. My wife and my son love me, how about you Pit. How is it being a isolated freak? Much love where you come from?' Pit remains silent in the tree with the look of a wild animal in his eyes and letting out a small growl to let his feelings known. But he speaks again from his perched position, well out of reach from Salaok. 'I knew you would come here today child. You act like you are an emotionless monster but I know you Salaok. I know what you are all about and I know exactly what you are feeling right now boy. You can deny it, but deep inside you know that feeling of pain haunts you.' 'What is it you want Pit? You want revenge for what has happened to my father? you want the school back? You want me to turn my back on everything I have become?' Not a word is spoken from the man known as Pit, but a simple nod answers it all. Ever since his best friend, Salaok's father had been killed, he had vowed revenge on those who had anything to do with it. But until now, he had never made a move. 'I will always want that Salaok but I can't have what I want all of the time young Davis. Most of that can only come from your heart and your heart is as black as mine once was. But one day you will see the error of your ways and you will return to me. However, I will have vengeance on those who had a hand in your father's death.' 'You can try old man, but you stand no threat to neither Johnathon, Quartz or any of The Harvester's for that matter.' Grinning as if he knows something that Salaok doesn't, he just sits there looking at Salaok, knowing full well that is one thing Salaok can't stand. Even as a child, if he felt he was being left out of a conversation, he had to be filled in completely in order to know everything that was happening around him. 'Why is it you smile on such a sad day old man? Why is it you look at me from such height and smile down on me.' 'Because your father wouldn't be. Your father wouldn't be turning in his grave in which you stand right above. Can you feel him child? Feel his bones rattling as they turn in fits like he's having a bad dream. Guess what young one, this isn't a dream. And I do smile down on you because I know of things gone by already that have helped to shape you into the beast you have become. And foolishly, I have played a major part in that.' The white eyes pierce holes straight through the old man from Salaok who's face remains cold and emotionless. Knowing that Pit knew something was eating him but couldn't show it. Couldn't give the beast the joy of knowing that he was getting to Salaok. 'And pre-tell old man of these events which you have played such a big part of in the life of he you now speak to?' 'I fear what the answer to your question will do to you child. But know this. There are many things in this world and this life that you know not of. But let me just say this. That castle in which you have taken over and made into one of your many homes, didn't belong to Mephiston like you once thought. He wasn't the one giving the orders in the termination of The Lair one by one.' Finally emotion shows on the face of Salaok who's voice turns into growls as he barks at the man in the tree. 'Who was giving the orders old man.' Pit's answer is sharp and to the point without revealing anything in which he doesn't have to. 'The two men who started your journey.' Jumping up trying to grab a hold of one of Pit's dangling legs, Salaok begins to loose control. Time after time he tries but Pit just sits there without flinching. 'I have no time for your riddles old man.' 'Stop your jumping Salaok. I know exactly how high you can jump. I have been watching you long enough to know exactly what you are capable of. And jumping high enough to grab a hold of one of my legs simply isn't within your abilities. So stop wasting the energy that you could use more wisely.' Stopping his jumping, he feels that Pit once again knows more than what he is saying with that hint. Pushing the hair out of his face with his left hand in one sweeping motion, he questions the man in the tree. 'And what would that be?' 'Apologising to your father for the ill words you spoke to him before I drew your attention to my location. Your father was a good man Salaok and he doesn't deserve what has happened to him. You have benefited from the death of an innocent man.' Saying sorry to his father was something that just wasn't going to happen no matter who asked. Sorry was a word that was rarely used in Salaok's train of speech. His father had betrayed his father with his last creation which would find the name of Mani Shigeru. Even the mere thought of his father cheating on his mother in such a way drove Salaok to look down at the earth in which his father was six feet below and scream at the top of his lungs at. 'You want me to say sorry father? Yeah, I'm sorry your cheating ass was never brought out into the public eye for all to see. Bring out your ways of betrayal so that those who held you in such high esteem could see the fraud in which you turned out to be. You want me to be sorry for benefiting from your death father? Sorry father but, fuck you. If you weren't my father and I hated you as much as I do right now, I would have dug you up, just to shoot you between the eyes to make sure the point was known. But whether or not I like it, you are my father and respect must then follow. But it is a respect that treads a thin line father. A father that loves his family would have no need to find extra loving from some stupid Asian whore.' 'Mind your mouth child.' Salaok goes to look up at Pit with the hatred which he had just unleashed on the ground but he is gone. Disappeared into the nothingness in which he would magically appear again at some time down the line. He always did that. But now the thoughts of the past were beginning to flood Salaok mind as the questions in which Pit had answered had done nothing more than create more questions which needed answers. but at least now these were answers in which if looked into deep enough, could be found by Salaok himself. The two men who began his journey are the same two men who control the execution of The Lair members over a year ago causing Salaok and Johnathon to take the group's lead and began to rebuild. He stops and tries to calm himself down before finally turning back to the headstone again, lowering his head and stretching his arms out in the crucifix pose. His voice low and rough as nails.'See you again in a year father.'
