On top of the World.

1 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 1st, 2004. The World seemed to spin around me as I watched it flash by…of course for the first time in my life I could say I was on top of the World and things seemed to flow just a little faster. To be at the top of the World is simply amazing…a view that's unmatched by any other. I had suffered a thousand indignities at the hands of my enemies and countless injuries as well, yet despite all the times I had been knocked down I had only proceeded to stand up a little higher every time. After three years of Wrestling and a year of training…I had finally done the unthinkable. The skinny punk kid from a small Nebraska town had became pound for pound the World's greatest Wrestler…the World's greatest champion. There were of course other talented men that falsely professed themselves to be the greatest as their slavery to ego gratification forced them to carry on these foolish charades. I of course had been called egotistical as well for my claims as the greatest yet unlike the rest I could highlight things I had done that the other so-called great ones had failed to do. For example not only did I hold the highest title in the land of Global Wrestling but it seemed inevitable that merely days after my eye opening win I would also escape as the victor in a six way ladder match with the PTC Global title hanging in the balance. The Global Wrestling World Champion, the next successor to the PTC Global title and the new impact player on all the major charts I had indeed became a Wrestler to be respected. So it came as no surprise that I would of course celebrate with a few of my closest friends…or truthfully the only close friend I have that I still speak too…enter Ken Partridge Jr. For most World Champion's and world renowned celebrities their choice of restaurants would have been a little better than mine…it's not that I dislike exquisite place but rather the judgmental snobs that frequented such places. That is of course the reason I chose the place for my victory dinner, Papa John's. Jacking a quarter into the jukebox I pressed song number two hundred and fifty eight which was listed as 'Spin' by the great band, Lifehouse. As the strains of an acoustic guitar kicked in I happily dropped back into the booth I shared with Ken who sit across from me. Sometimes I find the differences strikingly odd between Ken and I. He sat there adorned in black Versace slacks a silver tunic of the same designer and a pair of black Chippewa boots with his baby blue eyes shining and his hair mussily spiked. I on the other hand employed a look that contrasted greatly with Ken's. Whereas he sat there in nice wrinkle free clothes I wore a pair of wrinkled Olive colored cargo pants with a cracked leather belt whipped through the loops, a tan 'Billabong' T-shirt with the sleeves ripped off and brown flat toed European leather shoes. His hair had obviously been structured the way it was while mine on the other hand shot about in messy black locks that stood up in places and were mashed flat in others. My bluish gray eyes however bore a rare look in them…a look of weariness that was all too common place but also a look of joy which seemed to be quite foreign. I smiled contently as I reached down for the large Hawaiian pizza in front of me and procured a slice of the delicious meal. Good music, good friends and good food…this seemed to be a moment of perfect bliss and I felt I must inform Ken of that. 'My friend…this is the life.' I said before sinking my teeth into the steaming hot pizza. 'The life?' He said with an amused chuckle as he snared his own slice of pizza. 'That's right…there's a great song playing, I'm spending time with someone who is a brother to me and there's great food. I said in between bites. 'Josh, you sure don't ask for much do you?' Said Ken as he grinned broadly at me. 'Well, maybe one of us is happier than the other, because he just won a World title?' I said winking playfully which immediately caused him to burst into laughter. 'Touch?.' He said with a playful salute added to it. My mouth closed once more as I began to chew my food yet it was still hard for me to wipe the broad smile of victory from my face. 'Speaking of World titles, have you talked to Ice?' I asked as I swallowed my food. ' No…he hasn't returned any of my calls.' Ken said twisting his lips into a look of confusion and disappointment. 'Nor mine.' I said with a sigh before adding, 'He's sure to turn up sooner or later though, it's unlike him to take a step back.' 'That it is.' Ken admitted only to continue on, 'But now that you've tasted your first World title, how would you feel after losing it? To one of your best friends nonetheless?' 'You do have a point, my friend.' I said in reluctant admittance praying that Ice wouldn't continue to ignore his longtime friends. 'But the important thing is that we stick together.' Ken said, knowing full well how much influence his words and actions had over me. 'Ice is gone…but even without him we're as strong as ever.' I said as I pondered where the former Champion was at this very moment. Ken nodded in response opting not to speak with a mouth full of food. But after swallowing he quickly spoke up. 'Don't concern yourself too much with Ice, you've got more important things to worry about.' 'Such as?' I asked rather intrigued by Ken's comment. 'Such as Brick, he's a former World Champion and a GW legend, damn it.' Ken said in an inspirational tone. 'Brick?' I asked with a sneer of arrogance. 'Yes Brick. You may be one of this industries greatest… Ken started before I interrupted him. 'I'm not one of the greatest…I'm the greatest, Ken.' I said with a playful grin dancing across my face. He dismissed my comment with a laugh before speaking again. 'As I was saying…you need to be focused solely on this match. You've always been the guy who gave one hundred and eleven percent out there…well you had better do just that if you plan on retaining that coveted World title. Ken said getting straight to the root of things. 'Believe me Ken…after all of the effort, after all of the hard work I put into winning this there is no way I'm going to lose it now my friend.' I forcefully stated. Yet the second I said that I felt a searing pain rip through my body…from my spine to the top of my head. A pain unlike I had ever felt before and as I closed my eyes something began to happen. The half-devoured slice of pizza fell from my hand as my body broke into a cold sweat. My minds eye flashed forward…there was my body lying prone, stretched across the canvas blood dripping from my forehead as I lay helpless in the center of the Octagon. Standing above me was Brick and around his waist my World title clung to his love handles. Then…as suddenly as the pain had hit it vanished, as suddenly as the vision had appeared it disappeared. My hands still shook as I broke out into a cold sweat my tan skin going pale. 'Josh?' Ken asked as he stared intently at me. 'Josh?' He repeated again, reaching across the table and shaking me, snapping me away from my vision. Lowering my head I shook it to break free from the sick captivity my predatory insecurities inside held on my brain. With my head down I sighed, letting out my breath violently. Josh, are you alright?' Ken asked yet again, knowing full well that something was out of the ordinary. 'I'm fine.' I assured him before quickly leaping from my seat. 'But Ken…I think I've got a match to prepare for.' I hastily said as I walked away from the booth I had just been seated in. Before Ken could get another word in I had bolted out of the Pizzeria and onto the broad sidewalk of this Dallas suburb. Dropping to my knees I raised my eyes to the heaven where I saw a horde of stars gazing back at me, with a soft sigh I bowed my head. I was on top of the World for now…but any day that could all be taken away from me if I wasn't prepared. But no…I would be prepared. I would fight for what was mine. I would go down as the greatest Champion in Global Wrestling history. I had begun that quest when I defeated Ice and I would continue along that path as I conquered Brick on Evolution. After all the work I had done, I was finally the World Champion…and it was nothing I ever intended to relinquish, Not to Ice, not to Brick…not to anyone, because I was finally where I belonged…on top of the World.
