Watching Overload

17 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/17/03 Brick sits on his couch, watching a tape of the recent edition of Overload. This was a particularly important Overload, because the main event was Derek Frost against Quartz, and he’d be facing the winner on the upcoming Evolution. Currently, he’s watching the footage from Trista Romano’s interview with Deacon Holmes. Brick has had his eye on Holmes since he came into the federation. Trista: Okay. Fair enough. What are your thoughts on the current World title situation and who do you expect to see win the big one at Dead Zone? Deacon Holmes: I don't think anything of it, seeing as how I'm not a part of the tournament, but if I had to choose someone to win the whole thing, I'd have to go with Brick. It's about time we had a black world champion around here. Trista: Good. That leads right into the next question: what are your thoughts on Brick? Deacon: Oh, well, don't get it twisted. Just cause I want him to win doesn't mean I like the guy, but on the other hand I've never had a problem with him either. He's alright, I guess. I wouldn't hesitate to stomp him out though if that's what you mean. Trista: NO, NO! I meant a lot of the Internet fans are comparing you to Brick. Some even say that Brick and Deacon would be the perfect tag team. What are your thoughts on what the fans are saying? Brick had himself heard such talk. The thought of taking young Mr. Holmes under his wing was an interesting notion. Deacon: Hmm, never really though about it before. I'm more of a solo act, but I guess we could be a good tag team. Of course, I'd be the one carrying most of the weight though. And as far as me being like him, there are only two things we've got in common, that we're black, and that we'll die. If anything, I'm better, I say what I want, do what I want, and don't need a bunch of cowboys backing me up to do it. Plus if I were him, I would've been had Gunner Addison back to my place. He must not have any game, but, then again, everyone's not Deacon Holmes. Just then the phone rings. Brick picks it up. “Hello. Hey, man. Just watching this tape of the last Overload. Yeah, I’m watching the interview with Holmes right now. I heard what he said. I’ve had cats run their mouths about me before. A lot of them didn’t last as long as it takes me to piss. This Holmes kid might have what it takes, but he’s still just a kid and I think he could stand to learn some respect. And I think I might just be the cat to teach him. Hold up a minute.” The footage shifts to the main event, the match Brick had been waiting to see. The crowd erupts in Boos.. Sights: OHH NO!!! Mark Kullypso comes to the ring. Frost leaps off the rope crashing into Kullypso. Frost climbs on top of Kullypso and starts wailing away. Sights: LOOK OUT!! Bishop: TOO LATE! Quartz kicks Frost in the back of the head and rolls him into the ring. All of a sudden the crowd erupts in cheers.. As BJP runs to the ring. Bishop: NO!!!! Sights: Yes! BJP Slides into the ring as Quartz is climbing over the top rope and BJP Sends him back out with a super kick? Kullypso staggers up and both men get back into the ring? Kullypso and Quartz attack BJP and send him into the ropes? NO!! Frost off the top turnbuckle and a dropkick sends Kullypso crashing into the turnbuckles. BJP Attacks Quartz Bishop: The ref is calling for the bell!! Sights: But who wins!!! West: Ladies and Gentlemen! The ref has deemed this match a double disqualification! Bishop: NOOO!!! Sights: This means both men are going to Evolution to face Brick. Bishop: NOOO!!! Brick hits pause and starts to reflect on this development. He was expecting him to face only one of them on Evolution. Although this development doesn’t really surprise him. In fact, Brick wouldn’t be surprised if it were all set up so that Brick would face two opponents in a triple threat match to stack the deck against him. But Brick has two things for him, going into this match: the drive to finally become champion and a knack for beating the odds.
