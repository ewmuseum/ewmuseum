A change of pace...

5 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-5-06 (ooc:I am dedicating the rp to KPJ... because he, much like Kanton, is a master of the pimpness) +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ Some times people want to tell you the story of their life. They want to tell you that sappy shit that’s supposed to effect you “emotionally”. Hell, I was going to tell you one of those stories today, but fate has taken a turn, and I will now follow her. When you think about it really? are we watching the life of our hero? or? are we affecting it and changing it to fit our will? I would rather think the later. Our hero needs our help I think? so it’s about time to help him. When last we left Kris Kanton he had met up with his old girlfriend, ah that gentle beauty known as Erika Kross? now, I was going to tell you all about how Erika and Kanton still loved each other, but Erika now loved someone else. Yeah, it was almost as if I was ripping off a soap opera, but that doesn’t even TOP it. Yeah, you see, Kanton has a kid! He doesn’t even know it, but I was thinking? why does he need to know right now? As a matter of fact? he doesn’t! He has too many things to worry about with in GW. Hell, this man has lost two straight matches so far’not a good start to the rebirth of his career I would say. Do you agree? I thought you would? So what should we join Kanton in doing? In being a part of this? one thing comes to mind. What do people love most about Kanton and what he has done? He’s a funny guy! He knows how to PARRRR-TAAAAAAYYY! Hell, HE DOES THE DEW GODDAM~! DO THE DEW~! This is also being brought to you by the official soft drink of Global? Mountain Dew. Now, onto what matters. Would you like to hear a realistic sappy love drama, or would everyone here rather hear about how Kanton parties, does the Dew, and nails all the hot chicks? I thought so? So let’s PARTY ON! Take a look around you, what do you see? We’re standing on the shores of California? there is of course beauty as far as the eye can see. Off in the distance you can see a bevy of beauties playing volleyball. The bright sun glistens in this southwestern wonderland. As though this is the land of beauty, it is odd that the beach is this crowded during the week, but it is beginning to come into focus. Off in the distance you can hear music playing, but now it seems to get closer. And there it is? can you see it? A large semi is pulling up to the edge of the beach as it brings in tow a large music stage! Already we can see a band playing? It seems to be Good Charlotte! The full band plays in tow as a giant Mountain Dew banner hangs behind them. The whole beach stands and begins to rock out hard! Asses a shaking, tag-o-bitties bouncing, oiled bodies glistening all as the music blasts. And there he is? our hero? Kris Kanton jumps out from behind a speaker with a microphone in tow. Other vans begin to pull up soon brandishing an MTV2 logo. Kanton hops down onto the sand and begins dancing with a group of extremely lovely ladies. His Nike’s kick up sand everywhere as his cargo shorts sway with his body as his form fitting white tank top flows with helps to show his impressive physique to the surrounding crowd. The music comes to a stop and Kanton backs up to the cheer of the crowd. “GOOD CHARLOTTE! EVERYBODY MAKE SOME NOISE!” Kanton hops up and down a minute as the MTV2 cameras begin to set up. With a smile and a glimmer in his eye Kanton lifts the mic back up to his mouth in an ever so familiar fashion. “I know why we’re here, and I hope you do too. That’s right, we’re her for a behind the scenes look at the next season of Mountain Dew’s Extreme Sports Tour. Today, I will be your host with the most” the LEGEND IN THE MAKING? Kris? KANTON!” Now this is what I’m talking about. The party man working his stuff?. Why are we here you say though? You can’t see what this has to do with Global? It’s simple, jealousy? You see, Kanton is one of the highest earning stars of Global. Not because of his contract with Global, but because of his marketing and his appearances? no other superstar has the national and worldwide appeal that Kanton has created for himself. This of course makes our hero a marked man. People like Kaden Calix, whom Kanton is facing at Turmoil, feel that Kanton has been given undue glory and they all seek to destroy and tarnish the legacy he has been creating. So this still doesn’t explain enough as to why we’re here today though does it? So let’s fast forward a little while, look just over there. You see Kanton surrounded by all of those people? They’re reporters? the media blitz has tackled Kanton into a corner while he was trying to work his Mojo! The audacity! Just look at those two beauties he has at his sides. I bet you’re pretty jealous of him too. Hell I am. The man in a freaking PIMP. But that’s not what’s important. What’s important are the questions, am I right? Well fine then Mr. Poopy Pants, we’ll listen in? “Mr. Kanton” how does it feel to be back in a ring again?” asked the older gentleman in the blue shirt. Sure, he’s a little out of place here, but that’s not important? right? “How the hell do you think it feels” It’s fantastic! The rush and the adrenaline coursing through your veins? it’s pure electricity! I live for it.” Kanton grinned wide at the thought. “Do you have any worries” Do you have any doubts about not staying in retirement?” Asked the older reporter once more. “Do I look worried” Really now man, look at my set-up? I have everything a guy could want.” Kanton remarked. “Everything except a winning record” or even a win since your return I would think?” This last voice came from a woman towards the back. Her bright gold hair glistened heavenly. Her soft heavenly voice flowed from her full luscious lips. A total babe Kanton thought to himself. He of course knew who she was. Damn, I would hit that in second? Back on subject, Kanton spoke up quickly? “Shit happens” nothing you can really do about it, but win the next time out.” “What if you don’t win” What if you? choke? again?” The woman smiled with a bit of a devilish grin. This did nothing to phase our hero though? he wouldn’t let such trivial things distract him from the task at hand? nailing these freaking hot chicks. At the same time? Because he’s a the man? “I wouldn’t be too worried about that” So I’m off to a rough start, but it’s much more fun the longer the climb to the top. Just that many more asses to kick.” Kanton laughed to himself as he locked eyes with the reporter. “So are we going to be seeing the return of the sixty minute man then” “Why don’t I show you myself” We could have a nice little party between the four of us.” Kanton grinned wide. SUCH A FREAKIN PIMP! If Chuck Norris were her right now, he would so roundhouse kick himself into submission at just the sight of Kanton’s pimpness? that’s how much of a pimp Kanton is? “Why don’t you try a one on one matchup first” I want you at a hundred percent. Just so I can know I broke you at your best.” Kanton left the two girls to their dismay that day. He took the reporter on her own? he would stand to the challenge and he would have flawless victory? This next Turmoil, Kris Kanton faces Kaden Calix, another man trying to regain past glory, but Kaden will fail. Why will he fail? Because Kris Kanton is the man? Even Chuck Norris would say that Kanton is the man. The sixty freakin minute man? oh yeah? Hail to the king baby?
