Date: 11-04-03 The Incarnation: Part 3 With the wind behind the young man he had barely any hope to keep going. With each passing day the life he once had was dying out and he was beginning to get tired. It would be the next best thing if he could rest, yet his main concern was that in which he wanted to get rid of, his history. The old man told Jesse a lot, about not only what had happened but also what will happen if he was found. It was as if Jesse was being chased, and yet he could not see the predator and continue in blindness. Now he has to face something he thought would be gone, and out of his life, a sickness to him, yet a blessing to some. It is the one he misses more than ever, yet at the same time, he's being dealt with the worst of his fears because of that one. Can he face his fear or will he crumble to the pressure? Story continues… In his heart he was crumbling, not a sight on earth would help him to move faster. His hands grasped the leaves as he crumbled them beneath his palm and he crawled desperately up the hill. It was not as if he'd not been in this position before, running all the time he was surely in this scenario three or four other times. Swaying his hair with every inch he moves, his body finally collapses. Lying there near the middle of the forest with his body completely aching, his hands covered in dirt and whatever other foul things lie on the ground, and all he could do was think. It had been four hours since he left the man's house and yet he'd only passed half way through the forest he once saw as his path to freedom. Then from a distance he heard something. Not a yell, a scream, or a screech, but a noise so familiar yet so distinctly forgotten to his mind. As he got off the ground he stumbled towards the sound, and it had to be the trees that he used for leverage or he'd have gone rolling. Swaying like a drunken man leaving a bar, Jesse stumbled over a land mound and then started to run on the straight surface. There he stumbled onto the rocky ground of train tracks. Before him was a long train with many box carts, and since the train was slow, Jesse jumped on the ladder and climbed into the box nearest. The train was that of which you called a hollow tree. Its inside was bare and just displayed the rust of many years. However from the corner Jesse was bound to be curious. He saw motion, but not like an animal’s movement, a more slowly and obvious motion. There Jesse saw a boy, maybe fifteen or sixteen, with a shaggy bowl hairstyle and a skinny body. The teenage boy was harmless to his eyes, yet Jesse cautiously made conversation. “Hello? Who’s there?” stated Jesse just trying to get something to start with. Seeing how Jesse was not someone who wanted to make things obvious he tried hard to make this look as if he didn’t see the child. For a moment Jesse turned his head and glanced back, yet no reply was given. Curious about the man, the boy reveals himself and crawls over into the light, where he lies back on the side of the box cart. “What?” replies the boy finally, with nothing but that word Jesse turns his head and knows there is no problem in speaking back, “Oh, I was just curious, because I heard a noise and it was either an animal or a man. Sort of glad it was just a boy, I would hate to have to take on some punk no brain idiot who thought he could beat me up just cause I was hitching a ride on a train… I hate people like that,” sighs Jesse. The boy’s eyes widen and then he turns his head so that Jesse can’t see his face. Staring out the door Jesse sees the trees as they pass by, and then sees from the far distance, a plane forming circles of white smoke in the air. Knowing how life could be no sweeter, his problems free him and for once he gets to enjoy some peace. However, the boy he remembers is beside him in the dark only a few feet away. Lingering in his mind, the boy was a problem Jesse had. He wanted to know why such a young man would catch a train and go somewhere unknown like Jesse was. “So… why are you here?” muttered Jesse as he turned from the viewing of the outside to the near complete darkness of the train. “Well… it’s not something I want to talk about,” mutters the boy in a crying tone, “See, my sister was murdered, and she’s all I had. Now I’m trying to find somewhere that’ll forget me of all those memories. It’s not like I can go to my home where my family would be, because there is no family. She was all I had, and now, it’s over…” the boy finishes with a tear running down his cheek. As the boy sobbed, Jesse realized that he looked much the same as the girl in his dreams, and in the memories he’d been having. However, he froze, and at the very instance his mind went blank and he dropped his eyes, only to forget who he was. Screaming is heard, and a few thuds as well, yet he can’t see a thing. All he can do is here. The voice he hears is so familiar, yet evil as if hatred was boiling on the tip of the persons tongue and they wanted to kill or hurt all in their way. “Die you little bastard! DIE!” Then a final yell was heard as it fainted away, and a thud to finish off what he heard. Growing tired Jesse fell asleep in the darkness. Waking up he saw life again. No more darkness, yet no more boy. Blood was on the floor below, it was in little dot like patterns as if someone stood there just letting the blood trickle down. Feeling his nose, he felt a sharp pain, and when he removed his hand from the nostrils, the answer he was looking for had been given. He had killed the boy, and had killed the girl, yet it was not him who was controlling the killings. Something deep inside has been taking his body over and using him to do there bidding. Looking at the empty cart he could only wish he could solve this problem. Staring down at the heavy armor floor of the train, as any good train was made, this one gave proof he had killed the boy. Muddy shoe tracks about half his size, were tracked to the end of the cart in a backward motion from where the blood dots lied. Holding his face with both hands, Jesse was in a bind of confusion. He could not accept the fate of killing someone, yet what he heard, what he sees, and what he has in front of him, proves he did. Could he be so blunt that he does not see any of this at all? Or does a demon lives within his soul, and is bidding the devil’s wishes. “WHY ME! Why do you torture me? I have never done anything so abusive to you! I am not a monster! I plead to you, please stop this!” Looking out the train door again, he is tempted to jump, but then knows he will not only lose his life, but probably satisfy yet another wish of the demon he may have inside. Leaning back he lies on his back and cries in emotional stress. Torture… Agony… Suffering… (To Be Continued…)
