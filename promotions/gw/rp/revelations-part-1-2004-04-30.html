Revelations Part 1

30 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 30 The weeks had been long. It had been a couple of weeks since the Souls of the Black Lotus had left their apartments in Dallas, Texas to return home for a short visit. However, that short visit was now running into its second week. Their uncle had offered them a chance to return to their old roots as Yakuza street warriors and they had graciously accepted. With the next week off from GW it would not be a problem for them to stay. Their uncle Gohei had given them an important mission; one that none of his personal street warriors had been able to crack yet. The Nakazuras were to find out exactly what was going on in the Tokyo underworld and find out the truth behind the rumours of this foreign investment in certain Yakuza empires. They had been visiting informants and local providers in order to find out more information. So far a lot of there sources had been coming up dry. It seems as though this new competition was almost completely foreign and completely hidden. Whoever they were they were keeping a low profile and they weren’t aligning themselves with very many of the local businesses and this was allowing them to keep this low profile. They were not yet ready to give up. There were many more informants in Tokyo, some were more or less reliable and some were more or less expensive. But, in Tokyo, there was always someone ready to snitch for a price, the key was coming up with the collateral. However, there was one informant whom they had visited before. But, he was not one who would give his knowledge cheaply. The two brothers had a way of persuading him to tell them what they wanted to know however. The door to the small rooms burst open. Jushin had his hands wrapped into the man’s collar and sent him sprawling into the wall closest to the door. The man hit the wall, then fell to the ground in a heap with a loud thud as he hit the ground. Ichiro strolled in behind them, shaking his head as he walked. He shut the door behind him, and pocketed his white wired and blue lensed Oakley sunglasses. By this time Jushin already had a set of handcuffs out and was locking the informant to a steel folding chair with his arms locked behind him. Ichiro grimaced as he saw the dazed informant lean over forward almost unconscious and saw a stream of blood coming running from his mouth. Ichiro thought back to only ten minutes ago, things would have been so much easier if the informant hadn’t have told Jushin off, it was not the right time. The caf? was a relaxed atmosphere. Ichiro knew why the informant had picked it, it was nice and open, and anything they did would be in complete view of the public. The informant had another thing coming to him if he thought that would stop the fury of his little brother. Jushin’s reputation was widespread across Tokyo and it did not offer favours to the Souls when they were trying to find out information. But it didn’t matter, Jushin had sent the message, he showed the informant that no matter what he thought, Jushin was in charge, not him. “We know why you are here and we do have the money to pay you,” said Ichiro. He had told Jushin to allow him to do the talking, the last thing he needed was to see Jushin get pissed. “That’s good, let me have it,” said the informant. “Not a chance, not until you give us what we want,” snarled Jushin. “The informant took a sip from the green tea he was drinking and then leaned forward. He whispered in a firm tone, loud enough for only the brothers to hear, but firm enough to imply the message that he was not kidding. “Listen, I know who you two are and I’m not going to play your little fucking games! I am in control of this meeting, not you, me!” “Be easy, we do not wish for any trouble to come about from this meeting,” Ichiro started. “However, these are matters in which we do not wish to wade in long. We just need to find out who we need to talk to so we can get out of here and take care of things, then leave Tokyo.” “Well aren’t you guys just the heroic brothers, back from the US to help out your uncle in Tokyo. Listen I don’t care why you need this info, I don’t need to hear your bullshit, there is only one thing in this world that is going to get me talking and that is giving me my fucking money!” Jushin had heard enough, even through the dark sunglasses which his little brother was wearing, he could see the flames in his brother’s eyes. Jushin grabbed the informant by the collar, he never really let go of the collar to tell the truth. “Listen you piece of shit! The information you are holding from us isn’t business, it is personal. So unless you want to lose some body parts I suggest you start talking right now. And I suggest you tell us who these foreign investors in the local Yakuza are!” Ichiro’s mind seemed to click in then. The weighing of the options, as he repeated Jushin’s words over and over in his head. The Lotus was trying to send him a message again, he could feel it, the same feeling of weight on his shoulders from when his father was killed, had once again returned. Could these foreign investors? involvement with the local Yakuza be the cause of his father’s death? He snapped out of it as the guy pushed Jushin over the edge, things were about to get ugly. “Listen you fucking Yakuza pig, if you want to hear a word from my mouth, you had better take your filthy fucking hands off of my suit,” he whispered, loud enough for only the brothers to hear. “Ichiro, let’s make this meeting a little more private, what do you think?” Jushin asked as he smiled over at his older brother. “Since this little piece of shit doesn’t want to talk, we’ll cut his fucking tongue out and he’ll never say a word again since he likes it so much! Let’s go!” Jushin stood on his feet and dragged the informant to his feet, before he started leading him out the door. “Please, no, I’m sorry,” muttered the informant, pleading for mercy. Ichiro smiled and tried to ignore the crowd that was staring at him as he began to rise from his seat. He smiled at them all and put on his sunglasses, as he left several notes on the table to cover the drinks. He then began to follow Jushin who was already half way across the street still dragging the informant against his will. And now they were here. Ichiro could only laugh at the informant’s stupidity. He had angered Jushin and this was not the right time to do that. Jushin took his watch off and it into his coat pocket before he backhanded the informant clean across the mouth. The slap echoed throughout the small room. Ichiro sat on the edge of the steel table that was inside the room. He watched as blood began to trickle out of the informant’s mouth. The man was truly in a daze and who could blame him? In a matter of no more than three minutes Jushin had pummelled him and dragged him across the street, up seven flights of stairs and now into this interrogation room. Ichiro reached his hand out to signal his brother to lay off for a minute. Ichiro pushed himself up from the table and stepped in between his little brother and the informant. “So, are you ready to tell us what we need to know?” asked Ichiro. “If you think this is the way I do business, than you are sadly mistaken. I want double the money for this abuse!” yelled the informant. “You are in no position to negotiate, nor to make demands. You will speak now, and you will speak for the agreed upon price. Now talk!” instructed Ichiro. “Double the money or I keep my mouth shut!” demanded the informant. Ichiro only shrugged his shoulders and stepped out of the way from in between Jushin and the informant. Before the informant could even really tell that Ichiro had moved, his vision was consumed with the silhouette of Jushin coming towards him as Jushin delivered a roundhouse kick right to the man’s head. The informant went slightly limp as the chair fell over onto its side. Jushin laughed as he grabbed the chair by the legs and pulled it back right side up again. “Talk now,” Jushin said sternly. The informant let out a soft chuckle, that was somewhat muffled by his dazed state and sore body. Jushin once again lost it, he could not take wasting his time for this idiot to dick around. Jushin grabbed a hold of his shirt and pulled him close, they stood there, fixated on one another’s eyes. Jushin felt the informant’s hot breath on his face as they stared face to face. The informant tried to get in a cheap shot, and head butted Jushin. It did not go over well as Jushin pummelled away at the man’s face for a few minutes before Ichiro finally stepped in once again. “That’s enough brother,” said Ichiro. “You lucky piece of shit!” Jushin snarled as he spit onto the floor, wiping the small bit of blood from his lip. Ichiro nearly threw up when he saw the man’s face for the first time since Jushin had stepped away. Jushin really had done some damage to the man’s face, he was going to need reconstructive surgery at the very least. But, if he didn’t start talking soon, that would be the least of his worries. The man had cuts in several places on his face, his nose was broken, there was swelling around both of his eyes already and Ichiro thought he could make out the outline of a tooth lying in the pool of blood at the foot of the chair. His thoughts were confirmed however, as the man let out a squeal of pain and his front tooth was indeed missing. Ichiro looked at his little brother and mouthed the words, “Holy shit,” to him. Jushin who was now leaning against the wall and rubbing his slightly sore and swollen knuckles only shrug his shoulders in response to his older brother. Ichiro then turned back to the informant. “Are you read to talk yet?” asked Ichiro. “Fuck you both! And your whole fucking family! I will never tell you anything now, I take this information with me to the grave!” the informant yelled at the top of his lungs. Jushin pushed himself off of the wall and walked right up to the man in the chair. The informant cringed and shied away bracing himself for another punch from Jushin, it never came however. Jushin simply pulled out his custom made pistol from its holster and took aim at the man’s head, clicking off the safety as he did so. “Well, you’re useless to us if you don’t talk, so that is straight where you’re going!” Jushin said, smiling sickly from behind the sight of his gun. “You won’t shoot me?” said the informant. “Oh no?” asked Jushin. “I wouldn’t count on that,” said Ichiro. “You can’t shoot me, I’m your last hope,” answered the informant smugly. Jushin smiled and turned the gun barrel down slightly and pulled the trigger. The bullet flew from the cartridge and straight into the informant’s foot. Blood spattered everywhere through the whole in the man’s shoes. The only thing louder than the gunshot was the informant’s scream of agony. “Now what?” asked Jushin. “F-F-F-Fuck you!” the informant managed to stutter before spitting at Jushin’s feet. “Wrong answer!” yelled Jushin. Once again Jushin took aim and let another bullet fly from the cartridge. This one however, made contact with the informant’s other foot and sent a puddle of blood pooling from both of the informant’s feet. “Where the fuck are these assholes”!” the informant mumbled to himself, trying to hold down the pain. “What did you just say?” asked Ichiro. The informant only laughs and smiles smugly, still fighting away the pain from both of his gunshot wounds. Jushin took aim again with his gun and let another bullet fly. This one went right through the man’s left hand. “Not so funny now is it?” asked Jushin, smiling broadly. “FUCK! God! Ok, Okay fine I’ll talk! I’ll talk!!” yelled the informant. Jushin moved away and sat down on the edge of the table beside his older brother. “Good, we’re listening.” The informant swore and screamed for a few more minutes as Jushin was sitting on the table he put his gun back into its holster. He stared at the informant, the informant was infuriated. If nothing else, he hoped to survive this meeting just to get revenge on the two brothers. He leaned back in the chair as much as he good, his body aching in extreme torment with every slight movement. The slightest change in position was like having a million pins stuck into one nerve. He swallowed the pain as best he could as he began to talk. Ichiro could see that he was struggling and he pulled out a small envelope filled with twenty yen notes and placed it on the table as a form of incentive. “Okay, you’re looking at foreign investment of the worst kind,” the informant started. “They are American.” “Where in America?” asked Jushin. “I don’t know,” the informant managed to spit out. “They are huge though, they have lots of money and they are throwing it around.” “What are they spending it all on” Guns, drugs, legal things?” asked Ichiro. “Mainly guns and ammunitions,” replied the informant. “That’s what father was in,” said Jushin, a strong rage glowing in him. He looked at the informant, “tell me this, have these guys put out any hits?” “I don’t know the exact targets, but they have been putting out hits. Mainly they are low profile but they have been peaking in revenues from Tokyo lately after a couple of big hits,” answered the informant. Jushin looked at Ichiro with death in his eyes, could these be the ones responsible for their father’s death? They couldn’t know for sure, until they asked. “Did they put a hit out on my father and his warehouse?” Ichiro asked slowly. The informant spit up some blood and opened his mouth to begin to speak. However, no words escaped as he immediately looked in shock as the door to the room swung open and the loud roar of automatic weapons rang out through the room. Bullets flew in and exploded all over the informant’s chest. His blood spilled everywhere and it was not long before his body hung limply over the chair. As soon as the two brothers heard the shots go off, they had immediately taken out their pistols. They had tipped the steel table over and they were now ducking in behind it. They were waiting for their attackers to enter the room and they did not have to wait long. They breathed heavily from behind the tilted table and they looked at the floor for the signs of entering shadows. Soon they saw the sign they needed. A stir of noise and footsteps as shadows begin to fill the room. Ichiro looked at his little brother, who nodded back at him. Speaking without speaking they counted to three, before they quickly popped up and fired several shots at their attackers. They took their foes by surprise and many of them fell to the ground dead, while the others scrambled to unload rounds of automatic fire back at the table. The stainless steel table did not let any fire through and allowed the two brothers enough time to quickly change clips. Ichiro threw his empty clip to the side of the table and drew the attacker’s attention as Jushin peered quickly out the other side of the table. He turned back to his brother and held up four fingers. Ichiro nodded and Jushin tossed his empty clips to the other side, a momentary distraction of their attackers. That was all his big brother needed, as Ichiro immediately capitalized on the distraction. He rolled out to the side and let off a full clip from each of his twin pistols and downed the remaining attackers. The brothers quickly stood up and got ready to leave. Jushin quickly picked up the envelope of money and Ichiro began to make his way towards the door. As Ichiro was about to walk through the door, he was putting on his sunglasses. But he was called back into the room by his brother. “Ichiro, come look at this.” Ichiro turned back to his brother and stood in the doorway for a second. “What is it?” “Look at these faces, they aren’t Japanese. This isn’t a Yakuza hit squad. They, they’re American,” said Jushin. “Looks, like our informer here, was working for our foreign investors. No wonder, it took him so long to talk,” said Ichiro. “That’s why he said, “why aren’t they here yet,”” said Jushin. “Things have just gotten more interesting brother, and I will not allow this to stand, we need to get back to uncle Gohei’s and inform him of the circumstances,” said Ichiro. “Let’s go!” The two brothers stepped into the small parking lot behind the caf?. They were almost running as they came around the corner, and their mouths dropped in shock at the sight when the lot came into view. Where their new Mitsubishi was parked not thirty minutes ago, was a flaming inferno of melted fabric, melted rubber and torn metal. “Apparently the stakes have risen brother,” said Jushin. “We definitely need to get back to Uncle Gohei’s quickly,” said Ichiro. “How?” asked Jushin. “We’ll have to try the subway,” said Ichiro.
