Where has Brick been?

23 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/23/05 Ever since he first walked through the double doors, Brick has been the most persistent member of the Global roster. So persistent and so steadfast has he been, the only time he ever took a break from Global was during the annual holiday break at the end of the year. But, this summer, for the first time in his career at Global... Brick was absent from Global Wrestling. The entire summer went by and not one appearance by him in the Octagon. As far as anyone knew, he was still alive, as was proven when he made his return on the most recent edition of Evolution as Christian Zenith's mystery opponent. Which begs the question: Where has Brick been? Now that question can be answered. ---- One June morning... Bubba sat in his office, sharpening his pencils. Suddenly, the door opened, and in walked Brick. Normally, Brick stormed angrily into Bubba's office. Very rarely did he enter calmly and politely. 'Ah, Brick. Have a seat.' Brick sat in the seat in front of Bubba's desk. 'You wanted to talk to me?' 'Yeah. See, Bubba, the reason I wanted to talk to you is because, well, DMAK is going on tour this summer...' 'Uh-huh,' Bubba replied, his hands clasped under his chin. 'And as it is, I've been neglecting them. Aaron does a good job as co-manager, but still, I've been invested in this group since I discovered them in the West 4th Street station. And this summer tour is maybe the biggest in their careers.' Bubba sighed and leaned back in his chair. 'Now, I ain't gonna lie to ya, Brick. This is a bad time for you to be takin' time off. On the other hand, if anybody in this company ever deserved time off, it's you. I mean, you've been a mainstay in this company for years, workin' through injuries and what not. Besides, I understand ya got other obligations. I always understood that.' 'So, do I get the time off?' 'Yeah, ya get the time off.' 'Thanks, Bubba 'I just need ya to wrestle one more match before ya go.' 'What is it?' 'I need for you an' Train t' defend the tag titles against the Herederos.' 'Yeah, sure.' As Brick rose to leave the office, Bubba stopped him. 'One more thing. I'd really like it if you could do some promo work for Global while you're on tour.' 'Sure, no problem.' ---- The first stop on the tour was Madison Square Garden. After Madison Square Garden, the DMAK summer tour began in earnest. Los Angeles. Atlanta. Honolulu. Chicago. Detroit. London. Rome. Tokyo. At each stop, they sang all their hits: 'Dance with Me', 'Before You Lose Me', 'I Love The Way You Love Me', 'Until You Want It Back', etc. The last night of the DMAK summer tour came in the first week of September. Like every night of the tour, they closed their set with an acapella rendition of the Manhattans' 'Kiss and Say Goodbye', the very song they sang when Brick discovered them all those years ago. Brick and DMAK had finally returned from their tour and had gone to their respective homes. 'You have three messages.' beep 'Hey, Brick, it's Aaron. Just wanted to see if you were back yet from the tour. I know this was supposed to be the day. Listen, I gotta pick Ashley up from daycare. I'll talk to you later.' beep 'Jamal, it's Mom. I just wanted to see if you'd gotten back from the tour. Call me as soon as you can, let me know if you got in.' beep 'Brick, it's Christina Reno. Call me when you get this message.' Brick had heard through the grapevine about Christina taking over as President from the retiring Bubba McCoy 'Hey, Christina, what's up?' 'Oh, Brick, thank God. It's Christian Zenith.' Brick sighed. He'd heard that Zenith and the Hunting Grounds had been running roughshod over Global Wrestling over the summer. Brick made his career tangling with such individuals: Jason Ayers, Eric Lansheer, the Nevermore, the Upper Echelon. The Hunting Grounds, in his mind, would be no different. 'What do you need?' 'What I need is someone to stand up to the Hunting Grounds. And I figured you'd be interested.' 'You figured right,' Brick said. 'Good, because here's the idea I had.'
