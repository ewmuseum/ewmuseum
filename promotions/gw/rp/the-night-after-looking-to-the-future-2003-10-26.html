The Night After Looking To The Future

26 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Night After Looking To The Future Jack Rocker Global Wrestling Promo #669 Date: 26th October 2003 Location: Streets Of Texas It is as normal a sunny, beautiful day in Texas. Many parents have decided to take their children to the local ballpark and the noise from around that area is filled with the screaming and laughter of little children. For a couple of minutes a young man can be seen peaking through one of the three cage walls of the ballpark. Jack doesn’t seem in a bad mood in fact he smiles for a short while but then his moments happiness turns to being jealous. Anger climbs up his body and soon his face is red and he has a crazed expression because of his anger, Jack clenches his fists and is about to open one of the four doors that lead to the ballpark. One of the fathers walks up to the cage and looks at Jack, Jack stares back looking at the mans short black hair, his works jumper, his black business like trousers and his black polished shoes, it reminded Jack of his own father. Father: “Excuse me, my son hit the ball over the park wall and it’s landed next to you. Could you pick it up and throw it over the fence please?” Jack shakes his head and spits in the man face. Suddenly Jack completely snaps and he grabs the man’s collar and pulls him, making the man smash into the cage wall, Jack repeatedly smashes the man against the cage wall by pulling his collar until finally the man falls to his feet having lost his senses. The man sits on the floor; a small cut on his face which soon begins to bleed but not a lot. The man breathes heavily as Jack gets on his knees and whispers in the man’s ear. Jack: “You think I’m a fool? I know what you people are like, you’ll play with your son for a little while but then you’ll get bored of it and you’ll just piss off. For the rest of his life your son will have no fucking family and will be afraid to let anyone get too close to him. If I find out you did that then one-day I’ll beat so much shit out of you that you’ll be in a hospital bed full time. Now go and get you fucking ball and make sure I never see you or hear about you again.” Jack stuffs his hand in his pockets and walks off down the road leaving complete chaos in the ballpark as people try and help the bleed and out of sorts father. Jack walks up to a crossing area and looks up at the lights, he waits for thirty seconds but doesn’t see any green man so Jack just shrugs his shoulders and walks across the road. Halfway across the road a trucks nearly runs over Jack, the truck driver’s horn goes of several times but Jack just ignores it and continues to walk across slowly. The truck driver franticly puts his foot on the break and the car stops just a matter of inches away from Rocker. The truck driver yells out at Rocker but Rocker just shows him the middle finger and crosses the road. Jack walks up the sidewalk but then takes a left turning and walks up a rather dark alleyway considering it was a sunny day. Jack walked up to the third door up the alleyway and knocks several times. The door opens more or less instantly as a middle aged man opens the door and signals for Jack to enter. Jack follows the middle aged man down the badly lit corridor and then into a large room with only three pieces of furnisher and walls that haven’t ever been wall papered or for that matter painted. The middle-aged man sits on a sofa with another slightly younger man sitting on the arm of the sofa next to the middle-aged man. Middle-aged Man: “Afternoon Jack. Surprised you could find us, in fact I’m surprised you aren’t in the gym for you match at Evolution.” Jack sits at the other sofa and sees the television is on the sports station. The GW logo appears on the screen and a young man with short jet black hair that is wearing a suit looks at the camera. The card for Evolution shows up on the screen including Jack’s first ever match, Jack nods his head as he sees his match and begins to crack his knuckles. Jack: “Looks interesting, never heard of any of those mother fucking assholes but still I can’t wait to enter that octagon. I just hope John Christie and Trent Ador aren’t expecting a wrestling match because all there going to get is a fight. I’ll won’t just kick their ass I’ll fucking give them more physical pain then they’ve ever had in their life, they’ll be quitting and begging me to stop well fuck them. Only one man is going to leave a winner, the guys I face will not just be losing a match they’ll be losing their lively hood. Plus the security must let me in the arena which means I can get my revenge on that fucking asshole, so what’s this I hear about you guys having weapons?” The middle-aged man looks around then nods his head to the slightly younger man. The younger man walks over to the television and switches it off. The middle-aged man leaves the room for a few minutes and then returns with a rather large box, the middle-aged man opens it and rummages inside for a few minutes. He pulls out two daggers, a couple of baseball bats, a nightstick similar to the ones used by policeman, a pair of handcuffs and surprisingly a cricket ball. Jack: “Contrary to what you may believe I don’t want to kill Tony, I just want to make him taste his own blood. The only possible reason I might need a weapon is incase his bodyguards get involved, because if they do then I’m going to have to take them on as well. Umm, I wonder. When I was in prison I remember a twenty-year-old man who tried to break free. He once stole a 2 by 4 from the yard and a roll of barbed wire. He wrapped the barbwire around the 2 by 4 and then tried to escape, destroying any police that got in his way, would it be possible to wrap some barbwire around that 2 by 4?” The middle-aged man nods his head and puts everything apart from the wooden 2 by 4 in the box and shuts it. Suddenly someone can be heard knocking at the door, the middle-aged man and his colleague run out of the room and signal for Jack to open the door. Jack nods his head as he watches the two men hide on the landing up the flight of stairs. Jack walks over to the door and opens it only to find his bodyguard standing on the doorstep. Jack: “What the fuck do you want and how the fuck did you find me?” The bodyguard just continues to look at Jack, he signals for Jack to leave the building and Jack shrugging follows the bodyguard as he walks up the alleyway. Bodyguard: “Why were you in that building?” Jack shakes his head and for the second time in one day his face turn red. Jack: “Can’t I do what I want? I’m fucking nineteen, I can’t do whatever I damn well please, oh and tell GW that I no longer want a bodyguard. If you must know I was looking at the building and thinking about buying it. Anyway, this is the last time I’m going to ask you without letting my anger really show, what the fuck do you want?” Bodyguard: “I’m making sure you don’t get in any trouble before your match. You have a big chance at Evolution and GW doesn’t want any troublesome teenager who’s getting in trouble on their TV shows. Anyway I’m going to show you the gym so you can get ready for you match.” Jack Rocker follows the bodyguard up the sidewalk and turn left. Eventually they make it to the gym but Jack doesn’t start his preparation for the match and instead both the security guard head off back to the hotel.
