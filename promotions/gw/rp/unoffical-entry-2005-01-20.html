Date: 01/20/05 Tribulations of Drake, Continued? -Unofficial Entry- I patiently paced around the mat waiting to face my challenge. Katie was all that ran through my head giving me strength and courage. It seemed as though I had been waiting for an eternity before my opponent stepped onto the mat. He was a short Hispanic man with long blond hair, and dark eyes. His dark martial art pants were similar to mine and tied around his waist was a golden belt that trenched over his left leg. We stared into each other’s eyes for the longest time trying to study one another. It was clear neither of us were afraid. He went into his fighting stance and I did the same. He and I were nothing but warriors at this point who had just one goal’to see the other face down on the mat. We charged and seconds later the fight had begun? He started things off with a jumping spin kick that was easy to avoid by simply ducking. As soon as he turned back to me I capitalized with a powerful kick to his left thigh to slow blood circulation through out his body. Now having him down to one knee I used another kick to nail him in the face sending him back onto the mat. With the defeat of one enemy came another. He came at me kicking high but I was able to block with my forearms. He made the mistake of kicking above the waist one too many times as I captured his foot and kept the leg high, so as not to give him an opportunity to use his fists. As before I kicked the left thigh to slow blood circulation then let the leg drop to move in for a hard right, which connected with his cheek bone sending him down. Before I had time to think another came in from behind locking in a full nelson. I pushed back once to get him to push forward and as he did so I used his momentum to flip him over my shoulders. In came another who swung but I blocked and reversed locking his arm under my shoulder. I used my free hand to reach behind his neck to push him downward as I brought my foot up behind my thigh driving it into his face knocking him out. I turned to see two more running in. The first swung; I easily caught his wrist and managed to get a couple shots in on the side of his ribs. The second man finally reached the battle and tried a front kick. Still having control of the other, I pulled him in as a shield. After he was knocked down I was open to deliver a high front kick and once again was the only one standing. This didn’t last long however. Another man tried for a low front kick and I blocked it just below waist level with the palms of my hands then quickly kicked the center of his chest sending him flying back as another grabbed me in a reverse bear hug. Before I could focus on getting out of the hold I noticed another man running in with a staff. As the man swung I guided my captor in a half cercal. I was then released as the staff came crashing down onto his back. Now there were only two opponents, the man and the staff. I had to get that staff on my side. I kept my stance as I waited for him to make the first move. He cam swinging and I managed to dodge at first but soon got hit in the face and a sudden sting shot through my body as I dropped to the mat. I held my face in pain but quickly had to roll out of the way barely avoiding the staff as it crashed down; I could feel its small breeze on my back. Acting quickly I rolled back over trapping it to the ground with my leg. I used my other to kick the staff out of the mans hands then kicked up standing face to face once again. He charged and I dove taking him down in a body scissors. I rolled once and drove the back of my leg into his face. After I made it back to my feet I wasted no time grabbing the staff. Seeing this some of the fighters steeped onto the mat with their own. They circled me but I wasn’t going to back down. Almost in an instant staffs were swinging through the air and the sound of wood smacking wood seemed louder to me than the music that was still playing. I was blocking in all directions and finally dropped down allowing one of the men to hit another taking him out for me. Before rising I used my staff to block another above me and rolled back kicking a foes knee from under him sending him ribcage fist onto the mat with a sickening thud. Two down three to go. All three cam in for overhead hits and I rested my staff on the back of my neck to block them all. I spun it back around catching one in the face twirling him into the air and onto the mat. I turned just in time to raise the staff in front of me to block the remaining two. I kicked one man in the gut to knock him back giving me enough time to reach my staff under the other foes arm. I kicked his shin out from under him and pushed up with my staff using it and his momentum to flip him forward onto his back. The odds were even now. Side to side our staffs swung colliding with one another. We battled for several minutes with everything we could think of. Finally I managed to jump up, using my staff as leverage, and locked his staff in between my feet. As I completed what I like to call the “staff cartwheel,” I flipped the staff from his grasp. I then used mine to sweep his feet from under him and brought the other side over hitting him on the collarbone. It seemed the fight was over but little did I know my biggest challenge was still ahead. He stepped onto the mat with monstrous steps. This black six foot four bald headed monster had more muscle than anyone I had ever seen. Just from looking at him I knew this was going to be difficult. He stopped and just smiled at me. He was dressed similar to the guards except he wore a black T-shirt rather than a tank top. All I could do was put my head down and sigh. Then I saw Katie in my mind once more and I was instantly refueled. I walked toward him swinging the staff around me to hopefully get a quick shot in on him and it worked’but not how I planned. The staff broke in half across his face and he didn’t even look fazed. I picked up the fallen half and studied it for a moment. When I looked back up he just smiled at me again. He came at me with his massive arms and I began using the two halves of the staff as escrima sticks. I hit every key area I could think of but it was no use. He had too much muscle. Seconds later both my wrists were being squeezed by his powerful hands and the pain made me drop the sticks. Then he swung me around throwing me through the air. I landed hard on the mat and suffered a small mat burn. I stood holding my ribs as the monster walked towards me. He swung. I ducked and hit him in the gut then the face probably hurting myself more than him. His powerful hand reached around my throat and I felt myself being lifted off my feet. He smiled as I suffocated. I cut this smile short, however, when I poked his eyes. I fell to the mat catching my breath as he held his eyes in pain. I landed near one of the staff pieces so I quickly grabbed it. I ran and jumped up onto his back using the stick to choke him. I wrapped my legs around his torso so he couldn’t flip me over. This was a small challenge in and of its self. He tried to shake me as he slowly moved around the mat but I couldn’t afford to let go. He tried reaching back for me but had too much muscle to be effective at this. I felt him beginning to fall back. I figured he would do this. As he fell back I released my grip and landed on my feet standing over him. Before I could move he grabbed my foot and tripped me. Moments later I was being thrown around like a rag doll. OOF! I landed hard, my cheek smacking the mat. I could feel my insides aching. He picked me up once again and began hitting me rapidity in the face. I landed back onto my stomach. I watched my blood pour onto the mat. Before I could get a decent breather, I found my self being held directly above his head. I tried to get myself free. It was no use and soon I was soaring through the air colliding into the wall just underneath the second floor. I dropped to all fours trying to gather myself. I watched my blood drip in front of me. I couldn’t let myself be stopped this was for Katie. I slowly made my back onto the mat trying not to collapse. I started hitting him with what little strength I had left. I finally dropped to one knee and then collapsed onto the mat. The club was silent, no music, people were no longer dancing. “Enough fighting,” A voice shouted. Man: Please. Help our friend up. He and I have much to discuss. The man who was once my opponent now pulled me off the mat and carried me away? To be continued?
