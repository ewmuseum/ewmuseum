Baby Blues

30 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30th October 2004 Immortality... Many people have wondered what it would be like to live forever. However most come to the same conclusion, that to live forever although fun for a time would soon drive a person insane. Think about it, you had an eternity to do what you wanted but there is only so much time that you could fill with doing exactly what you wanted. In fact you could do everything in the world but it would still only take a limited amount of time. So then what do you do? You see to be immortal would be nothing but a never-ending cycle of life and slowly but surely it would drive you mad. The reason for this talk of immortality is that there is only one way people can become immortal and that is to touch someone's life. It sounds simple but it isn't. We interact with thousands of people throughout our lives but we only ever touch a single person’s life who will make you immortal in their memories. If one person never forgets you then you will live forever, because a memory will last forever. Many people want to be remembered by not just one person but a whole society. Personally to be remembered and immortalised by one person is fine with me... On the other hand I have already made a step towards being immortalised by 'a society' and that is the society of the GW. It is a long way before I am remembered alongside names such as Jonathan 'The Scarecrow' Davis but at least I have made a step towards it. You see I am now a 2 time Cruiserweight Champion. It may not sound impressive but I went through hell at Dark Rituals to reclaim the title that I should never have lost in the first place. However I am not one to grumble and now I can proudly call myself, yet again, the GW Cruiserweight Champion. You have no idea how good it felt as the referee handed me that title, I felt a surge through my body that was indescribable. I put everything into that match and thankfully got everything out of it. My opponents pushed and pushed me, which I thank them for. I know I will face at least one of them again in the future as there is a match between the 2 at Evolution for a shot at my title. However I want to tell Winters and Curtis that I do plan on holding on to this belt for a long time. I know it is a bit clich? for me to say something like that but the only reason I lost it last time was because of the injury I encored thanks to the Flying Vergomovz, so whom ever is coming after this belt better be prepared for the fight of their lives. Anyway now that I have my belt back I can now concentrate on climbing the rankings of the GW and at this coming Evolution I face a monster of a man who in recent weeks has become quite the name around GW, Jackson Namenlos-Duisternis. Many say I don't stand a chance against such a beast of a man, that I will be lucky to come out of the match still breathing. It is yet another case of me having to 'defy the odds', which I have been doing all my life. People seem to forget what I have been through in my life, what I have over come, I am not saying that my life is just one huge struggle because that is just ridiculous, like everyone else I have had ups and downs. However I have always tried to look at the low points in my life as a chance to prove myself, yes it sounds sad but why let the low points get you down. We all have knocks in life and it up to us to get up and brush ourselves off. It is these moments in our life that decide what sort of people we are going to become. What I mean by my rambling is that although Jackson may seem a unstoppable monster but maybe, just maybe I can be the one to stop him! 'If you look carefully you can see the head, just there.' The midwife was pointing at the ultrasound image of my baby as she rolled the scanner over Rachel's jelly covered bump. Rachel giggled like a little schoolgirl as the midwife applied more jelly. 'God that is cold!' Rachel giggled again. 'Honey, come on, be serious for a minute. Look it’s our little baby.' I said pointing at the image whilst holding back a smile. I couldn't help it, it was good to see my wife smile again. It had been a tough month for her and that was all down to me, but now there was a sense of optimism between us and we are more in love than ever before. 'Sorry honey, it just feels funny,' Rachel said smiling at me and then turning to the midwife, 'So is everything okay? No deformities or anything?' There was a long silence, well actually it was probably only a silence of about 5 seconds but to Rachel and me it felt like forever. The problem was that ever since Rachel had fallen pregnant there had always been that question hanging over us. We had tried to ignore it but as we drew closer to the actual birth it has become harder to ignore. The question that plagues are minds is a simple one...what if? What if the baby inherits my disorder? What if my disorder effects the baby in other ways? I want our baby to have a normal life, to live a life without worrying about breathing in fresh air, to live a life where they can go out and play with other children. I want my baby to lead a life I never led, to be happy. But this baby's life all depended on this 1 question, 'Is everything okay?' I sat holding Rachel's hand tightly, both of us looking at the midwife in hope as she pressed a few buttons on the machine. She turned around with a very plain look on her face, I felt my gut turn. 'Is everything okay?' I said repeating Rachel's word in case the midwife hadn't heard my wife before. 'Everything...' The midwife paused, as if wanting to prolong our agony, '...is fine!' I lay my head on the bed, which my wife lay on and sighed with relief. I looked up at Rachel and I have never seen her smile so broadly. She looked down at me and gave me a wink as if to say she knew everything would be okay. I got up and moved over to her and held her in my arms. I looked at the midwife and she was smiling broadly also, she obviously knew how important this was to us. 'Would you like me to print you off a picture of the scan?' The midwife asked. I looked at Rachel who was in streams of tears, unable to talk. 'Yes thank you, we would love a picture of the scan.' I said smiling the biggest smile behind my mask. The midwife pressed another button on the machine and within seconds out popped a picture of our baby. She handed it over to me as I hugged my wife in one arm who was still crying tears of relief. 'I will leave you two alone. Take as much time as you need.' The midwife said as she left through the door. I stared at the picture as Rachel continued to cry away. As I stared at the picture I knew that when that baby was born, there would be no one in this world that will be loved as much as it will be. Rachel wept her last tear as she looked up at me. 'That's our baby Adam, our precious little baby.' 'I know sweetheart, I know. I will love our child with every bone in my body. I will let no harm come to it.' 'Just make sure you are around when this baby is born.' I looked down at my wife, who now had a worried look on her face. I sat down on the bed so I was staring directly at her. Her eyes were red from the crying and it looked like another wave of tears was on its way. 'How do you mean?' I asked wondering why my wife was so worried. 'I saw Dark Rituals Adam.' I had told Rachel not to watch Dark Rituals as I knew the match I was involved in was going to be one of the most intense of the night and I didn't want Rachel to worry. It was an intense match and it took me a hell of a lot to come out of that match the winner. Rachel saw how exhausted I was when I returned home that night. She was happy I was once again Cruiserweight Champion but she was obviously hiding her worry. 'I thought I told you not to watch it.' I said slightly disappointed that she had ignored my request. 'I know you did but it was a big match for you and I just wanted to make sure you were okay. What I saw Adam, really scared me, you were bashed over the head with steel chairs, your body was twisted in ways that it shouldn't have been.' 'But I came out okay didn't I? I have told you how brutal some matches can get.' 'But what if some of the matches get to brutal and you end up paralysed or worse dead?' 'Sweetheart, that only happens if you don't know what you are doing in that ring. Or if someone is actually out to kill you. I know what I am doing in that ring and I don't think I have made enemies that want to kill me just yet.' 'Just promise you will be careful for me and our baby.' 'I promise,' I said as I hugged her tightly again, 'And from now on I am going to win every match for our child and for us. With you in my heart I can over come anyone.' You hear that Jackson? You may be a monster, you may be a vicious S.O.B but at Evolution you better be prepared for the fight of you life, because you are about to step in the ring with a man who has no fear, a man called... Adam Myers!
