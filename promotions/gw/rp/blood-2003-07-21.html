Blood

21 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/21/03 The scene fades back in to a dark room feintly lit by a small glow at the end of a long dark hallway. The cameraman takes a step and the floor sounds off a thundering creek! The cameraman stops in his tracks. After a few seconds of waiting and nothing happening the cameraman begins to move once again. Slowly he makes his way down the long dark hallway, and reaches a doorway. The feint light is seen to be a small wax candle. The cameraman takes a step through the doorway and over the threshold to see that the candle is sitting on a small wooden table in the middle of a small room. The room's walls are cement and grey. The floor is wood like the hallway, and is warped and rotted in spots. The room is not lit all that well even though the candle is in the room. However a staircase can be seen by the camera. The sound of a door opening rings through the small room, and footsteps can be heard coming down the stairs. Although it can't be seen all that clearly, a womanly figure can be made out through the darkness. Two long tan legs enter the light, covered by a short red silk skirt. Just above the skirt a small naval ring in the shape of a 'PLAYBOY' bunny can be seen. Up from the naval ring is a tight white t-shirt covering two velupisious breasts. Across the chest of the t-shirt the word 'STAR' in big bold gold letters can be read. As the woman makes her way completely down the stairs her face is revealed, and it can now be seen the woman is STAR. 'I really don't think right now is a good time to come and try and talk to ICE. He doesn't appear to be in the best of moods. Maybe you should come back tomarrow or something.' 'Look, I don't want to be here as much as Ice doesn't want me here, but Ms. McCoy told me if I don't get something from him, I'll be fired.' Star looks around nervously, and begins to fidgit with her left earlobe. She then begins to nibble on her bottom lip, before letting out a small sigh. The cameraman walks past her and takes a couple steps up the stairs. Just then Ice's voice is heard, yet he cannot be seen. 'I don't believe you heard the lady correctly. I believe she said it wasn't a good idea to come and talk to me. Tomarrow may be a better time. Is this job that important to you? Well are you willing to risk your well being, and maybe even your life to get some comments from me? Well, are you?' The angered tone in Ice's voice stops the cameraman in his tracks. He nervously begins to look around the room, trying to find where Ice's voice is coming from. The cameraman slowly begins to walk back down the stairs. Star shakes her head and quickly walks over to the door. She slams the door shut and locks it, just as the cameraman steps off of the last step on the staircase. 'What the hell are you doing?! I'm gonna take your advice and come back tomarrow.' 'Sorry. You wanted your story, now you are stuck until Ice says he is done with you. I tried to warn you.' Star shrugs her shoulders and leans back against the door. Her nipples pertruding from the white cloth that keeps them from being seen by the world. The cameraman hurrily makes his way over towards Star and raises his right hand as if to strike her. 'I wouldn't do that if I were you. You are in enough of trouble the way it is now. I don't suggest getting yourself into anymore.' The cameraman slowly lowers his hand and Star smirks at him, almost in a taunting fashion. Ice's voice is then heard again. 'You want a story? You want my comments? Is that why you're here?' Ice steps out from the shadows and out from behind the staircase. Blood is still covering his face, and parts of his body. His long blond hair is tinted red from the blood that has been mixed in with it. His demeanor is not the demeanor he usually carries. His strides are long and slow. His face although filled with anger has a calm steady look to it. His cold icy blue eyes pierce through the camera lens, and the cameraman. Ice walks past the cameraman and makes his way to the door where Star is standing. Ice brushes her left cheek with the back of his right hand before turning back to the camera. Ice inhales deeply and cracks his neck from side to side. Slowly he closes his eyes, but reopens them just as soon as they close. The look of anger still resides on his face, but again and eerie calmness resides along with the anger. Star slowly brings her left hand to Ice's face and begins to stroke his cheek, as she begins to speak. Ice stops her in mid sentence by placing his index finger on her lips. Ice looks deeply into her eyes, and slowly turns back to the camera. His finger never leaves Star's lips as he stares intently into the camera lens. Silence fills the room for what seems like an eternity. The candle light flickers, making the low dim of the room grow even feinter. Ice pulls his hand away from Star's lips and slowly pulls the blood tinted hair out from in front of his face. 'The blood that stains my body and my hair. The blood that runs in my veins. It was meant for this. This life I live day after day, not for this company, not for those fans, but for me, and me alone. The days go by ever so slowly in this life, yet the years go by every so quickly. Day after day I've lived my life to be the best in this business, and soon I will be the best. It all starts with Deacon Holmes. Deacon, you are the begining, but you are nowhere near being the end.' Ice pulls Star away from the door and unlocks it. Ice then pulls the door open. Star walks over towards the candle and Ice follows. Ice looks down at the flicker flame then at the camera. Ice leans over and blows out the candle. Fade to black.
