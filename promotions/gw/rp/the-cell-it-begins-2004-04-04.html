The Cell: It Begins

4 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 04, 2004 The Cell: It Begins Prologue: On the past Global Wrestling Pay-Per-View Clash of the Titans, Jesse Jamester’s girlfriend Nikki had been gruesomely attacked. Jesse had left the arena in the ambulance to make sure his girlfriend was okay. It was rumored the two would get married but Jesse wanted to ensure that he had the time to be with her. However, the time had come oh too soon. The woman Jesse wanted to marry had been killed. In the hospital she died at approximately 12:23 A.M. Jesse wept for many hours after that. The doctors dared not to move the behemoth Jamester in his time of misery and despair. Though someone else did. The police. They told Jesse that he was the number one suspect for nobody saw anyone but Jesse near the accident and as well the questioned Global Wrestling employees stated they believed Jesse would do it. Along with that was the past history of Jesse’s mental illness of multi-personalities. Though the illness had been cured and was only a temporary thing do to stress, the police pinpointed Jesse as the suspect who was most capable. So Jesse was put under temporary arrest for his hands had scars left on them from past offenses and with this being a possible new one, the police had no choice but to cuff him. He was to spend the time it took to find the killers in a cell at the Mississippi penitentiary. However being that he was not yet found guilty he could attend Global Wrestling events, including his Evolution match with Josh for the #1 contender ship of the world title. It Begins: The sliding steel bar doors slammed shut one by one as the behemoth Jesse Fristik was escorted to his cell. Wearing handcuffs that were chained to a pair of ankle cuffs, he was being tied up quite well for the men who escorted him were very terrified. Two officers on each side held Jesse by each arm, and though Jesse didn’t sport an orange suit, he was still in jail, as he would be until the case was solved. The walls were quite too well known to Jesse, for this wasn’t the first time he’d seen the bars from the inside out. Almost a year before he’d been arrested for attempted murder, and was sentenced to three years in the pen. That is until he helped out in a devastating prison crime takedown leaving three men dead, two injured, and the prison warden locked behind bars in Arizona. It’d been so long, and though Jesse hadn’t been here for a while he remembered everything to an unfriendly eye. ’It’s been so damn long. Every time I think of this place I have the urge to rip my hair out. The bad memories surge through my body like drugs, they just take such a deep hold of my emotions. I sit here nearly crying over the blood I’ve seen spilt, over the rage I’ve seen dealt, and the little things that could have stopped me from coming here in the beginning. If I’d only just thought…’ Jesse is a strong man, someone you could call emotionally unbalanced. His strengths flood his body when his emotions are strong. Such as adrenaline, frustration, anger, and sometimes guilt or sorrow. When these emotions are strong enough to push Jesse over the edge, he does just that. He’s never been able to control them, yet sometimes is lucky to. In his eyes you could see those emotions tossing and turning as he sat there on the bed looking down at the floor. His long tender brown hair laid to rest on his shoulders as he thought. ’I’ve dealt with so god damn much, it feels as if I am going to explode. First dies my wife, now dies my girlfriend. Who next, me? I can’t deal with this as I use to. I’m thirty-five years old and I’m a working singles man with a daughter I have to support and a heart that beats just like all the rest of the world. If the stress doesn’t kill me then the gun I put to my head just may.’ It hurts Jesse to even think a man such as Josh thinks he can win a match of such caliber as this one they will have on Evolution. Josh is a very talented wrestler, and though small can and will beat the biggest men, but to think he can beat a veteran such as Jamester? That takes someone very special, and Jesse has proved that. Beyond every man’s normal abilities comes a certain special power or capability that is brought out in the climax points of their lives. Jesse has hit a climax in his life, and though yet to prove it, he shows it with his energy, his abilities, and his focus. That’s why he will defeat Josh, that is why he will go onto Wrestlecade 5, and that’s why he will go onto win the World Heavy Weight title from ICE… The Reminder of Victory (Narrator Change) This prison Jesse was in was no ordinary prison. It was more of a facility to help those who have been down or out to be rejuvenated. Jesse being somebody, whom has had past problems with the law, was sent to be examined by the psychologists who specialize in this area. However being that it was a place of more rejuvenating the penitentiary had a weight room where Jesse requested to go. When he entered the room his eyes opened wide, he was amazed. It had everything. From benches to squat racks to dead lift zones, all the way down to the barbell workout area that occupied it’s own space of a ten-foot diameter. Jesse began stretching, and when done jumped on his squat. Lifting people up and delivering the Jwister was no easy task. Whether it be a 200 lb man to a 500 lb man, the momentum of his finisher required his best mental and health performance. Loading up 350 lbs on the rack of squats, Jesse began to warm up. It’s troublesome being the best. Whether you are the champion of a wrestling world, or the champion chess player in the world, its tough to deal with stress. I’ve always found that to be my most difficult dilemma. Whether I be wrestling, hunting, or just being at home trying to get through life, it’s always been my puzzle of paranoia. I have dealt with pain, it’s no easy task to defeat, but isn’t as strong as stress. Even when the blood goosed from my open wounds after the barbwire bat was swung at by face. I know what pain is… Whether it be the pain I was covered in after I fell from the Lights Out Wrestling arena top onto a car and was in the hospital for 2 months, to the point that my head was pile drove onto a table of flames. I know what pain is… and yet no matter how much I consumed of it, I am sure the stress I have dealt with is much worse. Now was the hard part, doing the maximum weight you could. Jesse was hesitant; he knew this would be a one-time thing. Just like the match with Josh, he had to focus, he had to make sure whatever he did was to his finest performance. Loading up a new maximum weight of 575 lbs, Jesse got under the bar and stretched his neck and his legs. He looked into the mirror staring at a replica of himself. His eyes resolute on achieving this maximum weight as he stares down at his feet and position them just right and then stare straight up at the ceiling. It was do or die… A matter Jamester always did. Breathing deeply in, he took a few steps back from the rack and stared up still. He shook his head sideways, his legs trembling slightly, as he knew it was time to do this. Slowly angling down, his body held the bar on his shoulders as his calves and thighs made a ninety-degree angle at the knee. Not stopping at all he screamed as his legs, feet, hips and his upper body jolted up with all the energy in his body escaping him. He had done it. The fear of dropping it had never crossed his mind, and that is why he’d completed the weight. ’This may be the hardest match of my life… or may not. It really doesn’t matter. Because honestly, I just want to prove I’m the better man. When the pressure is on you as it was on me this past weekend, I showed exactly what I could be. When I defeated three other people in a global title match. It was not only the most demanding match I’d ever been in, but also it was a match that tested me as a man. How many people can say they’ve taken a beating from a woman, a man that’s over seven foot tall, and the best technical wrestler in the world, yet still say they won? Not many. And that’s why even if I somehow lose to Josh, it won’t matter to me. I’m the best… Life can’t get much sweeter than this…’ Jesse smiled in the mirror as he dropped the squat bar on the rack. 575 lbs, another goal accomplished. It was just too sweet. The Confrontation Jesse sat in a chair, nothing out of the ordinary, just a plain random chair. However in front of him were three people, two men, both wearing short sleeve shirts, one with a pocket, and the other wearing a tie. The man on the left had a white t-shirt, the other wearing a black. Both whom were conservative men who took pride in their work. However in the middle of them sat a woman, a very distinguished yet sophisticated woman. She was conservative as the men were, but not to the point that she enjoyed her work. She looked as if she wanted to tell every man on the planet to go die, for her face had the expression of a stuck up rich woman. She snarled at Jesse as she began to write down on the piece of paper everything he said about what his alibi was. ’Okay, this is all I can tell you about the attack. I was coming out to the backstage area to go out and wrestle in my match. Then out of nowhere this man, a global wrestling staff employee tells me their was an accident involving my girlfriend. Before I could even hear him say the ending I just jolted down the hallway I heard the noise. Sirens were going off, and I just jumped in with Nikki and left. It was all gut instinct.’ ’Where were you before that?’ ’I was in my locker room as I am before any match.’ ’Can anyone vouch for you being there?’ ’Of course not, I’m always alone before my matches… It’s a ritual of mine. As I am alone to focus, I prepare for the pain and exhaustion my body will go through so that even if I am to be hurt, I can deal with it.’ ’In other words you’re as guilty as charged?’ ’I wouldn’t kill a woman I was going to marry!’ ’Sure… You kill her, get her out of the way after you win the big title, and then laugh in the end when you say you didn’t do it and get away with it.’ Jesse’s face fills with wrath, his eyes narrowing down as his teeth clench, and his face begins to tense. He stands up and grabs the chair only to toss it at the black window across the room. The two men run at him, but he doesn’t care, he just forearms the one man and pushes the other aside. Slamming his hands down on the table he stares at the woman writing the actions and dialogue that takes place. His head facing down at the table and with a whimper like a dog does he speaks, only to the faintest hum. A tear drops from the tip of his nose and lands on the edge of the table as he says it. ’I… am… innocent!’ At that moment the tear rolls off the table and splatters on the floor now being heard as the woman stops writing.
