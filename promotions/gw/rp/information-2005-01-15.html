Date: 01/15/05 Tribulations of Drake, Continued? -Information- The storm never let up and continues to drench the city. People struggle to keep dry anyway they can while going about their daily lives. I didn’t bother to wear a raincoat or bring an umbrella; I don’t even own one anyway, because I enjoy the rain. Wesley came to my apartment early this morning and told me there was someone who wanted to meet me. I tried to decline but he quickly told me they have information. Needless to say I didn’t think twice and now here we sit, driving to meet a man known only as “King of the Underground,” which is kind of a cheesy name if you ask me. All I know is he holds fighting tournaments in his underground club and it was clear to me that I was walking into dangerous grounds. But if he could help me find Katie I didn’t care what I was about to face. The one thing that puzzled me is how Wesley came to know someone like this and he told me, “he’s an old friend of my fathers.” I could sense that I was walking into danger and it wasn’t going to be easy. But this was for Katie so I had to go through with it! Gazing out the window I felt as though I could hear ever raindrop popping on the dark, damp cement. It seemed time was slowing down before my very eyes’and it was beautiful. I knew that danger awaited me but for the first time in my life I was actually looking forward to it, which I know sounds a little strange. I felt like I was ready to take on the world, “go to hell and back” so to speak, and was feeling more relaxed than I had been in a while. I could feel it. This was the turning point I needed and would soon be holding her in my arms. For several minutes I didn’t have a care in the world. But I awoke from this comfort zone when Wesley informed me we had arrived. We pulled into a parking garage and there where plenty of empty spaces. We drove deeper into the lot and finally the car came to a squeaking halt. “You need to oil those breaks,” I informed him stepping out and breathing in the morning air. Rain poured like a miniature waterfall from the upper lever of the garage. We entered through a near by door into a small corridor. Our footsteps echoed as we made our way through this cold place. The dark tiles made our shoes squeak and the walls where damp. We made a corner and at the end of this corridor where two large men guarding a door. They wore black shoes, pants, and tank tops revealing their massive arms. One was white the other was black. “Is this the friend you told us about?” One of the men asked in a deep voice. “The one and only” Wesley responded. He guided me through the door. “Sorry about your cousin,” the other man said to me as I went through. I acknowledged him but didn’t reply. Dark cold cement was all that surrounded us as we made our way down a wide flight of stairs that ran from one wall to the other. I could hear water dripping all around us. After going down nine or ten steps, we came to another small door and made our way through? This place was dark except for the red and blue lights spinning from the rafters. A head of me, just beyond the dancing crowed, was a bright spotlight aiming straight down. It seemed to be moving. It wasn’t until my eyes adjusted to the darkness I realized the floor its self was revolving around a large mat in the center of the club, which explains the spotlight, where a fight was taking place. Some spectators enjoyed the competition and were betting their hard earned money hoping to make a decent profit. Others preferred to dance and have a good time. I watched as people dirty danced and kissed passionately. A few women where kissing multiple men as well as each other and flashing the fighters on the mat. As we walked up a small flight of stairs I spotted four more gorgeous women dancing in separate cages, which hung over each corner of the mat. We made our way behind one section of the circular bar that stretched around the entire second floor and through a dark red curtain. On the other side was a small staircase lit by blue aluminum effect lights, the same type you’d see on a street racer. Once at the top I found my self in a small circular area over looking the club. The carpet looked to be dark red and was very soft. The chairs and couches matched this carpet and it looked almost like a small living room. The wall surrounding us had a large fish tank placed inside filled with crystal clear water and all sorts of exotic fish. As I looked around I spotted a few more guards and many beautiful women all wearing silk. Some of the ladies had male friends as well. I was lead to the couch just in front of the railing. As I grew closer to the railing I spotted a man wearing an expensive dark suit. His undershirt was made from blue silk and had a matching tie. He had long dark gray hair with a beard to match. It was to dark to see the color of his eyes. He sat drinking some sort of alcohol, it was a glowing white, and was accompanied by two of the many beautiful women who were all over him. “Well, well” he started? Man: This must be the famous Jonathan I’ve heard so much about. I gotta say? He started to laugh Man: “You’re not quite what I was expecting. And at the same time exactly what I was expecting. My place is magnificent don’t you think” So much time and money and now I have the best club in Los Angeles. He gloated taking a sip of his drink. Man: And I have yet to see one person argue other wise. So tell me Jonathan, are you lonely? If so I have many girls who can change that. He cuddles the girls next to him. Man: Would you like a sample? Drake: I’ll pass. Man: Very well. So. On to business. Wesley tells me that someone close to you has been taken. I believe her name is Katie? So you’ve come here for my assistance. As matter of fact I do have information for you but before I give it to you I have a simple request. I’ve been told you’re quite the martial artist? I am a big fan of martial arts, big fan, and as you can see I even hold tournaments in the middle of my club. All I ask is that you demonstrate your skills and I will tell you what you want to know. Drake: I don’t spar. Man: You won’t be sparing. You’ll be demonstrating. Perhaps some of the younger fighters can learn a thing or two. Drake: But you’ll tell me what I wanna know regardless? Man: Yes. But I have much information for you. It depends on how much of it you would like hear. It is entirely your decision of course I won’t force you. I let out a sigh trying to restrain myself from beating out of him. But I was expecting something like this to happen. Again, this was for Katie so I was ready. Drake: Fine I’ll play your game. But if I find out you don’t know anything and were just using me as entertainment, I’ll break your legs! He laughs finishing his drink Man: Than it sounds to me like we have a deal. I turned and headed downstairs for the mat. Wesley followed behind me apologizing but I didn’t pay any attention. By the time I made it to the mat the tournament had been brought to a halt and the floor was clear just for me. I took of my trench coat and my shirt handing them both to Wesley. Lucky for me I was wearing the proper pants for such an occasion. I stepped out to the middle of the mat keeping my mind focused preparing myself for the fight ahead? To be continued?
