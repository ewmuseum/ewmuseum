It's a celebration

27 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/27/04 There was a spirit of celebration in the Dark Horse Saloon. The Upper Echelon, which had plagued Global Westling for the past year, appeared to be on its last legs. Their leader Maverick Reno went missing after the end of Master of the Mat. John Zero was sidelined with a broken leg. And yet another blow was struck earlier in the evening when Deacon Holmes, without question the most popular member of TUE, was suspended indefinitely and stripped of the Cruiserweight title. This came as the result of Deacon's growing anger at Brick, whose mind games had been affecting him over the past month or so. It all blew up into a physical confrontation in the ring, involving Brick, Deacon and Bubba McCoy. 'Celebration' by Kool and the Gang was playing on the jukebox. Sitting at the bar were Brick, Ely Braxton, Gunner Addison and El Serpenti. 'Who would have thought Deacon would have shot himself in the foot the way he did?' said Brick. Even though Brick, Ely and Serpenti lost their matches that evening, they still felt like celebrating. 'Seriously, this worked out better than I planned.' 'Well, good riddance, I say,' added Ely. 'Couldn't happen to a nicer guy.' Truth be told, Ely despised Deacon as much as Brick did. Probably more so in the past month or so, because of the constant insults towards Gunner. He even went so far as to beat him to a pulp, even though it cost Brick a qualifying match in the Master of the Mat tournament. 'I have to admit, my friend,' said El Serpenti, 'the way you handled Deacon was genius.' 'Yeah, but I wonder if Bubba thought it was genius. I can't help but think part of him blames me for what ahppened in the ring.' 'Hey, you said it yourself. Deacon shot himself in the foot. Deacon decided to suckerpunch Bubba. Deacon decided to hit Bubba in the head with the belt. He's a cocky, loudmouthed little bastard, like the rest o' TUE. An' I for one won't miss 'im!' 'Yeah, but maybe I went too far. Then again, it is good to know I got some payback on Deacon, one way or another. So, any word on Reno?' 'Not a thing an' again, I say good riddance to the son of a bitch!' 'Amen.' 'Of course, it is hard to imagine that he would be up and out after what happened at the end of Master of the Mat,' said Serpenti. 'You mean with his boy de Sarret? Perfect justice after what and them other punk bitches did to Brandi.' 'OK, Brick,' said Gunner, 'I have to know. Did you really sleep with Rhaiza, or did you make that up to get to Deacon?' 'That's a good question,' Ely added. 'I plead the fifth.' 'Oh no, you don't drop a bombshell like the one you dropped and then just get all tight-lipped. Oh no. You need to spill, my friend.' 'I plead the fifth.' 'Oh, come on, Brick!' Brick sighed. 'OK, fine. You really want to know? Well, the truth is...' Just then, the patrons all shouted 'HEY!!' Brick and the others turned around to see Bubba had come in. He walked right up to the bar. 'Jack Daniels. Leave the bottle.' 'Hey, Bubba!' said Ely. 'Hey, Bubba, are you okay?' 'Why wouldn't I be?' 'Well, there was the drama with Deacon. Plus, Christina making trouble.' 'Well, I know you guys want to celebrate TUE being gone. Hell, I'd like to join in, but we all know it might be a long shot. There ain't no guarantees that Reno's gone for good. Zero just has a broken leg. Deacon's probably the only one I can guarantee will be out for a good long while.' 'Right, so the only ones left are Tyler Pierce and Christina.' 'Don't forget that secretary Naria Xiou.' 'Please, like they're a problem.' 'Look, I'll be fine, okay. You might want to concern yourself with Whiplash.' 'That's good news at any rate. I figured I wouldn't have anything to do for the evening and I just couldn't have that. Not in my hometown.' 'Say, anyone know what's keepin' Donny? I told him we'd be here waitin' for him. Besides, I know he wants to celebrate gettin' one over on Train.' 'Now that was genius.' 'I think Donny's having a little private celebration of his own. Him and Alexis.' 'Well, let's just relax and enjoy the rest o' the evening. Because we might not get another night like it.' 'Amen.'
