Evolution...

3 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/3/03 (As the scene fades in we see Sean Price leaving the emergency room of the hospital in downtown Dallas, Texas. Sean has a sling around his right arm and a band aide over his left eye. As he slowly approaches the TUE limo waiting for him a voiceover is heard.) What does it mean to lose a big piece of what something was built up on? Does it mean that we are witness to the beginning of the end? Or perhaps does it mean that what we are now apart of is an end to a beginning? (The back door to the limo is opened up by one of the TUE body guards allowing Sean slowly get in the limo. The body guard shuts the door behind Sean and than gets in the front passenger seat of the limo and momentarily the limo pulls away and heads towards the air port. Within moments the camera shows Price sitting in the limo with a bottle of water in his hand.) “Personally last night was a disgusting display for the TUE. Sure Johnny and Deacon won, big ups to them. But myself and Tyler we were screwed, plain and simple.” A look of pure disgust and hatred comes across Sean’s face as he takes a sip of his Poland Spring Water. “Last night Salaok and Quartz decided that it would be their last duty in GW to make a fool of the TUE. And as sad as it is for me to say it, they did it. The two of them screwed TUE out of the tag team titles. Last nights match was not 3 teams battling it out for the titles, it was TUE vs True Evil and the team of Angus the Bull and Salamone Vation. From the second that match started we were out numbered, but not out talented.” “We lost, that’s the facts. But we were not beaten. Not by a long shot. We were not beaten because we can’t be beaten. It’s that simple. TUE is not just a stable, its not just an organization and its not just a banner that a group of guys wrestle under. It is the single greatest collection of wrestlers to grace the halls of professional wrestling. And last night was a testament to that.” “At any given time, in any given place the TUE can hand a beating the likes of which the world has never seen to the likes of the Nevermore or the Young Bloods, and both stables knew that. And that is why it took both stables to take us down”.” ??Down but not out. It took 8 plus men to momentarily stop the 4 members of the TUE. It has been shown in the past such as last weeks Evolution that the Nevermore can not hold a candle to us. The Young Blood can’t hold a candle to Santa and his Elves. But together they make a team that no one can beat. They knew it, and last night we learned it.” “But you see last night was a once in a life time deal because after last night, after the retiring of the tag team of True Evil and Derek Frost”.the Nevermore are just that. Nevermore, and the Young Bloods although they never technically reformed are also nothing more than a memory.” “To borrow a phrase from my friend John Zero’the memory remains.” “Sure the memory of the Nevermore and the YB will forever be in the record books of the GW. But they are now history. And just like everything else associated with the word history”.eventually it will be forgotten.” “Tomorrow when you all wake up and you look at the roster of the GW, you won’t see the Nevermore and you won’t see the Young Bloods. You will see one stable, one group in GW worthy of even being considered a stable, and that is the TUE.” “It’s just as well that both stables are no longer here because if they were I can assure you that they would both pay dearly for their actions at this PPV. They may of gotten the best of TUE for one night, but the fact of the matter is that now they are no longer around. You can listen to their reasons for leaving or you can listen to the truth.” “There are two sides to every story. The truth and the lie. I am here, as I have always been to tell you the truth. And that is that Frost, and True Evil did not retire because they have had their run in GW and can do no more in the fed. They left because for the first time in GW history they realized that they are no longer the best the fed has to offer. True Evil especially because they knew that the Nevermore was no longer going to be the powerful force it once was and so like any good cowards would do, they packed their bags and hit the road. A road that will be long and bleak because no one wants washed up garbage such as Derek Frost and True Evil.” “But there is no need to worry, because you see like any good ship captain will tell you, once you jettison the garbage, the ride will be much smoother. And that is exactly how it is going to be here in the GW, in what I liked to call the TUE era of GW.” “A little while ago the question was asked “What does it mean to lose a big piece of what something was built up on?” “And to be honest with you, the future I see ahead of us, is an amazing one. At least from the stand point of the TUE. You are all going to be witness to the end of the beginning. For four years the GW was nothing but a joke, with a joke for a stable running the show. But now, now everything changes. Everything you thought you knew about the GW will change in the blink of an eye.” “The Nevermore are no longer around, Bubba McCoy is no longer around. The TUE is the GW, plain and simple.” “You know its ironic that the name of GW’s weekly show is Evolution, because this coming week you will see exactly how GW is going to evolve’the TUE is the evolution of the GW.” (The scene fades out as Sean Price continues in his ride to the Dallas Airport.)
