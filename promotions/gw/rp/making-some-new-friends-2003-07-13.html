Making Some New Friends

13 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/13/03 (As the scene fades in we see Sean Price walking out of his hotel room in downtown Dallas. He is decked out in a black Italian suit with sunglasses on. The door to the elevator opens up the second Price gets to the door. He slowly walks in, looks at the elevator operator and signals to him for the first level. The operator pushes the button marked “L” for Lobby. A few seconds pass and the doors open, Price waits a moment and scans the lobby before taking his first few steps out of the elevator and out into the open. Within seconds of exiting the elevator three men all dressed in suits walk up to Sean.) “Sean Price I presume.” (Sean slowly lowers his glasses down his nose but not taking them off. He looks directly at the man and stares at him for a few seconds. His eyes move to the left and right, slowly scanning the men at his flanks. Price takes a step back and then reaches into his pocket. All three men make a quick move but Price motions for them to calm down.) “Rule one’never presume anything. When you presume you die. You of all people should know that. And yes, I am indeed the man your looking for.” The man directly in front of Price moves in a step and then points towards the front door of the hotel, “Come with us please.” “I think I should wait for my body guards to arrive first.” The man to Sean’s left walks next to Sean and puts his arm around Sean’s shoulder, “no need for that. There is plenty of protection where we are going.” Sean brushes the man’s arm off of his shoulder and stares him down for a moment, “I’ll take your word for it.” (Two of the men flank Price while the third walks in front of them, taking lead of the small group. They approach the front door of the hotel but the bell boy opens the doors for them. As they get outside of the a black BMW with pitch blank tinted windows pulls up in front of them. The man at the head of the group gets in the passenger side of the car. The man to Sean’s left walks around the car and gets in the back, the man to Sean’s right opens the back side door allowing Price to get in. Sean takes one last look around before slowly lowering himself into the car. After doing the man holding the door open shuts it. The BMW slowly pulls away revealing another black BMW with the same make and model pulling up right behind it. The man gets into the second BMW and then both pull out of the hotel driveway and onto the high way.) Sean takes the sun glasses off, opens his jacket and slips them into the inside coat pocket. “Where exactly are we going.” “You will find out in due time. Just sit back and enjoy the ride.” **Normally this sort of thing bothers me, being in the dark and all. But I figured with this type of business that this is all just routine and that I was in perfectly good hands. A few moments passed where there was nothing but silence in the car. About 25 minutes passed before we finally arrived at our destination. It appeared to be an abandoned building, a big sign above the main entrance said “Old Brewery”. The wood on the exterior of the building was all rotted, and damaged due to rain over the years. But I guess when business such as this is going down, an abandoned building is the last place you would expect the police to check.** (As the two BMWs pull up to the main entrance of the building the two huge doors open up and the cars slowly pull in and then pull off to the side. The car containing Price empties out first, followed by the other car. The men all surround Price, encircling him as though he is a prisoner. Suddenly a little opening in the circle emerges and a older man, bald on the top but with white hair on the sides of his head and glasses walks up to Sean.) “Mr.Price, the Don will see you now.” **Here it is, the long awaited meeting between myself and the Don. For weeks the Don’s men had hounded me about meeting with their boss, and up until recently I had no intention to carry on with their requests. But after the recent leaving of one of my closest friends, Tyler Pierce, I have found myself searching for answers. Or maybe it’s the not the answer I need, but rather the question. Either way, the time has come. The meeting will take place.** (The man takes Sean up a set of metal steps into an office that oversees the entire building. As they enter the office we see a man standing in the back of the office looking out his huge window that over sees the floor below. He continues to look out the window as if he is ignoring the fact that Sean has just entered.) “Here he is boss.” (The man that was looking out the window slowly turns around to be revealed as Don Salamone, a well known and very feared mafia Don from New York City. He walks over to Sean and extends his hand. Sean looks at it for a moment before reciprocating the hand shake.) “Thanks Paulie.” (The Don motions for Paulie to exit the office and so Paulie does, slowly closing the door behind himself. Paulie then stands infront of the door as you can see the shadow of his figure through the glass on the door.) “Hello Sean. I am glad you could make it here. I know you are a busy man, much like myself. You can have a seat.” “If it’s all the same to you, id prefer to stand.” “Then stand, I don’t care. I was just offering you a seat. Now, like I said, I am a very busy man, so if we could get down to business that would be appreciated.” “I’m all ears.” (The scene fades out as Price and the Don begin their long awaited discussion.)
