Family Greetings: Thicker than Water

undated

Date: #2 High Noon Thank God, the calm had returned. Train had felt good the whole day, despite all the pressures that were bearing down on his chest, threatening to collapse his rib cage under the shear weight of it all. Deacon Holmes and Brick... Christina McCoy and The M.O.B... Under any other circumstances it might have been too much to take. Especially after that Terrelle's errand boy Micheal Greeniso blindsided Train...showing up in the last place Train could've expected... Threatening his family. Cocksucker...he should've cut his balls off and shoved 'em down the bastard's throat. That dick licking muthafucka was lucky the cops were there. Too much secruity at the airport nowadays for that kinda shit and he knew it.... Bitch.... It ate at him...the whole way to the gate. But it all melted away... the second she wrapped her arms around his neck. Joanne, Anthony's little sister, was a sight for sore eyes. The world seemed to slow to a crawl, her approach unfolding in slow motion as she emerged from the airplane gate and raced over to her brother, greeting him with a hug that attempted to make up for time missed. It had been forever since anyone had called him by him by his God given name. For Anthony it felt good. Not to have to be who was most of the time. The tough guy... the monster... the blood thirsty bastard... himself.... Train. If there was one worry Anthony harbored before his arrival to pick her up it was the fear he and his sister would no longer be connected like they used to be. He'd been here in Dallas for months. Back in Chicago she was the one he could always talk to. The one who understood him when no one else did. The one he could always trust. Blood. To his delight it didn't happen. The walked along the corridors of the airport like they were back on the streets of the neighborhood in Chicago, reminising about the old days and about his adventures deep in the heart of Texas. Of course he couldn't enjoy it fully. Fear of disconnect was the fear he had before he picked her up. Now, even as he walked smiling, not letting on he had a care in the world, he could only partially enjoy the situation because his eyes were constantly scanning the horizon, scanning for the dark cloud of The M.O.B. over it. This time was prescious to him and he couldn't even trully soak it in....that was some other shit he was gonna have to pay those muthafuckas back for. And they better believe he'll be doing that shit in spades. But luckily the prescence of blood soothed him, the calm returning to him. Even as he looked over both their shoulders he somehow knew it would all be alright. To the baggage claim, out to the car and to the Olive Garden... no problems. No tails or uninvited company. Now they sat, the remnants of a hearty meal in front of them, the place a buzz with friendly conversation and service, the clinking of porclean and silverware coliding lightly filling the air. Every guy knows it's about nap time after a big meal like that and Train was at least day dreaming. His head was off somewhere...wishing the circumstances were different. It's not as if this wasn't enough, but he wanted more. Deep down...he wished she was here. His sister was great, but... 'You should give her a call.' Joanne stated in her soft, but forceful voice interrupting venture into deep water. 'Who?' Tony replied as if he didn't know. He got this lecture every time he saw Joanne. She was the peacemaker. That was her job in the family. She was good at it...but she might has well been Colin Powell trying to negotiate a peace between the Palestinians and the Israelis...that shit wasn't likely to happen. Not that Tony didn't want it to happen...but there was one thing standing in the middle, keeping the two sides seperated.... Train. 'You know who. I know you're thinking about her. Every time you get all quiet and staring off into space I know you want to talk to Momma.' 'Yeah well...too bad she don't wanna talk to me.' 'Yes she does. She's just too proud to admit it. Lord if you two ever figure out how much alike you are I swear you'll be able to get over this shit.' 'Whatever.' 'Tony... can't you just apologize?' 'Apologize?' Anthony inquired. 'Yes Tony, apologize.' 'What the fuck for?' Train replied with a hint of anger. 'Huh? What the hell I'ma apologize for Joanne?! What, putting food on the table? For keeping the lights on? Oh, I know... I should apologize for paying the rent and keeping a roof over our heads, right?' 'Don't play stupid Tony! You know why...you ripped her heart out. You out there playing games on the corner...after what happened to Leon.' 'Hey....' Train said, the hint of anger completely gone now. 'Don't talk to me about Leon, alright? She ain't the only one that hurt when he died. Couldn't everybody just collapse about the shit. Somebody had to be there and keep real life under control.... the world wasn't gone just stop cuz we hurt. Somebody had to take care of business.' 'I know that Tony... that doesn't change shit though! She loses one son in the streets, so what do you do? You head out into the streets. What did you think, she was gonna jump for joy or something?' 'Jo-jo... You just got here, aight? I don't wanna fight about this shit again....' Train stated plainly, attempting to put an end to the controversy that had been boiling for years. 'I know she hurt, but what about me, huh? Not only did I have to bury my brother, but I had to watch my momma, the glue of this family fall to damn pieces over the shit. Now everybody might of understood that we was hurting, but that don't change the fact they wanted they God damn money. Somebody had to go out there and get the shit. That's what the fuck I did and I ain't apologizing for a God damn thing. It ain't like I didn't want to, but I didn't have the time to fall apart.' Train locked eyes with his sister that just looked at him with that look. That loving looked that loved and ached for him...but that blamed him for the schism of the James family. There was nothing that ever shook Train... except that. 'Not that she didn't have a right to break.' Anthony caveated. 'She held us together for so long...endured more than any one person should have to.' There is a pause, a silence that is filled with pain for everyone, even those not involved in the actual conversation. But the Peacemaker isn't one to let that one last long. 'Well...if you ain't gonna call her you better let her know her son's a champ. You ready big bro?' 'You been watching...do I look ready?' 'You been looking pretty good.' 'Pretty good?! Watch you mean pretty good? Pshhhh... I been ready to break Deacon and Brick off... Come hell or high water I'm walking out of that arena with that belt over my shoulder. Fuck all the bullshit.' 'hahaha...ok, ok... I know you're ready. But ummm....' 'What?' 'What about those M.O.B. guys?' There is another silence. Train didn't want to talk about them. But Anthony knew his sister needed to be reassured. 'Don't even sweat that. I got them fools.' 'You sure? Tony... I don't even understand you sometimes. I mean if you wanted to get away from that like you say you do, why in the hell would you get hooked up with cats like them?' 'Sometimes you got to get back in the situation to remember why you wanted out in the first place.' 'Yeah, that's smart...' 'Yeah, yeah, yeah... I got this shit. Trust me. Aight?' 'If you say so.' 'I do. You ready to roll?' 'Yeah.' It didn't take long for Anthony to settle up the check, leaving a $40 tip to help out the waitress and they were on their way. Brother and sister exited the resteraunt and were headed to the car. Anthony was happy to be free.... Out and about, without a care in the world, the comfort of family there to keep him calm. But...underneath the surface...he was always lurking...waiting to take over...waiting to do what he does best... Train always wanted out. And he could do it on a dime... But it wasn't a dime. It was the cherry of the cigarette he saw flare up on the inside of the black Cadillac El Derado parked at the far end of the parking lot that unlocked him... Anthony might not have seen that shit...but Train did. What the fuck is this all about Train wondered for a moment...but it only took him a second to figure it out. Train calmly removed his keys from the pocket of his Sean Jean warmup pants and kept heading for the Navigator as if nothing was wrong. He pressed the button on the key chain and the lights of the Nav gave a quick flash as the locks on the truck audibly popped open. Joanne circles the front of the truck to the passenger side and opens the door, quickly climbing in...all as Train plots his move... These muthafuckas thought they was slick. Train was gonna prove he was slicker. He was gonna put these muthafuckas in their place...and she would never even know. 'Oh shit' Train said. 'What?' Joanne asked. 'Man...I forgot my damn credit card at the damn register.' 'Smooth.' 'Shut your trap woman.' 'haha...whatever...get yo' stuff so we can go.' 'Yeah, yeah... I'ma go back in...just put the key in and turn on the radio.' The normally nimble, agile Train makes a poor throw as he tosses the keys to Joanne. The jingle as they bounce off the passenger side window and tumble to the floor. Nothing agile about that...however, as Joanne instantly leaned down to pick up the errant pass, Train quickly, and subtley slid the holster, .45 and silencer firmly held within, from beneath the seat and tucked it in the front of his pants. 'Nice pass fool.' 'Whatever.' Train said, his mind half elsewhere as he pulled reached behidn the drivers seat to a cargo net attatched to it, retrieving his black leather gloves. 'I'll be right back.' Train closes the drivers door firmly, a moment later hearing the music inside begin to bump in through the customized system within. Train had the advantage, already wearing black as he slid the gloves snuggly into place walking only a little ways before ducking between two cars and into the bushes that lined the back of the parking lot. It only took him a couple of seconds to creep up behind the Caddy. He wasn't sure what he'd find, but the New York liscense plate confirmed his suspicions. Train sliding the holster to his back quickly pulled the .45 and screwed the silencer into place. You wanna play? Let's play muthafuckas. As he casually stolled up on the passenger side he caught the tail end of a conversation...something about where someone went and how 'the boss' would be pissed if they lost him. Tap, tap, tap... 'Don't you know smoking will kill yo' ass?' The car shifted as the two men inside quickly turned, apparently startled by Train's prescence. The driver quickly opened the door in a real state. Train wasn't even sure exactly what had come out of his mouth...he was sure it was dispariging, but it didn't matter. All that mattered was the driver hadn't spotted Train's arm tucked behind his back. As the man exited the car standing, the nickel plated metal of the .45 bounced of his skull like a basket ball off a hardwood floor. As the Greeniso lackey, his cheap, shiny suit blurting out his allegiance like a badge, doubled over, holding his head. He wasn't there for long when Train's knee flew up and caught him square in the nose, Train feeling the cartlidge give way under the force, the small spray of blood flying through the air. The second whip of the pistol put him down for the count though, his body collapsing limply to the ground. The second guy must've been new. As he played this out in his head before the scenario started he saw himself having to shoot the bastard...at least in the kneecaps. But this guy was no soldier...the whole thing shook him. Train quickly slid over the hood of the car to the passenger side as the second man struggled to find the door handle to release himself. By the time he'd figured it out Train was waiting on him. The second man, his hand moving for his gun froze as he popped up, his eyes locking with a cold gaze on the other side of the barrel he was staring down. Train simply reached out and snatched the gun from The mans belt. 'You Greeniso's boys?' 'What?' Click...Train's thumb pulls the hammer back, locking it into firing position. 'Ask me what one more muthafuckin' time.' 'Yes, yes! He...he wanted us to follow you...let him know what you were doing.' 'Aight...well...since you supposed to give him messages and all, why don't you deliver a message for me...' The confiscated gun became a hammer, gripped along the barrel it came crashing into the second man's testicles, instantly crumpling him to the asphalt. 'You tell him if he ever comes near my family again I'll cut his fucking balls off and shove 'em down his throat. Train quickly tossed the gun into the bushes and headed back around the car. The driver begins to stir...at least until Trains foot comes crashing down, driving his face into the ground...he can only moan after that. Train searches for a moment before retrieving his gun and tossing it into the bushes. Train cooly walks to the front of the Caddy, staring at the two downed men for a moment. 'Oh, and...' Pffft, Pffft!!! The front tires of the Caddy bleed air like a struck artery, sinking the front end of the car down at least two feet. 'Don't follow me no more. Shit like that gets on my nerves.' Train quickly surveys the scene, tucking the pistol away. He spots no one. Train rolls back over to his ride. The music billows forth as he opens the driver's side door and hops into his ride. 'You get your card?' Joanne asks. 'I always handle my business.' Train responds. 'You still like peanut butter and chocolate.' Tony asks his little sister. 'You already know the answer to that boy...where my ice cream?!' 'Put your seat belt on.' Tony says. 'Time to head to the store.' The Navigator pulls off into the night as the scene fades to black.
