My movie...my reality...

27 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/27/03 Saturday Night/Sunday Morning Approximately 1:00am (The scene slowly fades in to the Price mansion in Atlanta. It has been a few hours since the sun was seen in the summer sky. The lights that surround the Price mansion are all on lighting up the grounds and more importantly the drive way. As the camera zooms in we see a black Acura with jet black tinted windows slowly going up the driveway. It stops directly infront of the front steps that lead to the front door. The passenger side door opens and out steps Rocco, one of Don Salamone’s men. He walks up the front steps and towards the front door. He presses the intercom botton and waits a few moments before he receives an answer.) A noise is made for a short second and then a female voice comes back over the intercom replying to Rocco. “This is the Price estate, how can I help you?” “Yeah this is Rocco. Can you tell Sean Price that he is needed at his front door.” “Mr.Price is sleeping right now. He doesn’t like being woken up. Is this an emergency?” “It will be for Mr.Price if he doesn’t get down here in 90 seconds.” “Than I shall wake him.” “I thought you would see it my way.” (A few moments pass when the front door finally opens to reveal Sean Price standing there with a robe and shoes on. Behind Price are two TUE body guards. Price reaches over to the wall with his left arm and flicks on a light above the front door. Price’s eyes open up widely as he finally realizes who it is.) “Rocco’its 1 in the morning, what the hell are you doing here?” “The Don needs your services.” “Now?” “No, not now. You are to meet Paulie at the pear on 34th and Willow at 9am today. If you are later’well, lets just say you don’t want to be late.” “9 am today” That’s in 8 hours. I won’t be late, but I mean some notice ahead of time is not to much to ask for it is?” “This job sorta fell into the Don’s lap so to speak. It was not something he planned.” “I can understand that. By the way, haven’t you guys ever heard of a telephone” Its much more convenient.” “The Don doesn’t like using phones. He said they are too easily traced, too many chances for people to know what you are doing.” “You have to respect that.” “Yes you do. Have a good few hours Mr. Price, and remember 9am, at the Pear on 34th and Willow. Paulie will be waiting.” “Thanks Rocco.” (Rocco turns and walks down the front steps and gets back in the car. Price watches as the car exits the gate and then back onto the roads. Sean backs slowly into his house and than shuts the two front doors. Sean walks into the kitchen and grabs a bottle of water. One of the TUE body guards walks upto Sean and sits down next to him at the kitchen counter.) “You gonna go back to sleep boss?” “Not sure if I can Bruno. My heart is racing now. The don of a Mafia has just asked me to perform a job for him. My chance to show the family what I am made of has finally arrived. And they order is given to me in the Mafioso style. It’s almost like a movie.” “True, but remember boss. Not all movies have happy endings.” “Thanks for the advice Bruno. But there is a difference between the life I lead and the a movie.” “What’s that boss?” “The ending to a movie is written well before it begins. My life has yet to be decided. With each choice I make my life alters in one way or another. A week ago I made a choice to become a mobster. That has changed my life greatly, there is no doubt there. Tonight I make the choice to perform my first job as a member of the Salamone family. Another choice, with another alteration in the time line of my life. Whether this life has a happy ending or not all depends on me and the choices I make.” “That is true buss.” “That’s the best part about reality, in real life nothing in your life is for sure except that you will inevitably die. How and when is all upto you. Tonight I will go through with the biggest decision of my life. How it will end, who it effects and what consequences I will face have all crossed my mind. But in the end, its me that will deal with it all. Thanks for the talk Bruno but I must get ready. Because in a few hours, its GO TIME!!” (Sean gets up from his seat in the kitchen and walks upstairs towards his bedroom so he can get a few more hours of sleep before he goes off to pave his way as a mobster within the Salamone family.)
