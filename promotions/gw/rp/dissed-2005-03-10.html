Dissed

10 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/10/05 “He said what?” Train’s voice shredded the words as they exited, each vowel and consonant dragged over the gravel of his sleep addled throat. He’d only answered the phone on instinct, it’s ringing waking him from a dead sleep. In his 1/4 conscious state the ringing had been enough like an alarm clock that he reached out from beneath the black sheet and comforter that covered him and the mattress that sat on the large, dark maple wood bed frame, in order to take a swat at whatever piece of machinery had the gall to disturb him. A couple of hard slaps on the first, sizable piece of plastic he felt beneath his hand as it sat on the nearby, matching night stand caused enough synapses to fire that he suddenly remembered he’d long ago moved the alarm clock across the room. A valiant play at athletic morality. An attempt to keep himself from becoming too intimate with a sexy bitch named Ms. Snooze Alarm. Another few pops from another few circuits finally revealed the identity of what was irritating him so. His hand came down for another slap, only this time his large fingers latched on to the target and removed the black and silver, wireless handset from it’s base. His agility had been rather remarkable for a man still half asleep. He managed to press the power button to the phone with no trouble before rolling over and pulling it to his ear. Talking to anyone hadn’t been on the agenda for this morning. Hell it hadn’t even penciled in, but on the other end of phone a voice from the past called out to him in what had been the present at the time. It didn’t seem so at the beginning, but the voice was familiar. It’s favorite subject usually drama. This time would be no different... “Speak...” Train said blankly into the phone, the words a flatly repeated on instinct as opposed to conscious effort. “Get yo” ass outta bed nigga?.” said the bass filled voice on the other end of the line, quietly rumbling as if being punched from a woofer. “Huh?” Train replied, obviously taking as much exception to the curt comment as he could muster at the moment. “Who the fuck is this?” “Oh... so you move down south, become Mr. Wrestling Star and forget all about a brotha, huh?” “Biggie...” Train responded with a groggy chuckle. “What up playboy?” Train asked the question in a joking manner that intentionally shrugged off just how much concern he had about this phone call. The voice that finally snapped him back to a semblance of reality and awareness of his surroundings was one from what seemed like an old life. One he hadn’t heard from in months. At one time they’d been like brothers. Train would have trusted no other to watch his back and vice versa. Now they were virtual strangers. After hearing the voice, for a moment Train honestly wished they hated each other. It would made life easier. At least if their had been bad blood not hearing from the cat he’d come up with in the streets of Chicago, the couple of stupid kids they were, running and gunning like the negro version of Butch Cassidy and The Sundance Kid... well... it would have just been easier. Instead the didn’t speak because Train didn’t need the scrutiny being a normal entry on the phone records of a man like Biggie brought. It was ironic really. At one time they’d been the same type man. The type to never leave home without a gun on his waist. The type to never roll with less than four other cats with him. The type to keep the best lawyers in the country on retainer. The type that payed top dollar to have the best investigator in the cities to sweep his lines for bugs at least 3 times a week. A gangster. And the thing about any real gangster is they always managed to get what the rest of the world tell them they can never get. According to the novels and movies there was no out when it came to crime. But Train was the top of the food chain. He could have whatever the hell he wanted... mostly because the man that had his, the man next in line for the throne wanted him to have it. Problem was to leave... he had to leave it all. The cats he might as well have been related to included. Train knew his brother in arms understood that.That made this phone call all the more disturbing. A phone call meant there was an issue. An issue meant there was going to be a problem. “Chillin”,” Biggie responded with yet another rumble. “Ain’t gonna have you on long dogg. Probably shouldn’t have called, but... Shit I just had to let you know dogg. I don’t know what you planning on, but ahhhh.... you need to check that nigga” man.” “You got a specific nigga” in mind?” Train asked, wondering if it was just the sleep that was till trying to dominate him that was keeping him from knowing what Biggie was talking about. “Flip.” Biggie replied plainly. “I know you got yo” own shit you doing down... You doin? yo? thang and all that, bu ahhhhht.... Man you been through too much to earn them stripes on yo? name. You can’t just be letting that little muthafucka shit all on it. Maybe it ain’t even my business no more, but... hell man it fell like if he shittin? on you he shittin? on me and I ain’t the type of cat to just let that shit slide man.” “Flip” Who the fuck is flip?! I don’t know no damn Flip.” “Lil” Flip dogg.” “Lil” Flip.......... that rap nigga?”!” “Yeah.” “Man, what you talkin” bout? I ain’t even met dude.” “Hey may not’ve met you, but that ain’t stopping him from talking shit about you.” “What kinda shit?” Anticipating a return to sleep Train hadn’t even bothered to open his eyes. Until right then. That was the conversation that had lead up to this moment. This moment when shredded the words not just because his throat was clogged with sleep...but anger as well... “Basically,” Biggie began, already scoffing at what he was about to repeat. “That youse a wrasslin” nigga... and wrasslin? niggas ain’t real.” “He said what?” “According to some of my boys up in New York dude was on Hot 97 a couple days after you was on there. Whole thing is on their website dogg.” Dissed. For the world to hear. The moment was filled with silence, but it spoke volumes. In it the air was filled with all the words and sentiments virtual brothers would’ve, could’ve, should’ve said, but pride and manhood wouldn’t allow. “Good lookin” out dogg.” Train said, breaking the silence with a respect that permeated every word. “One.” Biggie replied equal respect before pressing the power button on his phone, returning the conversation to silence.
