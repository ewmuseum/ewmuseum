Going to get something to eat

27 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/27/03 We see Brick and Aaron walking down the street towards a soul food restaurant. Aaron: You don’t really think Kyle has something going on with this other woman, do you? Brick: I don’t want to think it, but I see my brother-in-law walking outta some place with a woman who isn’t my baby sister, what am I supposed to think? Aaron: Well, just because you see him with another woman doesn’t automatically mean he’s sleeping with her. Brick: Well, if he’s not sleeping with her, what the hell was he doing with her? Aaron: He could have just been talking to her Brick: Or he could be just creeping with her. She’s his ex-girlfriend. She may have it in her mind somewhere that maybe she misses him being around. And he may have it in his mind that he might miss her. Besides, you didn’t see the look on his face when I called his name after I saw him with her. He was nervous as hell. Aaron: He was probably afraid of what you might think. And you do tend to jump to conclusions. And, plus, Monica must have told him what went down between you and Cedric that night. Brick and Aaron reach their destination. They walk into the restaurant and right up to the counter. Brick: Yeah, well, he might wanna keep that in mind. Aaron: Don’t you think you’re being just a little overprotective of Monica? Brick: No, I am not being overprotective! Girl at counter: Good afternoon, may I help you? Brick: Yeah, give me a fried chicken dinner with macaroni and cheese and collard greens and an extra side order of yams. Aaron, you want anything. Aaron: Nah, I’m good Aaron: Yes, you are. You’ve always been that way. Ever since I’ve known your ass, you’ve been that way. Brick: Yeah, well, she’s my baby sister. I have to look out for her. Aaron: I know and I understand that, but you have got to chill the hell out once in a while. Monica is a grown woman. If she wants your help, she’ll ask for it. But you just got to kick back. Besides, you got more to worry about. Like this match coming up on the 31st. Brick: I ain’t worried about that. Aaron: Bullshit! Salaok has you in a triple threat match with Tragic Hero and Roz Blayze. I know it’s for contendership for the International title, but you know that bullshit stipulation Salaok has on all your matches now. Brick: Of course, I know! I ain’t a fool! Salaok ain’t slick. I know he’s using this match to try and set me up! He wants me out of Global Wrestling! That’s why he’s got me in a match where I don’t even have to be pinned to lose Aaron: So, don’t you think you should be just a little bit concerned, seeing as how the odds are kinda stacked against you? Brick: I am concerned. Tragic Hero is one of the best, and this Roz Blayze cat? He’s an unknown. But how long have you known me? Aaron: Since fifth grade. Brick: Then you know I have a habit of beating the odds. Who went through nine of the best Global Wrestling had to offer in a steel cage match? Aaron: You did. Brick: Who fell thirty feet to the ground and lived to tell the tale? Aaron: You did. Brick: Then I’m not gonna worry so much. Besides, I always fight my hardest when I got a lot on the line. Not just a title shot, but my livelihood. And Tragic Hero, Blayze and especially Salaok are gonna learn the hard way why you don’t mess with that. The girl at the counter comes back with Brick’s order. Girl at counter: That’ll be 12 dollars. Brick pulls out a twenty dollar bill. The girl gives back eight dollars in change. Girl at counter: Have a good evening. Brick: You too, sweetheart. Brick and Aaron leave the restaurant. Static.
