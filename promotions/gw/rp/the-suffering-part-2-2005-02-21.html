The Suffering - Part 2

21 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-21-05 Wake up! *The words echo threw Damians head as if it where one big hollow room......* Wake Up Brother! *The words get louder as Damian starts to crack his eyes open slowly.....Before his eyes open completly he notices the pain in his hand is still there, as if what he experience in the room was real and happened all over again.* Its time to continue on.......Road to completion lies ahead! *Damian gets his eyes fully open and sits on to find himself lieing on the same bed he was before entered the first door. He stands up and looks towards where the door was onto to find its on the opposite side of the room from where it was. Damian is confused at first but shakes it off and walks out of the door. Only to find Corvin still sitting on his pedestal as if he never moved. The same round sanctuary like room, pillars surrounding him with a large 9 sided start. Each point leading to another door.* Corvin:You know what must be done brother..... *Damian glares at him as if what had happened in the last room was his fault. But not sure if what he experienced in the room was a bad thing. Damian looks at the 2nd door and realises he wants to know whats behind that door now more than ever. He makes his way towards the door and goes to enter the room but before he makes it threw the door Corvin stops him.* Corvin: Be careful brother. You never know whats lurking around that twisted mind of yours......... *Damian doesnt think nothing of it, and grabs the door handle and slams the door open. Suddenly a blinding white light comes pouring threw the door lighting the whole room up behind him. Damian covers his eyes with his arm and doesnt even think about stopping he just plows threw the door waiting to see whats on the other side....................Damian makes it threw the door and as soon as he does the light suddenly doesnt bother him anymore. The room is all bright white, no floors, no walls, no cieling, just a big void of white with no end in sight. Damian stands not know what to do next. He wonders if this is just an illusion, is this just all in his head? or is this an actual physical place? how is that possible?........Damians past trying to reason at this point and just wants to know what this room has to do with his life......He stands still until he hears a voice from somewhere but he cant tell where. Its to faint to tell what the person is saying but he knows its there. As he stands looking around he hears the voice getting stronger it sounds like a man screaming. Damian sees off in the distance a man standing up pointing and screaming at something. He quickly takes off running towards the man, trying to make out what hes saying at the same time. Man: YOU BITCH! HOW DARE YOU GET INTO MY SHIT! I WILL FUCK YOU UP!! *Damian can tell what the guys saying the closer he gets the clearer it becomes to him. When Damian reaches the guy standing up looking towards the ground suddenly everything around him forms into walls a ceiling and a floor. It's some sort of apartment, mostly run down. Wallpaper chipped off the walls all over. Papers,Trash,Books everything imagineable scattered threwout the floors and on the counters and tables. Ripped up furniture facing a TV playing a football game. Damian looks back at the man to see him about 5'9 standing in a cut up pair of jeans and a dirty stained wifebeater looking over a woman who is cowering on the floor.* Man:WHAT THE FUCK WHERE YOU THINKING!?!? HAVE YOU LOST YOUR FUCKING MIND?!?! GETTING INTO MY SHIT LIKE THAT!!! *Damian notices the woman, hes seen her before........Its his mother. Laying on the floor in a old worn out flower patterend dress, cowering in the corner of the room. The man stands over her and then goes in and starts to punch her and kick at her.......Damian begins to remember this day. His mom was strung out on coke. She was living with this guy who was a dealer. Damian doesnt remember much of this. Its about the time in life where he started to black things out the most. He started working out and fighting because it was the only thing that made him feal better. Like letting all the aggression out on some poor bastard he didnt even know.* Woman:IM SORRY! ILL NEVER DO IT AGAIN! I COULDNT HELP MY SELF! Man: YOU FUCKIN WHORE! ILL MAKE SURE YOU DONT DO IT AGAIN! *The man begins to beat on Damians mom some more. Damian can do nothing but stand and watch. Damian has never felt any more hatred or anger before in his life than he is now. but theres nothing he can do and he knows it. Suddenly from the front door of the room comes flying in Damian....Only as a teenager now....Damian stands in awe as this is all just now coming back to him as he sees it happen in front of him* Young Damian: GET THE FUCK OFF HER!!!!!!!!!!!!! *Damian flys ontop of the man and takes him down to the ground and start unloading left hand after right hand into the mans face spewing blood out of his nose and mouth all over the floor. Over and over again he swings until the man has fallen mostly limp. He then grabs the guys head and picks it up and slams it down against the hard wood floor knocking most of the teeth out of his head and sending a gush of blood out of his mouth. Damian gets back to his feet and lifts the man up and runs with him in his hands and throws him threw the front window out onto the pavement driveway. Damian stands looking at what he has just done as his mother flys onto him punching and kicking him though Damian isnt affected by it* Mother: NOOOO! WHAT THE FUCK DID YOU DO!!! HES ALL I HAD!! YOU WORTHLESS PIECE OF SHIT GET THE FUCK OUT OF HERE!!!! WHY DIDNT YOU DIE INSTEAD OF YOUR FATHER!!!! *Damian stands starring at his mother. If he ever felt sadness in his life, this would be when. Damian drops to his knees watching the images in front of him hatred,anger,spite all running threw him at the same time not knowing what to do. All he knows is he wants to kill the first thing he can wrap his hands around. The rest of that day suddenly comes back to him. That was the day he left, Damian never saw his mom after that day. He never found out if he killed the dope dealer. Frankly he never cared. He didnt care if he died himself, why would he care if anyone else died? Damian just sits, on his knees, eyes clinched shut trying to fight off the pain he feals. not understanding what the point of this life is. all he knows is suffering. Maybe this is death for him. Maybe its his hell hes cursed with forever......For being a healing process so far this has just made him hurt more. At this point Damians insides feal like there going to explode. He just sits on the ground waiting for it to happen so that life will be over and there will be no more pain...........The pain continues to grow more and more. Every inch of his body is just filled with hatred. Blood is pumping threw his veins so fast there bulging out of his skin.* Damian:AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH MAKE IT FUCKING STOP!!!!!!!!!!!!! *Suddenly everything goes black. Damian falls face first to the floor and completly blacks out........................Is life just one big endless state of suffering????* _____________________________________ a pounding pulse stirs anxious feelings inside sick breaths of undead life 'help me angel evil' cried the children of the night take this wasted mortal life melt me into blacklight lie on the floor and let the body die become the beasts in which you fear 'help me angel evil' cried the children of the night take this wasted mortal life melt me into blacklight
