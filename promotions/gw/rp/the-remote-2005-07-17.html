The Remote

17 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 17, 2005 July 17th Home I pull into my drive way and get out of my black Ford Thunderbird. I close the door behind me and slowly walk up to my house. I walk down the side walk and I see three heads sitting on the sofa. I see long blonde hair, short brown hair buzz cut and blue hair. I shake my head knowing full well whose inside. I open the front door slowly and see the TV flickering through the channels. I close the door and turn to see three of my dufis buddies playing a game of tug of war with the remote. Its kind of like a wrestling match they had once but no ref and its more entertaining. “I really need to change the locks so you three don’t get inside.” I say with a smirk on my face. “Four Owen is upstairs.” Samantha says as she puts her hand on Chill’s face trying to push him back from the remote and Scott Vyper trying to dive over him. “You guys are tards.” watching the melee continue. Samantha manages to change the channel to Lifetime. Once the other two realize this they try even harder and both pounce at the remote. However Owen, comes down and snatches the remote from Samantha and turns off the TV. The three sit back up and glare at Owen. “If you guys can’t play nice you shouldn’t play at all.” Owen says laughing. “Asshole! I almost had it!” Chill shouts. “Why the did you turn the TV off”!” Vyper shouts. “I had the remote!” Samantha shouts at Owen just like the other two. “Don’t you know by now Owen not to make the monkey’s mad” This is worse then poking them with a stick.” I say sarcastically as I turn to Owen. “I’ll give it back to them. I just wanted to hear what you have to say.” Owen says. “About?” I ask knowing what the question is already. “The hanging up the boots. You had everyone that you knew believing you were gone and you go out and win the match.” Owen says a bit of set as if he was lied too. “I know I told you guys I was retiring but something changed when I hit the curtain. I just had to go out there and do what I do. When I told Samantha and Chill that I meant it but going down to that ring changed everything.” “Okay , I’ll buy that.” Owen says. “Can we have the remote back now?” Samantha asks. “What you guys didn’t care that he could have lied to us.” Owen responds. “Dude” we’re wrestlers we know how it works. We all think about retirement at one point or another.” Chill says. “So you find this perfectly normal?” Owen asks. “Pretty much” Chill says. “Crazy wrestlers if you say something mean it!” Owen says not understanding what this business can put you through. “Damn it! I want the remote!” Vyper shouts. “Fine?” Owen turns back on the TV and tosses it back to them as they all dive for it. “This is great”.. you guys could go watch TV peacefully in your own homes but yet you decide to break into my house and fight over the controller.” I comment. “We love you Freddy what can we say.” Owen replies. The three dog pile on each the remote which has made its way onto the floor. Poor Samantha Foxs is sandwich between Vyper and Chill. “You okay Samantha?” I ask. “Yeah, its not the 1st time I’ve been between two guys.” She responds jokingly during this scramble for the remote that is buried under Chill. “Alright”. Owen? follow me.” “Eye Eye Captain!” Owen responds. We go into the kitchen and open the wooden cabinets under the sink and I pull out a super soaker and hand it to Owen. “Take this.” He grabs it and pumps it a few times as you hear screams the other room. “I suppose you want me to squirt them?” Owen asks. “Yeah , and I’m counting on them to chase you.” I answer. “There going to kill me?” Owen says. “Just get them on the side of the house where I’ll cool them off with the hose.” I reply. “I’ll do it.” Owen says. I head down the basement and sneak out the side door. I grab the hose and turn the water. I await the 3 stooges and Owen to come around the corner as they do. Owen has lost the super soaker and bolting past me as all three are on his tail. They look like they want to kill him as I spray all of them including Owen. Owen falls backwards trying to retreat. “You traitor!” Owen shouts. Chill, Samantha, and Scott all stand on the sidewalk laughing as I spray Owen with the hose. Owen is attempting to get away but as a hard time doing so since I’m right on top of him with the hose. He eventually manages to turn the water off and looks at me soaking wet. “Why did I trust you?” Owen asks. “Because you’re a dumb ass” I reply. I’m so glad things seem to be getting back to normal around here. If I can stay this light hearted even when things are tough in my wrestling career I should be able to make it through any losing streak. I need to learn to separate my professional and personal life. If I can do that there will be no stomping The Original Pranksta Fred Grest.
