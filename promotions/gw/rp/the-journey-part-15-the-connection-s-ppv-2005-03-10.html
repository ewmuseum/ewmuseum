The Journey: Part 15/The Connection's PPV

10 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-10-2005 Aftermath Reality. Sometimes its hard to distinguish one from the other and other times we wish that we never lived in it. Sometimes what we want to be real isn’t and sometimes the things that are real we wish we could shatter like dreams that we know we cannot attain. Fiction is an even funnier game to play especially when we live in the aforementioned reality because sometime we want our fiction to be reality and sometimes we just would wish that it would be just that’fiction. It been mere minutes since the Apocalypse has been played on the TV set in the locker room of Joshua Curtis and Ami Mizuno and Joshua could do nothing to stop it except sit there and watch has his worst enemy became something that he has yet to attain in nearly 9 years and that is the World Heavyweight Title. Joshua had sat and watched the match with at one point unbridled joy as his sworn enemy was getting his ass handed to him and when it happened Josh was extremely happy because he can’t stand Christian Zenith worth a damn! Then it happened? Zenith was awarded the World Heavyweight Title and Joshua couldn’t do a damn thing about it! Joshua has been in the business for awhile and never attained what he felt was being handed to the man that he despised more than anything in life itself! When Joshua saw the credits come on the screen and the joyous face of Zenith still on there he ripped the TV out of the wall and threw it so hard and so fast that it splintered into about a million pieces and some of the pieces of glass that were splintered from the monitor got into Joshua and dug like a dagger into his flesh! Hearing the crash of the monitor in the locker room that she and Joshua share, Ami Mizuno ran like the wind to see what had happened and what she saw was one of the more alarming things that she could have ever seen! The room itself was still intact but the fact that Joshua ,still with his mask on lied in the room in a pool of his own blood and breathing erratically caused the prodigy to nearly have a breakdown as she thought that her brother had committed suicide but the reality was that Joshua was still breathing and when she got to her brother’s prone body she knelt by his side and held his face in her own shaking hands before speaking for the first time. “Joshua” Ami began “what did you do to yourself?” Ami asked almost in tears as she couldn’t believe what she had seen with the glass from the monitor stuck in Curtis like a dagger draining him of the life giving substance known as blood and Ami knew what she had to do? Reacting on instinct Ami went to go look for some medical help but found someone even better that she thought might be able to help her brother in the form of Brandi Sights, the GW commentator that Joshua befriended a while back. “Ms. Sights” Ami began “something happened to Joshua. Is there any kind of medical personnel around to take a look at Joshua?” Ami asked and Brandi reluctantly shook her head no but told the young one that she would go take a look at her brother for her. 10 minutes later Brandi was in the locker room of The Connection and seeing the sight that Ami saw minutes before with Curtis practically stabbed to death by the glass from the monitor but at the same time she knew that he would be ok as long as he didn’t think about it. With Brandi sitting with Joshua in the remains of the monitor and Joshua propped up onto her lap Brandi began to speak to Joshua in a manner that showed more concern than anger. “Josh” Brandi began “what did you do to yourself?” The announcer asked Joshua and Joshua, whom was still clad in his attire with his mask turned and looked at his friend before speaking. “Brandi” Joshua began “I’m sorry that I did this but honestly I did it because of Zenith. Zenith made me go this mad and do this to myself because he has made me the most miserable person out there and I’m not the kind of person to be upset at people but he has caused this and he must pay for the crimes that he has committed.” Joshua explained to Brandi whom by now was so in tears that she didn’t know if she could go on but Ami squeezed her hand tightly as she continued to hold Joshua closer and closer until he had to take his mask off for a minute just to listen to the sound of Brandi’s heartbeat and that helped him relax enough for a minute so that Brandi could carry him over to the couch and let him rest there because he needed all his strength that he could get and she wanted him to be strong again because GW needed a champion against Zenith and his band of misfits and that is what Joshua was to her. As Ami and Brandi walked out of the room and let Joshua sleep with the shards finally removed from his body the two decided to discuss matters as it pertained to the following week. “So Ami” Brandi began “you ready for the rematch with this “La resistance” team?” Brand asked and the prodigy thought about it long and hard before she could possibly answer the question. “Honestly Brandi I just want to deal with Joshua’s problem and that is the individuals that is Zenith and his minions but since we got booked this week we might as well entertain the husband and wife huh?” Ami questioned Brandi and the commentator nodded before slapping herself on the forehead for forgetting something! When Brandi walked back into the room she had a piece of paper in her hand that was from a friend and before she left the room again she kissed Joshua on the lips and walked away happy knowing that Joshua would be just fine and the Connection was really about to restart!
