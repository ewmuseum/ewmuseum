Perhaps he could make use of this time after all. He hadn’t slept in days; weeks even, he found that this heightened condition required little rest, but he could just drift away now, let his mind roam free in the waters of Lethe? in the peace of forgetfulness. He rarely dreamed, and if he did, he never remembered them. That could have been a good thing, but he never gave it much thought. He pulled down the window shade, banishing the starlight, and let his head fall back again, letting his eyes close as the weight of the world settled on their lids.
