mind over madness: Motives / Operation Rodeo Reginald Atkins Global Wrestling Promo #3066 Date: 4/3/07 Location: CoTT RP for Rumble and Platinum Title Match Remember when you asked me a few weeks ago why I do what I do? And I responded that I didn’t really have an answer? Well, I think I’ve realized why. And it might not be the kind of answer you’d expect from me. I do it because I’m a good friend. And what made me realize it was when Nathan Boswell looked me in the eyes a few weeks ago, when I called his girlfriend a bad name (bint, bitch, I don’t remember: I give out bad names like candy). He looked angry, but it was a subdued anger. Almost as if he was trying to force himself to be angry with me. Like he knew that was what was expected, and even if he didn’t feel it he had to show it. In laymen’s terms, he mailed it in. Went through the motions. And this is supposed to be over someone he loves. You’d expect something more. I do, at least, because I’ve seen that same anger unchecked, unforced. Let me take you back about two or three years, back when our friend was under twenty years old. He was a young little bugger, yeah, but that didn’t stop him in the slight. I remember seeing him look at a man who had done him wrong a lot of times. Ross Boswell, Nate’s abusive daddy. This man had ruined his life, killed his mom, tried to kill his other family members, and even joined Nate’s wrestling promotion to ruin his life in the workplace as well. And for awhile, Nate took it all sort of the same way that he is now. A little angrier, maybe, but that’s because he was younger and full of fire then. (It’s really sad that being in your mid twenties is considered old in wrestling circles. I feel like I need some sort of cane.) Anyway, eventually it got to be too much for Nate to take. So he found his dad, took him out to a secluded street, and tried to kill him. Literally tried to stomp the life out of his father. And Ross’s face was full of fear and surprise, as if he never expected Nate to get this mean. To get this angry. And Nate’s face? Now that’s what I’m talking about. There was hate. Rage. Anger. But more than that, there was power. He was stronger for it, and better. And after that, Nate was never the same. Ross Boswell was gone, and all of his anger had transferred to his son. Nate became a monster. But after awhile, the fuel subsided. The anger went into the background, and we were left with a man who only had one emotion. Love. Love does not fit well on the face of a professional wrestler, that much I can tell you. That’s what I saw on Nate’s face when he looked at me the other day. Not love for me, because I’m pretty sure he isn’t a tosser – although that might explain a lot. But he has love in his heart, and it won’t allow him to become the monster he was to his father. And that’s why I’m here. That’s why I’ve been doing what I’ve done to Nate and his tart. And why I’ll keep doing it. I want those emotions to take control. I want to see that face directed at me. That face that is geared to hurt me, tear me apart, and even kill me. The difference between me and Ross is, I’ll be expecting it. And once he gives me that face, I won’t succumb to it. I won’t be a victim. I’ll be his owner. Because after he gives in to his hate, he’s mine. ---- Operation Rodeo Date: 4/??/07 Time: 7:00 PM Location: Dallas, TX Hello, gentlemen. If you’re reading this now, you have been one of the elite members of our permanent staff selected to participate in this mission. This mission is a task that highly deviates from anything we have encountered up to this point. Before we’ve had our hands in smaller affairs, where the biggest crime we were committing was petty theft or some battery. This mission is the first where murder will not only be a possibility, but will actually be expected. As this is somewhat of an exclusive endeavor, I do not have to illustrate how important it is that this be kept a secret, even among fellow members. I have included a list of the included staff with this message, but do not even discuss it with them around others. I understand you may have a friend you work with that is not on the list, and you may have a brotherly bond; that thing is natural with work like ours. However, keep your conversations to football and women you’d like to fuck. I cannot stress the importance of this enough. As you scan the list of men on this mission, you will no doubt see that there are some names you don’t recognize. I have brought in three old friends from other places to help in this mission. You do not need to know how I know them or where they are from; only that they have earned my trust. I also don’t want anyone causing trouble or doing something stupid to prove loyalty to our group. I apologize to anyone who is miffed that I’m going outside the gang, but as I said before this is a special mission. The specifics of the mission deal with getting back what we lost last year when we employed two outsiders to participate in one of our heists. I know this has probably slipped your mind throughout the months, but I promise you I have thought about little else. I have been biding my time, waiting for the moment for the boys to slip and reveal themselves to us. And that time has come. Thanks to a friend of theirs who has an affinity for talking too much, I have found their residence in a small town outside of Dallas/Fort Worth area in Texas. In talking to this unknown friend, I have also found a weakness. Seems our little friend has found himself a girl. We know how to use that, and believe me, we most certainly will. There will be more specific details when we have an exact date to go to Texas. I don’t wish to inundate this letter further with more things that will incriminate us. Report to me Wednesday morning for a training and debriefing session, and FOR THE LOVE OF GOD, BURN THIS LETTER. We don’t need another incident like the Cherry Hill robbing, do we, Nielsen? JEP ---- Everyone is so fixated on asking me why that they never realize that “how” is the more important question. Motives are irrelevant. What’s really something to wonder is how I can hit all these pressure points, play to the weaknesses and soft natures of all these people around me? How can I figure out what hurts a person most, and use it to my advantage? How am I just so damned good at what I do? Well, the answer to that one is much simpler than the why question. Let me tell you what I believe. I don’t believe in most things. You can call it pessimism, but I think of it as keeping a healthy glass of reality by my bedside table at night. I don’t believe in miracles, I don’t believe in ghosts. I don’t believe in friends always being there for you if it conflicts with their own agenda. I don’t believe in deus ex machina; you get wherever you end up by your own wits and no one else’s. The idea of a higher power has occurred to me many times, but it always leaves without taking my sanity as a casualty. To restate, I don’t believe in most things. But this is what I do believe. I believe somehow in this great mystery of life, you are given qualities and tools. Every person has about five unique and above-average qualities about themselves that they can use to better themselves and make sure that their life is not wasted. I believe that not everyone finds out every quality about themselves, nor would they even if given a hundred, two hundred, ten thousand years. These are the people who ask you for change on the corner, the people who cause you to roll your eyes and think “what went wrong with this person?” I believe I know all five of mine. One: I am intelligent. So are you, I’m sure. Or you think you are. Seriously, I am much smarter than you. My mind is always working, formulating (machinating if you will), contemplating over every detail, every minute part of life. I know useless information. I know useful information. I can think up solutions to small problems, as I have proven many times on Fusion. And any larger problem that comes my way will not thwart me for longer than five minutes. Two: I know the human condition, including how to treat them and how to act around them. This is how I can find the weaknesses of my opponents. I know what makes them tick, and I simply take that out so they don’t any longer. I won’t name the other three for you, because I’m sure it bores you and while I really don’t give a damn, I’ve made my main point. These two points are the main reason how. Having full capability of my intelligence and knowledge of humanity, I can easily make my mark. And that’s how I know Nathan Boswell wants to be a monster again. I have seen him speak, I have seen his eyes. And those eyes are the eyes of a man who is growing numb and would do anything just to feel again. It’s also the reason he hasn’t grabbed me and ripped me in half yet. I mean, come on. What would you do if I hurt your girlfriend? You’d do more than shuffle from foot to foot and angst over it like an emo boy without actually accomplishing anything, I’m sure. I swear, he’s like a bloody 7 foot Hamlet. And one thing this Prince of Denmark doesn’t know how to do is fight back. Not yet, anyway. And even when he gets to be strong, even when he becomes a monster, even if he tears off Jake Hix’s head and throws it into the third row, he’ll be doing it because of me. So there’s the how. We now know the how and the why. The what? Well…we’ll have to see about that. But I have a pretty good idea what’s going to happen. After all, I know people well.
