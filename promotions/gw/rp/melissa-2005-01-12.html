Melissa

Date: 01/12/05 Tribulations of Drake, Continued? -Melissa- Welcome to the “Friendly Freeman’s Diner,” a cozy little place with some of the best food in all LA. Drake has stopped in to have some French toast. “Here you are miss,” he hears as he looks up to see a beautiful Asian woman being seated in front of him. It’s her, the woman from the apartment across the alley. She has a suitcase. He continues to eat his food trying not to look up but he can’t help it. He takes a quick glimpse. She is looking at the clock nervously. She notices him looking at her and he looks back to his food. He continues to consume his breakfast hoping she didn’t notice. “Excuse me?” He looks up to see her standing before him. “I know you from somewhere don’t I?” Drake plays dumb. “Mind if I sit down?” He stalls for a moment and reluctantly gives her the Ok. “I’m Melissa Wong,” she tells him extending her hand. “Drake,” he responds shaking hands. Melissa: So? Do you come here often? Drake: I like it. Melissa: This is the first time I’ve been here. I hear it’s quite good. He doesn’t respond and continues eating. Melissa: Do you have a full name or is it just Drake? Drake: Just Drake. Melissa: Oh. So how old are you? Drake: Twenty. Melissa: Twenty huh? That’s a fun age. Drake: You? Or is that impolite of me? Melissa: I don’t mind. I’m twenty-seven. Drake looks at her bag. Drake: Going somewhere? Melissa: (Taking a quick glance at her bag) Oh. Yeah. Just thought it was time to move. See what’s out there, know what I mean? Drake: No. Melissa is a little surprised by this answer. Melissa: Well that’s too bad. So Drake, is there anyone special in your life? Drake: No. Melissa: Is there anyone you like? He’s silent for a moment. Drake: Why do you care? Melissa: Just making friendly conversation that’s all. If it bothers you I’ll stop. Drake: No. There’s no one I like. I don’t like getting involved. Melissa: Why’s that? Drake: It just complicates my life even more. Melissa: What do you mean “even more?” Drake: You wouldn’t understand. Melissa: I might? Drake: (He sighs) I don’t wanna talk about it. Melissa: That’s fine. Drake: Is there someone in your life? She’s disturbed by the question. Melissa: There was’but I’m trying to leave him behind me. This leaves an uncomfortable silence between the two. Finally he breaks it. Drake: You gonna eat? Melissa: Oh, yeah. I just gotta figure out what I want. She grabs a menu. Melissa: Oh my. They have quite a selection, don’t they? As she reads the menu Drake studies her almost as if ashamed of himself. He continues on his breakfast but before he can enjoy it for too long? “Hi sweetie” Melissa and Drake are both startled by a deep voice. Melissa: (Nervous) Hi Tony? By the table stands a six foot five Asian monster named Tony. He seems a little agitated. Melissa: Um. Tony this is Drake. Tony just looks at Drake and nods. Drake gives him an evil glance. Tony: I’m glade you made it back Ok. Ready to go? Before she can reply? Drake: I thought you said you were moving? Melissa: Oh’yeah’well I am. I just came back to get the rest of my stuff? Tony: Let’s go! Tony grabs her bag and Drake notices his white-knuckle grip. Drake: What’s in the suitcase? If you don’t mind me asking? Melissa: Just clothes. Tony: I said let’s go! Melissa: Nice meeting you Drake. She stands and slowly makes her way to the exit but Tony pushes her along. Drake watches all the way until they’re out of site. Drake looks back to his food. Drake sits silent for a few moments’he SLAMS his fist down on the table in anger, drawing attention. He rests his forehead into the palm of his hand frustrated. To be continued?
