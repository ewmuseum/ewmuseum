Backstage boom

27 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/27/03 Brick should be happy. He has gotten over a hurdle and won his match against Derek Frost. This meant that he would be facing Sage Sabbot in a rematch for the World title at Whiplash. Now he had the chance to redeem himself after Dead Zone and finally win the World title. Now Sabbot could no longer continue to duck him as he had the week earlier. But there’s one part of this that doesn’t make Brick happy: how he won the match. The referee called for a DQ after seeing Frost with a chair in his hands and Brick busted open. But Frost was merely left with the smoking gun as it were. The real culprits were the Upper Echelon, particularly Deacon Holmes. Brick and Holmes have had a rather large amount of animosity towards one another in the few months Holmes has been in GW. It all started with some rather disrespectful comments he made about Brick on an edition of Overload. They’ve also had a few physical confrontations, some of the most recent happening before and during the match. Holmes told Brick that he wasn’t done with him after their clash backstage. And truth be told, Brick isn’t done with Holmes either. Brick had rolled out of the ring and headed backstage through the crowd when no one was looking. Just as he had gotten backstage? BOOM! “What the hell?” He sees a stage hand rushing towards the entrance. “Hey yo, what happened, man?” “Deacon Holmes just blew up the Octagon, with the Nevermore still in it!” “What”!” “I’m serious! He blew up the ring!” “That son of a bitch!” Brick hears Christina McCoy cry out. He turns to see her headed towards the ring, followed by Ely Braxton. “Calm down, darlin”,” says Braxton. “And you’re always talkin” about me losin? my temper.” “The whole Nevermore was in the ring?” “Every one of “em. Salaok, Quartz, Sabbot?” “Does anybody know if they’re all right?” “What do you care?” “What do I care” I gotta face Sabbot at Whiplash, and this shit might ruin it.” “Nobody knows yet.” “I’m gonna kill that little bastard!!” Brick growls. “First that bullshit with his bodyguards, then he jumps me during my match, and now this!” “You calm down, too, Brick.” “Besides,” says Christina if anybody’s gonna kill Holmes, it’s gonna be me! Do you know how much it’s gonna cost to replace that ring? Not to mention all the people who could have gotten hurt!” “Yeah, well, if that little bitch did anything to ruin my match at Whiplash?” “At least you didn’t lose your match tonight.” “It’s not that I didn’t lose, Ely. It’s that I didn’t get the pin fall. Damn TUE screwed me and Frost. Both of us deserved to win that match!” “Well, it ain’t like Frost is goin” home empty-handed,” says Braxton. “He’s still got Salaok at Whiplash. Just like you’ve got Sabbot, an” I know you’ve been lookin? forward to that since Dead Zone, so concentrate on that and the title.” “Yeah, you’re right. I got bigger fish to fry. And that bigger fish is named Sage Sabbot. But Deacon’s time in the skillet will come. And it’ll come soon.” “Forget about Holmes for now. Besides, he and the rest o” TUE got their hands full as it is. They done already pissed off the Youngbloods and the Nevermore.” Just then, a phone starts ringing. “Is that yours?” Christina asks Braxton. “No, don’t think so.” “It’s mine,” says Brick, as he reaches toward his belt and answers his phone. “Yeah. Aaron, hey, what’s up man” What? You’re kidding! It’s early, ain’t it? Well that’s great, man! I’ll be back in New York as soon as I can. OK, bye.” Brick hangs up. “That was Aaron. Felicia’s having the baby right now.” “Oh, that’s wonderful,” Christina says. “I thought she wasn’t due for two more weeks, or so.” “Yeah, well, apparently the baby didn’t care. He or she wanted out and they want out now.” “Well, you’re not gonna try and go out tonight, are you?” “Christina, I got a godson or goddaughter being born. What do you think?” With that, Brick heads for his dressing room to get ready to leave. In New York, his best friends are about to experience the birth of a new life. And at Whiplash, Brick will reach his destiny.
