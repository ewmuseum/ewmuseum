Consequences and repercussions: Never Mind.

8 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 8th, 2005. Consequences and repercussions: Never Mind. ---- SoHo, NY. June 6th, 2005. 1:18 PM. Rohypnol, the drug commonly known on the street as roofies is infamous for the effect it has upon your memory, a common reference to the evil concoction is 'the date rape drug' and I'm living proof that rohypnol can wipe your memory clean, leaving you dazed and confused. You only need to say words as simple as 'The Rome incident' to drag me down memory lane and back to a place that will forever haunt my existence. The details, while fuzzy, are absolutely horrifying. On March 23rd, 2004, I had - according to the staff of an Italian hospital - overdosed on rohypnol, ingesting a total of 26 pills. 24 hours later I opened my eyes, an IV flowing through my veins and a nurse at my bedside, I didn't remember how I arrived at the hospital or what preceded the ambulance ride, and to this very day I only remember the very minuet details of the nightmare. Most of my memories come from what I've been told. The stomach pump revealed 26 pills, Ken and Ice had kicked my door in and called the ambulance, rushing me to the hospital in time to save my life. When they broke the door down they discovered their friend lying face down on the floor, his body jerking with convulsions and flecks of foam at the corners of his mouth. They were horrified, almost as scared as I was when I woke up in the hospital. The details, as aforementioned, are rather fuzzy but the things that stand out the most are a smiling KPJ standing over me as I opened my eyes, and a ravenous appetite. I asked for an explanation and a strawberry milkshake, I got an explanation, I'm still waiting on the milkshake. But the most horrifying event of all was the fact that I had overdosed on rohypnol, a drug prescribed in many countries for insomnia or nerves, but a banned substance in the United States due to it's sedative-hypnotic effects and widespread club use. The drug had been passed on by an Italian fan who had learned of my insomnia, and thought he was doing a great service by providing me with a medical wonder, capable of curing my sleepless nights and finally allowing me to rest peacefully. But what's so horrifying? The fact that I can't remember if it was intentional or not. One of rohypnol's crazy little quirks is how it conveniently manages to erase your memory for a brief period of time, in my scenario, that time was almost long enough for me to die. The technical term is anterograde amnesia, or in laymens terms, a brief memory lapse. I don't remember taking the pills, I don't remember falling to the floor, I don't remember Ken saving my life, I just...don't remember anything. The doctor had asked me a question that I couldn't answer due to my amnesiac memory, he asked me if I had attempted to commit suicide and I walked away without answering the question, because the sad truth of it all was the fact that I didn't know. I had no recollection of the events that had provoked me to choke down 26 capsules of rohypnol but the fact of the matter was, I had done just that. 'What is wrong with you, freak?' I sneered at the grungy kid with shaggy black hair. He wore a pair of low slung Levi's and black Chuck's, his shirt was black with 3 white dots in the center forming a triangular pattern. 'You make me sick...' I spat out angrily, disgusted by the unshaven face before me. The face, while covered in a three day growth, could easily be deemed handsome even if the owner of the facial structure was absent of a smile. 'I hate you.' My voice was a low, angry hiss. 'I wish you were dead.' My sneer was returned to me, his bluish gray eyes sparking with anger. 'I can kill you.' My tone was cold, hateful. His eyes, like mine, were full of pain, bloodshot from the tears he had cried, the pain was masked behind anger and bitter hatred. My eyes could hardly stay locked on his due to the pain, the utter agony displayed therein, and my eyes shifted to the side, where I saw her picture. Raven black hair, big brown eyes, dark skin and a soft smile that matched her beautiful soul. Her name was Raki, she had kicked me to the curb, discarded me like an un-devoured piece of meat. She, no doubt, hated me, and for all of the right reasons, yet despite it all I loved her with an insatiable passion. My heart beat faster at the very thought of her existence, my soul aching for her touch, my skin shuddering in sweet memoria. As I turned my eyes back to the man before me, my blood literally boiled in anger, my already dark skin turning flush with hatred. 'Why...why did you do this to me?' I asked, raising an accusing finger. 'It's all your fault...' I shook my head slowly, my multicolored hair swaying as I did so. 'I never wanted to hurt her, I loved her...I still love her...' My voice, tweaked with confusion and hurt, echoed off of the rooms white walls, returning to me sounding even more insane than ever before. 'I'm sorry Raki.' I gasped, turning my eyes again to her photo. 'I'll kill him for you, I'll kill him so that you never have to experience an ounce of pain again, your life will be so much sweeter without him.' Curling my lips in anger, the man before me did the same and as I lunged forward he also made his move, raising his hands synergistically and driving them violently into the glass! The mirror shattered with a sickening impact, the sound of breaking glass blatant as shards fell upon the blue carpet. My hands, my wrists, were covered in blood, my own blood and the man in the mirror was gone. The thick, warm, plasma dripped to the floor in a crimson pool as I raised my hands to my head grabbing a thick lock of hair and tugging at it maniacally as I fell to my knees. 'What have you done?' I gasped, rocking back and forth on my knees bringing up a bloody palm in which I buried my face. 'What have you done..?' Collapsing forward I caught myself on my hands, crawling hands and knees across the floor leaving a track of bloody hand prints as glass crunched into the carpet, the pain lost on me. 'I wish it would stop...' I moaned, dragging myself forward to an unlocked chest on the other side of the room. 'Make it stop...make it stop...' My severed palm was placed upon a piece of glass and I winced in pain, emitting a soft gasp as I locked my fingers around the shard. It was roughly five inches long and two to three inches wide, gripping it tightly I raised it to my chest and ripped through my shirt, the sound of tearing fabric eclipsed by an animalistic groan. 'You deserve this pain you monster!' I choked out in a tearful whisper, my eyes clouding up with moisture, my vision blurred by the tears as they fell. The tears soaked into the carpet leaving no trace, unlike the blood that was spread across Ken's carpeted floor in excess. My knees drug across another shard of glass, cutting a hole into my jeans as I crawled closer towards the chest, the chunk of glass in my hand biting deep into my skin as I inched my way across the floor. 'You deserve it...you deserve to die...' I cried, the tears coming more forcefully now, accompanied by heavy sobs of desperation. The physical pain seemed to only numb my emotional senses as I drug myself closer and closer to the chest, reaching it, I collapsed into a broken heap of hysteria. Tearing at my wrists with the chunk of glass, my flesh tore away, leaving a ragged white gash that quickly filled with crimson as my life's essence spilled onto the floor. My fluttering eyelids glared down at my dripping limb, as I clenched my fist and punched the chest in front of me, crushing the glass into my fist. Pain shot through my hand, burning it's way up my arm and into my shoulder, where it felt cold and dead. Needles of glass pierced my skin as I grasped at the handle of the chest and threw it open. Reaching inside I locked a bloody hand around the butt of a 9MM pistol and jerked it out of the box, tossing it wearily onto my bed as I used the box to pull myself to my feet. Once on my feet I glared down at the weapon, my vision blurred with tears of regret and sorrow, my state of mind blurred by the aforementioned as well. The gun was black and ominous, ironically the gun had landed with the muzzle pointing directly at me. As I stared down at the instrument of death a thousand thoughts entered my mind. Today would be the day I would die. I always knew it would happen while I was young, I just didn't know it would be so soon and so violent. Another young kid with a great future ahead of him was going to splatter his brains on the wall and leave a tear stained legacy for millions of fans, a bullet would serve as the period of the final chapter of my life. It's strange, the things that go through your head in a situation like this. I always thought I would die at the hands of my enemies. Maverick Reno, John Zero, Leinad Soma - someone but myself. I never thought I would be the one who would end my own life. As I reached down for the gun I hear something... A hand, knocking rapidly at my door. 'Josh, are you okay?' Asked a worried Ken Partridge Jr. 'We thought we heard glass breaking...', added the lovely Gunner Addison no doubt at Ken's side. 'Yeah, what's up pal?' Ken asked, his voice filled with concern. I didn't answer, it would be pointless to answer, Ken would only try to save me, he would try to keep me alive, he wouldn't understand that it was my duty to die. It was my wish. Death would mean an abundance of glory for me, I would soar off into eternity carried away on the wings of angels as I watched the world slip away beneath me. I would finally be rid of the pain, the pain that I caused, the pain that I lived with...the unbearable pain. All it would take was one brief moment, a split second, the squeeze of a trigger and it would finally be over. The pain would go away, it would all just go away, and that was what I desired. I was tired of being miserable, tired of living with an aching soul and a conscience stained with the remnants of a sinful life. Tired of the memories of pain that haunted me, the howling ghosts that echoed through my mind every night as I tossed and turned, realizing that Morpheus wasn't going to visit me that night. 'Josh..?' Ken asked again, his voice louder, the pitch rising with excitement. 'Are you okay? I grimaced at his voice as I pictured him on the other side of the door. Ken and Gunner had been kind enough to allow me a chance to live with them and this was a cruel way to repay their generosity, yet I knew my mission. This is what I had to do... Staggering across the floor, weak with guilt and the loss of blood I reaching a dripping hand down to the bed, crimson staining the covers as I slid my fingers across the mattress and drew the gun into my hand. Outside of my room the voices continued. 'Gunner, call an ambulance, I'm going to check on Josh.' Ken demanded, his voice taking on a no-nonsense tone. 'But Ken, shouldn't we...', Gunner began before Ken cut her off. 'We don't have any time to spare, Josh's life may be in danger!' Ken snapped, Gunner's reaction blatantly obvious as I heard footsteps pounding down the hall. Ken knew me well, far too well, he had read all of the signs and taken my silence as a distress signal. He was truly my dearest friend on Earth, I only hope that he has a good maid to clean my blood off of his walls and that he'll be so kind as to say a few nice things at my funeral. I'm not asking for a list the size of Peace and War I'm just hopeful for a few kind words, a loving memory or two and a tear to be shed. As I reached down for the gun I heard Ken's hand on the knob, he turned it, but to no avail it was securely locked. Holding the pistol in my shaking hand I raised it up, turning my eyes to stare at the picture of Raki that somehow managed to cling to a piece of broken glass that hadn't cascaded from the mirror to the floor below. My eyes held me in limbo, enraptured by her beauty, oblivious to my surroundings. I was so swept away in her beautiful eyes that I didn't even hear the cracking of wood as Ken rammed his shoulder into the door, he drove his body into the door a second time, this time was successful as the oak submitted humbly to his assault, splintering into an opening that he quickly stepped through his baby blue eyes on fire with concern. 'Josh, what are you doing?' Ken asked, his face pale with shock. 'I'm sorry Ken...', was the most I could manage, my body trembling uncontrollably. Ken spoke with deep concern between deep, ragged breaths. 'Josh, it doesn't have to be this way.' Ken assured me, taking a step forward. 'I can't do this anymore Ken, the pain...the pain is killing me...' I gasped between tears. 'Just look at me Josh, I love you like a brother...' Ken said as he took another step towards me. 'You're a brother to me Josh, and to Gunner. We love you, we want you to be happy and make this your home, we're a family man, a family! Isn't that what you've always wanted? A family in which you could be accepted?' 'Yes, but...' My voice was slow, weak, I couldn't finish as Ken interrupted me. 'No buts, Josh.' Ken took another step closer, his voice pleading with me. 'Put the gun down, don't hurt yourself Josh, you don't have to do this...' 'But the pain, Ken, the pain...' I shook my head, Ken's face hardly visible behind my shining tears. 'It'll go away, Josh.' Ken said, taking another step forward, almost within arms length of me. As he took the step I raised the gun, pointing it directly at his head, warning him. 'Stop.' My voice was stern, Ken's eyes filled with surprise. 'I'm not going to let you hurt yourself Josh, you're going to have to shoot me before I allow you to shoot yourself and Josh...I know you would never hurt me.' Ken's voice was suddenly confident and fearless as he stood there unafraid of the gun I held in my shaking hand. 'Just stop right there Ken, I've got to make the pain go away..., I said, the room beginning to spin, my own voice sounding as if had came from underwater. Ken's distorted voice barely made it through my mind as the room began to spin faster and faster. 'Josh, you're going to put the gun down and I'm going to take you to a hospital, okay?' Ken held out his hand, expecting me to hand the gun over. 'No...' I cried out weakly, taking two steps back and collapsing against the wall, allowing the thick plaster behind me to support my body weight. Ken shook his head. 'To this day Josh, I'll never understand why you insist on doing things the hard way.' Ken's words were still transmitting through my weakened mind as his left hand snaked out and cracked across my jaw, his right hand snatching the pistol from my grip. I fell to the floor, my eyes closing involuntarily. ---- Room 1928. 6:38 PM. June 7th, 2005. 'Doctor Kenner to room 386, Doctor Kenner to room 386.', echoed through my head as I opened my eyes. I didn't know where I was at first but a quick view of my surroundings enlightened me. High ceilings, draped windows, tile floors, the antiseptic smell all pointed to one source...a hospital. I wasn't particularly observant at this point but the IV in my veins and the adjustable bed I was stretched out in also brought those conclusions to the light. As my weary eyes opened I spotted two familiar figures, Ken Partridge Jr. and Gunner Addison. Ken was asleep, resting in a chair, gunner seated next to him, both looked tired and weary, their clothes wrinkled and crumpled, a state in which I had never seen them before. As I uttered a groan I heard Gunner gasp, her hand reaching over and shaking Ken from a restless slumber. His bloodshot eyes quickly popped open and a smile shot across his whiskered face, obviously unshaven. 'Josh!' He exclaimed as he shot upright, quickly making his way over to my bed. One glance at his scuffed knuckles reminded me of the events of yesterday and explained exactly why I was lying in this hospital bed still alive. 'Ken...' I croaked, my voice hoarse and raw. 'Don't talk Josh, you need to rest.' Ken warned as he patted me gently on the arm. 'I can't believe you're here...after what I've done...' I stared up at him in awe, he smiled back down at me. 'It's okay Josh, lets just forget that ever happened, okay?' Gunner joined Ken at my bedside her smile matching the one Ken wore. 'We'll help you, we'll keep an eye on you and protect you...even if it's yourself that we have to protect you from.' My eyes teared up, as Ken smiled down at me, weakly I returned his smile my heart melting away at the compassion he displayed. 'I love you guys.' 'Josh, you know we love you to death.' Ken replied, Gunner nodding in agreeance. With a smile I asked a question that suddenly meant more to me than ever. 'When can I go home?' I had never asked this question before, because I had never truly had a loving home until this point of my existence. 'Tomorrow, that's what the Doctor's say, but you're going to have to take it easy for a while.' Gunner warned, a stern expression on her face. Ken smiled at her. 'Honey, you know better than that, Josh will never take it easy.' Glancing up I grinned. 'I'll sleep when I'm dead.' Ken shook his head and rolled his eyes, Gunner seemed taken aback by my morbid joke. 'Lets just delay that as long as possible, okay Josh?' Gunner said as she looked down at me. 'Deal.' I nodded back at her. ---- I knew this time it was the real thing, a bona fide suicide attempt, failed but an attempt nonetheless. Rome may be in question for the rest of my life but SoHo would not be. I would forever live on with the memories of a tragic night in SoHo. But I had no guilt, no remorse. I had simply made a mistake, and somehow these people that I called my friends were willing to reach inside and forgive me. Never mind the fact that I destroyed a room in their newly purchased house, never mind the fact that I had pulled a gun on my closest friend in the world. Never mind the fact that I had done nothing right and everything wrong... Never mind.
