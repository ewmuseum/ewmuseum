Training Session Part 1

10 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/10/03 The two men walked through the door leading to the roof. The door was marked 'Roof Access', when they walked through the door they were set atop the roof of their appartment building. They had permission to use the roof tonight, and were glad they needed the training. It was 7:00 p.m and the sun was slowly falling from the sky. The orange and purple glow from the twilight sun, set an intense mood as the two walked onto the roof, bags over their shoulder, ready to prepare for their training. The forecast for the night was calling for rain and the sky was littered with dark clouds. It did not matter to the two men as they needed the training and preparation, they could not let another opportunity slip through their hands. They did not enjoy the idea of rain, but they hoped that it would hold off long enough for their training. Each man was dressed similarly in 'Jordan 23' workout suits. The taller of the two men was wearing a white and blue workout suit, with a tight white muscle shirt on underneath the jacket, as well as a backwards white baseball cap. The shorter wore a black and red workout suit, with a black muscle shirt underneath and a black baseball cap turned backwards. They both were wearing matching white, K-Swiss shoes. The brothers Ichiro and Jushin began to unpack their bags. Both pulled out similar items and equipment. Each man pulled out a small green mat, presumably for meditation purposes, a small wooden sword, a pair of grappling gloves and a pair of shin covering kick pads. However, Ichiro's gloves and kick-pads match his attire as they are white, but Jushin's are black. The two men took their mats and walked to the far side of the roof. They placed their mats onto the ledge of the roof, the total highest point of the roof. They set them up so they were facing towards the falling sun. They went back to their bags and began to prepare themselves for their training. Jushin removed his shirt and shoes. He quickly tapped his wrists and then pulled his black grappling gloves on over the tape. He then removed his socks and pulled the kick-pads over his legs and used the straps to secure them on his shins. He walked towards the door and grabbed a large push broom, and began to sweep up the area of glass shards and rocks, so as to not harm their feet during their sparring. Ichiro watched his brother before beginning to prepare himself. He pulled his shoes off, and then his socks and slipped his kickpads over the workout pants. He adjusted the straps and secured the pads over his shin, he then removed his jacket, and turned his white cap backwards. Next, he reached for his white grappling gloves and pulled them on tightly over his hands, and stood there staring into the sun in his sparring attire. As he waited for his brother to finish sweeping the roof, he scooped up the wooden sword. He began to slowly make thrusts and swipes warming up and stretching out. Finally, Jushin finished sweeping and in a fit that it took him so long, he threw the broom over the roof. 'That was not necessary brother,' said Ichiro. 'It wouldn't have taken so god damned long to get going, if the lazy fuckers, would just clean up here once in a while!' Jushin replied angrily. Ichiro could only shake his head in reply. However, his brother was right there was not much sun left, and soon there would be very little light for their training, but it did not bother him, he was actually looking forward to a training session in the darkness of night. It had been many years since he had last done that, they had relaxed their training ever since leaving the temple. However, his loss last week to the 'Steel Lion' had made them both change their attitudes as they prepared for their tag title match. 'Meditation to start, just as the days in the temple.' instructed Ichiro. 'We aren't in the temple anymore Ichiro. We can do our own thing now!' answered Jushin. Why the hell was he so angry thought Ichiro. Well it seemed as if he wanted a fight, so a fight he would get soon though. Not yet. 'Yes brother, but do you remember those days when we used to be teased for being the best? When we would simply fight those who did not respect us?' 'Yes, of course!' answered Jushin. 'Well, did we ever lose a fight to those students? Hell I remember one time we beat up kids two years older than me!' continued Ichiro. 'Yes, you're right brother. Meditation first,' Jushin finally gave in. 'Tanaka's teachings have never guided us wrong before, we should trust in him now, as we need him more now than ever.' Ichiro mentioned as he walked over to his mat. Jushin nodded, he hated to admit it, but his brother was wise. He seemed to get smarter and stronger by every day they spent in the United States. He was starting to wonder if Ichiro could ever do it on his own. If he could actually be the Chosen one, the warrior of the Black Lotus, and that he himself was destined to complete the tasks that now stood before them combined. 'Hey bro! Do you ever think that you could do our tasks on your own?' Jushin felt he had to ask. Ichiro stopped dead in his tracks. He was about to hop up onto the ledge and onto his mat but instead he turned and stared transfixed on Jushin. 'What the hell are you talking about?! Of course I couldn't! We are here together, I can't do this without you. What in the world makes you think I can do this myself?' His big brother was angry. Angrier than he had seen him in a very, very long time. 'Well it just seems that you're smarter and stronger than me. Maybe, you don't really need me. I mean, you can't tell me I rival you in strength, nor even come close in intelligence.' His master's words ran through Ichiro's head as he listened to Jushin. Jushin was admitting just what his master had said he never would. Did this mean that they would succeed? Or did this mean his cut-throat brother was now going soft? No, Jushin was as hard edged as anyone he had ever met. He would never go soft. 'Brother, I need you around. You and I push each other to do our best, I am stronger, faster and wiser but only because of you. I do well because I know that if I slip up, you are there ready to be better, and because of that I cannot allow myself to slip up. Our pride is strong both as brothers and as individuals. My pride is what keeps me just above you, and your pride is what keeps you nipping at my heels, pushing me to go harder and get better.' replied Ichiro. Jushin could only smile, there was nothing he could say. His heart was warmed, he felt deeply touched by his brother's speal. He however, was not one to show emotion, so before anything showed, he bottled the feelings and stepped up onto the ledge, and sat in silence cross-legged on the mat and began to calm himself for meditation. Ichiro smiled. He knew his words had affected his brother, he saw a glint of emotion before Jushin had quickly capped them off. He had known Jushin for so long, even the slightest change of attitude was able to be read by him. Big brother's insight I guess, he thought. He then followed suit and hopped up into a cross-legged position on the mat. He closed his eyes and relaxed every inch in his body. Ichiro's mind raced. His mind showed him visions of his life so far in the United States. It showed him training in his apparment. It showed him getting off the plane, and wondering what the hell was he actually doing in this strange, new world. He saw he and his brother triumphing over all the odds in a Four Way Tornado Tag match. And then finally, finally he saw himself losing last week to 'The Steel Lion' Akodo Rokku. He could not let himself fail again, not for himself, nor for his brother. He would not let another failure happen at his hands. It seemed like only an hour, but had actually been several when his visions were interrupted. He began to notice that Jushin was beginning to stir. Jushin had finished his meditation and was ready for the physical training. Ichiro watched as his little brother hopped down, and grabbed his own wooden sword. He listened to the sounds as his brother slowly began to warm up his body. Well I guess it's time, thought Ichiro. Ichiro opened his eyes, and realized the entire world was now darkened in the 9:30 night. He smiled and spun around, watching his brother warm up. He then took his mat and rolled it up, throwing it right beside where his brother's now laid. Ichiro picked up his sword and began to follow suit with Jushin. He went through the stances, motions and techniques that his Master Tanaka had taught him. He was well aware that Jushin knew most of them, as he had the same teacher, but there was one which he did not know. The Light of Lotus, yes. That was what Tanaka had called it. However, he could only do that if he allowed the Lotus to take over his body, and he did not want to do that, not yet at least. Ichiro turned to face his brother, his brother was already turned and awaiting his older brother. Ichiro looked over at him before lowering his sword, and grabbing it strongly with his fist turned outwards. He glanced at his brother, he could only make out the shapes, he could allow the Lotus to just take over now, and he would be able to see clear as day, but instead he decided to fight fair. Maybe, he should teach Jushin the technique, Tanaka had told him to do so. But, only when Jushin was truly ready. Ichiro had no idea how to tell if Jushin was ready, but he seemed to be maturing more lately, not so much aging as a man, but growing and fluorishing as a warrior. And if there could be any indication, Ichiro was sure this night may just be it. He looked over towards his brother and nodded, he then received the same gesture from his brother. Ichiro tooks the sword at his side and crouches down. He spreads his legs farther apart and crouches awaiting his brother to make the first move. He used his hands to tell his brother to 'come and get it', his brother hated it when he acted like Morpheus. Jushin snickered at his brother's gesture. He knew that Ichiro wanted him to make the first move. And he would...soon. But, not yet, he first had to scout his position and come up with a game plan. He stared into Ichiro's eyes, he could tell it was still Ichiro that he was fighting and not a personification of the Lotus, he had not yet allowed the Lotus to control his body. Jushin smiled, at least not yet. He smiled, and decided that he would fight with the sword first, he would try to wear down his big brother before he decided to do anything he spoke to his brother. 'Good luck Ichiro,' Jushin taunted his older brother. He decided that he would try something along the lines of a role reversal. He planned on pissing his brother off, so that he could see how well his brother could fight while he was hot-headed. 'Thank you brother, but luck shall not help me in this dual,' replied Ichiro. 'Is that so? Well then, let's hope you fare better than you did against the Steel Lion!' Jushin smirked as he taunted his brother further. Ichiro's glare changed, his face grew colder. Jushin could tell that he had landed a low blow. Ichiro did not make any more comments. It was time to let their swords do their talking. Ichiro raised his sword and began to slowly approach his brother. 'Here we go,' thought Jushin. This would be the first fight in a long, long time that he did not make the first move. He threw his left foot back, and crouched down. He prepared himself for the attack, and it would soon come. To Be Continued....
