Welcome To My World

26 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/26/03 The lights go out and the cameraman begins to feel around searching for a light switch, or the door. His hands graze along the rough cement wall, before reaching a round object. Slowly he begins to turn the round object, and the wooden door, the figure exited from just a few moments ago, opens up. Past the threshold of the doorway is a long hallway that seems like it never ends. The majority of the long endless hallway is dark, but as the cameraman cautiously walks down its long and winding path a few windows break the monotony of the grey cement walls, and the white light of the moon shines through shedding some light into the darkness. As the cameraman continues his walk down the hallway statues of gargoyles, satanic figures, and angels all grey with no color begin to appear along the walls of the hallway. Their cold stares mixed with the almost complete darkness make the cameraman feel like he is being watched, and he begins to perspire in slight fear of his situation. Finally a bright light appears down the hallway. The cameraman picks up his pace, and begins to run towards the light. As he reaches the light a figure steps out of the darkness, and right into the middle of the hallway. The cameraman sees the figure and immediately tries to stop, in the process slipping and falling to the ground. A loud THUD! can be heard as the cameraman hits the wooden floor. The figure walks over, and stands over the cameraman's fallen body, looking down at him. The figure's silhouette is the most that can be made out, as the light coming from his back makes it difficult to see any details. Slowly, the figure begins to kneel down, and his long blond hair can be seen more clearly, and his cold blue eyes that peek through his hair also become more visible as he kneels. The figure leans in and begins to speak. 'Going somewhere my friend? No, I didn't think that you were going anywhere, I assume you were just trying to find me for some more insight on myself and my match. That is what you were doing, isn't it?' The cameraman's eyes grow wide as he shakes his head up and down, replying yes to the figure's question. The figure steps briefly into the light and smirks, and looks back towards the darkness, before turning back and looking back at the cameraman and speaking. 'Battle Royal...Eleven other 'superstars'...Cruiser title? None of it makes a difference on what is going to occur. None of it will effect the long term. None of it will stop fate from taking it's time, and slowly dealing it's cold harsh punishment.' The figure stands up, extending a hand towards the cameraman. Reluctantly the cameraman accepts and is helped back to his feet as well. The figure walks over towards one of the statues, and lays his left arm on it. He then begins to lean on it and gaze out of a window, into the dark night sky. ' Another new place, another new situation, yet the same results. Kid Flash and Blitz, two men, who know what I speak of. Two men who know who I am. Two men who will be the first, and the last to dispute the facts of the matter. However Kid, Blitz, you two are not the primary objective. You see there are eight other men who know nothing of us, nothing of me, and nothing of what is about to become of their oh so precious GW. Because this Saturday on my debut stage, I take over what is mine. At THE DEAD ZONE, I show this entire federation, when you step into my ring, you are in MY WORLD, and there isn't a single soul alive that can hang with me in MY WORLD.' Ice gets a angry look on his face and begins to slowly make his way towards the cameraman. The cameraman begins to back peddle away from Ice, but runs into something behind him. Ice walks up to the cameraman and slams his hands on the surface behind the cameraman. Ice leans in and gets nose to nose with the cameraman. 'The eyes are the window to the soul. Look deep into these cold blue eyes, and you will see nothing, but anger, nothing but contemptment, and nothing but coldness. The things I live are the things people like you the superstars of the GW only have nightmares about. They are my reality, they are my livelyhood. So this Saturday night, I am going to open the door, and you will walk into your fantasy, your nightmare, my relality, MY WORLD. You won't know what hit you, and you won't know how to escape. Once you enter, you won't care about looking good, or winning. The only concern you will have is surviving. There is no turning back now, because you have crossed over. The door is now open, and cannot be closed. Fate is no longer on your side. Fate is now your enemy. And your fate is in my hands.' Ice moves his right hand down the surface behind the cameraman, and all of a sudden Ice stands back and pushes the cameraman, who goes tumbling down a flight of stairs. The scene turns to fuzz.
