Apprehension

6 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/06/2005 Note: All text between [ or ] brackets is to represent Japanese being spoken Unlike most cities, Las Vegas doesn't get quieter at night...in fact, most evenings it really seems like there's more going then there ever was during the day. For that very reason the airport continues to function into the wee hours of the morn, accommodating all manner of passengers from all over the globe. Las Vegas also tends to attract all manner of strange characters, and since it accommodates vices of all manner, it's people have gotten used to seeing strange people coming and going. But there is a limit to strange, and a masked woman in tight fitting leathers is still certain to turn a few heads, even in Las Vegas. Kicking the kickstand of her motorcycle into the downward position, Nariko slung one long, leather clad, leg over the side of her motorcycle in a smooth dismount. She reached up to remove her helmet, black and yellow like the rest of her biking leathers, and set it down on the seat of her motorcycle. Long, straight black hair thumped against her back as she shook it out, trying to rid herself of the sweat of the road. The security guard at the front gates stared at her in suspicion, no doubt concerned by the piece of black silk that obscured the lower half of her face. Taking the front steps two at a time, she swiftly closed the distance between them, brushing past the security guard towards the front doors. 'Excuse me miss...' The guard reached out to snag her arm as she went by, 'You can't wear that in..AGH!' Reaching smoothly to the side Nariko seized the young mans hand in hers and twisted his wrist to the side, expertly forcing him to his knees with a painful wristlock. [I did not give you permission to touch me...] The door to the airport banged open, followed by an ominous 'ca-click', and Nariko turned her head to find herself looking down the barrel of a semi-automatic pistol, leveled at her by the guard who had been stationed inside the doors. 'Let him go...now.' The guard demanded. 'I don't think that's going to be necessary my friend, this is all a misunderstanding.' The rich, male, voice drew the attention of all three people to a very large man in an immaculate suit with a large smile. He held his hands up, palm out, as if trying to sooth all the participants. 'There's no need for that weapon officer, I can explain everything.' Officer Barnes had seem a lot of things during his time on earth, but he'd never seen a girl like this brazenly walk up to a secure point and assault a guard with such ease. And as such, he was in no mood to deal with whatever game was going on here. 'Sir this is none of your business, please take a step back.' The smile didn't disappear from the man's face as he reached into his breast pocket. 'Oh you're quite mistaken there I assure you, this is very much my business, if you'll just let me show you my pape...' He didn't get a chance to finish his sentence as Barnes switched the pistol to threaten him, unsure as to whether or not he was drawing a weapon. The tall man froze in mid motion, the smile quickly disappearing from his face, as Barnes took a step towards him. 'SIR! Take your hand out of you jacket very slowly!' A sharp pain in Barnes wrist signaled the loss of his weapon as Nariko snapped an agile front kick into the bottom of his wrist, the pistol sailing up into the air and clattering down the steps. The big man's smile returned in sudden force, and he withdrew two envelopes from his jacket pocket. 'Now that we've got that out of the way, if you'll just check these papers Officer I'm sure you'll find everything in order.' Nariko watched curiously as Barnes took the envelope, wondering in the back of her mind exactly what was going on here. He unfolded the piece of paper, his eyes skimming lightly over it, then going back to the top for a second read to make sure he had gotten it right. After a long moment he folded the piece of paper back up and held it out to the large man. 'Well...it's highly irregular, but I suppose we can let it slide just this once.' As he went to hand the second envelope back the large man held up his hands with a smile. 'Oh no officer, that's for you and your partner here.' Barnes knew what was in the envelope, and could tell from the hefty weight that there was an awful lot of it. He slid it into his front pocket and looked back to where his partner was still on his knees, eyes tearing from the wristlock. 'She gonna let Jimmy go?' The big man turned his smile on Nariko, bowing slightly. 'What do you say young Lady? Will you let him go?' Nariko looked down at Jimmy for a long moment before releasing her wristlock, turning back to the large man. [Fine. Who are you and what do you want?] He held up his hands again, laughing. 'Whoa now little lady, I'm sure we'll have time to get that all answered, but I think we should go get your friend out of a similar situation, don't you?' Nariko snapped her head around to look through the large glass doors. Just inside the checkpoint she could see they had detained a small woman in an ornate kimono and mask, several guards having weapons on her. [Sada!] Nariko leapt the remaining steps, throwing the doors open and sprinting across the floor to where the guards were threatening her partner. [SADA!] She yelled, drawing several guards attention towards her, several weapons turning from her partner to where she was running, stopping her dead in her tracks. 'Freeze!' One of the officers yelled, 'Not another step!' Nariko growled in the back of her throat, dropping down into an aggressive crouch, seeing her partner do the same. The same man interrupted her, stepping into her field of vision from the side and holding his hands out again. 'Gentlemen!' He called to the assembled officers, 'There's been a terrible mistake here and I can explain everything, if you'll just allow me to get the documents from my jacket!' The officer in charge, Officer Kennedy by his badge, stepped forward. 'Alright then, come forward. Just you! If that woman next to you moves a muscle, we'll open fire.' The suited man walked forward with his hands in the air, pausing only as he drew close to the officer in charge to draw the documents once more out of his jacket, with another, larger, envelope as well. The officer holstered his weapon, confident in the backup his men would give him, and read the documents once over, making an annoyed noise as he did so. 'Figures. Goddamn political bullshit is what it is...' He pocketed the second envelope without question, handing the documents back to the larger man. 'All right men, you can holster your weapons! We can let her go, she's got special permission to be dressed like that it seems.' He nodded to where Sada was standing stock straight, her luggage held in one hand. 'Ma'am.' Ignoring him completely Sada brushed past, hurridly crossing the distance to Nariko, where the taller girl slung her arms around Sada in a bone crushing hug. [I was concerned when they said your flight was delayed] Nariko breathed, holding the other girl close. [You are alright yes?] Sada grinned, the expression hidden behind her mask, but her eyes lit up all the same. [I'm fine thank you, I can take care of myself.] She winked to take the sting out of her words. Nariko nodded, her own eyes twinkling, turning to regard the large man as he walked up to both women, bowing low from the waist. 'Ladies I've been sent by Mr. Salvador to have you transported from the airport to your new living arrangements.' Both girls looked at each other with a raised eyebrow before Nariko shrugged. [Very well, show us to your vehicle.] The large man reached down to retrieve Sada's luggage, but the smaller girl put a hand on his arm and shook her head. 'Mine...' she spoke, her English halting. 'Name?' He smiled again. 'You lovely ladies can call me 'D'.' He stood back up, straightening his suit and brushed past both women as though he had never intended to pick up her luggage. Sada retrieved her bags and fell into step beside Nariko. [I did not know Master Luis knew anyone with this kind of...capital.] Nariko nods absently, watching D's back. [Nor I. Perhaps he was approached by one of the GW promoters? I would assume they have this kind of money, although the political connections...that's interesting for certain.] D led the two women out of the airport and down the steps to where a long black limo awaited them. Nariko paused, looking over to where her motorcycle was parked. 'My...Bike...' She forced out, using the same halting English that Sada had. 'Will be taken care of by one of my men I assure you. It will be safely delivered to your new residence.' D said with a smile. 'If you ladies will just step into the car, everything will be explained.' D opened the door with a flourish, ushering both women into the spacious interior of the Limousine. He followed in after them, taking the seat with his back to the driver, rapping lightly on the glass. “Driver” We’re all fine back here, if you could take us to the office please.” Sada turned to Nariko, keeping a suspicious eye on the man across from them. [Office? I thought he said we were going to our new living arrangements.] She whispered in hushed tones. Without taking her eyes off “D” Nariko answered, tilting her head slightly to the side. [I think we might be being taken to the GW head office, perhaps to get your paperwork signed. And I don’t think we need to whisper, the Armani Gorilla doesn’t speak our language I imagine.] Sada nodded. [So we can speak frankly then?] [I don’t see any reason why not.] [Why are we doing this? When you and I originally studied under Master Mifune it was to avoid this kind of American hooplah and grand exposure. Why would Luis bring us back here to place us back into the very thing we’ve tried to avoid?] Nariko stared out the side window for a long moment before answering, her green eyes taking in the unfamiliar cities landmarks as they sped past. [I’m not sure really. It’s not like we didn’t have options in Japan, or even on the Lucha Libre circuits.] Sada made a disgusted noise, [I don’t really consider Lucha Libre to full of opportunities for women like us. They certainly didn’t give us an opportunity to shine while we were down there, only Luis gave us anything approaching respect.] Nariko nodded again, this time more vigorously, [Well we both know it’s going to be different here. As long as we put our feet down on matters like costuming and parading around in ridiculous stipulationed matches, I think we’ll be fine.] [I don’t think we need to worry about demeaning stipulations here. I’ve done some research on Global while I was on the flight over, and they tend to treat women in the same class as their male wrestlers. In fact the current TV and Cruiserweight champions are both women.] [I remember reading that. It gives me hope that we’ll be able to move beyond just the Tag Team division and still have a chance to shine in the singles?] Nariko pauses mid sentence, her eyes clouding as if suddenly distracted. Sada reaches over to squeeze her upper arm lightly. [Neither of us wanted to come back’but I think now that we’re here we need to make the best of it right? We can be singles and still be together, we just need to watch each others backs’keep tags on who’s doing what to who.] Reaching up to squeeze her partners hands Nariko nods in response, pursing her lips behind her silk mask. [I agree, no reason to hold back our ambition just because of fear. That was the whole reason we agreed to come out here in the first place right? No fear?] Nariko turns her eyes back to D, who is smiling broadly at the sight of the two women holding hands. Her eyes narrow into slits and she starts to lean forward, but Sada places a restraining hand on her shoulder, shaking her head. [Don’t bother’it’s not like either of us care what he thinks right?] Grinding her teeth Nariko sits back against the plush sofa, growling low under her breath. [People like that are why we didn’t want to come back here in the first place?] “Is there a problem Ladies?” D asked, flashing his big smile at them again. Sad inclined her head politely toward him, “No problem.” “Because if there is something wrong, I’ve been ordered to do whatever I can to make you ladies more comfortable.” Leaning forward again, Nariko batted her eyes at him. [You can fling yourself out the passenger side door please, that would make me much more comfortable.] Sada swatted Nariko on the arm as D looked at the smaller girl quizzically. “What did she say?” “She say, thank you.” Sada forced out. D leaned back against the seat, his expression saying he didn’t entirely believe her. [There’s no point in antagonizing him right now, we don’t know what role he’s going to have to play in our getting settled here.] [Fine] Nariko huffed slightly, folding her arms under her breasts. [But if he keeps staring at us when we touch I might have to poke his eyes out. You’d think friends never had physical contact in America.] Sada patted her lightly on the upper arm again. [It’s fine, don’t let it get to you so strongly. A big part of our being here in America is to stick to our own beliefs. Luis thinks there is something we can accomplish here, so we indulge him for certain, but not at the expense of what we’ve learned.] Turning her head to stare out the window again Nariko sighed, her shoulders heaving slightly from the motion. [I’m not certain I’m ready to be back here?] [That’s something we’ll have to determine for ourselves, and only with time. It’s too early to say now, one way or the other. For the moment we just need to focus. Once we hook up with Luis again, that’ll help.] Sada reached out to squeeze her partners hands, holding onto it tightly and fixing her brilliant azure eyes on D in the opposite seat, as if daring him to comment on the bond the two women shared.
