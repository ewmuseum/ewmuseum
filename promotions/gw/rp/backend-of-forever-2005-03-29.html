Backend of Forever

29 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-29-05 (03-02-05) Michael was on a plane to New Mexico the next morning, and it was done without a spoken word to anyone. Few recognized him, and he sheepishly took pictures with those few people. Michael always loved the attention back when he was a star, but over the months, he had been reduced to no one again. It was hard to retain fan fare without doing anything to deserve. 'I guess that's why they're called fans.' Michael sat on the plane and soon found himself traveling first class back to what was his real home. With a sigh, he hoped that a decent amount of sleep would take his mind off of everything. In the end, however, all sleep did was further reinforce everything. As painful as it was, it needed to be dealt with, and he had to deal with it as directly as possible. ~~~***~~~ When Michael arrived home, he drove into the driveway to find a familiar face to 'greet' him. There, on the front steps, sat a moping Mindy Mercer. She looked helplessly up at the car that pulled up, and sighed. Michael saw her expression coming from a mile away. With a loud groan, Michael climbed out of his car and walked tiredly over to his cousin. 'What's wrong, Mope Master?' Mindy snarled at the nickname, and turned away from her older relative with distaste. 'What do you think?' Michael shrugged in defeat. 'I, I have no idea.' The answer made Mindy pout loudly in annoyance. 'What the hell is it?' Michael asked. Mindy bit her bottom lip. She didn't want to start explaining herself until he did first; after all, he was the one who had just upped and left! At the same time, though... 'I went to Italy,' she sighed, 'just got back a little while ago.' 'Oh.' That didn't sound too good. MIchael did remember the call she had recieved on Valentine's Day. 'So... you're still here. Did you set things straight with your dad?' 'Nope,' Mindy shook her head. 'I didn't talk to him.' She stopped her sentence short, and glared at Michael with a deadly serious look in her green eyes- a look Michael rarely, if ever saw from her. 'The only reason I came back was because of what another of our relatives told me.' 'Huh?' Michael listened to the description of the person Mindy saw, and quickly he started to remember. How could he have forgotten? The kid had pissed him off to no end, and he actually forgot about him... ~~~***~~~ (Somewhere deep in Michael's mind along with the rest of the past) Michael stood up across from his cousin, and from his mouth he began to whistle- one of those whistles that are normally heard in stereotypical westerns. He began to slowly approach the man who sat on the other side of the living room, reading his Survival Guide To Fighting Zombies handbook, not noticing what his cousin Michael was doing. Before he knew it, he heard a loud 'WHAA, WHAA, WHAAAAA', and suddenly he was blasted in the face by a large handful of flour. Fuming, Michael's cousin grabbed the first thing in reach and heaved it at him as Michael laughed and tried to get away. A split second later, he stopped. His cousin gasped as Michael turned, right arm in plain sight for the other man to see. It was then that Michael's cousin winced as he saw the pair of scissors he threw sticking out of Michael's arm. 'Oh, my God.' Michael stared in annoyance at the sharp object in his arm. Somehow, it wasn't bleeding and somehow, he wasn't screaming; that didn't mean Michael was all peachy about it, though, either. 'I cannot believe you just fucking did that,' he sighed. 'Now I have to go to the fucking hospital, dude. I have to go to the fucking hospital now.' Michael slowly turned and ambled his way to the front door of the house, staring at his arm as he went. 'There are fucking scissors sticking out of my fucking arm.' ~~~***~~~ (03-02-05) 'Yeeep,' Michael sighed, 'that's good ol' Justin, alright.' Mindy folded her arms across her chest and turned away from Michael. 'And you couldn't mention him to me before,' Mindy answered out of frustration. 'I don't know if that means I'm not worth it or what, but I wish you would talk to me about that kind of stuff sometimes, especially since you seem to do all the moping. Need I remind you that you were the one who didn't want to be bothered with me?' 'Oh stop putting words in my mouth, will you?' Michael snapped back. 'I admit, I can be a dick sometimes. But guess what? I'm a dick to everybody!' Mindy didn't reply immediately. She was set on refraining from eye contact, but finally she couldn't take it anymore. Mindy looked right at her cousin, and stammered. 'Well... stop it!' Michael cocked an eyebrow. 'I can't.' 'I'm tired of being happy with what people want of me,' Michael scowled. 'I want to be myself without having to say 'I'm sorry'. The fact is, I am the way I am for a reason. If you hate that, then maybe I'm not the person you should have ran to, but if you hate that, that also tells me a lot about you.' Mindy narrowed her eyes in question of what her cousin meant by that. However, she soon found herself on the recieving end of a remark she didn't want to hear. 'You're a hypocrite.' Mindy's emerald green eyes widened in shock. 'Just because I hate it when you're such an asshole, that makes me a hypocrite?!' 'No, it's because you know how fucked up it is to tell someone to change their ways to fit your fancy, and you're doing it to me anyway. You're doing the same fucking thing your father's been trying to do to you.' Mindy's eyes faltered from the tears that threatened to weigh them down. She blinked feverishly to keep the water from welling up, but it was no use. Michael sighed and looked away; he hadn't noticed Mindy stand up and walk down the front steps- not until she said something. 'I guess you're right.' Michael dared to watch his cousin walk to her hot pink Porsche, and wham- he felt bad. 'For fuck's sake...' Michael stood up, and slowly started after little Mindy. 'I'm sorry,' he grumbled. 'I guess being myself doesn't mean I have to thrash everyone's faults.' 'You're right,' Mindy sobbed, before she threw her arms around her cousin, emotionally exhausted. 'It doesn't. But I guess I need to learn to take constructive criticism.' Both cousins took a couple deep breaths, and with each of them calming down, Michael was able to change the subject. 'Yeah... well, I wanted to tell you some good news, too.' 'Oh?' Mindy replied with intrigue. 'What is it?' Michael soon told Mindy of his plans, and Mindy was ecstatic. She was glad that finally, she would get to wrestle along the side of her role model and her favorite wrestler. However, as happy as Mindy was, Jaden was terribly bitter about it What was worse, though, was how she heard the news- not being told about everything face to face, no... Michael told his cousin first, and not her. ~~~***~~~ (03-09-05) Mindy Mercer and Teresa Ramirez fight back and forth. Michael gets the tag from Mindy, as does Teresa to Juan. Juan leaps from the ropes with a springboard hurricanranna. NO! Michael puts on the breaks and whips Juan into the mat with a snap powerbomb,. Michael then flips over pinning Juan. Mindy rushes across the ring catching Teresa in a spear as Michael gets the 1?.2?.3? The Mercers then help the Ramirez’s up and the two shake hands as Train and Brick rush out to the ring. ~~~***~~~ (3-10-05) 'Eh, not too bad for my first match back in the ring,' Michael shrugged in disapproval of the wrestling tape he watched of High Octane. He was a part of much better wrestling matches, but he wasn't expecting himself to be perfect in his first match back. Besides, that stupid janitor wasn't the biggest help in the world, either. If he knew who the guy was, Michael probably would have gone out of his way to beat the snot out of him; too bad Michael could care less about someone with a feather duster as their most dangerous weapon. ~~~***~~~ (03-09-05) Brick rolls into the ring holding his eys. Michael staggers up and sees what’s going on and questions Gray? Gray turns and begins to pick his things up. Michael grabs the janitor and as he does Gray turns around and sprays him in the face with the same cleaner. .. Michael goes down as Gray picks his stuff up and walks away pushing his cart talking to his self. ~~~***~~~ (03-10-05) '...Damned deranged hermits.' 'That's no fair, Mikey,' Mindy complained as she peeked over her cousin's shoulder to watch the tape. 'We shoulda won that match! Nofairnofairnofair!' Michael groaned audibly. 'Well what are you crying for.... champ?' Mindy grinned, adjusting the title belt that was over her left shoulder. 'Eh, I dunno.' Maybe things were going to look up. Michael was satisfied enough with his showing, and it left him desperate for even more. It was all he thought about as he went to sleep at night; someday, he'd be at the top once again... and once that moment came, he would know what to do. ~~~***~~~ (03-29-05) And that led the two cousins to the day before Clash of the Titans. Mindy would find herself back in Italy once more, on a different sort of business. Now instead of finding her dad, she would have to find a way to win a shot at the GW World Championship. Mindy woke up that morning and looked to use the fancy bathroom that was a part of the hotel room she and her cousin were staying in over in Rome, but the door was locked. Strangely enough, Michael was actually awake before her. MIchael Mercer stared at himself in the spotless mirror in front of him, and smirked. His black hair had been streaked with a dark red, and his 'Ryan Reynolds' goatee was long gone. If he took away the extra three inches and ten pounds he added on since his SAWF days, he would be the same old Michael Mercer he always was. 'I'm too emo for my shirt, I'm too emo for my shirt, I'm so emo it hurts.' Michael chuckled as he messed around with his wild hair and threw on his trademark black and red 'I Hate Wrestling' tee shirt, before he heard his cousin calling him from outside. 'Miiiikeey,' she cried, 'when are ya comin' out?' 'When I'm good and ready, doggone it.' 'Bleh,' Mindy sighed, 'fine, be that way.' Her attention was quickly diverted from the bathroom, however, when there was a knock on the door. In her Nightmare Before Christmas PJs and 'Mean People Suck - Nice People Swallow' tee shirt, Mindy hopped to the front door and threw it open. There, the last person she ever wanted to see gave her a look of stone in return. It was going to get ugly.
