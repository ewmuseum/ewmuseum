The Life I Wanted.

13 February 2004

The Life I Wanted. Terrelle Green Global Wrestling Promo #875 Date: 2/13/04 Location: New York. As my boat approached the cold icy waters of New York in my head I thought about how many man slept underneath me in there watery grave. I thought how many men I put beneath the waters to live eternally. In my past I lived as a hostile youth with no intentions of ever doing nothing more than being a gangster. My father encouraged me to become a lawyer because that is what is needed in The Greeniso Family not killers but educated men. I made my own choice a choice I live with and a choice I love I became a legend. The waters rocked the boat as it approached the crime filled streets of the Brooklyn Docks. My men watched as I came to dock with the wooden deck. Then nightime breeze blew as my boat lights grew to shine and light up the deck. The streets of Brooklyn filled with some of the worst thugs,gangsters,and mobssters. I wasnt afraid not because I had protection cause I didnt care they knew who to fuck with and who not and I was the last person they would try. A legacy was made when I came to the world a legacy of a true killer a mindless heartless cold-hearted man. No I have no regrets cause I dont have to be educated to be great I get more publicity as a Mobster than a lawyer gets as a lawyer. I get free television time cause I am what the people wanna see whether I am alive or dead. Brooklyn as the black Mercades approached to pick me up and Micheal stepped out to greet me I looked as across the way another ship was pulling in. The ship wasnt a ship at all just a bunch of low rate Chicanos collecting there cocaine to get in buisiness. I sloly closed my door and begin walking through the stacked craters as Micheal and my men followed me. The Columbians got of their boat with two suitcases I knew then that the columbians had been sneaking behind my back making income on my turf. I stood in the shadows and watched as the men went inside the fish warehouse to make the exchange. I looked over to Micheal and he looked back as he signaled the men to get reinforcements. As they ran off me and Micheal walked towards the building where the only light you could see was the one inside the Warehouse not to strong but it could get the job done if you needed to see. As I walked inside through the side entrance I could here the conversation of the two men talking. 'Yeah man its Grade A shit we will only charge you ten five a key.' says The Columbian. 'Votos this is some good shit.' says The Mexican. Hey snorts a line whipping the powder stin from his nose he stands with two men behind him and the columbians stand with there two. Micheal walks out and the sic men look at him. 'Hello gentlemen allow me to introduce The Godfather Terrelle Greeniso.' says Micheal. He steps out of my way as I come walking out with my black trench coat and black hat lighting my cigar. I walked over to the table and pick up the cocaine as my men come from there hidden places holding the silenced SKs. 'Looks like to me you have quite a lot of stuff on the table Eh?' I said. 'Greeniso we are doing buisiness and we would appreciate it if you would look over this.' said The Columbian. 'You are on my turf....my country....and cutting in on my buisiness. Its hard for me to look the other may yous got about twenty five kilos on the table. I thought me and your boss had an agreement no one makes money on my turf unless I get a cut.' I said. 'I didnt know Godfather Greeniso I thought that......' explained The mexican. 'Quiet.' I said. I walked over and sat in the chair the guards moving the Mexican from away from me so that I could take a seat. I opened the suitcase and Micheal began counting the men looked as the guards looked around seeing the men standing ontop the crates aiming at their heads. I puffed my cigar as Micheal looked over the money couting it. He then stopped looking at me and shaking his head. 'What is it?' I asked. 'We have two hundred and fifty thousand dollars here all in small unmarked bills.' 'Well...well....well you columbian scum dont think that this is just a little overlook do you?' 'Godfather Greeniso I was just doing what Emilio told me I didnt have a choice.' The columbian explained. 'Micheal this is your call I cant do nothing seeing as you run the streets.' I stood up and walked away as Micheal walked behind mewhispering in the guards ear. As we exited the door we heard bodies hitting the floor and I got inside the car and Micheal followed. The guards came walking out of the warehouse wth both suitcases loaing them into there car and we pulled off. 'You handled that well Micheal.' I said. He looked over at me and smiled and turned and looked out of the window. I knew he felt bad cause that was the first time he ever had someone killed. I smiled as we approached Downtown Brooklyn everybody looked as we pulled in front of the huge hotel. the police stepped off the sidewalk and walked towards the cars as the four Lincoln Navigator SUV pulled behind us and the guards exited. 'Micheal listen to me. I said. You dont let what happened out there get to you in this line of work you have no time for remorse. Cause when you allow your feeling to get in the way you give the enemy a chance to whack you. I didnt get my name because I was a nice guy I got my nam because when it came time to do buisiness I didnt hesitate I shot first and thought later.' 'Godfather we could have worked something out with them.' Micheal said. 'Micheal the work has been done after you talk once there is no more talking. Its like when you got a dog you keep worning him to quit biting but he keeps on anyway. So what you got to do to keep him from biting......put him to sleep. You just remember this if that was you and they had you cornered they would have killed you so to ever prevent that you got em first.' 'Why are all these cops standing around?' 'Cause we are celebrities now come on lets not keep the crowd waiting.' I got out as the men opened both sides of the door the policed looked at me piercing a hole in the side of my head. I turned as they stood on the sidewalk watching me and Micheal walk into the hotel for the fundraiser banquet and smiled at them. One of the officer spit and I winked at him while he rolled his eyes. Inside the hotel the lights were lit up in the ballroom as the mayor took the podeum. He talked while I walked through the different political figures and they smiled trying to shake my habnd and I ignored and Micheal followed. Why should I shake there hands? They are rich and cocky people who like other rich cocky assholes and I hate that. I found my seat in the front as they announced me in. 'Ladies and gentlemen just arriving a little late is Terrelle Greeniso one of our top sponsers for the Read To Suceed program. Lets give him around of applause.' They clapped I just kept looking toward the stage as the Mayor kept talking. I really didnt care one way or another what he was talking about I just sat there for my own purposes. I was no ordinary gangsters some of the people that were suffering the worst was the italians. I wanted to see more progress in our race besides just being on the news as drugkings and killers. As he wrapped up his speech Micheal ordered us some champaign as he announced there would be refreshments and all the rich people stoofd talking to other rich people and the mayor came down to talk to me. 'Mr.Greeniso how are you doing today?' he asked. 'I am doing.' I replied. 'This must be Micheal.' 'Yes sir.' Micheal said. 'Mr.Greeniso your contribution is greatly appreciated by the children and we at the Town Hall is greatly thankful for you.' 'You pricks just make sure that the money doesnt buy you a new Benz cause I will be watching you you snake.' 'Mr.Green I assure you that the money doesnt go to us it goes directly to the schools.' 'Yeah sure it does Micheal get him out of my face he stinks like a dead rat.' He looked at Micheal as Micheals shoved him on his was and a drunk the champaigna and the police sheif came walking over. The guards stopped him as he showed his badge and sat at the table with me and Micheal. 'Hello Terrelle hows buisness?' he asked. I looked at him and kept drinking as he smiled in my face I knew he had something up his sleeve or he wouldnt be here. I whispered to Micheal and the guards and Micheal got up and walked away. 'What do you want?' I asked. 'Well I have received word that you have steeped up a little since our last encounter.' 'Dont try to grease me Cheif cause you aint got the power so if thats what your heading to I suggest you get out of my face.' 'See I like that about you Terrelle you got that fire and that spark but I got one of your guys downtown willing to tell it all. I know you know Joey huh Terrelle a former drug chemist for you we got him at the fish warehouse he knows everything about your little operation. I could very easily get him onthe front page and cause a lot of trouble your way.' 'Joey huh? He is gonna tell on me. Listen Chief Joey has and will never be a chemist for me cause I dont do nothing any information yous claim you got you better make sure its valid. Cause over the past moths I have been wrestling and havent touched the shores of New York. So do what you want but you keep harrassing I will file a lawsuit on you so big by the time I'm done you will be directing traffic. Now leave me.' He got up angrily slamming the chair against the table and I smiled because I knew he knew that I had the power to take his badge. I will never run from the police for what I am The Godfather I got ties everywhere I can get him fired with a word. I need to scare him a bit to let him know I can touch anyone anytime. Micheal was over there conversating with a young women I looked around seeing that everyone was partying. I stood up and from every side of the room my guards started walking over towards me. Micheal walked over but I motioned him to stop I was tired and ready to go. I walked to the side of Micheal and stopped he stepped over telling the young lady to hold on. 'Listen you be careful the men are gonna stay with you I am gonna go get ready to head out.' I said. 'You aint staying?' 'No I am gonna go to Dallas tonight to rest I got to go get on the jet I will see yous. Just remember take care of yourself copesce?' 'Copesce.' I kissed him on the cheek and walked out and got in the car the men followed in the SUVs as I headed for the airport. The funny thing about all this is the life of a Godfather is less killing but more people try to get more from you. Sure I got a little money but hell why would I wanna give them a piece of what I worked hard to take from someone. I was still on every cops, FBI agents, CIA agents hit list. Sometimes I felt like I was more dangerous to them than terrorists cause I have so much infulence in America they feared what I was capable of. I like to be a Mafioso to me there was nothing else to bey life was rich I did what I want when I wanted. So why have a regret in the world I wrestle and I live in a bigger house that the President of The U.S does I think thats what steams em. Hey we all cant have everything so we make choices I made a choice to kill they made a choice to politics. I got a million people who fear me they got a billion that points the finger at them everytime something goes wrong whose life is harder.
