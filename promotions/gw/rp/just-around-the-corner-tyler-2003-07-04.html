Just around the corner Tyler

4 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/4/03 The scene fades in and the cameraman is in the process of disconnecting the camera wires, and getting ready to pack up and go home. The cameraman sets the cameraman on the floor, and two large black shoes fill the camera's view. 'OH MY!' The camera is then jerked upwards, and quickly the view pans up from the large black shoes, up a pair of long black legs, which are covered by a pair of faded blue jeans, up further panning over a plain white t-shirt, and finally stopping at the face. The long, shoulder length blond hair, along the with cold, icy, blue eyes reveals the feet were those of ICE. Ice stands there with his arms folded behind his back, staring straight ahead at the camera. 'Tsk...Tsk...Tsk. I believed that you would show more fire than this. I thought you would not sit back and dwell on your defeat at Whiplash. I would have expected so much more by now from one of TUE. I guess my assumption of you was incorrect. Because it seems that the level you are at in preparing for our match, is well, sub-par to say the least. You see Tyler, since the day I walked into this company, I have been aiming for you. Since the day I stepped foot in this company, I have called you out week after week, to no response. Well now you have no other option. You can ignore me all you want, but come Evolution, you won't be able to allude me any longer. You see, that match between you and I, it is the only thing I have been training for. The only thing that I have been preparing for. And now that it is just around the corner, now that you are showing your TrUE colors. Now that that yellow stripe that has always been running down the middle of your back has be revealed. It makes me want this sooooo much more.' A slight smirk crawls across Ice's face. 'But you want to hide behind your precious TUE. You want to put out no effort in sparing words back and forth with me. That's fine. Stay silent. Ignore me while you can. Because in only a few short days, there will be no escaping the inevitable.' Ice pulls his right hand from behind his back, and slowly pulls any strands of his long, shoulder length, blond hair out from in front of his face. 'Sean Price, I heard your words, and I know what you are thinking, after the comments I just made. Well let me inform you as I inform Tyler. You can make all the idle threats you want. You can throw around the idea that you are going to do something special on Evolution. I will not shy away, and I will not back down. You see fate has its way of working itself out. And on Evolution, Tyler, fate is coming for you. Fate is ready to give you what you deserve, and I have been chosen by fate, to give it to you. TUE or no TUE Tyler, there is no escaping your fate. So you come to the ring alone, or bring your buddies with you, the results will be the same. Why? Because the fact of the matter is this. When the lights go down, and that music hits. You walk down that aisle, and step into that ring, you are in MY WORLD. No matter what city you are in that night, no matter what the type of match is, in MY WORLD I HAVE NO EQUAL.' Ice turns around from the camera, and walks down the long hallway. The camera watches until he turns the corner, then cuts to commercial.
