The Pick Up...(Evo #2)

4 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/4/04 2:34.... Damn, he was late. It had been a while since Train was home. He picked a good day to return, the sun shinging brightly while the cool Chicago breeze kept the city temperate. Unfortunately he picked hell of a day to be late. Train knew the hustle and bustle of the city like no one else. This town didn't wait for anyone and the girl he was meeting... Well. She wasn't the type you wanted to disappoint. He was supposed to be there ten minutes ago. No doubt the delay wasn't his fault. Add up the plane being late and all the damn traffic and it would be obvious to anyone it was unavoidable. Almost anyone. He knew she'd be upset if he didn't show at the scheduled time. Plane delays...traffic... She wasn't gonna be trying to hear all that. She never seems to understand the way the world works. But Train had always dealt with it. Problem is seperation break down trust. He'd been gone a long while. Being tardy on top of that could make for an ugly scene. But however this pick up went down... Train was prepared. The shade of the large tree's cover his rented SUV as it turns the corner. As he pulls along the curb he sees the sign posted...'Drug Free School Zone'. It hadn't been long ago that he ignored that shit. It was all about the money and you did what you had to do to make it. It wasn't like this was the first pick up he'd done in this type of spot. Train slows the truck as he pulls up on the mass of cars and buses. Parents and teachers keep a watchful eye as the elementary age students scramble around headed either to their bus or the waiting cars of their parents. The crowd gathered in front of the small school, erected out of beige bricks and white concrete. Train put the truck in park and hopped out of the truck. Train wasn't exactly sure why so many eyes were turning to him. Perhaps in his Tim's and baggy jeans. His massive arms and shoulders displayed proudly through his sleeveless white t-shirt. His dreads casually swung back and forth pulled back in its ponytail. The platinum chain around his neck slid back and forth as he walked just as freely. All the while the gleam of the diamonds in the 'M.O.B' insignia hanging from the chain and in the face of his platinum Rolley didn't exactly help keep a low profile. Naw, they definitely kept him from looking like the average cat in the crowd. Could have been the tats too, the syndicate S's on his arms with his mentor's name written boldly under the cross band. Of course they could have just recognized him. Probably all though he was some nut case wrestler ready to snap at any moment. Hell, for all he knew they could have been laughing at him. I mean who wouldn't? That damn bandage on his forhead wasn't easy to hide and knowing he got it from some punk like McNasty? Train might have laughed himself if the damn stitches didn't hurt like they did. But that was ok. Come Evolution they was gonna be all settled up. An eye for an eye. Tooth for a tooth. Scar for a scar. And Brick... oh that cocksucker would probably learn to keep his mouth shut the second Train's fist pushed it's way into the big motherfucker. Like he owed that goody-goody, sellout ass nigga an explanation for what the fuck he does to feed his family. Punk ass mutha... Whoa. Hold up. He quickly derailed that train of thought the second he felt the hostility creeping up inside him. Save it for later, cuz that had no place in this deal. Besides... Whatever it was that was drawing their eyes, fact is, as he waded through the little bodies running about headed towards the front door, he got the feeling this wasn't going to go easy. Security being the way it was in this day and age. It could be a problem. But as he stopped on the walkway leading up to the front steps of the school he figured he'd rather have trouble picking up what he came for than it being a snap. Security was always a concern. His eyes survey the crowd as it slowly begins to thin. Finally he spots his contact with the pick up...and she was pretty lively today. He approached from behind, scoping the two. The woman had all the ear marks of a teacher. The nice outfit, that complimented her petite frame and would have drawn the attention of every boy in the crowd had this been a high school. Her reddish brown hair blew in the steady Chicago breeze while her carmel toned skin glowed in the afternoon light. Next to her, a little girl. The multicolored berrets were displayed proudly in her pig tails as she stood, her head sweeping back and forth, obviously looking for something. The little girl, dressed in a pair of nice blue jeans with a pink top and her little back pack slung over her little shoulder seemed upset, the teacher apparently trying to lend some comfort. 'Sweety... If he said he'd be here I'm sure he will.' the woman said in a kind, understanding tone. 'No he's not. He's always gone now and he doesn't come home anymore.' said the little girl in a slightly quivering, 8-year-old voice. 'Well, I'll tell you what sweetie,' begins the woman, still dispensing more comfort. 'We'll look for a few more minutes and if he's not here by then we'll call your mom, ok?' 'Alright....' the girl responds. 'But where else are we gonna look?' 'Try behind you.' Train interjects. The new voice in the conversation spins both the little girl and her companion. The second she see his face her face lights up. Her mouth opens quickly and Train finally gets what he came all this way for.... 'Uncle Tony!!!' the little girl screams, running towards him. Train extends his arms and the little girl leaps into them, greeting him with a hug that's as bear like as a girl her size can manage. 'What up baby girl?' Train gleefully inquires. Her response is a wrathful one, pushing herself back from him and bonking him on top of the noggin. 'Owww!' Train exclaims. 'What is your problem?!' 'I don't like it when you're late.' 'I'm sorry baby girl,' Train responds, knowing that the deal has turned as ugly as he suspected. ' I couldn't help it munchkin. My plane was late.' 'Whatever.' the little girl responds. 'Excuse you? Whatever?! Girl....' Realizing that this situation called for swift action to keep it under control he knew he had to doll out some discipline. He quickly takes his free arm and begins to tickle his neice unmercifly. She struggles to get away, but there is no escape from her doting uncle, her giggling unmaskable. 'You know who you talking to girl?!' 'hahahaha...No!!!' 'What?! You know me girl! Who am I?!' 'hahahahaha...Uncle Tony!' 'That's right! Now who's the man?!' 'You!!!' 'Dag on right!' Deciding that control has been reestablished he relents to her assertions and gives her another hug. He glances over the woman who'd been keeping his neice company and observes her warm grin as she observes the scene. 'Thanks for looking after her' he says to the attractive young woman. 'No problem,' she says as she approaches, still grining at the tenderness of an otherwise intimidating man. 'Monique is one of my favorite students.' 'So I take it to mean she's keeping the grades up.' 'Of course I am!' Monique forcefully insists. 'haha... She's doing great Mr. James. Or should I call you Train?' It was interesting being recognized nowadays. It was happening more and more as the weeks go on. Not that he'd never been before, but when it was it was usually by the cops. Now it was different. It was different having to noteriety as opposed to being infamous. And, with a hot school teacher (who he knew was a freak underneath that little suit of hers) staring at him, fame was showing it's benefits. 'You can call me Tony.' he knowingly retorts. Her grin grows a little wider with his response. 'Tony. Monique's mom...your sister, right?' 'Baby sister. Course she ain't the baby of the family no more.' 'Hey, I am not a baby a baby either!' 'Alright, alright... You ain't no baby.' Train says with a grin. 'That's right,' The teacher adds. 'Like I said Ms....' Train says, pausing at the end...slyly inquiring her name. 'Ms. Anderson. But you can call me Erika.' she responds, seeing past the slyness. 'Wel anyway Tony, your sister called to let us know you'd be picking up Monique so you're set to go.' 'Cool. Right baby girl?' 'Yup.' 'Well...ummm, good. You guys have a good day.' says Erika. 'Bye Ms. Anderson.' 'Bye sweetie. See you tomorrow.' Train, his neice in tow starts to turn, headed back to the truck, but he's turned by the sound of Ms. Anderson's voice. 'Well...' Ericka says nervously. 'Your sister told me at our last conference that you like to keep up with Monique's progress too. So... here.' She pulls a pin from her pocket along with a small piece of paper. She quickly scribbles her name along with a set of numbers. 'Here,' she continues. 'Here's my number. Give me a call. I'll keep you up to date on Monique's progress.' She gingerly steps forward and puts the slip of paper into Train's waiting hand. Their eyes meet as her hand brushes his, her face blushing just slightly as a smile pushes past her shyness. 'Well, I'll tell you what.' Train says. 'I got to head back down to Dallas tomorrow. How bout you and me get together later tonight. You know...discuss things over dinner.' 'Well... Sure that would be...' Erika begins, before being quickly cut off. 'Are you guys flirting?' Monique inquires. 'Flirting?!' Train asks. 'Now how you gonna just mess up a brotha's game like that?' 'Cuz it's funny!' she proclaims. 'Oh really?!' Train again has to resort to discipline, busting out another round of tickling. 'Remind me to talk to you about yo' timing baby girl.' 'hahaha... Well, anyway, like I was saying before I was interupted,' she continues with a big grin. 'I'd love to.' 'Bet' Train says. He waves the little piece of paper. 'I'll call you.' 'Looking forward to it. Bye Ms. Busybody. 'Bye Ms. Anderson.' Train turns and puts his neice back on her feet. The two walk hand in hand back to the waiting SUV.... Pick up complete.
