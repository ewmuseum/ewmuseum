Coming Out (Part Four)

20 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01.20.06 Ken's right hand graspsed a crevice in the side of the rock face and pushes off with his left foot, swinging his body to the right and allowing his right foot to find a home on a slight protrusion, all the while letting his left leg swing free. He tightens his grip with his right hand and better places his left in a narrow opening before looking up and, then, down. The fall was forty feet, and the climb was the same. He was exactly halfway up the face of the stone. 'Damn,' he said aloud as he glaned back up at the climb in front of him, 'I may be late for dinner.' The engagement he spoke of was with Kelli Alberdeane, his publicist. They were supposed to finish the Ken's 'Coming Out' story tonight over dinner at Honmura An Inc, New York's top Japanese dining experience. He pulled his left hand free long enough to check his Tag Hauer sports watch, which read '2:15.' He had more time than he'd guessed, he thought to himself as his brow raised in surprise. He didn't have to meet Kelli until 8:30. He should have plenty of time to make the climb and still get home and clean up. He continued using the halfway point as a resting opportunity as he gazed out over the landscapes. Upstate New York really was very beautiful. Eventhough it was null and void of any buildings or taxi cabs, the two hours out was probably Ken's favorite place for mental and physical exercise. As he reached behind him and into a pounch, salting his hands with climber's chalk, his flight home from Manchester flooded his memory. ---- Refusing to speak with Kelli Alberdeane, a frustrated and angry Ken Partridge, Jr. shot back into The Elite's private locker room like a bullet, causing the door to slam shut behind him. The disgruntled KPJ immediatly made his way to his bags and began to pull out a white Calvin Klein button-up and a pair of black CK slacks with gray pin stripes before heading to the shower. 'Yo,' shouted Bill Moresi, 'you alright?' Without answering, Ken continued his stomp into the restroom before coming to a halt in front of the love of his life, Gunner Addison. 'Ken,' she whispered ferociously, 'what in the hell is wrong with you?' 'You're kidding, right?' he resonded. 'You were out there for the damn match. You saw me blow my chance, once again for the Platinum Championship. I'm a former World Champion and the longest reigning International Champion of all time, Gun, but I can't get the last piece of tin I need to garner my Grand Slam. This is bullshit!' 'What are you talking about?' Gunner retaliated. 'You did one hell of a job out there.' 'Were you watching my match, Gun?' he questioned. 'I lost! Not only did I lose, but I lost to Jay Robson. I'll never hear the end of this.' ---- How Ken regretted speaking to Gunner like that. He couldn't believe he'd allowed his frustrations to get the best of him. He knew he'd get another shot at the Platinum Championship and he knew that he was good enough to win it. Now that he'd had time to cool off, hindsight was twenty-twenty. However, even with the way he spoke to his fiancee, there was something he was regretting more. ---- Upon arriving in Machester, Ken checked himself and Gunner into their hotel room after Josh, Bill, and Ace did the same for themselves. Once in the room, Gunner immediatly began taking a shower, asking Ken if he wanted to join. He, sleepily and reluctantly, turned the offer down. He kicked off his shoes and opened his smallest bag, which his laptop rested in. As he poured the Sony Vaio from the pouch, a white envelope fell out with it. Ken held it up and stared at the blank face of it for a few seconds before the aroma of a sweet and familiar perfume made it's way to his nose. 'Could this be from Gun,' he thought out loud as he opened it. However, the contents inside were entirely different. He held up the letter reading it to himself silently as a dread filled his gut. 'Ken, I know that we only knew each for a couple of short months, but I would be lieing if I told you that each day that we had didn't mean something to me. I don't know what it is I feel with you, but I do know it's real. You have no idea how much I could become Mimi to your Roger in our everyday lives. I longed to have you in my life as more than just my counterpart across the stage... I still do. I know that this isn't the most opportune time for me to tell you these things as you're engaged and probably in England by now, but I couldn't hold these feelings in anymore. I could tell that you felt something, too. Don't deny it. The kisses that we shared, eventhough they were on stage and in character, were the most intense kisses of my life and only solidified the startling fact that I have fallen madly and deeply in love with you. How I long to be Gunner, as I've seen the way you adore her, but you could have that with me. Ken, I will do whatever it takes for the two of to be together. Please, don't forsake me. I love you, Antonique' Ken simply sat with a blank look on his face, as though he'd just seen a ghost. 'Great,' he said aloud angrily. 'Fucking great!'
