Finding The Past (Part 2)

31 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08.31.04 The sunlight that beamed through the window of room 516 illuminated the expressionless face of Ken Partridge Jr as he was laying on his hospital bed iwth his arms behind his head. He gazed at the ceiling with his deep blue eyes that were full of both worry and confusion, but paid no attention to what was over him. The stubble on his face had become a good bit more than five o'clock shadow and was starting to irritate him a bit as he looked forward to tomorrow, when the doctors had finally told him he could shave again. 'My thoughts haunt me.' He had been running through the maze that was his mind nonstop at a hundred frames per second for the past few weeks. He simply wanted to find something... anything. A memory of his past that made him who he was. The answers to questions he didn't know. 'My lack of thoughts terrifies me.' --((Knock .. Knock))-- His endless race through the maze was interrupted by a rapping on his door, which was quickly followed by the squeek of the hinges as it opened, allowing the busy sounds of HealthSouth's athletic floor to flood the room. The noise pollution quickly died as the door was shut by a man whose face was familiar to Ken. A man that Ken remembered... and remembered well. A man who lived in Ken's past. 'B-Bill... is that you,' asked Ken in disbelief. 'Of course, who else would it be,' responded former NeWave Wrestling founder and owner and longtime friend of Ken, Bill Moresi. Bill was dressed as Ken remembered him dressed... white v-neck polo and a pair of light beige slacks by Versace. 'Man,' continued Moresi, 'you look damn old with that beard.' 'Thanks,' laughed Ken, 'but I feel inclined to tell you that your face makes you look like shit.' 'Well,' responded Bill, 'I see you haven't forgotten your sense of humor.' 'Not yet... at least, I don't think I have.' The shared a laugh again at Ken's lame joke before Bill turned serious in his questioning to Ken. 'What... What happened on that highway, Champ,' asked Bill. 'What brought you in here like this?' Ken's eyes drifted from Bill just as the smile had drifted from his face as he responded with a much more meloncholy tone of voice than before. 'I -- I don't remember. I spend days looking for clues that will help jog my memory, but i have no idea what I'm even looking for. I just -- I just wish I remembered-- --that night.' The rainw as hitting the dark pavement in needle-like form doing it's best impression of a typhoon as Ken puuled to a stop in front of a red traffic light. As he reached down to change the song on his CD player, a sense came over him to look up. The rearview mirror... The bright headlights... His face... His familiar face... 'Wake up,' shouted Bill as he shook a seemingly unconscious KPJ. 'Dammit, Ken, wake the hell up!' Bill looked up towards the door, screaming for help a he continued his attempt to revive his friend back into reality. 'Don't do this to me, man,' whispered Moresi. 'You're all the family-- --I got.' Ken smiled at his good friend's comment. 'Thank, Bill. You're like family to me as well,' responded Partridge. 'You have no idea how much it means to me that a member of family got to see me defeat Osiris for this Grappling God Championship tonight,' said Ken as he readjusted his newly won eXtreme Championship Wrestling Alliance title over his right shoulder. 'I'm not sure I could have beaten him without your help and training, Bill,' said Ken. 'Ahhh,' responded Bill, 'you had him all along. You're strong like that, Ken. You're--' '--strong.' The Global Wrestling resident 'Superstar' blinked his eyes open as the moonlight beemed through his window. He looked to his left to see Bill asleep in the room's recliner before noticing the extreme pain that pounded inside of his head. He shut it out quickly enough and closed his eyes again in an attempt to get more sleep. Hopefully, the answers would come in the morning. 'I am strong.'
