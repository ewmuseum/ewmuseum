Becoming 'El Mariachi' Pt. Uno

3 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/3/04 I can't believe they had actually done put me with this dutz, 'Fat' James! I'm not even allowed to talk when the freakin camera is around. Apperantly it seems that 'Fat' James, my new spokesperson of sorts has managed to squeeze out a good contract that puts 5,000 bucks in my pockets. 'Hey you can speak now buddy, the cameras gone away,' 'Fat' James meshes his big blubbery body into the back of the white limo. 'You keep working with me and you'll have all the cans of beans you want buddy.' 'Look here Fat James! You can quit the games esse. I'm not some ignorant Mexican boy that doesn't know my ins and outs...' 'Fat' James holds up his hands, 'Whoa! Slow down partner. I aint trying to see your red hot tamele burst! Now take it easy okay?' I knew I'd end up killing the man one day, but right now he was good use for me. Anytime I came into the view of a camera I was supposed to play the dumb ignorant masked Mexican wrestler, at the TV shows, and anytime I made an appearance. It was like being in prison without the bars. But this would do for the time being. 'So listen here okay Pedro, I just got off the phone with them guys over at GW, and it seems that your first match is going to be against this kid named 'Xtreme' Dylan Ford...heh-ghuh-ha! Reminds me of a kid I used to know in school, is name was Kasey Ford.' 'I don't care 'Fat' James!' 'Camera!' I slipped my mask on and watched as the door flew open. The hot Texas sun hit my face and I knew that I was going to hate wearing this mask. We moved through a crowd of reporters that had gathered around the limo, 'Fat' James through his coat over my head and covered me so the cameras wouldn't get a good look at me. 'Goddfrey James, why did you leave the car sales business and become a wrestling manager?' 'Who is that masked wrestler?' 'Is it true that you're now managing a Mexican terrorist?' We slipped past all the reporters and into a helicopter that was waiting for us. The wind gust from the chopper almost caused me to lose the coat that was covering my head. 'Fat James' screamed something at me, but it was nearly impossible to hear. Finally when we got into the chopper he leaned over to me and whispered... 'You stick with me boy, you'll be travelling in class Pedro. Everywhere you go.' 'Oh shut your mouth 'Fat' James! People like you are what make me sick! You think you have everything made, but the truth of the matter is that you're gonna burn in hell! Just like all the other sinners! Your fat red face will crave for water and food, but your fat tail will only be consumed by flames!' 'Howdy guys, I'm Cowboy Charlie, I've been hired by GW to fly you guys out of this run down Mexican town and get you guys to Dallas, Texas. Where the cowboys roam and the woman are free!' 'You get us there as quick as you can, you here me Charlie!' 'Yessum!' Pathetic! Those are the only words the can describe the situation. Here I have a former car sales man, and a grease monkey running this chopper! I had no idea what to expect, I didn't trust Charlie to fly this thing all the way to Dallas, but I was keeping my fingers crossed. 'So what you think about that first match I managed to get you?' 'You mean at Overload?' 'Yeah...' 'Well I figured a man of your stature would atleast debut me at the main show, but I should've known.' 'Hey-hey Pedro! I'm working on it, but you gotta pay your dues. They aint gonna throw a fired up hot tamale into the ring like that without seeing what he can do first. Plus boy, I've already got a HUGE debut for you lined up.' 'Fat' James pulled out a duffel back and tossed it to me, he said, 'Open it!' I opened it and pulled out a red mask, and red wrestling trunks, a red cape, red knee braces, and red arm tape. 'Do I look like the kool-aid man or something? Why'd you get it all in red?' 'It matches your firery personality!' 'Yeah I'll show you firery!' Great! I was going to look like a steaming red hot chili pepper. To make things worse the guy I was wrestling I had no idea about, I didn't know if he liked me, or if he liked woman. I had no idea what I was stepping into but I knew that I was going to come out on top. I might not look like the kool aid man at the start of the match, but when Dylans blood is running down my hands I'll sure look like him then. 'Just take a nap Pedro, we'll be arriving shortly.' Best idea that he's had all day! Atleast if Charlie crashed the chopper I'd be asleep when I died. I looked out of the window and closed my eyes. I drifted off into eternity...I drifted off into another world.
