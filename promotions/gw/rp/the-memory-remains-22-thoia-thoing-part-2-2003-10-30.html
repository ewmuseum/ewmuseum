The Memory Remains #22 (Thoia Thoing Part 2)

30 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 103003 Deep breath in. Deep breath out. Arms extended. Muscles relaxed. Concentrate. Breathe. Silence. Breathe. He moves, I move. Running now. Nerve ends firing. Muscles pumping. Punch thrown at my face. Block. Counterattack, punching. He moved his head to the side. Counterattack, crook arm in, changing jab into and elbow. Miss. Damn. Defense, catch the sidekick. Spin opponent. Lock arms around waist and fling backwards. Both of us go flying. The world spinning. Up again. Facing each other. Breathe. Silence. Breathe. Attack. Jab, jab, undercut, high knee to the face. Short spear to his gut. Roll with the momentum, flipping over the body, landing on my feet. No-look back flip to a knee drop. Shit. No one there. Pain jolts my body. Screams. Not mine. But the crowd. Silence. Breathe. Air moving, I can feel it, I can hear it. MOVE! Roll to the left. Noise. A thud. Two feet striking the platform. Another thud. Opponent falling to the ground. Shift weight to a handstand, falling, dropping knees onto opponent’s chest. Connecting. Kick back up into second handstand. Movement. Opponent moving. Reposition hands and weight. Calculated fall. Legs striking back of opponent’s knees, collapsing them. Opponent falling onto me. Jumbled heap. He strikes my face with his elbow. Once. Twice. Thrice. Four times. Jolts of pain traveling through my body. Rolling away. Both of us standing slowly. Breathe. Taking a fighting stance. Breathe. Charge. Fighter turning, running away. He jumps. I recognize the move. He bounces of the wall, aiming a fierce spinning kick to my face. Arms up. Catch the leg, moving with the momentum. Pulling him down. Down. Down, turning his kick into a massive throw. He bounces off the ground. I continue traveling after him, my shoulder landing in his gut. I roll, still holding onto the leg, pulling it painfully up before letting go. Breathe. Fighter up, with a limp. Breathe. Re-stance. Breathe. Run in, baseball slide to take out the legs. Fighter flips, up and over. Pain in my back. I fly forward from the force of the blow. I lay there. Movement at my legs. I feel my feet being locked in place somehow. He grabs my arms, pulling back. Momentum shifts, I got into the air, locked in a cradle. Pain flies through my body. I hear a voice. “Submit.” It causes me to pause. “Never,” I spit back. Oh. My. God. Pain. Moves. Fast. I. Try. To. Ignore. It. I. Push. It. Away. I block it out. Breathe. In. Out. Breathe. Move. I begin to rock. Back and forth. Ignore the pain. Shove it aside. Rock. Back. Forth. Harder. Harder. He begins to give. The motion forcing him onto his neck. The pressure on his neck creating his own pain. He releases and we collapse together again. A knee to my stomach. I am on my knees. Standing up now. A swift kick to my face. I fall backwards, landing on my back. I bunch up. A crouched hardstand turned into a handspring. Both feet plant themselves in the fighters face. We both lay there. Breath. Breath. I slowly move. On my stomach. On my knees. I began to stand. Shaky legs. Re-stance. Breathe. Silence. Breathe. We rush forward. I launch my self into a massive flying spinning kick. The fighter does the same. Our legs collide in mid-air, locking. We tumble down and strike the platform hard. Breathe. I roll slowly away. Breathe. On my knees. Breathe. On my feet. Breathe. The fighter stands on unsure footing. I cock my head to one side. He does the same. We bow towards each other. The crowd, silent, roars in approval. I motion to the man to remove his mask to that I might see the man who almost bested me. He hesitates before removing the heavy mask. Long raven black hair falls away, revealing light toned skin and almond shaped eyes. I take a step back. The fighter was a woman.
