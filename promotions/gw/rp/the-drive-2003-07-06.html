The Drive

6 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/6/03 The cool night air howls through the stars in the sky as the scene opens up. It's eerie tone is accompanied by the low rumble of what sounds like an engine. Brandi Sights and her loyal cameraman cautiously walk through the feintly lit parking garage, searching for Ice, who called them earlier and promised to be there. Brandi's heavy breathing, along with the cameraman's, describes the fear that they have walking around in the dark, unknowing of what is around them. A car door is heard opening, and then is slammed shut. Brandi freezes and quickly turns to the cameraman. Her lower lip is trembling and her heavy breathing has become more of a faster pace. From the shadows of the many cars all around her comes a hand. The hand grabs Brandi's right shoulder. She jumps, and screams all in one motion at the touch of the hand. The rest of the body attatched to the hand slowly enters the dim light. Ice stands with a serious look on his face. Brandi lets out a deep sigh and begins to chuckle in embarrassment. Just as she puts the microphone to her face and begins to speak Ice raises his left index finger silencing her before she can utter out a word. Ice turns and walks back into the shadows, and Brandi grabs her cameraman by the right arm and quickly drags him along as she begins to follow. They catch up to Ice as he opens the door to a black Ford Explorer. Ice pauses before entering the vehicle almost as if he is waiting for Brandi and the cameraman to enter it with him. Brandi rushes over to the Ford Explorer and is followed by her cameraman. She goes to the passenger's side door, but smiling through the window is Star. A scowl engulfs Brandi's face as she and the cameraman enter the back seats of the black Ford Explorer. Ice turns on the headlights, turns on the radio, and drives away out of the parking garage, and into the night. 'Ice, Star, what is the reasoning for this little trip we are taking? Where are we going? And why did you request this so late?' Star turns her head to Ice and smiles. Ice nods back in return and turns the volume up on the radio. 'Haunted' by Evanescence blares from the speakers. Dejected by the lack of answering to her questions Brandi huffs and sits back in her seat as Ice drives on. Star leans over and murmurs something to Ice that cannot be picked up by the camera or Brandi's microphone because of the music ringing through the Explorer. Star finishes whatever she was saying and Ice smirks. The vehicle begins to slow down and turns down a gravel road. Brandi leans forward again. 'Umm...excuse, but I don't like this right now, could you tell me where we are going? Please?' Star turns back and looks at her with a smile on her face, and Ice glances back with a smile as well. He turns up the volume again. And makes another turn. This time however the turn is not down a road, or a gravel path. This time the turn is taken into an open grass field of sorts. The song comes to an end, Ice stops the vehicle and steps out of it. Star follows in suit, followed shortly by Brandi and the cameraman. Ice looks around and takes in a deep breath. Star hops onto the hood of the black Ford Explorer and sits down, with her legs dangling off the edge of it. Ice leans up against the side of the vehicle and stares up into the dark star-filled sky. Brandi goes to make her way towards Ice but is stopped by Star. Star begins to smile, slowly leans back on the hood of the vehicle, and lays down looking up at the stars shining brightly in the sky. Victoria looks up briefly and then makes her way over towards Ice. Ice continues to gaze up at the night sky as he begins to speak. 'My dear boy Tyler Pierce.' Ice begins to shake his head. 'Look up at the sky, though the wind howls it is still calm and unwavering. Just as I do not waver, I do not get upset, I stay calm, and I stay focused. However Tyler, I would like you to look deep inside yourself and see if you really are prepared to live up to what is expected of you. Look at the facts. I, still have yet to taste defeat here in the GW. Tyler, are you capable of that? All alone, without the help of your precious TUE? Or are you expecting the same kind of help this Tuesday night at Evolution? If your answer to those questions is no, then you have a long night ahead of you on Evolution. Now as for your lack of remarks about me I will say this one more time, do not ignore me, and do not overlook me. Because I am not some run of the mill 'superstar' who will talk this talk, and not back it up. I will, just as I have week in, and week out since coming to this company, keep my word. And right here, right now, I PROMISE, that when Evolution is over, you are going to be looking at the new Cruiser Champ. And Tyler, when I'm done punking your ass out, I will climb up that ladder of success, to the next rung, and slowly continue my methodical, and unrelenting take over of this god for saken company. However, unlike you and your little group, I don't lose focus that easily. My eyes are set directly on you, and in time I will get to the man I am after, but believe me, he already knows that I am waiting for him. But that will come in time. Right now I am focused on you.' Ice looks down from the night sky and turns his attention to the camera. His cold icy blue, intense stare pierces through the camera. 'Tyler, you concern yourself too much with The Nevermore, or what's left of them. You concern yourself too much with that ....Genisis, and those Tag Team Titles. Myself however, I am concerned right now, with you and only you. I don't need sh!tty leather belts with gold spray painted tin on them. My talents in the ring speak for themself. But if beating your ass in the middle of MY RING, in the middle of MY WORLD gets me one of them, then I will gladly take it, because Star likes a little gold to accessorize. This Tuesday night, on Evolution, you will find out first hand, that I am not cocky, I am not arrogant, rather I am as good as I say I am.' Ice smirks and opens the driver's side door to the black Ford Explorer. Star jumps off the hood of the vehicle and enters in the passenger's side. Ice starts up the engine and Brandi and the cameraman rush to get in the vehicle before Ice decides to drive away. 'Wanted Dead of Alive' by Bon Jovi plays on the radio as Ice drives. The scene fades to black.
