A Double Awakening

21 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08-21-2005 For the first time in her life Dawn wished that all she could hear was the sound of her own heartbeat, the thundering of blood in her ears and the rasp of her own breath scraping her lungs. Anything would have been better then the cacophony of sound that seemed to surround her, although by far the worst of it was behind her’behind her and gaining. She stumbled on a loose branch, almost losing her footing, but catching herself at the last minute with a grace that had been learned from years in the ring. She risked a look behind her, only getting an eyeful of writhing shadows in the faint moonlight for her effort. She turned her head back to the task of dodging branches and put on an extra burst, winding around a familiar pine tree and hurdling over a row of bushes. Having spent most of her natural life on these grounds, Dawn was intimately familiar with the winding deer paths and game trails that dotted that forest that took up the majority of the McFarlane estate. When she was younger she had played with her father for hours in the woods, ducking behind bushes and climbing trees to evade “The Bear” as he called himself. It had always ended with him catching her, and then she had been mercilessly tickled until she squealed for mercy’even when she was a teenager. If she was caught this time though? Dawn shook her head forcibly at the thought, trying not to dwell on what would likely happen if the noises and the shadows caught her tonight. She was already slowing from the exertions of the chase, and her pursuers didn’t seem to be any closer to flagging then they were when the chase had started. Any chance she’d had of stealth was also gone, her feet were torn and bleeding, leaving a telltale trail of bloody footprints that easily showed where she had gone and how long it had been since she’d passed. “What was I thinking?” She asked herself, wishing for the hundredth time that she wasn’t in her sleeping clothes’even her usual sleeping arrangements would have been better, but she’d taken to heading to bed in a tank top and underwear’neither garment was well suited to tromping around the outdoors. “I might as well be fucking naked!” She grumbled, wondering again what she was thinking. A bright light came into view through the trees, a brilliant circle of daylight that seemed like the gates of heaven itself as far as Dawn was concerned. The Estate. Someone had activated the UV lights and hopefully that someone would open the back door when a crazy woman in her underwear came streaking across the back lawn with howling shadows biting at her heels. She poured on more speed, forcing her burning lungs to pump in more oxygen, forcing her aching muscles to push just that extra bit harder and forcing her thumping heart to push that much more blood through her system. Where were the dogs? Her mind was surprisingly lucid and she hoped that her mysterious visitor hadn’t let the dogs outside. They were a ferocious pair, but she didn’t want to risk either of them being injured or killed against’whatever it was. Aside from Lia being furious when she returned, Dawn had grown quite fond of Damek and Jirina while they’d been staying with her. She broke from the edge of the forest, her aching feet nearly slipping in her own blood as they hit the well tended, lush grass that made up the back yard. Again she caught herself, losing precious seconds as the howling “things” behind her continued to advance. She sprinted towards the light, wondering why she couldn’t see the house through the bright light. “Must’must be the angle of the light?” She gasped. She slid into the circle of light, her legs finally giving out from under her as her feet slipped in’nothing. As Dawn pitched towards the ground she suddenly realized it wasn’t there any longer, all around her was white, a brilliant white haze that obscured everything beyond it. For the first time that night she could hear the sound of her own breathing, hear the sound of her heart hammering in her ears’and she could hear a familiar voice, a voice she hadn’t heard in the better part of eight years. “Are you certain is has to be like this” Why can’t we just tell her?” The voice was deep and rich, a warming baritone that washed over Dawn and made her skin break out into Goosebumps. She rolled towards the sound, barely making out a fuzzy figure through the nearly blinding light. “Daddy?” She croaked, her throat still sore from the run. “James please, if there was any other way’don’t you think I’d be the first one to suggest it” I don’t want this any more then you do’but it’s for her own good, you know that.” The second voice was female, a melodic sound that surrounded and embraced Dawn, pulling her head towards the second fuzzy shape. Even through the blinding circle, Dawn could see a halo of golden light around her head, one that seemed to emanate from a spot on her forehead no bigger then a quarter. Dawn’s breath caught and she could almost feel her heart slow??Mom?” The golden light turned towards her and suddenly she could see nothing else, just a shower of golden light around her. “It’s alright Dawn, it’ll all be alright I promise. You go back to sleep, and when you wake up everything will be better.” Dawn stretched out an arm, reaching towards that distant shape. “Mom wait’please’please don’t leave me again?” ************************************************************************************ “MOM!” Dawn came up out of bed with a shout, waking both hellhounds and bringing Jirina up onto her bed with a startled “woof!”. She blinked twice, the haze of her dream hanging heavily around her, and stared at her outstretched hand. Her hand and arm were covered in golden light, bathed in the same warm glow that she remembered from her dream. She gazed around the room in numb surprise, seeing the same golden light everywhere. Her eyes settled on the door to her balcony, shut and securely locked, but with the drapes flung open to let in the morning sun. “You dork?” She grumbled to herself. “It’s just the sun.” She kicked her legs over the side of her bed, stretching her arms up over her head and reaching over to scratch Jirina’s head with one hand. “I’m sorry I startled you darling, I was just having a bad dream.” Jirina gazes reproachfully at her for a moment, those too intelligent eyes shining out from under the shaggy black fur of her face. Dawn laughed, ruffling the hellhound’s ears. “I’m alright, really.” Hopping from the side of the bed, Dawn paused as she caught sight of herself in the mirror. A familiar pair of black panties and a tight black tank top greeted her, and she scrunched her face up in confusion. “I don’t remember putting these on?” Shaking her head at herself Dawn padded across the dark carpet to her closet. She opened the doors, heading towards the back where her familiar pants and tops hung’and then paused, eyes catching on a bright swatch of colour just off to her right. She reached through the piles of punk and goth clothing to an unfamiliar sarong hanging near the back, pulling it out and holding it up to the light. “Normally it’s not my style but?” But since Lia had kissed her in the gardens, Dawn couldn’t deny that she was feeling a little softer’a little more feminine. She reached in to retrieve a small t-shirt from near the same place, holding it up to her torso in the closets mirror. Normally she didn’t like to wear tops that showed off her midriff outside the ring’but again, she was just feeling a little’a little more free. She looked back at the dogs, Jirina on her bed and Damek on the floor. “I’m gonna have a shower and then I think it’s breakfast time for all of us. I’ll be out in fifteen minutes, if I’m not out by then, send in the marines!” Dawn slipped into the bathroom, closing the door behind her. To Damek and Jirina it seemed that when she left the room, the sunlight became just the smallest bit duller. ************************************************************************************ Under cover of the dense haze of early evening, Shadow made sure the path was clear for Virgil, who bore Cyrus carefully on his back, balanced in between his shoulders, as though the seven foot Kiasyd weighed absolutely nothing. They were finally able to approach the McFarlane Estate; Virgil and Shadow might have had somewhat limited mobility during the daytime hours, but Cyrus still bore the curse of their kind, and having him along had slowed the journey to Toronto considerably. Not that Shadow was complaining. Plenty of times, Cyrus had done favors for her- it was about time she repaid him. His wounds were just now beginning to heal, but due to Lilith's concentrated brutality, it would be a long time before Cyrus would ever be able to go back to Zdice, much less be the charming, if hermit-like gentleman that he usually was. Shadow held up a hand in silent signal for Virgil to pause in his step as she shifted her weight on the doorstep. Right now, she was carrying both of their bags; the International Title hung around hers in the place where the Television Title once had, the slightly fancier script still pleasing to the eye even without sunlight gleaming reflectively off of its nameplate. 'Merde...' His eyes gleamed intensely in the low light; despite enhanced strength, you could only bodily carry someone who was taller than you for so long before it got a little unwieldy, to say the least. The French curse came to her lips easily, despite the fact that she probably couldn't have called to mind where, exactly, she'd learned the language from. She realized that the keys for the front door must have been in the pocket farthest away from her. Of course. Rolling her eyes, she set Virgil's bag down on the porch so that she could get to the zipped pouch that she needed. Dawn listened to the sound of a car roll into the driveway, the throaty purr of the engine noticible before it was even at the front gates. She gave no indication of movement however, as she didn't particularly want visitors right now...especially the uninvited kind. Footstep on the front step earned a sigh of annoyance as she turned the next page, refusing to budge from her comfortable spot on the couch. In unison the Hell Hounds heads came up and around at the same time, and the pair of monstrous canine's scrabbled to their feet and took off towards the front door, barking joyously. For Dawn this could mean only one thing...and her reaction was remarkably similar. The book she was reading found itself on a long spiral towards the floor as she launched up from her prone position, catapulting over the arm of the couch and speeding toward the front door. Nervous fingers fumbled with the deadbolt for a moment before she managed to unlock it, throwing the door wide...and sure enough, there was Lia. There was an uncomfortable pause as Dawn's mind sifted through a variety of responses...but in truth she could settle on only one. She threw her arms around Lia, squeezing the slightly smaller Vampiress to her with a hug that would have likely felled most mortals. 'Jesus Christ Lia, I've been frantic...' Her words were a strangled whisper, and lacked any of the bite her housemate had come to expect. Lia, honestly more than a little surprised, only wrapped one arm around Dawn before letting her heavy bag slide to the ground to embrace her stablemate (friend?!) with the other. The hug was short-lived, however, as you might have called Lia rather shy. She pulled away for a moment, perhaps feeling a little bit too much comfort from the familiar vitality of Dawn's scent. But... there was still a smile on her face, even if it was rather wry. She hadn't been expecting a greeting at all, much less a familiar, or even a pleased greeting. Perhaps this would last. She dug in her pocket and pulled out a cell phone, the body of it cracked straight down the middle, holding it up so that Dawn could see. 'You could say I got caught in the middle of a little too much action and couldn't call.' She jerked her head back towards Virgil, who was shifting Cyrus uncomfortably on her shoulder. 'Plus, Cyrus has been hanging between life and death for a couple of weeks now... We just got him stable enough to move.' Dawn's gaze went from Lia's face to the damaged phone and then towards the semi-concious Cyrus. She did a quick double take at the sight of the seven foot vampire draped casually over Virgils shoulders. Her expression warred between curiosity and concern, finally settling somewhere in the middle as she stepped out of the doorway. 'Bring him inside so you can put him down then. Does he need any special arrangements? Or Are the guest rooms on the third floor alright?' Lia stepped aside to allow Virgil in. Of course, Virgil was rolling his eyes and being wordlessly difficult, as per usual. He didn't appreciate the silent appraisal when he was sitting here carting around a package full of dead weight. Then again, he didn't appreciate much of anything when he was in that situation. He dropped Cyrus into his arms, draping Cyrus' long black hair so that it hung towards the floor- as Lia watched Virgil step past, she noted how horrified Cyrus would have been at his appearance, had he not been unconscious at the moment. Lia nodded at her paramour, and Virgil started up the stairs, as she turned back to Dawn, dragging in both of their bags. 'The only thing we really need is darkness. He's not quite as fond of the sun as I, and I'm afraid he'd be rather displeased were he to greet it. And in that case... the third floor will do just fine.' Dawn nodded, wordlessly scooping up one of Lia's bags and closing the door behind the strange entourage. 'All right. All the rooms on that floor are like yours, so you can put him in any of them, just make sure the shutters are closed.' As Virgil started up the stairs Dawn placed her free hand on one of Lia's shoulders, concern shining in the depths of her emerald eyes. 'Are you alright?' Lia looked at Dawn for a moment... and then looked away. There was no easy way to answer this question without setting off alarm bells. Her lips twisted into a displeased grimace. 'Yes and no. I'm alright in that I'm in one piece, and Virgil, myself and Cyrus are still alive, though barely in the last case. Other than that, I'm not faring so well.' She wasn't sure if that would be enough to satisfy Dawn's curiosity or not- but as of yet, Lia wasn't sure how to tell Dawn what she'd discovered in Zdice...nor who she had discovered it from. Dawn watched her companion for a long moment, her insides twisting around in a mixture of concern and nervousness. 'Can I help?' Instant reflex caused her to shake her head in a 'no' motion. She looked at Dawn, her jade eyes digging in intensely. 'This is nothing that you need to be involved in. And I say that not out of any disrespect for your courage or skill, but out of the simple fact that I'm not sure it's something I need to be involved in either, though I may not have much choice in the matter.' Dawn chewed her lower lip in frustration. 'Why wouldn't you have any choice?' Lia grimaced, throwing her hands up from her sides, though her frustration was not directed at Dawn. 'It's already started. Without any training or help on that... on Soma's part... I'm already right in the middle of this war, and it's already started affecting allies.' She nodded towards the staircase to indicate Cyrus- but then her irritation faded and something of a mix between fear and concern threaded through her eyes as she glanced back at Dawn. 'I don't want it to start affecting people that I care about too.' Dawn's throat constricted very suddenly and she found it difficult to speak. Memories of the kiss in the garden flooded into her mind, leaving her nearly speechless. 'Lia...' Her voice came out as a chaoked whisper and she grimaced, 'I don't want you to stand alone.' For a moment, the muscles in Lia's throat tightened. as she tried to deny the words that were threatening to spill out. 'Why?' She blinked forcibly. 'Why put yourself in the middle of this Dawn? You don't need this... extra. Not right now.' A thousand answers were in Dawn's mind and out again in the space of that blink, with only one answer...the truthful answer, sitting on the tip of her tongue. She struggled to force it out, weeks of worry and concern sticking in her throat like a physical block. 'Because...' She whispered, speaking through force of will alone. '...because I care for you...because I...' Again the words stuck on her throat, frustration building up behind them. 'She's right there!' Her mind screamed, 'Just TELL her!' 'Because I love you.' The words were little more then a whisper, a ghost of a sound that hung in the air between them. For a moment, Lia felt the adrenaline soaring through her veins. 'Did she just say that?' 'No... no she didn't,' answered another voice almost immediately. 'You fool, yes she did! Say something!' 'I...' She coughed, then looked back up at Dawn. '... love you too,' she finished. The words had always been difficult for her- but these words were true words. Why else would life at the estate have been so difficult? And why else would she have stayed despite that? Silence took over, embarrassed silence on the part of the slim, waifish vampiress. Dawn stood there for a long moment, her green eyes wide...she had been hoping, had DARED to hope that she wouldn't be laughed at...but only in her most intimate dreams had she imagined this would happen. A bright smile split her face, almost the first genuine smile she'd had in over a year, shining like a small sun in the foyer of the estate. There was only one thing left to do really... ...and she stepped forward, gathering Lia in her arms and pulling the Vampiress close. Her lips hovered close for a long moment as she scarcely dared to believe it was happening...and then she pressed her lips to Lia's, months of unspoken emotions pouring down her cheeks and over their lips. Lia had no choice but to kiss her back, enveloped by the warmth of her lips and their contrast to her cold skin. It was perhaps like an awakening- the awakening of a feeling that had been long buried, but a feeling that had needed its own moment of expression. Her fingers reached up to gently stroke through the ends of Dawn's hair, her hands shaking slightly. Dawn reluctantly broke the kiss after a long moment, taking deep breaths of air through her mouth. She kept her forehead pressed to Lia's, her arms encircling the other womans waist. 'I'm sorry...' She murmured, '...sorry it took so long...to say.' Lia nodded in agreement. 'So am I...' Her thumb ran gently across the small of Dawn's back. Even as calm as she was, there was a part of her that was very reluctant to drag Dawn into a deeper personal connection, what with all the excess in her life right now. Lia's cool finger against Dawn's bare skin elicited a shiver, and not from the cold. She gazed into Lia's eyes, emerald on emerald as if she was trying to plumb the depths of her thoughts. 'I don't want you to face this alone...' Lia smiled slightly, a bit of a smart-assy look if ever there was one. Her eyes flicked towards the staircase for a moment. 'And you're not the only one who feels that way...' She paused, stubbornness coming back to the forefront, after the initial shock. 'I dug my own hole... I don't want anyone getting in the middle of this who doesn't deserve to be.' Dawn scrunched her eyes shut at the mention of Virgil. 'Oh gods...this is going to make things difficult. ' She opened them again, flashing another smile. 'Not that I care.' 'I worry about you...and I want to help. I may not be like either of you...but I can hold my own...' Her voice was almost pleading. Slowly, Lia could feel the locks starting to break, the dam starting to crumble. She couldn't resist- not at the thought of having them by her side, both of them; for some reason, the thought was comforting far beyond what she felt was her duty to protect her friends. Her fingertips trailed softly down Dawn's forearm, giving a reassuring squeeze. 'I know that you can... there was never any doubt in my mind about that.' She paused again, the last of her resolve slowly fading into dust. 'Fine,' she said, '... but promise me one thing...' 'Anything.' The reply was swift, leaving no room for Dawn to second guess herself. Lia smiled... both at Dawn's willingness to please, and also a bit sadly- the coming months were not going to be pleasant. 'If you're going to get involved... please just listen to me. That's all that I ask. I believe you're a wonderfully strong person, a wonderfully talented one at that. But if things get too heavy, I'm likely to tell you to do something that you won't like... namely, to run. I'll promise you that I won't do it unless circumstances are dire... but you have to promise me that you'll do it if I ask, without asking any questions.' Dawn's jaw tightened up and her lips pressed together in a thin line. 'I've never run from a fight...and I've never left anyone behind...' Lia winced slightly- she should have known this wasn't going to be easy. 'Dawn, please... I'm not trying to insult your honor.' Her voice slipped, somewhat unflatteringly, into pleading. 'I know, I know...' Dawn's heart seized, trying to fight with the notion of having to leave someone she loved behind. 'But I can't...I couldn't just leave you, not when you needed me...' Lia shook her head softly, her eyes clouding with thought. 'The two of you are stubborn, and I know that you both know that.' She looked back up at Dawn. 'Know this, then... I won't let either you or Virgil be harmed, no matter the circumstances that it takes to prevent that. That means sacrificing myself if I must, and if you can't accept that... then I won't allow you to help at all.' The lines of Lia's jaw hardened slowly. She could be just as stubborn as Dawn if she wanted to. Dawn glared, seeing the telltale lines of her own famous stubborness in Lia's features. 'Dammit you're insufferable sometimes...' She sighed, her shoulders sagging a little. 'Fine...but I will fight tooth and nail to ensure that doesn't happen. You can't ask me not to...' Lia leaned forward, gently pressing her lips to the side of Dawn's neck, just under her chin. The gesture was intimate and affectionate, as though the two women had known each other for far longer than just the bare-bones year that WMW and GW had given them. Lia looked the inch-or-two up to Dawn, knowingly. 'I know I can't. And I won't. If I did, I'd be stifling one of the things that I like the most about you.' Dawn blushed, a light pink that crept up into her cheeks from her neck. 'Thank you...I won't let you down, I promise.' Lia smiled, but it was only slight, before it faded away into an expression of stoic neutrality once again. She stepped away from Dawn, letting her fingertips trail, almost reluctantly. 'I'm sorry... I have to go check on Cyrus, and make sure that the trip here didn't do any extra damage.' She started up the winding staircase, but paused only a few steps up to look back at Dawn, sincerity gleaming from behind her veneer. 'I missed you.' And just like that, she continued up the stairs, her step light, graceful and nearly silent. Dawn slid slowly to her knees, all of the strength having fled from her legs. She stared at her reflection in the polished tiling of the foyer, raising one disbelieving hand to her lips, her face splitting into another happy smile. She pushed herself up with wobbly legs, staggering to the front door and throwing it open so she could weave to the front lawn and fall to her knees in the lush grass. She turned her eyes to the moon, a tremendous feeling building in her chest. 'WAHOOOOOOOOOOOO!' She shouted, 'YAHOOO!'
