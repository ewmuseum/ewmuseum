Warming Up

30 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-30-05 Toronto, Canada, July 28th Dawn McFarlane's Estate - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - Virgil watched as Lia's body relaxed into the bed, slowly succumbing to the herbal mixture he'd concocted to relax her, finally proving the skill's worth to him. Something hadn't been right over the last couple of days, and Virgil knew very clearly what that something was. He had never met Dawn McFarlane before, a similar case with most of the humans he worked with. It was generally his hypothesis that were he to ignore the humans, they would do the same to him, and his affairs could proceed unhindered, now that he was finally in the company of his beloved Lia once again. He loathed being wrong. Dawn seemed to be one of the women who seem to attract attention from everyone... so why was it that she was forced into the life of someone who had no interest? Ah... yes. She wanted to monopolize Lia's time and affection. How could he forget? He was not quite as annoyed by her as by Leinad Soma, but there was an added element of jealousy when Soma was involved- that of Soma being more powerful. Dawn was just a human, a mortal. Virgil knew he could pull her spine from her body through her throat with little effort, and this was, perhaps, the only thing that kept him from doing so. Lia might not have understood her feelings toward Dawn, but Virgil could easily see what Dawn wanted. It was obvious, the way she acted like a pathetic little child whenever Virgil was around. He laid down next to Lia, and stretched his legs out, with a triumphant smirk. Dawn had decided to disappear, likely a result of whatever silly dramatics were occupying her this week, leaving he, Lia, and Alexis alone in Dawn's estate. Virgil was very cognizant of Dawn's intentions. She wanted the same thing he had at the moment... the ability to stretch out next to Lia, and love and be loved by her. Virgil's smirk widened as he imagined the petulant response an accusation of such would elicit... much like his simple presence. He enjoyed adding a touch of misery to Dawn's life, but at the same time chastised himself for allowing himself to resent a mortal. He closed his eyes. There was, after all, little stopping him from rectifying the situation. There was an endless list of ways to kill a human, and any of them would remove the only thing keeping Virgil from being content, the only distraction to the life he had planned to lead upon arriving in Las Vegas. The Cathayans were no longer a concern, and all of his time was available... except, now, there was just something else. A roll of the eyes accompanied the thought that it would probably not lie within the proper protocol to kill Dawn... not for any concern of civilized society, he simply didn't have any desire to offend Lia... 'No. You will stop thinking about that woman. She is not worthy of your concern. If need be, she will die within seventy years or so.' He forced a sigh, one of the few comforts of humanity that he indulged himself in, and allowed himself to drift off to sleep. Not having any pressing concerns had left him quite lethargic lately. - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - A fire burns lazily in a rather grandiose-looking fireplace, gilded along the edges, with not a trace of dust or ash in sight. The rest of the room is built along the same standards, high walls of polished stone blocks, typical of the dwellings of the wealthy during the middle ages. Virgil sits on a rather lavish looking couch, reading a dusty tome of some antiquity. Around the room, sunlight pours through the high windows, a sliver of it on his face, proof that this is obviously some time ago. Despite the difficult life that most of Europe is leading at this point, in this particular estate in southern Spain, there is comfort. Virgil looks up from his book as the heavy door at the opposite end of the room swings open, and a very familiar woman sets foot in the room. 'Ah, my beloved...' Virgil climbs from his seat, the words slipping from his mouth as though quite practiced at being spoken, and he bridged the distance between them quickly, and embraces her. They are still Virgil and Lia, but gone is the pale complexion, the sinister beauty... they are still human, and even Lia's porcelain skin radiates a glow of life. The smiles upon their faces are untainted by the abnormal amount of stresses placed upon them by the pressure of multiple lifetimes... it only seems to add to the pristine setting. The silence is broken suddenly. 'Papa!' A small girl of around seven years traipses happily into the room, making a beeline for Virgil, and wrapping her arms around his waist, barely tall enough to reach his hip with the top of her head. Virgil leans down and lifts the girl high in the air, and she laughs. 'I see you have run away from Maria again, hm?' She shakes her head, tossing her straight black hair back and forth dramatically. 'No Papa, I came with Mama.' Lia steps forward and nodded, taking the girl from Virgil's shoulder and setting her on the floor. 'Si, she tells the truth, Virgil. We haven't seen you all day, with your dealings in Madrid... I'm very happy to see you home again.' A bright smile crosses Virgil's face, a look he is no longer capable of. 'I share your happiness... I could not stand being separated from my beautiful wife and daughter, and still live properly.' The small girl looks up at Virgil, beaming with the happiness of seeing her father once again. 'Virgil...' The voice sounds low and disembodied, not the voice of a child. Virgil raises an eyebrow. He could not seem to remember her name... 'Virgil?' 'Why do you not call me Papa, hm?' - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - 'Virgil?' Alexis crept into the room carefully, thinking he would still be awake for some reason... after all, it was still night, and he shouldn't be asleep until dawn. Virgil's eyes snapped open, and he responded in a clipped, aggressive tone. 'What, Alexis?' She shifted uncomfortably. It wasn't so much that he was angry with Alexis, more simply annoyed... dreams that were not nightmares were not common for him, and something about this last one seemed... strange. He wished that he had been able to remember his 'daughter's' name. 'Have you seen...' Virgil held up a hand. 'If you are planning on asking me about that regrettable woman's whereabouts, no, I do not know where she is, and I do not wish to talk about her. She is supposedly of some years, and should be able to take care of herself. Wait for me in the hallway, I will be out shortly, so we don't disturb Lia.' Alexis nodded. 'Um... okay.' She stepped out of the room, leaving the door open, and Virgil quickly followed her out, not bothering to put a shirt on. Alexis looked around somewhat furtively, and Virgil stared down at her with his unblinking eyes, making her wish she could bolt from him at top speed. 'You wanted to talk to me, yes?' Alexis stumbled for a moment. 'Well, I kind of just wanted to know where Dawn was... but, um... how's Global working out for you?' Virgil looked at her incredulously. He had no time for pointless conversation... or rather, he'd prefer not to waste time with pointless conversation. In all honestly, there was little pressing him at the moment. 'I am quite invisible among the roster, and simply coasting week to week, until I find an opponent who is worthy of my talents.' Alexis continued to fidget nervously as he looked at her curiously. Something about Alexis had not been near so banal and irritating as when he had first captured her... in fact, he had actually grown to become somewhat protective of her, and even find her tendency toward air-headed moments amusing. Considering how ill-disposed he had immediately found himself toward Dawn, he assumed Alexis would be the same. However, this was quite the mystery. Alexis blinked. 'Would you be able to take me out to get some food? I was going to ask Dawn, but I forgot she wasn't back yet...' Virgil shrugged... he'd much rather be contemplating the strange familiarity of that dream... 'Food is a weakness, Alexis.' She narrowed her eyes. 'For you, yeah, but I kind of need it. You said you wouldn't kill me, I thought that meant not letting me starve.' Virgil nodded, stepping past her and looking into the bedroom briefly, checking on Lia. She would likely still be asleep when they returned. 'We will go, enough with your complaints. It's enough of a blessing to have the owner of this house gone, I will not have you spoiling my peace by acting like her.' Virgil stepped past Alexis, remaining somewhat cold and aloof for the moment. There was still a slight twinge of distrust for her, considering she had been part of a concerted effort to kill Lia, and him as well by extension. However, somehow, he couldn't quite shake the feeling that something separated her from other mortals, something made her feel different... which made no sense whatsoever. As he stepped out of the house, Alexis in tow behind him, he cursed his mind for being arbitrary and non-transparent with his consciousness.
