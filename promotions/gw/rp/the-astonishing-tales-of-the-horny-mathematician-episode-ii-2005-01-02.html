The Astonishing Tales of the Horny Mathematician, Episode II: The Origin of the Circus of DOOM~!

2 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/2/05 Two Days later... [The scene opens on a beautiful Las Vegas afternoon. James and his buddy the Horny Mathematician are walking down Las Vegas Boulevard after a radio session to promote the James von Doom DVD (which will be released as soon as it is HTML'd). They are making idle chit chat as passers by gawk at the hometown celebrity who left out on top.] JV: Can I ask you something? [The Mathematician nods his head.] Horny Mathematician: Shoot. JV: So what do you think I should do now? [The Mathematician laughs.] Horny Mathematician: Have sex with lots of women and create the next generation of von Doom spawns to invade the PTC in twenty years. [James laughs as they continue walking down the street.] JV: (laughing) We'll be like the next Spectres! [They both start laughing until James feels a tapping on his shoulder. He turns around.] JV: Yes? [He looks at the person who is tapping him on the shoulder. It's an unfamiliar face. It's the face of a young man in his early twenties who's wearing a James von Doom 'Assimilate into the Doom' T-shirt.] JV: Can I help you? [The man nods his head.] Man: You ruined it... [James raises an eyebrow.] JV: Ruined what? Man: EVERYTHING! [James still is confused.] JV: But how? [The man is now trembling with anger.] Man: YOU FUCKING RUINED WRESTLING FOREVER YOU SELF-RIGHTEOUS TWAT! JV: Huh? [The man pulls out a gun and shoots James five times in cold blood! The Mathematician looks at the blood on his hands and faints. Some concerned citizens run over to check on James. Two more who were just passing by stop their car and jump out, lunging at his crazed assailant and tackling him down! Twenty minutes later, James von Doom was pronounced dead at University Medical Center from gunshot wounds to various parts of his body, mainly his torso. Cold... Black... Darkness... Death... This is what it feels like to be James von Doom... FOREVER.] * * * Prelude to the Mathematician... Two days after the death of James von Doom the character, James von Doom the Handler came on national television and the internet and vowed to destroy all seriousness in wrestling. This sparked a war between the Serious Systems and the Comedy Systems that has spurned off into a galactic conflict of epic proportions! Both sides are flaming each other with no end in sight for this conflict. The zombie armies from the 'Night of the Zombies' storyline are everywhere and no one is safe. But, thus far, our hero the Horny Mathematician has stayed out of their grasp...for now. * * * NOW... 'Death is not forever...' [It is a dark and stormy night outside of a top secret science lab somewhere out there in the world. The Horny Mathematician had James von Doom's body transferred to his lab. There are weird sort of machines all around. Electrodes are sticking out of the side of something hidden under a cloth. James von Doom's former assistant the hunchbacked Igor is moving around and setting up the last part of the experiment as the Mathematician opens up the roof. The giant lightning rod he constructed mechanically moves out and into the outside as the storm gets even worse. Outside, there are loud bangs on his door. The Mathematician looks over with fright. He knows that the zombies that have been invading the world for most of the past few months have finally found him.] Mathematician: They're here...they've come for me... [The Mathematician looks over at Igor.] Mathematician: We must move quickly if we are to survive... [The Mathematician looks up at the lightning rod.] Mathematician: MOVE IT UP HIGHER! WE MUST HAVE THE POWER OF THE MIGHTY ROD! [Igor nods his head.] Igor: Yes, Master. [Igor does accordingly as the Mathematician scrambles over to his control board and moves a switch forward. Suddenly lightning strikes the rod and sends an electric shock through whatever is under the cloth. The Mathematician looks at whatever it is and sees something move.] Mathematician: IT'S ALIVE! IT'S ALIVE! BWAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA! [The thing begins to move as the machine is shut off and the rod is brought back into the house. Just then, the body under the cloth sits up and now the horror is shown. It's James von Doom. He's been brought back to life by the Horny Mathematician! James looks at his chest and touches it as the Mathematician smiles.] JV: What...what happened? Mathematician: You were shot six times at close range by a demented fan. But I was there...I brought you back. [James looks at his hands.] JV: But what about the others? [Just then, the zombies break down the door and stagger into the Mathematician's secret lab. The wander over to the Mathematician, who shields himself with Igor, and James. They look at James and do nothing. The Mathematician laughs and steps forward.] Mathematician: The origin of all life is the tip of my penis. I have just proven that today. Now zombies, you have a leader. [James looks over at him.] JV: What? [The Mathematician smiles.] Mathematician: Your other sidekicks are 'weak.' They did nothing to protect you when you needed it. They did NOTHING. All they did was sit on their asses on the Island of Misfit Wrestlers and do nothing but watch you die whereas I helped create...life. [A nasty scowl comes across James' face.] JV: Those mother f***ers... [The Mathematician reaches into his lab coat.] Mathematician: I have a present for you. [The Mathematician pulls out a real life lightsaber and hands it to James. James looks at it and smiles.] Mathematician: The only one in existence in the world. An actual lightsaber. (scowls) Your friends Adam and Matt stole my two prototypes the last time they were here when you three were screwing around in my lab. [James looks over at the Mathematician.] JV: What do you want me to do?' [The Mathematician smiles like a madman.] Mathematician: All of your sidekicks are now enemies of us. They did nothing for you when you needed them too. They all must be exterminated like the bugs they are. Even your friends Adam and Matt are now our enemies now. JV: I understand. Mathematician: I want you to go to the Island of Misfit Wrestlers and kill all of your worthless sidekicks so that I may rule the Island as King of the Jobbers! [James smiles hideously.] JV: Yes, Master. Mathematician: Take the army of the zombies there and kill them all. Make them understand that the origin of jobbers is my crucial head. [James heads toward the door with the zombies in tow.] Mathematician: Do what must be done. Do not hesitate, show no mercy. [James and the zombies exit the Mathematician's lab.] Mathematician: Now I will rule all comedy wrestlers... (badly acted cackle) * * * A few hours later... [On the Island of Misfit Wrestlers, a large army of zombies is marching up the steps of the Jobber Palace led by a cloaked figure wearing black. Some of the Misfit Wrestlers can feel a disturbance in the force, but they can't put the pieces together. By then, it's too late. The once peaceful zombies who were their army against the Serious Forces turn on them at once. The man once known as James von Doom is now in the Jobber Palace as his zombies are overrunning the handlers of all the jobbers. He enters the Jobber Council Chambers and the door closes behind him. Out from the shadows pop the Nookie Monster, 'Big' Dick N. Uranus, Count Fuckula, TAN in their carrot costumes, Quagmire the Barbarian, and the ever uncool Africools. They were hiding behind the council members' chairs. They look at James in stunned silence.] Count Fuckula: Master von Doom! You are still alive! There are too many of them! What are we going to do? [James gives him the evil eye and ignites his lightsaber. The fires from the attack on the Jobber Temple can be seen throughout the Island of Misfit Wrestlers. Most of the Jobbers were slaughtered by James von Doom himself. Very few survived. The image of Circus Tent of DOOM~! is shown flying off into outer space is shown. The image of a beastly Cave Hulk creature swimming deep into a cave is shown. The Chameleon is shown following him in the shadows. The image of the Giant Sperm floating through the clouds is shown. The last image is of Adam and Matt weeping. They know what they must do. They must face their best friend alone in a duel to the death.] Cut. End Episode II
