Sadness....something that haunts us all

29 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-29-05 Friday the 29, 2005 Somewhere in Texas to be exact?. The dry air that had rolled right on in was enough to make anyone feel uncomfortable. Breezing by the green tree’s that stood a little bit down each side of the road. The dirt on the streets, rattling by as it got kicked up by every little car and truck that whistled by my open window. The wind blew passed my ears, trying to seemingly rattle the vocals of Amy Lee’s, lead singer of Evanescence as she sang her heart out across the speakers. *(Wake me up) Wake me up inside (I can't wake up) Wake me up inside (Save me) Call my name and save me from the dark (Wake me up) Bid my blood to run (I can't wake up) Before I come undone ( Save me) Save me from the nothing I've become * Oh her voice, like a cheap heartache to my soul. It was as if it was the ease of all my problems. Turning up the radio a little louder, I came to my destination, pulling as close to the side of the road as I could. The place was rough looking. Dirt filled the front of the house, making it hard to even see the address that hangs up on the side of the house. It was getting even hotter in the day as I took off the outer part of my shirt. Keeping the white beater shirt that I had on underneath the red button up country shirt that was tied in a knot on the stomach. My hair was up a little, weird because I usually always keep it straight down on my shoulders. Walking up the long patio way that they had built on, I heard a little music coming from inside the house. The sounds of Saliva rattled around in my ears as it was clearly making an impact from my eyes. *carry the weight of the world as the past is unfurled, But I won’t stop to wonder. Going through this life on my own made me cold as stone. I’m a ship going under And I’d tell you this, but I don’t know how. I’m caving in, and I’m falling out! And I can’t resist, and I can’t rebound With the weight of the world as the world falls down! * “YA!” Stopping dead in my tracks, faster then anything or anyone I ever saw. That voice sounded familiar in my head. Almost as if it was one of those voices you forgot for a reason. Lowering my head for a bit as I continued to walk forward. As I did, the front door came open. Standing there, fantasy dressed with a pair of tan looking kaki’s with a blue and black button up shirt that had the top and next button popped open. His sleeves were rolled up a little on his arms, almost making me pop out of my clothing if you know what I mean. “Hey?” He says as he shuts the door, holding his beer in one hand. “Hey”:” Replying back with what little response I could make. “So what brings you over here again?” He asks with a soft voice, almost a light that I needed in a tunnel. You see, the story here is a long one. One that would take about two years to tell and who has that long to sit and hear that? Not me and I sure as hell don’t want to tell it. Looking at Mark, my ex and I mean ex in a long term feeling, as he stood there, waiting for my answer. “Well your mother asked for me to come over.” Trying to walk by and not get that close to him. “I just don’t know what for,” “Well she’s inside.” Responding with a short reply so he could take a quick drink of his beer. Opening the door to a world that no person, not me for sure, should have to walk into. Food on the floor, on the walls, even on the TV for some reason. A woman shouldn’t have to clean this up. And this is why me and Mark became nothing more then a dream after we split up. “Oh look who’s back.” Yelled one of Marks good friends who happen to be a bastard in so many ways towards me. “Don’t you think Mark is hurt enough right now rather then to see you again?” “Excuse me?”” My anger rolling out like a World War III happening. “I ain’t the one that hurt him cowboy. If you think I was anything it was the victim.” And I was. “Sure.” A simple reply from the man that hadn’t had a relationship ever. Forgetting about the little boy, I walked towards the other room, hoping the place gets a little better the deeper into it but it hadn’t. Clothing, food, and oddly oil on the floor, covering the once pretty tile that Mark had put down on the floor before we split. My eyes widened to the point were they almost fell out of my head. My heart almost raced like a horse in the Kentucky Derby . “What is wrong with you people?” Screaming out with my girlish power voice. I wasn’t hoping to get an answer, leaning down towards the floor, showing my ass a little and out of the cut off blue jean shorts that I made last night. As I was down there, a woman like me should always watch her back. Yes, I might be a wrestler but that doesn’t mean nothing when I’m in a very venerable position. “Oh man if I could get that butt one more time?” Mark said as he came in to the room, still carrying that beer. I popped up quickly, turning around to see his blood shot eyes a little closer. “You lost that chance when you decided to get on those drugs remember?” “What drugs” I drink and that is all. What drugs?” Downing his beer like it was a glass of tea that was just made. “You know what drugs. The ice you began smoking. The coke that you began to sniff. That is what drugs I am talking about.” Responding as I began to try and push my way back towards the other way. “Were are you going?” He asks as he wraps his arm around me, taking me in like I was still his girl. I felt hurt, almost violated in a way that I didn’t want to be. He was drunk, high maybe, but that wasn’t what scared me. I was in a house with three guys, one being a very horny man when he is drunk. I pressed him away with an elbow, trying to get free but it was to tight. “LET ME GO MARK!” “Settle down..” Mark said as he pulled me in closer. My arms and chest were starting to get played with, my shirt almost showing parts that he didn’t need to be seeing. I took my right foot and raised it up, sending it right back towards his crotch area, slamming it right into his leg, not legs. “AWE?” He screams out loudly, almost in tears right away. “Why did you do that B*itch?” “Because I don’t want something else disappointing me again.” Laughing a little as I began walking towards the front door. Mark came running for me, screaming out at me like I was some stripper or escort that didn’t give what she was supposed to. I saw his mother walking towards me from the other side of the house. Looking good like a sixty-eight year old woman doesn’t look like. Mark came to a stop as soon as he saw his mother. She nor me ever liked seeing him drunk, maybe that is why she asked me to come over. “Hi Elia?” Mrs. Martinez said to me like she still saw me on a daily basic’s. “Man is it good to see you. Hugging her like she was a mother to me. Perfume came off of her like she had been going out tonight. She smelt good, even to me. “Can we go talk somewhere?” She asks, looking at Mark who tossed his beer straight to the grassy grown as we began walking away, walking towards the car. What did she want to talk about? What could it be about other then me and Mark? But she knew me and him would never be anything else intimate wise again. He was nothing to me other then a guy on the street. Her eyes began to water a little. She pressed the feeling back down a little more, trying to pause the water works from coming. “I know you saw the house and now you saw the drinking. Do you know why he is drinking?” Her hand grasped mine. Making my hand warm up a little more. “Yes and no. I saw the house but why should I need to know why he is drinking?” Asking her but not really caring from deep inside. It might be a little cold but he hurt me like I was nothing. Her words came out, making me fill up from deep inside with the response. “He does it because of you. As weird as that sounds, he is doing it because of you.” “So?” Trying to also keep the tears from coming out. “I have nothing for him and even if I did, how could I go on knowing that everything I gave him last time is gone from him.” “Listen, I don’t have time to talk about this but try and think about talking to him. He needs you.” Her words rolled off like a missile racing at me ready to destroy. He needs me? I think not. I really think not. I gave a small nod as she walked away, walking towards the house as Mark looked out. Right at me as I turned away, physically that was but mentally I was still looking. I could say I still cared for him, not love but cared for him. Maybe he was what I needed for my life. Grabbing the handle of the car and climbing in, I got one last look at the past. His eye’s locked on mine it seemed, though it quickly faded away as I drove off, trying to figure out what to do? ** - Credit Saliva and Evanescence and Amy Lee for the two songs that were used in this.
