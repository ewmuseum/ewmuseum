The History of Max Roswell Part 1

14 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Jan 14 OOC: This is a new series i'm doing with my rp's) 19 years ago... I was just walking yesterday. Everyone was happy for me, especially my mother. She was almost crying. I was looking into her eyes with a smile. She picked me up and sat me in my high chair so that everybody could start supper. I was starting to eat real food instead of the baby food in the jars. Everyone was chomping down there food like it was nothing. My mother’s eyes never left my face. She wasn’t even eating her food. She was just staring, I could see the sparkle in the eye. After supper everyone went into the backyard for some more drinks. I was playing on a blanket layed out on the green grass. One of my cousin’s walked over to me and tried picking me up. The only problem is that my cousin was only 4 years of age. She picked me up but had trouble and dropped me on the ground. I smacked my head on the cement under the deck and started crying. My mother ran to me and picked me up off the ground and started rocking me back and forth. “It’s alright honey. You will be alright.” said my mother. Before I knew it I was laying in my crib with all the lights out in the room. I could see the mother laying on her bed a couple feet away form me. I layed down and crawled under my blanket going to bed. IN THE MORNING I woke up with my mother standing over me with a bottle in her hand. She gave it to me and I grabbed it and started drinking, while my mother picked me up and took me to the window. She told me all the things that were around us, I was fascinated. I started struggling free so I could walk around our little apartment. I climbed up onto the bed and started playing with my stuffed animals that were laying on the very side of the bed. I would always used to bash my animals together and I still did that day. My mom was so proud of me. She walked over to the side of the bed and kissed me on the cheek and said. “I’ll love you forever. I will never let you go.” Since I was just a little boy I just smiled and started laughing like a baby would laugh. Then something happened, I saw some kind of image in my head. It was like something that happened a long time ago, and it felt like I was there. It was a picture of a car. But the car wasn’t brand new and all that. It was all smashed up with parts scattered all over the grey street of Connecticut. I suddenly just saw my mom standing in front of me saying. “What’s the matter” Is there a problem, honey.” I just sat there and stared into her eyes. I put my arms up into the air and my mother knew exactly what I wanted. She picked me up and walked to the kitchen. She opened the fridge and pulled out a bottle. She gave it to me and I grabbed it and started drinking the milk. I then dropped the bottle and started wiggling out of the mothers hands. She put me down back onto the floor and let me run around some more before dressing me. She took me and shut all the lights off and walked out the door with her car keys in her hand. “Were going to see your auntie and uncle.” she said to me in a calm voice. She walked and opened the back door of the car. She put me into the baby seat I had and buckled me in really tight it was hard to breathe. She closed the door and got into the front seat and put her seatbelt on and started the car. She then looked to the back and backed out of the driveway. She started down the street and by the time you knew it she was at a big blue house with red trimming. “Were here sweetie pie.” she said to me. She took her seatbelt off and got out of the car. She opened my door and un buckled me and picked me up. I had my jacket on with a funny looking joker hat my mom had bought for me a couple days ago. She went up to the white door and rang the doorbell. Within 5 seconds the door opened and my auntie stood there with a smile on her face. She came up to me and took me out of the mom’s hands. She started rocking me gently and kissed me on the cheek. She took me inside and put me at the top of a few stairs. I had never seen her house before. I got up and walked into the living room and saw my uncle sitting on the couch watching wrestling. He looked at me and smiled. “Hey bud, how you doing?” my uncle said. I just looked at him, he knew I couldn’t talk yet and if I tried to it would just be the words, Mom and Uh-Oh. He picked me up and sat down on a rocking chair. I was in his arms staring at the television. I started laughing when a man on the television fell off the turnbuckle and onto the outside of the ring. He started laughing with at the sight. Suddenly, my mother and auntie walked up the stairs. “Turn that stuff off Wayne.” my auntie said to my uncle. He took the remote off the coffee table that was right by the rocking chair. He pushed a button on it and the television went to a black screen. My uncle put me down from his arms and let me walk around the huge living room. My mother sat down on the blue fuzzy couch and started talking to my uncle. Suddenly my auntie’s voice interrupted the conversation. “What would you like to drink Linda?” my auntie said to m mother. “I’ll just have some Iced Tea, thank you.” my mother said back. I ran up to my mother and hugged her leg. She looked down at me and started smiling. She picked me up and put me on her right lap. I was looking around the room with my hands in my mouth. They were all full of slobber and water. My mother put me back down. “Here.” said my auntie as she handed a cup of Iced Tea to my mother. “To Max. The best baby in the world.” my mother said. My mother, auntie, and uncle raised there glasses and banged them together lightly. They started drinking out of the cup as I just walked around the room.
