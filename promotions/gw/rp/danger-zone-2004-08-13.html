Danger Zone

13 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/13/04 'Jefe, I see him he's on the toilet...' A solid black stretch limo slowly pulls up beside a modern day Dallas ranchero mansion. The door slowly opens up and out steps J-Gonz. The bright sun shines off his solid black sunglasses. As the wind blows back his black overcoat, it reveals two solid silver pistols on holsters. He looks up at the ranchero mansion, behind him two more men step out, two men that are new to J-Gonz. 'It's gonna be a game of cat and mouse esse, we got a rat in the organization and I'm gonna start cleaning it up from the top.' The two men behind J-Gonz follow his lead, all threen men dressed in solid black suits continue up the drive way. Chocolate and Paco, the two normal henchmen join the group. 'His position is in the back of the house jefe, and he's on the toilet.' 'Is that all you saw Paco?' 'Si jefe.' 'You Chocolate?' 'Theirs three men in the living room boss, each one has a rocket on him.' 'And the back?' 'No boss, I only saw them three men.' 'Horale!' Paco and Chocolate break off from the group and sprint around the side of the ranchero mansion. J-Gonz points at the other two men, they run up and pull out their MK-49s and blast the front glass window open. Shots are fired back and one of the men is taken down. J-Gonz slams the door open and pulls out his two pistols. 'We gonna fight like men!' Using the pistol he blast a hole through one of the men firing out the window, a bullet barely misses the former Television Champion, and he responds by taking out another of the men. 'Jefe it's clear!' From the stairs Paco and Chocolate come down holding a chunk man, that's only in his boxers. The man is yelling for help, but all his men are down in their own blood. Paco slams the man against the wall, busting his head open. Chocolate pulls a chair up and ties the man down. 'Johnny! You remember me homes?' 'Gonzalez! What do you want! What is this all about?' 'Maybe you can tell me more about it esse!' 'Tell you more about what Gonzalez? I've been loyal to you since the first day you took over!' An iratated looking J-Gonz smacks Jonny. 'We almost got busted yesterday Johnny! We almost got killed esse!' 'I didn't know anything about it, I swear! Please don't kill me!' 'Johnny I hate liars homes! I hate being screwed over! I told you from the first day that I took over, that if anyone, and I meant anyone ever screwed me over!' 'But Gonzalez, I don't know...' Before Johnny is able to mutter another word, J-Gonz pulls out his pistol and puts a hole right through his head! 'Someons lying boys! And I'm gonna find out what's going on!' 'Jefe, what if he was telling the truth?' 'Atleast he's in a better place homes! Boys, head back to the palace, me and Paco have another visit to make.' THe other three men load into the limosine, Paco and J-Gonz walk over to an older looking Ford Explorer. They hop into the vehicle and speed off. --- 'In another shoot-out, police are investigating the scene inside a Dallas ranchero mansion. Police believe that this shoot-out may be linked to a drive by shooting earlier today in south cental Dallas. Two of the men were connected, in what police are calling the biggest crime network in North America.'
