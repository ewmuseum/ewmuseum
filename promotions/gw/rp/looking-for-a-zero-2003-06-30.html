Looking for a Zero...

30 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/30/03 **It has been a few hours since Paulie and I completed our task and I proved my value to the Salamone family. And even after being congratulated and thanked by Don Salamone. But the aftermath of the event still haunts me. Lighting things on fire or driving cars into the ocean is no big deal to me, but shooting someone is. I have never shot anyone before but something tells me that won’t be the only time either.** (As the scene fades in we see Sean Price’s Hummer pulling up John Zero’s long and winding driveway towards his estate. The Hummer stops infront of Zero’s steps leading to his front door. Sean gets out of the driver side door and then makes his way upto the steps. He knocks on the door a few times, yelling out for John Zero each time. A few moments pass with no answer. Sean begins to knock again but notices that the door is slightly ajar so he slowly opens the door as he continues to knock and call for Zero. When the door finally opens Sean sees a dead body full of multiple bullet holes laying on the floor in a pool of blood. Sean continues to make his way through the house and past the dead body. At the end of the hallway Sean turns right into the family room where he see’s John Zero’s secretary ontop of fellow TUE member Deacon Holmes. Deacon looks over at Sean and quickly turns, throwing the woman off of him and onto the floor.) “Where’s Zero?” “Not even a hello Sean” Zero isn’t here.” “Hello Deacon, but I figured you were hard at work so id keep it short and simple so you could get back to what you were doing.” “That’s nice of you but yeah John’s not here. I’m not really sure where he is. Let me see if his secretary knows.” (Deacon gets up and helps Zero’s secretary up off the floor. He brushes off her backside and then sits down with her on the couch.) “Sean this is John’s secretary’John’s secretary this is Sean.” “How do you do Mr.Price?” “Well at the moment not as good as you I would imagine. But we have Deacon to thank don’t we?” “Whatever do you mean Mr.Price?” “Deacon can I see you over here for a minute?” “Yeah sure thing. Sit tight baby, ill be back in a minute.” (Sean and Deacon take a walk to the living room of Zero’s estate. Deacon takes a seat in one of the chairs while Sean starts pacing back and forth in the room.) “Sean what is up with you man” You look all frazzled? Something wrong?” “Is something wrong” What isn’t wrong is the question. I just finished my first job for Don Salamone?” “How did that go?” “Well aside from having to shoot someone in the arm, lighting perfectly good Heroine on fire and driving a truck full of more perfectly good Heroine into the ocean I’d say it went pretty fucking well.” “You had to shot someone” Way to go, welcome to the dark side my friend.” “Sometimes that’s just how I see it, the dark side. But sometimes I don’t see it that way at all. Sometimes I see it as the right side. I see that there is no wrong in what I do.” “Why should you” You are only doing what you are asked to do.” “That’s right. The way I see it, there is no such thing as evil as long as the person doesn’t believe that they have done anything wrong. If they feel no remorse for their actions than what makes them evil?” “Nothing.” “You know Dec this mobster thing isn’t half bad. It has always been something that I have wanted to do my entire life. And now that I have had my first taste of the mob life’I’m loving every minute of it.” “As you should. So what do you need John for?” ? I need him to help me with something, actually I need both of you. But I figured you would be here so Id come here first and low and behold you are here’but the person who owns the house isn’t.” “Yeah I woke up this morning and he was gone. That’s John for ya though.” “Yeah he is pretty stealth like that. But anyway you need to come with me so we can get this project done.” “What exactly do we have to do?” “I’ll tell you in the car.” (The scene fades out as Sean and Deacon head towards Sean’s hummer.)
