Not washed up

23 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/23/04 Fists pound the already battered heavy bag. Sewat runs down his face and body, stings his eyes. His muscles ache. But Brick is determined to keep pushing forward. He's been working out for hours, going through sparring partners with the same speed a person with a runny nose goes through Kleenexes. Dark Rituals is coming up. Brick is in the first match of the evening, the Graveyard Mayhem match. The offical press release says it best: All men are placed in a graveyard setting. The only way to win is to push your opponents into one of the four large mass-grave style pits, blocked off behind large cement barracades. Itagaki Freeze, Christian Zenith, Fushichou No Nazo, Jackson Namenlos-Duisternis, Jesse Jamester and Dagoth Kinslayer are all Brick's opponents. Itagaki Freeze, the Puppet Of Destruction, servant to Lord Akuma. One of the truly sinister figures in Global Wrestling. Brick knew of Freeze's sadistic streak from his earlier run with the company. Christian Zenith, the Midnight Messiah, head of an underground cult and the sole heir to the Zenith Weapons Corporation fortune. Fushichou No Nazo, master of Flight of the Phoenix. He was a newcomer to Global Wrestling, someone Brick knew very little about. Jackson Namenlos-Duisternis, the Darkness. The newest monster to walk through Global Wrestling's double doors. Brick had just had a match with him recently. Of all the participants in the match, Brick will most likely have to fight Jackson the hardest. Jesse Jamester and Dagoth Kinslayer. Originally, they were to be in Grimm's Wrath, which was to be the starting match of the night. 3 men, 2 inside the ring at any time. All three are able to tag in and out of the match to anyone else. The match was to been between Jamester, Kinslayer and a mystery third opponent. But deals with said mystery opponent fell through and as a result, the Grimm's Wrath match was cancelled and the remaining participants of that match were placed in the Graveyard Mayhem match. This leaves Brick with two new variables. Brick decides to move to the speed bag. Each blow to the bag comes faster than the one before it. Brick wants this victory at Dark Rituals. In fact, Brick needs this victory. As of late, Brick has been on something of a downward spiral. He's suffered loss after loss after loss in the ring for months. Even though many predict he'll pick up the victory, the comments have been passed around. They're the same cracks he's had to endure for about a year. He heard them from Deacon Holmes. He heard them from Tony Stecchino. He hears them every week from Mark Bishop, among others. Washed-up. Has-been. Broken-down old bum. Brick has heard them all. These insults are what drive him to push himself, what drive him to such excess. 'I'm not washed-up,' Brick thinks to himself. 'I'm not a has-been. I'm not a bum. I'M NOT!!!!' The last punch breaks the chain holding the bag up and the bag goes flying. Brick glares in the direction the bag flew, breathing heavily. Many of the other patrons in the gym are staring at him. Almost right away, Brick notices. 'FUCK Y'ALL LOOKIN' AT???!!!' Brick roars, causing the onlookers to jump back. At Dark Rituals, Brick intends to win. At Dark Rituals, Brick intends to throw each and every one of his opponents into the pits, one by one or all at once, if he has to. And at Dark Rituals, Brick intends to remind the world just who the hell he is. Two things Brick does not intend to at Dark Rituals: Lose and quit.
