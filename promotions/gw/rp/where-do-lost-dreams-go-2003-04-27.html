Where Do Lost Dreams Go?

27 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/27/03 Where Do Lost Dreams Go? They Go Into The Back Of Your Mind And They Lie Dormant For They Are Only Alive In The Hearts Of The Youth They Try To Escape Reality The Reality Of Knowing That Those Dreams Will Never Become A Goal But They Will Remain Dreams For They Die In Your Desire Because The World Can Corrupt Your Dream And Society Can Corrupt Our Opinions Shatter My Dream Shatter My Life For A New Tomorrow Has Begun And For My Dreams For They Are Over It Is Over Oh Lord, It Is Over “Dad?” He searches around, glancing left and right. “Dad?” A lost sheep searching for his Shepard. “Where are you?” Sweat pouring down his face. He franticly searches all around. “DAD?” With every step, he gets more nervous. Drips of sweat fall from his face and his eyes get wide. “Fath?” A 6’5 man, weighing approximately 230 pounds jumps in front of the young child. He hoists him up in his arms and the child hugs the man with all his might. He squeezes the child in his arms, calming him down. The child takes a deep breath and relaxes. “It’s alright Sage. Calm down buddy, I was just checking the traps to see if we got anything.” The child takes another deep breath and hugs the man, squeezing the life out of him. “What do you want, buddy?” He sets down the child. The boy wipes off tears and sweat from his face. The man awaits for the child’s response. “Dad, I did a spinning roundhouse kick.” “Really” Show me how you do it.” The young Sage Sabbot stands back. He concentrates, his tongue sticking out. Every single move running through his head. He takes a deep breath and jumps up with all his power and sends his left foot flying in mid-air into the sky. He lands with his right foot on the ground, but his left foot does not make the full rotation, and he comes crashing towards the ground, landing on his back. He slowly stands back up? “No really Dad, I did it before’I swear it.” The man chuckles a little bit? “I believe you. You better stop practicing those jumping round kicks and start plowing the fields. Practicing to kick and punch won’t help you at all when you are my age. But eating your vegetables and manual labor will.” “But what about if I don’t want to grow up to be a farmer or to work on a field. What about if I want to be a martial artist or a Thai boxer?” The man chuckles and puts his hand on the child’s shoulder? “Keep dreaming Sage. You will never be a martial artist in this world.” The young Sage crosses his arms across his chest and frowns and stomps off towards the field. His father stands back and watches him storm off and smirks. “That boy sure is stubborn, and stubbornness will never get him anywhere in life.” --------------------------------------------------------------- All the thoughts running through his head. But this one, that dream that he wanted to accomplish since he was a child’it was held back by society. His father wanted him to be a normal man. An average human being who worked on a farm, plowed the fields, and harvested crops. But he wanted something more. He wanted to be what the world told him that he would never be. That pushed him further then anything ever could. It fueled his veins with adrenaline and it extended his boundaries when he heard the words??You’ll never make it?. But when he was put up to the task’he accomplished this goal. He did not become a world famous martial artist, but he is a black belt in Jujitsu and Thai Boxing. For he learned the hard way, overcoming every obstacle that stood in front of him. His father said he was stubborn and that he wouldn’t get anywhere in life. But now, now he looks back at what his father said and he realizes where his father was at in his life. His father was a peasant. His father was a farmer and an average human being. But he, he is something more my friends. He is the GW Cruiserweight Champion. He is The Soul Survivor of the Global Wrestling. And he is Sage Sabbot. “I have pushed myself farther and farther in life. People back home said that I would never be anything. But now, now I am more then they are combined. My dreams did not lie dormant in my mind. For they are still active inside my heart and in my desire. And nobody, and I mean nobody can take that away from me.” He pauses’he sets his head down. He is concentrating very hard. “Since the first day I stepped into a Global Wrestling ring, I stepped up against Luke and Alex Kitagami, the Blue Dragons. Along with my partner, Shynobi Kai, we felt our first defeat. And people around the world said that we would never be anything. That a 6’3 and 215 pound man will not survive in the land of the giants. That we would no survive in GW. But weeks later, Shynobi got fired and I stepped into a match against 5 other cruiserweights and I walked out with my first victory. By myself, I survived. A couple weeks later, I stepped into a match against Salaok. A man who is a legend in the GW and a Harvester. He paved the way for my future. But not that night. I defeated Salaok. And I also defeated Quartz. A man who deserves just as much credit as Salaok does. A man who accomplished so much more in his career then I ever dreamed. And I defeated Jay Vyper in a steel cage match. When I beat these 3 men, many people opened their eyes, but they still said that it was luck and that I still will not survive in the GW. I captured the Platinum Title that night. And I looked at it in my hands and I realized that the Platinum Title just won’t be enough. That I am going to strive for something greater. To better myself and to make every single person who doubted me choke on their own words. But still, I heard that I wasn’t as good as Salaok and Quartz, even though I beat them. I heard that the GW is made of legends. So I closely scouted al of those so-called Legends. And I seen guys like Brick, Jay Robson, Jesse Briggs, Ely Braxton, and Silent Wind. And I set my attention and my focus upon these men. And I defeated Brick. I defeated Jay Robson for the Cruiserweight Title. And I defeated many more. I laid the GW Heavyweight Champion on his back lying in a pool of his own blood. But still, everybody says that I won’t be as great as all of these legends. When I was a child, I wanted to be something that nobody else wanted me to be. But I did it, simply because I wanted it. Many people say that I will never be a legend in GW. But my goal is to become a legend’because I want it. At Wrestlecade III, I will step into the ring against 3 other men that I have not had the chance to face for the GW International Title. I will step into the ring against the International Champion, Derek Frost, Christopher Camero, and the Tragic Hero. These 3 legends will feel the same pain and the same wrath that Brick, Jay Robson, Salaok, and Quartz once felt. Now, I aligned myself with The Nevermore’and they have taught me many things. They have taught me patience. And they have taught me skill. And at Wrestlecade, I will showcase my talents in the greatest battle of my career.” He smirks? “Growing up, my father wanted me to be a farmer and a regular Chinese man. But instead, I pushed myself to be better then the average man. And I wanted to be the best. I wanted people around the world to open their eyes when they hear my name. For when the word, great, is spoken, my name will clique inside their heads and they will remember all the matches that I’ve won. And they will remember all the arenas I have sold out because of my talent.” He lifts his head towards the sky and closes his eyes? “This match, this match will be the pinnacle of my career. And it will be an example. I will prove to the world once and for all that I can survive here in the Global Wrestling. This is my opportunity to step out in front of shadows of guys like Salaok, Quartz, Johnathon Davis, and the Tragic Hero. For I will be the leader and not a follower. And the world, oh, the world will realize it at Wrestlecade’even my father. He will look down on my from the heavens and he will witness me winning the International Title. And he will not laugh or chuckle. He will remember how hard I wanted to be something and he will regret not patting me on the back for my accomplishments. And then, then he will be proud.” He will be proud’for the first time. The SCREEN FADES TO BLACK
