Doesn't he ever calm down?

17 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/17/05 The date: October 10, 2005 The place: Global Arena Brick was in the opening match of that evening's edition of Evolution: a triple threat match for a shot at the Platinum Title, which was to be merged in the Platinum Title Convergence match later in the evening. His opponents were Blaise McClancy and Virgil Ambrosi. The match started off with all three men getting a feel for each other' style. The similar martial arts backgrounds of both Ambrosi and McClancy showed, and it was something that threw Brick off with his more wrestling oriented background. Brick was able to hold his own though, using the power of his larger size, being a full 40 lbs bigger then Ambrosi, who is bigger then McClancy in his own right. Brick's power and resilience forced the other two to concentrate on him for a bit, going into a double team that wouldn't normally be expected. However, every time the duo of McClancy and Ambrosi got the big man down, neither would give the other a chance to get the pin and they'd instantly start fighting with each other again. It all came to a head though, after a series of powerful throws on the middle man in the match, Ambrosi, Brick was setup to take the Ambrosi down for a German Suplex. As he lifted him up, Ambrosi managed to hit the referee with his foot. While Brick nailed the suplex, the Referee was not there to make the count. Ambrosi retaliated by a low blow back kick that stopped the pinning bridge from happening for too long. As Both men came back up, McClancy moved into the play, and appeared to be trying to get a repeat of last week's shot on Ambrosi. Ambrosi pulled Brick into the mist that came from Blaise's mouth, blinding him with it. Ambrosi then whipped McClancy to the ropes, and followed him in, sending him over the top rope, to the outside with a lariat. Once McClancy was taken care of, Ambrosi used the opportunity with Brick blinded to hit the Cocytus Drop. The referee recovered at just that time, and McClancy missed the save on the pin, allowing Virgil Ambrosi to claim his shot at whomever is the Platinum Champion after tonight's event. ---- Some time had passed since the match ended. EMT's were trying to clear Blaise McClancy's mist from Brick's eyes. 'He's a dead motherfucker, next time I see him!' 'Ambrosi or McClancy?' asked one of the EMT's. 'Either one! Right now, I don't really care!' 'Brick?' called a feminine voice. It was Brandi Sights, GW on-site manager and spokeswoman for the Global Committee. 'I figured you'd be raging again, so I decided to come and help calm you down.' 'I don't want you going after Blaise McClancy for no good reason.' 'No good reason, my black ass!!!' Brick roared. 'He spit in my face, Brandi!!!' 'Brick, it was an accident,' Brandi sighed, rolling her eyes. 'McClancy was aiming for Ambrosi. You, better than anyone, should know that.' 'He still spit in my face and the shit still stings!!!An' what's to say he wouldn't have tried that shit on me anyway?!' 'I swear, you can be such a big baby.' 'You try havin' this shit in your eyes an' see how you like it,' Brick replied, his eyes blinking as his vision slowly returns. 'I swear, Ambrosi's a dead man.' Brandi rolled her eyes and walked towards Brick. 'Just two weeks ago, you were looking to rip into Mike Biddle, Back Alley Brawler and all of the Hunting Grounds. Now you're looking to tear Virgil Ambrosi a new asshole. Is there anybody you're not looking to beat the hell out of?' 'Well, I already beat the hell out of Biddle last week and Back Alley Bitch is no longer a factor, but thanks for reminding me about Hunting Grounds. In fact, now that I think about it, wasn't Ambrosi hanging with Shadow a few times? See, I knew I didn't like that motherfucker!!!' 'Brick, calm down, for God's sake!' 'Calm down, hell! He screwed me out of a shot at the Platinum Title! I need to get my hands on some 'Brick, you've barely been back a month.' 'So has Train and he's got a World Title shot!' 'Well, look on the bright side.' 'What bright side?' 'Whoever wins the Platinum Title Convergence match faces Ambrosi and chances are they'll beat the hell out of him for you.' A small smile began to form on Brick's face. 'That does sound a bit okay. Well, I guess I oughtta go see if Train's ready for his match.' As Brick walked away, Brandi breathed a sigh of relief. 'Bubba, how did you ever do it?'
