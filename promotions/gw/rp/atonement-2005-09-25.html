Atonement

25 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09.25.05 Michael stared in disbelief. Part of him continued to refuse that any of this was real. For all he knew, he was still lying in a hospital bed, recovering from the nightmare that was Whiplash. Maybe he never won the Television Title at all. Maybe he was dead, even. 'Hmph, I can't be that lucky.' Nope; if Michael was lucky, he wouldn't have been put in such a spot as he was right then and there. This was not something anyone deserved to go through. Ever. 'I...' he started, trying to mount some kind of way to express his confusion. It was no use. 'You... what?' his wife cut him off angrily. 'Have to go again? Another wrestling match? How would that surprise me for one second?' Quickly, Michael's expression changed to one of frustration. Was that what everything was about? Before he could open his mouth to shoot back, he was interrupted once more. 'You understand now, Michael?' Liang asked. 'I merely did this as a favor of your wife... I figured you showed enough caring for your family that I should tell you that. It's too bad you don't deserve such people as your family.' Michael's fists clenched as he could only stand and take the verbal beatdown he was getting at the hands of Liang and the woman he thought loved him more than anything. As hard as it was, Michael remained stone-faced in expression, not wishing to drag himself down to tears for their viewing pleasure. 'I think I'd like to talk to Michael alone,' Jaden whispered, speaking to Liang with a pleading gesture. T'sao nodded, and made his way to the exit of the bedroom- but not before taking Gabriella by the hand. 'Come, little one,' Liang smiled, 'let's go.' Needless to say, Michael wasn't fine with that. ok her He stepped in front of Kongde's way, and lashed out; something he really needed to do. 'Get your fucking hand off of my daughter before I break it.' Almost breaking out into laughter from the threat, Liang raised his hands defensively, and brushed past Michael with ease. 'No worries. I'll just be outside.' Stepping out and leaving the young girl with his parents, Liang smiled as he disappeared from Michael's sight. Mercer glared sadly at his wife, and slowly sliding one of the wedding rings from his hand as he did so. 'I guess you don't need this anymore, do you?' Michael queried, in doing so allowing a tear to swell up in his left eye. Jaden was unimpressed, not letting Michael's emotions sway hers. 'No. I don't.' Michael looked into his wife's green eyes, and tried as hard as he could not to cry. Michael had been through everything; being a New Yorker, he had to be put through everything. The only thing he never experienced, however, in all of his years as a wrestler, through all of the broken bones and all of the stitches... he never once felt his heart shatter in the way it just did. 'Oh..' Sighing, Jaden squatted down level with her daughter. 'Honey, do you mind leaving mommy and daddy to ourselves for a second?' Vigorously, the girl shook her head. 'Nuh uh.' 'Atta girl.' Jaden kissed the child on the forehead, and tapped her over towards the door. 'We'll only be a second.' Michael's eyes turned to stone once more as he looked over Jaden suspiciously, to which Jaden groaned. 'She doesn't like T'sao either,' Jaden grumbled, 'you have nothing to worry about on that end.' With Gabi out of the room, Michael swiftly stepped forward, cutting the distance between the two young adults. 'Why?' Michael boomed; loud enough for Gabriella- and Mindy, Markus, and T'sao for that matter- to hear. 'I'm a wrestler, so you turn around and put me through the ringer for it?' Michael's voice cracked; all of his frustration, his anger, his sorrow, his regret, it all poured out for Jaden to hear right then and there, in the sound of Michael Mercer's broken voice. 'What the hell is that, huh? Why do that to me for such an asinine reason? I love you, and I love what I do.. why can't that be okay in your eyes, Jaden?! I made it perfectly clear I wouldn't stray from you or Gabi.. just.. why now, Jaden? Why do this to me?' As emotional as Michael was at that point in time, Jaden was just as stoic. She obviously felt no remorse for what she did, and in the back of Michael's mind, he saw that reaction coming from her. Still, it did not mean that he wanted Jaden to be so cold. Was that something he truly deserved? He didn't think so. 'It's harmless to you, Michael,' Jaden began slowly, turning her head away from her love, 'but did you ever stop to think how it effected our daughter? Gabi needs you.. and every night when you're not around, do you know what she asks me? She asks me, 'Is daddy going to be home in the morning?' And I always told her that 'Michael would always be home in the morning, never worry about your daddy.' And no matter how much I tried to comfort our daughter, Michael.. our daughter cried herself to sleep every single night. No matter how many times I tucked her in, no matter how many bedtime stories I read to her, she never could sleep without you around until she ran out of tears.' Word by word, Michael himself was in tears. It was no use; the thought of his daughter, of all people, being hurt.. it killed him inside. 'You made our daughter miserable, Michael,' Jaden scorned, turning back to look Michael in his deep, brown eyes. 'You never needed to worry about me. It was Gabriella who needed you all along... and you weren't there. How could you possibly expect me to forgive you for that?' Slowly but surely, the grip on the ring Michael held in his hand- Jaden's ring- had loosened, until it fell to the floor. 'I..' Michael fought his urge to beat himself up. Who was he kidding? It was his fault that Gabriella was sad if that was the case. And yet, he could bring himself to drag himself down because of it. 'I.. I never would hurt her on purpose,' Michael shot back finally, 'I love my daughter more than anything, and if I didn't, I wouldn't have been crying my fucking ass off all this time over it, would I have?' 'You continued to wrestle anyway, Michael,' Jaden shrugged, 'what does that tell me about you? Yeah, look at that, you even have yourself another title, that's great, Michael.' Swiftly, Jaden picked up her wedding ring, and threw it harshly over her shoulder, causing it to fly into the shadows surrounding the room. Soon after, Jaden then lifted up Michael's Television Title and shoved it into his chest. 'There. Take your title and go. Be happy. I'd rather live my life with a killer than you... at least he's fucking here to be a father.' Michael blinked. Did she just insinuate what he thought she just did? With tear-stained cheeks, Michael looked at his belt, and over to Jaden. 'Okay.' He backed up, and wrapped his right hand around the doorknob to the bedroom, twisting as he continued to speak, making sure to get the last word in; if this was the last time they spoke, he was going to make sure he got that last word. 'I'm taking Gabriella with me, Jaden. It's obvious by how you speak of me, she wants to be with me more than you.' Michael pulled his body halfway out the door, and angrily lashed out the last of his frustration before he left. 'Besides, at least with me... she'll only have to know one father.' The bedroom door slammed shut. -------- 'Where are we going, daddy?' Gabriella asked, as she lay cradled in Michael's arms as he walked outside. Behind him walked his cousin, and her boyfriend; Mindy held her bag and title over each shoulder, whereas Markus was holding Michael's own belt for him. Both of them seemed just as clueless to Michael's plans as Gabriella was. 'Yeah, Mikey,' Mindy continued, 'where are we going exactly?' Michael looked down at his feet, the question bothering him as well somewhat. However, he did have a plan. A weak smile crossed his face, as he turned around and faced Mindy and Markus; his family. 'New York...' he mumbled '...we're going home.' Reassuringly, Michael leaned down and kissed his daughter on the forehead. They would start again, and this time, there was no room for failure.
