Having a party

6 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/06/03 Brick walks up to the door and rings the bell. He can hear faint sounds of music and voices from behind the door. Thirty seconds later, the door is answered by Aaron Foster. “Hey man,” says Aaron, as he embraces his friend. “About time you got here. It’d be rude not to show up for a party in your honor.” “Hey, couldn’t be helped,” Brick replies, as he comes in and the two walk through the foyer. “I got held up.” “I hear you, man, but as long as you’re here, let me introduce. Hey, look who’s fashionably late!” The room is filled with many of Brick’s friends and family, including Aaron’s wife Felicia, Brick’s sister Monica, her husband Kyle, DMAK, and Brick’s mother Coretta. “Bad Boy/Having a Party” by Luther Vandross is playing on the stereo. “Hey, big brother,” says Monica, as she hugs Brick. “Hey, Jamal, good job in that match at Wrestlecade” says Kyle, shaking Brick’s hand. “For a while, it looked like you weren’t gonna make it.” “Man, I was looking forward to that match for years. You think I’m gonna let some hold-up stop me” Oh no. Salaok was getting his ass kicked one way or the other.” “Watch your language,” says Coretta. “Sorry, Mom. Hey, how are you doing?” Brick says, as he hugs his mother. “I didn’t think you’d come.” “I’m just glad my baby is home,” she replies. Brick’s victory at Wrestlecade was a victory long-awaited. For years now, Brick had been looking to get some retribution against the leader of the Nevermore. He’s been looking for it since November of 2001, when Salaok robbed him of his first-ever shot at the World title. Since last summer, when Salaok used Aaron as a pawn in his depraved game of human chess. Since Salaok became co-owner of Global Wrestling and determined to get Brick eliminated from the company. Since he began to taunt Brick about Aaron and claimed to be at Monica’s wedding. At Wrestlecade, he finally got to claim his payback. Many are celebrating that Brick beat Salaok. All of them, especially Coretta, are just glad that he made it back in one piece, save for the bandage on his forehead. Aaron brings out two bottles of champagne and sparkling apple cider. He and Felicia begin to pour glasses for everyone. Felicia then pours herself a glass of cider, as she is 7 months pregnant. “If I can have everyone’s attention, please,” says I’d like to offer a toast. Let’s all raise our glasses to my boy Brick, for beating the hell out of that punk Salaok. To Brick!” “To Brick!” They all clink glasses and drink. “And now,” Brick adds, “on to the World title, and beating another punk down who’s got an ass whipping coming to them.” “How’s Gunner, by the way?” asks Aaron. “She’s got a few bruised ribs from that shot to the back that punk Briggs gave her. Now I got three reasons to want to beat his ass. I already owed him an ass whipping for walkin” out on me, Ely an? Logan.” “And you’ll do it, too.” “Damn right.” “OK, now, how many times do I have to tell you to watch your language?” “Well, I’m sorry, Mom, but that’s how I feel. Jesse Briggs has such an asswhipping coming to him, it ain’t funny. Ah, well, is this a party or what, y’all” Let’s celebrate!”
