Trials and Tribulations (2 - Stealth)

15 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15/08/03 Stealth Jushin smiled at the memories of the mission that his brother had just spoken of. He remembered the mission now, it was one of his favourite missions of all time. After that mission the clan was never heard from again, it was one of their most successful missions. The smile on his father's face was priceless, he loved the vengeance. Never again did any of the younger Japanese kids try to rob from a respected Yakuza member. 'Now Jushin, my student. Are you ready to tell your tale?' asked Tanaka. 'Yes, master. I am ready, for I have long chosen this tale, as it is one of my personal favourites,' replied Jushin. 'Well then my son. Which merit does your story depict?' asked Tanaka. 'Stealth,' Jushin's answer was short concise and sweet. He gathered his mind and collected his thoughts. Flashback The sunlight glared in his eyes as he stepped out through the doorway. He walked out first, followed by his brother. The two were dressed almost identically. Jushin wore a black Gucci track suit, with a white baseball cap turned backwards and a white undershirt. Ichiro standing behind him wore a white Gucci track suit, with a black undershirt, and a black baseball cap. They were both wearing white K-Swiss shoes. Jushin wore a large smile. Finally he had been awarded a car of his own, it was about time since the Ichiro had gotten a brand new car, since they had gotten attacked by a group of their father's enemies. A fight that ended up ruinning their black Cadillac CTS. Now both brothers, ahd their own individual car. But still, today, they would be riding together. They walked towards Jushin's car. Ichiro had no problem since for the first half of a year in their father's service it was he who had been doing all of the driving. Ichiro followed Jushin to his new car. It was a red and black Mitsubishi Eclipse. Ichiro stepped into the passenger side of the car, and Jushin took his role as the driver. it was the first time that Jushin had driven since the two had begun working for their father. Jushin revved the engine as the car roared to life. He reversed out and pulled into the Tokyo streets. The streets were congested and the trip was slow. Ichiro spent most of his time focussing his mind and energy on the mission they had just received. Although they had until the night to prepare, he could not shake away a bad feeling. He stared out the window, as they passed the many buildings and skyscrapers that lined the streets of downtown Tokyo. Jushin watched the road impatiently. He could not stand the heavy traffic. He waited impatiently and sulked through the whole ride home, at his inability to unleash the true power, and test out his new car. The car finally pulled into the underground parking garage of their appartment complex. The two brothers stepped out of the car as soon as they had parked. Ichiro smiled as the pair walked past the car which had been delivered to him this morning. The white Lexus SC430 was parked beautifully, untouched and unblemished in the underground garage. The two walked side by side towards the elevator shaft. They walked up and pressed the button to go upwards. After a few minutes of waiting the doors opened and the two brothers entered the elevator. Jushin pressed the button labelled twenty-seven. The doors shut and the elevator took them to the floor. The brothers exitted the elevator and walked together towards their rooms. Jushin lived in appartment 2720, while Ichiro lived in 2721. They each unlocked their seperate doors and went inside. Each had a different way of preparation, and they would meet in a couple of hours to discuss their plans for the assassination set to happen that night. Ichiro opened the door to his appartment and walked inside. He shut the door and immediately walked towards his small stereo. He pressed the button and from the speakers came smooth, calming guitar melodies. Ichiro relaxed his mind, and slowly punched through the air, and practised many of his Shao Lin Arts. It had always helped him to relax and concentrate. He focussed all of his inner energy on his mind, and let the instincts of his body handle the movements. Jushin pushed the key into the lock and opened the door. He walked through, and then closed and locked the door behind him. He went in and threw on some music. Then he walked over to his television, turned on the set and began to pull out his Playstation 2 Console. He popped in Metal Gear Solid 2 and began to play. It was Jushin's usual pre-mission routine. He would always warm up with some intence simulated violence and MGS always was his favourite game to play. The two brothers remained in their own rooms for about an hour. It was after about an hour, that Jushin was interruped from his game from a knocking on the door. He opened the peep-hole. A ritual to keep himself safe from enemies who might have happened to have found his address. He saw his brother standing there smiling, pizza in one hand, maps and markers in the other. 'Open the door,' said Ichiro impatiently. Jushin decided to co-operate. Other nights he may have played around with his brother, but he was hungry, and the box of pizza looked ever so inviting. Jushin opened the door and shut it behind his brother. Ichiro walked over to the small dining table and plopped everything down onto it. He first opened the pizza box and grabbed a slice. The two brothers began to scarf down the pizza. After about ten minutes, the pizza was finished and Jushin took the box over to his small recycling pile. When he returned to the table he saw that his brother already had the maps and blueprints of the hotel open. Their target was one of his father's dirty business rivals. He had often stolen product and inventory from their father, at the expense of their father. Now it was time for that to stop. They had received a tip-off that the rival, one Masato Kazuyashi was holding a small 'business' meeting for some of his employees. They would be in the luxury suite at the Holiday Inn Tokyo. Ichiro was studying the maps hard, when he noticed Jushin sneak up. 'Listen bro, there is no way we're going to be able to walk through hotel security with a whole lot of fire power, so we got to find another way into the hotel, and into the room,' stated Ichiro. Jushin leaned over and looked at the map. He pointed to a small door at the rear of the building, 'What about that place?' he asked. 'It leads to the guard room and the main security panel, no dice.' replied Ichiro, as he continued studying the map. Jushin looked closer at the map and continued looking over his brother's shoulder. Finally Ichiro smiled and pointed to a small cargo door for food deliveries and such. 'There! It's a small cargo door, we should be able to kick through the door, and get in. Now we've just got to find a way into the Luxury Suite.' Jushin nodded as he assumed that the cargo door would work. However, it was true they still had no idea of how to get to the room. It was then he remembered all the great James Bond flicks and such that he had seen. It was this thought that gave him his idea. 'Let's go through the vents.' he said. Ichiro looked at him questionably at first. Then the thoughts ran through his head, it could work. It was crazy but it might work. 'That would get us there but how are we going to get down?' 'We'll find a way, it can't be too hard? Come one bro, we've done crazier things before, we work in the Yakuza. Let's go in shooting!' exclaimed Jushin. 'Alright then. Let's Do it.' 3 Hours Later.... The car pulled up outside the hotel. Jushin drove by slowly, but made sure not to attract attention to the car. They drove past the lobby and turned down the dark, quiet alleyway at the side of the building. They pulled in and killed the lights as they exitted the vehicle. Both of the Nakazura brothers were dressed in black athletic jumpsuits. In the darkness of the alleyway they were barely visible to anyone up close, and completely invisible to anyone from a distance of more then ten feet. They went to the back of the car and each man pulled out a rope, a carabeanor, and four pistols. They loaded the guns, and checked the ropes for rips or tears. Before going any further each of the brothers, put on a brand new pair of black gloves, to cover their finger prints. They okayed all of their equipment, and then made their way towards the cargo door. Ichiro stepped up and kicked the door directly on the handle. As it had in so many occasions with shifty carpenter work, the door fell off the hinges, opening a hole for the two men to crawl through. They walked into the dark room and passed through the open space. They found the next door leading to the back staff hallways. Jushin turned the knob and slowly opened the door. He peaked outside, and saw the corridor was empty. He quickly walked out and ran silently towards the small shelfing unit underneath the venting unit. Ichiro followed him closely, with a quick tug, the cover panel fell out and with Ichiro's help, Jushin climbed up into the ventalation ducts. Then he reached down and pulled Ichiro up quickly. They jimmied the cover panel back into place, and then made their way through the vents on a route they planned out. They were sweating inside the warm and tight vents. They had been crawling on their stomachs for at least ten minutes, when by their calculations they would be right on top of the room. Ichiro took out a small drill, it made a soft noise, but nothing that wouldn't be mistaken for the humming of the ventilation system. He drilled a small circle through the aluminum of the vent. Then through the drywall, he pulled the floor panel that he had drilled upwards, and peeked inside. Below him he saw a room that looked luxurious to say the very least. He saw about six men, but none of them were his target. That is when they saw him, Masato Kazuyashi they were in the right place. It was time to go to work. The two brothers quickly inserted their carabeanors into the roof of the ventilation ducts. They looped the rope through, and checked the safety. When they were satisfied that the carabeanors would not give out they began to unscrew the bottom pannel of the vent. When the ten foot by five foot panel was completely removed, they slid it aside, and looked at the roof panel. They looked at each other before slowly beginning to speak. 'I know you're listening,' began the brothers in unison. 'Come to me in my time of need, when times are tough and those around me bleed. Let not my enemies conquer me, give me strength so I can see. Guide me in this final flight, from all the darkness into light. Through desperate days, through all the haze. Carry me to my success.' With that they pushed the rooftop panel down and with a loud crash all the attention in the room was drawn to the roof. Then they dropped quickly, locking their carabeanors in place, hanging upside down just above seven feet off of the ground. They each reached into their pants and pulled out pistols they immediately began shooting. After they finished the clips in their first guns, they reached behind their backs and pulled out two more pistols, and again littered the room with fire. They dropped their guns, and with a swing of momentum the brothers turned right side up. They quickly undid their carabeanors and dropped down. They quickly pulled knives out of the boots, and began to quickly search the floor for any survivors. They kicked bodies over, and rummaged through the blood. No one left alive, except for one survivor, who lay bleeding to death. With a slit of his throat, Jushin ended his misery. The two brothers began a quick clean up of the mess. They carried each of the bodies and threw them into the bathtub and the bathroom floor. All in all seven bodies lay heaped in the bathroom. On the very top, as a message to all those who dared oppose their father, they left Masato Kazuyashi dead on top of all his loyal servants, all dead at his feet. And on his chest lay his wallet, emptied as a sign of the payback for robbing their father so many times. 'Good work Jushin, now let's get rid of these clothes and get the hell out of here!' said Ichiro. 'Agreed,' replied Jushin. The brothers stripped off the jump suits and threw them into the toilet, to rinse off the blood. They were now dressed casually each in a pair of blue jeans, and a plain white T-Shirt. With the gloves in their pocket and the room in ruins, they walked out the door, making sure the door locked behind them as they left. On the way out through the lobby the doorman wished them well. They smiled at the irony in his words, as they walked out the door. Ichiro handed the guy twenty yen, and smiled as they walked out the door and towards their car. END FLASHBACK 'Another fine tale, I believe that we shall not need all ten merits as your first stories have faired my ears well,' began the Shao Lin monc. 'Now you shall each tell me one more story, and your judgement shall be passed.' Both brothers gave a sigh of relief. Ten stories was a lot and now it was significantly easier. Ichiro, racked his mind. What story could he tell next? To Be Continued
