Training Session Part 3

10 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/10/03 Jushin watched as his brother's eyes rolled back into his head slightly. His body began to twitch slightly, and he could see what he thought was his muscles pulsating and bulging through his skin. He knew what it was, exactly what it was. He knew because he had seen it so many times before. Ichiro was using the technique which their master Tanaka had taught him. Jushin had never really known what it was called, only knew what it was and what it did. What it was, was an ancient Shao Lin technique, part of the Forgotten Arts. And what it did was allowed oneself to become stronger, faster, and essentially better at everything. By opening one's mind to the Lotus, that one could allow the Lotus to take over the soul and essence of his being. When Ichiro did this he was able to anticipate his brother's moves, almost as a sense of precog. He was quick to react, and his blows struck stronger than ever. He had used the technique many times, when engaged in combat with his enemies. Ichiro had been taught the technique going into his final years in the temple. Tanaka had received word from the brothers Nakazura's father, that he would be wanting to see his sons. He also informed Tanaka, that he intended to put them to use, in his Yakuza service. And so, this in a way was the monk's parting gift to Jushin and Ichiro. Although Ichiro was the only one to learn it, as he was the only one ready. Tanaka had instructed him to teach the technique to Jushin as soon as he felt, that his little brother was ready. Jushin readied himself for another attack. He could try to attack his big brother right now, while he was not ready and would not be able to strike back. But, Jushin would fight with honour, honour and strength. He waited patiently as his brother began to come back down to earth. His brother stopped the minor twitching, and the electricity seemed to be inside of him, rather than outside. His eyes came down and were now cold and dark as they stared back at his little brother. Ichiro felt the adrenaline and electricty running through his body. He felt the surge of electricity and a slight thirst for violence. He knew what the Lotus could do, and could not allow it to completely take over. He was now much stronger and much quicker than Jushin, but still not at his full potential. With the Lotus in full control he might very well have killed Jushin. And that was the last thing he needed, this was a test to see if Jushin was truly ready, there was little to no chance of Jushin defeating him, but depending on how he defended himself, would prove his readiness. 'You almost done there, bro?' asked Jushin. Apparently Jushin was more observant than Ichiro first thought. Jushin knew exactly what his big brother was doing, and he allowed it to happen. It was honourable, but it may have been foolish. 'I am done, I'm glad you allowed me to finish my transformation technique. But it was very foolish, now I hope you are ready to face the consequences,' answered Ichiro. Jushin could only smile. He bent his knees and distributed his body weight. He prepared for the quick flurry that would soon come his way. Ichiro watched his little brother prepare for his attack. Jushin was smart to not rush into this fight. Without thinking this was a fight that he would surely lose. He could only hope to anticipate and find a way to disrupt Ichiro's train of thought. That would be the only way, his brother would find success. Ichiro decided that his little brother had sufficient time to prepare, and began to slowly walk towards his Jushin. Jushin was not intimidated, he wasn't sure if he should be or not. Although he had seen his brother use this technique many times, this was the first time that he would have to go up against it. Ichiro now began to run at Jushin. Jushin was quick to react and popped up onto his toes. As Ichiro neared he leaped forwrad and threw a strong left hand at Jushin. Jushin threw up his right forearm to block the punch, and followed through with a left of his own. Ichiro was caught slightly off guard but still managed to duck the punch, and come around with a quick roundhouse kick. The kick got there just before Jushin's block could, and it sent Jushin reeling backwards. Ichiro smiled, and wiped his nose. He did a slight shuffle, and got in line with his brother. He jumped into the air, and tried a flying kick. Jushin reacted quickly by raising his left leg, and kicking Ichiro's out the way, but Ichiro followed through with a right hand, which again Jushin blocked. Ichiro landed, and threw another left, right, left combination at Jushin, who reacted quickly to block all three. The lightning flashed again, as the rain continued to pour down. Ichiro's face was stern and vigilant as he ducked down and swept his leg around to trip Jushin. Jushin hopped, and delivered a kick right to the jaw of Ichiro. His big brother was barely phased however, and quickly stood up, and again continued with a flurry of punches. And so it went for a good half an hour. Ichiro would attack, and Jushin would attempt to block and defend himself. By the end of the half hour, the rain was still pouring down, but the puddles on the roof had a red tint to them, as the Crimson liquid of blood poured from several cuts on Jushin's face. The lightning flashed and the thunder roared as Ichiro finally stepped away from his battered and beaten brother. Jushin watched from the ground as his brother stepped away. He knew it was over for now, he wiped the blood from his lip, and from the small cut above his eye. Ichiro stepped back and looked at his brother. He then closed his eyes and began to regain complete control of his body. The adrenaline and electricity left his body. His limbs felt suddenly tired and aching, it was the normal for this type of occurence. His eyes felt heavy, and the cold, dark glare left his eyes. The rain continued to pour down onto the two beaten and bruised brothers. Ichiro's mind was tired and moreso was his body. But, his brother had proven it to him, and he had a vow with Master Tanaka that he could not break. He had sword to the monk, that the second he felt that Jushin was ready, he would teach him the technique of the Lotus. Ichiro had agreed not to wait, not to sleep, not to do anything but teach his brother the technique of letting the Lotus control his body. The rain began to let up slowly, and the flashes of lightning and thunder were long between. 'Congratulations brother,' said Ichiro as he walked towards his brother lying on the ground. He grabbed Jushin by the hand, and pulled his little brother to his feet. 'Why congratulations? You kicked the shit out of me,' asked Jushin. 'Yes, but you were able to put up a good fight. You fought better against me, better than any who have opposed me before. When I allow the Lotus to control my body, I am near undefeatable to those who oppose me. And congratulations, because you are now ready to learn the same technique,' answered Ichiro. 'Really? I thought I was not to be trusted with such a power. Tanaka would never trust me to use this techniqe. He would be afraid I would abuse it, and use it for no good!' said Jushin. 'Nay, for it was him who told me to teach it to you. He told me 'as soon as Jushin is ready, and you'll know when he is. The second you feel that he is ready, you teach him. He is your brother, and your partner, if both of you can use this technique you will be a great force to be reckoned with. I say this to you now, in all honesty. Do not sleep, do not eat, do not rest, as soon as he is ready teach him, Ichiro.' That is what I was told, and thus you see. I have already done too much by talking to you, now come my brother, you must now be taught the technique of the Lotus,' answered Ichiro. Jushin smiled and stood beside his brother. He had longed to learn this technique for a long, long time. And now his chance was finally upon him. He could not pass this chance up, nor could he screw this up. With both of them using this technique, when they went back to Tokyo, they would be untouchable. To Be Continued...
