Uncertainty is a Bitch ...

25 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05.25.05 I suppose I'll let it go Until I have something more to say for me I'm so afraid of defeat And I'm out of reason to believe in me I'm out of trying to defy I'm so afraid of the gift you give me I don't belong here and I'm not well I'm so ashamed of the lie I'm living Right on the wrong side of it all - Seether . . . “Thank you, Mr. McCoy; I will see that the message gets to him to call you back when he gets in.” Rhianna showed a discouraged frown as she looked towards the couch to see Corvus? brother sitting there watching with interest. She set the phone back on the cradle and walked over to the couch and sat on the edge of it trying to figure out how she would tell Corvus about the phone message when he returned. He wouldn’t be happy and she knew that much already. “I take it that was my brother’s boss that just called.” “Yes, he was calling about the police incident last week and wants to talk to Corvus about it.” She couldn’t really sense if he was in trouble with Global Wrestling but just receiving a phone call about it was bad enough. “I just hope that nothing bad happens when he returns the call.” “What’s the worst that could happen” He gets fired and then goes on a rampage killing some of the GW personnel. I don’t think they take him very seriously at times.” He laughs at the thought of his brother going on a killing spree. “I just think that he should step away from trying to find out information about Brennan; I know you agree with me.” Rhianna slipped from the end of the couch and walked over to the large window that hold the view of the front yard and fountain. Sometimes the truth was pretty painful but yet everyone had a right to know why things happen as they do even if some never reach that point. “He’s stubborn and until he finds what he is looking for he’s not going to stop.” “If I were him, I would focus more on who is trying to set him up.” He suspected the woman as a suspect knowing how much his brother hated her but lately his demeanor changed towards her and he was unsure as to why. Did she have some kind of hold on him that he was not powerful enough to shake? Maybe she changed her ways ... that is sometimes possible. “I could take a stab at a few guesses but who knows if this place is bugged or not so I’m just going to keep it to myself.” “Actually, I had some of my people sweep the place and so far it has managed to stay clean.” She turned towards him to give him reassurance that it was in fact a safe place. She had just as much secrets as both of them did as vampires are not supposed to exist except in the minds of people who need creativity to keep them sane. “What do you know about this cop that has been doing everything in her power to try and bust Corvus?” “Other than I heard that she was hot ... not a whole lot else.” “Was that a direct quote from someone we know extremely well?” She didn’t want to sound jealous over it since she just was able to get Corvus to trust her finally; she knew that anything she said to Ash would probably be repeated to him at one point or another. “I didn’t think she seemed all that special really ...” “I’m not going to spend too much time worrying about if it is true or not.” He shifted his train of thought from women back to what they had been speaking about a few moments previously. “Corvus seems to really enjoy wrestling and at times I think it is such an unhealthy habit for our kind ... it paints a target on our backs for easy annihilation for the smarter hunters.” “I agree but telling him what to do is not exactly a genius thing to do. He doesn’t listen very well and he won’t exactly listen to me given our history but I will give him enough credit that he’s intelligent enough to keep himself out of harms way.” She tries to convince herself with the statement but even she knows that even the smartest kindred have met undesirable fates on just one mistake. . . . The Hard Rock Caf? was one of those places that Corvus always seemed to meet up with Detective Van Buren. This time it was actually an arranged meeting between the two. He had reservations about it but he needed to clear himself in her mind otherwise she would probably not stop until she uncovered the truth of what he was and that there were a lot more where he came from. “Can I get you a beverage, Detective?” “I’m fine for the moment but thank you.” She wanted little to do with him for the fact that he creeped her out. There was also this odd feeling she had about the interrogation session she had with him as well. ? Let’s cut to the chase here ... why should I believe that you are completely innocent and that someone is trying to frame you for the death of a poor young girl that you were seen with nearly up until the time she died.” “Look, I’m a professional wrestler and was once an acclaimed musician. Do you think with all the amounts of girls that I’ve had access to over the years that there would be more of a trail if I were such a psychotic and deranged killer?” He wanted to hit the point home to her that there was no way he could have done it even if the evidence was surmountable. “I just need you to cooperate with me and I can help you find who you are looking for but you have to trust me and I can see by the way your eyes are at this moment that it is going to take a shift in momentum for you to do that.” “You’re right; I have every reason NOT to trust you.” She says as she tries not to directly look into his eyes. It was partially a superstition that she thought he had some kind of ability that he could talk people into things like a sort of hypnotic type of thing. “To be honest with you, ever since we first came into contact ... I’ve thought there was something strange about you. Two girls are dead and you’re indirectly involved. How does that look to you?” “Not very good obviously, but honestly it’s a fucked up situation. I was just at the wrong place at the wrong time.” He plays with his family crest ring a little bit; it is something he does when he is nervous. “I am a wealthy person and there are sick people out there that try and exploit things like that. Maybe someone will try blackmailing me or something ... I don’t know but what I do know and if the conviction in my voice isn’t enough to tell you that I’m not guilty of these crimes then you must have nothing but air between your ears.” “Earning my trust by insulting me is not getting you any closer to me believing you. Yes, you are wealthy and have vast resources at your disposal; that is why your lawyer came to your aid so quickly.” She sighs in frustration as it never seems to get anywhere with this man. He’s always so condescending and never acts serious about things. The one thing that scares her the most though is that she might be feeling a little bit for him which would be against every rule she has for herself and of the department’s. “If you can show me that I can trust you then I will but until then I am still under the impression that you are a murderer ... I have to go, there’s other police work that needs doing.” “I need to get going too, my brother is staying with me and I am not so certain that he hasn’t burned my house to the ground yet.” “Bad habits must run in your family, maybe I’ll be bringing him in for a crime he didn’t commit either.” She said as she scooted her seat back and stood up. “I’ll give you a call if I know anything more and if you get any ideas on who you think might be framing you then you know where to reach me.” She put her chair back in place and walked away from him, leaving him alone. A range of things came into Corvus? mind after she left. One of them was that she drove him crazy but she was mortal and he could never seriously have a relationship with her. On the other hand there was Rhianna, whom he just started to let in but did it slowly because of their sketchy past. Lately living the life that he did was becoming more of a burden and sometimes the thought of final death came to his mind. He tried to shake that away but in the long run it might lead him to go into hiding, to fall into the slumber that so many of the older ones have done for fear of those who conspire against them. “I’m starting to really hate Las Vegas ...” He said as he got up from his seat and left the place to head back for home. It was kind of sad when he didn’t think he could trust his brother to be there with Rhianna alone. The past was an indicator of that. . . . “Did you ever have any children?” Rhianna turned towards Ash to ask, knowing that of course Corvus had two children and one of them had died and the other is the one he was asking for help in finding information about. “No, I was going to start a family but well as you can see things turned out differently. I was about to be married right before I was embraced, I never was able to become a husband or a father in my mortal lifetime.” Rhianna pondered on it for a few moments and realized she didn’t have any herself before she had become a vampire and that was the one thing she regretted truly in her entire existence of being cursed. “I’m sorry to hear that ... sometimes it is best that maybe we didn’t.” Her voice is sullen and retrospective. “Just look at what your brother is going through. He had an entire family and it all fell apart on him.” Ash nodded, it was the mortal concerns that he should have left behind which are his downfall mostly. “He worries too much about the mortal world and something that has been long done with.” Talk of anything other than music really held no interest with Ash. “I think I’m going to put on MTV and see if it’s watch-able tonight. I was so much better back in the early nineties.” Rhianna nodded politely not wanting to really say anything about his lack of concern for his brother’s bad habits. She went about her business and continued looking out the window waiting for any sign of Corvus. Her wish had been granted as the beautiful sound of his Plymouth Belvedere GTX Convertible pulling through the gates that opened for him with a touch of a button. The car pulls into the garage and she continues to look out the window patiently anticipating his return and give him the message that was left for him while he was out. Corvus walked in and put his coat on the rack and looked around to see what was going on. He noted his brother was glued to the television and Rhianna stood by the window looking out, captivated by something or was doing a very good job pretending. He walked over to her and placed his arms around her waist and rested his chin against her shoulder, whispering softly into her ear. “Did anything exciting happen while I was away or have you two been this way all evening?” For the first time in a while he was feeling contentment as she placed her hands on top of his. “Your Boss called; he wanted you to get a hold of him tonight still if you were not going to be too late.” Corvus was in a state of wonderment hearing the news that Bubba McCoy had personally called him. Usually most people in the management portion of GW didn’t talk to him other than making sure he was at the events he was scheduled at and other boring shit. “Did he say what he wanted?” Rhianna hesitated for a second; she didn’t want to flat out tell him that Bubba knew about him being arrested for murder. “He didn’t really say ... you should call him.” She turned around in his arms so that she was facing him, resting her head against his chest. There were feelings festering inside her that she never quite realized she could be capable of. “I’m going to let you take care of your business; I’ll be in my room if you wish to talk about whatever business it is between you and Mr. McCoy.” Rhianna slipped from his grasp and started walking towards the stairs when he stopped her. “You can wait for me in my room if you like?” She considered the option for several moments and conceded to her thoughts. She walked over towards the master bedroom and entered it, closing the door behind her unnoticed by anyone other than Corvus. “Ash, turn the television down a tad could you” I’m making an important phone call.” He turned the television off and stood up nodding to his brother. “I need to check my e-mail anyway, the record company is probably pissed that I took off; I’ll need to work things out with them.” He walked out of the room to give his brother privacy and to take care of his personal business as well. Corvus picked up the phone and dialed the number he had that went directly to Bubba McCoy’s office and his secretary was still there to answer the phone at such a late hour. “This is Corvus calling in return to the call I received earlier while I was out.” She put him on hold for a moment until Bubba himself got onto the phone. “Bubba, Corvus here ... I was told that you called?” He listened as the boss went on a bit about how he needed to lay low a little bit now that he’s got some heat on him about being arrested for a possible homicide. How Bubba found out about this so quickly amazed him. “I understand and I have been keeping a low profile. I don’t do interviews or anything like that unless it is for GW specifically. I’ll make sure that something like this never happens again ... you have my word.” Corvus goes silent as he continues to listen to Bubba. He wasn’t quite sure what he was hearing at first so he asked him to repeat himself. Sure enough ... he had a match for this upcoming Evo and it was high profile. With the way things have been going lately, high profile was not really something Corvus wanted to have to deal with. “Are you serious, a main event match against them” Damn. I’ll let my brother know right away so he can try and get back into the right frame of mind for this sort of thing. He’s been out of the ring for almost a year or longer.” He listened on as Bubba went on to wish him and his partner luck in their match and also stay out of trouble. Corvus chuckled a little but did it so that Bubba didn’t think he was laughing at him as much as he was the situation. They finished and he set the phone down just as Ash walked back into the room conveniently. “You look like you’ve just been told the blood bank was blown to pieces, what’s up?” He looks at Ash not sure how to tell him that he was getting back in the ring again and didn’t have much choice about it. It was one thing for Corvus to be off his game but if Ash was not up to it then it would be a complete disaster. “You still have your gear, right?” “I think it is in one of the bags that I brought with me, why?” Ash said with much skepticism and a little fear of what might come out of his brother’s mouth. “At this upcoming Evo ... you and I are booked in a tag match.” Ash shook his head a bit then walked toward the kitchen to get something to drink and was followed by Corvus who had more information about his apparent worry over the match they were chosen to participate in. The younger vampire poured two glasses of blood and set one on the table for Corvus, who grabbed it quickly and drank it like it was going out of style. He wasn’t exactly afraid of Ruckus Inc as people but in the ring they were respected by him even if he wouldn’t tell them personally and of course he wouldn’t. “We have a non-title match against the current tag team champion in the main event.” “No shit?” Ash didn’t seem upset at all when he heard the news. He was a little more optimistic about it than Corvus was. Was he worried about this being his first time in the ring since he was a part of PTC’s Survival Event for WMW last August? He wouldn’t give the impression that he was. “Ok, so we’re facing the tag team champs ... what’s the big deal?” “The big deal is you may not be ready and as you can see I’m having a hard time winning matches as of late.” He finally admitted to his losing streak as if it were a problem. Corvus was someone who had so much pride in himself that admitting defeat was hard to do. “Something just hasn’t been right and to face off against two guys that are at the top of the food chain is just not something I think that you and I as a team are ready for.” “You know what I think big brother” What you need is to get back into that seek and destroy mind frame that I used to know you had.” Ash was doing his best to rally his brother so that this wasn’t a one sided effort on either of their parts. “Tomorrow you and I are going to go down to the arena and work on some things. You tell me that they are tough and tag champions ... I don’t see how that’s a problem. We’ve been tag team champions too ... one of the best if you remember correctly.” Corvus took the words into perspective and as much as Ash was right about them being good as a team, it was a long time since they were together. They both put their glasses in the dish washer around the same time. Ash slaps his brother in the back and grins. “Don’t look so discouraged ... the lost boys are back in town!” He said as he started to walk towards the living room. “We need a name, Ash. I refuse to go back in the past and use our old name.” “I’m in agreement with that. This is a new age for us as a team and we need a fresh start.” The two men reached deep into their minds to come up with something that made sense and instinctively at the same time they looked up at one another and knew what the other was thinking. They allowed one another access to their minds because they could trust each other. “I like that that, it works ... Project Mayhem.” Corvus smiles as he folds his arms across his chest. “I think we can get away with it.” “We need to twist it around a little, spice it up a bit. How about removing the c and using a k instead?” He offered the comment up rather enthusiastically. “What do you think?” “I say let’s go with it.” He nods several times not wanting to change anything about it. “Maybe I should go out and get a pair of pants so we can match, what do you think?” Corvus was unsure though that he wanted the two of them to look alike so he asked for an opinion. “I think we should stick to our usual. Though if you were going to go with wearing pants, there are those leather ones you always used to wear back in the AWA days.” Ash recalled as he remember their time as at team. “That’s ok, I prefer my current gear. I think we should keep our styles different since the two of us as individuals are. We can show our unity in the work we do in the ring and nothing else will need to be said.” Ash nodded, “Good idea, before I head out for a while I just wanted to let you know that Deacon’s back.” There was a pause between sentences because he wasn’t sure how Corvus would react. “I told him about what’s been going on ... well at least with you looking for info and he said he’d help.” “You didn’t mention anything about my house guest did you ...” A chuckle came from Ash as he shook his head, “No, I didn’t tell him that Rhianna was staying here. I know enough to leave that up to you big brother.” He holds himself back from busting a gut over the situation. “I’ll leave you guys to talk or whatever you want to do ... I need to find some action.” “Just keep your eyes out, if someone is looking to get at me they won’t hesitate to try and do something to you. If you need anything ... call me.” He knew there was a possibility that the person who was trying to set him up would use his brother to get to him in an instant. “I appreciate your concern but I can handle myself ... but if it makes you feel better if I feel that I’m screwed then I’ll call you.” Ash didn’t give him a chance to say anything before he disappeared out of sight. All that Corvus heard was the front door open and close and the sound of Ash’s motorcycle engine revving up before taking off. Corvus made his way to the bedroom and to his surprise Rhianna was laying in the bed reading a book. The most surprising part was that she wasn’t wearing anything from what he could tell. He closed the door behind him and she looked up as she closed the book. “I hope you didn’t mind me getting comfortable ... I guess I’m just so used to doing it in my apartment that I just went ahead without asking. I’m sorry, I can ...” She wasn’t able to get another word as he took his clothes off and joined her. “I don’t mind really, I could use someone to have next to me right now.” Rhianna sets the book off to the side on the daystand next to her and turns back to him, curling up to his defined, muscular body. Her head rested on his chest as her hand came up to run along his pectoral muscles. “Did everything go alright with your phone conversation?” “Yes, it went pretty well. Bubba told me to kind of keep a low profile until things get sorted out with the police.” He was silent for a minute, as he was formulating trying to relay his fears about going up against Ruckus Inc. on Evolution this week. “Ash and I were also paired to take on the current World Champion and his partner that make up the tag team champions as well.” She slowly moves her hand up to his face, caressing his cheek while she catches a glimpse of his aura. The concern of the match is what is making the aura stand out with the color it is. “I feel your concern, but I think you should not let it weigh you down so much. You shouldn’t worry about this situation with the police either, there are plenty of things we can do to wash this all away.” Calming him was not going to be easy but she would at least try as mightily as she could. “Public image is everything to our kind ... you know that and I’m beginning to lose the appeal I had and it bugs me.” He brought his arms under his head and used them as a pillow as he continued to use her as a sound board for his troubles. “I feel like I’m slipping in the mortal world and if I do it any further I should go into the ground and hope that I’m not found.” “Darling, don’t give up just yet. Wait and see what your brother and I can do for you. I know you wanted to be this super bad fighter but sometimes it’s not about how you fight but why you fight.” She had his attention and needed to keep it now. “It’s fine to want to go out and slug someone but when you don’t have a reason for doing it then you become lost and that is what you are right now, lost.” “How do I go about getting found then?” He said in a perplexed tone. Admitting that he needed someone was the hardest task for him to do. “I have to get back on track, I have to be who I was ... who I am.” Rhianna turned his head towards her and leaned closer to kiss him with soft, sensual, lips. She broke the kiss slowly as she looked deeply into his eyes. “You’ll find your way on your own but you need that extra little push.” She resists for a moment what she was going to say but she couldn’t stop herself from doing so. “History has proven that you’ve just needed a good woman behind you to get you moving ... not all of them were good in ways and I cannot say that I am completely that either but I can get you where you need to be.” She touches his lip lightly with her finger, tracing an outline slowly. “I may have done terrible things in the past but unlike anyone else ...” She pauses once again. “I know I can ...” He stopped her from speaking because he knew what she would say and it wasn’t something he wanted to hear at the moment. She had gotten closer to him than most people could have and that was strange enough fiction. “You don’t have to say it ... at least don’t say it right now. I’ll let you know when the time is right. Just be here for me because I need ... you.” He reached over quickly and turned out the light that came from the lamp and returned back to her in the sanctity of the dark as they made their alliance stronger in away. The wind could be heard blowing harder against the window and rain began to pelt the glass. Every other moment or two thunder could be heard but neither of them were paying much attention to it. . . . Los Angeles, California Inside an office building late in the evening a man is running to a large set of doors and opens it without knocking. He has a manila folder under his arm and approaches someone sitting at a desk and tosses the folder onto it. “I found the information you requested, Mr. Scott.” The man bringing the file took a huge breath after having run all the way from the elevator which was quite a good distance. “Miss de Vorace is in Nevada at the moment with a guy named Corvus.” He points to the file. “It’s all in there, sir.” The unknown Mr. Scott brings the folder closer to him and opened it up to look at the intel that had been gathered on Rhianna and her whereabouts as of late. He closes the file and the sound of the leather shifting as he moved could be heard. “Excellent. Thank you, Kyle ... this information will come in great use for me.” He began to tap his fingers against the desk as a sign that the young errand boy should leave his office immediately. The door closed as Kyle left to go and run even more errands for his boss. Mr. Scott stood up and walked over to the large window that looked out at the city ... city of angels as they called it. To him it was a city of filth, decay, and miscreants that were easily controlled by offering them money, fame, anything that would get them at least fifteen minutes worth. He did not cast a reflection at all in the glass, which would give anyone a good indication of his true nature. “Rhianna, you’re still infatuated with him after all these years are you?” He placed a cigar between his lips and lit it, puffing smoke out of his mouth before he spoke another word. “We’ll see how long it lasts and how much you’ll beg me to spare him to come back to me ...”
