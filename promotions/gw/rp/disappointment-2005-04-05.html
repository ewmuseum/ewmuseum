Disappointment

5 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04.05.05 'I can't believe this,' shouted Ken as he walked down the aisle and to the back after being eliminated from his Clash of the Titans World Title Match. On his way, a fan threw a shirt at him, which the disgruntled KPJ immediatly snatched out of the air and threw back without even looking. The disappointment had settled into his stomach as he walked through the curtains and to his locker room with his hands on his hips and a tear in his eye. 'Good match,' said a half-smiling production assistant holding on to his clipboard with both hands in the hallway. He was backing into the wall enough to move his headset off of his ears slightly. 'Shove it,' mumbled Ken under his breath, once again without looking, as he approached his door. In his eyes, Ken was a failure. He could accept losing his Title once, but a second time was nearly too much to take. The thought of a disappointed Bill Moresi and, worse yet, a disappointed Gunner Adisson danced across his mind as he stood staring at the door that read 'The Elite.' All that he could ponder was how he could face them. He could not justify in his mind entering that room as a loser. However, he had accepted far more disgracing things in the past and finally convinced himself that he could, indeed, face this one. He just didn't want to hear the tone of Bill's voice as his friend attempted to cheer him up and give him hope with a hint of sorrow in his voice. Nor could he stand to look into Gunner's eyes... eyes that were filled with happiness that he was okay, but were sadened by his elimination. Before he could bring himself to turn the knob, the door opened on it's own with someone he didn't expect standing in front of him. 'Ace,' he said in a surprised tone, 'what are you doing here?' Ace Harding was hardly a small man, standing at six feet and four inches with his muscles still as tone as they were the day he was forced to retire by Ken himself. Bill stood behind him, cautiously, with an untrusting look on his face. Ken nodded to Bill, who took a seat next to Gunner, but still would not let his eyes leave the man who had once piledriven Ken's girlfriend right in front of his face. 'Well,' Harding began, 'I was hoping to come see you win back the World Heavyweight Championship. But, I can see that didn't happen.' Harding finished his phrase with a bit of a chuckle just before Ken responded aggressivly. 'Save it, Harding,' said the negected Superstar as he walked past him untaping his wrists, trying his best to hold his temper in check. 'If all you're here to do is start trouble, I suggest you leave before it finds you.' With this phrase, Moresi stands back to his feet, shadowing over Ken's former Elite running-mate. 'I'm not here to start trouble, Ken,' Ace responded in a calmer tone of voice. 'I guess what I said was a little out of line, but hell, you know I was never one for speaking softly.' As he finished unwrapping the opposite hand, Ken began taking off his boots as he looked up at the man born as Andrew Harding. 'So,' Ken responded still irritated, 'why are you here?' 'Truth is, Ken, I really did come here to see you win that World Title back,' Harding admitted. 'I can see now, though, that maybe I was brought here for some other purpose. Maybe I was brought here to help you in some way.' 'Hey, Genius,' Moresi finally chimed in, 'unless you forgot, this is the man that not only made you tap out for the first and only time in your life... but the man that retired you from the business all together. You were under his wing... what in the hell is he gonna' learn from you?' Ace simply looked up at the intimidating Moresi's face before looking back at the GW Franchise. 'This is who you got to replace me, huh,' he said, nodding his head towards Bill. 'Bill wasn't your replacment,' KPJ began, 'he was an improvement. I will never deny that you were great, Ace. But that man right there has the tools of a future champion and, unlike you, knows how to utilize them.' 'Well, that's good to hear,' said Ace. 'Maybe that means that he can bring you something that I never could.' 'Yeah,' Moresi said, 'what's that?' 'Big guy, why don't you sit and wait your turn, huh,' said Ace to Bill. 'You do realize that he could break your neck, don't you,' chimed in Gunner from her still seated position at the table. Ace turned to look at her, smiling a bit. 'I will admit I was so happy to see you two get back together, Gun-Gun. Especially after that sidelining neck injury you suffered,' pushed Harding in reference to the piledriver that she had suffered in XCWA at his hands. 'Get to the root of it, Harding, before you're no longer able to speak,' said a now standing Partridge through gritted teeth. 'Think, Ken,' he said. 'What's the one thing you have left to claim in Global. You... a former World Champion, International Champion, Television and PrimeStar Champion... really... what's left?' 'My Grand Slam,' responded Ken matter-of-factly. 'Exactly,' rang Ace with a smile. 'And what stands in the way of you acheiving that? What did I not deliver you that could have made it possible to have already attained that Title?' 'The World Tag Team Titles,' responded Ken. 'Plant a seed and it will grow,' replied Harding with a smile on his face. 'I trust you, the wrestling-genius, knows what to do now.' With those last words, Harding stepped out of the room and into the hall, where he made his way out of the arena. 'You know,' began Bill, 'as much as I've never liked that guy, he has a point, Ken. Two-on-two, you and I could claim that Tag Team Gold.' 'I trust we could, Bill,' said Ken. 'However, I would hardly want to burden you in pursuing the final crowning jewel in my quest. Especially when there's still a cornucoupia of titles for you to claim yourself.' 'I can't think of any one of them I'd rather hold right now than the Tag Team Championships,' said Moresi. 'And I can't think of anyone else I'd rather win them with than you. We can take Brick and Train. Hell, they'll end up beating themselves. Ken... we have the edge... psychologically and physically. We can wrestle circles around both of those guys. I mean, with no offense to Brick, I can out-power him easy, and Train... he's no match for you Ken.' 'He just beat me in a Triple Threat Match on Evolution, Bill,' said Ken. 'Train didn't beat you,' said Gunner, 'he beat Derek Frost.' 'That's true, Ken,' added Bill. 'We all know you've got his number.' After a bit of contemplation Ken finally nodded as he looked to Bill. 'Go request the match for Evolution, Bill,' he finally stated. 'We're gonna' do this, and we're gonna' do it right.' It's funny how life and death are used, as if they're opposites. Truly, the opposite of death isn't life, but birth. Life... has no opposite.
