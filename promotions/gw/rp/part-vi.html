Aion stands in the middle of the woods with a grin on his face. “It seems that the Deathscythe Outlaw was busy.” He noticed all the corpses of The Organization guards that Kyo killed not too long ago. But he soon sees the robot that now came in two pieces was burning. “I can now see why Mother wants you?” A cold wind blows? “There’s no of the target, Lord Aion.” The same dark figure from before appear kneeling behind Aion. Aion sighs. “I guess he got away again.” He took a close look of the destroyed robot. As he notices that robot was slashed in half by the swing of a sharp sword. “But he wasn’t alone when he fled.” “Do you want me to continue to search for him and his ally?” Aion stretches. “Not today” I’m pretty exhausted after all this was a very long and fun night we had.” He glanced over to the figure with a grin. “Wouldn’t you say, Zel?” The dark figure, Zel nods. “Yes, my Lord.” Aion walks away. “We will resume the search tonight.” “Yes?” A cold wind blew once more? Zel disappears with the wind. The night had finally ended. As it was the start of a brand new day. But still a question remains. Where was Kyo? Did that woman take him to safety? Or did she take him somewhere to execute him herself? The scene switches to a large ocean. A boat can be seen sailing. The scene changes to the boat. As Kyo was standing at the boat’s helm staring out at the ocean. He closed his eyes sensing the presence of that woman. “Where are we going?” The woman approaches Kyo. As she stand beside him. “We’re heading to an unknown island that’s outside of Japan.” “May I ask why?” She places two cigarettes in her mouth. “It’s for your own protection.” She lit them up with the use of her zippo. As she hand one of the cigarettes to Kyo. Kyo looks at her with caution. But he took the cigarette from her. As he begin to smoke. “God, I missed one of these.” He looked at her. “And I don’t need your any protection.” She chuckles. “Oh really then why couldn’t you take care of that robot?” Kyo looks away from her. As he didn’t like to be remind of being powerless. “Just who the hell are you?” “You don’t remember me?” Kyo looks puzzled. “I have a feeling that I know you but I don’t know from where.” She pouts. “I’m hurt.” She leaned over to him. “To refresh your memory?” Her lips were at his ears. “We met in a hotel room, Masked Avenger?” Kyo looks at her with surprise. “You were that assassin that was trying to kill that little girl!” He stepped back. As he remove one of his guns from the holster. And aim it at her. “Came here to kill me, huh?” She wasn’t intimidated by the sight of Kyo’s gun. As she continue to look out at sea. “On the contrary my client hired me to protect you.” “Who’s your client?” He glared at her. She closes her eyes. “You know an assassin doesn’t reveal their clients.” She opened her eyes. “Besides you’ll be meeting my client soon enough.” What did she mean? Kyo saw her point out. He looks at her with puzzlement. But he soon looked out at the sea. As he seen a large island with some kind of a grand palace that could be seen from where they were. Just exactly who was this woman’s client? “And don’t think I forgot about you, Masked Avenger.” She glared at him. “Once this job is finish then I settle the score with you.” She lightly touched her stomach. “I’ll make you pay for this mark you gave me.” As she soon walked away. Kyo watches her leave. “What should I call you?” The woman stopped. She looks at him with a grin. “You can call me Chi.” “Blood, huh?” As Kyo know what that her name meant in Japanese. “I’ll remember that.” Chi nodded. As she resume to walk away. Boat soon docked at the unknown island. The scene switches to the island grounds. As Chi escort Kyo through the island. Kyo saw that this island had tropic features. As he start to wonder more about this mysterious client. He looks puzzled from sensing a unique vibe that this island was giving off. “You’re sensing the Holy Barrier, aren’t you?” Kyo looked at Chi. “Huh?” “The owner of the palace and this island placed a strong Holy Barrier here to keep out all things that want to disturb this place.” Chi walked ahead of him. Kyo looked amazed. As he realized that a person who could a barrier like this around an entire island must be extremely powerful. He becomes more anxious on finding out the identity of Chi’s client. They soon reached the palace. The scene changes to the palace’s Main Hall. A young maid approached Chi along with Kyo. Chi looks at the maid. “Is my client here?” “Her Highness still has returned from her trip yet. She asked to me to tell you may continue to make yourself home here.” The maid bowed her head to Chi. “Just hope she isn’t trying to avoid paying me my feed.” Chi glared. The maid turns to Kyo. “Mr. Kushinaga, a room has been prepared for you.” Kyo blinks. “That’s nice and all but I must be going now.” “And where will you go?” Chi crossed her arms with her pink colored eyes locked on him. “Your secret hideaway in Arizona has been totally destroyed.” Kyo snaps his head at Chi. “When!”” “It was destroyed while you were sleeping.” “What about Sephiroth!”” As Kyo remembered that he was still there before he disappeared. He hoped that another friend was dead. The maid blinks. “Sephiroth” Do you speak of a wolf?” Kyo looks at the maid. “Yes!” “He’s safe. It seems that Sephiroth ran away from the danger and took shelter in a cave until we found him days later.” “Thank Goodness.” Kyo sighed. “Where is he?” “He’s in your room waiting for you.” Chi looks at Kyo. “A wolf for a pet. It suits you.” Kyo glares at Chi knowing that she was making fun of him. “I know you must be starving after your ordeal, Mr. Kushinaga.” Kyo places a hand on his stomach. As he looked down at it. “With all the excitement I forgot to get a bite to eat.” “A feast has been prepared for you in the dining room.” Kyo holds out his hand. “Lead the way.” The maid soon escorted Kyo to the dining room. But she stops. As she looks at Chi. “Will you be joining us, Chi?” “Sure, but I only want a sip of some red wine.” The maid nods. “Very well.” They head to the dining room. The scene switches to the large dining room. As the sound of someone chewing loud along with dishes being placed on top of each other could be heard. Chi sits at the dining table with an amazed look on her face. The maid had a smile on her face. As they were watching chomping Kyo down a lot of food without slowing down. Chi blinks. “Hungry?” Kyo eating. “What do’you’expect?” I’haven’t’had’a meal’in’a’while?” He burped. But he resumes eating along with drinking wine from the bottle. Chi closed her eyes. “This has to be one of the most disgusting things I ever seen.” She took a sip of her wine. The maid smiles. “I’m just glad that he’s enjoying his food.” “Don’t you this just a tad disgusting?” Chi looked at the maid strangely. The maid shakes her head. “High Highness said that he would be eating like this for a while he’s going through his transformation.” Chi looks puzzled. “Transformation?” She looked at Kyo. As she wondered what the maid mean by that. What kind of transformation would make him consume a lot of foods without him even getting a stomach arch? Kyo continued to eat along drink in delight. The scene changes to the inside of a room. As the room door opens? The maid held the door open. As Kyo enters the room with an apple in his hand. Can’t believe that he was still eating. “And this is your room?” Kyo sees that the room had a lot space. And it was full with elegant looking furniture. “It’s too big for my taste but I guess it will have to do for now.” He took a bite out of the apple. But he stops. He saw Sephiroth run to him. “Sephiroth!” Kyo dropped down to greet him. As he started to pet him. “If you need anything you can call me.” The maid bowed her head at him before she left the room. She closes the door behind her. He listens to Sephiroth’s knowing that he missed him. “Yeah, I missed you?” He had a look of sorrow on his face. As he starts thinking about the hell that he had to endure to get this point. Kyo soon stands up. He walked to the bed in his room. As he takes a seat at the end starting to reflect more on what happened to him. He lost some of his powers. He lost Kairi. He lost everything else that once belonged to him. And he had The Organization to thank for that. But all wasn’t a lost. He was still alive, wasn’t he? And he got back his beloved’s wedding ring that Yosho was sporting like a trophy. Also he discovered that he can do stuff without knowing about it. Still this didn’t make crack a smile. Can’t really blame him. From being trapped in a coma until the New Year to watching a friend die would shake anyone up. He goes into his shirt. And pulled out his beloved’s ring. As he look at it with sorrow. “Rain” I’m lost. I don’t know to do. I almost have no powers. I don’t have my apparentice to help me. I’m pretty much alone. I need a sign. I need to know to do what to do.” Sephiroth growls? Kyo saw that Sephiroth was pulling on his pants with his teeth. It was like Sephiroth was trying to tell him something. He looked at him with puzzlement. “What are trying to tell me?” Sephiroth soon howls? “Fight?” Kyo still looked at him with puzzlement. Sephiroth does a gesture. It looked like he nodded. “You want me to fight?” He looked at him. “But I don’t have any powers.” Sephiroth nudged him. Kyo closes his eyes. As he had a smirk on his face. “You’re right. I shouldn’t be giving up now. I still have to pay The Organization back for what they did. Also dish out some major payback Aion.” He had fire in his eyes. But he still needed to figure out how he would regain his strength along with his combat smarts. Kyo soon figured out what could help him in his goal to become strong again. He knew it was strange idea. But when he did this the first time he noticed that he got a lot stronger along with sharper. “Later that day” After Kyo hijacked the island’s boat he took a long trip to the states. Usually it would take forever for a common boat to reach the states. But it was revealed that the island’s boat was nowhere near being normal. It had been advance by some unknown technology that made it a speed demon on the sea. The scene shows that Kyo was standing tall with seriousness on his face. There was something about Kyo. Guess it would be that Kyo has shaven his beard that probably made him unrecognizable. Kyo sighs. “Didn’t think I would go back to this profession?” He started to walk forward. Kyo approaches a building. But this weren’t any building. As this was the main headquarters of Global Wrestling that was located at the place that people called Sin City’Las Vegas, Nevada. Kyo entered the headquarters. As the workers of the headquarters immediately gave him looks. But Kyo ignored their looks. And continued to make his way toward the front desk. “You know who that is” That’s the Deathscythe Outlaw Kyo Kushinaga.” “I thought he was with the 4WW?” “I thought he was dead?” Kyo heard all the workers chatter about him. As he didn’t pay them any more. He stands before the desk. As he looked at the young secretary. “Is Bubba McCoy in?” “Uh, he is” May I ask who is asking for him?” “Tell him that the Deathscythe Outlaw is here. He’ll understand.” He grinned. Why did I come back to wrestling?” I figured that this would be the best way to regain some of my combat skill? When I was in 4WW I have gotten stronger with each opponent I defeated? So why not try it here too? People wonder why I didn’t go back to 4WW? Guess I can say that I outgrown the place? I needed a new stomping ground to make my plan work? “I soon met Bubba” He was very excited to me since he has been following the Deathscythe Outlaw for some time? He has always wanted me to join his wrestling federation? And on that day I made his dream come true with the signing of a Global Wrestling contract? “I soon met the new president” Quite the looker that Global has as president? Still saw my antics in 4WW? She wasn’t really pleased on how I handled business? I have to swear that I wouldn’t be too brutal on the other wrestlers? Of course I made that promise with my fingers crossed? I mean come on I’m an Outlaw? The only orders I follow are my own? And Miss President was soon going to learn that? “The deal was made” Now I all needed to do was to wait word for a match? I wouldn’t have to wait too long? As I would be informed that I had a match for Global’s comeback PPV? A match for a shot at the Platinum Title? It’s a great opportunity for anybody? All I have to do is go through eighteen men to get the shot? “Heh, that doesn’t sound hard” Kyo has gone back to the profession of a pro-wrestler... Will his plan work? Will this upcoming match make him stronger? Or will it make him weaker? But the biggest question is will he still be able do this after being away from the ring for so long?
