Death Omen V

22 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 22, 2004 Prologue: You ever been down and couldn’t get up? Did the tears you had keep on rolling even after you’d shut them? Have you ever felt a loss so great it could suffocate you in it’s aftershock? Some say a man has the ability to do whatever he chooses. I say a man can do what he wants as long as he fears not being hurt by it. However, Jesse Jamester is afraid of being hurt by this person he has crossed paths with for the loss he may encounter will kill his heart and his soul if he loses. Jesse’s daughter is his life, and wrestling is his passion, but between the two what can one do to stop Jesse and screw his life up? Just guess… Story Continues: An hour after the phone call Jesse is seen dressed in a tuxedo and has the roses in hand as he walks down the hallway in a very uncomfortable looking way. His hair pulled up in a ponytail, his eyes moving up and down the hallways sides, as he is not in the right state of mind. He reaches the elevator and hits the down button, the doors open, and he steps in. He being the only person in the elevator he paces in a circle panicking over his daughter’s kidnap. The elevator stops and Jesse steps out walking through the lobby of the hotel and out the front doors. He turns the corner and heads down the street to a fine restaurant that is popular to the public in this part of the city. Every step Jesse is walking is different than the other and if he were to be sawn he’d look like some teenager about to meet his first date, but much older and a little less clumsy. Jesse’s eyes now stare at the ground as the streetlights above him are now on and he moves quicker at each light he reaches. As Jesse turns the corner three guys grab him and throw him into a near empty ally where only a bunch of leftovers from the restaurant are disposed in a dumpster. Jesse looks around and then turns to try and escape through the opposite way but he runs straight into a knee to the balls from a woman. The woman dressed in a red dress that stops at her mid-thigh region, and have spaghetti straps going over her soft shoulders that have a nice white complexion. She kicks him in the balls again and he finally falls from his half hunched position to his knees where he holds himself in pain. She grabs a rose from the bunch as the three men grab Jesse by the arms and hold him. Looking down at Jesse she smirks before speaking. ’I’m sorry I tricked you Jesse… I only wanted to get back at you for challenging my favorite man, Ice. It’s nothing personal… well yeah it is, but it doesn’t have to be anymore. I’m just looking out for an investment, and even though Ice can beat you all on his own, I want to make sure he does it!’ She bends over and kisses him on the cheek before slapping him just as hard as the warmth of her lips on his cheek was. ’I’m also sorry you were worried about your daughter, for I’d never kidnap someone’s child to hurt them. That’s wrong and I myself have had that unpleasant discomfort. However, tomorrow night at Evolution, I want you to take a dive… I want you to lose to Ice! I need him to win you see, because as a business woman I’ve got odds on him, and as you are his opponent I need to insure he wins. If he does, maybe I’ll cut you a break, if not, then I’m sure you’ll see me again. So what is it?’ The woman’s eyes so devilish as she sways her dark hair over her shoulder and waits for a response. ’Never! I don’t fall for anyone! You will regret ever doing this you-‘ The woman interrupts as she kicks Jesse straight in the face with her heel. She nods her head side to side in an unproven way before speaking again. ’YOU DO NOT DEFY ME! I AM NOT THE PERSON TO DEFY MR.JESSE! So, I take back everything I was sorry about and I leave you here to rot! Take care of him gentlemen, make sure he can’t wrestle tomorrow.’ The three men begin to pound Jesse down to the ground with fists of massive size. Each man near 6’2” and definitely built stand there pounding on Jesse. After knocking him down and making him bleed from the forehead, two of the men pick him up and slam him into the dumpster head first knocking him out, an then dump him into it and laugh as they make their exit. *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** Waking up near midnight, Jesse’s eyes are scrambled along with his head as far as sight goes. He grabs the edge of the dumpster and flips over it landing on the concrete below. Taking off the jacket and wiping the blood from his face he is a disaster. He lies there looking as if he were a poor man who’d been dumped from a police station because of the stench. Pulling himself up he looks down the alleyway to see the roses he’d dropped when grabbed from the street still lying there. Now only 11, for the woman who’d ordered his demise had taken one. As he walked by them he kicked them to the side and kept walking until he reached the street where he saw the streetlights and buildings. Walking down the street his eyes shifted from right to left to down and up various amounts of times. He knew he was a wreck, and though not many were on the street, Jesse felt like a clown. However he tuned it all out and kept walking in a slow way to his hotel as rain begins to shatter the ground under his feet. Never forget who those people are, Never remember who I once was, Never die without those demons in my head, Never live on without fight in the heart… Call me crazy... But does she know who I am. I’m the myth, the legend, the one and only man to walk after the elite beat me down! I can’t be bought nor controlled… This world isn’t big enough for a woman like her and a man like me… Ice will fall no matter what the problems I have to face or the mountains I have to climb. Defeat is not a word in my vocabulary come Evolution! Pity the mind that believes in me, Pity the soul that loves this face, Pity the love I once was given, Pity the man within this skin… She doesn’t know my name… She doesn’t know why I’m called a living legend… Nor does she realize what that mask can do to me; I’m a freak of nature in that thing. It’s not like a personality change; it’s a mask of freedom. It let’s me use my evil without feeling the guilt! It’s my darkness, and I’m its possessor! Remember the past I once had, Show me the glory I now have, Give me the world in my hand, Then watch me crumble it into sand… This is my choice of life. I don’t fight in that ring to lose for money, nor do I fight to win money. I fight for my existence of being someone… Just like my father was, just like I shall become, I will be the best, if it kills me in the end. Death is only another beginning for the path I will finish down the line. Pity those who trust me, Pity those who idolize this, Pity those who try to become me, Pity those who are to young to know the truth… I don’t have to fight to live I choose it… Just like Ice chose to hurt me before our match last week, I will choose to end him in the cage this week. My weakness is my strength, and my strength is my victor. So to every battle I win I get stronger, and one day that strength will guide me to the gold I must claim! Never relive the memories, Never go back to the bad habits I had, Never give in to the pressure around you, Never do it because I said to. Life goes on… Memories fade away… but legends never die in the book of wrestling. Ice will learn this all soon enough. Jesse stands there at the doors of the hotel and look up into the sky peering into the dark hole as rain keeps falling from it. Looking up a cloud passes by the moon, and the light shines down on Jesse’s face revealing even after all the punishment, a smile on his face. He closes his eyes and holds his hands up in the air as the rain soaks his white shirt he had under the tuxedo, and just stands there smiling. (The End)
