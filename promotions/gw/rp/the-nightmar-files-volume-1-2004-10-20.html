The Nightmar Files: Volume 1

20 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-20-04 LATE NIGHT Ever have a night where sleep just wasn’t an option? I mean you know that you NEED to get to sleep but for the love of God Almighty you just can’t? Imagine what our good friend and tormented soul is going through right now! Interesting introduction huh? Well this is the “Nightmare files” and in the spirit of “Hallowed Eve” I figured that I would do a little something in “Honor” of the “Day of the Dead” and also just to give you an idea on just what kind of life Joshua has WITHOUT his beloved Zack Johnson! Now it is in fact late night, and to be exact its about 1:30 in the morning when a silhouetted figure is walking around a pitch black house and brother I mean PITCH BLACK! The figure is seen feeling its way through the house almost tripping over a stool that he forgot to put away earlier and you can hear it yelling off the top of his lungs because well if no one can hear you scream they can’t criticize what you say right? ?FUCK!? Is all you hear as the figure screams in pain from hitting the stool with his leg, for the simple fact that the person has a bad knee already he just refuses to wear a brace on it. Remind me to take that piece of shit stool and sell it first thing tomorrow! Is what the figure is thinking to himself when he finally makes his way to the kitchen and turns a light on. When the light DOES come on the figure’s identity is revealed and of course it’s the Number one contender to the Cruiserweight title and newest rookie sensation in GW, Joshua L. Curtis. Ok ok so two singles wins doesn’t really constitute one as a “sensation” but think about it in this respect: How many pure rookies go undefeated in their first two real matches? Not many the last I checked and its not like he is a big guy or anything either! 6 feet even and about 190 and Joshua’s skill was good enough to impress the brass in GW to bring him up from GU in one of the quickest time spans around: about less than a WEEK! Pretty damn good huh? Well unfortunately success does have its price and lately our hero has had some problems with sleeping and if you want to know how bad try this: In 5 days prior to us getting back together he has had about’3 hours TOTAL where his head was on a pillow and his eyes shut! Now its no secret for most of us that Joshua is somewhat of a “night owl” anyways where he stays up all hours of the night and he studies opponents and styles harder and longer than most of the wrestlers that he has met in his years in the business! Joshua has been dedicated for too long to allow for distractions, either physical or emotional to the point that his boyfriend, whom returned from San Francisco just hours before can’t get through his mental barriers that he built up! Joshua went to the kitchen and got himself a glass of water and quickly drank it hoping to steady himself and get right within himself as, while taking his medicine suppresses his demons it can’t hold “him” back for too long alone, but then again that is what “he” wants! “HE” wants to make sure that Joshua is too weak to fight off his psychological advances and make Joshua do his will and the fact is that some say that “he” was born from a death that was very close to Joshua and that death was of his grandfather Curtis, whom some will tell you is why Joshua is a homosexual but truthfully that is such an asinine assumption that anyone that believes it should be smacked! As Joshua finishes his glass of water a slightly younger male walks into the room and places his hand on Joshua’s shoulder, which causes Joshua to flinch before realizing that its only Zack and that causes him to calm down almost immediately as he leans back and settles into Zack’s arms. “Baby” Zack begins “what’s wrong” You seem really jumpy lately and Juri told me that your sleeping patterns have been worsening over the past couple of days so I want to know if there is anything wrong honey?” Zack told Joshua and for only the first time ever in their relationship Joshua said nothing and I mean he was mute! Joshua released himself from Zack and went back to the bedroom to try and get some sleep because it was about 2:15 according to the kitchen clock and he felt like he HAD to TRY and get SOME sleep! Did this bother Zack? No, but only because of the fact that Joshua told him about the problem that he had before with this demon that lives within him and the fact remained that Zack cared enough about Joshua to try and help him through the problem. When Zack got back to the bedroom Joshua was out like a light and Zack had to know that that would not last long with the way things have been for Joshua the past couple of nights? Unfortunately for Zack he’d be right! ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- It’s a semi-clear day in New York City and everyone is out and having a good time as there wasn’t a cloud in the sky! It might have been a little chilly out but nothing that would worry the populace because to them everything was pretty much up to standard and nothing was out of the ordinary. The only thing that would surprise anyone is if it actually snowed while the sun was out! About a block away from the MTV studios in Times Square we find Joshua Curtis walking alone and he appears to be looking for someone but who that is the question isn’t it? “JOSHUA!” A voice cries out in happiness as they spot the slender young man now across the street from the Virgin Megastore. “Hey Brad how are things going for you?” Joshua hollers across the street at the young man that Joshua can proudly say at one time was his lover and the two had separated on good terms. “Good Josh’hold on a sec ok?” Brad said as he looked both ways and hurried across the street to meet up with Joshua. When Brad got there he was greeted by a gentle kiss from Joshua as while Joshua might’ve had Zack now Brad will always hold a special place in his heart. As the two former lovers walked down the street Joshua’s eyes started playing tricks on him’at least that is what he believed when he looked up in the sky and saw what looked to be blood coming from the heavens! Did Joshua understand it? Not quite but the fact remained that it started worrying Joshua and then when he heard someone hollering about 2 blocks around the corner in pain he really didn’t understand what was going on! As he rounded the corner he saw the person he was looking for face down but not bleeding. Joshua TRIED to get to the person but, as if just running in place he couldn’t get anywhere as he saw someone pick up the body of the man and put it in a car. Joshua tried to scream but as if someone had put a “mute” button on him nothing came out! ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- ?AHHHH!? Joshua screamed as he woke up and when he looked at the clock it was only 3:30 in the morning! Joshua blinked and looked before realizing that he was home and seeing the concerned face of his lover in Zack Johnson confirmed that. “Josh what’s wrong” I felt you punch me in the back and I knew something had to be wrong cos you never do that except when we?” Zack trailed off as he let Joshua figure out the rest. Sigh “Sorry honey but I had one of those dreams again and oddly enough I think it had to do with a friend of mine.” Joshua told Zack and he pretty much knew who “Ken Partridge Jr.”” Zack asked and Joshua put his finger on his nose to tell him that he was right. “I don’t know why I had it but its like someone told me that something was either going to or did happen to Ken’I don’t know which but if Ken went somewhere with the person I saw in the dream he must know them right?” Joshua asked Zack trying to reason within him what he had seen. “More than likely now I admit I don’t know Ken like you do but Ken wouldn’t go anywhere unless it was with someone that he knew. I think its also the fact that since you have grown close to Ken in my absence its only natural that you worry about him you know?” Ken explained to Joshua before seeing his lover exhale and lean in to him. “Thanks Zack I knew there was a reason I liked you from day one and you know something” I think this trial is going to bring us closer together.” Joshua said before kissing Zack softly on the lips and whispering “Good Night” to his eternal companion. “Good Night sweetie” Zack said in reply before laying his head back down on the pillow and slowly drifting into a deep sleep.
