Ideas and ideals of the past

6 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/6/03 There he stands. His long black hair draping over his shoulders and down his back as he looks towards the massive castle in the distance. Since the battles, the ground of the castle rarely had people walking on them, but with time that will change. The grounds of The Lair would swarm with it's warriors once again as alliances are made and souls sold. The ideas and ideals of the past meant nothing anymore. Once upon a time, history would be worshiped as it was the only thing that was taught. But now much has changed. The codes of ethics which once run through The Lair were nothing more than a mere memory to those who were around at the time. Those who had seen the battles take place for many years. those who were lucky enough to survive. Was their survival a result of there ability to protect themselves, or the result of having others do their biding for them? It was their own ability. They were the ones who had stood together on that fateful day. The day when Mephiston's army would finally come undone as The Harvesters would reunite for one last effort to take down their attackers head. Without communication, they had all gone to the castle and taken control of it. They hadn't spoken a word about it and yet they all knew that the time had come and all knew what had to be done. The legnths that they went to that day were beyond belief and the next generation of The Lair will hear the story and think it nothing more than a fabel. But those who were there know it to be true. The pain and suffering that went into protecting their world would raise the bar to another level and bring them, as a group, back together for the world to see and fear. For before this fateful night, The Harvester's had never stood together before as such a large group. It wasn't how The Harvesters worked. The Harvesters were individuals that had their own skill that was unmatched by others who tried to match them. Each a master of his own atmosphere and therefore, not needing the others to stand by their side. So when the time came and they all found themselves standing inside the enemies castle, they knew that what was about to take place would be big and would forever change the way in which The Harvesters would behave. The armies of The lair were unable to stop the attacks from the opposition, but one by one, The harvester's took them down. Took them down the same way in which that the major figures in The lair had been killed off. It had been a painfully slow process until that night. But that night would see a full powered group of Harvester unleash like never before. It would be the Night of The Harvester. The night that Salaok tortured and then killed the leader of their rivals. But that night would also forever go down in history as the day that the leader of The Lair would die. He passed on the information that he needed to the two brothers who had been choosen to lead The Lair to the next generation. It had been put into the hands of Johnathon and Salaok to rebuild the once great power that was known as The Lair. A task which they accepted with pride. And now, Salaok stands infront of the castle which his former master had occupied and ran The Lair from. Now inside that very same castle, The Harvester of Silence and the Harvester of Suffering were keeping an eye on things for the brothers. His hand pushed against the huge gate which creeks open like always. No one had ever bothered to oil the gate as it was something that their former master had always liked. Although annoying, he said it gave the place a certain character which was why it had never been fixed. Something aboutt he old squeeky iron gate looming above all as they enter the grounds was appealing. Regrowth of the once burnt grass glimmers in the low sunlight. A sign. The grounds are starting to regrow, and therefore, it was time for The Lair to do the same. Not that it hadn't already. Since that fateful day when the war ended, some of The Nevermore's finest warriors had been made into Harvesters and would carry the ideals of The Lair through to the next generation. The likes of Draven, Sage Sabbot and of course Quartz's own son Jude. Three men who were worthy of such a title and a title which would stand above all others. Yes the Lair was once again growing, but growing all too slowly. The smell of burnt wood still lingers in the air as a haunting reminder of the hell in which took place here. The sound of the window blowing through the bare trees seems to create a small screaming effect. Either that or the souls which were lost here still walk the grounds, crying out for help as their place in The Heavens would never be allowed and the worst hell that they could be sent back to was right here. Back to the grounds were they died in order to relive their pain every moment until rebirth. One hundred feet up the path, the gate slams shut behind him but Salaok pays no attention to it as he knows full well what he would see should he do so. The war had played many tricks on his mind and none more painful than a image of Grendel's head severed and stuck on top of the gate. An image which he had seen so many times until he found Grendel alive as The harvester's came together to fight that day. But the image was still there. There in the back of his mind and until he had seem Grendel in person again, he wasn't wanting to have the image replay in his mind again. Smoke billowed out of the top of the castle but from the position of it, there was no need of alarm as it was simply the smoke from a fireplace. That was one thing about living inside of a castle, the stone walls didn't do much for heating in the middle of the cold season, or in this case, the start oft he hot season either. Finally reaching the door, he raises his hand to grab the door knocker but before he can, the door swings open and a large man stands there. A man the size of a giant. Big enough to dwarf Quartz and for Salaok, that simply didn't seem possible. Salaok was a big manhimself and Quartz was near a foot taller than him. So the look of surprise on Salaok's face as this eight foot giant stood in his way with his massive arms folder across his chest looked down on him. But the big man's eyes suddenly widen and he steps aside and lowers his head. Almost like a child's responce after being embarressed, the man moves out of Salaok's way and doesn't allow his eyes to reach Salaok's stare. The big man speaks as Salaok moves into the castle foyar. 'Sorry sir. I didn't realise it was you. Flare just told me to go and get the door.' 'Where is Flare and Grendel?' Salaok reply was cold and full of business. Whether it was simply becuse he had just been dwarfed by a giant, or if he was here for important matters was still unsure. Pointing the big man speaks. 'They are in The Hall, awaiting you sir.' Salaok nods to the big man before making his way through the castle's hallway system which was confusing to say the least. And why were Grednel and Flare waiting for him in the hall? They hadn't known of his visit as Salaok always kept them a secret. But if they were in the hall, then that is where he was going. His favorite room in the entire building simply because of the history which it held. History was something that was always important to Salaok. As without the history of things, you could never learn the wrong and right ways to do something. Without having a past, how could you ever expect to change for the good? You couldn't and that is something that The newer member of The Lair would soon have to understand. New Harvesters like Sage new barely nothing about the title, except that those who have one have instant respect from those who hear the title. Being harvester wasn't just about having a title, but about being able to stand on your own against anything. About being a mster of that which your title so claims. For Salaok, he had had two harvester titles in his life. The first one was also the one which was giving to his father before him during his time in The Lair as the master's body guard. The Harvester of Pain. A title to those who not only knew how to use pain to their advantage, but how to control it, how to create it, how to find someone else's and benifit from it. And it would be this Harvester title that would lead him onto his next title which was handed down to his from his elder brother Johnathon. The Harvester of Fear. That is what Salaok had become over the years. No longer was it just the pain which he was able to control in people, but the toughts which would create their fears. So many times had he displayed this for all to see and none more so than with the life of Brick. The fear of one's family being tormented by your swron enemy is a strong one and one that Salaok found great pleasure in using against Brick. Being a Harvester was about having a title, it was about a lifestyle that no one else apart from you could ever understand. But now he would step foot into the room with two Harvesters. One of Suffering, the other of Silence. Two of his life long friends in The Lair and men in which he trusted with his life. Not always had the harvester's worked as a unit in order for the benifit of The Lair. They had always been seen as the protectors of The Lair but never had they been called upon to work together in anything more than a group of two or three. But now with The Harvesters working as a unit of ten, they were a power that none could match. But in the original days of The Harvesters, the pride in which was installed into these men was so great that battles between Harvesters would often take place. These battles were not out of hatred, but the fact that each Harvester wanted to be The Harvester to beat. The Harvester that would be given the jobs that were deemed too dangerous for others. But the thing is, there was never a job too dangerous as The Harvesters had always sworn to serve and protect The Lair at any cost. They were the inner circle of The Lair. The group which was feared by those even within The Lair and for good reason too. But now with history being just that, they stand together unitied in order to protect and rebuild The Lair. A Lair which they should have been able to protect greater during the war but with so many lines of attack, there was no way such a small inner group could protect those who needed it most. In truth, The Harvesters had felt as if they had dishonored themselves and their titles by the fact that so many had died. And on the day that their master had passed, the feeling that it was now their responcibility took over. And that is where they find themselves now. He stands facing the doors of the 'Great hall' and takes a deep breath before pressing both hands onto the wooden mass and pushes. His knuckles turn to white with the pressure of these massive heavy doors which slowly swing open and allow him to step inside. Inside the Great Hall Either side of this massive room stood statues of the warriors of The Lair. Each one just as threatening as the next in some way or form. The statues were starting to show their age now as Salaok caught a glimps of his which looked so much younger than he does today. The newest statue however looked like it had only just been set in place yesterday as no signs of wear and tear had formed. The statue of Sage Sabbot. The newest of all of The Harvesters had finally been added to the Lair's Great Hall. Salaok eyes however don't get long to overlook the statues as he normally would as he sees Grendel and Flare sitting at the very end of the Great Hall around a small meeting table. He turns and begins to walk towards it, wondering in his mind what sort of news they would have this time of the rebuilding of this once geat band of warriors. He grabs the free chair and drags it out before lowering himself down onto it. His hands, more like steel claws with the amount of rings and finger armor which cover them clasp on the table infront of him. 'Gentlemen, how are we?' Business. That is the tone which runs straight through his single word. One word which manages to tell them both of the mood in which he is on. Both men nod without speaking a word. 'So I'm going to get straight to the big question. Plain and simple, are we ready?' Grendel looks straight at Salaok with a blank expression. Not knowing if what he has to say will annoy Salaok at all but he gives the only answer in which is right for the situation. 'Well Salaok, yes and no. The rebuilding has begun and begun well and we are ready to start taking a hold on things again. However, there is word of an uprisal lead by those who once stood with us.' Salaok eyes open wide but his eyebrows instantly lower. 'Those who once stood with us? The beast known as PIT. This is his doing isn't it?' 'I don't know Salaok. My sourses haven't come up with any names yet. Only what they hear in passing without bringing attention to themselves.' Grendel's responce is fast as he tries to get the attention away from him knowing full well that Salaok isn't happy. But then Salaok slumps down into his chair and grins slightly while noding to himself. 'Let them come. Those who have turned their back on the Lair deserve what ever we give them. They want to try and take us down, then I welcome it. Have your men find out as much as possible without drawing attention. Get them inside with the traitors if possible but don't risk their lives. As long as we know roughly when, we can handle it.' Grendel nods and doesn't get to respond before Salaok talks again. 'How go the new recruits and the students?' 'Very good Salaok. Diasporo is taking them for weapons use right now in fact. Shall we go take a look?' Grendel's voice sounds more relaxed now and he indicates to Salaok as he gets up to follow him. As they begin to walk off, Flare gets up out of his seat and follows, always silent and yet always watching. Knowing full well that the students and new recruits which were now being trained for a new war would be a site to see, Salaok seems almost rushing to get their. His own students in The School of Pain were destructive enough, but those being taught here on the grounds of The Lair would be something worth a look. In the back on his mind, he knew that the time of The Lair was soon at hand again and no matter who tried to stand up against them, there would be no stopping them this time. Even if the attack came from those who had once stood beside Salaok in battle, they would still find themselves met with the same nature as those who The Harvester's had destroyed last time. If not more so.
