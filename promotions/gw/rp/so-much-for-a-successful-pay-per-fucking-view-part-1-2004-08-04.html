So much for a successful Pay-Per-Fucking-View Part 1

4 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 4th 2004 “Get up!” Constantino Zergolov stands at the door of Arefiev’s room with his arms crossed. He looks sown at the bed, with a look of impatience in his eyes. Arefiev groans and rolls onto his front. “Igor?” snaps Constantino. Another groans resounds from beneath the covers. Constantino sighs and turns away from the door. “Just because he’s a fucking star now!” mumbles Constantino as he crosses the studio plan apartment. Zergolov lifts a blue sports bag onto his shoulder and pulls open the door. “Igor” I’ve gone to training!” exclaims Constantino back into the apartment, not really trying to let his tag team partner hear him. Zergolov steps through the door and closes it behind him. There is silence in the apartment. The silence extends. Igor Arefiev stumbles out of his bedroom thirty seconds later, wearing a pair of silky boxers and nothing more. He wipes some sleep from his eyes and crosses the apartment watching for the unpacked brown boxes that line the walls. He groans as he steps into the light of the kitchen window. He turns on the cold tap and bends down to search in a brown box. He pulls out a small glass and fills it with water. “Constantino” Where are the pain killers?” His voice echoes throughout the white apartment. Silence is the only reply he receives. Igor shrugs and opens a cupboard above his head. He looks around the shelves and pulls down a small box of headache tablets. After struggling with the silver seal for a number of seconds, he finally pops two tablets into his mouth. The tablets are quickly followed by a mouthful of water. Igor places the glass onto the sink and turns back towards the leather sofa in the centre of the room. He throws himself down onto the plush cushions and lifts the newspaper, beginning to flick through the pages. Quiet engulfs the whole room, expect for the occasional rattle of newspaper being turned and groans sounding from Igor’s throat. The front door clicks open and Constantino Zergolov bounds into the kitchen without even paying attention to Igor on the sofa. Igor immediately sits up and watches Constantino walk back across the apartment. “Where are you going?” enquiries a hoarse Arefiev. Zergolov stops dead in his tracks, evidently not expecting anyone to be in the room. Constantino turns to face the voice of his best friend. “I was just on my way to training but I forgot my elbow pads,” exclaims Zergolov as he stands in the apartment stuffing two pieces of cloth into his “Vergomov” sports bag that matches his navy “Vergomov” tracksuit. Igor seems disappointed. “Thanks for waking me?” “I did” Look at the clock, it’s one! I’ve been calling you since ten but all I get is a groan and then you roll over!” Igor groans and returns to his newspaper. Constantino shrugs his shoulders and leaves the apartment. “Fucking asshole!” grumbles Constantino. Zergolov steps into the hall of the apartment building and presses the elevator button. “Who the fuck has he become” He performed well at one big Pay Per View event and now he is regarding himself as the saviour of wrestling? Yeah right? Gimme a break? He got to the semi-finals of a tournament? There were eight other people in the semi-finals? He’s isn’t anything special? Why is he treating himself as something special?” Oh so what? He went through a gauntlet of six or so people? But come on? I could have done that with my eyes closed? I really could have? It pisses me so much? We are a team, but it feels like he is the one getting the praise?” “DING!” The elevator doors open and Constantino steps into an empty lift. He presses the ground floor button and leans against the wall. “I mean” If I didn’t agree to this whole wrestling thing he wouldn’t be here either? He always needs a back-up? He wasn’t able to go for this by himself? And now that he even smells single success that’s all he wants? What ever happened to the ultimate team in the world?” What ever happened to being the pinnacle of a generation?” What ever happened to “us?””” “DING!” Zergolov reaches the ground floor. The doors slide open and Zergolov steps out, throwing the sports bag over his shoulder and across the lobby of the building. “We have a qualifier for the tag team title match on Monday and he couldn’t care less” We are a pure breed team and he doesn’t want to know? He doesn’t realise that the tag team titles are ours for the taking now that Reno has “retired?” Jamester and Roswell? The Christian Beasts? Don’t make me laugh! Train and J-Gonz? Don’t make me laugh anymore! They threw these teams together? They don’t want it to look like they are handing us the titles? But they are? And does Igor care?”” Zergolov pushes at the door of the building and out onto the street. He jumps down a couple of steps and then crosses the road into the park. “No” He doesn’t? He couldn’t care less? He is only interested in himself? He doesn’t realise that we could hold these tag titles for eternity? No, no? He shows promise in two matches in one night? He receives a little bit of praise for doing well? And now he’s Mr. big shot? Now he’s a legend? Now he doesn’t care about me? “Bye bye Constantino!” Aarghhh! Why does he always have to such a tool?” “Hey dude! There’s that guy Arefiev!” shouts a kid sitting on a park bench to the right of Constantino as he walks along the path. “What the fuck did that kid just say” Grr! Little bastard!” Zergolov throws the kid a stern look and continues walking. “I’m not Arefiev you little prick” I’m his tag team partner? The just as talented tag team partner? The tag team partner that if had of had the opportunity would have done just as well? The tag team partner that should be recognised as much as Igor is? The guy that does all the hard work for no reward? Aarghhh! I’m so fucked off with him! So much for a successful Pay-Per-Fucking-View!” Zergolov reaches the end of the park, and steps out onto the street, before turning into a coffee shop.
