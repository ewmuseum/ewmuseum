The Echoes Shatter (Ch. 2)

6 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06-06-04 Prologue. It’s natural for one to hate his self, especially after doing something he wished not to. But is it fair to one selves to keep on hating themselves after all of the boiled hate simmers down? Jesse Jamester has paid a price in life that is greater than his achievements; yet still he suffers because of that loss. His girlfriend Nikki died at the hands of paramedics, however caused by a man named Takahashi Jetsui. The rage of these two men goes deeper than the ocean and farer than the moon, but it doesn’t stop their, oh no. These men have more than the agony and victory on their mind. They want their pride to show, and they want to be recognized. That’s exactly what they’ll get too… Story Continues. ‘Hate comes in many forms… Some good, some bad, and some with a bit of both. People consider hate to be what makes people bad. It’s not the hate that makes them bad; it’s what they do with it. To a schoolboy hate can be distributed by pinching the girl he dislikes. To a wrestler it can be winning a match and making money to live off of.’ A surrounding light of orange surrounds the man as he sits on the edge of a lumpy couch staring into the fire with an expression of no worry resting on his face. The world means nothing to him anymore, and the place surrounding him describes that. A thrashed room, broke bottles lying on the floor, holes in the apartment walls, and the door holding up a ripped playboy calendar. ‘I can’t stand knowing this is my life, but I deal with it because now I can’t do anything else. Wrestling has become my world. Without that in the passenger seat I would be lonelier than a fat whore on the main street corner with only her donuts to fulfill her.’ His eyes gaze at the fire still, not even moving, showing how he’s lost in a day dream as normal. It’s been like this for over an hour, and the final log in the fireplace is finally burning out. ‘It’s not like I can’t change this… However knowing that I won’t have anything to look forward would just lead me to more dead ends. That’s another path I wish not to follow… Not now, not ever.’ Finally the log burns out and his eyes snap from the orange glow he once was looking at. Now it’s all just a pile of ashes, and he can only sit their wondering to himself and asking the same questions over and over again. Getting off the couch, he throws on an old denim jacket that has holes in it’s sleeves, and he walks out the door. As the door shuts the playboy poster falls off and the room lies motionless as if it were just trashed by a college party. *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** A reflection of life, It stings your mind and heart, Leaving you vulnerable to assault, Glimpses of life you never knew, All fade into the open wounds, Sinking in and taking coarse, Of their lethal virus of unnatural force, Until finally you pull out the blade, Slitting your wrists, Ending everyone’s… parade… ‘This poem is called Metabolic. I wrote it in my journal one day to past the time and to express how I felt about Nikki’s death. It aches me to know she’s gone 6 feet under and I had no way of stopping that. It’s the second one of my loves that’s died… and so I wrote the poem reflecting on what I felt like doing.’ Darkness is the constant vibe, Those afraid would never hide, Like a dream of endless halls, Fear lurks as the darkness calls, Games the head needs no eyes for, Opening more than just another door, One may seek light, Gauging in an unholy fight, Eyes of a crazy gaze, Filled with a dream glaze, You peer into the soulless eyes, That dark begins before it ends, A choking of guilt arises, Leaving you to realize… The dreams of that one… Whose only wish is to see agun… Blind. ‘This is another poem I wrote… The way I felt when I had lost my Global Title. It was a hard ache I could not bare with. My dreams had been shattered and my eyes seem to be that of a blind man. They knew not where to go but where the dark was, and I crawled into a corner and as I pondered over my loss. That was the last time I’d cried… Not for the loss, but because of the dreams I’d shattered without even touching.’ This is the end, Forgive me my friend, You’ve ran too far, Letting the fear hit you like a car, Turn around while you still can, Or bare with the pain man, It’s now or never… Be Dead, Or Be Severed. ‘This one goes out to Takahashi Jetsui… Let hell rest in the back of your mind, because I wish you no mercy, no prayers, and no relief on your trip their.’ Closing his blue eyes he smiles sadistically as he closes his journal. The moon shines down on him as he stands in the middle of the bridge staring down at the water. A smooth complexion of life that is not disrupted unless bothered. Oh how he wishes that was his life…
