Escape

6 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/6 In the searing heat of the Florida sun on a near empty stretch of road framed by pine trees, trailers, and rusted out farm equipment right at the exact point where the cement turns to dirt sits a rickety old house that used to belong to my mama, Miss Hattie May Jackson. Deacon’s grandma. She’s been gone for a year now but Deacon didn’t want nobody else livin in the house so he bought it. Hattie May drank and beat him but since my sister died giving birth to him and his father disappeared when he heard Deacon was on the way, she was all he had. He grew up confined to the rectangle between the front door and that shabby old gate out front, cut off from everyone else. This is where he spent the first sixteen of his eighteen years before running away. Now the place he spent the majority of his life trying to get out of is where he comes to escape. You go in, past the Bible verse on the door with the important words underlined in red ink. You go past the wood burning furnace and there he is on the bench, throwing up weights like liquor on an empty stomach, trying to make each rep touch the ceiling. This is what he does when things are bothering him, and the only thing that really bothers him is losing. He knocks out each rep faster than the last. The bar is beginning to bend from all the weight he’s got up there. I tell him he’s gonna kill himself one day working so hard. He’s been down a road that’s taken him through more tough times than a dozen people should ever have to bear and now it’s taking him to the top in GW. My nephew D, nobody deserves it more than him. Unfortunately though, he lost to Leviathan again at Wrestlecade, but this time is was for the Primestar Title. So he’s here again, workin hard to make sure it don’t happen again. Whatever he did for those two years where no one knew where he was has turned him into a nasty little hurt machine. I feel for Chaz Bryant, poor bastard has to face him on Evolution. D says he’s gonna rip his vertebrae out one by one and sell “em on Ebay, and I believe him. I’ve never seen him like this before. A lot of people say he’s too cocky for his own good, but the scary thing is, he’s every bit as good as he says he is... (OOC: Sorry so short, but if anyone actually reads this drop me a PM to let me know what you thought... I tried something new)
