Wrestling Tournament

22 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 22 Ring Ring Ring goes the purple alarm clock. Suddenly from under the bed Roswell quickly sit’s up. Roswell hits the alarm clock about 7 times to make it stop. Roswell who is in blue pajamas walks out of the room and down the spiral staircase. Roswell walks into the light yellow kitchen and opens up a cupboard. He takes out a bowl, spoon, and milk from out of the fridge. He then walks over to a different cupboard and takes out his favourite cereal Froot Loops. He walks over to the wooden table with all the stuff and sit’s down. He pours the cereal into the bowl and then the milk. He gets up from the chair and put’s the milk back into the fridge. He walks back to the table and starts gulping all the cereal down his throat. Roswell: Can’t be late for the tournament. Roswell run’s to the white dishwasher and put’s the empty bowl, and spoon into it. Roswell opens the cupboard below the dishwasher and put’s a little tablet in so it can wash the dishes. Roswell walks back up the staircase and walks into his bedroom again. Roswell walks over to a mirror and look’s into it. He pick’s up some shorts and a shirt and leaves. Roswell walks out of the house locking it and putting the key into his pocket. He walks over to a nice blue convertible and hops in it. He put’s the key into the hole and back’s out of the driveway, he starts driving down the street to where Larry’s Training Centre is. Roswell stops the car and hops out, he walks over to the door of the building and runs in. Roswell walks over to the long brown counter and gives a lady the paper. Lady: Your name is Roswell? Roswell: Yes it is. Lady: Walk over to door 5 that’s where ever body is. Roswell takes the paper from the lady and starts walking over to the brown door # 5. Roswell opens the doorknob and walks in. Roswell looks around and counts how many people will be competing today. Roswell: 6 people including me, I thought there would be more than that. Roswell walks over to the crowd of people they are looking at a piece of paper taped to the wall. On the paper it show’s who will be wrestling against who. Roswell pushes through the people and look’s at the paper. Joey Flash V.S. Cross Fang Roswell V.S. The Red Dragon Frost V.S. Fire Roswell: The Red Dragon I’m against , I don’t know who he is. My match is first. Roswell then walks over to another crowd of people, they are staring at a black leather belt in a case. It has gold stuff all over it and it says. “Ultimate Wrestling Championship.” Red Dragon V.S. Max Roswell Roswell and Dragon lock-up with Roswell kicking him in the face, Roswell picks him up and hit’s a german suplex. Dragon flips to his feet and starts punching Roswell he clotheslines Roswell making him fall to the ground. Dragon picks Roswell up and kicks him straight in the groin! He picks Roswell up again and hit’s a modified stunner on him. 1................................ 2................................ THR! NO!!! Roswell also flips to his feet and starts fighting back, he hits Dragon with a spinning heel kick which makes him do a flip. Roswell hit’s a spinning leg drop on Dragon, but doesn’t go for the pin yet. Roswell quickly brings up Dragon and hit’s him with a Vicious Spike Double Arm DDT. 1..................................... 2...................................... 3.............................. Ding Ding Ding Winner: Roswell Joey Flash V.S. Cross Fang Joey and Fang lock-up and Joey hits Fang with a stiff kick. He follows up with a flying forearm and clothesline. Joey quickly brings Fang up again and smacks him with another kick to the jaw. Joey runs up the rope and jump’s off with a Shooting Star Press known as the Flasher but Fang moves out of the way and covers. 1.............................. 2............................. THR!! No! Fang quickly bring up Joey and kicks him in the gut and hit’s a Vicious Cradle Piledriver. 1...................................... 2........................................... 3.................................... DING DING DING WINNER: CROSS FANG! Fang runs up the rope and waves to his mom, and grandma. He goes to every turnbuckle and does that. FIRE V.S. Frost Fire and Frost lock-up and Frost scoop slams Fire. He picks him up and hit’s a jawbreaker. He does a elbow drop onto Fire and a knee drop. He picks Fire up again and hit’s a stiff running kick. Frost knee’s Fire in the face and trip’s his leg making him fall. Frost picks Fire up again and takes him in a powerbomb position but drops Fire on his neck. Frost just hit his finisher The Frostbite. 1........................................... 2............................ THR!! NO! Frost looks confused and picks Fire up yet again and again knee’s him in the face. Frost hit’s a huge DDT, Frost walks over to the turnbuckle and just sit’s on it waiting for Fire to get up. Frost jumps off with a Double Axe Handle but get’s kicked in the midsecton by Fire! Fire picks Frost up and takes him over to the rope, Fire takes Frost in a DDT position and jumps off with a Tornado DDT known as The Fire Cracker. 1....................................... 2..................................... 3....................................... DING DING DING Winner: Fire Larry: Ok listen up the final 3 people left for the belt are Roswell, Cross Fang, and Fire. They will be battling in a Over The Top Rope battle to determine who is the champ. Roswell v.s. Cross Fang v.s. Fire for Ultimate Wrestling Championship. Roswell stares at Fang who is staring at Fire. Roswell quickly goes after both men and clotheslines them. Roswell picks Fang up and kicks him in the gut and also spinning heel kick’s Fire. Fang quickly get’s up and hit’s a flying forearm onto Roswell. Fire run’s after Fang and kick’s him straight in the ball’s. Fire pick’s Fang up and dropkick’s him. Roswell get’s up and power clothesline’s Fire making him do a backflip. Roswell pick’s up Fire again but get’s kicks in the face. Fire takes Roswell to the rope and hit’s The Fire Cracker. Fang run’s to Fire and Cradle Piledriver’s him. Fang pick’s Fire up and throws him over the rope and his feet touch. FIRE ELIMINATED! Roswell comes from behind Fang and hit’s a Crucifix Powerbomb so he goes limp on the mat. Roswell pick’s up Fang but get’s low blowed making him fall. Fang Pick’s Roswell up and Cradle Piledrivers him into the mat. Fang pick’s Roswell’s limp body up and dumps it over the top rope. But Roswell’s feet didn’t touch yet, Fang starts celebrating but doesn’t hear the bell. Roswell comes back over the rope and turns Fang around and Double Arm DDT’s his head straight into the mat. Roswell pick’s Fang up and Dumps him over the top rope with his feet touching. DING DING DING Winner: Roswell
