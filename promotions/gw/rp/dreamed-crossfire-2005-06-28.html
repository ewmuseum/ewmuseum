Dreamed (Crossfire)

28 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/28 'It hurts to find out that what you wanted doesn't match what you dreamed it would be.' Randy K Milholland, Something Positive Comic, 09-07-04 I pop a tape in the VCR I sit down watching my cage match against Syberus. It wasn't a match.... It was a war between us. When I climb up to the cage and seen him lying there I had one thing on my mind. Not winning the match but leaving the fans with a moment to remember. I remember muttering the words Oh Yeah as smile came across my face. Oh yeah.... I lived for the moment as I always do and instead of climbing out and winning I gave a moment to the fans that they'll never forget. They will remember not who won the Cage Match that night but who it a move that could inspire a fan to do what I do. Once The Frog Splash connected the fans went crazy.... but somehow I could not get out the door.... That's what I'll remember.... Me...... and Syberus. As I see Syberus getting his hand raise I lose my cool. I quickly eject the tape and throw it across the room busting it open. 'What is wrong with me?! This isn't what I thought it would be!' I shout to myself. I walk out of my house and sit on my front porch alone watching the kids acrossed the street play football in its purest form. No helmets, no pads, no cheerleaders, no fame, no money.Yet they'll give it a 110% every fucking time. They won't wine about not getting the ball enough like Keyshawn Johnson. They won't call there quarterback gay because there a homophobe like Terrell Owens. They won't bitch at the fans for not standing behind them like Jeremy Roenick. They don't think there bigger then the game like Kobe Bryant. They do it for the love of the game. If they dream of being an athlete one day regardless of what sport it is I'm willing to bet all that will change. Its simply about bragging rights here and that's what I want. The Mike Biddle's, Train's, Noah Hanson's and ... Syberus' of the world. A young sexy British girl walks up onto my porch and I give her a smile. She sits down next to me and rests her arm on my shoulder. 'Hey Sammers what brings you all the way over here.' I ask. 'You silly' Samantha Foxs replies. I smile as I think this woman is starting to like me or maybe I'm just now noticing it. I've know her quite awhile now and it just seems different. 'Thanks... I could use some company right now.' 'Hey, you know I'm always ready to watch football especially if its my Patriots.' she smirks. 'Come on I have a huge match coming up at Crossfire. I'm flying out later tonight the least you could do is not tease me unless you had another form in mind.' I jokingly reply as a big smile comes across my face. She now rests her head on my shoulder as we watch the kids play. The child like teasing much like how we were playing street hockey two weeks ago. My head is feeling much better after the events taken place at Wrestlecade however I have a feeling that could change after Crossfire. I've never had a problem with taking my lumps in the ring but I plan on giving more at Crossfire then receiving them. If Syberus isn't in tip top condition he's already lost as far as I'm concerned. I'm doing well in a promotion that kicked WMW's ass last year. I figure that'll be enough of an edge to put Syberus in his place. 'Ready for Crossfire?' She asks. 'I've been ready since of Ides of March.... all these matches were simply to train me. After this I'll truly focus on being the best in Global Wrestling.' Part of that was true and part of that wasn't. I have been ready for a rematch since Syberus and I last hooked up but I have been focusing on Global Wrestling. I've just haven't been doing that great. I've already managed to blow to more title shots at the TV Title so I feel like its more of the same but I know that not to be true. Nobody ever dreams of being the lovable loser but that's what I seem to be. I'll be the guy dropping the pass in the end zone when it matters most. I'll be the guy who gets beat glove side in the closing seconds of a tied hockey game. I'll be the guy running out of gas on turn four of the last lap. If that's not true just one time...... I hope its Crossfire. I hope its in Germany.... If its not I don't know if I'll ever have that bounce in my step again. I see the blue haired freak Scott Vyper and my best friend Owen walk up on the porch. Owen is holding a tape and tosses it at me. 'Watch it' Owen replies. 'What the hell did you two bring me?' I ask. 'Hopefully its lesbian porn' Samantha replies. Scott Vyper winks at her. 'Hey she said lesbian which means she doesn't want a guy as ugly as you.' I reply 'But it means she wants some and I don't see any chicks around here' Scott says with a smirk. 'True and I'm the one she can't keep her hands off of.' I say with a smile. 'Bah! Go watch the damn video!' Its Syberus!' Owen shouts. 'Sybie?' I respond. I go inside and my three buddies follow inside. I pop in the tape of the latest promo and watch intently without saying a word. Sammers, Owen, and Vype glance at me too see my reaction to his comments. Each word getting more arrogant then the last which is typical Syberus. As he finishes up I smirk and grab the tape as it ejects. I walk over to the trash can and I drop the Syberus promo in there. 'Syberus that's the worst thing I watched since Sandlot 2. Why? Predictable! You slammed my promotion, you slammed the champion, and you had the lamest plot since you talked to your conciseness.' I pause for a moment and shake my head. 'Freddy why don't you tell him how far you and Train go back.' Owen chuckles. 'Oh yeah we dominated the mean streets of Little Rock. We even put Bill Clinton in his place.... well, before he came President. Yeah, we had to dodge a few hitmen there for a while.' 'Dude, he's from my hometown Chicago. Be a smartass but get it right.' Vyper says slightly annoyed. 'That's what I said.... Chicago.... The Big Apple..... Sin City!' 'I hope you get lost in Germany and never find your way back.' Vyper says shaking his head. 'Seriously though Syberus you seemed to be shooting on everyone. So why don't I do the same?' 'Hey, you shouldn't be using industry words in promos! The older wrestler folk will be soooo mad at you!' Samantha replies with a very smartass tone. 'I'll get to Noah Hanson in a minute besides if Syberus can say jobber every damn promo I'll say shoot, mark, face, jobber, heel and whatever the hell else I want!' I'm a pissed to say the least but not at Sam but at Syberus. It may sound like I'm taking it out on her but I hope she knows better. 'Syberus get under your skin already?' She replies. 'Syberus has been under my skin pretty much from the 1st day he stepped into WMW. If I want any chance of winning I have to get it all out now.' 'Let it all out man.' Owen comments. 'Syberus..... you're an asshole. Train isn't credible? Train is a more credible of a champion then that crazy old bastard that holds your title will ever be. He'll prove it at Crossfire too!' I pause for a minute to calm down. 'Calm down Grest.' Samantha replies a bit worried about him. 'Right.... And to think he'd actually hint to himself as a God. Well that's what I got out of it anyway. Wrestling Midwest is nothing more then the minor leagues when compared to Global Wrestling and I'm adapting rather quickly to it. I don't get it something just never clicked for me since I made my return last January in AK Cirrone's promotion. However I have a feeling..... and I know I've said this before but I never believed it more.... I know Crossfire will be the start of great things.' I look down at my feet for a moment then bring it up looking at the ceiling frustrated. 'Don't worry Fredster.... things will work out.' Owen says encouraging him. 'Shut the fuck up Owen! The man has his head up again!' Scott Vyper shouts. Samantha, Owen, and myself turn to Scott Vyper quickly. 'I know that's way out of character for me but you beat me a former World Heavyweight Champion I didn't face this man. You have zero confidence. Where is the confidence I know you have? The confidence I seen when you were CWA Extreme Champion.' Scott asks. I have my eyes locked on Scott Vyper because I know he speaks the truth. 'What do you think this is about?! Not revenge! Not Ego! Not Money! Pride! Confidence! The things I've seem to have lost. I want them back!' I crash on the sofa between Samantha and Vyper. My hands covering the top of my head clinging on to my long bright red hair. I feel like I'm about to lose it but to the people around me probably think I already have. I've always been a man of emotion and now more then ever. 'You can pull it off, Fred.' Samantha says as she puts her arm around me. 'Even if you don't I'm sure you can bounce back like you always do.' Owen says. 'No.... I have to win this time.... I don't know if I'll have the drive otherwise to continue. I wonder if this dream job is what Syberus imagine it would be? I'm willing to be so just by the way he acts. Its not what I thought it would be. When I was five this would have been so much more. I was supposed to be the hero. I'd go down to the ring save the underdog from a beatdown and get cheered throughout the arena. Instead I'm the underdog and its something I've come to enjoy. Crossfire.... I'm willing to bed everyone has WMW as the underdog. Well, that is every match but one. Mine.' I refuse to let myself down this time. This time its about my pride and my confidence nothing else. There is no title on the line. I will climb that ladder and win this match regardless of what Syberus may think. I'll get his Incredible Y ring gear, I'll kick my confidence up a few notches and knock down his a few. It will be a night to remember for me. It will mark the return of 'The Extreme One' Fred Grest. I have so much to do for my profession I can't lose it all now. I feel if I lose this I don't know what future I will have in this business. What's becoming a broken dream hurts more then any chair shot.
