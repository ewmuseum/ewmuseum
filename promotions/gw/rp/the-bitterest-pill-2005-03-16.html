The Bitterest Pill

16 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/16/2005 'No... NO! Please, I don't want to!' 'But sweetie, it's for your cold. You'd like to feel better, wouldn't you?' 'But it's YUCKY!' 'It's medicine... it's not supposed to taste good, or it wouldn't work. One spoonful, and then you won't be coughing, your head will feel better, and you can swallow easier.' 'No! It's yucky! Please take it away! I don't want to! NO!!' --typical dialogue between most American mothers and their children when the child is ill and being given cold medicine March 16, 2005 1052 hours GW Training Facility *BIFF!* the instep-guard on Juan's left foot slammed into the heavy bag. Stuart noted with some surprise the flexibility of the Crimson Comet. Not many men could execute such a high roundhouse kick, even though it wasn't unheard of. Juan and Teresa seemed to have some solid training in the martial art of Judo, which aided them tremendously in their battles against larger opponents... 'but Judo is largely grasping and throwing,' thought Stuart to himself. 'Striking and kicking is mainly Kempo and Karate.' *BIFF!* an outside crescent kick rocked the bag again, from the same side. Stuart turned to glance at Jos?, who returned the glance in a way that showed he also noticed. Jos? made a small gesture to Stuart signaling that he was going to let Juan continue. Hitting the bag would get out whatever had gotten inside him. Then Jos? glanced over his left shoulder to see if Teresa had it too. Sure enough, she did. *BIPPITY BIPPITY BIPPITY BIFF!!* Teresa was working the small, lighter bag, used for improving accuracy and speed... but she was smacking it quite a bit harder than was the norm. Both Ramirez spouses had something on their mind... something that wasn't a pleasant thought. Jos? turned to Stuart again with a knowing look, which Stuart returned. Figuring out what was bothering La Herencia de Herederos wasn't difficult for their mentor and their agent. Neither Juan nor Teresa had expected for them to be in the Tag-Team Gauntlet match for the titles at High Octane... but once finding out they were in it, they had every intention of going it to the end. Due to their late seeding in the Gauntlet, they would have only had to wrestle three matches, including the match for the title. They almost made it. Almost, but not quite. Mike and Mindy Mercer had seen to that. Never mind that Brick and Train were the ones to actually win the tournament and the championship. Juan and Teresa had shaken hands with Mike and Mindy after the pinfall loss, but they returned to their locker room smarting about it. And it still smarted now. The 'pot of gold' alluded to by Stuart and Jos? in a pre-show training session was in sight... but evaded their grasp. Now, the two spouses had only sand-filled punching bags to spar... and punching bags didn't fight back. The consolation it provided was there, but it was minimal. 'Time,' called Jos?. He signaled to two young men seemingly in their early 20's; probably between 22 and 24 years of age. Then he gestured for Juan and Teresa to follow him. The Ramirez spouses couldn't avoid both giving a small smile as Jos? led them to a regulation boxing/wrestling ring. A little different than GW's famous 'Octagon,' but it would do. Juan and Teresa had to force themselves to pull their punches... soften the throws and holds. This was just a sparring session, and the two men just had basic wrestling training. They didn't have the knowledge or know-how necessary to mount a serious offense. Jos? handed Stuart a few dollar bills and leaned into his ear. 'Gatorade,' he whispered. Stuart nodded and went to get the drinks. When he returned, Jos? called time. He led his two prot’g’s and Stuart out of the workout room to a small circle of chairs in the main hallway. 'That was a good spar,' said Teresa. 'We should be ready for the rematch with the Connection now. Zack and Josh will get more of what they got the last time.' 'You won't be wrestling Zack and Josh... or at least not Zack,' pointed out Jos?. 'Josh will have a different partner now... Ami Mizuno. That's why I had you study the tapes of Ami... she will be watching Josh's back in the ring; not Zack.' Juan's brow furrowed, but then he nodded. 'Young Japanese lady--' 'Young... she's practically a kid,' laughed Stuart. 'Just turned 19 less than a week ago.' Teresa looked surprised. 'Her birthday was that recently? Juan, we've got to give her a nice present.' 'I agree,' said Juan. 'What do you think... your Estoque, or my Estrella que Dispara?' Jos? and Stuart chuckled. 'That's the spirit,' said Jos?. 'But keep in mind she's well-schooled in grappling arts. She's got a few championships under her belt. Young doesn't mean pushover... you, Teresa, of all people, should remember that.' He winked. Teresa grimaced and squeezed her husband's hand. Jos? had a penchant for connecting little things to the cantina fight that ultimately started her down the path she was on now. He didn't do it to antagonize or insult her... well... maybe it was to antagonize just a little... to get the fighting spirit burning and the fire flowing in her veins. The spirit of the Luchador. Stuart noted with some interest that Teresa always seemed to hold hands with Juan when such connections were made. The savvy agent had learned a few things about Jos? himself in the time he'd known the man... the way he mentored and trained the husband-and-wife team. It wasn't really his business to pry into, but he had eyes to see and ears to hear... he hadn't gotten to be one of the top talent agents in the business just because of his friendly demeanor and aptitude for negotiating good salaries. GW should have gotten a load of Katie O`Clare, if she hadn't been content to retire to the life of an innkeeper, wife and mother in Ireland, he thought to himself. These two Mexican spitfires were a whole other strip of road... but they had a sense of oneness about them he'd never seen before. Stuart understood that Jos? often brought up the cantina fight to set Juan and Teresa to the fighting spirit in them... and the way they always seemed to grab hold of each other's hands and draw strength from one another during those moments... Stuart made a mental note that he'd have to speak to them about it one day. 'Now let's talk about Absolutely Merciless,' said Jos? seriously. Juan glanced back up and blinked. Slowly a small smile crept across his face. 'How long have you known?' he asked. 'Ever since the double pin on both of you at High Octane,' said Jos?. 'Or, at least I suspected ever since then, and the way you've been pushing harder in training sessions since than has confirmed it.' He leaned in a little, looking closer into the eyes of his prot’g’s. 'It's more than just that you lost the shot at winning the Tag-Team titles,' he noted. 'What is it about the Mercers that makes losing to THEM more painful than losing to another tag-team, such as Brick and Train, or Ken Partridge Jr. and Bill Moresi?' Teresa smiled sardonically. 'I think you can figure it out, Jos?,' she said. 'You know how we wear our hearts on our sleeves... yes, losing to Mork and Mindy hurt--' Stuart had to laugh loudly at the remark. 'Nobody's ever called them THAT before, Teresa,' he said. 'A number of wrestlers in GW weren't even BORN when that show debuted on television.' 'We're not as young as some of the other wrestlers around here,' said Teresa. 'The show was broadcast in many countries, and don't forget that we were still living well in 1979 and the early 1980's. We had a television, an automobile, a 35mm camera... even a record player.' A few GW personnel, perhaps some of them wrestlers, paused briefly to listen at the elevated volume of the conversation. It was an accepted risk that all the wrestlers took when having little strategy pow-wows at the GW training facility. 'So you bear some animosity toward Mike and Mindy Mercer because they defeated you?' said Jos?. 'Because two cousins outwrestled husband and wife?' 'I wouldn't say animosity; no...' said Juan quickly, raising his thin, dark eyebrows. 'We don't dislike the Mercers, and we don't bear a grudge that makes us want to ambush them in a dark alley and leave them bleeding in a dumpster.' 'Then?' asked Stuart, somewhat curious now. 'They're not our enemies,' said Teresa, 'but I would say that now, they're our biggest rivals. Yes, we want to be the biggest and the best family-unit tag-team on the scene. Yes, we know we'll have to work for it, and pay our dues. I would say that Mork and Mindy are our biggest rivals, because they beat us cleanly at High Octane. Sooner or later we will have to face them again; sooner or later, we will have to have a showdown. Who is the better family-unit tag-team? I'd like it to be us... Juan and I.' 'As would I,' said Juan, again squeezing his wife's hand. 'We will have to beat them to get there.' Stuart was glad to have done his homework on Lucha Libre. Mexican wrestling ran in families... the prolific Guerrero family? being the most marked example. Every son born in the family went into the business. The strong importance of family-units in Lucha Libre families was instilled in Juan and Teresa... hadn't they said repeatedly that they were on a mission to reclaim a legacy once held by Teresa's father? Stuart made a note to himself that he would have to ask Juan about his own family one day... perhaps HIS father was in the business too... and any brothers he might have. But what greater family bond was there than husband and wife? Certainly a stronger bond than two cousins, Stuart knew. If his whole family were drowning and he could only save one of them, it would be his own wife, Margaret. Of course, one other type of family bond was likely stronger than that of two cousins.... 'You'll also have to get through Josh and Ami... The new Connection,' Stuart pointed out. 'Josh hasn't gone straight... but Ami's his sister.' 'Sister?' Teresa sounded very surprised. 'Josh doesn't look Japanese, and his name doesn't sound Japanese.' 'Both you and Juan had two Mexican parents,' Jos? quickly pointed out. 'Josh and Ami might have had one Japanese parent and one American parent, or one parent from a European country. I'm not going to speculate on it, because it's not our affair. But if you want to be the main family-unit tag-team in GW, you'll have to go through more than cousin and cousin... but brother and sister as well.' Juan and Teresa looked at each other; their brown eyes seeming to soft-focus as the seconds slowly accumulated into a full minute. Jos? and Stuart paused and wondered. The two Ramirez spouses seemed to be fretting over something. The soft-focused look between Juan and Teresa told unspoken volumes, though only the two of them shared the knowledge of what it meant in the way they looked at each other. Something else was on their minds now, though even Jos? and Stuart didn't know what it was... yet clearly, SOMETHING was amiss. 'Juan! Teresa!!' Jos? spoke and clapped his hands twice sharply. The two Ramirez spouses snapped back to attention, turning their eyes back to their mentor. 'S?, Se’or,' said Teresa quickly. 'Sorry,' added Juan. 'We understand. Connection this Tuesday... the Mercers afterward.' 'I still believe the rivalry is greater with Mike and Mindy--' Stuart and Jos? noted that Teresa was calling Mike by his actual name now-- 'simply because they've beaten us, and Josh and Ami haven't. The loss at High Octane was the bitterest pill we've had to swallow. I still believe we will have to face them down the line, to see if we can beat them, or if we still have more to learn. But Tuesday we face The Connection.' 'And we'll be ready to disconnect them,' said Juan, touching the side of his right fist against the side of Teresa's left. 'Herencia de Herederos.' 'You do know that they like to call YOU, 'La Resistance,' right?' asked Stuart. 'Huh? What is that all about?' asked Teresa. Stuart chuckled. 'I'll tell you about it later... for now, I'd suggest you tune into a certain cable channel Monday nights...'
