The Thin Line

HG Headquarters Just outside of Truth or Consequences, New Mexico Vivica Valentine fiddled idly with her new cell phone, flipping it open, staring momentarily at the pretty windmills and tulips in the background, and then shutting it again. She hadn’t quite worked up the gumption to call her tag team partner yet. After what happened at Whiplash, she wasn’t sure exactly what sort of approach she should take’what sort of approach a friend would take in this situation. Did Shadow want an ear to listen to her troubles? Did she want someone to tell her she was damn right for beating the hell out of Emoticon? Vivica probably could have done both of those, maybe even at once, but she was still so unsure. Maybe there was a little bit of guilt there too. What was it that Shadow had said at the end of her match? Vivica couldn’t remember the exact wording of it, but it had been something that was appalled and sneering’how could the crowd call Virgil and Shadow beasts after what Emoticon had done? Vivica remembered a couple of times when she herself had been rather unnerved by Shadow, and thought perhaps less-than-flattering thoughts about the inherent humanity of her tag team partner. She’d always assumed there wasn’t much of that left, but evidently, she’d been wrong, since there was at least enough left for Shadow to get offended about being labeled a beast. She snapped her phone open again, and stared blankly at the splash of red tulip. She was so absorbed in her concern that her mind only dimly registered Christian entering the room. “Is that thing not charged up yet?” he asked. Vivica started. “Huh” Oh, the phone? Yeah, it’s charged.” Christian’s arms were folded across his chest as he looked at her, concernedly. Usually, Vivica didn’t let too much zone her out. “Then how come you’re not using it” I thought you were going to call Shadow today.” Vivica glanced at him for a moment, then snapped her phone shut again. She turned slightly on the sofa to face him. “Chris?” “Yeah.” “Do you really think everything’s going to be okay” I mean, she’s probably still pretty upset after last week?” Christian chuckled slightly, running his fingers through his shock of dark hair. “She’s a big girl, Viv, she can take care of herself.” Vivica shook her head. “It’s not that, I just want to give her time to recover from” well, you know all that BS with Emoticon. She’s probably spending time with Virgil, and I’d hate to interrupt?” Vivica sounded a lot more uncertain than normal. Christian took that to mean that she was honestly concerned about Shadow’s well-being. That worried him--but he wasn’t going to let it show. He shook his head. “Nevermind that. Shadow’s a professional, and she’s not going to think that you’re being annoying if you call her about the match. Above all else, she knows she’s got a job to take care of this week with you.” Christian started out of the room without further comment, but then turned back from the doorframe to glance back at Vivica, who was still focused on him. “I’m counting on you two this week” I know you’ll do Hunting Grounds proud.” As soon as he was out of the room, Vivica made a face. But she forced herself to flip open the cell phone and dial the phone number that Dawn had given them for the main line at the McFarlane Estate. It rang once? twice? Vivica was just about to give up when someone answered the phone, rather breathlessly. “Hello?” It was an unfamiliar voice. “Um” Hi, this is Vivica Valentine.” “Hey,” broke in the little female voice on the other end of the phone, “I remember you, I met you at Crossfire. This is Alexis.” Vivica nodded. “Yeah, I remember you too” listen, is there any way I could get ahold of Shadow?” Alexis drew in a deep breath. “You know, I wish I could help you with that, but she’s not taking calls today.” “She’s not, huh” She’s up there though, right?” “Well, I guess so” I haven’t seen much of her all week, to be honest.” Vivica bit her lip. “Is Dawn in?” She hated the idea of doing a long-distance intervention, but if it would save the sanity of her tag team partner and comrade, she’d get Dawn to help her. Alexis laughed, a genuine and hearty sound. “You know how to pick “em today, Vivica. Dawn’s out getting groceries.” “Oh,” Vivica said, the tone of her voice audibly falling, “Well” let Dawn know that I called please.” “Sure. Should I tell her to call back?” There was a rustling of paper as Alexis presumably pulled out a notepad to take down the message. “Nah, don’t worry about it. I’ll call back later.” She flipped the phone shut without waiting for Alexis? answer. Now, she was worried. .:: ::: ::. McFarlane Estate East Parlor The light filled in the cracks around the big windows that were set to catch the sunrise coming in each morning. The curtains weren’t open this morning because Lia didn’t want to see the light. It reminded her too much of fire. Again, her face contorted in rage. Who in the hell did Emoticon think he was, pulling that stupid stunt with the fire? No matter how hard she dug for one, there was no excuse. If Virgil hadn’t thrown an arm up to block, then Emoticon could have killed him. The rage had been seething inside of her ever since she had stalked out of Whiplash without so much as a final word to anyone. She’d come back here, of course’and Dawn said that she’d been quite worried, what with Lia leaving without even leaving a note, or a message. What was she supposed to say? Or do? Every time she thought about why on earth Emoticon would have done what he did? she hated him more. She hated him for trying to force her hand. She hated him for trying to force her to declare her feelings for him, one way or the other’that was the first of it. Nowhere did chivalry attempt to force the lady’s hand. It was from that moment that she knew that what she had been attracted to in him was nothing more than a ruse, a veil that Emoticon put over his actions in order to win women’s trust. He was a fake, and a charlatan, and she hated him for trying to make her hurt other people. She hated him even more for selling himself out. He was a brat, that much was true, a brat for pitching a fit about Virgil showing his true knightly colors before his title match. Rumors traveled quickly backstage, and Shadow knew every last word of Emoticon’s tirade’the tirade that, in fact, had produced the street fight between Virgil and Emoticon. The street fight where Emoticon had shown his ultimate true colors. The street fight where he’d sold out what he claimed were true, and honest feelings for Shadow in order to get a cheap plug from the fans. It made her sick to think of it! There he was, hating Virgil’s guts for nothing more than Virgil being himself? hating Virgil for Virgil playing Emoticon’s role more aptly than Emoticon could have hoped to. And he hated him so much that even after all those times Shadow had sat there pouring her heart out to Emoticon? even after Shadow had spilled out her true feelings about Virgil, deeply and honestly’Emoticon had only used those words for fuel to encourage himself to take Virgil away from her? for good. It wasn’t what Emoticon did. It was more how and why he did it. Virgil was fine, if irritable? but the scars that Emoticon had put on Shadow’s heart were wounds that she would carry for years. He’d broken her trust. He’d attacked one of the people who meant the most to him, and was still trying to woo Dawn away’she knew all about him telling her he was going to go away for awhile, but it was all a see-through attempt. He wanted Dawn to come begging at his feet for him not to leave? and thankfully, Dawn had more sense than that. Lia was beginning to wonder if she was the only person around here who wasn’t playing games. Or at least, she hadn’t been. After this disaster at Whiplash, she knew that the next gambit was hers to take. The game would hold in stasis until she took it. What Emoticon perhaps did not know is when a woman is struck in the heart, she will strike back with aim true enough to make certain her enemy lies dead on the battlefield once the smoke clears. .:: ::: ::. HG Headquarters Just outside of Truth or Consequences, New Mexico Vivica paced the living room. The sunlight was still pouring into the place. She wished whoever had had it built hadn’t put in so damn many windows. They were in the middle of the desert, for Chrissakes, so why make it hotter than it already was? Half-thinking out loud, she walked over to the blinds, and absent-mindedly closed them with a twist of her wrist. The room became significantly dimmer, of course, but she could still feel the heat pouring in. For the past hour or so, she had been giving it the back-and-forth: should she call Shadow on her cell phone? Shadow hated the thing, had said she wanted to use it for emergencies only. Vivica couldn’t figure out at the moment whether or not Shadow’s current mental state constituted an emergency or not. She plopped back down on the white leather couch with a sigh, and resigned herself to calling. Nimbly, her fingers flipped through the numbers in the phone book. She took a deep breath and pressed “Send” when she got to Shadow’s name. Ring? Ring? Ring? With each successive pause after the ring, Vivica’s breath wound tighter in her chest until she felt like she was going to explode. Why the hell wasn’t she answering? Ri-- “Nazdar, you’ve reached Lia Ionescu. I am not available right now, but if you will please leave your name and number, I will call you back when I am not occupied. If it is urgent, please call the McFarlane Estate and leave a message there.” “Well I already tried that, Shady, and nobody was answering there either.” Vivica snapped the phone shut irritatedly, tossing it across the couch where it promptly wedged itself in between two cushions. .:: ::: ::. McFarlane Estate Guest Bedroom Cyrus? eyes had not been open long. It was just reaching dusk, and he often awoke before the sun had wholly gone down. She supposed that had something to do with the Otherkin blood that his kind supposedly had, but it didn’t matter much’it just meant that she needed to be down a little earlier to sit with him when he awoke. He was not a difficult patient to care for. He was well on the way to recovery’there wasn’t much more she could do for him. Still, she liked to come and talk to him for a little while after he woke up. She was in a rather dark humor today. It was almost as though the darkness gathered just under her eyes and clung to her slim form. He didn’t know quite what was wrong with her, but he had a good guess. Cyrus cleared his throat, and blinked his deep black pool-eyes twice. “Have you settled on your course of action for that Emoticon fellow?” Lia looked up. He seemed to have caught her off guard with his question. She swallowed, her eyes looking half-wild. “I’m afraid I’m at a loss.” Cyrus pushed himself to a sitting position against his pillows. He just barely fit in the long expanse of bed, at a hair over seven feet tall, and his limbs all looked angled and knobby under the covers. Cyrus was all sharp edges and dramatic, sweeping colors’his blue-tinged skin was just the most obvious evidence of that. He ran his fingers through his long black hair, his pointed elf-like ears coming into view for a moment before the loose strands fell down again. “The Cainites who have taken to paying attention to things like this will not be pleased.” Her heart sank. She hadn’t even thought of that. “Do you think that they will?” “I think nothing,” Cyrus said, in a rare moment of interruption, “I know that if swift retribution is not taken, and taken immediately, you and Virgil will have a horde of animals breathing down your neck.” She sneered. “It had best not be the Camarilla. I have no patience for that nonsense.” Cyrus snorted slightly, shaking his head. “Sect will not make a difference once they find out you’ve taken no action. Camarilla and Sabbat alike’for the Camarilla, you have broken the Masquerade, for the Sabbat, exposed a weakness to the kine. For both” well, I am infinitely surprised that there has been no Blood Hunt called.” He paused. “They know both of you are powerful. I’d warrant they know next to nothing about Soma, but they know that you and Virgil are elders. Take care of your business, and you may be able to get out of this mess with your skin.” Lia gritted her teeth, and nodded. She didn’t really intend to be much more talkative than that. The problem was already big, and it was already ugly. And she definitely intended to deal with it, but it didn’t have anything to do with sectarian divides that were just different names with similar goals among the vampiric community. This was personal. This was a vendetta. .:: ::: ::. HG Headquarters Just outside of Truth or Consequences, New Mexico Vivica paced the floor of the bedroom. Her bags were already packed, she was just waiting for Christian to come by so she could tell him where she was going. And there’d be no argument here’Vivica had a job to take care of. Christian might not have been worried, but Vivica was, and she wasn’t going to let something happen to an HG member on her watch, much less her tag team partner, and a woman who had even grown to be her friend, despite marked differences in the two of them. Christian, his eyes half-slitted with easy contentment, strolled into the bedroom with his hands in the pockets of his slim-fitting black dinner jacket. Of course, he paused when he saw Vivica’s name-brand luggage laying on the bed, packed up. His eyes widened slightly. “Viv, what the hell” You running out on me?” He said the second question with a laugh’they both knew that she wouldn’t, but the appearance at the moment was rather to the contrary. Vivica shook her head. Her blue eyes looked fraught with worry. “I’m flying to Toronto” tonight.” “Viv?” She held up a hand to stop him. “Just please don’t argue, Chris. I’ve got this feeling that I need to be there, and if there’s any time you want to treat me like a woman, do it now, and just write this off by saying I’ve got women’s intuition. Something is going on with Shadow, and I don’t care how badly she’s trying to shut me out, I’m going to go force her to let me in.” Christian shook his head. “If you say so.” He knew better than to argue with her when she was in this kind of mood. Vivica smiled at him softly. “I may not be back here before Evo?” She walked over to the bed and started shouldering her luggage. Christian, being the gentlemanly sort that he was, walked over to the bed himself and picked up the bigger bags. He paused. “I’ll hold down the fort. But Viv?” She smiled back at him from the door. “Hm?” Christian wasn’t smiling. He looked rather serious, in fact. “There’s a reason that a woman like Shadow makes sure nobody can get in contact with her. She may not have wanted us to see her in the state she’s in right now. I wouldn’t expect a cheery reception.” Vivica’s smile faded, and there, again, was that look of persistent worry. “I don’t” but she needs a friend right now. Whether she knows it or not.” .:: ::: ::. McFarlane Estate Virgil’s Room Lia sat down gingerly on the edge of the bed. She reached for the dressing on Virgil’s right arm, and he yielded it to her without so much as a glance. The rest of the bed was inundated by a swath of medical equipment, and jars filled with oddly colored mixtures. Those didn’t bother Virgil near as much as they used to. She reached for the end of the bandage and began to unwind it, looking up at his eyes as she did so. Her fingers were so practiced at this sort of thing that she didn’t have to watch every motion like a more inexperienced doctor might have. “Does it hurt as much today, love?” He shook his head. “No. It is not pleasant, but it does not hurt as much as it has for the past few days.” She unwrapped it and winced at it. Still, the skin was rather red an angry’and in places, dying. She’d never attempted a graft on dead flesh before, and didn’t know if it would take. She didn’t know how he could possibly say that it hadn’t been hurting. It obviously had. “Virgil?” she said, haltingly. His eyes were focused on her, as they were almost any time she was in the room. She sighed. “It is not looking well. You might need a graft.” He smirked slightly. More than likely, a graft would entail finding a live “donator.” The thought didn’t bother him much. Lia spread a beige-ish cream across the wound and then wrapped it tightly with two layers of cloth bandaging, securing it with a small strip of adhesive tape. She looked up at Virgil for a moment, and smiled half-heartedly. She knew that he’d sense something was wrong, but she couldn’t talk about any of this just yet. She was still too furious to put it into words. The old bandages fell into the bedside trash can with a heavy thump as she walked over to the writing table and picked up a decanter. Inside it was a liquid so warm it had begun to steam the clear crystal of its container. It was a dark crimson, which meant one thing’blood. She brought it over to the bed, and poured it into a glass. He took it from her hand. Their fingers barely touched before she pulled her hand away, and that made him frown. His arm slunk back into his own personal space as he looked down into the glass, swirling the liquid about as though he were testing its vintage. “It is vitae” drugged, but that will help you heal a little faster than your normally would. It may make you a little sleepy.” She pressed her hand gently to his and squeezed, managing only a weak smile. He was about to say something to her about it when the door to his room flew open, nearly squealing off its hinges with the force of the push. Standing in the doorway was the small form of Vivica Valentine, laden with luggage, and panting after the exertion of hauling it up all those steps to get to Virgil’s room. “Evening Virgil” Shadow?” she huffed, “Sorry about the interruption.” Shadow couldn’t think of anything to say, so she just stared, disbelievingly. .:: ::: ::. McFarlane Estate Second floor balcony Vivica Valentine stood at the edge of the stone balcony, looking out across the McFarlane property. She was much more comfortable now, dressed in a flowy end-of-summer dress that matched near-exactly the color of her blue eyes. In between her fingers, she held a champagne flute. She’d been holding it for so long that the liquid was starting to warm. “Are you sure you’re okay?” The vampiress folded her arms over her chest, and looked at Vivica with a hint of warmth, and almost amusement. It faded very quickly. “Okay” I do not believe I would quite go that far.” Vivica nodded. “Don’t blame you. I guess if somebody did something like that to Chris, I’d be pissed off too.” Lia’s jaw clenched. Vivica watched the fine line of the muscle tense on the bottom of her chin. “Pissed off is not the word for it either. He could have killed him.” “I didn’t figure Virgil would really be freaking out like he did if there weren’t something deeper to it than just being set on fire” So the legends are true?” Shadow snorted. “Nevermind the legends. What is important is that Emoticon did something that could have been very deadly, and he did it because he was angry that I did not give him what he wanted.” Vivica rolled her eyes. “I think that little bitch is just looking for trouble. First Dawn, then you” let me tell you one thing, if he ever comes barking up my tree, I’m just gonna kick the hell out of him and be done with it.” “I wish I had,” Lia said. Vivica had no trouble believing the sentiment behind those words. But there was something about Shadow’s eyes that made her look sad, so sad. She wasn’t sure what Shadow was feeling at the moment’Shadow was doing her best to block out her tag team partner, to keep her from knowing what, exactly, was going on in her head. Vivica took a step closer, laying an arm on Shadow’s forearm. She could feel the muscles tensing tight over the bones, as though Shadow’s reflex was telling her to jerk her arm away from a human touch’and perhaps it was. “You know you can talk to me.” How many times had Emoticon himself said that? “He told me I could talk to him to. I suppose that was only a convenient ruse for him to use my feelings and emotions against me to his own gain.” The vampiress pulled away from her tag team partner, and walked across the balcony to the edge of it, staring darkly into the forest herself. “I guess everybody’s got their own line,” Vivica said starkly from behind her. Shadow nodded. There was silence for a moment, and Vivica approached her tag team partner again. They leaned across the balcony together, no more than six inches apart. When Shadow turned to look at Vivica, her eyes were blazing furious green. “Why” Why does everyone have their own game? Why am I the only one who is not playing along?” Vivica shrugged. “It’s hard not to these days. I guess he felt like he had to protect himself. I guess he felt like he had to play the game to see if the numbers would turn in his favor.” “The game, the game” I am sick of the game!” Shadow snapped, throwing her hands up, “I tried my hardest to be truthful, and this is what it has gotten me.” Vivica tried to observe Shadow without getting overly upset. Despite the fact that her tag team partner’s state of mind was very much her concern, she realized that if she got overemotional, she’d lose her chance to help. Obviously, Emoticon had taken a gamble. But as Vivica looked into the eyes of the woman standing next to her, she thought to herself that he more than likely wasn’t prepared at all for the consequences of his risky move. “God help me, I hate him, Vivica,” Shadow said slowy, her eyes seething and her tone measured, “I hate him so much I can hardly stand the thought of him. He disgusts me. They want to tell me I’m inhuman, but he can try to kill Virgil, and people cheer him. Why are we the ones who get called monsters?” That was a question that Vivica couldn’t answer. .:: ::: ::.
