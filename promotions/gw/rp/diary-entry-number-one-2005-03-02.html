Diary Entry number one

2 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 2, 2005 The Global Wrestling headoffices stand in the darkness of night, misleading those who pass by. The lights in the windows of the countless offices allude to life within the walls, but once inside you will quickly discover the absence of life among the maze of rooms...Atleast most of the rooms. Deep within the core of GW headquarters rests one room occupied. A dark boiler room has become the residence of one lone man. Adam Grey, the recently hired janitor, has began spending his nights alone, sitting at a desk he's put in a corner of the bare room. We come up behind him, hunched over the desk writing away in a small book. On the desk a lamp sits, casting a small pool of white light with it's bare bulb. The room outside of the white pool holds an orange tint from the dreary utility lights reflecting off of the dust in the air and rust covered steam pipes. Silence fills the room except for the occasional psst of steam from various pipes. He finishes writing in his book and stands up. He pauses and sighs before he closes the book and walks away from the desk. His footsteps echo off of the cement walls and floor. Soon the sound of a steel door opening and closing is heard, and the echoing footsteps are gone. We move closer to the desk and look upon the single book on the desk. The cover is worn and brown, the edges worn down and torn. Opening the book, we scan through pages of hand writting. Coming upon a blank page we stop, and flip the page back to the previous one with writing on it. We begin reading it, discovering the book to be Adam's diary. January 13th, 2005 I've sat inside watching it snow for the past few hours. My apartment has already betrayed me and dropped to forty degrees, leaving me with the numbness familiar from years past. I've been sitting at the window watching the snow pile up; at first a light dusting, but now a foot of snow as accumalated. The streets are void of all life, no one dare challange nature, not today. I begin thinking through my past, through the days that were filled with the icy touch of winter. The touch that seems to be a better companion than anyone that's passed through my life. As the snow continues falling I get up from my seat at the window and stroll across the small living room. I grab my navy peacoat and put it on as I walk out the door. While I'm putting my hat and gloves on all I can think of is that piercing bitterness against my face: The ice crystals forming on my face from the frozen wind. I begin walking faster down the steps towards the outside door. My mind longs for that comforting touch of winter, like a herion addict longs for his next fix. The numbness of the winter has become my escape from life. As I walk down the sidewalk, snow blowing in my eyes, my mind begins finding solace. My feet and hands begin hurting, I want to warm them but I know after the pain comes the numbness, and that's really what I'm searching for, and when I find that everything will be all right. That's how I feel when I'm with her, lady winter. How alluring she looks on the coldest of days. The ability to freeze life, love, hope, faith, and dreams within the blink of an eye.. how powerful and painfully erotic. Somehow I know everything will be all right with her. She makes the pain dissapear, she makes the hurt dissolve. So many times I've found her to be the only one for me. She leads me past the roads of sorrow down towards the valleys of personal satisfaction. As we hasten past the pain together, we somehow find acceptance in ourselves. If it weren't for her, perhaps the pain would never seem to die. I continue walking, passing empty storefront after empty store front. She has taken over the streets, tearing them away from the people of the town. They have all boarded themselves away from her icy embrace. Walking out here by myself I feel like I'm the only one alive, that the world is mine. The desolate white landscape offers its acquaintance to me, to be my security blanket. I take its offer, breathing in the bitter air, feeling the burn in my lungs. Out here the world is beautiful, pure, and perfect. The untouched snow drifts, the roads absent of the dirty grey that accompanies exhaust vumes. At this moment the world regains her virginity , and I'm the only one she allows to take it. The only one whom she allows to wander on her frozen skin. I pause for a moment, realizing I'm in the middle of the park. The snow comes down around me, blinding my vision with it's soft touch. I raise my head and let the snow rest on my frozen skin. My eyes water and burn from the cold and my nose is numb, but this is it. This is her pruity at it's fullest; her cold purity. My mind is cleansed of all problems, the world has frozen in time and drifted away. Thoughts of Catherine are frozen and erased. My mistress has taken me into ectasy once again. Her touch offers thoughts of splendor and serenity. Eagerly I grab them; a child with his hand outreached to a hot chocolate chip cookie. My mind slowly flows through time. In my thoughts appear the lives of thousands who have witnessed this spendor before me. I race through the moutains; standing atop of a frozen peak I can see for miles in every direction. All I see is the frozen landscape, laid out perfectly before me. She takes me away, she shows me the world where she was queen, where the land was entirely hers. My mind races again, she pulls my thoughts back to the present, only the light has dissapeared around me. All I can see now is the round orange blur of the street lights. How long have I been with her? It never seems to matter, time stops when I embrace her touch. The snow still falls, but my skin has become numb to the touch of her tiny fingers, so all I can do is watch as they tumble down to join the rest on the ground. I look around and smile, appreciating every moment she holds me in her cold bosom. I turn around in my spot and begin walking home. She guides my legs back towards my aparment, allowing my mind to wander. I wonder what there is in store for me, wonder what my future will hold. Will I be able to survive. My mind begins worrying: worrying about Catherine, worrying about my spot in the world... even though I know there's little to do about either. Soon I begin to feel her icy fingers resting on my cheeks, telling me it'll be all right.. Because that's what her numbness does, it makes everything feel all right. We close the diary and back up, leaving the boiler room and the GW headquarters. Pulling back further and further, leaving the building with it's empty eyes amongst the dark veil of the still night.
