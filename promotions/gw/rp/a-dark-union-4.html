Silence I sit there in silence in the room looking at the man that John called Robert. The blood spews from his neck. I begin to circle the man. He tries to watch me but also tries to stop the bleeding. “Its an amazing thing blood is’The source of humanity”.and for some the source of immortality. For you it’s the very fiber of your being, to me its just a late night snack.” “What are you?” He says spitting out blood as he speaks. “As you most eloquently put it I’m a lowly nigger. People should not associate with me, but not because of the color of my skin you see”..” I open my coat fully showing the large tattoo of the dragon on my chest. He looks at it becoming entranced. ?”.but because I am a killer. Not your average killer but I’ll eat your flesh, drink your blood, and steal your soul. I am a monster unlike any other and my power grows every second.” “Please do it quickly??The man speaks as he begins to choke on his own blood. I watch the man as he chokes and finally dies. I walk over to his body and remove the knife from his neck. I take his head in one hand and rip it off and sit it on the floor. “To feed or not to feed that is the question. Seeing that I have done my feeding of what sustains me I choose not to feed this time. But lets get a little interesting shall we. This place could use a new paint job. How much blood does it take to cover a room?” I begin to dismember the body in the most gruesome fashion, body parts being flung everywhere. The blood falls like a light mist in the middle of summer. I let the blood fall on me as if cleansing my very pores. As Elizabeth Bathory thought it would give her eternal beauty to bathe in blood I took take in the think red liquid as a fountain of youth. In a twisted almost orgasmic feeling I stand there rubbing the blood all over my face. The overwhelming feeling begins to fade and I look at the room. I smile at the accomplishment and walk outside. Zero and Holmes stand there looking at my in a calm horror they look at my face covered in blood. “What the fuck?” Holmes says trying to keep his calmness. “Do you really wanna know?” I ask. “Hell no”.” “Shall we leave now?” Zero says quietly. “You two go ahead. I think I have shown my worth now. And now I must go find someone. I’ll be in touch.” I say with the sadistic smile on my face. “Fine with me. You’re one sick motha fucker”..glad to know your on our side? Holmes says starting to walk off. “That indeed.” Zero says. Both men walk off and I leave the Zero estate the same way I entered it in darkness and Silence. I begin to walk off toward the distant lights of a city. To go there where all that I need lies. Power, money, and millions to feed on. On this night a dark union was made. Leinad Soma truly showing his worth to TUE. The nights have just gotten more interesting. Men like Zero and Holmes have no clue what’s in store for them now that I am aligned with them. They will see things they thought never existed. And it will be an interesting time for all. I walk into the darkness?.in silence.
