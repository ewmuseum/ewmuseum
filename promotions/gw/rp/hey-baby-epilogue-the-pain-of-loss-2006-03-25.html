Hey Baby: Epilogue: The Pain of Loss

25 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03.25.2006 ---- THE PRESENT- Friday, March 17, 2006 2307 hours O’Malleys on Fifth, Chicago, IL ---- Jackson Namenlos-Duisternis sat in front of the laptop, the blue glow from the screen lighting his features with a less than flattering glow. A bottle of beer sat on the table in front of him, condensation pooling on the stained tabletop while his fingers struck the keys with angry precision. The phone call to his lawyer had not gone well. Kitty, the woman he refused to consider as his soon to be ex-wife, was trying like hell to force Global to terminate the three-year contract he’d just negotiated. She was trying to have him banned from the arena, so that she could make her own comeback. The entire situation, for all its drama, was one shade away from being absolutely ludicrous. “Like she has a chance in the ring,” he muttered, “Who’s she gonna fight?” For a moment, he almost cared about her safety and wellbeing, and then the reality of the situation crashed down around his ears, making him sigh as he picked up the beer and took a generous swallow of the warmish brew. He was well on his way to getting drunk, and right now, he didn’t give a rat’s ass about training regime, or his carbohydrate intake. “Fuck it.” he snapped as he resumed his typing, fingers all but flying over the keys now as a small part of his mind wandered over the past, and the last time he’d seen Kitty. ---- FLASHBACK- Monday, August 15, 2005 2307 hours …somewhere in New Mexico ---- She sat alone in the dark, feeling the press of the humid air, smelling the heady scent of roses. One hand found its way to her mouth, and she began gnawing on her already ragged thumbnail. This was stupid, probably even monumentally so, but she knew that Gabriel wouldn’t know about this place. She just hoped, as she sat there in the dark, that Brad did. It had been the first place to cross her mind; really, it had been the first place she’d realized her feelings for the man who lately she’d come to hate as much as she’d loved… ---- FLASHBACK- Thursday, August 14, 2003 1313 hours ---- The desert air was arid, the merciless sun beating down upon the parched, sandy earth with relentless force. The sky was clear and cloudless, impossibly blue. Inside the greenhouse, the air was humid, thick enough to drown in. Brad smiled as he stood in the doorway, ushering Kitty inside. Averting his eyes, he fiddled with the door as Kitty peered at the brightly lit interior. The scent of roses filled the heavy air, choking in its intensity; rows upon rows of the sweet smelling bushes filled the greenhouse, a rainbow of petals littered the floor. Brad took her hand, pulling her deeper into the maze of bushes, his voice filled with a hushed urgency. “I want to show you something…” Here, in the middle of the greenhouse, was an open area, with only one plant resting in the dead center. The air here was barely breathable, each breath a choking gasp, having to be bitten and chewed like a fatty, grisly hunk of meat. Reverently, he released Kitty’s hand, crouching beside the plant, cradling one of the delicate flowers in his hand. The petals were exquisite, yet unsettlingly odd. The rose itself was a deep indigo with hints and striations of a rich pink. A black rose, with pink streaks. Kitty bent to inspect the flower, drawing its heavenly scent into her lungs. After a moment, she looked up at Brad, waiting for his explanation. She could read his expression and could see the weight of the words he so desperately wanted to say. She knew, even after such a short time that he’d wanted to say something all morning but he seemed afraid. A smile graced her lips as she peered at him. Finally, he broke eye contact, looking back down at the delicate petals of the exquisitely formed flower, speaking absently. “I called this variety Kaitlynn… but its loveliness pales in your presence. It was no easy task… the black rose is a rare breed indeed… but Kaitlynn here is one of a kind, much like you. Do you have any idea how special you are?” His words were faltering, his voice trembling as they fell from his lips, filling the silence. He looked into her eyes, his filled with the power of his emotions, startlingly violet. She shook her head, looking embarrassed. “No… I’m not.” He stood back up, in one fluid motion he was beside her, towering over her, his expression deadly serious. Gazes locked for a long moment, and then he turned his back, cursing softly, his left hand absently stealing towards the black elastic band around his wrist. The only sound was his heavy breathing, deafening to him and then Kitty reached out, touching his shoulder, her fingers brushing lightly against his worn cotton t-shirt. She could feel the dampness of his sweat on the thin fabric, but beneath that she could feel the muscles bunching under her hand as he tensed. Otherwise he remained perfectly still. “Brad, what is it…? There’s something you’re not telling me…” He was trembling now, but why, Kitty was uncertain. He jerked away from her touch, his breathing ragged as he raked a hand through his hair. Suddenly he turned towards her, his eyes blazing in his pallid face. Kitty shrank away from the fierce intensity of his gaze, but he seized her arm before she could turn away. His grip was like iron as his eyes burn into her, prying into her deepest recesses, staring into her soul. He spoke, and his voice sounds oddly choked, as though he is about to fall apart in the face of some intense emotion. Perhaps that was the truth, after all. She flinched, as though expecting a blow; he seemed so volatile then, so angry. “Listen to me, Kitty. You’re amazing… the things you make me feel… I… I’ve never felt before. I believed myself to be cold and remote… to be incapable of love… yet here I stand, evidence to the contrary. You deserve someone who will cherish every moment in your presence… not some foolish, bumbling child. You deserve someone who will treat you like the goddess you are… you-” She opened her mouth to protest, shaking her head, “Brad, I-” He pressed a finger against her lips, silencing her protest, continuing his confession. The flood gates had been opened; there was no going back now, he was ready to tip his hand, lay all the cards out on the table and tell her everything. “Please, let me say this. I… need you to hear this. Kitty… I’ve been yours from the moment I saw you, sitting there on my stool in that seedy bar. I’m willing to forgive your dalliance with Croix… we both made mistakes… I’ll be the first to admit it. I hesitated, and the moment was gone. Please, Kitty… give me another chance… I want to be in your life… as more than an outsider looking in. You don’t have to answer me now… think about it… and Kaitlynn…” His voice dropped to a whisper, one that Kitty couldn’t hear as he turned away. “I love you… more than you’ll ever know.” The exhaust fans kicked on at that exact moment, drowning out his words. “What? I can’t hear you…” She reached out, her face a mask of misery, and she touched his hand, entwining her fingers in his. He took a shuddering breath, and then whirled around, his eyes glimmering with unshed tears, his features stony with anger as he wrenched his hand from her grip. He glowered at her, but this time she doesn’t even flinch, meeting his gaze with her cool green eyes. “Brad, you know we can’t do this… not right now… the timing is all wrong.” “Are you sure about that, princess? You don’t sound convincing at all.” She snapped at him, her eyes flashing with ire. “So what? All you care about is… is your own personal gain! To you this is all a game, with me as the prize… both you and him, fighting over me… it’s so stupid! I feel like a fucking piece of meat!” He sighed, the anger running out of his body, leaving him drained. “You have no idea how wrong you are… this isn’t a game to me… it never was. This is about matters of the heart… and it’s time for some truth between us, here and now.” He closed the distance, wrapping his arm around her waist, bringing her close. He could see the beads of sweat on her forehead, and trickling down her chest as he looked down at her. His voice was soft, yet compelling as he spoke again, still looking deep into her eyes. “Tell me what you feel… here…” His other hand touched her chest, softly, feeling the racing of her pulse there. Kitty sighed, trying to pull of out his grip, but he only pulled her closer, both hands gripping her slim waist, his grip like immovable iron. He bent his head, kissing the hollow where her neck met her shoulder, then moving down to her collarbone. Kitty’s head fell back, her eyes closing as a soft gasp escaped her lips. If his hands hadn’t been there to support her, she would have fallen. He kissed her softly, his breath oddly cool against her feverish skin, moving towards her ear. One hand roams up her back, pulling the elastic from her messy ponytail, and plunging his hand into her soft hair. Finally he whispered his secret in her ear, lost in the scent of her skin, oblivious now to the fear of rejection. “…I love you…” ---- FLASHBACK- Monday, August 15, 2005 2355 hours …somewhere in New Mexico ---- Like a slap in the face, she returned to reality as those same fans kicked on overhead, the sound almost ominous as it rumbled through the rafters. She touched the ring on her finger with the tip of her thumb, turning it slightly on a finger that was too small for the ring now. An unflattering sneer curved her lips as she looked down at the luminous face of her watch. She’d wait five more minutes, and then it was over. Really and truly over. She watched as another minute passed, and the fans silenced themselves, letting that unsettling hush fall over her again. The silence was oppressive and she was beginning to think that maybe this wasn’t a good idea after all. Still, something kept here there, standing among the thorns. “Maybe,” she murmured, “maybe I still have some feelings for him after all.” Pain throbbed in at least half a dozen places as she shifted again, trying to keep her legs loose. She pressed her hands into the small of her back, a gesture left over from pregnancy and stretched her spine, listening to the wind outside, hearing the whisper of the dust scraping across the window. It sounded like voices, whispering too low to hear. She shuddered suddenly, looking over her shoulder as goose bumps rose on her arms. She wrapped her arms around her middle, hugging herself, suddenly chilled. With a sound of irritation, she crossed to the window, peering up at the night sky, hidden by the reflective tint of the glass. Stars dappled the indigo sky- indigo, deep blue just like Brad’s eyes. She found herself smiling, but the smile faded as she looked at the moon where it hung in the sky, bloated and diseased looking, deep orange, with a jaundiced glow around it. Haze on the moon, a bad omen- very bad. She could see a plume of dust on the horizon, and somehow knew that it was him. That thought brought a heavy feeling into the pit of her stomach, making her feel ill. Suddenly she wanted to leave, this was a bad idea after all. After all the months of crying out in the dark, begging for him to come save her, she’d realized that he wasn’t coming. More so, she’d realized that she didn’t need him. She looked at the approaching headlights, tears blurring them into indistinguishable blobs as she pulled the wedding band from her finger, letting it drop to the soft ground. The door was right there, but she didn’t go to it as the car fishtailed across the dust, coming to a stop before the greenhouse. Jackson was out of the car in seconds, already loping towards the door. She put her hand to her mouth, stifling a sob as she retreated back into the darkness. “Kitty?” Nothing but silence answered him as he stepped across the threshold, his hands by his sides, ready to draw his guns if necessary. “Baby? Are you here?” His boots crunched on the gravel walkway, crushing the fallen petals, making the scent of roses more oppressive as he skimmed the perimeter of the building. He’d locked the door behind him, but she didn’t know that. Something glimmered beside him, and he stopped to pick it up, recognizing the wedding band at once. Something hit him hard in the chest, leaving him gasping. For a moment, he wasn’t sure what it was, and then saw it for what it was. Grief. “Baby?” He caught a glimpse of something in the shadows, and moved to intercept, instead finding nothing but the Kaitlynn rosebush, moving ever so slightly. Someone had just passed by here. He threw his head back and bellowed, the sound more sorrow than anger. “KITTY! WHERE ARE YOU?” She grasped the handle, twisting it only to find that it wouldn’t budge. “No,” she sobbed, drawing his attention as she drew back her leg and kicked it hard, rattling the frame. “Kitty!” His face was radiant with a happy grin as he turned, spotting her at once. Somehow she managed to tear the door open, and burst out into the night, leaving him behind. “Wait! Goddamn it! Wait!” He wailed as he reached the door himself, wrenching it open so hard he tore it right off the hinges. He could see her by the light of the feeble moon; saw her loping across the compound like a wounded animal. She rounded the house, and was gone before he got there. He spun around, driving his fist into the concrete wall, hard. A sickening crunch followed as his knuckles cracked, nearly breaking. A hiss of pain escaped through his clenched teeth, and then all was silent. He let his head fall forward, resting his forehead against the coolness of the wall, allowing his shoulders to sag as he breathed deeply. Red spots danced before his eyes, and then someone dared to touch his shoulder. With a primal roar, he whirled around, seizing his assailant, pinning them to the wall. His free hand swung back, and then his eyes widened as he realized that it’s Kitty that he holds captive. His fist smashed into the wall beside her face, and Kitty winced as she heard the sickening sound of breaking bones. His eyes narrowed, the pain lost in a cloud of rage as he leaned in close, viciously spitting his words in Kitty’s face. “What the fuck do you want from me? I came as fast as I could. I tried to find you for months! It wasn’t fucking easy, you know… Gabriel was moving you all over the goddamn country. I… fuck! I’m sorry, Kitty. What do you want me to say?” She said nothing, simply looking at him with wide eyes. “You want to leave me, after all this? Is that what you want?” She still said nothing, tears falling from her eyes now, trailing down her cheeks. He released his hold, backing away, absently rubbing his broken hand. He stared at Kitty, noting the red marks around her neck, and the smile that crept across his features was cold and sickening. He spoke again, his voice filled with disdain as he held up the wedding ring. “What the fuck is this all about? Huh? I suppose you’re gonna tell me you just dropped it by accident, right? You want out of this, is that what you want? Good, I don’t fucking care anymore.” He reached inside his jacket, withdrawing his lighter and cigarettes with absent motions. As soon as he had one lit, he started in again, venting everything, all the pain, all the anger. “Open your fucking mouth, woman. Tell me what the hell you want!” Kitty’s hand lashed out, slapping him hard across the face. She snarled a reply through clenched teeth, her hand numb from the force of the blow. “Fuck you… you bastard.” He chuckled humorlessly. “No thanks… been there… done that. Made that mistake one too many time, I think.” The venom in the words cut her to the quick. She paled at the comment, tears still swimming in her eyes, but she forced them back, poking a finger at Brad’s chest. “You left me with that monster.” Her chin trembled as she continued, building up the courage. “You’re an asshole! I thought you loved me, you worthless piece of shit! You never… neh-never fucking cared about anything… duh-did you?!” She punched him hard in the chest, driving him back against the wall, pummeling him as the words came out. “Eight months. Eight fucking months of hell. Don’t tell me you couldn’t find me. Gabriel turned on the television for me. I saw you on Impact. I saw you on Wreckage! Wrestling. That’s what you care about! Your stupid fucking career. I was just an inconvenience, wasn’t I?” Brad grabbed her shoulders, his face filled with rage, nostrils flaring as his breathing grew ragged, his own eyes filling with tears. A single tear coursed down Kitty's cheek, and she whispered the last words, her voice filled with misery. Although her voice was low, Brad was able to hear every word, and it cut him to the core, dissolving his anger as the reality of loss filtered through the haze of red. “I hate you, Bradley Jackson…” A strangled sob escaped her trembling lips, and she began to cry in earnest, still pinned against the wall by Jackson’s hands. He glared at her, and then his eyes softened, his face taking on the expression of a whipped puppy. He stumbled back a step, his mangled hand moving towards her face, the pain forgotten. “No. Don’t say that, please don’t. We… we can work this out, Kitty. I…” “Shut up.” She snapped as she stormed over to the car. “You can take me back to Chicago, let me get my things, and then I’m gone.” She pulled the door open and slid into the passenger seat before slamming it hard enough to rock the car. “I love you,” he muttered, swiping the back of his hands across his eyes to banish the tears. It couldn’t end like this. Not after the lengths he’d gone to, only to get her back. ---- …as seen on [detail withheld], an open letter to Global management… ---- To Whom It May Concern: Anyone who knows me well knows that I’m not a blogger. I never jumped on that bandwagon, I never signed up for chat programs and the like. When ICQ was all the rage in 1997, you wouldn’t catch me logging on for a little chat session. That was simply never my style. The only reason I have an AOL account is because it was part of a past contract. Recently Myspace has become all the rage, with everyone from rappers to high school kids participating in this craze. I am not going to comment on the juvenile situation, or how it’s escalated. That is neither the purpose, nor the intention of this letter. The reason I’m writing this is because I can only hold my peace so long. It’s either putting these words on a page or exploding. To steal a line from an old television show: 'You won’t like me when I’m angry'. First and foremost, I would like to address the current situation, in relevance to how it has impacted me. Fuck Kyo Kushinaga, frankly I don’t give a shit about how slighted that little Jap feels. I did what I had to do at High Octane. I will not justify my actions, nor will I honor any of his whiny comments with any of my own. He does not deserve a rematch any more than he deserves more push than he’s already seen. Over two months ago, I came back to this place, with the idea that I was to regain my rightful place. Some of you may recall that when I left, I was ranked for the World Heavyweight title, along with Christian Zenith. That was a promise made by former bookers, backed up in writing. The contract was legally binding, or so I thought. Apparently appearances can be deceiving, or maybe I’m just a victim of the same machinations that have screwed over others in this place. Instead, I was forced into a four-way match for the Platinum title. That, however, is not my point of contention. I appreciate that, as well as the respect you’ve shown me in allowing me to return to this company after such a long absence. What I am referring to is the current situation with another member of your staff. The woman who wants make my life a living hell, my wife, Kaitlynn McIntyre-Jackson, or Kitty Mac as she is billed. She wants my contract to be terminated so that she can return to the octagon. That’s not my problem. That’s your problem, as management you are required to manage your talent. That being said, I refuse to work under these conditions. As such, I am appealing to whomever is in charge this week, tongue planted firmly in cheek of course, to make right on this situation. I want, hell, I deserve a shot at Eryc Morgan, one-on-one. I was, after all, the man responsible for his rather questionable win at High Octane. I’m not one to stir the pot, as you well know. My tenure with Global has been exemplary, as has my past employment with the WCWF, barring one drug related incident that was subsequently dropped. I have attended every event I was scheduled for and have worked my ass off for this company. All I am asking for in return is fulfillment of a simple promise. I’m not asking for a raise, nor am I demanding changes to my existing contact. I am simply requesting that you honor my request for a Platinum Championship Match, as well as consideration to the dropping of Kitty Mac’s contract. I trust you will do what’s right for the company and meet with me personally to discuss this situation to avoid further escalation. Until then, I remain. Respectfully yours, B. Jackson
