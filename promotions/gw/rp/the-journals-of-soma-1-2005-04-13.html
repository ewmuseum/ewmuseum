The journals of Soma#1

13 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/13/05 It was like ordinary nights for her. As soon as the sun went down, she became as fine as a moon goddess. Her presence strong and defined. It would be hard for the males that she attracted not to know what she wanted. She was a raver, it was apparent in the clothing she chose, but many of her kind chose to portray their lives as such, but this the genuine article. The peircings. The black clothes. The make-up. She knew the streets and the streets knew her. Tonight she would go clubbing and tonight she would meet a man who would change her life. She walked to the club. The club name... Mirage. And that is what she was. A living mirage. Her walk flowed as if walking on air. Her long black hair covering most of her face, the other half showing a beautifully pale, crimson eyed woman. The bouncers, one too large to do any good and the other a little too inexperienced to know what to do if something were to happen, watched as she pulled a couple of twenty dollar bills from the bustier under her leather trench and handed it to one of them. She gave them a curt smile before walking in. The club was jam packed with potential. Everywhere one could look there were men and women dancing to the sounds of the local DJ.The club itself had an intense environment. It being the type of environment she craved for. The type of environment she lusted after. It was obvious to anyone who met her that she was the type that went out looking for trouble. She was always in the right place at the right time and things just seem to happen. With the music blaring above the norm, and the way her dance seemed almost hypnotic, it wasn't long before men started trying to see if they could be her next play thing. Many of the guys tried to dance with her, and almost everyone of them got a harsh no, or a smile then the brush off. As she danced on she began to think there would be no meal tonight. She would find no pleasure in whomever she ended up killing. She almost thought the night a waste until she saw him. He didnt look like most men. Hell, there could be no other man that could compare. He was standing, watching from the second level, it almost seemed as if he had already been watching her. The man stood about at least 6'5 or 6'6, his skin the color of a fine chocolate, blood red hair that was about shoulder length, and under the suit one could tell he was muscled rather well. As she began to wonder about this man, his eyes locked with hers. For a brief moment, she thought she recognized who he was before shaking off the strange feeling. She kept dancing, hoping she would attract his attention. And it was his attention she caught. He watched her for a while before walking down to the dance floor. It was beautiful and scary to watch him move through a crowd. Every second seemed to move twice as fast with him, and he seemed to be in front of her within moments. It startled her for he could move faster than she ever could. Then she realized, his eyes were the same crimson color as her own. Could this man, this silent creature of the night be like her? Nonsense. There was no other like her. She was the only one of her kind she had ever met. Vampires come and go everyday, but none like her. Her power seemed stronger like it had been there for longer than she could remember. She could remember no sire. She could remember nobody ever teaching her the ways. She just always knew. Now standing in front of her was this man who, at the time still said nothing. 'Care for drink?'The man asked. 'Sure. A bloody mary please.' The man walked over to one of the bartenders who quickly made the drink,almost shivering handing it to this mysterious man. The more she watched him the more lust started to take over. She had found her prey for the night and it would be an interesting one. 'So do you come here often?' 'This dump? Only on days where I can't get into a good club.' 'I'm sorry to hear that.' 'Why?' 'Because I own this establishment.' If she didn't do this often it would have shocked her. Sticking her foot in her mouth while trying to catch a meal. Instead of blushing, she gave an apologetic smile. 'I'm sorry. I wasn't aware.' 'That is fine. I like honesty. It lets me know what I need to change.' 'I'm sorry. Where are my manners the name is....' 'Keira. Keira de Vries. Right?' This time she could stop herself from looking shocked. He knew her name and she didn't know who he was. Was he a vampire hunter? Was he part of the police force and wanted to bring her in for questioning? 'Yes, thats my name. And you are...' 'My name is Leinad Soma. You don't remember me at all do you? 'I'm sorry but I do not.' He smiled, nonchalantly showing his fangs, before looking her straight in the eyes. 'Come with me Keira and I shall show you things. You aren't who you think you are. Follow me.' It frightened and intrigued her. She followed behind him slowly, watching his every move, making sure he did not try anything that would harm her. They walked out of the club and Soma turned and faced her. 'You live close am I right?' Soma asked. 'You sound like you already know the answer to this so why play games?' 'Games teach. Games make people understand. Games show a persons true nature. You of all should know this. She soon became silent, realizing she too plays games all the time. She has to play them to get her meals. She walked beside him quietly taking this unknown vampire to her apartment. They walk until the get to an apartment complex, room 1A. She opens the door and they walk in. 'I'd offer you something but I haven't anything to give. I was looking for a meal when I saw you.' 'It is fine, these days my meals are a lot more dietary. I select more carefully than you I would think. Although that is not why I called for you tonight.' 'Really. You called for me?' 'That is correct. My power far exceeds yours and I can call family at will. My sister.' She looked at him before smirking. How could this be right? She has never known of any family by the last name of Soma. She is the only child of a lawyer and a soccer-mom. Then one day she awoke and she was what she is now. A vampire. 'Look, Mr. Soma. I can't be your sister. I don't have any siblings. And from what I know it is not possible to have full vampire siblings. Are there even such a thing as half vampires?' He smiled that same devilish smile, showing off the fangs before sitting down on a small leather sofa.He watched her and began to laugh. 'How long do you think you have been a vampire Ms. de Vries?' 'At least twenty years. One day I just woke up and was this way.' 'Do you ever remember the last time you walked in daylight?' The question was a good one. She stayed quiet. 'That's exactly what I thought. Keira, you true name is Sasha Sadim, and you are more than just your normal vampire. But there will be time for that soon. Will you trust me?' 'I can not say at the moment I will trust you Mr. Soma, but you have caught my interest. Now how shall we proceed?' 'Follow me. We must get you a meal.' She was impressed. Not many men could just come into her life and just tell her what to do. Something was different about this man, this vampire she knew nothing about. And yet he knew her whole life. He could explain things she couldn't. What other choice did she have? She smiles at him. She would use him till she found out all she could. Right now, she wanted her meal.
