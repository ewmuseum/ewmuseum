Biddle Reload Hype

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
August 22, 2005: a date that Josh will remember for the rest of his pathetic little life. It was the day that Mike Biddle forced Josh to leave the Global Arena on a stretcher. The event had scarred Josh’s mind with unspeakable fear. The beat down had set up a match between Biddle and Josh at Whiplash. The match never happened. Josh was too scared to step into the octagon with Biddle, too scared to even show up at the arena for the pay-per-view, too scared to even show his face in GLOBAL for months. Josh is supposed to be standing in Biddle’s way? Riiiiiiight. The GLOBAL committee might as well just give Biddle the International Title now, it’s not like Josh is going to show up for the match anyway. September 26, 2005: a date that neither Jake Hix nor Train will ever forget. It was the day that “Mr. Main Event” Mike Biddle sent both of the so-called “superstars” out of the arena on stretchers. Of course Biddle didn’t act alone; he had the help of his trusty bodyguard The Back Alley Brawler’something those in the Tag Team Titles match should keep in mind. January 11, 2006: the date when Mike Biddle finally claims the International Championship and starts his climb to the very top of the GLOBAL Mountain. Mark it down on your calendars folks, it’s going to be a historic night.
