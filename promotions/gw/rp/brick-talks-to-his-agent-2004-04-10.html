Brick talks to his agent

10 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/10/04 RRRRIIIIINNNGG! RRRRIIIIINNNGG! Brick scrambled to his phone as fast as he could. 'Call from Rosencrantz Saul,' intoned the talking Caller ID on Brick's phone. Saul Rosencrantz was Brick's agent. When he decided he wanted to go into acting, he auditioned for Saul, performing a monologue from A Raisin in the Sun. Brick immediately picked up the phone. 'Hey, Saul, what's up?' 'Brick, glad I caught you. I just called Avi Arad.' 'Yeah?' Brick answered. 'I asked about where you stood as far as getting the role of Luke Cage . . . ' Brick had been waiting to hear from Arad for some time now. Arad was the head of Marvel Studios, which oversaw film and television productions based on Marvel Comics properties. The property Brick was interested in was Luke Cage: Hero for Hire. Sony Pictures was in the process of developing a live-action motion picture based on the character. Ever since rumors about the movie came out, Brick (a huge fan of the character) had been active in trying to get cast in the lead role. When he had heard that John Singleton was directing the film, he had wanted to appeal to the fact that Singleton, too, was a fan of Luke Cage. 'Well, it's close. You're in the running, so that's good news at any rate.' 'Did they mention my audition tape?' 'Yeah, they liked the audition tape. You'd think that kind of thing would turn directors off, after that whole Sean Young Catwoman thing.' 'At least I didn't show up on any talk shows dressed up like Cage dissing John Singleton.' 'Yeah, that's good at any rate.' 'Who else is in the running?' 'Well, from what I've heard, LL Cool J, the Rock and maybe Ving Rhames.' 'Anyone in particular they might be leaning towards?' 'LL, I think. He's got the look and the acting chops.' 'Yeah, but he's bald. Whoever plays Luke Cage needs hair.' 'That can probably be dealt with. There are hairpieces, you know.' 'Well, I wouldn't feel too bad if L got the part and I didn't.' 'Hey, don't say that! You are not gonna lose the part.' 'Saul, I appreciate your enthusiasm, but I think I need to prepare myself for that possibility. That's why I need you to look into a few more options.' 'Such as?' 'I need you to get me in touch with Jeremy and Robert Love.' 'The heads of Gettosake Entertainment?' 'So you're familiar with them.' 'One o' their properties is being made into a movie by Fox.' 'Yeah, I know. Venus Kincaid.' 'So, do you need me to try getting you a part in the Venus Kincaid movie?' 'Maybe, but it'd be cool if you could get them to let me option some of their other properties?' 'Like?' 'Like, maybe, Credence Walker.' 'And that is...' 'Sci-fi story, about a guy from our world who gets whisked off into an alternate reality version of Africa.' 'Interesting. I'll see what I can do.' 'Oh, and Saul?' 'Yes?' 'Be sure to send a video of my matches to Arad and Singleton.' 'Sure thing.' 'And tell them to watch Evolution this Monday and keep a close eye on my match.' 'Who are you facing?' 'It's a triple threat match. I'm taking on two newcomers: Rich Sherwin and J.C. Diamond. If I win, this match, it might just make all the difference.' 'Really pulling out all the stops, huh?' 'You know it.' 'OK, I'll do that. Take care.' Brick then hung up the phone and pressed Play on his VCR. He was watching tapes of his opponents for Monday night. He needed to find any weaknesses he could exploit in that match. Too much was riding on this match for him not to be prepared.
