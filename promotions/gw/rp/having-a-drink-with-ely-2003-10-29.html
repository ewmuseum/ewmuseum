Having a drink with Ely

29 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-29-03 The air in the Dark Horse Saloon was thick with excitement. As usual, people were dancing and drinking and having a good time. “American Bad Ass” was playing on the jukebox. Two men, in particular, were soaking in the ambiance. Two men who often mapped out battle plans and drank over discussions about life. These two men were Brick and Ely Braxton. Brick had a glass of ginger ale while Ely had his usual Texas mudslide. This night, their topic of conversation concerned one particular topic: the Upper Echelon. For months now, the group had been running roughshod throughout Global Wrestling, looking to dominate the organization. Brick and Ely were just two of many individuals looking to prevent that. “So, how’s Christina doing?” Brick asked. “She’s still upset about all the crap from TUE. Still blames herself for all the damage they caused.” “Hey, all the dirt TUE does ain’t on nobody but TUE. So she brought them in to help deal with the Nevermore. I can understand. She didn’t want Salaok and the rest to ruin the company. And I’ll be honest. It was hard to keep dealing with them over and over again. But now we got to deal with a bunch of motherfuckers who might be just as bad as the Nevermore. But I can understand why she did it. Although I’m trying to with what Johnny Cruse did. Judging from the fact that he was apologizing, it can’t be good.” “Yeah. Real shitstorm, ain’t it” Way they dangled the man’s wife? “TUE’s a bunch of punks. Stecchino, Price, Zero, Pierce, Soma, Ice, Black Lotus and especially that little faggot Holmes.” “Still can’t stand him, huh?” “Ely, when could I ever stand that little bastard?” “Didn’t you want to take Deacon under your wing at one point?” “Yeah, I did, but then Deacon had to go and make shit personal. Well, the day is gonna come when he and I settle our score. And when that days comes, he better go get saved, because the only thing that’s gonna stop me from stomping that boy into wine is divine intervention.” “Now Salaok was a sadistic piece of shit, but at least when he did have the balls to face me man-to-man, he never had bodyguards with him.” “Damn right. Speakin” o? bodyguards, I heard you put security detail on your family and friends again.” “I ain’t taking any chances, especially after what happened with the Nevermore.” “That’s good.” “Still no word on Donovan?” “Not a damn one. Not since that videotape” “That whole thing just screams TUE.” “Ain’t a damn thing I’d put past those sons of bitches.” “Well, we were ready to take on the Nevermore. We’ll damn sure take on the whole damn Upper Echelon, too.” “Things seem a bit better now that Bubba’s back.” “Gotta say I wasn’t expecting him to be there. Damn sure didn’t expect Jesse Briggs to be there.” Brick had remembered how Briggs had betrayed him, Ely and Logan Creed when they were facing off against the Nevermore one night. He also remembered hearing how Jesse had turned his back on Gunner Addison. “Yeah, I know what you mean. But Jesse’s been good since Bubba started gettin” better.” “I can imagine. Well, with Bubba back, maybe the games’ll slow down, if not stop completely.” “Of course they ain’t gonna stop. They didn’t stop with the Nevermore.” “Listen, I hear you’re the special guest referee in my match with John Zero.” “Yeah.” “Well, listen. This could be a chance to send a message to TUE. So I don’t want you to play favorites. I want you to call the match right down the middle. If I get the pin fall, I want it to be a clean one.” “Well, I was gonna do that anyway. But at the same time, you gotta realize that this is a war we’re fightin” with TUE. In war, there ain’t no clean victories. Just victories.” “I guess, if anybody would know that, it’s you. Let’s just go in and do what we gotta do.”
