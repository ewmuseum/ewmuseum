Putting out the fire...

23 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/23/03 **It has been a few days since I have seen Don Salamone but already the pressure is mounting inside me. At any moment I could just explode’or could I? All my life I have wished and dreamed for the opportunity that has finally been given to me, I will not fail, I can not fail because failure is not an option. Tonight I get my first big chance to prove myself to the family. My name is Sean Price and I am a mobster. This is the life I have chosen and this is the life I will lead.** (As the scene fades in we see an alone Sean Price getting out of his black 2003 Hummer. Price is as always decked out in a business suit and tie. Sean shuts the door behind him and then presses a button on his key chain to ensure that the doors have locked behind him. He then takes a step forward and looks up at a big sign that says Twister Pizza. He then looks down at the main entrance and sees that the place is packed as it usually is. He walks around to the side and knocks on a metal door. A few seconds pass and then a little metal plate on the door slides open and a man’s eyes can be seen staring directly at Sean. Sean looks back at the man and then the metal plate shuts and the big door opens up. Now standing infront of Sean is Camelo, another one of Don Salamone’s men. He leads Sean down a hallway and then down a short flight of stairs into a dark room where we can see Vinny polishing a gun at his desk. Sean stops for a second to look about the room and then walks upto Vinny at the desk and begins a conversation with the man.) “Well if it isn’t the new guy Sean Price. Luige told me you were going to be by today. He told me you needed some supplies for alittle adventure you and Paulie were going on.” “That’s right. What have you got for me?” “What have I got for you” More like what haven’t I got. Take a look around, anything you think you might need just grab. Simple as that.” **Simple as that he says. Except it wasn’t that simple at all. How simple can it be for me to walk around a room and pick out weapons, most of which I didn’t even know existed and hope that they will be useful for the task at hand. But I couldn’t falter, I couldn’t delay, if I did Vinny might see it as a weakness and I can’t be exposed. Not now, not ever. So I made the best of the situation, I picked out a black duffle bag, two Colt 1911’s, as well as some extra clips, a pair of black ropes, a baseball bat and some tape. You can never go wrong with tape my father used to say.** “That should be it Vinny. I think i’ve got everything we need for our job.” “Good, if anything comes up and you need some last minute supplies you know where to find me. Camelo, walk Sean to the door and make sure there are no blue shirts around. We wouldn’t want any trouble now would we.” “We sure wouldn’t. Come on Sean, follow me.” (Camelo and Sean head back up the stairs and then down the hallway towards the side door. Camelo opens the door slowly and then walks out about 5 feet and looks in both directions as well as infront of him looking for police officers. After being certain that there are none around Camelo points to Sean acknowledging that it is ok for him to leave. Sean then walks out the side door, opens the front door to his hummer, throws the back onto the back seat. He shuts his door and then pops a cd in the cd player, the song “My Reply” by the Atari’s starts playing. Sean turns on his car and then shakes hands with Camelo who then walks back inside, shutting the door behind him.) “Now with that out of the way I can focus on my duties as a professional wrestler and member of the most elite group ever assembled’The Upper Echelon. Last week was a highlight week for the TUE, Deacon Holmes got a hard earned tie against the world champion Brick in one of the hardest matches in GW history to win. Myself, John Zero and Tony Stecchino all advanced to the second round of the International Title Tournament, and the best news of all was the announcement that Tony Stecchino had become a member of our great organization.” “B.J.P considers the YB to be the grand players of the GW, the best of the best. B.J.P would have you believe many things, few of which are true, all of which exaggerated. Sure the YB, the former YB was a great group, atleast that is what the history books would tell you. But how can a group lead by the very man that was the reason for their demise the first time be any good the second time around?” “They can’t’and they won’t. History will see to it, and if history does not, the TUE surely will. B.J.P is the self proclaimed Firestarter, well if that’s true, than consider me the fireman. Because BJP can no longer fuel the fire, not like he used to, his flame is slowly dwindling down to nothing and the TUE will be there to put it out.” “Tomorrow night is a night full of potential for both groups, the team of BJP and TLM have a chance to do what it was Tyler and I could not do, and that was capture the tag team titles, albeit we were part of a screw job, but we did not walk out the champs and that is what we were planning to do.” “But the harder task falls upon the hands of Trey Reed. A man with one face, but many foes. He has so far meet many of the members of the TUE in the ring, a win against Tyler, an tie against myself. His past with Tony is well documented, but tomorrow night non of that will matter. What will matter is that Trey Reed will be facing off against a no name and two members of the TUE in an attempt to advance to the final match for the International Title Tournament. Could Tony and I have a hidden agenda in the match” Perhaps yes, perhaps no. I guess you will all have to wait until tomorrow to find out.” (As the scene fades out we see Sean continuing to drive down the highway, presumably to meet up with fellow TUE member Tony Stecchino to train for their match that will take place tomorrow against Sergei and Trey Reed.) “Oh and one more thing, and this goes out to Trey Reed himself, remember come match time, you have no friends, if the YB is smart they will keep their noses out of our business because if they don’t it will be the last time that they interfere in anything. That is not a threat, it’s a promise.”
