Bad Moon Rising

9 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/09/05 Prelude: Whiplash Rammifications The steril white walls almost burned the pupils of your eyes when you looked around the room. Everything was bright white, cluttered with splashes of red... reminders of previous events in the evening. Chaos impersonated. That was all that could describe the general feeling and sound that bounced from wall to wall. Nine people shoved into a room didn't make for a very comfortable enviornment, but either did his return. Why now? Sure, it didn't matter much in the long run... but it was just an inconvience to something that seemed to be running so smoothly. A cough could be heard over the hysteria. He wiped away the blood that began to trickle off the side of his lip in a stream. The room had splatters of it around, no matter how much he tried he couldn't hide the fact that he was hurt. 'The internal bleeding will stop eventually.' A man wearing a white mask said as he looked at the mans chest, 'You have a couple broken ribs and the rest are bruised or damn near it. I don't know how you were still fighting out there... but I'm glad security and our EMTs got you while they did.' 'I'm fine.' Christian Zenith said out loud as the doctor continued to look over his chest. His entire mouth tasted like he'd drank a 20 oz. bottle of blood... one of those rare occasions that he wished he were a vampire like Shadow, so it wouldn't taste so bad. Pain shot up through Zenith's body that made him want to scream, but he didn't even make a face. His eyes looked over towards the side of the room... where Vivica J. Valentine stood patiently along side Tessa Windsor, Demitri Miasnikov, and Jason LeMaster. Dawn was still fighting in the middle of the ring, as he sat there all he wished for was that Train was going through the same hell he was right now. Security finished him from finishing the job he started, and now he had to start all over again. As the adrenaline subsided in his body it became harder and harder to breathe. Another cough started to rise inside Christian, his efforts to subside it were useless. Coughing felt as if somone was slamming him in the chest with a sledgehammer. The doctor began to wrap tape around Zenith's chest as he tried to breath as softly as possible to ease the pain. He didn't know what to tell Vivica and the rest of them who sat on the other side of the room waiting patiently. All he could do was wait for Dawn to win the World Championship, and maybe all the pain would go away. Maybe... DING! DING! DING! The entire locker room grows to a grim silence as the final bell rings for the main event World Championship match. Even the doctor stops wrapping tape around Zenith's chest... 'The Winner of this match and STILL World Champion, Mindy Mercer!' Brian West exclaimed from the middle of the ring. A scowl crossed Christian Zenith's face as he pushed the doctor onto the floor and made an attempt to escape from the medical room, where Global Security awaited on the outside for just such an occassion. Before he could get to the floor Christian Zenith collapsed onto the floor on his hands and knees. Blood began to run out of the sides of his mouth while he tried to crawl to the door. Everything began to spin around him and everything began to fade to black. ---- Chapter One: The Bounty Proclaimation ---- 'Christian! Are you alright?' He could hear her voice ringing in his ears, before opening his eyes. Everything around him became a blur. Inside of him his stomach turned, even with the rising blood levels. Christian needed rest bad, and he certainly wasn't going to get it here. Before he realized it, his body began to stop listning to his commands. Breaking into a coughing fit... Zenith saw splashes of red against the tile floor with his blurred vision. 'Aren't you going to help him?!' The doctor looked down at Christian Zenith, eyes opening and closing and splatters of blood on the floor below. He looked up at Vivica J. Valentine after taking his eyes off the fallen Conscious Crisis. Vivica could see that Christian needed rest badly, and it was the only thing that was going to heal his wounds. She also knew Christian well enough that he wasn't going to do it. She walked across the room while Hunting Grounds looked concerned behind her. Getting down one knee she placed her hand on Christian's back next to him. 'I can't do much for an injury like this. He has to do it himself.' The doctor said looking down at Vivica. 'Stubborn bastard...' Vivica said to Christian, who seemed to be consumed by his intentions to hide his obvious pain, 'Train'll get his... they're probably doing the same exact thing to him right now. Right now you need to rest and prepare. At Evolution... we have a lot of work to do. Mindy beat Dawn by some fucking fluke miracle, we have to get that championship back where it belongs. Train can wait.' 'Train's waited for long enough...' Christian managed to choke out, as his icy cold eyes tried to focus on the face of Vivica J. Valentine, 'It's time for Train to die. The entire damn world is against me and it's about time I even the odds. We're not doing this the Christina Reno way anymore... no, now we are going to do this the Chicago way.' 'What are you talking about?' Vivica asked, curious. 'The fans love Train... but he's just a useless piece of trash to me. They cheer him only because he's facing me. The same reason everyone boos the Yankees, everyone hates dominant winners. People have no respect for work ethic and excellence. Everyone wants a hero who is a nobody like them. They all see that in Train. He's a self-proclained thug. He's all about being 'hard' and 'representing his people.' All everyone needs is a little incentive and we'll see how loved Train is by his 'people.'' Christian responded trying to regain control of his body before sitting down on the tile floor, leaning up against the bed that he'd pushed himself off of earlier. 'Money?' Tessa Windsor asked from across the room. 'No, no...' Zenith said closing his eyes for a second, fighting off the pain, 'Any idiot can get money off a scratch off ticket. If you want results you have to offer them something that they can only get through me, and through doing what I ask. It's something much, much more valuable than a thouand dollars. I can give them something that no one else in Global can give them. A guarenteed International Championship shot for the first person who can have Train carried out of the arena on a stretcher.' ---- Chapter Two: The Scars Of Yesterday ---- His cadet blue eyes narrowed on the chromed reflective surface that lied on the wall in front of him. Surrounded by white, his bruised body stuck out like a lone demon rising up through the heavens. A thin layer of mist clung onto the walls around him, including the mirror he gazed into. Peering at his battered and bruised ribs through a open area that was wiped away with his hand a few seconds earlier. As he looked at the scars of yesterday, the thoughts filled his mind on how they got there... and how he got where he is now. Drops of water fall onto his forehead while he examined the criss crossed, almost artistic patterns of black and blue across his ribacge. Every breath became increasingly difficult it seemed. Sometimes the thrill of the fight was the only thing that'd make the pain go away. He remembered the night that he watched Train come down to the arena. The intentional aggrevated shock quickly went away as he relished his opportunity to get his hands around Anthony James once again. How long he'd tried try to ignore James, it was for Train's own good. He just wouldn't go away. Some called it courage, Zenith called it ignorance. Almost as aggrevating was the time and effort he'd wasted on Mike Biddle incompitance. Not only could he not manage to keep Train away from more than a few days, he was walking around with Zenith's International Championship shot. What a waste of hard work. 'If you want something done right... you have to do it yourself.' Christian Zenith painfully said to himself as he adjusted the towel that hung around his waste before exiting the steril white master bathroom. His eyes adjusted quickly to the dimly lit Zenith Manor. It'd been far too long since Christian had spent a night by himself in the Manor. Nights traveling between Zenith Corporation, Global Arena, and HG Compound in New Mexico had taken away all the nights he used to have to himself. Then again, he was never one to stay put for very long. As he sat down on the bed, he could feel his battered ribs shoot painful spasms through his insides. RING! Looking across the room Christian Zenith saw the telephone light up and spring to life. Slowly he got up off the bed, running his right hand painfully through his black hair. He wraps his right arm around his ribcage as he moved towards the phone. No pain, No gain. RING! Zenith reached out for the phone with his free left hand and picked it up off the charger. Another movement, another shot of pain. It was amazing he fell asleep at night. Then again... he was used to the pain. He'd been fighting most of his life. 'Zenith Manor.' Christian said professionally. 'Hey Chris... this is Viv.' Vivica J. Valentine paused a bit before she continued, 'You havn't happened to see this weeks Evolution lineup have you?' 'No Viv, I've been busy.' Christian responded, intrigued on what may cause her to call on such a mundane notion. She knew he always waited until the night before to take a look at what was going on. Afterall, he had other signifigant things to do with his life outside of Global. 'It doesn't look like Mike Biddle is getting that International Championship shot afterall. Seems the new council thought it would be cute to go another direction.' Vivica responded, now audibily irritated. 'What?' 'The great new 'impartial' and illustrious council decided that they wanted to see you and Shadow go at it for her championship. Impartial my ass. The day someone in Hunting Grounds is treated as an impartial member of the roster is the day that Mark Bishop wins the GW World Championship.' Vivica went on, 'But it gets even better than that.' 'So much for the recognition of official Global Wrestling paper work.' Christian Zenith paced back and forth in the master bedroom now, listening to Vivica J. Valentine speak the words that he'd through he would hear sometime soon. So much for the new impartial council changing everything. It's the same thing wherever he goes. Everyone hates a winner. People claim to be so innocent and open-minded, it's a different story when it comes to success. The masses foam at the mouth when it comes to watching the great ones fall from grace. A sick side mode people would ever admit. Zenith would be damned if he's let his success turn into a freak shot for the public. 'They show has been dubbed the Global Wrestling Night of Champions. Instead of putting Dawn McFarlane in another shot or finally giving you your fucking rematch, they decide to go ahead and give the shot to Train. I guess a complete lack of respect for the English language and a catch phrase is all you need to succeed in Global afterall.' Vivica rolled her eyes after speaking those words. Zenith could see it in his mind, even though she was almost on the other side of the country... sitting in the HG Compound in New Mexico. '...' 'Chris?' Vivica spoke, but it was too little to late. The phone now lied on the wood paneled floor at the foot of Zenith's King Size bed. ---- Chapter Three: Cause And Effect ---- The clamoring sounds of conversation were almost like a roar as you made your way into the restaurant on a Saturday night. Everyone had a different story, a different mood, and a different reason to celebrate. Most people had their eyes fixated on college football, but a certain group of people had another reason for being there. 'I'll take a Ginger Ale.' A lanky man with black thick glasses and short blonde hair said to the beautiful waitress who quickly jotted down his order with a plastic smile. She looked up after filling up her small notebook. 'Will that be all?' She responded, looking around at the three men around the table. 'That'll be it ma'am. Thank You.' A bigger man with a goatee responded, burying his head back into his menu as the perky waitress walked away. It wasn't long before the lanky man looked across the table towards the third man seated across the table from him, Global Commentator Mark Bishop. 'Have you heard anything about what kind of crowd we're looking at for Super Series?' The man asked Mark, who finally took his eyes off the waitresses ass and back to the man sitting across from him. 'Looks good from here.' He said with a slight chuckle, 'As for the Super Series PPV, the numbers look good. I'm sure with Shadow being in the main event representing Global, it should turn some more eyes towards the Dawn McFarlane vs Jake Hix match. The feed back from Primetime has been good so far. Might be a record setting I-PPV.' 'That's good to hear...' The larger man said, 'Has anyone here had the Philly Cheesesteak?' 'It's excellent.' Mark Bishop responded before glancing back down at his menu, 'What are you having Dave?' The lanky man looked up from his menu back and Mark before looking back down again. Researching all the options for his meal. Of course they all wanted some of that waitress. Deep in the back of their minds though, they all knew it was nothing more than wishful thinking. 'I don't have a clue yet.' Dave finally responded, 'But I do have to take a piss, if she comes back while I'm still in there have her wait up for me.' Mark Bishop and the large man next to him looked on as Dave Harris got up from his seat at the table and disappeared into the crowd of ranting friends and beatful waitresses. It sure was a good day to be alive. Navingating his way towards the back of the restaurant, he saw a door marked Mens closing shut ahead. 'Excuse me.' Dave muttered as he jukes to his right, narrowly avoiding a burly man and his wife getting up from their table. A stern look back in his direction was ignored. In most circumstances he'd take the time to apologize, but this time one too many Ginger Ales were calling his name. He pushed open the Men's Restroom door to find an empty off-white colored restroom. Ripped articles of Sports Illustrated, Local Newspapers, and Men's Magazines lined the off-white walls... a sure tell tale sign that he found himself on the inside of a sports bar. He walked up to the urinal, a bit confused. He thought for sure he'd seen the door closing as he approached... but there was no one in sight. He closed his eyes as he stood in front of the urinal, looking to find the relief in the only quiet room in the sports bar. Sometimes you could find relaxation in the strangest ways... CLICK! At that exact moment it became apparent to Dave that this wasn't one of them... 'Don't move.' A familiar voice said from behind him. Dave paused in front of him... frozen and uncomfortably exposed. What a perfect time to take advantage of someone, 'Put your hands up on the wall.' Slowly Dave Harris put his hands on the wall in front of him. Behind him he could hear the man's slightly staggered breaths, almost as if he was in pain himself. Dave tried his best to take a look at the man behind him. All he could manage to see was a black blur, but he knew for sure one thing. There was a gun pointed towards the back of his head. It didn't take a genius to figure that much out. 'Dave Harris. Global Wrestling Executive in charge of travel arrangements for personnel. You're also in charge of courtesy at Global Arena... setting up the locker rooms and suites to everyone's liking. You see Dave, I have a very simple question to ask you... if you answer right, you can go back and sit down with your friends Mark and Hank. Eat some chicken wings, drink some more ginger ale.' The man's voice continued on, 'That is... if you answer right.' 'If I answer wrong?' Dave paused, he could feel his heart beating up in his throat as beads of sweat began to drip down his forehead. 'Well, you don't want to answer wrong Dave.' The man responded. 'You can't do anything to me Christian.' Dave spoke out, 'We're in a public restroom, someone can walk in at any time. If you dare shoot that gun this entire place will hear it. They'd have you locked in here and you'd go to jail for a very long time Zenith.' 'There is a lot about me you don't know Dave,' Zenith reponded, 'As for someone walking in here, no one is getting into this bathroom without my permission. I've already taken care of that. As for me not having the guts to fire Ares... are you willing to take that chance? We really could avoid all of this if you just give me what I want to know.' 'What in the hell is that Christian?' Dave responded, his nervousness made him begin to shake. Zenith watched Dave twitch as he had his hands up against the wall. He had him right where he wanted him. 'I want to know the names of every member of this 'impartial' committee.' Zenith demanded of Dave Harris. Christian could almost sense Dave's answer but the large gulp he took. 'I...' Dave paused, 'I don't know Zenith.' 'Wrong answer.' With those words Christian Zenith grabbed the back of Dave's head with his free hand and smashed it into the off-white wall in front of him. A sickening thud echoed through the bathroom as Christian pulled back on Dave's hair looking into his newly broken nose and blooded forehead. 'Funny thing Dave, I heard a little rumor going around that you might even be on it. The person in charge of all travel arrangements for Global Wrestling I find it very difficult to believe that you do not know who is on the committee. Surely, they have to meet somewhere. Tell me... now.' 'I don't know Christian! I swear to God please put me down.' Dave begged as Zenith kicked the back of Dave's kneecap, sending him down to the cold tile floor on his knees. Continuing to pull back on Dave's hair and watch the blood flow, Zenith pointed his Desert Eagle Ares right against the forehead of Harris. 'Do not lie to me Dave...' Zenith said, his icy cold stare seemingly looking down into Dave Harris' body and into his soul. 'I can sense when someone is lying to me Dave, it's like a sixth sense I've picked up from years of hanging around with liars and beaurocrats. Now tell me everything you know about that committee Dave. Your family will thank you for it someday, I assure you.' 'They don't tell us anything...' Dave says as tears from the pain of his broken nose begin to trickle down his blood soaked face, 'They keep everything secretive, I don't even think Brandi knows who the committee is made up of. She's just a mouthpiece, she doesn't know a thing. I don't know what Global is doing or even if it is in good hands. I just know that Brandi recieves word through e-mail or in her mailbox, and then she tells everyone what to do from there.' Christian Zenith stares down at Dave Harris, who looks up at him almost like a child pleading to stay up for 10 more minutes. It was moments like these that Zenith relished. To be in charge, to be in control. Not being shoved around by some group of idiots with secret agendas and their heads shoved up their ass. Too big of cowards to even tell the world who in the hell they really are. 'You know what Dave...' Christian Zenith says, moving around a little bit to get more comfortable in his flack jacket, 'You are lucky that I believe you.' With one quick motion Christian Zenith jerked the head of Dave Harris foreward and slammed the handle of Ares down on the back of his skull. His body bent over limply on the floor as Christian looked down at him. Another hard effort for absolutely nothing. It had become a reoccuring incident that was aggrevating him to no end. About 10 minutes later Mark Bishop checked up on Dave in the bathroom to find him unconscious on the floor with smears of blood. It was about another 30 minutes when the paramedics arrived. Although the police questioned Dave Harris after he was released from the hospital with a concussion, he never did tell them what happened that night... nor did her pursue to file charges. It was something the police never did understand. It was something that made a lot of sense to Dave though. Christian Zenith had mentioned his family. To him, that was enough to keep his mouth shut... no matter what the cost. ---- Prologue: Vindication In Sight ---- Christian Zenith made his way down the hallway, shooting looks at various members of the media running over to him trying to get the big interview or the big picture. But even the desperate paparazzi knew when to keep their distance. It was obvious from the very beginning that this was going to be one of those nights. Without hesitation Christian shoved open the door in front of him marked with his logo. Inside he took by surprise Vivica J. Valentine who was enjoying the view of the octagon from their Hunting Grounds suite. The view never grew old, even after all this time in Global. It wasn't a smile in which Zenith greeted Vivica on this night. It was a look of focus and intensity. She could only imagine what was going through his mind. 'This ends tonight.' Christian said as he looked over at Vivica, who replied simply with a nod. 'Tonight... I don't care who it is. Someone is going to pay for this.'
