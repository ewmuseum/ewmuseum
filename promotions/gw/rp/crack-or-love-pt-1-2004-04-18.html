Crack or Love Pt.1 J. C. Diamond Global Wrestling Promo #1180 Date: 4/18/04 Location: Miami April 5th, 2004- Miami Florida (Jason Ortiz’s Home). “Where the hell did you put my shirt John,” screamed J.C as the scene fades into his messed up bedroom, that hadn’t been cleaned in so fucking long. He had searched every where for it. The past few minutes that's all he has been doing. Searching. He can’t find anything in this god damn room. Jayson reached down, yanked the shirt he was looking for away from the hands of John Scott as he entered into Jayson’s bedroom. John Scott seemed amused. “You know if you look harder, you might find things around here.” “Whadda ya mean, ‘look harder?” asked J.C, taking the shirt and pulling it over his massive arms. He never seemed to find an actual shirt that would fit him. But he did today. A nice shirt he might add. The shirt was a white collared shirt that had been neatly ironed a while ago, but as it laid on the floor for however long it had been there, the wrinkles had wore into it, even into the seems. Without getting into a fight with the thing, Jayson just put it on. He had to live with the wrinkles. He was becoming late as it is. Trying to find another one would just make him even more late, so he dared not to even attempt that. He wished that today, John Scott would actually do something around the house. J.C knew that the Nazi would do the same thing he always does, which is...nothing. Jayson’s heart races and he can’t even think. Sweat began to pour down his face. “Do something John. Find me my shoes,” ordered J.C. “Now!” Jayson was kneeling on the floor right now, and outside of his bedroom window, the sun hard started to come up, and was now blinding him in the face. He hated when that happened. He fought through it. “-You told me to leave you alone,” his voice cracked. “Jayson you told me I would just make you aggravated if I tried to help you. You said I couldn’t help you find anything. So I wont.” Jayson, for the first time in his life, had nothing to say to the tall Nazi. He was speechless. “.......” Nothing came out of his mouth. Still no words coming out of his mouth. “.......” Finally, Jayson figured out something to say. He blurted it out. “Fine. I’ll do it myself.” † In the Mind of a Crack Head † On the road again, from his Miami home, all the way to Steven Morella’s office in downtown Miami, Florida. Sure it wasn’t a long drive, a few minutes you could add, but in this warm weather, it seemed like forever before he would get there. He wouldn’t stop any where, knowing if he did, he probably would end up missing his appointment, and also missing his chance for the job. He would stay on the main road there, the main road that connects the outskirts of Miami, which is where Jayson lives, all the way to downtown Miami. It was a quite and calm road. A place that Jayson could sit back in his black seat and relax. Look out the side window, but only for a few seconds, as he could get on a head on collision and end his life right there. This had really been the first time since the death of his former girlfriend; Daisy since he had drove on this road. Her favorite thing to do was drive on this road- with no cause. Just to see the view. Ever since then, Jayson had been avoiding the whole situation by taking side roads that lead into Miami, which in return, actually take longer than they really are. “I shouldn’t have taken this road,” J.C continued. “To many bad memories.” So here the crack head was, driving, just sitting back and enjoying the road. But he couldn’t really enjoy it, as everyone was focused still on his girlfriend Daisy. The person that he had loved so much. With his leg stepped slightly on the pedal, the car doing about 60 miles an hour, on a 50 mile an hour road, he wonders if there is really people out there just like him. Driving a road that is empty. No cars. Just him and the road. Thinking about his god damn life. There has to be someone out there that is just like J.C. He hasn’t found out who they are, but he will. He will try hard. Jayson removed his left hand from the wheel. “Everything seems like a blur to me on this road. The trees. The houses. Everything. I can’t focus on one thing; instead, I am focusing on things that are moving to fast.” He must have smoked something before driving. When you first get your driver’s license, they tell you what it is like to drive when you are under the influence of drugs and alcohol. They show you pictures of accidents that happen to people when they are drunk or stoned. They tell you the risks and how to avoid it. They tell you what you should do when you are drunk and need a way to get home. They tell you to call a friend who is sober, or call a cab. But is that really necessary. Say you had a few beers in your system, you are a little buzzed, and a little tired as well, and do you think you really need to call a cab. Fuck. Spend all that money when you can drive home drunk. But then, there is that possibility of Jayson killing someone at the hands of his Benz. Would that really be worth a couple of beers? It is fucked. They put in you a situation where you can’t choose. There is actually, not a good answer. They fuck you over so you have to pay money. They fuck you over so you won’t cause a head on collision, breaking someone’s neck, leaving them for dead on the side of a road. “That is so right.” They put it in your head that things go wrong when you are drunk or stoned, but what is the difference. Everyone in this world dies one time or another. With his eyes firmly on the road still- but yet, wondering off in the distance, he turns the wheel to the left a little more, correcting his steering as his Benz was heading towards the other lane. Jayson smiled. Well the good thing about today was, he had something to be happy about. A new opportunity. A few minutes he would find his fait. April 15th, 2004- Miami Florida (Steven Morella‘s Office). He had made it there all in one piece. He was relived, with a sigh off of his chest. He could breath now. Only a few more minutes until the meeting. J.C was nervous. As the scene opens up, to a familiar face of everyone’s favorite Crack Head, the scene even fades in more dramatic, almost like one of those slow action movies. Instead, this movie was starring J.C. Right away, from the sign on the door, it is inevitable that this is the office of Steven Morella- the guy that would get J.C started with his new life and career. He had just arrived a few minutes ago, and all ready he was starting to sweat. He looked up and noticed that there were two doors. You enter through the main one, inside there is his secretary, sitting there, answering the phone. The other door, which is oblivious, leads to Steven’s office. A place that Jayson has seen before. His body told him to enter the room, but his mind, it begged the differ. It wanted to turn around and get the fuck out of there before anyone spotted him. Jayson pulled the door open slowly. The eyes of J.C, turned towards Steven’s secretary as she stared at him for a few seconds. A few split seconds. He didn’t say anything. “You must be Mr. Diamond;” she called to Pretty Boy. Jayson wasn't paying attention; instead, he was looking around the room, examining all the paintings. J.C grinned. “Mr. Diamond...me. That is correct. I am here to see Steven Morella,” Jayson sighed. “Here for the meeting between him and me. Did I come late? I did... didn’t I? Fuck!” At that split second, she didn’t laugh at J.C. She didn’t smile at him. Instead, she gave him a quick look and then turned her head down and stared at what looked to be an appointment schedule on the table. It took her a few seconds, as she was an old lady. You can tell right away, she had a hard time seeing through these bottle tip glasses. She squinted. He looked really hard to read the name off the sheet. She finally looked up at J.C. His long un-braided hair was blowing in the wind as the main door finally shut behind him. Today, he was wearing all his black suit with a white shirt and black tie. On the feet were some white shiny gator shoes, knowing that if he wanted the job he had to be presentable for Steven. This was the day to do it. The meeting was set, and everything was in place. She glanced at him incredulously. “You’re not on the list.” Already he had made the decision to do it, so he wouldn’t turn his back on. It was something he had to do, in order to have a normal life of his own. It was there. His life. But now, J.C had to pick it up and run with it. Once he had it, just keep running, don’t look back. With his eyes moving around the waiting room, he could only help to think how much this empire of Steven Morella’s had cost him. Jayson began to slither towards the front desk where his secretary was sitting. He leaned down, pulling his body forward on his elbows. She hesitated. Then, drawn by some irrepressible force; he began to grab the schedule sheet, pulling it towards him. He read. “...Look, it says my name right here lady. Can’t you read?” He muttered bleakly. “Can’t you read my own name? It says Diamond right there.” Pretty Boy slides the schedule back towards her. The thing that he didn’t get is, when he walked inside of the main room, already, she knew his name. Now, if his name wasn’t on that fucking sheet, then how did she know his name? Strange. Weird. Who knows? She tried to read it, but still nothing. Jayson was tried of people stalling on him. “Nothing. I don’t see your name here.” Jayson started to become pissed, knowing that HIS name is on that list. He tries to sum up the best explanation to the lady. He shrugged. “FUCK YOU!” Those words echoed throughout Steven Morella’s main office, and once he said that, it was clear that J.C was really there. The door inside of the main room popped open, and the first time, in however long it has been; Jayson finally got to see Steve’s face again. He hasn’t changed one bit. Jayson has rarely changed only his appearance really. Steve smiled at J.C. Why was that? Why do people always smile and greet you when you first meet up with someone. Why do people always have to be nice, but the fact was, they were assholes. Steve was like that; he put on a face for you. He wasn’t tricking anyone. He wasn’t tricking J.C. He knew what Steve was playing. It was all a trick. Just like the last time. But Jayson was desperate for money- that’s the ONLY reason why he was here. With his arm stuck out to its farthest position, Mr. Morella pointed at J.C. He walked towards him. “J.C. You made it,” he laughed. This was the first time he remembered Jayson ever being on time. “What did you expect Steve?” “I really don’t know. Didn’t think you would take my offer that seriously,” Steve observed, leaning against his door in his black suit and tie. He was dressed the same, as Jayson remembered. Silently, he crept towards the door where Steve was standing. He didn’t want to move to fast. Right there, Jayson could hear water drip a mile away. “Come in J.C, we need to talk about your new job.” Jayson followed Mr. Morella in his door. Steve walked towards his desk with his feet gliding across, what looked to be new carpet. While Steve moved towards his desk, the crack head was busy observing his new office. A nice set up for a guy who produces television shows. Strange. It was completely strange how a man like this, who sits on his fucking ass each day, doing nothing but watching television and bitching about RATINGS...makes a better living than a guy like J.C. Every day in Jayson’s life he has busted his ass. What does he get out of life. A home and a Nazi as a friend. “Great pad you have here Steve. You done yourself well,” says Jayson Diamond- talking like he was some kind of high roller. “Done yourself well...indeed.” He glanced over towards the Crack Head, who had finally sat done. It was all business from here. “All right Jayson, basically I brought you here for on reason. A job that could make you happy again. I know you and me aren’t on the same side. I never ask for us to be. I just want us at an understanding where nothing goes wrong between you and me. What I have here is a contract, for another television show. Ten episodes, more if you are interested. What the show is about...” he licked his lips. “Jayson you will find yourself a new WIFE. The show is called, CRACK or LOVE. You will have to choice at the end of the show who will you take back to your home to live with you. 5 women. Each episode you will go on a date with them. If you don’t like them at the end of the date, you can shut them down and they will be taken off the show. If you like them. They will move on to the next round. Once you have been on all the dates with the five women here is the tricky part.” Jayson was trying to figure out this picture in his head. He figured it out. 5 women + 1 Crack Head = LOVE. Makes sense. “Go on Steve, I am starting to get the picture in my head. Well sort of,” prompted J.C. Steve leaned back in his nice tall black chair. “Basically after the five dates you will have to choose three to go to the next round. This is where you take them home and introduce them to your soundings and yourself better. After those are done. You will pick one of them for your new love in your life. I think this can produce big ratings for everyone. When is the last time you ever seen a Crack Head dating on television,” he scratched his head. “NEVER!” “It has never been done J.C, and at the end you will end up with something. Money, and a new girlfriend. I know how much you want your life to move on and that will be the start of things. The bad part is, after you pick these women, you will continue to be filmed for several weeks. Even months maybe. We will follow you and see how you and your girlfriend cooperate and live together. The only part is Jayson..” Of course...there is always a plot to these shows. Steve sighed. “You can never tell her you are a CRACK HEAD. If she finds out, you and her will be forced to be split apart. So no matter how hard you try, you can’t do that shit. Or else you lose.” Jayson was completely stunned. How the fuck would he go months without drugs. It was impossible. He knew that Steve had something up his sleeve. This was how he would bring in the ratings. Force Jayson’s addiction to choose over CRACK or LOVE. It was fucked. But it was a job, and he was ready to try everything to get his life squared away. “I guess you got yourself another deal. It will be hard...but what the hell. You know I will choose love over crack any day,” murmured J.C. He sat heavily still at his black chair. “We will see J.C. We will see. Until then. Keep this on the down low. We will inform you when your first date is. Until then. You will have a personal assistant that will follow you around and video tape your every move. Starting now.” This was it. THIS WAS HIS NEW LIFE HE HAS BEEN WAITING FOR. † In the Mind of a Crack Head † He had not forgotten about his match at Global. It was still thick in his head, and he knew, after today it was all work out and no play. He had to win this match. It was a way to get his career higher than it already is. Sure he has beat Rich Sherwin, but if he beats him again, he will hold more victories over him. He will hold more pride over Rich. Jayson will look at himself as a man that is on the track to the top. This was his chance to make it back to the top of Global Wrestling. Already he was making a name for himself, but it was time to go bigger. Bigger is what he was going to do. Rich Sherwin was going to find out, what it’s like to be a true wrestler. Jayson was a true superstar. J.C handed Rich the belt, even he knows that. Everyone else does. After Evolution, he will look up at J.C and see that J.C should be the Crusierweight Champion. It was a fact. People will see that he is better. Smarter.
