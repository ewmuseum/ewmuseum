Chuck the Pikachu... Revealed

25 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-25-2004 The sweet taste of victory. A legacy in the making. A match not soon forgotten. But is that ever enough... to make a legend? The scene fades in to a hotel room. Chris Steel is up busy moving around and about the room, packing all of his personal belongings into several boxes that are marked with the trademark GW logo. He has a smirk on his face. He leans back to crack his back, as if he had been crouching over constantly. 'Ah Jeese, Who would have known that this was what I would be doing after my win.' Chris said to himself. 'O well, The impression has been made, and I'm here for good. You did good Chris.... Real Good...' Chris Continued. At that moment Chris plopped himself onto his couch, breathing out a large sigh before he turned on his television set, pressing play on the remote and starting a VHS tape that had been previously in the machine. It had been watched midway through. Chris leaned foward and watched intently on his victory move. 'BOOM!!' Chris began to chuckle. Rewinding everytime it played through. After indulging several times in the victory he turned the VCR off. Channel surfing until one advertisment caught his eye. (A couple of men are on the screen. They are sitting around casually in a bar, some of the GW roster is there, along with some applicants of other federations, they are all drinking and laughing. Making a lot of noise and having a good time.) 'If you want to have a good time, meet some new people, and drink a few friends under the table. Then come on down tomorrow night to the Dark Horse Saloon and show your nerve in a 3 day drinking contest!' A narrators voice said. 'Because... It's Free!' The narrators voice concluded. 'Humm...' Chris thought. 'Interesting... well I guess it's a way to get my name out there. Even though I DID control my entire match, people just don't seem to be biting that it's all about me.' (The next second Chris flipped to a kids television channel. A ball is flying through the air until Chris notices it is Pokemon.) 'Holy Crap! What the hell is up with this?' Chris began to rant. 'Why can't pikachu have a REAL name.... something like... Tom or Chuck...' Chris was mesmorized by the program and began to crack a smile. 'Yea, I can see it now... 'Chuck the Pikachu... I CHOOSE YOU!' Chris Exlaimed. (At that moment Chris's doorroom handle began to turn and another figure began to appear from the crack. Chris flung the remote up and juggled it for a second, trying to shut off the television before his guest would notice what he had been up to. As the door opened all the way, it revealed Christina McCoy, president and founder of GW. He took a couple of seconds to piece it together and gave chris a suspicous look...) 'Dude, were you just watching.... pokemon....?' Christina questioned him. Almost as quickly as Christina had finished asking him Chris snapped back with an answer. 'No!' Christina sat quiet. Staring at chris for a long... awkward pause.... 'Are you sure?' Chris began to go on the offensive and starting trying to change the subject. 'Well uhhh... YOU... ummm, were YOU watching pokemon?' 'Well, now you know I wasn't because I was just coming through your room, and I think I heard something... maybe something about pikachu?' Christina explained. 'You mean Chuck the Pikachu?' Chris said. 'What?!' 'I mean uhhh.. ummm... Nope. I was just uhh... playing my...' Chris quickly reached into one of the packed boxes and pulled out the first thing that he felt. Which was coincidently a jar of pickles. 'Playing my... pickles?' Christina had been thrown off track by this unexpected conversation and changed back to what she had come to speak with him before. She handed him a peice of paper with several names of in. 'Here you go, It's a copy of everyone who will be in the rumble. Good Luck!' She said, leaving the room. Chris stood silent, knowing he had been caught. He had already planned for a way to cover it up and gain what little dignity could be saved from such an insident... 'World Title.... Here I come...' Chris said as the room fell quiet. Chris reached across the table, grabbed his remote, walked to his door and locked it... and turned the television back on...
