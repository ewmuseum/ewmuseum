Faint of Hearts

29 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-29-05 (02-15-05) Even Michael Mercer had to admit, there were times in his life where he needed some sort of guidance. He turned his back on the possibility of fate long ago, along with the loss of faith in God. It would've been hard for Michael to feel any differently about such things; he lost his parents, then the second closest thing to a father he had years later. Michael's only true family left came from Italy, and he wouldn't have even known of Mindy's existence if she hadn't been so dead set on meeting him. What was his purpose? Why was he left contemplating killing himself in a home that was his before six months ago? 'What am I doing?' ~~~***~~~ (03-22-05) Michael was glad he made it to Italy for Clash of the Titans. After the disappointing loss in his debut back in wrestling at High Octane with his cousin, he wasn't exactly the most uplifted of spirits. The more he thought about it, the more he truly didn't care. Yeah, it wasn't something Michael could be happy about, but why should be lose sleep over it? He had already proved himself to be the best; he accomplished that almost eleven months ago when he had finally won the one thing that eluded him ever since he started out in the business five years before. For him to lose his first match back in a wrestling ring in nearly a year- after someone he didn't even care to know interfering, no less- it wasn't that big of a deal. Besides, Michael was worried about much more than losing a measly wrestling match... ~~~***~~~ (02-15-05) 'Damn...' the purple haired Frenchman mumbled slowly. He proceeded to mumble a few obscenities in his native language that Michael didn't dare to bother trying to decipher; his headache was killing him enough as it was. 'Wh- what are you doing here?' he asked. 'Shit, it's been a long time...' He looked down at Michael with a justified sense of confusion in his blue eyes. All he saw of his friend after eighth grade was on television when Michael wrestled. He was as much of a high profile star as could be in New York, and for a moment, it took the man standing right in front of him for Eddie to remember what was most important: he actually knew this person. Not that Michael seemed to be the same person that he was before he got famous, but the fact that they hadn't spoke in twelve years surely didn't help that much. There Michael sat on the kitchen floor, in a house that was once his, glancing up at a friend that he once knew for every day of his life since the first grade. That was when it hit Michael how much he'd changed, how much he matured. The long blue hair became short and tame black spikes. His overspoken self-confidence had shriveled up into a modest ball of long harbored negative emotion. There he sat, humbled in front of his friend. Not his wife. Not his daughter. Not his cousin. He was about to kill himself, and the only person around who could possibly help was someone he hadn't seen in nearly half of his life. 'Uh... hey, Eddie.' The uncomfortable silence created a tension that one could almost actually see and feel, it was so foreboding. It wasn't so bad when Michael had to deal with it with his cousin; at least in that situation he'd usually know of something to say, but was just too lazy to deal with the potential neverending reply she'd more than likely give him. But this time, the silence was much different; he truly couldn't think of what to say, nothing that could explain what he was doing trespassing his friend's house. 'Fuck yourself for this, Michael,' he thought bitterly to himself. 'I'm going to remind myself to ram my head through a wall, preferably one in the wife's house, since I still actually give a shit about this one. That way Jaden will probably punch me in the face for it, and maybe it'll jar some sense into my fucking head. Yeah. That sounds like a perfect plan.' Michael's eyes glazed over as he tried to plan out a method of sneaking out of his former home, all the while his eyes remained looking in Etienne's direction. Although Eddie assumed his friend was staring off into space at that point, it only made him wonder further what was going on. Michael Mercer, his former friend and once a badass wrestler snuck into his house and had a nervous breakdown. But why? Etienne's mouth hung open for a moment as he tried to think of something to say; any way to properly question what Michael was doing, or even thinking. However, all that came out was... 'Do you like, want some French toast or something?' Michael snapped out of his daze, and blinked slowly. 'Of course I don't want any pussy French toast.' 'Okay, so the Michael I know is still around... I wonder if that's a good thing.' 'Hm,' Eddie answered slowly, 'I guess I'll just get you a cigarette and some coffee then.' Eddie leaned down and took the knife Michael had dropped to the floor. He looked at it, then at Michael, then back at the knife. 'Maybe it's best I don't ask what he was going to do with this.' He placed the knife in the sink, and then searched through a couple of cabinets to find a half full Maxwell's coffee can. He set up the filter in the coffee maker, poured a load of coffee beans in, and started boiling some water. Michael watched his friend do all of that, still seated comfortably on the yellow and white tiled kitchen floor. 'Nice coffee machine,' Michael noted, 'mine sucks.' Eddie glanced at his friend and tilted his head. 'Thanks for that observation,' he replied, before he promptly grabbed a pack of Newports off the nearby counter and lobbed it in Michael's direction. Michael's unnaturally dark brown eyes gazed at the pack, and at one of the cigarettes as they slid out into view. 'I don't smoke,' he mumbled. 'Don't you have any beer?' 'Sorry,' Etienne retorted, eyes concentrated on the coffee maker. 'I'm designated either to cigarettes or Pinot Noir. I'm sure you can understand why.' Michael blinked, allowing the remark to sink in. 'Pinot Noir... no beer, but Pinot... Noir...' Michael threw the cigarettes across the room. His headache had just worsened. 'Do you at least have any strawberry pocky?' 'Never tried that stuff before,' Eddie confessed. 'Can't find any stores that sell it.' Etienne turned to face Michael, and he could have sworn he noticed Michael's complexion form a faint shade of red. 'You... deprived... fu-' The kettle over the stove began to whistle, interrupting the end of Michael's sentence. 'You were saying?' Eddie smiled. ~~~***~~~ 'I guess allowing the wife to dictate what I do with my life has gotten to me,' Michael mumbled as he sipped his light and sweet coffee. His friend sat across from him on a comfortable leather sofa, while Michael had leaned back into a dark green Lay-Z-Boy. 'I figured that all I wanted was a World title reign, and after I finally got it last year I figured I was done with everything. I could retire in peace and just kick back, maybe get an acting gig here and there.' Michael sighed and tapped his coffee mug with his fingers. 'I guess it wasn't until lately that I realized I haven't done everything I wanted to do as a wrestler yet.' Eddie blinked. 'What do you mean?' he questioned. 'If you won the highest ranked title in the world of wrestling, what else do you have to wrestle for? I'd say just take your wife and celebrate in greener pastures, but then again I'm French and, well... do as the French do, I guess.' Michael shrugged and shook his head slowly. 'I wish I could decipher everything myself. Lately though, I've just been feeling mindfucked whenever I try to make sense of how I'm feeling. All I know is that I have one way to vent, and that one way is through wrestling.' 'Understandably so,' Etienne replied. 'You've been on TV for awhile now. My girlfriend used to watch your matches religiously. SAWF fanatic, you see.' 'Tell her I said that I'm flattered,' Michael answered as he drank his coffee, careful enough not to burn his mouth. 'The one woman who matters though is probably going divorce me... again.' 'Yeah... I guess she doesn't like you wrestling much, eh?' 'I guess you could say that,' Michael said, voice dripping with sarcasm. 'Shit, she probably packed her bags and left already.' 'Sucks,' Eddie replied with a frown. 'Eh, women are usually like that sometimes, though. They profess their love for you, but if you do what you want, they'll hate you forever for it. It's nothing new.' 'Didn't say it was,' Michael mumbled. 'I just wish I knew how to explain why I feel like I gotta wrestle again.' Etienne nodded in approval. He never actually felt the way Michael did at that moment, but he could understand how confused he must have felt. If he chose to be with his wife, Michael would basically betray the one thing he grew up with. And if he chose wrestling, he would lose a woman he undoubtedly loved a lot. What was a twentyfive year old to do? 'Well, you can stay here until you figure out what you're going to do, if you want,' Etienne offered. It was the least he could do, considering the man had been contemplating suicide. hell, maybe it'd be the perfect chance to catch up on the years since they last saw eachother. Michael must have been thinking the same thing. 'Yeah. Yeah, that's cool.' ~~~***~~~ (03-01-05) Until the end of February, Michael spent his days sleeping on the sofa, and his nights were confined to silently walking around the home and thinking about the past. He almost felt like a vampire after a week- night after night, he tried to remember what the true reason why he wrestled was. What was sad, though, was that with all the stories of why he started wrestling that he told media over the years, he forgot what the real story was all about. It made him look at his life as a whole, and soon he realized how pitiful he was. 'I wanted to wrestle because my uncle was a wrestler, and a damn good one at that. He taught me how to be as good as him, and in a way, I guess you could say I surpassed his talent. So I guess he was the reason I wrestled.' 'I wanted to wrestle because I simply want to prove I am the best fighter in the world today. Shit, you think Mike Tyson biting off someone's ear is extreme? I superplexed a six hundred pound fatass for the world to see. You haven't seen shit until you've seen Michael fucking Mercer in action, trust me.' 'I wanted to wrestle because... if I don't find a way to fight without being arrested, I think I'll do something I'm going to regret.' Michael turned on the dim bathroom light and stared into his own reflection. Gone was the flamboyant outer shell and inner soul of Michael fuckin' Mercer- all that was left was for Michael to stare at who he really was- Michael James Mercer, the true person. He was the man who didn't want any titles- just bloodshed. Anything to take his mind off killing whoever was responsible for the loss of his parents. Well, at least he was on the road to straightening things out. Michael left the house that night. He decided it best that he didn't wake his friend, and instead simply left a post it note stuck outside of Eddie's bedroom door- the one that used to be his. Thanks for helping me remember, friend. Michael
