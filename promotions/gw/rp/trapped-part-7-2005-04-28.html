Trapped (Part 7)

28 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28th April 2005 Image? It has been increasingly important in our world today. Everywhere we look we see beautiful men and women on billboards advertising some new perfume. We see beautiful people on the television, all making us feel that little bit worse about ourselves. No matter how much you try to deny it, image is important in this world. It is amazing where a handsome face can get you, all it takes is a flirtatious smile and before you know it you have yourself a promotion. Image is one of the reasons I am thankful that I wear this mask. Now don’t get me wrong as I said before I am a good looking guy underneath this mask but I would hate the fact that my image is what got me where I am. Okay looks don’t have much to do with my job, in fact you are lucky to hold onto your looks in this line of work but do be pushed up the roster because of looks or because you flirted with the right people is just wrong. Wearing this mask takes away my looks and makes people concentrate on my fighting and nothing else. When I look around at the roster I see a lot of guys and girls who are not obsessed about their image that is apart from one man, a man that sticks out to me for other reasons than simply image, that man is Mike Biddle? Biddle thinks he is god’s gift to the world of women. Everywhere he goes he thinks he is the centre of attention. He probably spends more time checking himself in the mirror than he does actually training. I don’t care if he is a former PTC Extreme Champion, I don’t care what he has done in the past, all I care about is what he knows now, the location of my family. I can’t take seeing Biddle’s arrogant smile anymore, every night it haunts my dreams, every night I see his ugly face, it is all that is on my mind. It has become a matter of transference, for the first couple of months I had no one to blame but Doctor Wood and I could not necessarily fight him and then I figure out that Biddle is involved in it all and all my anger switches to Biddle because now at least I can take my anger out on someone in a way that is considered legal! I hate feeling like this I hate the fact that hate is the only emotion I have left, hate is what is driving me, hate is what is making my judgments and hate is what is driving me towards Evolution? Evolution is possibly what is keeping me from breaking down; it is a place where I channel my hate. As I have said before I am not a fan of the emotion of hate as it leads to misjudgement but since it is the only thing that is running through my mind I have to work with it. However I think I will need more than hate to topple the “man” that is Corvus. Corvus is someone I have faced before, at the beginning of this Platinum Series in fact, back then I was a different man I had a family but after that match it all changed. Now I don’t know what I am, all I know is to fight and keep fighting. Corvus you had a shot at the World title at Clash Of The Titans, you are seen as the hottest thing in the GW today but do you know what? I don’t care, I don’t care what you have done, I don’t care how the rest of the roster sees you, all I care about is the match. I do not fear you Corvus and I proved that in our last meeting and I will prove it again at Evolution? “He is coming round.” Slowly I opened my eyes, my mouth felt like the Sahara desert and my head felt like it was floating. Blurry figures surrounded me, I tried to rub my eyes to gain a clearer view but I couldn’t move my arms or my legs for that matter. Slowly but surely my eye sight returned and the blurry figures snapped into focus. Two surgeons and a nurse stood over me; I was lying down on a trolley bed being pushed down a long white corridor. Suddenly next to the nurse on my left hand side walked Wood who looked down at me with what was a slightly worried look “How did it go?” Wood asked as he looked over at the two surgeons. “Absolutely fine,” One of the surgeons reassured him, “He is in perfect medical condition.” I mumbled trying to speak but nothing came out, all four of them looked down at me. “What is it Adam?” The nurse said gently. “Www?” I mumbled again struggling to say something. “Get him some water nurse.” The surgeon closest to me said. The nurse quickly disappeared and within seconds reappeared with a plastic cup filled with water. She placed it to my mouth and I took a few sips of water. “Now slowly Adam, what were you trying to say?” The nurse asked gently again. “Www’What happened to me?” I asked finally getting some words out. “You’ve just had a check-up Adam.” Came Wood’s reply who I didn’t really want to answer my question. “Why does my head feel like it isn’t attached to my body?” “That will be the affects of the anaesthetic we gave you earlier,” One of the surgeons said. “Why did I need an anaesthetic for a check-up?” I asked still feeling dizzy. “Erm?” The surgeon closest to me hesitated looking at Wood. “We needed to check your throat for any blockages, so we were sure you were in perfect health.” The explanation didn’t make much sense to me at the time but in the state I was I didn’t try and challenge it. “Get some rest Adam; you have a big few days ahead.” Wood said regaining that arrogant smirk on his face. Again if I could I would have jumped up and smacked Wood across the face but the allure of sleep was too much as I closed my eyes and let my mind wander? I stood in a dark black room in front of a mirror that was illuminated by a beam of light that didn’t seem to have any source. The strangest thing about this mirror was that it didn’t seem to reflect my image. “Hello!” I shouted into the darkness. “No one can hear you.” A familiar voice replied. “Who is that?” I heard laughter from the darkness. “What is so funny?” I asked feeling unnerved by the situation. “It really is a sign of madness you know?” “What is?” There was a strange silence as I heard footsteps approaching me, I darted my eyes all around but couldn’t see anything. Suddenly my eye was drawn to the mirror as a figure walked to the centre of it. I looked in shock as staring back at me from the mirror was none other than myself, Adam Myers! “When you talk to yourself.” The other Myers smirked finishing his sentence. Now you may think my reaction strange as it is not the strangest thing to see your reflection in a mirror. However it is strange when your reflection starts talking to you. “What the”!”!” I said astonished. “Oh calm down Adam, you already know this isn’t real so don’t start having a fit.” The other Myers said staring at me still with the grin on the face. “Who are you?” “You.” “No, no, I am me!” “But I am you as well.” “But’but’how?” “Jesus Adam, take a look around you. Does this place look like your cell” Does this place look like any sort of place in Wood’s laboratory? No it doesn’t and you know why? Because it is a dream!” Even though it had to be spelt out to me, it hit me like a ton of bricks as I began settle down. “What has happened to you Adam?” The other Myers asked no longer with the smirk on his face but a concerned look. “I don’t know. I just...” “This isn’t us Adam, this is not what we envisioned all those years ago. We were supposed to be living life to the full, you remember” Take the risk and forget everything else? You remember that saying Adam? That is what you used to live by, that is what got you into the GW. Now look at you, locked up in some cell like a caged animal, what sort of life is that?” “I know all that but there is nothing I can do about it is there” I signed that contract and if I don’t go along with it then I lose everything.” “Well we need to find away to get out of that contract don’t we?” “But how?” I asked hearing the desperation in my voice. “We’ll find a way, don’t worry.” “What do I’I mean we do in the meantime?” “What we do best.” The reflection replied smirking again. “Fight!” I replied with my own smirk. “One more thing before you go Adam.” The reflection said looking down at my right arm. “What?” I asked looking down at my arm also. “Do you really think you needed a general anaesthetic for a check-up?” “What?” I asked confused holding up my arm but the reflection was gone. As I drew my arm up closer to my face, I noticed a small bump on my arm. As I touched it with my left arm it began to beep and flash. The flashes became quicker and quicker until the formed into one bright light? I jerked awake back in my steel room in a sweat. I looked down again at my right arm and again I felt the same pump? What had they done to me?!”!
