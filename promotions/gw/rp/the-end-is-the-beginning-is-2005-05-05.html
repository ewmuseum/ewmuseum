The end is the beginning is ...

5 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/05/05 Time will remember And time will find you And time will turn around Everything you’ve done You will bend In the end - Mercy Drive {The End} Here I am, sitting in the lock-up at the Las Vegas Police Department. It’s getting well close to sunrise and they are still trying to work out some bullshit charge to stick me with. I’m really not very keen on staying here much longer if they don’t get their shit together. Of all the places I’d like to meet final death, it sure as hell is not in a police station. That is a lot like Elvis dying on the toilet ... how degrading. There’s a little bit of sunlight creeping towards the window that has bars to ensure that nobody can escape the cell. That would be well and good for a mortal but I could simply snap those bars like a twig if I chose to. However, I don’t really want to bring any unneeded attention to myself. It gets messy that way. “Corvus, looks like you are free to go.” I really was in a bind since it was close to becoming daylight and getting back to my place would take longer then I possibly had. I don’t think that I could get a phone call out to my brother, or even Deacon to come and get me. It was sure a warm, fuzzy, feeling to know that I was moments away from being fricasseed. How the pressure of things weigh you down when you’re this close to being f--ked in the ass without even getting a kiss for it. One of the officers unlocked the cell and I gave him a rather nasty glare as I slowly walked passed him to speak to the detective who told me that I was free to go. She looked me over and handed me a plastic bag with my personal belongings. “I don’t know how you do it, but you sure are one lucky guy. Just remember that I’ll be keeping an eye on you but you already knew that before, didn’t you?” “I don’t believe in luck, Miss Van Buren, but as far as you watching me ... wouldn’t that be considered voyeurism” I thought you were one of those by the book cops but I could be wrong?” The look on her face was priceless as I could tell she wanted to watch me but not because I was a possible murderer. Every time that we were in the same room her heart beat was very rapid. She was intrigued by what I claimed I was and there might have even been that hint in her mind that I could be the real deal. What can I say? I’m just one of those people that draw others to them even without using any of my abilities. I’m not going to go out of my way to say I’m a chick magnet or whatever most of the bubble headed wrestlers these days claim to be. Anyway, it’s time to get the hell out of this place before I get hungry. “I trust you’ll have a better time with him than I did, Miss de Vorace.” My head snapped to my right after hearing the detective say the name. Sure enough there was Rhianna standing there. She seemed to have toned her looks down from the last time I saw her, not so whorish this time. Perhaps she listened to what I had to say and it finally seeped into her thick skull. “I’ll make sure that he stays out of trouble, Detective, I can assure you.” She smiled as I reluctantly walk toward her as she finalizes whatever it was she was doing to get me out of that craphole and we proceed to go outside and get into the Rolls that she brought with her. The driver that accompanied her opened the doors for us and we got in just before any traces of smoldering would present themselves. I needed to eat and also wanted to sleep as well but being in Rhianna’s clutches made me wonder just what she wanted in return for getting me out of a jam that I could have done myself with just a little more effort involved. {The Beginning} I sat waiting in my car for my brother to find me in the airport parking lot. I wasn’t about to go in there and have to deal with any of the fans that might recognize me from my music days or even from my wrestling career. When you can just relax in your car listening to some tunes, then you know things are just going to be good. The top is down and it is a gorgeous night in Sin City, and even though the weather has no bearing on me since I don’t have a body temperature, I can at least appreciate a nice breeze that blows by. I hear something being thrown in my car and it is followed by my brother jumping in the car to sit next to me. He punched me in the arm as you could say it was a family greeting of sorts between us. “It’s my ever popular big brother, what’s new” I guess I shouldn’t have to ask that since I hear and read a lot about you these days. I almost thought you would be here with Lia to pick me up but ... ? I started the car before he could finish, drowning out anything he might have to say about my wife ... or should I say ex-wife at least in my mind anyway. There were so many things I could say in return to comment about the situation but I wanted it to be the right one. I mean after all I was supposedly sulking over my humanity or whatever it was the deluded bitch said. “I have other things to worry about right now, dear brother than my lunatic wife. She’s probably off fleshcrafting a penis and balls for herself since she now thinks she’s impressing people by stabbing me in the back.” “Ouch, that was pretty harsh ... funny but harsh.” Not all of Rhianna’s words fell upon deaf ears. She might have been right that I should fight back against my former flame and at least gain back some of the dignity she tried to rob from me all these years. I never lie down in a fight and that is what Shadow wanted, she wouldn’t be disappointed. My brother may have heard some things but I doubt he knew the whole picture with Celine being back and my desire to find out what exactly became of my son. It would be a lot for him to digest in such a short time, I’m still really trying to chew on it in a sense. “I have a funny feeling that there’s more going on than what you are telling me. Would you mind speeding me up on a few things here?” “Do you want the long version or the abridged version, which I would highly recommend at the moment?” He took a few moments to decide if he wanted to get all the facts right away or if he wanted to hear a boring story. It was a good thing my brother didn’t like to listen to stories that took forever and a night to tell so he opted for me to tell him in as little words as possible what was going on. The look on his face when I said, “My sire is back and one of the reasons I called for you to come back is because I need you to help me with a few things. One of them being I want to find out what happened to Brennan and the other is I need you on my side, there are forces in GW that I just simply cannot topple on my own.” I watched as Ash reached over and turned the music that I had playing off. I don’t think it was disturbing him but he needed to think about what I said to him. He had wanted out of the wrestling business for good after a failed run in Wrestling Midwest. But to me, it was more than just wrestling with Global because there were forces at work that were just a bit wrong, even for me. Even admitting that I needed help was a huge step for me. I was considered the one man who never needed help with anything with as strong and crazy as I have been. Some battles are just impossible to go alone and my brother and I have waged many wars together and have always succeeded. “Ok, so let me get this straight ... the one who made you is back” I didn’t think you knew who made you?” “That may have been what I said before, but I knew it was her when I saw her and felt it within my body. It is something you cannot deny and it is her ... I just didn’t think with the way she left me (that) she could have lasted this long.” “What reason did she make her presence known to you for” Obviously it wasn’t because she was taking a survey on her siring skills; does James know about it?” It then occurred to me that I hadn’t talked to James “Deacon” Black for quite a while, but I knew he took a trip over to Europe to take care of a few things. His return would have been something I would have known about as we were practically brothers in a sense ... we shared the same sire and some of the same blood. “No, he’s over in Europe at the moment and he hasn’t notified me that he is back. I’m hoping that she decides to take off before he finds out that she’s here in Vegas. That is on confrontation I do not want to be involved in.” His finding out that Celine was still alive would be very caustic. Knowing him, he’d risk his own undead vessel to take her down and likely die in the process without any help. As much as I hated her and what she wanted me to be, I couldn’t conceive the notion of placing final death upon her; it wasn’t my decision to make. We were now at the house and it wasn’t long before we were inside and he threw his bags on the couch and jumped over it, sprawling out like he owned the place. Ash was always welcome in my haven and there were no restrictions on what he could and couldn’t do there ... within reason that is. By the look on his face I knew he was doing some serious thinking about me wanting him to help out with GW and also looking for information concerning Brennan. He wouldn’t say it but I knew that he wished I had not brought the topic up. One of the things about still having a lot of humanity left in me is that you tend to annoy those around you without realizing it because they don’t understand or feel what you do because they have lost it completely. At least if he wouldn’t help me, it gave me a chance to take my mind off of everything for a few days before he left for Minneapolis again. When the boys get together nothing is ever safe around us, especially the girls. “If you’re going to convince me to help you out, I need you to really think about what kind of can of worms you would possibly be opening by doing all this. You don’t eat, sleep, defecate, or breathe anymore ... none of this should be your concern.” “Ash, that sounds too much like Lia ... don’t make me punch you in the mouth. I just need to know if we have any descendants out there. Are you not even slightly curious if we have great (add a lot of those in) ... grandchildren and in your case nieces and nephews?” I couldn’t help but feeling that I was giving a sales pitch like you would see someone who sells used cars might try to do for a customer on their lot. My brother was just as stubborn as I was so I knew I had to really get him to understand the complexity of the situation. “You are asking a lot of me, Eryc, and you can’t expect me to just jump on this. We could possibly uncover things we don’t want to know about our family. Just look at our half brothers ... they ruined the family line and in a sense so did we when we became vampires.” “I understand that, but the knowledge of knowing that my son went on to have a happy life would be all I needed to know and nothing else. That is as far as I would go with it and at least trace through history to see if there is any blood of the Martel family that still exists today.” What was I thinking? What could I possibly do if I found anyone that was from my family tree today? It wasn’t like I could just go up to them and say: “Hey, I’m your long lost so and so and I’ve been living on this earth for over a thousand years ... how’s life?” I was crazy even thinking it. It wasn’t any fun going back and forth with what I should and should not be doing. I had a set of rules to live by as kindred and I was still trying to stay under the rules of the mortal world and there in lies the conflict of oneself. “We should just think about this a little bit at a time. For now the best idea would be for us to go out and have a good time because I’m getting hungry.” “I’m a little hesitant about taking you anywhere, the last time I did that you ended up with crooked security guards trying to rough you up. We need to stay as low profile as possible and you know that.” He was on to something about being hungry and it was a good while since I last had anything to eat. What better place than a club to find a quick meal to sustain myself? My brother knew when I needed a break; it was good having him around. “It was a screwed up situation that is all I’m going to say. I know you want to go out ... you stick yourself in this house all the time other than when you need to feed; you can’t expect to be happy in any capacity by carrying on that way.” Happiness, what would that be? For a kindred it is few and far between. I really don’t intend to dwell on it because I do not consider myself someone to be happy but I prefer to consider myself being content. “Grab what you need so we can go.” “I’m taking a separate vehicle. You really never know what I’m going to end up doing so I don’t want to get stuck having to leave when you decide it is convenient.” “If you get into anything you shouldn’t be don’t expect me to bail you out this time, little brother.” (Somewhere In Between) I left him to try and catch up to me because I was taking the bike tonight. It was one of the many things that I could get just enough attention to find a decent meal that I wouldn’t regret later in the evening. After the last time I fed on a human, I realized that I needed to be more careful. Drugs are bad ... even for vampires. (There’s your public service message for the day kids!) Once I parked the bike and stood outside of the club I was already beginning to wonder what I let my brother talk me into. The overall look of the place stank of trouble but at least the music was pumping through the building just as I liked it. The bouncers at the door recognized me because I was let in without hassle. Another perk of being one badass son of a bitch that is on television for all to see. The club had this industrial look to it like you would see in the original Crow movie. Overall it was a dive and I don’t know how they could operate legally without the fear of being shut down for building violations. Patrons were dancing all over the place and in some of the darker corners a few kids were making out like it was going out of style ... another abortion waiting to happen in the good “ol U.S. of A. I scanned the room looking for potential donors (vampire term for those who didn’t know) but the pickings at the moment looked rather slim. My next step was to just see what happened as I found a table to sit at and see what came from it. I also wanted to keep an eye out to make sure that my brother made it in thanks in part to my little mental suggestion to the guards on my way in (to the club). I wasn’t paying much attention when a gorgeous looking young woman came up to my table. It took a few moments for her to get my attention but when I turned towards her I knew I had my meal ticket. She was about 5’8? and real thin with black hair and a pretty pale skin complexion. Not exactly your typical goth but maybe more like a wannabe ... there were plenty of those roaming the club too. She had this school girl type thing going which reminded me a little bit of the whiny airhead from that popular group ... I think it is Evanescence or something like that. I really don’t pay much attention to music these days since I am no longer playing (music) myself. “You look familiar, have I seen you someplace before?” “I’m not sure, where do you think you have seen me before ... it is my first time coming to this establishment.” “I think I might have seen you on television before ... aren’t you in a band or something?” “Used to be ... but I do other things now.” “Mind if I ask what?” “I get paid to beat people up live on cable television.” “So you mean like boxing or something, or wrestling” I think my cousin likes some wrestling thing that’s based here in Vegas but I can’t remember the name of it.” “Global Wrestling or GW for short ...” “That’s it! My cousin watches it every week with my brothers! Who are you on there since most guys don’t go by their real names or anything from what I was told.” “My name is what I wrestle by ... I am Corvus.” I was starting to think this conversation was going to take all night with the twenty questions I was playing with her. But she had stars in her eyes after I told her who I was. Fame tends to bring out friends and relatives you never knew you had. I could feel it deep down that it wouldn’t take long for me to get her somewhere that I could feed. She wasn’t giving off an easy vibe but she wasn’t very bright so a little mind manipulation would do wonders for my benefit. “Oh wow ... you’re the vampire guy! I love watching you fight!” “Thank you, I appreciate hearing that from the public. Normally I don’t go out because I am very busy but I picked the right night to do so. Would you like to join me?” “Sure, if you are not too busy?” “Have a seat and a good time is all I require. I haven’t caught your name yet; would you mind revealing it to me?” “Kelly, my name is ... Kelly.” Of all the girls I ended up having the chance to feed on I end up with one named Kelly. That is one of the most basic and dull names in the human vocabulary ... it’s on par with the Bob’s and Sue’s and Tom’s of the world. I should make this quick and I’m starved anyway. “The pleasure is all mine, Kelly. How about a drink?” Little did she know that the pleasure would be all mine. That’s just how I do you could say. In the realm of the kindred there are many win/win situations and hardly ever are there any losing ones unless a hunter gets you but that’s only if you are stupid. Yes, stupid vampires are the easiest to kill. (Now you know why Brick had an easy time a few months ago) “That would be great. A rum and Coke if that wouldn’t be too much trouble?” “No trouble at all, coming right up.” I cast a glance over towards the bar and the bar man poured a drink and sent one of the workers to bring it over to my table. The girl set it in front of Kelly and I spoke my thanks and she walked back to the bar. It might have looked odd that I didn’t have anything sitting in front of me and I was prepared for any questions that would be fielded my way. If only I could publish a handbook ... “You’re not going to have a drink” I thought that is what people came to a club to do ... drink or dance themselves into stupidity?” “I’m not very fond of alcohol. I come for the atmosphere to watch and observe people. It may sound creepy but on a psychology standpoint it makes sense.” “You’re not one of those straight edge people are you?” “No ... heck no, that would be the last thing on earth that I would ever be.” The last thing I ever wanted to be compared to is one of those straight edge emo kids. They may have the right idea but they are going about it all wrong and they dress like morons too ... no wonder my brother likes them a little bit. I let her take a few drinks from the glass and figure that it is time for me to work my wiles on her before I starve to death. I use my finger to motion her closer so I can whisper in her ear. I doubted this would work but if it didn’t then I was going to use some heavy mind control. She gets close enough for me to ask her if she’s ever had any vampire in her and if she didn’t would she like some? Her face turned bright red from blushing and I would have thought that it would get me slapped in the face but maybe this girl really wanted to see what I was about. The vampire mystique really does have its advantages and I use them quite well. It’s a mastered art you could say. I stood from my seat and motioned to her with my head before holding out my hand. The thirst began to really pain inside me and I didn’t want to leave too much of a mess behind so I had to feed pretty quickly. We made it undisturbed to the ladies room where I cautiously let her walk in and I followed in behind her, locking the door so nobody could disturb anything that would be going on. It would have become bothersome to have to deal with witnesses and if there was a choice I’d rather get rid of a body before I left a witness. Needless to say I found it interesting that Kelly was an aggressor and pulled me into the stall. I didn’t bother wasting time with taking off clothes or any such nonsense. I could have just fed and ran but I figured I might as well get out of it what I could. Too bad when I was finished she wouldn’t remember the wild time she had in the bathroom when a vampire ripped her thong off and had a wild time then drank her blood. Sometimes life is just hit or miss like that. During the middle of things, I sank my fangs into her and the ecstasy of the dark kiss put her in a blissful state. She reached the closest thing to nirvana and I drank from her like a certain Irishmen would while standing under a Guinness tap. I took what I needed and nothing more. In any event I could always find her again if I decided to come back to this dive for shits and giggles. I straightened myself out making sure everything was back in order and looked her in the eyes, working my mojo. “You will not remember anything prior to sitting down and having a drink with me. As far as you remember you had a nice conversation and when we were finished I left. As soon as you hear the door open and close, your mind will be blank.” “Ok ... ? I licked the wound closed on her neck and any traces of blood on my mouth that I could feel. I moved passed her and exited the stall and unlocked the door and slipped out. I found my brother as I worked my way back through the crowd and he was dancing with some wild looking brunette with pink streaks in her hair. “I’ve had enough to drink for the evening. I’ll see you when you get home; if there’s any trouble, call me.” “Sure thing ... I shouldn’t be too long.” {Towards the End} I was relaxing on the couch in the front room reading the paper when I heard the call button on the front gate being sounded. I went to the door and spoke through the intercom. “How can I help you?” “It’s Detective Van Buren from the LVPD, would you mind letting me in we need to talk.” “Sure, I was hoping you would have found my jacket by now. I’m opening the gate, pull right in.” I opened the gates and watched through the door as a van pulled in through the driveway. I thought to myself that usually police officers come in squad cars but maybe they were all in use going on a donut run or something like that. As soon as the Detective came to the door I opened the main one but did not let her go any further than that. I wasn’t able to fully read her thoughts and that spelled trouble with a capital T. I kept my arms behind my back and little did I know they would be that way for a while. “I’ve received some interesting information that concerns you so I’m going to ask you a few questions and expect that you will answer them truthfully.” “I take it this means you didn’t find my coat” I’m disappointed in you ... how did you manage to make Detective if you can’t even find a simple coat?” I don’t think she saw much humor in my words but at least I tried. Humans can be so bland at times; it really bores you to think that you can go through it every day for as long as you can stay alive. “Very funny, you should have taken up being a comedian instead of a wrestler. Let’s get back on track here. Where have you been tonight?” “Sitting around reading on the couch, why do you ask?” “I have received several reports that you were at a club earlier in the evening. It just so happens that a woman who was also at that club tonight was found in the alley with bite marks on her neck. We are still waiting on some preliminary testing but since you are a prime suspect, I’m going to have to ask you to come downtown with me.” The first thing on my mind was “shit, how in the hell did this happen?” Sure I had a meal but I did not leave any evidence behind and I didn’t kill her and leave her in the alley. I didn’t want to have to go down to the station but I figured I would handle this their way so there wouldn’t be any raised suspicion. I held my finger up to let her give me a second so I could text my brother to tell him that I was in a jam but I would probably get out of it before the sun came up. After I finished I opened the screen door but locked up the house and stepped outside. “I’m sorry I have to do this to you ... Corvus, but until we can get things sorted out, I don’t have any other choice.” “I’m even sorrier that you have to do this ... I was just beginning to like you. I guess you won’t be too upset if I don’t send you a Christmas card now will you?” “Ok, enough with the jokes. Walk to the van and put your arms up against the sides.” “Fine ... but no funny business got it” I’ve seen and heard things on television about the police.” I did as I was asked and walked to the van then placed my arms up against the sides. She quickly checked (frisked) me for anything that might be considered a weapon. I bet she enjoyed it too. My hands were brought behind my back where I was handcuffed and then lead to the back of the van. The doors opened and I was escorted into it. I really hated most vehicles because they were so constrictive because of my height ... blasted contraptions. I had the whole ride to the station to figure out how I was going to handle this. The one thing I knew for certain was that I had to be cunning otherwise I was going to end up in a position that I really would not like. To be continued ...
