The Time Is Now (Crossfire #2)

24 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/24/04 Sage Sabbot Times ago, if you heard my name, you’d shiver in fear. Chills would run down your spine. And the hairs on your forearm would stand up. I used to be a GW World Champion. The GW International Champion. The GW Platinum Champion. The GW Cruiserweight Champion. I’ve accomplished more in one year of my career then people have accomplished in their whole entire lives. The Nevermore A group of sadistic individuals. Individuals that annihilated and destroyed anyone or anything that opposed their power. “we ruled the days. “we rules the nights. The Harvesters The few chosen ones. Chosen due to their strength and power. Chosen because they possessed what nobody else had. They don’t call me The Harvester of Tears for nothing. I sit back in front of a fireplace in the house of Soma. “the time is now. This is the first time you’ve seen Sabbot’s face outside of the Global Wrestling world, besides what we’ve watched after Sabbot died. The time is now, after Sabbot returned from the Global Wrestling. After he shocked the world by returning to the grave. After the battles that he and Soma have conquered over the past couple weeks. After the match of the year contender involving him, Soma, Partridge, and Josh. After fuel was put on the proverbial fire of anger that is, Sage Sabbot. The time is now. I watch, as the flames blanket the two logs of wood inside the stone fireplace. The flames spark and crack, somehow this is supposed to be relaxing. The light of the fire dims the room in an orange aura. I sit there, in silence, like Soma told me. Watching, waiting for a chance to control your life. All of your life, people have always told you to take life as it comes. I despise that saying, in my opinion’you shouldn’t take life as it comes. You take it how you want it. And that is what I’ve always done. In my life, I won countless belts. I’ve dominated the Global Wrestling. I’ve destroyed those who challenge me. I’ve earned the right to be a Harvester. All of this, I’ve done by myself. I didn’t wait for life to hand me anything. I took some initiative and controlled my destiny. Sitting here, supposedly relaxing, it gives me enough time to think about everything that is going on around me. Whenever I have this chance, all I can think about is The Echelon. I could have had my shot at Reno. Blasted Rokku, I never had my shot at him at High Noon. If Reno told me that the only way to get to him is to destroy Rokku, I would have killed him myself. Rokku was just a stepping stone for me to get to Reno. In my opinion, that is the only reason why I came back to life. To seek revenge on him for what he’s done to me. Nobody is going to get in my way, not Rokku, not Josh, nobody. And with Leinard Soma’s help, I can do anything. He knows secret things about The Echelon and how they run things, that nobody knows. Having him around will make things very easy considering The Upper Echelon was formed with the help of Soma Enterprises. Soma promised me that we’d get our revenge on them’and destroy the rest of the evil that lies in the Global Wrestling. Watching Rokku die only brought back memories to me. It brought flashbacks of gurgling my own blood and trying to hear what they were saying through the bones that were broken in my skull. As soon as Rokku died, I remembered the sound of the gun, firing at my head. I remembered how it felt, knowing I was going to die. I remembered each and every ounce of pain I endured that night. And it pissed me off. It made me want to get my hands on them even more. I was backstage that night with Soma and I was furious. He taught me patience and that we’d have to wait until the right time. If not, our plan would ruin. I worry if I’ll ever get my chance to get my hands on Reno. But the Bible says to not worry about anything. To let God take your worries in his hands and take care of them for you. It’s hard to adjust to this new lifestyle. I” m so used to taking control of my own life. God also says to love your brother and forgive your enemies. But I’ll never forgive The Echelon. Just seeing Holmes, Zero and Reno backstage, just makes my blood boil. I’ve prepared a mission’and that is to destroy them. Once they lie on their back in a pool of their own blood. Once their blood stains my hands. And once their eyes will never open ever again, then my mission is over. With Soma’s help, he promised that we will do just that. But for now, there are other missions set in front of me’missions that I’ve made myself. “things that will send The Echelon a message that they will never forget” At Crossfire, I will once again represent Global Wrestling along with Leinard Soma against not one, not two, not three men’but four. We step into the ring against, well, representatives of another federation that can’t come close to the talent of Global Wrestling and the talent of Sage Sabbot. Back then, you’ll probably have to order it on VHS, I doubt DVDs were that big back then. You’ll find Team GW VS a team from another federation. Team GW consisted of “The Canadian Icon” Jean-Claude Dafoe, Christopher Camero, “The Ladies Man” Jay Robson, Brick, and me. I was put into the match by Bubba McCoy. You see, back then, I was a nobody. A rookie in the GW. Bubba had me fill in for somebody who was injured. At the end of the night, I was a star. When it comes to business and representing Global Wrestling, I am on top of my game. Together, four men that you’d never see become a tag team for one night and one night only, defeated wrestlers who were called “the best”. When we are risking everything we have and when we get to wear the GW name on our tights, then we give more then we have ever given. We drip more sweat then we ever have. And we bleed GW blood. RINGGGGG? RINGGGGG? I wrestle my cell phone out of my pocket. It’s harder to grab something out of your pocket when you are in a car, let alone Soma driving at what you think is 300 miles per hour. I end up grabbing it with my fingertips and pull it out. I fumble around and place it to my ear? “Sabbot?” “You ready?” “Your damn right I’m ready.” I don’t even have to ask who it is, I’ve heard this voice enough to know. “Good, because this is a big match.” “Don’t worry, you know you can count on me and Soma. We don’t play around.” “I know you don’t. I just want to make sure you both have your head in the game.” “Trust me, we won’t let you down.” “So wh’re’u’tra’br’ng?”””””” “Hey! I can’t hear you, you’re breaking up. We are in New York right now, taking care of some personal business before Crossfire. We’ll meet up with you in time. Later.” I press a button on my cell phone and put it back in my pocket. “Who was that?” I look over at Soma, who is driving like a maniac still. You’d think he has enough experience? “Papa McCoy.” “What does he want?” “Just wanted to wish us luck at Crossfire.” “Oh yeah” That’s nice of him.” I chuckle? “Yeah, too bad we won’t be needing it.” Soma joins in and shares a laugh. “I think Bubba relies on us to take care of these guys. He DID put us in a tag team handicap match against some of the best guys they have to offer.” “Well, he has relied on me before for interfed activities. He knows he can count on me. How the hell do handicapped tag matches work anyways?” “What do you mean?” “I mean, is it a tornado match, where everybody is in the ring at the same time” “or is it an actual tag match with one person in for each team” Is it elimination? Do they have two men in the ring with one of us?” “Beats me, those are some of the facts that we should have been informed about. Apparently Bubba has a lot of trust in us.” ??and we won’t let him down either. It really doesn’t matter, we’ll be the ones walking out on top of this. I’m not worrying at all. In fact, I have a little feeling inside my head that we are going to win this thing.” “How do you know?” I chuckle again. “Well, the same way I know that it’s 8 at night. “magic.” Soma shakes his head side to side, laughing?
