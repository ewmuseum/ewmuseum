Dragon?

18 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-18-06 February 13th 8:00 P.M. Dallas Texas The drops of rain hit the limo as it pulls into an alley. The limo driver opens the backdoor of the limo and a medium sized man steps out of the back he has black solid spiky hair with a black goatee. He is wearing a button up shirt with nice cargo pants and diamond watches, rings, and necklaces. “Mr. Hammer”. The limo driver says shutting the door the place you wanted to go sir. “Yes you can go now Edward”. The man says throwing the limo driver a stack of twenties. Yes sir?! The limo driver says walking towards the limo. “Edward before you go get me the Briefcase”. The Man says pointing to the back door of the limo. “Yes sir”! The limo driver says running towards the back door and opening it. “So Dragon happy 21st birthday or will be in a few days aye”! The limo driver says handing the Man named Dragon the suitcase. “The 28th of February will be my birthday”. Dragon says taking the suitcase. “Edward”. Dragon says. “Yes!” Edward says looking at Dragon. “I will mess you”! Dragon says hugging his limo driver. “Well when you’re ready call me and I will come”. Edward says trying to escape. “Ok!” Dragon says finally letting go. “See you sir”! Edward says getting in the limo and driving off in the thunder of the night. “Well its time for a family reunion without the family”. Dragon thinks and then chuckles. Dragon then goes in the lobby of Hotel Dueflae. “Yes”! The lobby man says. “I’m looking for the Hammer Brothers”. Dragon says looking the lobby man right in the eye. “There not here”. The Lobby man says looking at his computer. Nah it’s supposed to be a surprise stupid lobby man. Dragon says leaving and going in to the bar. Dragon walks up to the bartender. “Yes”! The bartender says. “Tell me when the Hammer Brothers get here”. Dragon says sitting in a seat. “K”! The man says turning to polish his cups. “Get me a drink two”! Dragon says turning the man back around making him break his cup after that he smirks then lays his head down. “Right away sir do you have a hotel here”! The Bartender says giving Dragon an evil look for breaking his cup. “Uh”! “Yeah”! “Gorn Ice Hammer”. Dragon replies. This well be charged to the Hammer Brothers account then. The man says giving the so-called Gimley Ice Hammer his first cup.
