She's The Blade: An Evil Monkey, A Dog, And A Ring

13 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 13, 2005 Mike felt helpless as he sat in his living room. William Bastafar had left town on a business trip and had left his idiotic wheelchair bound grandson, Carlos Fraga, in Mike’s care. Somehow Carlos had suckered Mike into watching some show called The Family Guy, and it was the most inane entertainment Mike had ever been faced with. “Mike,” Carlos said, “There’s an evil monkey in my closet too,” Carlos finished the sentence with a look of fear thickly spread across his fat face. Mike sighed in annoyance as he buried his face in his hands and quietly cursed William for forcing him to keep an eye on the boy. “There’s no such thing as evil monkeys you idiot,” Mike said through clenched teeth. He then grabbed the remote that rested next to him on the plush leather sofa and changed the channel to something slightly less idiotic, Mork and Mindy. Once the channel was changed Carlos looked down at his useless legs with a frown, it seemed that no matter how hard he tried no one would ever believe that an evil monkey bent on world domination lived within the confines of his closet. As Carlos was about to open his mouth and spew more idiotic drivel, the doorbell rang. Mike quickly rose from his seat and rushed towards the door, anything to get away from that disabled idiot for at least a moment. Mike opened the door and was both happy and aroused by who was on the other side. Lisa looked more attractive than usual, her long brown hair fell over her shoulders and her bangs seductively hung over her almond colored eyes. Mike was so lost in her beauty that he didn’t notice the very large poodle that obediently sat beside her. He leaned in and kissed her soft ruby lips. “I thought you had things to do today and wouldn’t be by until late tonight?” Mike asked with a smile glued to his face, he still hadn’t noticed the dog. “That was before I bought Jacque,” Lisa said with an excited tone as she pointed to the large white French poodle that sat beside her. Mike looked down and for the first time saw the large poodle. He reached out to pat the dog on the head but the dog barked at him and he quickly withdrew his hand. “So why did you get a dog?” Mike asked with a concerned look as the dog began to sniff his crotch. “I’ve always wanted a French poodle,” Lisa replied. She then looked at Mike with the look she always gave him when she wanted him to do something for her. “I need you to watch him for me while I’m out today. Can you do that baby?” Mike nearly died inside. First he had been stuck with Carlos for the day and now he was going to be stuck with some big stupid dog’he didn’t even like dogs! “How come Charles can’t take him?” Mike asked. “Because it’s his day off,” Lisa said, “Please take him for the day sweetie, he’s a good dog,” she begged. “Fine,” Mike agreed against his better judgment. Lisa handed him the leash as she leaned over to kiss his cheek. “Thank you baby,” she said, “I’ll be back later tonight.” She then turned and walked away. It was official, today was the worst day of Mike’s life. Mike closed the door and looked down at the dog. “If you shit on my floor I’m rubbing your nose in it,” Mike said with a stern voice. He unhooked the leash and the dog took off towards the living room. Mike sighed as he watched the dog run through his home. He ran his fingers through his thick blond locks as he wondered what he had done to piss God off this time. He then heard a loud scream that emanated from the living room. Mike slowly made his way into the living room and found Jacque propped up on Carlos, the reason Carlos had screamed was because the dog humped the poor slob’s leg with enough force to knock him out of the wheelchair. Mike broke out in laughter as he watched the poodle violate Carlos on the floor. “Save me!” Carlos exclaimed with great fear. After a few moments Mike was in control of his laughter and dragged the dog off of Carlos with one hand and helped the portly young man back into his wheelchair with the other. “Why did the dog do that?” Carlos asked as he shivered in fear, it was not the first time he had been violated but at least the dog was slightly better than the overweight, one-eyed, one-legged hooker that Mike had got him a few years ago. “Well,” Mike began, “he is a French poodle.” “Where did it come from?” Carlos asked. “Jacque is Lisa’s new dog,” Mike explained as he firmly gripped the dog’s collar and tried to prevent another disturbance, “she can’t watch him right now so I’m dog sitting.” Carlos squirmed for a moment as he searched for a comfortable position in his wheelchair; once Carlos was situated Mike let the dog go. Jacque began to sniff around the room. “Oh man,” Mike said with annoyance, “I think he needs to take a crap.” Mike then turned and looked at Carlos with a thoughtful look. “What?” Carlos asked. Then it suddenly dawned on Carlos and he began to wheel himself back. “N-no,” he said, “I can’t walk the dog! How can I walk a dog when I can’t even walk?” “Stop being a whiny little queer and make yourself useful for once,” Mike said as he reattached the leash to the dog’s collar and handed it to Carlos. “I need to make a phone call,” Mike said, “so I could do with a few moments of quiet. Take him down the street to Regis’s house and him do his business there.” At the mention of the word business Jacque barked and bolted towards the front door. Carlos held on for dear life as the dog dragged him away. With Carlos gone Mike pulled out his cell phone and quickly dialed a number. He put the phone to his ear and waited for someone on the other end to answer. “Hello,” Mike said once there was an answer on the other end, “I need to speak with Dr. Wood.” ***** As Mike spoke with Dr. Wood and Carlos was wheeled all over Beverly Hills by Jacque, Lisa stood in a jewelry store on Rancho Drive and searched for the perfect engagement ring for Mike to give her. The ring needed to be the most expensive ring in town, one that would cause heads to turn when she wore it in public. She had searched all over town and she thought she had finally found the most expensive ring in Los Angeles. The price tag was several million dollars. She smiled to herself and then told the shopkeeper to put the ring on hold, she also instructed him not to let Mike know the ring was on hold when he came to buy it. She then left the store; all she needed to do now was trick Mike into buying the ring. ***** A few hours had passed since Mike had sent Carlos out with Jacque. Carlos had returned cut up, scraped, and bleeding. On the other hand the dog was perfectly fine and that was all that mattered. Carlos claimed that the dog had physically dragged him all over Beverly Hills and had knocked him from his wheelchair several times in the process. Mike had simply laughed at him and sent him to bed, of course Carlos almost cried since he still believed there to be an evil monkey in his closet. Mike had checked the closet over and he saw no evidence of a monkey so he told Carlos that if he ever made that outlandish claim again that he would be locked in the closet overnight. With Carlos now locked in the closet for the night Mike sat in the living room with Jacque and waited for Lisa to return. An hour later Lisa had knocked on the door and Mike had let her in. Mike noticed right away that she appeared to be excited about something other than the fact that she now owned a French poodle. “I went to the jewelry store today,” she said with a broad smile. Mike thought nothing of the statement at first; he figured she planned to purchase either a set of earrings or a necklace to go with the dress she planned to wear to the benefit dinner that weekend. “Did you find anything interesting?” he asked, trying his best not to let on to the fact that he was bored with talking and wanted to have sex. She then scooted closer to Mike, which sounded like a fart since it was a leather sofa. She giggled softly and then laid her head on his strong shoulder. “I saw the most beautiful ring there baby,” she cooed, “the kind of ring I hope you get for me when you propose.” Mike gulped. He loved Lisa but he hadn’t thought of marriage, but he knew he had no choice but to buy the ring and ask for her hand. “I hate to change the subject sweetheart,” he said, “but I ran into the reporter from The Star again?” “Let me guess,” Lisa cut him off, “she told you that I’m only with you for your money?” He nodded his head yes. “Don’t believe a word that little girl says,” Lisa said, “I’m with you because I love you, not the size of your bank account.” She wrapped her arms around him and passionately kissed him. Jacque intently watched, as all dogs do when his or her masters make love, from his spot on the chair by the window. Lisa knew from prior experience that it would only be a matter of days before Mike asked her to marry him. For now she’d just sit back and enjoy the ride.
