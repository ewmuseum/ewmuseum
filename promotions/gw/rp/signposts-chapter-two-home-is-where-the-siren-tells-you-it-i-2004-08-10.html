Signposts Chapter Two: Home is where the Siren Tells you it is?

10 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/10/04 Winters made his way out of the diner and over to the parking lot. He tentatively played around with the keys whilst looking over the parking lot for the black car. He couldn’t help it, but his mind continuously insisted on refocusing upon the beautiful image of the Brunette woman, who was apparently my “guide” through this ordeal. I had to wonder where my “guide” was when I was beaten and left for dead in a ditch back in Kansas. I had certain reservations about believing her story because of this. If she was here to protect me, or to guide me to where “I was supposed to be”, then why did I not see her in Kansas? Why was it that I only saw her on the outskirts of Kansas, whilst trying to escape the hell hole. No, it didn’t sit right with Winters one bit. But there was something there. There was something between he and brunette he could just feel it. There was something at the back of his mind that screamed every time she was within a couple of inches from him. Was there a past between them? Did she really know me before my bout of amnesia? Questions. Always questions and never any answers. A thought struck Winters. What if this woman was playing on his weakness; playing on his amnesia as a means to hurt him? Perhaps she was a relative or friend of the sheriff from back in that hell hole of town he just left? It was a conceivable notion. Winters decided that he would find out for himself. He made his way back into the diner and walked right up to the counter. An old woman, with obvious wrinkles and a browny-blonde air came over to serve him. “What can I get ya, hon?” She asked, already reaching for the pot of coffee. “I was just in here and I was talking to one of your waitresses’she was a pretty brunette?” he replied. The waitress developed a confused look. “I’m the only waitress working” today, hon.” Interesting. “But she was just talking to me’over there!” “I’m sorry. I don’t know what you’re talking about.” Either this waitress was covering her friend’s ass or the brunette was really from Winters? past. Winters decided that he had nothing to lose. Without the car parked in the diners parking lot, he couldn’t get anywhere, he was stranded in the middle of nowhere. However, if he took the car there was the possibility of finding out a little more about his past - if his “guide” was indeed genuine - and besides, he was kind of looking forward to seeing the brunette again. He decided to trust his instincts. At the very back of his mind, there was something there telling him that this woman could be trusted in some form. To what extent was unknown to him, but some trust was better than none at all. “Never mind.” He said, making his way out of the door again. He stood on the outskirts of the parking lot eyeing up the car that had been assigned to him. The car looked normal enough to him, but why would he think anything else? It wasn’t exactly going to have a massive sign hanging over the top of it with the words “This is a Trap” in bright neon letters , was it? Winters had to make a decision. Should he stay at the service station and wait for another truck to come and try to hitch around to wherever the hell it’s going, or should he take the car, take it to wherever the brunette had planned for him and face the possibility of understanding his past? The choice was undeniably simple. Winters walked over to the car, stuck the key into the lock and opened the door. Upon entering the car he instantly noticed the rather sizeable laptop on the passenger seat. The laptop was already turned on and had the word “play” in large letters. Winters hit the “enter” button and the clip began to play. It was the brunette again. “I want you to take this car and drive to Chicago. It was where you lived before the whole amnesia thing and you will be safe there. I will contact you upon your arrival. The car has a GPS system that will lead you there. I’ll see you later.” An answer. Finally. Before the amnesia struck him, he used to live in Chicago’interesting. Winters jammed his keys into the ignition and started the car up. He was going home.
