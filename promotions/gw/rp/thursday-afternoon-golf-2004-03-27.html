Thursday afternoon Golf...

27 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-27-04 With Clash of the Titans on the horizon, Donny J McNasty, his friend Tim and Hardcore Harv decide it's time they go do a little a golf, just what Mrs. McNasty, Donny's mother prescribed. Although McNasty won't come out and say it, his upcoming brawl with Train is harking on him, a long with that, relationship problems are occuring between him and Julia, and he's also worried about the Drinking contest finals in Rome. The trio of bad golfers are making their way out to the first hole. It's a par 5 500 yards, with a stream running in the middle of the fairway about 300 yards out. Tim tees up first, looks down the fairway and lines up his club and swings away, the ball is almost dead straight down the fairway, and lands about 5 feet away from the stream in the fairway. 'Not a bad shot Tim' says Harv. Harv now sets up his tee shot and looks down the fairway, now he takes his swing at the ball, it's not as good as Tim's shot, it's veering off to the right and it's in the stream running through the fairway. 'Well Harv you can either take a 1 stroke penalty, chug a beer, or pay me 20 dollars. You know how we work this out' said McNasty as he watched Harv's shot go out of bounds. Harv looks at McNasty flips him off and pulls out a bottle of Killians Irish Red and begins to chug it on down... 'Fair Enough', says McNasty. Now it's McNasty's turn to take his tee shot, he get's his tee and ball ready from where he wants to hit it from, looks at the fairway and takes his shot. For once in his life while playing Golf, he hit it perfectly straight and long. The Ball landed on the other side of the stream running through the fairway. It was an absolutley perfect shot. Maybe this is a sign of things to come in the upcoming days for McNasty. The trio begin to carry their bags and walk down the fairway to where the balls had landed. McNasty is in a good mood at least on the out side. Tim is just happy to get away from his wife for a day and she doesn't mind it either, and Harv, well he's happy doing anything that he can drink beer while doing. 'So Donny what do you think is going to happen while in Rome?' asks Tim. 'The future is unpredictable. What I would like to happen is at least beat Train. Second on my list is win the Drinking tournament. Third is win the Over the Top Rumble.' Harv looks at Donny with confused look on his face and questions him, 'Why isn't the rumble one of the top priorities?' Donny replies, 'It's simple Harv, look, we all have to work from the bottom up, it's just not my time right now to go for the World Championship, that's Ice's title right now, and if he still holds it when my time comes for a chance at that then great. The Platinum title I'm not that far from getting a shot at. A little more work and I will be there. It's all about paying your dues Harv. You know that as well as I do.' Tim chimes in, 'It's kind of like you have to date the ugly girls first before you get the really hot ones.' Harv and Donny look at him like he's crazy. Donny then proceeds to set the record straight to Tim. 'What in the hell are you talking about? I never dated the ugly girls first then the really hot ones. I'm sorry to hear that's how it worked out for you. I never had that expirience. What about you Harv?' Harv just shakes his head no. 'Look at it this way, I started off as a young man working on a potato farm my every moment I had off from school to begin with. Until one day a man by the name of Nick Soapdish came up to me. He seen me outside of a bar, beating the shit out of someguy who wanted to start a fight with me. Hell this guy thought he was some sort of real bad ass and tried using nunchucks on me and well I still beat the hell out of him. So Nick comes up to me and said, hey I am a Professional Wrestler and we're looking for more guys much like yourself who can hand out an asswhooping, would you consider joining. I told him I would have to think about it, so I took his business card and he handed me some information for prospective recruits, it seemed legit to me and then the next thing you know, I was making millions of dollars over night. Why? Cause the TIME was RIGHT to do so. Believe it or not, my Mother encouraged me NOT TO DO IT. But I did it anyways. What I learned is that sometimes you have to do what your gut instincts tell you to do. If I would have let that oppurtunity slip past me, there would have been a lot of different things that would have happened or never happened. For starters, I would still be working on the farm, secondly Harv would be the only owner of the Drunken Leperchaun, and third of all I would have all the money I do, that I have a such a good time with.' 'Never really looked at it that way' replies Tim. McNasty also tells them 'You know ask Ely Braxton or Brick next time they're in town, I know for a fact that those guys appreciate all the hard work they put in, and waiting for their moment at glory. I know those guys didn't expect to go into GW within in a month and win the World Championship. All I wanted to do in the first month was build a name for myself, get the fans to like me. So far I have succeeded. I'm not a champion yet, but I will be before you know it. It's all about hard work, patience and seizing oppurtunity's when the arise.' The Trio arrive at Tim's ball he's getting ready to hit his second shot, he pulls out his 2 iron. He swings and he's hooked it to the left into a bunker. 'Well I guess my long talk didn't help you succeed today on the golf course, but use everything I tell you in everyway possible and you'll see the difference in your life.' Donny said. 'I'm more worried about it making a difference in my golf game right now, my lifes already messed up as it is. I don't see you sending out 5 child support checks a month.' quips Harv. Harv takes out a golf ball from his bag and drops it down on the ground, he to breaks out his 2 iron and takes his swing and hit hooks right and lands in some deep rough. Donny quickly jumps on the situation, 'See landing in the deep rough, something I'm sure you used dealing with Harv, Deep shit. It's not my fault you have 5 children you send checks out for each month, you should have worn a jimmy hat on your rumpleforeskin.' The trio crosses a bridge and they get to McNasty's ball and Donny breaks out his 3 iron. He lines up his shot, swings and yet again hits it straight down the middle in fact he's on the green in two. 'You guys can pay up now for that last shot, you're about to lose the hole.' remarks Donny. 'Fuck you' both Tim and Harv remark. The trio played all 18 holes Thursday afternoon before McNasty and Harv caught a late flight out of State College to Lagaurdia International and then to Europe they went. The score for the day, McNasty an even 72 for Par. Harv 86, 14 over par, and Tim 87, 15 over par. Tim and Harv ended up paying out over $100 each to McNasty who of course was the winner. This could be a sign of things to come, first McNasty wins Monday night on Evolution against Train and Makonie, then he wins a great game of golf. Could this be the beginning of a hot streak for the King of Hardcore or is this just a false omen? We'll have to wait and see Monday night at Clash of the Titans 5, live from Rome, Italy only on Pay Per View.
