In the Valley of Secrets (Part 2)

17 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 17th, 2006 December 20th, 2005 Eryc stirred in bed for a moment just to turn over, but something didn’t feel right. The temperature of the room suddenly cooled down and felt like it could be nearly below zero. He opened his eyes and everything appeared fine. His eyes moved over towards the space in the bed next to him where Krissy slept and there was nothing, an empty space; he could have sworn she was there a little bit ago. “Krissy,” he called out and continued to look around. No response. Eryc slipped out of bed and started to look around but the room felt solitary to him. He slowly made his way to the window and pushed the curtains aside to reveal the pitch black night he thought it was and let go of it so it closed again. His eyes moved towards the alarm clock and the digital readout told him it was 3:42 a.m. “Krissy wouldn’t have left without waking me up”, he thought. “Something’s not right.” His bare feet treaded against the beige carpet of the floor until he reached the cold porcelain of the bathroom and poked his head in: nothing. Her things were still there so she couldn’t have gone far. The worry was setting in and his heart began to beat aggressively as he began to panic. “Krissy,” he called out again only this time louder. Eryc hoped she had just gone out to get some ice or something of that sort and she would be right back. Five minutes elapsed and still she had not come back; Eryc had all he could take and decided to put some clothes on and go down to the desk and ask if they had seen anyone who matched Krissy’s description recently. He threw on a simple white t-shirt and the black karate pants he wore to exercise in even if he was shaking like an ice cube. He’d also tell the concierge to have maintenance look at the thermostat the next day because he was freezing and even tried to turn up the heat but the dial wouldn’t move an inch. Maybe the whole heating was shot, he thought before deciding that maybe his trench would provide him with a little extra warmth to go down to the lobby. He opened the closet and suddenly staggered back. “Oh god no....” he uttered and fell back onto his ass. His eyes and brain told him not to look but like a witness of a train wreck, he just couldn’t put his hand over his face and peek between his fingers. This felt real and the horror of what was in front of him was tearing him to shreds. Krissy laid in a slump on the bottom of the empty closet. All the clothes that were inside had been removed somehow and she was covered in blood and above her head written in her vitae was the most startling thing of all... CORVUS IS BACK! Eryc started to push himself backward to get away from what he saw and quickly brought his fingers to his mouth and checked for sharpened canines that were not present. He pushed back until he couldn’t any longer and felt what could be feet behind him. He turned around in a delayed manner only to see himself looking down upon him. This version of him still had the long hair and sharp teeth and was the epitome of evil. Eryc couldn’t believe what he was seeing and tried to scramble away from the visage of what once was. He could not get away. Corvus grabbed him and pulled him up with unbelievable strength, which time had made Eryc forget how powerful he used to be. While being held up, Corvus? piercing red glowing eyes cut through him and pulled him closer just to whisper in Eryc’s ear. You can take the man out of the monster...but you can’t take the monster out of the man, just remember that because you will and whether you like it or not, succumb to who you really are. Eryc struggled to break free as Corvus? grip on his throat became tighter but before he could no longer speak he let out a horrendous scream. NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! He felt his body shake as his eyes slowly opened and turned to his left to see Krissy looking at him worriedly. “Eryc,” she asked, “are you alright?” He exhaled long and hard realizing that this had only been a dream...no, worse then that: a nightmare. There had been a few disturbing nights over the past six months but none of them had been as twisted and vivid as this one. “I’m fine,” he spoke. “It was just a bad dream...nothing more.” Krissy was not so certain that it was just a bad dream and blood trickled from both of his nostrils down to his upper lip. “Eryc,” she gasped. “You’re bleeding, let me clean you up a little bit.” He couldn’t be sure if this was a sign that he was losing his mortality again and couldn’t take the chance of her touching his blood and possibly getting sick from it or something worse. “No!” He shouted, “I can handle this myself...I just need to go wash my face up in the bathroom.” Krissy shook her head; she was only trying to help him...there was no need for him to get jumpy on her. So it was a little blood; it’s not like anything was going to happen to her if she went and wet a washcloth to clean his face up. “Are you going to be alright?” she asked. “I’ve never seen you so scared before.” Krissy stood up and walked to the bathroom just as Eryc dried his face off with a towel and stared into the mirror as if he’d just watched someone die and in essence, he did. “Go back to bed, love.” He said in a soft tone. “I just need a moment and I’ll be right there.” She took a few steps closer to him and kissed his shoulder blade before turning back and going to bed again because she had to be up for classes the next morning. It was close to Christmas break so she’d have more time to spend with Eryc then and maybe get him to get away from all the hectic things that were in his life at the moment. Especially the wrestling, (or fighting...whatever he referred to it as) because it led her to believe that was why he was restless most nights. All of the violence tends to stick with a fighter no matter how strong mentally or physically they are. Krissy didn’t want to analyze his dreams; she wasn’t a psychiatrist...all she wanted was for her man to be ok and from the signs he exhibited in the bathroom that may be furthest from the truth then she could realize. Eryc stared in the mirror long and hard before he went back to bed. This nightmare had taken its toll on him and he felt it was leading to some awful foreshadowing of the future...a future he wanted no part of. ---- 1:12 p.m. Eryc was still in bed after the rough night he had; the last time he was up was to see Krissy off and then crawled back into bed. His mind was a little at ease because he took some over the counter sleeping medication he bought the last time he had trouble sleeping. His cell phone rang which he groaned about but rolled over and picked it up off of the night stand to look at the number on the caller id: it was Krissy. “Hello,” he said sleepily, “I’m still in bed...I needed the extra rest, what’s up?” She told him that when her classes were finished (she had one more to go yet), she was coming to the hotel and they had to talk. Of course this set Eryc’s mind spinning in a million different directions. Did the events that happened during the night scare her into rethinking their relationship; was she afraid that he would hurt her? “Everything’s alright...right?” He asked with uncertain tone. It made him uncomfortable when anyone said that they needed to talk but she seemed not to be upset and was in a cheerful mood. “Don’t worry, it’s nothing bad.” Krissy continued with a little more reinforcement. “I think it will be good for the both of us but I have to go, now get out of bed lazy bones.” She said jokingly. Eryc groaned, “Alright...I’m up; I’ll see you when you get here then.” He responded to her telling him that she loved him by returning the sentiment before closing his phone. He looked at the sheets a moment before throwing them back as there was no sense in trying to get any extra sleep out of what was pretty much a wasted day as it was. The plan for now was to shower and trim his facial hair before she came back, which wouldn’t be too long he figured. Showering and shaving didn’t take him very long; maybe only an hour at most and Krissy wasn’t due back to the hotel for at least another hour so he had time to kill. There wasn’t enough time for him to go out and buy anything at a store without risking coming back and making her wait so he decided upon doing his meditation routine early instead of before his workout because there wouldn’t be time to do it that day. Eryc needed all the spiritual energy he could manage after the horrifying ordeal he went through the previous night. If he needed to he’d even go as far as pray to whatever god would listen if it meant not becoming kindred again. Of course he couldn’t tell anyone about it so there was no one to talk to about the situation. He left the world of the undead behind the minute his heart started beating. He couldn’t call his brother nor could he call his student, Deacon Black. There’s nothing like the sensation of your conscience eating away at you and did Eryc know how that felt. He sat down on the ground and positioned himself as he did a million other times before to close his mind off to the outside world and relax, releasing his body so it could drift into the cleansing mode of his routine. The more he tried to concentrate the harder it was; images of his past kills started to hijack his brain. He did the best he could to fend them off but they were just too powerful for him and finally he managed to move his body and curled up into a fetal position with tears starting to roll down his face. Why did this have to start happening now all of a sudden; just when things were starting to be normal for him? It wasn’t fair for a past he was no longer a part of to haunt him in his moment to enjoy the future. No one would ever accept him for who he truly was and if they had any understanding of that he would be alone for the rest of his days. Who would want to share their life with a murderer of many not only with weapons but his bare...fangs if you will. When he was a vampire it didn’t make a difference (to him) because he was evil and for the most part needed to kill to stay alive. He didn’t always have access to a blood bank or butcher shop. “I can’t let this get to me,” he softly spoke to himself, “if I let the beast win even after it has been purged from my body then I am nothing more than a defeated and broken man.” Eryc rolled onto his stomach and clenched his fists. He used them to do push-ups and get his mind off of what he again had experienced with visions of him...or rather of Corvus. He now considered them to be two separate entities even if they were in the same body. They were nothing alike...polar opposites. He would find peace and inner strength through positive things, like the love he gave and received from Krissy. She was his world now and he would appreciate her as long as the higher powers allowed it. Eryc never thought much about god after he became human again...he had forgotten how. This was a large step away from the catholic boy he was raised as and the man he grew up into before becoming tainted by the dark gift. ---- Krissy walked across the street from the Starbucks she visited to get a pick me up (she was tired from being up in the middle of the night with Eryc). She pulled her keys from her purse and got into her black 1979 Pontiac Firebird when her cell phone rang. She thought it would be Eryc calling to see where she was but when she looked and saw it was her Mother she panicked a little bit. Krissy had not called home yet to tell them she was thinking about not coming for Christmas being that she was with Eryc and wanted to spend time with him but at the same time knew her family would be devastated if she didn’t visit for the holiday’s. She had to think quickly...there must be a compromise she could come to. “Hi Mom,” she said as she answered the call. “I’m fine...just about to meet someone.” She just had to know that her Mom would be inquisitive when she said she was meeting someone and of course the usual question of “do you have a boyfriend” came up, which made her slightly uncomfortable. “Yes Mom, I’m seeing someone...” She paused wondering if now would be a good time to tell her that she wasn’t coming home for the holidays. “I don’t know if I’m going to be able to make it home for Christmas.” Krissy leaned back in her seat as her Mom gave her all sorts of guilt trips about not coming home but the fact that if she went home and left Eryc that something might happen to him. “I was thinking about coming to the cabin and bringing my boyfriend with but there are a few problems that might come up.” She wasn’t worried about going to the cabin with Eryc and them not staying in the same room or anything of that nature but more of her family talking to him about her and telling him things she did not want him to know. “Well he thinks I’m a college student at the University...I just can’t tell him what I really do. I can’t really tell anyone because it would jeopardize my job.” Her mother explained to her that if she wanted to come home and bring her boyfriend with that they would make sure not to talk about her real job and do whatever Krissy asked them to in order to keep everything running smoothly until the time came where she could tell him...which was to be never if Krissy had anything to say about it. “I’ll think about it Mom and I’ll call you back tomorrow, I promise.” Krissy looked at her watch and realized she needed to get back to the hotel where Eryc was staying. “Listen, I have to get going alright” Tell Daddy I love him and miss him and hopefully I’ll see you all soon...love you Mom, bye.” She ended the call with a sigh and set it in the passenger seat, starting the engine and letting it warm up in the cold weather before she took off. Luckily she had the coffee to keep her warm because she forgot to wear gloves. On the drive to the hotel, Krissy thought more about taking Eryc back to Canada with her; she thought he could use a break from doing his wrestling and the other projects that he had going on. It would do him good and give them a chance to become even closer without everything being in the way. When she arrived at the hotel, she sat out in the car for another five minutes figuring out the best way to ask him to come with her. She knew it wouldn’t be easy with the busy schedule Eryc was always mentioning to her. She walked into the hotel and took the elevator up to the top floor and then went to room 170 and knocked on the door and waited for Eryc to let her in. He came to the door and opened it only to be pushed back by Krissy, who kicked the door closed with her foot as she wrapped her arms around him after she shed her coat off onto the floor. “Hello there,” Eryc said as he smiled warmly to her. “What do I owe the pleasure of your glowing radiance?” She kissed him deeply before he could say anything more and he returned it with just as much equal vigor. Krissy slowed and pulled her lips away from his figuring now that she had his attention that she would try and talk to him about wanting to go to her family’s cabin. “Just missed you that’s all,” She lured him over to the couch that was in the room and sat him down. “I told you that I wanted to talk to you about something over the phone...so here goes.” Eryc did the best he could to relax and if this was going to be the end of their relationship he’d keep calm and wait until she went back to her apartment before he took out his frustrations on something or someone...whatever came first. “A few days from now is Christmas and all,” she hesitated and squeezed his hand, “and my Mom called today asking me if I was going to come home to be with the family.” It didn’t seem so bad just yet, Eryc figured but women always take the longest to let someone down easy. “I told her that I had met someone and that I really wanted to be with him...” Eryc jumped into the conversation before he could give her a chance to finish. “If you want to go home to be with your family, I understand.” He gave her a look of assurance. “I couldn’t keep you from your family and besides I have a lot of things coming up.” This wasn’t at all how Krissy was hoping it would go; was he pushing her away? Was there a reason why he was so jumpy all of a sudden after last night? Too many questions began to fill her head but she wouldn’t give up just yet. “Eryc, I was thinking that maybe you should come with me back to Canada.” Her hazel eyes shimmered as they looked into his. “I thought maybe you could use a break because it always seems you are wrestling or traveling for this and that and after last night I think you deserve to rest.” He thought about it for a moment and even though he loved her he wasn’t sure how it would go if he went with her to see her parents. Sure he only looked to be a few years older than her but in actuality he was a few millennia older than all of them combined. “Can I think about it?” He asked. “It sounds like a good opportunity but I just feel a bit awkward about meeting your family.” Krissy laughed and poked him in the ribs. “You mean to tell me that you’ve never dated a girl and been brought home to meet her parents?” Eryc took a hard swallow. How could he really explain that the only time he had really ever been in a situation like that was when he was a young man in his time when families arranged marriages and that was final. “Not exactly,” he did his best to avoid eye contact. “It’s probably a bit of a shock but I really haven’t dated a whole lot.” Krissy couldn’t believe what she was hearing. He had to have been in some kind of relationship if not recently then in school. “What about in High School?” Eryc shook his head soundly. He at least could find a fake answer for this one. “I was home schooled,” he then thought about his last relationship, “and the last girl that I dated...well I was engaged to the drummer in my band; her parents were deceased though.” She learned something new about him that she didn’t know before...he had been engaged before. It couldn’t help but cross her mind why he wasn’t married being famous and good looking but it wasn’t any of her business. “You don’t have anything to worry about,” she explained. “My parents are great and they’ll love you.” Krissy laughed and put her head on his shoulder. “They probably wouldn’t even have a clue of who you are; so you could tell them you were anything you wanted to be.” Her parents not knowing who he was could be a plus. The less people that knew the better he figured. Not one parent on the planet would probably let their daughter date a professional wrestler/fighter who was also a rock musician as well...the press gives guys like him a bad rep. “I don’t have any upcoming scheduled dates for the wrestling promotion I’ve been working for,” he said as he thought about it more. “My publicity staff and my agent have no idea where I am because I told them I needed to find myself so I think maybe I can swing this.” Krissy let go of his hand and wrapped her arms around him tightly nearly squeezing the life out of him for such a small girl; she really had a grip. He could have possibly trained her to be a great fighter if she didn’t have such an angelic face: it was too beautiful to get bruised up or worse. “I promise you won’t regret this, baby” Krissy said excitedly. “My parents have a cabin in Red Lake and that’s where we would stay; besides it’s only us them and my younger sister won’t be able to make it...I don’t think you’ll be bothered too much.” Even though this would be treated as a vacation, Krissy didn’t want to completely make him feel trapped while he was there with her. “If you need to, you can always do something work related while we are there but nothing too strenuous.” She grinned and patted him on the leg. “This is meant to be a time for relaxation, understood?” Eryc was resigned to the fact that he would probably be miserable but at least he’d be with the girl he loves and for that he’d make the best out of it. The thought of not having to do anything other than just sit around and do his basic exercise routines was appealing to him too. “I’ll be completely yours the whole time more than likely.” Krissy smiled at him once again, “good, I wouldn’t have it any other way but I better call my Mom back and buy us plane tickets.” She felt around her pocket and didn’t have her phone. “Eryc, I left my phone in the car...I should go and get it and by the time I’m back I should be done talking to my Mom.” Eryc reached into his pocket and pulled out his phone and offered it to her. “It’s ok hon; if my Mom sees a number she doesn’t recognize she won’t answer it.” He didn’t think much of it but instead of having her pay for plane tickets he had a better idea. “While you go take care of that I’ll take care of the traveling arrangements.” Krissy looked at him and seemed insistent on getting the plane tickets. “I can’t let you do it this time Eryc; I want to do something for you this time instead of the other way around.” He was stubborn of course. “You do plenty for me and you don’t have to worry about it, I have my own private jet.” He laughed as her jaw dropped a few feet onto the floor...seemed he had that effect on her a lot. “You’re always full of surprises, Eryc Morgan, and you never cease to amaze me.” She shook her head and stood up to walk to the door. “I’m going to get my phone, be right back.” Eryc smiled at her again; it seemed like he did that a lot around her. Nobody else even came close to that feeling except one other person but that was a long time ago and a place he didn’t want to go mentally. The old saying was true in Eryc’s case concerning Krissy: He hated to see her go but he loved watching her leave. She picked up her coat and slipped it on before she left the room to get her phone and call her Mom. In the meantime, Eryc got on his phone and called to make preparations so his plane would be ready to take off at a moment’s notice. As he sat there on the phone he couldn’t help but think that before the end of the year something special would happen.
