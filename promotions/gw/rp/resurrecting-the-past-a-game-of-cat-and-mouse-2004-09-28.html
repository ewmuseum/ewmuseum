Resurrecting The Past: A Game Of Cat And Mouse

28 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/28/04 Maybe they'll come back again Maybe they're all gone One day you'll find me sending This message strong Under your feet the city falls Down so far the ground is sky Red... Green Stop... Go The brilliant red of brake lights, although a thing of beauty are often the last thing a person sees before they die. To some they are just an instrument of the modern automobile. Others they are a warning of the grave fate they are about to be dealt, but tonight... they marked an arrival, a destination. The black rented Pontiac Ram Air purred under the moon high over Dallas, Texas. Black clouds moved slowly across the sky, and eventually across the moon. Shadowing it's brilliance and glow with an ominous darkness. The four doors opened all at once, like an approaching army. Out of the back passenger side seat arose Jason LeMaster. Even though it was pitch black, he still sported a pair of black designer sunglasses. Demitri Miasnikov came out of the opposite back seat. His huge 7 foot frame now hung over the car. Demitri cracked his knuckles as he made his way around the car, approaching the sidewalk. Vivica Valentine, emerged from the passenger side of the car. She wore a black baby tee, with an Anarchy A on the front of it. A gust of wind blew her hair into her face, and into her mouth. She blew the wind out of her mouth and pulled it back around her ear, as she looked across the car at Christian Zenith, who closed the driver's side door. His black shoes clicked as he walked past the nose of the car, holding the keys above his head. A startled Jason LeMaster realized the car had an alarm the second Christian hit the button. The three others waited in a group for Zenith as he pulled a small white piece of notebook paper out of his pocket... '[detail withheld]...' Christian read off the small torn notebook paper. 'I think we found it boys.' Vivica said, looking over at Jason and Demitri. '376... that's a 3rd floor room.' LeMaster said... investigating the windows. All the 3rd floor lights appeared to be off, which either meant that she was sleeping, or she was out. Hopefully the first, Jason thought to himself. 'Let's go and get this over with...' Christian said with a smug look of satisfaction as he adjusted his custom fitted black suit jacket. Demitri was extra cautious as the small mob followed Christian into the broken down old apartment. A sign on the front announced space for rent... Vivica couldn't help but think they were going to have one more here in a second. Once inside, there was a door directly to their left, which Christian opened. Inside was a gray concrete stairwell... no markings, just a black wrought-iron handrail. The room was so bare, and tall, every noise made would surely resonate off the walls and to the top. Vivica pulled a little handgun out of the waist of her pants before the made their way up the stairs. Every step they went, it seemed like would echo at least 4 times. Hopefully, for their sake, Kitty wasn't expecting their company, or she'd surely know of their arrival. Christian thought of the possibility and pulled the .357 Magnum out of the inside of his jacket pocket, following Vivica's lead. Trying not to look too suspicious, Christian opened the door slowly and walked into the carpeted hallway. The floors were made of concrete with a very thin blue carpet covering as far as the eye could see. No one seemed to be up and about this time of night, as every other light in the hallway was turned off. Zenith pointed to the left before he began to walk in that direction. Lined up 3-wide behind Christian were his group of professionals. Demitri looked up at the ceiling as one of the lights overhead flickered on its last leg. '360... 362...' Christian whispered the room number as he walked by them slowly. Vivica's eyes on the other hand were straight ahead, always looking for the possibility of an ambush. A nervous habit made Jason LeMaster take a quick glance behind them. This was all too easy, but then again it was highly possibly that Tera... or Kaitlynn, whatever she likes to be called these days had no idea they were after her. Christian raised his hand with the Magnum in it as he came to an abrupt halt. '376.' Vivica said out loud, reading the room number. Christian reached for the doorknob with his left hand, and began to turn it slowly. His right hand firmly clinched the Magnum. Christian's expression changed when he found the door to be unlocked. No light shined on the carpet at his feet, giving him the impression the lights were off inside the apartment. He gave one quick look back at his associates before he slammed his foot into the door, slinging it wide open and busting a hole in the dry wall. Christian and Vivica both pointed their guns into the darkness... but dropped them immediately when they saw what was inside. 'What in the hell?!' Jason LeMaster said in frustration. The room... was completely empty, other than a single retro olive green couch in the middle of the room. Christian slid the gun pack into his jacket pocket in disappointment. She must've packed up and left... surely she must've known they were after her. Or maybe... it had something to do with the pictures he saw back in Doc Bailey's apartment. Regardless, he just ran head first into a dead end. Vivica flipped her cell phone open and read exactly what Christian wanted to hear... 'KI: [detail withheld]' We're under Zenith again It's healthy if Not for long Leave a little room For us to breathe Vivica Valentine opened up the laptop computer. The indigo glow of her desktop reflected off of her face, highlighting her beautiful blonde hair with a blue glow. She took a small circular shaped object out of a black cargo bag, sitting down in the back seat of the Ram Air with her and threw it out the door to Jason. He caught the disc and looked back at her with confusion... 'It's a tracer. Put it on the back of your cell phone near the top by your antenna.' She said as she typed vigorously on her laptop. 'When you make a call... a little red light on the back will begin to blink fast, when the blinking stops, I've picked up the location of the person on the other end. I'll then use the coordinates to get a map on my computer, and we can follow whoever... hopefully Tera, to her exact location.' Vivica looked back at Jason, who wore a blank stare. He just did what he was told, realizing at this point he'll never figure out what in the hell she was doing. He placed it on the back of his cell phone near the antenna and showed it to her. Vivica nodded in approval. 'Kaitlynn McIntyre' he said out loud. The number flew fast across the screen of the cell phone. [detail withheld] The phone rang as the tracer began to blink red, much like the brake light of the Ram Air. It rang a second time, as Jason looked over at Christian Zenith, who stood motionless, with his arms crossed, staring up at the moon. Suddenly, the ringing ceased... 'Hello?' Answered the voice of a girl. She sounded confused... and the background noise almost faded out her voice completely. Christian looked over at Vivica, who held her right hand up as she typed with the left... 'Are you going to answer? Hello?' She asked again, seconds before Vivica smiled and gave a thumbs up. Jason LeMaster closed the cell phone, and looked at Vivica, who was deeply involved with a picture of a map. She clicked a button and the map zoomed out. Suddenly, a red X appeared and looked back at Jason... 'That was Kaitlynn, she's still in Dallas. A bar downtown, called July Alley... 2809 Elm Street, the phone number is [detail withheld]. We won't need it, it's only about 3 miles away.' She said before looking up at Christian. 'Good work Vivica.' Christian said as he walked towards the driver's seat. That's all she needed to hear to make her smile with satisfaction. Go The brilliant red of brake lights, although a thing of beauty are often the last thing a person sees before they die. To some they are just an instrument of the modern automobile. Others they are a warning of the grave fate they are about to be dealt, but tonight... they marked an arrival, a destination. Music overwhelmed the soul and the smell of food overwhelmed the thoughts of Nu Christ as they exited their Ram Air, parked across the street from where all the action is. July Alley was the name... the sound of rock music could be heard from outside as a crowd was gathered outside, waiting to get in apparently to have some drinks, listen to some music, and have a good time. But a good time would not be had by all tonight, if Christian had anything to do with it. The Quad walked across the street, lead by Christian and Vivica. Once they reached the other side, they looked down the street, and saw the line was wrapped way around the corner. Christian looked at the bouncer guarding the door, and then back at his associates... 'You check out that line, see if Tera is there... I'll scope out the inside.' Christian said. The group looked back at him puzzled... 'How in the hell do you think you're going to get in there?' Demitri said. Christian pulled his wallet out of his pocket and opened it up. To expose a wad of $100 bills that could choke an elephant. Demitri smiled and nodded as they walked away, to inspect the line. Christian hoped in the back of his mind they'd find Tera in line. She don't know them... it would be easy to take her unexpectedly. But if she saw him, there would definitely be a struggle. Zenith approached the front door as a bouncer stopped him immediately. Zenith adjusted his suit jacket and looked back at the bald, slightly heavy set bodyguard. 'Get in line with the rest of them. Nice try through boy, very nice.' The bodyguard said. 'I don't do lines.' Christian responded showing the bodyguard a hundred dollar bill before slipping it into his pocket. The body guard nodded and Christian walked past into the July Alley. A band named The Vines were playing a song called 'Get Free' as Christian walked down the brick bar. Neon picture frames hung from the wall, and the smell of cigarette smoke filled the room. Christian was as cold... her nerves were steel, as they say while he scoped out the bar. A big busted girl with straight red hair smiled at him as he walked by, without even a glance. Zenith looked over the crowd near the live stage, watching The Vines play one of their biggest hit singles. Nothing... she wasn't watching the band, maybe she was outside. Christian turned around to leave, but then stopped in his tracks. Turning around slowly... he could feel the eyes of someone staring at him. He turned around, and he locked eyes with Kaitlynn McIntyre, who spotted him from the end of the bar. We're under Zenith again Let the cycle run We won't die Leave a little room for us to breathe His cold eyes told the story, as Kaitlynn looked right though and into his soul. She could see the vengeance in his soul, it was now she realized her immediate danger. Kaitlynn McIntyre... AKA Tera Zenith jumped off her barstool and bolted into the kitchen. Christian went full sprint, knocking over two customers and a staff member on his way. The adrenaline pumped through Tera's veins as she jumped on top of a stainless steel counter and slid to the other side. She ran full speed as Christian approached the counter, and slammed her entire body into the emergency exit. An alarm sounded, the panic of everyone inside could be heard screaming and shoving. Zenith dove over the counter and out the door and back up to his feet. Tera ran down the back alley full steam ahead. She could hear the quickening footsteps of Christian growing louder and louder as he grew closer. She shoved a series of trash cans down as she ran by. Looking back, she caught Christian jumping over them, but a bit slowed. She could see the street head. If she could get around the corner, she could get lost in the line, or maybe sprint across traffic. You have to get there first, she said to herself as she began to lose her breath, but maintained her speed. Click Click Click Click Click Click She could hear Christian getting back up to speed behind her. The clicks seemed to mimic her heartbeat or vice versa. Her stomach was climbing up her throat, and the speed was starting to make her eyes water as she spins at the last second to avoid a dumpster. Click Click Click Click Click Click Click Click Click Click Click Click She could feel Christian closing down the gap. She could hear his heart beat, and feel his breath on her back. Just out of his reach... she began to catch her second wind. Step for step with Christian, she couldn't look back to see where he was, she had to lose him... she had to keep speed. The street was just a few feet away... she could make it... no... 'I will make it.' Tera said to herself as she ran carelessly into traffic, not even looking. A car slammed on the breaks and honked in anger. This was his break, the car stopped he could get around it. Christian churned on, beginning to lose a few steps on Tera as he neared the street. Suddenly, a large man and Christian slammed into each other. Both men instantly put their guns into position, right between each others eyes. Zenith looked into the eyes of the large man... a few inches taller than himself as Tera ran away to safety. Christian's eyes were cold... unintimidated by the Walther p99qa pointed a few centimeters away from his brain. The .357 Magnum of Christian Zenith was pointed at the same place on the large man. The large man's black hair blew in the wind as a sprinkle of rain began to fall from the sky. Both men stood silent, holding their ground. Sweat began to drip down the huge man's right arm... across a tattoo of a dragon flying across bulging muscles. A tight black tank top covered the man's rippling chest. He was like he was straight out of a comic book, a dark demonic superman, with a gun and one hell of an attitude. Christian didn't back down, he's been in much worse situations before. Sweat dripped down the forehead of Zenith, perspiring from the chase, his adrenaline still pumping... it was amazing Christian kept his gun so still. It seemed like time itself froze still as the men were in a staredown. Nothing else mattered, it was a matter of respect, a matter of pride. Until Vivica J. Valentine, Demitri Miasnikov, and Jason LeMaster rounded the corner. They made a circle around the large man, whose eyes darted back and forth to get a glimpse of his new company. Both men slowly lowered their guns at the same time, but didn't break eye contact. Christian put his gun back into his jacket pocket, and spit on the ground next to him before walking around the man. The trio quickly followed Christian out of the dark alley. Demitri, standing at the large man's height, looked back at him as they walked away. The man turned around and watched them leave, as if he was getting a mental picture. It didn't take much effort to shake the feeling they were being watched as they walked away. The man, standing in the alley became out of sight as they approached the Ram Air without a word. Vivica stopped behind the car and reached her hand under the license plate... where she then pulled off a white disc-like object. 'Someone is following us.' Vivica said looking back at Christian. 'Good... let them come, I'm always up for a good challenge.'
