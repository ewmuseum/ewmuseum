?| Black Inevitability |?

6 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6.6.04 ---- :| Itagaki lifted the limp, lifeless body of the lady. Her head hanging back like a puppet without a master. From there, they shared a blind stare. Pendragon's sword fell from her clutches into the cold hearted snow as Itagaki then tilted her head to the side exposing her neck and then bit her. |: ~? He is what kills on a moonless night The tears that they cry ?~ Lately Itagaki has been showing Vampiric signs. The biting and feeding off blood that really does nothing for him physically. I'm starting to believe it's one of the phases he's going through. :| Akuma peers out the window again to see Itagaki closing the womans eyes and laying her down to rest. |: The connection between two dead people is remarkable. They feed off each other, they need each other, they can... relate. Caught in a barrier between Heaven and Hell, their choice has already been made. All they're waiting on is if they're ready to go or not. You have to accept the inevitable. :| Akuma continued to update his experimental diary with data and observations. |: It is my duty to deliver Itagaki to the chief spirit of evil, the ultimate adversary of God. And he will bask in purgatory and enjoy it. Training him for Lucifer's army to take over is my task but I feel as if I'm becoming scared of Itagaki. Though I do not believe it is possible for him to develop thoughts of his own, I believe he is merely mimicking what he has seen. Of course he will have to under-go more surgery to fixate him into the mere puppet he is. Itagaki will forever be a puppet, anything else could be catastrophic and unpredictable. But there is other matters that I must deal with. Pendragon... he who relies on God's power to fuel him. Glowing with Heaven's faith behind him, it makes me want to throw up. He is merely another stupid Christian. How dare he think he is the one to bring me down?! Eternal damnation will come, and one of us has to go. He has to go. I'm tired of watching him corrupt society with such stupidity as... believe in yourself? I laugh at the thought. Human's are to fragile which is why I deserve their minds. I gave Pendragon a chance to be free. He rejected and now he must suffer the consequences. And after Itagaki is done devouring the last remembrance of Ven Pen, there will be no one to blame but Ven Pen himself. He brought this dark plague upon himself, he cast this deathly spell upon the people. Because of him I, I am more alive than ever. ~? Who else can put themselves in my place? Who else can stare into deaths cold hard face? ?~ ~? Whether or not you want to Let your mind unbend ?~ Die Pendragon... ~? For you know as well as I do That your life will inevitably end. ?~
