Hype: Ken Partridge, Jr.

4 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01.04.06 The scene opens in The Elements, the New York nightclub owned and operated by Global's 'Superstar' Ken Partridge, Jr. The music of Paul Oakenfold blares overhead as he spins in the DJs booth above the crowd. The camera turns and goes through a mob of gorgeous ladies, metro men, and fun-loving packs, all dancing on the gigantic glass surface between them and what has now been revealed as something of a giant flat fishtank that is the elevated dancefloor. We finally make our way through the mob and into a rear room, warmly lit, with some fifteen or twenty candlelit tables. All the way in the back, sitting at a huge round table with the likes of Jake Gyllenhaal, Jason Mewes, Ceci Braden, and, of course, Ace Harding, Bill Moresi, Josh and the beautiful Gunner Addison, is KPJ, himself, clad in a white untucked tuxedo shirt and black pinstripe slacks with the gray suspenders hanging free, both of which came from Banana Republic. His feet, as usual, were wrapped in a shiny new pair of black Prada flat-toe shoes. He looks up from laughter and spots the camera. KPJ: You know, when I was told Global was going to be making their way here to shoot 'a few quick comments' for Reload, I thought to myself... 'what better time to reintroduce yourself to the entire world than when Global's doing the same.' Looking around, one would think that this is what 'Superstar' Ken Partridge, Jr. is all about. They spot movie stars such as Jake, Jason, and Kirsten... or the music industry's finest in Ceci or Paul... or simply the entire nightclub atmosphere, and what they see is celebrity... glamor... heirarchy. You know, they'd be half right, but what so many fail to see is my work inside of the Global octagon. The neglect to remember my list of accomplishments, and it is a long one. In Global alone, I captured the now defunct PrimeStar Championship in my very first GW match from Kid Flash, the man later known as Josh. I've held the Television Championship a record setting three times. I'm a two time International Champion with a the longest reign in Global history... and... I'm a former GW World Champion. I didn't acheive all of that by being a celebrity. However, if you look at that list there is one singles title that has eluded me throughout my career... the Platinum Championship... the final piece of singles medal that stands between me and the illustrious Grand Slam. You see... what KPJ... what I am all about, is being the best... the absolute best. I'm about legacy and tradition and, at Global's Reload, I'll be one step closer to cementing my name in history. Serpenti... Grest... and Jay Robson... all of you... be prepared. The evolution's over... now, it's time for this star to shine! fade to black
