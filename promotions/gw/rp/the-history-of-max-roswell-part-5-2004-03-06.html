The History of Max Roswell Part 5

6 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 6 Present... I was standing on the sidewalk, sun shining straight into my face. I started walking down the deserted sidewalk. No one was walking on it but me. I sat down on a bench and a paper came right onto my lap. I picked the paper up and read it. It said that Global Wrestling was recruiting people for there fed. I folded the paper and tucked it neatly into my jacket pocket. I got off the bench and started walking back to my house. I passed one lady on the sidewalk but it didn’t look like she was in a good mood. I turned onto the next street and there was a blue house with white trim. It was my house I had just purchased last month. A whole bunch of stuff was still in its boxes cause I didn’t have enough time on my schedule. Besides, my mom came over every day and helped me unpack. I walked up to the front door and turned the knob. “Hey mom!” I yelled over the house. There was no answer. “Hey Mom! Are you there!” I yelled. There was no answer again. I walked over to the kitchen table and saw a note taped onto the side of the wooden chair. I ripped the note off the chair and read it. “Hey Max. Sorry I’m not here organizing all this stuff. The hospital needed me to work overtime. Lunch should be in the fridge. I did some stuff. Hope you like what I did. Love mom.” The hospital always needed mom to work overtime. Its like she is the best worker in the hospital. I never really did get to see my mom now. It was like she never existed. I only see her late at night. She walks into the door at like 12:30 at night and wakes me up. I don’t really mind it anymore. I’ve gotten used to it. And, I always stay up longer than 12:00 at night so there isn’t really a difference. “Now, what should I do today?” I asked myself. Then I remember the paper I had stuck into my jacket pocket. I opened my pocket and pulled the piece of paper out and layed it on the wooden table. I read it over again and asked myself a question. Do I want to be a wrestler? Suddenly the door flung open and my friend Brady raced into my kitchen. He was out of breath. “Hey Max. Wanna come see Rob Zombie. There playing tonight and I got 3 tickets. I wanna take you and Mark. You in for it?” he asked me. “Sure. When is it tonight?” I asked him. “At 7:00. Its going to be awesome.” he said. He raced back out the door. I looked at the clock that was hanging on the wall by the fridge. It read 4:00. I still had lots of time for the concert. Then I thought, did I really want to be a wrestler. It would be kind of fun, but lots of pain. Then I thought, in school I was always a daredevil showing off for my friends. “Its final. I’m going to be a wrestler.” I said. I ran out of my house again and down the street. It had said on the paper that GW Headquarters was in Chicago on main street. I had moved to Chicago a couple years ago. I stopped to catch my breath and then started running again to the headquarters. I stopped in front of a huge building. At the very top it had a big blue and black sign that had the abbreviation GW. I ran into the door and saw a long brown counter where there were 5 people working behind it. “Hello. Can I speak to the owner of Global Wrestling.” I asked one of the ladies that was standing behind the long brown counter. “Just a second sir.” she said back to me. She walked to the back office and came back a few minutes later with something in her hand. She went to where I was standing and handed me a card. “This is a key card to get into the office of the owner of GW. Please bring it back when you are done talking to her.” she said. “Her?” I asked. “Yeah. Christina McCoy is the owner of Global Wrestling. Her dad Bubba McCoy gave her the company.” she said to me. “Oh” I replied with. I took the card out of the ladies hand and ran to the elevator. I pushed the button and a little orange button came up. The doors opened and I walked in and waited for the doors to close. They closed and I start rising from the ground. The elevator stopped and I walked out and was standing face to face with the owner of Global Wrestling’s door. I turned the knob and walked into the room with confidence as the scene faded.
