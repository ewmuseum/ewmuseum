Once a Blood, Always a Blood

10 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 51003 Days may come and days may go, but these are the days I will forever cherish, for these are the days I've dedicated to you. ---------- Thunder rattled the glass pane of the window nearest my bed. My eyes snap open, facing the ceiling above me. I look around slowly, gaining my bearings. I'm in bed, at home. Something doesn't feel right. The storm raging on outside? No, it's been this way for the past few nights. The fact that I'm listening to a cd I've never fully listened to before? No, music doesn't affect me this way. The empty spot next to me in my bed? Shit, where is Cindy? I get up and step into my slippers, not bothering to put on more than my grey boxers and white tank top. I move toward the door, listening for her downstairs. Nothing. I run down the stairs, trying to keep from tripping over my own feet, and do a quick check of the floor. Still nothing. Two places left to check. The basement theater, and the garage. Listening carefully, nothing seems to be playing downstairs in the surround sound filled movie room. I slide across the kitchen floor and pull the garage door open. Stingray. Z06. Spyder. No Cindy. Fear fills me now, knowing Kullypso and his fellow Harvesters would love to get ahold of my wife in an attempt to get to me. I spin around on my heel and see a yellow piece of paper stuck to the refrigerator. 'Derek- Cindy's with me. Don't worry, I'll take care of her. You know how to reach me if you need to. Love, Tiffany.' Tiffany. Thank god. Cindy went to the mall with my cousin. Damn, why didn't she wake me up before she left? If nothing else, left the note on her pillow so I wouldn't freak out like I did? Anyway, now that I knew where she was, I relaxed a little. I open the fridge and grab the orange juice. I blindly open the cabinet overhead and pull out a glass, then fill it with juice before replacing the carton and closing the door. Walking into the living room, I decide to watch a little TV before getting dressed. Damn I'm lazy. I really hope nothing has happened involving GW since Evolution. I'm certainly a marked man right now. Kullypso's reappearance and alignment with the Harvesters makes them all after me. Those guys never could do anything alone. The surge of talent shooting up the ranks makes me a prime target for guys attempting to make a name for themselves, as I've been in this same company for longer than 99% of the roster. BJP. BJP. BJP. BJP. I don't trust him. He wants to be my friend. He and Jay are allied now. He wants me to give in to the fact that I feel the surge of energy flowing through my veins. He wants me to accept fate and rejoin him under the green Y pyros. I have never left the YB. The YB have never left me. I'm one of the few to ever call himself a Blood and never leave the group. Jay. My best friend. Well, best friend that isn't also family. Jay wants me and Ben to make amends. He wants to reform the best stable to ever grace the wrestling business. The Young Bloods. I've been a Blood since the first televised show I ever wrestled on in the GW's predecessor, UCWF. I beat my opponent, Iceman, and went to the back. Later on, Iceman attacked the Bloods, so I came out to fend him off, and when I was out there, Cruse gave me a YB shirt. Not just any shirt with Young Blood written on it. He gave me the shirt off his very back. He'd previously known me for years, as I was dating his neice. However, I wanted to make it on my own. Have I made it yet? That is a matter of opinion. For those who say 'making it' is winning the World Title, I've never been given a fair shot at making it. Now is my chance. Quartz and I will face off soon in the World Tournament. Now, to those who say I've become one of the most recognizable and favored superstars in the business, that is making it. I'm the best GW Superstar to never hold the World Title. If anybody wants to challenge that, I'll be more than happy to prove exactly what I've meant for years. You don't fuck with a Young Blood.
