A Return to Patience...Exercises in Futility

13 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-13-2004 The sun crept slowly over the horizon, a brilliant birth after a single nights unaffected slumber. A whole spectrum of colors break across the land as the sun peeks its uppermost curve above the horizon. The sound of metal clashing against metal can be heard in the background, and the shot pulls back until we are met with two very different warrior’One gleams resplendent in golden armor; the other, wears samurai dress. Both seem to know their way around a swordfight’The blonde haired, golden armored fellow constantly attacks to be turned away in defense by the samurai. Each smooth stroke is countered and turned aside, as sweat begins to break out on the faces of the two combatants. The armored fellow raises his sword for a huge stroke, and the samurai stands ready to deflect once again, when the larger man stops his stroke, and reaches in with his free left hand, grabbing the throat of the samurai?”. Pendragon: You are always predictable, Somitsu?. Pendragon lets loose of the throat, and Somitsu bows low before him?. Somitsu: And you the opposite, my young lord’And yet, of patience, I would say you have much to learn. Pendragon shakes his head and claps the man on the shoulder?. “Seeking knowledge, I hunted down the best masters of combat still left in this passing and dying world. Somitsu would teach patience and meditation. Two things that the defensive Japanese were known well for?” **********FLASH************* Much like a movie, the flashback plays out’IN turn, Somitsu narrates, and then the viewer merely watches the happenings. What occurs is a fluid merge of past and present? “I am Somitsu, master of Samurai lore, and friend, vassal and teacher to my liege lord, the Lord King Venneman Pendragon. It is here that I shall pick up the tale of my lord, for this is how he would wish it” It was long ago that my friend first sought me out’In the land of Japan, where I had sought solitude from the masses?” “Once more, dawn. Clean and bright on a crisp spring morning. A rider climbs an unseemly pass up the side of a rambling mountain. Clad in a gray cloak, and leather pants, I was met with a younger visage of Venneman Pendragon. Tall and straight in the saddle, yet not quite so large and strong as he is today. I let him come to me, as I knew one day he would’Like so many others had before him. They sought me to teach them death, and death is all they left me with. This one, as was foretold, was different. He had a light about him. So was the coming of the White lord, as my scrolls passed from my father foretold. He rode tirelessly upon a white steed, never heeding the chill as he advanced upon the mountain. Proud and fair, he was a young rock. I waited at the mouth of my home, a mountain cave, near the top of the pass. At last, he arrived?” Pendragon: I seek the master’Somitsu. I have come many miles and I wish to speak with him? The smaller Japanese man, clad in assorted skins, grunts and doesn’t look up from the fire with which he sits behind. Suddenly, a great burst of flame bursts from the small fire. Pendragon flinches and the smaller man laughs openly at him? Pendragon: Art thou a wizard, who would laugh so openly at me? The small man chuckles again and motions for Pendragon to get down off of his horse. Pendragon shakes his head and dismounts, hunkering down across from the smaller man? Pendragon: I seek Somitsu?. Somitsu: and you have found him? Pendragon: It was said that I must come here’to learn. Somitsu: and learn you shall? The scenes switch quickly. We see Pendragon’s ascent into ancient knowledge under the tutelage of the wizened Somitsu. In firelight, Pendragon moves through the forms of a martial art, clad only in loose black pants. Somitsu nods approvingly as the man goes through each move flawlessly? Next, the two fight with wooden kendo sticks. Back and forth the battle rages, with Somitsu deflecting each stroke of his stronger foe. At last, Pendragon leads to far and Somitsu strikes, taking him down with a whack to the head? Somitsu: Patience?.You must learn patience. Youth and eagerness are two valuable tools, but no more. You must anticipate what your opponent is to do if you are to survive. Pendragon:”. Again, the scenes flash by, and Pendragon seems a bit more sure of himself in each one. Finally, they fight with real swords. Back and forth they go, with Pendragon lying back a little but more this time. Once more, Pendragon over extends, and Somitsu pounces, only when he swings, Pendragon deflects the blow as he races in close and tackles the smaller man. Somitsu grunts with surprise as Pendragon smiles down at him? Pendragon: Patience?.. Somitsu:”.. **********flash*********** Somitsu: he was a strong youth, and intelligent. Finally, he had learned all that I had to teach. The time had come at last for me to pass down my mantle to him’And for he to do as he would, go whither he would? Pendragon kneels before the smaller man, wearing a kimono of blazoned red. Somitsu wears a black kimono, and he carries a samurai sword. With a nod, he extends the sword, hilt out, to Pendragon. Pendragon accepts the sword with a nod, and he rises. In turn, Somitsu nods before the King? Pendragon: Thou shalt always and forever have mine protection, master Somitsu’Thou hast taught me more than I have learned in an age’And so as thou would raise me, so to shall I raise thee?.Rise, Somitsu’By mine right hand thou art now sir? He touches the blade to each of Somitsu’s shoulders, and Somitsu nods his acceptance? *****Flash***** Somitsu: And now you return to me, young lord. Little changed you are, except in your eyes? Pendragon: “tis true. Mine eyes have witnessed untold miseries; both on the battlefield and in the so called peaceful parts of this world. I have a new quest, and mine enemies now grow strong before me. Patience was a virtue which thee taught well, but now I have a need for swiftness. Lest I be consumed with the darkness that plagues the world” Pendragon lowers his head, and his eyes blaze?. Pendragon: And the darkness is cold? Somitsu: Your enemies will come to you soon enough, my lord. It is written, it shall come to pass. Pendragon: Freeze. A puppet of a lesser man plagues mine dreams. The dastardly duo worries me. They have become an unforeseen distraction. I must concentrate on the tasks at hand, and the current task is a knave who calls himself Jetsui?. Somitsu’s eyes widen at the mention of Jetsui, but if you didn’t see it, you would not guess his surprise by his tone of speech? Somitsu: A distraction’And yet, is it not so, that as the Lord of the White, your job is to rid the world of evil where it would fester as an open wound? Pendragon: Aye??tis so, and Freeze shall meet his end soon enough, but why the surprise over Jetsui? Somitsu raises his eyebrows in a “Whatever do you mean?” look? Pendragon: Come now’I have known thee for too long for thou to mislead me? Somitsu: He is Japanese, and yet he has the ire of a westerner. He is a mix of both worlds, and he is evil. Do not fear distraction from Freeze’Jetsui is your number one target of evil? Pendragon: The man mocks all that is good, and laughs at death. Death being the easy way out he claims. And yet, he knows nothing of me. He knows not the cleansing power of the white’But he shall’And yet, continually mine thoughts turn to Freeze’The puppet of Akuma. He is the kind of soul who would use the weak, whom I am sworn to protect, against me’Questions cloud mine mind, and I must have utmost concentration. The game of chess has begun, and mine shall begin as the defensive play. Patience, so you teach so ardently, shall be mine number one weapon as of now. How long it lasts is another story? Somitsu claps Pendragon on the back, shakes his head, and smiles. The two chuckle as they descend into further conversation? *******FLASH******* “Somitsu is an excellent teacher, and as always his advice rings with chords of truth in mine mind. He taught me patience and quickness. Two virtues seldom melded together. Patience”.” The shot clears and we see Venneman Pendragon, atop his steed Xerxes, as always, decked in golden armor, Excalibur belted to his side’For some reason, he is in the middle of a huge traffic jam. He looks this way and that, but nobody is moving. A wry grin passes over his countenance?. Pendragon: Patience’For some reason, it is lacking at this particular time’This world moves too quickly at most times, and too slowly at times like these?..Good Christ!
