The Release

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-27/28-03 Day three of the 'Spread the Word' campaign. First it was Johnny Cruse, my mentor, uncle, and best friend. Then it was two of my long time stable mates, TLM Jay Robson and Ben J. Pailthorpe. This time I'm going to try and make it official. Yes, folks. I'm going to ask for my release. Christina McCoy? Hell no. I'm going to the office of one of my bitterest enemies ever. Once again, yes ladies and gentlemen. Derek Frost is paying a little visit to the offices of none other than the GW Co-owner, leader of the Nevermore, and my opponent in 3 days. Salaok. ---- Stepping out of the driver's seat of my Chrysler Spyder, I walk toward the office building that looms before me. The closer I get, the more nervous I get. My head is a swirl of emotion, with questions clogging my brain. What will he say? Why am I asking him instead of McCoy? I've never had a problem with her, or her father. So why am I going to one of the most hated men in our business to do this? What will I do after I leave here? Does that even matter right now? The only thing that seems to be constant in my mind is a set of lyrics I heard on the way over. You speak your mind to me again, You force your words so deep and then You try to tell me how to live But it's my life. 'My Life' by 12 Stones. More questions for myself. Why are these lyrics so persistant in my mind right now? Is this just a way for me to get back at all the guys who have tried to hold me back in the past? If so, why am I so uncertain about it? I finally reach the door, and pull it open. I walk inside and over to the desk in the middle of the lobby. The receptionist recognizes me. 'Yes Mr. Frost, how may I help you today?' 'You could start by getting me into Salaok's office, and fast.' 'I'm sorry, Mr. Frost. Salaok is not in right now, but if you'd like you may wait for him.' BZZZZ The receptionist picks up the telephone on the desk. 'GW Main Offices, this is Terra, how may I help you?' (voice over the phone) 'Let that man into my office, NOW!' 'Umm, Y-yes sir. Mr. Frost, Salaok will see you now.' 'I thought you said he wasn't in.' 'I.. Well he..' The receptionist ends with a sigh as she pushes a button on the side of her desk and a door behind her slides open. 'Right this way.' I follow the young secretary into the office. The desk is rather orderly, with only a small stack of papers to one side. The back of the huge black leather executive chair is to me, covering the bottom portion of a painting. This painting is unique, as it has the typical style of a piece done in the 1700's, yet is of all of the Harvesters together. I no more have a seat than I hear a voice from behind the chair. 'I know why you're here.' 'You do?' 'Yes, Derek. It's obvious.' I didn't think anybody but my closest friends and family knew. 'You are here, Derek, to see if I will reconsider changing the booking of the PayPerView. You don't want to face me. Honestly, Derek, I don't want to face you either. I would much rather be teaching that child we know as Brick a lesson, or keeping Sean Price and Deacon Holmes in line.' 'Salaok, I'm only here to ask...' 'No. I will not change the match between us. Mainly because I have no authority over what that daddy's girl Christina McCoy and her redneck shadow do.' I stand up and take a step toward the desk. 'I want a release. Are you happy? I try to do it calmly but you jump to fucking conclusions, assuming I'm too scared to face you one on one. But you know what, Salaok? I will be there at Whiplash, and I will take you to a level you've never seen me at before.' Salaok smiles his sadistic smile, then motions for me to sit down. 'Good. I'll welcome a little more competition. It's just too bad to see you say that now, when asking for permission to leave, than months ago when we really needed you.' 'Bullshit, GW never needed me.' 'You say that now, but think back to right after Bubba was put in a coma. Our top line guys were on the fringe. Who else had the ability to step up and take the position of top hero of the GW? Washed up TLM Jay Robson? Pailthorpe perhaps? It was either you or Brick. Personally, I'd rather have seen you do it. I've worked with you, I know your potential.' Salaok and I stare at each other for the longest time, before finally I extend my hand to him. 'Thank you, Salaok. I never thought I would get the chance to say this to you, but of all the guys in that locker room, there is none I respect more than you. Despite the hell we've put each other though in and out of the ring, if I could pick one tough son of a bitch to have on my good side, I would have to go with you, hands down.' 'Frost, why are you asking for this release? I want reasons, and they better be good if you want to walk out of here with a paper in your hand.' 'Well, I just feel that I've done all I can do in the GW right now. I need to broaden my horizons a little. You know, find some fresh ass to kick. Get a bit more experience against guys I don't know, which will help me for when I return to GW in the future.' Salaok looks kind of shocked. 'So you want to return in the future?' 'Oh definitely. I refuse to retire from this business until I have at least one GW World Title reign under my belt.' 'That is great to hear.' He looks down and scribbles a little bit, then looks up at me, smiles, then stands up. 'Here you go. I made sure to add a clause in it so that you have to appear at Whiplash, so I can get my final match out of you before you leave. You think I'd let our tag history go to waste? The fans will eat it up.' I laugh a little, then shoot a remark at him. 'You mean my fans. You don't have any, remember?' 'True, true. Although, it appears I do have one fan.' He looks at me and smiles a little, the evil faded out of his eyes thsi time.' 'Yes, you do. I always have been.' And with that, we shake hands, transferring the paper in the process, and I leave.
