Wrongly Accused

29 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-29-03 “You don’t understand man” I have not been able to find her for months. Its like she upped and Vanished with my Son.” Johnny said as he sat in the small room hand cuffed to a table. A men dressed in black and white suit walk around. A recorder, and a file sits on the empty table. “Look Mr.. We know your history of Violence. We have the reports from a few years ago where you went off the deep end. When your Wife had so called cheated on you with one Cain Tapp and Davis Storm. We know about your connections to a few high profile crime lords. You cannot deny it can you. Mr. Cruse. Or should I say Mr. Cruisecinne!” The man slammed his fist downs on the desk causing the recorder to bounce. Johnny hangs his head down for a bit as the man stair intently at him. Johnny looks rather bad and it is noticeable he has not shaved for a while as he has a rough beard going. “Look man. I told you. I was training some students at my Do jo. I got the call from my brother Bobby. Valire had called him sounding very worried. I rushed home as fast as I could” Johnny says ++++ The rain pores down as Johnny speeds down the road in the dark. Visibility; near nothing. But Johnny has made this drive hundreds of times that the way is mostly burned into his brain. He could drive it blind. All of a sudden there is a loud popping sound and Johnny slams on the breaks causing the car to fly in a tailspin. The car slides to a stop sitting in the middle of the road high beams on. “Damn it! What the fuck was that!” Johnny screams in frustration, as he slams his fist into the wheel of the car. Johnny opens the door leaving the car on. He looks and all four tires are blown out. He scratches his head in disbelief as he reaches back in the car for a flashlight. In the glove box is a Pistol. Johnny grabs that as well as the flashlight and leaves the emergency flashers on the car. He then gun in one hand and flashlight in the other runs in the rain the last stretches to his home. Faster to cut though the trees he thinks and turns and heads up the side of the mountain. He gets up to the top of the hill that he lives on and notices that there is a row of black cars sitting in the driveway. A Black Hearse sits there as well. “I don’t remember there being any funeral services”. Salaok. Fuck.!” Johnny spits out as he races? As he walk runs his mind drifts. Why would Salaok be here? What reason would he have to be here? Hell I am not a active performer no more. Jay. Must be something to do with Jay. The screams of his wife bring him out of his thoughts. As he does Johnny feels the volts of electricity before falling face first. Forcefully he makes his self-staggers as men in black faceless mask pull his wife and son to the hearse. Johnny starts to run at the men when another wave of electricity slams into his body dropping him to his knees then down face first to the around. The screams of his wife fill his ears as he drifts away into unconsciousness. He sees the hearse pull away. ++++ “That’s what I saw. Honest to god. That is what I saw. You have the statement from my brother. Now I would like to speak to my Lawyer please. ? Johnny says. “A few more questions first. Mr. Cruse. ? Says the man “First off why did you not report thi.. ? “I did! I did report it. To the Miami police department.”, Johnny looks up his eyes on fire. “Interesting. There is no evidence to prove this. We show no records of a report to the Miami Police department. We checked your house you say you have not been back since you left when you woke. Well we have no signs of a forced entry. No signs of struggle. No jewelry, no valuables. Nothing missing from the house. We check the ph9one logs and No call was placed to your brother from that home.” The man says as he sits down and opens the file. “Look. Are you saying I had something to do with this” If you are your crazy! I have looked since you law enforcement officers have said jack squat about this. Now you bring me in here question me. Saying that you got a tip of foul play. Who is paying you huh! Is it Salaok! Look man all I want is my family back. There is nothing to gain for him to be doing this! Look The Davis’s are known for this shit. Look into them. “We have. They are clean. IN fact Salaok. This man you claim for kidnapping your family. Just recently lost his wife. I doubt he did this.” Says the man in black. “Whaa” Lisha is dead. How? Tell me man HOW!” Johnny screams. “Murder. Some one shot her inn there home.” He says looking at the files. “Look I have to get out of here. I need to see my lawyer.. I need to make a phone call it is my right to do this. ? Johnny says with a now concerned and worried look. The door opens and in walks a man in a very nice blue silk suit. “Excuse me there is a interrogation going on in here sir.” Says the man in black The man in blue silk puts down his briefcase and takes a seat. “I know this sir. I am Harry Malfoy. I represent Mr. Cruse here. I believe my client asked for his phone call.” “Fine one call.” Says the man. Who steps out as Johnny turns to Harry. “Harry! Good to see you harry.” Says Johnny. “I know Mr. Cruse. Some mess. Do you know how hard you been to find.” Says Harry in a annoyed tone. Johnny starts to say something when a round officer steps into the room a file in his arm. He looks at the two. “Ahh good your Lawyer is here. We can get right down to business. Mr. Cruse do you know why you are here. Johnny and Harry both look at each other. “Officer. Your men have already questioned my client.” Says Harry The officer looks at Harry with a strange look?. “I am sorry but we hav..” Johnny and Harry look at each other once more as the police officer makes his speech. What is going on here? Is the only thing Johnny could think of?
