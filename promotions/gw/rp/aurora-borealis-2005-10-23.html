Aurora Borealis...

23 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/23/05 One match lost, last week versus Jake Hix. But it didn't effect Danielle like most of the matches she lost did. She was proud of herself in the fact she still gave it her all. Hix was one of the finalist for the Master of the Mat Tourney and one of Global's supported bread and butter men. She did all she could to try to win against him, just like she had the week before that where she had secured her first solid title defense and in spirit, picked up two more titles and merged them into the Platinum Title. And for some reason, to her, that is what counted. But this week would be different. She would face the man she took the pre-merged Platinum Title belt from: Virgil Ambrosi. She was sure he would be out for blood. His attempt at revenge had been nixed by the coming of Blaise McClancy and he'd never got to get that one over Josh after the insult to his woman, Shadow. Along with the other members of the Hunting Grounds Stable, especially that woman who was in love with Ambrosi. She was sure they would interfere one way or another. Unlike her little group of people, which had an honor code that went into the deep roots of the beginnings. Hunting ground could not ever boast such a thing. They were nothing more then a means of one man to proclaim his greatness. Thanks to the tranquilizers that the doctors had long ago given her, Dani was asleep before the plane took off from Las Vegas. She had only woke up a few minutes after the plane landed in Fairbanks. Luckily she was used to cold and snow, albeit this was close to what she usually had in South Dakota for winter, and this was only Fall up here in Alaska. The winter wonder land that laid before her was inviting and asking for her to have fun. But she had to remind her self she was there for work, but there was always a little time to play. And Danielle already knew what she wanted to do. It was the beginning of the time that the Aurora Borealis was shown just up here in Fairbanks, and everywhere had places to go to see them during the main parts of the evening. The Northern lights where here and she wanted to at least see them once before they had to go back to Vegas. The ride to the Inn that Ukyo had booked for them was pretty uneventful. Unlike Ukyo and Blaise, with her from South Dakota and the Brands from Washington State, the cold wasn't anything new to them. Once they were at the inn though, that's when the activity levels went up. As they all started to thaw out, they started to take care of things. Danielle got her things unpacked first then headed out of the room a little peeved her Cell phone wasn't working, which meant she couldn't check on Angel. It was her first time away from him and she wanted to make she he was handling Kaylee okay, and was happy. Then when Ukyo and Blaise headed out, she just had this feeling of things going down hill quick. The good news came when Blaise told Gray to use his laptop with the Order's Sat-link to talk to Kaylee. That brought her up a few levels on her happiness factor. But she had to wait for Josh and Gray to get their stuff ready, so she finished up making the other fires and getting the place warmed up. She noticed that every time he got a chance though, Josh checked in on her, even if it was just a glance off to the side. Since her manifestation in the Shopping Mall, last week, Josh hadn't really not let her out of his sight. He'd taken to not even messing with the recording room that Blaise had setup. He was obviously worried what would happen if she manifested again and wanted to be there if it happened. She had confided in him all that led up to it, and the fact that it wasn't painful. She was glad Ukyo was there, otherwise she would have been up the creek with out a paddle, stuck there until the veil closed. It wasn't exactly her idea of fun. But over all she got a few nice dresses for herself. She eventually had gotten out of it all when Annie had shown up. They couldn't get her wings to go back in, so Annie had used some of her magic of Glamorie, similar to the type that she used to hide Kaylee's real hair color of sea foam green and made it blonde. Using the glamorie, she's made it look as is Dani had been wearing a backpack of some sort and had made the lady in charge of the dressing room forget about seeing her wearing it, since a backpack wouldn't be allowed back in the dressing area. A few hours later, after getting home, the wings had faded into nothingness while Dani was pacing in her room. She found she could do just about anything with them in movements that she wanted them to, within their designs. She had a few hours with Angel where they both could flap a bit and it seemed to allow her to bond with her feathered friend a bit more. Now, they were in Alaska for five days, coming up on what would be an eventful week with their matches and all. Blaise and she were booked, the boys were up there for support and also just in case Global needed them. After the boys were done unpacking, Gray showed Dani how to use the Voice over IP service the Order provided and talked to Kaylee about her baby that she was watching. She had two others also, two handfuls named Kaylie and Kiley... red haired twins who were from Blaise's ex-finance. Shortly after that Blaise and Ukyo showed up with a slightly late dinner and Blaise seemed to be working things through his head about everything else. He was a bit detached, even when he called Kaylee to check in on his young ones. Dani decided it was best to let them take care of things, and she wanted to get out a bit also. They would be here for five days, and there was still one more thing that needed to be taken care of. They had a rental car waiting for them, that had to be picked up at a certain time. ?Ukyo?? Dani caught her out in the living room, putting away some of the leftovers from the dinner. ?Yeah? What can I help you with, Dani?? Dani pulled out the paperwork for the rental car. ?It's almost time for this. I'm thinking about heading out to go get it. You mind?? “Oh, it is almost time to go pick that up. I don't think Blaise is ready for treading out anymore tonight. You go ahead and head out for it. I think we had agreed you'd be the one to rent it anyway. I don't have a normal U.S. License yet, mine's still international. And the others have too many marks on their record. Go ahead. The place is right next to the airport, only a few miles away.” “Yeah, I know. I'm going to see if Josh wants to join me. But, this time of night... I can't guarantee when we'll be back.” “Sounds good to me. Just remember, keep the heater on.” Dani chuckled at her comment as she went to collect her boots and jacket from the foyer. Josh walked out of their bedroom with his Xbox's travel case in his hands. He and his brother looked to be getting ready to use the high speed internet provided by the inn, as Gray was ass up while he dug around the area of the entertainment center, probably looking for the high speed cable. Josh looked down at her, as she was seated, fastening her boots up. She looked up at him and made the offer to go with her. ?Want to go brave Alaska with me?? Josh looked up at her and smiled, Yeah he was from a place that got cold in the winter, but snow didn't always fall. He turned and looked out the window of the room. “I've got a better idea, I give this to Gray so he can play Halo 2 all night, and then you come here and we stay warm and snuggle all night.” Danielle just smiled coyly at him, then stood up to put her jacket on. “Suit yourself. I didn't come here to stay in a hotel all weekend. There is more to see to this place. I know that Mondy will be spent doing all the promotional stuff. Sunday night we're having to do that dinner at the Blue Loon Club. But so that cuts us down to tonight tomorrow to go see stuff. We've got the Northern Lights, the museums, and history of this place. I've also got to go get the rental from the place we arranged it for. They said it would be ready after seven o' clock, should be ready by the time we get there.” Josh nodded at her, and then dropped the case for his Xbox onto the couch. ?Hey, you know how to set this thing up?? Gray's head came out from under the entertainment center. “Yeah... I'm the one that showed you how to use the network setup.” Josh smiled. “Good, I'm heading out with Dani to get the rental. But I don't know when she's planning on coming back.” Gray smiled. “Good, I'll give you two till 2 am. Not back by then, you get the couch when you walk in, cause I'll be in the room in bed.” Josh just shook his head as he grabbed his jacket, and then slid his feet into a set of warm boots. He made his way to the door with Dani. “You heard the man, now lead the way.” Danielle grabbed her key for the room and just walked out of the building with Josh hot on her heels. They walked out and flagged down one of the Taxi's for the area. It wasn't a long trip back down near the airport to where the rental agency was housed out of. A half an hour later, some paperwork in triplicate and flashing of the credit card provided for them by the Order under the name of the company they supposedly work for and they were leaving in a 2005 Ford Explorer Envoy. As both climbed into it for the first time, taking in that new car smell, Josh asked the obvious question. ?So where to first?? Dani turned the rental car on and it purred to life. She looked at the digital clock. ?How far is it to Chena Hot Springs?? Josh seemed to think about it for a moment before answering. ?I heard only about an hour or so. Why?? “The Northern lights are best seen outside the town thats where we will be heading. I've heard they have a setup where you can inside this big auditorium and just look up and see them.” She tosses a tourist map in his lap. “You're navigating.” At that, she pulled out and started heading up the Steese Expressway towards Chena Hot Springs. The night sky was already starting to change, the veil of light that hid the stars from view was starting to give way. And as the darkness crept across the land the lights of the city came on behind them as they drove off. The trip took a little more then an hour to get to a place where the city lights didn't veil the stars, the crisp air fresh clean and clear, made the stars in the sky bright. The place they ended up was a building at the Chena Hot Springs resort called the Auroratorium. A building that had large plate glass windows looking towards the North Easterly sky with a heated interior. There was about another 15 or so people there, looking for the same thing, a chance to see something you only read about in books or see in pictures. The experience of the Northern lights is a breathe taking, awe inspiring thing. As the ribbon of lights started to dance across the sky, the formed a veil of curtains between this world and the world of dreams. The lights danced in Danielle's eyes. ?You know this isn't the only place you can see them, right?? Dani just nodded at him as she stared up to the sky. She'd seen the lesser hints of them when she was in the Dakotas. They can sometimes be seen in bits as far south as the US/Canada border, but nothing like they could be seen here. To go further north in Alaska meant you went with all your fuel for your four wheel drive vehicle leaded up and with some means of calling for air support if you got lost or stuck. “You can see it from Siberia, Finland, Norway and a few other places too. It's almost as if at this spot on the earth there is a ring with an electric crown being worn by the planet. Basically the beginning of the Artic Circle.” Dani was enthralled with the view up in the sky, but Josh was bored, actually thinking of other things. His hand traveled to the large pocket of his unzipped jacket and the small package that had been given to him a few weeks ago by Dani's grandfather. It contained something that was his way of approving their relationship. ?So, you ready for this week?? Dani didnt answer right away. ?You ready for this week?? She snapped out of it slightly as he touched her shoulder. ?Huh?? ?You ready for your match this week?? “Yeah, what's there to worry about? Ambrosi always seems to be thinking about other things. He thinks that he's so bad ass because his kind were here for so long compared to the 'mortals' he sees a food. I'm a higher life form then him. As we've found out. After these last few weeks I don't think there is anyone in Global I actually fear.” One of Josh's hands was on her shoulder and the other on the small case in his pocket. He had her off guard, maybe it was the time. ?Danielle, this is beautiful isn't it?? Danielle just nodded her head. He nuzzled into the back of her neck, moving her long hair out of the way. He kisses it lightly and catches her a bit more of guard at his actions. “Josh... there are people here.” Dani looked around, seeing the others in the room. Mostly couples from the looks of it. One seemed interested in the younger couple and what they were doing. An elderly couple who sat there, snuggled into each other and enjoying the view, and watching those that act like they did so many years ago. Josh moved around towards the front of Dani and looked to be getting a bit nervous. “Dani...” She looked up at him. ?Yes, Josh?? “Dani... as you know a few weeks ago I got to have a nice long talk with your grandfather. About you and me.” “Yes, I know this rather well.” He fumbles around with the case in his pocket and looks down at the ground. “Well, Dani... I was wondering.” He started to drop down, using the railing next to the window for support. Dani's eyes went wide, but... Something was wrong. Dani could feel it coming from Josh. Josh started to pull the case from his pocket, but something got caught on the inner lining. He turned around slightly to work it loose and then saw something out of the corner of his eye... A woman, standing there out in the falling snow. But not just any woman. Through the vision that Josh had been gifted with when he was born, that of a sensitive and an empath, Josh saw the woman wearing long flowing robes and a pair of wings stretching from her back bearing the markings of that of an eagle. ?What the hell?? Josh came up from his partial crouch and pulled his hands out as he headed for the door out to the field of snow. As he did, the woman snapped her looks from the window... looking at Dani, to looking at Josh as he came out. He burst through the doors and headed right for her. ?Who are you?? The woman had a look of surprise on her face as he came out. She couldn't believe she'd been seen. But then again she didn't know about Josh's other abilities. She turned on heel and headed right for the forest ridge a couple hundred yards away. Josh wasn't setup for running in the snow, and she was almost walking on top of it, as it fell. Needless to say, he fell far behind and lost the woman from view as the wind suddenly picked up and started to blow the newly fallen snow right at him. ?Damnit!? Ten minutes was all it took, and he was heading back to the Auroratorium with out anything to go for, and a ruined opportunity for what he wanted to do up here. “Who ever you are, you owe me now.” He'd have to report it to Blaise, but somehow he'd need to get out of it with Dani. He walked back into the Auroratorium and noticed that she wasn't there. ?Dani?? The old man, sitting over on the other side with his wife motioned for him to walk over. “I think your lady friend headed for the little girls room.” He pointed at the two marked doors on the back wall of the room. “Thank you.” The old man chuckled. ?You had the ring all ready, didn't you?? Josh's face went red with that comment. ?Was I that obvious?? ?Yeah. Too bad. What was that all about?? “Nothing, just thought I saw something, one of our friends staying back in the inn in Fairbanks. They threatened to ruin it for me.” The old man chuckled again. “Looks like they did. Better luck next time. Oh, and next time... just spit it out, and have the ring already out when you do it. Don't let anything get in the way.” Josh nodded at him. “Thanks for the advice.” “Hey, we've not been married for 57 years by letting the lining of a jacket pocket get in our way.” The old man smiled a partially toothless smile at him, and he and his wife held each other close while they looked up at the Aurora Borealis and Josh nodded to them again. In the bathroom, Dani was there. But she wasn't there for the obvious reason. She stood there looking at herself in the mirror, trying to absorb what had almost just happened there, with a big, wide, smile on her face. Outside of there, Josh pulled the ring's case out of it's pocket and opened it up. The large diamond glittered in the lighting provided from the electrical show above. He pulled it out and looked at the band under where the diamond was mounted. Engraved there, barely able to be seen by the unaided eye were two names with a heart between them... Daniel and Lynn. He put it up as Dani walked out. She still had the smile on her face, especially as she saw Josh fumble to put it up. ?What time is it?? Josh looked at the clear window on his left jacket wrist that showed his watch clearly through it. “Midnight.” Her smile didn't break. ?I guess we have just enough time to head back before we loose our bed to someone who'll shed all over it. Let's get back. Maybe we can head back up here tomorrow?? “Sounds good to me.” Josh got up and they both took each other's hand as they headed out towards the parking lot. ~~To Be Continued~~
