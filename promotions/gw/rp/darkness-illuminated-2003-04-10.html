Darkness Illuminated

10 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-10-03 Darkest Deepest Depths Of HELL! The voice scream at me when I close my eyes... They scream for vengeance.... They... They are those that I have killed in the life I lead as The Lost Angel... The ice in the glass cracked and slid as Mani sat near a fire place. This isn't where I belong.. I am a shadow and I belong in the shadows... He may be my brother but it doesn't mean Salaok should house me. Plus this suit is irritating. It's just a reminder of him... The son of a bitch... Mani suddenly stood up as Lisha looked over at him.. 'Where are you going' Her voice came soft like he had never expected it could. She had been through a lot and when the message got to me I was busy... Busy dealing with my memory... But now it was all there... The fight with the boys who were 'Connected' to the original cell. The Original Cell and his location. Mani smiled sadistically.. looking down at Lisha who had kept her eyes locked on him the entire night. 'I need clothes little sister... The wardrobe I possess smells of blood...' Her eye widened slightly 'Can I Come too?' Her voice sounded almost eager. 'Lisha it is unbecoming of you to be eager. I can read thoughts and your hunger for knowledge of me is understandable but right now I need time to think and despite how harsh it might sound... You would only be a distraction...' Mani smiled again as he stepped out through the door into the massive frame of Quartz. Mani slowly looked up and down the massive man and laughed slightly.. 'Why hello large one..' Quartz simply looked down into the eyes of Mani. 'Hey man no hard feelings.. I mean I kicked your ass.... You took it like a good boy...' Before Quartz can respond Mani steps past him and walks away down the hall towards Jonathan's room. Inside the room Mani started searching for something simple to wear and came out with a mesh shirt and black jeans. A few moments late he was down the hall again and at the bottom of the stairs. When he reached them Salaok stepped out from the next room and looked across the room at him.. The two had been mortal enemies at one point... Hatred unfiltered flowed for each other at one point... Now they had a bond stronger than any friends could share. 'Mani....' Salaok's voice trailed off as Mani stared at him.. 'Than... thanks for saving Lisha..' Mani slowly nodded his head and unlatched the front door stepping outside into the cold night air. The door of the Tommy Kiara ZZ2 opened up and as he sat down memories rushed at him. ~-~ On a rain drenched roof the figure stood.. His eyes glowed a strong green and a mask covered his face. Slowly Jindoichi Kitagami stepped out onto the roof and glared at the green eye demon.. His voice came strong like a thousand crack of thunder at once... 'Shigeru, I won't let you take my boys without a fight!' The rain pelted the two men as the green eyed demon shrugged and leapt across the gap between them ripping the man's head off in a single swing. The two boys ... At that time 17 and 15 both stepped forward to meet the green eyed demon but before they could rush him their mother stood in the way taking a dagger aimed for younger boy's heart. As she fell the elder fell with her a dagger lodged in his upper leg. The elder slowly crawled to his mother as the Green eyed demon stared at them.. His voice came harsh like grinding steel as he spoke these words, 'I am called The Lost Angel... My name is Mani Shigeru..' The rain continued to pound as the younger looked down at his brother and his mother and then to the demon before him.. 'I have no title... I am just a young man...' The younger's voice came as a whisper as the words escaped his lips the tone filled with sorrow.. 'But you have taken from me.... Taken everything I care for.... Taken all but my life and my brother.' His voice suddenly crescendoing at the end 'YOU WILL NOT TAKE MY FAMILY FROM ME!' As the Demon stared at the brothers smugly the younger seemed to almost light up with a blue glow of rage as he sprung across the gap to the demon... Fist came fast knocking Mani from the roof and sending him to the ground. Fist after fist after fist... Again and again. Finally the loss of light... The darkness surrounded Mani and he blacked out.. This was when he lost all his memories..... The two connected to the Original Cell... His only failure... ~-~ Mani slowly turned the key as the car lurched to life... 'When will it end' To be continued....
