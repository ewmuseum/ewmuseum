Date: 01/07/06 Your quick guide to ye olde' yorkshire slang! (Lesson#1) A'm = I'm/I am Narh= Now t' = the Yer= You Ar' = I Ter'= to 'im = him Yorkshire Fact = The Yorkshire Pudding is a home favourite in england and was of course created in Yorkshire! Prelude: Man has witnessed many changes in todays evolving life, as the world turns and the days roll by things become clearer to the eternal sense that we have been brought up with. Maybe our strongest sense lays deep with in our souls, this sense is the pride that makes you the strongest force known to man. It makes your soul the most unbeatable force on gods forsaken planet. But with so many souls how are we to know which one is the strongest? which one can really survive battle and take on all oncomers? the answer is the Eternal Punk, Shane Wethers. A man who has shrived every last effort to make his efforts known, not because of survival...but to be the best you've got to beat the best...still...after beating stars of the past such as Carl Ca$h, Tyler Davidson, Skylar Green and many more oncomers, he still feels the need to prove that he is one of the strongest forces known to man. Back in the days of Eternal Wrestling he made the biggest impact, his face became known across the world as being the newest material on the market... it caused masses upon masses of sales on Shane Wethers merchandise, such as t-shirts, posters, baseball caps, sweaters, authentic Shane Wethers jeans and also a Flag with the official Shane Wethers trademark logo. It became obvious that the Eternal Punk would reign supreme at the top of the ladder, he had proved so much in such a short space of time. From discovering his brother Silus, whom was thought to be dead, actually framed Shane for an expired visa and as if that was not enough, Silus kidnapped his main squeeze. All ended fairly when Shane evened odds with Silus. Now the only place Silus will be watching Shane from is the television in the county jail. Things didnt begin to looks so bright for Shane after the whole melodramatic sequence of events, as his career slowed down when EW towers decided to close down and Shane became a free agent. Many offers followed from PCW and many other companies. His childhood dream was to be a PCW legend, but things were not the way he planned as arguments with The Phantom Booker caused Shane to quit PCW. His next venture consisted on joining the new SCW, ran by Fedwars owner 'Dave Jacobs' and sidekick 'Cyrus Storm'. Things went from bad to worse when SCW had to close its doors due to managerial problems. This is where Shane began to compete in local house shows until he had another offer...sadly an ankle injury caused him to be sidelined for just under 6 months. Since then he returned to fedwars hungry to get back in the ring. $oldout Wrestling were the first to approach Shane and before long the had his name in writing. He became quite quickly a big name, fighting off all members thrown at him... even stables like 'The Infamous Clique' were helpless to stop the demonic dare-devil known as Shane Wethers. Before long he was beating big names such as 'Carl Ca$h', 'Tyler Davidson' and his sidekick 'Daniel S.'. At a long awaited event at Madison Square Garden, Shane defeated Davidson to become the number one contendor for the $W Global Title. It'd be Shane Wethers versus either Tony Rolo or Shane Lawless at the next show, depending on who was the champion by the time the show came around. Bad news struck again for Shane when $W ownership was thrown around like a cheap hooker on stag night. Now after a month, Shane has found his newest venture...that being Global Wrestling. Shane has plently of history with GW and much of it isn't as bright as he would have liked it to be, but now Shane has touched land on the GW surfaces, it will only be a matter of time before the New Era proves once again that he is undoubtably 'All that and a whole lot f'n more!'. 'He's without a doubt one of the biggest icons of the new generation' -(Graham Walsh, Times magazine) 'He's very unpredictable, the only thing you can count on is being entertained by his in-ring talent' -(Luther Thomas, WOW magazine) 'I've never seen such dedication and will to win, he's got a pure appetite for destruction!' -(Darren Fisher, Real Wrestling magazine) 'Global Wrestling will be cherishing its latest franchise!'- (Dean Alberg, reporter) :: The sun can be seen still descending upon the horizon as the sky becomes clearer as usual on a summers night. People can be seen walking up or down the busy streets doing there own thing as cars roll by and try to get passed the busy road with out causing any accidents. The atmosphere of the busy town is red hot and there is the loud echo's of people talking and in lively mood. On the long winding street is a large theatrical-looking building that overlooks the city made from expensive white stone, there are masses of crowds, many people dressed up in smart clothing. There is a long red carpet that leads towards the doors, surrounded by gold stands attached by thick red ropes as a sort of isle separator. Every couple of minutes are limosine pulls infront of the isle way and a famous star walks out surrounded by security, bodyguards and other deligates. Stars such as Russell Crowe, Mick Jagger, the band members of the Foo Fighters and many many more are seen entering the building all gracing their individuality with different outfits which are sure to put them in the next copy on the Times magazine. At the side of the doors is a poster which reads 'Shane Wethers- Welcome back to GW'. A white limo suddenly pulls up at the side of the kerb, in front of the isle way. The chaffeur steps out of the drivers seat and walks around to the doors at the back of the limo, he opens them and to the crowds pleasure they see none other than Shane Wethers step out of the vehicle wearing his usual black baggy jeans, and a white official Leeds Utd football shirt, which he wears with pride for his favourite football/soccer team. Shane also has his chain hangin' from his pocket to his belt hook. His hair is in the usual Shane Wethers trademark haircut, spikey and sort of messy, but still a fashionable thing to people. Shane walks to the isle with a cocky grin on his face as a swarm of photographers and papparazi come over to him like Bees gathering the honey. The voices of a variety of questions are heard coming from the mouths of the excited press:: 'Shane...can you give us any comments on your venture into Global Wrestling?' 'Mr Wethers? hows it feel to be the latest signing to GW' 'Shane? Shane Wethers? you've recently signed a contract with Global..hows it feel to have joined the successful company?' ::Shane replies in the typical broad yorkshire accent he is known for:: Shane: OK! one at a time... Ar' know yer' all pretty excited that I 'av returned t'Global Wrestling and that'm offerin' t' take it t' the highest level physically possible, but Ar' can't answer all yer questions at tha same time. What ah can tell yer for starters, is that 'am pleased t' 'av joined GW. Its such'n endearing company, with such prospects...all it needs it someone t' turn those prospects into gold dust...and when Ar' seh' gold dust, i aren't talking about that retarded love child of Dusty Rhodes and Liza Manelli, no...no way... A'm actually talking about the sort'o gold dust that people wish would rain from the sky. A'm taking GW from a 'could be good' in t' a 'simply the best', but not like Tina Turner...or Chris Eubanks. Just observe the ratings after Ar' make my appearence soon. yer will see exactly why a'm the Eternal Punk... A'm the blood of GW, and GW just got a new donation of blood into its veins, its called the 'The New Era' Shane Wethers. Ar'm the prodical son of the GW. All the stars of GW are just stepping stones for me t' follow across, and soon enough Ah will be crossing that river. ::Once again many questions are fired at Shane by various reporters:: 'With the release of your bands fifth album 'No Saints, All Sinners' due for its release at the end of January, do you think Wethermania will once again sweep the world off its feet?' 'Is the title of your New Album any indication of what you are gonna be like in GW? :: Shane grins with the usual cocky smile well known by Wethermaniacs accross the world, and shrugs his strong shoulders:: Shane: umm... of course. Ar' have been the worlds greatest for many many years narh and just because Ar' haven't stepped in the ring for a couple of months, it does not mean Ar' have lost my special touch. Ar' am Shane-freakin-Wethers for cryin' out loud... there's not one single person who could replace me, A'm one of a kind. Who else would yer watch these days and actually see pure class? there's no one else yer can watch. This in't Lamb chops play around, i ain't gonna play around with no jacked up, dope smoking Lamb. Ar'd leave it until Silus had finished humping the damn thing and then Ar'll kick the lil' b@stard in the scrotum. Narh if yer don't mind, I've got a few awards t' collect. ::Shane walks up the isle as the bright silver lights of camera flashes light up the small area. Photographers change angles to try get a unique picture of Shane for the front cover of 'Happening magazine' and other news magazines.:: < Now all these Pickledicks can leave me alone whilst I kick back with a couple of drinks...oh, and also whilst I collect the awards for 'Best Band', 'Best video', 'Best Album' and all that stuff.> ::Shane steps through the double doors and turns left into the V.I.P lounge. The security men acknowledge Shanes presence by shakin' his Hand. Shane walks inside the bar area. Robert Downey Junior spots Shane from across the room. He is dressed in scruffy yellow farrah trousers and a stain white shirt, his hair looks like a total mess and makes peopel wonder how he got into the place. He walks over to Shane as if they know one another:: RDJ: Mr Wethers! It's good to see you again! ::RDJ puts out his hand, in the hope of a gentle handshake. Shane stares at his hand and frowns:: Shane: Again? Wiv never even met in the first place y'freak. and I in't shaking that hand of yours, you've probably been fingerin the bartender again just in the hope he'll give you a bottle of Jack Daniels. ::RDJ steps back:: RDJ: So you know me then? but for your information, I don't finger bartenders for alcohol!...I give them handjobs! ::An expression of pure repulse and horror appears on Shane face as he also steps back:: Shane: Don't yer ever say that again you damn pickledick! I ought ter kick yer ass right now for just sayin that. Damn..so many freaks, not enough circuses. ::Shane picks up a chair and smashes it over the head of Robert Downey Jr. Everybody turns around and watches as RDJ falls to the floor and lays there motionless. There is a loud round of applause from the people at the party as they seem to cheer Shane's antics. A few paramedics rush over with a strecture. They put the chair on it and leave RDJ on the floor. Shane smirks. Sean Oldfield is stood across the room leaning on the bar. Sean is Shane's friend, back from the college days. Sean is wearin a smart blue armani suit with a yellow shirt and matching tie, something suitable for a lawyer. Shane walks over to him and taps him on the shoulder, Sean turns around:: Sean: Hey bro, it's good to see ya again! Shane: Yeah, hows yer doing kid? Did you have any troubles gettin' in here? ::Sean shakes his head:: Sean: Not at all. Thanks for inviting me here though, it's not everyday you get to be backstage at the 'Kerrang Awards'. This is gonna be huge. Wait a minute...is that Robert Downey Junior laying on the floor there? ::Shane grins and nods his head:: Shane: Yup.... he pissed me off so I knocked out the pickledick. ::Sean points over at him:: Sean: Well you may have done, but he looks to be awake and now he's humping a wine bottle. ::Shane shakes his head in disbelief:: Sean: So, I think we should be celebrating now your officially back in wrestlng again and theres no better place to be than GW by the sounds of things. Shame Bobby Stormbringer is not in it, I would like to have seen you put the final nail in his coffin. But this week you are in the Elimination Gauntlet at Fusion and the winner faces the Platinum champion at Fusion! ::Shane takes a deep breath and looks straight ahead:: Shane: That don't bother me, eighteen opponents ter' kick around the ring like a second hand football. Everyone of them; just a stepping stone. ::Sean nods his head and Shane grins with pure confidence in his eyes :: Sean: Funny how you should mention that because there's a few familiar names. ::Shane looks at Sean with curiousity:: Shane: Well? go on then... break the news. ::Sean smirks:: Sean: Carl Cash... ::Shane chuckles to himself:: Shane: Au contrare! how convenient that there's a has-been in the gauntlet! not a problem fer me though! Ar've beatem 'im once and Ar'll beat him again, not a problem. But I do actually feel sorry for the bloke, he tries so 'ard(hard)...almost too 'ard! but thats nuttin' of a problem. I'll give 'im a lesson in Wethermatics, Ar'll mek' him a star! ::Sean grins, almost feedin off Shane's confidence:: Sean: Also Tim Walden... ::Shane pauses for a moment, falling into passed memories:: Shane: Ahh...Walden, t'kids got alot of go in him Ar'll give him that much, but he better not expect any niceties when come Sunday. He's just gotta understand that this is my world and eez just living in it! Anyways, who else is in the equation? Sean: Erm... Chenzo Stecchino. ::Shane falls into a fit of laughter, bringing his clenched fist down on the table:: Shane: Cushty! Chenzo Mocha-Choca-Latino. Y'know arh'd usually give him lots of credit after the sad news of Tony, but Chenzo always was the weakest link. Everywhere I've been he's never been able to compete at my level. Even in the NIWC days when Ar' helped them get their first major victory, it was only cos I knew Tony 'ad some talent. As for Chenzo, he was just thr tag along, a free gift if yer like! not so much of a gift than a throw-away! Anyway, he's gonna 'av his chance to try impress me come this Sunday, but arh' won't expect too much, arh don't care fer' disappointments! ::All of a sudden someone taps Shane on the shoulder, Shane turns around with a look of suprise and ridicule in his eyes:: Shane: Silus? ::Silus, Shane's mentally defunct and pyschotic older brother, is stood next to Shane wearing a banana costume looking like the goof ball off Blink 182's 'All the Small Things' video. He has a scrunched up look on his face like he's chewing on a wasp. Sean stands next to Shane speechless:: Shane: I thought you were in jail, and why the hell are you dressed up like a retarded banana? ::Silus scowls at Shane:: Silus: Firstly, I'm on day release from jail and secondly...some fool told me it was fancy dress. ::Sean raises his hand:: Sean: Yeah, that would be me! ::Sean begins to laugh hysterically as Silus just continues to look at him with spite:: Shane: I see they do days off from Jail, why don't you take a day off from being a complete muppet and maybe you wouldn't have so much trouble living a normal everyday living life. ::Silus turns his focus back onto Shane:: Silus: Oh you are so funny, do i look amused? Shane: No....yer look yellow and butt ugly too. ::Sean bursts out laughing again and Silus begins to get irrate.:: Silus: You two are nothing but morons! I have style, now excuse me I have a date with Judy Whittaker. ::Sean stops laughing as him and Shane look at one another.:: Sean: Judy Whittaker? Shane: Wasn't she th'actor with facial hair. She showed me the beauty of foreplay! ::Shane and Sean look very disturbed by what they just heard.:: Shane: Why do I get the feeling I've seen this in a movie? Silus: ...and one day at band camp she shoved a flute up my.... ::Shane interupts:: Shane: OK STOP!!!! NO MORE!!! I CAN'T LISTEN TO ANY MORE OF YOUR SICK FETISHES! ::Silus' frown fades and he looks at Shane as if he's something he just stepped in on the street.:: Silus: What's wrong with you? She's a MILF! ::Shane turns away, turning a greeny color...he walks over to the table to get some thing else to drink:: Silus: MILF! MOTHER I'D LIKE TO..... {SMAAAAAAAAACKKKKK} ::Shane turns around in the nick of time to see Sean jawwing Silus. Silus falls backwards and rolls about on the floor in his banana costume. Sean: Don't mention it bro. I couldn't listen to another word from that jack ass. Shane: You hungry? Sean: Starved. Shane: Let's go get somethin' to eat off that buffet over there. ::Shane and Sean walk off across the room as the scene fades::
