'Hey, Enrico Felsen..Oí que usted tiene algunos problemas con mí' 'No, usted funcionó en mí el otro día y dije si usted deseó siempre some..I'd sea listo traerlo.' There was a small altercation between me and this other guy..a latino man named Bend...He had a medium build and long slicked back black hair...Pretty much how you think of most mexican males..He bumped into me and Then wanted to talk a little trash..So i told himif he wanted to go...I was ready to go...The two seconed long conversation had been held while i was still doing my curles..When i stopped talking he stood uyp and droped his arm weights..Then stormed up to me.he stood in my face for a short seconed and then threw a left...It asent verey long untill the garuds ran and pulled me off of him..We were both promptly sent to the warden...I got the rest of my sentance in solitary confine ment and he was put on road crew..Myself and the warden of the Jail had knowen each other for a little while. He knew he couldent just let me off and he knew i diddent mind being alone.. 'So what was the fight about Mr. Blayze?' 'He bumped into me..Then today we were out on the beach and he wanted to talk alittle bit of trash and get up in my face and he swung..So i cleaned his clock.' He took that at face value..He had knowen me when i had first met Laura..Hell...He was even at the hospital when Angle was born..and he called and gave me his condolences when Samual died.. 'I know some one that is goingto be very happy to see you when you get out.' My daughter...It had been a little while now that i had been in the jail..i think two and a half months..I had only seen her once...My sister had brought her one day. The warden, Whos name is John Regan..Let us in his office for ten minunets..It was the first time i had hugged him in a month..You never really know what and who you love untill your freedom is taken from you..In very few days i would be out of this hole and back to my wrestling ways.. 'Yeah, I know Angle will be happy.' He had kind of a puzzled look on his face.. 'Angle...No not her..Laura.' 'WHAT!' No...I had no clue that Laura had come back..Just when i get used to her being gone. she shows up..Like an old westren movie..Every three months they have to go for the other nine months and JUST when you get used to being alone..They show up.. 'What the hell did she get back?' a question that he apprently diddent have an answer to. 'She contanced me the other day and told me to tell you she was back..Happy to see Angel..' My daughter..I told Rita not to let her take Angel anywere..i'm alaways a skeptic..I never know when people show up..My whole life is like a westren movie..I just never get the happy ending that they get..I get the ending that screws me...Like i stole a million in golkd got shot and as soon as i get out of the hospital....The Police are there to catch me. The end..and now my daughter..I had no clue if Rita just let her go to get gas or something..I just diddent know any more...I really diddent know..Was my life as bad as i pictured it and was it about to get worse... 'John..You call Rite right now and Let me talk to her..' he grabed the phojne and diled frantically..only to get nothing..He tried again and again while i sat in that office..Almost tothe point of tears...The only thing that was kepping me from going crazy was knowing that on the day i walked out of the gates and into my daughters awaiting arms..That was the only thing hat kept me going...That kept me from walking outside and plowing the garud on the outside in the face,.,MY daughter.,..being able to see her..That and Global Wrestlking...The other passion in my life..Ilived to have gold around my waist..But right now i wasent thinking of that..I wanted to speak with my daughter... 'Hello Rita..' He diddent have time to say anything else..I grabed the phone 'Rita, donde está Angle..Has ella ida con Zlaura era!' The voice that came from the other end of the phone told me that Angle was sitting next to her..i was entirely relived by that notion 'Let me talk to her.' I heard her rustling for a moment and then my daughter voice came on the phone. 'Hello daddy.' 'Hey Doll...Listen..have you seen mommie?' 'No, Why is she here?' 'Yes baby...Dont go anywere with her..You wait untill daddy is there..Do you understand?' 'Yes Papa..' I wanted to talk more..But John had to cut me off.. 'I'll be out in ten day...Ten...I'll see you then...I love you.' 'I love you to Papa.' and the phone was hung up..I walked out of the wardens office a little more happy then i had walked in..Now i was off to Solitari..Which diddent bother me..I had thoughts in my head..Angel...Global Wrestling and most of all...Laura...Laura Vendacraft Azrul...
