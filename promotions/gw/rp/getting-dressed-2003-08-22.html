Getting dressed

22 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/22/03 The sound of running water fills the background as the scene opens up to a plain white room, with jungle green curtains covering the only window in the room. The couch, and the sheets on the bed, are a matching jungle green, while the carpet, is an off white, attempting to match the rest of the room. The running water stops, and the room becomes eeriely silent. The bathroom doorknob turns, followed by the bathroom door opening. Dripping wet, covered only by a jungle green towel, around his waist, Ice walks out of the bathroom. He raises his right hand, and slowly pulls any strands of his dripping wet, blonde hair, out from in front of his face. Ice walks over to the bed, and takes a seat on its edge. He turns his head to the left, and then to the right, as if he is looking for something before shrugging his shoulders and turning his attention to the camera in front of him. 'Relaxed, rejuvenated, refreshed, and ready to go. Last night, I gave you an exclusive listen to the thoughts that flow through this mind. Last night, I gave you an exclusive glimpse into mind, of what many presume to be a meniacle, sick, sadistic psycho. But you see, to be psychotic, you have to be unaware of what it is that you are doing. I on the otherhand, know exactly what I am doing, when I am going to do it, and who it is going to happen to. So to say, that this mind, which you are beholding, this mind, which I stake full claim to, is psychotic, is absolutely positively incorrect.' Ice stands from his seated position on the end of the bed, and walks around to its left side. He bends over and pulls up a small black gym bag, and tosses it onto the bed. Ice reaches inside of it and pulls out a pair of grey boxer-briefs, and slides them on underneath the towel, which is covering him. Ice loses the towel, and reaches back into the small black gym back. Standing, now in a pair of grey boxer-briefs, Ice pulls out a pair of navy blue gym shorts, a white tank top shirt, and a pair of white socks. Ice tosses the socks onto the bed, and proceeds to put on the navy blue gym shorts, and then the white tank top shirt. Ice again pulls the hair out from in front of his face, and proceeds to comb his fingers throught it. Ice walks back over to the end of the bed, and once again takes a seat on its edge. He reaches back and grabs the pair of white socks. Starting with the left foot, Ice slowly begins to put the sock on. 'You see, everything I say, everything I do, it all has a purpose behind it. I don't give a reasoning, nor do I have to. The simple minds, which many of you have, don't want to understand why I do the things I do. The simple minds, don't want to look deeper and find the reasoning for my actions and for my words. The simple minds would rather just look past what I do, and onto other things which they can comprehend more easily. The simple minds would prefer to not think about the things I do, and the words I say, because they make the simple mind uncomfortable, and a little uneasy. The simple minds like Christina McCoy, and Jin Akiyara would like to pretend that my actions, my words, that I just don't exist so they can go on their merry little way and live a better, happier life. Well it is because of those simple minds, and those people I do the things I do, and say the things I say. Do you think I thought I would become the man I am? Do you think I dreamed that I would be like this? I wasn't like this before....I used to act like you did. I used to have the simple mind, that ignored the uncomfortable. I used to be able to block out and pretend that all those bad things I heard, all those nasty things I saw weren't true. I used to come out into an arena, look around, and see the thousands of fans sitting in the seats, wishing they would chant my name. So I did everything I could to please them. I used to go back into the back, and ask my boss, what they thought of my performance every night. From both, I received NOTHING! The fans cheered me for a while, until they grew tired of me. The boss gave me praise and praise, until he found someone new that fit the fans' fancy. I went home night after night, asking myself what I did to lose them, and their support. I gave them MY BLOOD, MY SWEAT, MY TEARS! I writhed in pain, worked throught broken bones, torn ligaments, and for what?! FOR THEM TO TURN THEIR BACKS ON ME! Do you not get it yet JIN! You are just another flash in the pan, for the fans to cheer for right here, right now, until they grow tired of you. They will suck you dry of all your blood, all your sweat, and all your tears, and not even look back when they move on to the next 'Johnny Come Lately' who takes your place. The same goes for that little stuck up bitch you continue to stand up for. As soon as those fans turn on you, she will too. And you will be left naked and barefoot, wondering what the hell happened to you.' Ice finishes putting on his socks and black sneakers. He stands from the end of the bed, and walks over to the door. Ice places his right hand on the doorknob, and pauses before turning back towards the camera once more. 'Jin, I know you don't want to hear the words I speak. I know you don't want to believe the truth that comes behind them. But sooner or later, you are going to have to wake up, and look around you and see that what I speak of is not a farse. But until that time, I bring to you the pain, and tribulations from which I have already suffered. I do so, not for your own good, not to entertain the fans, and definately not to prove something to Christina McCoy. The pain, the suffering, the anguish, and the torture I am going to put you through, make no mistake about it, I do, for MY OWN PLEASURE. You are in MY WORLD now Jin, and in my WORLD, I am the keeper of the key, and the guardian of fate. Through me you live, throught me you die. Anytime.......Anywhere.......Anyone.' Ice opens the door, and turns. He methodically walks out the door, closing it behind him. The camera zooms in on the white door as the scene cuts to commercial.
