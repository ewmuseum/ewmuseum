The Tea House

6 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Week Of 11-6-05 A Strange Fit Lights were off inside the small storefront. Only a few overhead lights, along with the lights for the three-way mirror, were on in the back of the store. Normally, Vincenzo would not work to such a late hour, but this was no ordinary customer. He was a special client, who not only spent copious amounts of his own money, the referrals the man had provided over the years were obscene. Friends listened to his advice, and Vincenzo had brought on four extra tailors to handle the work. The Hix family enjoyed getting their suits from him, at least whenever they were in Vegas. Ever since Jake had relocated, he had bought at least five suits per month, and he had referred countless others. No, as Vincenzo looked the man over from behind he was curious why this suit was so important. They had gone through three fittings, and normally he only had to go through one to make a few final adjustments. Jake’s mood was more focused than usual, and he eyed every detail carefully. Seeing himself in the three-way mirror, he checked over every aspect carefully. The meeting set for the evening was very specific, and in the Japanese tradition everything he did would be interpreted. Entire conversations were spoken before a word was exchanged. He checked each line of his clothing, ensuring he was clean with no fray or stitch not perfectly done by Vincenzo. “Jake, what is so important about this suit?” Vincenzo could no longer resist asking. Jake looked at him the mirror. His face was serious, thinking about the actions he must take tonight. “There is a saying my friend. Matter of great concern should be treated lightly. Matters of small concern should be treated seriously. The man I meet tonight will know this, and he will look for it. I must represent myself perfectly, in every aspect. We will lay groundwork in silence, through movement, manner and appearance.” Vincenzo understood now, as often he had experienced similar visions in dealing with his customers. People spoke with their bodies before their mouths, and his skill in reading them made him an even better tailor. Sometimes, their bodies knew what their minds could not understand. “I see. I believe we have finally perfected the suit my friend. However, to be sure, let me give the suit and shirt a final pressing.” Jake nodded, “Excellent. Then I shall take my leave.” Vincenzo took the clothing from Jake as he stripped. He disappeared into the back to give everything one final pressing. The black suit and blue dress shirt were paired with his John Lobb shoes, but even Jake admitted he looked a little funny standing in his black boxers, black socks and dress shoes. The tailor returned with Jake’s clothes. The suit of clothes quickly went on over his powerful physique, and he rechecked himself in the mirror. He pulled a small bottle of cologne out from his bag. Dabs went behind his ears and around his neck, just enough to be pleasant, yet not overwhelming. He was satisfied, and looked over at Vincenzo. “Thank you my friend.” Jake ran his hands down the suit front and flared his arms out. “As always, the suit is perfect.” “Of course sir. Is there anything else you need?” Jake smiled as he slipped his money clip and wallet into his interior suit pocket. “We can all use a little luck, but you can’t give that to me. Thank you though. Go home to the family Vincenzo.” The tailor nodded as Jake headed to the back door. He walked through into the cold air of the evening. The sun was hidden behind the planet, and so the temperature, which dropped significantly during summer months, dropped down so much that a jacket was needed to be comfortable. The suit was well made, and provided the warmth he needed. There was a new chill in the air, something not brought out by nature’s breath. The new feeling running through his body came from somewhere else, running through his flesh and up his spine to warn him. I am not alone? Jake looked around, but there was little light in the alley. The edges provided all sorts of dark hiding places for anyone to hide in. Jake felt something from one corner, and he looked in that direction. “Who’s there?” Elia stood in the dark shadows of the depressing dark alley in her tight black leather outfit, not revealing herself to the tiny light that illuminated from above... “Someone who has been waiting here for you.” The mind knew the voice, somewhere in the dark recesses of familiarity and dismissed nuance he knew the name matching the voice. He couldn’t bring the information forward though, and so he stepped forward to force the issue. 'Don't be shy big man, come on out and say hello.' The invite was pleasant to Elia as she reveals herself to the shock of Jake. 'Shy is not in my nature Mr. Hix. Though many other things are...' 'I take back the big man comment.' The feeling, a cold wind rushing through his body as though it were paper-thin. The tingling at the base of his spine became a crackling surge of energy lighting his inner self aflame with warning of what was here before him. Jake wondered why she was here. Could she be stalking him? Maybe he had a new admirer. For her? She is nothing more than a young woman, troubled perhaps, but? Jake forced the feelings away, looking at Elia as he came slightly closer, within a step of grabbing her if it was needed. He never knew what would be thrown at him from his enemies, and there were enemies. Jake remained coy and playful, 'As for the other things in your nature, perhaps the dark alley is not the best place for such a conversation Ms. Smith, or can I call you Elia?' 'Elia...?' Pausing for a moment as a gust of cold heavy wind blew through her hands and hair, making her close her eyes for a moment. 'For you Mr. Hix, you may call me Ms. Smith. Though deep down you may know me in a much different way.' Jake tried to pierce the mysterious veil surrounding the small woman in the shadows. He felt something, but he couldn't quite place it. She was something more than he had thought, but was it damned or divine? 'Well, I have to admit that whenever we meet I feel some sort of...connection to you Miss Smith. Of course, sometimes you blame bad Thai food, or something else seems wrong. You've...changed...since I first joined Global. To what do I owe this mysterious pleasure?' Moving up a little closer, she came fully out of the darkness that was filling up the space between her and Jake. Her eyes, watery blue with a little touch of red, were locking on to Jake like they were two arms. 'Why am I here? Why am I standing in front of you in this deep deserted palace of darkness that surrounds us, waiting for you?' Elia, or so it seems, took a deep breath, 'I'm here to ask you for an answer. An answer that only you will know...' Jake raised his eyebrows as a little smirk crossed his lips. He couldn’t resist such a playful banter with the young woman. He wanted to see where she was headed, 'You'll have to forgive me Miss Smith. You see, I haven't had dinner tonight, and so I don't have a nice little fortune cookie saying to shoot back your way. I could make something up, be quick and deep in my hazy yet striking observations. Of course, what would the point be? If you have a question, ask it and I shall answer if I am able. If you have some other need...' Jake looked her up and down, then his own striking blue eyes met her own, 'Well, seems a little early in our relationship for that, but at least calling you Miss. Smith would be a bit kinky.' Elia's eyes, going glossy a little, caught the eyes of Jake going back up from her small feet. Her heart, racing with the beat of the night, licking her lips toward Jake's foolish comments towards her. 'Your comments, as kiddish as they seem, are not wanted. Nor is your invitation for bodily touching either. You see Mr. Hix, your mind is what is needed and all it's needed for is the information it holds.' Stepping back into the darkness that had reclaimed her body, Elia's voice remains the focal point for this depressing and awkward encounter. 'And the information it holds about one person.' 'Touch?.' Jake shrugged as he nodded at her. She wasn’t a fan, a stalker or someone who was looking for his “special” attentions. Elia had some kind of agenda, but she wanted to be a little coy about it. 'Women have sought after me for many things, but never been so blunt as to look for me for 'my mind'. I think I’m flattered Miss Smith. What can I help you with, since there is obviously some mental marathon you feel the need to run through.' 'First and foremost Mr. Hix, just like the freezing...cold...night that has been so brutal to shadow us tonight, your mind has the same qualities. It has the something that I desire more than anything and for that very reason Mr. Hix, our relationship will be more different that the soulless, deceased, females that you infest in your tiny word.' Brushing her hair back, making her eye connection with Jake to remain the center of this little chat. 'You Jake, the information that I require you will give me.' Remaining inside, filled up to the top with the pleasant flying creatures of the night going around her head. 'For weeks now Mr. Hix, I've been hearing the same name over and over in my head. Trying to figure out what it is. listening to the name and all I keep hearing is the name Thomas...' Jake's eyes narrowed slightly, his face slackening as the reaction took physical control before he could stop it. The name couldn't mean anything to her because no one knew. She was throwing something out to him, trying to bait him. Jake had hidden the truth from everyone, even managing to finally forget about it himself. He had very few subjects he considered taboo, but that name was certainly on the list. No one could know about it. He didn’t want to deal with that memory, that moment in time ever again. 'Well, maybe the rumors about the voices in your head are true. I'm not sure what I could do to assist you.' Trapping herself off from the words that Mr. Hix was tossing out to her, blocking the shadows that were being cast down from the surrounding walls of the buildings around her. 'Mr. Hix...' Pausing for a brief moment. 'Your knowledge is far too great for you to deny the name. Even I, a lonely soul that is being compressed by things that are not of my will know that you understand me. Why do you deny me the information...' Jake's entire body relaxed, a natural reaction for him whenever danger was present. He would relax, ready for the fight he expected to come, but here the battle had nothing to do with the bulging muscles on his body. Jake's face was washed over in a dark threat, 'I don't know who you think you are little girl, but sometimes looking in dark places can bring on unexpected consequences. Whatever you're thinking, do your fractured sanity a favor and move on.' 'Unexpected consequences...' It was weird. As if it was being controlled by someone else. The black covering shadow that Elia's body was hidden in lit up like Christmas. The eye color, the watery blue, was suddenly changed to a cloudy white, outlined by a fiery red that was trying to take over. Elia stood there, fixing her eyes, not on the eyes of Jake's anymore but on a name that was printed on a wall just yards away. 'Mr. Hix...you have nothing to fear. I'm not here for action. I'm here for a deeper purpose. As for who I am Mr. Hix...' Walking up towards Jake just a few feet. 'Maybe you should be asking Thomas...' 'Fear wasn't my concern Miss Smith.' Jake stepped forward, he could feel something emanating from her, like waves of heat washing over him. 'I have no fear in this world, or the next. However, for someone who insists I refer to her as Miss Smith, you seem particularly interested in parts of my life that are of no concern to you. While you may need a therapist, I’m not one. We’re not in group therapy either, this isn't a group circle, and you seem to be the absolute last person to speak with about anything of...substance.' 'Group circle...' Elia was changing. Not so in physical features but in the small, reviling, words that she was eliminating from her mouth. 'Mr. Hix, my concern isn't for YOU.' Making her words strike the very heart of Jake. '...But of the world that you have lived in. I do not do this by choice Mr. Hix but I do it because it's of the greater importance. You see, I don't require answers for myself. I require answers for a greater purpose...and a purpose I'm sure you can understand.' Violence flashed through his mind, wanting to lash out against the woman and her words. Silence her, and at the same time silence the guttural cries of the demons tormenting his soul in his memories. His eyes narrowed dangerously, 'You know, we could banter back and forth all night long, and still get nowhere. What is it you want Smith?' 'In due time Mr. Hix...in due time. But I believe out time right now is up. But I will leave you with one lasting question. How far down does the Thomas name go...' Her lips, slowly peeling up on each side, forming a smile that wasn't in Ms. Smith's nature. Her eyes, lowered down, took her off guard of Jake before taking a few steps towards Mr. Hix, walking right by him and towards the nasty and breezy night. Jake grabbed her arm as she tried to pass. The world shifted. The entire landscape before his eyes was replaced by memories flashing through his mind. Demons and devils, slaughter and suffering, and through it all he finally recognized the feeling he was experiencing. Jake came back, shaking away the assault of memories and experience as he looked down at Elia, 'You aren't Elia Smith. You're...something else...something else entirely. No wonder you cling to the darkness.' Elia's eyes wondered to the face of Jake once more. This time...almost looking deeper inside his soul. Trying to flesh out the real story that he was hiding. 'Mr. Hix...sometimes the answers that you seek are laying in the past. In due time all your questions will be shown to you. In due time...' Pulling her arm away from the warm grasp hand that Jake was clinging to her. She began the long journey into the world of darkness that was in front of her. Jake Hix watched as the darkness enveloped the slight, almost frail body containing the power wrapped within withering flesh. 'So, it begins in darkness and an unspoken truth. Oh, I know you darkness, I know where you come from monster. The hour has come round at last, and I stand before you on the road to Bethlehem.' Walking off into the darkness himself, he tried to push the racing thoughts out of his head. He had a ceremony to prepare for with a very important man. The future of his battles with his brother depended on it. The Tea House Heated air came out at the floor, warming the interior of the sports utility vehicle against the frigid night air. None of them were speaking. Inside each of their minds, they considered what must be done to win the support of the man within the luxury home. He was a man they had all known for many years, and those years had been both good and bad. Unfortunately, they were not on the best of terms at this time. Takeo Kentaro was a very strong man, long ago having worked as a professional wrestler and mixed martial arts fighter. However, he long ago had given up such a rough and tumble life to pursue his own business interests. The choice had been most fortuitous because he had become very wealthy through several areas of business. Many years before the big boom in Las Vegas, he had bought property and developed many neighborhoods that served the sudden explosion in population with the return of “Sin City”. Enjoying the lifestyle he was afforded here, he had remained as one of Las Vegas? favored sons. The relationship between Takeo and the Hix family had been in several areas. Takeo had been the owner of the wrestling federation Sid Hix had been injured in. Xavier still resented the man for allowing “his” men to get so rough with a gaijin. Pitt and Jake had friendly relations with him, but then Pitt and Kentaro had butted heads in the business world. Luckily, they knew of no reason for Kentaro to be angry with Jake, and so Jake had requested the meeting for the evening. Jake looked at his brothers in the front of the vehicle, “Are you ready?” Both of his brothers nodded. They all stepped out of the car into the cold night. Each of them was dressed in a tailored suit; Jake and Xavier choosing black while Pitt had gone with a pinstriped gray suit. Walking up to the front gate of the estate, the two security guards, with easily spotted bulges under their arms where pistols were holstered, went on the defensive at these three large men walking up. “Gentlemen, may we help you?” The first security guard asked. He was slightly older, hair graying at the edges. Jake nodded at the man, “My name is Jake Hix. My brothers and I have a meeting with Takeo Kentaro this evening.” The men relaxed, “Of course sir. We’ll need to check you for weapons before we let you in.” Jake held his arms out wide, and the security guard quickly frisked him. The guard was satisfied, and he repeated the procedure on each of his brothers. The second guard entered an electronic code into the keypad, and the gate opened. “Please proceed to the right. Follow the rock path to the tea house, and you will be met.” The guard explained. No reply came from the brothers as they simply proceeded to the left side of the house. Solar lights were strung out along the path, the bright Nevada sun having powered them all day. There were strict structures of behavior in a meeting such as this one. They would be quiet, waiting for the occasion when words would be required. Waiting on the path as they came up over a rolling hill stood a man in a black suit and black tie. The man looked very formal, with his hair carefully combed and warm eyes greeting them. “Gentlemen, welcome to Takeo-san’s home. I am Reizo Michio, and will act as hanto to Takeo-san. Please follow me.” He turned sharply. Sitting among the estate in a city where water was precious and the county paid people to have no lawn they saw carefully laid gardens maintained by a staff of a dozen. The delicate plants were treated with care and respect, with four of the gardeners seeing the rock gardens as an expression of their own religious faith. Reizo-san opened the sliding door to the tea house, and the brothers followed him into machiai. The room was simple, with padded mats on the floor and a fire in the hearth on the Eastern wall. Each man removed his shoes, and set them on the small raised bench beside the door. They all donned the black slippers provided. Reizo-san kneeled down, and looked at the three mats across from him. Jake Hix took the front mat, kneeling down at the point of the triangle formed by the arrangement. His brothers took subordinate positions behind him. Reizo-san bowed deeply, his head almost touching the floor. He raised, and Jake bowed down a quarter of the way to the floor, then returned. Reizo-san turned, pulling a kettle out from the fire. He pulled the kettle and three small ceramic cups out in front of him. He poured the steaming hot water into each cup. Reizo-san placed each cup down before the guests, first Jake, then Pitt and finally Xavier. Each man took a sip of the hot water, testing the quality that would become their tea later. Pitt and Xavier remained silent, but Jake set the cup back down on the floor, giving a slight grunt to show his approval. Reizo-san bowed again, and removed the cups. He stood, waiting for his guests to join him. They were led back out of the tea house. They followed another rock path out past a grove of bamboo. There, Reizo-san led them to a garden devoid of all flowers. On the earthen ground, there was a fine mist of dew surrounding the hand-carved stone koshikake machiai (waiting bench). The roji was meant to be a place of respite, and it was perfectly suited for this role in the ceremony. Raizo-san stepped aside. Jake stepped forward first, moving his hands down the front of his suit, symbolically ridding himself of the dust of the world. He moved over to the stone bench and sat. Pitt and Xavier did the same, and then sat down at the bench behind him. Reizo-san nodded deeply once again, receiving the quarter bow from Jake in return as he disappeared. They waited, focusing on the sublime emptiness of their current existence. Soon their host, the teishu, would arrive. After a long wait, he came walking up from the South. Even aging, Takeo Kentaro was a powerful man. Early on, his hair had turned gray, but he kept his six foot two hundred pound body in top physical form. The lean muscles were still capable of inflicting great pain, as one man in the trio could attest to. Takeo Kentaro walked up to the trio of men, and Reizo-san was several paces behind. As Kentaro approached, the three men stood. Kentaro looked Jake Hix in the eyes, neither man giving any slack as they stared deeply into one another. The stare was a physical struggle in this environment, but neither sense weakness in the other. Takeo-san bowed to Jake, and the gesture was immediately returned. Kentaro turned, and then Reizo-san followed behind. Jake, then Pitt and finally Xavier all came walking behind them. No one spoke or made a sound. The traditional ceremony meant they were now moving away from the physical world into the spiritual realm of the tea ceremony. Takeo-san led the party through a bamboo gate. The chumon was used to specifically signify the transformation of the trip. They moved to a clearing where low stone held a large stone basin. In order, each man took a ladle of water and poured it over his hands, and then took some into their mouths. They wiped away the water, and spit the water out to carry the last taints of the physical world away. Finally, they were led to the tea house once again. They entered through the rear sliding door, and each man was forced to bow his head because the clearance was only thirty six inches. Each man was now equal in the tea house, there were no social statuses to distract from the occasion. The room was white, with no decoration until the walked around to a small alcove. Hanging on the wall was a scroll painting. The master who had painted the work of art showed two strong men engaged in battle, but beneath them the great maw of a dragon was ready to consume them both. They all could read the Japanese kanji to the right of the picture, and each man was suddenly struck by the importance of the meeting. He knows’somehow he knows? Jake ignored the troubling thought as he continued to examine the kettle and the hearth. With the chill of winter in the air, a charcoal fire had already been set for them. Satisfied, they took their respective positions around the low table. As always, they were arranged according to the importance they had chosen. Takeo-san sat last, and looked down the line at his guests. “Hix-san, it has been too long since we have sat next to the fire to speak. On behalf of myself and the entire Takeo family, I welcome you into my home once again.” Takeo-san bowed his head, holding it before looking up. Jake Hix returned the bow, holding his position longer out of respect to the host. “Takeo-san, you honor me and my family.” Takeo-san looked down at Pitt, “My good friend and dangerous adversary in the business world. Finally, we meet again without swords of ink and shields of attorneys.” They exchanged bows. “I much prefer meeting as this, and there are less paper cuts my friend.” They smiled at each other. Finally, Takeo-san locked his eyes onto Xavier Black’s steely gaze. Neither man would turn away, and normally such an affront could be ruin, but tradition demanded otherwise. “Black-san, it is good to see you again.” A nod of the head, barely perceptible was given. “Yes, good to see you again.” Xavier almost did not bow, but he finally lowered his head all the way down, paused, and the raised back up again. Reizo-san turned, and removed a silk covering from the first tray. He lifted the tray, and brought it over to his master at the table. The tray had freshly cut cedar chopstick on it along with the meal. Takeo-san set a large, white ceramic dish of rice down in the middle of the table. A covered lacquer bowl came next, and finally a tray with raw fish lay across its white surface in a design of Takeo-san artistic flair. Reizo-san handed a black bottle to Takeo-san, and he served each of the guests warm sake. Takeo-san then served each of his guests miso soup in individual ceramic bowls. Then, a portion of each fish was put on small ceramic plates and served to each guest by Takeo-san. The host took great care in ensuring nothing was spilled or misplace, not wanting to insult these men. He returned to his position, and they began to eat. Silence was not required, but so far no one seemed interested in speaking. They ate the exquisite meal silently. Finally, as each guest was finished, Takeo-san finally spoke. “Would anyone care for more soup?” Shakes of the head were his only reply. Jake looked at his host, “Takeo-san, I appreciate you hosting us here this evening. We asked for this meeting to set aside old differences from other worlds, and inquire of you as a man of great knowledge.” Kentaro smiled, “You flatter me, but have we set aside our differences” While you and I have never been at odds, certainly Pitt and I have. What say you Pitt?” Pitt looked at his host, then allowed s creeping smile to come on as he watched for a reaction from Kentaro. “As we both know, business is war, but this is not business. Here we are not at war, but rather two friends walking in the garden. Admiring the cherry blossoms and reflecting on our place.” Kentaro grunted in approval, Pitt bowing his head. “For all of this good will between the three of us, none of us has any doubt what Black-san feels in his heart.” Kentaro looked down at Xavier who was staring at the wall across from him. “Black-san has not set aside the differences between us.” Xavier did not respond. Jake waited, trying to give his brother the chance, but then he responded. “My brother still holds you responsible for many things, and the assault in Japan is fresh in his mind even after all these years.” “Assault is a harsh term.” “Yet accurate.” Jake countered politely. “We must move beyond these issues because there is a greater need men of responsibility such as ourselves must follow.” “As you say. Let us have hassun.” Takeo-san cleared away the bowls. He retrieved a wooden tray and handed it to Jake. Jake refilled each guests? sake, and then opened the two large ceramic bowls. The course was called uminomono and yamanomono, to signify the mountains and the seas. As always, both were bountiful as he served the beef and lobster to each guest. They ate quickly and quietly. Kentaro-san barely touched his food, “What is this higher purpose of which you speak?” “How can I say this and not sound ridiculous?” Jake wondered. He took the final bite of his lobster. “We have been chosen to fight against something beyond us. There is an’evil’we must meet head on. To lose against the evil is to forfeit the world, but to win is to save the world.” “Is there not a saying that to save a single human life is to save the world?” Kentaro offered. Jake took a deep breath, and let out an audible sigh of disappointment. “If there is no world remaining, there will be no lives to save.” “You must explain this to me so I can understand your need Hix-san.” “Perhaps you would allow me to explain this during konomono?” Jake offered. Kentaro nodded, “Of course.” The table was cleared. Reizo-san brought out the final lacquered tray. Small ceramic bowls were placed all around with a large soup bowl and a covered dish. Pitt took his turn, refilling drinks for everyone. The fragrant foods in the bowl were provided to each person, then Pitt provided browned rice in a large pitcher as a signal of the final course. Each guest took some of the salted rice and ate the meal. Jake only took a few bites, and then he continued. “We walked through the chumon, did we not?” Jake asked his host. Takeo-san replied in stiff, formal tones. “We have followed the traditions.” “I meant no disrespect Takeo-san.” Jake soothed his hosts nerves. “The chumon represents the passing into the spiritual realm. We are no longer simply men on the physical plane, we have come to a higher place in this ceremony.” “Tradition states this.” Jake nodded, “This is good. We have been fighting against a growing darkness. The beast, called master and dark messiah, has been gaining power. He has come to destroy the world. These are not times of technology, but rather times of spiritual battle. He cannot be defeated. We have stopped him once already, and his followers raised him back from the beyond. We are trying to find a way to destroy this beast, and stop him once and for all.” “What can a man such as myself do?” Takeo-san laughed. “As you said, this is a time of technology. I am a man of the present. I have nothing to do with this.” “You created the beast.” Xavier spoke angrily. “You are the reason he came about! All because you couldn’t control your employees in?” “SILENCE!” Jake yelled angrily. Xavier turned to Jake and Kentaro, but Jake’s sudden raised hand sent him back to stare at the wall. “I must apologize for my brother Takeo-san. I am embarrassed by his behavior. We have dishonored your house.” Jake hung his head in shame. Kentaro looked sad, “No, it is I who have offended you. You and your brother speak of Sid, and what has become of him.” Jake’s raised eyebrows, Pitt’s sudden pausing and Xavier’s look were all the reaction they would get. Kentaro watched each man carefully. The silence hung in the air, the tension building as thoughts from each man preoccupied them. How do I explain this to them? He does know? I have underestimated him once again. What is he doing? My master looks ready to cry? Kentaro hung his head, letting a long sight escape. He dropped his shoulders, looking dejected. Jake took in a loud breath, and held the air inside his lungs. He silently let it escape through his nostrils. In response, Kentaro turned to the right, away from his guests. Jake Hix raised his head high, clearing his throat and then making a small humming noise as if were pondering the situation. He let out a sigh, soft and inviting. Kentaro looked back at his guests. He pulled a small white cloth from his pocket, and wiped clean the chopsticks. “Black-san, I believe you are to serve omogashi.” The Hix brothers all pulled out handkerchiefs, and did the same as did Reizo-san. They set the utensils down, and with trembling hands Xavier uncovered the final platter. He served the dessert to each person. Kentaro did not even try to eat his, “Sid Hix came to see me recently.” A chill went through each of the brothers as they heard this news. “I am a man of science. I have built my entire life. From organizations, to homes, to business and even the industrial tools I spent years overseeing. I have watched as man ahs spread forth like some black, mechanical beats upon the earth. I made my fortune, and I had no concern for this inequity. I saw mountains made humble by man’s hubris, and I never paused.” “Then, the world seemed to stop moving all on it’s own.” Kentaro had a far away look in his eyes. He was feeling the sadness overtake him. “When I was a child, my father took me to a temple in Kyoto. He spoke of the gods, and I ignored him. I was more interested in the building itself, such great care and beauty in its construction. I marveled at the beauty I saw, and I wanted to create that same beauty. I never understood much of the spiritual side of life. Then the spiritual life found me.” “I was visited by something, not someone.” Kentaro had a tear falling from his eye. “I now understand why you harbor resentment toward me Black-san. Whatever happened to Sid, he was overtaken by something evil. The man was banished, and I could feel the hatred rolling off of him. The world stopped all around me as the thing asked for my help.” Takeo-san looked down at Xavier, “If I was responsible for that in any way, then I offer my deepest apologies. My words are too weak to make-up for what happened. Perhaps you could retire to the garden while I prepare for the tea?” Xavier opened his mouth to speak, but Jake understood. “Of course Takeo-san. We will return when called.” Xavier led his brothers out of the tea house. They returned to the garden. “What the Hell is going on?” Xavier shouted. Jake got right in his brothers face, “You will control yourself, or I will remove you from this estate myself.” Xavier took several deep breaths, “What was all that” Why was Sid here?” Pitt stepped between his brothers, “Sid is smart. He must know what we are looking for, and he is trying to prevent us from getting it. He knows we will rely on Kentaro because he knew him before the change.” “Luckily for us, Kentaro saw what he was. The tears worry me though, because we do not know if he told Sid because he had to, or if he realized the truth of what he helped set in motion. He is ashamed, that much we do know.” Jake walked over to the side of the garden. “If he told Sid we may have already lost.” Jake turned, leveling his older brother Pitt with a harsh gaze, “Never say that to me. I’ll never believe it. We’ve given up too much, lost too much to start doubting everything now.” Xavier moved to the stone bench. He sat down, putting his head in his hands, “What are we going to do if Sid has what we need.” “He can’t have it, but he might know about it. We’ll beat him to it, but I don’t think Kentaro gave him anything. Kentaro would have put him off, spoken of what he couldn’t have knowledge of. Who doubts a man of technology knows nothing of God?” Pitt realized. Sid was still out in the cold’hopefully. The bell rang five times. The Hix brothers returned to the area with low stones, and re-purified their hands and mouths before returning to the tea house. Where the scroll had once hung, there were now flowers in a large arrangement. Reizo-san was bowing deeply as they retook their spots around the table. The opposite sliding door opened, and Takeo-san entered with the tea tray. The items were exquisite, each individual item considered a work of art from an ancient time. He moved to the table, and set the tray down. Takeo-san arranged the items on the table. The tea bowl holding the tea whisk, tea cloth and tea scoop were set down next to the water jar. Takeo-san left once again, returning to the preperation room. He came back with the waste water bowl, bamboo water ladle and green bamboo rest for the kettle lid. He closed the door behind him and kneeled at the table. Kentaro purified the tea container and scoop with a wave of a fine silk linen, the fukusa. He carefully refolded the fukusa and set it down. He ladled hot water into the tea bowl, then rinsed the whisk in the bowl. He set the whisk down and emptied the bowl into the waste water bowl. He unfolded the fukusa again, and wiped down the bowl to ensure it was ready. Lifting the tea container and scoop, he placed three scopps of tea for each guest, fifteen in total, into the tea bowl. Hot water was aldled in next, and he whisked the micture together until it formed a fine paste. More hot water came next, thinning it out until it was thin enough to drink. Kentaro passed the tea bowl to Jake Hix. The man who was huge, looked small in this ceremony as everyone did. Jake raised the bowl, admiring the ancient artifact and the artisan who had crafted it centuries before. Jake took the bowl to his lips, and took a deep drink of the tea. He wiped the rim and then passed it on to Pitt who repeated the same as the bowl was passed around. Finally, Kentaro took the bowl and had his own tea. He placed the bowl down, and looked at his guests. “I have done much good in this world, but I have also done things I am not proud of. You have come to find a book, am I right?” Xavier nodded, “Yes we have.” “Sins of our fathers were committed during the Great War, and what was once held by the Chinese monks was stolen. I can help you, and I must help you. There is much you should know.” As Takeo-san began explaining, tears welled up in Xavier’s eyes. Takeo-san’Kentaro’was making up for what he had done so long ago.
