Training Session Part 2

10 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/10/03 Jushin braced himself as his big brother began to run at him full force. Ichiro raised his wooden sword and charged at Jushin with the blade pointed directly into his chest. He was mere strides away when Jushin quickly bolted to the right by side-stepping, and then followed through by hitting Ichiro in the spine with the butt-end of the sword as he zoomed by. Ichiro fell to the ground upon impact. Ichiro smiled to himself on the ground. What was he thinking, attacking blindly wasn't his style, it was something more like Jushin to do. But, this time Jushin had forced him to rush into battle blindly. It seemed as if his little brother was beginning to learn from his past mistakes, and reverse the roles. Ichiro quickly pulled himself off of the ground. He dusted himself off and quickly readjusted his shin pads, as he straightened himself to prepare for battle once again. He smiled and nodded at his brother, who was smirking looking back. 'Doesn't look like you're the only one who has a good defensive technique, huh big bro?' Jushin mocked his older brother. Ichiro could only try to contain his anger. He knew what his brother was doing, and although he had done it many times, he now found it much harder to resist. He tried to shrug it off, but after so many years his little brother had reversed the roles. 'Well then what the hell are we waiting for?' asked Ichiro. 'I'm waiting on you sweetheart. Afterall I wasn't the one who lost to the Steel Lion,' taunted Jushin. The anger grew too much, he knew he shouldn't but Ichiro raised the sword and leaped towards his little brother. Jushin simply ducked and raised his sword over his head to block the downwards swing of his brother's sword as he leaped over top of him. Jushin then sprang to his feet, and delivered a swift kick to the gut of his older brother, and then followed up with another butt-end to the back, which dropped Ichiro to the ground again. Jushin side-stepped away from his fallen brother. He looked down at his brother and smiled, he had a distinct advantage for the first time in a long time. Jushin stepped back and examined the scene. His brother was slow getting up and his mind began to race slightly. He tried to focus but memories of his childhood began to kindle. He remembered seeing the exact same scene only fifteen years earlier. Jushin looked down at his older brother lying in the dirt. He was breathing heavily after being hit by the older boys that were now standing around him and laughing at him. Jushin felt helpless as he watched the boys kick his brother several times, finally he could watch no more and stepped in. 'Get away from him! What is your problem anyways?!' Jushin screamed at the boys. The boys could only laugh at the pathetic display. They knew who the little kid was, it was Ichiro's little brother. Another one that got special attention. They began to move closer to Jushin, and Jushin began to worry as he noticed them beginning to surround him. Ichiro slowly stood, and noticed the boys no surrounding his younger brother. He wasn't going to allow them to hurt him too. They were mad because he and his brother were stronger and wiser than them. They were jealous of the attention that he and Jushin had been getting from the monks of the temple. Ichiro came and stood beside his brother. The other boys had now almost fully closed them into a circle and were now beginning to move in closer. Ichiro leaned in and whispered to his little brother. 'I know you're scared, but..' began Ichiro. 'I'm not scared!' interrupted Jushin. 'Okay well then. We need to get out of this, and there is no way out without a fight. So we're going to have to fight, but if we want to beat this many guys you have to do as I say. Okay?' Ichiro continued, as he watched the boys move in closer and closer. 'Yeah, okay big bro. What do you want me to do?' asked Jushin. 'Stand back to back with me, don't turn away no matter what. Make sure your back stays firmly pressed against mine, and fight one guy at a time. Punch for the nose, it'll finish them quicker,' answered Ichiro. 'Okay,' was all that Jushin could manage in reply as he tensed up and awaited the fight that was soon to ensue. Ichiro felt his younger brother's body tense up against his. Maybe, little bro wasn't as fearless as he thinks he is, thought Ichiro. But, it did not matter much, Ichiro saw that the boys had stopped moving and were now standing awaiting movement from the two brothers. They stood waiting for what seemed like an eternity. Their eyes looked over each other, as they all stood within striking distance, finally one of the other boys broke the silence. 'Well, I guess you guys aren't as tough as the monks all seem to think you are, huh?' the tallest of the other boys snickered at the brothers. With that it was on. Jushin lashed out and struck him square with a punch right into the nose. Immediately the boy's nose began to bleed, and tears welled up inside the boys eyes. Jushin followed the right hand with a quick left, that caught the boy's cheek and sent him to the ground. All of the other boys watched as their leader fell to the ground. They stood in awe at first. But, that soon changed to anger as they realized what they had done to their leader. 'How tough are we now!' yelled Jushin. Ichiro's mind raced as he saw the fury grow in the other boys eyes. Jushin had downed one of them, but there were still six more left standing. It was no matter now, he swung at the bopy standing directly in front of him. His right hand caught the boy right in the eye. The boy immediately clutched his eye, only to be met with another right directly to the nose, and then a left to the cheek. The boy was dizzy, but still standing so Ichiro delivered a kick to the boy's side, which finally sent him to the ground. Meanwhile three of the other boys rushed at Jushin. Jushin quickly grabbed his brother by the hand and pulled hard. Ichiro knew what this meant, and with the whip that his brother had created he swang around and jumped, delivering a jump kick right to the jaw of the left-most boy. The boy fell to the ground, unconcious upon impact of the kick. Ichiro landed and he and his brother both followed up the kick with a flurry of punches to the two boys standing in front of them. The boys were quickly defeated by the high amount of punched being swung at them in rapid succession. When the two boys fell to the ground, Ichiro and Jushin felt a sharp pain in their spines. The two boys behind them, were at first stunned and watched their friends get pummeled but they had now snapped out of it, and had both delivered hard front kicks to the spines of both Ichiro and Jushin. The two Nakazura brothers fell, but they were quickly back up to their feet when they realized the onslaught that would ensue if they stayed down. They quickly hopped up, and faced their attackers. One of the two remaining boys was tall and skinny, while the other was shorter and stalky. 'I'll take the fat one,' said Jushin with a smile. He immediately saw the displeasure in the short one's face and saw the anger grow in his eyes. Ichiro saw the anger growing and realized what his brother was doing. He decided that any help, would be worth a shot so he tried to play the same game. 'I guess that leaves me with the walking, talking toothpick,' laughed Ichiro. Instantly the tall boy's face went red and he grew very angry. Ichiro and Jushin both smiled. Jushin's plan had worked, and they awaited the attack of their enemies.... Jushin was interrupted from his day dream to the sounds of Ichiro's battle cry. He quickly reacted in just enough time to get out of the way of the hard swing of Ichiro's wooden sword. Jushin quickly reached over with a kick, and broke Ichiro's sword clear in half. Ichiro looked up at his little brother with a look of shock in his eyes. Jushin smiled at his brother's look of shock. He simply left his foot hanging over the broken sword. Jushin dropped his sword and delivered a kick to his brothers jaw. Ichiro took the shot to the jaw and fell backwards sprawled out on the cold pavement. Jushin backed away and as soon as his brother was standing he rushed towards him, throwing a flurry of punches and kicks. Ichiro moved quickly throwing up his hands and shins to block the fury of his brother's attack. He then ducked and connected a swift knee to Jushin's gut. Jushin bent over and clutched at his stomach. He then received a shin kick, and went down to his knees. Only to be met with a light kick, but still strong enough to knock him back onto his back. And so it went back and forth for close to an hour. The bells of a nearby church began to toll the hour of 11:00 p.m. The two stopped their sparring for a brief moment to listen to the bells. The bells were soon interrupted by the roar of thunder. A blue light, lit up the entire night sky. The lightning cracked and the thunder rolled once again. Then the silence of the night was interrupted by the pitter-patter of rain drops hitting the cement. The rain began to fall heavily and the two brothers began to ready themselves once again. 'Fifteen more minutes?' asked Ichiro. 'However long it takes me to knock your ass out,' replied Jushin arrogantly. The two brothers stood about fifteen feet apart. They circled each other slowly, their eyes locked in a stare. The lightning flashed and their stern and serious facial expressions could be seen in the blue light. The rain patted off of their bodies and faces, but their faces remained stern and their stare locked. They paced back and forth and Ichiro was reminded of a day in the past. Similar to this, but many, many years ago. The rain was pouring down hard. But the two brothers were still sparring on the roof of the temple. Ichiro was about 15 and Jushin was about 12. Their master Tanaka would not allow them off of the roof, until one brother could get a decisive advantage over each other. Although the rain bothered both of them, neither of them was willing to accept defeat in an attempt to get out of rain. They were much too proud for that. And so the two teenagers stood, preparing to engage in combat. The rain poured down hard, the lightning flashed and the two ran at each other. Their faces were stern, and their looks intense. Their minds raced as to what to do, but they could do nothing more then hope and fight. They ran towards each other, as their master monk Tanaka watched on. This time there was no one watching though. There was no one here to see. This was simply for training and for respect. Jushin had never triumphed over his brother, and he was now putting up the best fight of his life. And the brothers needed the training, it was a harsh realization last week that they were not as invincible as they first thought. GW and the USA were definately not Tokyo, they were no longer untouchable, and they now needed to fight to earn respect one again. It was time. Jushin awaited his brother's movements, watching and waiting. Lying in wait, like a lion stalking it's prey. But his brother merely relaxed and paced in a line with him. Ichiro decided it was time. Jushin had put up a great fight, and he only needed to see one more thing to be certain that Jushin was ready to learn the technique of the Lotus. The only way that he could be certain was to see how Jushin would fight against him, when he allowed the Lotus to take over his body. Ichiro didn't know what the results would be. He had no idea of knowing. His own master could not even defeat him when he had used the technique. When he allowed the Lotus to take over his body, he seemed undefeatable. And so he relaxed and shut his eyes. He felt the usual adrenaline rush, and electricity seemed to surge through his veins. It was happening, it was beginning to take over. To Be Continued...
