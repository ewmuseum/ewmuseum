Down at the dark Horse again

6 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/6/04 As always, there was a great deal of energy in the Dark Horse saloon. The sounds of “The Devil Went Down to Georgia” filled the air. People were dancing, laughing, drinking, eating, just having a good time. The mechanical bull was, as always, being put through its paces. The doors had swung open and in walked Brick. “HEY!!!” shouted the patrons. Brick then walked over to the bar and sat on a stool. At the bar sat Jesse Briggs, who was nursing a beer. “Hey, Jesse. Ely here?” 'He's on the bull.' Brick shrugged his shoulders. 'Of course.' Riding the mechanical bull at the Dark Horse was one of Ely Braxton's favorite pastimes. “Hey, Brick, what’ll ya have?” “Just a ginger ale.” 'Heard about your match with Black and White next Evolution.' 'Yeah. Christina wanted to see how well they did in the ring. If they win, they get a contract with Global Wrestling. If we win, they get to tear Mark Bishop apart for hustlin’ ‘em .' 'So, either way, they win. I don't really know whether I want them to win or lose.' 'Yeah, the idea of seein' Mark Bishop get his ass whupped almost makes me wanna throw the match. Almost.' “Here’s your ginger ale, Brick,” said the bartender. “Thanks.” Just then, Ely walked up to the bar. 'Hey, Brick.' 'Hey, man.' Ely sat right between Brick and Jesse. “Texas mudslide, Ely?” asked the bartender. “Of course.” “Me and Jesse were discussing your match on the next Evolution.' 'Yeah, I know. I just don't feel right, beatin' on women.' 'You obviously didn't hear what they said after Christina gave 'em the match.' 'What did they say?' 'Somethin' about makin' us Southern Unisexuals, or somethin' like that.' 'What the hell is a 'unisexual'?' 'I don't know.' 'My philosophy is that I don't believe in hitting ladies,' said Brick. 'Once you hit me, you ain't a lady no more.' 'What about that ex-boyfriend o' your sister's? The one you had to get her away from.' 'You mean Cedric? That's different. Cedric was a punk who was too scared to hit another man. And for the record, couple of nights after I got her out of that apartment, Monica whupped his ass.' 'Get out!' 'I'm serious, man. She took off her shoes. She took her earrings and she whupped his ass.' 'Wow.' 'So, fellas, don't underestimate women. When the hell are they gonna play something different?' 'Brick, this is a bar owned by a redneck. You oughtta know there's gonna be country music playin'.' 'I know that, but at least play some kind o' music I can get with. I can handle some Kid Rock every now and then, but this 'Devil went down to Georgia' mess is making me sick.' Just then, the song changed to “Desperado” by the Eagles. 'So what about your match, Brick? Three-way dance with Rich Sherwin an' J.C. Diamond?' 'Yeah, I'm getting ready for it. I feel good after winning that tag match. Plus, both of them, Sherwin an' Diamond, have been on kind of a tear, so Christina wanted to see how they did against me. Plus, I like putting the new blood through their paces.' 'Heard you might have your eye on Diamond. Might wanna take him under your wing.' 'I might,' Brick replied, slyly. It wasn’t a really well-kept secret that Brick had thoughts about bringing back Ruckus Inc. He had eyed many up and coming GW talent to recruit: J.J. Jarone, Gabery Breed, Roz Blayze. He had a history of taking newcomers under his wing. One such newcomer was Top Dawg, his tag team partner in the Dogg Pound. 'Careful, Brick. Remember what happened with the last guy you had your eye on.' 'Please don't remind me. I don't know what the hell I was thinkin'.' 'You were probably thinkin' you could probably do a better job with Deacon than you did with Top Dawg.' 'Hey, you hear next Evolution's gonna be another Deaconpalooza?' 'Deaconpalooza,' Brick growled. 'Ooh, that's the sound of a pissed off man.' 'No, I just remember what happened the last Deaconpalooza. Little bastard kicks me in the nuts, then in the middle of my street fight with Jesse, Terminator an’ Pete Hardy come in an’ ruin everything. Damn near hanged me. Well, this one is gonna be different.” “How so?” “I know, sooner or later, Deacon’s gonna say or do something that’s gonna make him overstep his boundaries, and I’ll be there to put him in his place.”
