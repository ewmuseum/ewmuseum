How The Max-Attack was born.

28 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 28 (OOC: This is the beginning of a new RP series i'm doing. Hope you ENJOY!) The scene opens up to the hotel room where Roswell was staying for the last couple of days. You can see a chair at the desk with Roswell sitting on it. You can see that he is writing on some papers with a green pen. Then from the chair Roswell gets up and walks out of the door of his hotel room. Outside the hallway Roswell is seen opening the door of the “EXIT” sign on it. He then walks down some stairs and opens up a different door that goes to the lobby. Roswell slowly walks out of the hotel and to the outside on the sidewalks. Roswell is seen walking into a old parking lot that has crushed cars and old trucks in it. He walks over to the end of the parking lot and sees a sign on a metal fence, “152 Old Street.” “This must be it” “That’s what is says on this piece of paper” Roswell walks into the open fence and sees an old man sitting on a chair. Roswell walks over to the man. He has an amputated arm and an old green vest on. He wears brown ragged up jeans and a fishing cap on. “Are you Charles Munn?” “Yes, how can I help you?” “Well, I got a letter and it said to come here for something” “That was my assistant who sent it to you” “So what do you want?” “Well, I’m not the one you want. You have to go to that shed back there” “What’s inside?” “Just go!” Roswell walks away from the guy and walks back over to an old brown shed by the road. Roswell knocks on the door and there isn’t an answer. He knocks again but there isn’t an answer again. So Roswell walks to the back of the shed and looks in the window and sees a guy sitting at a table writing on something. Roswell knocks on the window but the man doesn’t answer. Roswell starts getting mad and throws a rock at the window breaking it. The man inside gets angry to and throws the rock back out hitting Roswell. “Ow! What was that for!” “For bothering me!” “Your worker outside said you sent me a letter and I had to come to you” “Your Max Roswell?” “Yeah, what do you want with me?” “I wanted you to test something” “Why would you pick me” “I saw your ad in the paper about you saving that baby” “Yeah so I saved her. Still why would you pick me?” “I wanted to see if it would do the right thing it said it would” “So what’s it supposed to do?” “Just drink it!” Roswell takes the cup off of the desk and takes a sip of it. Roswell’s mouth starts souring up, and Roswell falls to the ground with a big thud. The man looks down at Roswell and smiles. The man gets out of the shed and walks out of the parking lot. Suddenly from the ground Roswell rises but he has a mask on. It is a red mask with a spike design going throw it. Roswell opens his mouth and a groan comes out. Roswell gets up fully from the ground and also has a blue suit on, with lots of spike designs going throw it. “WHAT HAPPENED TO ME!” Roswell yells as he looks back at the man and his worker. Roswell picks up the piece of paper he had and tears it into 20 pieces. Roswell starts walking out from behind the shed and jumps. Suddenly Roswell starts flying around the parking lot. The man looks back at Roswell and turns back with a horrifying look on hid face. “It was supposed to kill him! It only made him stronger!” “IT WAN’T SUPPOSED TO WORK! NO!” Roswell jumps back down to the ground and has a confused look on his face. “What happened to me. I can FLY!” “I think the potion made me kind of a super hero!” “From now on. By day I am Max Roswell. And by Night and kind of day I will be The Max-Attack! SCENE ENDS
