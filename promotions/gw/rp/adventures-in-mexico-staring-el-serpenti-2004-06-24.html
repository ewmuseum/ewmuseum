Adventures in Mexico [Staring El Serpenti]

24 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-24-04 Ely stands next to his truck out side a Mexican cantina in Tijuana Mexico called the Chicago Club of all places. He looks down at the small dwarf Logan Creed unsure. “Are you sure this is the right place Dwarf?” Ely bellows down Logan snaps out of his trance as he spies the scantily clad ladies walking in and out of the place. “Yeah this is where he should be. At least by the reports from Talon. Anyways lets go in. I am dying for a drink. “Your always dieing for a drink. Very well lets get this over with.” Ely said frustrated. La Serpiente De la Muerte? Ely thought. Quite a ominous name for the person they were about to meet. The two walked into the club, Ely getting eyes up and down by the horde of Mexican ladies. “Hello Cowboy.” Said a lady dressed in red “Wanna play?” “Ummm” No.. Definitely no, sorry I am taken..” Ely replied. “Ahh too bad, maybe your little friend here.” The lady says. “LITTLE! Miss I may be small in body, but I am lar..” Logan starts but is cut off by a hard slap on the shoulder. “No! We are here for business, that’s all today Logan.” Ely says pulling the Dwarf away from the scrumptious woman in red. “BAH! Always ruining any chance of fun. I tell you I don’t know what Gunner sees in you sometimes you big oaf!” Logan says knocking Ely’s hand from his shoulder. “Gunner.. She would kill me for having fun here.” Ely says. “Yeah she sure would.” Logan says. “And I do not wish to die just yet. Now come on lets meet this La Serpiente.” Ely says. “Your right we got to meet this guy. Bubba wanted you to find him. Why I have no clue.” Logan says The two walk into the darken club the sounds of Smooth by Santana and Rob Tomas fills the air as they enter. Smoke hangs in the air. Here and there ladies dance on small stages for the viewing pleasure of the men, and even some of the women. The Music pumps sop laud it vibrates. Ely and Logan make there way to the bar, Logan getting a much better view of the ladies then Ely. Finally Ely pushes his way to the bar and catches the attention of the bartender. The Bartender turns dressed in jeans and a black T Shirt and a Black Eye patch over his eye. “Hey Cowboy what you want” ? The Bartender asks. “Give me two glasses and a bottle of Jack!, And some information.” Ely replies laying down a hundred. “Ahh Sir. You speak my language. What kind of information you wish?” Says the bartender in the eye patch. “I am looking for a man by the name of La Serpiente De la Muerte.” Ely says sliding over the hundred. The man looks around nervous. And slowly pushes the hundred back. “Not enough there my American friend. You ask me to place my life on the line for you.” The Bartender says. Place his life on the line. Exactly what was Bubba getting him into? Sending him all the way to Mexico to meet a Mystery man who has fear at the sound of his name. This trip was going to get costly. Ely nodded his head and slapped down another hundred. The bartender narrowed his one eye, with a grunt Ely slowly placed another. The Bartender looked around and took the money. “Two glass and Jack Information coming up.” The One eyed Bartender said. He placed the bottle and glasses on the counter top and leaned in close. La Serpiente De la Muerte, He be in the private rooms. He comes here once month for the Lucha matches and then stays here for a few days after enjoying his winnings in the back before disappearing again.” The Bartender says. “You need to get a girl or girls to take you back there though” The Bartender says smiling flashing a few gold teeth. “Thanks Pal.” Ely says and grabs the glasses and the bottle “Come on Logan!” The two work there way to the corner by a back door that says Private. Two large men stand on each side, their arms crossed. Logan and Ely take seat and Ely pours the whiskey in the glasses and sits back. “Look Logan I do not know what to expect. But I need to reach this man. I don’t need you drunk I need you sober.” Ely says looking right into Logan’s Eyes. “Look Ely I know. Don’t worry I have your back.” Says Logan “Good. I am counting on you. Don’t let me down Logan.” Ely says and gets up.. Logan puff on his cigar watching Ely leave. “Don’t worry Ely. I got this. I do.” Logan says as he slowly sips on his glass ==================================+++++++++++++++++++++++ Ely walks back out the door of the club and heads towards the Lady in red he first saw. “Oi! Cowboy” change you mind? I knew you would? She says. “Well my little friend has found his self into a bottle of booze. So I think I might as well take a Drink while here in Tijuana, if you get my drift.” Ely says. Ely flashes the lady a smile not common to his nature, but one that can make a woman melt. The woman walks up and places her arms around the large cowboy Ely Braxton. She laughs and giggles as she plant a kiss on his cheek. “Ohh I think I do cowboy!” She says taking his arm in hers and prancing into the club. The two head back into the smoke filled club and head to the bar. Again the patched eyed bartender comes up. ? Ohh Rita! I thought you got your self a big Bull this time.” He says a smile coming across his face. “Oh Hector I think I can handle this one.” Rita laughs as she rubs Ely’s chest. “So” Hector. Drinks for me and the lady.” Ely says laying down a fifty-dollor bill. “Si. You’re quite the big spender cowboy. What would you like?” Hector the Bartender says. “What ever the lady wises for her. And for me.. I’ll take a Pepsi.” Ely says. “Don’t want drinks to dull any moments with the lovely Rita here. “Ely says Rita laughs again and smiles up at Ely. ? Give my usual Hector.” Rita says Soon a Pepsi and a large glass filled with a blue liquid are placed on the counter. The two grab there drinks and head off. “So... Rita. Think you can find us a private place to get to know each other better?” Ely asks again flashing her his uncommon smile. ? Si! I thought you never would ask! Follow me cowboy!” Rita says as she leads him to the door with the two large men, walking right past Logan. Logan smiles and laughs “Boy Gunner is going to kick him.” Logan thinks to his self as the two walk by. “Good tastes though” Logan catches Ely’s look and nods “Hey you two! Have fun!” Logan shouts. “Paco, Juan. Let us by. ? Says Rita. The two large men nod and side step the door letting the two in. Ely and Rita walk down a door filled hall sounds of pleasure fill the hall coming from the room behind the doors. “So Rita. Have you met a Champion Mexican Wrestler by the name of El Serpenti before?” Ely asks I here he comes here a lot and I am a big fan.” Ely explains. Once again flashing his smile to Rita. “Si! I have met him! I could introduce you to him if you would like my cowboy!” Rita says happily. “You could” That would be great. I have wanted to meet this man for a while. ? Ely says giving Rita a big hug. “Si. I believe yes right over here is his room He pays Hector for his own room back here” Rita explains. There is a loud crash and a scream as Rita reaches for the door. Ely picks Rita up and moves her aside and goes to kick the door open. ==================================+++++++++++++++++++++++ Three lovely ladies surround a man lying on a table on his stomach in a towel. One with blond hair, one with red hair, and one with black hair. They laugh and giggle as they run their hands all over his body. The red haired girl climbs onto his back and starts rubbing his neck as she leans down to talk to him. “Come on Mister. Lets play!” Says the red haired girl. “Si! Take of you mask!” Says the blond girl. “Ohh No, no, no! Luca Tradition. I cannot take the mask off. Slowly the three ladies pull out a few knifes and raise them in the air. One of them climbing The man looks into a pool of water that has collected on the floor” Seeing the reflection of his Green and blue masked face. Suddenly his eyes go wide as he spies the ladies above and behind him. Quickly he rolls out of the way sending the woman on his back flying in the air. She screams as she crashes into the wall and slams into the ground. The masked man spins and ducks barely dodging a slash of a knife. He takes a fighting stance. Standing there in a towel and a mask. “What the hell is this” Hector has some answering to do!” The Masked man says “Hector did not send us! We come for the map! Black Panther has sent us!” Says the black haired girl. “Oh he has, has he. Well you’re not getting the map. And you can tell Panther he will never get the Treasure of the Silent Dragon.” The mask man says comely. “Then In that case” You’ll have to die and we will take the map! “Says the black haired girl. “Well. If that is what you wish lady. Then by all means try. Behind the masked man the red haired lady climbs up and leaps at the masked man nailing him with a spin kick to the back of the masked man sending him forward and over the table he was on moments before. The Blond rushes the man but he sends her up into the air with a backdrop. The woman screams as she fly’s over and crashes though the table. “So I guess the foursome is really out of the question now.” The man says. ? Well threesomes are okay to.” The Red haired woman leaps over her fallen partner. The masked man ducks a flying knife slash and slams an elbow into the stomach. He quickly hooks her head as the back haired girl charges him. The masked man leaps in the air nailing the Black haired girl with a dropkick and flipping off. The black haired girl crashes into the door as the masked man flips in the air and takes the red haired girl down with a bulldog. The masked man gets up and looks at the black haired girl and smiles. “Two down one to go.” He says as he approaches the girl Quickly the gill pulls out a gun and points it at the masked man. “And that one is you. Goodbye El Serpenti!” Says the woman Suddenly the door explodes from behind the woman and she is sent crashing into the ground. Though the door steps the large frame of Ely Braxton. The Masked man tilts his head to the side measureing up the new face in the room. “Back up plan from the Panther are you. WELL COME ON!” shouts El Serpenti motioning for Ely to bring it. El Serpenti crouches down and gets ready to fight “What?” Ely asks. “I don’t know what the hell your talking about?” Ely says looking at the small masked man. “So you don’t work for the Black Panther?” El Serpenti says. “NO. I work for Bubba McCoy.” Says Ely. “Ahh. Your the man then. So Lets get out of here. Hector will clean this up.” El Serpenti says. El Serpenti starts to dress as Rita walks in. “Ohh!! El Serpenti.. What happened Are you okay?” Rita says startled as she walks over to El Serpenti. “Yes I am okay.. And don’t worry about it. Look, tell Hector to bill me for the damages. Tell him I will be back but he better screen the girls better.” El Serpenti says as he puts on his khaki pants, black boots, and white shirt. “Okay. We will miss you.” Rita says. “I know Rita. I will miss Tijuana as well, Ill be back. Till then I bid you farewell” El Serpenti says as he kisses the hand of Rita. “Come on Cowboy. Lets get out of here before more come to get us.” El Serpenti says as he heads out the door. “WHAT THE HELL IS GOING ON HERE!” Ely Bellows. El Serpenti stops and spins and looks at Braxton. He tilts his head a bit and laughs. “Did Mr. McCoy not tell you?” El Serpenti asks. ? Come I’ll explain One the way away from here.” “Wait a moment” LOGAN!!!!” Ely bellows again and storms out the door. Ely storms down the hall with El Serpenti close at hand. Ely bursts though the door causing Logan to jump up right from the arms of a few ladies. “YOU!” Ely scream. “Ohh Shit!” Logan says and starts to run away. Logan don’t get far as Ely grabs him and lifts him off the ground. “Explain this Logan!” Ely demands shaking the dwarf. Ely stops as El Serpenti places his hand on Ely’s shoulder. “Come my big friend we must go. If you want to kill the Dwarf you can on the way out of here.” EL Serpenti says Ely nods and the three leave. Quickly they make it out of the club and here the sirens of the Mexican police. “Come we go! I for one do not favor Mexican Prisons.” El Serpenti exclaims. “Agreed” Says Ely tossing Logan into the truck and hoping in. El Serpenti Hops in on the passenger side and Ely guns the truck speeding away. “To the airport ummm.. Your name?” El Serpenti asks. “Braxton, Ely Braxton. Bubba not give you my name?” Ely asks. El Serpenti shakes his head no. “No My Friend. He said he knew a man with the skills to get the job done. HEY this is not the way to the docs!” El Serpenti exclaims. “We have another passenger to pick up. I am not leaving her behind and going off to god knows where.” Ely says grimly. “Her?” El Serpenti asks. “Yes” My girlfriend Gunner Addison, You did not think I would bring her to that brothel did you?” Ely says rather then asks. “Ahh Good point.” El Serpenti says. The truck fades off in the distance. -------- What a strange alliance here. What has Bubba sent Ely and Gunner into? Who is this El Serpenti and what will happen next time? Wait and see. -----------
