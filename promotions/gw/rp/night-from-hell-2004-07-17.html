Night from hell

17 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/17/04 'Crazy cowboy!' Those were the words running through Brick’s mind as he was losing his Round 1 match in the Master of the Mat tournament. Brick was in bracket B of the tournament. Like every other Global Wrestling superstar, he was teamed at random with another superstar to take on another team of randomly picked superstars. The catch: his partner was Deacon Holmes. Aside from Salaok, Derek Draven and Mark Makonie, no other superstar in the history of the promotion inspired more hatred and anger in Brick than the poster child for 'Don't Try This At Home'. Now, the two rivals had to work together. So, Brick had planned to ensure, much to his own chagrin, that nothing happened to Deacon before their match. Of course, given Deacon's rather obnoxious and conceited personality, this was no easy task. The line of people who wanted to beat Deacon Holmes into a bloody pulp started with Brick and ran around the block...twice. Not to mention, Deacon's recent disrespectful comments to Gunner Addison had her boyfriend Ely Braxton on the warpath. Brick did his best to calm Ely down. Meanwhile, he had to keep his own personal feelings in check. So far, things were going according to plan. But, of course, as the old saying goes, 'The best laid plans of mice and men often go awry.' As Brick was in the ring ready for the match, the Globaltron came to life with an image of Deacon on his way to the arena when out of nowhere, Ely attacked him with a steel chair. Threatening to kill him if he ever again talked to Gunner like he had been, he left the young superstar unconscious and in a bloody mess. As a result of Ely's rash actions, Brick was without a partner and, in the end, lost to Jesse Jamester and Sage Sabbot, thus taking him out of the tournament proper. Needless to say, this turn of events did not please Brick. When he got backstage, however, his expression was slightly changed by the sight of GW security and paramedics rushing down the hall. He managed to stop one of the security guards over. 'Hey, man, what's going on?' 'It's Ely Braxton! He got jumped by the Consiliu!' 'What? What happened?' 'After he jumped Deacon Holmes...' Brick sighed heavily upon hearing that. '...Reno and Zero came outta nowhere and started beatin' the hell out of him. El Serpenti tried to help, but they took him down, too. And Christina McCoy forced Gunner to watch the whole damn thing. They should be putting him in an ambulance right now.' Minutes later... Brick rushed into the emergency room and up to the desk. 'Someone was just admitted into the ER from the Global Arena. His name is Ely Braxton.' 'I believe he's with the doctors Brick then saw Gunner Addison sitting in the waiting area. She had the look of someone who had gone through a night of hell. Her eyes were red from crying. Her hair was tousled. She kept rocking back and forth in her chair. 'Gunner?' 'Brick?' 'Hey.' Brick and Gunner hugged each other. The two had been friends for quite some time. Very close friends, in fact, which caused some speculation as to the true nature of their relationship. 'How is he?' 'I don't know. They haven't told me anything.' 'How are you doing?' 'She forced me to watch it. That little bitch.' Just then, Ely entered the waiting area after getting patched up by the doctors. 'Ely!' Gunner exclaimed, running up to Ely and wrapping her arms around him. 'Hey, darlin'!' 'How you feelin', man?” “My head rings a little and I’m a little sore, but other than that, I'm fine.” “Good. Now I can ask this question. Did I or did I not make myself clear that nothing was supposed to happen to Deacon before or during our match in the tournament?' 'Brick...' 'Yes or no, Ely?' “Look, Brick, I’m sorry if I cost you your match tonight...” “If” Jamester and Sabbot demolished me!” “Brick, take it easy,' said Gunner. “Like I said, I’m sorry I cost you that match, but I am not sorry for beatin” the shit outta that little weasel Holmes!” “I don’t see why not! That's what cost me the match! Look, I can understand you wanting to stick up for your lady. If it was my lady, I'd feel the same way. But did you have to do it before my match” You couldn't have waited until after the match was over? Shit, I'd have handed him to you on a silver platter, then!' 'Yeah, well, I couldn't wait that long. If you'd heard what he and Christina said and did to Gunner, you wouldn't be so damn angry at me.' 'Wait, Christina was in on it, too?' 'Yeah, this week, she was,' added Gunner. 'Called me the whore of GW.' 'I know she's not talking.' 'Started choking me, too.' 'What?!' 'Now, do you see why I lost it?' 'Well, I guess I can't blame you that much. Besides, it is Deacon, after all. I might have beat his ass myself and cost us the match. In fact, when he burst into my dressing room and started talkin' shit, it took everything in me not to strangle the boy.' 'He burst into your dressing room?' 'Almost beat his ass right then and there. Came in, talkin' about he didn't want me messin' up the match for us. I told him he needed to watch his mouth considerin' I kept Ely from beatin' his ass last week.' 'What did he say to that?' 'He said he didn't care if you started something with him and that you couldn't even beat Juri Hyobanshi.' 'Little bastard.' 'Don't worry. I reminded him that neither could he, with his bodyguards. I then let him know that once the match was over, all bets were off. When he left, I kept reciting the sixth commandment over and over.' 'Which one is that, again?' 'Thou shalt not kill.' 'Yeah, well, if Deacon don't watch his mouth, I might just break that particular commandment, myself. I do have your permission now, don't I?' 'Oh, by all means.' 'Anyway, Brick,' said Gunner, 'look on the bright side. There's still the Lottery match.' 'Yeah, but right now, I suggest we plan our next move.' 'Next move in what?' 'In dealing with TUE, because I, for one, am tired of their shit.' 'You ain't the only one,' a voice said. Brick, Ely and Gunner turned to see the owner of Global Wrestling, Bubba McCoy. Bubba walked up to Ely. 'How ya doin', Ely?' 'You know me, Bubba. Head like granite.' 'Tell me somethin' I don't already know. Hangin' Brick out to dry like that. That was just plain crazy.' 'Yeah it was, but we've squashed it, Bubba.' 'Good, 'cause we all gotta be on the same page. I'll be damned if that bastard Reno and that ungrateful little bitch I raised run my company roughshod!' 'We won't, either, Bubba.' 'An' I think we all owe 'em some payback.' 'Well, then, as soon as Serpenti checks out, let's get started.'
