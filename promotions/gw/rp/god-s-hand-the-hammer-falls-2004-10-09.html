God's Hand...The Hammer Falls...

9 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-9-2004 Looking up at a wooden cross, Pendragon kneels before a simple altar. He rests his hands upon the hilt of the dark blade Lucien, supporting himself as he gazes at the cross. A simple altar indeed, made all of wood as is everything else in the sanctuary. A confusing trifle, as this goes against the picture that many churches have begun to present; this small cathedral boasts no gold, no gilt. The only gold in the room is that of Pendragon’s mail shirt. His eyes are fierce as he looks up, and he doesn’t notice a brown robed clergyman entering the sanctuary. He stands just inside the door, eyeing the room’s only occupant. Two lines of wooden pews make up the seating area, but they sit empty’Evidently it isn’t Sunday here. Pendragon appears to be whispering to himself’Praying would be the word for it, but what need has he of prayer? Bob revealed himself to him and set the man to doing his work. Why pray? Finally, Pendragon has his answer, or not, by the look in his face, but he rises and bows his head? “By mine blood?” He whispers in a fierce’Well, whisper I guess’A fierce whisper. He pushes the left sleeve of his mail shirt up, revealing a bit of forearm. With a deft motion, he slices a thin cut along it’s length and presses it to the blade, allowing it the taste of blood. He scabbards the bloodied blade and turns to go when he notices the now white faced clergyman. With a sigh, he starts towards the man, his hand palm out? “I meant to scare thee not, mine friend. Do not fear what it was thee bore witness to. Merely an archaic way of sealing a deal, so to say. Only for show, I assure thee?” Yet his eyes held something more. His eyes said that this was all very sane and necessary. His eyes spoke the volumes that his lips did not. His god demanded this. His god would be appeased at whatever the cost. God’Bob’God’He was not the loving Christian god that Pendragon had grown up with’Oh no. Bob was a god who wished to hold his sway once more upon his flock. He had unleashed Pendragon against the darkness of the world. The perfect tool, the perfect weapon’He was to be god’s Hand on earth’His Hammer. Pendragon knew he was being used, yet he cared not. Let Bob have his power back. After all, it was his. Conquest is what Pendragon cared about. The conquest of the White. Enlightenment would spread once more because of his glorious actions, his merciful deeds. The clergyman, however, believes the words. He looks very relieved in fact, and smiles warmly at Pendragon, if a little timidly?“I knew it as such. This is a very old Church, built long before they became the monstrosities of today. Those times, however, have moved on. Only we few monks have kept it unchanged for these long centuries. You seem a troubled fellow, brother, and I would like you to know that there is no trouble that the lord cannot help you with. Trust in him, and he shall trust in you. And in the meanwhile, if you feel the need to talk, I’m a good listener. Brevard is my name, and I am pleased to meet you?” The man looks abashed at all that he said. Pendragon raises an eyebrow and the man sputters?“Mind you, I don’t mean to take confession’only to talk with you should you wish it”.Of the lord God, of troubles?” Pendragon shakes his head?. “It would be better for thee to forget that thee saw me here. It may bring to thee unwanted’Attention. Faith is not mine problem anyhow, Brother Brevard’My problem is what happens when faith becomes personified’Forget me, brother’lest thee fall under the eyes of darkness. I fear that they know me now’And Bob”.” He brushes past the monk and walks from the sanctuary. He pushes open the outer doors and is bathed in sunlight. The view is quite regal as he is silhouetted against the white for a moment as the door reaches its nexus, halfway open and halfway closed, where it hinges upon itself. The door closes and the monk sits down with a wheeze. Bob? Who was this Bob? And why would darkness dare watch this place, so far from civilization? Better to not dwell on those issues’Better to forget the shining man. After all, there were always tales to translate rather than compose. Brevard smiled as he pushed the man from his thoughts, and much easier than he should have. Pendragon does have a way about him?. Pendragon looked back at the church and sighed, thinking to himself?“Forget me??Tis for the best. Never know what problems thee might be presented. To this place I shall not return?” Down a narrow dirt road, he walks a ways until he comes to Xerxes tethered to a tree. The warhorse grazes on some of the lush grass that covers this hillside. Pendragon shakes his head, but smiles?“Thee art going soft, mine old friend. Grazing such will only get thee fat.” He pats the horse on the neck and un-tethers his reins. He begins to lead the stallion down the road, not riding. Troubled as he was, walking was always want to clear his head. He paused as they crested the hill and the view spread out before him. The village of Brathemen lay below, centered around a spring. Cool water flowed from the ground and ran through the village as a small stream. The people knew it not, but further south this would turn into the mighty river Bashelle. These villagers were quiet folks, and cared not what happened in other lands, far away’The view though, was magnificent? “Mine breath always catches when I look out over this vista. Many times have I come here to pray, and now it appears I have made mine last trek. I cannot put something so beautiful in danger; this war with the shadow is only going to grow more violent, and blood will be spilled. I only hope that I can keep the innocent blood to a minimum. Already there is blood on mine hands’And where is Bob” Surely, this cannot be what he wants!!” *****************************FLASH************************ Pendragon presses against the wall, cloaked in shadow. All is well as he seeks out the first name that Bob had given him. Nolan Sims. Pedophile and murderer. Also the CEO of a rather large computer component company. He had scouted the man for a couple of days, and he always passed this alley in his car. In front of a seldom busy intersection, this place afforded the perfect view of the stop sign and any cars that came through. Pendragon, silent as death, awaits the arrival of Sims. The man knew it not, yet his days upon this earth were numbered. Pendragon had a tough time with this? “To kill on the word of another’Even if that man do be God’THIS is not right. There should be something by which to judge the man. What if he is not this evil apparition that I have been taught that he is” What if he is no demon? “Tis blasphemy for me to question the word of God, but I wish I could see the man as he truly is with mine own eyes. Death is not something easily remedied, and I do not wish to cleave one who is undeserving of the attack?” A pearl colored Bentley slows down at the stop sign, and Pendragon moves? “Perhaps it is not mine to question?” Before he reaches the car, the man turns to him and grins’Pendragon skids to a halt. It is the wrong man. Sims never smiled at him that way. Sims was not supposed to pull such a large automatic weapon from the passenger seat. Sims was not supposed to open up at Pendragon, caring not who or what he shot. The intersection is never busy, but at this time, the sidewalk was doing a fairly brisk trade. Screams rang out as Pendragon deflected the few bullets that would have found him with the blade of Lucien. Blood spattered as bodies fell, men women and children alike. Finally the Bentley speeds off as Pendragon rushes towards it. He stands in the road, his chest heaving as the rage takes him. Only the sound of a revving engine makes him turn. In another car, a BMW, sits Sims. He smiles at Pendragon, the new champion of the White. Good and evil have been adversaries so long, and now it appears that the battle has been renewed. Tires scream as the car speeds towards Pendragon. He falls back into “Herron Wading in the Rushes”, the balance Blade form, and waits the speeding car. At the last moment he sidesteps and swings the blade. Sparks and glass fly as the blade slices through the windshield. The car passes, swerves, and careens into a telephone pole. Lucien glows with a dull light as Pendragon approaches the car. All he finds is a headless Nolan Sims. With a frown, he looks around at the carnage that the gun caused. People who can still stare stare wide eyed at the man with the sword. He shakes his head as he notices the children lying dead upon the ground. He sheathes the blade and walks into the smallish crowd, memorizing each face of those who lay dead. By the time he finishes, ten faces are bored into his mind. Ten children. Men he can handle, even women’After all, they had a chance to live a bit. These children, though. They never had the chance to grow up. To read the masterpieces, to learn who they truly were’To find love?.Love. It has eluded him as well. He wipes away a single tear as he walks away, avoiding the authorities. They would ask questions, and they would not like the answers Pendragon gave them. ******************************FLASH******************* And just like that, Bob was standing next to Pendragon in the meadow, although it was a different meadow now. Wheat fields stretch out as far as the eye can see, and here and there barns dot the countryside. As per the norm, Bob is clad in his white suit. His eyes are a touch sad as he stares into the distance, Pendragon looking startled as he was roughly jerked from his revelry AND his former location to be brought to this tranquil place. Xerxes doesn’t seem to mind as he begins contentedly munching on some of the wheat? “Sometimes, bad things happen, and I can do nothing about them. You cannot either, so do not be put off by the fact?” Bob says this sternly, and Pendragon looks at him? “Sometimes things happen” Thee set me upon this path, and innocents died’This does not just “happen”.” bob shakes his head, a bit irritated? “Do you know nothing” Do not question me or my motives. You saved lives with what you did, though you see it not. His computer chip held a secret program. One that was to be activated by a master control that he had grafted onto his arm?” Pendragon stares in disbelief? “Control” Grafted?” He scratches his head as Bob sighs? “No TV either big fella” No matter’He had a switch that he would flip when he thought the time was right. It would turn every computer that held it into a mind-trap. People would look at the screen, and then leave their homes with the intention of going for ice cream. The true purpose of the program, though, would make them attempt to kill the first person they saw. Kill them or die trying. The program was a loop, so if the person survived, they would move on to something else. A vile thing created by a vile man?” “But how did thee know it would work?” Pendragon asks this question timidly, and Bob merely arches a eyebrow at him. “Oh yes’That’Sometimes I forget that thee art an omnipresent being?” Bob looks wonderingly at Pendragon before cutting him off?“Let us speak of you now. The staging ground, GW” You are a champion twice over, are you not?” Pendragon nods. “Glory follows your very being and will only grow the more time you spend in the presence of divinity. You are not to be my tool all the time, yet sometimes our wants will actually coincide. You have a match’Against Jackson?” Pendragon perks up and smiles?“Aye’For mine dearly won Platinum. I’ll not give it up to the likes of he.” God smiles’He pats Pendragon on the back as a father would a son. In a way, I guess that is what he is?“I do not expect you to. Do you know what Jackson is?” Pendragon looks but doesn’t answer. Bob goes on?“Jackson is dead, Venneman. Namenlos’Nameless’The man journeyed to hell after he died and was sent back. His very presence on this plane of existence is an affront to all that is good. He know you for what you are. Darkness knows light as sure as night replaces day. He will try to quell you’He will try to take what is yours?” Pendragon’s hand tightens on the sword hilt and his eyes burn?“Take mine gold then” Never’Not even were he to wrest it from mine dead hands?” Bob sighs as Pendragon cocks his head?“Something the matter” Thee looks as though the cat was after thy tongue, and when he got into thy mouth, you bit down upon him and realized that it was a dead cat, and?” Pendragon “harrumphs” into his hand’Sigh turns to frown? “Witty.” Pendragon colors a bit?“He will try to take your soul. He will try to kill you’To make you serve his master. Let it not happen’Not everyone seeks only gold. Sometimes, it is much more complicated than we can ever hope for it to be. He is an emissary of Hell’You, the champion of the Heavens’It will indeed be a battle?” Pendragon relaxes his grip and folds his arms across his massive chest. He uses this puffed up pose to stare down at Bob, to loom as it is, and Bob seems to grow larger as Pendragon sees him first at eye level, then above, until finally he is craning his neck to see. With a gasp he drops his arms?“Use not thy witchery here, Bob. I am thy friend’I only wish to know why if thee knows everything that thee claim, why you have done nothing. Why leave it to me, when with thy power you could save the world with a thought!” Bob shrinks to normal size and sighs?“I cannot touch the world. It is the price of my power. My touch would rupture the prison where true evil lies hidden. My touch would end the world in darkness. Right now, only trickles of evil can get through. My touch would rip the fabric of existence and make death and life as one’it is not a risk I dare take. For that reason I have chosen you to help me’To do what is right for yourself and the world”.” “But mark my words”.Jackson?.The Nameless One?..Danger surrounds him. You must be on your guard?” ~TO BE CONTINUED~
