Lunatic Fringe

27 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-27-05 Dear Jay Robson, My name is Timmy Tucker, I’m twelve years old and I am your biggest fan. I have watched Global Wrestling since I was six years old when it was called UCWF. Ever since I saw you the first time I have always rooted for you’even if you were a bad guy sometimes. I was wondering how come you don’t wrestle anymore in GW? Did you retire? Did you get beat up so bad that your body won’t allow you to perform anymore? Or did you get so out of control by getting drunk all the time that you lost touch with reality and don’t know night from day? I’m not trying to make you feel bad Mr. Robson, I just wanted to know why you cheated all your fans and yourself out of being the greatest wrestler GW has ever seen. Thank you. Your Biggest Fan, Timmy Tucker P.S. Come back to Global Wrestling, if not for me’do it for you *** “That pretty much sums it up in a nutshell as to why I’m here, so maybe I’m a little bit rusty and maybe I’m a little bit out of shape. So what everyone has high tides and everyone has low tides’right” am I right? The man looked at me puzzled and confused, I was trying to do something different here, I was trying to do something some would call “thinking outside the box” but maybe I have killed to many brain cells from booze and getting punched in the head for all these years to even make any sense’even to myself. “Son’I don’t know who you are’I’m an insurance agent, I sell insurance, I don’t know what you are looking for but I defiantly don’t have it” The man exclaimed calmly, as he sat snuggly in his coffee colored chair on the further side of the oak desk. I stood up, infuriated at his comments and ran my hands through my scruffy dirty blonde hair and simply glared at him with my baby blue eyes. I don’t know if I was trying to think of something to say or if I forgot why I was even there, but then it came to me like a slap to the back of the head. “You’re Global Wrestling!! How could you forget about me?” I’m in the Hall of Fame damn it!!” I Screamed at the man, pointing my fingers crazily at him as I slammed my fist on top of his desk, his look abruptly altered from clam and collectiveness to panic and terror as I moved around the small office like a wild man. “This is Global Insurance’not Global Wrestling” He announced sharply as he backed himself up into a corner as if trying to keep himself out of harms way. I stopped in my tracks, as if a switch was turned off and looked at the man blankly. “You mean to tell me that this isn’t Global Wrestling?” You mean to tell me that this isn’t the office of Bubba McCoy?” Ha..Ha..Ha’you?” I fanatically proclaimed to the man with a faraway look in my eyes as I moved closer to him wagging my finger aggressively “You’you’your good you’don’t play with me like this’can’t you see that im not in the playfully, cheery, happy, bouncy, mood?” I muttered out to him, by this time I was right up in his face as he was now backed up as far as he could go into the corner. “I’I’I think you need some professional help sir’just please don’t hurt me. I will do anything.” He said petrified, as he turned his head and closed his eyes as if I would disappear like his vision “Anything?” I softly whispered into his ear only to be followed by? “ANYTHING!!” The word boomed from my mouth and into his ear as he dropped to his knees holding his hands over his head in sheer terror’as I laughed as I drove my head through the sheet rock wall where his skull would have been a few moments earlier. A few pieces and crumbs fell onto the man who was still crouching in the floor as if in fear for his life. “I am a professional’a professional wrestler’im Jay Robson damn it’The Ladies Man’The Hero of Heroes’The Self Righteous God Damned Superstar” I declared, ranting and raving as blood began to trickle down my forehead and drip from my nose as I flipped over a chair and kicked the desk in a frenzy only to turn back to the man hunched in the corner. I pointed my finger. “You get Bubba McCoy on the phone right now’RIGHT NOW” I yelled at the man as he trembled like a little girl in the corner “But I don’t know Bubba McCoy. I’I don’t know his phone number, why are you here I sell car insurance that’s all, I don’t know you or anything about Global Wrestling” He said, as I looked vacantly at him, my eye twitched a bit as blood continued to pulsate down my face. By this time the situation has spun wildly out of control, obviously this guy didn’t know who I was or where I came from, just he was frightened for his life and I was the one that did it’I didn’t know what was going on. (Seven months later) “That’s a pretty wild story Jay” Said Dan Roland, an executive with Global who was looking over the police report from the incident that happened a few months back “So you were delusional” Psychotic?...” “Yes...Yes’crazy, mental case, idiot’you name it, I was all of the above” I said to Dan, finishing his sentence for him as I sat up straight with in an uncomfortable wooden chair looking across a mahogany desk at a man I have never saw in GW before “Well’Jay, I will tell you, we would love to have you back in the ranks here in Global, but it seems like every time you want to come back it is because you are broke and want some quick cash and then “poof” you disappear again with out a trace? He claimed with a stern look on his tanned face and unsympathetic gaze from his beady brown eyes that were cutting a hole directly through me. “Its not like that this time I promise, so I have made some mistakes, I am aware of that, but just one more shot is all I need’its all I want is one more chance” I pleaded with the man across from me, almost to the point to where I was on my knees begging, but he just sat there’stone faced, and with no remorse for my situation “Well Jay, I will tell you what, I will go and talk to Bubba and see what he has to say about this matter, its not up to me, its up to him, he makes all the final decisions around this place, you know that, but I just don’t know how many “one more chances” he can give a guy with out just cutting him off completely? He told me with a sigh as I simply nodded my head and stood up and shook the mans hand “Ok thanks, I would appreciate any help you can give me” I abidingly said to the man as he stood up in his suit and tie to receive my hand. “No problem Jay, I will see what I can do” I walked out of the front office that day with my head down and my hands in my pockets like a little kid that just got expelled from school knowing that it was my last time ever to step on Global Wrestling property. (2 Days Later) **Ring **Ring “Hello?” I said as I picked up the phone nonchalantly and a thunderous voice erupted in my ear with a thick southern accent “Yello’Jay Robson” This is Bubba McCoy here?
