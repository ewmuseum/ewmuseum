Date: 2-19-06 {{Don't know if it counts or not, but enjoy.}} The scene opens on the rooftops of tall buildings that made up this metropolis. Something was leaping on the rooftops with incredible swiftness. It stops moving, as it was standing on the edge of a tall building. It’s soon revealed that the certain it was no other then the Deathscythe Outlaw. He have arrived to Tokyo after he almost caused the twin hyper engines in the Light Maidens? boat, Wave Cruiser to explode from going so fast to get here. Kyo was looking out at the city in deep thought, as he started thinking back to what happened at the bar in Okinawa that made him come here in the first place. ---- “This isn’t funny.” Kyo glared at Aion and Mother. “I want to know what the hell you two sickos are planning and I want to know now!” Everyone at the bar stops what they were doing and looked at them with puzzlement once again. Aion laughs a bit. “Easy, Mr. Kushinaga?” He smirked. “You’ll see soon enough.” Aion took a sip of his wine, as he stared at Kyo with a smile on his face. “Just sit back and enjoy the show.” Mother clings on to Aion’s shoulder while she had her seductive eyes on Kyo. Kyo looks at them with great frustration. “Enough of this shit.” He pulled out the large handgun with the white cross on it and aimed right at Aion’s head. Everyone in the bar goes into a panic at the sight of Kyo’s gun. Some of the customers drop to the floor where they hid behind their tables and chairs while the others ran out of the bar. “Tell me what the hell you two are planning!”” Kyo eyes flared up in a crimson colored aura. Zel lunges at Kyo with the intentions to cause bodily harm for pointing a weapon at his Lord. Kyo shifts his cold eyes over at Zel, as he spun the gun halfway around in his hand. He swings and struck Zel across the face with the hard handle of the gun. Zel stumbles back while he favored his face. Kyo leaps up and nailed Zel with a powerful mid-air spin kick that thrown him into a wall! Everyone in the bar becomes more scared after they witnessed that. Zel lies on the other side of the wall covered with debris, unconscious. Aion doesn’t even shed a look of concern for Zel. Kyo stares at the large hole in the wall. “I told you I’ll knock your punk ass through that wall if you stepped up to me again.” Kyo turned his attention back to Aion, as he spun the gun back in its? normal position and aimed it right back at his head. “With that little distraction out of the way, tell me what the hell you are planning.” “Say please.” Aion smirked, as he didn’t feel threatened toward Kyo. Kyo responses by pulling back the hammer on the gun with his flaring eyes still locked on Aion. Mother clings on to Aion’s arm tighter, as she was finding all this quite arousing in a very disturbing way. Aion sighs with slight disappointment. “Very well, but you do realize that it will ruin the surprise.” Kyo has a non-caring look on his face. Aion takes another sip of his wine with his eyes closed. “Go ahead and tell him, my dear.” Mother looks at Kyo with a disappointed look on her face. “Aww’You’re no fun, Mr. Kushinaga.” She sighed softly to herself with her eyes closed, as she pointed behind Kyo. Kyo glances over in that direction and saw the plasma screen TV, but he soon turned his serious eyes back at Aion and Mother. “It’s news so what.” “It’s what the news is talking that intrigues me.” Mother smirked. Kyo looks back at the TV and saw that the news was making a report about the new bullet train that was going to debut tonight in Tokyo. He blinks and looked with slight puzzlement while he kept his seriousness. “The Ricochet Express?” “She’s a beauty, isn’t she?” Aion watched the new broadcast. “I heard that this train will usher in the new era of transportation. I also heard that The Ricochet Express is said to be much faster than its processors.” Kyo glares at Aion. “Yeah, yeah, I heard all that crap but that still doesn’t tell me what the hell you two have planned.” Aion looks at Kyo with a grin. “Let’s just say that the people who are boarding that train will have their first and last ride on The Ricochet Express.” “What did you say!”” Kyo looked with great shock. “Think about it, Mr. Kushinaga. People gathered at the central of Tokyo to witness this great moment, but it actuality they’re really witnessing just the beginning of the end of their worthless existence in this world that would soon become our world.” Aion stared at Kyo with a dead serious look on his face. Mother nods. “And what better way to usher in the New Order with the deaths of over a hundred people.” “You sabotaged the train to crash?” “Oh no, Mother here just sent one of her Ghost Men to board the train.” Aion looked with puzzlement. “And I believe that he should be taking over the train and leading the clueless sheep to nothingness anytime now.” Kyo gives Aion a cold glare, as he wanted to blow his brains out right here and now, but he knew stopping the train was more important than satisfying his own rage. He holsters his gun back in his trench coat and began heading to the door. “You know there isn’t a way you can get from Okinawa to Tokyo before the train leaves the station.” Aion moved his glass around, as he watched the remaining of his wine moving inside the glass. “So, you made as just stay and watch the end of hundreds lives here with us.” Kyo ignores Aion by pulling out his sunglasses from within his trench coat and put them on over his serious brown colored eyes. Mother pushes a chair out beside her while she watched Kyo leave. “Yeah, you can sit here next to me.” Kyo still ignores them. Aion begins growing angry not liking how Kyo was ruining their fun. “Why!”” He glared at Kyo. “Why!”” Aion pushed Mother off of him, as he stood up from his seat and threw his glass right at Kyo with his back turned. Kyo moves his head to the side and watched the glass shatter on impact against the door in front of him. He stops walking, as he stood at the closed door. “Why you want to help these pathetic fools!”” Aion glared at Kyo’s back. “What has these weak mortals ever done for you!”” Kyo stands there in silence, as he placed his hand on shirt and grabbed hold of the wedding band that rested within his shirt. “You wouldn’t understand even if I told you.” He smirked. Aion still glares at him with much anger. “Aion.” Aion looks at Kyo with puzzlement. “Be sure to have your neck clean the next time we meet.” Aion looks with more puzzlement. Kyo looks over at Aion, as he took off his sunglasses where he revealed his eyes had death in them. “I don’t want my swords to slash off of a dirty neck.” Aion laughs a bit. “That is if you can call those swords out from your arms.” He smirked. “But I hope you know that you won’t be able to stop the train from its” certain demise after all you still only have half your powers and that won’t be enough to stop it.” Kyo puts his sunglasses back on. “I’ll make it enough.” He exited from the bar. “You won’t succeed.” Aion sharpened his eyes with seriousness mixed with anger. “You won’t succeed at all.” ---- Kyo was still standing on the edge of the tall building. He soon notices The Ricochet Express was departing from the station in the distant. Kyo nods, as he sharpened his eyes in seriousness. “I won’t let it happen.” He dove off the building. ==|Part II: The Golden Light|== On the moving Ricochet Express, some of the passengers are enjoying the sites of their city passing them by in their windows while the others were watching a movie on a screen at the front of the train. The Ghost Man that Aion mentioned sat among the passengers with a serious look on his face. “It’s time.” Ghost Man stood up from his seat. Within the train’s control room, one train conductor was keeping the train steady, as the other conductor watched for errors on the train’s computerized controls and meters. “How’s she holding up?” “All green here.” “Good.” The conductor leaned back in his seat with a relaxed look on his face. “It looks like we’ll be in store for a pleasant ride.” The other conductor nods with a smile. Gunshots are fired! The passengers scream in great fear and terror. Both conductors look at the close door behind them with puzzlement. “What the hell?” The door was suddenly bust open! Both conductors look with great surprise. Ghost Man enters the train’s control room with his large handgun aimed at both conductors. The conductor that was near the controls reached for the compartment that contained an emergency box with a gun inside. Ghost Man fires his gun! Back of the conductor’s head explodes in blood, as he slumped down in his seat with a frozen look of death on his face. The remaining conductor nearly falls out his seat in response to that. He soon regains his senses and gone to the stop the train, but he didn’t in account of the barrel of the Ghost Man’s gun that he felt on the back of his head. “What do you want?” The conductor looked at the Ghost Man. “To bring on the New Order.” The Ghost Man smirked. [:: ----- :: ] People are gathered at the central of Tokyo, as they were watching news report about The Ricochet Express with great enthusiasm on a giant screen that was attached to the middle of a large building. Aion and Mother were sitting outside of a restaurant that was across the street, watching the news broadcast while they drank white wine with smiles on their faces. Zel whose clothes were covered with debris stood by his Lord’s side with a serious look on his face. “This just in! We’re getting word that The Ricochet Express has been just hijacked moments ago!” The people were shock to hear this while they continued watching the broadcast. “We don’t know if this is an act of terrorism. Please be sure to continue to stay with us while we get more information on this situation.” People talk among themselves about this, as they can’t believe that something like that was happening. Aion holds up his glass with a smirk on his face. “To the New Order.” “To the New Order.” Mother smirked, as she held up her glass too. They toast. “This celebration is too premature. You haven’t won yet.” Aion looks over to the familiar female voice and saw that Mother’s twin sister, Megumi stood before them with seriousness on her face. Mother glares at her sister. “Aion.” Megumi kept her serious look on her face. Aion smirks. “Ms. Wind.” Megumi looks at her sister. “Masaki.” Mother turns away with an angry look on her face not wanting her to be here. Aion takes notice of Megumi’s attire, as he saw that she weren’t wearing her usual Light Maiden’s kimono instead she wore an white colored tuxedo. “Quite an interesting look for you, Ms. Wind.” He smirked. “But I thought Light Maidens weren’t supposed to leave the island without the Supreme Maiden consent.” Megumi stares at Aion. “I’m now the Supreme Maiden since you and the rest of your bullies killed her along with the almost the rest of her maidens throughout the world.” “Ah, then you were promoted all thanks to yours truly.” Aion smirked, as he held up his glass again. “This calls for another celebration.” “What do you think you can achieve for ending the lives of those over hundred people on that train.” Megumi glared at Aion. “What do you think you can possible achieve from doing this.” Aion takes a sip from his wine. “The extinction of the human race.” “Why do you hate the humans so much?” Megumi looked at Aion with puzzlement. “Have you experienced a traumatic incident with humans to give birth of this hatred?” Aion laughs. Megumi looks at Aion with more puzzlement. Aion stops laughing and looked at Megumi with some disappointment. “So, this is your little your plan trying to probe my mind to see why I’m doing this.” He sat his glass down on the table and leaned back in his seat with comfort while he kept his very light cerulean eyes on Megumi. “You don’t need to probe my mind to see why I hate these humans. Just look around you and the answer is there.” Megumi looks around her surroundings. “These worms aren’t worth protecting anymore. They make war that destroys everything. They’re killing themselves and killing this world.” Aion looked over to Mother with a smile. “Like I told your sister, we’re saving this world for the better good which is us.” “Saving it?” Megumi snapped her head at Aion and glared at him. “That’s what you think you’re doing. You think you’re saving it. Saving something doesn’t mean to kill it.” Aion sighs. “The humans will die bringing forth the New Order and there’s nothing you can do about it.” Megumi closes her eyes halfway with a smirk on her face. “Maybe you’re right, but there is someone that can stop you.” Aion blinks with a look of puzzlement on his face, but he soon realized who she was referring to. “Talking about the confused, Mr. Kushinaga.” He laughed. “He honestly believes that he can stop me too, but he can’t see that he’s fighting for the wrong cause. You know his past. You know what he did. I mean there shouldn’t be any reason for him to take the side of these cruds” over mine.” “He wouldn’t because of what he is.” “And what’s that?” Aion looked at Megumi with amusement. “An angel of mercy” You should know that man isn’t an angel. Angels aren’t savage killers.” Megumi stares at Aion with seriousness. “He made a mistake and he’s making things right for what he did back then.” She looked away with a look of slight concern. “Even if he doesn’t remember.” Aion smirks. “That man is a demon who vibes on death and destruction of the weak.” He leaned forward in his seat and looked at Megumi with his elbows on the table. “When he fails and I have doubt that he will, what do you think he will do?” Megumi looked at Aion with slight puzzlement. “Smell the wonderful scent of death, as he shows us that he could give two shits about these mortals.” Aion grinned. “When that happens I will welcome him as a brother to our cause. And we’ll both bring in the New Order for us, superior beings while the humans rot to nothingness.” Megumi glares at Aion with some hope in her cerulean colored eyes. “He won’t allow it.” Aion laughs a bit. “We shall see, Ms. Wind.” He held out his hand toward a chair that was near him. “But for now kick back and enjoy the show along with the possibly rebirth of the real Kyo Kushinaga.” Megumi declines Aion’s offer by sitting at another table with her eyes locked on the giant screen. Aion smirks before he turned his attention back to the screen. “Is the welcome committee there?” Mother nods with a smirk. “There and waiting, My Lord.” “That keep should keep him occupy until the train reaches its” final destination.” Aion took a sip of his wine while he watched the news. [:: ----- ::] Kyo was running quickly across a long rooftop on top of a very tall building, but he soon stopped at the edge where he dove off with his arms spread out. He flips in mid-air and landed on a low rooftop hard enough to make the ground underneath him to shatter. “That’s quite far enough, Mr. Kushinaga.” Kyo looks at the male voice, as he saw that a Ghost Man stood at the far end of the rooftop. “Get the hell out of my way.” He glared at the Ghost Man. “Afraid that I can’t do that.” Ghost Man returned the glare at Kyo. “After all I do have my orders to keep you here by any means which enables me to use force.” Kyo nods. “I figured you would say that.” He pulled out his large handguns and opened fire on the Ghost Man. Ghost Man swiftly maneuvered through the bullets like the agents does in The Matrix films. Kyo stops firing his guns, as he had a stupid look on his face. “And I should’ve known that you were going to do that.” Ghost Man lunges right at Kyo. While he was running, as seven more Ghost Men mysteriously leaped from behind him, as they all were heading straight for Kyo too. Kyo looks with surprise. “Shit!” He holstered both guns and began fighting off the Ghost Men. However, the Ghost Men was too much for him, as they jumped him and formed a pile on top of him, keeping him from going anywhere. “Get off me!” Kyo struggled to free from their grasp. “Get off me, damn it!” His eyes flared up in a dark crimson aura, as he burst through the pile and thrown the Ghost Men off him. He spins high in the air with both arms covered in fire held out, as it almost looked he did the same fire attack that the Kusanagi character does in the video games. Four of the eight Ghost Men were vaporized after getting fully struck by Kyo’s surprise attack. Kyo slowly descends and gently landed on the ground with his eyes closed. He opened his eyes and looked with surprise when he saw that the Ghost Men were stun. “What the hell just happened?” Kyo smelled burning leather, as he looked down and saw that the sleeves on his trench coat along with his gauntlets were completely burnt off. He notices that the markings on his forearms glowed for a brief moment. Kyo still looks with puzzlement. “Pay attention, Mr. Kushinaga!” Ghost Man lunged at Kyo and thrown him back with a brutal punch. Kyo crashes hard against a metal door! Ghost Men begins approaching the fallen Kyo. “I don’t know how you were able to do that, but this is the end of the line for you.” Ghost Man drew his gun along with the others. “Farewell, Mr. Kushinaga.” Kyo winces, as he stared at the Ghost Men with great anger not believing that his life was going to end like this. “Blood Storm!” Thousands of sharp blood needles suddenly comes out of nowhere and struck the Ghost Men, as they vaporized. Chi flips out of nowhere and landed before Kyo with her sword drawn. “I can’t just let anyone kill you. I want to have the pleasure on doing that.” She looked at him with a smirk through her hood. “Get in the line with the rest of the bastards who want me dead.” Kyo stood up, as he wiped the blood off his bottom lip. “So, what’s going on, Avenger?” “Oh, just the usual.” Kyo reloaded his guns with a dead serious look on his face. “People will die if I don’t save them.” “Why should it concern you if people die I mean they die all the time.” Chi looked at Kyo with puzzlement. “I dunno.” Kyo holstered his guns. “I just feel like if I don’t do then I’ll make her sad.” Chi looked with more puzzlement. “With you here you can take care of these guys for me.” Kyo began walking away. “What guys?” “Those guys behind you.” Chi blinks. “Huh!”” She looked behind him and saw that ten Ghost Men were standing behind her. “Try not to die, Chi!” Kyo jumped off the building. “What!”” Chi looked with great anger. “Avenger, you bastard!” Ghost Men lunges before they leaped up in the air, as they were coming straight down at Chi. Chi glares up at the Ghost Men, as she held the sword before her in a defensive stance. “C’mon!” Her eyes flared up in a sapphire colored aura. [:: ----- ::] In the train’s control room, the Ghost Man still stand behind the train’s conductor with the gun aimed at the back of his head. He soon pushes in his earpiece, as he listened to a voice on the other end. Ghost Man looks out of the control room when he heard something moving above the train. “He’s here.” He nodded. “Understood.” The conductor glances over at the Ghost Man with puzzlement. Ghost Man shoves the gun on the conductor’s face. “Eyes forward.” Conductor nods and quickly did what he was told. Ghost Man looks out of the control room once more with a serious look on his face. [:: ----- ::] In the central of Tokyo, the people still watched the news on the giant screen in hoping to hear more about the hijacked Ricochet Express. “I’m now getting word that someone has just jumped onto the moving Ricochet Express!” “So, he was able to get this far.” Aion looked at Mother with a smirk. “It appears that the Ghost Men weren’t able to match his skill.” “But that’s impossible.” Mother looked with puzzlement. “The Ghost Men are nearly invincible. He couldn’t defeat them even at his current power level.” Aion has a thinking look on his face. “Perhaps, there’s nothing left of the seal on his power, as he probably ready to explode with his demonic powers.” Mother didn’t say anything, as she kept her serious eyes on the giant screen while she sipped a hot cup of tea that she ordered. [:: ----- ::] The Deathscythe Outlaw was running carefully on top of the moving Ricochet Express, heading to the front cart, as he knew that the Ghost Man would be in the control room. The train passes a bridge. Kyo stops running when he sensed something behind him. He looks behind him and saw that a Ghost Man was there. “Didn’t I leave you guys with Chi?” Kyo looked with dullness. “I can’t allow you to stop this train.” “I hate it when people tell me what I can and cannot do.” Kyo lunged right at the Ghost Man. Ghost Man lunges at him. They start fighting, but the Ghost Man was countering all of Kyo’s moves. The Ghost Man soon pushes Kyo at the edge of the train with a strong punch to the chest. Kyo reaches out, as he tried to grab onto something, but he soon fell off the train. Ghost Man pushes in his earpiece. “The target has fallen off the train and now I’m recovering the body.” He began approaching to the place where Kyo fell from. Ghost Man goes to leap off, but he felt a pair of legs wrapped around his neck. “What!”” “I really had you going, didn’t I” Maybe I should be an actor.” Kyo hanged upside down, as he glared up at the Ghost Man with a smirk on his face. “It’s going to take a whole more to take me down. I hope your processors remember that the next time they go after me!” He threw the Ghost Man off the train. Ghost Man falls and crashed into the windshield of a parked car. Kyo pushes off the side of the train and flipped back on top of the train. He resumes running towards the front cart. But before he reaches the cart, a Ghost Man burst right through the roof. Ghost Man throws something at Kyo. Kyo catches it and noticed that he held some type of lever in his hands. “What the hell?” He glared at the Ghost Man. “What did you do!”” “Just something that could’ve stopped the train.” Ghost Man grinned. Kyo soon feels the increase speed from the train. “Bastard!” He lunged at Ghost Man. Ghost Man pulls out his weapon and began firing at him. Kyo continues running and ignored the bullets that grazed his body, as he leaped up where he connected a powerful spin kick that not broke the Ghost Man’s thick shades, but also thrown him right off the train. He runs at the hole that the Ghost Man come from and looked down with shock, as he saw that the train’s control were destroyed along with both conductors dead. “Shit!” Ghost Man without the shades grabs Kyo from the behind and moved him away from the hole, as he had Kyo in a tight bearhug. “Face it, Mr. Kushinaga. It’s over. You lost.” Kyo struggles to free from his tight grasp, but he stopped when a vision entered his mind. In his vision, he saw that the train was heading to end of tracks that have two strong springs, but not even those can stop the train from crashing. He shakes his head, as the vision left him. Ghost Man smirks. “Ah, you saw it, didn’t you” You saw the end of the line.” “You won’t get away with this.” Kyo glared over at him while he felt his bones cracking from the Ghost Man’s tight grip. “I don’t understand. Why would you want to save these humans?” Ghost Man looked at Kyo with puzzlement. “You supposed to be a demon and their kind could careless what happens to the weak as long as they’re part of their demise.” Kyo sharpens his eyes with seriousness. “Demon or angel!” His body started pulsing without him knowing. “I’m going to save these people!” Kyo exploded in a bright golden light! [:: ----- ::] “There has just been an explosion on The Ricochet Express!” The people were able to see the explosion from where they were. However, there was no explosion, as a bright golden beam of light shot up to the night sky. Aion, Mother, and Megumi look with great shock, as they were sensing an incredible power from where The Ricochet Express was. “What is this?” Mother’s eyes were quivering with fear. “You surprised me, Mr. Kushinaga! I didn’t think you were like me!” Aion began laughing while she stared at the beam. Megumi looked with surprise for a moment longer before she sharpened her eyes in seriousness, as she kept her eyes on the beam. The beam explodes! Tokyo was swallowed up in the blinding light! [:: ----- ::] Kyo shoots up in a bed. He looks around his surroundings with a distorted look on his face, as he saw that he was in a large bedroom. Kyo soon winces and saw that purification bandages were wrapped on certain parts of his body. “You’re finally awake, Avenger.” Kyo looks and saw that the cloak wearing Chi stood in front of the door. “Where the hell am I?” He stared at her with surprise. “What about the train!” Did it stop!”” “You’re at one of my safe houses.” Chi folded her arms across her chest. “To answer your question, the train did stop, but I don’t how. I mean I found you lying in front of the train, unconscious. Your hands were covered in blood.” Chi looked at him with puzzlement. “You mind telling me what happened?” Kyo favors his head. “You tell me.” He looked at Chi with great puzzlement. “How long have I been out?” “Three days.” Kyo gasps, but he soon threw the cover off him and started heading to the door. Chi blinks. “Where are you going?” “To the states.” “For what?” “I need to clear my head.” Kyo left the room. Chi looks with puzzlement. It’s obvious that he didn’t know what was going on, as he remembered everything that happened, but everything went blank after the Ghost Man grabbed him. He soon figures out a perfect remedy to cure his cloudiness. [:: ----- ::] My next dance partner has been named? For a moment there I almost thought I missed the fight.
