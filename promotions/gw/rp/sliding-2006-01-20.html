Sliding

20 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/20/06 Aris stares into the pouring rain, breathing deeply as he drags a single hand over his face, attempting to revive himself. The pouring of the rain beats against the metal roof of his rental car as if attempting to find a way inside. Aris? eyes are bloodshot, watery from the near twenty hours he has spent on planes to arrive at his current location. Toyama, Japan. Then it’s another hour back to Tokyo, and then seventeen hours later he’ll touch down in Seattle. There he will kiss his fianc? then its back on another plane for Dallas. His one hope, a possible nap on any of the flights, enough time to prepare mentally for what awaits him in Dallas. A fight. Not with one man, but two. Shane Weathers and Kris “I’m Ambiguously Gay” Kanton. Not a prize that one would ever consider, but something that fate has provided him. Aris knows there will be no sleep on the flights; his mind must be able to reach a state of rest for him to do so. With the coming feelings of terror, anxiety and paranoia -- and the thought of what must be done to protect him and his family brings exhaustion? Mental exhaustion. The windows of the rental car begin to slowly fog up. The sights of the city leisurely disappear behind a cased window of fog, the sounds on the other hand enhance. Aris pays no mind to any of this, his thumb and index finger rest at the bridge of his nose, his eyes closed, his thoughts alive. The car door opens and slams shut before Aris can open his eyes and look over at the now filled passenger seat next to him. Aris looks up and is stunned at the audacity of this man. The man wears black sunglasses, his long flowing blonde hair hangs over a black leather trench coat. The man wears a black on black suit under the trench coat. He smiles over at Aris, the man is beautiful, striking -- gorgeous if there was ever a man who could be defined by that word. “Hello Aris,” his voice as smooth as silk. “I thought you were in Seattle.” Aris replies. “Why did you come?” “To make sure everything is done to perfection, you have much to learn.” “About?” Aris, trying to remember their previous conversation they had before Aris boarded his plane for Tokyo. They man had sent him a letter, begging for him to meet him at the airport. Saying the past had to be redone right and it all started with the bill that he received from Japan. Aris had always wondered about how he knew about the bill from the plastic surgeon, but figured that guy was a snoop or his mailman, he’d never seen his mailman, so it very well could be him. “Listen, I don’t even know who you are.” “Of course you do,” his voice was so smooth and soft it could put you into a hypnotic state if you allowed yourself to be put into one. This man always got what he wanted whether by reason or force. The man grabs Aris black and brown track jacket, giving it a tug, “Glad you could dress up for the occasion.” “It looks like you are ready for a funeral.” Aris snaps back, not appreciating the rudeness of the man he barely knew. “Testy aren’t we Mr. Saint?” slight hostility in his voice, his eyes break into Aris? telling that if he gets to far out of line he’ll end up dead and no one will ever find the body. “Anxious, I can’t believe I’m here, I have a match tomorrow.” “To save your wrestling future is why you are here,” the man’s voice once again sooth and calming. Aris relaxes a bit in his seat. The words wash over him like a gentle river, slowly putting. “You know you have to protect yourself, you don’t want the whole world to know about that bill do you” It could ruin everything, everything you’ve worked so hard for, ruined by one letter.” “I don’t get how you know all of this.” “Aris -- the will be revealed in good time, but for now I must leave. I will see you soon.” The man slowly opens the door, looking over his shoulder out the door to make sure there is no traffic, he steps out into the rainy street. “Remember Aris, you’ve never seen me.” The door slams shut. A million thoughts flood into Aris? mind, causing a great pain all over his brain. A thousand drums pounding a thousand times a second echo through his brain. Aris closes his eyes, placing his thumb and index finger at the bridge of his nose trying mentally to get rid of the pain.. The door opens again, in slides a Japanese man wet from the rain, in his late thirties, well groomed and in a nice grey and blue suit. He smiles over at Aris, patting him on the shoulder as he closes the door. “Good to see you again my friend,” the man’s voice excited, as if Aris is a great, personal friend of his he has not seen in years. Though they saw each other three months ago. “Good to see you again, Dr. Yamaguchi,” Aris says quaintly as he opens up his eyes, removing his thumb and index finger from the bridge of his nose. He looks over at Dr. Yamaguchi, amazed that a man that has lived his whole life in western Japan, yet he speaks fluid English. “Please call me, Juichiro. I’ve told you that.” Juichiro stops patting Aris on the shoulder, reaches around himself, grabs the seatbelt and buckles himself in. “Where are we going?” “The Kurobe Gorge Bridge,” Aris replies. “Great, I have not been into the Tateyama Mountains since I’ve started my practice. How I’ve missed the greens, the never ending rocks, the bottomless pits,” Juichiro laughs at this last remark, Aris too nervous to laugh only offers a smile, fearing that a laugh would let on to his intentions. “Please turn on the air, people will think that we are as you American’s so koyfully say, making out,” Juichiro says making out with a deep, playful accent, almost seductive. This creeps Aris out a little bit, as he switches his gaze from the doctor to the controls on the front of the dash. “Here.” Juichiro reaches down and turns the air on, seeing that Aris was having a hard time figuring out which was which. Aris embarrassed, he knew how to turn on the air, he lived in Japan for almost three years. He says nothing, he sits waiting for the air to finally rid the windows of the fog. The fog that stands between him and the day’s destiny. Slowly the fog from the windows begin to lift, revealing that the rain has stopped, the sun has begun to peak out over the mountains and the sprawling delta city. A smile breaks the hardness of Aris? lips, the Japanese sunlight warms him inside, he won’t go through with it today, making the trip for nothing, but Toyama is a beautiful site that eye must behold to completely understand it’s magnificence. Aris puts the car into drive, turning his head to look behind him, it’s clear, he pulls himself into the street and begins driving through the maze of the delta, bridge after bridge over island after island, through city streets to residential, to rural, finally into the forest on the way to the mountains. The entire way Juichiro talks about how a mudslide had taken out his garden and how is never going to be able to restore its beauty. Aris never paid attention to a single word from the doctor to his left. The sun had disappeared behind dark rain clouds. The thoughts of his conversation with the long haired blonde man once again run through his mind. “I can’t, I could if I wanted to, but it’s not who I am, though he says it is,” Aris mind goes blank for a few moments as he drives the windy roads of the mountains toward their final destination. The rains begin to fall again, Aris switches on the wipers, still Juichiro continues to have a conversation with himself. Aris finds that he has been doing the polite nod and the “yeah” and the “uh-huh” to keep the conversation going, he’d prefer silence, but that won’t obviously happen, not here, not now at least, not til later. “There are all these mounds of mud and rock scattered through my backyard. My garden ruined,” Juichiro continues. “Uh-huh.” “I think it could give it character to just replant over the mounds and maybe clean up the rocks, sure the kids will not have room to run and play like before, but there are other places to play, there are fields not too far away that I played in as a boy,” Juichiro stops, his eyes show that he is reflecting on something. “When I was a boy, there were fields and fields outside of town where everyone ran and played, lovers would make love in the tall silky grass, it’s where I kissed my wife for the first time, it’s where we made love for the first time actually,” He smiles. “I can’t believe I am telling you this.” Aris suddenly paying attention, continues, “It’s alright, sometimes you need some one to talk to.” “I know, that’s what my therapist has said.” “Was my plastic surgeon insane while he worked on me” Nah, he’s?” “Aris, I’m horribly depressed, I never get to see my wife and children anymore, my work has consumed me. In fact,” There is a long pause, the rain pelts the car, and the air blows on the windows to keep them from fogging up. If they were to fog up the car ride would be a bit more dangerous. In this time of silence Aris imagined that happening and car speeding off an embankment into one of the never ending trenches that run along side it. “My wife has left me.” “What”!” Aris almost slams on the breaks and pulls the car over, but he knows there is only one mile to go. “She took my children and has gone” To her mother’s I believe. Isn’t that where they always go?” Juichiro forces a sad smile. Aris feels like crying, his heart is broken, he feels he can almost relate to the pain that Juichiro is going through. Aris no longer wants to toss Juichiro off the bridge, he wants to help his friend. Was Juichiro actually his friend or did he just make up this sob story because he knew what was happening and he was lying through his teeth to save his life. Aris became enraged and began to drive a little faster. “What are you doing?” Juichiro almost cries, his eyes welling up with tears, he was telling his truth, he received the call this morning from his wife. To him right now death wasn’t something to fear it was something to be welcomed. The cars tires begin to slide over the wet pavement through each corner. The back end of the car feels as if it could go off the road at any second. Aris still angry grips the steering wheel tightly, his knuckles as white as his soul, his plans are still going forward, but now he no longer cares if he takes his own life. “Aris, please slow down. I beg of you.” Aris shows no signs of slowing down. Juichiro’s previous thoughts of death begin to fade now that he is actually put into a life and death situation. Now life is his choice to keep it, he doesn’t want to die, he wants to live. He reaches over and grabs the steering wheel, Aris quickly elbows the good doctor in the nose breaking the grip, but it’s too late, the car begins to spin out of control down the highway. Aris cranks the wheel this way and that trying to catch a bit of traction on the pavement below, every few seconds upon a complete turn he sees his destination, the gorge, nearly a thousand feet to the bottom, you’d be dead of a heart attack before you hit bottom if you were so lucky. Images begin to race through Aris? head, time slows down. For a second he saw Andrea, he was holding her in his arms, she was casting a beautiful smile upon him, Aris lit up inside, maybe Juichiro was telling the truth, he didn’t deserve to die, not today at least, not until he was ready. Obviously he wasn’t ready. The car slides off the road into a grass gravel mixture on the side. The gorgeous blond man leans against the rail of the bridge, watching the car slide to its impending doom. They say that when a man is about to die, his life parades before him; tonight, there were two parades. The car is on a dead slide, Aris furthest away from the edge, he looks over to the passenger side of the rental. Juichiro’s face pressed against the glass, his eyes wide with fear watching the car slide toward the edge, then after that the trees, the rocks that it will hit on the way down to the river, deep and cold -- hopefully he would be dead by then. Aris eyes had met the gorgeous blond man’s as the car began to slow down on the mixture of grass and rocks. Aris suddenly felt at peace for whatever reason, an eerie sense of calm. He has no idea the car was beginning to slow, but all the same, Aris reached out with his right hand and opened the door, Juichiro paid no attention, his eyes never leaving the cliffs edge. A second and half later the car topples over the edge, slamming against trees, rocks and sharp boulders down nearly one thousand feet to the bottom. Juichiro had been killed when his side of the car hit a pointy boulder. He was killed instantly, he felt no pain, but his heart full of regret and his mind weighed heavily with sadness. Aris sat quietly in the rain on a spot of grass a few feet from the road. The gorgeous blond man was now walking toward him. Aris listened to the rain in the valley, along with the echoes of crunching metal rolling down a ravine. Aris did not feel guilty, he was almost glad the Juichiro Yamaguchi had died today, he was relieved he would never have to do this again. The gorgeous blond man has taken a seat next to him, he begins to rub his shoulders, Aris was tense and this helped. “Good job, Aris, good job,” The man’s voice, light and still silky smooth. “It’s was an accident,” Aris shrugged the man’s hands off of him, “I was driving too fast, I lost control, I bailed and he didn’t. If he did, the car would have crushed him all the same.” Aris looks over to the edge of the cliff seeing that he was a lot closer than he thought he was, ten feet to be exact. Ten more feet, one and half seconds longer, he would have been tumbling down the rocky cliffs into a river that was ageless and cold. A muffled splash is heard as the car reaches its final resting place in the bottom of the greenish-blue river that lies at the bottom of the ravine. “You had to do this.” “I did nothing,” Aris was beginning to grow agitated with this man, saying that he has killed another man. It was an accident, it would prove to be an accident, this was not a murder. “Why can’t you understand the roads are windy and wet, I’m sure this has happened a bunch.” “How are you going to get back to town?” Aris was now frustrated, he was hoping for a different response, he tried to push an angry tirade at this man, the man with no name, but he couldn’t, all he could get out was a soft and muffled. “I don’t know.” “Well you better start thinking about it,” The blond man got to his feet as a black limo with Florida license plates made it’s over the long bridge. “My ride is right there, I’m not sure what you are going to do, but you can’t ride with me.” “Why?” Aris was now confused. “It would be impossible.” The man made his way to the road, sliding both hands into the deep pockets of his black suit pants. The wind caught is blond hair; pulling it away from the leather trench coat that he wore. It was getting dark, yet he still had on those wire framed sunglasses. The limo was taking forever. “The limo looks big enough, we should both be able to fit in there.” The blond man shook his head, licking his lips as he did, then cut Aris a brief smile. “Aris if you think about it, there is no way you can ride in this limo with me,” he said. Aris grew confused and the look on his face told the blond man as such. “Listen, not everything can be explained, but for a good reason, there is so much you need to know and a lot more you don’t. If you did you’re head would hurt. Make sense?” The limo pulled up to the blond man, the door swung open as it stopped, but there was no one in the back. Must be some damn good Japanese technology; Aris would have normally though, but not now, his mind was wrapped around what the blond man had just said. It made sense, a lot of sense, but all the same it didn’t. Aris headache returned. The limo door shut and the blond man was out of site, tucked away inside, in the warm, possibly with twenty others inside, that’s why he couldn’t ride back. The limo began to drive away. Aris decided that he needed to stand up and start walking back toward town his flight to Tokyo left in a little over two hours, his flight to Seattle in seventeen, his flight to Dallas in eighteen. It was going to be a long, cold walk back to Toyama, but that’s OK, he needed the exercise, but what he really needed was sleep. Aris stood, walking to the road, watching the tail lights of the tail lights disappear behind the first bend. Aris licked his cold chapped lips and began to walk. Hopefully a car wouldn’t hit him on the way back to town. Hopefully one would pick him up and take him the rest of the way to the end of his journey. Wherever that maybe.
