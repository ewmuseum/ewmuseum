In the Parking Lot

18 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/18/03 The scene opens outside of the arena in the parking lot, just after the conclusion of Wednesday night's House Show. The camera is bobbing up and down and the cameraman runs after Overload commentator Brandi Sights. Her hair sways from side to side as she runs and chases after Ice . Ice is pulling a blue travle bag, while wearing a long silver jacket, a skin tight white t-shirt, and black gym pants. His black gym bag is slung over his right shoulder as he hurrily walks throught the parking lot. Brandi, screaming and yelling for Ice to stop while chasing them finally catches up to him just as he reaches his rented white Ford Bronco. Ice's hair is a little messed up, and some sweat still remains from his match. Brandi grabs Ice's left arm just as he opens up the driver's side door to the rented vehicle. Ice throws his black gym bag into the Bronco and slams the door shut. He violently snaps his head and stares at Brandi with anger. Brandi jumps back in fear. As she jumps back she stumbles and almost falls, Ice reaches out and grabs her right wrist and catches her before she can fall. Brandi's face turns red from embarrassement, but Ice's angered demeanor does not change. 'Can I help you?' Brandi gets an embarrassed humble smile on her face and tilts her head to the left, as if she was a little kid asking her father if she can stay the night at one of her girlfriend's houses. Ice's angered demeanor still does not change. He folds his arms in front of his chest and snears. Ice's stare does not waver from Brandi, and it almost appears that he doesn't even acknowledge that the cameraman is standing next to him. Brandi brushes off her black coat, shakes her head, puts the microphone up to her mouth and begins to speak. Her words come out first as if she is whispering in attimidation, but then she regains her composure. Brandi: 'Um.. sorry to bother. But you se.....eee... I ...I was wondering about your comments on your match on Overload against Christopher Canada, and Angus the Bull.' Ice tilts his head to the side, still with that look of anger and just stares at her for what seems like an eternity, but in reality is only a few seconds before answering her question. 'You want my comments on my match Friday night on Overload? Is that right? And who are you to bother me, while I am leaving to go to my hotel room? You think because because I have a contract here, and occassionally allow people like you interviews that you can just come and run me down and ask me whatever the hell you feel like, whenever the hell you feel like asking? Well you want your answers, I'll give them to you. My match, it's this simple, Angus doesn't have the tools to hang with me in the ring, and as for Christopher Canada, he's lucky to even be scheduled in a match with the likes of me. You see, I've told you what was going to happen, and I've forewarned you about the consequences. Have you listen? No, you haven't. I'm tired of not being heard! I'm tired of not getting the recognition I DESERVE! But to prove my point, ONCE AGAIN I AM MAKING A PROMISE. I PROMISE THAT I WILL BE VICTORIOUS THIS COMING FRIDAY NIGHT. I PROMISE THAT I WILL BEAT BOTH CHRISTOPHER CANADA, and ANGUS THE BULL! You see, I am a man of my word, and when I promise something, I always keep it. So what happens Friday night at Overload is a message for anyone in the GW who want to step up to the plate and get in my face. You will get hurt...HANDS DOWN!' A low rumble rolls through the dark night sky. A small drizzle of rain drops fall from the silky silver skies above. Ice looks up at the sky and looks to be in a daze as he just stares and stares. Brandi looks at him, then looks over at the cameraman and shrugs her shoulders before looking up at the sky to see what Ice is looking at. Ice's daze is broken as he begins to speak, however his gaze does not waver from the cloud filled night sky. 'At Overload I will take out two nobodies, who have no business even being in the same arena as me. And I will prove that none of the so called superstars of the GW have what it takes to even think about being in my league. This Friday night, to those that know me, and my history I will reminded you of one thing. And to those of you who don't know me, I will inform you for the first time that this is MY WORLD, and only I rule this world. The future of the GW is in serious jepordy because, though it may be summer, the temperture is changing, and it is growing colder. And there isn't a damn thing any of you can do to stop it.' Ice stops speaking and just gazes up at the sky again. Another low rumble of thunder fills the night sky. Brandi: 'Speaking of things to come, what about your upcoming match next Friday night on Overload against Venom Alexander?' Ice slowly lowers his head and turns it towards Brandi. He gives her a quizzicle look. 'WHO?' Brandi: 'Venom Alexander.' 'No, I heard what you said, and I still have no idea who in the hell you are talking about. Again another nobody wanting to make name for themselves by stepping into MY WORLD and trying to match up with me. Well son, you bring your little wanna be somebody ass to Overload next Friday night. You walk your scrawny little ass down that aisle and step into the ring. Then you look deep, and you look hard into these cold blue eyes and you will see your fate standing before you. It will be then that it hits you. It will be then that the cold strikes your body, sending chills up and down your spine, making the hairs on your arms and the back of your neck stand on end, and the fear will settle in. At that moment in time you will look around, hoping for someone to come down to save you, but no one will come. And it will be at that moment you will think to yourself , oh sh!t what have I gotten myself into. And then before you know it, your fate will have occurred, and you will have been put on ICE.' Ice takes a deep breath and opens the driver's side door. Ice turns and looks back at Brandi Sights before entering the vehicle. 'If your questions have been answered, and we are done, I think I will be on my way.' Brandi nods in response and steps away from the white Ford Bronco. Ice slides into the driver's seat and starts the engine. The white Ford Bronco speeds away into the night. Brandi stands and watches as it can no longer be seen. A smirk grows across her face, and she heads back into the arena. Scene fades to black.
