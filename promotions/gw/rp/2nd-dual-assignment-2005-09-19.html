2nd Dual Assignment

19 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/19/05 2nd Dual Assignment ? Father Of All Battles Deep inside the confines of the Asaka Ninja Clan’s stronghold, the entire Asaka Ninja Clan clad in gray and blue silently sits on their knees with their head lower on the hard wooden floor of a large room in four long rows with their Head Priestess, Athena Asaka who’s the mother of both, Kagome and Katsumi Asaka in her blue and white priestess kimono sits before them in silence too. Suddenly, the paper screen doors behind them opens, all the ninjas and the Head Priestess look and see a serious Katsumi Asaka in punk rocker attire enters the large room. Close behind Kat is her associate and her Global tag team partner, Ginger Ninja better known as Heather Burnside in street clothes. Heather curiously looks around the room before she follows Kat inside, but she stops suddenly by the sharp swords from two standing guard ninjas. “Only those of the Asaka Ninja Clan may enter here.” Guard Ninja declared. Heather cautiously eyes both Guard Ninjas. “I’m with Katsumi.” “Regardless, only those of our clan may enter beyond this point.” Other Guard Ninja replied. “But?” Heather began. Kat stands in a clear aisle with her back turn on Heather. “It’s ok. I’ll be fine.” “Are you sure?” Heather queried with concern on her face. Kat nods. “Ok, I’ll be outside if you need me.” Heather said. Heather eyes both ninjas before she exits the room, as one guard ninja closes the paper screen door behind Heather. She stands outside staring at the close doors with concern. Heather emits a soft sigh and casually walks away leaving Kat alone with her fellow ninjas and her mother. Behind the close paper screen doors, Kat walks further down the clear aisle keeping her serious eyes on her mother not paying heed to the other ninjas around her. “She seems nice and concern for you, Katsumi.” Athena complimented with a smile. Kat sits in the middle of the aisle on her knees with impatience on her face. “You didn’t call me here to praise my English associate. You call me here on official clan business, so give me my assignment and I’ll be on my way.” “It has been quite a while since we last seen each other. I figured we could catch up on things before I give you your assignment, my daughter.” Athena said in slight sadness. Kat dully looks around the room and takes heed of the ninjas. “With the entire clan here, you got be kidding.” Athena holds up her long kimono sleeve to her face slightly as she sniffles and cries. “I see” You don’t want to catch up with your mother. It has only been three years since we last talked.” “Mother, we just talked when I became a full-pledge ninja two months ago.” Kat softly said. Athena still crying, but it’s reveal that she’s holding a tiny bottle within her other long kimono sleeve as the bottle contains a substance to make a person cry instantly. “Regardless, it has been forever since we talked. Why is that” Do you not love me anymore?” Athena cries much louder. The entire clan looks embarrass. Kat too shares embarrassment. Kat clears her throat and dully looks at her mother. “No, it’s always great to see you again.” Athena suddenly stops crying. Kat closes her eyes and chuckles softly with a look of modest on her face. Athena furiously stares at her daughter. “I can’t believe that she’s going to make me do this.” Kat said to herself with a bore sigh. Kat takes a deep breath before she quickly becomes overwhelm with joy and has tears in her brown eyes. “Mommy!” The entire clan falls over. Athena’s furious look disappears and it’s replace by the same overwhelm look of joy that Kat has. “Daughter!” Athena contently said. They both get up and hug each other in the middle of the room. The clan simply watches in disbelief. Kat changes her look to dullness while her mother hugs her. “Why me?” Kat questioned under her breath. Meanwhile, at the stronghold’s courtyard, Heather wanders around in deep thought about what’s happening to her tag partner right now. “Excuse me.” A gentle voice said. Heather turns and sees Kat’s older sister, Kagome in her red and white priestess kimono stand at the far side of the courtyard. Kagome smiles kindly. “Are you lost?” Heather blinks and slowly shakes her head. “Oh no, I’m here waiting on Katsumi.” “Oh” What happened to Jade?” Kagome questioned. “They broke up.” Heather softly replied. Kagome nods. “I see. I actually thought those two would be together. Are you my sister’s girlfriend?” “Oh no, we’re just friends and tag team partners.” Heather answered. Kagome approaches Heather as she holds out. “I’m Kagome, Katsumi’s older sister.” Heather kindly takes Kagome’s hand and shakes it. “I’m Heather Burnside. Nice to meet you.” “Likewise.” Kagome replied. Heather tilts her head with a hesitant look on her face. “Forgive me for asking, but I was wondering if you could tell me what’s going on. Why did you summon Kat?” “I’m sorry, but I can’t tell you anything since you’re not with our clan.” Kagome explained. “Oh.” Heather softly said. Kagome stares at Heather, but she turns away from her and tranquilly stares down at the courtyard’s pond. “The only thing I can tell you is that Katsumi has to retrieve something of great importance to us. Father is briefing her now about it.” “Father” There was a woman there who wore the same clothes as you, but hers was blue and white.” Heather commented. Kagome curiously looks at Heather. “Blue and white” That’s our mother, but what is she doing there. Unless? She’s not.” Heather looks confuse. Back at the room, the clan is still sitting on their knees on the hard wooden floor while Athena sits before Kat. Athena looks at her daughter, but mainly at her hair. “Kat, I love what you did with your hair. Though I’m surprise that you cut it this short since you always gone on how you loved your long hair.” “Just figure I would try something new.” Kat embarrassingly said while blushing. Suddenly, a paper screen door slides open from the side of the room. The clan, Athena, and Kat turn to the door and see that a serious Hayato Asaka who’s Athena’s husband and the father of Katsumi and Kagome in his traditional black robes stands in the doorway. The clan respectively bows their head at Hayato. Athena moves back to the front of the room with her head lower. “You could’ve at least worn your ninja attire to this meeting.” Hayato seriously said. Kat glares at her father. “Hello to you too, Father.” “You must know one thing about my sister. She doesn’t get along with our father.” Kagome said. Back at the stronghold’s courtyard, Heather curiously looks at Kagome. “Well, what family doesn’t have a problem like that?” Heather asked with a smirk. Kagome looks at Heather. “The only difference between those families and ours is that we don’t make up too easily.” “Fighting is a way to strengthen a love between two family members.” Heather commented. Kagome lets out a soft chuckle. “They love each other dearly, but they just can’t stop fighting.” “What started this?” Heather questioned. “I guess you can say it all started when Katsumi was born. When Katsumi could walk, Father started her intense training of becoming a ninja. Even when Kat went to school, she was doing ninja training since Father signed her up sports that included gymnastics, swimming, track and field, Judo, Kendo, and any other courses that will her training.” Kagome explained. Heather surprisingly looks at Kagome. Kagome sighs. “You can say that Katsumi had a controlling childhood. However, Katsumi wasn’t going to have it as she decided to take a course of her own which would help her become one Japan’s top popstar idol. Of course, Father wasn’t too please about this since he figured that this would ruin her training on becoming a ninja. Kat continued her training at home and school, but with a new aggression. I seriously think because of our father being controlling made her despise all men which lead her to beat every guy in the courses that our father signed her up in.” “I didn’t know all of that.” Heather said. All of the sudden, a cell phone rings in Heather’s clothes. Heather blinks and curiously stares down at her clothing as she pulls out her ringing cell phone. “Hello?” Heather answers. Heather looks content with a smile. “Really, that’s great. Kat is going to be thrilled when she hears the news.” Heather said. Back at the room, Kat still sits on the hard wooden floor staring at both her mother and father. “So, you want me to receive an old sword from a museum that’s close to home” Why? What is so important about this sword?” Kat questioned. “It’s because?”” Athena started. Hayato glares at Kat. “Why you want to know” Do you have something far more important than doing for your clan?” “Excuse me for having a fucking life outside of being a ninja.” Kat furiously said. “Katsumi!” Athena shouted. Hayato looks away from her daughter with dullness. “And it’s that life that’s making you lose sight at what’s important.” Kat stands up and glares at Hayato. “I knew it was a mistake to come here.” “Katsumi! Hayato!” Athena shouted. Kat heads to the back paper screen doors. “Forget it.” Hayato waves his hand down as one of the standing guard ninja approaches Kat with a wooden box in his hands. Kat curiously stares at the ninja then the box. “It’s your new ninja uniform. It’ll be sure to protect you on the danger ahead and do be sure to bring the sword back to us before the next full moon.” Hayato said. Kat snatches the box from the ninja as she resumes heading to the door. “One more thing.” Hayato seriously said. Kat glance at her father with furiousness on her face. “Be sure to use our family sword rather than that old Reverse Blade Sword of yours.” Hayato said. Kat slides open the door and exits the door, but she slides the door close hard behind her. At the courtyard, Heather lights up with a smile when she sees Katsumi approaching them. Kagome watches her young sister approach from the side. “Guess what, Kat. Global has put us in the big tag team tournament and our first opponents will be the Global Champion, Mindy Mercer and her cousin, Michael Mercer.” Heather excitingly said. Kat simply walks by Heather with a furious look on her face. Heather blinks and looks in confusion. Kagome sighs softly and looks down with disappointment. Heather soon turns and is staring at the back of Kat’s head. “Kat, did you hear us?” Heather shouted. “We’re facing Absolute Merciless!” Kat stops walking and turns slightly as she glares at Heather with a great fury in her eyes. Heather looks a bit nervous. “Absolute Merciless” That’s what we’re going to be. They will fall to us, the Divine Darlings.” Kat said coldly. The scene fades with Kat walking away with the crimson box under her arm as Heather quickly runs after her partner while Kagome looks disappointed. ==To be continue==
