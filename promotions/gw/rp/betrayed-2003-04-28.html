Betrayed

28 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-28-2003 A lot of things passed though Ely Braxton’s head as he walks down the dirt road. Braxton really did not care about the cold or the rain, hell he did not even care about the lightning sticking the nearby fields.. He just had to walk? As he walked Braxton pondered the events of the last few weeks? It all started when he left Briggs and Logan to tend to Donovan McCoy. ============================ Braxton grabbed Briggs by the arm as he entered the office of the Dark Horse Saloon. “Get him to a hospital, and make sure the McCoy’s are safe. This is going to be a war Briggs. You best sober up.: Braxton said. Looking into Briggs eyes. “I’m no dumb ass Braxton. I know what to do.” Jesse said with more venom then needed, Then again he was drunk. Jesse pulled his arm, but Braxton held on, Briggs looked down to Braxton’s hand and back up, both men eyed each other and slowly Braxton released Briggs’s arm, and turned to leave. “Wait! Where are you going Braxton?” Asked Briggs in a harsh tone, as he kneeled down to an unconscious Donovan McCoy. Braxton stopped. “Back to my roots Briggs. Something I did not want to do, but now. Now I have no choice. Take care of them Briggs, contact Brick. Tell him what has happened, and to meet me at the airport Saturday at ten o’clock am. I am counting on you Briggs. Don’t let me down. This is important.” Braxton said with out looking back. Not waiting for any reply Braxton descended the stairs.. ============================ “Why did I not see it then” The cold look in his eyes. Briggs has had that same look in his eyes since he returned out of the blue.” Braxton says to his self. His head hanging low as he walks in the rain? ============================ After heading home and making some calls, Braxton walks out to his truck. He stops before entering and pulls out his cell phone. Better call Christina and check in on Donovan. Braxton punches in the numbers and waits.. He leans up against the truck as he does. Finally. A female voice is faintly heard on the other end.. “Chris. Have you heard” Yeah. Looks I am going to get these guys. NO matter how long it takes. I have a good idea on who did this attack. I just got to make sure he doesn’t do it again? Yeah, I will be careful Chris. Look, Have you talked to Briggs? “. What” What do you mean he took off. Damn him. I got to go Chris. NO. Everything is okay. Yeah, See you Sunday.” Braxton says as he clicks the cell phone off. A few more quick punches and he is on another call. ? Hello, Is Brick there. Yeah, This is Ely Braxton. Okay. I will wait.” Says Braxton as he climbs into the truck and pulls out.. ? Hey Brick. Look man I need some back up. Donovan was assaulted and left for dead at the Saloon. Yeah I have a good idea who the basterds are to. I just need some one I can count on to cover my back. Okay, know how to shoot a gun? Yeah it is that serious. Okay make sure you’re at the airport at ten am in the morning, I will pick you up. Okay thanks hoss.” Braxton clicks the phone off and tosses it into the passenger seat. ============================ Braxton approaches the small river about a mile into the land that Bubba’s ranch was located on. He liked this place. It was? It is his home. Braxton walks up to large bolder that looks to have steps in the side and he climbs it and takes a seat above the river. He thinks back to his and Bricks invasion of Omni Tec facility in Dallas. Unfortunately the place had already been attacked and was nothing but a burnt out shell. Failure. Braxton watched the rain fall into the river. Letting the sounds ease his tension. All that ended as he heard a crack of sticks.. Braxton rolled and dropped off the bolder. Ready to attack. He let out a deep sigh as he spots Bob Blackburn. Bob was not much of a sight. More like a small skeleton of a man. Not the greatest looker in the world and had to where eye glasses. That would not have been bad save his was wired and taped together. “Braxton!” He shouted in a weasely rasping voice. “Behind you” Braxton spoke out of the darkness. ? Ohh hey there you are. Look you told me to find you if I found anything out about the Fire at Omni Tec?” He says rubbing his hands together and looking as if to expect some monster to emerge from the bushes. “Yeah” Braxton replies folding his arms. Bon gulps hard, Maybe the monster was already here. He takes a breath?. “Well the night before you and Brick went there. Tree men were seen in that area. I got footage from a guy who was filming the place for a news report, and You wont believe this, But those guys are from that federation that Bubba was running.” He says his interest showing. Bob may look like the underside of a rock, But he was the best damn privet detective that Braxton knew. More important, Bob would keep his trap shut, and not quit till things were found out. Plus he was not unknown to bend a few laws to get to the bottom of things. Bubba called him a truth seeker. That’s what Bob was. No matter how bad that truth reviled its self to be, Bob would find it. “You wont believe this Ely. But one of those guys was Jesse. He was there right before the fire started.” Bob said in a worried tone. “Who were the others?” Braxton asked anger rising in his voice. “Quartz and Derek Draven.” Bob said with a deep look of concern on his face. “Harvesters!” Ely bellowed. “Yeah. But the question is was Briggs meeting them” ? ? Ely ELY!” Bob looks around and Braxton is no where to be seen. “Damn it! Now I got to walk back alone! Shit!” Bob says and heads back down the dirt road? ============================
