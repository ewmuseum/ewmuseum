The Willing Informant

18 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/18/2005 June 18th, 2005 Somewhere near Salem, Oregon Aboard the Jaedr -Currently being written in a florid script by Virgil, in a leather- bound journal I have such efficient help. Certainly, without my veritable legion of ghouls and otherwise coerced or beguiled minions, moving about this sprawling wasteland of a country would be much more difficult... this is most important, perhaps, during times like these, when I have so little time to waste. The woman who was creeping about my old estate now sits firmly restrained in my warehouse in Seattle... according to David, with little trouble involved in the capture. This is most fortunate, because I liked that mansion... it was a beautiful piece of architecture, filled with my trivial possessions and memories... my books, my musical instruments, all gone to a cruel act of arson. But now... yes, now there will be answers. I know who launched the attack, but with the help of my new captive, I will know where they launched it from. Shadow will be just as interested in this information as I am, for, after all, it was her life they were attempting to stamp out. Now that she has discovered her true self, and thus her true capabilities, they will find themselves confronted with an entirely different being... along with myself, of course. Another hunters' guild, crushed between the fingers of their 'prey'. It will more than likely be too simple... but vengeance does not take sport into account. I was finally reunited with my dearest Shadow, the last week. Certainly, I assumed she would be poised to rip my throat out and feast on what organs I still have, but it appears she acknowledged it as the same mistake that I had. What misfortune and pain that could have been prevented, had I not written that loathsome letter! Were I able to hold myself in such disgust, I would... but self-reproachment has long been purged from my psyche. The deed can only be undone by the actions I have since taken, and thus the issue matters little now. More important is that the act of living is once again bearable, even enjoyable, now that she is back by my side. The very night we were reunited, the rest of our time together until daybreak was spent in each other's arms, releasing the passion that had lingered so long with no avenue of escape within both of us... however many suitors attempted to be such for her... they will be dealt with later. Importance does not rest upon the wastes of time that cross our immortal paths for fleeting seconds, before their pathetic lives are snuffed out like a candle in the wind. I've begun to enjoy this ridiculous hobby that wrestling has proven itself to be. Watching my opponents twitch and shift nervously in my presence is simply comical, and none of them even know exactly why they are so timid in the first place. If only they knew what powers I suspend when I trifle with them. It is nothing, if not a simple diversion. The time for Global being my vehicle to re-unite with Shadow is past, and now, I know not exactly what I shall do with my remaining time. I suppose it is amusing to watch the posturing and taunting from the musclebound humans, attacking each other like bettas in a small bowl. There are interesting beings roaming about, however. Leinad Soma among them... I am not entirely certain how to perceive him. He is, obviously, a being of great power and importance, if his word is to be believed. It is difficult for me to reliably assess him. After all, while he does sustain himself with blood, he is not one of my kind. However, I shall bide my time as he is involved. Should he prove a useful ally, then I will scarcely deny him the opportunity. His history with Shadow is somewhat disconcerting, and he is still untrustworthy... if the worst should occur, we will simply come to vulgar combat. I must keep myself prepared, should that time actually come. There is little time left to chronicle my life, for now. Business concerns must take some consideration tonight... after my interrogation. I have invited Shadow to join me in the proceedings... certainly it shall be enjoyable. V. Ambrosi June 18th, 2005 -------- Virgil stood in the slightly cramped cargo compartment of the Jaedr, the small plane belonging to his only trusted pilot, Rune Hvalstr’m. He closed his journal, and slipped it quietly into the briefcase containing his other various belongings that traveled with him, and pressed on the heavy metal plate, pushing it up with relative ease. Rune barely noticed the noise, used to ferrying about the restless vampire reasonably often, when he wasn't running explosives, weapons, or other illegal provisions. Virgil's heavy boots fell against the hollowed steel floor, and he stepped into the cockpit, his tall frame somewhat cramped by the low ceiling. Rune glanced over at him, but didn't say anything at the moment, as he was still piloting the plane manually. Virgil sat down in the opposite chair, still managing to look elegant and sophisticated, despite the small quarters. He looked over at Rune, something which Rune wasn't exactly comfortable with, from him or Shadow, considering they had no need to blink. He felt as though they were peering straight into his mind... and didn't enjoy the thought. 'You look troubled, Rune.' Rune still didn't look at Virgil, concentrated on keeping to the flight path. 'I guess things haven't been going so well lately.' Virgil glanced out the window briefly, looking over the mountainous expanse of the forests beneath them. The moon glinted on a small river that ran through the area, and illuminated the surroundings with an ethereal glow. 'You are too talented to have business concerns... problems at home?' Rune nodded slightly. He was beginning to remember exactly why he felt like his mind was being rifled through whenever he talked to Shadow or Virgil... they did, after all, seem to always know what was going on in his personal life. 'You could say that.' Virgil smirked. He enjoyed this game of cat and mouse that he had to play to keep Rune from exploding from the tension his life generally brought him. He was too good of a pilot to lose to a mental hospital somewhere. 'Heidrun again, I would suppose.' 'Yeah.' Virgil raised an eyebrow. Rune's wife was one of the more loathsome and irritating women on the planet, if the previous things Rune had told him were any indication. Sleeping with his friends, turning his child against him, among other things... obviously not the most compelling reason to return home. 'Tell me, Rune... why do you continue to go back to her, knowing completely well that your life will be miserable for the duration of your stay?' Rune shrugged. 'I used to do it to see my daughter... now I'm not doing it anymore. Even if I can't see her, she won't have to listen to us fighting anymore. I've got the divorce papers signed and waiting to shove down her throat.' Virgil smiled, rather amused at Rune's caustic tone. 'Good. Upon escaping from it, I realized how short my life would have been, had I taken the same course as you.' Little else needed to be said, at the moment. Rune rarely allowed his home life to interfere with the only thing he enjoyed, and that event had been the only one weighing on his mind. He would see his daughter again, someday... perhaps when she was old enough to hear her father's side of the story. Right now, though, he would just keep working, dulling the pain the same way as he always had. ---------- The same day, a few hours later Seattle, Washington Warehouse #76 Alexis sat bolt upright and still in the small wooden chair she'd woken up in, remarkably, free to move. However, the room she was shut in wasn't exactly very conducive to escape. No windows, and one door out, with several bolts on the outside. Ventilation shafts dotted the ceiling, but they were too small to even consider climbing through. She was trapped... and the worst part was, she couldn't even remember exactly how she'd gotten here. The last thing she remembered was the scratchy brush grating on her nerves as she lay in the burned out vineyards behind Gideon de Medici's mansion. She remembered the sun still feeling warm against her back as she drifted off... and now she was here. There hadn't even been any noise outside... suddenly, the thought that she was going to die soon crept into her mind, and she started to panic, mostly influenced by the tiny, inescapable room. Her fears were simultaneously allayed, and multiplied in the span of a few seconds. The fear of slow starvation was suddenly replaced by the more lively fear of the large, orange tiger now occupying the room with her. Alexis screamed as it approached her, but it only laid down in the corner, eyeing her warily. She blinked at it for a moment. 'What...' The door opened fully now, and Virgil stepped into the room, no longer covered in his usual long coat, but still decked out in his usual business suit. After all, he had to look professional for an interrogation. Alexis pulled back into her chair, finding something extremely unnerving about this man. He wasn't a Den member... she would have recognized any of the group she trained and worked with. Virgil closed the door behind him, and she could hear the latches being reset. He took a couple of steps closer to her, and smirked as she squirmed in the chair. 'There's no need to scream at Muzhi over there... he's only accompanying us in case you don't tell me what I want to know.' Alexis' eyes widened. Was this what her parents saw before they died? Virgil narrowed his eyes at her. 'Tell me who you are.' Alexis swallowed hard, the threatening tone of her questioner making her lose her ability to think properly. 'I'm... Alexis... Key.' Virgil smiled, purposefully showing his fangs rather prominently. 'Do I make you nervous, Alexis?' She nodded. 'Why? Do you think I'm going to kill you?' Her eyes widened once again, and she pulled away from him. 'I... I don't know.' 'Good... I'll let you continue to contemplate that.' She managed to find a small bit of courage, somewhere inside. 'Who are you?' Virgil turned around to look at her, his dark blue unblinking eyes staring into her soul, much the same as he'd done to Rune a few hours ago. 'My name is Virgil Ambrosi.' She felt a slight twinge of relief. At least he was still a stranger, and she wasn't having to contemplate an immediate threat. Virgil continued staring at her, as he paced back and forth. 'So, Alexis... what were you doing at my mansion?' She looked at him confusedly. 'Your mansion? I thought it belonged to a vampire called Gideon de Medici...' Virgil's lips spread into a cruel smile, as he realized what he had suspected from the moment Mobius had come to report her rummaging around the mansion. 'Ah, yes... well, he and I are the same person, you see. I rarely allow too many people to know my real name. You are now one of the few.' She pushed back away from him, backing up against the wall. Muzhi stood up, and prepared to lunge, but Virgil held a hand out at him. 'No.' Muzhi relaxed into the corner again, and Virgil turned a piercing gaze back to Alexis. 'That was foolish. If I hadn't been here, he would have just disemboweled you... and I'm sure you don't want that.' She shook her head. 'You have nothing to fear from me, so long as you tell me everything I want to know... I will not repeat this again.' He stroked his chin furtively, as Alexis sank into the wall under Virgil's watch. 'Now then... what, pray, were you doing at my mansion?' She pondered not telling him... after all, she'd been sworn to secrecy about the Den and its workings, and most certainly its missions. But then again, they were trying to kill her at the moment. 'I was sent on an assignment... to hunt down and kill Gideon de Medici and Shadow. But I'm not really part of the Den anymore.' Virgil smirked coldly. 'Do you think that absolves you from all guilt? I rather liked that mansion.' She shuddered slightly, and glanced at Muzhi, who looked at her boredly from the corner. 'No...' He held up a finger. 'But, I will be much more forgiving if you will explain to me the nature of your assignment, and, perhaps most importantly... where my companion and I can find your former compatriots.' He grinned once again, at the thought of tracking them all down, and watching them die painfully at his feet, possibly with their facial features rearranged by Shadow's delightful abilities. 'I... I don't know.' Virgil growled, and Muzhi leapt from the corner, bridging the distance between them almost instantly, before raking a razor sharp set of claws across her chest. Alexis was aware of searing pain, and her head hitting the floor, slowly being surrounded by a pool of warm blood. Virgil snapped his fingers, and Alexis blinked, running her hand over her chest, and finding it completely intact. She sat up, the look on her face a mask of pure bewilderment. 'Lie to me again, and that may actually happen.' She shrunk away from him, but felt slightly more willing to give him the information he wanted... the Den would kill her either way, but Gideon... no, Virgil might actually spare her life... possibly. 'We were supposed to kill you both, obviously... but after the fire, we found that you had both escaped. After that... the hunt was ended. I don't know why... when I asked LaBatte about it, I suddenly started narrowly avoiding being killed, probably by them... and they're in Cleveland. They're too pompous to give up that hunting guild... I think Shadow found it.' Virgil nodded, rather interested in the drama of her story. Much like his casual interest with Rune's life, he was now rather intrigued by Alexis'. 'You fled from Cleveland... to the ruined mansion of a kindred, whom you tried to burn along with it?' She nodded, feeling slightly more comfortable around Virgil, for some reason. 'I wanted answers... I wanted to know why the hunt was called off, why we were chasing you in the first place... and I would have been killed anyway.' 'You are a brave woman, Alexis. Confronted with death on both sides, you plunged headlong into the more violent one... such boldness is something to be admired... but I cannot give you all of the answers myself. Perhaps, if you are willing to wait a few hours for Shadow to arrive...' Her eyes widened once more at the mention of Shadow's name... would she be as calm as Virgil? Virgil grinned. 'Ah, yes... you have little choice but to wait. However, since you were cooperative... I will have you moved to another room. You have earned a place to sleep, and perhaps some food as well.' She wasn't entirely sure what to say to him. Thanking him seemed rather... strange, considering he was still her captor, and had no intention of letting her go... and in fact, was probably exposing her to even greater danger. She wasn't left much time to contemplate, however, as the door opened, and Muzhi padded silently out of the room, giving one last, perhaps hungry, gaze at her. 'Thank you.' Virgil turned his head toward her slightly. 'I do not allow cooperative informants to die, Alexis. Do not thank me until Shadow has extended you the same courtesy.' Alexis' eyes widened once again, as the door slammed shut.
