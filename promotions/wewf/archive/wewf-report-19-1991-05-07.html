WeWF Report #19 5/7/91 DiMadMan Wins World Title! DiMadMan beat the General this week to win the WeWF World Championship! He also successfully defended his Africa/Australia Title, so it has been declared vacant, to be filled by tournament! The General beat Frank Steel at the Supercard and again this week, but lost to DiMadMan. DiMadMan held the Africa/Australia Title for a long time, but how long can he hold on to the world title? Next week he will defend against the General in a return match, the Re-Animator, "Irish" Mike Flint, his own partner, BudMan, and the Crusher! The Law Takes Tag Titles! Judge Dredd and Judge Anderson became full time tag team specialists, and their training paid off. They won the Tag Team Championship from Flint and Steel in their first defense, then defended the belt successfully for the other two matches. Supercard Results! There were a lot of matches at the Supercard, and here are the results for the big matches: The Big Kahuna beat the Romulan Mauler in the final round to win the Iron Man Tournament, the Four Corners and the Abattoir beat Sword, Shield, Axe, Chain and the Elementals, and in the main event the General beat Frank Steel! Tag Team Battle Royal! So far the following teams have registered for the Tag Team Battle Royal: The Intoxicators, the Highlanders, the Exterminators, the Law, the Steel Monkeys, the Siamese Twins, RnR, the Maniacs, Alpha and Omega, and the Hoosiers! Comments The Report is shrinking, mostly due to lack of comments! Use the comments space to rebut other wrestlers' TWIW quotes! I would prefer to see more comments and fewer matches, but I doubt that will happen, so I'm willing to settle for more comments! Errors Last week I left out one manager's comments, left Alpha and Omega off the rankings, and missed one manager's matches. This week I got the report out late, but hopefully I didn't make any other mistakes. Send me your matches/comments by 5/12/91! This Week's Matches World Title: The General survived Frank Steel.
