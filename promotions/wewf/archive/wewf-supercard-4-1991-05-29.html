WeWF Supercard #4: Summer Breakout

29 May 1991
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
For the first time I have decided to post the WeWF Supercard, mostly for EMWA and PWGeA wrestlers interested in scouting out the competion. If anyone objects, I won't post Supercards anymore, just weekly reports. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- WeWF Supercard #4: "Summer Breakout" May 29, 1991 The Meadowlands Baboon: "Hello everyone and welcome to the 4th WeWF Supercard! I'm Baboon Tempest and here's my co-host, Robby the Lame!" Robby: "Baboon, this place is going to erupt because we have some great matches scheduled! The Hoosiers vs. The Siamese Twins in a losers learn the language match, Slimm vs. Sour Krout, The Three Man Wrecking Crew vs. Capital Punishment, The Exterminators vs. Flint and Steel in a steel cage Match, The 007 Special vs. The Intoxicators, and in the Main Event, with the Americas' Title on the line, The Big Kahuna vs. The Re-Animator in a no DQ, no time limit match! Not to mention Bjorn "the Sweedish Pretzel" will be defending against the highest ranking contender for his belt!" Baboon: "How about the upcoming Summit of E-Wrestling, Robby?" Robby: "THAT I know about, Tempest. PWGeA greats are going to be taking on WeWF greats, and I'm going to be there!" Baboon: "Do you know what any of the matches are going to be, Lame?" Robby: "No, Baboon, I don't know yet. But you can bet the Four Corners will clean house!" Baboon: "It's going to be a happening! Let's go to the ring." The Hoosiers vs. The Siamese Twins in a Losers Learn the Language Match Baboon: "It's time for our first match, Lame. And it is a most interesting encounter, the Hoosiers against the Siamese Twins, and the losers must learn their opponents' language!" Robby: "Yeah, the Hoosiers are losers, but why should they try to learn Japanese? They still haven't mastered English!" Baboon: "Give me a break." Robby: "It's true! What do you expect from people from Dan Quayle's home state?" Baboon: "Don't let the General hear that, Lame. Let's get to the action." The Siamese Twins came down the aisle first, accompanied by Mitsubishi Zero. The fans went crazy when "Back Home Again in Indiana" was played over the speakers and the ring announcer shouted out: "Being lead to the ring by their manager, three time former Heavyweight Champion of the World, the General, at a total combined weight of 497 lbs., the dynasty of the 90's, here are Erik and the Daemon, the Hoosiers!" The Siamese Twins attacked before the bell with chops and karate kicks, but Erik slammed Yin and Daemon brought Yang down with a headscissors. Yin and Yang both rolled out of the ring to regroup, but the General met them on the outside and rammed their heads together! He rolled them back into the ring, and when they got back to their feet, Daemon monkey flipped Yin across the ring and Erik clotheslined Yang over the top rope! Yin stayed in the ring and raked Daemon's face, then hit him with a stinging reverse knife edge. He bounced off the ropes and hit Daemon with a jump kick, then swept his legs. Yin covered, but Daemon got out at the one count. Yin wore his opponent down with a reverse chinlock, then tagged out to Yang. Yang bounced off the ropes and clotheslined Daemon down, then went for an elbow drop, but Daemon rolled away! Daemon dropkicked Yang, who fell back against the ropes, then tagged out to Erik. Erik put a hammerlock on Yang, then changed it to a flying hammerlock. Yang howled in pain and Erik followed it up with a shoulder breaker! Erik brought Yang into his corner and tagged out to Daemon. Erik slammed Yang, and Daemon dropped a knee on Yang, then tagged back out to Erik. Erik came in via a top rope legdrop onto Yang. Erik went for the pin, but Mitsubishi Zero put Yang's foot on the ropes, and the referee stopped the count. The General complained that Zero had interfered in the match, but Zero denied it and the match continued. The General vented his frustration by throwing a chair at Zero, then chasing him back to the dressing room! Erik tagged Daemon back in, and after they double clotheslined Yang, he whipped Yang into a corner. Daemon went for a suplex, but Yin pulled his leg out from under him, and Yang fell on him. Daemon escaped at the two count. Yang rolled out of the ring, then back in. Daemon whipped him off the ropes, but Yang seemed to have miraculously recovered, and he hit Daemon with a body tackle. Erik came into the ring to protest, but the referee stopped him. Just then, the General came back to the ring and DDT'd the real Yang outside the ring. Yin, on the inside, stopped to shout at the General, and Erik put him in a full nelson. Daemon lifted Yin's legs up for extra pressure, and Yin submitted. After the match, the General gave both Siamese Twins copies of "Learning English, in Only 10 Minutes a Day". Robby: "How about that comeback by Yang, huh?" Baboon: "Be serious, Lame. The tried to cheat, but it didn't pay off. Now they must learn English or be banned from the WeWF!" Robby: "Don't worry, they already know English. I'm sure Mitsubishi Zero taught them words like Panasonic, Suzuki, and Kawasaki! Slimm vs. Sour Kraut Robby: "Here's a matchup I can predict with 99.9 % accuracy, Baboon, Slimm the blob vs. top contender Sour Krout. Now, Slimm standing up or lying down is almost as tall as Sour Krout, but he can't compete in strength." Baboon: "You really think Sour Krout can push Slimm around the ring? It's hard to get that much mass moving, Robby. Sour Krout won the last time, but Slimm has been eating - I mean training hard for this one!" Slimm walks to the ring first, his trademark wheelbarrow full of food in front of him as "Don't go messin' with a Country Boy" by Hillbilly Jim plays over the loud speakers. Baboon: "Listen to these fans go wild! They love this guy!" Robby: "Yeah and there sure is enough of him to love too." Baboon: "Hey, you're no Ghandi yourself ya know." Robby: "What? Oh look, here come Sour Krout and his manager The Red Baron." The Red Baron leads Sour Kraut to the ring while "Rock You Likw a Hurricane" by the Scorpions blasts through the arena, just above the chorus of boos from the crowd. The ref checks both men for international objects and the match is underway. Sour Krout and Slimm lock up in the middle of the ring and Slimm hurls his opponent into the far turnbuckle. Sour Kraut rolls out of the ring to confer with The Baron. Baboon: "What a show of strength by Slimm!" Robby: "Strength? The only thing strong about Slimm is his odor." Baboon: "Then what would you call it?" Robby: "He put his weight behind it. All 649lbs of it." Sour Kraut slips back into the ring and locks up with Slimm again. Sour Kraut delivers a knee to the stomach, then he rakes Slimm's eyes but Slimm seems unphased. Robby: "What great technical wrestling by Sour Kraut!" Baboon: "Yeah, technically illegal." Slimm gets Sour Kraut in a headlock and whips him to the ropes, delivering a beautiful clothesline. He scoops Sour Kraut and a punishing body slam. Sour Kraut rolls out of the ring again and the crowd goes wild! Baboon: "We could be looking at an upset in the making here Robby" Robby: "Yeah I know. Slimm is making me sick to my stomach too." Baboon: "Hey what's The Red Baron doing on the apron?" Robby: "He's complaining about the crummy officiating. What's it look like he's doing?" Baboon: "Looks like he's distracting the referee to me. What's that Sour Kraut has in his hands?" Sour Kraut sneaks back into the ring and as the Red Baron and the referee argue, he pearl harbors Slimm with a steel chair. Slimm falls against the ropes and Sour Kraut tosses the chair out of the ring just as the referee turns around. Slimm is still dazed and Sour Kraut delivers a series of rights and lefts, then kicks to the stomach, but Slimm won't fall. He hits Slimm with a clothesline, and then another as Slimm begins to look a little wobbly. Sour Kraut to the second rope. Flying clothesline! Slimm staggers and slips to one knee, but won't go down. Robby: "I guess it's true." Baboon: "What?" Robby: "Weebles Wobble but they won't fall down." Baboon: "Oh please! That's enough!" Sour Kraut up to the top rope this time, but Slimm catches him and delivers a earthshaking powerslam. One! Two! Sour Kraut manages to get a foot up on the ropes. Slimm is staggering to his feet, but Sour Kraut has one of his legs! He can't get his balance and falls to the mat crushing the referee underneath him. Slimm rolls off the ref but he's out cold. Baboon: "Now what?" Robby: "Well I think that fat slob should be..." Baboon: (interrupts) "Wait! What's this?" The Hit 'n Run Rabbi runs to ringside in full referee attire. He gets Slimm and Sour Kraut into neutral corners just in time for The Red Baron to hammer Slimm in the back of the head with the steel chair but the Rabbi seemed too busy looking for something in his pocket to notice. He finds what he was looking for and pulls out... a pair of brass knuckles! The fans watch in shock as the Rabbi delivers a right to Slimm's forehead. Then another. And another. Slimm is bleeding severely from the head. Sour Kraut is on the top rope and his flying clothesline sends Slimm to the floor hard. The real referee seems to be reviving and recovers just in time to give Sour Kraut the easy three count as the Rabbi makes a hasty retreat back to the locker room area, followed close behind by Sour Kraut and the Baron. Slimm remains unmoving in the center of the ring. Robby: "Well I guess we know why he's called the "Hit 'n Run" Rabbi now, eh Tempest?" Baboon: "I wanna know how he could turn his backs on his fans like that? That's unbelievable!" Robby: "All I wanna know is how they're gonna get that beached whale out of the ring before the next match..." Capital Punishment vs. The Three Man Wrecking Crew Baboon: "A six man match, Lame, and you have to give this one to the team with the two time tag team champions, the Law." Robby: "They are two time champs, Baboon, but I didn't know you could count that high! Still, I think it's a toss up. The Maniacs have perfected the cheapshot, just remember how they won the titles at the first Supercard!" Baboon: "I remember, they threw powder in the face of the Leaping Lords! I hope the referee can keep control of them this time. Let's go to Green Jean." Green Jean: "Any comments before this match guys?" Dredd: "Yeah, just one. Maniacs, judgement day is at hand. You can run but you can't hide." The Crusher and the Maniacs get to the ring first. A little old lady at ringside climbs into the ring and starts screaming at them. Simon Bolendorf is about to slap her when Capital Punishment comes running to ringside. The Maniacs and the Crusher all hop out of the ring, but the Grim Reaper manages to catch Simon Bolendorf in the sleeper! The Three man wrecking crew wait, stunned, and finally grab chairs and clean house in the ring. The match finally starts with the Crusher and the Grim Reaper. The Grim Reaper goes to pick the Crusher up, but the Crusher blocks it. The Crusher then picks up the Reaper, and with great effort, body slams him. He turns his back, and starts posing for the crowd. (Greeted with boos, of course) The Grim Reaper stands up and clotheslines the Crusher from behind. He goes to work punching, kicking, and then choking the downed Crusher. He then tags out to Judge Anderson. Judge Anderson throws him into the ropes and then dropkicks him out of the ring. Judge Dredd meets the Crusher outside the ring with a running clothesline. Then he throws the Crusher back into the ring. The Crusher swings at Judge Anderson, but the Judge ducks, and then atomic knee drops the Crusher back into his own corner. Maniac #1 tags in, and immediately puts Anderson in a headlock. He grinds it a bit, but Judge Anderson thows him into the ropes. Anderson then bounces over the opposite ropes, but Maniac #2 pulls the rope down, and Anderson goes sailing over the top. Maniac #2 then picks up the downed Anderson and drops him on his knee. The referee's count gets up to 9 before Anderson can muster the strength to get back into the ring. Maniac #1 charges him, but Anderson crawls between his legs, and makes it to the corner to tag in Judge Dredd. Dredd picks up Maniac #1 and powerslams him. Simon Bolendorf gets on the ring apron, and starts distracting the ref. The Crusher comes in, but Judge Dredd powerslams him too. Maniac #2 comes in, and he takes some pouder out of his trunks! He goes to throw it at Judge Dredd, but Machiavelli screams at Dredd. Dredd turns to look, and the powder flies past him, and into Simon Bolendorf's eyes. Dredd running clotheslines Maniac #2 over the top rope. The Judge then clotheslines the Crusher over the top rope too, and then starts trading punches with Maniac #1. Outside of the ring, Machiavelli goes after Simon Bolendorf, but Simon hits him with the ring bell! He fails to see the Grim Reaper sneak up behind him, and put him in the sleeper. Maniac #2 and the Crusher are too tired to help, and Maniac #1 is losing the slugfest in the middle of the ring. It's only a short matter of time before Bolendorf is unconscious, and on the inside of a coffin. The Grim Reaper carts him off out of the Arena for a propper burial. It's 3 on 2 inside the ring, however, and after a viscious double dropkick, it's all of the Three Man Wrecking Crew on Judge Dredd. The three wrestlers all take their turns giving Judge Dredd the DDT, when the Crusher puts him up on his shoulders. Both Maniacs climb to the top turnbuckle, and give Judge Dredd a double Clothesline! Dredd is unconscious in the center of the ring. Crusher goes for the cover. 1-2- the Crusher pulls Judge Dredd's shoulders up! He's looking to dish out more punishment. He tags Maniac #1, and puts Dredd in a bearhug, as Maniac #2 climbs to the top rope. Maniac #1 bounces off the ropes and clotheslines Dredd. Maniac #2 then does a leap onto Judge Dredd that would make Jimmy "Superfly" Snuka jealous. Machiavelli is very nervous, so he hands Judge Anderson some brass knuckles and then goes to distract the ref. Judge Anderson charges the ring and decks the Crusher, ducks a clothesline from Maniac #2, and decks him too. He then hides the knuckles, as the ref escorts him outside of the ring. Maniac #1 goes to cover Judge Dredd. 1-2-2 and a half- 2 and three quarters-Dredd kicks out! Maniac #1 looks at his hands! The crowd starts chanting "You're no Ultimate Warrior" as Judge Dredd rolls over to his corner and tags the Grim Reaper, who just returned to ringside from the locker room. The Reaper proceeds to grab Maniac #1 by the throat and lifts him several feet off the ground. Maniac #2, just awakening, runs into the ring and slams the Reaper from behind.The Reaper drops Maniac #1 and lifts Maniac #2 by the throat. The Crusher goes to the top rope to try something, but the Reaper drop Maniac #2, and gives the Crusher a shot to the gunsack. The Crusher lets out a high pitched yelp, and the Reaper throws him across the ring like a sack of potatoes. The Reaper then tags Judge Anderson who quickly climbs up the top rope and plants a devastating flying dropkick on the Crusher. Maniac #2 climbs to the top rope and gets poised for a sneak attack. Meanwhile Judge Anderson puts the Crusher in a headlock. Crusher throws him into the ropes, but the swinging of the ropes causes Maniac #2 to fall and catch the family jewelbox on the top rope. Crusher meanwhile goes to backbody drop Judge Anderson, but Judge Anderson takes the opportunity and executes a devestating neckbreaker on the Crusher. He takes advange of the momentum lands a picture-perfect dropkick on Maniac #1, who falls off the ring apron. He then tags in Judge Dredd, and the tag champs of the world then do a spike piledriver on the Crusher. They tag in the Grim Reaper, who puts the lifeless Crusher in a sleeper. It's just a formality for the referee to lift and drop the Crusher's arm 3 times. The Maniacs charge the ring, and the Judges are on them in a second. The melee continues as both teams fight on the ring floor, and then back to the dressing room. Machiavelli looks at the camera, asks "Anybody got a spare coffin?" Seeing none, the Reaper grabs the Crusher, hoists him over his shoulder and exits the ring area. Baboon: "Capital Punishment beats the Three Man Wrecking Crew!" Robby: "Yeah, but what's happening to the Crusher? This is inhuman! The referee should be stopping this!" Baboon: "The match is over, he can do whatever he likes! Didn't you condone burying Sasquatch at the last Supercard?" Robby: "Sure, but that was Sasquatch, not a top wrestler like the Crusher! Well hopefully this will just make the Crusher meaner! Reaper, watch out!" Flint and Steel vs. The Exterminators in a Steel Cage Match Baboon: "In a 15 foot high steel cage, two of the greatest wrestlers of all time taking on two of the most sadistic!" Robby: "Where do Flint and Steel fit in? The Exterminators are two of the greatest of all time, and they're two of the most sadistic wrestlers I've ever met. Just ask the Hit 'N' Run Rabbi!" Baboon: "The Exterminators are a good team, but they aren't better than Flint and Steel." Robby: "Right. Flint and Steel both want Flint's World Title, and I think that is going to split up their team sooner or later!" The Exterminators got in the cage first, and shook the walls to test if they were solid. The Butcher wanted to be safe for the Re-Animator's match, so he returned to the dressing room. The boos and catcalls from the fans were defeaning, until Flint and Steel came to the ring with Molly Maguire and the crowd cheered like crazy. They threw their Union jackets and tools at the timekeeper, and climbed into the cage. The referee put a lock on the door, and the Exterminators tried to get out of the cage quickly by climbing over the walls, but World Champion "Irish" Mike Flint grabbed the Law-Maker's foot and pulled him off the cage, while Frank Steel slammed the Enforcer face first into the cage wall on the other side of the ring! Both teams began tearing at each other tooth and nail, and the Exterminators seemed to be getting the better of it. The Enforcer threw Flint into the cage, and Law-Maker atomic dropped Steel. Steel turned around, and slammed Law-Maker into the mat face first, and then picked him up for a piledriver, but the Enforcer knocked him down before he could complete the move. Flint bounced off the ropes and shoulderblocked the Enforcer, then put a step-over toe hold on him, the first technical move of the match! Law-Maker broke the hold by slugging Flint, but he was axehandled from behind by Steel. This general mayhem continued for a few minutes, and both teams were bleeding. The Butcher came back to ringside with the Savage and the Re-Animator, and the two wrestlers began menacing Molly Maguire. Savage went around one side of the cage, Re-Animator went around the other, and Molly was caught in the middle. The Butcher, though, kept his distance. Flint and Steel were distracted by this unsportsmanlike conduct, and they were easily blindsided by the Exterminators. The Enforcer bloodied "Irish" Mike Flint with a roll of dimes, then went to help the Law-Maker with Steel. They gave Steel a double stomach breaker, then a double elbow drop, and Enforcer tried to climb out of the cage. Steel got back to his knees and rammed a shoulder into the Law-Maker's midsection. Flint grabbed Enforcer by the trunks just before he got over the cage wall, and yanked him off the cage. Flint crawled to the door and signaled for the referee to open it, but the Savage held it shut. While the Savage was holding the door, Molly Maguire ran past him back to the dressing room area. The Re-Animator handed brass knuckles to the Law-Maker, and he hit Flint in the lower back with them. Flint dropped to his knees and the Law-Maker put a chinlock on him, then punched him in the face with the brass knuckles. Steel recovered the roll of dimes, and slugged the Law-Maker. The Enforcer tried to climb out of the cage, but Steel climbed up the cage, met him at the top, and choked him on the top edge of the cage! It looked like Steel might get out of the cage, but the Re-Animator shook the wall and Steel and the Enforcer fell back into the ring. Flint and the Law-Maker exchanged rights and lefts, and then Flint put Law-Maker in a headlock. Law-Maker pushed Flint to the ropes, but the World Champion held onto Law-Maker, and they both collided with the cage! For a few minutes none of the wrestlers moved. Frank Steel was the first to get up, but he was hurt too badly to climb out of the cage. He motioned for the door to be opened, but the Savage was still holding it shut. Just then Molly Maguire returned to ringside with the Neutralizer! The Neutralizer grabbed a chair and chased the Savage away from the cage door. The referee opened the door, and Steel crawled out for the win. Robby: "Hah, Flint and Steel will never wrestle again after that beating!" Baboon: "The Exterminators really proved that they can handle wrestling with the big boys, but they don't look any better off than their opponents! Call an ambulance, those guys won't be leaving the ring under their own power!" Robby: "Flint and Steel are just lucky the Neutralizer was there!" The 007 Special vs. The Intoxciators Baboon: "And here's a feud that began at the last Supercard when Mr. B ran in and attacked BudMan after the big Triple Dome of Terror match!" Robby: "Remember what happened to Dr. Down-Under during that match? Well let's just say it was an ELECTRIFYING experience, and I will be SHOCKED if his team beat the Intoxicators!" Baboon: "WATT? Have you been keeping up on CURRENT events? The 007 Special has come a long way, and I wouldn't be surprised if they did beat the Intoxicators!" Robby: "The 007 Special are tolerable cheapshot artists, but the Intoxicators are the Michaelangelo's of sneak attacks!" Baboon: "You mean they like pizza?" Robby: "Mutoid... Before we go to the match, let's hear some enlightening comments from BudMan." BudMan: "Arrrrgh Arr!" Green Jean: "Huh?" Smooth: "What he said was: Nothin' beats a Bud! Man! Come on BudMan, let's go." Zoo by the Scorpions came blaring over the loudspeakers as the Intoxicators and Smooth made their way to the ring. They laughed at the tremendous boos they received from the crowd. Then the 007 Special came down the aisle with Dr. Down-Under, who was waving his TASER around, and Action Man and "Crusher" Cootus to "007 Shanty Town" by Desmond Dekker. The two teams started off the match quickly, with DiMadMan and Mr. B in the ring first. Mr. B reversed a wristlock by DiMadMan, and forced him down to his knees. DiMadMan got out of it with a face rake, then fireman's carried Mr. B down and re-applied the wristlock. Tom "the Animal" Brown rallied the crowd behind his team, and Mr. B responded to their cheers by powering up and throwing DiMadMan to the mat by his hair. DiMadMan tagged out to BudMan, who came in cautiouly. Mr. B bounced off the ropes, but Smooth grabbed him by the ankle and tripped him. BudMan dropped an elbow across Mr. B's back and tagged out to DiMadMan. BudMan put Mr. B in a front facelock, and DiMadMan jumped off the top rope with a double axehandle into Mr. B's back. DiMadMan chicken winged his opponent, and dropped a knee on Mr. B's arm. He whipped Mr. B into a corner and charged in, but Mr. B put his foot up! DiMadMan showed his ring savvy by stopping himself before he hit Mr. B's foot, and yanked Mr. B out of the corner. He scooped Mr. B up for a slam, but the wily Mr. B went over DiMadMan's back and tagged out to Tom Brown. The Animal charged in and leveled DiMadMan with a clothesline, then hit BudMan with a hard right hand, knocking him off of the apron. DiMadMan got back up and the Animal suplexed him, and rolled over for a quick cover, but DiMadMan got out. The Animal body slammed his opponent and then tagged Mr. B. The two whipped DiMadMan off the ropes, Tom Brown punched DiMadMan in the gut, and Mr. B followed it with a knee lift that made DiMadMan almost do a 360! DiMadMan crawled to his corner and tagged out to BudMan. BudMan pulled Mr. B into his corner by the hair and rammed his head into the turnbuckle. He picked Mr. B up, and draped him in the corner. The Intoxicators then punched Mr. B in the midsection until he fell to the mat. DiMadMan tagged in, and piledrivered Mr. B. He went for the pin but didn't hook Mr. B's leg, and Mr. B got out. The fans got behind Mr. B and he got to his feet, only to go back down when DiMadMan clotheslined him. DiMadMan tagged BudMan, who had slipped something into his mask. DiMadMan held Mr. B, and BudMan hit him with a thunderous headbutt that KO'd Mr. B. He went for the cover, but stopped when he saw Action Man and "Crusher" Cootus trying to get into the ring. While the referee went to stop them from interfering in the match, BudMan choked Mr. B. Dr. Down-Under zapped Mr. B with his TASER, but that temporarily paralyzed BudMan and he was still choking Mr. B! Tom "the Animal" Brown charged in, but DiMadMan blocked him off. BudMan's muscles finally relaxed and he slumped off of Mr. B, who was gasping for air. Smooth tried to get into the ring, but Dr. Down-Under got him with the TASER! The referee tried to restore order, and he succeeded in getting DiMadMan and Tom "the Animal" Brown out of the ring, but the Plaid Powerhouse ran to the ring and rolled BudMan onto Mr. B! The referee turned around, but the Plaid Powerhouse was already out of the ring. The referee counted the pin, and the Intoxicators won the match. Robby: "Hey, a clean win for the Intoxicators for a change!" Baboon: "That was hardly a clean win, Lame. You saw the Plaid Powerhouse run in!" Robby: "Yeah, but for one of their matches, it was clean!" Baboon: "What's going on now?" Robby: "I love it! DiMadMan is making Mr. B admit that American beer is better!" Baboon: "But "Crusher" Cootus grabs a chair and cleans house! That was despicable by DiMadMan!" Bjorn "the Sweedish Pretzel" vs. ??? Baboon: "So who is Bjorn's opponent, Lame?" Robby: "No one, he won't show up!" Bjorn entered the ring first, accompanied by Smooth. The ring announcer called out: "And now, his opponent..." followed by a long pause, "the four time Africa/Australia Champion, one half of the winning team in the Tag Team Battle Royal, Mr. Roddy!" Mr. Roddy came to the ring without Keith Skar and got a favorable reaction from the fans, mostly because they hated Bjorn more. He tried to climb into the ring, but Bjorn pushed him off the apron every time he tried to get in. Mr. Roddy grabbed his opponent's legs and dragged him out of the ring, then slammed him face first into the ring apron! Roddy then kicked Bjorn in the gut, clotheslined him, and then rolled him back into the ring. When Mr. Roddy tried to get into the ring Smooth nailed him from behind with a chair! Bjorn took the opportunity to suplex Mr. Roddy into the ring, then fistdrop him. He signaled for the Sweedish Pretzel figure four, but Mr. Roddy crawled to the ropes before he could slap it on. Mr. Roddy used the ropes to help himself back to his feet, but Smooth hit him with the chair again! Bjorn dropped an elbow on his foe and suplexed him again. He covered Mr. Roddy, but pulled his shoulder up at the two count! He whipped Mr. Roddy to the ropes and powerslammed him, then argued with the referee for no apparent reason. The reason became apparent when Smooth choked Mr. Roddy over the bottom rope, while the ref's back was turned. Smooth turned to go back to Bjorn's corner, but Lord Roderick grabbed him and hit him with a punch that left Smooth's head spinning! Bjorn stopped the match to hurl insults at Lord Roderick, but meanwhile Mr. Roddy was recovering, and he atomic dropped Bjorn! Mr. Roddy then caught Bjorn with an elbow to the head and whipped him off the ropes into a sleeper! Bjorn countered by grabbing Mr. Roddy by the hair and dropping down, almost breaking Mr. Roddy's jaw! Bjorn got back to his feet, but Mr. Roddy surprised him with a small package. Before the referee could even count to one Bjorn had reversed the move pinned Mr. Roddy. After the match, Lord Roderick got into the ring and hit Bjorn with a chair. Bjorn rolled out of the ring before any further damage could be done and DiMadMan, BudMan and the Plaid Powerhouse ran down to the ring before any more damage could be done. The Millionaire ran down the aisle and smacked BudMan in the head with the Eurasia belt, while Keith Skar jumped out of the second row and threw a fireball into the Plaid Powerhouse's face! RnR jumped out of the ring and brawled with the Four Corners some more. The Corners helped Smooth back to his feet and beat a hasty retreat back to the dressing room area! Baboon: "Well it looks like RnR have avenged themselves for what took place after the tag team battle royal! The Four Corners finally got what they deserved!" Robby: "Bjorn still has his belt, and it's just a matter of time until BudMan destroys that usurper the Millionaire, and gets the Eurasia belt back around his waist!" The Big Kahuna vs. The Re-Animator in a No DQ, No Time Limit Match Baboon: "This feud has been going on for a long time, Lame. It all began when the Abattoir viciously attacked the Surfer Alliance in the Meat Locker and put Malibu out of wrestling!" Robby: "They put him out of wrestling, and now it's time for the Re-Animator to put The Big Kahuna out of wrestling." Baboon: "I don't think he can, Lame.
