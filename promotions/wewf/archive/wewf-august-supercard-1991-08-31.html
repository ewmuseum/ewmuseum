WeWF August Supercard: Gold Belts and Iron Men

31 August 1991
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WeWF August Supercard: "Gold Belts and Iron Men" The UIC Pavillion in Chicago, Illinois Saturday, August 31 Baboon: "I'm Baboon Tempest and it's time for the 7th WeWF Supercard! Joining me at ringside is my co-host, Wince McLady!" Wince: "We'll be bringing you all the action, so stay right there!" Baboon: "There are some great matches signed for this, the last official WeWF Summer event. One of them is the Iron Man Tournament, where defending Iron Man The Big Kahuna, The Executioner, The Plaid Powerhouse, and Photon Torpedo will go at it. Each wrestler will face everyone else once." Wince: "The winner for this event will be determined by their win-loss record in the tournament, unless three wrestlers come out 2-1." Baboon: "Also we've got a big six-man match, The General and his proteges The Hoosiers will take on Bjorn "The Swedish Pretzel" and The Law inside a steel cage!" Wince: "That should be a great match. The Law and The Hoosiers both held the tag team belts during the summer, and the team that wins this match will get the first crack at the belts on Sunday!" Baboon: "But who will they face? That's something we'll find out later on, when World Champion Mr. Roddy and his partner The Millionaire take on World Tag Team Champions "Irish" Mike Flint and Frank Steel!" Wince: "If anyone can take the belts from Flint and Steel it's going to be RnR (or was that RnM?). They are the only team to pin Flint and Steel this summer, and they did it twice!" Baboon: "And this time the belts will be up for grabs. And speaking of belts, Mr. B has agreed to put his US Title on the line against The Red Baron. You can't deny that Mr. B is a fighting champion!" Wince: "The Red Baron should be a good test for him, though. He's really made waves in his first few weeks in the WeWF." Baboon: "Speaking of making waves, The Secret Agent Man certainly did that when he exposed The Law's unlawful activities! Their friend The Grim Reaper will try to close S.A.M's mouth for good." Wince: "The Grim Reaper put The Killer Whale out of action for good this past week. I wonder how the Agent will fare considering his career-threatening knee injury incurred at the hands of The Law?" Baboon: "We'll have to wait and see. How about the chilli injury suffered by The Wyld Stallyons at the hands of The Mexican Delights? They'll have their chance for revenge in a no holds barred match!" Wince: "Let's go to ringside!" The Wyld Stallyons vs. The Mexican Delights in a No Holds Barred Match Baboon: "We're starting it off with two hot new teams in the WeWF. This match was prompted by The Mexican Delights unwarranted attack two weeks ago on The Stallyons!" Wince: "No one has seen The Stallyons in the building, Baboon. Green Jean Oktoberfest is standing by with their manager Rufus." Green Jean: "Rufus, nobody has seen The Wyld Stallyons here tonight? Where are they?" Rufus: "My boys will be here. Relax, they'll make it... I hope." Baboon: "I wonder where they are? Their opponents are standing by." Nabilio Tortilla: "We got the chilli powder... come and get us..." The Nacho Kid: "Wyld Stale loins, if you feel too intimidated, if you want to back out, we give you a deal! You pull the no-show, we call it a draw! But if you come to the ring, we have the chilli powder, we the other tricks, and it will be Adios, Senors!!" Wince: "Do you think The Stallyons would go along with that? This is one confusing situation!" Baboon: "The Wyld Stallyons may have chickened out, Wince. Meanwhile, The Delights are on their way to the ring!" Wince: "The announcer just called for The Stallyons, and they're getting a lot of cheering, but I don't see them anywhere. He's announcing them again, but where are they?" Baboon: "Uh-oh. They only have 30 seconds left, or else they forfeit the match." Wince: "I hope they haven't chickened out... Just ten seconds left. Nine, eight, seven, six, five, four... hey, what happened to the lights?" Voice: "And now... appearing at their first supercard... here are Bill S Preston esquire and Ted "Theodore" Logan... The WYYYYYLLLLDDDDDDD STALLYONS!!!" Baboon: "Spotlights are shining down on the runway, and here come Bill and Ted, they're sprinting to the ring!" Wince: "That's 'Fight like a Brave' by the Red Hot Chilli Peppers playing in the background, and Bill and Ted are Red Hot all right." Baboon: "All four wrestlers are going at it already! We've got a pier six!" Wince: "Bill clotheslines Nabilio, and Ted slams The Nacho Kid! The Mexican Delights are taking cover outside the ring!" Baboon: "Bill and Ted follow them out, Ted taking on Nabilio, and Bill locking horns with The Nacho Kid! Tacoglio Madridos, The Delights' manager, just clocked Bill with a chair! Now he hits Ted!" Wince: "It's a good thing there's no DQ in this match, otherwise that would have cost them the match." Baboon: "Rufus just grabbed a broom from under the ring, and he breaks it over Tacoglio's head! That should keep him out of the action!" Wince: "But The Nacho Kid just leveled Rufus with a haymaker! Both managers are out of it!" Baboon: "Ted lunges at Nabilio, and they tumble over the barricade into the stands! They're still brawling! People are running for cover!" Wince: "The Nacho Kid is going for a bulldog on Bill, but Bill pushes him, sending The Kid into the ring pole!" Baboon: "Bill rams Kid into the pole! But The Kid grabs the ring bell and rings Bill's bell!" Wince: "Nabilio Tortilla just grabbed a chair, and he and Ted are fighting for control over it! Tortilla rams it down on Ted, but Ted doesn't let go of it! He slams it on Nabilio's head! Both wrestlers drop the chair, and Nabilio Tortilla rakes Ted's face!" Baboon: "The Nacho Kid swings the bell again, but Bill rolls under the ring!" Wince: "The Kid follows him! We've got action under the ring, we've got action in the stands, and the referee is dumbfounded! He never expected this!" Baboon: "In the stands, Nabilio Tortilla is chasing Ted up the stairs with a monkey wrench! He hits Ted in the back! Ted goes down, and Tortilla goes to work on him!" Wince: "Meanwhile, Bill crawls out from under the ring dragging a bloody Nacho Kid! Bill rams The Kid face first to the ring barrier! Then he rolls him back into the ring!" Baboon: "A bloody Ted lashes out with a kick... and knocks Nabilio Tortilla off balance! Tortilla rolls down the arena stairs, and ends up battered, bruised, bloody, and back at ringside!" Wince: "Bill DDT's The Nacho Kid! The referee finally has a something to do! One, two, three!" Baboon: "The Wyld Stallyons win a wild match! Ted is being helped back to the ring, he took some vicious shots with that monkey wrench. It looks like he, Nabilio Tortilla, The Nacho Kid, and both managers will be stretchered out!" Wince: "A big win for The Wyld Stallyons!" Iron Man Tournament, Round 1 The Big Kahuna vs. The Executioner Photon Torpedo vs. The Plaid Powerhouse Baboon: "There you have it, the match-ups for the first round. Our first match will be T.B. Kahuna, who won the last Iron Man Tournament, against The Executioner." Wince: "T.B. was a rookie when he won that tournament, and I think it did a lot for him. He's held the World Title twice now. The Executioner may get the same opportunity right here." Baboon: "We've got comments from both wrestlers. They're looking forward to quite a fight!" The Executioner: "My fellow ironman entrants... fight your battles, little men... struggle for glory... for when you face me the blade will fall and I alone shall stand... victorious..." Green Jean: "T.B. you certainly do look fired up for the upcoming tournament, and I'm glad to see you remembered that it was wrestling, and not a tri-athalon this time." T.B.: "What?!? Like I knew I forgot something. Most radical, now I can get out of these bogus bikini shorts. They're giving me a most uncool wedgie." Green Jean: "You mean... never mind, back to you Wince." Baboon: "The Executioner is walking down the aisle. A lot of people are afraid of this guy, Wince." Wince: "They should be, he's off to a very good start in the WeWF. And here comes his opponent, The Big Kahuna, accompanied by Val Lee." Baboon: "Val is grossed out by The Executioner! That's certainly an advantage for him." Wince: "The Big Kahuna doesn't seem to be bothered, he's hammering away on The Executioner!" Baboon: "The Executioner puts a claw on The Kahuna, but I think it's a little early. It's having some effect... and T.B. grabs the ropes. But that leaves him wide open to a shot from The Executioner!" Wince: "Now The Executioner is going to work through agressive moves, but T.B. comes back with a headbutt to the midsection. And he backdrops the big man!" Baboon: "The Kahuna presses his advantage and drops an elbow on The Executioner. The Executioner gets up, and The Kahuna whips him to the ropes..." Wince: "Clothesline by The Executioner! It looked like he tried to take The Kahuna's head off with that move." Baboon: "Now The Executioner throws T.B. out of the ring! That's probably a good tactic." Wince: "The Big Kahuna gets back into the ring, and he forearms The Executioner! Irish whip by T.B... and the wipeout powerslam!" Baboon: "One, two, three! It's over! Chalk one up for The Big Kahuna!" Wince: "Now it's time for our second match of round one, the high-flying Photon Torpedo against The Plaid Powerhouse." Baboon: "I think Photon was always better at singles wrestling than tag team, but double P has always been good at both." Wince: "Here comes The Plaid Powerhouse, and that slime of a manager, Victor Rivera Jr. Listen to the response for his opponent! Photon Torpedo and Admiral Chekov are beaming to the ring!" Baboon: "Whoah, Chekov had to duck for cover because the match got underway quickly! The Powerhouse grabs Photon Torpedo by the throat!" Wince: "Come on ref, get in there! Double P whips Torpedo off the ropes, and Torpedo cartwheels away! What an acrobat! He jumps up and hits The Powerhouse with a dropkick, knocking him out of the ring!" Baboon: "P.T. slingshots himself out of the ring onto The Powerhouse! This could be an upset in the making!" Wince: "Hey, Victor Rivera Jr. just blew smoke in Torpedo's face! Photon Torpedo can't see!" Baboon: "Starfleet medical personnel beam down and fix his eyes, but not before Double P lands a chair into the Torpedo's back!" Wince: "Torpedo tries to roll back into the ring, but The Plaid Powerhouse won't let him. The Powerhouse winds up for another swing of the chair, but Torpedo rolls away! He monkey flips The Plaid one onto the steel barricade!" Baboon: "But Victor Rivera is in the way again, raking Photon's back! That was a mistake! Photon presses the manager and slams him down on the concrete! But meanwhile The Plaid Powerhouse rolls back into the ring!" Wince: "Photon didn't hear the ref counting, and he gets counted out! That means Double P gets the win, tying him with T.B. Kahuna at one apiece." The Secret Agent Man vs. The Grim Reaper Baboon: "This feud has captured the headlines of the wrestling world all throughout August! Ever since The Secret Agent Man revealed the after- hours activities of The Law, they and The Grim Reaper have been out to get him!" Wince: "S.A.M is a model of integrity, unlike those two slimeballs! They sent The Grim Reaper, who's been rising meteorically in past weeks. Last week he destroyed The Killer Whale! I hope S.A.M does better!" Baboon: "Let's hear from The Reaper." Reaper: "Secret Agent Man, the good book says it well, 'it is appointed unto men once to die'. Your appointment is but heartbeats away. But have no fear for the future SAM, because I WILL bury you." Baboon: "This should be an interesting match-up, the skill of The Secret Agent Man against the size and raw power of The Grim Reaper." Wince: "S.A.M wastes no time whipping off his trenchcoat and attacking The Reaper! But his blows aren't having any effect!" Baboon: "And The Reaper grabs S.A.M. by the throat! The Secret Agent Man is struggling, but he can't get free... Ear clap! That will slow anybody down!" Wince: "The Secret Agent Man is loose, and he hiptosses The Grim Reaper! But The Reaper gets right back up! The Agent bounces off the ropes and clotheslines The Reaper!" Baboon: "That put The Reaper on his back, but he's getting up again! What does it take to stop this guy? He presses S.A.M. and drops him on the mat!" Wince: "The Agent gets back up too! Look at the intensity in his eyes! He elbows The Reaper on the top of his head, but The Reaper scoops him up and slams him!" Baboon: "The Grim Reaper axehandles The Agent repeatedly! That must be taking it's toll on S.A.M's back!" Wince: "Hey! Two more Grim Reapers just showed up at ringside! What's going on?" Baboon: "The two wrestlers in the ring see them, but they aren't paying attention. The Secret Agent Man rolls out of the ring and climbs to the top rope... clothesline off the top! That put The Reaper down!" Wince: "The Reaper is about to get up, but S.A.M. has a plan this time, he's keeping The Reaper down by working on his legs!" Baboon: "The Secret Agent with an elbow drop into The Reaper's hamstring muscles! But The Grim Reaper reaches out and grabs S.A.M. by the hair!" Wince: "He slams S.A.M. into the mat! The Grim Reaper gets back up, and he throws The Agent out of the ring! S.A.M. lands hard on the concrete, and I think he landed on his injured knee!" Baboon: "Yes, he did! He's in pain! The two Reapers on the outside roll him back in, and The Grim Reaper bounces off the ropes... big splash!" Wince: "He goes for the cover. One, two, three! The Grim Reaper wins!" Baboon: "The two imitation Reapers on the outside just took off their robes... It's Judge Dredd and Judge Anderson... The Law! They grab chairs and roll into the ring!" Wince: "The Reaper and The Law are stomping, kicking, and hitting The Secret Agent Man's knee with chairs! This could be the end of an illustrious career!" Baboon: "They're doing a lot of damage here! Will anyone stop them! Judge Dredd just grabbed the mike, let's hear what he's got to say." Dredd: "Secret Agent Man, you've been judged and found guilty. It's time for the death sentence." Wince: "Oh no! What could that be? Wait, here come The Groundbreakers! Here comes Mr. B! Here comes The Big Kahuna! And The BattleCruisers are on their way too!" They're calling for a stretcher and here it comes! Look at his knee! It's way out of joint!" Baboon: "What despicable actions on the part of The Grim Reaper and The Law! They should be fined and suspended!" Iron Man Tournament, Round 2 Photon Torpedo vs. The Executioner The Big Kahuna vs. The Plaid Powerhouse Baboon: "It's time for round two.
