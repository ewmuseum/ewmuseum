WeWF Report #9 2/24/91 General Takes World Belt! The General beat Juggernaut to become the 10th WeWF World Champion! Juggernaut had held the belt for almost a month! The General lost his Africa/Australia belt to Mr. Roddy, but defeated Juggernaut by 5 points! Photon Torpedo beat Juggernaut by DQ in the first match, and "Irish" Mike Flint had only lost by 3 points before the General won his title match! Next turn he will defend against Juggernaut, Frank Steel, Destruction, Mr. Roddy and Volcano. Leaping Lords Retain Tag Belts! The Leaping Lords beat their three challengers and retained the belts! They had some close matches, but became the first team to hold the belts two turns in a row! They will defend the belts against the Maniacs at the February Supercard, and the winners of that match will defend the titles next week! Unknown Masked Wrestler Wins Battle Royal! The Unkown Masked Wrestler, managed by Johnny the Geek, won the WeWF Battle Royal on Friday, and got $ 100,000 from Mr. Fujikuchi! Who is this masked man? More Supercard Matches Signed! Nine Supercard matches have been signed! The new ones are FireLord vs. Lord Roderick to determine the true lord of the WeWF, the Newfie Bulletts vs. Flint and Steel, Death & Destruction vs. Angus "the Caber" Campbell and Jake "Canadian" Shield in an Iron Man match, and the Legion of Super Wrestlers with Juggernaut vs. AquaDemon, TerraDoom and WindWarrior! The Supercard is now just four days away! In case this hasn't been clarified, Supercard matches count on wrestler's records (except six-man matches). Also, if a champion is in a match at the Supercard, it will be a title defense. New Stuff I started picking the hold values this turn, so they are no longer random. If your wrestler isn't doing well, change his repetoire, but if he's doing fine, don't change it! I had planned to start adding experience this turn, but it made the results look very, very, weird, so I dumped it for now. The List of Managers, the List of Champions, and the Handbook are all available upon request. Errors Two matches between Lord Roderick and Strike Eagle were listed last turn, but it should have just been one match. The Feral Frog and the Neutralizer had two matches, and the Feral Frog should have won both. Neither of these errors had any effect on the records, however. Send me your matches/comments by 3/3/91!
