WeWF Report #6 1/31/91 Juggernaut New World Champ! Juggernaut beat the former World Champ Volcano by 5 points to become the 8th WeWF World Champion! Volcano had defeated the former champ Joe "Brick" Wall and all three Continental Champions before losing to the #2 contender. The Leaping Lords were also successful this turn, taking two Continental Titles! The Crusher, managed by their arch-enemy Simon Bolendorf took the Africa/Australia Belt! Next turn, Juggernaut will defend the title against Volcano, The Feral Frog, The Lethal Lemming, The Crusher, and Joe "Brick" Wall, in that order! The Dynamic Machine Regains Tag-Team Belts! The original WeWF tag-team champions, Black Tiger and World Champion Juggernaut regained the tag-team titles this turn! They had 145 points! Questionnaire Results: Based on the results from the questionnaire that I sent out last turn, I have made or planned to make several changes: 1) Starting with turn 9 (I have already rolled up the values for the next two turns), I will be picking the hold values rather than rolling them. I will keep them somewhat consistent, but I will shuffle them around once in a while so that no one dominates the league. The holds that I think are more effective will tend to have high values, and the holds that I think are ineffective will tend to have lower values. 2) Champions now have +5 points when they are defending their belts, and no wrestler can challenge more than one Continental Champion per turn. Hopefully, this will slow down the rate at which titles change hands. However, the world champion must now always make five title defenses each turn. This means that the #2 contender will sometimes get a title shot. 3) I will add experience on a trial basis starting with turn 9. More experienced wrestlers will have an extra five or ten points, no substitute for a good hold values, but still a benefit. 4) Several people expressed the desire to have the e-mail addresses of their fellow managers for trading comments, etc. I don't want to infringe on anyone's privacy, so I will make this available on a voluntary basis. If you want your name and e-mail address to be available to other managers, let me know. 5) I will add a supercard at the end of every month, starting with February. Supercard matches will include a match description, and each wrestler is limited to one match per Supercard. The idea of Supercards is to hold special matches such as 6-man tag matches and feud ending type matches. When you request a Supercard match, I will tell your potential opponent that they have been challenged to such and such a match. If they accept, I will send both of you more information, and add the match to the card. Its probably a good idea to request supercard matches as early as possible so that your would-be opponents haven't already signed a match. 6) Smaller changes: I trimmed down the list of addresses at the top of the bulletin, and I shuffled the order of the match results around. 7) Reminders: You are limited to 5 matches per turn, however, you may now have up to five wrestlers or four and a tag-team! Also, don't change you wrestler's repetoire unless he is doing poorly. Send me your matches/comments by 2/10/91! Note: After that we go weekly, with matches and comments being due every Sunday!
