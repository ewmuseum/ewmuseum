WeWF Report #17 4/21/91 No Belts Change Hands! Once again Frank Steel took on and beat all of his challengers, except his partner "Irish" Mike Flint. All three Continental Champions hung onto their belts, so Steel will face much the same opposition next week. Flint and Steel held onto their tag team titles again this week, despite the Highlanders coming close to taking the belts away from them. Apocalypse Now won their title matches to remain champions, beating even the Ultimate Union! Bjorn "the Sweedish Pretzel" Wins Battle Royal! Bjorn "the Sweedish Pretzel" won the Team Challenge Series Battle Royal! In the end he overcame Flint and Steel, although he had some help, and eliminated them both! This week he beat Mike Flint. Could he be the next World Champ? More Supercard Matches Signed! The Thunder Brothers and the Steel Monkeys have signed for a three falls match; Sour Krout and Slimm will wrestle each other; the Steroid Centurion and Samson Goliath will go at it in a special cage match; the Dynamic Machine will take on the other two members of the Chain Gang; and Amazon Amy and Molly Maguire will wrestle on the 30th! I'm looking for help in writing this Supercard, if you want to write up a match for it, let me know! New TWIW Editor!
