WeWF Report #16 4/14/91 Frank Steel Still Champ, Beats General! Frank Steel continued his title reign, the WeWF's longest ever, by winning four of his title matches, and fighting to a draw against his partner, "Irish" Mike Flint. In his final match of the week he beat the General, who had beaten him by DQ the week before. However, the General hung on to the #2 contender spot, and will get another shot next week. The Re-Animator and "Irish" Mike Flint held onto their Continental titles, they will also get shots next week. DiMadMan, after losing the Tag Team belts, came back to regain the Africa/Australia Championship! Flint and Steel Take Tag Belts! Flint and Steel defeated the Intoxicators to win the Tag Team Titles for the third time! This means that they hold almost all of the major titles except the six man belts, which Apocalypse Now successfully defended. Four Corners, Elementals Tied For TCS Lead! The Four Corners moved up to a tie for first place with the Elementals in the Team Challenge Series. Neither of these great teams has faced the other yet, and that is the matchup many fans are looking forward to. Four Corners Fined! The Four Corners have been fined $10,000 each for their vicious attack on the Circus of Pain which put four men in the hospital! The members of the Abattoir have been fined $1,000 each for their part in the attack which took place at the Awards Ceremony. The $44,000 will go to pay the medical bills of Dr. Pain and his Circus. Meanwhile, Dr. Pain has been fined $1,000 for shaving Robby the Lame's head! Robby was seen recently asking an unidentified man, said to be Lanny Poffo, about toupees. Supercard 3 News! Two matches have been signed, and several other challenges are still out there. The matches signed so far are: The Four Corners and the Abattoir vs. the Elementals and Sword, Shield, Axe, and Chain in a Triple Dome of Terror Match with the Hit 'N' Run Rabbi as referee! And Doug "Crusher" Corey vs. the Crusher in a Loser Drops Name Match! There is still plenty of time, the Supercard will be held the last day of April, so send your challenges now! Summer Vacation? A lot of managers will probably be away from terminals for the Summer Break, so when you send me your matches and comments, let me know if you will be gone, and when so that I can get an idea of who will be here this summer, and what will happen to the WeWF. Time I have been trying to save time, so I have a couple of programs doing the points and match results, and I would really appreciate it if someone could write a program to do the rankings. I removed 27 wrestlers this week, since I hadn't heard from their managers in the past month, and I would like all managers to not create any more wrestlers for the time being, since there seem to be plenty already. There are a few simple things that you can do to help me, which are: 1) make as few strategy changes as possible. If it ain't broke, don't fix it! 2) when you are listing your matches, please keep title match challenges on one line, since I grep through a 70K file looking for them, and it's really annoying having to search through the entire file... Please list your opponent's name, and your wrestlers name on the same line so that I can send title challenges to the champions faster! Send me your matches/comments by 4/21/91! Ross Hibbs, manager of the Neutralizer, was seen at ringside of all the Continental Champions' title matches... with a notebook and pencil. He also was on hand for the title defenses of Frank Steel, with the same notebook and pencil.
