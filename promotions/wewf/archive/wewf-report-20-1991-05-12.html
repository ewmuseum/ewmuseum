WeWF Report #20 5/12/91 DiMadMan Retains World Belt! DiMadMan held onto his title this week, despite losing two matches by disqualification! According to the new rules, if he loses to someone by DQ he must face them again next week, so the General will get a title rematch. BudMan declined a title shot against his partner, so the Neutralizer took his place. Will fellow Four Corner and new Africa/Australia Champ Bjorn "the Sweedish Pretzel" decline his shot this week? If he doesn't, DiMadMan will face the Big Kahuna, "Irish" Mike Flint, Bjorn, the General and the Re-Animator! If he does turn it down, BudMan would be offered the match, and if he turns it down, Mr. Roddy will get the shot. Highlanders Take Tag Belts! The Highlanders beat the Law this week, then won the other two title matches! Both teams are entered in the Tag Team Battle Royal this week, along with 13 other teams! The Plaid Powerhouse came very close to winning the Americas' Title this week, and Bjorn "the Sweedish Pretzel" won the Africa/Australia Tournament. Will the Highlanders dominate the league the way Flint and Steel did? Bjorn "the Sweedish Pretzel" Wins Tournament! Bjorn "the Sweedish Pretzel" won the tournament to win the Africa/Australia, which was vacated by DiMadMan last week! The tournament took place in installments this week. Bjorn beat Virus in Round 1 (Wednesday), Mr. Roddy in Round 2 (Thursday) and Frank Steel in the final match (Friday)! Note to rec.games.pbm readers: if you want to read the tournament, I will send it to you by e-mail. The Big Kahuna Wins Americas' Title! The Big Kahuna won the Americas' Title from the Re-Animator! The Re-Animator had held on to that belt for quite a while, until losing it to the Big Kahuna in his second defense this week. In the Big Kahuna's first title defense, the Plaid Powerhouse tried to bribe the referee, but the ref disqualified him and kept the money! Supercard Scheduled!
