WeWF Report #15 4/7/91 Frank Steel Still WeWF Champ! Despite losing by DQ, and a time limit draw against his partner, Frank Steel held on to the WeWF Title for another week! The General was the one to beat him by DQ, and the General, although he lost his America's belt to the Re- Animator, will have another shot next week as #2 contender. Intoxicators Take Tag Belts! The Intoxicators took the WeWF Tag Titles this week! DiMadMan and BudMan beat RnR after the Highlanders had beaten RnR by DQ. Apocalypse Now Take Six Man Belts! The three masked men calling themselves Apocalypse Now beat the Superstars to take the Six Man Titles. Who are these masked men? Elementals Winning TCS! The Elementals are currently in the lead on the Team Challenge Series Scoreboard, after a sweep of the Circus of Pain! How long will they be able to hold on to the lead? One of their members, WindWarrior, took the Africa/ Australia Championship this week. Supercard Coming! The 3rd Supercard is coming on the 30th of April! Start making your challenges now! Several challenges have already been issued, I will send them out officially soon. Errors In the last handbook, there was a statement saying that wrestlers with more wins get more points. That statement is false, and has been corrected in the new version. Send me your matches/comments by 4/14/91! Matches This Week World Title: The General beat Frank Steel by DQ.
