WeWF Report #18 4/28/91 The General Wins World Title! The General beat Frank Steel to regain the WeWF World Championship! Steel appeared to be exhausted from his long run of title defenses, winning just two of his title matches this week. The General, after being beaten by Steel several times during Steel's reign, finally pulled out an upset and beat Steel! This is the second time the General has held the World Title. Other Titles Defended Successfully! All of the other champions made successful title defenses this week. Malibu beat DiMadMan by DQ, coming very close to taking that belt away from him, and Apocalypse Now nearly lost their titles to the Abattoir. Supercard News! The final card for Tuesday's Supercard was given in TWIW, but this match has just been added: Frank Steel vs. the General for the World Championship! Steel is out to prove that the General's victory was just a fluke, the General will try to hold onto the belt and take a step toward breaking Steel's record for successful title defenses! Both wrestlers have over 50 wins, and both are several time Champions! This battle of the superstars should be exciting! Also, the Legion of Super Wrestlers vs. the Empire match is now a scaffold match, and Mugambo Squid will be crowned the Most Mugambo Squid! And don't miss the last matches of Sword and Shield, Mugambo Squid, and with all of the special matches signed, will anyone else have their WeWF career ended? The Hit 'N' Run Rabbi has not been seen since his dissappearance from the Meat Locker during This Week in Wrestling! If he doesn't show up for the Supercard, the Elementals have recommended that Vito Gianelli take his place! The post-supercard This Week in Wrestling will come out on Friday so that there will be time for managers to send post match comments. Speaking of comments, I'm still looking for more pre-match supercard comments! Also, if you volunteered to write a Supercard match, and/or have Monday night and Tuesday morning free, and haven't gotten any information from me, remind me, and you'll be able to write a match! New Championship Rules It seems like every week the same wrestlers get title shots for being top contenders. From now on, though, things will be a little different. The World Champion's first defense will be against anyone who beat him by DQ the previous week (unless they already have a title shot), or the former champion, if there is one. Then he will defend against the Continental Champions, just like before. But then he will defend against the two highest ranked wrestlers who he hasn't faced that month. Next week, the champion will face Frank Steel (or the General), the Re-Animator, "Irish" Mike Flint, DiMadMan and Virus. The next week, they will defend against a top contender other than Virus, even if he holds onto the #2 contender slot. Send me your matches and comments by 5/5/91!
