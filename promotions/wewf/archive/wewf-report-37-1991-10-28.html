WeWF Report #37

28 October 1991
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WeWF Report #37 10/28/91 Wince: "Welcome everyone to another WeWF Report! You didn't think we'd schedule an event against the World Series, did you?" Baboon: "Well, actually..." Wince: "This week we've got lot's of wild action... We've got three title matches once again, including that big title rematch between Steelgrave and The Sandman." Baboon: "And a title rematch between new champions The Klingons and former Tag Team Champions The Maniacs." Wince: "And hopefully we'll have an update on what The Four Corners have done with Val Lee! No one has seen here since they abducted her last week!" Baboon: "Let's got to an 'old friend' of mine, Robby the Lame! He has a very special interview for us." Robby the Lame: "This is Robby the Lame here, doing a special interview with Machiavelli, and the Grim Reaper. Machiavelli, isn't it about time that you ended the career of that no good hick, the General?" Machiavelli: "We have plans for him. The Reaper has been scouting out his weaknesses. He will get what's coming to him." <The General comes to the interview with a mike of his own> General: "OK, tough guy, it's time for this feud to come to an end. The Hoosiers and myself would like to challenge The Grim Reaper and The Law to a Steel Man tournament. Each individual from our side will wrestle each individual from yours 1 on 1 in a steel cage. Whoever gets the most wins wins the feud. End of story!" Robby: "We don't have to take this, get out of here you flea-ridden vermin!" <Suddenly, the Hoosiers come up from behind the interview area. They double-clothesline the Grim Reaper from behind, and then proceed to Spike Piledriver Machiavelli and Robby the Lame. The General has disappeared, but has been replaced by a masked man weilding a samurai sword. By the time the LAW can arrive, they are outmanned and out armed, and both find themselves on the bad end of Spike Pildedrivers. Finally, the General Machine chases everyone back into the dressing room with his samurai sword.> Baboon: "How about that! Things are rolling before any matches have taken place!" Wince: "The General Machine is dangerous! Don't you think he should be suspended, Baboon?" Baboon: "But who IS that masked man?" Wince: "Sparks continue to fly between The Law and The Hoosiers." Mr B: "I'd just like to congratulate The Klingons on the eve of their first title defense! These guys epitomise the true spirit of wrestling, and they have a great fan in the B! Guys, I still owe you one from a few weeks back when you saved my butt from that scum-sucker Mr Shoddy, and so I'm inviting you to act as lumberjacks in my Supercard match! How about it? Wouldn't you just LOVE to nail the Dead Moron with a length of chain? I know I would!!" Baboon: "Mr. B is inviting The Klingons to be lumberjacks for his match!" Wince: "They would be armed with chains... as if those ridged heads weren't bad enough." Baboon: "Mr. B is looking to thrash The Red Baron, and The Baron is out to finish what he started two months ago. It should be quite a match." Wince: "And here's another great match: The Wyld Stallyons and Apocalypse Now against The Law and The Highlanders!" Bill: "Whel Hail, Ted, ah'm, ah say ah'm a Hoosier." Ted: "Bill, my friend, that attempt at an "accent" may be bogus, Dude, but you sure sound stupid enough, dude!" Both: "We're... WYLD STALLYONS!!!!!" [air guitar] Wince: "At the Supercard, The Stallyons will be teaming up with these men." Death: "Our most excellent team partners and Apocalypse Now are going to bodily remove parts of the Four Corners to the four corners of the earth, get ready for Death is amongst you." War: "When you the Law, expect an easy cruise to victory expect War to come between you and your closest allies, we W I L L win, you will be pinned to the four corners, of the ring." Death & War: "Feel the POWER of the APOCALYPSE!" Baboon: "That should be some match." Wince: "I hope the other two Apocalypse members are there, because you can bet The Intoxicators will be at ringside." Baboon: "And they won't be selling pizza." Metal: "Mr Baron, you think your hot with that plane, well I got aces high and I'm gunning for you, you might have Mr. B in your sights but watch your flanks because that's where I am, and I'm gonna crush your aeroplane sour-kraut!" <leaves with Aces High by Iron Maiden playing.> Wince: "That match will take place right here! Metal is certainly off to a fast start, battling rugged Johnny Granite to a draw and now challenging The Red Baron." Baboon: "He's got some tough matches signed for this week, so let's see how he does." Dr Down-Under: "I knew the WeWF was a JOKE but I didn't think that Slim Heard would allow the fact to go public!! How many times are these so-called "champions" going to be allowed to dodge challengers by participating in pointless, non-title tag matches at Supercards?? Bjorn, The Animal has shown you to be a pussy-wimp of the first order!! This is your last chance... respond to the Call of the Wild, meet The Animal in a Shock Treatment match, or be remembered as the greatest CHUMP in the sorry history of the WeWF!!!!!!!" Wince: "Oh my! Slim Heard and fine and suspend Dr. Down-Under for saying those things!" Baboon: "I agree! But he is right that Bjorn has been ducking him." Wince: "Speaking of Bjorn, remember what happened at his last title match? The Plaid Powerhouse grabbed Val Lee and ran off while Tom Brown, Bjorn, Dr. Down-Under, Smooth, The Big Kahuna, and The Intoxicators brawled!" Baboon: "Let's hear from former World Champ T. B. Kahuna." T.B.: "Dude, I don't know what was going through that moth eaten braincell of yours Plaid Powerhouse, but you don't touch Val. You did the crime Powerhouse, get ready to do the time. I am NOT a happy camper. I AM gonna Wipe YOU out!" Wince: "I almost feel sorry for Double P! The Big Kahuna will certainly be after him with a vengeance!" Baboon: "The Plaid Powerhouse will have The Four Corners with him, you can count on that. The Big Kahuna is in for a fight." Reaper: "They say history repeats itself... Well Crusher I guess they're right, cause I'm gonna bury you. Again!" Wince: "The Crusher vs. The Reaper! Right here, in just a few minutes! I can hardly wait!" Erik: "Team Supreme, it's time to see what you masked men are really made of." Daemon: "You should know that to wear a mask, you should be called Something Machine." Erik: "So now we'll call you Big & Ugly Machine," Daemon: "And Bigger & Uglier Machine!" Erik: "See you at ringside." Baboon: "That's another big match for this week, The Hoosiers against Big and Ugly and Bigger and Uglier!" Wince: "I understand The Law are feeling better, after that attack by The Hoosiers. Let's go to them." Dredd: "You know, in all of this federation, there are not more than two men who can't get it through their thick skulls that the WeWF tag belts belong around these waist." Anderson: "And those two men are Erik and Daemon, The Loser Hoosiers. Well juves, come the next Supercard you're gonna find out the hard way." Dredd: "Cause no one is above....." Both: "THE LAW!!!!" Dredd: "No one!" Wince: "It certainly sounds like that Supercard match is shaping up, but what about The Law teaming with The Highlanders against Wyld Stallyons and Apocalypse Now?" Baboon: "This match is full of questions. Hopefully I'll find the answers to them later, when I talk to The General." Metal: "Johnny Granite, you might have been able to tie me up on the outside of the ring during our last encounter, but believe me when I get hold of you again, you're gonna fade to black, I strongly dislike cheats and you're it, get ready to face me in the supercard, I CHALLENGE YOU TO BE A MAN FOR THE FIRST TIME IN YOUR LIFE AND ENTER THE SQUARED CIRCLE WITH ME." <Leaves with Fade to Black by Metallica playing.> Wince: "That was a challenge, plain and simple! Metal is challenging Granite!" Baboon: "We haven't heard much from Granite lately, so I don't know if he'll accept." Wince: "Take it away, Green Jean!" Green Jean: "With me now is The Crusher, and his manager, Simon Bolendorf!" The Crusher: "Well, you know you're close to the top when people start challenging you instead of the other way around. The Maniacs let their guard down. They lost their belts as a result. But Simon here has taken care of that problem... the Klingons are going to pay, but that's not what I'm here to talk about. First we have to take care of The General. He and his Loser Hoosiers couldn't handle us, so he had to get a 4th man. And now we have this "Natural" Crusty Rustin Nose and the KingKongs. If you want a piece of us, you've got it... You'll remember it for a while!" Wince: "That could easily be another Supercard match! As he promised, Baboon has gone off to interview The General. Baboon?" Baboon: "This is Baboon Tempest here with the General. You issued a hefty challenge to the Grim Reaper and the LAW. Do you think that they will accept?" General: "I hope those pansies (Hey, this is PG-13) accept. It's about time that we get them into the ring." <The LAW suddenly appear from behind and jump on the General. They slam his head through the podium and start kicking the snot out of him. The Grim Reaper then appears and puts him in the sleeper. Machiavelli walks forward with a mike> Machiavelli: "HELL YES, WE ACCEPT!" <The Grim Reaper drops the unconcious General as Erik and Daemon, the Hoosiers appear with chairs chasing the LAW, Reaper and Machiavelli away.> Wince: "Oh no! I certainly hope The General is alright. He has a title match coming up! Here's what happened in this week's matches:" Singles: The Big Kahuna over The Plaid Powerhouse. The Plaid Powerhouse over "The Natural" Rustin Nose. BudMan over "The Natural" Rustin Nose. DiMadmMan over "The Natural" Rustin Nose. The Red Baron over The Terminator. The Crusher over Tom "The Animal" Brown in a Brass Knuckles match. Metal over Mr. B. The Grim Reaper over The Crusher. The Crusher over "The Natural" Rustin Nose. The Red Baron over "The Natural" Rustin Nose. Metal over The Red Baron. Tag Team: The Intoxicators over The Wyld Stallyons. The Highlanders over Apocalypse Now. The Hoosiers over The Law. Hoosiers over Team Supreme. Team Supreme over Apocalypse Now. Wince: "Tough week for Rustin Nose! It's time for The General's US Title match against Bjorn "The Swedish Pretzel." Let's go down to ringside." Vague: "The General is coming to ringside with a heavily bandaged head... Listen to the ovation, Rusty!" Rusty: "Sure, Lord Winfred, they're cheering The General, but he is in no condition to wrestle. This should be an easy title defense for Bjorn." Vague: "Bjorn must think so too; look at him strut around the ring." Rusty: "The Hoosiers are watching The General. I think Daemon is telling him not to wrestle, but The General is going to anyway!" Vague: "What intestinal fortitude! But Bjorn suplexes The General! That could be all!" Rusty: "One, two, kickout! Bjorn drops a knee on The General's head! Ouch!" Vague: "But The General kicks out again! Now Bjorn is going to the top rope... He's going for a big fistdrop!" Rusty: "The General rolls away! Bjorn wasn't taking the match seriously enough, and now he's in trouble!" Vague: "The General lifts him up and gives him a brainbuster! One, two, three! What an upset!" Rusty: "Here is your winner, and NEW US CHAMPION, The General!" Vague: "A stunned Bjorn is making his way back to the dressing room, but here comes Tom "The Animal" Brown! He was hiding in the crowd!" Rusty: "Brown is all over Bjorn! And Dr. Down-Under is on a rampage with TWO TASER's! He zaps The General! And he zaps Smooth!" Vague: "The Hoosiers are after him, so he escapes back into the crowd!" Rusty: "Tom "The Animal" Brown lifts Bjorn up and brainbusters him ON THE STEEL RAILING!" Vague: "You don't get up from that one! Brown escapes into the crowd! The Animal Liberation Front members are screening him from would-be persuers!" Wince: "I don't believe it! The General wins the match, despite a serious injury, and Tom Brown causes some grievous bodily harm to Bjorn!" Baboon: "Do you think Bjorn will be feeling well enough for the Supercard match?" Wince: "I don't know, Baboon. We'll have to wait and find out. Right now, let's go to Green Jean." Green Jean: "Well Simon, it seems like The Maniacs were taken by surpise last week!" Simon Bolendorf: "Well well well.... the Klingons are back. I thought I smelled something. Well guys, The Maniacs were nice to you last week... This week they're not only going to beat you, they're going to send you back where you came from!" Maniac #2: "Yeaaaahhhh boyyyyyzzzzzz...." Simon Bolendorf: "Sorry Green Jean, he forgot to take his medicine this morning..." Baboon: "Now let's go to pre-recorded comments from WeWF World Tag Team Champions The Klingons!" (Scene: the Trekkies' dressing room after the Klingons' World tag-team title victory over the Maniacs. The Romulan Mauler and The Ultimate Lanny are showering the new champs with champaign.) Green Jean: "I am now in the dressing room area with the new WeWF Tag-team champions, the Klingons, and their partners; Admiral Chekov, what are your comments?" Chekov: "Green Jean, you saw it just like we did: the Ultimate Lanny and the Klingons went from nothing to the top of the world, and Trekkiemania is sweeping the whole Internet!" Green Jean: "Admiral, is it true that your Klingons and The Natural, Rustin Nose will team up at the next SuperCard?" Chekov: "Hum... I don't think so, Green Jean. The Natural is a fine youg man, but he is not of major-league caliber as of yet. The only reason for his being in the WeWF at all, at such an early stage in his career, is Rusty Nose's influence on the board of directors... No, we have some other projects for the SuperCard..." Green Jean: "Which are?" Chekov: "We hope to STILL be the World tag-team champions and to defend the belts against David Lightfinger's cronies, the New Gods. They aren't worth sh*t right now, and they will be worth even less when we are through with them! Lightfinger, the problems of the NeWA prevented my Klingons from going to YOUR territory; we will just have to wipe you out of this one. The Ultimate Trekkies always prevail!" Green Jean: "Well, thank you Admiral; let's go back to Baboon and Wince." <The Mauler showers Green Jean with champaign, and the Ultimate Lanny plants a BIG kiss on her mouth!!!! Green Jean looks dumbfounded... Fade out!> Vague: "The two teams are starting off cautiously... Klingon Disrupter ties up with Maniac 1, I guess, and the Maniac forces Disrupter to the ropes by the hair." Rusty: "The referee asks for a clean break, but Maniac 1 punches Disrupter, but Disrupter pushes Maniac 1, almost knocking him across the ring!" Vague: "What power! Maniac 2 tags in, and he rakes Disrupter's eyes. He tags out to Maniac 1, who dropkicks the big Klingon." Rusty: "They're showing the teamwork that made them champions!" Vague: "Klingon Phaser is going crazy waiting to get in there! A Maniac slaps him in the face! While the referee is trying to keep Phaser out of the ring, The Maniacs have a chance to do some dirty work." Rusty: "But Disrupter bangs their heads together! He tags Phaser in, and body slams a Maniac!" Vague: "Klingon Phaser from the top rope... Klinshai Bombing! That diving headbutt will put anybody out! One, two, three! A successful title defense for The Klingons!" Wince: "The Klingons retain the belts! They could be on their way to a long reign." Baboon: "Now it's time for our World Title match. Last week The Sandman beat Steelgrave by countout, but this week could be a different story. Let's go to pre-recorded comments from The Sandman." [Roll Tape] The screen is black for a few moments. It then resolves into a darkened room. Steelgrave sleeps in one corner. He begins to moan, quietly at first, then louder and louder as he starts to thrash about, tossing and turning. Suddenly he lets out a yell, and sits bolt upright in bed, sweating slightly. <image fades, replaced by the Sandman and Death, lounging around.> Sandman: "Hmmmmmm... not my best piece of work, but a job well done..." Death: "Whatcha gonna give him tomorrow night, Dream? Huh? Huh?" Sandman: "I think perhaps the Corinthean will visit him tomorrow night, and the night after that, I will take his belt..." Vague: "The two wrestlers are going at it toe to toe! Steelgrave decks The Sandman!" Rusty: "And Steelgrave is choking The Sandman out!" Vague: "Sandman rolls away and stops to talk to his sister... Death." Rusty: "Steelgrave is coming closer, and The Sandman turns around and kicks him! That didn't seem to have any effect!" Vague: "And now Steelgrave is going for a piledriver! He nailed it! He goes for the cover, but Death puts The Sandman's foot on the ropes!" Rusty: "Steelgrave whips The Sandman off the ropes and gives him a big back body drop! Now he grabs Sandman by the throat and forces him back into a corner!" Vague: "The Sandman is having the life choked out of him! He's trying to break free, but his blows are having no effect!" Rusty: "Death hands him a chair! The Sandman hits Steelgrave with the chair!" Vague: "That barely had any effect! Sandman rolls out of the ring and is gasping for air." Rusty: "The referee disqualifies The Sandman for using that chair! Steelgrave is the winner!" Baboon: "Steelgrave wins by DQ! Let's see what matches have been signed for the October Supercard on Wednesday:" Mr. B vs. The Red Baron in a lumberjack chain match The General and The Hoosiers vs. The Grim Reaper and The Law in a Steel Man match Apocalypse Now and The Wyld Stallons vs. The Highlanders and The Intoxicators in a three falls match Wince: "That's all?" Baboon: "Yep. So long everybody, see you Wednesday!"
