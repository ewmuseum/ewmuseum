Dirty South Wrestling Association: Founding Handbook

DSWA Dirty South Wrestling Association Table Of Contents:: 1.Rules 2.Role-Playing~Strats~Angles 3.Weekly Cards 4.PPV 5.Titles 6.DSWA Application 7.Men behind the scenes 8.Final Words _____________________________________________________________________ 1)~~Rules~~~ .A)Role-play! This is a fed where you must rp to win. We will not award wins to just anyone. You dont rp, you dont win. Your rps set off everything that you do in DSWA. The more, and the better you rp, the more and the easier it will be for myself and the other co-prez's to give you pushes, and angles, and strats that you want done. B)No, and I mean NO COLOR IN ROLE-PLAYS! White background with regular black text, and font. C)This is an original fed, we do not accept real wrestlers. I know sometimes it is hard, but I would like everyone to at least try to think up there OWN gimmick for there own wrestler, we dont need 15 "Rattlesnaking Stone Cold
Rocks", wrestlers will not exceed 7'4, and 550 pounds. D)You cannot break Kayfabe in Roleplays, for those of you who dont know what
Kayfabe is, it is staying in Character. If you rp and if KAYFABE is broken I will not send the rp out and warn you the first time, time number two will result in what the Tri-Presidental Committee decides. E)Follow your basic instincts really. A fed is not hard and we do not wanna make it hard. Use your brain cells and it will be fun for all of us. F) THE LAST MAJOR RULE!!!! PLEASE, AND I REPEAT PLEASE DO NOT TAKE THIS TOO SERIOUSLY. It is only a fed. I dont mind at all if you take rping serious, but, do not take a lose like a real life lose, dont take a win like a real life win and go bragging like you won Publishers Clearing House. This should be FUN for all! ______________________________________________________________________ 2)Role-Playing~Strats~Angles 1.A)Roleplaying~~I should not have to explain to any of you what ROLEPLAYING is first of all. You all KNOW, and if you dont know what ROLEPLAYING is, please dont bother reading further. B)No and I mean NO COLORS IN RP! Rps will be white background with black text and correct font. C)I dont see a reason to put a minimum line length on rps. Just dont be trying to send in no damn 5 liners. Lets get real people, we all know what normal is. 2.A.)Strats~Now I know some of you think a strat and an Angle are the same thing. Well wrong, All a strat is, is a one card, strategy. You send it in to attack someone, or do certain things to your opponent, or not do this and whatnot. B)The Tri-Presidental Committee decides whether a strat will be used or not. So some will be done some may not. It all depends on, rping, time, number of strats, quality of strat. 3.A) Angles~ My personal Favorite. A fed is just not a fed if there are no
Angles. Angles are story lines that can be drawn out, in a way to keep them intresting, exciting, and leaving a reader waiting to see what will happen next. You the Handlers, and sometimes even the Tri-Presidental Committee work angles. You got a good story line, you got an angle, you got something that looks like crap(i.e. Flair heart attack and series) you got not a thing. _____________________________________________________________________ 3.Weekly Cards Right now we are only going to have one card a week. This card can happen any night of the week it just all depends on what happens and what doesnt happen with the fed. The weekly card will be called Presence Of Persecution. This is the only card of the week so it has to be good. Maybe, just maybe, if the fed gets rolling along, another card could be introduced to keep the rping going. _____________________________________________________________________ 4. PPV There will be a PPV every two months. Every once a month is just too hard to get good angles and fueds going, plus it kinda kills at least one weekly card a month. On PPV's all Titles will be defended unless told other wise by the
Tri-Presidental Committee. All matches on a PPV will be high class. I will give a list of the PPV's once we get the fed going. _____________________________________________________________________ 5.Titles The DSWA Universal Championship~~This title is reserved for the best. You have to rp, and rp good to take this title. There will be plenty of good rpers challenging for this title so good luck. This title will be defended at least 2 times a month, plus at every PPV. Sometimes to settle a fued, or keep something going you may defend mote, be ready for anything. The DSWA Southern Championship~(This title equals about the WCW TV Title)~This title will be defended at just about every card. So if you get this title you will definitly have your work cut out to keep it. It may not be the top title but it is still a prestigious title that everyone will be looking to take. The DSWA Lightweight Championship~~Only a lightweight(Wrestler under 225) can hold this title. This title will be defended at least twice a month and at
PPVs. Little men be ready for feirce competition. The DSWA Tag Team Championship~~This title is reserved for the top tag team in
DSWA. The title will be defended at least twice a month and and PPV's. _____________________________________________________________________ 6.
