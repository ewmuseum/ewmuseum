Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Dirty South Wrestling Association's own Handbook, posted 16 January 1999, described a fed run by three co-presidents together as a “Tri-Presidential Committee,” built strictly around original characters — no real wrestlers, and a hard cap of 7 feet 4 inches and 550 pounds — with kayfabe enforced in every roleplay on pain of a warning, then whatever punishment the Committee saw fit.

One card a week, Presence of Persecution, carried the promotion, with a pay-per-view every two months at which every title was due to be defended.

Four championships ran the roster: the DSWA Universal Championship at the top, defended at least twice a month and at every pay-per-view; the DSWA Southern Championship, defended on almost every card; the DSWA Lightweight Championship, restricted to wrestlers under 225 pounds; and the DSWA Tag Team Championship.

No roster, card, or further document survives beyond this founding handbook.
