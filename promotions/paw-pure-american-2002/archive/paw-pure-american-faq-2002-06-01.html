Where do I send things? 7. The Application ------------------------------ 1. What the _fuck_ is this? (IN-CHARACTER) This is Pure American Wrestling, a new national wrestling organization based out of Albany, New York, attempting to fill the void left by the failed organizations in the past. We at Pure American are dedicated to giving the fans something new and different to watch in a time when it seems the "same old, same old" is all that's left.
(OUT-OF-CHARCTER) This is Pure American Wrestling, an e-wrestling federation we're hoping will give people something new and different to read/participate in. Instead of tons of action on every card, and mammoth cards that take hours to read, we're devising a plan that's more
WWE/WCW/NWA based. The cards leading up to the pay-per-views will be simple in structure, but clean in execution. Five or six matches, all of them relatively short, all of them building an overall angle that will be resolved at the pay-per-view events. This might seem like the basic model of every other e-fed out there, but really... when was the last time you saw it done correctly? Every match seems to get more and more "daring" and "risky", and spots that used to mean something big have become watered-down.
Pure American isn't about stringing a ton of spots together and pretending it's a wrestling match. We're about the build and execution of intriguing feuds, leading to big pay-per-view matches that will be a joy to read.
To put it simply, Pure American Wrestling is just that... pure wrestling at it's finest. ----------------------------- 2. Who the _fuck_ is running this? (IN-CHARACTER) Pure American Wrestling is jointly funded by "The True Living Legend" Brian J. Blottie, Anthony Virtue, and nameless benefitiaries. But the day-to-day business is run by Blottie and Virtue, who operate as co-presidents. They're not friends, and in fact, have feuded in the past, but both men understand that without the other, they're nothing. Thus, they work for the greater cause; making Pure American run smoothly.
(OUT-OF-CHARACTER) Pure American Wrestling is jointly run by [name withheld] and [name withheld]. They will be handling the writing of the cards, the win/loss decisions, the egos of the handlers, and whatever else happens to pop-up.
Sometimes, they may ask for others to help with writing cards, especially pay-per-view events, but for the most part, they will handle the day-to-day running of the federation themselves.
------------------------------ 3. The Shows Pure American Wrestling will be running bi-weekly promo shows, and bi-weekly wrestling shows. PAW
Fireside, the promo show, will run one week before the wrestling show, entitled PAW Unleashed. Both these shows will be released on or around Thursday.
Sometimes the shows will be late, but that's par for the course in e-wrestling, isn't it? ------------------------------- 4. Characters Co-Presidents: "The True Living Legend" Brian J. Blottie and Anthony Virtue Brian J. Blottie is your usual rich, cocky owner. Anthony Virtue earned every cent he's investing into this company by working his butt off for the past fifteen years as a pro-wrestler. These two men don't get along, apparently, but nothing's perfect.
Announcers: Tommy Ganz and Chad Jackson Tommy Ganz covers play-by-play, and because of his pro-wrestling background (he's a pro-wrestler himself, and comes from a pro-wrestling family), he does it very well. Chad Jackson, on the other hand, is your classic heelish play-by-play announcer. He's not that proficient with actual move names, but he knows how to put over the heels like a champ.
Head Referee: Terry Danson He calls the match straight down the middle. He also isn't afraid of taking a bump to make sure that everybody plays by the rules. He _is_ easily distracted, however.
Interviewer: Mickey Grey He'll dig deep and try to get the dirt that the announce team doesn't. He's very straightforward, and isn't afraid to ask the tough questions. He acts like a hard-ass at times, but when threatened, he'll cry like a baby.
---------------------- 5. Titles At the start, there will just be two titles in PAW. The PAW National Championship, and the PAW National Tag Team Championships. As people join, and as the fed grows, we may add a Skyweight division, and possibly a Television title. But this all depends on how things go, and whether you guys feel we need more titles.
