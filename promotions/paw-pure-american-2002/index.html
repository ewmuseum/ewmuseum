Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A federation calling itself Pure American Wrestling introduced itself on Usenet on 1 June 2002, based, its own founding FAQ said, out of Albany, New York, and offered as an alternative to the two biggest promotions of the day. Its own writers framed the venture in national terms, aiming to fill what they called the void left by promotions that had already come and gone.

Its house style split each fortnight between a promo show, PAW Fireside, and a wrestling show, PAW Unleashed, both released on or around a Thursday. The founding FAQ set out two prizes at the promotion's launch, the PAW National Championship and the PAW National Tag Team Championship, and named its own on-screen staff: two feuding co-presidents at the top of the card, a pair of announcers, a head referee, and an interviewer.

A first wrestling card followed within days of the FAQ: a four-team Path to the Crown tag tournament and a separate singles match, both set for Irving Plaza in New York City on 20 June 2002, with the tournament's own winner to become the promotion's first National Champion. No result from that card survives here.

The initials belonged to this promotion in name only. A federation of the identical acronym, and no other tie, had already opened and closed five years earlier in Massachusetts; a second, unrelated Texas independent was running under the same three letters at the very moment this one introduced itself.

By September 2002 the promotion's own recruiting notices described a roster still filling out its tag division and a search for a creative consultant to help write cards. No further activity or closing notice survives here.
