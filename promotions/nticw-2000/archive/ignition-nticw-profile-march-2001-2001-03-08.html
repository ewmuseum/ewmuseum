profile: Ignition Email adress: [detail withheld] Entrance music: "Fear" by The Offspring Messenger info: [detail withheld] [detail withheld] Wrestlers name: IgnitioN contract lenghth: ends on march 31 2001 finishing move: The 1rd Degree Burn - A sitdown pedigree, could also be described as a duble underhook faceplant. other moves: 1) 3/4 Turn Neckbreaker from top rope (Sometimes used as set-up move) 2) Jacknife Powerbomb 3) Implant DDT 4) Guillotine Leg Drop 5) Reverse DDT 6) Powerbomb Pin trademark move: Boiling Point (Interchangable as finisher) - A reverse suplex is dropped into a stunner. type of wrestler:: PowerHouse wrestlers Height: 6'5" wrestlers Hometown: Santa Fe, New Mexico wrestlers allignment: Neutral wrestlers weight: 265lbs wrestlers appearance: Head-Short brown spiked hair, average features except for one large burn scar across his cheek. But that is covered by a kane- like mask. Orange flames come up from the bottom of the black mask. Upper Torso-Sleevless lightweight chain shirt. Another large burn scar can be found across his chest. Lower Torso-Loose-fitting black pants also with large orange flames shooting up the side. Boots-Opposite of pants: Orange with black flames on them. Entrance: The lights in the arena suddenly cut off as if someone had taken an axe to the cables. After a few moments of confusion with the fans, The Offspring's "Fear" blasts and with each bass drum beat, orange pyros fire from each side of the stage diagonally forming an "X." As soon as the vocals of Dexter Holland start in the pyros stop and a fire wall is formed on the top of the stage. While the music still plays, IgnitioN walks through the fire and slowly makes his way to ringside. your name: Ryan E.
