Entrance: The lights flicker and then go out, The crowd cheer as a mans voice speaks over the arena, He says "Welcome to my world of PAIN!" The Titan Tron area and the ramp glows red and as they do Papa Roach's 'Last Resort' blares out over the PA system The titan tron shows Flame hitting another wrestler with a chair and then him putting the same person through the roof of a car before the screen freezing and turning into black and white, The screen the goes black and the words 'Dark Avernger' scroll across it, When the two words collide they explode and the Word Flame is left on the screen, The lights come back on. Flame makes his entrance and Fire works erupt from the ramp, The crowd go crazy. Flame is wearing Black pants with a Flame going up each leg, black boots, Black elbow pads and wristbands and he is also wearing a customised american football shirt with the words 'Dark Avenger' on it and the number '00' on it aswell. He is also wearing a pair of stylish Dark Blue/Black shades and he is chewing some gum. Flame walks to the top of the Ramp and throws his fist in the air, The crowd explode and so do two rows of fireworks down the ramp as he walks down it in time with his foot steps. Flame reaches the end up the ramp and walks around to the side of the ring, He reaches up to the middle rope and pulls him self up onto the apron before turning around and climbing the turnbuckle and staring out into the crowd. Flame then jumps off of the turnbuckle and into the ring, He makes his way to the opposite corner and climbs to the second rope before raising his arms in the air to a loud cheer from the fans. Flame the climbs down and makes his way to the opposite corner, As he does he take off his shades and when he climbs the ropes this time he throws them into the crowd. That part of the crowd all surge together for a while trying to grab the sun glases before getting back to thier seats. Flame then raises his arms into the air and receives another loud cheer. Flame climbs down and walks around the ring into a corner, Flame pulls the Football shirt over his head and takes it off, Revealing his muscular body, He reaches out of the ring and hands it to a Timekeeper. Flame then stands in the corner waiting for his opponent. your name: Rael H.
