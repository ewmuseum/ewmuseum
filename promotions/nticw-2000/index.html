A fed that announced itself on Usenet in November 2000, ran Monday Night Mayhem and Thursday Night Showtime from its Homestead site, and in September 2001 moved into Primetime Championship Wrestling's family, where it shut down that December, came back in the new year and sold its roster away in June 2002. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The NTICW introduced itself on 21 November 2000 with a recruitment post whose owners described themselves as “experienced efedders and owners,” looking for skilled roleplayers and staff, particularly card writers, and pointing readers to its own site.

The post makes no claim beyond its own confidence, promising visitors would be “surprised by all we can give you, its all you ever wanted,” without naming a roster, a title, or a schedule.

No further post, roster, or card survives; what the NTICW actually ran, if anything, is not part of the record it left behind.

Successor to NTICW (2000) (2002-12)
The same name, a later fed: the NTICW of nticw.theewe.net calls itself the return of a jinxed fed that many owners had tried to start or revive, under new management at a new address, and crowned its own World Champion in a 2003 tournament rather than continuing the earlier NTICW's titles.
