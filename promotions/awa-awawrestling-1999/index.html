Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The American Wrestling Alliance, AWA, ran a website e-fed at awawrestling.net from 1999; the copyright line on its news page of February 2008 reads 1999-2008. Players applied, were accepted and roleplayed their matches on its boards, and a match went to whoever out-roleplayed the opponent. Strats, short plans sent in before a deadline, and run-ins added to it. It ran a weekly television show, Shock, and a second, all-strat magazine show, Exposed, which its FAQ likens to WWE Confidential, along with house shows and pay-per-views. The roleplay deadline for Shock and the pay-per-views was Saturday at 6 PM Eastern, and for the house shows Thursday at 6 PM Eastern. Its menu offered rules, an RPing guide, a history, title histories, pay-per-view indexes for 1999 to 2008, a hall of fame, a roster and an event calendar.

Its news page of February 2008 is a run of bulletins by its reporters. Travis Gray, owner and president, released the stipulations for the Vortex pay-per-view; a rift opened between Jeff Christianson and the AWA management; Quade deSade and Jeff Christianson joined a Montreal show run by Jeff Christianson Sr., where Gray's NPD, Rawstarr and Draven Shadows, attacked; and after the Triple Glass Table Match at Vortex the No Limits champion, Sir Nathaniel Henry Lee, was left in critical condition. By July 2008 its champions panel read Low as World Heavyweight Champion (won 30 June in London), Jason Storm as United States Champion (18 May), Quade deSade as No Limits Champion (13 July, in Paris), with the Cruiserweight and Tag Team championships vacant. The final show, Classic, was posted on 25 August 2008 with word that the AWA would close after nearly nine years. The card for a reunion show, Wrestlefest X, was posted on 8 December 2008, and the show itself on 21 January 2009.
