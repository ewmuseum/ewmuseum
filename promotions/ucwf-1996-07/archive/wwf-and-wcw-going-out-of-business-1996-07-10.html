Ultimate Championship Wrestling Federation — News

"WWF and WCW both going out of business!!"

10 July 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
**Well.. fantasy news that is. Read on. If you are interested in joining this new and exceiting e-wrestling fed simply e-mail [name withheld] at: [detail withheld] Or you can also get more info. by seeing his above post referring to the UCWF** [SCENE: The busy office of Vincent K. McMahon Jr. President and CEO of Titan Sports and the World Wrestling Federation. Vince has and outraged look upon his face as he talks to The Undertaker and Paul Bearer] VINCE: Mark.. you can't do this to me. Come on, I created you for cryin' out loud! It was bad enough when Hall and Nash jumped ship.. but now you? UNDERTAKER: There are only two things you can be sure of in life.. death... and taxes. All of my lights going on and off and on and off.. and the disappearing.. with the smoke. Over all these past years my bills have just skyrocketed! Its time that The Undertaker started making some real money.. my taxes need to be paid. Rest.. in Peace. [Undertaker and Paul Bearer disappear from Vince's office in a flury of darkness and smoke] VINCE: Wait! I... I... [Suddenly a buzz noise is heard. Vince answers the intercom.] SECRETARY: A Mr. Hickenbottem to see you Mr. McMahon. VINCE: Yes.. send him.. send him right in. Good 'ol Mikey.. won't fail me now. [Shawn Michaels comes strolling in with the WWF World Title over his shoulder] VINCE: Thank God your finally here! First it was Hall and Nash.. then Warrior, then Vader, then Austin, and then Undertaker of all people! Your practically the only guy I have left! SHAWN: Sorry Vin-Man.. but it looks like 'ol HBK is onto bigger and better things as well. VINCE: No!! How could you.. after all I've done for you! I'll see you all in court! SHAWN: No prob' Vin-Man.. with the amount of money this guy is throwing around, I can handle whatever your lawyers can throw at me. VINCE: What guy!?! Who is this man that is buying up all my talent, and stealing my business right out from under me?!? SHAWN: Settle down there big guy.. thats classified information there. VINCE: Does he think he can really waltz on in, with all his money and steal all my wrestlers.. its Turner isn't it? SHAWN: Sorry McMahon, but Billionaire Ted is in the same boat as you. VINCE: Well.. atleast if I'm going down I know I'll have someone else to drag down with me. Please Shawn, tell me who it is.. I need to start preparing little comedy sketches to make fun of him.. I need to think up a catchy name just like 'Billionaire Ted'! SHAWN: Sorry chief.. no can do. [Michaels reaches over and picks up a nearby trashcan which he proceeds to drop the WWF World Title into. Shawn struts out with a happy look on his face] VINCE: My empire.. it's crumbling. [Meanwhile.. in a place not much diffrent than Titan Towers, but somewhat of a distance away..] BISCHOFF: Mr. Turner you can't! TURNER: Look Eric. I've thought this out long and hard.. and my decision has been made.. it will be final. I'm dropping my control of WCW. BISCHOFF: But you can't! We already lost Hogan and Flair.. without your billions of dollars were absolutly nothing! TURNER: Look.. I've spent more money on this crap company than it is worth. I just can't invest anymore. I'm sorry Eric, you'll just have to go back to selling meat out of a truck in Minneapolis. [Bischoff looks at Turner as if he is a father figure, and with a tear rolling down his cheek he runs from the office. Turner smiles to himself before getting up from his desk, tearing down a WCW poster, and replacing it with a picture of the planet Earth] TURNER: Ahh.. my next big business venture. Should cost less than that wrasslin' federation anyways. [Fade out]
