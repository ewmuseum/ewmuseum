UCWF "Bash for the Belts" (15 Jul 1996), Part 3

15 July 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FINKEL: This next contest is set for one fall, and is the first match in the first round of the UCWF World Heavyweight Title Tournament! [Huge crowd pop] FINKEL: Introducing first.. from West Memphis, Arkansaw.. weighing in at 318 lbs. here is Psycho Sid!!! [Small pop as the 'Psycho' music fills the arena] FINKEL: And his opponent.. from Charllote, North Carolina.. acompanied to the ring by Woman and Miss Elizabeth.. and weighing in at 243 lbs. here is 'The Nature Boy' Ric Flair!!! [Pretty big pop as Flair makes his way to the ring 'stylin' and profilin'] [The match started with Sid trying to get his hands on Flair right away, but Slic Ric just dodged out of the way and escaped him to let out a huge 'Wooooooo!' which of course was followed by a 'woooooo!' from the crowd. After a few more minutes of this, Sid finally got his hands on Flair, and choked the life out of him. Flair escaped with a thumb to the eye, then backed Sid to the corner with a few punches followed by a few reverse knife edges. Flair climbed to the second buckle and pounded on Sid's forehead while the crowd counted '1,2,3,4,5,6,7..' Sid stopped it there by grabbing hold of Flair and dropping him with an inverted atomic drop! Flair crumbled to the canvas and Sid covered.. 1... 2.. kickout. Sid brought him back to his feet only to slam him back down once again, then off the ropes went Sid, coming flying back in for a elbow-drop.. but Flair moved, and Sid crunched to the mat! Flair then covered for a pin.. 1... 2... kickout! Flair kept the big man on the mat with a few more stomps and knee drops, before bringing him to his feet and whiping him off the ropes.. Flair went for a shoulderblock, but Sid was just to big, and took Flair down like nothing. Sid snapped him back to his feet, and hit a great suplex followed by a pin.. 1.. 2... kickout. Sid continued to work on Flair from there with bodyslam after bodyslam followed by a vicous choke-slam! Pin.. 1... 2... kickout. Sid, still in control, shot Flair across the ring to the corner, following closely behind him.. Flair hit the corner and flipped up over and onto the apron. Sid was coming up fast from behind, and connected hard to the corner with a high knee! Sid stumbled to the mat in pain, while Flair climbed the ropes and came off with an elbow right to Sid's leg! Flair grabbed hold of the leg, dropped a few more elbows, then gave another 'Woooo!' and wrapped big Sid up in the figure-4-leglock.. Sid gave within a matter of minutes.] [TIME: 22:32] FINKEL: The winner of this match.. 'The Nature Boy' Ric Flair!!! ROSS: A hard fought battle by both men, with Flair coming off on top! Surprisingly, no interference from Woman or Miss Elizabeth. HEENAN: Woooo! The Natcha' Boy has still got it! Wooooo! ROSS: I see your still a loyal Flair supporter. Well, Ric Flair will certainly need that support, especially since he will face the winner of this next match.. two men that Flair has really had problems with in the past.. Vader and Bret Hart! HEENAN: That's.. that's.. that's not fair.. it's not fair to.. ROSS: Bobby, we've heard that saying enough, it certainly is fair, and one of these two men will meet Flair at Wrestlemania!! FINKEL: This contest is scheduled for one fall, and is part of the first round of the UCWF World Heavyweight Title Tournament!! Introducing first.. led to the ring by his manager Paul Bearer.. from Denver Colorado.. weighing in at 450 lbs. here is Vader!! [A huge pop from the crowd as 'Vader time' echo's through the arena] FINKEL: And his opponent.. from Calgary, Alberta.. weighing in at 234 lbs. here is Bret 'Hitman' Hart!! [Big pop for Bret as he comes down the aisle slapping hands] [The two men circle each other, preparing for battle.. Vader strikes first going for a clothesline, but Hart ducks it and Vader smashes to the corner. Bret is fast to take advantage, planting several kicks into the big man's back, then rolling him up from behind for a pin.. 1.. 2.. kickout. Vader shoots right back up to his feet, lunges at Bret, and this time connects with a hard clothesline! Bret hits the mat hard.. Vader goes off the ropes and comes flying back in with a big splash and a pin.. 1... 2.. kickout. Vader stays right on top of Hart, dropping several knees, and nailing him with many hard fist shots. Vader finally drags him to his feet by his long string hair.. and with a real display of power.. presses Bret high over his head, and tosses him right over the top rope and to the floor! Bret lands hard, and Vader follows quickly. Outside the ring, Vader pounds on Bret for a few minutes before setting up for.. a powerbomb! Vader lifts Bret high over head, but Bret grabs hold of one of the ring ropes, and Vader follows through with the powerbomb.. with Bret not there of course.. he's hanging from the ring rope. Vader lands flat on his face on the cement floor.. and soon his face is pushed even further into the floor as Bret flies off the apron with a knee-drop right to the back of the head! Bret, now in control, throws Vader back into the ring.. he quickly follows.. grabbing Vader's legs and setting up for the Sharpshooter! Vader quickly powers out of it though, so instead Bret simply drops an elbow across the knee, then continues to work on the leg. Bret works away with elbow-drops and leg snaps before he ties Vader up in the figure-4-leglock! Vader seems to be in a whole lot of pain, as he rips the mask off his face, and tosses it at Bret. Vader eventually makes it to the ropes and Bret is forced to break the hold. Vader has problems getting back up to his feet, and instead drags himself along the mat to the corner.. Bret leaps over top of him though, plants a boot in the face, then climbs to the second buckle and comes off with a elbow right to the forehead! Pin.. 1... 2.. kickout. Bret, senceing victory, drags Vader to the centre of the ring and begins to tie him up in the Sharpshooter. Vader begins to power out, so Bret gives him a swift kick before quickly rolling him over and sinching in the move! Vader screams in pain as he tries to break the hold uneffectivly. The referee drops to the mat to be certain he can make the call the moment Vader gives in. While this is happening, Paul Bearer has climbed to the ring apron.. he grabs for Bret, but Bret moves his head.. again another grab and Bret avoids it.. and again. Finally Bret is so fed up he breaks the hold and goes after Bearer.. when he gets there though, Bearer has a little surprise in the form of an urn to the head! Bret collapses to the mat. It takes a few minutes before Vader can make his way to his feet.. once he does, he drags Bret to the corner.. scales the ropes to the second buckle.. and comes flying down with a Vaderbomb! After that it's 1.. 2... 3!!!] [TIME: 9:54] ROSS: Vader with the victory.. somewhat marred by interference though. HEENAN: Hart had no business going after Paul Bearer.. he got what was coming to him. ROSS: You really do believe that don't you Bobby? HEENAN: Of course I do.. what goes around comes around, and believe me, that stinkin' Bret Hart has had that coming for a loooong time! ROSS: You certainly are unbiased arn't you Heenan? Well.. I guess that makes it Vader against Flair in the semi's at Wrestlemania.. what a match that will be. Let's just see who will be the next to qualify for those semi-final matches at Wrestlemania.. two more matches to go, and they should both be great! FINKEL: The following contest is set for one fall, and is part of the first round of the UCWF World Heavyweight Title Tournament!! Introducing first.. from Las Vegas, Nevada.. weighing in at 356 lbs. 'Big Daddy Cool' Diesel!!! [Fairly big pop.. with a fair amount of boo's as well] FINKEL: And his opponent.. from Venice Beach, California.. weighing in at 275 lbs. here is 'The Immortal'... Hulk Hogan!!! [Mixed reaction] [Hogan doesn't even have time to tear his shirt off before Diesel is right on him with a hard knee to the back that sends Hulkster crumbling to the mat. Diesel continues to work on him with sevearl more stomps, the places the big boot on his throat and chokes the life out of him. Diesel pulled Hogan back to his feet, threw him to the corner, and placed the boot under his throat there, choking him once again. The referee finally got Diesel to back away, and Hogan got some time to recover. Diesel backed off for a few seconds while Hogan knelt on the ground, gasping for air, then BDC went back on the attack again as he attempted to pull Hogan back up to his feet.. Hogan fought out of it though, and caught Diesel hard with a fist right to the throat. Diesel lumbered back to the centre of the ring, while Hogan recovered, got to his feet, tore of his shirt, and went after Diesel with it.. chokeing him with it from behind. Hogan choked out Diesel all the way to the mat, then pounced on him for a pin.. 1... 2... kickout. Hogan got back up, dropped a few fists, then went for a pin again.. 1... 2... kickout. Hogan shot back up once again and flew off the ropes for an elbow.. Diesel moved though, and Hogan jammed his elbow hard into the mat! Diesel was quick to capitalize on the fallen prey.. stomping him right in the face sevearl times, then hitting him with a legdrop and a pin.. 1... 2... kickout. Diesel dragged Hogan back to his feet.. whipped him off the ropes, and went for a clothesline.. Hogan caught it though, and attempted a crucifix.. but missed and landed on his head. Diesel let out a big laugh, before lifting up Hogan, sending him off the ropes and catching him with a bearhug. Hogan tried to fight his way out, but Diesel was just to tough for him.. Hogan went limp in Diesel's massive arms.. the ref' raised Hogan's arm once... twice... a thir.. no.. Hogan stopped just in time and with a rake to the face got Diesel to release the hold. Hogan took a few steps and nailed BDC right between the eyes with a hard left. Hogan flew off the ropes and came back in with a clothesline. Diesel looked wobbly on his feet.. but still didn't go down. Hogan again with another clothesline.. BDC still wouldn't go down. Finally Hogan called for a powerslam, and with that.. executed it on the big guy, slamming him hard to the mat! Hogan posed to the crowd for a few moments before diveing onto him for a pin.. 1... 2.. kickout. Hogan tried to bring Diesel back to his feet.. but BDC used the ropes and Hulk's tights for leverage and sent him crashing to the floor. Diesel stepped out of the ring after Hogan and came down onto him off the apron with a forearm to the back. He then grabbed Hulkster and tossed him back into the ring. Diesel quickly followed.. picked up Hogan, and gave the sign for the jacknife! He set Hogan up.. went for it.. but Hogan blocked! Again another attempt.. again Hogan blocked.. and a back-body-drop by Hogan! Hulk began to get pumped.. doing his best imitation of a blowfish. Diesel charged back in for a punch.. Hogan blocked.. again.. another block.. Hogan with a left.. then a right.. and another left. Diesel stood in the centre of the ring looking groggy until Hogan shot off the ropes and nailed BDC with the big boot! Diesel fell hard to the mat and Hogan shot off the ropes and came flying back in with the big legdrop and a pin.. 1... 2... 3!!!!] [TIME: 21:51] ROSS: Hogan has proved right here who the best of the big men is.. it is the Hulkester, right there, your looking at him! HEENAN: I still lots of other big guys that could put Hogan to the test.. Diesel may have fell, but there is still Vader, Warrior, Undertaker.. and let's not forget the man he will have to face in the semi-finals at Wrestlemania.. either Luger or Michaels, and both of those men could very easily knock Hogan off his throne! ROSS: We'll just have to see, as we head down to the ring for our final match of the night! FINKEL: Ladies and Gentlemen, this contest is scheduled for one fall, and is the final match in the first round of the UCWF World Heavyweight Title Tournament! Introducing first.. from Chicago, Illinois.. weighing in at 265 lbs. here is 'The Total Package' Lex Luger!! [Small pop, but mainly booes] FINKEL: And his opponent.. from San Antonio, Texas.. weighing in at 234 lbs. here is 'The Heartbreak Kid' Shawn Michaels!! ['Sexy Boy' blares as Michaels makes his way to the ring with a strong crowd reaction] [Luger charged in before the bell had even rang.. Michaels was quick to catch him doing so though, and with a quick go-behind hit him with a belly-to-back suplex that landed Luger right on his head! Michaels shot back to his feet, removed the rest of his outer ring attire, and was just in time to meet a Luger clothesline right to the jaw. Michaels flipped over almost right on his head, and Luger jumped on top for a pin.. 1... 2... kickout. Luger brought him back up and sent him off the ropes once again, going for a clothesline.. Michaels ducked it, and a clothesline from Michaels that sent Luger to the mat! Shawn came running back in at Luger, but Lex was fast and hit Michaels with an armdrag out of nowhere. Shawn was back up again with Luger charging in, and this time Shawn hit an armdrag. Luger charged in once more, and again Shawn with an armdrag and into an armbar. Shawn held that for a few moments before Luger powered back up to his feet.. sending Michaels off the ropes once more, he caught him coming back in with a very impressive bodyslam! Luger then went for the pinfall.. 1... 2.. kickout. Lex wasn't done with him yet, as he picked him up to his feet.. flew him off the ropes once more, and caught him with a tilt-a-whirl type manuver which Shawn reversed into a headscissors taking Luger down to the mat. Shawn ran right back in again, nailing Lex with a running neck snap followed by a pin.. 1... 2.. kickout. Shawn dragged him back up to his feet and sent him off the ropes.. Luger ducked the clothesline and snuck in behind for a sleeperhold! Shawn was caught right in the centre of the ring with no escape in sight. Luger dragged him closer and closer to the mat until it looked like Shawn was totally out. The referee dropped the hand once.. twice.. but not three times as Michaels regained himself, and jammed Luger's jaw right to the top of his head! Luger fell backwards in pain as Michaels got to his feet and leveled him with a standing dropkick! Shawn jumped right on him once again for a pin.. 1.. 2... kickout. Shawn was still in control, and looked like he had the match in the palm of his hand as he sent Luger off the ropes once again.. Michaels leapt up in the air landing on Luger's shoulders for a frankensteiner!! But Luger blocked it.. reversing it into.. a powerbomb!!! Shawn's head connected to the mat hard, and Luger rolled on him for a pin.. 1... 2.. 3!!! [TIME: 13:59] ROSS: What a victory for Luger, I cannot believe it! HEENAN: Well start believeing it Ross, because Lex Luger is going on to Wrestlemania, and I predict that he will end Hulkamania once and for all! ROSS: You've predicted alot of things Bobby, but one thing that I can predict for sure is that Wrestlemania is going to be one hell of an event! So far we know it will be: Road Warriors-Horsemen, Ramon and Sting-Harlem Heat, Undertaker-Ultimate Warrior, Lord Steven Regal-Sabu, Ric Flair-Vader, and Hulk Hogan-Luger! HEENAN: Should be incredible! ROSS: There will also be many other added matches, and all the UCWF titles will be decided in one night! For Bobby Heenan, I'm Jim Ross saying, we hoped you enjoyed 'Bash for the Belts' from Osaka, Japan, and we will see you in New York for Wrestlemania! HEENAN: Come on Ross.. we gotta go, it'll take all tonight and tommorrow on the plane just to get to New York! ROSS: So-long everybody!! ******************************************************************************* [name withheld] "Education is a great shield against experience.
