UCWF, wrestling the way it should be

8 July 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
from the handler of such great e-wrestlers as Sinister Sam, the Headhunters, Cactus Kid, KO Kid Kevin Allan, Little Buddha and Wolf Storm Hey all, Just wanted to drop a line that the UCWF is still accepting wrestling for its new fed. Ever scream at the TV after another of VinnieMac's or Eric Pisshoff's blundered angles? Well now's your chance to show them you have what it takes to put together angles that'll fill the arena's. We're heavy on player participation, using our growing list of the sport's top names as our draws. And with recently acquiring the talents of [name withheld] as co-fed head we're excited with what the UCWF will be able to bring you the fans. So join up, all you have to do is send me the names of your four top wrestlers. As soon as we get enough I'll be holding tournaments for the feds three belts, the IC, World, and Tag Team belts. Wrestlers already spoken for: Hulk Hogan Mr. Perfect Ric Flair AA Chris Benoit Shane Douglas Justin Hawk Bradshaw Steve Austin Giant Dean Malenko Vader Undertaker Razor Ramone Sting Shawn Micheals Smoking Gunns Rick Steamboat Eddy Guerero Bret Hart that's it for me [name withheld] P.S. Hogan did it!!!! He turned heel!!!!!!!Truly the greatest angle in the history of the sport.
