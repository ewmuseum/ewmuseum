UCWF "Bash for the Belts" (15 Jul 1996), Part 2

15 July 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FINKEL: This next contest is set for one fall, and is the opening match in the UCWF Intercontinental Title Tournament!! About to enter the ring.. lead to the ring by Woman and Miss Elizabeth.. from Minneapolis, Minnesota, weighing in at 249 lbs. here is 'The Enforcer' Arn Anderson!! [Minor heel pop for AA] FINKEL: And his opponent.. *BONG* FINKEL: From Death Valley, accompanied by his manager Paul Bearer.. weighing in at 328 lbs. The Undertaker!! ['Taker gets a huge pop] [Things start out fast with Arn going right after UT with a clip of the leg, the several more hard hits to the back of the knee. Arn continues to work over on the leg of UT before he goes for a figure-4.. UT has to much fight in him though, and easily pushes Arn to the mat with a kick. UT does the zombie sit up and were back in business. Arn charges in again, but UT grabs him by the throat, climbs to his feet, and hits Arn hard with a chokeslam! Pin.. 1... 2... kickout. Anderson fled the ring to the comfort of his two lovely female escorts.. but there was no hideing from the 'Taker who followed him right out, and continued his attack on the outside, nailing him with everything before dumping him into the ring. Undertaker climbed back into the ring as Anderson backed to the corner, pleading with the Undertaker. UT hesitated for a moment, and that was all that Arn needed as he planted a fist right into his gut. Arn hit him with a knee lift and then a pin.. 1... 2... kickout. Arn worked over on him for a few more minutes, but it wasn't long before 'Taker was back on top again. UT pounded Arn all around the ring, and it looked as if things were just about wraped up when.. fellow Horsemen Cactus Jack burst onto the seen with a chair in hand! The crowd went nuts with a heel pop for Jack, and Bearer turned around just in time to dodge a swinging chair. Cactus climbed up onto the ring apron and held it ready to strike the 'Taker.. but Bearer was quick to grab hold of Jack's leg. Cactus tried to shake loose Bearer with no luck, and soon was nailed by a hard kick via The Undertaker! Cactus fell to the floor, dropping the chair which Bearer picked up and clocked him directly in the head with! With all the comotion outside the ring, Anderson managed to recover, grabbed Undertaker from behind and rolled him up for a pin.. 1... 2... kickout! Anderson, with a look of shock on his face, ran right back in again.. 'Taker caught him though, flipped him around and... Tombstone! Pin.. 1... 2... 3!!!] [TIME: 7:07] FINKEL: Here is your winner.. The Undertaker!! ROSS: The Undertaker advances in the tournament.. he will be in the semi-finals at Wrestlemania.. you are not going to want to miss this! HEENAN: Of course I won't miss it.. I'm a broadcast journalist for cryin' out loud! ROSS: I'm sure you will be there with bells on Bobby. HEENAN: I sure will be, but The Undertaker better be there with his working boots on, because he is going to have one hell of a fight against whomever he will face from this next matchup! FINKEL: Ladies and Gentlemen, this next contest is part of the first round of the Intercontinental Title Tournament! Introducing first.. from Warner Robbin, Georgia.. weighing in at 260 lbs. here is Ron Simmons!! [Simmons gets a decent pop for a man out of the ring for so long] FINKEL: And his opponent.. from Parts Unknown.. weighing in at 270 lbs. here is the Ullllltimate Warrior!! [Warrior gets a good pop, but nothing compared to that which he recieves in North America] [Warrior bursts into the ring and the bell sounds right away as Simmons charges in at him with a clothesline.. it barely phazes the Warrior who simply continues to shake the ropes. Simmons goes to the attack again with a punch, but Warrior blocks it.. another attempt, again Warrior blocks it. Warrior with a punch of his own, and onther, Simmons blocks that one.. Simmons goes for another roundhouse, but Warrior ducks under and nails him with an atomic-drop! Warrior hits the ropes, crosses the ring, hits the other ropes, and slams into Simmons with a hard shoulderblock! Pin.. 1... 2... 3!!!] [TIME: 00:30] ROSS: I can't believe it.. Warrior just walked through Ron Simmons like he was nothing! HEENAN: I thought Simmons was a tough guy, but wow! I can gaurantee you though, he will not have that easy a time with Undertaker! ROSS: Indeed I don't think he will.. remember folks, you can see it live from MSG in New York City, this Monday night.. Warrior against Undertaker, and only at Wrestlemania! HEENAN: I can't wait for this next match.. talk about technical wrestling, these men are the men you want to talk about! FINKEL: This next contest is scheduled for one fall, and is part of the UCWF Intercontinental Title Tournament! Introducing first, weighing in at 247 lbs. Here is Lord Steven Regal!! [Small pop] FINKEL: And his opponent.. from Minneapolis, Minnesota.. weighing in at 240 lbs. here is.. Mr. Perfect!! [Rather large crowd pop.. Mr. Perfect plays to the crowd] [Things start with a lock-up, quick go behind by Regal, drags the leg out from under him.. Perfect spins around right away, kick to Regal's chest sends him crashing backwards. Both men back up, Perfect right into Regal with a headlock-takeover. Regal tries to get out of it with a headscissors.. Perfect blocks it. Finally Regal gets him up, sends Perfect off the ropes, drops him with a shoulderblock. Regal covers.. 1.. 2.. kickout. Both back up again, Regal with a takeover.. but Perfect gets out with a headscissors. Regal tries to fight his way out and finally does, grabbing hold of one of Perfect's legs and snapping it back. Regal then drops several elbows, then turns Perfect over with a half-crab on the injured leg. They struggle for several minutes before Perfect finally reaches the ropes. Regal takes a few moments before finally releasing the hold. Perfect struggles to his feet before Regal kicks out his bad leg, and Perfect flips right over landing on his head. Perfect gets up again, and once again Regal does the same thing. A third time Perfect gets up, Regal goes for the leg sweep again, but Perfect moves his leg, and Regal falls flat on his back! Perfect drops a leg and gets a pin.. 1.. 2.. kickout. Perfect is righ back in and hits Regal with a running neck snap.. and a pin.. 1... 2.. kickout. Perfect gets back up and goes to grab Regal, out of knowhere though, Regal with a small-package.. 1.. 2.. kickout. Regal back in control whips Perfect to the corner.. Perfect hits hard and flips right up on the buckles connecting with his head to the ring post! Perfect sits atop the buckles looking dazed for a few moments before Regal runs back in and nails him with a belly-to-back from the top rope!! Both men land hard.. the referee begins to count both men down.. 1... 2... 3... 4... 5... 6... 7... 8... Regal rolls over and drops and arm onto the chest of Perfect.. 1... 2... 3!!!] [TIME: 10:19] ROSS: An awesome match that truly could have gone either way! HEENAN: Like I always say, if Perfect had kept me as a manager, he really could have gone places! ROSS: You, me, and everyone else knows that that was the best career move Mr. Perfect has ever made. HEENAN: What match were you watching Ross? Who won 'huh? I rest my case. ROSS: I guess we will just have to see if Regal winning was simply a fluke, or if he really has the talent to win in this tournament. To very worthy opponents are set to square off for the chance to meet Mr. Regal in the semi's. What a match this is going to be, I only wish Benoit were fresh for this match, to bad he already wrestled once tonight in tag action against the Roadies. Let's see if he can pull off a win for Robert Parker this time! FINKEL: Ladies and Gentlemen.. this is the final match of the first round of the UCWF Intercontinental Title Tournament! Introducing first.. led by his manager Col. Robert Parker.. from Edmonton, Alberta.. weighing in at 218 lbs. here is 'Crippler' Chris Benoit! [Huge.. I'm talkin' HUGE crowd pop from the japanese fans] FINKEL: And his opponent.. weighing in at 220 lbs. from Bombay, India.. here is Sabu!! [Almost as big a pop as for Benoit.. but not quite] [Sabu leaps into the ring and Benoit was right on top of him with several forearms to the back. Benoit grabbed Sabu by the hair, pulled him up to face him, and nailed him hard with a headbutt! Sabu seemed only a little stunned, and fought back with a headbutt of his own along with a few reverse knife edges. Sabu finally took Benoit to the mat with a dropkick that sent him almost sprawling through the ropes. Sabu leapt onto him with a splash and an attempted pin.. 1.. 2.. kickout. Sabu continued to work on him with several more kicks and stomps before bringing him to his feet, and shooting him across the ring and off the ropes. Sabu ducked down for a back-body, but Benoit stopped short and scooped Sabu into a back-slide for a pin.. 1.. 2... kickout. Sabu rolled backwards to his feet and was quick to get up catching Benoit with a kick to the jaw, then slamming him hard, dropping a leg, and going for a pinfall.. 1.. 2... kickout. Benoit was quick to take an advantage, grabbing Sabu by the leg and dragging him to the mat.. from there Benoit rolled him into a Boston Crab. Sabu was quick to get out of that though, as he made it to the ropes, and dragged both himself and Benoit to the floor. Benoit still had the advantage though, and went for a piledrive on Sabu onto the cement floor.. Sabu got out of that though, and sent Benoit to the floor with a back-body. Right away Sabu went for a steel chair. With a terribly loud *whack* Sabu caught Benoit hard atop the head with the chair. Benoit recoiled in pain while Sabu tried to get him to his feet. He finally did, and sat him down in the chair. Sabu ran back into the ring.. hit the ropes.. and came flying back out with a plancha onto the seated Benoit.. but 'The Cripper' had moved! Sabu landed face first on the chair and stopped moving entirely. Benoit had to drag the motionless Sabu back into the ring for the cover.. just to add the final touches though, he went up top for a flying headbutt.. of course.. Sabu moved. Seems Sabu can take a whole lot of punishment and still play possum. Sabu rolled Benoit up for a pin.. 1.. 2.. kickout. Sabu then decided it was his time to end this, he set Benoit up on the second buckle.. setting up for a frankensteiner.. Sabu leapt in the air, catching him with his legs, but Benoit blocked it and sent Sabu flying to the mat landing practically on his head. Benoit leapt off the second buckle for an elbow drop, but Sabu blocked with a kick to the face! Benoit collapsed to the mat and Sabu shot into the air again, leaping up top and splashing down onto Benoit with a split-legged moonsault! Pin.. 1... 2... 3!!!] [TIME: 14:39] ROSS: What a victory for Sabu.. looks like Parker is 0-2 tonight! HEENAN: Hold on there Ross.. don't forget about Harlem Heat winning. ROSS: How could I forget.. well, I guess that makes Chris Benoit 0-2 tonight.. but give The Crippler credit for a good showing here tonight. As you can plainly see, the fans here just love him! HEENAN: Those cheers are for me Ross. ROSS: I don't think so Bobby.. those cheers might be for Benoit, or they might just be for the fact that our first round of the World Title Tournament is coming up! ----- [name withheld] "Education is a great shield against experience.
