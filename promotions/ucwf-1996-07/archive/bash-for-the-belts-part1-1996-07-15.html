Both the beautiful landscape as well as some of the culture. We then see many shots of some of the best Japanese wrestlers in the world as well as the very talented wrestlers of the UCWF. Finally we fade into Osaka, Japan where there is a sold out crowd screaming at the top of their lungs as fireworks explode and loud music blares. Yes folks, UCWF is here. The camera pans across the crowd before coming to rest on the broadcast table where Jim Ross and Bobby 'The Brain' Heenan stand looking on..] ROSS: Welcome everyone to the 'new world order' as you might want to call it! Welcome to the Ultimate Championship Wrestling Federation! You are watching this broadcast live on the KKWW network, from a secret location here in Osaka, Japan. Only the wrestling elite have been invited here, and consider yourselves very lucky to even be viewing such an event. HEENAN: Usually I like lots of exposure.. but for the amount of money I'm being paid.. ha! ROSS: Easy there Bobby. Folks we have got a great card for you tonight. A card that maybe you had once dreamed of seeing. Why you might ask? For the simple reason that all the greatest wrestlers in the world are right here tonight.. all in one building, and all after one thing, and that is winning the UCWF gold! HEENAN: You want to see Heavyweights? We got 'em! You want to see tag teams? We got 'em. You want to see the greatest athletes in the world? We got 'em. They are all right here, right now and they are ready to go! ROSS: We are almost ready to go right here in a few moments with the tag team title tournament! Eight tag teams all battle it out tonight, and the winners of tonights matches will go on to face each other for the titles at our first ever Pay-Per-View event that will be going live around the world.. Wrestlemania!! HEENAN: That sounds familiar.. but in the summer? ROSS: Hush up Bobby. The UCWF purchased all the wrestlers from the big two federations, they might as well purchase all the PPV names as well. Anyway, you can catch UCWF Wrestlemania live from MSG in New York City, New York, Monday, July 15! HEENAN: Don't forget though Ross, that not only will the semi-finals and finals of the tag tournament be decided, but the same goes for the World Title and Intercontinental title as well! ROSS: Very true Bobby. Good to see your keeping on the ball. Now without any further ado.. let's get down to action with our first ever UCWF match!! [The fans all pop as the lights fade off of Ross and Heenan and the ring becomes flooded with light. Howard Finkel steps into the ring along with a Japanese interpretor whom repeats everything that Howard says..] FINKEL: Ladies and Gentlemen... this contest is scheduled for one fall, and is our opening match of the first round of the UCWF World Tag Team Tournament! Introducing first.. led by their manager Col. Robert Parker.. weighing in at a combined weight of 478 lbs. "The Crippler" Chris Benoit and Diamond Dallas Page!!! [Benoit gets a huge pop from the Japanese fans as he struts down the ailse taking it all in. Parker and Page get little reaction, and the little reaction that they do get are all boo's] FINKEL: And their opponents.. ['Iron Man' fires up over the P.A. and the fans go nuts] FINKEL: At a total combined weight of 587 lbs. From Chicago Illinois.. Hawk.. Animal.. The Road Warriors!! [The Road Warriors come charging down the ailse looking pissed as usual and do the Road Warrior slide under the bottem rope into the ring. As soon as they have slid in, Benoit and Page are on the attack! Benoit and Page pounded The Roadies to the mat before the two doubled up on Hawk and through him from the ring, then concentrated thier efforts on Animal. The referee finally gained some order and forced Benoit to take his place in the corner. Page continued to stomp on Animal, taking time in between each hit to strut around the ring and play to the crowd. Eventually the strutting gave an opening to Animal, and he seized it, grabbing Page from behind and rolling him up for a pin.. 1... 2... kickout. Page fired Animal off the ropes, went for a clothesline, but Animal ducked it and leveled Page with a clothesline of his own! Animal then splashed down onto him for a pin.. 1... 2... kickout. Animal quickly made the tag to Hawk who stormed into the ring and leveled Page with another clothesline.. Benoit dove into the ring and caught a stiff uppercut followed by a hard powerslam. Enough time was given to Page to recover though and he quickly clipped Hawk's knee out from under him. Page and Benoit were back on top again with a series of hard shots to Hawk as he lay on the canvas. Without a tag being made Benoit and Page switched places and it was now Benoit in the ring with Hawk. Benoit worked over on the leg of Hawk for a few minutes before lifting him to his feet and trying to put him away with a german suplex.. 1... 2.. kickout. Benoit pounded on him for a few more minutes before tagging out again to Page. DDP raced in and continued to work on Hawk's let with several elbow drops and some hard kicks. Page, feeling he had put enough hurt on Hawk, sat back in the corner and watched as Hawk tried to make it to his feet. Page gloated to the crowd and strutted around some more.. and once again this cost him as he came in to grab Hawk he caught a hard shot to the stomach that sent him stumbling backwards and falling through the ropes. This gave Hawk the chance to tag to Animal who came crashing into the ring and clotheslined Benoit right off the apron and to the floor. Col. Parker was racing about the ring screaming and carrying on as he tried to get his men to their feet. Finally Parker got Benoit back into the ring. Benoit and Animal locked it up almost evenly for several minutes before Animal finally got the upper hand and began to pummel on Benoit. Eventually Chris got in a quick shot to releave some of the punishment and made his way over for a tag to Page. Page raced in at Animal, but a shoulderblock drove him to the mat hard. Animal hit the ropes and came flying back in for an elbow-drop.. and a pin 1... 2.. kickout. Animal went to pick up Page once again, but instead caught a thumb to the eye. Page regained himself and for the next few minutes went to work on Animal.. eventually calling for Benoit to come off the top rope for some sort of manuver, while Page held Animal. Benoit complied and scaled the ropes, flying off for a headbutt right to Animals face.. but Animal ducked and Benoit connected with Page! Both Page and Benoit were nearly knocked right out. Benoit fell to the floor, while Page fell on his face in the ring. Animal raced over and tagged Hawk who climbed up the ropes while Animal put Page up on his shoulders for the... Doomsday Device!! Hawk pinned Page in the centre of the ring 1... 2... 3!!!] [TIME: 25:15] FINKEL: Ladies and Gentlemen.. the winners of this match.. The Road Warriors! ROSS: What a match! The Road Warriors, one of the greatest tag teams of all time advancing in the tournament over some very tough opponents, Page and Benoit! HEENAN: They are really going to have their work cut out for them no matter who they face from our next match up! ROSS: Indeed, the winners of our next match will face the Warriors.. without any more delay lets bring you that match! FINKEL: Ladies and Gentlemen.. this next match is part of the first round in the UCWF World Tag Team Title Tournament! Introducing first at a combined weight of 500 lbs. Led by their managers Woman and Miss Elizabeth.. 'The Franchise' Shane Douglas, and Cactus Jack!! [Both men get a fairly warm response from the crowd, as 'Perfect Strangers' by Deep Purple shakes the arena walls] FINKEL: And their opponents.. at a combined weight of 476 lbs. here are Ricky 'The Dragon' Steamboat and Eddie Guerrero!! [They get basically the same pop as The Horsemen did] [Things get under way with Steamboat and Douglas in the ring. The two former tag team partners stare each other down before finally locking up. Douglas shows his strength over the somewhat 'rusty' Steamboat as he shoves him back to the corner. Steamboat charges right back out again though, but once again showing the ring rust, steps right into an armdrag from Douglas. Both men get right back up and Douglas armdrags him again.. into an armbar. Steamboat quickly works his way out of it and sends Douglas off the ropes.. Steamboat drops down, then takes Douglas over as he comes back in with a hiptoss. Steamboat twists the arm of Douglas and drags him to the corner where he tags out to Guerrero. Guerrero leaps in the scales the ropes and drops a double-ax-handle across the arm of Douglas. Steamboat steps out and Guerrero goes to work, twisting the arm some more with a wristlock before he scales the turnbuckles, walks the ropes, then drops down on Douglas with a great Frankensteiner! Pin.. 1... 2... kickout. Guerrero works over on Douglas for a few more moments before slamming him and going out onto the apron. Guerrero stands for a few moments, playing to the crowd, before slingshotting himself over the top for his patent sommersault splash.. but landed hard on the mat as Douglas had moved. Douglas was quick to make the tag out to Cactus Jack.. the least Horsemenish of the Horsemen. Cactus took it to Guerrero hard.. pounding upon him inside and outside of the ring with Guerrero getting little offence in. This onslaught continued througout most of the match.. for about 10 minutes or more Guerrero was alone in the ring being pounded on by both Douglas and Jack. Finally by almost the 22 minute mark Guerrero got back into the game as he reversed a whip to the corner by Douglas then flew in and hit him hard with a monkey-flip! Guerrero quickly scaled the ropes and flew off for a frogsplash! He connected and went for the pin.. 1... 2... Save by Cactus. Finally Guerrero made the tag. Steamboat entered the ring in a big way, climbing the ropes and flying off with a double-ax to the head of Douglas. It was all Steamboat from there with several snap-suplexs and covers. Douglas was not giving in so easily. The end of the match came when Steamboat shot off the ropes coming in for a cross-body-block.. Douglas blocked it though, and out of knowhere hit a hard belly-to-belly suplex followed by a pin.. 1... 2... 3!!!] [TIME: 27:50] FINKEL: The winners of this match.. Shane Douglas, and Cactus Jack! HEENAN: Wooooooo!! Just more proof that Horsemen still rule the world! ROSS: We'll just have to find out about that come WrestleMania.. Shane and Cactus will really have their work cut out for them against the Warriors who scored a very impressive win tonight! HEENAN: Not to mention whomever they will have to face in the finals.. we still have two more tag tourney matches to go, both featuring some great wrestlers. ROSS: You gonna take back what you said about Horsemen ruling the world? HEENAN: Not a chance! I still got Anderson and Flair if we don't get the tag titles! ROSS: We'll have to see about that. Let's get back to Howard Finkel once again for our next matchup! FINKEL: Ladies and Gentlemen.. this next match is one fall, and is part of the first round of the World Tag Team Tournament! Introducing first.. led by their manager Paul Bearer.. at a combined weight of 547 lbs. Here is Sting, and 'The Bad Guy' Razor Ramon!! [Both men get a huge pop from the crowd] FINKEL: And their opponents.. at a combined weight of 352 lbs. here are the 1-2-3 Kid and Rey Misterio Jr.!! [The crowd pops huge for both men] [The Kid starts things off in the ring with Ramon.. the two long time enemies stare each other down before Razor lunges in for an attack. The Kid backs him off with several martial arts style kicks.. Razor takes a few steps back, then mocks the Kid with a few kicks of his own. Razor and Stinger both have a good laugh at that before Razor is blindsided by the Kid with a hard kick to the head! The Kid pounded Razor to the mat, then flung himself on him for a pin.. 1... 2... kickout. Razor sent Kid flying with a huge power kickout. Kid was right back up to his feet, and once again caught Razor with a kick to the temple. Kid brought Razor to his feet, then shot off the ropes coming in for a cross-body.. Razor caught him though, and set up for a fall-away slam.. Kid reversed that, and hit Razor with a headscissors that sent him spinning to the mat! Quick tag by the Kid to Rey Jr. who flew into the ring with a sommersault over the top rope into a leg drop! Quick cover by Rey.. 1... 2.. kickout. Rey kept Razor on the mat with a headscissors, Razor escaped that though, and powered his way atop Rey for a cover.. 1.. 2.. Rey bridges out and spins around for a backslide.. but it was reversed by Razor for a pin of his own.. 1.. 2... kickout. Both men shoot back up, but Ramon quickly catches Rey with a discuss punch that sends him down to the mat. Ramon quickly tags out to Sting for a huge pop from the crowd. Sting flies in and goes to grab Rey, but he's alread recovered, nailing Stinger in the face with a kick. Rey jumps out onto the apron, and comes flying back in with a springboard off the top.. Sting dodges it though.. or does he.. Rey grabs the arm of Sting as he comes flying down, and takes Stinger over with an armdrag! Both men up again, this time Sting the faster one, hitting Rey with a standing dropkick that sends him through the ropes to the floor. Before Rey has time to recover Sting slingshots over the top rope for a plancha! But Rey moves! Sting connects hard to the floor! Quickly Rey dives back into the ring and tags out to The Kid. The Kid flies up to the top rope and sommersaults off at Sting.. but this time Stinger moves, and The Kid lands hard to the floor right on his back. Sting takes a few moments to recover before tossing The Kid back to the ring. Sting works on him for a few minutes before tagging back out to Ramon. Razor comes in and has his way with The Kid.. finally hitting the fall-away-slam just as he wanted to before. Pin.. 1.. 2.. kickout. Ramon and Sting doubled team on The Kid before it is once again back to Sting and The Kid. The Kid tried to mount some offence but it was little too late.. Kid came off the top rope with a flying body-press, but Sting had enough in him to reverse the move and score a pin.. 1... 2... 3!!!] [TIME: 11:27] FINKEL: The winners of this match.. Sting, and Razor Ramon!!! ROSS: Another team bites the dust.. I had really high hopes for the team of Rey Misterio Jr. and 123 Kid. HEENAN: I think power just won out over speed tonight.. as it always did for me in my wrestling days. ROSS: Yeah, your opponent was always the one winning out, although I don't know how you could consider yourself 'speed'. HEENAN: You should of seen me in those days Ross.. I put these kids to shame.. ROSS: Enough Bobby, its a long night and we have quite a way to go still.. this is the last match of the tag tourney, winners will meet Sting and Ramon at WrestleMania! FINKEL: This contest is for one fall, and is the final match of the first round of the World Tag Team Title Tournament! Introducing first, at a combined weight of 499 lbs. Here is the team of Goldust.. and 'The British Bulldog' Davey Boy Smith!! [Gold glitter falls from the ceiling as Goldust makes his way to the ring to a chorus of boo's. Davey Boy tries to avoid the glitter.. and Goldust as much as possible as he gets somewhat of a pop from the fans] FINKEL: And their opponents.. accompanied by Col. Robert Parker and Sister Sherri.. at a combined weight of 550 lbs. Here are Booker T.. Stevie Ray.. Harlem Heat!! [The Heat's theme music rocks the arena as they get a mixed reaction from the crowd.] [Things start out.. strange (of course with Goldust..) as the Golden one tries to become good friends with The Bulldog. Davey doesn't seem to like that idea too much, and exits the ring. This leaves us with Goldust starting with Booker T. Goldust does his usual stuff, and recieves a punch in the mouth from Booker. Booke whips him off the ropes and catches him coming in with a back-body-drop! Booker quickly tags to Stevie Ray who jumps into the ring and begins to stomp on Goldust. After a few more minutes of beating the crap out of Goldust, Ray goes for a cover.. 1.. 2.. kickout. Ray begins to bring him to his feet, but catches a hard shot to the groin! Goldust drags the doubled over Ray to the corner and tags to Davey Boy. Goldust holds down Ray, fondeling him in the corner before Davey shoves Goldust out of the way and begins to go to work on Stevie. Davey drags him to the centre of the ring and slams him hard before hitting the ropes and coming off with a legdrop.. and a cover.. 1... 2... kickout! Davey pulls him back up and goes for a suplex.. blocked by Ray, and turned into a small package.. 1... 2.. kickout. Ray drags Davey to the corner, slams his head to the boot of Booker T, then makes the tag. Booker comes in and levels Davey with several chops, followed by a devistating spin-kick! Davey collapses to the mat and Booker goes up top! Once the Bulldog had finally reached his feet, Booker came down hard with a double-ax to the head that sent Davey falling back to the mat.. another pin.. 1... 2... kickout. Booker goes back to the corner and tags out to Ray who blasts into the ring and knocks Davey to the mat once again with a hard shoulderblock. Ray scoops him back up again and slams him down hard to the mat.. once again Ray picks him up and this time.. a bulldog to the Bulldog! Ray makes the tag once again to Booker who begins climbing the ropes again. Ray slams Davey to the mat as Booker sets up for The Harlem Hangover! Booker flies off the top.. but Bulldog moves! Booker lands hard to the mat. Bulldog takes this chance to go for a tag. He begins to walk to the corner.. sees Goldust who stands and gives Davey a "WSSSSSSHHHHHH" and Bulldog changes his mind about that tag, mouthing the words "forget it!" Before he knew it though, Booker T came charging in with a superkick right to the jaw of the Bulldog followed by a pin.. 1... 2... 3!!!] [TIME: 9:47] FINKEL: The winners of this match.. Harlem Heat!! ROSS: Booker T and Stevie Ray advance in the tournament, they will meet with Sting and Ramon this coming Monday only on PPV.. it's WrestleMania!! HEENAN: This is going to be great.. most of the matches from WrestleMania aren't even signed, and already this is a great PPV! ROSS: So far we have Road Warriors against Douglas and Cactus, as well as Ramon and Sting against the Heat! HEENAN: And we still have the first rounds of the I-C Tournament, and World Tournament to go! ROSS: Let's not waste any more time.. let's get back to the ring!
