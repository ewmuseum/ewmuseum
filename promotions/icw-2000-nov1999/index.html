Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

International Championship Wrestling 2000 introduced itself on 14 November 1999 with a full recruiting handbook, and opened a week later with a sixteen-man tournament to crown its first International Champion.

Its debut broadcast, ICW Conflict (21 November 1999), was run over almost as much by its own on-screen Commissioner, “The Goldenboy” Christopher Scarlet — introduced as a former XBWL World Champion — as by the bracket itself: Scarlet overruled the tournament's own results more than once, ordering fresh triple-threat matches so a wrestler he felt deserved another look could keep advancing. One of those rulings favored The English Assassin, who won his own do-over match after an outside tag team, Buzzkill and X Factor, ran in and attacked his opponent mid-match.

That interference came back on its authors: after the broadcast, Beanie and Johny — the two wrestlers Buzzkill and X Factor had targeted — left X Factor busted open on camera, and Scarlet closed the show by ordering all four men into a tag match the following week to crown the promotion's first International Tag Team Champions, alongside the tournament's second round.

No further document survives; whether that second card, or the promised tag title match, was ever held isn't in the surviving record.
