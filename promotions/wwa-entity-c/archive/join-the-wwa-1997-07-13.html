I am the Prez of an e-fed called the World Wrestling Association. If you want to join, please go to http://www.geocities.com/colosseum/loge/1716/index.html Real or fake wrestlers are allowed, and only Jimmy Garvin and Papa Shango have been taken. There are currently 10 wrestlers in the fed. The homepage is not currently finished, but that's because I need about 10 more people to run a card. If you don't have frames capability, I suggest you upgrade because you will not be able to see the homepage. It is booked and simmed, so you must roleplay, and it is ran by the best wrestling sim there is, TNM. The webpage is usually updated daily and will soon have a java based chat. There are 2 cards a week, one on Sunday, and one on wednesday. One of them will be live. It would help if you were a face, (we only have one). If you have any questions E-mail me and I will be happy to help. The current belts are World IC US World Lightweight/Cruiserweight World Tag US Tag Please consider joining. We might have more cards or house cards in the future. Also there is one PPV a month. Prez Sir Joshua P.S., I'm also looking for feds to join, I have a tag team named the Pretty Boys, and a single wrestler named Erric "Doc" Strange. Let me know if there is room for me.
