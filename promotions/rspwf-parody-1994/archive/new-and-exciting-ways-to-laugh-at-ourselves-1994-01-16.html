New and Exciting Ways to Laugh at Ourselves

16 January 1994
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
UH... guys... with the amount of bitching that's been going on here as of late,
I think it's time to bring back one of the funnier things I ever remember seeing on here...
The RSPWF Bandwidth titles. Seriously. Since [name withheld]'s thing has faded into oblivion, it's time we laughed at ourselves again.
My (brief) list of matches are as follows: A 4-corners match to fill the vacant RSPWF Bandwidth Title... "Killer" Herb Kunze vs. Evan "Tex" Schlesinger vs. "Diamond" Dave Scherer vs. Andy "The Animal" Patrizio To confuse things a little, you have to be pinned (or whatever) twice in order to be totally eliminated, and a barbed wire baseball bat will be suspended 15 feet above the ring, available to whoever nabs the ladder waiting outside the ring. And to add insult to injury, Ric Flair will be the guest referee.
"My Odds Are Better. No, _MY_ Odds Are Better" match for the RSPWF Flyweight title... [name withheld] vs. Tony Shubert (aka That JAMMA Guy) Best of three falls. Steel cage 2-of-3 falls match for the vacant RSPWF Tag Titles... The [name withheld] Brothers, Barry and Jeff vs. The Ohio Connection: [name withheld] and Pete Barlow And of course the RSPWF Southern title, with "Quebec Province" rules... "Hacksaw" [name withheld] (with "Little Cactii" [name withheld]) vs. Chrispy Palacios There will be a special added feature for that match... the ring will explode after 15 minutes.
Sorry. I'm really bored. Pete.
