-Zach NBW Prez and Handler of Rory Braden (UWF, MVW) and Super Deluxe (KCW, APWA) **** NBW PLAYER'S GUIDE v. 1.0 --- NEW BLOOD WRESTLING: The rules have changed. --- The year is 2001. The sport of wrestling has hit its peak. The decline is inevitable. In fact, some say it's already begun. Talent is spread thin.
Ratings are down. Interest is dropping. And the more time progresses, the more it becomes abudantly clear: the status quo CANNOT remain. The industry cannot progress by standing still. It MUST move forward.
For wrestling to remain mainstream, a change must take place. In the mid 90's, wrestling became a part of everyday life. Today, as wrestling ratings slide and interest fades, a drastic change has to be made in order for professional wrestling to succeed.
That change has been made. --- THE ESTABLISHMENT --- NBW is the brainchild of two of the most notable figures in pro wrestling over the last several years -- Dallas "Perfection" Reed and Jeremy Steele. Friends since their professional debuts in the late 90's, the duo have collaborated to create their greatest contribution to the industry. After both retired from active competition due to its stale nature, they decided that something had to change -- they loved the business too much to watch it die.
So they took it upon themselves to say it. NBW is in their control and theirs alone. They have final power, shared equally. And they are the ones that created the system. --- THE SYSTEM --- The System. A simple set of rules and innovations created by the Establishment to change the way the business works and the wrestlers think. The wrestlers that compete in NBW will be locked into the wrestling arena hours before each show and will be forced to stay two hours after each show. Lock in times will be announced, and if wrestlers do not show up by the appropriate time, they will not be wrestling that night. There will be a rankings system based on overall peformance. Wins and losses will matter, but a strict record won't be the only basis for these rankings. Without a top ranking, wrestlers will not recieve a title shot.
By holding a title, not only does a wrestler earn prestige, but he has the opportunity to earn more money. Each day a wrestler holds the World title, the per diem amount of his contract will be doubled. For the secondary title, the amount increased by fifty percent.
Rules inside the ring will be strictly enforced. Disqualifications and countouts will be looked down on heavily. Inside the ring, things will be run like they used to be -- fairly -- but backstage, anything goes. The wrestlers have free reign to do as they please. However, as stated in their contracts,
NBW will not be liable for any injuries that happen outside of the ring. In addition, all wrestlers under contract must agree to wave their right to privacy while in the arena where the NBW event is being held. Cameras will be placed in every locker room and every hallway. Everything will be monitored and fair game to be shown to the viewers. A reporter will roam the backstage area to get word from the wrestlers in between matches.
The envirornment will not be pretty. But it will breed competition. It will force wrestlers to rely on their skill and toughness. They will EARN their glory in NBW.
--- THE PRODUCTIONS --- NBW will produce two weekly television shows each week. Most shows will emulate from the rennovated warehouse turned wrestling arena -- the Cell -- in
Minneapolis, Minnesota. Dallas Reed has brought the Diamondviewer from his previous promotion to provide a wide view for all the audience to what is going on both in the ring and behind the curtain.
Tommy Vaughn and Mitch Decker will host SEEK AND DESTROY on Wednesday nights from 8-10CST, with the lock in beginning at 6PM and ending at midnight.
Tommy Vaugnn and Tony Adams will host NO ESCAPE on Sunday nights from 9-11CST, with the lock in beginning at 5PM and ending at 1AM.
Each show will feature an undetermined amount of matches -- some booked in advance, some made up on the spot. Wrestlers must be ready at all times. But only those that impress will get the chance to prove themselves. Only the wrestlers with a true passion will be driven enough to shine in NBW's spotlight.
--- THE TITLES --- NBW has three titles up for grabs. The World and Tag Team championships will ensure their holder double the pay day, while the Northern title will lead to a
50% increase. Only the elite will get a chance to hold these titles, and as such, only the very best of the best will wear them.
--- THE STAFF --- JEREMY STEELE, co-owner. Thirty years old, retired, with wife and young son. Old school wrestler with an amateur background and Midwestern values. Fan friendly, but has an aggressive side when pushed.
DALLAS "PERFECTION" REED, co-owner. Currently inactive from active competition. His ego is almost as large as his list of accomplishments. A hometown hero in Minnesota, but still the quentessential bad boy to everyone else.
TONY ADAMS, head announcer. Long the voice of GFWA, Reed brought him along to ensure an experienced voice was in the booth to call the action.
MITCH DECKER, play by play, late of A1 Pro, a DJ-turned-announcer from California who is knowledgable, but easily caught up in the excitement. NBW hopes his enthusiasm is contagious.
TOMMY VAUGHN, color commentator, also an ex A1 Pro employee. His cynical edge and East Coast background provide the perfect foil to both Mitch and Tony, allowing him to slip into the co-owner's former spot as #1 color man.
JACK KING, ring announcer. Another Reed holdover. --- THE FORMAT --- Let us know step out of character for a moment to explain things further. The premise is interesting enough. You're intregued. But how does this thing come to life in the world of e-wrestling? Very simple.
Cards will be written in a specialized summary fashion. Imagine reading an online recap of a show, part commentary on the moves and holds, part analysis of the goings on with analyzation, part transcription of interviews, part humorous observation. This is what will be presented twice a week. Pre-face it with a short packet of RP's that take place before the lock down occurs and that's really it.
Only PPV's will be full commentary. Saves both you and us time and creates more interest and an air of importance to the bigger cards. And it's different.
To add the element of realism, some matches will be announced ahead of time, some will not. Usually, the handlers themselves will always know, to ensure that they write up something for the show. A pre-card RP is fine, as are summarized in-rings, backstage interviews, or any attacks, guest appearences, et cetera that you can think of.
Basically, we want NBW to be highly interactive. It will be angle intensive and character driven. Strong gimmicks, strong storylines, and smart writing.
