NWI Turbulence (2/27/06)

Let me start with the obvious question: exactly what were you and Kevin Richards discussing so heatedly during last week's battle royal. JACOBS: Simply put, I was trying to tell him that I wasn't going to go after him out there; that if it came to it, I'd eliminate myself. He was saying something about Lura when we were attacked, so you'll have to ask him what point he was getting to. Yes, the violence level is beyond what many would call "normal", but he is far from insane when he's in the ring. Same with me. Anyone can pick up a chair and swing it, but it takes a certain degree of thought to set up a table in the middle of a match. You don't set up your mayhem if you don't have your head on straight. LR: But would you allow a Kevin Richards to headline a promotion like NWI? JACOBS: Absolutely. We do the hardcore stuff because we want to, not because we have to. LR: And what does Dixie Hart think about this? JACOBS: She and I had a long talk about Kevin long before we had that contract made up. She knows how miserable I was when I split up the Blood Brothers last year.
