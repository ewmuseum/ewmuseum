Wishbone Wayne Hanson vs. Cooter Lee Roy 1.1 Look Back AAPW PRIMETIME, Saturday, September 27, 1997 Cameron Indoor Stadium, Duke University, Durham, N.C. - "Too Slick" Rick Brown defeated "The Ambusher" Mike Trent at 13:55 with The Landing Post II <shooting star press legdrop> - The Activists From Hell defeated The Choir Boys when Rage gave Murff 'The Censor Killer' at 4:27 - J.J. Wishbone defeated "Hands of Stone" Vernon Stancil at 16:55 with the Attitude Adjustment Piledriver - "Cactus" Bubba Steed defeated "The Demon" Damien Lestat at 8:01 when Black Flagg interfered - "The Miracle Man" Tyler Smythe and K-Solo fought to a no decision - The Suicide Blondes defeated Apocalypse Now at 15:13 and retained their Atlantic Tag titles - Junior Mafia defeated "PRIMETIME" Danny Ice at 13:49 with the top-rope piledriver - "The Technician" Michael Leonard defeated The White Angel and "Dangerous" Danny Dynamite in a three-way-match at 20:55 - The Punishers defeated The Trailer Park Assassins when Frost rolled up Orbit after Somerset blew him a kiss.. 1.2 House Show Reports AAPW CAMPUS TOUGHMAN CHALLENGE!!, Sunday, September, 28, 1997 Stephen O'Connell Center, University of Florida, Gainesville, Florida -'The Ambusher' Mike Trent defeated 'Hands of Stone' Vernon Stancil in 13:09 after a piledriver on a chair. Trent moves on. -The American Enforcer defeated 'The Perfect Male Speciman' Jason Speece after 11:28 while pinning Speece in the third row. Enforcer moves on. -The Overgiver lost to 'The Demon' Damien Lestat after Lestat hit the 'Hoof' Elbow drop off the top rope through a table. Lestat moves on. -Black Flagg pinned 'Maverick' Michael Reyna in 10:01 after nailing a powerbomb on the concrete floor. Flagg moves on. -Junior Mafia squeaked past Gemini in 19:32 after the suplex into a piledriver on Gemini. Mafia moves on. -'Yukon' Jack Roberts and K-SOLO battled everywhere and FINALLY Roberts got the pin after throwing Solo into the bathroom mirror. Roberts moves on. -J.J. Wishbone was defeated by 'Cactus' Bubba Steed after a 'Cactus Drop' off the top rope. Steed moves on. -AAPW Heavyweight Champion, Wayne Hanson defeated Cooter Lee Roy after nailing the guilotine leg drop off a ladder in 20:02. Hanson moves on. PRIME Monday Night, Monday, September 29, 1997 Hampton Coliseum, Hampton University, Hampton, Va. TMM=Toughman Match. -'Too Slick' Rick Brown defeated 'The Italian Senor' Pablo Santuccio in 10:24 after a moonsault. -The Dynasty of Destruction defeated The Bonecrushers in a no-DQ match after a Falcon chokeslam on Aaron Lankford in 23:07. -TMM: 'The Ambusher' Mike Trent defeated The American Enforcer after nailing The Enforcer with a diving chair. Trent moves on to the semis. -The Suicide Blondes retained the Atlantic Tag-Titles after defeating The East/West Coast Killaz in 16:46. -TMM: Black Flagg defeated 'The Demon' Damien Lestat, after Diamond Cutting Lestat onto his own cooler in 23:43. Flagg moves onto the semis. -'The Showstopper' Simon Lebec retained his Atlantic Title by defeating J.J. Wishbone. -TMM: 'Yukon' Jack Roberts pinned Junior Mafia after powerslamming him through a table in 15:32. Roberts moves onto the semis. -The Overgiver retained his TV Title by defeating Dane Phipps after nailing the 'Overbomb' in 12:34. -TMM: 'Cactus' Bubba Steed shocked Wayne Hanson with a small package in about the 14th row in 25:08. Steed moves onto the semis. Tuesday Night Superstars, Tuesday, September 30, 1997 UTC Arena, University of Tennessee-Chattanooga, Chattanooga, Tn. -'Dashing' Derek Lankford retained his Light-Heavyweight Title against Pablo Santuccio with the Lankford Leglock in 10:42. -The Cutting Edge defeated The Players Club after nailing Direct Hostility in 12:04. -The Crow defeated 'Hotdog' Mack Brown with 'The Big Goodbye' in 6:52. -Michael Leonard defeated The Fallen Angel in 22:38 after a gordbuster. -'The Miracle Man' Tyler Smythe defeated Gemini in 19:35 after hooking on the 'Smallest Package' -The Punishers retained the Tag-Team Titles by defeating The Trailer Park Assassins by DQ, when The Dynasty of Destruction interferred. -'The Showstopper' Simon Lebec retained the Atlantic Title by defeating The White Angel in 20:35. -The Dynasty of Destruction and Apocolypse Now went to a double DQ when The Punishers and The Trailer Park Assassins ran-in. -Junior Mafia and 'Cactus' Bubba Steed went to a 20:00 draw. -Wayne Hanson retained the AAPW Heavyweight Title by defeating Black Flagg with the guillotine legdrop. 2.0 PRIMETIME Review with "The Franchise" [name withheld] A R E V I E W O F * * ****** * * * * * * * * * * * * * ** * ******************* * * * * * * * * * * * * * * ** * * * * * * * * * * * ****** P R I M E T I M E ****** LIVE!!! From Cameron Indoor Stadium, Duke University, Durham, N.C. Interview #1: Somerset Somerset had The Suicide Blondes ready for their match with Apocalypse Now. She also spoke of The Tokyo Blades. In any case, the Atlantic Tag-Title match should be a "breaze" if you listen to Somerset.. but a good match if you listen to others. :-) Interview #2: "Cactus" Bubba Steed This guy is cool. It's always great to see a different style of "talking" such as Steed's. He spoke of his match with Lestat as well as a probable rematch with The Overgiver. It was funny when he was drinking a coke and then it said (a coke?) again.. due to the fact that he usually drinks "stronger" things. :-) Interview #3: "The Demon" Damien Lestat I liked the greenhouse scene in this one. He grabs a cactus (since he's facing "Cactus" Bubba Steed) and his hand bleeds.. cool. A little too much swearing in it for my tastes, but a nice flash none the less. Match #1: "The Demon" Damien Lestat vs "Cactus" Bubba Steed Fisticuffs.. pure fisticuffs and out and out brawling.. Even a Garvin Stomp in there. Then it went outside, which doesn't matter with Scarb as the ref. Lestat had a cactus in his cooler and threw it at Steed's groin... hope he was wearing a cup. The ref got laid out as well and Lestat used his cooler to bash Steed over the head repetitivly. Then Black Flagg came out and hit his Dominator diamond cutter on Lestat. He puts Bubba on Lestat for the 3 count. Anybody see a Flagg/Lestat match coming up? Interview #4: Black Flagg Attack the interviewer.. how nice. Real short interview. Basically just said Lestat shouldn't have interfered in his business. Two guys the fans don't like, but it should be quite a match when they meet. Interview #5: K-Solo Solo said he was happy because he got this matchup. He also spoke about J.J. Wishbone and his joining of The Trifecta. Not to mention speaking of The Overgiver. K-Solo definitly has picked a group of enemies, which he hoped to start eliminating with Smythe.. Interview #6: "The Miracle Man" Tyler Smythe The former Atlantic champion asking Lebec how he liked having something that belonged to him, stolen. He then went on to talk about Solo and his manager, Miss Patty (ie: the kissing incident). He said how it "pained" him because of that very incident. Tyler then went on to reflect back on the prior matches between he and Solo.. K-Solo's win in the EPOW tournament and the GIWF U.S. Title Defense by Smythe. His closing coment was cool too.. "The Trifecta is complete and we're going to put a chokehold on the AAPW." Match #2: K-Solo vs "The Miracle Man" Tyler Smythe K-Solo started out strong with an attack.. A quick Belly to Back on the concrete even. But eventually, he missed a 2nd rope elbow drop and Smythe took over. Later, after Solo regained the control, J.J. Wishbone came down. Solo hit a Tigerbomb and went for The Solo-Breaker, but Wishbone came in for the DQ. Smythe got the Figure 4 on Solo as Wishbone and Rogers stomped his head. Junior Mafia then ran down the aisle with a chair to clear house, but then he and Solo went at it! Wishbone and Smythe talked in the aisle about the scene, which was quite interesting. They even challenged Mafia and Solo to a match next week. Interview #7: Apocalypse Now Apocalypse Now are ready for their title match. They wanted The Styles, but as we know, they're contracts have been terminated. So, they get something even better.. A chance to become Atlantic Tag-Team title holders. Unique thing in the flash, with the heartbeat getting faster and faster and then flatlining. Match #3: Apocalypse Now vs The Suicide Blondes for the AAPW Atlantic Tag-Team Titles Conner took the early advantage against Warlok. Then King did the same. However, once Karnage got in he started taking The Blondes apart. That didn't last too long though. It was back and forth the entire match with great moves performed by both teams. A Suicidal Superplex couldn't get the 3 count because Karnage broke the count. On the outside, Somerset hit Karnage with her phone, but with no effect, and he grabbed her! Gemini then hit Karnage with a chair though. King hit a Michinochu Driver with an assist from The Massive One and got the 3 count. Interview #8: Junior Mafia His reaction to losing the title.. Basically, just that Hanson got the title with outside help, and Mafia did it all himself. Then Mafia got upset because he'll be facing J.J. Wishbone at the PPV instead of getting a rematch for the title. The coolest comment was directed towards Danny Ice as well as the whole AAPW, it was: "The AAPW is the house that Mafia built, and you're gonna get evicted." Match #4: Junior Mafia vs "Primetime" Danny Ice 2 former champions.. power vs speed.. brawling vs aerial.. Mafia dominated most of the early matchup. Ice was on the defensive most of the match. He'd hit a few moves but then miss something or have to recouperate. Junior Mafia pulled a page out of Ice's book with a Rana, but just a 2 count. And Ice did the same as he executed a Tigerbomb out of another Frankensteiner attempt, again just for a 2 count. Mafia hit a piledriver off the top rope for the 3 count. Commercial #1: The Cutting Edge vs The East Coast/West Coast Killaz Advertizing the match coming up on SNS.. Laser and Blade had their biggest win ever last week. We'll have to see if they can pull an upset again against 2 of Street Level. Interview #9: "The Technician" Michael Leonard Very short flash from Leonard. Basically just said he was going to win this match. Interview #10: The White Angel The man who's been in AAPW the least amount of time probably the favorite in the upcoming match. It was another short flash as Fugiama and White Angel said that they were looking to win the match. Match #5: "The Technician" Michael Leonard vs "Dangerous" Danny Dynamite vs The White Angel Derek Lankford with guest commentary during this match. The winner was billed to get a title match against him next week. Pretty even match to start. Dynamite then got a garbage can and although not really his style, he took control and hit Leonard in the head with it and then suplexed White Angel onto the can. The White Angel hit his moonsault on 3-D, but he kicked out. Leonard hit The Techdriver on White Angel, but a kickout there as well. Dynamite Death Drop on White Angel, and Leonard jumped on for extra weight and eliminated White Angel. As per my original comment, this surprized me that he was the 1st one out. Techdriver on the garbage can again followed by The Technical Chamber submission move and gets the submission. Quite a matchup. Interview #11: "The Showstopper" Simon Lebec Although banned from TV, he showed up anyway. The Atlantic champion came out saying he was told to appologize, but instead he came out and brought out Tyler Smythe's so called son, "Joey". A leash around the retarded young boy.. absolutly dispicable. Obviously, it wasn't true, but it still makes the fans hate Lebec even more (not that they like Smythe, mind you). He even made up a song and laughed about the kid and all. Sheesh. But that wasn't even enough, he headbutted the boy. Man.. This guy shouldn't just be suspended.. he should be kicked out of the AAPW all together. We don't need that kind of stuff on tv.. but then again.. :-) Interview #12: The Trailer Park Assassins Orbit preoccupied with the RSPW-F Top 200 tag-team list, but got back into it with his smile and spit. These guys showed why they were ranked so high on it.. another great flash from the men from Weasel. Interview #13: The Punishers The Punishers as confident as ever, as they probably should be since they're the champs again. They spoke of their match with TPA.. insulting them and saying how they shouldn't be getting a title shot til they've proved themselves more (or something like that). As usual, they spoke for a long time about how great they are. They also said things that have gone down with them in this and other federations. Match #6: The Trailer Park Assassins vs The Punishers for the AAPW Heavyweight Tag-Team Titles This was a very good matchup and looked to be a clean one too, until we saw Somerset walk down the aisle. Orbit was gonna go for his STF, but Somerset blew a kiss to him and as he drooled, Frost came up behind him and got the rollup 3 count. Odd end to the match, and the show. But left something to think about. All I know is that Somerset is really making her mark on the AAPW tag scene. 2.1 AAPW Virtual Hotline Option 1: Word on The Street *BEEP* Grapevine: What's up AAPW fans, this is A.J.
