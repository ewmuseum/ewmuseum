AAPW LIVE!! Sunday, July, 20, 1997 Baltimore Arena, Baltimore, MA -The Crow defeated Pablo "The Meat" Santuccio in 12:37 after Santuccio missed a moonsault and The Crow locked on "The Big Goodbye" and Pablo gave it up. -Fallen Angel/TALM defeated The Brown Brothers in 8:38 after The Fallen Angel rolled up Mack Brown with a Frankensteiner pinning combo. -Cutting Edge defeated John Jennings/Chris Green in 3:46 after nailing Direct Hostility. -"PRIMETIME" Danny Ice defeated "The Italian Love Machine" Tony DeLuccia in 12:38 after nailing a Death Valley Driver from the top rope. -"The Miracle Man" Tyler Smythe defeated Gemini in 19:08 to retain the Atlantic Title, he won with "The Smallest Package" -"Yukon" Jack Roberts defeated Brian "Scabs" Rickard after a powerslam in 1:28. -The Punishers and The Players Club went to a no-contest when The Grinders attacked The Players Club. -"The Perfect Male Speciman" Jason Speece retained the TV Title by beating K-SOLO, SOLO submitted after the Camel Clutch was applied at 29:19 -The Suicide Blondes and Dynasty of Destruction went to a 20:00 minute draw in a non-title match. -Junior Mafia defeated The American Enforcer after a somersault leg drop on the top rope at 13:45. PRIME Monday Night, July 21, 1997 Maple Leaf Gardens, Toronto, Ontario - Gemini defeated K-SOLO at 18:19 with The Gemini Drop. - Brian "Scabs" Rickards defeated "Cashpot" Chris Green at 9:23 with The ScabBomb (Powerbomb from the top rope). - "The Silent Assassin" Kurt Ryder made Matt "Techman" Sutherland tap out to The Pain Chamber at 23:34. - "Mr. AAPW" Pablo Santuccio defeated "Hotdog" Mack Brown after a flying tostido from the top rope at 5:34. - "The Amazing Love Machine" Dane Phipps defeated "The Italian Love Machine" Tony DeLuccia at 23:12 with The Lovetap. - "PRIMETIME" Danny Ice defeated The Overgiver at 34:12 with a Shooting Star Knee Drop. - The Suicide Blondes successfully defended their Atlantic Tag titles against Cutting Edge after The Suicidal Superplex at 27:19. - Wayne Hanson and "Cactus" Bubba Steed couldn't make it to the ring as they ran into each other in the locker room and decided to fight there instead. - The Trailer Park Assassins defeated The Players Club at 15:16 with The White Trash Compactor - "The Technician" Michael Leonard forced Kevin "The Cobra" Richards to submit to The Scorpion Deathlock at 22:23. - "Dashing" Derek Lankford defeated "The Ambusher" Mike Trent at 25:02 when he got trent into a small package. - The Dynasty of Destruction defeated The Stylisitics and The East/West Coast Killaz at 43:12 when Falcon nailed The Hospitalizer on Powers - "The Miracle Man" Tyler Smythe pinned The Fallen Angel at 49:01 with a German Suplex. Tuesday Night Superstars, July, 22, 1997 Marine Midland Arena, Buffalo, N.Y. - "Dashing" Derek Lankford defeated "Desirable" Danny Dynamite by countout at 21:19. - The Grinders (Trent & Leonard) defeated The Brown Brothers at 10:19, when Leonard made Rick Brown submit to The Technical Chamber. - The Suicide Blondes defeated The Punishers by countout when The Bonecrushers interfered at 12:12 - The Crow defeated "The Chocolate Lover" Joey Zochenspitz at 0:34 with The Angel City Blackout. - Los Pantera de Guerrea defeated The Choir Boys at 3:23 with A Double Spinwheel Kick. - Wayne Hanson and Gemini fought to a 20 minute draw. - "The Silent Assassin" Kurt Ryder made "The Amazing Love Machine" Dane Phipps tap out to The Pain Chamber. - The Dynasty of Destruction defeated The Bonecrushers at 28:23 when Slasher nailed Lankford with a clothesline from the top rope. - Junior Mafia defeated "PRIMETIME" Danny Ice with The Death Sentence at 19:59 1.1 SNS Review by [name withheld] ( [detail withheld] ) A R E V I E W O F * * ****** * * * * * * * * * * * * * ** * ******************* * * * * * * * * * * * * * * ** * * * * * * * * * * * ****** S A T U R D A Y N I G H T S U P E R S T A R S ****** LIVE!!! From The Nassau County Coliseum, Uniondale N.Y Readability/Style: A nice show, but a couple of things stood out that could have easily been changed for smoother transitions.. mainly the 2 interviews in a row (not to mention there was a commercial after the 2 interviews). The "Yukon" Jack Roberts interview could have easily been moved to later in the card.. perhaps in between matches 4 and 5 or 5 and 6. It would have spaced the interviews and matches out some more. Ok.. maybe I'm being nit-picky. ;-) (Editor's Note: Sheesh..:) Match #1: "Primetime" Danny Ice vs "Dashing" Derek Lankford Nice counter into a Gut Wrench Powerbomb by Lankford. He then hits a DDT! But instead of covering, he executes a Backbreaker and THEN covers??? Come on.. You should cover after a DDT, not after a Backbreaker. If they were done in the opposite order, that woulda been fine.. but I've never seen anybody pinned after a backbreaker. Ice's Handspring Plancha over the top (ala Great Sasuke) was cool, and then followed with an Asai Moonsault. Umm.. then he got a chair. Weren't those 2 moves enough? Anyway.. he brought the chair into the ring and was gonna use it, but then Lankford Superkicked the chair into Ice's head.. Just one question.. why wasn't there a DQ? Then Derek Hotshots Ice into the Chair.. ok.. why wasn't there a DQ there? Then a drop to hold to Danny onto the chair.. again.. no DQ! I could understand if it was a toughman, no DQ match.. But it wasn't. I think I'm going to ask the AAPW to let Lantz Scarb referee all of Wayne Hanson's matches from now on. <Grin> Anyway, the WWW Champ came back with a top rope Frankensteiner followed by The California High (Somersault Legdrop), but Lankford kicked out!? Wow.. Then 3-D trips Lankford? Although I wasn't too surprized.. Lankford slapped on the Lankford Leglock and got the victory. I guess Danny Ice doesn't only wrestle on Primetime when there's a title at stake.. My words for him.. stick to Primetime.. at least you win on that show. (Editor's Note: OUCH!!) Match #2: Matt "Techman" Sutherland vs The Crow The Crow started out mat wrestling with Sutherland.. probably not a good idea.. The Hot Shot over the top rope by The Techman was cool. Then he went to work with a submission with the Cross Face Chicken Wing.. then went for a German throwaway suplex and then a Running Bulldog. Matt pulling out all the varieties of moves. The Crow came back with a Spinning Heel Kick that busted/fattened Matt's lip. And continued the aerial assault.. Asai Moonsault knocking Matt into the guardrail, Super Frankensteiner (The Techman kicked out!!), and a Senton Legdrop. Then he hit The Angel City Blackout (Fisherman's Buster) BUT MATT KICKED OUT! Matt's dropkick to the handspringing Crow was cool.. I'm surprized people don't do that more often to those flipping wrestlers. The Crow finally went for The Big Goodbye (Scorpion Deathlock, facelock combo), but Sutherland countered it into a Small Package for the 3 count! It was a tremendous match with great moves on both sides. Interview #1: "Yukon" Jack Roberts A nice interview by a "face". Saying how Junior Mafia's a good wrestler and all. Roberts is obviously looking to snag that AAPW Heavyweight title from Mafia at NOC V. The 2 out of 3 falls count anywhere match should be a highlight of the card. Interview #2: "The Showstopper" Simon Lebec Lebec telling "The Kid" that he's not only going to win the match, but he's going to "beat him to death". I liked how he ended the flash with "Crystal, warm up the hot tub and chill the champagne. I'll be with you in a few minutes. Francois, let's go make some noise!" Especially that hot tub/champagne comment. :-) Commercial #1: K-Solo vs Wayne Hanson Nice little commercial. Seemed to favor Hanson in the upcoming match though.. Although he is the AAPW veteran as compaired to K-Solo. Anyway, if it didn't have the word "attempt" in there, it would have been blatant favoritism. Match #3: Luis "The Kid" Garavello vs "The Showstopper" Simon Lebec The Kid starts out strong, but that could only last so long as Lebec went to the power moves. Pretty powerful for a 239 pounder.. not that Luis is a heavyweight though.. Lebec took it to the floor and got the advantage out there, working over Garavello's back. He continued to work the back once in the ring.. Boston Crab, Irish Whips, etc. The standing Moonsault by The Kid was pretty cool, as was his leaping swinging DDT off the springboard. He was flying all over.. Hurrican-Rana, but a kickout.. and then Lebec out of practically nowhere hits The Blackball Enziguri for the 3 count. I think Lebec should be in line for a title shot since he beat a man who's getting a TV title shot. No? (Editor's Note: Like Wrestling ever makes sense :) Commercial #2: AAPW First Blood Tape It was nice to see a different kind of commercial. Probably inspired by the Stone Cold Says So tape, but still.. A nice change from the past commercials. Match #4: The Choir Boys vs "TALM" Dane Phipps & Fallen Angel Looks to be a clean matchup. First big move.. Spinning Heel Kick by Fallen Angel! Followed it with a Snap Legdrop, which, although a clean move, was devistating.. Murff suffered a bloody nose. The Choir Boys with an impressive Neckbreaker Powerbomb Combo (it was weird to see Wrath and Mortis do the same unique move just days later) FA kicked out though. He and TALM can both take a lot of punishment. One of the reasons they're tough to beat. Dane hits The Love Tap on Johnson.. And FA came off the top with a Shooting Star Press. Love Tap to Murff on the apron, and Fallen Angel gets the 1,2,3. Look for a spectacular ladder match between these 2 and The Suicide Blondes at NOC V. Match #5: 4 way tag team dance between The Stylistics, The Bonecrushers, The Suicide Blondes and The East Coast/West Coast Killaz The Blondes and Killaz start the match. Eventually the other 2 teams come in and The Stylistics hit a great Side Suplex/Legdrop combo on Aaron Lankford. Eventually, E-Rock clotheslines Powers over the top rope.. Then The Trailer Park Assassins showed up and nailed The Stylistics with chairs. The Stylistics were then eliminated by countout as they went after the TPA. King and Lankford continue the match and King gets suplexed to the concrete floor! The battle then spilled to the outside. I thought there was going to be another countout..there should have been for how long everybody was out there. L-Train hit an asai moonsault which put Reed and King though a table. Quite a move there. Meanwhile, I guess Aaron and Steve Conner were legal in the ring.. however that happened.. E-Rock clipped Lankford's leg and Conner hit The Super Special Driver slingshot brainbuster and got the 3 count. The Bonecrushers are gone. Would it be The Killaz or the Welch Cup winners? It would be The Blondes as King moved out of the way of a Moonsault and rolled E-Rock up and got the 3 count with the aid of the tights. Quite a matchup. Kinda hard to follow with all those people, but was exciting. This adds another dimention to the Ladder match against Fallen Angel and The Amazing Love Machine at NOC V. It's also for the Atlantic Tag-Team Titles. Will the TALM/FA team continue their somewhat surprizing tag-team success? Find out at Night of Champions Five! Oh great.. Now I'm sounding like a comercial.. Match #6: Matt "Techman" Sutherland vs "Dashing" Derek Lankford The Techman living up to his name early on with the mat wrestling. However, Lankford gets a Piledriver to turn the tables. A Bulldog causes Sutherland's nose to bleed. First his lip and now his nose.. Matt took quite a beating that night. Anyway, when Lankford smells blood.. well, he hits a delayed corkscrew verticle suplex! (Editor's Note: Yeah he's "Dashing" Derek Lankford, not "Logical" Derek Lankford) Then he continues to go to work..however a Swinging DDT counter of a Spinebuster put Matt back in control. He later hit a Reverse DDT, but couldn't put Lankford away. A Double Underhook Tigerbomb couldn't put Lankford away.. a Cradle Suplex couldn't put Lankford away.. What could put SUTHERLAND away was a reversal of a sunset flip. Derek pulled it out against the former Heavyweight champ and became the 1st AAPW Light-Heavyweight Champion. Interview #3: The New Players Club Derek Lankford quits TPC. Not really a surprize as many saw it coming. Is Danny Ice going to replace him? It was hinted that he was, but we'll have to see. We all know there has been problems with he and 3-D in the past. 1.2 AAPW Virtual Hotline Option 1: The Word on The Street by A.J. DUNDEE: G'day fans and welcome to another edition of AAPW's Looking Glass, where we'll be taking a look at an interesting match for this weekend's Main Event. (Bruce turns to a new angle. The graphic changes to one of Jason Speece, holding the TV title belt aloft.) DUNDEE: Jason Speece defends his TV title against the Overgiver this weekend, and you blokes have gotta be wondering just how he intends to do it. Every time we see Speece on TV, he's barely in the running, just able to keep himself afloat so that Dashing Derek Lankford can flip him some brass knuckles to finish the job. The Player's Club has instrumental to Speece keeping his title. But there seems ta be some confusion about what's happening with the Player's Club these days. In public show of spine, Dashing Derek Lankford disrespected the Player's Club and resigned his membership last week. He and Speece have been long time friends, and Lankford was instrumental in Speece gettin' the belt in the first place. Speece, while he has some technical skill, and has proven a tough nut to crack since he won the belt in May, has relied heavily on his friends outside. The Player's Club prides itself on the attitude "win at any cost." With Lankford gone, the Player's Club have only Dynamite and Reyna to call on, as well as Speece's new piece of arm-dressing Rayne. That *has* to impact the ring strategy for Speece, and even the most sympathetic observer would be forced to conclude that at first glance it looks like Speece, un-gentle giant that he is, is due for a big fall. (Bruce turns to a new angle. The graphic changes to the Overgiver.) DUNDEE: Th' Overgiver is young and hates all things Player's Club. He has a list of friends from Kevin Richards, Tyler Smith, Wayne Hanson, and the Punishers, and *they* all hate all things Player's Club. So right there you have to assume that anything the Player's Club could do from the outside to help Speece is going ta be negated by the presence of twice as many Overgiver allies. From that angle, it doesn't look good for the current TV champ. The Overgiver is lean and hungry. He hasn't held a belt in some time, and is coming off high-profile losses. The word on the street is that the Overgiver is looking ta deliver a large package of hurt to Jason Speece, regardless of whether or not he gets a belt out of it. While he doesn't look all that big and nasty, the Overgiver survived 60 minutes of wrestling Kevin Richards, at a time when Richards was ready to end the Overgiver's career. The Overgiver has a drive that is hard to understand, and in my opinion, it's a drive Jason Speece can't match. (The graphic changes to the AAPW logo.) DUNDEE: So there ya go. The only question in this match is how long Speece can hold out against the Overgiver. Look for lots of high-impact brawling, look for Speece to need an assist from the outside, and I'd bet that the Overgiver is going to be prepared for it. If the Player's Club can set up the assist, Speece might hold his title, otherwise, it looks as though there's gonna be a change in the weatha. I'm Bruce Dundee, with your view through th' Looking Glass.
