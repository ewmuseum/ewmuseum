PRIMETIME/MSG pre-game (Silver Circle Awards), 23 December 1997

23 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Open up to a jam packed Primetime from MSG. the lights dim blue and "Heart of Glass" by Blondie starts up. Out walks Danny Dynamite, Micheal Reyna, Jason Speece, and Shawn Masters all in "Four Aces- TPC" shirts. The crowd pops a nice pop, somewhat heel, but somewhats face as all four walk down and into the ring..Dynamite, of course, has the infamouse wireless mic) DDD: New York City, tonite, history will once again be made, and its gonna be made Players Club Style!!! *pop* AAPW, tonite, we will turn this federation on its ear, and there's NOTHING anybody can do. But first off, lemme address ONE thing. LeonarDust, lemme tell you ONE thing. I have no beef with you, but I do want MY belt. Next week, your my partner. And I'll let it sit aside, but you look at me wrong, try and pull anything funny, or your two bit whore starts anything.. Son I'll hit you so hard I'll make you straight once again! *crowd pop* As partners I'll play it cool, but [name withheld], as these men are my witness, I'm coming at The Crow with everything I have, and his reign as AAPW LightHeavyWeight Champ WILL be a short one.. ("The Perfect Male Specimen" takes the mic as the crowd starts chanting JASON SUCKS... JASON SUCKS) Speece: (clearly agitated) shut the hell up!! You wouldn't know perfection if it came up behind you and stole your Doritos! (the crowd continues chanting) Speece: Right now I want to address a little problem that we're having with the AAPW offices. It seems that even though I was the Perfect Television Champion for three months, that counts for nothing upstairs. You see... champions are entitled to a rematch after they lose the gold, but was that opportunity ever presented to me? Hell no! You want to know why? Because The Overgiver has got it in good with the brass upstairs!! (The JASON SUCKS... JASON SUCKS... JASON SUCKS chant gets louder) Speece: What do you morons know anyway. Steed... you tabacca chewin' slack jawed maggot! Listen up and listen up good... you've got my belt around your filthy waist right now and I want the damn thing back. I was never given my rematch against the Overgiver because he's a coward. You... at least you appear to have somekind of backbone. Anyone who readily admits they're from Texas has something. I'm challenging you right now... Night of Champions VI... I want my Television Title back... the people want to see the Perfect Television Champion again... because everyone in AAPW knows that when I was the TV Champ, I was the best TV champ anyone could hope for. (Speece hands the mic to Reyna and Masters) Masters: AAPW, your looking at the BEST stable this federation has EVER seen! *semi pop* and Punishers, your looking at the two men who will take those belts away from you at any given time! Myself, and the Maverick, we're coming at you two inbred goofs FULL FORCE, and kids, your going down to the BEST! Reyna: There's a reason why they call us Bad Company, and Punishers, your gonna find out why! Mr. Prime Time himself, and me, one half of the team who you know can beat you.. Punishers, you've done it before, so do it..again..Give your hearts to God.. Masters; Cause baby, that gold is OURS!!! (Masters gives the mic to Speece as the crowd boos some more) Speece: Now if I may... Ladies and Gentlemen... the Player's Club wouldn't be complete without this man... The Player's Club wouldn't be the Silver Circle Stable of the Year winners without this man... and AAPW just wouldn't be the same without this man... the Player that the brass doesn't want you to know about... the Wild Card of the Player's Club... my good friend... "DASHING" DEREK LANKFORD!!!! (the crowd erupts in a chrous of boos) (Afraid by Motley Crue cranks up on the PA as "Dashing" Derek Lankford walks out. He's wearing a black Joker T-shirt and khaki pants with sunglasses. When he gets to the ring he high fives Dynamite, Masters, Reyna, & Speece and takes the mic) Lankford: Well... well... well... This is a moment that the people in the front office of AAPW have been dreading. "Dashing" Derek Lankford standing alongside The Player's Club once again. You know... when they first tried pawning off the idea of me leaving this illustrious group, I played along only because it served my best interests in becoming AAPW Light-Heavyweight Champion. I mean... I really couldn't hate these guys... me and Jason were the IFWF International Tag Team Champions for the last 4 months for crying out loud. Speece: Ain't that the truth. The front office has to be insane to think that we'd ever really feud with each other. Lankford: Well... that belt is gone so I figured it was no use hiding the obvious anymore. Much like the Triple-H/HBK behind the scenes friendship that became public... once more The Dashing One is standing alongside The Specimen... 3-D... The Maverick... and the REAL Primetime player Shawn Masters. Now... all of you out there in TV Land know that I didn't come out here just to smell the roses and bask in the glow of us being named Stable of the Year. Hell no... (Lankford takes off his sunglasses) Lankford: I held the AAPW Light-Heavyweight Championship for 6 months so I don't want that title anymore. I've proved all I needed to prove there... besides... 3-D has got that little birdy in his sights right now. 3-D... when you've got him beat... drop a Dynamite Death Drop on him for me, will ya? (Lankford & Dynamite share a laugh) Lankford: I'm not chasing the TV Title... as you heard my man earlier... The Perfect Television Champion is looking to regain that mantle from that Hayseed. No... I'm setting my goals REAL high this time. I'm looking at you Wayne Hanson and the AAPW Heavyweight Championship! (most of the crowd is stunned, those who aren't chant TRI-FEC-TA... TRI-FECT-TA..) Lankford: Chant all you want folks... you see Hanson... I've never thought much of you. I've always said that you were nothing but a follower... Tyler Smythe's lapdog... his gopher... but you managed to do something no one thought possible. You beat Junior Mafia, and now your standing there with something I want. I'm not standing in this ring to boost my ego... you talk to the man on the street, they'll tell you it doesn't need boosting. I'm standing here because unlike you... I'm not stupid. I see that to get to you, I'll have to deal with Smythe... Wishbone... and The Overgiver. That's why The Player's Club is standing with me now. (Reyna can be seen dropkicking some of the trash in the ring out to the crowd) Lankford: Overgiver... I've got you at Night of Champions VI... the Skydome crowd is going to witness my first step in becoming AAPW Heavyweight Champion when I drop you like the bad habit you are. I used to kick your ass in NCW back when you were just a punk kid... and I'm going to do it now that your just a punk. Your sacrifice is going to serve as a message to Hanson. Get his attention so to speak. Once he's done playing around with these fat bar room brawlers that pollutes the Heavyweight Division, he's going to have to step into the ring with a real wrestler.... and Hanson... My old man always said the bigger they are the harder they fall. As of this moment The Trifecta is finished. The Player's Club is reasserting themselves as the big dogs of AAPW. DDD: AAPW, your looking at the BEST stable in 1997, and your looking at the FUTURE champions of this fed.. Big D, Perfect, PrimeTime, Mav, and myself..It doesnt get any better than THIS!!! (camera fades) Wrong Dynamite...The Player's Club might be good.. But there's always something better... And the only PRIMETIME players in this town come from the * * ****** * * * * * * * * * * * * * ** * ******************* * * * * * * * * * * * * * * ** * * * * * * * * * * * ****** P R I M E T I M E : P R E - G A M E ****** LIVE!!! From Madison Square Garden in New York City.. Reed: WELCOME TO THE PRIMETIME PRE-GAME!! We've got a great set of early matches tonight including the feature match of Pre-Game as Taifun goes up against "Powerhouse" Scott Rogers... Grapevine: The first contest should also be a good one as The American Enforcer goes up again "The Perfect Male Specimen" Jason Speece, who was heard earlier along with The Players Club, who are also back and a seemingly ready to re-take their place at the top... Reed: Let's go up to the ring.. *************************************************************** AAPW PRIMETIME: The American Enforcer vs. "The Perfect Male Specimen" Jason Speece "The Perfect Male Specimen" Jason Speece's run for the TV title was going to run into a big 6'5" 276 pound roadblock in the way of The American Enforcer who quickly took it to Speece with heavy right hands and a shortarmed clothesline...AE's assualt on Speece continued as AE whipped Speece to the ropes and nailed him with a powerslam for a two count...The American Enforcer climbed to the top ropes but Speece recovered in time to kick the top rope to crucify The Enforcer and Speece followed it up with a superplex...Speece than went on control with boots to the head and climbing the top rope and nailing AE with a vicious axhandle...Speece than pulled The Enforcer up and nails a big brainbuster for a two count...The assualt didn't end as Speece whipped AE to the ropes and hit him with his patented belly to bell overhead suplex...Speece wasn't done as he followed the suplex up with his fisherman's powerbomb he calls 'The Perfectbomb' and a three count.. Winner: "The Perfect Male Specimen" Jason Speece (via pinfall) and 7:16 ************************************************************** Reed: Talk about impressive, Jason Speece quickly takes care of the former multiple time champion... Grapevine: The reunification of The Players Club definately had something to do with Jason Speece's impressive performance against The American Enforcer..With some focus, this guy is dangerous to any titleholder... Reed: Up next is a reunion of sorts as INWI bitter enemies, James "Simply The Best" Pillow goes up against "Too Slick" Rick Brown Grapevine: Definiately a good matchup..Brown was a huge thorn in Pillow's side when he entered the INWI but Pillow won their final encounter in a steel cage and went on the be NEW and INWI Light-Heaviwehgt champion later in that night... Reed: We got these words from "Too Slick" just moments ago (Camera flips to the backstage area where "Too Slick" Rick Brown is standing by with Brad Shannon) Shannon: Thanks Jeff and please welcome my guest right now to 21 year old exciting superstar "Too Slick" Rick Brown...Well Rick, the scene has changed but it looks like the name has remained the same.. Brown: You better believe it because no matter where "Too Slick" goes that punk, that ingrate James Pillow has to come tracking behind...Everywhere it's Pillow Pillow Pillow Pillow...I swear to god, when I see him tonight, he's going to be turned into fluff... Shannon: Thank you "Too Slick", back to you Jeff.. (camera fades back to Reed and Grapevine) Reed: Let's go up to the ring *************************************************************** AAPW PRIMETIME: "Too Slick" Rick Brown vs. James "Simply The Best" Pillow These two old nemesis' squared off in a breakthrough bout in the Light-Heavyweight division and right away they started with a quick brawl that Brown happened to get the best of and he nailed Pillow with a standing spinning heel kick and got a quick one count...Brown continued his early assualt as he springboarded to the middle rope and landed a missle dropkick that sent Pillow outside to to the floor and Brown charged and landed a Suicide dive on top of Pillow...Brown rolled Pillow back in the ring but Angel caught Brown with nails to the eyes as he was trying to enter and Pillow quickly took advantage of the situation with kicks to the chest and a whip to the buckle followed by a handspring elbow that got a two count...Pillow pulled Brown up and gave him a snap suplex for another two count but Pillow wasn't down as he nailed a corkscrew legdrop for ANOTHER two count...Pillow pulled Brown up and gave him consecutive forearm shots that eventually sent Brown through the ropes as Pillow charged and landing a beautiful somersault plancha onto Brown...Pilow pulled Brown up and whipped him to the rail but Brown springboarded to the top of the railing and moonsaulted onto Pillow...Brown pulled Pillow up, rolled him back in the ring and gave him a side backbreaker...Brown springboarded to the top but Angel distracted him enough to miss Landing Post II <shooting star press legdrop> and Pillow quickly pulled brown up and nailed a superkick for the victory.. Winner: James "Simply The Best" Pillow (via pinfall) at 15:32 *************************************************************** Reed: WOW...what a great match between two stellar athletes as the woman provided the different as James Pillow gets one fall over Rick Brown... Grapevine: Both men showed excellent athleticism, Pillow is a future threat to The Crow for that Light-Heavyweight title... Reed: Another great athlete is Brad Vandal who dragged "The Irish Homeboy" away last time he was on AAPW television.. Grapevine: Doing us all a favor.. Reed: Let's go up to the ring and watch Vandal take on "Rough" Russ Thunder *************************************************************** AAPW PRIMETIME: Brad Vandal vs. "Rough" Russ Thunder Vandal's second TV match was against less than prime competition and like the last time Vandal walked through Thunder...Vandal started quickly with hard uppercut and a vicious bodyslam...Vandal pulled Thunder up and gave him a Northern Lights Bomb just shrunk Thunder about two inches...From that Vandal set Thunder up for a delayed vertical suplex and flipped it into the Vandalizer for the three count...Vandal continued his assault as he gave Thunder an airplane spin into another Vandalizer...Vandal than proceeded to kick Thunder out of the ring and drag him back to the dressing room.. Winner: Brad Vandal (via pinfall) at 3:12 *************************************************************** Reed: Vandal drags another poor competitor to the back, what's he doing with them?? Grapevine: Well you know Long is making cuts...I guess Vandal is showing them to the employment office... Reed: Maybe so, what about our Feature Match.. Grapevine: Should be a real intense matchup between two real powerhouses, Rogers is a bit upset about Taifun pulling the tights to get the win...Let's hear what he had to say.. We fade in on "Powerhouse" Scott Rogers sitting on the top turnbuckle in the ring in an empty arena. He is dressed in a white tank top, blue jeans, and sneakers). SCOTT: Taifun, you and your Triad buddies may of beat us in that two out of three falls match a couple of weeks ago, but you had to pull my tights to do it. Now the AAPW puts in the ring with you..one on one. Fine by me. The Corps and I are going straight to the top of the AAPW, and YOU aren't going to stand in my way. Taifun, I'm going to show you that I am the better man...I'm going to show you that I was born under special stars and I do have the ability and the power to TAKE YOU OUT! You may have gotten the win last time..but it's payback time now BIG MAN! There is no doubt in my mind that I can beat you and leave you in a heap in the middle of the ring..but if any of your Traid buddies want to interject themselves..I've got a few friends that will stop them cold. (At this point, Scott leaps off the turnbuckle and rips off his top, revealing a very muscular upper body). Taifun..get ready to feel the power...OF THE POWERHOUSE! (As Scott says these last words he flexes a la Lex Luger and we fade out). ****************************************************************** AAPW PRIMETIME: Taifun vs. "Powerhouse" Scott Rogers Both men stood face to face as they begin this bout and Taifun quickly got the upper hand with a chop to the throat and a side kick to the chest...Taifun, quickly went with his methodical game plan and held Rogers down with stiff forearms to the head...Taifun picked Rogers up and gave him a belly to back suplex but could only manage a two count...Taifun pulled Rogers up but Rogers blocked a few Taifun shots and started whaling the Thai fighter with right hands and he whipped him to the ropes and gave him a powerslam...Rogers pulled Taifun up and showed his strength by giving the 298 pounder and big vertical suplex...Rogers dashed to the ropes and gave Taifun and elbowdrop that got a two count...Rogers pulled Tafiun up and gave him a powerbomb but again he was only able to get a two count, however, that was more than enough for The Mandarin to jump in and both men began pounding on Rogers...Taifun whipped Rogers to the ropes and gave him the Gorrila Slam but shortly thereafter Travis Turner ran to ring and cleaned house.. Winner: "Powerhouse" Scott Rogers (via disqualification) at 8:58 ************************************************************************* Reed: Well now we know this won't be the last we've heard of the situation between Taifun and "Powerhouse" Scott Rogers.. Grapevine: Are you read Jeff.. Reed: You know A.J. I think I am Grapevine: Thw Worlds Greatest Arena, The Worlds Greatest City, and The Worlds Greatest Wrestling Federation.. Reed: PRIMETIME from MSG is coming up..NEXT! (PRIMETIME is coming)
