AAPW Perimeter (return show narrates shutdown), 13 November 1997

13 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
T H E * * ****** * * * * * * * * * * * * * ** * ******************* * * * * * * * * * * * * * * ** * * * * * * * * * * * *** P E R I M E T E R *** LIVE!! From The Cincinnati Gardens Restaurant in Cincinnati, Ohio Hosted by Jeff Reed and The Overgiver Reed: Welcome to the AAPW Perimeter, I'm Jeff Reed and we are here in the beaitufil Cincinnati gardens restaurant right outside of the Cincinnati Gardens where the first AAPW Invasion will take place..Joining me as always, The self-proclaimed ICON of the AAPW and TV champion, THE OVERGIVER!! Overgiver: Shut up.. Reed: Jovial as always I see.. Overgiver: Yeah, I'm pissed off, I was enjoying my vacation in the Virgin Islands when these capitalist bastards said the AAPW back...I've been drunk off my ass for three weeks and I'm not happy to be here. Reed: Well you should be because we have a super card.. Overgiver: I heard... Reed: Well Overgiver, the first match on this super card is very close to you as you defend your AAPW Television title against your friend "Cactus" Bubba Steed.. Overgiver: Friend my ass, I'm just happy things are being done MY way, no DQ for ME!! So I can beat Bubba Steed with everything including the kitchen sink and he'll have to TAKE IT like a man.. Reed: Let's hear about The Overgiver and "Cactus" Bubba Steed for the AAPW TV title.. **PERIMETER: Overgiver vs. "Cactus" Bubba Steed for the AAPW TV title** ("Cactus" Bubba Steed in a familiar spot. His ranch. He has just finished loading his truck and is preparing to head North for the AAPW card, where he gets his last chance at the AAPW Television Title. He faces his nemisis, the Overgiver for that strap. In this match, the Overgiver can't be DQ'd, but Cactus can. Cactus has a pair of jean coveralls on with an AAPW t-shirt underneath it. He has a John Deere hat on and has a wad of chaw in his mouth.) CACTUS: Howdy y'all. Well, I guess I'm a headin' back up North after this lil' vacation I jus' had. An' well O'Giver.... yer time has finally come. Ya see, this jus' bought you a lil' more time as champ. An' well, now it's time fer ya ta shine up that there belt an' get it ready for me. Cause I do believe I'm a gonna take that strap from around yer waist.... (The Overgiver sits back in his new house in surburban Philadelphia, he's watching TV and whats looks to be an ECW broadcast...he's wearing the brand new Trifecta "finger" shirt...He glares up at the camera) Overgiver: Oh yeah AAPW, whatever..WHo's there this week, oh yes it's my buddy "Cactus" Bubba Steed, that country bumpkin moron from Arkansas or Texas or one of those hick states...Ya know Steed, you truly define the word loser...LOOK AT YOU, you dress funny, you talk funny, you're ugly...You live in a Trailer Park, you aren't educated...You are everything I'm not...And more importantly, you aren't a member of The Trifecta.. (Cactus throws his last bag into the back of the truck and his dog, "Suds" comes over to him. He pets Suds as he is getting ready to leave.) CACTUS: Now Suds, you watch after the house. Ya all know people will be here, but this is yer terrain. Now then O'Giver.... you are next. Me and yu have had some battles. Some gooders...an' well, I've beaten ya 'fore and I'll do it 'gain. So get ready, cause I'm on my way. And when I leave that Arena this weekend, I'm gonna have me a new piece of leather. A piece of AAPW leather with some nice gold on it. And well, yer gonna be leavin' empty handed. (The Overgiver reaches into a gym bag next ot him and pulls out the AAPW TV title) Overgiver: You see this Steed...the All Atlantic Pro Wrestling TV title, and it's MINE...You see Steed, I'm the best wrestler on TV, I'm the best personality on TV and I'm got all the cards in my hand...This is your last call Steed, but you're too drunk to answer the phone... [With that, Cactus hops into his truck and looks back at the camera. He spits to the ground and you can here is splat on the ground. He starts up the truck and puts it into gear and gets ready to go.] CACTUS: Hey, O'Giver... I'll be seein ya.... [With that, Cactus heads off for his match this weekend. The camera follows the truck as it gets farther and farther away and then the camera fades into an AAPW Logo...] Overgiver: Listen you country piece of turd...This weekend in Cincinnati is going to be your worst nightmare...I'm taking your career and the farm... (camera fades out) ************************************************************************* Overgiver: YEAH!! I know you liked that Reed...I was great, I mean I was truly phoneminal in that spot... Reed: Yes, you were just great.. Overgiver: Shut up Reed, I already said that.. Reed: Out next match to review will be with the AAPW Atlantic Tag Champions, The Suicide Blondes, as they take on two of the three members known as Team Firestorm making their AAPW debut.. Overgiver: Team what?? Who the hell are these punks...Are we recruiting from The Toughman Contest now or what?? Reed: No, these guys are tough powerhouses..Cyclone, the leader of the group is 7'1, 342 pounds, but the wrestlers Hurricane and Fury make up the tag team...Both are very disciplined fighters..Let's check this match up out.. **PERIMETER: AAPW Atlantic Tag Champions: The Suicide Blondes w/Somerset vs. Team Firestorm** (The scene is in the forests of Redcliff, Alberta, Canada where Cyclone, a large, bulky man wearing blue jeans and a plaid sleeveless shirt watches the other two members of Team Firestorm, Hurricane and Fury as they chop wood piece by piece to use for fire...) Cyclone: Chop the firewood men, it will be dark soon and we need to make the fire and stay warm under the cool night... (Fury and Hurricane diligently chop the firewood) Cyclone: That's discipline...Team Firestorm stands for discipline...Discipline is what makes winners and that's exactly why we are in the AAPW...We want to be at the top of the wrestling world and the AAPW is exactly where the top is... (Fury and Hurricane, both wearing jeans and plaid sweatshirts walk up behind Cyclone, sweating profusely) Cyclone: The Tokyo Blades, a great team who stresses discipline and fair play...The Punishers, the elite tough men who don't care about the pain the suffer or the ones who feel their wrath...The Bonecrushers, The Dynasty of Destruction, The Cutting Edge, The Killaz...All great talents in the AAPW...And our opponent, the AAPW Atlantic Tag Champions, The Suicide Blondes.. Fury: BLONDES!! You are tough men, dynamic men, acrobats and tag team specialists, plus you got a manager with a real big trap that gets you places...This weekend, you will fall victims to Team Firestorm and our brand of discipline... Cyclone: Team Firestorm isn't about taking second best...We didn't want The Choir Boys in some squash..We want the absolute best the AAPW has to offer...We wanted the best in the AAPW.. Hurricane: The best are you Suicide Blondes, we don't want your blond hair, and we don't want your titles..We want your fight, Suicide Blondes, we want your effort, we want your best shot because we're sure as hell going to give you ours.. Cyclone: Team Firestorm has Arrived... (Camera fades in burnt orange) (Camera opens up to a beachhouse in Long Island, New York where Somerset is sitting on the deck overlooking the Ocean..In the background, you can see Gemini and "Massive" Steven Conner doing some sparring on a mat in the living room) Somerset: *sigh* Thank God for that Robert Welch Cup title, and the million dollars it brought...I needed to buy this beach house to get away from the phone calls...Geez Somerset, what's up with you and Dane...Geez Somerset, I thought you were through with Phipps...God, the Enquirer, the Sun, and all the British tabloids want this story...It's not that big a deal..Let's get on to MY most important things....The Suicide Blondes and the match against Team Firestorm... (Somerset opens up the fax of the match list) Somerset: First of all, why are we fighting a couple of newbies...Non-tilte or not it doesn't matter, why should we have to be the first to beat the crap out of the new guys....Normally I wouldn't show, but we do have a match against Twiddle Dee and Twiddle Moron at Coastal Combat, if that show ever happens, and this is a pretty good warmup for those doofus...So okay Team Firestorm, whomever you are..Bring your fire, bring a storm, bring a damn ark if you need to but remember, in order to beat The Blondes, you have to be able to walk...Ain't gonna happen...I hope you enjoyed your career.. (Steven Conner walks out, sweating) Conner: Chelesa, it's the phone again.. Somerset: I'm not home.. Conner: That's what I told them, we're going ot be out, busy cooling off Team Firestorm this weekend... (Camera fades to white) ************************************************************************ Reed: Somerset should have her Blondes ready after a lengthy layoff...That match will be more intriguing.. Overgiver: Oh yeah, I love watching two sorry ass teams try to attempt wrestling moves on each other.. Reed: Well in that case let's go on to another one of your friends, "Dashing" Derek Lankford?? Overgiver: Oh yeah, he always comes to my house for beer and pizza...Lankford is strictly an Overgiver wanna-be... Reed: Well this wekend he'll be going against "Too Slick" Rick Brown in a non-title defense.. Overgiver: Now that "Too Slick" guy is impressive, ever since he dropped his lard as sbrother, he's shown that he does have some ability.. Reed: Let's review this match... **PERIMETER: AAPW Light Heavyweight Champion "Dashing" Derek Lankford vs. "Too Slick" Rick Brown (non-title) ** (The camera is back stage after a Motley Crue concert. "Dashing" Derek Lankford and Stacy Kash are chatting with members of the band. They see the AAPW camera crew coming) Vince: Hey champ... we'll see you back in the back. Lankford: Keep the beer cold and the women hot until I get there. Vince: Hey... you know us. (Vince, Tommy, Mick, and Nikki all leave) Lankford: So... you guys come disturb me on my night off to talk about some joker Rick Brown. Too Slick he calls himself. Is this the same "Too Slick" Rick Brown I manhandled back in June? Give me a friggin' break. I've got bigger and more important things to take care of besides him. Ka$h: Like your titles... which one are you defending this week? The IFWF International Tag Team Title? Your REAL NCW Internet Title? Or your AAPW Light-Heavyweight Title? (Camera flips to "Too Slick" Rick Brown in a city gym in Washington, D.C., he has a title over his head and his face is dripping with sweat as he stares into the camera...) Brown: Titles...Titles...Titles, that's all I hear when people talk about "Dashing" Derek Lankford, yeah I know he's a 7-time tag champion and a 5-time singles champion..I know this...Sure he's got skills and yes he manipulates a lot of people...I know that...But i really don't care...the bottom line is, that Lankford, just like that punk James "Simply the Best" Pillow, is about about an image....You see Lankford knows he's nowhere near as good as he claims to be...But this false, Ric Flair wanna-be, image that he carries around tends to win matches for him.. (Brown wips his faces clean on the sweat).. Brown: That image is also known as intimidation, what guess what Lankford, you don't intimidate me...I know I could beat you and this weekend I will do it.. (Camera flips back to Lankford) Lankford: You see Stace... that's my point. This match with Brown doesn't have anything at stake for me. I mean... why risk my well being... my chance at sustaining an injury that might force me to lose my belt to The Crow or Mikey at the end of the month? Jas can cover my back in IFWF, but here it's just me. Why should I take that risk? Ka$h: I don't know... why? Lankford: Plain and simple... to send a message. A message to Rick Brown and his quote... skyrocketing career... a message to that painted freak The Crow... a message to the man who claims to be a better technical wrestler than me [name withheld]... a message to all those NCW chumps who I know watch AAPW on a weekly basis... and a message to the AAPW office. You don't tell "Dashing" Derek Lankford when he's going to wrestle with nothing on the line. I'm the AAPW Light-Heavyweight Champion... I'm obligated to defend this title, nothing more... nothing less. "Too Slick" is going to suffer for your arrogance. You need me on that show for ratings... well...everyone out in TV land will think they're watching an episode of ER instead of AAPW Invasion when I'm done with Brown. Let's go Stace... we've got a party to go to. (Lankford & Ka$h walk away) Brown: I'm not going into this match like Barry Horowitz and saying "Oh golly gee, I'm so happy for the opportunity"...Forget that, it's me and Lankford one on one, and it's gonna remain one on one because I know you don't have any friends...You see Lankford, you've been in the game too long, your image and your build-up can no longer hide your slipping talent...This time Lankford, reality has caught up with "The Dashing One"... (camera fades out) *************************************************************************** Reed: That should be a whale of a match-up as Derek Lankford will try to use his tactical skills to disrupt "Too Slick's" air show... Overgiver: The match will suck, why?? Because Lankford's in it and to be honest, he's so slow that he may get counted out during his ring entrance... Reed: Uhh yeah, we'll lets take a look at a match featuring your friend J.J. Wishbone.. Overgiver: YEAH! Reed: As he takes on Junior Mafia.. Overgiver: J.J. told me to tell you that he was going to beat that punk so bad, his momma is going to get black eye.. Reed: Let's hear from Junior Mafia right now ** PERIMETER: Junior Mafia vs. J.J. Wishbone ** (The cameras fade into Junior Mafia's home in Chicago, IL. He sits in a leather chair wearing jeans and a Junior Mafia T-shirt.) Junior Mafia: I'm just gonna take this time to say a couple of comments about my upcoming match. No flashy lights, crowd chants, expensive restaurants, or training facilities. This is just Junior Mafia doing what he does best, keeping it real. (Junior Mafia leans up in the chair a little closer to the camera.) Junior Mafia: You know, a couple of weeks ago I think that I gave the AAPW a false impression on how I feel about things. I gave the fans the impression that my upcoming match with J.J. WishHeWas is a big joke. I made it seem like there was a serious scam going on to give this loser some credibility. And for that I would like to apologize to the fans of the AAPW. (Junior Mafia pauses and laughs a little) Junior Mafia: The fact is that, although J.J. is the fed heads little lap dog, he actually thinks he has a chance at beating me. And he had the nerve to think his future embarassment was worthy enought to be on PPV. I'm glad this match is moved up. Because everbody deserves the chance to see me put the wrath of God on this guy. I want to end this whole deal in one night. J.J. will fall like everybody else and Mafia will move on. But this match is a little bit more personal. J.J., you betrayed me in the title defense that meant the most to me. You sit back and call me a sell-out while you sing and dance for the man to get a little protection. Well, you can bring all the protection you want, which I sure you will. I have yet to be cleanly pinned and I will DIE before you be the first. It's not about rankings or props, J.J. It's about respect and I don't respect you or the manner in which you got involved in my business. You will be dealt with! Come hell or high water, your ass will beg for mercy from a man driven by revenge. And ya ain't gonna get it. No more playing games, it's almost time to step into the ring with your superior. And when you leave the arena a bloddy shell of yourself, think about the next time you try to pull one over on Junior Mafia...and tell a friend! (Camera fades to black) ********************************************************************** Overgiver: Whatever, Look at J.J. Wishbone and than look at Junior Mafia and tell me who's accomplished more as a wrestler... Reed: Uhh yeah, well let's take this break and we'll be back to talk about the other two matches on the card.. ************************************************************************ VO: 32 teams...one winner... (A shot of a silver trophy is shown, standing on a pedestal, light reflecting off it onto piles of money) VO: ...and to the winner, $1,000,000. And recognition as one of the top teams in the world. (The camera switches to Laser and Blade, clad in their wrestling gear, standing in front of the AAPW banner) Laser: When you want to send the very best... Blade: ....send the Cutting Edge! (The screen explodes into the words "Wayne Carpenter Memorial Cup") VO: Novemeber 21-23, the Cutting Edge will take on teams from 5 other federations, as the Cyberspace Wrestling Organization hosts the Wayne Carpenter Memorial Cup! Finals to be held on November 23 at NOVEMBER PAIN in Wembley Stadium! Live on PPV! (Back to Laser and Blade, arms crossed, wearing AAP f'n W T-shirts) Laser: Hey, look out world... Blade: ...comes here comes the AAP F'N W! ************************************************************************* Overgiver: Oh no, don't tell me Luke Skywalker and Han Solo are representing the AAPW in some tournament.. Reed: Well yes they are and we wish them lots of luck.. Overgiver: Speak for yourself moron, I personally hope they get squashed, how come The Trifecta weren't invited to the scrub tournament.. Reed: Why don't you ask the people running it?? Overgiver: Why?? One, because I don't want to be in the stupid tournament anyway and two, because they should be begging the Trifecta to join... Reed: Well okay, the next match we are going to talk about is between the young stallion "Powerhouse" Scott Rogers as he takes on "The Irish Homeboy" Tyrone Patterson.. Overgiver: "The Irish Homeboy"??? Reed: That's what the paper says, Let's hear from The Powerhouse as he met up with Brad Shannon during a promotional appearance this week. **PERIMETER: "Powerhouse" Scott Rogers vs. "The Irish Homeboy" Tyrone Patterson ** (We fade in on "Powerhouse" Scott Rogers standing in front of the AAPW logo on the AAPW Soundstage. He is a massive caucasian with short black hair. He is in his wrestling gear. Union Kane stands with him dressed in his ususals. Brad Shannon stands with them, stick microphone in his hand.) SHANNON: This is Brad Shannon, and I'm here with one of the rising stars here in the AAPW, "Powerhouse" Scott Rogers, and of course his manager..the leader of the Hard Corps Union Kane. Union Kane, it looks like you found a winner here in Scott Rogers. KANE: Brad Shannon, you should know that I don't waste my time with losers. When I saw this man(points to Scott) wrestle in some small independant fed down South..I knew that this was the man to take the Hard Corps into the twenty-first century. Scott Rogers is the future of the Hard Corps. He's taken on the best this sport has to offer. And while he hasn't won all of those matches..he's learning and becoming a force the AAPW has best be reckoned with. Mack Brown found out the hard way what Scott Rogers is all about. And now "Irish Homeboy" Tyrone Patterson is up next. Patterson...if you only knew what you were in for..you would be running for the Mexican border as fast as your feet could carry you. But don't take my word for it. Hear it right from the horse's mouth. ROGERS: Irish Homeboy? Is that supposed to scare me, Patterson? Am I supposed to be worried about some leprechan running around the ring. When, Patterson, your Irish eyes won't be smiling after I get done with you. Cause I've been up half the night thinking of all the ways that I can make you suffer, all the ways that I can make you cry out in pain. It's a dog eat dog world out there, and you're looking at one of the biggest dogs on the block. Patterson, get your friends and next of kin together, cause you'll need somebody to carry you from the ring once I get done with you. SHANNON: Rumor has it that the AAPW will not put Tyrone Patterson in the ring aganist you. Are you worried that this supposed change of opponent? ROGERS: Let's get one thing clear, it don't matter to me who I have in the ring. I ain't scared of nothing or nobody. I go in there with one goal in mind, to beat my opponent no matter who that person is. Now the AAPW may pull Tyrone Patterson and replace him with another wrestler. And I could care less. AAPW, you want to save Patterson from the beating of his life..GO RIGHT AHEAD! Replace him with whoever you want and I'll beat the living daylights out of that guy just I would have Patterson. SHANNON: Any Last thoughts. ROGERS: Whoever steps in the ring with me..be it a big man..or a small man..you'll all gonna end up the same way..BEATEN! SHANNON: And on that note..we'll bring this interview to a close. I'm Brad Shannon. *********************************************************************** Overgiver: Tell "Powerhouse" Scott Rogers that Lex Luger wants his gimmick back... Reed: (trying not to laugh) Okay, why don't you tell him yourself, he's only 6'6" 294 pounds.. Overgiver: Well I would be I don't want my belt to get de-valued by hanging around him, after all I am not only the ICON of the AAPW, but the AAPW TV Champion.. Reed: Really, I didn't know that, you've only mentioned it once this entire show, I'm proud of you... Overgiver: Well I figure everyone knows how big an ICON I am to this point, so I quit reminding the morons... Reed: Well the last match we'll review is between "The Technician" Michael Leonard and a member of The Players Club, "Maverick" Michael Reyna... Overgiver: Oh boy, here's a match to stay up for...The Electrician and a medicore tag wrestler going at it.. Reed: Let's hear from the Technician ** PERIMETER: "The Technician" Michael Leonard vs. "Maverick" Michael Reyna ** (The Techinician Michael Leonard is doing push-ups at his gym in Bedford, Ohio. He looks more perturbed than usual. He grunts out "100!", stops, sits up and looks intently at the camera.) Leonard: It's coming very soon, Player's Club! My boys are set to take you sorry behinds to the glue factory in just two weeks! And AAPW was so kind as to set me up with a warm-up for my Light heavyweight title match that can aid my horses before they get into the cage. Leonard: I've seen your best moves Reyna, I might almost respect you if you weren't associated with the biggest pieces of SCUM in the wrestling world! Your going to try and fly from every turnbuckle to take me out with that high flying offense of yours. I say go ahead Reyna, because in My book high flying equals HIGH RISK! (The look in Leonard's eyes could bore a hole through steel!) Leonard: I DARE you to take those risks, Reyna! Because WHEN you miss, I will take GREAT pleasure in disecting you carcass bone by bone! You've never felt the full measure of the most painful hold in wrestling. Ask Speece how the Chamber feels. He'll give you a full description of what it feels like to feel your shoulder slowly seperating.... (Leonard gets a sadistic look in his eye for a moment, but realizing what he is saying he quickly snaps back to his normal focused self.) Leonard: (trying hard to compose himself) Be forewarned Reyna, NO OUTSIDE Interference. I'll be coming to the ring with the ENTIRE membership of Grinders watching my back! Defeat is inevitable, Reyna. Prepare to be PURIFIED BY PAIN! (camera begins to fade out....) Leonard: Hold up! A.J. Grapevine I suggest you watch your tone, son! I don't take kindly to sorry Aussie wannabees disrepecting my style. Consider this.....(coldly)a warning. ************************************************************** Overgiver: The Electrician is now threatening broadcast journalists huh?? What a punk, if he was here right now, I'd kck his ass.. Reed: Of course he isn't Overgiver: I know, that's why I said it.. Reed: So what's your opinion on the card this week?? Overgiver: Well, to be honest, I don't care..After I beat the crap out of Steed, I'm going to L.A. to hang with the Trifecta... Reed: Thanks for the analysis.. Overgiver: Anytime.. Reed: Well with that, it's time to close the book on the first-ever AAPW Perimeter, For The Overgiver, I-.. Overgiver: Hey I can say bye for myself..
