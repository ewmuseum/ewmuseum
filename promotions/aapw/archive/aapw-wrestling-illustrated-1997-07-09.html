From The USAir Arena in Landover, Maryland Match #1: Toughman Match: Wayne Hanson vs "Cactus" Bubba Steed Wow! Wayne really took over the early goings of the match.. Sent steed over the top rope and then nailed him into the steel barricade. But Steed was quick to come back.. badly. Busted Hanson open with a chair. But then Hanson returned the favor. PILEDRIVER in the middle of the crowd was great! "Both men bleeding like pigs here.".. Nice comment there by Reed. Bubba with a Tiger Bomb through the table! And you thought Ahmed was the only one who did that. Hanson's Guillotine through the table was equally as devistating! Then K-Solo came down, causing Hanson to lose via the screwjob. I'd hate to be K-Solo in the coming weeks. Commercial #1: The Punishers vs Lankford/Speece Well, since they're guaranteed a title shot the following week, The Punishers should be able to defeat Derek & Jason. But if The Punishers lose, does that mean Lankford and Speece should be getting a title shot? I think so.. we shall see.. Interview #1: The Punishers That cage match is going to be something. TPC are going to have their hands full, that's for sure. The Punishers look and sound ready. 3-D and Reyna are great wrestlers, but after being punished by the schedule, they're going to be punished by The Punishers. God help them. Match #2: The Stylistics vs The Trailer Park Assassins I've been looking forward to this one. My old favorites against one of my new favorites. I've seen The Stylistics since.. man.. it's been over 2 years ago now I think. And my first glimps at the TPA was when they won the NFW Tag-Team Titles.. no small feat. But anyway, on with the match.. The one on one stuff goes The Assassins' way.. the teamwork goes The Stylistics way.. at least pretty much. Nice Double Bulldog by Curtis and Powers! And Cooter's Powerbomb was devistating. In the end, Powers held Cooter, and Curtis hit Powers with a Dropkick as Cooter ducked.. roll up.. 1, 2, 3. I would have liked to have seen a longer matchup between these two great teams. And Apparently, so did The Stylistics.. cause they continued the match! Double Slingshot Brainbuster! Spike Piledriver! Somersault Plancha sending Orbit through a table! TPA might as well become the PTA (members) now.. cause they might not be wrestling anytime soon! Anyway.. can we say.. Heel Turn? Interview #2: The Stylistics Uh oh.. Don't tell me Powers and Cutris have gone homo. Well, they say they haven't, but it does look like they're becoming the AAPW's Gorgeous George, Adrian Adonis, Golddust. The Stylistics sound like a completely different team now.. and the dislike for them is already apparent. Maybe not they can call themselves the Hair Stylists instead of The Stylistics. hehe. Match #3: Kevin "The Cobra" Richards vs The Crow Wow! The Cobra took control early, but then The Crow came back. Liked the Slingshot legdrop over the top onto Richards who was on the apron. Richards with a SUPERBRAINBUSTER! I knew a guy once who did that move.. and he and his partner held gold for an eternity (ok.. almost a year.. but that's a LONG time in e-wrestling). Spinning Heel Kick on the ringpost! MAN! Guess that's why they call it "high-risk". The Cobra shoulda had it after The Cobra Strike, but The Super Frankensteiner won it for The Crow. Best matches on the card thus far. Interview #3: [name withheld] The Atlantic Tag-Team Titles have been created. And 4 teams could win it. Bonecrushers.. Blondes.. Stylistics.. Da Killaz. Hmm.. My pick would be for The Suicide Blondes to win it, but we'll see. Also anounced, TPA gets a shot at the AAPW Tag-Team Champions at NOC V. Match #4: Matt "Techman" Sutherland vs "The Showstopper" Simon Lebec Best technical match on the card. Although you might have suspected that. Lebec with a Piledriver and then dropped Sutherland groin first on the top rope then dropkicked him out to the floor then suplexes him on the floor. He really started taking it to The Techman. But Sutherland with that Leaping, Swinging, DDT turned the tide. Garavello came to Matt's aid after the ref got CLOCKED by Lebec. But was it to help Sutherland or just to take out the man who defeated him in round 1. Maybe Sutherland will be a Grinder even though he said he didn't want to be? Match #5: "Dashing" Derek Lankford vs "The Amazing Love Machine" Dane Phipps Phipps in the early going takes a page out of his best friend's book with a Frankensteiner! Later with an Asai Moonsault to the outside! TALM just dominated the Dashing One for the whole 1st part of the match. Then Lankford took over for a while.. a nice DDT, and avoidance of some Phipps moves. Then.. THE LOVE TAP! But DEREK KICKED OUT!?!?! WOW! The counter of the top rope frankensteiner into a superbomb did it for Lankford. He finally defeated TALM.. and he may end up being the AAPW Light-Heavyweight Champion. Post Match.. Kevin "The Cobra" Richards and looks to join The New Players Club, but then THE OVERGIVER ATTACKS LANKFORD! And then The Overgiver AND Richards TEAM UP ON LANKFORD!?!!?! That Arabian Facebuster move was cool too! And then The Cobra Strike and an Overbomb and the Poison Pleasure! I couldn't believe two men who have hated each other so much could team up like this. And even more so.. I couldn't believe TPC didn't come to Derek's aid! Maybe Lankford won't be winning the LH title.. He may not even be able to compete in the next round! Commercial #2: The Overgiver vs "Primetime" Danny Ice Not as "cool" sounding as most, but it'll do. In any case, after Danny Ice beat Tyler Smythe, I don't see any way he'll lose to The Overgiver. Match #6: The Fallen Angel vs The American Enforcer Nice Angel Death Roll counter into a Tiger Bomb! Frog Splash by Fallen Angel! Both with kickouts. Then The Enforcer with a Belly to Belly Superplex! And a Piledriver! And FA with a Springboard Angel Death Roll and the 1, 2, 3!! And great sportsmanship after the match as the 2 shake hands and embrace! Gotta love that between 2 "good guys"! Post Match: The Suicidal Society ATTACK FA! Head & Arm Suplex! Jumping Chokeslam by Gemini followed by a Powerbomb! Ryder locks on the formerly "Methodical", but still the Pain Chamber! TALM and The Techman come to the aid of FA, but unfortunatly, it was a little late. But I have a feeling Fallen Angel will be back.. Commercial #3: The Punishers Buy the t-shirt commercial. Ok, so it's been done before. But at least it was something different than just advertizing upcoming matches. Not that I don't like that, but variety is good. :-) Match #7: Atlantic Champ, Tyler Smythe vs Heavyweight Champ, Junior Mafia Lame! Tyler Smythe shoulda won this one! I mean, Junior Mafia didn't even have comments on the WIR and Tyler had another stellar flash! Maybe roll-playing doesn't help. Some nice moves by both guys, and an intense overall matchup. Pretty brutal match too.. Smythe got busted up all over. Oh well, it was a good main event, just didn't end as I'd had hoped. :-) 1.2 AAPW Virtual Hotline Option 1: The Word on The Street with A.J. (Shannon shuffles through some papers) Shannon: First of all let's take a look at The Dynasty of Destruction, the number one ranked contenders for the belts...This powerful team of Falcon and Slasher, led by Don DeLite, is a classic example of two fighters...The idea of going first in a Texas Death Match should suit them fine...Let's analyze their chances: (Shannon looks at the TV screen behind him which shows a picture of The Dynasty as he continues talking) Dynasty of Destruction: Falcon and Slasher Strengthes: The Dynasty are 666 pounds of solid mass, they have an obvious advantage in the Texas Death Match, besides being strong, they also have very dangerous killer instincts, in the past they have used tire irons, chairs, and bats to hurt their opponents...They are very hungry and aggressive Weakenesses: Their usual problem of speed has been offset by the rules..Eventually TPC and possibly The Punishers, will have to come to them...But their problems come in pyschology having never beaten The Punishers and cleanly pinning TPC only once...Confidence will be a big problem and "The Chairman" is going to have to make sure they are mentally prepared. Shannon: Overall, I think The Dynasty are 50/50 bets to win it all, they are certainly capable and they are pyhsically imposing, but their only questions are fatigue and mental preparations... (Shannon flips through more papers) Shannon: Next let's look at the champs, Dynamite and Reyna, The Players Club...One of the storied teams in history. They are multiple time champions and excellent tacticians. They are into their 3rd month as champions and they have taken on all competitors...Let's breakdown the champs (Shannon turns away to watch the screen that shows The Players Club with Sara Simpson) The Players Club: Dynamite & Reyna Strengthes: Well they are the champions and they've got all the tools a good tag team needs...They are quick, and they have descent strength for a couple of 235+ pounders...Their biggest strength is confidence, as the cornerstones of The Players Club, they don't feel like they can lose... Weaknesses: Usually I would say they have few weaknesses because they've defeated both teams on a few occasions, but in this scenario it's different..A Texas Death match against The Dynasty?? That's a dangerous situation for TPC who are giving up 200 pounds and really don't have the adavntage of doing in-ring double teaming because this sucker can end up anywhere...If they survive the match with The Dynasty, a steel cage should provide more comfort because they will be in-ring..But The Punishers will give them EVERYTHING they can handler.. Shannon: I think TPC's chances of retaining are very good but their chances of getting hurt are so much better... (Shannon ruffles through more papers) Shannon: Lastly we will talk about The Punishers, the bad asses of the AAPW, Jack Williams and Mason Frost are two of the hardest workers in wrestling...Great tag team because they compliment each other so well, they have the clearcut advantage in a steel cage match because I feel they can beat anyteam in a fair situation...Let's look at The Punishers (Shannon turns back to watch the TV screen and The Punishers faces flips on it) Strengthes: The Punishers are a classic tag team...One of the best to come down the pipes in a long time, they have basic tag skills and superlative double teams...They do not excel in one particular area, they are excellent in every area... Weakeness: Their first match will be their weakeness..Lankford/Speece are multiple-time champs themselves and they are going to be quite a challenge for The Punishers, win or lose The Punishers get the shot llater that night, so they just don't want to get injured.. Shannon: The Punishers have a great chance of being two-time champs if they don't get injured in the earlier bout, but The Players Club as a whole are very devious and you can guarantee they are going to do whatever it takes to keep the titles in the group.. (Shannon turns back as a camera switch occurs) Shannon: This is history in the making folks, this week the most creative tag team titles matches and next week two new champions in the AAPW...It's going to be a great time before NOC V...For AAPW Wrestling Illustrated, this is Brad Shannon for Break-DOWN and a side note that this show will have a new host next week... *END* AAPW Wrestling Illustrated is a: Hailstorm/NewJack Production Copyright 1997 AAPW Homepage: http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Field/4975 Special thanks to [name withheld] for his contributions on the magazine..
