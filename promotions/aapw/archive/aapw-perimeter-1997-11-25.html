AAPW Perimeter, 25 November 1997

25 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
T H E * * ****** * * * * * * * * * * * * * ** * ******************* * * * * * * * * * * * * * * ** * * * * * * * * * * * *** P E R I M E T E R *** LIVE!! From The Brickhouse Pizzeria in Downtown Chicago, Illinois Hosted by Jeff Reed and The Overgiver Reed: Welcome to AAPW Perimeter as we are live from The Brickhouse Pizzeria in the middle of CHI-TOWN <crowd pop> and introducing my co-host...THE OVERGIVER!! (A mixed pop as The Overgiver walks through with three very large sausage and pepperoni pizzas) Reed: Welcome Mr. Overgiver, I see you brought dinner for us all.. Overgiver: I did what?? Excuse me, three pizzas...The Trifecta...None for you or any of these other freeloaders, just because Wayne Hanson is from here, don't mean I forgot Junior Mafia is from here too.. (crowd boos a bit) Overgiver: Oh shut up..And Reed, if you talk about my match with that fat hick, I swear I make your tongue lick your ass... Reed: And on such a positive note, let's go to our first match to examine as The Fallen Angel and "The Amazing Love Machine" Dane Phipps take on The Stancil Brothers.. ** AAPW PERIMETER: "The Amazing Love Machine" Dane Phipps vs. The Fallen Angel vs. The Stancil Brothers ** (The camera opens backstage to Dane Phipps and The Fallen Angel wearing their ring attire. They have seemed to have their differences put aside for now, and look like the solid unit they are.) FA: Well, back in action! It's been FAR TOO LONG! It's time for the high flying Fallen Angel and The Amazing Love Machine to go to work and show Jayson and Vernon what All-Atlantic Professional Wrestling is all about! TALM: That's right, The Black Dragon and Hands of Stone have been running around the west with success but TONIGHT they are stepping into enemy territory and we are the home team.. FA: You know it, my man! The Black Dragon and The Hands of Stone are going to have quite a match on them. A match that will be exciting and fast paced. Why? Because Dane Phipps and FA are the 2 best pound for pound wrestlers in this sport. And together, we're twice as good. Tell um, TALM! TALM: Not only are we the two best pound for pound wrestlers in the sport but The Tokyo Blades are scared of us...Hey Katana...Wak...Wa...Whatever the other one's name is..What are you waiting for?? Why are you scared to leave your toys at home and come out like men?? FA: With that said, let's do this thing! <Fallen Angel and Dane Phipps walk out of camera view, giving each other a high five as they go.> ************************************************************** Reed: Well TALM and The Fallen Angel are ready to get back into tag action as FA preps up to take on "The Demon" Damien Lestat for the vacant AAPW Atlantic title at Coastal Combat... Overgiver: The Atlantic Champion is Tyler Smythe and only a moron wouldn't know that...He was cheated out of his title and these guys are strictly frauds.. Reed: Well, it's time for a special Perimeter interview, with the newest AAPW sensation... Overgiver: HEY! No one told me I'd have to do a damn interview! Reed: Actually, it's not with you, Overgiver. Overgiver: You mentioned the Trifecta. Reed: No, I said the newest AAPW sensation... Overgiver: Yeah, the Trifecta! Reed: I mean the Cutting Edge, I went out to their training facilities this past week and caught this interview with them.. Overgiver: WHAT! YOU'RE CALLING THOSE DUMBASSES THE NEWEST SENSATION? If I wasn't allowed to hurt you, Reed, I'd break your neck... Reed: Uh, let's just go to the interview. ** AAPW PERIMETER: The Cutting Edge vs. The Bonecrushers ** (The camera opens on the training facility of the Cutting Edge, Laser and Blade, and Jeff Reed. All three men are sitting in a wrestling ring in in the middle of the gym. Laser and Blade are both wearing street clothes, jeans and T-shirts, and Jeff has on an AAPW blazer) Reed: Laser, Blade, thanks for letting us give this interview. Blade: No problem. Laser: Always up for some free publicity. Reed: I guess we should start with an obvious question...during the AAPW's downtime the past month, what has the Cutting Edge been doing? Training, or taking a vacation? Laser: Training. Always training. In this business, and at this level of competition, you can't afford to spend two weeks sunning at Long Beach. Blade: We've been doing what we do best...perfecting our craft. Since we didn't have the luxury of having laurels to rest on, and we're one of the few tag teams here that doesn't wrestle in any other feds, the Cutting Edge did it's best to keep up the pace. In this case, when AAPW went on hiatus, we went back to our roots. Reed: Your roots? You went back to indy federations? Laser: Just a couple here and there, nothing major. Small-time feds, like IWC, XCW....although we did turn down an invite from the NCW. TOO small time, if you get my drift. (Reed smiles at that comment, then regains his composure) Reed: So, you've been wrestling these past few weeks? Blade: We have no choice. At this point in time, although we're in the big leagues now, Laser and myself have very little ring experience to fall back on. Everyone saw that when we took on the East/West Coast Killaz a few weeks ago. We hung with them for a while, but they just could do more in the ring. The mark of a great tag team is the ability to improvise, and that's what we were working on. Laser: When you have no clue, or have never fraggin HEARD of the team you're supposed to wrestle, you have to make up stuff as you go along. And me and Blade learned a few tricks while touring the minors. Reed: Well, not everything you did was minor. You missed the re-opening of AAPW because you were invited to a tournament in England. Blade: Ah, the Carpenter Cup. Reed: Yes, we all saw the commercial this past weekend. Laser: What happened there was a couple of friends of ours are in another federation, the CWO. I don't know if you're ever heard of the Wild Cards, Jeff? Reed: The name rings a bell. Laser: Anyway, they told us that the fed was looking for a couple of tag teams from other federations to fill out the roster. After talking with AAPW officials, we went ahead and applied for the tournament. We were accepted and flew over to England. Reed: Alas, you didn't fare so well, did you? Laser: Nope. We got our asses handed to us the first round, by the Mercenaries. Reed: Now, you made a big deal about this tournament this past week, and how does it feel to be knocked out so early? Blade: It's a downer, but we looked at it as a learning experience. We didn't have a choice. Sure, we talked earlier about perfecting our craft over the past few weeks. And it may sound like we didn't do a damn good job. But you learn more from a loss then a win. We got more from getting pinned by the Killaz then from kicking the Player's Club around the ring. Laser: It sucks to have lost, but we're young, and we've got all the time in the world to learn. Reed: You're back in All-Atlantic. What are your goals in the upcoming year? Laser: Well, we definitely plan to improve. People in this federation look at us as the rookies, always destined to lose. Hell, why did Team Firestorm get a shot at the Suicide Blondes for their first MATCH? So, respect is the big one. Blade: Also, we plan to get those Atlantic Tag team belts. Reed: Big words. Blade: Definitely. We were #1 contenders for a week, and we looked forward to that matchup. But we got cocky and got knocked off. So, we're going to get back up to that perch, and nothing is going to knock us down. Laser: We've faced the Blondes before, on the house tour. They know we can give them a match. We can't beat them...yet. Blade: So, we have to get better. We plan to take on the fed's elite. Guys like the Grinders, the Trailer Park Assassins, the Punishers, the Cashinators... Laser: All in the name of the persuit of perfection. Reed: Well, I wish you guys luck in 1998, and thanks for having me. **************************************************************** Overgiver: Oh yeah, great sensations, one tournament and one first-round exit, what a couple of jokes...The Bonecrushers will rip them to shreds... Reed: In other action we have The Soultaker going one on one with Grinder, Luis "The Kid" Garavello.. Overgiver: A couple a pre-lims.. Reed: And The Soultaker's partner, The White Angel will take on "Thriller" Travis Turner of The Hard Corps, let's hear from The Thriller right now.. ** AAPW PERIMETER: The White Angel vs. "Thriller" Travis Turner ** ("Thriller" Travis Turner is standing in the locker room area with Union Kane) TURNER: What the hell am I doing in the pre-game!?!? And taking on White Angel no doubt!! UK I'm better than that! I should be the main event man! KANE: I know you are!! And YOU know you are!! Travis what you've gotta do now is bust your butt and show the suits that you are!! You haven't showed up for photos...you haven't showed up for your contract signing... it's a wonder you're gettin PAID for this bout!! TURNER: Dammit what do I have to do? I've been dealing with my brother all this time trying to knock some sense into him, but you know what? He's right about one thing...this whole respect thing has gone down the tubes....WHERE'S MY RESPECT?!?!? I am the Attitude with Lattitude!! I'm the best high flyer there is!! KANE: Trav, you're jumping the gun!! TURNER: No UK!! I'll tell you what! I'll hop in that ring and I will clearly open up a can of whoopass all over The White Angel and make sure he can never walk again...I'll step out of the ring as the winner and if I'm not on the main card next week....heads are gonna ROLL!!! **************************************************************** Overgiver: Someone needs to tell him the reason he's not on the Main Card is because he sucks... Reed: Why don't you tell him?? Overgiver: Well, because...errr...I don't have his phone number, yeah that's the ticket... (One crowd member shouts...I got his number!!) Overgiver: OH YEAH!! Where's a phone genius.. (About six people pull out cellular phones..YOU CAN USE MINE!!) Overgiver: Yeah, I'll use them allright...I'll shove 'em right up your respective asses... Reed: In other action on AAPW PRIMETIME, we'll see the AAPW debut of Taifun, the MaiTai specialist who will bring plenty of torture to "Hotdog" Mack Brown.. Overgiver: You mean he won't feed him?? Reed: Will you stop...Anyway let's here from James "Simply The Best" Pillow.. ** AAPW PERIMETER: James "Simply The Best" Pillow vs. The Man They Call Pablo ** (Camera flips to inside the locker room of James Pillow before his match at Invasion. James and Angel are sitting on the sofa in his dressing room. Angel has on a long white skirt and a "SIMPLY THE BEST" T-Shirt on. James is wearing long black tights and no shirt showing how well in shape he is, the N.E.W. World Title is sitting on the table across from him. He looks into the camara and begins to talk. . .) James "Simply The Best" Pillow: Well my undefeated streak here has come to an end but that will soon be rectified. I start tonight at Invasion against The Man They Call Pablo. . .<wipes forhead> PHEW! Just saying his damn name takes the breathe out of ya <grins> Well Pablo, you seem to be nothing more then a little piece of garbage the AAPW has stuck in front of me just to get me on the card. Cause everyone knows when I'm on the world watches with amazement. Being put in with you is qute frankly a waste of my time, but the fans have paid there money to see the man that is Simply the Best in action and I won't disappoint them, I could make very short work of you but then they would be disapointed so I'll just drag you around for ten minutes or so before decideing to put you out of your misery and get the 1-2-3. Pablo, you illegal immigrant, you are just a little bump in the road to my highway of success in the AAPW and I am going to run right over you...See you in the ring. (Camera fades) *************************************************************** Reed: James "Simply The Best" Pillow current champion in the NEW and INWI is ready for action against The Man Called Pablo.. Overgiver: Inferior wrestler, inferior titles, inferior organizations....Bottom line, Pillow, leave those chump titles home when you step in the AAPW.. Reed: This week on PRIMETIME will also see the debuts of The Space Brothers and the powerful Austrailan "Gentleman" Jack Rackham...Also on this card is an AAPW favorite...The three-way dance as "The Perfect Male Specimen" Jason Speece, K-SOLO, and "The Ambusher" Mike Trent go against each other...K-SOLO had a few words to say about this match.. ** AAPW PERIMETER: "The Perfect Male Specimen" Jason Speece vs. K-SOLO vs. "The Ambusher" Mike Trent (3-way-dance, special challenge) ** (K-Solo is seen sitting at his laptop computer when the phone rings) Solo: Hello? Hey Patty wassup? I got a what? A 3-Way Dance with who? Speece and Trent. Those glorified jobbers?! What is the Pres. thinking about? He needs to put me in that match with Smythe for the VACANT Atlantic Title! Yeah I'll start training. Yeah, I'll see you in a few...Peace. (Camera fades to black) ************************************************************** Overgiver: HA!, if he wants to see a glorified jobber he needs to look in the mirror...He's the biggest glorified jobber in wrestling... Reed: Well a few off the cut remarks with a seemingly disappointed K-SOLO...That match will be on PRIMETIME as well as The Trailer Park Assassins will be using The Cashinators as their warmups for Coastal Combat... Overgiver: Yeah but who cares...It's Main event time...My friends, honorary Trifecta members, The Punishers take on those Japanese defects The Tokyo Blades.. Reed: Yes, and this is a HUGE match going into Coastal Combat as The Punishers have put their prized gold on the line a week before they will see this same team in the triple-caged Tower of Doom...Let's hear from both teams now.. ** AAPW PERIMETER: The Toyko Blades vs. The Punishers (AAPW Tag title match) ** (The camera opens on the Tokyo Blades and the Old Man standing in front of an AAPW banner with a large 'X' over the AAPW in red paint. Both Blades are wearing Decapitator 3:16 t-shirts with the Wild West Wrestling logo on it...) Katana: This weekend, at long last, we face the one team we want the most in AAPW: The Punishers. Punishers, you came to WWW, and 'called' us out, as you gaijin say. We answered, and you hid behind the Yakuza rather than face us. Now, you have no Yakuza to protect you. It is just you, and the Tokyo Blades. Now we face, as it was meant to be. Wakizashi: Punishers, this weekend, the first battle in our war shall be fought. Win or lose, both teams will never be the same. Two of the best teams in AAPW will face, and the winner walks away with the titles. Will you keep them, or will the Tokyo Blades take them from you? We shall see. It shall be a glorious battle between us, that we know. (the Old Man says something, and Katana nods to him) (The scene is the familiar environs of the La Grange Gym. Little has changed in the past month. The gym is still rather dingy and dimply lit. Over the PA system, "(Can't You) Trip Like I Do" by Filter and Crystal Method can be heard blaring. In the foreground, Mason Frost and Jack Williams stand before the big screen television, watching footage of the conclusion of the recent WWW Three Way Dance between The Yakuza, Tengen/Asai and The Tokyo Blades. Both men are talking quietly to each other, and occasionally point at the screen. As the match ends, ultimately with Tengen/Asai winning, Williams reaches up and flips the VCR into pause mode. Both men turn to face the camera. Frost is wearing his trademark cowboy boots, leather chaps over blue jeans, and ten gallon hat, along with a black t-shirt which says "Second Place Is First Place For LOSERS!". Meanwhile, WIlliams is wearing biker boots, black leather pants, and a black KMFDM t-shirt with the cover of the new album on the front, and 'MEGALOMANIAC" on the back.) WILLIAMS: Well, it's been a LONG time, AAPW.... TOO damn long if you ask me! But... we're back! And we're still holding the gold! FROST: WOO-HAW!!!! The Punishers'r still the nastiest, ass-kickin'est, lady-lovin'est and gold-wearin'est team in the land.... and there ain't NOBODY who'll tell y'all different! Yeah, AAPW may've taken a l'il time off, but the Punishers don't NEVER take breaks! Yeah, we may have taken it a l'il EASY fer a few weeks, but we ain't NEVER stoped preparin'.... and look who we get first night back? The Tokyo Butter-Knives!! I guess yer yellow-bellied back-stabbin' buddies in The Triad'r still runnin' scared from us, 'cause we ain't heard nothin' outta them in a LONG time!!!! But, that's alright with us... if'fn they want y'all ta be a sacrificial offering ta the Punishers...... THEN WE'LL SEND Y'ALL BACK TA YER RICE PADDY IN A CARDBOARD BOX!!!!! (Camera flips back to the Blades in front of the AAPW banner with the X across it) Katana: But now we must move on to other matters. Dane Phipps. You claim that we fear you? [chuckles] Hardly. We have long pointed out your hypocrisy, and you proved it by revealing your true nature. You are still Somerset's pawn, and Derek Lankford's new lackey. You may still fool the fans, but we know you for the scum that you truly are. At least the Yakuza are open about their vileness, while you, you DARE to feign innocence to the world? You are pathetcic [spits on the ground] Wakizashi: Dane Phipps, you may decieved Fallen Angel all you want, but we shall not fall into your trap. We still refuse youir stipulations, because deep down, we know you fear us. You beat us because your WHORE aided you. Without her, you are nothing. We are far from through with you Dane Phipps, and the meaning of that shall come clear soon. Prepare yourself Dane Phipps, for until we get what we want, your life shall be hell on earth. (The Old Man says something, and the Tokyo Blades bow to the camera, then walk off camera as it fades back in the La Grange Gym) WILLIAMS: That's right Blades..... now, it seems in the last few weeks that you guys have been flip-flopping on which side of the fence you're sitting on for a few weeks now.... one week you come down to the ring with The Mandarin, the next you say, we're not Triad, we don't need them..... well, boys..... good, bad..... WE'RE the ones with the BELTS. And YOU think you're going to waltz into OUR turf, and take OUR belts? You're DUMBER than you look. Punks, as far as we're concerned, you're still part of the Triad, until we see them kick your sorry asses! So we're going into this next match not just looking to keep our belts... we're looking to send a message toyour buddies in WWW. NOBODY..... and we mean NOBODY..... comes into OUR turf and thinks they can mess with the Punishers!!!! And you two clown-boys will be the first two to feel the TRUE wrath of the Punishers!!! And we have one message for you two.... yeah, you're fast.... you've got the moves.... and you think the Decapitator is unstoppable.... but, just like a frankensteiner can be reversed into a powerbomb....... the Decapitator....can be reversed into Total Punishment. Think about that...... think REAL HARD.......... because we'll be waiting......... in the ring. (camera fades) ************************************************************** Overgiver: YEAH! Punishers kick ass...AAPW Tag Champions and friends of The Trifecta, speaking of my boys, someone get Little Nero's square ass over here and box up my pizzas so I can head over the Hanson's pad and watch The Bulls kick someone's ass... Reed: Well while The Overgiver gets that taken care of let's listen to Brad Vandal, the GIWF Heavyweight Champion making his debut very soon and also a very pissed off Rocky Mountain monster, Black Flagg.. ** AAPW PERIMETER: Segments by Brad Vandal and Black Flagg ** (The camera opens in an AAPW ring it is dark and a lone figure can be seen standing in the ring. Just outside of the ring another dark figure can be seen. A spotlight shines on the ring and standing in the center is a man some of you will know but most will not. He stand about 6 and a half feet tale wieghing a little over two fifty. He has shoulder length brown hair that is tied back. He is currently wearing jeans and a black t-shirt. On the t-shirt are the words in bold white print VANDAL as the light shines on him he turns looking at the arena. As he does this you can see the back of the shirt is says again in bold white "Architect of Agression" The man turns again to face the camera.) Brad Vandal: Hey AAPW for those who know me you can go clean out your drawers, but for those that don't, all four of ya, let me introduce myself. I am Brad Vandal. Now I won't come out and brag about how great I am just ask a few of your stars, they can tell you about my rep. (Brad tugs on his shirt) Brad: This is something that you will come to recognize. Ya see I got this little fan base that has made me a house hold name. I will say right now I am here for only one thing and that is G.O.L.D.. Thats right I say right now that before 1998 is done everyone in the AAPW will know the name Brad Vandal. (Brad reaches into his back pocket pulling out a small box. He waves to the camera presses a button and the screen goes fuzy.) **************** (Black Flagg steps into the broadcast booth interrupting an AAPW Studio broadcast) FLAGG: How many more people are gonna get hurt before you all understand that Black Flagg is championship caliber!! I'm not even on the card this weekend!! I'll tell you what I'll run over into the broadcast truck and make them noodle arm board operators run Black Flagg highlights until I get on the card... or you know what? maybe I'll just take care of one of you little runts!! (security comes and detains Flagg) I'll get my respect!! You all better be ready!! When The Black Flagg rises....it'll ALL BE OVER!!! ***************************************************************** Overgiver: Oh quit your bitching.. Reed: Well this weekend, AAPW PRIMETIME returns to Chicago, The United Center and it's going to be a great card...After this break we have a special Ragtag Central on tap and the topic was the sudden closing on NCW...For The Overgiver, this is Jeff Reed saying...Good Night.. (camera fades out) AAPW Perimeter is a Hailstorm/NewJack Production
