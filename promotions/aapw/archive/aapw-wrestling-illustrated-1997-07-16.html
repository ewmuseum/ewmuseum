Friday, July 11, 1997 Legion Stadium, Wilimington N.C. -The Cutting Edge defeated The Choir Boys in 11:28 after Cutting Edge nailed Direct Hostility. -"Yukon" Jack Roberts defeated Gemini in 13:38 after he nailed a nasty powerbomb. -Luis "The Kid" Garavello defeated The Super Destroyer in 11:37 after a moonsault. -The Suicide Blondes defeated The East Coast West Coast Killaz in 21:34 after 1-900-SUICIDE. -The Fallen Angel defeated "The Amazing Love Machine" Dane Phipps in 31:48 after a Frankensteiner into a roll-up. Both men embraced afterwards. -Kevin "The Cobra" Richards defeated "Cactus" Bubba Steed in 16:08 after the Cobra Strike Piledriver. -"The Perfect Male Speciman" Jason Speece retained his TV Title after defeating "The Italian Love Machine" Tony DeLuccia at 19:23 with The PerfectBomb -The Trailer Park Assassins defeated The Stylistics in 22:34 after The White Trash Compactor. -"The Miracle Man" Tyler Smythe defeated "The Silent Assassin" Kurt Ryder in 20:00 to retain the Atlantic Title after "The Smallest Package". -Junior Mafia defeated "The Showstopper" Simon Lebec in 16:08 after he nailed "The Hit" to retain the AAPW Heavyweight Championship. AAPW LIVE!! Sunday, July 13, 1997 The Civic Center, Dover, Delaware -The Overgiver defeated Pablo Santuccio in 2:38 when Pablo Santuccio missed a moonsault and The Overgiver Overbombed him. -The Players Club defeated The Brown Brothers in 5:42 after Danny Dynamite nailed Mack with a leg drop off the top. -The Punishers defeated The Bonecrushers in 19:09 after nailing Total Punishment. -"PRIMETIME" Danny Ice defeated The Crow in 23:09 after a Death valley Driver from the top rope. -Wayne Hanson defeated The Super Destroyer in 6:07 after nailing a leg drop to the back of the head. -The Dynasty of Destruction held on to the Tag Titles by defeating The Stylistics in 23:47. -"Dashing" Derek Lankford defeated Kevin "The Cobra" Richards after 11:45 when Richards submitted to the Lankford Leglock. -Gemini defeated K-SOLO at 15:04 with The Gemini Drop. -"The Ambusher" Mike Trent fought "Cactus" Bubba Steed to a no-contest after weapons came into play. -"The Miracle Man" Tyler Smythe defeated Matt "Techman" Sutherland after 25:07 with a surprise backslide to retain the Atlantic Title. -"The Silent Assassin" Kurt Ryder defeated The American Enforcer with The Pain Chamber after 15:39. -Junior Mafia defeated "The Techician" Michael Leonard after 20:30 after nailing a suplex into a tombstone piledriver. PRIME Monday Night, Monday, July 14, 1997 The Civic Arena, Asbury Park, N.J. -Dane Phipps/Fallen Angel defeated The Cutting Edge after Phipps nailed The Lovetap in 12:38 -The Crow defeated Luis Garavello after a Super Frankensteiner in 22:34. -Gemini defeated "Dashing" Derek Lankford in 17:03 with the Gemini Drop. -K-SOLO defeated "Cashpot" Chris Green easliy in 6:56 after the SOLO-breaker. -"Cactus" Bubba Steed pulled a upset by defeating "The Miracle Man" Tyler Smythe in 25:45 after a Reverse DDT in the non-title match-up. -The Dynasty of Destruction defeated The Punishers in 30:21 after Falcon nailed a Superbomb. -The American Enforecer defeated The Super Destroyer in 8:09 after nailing a clothesline from the top rope. -"The Perfect Male Speciman" Jason Speece retained the AAPW TV Title by defeating "PRIMETIME" Danny Ice in 34:57. -The East Coast/West Coast Killaz defeated the Trailor Park Assassins in 11:28. -Junior Mafia defeated The Overgiver in 23:45 after Mafia frankensteinered The Overgiver out of a powerbomb attempt. Tuesday Night Superstars, Tuesday, July 15, 1997 The War Memorial, Rochester, N.Y. -"The Showstopper" Simon Lebec defeated Tony DeLuccia in 13:30 after he nailed a Northern Lights Suplex. -The Stylistics defeated The Players Club in a superb match-up in 40:30 The Styles won after Buck Powers rolled up Danny Dynamite after a missed leg-drop. -"The Ambusher" Mike Trent defeated John Jennings in 9:47 after a powerbomb. -"Yukon" Jack Roberts defeated The Overgiver in 19:53 after a piledriver off the top rope. -The Trailer Park Assassins and The East Coast/West Coast Killaz fought to a 25:00 draw. -Kevin "The Cobra" Richards defeated "Dashing" Derek Lankford by a roll-up countering the Lankford Leglock in 16:53 -"PRIMETIME" Danny Ice defeated The Fallen Angel in 20:57 after a super brain buster. -Wayne Hanson/Tyler Smythe defeated The Cutting Edge after Hanson nailed a leg drop to the back of the head in 12:48. -Junior Mafia defeated Gemini in 19:35 after nailing "The Hit" 1.1 PRIMETIME Review by [name withheld] < [detail withheld] > Interview #1: The Players Club 3-D and Reyna telling everybody how great they are. The tag-team champions are good.. hell, they've held titles in just about every federation known to man. However, they could be in trouble tonight.. Not just 1 tough tag-team, but TWO.. that is.. IF they survive the first matchup. Match #1: The Dynasty of Destrution vs AAPW Tag-Team Champs, The Players Club in a Texas Death Match Blood EARLY in this one! Reyna busted open with the tag team title belt! Then it went into the crowd! Dynamite hit with a chair! With all these shots, I didn't think TPC were gonna last long. And then 3-D gets busted all up after getting thrown into the wall by Slasher! Man, if you like blood and violence.. this was a match for you. Even going into the bathroom, and then Slasher cut up by a mirror shot! Reyna with the chair on Falcon, in the ring. Slasher with a Powerslam on the floor! Man! Dynamite got up before the 10 count though. DOUBLE SUPERPLEX off a COUNTER by TPC! WOW! SUPERBOMB OFF THE COUNTER TOO! And The Dynasty finished TPC off with a Gordbuster off the counter on top of 3-D and then the EACH splashed them off the counter! NEW CHAMPS!! The Dynasty rules! They definitely had the edge in this matchup.. the biggest and baddest team in this sport win the titles! What a Match!! Interview #2: "The Showstopper" Simon Lebec This was a cool comment... "What I don't understand is trying to grab the biggest bull in the herd by the horns, and thinkin' it's rodeo time......especially when you're nothing but a calf." And it was hillarious when Lebec challenged Brad Shannon! hehehe! I look forward to the next Garavello/Lebec matchup. Commercial #1: The Crow vs Matt "Techman" Sutherland Another great commercial.. they may be small, but they sound cool. It should be quite an interesting matchup.. Its going to be a close matchup, that's for sure. Match #2: "The Silent Assassin" Kurt Ryder vs Bob Big Bad Bob with a 1 night AAPW license. Is that a good sign, or a bad? I personally like Bob. He's as hillarious as Brian "Scabs" Rickard. A decent matchup.. back and forth with some good moves... Including the 271 pound Top Rope ELBOW DROP! Then missed a big dive off the top, and Pain Chamber time.. I liked how Reed described it this time (Indian Death Lock and hooking a hammerlock and crossface). Those who don't know what the hold is, do now! :-) Bob of course, had to tap out. But Ryder wouldn't break it. And then Somerset yells that that's going to be Fallen Angel on the 28th.. I think FA might have something to say about that. But we shall see.. we shall see.. Match #3: The Punishers vs Derek Lankford & Jason Speece Gotta love the start.. Slaps to the face! Double Brainbuster! Probably wouldn't hurt anymore than a regular one, but a cool double team move by The Punishers, none the less. Lankford kicked out though.. probably too early in the match. Speece with that cool Belly to belly overhead suplex! And executes a Hangman's Neckbreaker.. I'm not really sure what that is, but it sounds cool. It sounded like something was up with the new players club here.. they weren't entering the ring from the apron.. TOTAL PUNISHMENT!!Cool that the description was added too. After that move.. well.. can you say 1,2,3? And after the match, Stacey and Rayne get into it verbally. Things definitely aren't 100% with these guys. Interview #3: Wayne Hanson Man, the fans hate this guy. Should be a good matchup between him and K-Solo at the NOC Free 4 All. I liked the comment "These moronic fans cheer Mafia like he's some kind of God. Well, if that's true, then I'm the #(@*in Devil.. and I'm gonna turn Heaven inside out to get what I want!" Although, I'm kinda biased on this one. :-) I also thought "Watch your back, Sutherland.. Or better yet.. DON'T watch your back. It'll be easier to stick the dagger in." was quite unique. Commercial #2: The Overgiver T-shirt Nice opening for the commercial.. telling the potential buyers to shut the hell up isn't how you win over customers.. Although, with the Overfans.. I guess it is! :-) Match #4: The Suicidal Society vs The Bonecrushers The Punishers attack The Bonecrushers! What is this, a regular event in The Bonecrusher's matches? The Blondes go to work.. Nice Tilt-a-whirl and that kick to the gut/rocker dropper combo was nice. The Suicidal Slingshot! A devistating move, but no cover attempt. But then Aaron Lankford took control with a few good moves.. a BIG string of them even, then tagged Reed in who continued the assault. But then The Blondes came back and hit a Suicidal Superplex to Lankford! But Aaron KICKED OUT!! He's a tough one as he kicks out of a Bulldog too! Then the Punishers came down AGAIN and took out Lankford.. Reed goes out to help, and The Blondes win it by countout! Quite the matchup! Interview #4: "Primetime" Danny Ice Another ridicule of the fans. And of The Overgiver. A dispicable.. short.. although good.. flash. Congrats to him winning the WWW Heavyweight Title, by the way. Match #5: "Primetime" Danny Ice vs The Overgiver The help up, short arm clothesline by The Overgiver was great! As was the Suplex drape over the top rope followed by an uppercut which sent Ice over the top! I thought The Overgiver would get the advantage out there, but it was Ice with a springboard dropkick sending The Overgiver into the guardrail, busting him open. But that didn't last as The Overgiver moved out of the way of an asai moonsault and Danny Ice nailed the guardrail. Then a chair shot.. Clothesline off the top, but a kickout by Ice. Frankenstiener by Ice, but kickout. OW when they both straddled the top rope! SILICON VALLEY SPECIAL OFF THE TOP AND THROUGH A TABLE!! ACK! Ok, that should finish it. It was just a formality to hit The California High Moonsault Leg-Drop. A pretty good match, all in all. Match #6: The Punishers vs Tag-Team Champs, The Dynasty of Destruction in a steel cage The Falcon flies early, curtesy of The Punishers.. and flies into the cage! But later, Frost gets Slingshotted into the cage by Falcon! And then the Bear Hug/Flying Clothesline move was cool. Slasher HOTSHOT onto an exposed turnbuckle!! I think I see Williams teeth in the 5th row.. Then a chair gets into the action. Piledriver onto the chair! OW! Then The Bonecrushers arrived.. obviously to try and cost The Punishers the match.. And that they did as they pulled Mason Frost out of the cage and Lankford Bulldogged him on the concrete! Meanwhile, that left The Dynasty to hit a Super Brainbuster for the win! I'm glad for The Dynasty. They are one cool tag-team and deserve to be the champs. We'll see how long they can hold onto them now! 1.2 AAPW Virtual Hotline Option 1: The Word on The Street with A.J. Bruce gives the camera a big grin.) DUNDEE: G'day AAPW and welcome to th' LOOKING GLASS, where we'll be takin' a closer look at the upcoming bout b'tween Derek Lankford and Danny Ice for the finals of the Light-Heavyweight tournament. Should be a big one right up, and you, the viewer wants the inside scope, eh? (Bruce truns to a new angle. Behind him pops up a graphic of Derek Lankford.) DUNDEE: Derek Lankford, you know 'im, ya hate 'im, right? What's really to say? He held a coupla belts and everything. Tags with an overrated partner who barely manages to keep the TV belt in hand ('long with a chair). Member of the New Player's Club, gets managed by the fine sheila Stacy Ka$h. Should be a shoo-in for the finals slot, right? Well, lets take a closer look. (Clips of Derek Lankford in WWW appear on the screen.) DUNDEE: Derek Lankford, like his opponent, wrestles both in AAPW on the East Coast, and in WWW on the West. Earlier in the year he was more active out West, trying to take command of the cruiserweight division in Wild West Wrestling. Out there he showed some true grit, mixing it up with the Fallen Angel, the Amazing Love Machine, and the Mand'rin. Buncha tough bastards they are. Based on that, you'd think that if he didn't have the talent, (and he's got that) then that pair of brass knuckles and a chair would see him through to some gold on a strap. But it didn't happen. Lankford was denied and so he turned his energies East, trying to muscle in on AAPW. Again he mixed up with TALM and the Angel, but here the pickin's were a little easier. After a tear-jerking grudge match with long-time flunkie and pal Jason Speece, Lankford started wheeling and dealing again, amassing an impressive record. But there's a hidden catch, and you have to look closely to find it... (Clips of Lankford/Speece vs the Punishers (Primetime 7/13) are shown.) DUNDEE: What we see here is a different Lankford...one who wants to use his body to mix it up with the opponent. One who is willing to stand by and watch his partner get clobbered and almost pinned. In fact, one who seems to have lost his whole game-plan. There was no chair. There were no brass knuckles. There was just wrestling, and while he showed technical competence, it wasn't just competence that was going ta save the Speece/Lankford team. In fact, the Punishers man-handled them severely. Lankford limped away...Speece looked pissed. So what's happening here? Is this a new side to Derek Lankford? What looked like a sure bet now seems a little questionable... (Bruce turns again. The graphic is of Danny Ice.) DUNDEE: Especially against this man, the Primetime boy himself, Danny Ice. Ice is a newcomer to AAPW, and has a busy schedule trying ta keep up with some minah leagues and the competition on the West Coast in WWW. But after a upset victory over Heartbreaker Matt Foxx at the Welch Cup tournament, Ice is showing that he can mix it up with the biggest boys around. He flys high, he hit hard. Matt Foxx found out, s well as several WWW wrestlers when Ice challenged anyone in the uilding to try and take him out. Danny Ice is a hard fella to figure but. Three weeks ago he's a nobody making noise, now he's a champion and people are starting to pay attention. He's got the moves, he has the will, now can he bring it together against Lankford, especially when Lankford looks so weak? (Clips of Danny Ice in various matches are shown, focussing on his use to the Cherry Blossom Bomber.) DUNDEE: That move is one of his trademarks and possibly the most dangerous move in his arsenal. He's quick to lay that one on, and it is devastating to whatever it hits. You wouldn't think that Lankford could survive much of that punishment. He does have a weak spot or three though... (Clips of Danny Ice getting pinned in EWA are shown) DUNDEE: Ice is by no means unpinnable. He's a flyer, sure, but he can't handle being cooped on the ground. He needs time to set up his game. That's time that Derek Lankford can use to his advantage, if he can lay a finger on Ice. Also Ice is a streaky wrestler. He has a method to him, and right now, he's set up to wrestle only on AAPW PRIMETIME. That's fine enough, but the semi-finals and finals are on Saturday Night Superstars, and Saturday is a day Ice usually has reserved for the West Coast. That being said, it's pretty easy to understand that when he's out East this Saturday night, his body'll wanna believe it's in LA. Not a pretty sight...like assuming you're chundering of Bondai Pier instead of bein' in New Zealand...could give ol' Derek the chance he needs. All he has to do is throw Ice off his game for a couple of minutes and he can likely turn it into a win... (Bruce turns to front again) DUNDEE: So you're askin' "What bloke's taking the show, mate? Which one of these wrestlers is going to make the finals?" That's a tough question. Based on the most recent performance, ya havta say Danny Ice. But good ol' Lankford isn't going to make it easy on him. No matter which one takes this match, his opponent will make it so hard for him, that the winner of the Crow/Sutherland match might be wrestling only a shell of what he could be. I'm Bruce Dundee, under the LOOKING GLASS!
