Wishbone tricked Mafia and gave him a chair shot to be followed by Hanson's Guillotine Legdrop.. 1.1 House Show Update AAPW Weekend Warriors, Sunday, September, 13, 1997 Marine Midland Arena, Buffalo, New York -Pablo Santuccio and Joey Zochenspitz went to a ten minute draw. -The Cutting Edge defeated The Choir Boys in 12:08 after nailing "Direct Hostility" -"Yukon" Jack Roberts defeated John Jennings with a the "Yukon Slam" in 4:59. -J.J. Wishbone defeated "The Italian Love Machine" Tony Deluccia after a piledriver in 6:54. -The Tokyo Blades and The Trailer Park Assassins went to a double count-out after all four men failed to answer the 10 count. -"Dashing" Derek Lankford retained his Light-Heavyweight Title by defeating "Electric" Eric Starr in 13:47 with "The Lankford Leglock". -The Overgiver retained his TV Title by defeating Gemini in 17:43 after a "Over-cutter" -The Punishers and The Dynasty of Destruction went to a no-Contest after The Bonecrushers attacked both teams. -Junior Mafia defeated Michael Leonard in 14:58 after nailing the sommersault leg-drop off the top. -Wayne Hanson retained the AAPW Heavyweight Title by defeating "The Perfect Male Speciman" Jason Speece after a Guillotine legdrop. PRIME Monday Night, Monday, September 15, 1997 Catholic Youth Center, Scranton, Pa. -"Cactus" Bubba Steed defeated "Hotdog" Mack Brown in 3:47 after the "Cactus" drop. -The Bonecrushers defeated Apocalypse Now when the Bonecrushers nailed a bulldog off the top after 19:05. - The White Angel and "Too Slick" Rick Brown fought to a 15 minute draw. -The American Enforcer and "The Demon" Damien Lestat went to a double DQ after 11:27. -K-SOLO defeated The Crow in 15:04 after SOLO nailed the SOLO-Breaker. -"The Showstopper" Simon Lebec retained his Atlantic Title by defeating Luis "The Kid" Garavello. -Black Flagg defeated "Primetime" Danny Ice after a tombstone piledriver in 12:39. -The Suicide Blondes retained the AAPW Atlantic Tag Titles by defeating The Grinders in 18:53. -The Overgiver and The Fallen Angel went to a no-contest in a match for the TV Title. -Dane Phipps won a 10-man battle royal in 24:58 eliminating last J.J. Wishbone to get a shot at the AAPW Heavyweight title. -Wayne Hanson defeated Dane Phipps in 19:21 and retained the AAPW Heavyweight Title. AAPW Tuesday Night Superstars, Tuesday, September, 16, 1997 Springfield Civic Center, Springfield, Massachusetts -The Cutting Edge defeated The Choir Boys in 10:16 after Cutting Edge nailed Direct Hostility. -Luis "The Kid" Garavello defeated "Cashpot" Chris Green in 11:37 after a moonsault. -"Yukon" Jack Roberts defeated Gemini in 7:38 after he nailed a nasty powerbomb. -The Suicide Blondes defeated The East Coast West Coast Killaz in 14:08 after 1-900-SUICIDE, to retain the AAPW Atlantic Tag Titles. -"The Perfect Male Specimen" Jason Speece defeated "Cactus" Bubba Steed in 16:08 after The Perfectbomb. - The White Angel defeated "The Technician" Michael Leonard at 19:59 with a top rope sunset flip. -The Punishers retained the Tag-team titles by defeating The Tokyo Blades in 21:34. -The Overgiver retained his TV Title after defeating "The Italian Love Machine" Tony DeLuccia at 21:29 with The Overbomb. -The Trailer Park Assassins defeated The Stylistics in 22:34 after The White Trash Compactor. -"The Showstopper" Simon Lebec defeated Travis Turner in 18:43 to retain the Atlantic title. -Wayne Hanson defeated Junior Mafia in 23:52 after he nailed "The Gullotine Leg Drop" to retain the AAPW Heavyweight Championship. AAPW Arena Revisited, Wednesday, September 17, 1997 AAPW Arena, Secaucus, New Jersey -The Overgiver defeated Pablo Santuccio in 2:38 when Pablo Santuccio missed a splash and The Overgiver Overbombed him. OG retained the TV Title -The Players Club defeated The Brown Brothers in 5:42 after Danny Dynamite nailed Mack with a leg drop off the top. - "Cactus" Bubba Steed defeated "The Demon" Damien Lestat at 16:17 with The Cactus Drop -The Bonecrushers defeated The Trailer Park Assassins in 19:09 after nailing a bulldog off the top. -The Crow defeated Mike Trent in 23:09 after a locking on "The Big Goodbye" -The Punishers held on to the Tag Titles by defeating The Stylistics in 23:47. - Black Flagg defeated "Yukon" Jack Roberts at 17:58 after a powerbomb -"The Showstopper" Simon Lebec retained the Atlantic Title by defeating "The Miracle Man" Tyler Smythe in 25:58. -The Punishers and The Suicide Blondes went to a 30:00 minute time-limit draw in a title vs title match. - Junior Mafia and Wayne Hanson fought to a double disqualification at 16:46 when various weapons got involved..They fought for about 10 minutes and into the concession stands until security could break them up.. 2.0 AAPW Arena PRIMETIME review by "The Grim Reaper" [name withheld] Start: Nice change of pace again as this time Jeff Reed and Jeandra were IN the ring to talk about what was going to go down on the card. Match #1: "The Jewish Heartbreaker" Joey Zochenspitz vs The White Angel Battle of the fan favorites here. The White Angel a definite favorite coming into it, even though it was his AAPW debut. Joey has looked impressive in prior matches here though, and did so again in this matchup. A number of lucha libre type moves by both men. Eventually a standing Hurrican-Rana followed by a springboard moonsault wins it for the former FoPEW Light-Heavyweight Champion. Match #2: "The Demon" Damien Lestat vs "The Russian Superman" Petor Andresku Both guys I feel could be contenders for the Heavyweight Title, given time, but only 1 of them can go another rung up the ladder here. It went back and forth both in and out of the ring. Lestat actually got busted open by a chair shot and ring post whip by Andresku. It continued to go back and forth until a leaping Chair Shot busted Petor open. Lestat then hit that top rope "Hoof" elbow drop and got the 3 count. Match #3: "The Showstopper" Simon Lebec vs "Miracle Man" Tyler Smythe for the AAPW Atlantic Title Well, The Overgiver and Wayne Hanson attacked Francoiis before the match. Hanson even Piledrove him on the concrete! Smythe got a shot on Lebec from behind. The TV Champ and Hanson then carried Miss Crystal to the back. The Atlantic champ cheating early by choking Lebec with his own jacket and then hitting him in the head with the Atlantic Title Belt! Before the bell even rang, Lebec was out. Smythe continued to dominate the match until a surprize flying forearm turned the tide. I don't know how he came back, but Lebec got a string of moves together and worked the champion over on the floor. Smythe regained control after a reverse DDT off a counter, but it didn't last long as Lebec countered a move and hit The Blackball Back Brain Kick and got the 3 count. I don't know how he was able to take all the punishment and still win, but history was made, and we have a new Atlantic Champion. After the match, The Overgive, Wayne Hanson and Tyler Smythe messed Lebec up even further. Smythe took the belt after this and they walked to the back. Commercial #1: "Dashing" Derek Lankford vs The Crow This advertized the upcoming Light-Heavyweight Title Defense of Lankford. This should be a good matchup on SNS next week. Interview #1: The Cutting Edge Laser and Blade talked about their match with Da Killaz and the loss they suffered, but the focus was on getting telivised matches for more exposure. [name withheld] even came down and offered The Cutting Edge a match against The Player's Club next week! These guys will be huge underdogs, but these guys just might be able to pull off an upset. I guess we'll have to wait and see! A nice face interview all in all. :-) Match #4: The Tokyo Blades vs "The Amazing Love Machine" Dane Phipps & The Fallen Angel The Blades with a sneak attack spit red mist into the eyes of FA & TALM. Then went to the choke and finally with some great tag-team moves. The Angel and Phipps regained control though and even showed some good teamwork of their own. The Kamikaze Drop diamond cutter coulda had it, but FA broke the count. Somerset joined the broadcast booth mid-match. The Blades then pulled a move out of The Suicide Blonde's playbook at Wakizashi superplexed Katana onto Phipps. Phipps almost took a Lightning Strike, but was able to somewhat avoid it. Some great moves by both teams leading to Somerset entering the fray by hitting Katana with her cell phone and Phipps hits The Lovetap and gets the 3 count. The Mandarin attacked FA after the match, and The Blades hit a Lightning Strike as well as a Decapitator on TALM, and even a Lightning strike to the referee. Commercial #2: The Cutting Edge vs The Players Club We heard the announcement earlier in the night, and now this little commercial just enforced what was already known. The Favorites vs The Underdogs.. who do most people root for? You got it.. Match #5: The Stylistics & "The Original Studmuffin" [name withheld] vs The Trailer Park Assassins & "Cactus Bubba Steed Steed and Curtis started it out but there were a lot of tags and saves in this one. Spare was very funny in the match too (or the comments about him). We finally got to see "The Original Studmuffin" get his butt hicked by Cooter Lee Roy, and then Bubba Steed, and then Orbit. White Trash Compactor and an easy 3 count. Next time, The Styles should get a better teammate before going into a 6 man match. Match #6: Black Flagg vs The American Enforcer Flagg took control early and didn't relinquish until The Enforcer reversed a Vertical Suplex. A German Suplex was followed by the Flagg Bomb but a kickout! The American Enforcer did something uncharacteristic as he went downstairs and then hit a belly to back superplex. He then went for his finisher, but Flagg ducked it and hit a Tombstone Piledriver for the 3 count. Black Flagg could be the first man to be both NCW (original) World Heavyweight Champion as well as AAPW Heavyweight Champion.. We shall see.. Match #7: The Dynasty of Destruction vs The Punishers for the AAPW Heavyweight Tag-Team Titles The Champions' double gordbuster was way cool. It was a back and forth matchup as expected. Then Aaron Lankford came down with a chair.. maybe stuff like that DOES run in the family.. Falcon goes face first into the chair off a reversal and Mason Frost got the roll up and the 3 count as history was made for the 2nd time tonight. After the match, Aaron tried to apologize, but The Dynasty took him out instead. Commercial #3: Tower of Doom A match coming up at the AAPW/WWW Coastal Combat. 40 feet high tower of doom enwraped in steel. It'll be 10 men in there.. I can't wait. Match #8: Wayne Hanson vs Junior Mafia for the AAPW Heavyweight Title Mafia took control early, head to the steel steps cut Hanson over and then went to work back in the ring. A reversal of a whip in allowed Hanson to hit a Hotshot to turn the tide. He then took it outside and used a chair on Mafia. He hit a Piledriver and then a Gordbuster, but only got a 2 count on the champ. Later, he missed his top rope legdrop finisher though and Junior Mafia went back on the attack.. Chokeslam and Superplex, but no 3 count. The referee went down and Tyler Smythe ran down from the back. J.J. Wishbone then came down and WISHBONE SUCKERED MAFIA WITH A CHAIR SHOT!!! Oh Man! That came out of left field! Wayne Hits the Guillotine Legdrop, and Smythe rolls the ref over to count the 3. History made for the 3rd time tonight! What a turn of events there.. I was just totally shocked that J.J. would do such a thing! The Overgiver came down and the 4 celebrated. If there was ever going to be a 4th man in their little group, J.J. Wishbone would have been WAY down the list of possible candidates. But it may be true. In any case, we have 3 new champions to a very exciting AAPW Primetime. Until next time.. 2.1 AAPW Virtual Hotline Option 1: The Word on the Street with A.J. Word on the street is that Somerset uses these matches to scout both Phipps & Angel and The Blades, being that The Blondes have had minimal success with The Tokyo Blades at best...The femme and self-proclaimed victim of harassment will make an appearance this weekend.. - J.J. what have you done??
