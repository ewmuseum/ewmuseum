LIVE!!! From The Fleet Center in Boston, Massachusetts Free 4 All ---------- Match #1: The Brown Brothers vs The Cutting Edge Ok, I pretty much figured Cutting Edge would take this one easily.. Even though we haven't seen them before (in play by play action), The Brown Brothers aren't exactly in the top 5. It was much closer than I thought it'd be though. Laser's Moonsault onto both of the opponents was pretty cool. Mack Brown's Spinebuster was great too. Mack Brown then missed a headbutt off the top.. I think I have some extra advil(tm) for Mr. Brown.. Good tag teamwork to finish the match as The Cutting Edge hit Direct Hostility.. A cool Spin Kick off the top/Powerbomb combo. The AAPW tag-team scene is hard to break through in, but these 2 may be able to do it, given time. Interview #1: The Grinders & "Dashing" Derek Lankford I loved how this incorporated both The Grinders and Lankford like that. I actually thought Lankford might join The Grinders.. but then, that would have been just too weird. But they ARE going to team up in a 6 man cage match..That will be something. A nice interview all around from all parties. Match #2: Wayne Hanson vs K-Solo If you wanted brawling, this was a match for you. Both men started out with it, then a Gordbuster busts K-Solo's nose! OW! I thought all the comments about Lantz Scarb were pretty funny.. A direct reflect of my last AAPW Review perhaps. :-) They fought on the outside for quite a while.. Hanson's Clothesline to the back of the head was cool. He then went to work with a Chair on Solo, busting his forehead open! I hope Solo picked up some Ace Bandage Stock, cause he may personally make it go up 10 points. It was funny that people actually were getting behind Hanson a bit, as was Wishbone's comment about it.. "First The Overgiver NOW Wayne Hanson..These people really have to straighten there minds out." Hanson hit a Clothesline off the top? Rarely do we see him go up there, but it worked (but not enough to get the 3 count). K-Solo's Release German Suplex was great! Then a Running Powerbomb! Wow! But his offense was short lived once they got back to the floor.. Eventually Wayne hit a Piledriver off the apron through a table! K-Solo had to be dead.. A fan had a Wok?? Uhh.. ok! Hanson got his head busted open with it too. Now, both men are bleeding from the head. Solo hits the ref over the head with a steel chair. I see a fine in K-Solo's future. Even Lantz Scarb calls for the bell after that. Hanson with a DQ win. I don't see how anything was settled between the 2.. Main Card --------- Match #1: The Grinders ("The Ambusher" Mike Trent & "The Technician" Michael Leonard) vs The Players Club Trent started out a house on fire, then each team tagged. 3-D started taking it to Leonard. Shortly afterwards, there was a surprizing blind tag by The Technician.. Something I'd expect from a vetran team like TPC, not a new team like Trent & Leonard. The Grinders then SPIKED PILEDROVE Reyna! It woulda been over if Dynamite hadn't have broken the count, IMHO. Trent then busted Reyna open with punches? I woulda thought it was from the Piledriver.. But anyway, Reyna hit Trent with a DDT! 3-D's Somersault Plancha over the top to Leonard was cool, as was the Double Dropkick, Danny Dynamite's swinging DDT off the 2nd rope, and of course Leonard's Fisherman's Buster off the top. I thought for sure The Players Club were going to win this one, but it was The Grinders who got more near falls. Trent with a low blow and then a Tiger Bomb and he got the 3 count!! They may not have been legal, but as I was reading the match, I didn't even realize that. A big upset to open the card! Interview #1: Lady Rage w/ Apocalypse Now Apocalypse Now is returning to action. And they are looking at The Stylistics to start. A cool come back.. unexpected entrance. We'll see if they'll be able to do well in the highly competitive tag-team scene here in the AAPW. Match #2: The Overgiver vs "The Perfect Male Specimen" Jason Speece for the AAPW Television Title Speece started out right, going to the technical wrestling book, but then he decided to brawl with The Overgiver.. big mistake. Short Clothesline and a 2nd Rope Knee Drop. The Overgiver took over once it went into that department.. Cleanup on Aisle 4. However, a reversal of an irish whip turned the tide and the TV Champ went back to work hittins a Swinging Neckbreaker and then a Piledriver. Later a Powerbomb couldn't put The Overgiver away. Then, Speece went to the Suplexes.. Hit just about every type of suplex.. ok, maybe not that many, but still.. it was quite a few. The Overgiver countered a pick up with a Slingshot into the ringpost and then a cover, and we have a new TV Champion! I didn't think The Overgiver would be able to pull it off, but he did! Congrats to the only new champion of the night! Commercial #1: Best of Night of Champions: Volume One A nice commercial here. I liked the clips of the different wrestlers dueling it out. Also, how it gave us a look back at some of the past matches and angles in the AAPW. Match #3: The Fallen Angel & "The Amazing Love Machine" Dane Phipps vs The Suicide Blondes in a Texas Tornado style, no DQ, no Countout Ladder Match for the AAPW Atlantic Tag-Team Titles The Blondes with the tag-team experience, although FA & TALM have the wrestling experience. A rematch of the finals of the Welch Cup. And with all these stipulations, it was sure to be quite a matchup. It was back and forth from the opening bell. The Double Handspring Suicide Dives by Fallen Angel and Dane Phipps were cool! They were showing good teamwork.. Double Suplex Conner onto the ladder and then give him a Reverse Suplex, face first on the ladder! Man! Then King came in with a Chair and nailed FA.. Talm gets hit with a Chair Spinwheel Kick! And then he dove off near the top of the 11 foot ladder onto Phipps. Conner Tigerbombed FA on the ladder! I bet he had to re-arange his mask after that one. Both teams really used the Ladder against their opponents well. King got hit with The Lovetap and the Angel hit a Moonsault onto Conner over the railing! A Suicidal Suplex off the top of the ladder, but FA broke the pin with a Plancha. FA & TALM hit a Double Love Tap to The Blondes, but they both KICKED OUT of the pins! An Elbow Drop off the top of the ladder onto Phipps won it for King and Sommerset's Suicide Blondes. It seemed like The Atlantic Tag-Team Champs were getting bigger and more pops than the fan favorites.. other than that, it was a great matchup. Afterwards, The Tokyo Blades attacked FA & TALM!! They hit The Decapitator on Dane Phipps and then took off! Does this mean The WWW Tag-Team Champs are in the AAPW now too? If so, it spells bad news for some of the teams here.. Match #4: Kevin "The Cobra" Richards vs "Dashing" Derek Lankford for the AAPW Light-Heavyweight Title The start of this one was ALL Richards. He even busted Lankford open early. But shortly after that, Lankford took over with a long string of moves in his own right. The Cobra Strike on the table woulda been cool.. probably would have ended the match too soon though. Richards pulls a Bret Hart with the ringpost figure 4 (which was way cool the first time I saw it). Lankford came back, but then got hit with a surprize Cobra Strike European Piledriver. But didn't go for the pin? Mistake? It sure was. He went up top, and missed an elbow drop. Lankford sinched in a Fisherman's Suplex and retained the title. Not a bad matchup.. Much like the Hanson/Solo match, I don't think this settled anything.. Interview #2: Kevin "The Cobra" Richards Wow.. a class act as Richards congradulates Lankford on the win and shakes his hand! But NO! The Cobra hits his finisher after a swift gut kick! Then he humiliates him by slapping him in the face! Even Lura got into it with a Legdrop! That was pretty cool.. :-) I was VERY surprized when I saw Kevin shake Derek's hand.. And now I know WHY I was so surprized. :-) Commercial #2: The Bonecrushers vs The Punishers A nice little commercial, as always. It is a cool way to promote the upcoming matches. I look forward to this upcoming match between 2 former tag-team champions. (NCWF & AAPW respectivly) Match #5: Matt "Techman" Sutherland vs "The Miracle Man" Tyler Smythe for the AAPW Atlantic Title Quite the back and forth battle here. A great matchup. The Half Nelson Belly to Back Suplex by the champ was great! Smythe pulling out all the stops, taking it to the air with a spinning heel kick.. using the power with a Tiger Bomb.. brawling with the stomps and chps and clotheslines.. Even cool moves like the Slingshot Gordbuster. But Matt came back. I loved where he slinged himself over the top rope onto Smythe and his manager. And of course the Reverse DDT was great too. Then, Simon Lebec came out.. A cool little run in flash in the middle of the match. He says he's going to take out the winner on August 9th.. I don't know about that, but he does have the shot. A Superplex by The Techman looked good, and then a Dragon Suplex.. but The Champ just kept kicking out. However, Sutherland went for a backdrop and got caught with the Smallest Package Suplex for the 3 count. Congrats to Tyler Smythe. He deserved that one. But now what's going to happen with Matt Sutherland? Hmmm.. Interview #3: Damien "The Demon" Lestat I wanna know if this guy can say a sentence without swearing.. Sheesh! ;-) He has a cool talking style though.. calling American Enforcer a Yahoo all the time and The President, Prezzy.. The dude seems pretty mean.. plus he carries a human heart in a bag. Keep it away from me.. I hate the smell of those things when they aren't in the body. Commercial #3: "Primetime" Danny Ice vs The Crow Two champions gonna lock it up. Although neither did as well as many thought they would in the LH Tournament, The Crow is a former TV Champ, and Danny Ice is the WWW Heavyweight Champ. It should be quite a match. If only The Dashing One and/or The Grinders keep their noses out of it. Interview #4: The Trailer Park Assassins in The House of Cardz with "Ace" Alex Porteaux They talked about their World Tag-Team Title match. However, TPA also want The Stylistics in the ring.. That will be quite a matchup. I love that Orbit guy.. He has some of the wittiest "comments". ;-) Match #6: The Trailer Park Assassins vs The Dynasty of Destruction for the AAPW Tag-Team Titles My 2 favorite tag-teams in the AAPW went at it in this one.. Cooter Lee Roy Powerslammed Falcon! Man! And then Orbit with an Inverted DDT. Falcon with the equilizer.. a Chair, which he used on Orbit. That busted him open but that didn't stop Orbit from hitting a Bulldog off the top rope as a counter to a Falcon Superplex.. However, Cooter wasn't so lucky.. Falcon nailed the Superplex on him. The Slasher Chokeslammed Orbit, which was cool, but Orbit kicked out! The Hospitalizer put an end to Orbit and The champs retain the titles! A great match that amazingly stayed in the ring! I was glad to see it.. And only 1 chair got involved too.. Whew.. I was thinking this was becoming ECW or something.. (not that I don't like ECW, but..) Match #7: "Yukon" Jack Roberts vs Junior Mafia in a 2 out of 3 falls, no DQ, falls count anywhere match for the AAPW Heavyweight Title The main event looked to be quite an encounter. It started off slow as both men had respect for each other, but quickly it turned to brawling. Roberts got the advantage there, but it wouldn't last. A slingshot plancha by the champion and then worked him over on the outside, but Mafia missed a move, and "Yukon" took over, hitting a Gordbuster onto a row of chairs! He then proceeded to Powerbomb Mafia through a table and got the 1st pinfall. You know.. If it would have been a 1 fall match, we'd have a new champion! But there was at least 1 more fall to go.. The Bloodied Junior Mafia got a chair and hit him over and over in the head, breaking the challenger open as well. They were well into the stands when Mafia used a video camera to the back of Roberts. He then drank a soda a fan handed him. Nice little addition there. Roberts sent into a wall and then into the bathroom stalls. Mafia slingshots him into the mirror and covered for the 3 count after that.. 1 fall a piece. Mafia got a toaster from a fan and hit Roberts in his busted up head.. I agree with Reed when he said, "Where in the hell did they get that...". Jack sent Mafia down a staircase and then back to ringside where they went back to brawling. A Squaredriver by Mafia through a table finished Jack Roberts off. Junior Mafia is still the champion! A very cool and violent matchup. Additional Comments: Well, guess what? All members of the AAPW team for the upcoming NCW/AAPW War Games match are Champions now.. [name withheld] AAPW, Well after six months of staying relatively quiet, I'm finally doing one of these State of the Federation addresses...FIrst I'd like to say that we've definitely come a long way from that first (and forgettable) Night of Champions I, which was probably the worst card ever at anything :)...The players we had then and now put a lot of faith into Kevin and I to put out the product that we promised and overall I think we've done that... In the last six months you've seen new champions, two-time champions, tons of 3- and 4-way dances...My personal favorite was the ten-man tag in Disney World and everybody seems to enjoy the original 60 Minutes of Hell match between The Overgiver and Kevin "The Cobra" Richards...Kevin and I have tried to keep the ideas as fresh as possible, with speciality matches, new titles, a combination "young, new" and "old, established veteran" talent.. I can safely say that I have the luxury of having some of the most involved and creatives players of any fed and with such great participation my job has become that much easier...The reason for the new titles, the good shows, the great action, and the fun times are because of YOU...You guys makes things happen here and to all of you that have been champions so far, I say that you have been the best of the best and congratulations...
