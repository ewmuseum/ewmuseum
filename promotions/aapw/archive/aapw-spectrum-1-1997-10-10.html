Welcome to the first edition of The AAPW Spectrum, a new name for what will pan out to be a new upgrade to the AAPW Magazine and it will be come more interactive as you, the handlers will have a much larger part in the production of the new issue...This is the first of what will be several upgrades and I hope you enjoy them all...A pre-warning, the first issue is under construction of it won't usually be this small and disoriented... Corey 1.1 House Show Updates AAPW Weekend Warriors, Sunday, October 5, 1997 Rupp Arena, University of Kentucky, Lexington, Kentucky -Apocalypse Now defeated The Grinders (Leonard/Trent) in 10:23, after a small package on Trent. -Gemini destroyed Pablo Santuccio and one a 'Mexican Death Match' after powerbombing Santuccio through a table in 3:48. -'The Miricle Man' Tyler Smythe defeated 'Cashpot' Chris Green in 10:08 he won with 'The Smallest Package' -Jack Roberts and J.J. Wishbone went to a double countout in 12:54. -The Dynasty of Destruction and The Bonecrushers also went to a double countout in 17:28. -'The Showstopper' Simon Lebec retained the Atlantic Title after defeating Jason Speece in 21:03. -The Suicide Blondes defeated The Cutting Edge in 23:41 after nailing 1-900 SUICIDE. -Junior Mafia defeated 'Cactus' Bubba Steed in a 'Toughman Style match' with a DDT onto a chair in 13:47. -The Punishers retained the AAPW Tag-Team CHampionships by defeating The Tokyo Blades in 30:13. -Wayne Hanson retained the AAPW Heavyweight Title by defeating The American Enforcer in 19:58. PRIME Monday Night, Monday, October 6, 1997 Gampel Pavillion, University of Connecticut, Storrs, Connecticut -Pablo Santuccio defeated Brian 'Scabs' Rickard in a street fight after choking 'Scabs' out with his PTA T-shirt in 12:08. -The Trailer Park Assassins defeated The Italian Clown Mafia in 5:42 after nailing The White Trash Compactor. -'Dashing' Derek Lankford retained his Light-Heavyweight Title by defeating The Overgiver in 31:29 with the Lankford Leg Lock. -The East/West Coast Killaz and The Cutting Edge went to a 20:00 draw in a spectacular match-up. -The Fallen Angel defeated Luis 'The Kid' Garavello with a moonsault in 15:46. -K-SOLO defeated The Crow with a small package in 22:38. -The Tokyo Blades defeated The Choir Boys in 12:38 after nailing 'Lightning Strike' -The Punishers defeated both The Bonecrushers and The Dynasty of Destruction to retain the tag belts after 45:06. -'The Showstopper' Simon Lebec defeated 'The Amazing Love Machine' Dane Phipps to retain the AAPW Atlantic Title. -Wayne Hanson retained the AAPW Heavyweight Title by defeating former Champion Junior Mafia. Tuesday Night Superstars, Tuesday, October 7, 1997 duPont Pavillon, Villanova University, Villanova, Pennsylvania -Black Flagg defeated 'Hotdog' Mack Brown after nailing a Diamond Cutter in 11:06. -The Overgiver retained his TV Title by defeating Michael Leonard in 13:49 after nailing The Overbomb. -'The Demon' Damien Lestat defeated Pablo Santuccio after nailing 'The Hoof' elbow drop. -'Cactus Bubba Steed defeated 'The Original Studmuffin' [name withheld] in a special challenge match. -The Fallen Angel/Dane Phipps fought to a draw with The Tokyo Blades in 30:00. -Jason Speece defeated K-SOLO in 24:59 after locking on the The Perfectbomb. -The Suicide Blondes defeated The East/West Coast Killaz to retain the Atlantic Tag Titles. -'Dashing' Derek Lankford defeated 'Too Slick' Rick Brown in 12:38 to retain his Light-Heavyweight Title. -The Punishers retained the tag titles after defeating the combo of Wishbone and Smythe. -Wayne Hanson retained his Heavyweight Title by defeating Jack Roberts in 23:46. 2.0 The Pressure Cooker with A.J. From Cole Field House, University of Maryland, College Park, Md Interview #1: The Cutting Edge The Cutting Edge saying how offensive Simon Lebec is. I couldn't agree more. I hope Lebec does get in their way, so they can beat him to a pulp. They then went on to talk about The Suicide Blondes. Since they are the #1 contenders, they want an Atlantic Tag-Team Title Shot.. but first, they have to beat Da Killaz.. Match #1: The Cutting Edge vs The East Coast/West Coast Killaz Cool Dropkick in mid-handspring by Laser on L-Train. The Backdrop hold and sommersault legdrop combo by Da Killaz was great. I liked this match, cause I didn't have any idea who'd come out on top. What a move with the elbow drop to the outside by Laser! Man! The Springboard Moonsault onto L-Train was cool too. L-Train Superfrankensteinered Laser onto Blade and got the 3 count. Interview #2: "The Ambusher" Mike Trent Trent saying that Black Flagg was going down. Easier said than done. He also spoke of the breakup of the Grinders after one more match with The Players Club. Match #2: Black Flagg vs "The Ambusher" Mike Trent in a semi-final toughman match Trent with the early advantage.. even BIT Flagg! And it wasn't even to get out of anything.. he was on the offensive. It didn't last too long though, as Flagg came back with heavy blows and illegal objects. Trent used a bunch too.. even a cookie sheet! A Top Rope Bulldog was cool, but Flagg got out of the pin attempt. A Moonsault was caught and Flagg hit The Eliminator Tombstone. He then Flaggbombed Trent through a table and got the 3 count. Interview #3: K-Solo & Junior Mafia Solo stating he was coming for one of the AAPW singles titles. Then, Junior Mafia came out in the balcony. No match tonight. The match was accepted, with a cage stipulation so The Trifecta couldn't interfere, but they also wanted it at the PPV.. Also, Solo wanted to take Mafia on.. and they're supposed to be teaming up? Interview #4: J.J. Wishbone & "The Miracle Man" Tyler Smythe The 2 told Mafia and Solo that they were cowards (and much worse words) for not having the match here. Tyler and J.J. said they'd have a match with The Choir Boys tonight.. oh boy. Match #3: J.J. Wishbone & "The Miracle Man" Tyler Smythe vs The Choir Boys Smythe and Wishbone called this match as they participated.. Reminicent of what Hall and Nash used to do. They did an excellent job of it however, and dominated the match as most would expect. Man, a Swinging, Leaping, Reverse DDT by Smythe! Quite a move! Attitude Adjustment Piledriver by Wishbone got the 3 count... Commercial #1: "Powerhouse" Scott Rogers vs "Hotdog" Mack Brown Scott Rogers finally making his debut. For those of you who haven't seen him before.. you'll want to catch this match. He should show total domination over the Hotdog man. Match #4: "The Technician" Michael Leonard vs "Dashing" Derek Lankford in a submission match for the AAPW Light-Heavyweight Title Leonard started out strong, using the L-H Title belt against Lankford even. It was a submission match, but also no DQ and they used everything you could think of against each other. Leonard then went after the knee.. This could help in a submission match. Michael used a ringpost figure 4, reminicent of former AAPW Heavyweight Champion, Matt Sutherland. No submission though.. The Crow then came down and attacked Leonard! Derek Lankford then put on a Texas Cloverleaf and got the submission because Leonard was out of it after a Crow Chair shot. Match #5: Wayne Hanson vs The American Enforcer for the AAPW Heavyweight Title Before the match even started, Black Flagg came out behind The Enforcer, and then Damien Lestat came out and hit Flagg with his cooler and then DDT'd him. The match then started with The Enforcer getting the early advantage. A low blow eventually turned the tide and Hanson worked The American Enforcer over on the outside, even busting AE open. The Enforcer moved out of the way of the Guillotine though, which turned the momentum again. The American Revolution eventually was locked on, but The Overgiver and Tyler Smythe came in to give The Enforcer the DQ win. They hit a spiked piledriver on AE and then stomped him. The lockerroom cleared and The Trifecta took off. AAPW Spectrum is a Hailstorm/NewJack Production Copyright 1997
