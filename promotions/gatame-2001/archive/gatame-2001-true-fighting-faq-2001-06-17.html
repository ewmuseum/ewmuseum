COMPANY OVERVIEW -GATAME was started through a joint effort of retired Japanese fighting legend Kentaro Asashi and American business man Blake Johnson. With the financial backing of Johnson and the knowledge if Asashi they hope to package a highly competitive atmosphere in which the world's best true
fighters can compete. Karate, kickboxing, Jujitsu, Sambo, pancration, boxing, wrestling and all others shall be represented in GATAME. Fighters from around the world shall be welcomed with open arms. Politics and behind the scene backstabbing will have no place in GATAME. GATAME exists as a pure, unfettered battleground for the 'true fighter.'
2. RULES AND REGULATIONS a. Weight Classes- GATAME shall open with a free weight system. However when more members are obtained weight classes shall be implemented to bring the highest possible level of competition.
b. Protective Gear- All fighters shall be required to wear open fingered gloves as provided by GATAME. Groin cups and mouthguards shall also be mandatory. c. Fighters are allowed to wear wresting singlet, kneecaps, elbow pads, shin guards, sticking plasters, or ankle supporters. The guards and pads mentioned above must be checked by referee. Fighters must not use sticking plasters and bandages over fists. Fighters who use sticking plasters must be
checked by referee before starting the match. Fighters must not apply anything on the surface of the body (oil, Vaseline, pain reliever, massage cream, hair-dressing, etc.) Shoes will be permitted but kicks will be prohibited unless wearing certified kicking shoes as deemed by the GATAME officials.
d. Combat Ring- A ring should be a seven-meter square, which is approved by the sponsors. Ropes will circle the ring. e. Match Duration- All matches shall be deemed 15 minutes unless otherwise approved. After 15 minutes the decision shall go to a panel of judges. If there is a tie then another 15 minute round shall be fought. Again if the
time limit expires the winner shall be decided by the panel of judges. The match will go under 15 minute rounds until a winner is decided. f. Victory Conditions- Victory or defeat is decided by the following conditions; f.1 Ippon - Intention of giving up or losing one's fighting spirit is expressed orally or tapping opponent or the mat three times or more.
f.2 Technical Knockout- the Following shall be deemed a technical knockout (TKO) -Referee stop - In case that the referee sees that one side completely dominates another, and that the inferior side may fall in danger. -Doctor stop-In case that one side gets injured by fair blows of the opponent and cannot continue the match, his opponent will be declared winner. It is the ring doctor who will be the one to decide whether the fighter can continue or not. In case that the injury was made by illegal actions, the one who commits these actions will be declared loser.
-Forfeited match - In case that second of either side throws a towel in the ring during the round. If the referee does not notice this, it is the judges who stop the match.
f.3 Decision- Decision is made by three judges. Decision is made according to the damage given to the opponent, combination & ground control, down-taking & defense, aggressiveness and weight (in case that the weight difference is 10kg or more). (Match may end in a draw.)
f.4 Disqualification -A "warning" may be given when a fighter commits an illegal action or does not follow referee's instruction. Three warnings make fighter disqualified.
f.5 No contest (Invalid match) -In case that the both sides commit violation of the rules, or that the judges and sponsors decide that the match cannot be continued due to contingent matters.
g. Illegal Actions- The following shall be deemed illegal. When fighter commits any of these illegal actions, he will be given a "warning" from referee. Warning will be considered negative points at making decision of the match. Three warnings make a fighter disqualified.
-Biting, throwing something into eyes, headbutts, Groin strike, Hair pull, Fish hooking, Eye pokes, Hard blows to occipital region of head, medulla oblongata, and spinal cord. (Occipital region of head indicates the back of head, and that does not indicate the side of head or around ears.),
Holding the rope purposefully, leaving the ring, throwing the opponent out of the ring, Kicking or knee kicking to the head or the face of the opponent who falls on his face. (Fighters are prohibited from blowing against the face and the head when four or more positions of the following places touch the mat; both hands, both legs, both elbows, or both knees.)
h. Seconds- A fighter may be accompanied to the ring by a maximum of 4 seconds. They must stay in the corner at all times and are disallowed from touching the fighter during the match. i. Doctor- All fighters will be required to see the company Doctor the day of the fight for a full medical. 3. HANDLER PARTICIPATION OVERVIEW -Handlers are the back bone of any solid federation. The same is true with GATAME. By organizing a solid base of handlers willing to have fun and try their damnedest I am hoping to make GATAME something special, bringing it to
the forefront of e-MMA/puro. There are three main ways to win in GATAME- Flashes, strategy sheets and Press Conference. 3a) Flashes- Flashes actually will play the least part in GATAME. These are used to develop your character you will use in GATAME as well as bring across points regarding feuds/angles etc. This is the fun part. MMA is not quite the show professional wrestling is and doesn't quite have the
'characters'. I am hoping to change that here. I want the fighters to be fleshed out characters and not just 'Insert Style here' fighters. A weekly flash packet shall be sent out to the mailing list.
3b) Strategy Sheets- These are the big factor in GATAME when deciding matches. How will your fighter go through the motions? How will he counter the opponents style? Will he stand and fight or take it to the ground? Will he come out guns blazing or will he take his time, goading the opponent into a submission? What tactics and moves will he use? Strategy sheets should be detailed, incorporating things like the 'tale of the tape', offensive tactics, defensive tactics, counters, etc etc. There is no real solid format as each has their own way of doing things. However they should be well thought out to get a win.
3c) Prior to each show a press conference style event shall be held. This is mandatory for all fighters and an important part of the entire show. If you aren't in this you probably won't win.
4. EVENTS AND MATCH MAKING -Again, this is an MMA/shoot fed so there will not be weekly events. However monthly or even every 3 weeks is possible. It all depends on who wants in the show. There will be no real set 'roster' for GATAME, rather fighters will be put under contract for fight deals, a 3 fight deal for example. This will be much like PRIDE with the best fighters in the world coming and going. I think this will make for some awesome action.
-As far as match making goes this is fairly open. If there is someone in particular you ant to fight then set it up. Otherwise I will look at all interested fighters and make the matches based on what I think would be best.
-Roster: GATAME will have a roster of sorts, with fighters regularly fight for them. However 'journeymen' are welcome to apply and compete in our events. -All shows will be written in a summary style but will be rather detailed and in depth. 5.
