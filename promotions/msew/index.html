Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Mid States Extreme Wrestling's sole surviving document, posted 17 September 1997, asks for testers for a wrestling simulator its own operator was still building, pointing applicants to a roster questionnaire on its own homepage.

A companion roster line from the same week names four wrestlers already signed to test that engine: “Rugged” Dan Canyon, Bret Taktarov, Creed, and Bad Dog Joyride.

The same recruitment call was reposted once more on 22 September 1997, five days after the promotion's own opening. No card, match, or result of any kind survives, and no closing notice is on record.
