Drake Wants Revenge on Case, Part II

30 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera once again fades into beautiful Beverly Hills, California. We see a majestic orangish/red sun in the Heavens, palm trees blowing in the wind..pedestrians walking down famous Rodeo Drive...many tourists. But, then we reach the up-town section. Homes of celebrities and millionaires alike..probably the one person who lives in this lavish section of town that doesn't belong here is "The Cornerstone" Jeffrey Drake. A man, who by all rights, should be the LAW Skyweight champion today..but due to a selfish act by reigning champion Devon Case...what should have been, never happened. Our cameraman enters the grounds..and Drake seems to be waiting for him...and he seems to be not the least bit angry over the events of the past week. Drake, knowing that he can get what he wants, leads the cameraman into the mansion..and our cameraman, is once again, in awe over the money put into this dream home. Drake leads the cameraman into the bar area, where Drake pours a glass of champagne for himself. He adjusts his black blazer, straightens the legs of his black Armani slacks, and checks his Gucci loafers...Drake, very calmly begins to speak about what has happened...and what is yet to happen to Devon Case.] ["TC" JD] Now, Devon...you got away last week...yeah, you got away... ...with murder. Now, as almost anyone out on the streets would know, murder is punishable to whatever extent of the law that a judge in a court of law decides is suitable...but, Devon..you see, in my world... ...I'm the judge... ...Jury.... ...and the EXECUTIONER... and I want revenge, because by all rights..I should be the LAW Skyweight champion RIGHT NOW. ...But am I? ...Apparently not... Because in onr of the most selfless acts I've seen in a long time..you knew you couldn't beat me, so you took off with your tail between your legs, and saved your belt..and, let's face it, you saved your ass as well. [Drake cooly and calmly rolls the bottle of champagne in his hand..studying the label closely.] ["TC" JD] Now, you knew that you got in way over your head when you saw I wasn't exactly going to be a "pushover"...you knew that you were going to lose your precious strap to someone who many would consider "beneath" you. ...Maybe they're not looking deep enough below the surface... ...Because I was about to do something that shouldn't have been done... And let's face it... ...You knew it was gonna happen... But, no, you had to play the big star and say how I wasn't good enough for you..yeah..whatever...but..the ball is in MY court...because I get another crack at you..and I plan to make the second time around count..and I won't be content until I have that belt around my waist ...Because.... ...LORD KNOWS I DESERVE THE S** OF A B****!... October 3rd, 1999 is the night... ...WHEN MIDNIGHT WILL NOT STRIKE... October 3rd, 1999 is the night... ...WHEN TIME WILL STOP AT 11:59 PM.... ...and I will hold that belt in my hands... ...Cheers. [Drake sips the champagne and the camera fades out.) [name withheld] H. ——— Handler Of: "The Cornerstone" Jeffrey Drake (GLOW, LAW) #179 1998 RSPWF 200 The Most Underrated Person in eWrestling Aw dammit...gimme a good angle!!! Britney Spears mark :-)
