Drake Wants Revenge on Case for the Skyweight Title

14 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We see "The Cornerstone" Jeffrey Drake walking around his Beverly Hills Mansion's trophy room. He is obviously admiring his many trophies and titles that he has won over the year. Due to people trying to hold him down..there aren't many major titles. Drake is wearing a pair of white carpenter jeans, and a black dress shirt...he was obviously at some formal occasion earlier. The cameraman tries to do a zoom-in on Drake...but Drake simply raises his hand to tell the cameraman to stop where he is. Drake then seems to clench his fists and curl a half smile/half frown.]
["TC" JD] Destiny...seems to have shifted the tables a little bit. [Drake pauses, shifts...and puts his back to the camera. Face looking at the title rack.] ["TC" JD] Everything is turned around. [Drake almost seems to stutter saying those words.] ["TC" JD] Because after three years of putting all I've ever had into this thing...people have finally noticed that maybe I was never given a fair shot..but ya know what?
…Screw 'em' …In plain English. …Screw the fans …The past jerk-off promoters who held me down. …Screw 'em' ["TC" JD] The fans? Yes...they all turned their backs on me, and complained that I grew an ego..maybe if they realized they're the reason I grew one...
…Things would be a little different. ["TC" JD] They're so simple minded... …Like Devon Case. [He is obviously referring to his opponent for Breakdown on September 19th..the L.A.W. Skyweight Champion "Dangerous" Devon Case.]
["TC" JD:] Oh Devon, aren't you so freaking special....you're going to the UWF..wow...you can sign a contract with one of the top promotions in the world.
You've arrived... …Or have you? ["TC" JD] No...you haven't. You haven't put in half as much as I have...I've bled, sweated and shed many a tear throughout my career..but some punk-ass b*tch like you gets everything handed to him on a silver platter.
…Yes, but I can still get some revenge. …By taking that skyweight title. ["TC" JD] No, I'm not going to take it from you just to keep it within L.A.W. territory. I'm taking it... …To silence the critics. …Tell the nay-sayers to shut the hell up. ["TC" JD] Case... …I'm coming full circle come the 19th..and I will have arrived..because I've obviously deserved more than what's on my plate and at Breakdown... …My plate gets filled. ["TC" JD] Call me jaded, conceited whatever you want. Doesn't really make a difference to me, because I know...
…They're all jealous. ["TC" JD] Now, you're all thinking.."Well well well, isn't Drake the big man on campus now?" The answer is yes, and maybe a win over Devon Case is what it will take to send a message out to everyone..everyone in the UWF...the EMWC...GLCW...all over the place! …Like they say in that one song …We're gonna make it, we're halfway there. ["TC" JD] Only difference is that I'm a little further ahead of everyone else..and *smiles* come Breakdown...
…Case is gonna get a going away party... …In the form of my shoving my foot up his ass!... [The camera begins a slow fade. Which eventually fades out with Drake sitting down into a chair and thinking long and hard.]
[name withheld] H. ——— Handler Of: "The Cornerstone" Jeffrey Drake (GLOW, LAW) #179 1998 RSPWF 200 The Most Underrated Person in eWrestling
