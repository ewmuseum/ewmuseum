T H E S T O R Y O F L . A . W L.A.W began in the summer of 1996. We opened it up to a small crowd of 340 people. The card was named "Sandblast 1996". The crowd was mostly into it, as Jack Manson fought Alan Cool, Anthony Brigano battled The New Avatar and Eric Cooper eliminated 9 men to become the first L.A.W/RCW Heavyweight Champion. It was trully a moment in history. The RCW Belt became the L.A.W belt and things changed. Right off the bat, L.A.W had success and was gaining fan attention. It was like a new fad, everyone was into it. But when [name withheld] went on a 1 week vacation,he returned and decided that he needed to put L.A.W on the back burner to explore his talents in Japan.[name withheld] has returned from his vacation and is now ready to grow L.A.W all over again. An L.A.W that stands for tradition. He wants to make wars, he wants to get those seats feeled, and he wants world wide attention. With your help L.A.W will do just that. All we need is your signing and trust me, we'll do the rest. Yes, L.A.W is booked by [name withheld], which means we need roleplays from you. We can't stand lazy people, if you want get on TV, you must roleplay, or for that matter WIN, you have to roleplay. (See Workrate System for more info..) 3.C H A M P I O N S H I P T I T L E S L.A.W HEAVYWEIGHT BELT- This belt is the most important in L.A.W. We will not be having a tournament, or a Battle royal anytime soon. In order for this belt to go up for grabs, we must look at your record,performance,and roleplaying skills. The ones with the best record (The ones who roleplay the most) will be put into a Battle Royal that will take place somewhere next or 2 months from now. L.A.W WESTERN STATES BELT- This belt will be given away in a Battle Royal sooner than the Heavyweight Belt. Although, popular, it is not as popular as the Heavyweight Belt. This belt represents the Western state, including California. L.A.W UNIVERSAL TAG TEAM BELTS- These are won by the best recorded tag team in L.A.W. The L.A.W is really looking for talented tag teams, so if you want to enter them, or are interested with a tag team. That will be a big help. 4. T E L V I S I O N S C H E D U L E Every Saturday, L.A.W will hold a very exciting 6 match card, at 6 pm EASTERN timing on eSN. A sports network dedicated to wrestling action. HHWO,and EWC are already booked, so just add L.A.W to the list. The show will be called L.A.W TV and will be a 2 hour show. Stay tuned for more info. 5. S U P E R C A R D- PPV D E A L S We have been rejected by the FCC, and there for cannot and will not be on Pay-Per-View anytime soon. However, thanks to the eSN network, we will broadcast live shows with PPV calibur matches every 2 months from the L.A.W Civic Arena in Anahiem,California. The shows are as follow: August 29th- Sandblast 3 (Our first show) October 31st- Hell in Hollywood 3 "Unfinished Business" (Will be in Hollywood, not Anahiem) December 19th- December To Remember 2 (This event is our Wrestlemania like show, only without all the hype and celebrities) More events to be named. 6. W O R K R A T E S Y S T E M The workrate system is easy. You work your butt off in roleplays, and win matches decently, and of course start heat, you'll earn a chance to write an in-ring for our cards. It's simple,easy,and fun. Updates will be sent out after every card so you can look at your record, and others records. You have to be somewhere between [5-0 and 5-5] This is of course, an innovative idea from [name withheld] and friends. Just another reason for you to join. 7.
