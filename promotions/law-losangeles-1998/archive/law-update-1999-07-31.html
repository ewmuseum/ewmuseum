[Main Event- Match for vacant L.A.W Heavyweight Belt] "The Spirit Warrior" Jin Tao vs. PECKER w/ Kieran Erotikka - The history of the L.A.W belt, goes back to 1996, when Submarine won the first title. He was a _BIG_ man. 450 pounds, 7'4. He annihilated his opponents with his Chokeslam and Power Bomb. That similiar, to PECKER, the silent, but violent bodyguard of Kieran Erotikka, the girl who is no stranger to the L.A.W. She led Liam Rogan and POOP to a very hardcore feud with Shane Alexander last summer, and boy was it brutal. Back to the story, Submarine lost that belt to the much smaller James Pillow. The point is, can Jin Tao over come the goliath PECKER and win the L.A.W Heavyweight Championship? Time will tell folks. 2. MAN VERSUS WOMAN Match! Kieran Erotikka w/ PECKER vs. The L.A.W PC Champion FOREVER Scotty Drake - Scotty Drake, who was the first Pacific Coast Champion, and the last one. He is since, running around with the moniker "The Pacific Coast Champion FOREVER". Kieran Erotikka will be wrestling her first one on one match with a Man.. EVER. But you gotta wonder if PECKER will play a role in this match. It's the greatest and only Pacific Coast Champion against The Mean Streak Mad Woman! Promises to be an explosive battle. 3. [GRUDGE MATCH] Derek Douglas takes on Eric Cooper - There is no love loss between Derek Douglas and Eric Cooper. Both want to be on the top, and both want to prove that they can achieve success in L.A.W 2000. So when they both enter the ring, in a No-Rules L.A.W Style Grudge Match, expect all Hell To Break Loose. 4. [OPPOSITES ATTRACT] "The Sextreme Icon" Levi Ash w/ Johnna vs. Duncan Shearer - Levi Ash is a hardcore/brawler, and Duncan Shearer is a New England sensation who is shocking fans with his high flying style. The link is below: LAW - Los Angeles Wrestling. ~...~...~...~...~...~...~...~...~| |What to Send Us | ~...~...~...~...~...~...~...~...~| - Roleplays: They are an essential part of success in the L.A.W. This is a booked fed, based on angles and roleplays. Without roleplaying, little heat goes into matches. - Special Interviews: In-rings, in-locker room interviews. Things of that nature. Stuff that goes on the card and makes it that little bit more interesting than just match after match. Note: If you'd like a backstage or in-locker room interview, then use Jake Owen as the guy interviewing you. That should make it easier to use. Also, if you watch the WWF, Michael Cole usually goes backstage to talk with superstars. - Extra Stuff: Send something like an advertisement for merchandise involving your wrestler, or a promo. - Strats: See form towards the end of the update regarding strats. Basically they'll include anything you want to happen in a match that would be a little out of the ordinary, some suggested spots and maybe a game-plan for the match. - Angle Suggestions: I like to see participation from handlers regarding angles. Ideas about what you want your e-wrestler to do, or what story-lines you want followed, possible feuds, alliances and other things of that nature fall within this bracket. It helps establish your character as he get's more popular or more hated. ********************************** WHERE TO SEND IT? ********************************** Basically everything goes to this email addy: [name withheld] You can also send it to the Vice-president: [name withheld] and we will both discuss the situation. Whatever you send, please help to keep us organized by labelling it. For instance if it's a roleplay: [L.A.W] [name withheld] Roleplay If it's a strat: [L.A.W] Barnes Strat etc. You get the idea... :) If you're thinking of sending in a strat, then by all means send it in any style you wish. However, below is a suggested format which in my opinion is both easy to use, and covers anything you might wish to put in a strat. Strats are not essential in a match, but can often help if the roleplays are close. Wrestler Name: Match: Match Date: Entrance: ( Include entrance music, introduction, description of entrance ) Plan for Match: ( Include what strategy you want to use during the match ) Spots: ( Include some suggested spots ) Winning/Losing Situation: ( Include what you're wrester will do if he wins the match and if he loses it. ) Extra Plan: ( Use this for any interferences, interviews, or any plan going beyond your own match ) All Gameplans go to [name withheld] . ~...~...~...~...~...~...~...~...~| |In Conclusion... | ~...~...~...~...~...~...~...~...~| I hope I've covered everything, in order to insure a fun, and easy way to the top of Los Angeles Wrestling.
