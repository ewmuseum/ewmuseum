-- CWC Advance Warning --

4 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The lights are very dim and inside the huge CastleKeep Arena, the atmosphere is almost spooky. It looks positively barren as the camera moves around, the arena is shaped like a giant English courtyard but looking at all the empty seats - its hard to imagine that pretty soon the building will rumble with the sounds of the CWC. =========================================================================== THE MAIN GUITAR RIFF FROM KISS's `DOMINO' PLAYS, TITLES BEGIN TO ROLL FOR: Chaos Wrestling Confederacy _______ ________ / ____/ | / / ____/ / / | | /| / / / ADVANCE WARNING / /___ | |/ |/ / /___ \____/ |__/|__/\____/ ========================================================================== The camera zooms down into the middle of the arena, into an all black wrestling ring, where one of our female commentators, `The Tokyo Angel' is ready. IRIA: KONICHI-WA, I'm IRIA MORIYAMA, The TOKYO ANGEL, and I'm here in the Castlekeep arena to bring you this special edition of CWC ADVANCE WARNING. Before long, this very ring will house the greatest matches of the CHAOS WRESTLING CONFEDERACY. And to all those wrestlers out there watching, you still have time to become part of the 'CHAOS'. Another female commentator has appeared at ringside and is entering the ring. IRIA: Well, YOU'VE arrived then!?! TIME: Jeez, you're knickers are obviously in one MIGHTY twist. Hi folks, ISSABELLA TIME here with my assistant iria yori...something.. IRIA: ASSISTANT??? TIME: Thats right. Anyway, I'm... WE'RE here to bring you the latest CWC news, prior to its official opening. IRIA: I've done that bit... TIME: ... This all BLACK ring I'm standing in, is set for some of the HOTTEST eWrestling action in history. IRIA: I've done that bit too sort of... TIME: ... ... ... IRIA: Why do I get the feeling we're going to have DIFFICULTY working together? TIME: Because you can't stick to a SCRIPT, I was supposed to do the introductory speech. IRIA: Do the NEXT one! We're going to be here every week commentating on the CWC matches, so next time, I'll let YOU do the introductory comments. TIME: Well I'm afraid thats NOT good enough. CAMERA MAN, PRODUCER reset everything - we're going to do this again. IRIA: Are you going to be MOUTHING off every week? Because, you know, I don't think I can take it. TIME: I'm the main PLAY-BY-PLAY women here, what you THINK doesn't matter. IRIA: Why don't you tell the people watching, what kind of commentary they can EXPECT from you? TIME: Well, I THINK you'll find I'm firm but fair, and I call things as I see them. IRIA: Maybe so, but you don't know ANYTHING about eWrestling do you? TIME: [Blushing] Well what about you? IRIA: As a former Ladies Wrestling champion I think I'm more than qualified. I'm sure I can deliver some in-depth play-by-play during each match. Okay, why don't you tell everyone watching about the FIRST Supercard set for the CWC. The INITIATION DAY! TIME: ... ... ... IRIA: [Smiles] YOU don't know what that is do YOU? I didn't think so! eWrestling fans, INITIATION DAY will herald a new era in wrestling - the start of the REIGN OF CHAOS. On that night, we will crown a CWC CHAMPIONSHIP TITLE holder, AND an INTERCONTINENTAL CHAMPION. TIME: Oh THAT Initiation Day? Of course, I know about THAT! IRIA: [Still Smiling] So why don't you tell everyone HOW we're going to decide these titles? You CAN'T can you? Well, I'll tell YOU. Every CWC applicant will be entered into a Royal Rumble. Wrestlers will come in one by one, and will fight until eliminated by being thrown OVER the top rope. The LAST man in the ring will be crowned the CWC CHAMPION and the second last man will be crowned the INTERCONTINENTAL CHAMPION. TIME: What about the TAG TEAM TITLE, smart arse? IRIA: Okay, you've got me on that one. At present, the WARRIOR KING - our federation president, hasn't decided how to run that. TIME: The WARRIOR KING? He's a fucking screwball if ever I saw one. Thinks he's King Arthur or something. IRIA: I'm going to slap you in a minute. TIME: Well its TRUE. You know, I hope there's some decent looking men enter this shithole company. Perhaps I'll think about managing. IRIA: Perhaps you should think about a lobotomy first. TIME: Very very funny!?! I guess comedy's just not your thing. IRIA: I think we better leave things here until I see about getting a DIFFERENT co-commentator. They were scraping the barrel when they signed you weren't they Issabella? All the rest must've signed for other eWrestling feds. TIME: [Patronising] Hopefully, by next time we'll have some more INFORMATION to give to you LOVELY people at home, and perhaps some wrestler details. IRIA: [Laughing] And a new CO-COMMENTATOR So its, SAYONARA from me and... TIME: GOODBYE from me. ============================================================================ END CREDITS ROLE: If you're interested in joining the CWC - then either email [name withheld] at [detail withheld] or HTTP://WWW.FEKLORE.DEMON.CO.UK/CWC/INDEX.HTML ============================================================================ TIME: You know, I can speak INDIAN as well as you, you know.
