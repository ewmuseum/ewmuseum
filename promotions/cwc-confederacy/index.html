Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Chaos Wrestling Confederacy's earliest surviving call to arms, posted 2 December 1996 under the title “Begin The Chaos Reign,” framed itself as a restoration: “In these days of tourment and frustration, we must return to the traditions of our ancestors… searching for warriors to revive the spirit of combat.” It built its matches on Blitzmatch 2.0, a combined roleplaying-and-simulation engine supplied, the post says, by the operator of a same-era fed of his own, and pitched reliable weekly cards, monthly bonus cards, supercard pay-per-views, and, in its own words, “fewer restrictions than many feds.” Its very first event was set to be a Royal Rumble deciding both a championship and an Intercontinental title.

Two days later a scripted broadcast, “Advance Warning,” introduced its own commentary team — Iria Moriyama, billed as “The Tokyo Angel” and a former Ladies Wrestling champion, and Issabella Time — bickering through a segment that doubled as its own house preview. Between jabs at each other, the two laid out the plan for its first Supercard, Initiation Day: every applicant entered into a Royal Rumble, the last wrestler standing crowned champion and the second-last crowned Intercontinental Champion, with a tag team title left undecided because, in Time's words, the federation's own president, billed as the Warrior King, “hasn't decided how to run that.”

A homepage correction on 15 December fixed a case-sensitive URL typo and signed off with the same pitch — “Join Us, We'll Be Good — Honest” — and by 18 January 1997 a further notice was still selling the promotion as pre-launch, reposting the same Advance Warning broadcast verbatim to reintroduce Iria and Issabella to anyone who had missed it the first time, still touting the same Blitzmatch system and still awaiting what it called “its big opening.”

How or whether that opening happened isn't in the surviving record. Every one of its four documents predates the promotion's own promised launch.
