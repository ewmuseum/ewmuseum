Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

NMSEWF survives in this record through one broadcast: Tuesday Night's Main Event, taped 17 March 1998 at the Louisiana State Fairgrounds in Baton Rouge. Its own commentary team opened by razzing the promotion's absent top promoter, Brad Morrison, for leaving the office to handle personal matters and letting the card fall into disarray — a running joke the broadcast never let go of.

The night built to a fourteen-man battle royal for the vacant NMSEWF Heavyweight Championship, the only title this record attests. The final four came down to Zach Zagar, Eric Righteous, Simon McQuaid and Hampton, trading suplexes, superkicks and a Thundercracker for over thirty minutes before Zagar eliminated McQuaid and then Righteous last, at 38 minutes 41 seconds, to become the promotion's first champion.

The broadcast closed by announcing a full card for the following week, headlined by a championship triangle match among Zagar, Righteous and McQuaid. No further document survives to say whether that card, or the promotion itself, continued past 18 March 1998.
