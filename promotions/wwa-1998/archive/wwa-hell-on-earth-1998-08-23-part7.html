GP: Well, all the stuff thats been happening tonight with TX and Rames and Stanfield might come to a head here. Because up next is Dynamic's final WWA match. No title. No stipulation. All thats on the line is Fluff Daddy's undefeated streak. And Dynamic is one hell of an athlete. Its too bad the WWA has to lose that man. Wai... hang on folks..... no.... he didn't.... why? DQ: WHAT???!!! GP: hang on Dave.... I can't believe it..... DQ: WHAT DAMN YOU??!!!! GP: Folks.... lets go back to Shaina in the backstage area. [Backstage, Shaina stands there looking depressed] Shaina: Folks. Dynamic has left the building. Neilsen Moutrey stormed into his locker room and was furious at his release. Neilsen said that the entire match was ruined because of it and his fans were becoming disappointed. Lets just say, it was a heated arguement and Dynamic stormed out. Packed his stuff and left. Dynamic is offically gone from the gone from the WWA. Fluff Daddy is apparently on his way to the ring for some big announcement. I hope for some fans this doesn't ruin Hell On Earth. Back to you guys. [Scene comes back to a sad looking David and George. Fans are heard booing] George: Its the biggest Pay per view in WWA history and this happens. I.... I don't know what to say.... get Fluff Daddy out here.... ["Tha Shiznit" by Snoop Doggy Dogg plays and the fans boo. Golden colored lights and white lights zoom around the arena and some pyros explode off the entrance way as the motorized ring brings Fluff Daddy to the ring. He is in a white "Wife Beater" (Undershirt), and big shiny gold pants. The TV title over his shoulder] DQ: Here comes the TV champion. I wonder what hes gonna say about whats going on tonight that he didn't say earlier. [Fluff enters the ring and the golden lights hit the ring and white pyros shoot out of the turnbuckles 4 times as a 3 prong spray shoots down from the light rigging. Fluff is handed the mic by Victoria] Fluff: Ladies and Gentlemen... your World Wrestling Association Television champion.....AND STILL UNDEFEATED...... the man of the hour... the tower of power... too sweet to be sour....... folks... its your pleasure... THE LEADER OF THUGS XTREME.... FLUFF..... DADDY!!!!!! [Fans boo as he smiles at the all] Fluff: You know, this night has held a few ups and downs for me. See.... its still official. I win this match by default. by Forfeit. And that whole story that Shaina Gregory just told...... ready for it George... GP: What? Fluff: BUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUULL SHIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIT!!!!! [Fans boo] Fluff: The squirt was simply scared that he'd be another notch in the gun of the daddy if you what I'm getting at. [Fans boo] Fluff: Boo all you want. Its true. But like I was saying, there are some downs, see, my best bud, Chris Brock is up at the hospital right now... [Fans cheer] Fluff: Fuck off. He's there cause Donnovan Rames and Casey Stanfield made a little apperance. Well, we turned on you for a reason Stanfield. You and Outta Sight, and you're little Bad Boy Family. It sucked. PERIOD. And we did the right thing. And thats show you how much you suck. So you attack us to show how angry you are.... that you suck. And I'm sorry but.... well... not really... but no matter how much you attack TX, you're still gonna suck when its all said and done. [Fans boo] Fluff: Its true. I don't lie. I'm undefeated. I don't need to. But I know, that we're all human and we all make mistakes... even though I've yet to make one but.... but still I know a good injury could take me out so what I've done is gotten TX a BRAND... NEW.... MEMBER!!! And he ain't the biggest guy.... but he's had a title shot and would have won if some dick didn't stick his ugly face in..... LADIES AND GENTLEMEN.... PLEASE WELCOME....... MASTER!!!! GP: WHAT??!!!! DQ: WHA-HO!!!!!!!! MASTER HAS JOINED TX!!! ["Tha Shiznit" by Snoop Doggy Dogg plays and the fans once again boo.] DQ: Where's he at??! Don't tell me Rames and Stanfield got to him!! [Master comes out from the crowd and enters the ring and high fives Fluff Daddy] Fluff: Sure... Master lost tonight... but this man WON THE BLOOD BOWL!!!! And that damn sure says something to me. And I personally know what he is capable of!! So, right now, Stanfield.... Rames.... WE WANT A STREET FIGHT!!! AND WE WANT IT NOW!!! GET OUT HERE!!!!! DQ: LOOK OUT!! THEY'RE COMING OUT OF THE CROWD TOO!!! AND WE GOT A BRAWL!!! GP: GOT SOME SECURITY OUT HERE!! WE GOT A SHOW TO DO!!! COME ON!!! BREAK IT UP IN THERE!!!! DQ: They can't hear you.... TO BE CONTINUED..................
