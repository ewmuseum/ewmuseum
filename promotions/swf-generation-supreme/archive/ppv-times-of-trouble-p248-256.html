SWF Times of Trouble 2008

Thursday, Week 2, June 2008

Nevada State Armoury (South West) - 22,828

Announcers

Peter Michaels - Melanie Florence - Jerry Eisen

________________________________________

Peter Michaels: Welcome to Master of Puppets 2008! I'm Peter Michaels. With me tonight are Melanie Florence and Jerry Eisen.

Jerry Eisen: Where's Phil?

Peter Michaels: He's with your father tonight, Jerry.

Melanie Florence: Nice to see you too, Jerry.

Jerry Eisen: Oh, hi.

Quote:

Jack Bruce, Eric Eisen, Christian Faith, Chris Morrisette, Steve Frehley, Rich Money,

Remo, + Big Can Brandon

Pay Per View Promo Video

A high-quality promo video plays, showcasing the top talent in the Supreme Wrestling Federation while the pay per views theme song plays.

Grade: B+

Peter Michaels: As you can see, the double-ring and double-cage are set up, but the ceiling of the has not been put in place yet. That will happen after our first match.

Melanie Florence: I like this Devine chap.

Quote:

Kid Toma vs Enygma vs Darryl Devine

SWF Sky Club Division Ladder Match for the SWF Shooting Star Championship

The opening match is the first Sky Club Division match, which is a unique ladder match within the double rings and cage. There are two ladders set up in the ring, with the Shooting Star belt suspended several feet above the steel crossbeam that follows the joining of the two rings at the top of the cage.

The match itself is an entertaining affair, with all three men taking some crazy risks. Devine hits a shooting star press from the top of the cage onto both of his opponents. Enygma gets knocked off the crossbeam at one point and lands where the two rings join, getting tangled in the two lines of ropes. The moment of the match belongs to Toma, however, as he stands atop one ladder in the middle of one ring, jumps to the crossbeam to attack Devine, jumps to the top of the other ladder in the other ring, and back to the crossbeam again to continue battling Devine. Toma gets knocked off shortly after, allowing Devine to grab the belt and take the win. He celebrates atop the cage before climbing down the outside.

Darryl Devine Wins at 13:17 via Item Retrieval

Grade: C+

Peter Michaels: We will now take you to the negotiations between Richard Eisen and Phil Vibert...

Quote:

Richard Eisen + Phil Vibert

Dark Room Segment

Richard Eisen and Phil Vibert are sitting across from each other in a dark room. They stare each other down. Its unclear where exactly they are, or whether there is anyone else in the room.

Phil Vibert: Are you ready?

Richard Eisen: I'm ready.

Phil Vibert: And you understand the rules?

Richard Eisen: Of course I understand the rules.

Phil Vibert: Fine. Then let's get this started.

The camera pulls back to reveal the two men are seated at a poker table. Duane Fry is dressed up as a poker deal. He starts dealing the cards. The game being played is a quick version of Texas Hold 'Em. The first hand goes to Eisen, with three of a kind to Vibert's two pair.

Phil Vibert: Look at that... You didn't even want to play and you get first choice.

Richard Eisen: First choice. Hmm... well, I believe we should definitely keep the SWF World Heavyweight championship as part of Supreme TV, so I'll have to pick Jack Bruce as he's the current champion. For the moment, at least.

Vibert seems genuinely surprised at the choice.

Phil Vibert: Well then, a bit of a swerve.

The second hand goes to Vibert. He grins as he lays down two pair. Eisen grimaces.

Phil Vibert: My turn. Well, what would an SWF show be without an Eisen? I can't pick you and I can't stand Jerry. So I'll take Eric.

Eisen looks surprised and looks about ready to argue, but he shuts his mouth instead. The next hand is dealt. It goes to Vibert as well.

Phil Vibert: Another choice... Well, Christian Faith said last night something that resonated with me - "I am legend". Its true. So I'll pick him.

Richard Eisen: Enjoy the ancient has-been.

The following hand is won by Richard Eisen with a full house. He takes a moment to gloat a bit before making his pick.

Richard Eisen: I should have came in with a list, like those guys do for fantasy football. Well, I think I'll have to choose Big Money Incorporated.

Phil Vibert: All three of them?

Richard Eisen: All four. Emma, too

Phil Vibert: Okay then...

Vibert looks like he wants to argue the point, but they continue playing instead...

Grade: A

Peter Michaels: We'll be back to Richard Eisen and Phil Vibert in a bit. Now for the SWF World Tag Team championship match. The roof is one the double cage and we're ready!

Quote:

Badd Kompany vs The Amazing Bumfholes vs Sin Inc vs Valiant & Giedroyc

SWF World Tag Team Championship Match

The tag match features no tagging as all eight men are in the double-ring at once. When the bell rings, chaos ensues. The man is an outright brawl that leaves several of the wrestlers bleeding. Randy Bumfhole and Joe Sexy spent a significant amount of time battling each other. One man who acquits himself pretty well is Sean Deeley. Its actually Deeley's partner Thompson that gets choked out cold by Giedroyc, leading to the referees stopping the match and the champions retaining their belts.

Giedroyc Wins at 16:33 via Stoppage

Grade: B-

Quote:

Rich Money, Remo + Brandon James

Backstage Segment

Rich Money, Remo, and Brandon James are conspiring in a dark hallway. There is a partially open locker room door just a bit further up the hallway.

Remo: Why are we doing this crap again?

Rich Money: Because we need to find out who their mystery guy is, and we need to slow down Frehley.

Remo: Is Frehley even in there with them?

Rich Money: He should be. He's part of their team, right?

Brandon James: I hate their team.

Rich Money: Maybe we should get Vengeance...?

Remo: Oh hell no, I don't want that idiot around.

Discussion over, the trio make their way down the hallway. Standing beside the door, they listen to the voices coming from within, but seem unable to tell for certain who is inside. After some frantic hand-signal attempts between the, Remo just gets up and kicks open the door.

Christian Faith + Chris Morrisette

Faith and Morrisette and the only two inside, playing together on the recently-released SWF The Supreme Challenge 2008 game for the Playstation 3. They drop the controllers and stand up, staring at the Big Money Incorporated trio as the stand in the doorway.

Christian Faith: Get the hell out of here, you jerks.

Rich Money: Who's the mystery guy?

Brandon James: We want to know.

Chris Morrisette: You'll get to find out with everyone else later tonight.

Christian Faith: Now get the hell out of here.

Remo: Maybe we'll beat the hell out of you instead.

Christian Faith: You're welcome to try.

Rich Money: Just tell us what we need to know.

Chris Morrisette: You need to know to leave.

Remo: You can't tell us what to do.

Christian Faith: Yes we can. Its our locker room.

Rich Money: We'd teach you a bit of respect right now, but we don't you to have any excuses when we destroy you later tonight.

Big Money Incorporated storm out of the locker room as quickly as they came in. Morrisette and Faith are left shaking their heads. They go back to the video game.

Grade: A*

Quote:

Ace Newton, Ash Campbell, Cameron Vessey + Casey Valentine vs Robbie Retro,

Swoop McCarthy, Bart Biggz + Brett Biggz

SWF Supreme Warfare Match

The match starts with Bart Biggz against Ash Campbell. They batter away at each other. With Retro's team having won the coin toss, Brett goes in next and they punish Campbell. Cameron Vessey comes in next, followed by Swoop McCarthy, Casey Valentine, then Robbie Retro. Ace Newton is the last one in. He goes right after Retro and the two men battle with venom. The match ends before they can really get too much after each other however, as Bart Biggz makes Ash Campbell submit with a figure four leg lock.

Bart Biggz Wins at 21:11 via Submission

Grade: D+

Quote:

Richard Eisen + Phil Vibert

Dark Room Segment

The duo are still playing their game of poker. Eisen wins a hand and selected Marc Dubois and his North American championship, then he chooses Valiant & Geidroyc after winning the next hand. Vibert wins one, he selects the full Warriors group, including Chris Caufield. The game continues...

Grade: A

Quote:

Runaway Train vs Antonio Marquez vs Marc DuBois

Threeway Threat Match for the SWF North American Championship

The Threefold Threat match starts with all three men in the double ring. Runaway Train controls the early going with an impressive display of power. Marquez and DuBois battle back as Train begins to tire a bit. A bit of teamwork from the younger duo put Train face-first into the cage and the former champion is bleeding. Marquez and DuBois then put on an impressive display of quickness, athleticism, and daring as they move about the double-ring cage. Train gets involved again and the match slows down. DuBois manages to successfully defend his belt after he gets his Model Solution on the Train and gets the tap, as Marquez is unable to interfere due to be tied up in the ropes.

Marc DuBois Wins at 14:54 Via Submission

Grade: B-

Peter Michaels: Next up is the Supreme Warfare match and we get to learn who the mystery participants are!

Melanie Florence: Can't wait, love.

Quote:

The workers come out one at a time, to build up suspense of the the reveal of the surprise participants.

Rocky Golden

Rocky Golden gets some pretty serious heel heat from the crowd. The Golden Boy eats it up and eggs the crowd on.

Troy Tornado

Tornado gets a notable bit of heat as well.

Texas Pete

The reaction for Texas Pete is a bit smaller. But the crowd is anxious to see who the threesome's mystery partner is.

Bloodstone

The crowd doesn't react at all to the unfamiliar music. Then ring announcer introduces the next wrestlers as "Bloodstone" and Johnny Bloodstone appears at the top of the ramp. There is a loud pop from small sections of the crowd.

Peter Michaels: That's Johnny Bloodstone! Johnny Bloodstone!

Jerry Eisen: I don't know who that is.

Peter Michaels: You will soon enough, Jerry.

Chris Caufield

Caufield comes out with his SWF Legends championship and he gets a pretty good pop from the crowd.

Eric Tyler

Eric Tyler comes out looking surly.

Guide

Barrowman gets a big pop with the ring announcer introduces him as "Guide". He comes out in the old army fatigues and camo facepaint that so many fans from his DaVE career recognize.

Peter Michaels: Barrowman has gone old school here. Does that mean...? No, they're fourth couldn't be...

Scout

The crowd gives a big reaction to the second member of The New Wave, making his SWF debut.

Grade: B

Rocky Golden, Troy Tornado, Texas Pete + Bloodstone vs Chris Caufield, Eric Tyler,

Guide + Scout

SWF Supreme Warfare Match

The match starts with Troy Tornado facing Guide. The two men engage each other slowly and engage in some technical grappling. Scout comes in next, and The New Wave display some of their world class teamwork on Tornado. Pete comes in next, and he engages Guide in some old school heavy-handed brawling. Caufield comes in next, followed by Rocky Golden, then Eric Tyler, and finally the debutant Bloodstone. Tyler and Bloodstone show some great skills as they engage each other. Golden battles against Caufield and the two men are soon both bleeding. The match is wide open until Bloodstone gets Tyler to submit to a painful looking ankle lock.

Bloodstone Wins at 23:31 via Submission

Grade B-

Peter Michaels: Bloodstone! Scout! These are huge additions!

Quote:

Richard Eisen + Phil Vibert

Dark Room Segment

The poker game is still going on. Phil Vibert gets a full house, and he selects Rocky Golden's stable, including the new addition of Bloodstone. Eisen wins a hand and says that he's taking the Bumfhole brothers. Duane Fry is writing down every selection that is made, in addition to his duties as the dealer. Both Eisen and Vibert seem to be taking the proceedings very seriously, as there is no banter or friendliness between them.

Grade: B+

Quote:

Eric Eisen vs Jack Bruce

SWF World Heavyweight Championship Match

Jack Bruce and Eric Eisen engage in a fairly entertaining match that makes good use of the big double ring. Both men spend time in control, and Eisen proves very willing to try to get the advantage in any manner. It takes more than the occasional eye rake and low blow to take down the Long Island Angel, however. The highlight of the match is when Bruce climbs into one of the corners of the cage and while clinging upside from the corner, launches himself at the challenger and nails him with a spinning lariat. Eisen just doesn't have enough to get past Bruce and the champion retains via pinfall.

Jack Bruce Wins at 17:05 via Pinfall

Grade: B-

Jack Bruce

In-Ring Celebration

After his win, Bruce exits the cage and grabs his belt. He scrambles up the outside of the cage and spends several minutes walking around the outside of the cage, showing off his belt and playing to the crowd. The crowd loves it.

When Bruce climbs down, he finds himself face-to-face with Vengeance.

Vengeance

Bruce and Vengeance find themselves face-to-face, staring at each other. Neither man moves or says anything. They are quickly surrounded by a number of SWF officials who get them apart. The Harbinger points at Bruce's title belt, at which point the champion begins yelling that Vengeance will never get his belt! Never!

Grade: A*

Peter Michaels: Vengeance refuses to give up on getting that belt. He just refuses!

Quote:

Rich Money

Money comes out first and he gets some serious heat. "Hard Cash" is skilled at working the crowd into a frothing frenzy. He struts and gestures as he takes his time getting down to ringside.

Remo

Remo moves quickly to get down to ringside. He looks serious and focused.

Brandon James

Brandon James manages to find his way down the ramp to the ring without anyone helping him, which is a bit of an accomplishment.

Vengeance

Fresh off his face-off with Jack Bruce, Vengeance makes his way down to the ring. You can't tell if he looks serious, but his mask certainly does.

Christian Faith

Faith gets a monster reaction from the crowd as he appears and hustles down to the ring. He looks like he might go after the foursome himself, but there are officials between them. Faith instead gets the crowd further jacked up.

Chris Morrisette

More popular than ever, Morrisette is escorted down to the ring by the adoration of his fans.

Steve Frehley

Frehley gets down to the ring in a hurry. He looks determined and focused as well.

There is slight pause and the crowd holds their breath for the revelation of the mystery partner.

Angry Gilmore

The crowd goes insane when Angry Gilmore's music begins to play. He appears at the top of the ramp with a smile, then gets down to the ring to join his partners!

Grade: A*

Christian Faith, Chris Morrisette, Steve Frehley + Angry Gilmore vs Rich Money, Remo, Brandon James + Vengeance

SWF Supreme Warfare Match

Christian Faith chooses to start against Rich Money. The two men go it in both rings, brawling hard for the first five minutes. Money is joined by Remo, and they have Faith at their mercy. The Iron Man doesn't back down and he keeps fight. Morrisette comes in next, and he goes after Remo with some fury. Vengeance enters next, then Angry Gilmore, and then Brandon James. The match is a chaotic brawl, with several men bleeding heavily. Everything stops when the chime sounds for the final entry into the match. Looking eager, Steve Frehley starts to enter... and then stops. He backs out and tells the referee to lock the door. Frehley then turns and walks away. A massive crowd reaction sees him stroll up the ramp and abandon his team.

Being a man down puts the faces at a serious disadvantage but they keep fighting. A highlight of the match is Remo and Gilmore going at each other with furious vengeance and righteous anger. At ringside, Emma Chase grabs Vengeance's chain and feeds it through the cage. Its used by several wrestlers before Vengeance finally gets it back. He uses it to choke Chris Morrisette, then hits the former Lobster with a series of powerbombs. Morrisette doesn't move after the battering and this leads to the referee stopping the match.

Vengeance Wins at 32:41 via Stoppage

Grade: A

Steve Frehley + Ray Diaz

As the team of Big Money Incorporated and Vengeance celebrate their victory by continuing to beat on the faces in the cage, Frehley and Diaz appear at the top of the ramp to watch. Both men are grinning. But then...

Rocky Golden, Troy Tornado, Texas Pete, Bloodstone + Kristen Pearce

Rocky Golden and his cohorts appear at the top of the ramp. Frehley and Golden stare at each other for a moment, then go after each other. The rest of the stable go after Diaz. The officials are busy trying to deal with the fater-match proceedings in the ring, so no one is there to break this one up.

With Frehley and Diaz heavily out-numbered, they take a heavy beating. No one arrives to help them.

Grade: B

Peter Michaels: They're taking a beating! The traitor is getting hammered!
