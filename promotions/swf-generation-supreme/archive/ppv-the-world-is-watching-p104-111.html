SWF The World is Watching

Thursday, Week 2, April 2008

New Hampshire Garden (New England) - 24,891

Announcers

Peter Michaels - Phil Vibert - Jerry Eisen

________________________________________

Peter Michaels: Welcome to The World is Watching! I'm Peter Michaels. With me tonight are Phil Vibert and Jerry Eisen.

Jerry Eisen: I think everyone in the world is watching!

Phil Vibert: They d*mned well should be.

Quote:

Jack Bruce, Remo, Vengeance, Christian Faith, Rich Money, and Steve Frehley

Promo Video

A high-quality highlight video of some of the main feuds leading up to the pay per view opens the show. The shows theme song provides a soundtrack.

Grade: A

Quote:

Steven Parker vs Lobster Warrior

The youngster puts in a pretty good fight against the technical veteran. Parker just doesn't have quite enough and the Crustacean controls most of the match. He looks like to get the clean win, when Remo runs down to the ring and nails Lobby with a spear. The ref calls for the bell.

Lobster Warriors Wins at 9:27 via Disqualification

Grade: B

Lobster Warrior + Remo

In-Ring Segment

Remo clears out of the ring after causing Lobster Warrior's match to be called. He stands on the outside of the ring. The two man stare each other down. Lobster Warrior invites him back in, but Remo laughs. He grabs a microphone.

Remo: You thought I forgot about you, didn't you, Lobster Man? Don't worry - I won't forget. I have other business to take care of tonight. Like take Jack Bruce's World title. But I'll be sure to make time for you soon enough.

Lobster Warrior: Get in here and face me like a man, Remo!

Remo: Alpha Dog against a lobster? That's no contest. You're day will come soon enough.

Still laughing, Remo throws the microphone at Lobby and makes his way slowly up the ramp. Lobster Warrior is red with anger in the ring.

Grade: A

Peter Michaels: That Remo is going to get his!

Jerry Eisen: Lobster is as red as a lobster!

Quote:

Darryl Devine vs Marc DuBois

SWF Shooting Star Ladder Match for the SWF Shooting Star Championship

The two young wrestlers put their bodies on the line and disregard their own safety as they throw themselves into every spot. It is an impressive display that grabs the crowd quite effectively. Neither man backs down and each takes some rough bumps. DuBois comes within inches of winning the match on three occasions, with Devine mainly just trying to keep the champion from grabbing the belt. The final time, DuBois is kicked off the ladder and bounces off the top rope and out of the ring. With the crowd roaring him on, Devine sets the ladder back up and scrambles up it to grab the championship belt.

Darryl Devine Wins at 11:45 via Item Retrieval to Win the SWF Shooting Star Championship

Grade: B-

Darryl Devine, Elmo Benson, Groucho Bling, + Enygma

In-Ring Celebration

As the referee awards the Shooting Star championship belt to Darryl Devine, Marc DuBois looks on in shocked horror. He clearly can't believe what just happened. Devine looks almost as shocked, but also purely joyous. As the new champion celebrates, the previous champion throws a tantrum on the outside.

Devine is soon joined in the ring by Elmo Benson and Grouch Bling, as well as Enygma. The three men congratulate the youngster. As Benson and Bling bend over to stare at the belt, Enygma grabs one of the ladders and throws it at Devine. It sails over High Concept and catches Devine in the face, knocking him cold. Enygma stares at them as the duo tend to their fallen friend. After a moment, Enygma slowly leaves the ring to a chorus of boos.

Grade: C

Peter Michaels: I can't believe that!

Jerry Eisen: Is that Devine guy okay?

Quote:

Jacob Jett + Mainstream Hernandez

Backstage Segment

Jacob Jett and Mainstream Hernandez are backstage, watching the previous match on a monitor. When Devine wins, Jett celebrates. A bit excessively. Hernandez just watches him.

Mainstream Hernandez: You know that guy?

Jacob Jett: Yeah, he's a friend of mine. We used to wrestle together.

They watch the ladder-throwing incident and Devine get knocked out.

Mainstream Hernandez: I don't think he's going to be able to help you tonight.

Jacob Jett: I don't need help.

Mainstream Hernandez: You do if you want win this thing tonight.

The pair stop talking and stare at something off-camera. The camera pivots to reveal...

Vengeance

The two youngsters are clearly a bit nervous as The Harbinger stares at them, unmoving.

Jacob Jett: Uh, hey Mr. Vengeance. Nice to meet you. I'm Jacob.

Mainstream Hernandez: Stop talking to him. Stop it!

Jacob Jett: I'm trying to keep him calm.

Mainstream Hernandez: Why don't you try offering him candy then?

Jacob Jett: I don't have any or I would.

Mainstream Hernandez: You're an idiot.

With lightning quickness, Vengeance grabs the monitor and heaves it at the duo. They narrowly avoid it and he charges at them. The camera is knocked down and shows nothing but concrete floor, as the sounds of violence can be heard in the background.

Grade: A*

Peter Michaels: Vengeance is at it again.

Jerry Eisen: Aren't those two scheduled to wrestle next?

Quote:

Ace Newton vs Andre Jones vs Avatar vs Gino Montero vs Greg Rayne vs Jacob Jett vs Mainstream Hernandez vs Paul Huntingdon vs Robbie Retro vs Swoop McCarthy

10-Man Battle Royal for the SWF Generations Championship

The ten youngsters eye each other before the match starts. Except for Jacob Jett and Mainstream Hernandez, who are in rough shape after their encounter with Vengeance. Both had to be helped to the ring. When the bell rings, the chaos begins. Its actually Avatar who is eliminated first, as he charges at Robbie Retro but gets himself tossed over the top. Both Jett and Hernandez are soon eliminated, as neither can do much. Rayne soon follows. Retro gets Huntingdon out as well. Andre Jones is tossed out by Swoop McCarthy, while Ace Newton uses his quickness and smarts to eliminate Gino Montero. Newton holds back while Retro and McCarthy go at it. When Retro gets the Aussie over the top rope, Newton attacks. He gets Retro chasing him, eventually getting Retro to charge across the ring and a very nice hip toss eliminates the final competitor to leave just Ace Newton. An entertaining and fast-paced match that never really grabs the crowd. That doesn't top Ace Newton from celebrating as he is award the SWF Generations title belt.

Ace Newton Wins at 12:04 by Eliminating Robbie Retro to Win the SWF Generations Championship

Grade: D

Phil Vibert: I'm not the first to say it, but that Newton kid has a future.

Quote:

Bad Kompany

In-Ring Promo

The crowd reactions minimally as the music hits and the two unknowns make their make way down to the ring. Both youngsters are stylishly dressed in suits and they carry themselves with obvious confidence.

Joss Thompson: For weeks now, you've been wondering one thing - who is B.K. Well, we're here to finally show the world that we're Badd Kompany.

Sean Deeley: That's Kompany with a K!

Joss Thompson: You might not know us yet, but you will. You will know us very well.

Seen Deeley: We're a pretty big deal.

Joss Thompson: Badd Kompany is about to become the dominant tag team force in the Supreme Wrestling Federation. So you'd be well served to pay attention. I'm Joss Thompson. You might not recognize me, but I just came back from a couple of years dominating England.

Seen Deeley: And this specimen is Sean Deeley.

Joss Thompson: You might recognize Sean here from his two NCAA wrestling National Championships. Or as a three time All-American. You will soon recognize him as one of the greatest technical grapplers that the SWF has ever seen.

Seen Deeley: We're here to dominate.

Joss Thompson: And dominate we will. The Biggz Boys. Sexual Aggression. The Bumfholes. High Concept. These are all the teams of the past. Its time to look into the future. And that future is Badd Kompany.

Grade: B-

Phil Vibert: Well, they certainly don't lack confidence.

Quote:

The Biggz Boyz vs Sexual Aggression

SWF World Tag Team Championship Match

The four-time tag champions give the current champs a decent fight. The match turns dirty as Jessie is encouraging the Biggz brothers to win by whatever means necessary, a tactic that Joe Sexy adopts naturally. Jessie tries to interfere more than once but with no success. Sexual Aggression successfully defend their titles when Sexy gets the pin on Brett Biggz after a low blow. The champions celebrate and Jessie looks extremely unimpressed.

Joe Sexy Wins at 13:41 via Pinfall

Grade: C-

Quote:

The Amazing Bumfholes vs Supreme Nightmare

The Bumfholes start out with a determination and drive that puts Eisen and Train on their heels. But the Supreme duo battle back and a pretty solid match ensues. The brothers show an ability and willingness to work with Train and make the big man look good. Eisen looks solid against the former tag champs as well. Despite their determination, the Bumfholes seem unwilling to fight dirty and that gives their opponents the edge. A bit of underhanded teamwork is enough for Eisen to get the pin on Zimmy. The Bumfholes look crestfallen after the loss.

Eric Eisen Wins at 10:04 via Pinfall

Grade: B

Peter Michael: Next up, Big Smack Scott returns after several months away from Supreme.

Quote:

The Warriors vs The Pain Alliance

Same old Big Smack Scott. The botch-machine drags down what would have been an otherwise solid match. He obviously infuriates his opponents by no-selling several moves, leading to both work a bit stiff. Laramee is game and brawls in an entertaining manner with both Tyler and Caufield. The fans are focused on Scott, though. Caufield gets the pin on the muscle-freak to put the match to a thankful end.

Chris Caufield Wins at 7:44 via Submission

Grade: C

During the match, Eric Eisen and Runaway Train make their way back to ringside, watching Caufield and Tyler. The Bumfholes come out as well, followed by Gilmore and Sexy.

Quote:

Eric Tyler, Chris Caufield, Angry Gilmore, Joe Sexy, Randy Bumfhole, Zimmy Bumfhole, Eric Eisen + Runaway Train

In-Ring Brawl

After the match ends, Eisen and Train go after Tyler and Caufield. Laramee and Scott bail as everyone pours into the ring and a brawl erupts. Its a bit of chaos as the four tag teams all go after each other again. Once again, Tyler and Train seem intent on ending each other. The officials take some time to get it all broken up and everyone separated.

Grade: B

Quote:

Brandon James vs Thom Barrowman

Originally scheduled to face Christian Faith, Brandon James does not seem impressed. Or smart. But Barrowman seems to put the big man on his heels a bit with a strong offensive attack from the bell. James soon counters and takes control of the match. Its a bit back-and-forth as the fans get an entertaining brawl with a bit of technique thrown in for fun. James overpowers Barrowman to get the win.

Brandon James Wins at 12:04 via Pinfall

Grade: B-

Quote:

Jack Bruce

In-Ring Promo

The crowd is hot for the champion. As always, the charismatic Bruce does a masterful job in playing with the crowd and revving them up further. He doesn't move quite as quick as usual on his way into the ring, but he doesn't move with a limp at all.

Jack Bruce: Who loves the Long Island Angel?

The crowd roars, making it clear that they do indeed love him.

Jack Bruce: And I love you right back. Now I didn't come out here to tell you all that I'm going to beat Remo tonight. Because I know that and you know that already. He's not taking my belt and even he knows it. The reason I'm out here and talking to you all is that I have a few words for Vengeance.

The crowd roars again, as Bruce toys with them before continuing.

Jack Bruce: Vengeance, I'm tired of you trying to take shots at me all the time. You can't beat me like a man here in this ring, so you try to hurt Show Time in any way you can. You moan about wanting the belt but you can't take it from me. You can't get us apart. Now you're down to beating up kids in the back. What the hell is wrong with you? After Faith finishes with you tonight, there probably won't be much left. But if there is, and you want another chance at me and this belt, then just go beg Eisen for it. If you want to lose to me again, then I'm more than willing to beat you one more time at Master of Puppets. But tonight, it's Show Time!

The crowd cheers him all the way up the ramp.

Grade: A

Peter Michaels: This next one should be great!

Quote:

Christian Faith vs Vengeance

An absolute war as both men try to tear each other to pieces. The battle rages inside and outside the ring with few breaks. The two big men simply have at each other with everything they have. Neither relents. In the end, Vengeance has to grab his chain and wrap it around his knuckles to deliver a near knockout blow to Faith while the ref is on his back. Even still, The Harbinger barely manages to get the pin. Faith does not look impressed after the match.

Vengeance Wins at 18:54 via Pinfall

Grade: A

Peter Michaels: An absolute war.

Phil Vibert: Faith can sure still lay it on the line for an old guy.

Jerry Eisen: He's not that old.

Peter Michaels: For a wrestler he is.

Jerry Eisen: Oh yeah, I guess.

Quote:

Phil Vibert

In-Ring Segment

Vibert has a slight grin on his face as he makes his way into the ring, escorted by his music. He gets a pretty strong positive reaction from the crowd and he lets it quiet before he speaks.

Phil Vibert: I'm quite certain that you perceptive fans may have noticed this already, but it seems like there may be some collusion between Big Money Incorporated and The Genuis Mr. Eisen. Its shocking, I know. But its true. And I was thinking last night about that apparent collusion, and I was thinking that it could come into play in match tonight between Rich Money and Steve Frehley.

The crowd sound sits appreciation.

Phil Vibert: Now I'm concerned that Steve Frehley won't get a legitimate chance to beat Money. I believe that Money will do whatever it takes to retain his title, including getting himself disqualified or counted out. And we know that Big Money Incorporated won't hesitate to interfere. Phil Vibert: So as I was thinking about all this, I realized it was up to me to do something. To make sure that Frehley gets a legit chance in that match. Which is why I'm flexing my creative muscle and making some adjustments to that match. That match will now be no disqualification and no count out.

The crowd apparently approves, as they roar again.

Phil Vibert: I am also banning Remo and Brandon James from ringside. If Rich Money wants to beat Steve Frehley, he's going to have to do it on his own. I realize that The Genuis Mr. Eisen won't be too pleased with the changes I've made. But you know what? I don't give a damn.

Grade: B+

Jerry Eisen: I don't think dad is going to be too happy.

Peter Michael: I kind of think that's the point, Jerry.

Quote:

Steve Frehley vs Rich Money

SWF North American Championship Match

Another war as the two men battle over the North American title belt. Rich Money proves the superior technical wrestler as he manages to control longer stretches with a combination of technique and stalling. The champion continually slows down Frehley's powerful attacks by slipping out of the ring, mugging to the crowd every time he does. Frehley displays impressive power and tremendous heart, as he battles through everything that Money throws at him. Taking advantage of the no disqualification rule, Money grabs a chair from ringside and uses it repeatedly on Frehley. The big man is left down and out for an easy pin. The ref wrestles the chair away from Money, so he doesn't immediately get the pin count going, and Frehley manages to kick out as the crowd encourages him on. Still groggy, he batters at Money and manages to hit the champion with a particularly vicious Dark Destroyer Spear which gives him the pin and the victory. And the belt.

Grade: A

Steve Frehley Wins at 21:23 via Pinfall to Win the SWF North American Championship

Peter Michaels: He did it! I didn't think he'd do it, but he did it! He really did it!

Phil Vibert: He sure did.

Quote:

Remo vs Jack Bruce

SWF World Heavyweight Championship Match

The match is open and entertaining, as both men have a chance to control the pace. Neither can get a real edge in the early going. Remo's advantage in power is countered by Bruce's quickness and athleticism. They batter away at each other, with Bruce showing off the flashing moves that made him Show Time. Remo keeps coming though, and he slows the champion by beginning to work over the knee that was apparently injured two weeks before. It slows Bruce considerably and the challenger takes control. For a long spell, Bruce is battered around and Remo goes for the pin several times. It looks like Bruce could be done, but he stuns Remo with a series of quick shots, then manages to land his New York Minute. Remo is not done, and literally tosses Bruce off when he goes for the pin. Remo continues to maul Bruce and then performs a nasty power bomb. The crowd is quiet, fearing the champion is done. And he is - he doesn't kick out of the pin. Instead, Vengeance charges into the ring after appearing out of nowhere and he tosses Remo off of Bruce. Remo rolls out of the ring, confused. The ref looks unsure of what to do. When Vengeance begins to attack the unmoving Bruce, the ref calls for the bell to signal for a Bruce victory via disqualification.

Jack Bruce Wins at 23:05 via Disqualification to Retain the SWF World Heavyweight Championship

Grade: B

Jack Bruce, Vengeance, Remo, Christian Faith, Rich Money, Brandon James, Lobster Warrior, and Steve Frehley

In-Ring Brawl

Watching from the outside as Vengeance begins to attack Jack Bruce, Remo is stunned and angry. Just as Christian Faith races into the ring to pull The Harbinger off the champion, Remo goes after Vengeance as well. After Vengeance slips out of the ring for a moment, Faith and Remo stare at each other, unsure of what to do. Both go after Vengeance, who doesn't back down.

Brandon James and Rich Money appear, going after Christian Faith. Bruce is still in the ring, but barely moving. James attacks Faith while Rich Money attempts to separate Remo and Vengeance. The situation escalates as Steve Frehley and Lobster Warrior race down the ramp and get involved. It dissolves into an all-out brawl on the outside of the ring. Vengeance is trying to get back into the ring to go after Bruce. Faith and Frehley hold him out, even while fighting with James and Money. Staggering to his feet, Bruce suddenly launches himself across the ring and flies over the top rope, crashing into Vengeance who is a good 8 feet from the edge of the ring.

An army of officials have descended upon the mess, trying to get everyone separated and calm. But there is no calming down this war. The men continue to battle around the officials.

After several more minutes, Faith and Warrior manage to get the battered Bruce into the ring, while Frehley yells at Rich Money. Big Money Incorporated are on the outside of the ring with Vengeance. Money looks like he's about to go after Frehley in the ring when Remo attacks Vengeance again and James and Money have to separate them. Emma Chase is with them at this point, screaming at everyone.

Grade: A

Peter Michaels: Chaos! Its chaos here at the Garden! There's going to be some fallout from this night!
