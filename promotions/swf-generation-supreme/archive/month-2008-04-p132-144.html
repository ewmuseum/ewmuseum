SWF Supreme TV

Tuesday, Week 4, April 2008

V. Thompson Arena (South West) - 8,632

Announcers

Peter Michaels - Phil Vibert - Jerry Eisen

________________________________________

Peter Michaels: Welcome to Supreme TV everyone. I'm Peter Michaels. With me tonight are Phil Vibert and Jerry Eisen.

Phil Vibert: Looking forward to this tonight, Peter. Its going to be amazing.

Jerry Eisen: I'm excited! I'm excited! I'm excited!

Peter Michaels: I think we get that, Jerry.

Quote:

Steve Frehley

In-Ring Segment

Frehley storms out to the ring to start the show. He looks rather unhappy.

Steve Frehley: There are some people backstage who have explaining to do. Some men who call themselves my friends. But friends are supposed to be there when you need them. And as everyone saw last week, they weren't there for me.

A short, loaded pause. The crowd is quiet but attentive.

Steve Frehley: Christian Faith. Lobster Warrior. Jack Bruce. You need to come out here right now!

Lobster Warrior, Christian Faith + Jack Bruce

The trio make their way down to the ring. Christian Faith is in his ring gear but both Lobster Warrior and Jack Bruce are in street clothes. Lobby is wearings bits of his lobster costume with the street clothes. Bruce seems to hobble a bit. Frehley glares at them as they get into the ring.

Steve Frehley: Now, I don't know how you three define the word "friend". But I've considered a friend someone who would be there for me. Who would stand up for you. Someone who has your back. Do you three consider me a friend?

Christian Faith: Of course we do, Steve.

Steve Frehley: Then where the hell where you three last week?

Lobster Warrior: We... we didn't know if you wanted help.

Steve Frehley: Didn't know if I wanted help? What? Are you serious, Lobby?

Jack Bruce: Do you think that was easy to watch, Steve? The Long Island Angel prides himself on protecting his friends. All three of us wanted nothing more than to run out there and get involved. But we didn't know if we should.

Steve Frehley: You guys thought I wanted to get beat down by those three Big Money thugs?

Christian Faith: No. But we didn't want to get involved in your business. You've told me - told all of us - repeatedly. That you don't want other people sticking their noses in your business.

And that's what we would have been doing.

Steve Frehley: Maybe sometimes you should!

Christian Faith: And how are we supposed to know when, Steve? We don't read minds. What is it you've told me a number of times? 'Steve Frehley takes care of his own business'. You've told me that time and again. You repeat it like a motto. So I believe you. As your friend, I respect that you want to deal with things on your own.

Lobster Warrior: When you want help, all you have to do is ask.

Jack Bruce: Show Time is always there for you, Steve.

Steve Frehley: It sure doesn't seem that way, Jack.

Christian Faith: Steve, I even told you last week that Money would try to get back at you after you laid down that belt. That he would respond, that all of them would respond. You told me, "I'll deal with it". You didn't want my advice and you didn't want my help. So how am I supposed to do the opposite of what you've already told me? Isn't that sticking my nose in your business.

Steve Frehley: Well, I'll tell all of you this... You won't need to worry about "my business" anymore at all. Because after last week, I made a phone call. I reached out to someone who helped me out when I first got started in this business. A true friend. Someone who I know will have my back at all times. Someone who won't hesitate and stay in the back to watch on a monitor...

There is a loaded silence, then....

Ray Diaz

The crowd doesn't recognize the music, and they don't really react to the hulking beast that makes his way down to the ring. Diaz climbs into the ring and stands behind Frehley. The big man radiates intensity as he glares at the trio.

Steve Frehley: This is Ray Diaz. He helped me out a lot when I got started in wrestling. Taught me a helluva lot. He's been taking care of things in Japan for a few years, but you'll all get to know him very well. So now none of you three have to worry about coming out to help me ever again. I have my backup. I have the only friend I need. That's just the way it is.

Frehley drops the microphone at the feet of Faith, Warrior, and Bruce. He stares a them for a moment, then rolls out of the ring. The monstrous Diaz glares for a moment longer, then follows his friend.

Grade: A

Peter Michaels: That Diaz is a monster!

Jerry Eisen: I have no idea who he is.

Phil Vibert: Why doesn't that not surprise me?

Quote:

Joe Sexy vs Christian Faith

The two veterans brawlers put on a memorable slobber-knocker battle. It was back-and-forth, with Sexy taking an upper hand before Faith battled back as he has so many times through his amazing career. The crowd is hot for the match, which drives both wrestlers forward. It is a fantastic start to the night.

Christian Faith Wins at 12:05 via Pinfall

Grade: A

Peter Michaels: What a battle! What a war!

Quote:

Richard Eisen + Emma Chase

Richard Eisen struts out to the ring, accompanied by Emma Chase. Chase carries with her the SWF North American title belt. Chase looks pretty pleased with herself.

Richard Eisen: You all know why I'm out here. The actions of Steve Frehley lat week, the regrettable actions that I never expect out of an SWF champion, left me with a conundrum. But that's the kind of thing that I can handle. Because I didn't build this promotion into the biggest and best in the world by being a better businessman or more ruthless than anyone else. I built it by having better ideas.

Emma Chase: You certainly did.

Richard Eisen: So this was a minor problem for me to tackle. Not something I'm about to lose sleep over.

Emma Chase: And, of course, you came to the best possible solution.

Richard Eisen: Indeed I did, Emma. Starting next week, we will have an 8-man Grand Prix elimination tournament for the SWF North American championship.

The crowd roars their approval. Eisen smiles and allows them to settl down again. Its odd for the CEO to get anything but heel heat, but the crowd seems to approve this time.

Richard Eisen: The 8 participants will be randomly selected at the start of Supreme TV next week. The first two quarter final matches will take place next, the other two on the show following. The semi finals and finals will take place at the Master of Puppets show in Week 2 of May!

The crowd roars again.

Emma Chase: Eight men! One champion!

Richard Eisen: Only one man can reign. Now, anyone wishing to enter the tournament will have until the end of the show tonight to submit their names to me backstage. The only stipulation is that any current belt-holder will have to vacate that title to participate.

Emma Chase: So choose carefully, boys, but choose quickly!

The two depart the ring, with a surprising and lingering roar of approval following them up the ramp.

Grade: A

Peter Michaels: It all starts next week! You can't afford to miss the next Supreme TV!

Quote:

Thom Barrowman vs Vengeance

Barrowman uses everything in his offensive arsenal on the big man, but Vengeance just keeps coming. The Harbinger will not be deterred on this night. Barrowman manages to just barely slow Vengeance down, and never for long. The match ends when Shane Stones calls the match after Vengeance chokes Barrowman out cold.

Vengeance Wins at 9:02 via Stoppage

Grade: B-

Quote:

Rich Money + Emma Chase

In-Ring Promo

No cheers for Emma Chase this time. Both her and Rich Money got a heated reception from the crowd. As always, Money's strutting fires up the crowd. When they get to the ring, the former North American champion shushes the crowd, which gets them further riled.

Rich Money: Now, let me start by saying that I'm not the kind of man who bothers much with self-reflection. When you're as talented and successful as I am, its just not necessary. But for the past week, I've been thinking long and hard about the SWF North American title belt. Emma and I have been having some serious discussions about my future.

Emma Chase: Obviously, we knew about the Grand Prix tournament for the belt well before anyone else. Those are the benefits of being as well connected as Big Money Incorporated is.

The crowd again showers the duo with heat.

Rich Money: Now, we're completely confident that if I am going to enter this tournament, I will emerge victorious. I am money, after all. But the simple question - do I want to? Is being the secondary champion in the greatest wrestling promotion in the whole d*mned world good enough for Rich Money?

Emma Chase: Of course its not!

Rich Money: No, it isn't. Its time for Rich Money to move on. Just like I've moved on from worrying about Steve Frehley. He might have won my belt, but you all saw me kick his *ss last week on Supreme TV. So I'd say we're even. And to be perfectly honest, I have more important things to worry about. Like winning the World Heavyweight championship.

The crowd lets Big Money Incorporated know how they feel about that possibility.

Rich Money: As the Alpha Dogs of the SWF, its only a matter of time before one of us gets that title from Jack Bruce. And I figure it might as well be me.

Emma Chase: Someone who knows how to be a champion. Unlike Jack Bruce!

Rich Money: That's right. Someone who will be prestige and pride to the belt. I've done all I can for the North American title. Its time to make the World Heavyweight belt mean something again!

Emma Chase: After how much Jack Bruce has tarnished that championship, its going to need a lot of work!

Rich Money: That's true, Emma. But I'm confident. Confident about getting that belt and confident about being worthy of it. I'll take down Jack Bruce and become the next champion of the SWF. You can bank on it.

The duo take their team leaving the ring and getting up the ramp, as they rile up the crowd the entire time. Both show master manipulation skills.

Grade: A*

Peter Michaels: Those two are no good!

Jerry Eisen: I dunno, I think Mr. Bruce keeps that belt pretty clean!

Quote:

The Amazing Bumfholes vs Supreme Pain

The Bumfhole brothers are absolutely ruthless as they attack and pick apart the veteran duo of Roberts and Train. They have the look of a tag team on a mission. Eric Eisen doesn't even have a chance to interfere properly. The crowd loves the all-action style the Bumfholes put forth as they simply outpace the two veterans. Randy gets an unlikely submission as he gets the technical Enforcer Roberts to tap.

Randy Bumfhole Wins at 9:54 via Submission

Grade: B

Phil Vibert: I think those two are a little bit motivated.

Quote:

Lobster Warrior, Remo, + Brandon James

Backstage Attack

Lobster Warrior is backstage, still only half in his costume and half in his street clothes. He's watching Rich Money's segment on a monitor and obviously getting riled up at Money's brashness. Lobby hears something and glances over his shoulder.

Remo: Remember me?

Remo flattens Lobster Warrior with a vicious sucker punch. He and Brandon James then go to work on the Crustacaen, kicking and stomping. Lobby tries to get to his feet under the reign of blows, but simply gets knocked down again. And again. And again. Then...

Christian Faith

Christian Faith comes racing in and absolutely levels Brandon James with a perfectly-placed elbow to the side of his head. James drops. Faith then faces off with Remo. The two circle each other, then go at it. They brawl and batter each other as Lobster Warrior recomposes himself. Then he joins in the fray. Now outnumbered, Remo soon bolts. James recovers enough to follow him.

Grade: A

Phil Vibert: Well, those Big Money Incorporated guys sure are good at sneak attacks.

Jerry Eisen: I think they must practice a lot or something.

Quote:

Eric Eisen vs Antonio Marquez

Eisen faces off against Marquez for the second time. The first match Eisen won handily just after Marquez's SWF debut. This time its not so easy for the bosses won. Marquez shows a wealth of offensive skills that has Eisen bewildered. Its nothing that a bit of cheating can't take care of, and a few cheap shots slows Marquez down. A bit of interference from Runaway Train at ringside and its all Eisen needs to record another victory.

Eric Eisen Wins at 8:31 via Pinfall

Grade: B

Quote:

Jack Bruce

In-Ring Promo

Jack Bruce comes out and does his standard "It's Show Time!" routine, which gets the crowd very riled. He then talks a bit about what Vengeance has been doing of late, and that the Long Island Angel will have to teach him about respect. The champion is interrupted by...

Richard Eisen

Richard Eisen: You are going to teach someone about respect, Jack? That's rich. That's truly golden.

Jack Bruce: Is coming out here and mocking your federation's champion really good business, Eisen?

Richard Eisen: If you were the kind of champion that this federation needs, I wouldn't need to do that, Jack. If you were the kind of champion that these fans deserve...

Jack Bruce: You sure do know how to treat your Supreme Legends...

Richard Eisen: Don't deceive yourself, Bruce. You aren't a Supreme Legend yet. Not by a long shot. One title reign doesn't make you a Legend in the SWF. You could be. From the moment you walked into Supreme One, I wanted to make you the centerpiece of the Supreme Empire. But you didn't want that. You didn't want to work with me. I still remember your "Show Time isn't a puppet" speech from a year ago. You wanted to forge your own path. So instead of being a true Supreme Legend, you get to be another talented guy who held the SWF World title for a bit and that's it.

Jack Bruce: Jack Bruce isn't a puppet! And he d*mned sure isn't your puppet, Eisen. I would rather lay down this belt and walk away than be the kind of champion you wanted me to be!

Richard Eisen: That can be arranged.

Jack Bruce: You think I would make it that easy for you? You can keep sending your puppets after Show Time and keep watching them fall. You'll never take this belt away from me!

Richard Eisen: Don't be so sure. I have someone special for you tonight.

Jack Bruce: I'm not wrestling tonight.

Richard Eisen: Last time I checked the booking sheet, your name was on there. Let me see here... main event... Jack Bruce... versus To Be Announced for the SWF World Heavyweight championship. Yup, that's definitely you, Jack.

Jack Bruce: My doctor...

Richard Eisen: Yes, yes, I know. You gave a note from your specialist about your knee. Your bad right knee. That's all well and good, Jack.But while a doctor's note might enough to excuse from class at school,it doesn't supersede the Supreme Wrestling Federation Charter. I know the Charter pretty well, being that I was the one who wrote it. And that charter states that an SWF title-holder who is unable to defend that title, by reason of injury or persoal matters, in a match booked with advance notice, is subject to forfeiture of said title. Which is really just a fancy way to say that if you can't defend tonight, you give up that belt.

Jack Bruce: You're just looking for a way to steal my belt, Eisen!

Richard Eisen: I prefer to say that I'm looking for a way to have the belt around the waist of a wrestler than Supreme can be proud of, Jack. And that definitely isn't you. Now, since you've been scheduled to defend your precious belt here tonight for several weeks, you have two options. Its really up to you.

Jack Bruce: Show Time doesn't back down from anyone.

Richard Eisen: Excellent, Jack. It will be so much more satisfying to see you lose than to strip you of the belt.

Jack Bruce: Only in your dreams, Eisen.

Richard Eisen: My dreams have a way of coming true. Oh, and you'll love your opponent for tonight. He's going to be what you could have been, Jack. He's going to be the new face of SWF.

With a malicious grin, Eisen slow makes his way back up the ramp. Bruce takes his time, playing up to the crowd and screaming "It's Show Time!" at them. He does take it easy on his knee, however.

Grade: A

Peter Michaels: Who could it be?

Phil Vibert: I don't know, and that worries me.

Quote:

Eric Tyler vs Remo

The two men go after each other from the opening bell. Remo uses his explosive power to push Tyler around. The veteran Tyler repeatedly used his technical grappling skills to slow the match down a bit, but he can't contain Remo for any length of time. The match reaches a bit of a stalemate, but some indirect interference from Emma Chase allows Remo to take advantage again and he uses that to take the victory.

Remo Wins at 11:19 via Pinfall

Grade: B

Quote:

Angry Gilmore vs Chris Caufield

Another classic battle between two veterans. The match has a bit of everything, as Gilmore displays his versatile technical excellence and Caufield shows his impressive toughness. Neither man gives an inch. The crowd loves every moment of their encounter, the intense crowd driving both men forward. A series of beautiful transitions from Gilmore gives him the opportunity he needs to get Caufield in a front face lock in the middle of the ring, forcing a submission.

Angry Gilmore Wins at 14:05 via Submission

Grade: A

Peter Michaels: Who could the opponent for Jack Bruce be?

Phil Vibert: It has to be someone new! Eisen doesn't have anyone else up his sleeve from within the SWF.

Quote:

Rocky Golden vs Jack Bruce

SWF World Heavyweight Championship Match

The crowd has no idea who to expect as unfamiliar music plays... Then Rocky Golden strolls out and gets a pop of recognition. Then a bit of heat as the fans realize that he is the opponent for the match.

The two men size each other up slowly, engaging gradually. Neither man knows the other, and its a cautious start. The power of Golden carries him early. Bruce has a problem not only with the size and power of the newcomer, but also with his knee. Its clearly bothering him throughout the match. The champion works past the pain, however, and the fans love him for it. Golden slowly wears Bruce down, until Bruce takes the match off the mat and to the air. Golden has no answer for that. At one point, Bruce leaps off the top rope and crashes onto Golden, who is down outside the ring. Bruce gets a clean pin shortly after and begins to celebrate.

Jack Bruce Wins at 15:05 via Pinfall

Grade: B

In-Ring Face-Off

As Bruce celebrates, Vengeance comes storming out to the ring. Both Rocky Golden and the referee get in his way and attempt to stop The Harbinger. Several other officials get involved as Bruce and Vengeance glare at each other across the ring.

Grade A*

Peter Michaels: This feud isn't over! Its far from over!

Quote:

Random thought of the day... for every action, there has to be a reaction. It doesn't seem like the reaction has to be equal, and sometimes its something you don't expect, but there is going to be a reaction.

It didn't take long for the fallout from my explosion at Sinclair to begin dropping from the sky. Within days, half the roster seemed to be looking at my differently. Most of the looks seemed to be a bit more respectful, but a few of those looks seemed to a harbor a bit of suspicion as well. It definitely feels like things are changing with how most of the roster regards me, and I'm not certain yet if it's a positive development or not.

What I am hearing is second second or even third hand. But it seems as though there are two different versions of the meeting circulating about. One is cleary sourced from Sinclair himself, as it has him laughing off all my blustering, calling my bluff, and generally coming out of the meeting looking good while I played the fool. The other version is shockingly accurate. But that didn't come from me and I can't imagine it came from Sinclair. Unless he told the real story to someone like Laramee and they circulated it? More likely is that someone with an office near mine overheard everything and passed it around. I did leave my door open and I was definitely loud enough to be overheard. At least most people seem to be buying the real story and not Sinclair's work of fiction.

Being looked at with increasing respect is definitely not a bad thing. But the consequences of that might be. It seems like some of the younger guys that I know pretty well, who I've basically started out with, are treating me a bit different. A bit less like a friend and a bit more respectful, like they would with a superior. Which I guess I am, but I don't want to be treated like I'm just their superior and no longer a friend. But now I've got guys like Devine and Parker calling me "sir" all the time, and they mean it completely with respect. Are they ever going to see me just as an equal again? Unlikely.

Of course, I can count on Robbie not changing his attitude towards me. We've become pretty good friends and are too honest with each other for him to start showing any respect at this point. I also like that he has a different perspective on things. He's the same age as me, but he's been in the business longer. When we grabbed some food the other day, the Sinclair incident was the primary topic.

"So did you really tell him that Cornell would laugh in his face if he ever tried to pick up with TCW, and that he'd end up jobbing to Valentine in USPW?" Robbie asked as he munched on his quesedilla and made eyes at a couple blondes a tables over.

I shook my head, saying, "I told him Cornell wouldn't take a chance on him. Did say he could end up in USPW jobbing to Bruce or Valentine."

"Nice. Can you imagine Valentine versus Sinclair? Now that would be a match to see."

"Talk about a train wreck. And those girls are high school, dude."

"Yeah, they probably are. Anyway, you got the roster talking. That's a story that's going to be circulating around for a bit," he told me. "I even heard that a version of it made it onto a couple of the wrestling boards."

"Great. Probably the one where Sinclair looks good. Anyway, what's the locker room saying about me?"

Robbie shrugged. He said, "Everyone knows that Scott juices and that he's been given too many chances already. Most people that I've heard talk about it think its great that someone finally got in his face about it. The guy's a menace and honestly, pretty much everyone thinks he doesn't deserve to be in Supreme at all."

"But what about me? Are they scared of me now?"

"Are they supposed to be?"

"No! I don't want to be seen as any different from any other young guy on the roster. Just another wrestler paying my dues! I don't want everyone looking at me with fear."

Robbie put his food down and gave me a serious look over his sunglasses. He said, "Looks, its pretty d*mned clear that you're not just another guy on the roster at this point. I think even Brandon may have it figured out. You never wrestle but you're at every show. Everyone on the Creative Committee defers to you, even Michaels and he's supposed to be the Head. You even have your own office at Supreme One! There's no hiding it. No use pretending. It would be like Eric E. trying pretend he's not part of the Eisen clan."

All I could manage to mutter was a weak, "D*mn. I suppose"

Even Richard Eisen gave me a thumbs up on the way I handled the Sinclair deal. "Heard it went really well," he told me. "Knew you could handle it."

I nodded, but said, "I got my point across, but I doubt he's going to change. I would be shcoked if we don't get another positive back in the next couple of months."

The Supreme supremo nodded and shrugged, saying, "Probably. We'll deal with it if it happens."

Will Eisen really let me fire Sinclair when he screws up again? I sure hope so. At this point, I dislike Sinclair more than I dislike Bowen. I wasn't exaggerating when I told Big Smack Scott that I would fight to get him fired if he tests positive again. I absolutely will. In the meantime, though, I have other things to worry about.

Robbie is right - there's really no way to hide my role anymore. With increasing frequency, workers are coming to me with stuff that should be brought to the Head Booker. From complaints to concerns to ideas, more of it is being brought directly to me rather than arriving indirectly. Michaels seems happy with that, too. I'm not catching the feeling that many are having an issue with it, which is a relief. I guess there's no use pretending I'm just another wrestler anymore. I guess is Eisen and Michaels were hoping to force me to establish my authority a bit more by having me deal with the Sinclair situation, it worked. Even if they had me deal with simply because they didn't want the headache, I guess it still worked. Is there going to be further fallout? I have the feeling there will be, but I just don't know what yet.

Sunday, Week 4, April, 2008
