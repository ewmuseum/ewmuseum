SWF Master of Puppets 2008

Thursday, Week 2, May 2008

The Gardens of North Dakota (Mid West) - 23, 683

Announcers

Peter Michaels - Phil Vibert - Jerry Eisen

________________________________________

Peter Michaels: Welcome to Master of Puppets 2008! I'm Peter Michaels. With me tonight are Phil Vibert and Jerry Eisen.

Phil Vibert: I'm no one's puppet, Michaels, but I'm happy to be here.

Quote:

Pay Per View Promo Video

A high-quality promo video plays, showcasing the top feuds in the SWF. It includes several minutes of footage from the feud between Jack Bruce and Vengeance, and serves to get the crowd fired up.

Grade: A

Quote:

Chris Caufield vs Runaway Train vs Christian Faith

The opening match is a Threefold Threat match and its an all-out brawl. All three men end up bleeding heavily. The fans truly loved the battle. Christian Faith begins to bleed late in the match when he's thrown into the stairs by Runaway Train. When he starts to show red, Faith goes into Raging Bull mode and tears through his two opponents. He uses the momentum to hit a Leap of Faith on Train while Caufield is out of the ring, and gets the pinfall win.

Christian Faith Wins at 11:32 via Pinfall

Grade: B+

Peter Michaels: Next up we have the draw for the North American Championship Grand Prix.

Phil Vibert: The rigged draw, you mean.

Quote:

Richard Eisen + Jessie

In-Ring Draw

Richard Eisen again brings out Jessie to help him with what will be the final draw of the Grand Prix. The SWF CEO is has a serious swagger and he looks confident. He starts by telling the crowd that they are going to witness history tonight and its a night they will always remember.

This time, there are only four names to be drawn. No ping pong ball cage this time. Just four envelopes. They are put into a small bin, mixed up, and Jessie draws them one at a time. The first name is...

Rocky Golden

Golden comes out to the top of the ramp. The fans already dislike the new SWF "Golden Boy".

Golden's opponent will be...

Marc DuBois

The youngster comes out to the top of the ramp with a great deal of energy. He actually get a decent pop from the crowd.

That means the last match is already set, but Eisen and Jessie go through the motions regardless. The next name drawn is...

Eric Eisen

The "Chosen One" Eric Eisen. This gets a knowing nod from his father, Richard Eisen. And that leaves only...

Valiant

Valiant makes his way out to the top of the ramp. He looks determined. The Semi Finals and potential Finale are set!

Grade: A

Quote:

Valiant & Giedroyc vs Consequences vs The Biggz Boys

A well-worked and entertaining three-way tag battle. Despite having another match later in the night, Valiant doesn't hold back and he takes a bit of a beating from the duo of Parker and McClean. The teamwork of the Biggz brothers allows them to control portions of the match, but they find themselves overpowered. In the end, its Valiant & Giedroyc taking the win to become the number one contenders for the tag team titles.

Valiant Wins at 12:03 via Pinfall

Grade: B-

Quote:

Rocky Golden vs Marc DuBois

SWF North American Championship Grand Prix Semi Final

Rocky Golden looks as though he expects an easy match, as he seems to take DuBois lightly. The Golden Boy quickly overpowers the youngster and goes for a pin. Despite a quick count, DuBois gets his shoulder up, and begins to fight back. The Canadian flat out-wrestles the bigger man, making Golden look almost amateurish at times. Richard Eisen is at ringside and he gradually looks more and more foul through the match. When DuBois finally gets Golden to tap, Eisen looks furious.

Marc DuBois Wins at 9:43 via Submission

Grade: C

Peter Michaels: The kid wins it! No one saw that coming!

Jerry Eisen: Uh-oh...

Quote:

Chris Morrisette

In-Ring Promo

The crowd doesn't react to unfamiliar music, and seems confused when an unrecognized wrestler comes to the ring in street gear. It becomes apparent that its Lobster Warrior without his familiar costume, and the crowd roars. But Lobby looks oddly intense. He grabs a microphone.

Chris Morrisette: Remo! You better be listening to this. This is it. This is what you get now. No more Lobster Warrior. The Undersea Superhero is gone. I'm just Chris Morrisette now. This is what you wanted, Remo. You wanted to kill Lobby. Well, you have. Consider Lobster Warrior dead. Now you have Morrisette to deal with. And that's not a good thing for you, Remo. Or any of Big Money Incorporated. Its really bad news, actually. Because at least Lobster Warrior had a sense of humor. A fun side. Even a soft side. Morrisette doesn't. Morrisette doesn't worry about anything but kicking ass in this ring. And I'm starting with you, Remo!

The doesn't seem certain how to react as the man former known as Lobster Warrior drops the microphone and leaves the ring. After a moment of silence, they roar.

Grade: A

Quote:

Eric Eisen vs Valiant

SWF North American Championship Grand Prix Semi Final

The match is an open back-and-forth affair, with both men getting plenty of offense. The obvious exhaustion of Valiant after his earlier match and the willing cheap-shots of Eisen give the edge to the "Chosen One". When Eisen tosses Valiant from the ring, Runaway Train works him over while Eisen distracts the ref. That's enough for Eisen to take control and get the win. Richard Eisen looks pleased while Eric Eisen celebrates excessively.

Eric Eisen Wins at 11:54 via Pinfall

Grade: B-

Phil Vibert: Well that was predictable.

Quote:

Jack Bruce + Dani Nevada

In-Ring Promo

Jack Bruce is interviewed in the ring by Dani Nevada. The lovely young lady is barely able to get a word in edge-wise as the champion rants and raves. He gets the crowd revved up as a he screams about it being Show Time and how no one will take his title. He apparently isn't afraid of Vengeance, even if its in a Supreme Cell. The Long Island Angel is ready to bleed! Because its Show Time!

Grade: A*

Quote:

Enygma vs Darryl Devine

SWF Shooting Star Ladder Match for the Shooting Star Championship

A rather unexciting match that brings the crowd down a bit. The wrestlers try, but the crowd seems to deflate a bit after the excitement of the Jack Bruce promo. Devine comes back to get the win, which boosts the crowd a bit.

Darryl Devine Wins at 9:53 via Item Retrieval

Grade: C-

Quote:

The Amazing Bumfholes vs Sexual Aggression

SWF World Tag Team Championships Match

An entertaining match between two tag teams going all out. The Bumfholes seem almost desperate in their determination. Joe Sexy once again proves quite willing to cheat his way to victory, but Angry Gilmore is unwilling. He focuses on wrestling instead, and gets into something of an argument when Gilmore won't takes cheap shots at Zimmy when the ref is distracted. Despite the best efforts of the Bumfhole brothers, the champions prove too much and Gilmore eventually gets the submission on Zimmy.

Angry Gilmore Wins at 13:54 via Submission

Grade: B

Sexual Aggression

Rather than celebrate their title defense, Sexual Aggression end up in a heated argument in the ring. Sexy is the instigator and he's clearly unhappy that Gilmore refused to help out his partner with a big of good old cheating. The argument continues as the duo make their way into the back and head into their locker room. The camera follows them, until they slam the locker room door. The shouting continues behind the closed door...

Grade: B

Jerry Eisen: I don't think those two are getting along so well...

Quote:

Marc DuBois vs Eric Eisen

SWF North American Championship Grand Prix Finale

The crowd gets behind the youngster early and stays behind him. The Canadian wrestles clean while Eisen tries every dirty trick in the book. DuBois puts on a wrestling clinic as he throws a bit of everything at Eisen. All Eisen can do is throw around cheap shots and low blows. Runaway Train gets involved again, but the referee isn't distracted enough, and he orders Train from ringside. Eisen's main advantage is gone, and his dad doesn't look pleased. DuBois hits a moonsault at one point for a huge pop. He gets the win after he manages a quick reverse on a nice attack by Eisen, managing to get the pin out of a bridging suplex.

Marc DuBois Wins at 15:05 via Pinfall

Grade: B-

Marc DuBois

Celebration

The young Canadian goes insane after he is handed the title. He drops to his knees, cradling the belt. Eisen glares from outside the ring and looks like he's going to go after DuBois, but his father stops him.

Then DuBois jumps up and scrambles outside the ring, jumping into the crowd to celebrate. The crowd gives him quite the reception. He hugs all three men at the announcers booth. He then races backstage as the camera tries to keep up. He finds Eric Tyler and Chris Caufield, both in street clothes, and celebrates with them.

Grade: A

Jerry Eisen: I just got hugged.

Phil Vibert: You enjoyed that too much, Jerry.

Quote:

Mass Effect vs Big & Rich

A long, out-and-out brawl where all four men battle hard. Both Diaz and James end up busted open. The battle is near-chaos at times and moves outside the ring frequently. Frehley and Money have some special viciousness for each other. James eventually when he goes into Raging Bull mode on Diaz and then hits a Big Cat Pounce. The way that Frehley and Money glare at each other makes it seem like they aren't done with each other yet.

Brandon James Wins at 15:32 via Pinfall

Grade: B

Quote:

The match is a war of attrition. The two men go to battle with what seems like genuine animosity. The man formerly known as Lobster Warrior looks absolutely done more than once, including after Remo hits The Destroyer hard. Morrisette kicks out and refuses to lose. The tenacity of Morrisette begins to wear on the bigger, younger man and the Canadian takes advantage. He surprises Remo when he unleashes the Lobster Trap and then get a quick pin. The crowd goes insane and Remo looks more than angry.

Chris Morrisette Wins at 19:13 via Pinfall

Grade B+

Jerry Eisen: I miss the crab guy.

Quote:

Vengeance vs Jack Bruce

SWF Supreme Cell Match for the World Heavyweight Champion

The two men enter the ring and the massive cell is lowered down over the ring, then secured. Referee Darren Smith is there simply to call the end to the match and doesn't even start in the ring. Vengeance goes after Bruce before the bell rings, and the two men go at each other. The bigger Vengeance gets the better of the exchanges, so Bruce begins to use a strategy of keep-away, making The Harbinger chase. It works for a bit, but Bruce's knee clearly isn't right and Vengeance works it over when he gets ahold of the champion. A nasty head butt from Vengeance's gleaming mask busts the Long Island Angel open. His long tresses of hair are soon coated in blood. But he keeps fighting. He bleeds more when Vengeance takes him outside the tosses him into the steel bars repeatedly. Bruce looks out of it and referee Darren Smith looks like he might stop it. The champion continues to fight, and when he regains his legs, he manages some offense and smashes Vengeance's face into the cell several times. Blood drips down from beneath the mask, and the challenger is bleeding as well. The war continues, and the mat is soon coated with blood. Emma Chase makes a surprise appearance at ringside, and tries to hand Vengeance his chain through the bars. Referee Smith grabs it instead and it becomes a three way tug-of-war between the ref, Chase, and Vengeance. Bruce takes advantage of the delay to regroup himself. He even plays to crowd a bit. When Vengeance finally gets back into the ring, sans the chain, Bruce plays down. He catches Vengeance unaware and takes control. He hits the New York Minute twice and looks like he has Vengeance down. But when the ref is about to call the match, Vengeance suddenly sits up and begins to no sell everything that the champion throws at him. He grabs Bruce, lifts him above his head, and throws him out of the ring into the steel wall. The cell shakes but holds. Bruce is in a heap and he looks done. But while on the mat, he finds the chain on the floor where Smith left it. He grabs it and slowly makes his way back into the ring. When Vengeance comes after him, Bruce trips him with the chain, then wraps it around The Harbinger's throat. As Vengeance had done to so many victims, Jack Bruce used the chain to choke the big man unconscious. The referee checks on Vengeance and calls the match!

Jack Bruce Wins at 33:54 via Referee Stoppage

Grade: B+

Jack Bruce

Celebration

Bruce hits his knees in a bloody mess as the fans go crazy and the cell is slowly raised. The officials manage to wake up Vengeance and help him from the ring. As Bruce is handed the SWF World Heavyweight championship, The Harbinger tries to go after the champ one more time but the officials intervene and get the still-wobbly big man to the back. Bruce takes his time playing to the crowd and celebrating with the fans. Christian Faith and Chris Morrisette show up at the top of the ramp to add to the applause.

Grade: A*
