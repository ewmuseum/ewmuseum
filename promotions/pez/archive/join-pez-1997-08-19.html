This is in no way your average e-federation, I think I could explain that in just one word. PARODY. Ya know, I have been fantasy wrestling for over 2 years now, a lot longer than a lot of you guys getting this mail has been. But probably the funnest time I had ever had was a parody wrestling federation that I joined for 3 months as the wrestler, The Oscar Meyer Weiner. Although the cards were all dark matches (summery form) it was a blast. Great angles, hilarious gimmicks, and the GREATEST dedication I have ever seen from the federation committee, made it the best federation I had ever been in. Now days fantasy wrestling is pissing me off. All these federations using simulators (Which I must confess I had used once) and real wrestlers. I like it back in the old days, where everything was original, there was no Stone Cold Steve Austin wanna-bes, and dedication from both wrestlers and committee members. Now days you can go out in the rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy newsgroup and find about 3 new feds every day. And the feds form with one president just wanting to make a splash in the e-wrestling world by trying to make a name for himself. Well, having 1 person seldom works. Feds fall daily, there is no dedication from the wrestlers, because either they don't care, or they are in 5 different federations. But this will be different, we have a committee that is highly dedicated to their work. And we are hoping you guys aren't a bunch of free loaders. Cause one thing is for sure, I LOVE to fire people! You could say I sure as hell don't have a big heart for freeloaders. First of all, we want the STUPIDIST and FUNNIEST gimmicks you could ever think of! Ok, now you may be sayin to yourslef, Yo Merc, I need some good ideas! Could ya be cool and gimmi a few exampes before I bust a cap though the back of your head? Ok, well, I say shut up. I will give ya a few examples, here are 3 of my personal favorites in order of how much I like them, do NOT use any of these… 1: The Trekker- A short Star Trek fanatic with thick black glasses. Every one of his role plays talks about a certain episode of Star Trek. 2: The Bull- A guy who thinks he was raised up like caddle. 3: Fire Marshal Bill- If you are a total hardcore fan of In Living Color like myself, you should know who he is. If ya don't, watch the show on cable station FX! Jim Carrey stars in it for all you ignorate queer bangs. From that you can start to think of your own. Another notable ones are The Warner Brothers tag team. Wakko and Yakoo Warner, managed by Dot. If you watch the cartoon Animaniacs, you know who they are. Also is my wrestler, The Oscar Meyer Weiner. Allright, so I guess you get the picture on the gimmicks.
