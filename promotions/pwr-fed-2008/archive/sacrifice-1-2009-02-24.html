Sacrifice — Card

Sacrifice #1

24 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

English gloss: the Sacrifice brand's own first show: a new X Division Championship tournament begins, the Tag Team Champions retain, and an Elimination Fatal-4-Way for a UWF Championship match closes with the champion, an authority figure and two top stars turning on each other.
