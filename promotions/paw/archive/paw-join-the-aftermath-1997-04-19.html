Join the Aftermath... Looking for Wrestlers

19 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
/\ /\ /\ / \ / \ / \ / \ / \ / \ ^^^^^^^ ^^^^^^ ^^^^^^ ^^^^^^^ ^^^^^^ ^^^^^^^ ##### ##### ###### the PAW ####### ####### ####### ###################################### ###################################### ####### Post-Apocalyptic ####### ##### Wrestling ##### ################################ ############################## ############################ Hello E-wrestlers, I'm Chronos376,and I'm the president of the new fed,Post-Apocalyptic Wrestling,or the PAW.You may remember me from my venture as president of the WAIWA.Though,on the WAIWA's anniversary of 1 year,it was closed down. I hope the PAW will be different.I hope this fed is to become as big as feds like EWA,PCW,UWF and IIWF.I don't expect you to join a new fed without a little info on it and me,so here goes. Post-Apocaylptic Wrestling consists of 2 weekly shows:Friday Faceoff and Tuesday Night Aftermath.These card will each consist of approximately 5-6 matches.Pay-Per-Views will happen every 6 weeks,not every month,cause I think a month is too short. We have 3 Singles Belts(World,United States,Televison)and 1 tag Team Belt(World).Another Title will be a KOTR type Title that's defended at PPV's once a year like the KOTR in a Tournament.This is still to be decided. One screen name can handle up to 2 wrestlers.This can be either 2 singles or a tag team.I think this will allow people not to have too many wrestlers that they cannot RP for them all.Unless under ceratin circumstances,Each handler will have only 2 wrestlers.If you want more than the 2,send me a letter,arguing your point on why you should. RP's are required to win matches.Say if you submit 2 Long RP's,but your opponent has submitted 3 short RP's,you would win,as long as the RP quality is good.So,if you submit 2 good Short RP's,and your opponent submits 2 Bad Long RP's,you will win also.It's all a matter of the following....... -Creativity:I don't want the same thing in an RP every week -Detail:The More Detail,the better.If your RP makes me feel like I'm there as the camera man,It's good.However,If It has no details and just reads.... "I'll kick Yer Ass!!!" ..........You will LOSE!!!!!!!! -Spelling/Grammar:If the wrestler speaks like....... "I dun't kare.I will Kic Yer Arse into oblivioinn!!!" ..........Again,you will LOSE!!!!!!!! RP's should be done at a minimum of 3 times a week.If you don't RP for one match,you will be issued a warning on wrestler dropping,and if you don't for a straight 2 matches,the wrestler who didn't RP will be dropped. Strats(Strategey)should be handed in too,as without them,no feuds would start.A Strat is like a Pearl Harbor Job,A Run-In,a Turn to Face/Heel,or a Feud starter.We can't always do Exactly what the wrestler wants,but We'll try.You are required for a Strat at least for every 4 shows at a minimum.So if someone dosen't send a Start for 4 shows,they may be dropped(Unless they send me a reason on why they don't send a strat,like if their a solid face or just wanna stay the same as they are). I've been in E-wrestling for over a year now.I have handled many famed wrestlers(Robbie Greer,Psychosis,Marc Stevens).I was a part-time write for the old AOL fed,OCW.I started my fed,the WAIWA while in the OCW.When my fed and his had a joint card,WAIWA won in a 10 match series,8 to 2.The OCW prez fired me for winning.I then joined the UWF,and won the Tag and Crusierweight Titles.I then joined the KWA,which changed to the NCWA,which changed tom the current ECWA.I'm an emergency writer for them,but my fed comes first.I joined my third fed,the DPW,until it shut down.Then I got an invite to the PCW and took it.About a month before I joined DPW,though,WAIWA was shut down due to lack of interest.No one RP'd,so I shut it down.Now,I'm just gonna drop any wrestlers who RP. I think a year's expierience in the E-wrestling is good enough expierience to start a fed,don't you?? :) Anyway,if you think you like what ya here,and wanna join the PAW,E-mail me and I'll send you an application.If I approve your wrestler,you're in.I hope I hear from many of you,and I hope you wanna join the Aftermath of.... THE PAW!!!!!!!!! PAW President, [detail withheld]
