Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

World Extreme Wrestling's earliest surviving document, a writers-wanted post of 24 June 1997, presents it as already running under its president and vice president and pitches it against the era's own arms race of “quality” roleplays: “You can write as many 10K 'Quality' RPs as you want and, if people don't CARE about your e-wrestler, why should you 'deserve' to win?” It called itself built on “Interest” above all, favoring “a cool angle rather than a cool flash” over the pressure to out-write an opponent.

A week later, on 2 July 1997, a community reply describes the same promotion as a revival under its same leadership, and predicts its future bluntly: “This is just another WEW that will most likely close ASAP.”

That prediction is the last word on it in its own voice. What survives after is a single passing mention, buried in an unrelated fan's own 20 October 1997 retrospective about several different characters across several different federations: one of them, sidelined at the time on another fed's injured list, is named there as having been “the last WEW World Champion.” No document naming a closure, a final champion crowned in the promotion's own words, or any further activity survives.
