Has Your e-Wrestler Fulfilled You? (community reply)

20 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm throwin' out my stuff, too. To the question, mostly I can answer yes. First off, there's Alex Reaver who I think has fufilled me most so far. He's probably the deepest character I have, the one I put my emotions into, and the one who earns his wins the most. 6-4 lifetime, recently won the GFWA World title in his first and only fed, SIX WINS in a row, for what it's worth. The babyface every fed needs to grow off of. He paid his dues, stablized his persona, THEN won a major title. Rags to riches the way it oughta' be. Nothing was more enjoyable for me than reading his World title win - which was just last night. Next up is Jason Knight, who I created based off of the Highlander TV show. 15-3 lifetime, won the MLWO World title a week ago. He's beaten a lot of big names - Steve Sampson, Alex Adams, Todd Newkirk, Cherokee Chief, Jay Cal, Bishop, and more. Next is J.P. Steele, who, this summer, _WAS_ e-wrestling for me, basically. He's gone from a shit fed champion to a bona fide top dude, IMO. The face with an attitude and a temper, generic, but effective. Held the PCW Columbia Valley title in August, also won the first Unified World title and was the last WEW World Champion. Definantly won more than he's lost, but currently on the EWA's DL. Wylde Dreams have been all laughs for me. 2-2-1 lifetime, GFWA bred and born. Began in April as a regular cocky heel and ex-face team, but went on hiatus before they debuted. Came back in July, retooled, with Joey Cadwall being, well, a complete goof, and Scotty B. Wylde becomming a little more tweener. Went on hiatus again, placed 156th in the RSPWF 200 after FIVE matches, and then came back last night to the GFWA. An odd team, but from what my peers have said, they seem to go over pretty well. :)
