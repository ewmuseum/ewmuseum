WEW is in Seek of Writers

24 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
___ _____________ ___ \ \ / _____ \ / / \ \ /\ / /| <____ \ \ /\ / / \ \/ \/ / | <____ \ \/ \/ / \___/\___/ |_______| \___/\___/ <WEW>----**WORLD EXTREME WRESTLING**----<WEW> World Extreme Wrestling, one of the most established leagues on the 'net needs YOUR help to bring it back to the top! As you all know, there's about two... hundred... THOUSAND other e-wrestling feds out there and they all need one thing: Writers. Well, this one, no matter how much of a "quality fed" (Whatever THAT means) it is, is no different from any of these other feds out there in the sense that writers are needed. Now, of course, I realize not everyone wants to be a writer. If that is indeed the case, you don't need to read further, because that's basically all this message is about. The WEW is different from most of the other feds out there in the sense that it runs on the most important factor there is in e-wrestling: Interest. You can write as many 10K "Quality" RPs as you want and, if people don't CARE about your e-wrestler, why should you "deserve" to win? That's the main focus of the WEW. We like to concentrate more on the fun side of e-wrestling instead of the stressful side where egos grow and you're pressed to "Out-RP" your opponent. It works a lot better when you challenge your creativity to come up with a cool angle rather than a cool flash. The WEW is already up and running. Things are going great and all, but we still have the big problem with the "lack of writers" thing. If you're willing to help us out and produce more of a quality e-wrestling federation where the people can enjoy the fun of particpation instead of cranking out RP after RP and giving themselves enormous ammounts of UN-NEEDED stress, e-mail fed head [detail withheld] about a writer's position and, hell, maybe even an app if you're interested. WEW President SKLOK A. ZOID ( [detail withheld] ) WEW Vice President Matt ( [detail withheld] )
