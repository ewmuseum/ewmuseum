World Cup of Wrestling — Retrospective

"A Night to Remember after-thoughts"

10 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, there it is. Whether you liked it or not, A Night to Remember is now assigned to history. Like I said in the message before the PPV, A Night to Remember was dreamt up in response to "dream match" postings on RSPWF. It was a way to make some of these matches actually happen, and a way to bring together all sorts of wrestlers from feds all over the net. It was not meant to be another IIeW, and I'd doubt anyone could do what Ronnie did with IIeW as successfully. Unlike that event, A Night to Remember was never going to be confined to the biggest names in the business. We wanted to take some big names (ala Chris Myers, Siberian Express), some semi-big names (The Ronin, Fairchild Foundation, Chris Hopper etc), and mix it with some up-and-comers from small feds (Shaolin, Chris Miller, Rune etc). So, did A Night to Remember work? Well, in all honesty, not as we planned. We found that having matches with wrestlers who had never met in the ring before led to little or no heat in some instances. Also, some handlers didn't RP, which made matters all the more difficult. You'll notice that the early matches in the show were quite short, and that's because we decided early on that if handlers didn't RP, we wouldn't write matches making them look good. Those who RPed well got the better matches, later in the card. How could we have improved it? Well, it's always a bit of a gamble doing this sort of thing. We could have gone for a few more slightly recognisable names, which may have led to more RPing, but then again it might not have. I'm not saying we regret holding A Night to Remember, though, because without this show the Main Event would probably have been confined to the "Imagine if...." status, and some other good matches might not have happened. Now, I'm going to go out on a limb here. A Night to Remember offered a group of wrestlers the chance to prove themselves and get recognised, on a wider basis than normal fed wrestling. Some made the most of that chance, and others didn't. The ones who did, I would recommend for any fed-head to sign. The ones who didn't....well, you get the picture. Anyway, this leaves one question - will there be another event like A Night to Remember in the World Cup of Wrestling? The answer is probably not. We tried it, and partially succeeded, but it has become clear that the real part that the WCoW has to play in e-wrestling is holding events like the I-Crown and Zielinski Cup. On that note, keep an eye out for the next WCoW event, the I-Crown, which is already creating a lot of interest. The heat is already there, the RPing is top notch, and the event promises to be truly spectacular. June 1 is the regional finals.....don't miss it! Those are my thoughts.....thanks for reading. Fletch, World Cup of Wrestling co-organiser.
