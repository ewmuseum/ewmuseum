Wug — I-Crown competitor profile

13 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wrestler's Name: "Bullywug" Glenn Hudson Height: 5'11" Weight: 229lb Orientation: Rogue-ish face Origin country: Australia, hailing from Melbourne Appearance: Caucasian.. lithe build.. brown hair (spiked, but brushed-back).. blue eyes that kinda twinkle with mischief.. has a few days' worth of stubble as well as a devilish grin on his face, and a few old scars on his forehead. Ring Attire: While wrestling - pair of old jeans with holes in the knees, white t-shirt with 'I THINK, THEREFORE, I-CROWN' written on it in bold lettering. Black boots and elbow-pads. To-ring - long, grey trenchcoat, worn open Theme Music: "Young Bodies Heal Quickly" by Regurgitator Five favourite moves: 1- The Springboard, Wug's previous finisher. A rapid moonsault executed by running up to the top rope. 2- Glennzuigiri. An enzuigiri kick (-: 3- Huracanrana. 4- Any kind of dive out of the ring 5- Series of rapid punches Finishing move: "No Hard Feelings" - Wug leaps off the top-rope, grabbing the opponent in a front-facelock while coming down. He uses the momentum to spin around the opponent, drilling them into the canvas with a DDT. Used frequently as a counter out of nowhere. Primary Attributes: (Choose three of the following or make up your own): Brawling Aerial Endurance Profile: (wrestler's personal and wrestling background) Wug likes to project himself as a smart-arsey punk, someone who doesn' t have much to back his talk up with. This doesn't always work as much now, since his reputation has grown, and people are a little more cautious when dealing with him. This doesn't stop Wug from running his mouth, as he loves to have fun at other people's expense, as long as he feels they deserve it. Deep down, Hudson really isn't such a bad fellow. But he is realistic in accepting that if he isn't ruthless in his profession, he'll get walked over. He often takes advantages of situations where it's a "Them or Me" decision, giving and asking no quarter from the opponent. When he feels that the situation warrants it, the ends will justify the means. Those who aren't on the receiving end of Wug's mind-games often find that the Aussie is a pretty cool guy. Wug appreciates those who appreciate him. He is very loyal to his friends, but doesn't get distressed if the crowd show a negative reaction to him. Of course, he'd prefer to be liked, but being liked won't win matches. He is confident in himself and his abilities, and posses a tremendous drive and will to fight. He has only submitted once in his career, in his debut. That moment has haunted him ever since, and he has made a private promise to himself never to give up a fight again. STRENGTHS: 1). Speed and Agility. Wug is very fast on his feet, and can slip out of an opponent's grasp in a flash. He suffered early in his career by under-utilising this talent, but has since been developing his aerial skill and using them frequently. 2). Psychological Warfare. Wug loves to rattle his opponents and throw their concentration. His trash-talking has a range of effects, from causing the opponent to lose their temper, to getting them to under-estimate him as a genuine competitor. From experience, Hudson has also taught himself not to take any opponent lightly. He likes to think of himself as one of the smarter wrestlers around, and is a solid tactician. 3). Fighting Spirit. Hudson puts 150% into every match. He knows how damaging a loss can be. He is extremely stubborn when it comes to not staying down, and has only ever submitted once in a match. Hudson will gladly take all kinds of punishment if there's a chance he can still come-back and get the win. For example, Glenn came back and won a brutal "Stretcher Counts Anywhere" match where his opponent hit him with a car. His mental reserves are very deep, and tends to shine in matches where he is really put to the test. WEAKNESSES: 1). Not very powerful. In a test of strength, Wug can be easily out-muscled. 2). Not well schooled in classical mat-technique. Wug is quite happy to jam a thumb in someone's eye to break a hold. 3). Finds it hard to let go of a feud. Wug's first real feud (with "Substance" Chad O'Donnell) lasted 15 months and spanned three wrestling federations. He may find that other rivalries may be prolonged also. Wug has been wrestling in the US for just ovrt 2 years. Although he has worked for many organisations, his most lengthy campaigns have been in the XWC, GIWF, LRPW/ICBINR, AEWA and LWC. He was recently voted the #14 Wrestler in the 1997 RSPWF Top-200 Poll. Hudson currently is returning to action after a neck injury. Although he has allowed himself to heal, Wug is going through a period of professional uncertainty, and feels that a change of setting such as a tournament would encourage the professional spirit to reappear. Hence his interest in the I-Crown (-: Previous/Current Titles: Former LWC Six Gun Champ (1 and a bit months) Former AEWA Television Champ (5 months) 2 x Former/Defunct LRPW/ICBINR Cruiserweight Champ (6 months, neg) Former XWC World Champ (7 months) Defunct UHWF United States Champ (1 month) Former IWWL Television Champ (1 week) Has also won several Awards in the XWC and GIWF (including XWC Best Wrestler, and back-to-back GIWF Match-of-the-Year in '96 and '97) [Important] What leagues your wrestler is currently in: Wug is officially in the GIWF, but the fed has been on shaky ground for quite some time. Wug has recently been in LWC, and is pretty much teetering on returning to active competition. Because of these factors, Wug is declaring himself an independant in this tournament. ================================================================= If you need any more info, or I've left something out, please let me know.
