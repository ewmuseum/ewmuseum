World Cup of Wrestling — Preview

"Control Center"

3 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The black screen crackles for a moment, before the keyboard intro to "The Final Countdown" by Europe begins playing, and the screen comes alive with the image of two men sitting in a studio. The man on the left is middle-aged, with a visible receding hairline and moustache. He is wearing a simple suit. The man on the right is younger, with curly sandy-brown hair and good-looking features. This younger of the two is wearing a white polo shirt, and looks very casual next to his suited colleague.] [The camera starts out with a wide shot of the studio, and the lights are dimmed. But the lights brighten and the camera zooms in on the desk, as the music dies down. On a large TV screen behind the two men, the words..... . __ ______ __ __ \ \ / / ___| \ \ / / \ \ /\ / / | __\ \ /\ / / \ \/ \/ /| | / _ \ \/ \/ / \ /\ / | |__| (_) \ /\ / \/ \/ \____\___/ \/ \/ __________________________ ____| [name withheld] |____ \ \ & / / \ ___\ FLETCH /___ / \ _____ _____ / ___________\/___________ | | | PRESENT THE | | WORLD CUP OF WRESTLING | |______________________________| < http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Arena/1165/WCoWmain.html > . _________ __ .__ \_ ___ \ ____ _____/ |________ ____ | | / \ \/ / _ \ / \ __\_ __ \/ _ \| | \ \___( <_> ) | \ | | | \( <_> ) |__ \______ /\____/|___| /__| |__| \____/|____/ \/ \/ _________ __ \_ ___ \ ____ _____/ |_ ___________ / \ \/_/ __ \ / \ __\/ __ \_ __ \ \ \___\ ___/| | \ | \ ___/| | \/ \______ /\___ >___| /__| \___ >__| \/ \/ \/ \/ appear. The older of the two men starts speaking.] ??: Hello wrestling fans! You’re tuned to the World Cup of Wrestling Control Center, where we’re counting down to May 9, when Chicago Illinois will have A Night to Remember. I’m Bryan Nucci, former NECW broadcaster, and I’m joined by my commentary colleague and former professional wrestler "Flaming" Lance Hart. LH: Hi everyone! Well Bryan, like you said, the first World Cup of Wrestling event - A Night to Remember - is little more than a week away. I know I speak for a lot of fans when I say I can’t wait for the night to arrive. BN: Folks, if you haven’t already ordered the biggest wrestling event of the year, you’ve still got time. Ring your cable operator, and get ready for a night of action like you’ve never seen before. Speaking of which, I hear the ticket sales topped the 20,000 mark yesterday, and we’re still a week out from the event! LH: Doesn’t surprise me Bryan. BN: OK folks, let’s talk about a controversy which is gripping the World Cup of Wrestling right now, and could have an impact on A Night to Remember. I’m talking about the sudden transformation of Mark Slade. When Slade was signed to wrestle John Smith a few months ago, he was an up-and-comer in the NECW, looking to make the big step up with a win over the madman Smith. But since then we’ve seen this talented young man transform into a depressed, drunken loner who disappeared off the face of the earth for a matter of weeks. The latest news is that the World Cup of Wrestling organisers aren’t willing to take the gamble of Slade turning up to A Night to Remember, and so they’ve cancelled his match against Smith. LH: That’s a real pity, but I can’t say I blame Fletch and Karol. Slade is a mighty talented wrestler, but it seems he’s throwing all that away because of his inner demons. From what I’ve heard Smith wasn’t too disappointed about the match being canned either, because he recently retired from the ring, and was only appearing on May 9 because of contractual agreements. BN: That’s right. Smith is also appearing in the I-Crown because he’s hooked into a watertight WCoW contract, but he won’t have to wrestle at A Night to Remember. Of course, the big match at that event that everyone is talking about is the one dubbed "Total Chaos". 12 current or former wrestlers from the Northeastern Federation of Wrestling do battle in one of the most exciting, yet disturbing, matches of the year. LH: I tell you what - it makes me glad I retired when I see matches like this one. Weapons, barbwire, a ladder, bloodthirsty wrestlers, and three rings on a revolving platform! Now that’s gotta be bad for your health. BN: He he.....you’re not wrong there. Let’s look at some of the wrestlers who’ll be taking part in that match. The first team is Future Warrior, Aries God of War, Old Brown Heineken and Chris Douglas. LH: Some strange names in that lot. The only ones I’ve seen wrestle are Douglas and Future Warrior. Douglas is a talented wrestler, but he’s got an ego the size of Abdullah the Butcher’s gut. And no doubt he’ll have his humongous German bodyguard Titanicus with him. BN: Interesting that Douglas is hated with a passion by the fans, but his teammate Old Brown Heineken is a fan favourite. LH: The big Dutchman is sure to make an impression in this match, but yes you’re right Bryan, a strange mix. As for Future Warrior, we’ve both seen him in action during a very short stint in the NECW. He never really got the chance to show his skills, because he was gone before he could make an impression, but you’ve got to rate him a chance in this match. BN: And Aries God of War is a man neither of us know too much about, but he’s talked about with respect in NEFW circles, so keep an eye out for him. The next team is collectively known as Riders on the Storm, and the fact that they’re the only team in this match that is a fulltime stable must go in their favour. LH: Sure does, but just the fact that they’ve got "Diamondback" Chris Myers on their team is an even bigger plus. Myers surprised everyone by beating a strong field to take out the inaugural Deschenes Cup, and now he’s rated as one of the top men in the sport. BN: His teammates are of course "Airborne" Mark Fulcrum and the Wild Cards, Ace and Deck. If the Wildcards end up being randomly picked in the same ring together, they’ll be a force to be reckoned with, but otherwise it’s yet to be seen how they can compete as singles wrestlers. And Fulcrum is a wrestler who could turn a few heads in this match. He’s been overshadowed by Chris Myers in this team, but I’ve got a feeling that this could be his chance to step out of the "Diamondback"s shadow and make a name for himself. LH: And what about the third team? Four guys I must admit I’ve never heard of, but again they’re rated highly by NEFW fans. The big question remains ‘can these guys work together’? BN: Indeed, can they? Mondo Somatic, Rude Dude, Dr Meloncholy and Dublin Daredevil have a past riddled with alliances, feuds and bitter battles against one another, and now they must come together and put all of their differences aside. LH: If they can, they’ll go close to winning this match, but if they can’t, I’d say they’ve got no chance. BN: OK, moving right along, and let’s now talk about a late replacement in one of the matches on A Night to Remember…..The Ronin. They have stepped up to the plate to "pinch-hit" shall we say for the Tokyo Blades, who were forced to pull out through injury. What do you know about The Ronin Lance? LH: I know that they’re a damn fine team, and that the fans are going to get a real treat in getting to watch them in action against the Siberian Express. Man, what a match-up! BN: The big Siberians are rated as probably the hottest tagteam in the sport today, and looking to knock them off that perch will be the Kabuki Kid and "The Samurai" Kensei Tokugawa, two of the Orient’s finest. LH: As a former tagteam wrestler myself, with the Canadian Rockers, I can tell you from watching the Kid and Tokugawa that they have molded into a great duo. If I had to compare them to anyone, it’d be Arn Anderson and Chris Benoit on the few occasions that they teamed up while in the Four Horsemen. Kabuki Kid is the Benoit of the team - fast as a cheetah and as cunning as a fox. Tokugawa reminds me of Anderson in that he’s slightly bigger in build, but is the consumate thinking-man’s wrestler. He’ll find your weak spot and manipulate it until you’re crying uncle within no time. BN: Everyone knows how powerful and innovative The Siberian Express are, so there’s no doubt we’re going to see a great match. Injuries are something that can’t be foreseen, and unfortunately they’ve struck again with one of the night’s biggest names, "Demon" Damien Lestat. Lestat has had to pull out with back trouble, and his match with "Ice Storm" Chris Winters has been cancelled. In its place will be a match which I think the fans will really enjoy Lance. LH: For sure. Two of the finest lucha libre wrestlers south of the border right now will duke it out for a spot in the upcoming I-Crown tournament. Sanctioned by the CLLL organisation as well. Should be quite a match. BN: Two names which are probably unfamiliar to a lot of people - La Pulga Gigante and El Hijo del Satanico - but two wrestlers which are getting their big chance to show the greater wrestling public what their brand of the sport is all about. I’m a big fan of Mexican wrestling, so I can’t wait for that one. Well, that’s about all we’ve got time for tonight folks. Make sure you join us again in a couple of days when we bring you more news on the opening night of the World Cup of Wrestling - A Night to Remember. And once again, if you haven’t already ordered it on pay-per-view, it’s not too late…..call your cable operator for details. Thanks for watching folks. For Lance Hart, I’m Bryan Nucci saying so long and see you next time!! [Screen fades to black as the intro of Final Countdown starts up again, before fading out.] © Fletch/[name withheld] production 1998.
