Kashan Akuma — I-Crown competitor profile

13 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wrestler's Name: Kashan Akuma Height: 5'11" Weight: 195 lbs. Orientation (face, heel, tweener): heel Manager/Valet (Optional): Foxxy Origin country: Tokyo, Japan Appearance: He has short dark hair with a brown tint in streaks. He has brown eyes also. He is Japanese and has a medium build. He is only 22 so he looks almost still like a teenager. On his arms are tatoos. His wrestling attire is a pair of long black tights. On the black tights, there is a blue flame on each leg with a silver outline. Also on the side, it says "daredevil" in Japanese characters. Along with the black tights, he wears black kneepads and black boots. He wears a black band around each bicep. On his way to the ring, he wears a black robe with blue and silver. The style is very similar to his tights. There is a huge blue flame with silver outlining on it. On the back is his name in Japanese charactes. Theme Music: His theme music is an original composition. The song consists of taiko drums and classic Japanese strings. Added in is some guitar to give it an overall rock feel. Five favourite moves: 1. Moonsault: He like to use every variety of the moonsault. He will use an asai moonsault, a springboard moonsault, or a corkscrew moonsault. Any type of moonsault you can think of, he can do. He has been known to invent his own type of moonsaults and also execute moonsaults from the most unpredictable positions. 2. Spinning Wheel Kick: This is his favorite kick to use. This is one of his most unpredictable moves. You never know when it is coming. He can hit you straight on, whip you into the ropes first, or even go off the top. 3. Hurricanrana: He loves to use this moves. He is one of the best executers of this move in the world. He will use it off the top or off the mats. Sometimes he will role through for the pin but sometimes he will just launch his opponent hard over him. 4. Dragon Suplex: This is his favorite suplex to use. He is one of the suplex masters in the world. The dragon suplex may be his favorite but he has an incredible array of suplexes that he uses. Some include, german suplex, exploder, belly-to-belly, double underhook, reverse, facefront, northern lights, and many more. 5. Powerbomb: Kashan loves to see his opponent's head bounce hard off the canvas. He uses a wide array of powerbombs. They vary from cross arm powerbombs, jacknife powerbombs, tiger bombs, and liger bombs. This is one of the few power moves he uses. Finishing move: Finisher #1: Cyclone Fury Driver - This is one of the most deadly moves in the world of wrestling. The Cyclone Fury Driver is a spinning tombstone piledriver. This move has been known to end careers. As the victim is spun upside down he is disoriented. When his head is driven into the ground it creates a chain reaction that severely harms them. Finisher #2: Tiger Driver '91 - This has long been recognized as the most dangerous move in all of pro wrestling. He has perfected it and brought it to the next level. This version of a double underhook powerbomb is just devastating. Finisher #3: AkumaPlex - The AkumaPlex is an amazing version of the Uranage suplex made famous by Hiroshi Hase. He has added his own twist to it though. Instead of simply slamming his opponent down, he actually jumps in the air while holding his opponent and levels them. This move nearly knocks a man out with the incredible pain to the spine. Primary Attributes: (Choose three of the following or make up your own): Aerial: He is one of the best aerialists in the world. Few can take to the skies like he can. His main attack is actually aerial. He is never afraid to take it to the air. He will use everything from a moonsault to a frog splash. Not many people are able to counter his superior aerial ability. Technical: Kashan is a master technician. That is one of his trademarks. He can beat a man in almost anyway. Due to his technical excellence he will never make a mistake while on the mat. He knows exactly how to beat his opponent and when. He is an amazing counter wrestler and is great at submission holds. Endurance: This is possibly his most important attribute. Because he is so quick and active with his aerials he has to have lots of endurance to survive in a match. Without endurance he would be much too fatigued and worn down. He has gained so much stamina that he could go on and on for hours. Profile: (wrestler's personal and wrestling background) Kashan Akuma is one of the greatest rising stars in the world of wrestling. He has already made himself a household name in Japan and all around the world. He is only 22 but he has lost of experience. He can make them laugh, cry, cheer, or boo. He is cocky, arrogarnt, and confident of his abilities. He bows down to no one and does whatever he wants to. He has also been known as a ladies' man but does not choose to brag about it. At times he is cocky but at other times he is very concentrated and serious. Akuma has wrestled all over the world. He has wrestled in Europe, Mexico, the US, and Canada. He has garnered a large following of "Akumamaniacs" aroun the world. On his journeys around the world, he has captured every title he has ever contended for. He has never admitted to losing a single match even though that is a possibility. Akuma has had a very interesting personal history. He is the son of the late great pururesu legend, Haohmaru "Ikon" Akuma. Haohmaru Akuma is probably the greatest wrestler in the history of Japan. No other wrestler in history has been more popular. He was a champion time after time. Many see him as the greatest champion of the sport. Unfortunately, during the peak of his career, he died tragically in the ring. This affected a young Kashan very severely. When he was of age to wrestle, he vowed never to do what his father did. He would never be a "good guy". He would never trust anyone. He would do things his way and promise never to fail the way his father did. Sickened with the low class federations he was dominating he decided to move onto bigger things. That is when he signed a huge undisclosed contract with Pro Japan Wrestling out of Tokyo. Pro Japan Wrestling became one of the only juniorweight federations, in the vein of Michinoku Pro. He dominated all his opponents but eventually he couldn't get one thorn out of his side. One Carlos Liger Jr. His bitter arch enemy. Soon he returned to North America and signed with the Canadian Internet Wrestling Federation. His draw there was his friend, Shaolin. The gaijin had wrestled in Japan and struck a friendship with Akuma. Akuma has yet to wrestle a single match there but has made his presence known. Kashan Akuma has been known for many things. For his treachery. For his ability. For his cockiness. He has strived to be the best and will never give up until he can right his father's wrongs. Among his famous sayings is his, "Friends, countrymen, lend me your ears!!", from the great story Julius Caesar. Right now, Akumamania '98 is sweeping the world.
