Wuzho — I-Crown competitor profile

13 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wrestler's Name: Wuzho Height: 6'5'' Weight: 257lbs. Orientation (face, heel, tweener): Face Manager/Valet (Optional): nil Origin country: Romania Appearance: A tall, physically well-built individual who wrestles in ragged, tattered, holed, black and grey tights. He also wears a black baggy wool sweater as he comes into the ring, and a long tired old leather coat. His hair is long, brown, and somewhat curly. He paints his stoic face pitch black with two very thin red crosses which go over his eyes, extending down to the middle of his cheeks. He shows much expression, especially his rage and anger, but prefers to remain mostly impassive, which suits him fine because it is extremely contrastive to his intensity. Theme Music: "Serial Thrilla" by The Prodigy Five favourite moves: PALM BLOW: Avoiding the illegal punch-throwing, Wuzho uses this open-hand technique with ease and speed, being just as effective as punches with a little extra nasty "oomph". VERTICAL SUPLEX: The classic is definitely one of Wuzho's favourite moves. He often enjoys keeping his opponent in the air leaving the blood rush into his head for maximum fatigue and impact. SIDEWALK SLAM: Definitely a patented Wuzho move. Always thrusts down a little harder than most guys, with more intensity, while spiking an elbow in his opponent's ribs as he crashes down. Another Wuzho alteration is when he does three consecutive slams, one right after the other, quickly rising up to his feet and just as quickly slamming his opponent back down three times in a row. DDT: A wrestling commentator once said "nobody gets a DDT faster on someone than Wuzho". Mostly used to get himself out of tight spots, Wuzho has been known to drive DDTs on just about anything and anyone, with a nastiness which is all of his won. FLYING ELBOWSMASH: Wuzho does not go airborne much, aside from dropkicks, jumping lariats and such, but this is one he has mastered and enjoys using a great deal. Finishing move: Quell The Fire – quite simply put, a jack-knife power bomb with which Wuzho executes with all the strength he can muster up. He also sometimes lets himself drop down as well to add that momentum to the impact. Wuzho has also been known to drop people he really hates a little closer to the neck area than usual. ///OR\\\ The Knife – Wuzho places his opponent in a full nelson position; then, he usually hooks a leg with his own leg, forcing the opponent to trip and crash directly on his face, holding the lock tightly in the back of the opponent's neck for extra damage. Primary Attributes: (Choose three of the following or make up your own): Endurance Wuzho's physical robustness and never-say-die attitude have made him almost legendary in the many organisations he has been a part of and feared by most of his opponents. Just when the world thinks that he's out of commission, he pulls out a small tidbit of energy from god-knows-where which can make the difference. Brawling Having grown up in a rugged environment, Wuzho learned to brawl at a very young age. He can go toe-to-toe with roundhouses and fisticuffs with the best and sometimes loses all control when out beating the crap out of people he hates. Technical Through his time in wrestling rings, he has acquired a certain level of technical skill to compliment his harsh brawling style. Suplexes, slams, and off-the-ropes moves mostly, but has also acquired a few basic submission moves like armbars and toe holds. Profile: (wrestler's personal and wrestling background) Wuzho's real name has never been revealed to anyone, for the word "wuzho" means "pure" or "untainted" in the Rom (i.e. gypsy) language. However, his background is rather well known. He was born in Romania, part of a kumpania (wandering band) of gypsies. He spent most of his life with his family growing up on the road, without a stable home, across the great lands and forests of Romania. Being a gypsy has never been easy, for much racism and segregation against his kind have always marked their travels and tales. So his kumpania felt that it would be best for them to leave Europe and settle in America, where they seemingly could exist much more in peace. They were wrong. They found the poorer areas of the United States much more difficult to deal with and people just as intolerant as they were in Romania. This caused the unfortunate event of the kumpania to break up and for each to go their separate ways. Having vowed his honour to his troupe, Wuzho felt disheartened and destroyed. He left on his own, alone, taking what was left of his heart with him to go onto other things. The kumpania separated, he felt that all that was left for him to do was to move onto other things. He spent many years after that toughening up to the rigorous life America had to offer him. He was subject to hate and dishonour, something to which he has never succumbed to. But he quickly found out how one is to survive in this type of wilderness: one has to fend for himself. To earn money to eat, he offered his services to anyone who needed to "get even" with someone else, honing his fighting skills and toning his body up in the process and gaining a mean streak which was only fuelled by the frustrations he had accumulated ever since arriving in America. That's when he got the idea to enter professional wrestling. By becoming familiar with it and by imitating moves he had seen on television, Wuzho figured that he would offer his services to anyone who needed him. He quickly made contacts with some of the best in the world and trained very hard to become what he is today. Wuzho joined a few federations like WEW which folded some time after he joined and the small, up-and-coming RFW where he won his first honours and became World Champion. Unfortunately, the RFW closed down and Wuzho could not use it as his way to make it to the top. His first big breaks came when he joined the WWO and the GEWA. Wuzho spent only a short time in the GEWA, but long enough to develop a history with mentor/enemy Robert d'Artois. The WWO was the place which Wuzho catapulted himself into the limelight and felt as though he was at home, where he became one of the longest-running Television Champions of the federation's history. Also slowly becoming active in the IWF/WOW and being invited to join the World Cup of Wrestling's promotions, Wuzho began to feel as though his accomplishments for his hard work were finally taking shape.
