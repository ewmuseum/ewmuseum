Countdown to the I-Crown, Part Three

28 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We’re back with Bryan and Larry in the studio.] BN: You’re back with the Countdown to the I-Crown ’98, where we’re profiling the eight men who later on tonight will battle for the coveted I-Crown title. Two of the eight have been virtually silent in recent weeks, obviously taking the surprise approach, and keeping their tactics to themselves. However, we do have footage of them winning their I-Crown pool matches. First, here is..Fabrice Duvalle.... [The screen goes blank as we cut to the video, and then snaps back into life, as the following words appear at the bottom:] VIDEO FOOTAGE: I-CROWN NIGHT ONE [JUNE 13 1998] ============================================== [Instead of covering, Duvalle climbs to the top rope, and stands on the top turnbuckle facing out of the ring.] LVK: Lightning Strike coming up!! [He leaps back, flipping over in a moonsault. However, instead of landing across d’Artois, he lands feetfirst on him in a vicious stomp. Duvalle then falls to the mat, hooking the leg as the ref makes the count. His hand falls once, and the crowd chant "ONE".....] BF + LVK: ONE! [His hand falls twice, and the crowd chant "TWO"....] BF + LVK: TWO! [The hand thumps into the mat for a third time, a fraction before d’Artois lifts his shoulder, and the fans scream "THREEEEEE", and then POP!] BF + LVK: THREEE!! Fabrice Duvalle! He wins it!! SW: No, no, no!! He cheated, he’s on steroids, it’s not fair!! BF: Fabrice Duvalle, the relative unknown from the WAR! league, has pulled off a minor upset here tonight by pinning WWO World Champion Robert d’Artois to become the I-Crown Mediterranean Champion!! [d’Artois is only just stirring, as the shiny Mediterranean belt is handed to Fabrice Duvalle. Duvalle thrusts it into the air to the cheers of the fans, as the French flag drops from the rafters and the French national anthem starts playing.] <<<<video footage ends>>>> BN: Duvalle would be rated as one of the underdogs in this tournament, but as his country’s soccer team proved, underdogs CAN win. Another underdog, simply because of his size, is Mexican La Pulga Gigante. Here is how he got to tonight’s event....... [The screen goes blank as we cut to the video, and then snaps back into life, as the following words appear at the bottom:] VIDEO FOOTAGE: I-CROWN NIGHT TWO [JUNE 25 1998] ============================================== [Scorpio swings Estrago into the powerbomb, as Pulga leaps off and flies through the air with La Gota De La Pulga, his patented somersault senton bodyblock, landing on the back of Estrago as he is in mid-air, pushing him down on the top of Scorpio!] BF: "OOOOOOOOOHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!" LVK: "FREAKING UNBELIEVABLE!!! DID YOU SEE THAT?" BF: "Pulga just flipped off the top rope and landed right on top of Estrago as he was being powerbombed!! All three men are down on the mat, and Estrago, the man who hit the hardest, isn't moving at all!!" SW: "What is going on here? This isn't wrestling anymore!" [All three men are backdown on the mat, with Estrago and Scorpio not moving anymore.] LVK: "That's crazy! I can't believe La Pulga pulled that off!! That guy has got to be as mad as a meataxe!!" BF: "Get the medics out here!! We could have some seriously injured wrestlers here!" [La Pulga slowly gets up on his hands and knees and crawls towards his opponents, who are both laying on their back next to each other. La Pulga falls on their bodies as the ref makes the count.] LVK: "ONE!" BF: "TWO!" LVK + BF: "THREEEEEE!!!!!!" SW: "YES! La Pulga Gigante wins the Americas Pool match! I knew it!!" <<<<video footage ends>>>> BN: Not the most popular win, but convincing nonetheless. Another who came out of the I-Crown Regional Finals Night Two with his reputation intact was Welshman Marty Warnett. He overcame the plans of the devious Smith brothers, with the help of fellow competitor Doug Milne. Take a look.... [The screen goes blank as we cut to the video, and then snaps back into life, as the following words appear at the bottom:] VIDEO FOOTAGE: I-CROWN NIGHT TWO [JUNE 25 1998] ============================================== LVK: Hey! Jim Smith just grabbed the ref and broke up the count. The ref is arguing with Jim Smith, and Doug Milne comes up and clobbers Jim with that cane! BF: Warnett now, drags Smith to the center of the ring! He's going for it once again, The End of The Party figure four! And he's got it! Smith is tapping out! Marty Warnett has survived three of the best wrestlers the UK has, and he will represent the UK on the last night of the I-Crown tournament! SW: Doug Milne has a huge smile on his face as he leaves ringside. What is he so happy about? The poor Smith brothers are left in a heap in front of this Dallas crowd. That's no way for true English Men to be treated! LVK: The "poor" Smith brothers got exactly what they deserved. John Smith doesn't deserve to represent the fine people of the UK. Marty Warnett moves on to the next round! [Warnett's music starts playing, as the ref hands him the UK pool belt. Warnett hoists it high, bringing a pop from the crowd, and the ref raises his arm in victory.] The Mouth: Your winner........and 1998 I-Crown United Kingdom champion.......the PARTY MANIAC.......MARTY WARNETT!!!! <<<<video footage ends>>>> BN: Earlier in the week we caught up with the former IIWF superstar.... [cut to tape] ______________________________________________________________ _ _/ __| I-CROWN | | | (__ THE Marty Warnett (UK champ) | |___\___| FINALS______________________________________________| [ SCENE : A crowded pine clad room. At the far end resides a large table, containing several clustered microphones, glasses of water and several chairs. The room is packed with journalists, all eager for an exclusive report. The noise level reaches to a crescendo, then falls, as several nondescript PR players enter and sit, followed by a pensive Marty Warnett. Marty has foresaken his trademark jeans and T-shirt for a three piece suit, official "IIWF Forever" tie and thin gold rimmed glasses. His hair is tied back. He sits down, and takes a sip of water. ] MW : Greetings, one and all. I have a statement to read, then it'll be open floor for questioning, I naturally want to keep this as brief as possible. Firstly, thank you for turning up. This is something I should probably have said at least, possibly a year ago, something that's been weighing on my mind for a long time. A little over two years ago, I made my wrestling debut in the IIWF, the dubba-eye. I owe a huge debt of gratitude to the fed owner and erstwhile President Daniel Spreadbury ... he took a chance on me, took me into the IIWF training camp, offered me a six month initial contract, and basically encouraged me to get out there and do my stuff. To me, he'll always be Prez Man Dan ... To cut to the chase, over the last two years, I've seen a lot of things in this industry that I plain don't like ... braggers, liars, cheats. Half assed promoters who start their little feds, promote their relatives and favourites ... running feds for their own self gratitude. Promoting "hardcore" bouts that have no meaning or relevance that denigrates the sport through their often homophobic, racist or plain sexually immature view of life. Half the wrestlers out there, too, lacking in imagination, and dammit, just the skill to entertain people. Wrestlers cometing in six, seven feds at the same time, having to pull out of bouts because of prior commitments, showing loyalty only to themselves. That all didn't matter to me, everybody out there, except possibly the EMWC, knew the dubba-eye was the place to be. Where if you tried to bs the staff, you'd be on the way out. The fed where many talented people such as Sabin "Multiple World Champion" Figaro had their workrate found out. Where you got on through your charisma, and your talent. In short, wrestling heaven. And, just like any paradise, the snakes were out there, and slowly the fed turned. Too many athletes in the back would publicly be supportive, whilst bitching in private. People whose overwhelming priority wasn't wrestling, or the dubba-eye ... but their ego, and their demand for title shots ... their demand for title before co-operating in any storylines, and, yes, Chris Quigley, this is a shoot on you. The IIWF still retains itself above the common herd of federations out there. No question of a doubt. Due to the change in attitudes brought in by many "name" wrestlers ... the continual loaded barbs from other federations who hide behind "well, it was our 'heel' commentator" whilst condoning and supporting those comments by not cutting them ... the dubba-eye is closing. To cut to the chase ... this isn't what I got into this sport for. And I don't like it one bit. Therefore, rather than sell myself out morally and psychologically ... the I Crown and IIWF Forever will be my last ever activities in the world of wrestling. [ A sharp intake of breath occurs as the scribes make brief notes. ] MW : Well, I guess we'd better get the questions out of the way ... first batter up. [ Several grizzled newshounds immediately shoot their hands up in the air. Marty naturally picks the young blonde reporterette. ] NH : Linda Michaels, BLOW. Marty, why now? MW : What better time? Do I drag on, fed up with the industry just to make money? I have other irons in the fire, so to speak, so I'm not going to continue contradicting my beliefs anymore. NH : Scott Simpson, INLS. Can you elaborate on the egos? MW : Yeah, sure. The greatest ego I've ever seen, and the one person I really don't like, and this has to be so, because usually I get on with other grapplers pretty well, is Chris Quigley. Not only is the guy quite content to completely rip off characters in other federations to the point where you'd have be deaf, dumb and blind not to notice, but he's one of the most uncharismatic performers out there, who relies upon "being a name". That's what I mean about people spoiling this whole thing. NH : Osvaldo Esteban, Argentinian Times. How do you feel the fellow competitors will feel about this annoucement, and do you think it will stop your chances of winning? MW : Don't care, and no, I'd hope not. I'm a bit pressed for time, now. One more question ... you. [ A swarthy man dressed in military fatigues, smoking a cigar stands up. ] NH : Esteban Lopez, Cuban News. Why must your capitalist wrestlers in the II-<spit>-WWF always oppress the downtrodden Nicaraguan announcers by preventing these honest proletariat from doing their job for meagre ages by detroying their hard fough for equipment? MW : [ Puts hand over eyes ] Sheesh ... [cut back to studio] LH: Whoa! Warnett’s retiring after the I-Crown and the last IIWF pay-per-view? What an announcement! BN: You can bet that Warnett will be looking to turn up the tempo accordingly. He’ll want to go out with a definite bang. The Party Maniac is aptly named, and he has been criticised in the past for not taking this sport seriously enough, but what side of Warnett will we see tonight? The laid-back party animal, or the serious competitor looking to end his career on a high? LH: We can only wait and see. Well, that’s seven of the eight competitors, leaving just one. Here’s what he had to say a week ago at an event in Japan...... [cut to tape] ______________________________________________________________ _ _/ __| I-CROWN | | | (__ THE Kashan Akuma (Asia champ) | |___\___| FINALS______________________________________________| (The scene opens in a hallway with doors lining its walls. At the very end of the hallway, there are bright lights and a large crowd. Kashan Akuma steps into the frame. Right behind him is the documentary cameraman, James Stevens. They approach the end of the hallway.) KASHAN: Let's set it off. (Akuma continues to walk down the hallway. As he gets closer and closer to the end of the hallway, you can begin to make out what is at the end. Finally, he steps out of the hallway. As you can now see, it is a huge stadium. In the centre is a wrestling ring. The crowd is immensely huge and the cheers are deafening. In the centre of the ring are two heavyweight wrestlers. Akuma lifts his arm up and a microphone is in his hand.) KASHAN: Surprise, surprise, guess who's here. Yes, it is I, the Emperor of Puroresu. The "Ikon" of the Ring. The one and only Kashan Akuma. Now, it's time for all you marks to direct your attention away from the two "overweights" in the ring and re-direct your attention to real main event. The real star attraction. Yeah, that's right. Turn your heads this way. I think good ol' Giant Babs should be proud that I have decided to save his faltering promotion. Every single person in this building here tonight just got their full value of their ticket. And those at home watching on Pay-Per-View, you just got your yen's worth. I can hear the registers ringing right now. (Akuma begins to make his way down the ramp. James Stevens is not far behind him. Akuma stops in the middle of the walkway and poses to the crowd. Most of the fans are not paying much attention but that does not phase Akuma.) KASHAN: Yeah, those buyrates are skyrocketing as I speak. A big fat old check better be in the mail, Babs. For you bums jacking this event on your free cable, you should be calling up your cable company right now and apologizing for stealing cable. Stuff this good, don't come free. But on to the topic. As you know, Japan will be the home to the first ever I-Crown champion. I just came here to promote this event for good old Clint Fletcher. I know all of you will be ordering this event on PPV. It's just time for you to all wish me luck. (Akuma turns to James Stevens and gives him a thumbs up signal. He has a huge cocky grin on his face. The crowd still doesn't appear to be paying much attention to him. In his best announcer voice, he begins to speak again.) KASHAN: In one week, the greatest event in the history of this sport will transpire. Only one man can be crowned as the king of the world. Only one man can become the I-Crown champion. 8 men stand against each other, each hoping to be recognized as the best in the world. Each man has given their blood sweat and tears for the sport of wrestling. But only one man can be victorious. Find out on Sunday, July 26, the first ever I-Crown Finals, only available on Pay-Per-View! Brought to you by Clint Fletcher and the World cup of Wrestling. Call your local cable company now. (Akuma senses that the crowd is not paying attention to him. He does not break his smile though and just shrugs it off. He instead just walks farther down the walkway.) KASHAN: You know, I just got a little something bothering me right now. I just got to get this little something off my chest. I heard that Derek Mota was saying a little something about me. That little something happened to be about my authenticity. Hey Mota, what do you know about the "Ikon". Nothing much, you probably don't know nothing much about Japan either. Hey, I'm as Japanese as that working class bum you bumped into. I'm just not paying the taxes. If you are questioning my Japanese-ness, why don't you try translating this. Futomaru, koshinuke daiben!!! Ask Enigma about that one. (Akuma smiles to himself and lets a little chuckle out. He then yells out some Japanese curse words towards the crowd.) I wonder how authentic you are. How do I know you're really a fat piece of Canadian bacon. For all I know, you could be some hick from Texas or an orangeneck, oh, I mean redneck from Arkansas. I can't really tell by your accent or your cheap clothes. You don't even say eh!! (Akuma once again moves farther down the walkway until he is practically ringside. He glances at the wrestlers in the ring for a minute but just shakes his head and laughs at them. They pay no attention to him either.) KASHAN: But enough with that crazy Canuck. Now it's some freakin' Aussie who's talkin' trash. Good'ay mate, Wug. I'll get straight to the point buddy boy. You should stop "sucking" up to Wuzho. He ain't gonna pay you nothing fool, if you understand that concept. I guess Wuzho will be "beating" you. Sorry kids, you should ask your parents about that stuff. You better get your ass in the gym soon or you'll be lying on your back the whole I-Crown Finals. Maybe you should stop spending your time in theatres doing a Pee Wee Herman impression. You might get some exercise from that "pumping iron" I guess. It could make something out of those pencil arms of yours. The rapid arm movements can really build up those muscles. (The crowd is still totally focused on the match going on behind Akuma that he is forced to actually stand right beside the ring. The action then spills to the outside. Akuma is totally unphased as one wrestler powerbombs the other directly behind him.) KASHAN: And lastly, I just have to address Wuzho. I guess cat got your tongue, huh Wuz. It better heal up by the 26th because I'm going to rip that sucker right out of your ugly mouth. Maybe I'll debut my new submission hold. The Salivary Gland Claw. I'm going to have a pleasure smacking that paint off your face. Real men don't wear makeup Wuz. But enough of that crap, though. I'll let you pieces of daiben re-direct your attention to the two jabbronies in the ring. Thank you!! (Akuma struts back up the ramp, showing off to the fans. He gives a few "call me" hand symbols to the females in the crowd. He even tries to flirt with one right by the entranceway. He gets a slap for his trouble and winks at her as he disappears behind the curtain. The scene fades to black.) [cut back to studio] BN: One of the cockiest wrestlers in the business folks....Kashan Akuma. Here’s how he got to this point, overcoming big names like Super Tengu and Takezo Musashi...... [The screen goes blank as we cut to the video, and then snaps back into life, as the following words appear at the bottom:] VIDEO FOOTAGE: I-CROWN NIGHT TWO [JUNE 25 1998] ============================================== <Akuma is set up and floored by another lariat..and then set up for another> LVK: No mercy shown by Cesar Montano! <Akuma ducks under the last attempt and counters with a bridging back suplex for a two-count> BF: Huge kickout by Montano! He's fighting like a man possessed! <Montano prepares to hit Akuma with his own Cyclone Fury Driver to the cheers of the crowd> LVK: He's gonna beat Akuma with his own maneuver! SW: NOOOOOOOOOO! <Cesar Montano prepares to finish Akuma off, when Akuma shifts his weight and delivers his own Cyclone Fury Driver to the boos of the crowd> LVK: NO! SW: YES! BF: Stunning reversal by Akuma... 1 2 NO! LVK: He kicked out! He kicked out! BF: Unbelievable! SW: This can't be happening! <Akuma gets nailed by a kick to the midsection, then is set up for a powerbomb..however, Akuma is ready and nails a facerake followed by his patented Tiger Driver '91 <double underhook powerbomb> > BF: Out of nowhere... 1 2 3!! KASHAN AKUMA WINS IT!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [The crowd breaks into polite applause, showing that although they dislike Akuma they appreciate his effort in winning the match.] BF: Kashan Akuma is the Asia champion folks! Cesar Montano came so close, but was just pipped at the post.....nothing to be ashamed about for the Phillipino though, because it was a fine effort by him! LVK: I reckon! What a match, and what a fight to the finish by Montano and Akuma. I still can't believe that Montano kicked out of the Cyclone Driver! Amazing! SW: Kashan Akuma is a very talented young man, and he proved that tonight! [A Japanese flag falls from the rafters and hangs above the ring, as the ref holds Akuma's arm high, while giving him the glittering Asia pool title belt. Foxxy runs into the ring and gives her wrestler a huge embrace, as they celebrate the win.] <<<<video footage ends>>>> BN: Akuma is one to watch tonight folks - he may not be as recognisable as Mota or Hudson, but the self-proclaimed "Ikon" has all the skills to get the job done. LH: He certainly has folks. Just a couple of hours ago we caught up with Akuma as he arrived at the United Center. Here’s what he had to say...... [cut to tape] (The scene opens in the loading gate just outside of the arena. The I-Crown crew is busy setting up for the big finals. People are going in and out, hauling large pieces of equipment. A jet black Lexus GS300 pulls up right in front of the large docking bay. The driver is Kashan Akuma. In the passenger seat is film maker Shane Redding and right behind him is camera man James Stevens.) KASHAN: Hey, hey, where's the red carpet!! I made certain demands to Fletch and I expect them to be satisfied. Who the hell is in charge here!? Whoever is better get this straightened out or their ass is fired. I'll make sure it happens. Hello!! Is anyone listening. This is unsatisfactory. You fat lugs better get going and do this. (Akuma stands there with an annoyed look on his face while leaning over to one side. He is wearing casual shoes, Ralph Lauren Polo khakis, and a white polo shirt. Adorning his face are black Hilfiger sunglasses. He has a duffel back slung over his shoulder. James and Shane are directly behind him. Both are wearing a t-shirt and shorts.) KASHAN: You know what, this job obviously isn't being done. I'm not going to stand here all day waiting for you to get off your asses. So, just get the hell out of my way and let me get to my locker room. You've wasted enough of my time already. Now where the hell is the valet guy? I'm not leaving my car out there. The fans will tear it apart. Everyone wants a piece of Akumamania. My hood ornament could fetch well near a couple G's. Who knows how much my bumper will get in the open market. (The three men walk into the large arena complex. The workers around them just ignore them and continue to set up. Akuma stops at an intersection and looks around him. There are 3 different hallways to choose from. He guesses and takes a left.) KASHAN: So here I am, finally at the I-Crown. The day of reckoning has finally come. You know, I have worked my ass off these past few months getting here. I've had to train day and night. I've had to take tons of beatings. I've had to dodge the media and crazy fans. I've had to decide how to spend my money from my endorsement deals. Being a superstar ain't easy. When it's time to get down to business, it ain't easy either. Having to destroy the hope and dreams of a fellow wrestler is very hard on my conscience. On one hand, I don't really give a damn about the piece of trash in the ring with me. On the other hand, the ladies don't like it when I have to get middle ages on someone's ass. SHANE: That's medieval on someone's ass. KASHAN: Whatever. It's hard on my personal life you know. But, there comes a time in a man's life when he has to forget about all of that. I have to think of my priorities. And my main priority is taking the I-Crown home to Japan, where the wrestling is real, not a joke in society. This is headline news material. This isn't going to be treated the way it is in the United States of Asses where people mock puroresu. I will be rightfully praised as a legend, no _the_ legend. Like I stated before, only one man can win. Friendships mean nothing. You may all want it, but only the man who wants it the most can claimit as his own. That man is Kashan Akuma. (Akuma and the other two continue walking down that hallway. At the end of it is a door. It is unmarked but it appears to be some kind of locker room. Akuma looks at James and then opens the door. It just happens to be the women's change room where the valets are getting dressed. Akuma begins to blush. Some are caught in the middle of changing.) KASHAN: Sorry ladies. I'm really, really sorry. Seriously. I think I'll be going now. I didn't mean to disturb you lovely ladies. My full apologies. (Kashan slowly closes the door. He continues looking inside the locker room as he is closing the door. He has a huge smile on his face. Akuma nudges his head to the right and they go back towards the intersection.) KASHAN: Hey James, did you catch that on video? You have to put that in the documentary my man. Sex sells and personally I don't mind seeing that footage again. I'm glad I made that wrong turn back there. But back on to the topic. Wrestling. I've caught a lot of heat recently for my attitude towards the I-Crown. It seems everyone is gunning for me. I guess people are easily put off by someone who is far superior to them. I guess it's some kind of jealousy complex that people develop during competition. Why is it that they look down on someone who is doing good? It just boggles the mind sometimes. (Kashan, James, and Shane are back at the intersection. This time they go straight forward as Shane noticed a sign that said, "Wrestler Locker Rooms". They proceed down that hallway, looking at the various signs on the doors.) KASHAN: Jealousy, Envy, Lust, and Hate. The Four Devils are definitely evident here. Everyone seems to be envious and jealous of me. Wuzho damn well hates me. And Wug seems to have lust... sorry kids, I can't talk about that stuff anymore. These fools seem to be afraid of something. They are afraid of admitting that they want to win. They are afraid of being criticized for being cocky. I'm not afraid of anything. If they want to cower behind a rock, let them. When it all comes down, their confidence will be shattered and we all know who the champion will be. Bottom line, if you aren't willing to say what's on your mind, you won't get anywhere in life. In the centre is a coffee table with a bowl of nuts in the centre. The three men slide into the room and close the door behind them.) KASHAN: Where the hell's the Crystal. I specifically asked for a bottle of Crystal chilled in my locker room. Another one of my needs is not fulfilled. When will they learn. I'll have to live with it though. At least I won't be a bit tipsy before the battle royal. SHANE: Hey, I think we should let the people know what's on your mind about your opponents. Let's close this off with a little word association again. Who knows, your opinion of someone might of changed. KASHAN: No prob, just shoot. SHANE: King Apples. KASHAN: Who knows. The guys too busy to bother with the I-Crown. He'll be done for the night in the battle royal. Next. SHANE: La Pulga Gigante. KASHAN: I'm disappointed with the luchador. The exact same thing as Apples. His finish is eminent. SHANE: Fabrice Duvalle. KASHAN: Come on. The Frenchmen is too wrapped up in the World Cup victory. He's going out like Brazil. He better not complain about convulsions though. SHANE: Derek Mota. KASHAN: A plain idiot, eh. He tries to question my identity, eh. The canuck might be cocky, but he's foolish, eh. There's a difference, eh. He'll see how the Japanese handle their business. SHANE: Marty Warnett. KASHAN: Deranged Welshman. There has to be something wrong going on in that head of his. I wonder if he can imagine how it feels to get his head driven into the mat one second into the match. This isn't a TV show Marty. SHANE: "Bullywug" Glenn Hudson. KASHAN: I don't want to be near that guy. Who knows what that guys been doing with his spare time. He's been spending more time "pumping iron" than getting ready for the match. Let's hope he doesn't "suck up" to Wuzho anymore than he already has. SHANE: Wuzho. KASHAN: I have him in my sights. I'm going to have lots of fun tearing him apart. His arima won't be able to help him this time. I'll send him back to his travelling caravan because he's better off singing and dancing all over Europe than stepping in a ring. SHANE: There we have it. It's been great working with you Kashan. I'm probably going to get some more footage during the night and then it's onto the cutting room. KASHAN: I see a blockbuster. Well, I see that I've got some time on my hands. I think I'll go out looking for the groupies. Maybe I can get some "action". (Akuma has a sly smile on his face. He saunters out of the locker room and around the corner. The scene fades to black.) [cut back to studio] BN: Well, there we have folks. The eight men who will battle for the right to be called I-Crown champion in 1998. Who will win? Who will lose? Don’t worry, the waiting is just about over - the questions will all be answered. In just a few moments we’ll be crossing LIVE to the United Center, where Bob, Larry and Sir William will be standing by to get it all underway. [fade to black]
