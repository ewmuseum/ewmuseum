Countdown to the I-Crown, Part Two

28 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[As we come back from the commercials, Lance and Bryan stop talking amongst themselves and turn to the camera.] BN: Welcome back, as we continue the countdown towards the big I-Crown Finals megaevent. One man who the fans are definitely looking forward to seeing later tonight is the gruff young Australian "Bullywug" Glenn Hudson. Hudson has been out of the wrestling spotlight in recent times, and is using the I-Crown as his big chance to get back where he belongs - among the truly great of our sport. LH: For a young man Hudson has achieved a helluva lot, but winning the I-Crown is top of his priority list at the moment. To get to this point, the Bullywug had to overcome great odds. Take a look..... [The screen goes blank as we cut to the video, and then snaps back into life, as the following words appear at the bottom:] VIDEO FOOTAGE: I-CROWN NIGHT ONE [JUNE 13 1998] ============================================== [Slade climbs to the top rope. Slade attempts a double axehandle, but Hudson hits a headspear while he's in mid air] LVK: IN-FREAKING-CREDIBLE! Did you see that?! The timing had to be perfect..and it was! BF: Indeed! THat manuever may have been the turning-point in this matchup.... SW: Ahh...yes...but you're overlooking one thing... LVK: What would that be? SW: The referee is unconscious as well.... <the camera focuses in on the official who is lying unconscious on the ring apron> BF: Oh no! [Both men struggle to their feet] BF: I don't think these two even realize it! [Slade whips Hudson into the ropes...Slade slaps on a clawhold and then slams Hudson chokeslam style into the mat before collapsing again] BF: Nice move by Mark Slade..but again..it's a battle of who can stand up first! [Hudson tackles Slade down and then slaps on an anklelock!] LVK: Hudson manages to be the first to gain the offensive..and in a big way...oh what's happening in the aisle? ["Bad Apple" Bobby Durango makes his way down to ringside holding a ringside chair] BF: Oh geez..this can't be good! [Both men are again up and punching eachother...Durango enters the ring unnoticed and swings at Hudson] LVK: NOOOO! [Hudson, ducking a punch, misses the chair as it smashes into the face of Slade with a loud, crushing sound. Hudson sees what happens and runs to the ropes!] BF: HE HIT SLADE! LVK: There's some poetic justice! SW: No! [As Durango trys to wake Slade he sees Hudson just in time to get driven into by the "No Hard Feelings" finisher] BF: The ref is stirring..he sees Hudson......Hudson is pinning Slade! 1 2 3!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! LVK: YES! We have an I-Crown South Pacific Champion! GLENN HUDSON WINS!!!!!!! SW: NO! NO! Slade was hit by a chair! It was illegal! ILLEGAL! Restart the match! BF: Give it up..it was his own man that hit him! The Wug wins! [The screen cuts to Hudson who is slapping the mat in excitement as "The Mouth" makes his way to the ring] "TM" Your winner....and 1998 South Pacific I-Crown Champion...."Bullywug" Glenn Hudson! <HUUUUUUGE CHEERS> [Hudson walks over and picks up his belt as Slade and Durango lie unconscious in the ring. The fans are going beserk for Hudson, who drapes the gold belt over his shoulder as the Australian flag falls from the rafters and "Advance Australia Fair" starts playing. With an exhausted yet overjoyed look on his face, Hudson mounts the turnbuckles and salutes the fans.] <<<<video footage ends>>>> BN: It sure was a great effort by Hudson, overcoming not only the devious Mark Slade, but also Bobby Durango, who had earlier been eliminated from the match. LH: Slade’s plans almost worked, but justice prevailed. BN: The young man from Australia has shown real focus in the lead-up to tonight’s event. While others have turned to parody and mocking to try and rile up their opponents, Hudson has concentrated on what awaits in the ring. For a young man he sure seems to have his head screwed on straight. We got these comments from him earlier in the week...... [cut to video tape] ______________________________________________________________ _ _/ __| I-CROWN | | | (__ THE "Bullywug" Glenn Hudson (Sth Pacific champ) | |___\___| FINALS______________________________________________| [Dark. A pretty ordinary singing-voice can be heard- ] Lean on me.. When you're not strong, And I'll visit France.. Then get the scene-'ry wrong.. [We fade in to see "Bullywug" Glenn Hudson leaning casually up against a wall. He is conforming to the Wug dress-code, which of course consists of a white T-shirt and a pair of jeans. Glenn grins widely and has a chuckle under his breath. It wouldn't be a foolish leap of logic to assume that the voice heard just seconds ago was his own.] Wug: Hehe.. Mota.. [Another snicker.] Wug: Mota.. Congratulations, mate. You've made my Yearbook. And you managed to get in pretty cheaply, I might add. All it cost you was "a lot of time researchin'".. [Glenn pushes himself up and off the wall, and back to his own two feet. He holds his palms up, as if to say "whoa".] Wug: Relax.. I don't need to pick your bones clean for whatever is left from the vultures who got to you before me. I'm sure you've been getting every man and his dog having a good ol' laugh at your expense.. But me.. well, I'm not like that. I know where to draw the line and let a dead horse decompose. [Hudson nods, and with a oddly caring demeanor, says- ] Wug: I _understand_ you, Derek. I really do. I _know_ your secret, the secret that you've accidentally uncovered.. [A pause, long enough for Glenn to crack a devilish smile.] Wug: I watch "Sliders". [He sounded a little too proud to make that announcement, but continues.] Wug: I also watch "Spellbinder". [For those who have never seen the Children's Science Fiction series "Spellbinder", it's actually pretty damned cool. I hope they make a third series.] Wug: This isn't the first time I've had to cross paths with someone like yourself, Mota. In fact, a large number of my former opponents have proven that they are obviously experiencing some kind of parallel world, or worlds. And I'm sure many, many other competitors out there are nodding and agreeing with me. [Hudson settles down a little, and takes a few steps towards a conveniently-placed chair. He sits down.] Wug: But.. in _this_ particular plane of existence.. in _this_ time and place.. the finals of the I-Crown tournament are about to kick into high-gear. And between you and me.. [He melodramatically looks over both shoulders, making sure that his words aren't being overheard..] Wug: I'm just a teensy little bit anxious step into that ring and get some wrestling done. There's seven other guys whose shoulders are in desperate need to spend some quality time with the canvas, and I'm only too happy to be the go-between guy. [Glenn takes one last little peek over his left shoulder.] Wug: Eight of the world's finest.. we hope.. battling it out.. Who will square-off against who? Who will win, and progress to the next round.. and eventually triumph? Who will lose, and be mercilessly mocked and taunted until my throat is sore? [He smiles.] Wug: So much mystery.. So much chance.. And no sign yet of a Ronnie D match billed above it to stink out the arena! In my humble opinion.. Somebody up there in the head office has stumbled onto a winning formula.. Somebody up there in the head office.. er.. is good. Who said I could never be a company man?! [Glenn cracks his knuckles with a satisfied look on his face, though he really shouldn't.] Wug: Bring on the fun, guys. Bring on the fun. [Fade to black, just as he starts to hum a few additional bars.] [cut back to studio] BN: Those comments obviously aimed at a certain Derek Mota. The fans would love to see Mota and Hudson face off in the ring tonight. LH: Mota’s got a way of rubbing people the wrong way. I’m sure all of the other seven wrestlers tonight would love to get their hands on the "Heatseeker". BN: Let’s take a look at what the Heatseeker had to say earlier this week. Or should we say.....here is the latest edition of the "Young and the Wrestlers". He he..... [cut to tape] ______________________________________________________________ _ _/ __| I-CROWN | | | (__ THE Derek Mota (Canada champ) | |___\___| FINALS______________________________________________| VOICE: Waaaaatttteeeeerrrrrr.... [The man's cries go unheard as he crawls on his hands and feet through the desert. As the cameraman pulls back, we barely recognize the man as the "Heatseeker" Derek Mota, through the multiple layers of sand caked on his face and in his hair. Mota's clothes are almost worn through, thanks to the constant sandblasting from the strong winds. Mota looks ahead of him and sees and endless stretch of desert, puts his head down and continues crawling...] DM: Dammit Chuck, how come I'm just about dyin' here and you can just walk along filming? CHUCK: Someone's gotta put this on record, Derek. That's why I'm keeping all the food. At least your death won't be in vain, we'll always have your struggle on tape... DM: That BETTER be a joke, ya little bastard... just keep filmin' then, I'll kill ya later. CHUCK: I know you're always up for the dramatic, Derek. But why walk through the desert? You could've just called Fletch and asked him to send you out one of his chartered planes. DM: Oh yeah, and feel like I owe him something... I don't think so. I made it through seven... well, six countries in the past week, and damn it, I'm gonna make it back home to Canada on my own, even if it kills me. CHUCK: And me. DM: Does anybody really care about you, Chuck? I'm gonna fire ya as soon as we get home anyways. [Poor Chuck seriously wonders why he took this job as Derek continues to crawl... and then puts his head down.] DM: I just need a break. Just let me rest one eye for a sec... I swear I'll continue crawling... [Chuck sits down on the desert floor as Mota closes one eye ... and then the other. However, this doesn't last very long, and Mota quickly re-opens one eye just as he promised.] JL: HEY!!! Wake up Derek! You were resting both eyes there! [Mota sits up suddenly, looking strangely at the man standing before him.] DM: Jerry Lawler? JL: Don't call me Jerry Lawler, tough guy! Just call me the King! King Apples! DM: Oh man, I've gotta be dreamin'... JL: I had a dream once... best weekend of my life! HAHAHAHAHHAHAA! Best weekend of my life! Get it? DM: Oh man, I've GOTTA wake up! CHEECH: Here man, take this! It's gonna help you out! [Mota turns to his left and sees the infamous Cheech standing there, offering him a joint.] DM: What the hell are you doing here, Cheech? CHEECH: I ain't Cheech, man! I'm La Pulga Gigante! Sometimes when I'm not busy shaking my sexy ass around the place, mang, I gotta smoke a joint now and then! JL: Mind if I help you there? [Jani Lane, the singer from the 80's hair band Warrant, puts his arm around Cheech, hoping to get a toke of the joint.] JL: What? Sometimes being an 80's reject gets you down, you gotta go the extra mile to get you back on your feet again. DM: Lemme guess... Marty Warnett. JL: How do you know? DM: Oh, lucky guess, I suppose. JL: You gotta remember Derek... when you come from Wales, you're 10 years behind everything! Glam rock is just starting to get hip! I'm cutting edge! [Lane fluffs back his hair and plays a little bit of air guitar until someone walks up from behind him and stabs him with a giant utility knife! Lane collapses to the desert floor, blood pouring from the gaping hole in his side, and Derek looks around in shock as he realizes that Lane is dead.] PH: Now THAT was a knife! DM: This all sounds so familiar... you killed Marty Warnett! ["Bullywug" Glenn Hudson steps into the scene, wearing his camouflage clothes and large rimmed hat, typical of the Australian stereotype. He looks surprisingly like Paul Hogan, star of such famous flicks such as Crocodile Dundee, and ... Crocodile Dundee.] PH: That Warnett fellow was asking for it, mate! Being the gentleman that I am, I figured I'd end it for him! [Right out of the blue, Jackie Chan flies into the scene, attacking the other wrestlers. A huge brawl breaks out as the international wrestlers all try to gain an advantage. Mota shakes his head in amazement, before laying back down on the desert floor, hoping to get a nap until these gentlemen can get things started. He suddenly opens his eyes, finally awake from the strange dream that took him over for several minutes. Mota's forehead is covered with sweat and sand, as he looks awake for real here.] DM: Dammit, things've gone too far... I'm gonna have ta make a change... Chuck, call a cab, we're going home. CHUCK: A cab? A cab? [Chuck pulls out his cell phone from his pocket, and it works flawlessly.] DM: Too much... Sunday night, I'm making an announcement. Like it or not, I think I'm too old for this crap. [The flash ends as a small airplane flies by, stopping to pick up both Chuck and Derek. Fade.] <<<video footage ends>>> LH: Did he ever get back from that desert? BN: I don’t know. Mota said in that interview that he would be making an announcement tonight, so we’re going to cross live now to the Canadian superstar’s dressing room at the United Center....... [The screen is suddenly taken up with the view as a cameraman walks down a darkish hallway. He stops and turns, and we see the words "Derek Mota" with a star underneath, on a plain door. The cameraman’s hand reaches into shot and knocks, but there is no reply.] Cameraman: Mr Mota? Are you in there? We’re here for our interview. [Another couple of knocks bring no answer, so the cameraman tries the door handle. The door swings open, revealing a plain dressing room, with dressing table and mirror, a plain couch and chairs, and a mini-bar. It looks untouched.] BN: Doesn’t look like Mota has arrived yet. We’ll make sure to get comment from him when he arrives. [The camera cuts back to the studio] LH: Let’s see how Mota got to the finals........ [The screen goes blank as we cut to the video, and then snaps back into life, as the following words appear at the bottom:] VIDEO FOOTAGE: I-CROWN NIGHT ONE [JUNE 13 1998] ============================================== [Mota takes a couple of moments to regain his breath, and then starts slowly climbing to the top rope. But before he can balance on the top turnbuckle, Leshenn is climbing after him, and stops him up top with a forearm. The fans gasp in anticipation, and Leshenn grabs Mota's forehead in a clawhold. He then jumps off the top while holding onto Mota, driving him into the mat backfirst.] BF: OHHHHH!!!! Super Talon Claw!! One of Leshenn's finishing moves!! It's all over!! [Leshenn makes the cover without hooking the leg.] LVK: ONE......TWO........ BF: Did he get it? LVK: NO! Two count only!! AMAZING!! BF: Mota kicked out of a clawhold slam from the top rope!! Words cannot explain what we just witnessed folks! SW: Mr Leshenn made a sloppy cover! He should have hooked the leg, then he would have made the pin! [Leshenn can't believe it. He climbs to his feet and rolls out of the ring, reaching under the ring and bringing out a folded up ringside table.] LVK: OH NO!! Not another table!! [Leshenn throws the table into the ring and slides back in. The referee starts telling him to get rid of it, but then gives up and lets him continue.] BF: The referee is going to let Nick Leshenn use the table in the ring!! SW: How barbaric! [Leshenn folds out the table and sets it up mid-ring. He then rolls Mota on top of it, and starts walking towards the nearest corner. He gets to the second turnbuckle, before Mota gets up and runs over to stop him. The two fight in the corner, and the camera suddenly pans to the aisle, where Mark Slade is running to the ring with a chair.] LVK: SLADE???? BF: Slade hates Leshenn, so it's not too hard to guess why he's coming out!! [Slade slides into the ring with the fold-up chair and runs towards Mota and Leshenn. But Mota sees him coming and turns around and launches into a huge superkick, which sends the held-up steel chair straight into Slade's face. Slade goes flying through the ropes and out of the ring, coming to rest on the floor.] LVK: OHHHHH!! BF: Slade is sent sprawling by a Mota superkick!! I'm guessing that Slade was here to help Mota beat Leshenn, but Mota wanted nothing of it! SW: This is getting out of hand! [Mota turns back to Leshenn, who hooks him in position for a Diamond Cutter/Stone Cold Stunner. Leshenn gets ready to nail the move, but Mota reaches up with his left arm, hooking Leshenn's head by the throat. Before Leshenn can react, Mota nails him with a Scorpian Deathdrop.] BF: DEATHDROP!!!!!!! Mota nailed him!! LVK: PIN HIM!! PIN HIM!! [But rather than go for the pin, Mota rolls Leshenn onto the table, and quickly leaps to the top rope. The fans give a huge face pop as Mota signals, then leaps into the air. From his original moonsault position he leaps up, turns around and nails a somersault splash onto Leshenn, driving him through the table onto the mat.] BF: MAIN ATTRACTION!!!!!! Mota's patented finisher!! [Amidst the wreckage of the table, Mota rolls on top of Leshenn, draping his arm across his chest for the cover. The referee falls to the mat, and makes the count. His hand hits once......] BF, LVK and crowd: ONNNEEEEE!!!!! [.....his hand hits twice.....] BF, LVK and crowd: TWOOOOOO!!!! [.......his hand hits three times, and at the same time Leshenn kicks out.] BF + LVK: Leshenn kicked out!! But was it in time? [The ref climbs to his feet and signals for the bell! The fans ERUPT!!!!!] BF: NOOOOOO!!!! It was three! Mota wins!! LVK: Derek Mota wins with the Main Attraction through a ringside table!! What a way to end this event!! [Mota is on his knees with his face in his hands, while Leshenn lies amongst the table debris. The ref has the Canada pool belt, and he hands it to Mota, who holds it in both hands, looking down at the gold faceplate with a look of exhaustion, exhiliration and relief on his sweaty, bloodied face.] The Mouth: Your winner, and 1998 I-Crown Canada pool winner........DEREK MOTA!!!!!!!! [The fans are continuing to give the biggest face pop of the night, as Mota stays on his knees, getting his arm raised by the ref.] <<<<video footage ends>>>> BN: The Canadian fans were overjoyed at seeing Mota win on that occasion, but I’m not so sure the US fans will take to the "Heatseeker" in the same way. The one wrestler who will have the hometown advantage tonight is also unlikely to get many cheers. I’m talking of course about the cunning INWI World champ, King Apples. Here’s how Apples qualified for the finals……. [The screen goes blank as we cut to the video, and then snaps back into life, as the following words appear at the bottom:] VIDEO FOOTAGE: I-CROWN NIGHT TWO [JUNE 25 1998] ============================================== [de Sade refuses to give up, and reaches out one hand to the ropes. The ref makes Apples break the hold, and Apples yells at the official angrily. de Sade drops to the mat on his stomach, and Apples stands between his legs and waistlocks him from behind. He then lifts de Sade up by his waist, falls back and drops him on his back. The fans pop for this massive move.] BF: Snow-Plex!! What a devastating blow by King Apples! He's hooking the leg....... BF + LVK: ONE.....TWO......THR....NOOOOOOO!!!! Kickout by Ethan de Sade!! The fans are going wild at the determination being exhibited by de Sade here tonight! [de Sade is once more facedown on the mat, and Apples takes his right leg and hooks it into the crook of his left knee. Apples then pushes the left leg down and forward with his own leg, and then reaches forward to get a chinlock on de Sade. But de Sade sees it coming, and lashes out with a back elbow, which catches Apples on the bloody face, sending him to the mat and breaking the hold.] BF: de Sade saw the inverted Indian Deathlock coming, and he knew that if King Apples could get the hold sunk on, it would be all over!! [de Sade clambers to his feet, wincing as he puts his weight on the injured leg. Apples gets up as well, and de Sade slugs him in the chin, staggering Apples. He follows up with a couple more, but then crumples to the mat when he advances, clutching at the leg.] LVK: Oh no! de Sade was on the offensive, but his leg won't hold up!! It's taken too much damage! SW: Damn shame! [Apples sees de Sade clutching at the leg and walks over and hooks on a figure four leglock, bringing a loud gasp from the crowd. He sits back, sinking the hold on.] BF: FIGURE FOUR!!! LVK: Surely de Sade can't resist this!! [de Sade thrashes about on the mat in absolute, bone-splintering agony! The ref pleads with him to give up, but de Sade refuses. Outside the ring, Mr Flamboyant grabs a chair and reaches it into the ring. Apples grabs the chair legs, and Flamboyant leans back with all of his weight, adding double the pressure on the hold. The ref is too busy asking de Sade to give up to notice, and the fans scream in protest.] BF: Mr Flamboyant with the assist!! LVK: COME ON REF!!! Open your eyes!! SW: HA ha ha! BF: de Sade refuses to give up...... [Finally the pain is too much for de Sade, and he gives up. The ref calls for the bell, as Mr Flamboyant drops the chair and scurries away. de Sade rolls into a corner, clutching at his leg, and Apples thunders to his feet, a look of total ecstasy on it.] DING DING DING BF: He held on for as long as he could, but in the end the overwhelming pain would have been too much to bear. LVK: de Sade gave it his best shot, but in the end it was his daredevil tactics, plus the cheating of Apples and Flamboyant, which were his undoing. The Mouth: Ladies and gentlemen.....your winner, by way of submission.......and 1998 I-CROWN US POOL CHAMPION......KING APPLES!!!!! <<<<video footage ends>>>> BN: Whoever faces King Apples tonight will have to be wary of Mr Flamboyant at ringside. Unless they want to end up like Ethan de Sade of course. LH: King Apples has been fairly silent in the build-up to tonight’s big event, but he came out of hiding this week to speak his mind. Take a look... [cut to tape] ______________________________________________________________ _ _/ __| I-CROWN | | | (__ THE King Apples (US champ) | |___\___| FINALS______________________________________________| [King Apples is in a dark surrounding and only a little light shines on him. You see he holds the INWI World Championship and also the I-Crown US Belt.] King Apples: The I-Crown Finals. Just where I belong. With the elite. I know some, I don't know others... but I am pretty sure that it is the same both ways. However, I have a plan. A major plan. One that will make sure I am ready for the matches itself. The seeding is all academical. You have to go through all the big guns to win this damn thing anyways. So it doesn't matter where you start. It's not like there will be any "easy" matches. These people are all the elite. All the ones that had to go through hell and back to get to where they are. The I-Crown finals. And I am here as well. Not here to lose. But here to win... [Apples stands and walks towards the camera. You can see him better now. He stands with both belts over his shoulders.] King Apples: Bullywug. You and I face again. Yes,, formerly you were a member of the power "Brotherhood" during your stay in the INWI. I remember that and I remember you well. And I think that you know me pretty well. No doubt me and you will lock horns... ...Derek Mota... I know who you are. I know what you can do. I have seen what you can do. But the question is, have you seen what I can do? I doubt that you have. But you are a worldly compeditor, so maybe you have. No matter, I have been told about you. I know you are a champion. And now, we will meet in that ring at one point or another... ...Wuzho... I haven't a clue who you are. But you can't be a scab... for you are in the finals. I have heard your name before... but never seen you. I am currently trying to find tapes.. I assure you I will have you scouted. And I am one person who gets ready... after all I am a World Champeeeeeen.... ...Fabrice Duvalle... my French Fried friend... well... I think you better bring the Eifel Tower with you, because you'll need it by the time I beat on your French ass! We saved you many times Frenchy and you can better believe this time the good ol' US of A is gonna kick yer ass!!! ...La Pulga Gigante.... ENGLISH MAN! You and your jumping beans better get ready you twerp. Cause when I get my mitts on you, I'm gonna jump all over you! [Apples laughs to himself] Now then, I don't know if you are a Luchadore or a big man... all though with a name like "Gigante" I imagine you aren't some little kid. So, "big man," prepare, I'm gonna bring you down to size... ...Kashan Akuma... BANZAI! [Apples again laughs...] Man... I'm not even gonna go here.... ...Marty Warnett... you Marty... I'll give a little credit. You came through some big guns to get where you are and obviously deserve it. HOWEVER.... you are in my house now. And let me make this in your lanquage.... I'm gonna kick yer bloody arse!!! [The lights shine all the way on now and Apples is seen wearing a shirt that says "The next I-Crown Champion" on it. He takes both belts and adjusts them on his shoulders...] King Apples: Eight men. All top of the world. All gunning for one thing. The I-Crown. And I am gunning for the same thing. And well, that means I am gonna win it. Because I am in a class of my own. And well, that's an "International" Class. So people, buckle up. Yer in for a long ride. As Apples is ready to go. You see, I have the plan that is gonna help me win it all. So, get ready boys... it will be unveiled soon! I'll be seein' ya! [With that King Apples turns and walks out of the room. Camera fades.] [cut back to studio] BN: The King sure sounds confident, but then.....that’s King Apples for you. We need to take a break folks, but we’ll continue our countdown to the I-Crown finals after this........ [cut to commercials]
