Pulga — I-Crown competitor profile

13 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wrestler Information ============== Wrestler Name: La Pulga Gigante Height: 5'7" Weight: 180 lbs Age: 28 Hometown: Guadalajara, Mexico Alignment: rudo Appearance: (what the wrestler looks like.. please be as detailed as possible. If a wrestler is masked the relevant information will be confidential.) Pulga is a small but toned wrestler, with a build not unlike that of a Rey Misterio Jr. He is a light skinned Mexican, but does NOT wear a mask. He is not necessarily an attractive man, but he carries himself like one, flashing a big smile often. He has shoulder-length black hair, full of grease, and pulled back into a tight ponytail. Ring Attire: (what your wrestler wears _TO_ the ring, and what he wears _WHILE WRESTLING_) Pulga wears a black, single singlet unitard, like the Giant would wear. In fact, his entire outfit looks similar to the Giants...black unitard, black boots, and a black glove on his right hand. Coming to the ring, he wears a extravagant ring robe, similar to one Ric Flair would wear. Description of the wrestler's personality, behaviour and attitude: (please be as descriptive as possible) La Pulga Gigante is basically a very cocky individual. He believes that he is the biggest, the strongest, and the most beautiful wrestler in all of Mexico, if not the world. He does not wear a mask, because he believes that if he wore a mask, he would be doing a crime to all the women of Mexico, hiding his face for just a moment. He also wrestles in a way that he claims to have the strength of a MUCH bigger man. Background Information on Wrestler: (everything you can think of) Pulga is...oh, I'd say about 26 years old, and therefore has been wrestling in Mexico for 32 years. Seriously, Pulga has been small all of his life, and was teased endlessly in school for his small stature. This, in turn, made him more aggressive, almost to the point of making him slightly delierous, believing that he is a much larger man than he actually is. As for Pulga's beauty fetish, Pulga wrestled for one match under a mask, but the mask was extremely ugly, and did not fit well. Pulga struggled, and the promoter at the time told him never to wear the mask again. Not wanting to hurt his feelings, as his mother had made him the mask, the promoter told him that he shouldn't wear the mask because his "beautiful face would be hidden from all to see" Previous/Current Titles: (just so we know) none (I just made him up three days ago) Entrance: (how the wrestler acts and behaves while approaching the ring) Pulga is extremely cocky. He wears a flowing, gaudy ringrobe to ringside, and gets angry when fans try to touch him, except for the beautiful women. He especially insults ugly women who may want to touch him and any man who may be larger than him trying to taunt him, yelling "No hay hombre más grande que La Pulga Gigante!" Theme Music: (no WWF or WCW themes, please) (Still to come...I'm doing the research now) Three Strengths/Talents of the wrestler: 1) Ring psychology: Pulga is devious, and is very smart when it comes to his actions in the ring. He actually knows what he's doing while there. Pulga does little things while wrestling that can annoy or throw an opponent off his gameplan. 2) Speed and agility: Pulga is a small man by all means, but is very quick. His agility can counter many moves (ie. landing on his feet in a release German suplex), and his speed can lead to quick chains of moves unleashed before the opponent has time to react. 3) Surprising strength: Pulga has actually worked on his strength, having in the past failed miserably in attempting to do his power moves. Now, he's able to pull off many of the moves, but still can not execute them with any impact. Still, if he continues to work at his strength, he will possibly, in the future, pull off a lot more of these. As it stands now, Pulga has great strength for a man his size. Three Weaknesses/Flaws of the wrestler: 1) Wrestling style: Pulga wrestles the style that is more fitted for a wrestler twice his size. Therefore, he does not execute moves properly, and does not have the offence or defence that a more fitting style would gaive him. 2) Cocky: Pulga thinks he is God's gift to wrestling, and with women. He has a tendency to lose focus on the match once he gets on a roll, and it takes him not only out of his game plan, but can get him pounded in the process. 3) Drinking: Pulga drinks a lot after the matches (to cure the pain), and can often be found hungover the day before an important match. Nothing serious, he just plays through the pain. One thinks that this may affect him someday, though, whether it be through a missed spot or two to a missed few matches due to a bar fight. Finishing Manoeuvre: (please be as descriptive as possible, including s ituations where the move is usually attempted, possible set-ups etc.) La Gota De la Pulga: Literally translated, it's the "Flea Drop", a high angle senton from off the top rope, but instead of the back landing on the opponents chest and face, it's more of a tightly tucked drop, dropping Pulga's ass on the opponents face with full impact. 5 Signature Manoeuvres: (please list 5 moves or holds that aren't necessarily finishers, but are "part" of the character.. Examples include Flair's chop and Cactus Jack's elbowdrop off the apron.) 1) Pulgaslam (chokeslam): The "finisher" that will never seem to get pulled off, unless the opponent is smaller than him. Pulga always threatens to use it, though. 2) Slap: Pulga will back an opponent into the corner, then slap him hard across the face, yelling "Nadie es tan hermoso como La Pulga Gigante!" (No one is as beautiful as The Giant Flea!) 3) Butt-butt: Pulga will throw the opponent into the ropes and hit him with his ass, knocking the opponent down, hopefully. After doing it, Pulga will rub his butt with a smile on his face. If the opponent is too big, Pulga will bounce off, and rub his ass as if it hurt. 4) Pulga Strut: Not actually a move, Pulga loves to strut in the middle of the ring to piss off his opponent. It's kind of a mutant combination of Parka's strut fused with Syxx's rapid groin chops at the end 5) Pulga Pose: Also not a move, it's the way Pulga pins an easily defeated opponent. He places his foot on the opponents face, and tilts back with his arms outstretched (as Lyger has done on entrances) for the three count. Common Move-set: (please list at least 20 moves or holds that the wrestler frequently uses, giving a clear indication of the wrestler's style and tendencies.) 1) Japanese Cyclone Suplex 2) choke slam 3) lariat 4) release tiger suplex 5) bulldog 6) rolling head scissors 7) K driller (sitting tombstone, ie. Owen on Austin) 8) Rocker Dropper 9) hurricanrana 10) release power bomb 11) La Magistral cradle 12) senton 13) Jumping DDT 14) Butt-butt off ropes 15) Face rake using boots 16) Somersault Drop (moonsault into senton) 17) Fujinami-style dropkick to back 18) tilt-a-whirl slam 19) rolling leg lariat 20) enzugiri 21) powerslam 22) legdrop off top rope 23) railing plancha 24) flying tope to the floor 25) Asai butt-butt (I couldn't think of a better name...he runs at the second rope, and bounces back into a butt-butt) Anything else you might like to add?: Um...nope, but feel free to direct any questions to me :) Manager Information =============== (note - managers seem to be rather rare in Lucha Libre for some reason.
