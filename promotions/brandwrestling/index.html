BRAND Wrestling, a PTC-network website federation whose champions page names SHARC and Jake Keeton titleholders in the autumn of 2008, ran its Chainsaw Massacre pay-per-view on 7 February 2009. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

BRAND Wrestling ran a singles and teams roster, three championships, and a backstage locker system where each wrestler submitted their own promos and segments under their own character’s own folder. Its own links page bannered New Frontier Wrestling, PRIME and Primetime Central among its affiliates, placing it inside that network. By the autumn of 2008 its own champions page named SHARC the BRAND Name Champion and Jake Keeton the Mountain Dew Champion, with its BRAND Texas Heavyweight Championship vacant; no lineage survives behind any of the three belts, whose history pages drew on a news database that hasn’t survived.

Its backstage board opened on 2 November 2008 with a rule against using HTML and an invitation to post. Two wrestlers used the locker system to cut promos that November: Mary-Lynn Mayweather challenged Jake Keeton for the Mountain Dew Championship in “Church Bells Calling,” and Steve Solex, after losing his debut, vowed revenge on Keeton for the same belt in “Booked or Not” before turning to a stand-alone piece of character development, “Why?,” at the end of the month.

On 13 November 2008 the board announced a move to a monthly, ROH-style schedule for lack of interest and movement, paired with an in-fiction ownership angle giving J. Leslie Voss full control of the promotion. The next day, the handler behind Jake Keeton posted that the character had never been his own angle-fed character and stepped back from the roster.

Its Chainsaw Massacre pay-per-view is dated to 7 February 2009 by its own splash page, with no card or results surviving for it. How BRAND Wrestling ended isn’t in the surviving record.
