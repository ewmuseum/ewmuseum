The status of BRAND wrestling

13 November 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A locker board post announcing a move to a monthly schedule and an in-fiction ownership angle for J. Leslie Voss.
Posted by: VossMan
Date: 13 November 2008

Steve and I have decided that due to lack of interest and lack of movement at the station we’re going to run BRAND wrestling in a ROH style capacity. With a monthly show (aimed for) instead of this weekly thing. We’re going to run the ownership angle a little bit harder in order to prepare for this with J. Leslie Voss taking complete 100% ownership of BRAND wrestling with Pepper being signed as a wrestler.

If you wish to be a part of BRAND wrestling you can. We are going to be running all conversation through this board from now on so if you’re involved you can see this.

If you’re not then don’t worry about it.

Thanks for the fun times.
