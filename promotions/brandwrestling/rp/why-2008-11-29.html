BRAND Wrestling — Roleplay

Why?

29 November 2008

Starring: Steve Solex
Also Starring:
For: Character Development
Date: 29 November 2008

“I told you not to order that shit,” I tell my wife after I see the boxes upon boxes of useless crap sitting on the porch.

“But...but, it just seemed like such a good deal!” She attempts to justify her irresponsible behavior.

“Woman, I told your ass a hundred freaking times. If you are going to order in bulk, like this, you better have a good place to store all that shit. Apparently, since it’s still on the porch, you haven’t even thought of a place,” I inform her.

“Well, I was thinking we could put it in the basement,” She attempts.

“You know damn well none of that crap is going in the basement. That’s where all my wrestling shit is at,” I proclaim at a subtle yell.

She begins to stomp her feet on the ground like some sort of adolescent not getting her way. She then puts her fingers in her ears and storms out of the room.

“What are you? Five years old?!” I shout as she continues stomping around the house.

I walk over to the coat hanger and secure my black leather coat. I put it on and open the front door. Before I exit the house I look around and listen for my wife. She’s still stomping around, and even though I can’t see her...I’m sure her fingers are in her ears. I walk out of the door and close it behind me. I pause on the porch and take a look at the boxes scattered about. I haven’t counted them, but I would say there is about 50 boxes out there.

“50 fucking boxes of lotion,” I mumble to myself.

I walk down the steps and across the front yard to the garage. I pull the keys out to my black 2008 Ford F150 and hit the button on the garage remote. The garage goes up and I see my truck. I hit the button for the key less start and the engine begins to rumble. I walk up to the truck and get into the driver’s seat. I shut the door after I’ve fastened my safety belt and put the truck into reverse. I back out of the drive way, when suddenly it hits me. I stop the truck and ask myself:

“Where the hell am I going?”
