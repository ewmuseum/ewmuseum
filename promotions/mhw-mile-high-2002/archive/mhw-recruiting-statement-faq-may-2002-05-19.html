[MHW] Recruiting Statement + FAQ

19 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's been a while since I've posted one of these. Anyway, here goes. Mile High Wrestling is a regional fed that's been running for over a month. We know, it's not much. But give us a chance. With stars like "The Atomic Son" Sean Malcomb, Da' OutKast, Frank "The Falcon" Peregrine, and more, you won't regret it. The website is at http://milehigh.cjb.net It's being reconstructed at the moment but the key areas are there. The roster, rankings, FAQ, and rumors pages are up. We are updating the site all the time. The FAQ just took an upgrade today. The MHW's first PPV is Sunday, June 30th. There will be a mini-tourney on that card to decide the first MHW Champion. The top 4 records in the fed get to compete in that tourney. Join us and maybe you will make it. Normally I just post a link to the FAQ. Today, I'm going to make it easy for you. Here it is. MILE HIGH WRESTLING FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS VERSION 1.5 (c) 2002, Mile High Sports Inc. TABLE OF CONTENTS 1. What is the MHW? 2. Who are the real people behind the MHW? 3. Who are the kayfabe people behind the MHW? 4. What are the rules in the MHW? 5. What about in-rings? 6. How do I win matches? 7. What shows does the MHW hold? 8. What about PPVs? 9. What titles are up for grabs in the MHW? 10. How do I join? ***************** 1. What is the MHW? ***************** Mile High Wrestling is a regional promotion based in Denver, Colorado. Though it does travel around to various locations in Colorado, it will not venture past state lines. Its Headquarters is located in Denver itself. Although we are mainly an e-mail fed, i.e., roleplays are sent to the fedhead ( [detail withheld] ), and although posts going out to the fed are sent via e-mail, packets, as well as cards, are put straight up on the website. Members who are signed on for the mailing list receive a direct link to those posts. We don't simulate matches. They are hand-written using play-by-play and block format. *********************************** 2. Who are the real people behind the MHW? *********************************** Currently there is only one real staff member in the MHW. [name withheld] will perform all tasks required to make the MHW function like a well oiled machine. He designs the MHW website, books matches, etc. Hopefully, with the gradual increase in the MHW's roster, he will get additional help in running the MHW. ************************************** 3. Who are the kayfabe people behind the MHW? ************************************** * William L. Bradford - MHW Owner - Born a spoiled rich kid, Bradford was practically spoonfed his fortunes. All he cares about is money. He's only doing this for the money. If something doesn't work in his best interests, and in some way doesn't line his pockets, he throws a fit. He has no respect for his employees whatsoever. Either they do what he wants or they're out. * Rusty Waters - Lead Play-by-Play Man - Rusty is the head commentator for the MHW's flagship program, Firestorm. He's a real country boy who has traditional values. He doesn't like cheating, hardcore matches, and the like. He's the MHW's face commentator. * "Slick" Ronnie Vick - Color Commentary - The heel of the duo, Ronnie also works the Firestorm show. He loves the things that Rusty hates and more. If somebody cheats, he'll easily turn a blind eye to it. He's cocky and often annoying. * Dave "The Rave" Madison - Ring Announcer - Dave is called "The Rave" because girls come to the arena just to see him. He is immensely popular with them because he is a local pop music singer in a boy band. He was hired for this job because of his speaking ability and popularity. * Rebecca Howard - Lead Reporter - Rebecca is a professional but that doesn't stop her from flirting to get the hot stories. The faces practically drool over her. She occasionally does personal favors to get the big stories. Favors like accompanying them to the ring. * Alan Woods - Reporter - Alan is a former wrestler who never made it big. So now he reports for the MHW. He's a heel reporter who absolutely despises Rebecca for getting the big bucks. However, he secretly admires her for her good looks. * Lenny "The Leech" Lorenzo - Reporter - Lenny is probably the most annoying person you'll ever meet. He can't stay away from the wrestlers. He's always begging for interviews, promos, and the like. Everyone hates him. The only way he gets a story is by evesdropping on someone else's conversations. * Trey Philips - Head of Security - Trey is basically just a big redneck. He likes bullying people around, especially fans. He takes pleasure in beating down anyone who stands up to him. He's like a dog on a leash. If Bradford says "jump," Trey jumps. Bradford can't stand him but knows he does his job very well. * Jake Farrell - Head Referee - Hacob is the most down-to-earth referee there is. Most of the faces enjoy his company. Still, he remains strictly unbiased. He follows his own set of strict rules on how to conduct himself in the ring. He knows that the fans came to see entertaining matches, and so he won't throw one out without reasonable cause to do so. Jacob is primarily a Main Event referee. * Joey Caprelli - Backup Referee - Joey is the son of a Mob boss in Denver. The mob realized that there's big money in the MHW, so they forced Bradford to make Joey an employee. Bradford decided to make him a referee. Joeyis easily bribed. More money for the family. He's as corrupt a referee as they come. He mainly referees mid-card matches. * Ryan Miller - Backup Referee - Ryan is a learned student of the hardcore style. He knows the rules inside-out. These kinds of matches are his bread and butter. He is usually used for card-openers and hardcore matches. *************************** 4. What are the rules in the MHW? *************************** Roleplay at least once per week. Stay Active. Contribute to angles and feuds. Be creative and original. Use your spellchecker when roleplaying. Keep swearing to a minimum. This is family television, not an R rated movie. Refrain from using racism, extensive nudity, or other offensive materials in your roleplays. Very brief nudity is allowed but only in very rare instances. Use only what is acceptable in the WWF. Occasional brief t & a shots are acceptable but don't go overboard. Don't rip off other people's ideas, including characters and angles from real and/or fantasy wrestling. Don't just drop out without telling me. If you must, let me know and I'll put your character "On Hiatus." ****************** 5. What about in-rings? ****************** I don't want to see a ton of in-rings for a card. You are limited to only one in-ring per card. Make it count. Anything else, such as interviews, should be done backstage. In-rings will not hold any bearing on the outcome of your matches. Also, in-rings are not to be included in your ring entrance. You don't see wrestlers get airtime right before their match in the WWE. So I won't accept it either. ******************** 6. How do I win matches? ******************** First of all, strategies are HIGHLY encouraged, but will not affect your chances of winning a match in any way. They merely assist a writer in writing your match. Winners are decided based mainly on roleplays. Angles can also be taken into consideration. If the two parties involved agree to a pre-determined outcome to a match, then that outcome will happen. Roleplays are especially useful for title matches and Pay Per Views. They will be judged on realism (10%), readability (30%), and creativity (60%). Longevity will not be a factor in this. It is possible for your roleplay to be shorter than your opponents and you still win, but only if your performance in these three categories outperforms that of your opponent. **************************** 7. What shows does the MHW hold? **************************** The MHW has cut down to one show a week. This may change over time. The current show is Firestorm. It will air on Mondays. It will have 5 to 6 matches fully written in play-by-play format. Here is a rundown of the weekly schedule. Sunday - Off day for writing matches Monday - MHW Firestorm and Update due out Tuesday - Flashes due by Midnight Central time Wednesday - Spotlight Series due out. Strats / In-rings due in by Midnight Central time Thursday - Flashes due by Midnight as usual Friday - Spotlight Series due out Saturday - off day (you get the idea) Occasionally we will hold a Fuel show. The schedule will remain the same except for the fact that Fuel will be held on Sundays rather than Mondays. ***************** 8. What about PPVs? ***************** Pay Per Views will be held on the last Sunday of every other month. The entire week before it will be used for writing the matches. PPV names will be announced when the time arrises. Make sure to get yourselves an angle going that'll reach a climax at the next PPV. I can not come up with all of them. ********************************* 9. What titles are up for grabs in the MHW? ********************************* At the moment we only have two titles. However, more will be made available as our roster grows. MHW Heavyweight Championship MHW Tag Team Champions ************** 10. How do I join? ************** Fill out the following application, copy it into your e-mail program, and e-mail it to [detail withheld] The entire application must be completed in full before it can even be considered.
