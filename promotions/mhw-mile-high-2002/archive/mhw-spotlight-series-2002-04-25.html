[MHW] Spotlight Series [4/26/02]

25 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
--Mile High Wrestling Mailing List-- [MHW] Spotlight Series [4/26/02] MHW Firestorm Live from Colorado State University at Fort Collins Monday, April 29, 2002 Triple Threat Match "Premium" River Rockwell VS. "Gorgeous and Young" Lance Romance VS. Czar -- Czar is a newcomer who is currently under a probationary contract. Can he prove himself worthy enough to compete in the MHW on a full-time basis? Also, rumors have been circulating that Lance Romance is... you know... "GAY!" How will this affect the other two's gameplan going into this matchup? Singles Match "The Asian Sensation" Xu Jing VS. "Servant" Robert -- Both of these men are managed by Englishmen. What does that mean? How will these managers instruct their clients to approach this match? Both are the smallest competitors in the MHW. Tag Team Match Breaking and Entering VS. Sean Malcom/Thane Sloan -- Sloan and Malcomb aren't a natural tag team together. MHW Owner William Bradford decided to put them together to see how they'll do against the local boys. Malcomb and Sloan hold the size advantage. How will that affect the matchup? Tonight in the Spotlight: "The Atomic Son" Sean Malcomb "Premium" River Rockwell "The Asian Sensation" Xu Jing "Gorgeous and Young" Lance Romance Czar ***************************** "The Atomic Son" Sean Malcomb ***************************** [The scene opens up in the new penthouse apartment of Sean and Jennifer Malcomb. The living room is set up well after the move, with a large screen television centered against one wall, a large recliner at an angle to the right, a very cushiony couch facing it in the center of the room, a couple end tables on its sides and a lamp on one of them. A coffee table inbetween the television and a few magazines scattered about on it. A stationery desk stands against one wall. Jennifer Malcomb is seen buttoning the last buttons of a white blouse as she walks across the living room towards the bedroom door, she is also sporting a black, knee-length skirt. As she exits to the bedroom, before the door closes, Sean Malcomb steps out in a black three-piece suit with a black collared shirt and a white tie. He runs his hand along cheeks to check his shave around his thin goatee. A knock comes to the door and his head turns to the entrance to the spacious apartment. He walks up and opens the door.] Female voice: Hello, Mr. Malcomb! Sean Malcomb: Um, have we met before? Female Voice: Oh, not before now, no. I'm Rebecca Howard, head reporter for Mile High Wrestling. Sean Malcomb: Oh, well come on in then... [Sean holds the door more open and then Rebecca Howard enters the room, brushing a few strands of her blonde hair from her face, and straightens her tan blouse, which has a couple of the top buttons undone. A cameraman follows in closely behind her and points the large device right at Sean Malcomb. He removes one hand from the camera to close the door behind him and then places the hand back on the camera.] Rebecca Howard: Mr. Malcomb, we have noticed-- Sean Malcomb: Umm, please, just call me Sean. Rebecca Howard: Sean, we at the-- Sean Malcomb: Listen, would you like to have a seat. If this is what I think it is, I'd much rather be sitting down. Rebecca Howard: Oh. Very well. [Rebecca gives a large smile to Sean and follows him across the room. Sean motions to the couch for the two members of the Mile High Wrestling reporting staff. Rebecca straightens her brown skirts before sitting down, which runs just above her knees after she takes her seat, at the point of the couch that is less than arms reach from the recliner if she leans towards it. The cameraman takes a place next to Rebecca on the couch and keeps the camera pointed at Malcomb. Sean himself takes a seat in the recliner.] Rebecca Howard: Sean, you are the highest paid wrestler at the Mile High organization. You are the center of the marketing campaigns, and yet we noticed that you haven't yet done an official interview. So, I thought that now you've settled into your apartment here in the Denver area, we could finally get a comfortable sit down with what Mile High expects to be its biggest star. Sean Malcomb: Biggest star? I don't know about that. I'm good at what I do, but I don't expect to be the biggest-- Rebecca Howard: But you are a reknowned champion. You've been around the world and back with gold from other organizations, is it not right to assume you could be the most explosive talent to hit the MHW? Sean Malcomb: Well, I'm big. I can be explosive. And I've got talent. But I don't see myself as anyone that big. If you can get a few fans in there by selling my image, do it. If I can give the people a great show, I'll give it my best. I may have won a few belts before, but this is a new start... [Rebecca leans forward and shoots Malcomb a large smile. Only human, Sean's eyes shoot down Rebecca's blouse for an instant, but shaking his head he looks back at her eyes as she reaches to run her hand on his bicep.] Rebecca Howard: You've kept in good shape, you're still talented in the ring. The loss to Rockwell didn't seem to throw you any, you can handle a loss. You come out of a bad situation fighting. I don't see any reason why someone so strong, skilled, and handsome as you wouldn't be the biggest star. Sean Malcomb: Handsome? Heh, I don't know about that. I don't know if I really want to be the biggest star. I want to go out and compete. I want to be the best, but I can handle it if someone is the better man sometimes. It's a sport. You have to have good sportsmanship. Rebecca Howard: Oh, aren't you just the perfect man? Athletic, handsome, modest... Jenn Malcomb(from bedroom): Sean! Who was that at the door? [Sean Malcomb's head turned to face the closed bedroom door, and so does Rebecca's but she remains in her position with her hand running along Malcomb's bicep.] Sean Malcomb: Rebecca Howard from MHW. She's here to get an interview. Jenn Malcomb(from bedroom): Should I come out there? [Sean looks towards Rebecca. Rebecca thinks for moment and then turns to the door.] Rebecca Howard: No, thank you, Mrs. Malcomb. We're just here for a quick exclusive with Sean. We'll be out of here in a couple minutes. Jenn Malcomb(from bedroom): Alright. Let me know if you change your mind. Rebecca Howard: I'll do that. Thank you. [Rebecca turns her head back to Sean and sits back in the couch again and straightens her blouse. Sean then turns his head to face Rebecca once again.] Rebecca Howard: Well, back to where we were. This Monday, you're in the second straight main event, when we go to Fort Collins for Firestorm. But this isn't a main event you're used to. The MHW has teamed you up with Thane Sloan and put you in the debut match of Breaking and Entering. How are you going to adjust to this tag format after being out of wrestling for so long? Sean Malcomb: I'm going to say right off the bat, I don't like it. I never have liked to participate in tag matches. But if the MHW thinks this is best for not only me, but the rest of the league.... I'll give it a shot, why not? Hell, I'm just trying to get my footing out there. I think I could pull it off. Just hope I get used to having that extra man there so I don't try and overwork myself. I can't be getting too beat up before that world title is up for grabs. Rebecca Howard: Yes, the world title. So that is your ultimate ambition here? Sean Malcomb: With it being the only singles title...it's the only ambition outside of just plain competing. If I can't go for the world title, what else can I go for? Rebecca Howard: Very true. Well, is there anything we can expect to see from "The Atomic Son" in the weeks to come? Sean Malcomb: Just action. I plan to give it my all. Hopefully me and Sloan can find a balance and work with it. Maybe you'll see that star that you seem to want. I'm just hoping for a win and the crowd to have a good time. Rebecca Howard: Well, we at Mile High Wrestling and the fans at home, I'm sure, are looking forward to what we will see from you, Sean. Good luck. Sean Malcomb: Thank you. Rebecca Howard: Alright, cut the feed. Thank you, Sean. Sean Malcomb: You are very welcome. [The three of them stand and walk towards the door. Sean opens the door for the cameraman to exit and Rebecca Howard stops and lays a hand on Malcomb's chest. He shoots her a confused look, and Rebecca just smiles at him.] Rebecca Howard: This was fun. I hope we get to work together quite a bit while you're here in Colorado. Sean Malcomb: Umm....I'm sure we will. I don't plan on going anywhere soon. Rebecca Howard: Well then, it looks like this'll be a very fun job... Give your wife my regards. [Rebecca stands up on her toes and gives a small peck on Sean's cheek then smiles and exits. Sean closes the door behind her. He still has the confused look on his face. Jennifer Malcomb walks out of the bedroom in a conservative black and white skirt suit, fixing her earrings.] Jenn Malcomb: Did it go alright? Sean Malcomb: Yeah, I think it did.... I guess. Jenn Malcomb: Well, that's good. You almost ready? Sean Malcomb: Huh? Jenn Malcomb: For the meeting with Mr. Bradford, remember? We have to discuss the marketing campaign today. Sean Malcomb: Oh, yeah. Just waiting on you. Jenn Malcomb: Smart ass. [Jennifer shoots a smile at her husband and steps into the bedroom. She emerges in a few seconds with a black purse. Sean reaches in his jacket pocket and pulls out a set of keys. The couple exchange a little chit chat as they exit and lock the front door to their penthouse apartment. The scene then fades to black.] ************************ "Premium" River Rockwell ************************ [The scene opens in the checkout line of a grocery store. We see a man emptying the contents of his cart onto the conveyer belt. This man is "Premium" River Rockwell. As he unloads his cart onto the checkout line, he makes sure no one is looking and quickly pockets a pack of Wintergreen chewing gum that was on clearance for .10. He allows himself a smug grin at having outsmarted the system, and steps up to the cashier, who is a young man in his late teens.] Cashier : Did you find everything alright? [River, caught off gaurd by being spoken to, looks at the cashier for a second before responding.] River Rockwell : Oh... uh, yeah. Except the toilet paper should be in aisle 4. Who looks in aisle 8 for toilet paper? Cashier : Oh... well, it's with the rest of the toiletries. River Rockwell : Which also should be in aisle 4. [rolls his eyes and says "duh" under his breath.] Cashier : Uh, sure. Sorry bout that sir. River Rockwell : Don't worry about it, my good man. Nothing can ruin my mood. I'm trying to live a happy life, and not even a grocery clerk can ruin that! [The clerk stops what he's doing and looks at Rockwell.] Cashier : Hey, aren't you River Rockwell? River Rockwell : Why, yes I am. I have time for a quick signature if you'd like. Cashier : Oh... no, it's just that I saw you on the news last night. They were doin' a show on that new wrestlin' promotion. River Rockwell : Oh, well yeah. I'm a star in Mile High Wrestling, if that's what you mean. Cashier : Some lady was on it. She played back some of your interview clips. Are you gay? River Rockwell : I just told you I am. I'm trying to be happy as I can be. A happy man is a prosperous man. Cashier : No, I mean homosexual. Like Lance Romance. He's a homosexual, you know. River Rockwell : No, he's not... he's just... uh... he's... [Suddenly everything that Rebecca Howard, the MHW Reporter, said to him the other night begins to replay in extreme-fast-forward.] River Rockwell : uh... [He remembers : Rebecca Howard : There's rumors that he's gay.] River Rockwell : um... oh man. Cashier : Paper or plastic? [Suddenly, it hits Rockwell like a brick in the face what Howard was saying to him the previous night. And it hits like a brick in the gut when he remembers his responses. He can't breathe, and suddenly finds his eyes wide with horror.] River Rockwell : My interview... was on... the... news...? Cashier : Yeah. You know, you're not nearly as flamboyant as that Lance Romance fellow. Man, he must be packin' in the dudes after the show the way they showed him prancing around the ring. River Rockwell : Uggggghh.... I need to go. [River walks out of the grocery store in total and complete disgust leaving his groceries in the checkout line. As he leaves, he mutters a sentance under his breath.] River Rockwell : I'm going to kill Rebecca Howard. [Scene ends.] ***************************** "The Asian Sensation" Xu Jing ***************************** [The camera opens once again on the apartment of William Waterford, but this time the scene is noticeably different. Now, it is dark outside, and it would be pitch black inside the room were it not for all the lights of downtown Denver shining into the room. The alarm clock on the nightstand provides the only other light in the room. It reads 2:24 A.M.] [A silhouette of a short, round man appears against the lights provided by the city. We can only assume that this is Waterford. He appears to be speaking on the phone, both from the appearance of the silhouette and the fact that he's talking in the dark.] [However, we cannot understand a word that he is saying--he is speaking Chinese. Soon, his silhouette disappears in a cloud of smoke. As he does, the camera fades out.] [After a few seconds of darkness, the camera fades in on another place--the non-bedroom in Xu Jing's two-room house. Here, it is pitch black outside due to the rural environment, but Jing's house is illuminated by a lamp inside the room. Jing has the phone up to his ear, and, like Waterford earlier, he is having a conversation in Chinese.] [This room is quite different from Waterford's apartment. Besides the obvious difference in setting, there are other differences that are easy to spot. Jing is seated on a torn-up sofa as he chats on the phone. A 13-inch TV is on the opposite side of the tiny room. The room is also much less tidy, as Jing has things lying everywhere. Among them, the most obvious is a life-size dummy. It lies in a heap on the floor, and the stuffing is falling out of a giant tear in the head. The dummy appears to have taken its share of damage in other areas as well. Also on the opposite side of the room is a dartboard, which has a sheet of paper in front of it. The sheet of paper has Chinese characters written on it--once again, we cannot tell what it says. Does it say "Robert?" "Thane Sloan?" "MHW?" "William Waterford?"] [Next to the dartboard is a calendar, which has pictures of puppies on the pages. The camera zooms in to have a closer view of the calendar. Noticeably written in English on every day is the word "TRAIN." What good is a word written in English for a man who can only read Chinese? And who wrote it? But most of all, how has Jing been getting this message if the word is written in English?] [The camera zooms out as Jing's phone conversation continues. Now the camera zooms in on a sheet of paper on the floor. This is a copy of the latest MHW update--in English. What good does a man who speaks only Chinese have for things written in English?] [Finally, the camera zooms out and shows one final shot of the room. It then fades out.] ********************************** "Gorgeous and Young" Lance Romance ********************************** [The scene opens up in a lavished looking living room where a man sits on a white plush couch, a phone is pressed to his ear as he looks down at a rubick cube he plays with trying to get all the colors aligned. He wears a white wifebeater that's tucked into extremely short cut off shorts, his blonde curly hair is pulled away from his face as he speaks up his voice containing a very slight lisp] Lance: You're silly...no George there's no way I'll be able to make it!...Yeah, it's going to be great, I'll send you a video tape. It's just another small step to greatness that we all know that I'm destined for! [He frowns as he looks at the 80's toy and sighs a bit] Lance: Oh nothing...this rubick cube...I can never get all the colors straight... [he throws it half heartedly across the room and holds the phone to his ear with a smile on his lips] Lance: So you'll call me later right?...You better you bitch! I have some of the... [He glances over seeing the camera and the MHW crew in his living room and blinks] Lance: I have my public here....call me later...alright be good! [Lance quickly hangs up the phone and tucks it into the cracks of the chair] Lance: That was...uh...no one! But it's about time you arrived... [Romance slides a hand through his hair bringing it back around and letting a few strands hang infront of his face] Lance: It's none other then I Lance Romance...the Georgous and Young one himself, the lady's delight, the sweetest thing since twinkies! Now all of you have to have one thiing in mind, why is God's Gift to Women here in the MHW....well the answer is simple, because I like Denver! Denver is a great city! The people suck, but hey ya win some you loose some, and while I'm here in the Mile High City...I have an opportunity to send my wrestling career through the roof, and that's what I'm gonna do because I like to be on top! [Lance grins as he slides his hand over his smoothy chin and cheek, he looks seductively into the camera] Lance: But it's never that easy...and this Firestorm is just an example of that...my 1st match here is going to be a threesome...myself, the Czar and River Rockwell. Now I've seen Rockwell in action and I hear what I have to say and you know what, he's not a bad guy, so I'm gonna have a real issue fighting him tooth and nails, but the Czar...he's just an upstart who's trying to make a name for himself at the expense of 2 wrestling studs like Rockwell and myself...I don't think so mister, it's not gonna happen this week...not at my expense bub! [Lance waves his finger back and forth and shakes his head vigorously] Lance: So I can tell you this buddy, you're not going to get past Rockwell and you're certainly not getting past me, that's a fact! I've got no problem laying you down on the mat, turning you over and then making you beg for me to stop...with the most painfull hold ever...EVER know to man, woman or child...the SanFrancisco Treat Forever! [giggles] But I don't think I'll even have a chance to use it on you Czar...River is going to run through you like a bad Brad Pitt movie! [Lance licks his lips casually and grins] Lance: But until then...I've got calls to make...people to get ahold of...I'm a Superstar and my time's limited and you're just cutting into it! Shoo! Go now! [Lance waves the cameras out with the back of his hands as the camera slowly fades to black, Lance picks up the phone and puts it to his ear once again dialing] **** Czar **** (there is nothing but the sounds of horns honking along manhattan blvd. and some birds chirping in Central Park. The camera glides from the beautiful park to the alley ways and slums of the upper west side..There we see Czar, his hair a mess, his clothes a mess, wearing sneakers and levis, a sports coat and a wrinkled dress shirt. Beside Czar is a young hipster with a bandana tied around his neck and giant sunglasses on. Next to that man is a grizzly, older man, wearing the same rag tag look as Czar, and growing a giant beard which is half white and half brown. Hes going bald as well. The three men are each smoking cigarettes and staring at a dumpster.....) Old Man - So, I woke up, again...which is nice. At my age its great to wake up. Hipster - Man, oh man. This town has become so synthetic since when i first moved in, man. The Village was the happening spot, you know. And slums were true slums, man, with great minds trying to make it, man. Now its all, like Hollywood type stuff. Everyone here who is a "bum" has a pocket full of hundred dollar bills. I can barely breath if you know what i mean. Czar - Ive got to take a flight out to Colorado tonight. I didnt think id actually be stepping back in the ring.....they inticed me to. Old Man - Howd they do that? Czar - They pissed me off. They told me i could only wrestle for em if i could show how good i truly was. Hipster - How? Czar - They got me wrestling two men in my first match...in my debut. Can you believe it? As if my trophy case filled with polished and gleaming gold titles, belts, trophies, plaques....as if that werent enough for em. No matter what i do, Im seen as an upstart. Old Man - Get used to it. Im in my sixties....been here too long.....im still considered an upstart by most peoples accounts. It never ends, my friend. Hipster- Two other men, man? Well- Who are they? Czar - Well, one is into homoerotic porno...i think....maybe....no ones sure.... Old Man - The other? Czar - You know the type. He's young, fairly decent in the ring...so all of a sudden he thinks hes the best damn thing ever to hit the sport. He drinks beer and orders people around and thinks he can pysch me out....saying my contract was null because i had to prove something to him. Hipster - You dont gotta prove anything to him, man. Czar - No. Youre Wrong. I do have to prove something to him. Ive gotta prove something to him....something to the queer kid...and something to the whole damn MHW....if they want to walk into my ring...my den....and they want to poke the bear with a stick....well, they shouldnt be surprised if the bear wakes up and slaps em across the face with his claws. So River Rockwell.... Old Man - Who? Czar - River Rockwell...thats the guys name that i have to fight...River Rockwell....i know, i havent heard of him either. Hipster - River Rockwell? Isnt that a painting by Norman Rockwell? Czar - No, but i can tell the two are related... Old Man - How so? Czar - Theyre both sketchy at best...and hyped up way too much. Both Norman and River belong on a coffee table of an old woman, free for gazing by the bridge club. Hipster - Far out, man. Old Man - HAHAHAHAH (coughs and gags and stops laughing). Czar - River Rockwell....Lance Romance...heh....these two kids got something comin, and theyre too ignorant to see it. Theres a storm abrewin and there aint no umbrellas for these two children. (after a pause as the men smoke and stare at the dumpster more) Im glad....you know, that MHW put me on a developmental contract. (the two look at him confused) Yeah. Neither of these kids....well, none of the kids in the whole damn state of Colorado are gonna know what hits em once i smack em between the teeth. And Lance....River.......when i slam you on the mat, hit you with a Czar Splash or two or five......and when youre staring at the Rocky Mountains.....dont feel alone. Youll be wonderin what everyone else in the fed'll be wonderin....... how the hell the developmental guy beat made a fool out of you. Old Man - Well, you know what they say about you....ol buddy.... Hipster - That youre one cool cat? Czar - No. Im HALF MAN.......HALF AMAZING.... Hipster - OUTTA SIGHT!! (The camera pans away and we look at the Upper Westside of NYC before it fades to black) --(c) Copyright 2002, Mile High Sports Inc.--
