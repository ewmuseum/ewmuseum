^MHW^ Title Tournament Free For All - Mile High Rumble

23 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Okay, maybe I'm just impatient, or maybe its bad recruiting. Whatever. Mountain High Wrestling has been open 2 days now and we haven't received 1 application. Not one! So here's the deal. The first 8 singles wrestlers who join will have a guaranteed spot in the Singles Tournament for the MHW Heavyweight Title.. The 4 tag teams will partake in a match of their own for the MHW Tag Team Titles. First, the 8 singles wrestlers will be put into random tag teams. MHW presents: MILE HIGH RUMBLE Live from the Pepsi Center in Denver, CO Date TBA MATCH 1: SINGLES TOURNAMENT QUARTERFINALS 4 team Over-The-Top Elimination Tag Match Teams randomly selected. 4 people must be eliminated for this match to end. MATCH 2: TAG TEAM TOURNAMENT: SEMIFINALS 4-way Tag Team Tables Match - Both members of a team have to go through a table for the team to be eliminated. 2 teams must be eliminated for this match to end. MATCH 3: SINGLES TOURNAMENT: SEMIFINALS 4 Way No DQ Elimination Match - Anything Goes 2 people must be eliminated for this match to end. MATCH 4: TAG TEAM TOURNAMENT: FINALS Tag Team Cage Match for MHW Tag Team Titles Both members of a team have to escape the cage in order to win the match and the titles. MATCH 5: SINGLES TOURNAMENT: FINALS Last Man Standing Match for the MHW Heavyweight Title. Please join the MHW. This card promises to be one for the ages. Synthetic67 MHW Owner http://milehigh.cjb.net
