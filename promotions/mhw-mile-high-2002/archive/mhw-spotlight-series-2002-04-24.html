[MHW] Spotlight Series [4/24/02]

--Mile High Wrestling Mailing List-- [MHW] Spotlight Series [4/24/02] MHW Firestorm Live from Colorado State University at Fort Collins Monday, April 29, 2002 Triple Threat Match "Premium" River Rockwell VS. "Gorgeous and Young" Lance Romance VS. Czar -- Czar is a newcomer who is currently under a probationary contract. Can he prove himself worthy enough to compete in the MHW on a full-time basis? Also, rumors have been circulating that Lance Romance is... you know... "GAY!" How will this affect the other two's gameplan going into this matchup? Singles Match "The Asian Sensation" Xu Jing VS. "Servant" Robert -- Both of these men are managed by Englishmen. What does that mean? How will these managers instruct their clients to approach this match? Both are the smallest competitors in the MHW. Tag Team Match Breaking and Entering VS. Sean Malcom/Thane Sloan -- Sloan and Malcomb aren't a natural tag team together. MHW Owner William Bradford decided to put them together to see how they'll do against the local boys. Malcomb and Sloan hold the size advantage. How will that affect the matchup? Tonight in the Spotlight: Czar "The Educator" Thane Sloan "The Asian Sensation" Xu Jing "The Atomic Son" Sean Malcomb Breaking and Entering "Premium" River Rockwell "Servant" Robert **** Czar **** (Cameras are clicking and bulbs are flashing as about twenty reporters are hovering around a podium. Behind the podium is the American Airlines logo, which gives us the feeling that we are in an airport. The reporters are walking aimlessly and chattering a storm. The camera, which faces the podium unmanned and on a tripod, is shooting black and white film, and you can hear the film in the audio stream. Moments later, the reporters scramble for seats and the clicking of cameras grows more rampant. A caravan of men, possibly fifteen of them come around the corner and take seats. Somewhere in the midst of them is a man wearing a dark purple suit, lavender tie, lavender shirt, and purple shaded sunglasses. His shag hair is a mess, pointing this way and that, and he seems a bit out of it. Because of his height, no one sees anything except for his mishap hair until he takes a seat on the raised platform of the podium. The man is offered a cigarette from a silver case, and then as he takes it in his mouth, another man lights it with a zippo. Instantly, smoke puffs its way above the man, doing circles around his hair before fogging the area. An older man, perhaps in his forties, balding, with a pelicanesque nose and glasses begins to speak....) Spokesman - Ok, people. Lets keep this as sane as possible. Your reporters sometimes get out of hand. This is Czar.....Isaac Connur.....hes here at the Denver International Airport. He is here on business, and um.......he'll........Czar is an athlete, as well as a trubadour and artist...he'll answer questions ranging from wrestling to art to anything in between. GO! (A few chuckles from around the room as the reporters scramble through papers) Czar - Oh my god.......(he's upset at the amount of dead air) Reporter One - Czar.....uh, the obvious question is why youre in Denver. (pause) Czar - Is that a question? It-uh, it sounded more like a statement. I dont think i can answer statements for you. Reporter One - Well, the question is, are you here to join a fed and start wrestling again or- Czar - No no no no no no. Im not playing that. No. If we're going to play this game, then you can play with yourself cause Im not doing this. Reporter - Do what? Czar - Play this game where you peek into my working life...peek into my personal life and ask me-uh, ask me these idiotic questions, you know.....would you, uh, if, if Jimmy Carter went to the White House, would you ask him if he was running for president again? Reporter - No.... Czar - Why not? Reporter - Because I write for a wrestling magazine....Jimmy Carter isnt a wrestler.... Czar - He wrestled with high gas prices........look, im not trying to be a lousy guy, believe me. Im not. I just got off a plane in Denver, and the airport depressed me because it looks like Teepees, and it reminds me of old indians dying, and ive taken a lot of pills, and had to take an awful carride, and im tired....im not trying to be a lousy guy about all this...... Reporter Two - Rumors have been circulating that, now that your knee is better, there might be certain things you are after......That coupled with the opening of the Mile High Wrestling, well, these spark legitamite questions about- Czar - Ive read those rumors....I, uh, i know who wrote that....about my knee and such. You remember I wrestled for almost a year in Boulder, you know, in Dan Rose's Fed........Im.....Im not a huge fan of Colorado.....like i said, it reminds me of dead indians and yuppie skiiers. Reporter - So, youre saying you arent joining Mile High Wrestling? Czar - Im saying im not a big John Denver fan. Thats all. Reporter Three - You won 12 world championships....uh, you had an ongoing rivalry with many wreslters....mostly Ramon Lopez....you also faced Ricky Ryzing.... Czar - When was all that? A decade ago? Reporter - Two years ago. Czar - yeah, well. Two years ago. Whew! I....i cant help you if youre living in the past. Reporter One - Dont you think....... Czar - Yeah, sometimes. (laughter. Czar is given another cigarette. There are rings beneath his eyes that are hardly visible through his purple tinted glasses. Hes rocking back and forth and overall looks awful) Reporter One - Youre only 30. You have a lot of fight left in you, and, contrary to what you might be saying to us, a lot of us still believe you will be returning to the ring. Theres a lot of evidence to proove that. Czar - Well, Sherlock Holmes, if you have a lot of evidence, you can keep believing what youd like.......ive got a lot to do while im in denver..i certainly cant help you build your case, Pontius Pilot. Reporter Three - What, EXACTLY, are you in Colorado for, then? Czar - To.....to mountain bike. I have to meet with some old friends...ive got to visit my mother....uh, i have some people to talk to, you know, about where exactly my career is going. I, er, i dont have an EXACT reason. I mean, there are deals on the tables.....there are things that could happen...we'll see where the chips fall. I came to Denver to come to Denver...i didnt know there would be dead Apache waiting for me. Reporter Four - What - (Czar leans over to the older man and then gets up and walks out with his men....the spokesperson grabs the mic) Spokesperson - Czar says he needs to go laydown...there wont be anymore questions today. We'll keep in touch. (The reporters scramble and someone knocks down the camera. When the camera cuts back, Czar and his entourage are outside the airport, waiting for a limosuine. Czar looks aloof to everything thats going on. One of his friends leans over and says something and starts laughing hysterically, but Czar just nods and keeps looking at nothing. An older man dressed in rags walks up towards the entourage and begins to make a scene......) Old Man - Czar! CZAR! CZAR! IKE!! IKE!! Czar - What? WHo is that? (His men have the old man by the arms and are pulling him away from Czar) Old Man - Remember me? Its me, Sean! Sean!! Czar- Sean? You loook like hell. Old Man - After all that went down, ive been a little short of cash...... Czar - Well, Sean.....i guess you picked the wrong career. (With that, Czar coldly gets into the black Lincoln Limo alongside his men. The stretch, jet black car pulls off, leaving the old man on the sidewalk of the Denver airport alone.) ************************** "The Educator" Thane Sloan ************************** (The studio warning light changes from green to red. Alan: Not problem Thane. You looked old and slow Furthermore, it looked as though your heart didn't show up for the event. I have seen you in your prime and if your performance at Fuel is all that is left in your tank. Then you should take your old slow ass to the retirement home. Do not pass go and do not collect two hundered dollars. Retire now before you really get hurt old man. That is my assestment of your performance so what do you have to say for yourself. Thane: The Educator has not been the same since the turn of the century. The fire that once burned brightly in the soul of the Educator was doused by a bucket full of troubled water. Until recently all that remained of my soul was a smoldering reminder of my past and where I have been and what I have accomplished. So I rested on my accomplishments. I did not make the comimentment to excellence required to compete at this level. As the match began with "The Asian Sensation" I recall thinking that who was this old man in the ring with Xu Jing. It was if I did not recognize myself and if I had not been in the ring I would swear that it was not The Educatior who was being beaten from post to post. Xu Jing was faster and better than than whoever that old man was in the ring with him. Xu Jing beat that old man down and left for him for dead. Then I realized that I was the man lying dead in the ring. Over the last few days I have agonized over what took place last week. Then out of the blue, the soul exploration turned that smolering soul into a conflagration. Xu Jing heed my warning your education has only just begun. You can rest assured that I am not going to chain you to a car and pull you very far. Nor will I chain you to a post until you meet the holy ghost. However you will expierence the sesaiton of painless violence and the dreadful silence that follows an Educator. Xu Jing prepare to be educated by THE EDUCATOR. Thane takes the headphones off and tosses them on the chair as he walks out of the booth. ***************************** "The Asian Sensation" Xu Jing ***************************** [The camera opens in William Waterford's apartment. The apartment is spacious, with a queen-size bed that has gold silk sheets on it lying in front of the camera. Waterford is seated in front of the window, in front of the shining morning sun. The alarm clock on the nightstand reads 7:22 A.M. The Denver skyline is visible behind Waterford, through the window, as are the majestic Rocky Mountains. Waterford is in a chair, his feet on a round table in front of him. In one hand he holds a white cordless phone; in the other he has a cup of coffee.] WW(muttering): Come on, answer, damn you... [Waterford presses a button and places the phone on the table.] WW: I should have known. Every time I try to reach him, he's training, or he's getting accustomed to the altitude in Colorado, or he's trying to become more American by going to a Colorado Rockies game, or he's eating breakfast, or he's out on a date... hah, fat chance on that one. The guy can't speak English for crying out loud! That leads me to something interesting that happened last week. Now, as you know, I could not find Jing last week, try as I might. I searched all over that tiny town he now calls home, and I searched in vain throughout the Rockies, and I could not find the man. Yet, somehow, the man knew to show up at the Gerald Ford Amphitheatre in Vail to fight Thane Sloan. Something's wrong here. Xu Jing only speaks Chinese; how was he to know that he had a match? Nobody in the front office that I know of can speak Chinese, so they couldn't have told him. None of the friends I have who have connections speak Chinese, either. Yes, something is definitely wrong here. Either the man is a psychic or there's something fishy going on around here. Now, with all of his martial arts training, I would not doubt that the man may be a psychic. Perhaps the Chinese masters included that in his training. That training, by the way, was on clear display last Thursday night as Thane Sloan became the first of many victims of the Asian Sensation. Perhaps, though, his trainers also taught him the amazing ability to vanish into thin air and resurface whenever he feels like it. Let me recap the events of the past few weeks. Early March, I was in Hong Kong, discovering the man who was to become the greatest thing to hit Colorado since Coors Beer, even though he didn't know it at the time. Three weeks ago, I was in the final stages of negotiating his contract with Mile High Wrestling. Two weeks ago, April 8, I was downing a few Coors Lights with my prospect at the bar across the street. A couple of days after that, I got a call from the front office about his match. I tried to call Jing--but he'd disappeared! And since then, the only time that I saw him was in Vail last Thursday. Where has he gone? He's been leading me on a wild goose chase for the past two weeks, and this has to stop. I don't see why he couldn't just get an apartment down the hall. What's there not to like about Denver? Instead, he's way out in the middle of nowhere and I can't reach him at all. I call his "house", he's not there. I go to his house, he's not there. I search all through the town, he's not there. I search the mountains around the town, he's not there. But he came to this league to do one thing, as far as I'm concerned. It wasn't to come in here and run his mouth, no. If he were to do that, not a damn person would understand what he said! No, he came to this league to win some matches. I told him that his fighting style was superior to that exhibited by the American wrestlers, and so far he's proven that. However, one match does not make a man. Now, I'm as excited as everyone in Denver about the first edition of Firestorm, but I'm even more excited about Jing's match with "Servant" Robert. Sloan was the embodiment of the American technical wrestler, and Jing was able to defeat him. But Robert, on the other hand, is a lightning-quick, high-flying lightweight. Jing may have his hands full with Robert, but I believe he will prevail. He is out to prove that the kung fu style practiced by the Chinese masters is superior to any other fighting style. Now, I know that Robert is a servant, and I have heard of his master, George Martinson. Yes, Duke Martinson is an Englishman like myself, and I feel for him as he tries to rough it here in Colorado. I have respect for the man, yet I must warn him that his servant is about to have his bum served to him on a silver platter. [Scene goes to darkness.] ***************************** "The Atomic Son" Sean Malcomb ***************************** [The scene opens up in a dark an empty storage unit within Denver, Colorado. The figure stops as sounds can be heard from the otherside of the door. The door opens and a female silhouette stands in a much larger stream of light. We have to meet up with the movers at the new apartment. Sean Malcomb: I could have done this all with them, we didn't need their help. [The silhouette moves in and as she moves through the light to a more balanced place in the room, the light reveals that the figure is Jennifer Malcomb, in a white summer dress, and moving to wrap her arms around her husband. She looks up to him as she begins to speak.] Jenn Malcomb: We have the money, Sean. We may not be as rich as before, but at least we can pay to keep you from hurting yourself senselessly when you need to be at top condition. If we're going to make this work for you....for us both...you have to be able to accomplish the task, sweetheart. Sean Malcomb: But I could have done it just fine. I didn't need their help, I don't need anyone's help. Jenn Malcomb: This...isn't about the cost for moving men is it? [Sean Malcomb, looking a bit aggitated, tilts his head back and begins to look at the ceiling for a moment then focuses his sight towards the door.] Jenn Malcomb: It's about teaming with Sloan? Sean Malcomb: What kind of partner is that? I'm fine on my own, if they wanted to put me up against a handicap match, I would be fine with that. But I don't need a partner. I would think they'd figure out that Sean Malcomb goes it alone. Always. Jenn Malcomb: Always? What about me? Sean Malcomb: It's different in the ring. I go it alone, I don't need to hand off my challenge, I face it. I never need help to face my opponent. Jenn Malcomb: You've work with people before. Like this before. Sean Malcomb: No. Never like this. Everything was set up before, to accomplish another task. I've never intended to face off in a match like this where I didn't have something planned. A match I didn't plan to ignore the outcome and just take someone out. [Sean Malcomb looks down to his wife, the look of agitation gone. Now his face just had a kind of emotionless smile.] Sean Malcomb: I've had my reasons, but I've never intended to have to share my responsibilities. I've never needed anyone to help me where it should only be me that's needed. I don't need Sloan to take on some guys trying to catch a start in this league. Jenn Malcomb: Sean. I hate to break up this self-appreciating moment. But it's wrestling. Just shape up and fight the match. Go out there and win. Save the emotional stuff for when things actually get out of hand. Otherwise, we need to meet the moving men so we can get our furniture put in right. Sean Malcomb: Heh... [Sean Malcomb lets out a small chuckle and then smiles at his wife.] Sean Malcomb: Yeah, I suppose your right. Maybe I've seen one too many movies lately, I really went over board on the dramatics. Alright, dear. Let's go get that new home set up... [Sean Malcomb leans in to kiss his wife as the two smile at one another. Sean then puts his arms around his wife and the both face towards the door. They then walk out as the light turns them into just one connected silhouette and then they close the door behind them. The scene fades to black.] ********************* Breaking and Entering ********************* [The camera opens on the same garage seen earlier. Kenny "Breaker" Denmark and Quentin "The Entrance" Avery sit in two steel chairs. Quentin is reading some sort of magazine.] QA: "The local boys"? That sounds like the kind of name a librarian might have. Twenty-five years from now, people will look back and say, "Breaking and Entering, now THAT was a tag team." KD: Yeah, I have a feeling we're going to be the fifteen-time tag team champions. [Quentin smacks him in the back of the head.] QA: No, dimwit! We're not going to be the tag team champions fifteen times. To win the tag team titles fifteen times, we'd have to lose them fifteen times, too. Don't you get it? I don't foresee us winning the belts that many times. I think we'll win the belts and hold onto them for a long time. All of the tag teams are staying away from here because they realize that competing with Breaking and Entering is futile. There is nobody who can compete with us--and that includes Sean Malcomb and Thane Sloan. Malcomb and Sloan aren't a team. They are two wrestlers with different plans, different goals. This isn't a tag team match; it's a two-on-one-on-one handicap match if you ask me. Somebody tell the MHW to throw a real tag team at us. Denver is deprived. They're just getting to see us beat up two nobodies. But, hey, they've got it better than the people in Vail. They didn't even get to see us wrestle. KD: Somebody give us some damn competition.
