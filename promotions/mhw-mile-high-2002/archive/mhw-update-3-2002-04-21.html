--Mile High Wrestling Mailing List-- [MHW] Update #3 [4/22/02] CONTENTS 1. Notes 2. Hirings and Firings 3. Card Lineup 4. Schedule 5. Contact Me ******** 1. Notes ******** As I stated in the previous Schedule Re-Working post, there won't be anymore Fuel shows. At least not until we build a reasonably sized roster. At that time, I may consider reworking the schedule again based around a bi-weekly format. I can't tell you when that'll be. However, you will get a post regarding this when the time comes. ********************** 2. Hirings and Firings ********************** Since the last Update, we've signed on "Gorgeous and Young" Lance Romance. We've also signed a "Developmental contract" with Czar. The details of that contract are that he has two weeks (until our second Firestorm) to show that he can compete at a satisfactory level with you all. As far as Firings, we haven't lost any members fortunately. ************** 3. Card Lineup ************** MHW Firestorm Live from Colorado State University at Fort Collins Monday, April 29, 2002 Triple Threat Match "Premium" River Rockwell VS. "Gorgeous and Young" Lance Romance VS. Czar -- Czar is a newcomer who is currently under a probationary contract. Can he prove himself worthy enough to compete in the MHW on a full-time basis? Also, rumors have been circulating that Lance Romance is... you know... "GAY!" How will this affect the other two's gameplan going into this matchup? Singles Match "The Asian Sensation" Xu Jing VS. "Servant" Robert -- Both of these men are managed by Englishmen. What does that mean? How will these managers instruct their clients to approach this match? Both are the smallest competitors in the MHW. Tag Team Match Breaking and Entering VS. Sean Malcom/Thane Sloan -- Sloan and Malcomb aren't a natural tag team together. MHW Owner William Bradford decided to put them together to see how they'll do against the local boys. Malcomb and Sloan hold the size advantage. How will that affect the matchup? *********** 4. Schedule *********** This is the schedule leading up to our first Firestorm card. * Tuesday (4/23/02) Flashes due by Midnight Central * Wednesday (4/24/02) Spotlight Series due out Strats / In-Rings due by Midnight Central * Thursday (4/25/02) Flashes due by Midnight Central * Friday (4/26/02) Spotlight Series due out * Saturday (4/27/02) Off day (for card writing) * Sunday (4/28/02) Off day (you get the idea) * Monday (4/29/02) MHW Firestorm due out ************* 5.
