--Mile High Wrestling Mailing List-- [MHW] Update #2 [4/15/02] CONTENTS 1. Notes 2. Hirings and Firings 3. Card Lineup 4. Schedule 5. Contact Me ******** 1. Notes ******** All the flashes are in early so I've decided to put out the first Spotlight Series today. Use this extra time to work on your second flash, which is due in by Midnight tomorrow night. Be warned though, I will not be able to do it this way every week. Also, I am planning on changing the schedule after this week's Fuel comes out. With any luck, we should be able to have our first Firestorm show within the next 2 weeks. I'll let you all know following Fuel. ********************** 2. Hirings and Firings ********************** Fortunately, nobody has quit the MHW yet. However, we have hired another wrestler to the roster. He is "Servant" Robert. His bio page is on the site. As always, you can access it through the Roster. ************** 3. Card Lineup ************** MHW Fuel Taped from: Gerald R. Ford Amphitheatre, Vail, Colorado Thursday, April 18, 2002 SINGLES MATCH "The Educator" Thane Sloan VS. "The Asian Sensation" Xu Jing -- It's East VS. West as the Martial Arts Master, Xu Jing, takes on the big man from the United States West Coast. MAIN EVENT SINGLES MATCH "The Atomic Son" Sean Malcomb VS. "Premium" River Rockwell -- This match is a matter of size versus speed as well. It also pits an accomplished technical high-flyer against a technical powerhouse. *********** 4. Schedule *********** * Tuesday 4/16/02 2nd flashes due in by Midnight Central time. * Wednesday 4/17/02 MHW Spotlight Series + In-rings due by Midnight Central * Thursday 4/18/02 MHW Fuel out. * Friday thru Monday work on flashes. 1st Flashes due Midnight Monday night. * Tuesday 4/23/02 2nd flashes due at Midnight. * Wednesday 4/24/02 In-rings due Note: This schedule is subject to change. You will be notified of these changes. ************* 5. Contact Me ************* I won't put any of your AIM names on here. A few of you already have them listed on your bio page.
