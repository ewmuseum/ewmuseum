--Mile High Wrestling Mailing List-- [MHW] Update #4 [4/29/02] CONTENTS 1. Notes 2. Hirings and Firings 3. Card Lineup 4. Schedule 5. Contact Me ******** 1. Notes ******** I hope you all liked Firestorm. I personally thought it was great. I'll gladly take any constructive criticism regarding the card if you have it to give. I want to thank Pete (handler of Frank Peregrine) for writing the Xu Jing VS. "Servant" Robert match. He did an excellent job. The next Firestorm will be bigger still. So I need at someone to volunteer to write a match or two. That's how things will work around here. I'll not be able to write everything myself. I do need help . So please, somebody volunteer to write a match so I can get match assignments out. Finally, as per the William Bradford announcement on Firestorm, every win, loss, and/or draw that you incur until the next PPV will determine whether or not you'll participate in a mini-tourney to name the first MHW Heavyweight Champion. ********************** 2. Hirings and Firings ********************** The MHW continues to grow without a single loss to the roster. We have several newcomers though. Probably the biggest of them is Frank "The Falcon" Peregrine, who had a huge impact on the Firestorm Main Event. We've also signed "Luscious" Lance Justice, "The Rising Son" Terry Yakimoto, and "Smilin" DocSin. Also, Czar has been offered a regular contract as his performance has improved tremendously. ************** 3. Card Lineup ************** MHW Firestorm Live from Colorado Music Hall, Colorado Springs Monday, May 6, 2002 Singles Match "Servant" Robert VS. "The Rising Son" Terry Yakimoto -- It's England versus Japan. Yakimoto holds a considerable size advantage over Robert, weighing 243 pounds. How will this affect Robert's approach to the match? Singles Match "Luscious" Lance Justice VS. "Gorgeous and Young" Lance Romance -- It's a battle of the Lance's. Lance Justice was seen watching Romance's match from the stage. What's Justice's interest in Romance? And how will these two approach this match? Singles Match "The Educator" Thane Sloan VS. "The Asian Sensation" Xu Jing -- Sloan requested this rematch from the first MHW Fuel. He wants to prove that he he's not just an old man. However, he took a beating on Firestorm's Main Event. How will that affect his performance against Jing? Hardcore Match "Premium" River Rockwell VS. Czar -- Czar would've beaten Rockwell at the last Firestorm had it not been for interference from Lance Romance. Now they'll battle it out one-on-one. And with Hardcore rules, this kind of match is Czar's specialty. Singles Match Kenny "Breaker" Denmark VS. Frank "The Falcon" Peregrine -- We all saw how Peregrine made his debut at Firestorm last week. One of the men he double crossed was Kenny Denmark. Denmark gets a chance to inflict some more punishment on "The Falcon" for his actions. Singles Match "The Atomic Son" Sean Malcomb VS. "Smilin" DocSin -- DocSin had a few choice words for Malcomb following DocSin's run-in on the triple threat match. Now he gets a chance to back it up against the man himself. *********** 4.
