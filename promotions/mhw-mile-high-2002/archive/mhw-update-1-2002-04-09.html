[NAWC] Update #1 [4/10/02]

9 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This message was posted to the fed's members already. I'm posting it here to help you all follow the fed. If you'd like a constant link to the MHW, feel free to sign up for the Mailing List. You can subscribe to it via our website - http://milehigh.cjb.net Anyway, here's the post. --Mile High Wrestling Mailing List-- I've decided to get things going. So here's the deal. Next Thursday, April 18, 2002, we will host our first MHW Fuel show. This will comprise of only 2 matches. If there are any more roster additions before this Sunday, they too will be added to Fuel, unless specified only by me. No new recruits will interfere with the matches that are set down today. So, first of all, here's the lineup. MHW Fuel Taped from: Gerald R. Ford Amphitheatre, Vail, Colorado Thursday, April 18, 2002 SINGLES MATCH "The Educator" Thane Sloan VS. "The Asian Sensation" Xu Jing -- It's East VS. West as the Martial Arts Master, Xu Jing, takes on the big man from the United States West Coast. MAIN EVENT SINGLES MATCH "The Atomic Son" Sean Malcomb VS. "Premium" River Rockwell -- This match is a matter of size versus speed as well. It also pits an accomplished technical high-flyer against a technical powerhouse. Now for the schedule... * Wednesday thru Saturday of this week are off-days. * Sunday 4/14/02 MHW Update #2 out (to announce new members and possibly more matches) * Monday 4/15/02 1st flashes due in by Midnight Central time * Tuesday 4/16/02 MHW Spotlight Series (flash packet) + 2nd flashes due in by Midnight Central time as before. * Wednesday 4/17/02 MHW Spotlight Series + In-rings due by Midnight Central * Thursday 4/18/02 MHW Fuel out. In-rings are not mandatory but are encouraged. The schedule will remain like this until we have a sufficient amount of members to get Firestorm started. The schedule will be reiterated in every Update. You are only limited 2 RPs per week. That means I don't want to see 5 RPs by one character in a single Spotlight Series. Be creative. Remember, longevity is not important. I want quality over quantity. You have between now and Monday night to work on your first flashes. That should be more than sufficient. If you want to discuss feuds with other members, feel free to create a chat room on irc.starchat.net (IRC channel). But let me know as well so I can join you. If you don't have access to IRC, e-mail or IM me and I'll be happy to set up a chat via Instant Messenger. That's all for now. Neal --(c) Copyright 2002, Mile High Sports Inc.--
