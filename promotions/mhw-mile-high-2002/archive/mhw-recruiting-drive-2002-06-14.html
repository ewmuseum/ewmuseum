[MHW] Mile High Wrestling: Recruiting Drive

14 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As part of the MHW's new committee, I'd like to encourage anybody who is looking for a fed to join to consider Mile High Wrestling. We've been around for two months and are currently approaching our first pay-per-view event, Hostile Intent, which is scheduled for July 7th. We have one show and two Spotlight Series flash packets every week, and while it's unlikely that a new wrestler would have a match on the pay-per-view(not enough time to develop a feud), it's likely that you would debut in the two weeks prior to it. We're also looking to expand our roster in order to create the Rocky Mountain Title, which would be our secondary title, and any newcomer would probably be involved in the chase for that belt. In any case, I encourage you to view our web page and FAQ at http://milehigh.cjb.net , and, if you want to join, fill out an application. You can also view our latest show, Firestorm, at http://www.hockey-autographs.net/~mhw/firestorm6-11-02.html . [name withheld] Committee Member, Mile High Wrestling http://milehigh.cjb.net
