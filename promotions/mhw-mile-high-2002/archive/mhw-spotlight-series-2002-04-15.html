[MHW] Spotlight Series [4/15/02]

15 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
--Mile High Wrestling Mailing List-- [MHW] Spotlight Series [4/15/02] Today in the Spotlight: "THe Educator" Thane Sloan Breaking and Entering "The Asian Sensation" Xu Jing "The Atomic Son" Sean Malcomb "Premium" River Rockwell ************************** "The Educator" Thane Sloan ************************** The silence of the night is broken by the motor of a black and silver Chevy Tahoe pulling into the parking lot of the headquarters of Blood, Sweat and Tears, Inc. A very large silhouette of a man exits the vehicle and begins to walk toward a building. The silhouette approaches the door and you can removes a badge. The badge is placed it front of a sensor, the door opens. A security officer comes walking down a dimly lit hallway to see who who has entered the building. Security Officer: Good morning Mr. Sloan. Thane nods his head and walks past the security officer and up two flights of stairs and enters his office. Once inside the office he turns on a light and sits down at his desk and opens a letter from Mile High Wrestling. Thane then puts the letter down on his desk and grabs a copy of today's USA Today and flips through a few pages. He stops when he finds the headline "THE EDUCATOR, RETURNS, by Rick Jones. (The article reads); SEATTLE, WA - Today in front of a 100 or so members of the press. Thane Sloan, owner of a nation wide chain of gymnasiums has stepped down as the Chairman to return to the wrestling ring. Thane retired just over two years with a humiliating loss in the NAWA. After the match he indicated that his heart was no longer in the business. He had accomplished everything in the business and there was no point in continuing. Thane is a former three time WWA World champion, NAWA World Champion and two time UIWO World Champion. He now looks to add the title of Mile High Wrestling World Champion. Thane signed a one year contract with the upstart organization. When asked why he choose this company, Thane indicated "I have dealt with the owner before. He is as close as you will come to an honest promoter. Furthermore, this organization is negotiating with the man that started my downfall in the wrestling business over two years ago. A man that I made fly like a falcon." It should be noted, Thane is referring to "The Falcon" Frank Pedigrene. Mr. Pedigrene has not signed a contract with the Mile High Wrestling. Thane's first match will be a challenge for "The Educator" as he squares off against the upstart "The Asian Sensation" Xu Jing. Well see how the former Marine and Stanford Graduate does when he returns to the ring. The MHW has also signed superstars Sean Malcomb, River Rockwell and well as The Duke George Martinson and his servant Robert. The first episode of Mile High Wrestling's "Fuel" will be April 18, at the Gerald R. Ford Amphitheater in Vail, Colorado. Thane puts down the paper and reaches into his desk and pulls out airline tickets and a travel schedule. He opens the travel schedule turns to April 18th and makes the following notation. Event: "FUEL" Location: Vail, CO Notes: XU Jing - The first man to be "Educated by The Educator" ********************* Breaking and Entering ********************* [The camera opens on the inside of a garage. Kenny "Breaker" Denmark and Quentin "The Entrance" Avery are sitting down, facing the open door, and looking outside. Each has on a black "Breaking and Entering" t-shirt and black jeans.] KD: Quentin, I can't believe that the MHW didn't put us on the show. QA: Neither can I. We sell tickets. We ARE the show. We give the MHW the local flavor that it needs. The problem is that people aren't going to come see us beat the crap out of Knight Watch. We need some competition. KD: That's the problem. All of the other tag teams heard that Breaking and Entering was gonna be in the MHW, so they all stayed the hell away. To enter the tag team division of the MHW is an exercise in futility, unless your names are Quentin Avery and Kenny Denmark. The front office decided to save themselves some time and already put our names on those belts. Now they just need to do a "tournament," or maybe a "glorified giveaway" is a better word. Ha! QA: You see what you're doing, Kenny? You're keeping the other teams away. Now they know they have no chance of winning the belts. KD: Q, one look on the roster tells them that! QA: Good point. But you see, we've gotta sucker them in. We're really nothing more than a couple of guys in our early 20's who probably are less experienced and less talented than we think. That's what we want the "potential tag teams" to believe. So they'll come in here thinking we're nothing, and then BAM! That's when they see just how great we are. But it's too late to do that, because you already gave it away that we're the greatest tag team ever. But you're entirely right. We're the boys from Colorado, and we're the best the MHW has to offer. WE are what all those fans are paying to see. We put butts in the seats, so it makes almost no sense to me that we're not wrestling Thursday night. The fans in Vail shall be deprived. They will feel like they've just wasted five bucks. I mean, hell, the MHW could have put us in a match against a couple of 98-pound weaklings, because five minutes of Breaking and Entering is better than no Breaking and Entering! KD: Five minutes? You really think it would take us five minutes to beat up a couple of 98-pound weaklings? QA: No. Four and a half minutes for the entrance, thirty seconds to win the match. KD: Oh yeah... I guess they would be seeing us for five minutes. [Scene goes to darkness.] ***************************** "The Asian Sensation" Xu Jing ***************************** [The camera opens on the back porch of a small house up high in the Rocky Mountains. The house is made of blue-painted wood, while the deck is made of splintered lumber. There is a hot tub, but it is covered and spider webs grow from it--apparently, it is never used. William Waterford stands alone on the deck. The wrestler whose career he manages, "The Asian Sensation" Xu Jing, is nowhere in sight. He begins to speak in his heavily British accent.] WW: Well, it seems I discounted Colorado for the amount of distractions available to wrestlers. Jing just got up and disappeared this morning, like he has every morning. I bought an apartment in Denver to be close to the action, but he decided that he needed some space to himself and bought this cottage at 8,000 feet above sea level. What was he thinking? [He stops for a moment.] WW: Anyway, like I said he disappears every morning. I've called over here every morning bright and early but every morning, he is not here. Now it's three o'clock in the afternoon and he's still not here. What does he do for eight hours in a town of 200? I am completely stumped. Now, I am beginning to wonder why I brought him here in the first place. Jing needs to be somewhere that he won't be distracted all the time. I don't feel like playing "Where's Xu Jing?" every day. I have far too much to do to be chasing him around. [Once again, he stops. The camera switches to provide a panoramic view of the town in which Xu Jing is residing at this time. The view from the deck is clearly spectacular, overlooking this small hamlet. However, as the camera pans back to Waterford, he looks rather, well, unimpressed.] WW: I know, I know, great view? I don't think so. Look, the reason why I've needed to get in touch with Jing somehow in the past few days was that he's been signed to fight "The Educator" Thane Sloan in Vail on Thursday night. I don't know if he's gone to Vail already or what. He doesn't have a car here and it's over 50 miles down I-70, so maybe he's walking. The only thing is, he doesn't know he's got a match because I haven't told him. Well, Thane, if you must know the truth, I would have to say that you're about to get a nice education yourself this Thursday night. [William mutters "... if Jing decides to show up..."] WW: Thane, do not let looks deceive you. So maybe there is not much more to this tiny hamlet in which Jing has decided to live, but there is definitely more to him than might meet your eye. I realize that you have great experience and he does not. Chalk up a point for Thane Sloan... but who cares? If you ask me, experience is overrated. So Jing doesn't have much experience in the world of professional wrestling. I'll give you that one, but let me also point out that Jing has already been a two-time world kung fu champion at the young age of 20. Youth may mean inexperience, Thane; but experience goes hand-in-hand with old age. The knees are already going, and I'll see to it that Jing makes them go even more. And you will never meet a man who is as tough as he is. Many years of martial arts and shootfighting have molded a man who will never surrender, no matter how much pain he is in, and a man whom you may need to kill before you can pin him. The education of "The Educator" Thane Sloan truly begins Thursday night. We shall have a "cultural exchange," if you will. [Fade to black.] ***************************** "The Atomic Son" Sean Malcomb ***************************** [The scene opens with the outside of a bar in Dade City, Florida. There are dogs barking nearby, and the music in the bar is mixed with loud talking and yelling. The camera closes in on the front door as it flies open and a man flies out and lands face first in the dirt a few feet away. A few other men from inside step half out the door and look at the man, laughing and making quick jokes. The men are obviously inebriated and stumbling a bit as they move around each other at the door. The camera chases the few men as they go back in the door, the music is obviously loud, but the audio seems to focus on the camera's point of view. It follows through passing over the patrons drinking their shots and beers, catching bits of random conversations. It pans around to a few men around a man with his back to the camera on a stool. The men are dressed in jeans and different t-shirts, while the man on the stool wheres a plain white shirt and dark jeans.] Man #1: You see ol' Billy try and get out of that comment? I swear I ain't never seen him look so much like he was gonna cry since his wife got his truck in the divorce! Man #2: He never thought 'bout who was here tonight! Same way he always gets when he's drunk, starts mouthin' off 'bout who he can whup. Ain't never thought we got ourselves a real rassler here tonight! Man #1: And all those things he said about the girl? What a dang gum fool! [The man nudges the guy on the stool and cracks a smile.] Man #1: Ain't that right, buddy? [The bartender lays a full beer mug on the bar infront of the man in the pure white shirt. The man proceeds to take hold of the mug and tip it back to drink. He doesn't set the mug down and away from his mouth until the mug is empty, with nothing but bits of foam still inside. The man then kicks around on the stool to face towards the camera and stands. He towers over the other men and is in quite better shape. The camera then lifts a bit to get a clear look at the man's face. He is none other than "The Atomic Son" Sean Malcomb.] Sean Malcomb: God damn did I need that drink! Come out here for one of my final nights in Florida, and all I hear is stupid loud mouths talkin' 'bout how they're gonna do this, and how they're gonna do that. Then the sunnova bitch spit in my god damn eye. What the hell happened to Billy? Man #1: It's that divorce his wife had been pullin' him through. She took damn near everythin', now he spends what's left of his money here tryin' to feel like a god damned super hero. Usually ends up right where you left him, but that's almost near closin' time. He's a wily bastard, I tell ya. You got the record on tossin' him up on outta this place. Sean Malcomb: I also got the record for most pitchers of beer consumed in an hour and most shots in a night. I think that record will be quickly forgotten. Man #2: Especially with the main recreation around this here town...GETTIN' DRUNK! [The whole bar erupts with people yellin' "GETTIN' DRUNK" then dies down to the roar of conversations and music again.] Sean Malcomb: One helluva recreation at that. Ain't no better way to cap off the day, boys. Man #1: Gettin' away from the work day and the little woman for a few hours and throwin' back a few with the boys. Ah, the only thing left to get in here is some sweet things in tight clothin'! Man #2: You said it! [The camera pans to the front door of the bar again as the door flies open and a less than happy Jennifer Malcomb hastily moves through the door and through part of the crowd, she's wearing a pair of jeans and a decently tight tank top.] Man #1: Damn! Here comes one now! And a fine piece of-- [Sean Malcomb delivers a swift punch to the man's face and he falls to the ground. Malcomb looks at Jennifer as she walks right up to him and sticks her finger in his chest.] Jenn Malcomb: What the hell is this? Drinking like this again? Why? Because you're joining some low level gig in Colorado? I thought you were going to cut down on all of this mess! Instead I find you tricked into a contract while in Denver! What the hell is wrong with you? This little league won't pay for us to continue living this way! We may never get back down here to Florida except on vacation! Man #2: Man, Sean, you gonna get it! [Malcomb delivers a quick punch to the other man, who falls quickly, just like his friend.] Sean Malcomb: Now don't start with me again, its a good oppurtunity to get back into the game for me. I've been out of the loop for too long, I need to get my ring sense again. Besides, I'm the highest paid on the roster! Jenn Malcomb: Highest paid? Highest paid?! What does that mean? We can buy a duplex after paying expenses to fixate ourselves out there? Or the one bedroom bathroom combination shack that half these drunks live in?! [The crowd erupts with "GETTIN' DRUNK" and then dies down again.] Sean Malcomb: Now, honey, I just need to get out there for a bit, hell who knows? It might bring some of the old names back in, this thing could go god damned national like NAWA did. I did have a bit of a hand in that, if I recall right. Jenn Malcomb: No my dear husband, you were just told you had a hand in that. God, you don't have a clue what you got us into, do you? You didn't think once, not once, about how your family is going to survive out there after the savings from the NAWA stuff is gone, do you? What the hell am I going to do in Colorado? I was close to getting a new job out here, which is what I thought you wanted! You hole us up here in this god awfully hot place and don't let me know what you're planning to keep this union supported...then you run off to Colorado, get drunk and now we have to move out there! Sean Malcomb: Well, I thought it was best. I get back in the business, we can be in the show again. Just like we were before. I just wanted to get back into the life we've both known for so long. It pays decently enough, should go up soon. Hell, if this thing becomes popular, we'll be living like royalty again soon enough. I wanted to do what I felt was needed, and I thought you would like to see me back in the ring as well! Jenn Malcomb: If you wanted back in the ring, you should have at least asked me first! I was finally getting settled down here, having us both in a regular place. We finally didn't have to run from location to location, away from one another. Sean Malcomb: Well, we can travel together now, their reporting staff is full up at the moment, so you can just travel with me and be at ringside for my matches. We can still be together, we just follow the show again. Jenn Malcomb: And what about me, Sean? What about my ambitions? I'm a reporter, not exclusively a wrestling reporter. I want to go in pursuit of news. I could very well get a job out there and have to move in a certain area, while you'll be all over the damned state. How is this going to work out, Sean? How? Sean Malcomb: Well.... [Sean reaches for a beer on the bar and grabs the handle of the mug. Jenn uses one hand to push him in the chest as she sees this.] Jenn Malcomb: Oh, no. You've had quite enough for tonight, Mr. Malcomb. Sean Malcomb: We can work it out, it ain't like we haven't faced tough situations before, love. Jenn Malcomb: We better work it out, but until you have an idea, you're sleeping on the couch. [One of the men staggers up a bit and leans on a bar stool.] Man #1: Damn, man, you're in the dog house with your old lady... [Malcomb elbows the man in the head and he collapses down to the floor again.] Jenn Malcomb: We leave for Colorado in the morning. We have to check into the hotel by two, so you better be on the move tomorrow. No lagging because of all this damned drinking you've been doing. We have to look at a few houses before we do a damn thing for this little league of a circuit. I want this done on my schedule if you're going to go through with this. You made a commitment without telling me, you're going to follow my rules on this one. If you so much as slow me down tomorrow, you'll be on the couch again. Understood? Sean Malcomb: Jesus, honey, it isn't like we're planning an attack, no need to act like Heinrich... Jenn Malcomb: Don't you even mention that man....you're moving our home and you have the nerve to compare me to that fascist? You follow my rules until we've got ourselves a home out there, you got me Mr. Malcomb? Sean Malcomb: ....yes, dear. Jenn Malcomb: Good, now we leave. I'll have your truck towed back home, I'm driving. Sean Malcomb: But, I haven't had... Jenn Malcomb: My rules! Sean Malcomb: It's not like I'm that drunk! [The crowd erupts "GETTIN' DRUNK", and then fades back down.] Jenn Malcomb: Yeah, after being in this place for four hours, I can tell, you aren't "that drunk" at all. Come, Sean. Sean Malcomb: Damn.... [The camera follows the couple out the door and the crowd continues as they were. The two men start to stagger up and lean on nearby stools to hold themselves up. The bartender sets two beers next to them. As the couple walks through the door of the bar, the scene fades to black.] ************************ "Premium" River Rockwell ************************ [The scene opens to the sight of a somewhat dated car stereo. It appears to be a radio only-- no CD player, no tape player. It does have a seek button. We hear only static at first, and then a finger comes down and presses the seek button.] "......Don't tell my heart, my Achey......" [Seek.] "......What I don't understand is that if this is supposed to be a democracy, then why......" [Seek.] "......Only 19.95! What a bargain!......" [Seek.] "......That was the latest from Metallica, "I Hate Everyone" off their new album 'Unload'......" [Seek.] "......Next up, we have a special guest. He's a wrestler in Mile High Wrestling, a brand new independant wrestling federation based here out of Denver......" Voice off camera : This'll do. [The scene changes to the inside of a radio station. In the broadcasting booth, we see a rather scrawny man sitting behind a microphone. In the background are about ten million buttons and switches, all of which look important. The man is wearing a shirt reading "KXPW - Sports Rock."] DJ : His name is "Premium" River Rockwell, and this should be an interesting interview because Mr. Rockwell was actually the first wrestler signed to a MHW contract. And after this commercial, he'll be joining us-- stick around. [The DJ presses a button, and takes off his headphones. With a glance at the door, he can see another man waiting to enter. He gets up from his station, and goes to let the new man in. The new man is wearing a "University of North Florida" baseball cap, sunglasses, and a rather expensive looking watch. He has a smug grin on his face as he enters the studio.] DJ : Hi, you must be River. My name-- River Rockwell : Call me Rockwell. [The DJ laughs nervously.] DJ : Right, Mr. Rockwell. I'm-- River Rockwell : Not Mr. Rockwell. Just Rockwell. For a broadcaster, you don't listen too well. DJ : Sorry Rockwell. I'm Bitchin' Nick Royal. Nice to have you. If you'll just take a seat here. [Royal points Rockwell to a seat across from the chair he just left, and River takes the seat. Although Royal keeps waiting for Rockwell to take off the sunglasses, such a gesture never occurs.] River Rockwell : Bitchin' Nick? Do they call you that because you're a bitch? Nick Royal : Uh... no, it's just my on-air name. My actual name is--- River Rockwell : It's your on-air name? Heh. That's kind of a dumb concept. Why don't you just use your real name? Nick Royal : Well, the same reason that wrestlers don't use theirs. I mean, River Rockwell is a pretty cool name. River Rockwell : That's my real name. [Nick fidgets nervously.] Nick Royal : Oh. Sorry. We're on in about 15 seconds. Just go ahead and put these headphones on. [Both men put headphones on, and in about 15 seconds Royal presses a few buttons bringing them back on the air.] Nick Royal : You're listening to KXPW - Sports Rock. I'm Bitchin' Nick Royal, and with me is special guest "Premium" River Rockwell, soon to be pro-wrestling star. Why don't you tell us a little about yourself, Rockwell? River Rockwell : Soon to be? I'm already a star. You wouldn't believe the following I have back home, Bitch Nick. Nick Royal : It's, uh, Bitchin' Nick Royal. And where is back home, Rockwell? River Rockwell : Well Bitch, I'm originally from Jacksonville Florida. I'm an Osprey alum from the U of North Florida. Nick Royal : What did you major in? River Rockwell : Broadcasting. [Royal chokes back a laugh.] River Rockwell : You think that's funny? I'm a better broadcaster than you. Hell, I've known you for five minutes and I can already tell that. Nick Royal : Sorry. Anyway, what else can you tell us about yourself? You said you had a following back in Jacksonville? River Rockwell : Yeah, chicks walking around with my name on their sweaters, guys walking around with my name on thier jackets. Everyone loves me down there. Nick Royal : According to this card, this will be your first time as a pro-wrestler... how do you already have a following? River Rockwell : What? Oh.. uh, your card is wrong. Yeah, I've been doing this for ages. Nick Royal : Who's your favorite opponent of all time? River Rockwell : Umm... uh... well, it's... uh... Hulk Hogan. Yeah, before he became big, Hogan and I used to wrestle all the time in a gym down in Sarasota. Nick Royal : Randy Savage is from Sarasota. Hogan's from California. River Rockwell : I know that, Bitch! I said Randy Savage, not Hogan. Nick Royal : No, you said--- River Rockwell : Randy Savage. I know what I said. Why don't you stop putting words in my mouth? [Nick lets out a sigh.] Nick Royal : So what brings you to Denver, Mr.. uh, I mean, Rockwell? River Rockwell : Wrestling, what else? I saw an ad on the internet. I figured any startup promotion has to have one star that will hook the fans. I'm that star. Mile High Wrestling needs that one star that will have the fans making signs, and coming to every show just to cheer me on. They need that one guy who can hold the heavyweight title for all the monsters to chase after. Like Andrew the Giant and Hulk Hogan. Nick Royal : You mean Andre the Giant? River Rockwell : That's what I said. Andre the Giant. I'm the Hogan, everyone else gets to be the Giant. Hogan always wins. So does Rockwell. Nick Royal : I interviewed Hogan once... he was much... nicer. River Rockwell : Being nice only gets you so far in this business, Bitch. Nick Royal : Nick. River Rockwell : What? Nick Royal : Just call me Nick. River Rockwell : I did. I said, being nice only gets you so far in this business. If I want to get ahead, I can't be worried about what everyone thinks of me. I'm in this for me. I'm in it for the money and the fame that's due someone with my charisma. Nick Royal : Mile High Wrestling is probably the wrong place for you if you want money and fame. It's a brand new promotion with a fanbase that can probably be currently counted on two hands. River Rockwell : And that's why I'm here. Give me two weeks. I'll draw so many fans in we'll have to start having 2 shows a day just to keep all the fans happy. Nick Royal : Riiight. So have you heard who your first opponent is going to be? River Rockwell : No. But I suppose you have, haven't you Bitch? Nick Royal : Nick. And yes, I have. It's "The Atomic Son" Sean Malcomb. River Rockwell : Whoa, whoa, whoa... there are WAY too many words in that nickname. Who needs a three word nickname? Once you've passed two, you've lost the fans. He might as well call himself "The Atomic Man of the New Millenium Who Will Win Every Match" Sean Malcomb. He obviously has no experience in wrestling. My experience will overcome this amateur. Nick Royal : YOU'VE never wrestled professionally, Rockwell! How can you knock on his experience? River Rockwell : Bullcrap, I just told you I wrestled Jimmy Savage back in 88. Don't you pay attention? Nick Royal : You said Hulk Hogan, I said Randy Savage. And in 88, you would have been about 16 years old! River Rockwell : You've got quite the mouth on you, Bitch. Nick Royal : NICK! And for the record, Sean Malcomb has several heavyweight title reigns to his credit! Not to mention a career going back about 5 years! River Rockwell : Who does? Nick Royal : YOUR OPPONENT. THE. ATOMIC. SON. SEAN. MALCOMB. River Rockwell : You don't need to shout. I'm right here. And that's fine and dandy. I don't care who Malcomb beat. Obviously he lost a "Loser Takes Incredibly Stupid Gimmick" match. Anyone who loses that important of a match can't be trusted to win ever. I'll take him to town. Nick Royal : What's your gimmick? River Rockwell : I'm "Premium" River Rockwell. Nick Royal : Well what does that mean? River Rockwell : It means... It means that I'm premium! You know, the best! Nick Royal : At what? Wrestling? Your first match is 2 weeks away! HOW CAN YOU BE THE BEST already?! River Rockwell : I just am. Trust me. Nick Royal : Oohkay. Well, that's all the time we have. I'm Bitchin' Nick Royal, with "Premium" River Rockwell, new wrestling "star," and this has been Sports Rock. [Royal pushes a few buttons and takes his headphones off.] Nick Royal : You can take the headset off. We're off air. River Rockwell : Why'd you end it? The fans didn't get to hear me for long enough. Nick Royal : Right. Sorry bout that. Gotta get the commericials in. [Rockwell eyes Royal angrily.] Nick Royal : Corporate policy. [Rockwell begins to walk to the door.] Nick Royal : Thanks for coming, River. [Rockwell throws a glare at Royal before exiting the studio.] Nick Royal [under his breath] : Don't let the door hit you on the way out. You ass. [Scene out.] Excellent work all of you. Glad to see that everyone put in an effort. Keep up the good work.
