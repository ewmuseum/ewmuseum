[MHW] Recruiting Statement + FAQ

29 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mile High Wrestling continues its search for new members. Currently, only one person has signed on with us. He is "Premium" River Rockwell. I'm sure he is anxious to get his MHW career underway. The MHW staff put together an Exhibitionary Show to exhibit just what the MHW is capable of putting forth. I have failed to post the MHW's FAQ here in the past as I have it on the site. However, this company will get nowhere without a little more encouragement. So, I have included the FAQ below. What kinds of members are we looking for? At this point, we're looking for anything... singles, tag teams, lightweights, heavyweights, women wrestlers, hardcore wrestlers, power-houses, high flyers, brawlers, everything. So, without further delay, here's the FAQ. MILE HIGH WRESTLING FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS VERSION 1.0 (c) 2002, Mile High Sports Inc. TABLE OF CONTENTS 1. What is the MHW? 2. What shows does the MHW hold? 8. What about PPVs? 9. What titles are up for grabs in the MHW? 10. How do I join? ***************** 1. What is the MHW? ***************** Mile High Wrestling is a regional promotion based in Denver, Colorado. Though it does travel around to various locations in Colorado, it will not venture past state lines. Its Headquarters is located in Denver itself. The MHW is an e-mail fed. Roleplays are posted via e-mail rather than on a message board. Roleplays are sent to the fedhead at [detail withheld] He then compiles them and re-releases them in packets.We don't simulate matches. They are hand-written using play-by-play and block format. *********************************** 2. Who are the real people behind the MHW? *********************************** Currently there is only one real staff member in the MHW. [name withheld] will perform all tasks required to make the MHW function like a well oiled machine. He designs the MHW website, books matches, etc. Hopefully, with the gradual increase in the MHW's roster, he will get additional help in running the MHW. ************************************** 3. Who are the kayfabe people behind the MHW? ************************************** William L. Bradford - MHW Owner - Born a spoiled rich kid, Bradford was practically spoonfed his fortunes. All he cares about is money. He's only doing this for the money. If something doesn't work in his best interests, and in some way doesn't line his pockets, he throws a fit. He has no respect for his employees whatsoever. Either they do what he wants or they're out. Rusty Waters - Lead Play-by-Play Man - Rusty is the head commentator for the MHW's flagship program, Firestorm. He's a real country boy who has traditional values. He doesn't like cheating, hardcore matches, and the like. He's the MHW's face commentator. "Slick" Ronnie Vick - Color Commentary - The heel of the duo, Ronnie also works the Firestorm show. He loves the things that Rusty hates and more. If somebody cheats, he'll easily turn a blind eye to it. He's cocky and often annoying. Monty Hemrick - Backup Play-by-Play Man - Monty works the Fuel show and is occasionally seen on Firestorm when Rusty isn't there. He and Rusty are close friends. Monty is a straightforward guy. He calls the action as best he can while remaining unbiased. Nancy Fletcher - Backup Color Commentator - Nancy helps Monty work the Fuel show. She is a face commentator and she has to get her word in. Her intentions are admirable but she is known to talk too much. Dave "The Rave" Madison - Ring Announcer - Dave is called "The Rave" because girls come to the arena just to see him. He is immensely popular with them because he is a local pop music singer in a boy band. He was hired for this job because of his speaking ability and popularity. Rebecca Howard - Lead Reporter - Rebecca is a professional but that doesn't stop her from flirting to get the hot stories. The faces practically drool over her. She occasionally does personal favors to get the big stories. Favors like accompanying them to the ring. Alan Woods - Reporter - Alan is a former wrestler who never made it big. So now he reports for the MHW. He's a heel reporter who absolutely despises Rebecca for getting the big bucks. However, he secretly admires her for her good looks. Lenny "The Leech" Lorenzo - Reporter - Lenny is probably the most annoying person you'll ever meet. He can't stay away from the wrestlers. He's always begging for interviews, promos, and the like. Everyone hates him. The only way he gets a story is by evesdropping on someone else's conversations. Trey Philips - Head of Security - Trey is basically just a big redneck. He likes bullying people around, especially fans. He takes pleasure in beating down anyone who stands up to him. He's like a dog on a leash. If Bradford says "jump," Trey jumps. Bradford can't stand him but knows he does his job very well. Jacob Farrell - Head Referee - Hacob is the most down-to-earth referee there is. Most of the faces enjoy his company. Still, he remains strictly unbiased. He follows his own set of strict rules on how to conduct himself in the ring. He knows that the fans came to see entertaining matches, and so he won't throw one out without reasonable cause to do so. Jacob is primarily a Main Event referee. Joey Caprelli - Backup Referee - Joey is the son of a Mob boss in Denver. The mob realized that there's big money in the MHW, so they forced Bradford to make Joey an employee. Bradford decided to make him a referee. Joeyis easily bribed. More money for the family. He's as corrupt a referee as they come. He mainly referees mid-card matches. Ryan Miller - Backup Referee - Ryan is a learned student of the hardcore style. He knows the rules inside-out. These kinds of matches are his bread and butter. He is usually used for card-openers and hardcore matches. *************************** 4. What are the rules in the MHW? *************************** Roleplay at least once per week. Stay Active. Contribute to angles and feuds. Be creative and original. Use your spellchecker when roleplaying. Keep swearing to a minimum. This is family television, not an R rated movie. Refrain from using racism, extensive nudity, or other offensive materials in your roleplays. Very brief nudity is allowed but only in very rare instances. Use only what is acceptable in the WWF. Occasional brief t & a shots are acceptable but don't go overboard. Don't rip off other people's ideas, including characters and angles from real wrestling. Don't ust drop out without telling me. If you must, let me know and I'll put your character "On Hiatus." ****************** 5. What about in-rings? ****************** I don't want to see a ton of in-rings for a card. You are limited to only one in-ring per card. Make it count. Anything else, such as interviews, should be done backstage. The only way I'll accept an in-ring is if it will contribute to a current angle They will not hold any bearing on the outcome of your matches. Also, in-rings are not to be included in your ring entrance. You don't see wrestlers get airtime right before their match in the WWF. So I won't accept it either. ******************** 6. How do I win matches? ******************** First of all, strategies are HIGHLY encouraged, but will not affect your chances of winning a match in any way. They merely assist a writer in writing your match. Winners are decided based entirely on roleplays. Angles can also be taken into consideration. If the two parties involved agree to a pre-determined outcome to a match, then that outcome will happen. Roleplays are especially useful for title matches and Pay Per Views. They will be judged on grammar (30%), spelling (30%) and creativity (40%). Longevity will not be a factor in this. It is possible for your roleplay to be shorter than your opponents and you still win, but only if your performance in these three categories outperforms that of your opponent. **************************** 7. What shows does the MHW hold? **************************** The MHW holds two shows. The first is the MHW's flagship program, Firestorm. It will air on Mondays. It will have 7 to 8 matches bully written in play-by-play format. The second leads up to the next Firestorm. It is called Fuel. It will be out on Thursdays. Fuel will have 2 to 3 darkmatches (written in summary form). This show will be used to start, or continue current feuds. Here is a rundown of the weekly schedule. Sunday - Off day for writing matches Monday - MHW Firestorm comes out Tuesday - weekly Update will be posted & Flash Packet (called The Spotlight Series) Wednesday - The Spotlight Series Thursday - MHW Fuel comes out Friday - off day for writing matches Saturday - off day (you get the idea) ***************** 8. What about PPVs? ***************** Pay Per Views will be held on the last Sunday of every other month. The entire week before it will be used for writing the matches. PPV names will be announced when the time arrises. Make sure to get yourselves an angle going that'll reach a climax at the next PPV. I can not come up with all of them. ********************************* 9. What titles are up for grabs in the MHW? ********************************* At the moment we only have two titles. However, more will be made available as our roster grows. MHW Heavyweight Championship MHW Tag Team Champions ************** 10.
