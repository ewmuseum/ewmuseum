MHW Continues Recruiting Efforts [Free Agents Read]

11 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
No, this isn't a newsreel about the latest MHW acquisitions. This is a plea to any and all Free Agents out there. Mile High Wrestling has finally gotten under way. Currently we only have four singles wrestlers and a tag team. However, we would like to expand our roster with many more talented competitors, both in the singles and tag team ranks. Women wrestlers are welcome as well. Here's our roster at the moment. HEAVYWEIGHTS Quentin "The Entrance" Avery (part of Breaking and Entering tag team) Kenny "Breaker" Denmark (part of Breaking and Entering tag team) "The Educator" Thane Sloan "The Atomic Son" Sean Malcomb LIGHTWEIGHTS "The Asian Sensation" Xu Jing "Premium" River Rockwell TAG TEAMS Breaking and Entering Our first card, MHW Fuel, will be held next Thursday. He's the current lineup as well as schedule for the next few days leading to Fuel. MHW Fuel Taped from: Gerald R. Ford Amphitheatre, Vail, Colorado Thursday, April 18, 2002 SINGLES MATCH "The Educator" Thane Sloan VS. "The Asian Sensation" Xu Jing -- It's East VS. West as the Martial Arts Master, Xu Jing, takes on the big man from the United States West Coast. MAIN EVENT SINGLES MATCH "The Atomic Son" Sean Malcomb VS. "Premium" River Rockwell -- This match is a matter of size versus speed as well. It also pits an accomplished technical high-flyer against a technical powerhouse. Here's the schedule. Friday and Saturday of this week are off-days. * Sunday 4/14/02 MHW Update #2 out (to announce new members and possibly more matches) * Monday 4/15/02 1st flashes due in by Midnight Central time * Tuesday 4/16/02 MHW Spotlight Series (flash packet) + 2nd flashes due in by Midnight Central time as before. * Wednesday 4/17/02 MHW Spotlight Series + In-rings due by Midnight Central * Thursday 4/18/02 MHW Fuel out. In-rings are not mandatory but are encouraged. The schedule will remain like this until we have a sufficient amount of members to get Firestorm started. The schedule will be reiterated in every Update. You are only limited 2 RPs per week. That means I don't want to see 5 RPs by one character in a single Spotlight Series. Be creative. Remember, longevity is not important. I want quality over quantity. You have between now and Monday night to work on your first flashes. That should be more than sufficient. If you sign up before Sunday, you may be put on the first card depending on the number of other approved applicants. Interested? Then check out the MHW website... http://milehigh.cjb.net I hope to see you there. Neal MHW Owner http://milehigh.cjb.net
