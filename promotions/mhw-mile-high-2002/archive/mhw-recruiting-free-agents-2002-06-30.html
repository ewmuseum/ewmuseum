[MHW] Recruiting [FREE AGENTS READ!]

30 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings, This letter is for all the Free Agents. Mile High Wrestling is looking for you. We are a regional fed based in Denver, Colorado. We are a booked fed and we hold one show per week. These alternate between MHW Fuel and MHW Firestorm. Fuel is a show where matches are written in summary form. The second, MHW Firestorm, is our flagship program. It is written entirely in a play-by-play and block format. Currently we have three titles, with plans to start new ones in the near future. We currently have the MHW Heavyweight Title - the top gold in the company, MHW Tag Team Titles - obvious, and Unsanctioned Title - similar to a Hardcore title. We are currently planning to unveil a Rocky Mountain Title following our first PPV, Hostile Intent. The Rocky Mountain Title will be the second best singles title. We are looking for tag teams, faces, and maybe even a few female characters. Although there currently isn't a Women's Title, we feel that one could be put together in the future. Please give the MHW a try. I know you won't regret it. Our site is at http://milehigh.cjb.net Check it out. Neal MHW Committee Member http://milehigh.cjb.net
