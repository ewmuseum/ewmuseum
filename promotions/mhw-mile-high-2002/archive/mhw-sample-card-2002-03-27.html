MHW Sample Card

27 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Somebody suggested that I write a sample card to help promote the MHW. Well, it's complete. I'm posting only part of it here (I guess AOL has a posting size limit :( )but you can find the whole thing on the website - http://milehigh.cjb.net Just follow the instructions on the main page to access it. IMPORTANT NOTICE: This card is only a sample used to show the writer's ability. The characters within have been created solely by the writer, [name withheld]. They include: "Iron Will" Neal Jennings "Brutal" Brad Corbin Kaleb Knight Dante Krieg Scot Hatcher Mike Hatcher Crystal Bleu Max "The Beast" Cayn "Sharpshooter" Shannon Rourke El Freeko Big Bubba Brown Crazy Horse "The Cutter" Curtis Calloway "Relentless" Curt Winters CARD LINEUP Singles Match Neal Jennings Vs. El Freeko Singles Match Crazy Horse Vs. Curtis Calloway Table Match Big Bubba Brown Vs. Max Cayn Falls Count Anywhere Tag Match Knight Watch (Knight & Corbin) Vs. The Hatcher Brothers w/ Crystal Bleu Main Event Dante Krieg w/ Sarah Mayson Vs. Curt Winters w/ Shannon Rourke [The scene fades in to the parking garage of the University of Southern Colorado. A motorcycle is heard as it approaches. The camera moves to a shot of Max "The Beast" Cayn on his Harley Davidson. He is dressed in civilian clothes: black leather jacket over a white t-shirt, and green cargo pants. He pulls into a parking space and shuts off the Harley. As he steps off, he is immediately confronted by MHW reporter Lenny "The Leech" Lorenzo.] Lenny: Uh, excuse me. Mr. Cayn? Can I do a promo with... [Cayn cuts him off.] Max: No, you can't. [Max starts walking toward the door.] Lenny: Please? [Max turns briefly, only to shake his head "no" before continuing inside. Lenny follows him in, still pestering.] Lenny: Mr. Cayn, please, do an interview with me. I need the money. [Again Max turns toward him.] Max: I don't care. Now leave me alone! [Max continues down the hall.] Lenny: Well, at least tell me what you think of your table match against Big Bubba Brown. [Once more Max turns toward Lenny, this time with an angry look on his face.] Max: Look! You leech! I'm not giving you an interview! Now get out of my face! [With that, he turns and goes into his locker room. Lenny tries to follow him in only to get the door slammed in his face. Then, murmuring is head from within. Lenny puts his ear to the door as the murmuring becomes clear.] Max: Big Bubba Brown... what a joke. It's pretty simple. I step into the ring, beat his ass, put him through a table, and step out of the ring. That fat mother[bleep] doesn't have a hope in hell against me. Tonight, he'll meet The Beast. And The Beast is about to Cayn Bomb your ass. [Lenny backs away from the door as Max stops talking. He tries to look totally professional, even though he's just eavesdropped on Cayn's ramblings.] Lenny: Well folks, you heard it here first. Courtesy of Lenny Lorenzo. [Thinking that the camera is off, he continues.] Lenny: Chalk up a bonus for another great interview. Now I gotta find someone else to interview.] [He walks off screen.] [The scene cuts to two men and a boy sitting 'round a camp fire. They are talking about the fishing trip they're planning. The oldest man of the group wears a pair of dirty overalls over a sweaty white t-shirt. Atop his head sits a faded "Bass Pro Club" baseball cap. He looks old enough to be the father of the other man, and grandfather of the boy. The younger man looks in better shape. His clothes are clean and look new. The child, of course, is dirty. As we move in, we pick up on their conversation.] Old Man: Jimmy, go get some more firewood. Younger Man: Dad, it's big enough. We don't want to start a forest fire. Old Man: Yeah, but the wood we got won't last all night. Go get some of the good stuff Jimmy. Just like we showed you. Jimmy: Okay! [The boy stands up and starts to run, but trips on a twig by the fire. Pieces of burning wood fall out of the fire and light up the surround grass.] Younger Man: Now you've done it! Let's get out of here! [The trio run off screen as the fire gains ground as well as ferociousness. Soon, the entire screen is engulfed in flames. Cue in The Prodigy's "Firestarter" as a silhouette of the word "FIRESTORM" is briefly seen before being engulfed by the flames.] [The scene then cuts to a shot of a cheering crowd at the University of Southern Colorado. A commentator's voice is heard.] Voice: Welcome folks, to Mile High Wrestling! We're at the University of Southern Colorado at Pueblo. My name's Rusty Waters and joining me is none other than Ronnie Vick. [The scene cuts to the commentators at ringside.] Ronnie: That's _"Slick"_ Ronnie Vick to you Waters. Rusty: Yeah. Whatever. Tonight we have a great show lined up. Ronnie: Hey, don't change the subject! Rusty: Whether you like it or now _"Slick"_, I have to. As I was saying, we have a great show planned for tonight. Let's get to it. [The scene cuts to the announcer, Dave "The Rave" Madison in the ring. He is getting a HUGE ovation from the women in the crowd.] Ronnie: What's the big deal about this Madison guy? He's not even a wrestler but all the hotties in the crowd love him. Rusty: Didn't you know? He's part of the local boy band, "The Sno Boyz" is what they call themselves. Ronnie: ... "The Sno Boys?" What a ridiculous name. I suppose you like them, eh Waters? Rusty: [Before Rusty can answer him, "The Rave" chims in.] Dave [in a singing voice]: Ladieeeeeees and gentle-men... [He clears his voice.] Dave: Excuse me. Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to Mile High Wrestling! [He gets an even larger reaction from the women. "Rock You Like A Hurricane" by The Scorpions echoes through the speakers.] Dave: Making his way to the ring at this time, from Chicago, Illinois,... he weighs in at 225 pounds,... "Iron Will," Neal Jennings! [Jennings steps out from behind the curtain. Wearing a black with gold striped sweatsuit, he slowly makes his way down to the ring. Slapping hands on the way. He slides under the bottom rope and raises his hands in the air.] [A fast-paced dance song comes on. The song has a bit of a Mexican melody incorporated in it.] Dave: And his opponent, from El Paso, Texas,... weighing in at 180 pounds,... El Freeko! [El Freeko comes out wearing a torned and stained white t-shirt, black bicycle shorts, white kneepads, long green socks, and green sneakers. He stands at the entranceway, with a depressed look on his face. Then, in a quick instant, he does a series of cartwheels down to the ring. He jumps on the apron and does a flip over the top rope. The music fades as he stands in the middle of the ring, staring down his opponent.] Ronnie: That music sucks! Thank God they shut it off! <DING!> <DING!> Rusty: That's the bell! Ronnie: Yes Waters. We know what a bell sounds like. You don't have to tell us for God's sake. [The action starts in the ring as the two combatants stare each other down. El Freeko makes a foolish move, lunging at Jennings. Iron Will stops him with a deep armdrag. But El Freeko counters it by sommersaulting and landing on his feet.] Ronnie: What a counter! [El Freeko returns the favor. This time, Iron Will takes the move without countering. El Freeko immediately goes into a cross armbreaker, wrenching hard on Jennings' arm and shoulder.] Rusty: Clever tactics here. He's trying to keep his opponent off on the ground. Rusty: Don't you think we see that? What, do you think we're all blind here? Rusty: Lighten up. I thought you were "Slick." Ronnie: I am. So shut it. [In the ring, both men are up but El Freeko quickly has Jennings in an arm wrench. Iron Will counters by getting El Freeko in a fireman's carry. El Freeko jumps to his feet, showing great athletic ability. But Jennings catches him on the way up with a big standing dropkick that sends El Freeko tumbling out of the ring.] Ronnie: El Freeko's going to turn this around real soon. You just watch. [Iron Will attempts a springboard plancha as El Freeko stumbles to his feet. But El Freeko quickly staggers to the side, causing Jennings to miss the move entirely. Jennings hits the security wall. El Freeko quickly capitalizes on the situation. He pulls Jennings up, hits a boot to the midsection, and plants him with a DDT.] Ronnie: Told ya'! It'll be all downhill for Jennings from here on out. [El Freeko again pulls Jennings up and rolls him into the ring. El Freeko gets on the apron and performs a legdrop from the outside, in.] Ronnie: Right on target baby! Rusty: El Freeko goes for the pin! ONE!!!! TWO!!!!! KICKOUT BY IRON WILL!!!! Ronnie: That was close! But mark my words, El Freeko will get him! Jennings can't last long with moves like that! [El Freeko goes for a springboard moonsault off the middle rope. But Neal Jennings gets the knees up. El Freeko lands hard.] Rusty: Nice counter there. The fight's not out of Iron Will just yet Ronnie. Ronnie: It's Slick! Slick! Call me Slick! Don't f[bleep]ing call me Ronnie! Got it?! Rusty: If you say so Ronnie. Ronnie: Damnit! I do say so! [In the ring, Iron Will has taken command of the match while Rusty and Ronnie, I mean Slick, were arguing.] Rusty: Do you realize how stupid that makes you look? Calling yourself Slick? What kind of moron are you? Slick: What?! It's not stupid! It's f[bleep]ing awesome! Rusty: Whatever you say Chief. In case you haven't noticed, we're missing the match. [El Freeko just kicked out of a pinning predicament but Rusty and Slick missed it.] Rusty: See what I mean? [In the ring, El Freeko has regained control of this apparently fast-paced matchup. El Freeko puts Jennings down with a slam. He then follows up with a rolling headbutt to the abdomen. He then pulls him up sits Jennings on the top turnbuckle. El Freeko climbs to the second turnbuckle, preparing for something. Iron Will fights back with shots to the ribs. He then shoves El Freeko off, sending him crashing to the mat. Jennings then squats on the top rope, waiting for something.] Rusty: Looks like he's waiting for El Freeko to get up. Smart plan, forcing El Freeko to expend some needed energy. Slick [sarcasticly]: Oh yeah. Great plan. Only problem is it's going to backfire on him. When El Freeko gets up, he's going to beat the hell out of Jennings. [El Freeko staggers to feet. And at the exact same time, Iron Will stands up and jumps off, executing a big missile dropkick to the face of El Freeko. Jennings then makes the cover.] ONE!! TWO!!!!! THR....... NO!!! El Freeko just got the shoulder up! Slick: You can't keep the freak down! There's just no way in hell! [Jennings puts El Freeko in a reverse chinlock, taking care to keep his knee right in the spine of El Freeko.] Rusty: He's trying to wear him down. Slick: A feeble attempt by a feeble man. [El Freeko fights to his feet, landing elbows to the gut, forcing Iron Will to release the hold. El Freeko then goes for a spin kick but Jennings catches it. El Freeko then connects with an enzugiri.] Slick: Look at that! Perfectly executed enzugiri! [With Jennings on the mat, El Freeko goes into a figure-4 leglock. El Freeko wrenches back with all his might, even using the ropes for leverage when the referee isn't looking.] Rusty: There's no need for cheating! Slick: You're right. There is no need for cheating. El Freeko can beat this guy clean. Besides, he wasn't cheating. Rusty: Then what do you call that? Slick: What? [Rusty points at El Freeko's use of the ropes.] Rusty: That. [Just as Slick looks up at the ring, the ref forces El Freeko to let go of the ropes] Slick: I didn't see anything. Rusty: He was holding the ropes! Slick: So, that's not cheating. That's perfectly legal in my book. Rusty: Your book doesn't count. Slick: Sure it does! [In the ring, Jennings refuses to give up. He even fights to turn the hold, putting pressure on El Freeko.] Rusty: Can he turn it?! Slick: No way. No way! [Iron Will succeeds in turning the lock on El Freeko. But El Freeko is too close to the ropes. He quickly grabs the bottom rope and Jennings is forced to relinquish the hold.] Slick: You see that? Perfect sense of where he is in the ring! El Freeko wasn't in the hold two seconds when he found the ropes. Rusty: That's just pure luck if you ask me. Slick: Well nobody asked you. And no, that wasn't luck. That was true skill on El Freeko's behalf. [Both men are having trouble getting up. El Freeko clearly has the advantage because he wasn't in the leglock as long as Jennings. He gets to his feet first and assists Jennings in getting to his unsteady feet. But no sooner is he up, then El Freeko sends him down again with a vicious superkick to the face.] Slick: That's it! Now hurry up and pin him! Rusty: Apparently El Freeko isn't done yet. [El Freeko goes to one corner and starts removing the padding for the top turnbuckle.] Rusty: What's he got planned now? Slick: Whatever it is, it's going to be great! [El Freeko grabs Jennings by the legs. Then, he catapults Iron Will head first into the exposed top turnbuckle, busting him wide open.] Slick: That was f[bleep]ing beautiful! Just beautiful! And Jennings is bleeding heavily too! [El Freeko sits Jennings on the exposed turnbuckle facing the crowd. He climbs up himself. Then, in an amazing move, he executes a reverse tornado DDT off the top.] Rusty: Wow! What a move! Slick: That's sure to do it for the Iron Dummy! [El Freeko goes for the pin, hooking the far leg.] ONE!! TWO!!!! THREE!!!!!!!!!!! Slick: It's all over! I told you Jennings didn't stand a chance! Rusty: Yes, surprisingly, you were right! Slick: Surprisingly?! Listen, from now on, listen to me when I say somebody's gonna win! Because as these people know, I'm _always_ right! Rusty: Well that remains to be seen. [The scene fades to a commercial.] [The scene cuts to the backstage. Scot and Mike Hatcher are in their locker room preparing for their tag match later in the show.] Mike: It's gonna be a tough one tonight Scot. Two big guys like Kaleb Knight and Brad Corbin? Yeah, it's gonna be tough. [Scot walks over to his brother.] Scot: Are you nervous? Mike: Sure I'm nervous. Aren't you? Scot: A little. But the way I see it, this is our chance to prove that we can hang with the big boys. [Their manager, Crystal Bleu comes into the locker room. She is in a good mood, evident from her tone of voice.] Crystal: Hey guys. What's going on? Mike: Not much Crystal. Just getting ready for our match tonight. Crystal: Oh yeah? Who ya' fighting? Scot: Brad Corbin and Kaleb Knight. Crystal: Brad Corbin and Kaleb Kn-- Scot, Mike, those guys are huge! Are you sure you're ready to face them? Mike: We're ready. Right bro? Scot: You got it. Let's show those two meatheads what it feels like to go up against the Hatcher Brothers. Mike: Ahem to that. Crystal: Come on. Let's go grab some Cokes. Scot: Sounds good to me. Mike: Me too. [The trio turn to leave the room when the door flies open. Two behemoths step in, revealing themselves as Brad Corbin and Kaleb Knight. The Hatchers rush them but are quickly stopped with hard punches. Knight scoops up Scot and powerslams him through a table. Corbin grabs Mike by the hair and sends him head first through a wall. Laughing, the duo leave the room.] Corbin: We'll see you boys later. [As they leave, Crystal stands up from her hiding place behind the door. Her jaw has literally dropped to the floor as she surveys the carnage. She then hurries to her downed friends] Crystal: Scot, wake up. Scot! [She shakes him violently, trying to wake him up. But Scot is out cold. She then goes over to the hole in the wall and peers in at Mike who lies motionless, sprawled over a couch in the next room. He too, is out cold. The scene cuts back to the ring where Dave Madison stands with a microphone in hand.] Dave: The following contest is set for one fall. [A rock song intertwined with a native Indian melody comes over the arena speakers.] Dave: Making his way to the arena first, from the Navajo Indian Reservations, he weighs in at 280 pounds,... Crazy Horse! [Crazy Horse comes out wearing an Indian headset. He looks ready for action. He scowls at the crowd as he walks to the ring. As he climbs the steps and goes through the ropes, he raises his hands in the air, only to be boo'ed by the crowd. He scowls again as the music fades.] ["Bad Blood" by Ministry hits the speakers now.] Dave: And his opponent, from the bad streets of Chicago, weighing in at 260 pounds,... "The Cutter"... Curtis Calloway! [Calloway comes out with a hyped look on his face. He quickly makes his way down the ramp. Stopping occasionally to slap hands with some of the fans. He then slides under the bottom rope. Crazy Horse immediately goes in for the attack. He stomps away on Calloway, preventing him from getting up.] <DING!> <DING!> Rusty: We're back with this great one-on-one match-up folks. Crazy Horse isn't wasting any time in getting the upper hand. Slick: Quite a good plan wouldn't you say? Rusty: I must say, I was disgusted at the way Brad Corbin and Kaleb Knight handled themselves backstage moments ago. Slick: What was wrong with it? They were just letting the Hatchers know that they were around. Rusty: Letting them know? It looked to me like a beatdown. Slick: Well it didn't look that way to me. So maybe you should get your eyes checked out. [Crazy Horse is relentlessly pounding away on Calloway, backing him into the corner with a flurry of rights and lefts. The Cutter ducks one shot and shoves Crazy Horse into the corner. And now Calloway unloads on him. Calloway drives a knee hard into the ribs of his opponent before hip tossing him out of the corner.] Rusty: We have a great Main Event tonight folks. Two master technicians will square off. It's Curt Winters facing Dante Krieg. [In the ring, Calloway executes a DDT on Crazy Horse.] Slick: But at the moment, that match is of no importance. It hasn't even started yet so why talk about it? [Calloway tries to hit a vertical suplex but Crazy counters with one of his own.] Rusty: I'm just letting the fans know what's in store tonight. Slick: You're always so worried about the future. Why don't you just sit back, shut up, and let things happen when the time comes. [Crazy Horse continues to mount his comeback, hitting a big side slam followed by a knee to the face. He then backs off, poising himself for another attack. Calloway slowly staggers to his feet. As he turns around, Crazy Horse runs in and connects with a huge clothesline, flipping Calloway completely over.] Slick: What a clothesline! This Crazy Horse really is one hell of an athlete! [Crazy Horse lays into The Cutter with hard right hands to the face as Calloway lies face up on the canvas. Crazy Horse then applies a blatant choke, forcing the referee to start the mandatory five count.] Rusty: Now what kind of crap is that? Ref: One....... Slick: There's nothing wrong with a little choking every now and then. Ref: Two........ [At the commentary table, Slick starts choking Rusty. Not that hard at first, but gaining pressure.] Ref: Three....... [Rusty is gagging at ringside.] Ref: Four.......... [Slick lets off the choke on Rusty just as Crazy Horse releases his choke on Calloway. At ringside, Rusty slouches in his chair, gasping for air. Slick just laughs.] Slick: That was fun. Rusty [still recovering]: That was [gasp] unnecessary. Slick: Well now you know what Calloway felt like. Hehehe. Rusty: Do that again and you'll find my boot up your rear. Slick [laughing]: You couldn't put your boot up a donkey's ass let alone mine. [Back in the ring, Crazy Horse has Calloway propped up on the top turnbuckle, facing the crowd. Crazy Horse climbs up and executes a belly-to-back superplex.] Rusty: What a devastating move! [Crazy Horse quickly recovers and covers The Cutter.] ONE!!!! TWO!!!!! THR-- Rusty: No! Calloway got the shoulder up! And Crazy Horse is pissed! Slick: Wouldn't you be? He just hit a big move that shoulda' put his opponent away but it didn't. No wonder he's mad! [Crazy Horse goes back to stomping at Calloway. The Cutter tries to avoid it by rolling away. Crazy Horse follows him, still trying to stomp him down. Finally Calloway rolls out of the ring in an attempt to recouperate.] Slick: What a coward. He knows he can't beat Crazy Horse so he leaves the ring. Rusty: He's just trying to get some air. There's nothing wrong with that. Slick: Nothing wrong with it? tHERE'S NO TIME-OUTS IN WRESTLING! rUSTY: oH, BUT IT'S OKAY WHEN SOMEONE YOU WANT TO WIN DOES THAT RIGHT? sLICK: oF COURSE. [Calloway continues his assault, slamming Crazy Horse's head off the announce table.] Slick: Get out of here you big buffoon! [Calloway ignores him as he continues to drive Crazy Horse's face into the table. Slick looks like he's ready to jump in on the action.] Rusty: Settle down Slick. He'd kill you.
