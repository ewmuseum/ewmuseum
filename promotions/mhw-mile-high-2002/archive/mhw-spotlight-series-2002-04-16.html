[MHW] Spotlight Series [4/17/02]

16 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
--Mile High Wrestling Mailing List-- [MHW] Spotlight Series [4/17/02] Today in the Spotlight: "Premium" River Rockwell "The Atomic Son" Sean Malcomb "The Asian Sensation" Xu Jing *************** MHW Fuel Lineup *************** MHW Fuel Taped from: Gerald R. Ford Amphitheatre, Vail, Colorado Thursday, April 18, 2002 SINGLES MATCH "The Educator" Thane Sloan VS. "The Asian Sensation" Xu Jing -- It's East VS. West as the Martial Arts Master, Xu Jing, takes on the big man from the United States West Coast. MAIN EVENT SINGLES MATCH "The Atomic Son" Sean Malcomb VS. "Premium" River Rockwell -- This match is a matter of size versus speed as well. It also pits an accomplished technical high-flyer against a technical powerhouse. ************************ "Premium" River Rockwell ************************ [The scene opens in a small Denver condominum, looking out the window on a sunny morning. We hear a radio in the background playing light rock music, along with the sounds of banging on the walls. As the camera zooms out, we can see that the place is pretty, albeit barren. Suddenly, the door slams open, and we see a walking box enter the room. The box lowers, and we see the face of a young man, about 28 years old. He has short brown hair, and a beard-moustache. He struggles into the room, and sets the box on the ground. On the side of the box is a label reading "Playing cards." The box is a cube, probably about 2 ft in length. Behind him through the doorway, "Premium" River Rockwell enters the room.] Man : What the hell is in this box? Rockwell : Oh, that's my playing cards. Man : WHAT? The whole box? Rockwell : Yeah... I decided I didn't need the other card box, so it's just this one. Man : You had two boxes of playing cards?! Rockwell : Yeah... I collect them. Okay, Will... there's about 3 more boxes in the truck. You wanna get them, and I'll mix us up something to drink? [For the record, the other man is Will Runner, long time friend and personal butler to River Rockwell. Their backstory is for another time.] Runner : Yeah sure. Got any Bacardi? Rockwell : Yeah, I'll have one of those too actually, I think. I'll make them up, you go get those bricks. Runner : Bricks? Rockwell : Yeah, the last three boxes are my brick collection. [Will sighs and exits the condo, as Rockwell begins mixing drinks on the kitchen counter. A few moments later, Will enters the room carrying a very very heavy and large box. He sets it down next to the playing card box.] Rockwell : Be careful with that! Those are genuine! Runner : Yeah... whatever. Let me get that drink. [Rockwell hands Will the glass containing Coke and Bacardi Rum.] Rockwell : I can't believe the nerve of that DJ yesterday. He practically called me a bitch ON AIR! Runner : Yeah, maybe you should have been a little nicer to him. Rockwell : What? You're out of your mind, I practically bended over backwards to make sure his information was correct. Runner : He had a good point though, River. Your first match is against a multi-time world champion. I did some research on him on the flight up here. He's quite accomplished. Rockwell : It really doesn't matter. Look, whether I win or lose against Atom Man, what it all boils down to is that this is the start of something big for me. I'm going to lead the local wrestling community into a new era. The "Premium" era will begin at the Fuel taping, and it won't slow down until I say so. Mr. Melcombe will have to either join me, or get out of the way. I have no intention of catering to him. In the end, he's just like the rest of them. Runner : River, what the hell are you talking about? Rockwell : All I'm saying is that things are gonna change around here. Runner : How do you know? You just got here. Maybe things will stay the same, but you'll think they changed. Rockwell : Shut up, Will! [River laughs, but quickly shrugs it off] Rockwell : I dunno man, I just can't wait to get into the ring. I'll be there to christen the MHW ring. And from that point on, Mile High Wrestling will be synonymous with River Rockwell. I will be their banner man. I'll be the champion. Runner : By the way, you need to check out Malcomb's wife. She's really really hot. Rockwell : Oh? I might need to get me a peice of that. Runner : She's married... I didn't say you should DO anything about it. Rockwell : Marriage is only a state of mind. Rockwell is a state of passion. I'll have to *cough* introduce myself to her. [Will shakes his head and River laughs as the scene fades out.] ***************************** "The Atomic Son" Sean Malcomb ***************************** [The scene opens up with a dimly outlined silhouette of a man taking shots at a punching bag with a light moving in the background around the silhouette. "Rock Superstar" by Cypress Hill begins to play in the background as it the scene dissolves into a panning view of Denver, Colorado, which then dissolves into an arena entrace bursting with pyro.] Voice: Determination. [The scene flashes and fades into a bit more prominite lighting around the silhouette of the man punching the large bag.] Voice: Skill. [The scene turns to a compilation of different amateur wrestlers performing powerful moves in a training ring. Then the scene turns into a large crowd of cheering fans.] Voice: Glory. [The scene then dissolves into the silhouette of the man training, and the light rises high to a flash.] Voice: Victory. [The flash dies down and shows Sean Malcomb in a training singlet throwing hard punches into the large bag.] Voice: Mile High Wrestling. A mile above the competition. [The scene then places a graphic with the MHW logo and the information for the upcoming event in Vail, Colorado. The scene then pans out to show that this was all on a television screen in a fancy hotel room. Standing near the television is Sean Malcomb wearing a white, long-sleeved collared shirt, black pants, and fixing a black tie around his neck. As the camera continues to pan out, in a plush white robe on a couch facing the television, Jennifer Malcomb uses the remote control in her hand to turn off the television.] Jenn Malcomb: What the hell is that? Sean Malcomb: The first promotional commercial for Mile High. I thought it looked pretty good. Jenn Malcomb: So this is what you've been up to? Filming half assed promotional spots for this tiny organization? Sean Malcomb: They figured it would bring attention. Colorado's seen me wrestle before, so they thought I'd be a good choice to use in their advertisements. Jenn Malcomb: So you're going to be the poster boy for all this? Sean Malcomb: Well... Jenn Malcomb: You want to be the one all these people associate with this tiny traveling circus? Sean Malcomb: Circus? Jenn Malcomb: That's what they call a bunch of fools going from location to location, isn't it? Sean Malcomb: Jenn, it's not that bad. I'm not the only star they've had doing contract dealings. Hell, Peregrine was even negotiating with them for a bit, might still be. Jenn Malcomb: I doubt it. Frank seems to have the smarts to stay away until there's something to work with here. I know you like your adventures, Sean, but this is ridiculous. You took us away from home, Sean. I was getting used to Florida. I actually started to like Tampa. Sean Malcomb: I like it there too, and we'll get back there eventually. But right now, this is something I have to do, and I thought you'd understand. I have to fight, I have to compete. It's all I know, it's who I am. Jenn Malcomb: You couldn't do that in Tampa? You couldn't find some place that would allow us to stay at our home? Sean Malcomb: Maybe. Jenn Malcomb: Maybe? Probably. Most definitely. You were a superstar, Sean. You had your chance to go anywhere you want. Sean Malcomb: Honey, it's not about that. Jenn Malcomb: Then what is it about? What could possibly make you move our home so abruptly? Sean Malcomb: Don't you see? It's not about the fame, or the money. It's about the fans in a way, and yet it's about the competition. It's about victory and about defeat. I didn't come here to make money or be a star. Jenn Malcomb: Isn't that what wrestling is all about? Sean Malcomb: Maybe to all those other guys you've seen and interviewed over the years. It isn't about that to me, I grew up fighting on the streets, I see it differently. Jenn Malcomb: How? How do you see this as anything good? Sean Malcomb: Easily. It's new, you can forge your own way here. You can earn the respect all over again. The fights are new, the people are new. Even people I've fought before, we're in a new place. Here is where everyone starts over, doesn't have to follow what was laid before them. You make your name here and now. You get to build something here, help mold it in your own way. It's all about respect, honor, and the will to compete. Nothing more, before all the theatrics take over the show, here is where we get to put in the good hard work to show the world what you're made of. Jenn Malcomb: Oh god, this is way too early for a mid-life crisis. Sean Malcomb: Not a crisis, I'm not trying to feel alive... I'm trying to reforge myself. Build myself into the man I want to be. Jenn Malcomb: And you need to do this? Sean Malcomb: I believe I do. Jenn Malcomb: Well....if you need this... Sean Malcomb: I do. I think this is the only way. Jenn Malcomb: Alright. Then, my love, I'll be behind you. I'll support you with all my heart. I told you I'd be there for better or for worse a couple years ago, and I'll hold true to my vow. I love you, and that's what matters. But I have one question. Sean Malcomb: Yes dear? Jenn Malcomb: Why this specific upstart? Why out here? There's plenty of upstart organizations in Florida. Why do you feel this one is any better? Sean Malcomb: I just have a feeling. This is going to be something good. [With that comment, Jenn Malcomb finally lets a small smile form on her lips. Sean grins and leans in and gives her a kiss then strides off camera. Jenn then uses the remote and turns the television back on. She begins to flip through channels and the scene fades to black.] ***************************** "The Asian Sensation" Xu Jing ***************************** [The camera opens on a forested mountainside. It begins to scan the mountainous landscape, before spotting something that looks like a human within the mass of trees. The camera zooms in, and the closer view shows that this is a man wearing a white karate suit. He appears to be Asian.] Voice off-screen: That's him! Other voice: Let's go then! [The camera now shows the ground. The ground changes rapidly as the cameraman apparently runs toward the person that was just spotted. After about a minute of this, the camera again shows the green mountainside. Now, the view is closer, but the person spotted earlier is no longer visible. William Waterford stumbles onto the screen, breathing heavily.] WW: I know that was him! Where in God's name did he go? Cameraman: I have no idea. WW: Well, you've got a camera with a zoom lens. Why don't you look for him? Cameraman: Hey, buddy, I'M not the one who wants to find him. What do I need him for? The guy doesn't even speak English! You said so yourself! WW: Look, you're working for the MHW. Now, if Thursday comes and Xu Jing isn't in Vail to fight Thane Sloan, then some fans are going to be very angry. I don't want to deal with angry fans and I don't think you want to, either. I've been calling over to his house for a week, and now I've been searching around some town called "Empire" for two days. Cameraman: It took you two days to search that place? WW: Yes, as you can tell, I'm not exactly fit to be running around. Now, Jing doesn't know that he has a match Thursday night, and if we don't find him to tell him, he'll never know. So take that camera and FIND HIM!!! [Once again, the camera scans the mountainside. Now, the forest in the distance is still. There is no sign of the man spotted earlier, whom we presume to be Xu Jing.] Cameraman: I'm not seeing him... WW: Bloody martial artist! That forest is huge! He could be anywhere. We'd best get to starting to look for him in there now. Cameraman: Shall we take the van? WW: Yes we damn well should! I don't feel like walking all that way! [The camera then cuts to black.] Nice work you 3. Everyone, remember that there will be a special Update following Fuel on Thursday regarding a change in the schedule. Also, if you want to have an in-ring on this week's Fuel, you're welcome to send one in but I need it by Midnight tonight (Wednesday). Strats aren't really needed this week as Fuel is a summary show. Once we get started with Firestorm (hopefully in the next week or 2) strats will be more important. That's all for now. --(c) Copyright 2002, Mile High Sports Inc.--
