PVWF Monday Night Madness, 29 December 1997

29 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene opens to the crowd going mad. A brief chant of "Buddha" flares up. The camera catches signs that say: "FMW - Fully Moronic Wrestling", "Where's Split Top?", "Zanghi Wears Pink Pants" and "Bring Back Kurasawa".) F: "Welcome to the beautiful Taco Bell Dome on Pickup Hill for tonight's edition of PVWF Monday Night Madness! I'm Frenchy, along side Buddha and 'Mean' Mark Chase." MMC: "Folks, X-Mas Explosion last night was unbelieveable! We saw new tag team champions crowned! The Television and Intercontinental titles changed hands. Also, we saw the shocking return of Zwa to the PVWF. Could we see Zwa/Langworthy at the Royal Rumble? Only time will tell." B: "I'm still wondering why we let Big Mouth Chase into the broadcast team." MMC: "Buddha.." F: "Well, Buddha, we needed someone else in here who knew a little about wrestling. I mean, I was carrying the show. You're a complete imbecile." B: "Why thank you.. hey!" MMC: "Another interesting point from last night's X-Mas Explosion is that the Truth Commission got a couple more belts. Slim & Elle won the triangle tag team match, controversially, and become the new PVWF Tag Team champions. Locker room talk says that the Truth Commission is trying to grab as many titles as possible, and new members could be in the works." F: "Interesting. On tonight's show, new TV champ Pearl will defend against former champion 'the Bizarre One' Stephen McCoy! Tree will be in action, as will Big Dave! US champion Woody will defend his title against Nate 'Mongo' Smith! And in our main event, Slim & Elle put their new-found Tag Team titles on the line against Los Boricuas!" MMC: "Los Boricuas will be tagging up? What about the injury Phil Gier suffered last night during his loss to Nick Brown?" B: "Shut up, Chase. Not everyone is a little wuss like you." MMC: "Buddha, Gier broke his arm last night. Do you seriously think he can wrestle tonight?" F: "Stranger things have happened. Well, we've got an action packed two hours of wrestling ahead of us, and let's get to the ring for the first match of the evening." (Nation of Domination's music begins to play. KOD, Clifton Lynch, Nick Clark & Dark Meat all step out of the entrance way. Clark is wearing a neck brace.) F: "Nick Clark suffered a terrible beating at the hands of Tree last night." MMC: "Speaking of Tree, if you'll remember back at Valentine's Day Massacre, he nearly became the PVWF World champion. He is looking for another shot at that title, but I don't think Aaron Bolles is willing to give him one." B: "Bolles isn't willing to give anyone a title shot. He's interested in keeping the belt inside the Truth Commission, and keep that monopoly on the PVWF's titles alive." F: "Tree will be looking to add another victim to his reign on terror tonight at the hands of the Nation of Domination. Last night it was Nick Clark, tonight he goes after Dark Meat." (Tree's theme music begins to play. He comes out of the entrance way and walks without expression to the ring. He is wearing a black singlet.) MMC: "Tree is a phenomenal athlete. The moves he can perform are extraordinary, for someone of his height." B: "Big deal. I can do a 450 splash." (Tree stares down KOD, Lynch and Clark before getting into the ring.) F: "Sure. Tree steps into the ring, and here we go. Dark Meat tries to take out Tree's knee, but Tree caught him with a kick to the butt of the jaw. Dark Meat hits the mat ... and Tree connects with the Tree Stomp! It's over already!" B: "Not quite yet ... here comes the rest of the Nation." F: "Clifton Lynch gets up on the apron ... and Tree knocks him back down with a drop kick! KOD is up on the apron now ... but Tree gets him by the throat! Choke slam off the apron by Tree!" MMC: "Meanwhile, Nick Clark has remained at his spot at ringside. If he learned one thing last night, he learned not to mess with Tree." B: "Dark Meat is back up now, and he attacks Tree from behind!" F: "Yes, Tree was occupied with Clifton and the King of Diamonds, and he forgot all about Dark Meat. Dark Meat is trying diligently to take Tree down, but it's not working. A barrage of punches by Mister Meat leaves Tree leaning against the ropes ... and a big clothesline by Meat finally sends him over!" MMC: "Under normal circumstances, the NOD would be all over Tree now. Unfortuantely for Dark Meat, it looks like Clifton is the only one willing to do so." B: "Yeah, KOD is still out after that choke slam, and Clark knows better." F: "Clifton attacks Tree, but he doesn't seem to be doing much damage. All he's succeeding in doing is making Tree madder. Tree grabs Clifton around the neck ... choke slam! Clifton is left lying next to KOD, both unconscious! Tree climbs back into the ring and goes back to the offensive on Dark Meat. Tree hits a nice drop kick that takes Meat down hard. Now Tree signals for, and hits, the Tree Stomp once again! This time, there's no Nation of Domination to interfere! Tree pulls Dark Meat up by the trunks ... wraps that big hand of his around Meat's neck ... Choke Slam!" B: "Turn out the lights, it's all over for Dark Meat." F: "Tree makes the cover with one finger. 1......2......3! Tree handily defeats Dark Meat here on Monday Night Madness." (Tree looks directly into the camera and says, "Bolles, you're next.") MMC: "If I were Aaron Bolles, I'd be doing my best to avoid Tree." B: "That's because you're a crybaby." F: "Now, let's go back to the locker room area, where Jerry 'The King' Skurcenski is standing by with Zwa." (Camera cuts to backstage area. Jerry 'The King' Skurcenski is standing next to Zwa, who is in street clothes.) JTKS: "Thanks, Frenchy. Zwa, let me say that's it's great to see you back in the PVWF." Z: "Let me say that the pleasure is all yours." JTKS: "Arrogant as always, I see. Zwa, last night at X-Mas Explosion you made a dramatic entrance at the end of the card and attacked 'Nature Boy' Nick Langworthy. What were your motives?" Z: "Flash back to last year's X-Mas Explosion. That was the event where the nWo turned on me, and I made the challenge to Nick for a match. Nick ducked my challenges then, and every other time afterwards, and I've never gotten my revenge. Now that I've finally recovered from the broken leg I suffered earlier this year, I think it's high time I get my match with Langworthy." JTKS: "So you're challenging Langworthy to a match?" Z: "Yeah, stupid. However, I don't want the match at the Royal Rumble. I'd like to concentrate on the Royal Rumble match itself, and not worry about another match on the side. Therefore, I don't want the match at Valentine's Day Massacre, 'cause I'll be too busy getting my shot at the World title after I win the Royal Rumble." JTKS: "But you do want the match soon, I take it." Z: "That's correct. I want the match this coming week on Monday Night Madness!" (Zwa storms away.) JTKS: "How about that? Zwa versus 'Nature Boy' Nick Langworthy next week on Madness? Will Langworthy accept? I guess we'll find out soon. Back to you, Frenchy." (Camera cuts back to the announce booth.) F: "Wow. Fans, when we return, Big Dave will be in action! Stay with us!" (Commercial) (Return to Rok in the ring.) F: "Welcome back to Madness.." B: "You're kidding me! We have to watch another match involving Rok? Well, at least Big Dave will be here to spice this one up." MMC: "Big Dave must still be hurting from that grueling tables and ladders match last night at X-Mas Explosion. Rok is a talented wrestler, and he may be able to pull off an upset tonight." ("Tubthumping" by Chumbawamba begins to play over the loudspeakers. Big Dave struts out of the entrance way, wearing a South Park t-shirt and blue tights.) F: "Here comes Big Dave! It sure is odd to see him without the Television title draped over his shoulder." B: "He was robbed of the belt last night, and Zanghi said that there would be no rematch. Dave got screwed." (Dave argues with a ringside fan for a moment, then climbs into the ring.) MMC: "Buddha, Zanghi made that statement before the match began, so that comment holds no bearing." F: "How do you like that South Park t-shirt Dave is wearing?" MMC: "What's South Park?" B: "Forget the t-shirt, the match started. Dave is going to put on a show tonight. I have a feeling." F: "Collar and elbow lock up. Now Rok gets out of it and applies a waist lock. He tries for a german suplex, but Dave somersaulted out of it. Rok doesn't know where Dave went ... and Dave nails him with a spinning heel kick! Rok goes down, and Dave heads to the top rope. Dave comes off his a flying head butt ... but Rok got out of the way and Dave's head cracked against the mat!" MMC: "Dave's in trouble now." B: "Shut up, Chase. Dave's not in any trouble whatsoever. He could beat Rok with his eyes closed." F: "Rok is taking advantage of Dave's miscue. He whips Dave into the ropes ... and Rok hits a big back body drop! Dave is down. Rok comes down hard on him with a leg drop. Now Rok lifts Dave onto his shoulders ... samoan drop! Rok is dominating this match-up! Rok goes for a suplex now, but Dave blocked it ... and he suplexes Rok!" B: "I thought Dave was in trouble, stupid." MMC: "He was.." F: "Dave pulls Rok back up ... Northern Lights suplex! 1.....2.... and Rok kicks out at the last second. Dave goes off the ropes ... and he hits a nice sunset flip! 1...2... and Rok kicks out again. Rok is still down, though, and Dave stomps on him." B: "He's just setting him up for the Frog Splash. It's so obvious." MMC: "Dave has many great maneuvers he can use. He's got that Frog Splash, the fallaway slam he calls the 'Woohoo Slam', and the springboard hurancanrana roll-up." B: "Yeah." F: "Rok is back to his feet. Dave goes for a spinning heel kick, but Rok ducked and Dave flew right over top of him. Rok grabs Dave and locks on a sleeper hold. Dave is starting to go down ... no, he's lifting Rok's up on his back! Here it is .. Woo-Hoo Slam!" B: "It's all over now. Cover him, Dave!" MMC: "Dave is too much of a jerk. He wants to inflict some more punishment." F: "You're right. Dave is headed to the top rope ... Frog Splash! No, Rok got his knees up and Dave got the wind knocked out of him! Dave staggers back to his feet as Rok goes off the ropes ... bulldog! 1....2.... and Dave kicks out in the nick of time! Rok sends Dave into the ropes ... back body drop ... no, swinging neckbreaker by Dave! Dave goes to the apron. Rok gets back to his feet ... and there's the springboard hurancanrana roll-up by Dave! Rok rolled through, though! 1.......2........3! What an upset! Rok defeats the most decorated wrestler in PVWF history, cleanly!" MMC: "What a boost in the career of Rok!" B: "Chase, you jinxed him! You said Rok might pull an upset.." F: "Big Dave is livid! He is beating Rok into a bloody pulp in the ring. Now he props Rok up on the top rope ... top rope hurancarana by Big Dave! Now he goes out of the ring and grabs a steel chair?!? This is uncalled for." B: "Uncalled for? Wouldn't you be upset if you lost to a jobber?" F: "Dave sets that steel chair on Rok's face, and heads to the top rope once again! Dave comes off with a Frog Splash ... but Rok got out of the way and Dave hit the chair flush! I think Dave's head hit that chair. Rok is getting out of there while he can, and I can't blame him! Dave is holding his head in the ring, flopping around." B: "That little scoundrel ran away ... he knew Dave was going to kill him." MMC: "That'll probably go down in history as Big Dave's most embarassing loss." F: "Dave is back up now, and he's looking for Rok. He picks up the chair and heads back to the locker room area. I hope, for Rok's sake, that Dave doesn't find him.." B: "I hope he does find him, and I hope he breaks Rok's neck!" MMC: "Harsh." (Brett "Hitman" Miner's theme music begins to play. He comes out of the entrance way with the Intercontinental title over his shoulder. Justin Young is coming out with him.) F: "Here come Brett Miner and Justin Young.." MMC: "Brett is wearing the Intercontinental title belt that he won last night from Billy Dahl of Los Boricuas." B: "Very observant." (Brett & Justin get into the ring and do a little dance. Frenchy gets into the ring to interview them.) F: "Brett, congratulations on becoming only the second two-time PVWF Intercontinental champion. What's next on your agenda?" BHM: "Well, Frenchy, next on our agenda is getting a belt around this guy's waist. (points at Justin) You see, Justin feels like I am superior to him, because I have a title and he doesn't. Therefore, we are going to attempt to get him a title as soon as possible." F: "That certainly doesn't bode well for the rest of the PVWF's champions." BHM: "It especially doesn't bode well for Woody. Woody is wearing Justin's United States title belt like he owns it or something. Way back last November, Clifton Lynch cheated to get the US title away from Justin. That was the event that sent Justin into the rafters. Now that I've coaxed him to come down, he's planning on getting that belt back, and getting it back soon. That's all we have to say now." (Miner & Young leave the ring.) B: "I guess Justin can't speak for himself." MMC: "Fans, when we return, the afore-mentioned Woody will defend his US title against Nate 'Mongo' Smith! And later on, in our main event, Slim and Elle are scheduled to put their Tag Team titles on the line against Phil Gier & Billy Dahl! Stay with us!" (Commercial) (Return to Nate 'Mongo' Smith coming to the ring, haliburton in hand.) F: "Here comes Mongo. Last night at X-Mas Explosion, Mongo called upon the assistance of his brother and former tag team partner Gump to pull off a tainted win over Josh Ennis." MMC: "Yes, and I was half-expecting Gump to accompany Mongo to the ring tonight." (Woody's theme music begins to play, and he comes running out of the entrance way full speed. He has the United States title belt around his waist.) B: "He'd better slow down. He's apt to pull a muscle." F: "Woody jumps into the ring, and here we go! Woody is slugging away on Mongo, and Mongo is rattled. Woody floors Mongo with a big right hook, and Mongo went down like a ton of bricks. Woody pulls Mongo up ... piledriver ...... no, back body drop by Mongo takes the United States champion down. Mongo goes off the ropes ... and connects with a big leg drop. 1...2... kickout." B: "Do you think Mongo can win the United States title tonight?" MMC: "Well, he is a former TV champ, and a two-time tag team champion. Anything is possible." F: "Mongo pulls Woody up by the trunks. Now he executes a nice snap suplex. Now Mongo is pounding Woody's head viciously against the mat. Mongo goes for a powerbomb ... reversed into a head scissors takeover by Woody! Woody with an Oklahoma Roll! 1...2... and Mongo kicks out. Mongo gets back to his feet ... but Woody hits a nice sunset flip! 1... and another kickout by Mongo. Woody drapes Mongo's throat over the bottom rope, and now he's choking him out with it. 1...2...3...4... and Woody releases Mongo to avoid disqualification." B: "Why would he want to avoid DQ? He'd still keep the belt." MMC: "Yes, but a clean win of Mongo would give him even more credibility. Many people think this title reign is a fluke." F: "I wouldn't call someone with two victories over Tonzee a fluke. Woody lifts Mongo up ... gorilla press slam! That's going to do it!" B: "Not so fast! Here comes Gump!" F: "Gump is now in the ring, and he's all over Woody! There's the bell, this is going to be a disqualification win for Woody. Gump sends Woody into the ropes ... powerslam! We know what that's the prelude to! Gump lifts Woody up and puts him in the Rack! Woody is pleading with Gump to let him go, but Gump will hear none of it!" MMC: "Gump is making a statement here." F: "Gump finally releases Woody, and now he helps Mongo to his feet. Fans, let's go to Billy Pylpciw." BP: "Wow. What an event we've seen so far here on Pickup Hill. Fans, without further adieu, let me introduce to you the former US champion, the Mighty Tonzee!" (Tonzee steps out of the entrance way wearing street clothes. The crowd gives him a big pop.) BP: "Tonzee, let me begin by asking you.." (Just then, Woody walks by, holding his back.) T: "Hey, Woody! Nice match last night." W: "Uhh ... thanks, Tonz." (Tonzee extends a hand to Woody. Woody is reluctant to accept the handshake.) T: "C'mon, let's let bygones be bygones." (Woody shakes Tonzee's hand. Tonzee promptly pulls him in and executes a short clothesline. He then pulls Woody back up and powerbombs him on the steel entrance ramp.) T: "Ha! This interview is over!" (Tonzee walks out, leaving Woody laying motionless on the steel. Billy Plypciw goes down to attend to Woody.) F: "What a monster that Tonzee is. Fans, we'll be right back with a TV title match! Don't go away!" (Commercial) (Return to 'the Bizarre One' Stephen McCoy coming to the ring, dragging Marlena behind him.) F: "Welcome back to Madness. Here comes 'the Bizarre One', and he will attempt to regain the TV title here tonight against Pearl." MMC: "McCoy is too worried about people looking at Marlena to concentrate on winning the TV title for a second time." (McCoy steps into the ring and orders Marlena to remove his robe and wig. She reluctantly does so.) F: "What a jerk." (Pearl's theme music begins to play. He comes out of the entrance way holding the TV title high above his head. McRib is with him as always, and they give some high-fives to the fans on their way to the ring.) F: "Pearl won the TV title last night in a tables and ladders match with Big Dave. That match was non-stop action, and I wouldn't be surprised if it won match of the year." B: "Nah. My vote goes to Shootist/Slim from back at War Games." MMC: "I'd give it to Bolles/Pearl in the finals of the World title tournament from WrestleMania." F: "Well, I guess we'll find out at the next Slammy Awards coming up this spring. Well, let's get this TV title match underway, shall we?" MMC: "This is Pearl's first singles title in the PVWF, and it'll be interesting to see how long he keeps it." F: "Pearl has a wristlock applied on TBOSM. TBOSM gets out of it with some elbows to the kidney area of Pearl. TBOSM locks on a headlock ... but Pearl gets out of that with a belly-to-back suplex! La Magistral cradle by Pearl! 1...2... and a kickout by TBOSM. Pearl pulls McCoy back up ... powerbomb ... no, Razor's Edge ... no, it's some weird thing..." MMC: "That's a Gory Special! Invented by the late, great Gory Guerrero!" B: "Thanks, Tenay." MMC: "Shut up, tubby." F: "Boys.. Pearl has that Gory Special applied on TBOSM, and this could be it already. Now Pearl pulls McCoy over and he hits the mat hard, face first! What a manuever by the PVWF Television champion! Pearl goes up to the second rope ... and comes down on TBOSM with a double axehandle. Pearl sends McCoy into the ropes ... and nails a beautiful power slam! Pearl locks on a Fujinawa armbar, and McCoy is in agony. However, he is able to get to the ropes and Pearl must release the hold. McCoy just caught Pearl offguard with a low blow! Pearl goes down.." B: "Wouldn't you?" F: "McCoy is going for the Curtain Call! We're going to have a new TV champion! He lifts Pearl up ... there it is! I don't believe it! McCoy goes for the cover ... no, he goes outside the ring?!? What's going on?" MMC: "Marlena was fraternizing with PVWF ring announcer Andy Cusimano! McCoy's not gonna stand for that!" F: "McCoy is going after Andy Cusimano now! McCoy has him cornered ... and he knocks Cusimano down with a big left! He's a madman! TBOSM is now repremanding Marlena. Meanwhile, in the ring, Pearl has recovered from the Curtain Call, and is back to his feet. Pearl executes a plancha on TBOSM! Now he pulls McCoy back up and rolls him into the ring. Pearl heads to the top rope ... Pearl Jam! 1....2....3! Pearl retains the TV title in his first defense!" MMC: "Like I said before the match, TBOSM was too worried about Marlena to concentrate on winning this match!" F: "Fans, we have to take one last commercial break. When we return, our main event! Slim and Elle will defend the PVWF Tag Team titles against Los Boricuas! Stay tuned!" (Commercial) (Return to Los Boricuas coming to the ring. Their manager, Juan Vazquez, is waving a huge Puerto Rican flag. Phil Gier has a cast on his right arm. Billy Dahl looks determined.) F: "Welcome back. Here come Los Boricuas! Juan, Phil and Billy are coming to the ring for a Tag Team title match." MMC: "I don't understand how Phil is planning wrestling. He, of course, suffered a broken arm last night during his match with Nick Brown, when he missed on an attempted Elbow of Death and hit the ringpost." (Billy grabs a microphone before he and the rest of Los Boricuas get into the ring.) BD: "Obviously, Phil is in no condition to wrestle tonight. However, the executive commitee still saw fit to sign us for a Tag Team title match against Slim and Elle. We want those belts, and we've decided that we can't win them with Phil's broken arm. So, let me introduce my tag team partner for tonight ... Juan Vasquez!" (Billy throws down the microphone, and looks out toward the entrance way, waiting for Slim and Elle.) F: "Juan Vazquez?!? He's a manager, not a wrestler!" MMC: "My notes say that Juan does have some wrestling experience, down in Puerto Rico. This is the PVWF, though." (The Truth Commission's theme music begins playing. Slim, Elle, the Commandant and Aaron Bolles come out of the entrance. Of course, they are all wearing khaki outfits and red berets. Slim and Elle are wearing their Tag Team title belts over their shoulders, and Aaron Bolles has the World title belt around his waist.) F: "As the Truth Commission makes their way to the ring, let me comment on what Doug Zanghi said last night. Aaron Bolles must defend his World title at the Royal Rumble, against a yet to be determined opponent." MMC: "We do know who that opponent won't be, though. It won't be 'Nature Boy' Nick Langworthy, as Zanghi said he'd have to win the Royal Rumble in order to get his shot." B: "Where is Langworthy? I haven't seen him tonight." F: "Langworthy is at his Conewango Valley home, recouperating from injuries he suffered last night at the hands of Zwa. Slim and Elle are in the ring, and here we go. It looks like Elle and Billy Dahl will be starting this match off. Dahl tries to take Elle down with a Puerto Rican uppercut, but it has little effect on the massive Elle. Elle places his hand firmly on Dahl's forehead, and shoves him across the ring." MMC: "This match is an obvious mismatch. Slim and Elle are huge.." F: "Maybe so, but Billy Dahl is an excellent wrestler. Wish I could say the same about Vazquez. Elle lifts Dahl up with one hand, and sends him flying over the top rope! What strength this big man of the Truth Commission possesses.." B: "I could do that.." F: "I'm sure you probably could. You and your 40% body fat. Well, the Commandant and Aaron Bolles are beating the crud out of Billy outside the ring, but now Phil is over there to help his comrade! Elbow of Death with the cast on the Commandant! Oh my god! The Commandant is out, and Bolles is running for cover! Phil helps Billy back into the ring, and Billy is able to tag in Juan. Juan comes into the ring and starts slugging away on Elle. Elle stumbles backwards. Juan now takes Elle down with a big clothesline! Elle falls down, but he was able to reach back and make the tag to Slim. Slim gets into the ring, and now Juan looks frightened." MMC: "Juan knows the path of destruction Slim has created in the PVWF. He doesn't want to be the next victim." F: "Juan scampers back and tags in Billy. I don't think Billy's ready for a tag yet, but he's willing to get back in the ring. That guy isn't afraid of anyone. Billy goes for a cross body block, but Slim caught him ... and falls forward, landing all his weight on poor Billy! Slim now lifts Billy onto his shoulder ... Slim Slam! That's going to be the end. 1....2.... no, Juan made the save!" B: "I don't think that was very smart.." F: "Now Slim takes Juan down with a big forearm. He lifts Juan up, and executed the Slim Slam on him! Slim places Juan on top of Billy, and he covers them both. 1....2.... no, Phil Gier hit the ring and made the save! The bell rings, and we have a disqualification. Phil is going crazy, as he connects with the casted Elbow of Death on Slim, and Slim is hurting! Elle gets in the ring to try and stop this. Gier goes for the Elbow of Death, but Elle blocked it! He lifts Phil up and body slams him! Now he hits Phil with a big splash! The Commandant and Aaron Bolles are now in the ring, and it's full-fledged gang warfare!" MMC: "Dahl and Juan have gotten back to their feet, and they go to the attack on the Commandant and Bolles!" B: "This is great! Los Boricuas versus the Truth Commission!" F: "Fans, we have to go! Join us next week!" B: "No, don't go..."
