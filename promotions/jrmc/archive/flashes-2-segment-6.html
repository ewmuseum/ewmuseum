********************************* * "The Showstopper" Simon Lebec * ********************************* [Commercial cuts in with a blackened screen. And hey, if they fail to pass their gifts onto you within one visit, your next disease is free! GUARANTEED! [Cut back to the same guy in the opening, jumping up and down, yelling, "I've got four warts!" We then switch back to Lebec] You see, you're not gonna find excitement like that at the Blockbuster, and you're always guaranteed to get what you pay for. So guys, if you're bored with the way you spend your leak breaks, give us a call at: 1-800-ROTTEN2 [Camera cuts back to Lebec] And remember.........happiness is just a blister away!
