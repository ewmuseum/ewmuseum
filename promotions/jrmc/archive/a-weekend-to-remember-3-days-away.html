Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade into a sideways shot of a pajama-clad man,sitting in a big gray recliner. He's approximately 65 yrs old and looks severely depressed. Other pajama-clad people walk behind him in the background.As he sits he continuously pushes the button on a TV remote. His sad expression doesn't change as the channels do, pausing briefly to display the current show playing. It seems each station is playing news.] Announcer: Blah blah blah blahbiddiblah blah... [CLICK] Announcer: Blah blah blah blahbiddiblah blah... [CLICK] Announcer: Blah blah blah blahbiddiblah blah... [CLICK] Announcer: Blah blah blah blahbiddiblah blah... [The TV's blue light reflects on the elderly man's face as the picture cuts to a tight close-up. Suddenly...as if coming from the camera itself a voice, the kind of voice which sells those pocket fishermen deals, speaks to him.] V: Saaaaaaay...nothing good on TV? Tired of those stupid sitcoms and tedious news shows? Looking for some action? [The man nods yes to each question asked,becoming increasingly more interested in the voice's presence.] V: How'd you like to see some real action? [Cut to a montage of wrestling clips;Serge Annis throws a fireball at an opponent.Casey Jame nails the Blackheart punch on some poor soul. Cut back to the man's face..a smile slowly begins to grow.] V: Or maybe some of this? [More clips as Simon Lebec executes a jarring bodyslam on someone..Ken Curtis hits a vicious clothesline..Harley Robinson lands a devastating piledriver on a victim. Cut back to the man. He's visibly getting pump- ed up as he sits on the edge of the recliner now,intently staring into the camera.] V: Want more? [Still more clips: Ethan de Sade executes a suicide plancha over the top rope onto an opponent,sending them both crashing through a table. Cut back to the man. He slams his fist down into the arm of the chair his eyes are wide with excitement,his teeth set in a clenched sneer.] V: Well what do you think? [The man stands up,quivering from adrenaline. He looks around the room as a male nurse with an obvious attitude enters it.] Nurse: Alright ya loony-toons..med time! Get in line. [The man looks at the camera with a foaming smile and runs towards the nurse,clotheslining him off his feet! Cut to distorted facial shots of the other patients all with shocked expressions. The nurse gets up but is promptly bodyslammed by the old man. Then he's piledriven. All the patients gather around the two in a circle, shouting and clapping. Cut to a shot of the old man applying the scorpion deathlock on the now screaming nurse. Slowly a shaking hand reaches out to the old man. He looks up at the unseen owner of the hand with a gentle smile. Then he slaps the hand hard and releases the hold as the hand's owner,a little old woman,jumps on the nurse and applies a boston crab! Another hand reaches out and is tagged. Soon the nurse is in an abdominal stretch.] V: Well if you're looking to escape the doldrums of television, then we invite you to tune into all the bodyslamming, bone- crunching action of..
