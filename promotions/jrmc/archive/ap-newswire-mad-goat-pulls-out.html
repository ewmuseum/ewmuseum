[AP NEWSWIRE] Mad Goat Pulls Out of JRMC?

24 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Indianapolis, IN -- It seems IIeW fliers aren't the only things Poutine "Mad Goat" Janois [heh] are eating. Yes, this superSYDNICATED reporter believes that "Mad Goat" is also eating something else.. ...crow. Stories are breaking all over the beltway about the JRMC tournament and the forthcoming super-grudge match between "Mad Goat" and Ivan Ramius. It seems "Mad Goat" has pulled out of the match, throwing the entire Night Two Supercard into disarray! When asked if and/or why Mr. Goat pulled out of the match, Goat had only this to say: "No comment -- baaaaaaa". A certain high-ranking member of the JRMC Executive Council explained "It's not like Goat had all that much marketing value -- but the guy has a huge following! Could you imagine thousands of rabbid goats protesting the Memorial card if the big goat didn't show? I certainly can't. And I honestly dont want too". This source goes on to detail the reasons why Goat pulled out. It seems the "Mad Goat" walked into the JRMC event headquarters on Tuesday and had an arguement with the old, mutilated Jay Ralston himself! Another, related, story tells of Jay Ralston having to be rushed to the Indianapolis International Hospitol for a corinary reaction....a heart attack! Could "Mad Goat" have pushed Ralston over the edge? This reporter to think so. More information is forthcoming. J.R Hollis N.E.W. VP Handler Of: Ivan Ramius KNI tournament metalist.
