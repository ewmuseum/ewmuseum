Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Premier Championship Wrestling grew out of a Chicago promotion whose new owner, wrestling veteran Duke Williams, introduced himself to the FW Central hub on 3 March 2007, describing a fed run off his own MySpace page rather than a dedicated website and built around realism, character-driven roleplay and a weekly show. He had wanted to keep the initials PCW as a tribute to an earlier fed he had wrestled for, until discovering a real Canadian promotion of the same name; after a public naming debate on the boards, the promotion kept the PCW initials regardless, under a new expansion, Premier Championship Wrestling.

Its own debut broadcast, “Malice 2: Part 1” (22 March 2007), opened on the takeover itself: Duke Williams and the fed's outgoing owner, Ed Butkis, negotiating a 70/30 buyout on camera, Butkis retaining his own job, his brother Sheik Sandwich, and Big Elwood at their existing salaries in exchange. “PCW LIVE on WGN,” commentator Bob Pike announced, introducing the crowd to Big Elwood's own fifteen-year win streak, on the line that night in a tag match alongside his partner Burt Perlow.

The promotion's own recruitment posts through March 2007 called for twelve to sixteen wrestlers willing to build a character-driven world from the ground up, promising creative freedom and, in Duke Williams' own words, the same fun out-of-character atmosphere he said had defined both this promotion and an earlier fed, PRIME.

No further document survives past that debut broadcast; how the promotion, or Duke Williams' own new ownership, fared afterward isn't in the surviving record.
