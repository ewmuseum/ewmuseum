USWF Status Update, 21-22 July 1997

21-22 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I am the president of the USWF. And we are insearch of 5 single's wrestlers and 4 tag teams. There will be a tournament for the world title as soon as we get 16 wrestlers. Loser gets Transcontential Title. Same thing for Tag Team Title's. But there is only one. For more information check out the websight. http://www.angelfire.com/ny/ewrestling1/ As of now I have 11 single's and one tag team. The homepage will be updated on Wednesday. There is one major rule of USWF. All roleplays 3K long. USWF stands for United States Wrestling Federation, and it is one of many imaginary wrestling leagues that exist on the internet. It exists to provide wrestling fans with a creative outlet: don't like the way wrestlers' angles are handled in the big feds? Then create your own and guide him to the World title! 1b. USWF Staff 2a. Who are the hosts of Mayhem? Ed McMicheals - Face - Vince McMahon type guy. Steve Ross - Neutral - Jim Ross type commentator. Adrian Jackson - Heel - Like Jerry Lawler. But not afraid to get into a fight. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- 3. USWF Events 3a. What cards are there? Midweek Mayhem - This is the main show for USWF. This show aires every Wednesday. Each week Mayhem will have 5 matches as well as interviews and more. You also send your post match and pre match interviews to me for the show. We also talk about the upcoming card for Saturday. Any ideas or interviews should be sent to . The show is done in play by play. Ring Report- This aires on Saturday's. This is like a Magazine it tells you everything that goes on in USWF. Like Injuries, Arrivals, Rumours, and other things happening in USWF. Now you can send interviews for this show to. This show is like to build heat, talk trash, and talk about everything in USWF. Tuesday Interview Show- This show is strictly interviews and no commentary. Usually this is were wrestler's have open mic. Can say anything they would like to say. 3b. What about PPVs? PAY-PER-VIEWS: I try not to encourage specialty type matches on Midweek Mayhem. Maybe one every once in a while, but this is generally for regular 20-30 minute matches. Specialty matches take place on the Pay Per Views. Pay Per Views normally take longer to go out as well.. They are more in depth and can take weeks to do. Here's the plan for this coming year's Pay Per Views... In Your E-mail: March Summer Showdown: June USWF Hardcore - September Winter Explosion- Decemember I do have to state here that I reserve the right to change dates, change format, and add or remove pay- per-views for any reason. (what power, eh?) :-) -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= 4. Roleplaying in the USWF 4a. How do you roleplay? Roleplaying is basically playing out the role of your character. Anything you make your wrestler do is called roleplaying. This can be writing flashes/interviews, involving your wrestler in angles/feuds, and anything like that. All interviews must be 3K long. To be a "good roleplayer", you need to really be able to take the roll of your character. You need to really develop your wrestler's personality, as character depth is very important. It's also good to get involved in feuds-- these can get your wrestler noticed and it also makes e-wrestling a lot more fun. 4b. What is a strategy? A strategy is what you want your wrestler to do in his match or the match of a rival. Like how will he make his entrance how will he wrestle the match. Don't put down he wins by his finisher or nothing like that. 4c. Who can you use as a interviewer? For interviews on Mayhem, use Steve Ross. For in-ring interviews use Ed McMicheals. 4d. Who do I send stuff to?? _ALL_ Flashes should be sent to =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- 5. Match Outcomes 5a. How are Match's decided? It's all done on Heat. What do I mean by that? During the running of the league, we have a plan for the immediate future, who we plan to push, who is going to get the title shots, who wins what matches. The way you win in this league is by influencing our decision, you do this by creating heat for your wrestler. If your wrestler has Heat we'll push him. Why? The wrestlers with Heat are the ones who keep a fed going, they are the ones who are consistent in participation. Push the guys who are the life of the fed. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- 6. Titles 6a. What titles are offered in USWF? TITLES & RANKINGS: The USWF has titles.. The USWF Heavyweight Title The USWF Transcontinental Title The USWF Tag-Team Titles Titles must be defended every 4 cards (equivalent of 30 days), except the TC Title which must be defended every 2 cards. They must also be defended against a top contender or it doesn't count as a defense. You can, of course, still give unranked wrestlers a shot at the title(s). All titles except the TC Title must be defended against a wrestler (or wrestlers in Tag-Team) in the top 5. The Intercontinental Title must be defended against a wrestler in the top 10. The TC Champion is the #1 contender to the HW title, and can force a HW Title match at any time. If they lose, however,they forfeit another shot for 12 cards (3 months). If the TC Champion defeats the HW Champion, they must give up their original title to the Board of Directors (who will determine a match or matches for a new champion). =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- 7. Joining 7a. How do I join?? You can join by filling out this form and reading a few rules... ~ Because of the size of the USWF you may only have one singles wrestler OR you can have two if you bring in a tag team. ~ No Scott Nash's or Kevin Hall's or Hunter Hearst Hall's of anything like that. WE WILL NOT TAKE THEM!! ~ Wrestlers must weigh atleast 200 lbs and be over 5'8". Max is 7'2" and 500 lbs. ~ Please answer all questions! ~ Send the form to ~ Also, the USWF does _NOT_ have to accept all wrestlers submitted to us. If you start getting mail you are in, if not then please do not e-mail us bugging to get in. ~ Now fill out this form... ENJOY THE USWF! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- NOTE: I can't emphasize this enough: if you want angles done, make sure the person writing that card gets them! TRY to follow these guidelines when sending stuff in! It's NOT the end of the world if you DON'T but it IS nice if you DO! :-) WHAT TO SEND? - TO GET MATCHES: Challenge, Challenge, Challenge! - TO WIN: Roleplay, Roleplay, and (did I mention?) Roleplay! - ALSO IT HELPS TO: In Ring (Monday Night Mayhem) interviews! Just leave space for the announcers to ask questions (or even write them yourself). You fill in the answers or whatever and we'll do the rest! - AND IT NEVER HURTS TO: Send in a STRAT! That's short for STRATEGY. A strategy would include how you want your guy to go about wrestling the match, or any sneak attacks/double crosses that you want to see happen. This is not required every time ... just when you want something special to happen. It REALLY helps improve the angles and plotlines. We can't always follow your suggestion to a "T," but we will try. WHERE TO SEND IT? - ROLEPLAYS and CHALLENGES go to [detail withheld] Please LABEL it as a "Roleplay," a "Strat," or whatever it is in the subject line, followed by your character's name. Keeps us organized! :-) Also a "[USWF]" never hurts.
