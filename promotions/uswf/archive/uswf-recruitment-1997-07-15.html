Check out homepage at: http://www.angelfire.com/ny/ewrestling1/index.html 7. Joining 7a. How do I join?? You can join by filling out this form and reading a few rules... ~ Because of the size of the USWF you may only have one singles wrestler OR you can have two if you bring in a tag team. ~ No Scott Nash's or Kevin Hall's or Hunter Hearst Hall's of anything like that. WE WILL NOT TAKE THEM!! ~ Wrestlers must weigh atleast 200 lbs and be over 5'8". Max is 7'2" and 500 lbs. ~ Please answer all questions! ~ Send the form to ~ Also, the USWF does _NOT_ have to accept all wrestlers submitted to us. If you start getting mail you are in, if not then please do not e-mail us bugging to get in. ~ Now fill out this form... USWF SIGNUP FORM =================== Wrestler's Name: Height: Weight: From: Entrance Music: Looks: Attitude: Describe personality, motivation, etc.: Experience?: Ring attire: Finishing move: Describe: Do you have a manager or valet? What are your advantages and/or disadvantages? (Optional) Advantages - Disadvatages- Wrestler's style Primary style - Secondary style - What are at least ten, if not more, of your favorite moves? Please indicate your agreement to the following: "I understand USWF is a roleplaying league. Not everyone can win all the time, but those who roleplay well will have their opportunity to shine and amass victories. I understand that if I don't roleplay, I may become the unfortunate victim of Lance's periodic roster trimmings. While if I have problems I can contact Lance, I agree to live with all this." I agree (sign your name here): Okay, then. ENJOY THE USWF! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- NOTE: I can't emphasize this enough: if you want angles done, make sure the person writing that card gets them! TRY to follow these guidelines when sending stuff in! It's NOT the end of the world if you DON'T but it IS nice if you DO! :-) WHAT TO SEND? - TO GET MATCHES: Challenge, Challenge, Challenge! - TO WIN: Roleplay, Roleplay, and (did I mention?) Roleplay! - ALSO IT HELPS TO: In Ring (Monday Night Mayhem) interviews! Just leave space for the announcers to ask questions (or even write them yourself). You fill in the answers or whatever and we'll do the rest! - AND IT NEVER HURTS TO: Send in a STRAT! That's short for STRATEGY. A strategy would include how you want your guy to go about wrestling the match, or any sneak attacks/double crosses that you want to see happen. This is not required every time ... just when you want something special to happen. It REALLY helps improve the angles and plotlines. We can't always follow your suggestion to a "T," but we will try. WHERE TO SEND IT? - ROLEPLAYS and CHALLENGES go to [detail withheld] Please LABEL it as a "Roleplay," a "Strat," or whatever it is in the subject line, followed by your character's name. Keeps us organized! :-) Also a "[USWF]" never hurts.
