Glamour, Glitz, and Blood

2 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
New Orleans, Lousiana- It was supposed to be a peaceful night, and for the most part, it was. However, as we left New Orleans, one of RUSH's most important stars was left bloodied. Who left who bloodied, but more importantly.....why? Also, we re-lived 5 of the most requested matches of the last 6 months in RUSH, and much, much more.
