The Status of a Marshall

14 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After the shocking revelation of Josh Marshall making his presence known in the Mordred/Matt Elia match and saving Matt's life in a way, it is still not clear that Josh has signed a contract with RUSH just yet. Seeing as how he has had some trouble in the past with the now owner of RUSH, Sylkk, he is really beginning to consider not coming to RUSH fully. "I was in heavy talk before Rising from the Ashes, with Rush Mason and was nearly confident in signing with RUSH, but now that Sylkk has taken over I don't know what to think," Josh commented on his signing, "Things often seem to fail in the hands of this man and I'm not one to just jump into something I feel is going to be total let down for myself." After being told of the comments made by one Josh Marshall, the now CEO of RUSH had this to say. "If this mook thinks he even has a chance of coming into RUSH after his comments, he's got another thing coming cause I am not about to authorize some of his backstage ability and arrogance just to walk in here and become an annoyance to myself and the other hard workers of RUSH." Although, while listening into this conversation in the process of cleaning out his desk, another personnel member, working under Rush Mason at the time, and who had wished to stay anonymous, had this much to say. "The contract deal that had been drawn up by Mr. Mason and Josh was one that couldn't be broken once signed by Mr. Mason. Whether he was the owner of RUSH at the time or not, and he did in fact sign the contract. While, Josh never put the pen to the paper, the contract does state that as long as the CEO of RUSH at the dating of the paper had signed the contract, Josh could put his penmanship on it at any time in his life and still the contract would be in affect." Whether Sylkk likes it or not, Josh was scheduled, by someone unknown, to make his announcement live before the Adrenaline crowd.
