Sold!
November 24th, 2002

At PCW Alliances, RUSH Wrestling was represented by a team of four. However, many more RUSH members than that were in attendance. The most detested men of RUSH helped Xavier Kannon to a tainted victory at Alliances. After that, RUSH was announced as Kannon's early Christmas present to the federation. So, as of now, Xavier Kannon owns RUSH Wrestling and all related enterprises, but time will only tell whether the fed will live on, resisting hte power, or whether they will fall to the giant...

Glamour, Glitz, and Blood
November 2nd, 2002

New Orleans, Lousiana- It was supposed to be a peaceful night, and for the most part, it was. However, as we left New Orleans, one of RUSH's most important stars was left bloodied. Who left who bloodied, but more importantly.....why?

Also, we re-lived 5 of the most requested matches of the last 6 months in RUSH, and much, much more.

Click on the results image at the top of the page to witness the Anniversary show and all of the fun that the last 6 months of RUSH has given us...

It Takes Two...
October 25th, 2002

Lafayette, Louisiana- The main event slated for Adrenaline 22 was a lumberjack match featuring all of the RUSH roster. However, at the end of the night, there were two- count 'em, two- men left standing, but were they in some sort of alliance, or just victims of chance? One things for sure, it was two men you wouldn't have expected after last week...

Also on Adrenaline, two World Title Tourney matches went down, Matt Hunt and Josh Marshall got things set for AD23 in two weeks time, and The Masked Mullet did something surprising to Chris Matthews.

For all this and more, click on the banner at the top of the site to witness Adrenaline 22 in all of its glory!

The Sylkk Ages
October 18th, 2002

Tampa Bay, Florida- There seemed to be unfication against a single man at Rising From The Ashes, but all of that was gone this week. It was, once again, every man for himself, and Sylkk made sure that EVERYONE knew he was the one man in charge, by making an announcement that SHOCKED everyone.

Also on Adrenaline, Tyler Valentine and El Spiffy! teamed up against McGlocklan, but would anyone want to help the murdrerer out? Also, Matt Hunt and GOD got things for the new era started off on very bad terms, while Josh Marshall and Mordred started a new page in the book of violence.

For all this and more, click on the banner at the top of the site to check Adrenaline 21!

Ash to Ash, Dust to Dust, Fade to Black...
October 13th, 2002

Daytona, Florida- Rising From the Ashes. The infamous tale of the Phoenix, a fiery bird rising from its own ashes to live for eternity...and RUSH Wrestling kicked that off tonight, showing that they're not going anywhere anytime soon.

On tonights pay-per-view, surprises littered the card from beginning to end, starting with a startling flashback from RUSH's past and ending with an odd display of...well, you'll just have to watch and find out.

Brock Newbludd defended his World Title against Tyler Valentine and Chris Matthews, in the culmination of a bitter feud that has lasted over a month.

The masked hero, The Masked Mullet, took on RUSH's most heinous villain, McGlocklan in a brutal match.

All this and more transipred on Rising From the Ashes...and you can view it now by clicking on the Rising From the Ashes banner at the top of the site.
