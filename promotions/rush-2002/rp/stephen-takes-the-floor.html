Date: Today OOC:Tryed switching it up to a new style of RPing, whaddya think? :P Wendy has been in the employ of Woodman's supermarket for three months now, and she's so sick of the routine. She comes in every afternoon at two, clocks in, puts on her apron and then jockeys a register for the next eight hours as the same paceless forms consistently shove money and coupons at her. She is wondering where her life went so wrong (the momentary flashback to when she dropped LSD in physics class is a really good hint) when the next customer walks up with his grocery cart. She doesn't look up, instead picking his groceries out one by one and sliding them across the scanner. 'Four porterhouse steaks. . . one bag of potatoes. . . three pounds of jumbo shrimp. . . a 24-pack of Newcastle. . . another 24-pack of Newcastle. . . another 24-pack of Newcastle. . . a 24-pack of Grolsch. . . a 24-pack of Miller Lite-' 'What the F~CK??' the customer says in interuption. 'Waitasec. Brock, I didn't grab any Miller Lite.' The customer's companion nods. 'I know.' 'So why is there Miller f~cking Lite in the cart, hm?' the first asks. 'Um. . . 'cause I wanted some,' the second answers. 'Oh, okay.' The first one turns back to Wendy, smiling. 'Continue.' Wendy slides the rest of the groceries across the scanner, not really paying attention to them anymore, instead focusing on the two customers. 'Um. . . you guys are. . . The, um, Blood Brothers, right?' Stephen gives her a smile and a wink. 'In the flesh, babe.' 'Charmed,' Brock says, lighting a Marlboro. Wendy hits a bunch of buttons. 'Oooookay, two forty-seven seventy-three. You guys are the f~ckin' coolest.' Brock pulls out his chain wallet and grabs two C-notes and a Grant, handing them to Wendy. 'Gonna watch Thursday?' She gets his change. 'Hell, yes. I can't wait to see Chris Matthews totally get schooled by “Inno-F*ckin’-Vator” 'You wanna hurry up?' someone shouts from the back of the line. 'I'm getting accolades,' Brock responds. 'So f~ck off, douchebag.' Stephen and Brock grab their groceries and go out to the parking lot, stuffing them in the trunk of Stephen's torch red 1971 Dodge Charger. Stephen grabs one of the bottles of Grolsch and uncaps it, taking a huge swallow. 'Dude, I'm totally proud of the way we didn't start a brawl-for-all in the grocery store,' he says. Brock takes a drag from his cigarette. 'InDEED.' The two get in the car and put in Jane's Addiction's 'Nothing's Shocking', setting the volume to 11. Stephen revs the tyrannosaurus-like engine and peels out, thundering down the busyroads of Madison. ****************************** Stephen sits back at the table, clad in his baggy black leather pants and his Stephen Blood t-shirt with 'Piss-Drunk and Pissed Off!' on the back. He opens another Newcastle and takes a swallow before setting the bottle down by his plate. There are empty bottles of various types already strewn about the kitchen and dining area. Everyone's feeling pretty good. Brock lights a cigarette. He is wearing his baggy blue cammo pants and Inno-f~cking-vator t-shirt. 'That steak was totally mighty, dude.' Stacey takes one last swallow of Newcastle. She is dressed in her black latex pants and Rollerjam: Quakes t-shirt. She smiles sexily at her beau and gives him a soft kiss. 'Can I tell them now?' Stephen cocks his eyebrow and gives a half-grin. 'Yeah, go ahead.' Clad in her leopard-print bellbottoms and matching top, Lindsey furrows her brow. 'Tell us what?' Stacey smiles and puts her left hand under the table for a moment, then slowly drawing it up, revealing a gold ring with diamonds going around the center and a larger diamond at the top. The ring is on the second finger in from her pinky (ah-DUH.). Lindsey gasps and puts a hand over her mouth, her eyes saucer-like with surprise. 'Oh my god!!' she squeals. 'Idon'tbelieveit! Wheeee!' The two girls embrace and start screaming like kindergarteners with skinned knees. They run over to the breakfast nook and start discussing plans immediately. Stephen's pet wolf Skoll looks up, gives a questioning look and a grunt, then goes back to sleep. Stephen takes another swallow of beer and shakes his head. 'F~ckin' chicks.' Brock stares at him slack-jawed. 'Did you just do what I think you did?' Stephen gives him a look of indifference. 'Yeah, right before we went to that self defense ballet the other day, why? Guys buy stuff for their chicks all the time.' 'Oh,' Brock says, relaxing and wiping his brow. 'That's a load off my mind--I thought you went and proposed to her or something.' Stephen swallows hard. 'Ummmm, actually. . .' Brock's eyes widen. 'You did, didn't you?' 'Yes,' Stephen responds before powering the rest of his Newcastle. '$hit!' Brock screams. 'What the f~ck, dude?' Stephen says defensively. 'I totally thought you'd be supportive on this one!' 'That's not it, dude!' Brock counters. 'What's buggin' me is that Lindsey's gonna be expecting the same thing from me now!!' The New F~cking Legend opens another Newcastle. 'Life's hard, Mr. Brocko.' Suddenly the Promo Channel camera set up for all wrestlers by Rush begins to blink its signature red light, telling the Fully Functional Babe Lair occupants that there's thirty seconds to showtime. 'Oh look, the evil overlords are calling,' Brock mutters. Stacey scowls. 'A promo NOW?? Those jerkoffs! They're ruining our good mood!' Stephen takes another drink. 'Babe, it's Heinrich Kleinwurts Promo Channel crew. It could f~ck up a wet dream.' 'Good point,' Lindsey responds. Brock and Stephen park themselves on the floor in front of the camera with Lindsey and Stacey right behind them. Skoll lazily pads over with his rubber chew-toy made to look like Tyler Valentine’s head and plops down across Brock's legs. The light turns green as Brock screws another Marlboro into his lips and lights it up with his zippo. 'Ladies and gentlemen,' Stephen begins. 'We're piss drunk.' 'So bear with us here,' Stacey adds, rubbing her hand on Stephen's chest. The green light of the camera sparkles off of the diamonds. 'The way of life as you know it for Rush is changing,' Brock states through a cloud of smoke. 'And that is plain and simply Sad But True.' Stephen cracks his neck. 'It's gonna be the match of the f~ckin' century. It's the night when my bro hits the final plateau that will take him to the World Championship. The historical night when the lights dim, Static X's Bled For Dayst' rocks the doors off the f~cking arena, and he comes out to the ring with a handful of Bloodweisers. Here's the way it goes, Brock will stroll down to the ring, clean f*ckin’ house of the whole roster, get the 1..2..3 and then celebrate with me and the ladies after the f*ckin’ match. Stephen throws back another mouthful of Newcastle and wipes his mouth with the back of his hand and then remembers about Brock’s match with Chris Matthews on Thursday, as well. 'And for Chris, I've seen the tapes. I've seen what you have to offer and I'm familiar with the name “Matthews”. But you think that's enough to even so much as get you THROUGH a match with my bro, “The Inno-F*cking-Vator” ? Think again, bee-ya-oo-cha-chowie. He’s taken more hits than Trish Stratus's website. You have nothing for me to worry about. Nothing.' Stephen powers the rest of his bottle. 'Crossroads will go down as Brock’s greatest night, his turn to bask in the glory, and no ones going to prevent that. So keep one hand in the pocket. . .' 'ONE FOOT IN THE GUTTER!' the other three respond. 'Now F~CK OFF!' Brock yells, winging the empty beer bottle at the camera and cutting the feed.
