20 Questions with Me Pt. 1

23 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/23/02 20 Questions with Me Part 1 The scene fades from black showing large leather chair. The chair is black and looks rather comfortable and the camera slowly zooms out, showing more of the room. The room seems quaint, roomy and comfortable. Large statues are on filled bookcases throughout the room and expensive paintings hang carefully placed. The camera shows the chair again closer this time, in more detail. The stitching of leather to wood can be seen and the rivets that allow for comfort can be seen as well. Slowly the camera turns around and shows a small man sitting in the chair. He seems to be in his mid to late 50’s. His hair seems to be leaving him along with the pigmentation of his hair follicles. His legs sit crossed creasing his tan docker pants in a way that will need them to be sent away for proper dry cleaning. He wiggles his feet patiently, almost in a rhythmic pattern his loafer shoe bouncing up and down revealing his brown argyle sock. He pulls out a paper pad and pencil from his dress shirt pocket and flips it open. He lifts his glasses, dangling from his neck, and places them on his nose. He lets out a sigh and licks his drying lips. “Well let’s gets this under way,” the elderly man says breaking the awkward silence. The camera flips around and begins to pain the rest of the room. Across from the man in the chair is a long brown leather couch. The camera slowly pans up showing to shoes pointing towards the ceiling. As the camera slowly moves up the body, brown cargo pocketed pants are shown next and then a vibrant purple button down dress shirt. The body of the person is obviously male and nice physique although not gargantuan. Not even close all things considered. The camera continues to pan up and reveals the man to be Matt Elia. He continues to lie in the bed although he restlessly moves around trying to get comfortable. “Well,” Elia iritablely answers “where do you wanna start doctor? The man in the chair merely bats an eyelid as he replies, “Why not first state your name.” Matt Elia glances at the doctor with a confused look on his face.“Doctor Veloce,” Matt says “I have been coming to you for 14 years, you are a personally family friend and know my name” Once again the doctor refrains from moving at all, he continues to stare at his pen and pad, “Ah I do know your name dear Matthew, but in this session we will not be doing things for me, you will be doing things to discover your true self. Now, please, state your name.” Matt rolls his eyes and answers, “Matthew Ryan Elia, son of Alexander J. and Robin B. Elia, brother of Christopher Michael.” He pauses for a second waiting for a reaction from the doctor, there is none “Is that good enough?” Doctor Veloce lifts his head and smiles softly at Matt. “Continue…” the doctor softly adds. “Continue what?” Matt asks confused. “You asked for my name and I told you it.” “Tell me who in fact you are Matthew.” The good doctor adds. Matt lets out a long sigh, longer than before and begins. “Well, I am a pro-wrestler. Not very good might I add but none the less it is what I love to do. I am going to make my return to PCW in a few weeks, actually. Kind of like a small homecoming. I just don’t think that I can be a benefit for my team.” Doctor Veloce’s face firms tight into a frown. “You seem to be lacking the confidence needed for success. Your first goal should be to bring that back. First off let’s start with some questions. Matt how do you feel for RUSH currently?” Matt smiles and thinks to himself. “Well, I kinda feel at home in RUSH now. I guess I have found some success in singles competition and the locker room is filled with great guys. Tyler Valentine is one of my favorite guys to talk to, usually him I always talk sports. Marvin Harris is also quite a character and despite his constant rudeness backstage Stimpo is good guy at heart. The Masked Mullet is a crazy character and like I showed last week, I have no problem helping a friend out of a bind. My other two partners, Gene and Livewire are great guys too although I rarely speak to either of them. But the respect for them is there.” The doctor smiles and nods, “Yet, you haven’t been able to pick up any form of singles gold…does that make you angry or disappointed?” Matt shakes his head, “No, not really. I mean winning a title and proving you’re the best of your division or federation is a nice thing to have but I enjoy the backstage feel better. The ideas that I can go ahead and get paid to do something I love is my main motivation. The fact that I usually get my ass kicked every night well, only adds to it in a weird way. And besides, I get to write my column which I feel is up there with some of the best written throughout WT.” “Indeed you seem to have your straight on now but two years ago your were as cocky and as brash as many of the young superstars are today. What caused your change in attitude?” Matt sits silent for a bit…”Well I think I can pinpoint that to September 11th 2001. I know everyone has something to say about that awful day, but that day opened my eyes, when my fiancé, manager, and friend of 7 years was killed in that tragedy. I guess her death opened my eyes making me realize that, I, Matt Elia was just a tiny piece of a humongous puzzle. It made me tone my attitude down a lot.”“Interesting,” adds the doctor as he scratches his chin. “Now this Alliance pay per view is going to the biggest one in all of PCW history. What are your comments on it, and how do you think you are going to fare against some of the other teams that will be attending?” Matt stretches for a moment and continues.” “Well, I am honored to be picked bye my current boss Sylkk. I say current because Rush has a history of changing owners every semester. I mean, I feel that other wrestlers such as Marvin, El Spiffy, and Clem should be going before me, but…I wouldn’t decline this offer in a heartbeat. I guess to say that I deserve this would be an understatement after being in WT for 3 years now. So that makes me very happy, regarding the rest of the talent, well…at least I know I can fare well against JJ and his Men.” Matt chuckles to himself. He waits for the doctor to laugh at his joke but he does not. “Well, FSW has a really talented team, and unfortunately for some of the PCW teams, FSW is not the underdog they are the favorite to win which is unfortunate. FSW is a garbage dump for washed up PCW wrestlers. I could have decided to go to FSW but decided to drop the greed for fame and money, and teach and mentor some of younger wrestlers, help them gain the experience that I now have. But don’t get me wrong, TEAM RUSH is one hell of a team and we will be a major force to be reckoned with.” “Matt, a lot of the current PCW stars were in NTICW when you were there too. Why do you think they have found success and you haven’t?” asks the doctor curiously. “Well,” answers Matt “Like I have said before, I am not the best wrestler. I am small for my size and still rather cowardly. I have a stronger feel for the reporting scheme of things. Snooping for a scoop here or there. People like, Xavier Kannon, Rush Mason, and The Pharaoh all have exceptional ring ability. PCW picked them up when they needed new guys, and they have grown to become the cream of the crop. In fact I think I remember telling Xavier that he will be PCW about 6 months ago in an interview. And we all know were he is now.” The doctor remains quite and looks around for a bit more. He smiles at Matt. “Matt, where do you think you will be in 6 months….a year….10 years from now?” Matt goes to answer but the camera fades to black. And a small TOO BE CONTINUED is shown in the corner along with the RUSH LOGO. DID YOU FEEL THE RUSH?
