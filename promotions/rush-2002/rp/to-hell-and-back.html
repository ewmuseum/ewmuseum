To Hell and Back...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today. Something has been bothering me. Something has been tweaking at one of my last nerves. Something has been getting up inside of my head and playing around, and it is something that I am not even involved in. Do I have a say in the matter? Not normally, but this is something that I have created in a sense. Wes Outlett...finally showing his ugly face after weeks of idiotic ramblings and even worse attempts at being metaphorical. I am aware that he has been spawned from my own actions, and I am aware that you all out in TV Land feel that pain in your temples every time his voice is uttered, and the only thing I can't do is apologize. Yes, the blame does fall squarely on me, as he has attempted to steal my methods of evil tactics, my technique from me, yet lacks something needed for it. Charisma, intelligence, knowledge and a little thing that I like to call, 'flowability.' He is a rookie, he is trying his best, and for that I would applaud...if I wasn't covering my ears and withstanding the pain. Outlett, let me give you one last thing for you to know on while you listen. The people out there, the fans out there rather enjoy watching a baked chicken being cooked, then listen to your dribble. Sit down. Shut up. Find your own material, and try again... Now that I have that off my chest, and am able to get onto bigger and much better things, I would like to comment on what transpired at Adrenaline and what will transpire in the not so distant future. William Perry doesn't like me? Does he hate me? It is a big deal? Correct. It really doesn't matter if he has feelings for me one way or another because the High Executives won’t fire me because nobody wants to throw away that much cash. “Biohazard” Perry is not going to take MY TITLE SHOT away from me, because he can't, and nobody else can. He just isn’t good enough. So what does it matter that they hate me? What does it matter that he will screw me over in a match every week? What does it matter that he will continue to try to share the limelight with me? What does it matter that everyone who matters loves me and everyone who doesn't hates me? What does it matter that jealousy flows through RUSH like electricity? What does any of it matter? NOTHING! Perry, hate me all you wish. Perry, paint yourself green to show your envy. Perry, remove your foot from your mouth and STOP BOTHERING ME! Now I have been informed that I will not be getting a chance to get my title shot back as I had asked. I will not get my chance to repay the favor to Spiffy, for the shot that I EARNED! Spiffy, you must have rolled a seven, cause you are the luckiest man on earth, but do not think that you will be free from me forever. Do not think that I will never catch up, cause you keep playing your game, and eventually you will end up rolling snake eyes. Game Over. However, opponents I do know of, and it doesn't please me. Do not read farther into it then you should, because I am not saying that I am afraid or do not wish to fight them, because that is not the case. I am displeased because of the fact that my wishes, my demands, my needs are being over looked, are being whisked away like dirt and a broom. As opposed to getting what I need, I am handed what I could finish quickly. As opposed to giving me what I want and honestly, what will bring in greater ratings, they give me to jobbers and rookies trying their hardest to get to the higher spotlight. As opposed to giving me what I deserve, what I have worked hard for, they give me what I have beaten for the past five months. Losers, nobodies...wishful thinkers. Too bad the future has already been decided, and the belt soon already coming to where it belongs… (Our camera fades in to Scott Cage, sitting within the confines of his hotel room...in silence...when Steve “The Milkman” Carnage runs in...) Steve Carnage: Mr. Cage, Mr. Cage! Scott Cage: What? Steve Carnage: How'd you like us? Scott Cage: Us? Steve Carnage: Yeah! The “Jason Jameson Murder” Tape. How'd you like it? Scott Cage: Oh yeah...I thought…that was something else. Steve Carnage: That's okay...a lot of people mistake me for someone else. But still...how did you like it? Scott Cage: Well, you certainly made up for your fuck up during my match. Steve Carnage: Huh? Scott Cage: You were supposed to stop William Perry. Steve Carnage: Oh! I'm sorry Mr. Cage. Honestly, I didn't mean too...I was ... well, I was busy... Scott Cage: Busy with what...filming that damned murder? Steve Carnage: I'm sorry. I was busy. Scott Cage: I swear...if you weren't such a distraction towards opponents, you'd be in the welfare line faster than Rich Cannon. Steve Carnage: Huh? Scott Cage: Nothing...nothing at all... (Scott leans back in his chair, and throws in drink at Carnage…) Steve Carnage: Excuse me, Mr. Cage...you tossed your drink at me. Scott Cage: And you can't take that as a hint? Steve Carnage: Oh yeah! (Carnage leaps up off the couch and runs away, then runs back to him with the same drink...) Steve Carnage: Here you are Mr. Cage.Scott Cage: Steve, do you want to play a game? Steve Carnage: DO I?! (Awkward silence…) Steve Carnage: What's the game! Scott Cage: It's a scavenger hunt. Steve Carnage: Really? So what is it that I have to find?! Scott Cage: What I need is a car. Steve Carnage: That's it? Sounds easy. Scott Cage: Oh no, that's just the beginning...I need Jason Jameson's corpse.Steve Carnage: Jason Jameson's corpse...okay.Scott Cage: Can you do it?! Steve Carnage: I CAN DO IT! Scott Cage: DON'T TELL ME....SHOW ME! Steve Carnage: ALL RIGHT!!! (Carnage jumps out of his seat and rushes out of the hotel room...) Scott Cage: Fucking idiot...I swear. (Scott slides out of his chair...and walks towards the window as we fade…)
