The Spanish Inquisition! (Part 2)

23 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/23/02 As we left off last, I was just making my way back to my humble abode after being completely mocked for claiming that the latin way is the way of life. In my eyes, it should be all Latin, like some kind of latino manifest destiny. The TV was on where a man in a blue suit three sizes to small was talking with a fancy neck microphone reading his cue cards like his dumb job tells him to. Man in tight blue suit: Yes, it's today folks. The early, early, very early July 4th parade. Sure, it may only be September, but America celebrates today! Everyone come, enjoy the festivities! Come celebrate the American ways because we're better than everyone else! whoo hooo-- I clicked the remote to turn the TV off disgusted. I sat on my couch looking at all the posters and things on the wall of my American home. Sure, I knew that the things weren't all latino in my house, so, that had to change. I stood up angrily from my couch and thrusted my arms forward and tore down such posters as Britney Spears, then an LL Cool J poster, then a Justin Timberlake poster, I looked at it, then back up. Stimpo (smiling): Whoopsies. I put back up the Justin Timberlake poster and sighed as I rushed the other posters in a furious rage to the right of the camera and out to my garage where there stood a tall green garbage can full to it's brim with stinky, fly attracting trash. I shoved the posters down into the garbage can in anger making sure they got down there with a few pushes. Stimpo (pointing to the posters in the trash): YOU ARE NOT LATINO! YOU AREN'T WORTHY TO HANG IN STIMPO'S LATINO HOME!! I kicked the garbage can down and a huge amount of trash just emptied out into my garage plaguing the area with the horrible american smell of trash. Stimpo: DAMN YOUR AMERICAN SMELLY WAYS! I stomped my feet out the garage and into my driveway where there was a blue latino buggy as described in my last adventure. Stimpo: Time to make the announcement and make the announcement. I cock my head to the side in confusion. Stimpo: Did I...? Nah... I shook my head as if saying 'no' and opened the car door of the bug as it replied with playing 'la cucaracha' as three trumpet players appeared out of the back seat out of nowhere and kept playing their trumpets with pride for my arrival. I danced for a few minutes until I finally got into the car of latino meaning and started up the engine even if it took six tries and three trumpet players to get it started, I got it up and running. I pulled out of the american driveway and screeched away with my Mexi-mobile and was off to the big parade. I charged myself up for the big event and finally arrived at the place of the parade and I parked my car in the middle of the street awaiting for the parade to come... Stimpo: This'll show them my resiliance. It seemed that I waited forever until I finally noticed that an adjacent street was holding the parade and I was simply standing by myself... Stimpo: Well then... I ran down the street and ran straight through a crowd of people until I finally arrived at the parade, smack dab in the center of the commotion. I jumped onto a float that resembled a replica of the man they call Uncle Sam doing his patened point straight ahead with many skimpy women strutting on the float. I became enraged and I climbed up on the float and took hold of a mega-phone and stood on the tip of the top hat on Uncle sam and started to shout. Stimpo: Today is a great day! Crowd cheers Stimpo: Not for you Americans, but for Latinos everywhere! Crowd cheers Stimpo: That's right! Stimpo will be creating his own gang of Latinos to take over everything! We will be called....THE SPANISH INQUISITION! Crowd cheers Stimpo: What are you cheering at? I looked back and saw that there were women flashing their shirts up after everything I said. I sighed as I repeated. Stimpo: YOU'LL SEE! YOU'LL ALL SEE!! WHO'S WITH ME?! Silence...
