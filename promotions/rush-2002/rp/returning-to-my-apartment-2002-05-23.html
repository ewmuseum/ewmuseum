Returning to my apartment

23 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23-05-02 Yes! The good news is that Boda has seen the error of his ways and noticed that the decline in ratings for last weeks Adrenaline and my non-existence on the show for the first time in it’s entirety are no mere coincidence. Thus I have been booked for this weeks Adrenaline, live from the Hartford civic centre. So here comes the bad news, I have been booked to take on that nutcase Syn or should I saw Synhazard as that is now the name he now likes to be known by. But fear not Livewire-ites, I can handle my fair share of mad men and brainwashed psychopaths, heck I have wrestled in the same ring as Marvin Harris and Scott Cage for gods sake. Despite this however there seems to be something for more sinister and disturbing about Syn and his sudden alliance with Biohazard, a man that I know from my indy wrestling past. Biohazard is a master manipulator, a puppet master so to speak but of all the people minds in RUSH to meddle with why poor Syn’s? The scene opens with Livewire standing outside his expensive Beverly hills apartment. It has just passed twelve noon and the streets of Beverly hills are packed with tourists and some A-list celebrities such as Mel Gibson and Oprah Winfrey and some B-list ones like Roseanne Barr and Tom Arnold. Livewire seems oblivious to all these stars and former stars around him and just stands smugly outside his apartment with a Cheshire cat like grin on his face. It has been over three weeks since Livewire last saw the apartment that he calls home. In those weeks he has been involved in some of the most high profile matches of his wrestling career such as, a triple threat match to become the number one contender for the Network title and a eight man tag match where he got the opportunity to wrestle with his idol and boss Jeremy Howard. The trials and tribulations of the past three and a half weeks however has taken it’s toll on the personification of cool with one significant defeat in particular almost sending him over the edge and back into a battle with drugs and alcohol. However he has conquered the anxieties recent fame has caused him and has decided to take a slight break from his rigorous training programme to return home and pay his girlfriend who he also hasn’t seen for the past three weeks an unexpected visit. On appearance Livewire looks in a far better state than when we last saw him drunk and sullen sitting at the top of a steep hill contemplating his bleak existence in life. Then he was wearing a tatty pair of old stone-washed jeans and a ten year old red and black flannel shirt that barely fitted his wide frame. Today however he is wearing a baggy pair of black jeans that hang over his trainers and a white T-shirt that has the Union Jack flag printed across it. His hair is greased and slicked back into a ponytail showing off his angelic youthful face. On Livewire’s shoulder are a large bag, which carries the majority of his clothes, and a small rucksack that holds the other possessions that he has used over the past month. The bags in typical Livewire fashion are designers and have the words “Prada” written across them in red glitter. Livewire takes one final glance at the surrounding area of his apartment and nods his head forcefully knowing that there indeed is no feeling in the world quite life home. Livewire slips his silver door key into the lock and leans forward but suddenly notices that the door isn’t opening. Like a fat guy in an all you can eat buffet, the door will just not budge. Livewire tries to turn the key frantically and beads of sweat roll down his face as he tussles with the door. “F*cking American locksmiths!” Livewire says to himself as he tries to turn the key for the final time “You can never trust them to do a job properly”. Livewire senses that for all the twisting, thrusting and pushing he is doing something, far more conclusive is keeping him out of his apartment. So he does what any grown man in this type of situation would do. He falls to his knees and bangs his fists on the door, hoping somebody inside can let him in. The stars passing by who had earlier been oblivious to Livewire all become far too aware of his sudden desperate outburst and turn their heads. “Please….!!!” Livewire screams “…please somebody let me in, please somebody let me into my luxurious $550,000 a week apartment that has a nicam digital television, all my Quentin Tarratino DVD’s and my $2000 water bed that I never use please…please..PLEASE!!!” Livewire closes his eyes and continues to thump the door in a panic like state until the silhouette of a slender figure can be seen approaching the door through a pane of glass. Livewire momentarily stops banging his fists on the door and his anxieties are released when the door slowly opens to show the sassy smile of………. “Fiona!!!, damn am I glad to see you” Livewire laughs before sheepishly rising to his feet and carrying his bags inside. Bypassing a bemused Fiona in the process. Livewire immediately drops his large bag and rucksack to the floor and turns around to stare a hole through Fiona as she closes the front door. “What the hell have you done to my apartment?” asks Livewire with his hands on his hips in a schoolteacher like fashion. Fiona just shrugs her shoulders and frowns, her red-hair covering the right side of her face. “I like…changed the like..locks. For security reason of course.”she replies as she adds another bolt and latch to lock the front door. “It’s really like….cool” she adds “..I mean you were away for a long period of time and I was a little worried about being here on my own. So I like..decided to have a few extra locks added and got CCTV placed so that any intruders would be on film. Hey there’s a playback of you a few minutes ago crying outside and banging on the door on that monitor above the landing”. Livewire looks up at the small monitor that is indeed positioned and hanging off the top of the landing. He smiles and continues to watch “That’s really cool….I mean, what are you talking about? I wasn’t crying, my eyes were just merely extracting sweat”. Snaps Livewire, now turning away from the monitor to face Fiona again with a scowl on his face. “Oh yeah..” Livewire asks “….when where you deciding on planning to tell me about this new security system?” Fiona shakes her head angrily and raises her palm to Livewire’s face. “Listen…” she shouts moving her palm closer to Livewire’s face “…I was planning to like…..tell you like…next week, don’t forget you weren’t scheduled to arrive back until a fortnight tomorrow!!!” Fiona moves her palm away from her boyfriends face with a assertive look on her face. “True, ok you have a point there” Livewire says grudgingly. He then turns his head to look around his lush apartment. Much to Livewire’s pleasure the apartment is just how he left it. Everything from the African artifacts that line his walls to the signed soccer shirts from a host of top European clubs that are hung with great pride next to his garden. It is then that a smell catches Livewire by surprise. A strong smell of food wafts through from the kitchen to the hall-way that Livewire and Fiona are vacating. Livewire breathes in the smell and turns to Fiona with a wry smile on his face. “Baby, have you been cooking?” he asks almost surprised that he is even asking Fiona the question, after the infamous boiled egg and kitchen fire incident. Fiona gleefully claps her hands in enjoyment and winks at Livewire “Well, there are a few things that I have learnt while you’ve been away.” Laughs Fiona as she helps Livewire to pick up his bags. “Cool” smiles Livewire impressed by the new found independence of his immature girlfriend. He smiles until he notices a pizza box in the corner of his eye which has been dumped in the garden. Livewire picks up his rucksack and giggles at Fiona. “What’s up?” asks Fiona, unsure at why her boyfriend has burst into a fit of laughter like a pubescent teenager reading their first porn magazine. Livewire shakes his head and points to the pile of rubbish in the garden that has the pizza box strategically placed at the top of it. “You ordered a pizza didn’t you babe?” Livewire asks forlornly. Fiona pouts and shamefully nods her head. Livewire puts his arm around Fiona’s taut stomach and she rests her head on his shoulder as they both enter the kitchen to indeed find a medium sized pepperoni pizza placed in the centre of the kitchen table. Livewire and Fiona both sit down and start eating. Greasy cheese and meat are hardly the right nutrients for an athlete like Livewire, but after eating crappy hotel food for the last 3 weeks, something like a freshly baked pizza is a gourmet meal. After tucking into the first couple of slices Fiona breaks the noise which is the Lively ones slurping and chewing and asks him a question. “How has the last three weeks been for you then?” Fiona asks inquisitively as she leans forward in her chair. Livewire looks up from his pizza with a surprised look on his face. It is unsure if he is surprised by the question of the fact that Fiona dared to interrupt while he was eating. “Huh, Babe you do know that Adrenaline is on cable now don’t you?” replies the personification of cool as he wipes some of the tomato base off his chin. Fiona nods and pops a piece of pepperoni into her mouth. “Yeah, I did but I had some other things to do you know like… ..…manicures and my like….bikini line. “ “What?…” moans Livewire …so you didn’t bother to watch any of my matches while I was away?” Fiona takes a sip of her orange juice and clears the phlegm from her throat to answer Livewire. “Well I did see one of your matches” she smiles. “Good!!…” replies Livewire in a booming voice “..which one, my match against Cage?” Fiona shakes her head. “Er, the Shack-Pack with Jeremy Howard versus the Solution match? Livewire asks, again Fiona shakes her head to say no. “Which match did you see then?” asks Livewire not too sure of what Fiona’s response would be. Fiona takes a napkin from out of her pocket and wipes her mouth before reluctantly answering Livewire’s question. “I saw the match where you got your ass kicked by Ali Khadafi.” mumbles Fiona trying to avoid eye contact with Livewire. She is unable to do this however as Livewire instantly shows his annoyance by Fiona’s response and throws his last slice of pizza to the floor. The Lively one must be angry, he never throws good pizza away. Fiona looks up to see Livewire’s eyes turning a deep purple colour with every vein on his neck popping out. He manages to quickly calm down noticing the cowering lip of Fiona who is now standing up and looking rather frightened by the look on her boyfriends face. Livewire also stands up and starts to pace around the kitchen, talking as he does so. “Fiona, just for the record, I did not and I repeat DID NOT, get my ass kicked by Ali Khadafi. I lost fair and square I can admit that”. Livewire pauses and looks out of the kitchen window to see the beautiful view of the Beverly hills skyline. He looks back at Fiona who has now once again taken her seat by the table and for once looks interested in what her boyfriend has to say regarding wrestling. “You see Fiona..” Livewire continues ….my match with Khadafi was a no disqualification match. It was a match with no rules and everything from a right hand to the head to a steel chair to the head was legal. That was not how I was brought up to wrestle. I’m an athlete not a street thug Fiona. Put me in a straight up singles match like mine against Synhazard this week and I’ll show you and every other non-believer exactly what I can do in the wrestling ring.” Livewire sits back down at the table and takes Fiona’s hands. He holds them tightly and looks into her wild blue eyes. She breaks into a soft smile her rosy red lips curled up and indenting her innocent face. “You know what?” asks Livewire pulling Fiona’s hands closer to his chest. “I reckon that after I beat that half-wit Synhazard on this weeks Adrenaline Boda may be forced to put me back into the Network title picture. And sweetheart, this time no more fu*k ups, no more losses. I’ll beat Cage or whoever the champion is and I’ll bring that Network title home. I can see it baby, it’s destiny and destiny has been my guidance.” As Livewire says this Fiona suddenly tugs her hands away from the Lively one and dives for the kitchen work top where she picks up a saucepan. She raises it above her head and immediately goes to swipe Livewire. Britain’s finest export just about manages to dive out of the way as Fiona swings and misses him by only a few inches. Livewire rolls to his side and covers his face as Fiona prepares to swing the heavy-duty metal saucepan again. “Who the f*ck is Destiny!!” screams Fiona “Is she like…...some bitch that you have been like…playing around with while you have been away??!??” Fiona swings the saucepan again, but this time much slower giving Livewire enough time to grab it from her grasp. “You bastard!!!” screams Fiona as Livewire smirks using the sauce pan to block her closed fist from hitting his face. “What in the blue hell are you talking about?” asks Livewire as Fiona finally slows down and stops swinging her arms. “I’m talking about the slut that you have like…been cheating on me with, that Destiny is sssooo dead!” replies Fiona with venom before picking up a rolling pin to hit Livewire with. “Woah, hold on a second Fiona. I’m not cheating on you” laughs Livewire as he rises from his crouched position. Fiona drops her wooden rolling pin and suddenly her frown melts into a smile. Fiona tentatively approaches Livewire with her arms widespread in the form of a hug, but before she can get any closer Livewire says something. “Well….” Livewire starts “…I’m not cheating on you with a girl called Destiny anyway.” Fiona’s scowl quickly returns and she bends down to pick up the rolling pin again but before she can Livewire grabs her and pulls him towards his body. He caresses his hand through her short red hair before planting a slow kiss on her lips. The two kiss for barely thirty seconds before Livewire moves his head away and grins, his big brown eyes reflecting Fiona’s warm glow. “You know I’m only joking don’t you babe?” Livewire asks, panting in between words. Fiona nods and sniggers knowing at how easily she can get wound up at times. “Destiny, is my future, it’s the fate that I am dealt with and it is the reason why I am the most talented technical wrestler in the world and is the reason why I’m also the luckiest man in the world to have you.” Fiona once again stays silent but simply nods her head knowing exactly what her boyfriend is saying and fully understands the point he is trying to make…for once!!! “And finally…” Livewire laughs “….Destiny is not some bargain basement slut that I’m cheating on you with, gees sometimes your trail of thought amazes me, like the time you thought pasta was a famous artist. Anyway I think all this tension between us needs to be relieved, don’t you think?” Livewire playfully slaps Fiona on the bottom, his hand not connecting with a lot of cloth. Livewire has a wicked smile on his face and looks down at Fiona’s butt. “Are you wearing any knickers?” he asks, with trepidation in his voice. Fiona shakes her head and starts giggling like a schoolgirl. “What do you think?” she replies cheekily. The two lovebirds start laughing and Livewire decides to carry Fiona upstairs to the bedroom. The scene closes with Livewire and Fiona jumping onto their massive double bed, clinched in a hold of love. Steamy stuff huh? Will Livewire recover in time from his excursions to battle with Synhazard on this weeks Adrenaline? Who knows but I’m sure Livewire will enjoy trying to find out. Be sure to tune into this weeks Adrenaline live from the Hartford civic centre to some ass-kicking RUSH style. Take care Livewire-ites. The End
