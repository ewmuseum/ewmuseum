Damn public transport (Part 1)

20 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20-06-02 (Voice of Livewire) Everything for me at Ignition went just as planned. Everything for me at Ignition was like a dream come true. Everything for me at Ignition was just…perfect. I managed to achieve three goals that I had set out to do for quite a while. Goal number one was of course to win the Network title number one contenders triple threat match. Now as the victor of the match I get the chance that I have richly deserved from the moment I stepped into RUSH to go one on one with the Network champion. Goal number two was be a willing ally to my stable buddies El Spiffy! and Christopher Shackelton in their fight against the growing virus that is Playaz Inc. Statuz Quo may still be the Syndicate champion but it will only be a matter of time before either Chris, myself or the Spiffster gets our hands on that stupid moronic blood gang member and take his beloved title from around his waist and drape it across ours. Oh and of course goal number three. Goal number three was to thwart those village idiots Anti-Trust and in any plans they may have had in store for any unlucky victims during the course of the night. Call me stupid, call me idiotic..heck even call a fool for risking my own body to help Ricky Starr but a crucifixion is for sadists not wrestling purists. So now I as do all the members of Shack Pack get another opportunity to right some wrongs and do exactly what every RUSH fan would love to do and that is get our hands on The Solution or should I say what is left of The Solution. It is fair to say that these despicable bastards otherwise known as Tim Walden, Cassius Deamont, Chris Matthews and Steve Brent are not exactly currently reading from the same page. Sheesh they aren’t even reading from the same book. Right now, The Solution are a breaking team and it is up to us Shack Pack, the premier super-heroes of RUSH to continue our fight against all that is wrong in wrestling and on Thursday on Adrenaline throw the final well oiled cog into dysfunctional stable of The Solution. Anyway time to have a time-out. Time to relax and deal with matters outside the squared circle and silver haired egotistical world champions. It’s time to do my bit for society. For the people of Edmonton, Canada to be exact. I will be visiting Edmonton High school, the countries 2nd highest graduate achieving high school to give a talk on achievement. God knows why they choose me…Britains finest export, but I’m privileged and my agent Ted Wanstead believes it will strengthen my Anglo-Canadian links even further. Hhhmmm….I can just vision my T-shirt sales going through the roof in good old moose country…oh sh*t….I mean in good old country called Cananda where moose are breed….damn it…you guys know what I mean. Livewire is sitting on the train relaxed with his legs crossed and he is seemingly distant as if he is thinking about anything but the speech he will be doing in three and a half hours to over 1000 pubescent teenagers in a packed school hall. Livewire is dressed in a casual lime green shirt and a pair of long black jeans. Only the beaming sun through the trains transparent window manages to break Livewire from his comatose state. It is now that he rubs the sleep from his eyes and glances around the train. The train is jam-packed with all different types of passengers ranging from punks dressed in skin tight leather and nose rings to the average middle-aged business men who is married to his job and is trying their best to catch some sleep before they reach their workplace and work another 14 hour office day. The trains track seems to be rather rickety as the train wobbles from side to side on its journey from the outskirts of Edmonton to central Edmonton, a journey that takes just over 3 hours. Livewire breathes a sigh of relief as the train slows down and stops shaking from side to side. The momentary nausea that had built up from his taut stomach to the tips of his temples had now subsided and Livewire was now starting to relax once again, leaning back in his chair. Livewire had become accustomed to the more pleasurable things in life since becoming a professional wrestler and the rigors that accompany public transport don’t bode to well to those who usually travel in style in a jet black Alfa Rome Spider. Of course Livewire being Livewire, travelling in another country with his car would be too much of a pointless hassle. So a plane from the States to Canada topped off with a train ride to central Edmonton seemed the most feasible and cost-effective choice. Livewire delves into the small leather holdall that is by his side and takes out a letter. Livewire smiles for the first time of the journey and reads the letter whispering to himself as he does so. “Well I guess it’ll nice to read what Ted has to say about this little excursion. It’s a shame he has a few loose ends to tie up back in L.A but I suppose that’s life. Anyway he made me promise that I read the letter only once I was on the train. Livewire sits forward in his chair and closely reads the letter. A look of radiance comes across his face. Hey Wire, How’s it like in the cold of Canada buddy? I guess by the time you have read this you would have been told by about hundreds of different people that it’s never really ever this cold in the middle of June. Believe me, I’ve been to Canada six times in total and it’s freezing cold all year round. Ok, enough about weather and more on the task in hand. I expect this visit and talk of yours to go exceptionally well. I mean you are the number one contender now to the Network title, greatness such as yours doesn’t set it’s foot in Canada every day. By the way before you start strutting like a dog on heat that last comment was meant to be a joke. Anyway just be pleasant, informative and answer all questions that are asked. Oh yeah and please don’t offend, these school kids are a potential marketing gold mine. So please….no Canadian soccer team jokes…....please!!! Have a great time Wire and good luck on this weeks Adrenaline, I’ve got to go and fix a contract with this new baseball prodigy in about fifteen minutes so I’ll finish my later here. P.S Tell Fiona to stop ringing me and asking for me to help keep her bed warm at nights! Ha! Ha! Livewire chuckles to himself and puts the letter into his back pocket. He closes his eyes as he tries to get some rest before his big achievement speech. It is now that the trains speed starts to increase again causing Livewire’s head to jolt back and forth much to his displeasure. “Damn Canadian public transport” he says to himself before falling back to sleep. Adrenaline promises to be a great conclusion to all that happened at Ignition. Will Shack Pack add the final nail to the Solution coffin? Or will Matthews, Brent and Deamont put their differences aside for the night? One thing is for certain, the personification of cool is waiting, willing and able to kick and severely injure some Solution ass. Take care Livewire-ites.
