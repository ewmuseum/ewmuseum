I Hate The World, But Not The Same Way Faggy Goth Kids Do. [1]

1 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 1st, 2002 A.D. I hate the world. I hate the way people parade in suits thinking they own me. I hate the way people switch their loyalties at the last second to something else, to please others. I hate the way woman manipulate men into doing whatever the hell they want. I hate the world, but not the same way faggy Goth kids do. Not to mention I fucking hate Goths. I hate it how thirteen year old kids dress up like women with black lipstick on their lips, what are they? Girls? I hate teenagers; I hated every single person at my High School. I hated the Jocks, the Cheerleaders, and the Slutty Upper-Class Whores that didn’t give me a second look when I asked them to see a movie with me. Hell, I think everyone at my High School had something against me for whatever reason, reasons that I didn’t know. In High School I only had one or two friends, but even then I hated those two people. At lunch times I’d go into the Weights Room and pound the living crap out of a boxing bag just to release some of my frustrations. So this is where I leave you. Enjoy my crappy memories of High School, I’m sure you’ll enjoy just like I did....not. * * * * November 26, 1991Mind High SchoolMontreal, Quebec, Canada CRUSH! The sound of a locker door crushing against my back echoes through the dormant hallways of Mind High School. I shake my head, loose long black hair flying everywhere as I’m grabbed by the scruff of the collar. Standing in front of me, with his fists intertwined with my clothing was one of my greatest haters, a burly 16 year old known as Freddy Webb, nicknamed ‘Lucifer’ by me. A smug grin was spread across his face as he stared right into my eyes, my hate filled emerald green eyes stared right back at him without a care. He shook me, my back slammed into the locker once more. “Listen, you” he scowled at me, the lyrics of hate on his tongue. “I’ve told you once, and I’ll tell you again, do NOT fucking look at Leah again or you’ll be wearing my fist as new hat.” “But I wasn’t looking at her” I retorted back with a smile. “I WAS LOOKING AT HER TITS!” The big kid known as Lucifer’s eyes widen as I joke about looking down his girlfriend, one of the best looking girls in the entire schools, and the object of my crush’s top. He lets go of my collar, allowing me to slump down to the floor, my eyes wincing a little. He tightens a fist at my head, pulling back with all his strength ready to pop me one right in the face. “YOU DID WHAT, YOU LITTLE PUNK!?” he bellowed at me, fist clenched. I smirked a little, I knew what was coming. First he’s going to berate me a little, and then threaten to punch my lights out. Betting that, he’ll follow that up by shoving me a couple of times and then probably punching me right in the jaw. “I said I looked at her nice big rack.” I said back, smirking like an idiot. I know I’m going to get my head punched in, but hey, at least I’ll go down laughing. “Why I oughtta!” he screeched back. “I should punch your lights out you little punk!” See? Told you. “You seem to fancy Leah a bit, don’t ya punk?” “Of course, Lu-Freddy.” I said back, narrowing my eyes a little. “I seen the way ya look at her.” He said back, god his accent pisses me off. He smirked a little as I rubbed my lip, searching for the faintest sign of crimson water...blood. “I seen the way ya stop in the hallways when she walks past, pokin’ ya grubby little eyes down her tops. I seen the way ya wait out the front of the school and wait for her to walk past and stare at her ass. Fuckin’ makes me sick.” What is with this guy’s accent!? THIS IS CANADA! “Yeah, and your point is?” I fired back, without a care in the world. “So what, I think your ‘girlfriend’s good looking, got a problem with that?” “Yeah, YOU think MY girlfriends good looking.” He said, like I give a crap what this guy thinks. He’s a guy who’s been making my High School life a living hell, so I’m not doing anything wrong by finding his girlfriend attractive. “So listen here, Matthews, if I EVER catch you lookin’ at Leah like that again, you’re gonna be wearing my fist, kinda like right now!” And with that Freddy “Lucifer” Webb reeled his fist back and punched straight fist. As it came flying at my face, I closed my eyes and allowed it to hit. I heard it thump into my cheekbone with unstoppable force, like an anvil hitting concrete it thumped into my skin...pain, lots and lots of pain. I didn’t try and avoid it. I just let it hit me. I accepted my fate; this was my fate during high school – to get beaten up everyday. Joy. Time slipped by as I stubbled around in the locker rooms clutching my head, a bruise imminent on my cheek. The time was well after 3:00, the entire school was out save for the people who had detention. I had missed my last period English class by about an hour, but I didn’t care. I wondered through the hallways of Mind High School toward where my locker was, on the other side of the school. Slamming the door shut, I lock the locker back up and walk out the school, bag in tow. Heading down the steps I sigh and looked for a Bus. Not in the mood for walking. I leant against the stonewall of Mind High School, rubbing my cheek where the large purple bruise had formed. Check watch: 3:38 PM. No bus had come yet. I was reading one of my few Comics, I think it was Spiderman while I waited for a bus to come past and take me home. I flipped the page of the Comic for a while, mainly looking at the comic strip and not at the text – I will always hate reading. Check Watch: 3:58 PM. I rolled back the comic book and chucked it into my bag, still no bus. By now I was growing tired, very damn tired of waiting for Montreal’s pathetic public transport to come around to this district and pick me up. Little did I know that waiting for me around the corner was Freddy “Lucifer” Webb and his friends, shit. “THERE YOU ARE!” he yelled as he came around the corner, his friends standing at his side. “I’M GONNA KILL YOU, PUNK!” “What the fu-“ I said, turning around to see Lucifers fist slammed right into my cheek. Immediately I collapsed into the stonewall, dropping my school bag with a thud. Ike a pack of wolves devouring a fallen Deer, they pounded me with punches, kicks and almost anything they could. It didn’t really hurt that much I had a pretty thick jacket on; it was rather chilly that day. “FUCK OFF!” is what I screamed, but that didn’t chase them off, something else did. “HEY!” a female voice yelled beyond the pack. As they moved around, I could immediately tell who this was, the object of my crush for the past however long it’s been. “Freddy, just leave the kid alone. Stop picking on him constantly!” “Oh yeah?” He replied to his girlfriend, my object of affection, Leah. “And what if I don’t, Leah? You gonna leave me for him? You gonna leave me for this...WEAKLING!?” “I might” she said, my ears immediately pricked up as to why a girl a year older would even think about being my girlfriend. “You’ve given him so much hell, before I thought it was rather funny, but now it’s just pathetic.” “FINE!” Lucifer screeched back, I could see veins popping all over his body. Rather disgusting. “I don’t need you anymore!” Lucifer turned around, yelling orders to his group of friends to follow him to wherever they hung out after school. I shook my head and whipped some blood from my lips, smearing it into my t-shirt like a cloth. The girl of my dreams was still standing over me, glaring at her now ex-boyfriend. “Are you okay?” she asked reassuringly, whipping some of my blood off my lip. “Yeah” I said back timidly. “T-thanks for helping m-me back there.” “Hey, it was no problem” she said back warmly. “If you ever wanna hang out after school, just ask and I’d love to.” Jackpot, baby, jackpot. “See ya ‘round.” The girl of my dreams said, turning around with a warm smile on her face. She walked down the footpath a little, before jogging across the street and around a corner, now out of my site. With her now gone from my view, I leant back against the stonewall and rubbed my cheek, the large bruise flaming up on my skin. The girl of my dreams. Bitter enemies fighting. High School life. A true nightmare. Maybe not all of it was a crappy memory....
