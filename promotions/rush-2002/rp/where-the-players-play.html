Where The Players Play

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: I'm on a roll... “Welcome to Atlanta, where the players play, and…” For a town with all these supposed badass rappers in it, you’d expect they would have a song other than Ludacris abusing sound systems all the time. But no. It is Georgia, after all. In what seems like the eighth or ninth club that has been playing that piece of garbage on a loop, we find our subjects mid…uh…research. Because, you see, this isn’t any club. This club has a specific hue and reputation that makes it special. This is the Gold Club. … You know…THE Gold Club. … Come on, don’t you ever read the news? THE GOLD CLUB! The strip club where all the athletes had sex with the dancers? Like Patrick Ewing and Eric Bischoff? Damn…you people are hopeless. So anyway, this is where we are. The Gold Club. A strip club. As the camera pans across the room, we can make them out. At the bar – something that has become very common in recent weeks – sit the Spanish commentator duo of Juan Carlos Smith and Julio Diaz Perez Gonzalez. Each man holds a glass full of scotch and a microphone. The mics aren’t live, of course. Just props. Just to make their charge happy. Off to one side of the main room, a stripper named Dyamond is grinding up against the solid steel synthetic loins of RoboGates. The android of the Microsoft CEO does his best to replicate his human counterpart’s meek smile. Dyamond does her best to look interested, as long as it keeps the metal…thing happy, and slipping those bills into her g-string so her baby can eat, and her boyfriend can drink. But enough about the whore. In the middle of the room, on stage, swinging around a pole and gyrating along with the music, is our hero. The man. The myth. The Spiffy!. “Shake the bon bon!” His FUBU jacket flaps wildly open and the matching pants slide down around his knees, exposing the tights with “Spiffy!” across the back. He squeals and tosses money about like it’s nothing. The dancers alongside him…let’s call them, I don’t know…Jade and Misty. They giggle with each successive bill thrown their way. “Is getting hot in herre! El Spiffy! says take off your clothes!” No, he’s not drunk. This is just how he always is. Back at the bar, one glass half-empty, the other half-full, Juan Carlos and Julio just watch the preceedings. “Where did he get all of that money from?” “You remember the one location that they opened for Bill Gates’ Burrito Shack?” “In Iowa, right?” “Yeah. Well all of a sudden, it just got HUGE.” “Really?” “People are flocking there from all over the Midwest.” “How the hell did that happen?” “They just started the ‘Ex-RUSH Superstars’ action figure giveaways. Collect all 18.” “I guess that’s appealing to the Midwest.” “It could also be the small amounts of nicotine they’re putting in the meat.” “That’d do it, too.” “Yup.” On stage, El Spiffy!’s pants are now around his ankles. One of the strippers is wearing his jacket…but not really putting it to use for coverage. His ‘do rag is sliding down his mask. “Where is the Spiffy!’s commentary?!?” The announcers, without even giving each other a look, roll their eyes in stereo, put down their glasses, and raise their prop mics. “El Spiffy! grinds with the strippers!” “Nobody grinds with strippers like El Spiffy!!” Grinning happily, El Spiffy! continues to dance like a madman until the song comes to an end. He slaps his dance partners on their asses, hops off the stage, and turns back, pulling out a wad of money. “So how much does it take for the Spiffy! to…you know…” Yeah, he’s doing it. I’m as shocked as you are. “El Spiffy! propositions the whores!” “Nobody propositions whores like El Spiffy!!” The strippers shoot a nasty look at the announcers before turning back to the Spiffster. “We can’t do that any more, hon.” “Yeah, we got shut down once it was all over the news.” “Aw, c’mon, one more time for the Spiffy!, eh? For old times’ sake.” “We just met you?” “So?” He hops back on stage and begins negotiations. The announcers are still watching. “El Spiffy! badgers the whores!” “Nobody badgers whores like El Spiffy!!” “Say…” comes a voice from behind them. Both men swing around to the grisled face of the club bartender. “If you two are Spanish announcers, how come you only speak English?” Without an answer, the two men look at him. Then they look at each other. Then they look at the camera. Static. Fade.
