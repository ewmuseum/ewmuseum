Sadness (serious rp)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today Coach Tex Kelley is sitting in his locker room after the Ignition PPV. He is watching TV coverage of the PPV. He is hearing about the number of fans in attendance. The lady on the TV is also talking about how Cassius Deamont won the RUSH title. His head is down. There is a white towel draped over his head. He is shirtless and is wearing only a pair of green and blue plaid boxers. He is reclined into the locker. His legs are extended out in front of him. “One lead pipe shot, one chair shot and 2 matches later, I am the number 1 contender and on Adrenaline, I don’t even get a shot at Cassisus Deamont. I guess he is going to avoid me for as long as he can.” Tex falls silently for seconds. It is now apparent that he is on his cell phone. “What they get to face the Solution?” He throws the towel that was on his head across the room. “Ohh Walden ordered it. I have any idea about that one. I am going to fly from here to your office. We have another letter to write.” “Man another tag match? I am with who? Against? Well, if that is what Howard wants me to do, he is the boss.” Tex stands and continues to talk on the phone. He slides into a pair of jeans and a red and white T-shirt with cut off sleeves. His silence is interrupted by an occasional ok and a yeah or two. He closes up his rolling luggage. “I am on the move. I will see you soon.” Tex walks out his locker room and there are some fans still walking the hallways of the arena. They spot CTK and run towards him. They are so excited to see their favorite RUSH superstar. CTK would really like to keep going. In his heart Tex Kelley would love to keep walking but he understands. He understands his responsibilities. He breaks his otherwise speedy stride to allow the young fans to catch up with him. The 12-16 fans are all very excited to see Coach. They are all yelling his different catchphrases at him, “Red, white and the side of right!” “Matthews is exxxactly what is wrong with this culture!” As they run, the kids keep high fiving each other. “Holy crap that is really Tex!” says the skinny kid leading the pack down the ramp. “How the heck did you guys get back here?” One of the heavy set kids in the back pants, catches his breath and steps forward. “My dad is an employee of the Pistons so we kinda know this place pretty well, we know where to hide and where to go.” We see Coach Tex Kelley spend a good deal of time with theses kids they replay every near three count that Matthews had on CTK before CTK won the match. They talk about Matthews playing dead for Deamont. They all enjoy the time that they spend together. Tex signs a large number of autographs for the kids. As he signs one last autograph, ‘Gang – I got a private jet waiting on me! I gotta go!” High fives and hands shakes around as they part ways. “Thanks for everything Tex”, says one of the other boys. They walk away the youngsters buzz about meeting a superstar. A black sedan limo takes CTK to a small airport. He boards the plane. Tex is about to dose off. His phone rings and Mike Beardslee speaks to him about further developments regarding the next Adrenaline card. “Please call Howard. I want to guess ref for the handi-cap tag match. If anyone can guarantee fairness and the idea that this match can take place in a fair way – that is me!”________________________________________________________________________ Monday and Tuesday were spent by Tex training. He was back in Nebraska. He spent hours editing and looking at tape. Between wrok outs and tape sessions, he spent hours fielding calls from his friends and family. He studied the strengths and weaknesses of his partner Madd Maxx. Tex is impressed by his ability and has a few ideas for the match. He spends many hours working on Status Quo and Dante Jackson. It was difficult getting the PWA tape on DJ but he got it. Tex is impressed by Playaz Incarcerated! He has a plan for taking them out and will share it with Maxx. Things were fairly normal until the phone rang. Coach Tex Kelley’s eyes filled with redness during the phone call. This was not good news. He acted quickly. He put on a suit and rushed to the airport. Tex hoped a flight to St. Louis. Training be damned. Tape editing be damned. Tex landed in St. Louis. He took a cab to a very exact location. He stood outside the church and watched the entire funeral but made every effort to not make a big scene. He lurked into the back of the church. At the end of the funeral, he stepped inside a vestibule to avoid making a big scene. When he did see some people, they hugged. Tex quietly made his way to the funeral site by following the large crowd. It is sad when a great man paces. It is sad when sport loses a hero. From a distance coach observed the proceedings. After the funeral, he makes his way to the grave site. Tex drops to a knee. Tears fill his eyes. He prays: O Risen Lord, the way,the truth, and the life! Make us faithful followersof the spirit of Your Resurrection. Grant that we may beinwardly renewed;dying to ourselvesin order that You may live in us. May our lives serve as signs of the transforming power of Your love. Use us as Your instrumentsfor the renewal of society,bringing Your life and love to all,and leading them to Your Church. This we as of You,Lord Jesus,living and reigning with the Father,in the unity of the Holy Spirit,God forever. Amen. Please take this wise man into your home.We will miss him! Tex removes a set of rosary beads at the base of the headstone.The headstone reads: JACK BUCKSportscaster, father, and hero.WWII Purple Heart.KMOX LEGEND.Voice of the St. Louis Cardinals.Hall of Fame -1987. 1925 - 2002 The last thing we see is Tex lighting a candle at the base of the Jack Buck statue outside of Busch Stadium. “You have covered every big sports event in the history of radio and TV. I appreciate all that you did for me and all that you did for your family. God must need a great sportscaster! Our loss is heavens gain” FADETO BLACK
