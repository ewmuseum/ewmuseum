Kill Me. (Challenge to Statuz Quo)

15 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 15, 2002 What's this? Can somebody please answer me a few simple questions? First of all, is there some sort of conspiracy against me, is there some sort of reason why I have to be kept on the sidelines? Is Boda or JKH or any of the High Exec's a tad annoyed that I keep winning? That I kicked the hell out of Livewire, perhaps? Or is it because I pose such a threat to STATUZ QUO, the man that currently has the Syndicate Belt? I believe that is the case, all the fans believe that is the case, and most of the wrestlers in the locker room believe that to be the case. However, I have a little ditty of info that you might like to have JKH, that perhaps all of the wrestlers would like to have, and even perhaps Quo would like to have. I will never stop fighting, never stop trying, and never stop winning. If I am destined to be penalized for greatness for the rest of my life, so be it. If I am destined to have to defeat the odds time and time again, so be it. If I am destined to fight through every challenge, every struggle and then beat that illiterate scum Statuz Quo without a second glance, then so be it. So standing before me now is the next challenge, the obvious new obstacle on my rise to stardom is not an easy one for me to defeat. Facing off against a difficult fighter in Statuz Quo, the same man who defeated Brock Newbludd, possibly one of the biggest legends in Wrestling History. This, my friends is the next step, the next step that will further bring me to greatness. Yet all of us know the truth, know that no matter what they try to put in front of me, no matter what he will do, no matter what lengths he will go to stop me, I will duplicate that length in keeping myself whole, keeping myself cool. Understand that? Statuz Quo, I am not going to lie to you, I have never been this injured before, not by someone else at least. There have been times I have been left worse, but only by my own accord. Yes there were my rampages against Marvin Harris and Ricky Starr, but never have I been beaten, bloodied and bruised like I was at Hell on Wheels. Shot after shot after shot from Marvin Harris, to the head, to the face, to the ribs, to the stomach, to the back to any single little opening you could find. Do I blame you? No, I do not blame you however, I do not blame you for what you did, because I know without that, there is no way you can beat me, there is no way you can keep that title around your waist against me. You know that I am the only threat to your title. I am the only threat to your career. I am the thorn in your side for the rest of your life and you know that. So like I said, I do not blame you for what you did, because if I was as terrified as you are about having my career ended, I would stoop lower then you could even think of, lower then you could ever dream of. However don't think that since I don't blame you, I will not seek my revenge against you. Simply this, no matter what you can say about it, no matter what she can say about it, no matter what Playaz Inc. thinks about it, your Syndicate Title will be mine, and after that, will you blame me? I suppose that brings me to Rich Cannon. What you were trying to do out there, I couldn't tell you. However, what you did do out there was make me pissed, was ask a fight that I am sure you don't want to be a part of. Honestly, I know as well as you do losses, I am getting sick and tired of having to wait. Wait with disgusting blatant attacks with weapons, with trying to stop me from getting to the top, to the very top and from now on, I am not going to be as, well, settled as I have been in the past. Quite honestly, I am already the Network Champion and that belt will not be taken from me, so what do I have to work for until next week? What do I have to fight for? Nothing. So, nice little Scott Cage isn't around right now, the only thing left is the battered, bruised, bloody and beaten man that lays before you today. That will be laying in front of you until the day that I die, because I will never stop fighting. Smile boys, cause the fun has just begun, and it won't end until I get the final, and only laugh. I arrived home after a four-hour flight on the red-eye, exhilarated and ready for my next match. Yes, and while dreaming of my next match, while getting ready to take something that I believe I deserved, I receive a call from JKH's assistant telling me that once again I am sidelined for next week. What does that mean? It means that once again they are putting me away in an attempt to let someone like William Perry take on Statuz Quo. Some people say that I didn't deserve getting the Network Title, some people say that I was given a gift for signing, and for those that say that, I am not going to lie to you, it was a present of sorts and perhaps I didn't deserve it. But over the last few months, with a match every week, have I earned the present that I was given? Have I earnedthe gift that was handed to me? Have I earned the right to take on Statuz Quo? Yes, I have, no matter what you think, no matter what you believe, on matter what you wish to have in your mind, I deserve my stardom. Statuz, I can see why you want to stop me, I can see why you want to end me, because I am the biggest threat to your Precious Championship and career and you could never handle that. The fact that I know my limits, I know my ability and that is something that almost every other wrestler in the entire world doesn't. Statuz Quo doesn't, Ricky Starr doesn't, Marvin Harris certainly didn't and the rest of the locker room and payroll don't know it. I am a class unto myself, a class that quite honestly is even higher then you stand, even higher then Cassius Deamont stands. But once again...I don't blame you. How can I? The only good thing to come out of this is that now I am not the only one that knows how good I am, you do too and it scares you half to death. Ready for this? Ready for the epitome of wrestling to step forward, to prove he is what we say he is? Statuz Quo, you may say you are the best, but that is something you should stop lying about... you and I both know the truth, that I am the One, the best that there is, and not a knock against you, but you know it. (Standing in front of the clergy desk in a hotel, Sirius Cage is seen waiting for the Desk Clerk. The room around him is an off white color, almost a pale beige color in fact, matching the outer lines of the diamonds on his comforter. The window to the left is opened completely with no sun in sight, only clouds filling the sky with rain as emminent as it has ever been. The clouds are looking a darker gray color, and only seem to be darkening as Sirius becomes more and more exausted. His hair is ruffled from unknown work, yet without sleep. He seems to be in another state of mind, another place, another room, another body perhaps, as he is certainly not himself. Looking at the miserable day, the ugly sky and the disgusting weather altogether, yet for some reason, it seems to brighten him up just a tad...) Sirius Cage: I'd like to check out please. Desk Clerk: Certainly sir...name? Sirius Cage: Cage. Sirius Cage. Desk Clerk: Got it...you're bill comes up to...nine thousand and fifty six dollars. Sirius Cage: Excuse me? Desk Clerk: Nine thousand and fifty six dollars. Sirius Cage: There must be a mistake, may I see the bill? Desk Clerk: Certainly sir....but there is no mistake. (The clerk hands Sirius the bill, who begins to read off the list...) Sirius Cage: This looks to be in order...but these movies were never rented, nor were these phone calls made. Desk Clerk: I'm sorry sir, but if they're on the bill, they were made. Sirius Cage: This is absurd...look at the times though. Can you trace the calls? Desk Clerk: Of course sir... (The clerk turns towards her computer, and begins typing away, as the front door to the hotel opens, and Scott walks in…) Sirius Cage: Scott! (Scott turns his heads and speeds up towards the elevator...) Sirius Cage: Scott Francis Cage...stop right there. (Scott slows down and turns towards Sirius…) Scott Cage: Oh...Sirius, bro…I didn't see you there... Sirius Cage: I've been gathering up our bill for our stay here... Scott Cage: And what's the damage assessment, Sirius? Sirius Cage: Well Scottl...it is well over nine thousand dollars. Scott Cage: Well...that's not so bad. Sirius Cage: Not so bad? Scott, did you rent the movies and make the phone calls? Scott Cage: What are you talking about? Desk Clerk: Mr. Cage...I've traced the calls... Sirius Cage: Excellent. (Scott and Sirius walk towards the clerk's desk...and Sirius grabs the list...) Sirius Cage: Twenty 800 phone calls? To a gay sex line? Scott Cage: Well Sirius, we already know who made those calls... Sirius Cage: Who?Scott Cage: Let’s get back to the room and start packing, I’ll show you who it is. Sirius Cage: Okay then…thank you miss...it has been greatly appreciated. Desk Clerk: And, when are you planning on paying? Sirius Cage: Oh yes...the payments...uh…Scott Cage: Bill it to Monty Harris. Desk Clerk: Thank you sir... Sirius Cage : No, thank you. Come on Scott, let’s get the luggage. (Sirius walks towards the elevator...and the two get in...) Scott Cage: I just thought of something... Sirius Cage: What is it? Scott Cage: What if Statuz Quo snuck into our hotel room? Sirius Cage: Why would he do that? Scott Cage: Do make all the gay phone calls...and rent the porn. Sirius Cage: I think you're going to have to explain your logic. Scott Cage: Yeah, well think about it. What if Quo was really a closet homosexual, and he only covered it up by pretending to be a pimp thug? Sirius Cage: I don't follow... Scott Cage: I've known Statuz alot longer than you have...and I know he takes alot of drugs and stuff… Sirius Cage: Your point? Scott Cage: Everyone knows that the hardcore druggies do anything for their supplies...hell, I've seen Statuz Quo suck dick for crack. (Sirius stares at Scott as if he’s got porcupines growing from his head…) Sirius Cage: Scott, that was from “Half-Baked.” Scott Cage: All right..so I slightly exaggarated. But he'll have sex with men if he had too... Sirius Cage: So what are you saying? Scott Cage: He's a closet male whore. Sirius Cage: I see... so you're saying that Statuz Quo hides his homosexuality, stole into our hotel room, rented numerous gay porn movies, and phone calls? Scott Cage: Isn't it obvious? Sirius Cage: No, not in the least. Scott Cage: Well you just don't have the criminal instinct like I do. Sirius Cage: I see, and how did you achieve this criminal instinct? Scott Cage: I went to a night school, they were handing out degrees. Sirius Cage: And what school is this? (Scott frowns, obviously trying to come up with some name…) Scott Cage: Um…Harvord. Sirius Cage: I see…so, you went to Harvord, got a degree in criminology, that allows you to assume that Statuz Quo broke into our room and purchased thousands of dollars in gay material? Scott Cage: Do I have to repeat myself? Sirius Cage: No Scott, that theory makes perfect sense. Scott Cage: Whew...because I'm getting confused. Sirius Cage: Well if it was Statuz Quo, how can we pin him? Scott Cage: I'll call him. Sirius Cage: You'll what? (Scott whips out his cell phone, and starts dialing some numbers...) Sirius Cage: You have Statuz Quo’s phone number? Scott Cage: Shhh....it's ringing... Sirius Cage: Uh… Scott Cage: Hey there Statuz...it's Scott. I know you're the one who broke into my hotel room...no, you listen. I know you came here and let out your sexual frustrations. No, you can't apologize, you owe me nine grand. No...I don't want to talk it over...goodbye! (Scott slams down the phone...) Sirius Cage: Very assertive Scott, I’m impressed…Scott Cage: You damn right! I let his answering machine have it! Sirius Cage: Scott, Scott, Scott…when will you ever learn? (Scott shrugs as the camera fades to black...)
