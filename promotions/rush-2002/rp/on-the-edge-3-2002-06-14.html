Date: June 14th, 2002 A.D. Journal Entry:July 14, 2002 I can’t believe I did it again; I put that damned sleeper hold on her once more and you bet’cha, it knocked her out...again. I thought I would never be able to do something like that, but I guess I am wrong... .... damn it I hate it when I’m wrong. Gee, nothing interesting happened since then, that’s all for today. * * * * Ever since Chris Matthews had knocked out the beautiful damsel Kathryn, girlfriend of Cassius Deamont, he had been sitting on a large deck chair, outside on a balcony, over looking the city of New York. Only ever retreating back inside the hotel room, where he has been locked inside of for the last week, to get a drink or some food or to take a piss in the toilet. A recent New York Times paper has fragments of it thrown all over the desolate hotel room, the only headline we can see is of the weather for that day. Matthews sits on this deck chair, passing the time by reading Newspapers, Magazines and Books. No Television. Pulling the paper down from his gaze, Matthews looks at out at serene skyline, the time on his watch reading 3:49 PM. With a sigh, the paper was thrown onto a nearby glass-topped table, Matthews breathing heavily from his nose. “Nothing much to do here” he said, watching a bird fly past and onto a nearby ledge. The bird chirped a few times, bobbling its small head watching everything before leaping off the edge and back into the air. “F*ckin` birds” he mumbled the pigeons chirped loudly and energetically. “BAH!” Matthews yelled out loudly, causing a few of the Pigeons resting on the edge of the balcony to fly away, startled. He had heard of the concern that the public had taken for Kathryn since she disappeared 2 weeks ago. Even people on the street were pinning up flyers with her picture and description on it, that’s why he hasn’t left the safety of his hotel room. “I remember...” he begins to say, with a confident smirk on his now physically drained face. “...on the first Adrenaline when the alliance between myself and Cassius was formed. I remember him saying to Drachen, his first enemy in RUSH: ‘But I have Kathryn!’. He chuckles slightly after finishing that sentence. “Kind of funny when I think about it.” he chuckles out once more, the sleepless bags under his eyes evident. “Me, currently his most hated enemy, has his girlfriend. The woman he loves so dearly, is in the hands of his most hated person.” He chuckles once more, before pulling himself up off the deck chair. He walks a short distance toward the ledge, the metal white railing in front of the balcony, just making sure you don’t fall to the ground hundreds of feet below. A sneer spread across his smug face as he looked down at the people below, his hotel balcony wasn’t to high up so he could still see the people walking up and down the sidewalk. “Bah!” he said once more as a Taxi drove past, blowing its horn. Matthews began to drum his hands on the railing, in complete and utter boredom. “Screw this, I’m gonna get a Paper.” Pulling the glass door open, Matthews walked straight through the curtains, not even brushing them aside as he enters the dark, dark room. * * * * Down on the Streets... “Get’cha papers here!” a young boy, wearing a large brown coat, yelled out on the street corner. “Latest headlines and all!” People rushed past the young kid, looking about 11 or 12 years of age, he waved his arms in the air, desperately trying to sell a paper. Men and woman wearing business outfits and carrying their briefcase’s hurry past the smaller child trying to make an honest buck. “Come on people!” he bellowed out loud, waving a Newspaper in the air. “ONLY A FEW BUCKS A PAPER! DON’T BE SUCH CHEAP ASSHO-” “I’ll buy one” interjects an unknown person, startling the younger kid. He spins around and look at this man, who a lot of us know as Chris Matthews. Matthews is clad in all black, a leather biker hat on his head turned around backwards. “You will mister?” the kid asked back nicely, suddenly his eyes grew wide. “Hey I know you! You’re that Chris Matthews fella from Rush Wrestling!” He nodded back to the kid, not smiling at all, just nodding. “Yes, yes I am.” The kids face lit up with enjoyment as he told him these uninteresting facts. Matthews’s face stayed the same, no smile, just somber. “WOW! It sure is cool to meet you!” the kid excitedly said back to Matthews, who in return just shrugs. “I’m a fan of Rush too!” Matthews nods back at the kid, desperate to get away from the annoying small child. “Eh” he begins to say to the kid, scratching the back of his head. “That’s nice and all, but can I just get my Newspaper?” The kids face saddened, but he did as he was asked and handed Matthews a paper. Quickly handing the kid the money for the paper, Matthews tucked it under his arms and walked across the street, back toward his hotel room. * * * * The door swung open to Chris Matthews’s hotel room, the depth of darkness that is the hotel room now exposed to the light from corridors outside the dark hotel room. The gold plate hanging on the door, the number “76” etched into the gold signifying the room number of this hotel room. ‘FLICK’ He turned on the light switch, the electrical surge flickering the lights on and engulfing the room in a wave of light. Matthews lobbed the keys down on the bench and removed his leather cap. With a slip, he removed his shoes. He kicked them off; they flew across the floor and smashed into the one of the leather couches. Yes, leather. Walking around, his socks scraping against the carpet, Matthews entered into the kitchen, dropping the Newspaper down onto the kitchen bench. “Odd” he said, looking around a little in the kitchen. “Where is she?” She, Kathryn. Deamont’s girlfriend for the past 10 or so years. Matthews looks over the Kitchen, or what’s in front of him, and then looks to the make-shift living room that hotels have…. nothing. Then his gaze looks over to the doors that lead to the balcony, the door is obscure and the white curtains, ripped. His eyes grow wider in fear as he realizes what has happened, “Oh fuck” is what he says. Matthews quickly dash’s around the kitchen counter and makes his way over to the obscure open doors. He gets there, pulling them further open he sees her… …..just standing there, looking out at the city. The first rays of sun on her beautiful body seem to rekindle her life as he opens his mouth to speak. “Kathryn” he begins to say…. “What are you doing out here?”
