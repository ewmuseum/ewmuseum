Sweet Dreams..

20 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20/10/02 Wake up...... time to rise and shine. Time to introduce yourself to that big bold world out there. But wait..... let's take a moment to reflect on what we do. no, what YOU do, when you go and face that big bold world, and better still, what you see when you face that big bold world. You wake up and groom yourself, because God forbid you don't look your best. You wash, cleanse, bathe, relieve and refresh yourself. Once that is completed, you go to your oversized wardrobe. Every single piece of clothing in it suits the season that we are now in, because it simply doesn't bear thinking about that you would still have last Autumn's colours on your shirts and trousers. Your now groomed.... Your now dressed... Your now ready.... Outside you walk the streets from your spacious house , equipped with the usual white picket fences and the smell of freshly mown grass. Now you look.... You see calmness, tranquillity, and believe it or now.... you see peace. You see children playing on their lawns, you see parents watching them happily. You see young lovers hand in hand, obviously in love and staring hopelessly into each others eyes. Now your away from the neighbourhood that you call home. Now your in the city, everything picks up in pace a little here, everything is turned up a notch. You head to work, you look at all the magnificent structures and buildings in your field of vision, and simply marvel at them and the wonders of technology. You see a homeless person huddled on the floor near to where you work. 'Spare change please ma'am?' He politely but drearily requests. You turn your nose up at him and pretend you didn't hear him, you pretend he doesn't exist. You simply ignore him, because let's face it, people like him are not allowed to inhabit the same area as you, not even inhale the seem air, the sheer thought of it makes you feel unclean and dirty and cheap. Now your at your place of work. At the building where you do your profession, and do it oh so well. Your good at what you do, and you know it, your employers know it, because lets face facts, they promoted you, and they cash your three figure monthly pay checks, they wouldn't do that unless they knew you were good, and knew that your the best at what you do. You live a good life, you have everything you need. But you haven't seen how I see the world, how I wake up to it. I'm not even going to ask if you want to see it, I'm just going to show you, and your going to sit down, and pay attention, because this is the most important lesson your ever going to be taught. I wake up, the foul smell of mould pushes itself up my nose. I rise from my bed, which is simply a mattress on a floor. I have no need to change, for the simple reason I sleep in the clothes you now see me in. But wait, I'm here to give you a grand tour of my humble abode, let's fast forward. We're outside now. In fact we're in the same neighbourhood you just came from, but everything is a little....different. We see the same children playing on their lawns happily and merrily, smiling and giggling. Then out of nowhere, an image flashes onto the screen. The same children, but older, much older, in their mid twenties at least. Their pale, their faces full of stubble, their eyes are groggy and glazed over. They sit huddled over a small accompaniment of items. You realise what they are, the things you have never seen in person, only in the high priced moves. Their drugs, illegal drugs! The two boys sit crouched over them, sniffing them, injecting them and smoking them. You realise this is the future of these two boys, this is what I see as their future.. The image then fades, but then you see their parents, standing on the lawn smiling uncontrollably at their little bundles of joy. Another image, another piece of the future you don't want to see. The same parents, the mother, bent forwards over some decayed old couch, asleep and snoring, her make up applied sloppily over her face. Beneath the make up you can see several bruises though, along with numerous cuts, swellings and scabs, with a bottle of Vodka clutched close to her stomach. A loud slam is the next sound we hear. It's the husband, the same husband who just a moment ago was singing songs with his kids in the garden. He storms into the room, shirt half open and tie carelessly hanging around his neck. The wife wakes up with a look of fear in her eyes. She hoists her alcohol filled sluggish body up, but as soon as she does she is met by the clenched fist of her husband drilling into her forehead. The same hand she placed their wedding ring on to symbolise and seal their vows of eternal and everlasting love, would turn out to be the same hand that struck her down viciously numerous times. The husband then yells some profanities at her, before crawling on top of her and covering her mouth with one hand to muffle her screams as he forces himself inside of her. Mercifully, that image slowly fades away too. Had enough yet? Well, we're only just beginning. The same buildings that you marvel at everyday on the way to work greet us. Though I marvel not at them, I roll my eyes and force myself to see what really lies behind these structures, but more importantly, modern technology. You say modern technology, and what do you think? The internet? Well let's talk about the internet. The internet, the information super highway. Everything you ever needed, everything you ever wanted to know, is just at your fingertips. All the knowledge you ever wanted to know. Bullshit! The internet. The new found home of paedophiles, perverts and child abusers. A tool that to them is a dream come true. A tool they use manipulatively but effectively to gain the trust of young unsuspecting people. A tool they use to feed and nourish their wicked fetishes and needs. Still a wonderful invention? Decide for yourself. 'Spare change please sir?' Ahhhhh here we are. it's the same homeless person you just passed a few moments ago. The one you ignored, the one you pretended didn't exist. The one who you convinced yourself wasn't worthy to live on the same planet as you, the one who wasn't worthy to breathe the same air as you, the one who made you feel unclean and dirty just by looking at him. Well I choose not to ignore him. I walk over to him, smile at him, but he can tell the smile I give him isn't that of sympathy or pity. It's of wickedness and sadistic ness. yet still, he accepts the $50 I slip into his stained dirty and damp shirt pocket. Why am I so generous? Simple...... Another image flashes across the screen. An image of the homeless man, buying various strong alcoholic beverages, all covered brown paper bags. The scene then shifts, to that of the old homeless man lying dead in some dark damp cold alleyway, several brown paper bags surround him. After all, this old man is nothing but scum to you and your world isn't he? By me lending him the money to purchase the liquids to help drown his sorrows and drown his life away, well, I'm doing you a favour am I not? Then there is your job. The job that your good at..... more bullshit! You let them have their way with you. You don't dress up like that just to please yourself, let's face it. Yeah, you got all the right peoples attention, and you slept around a little, but that doesn't matter, because you have everything you wanted. A home... Money... Financial security.... Freedom... But the thing is, you have none of this. All this is a mirage, all this is artificial. Everything you see everyday is just a sugar coated wall plastered over the sadistic truth of the world. I've shown you that today. Now it's time to ask yourself... Do I want to carry on living in denial? Or... Do I want to wake the fuck up? Then again, your only a pathetic member of society. Your not ready to see my world, the REAL world. So go back to sleep...... .. .............. Sweet dreams.
