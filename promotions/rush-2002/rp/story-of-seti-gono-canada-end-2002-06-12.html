Story of Seti Gono: Canada: End

12 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 12th, 2002 A.D. From the start of my life, it’s always been an uphill battle. What may look flashy and glory filled from a far, desolate and ignorant distance is nothing more then putrid. A life which I only know to well, well, I would; it is my life. This life of mine, especially over the last eight years has been filled with nothing but deception and hatred. Hate; yes hate. You know that word? A single, four letter word which sends chills snaking down the spine of all those spoken upon. I hate many people. Several. Countless. Cassius Deamont. I hate him, that sick evil bastard played me for a fool. He tricked me into joining in his sick, twisted and foul mind games, trying to warp the inner min of his foe. Jonathan Drachen. His games manipulated people, screwing with many, many people. I was supposed to be his friend. His ally in destruction. All a fictional tale, nothing but a lie. A fairy tale created to fool the fans, the wrestlers and the owner. As much as I hate to say it, we achieved this. Deamont, Brent and Walden, teamed with I. All four of us played the onlookers and people within RUSH Wrestling for fools. Cassius Deamont. Jonathan Drachen. Tex Kelley. Three men I must conquer, win over to be declared the winner of the RUSH Wrestling World Championship. I’m not in it for the money, for the fame. I’m not in it for the exposure or for the fans, fuck the fans. There’s only one thing, one person who I am in this for.... His name is I. *** Just outside of Toronto, the long and desolate roads where nomads roam, or I like to think so; my car and my lady companion drive. After a short flight back from Koriyama, Japan and my unsuccessful training expedition, I am home in Canada. My country of love. She wears tight fitting jeans and a spaghetti strap top, adorned with sunglass’s raised up on her forehead. My car still sped along the road, the wheel’s thumping along the hot and sticky road. They rip up on the bitumen, further and further as my lady friend and I travel. The hot sun pouring down over us. She tilted her head backwards, her throat showing as clean as day. He long brown hair slowly moving in the quiet, hot and sweaty air. Her skin, glistening in the hot and burning light from above, small speckles of sweat all over her. Black mascara is running down her face, dark blue eye shadow melting into her tanned skin. She smiles. A smile. One of joy. Happiness. Her skin peels in the sunlight, that light above is the hot and fiery orb of energy collectively known as the sun. That smile on her face, how can people smile? I just don’t understand where happiness and joy come from, especially when the world is littered with scum and dirt, like Cassius Deamont. My heads tilts at her, a solemn and depressed look on my face. I haven’t shaved in days; small prickles of blonde facial hair have started to spring up on my face. Little prickles that run a soft, yet spiky sensation across my upper lip. I scratch them. She looks back at me, a smile again. We travel along in this poor and pathetic excuse of a car; to me it is anyway. “Not used to he heat, aren’t we?” she smiles at me. My face stern and expressionless, I hate the heat. I nod back as a reply of ‘yes’. She snuggles up on my arm, hot and red from the warm Canadian summer, if that even exists. “Well” she speaks again, “We’re back home after your training thing in Japan, glad we’re back.” Her question brings a smile to my face, what I thought would be the greatest experience of my life turned out to be a lie. Over there, I didn’t learn anything new really. Just that the fighters over there are exceptionally talented at.....taking a beating. “So am I” I coldly reply, adjusting my sunglass’s. I tighten my hands around the leather steering wheel as I turn a sharp corner. “Are you nervous about this pay per view in Rush?” her question rings through my ear, travelling into the impulses in my head. Several seconds go bye. No answer. Annoyance. “Hello?!” She annoyingly asks me, I ignore her once more. I don’t even want to start thinking about that place or my World Title shot, not right now, anyway. My head shakes back at her. “Not right now” I explain to her, she glares back. “I have no doubts in my mind, this Sunday in just four short days. Meh, Leah, who knows? I could win that damn title or I could end up in the Emergency Ward at the Hospital. So do you think I’m nervous?” I turn the steering wheel again; we get closer to Toronto and home, thank god for that. I am home in my country. My place of birth and my place of death. Detroit waits just beyond the border and so does my destiny as the World Champion....
