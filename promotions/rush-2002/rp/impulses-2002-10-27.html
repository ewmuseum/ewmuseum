Impulses

27 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27th October 2002 NB: The original format of this piece was written in German. It has been checked and translated into English, for the benefit of a predominantly English-speaking audience. Impulse. An act within a moment, which cannot be explained; an act which could be your benefit but to the harm to others and all within a few seconds. However there are good explanations as to why we act upon these impulses that suddenly take over your mind. In the last few weeks, there have been a few impulses that have suddenly covered my mind; like someone opening a can, which they passed wind into, and opening it square in my face. Impulse #1: My Decision to target GOD upon arriving in RUSH Here we have the biggest man in RUSH. A gentle giant if you will, who performs the act of technical wrestling while destroying those in his path with comfortable ease. I admit that this is not a man to mess with, especially down a dark alley, nor do I expect this individual to have come across somebody who isn’t intimidated by his meaty frame, someone who isn’t afraid of risking the wrath of GOD….etc, etc, etc. After watching and listening and reading everything that this man says he is, I decided that I should let him know that he isn’t the only “giant” in this company. Sure, there’s that English mooch Livewire too…but he isn’t high on my priority list at this time. I wanted to point out to him, his doctor friend and everybody else, that he is not as perfect as he thinks he is. I wanted to point out that he’s human…like the rest of us. I wanted to point out that the “wrath of GOD” means absolutely nothing and that I will eventually reach my ambition… To destroy GOD Okay, so this isn’t exactly an immediate impulse of such…but it’s my desire to get the job done and to prove that this camache is a fake, a lanky streak of piss who is being led by a leash by some geek, displaying fraud and pretending to be a doctor. Well, if he’s a doctor…why won’t he answer my calls about the back pain I’ve been getting for the last few weeks. Just as I thought, another damn fake. This isn’t an obsession; I just wanted to point that out to you. When I respond to those “what’s up with you and GOD” questions, I feel like I’m an alien who has just spoken in my native Zorg dialect. What exactly is the big deal here? Does everybody miss the fact that I am no small man myself? I’m only a few inches shorter and a few pounds lighter than Zoidman…so what makes the whole damn world think that I’m basically hitting my head against a brick wall? Do they expect the traditional, not to mention boring, approach of constant sneak attacks? Sure, I may be running away from that goon for the majority of the time but that’s because I actually have a brain and that I put it to good use. Why dig my heels in this early? It’s all part of a masterplan…and no, it isn’t any Mattitude Impulse #2: Why did I spit in Arglye’s face Yeah, why did that big disgusting German spit in Argyle’s…..oh, write a damn book and I’ll be sure to wipe my behind with it. Why did I do it? Simple, he just happen to be in the wrong place at the wrong time. Plus, I needed to clear my nose and I couldn’t block one nostril and snuff with all my might out of the other one, could I? That would be disgusting. But it’s not as if I tried to kill the man, sheesh Impulse #3: Why did I have intentions of using the Network Title on Livewire I’m not going to lie to you, because I feel that the people deserve the truth…but the reason I brought that title into the ring was because I just wanted to cheat for once. Yeah, there had to be a dodgy finish in a title match….because it’s the norm! You see dodgy endings all the time!! So I thought I’d continue the tradition and cheat my way to victory, however it seemed that Livewire caught sight of my plan in action and the mooch got DQ’ed!! How ironic is that?!?! Impulse #4: Why did I make my World Title Tournament Match against Josh Marshall an Oktoberfest Match? Don’t you people pay attention? I gave my reasons on AD last week, get a tape of the show and fast forward. But I know the laziness of the American culture; so in a nutshell, I’m celebrating the German festival here, as I can’t do it in my homeland. Plus, this match is going to be sooooooo much fun! And it’s funny how I get asked the same “what the hell is an Oktoberfest” match?” questions. Sure, I may have given the details on AD, but I left out the juicy bits…and there’s another 12 days to wait!!! Oh, it’s going to be an historic event for RUSH and this industry!!! Hunt’s Final Thought Okay, it’s not everyday that you get to hurt other people, mentally and physically, and enjoy it…so when you do get the opportunity, make it last. Remember that it’s not the taking part that matters…it’s the fact that you win and you rub the other person’s nose in it. Finally, the bigger they are…the dimmer they are likely to be, myself excluded. So with that in mind, take care of yourselves and each other.
