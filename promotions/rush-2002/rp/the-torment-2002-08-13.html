The Torment

13 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13-8-02 Andi smiled as he watches the balls spinning around and starts to make his way forward only to be shot back with an electrical jolt as soon as he had stepped forward and broken the current that actually kept the spheres levitated. The voice laughed manically as it watched form its safe haven. “Not as easy as it looks mortal.” Andi glared ominously into the air before dusting himself off and concentrating on the task at hand. Sweat slowly dribbled from the magician’s brow as the spheres continued to watch the globes doing his best on solving his little problem, but still the solution evaded him. Laughter continued to echo all round the room as Andi’s tormenter indulged itself on winding him up. ‘Just blast your way throw it!’ came a voice from know where. The young magician looked round the room looking to see where voice came from but to his surprise no one could be seen. Shrugging it off as just some sick my game being played by his tormenter Andi went back to working on the little puzzle. ‘Just blast the f**king thing open!’ The strange voice said almost in a hiss. Andi this time quickly spun around on his heels but instead of seeing the proprietor of the voice instead he is greeted with nothing but thin air. The young magician’s mind races almost out of control trying to think of a logical reason why the voice is terrorising him but again the rooms cold silence this time a almost evil sound came within the dialect. ‘Are you fucking stupid or something blast your way throw the f**king thing you’re a magician for Christ’s sake!’ Slowly Andi’s palms slower begin to sweat with anxiety at thought of him slower going mad in a strange world where no one could find him. ‘Blast through the thing, NOW!’ Again the voice came this time almost pushing Andi to the brink then to cap it all off the young magician’s tormentor starts again. “What’s wrong Andi is this too complicated for your poor weak deluded mind.” ‘Are you going to let him talk to you like that, show him you mean business blast it, incinerate it, destroy it!” This time Andi felt something stir inside him something evil, something that just the thought of it would any sane person feel sick. ‘DO IT NOW!’ That was the final straw slowly the normally rational and calm magician felt a rage build up inside him, something he had not felt for years. Struggling not to lose control Andi felt there was no other way but scream, and scream he did. “I WILL NOT GIVE IN TO YOU, I WILL NOT.” The young magician felt his small shade of reality in his short but crazy life as a magician slowly start to crack away, feeling totally vulnerable and alone the young man felt the a cold harsh tear roll down his left cheek as he listened to the two different laughs. ‘You are weak Magician.’ The voice came again this time full of scorn and disgust. Andi felt something warm and sticky begin to run down both palms of his hands, slowly he lifted them more in curiosity than anything else and to say that he was surprised at what he saw would have been a understatement as deep scarlet blood, his blood began to trickle from the creases of his palms. “What are you doing to me?” Andi screamed in panic. ‘This is only the beginning you weak fool.’ The voice replied. As Andi continued to look at his hand’s he felt a sudden pain in his eyes as the slowly began to blur. It didn’t take the young man long to realise he was now bleeding from his eyes and nose. Again he screamed in blind panic.“Please stop it.” ‘You are weak and pathetic.’ Slowly Andi found some invisible barrier break as the voice continued to spit insults at him. “STOP IT NOW!” Suddenly a loud bang, a bang as loud as thunder shook the very fabric of the room. Slowly as the sound began to fade, Andi felt his vision begin to clear as before him everything began to disintegrate into nothing, only to stop some two feet away from door at the far end of the room. Quickly Andi looked around and noticed nothing but empty space behind him. ‘Finally you have shown me some grit.’ As the voice faded away Andi was left more empty and alone than he had every done in his life.
