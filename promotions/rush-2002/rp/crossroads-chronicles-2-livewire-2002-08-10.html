CrossRoads Chronicles #2: Livewire

10 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8.10.02 Love thy crooked neighbor as you love thy crooked self.'-- Murray Humphreys Another loss. I know that it makes me sound conceited and it makes me sound arrogant, and it probably makes me look like a pompous ass. But truthfully, it is one more then there should be. I have a record that some would call the Apex of them all, one that marvels even Cassius Deamont. I have a record that, don't get me wrong, I am still very proud to have, but the only reason I lost was because I guess I didn't believe I could. Chris Matthews did something to me, I can't tell you what it was because I really don't know what it was. He didn't out-smart me, he didn't out wrestle me, he didn't frighten me, because I have gone through Hell already, and what I have had happen, nothing can scare me again. I was prepared, I was ready and I had it in my mind that I thought I could win. So what's the problem? I guess it comes down to the fact that I thought I could win. Before the match, Boda did something to me, that made me question my beliefs, and in that one second, in that little period where I thought instead of believed...I threw the match for the ratings. Do I think that I should have lost? Yes. Am I upset that I got beat? Of Course. Will I let it ever happen again? Doubtful. Matthews and everyone else listening, what it all comes down to, is learning from your mistakes. Learning from the actions that you did yourself, so that next time you will be better prepared. You know it now, as well as I do, that your odds of winning the World Title came up astronomical and you definitely crapped out on your roll. What else to say to you other then...Good-Bye. I really hope that right now, I have changed the opinion of everybody inside RUSH, inside the wrestling industry, and everyone that sends me hate mail everyday, that I really am deserving of the title that I hold. I really hope that I have shown, after defeating the wrestler after wrestler, after defeating everybody who said that I was undeserving, that I got everything handed to me on a silver platter. I hope that I showed everybody that you will have to do more then spill your own blood to take my title away from me. Livewire, who I have fought on many occasions, and who disgusts me to the extreme with his snotty, non-original, effortless way he found his 'calling' in the game. Good work Livewire, be what everybody else already is. Try what everyone else has already succeeded with, that way you know you can't bomb right? Wrong. However, I wish you the best of luck in our match at CrossRoads trying to become the Network Champion, but you do know what you have to go up against if you were to win. I will be watching on, I will be studying you, I will be ready for whatever you throw, and could throw my way in the future. To you a warning as well, for I feel it only fair to warn you. Being Network Champion these days, isn't the prestige it once was. It isn't the opportunity of holding onto the most rookie-coveted strap in the wrestling world. It isn't a stand-out position that really, all the intelligent wrestlers want to hold.. What it is, is signing down that you acknowledge you will be defeated and held back down to the bottom of the ladder. It is in a sense, a death warrant that you may wear proudly, showing off that you will in fact fall to my feet, a broken, deserted wrestler. A shame isn't it? That everybody is put to the same level: Below Me. Conceited? No. Convinced. (The camera fades in to an inside an air-conditioned building, carpets on the walls and photographs of Jeremy Howard, covered by slanted pictures of Chris Sloboda. The camera turns from the 'wall of fame' to see trio walking down the hall. Lars DeVille and Leo Hassler stand behind their boss, Scott Cage, who is opening every door that he comes across.) Scott Cage: This is the one. I can feel it. Lars DeVille: That's what you said about the last 60 doors, INCLUDING THE WOMEN'S RESTROOM.Scott Cage: Well someone should put a sign on that thing! Leo Hassler: There was one! Scott Cage: Shut the hell up! Now, as I was saying...this is the one. Let's go. (Scott smiles as he opens the door and in front of you, is an air-conditioned room filled with booze, red leather couches, and a big-screen television! Scott walks into the room, smiling and laughing. Leo and Lars follow, reluctantly.) Scott Cage: See! I told you guys this was the place! Lars DeVille: Who would have known that someone would have checked 60 rooms, just for a party... (As Scott is about to speak, the camera notices two men behind Scott. The man on the left taps him on the shoulder, as Scott whirls around…) Scott Cage: What do you...WHAT THE FUCK?! MARVIN HARRIS!? TWO MARVIN HARRIS'S?! WHAT THE HELL IS THIS?! I RETIRED YOUR ASS...BUT THERE'S TWO OF... Man #1: AHAHAHAHAHAHA! Scott Cage: Wait a second! I Know that laugh! (Scott pulls off the Marvin Harris mask...) Scott Cage: HARVEY QUINN! YOU MOTHERFUCKER! Harvey Quinn: AHAHAHA! I'M MARVIN HARRIS! Man #2: NO! I'M MARVIN HARRIS! Voices: NO! I'M MARVIN HARRIS! Scott Cage: What the?! (Scott turns around to see that a bunch of people in Marvin Harris masks have appeared as Harvey Quinn is on the floor, hysterical…) Scott Cage: You sons of bitches. I swear Harvey, you're going to get it. Harvey Quinn: But what about all the Marvin Harris's? Don't you have 'take care of them'?! HAHAHAHA! Voice: What's all this mess about Marvin Harris? (Scott turns around, infuriated as the whole room laughs hysterically. He turns to see the one man who he can trust...Sirius Cage.) Scott Cage: SIRIUS! Haha, hey at least there's someone here I can trust. Sirius Cage: Trust? Of course... Harvey Quinn: I'M MARVIN HARRIS! (Sirius quickly turns around, familiar with that voice…) Sirius Cage: Harvey Quinn? What in the blazes brings you here? Harvey Quinn: Cheap endorsements. And besides...I'M MARVIN HARRIS! HAHAHAHA! Scott Cage: Really funny guys. Real funny. (The entire room laughs as Scott stands there, next to Sirius, who is even chuckling too! The camera fades…)
