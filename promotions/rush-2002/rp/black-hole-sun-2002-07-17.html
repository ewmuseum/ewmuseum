Black Hole Sun

17 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/17/02 OOC: One of those one-a-year role-plays that have song lyrics for dialogue. Excuse it, but enjoy it. A light rain fell from the sky that evening – the evening in which it had all come back to him. McGlocklan sat under a bus station’s overhand, sitting with his knees up against his chest. The collar to his jacket was buffed up, and was covering most of his head. He had patches of darkness under his eyes – lack of sleep – and he was trembling. Withdrawal was settling in and he was coming down off another fix. Extremely depressed, he sat there… twitching… waiting for it all to just disappear. A bus pulled up to stop, its un-timed wipers scraping the moisture from off the windows. The doors flung open, waiting for Caleb to climb to his feet and enter. He continued to shake, taking no notice of the doors. From within, the operator bellowed in an annoyed sort of voice… Bus Driver: Coming, Sonny? Caleb turned his head towards the driver, acknowledging his presence, but made no motion to climb the steps and get onto the bus. Shrugging, the old man closed the door to the bus and pulled out of the drive and back down the street. McGlocklan watched as the bus drove for a while before climbing to his feet and shivering away from the bus station. Stammering up the pavement of the sidewalk, people under umbrellas gazed at him with shifty eyes. Making eye contact, he catches a man around the collar and meets him face to face. McGlocklan:In my eyes… indisposed… in the skies as no one knows…Hides the face… lies the snake…The sun is my disgrace… Frightened, the man shoved himself free and scurried away, fearing for his own well-being. McGlocklan sneered at him and continued up the street, the rain coming down like bullets upon the top of his head. In moments time, he thought to himself, it would be all gone and over with anyway. Looking at the gray and mournful sky, he frowned and resumes his slow, dramatic staggering up the pavement. A lone beggar stood at the corner with a Styrofoam mug in his hands, in desperate need for money. People shrugged him off and walked away, patting their fur coats and their Rolex watches. Caleb frowned once again and wandered towards the man. He reached into his pockets and emptied them out, handing the pauper was little cash he owned. Caleb took his hand and patted him on the shoulder. McGlocklan:Boiling heat… summer stanch.Beneath the black, the sky looks dead.Call my name through the cream andI’ll hear you scream again. Caleb gave him another pat and set off again, around the corner and further into the projects. Several bottles and syringes and pipes scatted the gutters. Caleb took in a breath of stale air before continuing down the way. Gang violence was extremely high in this area. He shook his head, spotting a pair of gangs with readied weapons. The leader of the first gang was holding a pistol in his hand and had it pointed at the fallen leader of the second. Caleb frowned and hurried over. The leader smirked and tightened his grip on the trigger, ready to fire – no. A hand was on the barrel of the gun. The leader looked up surprised and met eye to golden eye. McGlocklan:Stuttering… cold and damp…Steal the warm wind, tired friend.Times are gone for honest men,And sometimes far too long for snakes. Caleb touched the gangster’s hand, telling him to release the hold. He acknowledged, mesmerized by the soft glow of Caleb’s eye. McGlocklan nodded softly, and placed the gun on the floor. He shook the leader’s hand, helped the other to his feet, and continued on up the street. The others didn’t return to violence… not for the rest of their days. Caleb continued on, shaking many hands, and helping many people out of dangerous situations. Was he a miracle worker? Not quite. He was planning on something horrible and something cowardly. He had reached his destination – an abandoned, rather tall apartment complex. Grabbing hold of the cross around his neck, Caleb rubbed the figure of Jesus with his thumb before calling out into the wretched night… McGlocklan:In my shoes! A walking sleep!And my youth I pray to keep…Heaven send Hell away…No one sings like you anymoooore! Suicide. The words rang through Caleb’s head like a jackhammer on an ancient street. He took a deep sigh and nudged the door to the complex open. He had helped those people, thinking that perhaps the Lord would be lenient on him – drugs and the like wasn’t for heavenly people. He climbed up those steps, feeling tired – it had been a long day. They seemed to stretch on forever. Panting, he reached the top floor and climbed a smaller staircase that lead to the roof of the building. His feet met with solid rock… the time was now. He looked up the sky before stepping toward the edge of the complex. His feet met the ledge and he looked down… an abyss. A tear trickled down his eye as he wheezed out… McGlocklan:Hang my head…Drown my fear…Till you all just…Disappear… He stretched his arms like out like an eagle in flight, and leaned forward, falling off the ledge. A rush of warm came upon him as he plummeted towards the ground. It would all be over quickly. A shining light in his eyes… the wind in his hair… it was all coming back to him – how he had forgotten so quickly… How had he forgotten? It was all a strange feeling… being flooded with memories. Suddenly, he knew… 10 feet from the ground… The familiar tear in the back… the sound of wind… the rushing over him… he stopped an inch above the ground… Behind him, two fine feathered, silver and ivory wings batted with all their might. He had forgotten and he had remembered… he was back.
