Dr. Jenkins' Hatred For Promoters...

1 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 1, 20002 Dependability. Stability. Reassurance. They’re want keep us safe. On the backburner, letting us go farther and do riskier things. Letting us do what we want. Helping us out in time of need. Keeping us from falling too deep into the pit of life’s end. Keeping us safe. What happens when you lose it? What happens when it isn’t there? Where do we go? What do we do? How do we get back? How long will it take just to get back to square one? Pulling ourselves back up by our bootstraps. Like in the Great Depression that so many of our forefathers faced. Having the floor fall out from under us. Having to scratch, claw, and climb back up to the ground. Seeing nothing, hearing nothing. Harder than where we started. Having to reach that point once again that we were originally given and handed. Taking all that we had for granted and all that we have as well… And losing it… G.O.D. and Dr. Jenkins quickly reconciled once they realized what had come forth. Everything they had gained together in New True Internet Championship Wrestling was old and gone. A nothing. Nothing more. What were they now? Nothing. They were RUSH workers as opposed to NTICW superstars. In NTICW The Genetic Oriented Drone had been a former champion and Jenkins and championship manager, in RUSH they were apart of that NTICW crowd. Falling in with everyone else. Listening in the back at the final NTICW Mayhem Jenkins had been extremely disappointed, but more so, angered. Not at G.O.D., no, it was not his fault in any way. Dr. Jenkins was maddened at Jane Reagan, Jeremy Howard, Tyler Nelson, and most of all, Chris Sloboda. Boda had failed on his part of the bargain. When offered a job in NTICW Boda had said to Dr. Jenkins alone, “I don’t want you to leave me. But if you have to leave NTICW, then so be it. Go on your way out and watch NTICW run from wherever you end up going to.” Well, G.O.D. and Dr. Jenkins did in fact leave NTICW, but they certainly weren’t watching it run from a distant promotion. NTICW was dead. And so were all G.O.D.’s accomplishments. They were dead. Tyler Nelson, Jane Reagan, and Jeremy Howard have the same hatred from Dr. Jenkins though. None of them knew. None of them cared. None of them cared for The Genetic Oriented Drone. Tyler Nelson looked at NTICW as easy pickings for a division that has always plagued his company. Then he saw a few names from NTICW that he could snatch up. He didn’t see the talent NTICW had, he did not see the talent Gene had. Jane Reagan was the same. She saw the man who had won the World Title and grabbed him quickly. That was all, she wanted the best. But not the ones who would become the best, no. Not the people who were following what Scott Taylor had done and would possibly become him soon enough. No, no, no. When Dr. Jenkins had first brought G.O.D. to the Primetime Central federations he had set his sights on Old School Wrestling. An upstart promotion that would certainly increase into greatness… which it had as Jenkins had seen so clearly. However they were rejected. ‘Too young, too much of a rookie’ G.O.D. had been. No, ‘go to NTICW, start there, he’ll get better.’ And he had. Jeremy Howard deserved his hatred for the same reason as Jane and Ty. He doesn’t see the talent. He doesn’t see G.O.D. He sees a wrestler that has made it in another promotion. He sees an opportunity to expand his roster… not his talent. What Jeremy Howard sees is not what G.O.D. is and that angers the great doctor. Which is why Dr. Jenkins has found himself in his laboratory once again. However this time it is not to invent a killing machine of a human to become the perfect specimen. This time it is not to create a weapon to help the ultimate weapon in his destructive conquest towards Extreme Title glory. This time it’s simply… to get G.O.D. over with the RUSH crowd…
