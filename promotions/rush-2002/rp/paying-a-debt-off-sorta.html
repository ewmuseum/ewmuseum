Date: Today Gino is not amused. He's owned his liquor store in downtown Madison for many years and has thus put up with more than his share of asswipe customers. But never like this. 'Listen,' Justin Timberlake sneers, 'it's this simple. I want to buy some wine coolers, okay?' Gino keeps his arms defiantly crossed over his chest. 'Okay,' he retorts and says for the twelfth time, 'I want some identification.' Justin begins fuming. 'Damn it, I'm Justin Timberlake!' Gino stands ready and chews on his cigar. 'I want some identification, Justin Timberlake.' Justin throws his arms in the air. 'Come on! You have to know who I am! I'm from 'NSYNC! I dated Britney Spears!' Gino chomps on his cigar and shakes his head. 'Sorry. Nothing.' Justin's knuckles turn white as he grips the counter and scans the Wall Of Fame. His eyes pop out when he sees the caption '#1 Customers' underneath an autographed 8X10 glossy of The Blood Brothers. 'I don't believe this!' he screams like Luke Skywalker in Star Wars: A New Hope. 'Not only do you not know who I am, but your number one customers are the same jerks that assaulted and humiliated me at the Mall of America in January!' Gino blinks a moment and bites down on his cigar. 'Oh, was that you? Yeah, I remember reading about that in all of the wrestling magazines and the music magazines. Well, in that case, I'm definitely not selling you anything. Get your wussy ass outta my store.' Justin sneers and storms out. 'That jerk,' he mutters under his breath as he is greeted by the chill Milwaukee night air. 'Stupid booze store clerk. I'll make sure he's shut down. I-AAAAAHH!' Justin slips in a pile of dog $hit on the edge of the curb and falls flat on his face in the middle of the street. To his horror, he looks up and sees a pair of headlights coming straight at him— THWUMPITA-THWUMPITA-THWUMPITA The torch red 1971 Dodge Charger screams to a halt. The Reverend Horton Heat cd that was blaring over the stereo is stopped. 'Holy living f~ck,' Stephen Blood says, almost spilling his can of Killian's. 'What the f~ck was that? Dude, that better not've been my f~cking car making that noise.' 'It can't be, baby,' says Stephen's fiancee Stacey Blitsch as she traces her finger down his sharp jawline. 'It was just over at Stosh's Garage for a tune-up.' 'Yeah,' Rush superstar Brock Newbludd adds, taking another drag from his Marlboro. 'It could've been something in the road.' 'Good point--better check it out,' Stephen says, taking another swallow of beer and putting the car into reverse. Justin moans and tries to get up when— THWUMPITA-THWUMPITA-THWUMPITA Stephen nods, stopping the car and taking another swig. 'Yeah, that was in the road. Cool.' He puts the car back into gear and turns the cd back on. Justin slowly reaches toward his broken jaw and opens his good eye to see several of his teeth littering the street. 'Uhm uhluv,' he mumbles. 'Uh duhnt buhluv ud, uhm uhluv-' THWUMPITA-THWUMPITA-THWUMPITA 'So where are we headed again?' Lindsey Francis, Brock's girlfriend asks, leaning her head against Brock's chest. 'Craig High School,' Brock answers. 'I owed someone from that area a while back and he's chosen this time to collect.' 'I still don't f~cking believe it,' Stephen says. 'You owe a guy who would grow up to be a f~cking high school guidance counselor. That's like one of the most worthless jobs on the market today.' 'How true,' Brock says in agreement. 'If it came down to it, I'd rather be a jizzmopper at some hole-in-the-wall porno shop.' 'Me too,' Stephen says. 'Hell, I'd rather be Creed's f~cking bodyguard rather than be a high school guidance counselor.' They eventually pull up to the Craig High parking lot and pull into a space. They all get out and head for the auditorium. Stephen is clad in his baggy black cargo jeans, black leather biker boots, his classic 'I'm The New F~cking Legend, You No-Talent Assclown!' t-shirt over a long-sleeved Red Elvises tee, his skull and roses bandanna and his Harley jacket. Brock is outfitted similarly, wearing his gray urban cammo pants, black jump boots, 'Inno-f~cking-vator' tee, black leather Harley jacket and his Blood Brothers fitted ball cap on backwards. Stacey and Lindsey are dressed in like fashion. Stacey is wearing her tight jeans with the ripped knees, heeled boots, Stephen's red snakeskin collar shirt with the first four buttons undone and no bra underneath (snoochie-boochies), a Blood Brothers bandanna that her blond hair is pulled through and her black leather jacket. Lindsey is also dressed in a pair of tight jeans, heeled boots and black leather jacket, but her curly red hair is tied back and she sports her 'Brock's b###h' baby tee. They all make it into the auditorium and cannot believe their eyes. There are many amounts of people--adults and high school students alike--all filling the bleachers and staring at the gymnasium floor in the middle which is playing host to— A bunch of dorks in martial arts gis. 'What the f~ck. . ?' Stephen manages to get out, almost dropping his beer. 'Brock!' someone calls out. 'Brock, it's so good to see you!' Everyone in attendance and on the gym floor looks over as they see a short, portly man in thick glasses run over and grab Brock's hand, shaking it vigorously. 'Remember, Grubb,' Brock says in a very low tone and a very plastic smile, 'we do this and it's over between us. Settled. Finito.' 'You can't have alcoholic beverages in the high school gymnasium,' Grubb says to Stephen, looking over the top of his glasses as if he were looking at a problem student. 'Why not?' Stephen asks. 'I had some in the car, after all.' Grubb gasps and Brock sighs. 'Look, man. Just do this. Help me out here.' 'You're a dick, dude,' Stephen says, taking another swallow of beer. 'This is totally f~ckin' stupid.' Grubb's face falls. 'You walk out and the debt Brock owes me is not settled, Mr. um. . .' 'Blood,' Stephen answers, rolling his eyes. 'I know that,' Grubb returns. 'I wanted to know your real name.' Stephen gets really close to Grubb and gives him the darkest glare ever. 'REALLY Blood.' Grubb nervously adjusts his tie. 'Right, okay. Oh, and once more, there's no drinking on school grounds, Mr. Blood.' Stephen furrows his brow. 'Come get it, then.' Grubb's face goes pale and he begins to sweat. 'Uh, well, I-er, I guess exceptions can be made.' Brock shakes his head and takes a seat at the bottom bleacher and is joined by Lindsey, Stephen and Stacey. Grubb walks out to the middle of the gym floor and grabs the microphone. 'Ladies and gentlemen, I'd like to introduce to you, my favorite WWF superstars-' 'It's Rush, dumbf~ck, and I’m retired' Stephen mutters, burying his face in his hands. '-Our very own Brock Newbludd and Stephen Blood!' Grubb smiles at everyone as they give a thunderous applause. Brock and Stephen give weak smiles and wave to everyone. 'Thank you, and let's continue with these exciting self defense lessons!' The dorks in the gis continue with their dopey self-defense instruction which looks like it would only be effective if the attacker was told exactly how to attack someone so they could properly counter it with flair and grace. 'Tell me,' Stephen says quietly as he drinks from another can of Killian's. 'What did you do to that fat $hit so you'd wind up being in debt to him? You lose a massively stupid bet?' Brock shakes his head. 'I shoved him inside one of the lockers when we went to high school together.' Stephen shoots Brock a look. 'You mean we're degrading ourselves because you shoved that douchebag inside a LOCKER years ago?' Brock sighs. 'It was right after he chugged a bottle of prune juice for breakfast. Then I broke the handle. He was stuck in the locker for three hours before the custodian was off his hangover enough to cut him out of there. He'd $hit his pants six times.' Stephen has to clamp his hand over his mouth to keep from guffawing loudly and interrupting the demonstration. 'That's f~cking hilarious.' Brock smiles. 'Yeah, I still think so. Anyway, I got busted big-time for it. His parents were talking about pressing all sorts of charges, from assault to reckless endangerment to defamation of character to the soiling of his Dockers. It was a whole mess. Well, it was actually Grubb's idea to just say I owed him big time and he'd come collecting one day. So that's what this is all about.' Stephen wipes his brow. 'That's a load off my mind.' 'Mr. Blood,' one of the instructors calls out. 'Huh?' Stephen responds, shooting his head back up. 'How would you like to assist us with this next demonstration?' he asks. 'I wouldn't,' Stephen answers. 'Oh, come on,' the instructor says with a smile. 'It'll be fun. You know, it might be a good time to see if you're really as tough as you think.' 'OOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!' says everyone in attendance, happy that a challenge is being made. Stephen gives a very cross look and powers the rest of his beer. He removes his jacket and cracks his knuckles and his neck, then walks over as everyone applauds. The instructor offers his hand, which Stephen shakes. 'Stephen Blood, everyone!' he says into the mic, getting another round of applause. 'Okay, now. Stephen, you're going to be the attacker, okay?' Stephen nods. 'Sure, no problem.' 'Great,' the instructor responds. 'Now what I want you to do is just square off against me like you do in the gym or wherever you go to practice. Just like that, good. Okay.' The instructor taps his own solar plexus. 'Attack me here.' Stephen taps his own solar plexus. 'Here?' he asks, making sure he's got it. 'You got it,' the instructor responds. 'Hit me right there.' Stephen shrugs. 'Okay.' He quickly brings his boot up into the instructor's crotch. With a gasp and a shocked look, the instructor slumps to his knees. Stephen follows up with a snap jab to the guys' face, breaking his nose and sending him to his back. 'H-h-hey,' the fallen instructor stutters. 'You w-were supposed to attack my-' Stephen interrupts by bringing his foot up then driving his boot heel down into the instructor's solar plexus. He then grabs the microphone from the stand with a look that inferred that he had done nothing wrong at all. 'Okay, I attacked your solar plexus. Now what?' Another instructor storms over and angrily yanks the microphone away. 'You crazy bastard! You were supposed to attack his solar plexus like he told you to!' Stephen shrugs with a look of mocked confusion. 'But I DID!' 'Yeah, after you cheap-shotted him!' the other instructor retorts. 'He was prepared to defend against an attack to his solar plexus!' Stephen nods. 'No $hit, Sherlock. That's why I kicked the $hit out of him first.' 'You're such a disgrace,' the other instructor hisses. 'You fight dirty. Shoot, it's no wonder you got the boot from PCW.' Stephen's face contorts into a rictus of complete anger and he grabs the suddenly shocked instructor as he tries to leave by the hair, tucks his head under his arm and Stephenflow DDTs him onto the floor, getting a huge cheer from the audience. 'Hey Brock,' Stephen calls out. 'Even though you dragged my ass all the way out here, I'm still willing to share the fun.' Brock grins, eyeing the three remaining instructors. He charges forward and ducks under an attempted punch, countering with his corkscrew uppercut. He sees another right beside him and gives a quick snap superkick. The remaining instructor gives a weak smile to both of them and tries to back away. They both grab him and double-powerbomb him onto one of the stools. The audience is going insane. Grubb runs forward, but Brock shoves him back on his fat ass. Stephen grabs the other microphone from the stand and tosses it to his partner. 'All debts paid, you fat piece of $hit,' Brock says, glaring a hole right through Grubb, who nods weakly. Stephen looks up at the audience on the side of the gym where Stacey and Lindsey are. Stacey smiles at him and mouths the words, 'Go for it.' Stephen smiles and raises the mic to his lips. 'There you go, ladies and gentlemen. Elementary self-defense. Don't telegraph any move and fight like your life depends on it. You don't need some pissclam in his pajamas taking up your precious time. But since you're all here--' '-And not to mention the fact that we are the reason why,' Brock quickly adds before lighting his cigarette. 'That we're going to give you people an exclusive, live, authentic BROOOOOCK NEWBBBLUDD PROMO!' Stephen says in his Michael Buffer voice, which is actually quite good. “Letttt’ss geeet readddddy toooo trashh talllkkk! As soon as the audience dies down, they are riled again with Brock adding, 'InDEED!' Brock cracks another beer and looks around before taking a huge swallow. 'I have been a four-time PCW Tag Team Champion with, ”The New F*cking Legend Stephen Blood' he pauses until the audience pop dies down, 'as well as the PCW Extreme Champion.' Again, he has to pause until the audience settles so he can continue. 'And now I've finally come to where I belong. Rush. Those douchebags in PCW said I was too brutal. Too extreme. Plus they said I had to stop drinking beer before, during and after matches. Well f~ck that. I do what I want, WHEN I want, and at least Boda is smart enough to see that.' 'And that, my friends, is f~cking what?' Stephen asks. 'SAD BUT TRUE!' the audience calls out, giving another huge pop. 'Crossroads,' Stephen continues, taking another gulp of beer, 'the world is entreated to the rise of the man everyone called “The Inno-F*ckin’-Vator”. The man whose style was too much for the delicate palate of Primetime Championship Wrestling. Brock Newbludd sets foot for the first time in an Rush wrestling main event! Damned right!. Now there is no question in my mind that I have my work cut out forin this match. I've seen the tapes, the Match’s are hard-f*ckin-core. But sorry to my fellow wrestlers, you're no Brock Newbludd. I'm not bragging or anything, it's just a damned fact. I have little doubt that we could show the fans the match of the f~cking century out there on that faithful Sunday. But you give and give and give as much as you want. In the end, I'm Last Calling your asses through the canvas. And millions of Rush fans will look on as they realize that what I speak is f~cking undoubtable. Bring everything you possibly can, you bunch of f*cks. It damn sure won't be enough.' The audience raises its collective voice to a deafening roar in homage to the former PCW greats that are currently in their presence. They are already looking forward to Crossroads with great anticipation, as well as future matches within Rush….
