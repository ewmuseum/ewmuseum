Chemical Hazard...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: today Having had wars battled all around us, having millions die at the hands of battle, having ground explode and the rat-a-tat of fire all around us. Having looked death in the face. Having crawled through the trenches, face caked with mud, ears filled with the same, eyes burning because of it all. I look at that, I look at the disgusting nature of the war, I look at the blood spilled, and the bodies strewn the sight before me revolts all over and me. The worst sight that anyone will ever see that anyone could ever dream of or portray in his or her heads, right? Until this day. What you ask? Which is the heinous occurrence that has finally upended the previous most terrible thing in the world, the most disastrous ever. What could it be? Rape? Pillaging? An even more horrendous case of destruction? Nay... A Biohazard promo… Insert Scary Music Here... Dun Dun Dunnnn. What you may be wondering is how something could just hop out at me like this, how something could sever my vision, hearing and intellect for the rest of my life, just by watching and paying attention to a diluted, near ridiculous ramblings from a man who claims to be the exact same thing that everyone else claims to be. What do we have standing in front of us fans? Nothing short of another wishful thinker, another wanna-be, another second to my first, another man who can't be original enough to have his own techniques, so he steals mine. Let me guess, it has worked for you, it has aided you in garnering control of an opponent, garnering control of a promo. Of course it did, I wouldn't originate something that would do anything less. However the breaking point is upon you now, because you are going up against the originator, against the regulator, against the man who invented what everyone does today...against me, against the whole fucking world. It astounds me that you, as one of my loyal followers would miss the cardinal rule number one. Which one would that be? While creating essence for the promo, while enlightening the world for the interview which lies ahead. Which rule is it bitch, which one? Never begin with a lie. You broke it right away, you snapped it in half, and I assume that you most likely do it often, and unfortunately came up against the wrong person in this situation. It has been shown on more then one occasion, that the best time in life is when the teacher is beaten by the student, when the Student becomes his own teacher. Wrong time, because I am in the heart of my life, the thrill of my passions, and the beginning of my immortal reign over every other narrator in the land. Biohazard, I am in the prime of my existence, and do not think that you will topple me in this. Do not think that you will topple me, because the ludicrous and insane never occurs. I await your response, the typical one, the same one that everyone gives when they have been caught in the trap, when they have been slain by the better warrior. However, when you say that, you are breaking rule number one once again. Foolish really, plain foolish. (The door opens, we turn our heads, and Sirius Cage walks in...holding a few papers...) Sirius Cage: No need to get up on my part, Scott. Scott Cage: I wasn't...I was just shifting my ass. Sirius Cage: I see...well Scott, we have some work for you. Scott Cage: We? Sirius Cage: Myself actually. I've received this package from a member within RUSH...it requires your knowledge. Scott Cage: Mine? Sirius Cage: I know it seems odd, but yes...it requires your knowledge. Scott Cage: What is it? Sirius Cage: For legal purposes, I can't say. Scott Cage: Is it about Jason Jameson? Sirius Cage: Loosely Scott, you'd be surprised. Scott Cage: Not really, I asked Steve Carnage not to film his death...for fear that he'd hurt himself. Sirius Cage: Well Scott, like I said...it isn't truly about Jason Jameson. Scott Cage: Well, what the hell is it? Sirius Cage: Here, read this... (Sirius tosses the package to Scott...who opens it up...) Scott Cage: Wow...you mean, they trust me with this? Sirius Cage: Evidently Scott... Scott Cage: Wow, I can't believe it... Sirius Cage: Now I'd suggest you getting right to work on this... Scott Cage: Bah...I'll get to it. Sirius Cage: Scott, if you don't...you'll never remember in time... Scott Cage: I said I'll do it. Sirius Cage: Scott... Scott Cage: WHAT THE HELL? I SAID I’LL GET TO IT! Sirius Cage: All right Scott...you can do it later. Scott Cage: Damn right I can. Sirius Cage: No need for an attitude.Scott Cage: I know…I know. Sirius Cage: Now do you think you know what you want to do for this? Scott Cage: Are you doubting my experience? Sirius Cage: I'm not doubting you Scott.Scott Cage: I think you are. Sirius Cage: I'm not, you have my assurance. Scott Cage: You know what Sirius? Sirius Cage: No…what? Scott Cage: I'm going to show you ... right now...that I can do this when I want. Sirius Cage: What is that supposed to mean? Scott Cage: I'm going to do this, right now! Sirius Cage: All right... Scott Cage: I'LL SHOW YOU! I'LL SHOW YOU RIGHT NOW! (Scott grabs the package, rips it open...and starts to read it, diligently...) Sirius Cage: Very good Scott… Scott Cage: Leave me alone...I'm working... (Sirius smiles...as the camera fades to black...)
