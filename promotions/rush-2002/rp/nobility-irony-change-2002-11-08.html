Nobility, Irony, Change

8 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/08/02 Ricky Starr seats himself on the throne of the corporate world Proud Majestic Noble He deserved it, he damn well deserved it, and he knew it more than anybody else. Nobody could take that away from him. Ricky did old Josh proud and he carried the name that was handed down to him with pride that could shame a lion. With the board, the very core of the Enterprise behind him as his counsel and ministers of his corporate court, he stood as an ascended prince. He was at the top of the mountain King of the Mountain Yet there were a few more things he had to attain. And this time, he stood alone. Like his father, the first corporate Starr, Ricky knew that at the end of the day when it’s all said and done, a Starr will always be a Starr no matter what. Against the corporate world, he stood as a sole prince dead set on making his presence felt, and he did just that. However, he was waging war on more than one front. With success assured and secured on one front, the Enterprise front, he deemed it high time that he shifted his attention to another front. The front where Adrenaline is the fuel for the engines of war Impulses were the decision makers, although it reduced the amount of thought put into a decision, it definitely increased the amount of action. There was, is, and will always be only one law that governs all: Who dares wins Luck is more fickle than ever in this business and Hope gives more than just a sense of direction. Where personas wage war, where charisma takes one places, where being fight savvy reaps benefits and where mere men become great heroes, villains, and champions of their own causes overnight. This is War This is Entertainment This is RUSH It was truly a Crossroad for Ricky Starr. He was one step closer to capturing the Network Title, his division’s highest prize and on top of that, he had a chance to climb one more level to contest for the highest honour one can attain in RUSH Wrestling. The World Title Scott Cage had assessed the situation and knew that beyond the emotionless, racketeering exterior of his, deep inside that turbulent sociopathic core of his, he knows more than anybody else that this was going to be the fight of his life. He was under siege, every man for himself, all with nothing to lose. Scotty has two choices: To walk out bruised, hurt but still cradling that damn belt for one more night or to walk out bruised, hurt and nothing in his hands. Nice prospects? All to lose and nothing to gain, this was the scenario from the opposition’s point of view. Even a sociopath in need of a good shrink knows when the odds are all against him. One lesson in Sun Tzu’s Art of War is that when the opposition is cornered, rest assured, a vicious fight is at hand. A back against the wall and one against the legions Change governs history Good luck Where one truly goes against the world is when the world is tossed into the same pit as oneself. There are no sides with the barriers of race, creed and belief virtually non-existence. Everybody is given one chance and one chance only in this open field, a chance for another chance at some prize, which seems far beyond many who will walk down that lane. This is human irony at its finest. A chance within a chance that many would endure bruises, cuts and even broken bones for. Another piece of irony was the fact that a young entrepreneur by the name of Ricky Starr, an intellectual and a prince of an enterprise, would also be participating in potentially fierce spectacle that would only end when one man is left standing in the middle of that squared circle. Ironically, it also paralleled his scenario in the real world. To be the sole figure left. To be the King of the Mountain Once again, for Ricky Starr, it has come a full circle. Worlds have collided Worlds will collide. One against a myriad of personas One against the world At a crossroad of change Who is history marking out for?
