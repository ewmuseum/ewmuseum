Chapter 001 - The Beginning of The End

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Awhile Ago. Drip. Drip. Drip. What? Drip. Drip. Drip. What’s going on? Where Am I? [Sitting on the ground with his arms tied onto rusty bars was a young man, his face was dirty with dirt and blood. His eyes barely open with large black dots all over his face. His body, is sweaty, and it seems he hasn't token a bath for days. His clothing is ragged and all signs of accessories are gone. He gently moves his hand across the cement floor as he looks up as a loud shrieking noise is heard.] Who is it? [The door pulls open, standing there, wearing a black tuxedo and white collar shirt, with his visibly strong black hair neatly gelled back, looks to be a oriental fellow. His face is empty and his hands are warmthined by black leather gloves. He roughly slides a metal bar across the door and makes his way into the what looks to be a cellar. He smacks the dirty faced man on the left face cheek, giving him a strong grin.] Good Morning, Akinaga. [The man, known as only as Akinaga struggles to free himself but fails.] What do you want from me? Me? I don’t want anything from you, except your finger. The kumicho demands it. [Akinaga continues to struggle with the tightly secured ropes as he shreaks.] Never. I will never give my finger to him! And why not? I will not apologize for something I have not done wrong! But in the eyes of your brothers, your kyodai see that you have done wrong. The oyabun will not listen to your lies, do as you are told or you will suffer the consequences. You thick headed bastard. [The dressed man kicks Akinaga in the stomach and pushes Akinaga into the iron poll support the cellar. Dust collapses from the ceiling and covers both men. Akinaga groans in agony as the other man dusts his jacket.] One more from you Akinaga and I make sure, even if its my last mission, to put you out of your misery. [Akinaga wipes his cheek of blood and spits at the man.] Very well… enjoy this time for your time on earth is close to being finished. [The man swings at Akinaga but misses, Akinaga throws the man into the wall and grabs his pistol and quickly shoots.] BANG! BANG! [We watch as the man faints to the ground, with sweat dripping down his head. Akinaga feels his wrists which were once held by ropes. He throws the ropes and begins to tie the man as he feels in his pockets and takes out a wallet. He puts it in his ragged jean pocket and reloads the gun with a one of the man’s cigarettes in his mouse. He quickly makes his escape.] Even cherry trees tremble in the winds
