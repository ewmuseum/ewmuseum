Date: I always miss the deadline The scene opens on a bar outside of Hartford. Two men are sitting with their backs to the camera, talking softly amidst the cacophony of the other patrons. “And he killed them?” “Yeah. Every one of them. Cut off their heads.” “That sick f*ck.” “Said it was an analogy.” “That guy’s got something loose in his head. We might be next.” “I doubt it. He’s just…eccentric.” “Eccentric is stealing mail so you find out about people’s lives. Eccentric is not cutting the head off of a chicken.” “Five chickens.” “Whatever.” “At least Gates was keeping him sane. Now, now it’s ridiculous. That restaurant is going under.” “Well what do you expect? He said it was a franchise, then he only opened one place. And it was in Iowa!” “It might expand.” “Yeah. I’ll hold my breath.” “Biohazard’s gonna destroy him.” “I dunno. I mean, El Spiffy!’s been messed up by Quo before. But he was never thrown in a river.” “True. But he never focuses.” “Whatever. Perry’s a NTICW crossover, so he automatically blows.” “Also true.” And as long as the other guy is gone from ringside, we’re ok. I mean, we just have to hope he pulls it off, I guess.” “Great. Faith in El Spiffy!. That always pays off.” Behind them, the door to the place swings open. Dr. Dre’s “What’s the Difference?” blasts from outside, prompting silence in the bar. Without turning to see who it was, both emn hang their heads. They knew who it was. Before long, the Pelle Pelle-clad El Spiffy! saunters in next to them. He puts a hand over either man’s shoulder and greets them. “Ey yo.” “Do you ever remember that you wrestle?” “Que?” “You’ve got a match this week.” “Que?” “Against Biohazard.” “Que?” “In a…forget it.” Before long the door opens again. The same music picks up. A metallic creaking can be heard, along with a running motor sound. El Spiffy! turns as Bill Gates walks into the frame. Only it’s not Bill Gates. The figure is dressed like the Microsoft CEO, but looks very stiff. His joints have lines, and he hums a bit while he moves. Oh, and he’s wearing a shirt that says “RoboGates.” That gives it away. The Spanish commentators turn around to see the robot. Juan Carlos Smith nearly jumps out of his chair. “What the f*ck is that?” “Is, is, is…the RoboGates.” “The what?” “The Bill Gates he needs time to do his side project.” “The computer stuff? His empire?” “Si. So he give the Spiffy! RoboGates to use as lackey. RoboGates smiles meekly and slides his glasses up his nose. “I need another drink.” “Yeah, me too.” “Ok, you two alcohol-aholics keep with the fire water. El Spiffy! will take the RoboGates to be fitted for new clothing!” “Goddammit.” El Spiffy! pats both men on the back and heads out, with RoboGates in tow. As the rap music fades, Both announcers order another drink. And we fade.
