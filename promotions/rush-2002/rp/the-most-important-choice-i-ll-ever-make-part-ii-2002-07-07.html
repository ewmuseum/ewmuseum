The Most Important Choice I'll Ever Make - Part II

7 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 07, 2002 Tyler Valentine was offered the chance to find out his true identity. For some, it’d be a no-brainer. Hell, if something happened to you, you’d want to find out who you really are, wouldn’t you? Thought so. Obviously, something’s not right with Tyler, that’s for sure. For one thing, he thinks he’s a peanut butter and jelly sandwich, as has been explained before. Ever since he was beguiled into shelling out money for a so-called “cure” for depression, he’s had quite a few screws loose. If you think about it, though, why would Tyler want to find out who he really is? Logically, it would only cause complications. He’s a rather successful sports entertainer, he’s popular with the crowd, and couldn’t be happier believing that he’s a sandwich. On the other hand, maybe learning who he is could be better for him than he believes. It could bring out a personality trait, perhaps, that makes him even more successful. And, of course, he’d finally figure out his true identity that has been obscured to him by the veil of hypnosis for the past three months now. Now you see why this is an important decision. If we may cut to the chase, we join Tyler – sort of – on a marshmallowy cloud. I say sort of due to the fact that we’re in his subconscious, because of a horribly evil bar of soap that caused him to slip, fall, and knock himself out. Anyway, an angel gave Tyler an ultimatum the last time we saw him. You already know the choice…and now, here’s the answer. Tyler peers up at the angel in the white dress, kneeling on the puffy surface of the cloud. On his face is a look of deep thought as he peruses his choice, weighing his options. “Ugh, what to do, what to do…” Tyler thinks to himself. “What should I do…I don’t know WHO I could be other than a sandwich produced in the finest factory in the world in Orrville, Ohio at the Smucker’s headquarters…but the suspense is killing me…GAH! Fine. I’ll do it.” Valentine breathes deeply. His chest puffs out and then back in as the soft-as-satin angel’s brunette hair sways a bit in the light breeze up in this “Heaven.” Finally, a look of confidence glows on Tyler’s visage, and he looks up at the woman. “I’ll do it,” he says. A relieved smile crosses the face of the angel created by Tyler’s subconscious. She opens her cherry-red lips to speak. “Okay…” she says, sighing a relieved breath of air out of her lips. “Let me explain this to you. When I send you back to your body…you will not remember this at all. You will be freed from your hypnosis…but you will not remember anything about your history this point all the way back to your birth. Are you still sure you want to do this? There’s no turning back…” Valentine heeds the angel’s final warning, but nods confidently nonetheless. “Yes.” The angel smiles as she lifts her silk-clad arms whirling blue balls of energy above her head. As the beams become more potent, a look of worry crosses Tyler face. He quickly tries to interject. “WAIT! Will I lose my wrestling ab…” Before Tyler can finish his question, he finds himself falling… …falling… …falling… …blackness. Seconds later, the picture shows up again from Tyler’s point of view. It’s quite hazy – but Tyler shakes his head, clearing the cobwebs – and our picture. He peers around his usual locker room surroundings, the normal purple and brown paint, the Smucker’s jelly jars everywhere, et cetera, et cetera. But instead of hearing Tyler being ecstatic to be awake again… “Where the fuck am I?” inquires Tyler, in a distinctly different tone of voice. No longer loud, abrasive, and superhero-like as he used to be, but simply a laid-back, normal tone. “Heh, what are you talking about, man? You’re in your locker room…and what are you doing on the floor? You have a nasty bump on your head.” instructs a familiar voice to Tyler. Well, familiar to everyone but him, now. Tyler gazes up, the camera revealing the familiar face of the last NTICW World Champion, Scott Taylor, who has a grin on his face as though he were a child who just counted up all the candy he got after Halloween. “You alright?” inquires Scott. “Umm…yeah, I’m fine,” replies a somewhat groggy Tyler, staring at Scott with a confused look on his face. “Um…if you don’t mind me asking, man, who are you? I’ve never seen you before in my life.” Scott looks at him for a moment, then bursts out in laughter. “Hahaha…playing your tricks again, huh Tyler? You always were the clown back here in the locker room…it’s a shame I won’t get to see you again. I’m off to OSW.” Tyler’s eyes light up in admiration. “WHOA! You’re in OSW? Holy cow, I’ve always wanted to meet someone in OSW and…” Tyler begins. Keep in mind. The last time Tyler DIDN’T think he was a sandwich was back in his stint in the PWA. “Uh…dude, you’ve known me for two months now. Seriously, are you alright? You must’ve hit your head,” inquires Scott again. “Well, whatever bro. Keep in touch…and good luck in RUSH.” Taylor walks out of Valentine’s locker room, leaving him no chance to ask any questions. “RUSH…isn’t that Jeremy Howard’s place…what the hell? I have to talk to Hessian…” Tyler shakes off the rest of his grogginess and stands up finally. He looks around his locker room, taking in the surroundings. “Purple and brown…who the hell painted this?” thinks Tyler to himself. “What the…goddamned jelly jars all over the place, what is this? Ugh…” Valentine cautiously walks around, exploring the room. As he passes by a mirror… “WHAT THE HELL!?!?!” he shouts, grabbing a fistful of hair. “ARE YOU KIDDING ME?!?! PURPLE STREAKS!?!?” Tyler looks in the mirror, his jaw dropped in surprise and anger. His once ash-blonde hair is now an odd shade of brown streaked with purple – once again, the reason why unbeknownst only to him. Anger about the hair aside, Valentine decides to find something to get dressed in. After all, he did just get out of the shower, wearing a purple terry cloth towel. He heads over to a nearby mahogany armoire, searching for some clothing…perhaps a pair of ripped blue jeans, or a white muscle shirt like he’d normally wear. He grabs both of the handles and swings the drawer open… …Only to be met by a sea of purple clothing. “Okay. What the hell is going on here? First some guy pretending to have known me for three months…then me not knowing where the HELL I am…then the hair…and now…and now THIS!” Valentine slams the armoire door shut with authority, obviously frustrated. Keep in mind that Tyler has no idea that his conversation with the angel in his subconscious ever even occurred. The oddly colored dressing room. The unfamiliar wrestler. The jelly jars. The hair. The clothes. None of it. NONE OF IT makes sense to Tyler. Tyler buries his face in his hands, obviously frustrated. He clears his hair – purple and brown, of course – out of his face and stalks off out of the room after hopping into a tacky pair of purple jeans and a brown undershirt. And so Tyler goes…off on the quest for knowledge. The quest for his history. The quest for his life itself. And to do the one thing he knows… Wrestle. TO BE CONTINUED. -fin-
