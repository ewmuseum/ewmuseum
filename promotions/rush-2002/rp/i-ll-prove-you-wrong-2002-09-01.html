I'll Prove you wrong...

1 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/1/02 Tyronne Sharper is a man of few words, when he talks its simply straight to the point. He is a man that wants to be known for his wrestling not for his words. His favorite saying of all time is ‘Actions speak louder then words’ ‘The Never ending journey of Tyronne Sharper has reached a new chapter, this chapter is Rush Wrestling, The chance for me to showcase all of my talents. I am new around this scene and not many people know me’ says the biggest man in RUSH Wrestling Tyronne Sharper. He thinks to himself about his short but yet illustrious wrestling career, he has not been on the scene for too long but yet has made a name for himself where ever he has gone. Rush is without a doubt the biggest place he has been able to showcase my talent. With stars like Chris Matthews, Wes Outlett, and countless others, I need to prove myself everyday. I will one day be mentioned with Matthews in the same sentence. I know I can do it, now the only problem I have is convincing everyone else it. Sharper says ‘When most kids grow up they have hopes and dreams, they dream about what they want to be when they grow up, I never had dreams, I never had aspirations, I just wanted to make it through the next day, and if I was caught dreaming, I would wind up on the street corner, killed in cold blood.’ The things I went through in life were some of the hardest things a man could do, but the thing was I went through them as a kid, I survived I turned out all right, I was beaten up many times when I was a kid, I beat up many people when I was a kid, I never let any tell me what to do, I never let anyone push me around, because of my size no one ever really tried to push me around, the people who did usually had to have their friends help to keep me down. ‘Gang members approached me thinking they were all big and bad, I proved them wrong, at the beginning of my wrestling career, wrestlers approached me thinking they were big and bad, I proved them wrong. Ill prove you wrong” says ‘Picture Perfect’ Tyronne Sharper Proving people wrong is something I have always had to do, when my teachers said Id never amount to anything, I had to prove them wrong, when my coaches said Id never be anything for football or any other sports, I had to prove them wrong. They never let me do what I wanted to do, I turned out fine, I prove them wrong ‘Whoever you put me against Ill destroy them, put me against anyone, put me in any type of match you can think of, Ill beat them. Someone want a street fight, Ill take it to the streets, someone want a Ladder match, Ill climb that ladder and win, whatever you can think of I can compete in. You want some of this, I am right here and I aint moving’
