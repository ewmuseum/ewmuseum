Understanding fruit

9 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-09-02 (Voice of Livewire) Congratulations on your win last week Joe. It was good to finally see you, the hapless perennial loser win a match. It was a tainted win though wasn’t it Joe, my fruity friend? For it wasn’t the one on one victory that you have so been craving. It wasn’t the one on one man to man, former friend to former friend intense battle that you have been dreaming about since the fall-out of Shack-Pack was it? No, it was a curtain-jerking tag-match where we were both lumbered with pathetic tag-partners that neither one of us actually wanted. If you want me in the ring Joe, then all you have to do is ask. As they say, actions speak louder than words. Don’t wish too hard though Joe, because you might just get what you ask for. A small desolate room in Livewire’s Beverly Hills apartment adds the backdrop for what seems to be another one of those tedious rants that Livewire has become famous for during his distinguished wrestling career. As he sets the small video camera on it’s stand a table can be seen just behind him with an assortment of fruit placed upon it. Livewire turns around to look at the fruit laden table and smiles before pressing the red “on” button on the video camera and starting this home video of sorts. Livewire sits down behind the table and puts on his cheesiest grin before talking. “Hiya, gees I haven’t used one of these things since that night in the woods with Fiona. Anyway, hello and welcome to a little history lesson, a history lesson with a difference, a fruity difference.” Livewire takes a small Swiss army knife from out of his combat trousers and gently slices open a watermelon that was on the centre of the table. As Livewire slices open the watermelon he looks up at the video camera and once again delivers that cheesy grin before speaking. “Look at the fruit that is the watermelon my friends. Look at how it is so large, so difficult to penetrate. The watermelon is most probably that largest of all fruit.” Livewire cocks his ear to the camera. “Why is it so large and difficult to penetrate I hear you ask? Well it is because the world of fruit is a lot like the animal kingdom boys and girls. Fruit, just like the wild of the jungle has those who survive and those who don’t. Fortunately for the watermelon here, with it’s tough shell that required a Swiss army knife to cut and a large mass, the watermelon is almost certainly king of the food chain, or should I say fruit chain.” Livewire laughs smugly at his weak attempt at a joke and moves the sliced watermelon to the side of the table. He then moves a pineapple from behind the watermelon and places it in front of him. He rolls the pineapple from side to side, but the pineapples shape makes this very difficult and instead the pineapple just slumps to one side. Causing Livewire to shake his head and stab the pineapple with the tip of the Swiss army knife. “This my friends is a product of laziness and complacency.” Livewire begins as he takes the blade of the Swiss army knife out of the theoretical heart of the pineapple. “The pineapple was once the king of the fruit jungle, the leader in the fruit chain. It used to be powerful, it used to always try and defend itself. With it’s tough pine shell and sweet juice, the pineapple was one of the most respected fruit around. But it stopped caring, it became bored with it’s dominance. While other fruit advanced and became more powerful the pineapple stood still and became weak. Now the watermelon has over-taken it’s throne. It’s sad, but the harsh reality of the fruit jungle is that only the strongest survive, which is why the pineapple is on it’s last stumpy pine legs.” And with that Livewire takes the dripping pineapple and tosses it into a nearby bin with a thud. Livewire wipes the remains of the pineapple juice from off the table with his shirtsleeve and pushes out an orange from the rubble of fruit on the table so that it’s in perfect view of the recording video camera. “This here is the durable fruit of the fruit kingdom”. Livewire grins. “The orange is a lot like a tiger in many ways, as like a tiger an orange is not the biggest but packs a punch. It’s juicy, it’s feisty and has enough sassiness to take care of itself. The orange is a battler like myself and a fruit that takes no prisoners”. Livewire plucks the bright orange from the table and puts it to his cheek, laughing wickedly as he does so. “Aha, so we have found my favourite fruit of the fruit chain. Now to unveil the most despicable fruit of the fruit chain, the repulsive……banana!!!” As Livewire says this he thrusts a small banana onto the centre of the table, causing all the other fruit to seemingly scatter and fall off the table. The small yellow banana comes into focus and two eyes seem to have been drawn onto the banana peel with black marker pen. The bananas life-like face is finished off with a drawn frown, very similar to the frown sported of late by the wrestler with the same name, Joe Bananas. Livewire picks up the banana in his left hand and waves it angrily in the direction of the video camera. “This horrible little specimen is an example of all that is wrong with the fruit kingdom. The banana is a back stabber. A leech that hangs onto courageous fruit like myself….I mean like watermelons and uses them to get ahead. A banana should be avoided at any cost, especially those of Caribbean distinct like the ones from St Lucia or even god forbid….JAMAICA!! Bottom line, if there is one fruit that needs to be taken off the fruit chain it’s the banana.!!!” And with that Livewire once again takes out his dependable Swiss army knife and decimates the banana. Each swipe of the knife causes a swishing noise followed by flying banana peel in the direction of the camera. “I need to purify the fruit kingdom, I need to rid it of hanger-on’s!!” screeches Livewire in his broad English accent as he flicks the last diced piece of banana peel off the table. “Now I have you were I want you banana…die….die…DIE!!!” Livewire clenches both his soup bowl like fists and proceeds to thump the remnants of the banana into yellow mush. Suddenly a voice can be heard in the room as Livewire slowly stops his fists from simultaneously slamming into the squishy banana. “What the hell is going on?” calls out the voice as the room’s door slowly creeps open to show the perplexed figure of Ted Wanstead. As always, Ted is eloquently dressed in a dashing navy blue suit and a pair of dark shades. “Why are you in the basement?” asks Ted as he steps down the basement stairs “And why is there fruit all over the floor?”. Livewire raises his head from the fruity mess that is around him and raises his eyes at his dim-witted agent. “I’m trying to understand fruit” Livewire moans, as if it is a common problem that most people have. “Why?” asks Ted with a blank look on his face. Livewire slips his fruit pulp covered Swiss army knife back into his combat trouser pocket and turns in his chair to face Ted. “Because I’m feuding with Joe Bananas” replies Livewire, seemingly breaking kayfabe. “who?” replies Ted, still with a blank look on his face. Livewire mockingly pretends to bang his head on the table before snarling at Ted. “The Jamaican guy that used to be in Shack-Pack. You know, the one with that peculiar obsession with fruit”. Ted slowly nods his head in reply, giving across the perception that the penny had finally dropped. “Gees, as my agent you’re supposed to know who I’m having altercations with” Livewire adds with venom in an annoyed fashion. Ted puts his hands to his hips and smirks. “Yeah, whatever. Anyway has this little exercise worked? Do you understand fruit better now? Ted asks. Livewire puts his finger to his mouth and thinks for a moment before shrugging his shoulders. “Nah, not really, all I know is that I really want to kick Joe Bananas ass.” And with that Livewire vacates the table and presses the green “off” button on the video recorder. Causing the promo to lose it’s feed.. Three men, one title, who walks away the Network champion? This is Livewire’s fifth shot at the title that has eluded him from day one in RUSH. Will he be fifth time lucky? Be sure to tune into Adrenaline sixteen from the first union centre in Pennsylvania to find out. Take care Livewire-ites. The End
