Transgressions of the Mystique Unlovely

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 'wrapped up' huh? It’s late. Like at night. Actually, if you think about it, there’s a very distinct possibility that it could be early, depending on how you approach that whole midnight rollover concept, whether you consider 2 am late Sunday or early Monday. I guess that’s mostly a personal decision, depending on your upbringing, your familial history, and the frequency at which you find yourselves awake at the hours of the night that many only refer to as “wee.” So, in continuing these initial paragraphs that we in the business like to call “the setting,” we’re nestled safely in that grey area of night/morning hours, somewhere in New Jersey. More specifically, we’re close to the New York border. Even MORE specifically, we’re on a precise section of the Jersey Turnpike. Here on the side of the highway, El Spiffy! fans (both of them) recognize the return of our hero’s gold El Camino, parked on the shoulder of the road. The engine is off, but the lights are on. In the backseat, RoboGates is sitting idly, leaning forward enough into the front that he can “interface” his pointer finger with the cigarette lighter. Outside the car, oblivious to the corporate shill-bot’s activity, the Spanish Announce superteam of Juan Carlos Smith and Julio Diaz Perez O’Neil lean quietly against the side, their backs to to highway. They look out into the slightly-foliated ditch in front of them with their now-trademark looks of defeated apathy, a look only shared by fellow burnt-out babysitters. The reason for their disillusioned stares becomes evident with a rustling in the high grass in front of them. A loud whooping sound echoes in the relatively silent (save traffic) New Jersey night (or morning; see above) air. Before long a figure, matted with dirt and flailing wildly, emerges from the brush and into the view of the commentators. “WHOA!” Into a flickering streetlight the figure stumbles, and the sickly yellow light vindicates our assumptions. The stranger is, in fact, the Spiffster. “You two are not going to believe where the Spiffy! was!” “I’ll bet I can wager a guess,” Juan Carlos counters. “For the past five minutes, El Spiffy! was John Malkovich! I was IN his HEAD!” A collective groan is heard throughout the world. “Try again there, buddy,” Julio shoots back. “Que?” “You didn’t go anywhere, you infantile liability.” “Yeah,” chimes in Juan Carlos Smith. “you’ve been here the whole damn time.” “No, no, El spiffy! was in Hollywood! He was filming-“ “We stopped off at Denny’s, and you ordered a shrimp skillet and a strawberry shake. 45 minutes later, we’re driving down the road, and you start screaming about wonderbras and unicorns until we finally pulled over.” Julio nods in agreement. “So then you proclaimed yourself Lord Alcinoor Buttercup of the kingdom of Faz. You jumped out of the car and ran screaming into the landscape there, announcing that you were going to vanquish the Norwegians. That was about ½ an hour ago.” El Spiffy! stands paused for a moment, before letting his outstretched arms and gaping smile fall, leaving him a broken and confused man. “Was it the strawberries?” “I’ve got good money that says it’s the shrimp.” “Do they even serve shrimp skillets at Denny’s?” “Good question. We should look into that.” El Spiffy! sinks to an Indian-style (Native-American-style!) seated position on the ground, still letting everything sink in. Juan Carlos finally breaks the confused silence, eager to get the hell back in the car. “Now that the weekly psychotic hallucination/fit of lunacy is out of the way, maybe you should look into ,oh, I don’t know, preparing for your match? This is the long awaited Shack Pack/Playaz Inc. showdown. This is something big. Well, relatively big.” “It woulda been big about a month and a half ago.” “Well it’s still sorta big now.” “Sorta.” “Yeah. Well, anyway, we’ve got to get moving. First off, because everyone and their grandmothers expect Jackson and Quo to own you and LW on Thursday. And secondly because it looks like Jackson, for one, it responding to all the crap we’ve been pulling on them lately. He’s out for some sort of revenge.” El Spiffy! slowly rises back to his feet, slightly interested in the new development. Sensing the interest, Juan Carlos continues. “He’s backtracking you a bit. Going to the roots. Near as I can tell he’s gonna try and humiliate us like we did to them. Luckily, he’s already a bit off. He thought you were a part of RSW.” El Spiffy! cocks his head to the side, confused. “You weren’t.” Juan Carlos explains agitatedly. A light bulb goes off over his head. Before he can speak, the commentator moves on. “Anyway, right now he and a friend are on their way to Mexico, to scope out your…’hometown.’” A loud scoff can be heard from the luchadore. “That’s ridiculous! Doesn’t he know that I’m not really fro-“ Julio clears his throat loudly and nods at the unseen camera that’s capturing all of this. El Spiffy! stops mid-sentence and waves. “So, needless to say,” Juan Carlos covers, “we’ve got some work to do. So I suggest we stick to hotel food from now on, and see what we can do about garnering some respect around this dump.” “New Jersey? Or RUSH?” “A little from Column A, a little from column B….” And we fade like Mike Tyson’s career.
