The Super Valentario Bros. - Episode 1 - The Golden NOS Injector of Happiness

17 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 17, 2002 Our scene fades into a sunny day, a metaphorical parade of colors - deep, forest greens and earthy browns making up the grass and trees; vibrant, sky blue and snow white creating a light spattering of marshmallow clouds hovering in the clear sky; and cherry-red cardinals flying beneath the golden sun. Almost fit to be a scene in a beautiful impressionist painting, right? No! This is the land of Mario! Well, sort of. This is the land of the Valentario brothers, Tyler and Waldo - the Crunkstool Kingdom. Speaking of Tyler Valentario, look! There he is! The camera zooms to the left immediately, just in time to see an overweight director picking his nose in the midst of a sea of cameras and lights in a low-cost production studio. Seeing the camera is on him, he gets rather angry! Director: TO THE RIGHT, MORON! Oh, uh, yeah. The camera turns to the right, seeing our HERO EXTRAORDINAIRE, Tyler Valentario! He's decked out in a classic red pair of overalls and blue muscle shirt underneath, along with a pair of white plumbing gloves. Covering his shoulder-length blonde hair is a plain red baseball hat. He's walking along the grass, careful not to step on any of the tiny squirrels and rabbits promenading around. Tyler: (whistling to himself) La dee da, it's a beautiful daaaaaay...Hey, wait a sec! Where's Waldo? Oh, great. Not even 15 lines into the promo and we've already got a cheesy joke. After Tyler peers around for a bit, he notices a faint noise in the distant, obviously the bass of some speakers on very high volume. Tyler: Who on Marioland would play music that loud...? 'Gator boots, wit a pimped out Gucci suit... ain't got no job, but I stay sharp... can't pay my friends, cuz all my money spent... but that's okay, cuz I'm still fly...' Tyler looks to his right, seeing the source of the song - a custom-colored green 2003 Cadillac Escalade EXT, complete with 23-inch Spreewell tires and a neon-green glow coming out from underneath the chassis - bumping some Big Tymers. GET THE (EXPLETIVE) IN THE (EXPLETIVE)ING CAR!!! Waldo Valentario rolls down one of the tinted windows, screaming over the beats 'Still Fly.' Tyler, startled by the voice, quickly sprints over to the other side of the truck, opens one of the green doors, and hops on to one of the leather seats, looking over at his brother, Waldo. Waldo's flossed out in a green Armani dress shirt and slacks, a backwards Chicago White Sox baseball hat on his head instead of a matching green one. Tyler: Uh, hi Waldo! Waldo: Sup bro? I was just (EXPLETIVE)ing rollin', tryin' to (EXPLETIVE)ing find some (EXPLETIVE)ing (EXPLETIVE) to do. Tyler: Um...right. Well, we could always go stomp some...ACK! WATCH OUT! Waldo notices a black car pulled up against the side of the road, and slams on the brakes just in time to avoid hitting the driver - a little walking marshmallow. Waldo: %#*&(*#^&#!!!! The poor startled marshmallow begins to cry as Tyler and Waldo step out of the vehicle, walking over to him. They wait for a few seconds, and nothing happens. Finally, the marshmallow stops crying, and yells something. Uh...SPECIAL EFFECTS! Somewhere in the back, a lazy techie presses a button, and immediately, dark clouds roll in, ominously looming over the three and their cars before rain starts to fall. Tyler: Whoa, whoa, whoa buddy...what's wrong? Waldo: What's wrong? Dawg, I just wanna (EXPLETIVE)ing know who da (EXPLETIVE) this boy is, making me (EXPLETIVE)ing brake like that. The profanity only makes the marshmallow cry more, making the rain pour in harder. Marshmallow: My name's Marsh Mallowbludd...and...and...SOMEONE STOLE MY NOS INJECTOR OF HAPPINESS! On top of that, he slashed my tires! Waldo gasps with horror as Tyler looks on, confused. Waldo: (EXPLETIVE)!!! Someone stole your (EXPLETIVE)ing NOS?! (EXPLETIVE), dawg, you got a tight mother-(EXPLETIVE)ing car! The marshmallow stops crying - as does the rain - and he peers up at Waldo. Mallowbludd: Hey, thanks...it's a 1969 429 Boss Mustang...pretty Jet Black finish, vintage sunburst interior finish...what do you ride? Waldo: That's some tight (EXPLETIVE) right there, dawg...I got a 2003 custom green Caddy, decked out with... Tyler, looking agitated, interrupts. Tyler: GOSH DARNIT! We have to find the man who stole your NOS Injector of Happiness! Now, did you see the man? Get his name? ANYTHING?! Mallowbludd: Um, yeah, sorry...all I remember that he had a long, black ponytail, had a Southern accent... Tyler: Come on, there's tons of people that look like that. Mallowbludd: ...and he was REALLY ugly and had flaming purple pants. Upon hearing that, Tyler and Waldo gasp in horror, a look of pure terror and fear on their faces! Tyler: You mean... Waldo: No (EXPLETIVE)ing way... Both: OUR EVIL BROTHER, SCOTT TAYLORIO!!! Mallowbludd: Um, yeah. Anyway, do you fellas think you could help me find him? With your plumbing power and my magic weather skills, we can stop anyone! Tyler: Yes, we can! Waldo: (shrugs) I'll holla at that. The three hop into the Waldomobile, leaving the 1969 Mustang, sitting on entirely deflated tires, behind, as the go off in search of the evil Scott Taylorio and Mallowbludd's NOS Injector of Happiness... ... ... ...When all of a sudden, a blur comes flying into the windshield of the car and SHATTERS IT COMPLETELY! Waldo: (screaming as he swerves off the side of the road) MOTHER-(EXPLETIVE)ING (LONG STRING OF EXPLETIVES) GOD (EXPLETIVE-A-RAMA)!!!!!!! The car swerves off the side of the asphalt road, coming to a skidding halt on a bed of daisies on the side of the road. Luckily, none of our heroes are hurt - however, when Waldo goes to start the engine... *BANG!!!* A huge noise explodes from under the hood, and Waldo curses as he finds himself unable to start the vehicle. Tyler hops out first, just in time to see the perpetrator - A VILE BULLET CORG! Bullet Corg: Mwahahaha! Take that, scumbags! Tyler: YOU! BULLET CORG! WHY DID YOU DO THAT!? Bullet Corg simply cackles (no, no pinky held to his lip). Suddenly, Waldo steps out of the car, clearly displeased judging by the look on his face and the constant stream of obscenities flowing from his mouth. Waldo: YOU MOTHER-(EXPLETIVE)ING (EXPLETIVE) TELL US WHY THE (EXPLETIVE) YOU (EXPLETIVE)ING DID THAT OR I'LL (EXPLETIVE)ING BUST A GOD-(EXPLETIVE) CAP IN YOUR (EXPLETIVE)ING (EXPLETIVE)!!! Bullet Corg: HAH! Scott Taylorio sent me! He's busy racing the Koopa Krew with your sweet, sweet, NOS Injector of Happiness! Or should I say...NOS INJECTOR OF EVIL! MWAHAHAHA! Mallowbludd's face turns irreversibly somber. Mallowbludd: (choking up) WHY! WHY DO YOU WORK FOR HIM, ANYWAY, MEANIE!? Bullet Corg: Well, Scott works for Nazi Bowser...Nazi Bowser, as you know, has kidnapped sweet little Princess Crunkstool...(grins), and well, they don't call me the Speediest Bullet of Them All for nothing! HAHAHA! I'll see you suckers later! Bullet Corg speeds off after making a sick joke that has to do with the events at OSW Ascension, which I'll probably get yelled at for. NONETHELESS, our heroes stand, stuck in a rut, unsure of what to do next. Suddenly, an idea comes to our main hero's head! Tyler: Alright, guys. We've got to trek up this road and find King Crunkstool. Perhaps he'll be able to lend us a vehicle to go to Taylorio's lair! Mallowbludd: Good idea! Let's go! Waldo: A'ight. The three heroes trek up the road, in search of the majestic King Crunkstool, hoping to recover poor Mallowbludd's NOS Injector of Happiness... TO BE CONTINUED!
