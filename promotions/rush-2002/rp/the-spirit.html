Date: Today Victoria Caprice is standing in front of a RUSH tractor-trailer truck. She is looking damned good. Her hair looks great as it falls about her shoulders. She is wearing a tight black halter top with lace-up along both sides and the neck line. The lace-ups are hot pink and the untied one at the neck line draws ones eyes towards her chest. Her pleather hot pink pants match the lace-ups perfectly. She has a mic in her right hand and her left hand rests comfortably at her side. “Hello Wrestling Fans across America, Canada, Mexico and The US Armed Forces – as you know Rush TV will not be able to air our regular show Adrenaline because we are all preparing for the upcoming PPV. We have prepared a special show for you.” As she speaks, Coach Tex Kelley joins her. He is holding a black microphone with a red sound damper on top. His mic matches hers exactly. Tex is styling today. He is wearing a crisp black double-breasted suit, a red shirt with a white tie. There may even be gel in his hair! His brand new penny loafers make an annoying sound as the clink on the asphalt. He is wearing his huge NATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP ring from his time at Nebraska. The ring is gigantic. It has a red stone surrounded by diamonds. Encrusted across the red stone are diamonds in the shape of the number one. The etchings on the side can not be read from the present camera angle. It is clear, however that the ring goes from the base of his right ring finger to the very next knuckle, it completely covers the lower segment of that finger. The ring “That is right Victoria, Ignition is a short time away!” The wrestler and the interviewer hug in the awkward professional way that co-workers sometimes hug. Victoria releases the hug first, “Yes, everyone I am now joined by Coach Tex Kelley.” “Victoria, I am excited about to see this little piece of history that you and the boys in the truck put together.” The female reporter looks annoyed, “Actually, Coach, I have not explained the whole thing to the audience yet!” Tex looks down at the asphalt after the scolding. “Well, RUSH fans, CTK has been kind enough to offer a full inside look at the past week as he trained for the upcoming PPV. Our cameras met him when he landed in Lincoln, Nebraska. We put together a short montage about the build up to this match and his training.” “Yes, Miss Caprice, they followed me home. They taped me on the drive for Lincoln to Chicago. They even taped my training with Mr. Tojo.” “And fans, we have the tape of the last leg of Tex’s trip from Chicago to Detroit Rock City!” After he gives props to Detroit and looks for a pop, CTK realizes that the cameraman, Victoria and he are the only three people in this parking lot.” “Tex – did this sort of reporting distract you from your goal?” “Nope – nothing could distract me from my dream of getting the RUSH gold around my waist!” He leans back against the truck and takes an inappropriate gaze at the host of this special. “Tex, on behalf of my crew and the RUSH fans, thank you for the ascess that you have given us this week, we hope you enjoy the montage.” We are now watching the video. ** The screen starts black.It flashes with scenes from the start of RUSH.
