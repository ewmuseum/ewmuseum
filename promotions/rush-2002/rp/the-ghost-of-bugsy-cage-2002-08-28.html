The Ghost of Bugsy Cage...

28 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8.28.02 “Opportunities are seldom labeled.”--John Shedd Mediocrity is simply not being good enough. It is not being good enough to do what everyone else does so easily and so well. It is not being able to be successful in something that others can be. It is being laughed at every day, for not only not being the best at something, but being close to the worst, and doing whatever it is very poorly. In what word, I would say it is being a loser, being an outcast, being someone who gets looked over at every chance someone has, and only mentioned when the brunt of a joke or insult. Mediocrity is something that I enjoy to exploit, enjoy to destroy in a sort. What I am, is talent. I am ability, I am aptitude...I am genius. How? Does it not shine through everything I have accomplished through my smarts? Does it not show that my mind games have possibly taken 50% of my battles to the win column? Have I not planned for every opponent perfectly? With all of these factors leading to certain victory week after week and sometimes twice on Sunday, does that not lead you to see the light of day? That I am in fact a genius in a world of mediocre players? I am the Ace in the game of Blackjack, the Stud, the game breaker and the money card. I am what everyone wishes to have, and what so many lack in its entirety. Smarts. Beliefs. Reality. Self-confidence. Assurance. The entire package is one that any person alone, let alone wrestler, would love to be blessed with, work for or whatever else as long as they ended up with those qualities, those traits. However being blessed is not all you can use, work, effort, sweat and tears went into making Scott Cage the superstar that I am, giving the wins that I have under my belt and the chances that I earned. Giving the hype that people have, giving the praise that people have...and the reason why people do everything in there power to try to not respect me. The reason why everyone with any authority whatsoever and any wrestler with half an ounce of talent wishes to topple me. I am enigmatic; I am a sort of phenom without having to be the stereotypical gothic figure. I am a real man: the heart of every real fan beats inside of me, and the dreams of every little fan fills my body. Who else can you say that about? Who else could that possibly be about? Nobody. It isn't that I wish to brag, although I am. It isn't that I wish to boost me higher, although I am and it is impossible. It is that I want to make everyone else look smaller, I want to make everyone else feel bad. I want to make everyone else Mediocre, and truthfully and unfortunately...it worked. Spiffster, who quite frankly, I have nothing against, but by luck of the draw he was the one who was handed the target on his back. Spiff, you have potential and I am quite positive that you will make it in here as Champion Material, and I am sure you will realize after this match that RUSH is a much more stable federation than PURE of FSW ever could be. All in one night, I am going to demoralize you, by defeating you soundly. I am going to lower you from Championship material to a Curtain Jerker in the blink of an eye. Welcome to the Big Leagues, Spiff, where everything is twice the size as you are used to, and twice as good. Are you ready? You better be ready for walking into a world that you are not used to, a world where just being good will leave you fishing for a contract. A world where being talented will only get you so far. A world where Scott Cage is the king, the ruler and the Future itself! Just wait, let the suspense kick in for everyone who cares, and even for those who are trying to keep there eyes away. You may not be familiar with the famous vocabulary that I have created, and if that is the case, here is your free lesson for the day. I am not the Champion for falling into every little opponents traps, or there little facades of trying to make it look like they have a plan, or some mind games. Did I not mention earlier that whether or not you think you can win, I cannot lose? Of course I did, so as for your attempt at a grade three level mind game went, perhaps you should try a bit harder. Not to mention that you somehow have a crazy idea inside of you that you and I are the same. Is it because you see the unlimited potential that I have, the overabundance of talent that I possess and the fact that with every exhale of breath, I have more skill then you could dream of having, and you just want to see yourself as a great wrestler? Just because you wish for something, doesn't make it the truth, it doesn't make it real... Do I enjoy this challenge from Spiff? Not only do I enjoy it, but it is in fact something that will make me feel even a little more confident inside, something that is never bad. You are good. Right? You are Champion Material right? Which means that while putting your shoulders to the mat, I am elevating myself, like I said earlier, over you and your stupid Mexican federation at the same time. Perhaps after this match I won't need to stand on the ladder anymore, my over-inflated head will just keep him afloat at the top. Spiff, bring on your challenge, bring on everything you have, because do I enjoy the Challenge? I relish every single challenge I get, as few and far between as they come, and if you think that it is any other way, then you are only stretching for something else to fill your boring interviews with. It isn't a thought, it is a fact that the only thing I am afraid of, is nothing, and the only thing that worries me is the fact that there is nothing I’m afraid of. Bring forth everything that you have Spiff, because while you may have been top dog everywhere else you have been, you are nothing more then a little puppy. I cannot believe the boringness that is a Spiffy interview. You are sad Spiff, because you are trying to fool the fans, and that is where you have ruined yourself. The fans are everything about wrestling, the fans are who basically pay your checks. You try to fool them, you try to make them look stupid, look like idiots. Guess what...I did not let that happen, and I will not allow that to go down here. The fans are not stupid, the wrestlers are not stupid, I am not stupid and the Higher Ups in charge of this federation are not stupid. They all hear the same dribble that you try to pawn off as an interview, they all see the same slop that you call Gold. Once again Spiff, just because you want it to be true, doesn't automatically make it so. Do not try to fool the fans, do not try to fool me, and most importantly, do not try to fool yourself into believing your own bullshit. It is sad really, just sad. Ready for the instructions of the day, Spiffster? Here we go, follow me closely and you will surely not be able to mess this up. (Our camera fades into the mean streets of New York...actually, we zoom into Central Park...where Scott is laying down on a park bench...) Scott Cage: New York. Such history that this state has. All the battles that have went down here...and all of the ones that will occur afterwards…it's a mind-bender. To sit here, and imagine of what will happen...truly amazing. All of the Gangsters have been here...the Bonnanos, the Gambinos...all of the greats, and not to mention the Crime Families that have originated. Voice: I remember meeting with the Gambinos…Scott Cage: Huh? Voice: Down here in Central Park itself…Scott Cage: Whoa...you're.... Voice: Yes, it’s true… (Scott sits up from the park bench...and rubs his eyes…) Scott Cage: You're... Voice: Dead? Scott Cage: You're... Voice: Bugsy Cage? (Scott rubs his eyes...again...and looks again...) Scott Cage: Grandpa? What are you doing? Bugsy Cage: Well, since I've been dead, I really haven't had much work coming my way...so I just cruise around New York... Scott Cage: But why are you here? Bugsy Cage: To give you pointers... Scott Cage: What pointers can you give me? Bugsy Cage: I can tell you how to win your match at the MCI Center. Scott Cage: You...can? Bugsy Cage: Of course I can... Scott Cage: Hell, didn't you get shot? Bugsy Cage: I was New York’s “Most Infamous Gangster” for over five years... Scott Cage: But you worked for Arnold Rothstein… Bugsy Cage: And on occasion, Lucky Luciano. But...I do have the secret... Scott Cage: All right...all right, lay it on me... Bugsy Cage: Silence...or you shall not be deemed worthy of hearing the magic that is the secret to winning. Scott Cage: All right, all right...I'm sorry. Please tell me the secret... Bugsy Cage: Follow me...and I shall allow you to know of the secret... (Bugsy walks away from the bench...and grabs Scott's hand...as they are transported to the rain forest of Southern Brazil. The monkeys are seen, jumping over the rain forest floor...all the while swarms of red and black army ants move with passion over the floor. We see Scott, grasping a large tree...holding a mushroom that has made it's life attached to the tree, feeding off of it. Bugsy bends down, and grabs a tiny mouse running along the path...and holds it in his hand...) Scott Cage: Oh great Bugsy...please tell me the secret of how I can win my match. I am so exciting, my genitals have retreated into my body... Bugsy Cage: Sit down young one...as I tell you the story...but be careful of the ants. (Scott looks for a place to sit down...and finally just seats himself on the rain forest floor...) Bugsy Cage: Okay now, my young warrior. The secret to winning…is… >>TRANSMISSION FAILED>>TRANSMISSION FAILED >>BACKING UP PROGRAM… >>REBOOTING PROGRAM… >>CHECKING HARD DRIVE… >>DEFRAGMENTING FILES… >>SCANNING FOR VIRUS… >>REBOOT SUCCESSFUL. Scott Cage: So that's it? Bugsy Cage: That is the secret Scott… Scott Cage: That isn't a secret...they teach you that when you're starting out. Bugsy Cage: Then why...why did it take me so long to figure that out? Scott Cage: I don't know...tell me why you got shot by Joey Adonis?Bugsy Cage: Well my grandson...I have taught you everything you need... Scott Cage: You taught me nothing... Bugsy Cage: Fine...I see how it is. Scott Cage: Listen...can I like, go get some ice cream now? Bugsy Cage: You already have the secret... Scott Cage: Secret, yeah, if that's what you want to call it. No, I'm just hungry... Bugsy Cage: So be it...but remember, I shall put a curse on you... (An evil laugh flows through Bugsy Cage...as he fades into nothing...) Scott Cage: What a stiff... (Scott laughs, and starts to look for an ice cream vendor. He bumps into the tree, looks around and realizes he’s still in the Amazon rainforest! The camera fades to black as an evil laugh picks up...)
