CrossRoads Chronicles #7: D-Kay Calyx

13 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8.13.02 “The principles of war could, for brevity, be condensed into a single word: Concentration.”--Basil Liddell Hart I don't really know what it is, and how I do it. I don't really understand how I can keep going, how I can keep pushing myself to do this. I have had this cursed Network Title for months now, I have been ordered to fight for it, and to let the bookers figure it out. Yet somehow I keep going, somehow, and I don't know how, I still fight with every bit of heart that hasn't been broken yet. How? Or perhaps an even better question is Why? Do you all want to know? It is no secret that I have only been a shell of myself since I took this belt from Marvin Harris, it is no secret that while I haven't given up, half of me has not been here. But I do love what I do, and I do love holding onto my Network Championship, and I would kill to be the RUSH World Champion. I promised myself and the fans out there that I would not let you down or disappoint you, for I have done everything in my power to not do those things. Although I have recently realized something that I didn't see before, that I couldn't get a handle on. As much as this is going to make me sound selfish, egotistical and most certainly villainous which I don't have a problem with... I need to win. I need to be on top of the world, or else I haven't done the proper deed to myself, let alone the RUSH fans, as well as the fans around the world. Winning right now, is all that matters, and Sirius has helped me realize that. I thank him greatly for doing so, for without them I may have been lost forever. Knowledge is power. Now I hold all the power. D-Kay Calyx, let me begin with you for you have done something not only ridiculous, but like your character, unoriginal. Look at yourself...a poor gimmick, over-used by everyone and faltered so many times, playing an angle that is perhaps older then you. I hope that you will use stubbornness as an excuse as oppose to idiocy, at least that way you only look a tad mediocre. But now, acting as the literal ass-kisser for Chris Sloboda? Isn't that a bit overdone? What you have done is redundant Calyx, you think you have done something so clever, so smart, but what you have done is put yourself into a greater risk. Quite frankly, I am disgusted with what you have done since you entered the wrestling world. It makes me sick to see that a contender for the Heavyweight Title can't even find himself something that nobody has done, that nobody else is. Calyx, while this may sound complicated to your imbecilic ears, try your hardest to understand. You are a nobody because you decided to be. When you chose who you wanted to be inside of the ring, When D-Kay Calyx stepped inside of the ring for the first time, you could have been something special. You could have been heroic in a sense, be something that nobody else could think of, that nobody else could dream of, be something that nobody else could have. But you stepped into the IRS Faction and chose to be something old, something tired, something that needed to try to fool others into believing he talked to his personalities, and then you chose to be someone to admit in a sense that they were lying. You chose to be a loser Calyx...now live with your decision. (Our camera fades to the streets of downtown Orlando...where Scott, Sirius, and Steve Carnage are walking...looking for something...) Scott Cage: Will you calm down? Sirius Cage: Scott, I warned you two not to do it. Scott Cage: You warned me not to go buy that cocaine...and I didn't. Sirius Cage: But you sent Carnage to do your bidding... Scott Cage: Which you didn't advise against. Steve Carnage: I did good, didn't I Mr. Cage? Scott Cage: What the fuck? You're still here? Steve Carnage: Yes, sir. Scott Cage: Why? Steve Carnage: Well...I uh...thought I was part of the team. Scott Cage: You bought that cocaine, right? Steve Carnage: You bet! Scott Cage: And you got rid of that body, right?Steve Carnage: Definitely.Scott Cage: Exactly...now go wash in some turpentine... Steve Carnage: Then can I tag along? Scott Cage: No. Steve Carnage: …..okay. Scott Cage: Now go, scrub the death off of yourself... Steve Carnage: Yes, Sir. (Carnage turns around, and walks back to his milk truck...sulking...) Sirius Cage: Wasn't that a tad harsh? Scott Cage: What? Sirius Cage: With Carnage... Scott Cage: He’s gay. Now, come on...we have to fly back to Tampa Bay.Sirius Cage: Okay. Should I call a taxi?Scott Cage: No. First we must get a plaque. Sirius Cage: For what?Scott Cage: For Marvin Harris! (Scott turns into a conveniently located engraver store...) Engraver: Hello...can I help you? Scott Cage: Yeah..I'm looking for a plaque. Engraver: Well, you came to the right place...here, care to see what styles I have to offer? Scott Cage: I guess so...I mean, I am paying for quality here. Engraver: Follow me...may I ask what the occasion is for this? (The engraver leads Scott through an aisle, while he fondles the merchandise...) Scott Cage: Oh...it would be for a grave...I suppose. Engraver: Death in the family? Scott Cage: Not quite... Engraver: Well, either way...my condolences. Scott Cage: For who? Engraver: The death... Scott Cage: Oh…oh yeah...really sad. Engraver: How did it happen, if I may ask? Scott Cage: It’s unknown. The man has been missing for months now, so I assume he’s dead... Engraver: Oh my, how tragic... Scott Cage: Yeah...I really felt bad that I drove him to it...well, at least for the first few moments. Engraver: You....you drove him to run away? Scott Cage: Yea...see, I sort of…drove him insane. (The engraver cringes...and points towards a number of plaques...) Engraver: Well, if you see here, we have a fine variety of plaques. We have these nice silver ones...or the classic wooden style... Scott Cage: Well, see...I'm really not looking to spend much, because...well, he didn't really mean much to us... Engraver: Hmm...well, the cheapest we have is this grainy, wooden plague here, for around four hundred dollars. Scott Cage: Wow...four hundred huh? Do you have any used plaques? Engraver: Used? Scott Cage Yea...like...maybe a plaque that you messed up the engraving or something...like, we can flip that over and start again. Engraver: As odd a request as that is...I may just have some errored plaques in the back...let me go check... (The engraver walks towards the back, shaking his head...as Scott smiles at Sirius…) Sirius Cage: Don't look at me with that smile on your face. You know I don't approve of this... Scott Cage: For an evil genius...you're sure showing a lot of heart here... Sirius Cage: The man is presumed dead Scott...and you've yet to give him a proper funeral. (Scott rolls his eyes...as the engraver returns holding a plaque...) Engraver: Well, I found one...but it has a large chip in the right corner... Scott Cage: Cool. Engraver: So, would you like to keep it? Scott Cage: Sure...can we get it engraved today? Engraver: I suppose we can...what would you like to have engraved? Scott Cage: How about this? Here lies Marvin- (Sirius interrupts Scott…which annoys him a tad…) Sirius Cage: Scott...remember, be kind, the man is presumed dead. Scott Cage: I know, I know...all right, are you ready to write this down? Engraver: I'm ready...you can begin... (Scott clears his throat, and begins to speak…) Scott Cage: Here lies Marvin Harris...a great man, a respected man. He gave his life and soul, pouring it all into this fine sport we call wrestling. He accomplished everything he set his heart to..titles, championships, acclaims. He wasn't a family man, as he did his best to focus strictly inside the squared circle. He worked day in, day out, just to be noticed. Many years, many battles, until his hard work and diligence paid off. Blood, sweat, tears...given to the game, the game. But it was not a game, no, it was business. It was life. Marvin Harris’ life. A life full of pain and misery, which paid off in the end. Not truly in the end, but near. Where he accomplished his lifelong dream, and realized it was everything he imagined. That was the dream, and no longer a dream. For Marvin, he was happy, he was pleasant. Happy, tranquil. He was on top of the world...until the depression sat in. Scott Cage...he tried to save him. Tried to bring him out of the deep seeded depression...but it was no use...Harris had went too far, and the dark illusions had overpowered him...here likes Marvin Harris...dead before his time. (The Engraver sheds a tear…) Sirius Cage: Beautiful, Scott... Scott Cage: All right...how long do you think it'd be? Engraver: About five minutes in the back...not long. (The engraver heads to the back...and once again, Scott smiles at Sirius…) Sirius Cage: Scott, you need to stop spending your time with the deceased, and focus on the living. Scott Cage: What? Sirius Cage: You have dealt with Harris a while ago...time to focus on the pay per view… (Scott shakes his head in disgust...as the engraver brings in the plaque...) Engraver: Here you are Mr. Cage...your...'memorial' plaque... Scott Cage: Nice...thanks a lot man...it means so much. Engraver: Yes...well, that will be four hundred dollars. Scott Cage: Sirius...pay the man... (Scott holds his plaque out in front of him like the prodigal son...as Sirius groans and pays the engraver...) Scott Cage: C'mon Sirius...we have to get this to the casket...TO THE CASKETMOBILE! (“Batman –1960’s” music begins plaing as Scott runs out of the store...with Sirius walking slowly behind...)
