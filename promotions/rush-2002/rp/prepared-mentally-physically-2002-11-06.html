Prepared mentally & physically

6 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-06-02 (Voice of Livewire) I know what you are all thinking. I know why you are all sniggering. I can see all your smug smiles. Last week Thursday on Adrenaline I didn’t achieve what I set out to do. Last week on Adrenaline I didn’t beat Kareem Olsen and Scott Cage. Last week on Adrenaline that ghetto goon Dark Haze managed to blindside me and allowed for Scott Cage to cover Kareem Olsen for the pin. Both myself and Cage knew that Olsen was the weak link and that whoever pinned him first would get the win. Cage was lucky, Dark Haze and Allan Novakayne at Ignition will have no such luck. However not everything was doom and gloom last Thursday. No, quite the contrary….things seemed to get seemingly better. I have always admired those with the courage to take on those bigger than them, stronger and more powerful. I have seen many bully their way through RUSH since it’s re-opening such as the Solution and most recently those vile cretins Anti-trust. I respect the good work, fighting the good fight of men like Jonathan Drachen, Tex Kelley and the recently viciously injured Skye Stone. Even though these men have done good work there are two individuals that I admire and respect more than others. Those two men are Christopher Shackleton and El Spiffy, otherwise known as the team Shack Pack. They have combated the ghetto thugs Dark Haze and Statuz Quo over recent weeks and have excelled in giving something other than blood gang signs and other gang warfare snippets to the fans. Shack Pack like myself only want to entertain and only want to take RUSH forward, not backwards as others have done since the re-incarnation of the federation. So what do you get when you take a charismatic Antartican, a Mexican with a love of violence and driving opponents through tables and a good looking Englishman with superior intelligence and mammoth power? You get the most dominant force in the wrestling world today. The future is bright, the future is Shack Pack. The scene pans in showing Livewire’s face under heavy strain. He is leaning back on a weight lifting matted board and is trying to propel a heavy weigh over his head. Standing by his side is his agent and friend Ted Wanstead. Livewire and Ted have not spoken for two whole weeks as Livewire has been diligently preparing mentally and physically for his number one contender to the Network title triple threat match this Sunday in the Palace of auburn hills, Detroit. Ted spots Livewire and makes sure that Livewire is correctly lifting the weights and not doing any damage to himself. Ted is wearing a plain red T-shirt and a pair of casual black jeans that are finished off with black steel toe capped boots. Ted’s greying blonde hair as always is gelled back and his face bears a concerned look. Livewire is bare-chested and is dressed in only a pair of baggy yellow shorts and trainers. Ted lowers down to speak to Livewire as he finishes his repetition of straight arm lifts. “Don’t stop, keep on going…I want another twenty lifts from you” whispers Ted with very little emotion in his voice. Livewire frowns and seems to mumble something under his breath before continuing to lift and further increase the sweat that is now coming from every pore of his body. “Why are you doing this?” asks Livewire, sweat now seeping into his eyes causing him to squint. Ted puts his hands into his jeans and arrogantly strolls around the small gym. The gym is in the basement of the Lively one’s apartment in Beverly hills, Los Angeles and is very modern and stylish. Each piece of exercise machinery has the words “ Personification of cool ” etched on the metal framework. The floor of the gym gleams with a red light shining through the transparent Perspex while the walls are wooden and are glossed with a wax giving it a mahogany sheen. As Ted strolls around the room he places a hand on each apparatus as he speaks. “You ask why am I doing this?” replies Ted, a question annoying being replied with another question. Before Livewire can retort, Ted carries on speaking. “I am doing this for your own good. I am pushing you to your limit to better you. Listen man, I have known you for almost three years. In those three years we have had some good times and we have had some bad times…am I right?” Ted stares at Livewire who meekly nods his head as he continues to lift the weights, his tanned arms flexing muscle. Ted puts his finger to his chin and stops walking. He sits on Livewire’s bench press and continues talking, this time his eyes zeroing in on Livewire as he continues to pump iron. “Well we have had more good times than bad times, but this Sunday will definitely not be a bad time. There is too much at stake dude, I want you mentally and physically to be at the top of your game. Only then will you be able to put those dark days in the NTICW and PCW2 behind you. Wire, I need you focused, determined focused and fully prepared this Sunday for what will be the biggest match of your life.” As Ted finishes his sentence Livewire interrupts, his voice full of frustration. “ When you told me you were coming to see me I though we were going to go out and do what we used to. You know, ogle women in clubs, get drunk, talk about cars?” Livewire’s voice trails off as he struggles to fully extend his arms to push the steel weight from his chest. Ted spots this and helps to lift the weight from Livewire’s torso. Ted places the weight on the bench stand and smiles as Livewire rubs his sore shoulders. “How do you feel now?” asks Ted with intensity, fully expecting Livewire to have benefited from the grueling gym session. Livewire sits up and wipes his sweating brow before grinning at Ted and replying “Sober?”. Ted laughs and playfully hits Livewire across the back of his head. “Ok, take a break for ten minutes and the we’ll start the cardiovascular work with the treadmill” says Ted as he hands Livewire a towel. The camera pans out as Livewire sits on the lifting bench with his head bowed and breathing heavily. Livewire has the chance to cement a future Network title date with either Scott Cage or Marvin Harris with a win at Ignition. Will it be a victorious night for the new member of Shack Pack? Or will Livewire be forced to see Allan Novakayne or Dark Haze take the title shot that he deserves? All and much more will be revealed at RUSH’s first pay-per-view, Ignition. Take care Livewire-ites.
