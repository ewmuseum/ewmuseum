Being a champion

29 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29-08-02 New RUSH era, same old quest for the Network title. The question is, “how good am I? And how bad is G.O.D? So now we have a Genetic Orientated Drone as the Network champion. Well it’s better than having a mercenary like Scott Cage as Network champion. I hated everything about Cage, from his horrid lime green jacket to his hideous bleached blonde hair to his nauseating fake arrogance. Cage symbolised everything that was wrong with RUSH wrestling. He was a parasite that harbored thoughts that he was better than he actually was. Now with the recently departed Playaz Inc, Cassius Deamont, Boda and D-Kay Calyxx, RUSH has ridded itself of the egocentric cancer that threatened to run it into the ground. At Adrenaline 14 from the HSBC arena in Buffalo, it will be time for a new man to step forward and take RUSH forward as a leading federation. The cold damp mist of the hotel room acts as an air conditioner as Livewire and his gorgeous girlfriend Fiona Johnston lie asleep in their double bed. The hotel bedroom is rather spacious and has something of a modern look to it with a laptop computer set-up on the dressing table and a television and DVD player on a desk. Livewire is staying in the Arrian hotel in Buffalo, New York with his girlfriend as he prepares for his Network title match against the monstrous G.O.D in the HSBC arena, which is only a stones throw away. The large Brit continues to fidget and roll around in the bed much to the annoyance of Fiona who tugs on the bed cover to wrap it around her naked skin. Livewire suddenly sits up in the bed and wipes his sweating brow. He glances across at his electronic alarm clock which reads 4.00 am. Livewire grunts in frustration as a trickle of blood rolls down his face from a gaping hole in the side of his head. The cut is just one of the many bruises and scrapes that Livewire has, as a memory of the brutal pay-per-view that was Crossroads. He rolls out of the bed and holds his hand to his head as the furious bleeding continues. Livewire in just his silk black Jean Paul Gautier boxer shorts walks into the hotel rooms en suite bathroom to tend to his wound. The bathroom like the hotel bedroom is also very spacious and modern with an electronic shower and touch sensitive hand drier. Amongst all this five star wizardry was the medicine cupboard that Livewire had entered the bathroom looking for. By now the bleeding from the wide cut on the side of his head had stopped but unfortunately the pain hadn’t. Livewire frantically searched through the medicine cupboard to look for the very aid that would help him in his hour of need. After a few more minutes of throwing various medicines and ointments out of the cupboard Livewire finally found the roll of bandages that he had been looking for. With Livewire been the pedantic perfectionist that he is, he used the cupboards mirror to correctly apply the bandage to his head. As he slowly wrapped the bandage around the cut it was quite evident that Crossroads had been quite a violent affair. Livewire’s once boyish and almost flawless face that had acquired him many female admirers, was now covered in bruises and stitches. The chest area of his body was red and covered in small welts from many Ric Flair style slaps and chops delivered by many of his adversaries during the Crossroads main-event battle royal. Along the side of Livewire’s torso was a long wound that had required stitches and will most likely end up as a scar. Livewire shook his head in anger as he looked in the mirror at these many bruises and scratches. The wound that required stitches next to his torso distressed him the most. How could this blemish possibly disfigure his perfect well honed six-pack? Livewire continued to wrap the bandage around the stinging cut that had previously been bleeding profusely on the side of his head. And as his face started to resemble something from the film the Mummy returns, Livewire started to think, think about his match at Adrenaline 14 on August 29, 2002. His match against the unstoppable machine that they call…G.O.D. (Livewire’s thoughts) Here I am a twenty one year old man a month away from his twenty second birthday, and what have I accomplished? Am I the wrestling superstar that I promised my parents I would be when I left home and threw my academic career out the window? No way, far from it actually. I have of course tasted super stardom, I was in one of the most talented federations in the U.S.A when I got a roster spot in the now defunct PCW2. But what did I do, I threw it all away with my idiotic adolescent carefree attitude. So what did I do then, I cleaned myself up, stopped the partying and got a place on the NTICW roster during the golden era of one Jacob Yoder. And what did I do with this second chance, I threw it back in Yoder’s face. I got complacent, lazy, I fell into a comfort zone and was duly sacked for being nothing more than a nuisance to the federation. While the likes of the Pharaoh, Xavier Kannon and my new boss Rush Mason advanced their rising careers and used the NTICW as a stepping stone to stardom, I instead stood still, sulked, and felt sorry for myself. How could a six foot eight three hundred and ten pound power athlete like myself let this happen? So I got my third chance, a spot on the RUSH roster. One meeting with Jeremy Howard was all it took. A simple firm handshake and I was hooked, on the drug, the adrenaline that was RUSH wrestling, I could certainly feel the RUSH that the federations fans constantly talked about. And what have I accomplished in RUSH? Absolutely nothing, zero, zilch. But that has to change, I want to become a champion. I want to make my parents proud, I want to ram the taunts of all my non-believers down their self-absorbed mouths. How can I do this I hear you ask, simple, by beating a man seen as many as a god and becoming the new evangelical in town. “Honey, are you alright, you’ve been in there for ages” calls a familiar female voice from the bedroom that Livewire has just vacated. Livewire wraps the final piece of bandage around his head and winks back at the reflection of himself in the mirror. A wry smile breaks out on his battered and bruised face. “Yeah, I’m fine Fiona” Livewire yells back as he closes the bathrooms medicine cupboard and strolls back into the hotel bedroom. “I just had to face a few of my demons” Livewire adds as he climbs back into bed. Fiona sits upon her side of the bed and looks back at Livewire a little startled. “Huh?” she replies unsure at what she just heard. “Nothing” mumbles Livewire as he rests his swollen head on the pillow and falls back to sleep. Ok, RUSH wrestling has it’s fifth ownership, lets hope this one lasts well into the foreseeable future. And what place does Livewire have in that future? Will he be the priceless commodity that we all know he can be, or will he once again fail to live up to all the billing that his talent promises to deliver? A large chunk of that question will be answered at adrenaline fourteen. Take care Livewire-ites. The End
