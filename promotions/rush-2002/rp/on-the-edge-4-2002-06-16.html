On The Edge [4]

16 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 16th, 2002 A.D. His eyes grow wider in fear as he realizes what has happened, “Oh fuck” is what he says. Matthews quickly dash’s around the kitchen counter and makes his way over to the obscure open doors. He gets there, pulling them further open he sees her… …..just standing there, looking out at the city. The first rays of sun on her beautiful body seem to rekindle her life as he opens his mouth to speak. “Kathryn” he begins to say…. “What are you doing out here?” * * * * Kathryn still lent against the railing of the balcony, her wavy long dark brown hair flapping in the wind. She was still wearing the same clothes the last time Rush Wrestling viewers and fans a like saw her, black leather pants and a black sleeveless top. Kathryn hadn’t had a chance to change clothes, only wash them as she was locked inside of the hotel room. Standing behind her, the man whom has held her captive for the last week or so, Chris Matthews, he folds his arms as Kathryn leans on the railings, just looking out at the city. He repeated the question he asked her before; “What are you doing out here?” As she lent against the railing, a sigh came out from her lips. A frown cast down on his face, the damsel kept her back turned to Matthews who in return, folded his arms. “I hate repeating myself” he spitefully replied to her silent answer. The frown on his forehead growing deeper. “Meh” Kathryn replied, without a care in the world. She continued looking over the city, tilting her head as he leaned against the wall. “You hate repeating yourself, I hate being locked up in a hotel room for how many days.....?” “I lost track” Matthews replied to her, shrugging his shoulders after the reply. “Been a few....” “Quite” replied Kathryn. Who knew should was actually polite? The Kathryn people saw on Rush television was a different woman to the real one, for starters...she wasn’t so bitchy. He unfolded his arms and scratched his neck; Kathryn still leant on the railing. Her glossy pink painted nails tapping on the steel railing.... Tap....Tap....Tap....Tap...Tap She tapped her nails for a few more seconds, and then back to silence. Quiet on the balcony over New York. Only the cars and people below could break the silence, the people talking and yellow below could break the silence. “You do know....” she started to say to him, his eyebrow’s raising. “....you do know what Cassius is capable of? A smirk spread across his face, was it a confidant smirk or not? Leaning back against the wall, Matthews lets out a loud sigh. This question had been asked of him before, by now he’s grown tired of hearing it. “Yes” is what Matthews replied with, a hint of sarcasm in his tone and also, a hint of spite. “I know Cassius fair-” “You don’t know him at all.” Kathryn said, her voice trailing off at the end. Her palm still cupped over her hands as her gaze still stayed locked on the city buildings and streets. “Kathryn...” he began to say, his voice trailing off, then interrupted by her swift reply. “I want to go” she said, a breeze of wind blowing through the air and through her dark brown hair. “I want to go back to Los Angeles, back with Cassius...back home.” In the short distance behind her, Matthews frowns. He frowns as if all his plans, his “mind games”, or whatever you want to call them, have come undone in front of his eyes. “What if I said no?” Matthews responded, unfolding his arms. Kathryn let out a small, and soft little chuckle after Matthews’s bold question. “Then you’ll be sorry” Kathryn replied to his question, spitefully indeed. Matthews’s eyebrows raised after her spiteful comment, he walked around to her right hand side, but only for her to turn her back to him. “What ever do you mean?” he asked her, eyebrows raised still. Click...Click What’s this clicking sound you wonder? A trigger of a gun. Kathryn had a gun hidden in her pockets, it wasn’t a large gun, just your standard handgun. Matthews’s eyes grew wide as Kathryn turned around, holding the small black weapon. “Put it down...” is what he uttered to her, “....Kathryn, put the damn gun down!” A smirk spread across her face, an evil smirk as Matthews double backed against the wall. With a smirk spread widely across her face, Kathryn waved the gun in front of her face, then at Matthews. “Let me go!” she yelled at him, fire blazing in her eyes. “LET ME GO NOW!!!!!!” ....to be continued....
