Love Letter

7 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-07-2002 Yoshio Akinaga is digging through his letters from the past to his lost love.... He picks up a dusty piece of paper and begins to read... Dear Cough Drops, On that warm sunny day did you know I had my eyes on you? Last night I couldn’t sleep… I just thought of you all night, it was weird. I could still see you with my eyes, I could still hear you with my ears, I could still feel your slapping, I just knew you were here with me somehow. I thought of all the funny times we had. I remember the silly things you did and the silly things you said. I don’t know how I’m going to go on without you anymore… it was a hot and dry night, and I was biting my lip slapping myself just trying to think of you a lil bit less so I could fall asleep. I listened to music… bad idea, just made me think of you more… Grrr I didn’t fall asleep at all… must’ve went to bed at like 3:30 and woke up at 6:30. >_< God…. I’m going to school today against my own will. Your not going to be there… and I will be lonely once again. I won’t have somebody to look for, somebody to smile at, somebody to truly adore. You were my sunlight on those dark days, you were my inspiration when I was being stupid, you were my energy when I was tired. I depended on your smile… I miss you like CRAZY. I’ll be at school just wandering looking for you even though we know you’ve left. I’m going to just cry inside and wish you were here. I'd give up forever to see you for another day, hour, minute… Your presence still is felt… Feelings rush into me when I think of you over in Diamond Bar. I want you to be happy but then again I feel weak with you gone. I feel like part of me has been torn away. I shake my head hoping this was all a dream. I hoped you would be at school today, just down the hall… getting your books, getting ready for Mandarin with evil Mr. Miller but no, your not there anymore… a empty locker greets me. I still sit there in P.E. even though its swimming I would still wait for you so I could wave or smile or something to you… but your not there either. Man.. This is so harsh! My friends look at me funny, they ask me why I look like crap. I give them a fake smile and just tell them I’m tired but inside I’m just torn apart. I know feel me somehow… you’re the closet to heaven I’ll ever be. I didn’t ever want to go home. I just wanted to be there for you. I don’t want to miss you tonight. I just wished everybody leave me alone, I don’t think they would understand my feelings. I can’t take it no more… my face is filled with tears with you gone. You comfort me with your words but I miss you… there’s just a feeling inside that you’re the one and I can’t let you go. You aren’t a little crush… you were that special person I truly loved. I’m going to move on they say but in my heart I will always stay true to you. Maybe I sound stupid but from me, you are worth it. You might not think the same but I wish you were mine… still here… and in arms. You don’t have to turn away… I can always love you until the end of time ^_^ You can run, you can hide but you can't escape my love! I’ll shout to the skies “I LOVE YOU!!!” Haha… looking at Cough Drops, Grape Fruit, Pucca, anything reminds of you. Its scary… I just don’t want to go on anymore alone… I just want to be with you forever. Even the distance doesn’t matter cuz your so perfect. No one makes me feel like you do. You’re my heart girl! Nobody is quite like you Barbara… Don’t go twinkie on me! I’m going to go get ready for school now… my grandmother is nagging me… I might even miss my ride today. Heh oh well? Hope your first day isn’t a bad one, I hope you meet lots of friends. Just ahhhh!!! Don’t forget the guy who sat behind you in 4th period ok? ^_^ MEOW MEOW MEOW!!! Chant with me… I love chickenI love liverMeow Mix Meow MixPlease Deliver!!!! On this dark and cold night do you have your eyes on me? With All My Love, Nerrad Akinaga folds the letter into a neat square and puts it in his pocket.- End -
