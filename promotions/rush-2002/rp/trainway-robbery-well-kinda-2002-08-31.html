Trainway Robbery...well kinda

31 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/31/02 ** The scene fades from black upon a random K-Mart store, probably on the way to Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania the place in which Adrenaline 15 is being held. The camera pans out showing a full frontal view of the parking lot. Cars slowly pull in and out. Mainly containing normal everyday people who are interested in purchasing their things and moving on. The camera zooms in on these people, at first a young mother and her daughter who are carrying groceries out to the car. The camera then turns to an elderly man who has just purchased a fishing pole, obvious by the long fiber glass pole sticking out of the K-Mart plastic bag. The camera than moves to another family, a mother and 4 children. Bags of school cloths and supplies fill the bags. It’s obvious that these people are white trailer trash folks because I mean who else would shop for cloths at K-Mart? My grandma maybe but that’s it~! A Loud train whistle goes off the camera pans to the left showing a local train station next to the K-Mart. The whistle continues as a train slowly pulls in to the station. With a loud hiss the train comes to a halt and the doors open to the passenger carts. The camera zooms in to passenger cart b-12. Normal people with normal things to do all hustle and bustle off the train doing their normal daily activities. Out of the door walks a not so normal person, as in Matt Elia the newly signed RUSH wrestler. In his hand is a normal looking purse probably belonging to an unsuspecting normal person who had the unfortunate honor of sitting next to this wrestler on the train. Behind Matt walks his brother and manager Chris. Chris is utterly disgusted with Matt just shakes his head in hands. Matt continues to walk ahead with Chris behind him, a grin bigger then the CHESHIRE CAT on his face. Matt begins to open the purse and dig through the belongings. He pulls out tissues and old gum wrappers and carelessly tosses them over his shoulder on to the ground. It seems that our former tag champion of the world has now become a double felon, theft and littering. Matt continues to rummage through the purse unbenogsted to the dozens of people staring at him. Matt suddenly jumps back and withdraws his hand quickly, in an action similar to a child touching a hot stove for the first time. He slowly reaches his hand back in there and pulls out a packaged tampon. He disgustedly throws it away. Matt then pulls out the wallet which was obviously deep within the purse. Like a child at Christmas (or Hanukah got to be P.C. these days) his eyes light up. He begins to open up the wallet as the eyes of passers by continue to eye this peculiar man with the peculiar purse in his hands. (Oh yeah! Did I mention that the purse was leopard print? Let me check…nope I didn’t well it’s a leopard print purse which adds even more unique looks) As Matt begins to pocket the cash that was inside the wallet a local police officer on patrol notices the young Elia holding a purse. He slowly begins to make his way over to the man, obviously curious about the situation. Chris who, unlike Matt, is not preoccupied at the moment and notices the officer approaching them. Another loud whistle is heard in the background as the train the brothers arrived on begins to leave. The hissing and the steaming fade away just as the officer arrives nose to nose with Matt and his (leopard) purse. ** Officer- Excuse me sir, would that happen to be your purse? ** The officer, a young man who looks to be in his early 20’s is trying to give an intimidating presence to Matt. Any other normal person it would have worked on but Matt, being dropped on his head numerous times in PCW courtesy of MEANSTREAK AND IVAN isn’t really affected. (Is that good or bad, who knows? But thanks STREAK~!)** Matt- Well officer, um…I think….well what you see here is… **The cop looks very aggravated and pleased in the same sense. He feels that his intimidating factor is working, something that a new cop thrives on. (I know because I just got pulled over by a kid who graduated my high school when I was a freshman, God Bless RENT-A-COP University.) The cop feels that he has Matt blocked into a corner and can now easily make an arrest, most likely his first arrest. ** Officer: Sir, I think I am going to have to put you under arrest for theft, if I am correct to assume that the purse in your hands is not yours, correct? **Matt, the smooth thinker that he is, (thanks STREAK) drops the purse to his feet and raises his hands back, palms out in defense. ** Matt- WHOA~! Hold the phone girlfriend! I don’t think I know what you are talking about. I don’t see a purse anywhere. Officer- Look son lets not play games here just come clean and I will make sure that you’re only slapped with a minor fine. It will be quick and easy. No jail time necessary. Unless you continue to give me a hard time. Matt- Look dad, the pocket book is mine. **Matt begins to realize that a circle of people has surrounded him and the officer. Indiscreetly watching the confrontation, as they wait for their next train. The whistle of the train can be heard in the distance. The officer pays no attention to it and takes a step forward in confrontation. ** Matt- Look man it’s mine~! We are from Europe all the guys that I hang with carry these in my country. ** The officer’s lips curl into a smile. It even seems as if he is holding back a bit of laughter.** Officer- I highly doubt that sir. No please if you would just come with me we can finish this conversation at the station. ** The officer reaches for his hand cuffs and slowly approaches Matt who in return backs up. The whistle is heard again, this time louder and closer. The train will be arriving shortly is blared over the loud speaker.** Matt- Damn I swear I thought that Euro thing would have gotten me off. I wonder why it didn’t. **Chris is than seen next to Matt. ** Chris- Well, since we are from Boston, there is one slight problem about trying to pull off that we are from Europe. **Matt looks bewildered and is totally confused about what is going on. Between the cop and his brother he wishes he was just back at home playing Mario Sunshine on his game cube. Being served jell-o on a plate by the family butler Neil. (Wait is Neil a butler name? Probably not so how about Thaddeus. Yeah Thaddeus is better. And in a dish who eats Jell-O in a plate?) Being served Jell-O in a dish by his butler Thaddeus. With a loud hiss the train pulls into the station. ** Matt- And what is that? Chris- YOU HAVE A THICK BOSTON ACCENT! Matt- Whatever?! ** The cop slowly approaches them and lunges at them but just as he is able to get a hand on Matt the train doors open and the normal people doing their normal things shuffle off scattering the two brothers away from the officer. As the crowd clears the officer is shown standing alone with a look of total bewilderment. ** Officer- This has not been a normal day (Cue laughter for cute irony) **Mean while the camera than shows the two brothers sitting in a train car. They are back on their way to Pittsburgh where Matt will utterly embarrass his opponent Caleb Mcglocklan and who ever the special addition to the roster may be. Just as the camera pans out Matt pulls something out his pocket. The camera zooms in on it and reveals it to be none other than the officer’s police badge. Matt smirks as he tosses it in the air. The camera fades to black The camera then refades back in showing the TV show set of the Martha Stewart show. I know this might be a little weird but consider this part of the RP a lil tip from Matt Elia OOC.** Martha Stewart- Good evening ladies and gentleman. What you just saw was a promo cut by Matt Elia. **Crowd applauds** Martha Stewart- Now I am sure though there are a few unanswered questions about what took place before you eyes just now. Well I am here to clear them up. What was the point of the K-Mart in which you saw in the intro? Well folks Matt Elia has come up with a genius way to get extremely discounted items from you local K-Mart. The wonderful executives at K-Mart created a ingenious way to help their customers out. It’s called self checkout. So the way to a great discount is: 1) Find an item you want. Recently Matt used a Playstation 2. Than go ahead and find a sticker off the shelf of something that costs only 75 cents. Matt used a box of glue on nails in the makeup aisle. Than stick the sticker on top the barcode. 2) Travel to a price check machine located throughout the store and make sure that the new price code is working correctly. 3) Proceed to the self checkout machine and attempt to cautiously check yourself out. Now sometimes they might have a person watching over you. This is when you have a friend cause a distraction, like to help find something or price check something. Once the coast is clear you can go ahead and score your new PS2. If you don’t believe that you can do this just try it one day. And be sure to thank Matt Elia for this house hold tip with Martha Stewart. ** The rush logo flashes on screen and fades to black**
