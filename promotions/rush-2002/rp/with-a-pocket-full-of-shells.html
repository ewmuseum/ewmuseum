With a Pocket Full of Shells...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6.27.2001 'Success is relative: It is what we can make of the mess we have made of things.'-- T.S. Eliot One saying that is a famous one…that a lot of people seem to pay no attention to, even though it certainly is the truth. One saying that you have heard before, that you have probably said before, that you know you should live your life around it. However, for the usage of it now, we do not even need to use the entire saying, the beginning, the introduction, the essence of the famous saying, the part that reels everyone in, and the part that MAKES everyone think. The part that people live by, that people ensure that they partake in this... That one saying goes something like this… 'All Work and No Play...' When hearing that saying, some of you may begin to think, when you hear that saying, it may strike a nerve somewhere in your head, when you hear that saying, it may help you realize the truth of the life that you pretend to live. See, if you are one of the few out there, who live there job every second they are awake, who spend 20 hours a day at the office, who never see the wife and kids, then you are not living a life, you are living a lie. You are living something, that although you may seem like you enjoy it, although you may think you HAVE to do it, although you may think that what you are living IS your life. You are simply living a Death. It's true, hearing those words may make you feel uneasy, and may make you cringe a tad, it may send shivers down your pine, but first let me quickly explain exactly what I mean. If you are living out of your office, if you are working 20 hour days, if you are not seeing your wife and children, then you are not living your life, your life is living you. When you are not living, you are dying, which quite simply means exactly what I just said it did. You are living a Death. All work and no play... is exactly what some people in RUSH are doing. They are living their life at work, letting their work, their 'office' control what they do. Who you ask? Ricky Starr is a perfect example of this. Living exactly how the Fans want him to. Livewire is another perfect example, except he is much farther from the 'play' aspect then Starr. While Starr wins at his work, Livewire suffers in that part. His work lives him, to the limit of how it is said. His work beats, hurts and defeats him. Yes, the parasites attempting to contradict me right away, the poor souls who can't seem to understand, trying to make me understand what they still don't. The question hits hard, but falls down quickly as there is no backbone to it, the question is... What about Scott Cage? Where does HE fall? I am a special case in the wrestling world, and I will explain it all to cease the pounding in all of your heads. So please listen up, and stop trying to pollute the minds of the fans who believe what I say, and trust what I say...to the rest of you, here comes your truth. While I do live my work, my work to me, is not quite work. It is Play. Everything in the ring, I choose to do it, by enjoyment, by amusement, and by myself. I am not told what to do, I am not forced to do something, I enjoy every little bit of my work, which in itself is Play. In my case, the saying should be reversed just a tad, and should possibly be told like this... 'All Play and No Work...' So true, so true. Wrestling is my game, which means two things. It means that when the avid non-wrestler, and resident idiot of RUSH Wrestling is going to be in big, big trouble. I am not only a wrestler, I am the greatest wrestler and I can adapt to anything whether it be a technically flawless opponent, or one that has trouble trying to figure out how to get in the ring such as my current future now. Livewire, you are probably thinking now, that I am being hypocritical in commenting about your style, while I myself pride myself on being the most undeniably high flyer in the world of wrestling. It's true, I am just that, however what you aren't realizing is that while I do try to fly way above everyone else, that doesn't mean that I cannot wrestle. Livewire, I hope you are listening, and if you are, put this in your data bank for future reference. I spent five fucking years after high school learning, watching, practicing, feeling, giving, taking, bleeding, sweating and breathing wrestling. Five fucking years before I even had my first match, and in those five years, I found out everything that I not only needed to know, but what I didn't need to know as well, and some stuff in between there too. Why did I do this? Why didn't I just pussy-foot my way into something that I can't handle like you did? Why didn't I just try to half-ass my way through the best federation in the world? Because I love to win and not only that, but I need to be the best at whatever I do, I fucking crave the title of being The Best. Not only that Livewire, but I crave knowledge, I have always been the person who searches through everything, who finds out everything about anything, and who will pull on both sides of the rope to find out what I don't know in the middle. I have always been someone who will not quit anything, even if I can't find it on the surface, on the tip of the iceburg. I have always been someoen that will dig, and dig, and dig until I find out, what I want to find out. What am I looking for Livewire, what am I digging for all of the time? One word, a word I already said and that you probably don't even know what it means...intelligence. That is why I can adapt to whatever I need to, that is why I am the RUSH Network Champion, and that is why Ricky Starr is nervous to face me. Livewire, this may sound cocky and arrogant, and I am not attempting to be that way in any sense of the word, I am only trying to get the truth across to you. I am not nervous about our upcoming match, I am not concerned at all. For I know exactly what you can do in the ring, as limited as it is, and I am ready for any rare wrestling move that you can find. Why else? Because I know what I can do, and it far exceeds the limits which you have for yourself. You are in trouble, Big Trouble. Plain and simple kid, you can't talk your way out of this. I am sorry if I keep reverting back to the same subject, but you are not like any other wrestler, because any other wrestler would have half the brain to realize that I am exactly what I say I am, and I do, whatever I say I do. It isn't because of anything other then this...I try to be the best at wrestling, I strive because of how I was brought up. Is it because my mother had overdosed on cocaine shortly after Sirius had been born? No. Was it because my father left me to go on the road? No. Is it because I wanted to be better then my father ever could be, better then he could ever dream of? No. It is because my Godfather Silvio Manfredi, taught me the hard way that you are nothing unless you are number one, and ever since that day, ever since the day he stuck that into my brain, I have never settled for anything less then number one. Where am I now? Number one. Ricky Starr, I saw that little interview that you did the other day, the one that you snuck away to do without me knowing. Remember? Well, I wanted to say that I think you did an excellent job on it, and whenever you want to do your own interview, without me, to get your own pot shots in or whatever, go ahead. You have my blessing. While I do very well in the ring, while I think possibly the quickest in the Wrestling World, and while I am extremely intelligent, none of this is work. This is simply how I play. I play to win; I play to myself. I enjoy out thinking opponents, I enjoy out wrestling opponents, I enjoy being able to stand up and say my piece and have only half of the wrestlers in the back understand. I enjoy all of those games, because I am not only good at them, I am the Best at them. Playing games is fun, but winning at those games is even better. My whole life is a game, a game that I try desperately to pass. Within this game, I am slowly approaching the last level, the level where I win the Heavyweight Title and stand with my hand raised above everyone else in this game…because in this game that they can't seem to win... in this game where for them, it's simply…Game Over. (As we fade in, we see that Scott is with his Godfather Silvio Manfredi, both sitting down on a bench outside. The scenery behind them is beautiful and magical all at the same time. The artisticness of the picture is memorable, as the pink sky from the setting sun on the horizon. Just above the flock of trees in the distance you can see the tip of the sun, just trying to get below, just trying to sleep for the evening, giving up all of it's brightness and strength emitting the oranges and the pinks to keep the sky lit. The few clouds that are present, are heightened to a darker color by the opaqueness of the sky. As Scott takes a swig from his bottle of Heinekin, he sits on a bench made out of false wooden logs, he is wearing the same clothes as usual. The white wife-beater, the white shorts, his boots, the silver chains, yet the clothes are not the focus of the attention at the moment. His facial expression is the key focus right now, as his eyes stare hard off at the fading sun, squinting a tad, but seeming to overcome whatever pain and or suffering he is feeling from it. Don Manfredi looks at him from the side, thinking as well, only his thinking revolves back to Scott. He does not intrude nor cause him to break his focus, as one of Scott's 'pet peeves' is interruption, or more specifically, him being interrupted. The Don stares right at Scott'seyes, wondering what he is thinking and why it is taking so long, and then...he blinks.) Don Manfredi: Scott... how are you feeling? Scott Cage: What do you mean? Don Manfredi: Sirius tells me you've been acting strange lately, my boy... Scott Cage: Oh...so now Sirius has that in your head too, huh? Don Manfredi: No, no...it's just that I've noticed you talking to yourself more and more lately... Scott Cage: Is that a bad thing, Godfather? Don Manfredi: No, it's not...I was just making sure you were feeling all right. Scott Cage: I'm fine, damn it...now, can we change the subject? (Scott sets his beer down as Don Manfredi sits beside him...) Don Manfredi: Of course Scott...what do you want to talk about? Scott Cage: Let's talk about you...can we talk about you? Don Manfredi: Of course we can talk about me, what do you want to talk about? Scott Cage: Well, how are you feeling? Don Manfredi Well...I'm feeling fine...now that you mention it... Scott Cage: Well Godfather, it's nice that we had this little heart to heart talk... (Scott grabs his drink and stands up from the couch...) Don Manfredi: Wait, where are you going? Scott Cage: I'm going out... Don Manfredi: Going out? You just got here... Scott Cage: I know, and I was just checking in... (Scott bows down and kisses the hand of Don Manfredi, than slides out of the room...) Don Manfredi: Damn. Sirius Cage: Did you find out what he was up to, Godfather? (Sirius steps out from the kitchen, as Don Manfredi gets off the couch...) Don Manfredi: Sorry Sirius...I just couldn't get it out of him... Sirius Cage: Hmmm....it isn't like him to keep a secret... Don Manfredi: I know, I tried everything to get him to stay... (Don Manfredi slaps Sirius across the back, and walks off...as the camera fades to the blank look on Sirius.)
