The Dream of Desire (Ad 11)

20 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20/7/02 I believe… I dream… I desire… “Ricky Starr…” The words surround a figure standing in the center of the bright light that was seen. The bright light made a shape of an open door, unable to see what is inside. As you draw closer, the figure utters the two words repeatedly. Then…in a flash…the vision is gone. Just as he was about to enter the door, Ricky Starr woke up from the same dream he had for weeks. The dream meant something to him, and although it doesn’t look harmful, it was still a dream that haunted him for several nights. Hardly any of the staff members working in Starr Inc. noticed his condition every morning coming into work without enough sleep. Well, in general, most executives don’t look very active. Ricky made his way to the bathroom in the dark, flicking the light switch on along the way. He washed his face, just for the sake of it. He didn’t look very w. In fact, he looked like he went to hell and back. As he flicked the light switch off, he decided that therapy and psychology would be a suitable answer. “You shouldn’t play with the mortals…” said a cold voice, enough to make any men run. A figure appeared from the dark…looking straight ahead.Suddenly, another figure facing her back to the first, spoke. “And what would you know, sister? Isn’t this my job?”“No…this isn’t your job…and although I am your twin sister, I prefer to be called as what I am.” replied the first, identifying both of them to be of the female gender.“Then what is my job? I am what I am…and you can’t change that…” replied the second female in a lazy tone, as if this argument had been brought up before.“I do not deny the fact of what you are and your abilities to manipulate mortals…but…it isn’t right…” came the reply from the first female.“What is right? Who are you to judge? Besides, isn’t your job bad enough?” said the second female.The first female hesitated, and moved away, whispering her last words, closing the argument…“Just hope that you won’t regret this…”“What is to regret? I always win…” replied the other sister.Suddenly, a wild-eyed wild-haired female whisper under her breath, for she was listening to the whole argument.“Pathetic…” Ricky Starr moves through the busy streets, and finally stopping in front of the huge building with the sign “Starr Inc.” on the front. He enters the front door and silently slips in the elevator, pushing the button. The elevator slowly becomes empty, and soon, he was the only one left. Today was a usual working day, so nothing would’ve changed. When the elevator door opens up to his floor, he quickly got out and walked to his room, greeting a few people on the way. That was the start of work. By the end of the day, he remembered the dream, and made a decision to consult a therapist. Ricky decided not to book one, but just get any old psychologist off the street, that way keeping rumours about him seeing therapist out of the way. He immediately called his secretary.“Ms Jane, tell my father I’ll be returning a little late today…”“Yes Mr Starr” replied the secretary. Ricky then checked the directory and randomly picked one…“Mr Anderson 44 Creer St”Ricky then walked out of his office, and caught a cab, to his destination. When he got there, the place was dark but he could still find his way around. He moved towards the house that said 44, and rang the doorbell. After waiting for a few seconds, a man dressed in a suite answer the door.“May I help you?”“Yes, I’m looking for Mr Anderson…”“Would this be a therapy session?”“Yes…”“Well, please come in…my name is Mr Anderson…”As Ricky enter the house, he saw the certificates hanging on the wall in the hallway. He followed the man into his “office”, and took a seat. The man closed the door and started to ask him questions…“What would this be about?”“I would like to ask you about weird dreams I’m having…”“What kind of dream is this?”“I keep on having the same dream that, well, bothers me…”“Yes…describe to me what dream is this…”“Well, it is when I see this female figure standing in the center of a bright like, which looks like a door. As the vision drew closer, I suddenly wake up. Every single time…”He kept silent for a few minutes then made his way to the bookshelf, and drew out a book.“It says that whenever you see a door, someone is trying to get you into another place…”“Is this true?”“I’m not a dream interpreter, but a psychologist. Personally, I’ll prescribe you sleeping pills that will do the trick.”“Agreed” “This one is persistent…” said one female figure standing in a room full of chairs. Her face is not revealed, just like each of the figures in the room.“Nevertheless, I shall persuade him…”“What makes you so sure sister?” said a figure with wild-hair sitting on a chair.“Because I always win…”“…” A breathing sound came out from the female at the corner. She then walked out of the room.“What’s wrong with her?” said a male from the corner, dressed in a stylish suite.“Nothing brother…just another argument…” said the female standing. “My twin-sister is… “unhappy” with my job…”“You two never got along anyway…” said the wild-hair woman.“Never did, never will…besides, she was always the sad one…” said the male.Suddenly, another male appeared dressed in a long sleeved red shirt and black cargo jeans, in his mid-twenties, looking like a Eurasian, spoke:“Nobody invited me to the family argument?”“I take my previous words back…” replied the male in the corner.“Sister is still more…depressing then me…” said the other male.“Could you prove that?” said the wild-hair woman.“Certainly could…at least I don’t put on a frown every time I claim someone…” replied the male figure.“Agreed…” said the wild-hair woman.“Besides, thought I’m not the oldest, I have a high position, equalizing with bro…” replied the male figure. “Damn…” said the male in the corner.“So what was the argument about?” said the two males at the same time.“It was about another man…” said the wild-hair woman.“Oh…I thought you settled down…”No reply came from the female standing. She walked out of the room. That night, Ricky Starr woke up, even thought he ate sleeping tablets.“Damn…I need stronger tablets…”That morning he went to work like yesterday, except this time, when he walked into his office, he couldn’t help noticing that Ms Jane wasn’t there, and a new secretary, in her mid-twenties was there. He couldn’t take his eyes off her. The secretary turned to him, and looked at him. She then smiled and greeted him.“Good Morning Mr Starr. I’m Ms Jane’s replacement from her leave, my name is Alais”“Nice to meet you…”The second he got into his office, he couldn’t help thinking about her. As soon as he left work, he got out of the office, and tried to find her. Unfortunately, she left. The next morning, it was Sunday. Ricky decided to go for a spin around town, still thinking of Ms Alais. Ricky got out of his car, and decided to walk around town. As he turned the corner, he couldn’t help noticing the woman in front of him. He decided to talk to her.“Excuse me…” said Ricky.As soon as the woman turned towards him, he saw her.“Hi Mr Starr. What a coincidence.”“Yes…what a coincidence…say…would you like to have lunch…”And there goes the pick-up line. Two Nights Later Ricky was asleep with Alais by his side. He had the same dream. As Ricky reached for the door, he walked through it. Suddenly, he was in a room, alone with Alais. Suddenly without thinking…he proposed…“Alais will you…”“No, I can’t…” was her instant reply.“Why not?”“Because…you cannot marry Desire…”“Desire?”“Yes…I am the mortal’s desire…what you desire…”“But…why not? I will give up everything…”“It is no use, you cannot fight desire, and you know I will always win…”“But you are not the most strongest being in the world…”“Oh yes I am…I am the strongest. Because everyone desires…”“Then I shall wager marriage…”“It always starts out like this…”“If you manage to…”“Yes..yes…If I manage to make two people desire love, then…”“How did you know?”“Because that is what they all say…and their pathetic excuse? Desire and Love are not the same…”“Well, I will still wager marriage. If I manage to convince you that desire is not the strongest being, and if I win, will you marry me?”Without hesitation, Desire accepted.“This will all be over by next week…” Ricky thought of ways to overcome Desire, but decided that she is the strongest. Ricky Starr was determined not to be beaten. One day, he met a woman that looked almost like Desire. She said:“Life is a Dick…”“Yes, especially if you are doing the impossible…”“It is not impossible to beat Desire…”“How…”“I am Despair…”“Despair? What is this?”“I am her twin-sister, Despair. I can help you beat Desire, but she will never admit defeat…because she, technically, always wins.”“Then help me, if you must.”“Then we shall meet Destiny…”“Destiny?”“Yes, our older brother. But beware, Destiny is not a man to mess with, and don’t even go near our sister Delirium.”Ricky suddenly smiled…“But Delirium and Destiny always comes together…”“Exactly…” Ricky Starr then woke up into a library, where three figures are looking at him…“So this is the man you brought us?” said a male voice.“Yes…he is the one Desire was playing…” said Despair.“Then we shall see if we can help him…” said the woman.“What’s your name?” said Destiny…“Ricky…Ricky Starr…” Ricky said with a little hesitation…“What is your purpose in coming here?” said Destiny…“To find what is stronger than desire…” responded Ricky.“Why? Desire is strong and always wins…” said Destiny…“Because Desire is not the strongest…” said Ricky.“Let me ask you, what is your purpose in life?” Destiny asked Ricky who responded…“To find what my Destiny is…”“So you know about us…Despair told you…” said Destiny.“Not so fast…how do you know that none of us is stronger than Desire?” asked Delirium.“I don’t, I just know that everyone believes in Desire…” said Ricky.“Wise…this one is going to be hard for Desire. We shall help you, but Desire always wins…” said the three of them. The next night, Ricky went to the location that Destiny told him. In the dark cave, he found a door. He turned the knob, and went in. “Which mortal dares search for me?” said a voice. A guy in mid-twenties, cargo jeans and long sleeve shirt came out.“I am Ricky Starr, and Destiny sent me.” Replied Ricky…“Destiny rarely sends anyone to see me…tell me, what is your problem?” replied the man.“I believe you are Death, and you are the one that could help me defeat Desire…” said Ricky.“Well…you can’t beat Desire…” replied Death.“I will. I made a bet…”“We all know that…”“Then, they said that you were stronger than Desire…”“Yes, I am, but it usually takes Desire to form Death, so you’re wrong…”“Hmm…then how shall I beat Desire?”“You can’t…but there is one more person you could try. He’s always busy, but he could help you…” Ricky Starr walked up the steps and soon approached a big door. He knocked it. A voice shouted:“Go away!”“No, I am here on a purpose…Death sent me…”There was no response, but soon, the door opened. Inside, it was a big chamber with an old man sitting in the middle.“What brings you here, or why did Death sent you?”“He told me that you could help me…””Help you? How can I help a mortal?”“He told me that you could help me beat Desire, Morpheus.”“You cannot beat Desire, for she always wins…”“But you can…”“I may be the Sandman, Morpheus, King of Dreams and the King of this land, but everybody Desires…you desire to beat her…”With that Ricky returned to Desire. Desire had a smile on her face.“You failed?”“Yes and no…”“What?”“I failed because I desired love from you, and I went on an endless journey…of desire. Hoping I might find a way to beat you…”“And you didn’t, because I am the most powerful…”Ricky kept silent, until Desire was about to go, he said:“Wait…”“For what?”“I will tell you why I did not fail. You said that you, Desire, was the most powerful being…but you are not. Everybody mortal desires, but…every mortal dies…”“Yes, every mortal dies, but they desire many things…”“I’m not done. To form desire, one must have a passion, or dream. Morpheus, King of Dreams is the most powerful and you can’t deny it. Everybody, including immortals dream, and so, Dream, Sandman, Morpheus, whatever you call him…is the most powerful being…”With that, Desire kept silent, and soon vanished into thin air. Although Ricky Starr realized he could never marry desire, he knew, Morpheus knew, Death, Destiny, Despair and Delirium knew that Ricky Starr had beaten Desire. For even thought Desire denies it, it has been recorded down in the memory of the immortals as: The Starr Who Beat Desire…
