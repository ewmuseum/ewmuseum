The Road To....Suicide?

6 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 6th, 2002 A.D. The world screws me... The world has maxed out my final breaking point... I don’t know how much longer I can keep going... Living this life of brooding hate... It needs to end... For all our sanity... * * * I sit here, bloody caked all over my face and hands. My body, as it forever will be, is battered and bruised. Scars line my arms and legs, never really heeling from the pain that is my life. But it’s not only my body that is battered and bruised... ....it’s my heart. Even I should of won, I am forced to lose to an Eminem wannabe, all thanks to him. A blonde haired, fan adored young man ruins my life week in, and week out. And I know he enjoys it, my pain.... he loves every second of it. He made me lose to a nobody... another stab in my heart. ANOTHER FUCKING LOSS! BY HIM! ALL HIM! It makes my blood boil, oh yes it does. Why I’m bleeding, you wonder? I punched a glass door in. Great. On the outside, my skin may look cold, but underneath it’s bubbling about my body, simmering. The bloody is so very, very hot, so hot that it’s burnt my heart with each.... vital... pump. I am finished. Tonight is the end of everything I stand for, live for, breath for in my pathetic and unfulfilling life. Time is against me; it’s such a prick. I bet Valentine’s back in his hotel now, laughing it up. Laughing at my misfortunes. He’s probably toasting to my demise with some whore he picked off the corner. Every time, I see that fire in his youthful eyes. Every time I see it when I’m punching, kicking the life out of him. He has that fire in his eyes when I leave the ring stained with my own crimson blood. He is a champion among the people, a man always in total control... while I’m nothing. I had an edge over him, and now it’s gone. All gone... like my life is going to be. I reach into my duffel bag and pull out my Colt Gunsite Pistol. Yes, this is gun. A magazine is quickly loaded into it, the clicks barely making a sound. My hands shake, barely able to hold still the loaded weapon of destruction. It travels up to my chin; just underneath it I hold it still, trigger still left without an owner. Thoughts travel into my head as I’m on the brink of death, I think of how Vanessa’s going to react when she finds my body a caked bloody mess. She would probably be happy... why? Happy to see me freed of this hell I call a life... she always wanted to see me happy. I loved her sister so much; people never realized how much I did love her. She was the sole reason I hadn’t imploded like I am now before.... I don’t know how she did it, either. When I entered the wrestling business, I wanted to keep her away from it – especially in an on camera role. She probably would of liked it, though. To see my wrestling ‘family’. I had hoped she’d give my eulogy, I have one written somewhere... I’d like to hear it. Wait... Wait a fucking second... I am Chris fucking Matthews. I am Tyler Valentine’s beginning and his end. I am everything to RUSH, and Tyler Valentine is nothing. I have a job to do... My job is to destroy Tyler Valentine. When he’s dead and buried we will meet up. Once we meet after death, Tyler knows it’s for the best. For you ignorant fucks that are somehow hearing, what I am saying is this; it’s my duty to put the wounded, sick, tired animal to sleep. The animal is Tyler Valentine... heh, maybe I’ll do something before it happens to me. Before I... Painfully. Die... So here I still stand, the gun held up at my chin. The cold steel of the firearm pressing against my gullet, just waiting to release the raging inferno within. My fingers tremble, scared of what could happen next to me. My bodies scared, worried. My eyes watch the trigger slowly pulling back, fingers and hands shacking. ‘Click.Click.’ With that swift motion, I changed the face of things that would perhaps last forever. Screams echoed, a rush of fear and despair arrived. Screaming, shouting, confusion... all attributes for this situation. My body hits the floor with an almighty thud, handgun slapping against the floor free of my grip.... .... I am dead... or am I? My head suddenly snaps up, the pen and paper in hand. My sweaty thumb leaving an in print in the blue lined paper. I had been writing this...? It felt so real, so alive... so scary. My head shakes a little, eyes looking down at the scribbled markings I had made.... and sighed. ‘How odd’ I croaked to myself quietly.
