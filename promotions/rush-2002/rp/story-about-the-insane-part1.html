Story about the Insane...(part1)

undated

Date: 31/7/01 Why does the world have the insane? This is why… The scene opens up in a room; bright enough to see what is in it, yet the dark night strikes the room to conceal some figures and items. The room shows a few single beds lined up in order, enough for one fully-grown person each and meant for one person each. A few chairs were laid out throughout the room. The room was quiet enough to hear every footstep. Suddenly, the sounds of footsteps were approaching. No, not one person, but two. It was usual to hear people walking around at this time. Suddenly, the door open, and the light from the hallway lit up the room. Two male figures were at the door. One was a frightened person, and the other was a well-dressed man. The well-dressed man whispered something to the frightened man. The frightened figure entered the room shakily and walked to the bed at the end of the room. Suddenly, the door slammed, and the steps of the man walking down the hallway faded. The frightened figure approached the bed, as if it was his own, and finally settled down. He was still awake. Suddenly, a figure sitting on a chair right next to him started speaking.“It is quite dark in here…”The frightened figure tried to ignore it, but soon, he found out the figure sitting on a chair was talking to him.“Yes, quite the dark room…” The man spoke again. The frightened figure then started to speak.“You talking to me?”A silent moment before responding…“Maybe…nowadays nobody cares about who I talk to and more importantly…why I talk…”Suddenly, another figure sleeping on a bed right next to the frightened figure spoke to the frightened guy.“Ignore him…he’s insane…out of his mind. Ignore him if you want to sleep…”There was another silent moment when finally, the guy on the chair whispered to the frightened figure.“I know…everybody thinks I’m insane. So what if I am? Tell me…do you like stories?”The frightened figure hesitate in responding…“Yes…yes I do…”“Good, listen carefully, I’m gonna’ tell you a story. It was…a legend about insane people.”The guy on the chair then started to speak.“Once, there was a man…” The scene opens up in a street where people are rushing home. In that street, there were two people that this story focused on. The first is a young successful businessman that had a close to perfect life. If he were born a little later, he would have been called Ricky Starr; or maybe he was called Ricky Starr, just a difference in time and place. The young successful businessman was rushing home from a hard days work. The other was a poor soul that had “lost” his place in the world. He wore sunglasses and was constantly begging people for money. He was doing his daily routine before returning to the back of the parking lot to sleep. A poor homeless soul constantly yelling bullshit to nobody in particular. People thought he was insane and just ignored him.The scene turns to the first figure, the more successful one out of the two. The figure was rushing home when he crossed paths with the second figure.“Mister, could you spare some change?”The first figure didn’t have time to think and quickly reached for his pocket and grabbed all the coins he could get. He then dumped it on the ground, and rushed back to the same direction. The other figure just stared at him, finally thanking him, and whispering to himself.“Beware…I think you will be visiting me very soon…” A figure rushed through the block of houses, finally stopping in front of a house, nearly slipping. He then confidently walked up to the front door and searched for the keys in his pocket. He finally found the keys and inserted it into the keyhole, opening the door. The house was dark and was naturally clear in the hallway, but messy in the living room. He walked into the living room, looking at how he planned to clean it “Someday”. Suddenly, he just remembered why he had to rush home…his parents were expecting him in the mansion for dinner. Like last year, and every year, his parents expecting him to be late. He was determined to break this “lateness” spell, but it was too late. He quickly rushed up the stairs, walking into the left room and changed, praying that he won’t be late. He then got out of the house and quickly rushed into his car right outside the house. He rarely used his car because his working place was within walking distance and because the city is packed that a car won’t be a good idea. As he drives off, a dark figure was watching him zoom off from behind. The dark figure walked away slowly. At the mansion, two old figures, one male and the other female were sitting on a chair each and both talking to each other. It seems that they were talking about their son.“He’s late again…” said the female figure in a strict but calm tone“Well, we expected this. Don’t worry about the boy…” said the male figure, smoking a cigar.“Yes, but he has been working hard for the last few months…” replied the female figure.“Don’t worry about him…he’s busy all year round.” Said the male figure.Just then, a kid in his teens came into the scene.“Is bro here yet?” said the kid.“No, I’m afraid not.” Said the female figure.“Why, gettin’ hungry son?” said the male figure, taking another puff from his cigar.“Sorta’. I can wait…anyway, where’s sis?” replied the kid.“She’s upstairs. Call her to come down…your brother should be here any minute…” said the female figure.“Heh…here he comes now…” said the male figure, taking a third puff from the cigar before putting it out. A few seconds later, a man was walking into the house greeting everybody. Everyone in the room walks into another room where the food is laid out on the table. When everyone is seated, the old female figure says grace… Back in the streets, an old shabby figure with sunglasses decided that was all the money he could get and finally retire back to his bench at the back of the parking lot. The parking lot wasn’t usually full and the old shabby guy usually gets enough sleep. He recognized everyone near the parking lot, including the mall security guard. As he passes by the security guard, the guard gives him a nod and he just waves back. He has been here for nearly ten years, but never promoted. As the old shabby guy makes his way to the bench, he starts to count the money he made. Not enough to feed a typical man on a regular job, but more than enough to feed him through winter. Yes, he had made a profit from that young lad that had a curse in his family. Not to worry, soon he’ll be rushing back. A more important issue in his mind was where or when would he be able to find a decent occupation. Sometimes, the world was an unfair place you just had to adapt. Looks like the grass is not always greener. The beggar then went to get some old newspaper and wrapped himself comfortably with it, hopefully falling asleep without any soul bugging him. RING…RING…a loud slam was heard, breaking what was the alarm clock. A figure woke up slowly, looking around the room before getting up and making his way to the bathroom. A few minutes later, he appeared downstairs, dressed in a formal business suit, ready to go. He then looked at his watch…eight o’clock…must’ve downed a truckload last night. He decided that being a little late wouldn’t get him fired. He then made his way out the door and down the street. He arrived at an office building and suddenly slowed his pace, making his way into the building and up the lift. He then got into his office, turned his computer on, checked his files, and went to sleep. Meanwhile, at the same time, the beggar got up and started to beg for more money. This was his usual routine, only eating when he desperately needs to and only resting at night. He greeted the day shift mall security guard, walked down the corner, looked up at an office building, and finally decided that this was the street he would be conducting his “job”. At five o’clock the same day, the office worker bumped into the beggar for the second time, giving him all the change he had, and this time taking his time in getting back home. Every time the beggar saw the office worker, he would shake his head, thinking about how long till the tragic event will take place. The routine continued until one day, the father of the office worker called him.“Hey Ricky, would you be free after work to drop by the mansion?”“Let me see…I’ll be a little late.”“Ok, you usually are late anyway…”“What’s the event?”“Nothing, just a past tradition…”“I never heard of past traditions before…”“Yeah, because it’s only celebrated every 25 years.”“Oh, ok. I’ll come for this “historical” lesson…”“Don’t work too hard kid!”“Don’t gamble too much dad!”“I quit last year…although I did made a few bets just recently…”“Just…don’t spend too much…or else I won’t inherit any of your fortune…”“Yes…yes…”“Speaking of gambling, if it is that popular, why don’t you open a casino?”“Why would I do that?”“Two reasons. You win more and you actually made money without our help in your life…”“Who said that I never made money on my own?”“It was not till we were born that mom suggested you get into the stock exchange business…”“Yeah, but I bought the shares…”“And she sold them…remember, it’s not the certificates that count, is the green notes…”“Shut up son.”“Nice talking to ya’”The phone then hung up. At the end of that day, the figure identified as Ricky dropped his change to the beggar and quickly rushed home, praying for the second time not to disappoint his parents. At seven fifteen, he arrived at his parent’s mansion; surprised at the time he took to get there. His mother went out to greet him.“Good evening. So, this time you were only fifteen minutes late.”“I told ya’ I’m gonna’ be early one of these days..”“You’re still not early…catching up…but not early…” “Anyway, what’s this special occasion?”“Some kind of family history. To be honest, this is my first time…”Suddenly, a voice from inside the house called them in. Both figures made their way inside the house. They approached the dinner table, sat at the available seats, and started the dinner like it was a family dinner. Ricky noticed that an extra person was in the house. After the dinner, they moved to the lounge room. The head of the family waited for everyone to be seated, then started speaking.“Welcome to the Starr family tradition. This is a very special occasion to me and certainly will be to all of you. This occasion would only be held every twenty-five years. Some of you might not know what this is about, and since I am now the head of the family, I will describe to you what brought us here today. Two thousand five hundred years ago, our ancestors were said to be cultists. Yes, they were part of a popular cult in the dark times. A few years later, a representative of the ruling empire was sent to destroy this “cult”. He took most of the members from our ancestors’ village, but did not take our ancestors out. Our ancestors quickly turned sides, and claimed they never joined a “cult”. One might say its betrayal. However, it is a good omen for us, because after the incident, our ancestors were blessed with wealth, something rare in those days. And until this day, we still possess this wealth. The least we could do would be to honour them. It may not be something big, but it sure is something we should remember.”Everyone clapped and after a few more drinks, Ricky went home drunk. He didn’t notice the beggar outside of the house, looking straight at him. The next day, Ricky woke up late and the same routine began. However, today was going to be different. This time, the papers were actually delivered to him. He read the front-page… Joe Starr Killed In Alley To Be Continued
