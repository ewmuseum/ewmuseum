Misery, Moonlight

10 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11 - 10 - 2002 AD Insomnia...damn what a bummer!!! Life spins round inside my mind like a top on marble floors... Can't sleep, can't focus on anything... So weary I want to drop, and when I do I find the void inhabited by my own demons clawing and ripping peace from me. The sun rises, the sun sets... I have watched the eternal cycle for days...A lifetime it seems. No one is there to shield me from the voices screaming in my head...no one beside me as I vie for peaceful slumber. I rant and then I cry... And through it all are thoughts of happier times, one perfect moment frozen by sheer will. How can you sleep with such noise... Like a city on fire. It wasn't so very long ago...The air was worth breathing. Now it has turned to sand, filling my lungs with its coarse heat. God, give me back my moonlight... I am lost. Who do I rant to, to whom should my tears be shed? Alone.... In the hours and days and years... It is so hard to bare. But it wasn't always so... For the briefest of times ... Down the lines of my life ... There were some. Did they know? Did they cast me aside for it? No one can say. Not even them. I am Misery and my path is so fucking dark. I stumbled a time or two... Only the moon to shine in distant patches shows me where my feet must go. The moths ... like will-o‘-the-wisps ... I trusted you to guide me... To guide me even still. What a fool I am. Should I be angry... Angry for the way my path began? Mourn in constant black for what was lost so long ago? Cry... For the sunshine I can never have?... Devoured by cruelty and for the sake of a lie and a father who let his demons loose upon a child? Or do I hold my head high in proud defiance of a world who will never understand the depths of my soul... One who is more highly evolved and tormented for it? Your guess is as good as mine, love. I feel all things at once... Hate, pain, hurt, agony, misery, sadness, love, passion, pride. So damned crowded is my heart and so very confused. Shake my head to clear my mind... Fill it with smoke and alcohol... Never better...Will it ever be again? For there is something missing... Or is it that there is too much there to pin it down alone? I search the world for someone to understand, to care, to care for...And thereby stop the pyre burning my sanity to ash. Fleeting happiness... The cruellest thing of all. The fall so hard ... To shatter my soul... If only I hadn't climbed so high. And I miss my moonlight.
