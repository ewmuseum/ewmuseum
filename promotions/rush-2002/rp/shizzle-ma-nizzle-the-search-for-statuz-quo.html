Shizzle ma' Nizzle!: The Search for Statuz Quo!

Date: Huh? H to the izzo!'--Jay-Z Statuz Quo...I'm about to j-to-the-izzo in your mouth if you don't show up... But, this isn't going to be a 'I told you so', it isn't going to be a, 'I'm right, your not', because I am not going to try to put you down for me being correct. For me knowing that something smelled a bit foul from the very beginning, a bit translucent to the blind eye, and a bit fishy to be quite blunt. Quite simply, there is only one question that I need to ask you, just one little question I have, which you might possibly know the answer too, cause I can't seem to find it. What else did you all expect to happen? You didn't expect that Statuz Quo would find away to avoid me? You didn't expect that I would defeat Rich Cannon? You didn't expect that through Hell and High water, the High Exec's would still try to keep me in the Network Title Division? Although at the end of every thunder storm there is a spot of light, so you never know... Now, with only the future to look forward to and the present to run far, far away from, we stand on the brink of a Tag Team Match. Rich Cannon, the man who seems to not want me to ever make it above the Network Division, the man who seems to believe up in the jelly mind that he has, that he somehow defeated me before, something that everyone out there knows never happened. The man who seems to be very lucky to still have his arms intact, the luck, which will soon run out. Vulcan Flare? A man who just recently decided it would be appropriate to aid Cannon in the attempt to disembody myself. A man who has been in RUSH for merely weeks, a man who possibly is a little green with envy over how quickly I have ascended to be a Champion. Perhaps? Seems logical to me. How do I feel about still being in the Network Division? The only answer that comes to my mind is this...Am I upset? Of course, every time you are denied a challenge it pains you, and it is certainly paining me right now. However, not only was I denied my challenge, I seem to have another win under my column, which is something that pleases me to no end, something that makes me feel a tad homicidal, yet something that I can still keep under control. So, have I been defeated physically? No. Have I been defeated, mentally? Unfortunately, yes. Have I been beaten? Not even close. No more will I allow this to happen, no more will I allow this to continue, no more will I allow this to occur. I can see how some would think that, and I can see how some would feel that Rich Cannon has my number, and to them I have one question to ask. How can one have the upper hand, when one has already lost? Does that not show apprehensiveness towards the man that he supposedly, 'has the upper hand over'? It certainly does, and that is exactly how I feel. Rich Cannon holds no upper hand, because he cannot strike me face to face, he cannot attack me man to man. Quite frankly everything that Cannon has done, has painted the proverbial yellow stripe down his back, and is wearing it as proudly as he can. Cannon, you disgust me, you make me sick to my stomach, simply because you are living in a man's world, you are playing in a man's world, you are reigning in a man's world, yet you are as far from a man as I have ever seen. You are ridiculous in the sense that you play yourself off to be a Real Champion and everything to that extent, yet you need to seek refuge because you know that the truth is, you are no match for me. All of your self-praising, all of your self-influencing, all of the LIES that you have yourself believing will not work on me. For I am far from a moronic opponent that you are used to, in fact, I am perhaps the only person that not only rivals your intelligence, but also surpasses it with great ease. The New Network Champion? Far from it Cannon, because every time you use your desperate attempts against me, you fall farther and farther down... Now where does Ricky Starr land in this picture? I don't know. This is just another ploy on behalf of Rich Cannon by Boda, trying to finish me off, and I await this next attempt. This next message I pass on to Boda, to Jeremy Howard, to Rich Cannon, to Statuz Quo, to everyone. You will never stop me, you will never kill me, you will never be able to control me if I don't feel like being controlled. You can't stop me if I don't feel like being stopped, and that is not some gimmick, that is not some angle, that is not some way to make people believe that I am something that I'm not. That is the truth, the only thing I promised once signing with RUSH and the one thing that I will deliver to everyone that feels the need to stand up to me. I am as real as the pain that you feel in the ring, as real as your aspirations of being the best, and bluntly, I am as real as I am. If you want to try to have me run through your ploys, and give me a partner that I don't know, or need, then you do just that, but listen here and listen well. You can knock me down, but I will bounce back up. You can try to bury me, but I dig myself free. You can try to end me, but I will never stop fighting…ever. I see that the future is clear to me, and that the future is me. That on Thursday in front of the billions and billions or fans, I will once again defeat the odds and become a Champion again. I see that I am a threat to everyone here, including Deamont and Quo. I see that while I interview myself here, that others will realize something. I have had enough of playtime, and all the cockiness, all the arrogance, all the conceitedness. It is all well deserved, and the fact that I am convinced that this Thursday, I will take no nonsense from the head clown known as Rich Cannon or his puppet friends Vulcan Flare and Ricky Starr. Keep that in mind boys, because you can keep having all three of you against me and I will eventually overcome, not because I chose to, but because I HAVE to. It simply means that I have changed, however all for the better. I have realized things that I hadn't realized before, that I seemed to let elude me, and that is something that I am glad I have rectified. I have seen some things, that I hadn't seen before, that I was blind too, which I now can see, which I now have rectified. I have heard of some things, some things that I may not like, but I have learned to open my ears up more, open myself up more to understand everything that can possibly happen to me, and now that I realize it, truthfully, I am far more dangerous then I was before. Am I the same? I don't know. Am I different? I couldn't tell you? Am I better? I certainly am. With every second breathing it is a learning experience, and with everything I learn...it puts you all out at a greater risk. BE aware that I am dangerous, because I am like a trapped animal who will survive anyway possible. Will you Cannon? Will you? (The cameraman is going to begin this by opening up the floor for the men who seem to be fighting everyone these days: Sirius Cage and his personal bodyguards, Leo Hassler and Lars DeVille. As the camera fades back out, we can see that the trio are walking the streets of the rundown part of Atlanta. The stars are shining bright outside, and the only sound that you can hear are the occasional Ebonics language spoken by the natives of these slums. As we look at Sirius, his hair has changed from the past bed-head motif that he had been sporting for the past couple of days, and is now back to the more pleasant to the eye which makes it look even crazier then before. The bodyguards are wearing ordinary suits, each of them putting an arm in their coats in case of any danger. Danger isn't always the greatest thing to be consumed by, but it certainly pushes you in one direction. That direction is currently headed toward an interview of EPIC proportions.) Sirius Cage: Disgusting... Leo Hassler: Yeah, and look at all the poor people. (Sirius sighs, and pushes the bodyguards on...) Sirius Cage: Lars, do you know this region? Lars DeVille: This would be the Ghetto section of Atlanta. (Leo and Lars look at one another...and nod...) Leo Hassler: Are you thinking the same as I right now, Lars? Lars DeVille: Yeah...I could go for an apple martini right now... Sirius Cage: I second that motion… (The Trio walks to a little cove...) Sirius Cage: Hello...I'd like to get a drink… Some Ghetto Bartender: ...... Sirius Cage: Hello? Some Ghetto Bartender: ...... Sirius Cage: Are you alive? C'mon...answer me. Some Ghetto Bartender: ...... Lars DeVille: Uh, Boss? Maybe I should do the talking here… Some Ghetto Bartender: Dis here nig-(censored) iz what I’m talkin’ ‘bout cracka! (Sirius looks dumbfounded, as Leo pushes forward to the bartender…) Leo Hassler: Yeah…okay. Me...want…drink. Some Ghetto Bartender: ...... Leo Hassler: Lars, could you handle this? Lars DeVille: Yeah, all right. Let’s see, I haven’t really been in the Ghetto in a while, but…let’s see…let me try…Oh fo’ sho! Some Ghetto Bartender: Finally, a brother iz in da shizzle! (After sharing a high-five, the Ghetto Bartender leads the three inside, and sits them down at a table...) Sirius Cage: That was all I had to say, Lars? “Oh fo’ sho!”? Lars DeVille: Yep. Easy, right? (Leo squashes a rather large cockroach on the table, as a waitress walks up...) Ghetto Waitress: Uhhh…what da fock y’all crackas want now? Leo Hassler: What'd she say? Lars DeVille: She wants to take your order. Leo Hassler: Oh... tell her we need to see the bar list first. Lars DeVille: Okay…ummm. Bitch! Gimmie some of dat dare bar lists, bling bling! (The waitress nods and walks back to the back, kicking the barking dog as she goes...) Lars DeVille: Man, I am SUCH a token black guy…Leo Hassler: Well, this is a nice little family restaurant, don't you think Boss? Sirius Cage: Aside from the pile of handguns in the corner....I'm still trying to debate whether this is a restaurant or not. (A loud thud is made, as the little waitress slams the drink lists on the table.) Ghetto Waitress: Any other shizzle nizzle ya cracka friends want to fo’ hizzle? Sirius Cage: Excuse me? Leo Hassler: All right...well, I'll have an apple martini...and a burrito. Lars DeVille: Dis here cracka’ wanna martini wit’ an’ apple wit’ a’ burrito…bling bling! Little Ghetto Waitress: And what da’ shizzle nizzle ‘bout YOU…cracka?! Lars DeVille: Get da’ fock outta here, bitch! (The woman returns to her notepad, than turns back to the kitchen...) Leo Hassler: She seems nice. Lars DeVille: Stupid whore. She called me a cracker. Leo Hassler: How do you know? Lars DeVille: You forget that I know the lingo here, Leo… Leo Hassler: You're just overly conscious. Lars DeVille: And you're one to talk? Leo Hassler: What's that supposed to mean? Lars DeVille: You're underly conscious. Leo Hassler: What the hell does that mean? Lars DeVille: If you were intelligent, you'd know. Leo Hassler: Oh, so now I'm dumb? Lars DeVille: Now? Leo Hassler: Oh...it's on...you braid-headed Oreo! (Leo leaps up out of his chair...and pulls out a weapon...) Lars DeVille: A spoon? Pathetic!Sirius Cage: ENOUGH! Lars DeVille: Huh? What is it, Boss? Sirius Cage: Look…over there. (The bodyguards stand up and squint, but see nothing of importance…) Leo Hassler: I don’t see anything, Boss.Sirius Cage: Look! Over to the left of the man with the gold teeth! Lars DeVille: It’s an old lady, Boss.Sirius Cage: Not just any old lady… (Sirius digs into his pocket...and places a photo on the table...) Lars DeVille: Is that a picture of Statuz Quo…and his mother?Leo Hassler: Yeah, and that lady in the corner over there is Statuz Quo’s mother!Sirius Cage: Exactly. Now we can confront her and ask her about the whereabouts of Statuz Quo!Lars DeVille: Not exactly a good idea, Boss. If you look carefully, there’s about ten other thugs trying to pimp out Mrs. Quo… Sirius Cage: I see...well Lars, since you’re black, you get to confront her. Lars DeVille: Huh? No way, Boss… Sirius Cage: Why not? Lars DeVille: Well, first, I don’t feel like blending in with these…thugs here. Leo Hassler: Well Lars, the way I see it, there is only one thing you can do. Lars DeVille: What’s that? Leo Hassler: You have to pretend to be her son. Lars DeVille: WHAT? (Before Sirius can speak...the ghetto waitress returns and puts drinks on the table…) Sirius Cage: We'd like to see the old lady in the corner. Little Ghetto Waitress: .....? Sirius Cage: Lars, translate please. Lars DeVille: Yo bitch! Us homeboys wanna see dat dere bitch in da corna! Ghetto Waitress: Damn, nigg-(censored)! Da fock I do it but ya' gotta tip the shizzle if ta whizzle what da pizzle jizzle! (The waitress walks over the Statuz Quo's mother...and Leo starts chuckling…) Little Ghetto Waitress: Yo nigg-(censored)! (Everyone turns around to see the little waitress standing next to Statuz Quo's mom…) Lars DeVille: Uh…mom? Mrs. Quo: Statuz? Lars DeVille: Yeah…it's me…Statuz. (Lars stands up...and hugs Mrs. Quo…) Sirius Cage: Ah, it is nice to see families unite again. Leo Hassler: So...'Statuz', have you told your mother that you're an Oreo? Lars DeVille: MOTHERFUCKER! I AM NOT AN OREO! Mrs. Quo: Oreo? Leo Hassler: Uh...never mind? (The camera fades to black, as all we can hear are the raised, Ebonics yellings from Statuz Quo's mother...)
