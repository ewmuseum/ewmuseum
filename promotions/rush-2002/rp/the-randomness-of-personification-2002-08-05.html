The Randomness of Personification

5 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 5th, 2002 A.D. 2 days before Adrenaline..... I had slipped into a state of unconsciousness after the several blackjack shots from Cassius Deamont last week. Blood was spilled as I laid flat on my face in the middle of the cold ring, still breathing and awake. Not just a little blood like people see on TV all the time, this was real and there was lots of it. Blading is common in today’s wrestling; I’ve done it loads of times. The pain I felt from those blackjack shots. Incredible. My legs and arms were so weak then, gruelling match destroying my limb’s power. As I laid in the hospital, I had a chance to do a lot of thinking. About my options and what I could do....what I could do fight back at Deamont. No such luck really, while I was under the deep blankness of unconsciousness I just...slept. * * * I collected my things after being discharged from the hospital. Wrapped around my forehead was a large white bandage, a small faint red stain appeared on the middle, blood still drying. Waiting for me outside, my own car parked, was my friend Tyler Valentine. Tyler stood there, wearing his typical clothing, but his usual smile was gone as he shook his head. Why did he do that? I can’t even remember why I’m here. Damn, why was I in the hospital? I shook my head as I thought these questions in my mind; not being able to answer them myself always brought me down. I shrugged my shoulders as Tyler wearily watched me kick my ‘bag’. “So” I began to say, scratching the back of neck. The hair on my neck spiking up as it was exposed to the sun. “Can you tell me exactly why I was put in the hospital?” As I asked Tyler this question, I heard him scoff like he thought I was putting on an act. I wasn’t acting, not at all. I’m a terrible actor. * * * Personify Tyler Valentine Fucking bullshit he doesn’t know what happens, he just wants sympathy from me. He isn’t getting it. There’s no way that damned Blackjack could cause amnesia. Chris may have got a lot of attention in hospital from the staff or anyone inside the damned place. It was like he was a celebrity donating to charity.....damn why am I thinking like this about my best friend? “Well” I began to say, trying to not sound like an ass. “you basically had your ass handed to you by Deamont, Boda, Cannon and Calyx.” I would of smirked, but Chris’s face fell when I told him that. He frowned, and then quickly cringed when the deep scare on his forehead folded over itself. It had to hurt him still, though, no matter how much I thought he was a big baby about it, it would of given him a killer migraine. Chris’s response made me laugh, though. “Well that’s just fucked up.” * * * Personify Chris Matthews I looked at Tyler kind of funny. His expression usually was one of joy; he usually had a warm smile on his face. I brushed some of my loose black hair out of my face, and soon turned my gaze to my car. How exactly did he get this here? “Tyler...” I began to say. My eyes surveying over the dormant car. “How exactly did you get my car here?” Tyler smiled back at me, and then quickly pulled a set of keys out of his pocket. I recognized them immediately; they were my own car keys. A silver and gold key chain brightly shone in the sunlight of the summertime in Connecticut. My keys rattled against in the each other in the wind, as Tyler chucked them to me. My eyes watching them warp through the air and land in my firm hand. “That’s how” he said with a smirk, followed up by a small shrug of his shoulders. I was still confused, what had happened to me. Damn, no matter how hard I tried to remember I get nothing but blackness. All I can remember is walking out to the ring, wrestling against that son of a bitch Cassius Deamont, then waking up in a hospital bed. “Tyler” I began to say, blinking a few times. “Tell me what happened after the match with Deamont. I seriously can’t remember.” Tyler frowned severely at me. I don’t know why, maybe he doesn’t want to explain it to me, or maybe, he just doesn’t remember. He shrugged his shoulders and walked around to the other side of my car. He smiled as the yanked the door open. “I’ll tell you” he began to say, a grin on his face. “Just lets go to Burger King or something, I’m really hungry.” I chuckled, if this had been a Japanese Anime Cartoon, I would of fallen to the floor with my arms and legs locked up....but it isn’t. Now I’m gonna find out what happened, I just hope it isn’t as bad as this scare on my head. * * * Personify Tyler Valentine ‘Ugh’ I thought, ‘I am so not looking forward to telling him about this. Knowing Chris he’ll go about and break something.’ Oh well, I looked over at Chris getting into the car, I shook my head once more the thought of what he’ll look like after Crossroads scaring me more and more, each time I think about it.
