Yeah, Dominos?

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today (The scene fades in to show the inside of Brock Newbludd’s bedroom in his babe lair late one morning. Sun beams streak through the curtains of the half open window and onto the king size bed, where we see Rush superstar, “The Innovator” Brock Newbludd sprawled out across it. A cold spring wind blows through the open window and causes Brock to roll over and groan a bit. The camera pans around the room, we see that a huge, (By Brock’s standards even) pile of empty beer cans strewn all over the hardwood floor. We also see a empty bottle of Jack Daniels whiskey in the middle of all the beer cans. We focus back on Brock and we now see that he is now sitting up on the edge of the bed, rubbing his temples. Brock grabs a bottle of aspirin off the night stand and searches the floor for a beer can with some contents left in it, he finds one sitting right beneath his feet. Brock pops the pills in his mouth and swallows the remaining beer in the can. He winces as he throws the beer can on the floor and stands up. We see that he is only wearing a pair of black boxers and a blue old school MWF tank top. His normally spiked up hair is matted down and his face looks unshaven. Brocks crystal blue eyes looks bloodshot and weary. He lets out a yawn and lays down on the ground. Brock does twenty pushups, ten two handed and ten one handed. He then hops up and grabs his cigarettes and a lighter out of his pocket of his pants that lay on the floor. Brock pulls a smoke out of his pack and lights one up, he takes a long drag and walks out of the bedroom and into the kitchen. Brock gets a frown out of his face when he notices that none of the other four people who live in the apartment are nowhere to be found…) Brock: Lindsey? (No answer…) Brock: Hey, Lindsey! Where the hell are you!? (Brock receives no answer again and shrugs his shoulders and walks into the kitchen. He walks over to the fridge for a beer and then raises a eyebrow when he notice a note stuck to the fridge. Brock grabs it and reads it as the camera zooms in over Newbludd’s shoulder to see the note…) Brock, Me and Stace went summer shopping at the mall, ‘m gonna buy something’s for the paper-boy, the pizza delivery guy and maybe a couple milk bones for that stray dog that’s always roaming around outside the apartments. Stephen left early this morning and went up to Green Bay for some autograph signing deal. See ya this afternoon, Lindsey (Brock gets a curious look on his face and grabs his wallet off the kitchen counter, he opens it up and sighs when he sees there is no longer any money left in it…) Brock: She didn’t even leave me beer and cigarette money! And what the f*ck is summer shopping. Just another reason for them to spend money. (Brock balls the piece of paper up and tosses it into the trash bin that sits next to the fridge. Brock rubs the sleep out of his eyes and opens the fridge door. He digs around for a bit before pulling out a ice cold can of Old Milwaukee, (since Stephen took all of the good beer with him) he also pulls out a cold piece of pizza and some egg nog, probably left over from Christmas. Brock shuffles his way over to the kitchen table and sets the beer, pizza and egg nog down. He then walks over and pulls a electric blander and a huge beer stein out of the cupboard. Brock opens the lid of the mixer and pours the beer and egg nog in it. He then tosses the pizza slice in it and puts the lid of the blender on. Brock turns the blender on high and lets it run for a few minutes, turning the contents into a mish mashed, green liquid. He shuts the blender off and pours the soupy mixture into the stein. Brock then takes a long drink of the mixture and smiles…) Brock: Mmmm…Breakfast! (Brock lets out a yawn and stretches his arms out in the air. As he does that we see that Brock has received another tattoo, this one on his right forearm. It’s a tattoo with “Lindsey” wrote on it in gothic style lettering. Newbludd puts his arms down and walks into the spacious living room of the apartment. He strolls over to the large leather couch and plops down on it. Newbludd sprawls out on the couch and takes another sip of his “brew”. He gets a disgusted look on his face and sits up. Brock sets the mixture on the coffee table that sites in front of the couch and grabs the cordless phone that is also on it. He dials up some numbers on the black cordless phone and leans back onto the couch…) Brock (On Phone): Yeah, Dominos? I want two large pepperonis with some breadsticks…think crust… (Brock rolls his eyes and itches his unshaven neck…) Brock: Yes, this is “The Innovator” Brock Newbludd…great, to know I have fans…maybe if you throw in a free pizza I’ll give you a autograph…ok, deal…yeah, it better be no more than twenty minutes either…bye… (Brock hangs up the phone and tosses it back on the table. He looks around on the couch, and searches for the remote for the big screen TV until he finally finds it in between the couch cushions. Just as Brock holds the remote up to turn it on, the small web cam on top of his TV starts blinking a red light…) Brock: Why at the worst moments of the day? I sitting here in my f*cking underwear with my sack prolly hanging out and the damn thing wants me to do a promo!? BAH! Better fetch some pants… (Brock stands up to go get pants, shrugs his shoulders and sits down…) Brock: F*ck it, too early in the morning for pants… (Brock sits back down and lights up a smoke. Then, all of a sudden the flashing red light turns into a steady bright green light. This signifies that the camera has gone from dead air to live air. And for those of you who are unaware what the camera is used for, it is a web cam provided to the Blood Brothers by The Promo Channel so they can do their promos from the convenience of their own home. Brock gives a disgruntled wave at the camera and takes a drag of his cigarette…) Brock: Hiddy ho Bloodaholics. And if you’re wondering why I’m dressed only in my underwear, this handy little camera decided that I should cut a promo this early in the morning, yippee… (Brock grabs a blanket off the top of the couch and covers up his bottom half…) Brock: Well, let’s get down to business, eh? You know what I’m talking about, the tag team match in which me and Dante Motherf*ckin’ Jackson get the kick the sh*t out of two jobbers with weird f*ckin names. Joe Oranges and Andi the Marvelous, both from NTICW of course, which means that they ultimately are just gonna be pushovers. Hell, the only guy I really respect from NTICW is Valentine and he’s a dip-sh*t, (Brock puts out his cigarette in the ashtray and itches his nuts, forgetting he’s on camera…) Brock: Oops…hehe…Anyway, I don’t know much about Dante, except that he’s in the NAACP endorsed Playazs Inc. Which is totally legit with me, because that means we share the same enemies. Hey, I’m a cracka, I know it. But, this cracka can kick the sh*t out of most anybody in Rush if you give him the chance. (Brock cracks takes a sip of his “mixture” and grimaces…) Brock: If Dante doesn’t try anything funny and just does his damn job while we’re in the ring, you can just score another win up for “The Inno-Fuckin-Vator”…. Brock: (Brock grabs a empty beer can off the floor and chucks it at the camera, knocking out the feed and giving us static. To that, we slowly fade to black…)
