CrossRoads Chronicles #6: G.O.D.

13 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8.13.02 'Words are, of course, the most powerful drug used by mankind.'--Rudyard Kipling Nobody has ever said that the trip down the yellow brick road was a short or easy one, it has always been said that it was the path to glory, the path to victory...the path to certainty. The winding road that takes you through so many different places, some easy and some adventurous, yet all of them are worth the trip. A quarter of the road has been traveled now, some nice scenery been looked at, and some experiences that will never be forgotten. Now, as I begins his next travel towards the Gold, towards the answer to my wishes and dreams, towards being a rich man in the sense of vanity, experience and reputation. As I take the next stride towards the path to glory, I am matched face to face with one man who stands only as a mere pebble on my road to victory. Two paths in the road have met with only one path coming out, and standing at the head of that path, looking each other in the eye are myself…and G.O.D. G.O.D. I have been watching you, listening to you, getting to know you, so that I can accurately give my assessment to the rest of the wrestling world on exactly who and what you are. Would you like my early analysis based on your segments? I look at what you did, and I look at how you were built to be a supreme robot, I see how you feelings run about, and I see only one thing...lies. You try to let everybody know how awful your life has been, showing little flashbacks of sorts to give a little added sympathy for yourself…but then again, are you not a robot? Please...you are what I like to call an excuse. Your life is an excuse. Your past is an excuse. Everything you have ever done is an excuse, because you can't seem to face up to it yourself. G.O.D., you are something that many other people are these days, what too many other are getting caught being. You are playing behind a screen, trying to hide the true self, trying to give off the image that you want people to see, not the real one. Every time I say the word mediocre, G.O.D., I speak directly to you and of you. You sicken me in what you do, what you think you can do and what you think you are. The unrespectible nickname of 'Genetic Orinted Drone' that you wear everyday like a dirty, wrinkled shirt, that you have given yourself because you feel it will make you look bigger, make you look like more of a superstar. The fact that you could only become close to main event when everybody else had lost their chance. Who have you legitemately beat? No one. You are not Main Event, you are not in my league. Good luck in the Network Title Match, because if you actually make it to where I doubt you can, then you will find out once again that you are not in my league. Good luck in your future, because you are destined to be the caliber wrestler that will always be found in the middle. Good luck on Crossroads, G.O.D., because you will no doubt need it. I look forward to playing with you for the week ahead, because new blood is always a refreshing sight. For G.O.D., my friend, the countdown has officially begun. (Camera fades into a coffee bar...where Sirius Cage is pouring a pitcher in front of Scott…) Scott Cage: What’s this for? I haven't even been drinking... Sirius Cage: Scott, I can smell that Long Island Iced Tea. Scott Cage: But I took a shower. Sirius Cage: I can tell... Scott Cage: How? Sirius Cage: You have shampoo in your hair... (Scott frowns and runs his hand through his hair...removing the foam...) Scott Cage: It was conditioner...shows how much you know... Sirius Cage: Drink your coffee... Scott Cage: Is it Irish Bailey? Sirius Cage: No, it is not. It's black... Scott Cage: So you're a racist, huh? Sirius Cage: What are you talking about?Scott Cage: How come coffee is always black? Why do you have to be so racist? And then you say Irish can not be black... Siriu Cage: Scott...silence... (Scott frowns, and takes a drink of his coffee, while a stranger runs up and pats him on the back...) Stranger: It's you! It's really you! Scott Cage: I know... Stranger: Guess who I am! (The stranger makes a crying motion...and then laughs at Scott…) Stranger: Guess who I am!! Scott Cage: John Belushi? Stranger: No...I’m Statuz Quo! Scott Cage: Oh...the resemblence is...uncanny. (Scott leans in towards Sirius and whispers in his ear...) Scott Cage: I thought Statuz was white.Sirius Cage: He’s black, Scott…Scott Cage: But this guy...said he was Statuz Quo… Sirius Cage: I think it was a joke. Scott Cage: So he isn't crazy? Sirius Cage: No more crazy than you... (Scott smiles, and sets his coffee down...) Scott Cage: So...what can I do for you? Stranger: I want to be the next Quo… Scott Cage: Oh you do now? Stranger: I can be a better Statuz Quo than Statuz Quo could have ever been... Scott Cage: Well, that isn't really saying much, now is it? (The guy laughs at Scott, and extends his hand...) Stranger: I'm Brandon Selox...it's a pleasure to meet you Mr. Cage… Scott Cage: I'm sure it is... Brandon Selox: Now...about my tryout... Scott Cage: Oh, you really want to try out? Brandon Selox: Yeah, I want to be apart of the experience... Scott Cage: Why? Brandon Selox: I've always wanted to be a star in RUSH...but I have no skills, no style, and no originality. So I figured I would be an impersonator of Statuz Quo, since I’ve heard that you hate him so much... Scott Cage: Well, you definitely have the idiocy that Quo carries...all right, I'll give you a tryout. Brandon Selox: You mean it? What do I do? When do I start? Do I have to suck your dick? Scott Cage: Ummmm...no. But you will have a task. Brandon Selox: What is it? Scott Cage: Well..as you should know...Statuz is my bitch. Brandon Selox: Is he ever! Scott Cage: Well...at any rate...here is your task... (Scott whispers in Selox’s ear...and sends him on his way...) Sirius Cage: Tell me Scott, what did you tell him? Scott Cage: Oh...just something that any good bitch should do. Sirius Cage: Oh? Scott Cage: Yeah...he'll be back in a few... Sirius Cage: Which would give me enough time to talk to you... Scott Cage: About? Sirius Cage: Scott, you have a match against- (Before Sirius can finish...Brandon Selox comes running back to the table....panting...) Brandon Selox: Scott...I'm....back... Scott Cage: Ah...very well Jerome. Did you bring what was needed? Brandon Selox: It's Brandon...and yes...here's your requirement... (Selox pops a cooler onto the table...and Scott smiles as he opens it up...) Scott Cage: You did well Tyrone...very well... (Scott pulls out a bottle of Samuel Adams…and pops it open…) Brandon Selox: Thank you sir...but it's Brandon... Scott Cage: You know LeShawn...you could do really well as the next Statuz Quo... you even have the pattern baldness… Brandon Selox: Really? I didn't think people would notice...I use that rogaine... Scott Cage: It's all right Dre...I'm not holding it against you..it's actually an advantage... Brandon Selox: Oh...okay. So, how are the drinks, Mr. Cage? Scott Cage: Snoop...let's just check, shall we? (Scott grabs the neck of the bottle and tips it back...then spits it out just as quickly...) Scott Cage: What the fuck....warm? Brandon Selox: Is it warm? Oh....I...I was in a hurry... Scott Cage: You fucking want to be Statuz FUCKING Quo...and you give me warm alcohol!?! What the hell is your problem?! Brandon Selox: I'm sorry...really... Scott Cage: Listen Lil’ Kim...I don't need your kind here...now get the hell away... Brandon Selox: It's Brandon... Scott Cage: GET OUT STEVIE WONDER... (Selox slowly backs away from the table....and runs away...in tears...) Sirius Cage: And this is what you want to be hosting? Scott Cage: Ah...they're not all bad...it's just sometimes you get the bad guys with the good guys... Sirius Cage: I'm sure... Scott Cage: Sometimes I'd just like a cold drink though...that isn't so much to ask for... (Scott shrugs...and the camera fades to black...)
