The Texas Chain Store Massacre (Part 1)

17 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17th July 2002 (The doors opened with a swoosh and he walked in with a gallant stride that was designed solely to impress. His casual but tidy attire complimented his smiling face perfectly, and his beaming face was like a star reflecting endless rays of joy. This young man was not the ‘glass is half empty’ kind of person, neither was he one to keep himself to himself. Being out with the fellow occupants of this planet was what he lived for; his reflected image on the faces of others was the one he sought to perfect. His brushed back dreadlocks, thick and black, were highlighted by the buzzing overhead lights of the small grocery store. The thick and bushy beard, that adorned the bottom of his face, was the only thing unkempt on this early summer afternoon. The man pulled a shopping trolley stiffly from its holding bars and began to push it gently with the palms of his hands. He began to whistle a little tune, all the while grinning stupidly. This was truly a happy moment for Joe Bananas, it was as if all the anxiety had faded from the pit of his stomach and had flown away like a delicate butterfly on a gentle breeze. The gleeful Jamaican inspected the area around him, his innocent and almost childlike eyes on the lookout. When he confirmed to himself that the coast was clear he lifted his clumsy feet up onto the lowest bar on the back of the trolley he was pushing. He checked around one last time, as cautious as he could possibly be. He lowered his left foot down again to the smooth and over-polished floor below and began to push himself along gently. He was still wary, half expecting a rush hour type swarm of customers to head into the store and push him around and knock him over. He tried to put the thought out of his mind, letting it flow away. He didn’t want anything to spoil his happiness. He licked the tip of his index finger and held it up towards the ceiling, the air conditioning ruffling his white RUSH t-shirt. Joe nodded as if to substantiate the fact that the conditions were ripe and perfect. He began to push harder with his foot, gaining speed at a radical rate. As soon as he felt he had reached the right velocity, Joe lifted his foot and placed it back on the bottom rail. He held on as tightly as he could to the handle, his knuckles turning white under the pressure. The sickening speed was becoming too much for him, and he did not dare to put his foot down to break. He tightened his eyelids to protect his vision from the ghastly sight. He had no alternative to let out a loud and scratchy screech of terror.) Joe: Aaaagghhh!!! Voice 1: Look out! Voice 2: What the hell? Voice 3: He must be insane or something! (The clatter that almost immediately followed these voices was a deafening clatter of metal meets metal with Joe somewhere, lost in the middle. The crunching of cans, filled with dog food, lasted for what seemed like forever. It was like a bowl of rice crispies; with a special offer Joe floating in the milk. Joe’s eyes opened very slowly, not wanting to see the disaster he had caused. He found that the trolley had fallen and was now on its side, leaving Joe to struggle back up to his feet. He had collided, head on, with a large display of ‘Kay-Nine’ brand dog food and knocked every one to the floor to create the mayhem that lay before him.) Joe: Arr nah! Dis is bruck up nah! (Joe pivots his head on his neck, looking around to see who saw this very ungraceful act. The growing crowd begin to swarm around the disaster, muttering between themselves on the stupidity of the man who had been riding the trolley. Many quips and remarks could be heard, the word ‘dumbass’ was heard quite a few times too. Joe could think of nothing more to do than shrug his shoulders and look innocent.) Joe: Wha? (An angry old woman, who was obviously disgruntled by the disturbance of her weekly shopping, stepped up and shook her fist at Joe threateningly. Gnashing her false teeth as she did so, she frowned heavily at Joe.) Barbara: I’ll tell you ‘wha?’ What it is, is that you are being childish and stupid in this store. Joe: How am Ah bein’ childish an’ stupid ya ol’ wrinkle up woman? Barbara: You rode a shopping cart like a god damned skateboard! Joe: Ah don’ see how dat make meh childish. Barbara: You don’t? Joe: No. Barbara: Well, maybe I had better teach you! (She then stepped forward, closer to Joe in a much shorter and a lot less threatening manor. She took the black leather purse, which had been slung across her left shoulder, and raised it to Joe. She then gave him a stern frown before beginning an onslaught, beating Joe with her purse as hard as she could. Joe yelped like a small dog as he took the hard purse attacks. Some got him his stomach and chest but most were aimed at his groin area. After many attacks Joe fell to the ground, cradling his crotch in pain. Barbara continued, bring her purse down onto Joe’s head now. Joe could have sworn she had lots of keys or sheets or iron in there or something. As soon as the old lady is satisfied that the beatdown she gave the hurt Jamaican was enough. She then walked away and continued on with her shopping as if nothing had happened. After this most other customers carried on with their regular business too, except for one mother who was attending to her crying baby. She was stroking its head making ‘shhhh’ noises, but nothing would work. She stomped over to the now standing Joe.) Mother: You stupid loser! You made my baby cry! Joe: Wha? (Before Joe could make any argument the blonde woman in front of him was attacking him with a similar purse to the old lady named Barbara, but this time with much more force and rage. Joe was soon back on the floor, receiving yet more abuse and shots to the side of his head. She seemed to think it was Joe’s fault that her baby wouldn’t stop crying, causing all the commotion and noise. Joe raised his arms to his face, in an attempt to block the assault. After the mother’s hunger for violence was fulfilled, she walked away and tried to calm her baby down (who had enjoyed the beating mommy had given that man on the floor).) Joe: Uhhh (Joe slowly got back up, holding his stomach and feeling his head for bumps. He wandered around the store in a haze, bumping into boxes and bags on either side of ever aisle. He stumbled clumsily into the refrigerated area and finally remembered why he was even in the store. He shook his head and reasserted his equanimity, strolling over to a set of refrigerated shelves. This was the fruit section, filled with various melons, mangos, apples, oranges and more. Joe smiled and began rummaging through, trying to put the unpleasantness and handbags behind him. He picked up a cantaloupe from the melon section and began to squeeze it slightly. The light brown fruit was very soft and Joe could feel his fingers sinking in. He inspected this very soft fruit and found darker patches all over. He put it back in disgust and lifted another, only to find it exactly the same. He put this one back, the third one was even worse with even darker patches and some mould beginning to grow. Joe slammed this third melon back onto the shelf, squishing it as it hit other melons. Joe grasped a watermelon between his hands and found similar things on this, throwing it back. Joe searched through more and more of the fruit, only finding more and more bad and rotten food. Joe growled, this was disgusting and totally unacceptable. Many passing customers looked as Joe slammed bad fruit in a fit of rage.) Joe: Wha da hell is dis… dis aint right! Pimply-faced assistant: Hell sir, what seems to be the problem? Joe: Dese fruit aint right, none of them are any good. Ya can’t get away with selling such bad stuff! Pimply-faced assistant: Sir, I assure these fruit are fine. There is nothing wrong with them at all. Joe: Nuttin wrong wit dem? Jus look! (Joe pointed down to a disfigured pineapple, the green leaves turning brown and the body of the fruit warped and dark. His face showed the disgust he was going through, feeling shame that his beloved mangos and apples could be sold in such bad shape, none of these fruit should be even near a place of grocery. The assistant didn’t even glance in the direction of the pineapple; he didn’t care at all.) Pimply-faced assistant: All of the fruit we sell have been inspected by a health company and have been accepted for sale. Joe: Wha? How could anybody accept dese fa sale? Pimply-faced assistant: Don’t ask me sir; I’m just a shop assistant. I suggest you take your opinions elsewhere! Joe: What?! Pimply-faced assistant: You heard me, this isn’t the place for this kind of behaviour. Take what you think about our fruit and stick it up your own ass! Big trouble will come your way if your opinions and your fat mouth don’t get the hell out of here! Joe: Excuse me! Ya can’t talk ta meh like dat! Pimply-faced assistant: All our foodstuffs have been inspected, you have a problem… take it them. (The assistant gave Joe one last hard gaze, his face scowled up like a dried prune. Joe, without taking his eyes off the pimply face assistant, lifted an orange into his hands and stared down at the sticker that was spread across it. It was plain white with a green, white and red flag in the centre in the left hand corner. The writing was bright red and shot off the sticker in big bold letters ‘Aprobar: Mexicana tabla de alimento.’) Joe: Mexican board of health? (Joe puts the fruit back down and calls an assistant over, she is a small mousy woman with thick glasses. She walks over with a forced smile, Joe towering over her.) Assistant: Hello sir, how can I help you? Joe: Dese fruit, dey been approved by da Mexican board of health. Why’s dat? Assistant: I’m sorry sir, I don’t know what you mean. Joe: Well, why ya got da Mexican board of health ta see if ya fruit is ok? Assistant: Well sir, it’s like this… (The woman kicks Joe straight in the groin, forcing him to fall to his knees the force was so much, and thrusts a wet cloth into his face. Joe’s vision begins to fade into darkness as the chloroform begins to take effect.) * * * (Light begins to appear in Joe’s eyes, growing brighter and brighter as his eyelids open. Joe brushes a build up of saliva from his chin and looks around to try and find out where he is. He sees an overweight man, wearing a smart grey suit, silver hair and a large nose on his tanned face.) Man: I see you’re awake. Joe: Wha? Where am Ah? Man: It doesn’t matter where we are, the important thing is who you are. Joe: Who Ah am? Man: Well, all we know is you have a Jamaican accent… whether it’s fake or not is a different matter. Joe: We? Man: Oh come now, we know of those secret people who want to expose us… don’t pretend to be ignorant. Joe: Expose ya? Man: Well, being the bad guy… and after all you are tied to a chair, I’ll tell you. The store you were in is only a front, we have strong relationships with all the major Latin American countries and have a good business going. We have good ties with many Columbian drug lords, and all the Cuban imports we want. We have everything we could ever want, all sorted out. Joe: Wha ya talk wit? Man: Please, you can drop the accent. We know you aren’t from anywhere near the Caribbean, so there’s no need to pretend. Joe: Ah am from da Caribbean, don’ dis on mah patois foo! Man: Well, ok. Talk like that all you want, it won’t get you anywhere now. We are based in the heart of Texas, and have our arms spread across the world. We intend to crush it, just for our pleasure. Joe: Ah t’ink ya go’ meh mix up wit someone else mon. Man: Don’t play around, it won’t get you out of here. You’ll never be able to stop us as soon as Plan B is put into action. Joe: Plan B? Man: Yes, Plan B. Well, the fruit, which is imported from Mexico, is going to be loaded with drugs from various locations. We plan on selling this and setting plants to make sure that it is discovered and reported. We want to make as big a thing about this as possible and run it back to a certain ‘Loco’ Carlos. He has been a big threat to the organisation and we will put him away for a very long time, and putting him away as blame for the drug loaded fruit will give us credibility for putting him away. It sounds risky, but I think we can give ourselves a good image after it. Joe: Wha? Man: You heard me! Now, I’ll give you some time to take this all in. My genius must be overwhelming to someone as obviously stupid as you. I’ll be seeing you, mon. (The man leaves through the large, metal door and shuts it tightly. Joe is all alone and tied to the chair; he is an unjust prisoner with no escape. The dirty surroundings are an obvious prison cell, but he still has no idea where he is. He feels his eyes becoming heavy again, the weightiness of sleep pulling him back into darkness again.) * * * (The loud and obtrusive sound of knocking snaps Joe back into the land of consciousness. As he rubs his eyes, and generally wakes himself up, a short and stocky man walks in; pushing a small wooden trolley covered in various books. He stretches his arms out, grasping a glossy piece of paper.) Librarian: Would you like a pamphlet? (Joe snatches the glossy pamphlet from his hands and stares at the cover. In big, black bold writing are the words ‘So you’re the prisoner of a evil mastermind?’ with a picture of a super-hero looking very sad next to a barred up window. The librarian wafts his hand above the trolley.) Librarian: Would you like to take a sample of our various pieces of reading material? (Joe searches through the books, he considers the paper-back version of ‘little bobby’s trip to the ice cream store’ but thinks against it, and arduously lifts the full, hardback version of ‘war and peace’. He smacks the librarian in the side of his head with this large and solid book. The man falls to the ground, clutching his cranium in pain. His loud screams shattering Joe’s ears. Joe scrunches up the pamphlet he has next to him and thrusts it into the screaming Librarian’s mouth. Joe then moves the trolley of books just outside the open door, but before leaving goes back into his cell and pokes the librarian in the eyes with his index and middle finger.) Joe: At last, freedom. (Joe launches himself on the weak trolley and speeds down the hallway, reaching a door after a short time. Joe pushes the door open, allowing the blinding light to pour into his eyes.) Joe: Wha da hell? (As he grows accustomed to the light, he realizes he is still in the store where he was before. Everyone around him, including the pimply-faced assistant from before, is staring at Joe in awe and amazement. Joe steps out, with a smile on his face, before closing the door behind him. Joe finds that the door was actually a refrigerator filled with health foods. Joe shrugs his shoulders and sprints off as quickly as he can out of the store.) Joe (to himself): Wha was dat all about? Dis evil store has gotta be stopped, fruit and drugs shouldn’t be associated together an’ Ah gotta stop em!
