New Experiences: The Park

10 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/10/02 Lights. All buzzing and blinking. All of them in their wonderous zany outputs. G.O.D. had had a smile on his face long before they got to the park, but when they stepped away from the ticket booth it was more amazing than ever. Nothing he had ever seen before. The closest was the pyro at the beginning of a wrestling show or wrestlers entrance, but not like this. This was amazing. Since Gene Otto Droidman was “born” he had barely left the disclosed laboratory. He had stayed in hotels and that was mainly it. That was all he had done. He had done nothing but been trained because that is the way Dr. Jenkins thought he would fight best. However that became untrue. G.O.D. was a very gentle and friendly creature who was a hard worker. He got the job done when he needed to and sometimes when he didn’t need to, but he was fun loving. He was fun loving even when he didn’t really know what fun was. G.O.D. is like a loyal dog. His brain has not gone to the limits of knowing the world. He experiences joy that one cannot hide from anything. But he didn’t seek it. It was just something good that happened every once in awhile. But Dr. Jenkins had had an idea. G.O.D. would need to seek joy. He would seek it, but not be allowed to have it unless he journeyed himself. It was a simple plan. When G.O.D. won he would go have fun. When he lost he wouldn’t. Now Dr. Jenkins didn’t know what would happen if he was screwed though. It may cause extreme rage, which would not in the least bit be good. But he had to try his theory out. It was a very good plan. There was just one seed to be planted… A hunger. G.O.D. needed a hunger for the fun. He needed to know what the fun was. He needed to know what he could experience once work was successfully finished. And so they went to the fair. Here they were, bright lights, big rides, sweet smells, the whole works. Little kids ran around with sticks of cotton candy in their hands while parents chuckled as their children dragged them to the bumper cars. “What should we do first?” Gene asked excitedly. “Now Gene, we can only stay for about an hour. Then we must go,” the great doctor replied. The Genetic Oriented Drone’s face turned sour a moment. “But why?” G.O.D. pleaded. Dr. Jenkins smiled. “It does not matter. Now since we’re only here for an hour I guess we better do as much as possible in that time.” G.O.D. broadly smiled once again and leapt. “Let’s go!” Gene’s hulking figure stomped the grass below him making 21 shoe-sized craters below. The doctor followed as fast as a man who has devoted his life to cloning in a science lab coat can go. G.O.D.’s first ride he wanted to go on was the bumper cars. The line was short and if it were long Gene may have exploded. People stared at him with almost disgust at a man who looked about twenty-seven jumped and cackled about going on the bumper cars. As he entered the ride he still remained overjoyed and once in his car he started to make many vroom vroom sounds. He laughed wildly as he crashed and smashed into children’s rubbered cars. Slapping the wheel when a car made a funny sound as the bumpers collided. Once off he clapped his hands with pure, utter enjoyment. Never ever had G.O.D. been so happy. He was like a kid in a candy store, since he almost is just that. “That was fantastic!” G.O.D. screamed to his creator. “Let’s go again! Can we? Huh, huh? Can we? Please!” “Gene, there’s so much more to do. If we come back we can go on the rides again.” “Oh, okay. What’s next?” G.O.D. asked. “That’s up to you son.” Dr. Jenkins motioned with his hands around the whole park. “Pick whatever you want to.” G.O.D. scanned the area. “I’m hungry,” he said monotonously. Dr. Jenkins chuckled lightly. “What is funny?” With a smile: “You are really going to enjoy some snacks. Come with me.” The two men traveled to the nearest convenience stand. “Two cotton candies please,” the great doctor told the man behind the counter. “Strawberry or blue raspberry?” the man asked. “Blue raspberry?” G.O.D. yelped. “What kind of crazy show are you running here?” “Don’t worry Gene,” Jenkins reassured his creation, “We’ll have two strawberries, thank you.” The Genetic Oriented Drone made some yum noises. “I like strawberries.” “I know Gene.” They were handed their cotton candy and G.O.D. looked at it puzzled. “It looks like a cloud. How can I eat a cloud?” “Like this,” and Dr. Jenkins took a fluffy bite. G.O.D. looked at him in shock. One could actually see the sugar coming off of the cotton candy. He dove into his and started champing away at the fluff. “Delicious!” he exclaimed. The two finished up their cotton candy and each had a hot pretzel to top it off. Half of G.O.D.’s was covered in mustard and the other with just salt. He wanted to try both kinds. But when they finished their trip to the park was not over with just yet, no, no, no. G.O.D. wanted to go on more rides. This time a bigger, better, and faster ride. A roller coaster or as G.O.D. called it, ‘One of those big things.’ So they visited the line to what was called The Colossal. Dr. Jenkins wanted to sit out, but Gene wouldn’t let him. The ride was advertised as being the Greatest, Fastest, Hardest, and Superest Ride In North America. Dr. Jenkins had to a point out that superest was not a word. The ride was indeed fast, and hard, and G.O.D. thought it was great and if superest was in fact a word it would be that too. Dr. Jenkins however did not and his stomach agreed with him as after the ride he spent eight minutes and twenty-seven seconds losing the pretzel and cotton candy. At a vending machine he bought some gum for his breath. “I’m sorry you threw up Dr. Jenkins.” “It’s okay Gene, it’s not your fault.” He smacked his gum up and down in his mouth. “We don’t have to on a ‘roller coaster’ again if you don’t want to,” G.O.D. said. “Thank you.” Dr. Jenkins’ gum smacked even more. “Well,” he looked at his watch, “We still have fifteen minutes, what would you like to do?” G.O.D. thought. Between the concession stand and the roller coaster he saw many rides. What did he want to end this night on? What could possibly make this night even better than it already is? And then it came to him. “Can we go through the haunted house?” Steam almost flew from the surprised ears of Dr. Jenkins. “The h-haunted,” gulp, “H-house?” he stuttered. “Yeah. Can we?” “Umm… s-sure… I guess.” It was obvious Jenkins did not want to go. This was not about losing his lunch though, this was about sleep. Dr. Jenkins always seemed to have a problem with nightmares, but he went along anyway. The house was scary indeed. G.O.D. loved being scared by the ghosts, pirates, goblins, and other mystic creatures. Dr. Jenkins hid his head in his hands and in between his head the entire time. All in all it would be successful. Dr. Jenkins would only have one nightmare about the sounds in the ride. Droidman would have five, but he will deny every bit of it. He did have fun. The night was success. It was happy and fun, for the both of them. They had a great time at the amusement park and whenever G.O.D. won they could go back or they could go somewhere else. Perhaps they could go to an ice skating rink. Maybe a movie and they will snack on popcorn and raisenets. Who knows? They could go to a baseball game. The point is after the job was finished they would have fun, if the job was done correctly. And G.O.D. had a drive to win. Mission accomplished.
