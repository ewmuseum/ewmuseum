Which One?

3 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/3/02 The great doctor sits alone facing the wall, plain and white. His forehead buried in his right palm. Deep thought flows over him in a cloud of misery. Which one? he thinks. Only one can be won. He's not strong enough yet for both. Not yet. But which one? Which one? The risks are great. The power is great. G.O.D. is great, but which one? A guarenteed win for the bronze or a chance at gold. Only one can be one. Just one. Which one? Which one? Which One! Jenkins' fists ball up and slam down on the arms of the chair in rage. Rage caused by the one thing Dr. Jenkins never has... doubt. Dr. Jenkins has never doubted anything in his life, but now his creation is faced with two challenges and he can only win one. If he tried his damnedest to win both he would certainly lose both. Two cluster matches. He can get caught. It's every man for himself with five other men involved. He could get caught. Dealing with one foe and someone be beaten by another. Certainly the latter of the two matches was easier in theory. Sure there were more adversaries involved, but the concept was simpliar. To be eliminated one must be thrown over the top rope. Of course G.O.D. could throw people over and would be much harder to throw over, but it is a game of psycology. People see The Genetic Oriented Drone and think, 'Oh no! I must eliminate him!' He's may as well have a sign on his chest saying 'You're all going to lose to be if you don't do something about it quick.' G.O.D. is a target. Five men will rush him and try to get him over, if that doesn't work, two more will, then three, and so on until he is gone. If G.O.D. were to win he would need to work fast and be inconspicuous. But how can the biggest man in RUSH, standing six foot nine and weighing close to four hundred pounds be inconspicuous? Impossible. In most matches G.O.D. has the clear advantage in most people's minds, esspecially Dr. Jenkins'. However, it is all a game of psychology. In a contest of one versus one versus one versus one there are too many factors in play. Man A may hate Man C while Man C mildly hates Man A but just really wants to be out of the match as fast as possible. Man B and Man D may have a plan to work as a team so that neither Man A or Man C win. It's all very impacted. And so Dr. Jenkins thought and though. Which one? Which one? Which one?
