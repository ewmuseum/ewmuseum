The Bottom of the Ocean...

27 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6.27.02 'Don’t wait for the Last Judgment. It takes place every day.' -- Albert Camus Well kids, it has come to my attention, that in the recent week, while I have been accurately giving the portrayal of the REAL Livewire that none of you seem to know, he has been attempting to make some points of his own. Some points that all seem to center around the fact that I am an genetic defect, and then running scared near the end of his idiotic interviews, about how he totally believes in the back of his mind that he will win the Network Title, the same title that belongs to me. I must take offense to the fact that you said you have improved my invention. In case you didn't know, when something is created, it is created exactly how it is wanted. Understand? So therefore when you attempt to say you have improved my wrestling style, my technique, my invention, you are once again, making...that's right, yourself look more foolish then I ever could. It seems to me Livewire, that your worst enemy, and the man that you should be mad at, isn't me the one goating you into everything you are doing, but yourself…you are the one creating havoc on yourself thanks to your unstable mental status. You have not improved anything, all you have done is stolen my originality and attempted to pass it off as your own. I will applaud you as you have chosen the right style to rip off, yet you have chosen the wrong man to do it from. It seems to me, that with all your attempts, and all your lies, and all of your sissy ramblings, you have forgotten to do one little thing. Accuracy. You are true, I did say to focus on the more difficult thing then to hide from it, and I am looking forward to crippling you. Hypocrisy? Far from it. Even though I do not enjoy saying this, you are stories below the ability of Marvin Harris, FORMER Network Champion. What Champion are you Livewire? I see... I mean, you were RUSH's leading jobber, but what does that mean? It means that you are not good enough to get a wrestling title. So was I overlooking you? Of course. Was I being a Hypocrite? Of course not. Any other little tidbits you would like to mistake yourself on? I didn't think so… See Livewire, your puppy love rhymes towards me have been falling on deaf ears. Compared to what your in-ring ability could do for you against me, this could be murder, real murder. What does this mean Livewire? Simply, you do not scare me, not in the slightest. You cannot intimidate me Livewire, because I am not that kind of person. You do not frighten me Livewire, because I have seen what you can do. You do not worry me, because you ramblings are better then your wrestling, and fans around the world know about your ramblings. That simply leaves you in a ring with someone who doesn't feel the butterflies in his stomach that you are used to your opponents feeling. That simply leaves you in a ring with someone who knows the truth about himself and you. That simply leaves you in the ring, with another loss to your name. You have done something that I didn't think you could do, and that was annoy me. I have heard your interviews before, and although they caused me to change the channel, they never caused any annoyance towards you. Although they caused me brain cramps, they never caused me any annoyance. But recently, after receiving yourself handed to you on a silver platter by my bodyguards Leo and Lars, two people who have never before in their lives done a promo, yet seemed to embarrass you by raising above your low quality promos with an excellent one of their own. Did it hurt Livewire? Did it hurt to know that two bodyguards could diminish you to an even smaller man? To be completely insulted without even having a lie told, or an accusation made? To be embarrassed when someone only speaks the truth of you? It seems that it has embarrassed you enough to have to resort to petty name-calling and childish threats, the exact same thing that every other man has ever done when confronted by a woman. Could it be that Livewire has followed suit again, after everyone else has done it already, paving the way for unoriginal folk like yourself to follow? See kid, this is simple, and I want you to listen to the Truth. You had the truth told about you by a woman, and you couldn't handle it. You realized that you were embarrassed on national television by my bodyguards, and you couldn't handle it. You found it in yourself, the truth about you, the truth that you know you aren't what you portray yourself to be, and the fans around the world saw that you saw it too and you couldn't handle it. Well understand this. Not only will you be embarrassed today, but you will be hurt today. Now handle that! I am sure that you are getting sick and tired of having me parlay the truth on to your fan about your terrible in-ring antics, and sine I have you pegged on so many other thins, I will leave that aspect out from now on. You say you will not make bold statements, even though your interview was jam-packed with them. Livewire, I have said it before and I will say it again, no matter how many times people will feed this to you, and try to make you believe the opposite, Respect isn't earned, it is given. You can try and try and try to earn my respect, but I will not give you my respect because you not only don't deserve it, but you have lost even more then what I thought I had for you already. Your words that you use are limited as you seem to be compeled to repeat everything you do. Until you realize that you could possibly be better, you could possibly be good, you will get nothing from me. You seem to think that this gimmick will help you fly right through RUSH, right through the ranks and right through me, but no gimmick is superior to wrestling, no matter which way you look at it, no matter what direction it's from. As for the Punch to the Pec…I encourage you to bring every little bit of wrestling ability you have, because if you don't, it will simply be even easier for me to dominate you. If you do bring what little ability you have, then the domination may not be so evident... perhaps it will only be a devastation? Truth be told Livewire, you will never have my respect, no matter what you can do in the ring. One simple question, once again...Did it Hurt? (With that said, we fade into an indoor pool. The one and only Scott Cage sits now, in front of all of your eyes in front of the reception desk. His body is folded in the white, plastic furniture, his eyes closed tightly. His head is folded back on laying gently, resting on the top of the chair. His white clothes that he always wears have been totally soaked through, and we can see him as if he weren't wearing anything at all. His feet are bare, no shoes or boots, as he finally opens his eyes, and stares at the camera. As the camera zooms in closer on our RUSH Network Champion, and around his waist he has his belt wrapped around him. His hair, which is usually spiked in all directions, has had a drastic change now, as it is pasted to his head thanks to the current downpour which is upon us. His eyes are the stone blue that they always are, and the stare that he holds toward us now is stone as well. Looking through his pasted on wife beater, we can see his muscles more evident as his shirt is tucked nicely around each muscle in his body. The setting is picturesque, and as the camera slowly pans towards the reception desk and the large door, we see the lifeguard standing there, keeping herself warm and dry. The pan moves back towards Scott, who takes off his Network Title, and throws it over his shoulder, as he walks towards the Lifeguard…) Lifeguard: Hi, can I help you? Scott Cage: I'm here for my surfing lessons... Lifeguard: Excuse me? Scott Cage: I want to learn how to surf. Lifeguard: Here? Scott Cage: Where else? Lifeguard: I don't know where you got the idea that we have surfing lessons... Scott Cage: Oh, it was right here in this ad from the newspaper. Lifeguard: Can I see it? Scott Cage: Sure... (Scott pulls out a piece of paper from his pocket, flashes it, than stuffs it into his pocket...) Scott Cage: There, now...where are my surfing lessons? Lifeguard: I'm sorry sir, but I don't think that is an actually ad promoting the pool... Scott Cage: You calling me a liar? Lifeguard: Well sir, you certainly aren't proving me that you have an actual ad. Scott Cage: Okay, okay...I'll confess. Lifeguard: That would be helpful... Scott Cage: Okay, it's not a real ad for the pool... Lifeguard: I thought so... Scott Cage: But you know what? Lifeguard: What is that sir? Scott Cage: I'm tall...and I want to surf. Do you understand me? Lifeguard: I do believe I do... Scott Cage: Now, when do I start my lessons? Lifeguard: Come with me sir...we'll begin right now. Scott Cage: Whooooo! (Scott does a little dance as the lifeguard posts a 'Closed' sign in the window, and step out the backdoor to meet Scott…) Lifeguard: Alright, how much swimming experience do you have? Scott Cage: Experience? Well…I was in the 1995 Summer Olympics! Lifeguard: The 1995 Summer Olympics huh? Scott Cage: Oh yeah...I was great! Lifeguard: And what exactly was your event? Scott Cage: Swimming. Lifeguard: What style? Scott Cage: The one...with the moving of the arms like this... (Scott motions his arms around...and the lifeguard tries to hold back a laugh...) Lifeguard: You mean the breaststroke? Scott Cage: Huh? Lifeguard: The breaststroke…that's the event you competed in? Scott Cage: Hmm...well, I never really thought of breast stroking as an event... but maybe in the water it's more difficult. Lifeguard: No...it's a swimming method...but I'm sure you knew that. Scott Cage: Huh? Of course I did, I'm an Olympic champ. Lifeguard: All right Mr. Olympian...how about we get into the water... Scott Cage: Into the water? Lifeguard: Yeah...so we can begin the lesson. Scott Cage: But...but I thought we were surfing...where we're on the boards, and not in the water? Lifeguard: You still have to get into the water sir...you're not...afraid of the water, are you? Scott Cage: Oh no...I'm…uh…just concerned about the...the pH level in the water. You know, what with all the urine. (The lifeguard hides a laugh...and smiles at Scott Cage…) Lifeguard: The pH level is perfectly fine sir...we have the anti-pee device in the water. Scott Cage: What's that? Lifeguard: Well...if someone urinates in the water, the water around them turns red... Scott Cage: Wow, that's cutting edge... Lifeguard: But, I'm sure you knew about that, being around pools and such... Scott Cage: Oh, well...of course I did. Lifeguard: Okay sir, are we ready to get into the water? Scott Cage: I dunno...I think I just ate not to long ago. Lifeguard: What did you have sir? Scott Cage: A bagel. Lifeguard: Oh, you're fine. I'll assure you that you'll be fine... Scott Cage: What if I start drowning? Lifeguard: I will pull you out... Scott Cage: But...but you're a girl. Lifeguard: I'm a lifeguard. Scott Cage: Would you run in slow motion like they do in Baywatch? Lifeguard: Get into the pool. Scott Cage: You'd run in slow motion into the pool? (The lifeguard pushes Scott into the pool...and jumps in herself...) Scott Cage: Ah! GAH! HELP! Lifeguard: What's wrong sir? Scott Cage: Huh...oh...nothing. I thought I saw a leak in the lining... Lifeguard: All right, well, our first exercise is to swim across the pool, so we can warm up. Scott Cage: Across the pool? Lifeguard: Is that going to be a problem? Scott Cage: Oh...no, I'm already warmed up. Lifeguard: Humor me. Scott Cage: All right... (Scott 'dives' into the water...and when he surfaces, begins to doggy paddle to the other side...) Lifeguard: You'll have to swim back Mr. Olympian! Scott Cage: How about you warm up, and swim here? Lifeguard: Because the surf board is on this side. (Scott curses, and 'dives' into the water once again, and dog paddles back...as the lifeguard tries hard not to laugh...) Lifeguard: What medals did you say you won at the '95 Summer Olympics? Scott Cage: I won 2 Gold Medals...and a Diamond. Lifeguard: A Diamond Medal, huh? Scott Cage: Um…yeah? Lifeguard: Wow, that's amazing! (Scott flashes a grin towards the lifeguard...who fights the laughter...) Scott Cage: Isn't it? Lifeguard: Oh...yes. Now, onto the lesson...do you know how to approach a surfboard? Scott Cage: Is it anything like a lady? If so...I'll need two bottles of Coors and a bag of... Lifeguard: No...what you do, is you slide the board in front of you, than you gradually slide onto it, and paddle your way out into the water, similar to how you paddled during your laps. Scott Cage: That's it? Lifeguard: That's it... Scott Cage: Well, why the hell do people make this out to be so complicated? (Scott grabs the board from the lifeguard...and throws it out in front of him, and attempts to jump on it...) Scott Cage: Hey lady, I think something is wrong with the board, because it slipped out of my grasp and hit me right in the chin. Lifeguard: I'll call the company immediately... Scott Cage: I would complain to them too…HOLY SHIT! Lifeguard: What is it? Scott Cage: I'M BLEEDING!!! LOOK AT THE WATER! IT'S ALL RED!! Lifeguard: No...that...that would be the anti-pee device... (Scott gives a weak smile to the lifeguard and wades towards the edge of the pool, as the camera fades to black...)
