Chapter 002 - KyUr 4 Da BrOk'N

29 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08-29-2002 ~Guess Who's Back~ "Never wrote something like this.... looking back into my career its been hell. Nothing great has come out of it... just grief and sorrow. I wish life wasn't like this! I would have a pillow, comfort. None of that is here for me... life is too hard but then again I love this job. Damn, this is worth it! Even after the screaming and moaning... I still feel the RUSH!"@}~'~~ Fast Forward… "What? Where am I? My God...." "HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT!" "Are…you…fucking…kidding…me!" "YOSHIO AKINAGA HAS BEEN ELIMINATED!" At that time, they never expected Akinaga was hurt. His body crushed from Ricky Starr's unexpected splash shattered his ribs. His body was in disarray from the adrenaline pumping in his blood. He was being carried up the ramp by two large security men. “Are You Ok Sir?” “No.. I don’t think so, I think I broke something after that.” Akinaga groaned. “Not another one, we better get you out of here.”@}~'~~ “You’ll be fine Mr. Akinaga, we’re taking you to the local hospital.” “I don’t feel fine, I feel like shit.” Akinaga growled. “Where are we anyways?” “I believe you passed out while putting you onto the stretcher. The certified doctor did not understand the situation and luckily Boda decided to call the paramedics. Your quite lucky he did such a generous this for you.” “Typical, that Boda will do anything for his own profit, including endangering his own talent. God damn it!” Akinaga with a second slaps away what looks like tweezers, attempting to pull his broken body up. He moans in pain as he falls back into the stretcher looking at the man he was previously speaking to “Sir, I believe you should not try to move. It would just further injure your body.” “Um… thanks but I’m fine. Please tell that driver to stop and let me out of this car.” Akinaga groaned. “Sir, We can not let you out! Boda requests your stay tonight in the hospital until we can further investigate on your injury. Please stay still.” “Boda can stick it up where he likes it. I’m fine and nobody would know better then me anyways.” “Sir, please do not restrain.. This is a serious matter and Boda would not want to have any of his…” “Money? You believe Boda is a man who would care for his men? Hah.. don’t make me laugh, through my short time in this hell. I haven’t done anything but look at him pull his weight around and smile. Why? His big paycheck and power is all he really cares about. Let me out of this..” Akinaga’s stubborn ways and questionable mouth begin to aggravate his companions inside the vehicle. He tugs and begins to disturb the paramedics. “Enough, Boda warned us about you. Calm down now or we will be forced to stop you.” “Please… I’d like to see that happ… “ Akinaga’s mouth is covered by a plastic mask. His head begins to go into a dream land. His brain cells die in the short travel time. Akinaga lays on his stretcher peacefully. @}~'~~ |> |> Fast Forward… “Folks, I’m afraid we may have lost our TV Slot because of that outburst. In the event we have, I’m James Williams and this is Kris Raves, thank you for joining us on yet ANOTHER RUSH Wrestling Adrenal…” “They don’t know what they’re expecting now do they? Boy has it been quite some time since I’ve been in the ring. Boy do I crave blood of others. Finally the Yakuza returns to the ring….” Akinaga stands up wearing a brace around his ribs. He puts the remote down and walks away with a big grin on his face. His one eye looks into the camera as he holds his hands up. “The time for revenge is ticking, soon the ticking will stop and this bomb will explode. Will you be safe? No you will not… No you will not my friend, your life ends on Crossroads.”~ Even cherry trees tremble in the winds. ~
