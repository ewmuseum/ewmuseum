Date: Today (Our scene opens up to show the inside of a airplane, time unknown, location unknown. We see that we are in the passenger deck of the plane, it looks to be the first class compartment, for all of the people sitting in it are dressed up in nice business suits or other types of high expense clothes. It is a quite large section, probably sitting fifty people or so. Most of them are sitting in their seats, typing on lap top computers or reading some sort of newspaper, most likely the Wall Street Journal or some other type of business paper. While others it contently in their seats or have headphones on, listening to the in-flight movie, “Dances With Wolves”. While some are enthralled with the movie, mostly the chicks, most of the guys have taken their headsets off, since they would much rather listen to the whir of the planes engines than Kevin Costner’s “acting”. The sparse number of kids who are in the first class section sit playing game boys or listening to music. Stewardess’s walk up and down the aisles, asking patrons of the plane if they need anything, we focus in on one stewardess who is more toward the back of the seating area. She is pushing a cart full of refreshments and other miscellaneous items. The stewardess is quite young and good looking, probably in her early to mid twenties. Her tight fitting airline uniform and long skirt shows off her quite voluptuous curves. She pushes her cart down the aisle and stops where two men are sitting, not even paying attention to the movie. We can see that they both have Nintendo Gameboy’s in their hands and look to be playing a game in it via a link cable. As we zoom over to the two men, we see that they are none other than five time PCW tag team champions The Blood Brothers, now Rush superstar “The Innovator” Brock Newbludd and the now unemployed “The New Legend” Stephen Blood. Brock sits on the aisle side of the seats, while Stephen has the window seat. Brock and Stephen both pound furiously on the buttons of the Gameboy colors, we see that the two are playing the new Rush game out, Rush: Total Adrenaline. Brock pounds on the buttons furiously as Stephen kicks his ass on the video game…) Brock: Sh*t! Cock-lick…sonuva…AAH..NOOO!… (We watch as the video game Stephen pins the video game Brock after hitting the Stephenflow DDT…) Stephen: WHOOO! I’m up thirty-two match to twenty eight! Brock: Best of fifty! (Stephen sighs and puts down his Gameboy…) Stephen: Dude, this started out as a simple best of three series, and besides my thumbs are totally numb now… Brock: So I win, by forfeit? (Stephen just shakes his head at Brock…) Stephen: Sure, if we can stop for awhile… (Brock raises his fist in the air triumphantly…) Brock: That’s right! I win! You suck! WHOO! (Stephen chuckles at Brock...) Brock: Dude, I kicked your ass! (We focus back in on the stewardess, who taps Brock on the shoulder. Newbludd turns around and smiles at the sexy stewardess, looking her up and down…) Brock: Why…hello there… (The stewardess notices Brock eyeing her up and she smiles at him…)Stewardess: Hello Mr.Newbludd… Brock: Call me Brock. (The stewardess blushes for a second, but then shakes it off…) Stewardess: Ok, Brock, Would you or Mr. Blood like any refreshments during the flight… Brock: Sure baby, make it two Bloodweisers, a big bag of peanuts and…your phone number… (Stephen, who was looking at the crystalline sky, over hears this and punches Brock…) Stephen: Hey Brock, um, remember you have that thing some people call a wife? (Brock frowns and looks back at the stewardess…) Brock: Better cancel the phone number thing… (The stewardess gives a face of disappointment…) Stewardess: Your beers and peanuts will be right up… Brock: Thanks (The stewardess walks out of the shot and Brock rubs his temples in anxiety…) Stephen: What’s wrong with you? Brock: Just nervousness, I guess. Can’t wait for Sunday. Stephen: Don’t worry bro, you don’t have much competition in that match anyway, right? (Brock shrugs his shoulders…) Brock: I dunno man, Mohammed Nasir has the crowd behind him. Stephen: Sheeeit man, that’s nothing, the crowd are a bunch morons anyway. Brock: True… Stephen: That doesn’t matter, you have something that Nasir doesn’t have. (Brock looks at Stephen curiously…) Brock: What’s that? Stephen: Talent. Brock: Hehe, I suppose I do that have that on the little d turd. Hell, I know I have that on him! Mohammed calls himself “The Prophet”? Ha! The only thing I prophesize for him is a stiff ass kicking at Ignition. And who knows, maybe if I wipe the mat with this assclown, the upper brass will finally see that I have what it takes to be a main eventer in Rush. Stephen: Exactly man. Exactly…Brock: Sunday will be a good night for “The Inno-F*ckin’-Vator”… (We fade out as Brock leans his seat back and falls into a slumber…)
