And now the dust has cleared...

24 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8.24.02 'It is not enough to succeed. Others must fail.'- Gore Vidal As the crowd begins to disperse, splitting down in halves every week, we finally realize who are the wrestlers that not only have the talent to make it to RUSH, but who still have the gas to keep them going. After only being settled down right after the pay per view, many people have already slipped a tad, having to compete not only after a grueling battle royal, but after a tiresome week. Many people have been silent, resting up wishing not to break the image that they are attempting to hold up to everyone still left. Attempting not to show any holes in there plans, any chinks in there armor. It has always been said that you cannot prepare for what you do not know, and holding the fact that your opponent doesn't know who you are, what you are, where your from and the rest of those famous five W's, is something that you should rejoice about. Almost everybody could have an advantage right? Right. Almost everybody. Currently, and I say this stating only facts, I am the most well known wrestler going, I am known by everybody and hated by nearly every wrestler left in this. So I, before even stepping into the ring am at a disadvantage of being known, of being studied, of being famous? Quite frankly, it is turned equal again while every opponent I wrestle is at a disadvantage, skills wise. I am known. I am a favorite. I am a target. But most importantly, I am a winner, a wrestler and a champion and those are three traits that you cannot study for. I dare you to try. What it comes down to now is proving myself. I am sure many of you may be confused by that, may be asking yourself how else I could prove myself after the many titles and records I hold, and the wins I have over many of the top stars in the business. Many of you may not comprehend something, that although titles may be a great and marvelous thing to have, not everybody bases themselves around that, not everybody uses that as the yardstick to measure themselves with. I, am on of those seldom few who don't...or at least try as hard as possible not to. One of the things I try to base myself on is movement. What is movement? In my eyes, movement is a combination of things that include ability, devotion and persistence. Ability is whether I am stagnating myself, whether I have just leveled off or I continue to better myself, to learn more and adapt more. Devotion, that whether or not I am tied down and bonded from something so anti-whimsical as what will go down at this week’s Adrenaline, and be able to keep fighting for not the fact of needing to, but the fact of wanting to. The fact that you cannot live without it, because you don't want to live without it. Finally persistence...having your mental game nearly torn to pieces less then a week away from your biggest match. Crawling out of the night, my title intact, only to have to walk straight into a Battle Royal with a title defense to boot. Having all of that thrown together, and making sure that I accomplish all of that. That is what I judge myself on, and currently, I have to say...I am getting a Passing Grade. Titles aside, that many of you might not recognize, are ones that I hold dear to my heart, and would rather give my life away before this. Perhaps the hardest battles I ever fought were for RUSH titles, and winning it made me the star that I am today. The RUSH Network Championship Title. One month, the bodies piling up as I made my way through the contenders and on the road towards the then-champion, Marvin Harris. A road to victory that I was not favored to win, as I was just a bleep on the radar. That's why it made it so special, that's why it made it so much more powerful to me, being the underdog coming out ahead of Marvin Harris? Creating one for myself in the process. What is the difference then? This is where I have to use the movement, this is where I have to make sure I stay on course, this is where the determination of not advancing falls even heavier on my shoulders. I have El Spiffy, a man who, after searching out information on him, seems to be a waste of mass in RUSH. I have seen what he possesses, and I will not lie to you and tell you that it isn't talent, because I wouldn't want to fall into the ditch that he has dug for himself. Spiffster, you have talent, but much of what you possess is unused and that is your mind. Do not try to trick or fool your opponents into thinking something, go ahead at them, straight into there eyes and beat them like that. Beat them because you are good enough, not because you are tricking them. If you are good enough. We will see in a few days, if your trickery will work, or if my movement will prevail. Spiffster, I have for you a warning that I hope you listen to, because if you don't, you will be in a matching bed, a matching gown and laying up beside Rich Cannon where you belong. Spiffy, do you know what I think it is, do you know what I think your problem is? Jealousy. You were tickled pink the day you came here, the day that you gave up and hoped that your wrestling skill would be enough to keep your legacy elevated above everyone else. You were happy, and excited...but then you found out about me. The bar had to be raised, and I vaulted over that bar. Spiff, the spotlight shifted past you without you realizing it, it hopped from your career, your past, to the present and the future. To me, you are green with envy over what I have done in RUSH and how I am better then you ever could be. You cannot stop me Spiffy, many have tried, and I will invite all of you to continue with your efforts. Because the most exciting part of having you on my ass, is watching you drop to your ass. If you wished to be remembered in RUSH for your achievements, then you have taken the wrong path, because now the only memory people will have when they hear the name El Spiffy, will the suffering and misery he felt when he fought Scott Cage. You deserve everything you will get Spiffster, and I will be happy to give it to you. Spiffster, that Syndicate Title is something that you don't have, it is something that nobody in the federation has, and it is something that no one else but me will ever achieve. In weeks I will add one other title to this famous collection and make it a triad. I will be the RUSH World Champion, and I will hold the Grand Slam Championship every wrestler wishes for. Your 15 minutes have almost run out Spiff, and when I am done, it will be as if they never existed. Spiffy, as it stands you are so jealous that you are nearly green...we will see come Sunday if that color will change to yellow. Sirius Cage: Scott...will you please stop with the contract? (With the familar voice of Sirius Cage, our scene opens up to he and Scott walking into their Godfather's Beverly Hills mansion…) Godfather Silvio: What card? Scott Cage: Oh...nothing. Godfather Silvio: Sirius? (Sirius shuts the door...and hangs his coat up...) Sirius Cage: Scott has it in his mind that he can purchase items on the 'sole' basis of James Williams's soul. Scott Cage: And it will still work... it's just that bitch at the jewelry store didn't have an open mind... Godfather Silvio: The jewelry store? Scott Cage: Yeah...I was going to buy you a present... Godfather Silvio: That was nice of you. Scott Cage: It would have been...damn fucking... Sirius Cage: Yes...well, I told Scott it wouldn't wo... Scott Cage: IT'LL WORK! Sirius Cage: No...it won't. Scott Cage: Why won't it? Sirius Cage: Scott, we've had this discussion... Scott Cage: When? When did we talk about this? Sirius Cage: Lets see...in the store, in the store again, yet again, in the street, at the airport, in the plane, in the taxi… Scott Cage: Now you're just being belligerent. Sirius Cage: I'M being belligerent? This coming from the man trying to purchase a diamond with a soul... Scott Cage: I'm going to punch your fucking face in you little- (Scott jumps out of the couch, and Godfather Silvio jumps to attention immediately...) Godfather Silvio: Boys...please, calm down. Maybe Sirius is right, and maybe Sirius is right... Scott Cage: Yeah...you're right. I'm sorry... Sirius Cage: Apology accept- Scott Cage: ...That you're wrong! Sirius Cage: ...pted. Extremely mature bro, extremely. Scott Cage: Takes one to know one! (Scott sticks out his tongue and gives Sirius the dreaded raspberry...while Godfather Silvio goes to the kitchen, grabs a drink...) Godfather Silvio: Here Scott, drink this. Scott Cage: I suppose... Godfather Silvio: Now Sirius… Sirius Cage: Yes, Godfather?Godfather Silvio: I believe you owe Scott an apology. Sirius Cage: WHAT? Scott Cage: HAHA! IN YOUR FAC... Godfather Silvio: Scott...you're next. Now, Sirius, I think the only way to end this is for you both to apologize. Sirius Cage: It seems logical...so I suppose I'll comply. Godfather Silvio: Good...Scott? Scott Cage: What? Godfather Silvio: Are you willing to apologize? Scott Cage: Sirius was the one that started this...I shouldn't have to apologize. Godfather Silvio: So you'll apologize...okay Sirius… (Scott sets his beer down, and stares at Sirius…) Sirius Cage: I suppose an apology is in order... Scott Cage: AH HA! Sirius Cage: ...yes. Now, I'm sorry, it is your turn. (Sirius sits back down...as Godfather Silvio turns to Scott…) Godfather Silvio: Scott? Scott Cage: ....Do I have to? Godfather Silvio: Yes. Sirius apologized, it's your turn... (Scott looks down at his beer...back to his Godfather...than takes a large drink from his bottle...) Scott Cage: ...i'm sorry... Sirius Cage: What was that? Scott Cage: I said I was sorry. Sirius Cage: What? Godfather Silvio: Sirius...act with some dignity... (Sirius hangs his head...as Scott grins…) Godfather Silvio: Now, are you two good? Scott Cage: Yeah, I guess… Godfather Silvio: Sirius? Sirius Cage: He needs me... Scott Cage: And WHAT is that supposed to mean? Godfather Silvio: Scott...drink your beer... (Scott shrugs, and tips his head back...while Godfather Silvio leans in towards Sirius…) Godfather Silvio: Sirius...the sooner you apologize, the sooner Scott will keep his mouth shut. Sirius Cage: Yes, Godfather. (Godfather Silvio smiles...as he leans back into his chair...) Godfather Silvio: Now...Sirius, are you and Scott good? Sirius Cage: Yes Godfather, I do believe the two of us are on good terms. Godfather Silvio: All right, and Scott, are you still mad at him? Scott Cage: Mad about what? Sirius Cage: Do you...do you see what I have to deal with? Godfather Silvio: So you aren't upset with your brother? Scott Cage: Was I? Godfather Silvio: No, no you weren't. Not the slightest. Scott Cage: Well all right then, what are we doing here? Let's get on the hunt...let's go spend some money! (Scott leaps out of his chair...and heads towards the door...) Scott Cage: Are you coming, bro? Sirius Cage: It depends, where are we going? Scott Cage: I'm kind of hungry...and I saw a really classy restaurant a little ways down. Care to come? Sirius Cage: I am a little hungry, actually… Scott Cage: Great! Godfather...you want to come? Godfather Silvio: No thanks Scott...I feel tired, so I think I'm just going to stay here and watch the television... Scott Cage: Suit yourself...let’s go Sirius. (Scott bows on one knee and kisses his Godfather’s hand, and Sirius grabs his coat...as the two walk out of the mansion. Fade out…)
