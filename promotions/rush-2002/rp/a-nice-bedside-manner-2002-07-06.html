A Nice Bedside manner

6 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6 July 2002 Rp1 Adrenaline 9 Andi awoke with a headache that could rival any earthquake. Slowly the big man pulled himself out of the bed he shared with his valet Fluffy. It was 7:30am two nights after the events on Mayhem where Andi retained his title only for his beloved friend and colleague to get hit by a chair that knocked her unconscious for a few moments. Andi sat up and felt his head swim a little as he rested his feet on the carpeted bedroom floor. The young wrestler was also feeling slight side effects form the chair shot he too took from Jason Jameson. “What’s wrong Andi you look pale?” Andi looked behind him to see the concerned look on Fluffy’s face. “Nothing just gone a little dizzy that’s all, nothing to worry about.” Fluffy sighed as she too slowly got out of bed, Fluffy in herself at that moment was feeling completely fine. So instead of Andi making the breakfast she decided that the best thing to do to help him out a little was to make the breakfast herself. “Andi you stay there and I will make the breakfast you need to rest a little!” The young magician just nodded as he slowly laid back down. The little rabbit women smiled as she looked at the man she loved. Casually she walked into the kitchen and grabbed the frying pan and dribbled a little oil into it and turned the electric cooker on before placing a few rashes of bacon in that she had retrieved from the fridge. Andi looked up at the ceiling wondering on what he would need to do on Mayhem he knew Syn deserved a shot and Scott but deep down he felt sure that he wanted the shot just as much as anyone else in that match. The other thing was he had three people in the ring that didn’t like him in the slightest. One of the men was none other than Semore Smoke, the man Andi had ridiculed and upset by helping Scott retain the NTICW World Title. Then there was Carl Cash who had made his feelings known in Brazil when he attacked him during his US title match against Jameson, and also gave as good as he got during there match last week. The final one was the one person he was sure he had put to sleep along time ago but no, Jason had to come and interfere in his match and not only assaulted him with a chair but his beloved valet Fluffy. Andi at that moment was unsure what he was going to do Jameson but one thing he was sure on was that he would know before he entered the ring. The young magician’s nose twitched with the smell of bacon as Fluffy walked in with his breakfast, how long he had been brooding for he was unsure but he was sure ready for his breakfast for after training he would be doing a little bit of research and then looking into the fact behind his little dreamy chat he had with Merlin.Andi delved into his breakfast unknowing that at that moment Fluffy herself was struggling to keep herself upright as a sharp pain exploded in her head only to pass a few seconds later by a dull ache then to nothing. The young women steadied herself, as she looked an Andi brooding as he has been doing so much as of late, really ever since he fainted in the kitchen last week. The young magician looked up and just managed to catch a glimpse of Fluffy looking at him with those deep black eyes of hers. Andi smiled as he watched her eating her lettuce and cumber with grated carrot sandwich. The day wore on rather quickly the training went well, but Fluffy continued to go sometimes violently dizzy as well as the massive booms to the head which would almost feel like being stood next to a large amplifier that had been turned up to full just as someone strummed a guitar at the same time. But still Fluffy managed to hide her pain from Andi but for how much longer she wasn’t sure because every time they had happened each one in turn gradually got harder and more violent. Andi himself was working flat out trying to work out where in hell this house Merlin was talking about was. After some deliberation they both decided that seen, as it was now very late they should head off home where Andi could continue to work from his laptop at home. The time was 2am in the morning Andi was still working hard at the computer to no avail and he himself was struggling to stay awake as continued to browse around the Internet trying to find some reference to the house. Fluffy herself was still struggling with the headache’s and dizziness but was still managing to hold off the pain until the last thing she heard was Andi’s voice saying “Come on know lets go to bed.” That was the last thing she heard before everything went dark. It had been several weeks since Fluffy had fallen ill and hospitalised buy the good doctors had worked wonders in removing the blood clot from the brain that she had sustained from the head injury while Andi was wrestling Carl Cash back for the US Title on what eventually was the penultimate Mayhem. On what turned out to be the final Mayhem, Andi had been called while he sat at his bed and told that there was no need for him to turn up at Mayhem that week which to say the least was very strange. So 8 O’Clock came surprisingly quickly for Andi as he settled down while holding Fluffy’s delicate hand. To say Andi was surprised would have been an understatement of the truth. But when he really thought about it he realised that Boda had been left with no choice seen as with one show the Ultimate Opposition had ripped Boda and Smoke apart. Boda had lost control leaving him with no option but to sell. Andi sat there as thought about his time and what he would do with his time in RUSH.
