Birth of a Starr: End of a Game, Prelude to a New One (part 3)

18 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18/6/02 At the age of 16, Joshua Dubois, the first Starr to grace the annals of modern history was already running a very successful trade in a profitable commodity. Utilizing the aids of agents who ranged from John and Jane Doe Jr. looking to make a quick one or two right up to members of janitorial and secretarial stuff, some with knowledge of what they were doing, some who were unwittingly playing parts for a bigger field playing one game and one commodity only… Information History denied him the honor and fame, or possibly infamy, of being the icebreaker in the field of information trade. However, it could be for the best: Look what infamy did to Capone. Joshua had done his history homework well. By spring break, Joshua Dubois had already amassed a four-figured fortune, something of a big deal in those days when inflation was less of a commonplace problem. Most of the financial part of his assets were divided and stored under a number of bank accounts under intricately fabricated identities, courtesy of local forgers who were paid handsomely enough to reproduce the quality merchandise needed to answer any probing questions. He was also in possession of a Ford Mustang convertible, which he kept in a rented garage across town, also under fabricated ownership. In another month’s time, he was close enough to playing a silent sponsor to Hartford High’s football team. He had more greenbacks than an honest tie-wearing worker in the American Dream could amass in a decade, he had physical assets and a network to mobilize and utilize. He even had toys for a “big boy”, a concept that hadn’t really materialized at the time that came in the form of an automobile and soon, a sports team. All of that didn’t set him apart from everybody else around him. There were still the local rich kids. But what really set him apart from everybody else Was the fact that he could proudly call them his own. “But with the amount he has, even if they were under other names, wouldn’t it be all too obvious that something was up with all that he was carrying around? I mean, he was of course merely a high-school boy at the time…” Ricky Starr stares down from a wooden bridge in a well-manicured park. The sun was bright, the grounds of the park bursting with life. There were children running around, there are many sitting on picnic blankets eating, talking or just plain basking in the pleasant atmosphere. Some are walking on the pathways provided with some enjoying the solitude, some enjoying the atmosphere with the company of others. Others are jogging and breaking out in sweat. Beside Ricky Starr on the bridge above the pleasant swan pond is the attractive lady, in a crisp business suit as always with a dictophone at hand. He speaks, the words directed at her but his attention was focused on the reflections in the pond. “Another lesson he taught me in life was every plan, no matter how careful, no matter how calculated, no matter how water-tight one can swear it to be. It is never 100% foolproof. Of course there have been people with their suspicions but as always, he also said that every plan needs a defense mechanism in place to minimize risk and deal with it as it comes…” On occasion there would be an anonymous letter or two slipped into the complaint box or found in the desk of the pastoral care desk. After being read by the head of pastoral care, it would be forwarded to a secretary who would know the procedure of letters of this magnitude, especially one regarding a problem with the student body. The school counselor would be the next recipient and usually a familiar name would appear in the contents of the letter. The counselor would deem it necessary for the letter to be sent up the chain of command to the secretarial desk of the principal’s office. However as there was the Delegation of Authority referendum last term, problems of this nature were re-directed to the student council. The letter would switch hands, or more like switch desks again to the student council mess hall. Although the president of the student body would usually deal with problems of this sort, it would always be referred to another subordinate desk. The Vice President of the Student Council with the chair being held by no other than Joshua Dubois himself. All of this may sound as complicated as the English parliamentary system but it was the easiest way to explain an integral part of his defense mechanism. The rest were made up by his long lists of agents who acted as his arms, playing parts such as dispensers, dealers, acquirers right down to the simple-minded “fall men”. He rarely handled the merchandise or the acquiring of it and never took part in a physical transaction when his business matured. His term breaks and holidays were usually spent idling away in leisure, something that he gained great pleasure from, frankly because of the fact that he thought it a break well earned after weeks upon weeks of good business. When other people his age were earning their summer keep by doing odd jobs of sorts, he was spending his hard-earned fortune much to the surprise of some and the chagrin of others. He spent it cautiously of course, lavishly but modest enough to avoid being the focus of unwanted attention, something which he learned to steer away from at all costs since day one. “So how did you find all that? Doesn’t it befuddle your mind just thinking of how he came up with each piece to fit into the labyrinth he calls a business plan?” They continue journeying down the park with the sun glistening along the pond beside them. The lady eagerly awaits a response from Ricky Starr who still seems to be sipping on his coffee. As he casually savors a sip of the warm liquid, he looks at his attractive companion with a smile. “You know how some people say “Wanting something bad enough to kill”?” The lady looked at him with interest as if expecting Ricky to reveal something intimate, something shrouded in shadows along the narrative. “Yes, what about it?” Ricky casually glances at her and quickly replies. “He had enough sense not to take it literally” Come high-school graduation day, Joshua Dubois found himself the smiling recipient of a diploma that literally didn’t mean jack to him besides the fact that it showed he actually wasted valuable time doing something else rather than business. Being Vice President of the student council and a highly esteemed member among his countless peers, he had to make a graduation speech. Seeing what he does and his true character, one would expect a surprise or at least a break in tradition as he stepped up to the stage to deliver as an orator. He knew when not to go against the old ways. Instead he did what everybody expected, delivered some insincere hooey about “A Brave New World” out there with each and every one of you potential McBoys and Girls actually getting a shot at the real world. How everybody can be the best they can when the best they can hope to get would be a week’s worth of employment at the local sweatshop. It was sweet sounding bullshit but it was bullshit nonetheless. It all ended with a whole lot of clapping and cheering followed by the appalling excuse for a president going up to make his speech followed by more people of remote importance getting a chance to say what they want to say on the boomstick before some small festivity known as the cap throwing ceremony or something along those lines. Yes, it was a graduation indeed and surprisingly for Joshua Dubois, little did he know that it was literally a new world out there for him, a new field to place new pitches and start the game afresh. The old one had many a good memory but this was the real world, a place where he would be put to the test for real. Little did he know too of the foreboding worldwide event that would crank the game he played so well up to a whole new level. The face of America itself was yearning for change The youth were on fire The year was 1968 “So what was in store next for Joshua Dubois” The sun was setting on the horizon as both of them wait as if a couple at the park gates. Ricky Starr looks onto the panoramic landscape before looking back at his companion with a charming smile that usually said more than words “Like that quote, these are words I shall save for another time…” Or something along those lines…
