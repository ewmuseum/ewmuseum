Mandated Suicide.. (Part 1)

Date: NOW He had literally lost it all. From becoming one of NTICW's rising if not best star the company had to display, but since he had transferred over to RUSH his rank had been falling, being quickly beaten by a German-Suplex pin via. Andi the Mangificant in his debut match... Now he goes up against a man who like him is a partial rookie to the RUSH atmosphere. Although, Andi the Magnificant has been recently in the 'way' of John McBride's work he'll just have to bring his deteriorating rank to a higher level, with the odds up against him.. Now, not knowing which schizophrenic side had reigned for the stagnant days.. It was a dark afternoon; somewhere in Hackensack, New Jersey upon a high-rise building was a silhouetted character. It was raining viciously; thunder thudded the area, as the lightning fluttered from time-to-time, also being the only source of light to the rooftop of the high-rise building. The character, identified as a male was standing at a rigid position, atop of a concrete platform.. The masculine figure had his arms pursed to his thighs as sudden light came from the street bulbs beginning to regain it's energy as the storm became more and more serene as the figure began to be unveiled.. It was.. John McBride His head had been drooped down, as he stood there rebuking down about 1,000 feet below, as the rain that had once fallen began to dry.. It looked like an act of suicide but John began to speak to himself, the schizophrenic stage had transformed in to the dark, amnesiac persona. He had been profusely speaking to himself in a rather bare tone, but it was captured at the right time as he began.. 'Why..?' thought John to himself, as no one knew what he was targeting, what his opposition might've been? Burdening his speech were cars, screeching down the roadway as he stood within the center of a bustled city, entirely filled with people.. He continued in a low-key tone.. 'Why are they doing this to me..?' believed John to himself as the cars below had came to a stop, making stillness once again as the storm came to a haste start.. Rain poured downward, thunder roared, and lightning struck, in to a close range of John who had been propped near a satellite antenna.. It was like nothing was there, and if it were to be completely silent. He continued.. 'Do I have a reason..? Why do I continue to carry on?' catechized John within himself as he stopped for a moment, taking a look down as abrupt sirens started to ring out.. As John looked down he saw seniors pointing him out, as he still remained calm. Cop vehicles pulled up in front of the high-rise, as a horde of officers were seen getting out, running beneath John, in to the entry door.. Nothing stopped him.. So he went on.. 'I have no reason to live. Why should this be me here?' queried John to himself, as he began stepping closer of the peak of the roof, on the verge of falling.. The rooftop door blew open, officers rushed in, John was not rattled by the locus but continued as he concluded with.. 'GOODBYE'.. He stepped closer, nearing his right foot off the edge, as his left came at the same measurement.. He went for a sadistic jump but the officers had leaped to where he was, seizing him with all their arms, dragging him to the floor as he remained simple and discreet, wet from the rain that had dropped but sent a point to himself..
