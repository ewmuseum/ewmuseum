Prelude to a narrative

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26/4/02 “My inspiration for war comes from the late George Patton, it was never about dying or taking the fall for your ideals and those you hold dear… It’s all about taking the other bastard down for his” --Joshua Dubois or better known as Josh The Starr At least a chimpanzee has something you don’t have Mr. Cage. One foot still firmly grounded in reality Give or take a tail. “Two scotches on me good man and make it snappy” Lachlan McQueen, or better known as Lochie the Bung due to the size of his forehead plucked a seat on the counter area of the pub. It was a packed house, probably due to the fact that it’s the TGIF crowd rushing in from all angles, giving a flying sod about nothing else but their liquor. Quite an amazing sight actually, literally hours and hours worth of hard-earned greenbacks changing hands in the blink of an eye, faster than any Superman DC Comics can come up with. Pretty scary you know, the rate these bastards drink. I’d bet good money that more than half of these hearty sods are Irish. It’s really as if distilled alcohol was their nourishment, their life, their religion. Sometimes it can get a good person thinking, what motivates these loyal drinkers? Is it the kick that comes with a good pint? Is it part of the culture? Is life really that bad to make one want to escape? Is it truly the solution to all of life’s problems? But then again, one could understand. Life out in the rat race wasn’t really much of the hype they used to push so much at them high school career days huh? The silent questions are being thrown in now, Why didn’t I become an artist? Why did I take accounting? Why didn’t I follow my heart? Why, oh Why oh Why? Then come the “What-ifs”. What-if it all went the other way? What-if left instead of right? What-if this? What-if that? They call it alternate history A more appropriate term would be pondering spilt milk. Would it still be the same outcome? Or would the world as we know it now be a completely different picture altogether? Lachlan McQueen finishes both scotches in two quick downs. As painful as it was to him, he was no different from the scores of other world-weary individuals around him drowning their sorrows in liquor. What would his hardworking Presbyterian father say about this? “Nothing” Lachlan blurted out, “Bloody well nothing” as he downed signaled for another scotch with his Scottish accent coming out more and more as his level of intoxication increases. As the night goes on and on with heads popping in and out from the ever-lively pub, Lachlan faithfully sits on his stool, his head resting on the counter, his left hand nursing another glass of scotch. Between the occasional drooling and incoherent speech, one could actually hear some sense being spoken out. “I shoulda…I shoulda been a musician…I shoulda told ma’ and da’ to sod off…I shoulda…” In the world we all live, breathe and know, Lachlan is a drone in his employers accounting department, taking the 9-to-5 routine with scores and scores of other Americans like him with the paycheck that under-compliments his work. Could it be that in another world far stranger or quite similar to ours that Lachlan would be cruising in a million dollar automobile and signing a deal with the top country music record label? Or could it be entirely different from that altogether? “One more scotch for a pissed man up the front here”, Lachlan echoed out as his voice was drowned off by the voices of so many others.. Just like him.
