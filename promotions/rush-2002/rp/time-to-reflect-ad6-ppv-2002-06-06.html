Time to reflect (AD6 & PPV)

6 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-06-02 OOC- Sorry about the shortness of this rp. I have time constraints, e.g. coursework, part-time employment. I promise though to improve my work rate for Ignition. Oh yeah, read and enjoy. (Voice of Livewire) Over recent weeks I have let the trials and tribulations of my personal life affect my professional career, my wrestling career. Wrestling is my bread and butter, the profession that pays me the big cheques and recently my personal life has taken the edge off my performance in the wrestling ring. It is because of this that I have decided to sever my ties with Fiona for the mean time and put thoughts about an impending wedding to the backburner. In the last month I have been told my parents like Fiona and approve of the idea of marriage, her sister doesn’t and thinks Fiona isn’t ready while Fiona’s little cousin tells me after much bribing that Fiona finds me annoying and would never marry me in a million years or for a million Oreo’s!! His words not mine. Anyway now that I have put my personal life behind me it’s time to focus on wrestling again and more notably two triple threat matches in the next successive weeks. In the first this week on Adrenaline I get the opportunity to prove that my win over Scott Cage only a few weeks ago wasn’t a fluke while I have the task of proving that my loss to Kareem Olsen on the very first Adrenaline of the new dawning was exactly that, a fluke. This is a match where I can stamp a significant impact in the Network title division, by beating in Scott Cage a man who many regard as possibly the next Network champion and in Kareem Olsen somebody who was actually once ranked above me in the RUSH rankings. I mean can you actually believe that, that Haiti banana-munching goon was actually once seen as a better wrestler than me? Absurd I know, but this week on Adrenaline I get a chance to put both Cage and Olsen to the sword and prove to everybody that Britains finest export is in the mood to become the number contender to the Network title at Ignition. Speaking of Ignition, that is the event at where I will pit my wits in another triple threat match against Dark Haze and Allan Novakayne for the right to be the number one contender to the Network title. This match could potentially become my biggest match in Rush. I mean I have been in a number one contender to the Network title match before. In fact I was placed in one in only my second Rush match against J. Rish. Needless to say that I was screwed out of the opportunity by Kareem Olsen. Olsen interfered in the match by attacking Rish therefore granting Rish the win via disqualification. I usually wouldn’t be too bothered to see Olsen kick two shades of s**t out of Rish, I mean it’s the lesser of two evils, but when kicking the hell out of Rish leads to me losing my match and the chance to become number contender to the Network title, then I am unhappy. After losing three matches in the space of only two weeks I lost a hell of a lot of my confidence. I know, that’s one to tell your grandchildren..Livewire actually once lost his confidence, but it’s true. It was during a time when I often wondered….am I really the man to take RUSH wrestling forward? Am I really good as I say I am? I was in one of the top four federations in professional wrestling and I couldn’t get a win, I was distraught and could see my career plummeting faster than the U.S.A out of the FIFA World Cup. However something clicked, I picked up my first win against Scott Cage, followed that up with another win a week later and now all those questions can be answered. Of course I am the man to take RUSH wrestling forward, of course I am as good as I say I am. In fact I’m better, and I’ll prove that by beating Dark Haze and Allan Novakayne to become the number one contender to the Network title. Oh, and what if I do win the triple threat match Ignition. What possible match in the future does it present? I would be taking on the winner of the demolition derby contest, the winner being the man who will either be crowned the new Network champion or the man who will retain the Network title. So what if it is retained? What if Marvin Harris still is the Network champion? I respect Marvin Harris, I respect him a lot. He was the first ever Network champion and has held the title for many weeks. He has also tagged with me and won successfully. In that sense, a winning sense Marvin Harris is unsurpassed in RUSH. However, unlike Cage I don’t need to use dirty tactics and go past the means of wrestling to beat my opponent. No instead I settle all my issues in the wrestling ring, and in the wrestling ring I am in charge of my own destiny. That is why, even though I respect Harris I don’t fear him. Harris has yet to be challenged in the Network title division, in Livewire he will meet that challenge. Oh and what about if Marvin Harris doesn’t retain? What about if Scott Cage is victorious at Ignition and becomes the new Network champion? If that happens then I am a happy man. If you have understood anything that I have said then you will know that I do not fear Cage. I have beaten him before and I will beat him again on this weeks Adrenaline. A Scott Cage title reign would be like a candy bar, short and sweet, and just like taking candy from a baby I would easily take the Network title from Scott Cage. Now is a time to focus, now is a time to concentrate, now is a time to make my own destiny. Two triple threat matches, two very different goals. One to stamp authority the other to take the next step up the ladder towards title gold. Can Livewire defeat Kareem Olsen and Scott Cage this week on Adrenaline? Can Livewire beat Dark Haze and Allan Novakayne at Ignition to become the number one contender to the Network title? Only time will tell but it certainly two weeks of high importance to the Lively one. It is now that he must step up to the proverbial plate, but can he? Enough questions time for answers, and the first of those questions will be answered this Thursday on Adrenaline. Only then will we know that all this hype about a young impressionable man from England is true. Take care Livewire-ites.
