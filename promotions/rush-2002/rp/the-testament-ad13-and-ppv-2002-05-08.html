The Testament (Ad13 and PPV)

8 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/08/02 Dedicated to Stan Lee and the greatest freedom fighter in the world of comic fiction. Definition That’s what this world is all about right? Having to define everything we see, touch, taste, smell…basically everything our senses both conscious and occasionally, submerged, come across. In times long gone and passed, men of knowledge would balk at this, saying that it was wishful thinking to define everything and anything, reason being that the world was too big to encompass everything. In this day and age however… That is a different scenario altogether In this day and age, the world has become a much more familiar place, even to those so distant from the mark. It has become so much smaller despite the size of landmass. With the help of business entities such as IBM, AT&T and Time Warner, this greatly accelerated transformation has changed the world, as we know it completely. Knowledge is only a click away Pilgrims journeying with nothing more than their eyes Sending and receiving in a matter of seconds. Life has truly moved to the fast lane. And this is where the word Definition comes in Definition did not begin with the beginning of language It begin when two people sat in front of a glass filled to the halfway mark with water. Half empty? Half full? There is no such thing as the right answer… However, there are such things as stupid answers Just like there are no such things as stupid questions, merely wrong questions. Although one could spend a day trying to define everything around them, we shall to define one thing that has been in the minds of many craniums. It is what makes us truly human and subservient at the same time. Another one of life’s contradictions and truths. Something everybody has learned to live with and even appreciate with time. It is no other than… Respect What is Respect? Unlike loyalty, Respect can be fickle or made of stronger stuff. Does one have to give respect to get respect? Not necessarily, there can be times when one is compelled to give respect just because it is HIM or HER. Anybody who’s truly the next best thing since sliced bread sure deserves unconditional respect, do they? Respect can be mutual; respect can only be a one-way street with no return ticket at times. Unlike love, Respect may not be unconditional. Respect can be coerced. Stalin, Chairman Mao, Idi Amin, Papa Doc, Ghadaffi, Saddam… They beat, shot, raped and practically choked the respect out of their respective subjects. Yet Respect is still Respect and the point is they got it. Like an omnipotent God ruling over this world Like worshippers to a temple They gave them respect and that’s what counts Regardless of the depravity, blood and lives taken in the process. Hey, nobody ever said that respect was all for the saints. Moving on, a few can go the extra step and move beyond respect, dwarfing it as he next phase makes or breaks an individual. This one is for the gipper’ Everybody dreams it but not everybody gets it The stuff of dreams… Glory They say that I shall never attain such an honor, such a prize or to be more accurate, such a feeling in my life. I have not attained it as of yet and the odds seem to be against me once again. Desire and Reality are two different things altogether; one does not always manifest itself in the other. Yet somehow, one out of the two kept me going through life, dragging me out of a bloodstained land to what I could describe at the time as a “Utopia of Opportunity”. It made me the greatest protégé and a heir to a fortune. Although this is strike three, it could turn out three times the charm. To turn my back on an opportunity, a chance like this would be like shooting myself in the leg after all these time of strife, disheartening and continuous failures. At the very least, if I should collapse and die now, I can say that I was free from the bleak destiny that awaited me before. I am free from the trappings of what others expected me to be, which was most likely another casualty, another statistic in an ugly black book. To be free from those who taught they were in control of everything. To be free from reckless and irrational thinking. To be free from those who sought to hate my kind, deep-rooted prejudices. To be free those who were to put bullets in my body, like the fate of my father and my mother. My name is a summary of those freedoms Ricky Starr I think all of you should know my real name, Remus Slatkovic. I don’t think it should be hidden forever in the mists of time and the locked box of Denial. Remus Slatkovic was the sad boy who stood in the plains of Bosnia, looking out into the horizon where trees once grew, tanks soon rolled and red-capped men marched. Remus Slatkovic was the boy who saw all around him hopelessness and weakness. Oppression was the order for the day, for each and every day of his life. This was the lifetime that Remus was born into, his destiny, his Fate. Ricky Starr escaped that. He was strong enough to brave whatever the new world had to throw at him. He was surrounded in wealth that Remus could only dream of attaining, let alone imagine the shadows of it. Confident as rooster, walking the limelight as one would walk underneath the sun, creating his own opportunities and dictating his own future. This was, is and will always be Ricky Starr. When I entered the business that would bring my name to fame, I thought it would just go wham, bam, and time for paycheck. Just the same thing night after night, one fight and that’s it. That was how wrong I was then. I learned the truth when I fought my first match. As I looked into the eyes of the downed man, I saw pain but I did not see agony. In a minute, he leaped to his feet and we were at it once more. I knew then that we were never really fighting. We were just hurting each other for a paycheck then. We were given the freedom to shit-beat the opposition for the night, we were given the freedom to fill up the timeslot, we were given the freedom to lose – but never to fight. The fight I face now, the fight for glory, yes, that’s what it is, the fight for something they say I could never attain in a lifetime. The fighting now was not just limited to a timeslot consisting of a few minutes in primetime. It was also not restricted to the physical phenomenon that many perceive it to be. It was never just about bone, cartilage and blood. It is also a battle of minds, a battle of ideal. Some never bothered to think at all, just throwing punches, kicks and grabbing whenever they had the chance. And so they become nothing but puppets or at the very best, machinations at the end while some even have other, often lesser men to do the thinking for them. This is the reality of it all. The reality of the situation. The reality of more than one pair of eyes. My reality. If there was a way to win it without having to go through the fisticuffs and physical struggle, I would, everybody would have done it a long time ago. However, nobody has the right to just take that feeling and hog it for themselves. Earning it just makes it better.Thankfully, we have the freedom to make mistakes, to see the errors both technical and philosophical, to correct ourselves, re-light the fire and restart the engine… And go back on the road again. Hey, I never said that it was a straight, paved road. There will be many pitfalls, false leads, crossroads, junctions and others on the road as well. Some with intentions on the same lines, some with aims far more different and alien. Nevertheless one must keep an aim in mind when traversing this strange road. Someone else holds the glory now, holding on to it as tightly as he can but nothing lasts forever. He would most likely know that as well for he seems to be the rational, somewhat intelligent sort. No matter what the outcome, I shall never despair nor shall I take any positive result in too much haughtiness and pride. For there shall always be a next time. Then again, I have no business thinking so far Seven days to the mark and a dance with Dante Jackson in between. Living life for the moment does not sound like too much of a bad idea for now. 'They have Ohhhh what a feeling, but we have Ohhhh What a Rush!' -- The Legion of Doom on the Orient Express
