CrossRoads Chronicles #8: El Spiffy!

15 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8.15.02 “One person with a belief is equal to a force of ninety-nine who only have interest.”--John Mill I am on a quest now, a voyage that will only be ended when I am awarded the dubious distinction of holding a crown that only one other has held before me. I will not stop until I hold something close to my heart that I can feel is worth the pain and the frustration that I have felt for so long. This quest will not be fulfilled until I prove to myself, to RUSH, to the Executives, to the Champions, to the onlookers from a far and the front row watchers. To the people that hate me and all who love me, to the people who don't believe in me, to the people who do believe in me. To the people who just don't give a damn about wrestling at all, to everybody in the whole world. My quest is one that doesn't only become complete with victory, no...it becomes complete when I am complete. When I am known as the greatest to ever compete in the sport and the sound of my name brings jealousy to the heart of competitors. I want to not only be known as the pinnacle, I want to be the pinnacle, the apex, the first man to become the RUSH Grand Slam Champion. An accolade that any man on the planet would love to boast about, but only a possibility could do. I will be the first, as well as the only one. That is when my quest will be complete, that is when my Wrestling Career will be fulfilled. But will I stop? Don't bet on it. My career, like records was made to be broken...I await the man to break me. I suppose that makes you next El Spiffy, that makes you have the next chance to Break me. Would you like to know something Spiffy, something that might tell you a bit about me, as well as give you a slight warning in a sense. The greatest day in my wrestling life, was the also the worst one. How? Because I could learn from it Spiffy, because I could use it to my advantage and become even better at what I do. The first Adrenaline was the home of the Network Title Battle Royal. That match was something special, as Marvin Harris and I battled with everything we had, with everything we could. Until I was tossed out by Harris, and he won the match. I got outsmarted by a smart man, and that is where the learning came. I got defeated and I was angry. I couldn't believe that I had let someone, even the calibur of Marvin Harris defeat me. But that was when I realized, that I hold the key to my destiny, not somebody else, not Marvin Harris, not Jeremy Howard, not Boda... ME! That was when I realized that the only way to stay in control of the game, is to stay in control of the ball. The mind, Spiffy, the mind is where the power lies, and I was beaten because I was outsmarted. The day that I did not come out of Adrenaline with the Network Title, was the day that I knew what I had to do to win. Think. Mind Games, playing with people's heads and emotions, and it works Spiffy, it works very well. So now, up against you, a man who obviously enjoys trying to play with ones mind, I have come to a standstill. What I do hope for is the cunning naivete that comes along with being the famous El Spiffy!. I look forward to this Spiffster, for you are something that I am not used to, you are different from what I am used to fighting, and most definitely I am different from what you are used to fighting. Change is nice isn't it, Spiffy? Remember I know you Spiffy, I watch and pay attention, and I know you aren't used to losing, I know you are used to being on top, to winning. Like I said, change is nice. I hope you enjoy it. (The camera fades into an extremely expensive jewelry store...) Sirius Cage: Now Scott, what is the purpose of us being here? Scott Cage: Well...I want to get Godfather Silvio something really special. Sirius Cage: And just how do you propose to pay for that? Scott Cage: With my new contract…Sirius Cage: What new contract? Scott Cage: Well...it isn't... Sirius Cage: Scott...did you steal something? Scott Cage: Steal? Well...technically, I suppose. Sirius Cage: Scott...what are you talking about? (Scott sticks his hand into his pocket...and pulls out...) Sirius Cage: Scott, this looks like something a child would make in elementary school. Explain yourself... Scott Cage: All right...so I was watching Billy Graham... Sirius Cage: The televangalist? Scott Cage: Yeah...beside the point. Anyway...I was watching it, and well...they said that the soul was worth more than anything in the world...so I got to thinking... (Sirius grabs the paper from Scott's hand...) Sirius Cage: You own James Williams's soul? Scott Cage: It's priceless... Sirius Cage: Scott, I don't like the idea of you using our face commentator's soul for your comedic entertainment... Employee: Can I help you? Sirius Cage: I'm sorry...we were just leaving and-Scott Cage: I'd like to purchase your largest diamond. Sirius Cage: No he wouldn't...he has no forms of money... Scott Cage: You'll have to excuse my young brother...he's been drinking… (Scott winks to the employee...and she winks back...) Employee: Understood sir. Scott Cage: Now...about that diamond.. Employee: Ah yes, it's in the back...let me go get it... (The employee walks back to the storeroom, and Sirius hits Scott across the back of the head...) Scott Cage: Ow! What was that for? Sirius Cage: I feel I can ask the same... Scott Cage: Oh...the alcoholic thing..can't you take a joke? Sirius Cage: A joke? Do I look like Livewire? Scott Cage: No? Sirius Cage: Exactly. Now I want you to stop making up lies about myself, and stop with this whole fiasco about using Williams's soul as money. Scott Cage: All right… Employee: Your diamond sir... (The employee places a box on the glass counter, and opens it up...) Scott Cage: Whoa. I'll take it. Employee: All right sir...let me just go and ring this up... Scott Cage: Thanks... Sirius Cage: Scott, I wish you not to make a fool out of yourself... Scott Cage: How am I going to do that? Sirius Cage: Right now Nathanial, I'm not quite sure. But, I can imagine you will soon show me... (Scott brushes off Sirius...as the store employee walks back from the register...) Employee: Alright sir...your total comes up to be exactly ten thousand dollars. Scott Cage: I'll charge it miss... Employee: Will that be Discover, Mastercard, Visa? Scott Cage: A Soul. Employee: Excuse me? Sirius Cage: Scott...let's go... Scott Cage: Did you know that the soul is worth more than anything in the world?Employee: No...I did not sir. Scott Cage: Well then...see, this contract uses that soul to pay for my expenses. Employee: I...I don't think we can accept payments of the soul, we'll need cash. Sirius Cage: I told you Scott...now let us leave before we suffer more of an embarrassment... (The employee looks at the card and begins to laugh...as Sirius can only shake his head in embarrassment...) Employee: I'll make you a deal... Scott Cage: You will? Sirius Cage: YOU WILL? Employee: Yeah...I'll let you walk out of this store, and I won't show this video at the office Christmas party... Sirius Cage: That sounds like a deal...come Scott. Scott Cage: You know what...I'm going to leave. But ONLY because this diamond looks horrible... (Scott slams his fist on the counter, grabs his contract...and leaves the store...) Sirius Cage: I'm extremely sorry for all of that. I don't know what has come over him...it truly is an exquisite diamond... (The two storm out...as the camera cuts to the streets...) Scott Cage: What a bitch... Sirius Cage: I told you it would not work Scott.Scott Cage: It would have worked...if I could get someone to help me that was open minded.. Sirius Cage: Whatever. Now where Scott? Scott Cage: We're going back to the hotel, so I can grab something and leave you there… Sirius Cage: Sounds good to me. (The camera fades…)
