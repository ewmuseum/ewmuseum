It's my birthday, and I'll cry if I want to

24 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24th August 2002 The lights in the eerie hallway were fading, bringing darkness down and darkening everything in sight to a much more dull and unwelcoming shade. This was no deterrent, however, to the young man carrying two arm fulls of paper bags; filled to the brim with various goodies and treats. It seemed as though something had been planned, something spectacular. The look on the mans face was that of sheer excitement and anticipation for what was about to come. He forced his step into a much slower pace, obviously wanting to savour the following moments for all they were. A broad smile spreading across his happy face as the key entered into the lock on his hotel room door. The surprise was imminent, exhilaration pumping through his veins like some sort of drug. He couldn’t take it anymore, he couldn’t stand trying to milk it for what it was worth. he squeezed his eyes shut as tightly as he could and turned the key, pushing the wooden door open with all his might. As it crashed against the nearby table, Joe Bananas quickly opened his eyes. The darkness within his room was overwhelming as he turned back to close the door behind. He knew that the shock would be unbearable, but he had to take it. He positioned the brown paper bags on the floor and waited. He waited for a long time, it seemed too long; surely they knew he was here. “Hello?” Joe asked the darkness daintily. There was no answer, not even the feint sound of breathing. Joe screwed his furrow and swung round, flicking the switch and brining the light bulbs that hung from the ceiling to life. He looked again, still no sign of anybody. there were no streamers, balloons, part hats or even something that remotely resembled a present. “Whas goin on?” Joe asked himself, half hoping that it was just a trick and they would jump out at any moment. Joe began to meander through the furniture, looking underneath for any signs of life. there was nothing at all, just the same old hotel room he had slept in, and awoken to, this morning. Nothing different at all, not even a sign making best wishes was pinned to the walls. “Ah sure Ah invited dem, why wouldn’t dey come?” Joe asked himself, his eyes still darting around the room. His face becoming slowly sadder by the moment, Joe stomp around his room, his final check finding absolutely nothing. The cold truth was coming to him now, nobody had came to his pre-arranged and planned surprise birthday party! He even checked the calendar to see if the date was correct, and unfortunately it was, it was indeed his birthday. No mistakes at all, he had not even received any excuses from those he had invited. He had made the invitation cards on a computer, putting adorable images of bunny rabbits and puppy dogs along with a large flattering photograph of himself asking every member of the RUSH roster if they would attend his fake surprise birthday party. And not one of them, not a single person, had turned up. “Dem trifling fools, not one of dem came ta meh birthday bash?” Joe reaffirmed to himself, a lacing of venom in his voice. Joe marched over, back to his paper bags and set them comfortably on his bed. He grabbed them both by the base and tipped them upside down, allowing the contents to fall safely to the comfort of his divan. He took from the pile a green pointed party hat and attached to his head, hooking the string onto his chin. He tried to smile, but couldn’t keep it up. His lips turned to the floor and his eyes heavier. “Don’t worry lad, you don’t need friends like them anyway!” Said a little raspberry. “Yeah, you’ve got us guvner!” Chimed a small strawberry. Joe looked down at the two bag fulls of fruit. The feint sight of a smile began to emerge as he realised he did have friends after all. “Ah love you guys!” Said Joe, his voice full of emotion. “Ok, don’t come on too strong. People might talk, ya know?” Explained a shining red apple. Joe chuckled along with the rest of the fruit. This birthday wouldn’t be so bad after all, he was with the people he understood the most after all. He turned on the music and began to dance, the party continuing late into the night, Joe eventually falling asleep to the fading sounds of the ‘Bob Marley’s Greatest Hits’ album and the friendly banter of his fruitful friends.
