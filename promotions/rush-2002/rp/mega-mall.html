'Mega Mall...'

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today Mega-Mall 2000. The largest mall in five galaxies, right here on our very own planet Earth. Whee. It's a mall so large that it boasts its own gondola from one side to the other for the serious shopper (tickets run $2 for children 12 and under, $4 for adults, $3 for senior citizens and $8 if your name happens to be Lou). It's a mall so damned big it has three zip codes. And a Starbucks. 'I f~cking hate malls,' grumbles The New F~cking Legend Stephen Blood, clad in his leather pants and jacket, Stephen Blood t-shirt (the one that says 'Bloodweiser' on the front and 'Cold, Tough, and Ready To Kick Your Ass!' on the back) and his skull and roses bandanna. Stacey Blitsch is wearing her leather bell bottoms and red cutoff shirt as she clutches his hand. 'This place is huge! After the signing, we totally have to power shop, honey!' Stephen curls his top lip into a sneer. 'I f~cking HATE malls,' he growls. The Innovator Brock Newbludd is clad in his Harley jacket, baggy jeans and Blood Brothers t-shirt. 'Our first autograph session as a tag team in over a year. I'm pretty psyched. Still--why couldn't it be at a bar or something? Don't they know that the '2000' craze is over anyway and has been for the last two years, and besides don’t they know you’re retired?' There is a Heineken delivery guy who is walking with a dolly full of 12-packs of bottled Heineken towards one of the in-mall restaurants. As the four pass by the beer delivery guy, Stephen flings a quarter across the guy's path. As the beer guy stops to pick it up, Stephen quickly grabs a cold twelve pack of Heineken from the stack on the dolly, takes a can out, cracks it open and throws down a mouthful. 'I REALLY f~cking hate malls,' he mutters, wiping his mouth on the back of his sleeve. Lindsey Francis walks alongside Brock, wearing a black leather miniskirt and a t-shirt that reads, 'Rocked By Brock'. 'Okay,' she says as she unfolds a floor plan of Mega Mall 2000. 'The autograph table is just past the Orange Julius and right before The Pocket Protector, fashionwear for the fashionable nerd.' Stephen pounds the rest of the can and tosses the empty container over the railing, ignoring the cry of the child whose head it lands on. 'A clothing store devoted to nerds. I f~cking hate malls on the sub-atomic level,' he growls. 'Look at all of this,' Stacey says, looking around with a big smile as she squeezes Stephen's hand. 'The children--the celebration--the smells--the families--it's all so. . . gay, isn't it?' 'That's one way of putting it,' Stephen mutters, cracking and slamming another beer. Brock reaches into his back pocket and pulls out the program, unfolding it and reading it. 'Hey, DUDE!! We're sharing a table with Alice Cooper!!' 'I guess I can live with it if we're sharing a table with Alice, then,' Stephen says. He stops in front of the Kay-Bee Toy store and grins. 'Hey, as long as we're in this toilet, let's go in here.' They all walk in, past the Britney dolls, past the robotic doggies, past the Flower Ranger figures to a recently assembled action figure display. 'I don't believe it!' Brock exclaims. 'The newest wave of Rush action wrestlers!” Stephen sets his empty beer can in the maw of a giant tyrannosaurus head. 'Hey, awesome. Nice detail on Chris Matthews.” Brock's eyes almost pop out of his head when he sees the figure he's been looking for, Wrestlers and Managers wave 2, Brock Newbludd with Stephen Blood, Stacey Blitch and Lindsey Francis-4-Pack. 'Holy $hit! This figure's sweet! And they even gave me a pipe wrench for my weapon!' Stephen spots his, from the same wave as Brock's. ' They gave me that folding chair I always used in PCW matches. Boy, those guys were real b!tches about not being able to stand our level of extremeness, huh?' 'No doubt,' Brock says in a daze, still looking at his figure. 'They even got Lindsey's nipple prints on her shirt,' he mumbles in awe. 'Extremeness?' Stacey asks with a raised eyebrow. 'Yeah, extremeness,' Stephen answers. 'As in what Chris Matthews can't handle Brock’s level of. Chris prances around like the Canadian buffon he is, thinking he’s big shit with that belt.” 'Right on,' Brock says in agreement. 'I mean, he is a ok guy though.' 'True,' Stephen says. 'Unfortunately, he's in the opposite corner, so you have to kick the $hit out of him.' 'Boy, that sucks,' Brock says. 'I hate whipping ass on wrestlers I can stand.' 'Hey, I got an idea,' Stephen says, cracking another beer and taking a swallow. 'After the match, we'll score some smelling salts to wake Matthews up and invite him out drinking with us.' Brock smiles and nods. 'That'd be really nice of us, wouldn't it?' Stephen nods. 'Yeah. Let's do that.' Brock takes his figure and Stephen's up to the counter and digs out his wallet. He looks behind the counter at the video games and his jaw drops again. 'Holy $hitballs! They have the new Rush: Xtrememania for PS2!' 'The one THQ did?' Stephen asks. 'Hell yeah!' Brock answers, gesturing toward the display case so the clerk will get it for him. 'Cage matches, 5 on 5, random weapon on a pole matches. . . plus we're in this one.' 'I know,' Stephen says as the four exit the toy store. 'I read the company email, I’m a hidden wrestler.' 'What company email?' Brock asks. Stephen blinks and takes another swig of beer. 'Never mind.' 'Hey,' Stacey says, peeking her head inside a women's clothing store. 'Let's stop in here.' 'Is it Victoria's Secret?' Brock asks. 'No,' Lindsey answers. 'It's Lady's Day, a whole clothing line devoted to the-' 'Then there's no f~cking way we're going in there,' Stephen says. 'I know,' Stacey tells him before giving him a kiss on the cheek. 'I was just keeping you on your toes.' 'Right.' Stephen mutters, taking another drink. They walk past a few more stores--actually, The Blood Brothers do most of the walking while dragging their chicks away from the stores--until reaching the stage. Sure enough, several folding tables sit with pens and pictures sitting atop. Stacey and Lindsey unfold two chairs and position them behind Brock and Stephen's chairs so they can still be with their men and stay out of the way of the masses. Stephen slams the half-empty twelve pack of Heineken on the table, drapes his jacket over his chair and plops down on his ass. 'I am so not in the mood for any of this $hit,' he moans. 'You're flaking out again, I can tell,' Brock points out, taking his coat off and putting it on the back of his chair before sitting. Stephen shoots him a look. 'For the last time, I am NOT flaking out, Brock.' He cracks two more beers and passes one to his partner. He then digs a few bills out of his pants pocket, handing them over to Stacey. 'Babe, why don't you and Lindsey go get some coffee while Brock and I wait for this thing to start.' As the girls walk off, Brock turns to Stephen, taking a swallow of Heineken. 'Dude, I don't see Alice anywhere.' Stephen leans back and tries to get in a relaxed position. 'He's probably going to crash through the skylight at the last minute and rain broken glass down on everyone's head or something.' Suddenly a feeby little exec-looking putz with a headset and a clipboard walks over to the former tag champs. 'Hi, fellas,' he says in a rather nasally voice. 'I'm Roddy. Listen, there's been a slight change of plans. We just received word that Alice Cooper is not showing up today, due to a last minute decision that he did not want to be seen in a mall.' 'He's a smarter guy than we are,' Stephen remarks. 'No doubt,' Brock adds. 'Annywayyyyy,' Roddy adds, sounding rather annoyed that he was cut off, 'we have replacement celebrities sharing the stage with you.' 'The Beastie Boys?' Stephen asks. 'Metallica?' Brock asks. 'Red Hot Chili Peppers?' 'Pantera?' 'Reverend Horton Heat?' 'Corrosion Of Conformity?' 'The Red Elvises?' Roddy adjusts his tie and gives an incredibly smarmy smile. 'Only the hottest band on the face of the planet. . .' 'Why do I suddenly not like the sound of this?' Stephen asks through his beer. 'N'SYNC!!' Roddy screams as the five bubblegum popoids walk around the corner with their prettyboy smiles. 'That's why not,' Brock answers, shaking his head. A geeky member with a head resembling a tazed hedgehog thrusts his hand out to Stephen as a huge crowd gathers around. 'Hi, I'm Justin.' Stephen looks up at him crossly. 'Good for you.' Justin forces the smile to stay on his face. 'Listen, you little nobody,' he says through his toothy smile in a voice that only Stephen hears, 'you make me look bad up here and I swear you'll never work in show business again, you hear me? I'm Justin. . . freaking Timberlake, gosh darn it!' Stephen cocks an eyebrow. 'I'm not in show business, you musical turd.' Before the discussion can go any further, Roddy leaps out in front of everyone and adjusts his headset so he's broadcasting through the mall. 'Okay, everyone, thanks for coming today!! We have some very special guests today, super music sensation, N'SYNC!!' He pauses for an audiece reacion, which occurs with decent success (mostly among the 12-13 year old demographic). '. . . And a wrestler from the Rush wrestling federation,Brock Newbludd, along with his manager Stephen Blood!' The audience goes ballistic. Half yell resounding chants of, 'SAD BUT TRUE!' while the other half call out, 'WE'VE GOT BLOOD!!' Roddy waits until the people calm down, then continues. 'And they'll answer questions people have as they sign autographs.' Several squealing girls charge to the N'SYNC table, where they all get glossy group pictures signed by all five members--The Tough One, The Cute One, The Ugly One, The Ugly One and The Ugly One. On the other side of the stage, a line has formed where people are more calmly approaching the table of The Blood Brothers, who are now joined by Lindsey and Stacey. Roddy points at a man in line with his hand raised. 'Yes, you have a question?' The man puts his hand down. 'Yeah, I got a question for Brock. Hey, man. First of all I want to tell you that you guys are the hardest, baddest tag team I've ever seen in twenty years of watching pro wrestling, man.' He is drowned out with approving cheers from the rest of the people. 'Thanks,' Brock says as he absently signs a glossy 8 X 10. The guy nods. 'Yeah, so anyways, I noticed that Stephen has been seen moe on Rush TV lately, is he coming back?” Brock signs another glossy. 'That's easy, man. As long as Stephen is up too, I’m game for him to come back' Stephen nods and takes another swallow of beer. 'That and the fact that I’ve been out with a f~cking knee injury.' Roddy leans over toward them and puts his hand over his mouthpiece. 'You guys have to watch the language, okay? There are kids here.' 'Whatever, Robby,' Stephen says as he scribbles his name on a glossy. 'It's Roddy,' Roddy says snottily. Stephen spits a mouthful of beer into Roddy's face. 'Whatever your name is, f~ck off already!' The audience tries to cheer and laugh at the same time as Stephen kicks a reeling Roddy off of the stage onto a Krispy Kreme donut display. Roddy gets back up and brushes himself off hastily, trying to act like it didn't happen. He points to a young lady in the audience. 'Yes, you have a question, ma'am?' The girl puts her hand down and gives a shy smile. 'Yeah, um, I wanted to ask Stephen something.' Stephen raises his head from the glossy he was signing and cocks an eyebrow. 'Huh?' 'Yeah, um,' the girl stammers. 'Those girls back there. . . they your girlfriends?' Stephen leans back with a smile and Stacey gives him a big kiss on the mouth as Brock happily receives the same from Lindsey. 'You'd best believe it, toots,' he says. Roddy scans the audience. 'Okay, now for a question for N'SYNC, now let's see. . . yes, little girl, you want to ask an N'SYNC member something?' The little girl in line, perhaps eight years old, shakes her head. 'No, I wanna ask The Blood Brothers a question.' Roddy is taken aback. 'Oh. Um, okay.' 'Brock?' the girl asks. 'Are you gonna whoop ass Chris Matthews on Thursday and make him limp around like a dog sniffing his own $hit?' Brock, slack-jawed, drops his pen on the glossy he was signing. 'Are you here with a parent, sweetie?' he asks. The little girl nods and her father steps forward, not sure how to react to the situation. Brock merely thrusts his hand out to the man. 'You're doing a fantastic job raising your child, sir. The world needs more men like you.' He turns his attention to the little girl. 'You'd best believe it, sugar. I’m gonna make Chris wish his mommy and daddy wore Trojans on that ill-fated nights so many years ago.' Justin leans down to the little girl. 'Would you like an autographed picture of N'SYNC, princess?' The little girl kicks Justin in the shin. 'No!' she yells. 'You're a bunch of no-talent pissclams!' Stephen and Brock fail to contain their laughter and spray mouthfuls of beer all over the place. Roddy tries to get a semblance of order from the crowd. 'Okay, okay, let's settle down a bit, people. . . now is there anyone here who has a question for N'SYNC? Anyone at all? I'm not taking a question for The Blood Brothers right now, so can anyone think of anything to ask N'SYNC?' Stephen, Brock, Stacey and Lindsey are all fighting back laughter, but they let it loose when shot with angry looks from the New Kids--er, I mean N'SYNC. Roddy finally finds a man with his hands up. 'Finally! Yessir, do you have a question for N'SYNC?' 'Yeah,' the guy says, turning his attention away from Roddy and to the band that's not really a band (since 'bands' play instruments). 'I bought tickets to an N'SYNC concert last month. . . my daughter had been looking forward to it for half a year.' 'Thank you very much, sir,' Justin says, smiling. 'Shut the hell up, you worthless f@ggot, I'm not done,' the man shoots back. 'The concert was canceled because your candy ass had a stomach cramp.' 'Oh, yeah, I remember that,' Justin says. 'So what's the question?' The man clears his throat. 'How can you not handle prancing around like a fairy for a few hours and sing about beach love and soft drinks because of a stomach cramp. . . while Brock gives every match his all every night he is booked, even when suffering from actual WOUNDS suffered previous nights before?' 'Uh, um. . .' Justin stutters. 'It's because he has no scroat,' Stacey says, happily shocking everyone around. Justin shoves his chair back and throws his pen down. 'All right, that's it! I'm leaving!' Brock grabs a beer and cracks it, thrusting it out to Justin. 'Aw settle down, you big f~ckoid,' he says nonchalantly. 'You can handle a pain in the ass like this, I'm quite certain.' 'How do you figure?' Justin says, not touching the beer. Brock cracks a smile. 'Well you dated Britney Spears, didn't you?' A huge wave of laughter washes over the crowd. In a fit, Justin grabs the can of beer and dumps it all over the front of Brock’s t-shirt. Aghast, the crowd falls silent. Brock grabs Justin by the collar and shoves him up on top of the N'SYNC table, climbing up on it as well. 'Well well,' he says in a gravelly voice. 'Look who wants to play 'My Name Is Chris Matthews’.' One of the other band (that's not really a band) members taps Brock on the leg. 'I think you should let him go--' he starts to say. 'Shaddap, Mary,' Stephen says, whipping an empty beer can at him and nailing him in his gel-encrusted head. He rubs the tuft of hair that got knocked out of place and moves back. 'Yes, sir,' he whimpers. Brock looks out to the audience and smiles evilly, clutching Justin's throat. 'Thursday night, I beat Chris Matthews like a sally Canadian bitch, and take his world title belt!” Stephen pounds another beer. 'And that, my friends, is simply sad--' 'BUT TRUE!!' the crowd calls out. 'Nobody asked me this,' Brock continues, 'but I'm gonna say it anyway. You get to see it first here, modeled by the lovely. . .' He pauses to look down at Justin's legs, then back up. '. . . Pants-wetting Justin Timberlake--! Here comes the best finsher in the biz… This is--' His voice trails off as he spins Justin around, grabs a handful of his shellaqued-curly hair and yanks him backwards, tucking his head under his arm and sweeping him backwards, crashing through the table. Brock kips right back up to his feet, shotguns a beer, then throws the empty can out into the crowd. 'The OLD STYLE DDT!!!' Stephen grabs a box of the brand-new Blood Brothers t-shirts and throws it out to the middle of the floor. 'On us, ladies and gentlemen, on one condition. . .' Stephen puts his arm around Stacey. 'Keep one hand in the pocket. . .' 'ONE FOOT IN THE GUTTER!!' the dangerous four shout out to a cheering audience. The four walk off, leaving Roddy and the four conscious N'SYNC members to deal with the masses. 'By the way,' Stephen adds as an address to the two girls, 'we are absolutely NOT doing any shopping in this mall. Ever.'
