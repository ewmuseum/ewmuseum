Call me Sam...

9 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/9/02 Wes:Hello? VoiceMr.Ryan? Wes:Uh,yeah this is him. Voice:Mr.Ryan I am your contact,I will be speaking to you about your progress in our little....Mutual undertaking.I understand that you have made your presence felt within RUSH,that is good we want this to be very...public. Wes:Public?I thought I was to keep everything a secret. Voice:Yes,you are supposed to keep your employers a secret,but as far as your destruction of G.O.D. we would like that to be very,very public. Wes:Oh,I got you man.Have you been watching the past couple weeks?I believe I have accomplished my first objective quite well. Voice:Yes,I have been watching you closely and have reported back to my employer,he feels much the same way I do,and because of that we have..done away with those parts of your past that contain...questionable behavior.You are no longer a wanted man Mr.Ryan. Wes:THAT'S GREAT!!...Watching me...closely?How close? Voice(Chuckling):They said you were a sharp one Mr.Ryan,and you seem to be.I admit,I have been observing you from a much closer position than that of the regular T.V. viewer.In fact I have been observing you first hand. Wes:So you mean you are in RUSH? Voice:You didn't think we'd go on blind faith did you?Of course we're going to have a man on the inside to monitor you,to make sure you don't get to caught up in being a superstar to complete your mission,and more importantly to make sure personal your own personal feelings don't lead you to be...wreckless.Remeber,your not saving the world your working for us. Wes:Well you guys said you picked me cause I had conviction. Voice:Conviction,not zeal. Wes:I assure you I will stay controlled.Anyway you said I accomplished my first objective,what's my second? Voice:Your second objective is to anger him,bait him,badger him,make him furious.You are an intelligent man and he is little more than an animal,his rage will blind him and when it does then will be the time to strike.... ***CLICK**** ............. ............. Wes:Hello?....Hello!?!....Hello!!!
