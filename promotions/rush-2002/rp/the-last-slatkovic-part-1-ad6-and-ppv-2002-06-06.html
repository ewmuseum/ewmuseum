The Last Slatkovic (part 1) {Ad6 and PPV}

6 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/6/02 Steffan Slatkovic was an enigma within an enigma Upon arrival on American soil, the only thing he ever did that Ricky could recall was… He disappeared Not really that he didn’t want to be found. He just faded into the background. Or did he? This is his story The story of a person who could be the last in the line of a simple, hardworking Bosnian namesake… This is the story of Steffan Slatkovic The Last Slatkovic Mother? Father? Remus said they went to a better place These was the very last thought connecting Steffan Slatkovic to his brother formerly known as Remus, who chose to be re-born as a Starr in the state of Cameron, North Carolina, part of the United States of America… But that is another story The turning point went as far back as their native land, scarred by strife, blood and loss. Remus and Steffan, the two surviving members of a laborer’s family, emigrated to the United States on a refugee ship. They were given papers that entitled them to “Temporary Residency” Remus and Steffan knew more than enough to do better than that As soon as they got off the ship they were determined to stay in that new land But how they went about doing it… That is where the real story is Their separation was instant With that much people flowing out, it was just easy to get lost All too easy. We all know what happened to Remus, going off to orphanage and all, adopted by a well-to-do man and the rest is history… But Steffan… Now that is a narrative of it’s own Steffan was sent to an orphanage located in inner-city New York. He was just one of the hundreds of little heads residing in there, standing in line like the others, almost blending into the scene… Except for the fact that he spoke almost no English, was as pale as a corpse and had no idea where the hell he was when he first got there. It was almost automatic He became a mark to the sharps, fish to the shark, punk to the bullies, bitch to the thugs. Get the picture? How could one expect a disoriented, displaced and in many ways, innocent Bosnian kid to survive a week at a place like that? Let alone for 24 hours? Simple One doesn’t It was the blatant fact that in their eyes, Steffan was weak in every way possible. No will power, almost no physical strength, not even any form of knowledge to benefit him in any way. A normal day would go like this for Steffan at the orphanage He would be the first up but the last into the showers. Inside, he would be either physically abused, molested or both by the older and more powerful boys. Most of the time, he would end up using a soggy towel to dry himself and dab his wounds. By breakfast he would definitely be last in, berated by the matron for being “slow”. If it was the odd day out his tray would be tipped and all blame placed upon him. Any attempt at making it right would be responded with more berating by the matron and more physical punishment from those irritated with his pleas for justice. The rest of the day would be spend enduring verbal and physical abuse from the orphanage community besides in between attending classes taught by the brothers of the Marist following. At least they hurled no abuse at him for they were, at the very best, apathetic to everybody. They cared only for the preaching of their word and would stand by their conviction as if it were their sole purpose for living. Steffan would silently disagree with them every single time To him, if all men were truly equal in the eyes of the omnipotent creator, why were there still many individuals like him all over the world being suppressed? If we were truly brothers and sisters then why all the hate? Why all the strife? If the Marist Brothers did contribute anything at all to Steffan Slatkovic… They hardened him to the world At such a tender age, Steffan Slatkovic was a cynic. He lauded at the idea of a paradise and balked at equality, not that he hated them or anything. He just thought them too good to be true. His lifestyle at the orphanage just re-enforced the idea to him. Many a birthday spent like every other day, just trying to get by. Until his 15th Steffan woke up to a special surprise; his hands and feet were bound tight with rope, his mouth gagged. A group of older boys were sneering down at him. A beefy one speaks in his harsh, tough-guy voice “Wait ‘till matron sees you. Boy is she gonna blow her top on this one” Steffan tried struggling and pleading at them through muffled calls but they simply walked off, laughing as they disappeared down the corridor for breakfast. All alone in the sleeping quarters was Steffan Slatkovic, bound, rendered helpless, alone with only one thing to do Cry Tears ran down his eyes, knowing it himself that he was completely helpless and powerless against the aggressors. He knew that he was weak and he knew that whatever he said or did fell on deaf ears. There were footsteps Slow but audible Each step, getting louder as it is closer, brought a chill down Steffan Slatkovic’s spine. At first he thought it was the matron but matron walked to brisk to produce steps like that. He then thought it were those of a Marist brother but none would venture into this part of the orphanage at this time of the day. Cold sweat broke out on Steffan’s forehead as he pondered the near-infinite possibilities what would happen next Whoever it was, they had entered the room already The first thing Steffan noticed was the smell of cologne, fine cologne. Much finer than that used by the matron or any of the janitorial staff Soon, a figure stood beside his bed and Steffan opened his eyes. He found himself staring at a new, shining pair of leather shoes. The shoes seemed to have an owner. As he looked up he sees a pair of black GQ style trousers, a matching black belt with a small gold buckle with the words “GUESS?” engraved on the front of the buckle. As his eyes moved higher, he sees a blue collared shirt all buttoned up save for the top button from the Hugo Boss series with a fine black jacket to complete the attire. Steffan was impressed and in awe with all that he saw before him Finally, he looked up to find the final point of focus, his face. It was that of a man, a young man. His face was clean-shaven and handsome, his mouth forming a smirk hinting of arrogance with a small, thin and well-kept moustache serving to emphasize on elegance and class. His eyes seemed to have an expression of amusement as he studied Steffan’s position with the final notable feature being his ponytail, straight and well kept, just like the rest of him. The man sat down beside a bounded Steffan on the bed and asks him calmly and smoothly “Do you know who did this to you?” Steffan nodded in acknowledgement “Do you want to be untied?” Steffan nodded rapidly in acknowledgement, as if supporting the motion wholeheartedly “I don’t necessarily need to do that. The matron could in her rounds later…” Steffan shook his head sideways equally rapidly, showing a strong negative gesture “But if I untie you right now, your life could very well be changed completely” Steffan didn’t care. What could really be as bad as his life was right now? Even death seemed very appealing to him at that very moment. He nodded “Are you sure?” Steffan nodded again, not rapidly but calmly as if showing he understood his decision completely. “Very well then” The smooth man pulls out a flick knife and cuts the rope bindings that seem to be rendering Steffan helpless. With a few swift movements, Steffan was free. He removed the gag himself with his free hands and spoke straight away “Thank you very much” The man smiled at him, or it was more like an exaggeration of his smirk “You’re welcome” As Steffan leaped to his feet, the man puts a firm hand on his shoulder “Of course, I didn’t really say that it was a complete act of generosity” Steffan looked back at him wondering, as if puzzled by the man’s statement “But that can be easily repaid by doing one, simple task for me” Steffan asks out aloud “What would that be?” The smooth man, stroking his chin, speaks in a calm refined manner to the boy “The people who tied you up, you said you knew them…” “Yeah I do, I live with them” The man pulls back his left jacket sleeve as he holds the wrist up. He reveals a silver Rolex, gleaming with the sunlight coming in through the window. “I want you to make them atone for their wrongdoing against you” A look of shock comes over the face of Steffan as he slowly mutters “How?” The man puts his hand down and looks eye-to-eye at Steffan “You see yourself as a weakling, I know. But that can be changed and like I said, when I untie you, your life, as you know it will change” The man takes Steffan’s right hand and opens up the palm. Steffan then feels something cold and metallic placed on it. His hand closes in on it with the man whispering in his ear “Welcome to your new life” He stands back up straight smiling, or more like smirking at the boy and turns around. The man walks back down through the corridor from whence he came and disappeared around the corner with the same, slow lingering footsteps made against the tiled floor. Steffan looked down at his right hand, clutching an object that seemed small, metallic and shiny It sure as hell wasn’t a Christmas bauble Suddenly, Steffan felt different He felt… Strong “So how were your days in the orphanage?” Ricky Starr takes a sip of his tea with sweet milk at the all night diner. Opposite him was a person, looking like a freshman straight out of his teenage years but hardened far beyond his years. His companion looked at him with a sarcastic looking smile “They were my innocent days”
