Hangover Hangtime...

1 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9.1.02 'Success is the sole earthly judge of right and wrong.'- Adolf Hitler It's true that in most instances around the world, the people, men and women, that we hate the most, that society has decreed as evil and as a terrible person, are usually the smartest people around. Adolf Hitler, the man who directed the extermination of the Jews is perhaps the most hated man in the history of the world, yet it is true that he was very, Very intelligent. How did it come about? Simple. He did not use his book smarts or his IQ, he did and said what everybody needed to see and hear. He took grasp of a situation, and as much as I, like the rest of the world, was disgusted by what he did, he ruled an entire country without really having very much power over them... except intelligence. The situation is the exact same involving people like Joseph Stalin, Ted Bundy and Joe Bonnano. While people have labeled them outcasts, and while people hate what they have done, they were still very intelligent. It revolves strictly around intelligence, and sometimes the fact may be people fear what they cannot understand, and people hate what they fear. Take out the fact that each have committed horrendous crimes, and have attempted to physically slice up humans, and in some cases have actually been accomplished, and what you are left with is the nerd kid who was always smarter then you in school. The same kid that you always wanted to beat up because he made everyone else look inferior. The case revolves around still today, with society as a whole. People are afraid of what they do not know and what people do not know is what cause them the most grief. People may think that they know me. Marvin Harris thought he did and look where that has ended up. Brock Newbludd, I suppose this goes out to you as my next ring mate. You can do everything you want to do, you can search at the RUSH Website, and read off my biography, and find my statistics and my accomplishments and possibly even the size of my boxers if you'd like. But that doesn't mean you know me. Hell, Sirius and Godfather Silvio, don't really even know me, and that is an edge that I intend to keep. Brock, you do not know me, which means that you should fear me. The straight up truth is, the name of Scott Cage, although completely wide open in every aspect, is very closed to the world. I react differently then others and act differently then others. Long live the enigma of...Scott Cage. It is good to see that I have finally met up with an opponent who has a higher IQ than a rock. You, Brock, seem to be looking forward to this battle, seem to be looking at the possibility of toppling me and making your way all the way to being a RUSH Legend. Although let it not be overlooked that you do understand the task at hand, something that nobody wrestling today, can accomplish. You'll notice in a few hours after watching this promo that what you are dealing with is two different men. One who believes in his head that Scott Cage is the best wrestler, not because of skill, but because of the lack of skilled wrestlers in the world today. There is also a second who believes in his heart that Scott Cage the best, because he will not allow himself to be anything else. You will notice that one is a mean as shit bastard who has no other agenda other then to bring tears to eyes and stains to underwear. That the other is someone who even though he may sometimes be found on the wrong side of the proverbial wrestling law, he is a bad guy at heart and someone that will only speak the truth. You will see that those two personalities combine into one: Me, Scott Cage. You will also soon see stars in under a week's time. Wasn't that just spectacular? And I have done something that has lowered me to the level of you. Sure it may not be as big as a drop as I am used to falling, but one nonetheless is something to be proud of. Tactics Brock, tactics. What you have to learn, especially when dealing with someone like myself is that you need to have a 'secret weapon' or something else to grab the advantage. You yourself have realized that on paper your name is nothing more then overlooked, and on paper your chances of advancing aren't near promising, and are a little closer to an Oasis. What am I saying? You have to be a stand-out, you have to be someone that when people look at you, they will say, 'Wow, now that man is quality.' You have to have an edge, not a gimmick and not an angle, an edge. Control is a perfect word to use here Brock, it describes everything that I wish to say. Control yourself, control your opponent, control everything that could possibly go down, and control everything that won't. Control. Brock, you have already subsequently put yourself at a disadvantage of sorts, because you tried to strike first without holding control over me, without even trying to grab control. You through out your promo hoping for some sort of miracle perhaps, some sort of chance to get me to make a mistake, which in case you didn't know, is something that I rarely do. So what you have effectively done is given me a handle over the control, you have given me an edge that I didn't even need, and you have inadvertently signed yourself up for the losers end of the purse. See, in this 'game' it doesn't matter whether you love and hate, it matters how thick your wallet is at the end of the day. Mine will always be thicker then yours Brock, because you don't have an edge, but I do. (The camera fades into a the hotel room of Scott Cage. He is lying down on his bed, staring blankly at a television screen, his eyes seemingly glazed over. We hear the drunken ramblings of an unknown speaker, where the door opens...and we see Sirius walking in…) Sirius Cage: Scott…I thought you said you were working out.Scott Cage: I’m injured. Go away.Sirius Cage: And what exactly did you injure?Scott Cage: Uh…my brain.Sirius Cage: Scott, just how exactly did you injure your brain? Scott Cage: While watching a Newbludd promo. Sirius Cage: But how? Scott Cage: It's all so clouded... can I get some of my painkillers? Sirius Cage: Do you think you need them? Scott Cage: Yes please... Sirius Cage: All right... (Scott continues to glaze over the television...and stares as Newbludd gets drunk in some situation…) Sirius Cage: Here you go Scott. (Sirius hands Scott a handful of pills...) Scott Cage: How am I supposed to take these, without something to drink? Sirius Cage: A glass of water? Scott Cage: Nah, can I get a beer? Sirius Cage: Scott, you know you'll be knocked out like a white guy in a Mike Tyson fight if you take the two together.Scott Cage: Bah...he doesn't know the power of Scott Cage. (Sirius sighs, and walks to the mini-bar…) Sirius Cage: Here, take your alcohol... (Scott smiles, and pops the pills in his mouth...) Scott Cage: See? Nothing bad happened....nothing at... (Before Scott can finish his sentence...he's out cold...) Sirius Cage: I warned him... (Sirius shrugs, and turns off the television, as the camera magically delves into the twisted mind of Scott’s dreams…) Scott Cage: I hate this. (And with that, we fade out…) TO BE CONTINUED…
