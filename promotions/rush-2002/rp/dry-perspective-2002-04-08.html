Dry Perspective

8 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/08/02 Once upon a time before time, in a place within infinity, lies what seemed to be a yellow sun. To some, it seemed nothing more than just another beacon of light and continuously combusting energy. To a handful, it was a scorching curse brought down upon them on a near daily basis, with nothing to seek refuge from. To others, it was nothing more than a distant stars, merely another flickering piece in the dome some know as a sky. However, to quite a number, the sun was life. This tale is not about that sun It’s about an insignificant little blue-green planet orbiting the sun at a distance of roughly ninety-two million miles. Although life was plentiful in the planet, the significant majority of it consisted of ape-descended life forms. Out of the whole lot, they are an amazing bunch. For a species who still thought that sliced bread was the greatest idea of all-time, they were a perplexing enigma to all, even to each other. The real problem of that planet wasn’t the global warming or energy crisis that their egg-headed excuses for scientific intellectuals have been raving about in the past decade. And no, it was definitely not the massacre of other life forms as moaned about by green shirted, banner-toting brats who haven’t the slightest idea on how to de-louse their own closets or tell the difference from the clutch or gas pedal, and still have the audacity to call their paternal and maternal figures “parasites”. It was nothing like that at all. Those were just scapegoats for the “big picture”, as those who thought they had a clue described it. The REAL problem has always been the blatant fact that most of the ape-resembling life forms, which were quite ape-minded as well, were just simply unhappy for most of their existence. Those who actually had the time to spare between their bickering and moaning to barely notice that there actually was a problem, tried to come up with solutions for the problem, but many of these were very largely concerned with the movements of small, fragile, green pieces of paper which were worshipped and regarded most of the time as gods. It is quite odd actually because in retrospect, the green pieces of paper weren’t the ones wearing the frowns. The problem still remains, many are still mean and most in misery, yes, even the ones who had access to sliced bread. Increasingly, many of the individuals who have some amount of perspective resembling common sense were in the opinion that everybody had made a very grave error in coming down from the trees in the first place. Some who could be regarded as the most brilliant minds on that plane of life, in other words, having actually a healthy amount of common sense in them have concluded that even the trees had been a bad move and no one should ever have left the oceans. It still didn’t help to relieve the misery and self-pity that this particular world was wallowing in. However, there was truly a brilliant man, out of this world, who was literally blessed in both areas of true intellect and common sense, woke up one day in a dirty, smelly barn and thought how great it would be if everybody was nice to each other for a change. He went about telling everybody how great the idea was and how wrong everybody was in the first place. In retrospect, it really was a good idea In retrospect, it was a really bad move on his part. Although he did amass a healthy amount in his following, some who actually saw a wee-bit of merit in his words while the rest just came for the free food, drink and healthcare. In the end, for his enormous efforts, he was nailed to an ornament carved out of a tree just because the perpetrators were miserable enough to feel like doing it. Some say he rose from the dead after three days and ascended to the skies followed by more that could be best described as hogwash, leading to the beginnings of a cult that can be connected to monumental historical events of this world such as the Crusades, the Spanish Inquisition and of course, the pleasant backwater fraternity now known as the Ku Klux Kan. And for a fitting footnote, everybody was still quite miserable. He never did get a taste of that slice bread. Before anybody says anything about a cynical lecture on the history of this minuscule excuse for a world, let a few things be clarified. 1) This tale is not about the world as a whole2) This tale is about passionate struggles for prizes, glory and all the other nonesuch that falls into the category of the one thing that still keeps this miserable species entertained: Sports Entertainment A refugee of one of this planet’s bloody yet pointless struggles which led to more misery and frowns on faces in time to come now seats himself in a position of wealth. He is more than adequately covered in terms of having a large amount of small green pieces of paper, which is sought after by every other person in the world. He also has had more than his fair share of sliced bread. However, he also desires a few more things in life. A few belts with oversized buckles seem to be the objects of desire at the end of the equation. As strange as it may seem, what makes this fetish so interesting is that he is not the only one in pursuit of these strange belts. However, an inhabitant of this planet by the name of Maslow seems to have deciphered the slightly complex equation, which leads to the answer to why these ape-descended creatures desire things other than small green pieces of paper. Hence leading to the typical yet quite ignoramic term “The next best thing since sliced bread”. It seems that these oversized buckled belts have fallen into this particular category. Maslow appropriately named his formula, “Maslow’s Hierarchy of Needs”, very typical of the species, being the egoistical little things they are when they have not even colonized the reddish sphere next to them, let alone the tiny rock orbiting their own planet. Although he was merely re-stating facts of life, nevertheless it was a large step for these hairless apes to take. Taking the life of the former offspring of war as an example, one will examine how Maslow’s discovery works: This Hierarchy has five steps, five rungs on the ladder. Each above the other as one progresses through life yet each bears the exact same significance in an individual’s life as the other. The first has been dubbed “Psychological Needs”. It refers to what a large chunk of the species regards as the basic needs of ALL individuals. As the immigrant from a bloodbath emerges into a new world, he is given these needs in the form of food, water, clothing and shelter. Although these are general terms for thin broth, cold tea, donated wear and a halfway house, they are the basics nonetheless. The second step up Maslow’s ladder has been named “Security Needs”. Appropriately named, it refers to the needs concerning both physical and emotional security. The NYPD, Social Security, the orphanage and the likes remotely resembled what was supposed to keep our refugee safe from harm’s way. Next step up is “Social Needs”. Being the living contradiction these creatures are, although they keep ranting on about “My space, space and again, space”, they also seem to have a desire to belong to groups and be liked and accepted by others. An individual in possession of large amounts of small green pieces of paper seemed to have taken the liberty to adopt our refugee into his care, some say out of generosity while others carry the more realistic rational of seeking a heir to his hoard of green paper. He also made acquaintances along the long road of life, forming life-long bonds with some while others belong to another narrative altogether, one with darker undertones. “Esteem Needs”, another one up Maslow’s excuse for a philosophical stairway, refers to the needs for self-confidence, achievement, self-respect and respect of others, with the last point being in dispute for some time now. In many ways, it is like inflicting self-delusion in order to boost one’s ego. The refugee, now a man, thought that he was simply the best out there, hence giving him a sense which could be best described as “feelgood”, which is comparable to the state of some individuals when they have consumed certain substances on the chemical chart. He was also given incentives from his mock-paternal figure, his adopted role model, just as a scientist would give bananas to a monkey who has successfully completed a task. All these contributed to the fulfilling of this particular need in his life. Last but most certainly not least comes the part that explains everything. At the very top of his hierarchy, Maslow dubbed it “Self-Actualization Needs”. Closely related to the one that came before, it represents the highest order of needs and refers to the desire to realize one’s full POTENTIAL. After finding that one has the adequate and correct support in life, one would strive higher to achieve what they can in this world, to be the best one can be. In other words, it’s all or nothing in what somebody appropriately termed “A dog eat dog world”. From this point, our spark has ambitions to become a star, to capture those belts with oversized buckles and be at the top of the world, for a while at least. It is correct for one to assume that he is at this stage now. Although the world has already enough bickering to boot and overfed on strife and blatant stupidity, one man chooses to brave it all for a prize he might never get. Assuming he gets it all with blood, sweat and cup of coffee at the end of the day, will this be the end of his ambition? Or will he reach even higher? What is the answer to the increasingly annoying problems of humanity? Personally, rather than unnecessarily hurt another fragile cranium in the process of seeking such a monumental and epic answer to this equally monolithic question… One would contend with a piece of sliced bread and some decent coffee for once to wash it all down.
