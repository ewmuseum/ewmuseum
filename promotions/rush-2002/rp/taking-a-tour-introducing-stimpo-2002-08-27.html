Taking a Tour, introducing Stimpo!

27 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/27/02 Introduction: People say everyone welcome newcomers with open arms. How can this be true if you haven't even met anyone of your new place yet? In my case, it's wrestling. Coming straight out of my few days in PWA and CHZ, people told me that a new federation, RUSH will welcome me with open arms. I'd like to see that happen, but first, I needed to get to know my facilities that I'd be working with. I'd looked in some RUSH newsletters trying to obtain any new evidence of the federation and then I spotted what I needed most, a time for a tour guide of the latest RUSH show, Adrenaline 14. I knew that sooner or later, perhaps on the upcoming Adrenaline 15 show in RUSH, I'd be competing inside the squared circle itself up against one of the greats in the RUSH federation. I decided that I should get aquanted with my facilities so I took myself in my little honda accord they call a car and parked it in the parking lot at the site where RUSH usually holds it's performances. The entire building must have been ten stories high with seats all the way up to the ceiling. Windows were only found on the first few stories as the others were there to keep the fans or much worse, the seats from falling through the windows. The climate of that day was decent enough for me to last it for this tour guide. A group of people were standing outside the arena screaming and hollering waiting to get inside with great anticipation. They were all...large men and I looked at myself wearing a not so skin tight black shirt with 'Stimpo' printed on it, along with my nicely fitting around the waist black pants with a shiny new belt and also weighing at a mere 187 pounds compared to these, at least, 300 pound collasell human beings called men. I remotely locked my car as I walked out with my black shoes and popped open my trunk and pulled out my training bags and shut the trunk once more. 'What in the hell?' I said to myself astonished as I saw the size of these men all carrying what looked like tickets to either this tour I was going to or some interview or inside scoop on a wrestler in RUSH. I saw the door open as a woman came out wearing a completly red skirt, a short one at that with black hair as she swung it back in the wind, or lack of. Her brown eyes guided the men or rather dragged the men inside the building as she sent them down the hall. 'Was that the tour? Boy, if I'm on that one, lord help me if I get my hands on a microphone and that woman! She'll get the latino seranade she's been looking for!' I encouraged myself to pursue my latin singing career as I immediatly, took my bags and ran straight into the building. I used my free hand to pull the door open and ran straight in and stopped dead to see that the same men standing outside were now holding cameras taking non stop pictures, not of the pictures of past wrestlers in RUSH or the hall of fame memorabilia on the opposite wall, but of the same girl who I know saw was wearing a tag with the name, 'Elizabeth' and under that was who rank in the arena as a tour guide. I finally put two and two together and now saw that this tour was for not only for these men's eye candy, but for mine too! 'Excuse me? Will you be joining our weekly RUSH tour today?' The lady asked me twinkling her eyes in her devlish ways. 'Uh..Yes?' I said dragging my words being lost in her stunning womenly features. 'Then hop in line sir, and please refrain from flashing pictures as my hooters, they don't respond nicely, thanks.' She told me as if she was warning me of something. 'I'll try to refrain myself from doing so.' I said with a glint in my eye trying to be suddle. 'This way gentlemen, and laides.' Elizabeth seemed to look directly at me at that statement, but I couldn't put my finger on why. She guided the group ahead of me down the hall as I followed with my eyes now wandering from wall to wall to see many different things such as pictures of past champions and of past owners, such as Jeremy Howard or the latest, Chris Sloboda. I chose not to take out the camera that I had stored in my bag, instead, I'd keep these pictures of the famous people, I mean, Elizabeth stored in my mind for safe keepings. 'Now as you can see, on the walls to the left and right of you are pictures of men who have been managers and wrestlers within the RUSH Wrestling Organazation, or haven't, you pick. Now if we continue down this hall we can view the actual ring where the wrestlers duke it out whether it be for a title or simply because a bitter fued. Fueds are commonly found here, and many are to come with the new signings RUSH has made including the likes of John McBride, and Brendon 'Stimpo' Stimson.' She said in an explanatory type voice as she ended her small tale on RUSH. 'Umm, excuse me? Do you happen to know when the autograph signing is for the new RUSH superstars?' I asked in curiosity, and because I wanted to sign some autographs for some fans, if I had any that is. 'There was one last week, I don't know if there is one being held this week. Besides, why would you want to go? No one important from RUSH will be there.' Like a dagger to my heart, her words pierced through me as she practically said I was nothing! 'What are you trying to say?! That I'm nothing?!' I said almost screaming with slight tears dripping from my eyes. 'Who are you anyways?' The tour guide, Elizabeth, yelled back in response. 'Brendon, Brendon Stimson. New RUSH roster member.' The tour guide jumped back from her position in shock. All the men had already cleared for what they thought was going to happen, a fight. 'I'm so sorry Mr. Stimson sir.' Elizabeth attempted to apologize. 'But, I really haven't heard of you.' She spurted out the truth. 'Haven't heard of the latino PoP star himself, me?!' I threw my bag down in anger. 'I'll show you what it really means to be a latino PoP star!' I said raises my head slightly towards the ceiling. 'Are you that sure?' Elizabeth cocked her head to the side truly asking me I snapped my arm up as I snapped my fingers as if I were a bullfighter. Then, out of no where, three trumpet players came up behind me ready to play. I snapped my fingers once more and they began to play my theme 'Livin' la Vida Loca.' I sang the entire song without a microphone, but acted as if there was one there. I made my latino jerks with my hips as Elizabeth was shot back some from my sexual movements. After the song finished, I snapped once more and faced Elizabeth as the three trumpet players ran away. 'Yes, I'm quite sure.' I glared at Elizabeth with my latino eyes. 'You know nothing!' She screamed at me telling me I was wrong. She ripped off her dress to reveal another, redder dress than before and removed her black wig to reveal her scarlet red hair. She tried to remove her eyes, but to no avail, she stayed with her brown eyes. I shot back in amazement as I knew she was a latino star, but a woman one. She mimicked me and snapped her fingers as another three trumpet players appeared behind her. The latino war had begun. She snapped once more as I did and the trumpet players started to play the old song, 'Quiero' by Selena. Elizabeth sang the song perfectly and shook her hips and hooters better than I could ever do. After the song finished, the three trumpet players wearing their sombreros and mexican gowns went running away. 'Now are you sure?' She asked me again. 'Yo-yo-your Elizabeth the Latino! I've been searching for your for years!' I was amazed at meeting this person. 'Who's Elizabeth the Latino?' She asked in confusion. 'Uhh, erm. You!' I said as I quickly took my back and belongings and ran down the hall ending this promo introducing the newest star of RUSH, Brendon 'Stimpo' Stimson.
