CrossRoads Chronicles #4: Statuz Quo

12 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8.12.02 'The right to be heard does not automatically include the right to be taken seriously.'- Hubert H. Humphrey In this world, everybody has the right to be heard. That is perhaps the one thing that I will fight for, that I will never back down from. Everybody is given the right to speak, the freedom to have their voice heard, and I might just be the largest supporter of that fact. However, what people are saying while speaking, what they are trying to get across, the point they are trying to make, that is where people need to be monitored, need to be paid attention to, and in far too many cases, need to be stopped. In a business where you make progress through action and balance through your verbal skills, you need to be more then certain that the words you are speaking hold relevance behind them. I have been doing my job recently, making sure that I am on top of every possible opponent that I could battle inside of the MCI Center walls. I have watched on as each competitor that has shown his face, spoke words on how they believe they are the next RUSH World Champion, and how they are Champion Material. I will not come out and throw out blatant lies and absurd propaganda about how everybody doesn't know the difference between a tongue and an ass, because I don't want to turn into what everyone else seems to be. I am going to come out here, and say that out of the other sixteen participants that stand, waiting for the numbers to count down, there are possibly ten wrestlers in RUSH that have an actual shot of winning this, of becoming the Number One Contender. But I will give credit to everyone for believing in themselves, for if you don't believe in yourself, you don't believe you can win. It seems that Statuz Quo has decided on doing something that, quite frankly, I thought he wouldn't do, and that was to open his mouth. Keep in mind, I find it great that he believes he can win, but truthfully, I know that he doesn't stand an ice cube's chance in Hell against me inside of the ring. However, Quo, I wish you the best of luck for having been able to see I can physically do inside of the ring. I know that a lonely avenger like yourself, doesn't have the super powers needed to get past me, and you don't have the Kryptonite to stop me. Kind of unfortunate, isn't it? Don't get me wrong while I do this Quo, and while I say these words to you. I am already looking beyond you. It's true yes, you may be one of the greatest that you have ever laid your eyes on, and you may have won the Syndicate Title but doesn't that stick out in your mind? Doesn't it show something when you are comparing the little, insignificant and lowly titles, with the real prize? The RUSH World Title and even the name of Scott Cage? Doesn't it show you something when your friends, who know you the best, tell you to your face that you cannot win? Quo, don't misunderstand something here because I know that most certainly you will. I am over-looking you, not myself. I am the one recognizing that you aren't up to par, you aren't up to my standards. I am the one that has branded you a loser, right from the get-go. Statuz Quo, Let me ask you a question sir, am I wrong to be doing this? The thing that everybody doesn't realize, is that I am nowhere near 'the biggest dog on the block.' I am standing in a sea of oak trees, trying to spy the sun through the huge obstacles that block my sight. The point I am trying to make Quo, is that you don't have to be the biggest guy to be the best guy. In some cases, it is true, bigger may be better, and where that counts, I am still the best. The point that I am trying to make is that I accomplish everything, all my victories, with my brain. Not something that you see everyday, right Statuz? I am sure you have heard about my accomplishments and I am sure that you have decided inside of your head that you will not allow yourself to be victimized before even stepping in the ring. I am sure that you are trying to convince yourself that when you get in the ring with me, you are only standing in a ankle high mud slide, when in actuality you are stranded in an air tight cave, buried deep underneath a ton of greatness. If you are wondering why I am saying this, it is because I am giving you the greatest warning that you can receive. A little more protective then the ridiculous warnings on a pornography web site, which do nothing but egg you on to enter. What I am telling you, is what to be ready for, is what to be prepared for...is what to battle against in a sense. Don't be looking for powerbombs and gorilla presses. Don't be looking for figure-fours and armbars. Be looking up in the air for me flying as high as I can, and look into your brain, your mind, your intelligence where ever it lies. Whether it is in your ass or head, and be ready for that. Be ready for smarts to be used, and for you to be demoralized mentally. Although don't feel bad, you aren't anywhere near the first, and you certainly won't be the last. (Fade in to a shot of a deserted factory in the heart of Detroit, Michigan. Our hero, Scott Cage, strolls along the barrage of machinery, ignoring the brash noises, and focuses on heading one direction: that of an old, fat man…) Scott Cage: Hey Gerald! Gerald: Hello Scott. It’s been a while.Scott Cage: Gerald, why did you call me down here? You said it was urgent. Gerald: I did say it was urgent, and it is. I have something for you that I thought you might want before CrossRoads. Scott Cage: And that one thing would be…?Gerald: Actually, I lied, I have two things for you that I think you should have before CrossRoads. Scott Cage: Yeah, so what have you got for me? Gerald: I have for you, perhaps the greatest thing in the world. Scott Cage: Go on.Gerald: I have been watching your interviews, and with your recent challengers to Statuz Quo, this is the perfect thing to go with it. I have for you... (Gerald smiles once, to signify that he is ready to continue, and Scott ushers him on with a slight movement of his right hand…) Scott Cage: Continue... Gerald: I have made you a new shirt. A shirt that we began selling this morning, and have already sold more then any other RUSH superstar has sold in the last week! Scott Cage: You mind if I take a look at this bad boy? Gerald: I was hoping you would ask actually. Here, close your eyes. (Scott closes his eyes, and while he does, he shows that his annoyance level is rising…) Gerald: All right Scotty, keep your eyes closed. I lied to you a bit when I said it was a shirt. It is in fact going to be a hotass seller. Ready? Open your eyes. (He unfolds it and hangs in front of Scott, as he slowly opens his eyes, and a smile comes to his lips…) Scott Cage: Now THAT is a quality shirt... Gerald: Thank you, Scott.Scott Cage: I especially like the fact that I’m holding all five RUSH titles.Gerald: And the picture of Marvin Harris with a gun to his head in the background. It was specially requested by your brother, Sirius.Scott Cage: Excellent. Well Gerald, I’ll have to be going now. I’ve got a-Gerald: Wait Scott, there is something I have to tell you. First of all, you can't leave yet because I said I have two things for you. Scott Cage: What are you talking about? Did you make me a hat or something? Gerald: Not quite Scott. Close your eyes again! (Scott does as he is told, and waits…) Gerald: All right... open your eyes! (Scott opens his eyes, and bursts out in hysterical laughter…) Scott Cage: HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!! Gerald! That is just about the funniest thing I have ever seen! My friend, you have out done yourself again, this time! (Scott looks at the shirt a second time and begins laughing even harder. He bends forward holding his stomach as he laughs...) Gerald: It’s my finest piece of art. (Scott regains his composure…) Scott Cage: Sold any of these recently? Gerald: No, actually, I only made two. One for you, and one for Statuz Quo. If you want, I could manufacture them for you. Scott Cage: No, it's all right. I think that this one will be enough. Remember, I never forget a favor that has been done for me. (Scott grabs the shirts, then throws both of his presents over his shoulder and walks out of the building and into the sunshine. He walks straight ahead to his rented black Cadillac, and opens the door, as he stares back at the camera...) Scott Cage: Oh, you didn’t get to see the Statuz Quo T-Shirt? Here, I’ll give you a special glimpse…) (Scott reaches up to his shoulder and removes the shirt which Gerald had given him. He pulls it off his shoulder, and holds it up for the camera to see. It shows a very frightened Statuz Quo, clad in prison attire with his pants down, bent over in front of a line of tough-looking prisoners…) Scott Cage: Statuz, I hope you enjoy the little gift I have for you. Perhaps it will make you laugh as much as it did me. Spitting image of you isn't it? I have never seen such an accurate picture of you. Until I see you on CrossRoads...wear it proudly. (Scott laughs again, as he steps into his Cadillac, and drives off into the distance. Fade out…)
