Hereditary Matters {part 1, sequel to Waking Starr}

undated

Date: Today Ricky loved and hated rainy days. Hated it because he could not go out much unless for the most urgent of reasons. Loved it because he did not have to water the garden at the Residence. But being the restless individual he is, Ricky seats himself in the Residence study, a place built for his adopted father Josh as a place of meditation and personal study. Although Ricky never paid much heed to it unless he needed to entertain some dignitaries or guests, he found solace in it as he studies a small, black book. There is no inscription of any form on the cover, and it seemed plain enough at first glance. A seal binds the book shut with the emblem of a Starr, his namesake. Ricky undoes the seal and opens the book. The first page caught Ricky Starr’s attention… And for the rest of the night, Ricky stayed in the study alone save for a bottle of Baccardi and pack of Malboro Lights. An hour later, Ricky walks out, or to be more accurate, bursts out of the study, his facial expression carrying a serious look of just witnessing the unexpected. He takes a last drag from a nearly burnt out cigarette and extinguishes it on a nearby crystal ashtray, which always happens to be in somewhat strategic locations of the house. As he walks down a flight of stairs, Ricky glances at the antique timepiece on the wall and discovers that it is exactly 11:25 pm. “Carruthers!” Ricky calls out for the Residence’s live-in butler, an Englishman and a loyal employee of the Starr legacy reaching back decades when old man Josh made his first bundle of millions. A proper, regal man of a stern air, his gray-silver hair always seems to be meticulously set in place along with his attire. Although looking in his late 50’s at best, it is most definitely a fooling exterior for a man of fitness and intellectual sharpness. It was said that Joshua even confided and discussed many things with the butler including affairs of the enterprise, world events, right down to the simple pleasures in life such as cigars, drink and surprisingly, fast cars. Ricky saw what the old man before saw in him and decided to keep him around, besides, an extra pair of limbs; eyes and a quick mind wouldn’t hurt. Carruthers quickly stepped into the scene, ready to receive and carry out the orders of his employer. “Yes Mr Starr?” Ricky caught sight of the butler and walks in a brisk pace towards him. He puts a hand on the butler’s shoulders and speaks in an urgent voice. “Get Henry on the line, quickly. Tell him it’s urgent, all about the enterprise.” Carruthers nods his head. “Of course sir” Ricky rolls his eyes and dismisses him quickly to carry out the task. Ricky produces the same small black book from the study from the back pocket of his pants and hastily flips through the pages of the book. He runs his finger down a page and holds the page with his thumb as he shuts the book. Carruthers trots back into the room with a cordless phone and hands it to Ricky, who accepts it while waving a hand gesture to dismiss the butler. The butler curtseys and leaves the living room. Henry’s hearty voice, somehow always jolly, blares out from the phone. “Hullo there Ricky, your father made it clear to us once upon a time that hours like this were never meant for discussions of business…” Ricky didn’t hesitate “I’ve found what Josh wanted me to find” There was a pause on the other side of the line that lasted for a few seconds but it seemed like an eternity to Ricky. Henry’s voice sounded out again but this time it was more serious, lacking the warmth and pleasant tone of before. “I see, being the bright boy you are I supposed you understood completely what he intended for you to know” Ricky opens the book to the thumb-marked page. “I never expected so many names though…” Surprisingly, Henry reverted back to the jolly sounding voice of before. “Ah…on the way to the top, your father and us of course never doubted for a moment that there would be those who would stand against us be it in the open fields or shadows for reasons both known and unknown” Ricky’s eyes scan down the pages as he speaks into the receiver. “Yes, I guess that was expected that but some of the names there…” The sound of solitary laughter bellows from the other side of the line. “We stepped on all kinds of toes, some that we could quash at any moment, some who were…hmm…how should I put it…had quite the degree of “prestige” in their names” As Ricky flips to another page, Henry continues his revelation. “Your father was a man who does everything by meticulous planning and organization, right down to that little black book. If you haven’t noticed, he grouped the names in categories, each unique in their own way but all the same, they are those the Starr Enterprise would rather see as pieces of road-kill if not anything else” Ricky muttered to himself as he continues reading the pages… “Blackmail…Debts…Pillaging…Parasites…Scandals…” Henry continues speaking to Ricky “Your father was very aware of his mortality even before he set up shop downtown and made his first killing in the world of business. He knew that as brilliant and capable as he may be, time would get to him before he could finish everything. However, your father has a habit of planning for the future, even beyond the grave. So he elected you to finish whatever equations are left in the life of the enterprise and on top of that list was his little, innocent looking black book…” Ricky took a deep breath and exhaled again as he went through the book. He could only manage a short reply “I see…” Henry’s voice sounded more encouraging than anything else at the moment “Don’t worry about it too much Ricky. It’s not what it looks like. I know, we all know there’s a good deal of names in that book but remember, we’ll always be behind you. We made this enterprise and we will do what is necessary to keep the legacy alive for Joshua’s sake, for our sake, for your sake Ricky” Ricky nodded his head, although nobody else was there to acknowledge it. He manages another short reply to Henry “Thank you very, very much Henry…” The sound of solitary laughter can be heard again from the other side of the line. “Never a problem Ricky” Ricky flips the pages back again and after a moment’s hesitation, blurts out to Henry. “So….who’s first?”
