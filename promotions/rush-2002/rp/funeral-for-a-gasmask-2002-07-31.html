Funeral for a Gasmask...

31 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7.31.02 [i] 'I did it...my way.'--Frank Sinatra It's been a while since I have seen disorder, and I am the one that tends to enjoy it. Actually, not only do I enjoy it, I breathe every second of it, and when it rears it's ugly head and stares me directly in the eyes, I get a tad exilerated. I am, however, a master of restraint, and seeing as I am doing what I am doing for my life, my career, then I can absorb just a little at a time. Luckily, a little is all that we have had to face. Everyone has banded together, risen above the controversy, and as we stand now, we are back on solid ground, staring back at the past of the sinking sand. Lucky me, lucky you... Although I am facing TWO people on Thursday, there is only but ONE person left on the list, left on the ladder before making it to the top. The only man in my way, G.O.D. This one person is the one person that stands in the way, the one person that has needed, the one person that I have craved to destroy. He is one of the men that have literally taken RUSH by storm, and what I want is not a victory, is not only to rule over G.O.D., but to literally end G.O.D. Keep in mind, that I am a heel wrestler, I am a heel, I do whatever I want to do... but even sometimes you have to take something for the federation, do you not? This is that time for me, and it is only an Overdose DDT away from occuring. Over the past month, I have been beaten, broken, bruised and bloodied, nearly twice a week no less. I have taken chairshots nearly twice a week, I have taken the jobber-fests nearly twice a week, I have been attacked by two, sometimes three men at once. Of course, the only thing that Boda hasn't been able to do, the only thing that he couldn't achieve, the only thing that Rich Cannon and Ricky Starr weren't able to do...was successfully take the Network Title away from me. I still stand, at the brink of garnering the Heavyweight Title strap. I still stand at the edge of legendary status. I still stand at the verge of untapped greatness. I am getting ready to blast off the rocket that is my career, and the countdown will commense now... Do not think that I have not noticed while each and everyone of you rookies comes into RUSH Wrestling, thinking that they will 'borrow' some of the techniques that made us the solid, well-known, well respected, and great wrestlers that the original RUSH Wrestlers are? Do you think I will allow this to happen? Do you think WE will just let you try to be us? If you believe that since you are a rookie, since you are just entering into a world where you have never been. If you think that cause you are venturing for the first time into the deep end, we will just help you swim? Wrong, we are not the little minnows that you can swim with, we are the sharks, the eels and everything else big and mean. We will come for you NTICWers, because we do not accept imitations, especially not in RUSH where the best succeed and everyone else who thinks there the best, just annoy the Hell out of us that really are. But no, it isn't that I am getting scared, or nervous, or feeling the butterflies inside of mystomach. It isn't that I'm afraid or pertrified of fighting against the odds. It ins't that I am is frightened by the thought of either having to carry a large load on my shoulders in RUSH, or perturbed by the thought of having to step into the ring with someone who undoubtedly has a few of the other contenders waiting in the wings to hammer me down. It isn't that at all, it quite simply is this...I am is getting PISSED OFF. See, I have taken it all, and although I have accepted each match, although I have battled through each match, I have been prepared through each attack and that is what really matters in the big picutre, the grand scheme. Boda, I am out to get you, through all of your detours and all of your roadblocks. Through all of your swerves and all of your bookings. Through all of your attempts, and all of your own faults. I have been fighting and ready, and I will most certainly prove to you how much I am worth to this company. The fuse is lit, but the explosion has yet to come... [/i] (The camera fades in to show a gray sky covered in overcast. The mood is depressing. Rumblings of thunder are heard in the background and flashes of lightning is seen in the clouds. However, there are tiny flowers growing in the overpowering grass. The cameraman zooms in on the grass to catch the dew drops from the early morning mist and the buds hiding in the tall sleaves of grass. We turn around to reveal a winding dirt road. A red limosuine comes into view coming around a turn. It drives slowly, wrapping around the tight dirt curves, and passing over the rather insignificant pebbles that cover the road. The camera reveals a small field with tiny and cheaply made slabs of rock enclosed on three of four sides. Suddenly, the door for the limo slowly opens. Sirius Cage exits wearing a black suit, personally tailored for him. He wears no expression at all. Scott Cage steps out whearing a black striped suit with a pair of black sunglasses covering his eyes. The Cage brothers straighten out their suits, brush off the dust and walk towards the tombstone. They look at the inscription...) [center]MARVIN HARRIS[/center][center]1977-2002[/center] (Scott pauses and contemplates something, most likely the sudden disappearance of a great person and athelete. Sirius looks up at Scott with a saddened face on and wipes a fake tear from his eye. Scott just stands there, solid as a rock. Not budging, and not showing any feeling. Suddenly he breaks the slinece...) Scott Cage: Poor Marvin Harris...he always had the strength in him to succeed. For I have always believed that he has been my greatest enemy...yet my greatest friend. Pity he had to go insane...Sirius Cage: Scott! Have some respect for the dead. He may not be who you thought but at least give the man some dignity. He escaped from the hospital, and who knows where he is now?Scott Cage: Exactly, so why should we care? The chances that a man with as much will as Marvin Harris suddenly DYING would simple be...impossible.Sirius Cage: Maybe if we stay here and reflect, the answer will come to us. Scott Cage: Yeah and maybe if I kiss enough ass, I'll get a Syndicate Title shot at the next pay per view. Please, give me a fucking break. (Suddenly, lightning strikes down a tree nearby the limo and nearly crushes it. Scott and Sirius jump back, as Scott wipes his forehead...) Sirius Cage: Now, although I have never been here before, but I do know that you aren't supposed to curse. Scott Cage: Yeah...maybe you're right. Don't want to piss HIM off again. Sirius Cage: Excellent. (The Cage Brothers bow their heads in silence, remembering the late great Marvin Harris...) Scott Cage: Well enough reflecting, I'm bored.Sirius Cage: You have to admit, it's better than what we normally do. Watch wrestlers' interviews and then bumble around like an idiot?Scott Cage: Am I that predictable? Sirius Cage: More than you can imagine. (Scott looks around, sniffles, and then turns towards the limosuine. He walks over as Sirius follows. The brothers enter the limosuine and drive off, as the last picture we see is the silouette of the grave that supposedly inhabits Marvin Harris...)
