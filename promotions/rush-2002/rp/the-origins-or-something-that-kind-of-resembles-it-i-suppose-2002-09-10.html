The Origins, or.. something that kind of resembles it, I suppose

10 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sept 10th, 2002 The Origin! Or.. Something.. That.. Almost resembles it, I suppose. Slow steps, down a dark hallway. The dim light, hitting the leather, of a black and red luchadore mask. The tall, dark masked stranger's unshaved jaw is clenched tight. He slowly blinks, his muscles flexing, as he walks, in a constant state of readiness, prepared for anything. This.. Is our hero. The Masked Mullet. He is: tall, strong, muscular, brave and heroic. A shining, mulleted beacon to the American way, justice, and freedom, and the redneck way.. The only good thing to ever come out of South Dakota. Including their beauty pageant contestants.. I mean, c'mon have you seen them?! I think I'd rather see that cow in high heels again.. But, well. Um, never mind. It was late, I was really drunk.. Umm. Back to the story. Footsteps echoed down the dreary, dark hallways, with a strong, gorilla like hand, he reached out, and grasped the handle, and swung it open, out to the back lot, of a large arena. His blue eyes, surveyed the back lot, and a smile creeped slowly across his lips. And he spoke. 'Hello Friends! Pass me a beer!' The three men sitting on coolers looked up, at their long time companion, and co-worker, and with a nod, they reached into the pop-top, and through him a beer. But, unfortunately for our hero, this was no ordinary beer, oh no my friends. This beer was tainted with radioactive waste. Otherwise known as Budweiser. 'What the hell is this crap?' The Masked Mullet sneered, with his mighty lips, as he raised a slender brow, though.. you couldn't really see that, due to the mask and all. Paul Pluto, blinked and looked up at his friend, 'It's beer, my friend.' 'Paul, Paul, Paul.. My John Jupiter obsessed friend. This, is well crap. Where's the Coors?' The Masked Mullet smiled, and tossed the Bud at a production van, nailing it right on the side, and sending the production crew screaming, allowing 30 seconds of penthouse TV, to come over the CCW-Tron, and block the PPV, that was currently in progress. Which lead it to become the highest selling PPV that the original CCW ever made. Production crew, and porn, in a production truck, always meant trouble. A hearty laugh followed from the masked one, and the rest of the ring crew buddies. Slowly, yet surely, Bobby Conley spoke. 'I think.. I have some Coors and MGD, I'll toss you one.' Slowly, Bobby reached down into the cooler, and tossed the beer, towards his masked friend. It was at that moment, that the gods, and celestial entities struck. No, Not Akiyama, but other gods. The Masked Mullet's eyes widened, as he felt his senses opening, his mind growing, he felt.. Power, beyond his wildest imagination. Though, while his senses were opening, the cans of Coors struck him along side the head, sending him sprawling to the ground, the shock from his 'awakening.' He just opened his mouth, and gasped, as his friends surrounded him. To Be Continued. Cianan just blinked, as he put down the Comic. 'This, was by far one of the stupidest things I've ever seen.' He said slowly, pinching the bridge of his nose, and shaking his head. 'Hey! I think it's cool! It's all inked and shiznit!' Vanilla Skillz responded, with a nod, and grin. 'You also stare at lava lamps for hours on end.' 'They.. They’re cool... yo?' Skillz whimpered. 'Excuse me?' The timid artist, Nate Horsfall spoke up softly, and he nervously adjusted his hat. 'You know, I really didn't want to do this project, but the comic book company thought, that a character with.. Mullet's.. Attitude and persona would be perfect for a comic book. Almost all the popular wrestlers get comics: Chyna, Kevin Nash, Mankind, and Steve Austin...' Cianan raised a brow, then looked to The Masked Mullet, who is grinning ear to ear, reading the comic intently. 'Whoooie! I didn't remember that it happened like this! I just thought I got hit upside the head with a beer can!' He exclaimed happily. Cianan sighed, and rubbed his temples with slow care, 'Clem. Did you spend all week on this?' 'Yup!' He grinned, as he flipped a page. 'What about your match?' Cianan said slowly. 'Match?' 'Yes. For Rush?' He was slowly losing is patients. 'Um.. I forgot about it?' Mullet, looked up, if he wasn't wearing the mask, you'd see the sheepish expression on his face. 'Mark....' Cianan slowly turned towards Vanilla Skillz, who took a slow step back. 'Yo! It's Vanilla Skillz! The coolest white person on the planet!' 'You're supposed to be his manager, why did you let him.. FORGET ABOUT HIS MATCH!?' Cianan groaned, and placed his hand over his face, sighing heavily. 'Yo, yo, yo! I did manage him, Home Skillet! D'ya know how much green we're gonna for this deal, yo??' 'Wrestling, Skillz. You're supposed to manage him in wrestling.' 'Oh. Dayum.' 'Gah! That's it, I'm going back to Japan!' Cianan stormed out of the small office, and slammed the door behind him. Skillz and The Masked Mullet just looked at each other, blinking. 'So, Kevin Nash really had his own comic..?' The Masked Mullet asked slowly. The End (For Now.)
