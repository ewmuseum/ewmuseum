Date: in a long-ass time “I don't want to talk to you no more, you empty headed animal food trough wiper! I fart in your general direction! Your mother was a hamster and your father smelt of elderberries!” - The French Soldier guy from Monty Python & The Holy Grail I suppose there’s a subtle element in humor the cat-and-mouse game that wrestling can become. Not so much for the plebians, the common men, the guys who aren’t really talented enough to become exceptional in any way shape or form. But for those privileged few, those ubermensch, as Dostoevsky would put it, who have abilities and destinies that far succeed that of your average, run of the mill vanilla citizen, we in that small society are entitled to sit back on occasion and have a hearty laugh at the futile attempts of the unexceptional to exceed limits that have always – and will always - hold them back. For instance, say I win a title in an organization. Say it’s the middle title, the second best behind the World championship. Say it brings with it a level of exposure that I have not yet had, but that many people say I was ready to receive. And now that I’ve done so, after a few months of striving to attain that title, say that a brash, trash-spewing, unexceptional trout of a man, who believes himself to be superlative to the limitations set forth on him by nature and by genetics, say this man suddenly takes it upon himself to come after me and my aforementioned title, for the simple reason that his delusional mind believes him to be more deserving of it than the person who busted their balls to get it in the first place. And then, to further embarrass himself in the long run, say this glorified punk, say he has a belt of his own, some garbage belt that was previously held by a no-shower. So he parades this belt around like it’s worth a good goddamn, and spend his time winning the curtain-jerkers, beating out the new hirees and every other two-bit jobber that wants to contend for the belt, since no one comes after this guy unless they’re forced to, because it’s clearly the next closest thing to being buried. This guy, he thinks he’s hot stuff because he can hold his own against a bunch of transfers and no-showers, so he starts getting a swelled head. Thinks he’s better than he is. Then he goes so far as to start calling out the guy who had my belt, while I’m in the middle of killing myself to win it legit. So when his stupid-ass plan fails, and I’m the one walking out with the belt, he suddenly lets his balls drop again and he starts coming after me like he’s gonna stand some sort of chance. And if you’re wondering why that doesn’t sound so hard to believe, Scotty, that’s because it’s happening right now. It’s reality. You certainly do enjoy talking a lot, don’t you, Scotty? You like coming here and running your mouth about this and that, about how you’re so much better than everyone else, yet you have yet to prove such incredibly prowess in the ring itself. You’re mouth runs a lot of game when you’re far away from the action, but I think your track record alone should be enough to keep you quiet. Let’s review, shall we? You come into the place, first couple weeks, I don’t remember, I wasn’t here. You lose the Network title along with all the other up-and-comers-who-aren’t-awfully-that-good-in-the-first-place to Marvin Harris. Marvin disappears of the face of the earth, and you, by the narrowest margin and the stroke of luck as being the only guy who had a major match with that guy and likely the only guy who they could strap with the belt without raising some sort of commotion, right? I mean, if Marvin had been around, surely he would’ve pounded you into the ground with your feeble promo-cutting skills and your inability to do anything different than a bunch of long paragraphs in italics and then some half-assed pointless dialogue at the end, right Scott? I think it’s pretty obvious that if he had even showed up your ass would have been back at the bottom of the talent pool waiting for a push or a merger to spark you with something that you could work with. It’s not like you were going to get off your ass and do it on your own, were you? Of course not, just cut another ridiculously long and pointless promo, that will fix everything, won’t it? At least you thought it would, huh Cage? So you figured, since that’s the same style that gave you an awful heel run as the second, and second-most-forgotten Network champion, of course that would’ve carried you to separate heights altogether, huh? I guess what you’re scheduled to receive in the next 24 hours is the nice cold slap of reality across that ugly face of yours. One thing I’ve noticed you do entirely too much is put words in people’s mouths. Do me a favor and never speak another word to me for the remainder of your time in RUSH, huh? I’d appreciate it, really I would. Because I go to see what you’ve been saying in promos and all I see if big huge paragraphs. Most of the time I give up and move on to something else like television or masturbation, but on occasion I’ll throw you a bone and try and actually read one of your promos, but it usually isn’t a smart thing to do unless I have some ice or migraine tablets nearby, what with the ungodly length and mind numbing content of them usually making my eyes bleed and whatnot. But on these rare occasions, what I tend to do is skim the entire thing, and wait until my name is brought up. And when I do, I must say, I am very displeased. You speak to me as though you’ve had an actually conversation with me, like I’m somehow you’re friend. Clearly, Mr. Cage, you do not pay attention to what’s going on. I doubt if I’ve spoken to you once before. Hell, you should consider yourself lucky enough that I’m even using the first person to talk to you now. Even if this is a parody, it’s still rough for me to try and go a few sentences without blurting out “El Spiffy!” or “The Spiffy” but then again when I look at it you never really cared about that, did you. No, of course not, you’d just toss my name in at random intervals, then going on to some other inane point, a habit which you seem to be very good at. You put words in my mouth, like stupid crap, setting up for some stupid idea you have? Who does that? Who taught you to talk like that? Are you insane? Or retarded? Just a questions but you, Scott, should seriously have your head examined. And while I’m on the subject, let me mention that you need to quit living in the past. You reference Marvin Harris in every single damn promo. And while I might have understood that two months ago, it’s freakin August for God sakes. Live in the now for once. I mean, it’s understandable that you’d reference a victory over Marv because it’s clearly turned out to be the biggest of your career, no? Of course it has. I hardly consider Ricky Starr or J. Rish or anyone else that you’ve ‘defended the belt’ against to be on par with something I would consider to be huge in a career building way. But let’s be honest with ourselves, Scotty, it’s all been downhill from there, hasn’t it? Even when you were the champ, after that first day, it’s been like a downward spiral that you can’t snap out of. Always struggling for a way out, always cutting promos way above what you really can attain, writing checks with your mouth that your ass’s account has run out of money for. Isn’t it true? I mean, I won’t sink to your level and put words in your mouth, but you’re starting to realize that the Scott Cage train is running out of gas, right? You can see that your time as an elite member, even if it was by default in the first place, you can see that that is coming to an end, huh? You’re on the tail end of the success half of your career. Change with the times or get left behind. I mean, I’m sure you’re getting pissed that everyone else is getting pushed around you, but it’s about damn time you came to terms with the fact that there’s a reason for it. I hope you’ve enjoyed this little talk, Scott. I hope this promo gave you the same kind or headaches that your gave me. And now for some pointless dialogue.
