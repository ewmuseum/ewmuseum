The Last Slatkovic (Final) { PPV}

6 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/6/02 Without the desire to live, there is no love of life--Albert Camus L'Envers L'Env Ricky Starr, the self-proclaimed “Natural Born Talent” of the RUSH professional wrestling organization, awoke to one objective and one objective only in his mind To find out the decision of his younger brother Will he still drift away from him? Leading the very life the sought to escape from the torn land from whence they came? Or will he put it all behind him and lead the life they sought to live, a new life riddled neither with misery nor bloodshed. The life of a Starr Ricky didn’t even bother to do his morning calisthenics or rituals. He sensed something different in the air, an air of change as if something, or someone had shifted. It was an instinct in him that told him that through gut reactions, an instinct that slept for so long through his years as a Starr It was brotherly instinct As he raced down the stairs, sprinting through the corridor and leaping obstacles, he finally stopped at the living room. He looked around as if to gage if anything was out of place. Ricky caught sight of an open balcony door, the curtains previously shrouding it drawn aside hastily with their fabric moving to the gentle breeze. It was as if someone had broken into the house, or more like broken out of the house. Seeing it told Ricky more than he wanted to know. He paces slowly to the center of the living room, trying to digest and come to terms with his morning discovery. Ricky sat down on a cushy looking couch, or more like fell back onto it. The lone Starr ponders, most likely pondering his time spent with his long-lost younger sibling, hardened to the world and cold beyond his years. He was not the smiling visage of innocence that lifted spirits with him when times seemed bleak. Nor was he the epitome of youthful optimism in a time when the world just seemed to be giving up. The man, the blood brother Ricky found the other day was just a cynical, hard, cold individual just trying to get through each day intact. This individual would do anything for that, even if it means resorting the very thing they tried to escape: Bloodshed It was hard for Ricky to grasp it and take it all in. He had lost a father, three sisters and friends to the bestial, ruthless machination of war. His mother joined millions of innocents killed in crossfires around the world without justification. On top of that he was powerless to stop or at least make an attempt to right any of it. Upon stepping foot in the new land, he lost his brother to the bewildering maze of concrete, steel and glass known as New York. All of this contributed to him being what he is now A Starr He became a Starr in order to breath into the vassal the courage that the person before never had, the strength that he never possessed, the moves he could never pull off, the life he thought he could never live in a lifetime But most importantly It gave him what he never had since day one on this plane of existence A chance As Ricky Starr stood up gloomily from his previous seated position, he feels something as his hands reached for the cushion below him for support. It felt like paper to his touch. Ricky grasped it and pulled it up to his eye level as he stood up. It seemed to be an enveloped addressed to nobody in particular. Inside he felt something small yet dense sticking out. He opened it up and took out a few pieces of paper with writing on them. As the papers are unfolded, it is revealed that it seems to be a letter from Steffan Brother Remus, I leave your gracious abode happy with the fact that I have met you again after what seems to me a long period of time apart. It has been almost an eternity since I have last felt this way. Both you and I know that all that we have been through from the times back in our land of birth right up to the present have influenced, shaped and ultimately made both of us what we are now. In many ways you have become stronger, more confident and wiser too. If there was anything that remained unchanged within you, it is your determination and resolve. It was that which brought us out from the sea of chaos, strife and misery. In a way, not just have these years changed and molded us but they have also taken their toll on us. You only know too well what the years have done to me, making me almost nothing more than a desperate shell wanting nothing more than to just live through the night. As strange or as unacceptable it may seem to you, the years have exacted a toll from you as well. I do not blame you for being what you are now; in fact, it made you a better person, if not the best. However, for you, strength, courage, confidence and wealth came at a price Like you said about me, now I shall say the same. You are not the same Remus I knew from my innocent days as a boy growing up The Remus I knew in my living memory was not just one with strong determination and resolve but he was also proud of who he was and it didn’t matter how bad things were or how bleak his life seemed. At least he remembered who he was and didn’t shun it. He was a Slatkovic The you of the past, the Remus I knew, believed in the American Dream. You dreamt of freedom, you dreamt of living free from misery, you dreamt of no more unnecessary bloodshed You dreamt of being given a chance They were all the things you could ever want Now it seems that you have lived out your dream, if not getting more than you bargained for but at what price? You are no longer you. You have become another entity, one who goes by the name of Ricky Starr. You have not fully shunned what you were but prefer not to show it when possible. You are either here… Nor there I can see the subconscious doubt in your eyes every time I look into them. Although my life was far from good and most certainly far from what we came here to attain, at least I have something that re-assures me every night when I rest my head on a relatively flat surface That my name is Steffan That I am a Slatkovic And nothing can ever change that The pendant you gave me is enclosed in the envelope. I appreciate the offer for a better life and another chance but I think my identity is too large a price to pay for some superficial comforts. So you’re saying that you would very much rather live life by the rules of bloodshed and brutality again!? Being a Slatkovic doesn’t make a difference, bloodshed will always be bloodshed, and brutality remains unchanged no matter what name it goes under. However, please go out onto the balcony. Ricky walks out onto the balcony from the still open glass siding door Remember the places with nice chairs where we have our evening conversations? Ricky turns his head right and catches sight of a neat group of chairs, arranged around a glass table. He walks towards the neat setting as he continues reading the letter the white chair was always yours for some reason. Mine was the black. I have left some possessions of mine on that chair. Ricky looks at the black chair beside the white and for a while he just stood there dumbfounded at what he saw I do not need them anymore Ricky caught sight of two shining silver semi-automatic pistols arranged neatly with the barrels crossing each other. At least it’s a start Do not worry too much for me for I shall be fine indeed. If life cannot give me another chance, I shall give myself one. These fingers of mine shall no longer pull the trigger for me to live. I cannot undo what I have done before or compensate in full those I have hurt as a result of my desperate actions. I hope to start anew, whether life will give me that much grace or not, that is left for time to reveal Your Younger Brother Steffan Slatkovic Ricky sat down on his chair at the balcony and looked out onto the panoramic view with his eyes drifting in thought. The letter now rests on his hip as his mind ponders what he just read in an effort to digest them fully. As the sun rises to a high noon, Ricky Starr realizes he has not done anything else on that day. He quickly rises to his feet and makes his way back into the house but before he walks in, something gleaming on the glass table caught his eye It looked small and insignificant as Ricky Starr picked it up and held it in his palm but somehow it struck a chord in him It was a pistol bullet Tarnished by age but a live bullet nonetheless On its side was carved crudely but clearly in writing “Nevermore”
