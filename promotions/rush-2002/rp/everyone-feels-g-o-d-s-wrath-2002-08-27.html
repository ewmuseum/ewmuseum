Everyone Feels G.O.D.'s Wrath

27 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/27/02 They’d had a week off from work. Not entirely, Dr. Jenkins wouldn’t have allowed that, but they didn’t work as much. Adrenaline had been pushed back a week and they had longer to train, to work, to get over the fact that they were so much better than most everyone else… The Pay-Per-View had been a success. Most every person thought that G.O.D. would lose. That Livewire or Scott Cage or Joe Bananas would win. Dr. Jenkins had to scoff at that. No one could beat almighty G.O.D. In a fair fight it was simply impossible. This had been proven on so many occasions. G.O.D. was unstoppable. Nothing and no one was better than he was. This title reign proved that completely. Scott Cage, a man who did in fact get some shots in, was decimated. Scott Cage: The former Network Champion. Scott Cage: The favorite of the night. Scott Cage: The Syndicate Title number one contender. Scott Cage: The man that tried and failed. Scott Cage: The man that couldn’t beat The Genetic Oriented Drone. Scott Cage: The man who felt G.O.D.’s wrath. Scott Cage succumbs to the power of Gene Otto Droidman. He saw three hundred and ninety five pounds, just a shade under four hundred, fall onto of his chest. Doubts flew into the folk’s minds as Gene took that superkick like it was a lick from a puppy. All night he had been scratched, torn, beaten, and hit and enough was enough. This was the final man. A man who couldn’t win. A man named Scott Cage. That superkick was the last time he would touch the winner. A powerbomb, a table, and God Speed later Scott Cage’s title reign was finished. Over. No more. Done. Ended. That was at Crossroads. This was not. After Crossroads G.O.D. and Dr. Jenkins went off to celebrate just as Jenkins had promised. They stayed at that amusement park from when it opened until when it closed. The smile across Gene’s face was a child’s smile. It lasted the entire trip and more. He was happy. And it was not just a human happiness. No, it was more like a dog’s happiness. For G.O.D. is much like a dog. He is as loyal as anything is. He can and is disciplined greatly. He is eternally distracted. He is eternally happy. He is a dog. A great beast of a dog. A killer dog. One that one leaves as a guard dog to attack strangers, but will eat anyone. Except his owner. Yes, G.O.D. is a loyal dog and Dr. Jenkins is his owner. Dr. Jenkins is the one man G.O.D. will not eat or destroy. Crossroads was quite a success, but onto Adrenaline a new challenger awaits, and again people assume he will destroy The Genetic Oriented Drone. That The Genetic Oriented Drone cannot win. Oh how wrong they are. Livewire will feel the wrath. Livewire will know he just lost to the almightly. Livewire… G.O.D vs. Livewire. Feel… G.O.D.’s… Wrath!
