The Last Slatkovic (part 3) { PPV}

6 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/6/02 Merrick and Kelleher were standing underneath the small archway that protruded from the top of the backdoor to Club Silverheart, appropriately named for being a living, breathing heart of the New York underworld. It was everything an underworld joint could hope to be, sophisticated yet sleazy, elegant yet sordid, grotesque yet attractive. That was Club Silverheart, a hub of life for the underworld Also the site for an entrapment in the works Whoever was the mastermind behind this set up, he had chosen his pieces well. Merrick was a veteran enforcer in the McNamara collective. A die-hard Presbyterian with an enthusiastic zeal for his work, many call him the gangster’s idea of a workaholic. He became the chief enforcer within a month, an incredible feat considering he didn’t have to kill any of his superiors on the way to the top. His attitude was that of a true professional “Break door, break limb, talk later”. Although not really the sharpest tool in the shed, he generally meant well. His overbearing presence took up the whole doorframe as he waits at the Club Silverheart backdoor Kelleher on the other hand was also known as “Kid Blitz”. The name was pretty much self-explanatory. He goes in fast, hits hard and moves out fast. Just like Blitzkrieg except he was a one-man army, if not THE one-man army. The thing that set him apart from other hired guns was that Kid Blitz believed in minimizing risk, so his specialty, taking the whole house down before doing the running man into the night. Together, they form a vanguard duo, standing vigilant on a watch for their target. Kelleher reaches inside his jacket and pulls out a pack of Dunhills. He deftly pulls out a cigarette and sticks it in his mouth. A lighter soon emerges in his left hand and struck, producing a small flame, probably the closest and brightest form of direct illumination between them at the moment. As the cigarette is lit and smoke is puffed out, the lighter disappears back into the confines of a pocket. As the Kid Blitz pulls out the cigarette after a long drag, Merrick studies him carefully “Your hands are shaking” As Kelleher exhales a puff of smoke; he turns to Merrick and shoots him a dirty look. He takes another pull and as he exhales, he produces a reply “What is it to you?” An ugly smile emerges on Merrick’s face “You scared or something?” Kid Blitz lifts up his free left hand and confirms Kelleher’s observation. It seems to be shaking but as he takes another pull, the shaking subsides slightly. He looks up at Merrick with a smirk “Being Kid Blitz takes a big one out of you, especially when you’ve been at it for the last five years” Merrick lets out a hoarse chuckle from under his breath “So after five years then you pissed your pants” Kelleher shoots a look at him almost instantly, catching Merrick off-guard with his quick response “Keep up with the shitty wisecracks and you’ll be seeing first hand why I got that name” Merrick walks up to Kelleher, towering over him, as they stand toe-to-toe in the night. The chief enforcer’s form casts a shadow over Kid Blitz. Unfazed, Kid Blitz looks up and finds himself going face-to-face with a giant of a man looking back down at him with eyes which could have been easily chiseled in granite. “And you kiddo are going to find out why they don’t call me chief enforcer of the McNamara stock for nothing” Their confrontation proved to be short-lived as they both heard the sound of footsteps approaching the alley. Both of them shift their attention to the direction where the sounds emanated from. Kelleher lifts a hand to signal for Merrick to be quiet as he pricks an ear out to verify the sound. His suspicions were correct, they were getting louder as whoever was making them got closer and closer to them. The veteran McNamaran enforcer looks out in the distance. A shadow appears It seems to be that of a man Judging from the outline of the silhouette cast, it seems that the man is bent slightly forward, both hands in pockets as the tail end of his jacket flaps in motion with his trot. Merrick puts a large hand inside his coat, holding on to something in there, something which he most certainly intends to use in the not-too-distant future while his companion, Kelleher calmly flicks away the butt of a finished cigarette into the distance and waits coolly, his hands wrapped across his chest in a stern manner. Accompanying the shadow Was a man He was hunched, but that was obviously not his obvious posture. It seems that he is making an attempt to get out of the cold night, his hands tucked deeply into his mustard-colored jacket pockets. His focus seems to be shifting constantly, between his destination in his mind and his current condition, emphasized by his soft muttering to nobody in particular as he briskly trots down the alley. He is still unaware of the two individuals watching him from the distance. “Looks like him” “It is him” As the haggard man makes his way further down the alley, he suddenly stops and looks up as if sensing something in his immediate presence. He finds himself staring at a slim yet somewhat haunting figure of a man standing in front of him. The slim man seems to be staring at the ground to his right, producing a portfolio style image to the haggard man’s eyes. The slim man then lifts a finger, pointing straight at the other man as his mouth twists into a wide, sordid smile “I’m impressed. Any other fuckwit out there wouldn’t even have seen that coming from a footsie away…. until it’s too late of course” The haggard man looks back with eyes that seem to be undermining the arrogance of the person opposite him “It’s been a long day. I just want to go home and get some sleep, okay?” The haggard man, as if alerted by some inner sense, quickly turns back and finds himself looking at a towering humanoid figure in the shadows facing him. The giant walks out of the shadows, revealing a man in a long coat and a black scarf with one immediately noticeable detail on it, an elaborate stitched-on golden cross. “You must be from the McNamara stock” The big man produces a grin from his face “That’s right” The haggard man stands up straight and looks at both man, at one and then at the other. “If you two are looking for a fight then you best try your luck elsewhere. You won’t find it in me” The slim man turned slightly to get a better look of his mark. His air of arrogance unchanged with a smirk on his face. “The thing is, we’re not looking for a fight” The haggard man distinctly heard the sound of knuckles cracking from behind him as he shifts his attention to the other person “We just wanna get paid” The slim man speaks again, prompting the haggard man to shift his attention “We’re like you, we want to go home. We also want to go home with a fat wallet” ”That’s right”, boomed a deep voice from behind the haggard man “So the voice of the majority has spoken” The haggard man’s ears pricked up at the sound of a jacket rustling “Enough beep-bop, lets cut to the chase” The slim man speaks smoothly to an already alert mark “Don’t move, just close your eyes and think of flowers, rainbows and all that other pretty shit” He could hear something being pulled out He saw no point at reasoning at the moment He saw no other alternative In an instant, the haggard man pulled out something of his own, something small, shiny and metallic from his pocket. In fact, he had two of them. There were exactly four flashes and bangs before he made a straight dive for the corner. All four shots connected with their target Yet he did not fall The towering giant of a man lifted up his jacket to reveal a bulletproof vest. His slim companion grinned at him as both of them move to pursue their haggard mark. Merrick pulled out a large, mean looking automatic shotgun from his long jacket. He fired shot after shot in quick, mechanical repetition into the corner, each hitting random targets of their own including walls, garbage containers, bags of junk and other alleyway stuff. His companion stopped him. A hand signal told the giant to cease his fire as they slowly walked into the dark corner. The smaller man pulled out two guns of his own, Uzi’s with red target lights on them. The laser lights seem to scan every corner of the alley as they made their way down. Suddenly, a silhouette of a man ran across in front of them Uzi’s flashed rapid out death in an instant, spraying the rest of the alley in front of the two-men hunting crew, aided by auto shotgun fire. By the time they were done, almost every inch of the alley was covered in bullet holes. They continued walking forward as they reloaded their weapons. He never saw it coming It was in an instant. Two converged into one Both fired as one Merrick took a double-whammy right between the eyes As of half-a-second ago, another widow and three bastardized children were added to the list of New York life. Kid Blitz turned around to find his chief enforcer companion down and out for the night He sprayed more rapid death all over the alley. At that very moment, it was either he was enveloped in near-limitless rage Or was it fear? Kelleher yelled out “Give yourself up now! I got the bigger guns and more ammo than you can poke a fucking rod at!” At the very moment he spoke the syllable to that statement, a figure of a man strafed in front of him, his haggard eyes staring right into his, his tired look matching the arrogance Kid Blitz once projected. It was mano-e-mano And those were the last things Kid Blitz ever saw before the blinding flash and the deafening bang. James Dunbar heard loud noises coming from the alley behind his club. He excused himself from a lively conversation and went to inspect the alley. Excessive violence in his premises were not good for business and he knew that by heart, even in an underworld joint, that was an unwritten law. When he opened the backdoor to his club, he saw a haggard man with the most depressing of the night he had ever seen. James Dunbar stared in relative shock as he caught sight of the bloodstains on the man’s mustard colored jacket. They seemed to have been sprayed on him. His eyes widened as he caught side of the guns he held, an automatic in each hand. The manager’s mind started thinking like mad, what should he do? Should he make a break for it? Should he talk to the man? Should he attempt to resist? The man just looked at him dully and turned around. He walked off, down the alley, pass his backdoor, just walking off in slow, almost depressing steps. Two hours later, a tired husband would join a wife in bed and in six hours, a daughter will greet her father good morning. Why didn’t he do it? After all he had done before Why not then? Was it because he knew the guy would keep his lips sealed anyways? Was it because for a moment he actually stop to think and ponder that far? Or was it just because his clips were empty? “Much has happened to us since we came to this land” Ricky Starr spoke in a bittersweet manner as he sat beside his younger brother by blood on the golden sands of a beach. In front of them was the weekend life, crowds of people from all walks of life splashing in the sapphire-blue water, playing on the sand or just lying around, doing nothing in particular. Steffan, in brand new clothes bought upon insistence by his elder brother spoke in an equally bittersweet tone “Much has happened indeed, and all I wanted to do was to get through the day” Ricky Starr shoots a puzzled look at his younger brother. Steffan looks out into the sapphire-blue sea, pondering thoughts and memories known to him and him only.
