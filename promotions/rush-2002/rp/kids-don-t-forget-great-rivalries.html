Kids Don't Forget Great Rivalries...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today (The scene fades in to show the outside of Brock Newbludd’s fully functional babe lair in(AKA apartment) in suburban Milwaukee on a surprisingly warm July day. A light summer rain is in progress, melting the piles of snow that have heaped up during the course of the long winter, slowly but surely. It’s probably somewhere around noon, for we can see the sun can be seen peeking out through the overcast skies. We see a few kids running around on the sidewalk, the kids look to be portraying there favorite WrestlingTattler superstars. (These are “actor” kids, real kids don’t try this at home, unless you want Vinny-Mac coming down on your candy ass, NOW back to the RP) Two kids stand on one end of the sidewalk with sneers on their faces. One boy has a shaved head and appears to have used a red magic marker to color a “goatee” on his face. Standing next to him is a boy with short spiked up hair and used a black magic marker to color a goatee on his face also. Both boys have Blood Brothers t-shirts on and black leather Harley jackets on. The boys also look to have a pair of old leather belts with pieces of cardboard taped to the middle of them, each belt has “Champ” written across it in black magic marker. From further inspection, we can see that these two young boys are some huge Blood Brother fans. The one who is dressed up like Brock steps forward and smirks…) Mini-Brock: You two Assclowns want a piece of us? (The mini-Stephen steps forward next to the mini-Brock and gives a smirk also..) Mini-Stephen: You two gangsters think you can handle the five time PCW Tag Team Champions? (The camera pans over to show that the mini-Blood Brothers are standing off against the real life Blood Brothers tag team rivals, Da Westsidazs, in miniature size. These two young boys are dressed exactly like their favorite retired PCW superstars, complete with pants hanging around there knees and oversized “Fubu” t-shirts that hang around their ankles. Both of the boys have their baseball caps turned sideways on their heads and pairs of Oakley sunglasses cover their eyes. Long gold chains with gang symbols hang from their necks and gold rings cover their fingers. One of the boy’s struts into the mini Brock’s face and gives him the “westside” symbol with his fingers. We can see that he must be the Riptah of the two, for “Riptah” is written across his hat in “wite out”…) Mini-Riptah: Yo! Wat you say punk!? Mini Brock: I said you two are assclowns, can you understand that, or do I have to bust out some “gang slang”? (The mini Brock and Stephen high five and give the two Westsidazs the middle finger. The Mini-Short Circuit walks up and shoves the Mini-Brock to the ground. He starts laughing and grabs his crotch with one hand and gives the “westside” symbol…) Mini-Short Circuit: Ha! Eat that shiznit! (The min-Brock gets up and pushes the Mini-Short Circuit down to the ground. Then the four children began pushing each other and throwing punches and wild kicks as the camera pans up to a window on the fourth story of the large apartment complex. As we get closer to the window, the thundering sound of “More Human than Human” by White Zombie becomes more apparent. As the camera becomes level with the window, it zooms in and we are now greeted with the inside of the apartment. More specifically the living room of it. The camera pans around to set the scene. We see the five time PCW tag team champions, Rush superstar Brock Newbludd and retired Stephen Blood sitting in black recliners playing a game of “PCW: March Madness” for Playstation2. We see that they’re in the middle of a heated Hell in a Cell tag match against the now defunct Goovil. Sitting on the long leather couch watching the two friends battle it out is their wives, Lindsey Francis and Stacey Blitsch. We see that Brock is clad in a pair of baggy black and blue camo pants and a pair of plain everyday white socks. A old school MWF shirt with the sleeves ripped off of it shows off his impressive biceps. We notice that Brock has gotten a tatoo on his right bicep that we haven’t seen before. Upon further inspection we can see that the tattoo is of a two serpents with blood dripping from their mouths. The blood runs down their bodies and forms the words “Blood Brothers” underneath them. Brock and Stephen both let out a frustrated groans, for Da Westsidazs just won the PS2 match via pinfall, thus they win the digital tag straps from the Blood Brothers….) Brock: Well, at least we know this game isn’t true to life. (Brock grabs two cold cans of Heineken out of the cooler that sits next to the recliner, he tosses one to Stephen and the two men guzzle some down…) Stephen: No sh*t, we would totally k*icked these guys asses in real life… Brock: We DID kick those guys asses in real life! Stacey and Lindsey: inDEED! (The two blood sisters highfive as Brock and Stephen chuckle…) Stephen: They’re learning… Brock: inDEED! Man, that six-man match is already coming up this Thursday… Stephen: I know, I can’t wait to see you totally annihilate Tex Kelley and his buddies… Brock: Dude, how do you think I feel? Stephen: Those assclown look like they have the wrestling ability of Stewart Hix… Brock: Who? Stephen: You know, he’s that pothead who actually won the PCW belt, but then quit because nobody liked him. Brock: Oh, right him… ( A moment of silence passes over as Brock and Stephen reminisce about good ol’ Stewie…) Stephen: Man, that guy sucked… (Brock grows a smirk…) Brock: Mohammed Nasir sucked. And was a buttmonger to boot. Stephen: Tex Kelley sucks and is just…umm… Brock: He’s just a ….hmmm… Stephen: Who the HELL is Tex Kelley, really? Brock: I dunno, just your run of the mill everyday “pin me, pay me” jobber. (Stephen laughs, and nods his head in acknowledgement and then a loud “beep” is heard in the background and we see that the mini cam that is stationed on top of the big screen TV in the entertainment center of the apartment. A light on the camera flashes red as Brock and Stephen look at each other and shrug their shoulders…) Stephen: Promo time… Brock: Booyah! (Stephen gets up off the couch and walks into his bedroom, probably going to play Playstation. Brock is left all alone in the living room. He adjusts himself on the couch and gets comfortable just as the camera goes from dead to live air…) Brock: Welcome Bloodaholics! You know the bit, I make fun of my opponents until they want to cry in embarrassment like the little b*tches they are… (Brock smirks and lights up a cigarette, taking a long soothing pull off of it…) Brock: Let’s discuss the six-man match, in which the three baddest men in Rush, Brock Newbludd, Boda, and Cassius Deamont face off against a flaming queer, Tex Kelley, a Canadian buffoon, Chris Matthews and a no-name, Tyler Valentine. I mean, good god, could there be anymore of a miss-match? But, never the less, I suppose we will be forced to wax the mat with these three goons. All three of have scores to settle with someone in this match. I have something to prove to Kelley, Matthews and well Valentine as well. Basically it will be the same routine you see each week, I walk down the ramp, beat some ass and get the win. God DAMN! Life is good! (Brock throws a pillow off the couch at the camera and we cut out to blackness…)
