Kidney Checkup...(RP#4 for AD8 and #1 for AD9)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today (Our scene fades in to show RUSH superstar “The Innovator” Brock Newbludd sitting on the end of a doctors exam table in a small doctors office in a location unbeknownst to us. We pan around to see that it is your ordinary doctors office, a small room, with white walls. The exam tables takes up almost half of the room, while in the corner of it lies a small wooden desk with a yellow plastic chair pushed into it. Pieces of medical information and brochures lay cluttered on the desk, most of them about the kidneys and liver and the effects that alcohol has on them. We see other medical information posters pinned up on the wall, among many other things. We pan back to Brock who is now lying back on the exam table, staring at the ceiling rubbing his temples. We see that he is still clad in a pair of baggy blue jeans with snakeskin cowboy boots.. He is not wearing a shirt, and we see scars on Brocks lower back, from where Brock has had numerous surgeries on his left kidney. Brock’s usually styled up hair is now matted down, and his ice blue eyes have a tiresome look on them. Brock rubs his fingers across the scar on his back and lets out a sigh …) Brock: Look at the bright side Brock, chicks dig scars! (Brock lets out a small laugh, and puts his hands behind his head, trying to get comfortable on the somewhat small exam table. We can hear the paper sheet underneath him crinkle and tear as he shifts his body. Then, the door opens up and in walks a older looking man, maybe in his mid to late forties. He is wearing a pair of khaki slacks with a pair of white medical shoes, which does not match at all. A button down blue denim shirt is tucked in to the khakis, while a white lab coat is over top of that. He has a clipboard in his hand, probably with Brock’s status on it. The doctor shuts the door behind him and the man walks across the room, still kinda staring at the scar on Brpck’s back. The older man sits down on the plastic chair by the desk. He looks over the chart some more, and then spins around facing Brock, who has now sat up…) Brock: What’s the prognosis doc? (The doctor shrugs his shoulders and sets the clipboard on the desk…) Doctor: To put it in simple terms, you’ve really screwed up you kidneys… (Brock rolls his eyes and shakes his head…) Brock: Don’t you think I already figured that out ALREADY!? (The doctor gets a surprised look from Brock’s outburst, he puts his hands up in defense…) Doctor: Ease down now, man. What happened is, you’ve put so much tension on that one kidney, it stopped functioning, so, it’s just taking up space in your body. You satisfied? Brock: A little bit, am I going to have to sit out of RUSH, you better say no if you know what’s good for ya… (The doctor gulps and grabs the clipboard from off of the desk, he looks it up and down and gulps again, we can see a bead of sweat run down his forehead, down his nose and splatter on the clipboard…) Brock: Well? (The doctor wipes the sweat off his face and looks up at Brock nervously, who is just staring a hole through the doctor…) Doctor: Whaddya know? Heh…You’re cleared to wrestle…hehe…that’s great… (Brock’s facial expression changes completely and he grows his trademark cocky grin. He jumps up off the table, and slaps the now relaxed doctor on the shoulder…) Brock: Now that’s what I like to hear, I’ll make sure you get your fat overpriced bill, since doctors are greedy bastards and all… (Brock slaps the doctor on the shoulder again, and struts out of the doctors office, shutting the door behind him. We cut out, to show Brock walking down a long hallway inside of the hospital, towards the elevator. A few younger looking nurses recognize him and stop dead in their tracks, either giggling uncontrollably or staring in awe. One nurse runs up to Brock, holding a pen and a piece of paper in her hand. Brock just lets out a grin and sighs as she runs up and stops in front of him…) Nurse: You’re…you’re…” The Innovator”… Brock: In bed, I know. It’s widely known and you don’t have to praise me anymore. But, what can I do for “you” baby? (The young nurse almost melts on the floor, not literally, but you know what I mean. She just looks at Brock with awestruck eyes and holds her pen and paper up higher to him…) Nurse: Can…can I have your…autograph? (Brock shrugs his shoulders casually…) Brock: Sure, why not? (Brock grabs the paper and pen from the nurses hand and signs, “To my #1 fan, “The Innovator” Brock Newbludd”. He hands the paper and pen back to the nurse, who grabs the piece of paper, reads it and faints to the ground. Brock looks down and scratches his head. He looks around, and sees a toothless old janitor who is mopping a little way down the hallway…) Brock: Yo! Janitor! (The janitor spins around and looks at Brock, Newbludd points down at the fainted nurse and then points at him…) Brock: Mop this sh*t up when you get done, won’t ya? (The janitor flashes Brock a dirty look, and goes back to mopping. We slowly fade out as Brock walks towards the elevator doors of the hospital hallway…)
