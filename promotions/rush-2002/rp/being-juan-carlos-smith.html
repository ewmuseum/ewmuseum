Date: from the POV of the commentator... This is ridiculous. A joke. Ever since that hellish stint in the PWA, I’ve been waiting for El Spiffy! to fail and get out of the business. Or at least drop the hood. But now he’s in RUSH. And as much as RUSH sucks, it’s got history. And he’s going for the Syndicate title. And as much as the Syndicate title sucks, it means something to other people. The little bastard just doesn’t stop. First he joins the stable without telling us. Then he signs the endorsement deal with Gates, and decides to bring the guy on the road with us. And now he’s got Black Status and he’s claiming to be a rapper. Ungodly. It’s like if Rey Mysterio had sex with Mango from SNL. The end result is not pretty. Disposing of Brent last week was no problem. McGlocklan shouldn’t be much harder. The NTICW kids are puds. Plain and simple. They don’t belong, and they don’t hang with RUSH vets. But I can’t say that on-air because we’re the ‘faces.’ Good guys don’t trash talk. They just get f*cked up by the King Thug and his merry band of crackheads. Though, we did get some good shots in. The gang war. The bloodbath. The refried beans. All to piss of Quo and the goons. And it worked. We were working a program with Playaz Inc. week in and week out. And yet El Spiffy! STILL has to beat two guys for a shot at the belt. Goddamned Sloboda. We may has well have brought in Russo. Well, we’re in the spot now. Spiffy! Elbows have been falling left and right. We’re one of the top faces in the company. And whether or not people care about Deamont/Matthews is being debated. Sorry, guys. People want to see Quo/Spiffy!. Not Quo/McGlocklan. And sure as f*ck not Quo/Cage. You egotistically ignorant f*ck. Quo v. Spiffy! The man who defeated Shack and sent him home against the man who came to avenge him. Money. And since you administrators are too damned stupid to book it on your own, we’ll do it ourselves. We’ll beat Caleb and take on Quo next week. And then we’ll beat him too. That’s just the way it goes. It starts tomorrow night. Caleb McGlocklan against El Spiffy! for a shot at the Syndicate title. The Mick vs. The Spick. And just like usual around that little bastard… I’m not laughing.
