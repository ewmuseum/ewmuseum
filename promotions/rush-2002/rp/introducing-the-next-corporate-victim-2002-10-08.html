Introducing The Next Corporate Victim

8 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/08/02 Starr Enterprises was also in the law business, being the enterprising organization it is. In fact, quite a number of the board were lawyers by trade, with the most prominent being Dr Atkins. They handled quite a number of cases, ranging from the high profile headliners right down to the hushed dealings that go un-noticed by the everyday Joe and Jane Doe. As long as there was money and benefits to be made, anybody could be a client of the enterprise. However, there were those who both intentionally and unintentionally cross certain thresholds that go against the enterprise-client relationship etiquette of Starr Enterprises. Whether the perpetrators know this or not, they have been monitored and surveyed carefully, with the most minute details of them going into the notorious black book that was compiled by the man himself, Joshua Dubois, or better known as Josh The Starr. Ricky came into possession of this heirloom and took it upon himself to carry out what old Josh could never do in his lifetime. Accompanied by those closest to him and with the blessings and backing of the loyal board, he went on a crusade to finish off whatever equations his father left unfinished. Talking the talk, walking the limelight like it was sunlight, pulling out all the moves without flinching… He was Joshua’s perfect failsafe Dr Atkins waited in his office, the only time he would ever be present in his office was to receive a client. Normally he would be seated in his antique chair with a history of residing in a court of Mandarins, leaning back as he waits for the client to be ushered in. This was not a normal day. Dr Atkins stood up in his office, his stern countenance unblemished even as the CEO of Starr Enterprises, Ricky Starr, sat in the antique chair beside the standing doctor of law. To his left was a man sporting the familiar pair of lime-linseed sunglasses. Attired in a red-coloured zip-up T-Shirt with a pair of jeans and X-trainer shoes to match, he merely added on to the imposing presence in the room. He contrasted to the yuppie-attired Ricky Starr and the proper business wear of Dr Atkins. As the trio waited, the man in sunglasses spoke to Ricky. “This guy, you said he’s pretty famous” Ricky Starr lifted a wagging finger as if to correct his companion. “I said he was pretty AND famous” The young man beside him nods his head in acknowledgement. “Pretty boy heh?” Ricky nodded his head in approval, the trademark smirk coming over his face. “Just makes it better” A female voice blares out over the intercom speakers on Dr Atkin’s desk. “The client is here sir, shall I let him in?” Ricky pressed the reply button and speaks into the set. “Of course” Soon, the door in front of the trio opens and the secretary dressed in a dark green business suit, a young woman who seems dedicated to her job, steps aside to usher in the client… They anticipated this moment with intent. It was not the happiest of days for celebrity, self-professed musician and limelight walker… Justin Timberlake Earlier, he was accosted, harassed and publicly humiliated by the more than tactful duo, the Blood Brothers, one who would be part of the opposition that Ricky would have to go against come Crossroads. Recently, he was denied purchase by a liquor storeowner who was, on a good day, a greasy individual who would not hesitate to pull out a baseball bat at a less than compromising customer. On top of that he was involved in a hit-and-run or to be more accurate, a hit-maim-and-run attempt by the very same brothers who humiliated him in the Mall of America. Outraged, he went to the one place that could guarantee some retribution on his part. Ricky ran his thumb across a line, part of a page that makes up the dreaded black book of Josh The Starr. He knew this particular client would be coming simply by reading the headlines on the local paper and the trash tabloids that sell by the truckload to mothers and bored women across the nation. He also knew because his name happened to be in the book. As Justin Timberlake was ushered or to be more precise, wheeled into the room by the secretary, Ricky did not just see a beaten, maimed man in front of him… He saw a debtor Joshua had put up with more than his fair share of debtors who just seemed to ignore the fact that there was a big fat debt lying on their doorstep. Ricky would not put up with it. And plainly, the sight of Justin seated in that wheelchair, bound with casts, his face making a meagre attempt at a pretty boy smile just gave Ricky a feeling that the individual in front of him was more than just pathetic. Ricky replied with a roguish smile while the men standing on either side of him remained stern. Justin spoke with a voice that was struggling to carry any amount of confidence in it . “Mr Starr, I think you know what I’m here for” Ricky nodded in acknowledgement. “Yes, yes, I do know” The N’Sync member in front of him tried to sound as angry as possible as he spoke again. “Those jerks did this to me…I want payback and I want it NOW!” Ricky nodded in a gesture that was more of a mock-sympathy rather than that of a genuine acknowledgement of Justin’s issue. “You have my sympathies. But to get to the core of the issue, I must bring up something with you” A look of impatience is visible over the pop star’s injured face. “What is it?” Ricky calmly continues “According to some ledgers and files I have searched through, it seems that you have solicited the services of our enterprise before” Justin nodded his head impatiently “Yes…yes…” Ignoring the impatient gesture, Ricky continues “All matters had been resolved in a successful and productive manner utilising the enterprise as a medium” Justin rolls his good eye and blurts out. “Sure…sure…that’s real good and all….” Ricky slams his hand down on the desk, startling the bound Justin. He takes a deep breath and speaks as if he were to drop a bombshell “However, in all instances where you have engaged our services, according to these official and verified records, you Mr Timberlake have not paid a single cent for our services” Brushing away that statement, Justin speaks in an impatient tone “Yeah…yeah…when the next album comes out and goes platinum, I’ll keep your organization in mind…” Ricky Starr, the CEO of Starr Enterprises stands to his feet and walks slowly towards the seated figure of Justin Timberlake. As he draws closer with each step, Justin seems more puzzled and in many ways, intimidated. As he reaches within an arm’s length of the disabled pop star and idol of pre-pubescent swooning fans around the world, Ricky kneels down to face him eye-to-eye. He stares down at nothing in particular for a few seconds before looking up to face Justin in the eye. “Let me be real honest here for a second….” As he speaks, Ricky puts a hand on the side of the so-called musicians tortured face. “For starters, I’m not Josh. I’m Ricky” Ricky looks back at Dr Atkins, who merely nods and to his companion sporting a pair of sunglasses, who gives a thumbs up. “Now that we’ve cleared that up, let me introduce you to the way Ricky does things around here” He studies Justin’s face for a moment before continuing. Beads of sweat trail down the singer’s forehead as the intimidation continues. “For a start, I don’t take, if you don’t mind me saying, shit from other people. I know it’s a word that isn’t thrown around in your song too much but bear with me here” That statement was finished in a sarcastic note as Ricky flashes his ever-familiar smirk much to the amusement of his sunglasses toting companion watching and much to the disgust of Justin Timberlake. “You will be hearing a lot of words that are never used in your sugar-coated songs, which if I might add do not at all bear any resemblance to love in the real world. Now Joshua may not be a man of many words but he does not just forget” Ricky moves in closer, close enough to be able to whisper to Justin without much difficulty. The boy band member tries to lean back but finds that his wheelchair isn’t as flexible as he thought it would be. “Now, being that you already walk in here and had the audacity moan and disregard whatever we had to say to you, I think you owe us an apology” Justin tried to protest but was somewhat intimidated by an imposing Ricky. He opens his mouth meekly and reluctantly. “I’m…sorry” Ricky’s taps his palm gently on the boyish cheek of the maimed vocalist in a gesture of mock comforting. “That’s a good boy…” For a moment, it seemed like he hesitated. Then it was Justin Timberlake, who wouldn’t? The force of the impact knocked Justin back; his body sent sprawling to the ground from his wheelchair with an ugly thud. With his good hand, Justin clutched the bridge of his nose while yelling out words that one would never hear from a boy-band pop star. “…ing hell…you jerko!…” Ricky wrapped his hands across his chest and laughed at the spectacle in front of him. His companion joined in while Dr Atkins merely looked on emotionless. “Did I tell you? Sorry isn’t good enough for your case!” Both men move in on Justin, who suddenly scurries to a corner and cower. His voice reduced to a high-pitch muttering and his eyes welling up with tears. Ricky looked at his companion who nodded in acknowledgement and looks back at a pathetic Justin. “I’m sending a message to a select few, one of them whom you know very well. It’s basically saying that if anybody makes an attempt to shit on me, I stomp at them right back without hesitation. It starts with you…” Shadows loomed over him as he looked up Bloodied Maimed Completely and utterly helpless Two figures are alone in a room full of shadows. Both are men One is standing The other has fallen The good doctor looks down and for the first time of the day, produces a smile on his face. As if finding relief from the downed man, he looks down and cheerfully says. “Take care of yourself lad, don’t go walking about too much now” He laughs at his own sarcastic remark and walks down the corridor whistling. The fallen man tries to inch his way towards some form of support but after a few seconds, collapsed totally onto the floor and lies there sprawling, bloodied and motionless. “By the way, you still owe us a couple of grand. The invoice will be in your mailbox by tomorrow morning”
