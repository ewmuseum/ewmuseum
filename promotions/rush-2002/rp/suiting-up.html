Suiting Up

Date: by me The camera fades in to an empty locker room. The lights are out in the main area, with the lone source of light being some scattered rays from an adjacent bathroom. The camera makes its way through the relative darkness before stopping in front of a figure in the shadows. [A lot changes in a night.] The stranger is sitting on a bench, head buried in hands. Next to them, sitting in one of the rays of light, are a pair of white boots and some red and blue tights. A mask sits clearly in view on the top. [Three men are done for good. Another won’t be back for a while...if at all. In their place come five new faces. Well, new to RUSH anyway. And three of them are already causing trouble.] The figure raises his head and sits up straight. He produces a bottle of something and begins to rub it into his skin. [Rish, Novokayne, and now Selox are nailing people to crosses in a desperate attempt to keep themselves noticed. Deamont has mind-f*cked everyone and their grandmother on his way to the World Title. And his Solution is, well, watered down. And now Quo has added another two militant black man to his little Negro Parade. It’s only a matter of time before they all convert to Islam and start pumping their fists in the air.] He puts down the bottle next to the clothing. In the corner of the light, the label can just barely be made out. “Dr. Sun’s Artificial Skin Tanner” [Drachen is done for life. He should’ve stuck with pizzas in the first place. Then his only problem would’ve been the occasional giant martial-artist turtle. Harris has been mailing in his performances for weeks. Maybe that head on collision will knock some of the drive and talent that everyone claims he has back into him.] The figure rises, prompting the camera to pull back a bit. He grabs the tights from the bench and slips them on, shrouded by the darkness. [Maxx Crimson hasn’t made an impact in weeks. If I need a cage destroyed, I’ll give Maxx a call. But I’m trying to deal with about 11 assholes who want to be on top. Tex Kelley is holding his own. He’s around, at least. But damn, if you’re gonna get tricked by ‘what color is the ground’ then maybe you need to spend some time on the other side of the classroom. Ricky Starr is too busy telling everyone his life story. And Tracer and Flare need to get their feet before they can start using them. So who’s left?] The figure sits back down and begins to lace up the white boots. [Sometimes it becomes necessary for a person to supersede their boundaries. Sometimes you can’t get by on being just comic relief. Not when you’re needed.] Finally, the mask is taken from the bench and disappears into the shadows. [RUSH needs a hero. RAH is a little busy, what with his own job. Someone else needs to stand up against these bastards.] The figure stands, breaking into the light. He finishes tying the back of the mask, and stares into the camera. For the first time, El Spiffy! reveals himself. He looks into the camera, then gives a wink and walks past it, out of the room. [Maybe I’m just the man for the job.] Fade to Black.
