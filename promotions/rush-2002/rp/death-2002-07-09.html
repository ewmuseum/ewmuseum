Death

9 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07 - 09 - 02 AD I have seen the fear on peoples faces as they realize that the bush behind them is now standing. The torment and confusion on there face as realization breaches there mind. I have seen the life trickle from a persons body in my arms. The last few whimpers of pain as another passes away. Death has been a companion with me my whole life. My family was in the business of saving lives from the day I was born. My first recollection of death comes from the age of two. I can still remember the man's skull shattered open in a tear drop shaped wound from his own shot gun. The blood coming out like a volcano some student made for science class in first grade. The bitter reality still stands in my mind as I recall my inability to comprehend only record the events. My father told me that it was a suicide and as they put the man into the ambulance he was still alive. I also remember the burned bodies as fire-fighters pulled the burnt carcass of a human out of a car. The stench of burnt flesh is one of the most distinctive and nauseating smells known to man. The smell is close but worse then that of burnt hair. The body's arms being pulled out of the car stretched like a burnt turkey during thanksgiving. Many of times these poor souls were caused by the use of only small quantities of alcohol, but the tragedy it caused was enormous. Hundreds say that they understand the pain of loss, but none taste it more then those who have to watch there loved ones die in a gory death that was created of negligence. I will not preach to you pain or how it is a sin to kill for I can not believe this or even be a hypocrite. My own exploits I will not share completely for many reasons. The basic fact is that much of the pain and torment or daemons that one holds, embraces or is enthralled with are only fantasies. I understand there desires but so little of the population has it in them to kill, or has it in them to hold the body of a dying person. Even today in Russia the Spetsnaz an elite fighting force is forced during selection to go to crash sites. These men must care for hold and dissect the deceased. Touch a cold corpse as I have and smell the awful smell of decay. Many drop out even more are brought to the bring of insanity. However a few finish having seen, touched, tasted, and brought around the death of many. We think of people like theses as disturbed. We see scenarios where one of these people relishes in the blood bathing in it like a person would bathe in a waterfall of life. Other times we see the scenario where these people crack, break down, go insane, or commit suicide in grief. Many people who do touch death even only in the amounts I have commit suicide. Even less enjoy it. I will not say that death can not be enjoyed because it can. However most of us who have seen and touched it respect it and understand above all else that it is a thing of nature. One can not turn and look at it and be gleeful as a child receiving a toy, or terrified of it like a coward. For one who works with death we must accept it and let it flow through us but not touch who we are. For the even rarer few of who have experienced homicide one may take great pleasure but also lead a different life from that day…
