Damn public transport (Part 3)

26 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26-06-02 (Voice of Livewire) What did Marvin Harris give the Network title when he held it? Aha, now that’s a good question. Well, Marv was courageous, admirable and entertaining. We also successfully tagged together, so all in all Marvin Harris was a good Network champion and gave it some prestige when he held it. Ok, so what has Scott Cage given to the Network title since he’s held it? Errmm…well he defeated seven men in a double tier cage math….no that was Marvin Harris. Ummm…didn’t he have the accolade of being RUSH’s highest ranked wrestler in the WT polls?….no that was also Marvin Harris. So what has Scott Cage given to the Network title since winning it a fortnight ago..I’ll tell you what he’s given it….ABSOLUTELY NOTHING!!! Cage is the perennial over-achiever, he is somebody who was born to be a road sweeper and got lucky in wrestling. When the world looks back to the list of men that graced the RUSH roster many jaws will drop when they see Scott Cage’s name there, many will pass out due to shock when they find out that he actually had the audacity to become a champion. So it is my duty as the personification of cool and Britain’s finest export to restore some normality to RUSH and become the federations third Network champion. So that is clear, I am the next Network champion of RUSH wrestling, but all is not so clear. As I have mentioned before, black, gang warfare loving, fubu wearing, couversior drinking wrestlers don’t seem to mix too well with me. Ali Khadafi, the man of few words about from those beginning in F and ending in K. Ali, there is something just not right about you. You say you are not a stereotype, that you are not like every black person. Why try and be something you are not Ali? You are black therefore you must be generic and love fried chicken, listen to awful gangster rap music and smoke a blunt everywhere you go. That is what you are Ali a stereotype, a boring predictable stereotype. You are not even in the same league as me ability wise and I am already looking forward and counting the days until we meet in the ring so that I am able to give you the ass kicking that you so richly deserve. Livewire is seen sitting outside Edmonton High school on the schools steps. It is now late evening and it has been 2 hours since Livewire gave his speech to the pupils of Edmonton High school on achievement. There is a sprinkling of rain pouring down which causes Livewire to sneeze, his face filled with gloom. A few rain drops fall of his head as he turns to the camera to speak. “Ignorant high school geeks” Livewire says blows his nose with a tissue. “They think they know it all huh?” Livewire stands up and uses his hands to convey what he is speaking. “They doubt everything I f*cking say, they question if I have achieved anything in my career. How dare they mock my achievements” Livewire sits on the step again folds his arms as the rain continues to fall this time heavier than earlier on in the evening. “So one of the little snot-nosed punks asks me to show them a wrestling move…ha. I brought the kid onto the wooden stage grabbed him around the neck and dropped the Electricity on his skinny ass, let’s see what they consider achievement now!!!” Livewire laughs and slaps his thigh in a joyous motion as he remembers extracts of his speech. “I don’t think the headmaster liked it when I gave that kid the Electricity, from what I recall that was when he told me to leave and never come back to Canada again…oh well…all good things come to an end”. Livewire stands up and picks up his luggage and walks through the school gates. The rain is now lashing down and he hurriedly jogs down the street to get to the central Edmonton train station. After five minutes he arrives at the station only to see a train pass by. Livewire checks his watch and to his disbelief notices that he is a mere 2 minutes late for his train. “Darn!!!” shouts Livewire in annoyance before approaching a train guard who is chewing on a jam doughnut and is letting the majority of the jam drip all over his blue shirt. “Hey fatboy” Livewire calls out trying to get the attention of the train guard. “Who me..yah?” asks the train guard wiping the excess jam from his mouth. Livewire shakes the rain from out of his hair and nods his head “Yes, you..how many other obese Canadian men do you see on this train platform?” Livewire then glances across to the ticket office of the train station to see a flood of overweight train guards munching on a variation of deliquesces such as cream cakes and pork pies. “On second thoughts” Livewire adds “Don’t bother answering that question.” Livewire again looks impatiently at his watch and then the train guard “Listen, what time is the next train to Edmonton suburb?” The train guard polishes of his doughnut in one mouthful much to the disgust of Livewire and takes a clipboard from off the ticket desk. He reads from the clip board and looks up at Livewire with a startled look on his face. “You do know that you’ve just missed a train that was going straight to Edmonton suburb don’t ya..yah?” replies the train guard in a monotone voice. Livewire rolls back his eyes and replies in a sarcastic voice “Errr, well yeah..duh!” The train guard sighs at Livewire’s lack of manners and continues to talk “Well the next train to Edmonton suburb wont be for another eight hours I’m afraid sir”. Livewire’s jaw drops and he immediately shakes his head in disbelief. NO!!!NO!!! I have to stay in this moose infested country for another night…NO!!!” Livewire sinks to his knees and looks to the heavens as the train guard smirks and walks away “It seems you’re in for a long night boy, yah”. Livewire sits in a puddle as the rain pours down and the Edmonton sky turns a murky black. Livewire puts his head in his hands and turns to the camera for the final time “I really hate Canada” he says with frustration in his voice “and I really, really, really hate damn public transport!!!” Livewire bows his head into his hands again as the scene fades to black.
