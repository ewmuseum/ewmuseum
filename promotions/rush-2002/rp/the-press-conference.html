The Press Conference

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Poorly The scene in the conference room is packed. Reporters and fast food “dignitaries” fill the chairs and line up along the side. Flashbulbs ready for the arrival of their subjects. In the front of the room Are two tables and a podium. Hanging behind them is the banner with the now-familiar logo... BILL GATES’ BURRITO SHACK The logo is two young-looking kids with oversized heads. One has a luchadore mask on . The other has glasses and buck teeth. Both give a thumbs up to the giant cartoon burrito standing between them. A low buzz has started in the room, in eager anticipation of what is to come. As the crowd begins to grow restless, a side door to the room swings open and the source of all the commotion arrives. First comes one William Gates, CEO of Microsoft and proud owner of the new restaurant chain. He’s dressed in a smart gray suit, and smiles for all the cameras as he walks to his seat on one side of the podium and sits down. Behind him is the chief spokesman for the Burrito Shack, one L. Spiffy. Still clad in wrestling tights, boots, and mask, he hops cheerily behind the software mogul, sitting down on the opposite side and wasting no time in pouring himself a drink of water. Flashbulbs continue to pop as both men settle in for the address. As the hub-bub dies off, Gates gives El Spiffy! a look, then rises from his seat and takes the podium, readying a group of official documents handed to him by a secondary. He clears his throat, and begins. “I’d like to thank everyone for coming here,” he says. “This is an experimental venture for me, and I’m happy to have the support and the media coverage that I am getting. Now when I was sitting at home one night, having just hunted and killed another homeless man on the ranch in my Thursday tradition, I decided that maybe software wasn’t all that the world had for Bill Gates. After lots of thinking, I decided that the world of Mexican fast food has been held down by one company for too long. Taco Bell has a monopoly on shitty faux-Hispanic food. It needs to be stopped. And if anyone knows anything about monopolies, it’s me.” The reporters in the audience begin to chuckle amongst themselves. El Spiffy! shifts uncomfortably in his chair. Gates ignores them all and continues. “With that, I’d like to introduce the nationwide opening of Bill Gates’ Burrito Shack! Within the next few months, we’ll be opening about 200 locations across the United States, and will begin work on a global expansion following our first quarter of sales!” His excited announcement is met with light smatterings of applause from the crowd. Somewhat dejected by the lack of enthusiasm, Gates pulls his trump card. “And with that I give you our spokesman, El Spiffy!” he exclaims. The seated reporters rise to their feet. Video cameras flip on. Writers ready their pens. The flashbulbs warm up for a second round. All for a shot at...The Spifster. El Spiffy! hastily downs another glass of water and leaps up to the podium. He smiles sheepishly at everyone, and a flash goes off, dazing him. As he stumbles around a bit behind the podium, more flashes go off to catch the action, only making things worse. He bumps the wall and stumbles, grabbing hold of the Burrito Shack banner and ripping it down in an attempt to brace himself. Several large bodyguards in Microsoft polo shirts rush the stage and help El Spiffy! back to his feet. Still reeling from the flashes, he looks blindly out into the audience, leans over the podium and starts to speak. “El Spiffy! says eat Burrito Shack. Is...Spifftastic...” More cameras go off, and El Spiffy! slides off the podium and hits the floor, shaking and convulsing. A group of people jump to his aid, including Gates himself and the Spanish announcers, Juan Carlos Smith and Julio Diaz Perez O’Neil. “What’s he doing?” “He’s having a seizure!” Gates shouts. “No he’s not.” “What? Of course he is!” “He’s not having a seizure.” “What do you know? We’re obviously dealing with an epileptic man here, and we need an ambulance. CALL AN AMBULANCE!” Julio stops the security. “Don’t bother with the ambulance.” “Are you crazy?” Gates screams. “My spokesman is clearly in need of medical attention.” “He’s fine.” “He’s having a seizure.” “He’s not even epileptic, Bill.” “Well than what do you call this?” “Showing off.” Before Gates can manage another maniacal response, Juan Carlos stands up clears a path, and lays a boot into El Spiffy!’s side. The shaking stops instantly, but security instantly grab Juan Carlos from behind and prepare to lead him away. In a rage, Gates hops to his feet to look at him. “You sick human being, you could’ve killed him!” “He’s fine. Look at him.” Back on the ground, El Spiffy! has slowly gone back to normal, and is now wiping up the stream of drool forming at the corner of his mouth. Without warning, he kips up to his feet, all smiles. The guards unknowingly loosen their grip on Juan Carlos, allowing him to break free. El Spiffy! looks at the announcers, then at Gates, then the destruction he caused, and finally out into the crowd. “El Spiffy! could go for a burrito.” With a collective groan, the room empties in record time, with everyone cursing the hour of their lives that had been wasted. Before long, it’s just El Spiffy! and Gates in the room. The mogul surveys the damage, and El Spiffy! just looks dumbfounded. “What?”
