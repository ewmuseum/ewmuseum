A meeting...

3 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/3/02 The weeks ago in a small town somewhere in Eastern Europe.... 2 a.m. (The scene opens in the darkened streets of a moderatly sized Eastern European town.Although clearly of at least moderate size it still maintains many classical style buildings and cobblestone streets.Every few yards a street light is placed illuminating a small cone shaped area and shedding just a bit of light on the areas in between.The streets are completely empty save for a lone figure moving quickly down middle of the cobblestone street.Something is clutched in his right hand and he holds onto it as if his life depended on it.As he passes one of the many streetlamps on either side of the road he stops to look at the object he's holding.As he moves into the light it becomes apparent that the induvidual in question is Wesley Outlett.In his hand is a piece of paper he glances down at it.It reads,'806 Sceinhosten 3a.m.Come alone.Don't be late.'He looks back up and hurries on.) 806 Sceinhosten about an hour later.... (Wes nows stands outside a small office building in a more modern looking part of town.He stares at the glass door and the inky darkness behind it.He finally tears his gaze away and then with one final glance at the paper turns around to leave,but when he turns he comes face to face with a man wearing a suit and a bowler hat.The man is about Wes's size and hidden from closer examination by the shadows which he seems to be making a point to stay in.) Man:Mister Ryan? Wes:Uh...yeah that would be me. Man:Goood.I was begining to think that you weren't going to show.I think you'll find you made the right choice by coming here. Wes:Umm,thanks,but what exactly is it you want? Man:Ah,a little jumpy are we my American friend?It's to be expected.In fact I would be jumpy too if I were in your...position.I assure you though I mean you no harm,I in fact can get you out of the...predicament your in if your willing to help me. Wes:By predicament you mean.. Man(Cutting Wes off):Yes,all of it. Wes:Well then lets talk what do you need? Man(Chuckles briefly):Let us not talk out here,someone could be listening.Let us go inside. Wes:I don't see anyone.. Man(Again cutting Wes off):The first thing you'll need to learn is someone is ALWAYS listening,and because of that ANY informating pretaining to our deal will only be disscussed in the most private of conditions. Wes:Alright then lets go. Man(Glancing down):Is that the note you recieved from us? Wes:Yeah. Man:Give it to me. (Wes hands the man the crumpled piece of paper he's holding.The man snatches it up quickly and just as quickly pulls out a lighter and torches it.He leads Wes inside as the scene fades out.) A few minutes later.... (Wes and the man sit on opposent ends of a long plain wooden table.A lone light hangs from the ceiling and provides little more illumination than the streetlights outside.The man is still wearing his bowler hat,keeping his face covered with shadow.In his hands he holds a simple looking file stuffed with various papers.Wes sits acrossed the table from him,having lost his intital shock he now looks intently and clamly towards the shadowy figure acrossed the table from him.) Man:Mr.Ryan I know why you are here in this pathetic little town. Wes:Oh,do you really?Why is that? Man:There are many papers in this file Mr.Ryan.Everyone of them telling me a little bit more about you,all together painting me a clear picture of the young man who is James Wesley Ryan.I know what foods you eat,the name of every woman you've ever slept with,your favorite TV shows,but none of that's important.What is important it this. (The man pulls a small stack of papers from the folder and slides them acrossed the table to Wes.) Man:As you read you'll find out what we both already know,your wanted in several countires for a rang of several small scale terrorist acts and you are linked with several left wing extremist groups.Yes,much of it is simple vadalism and arson and none of it carries more than a couple years in prison,but together the numbers are staggering.Mr.Ryan I'm sure you already know this but when the authorities find you,you will be facing over 200 years in jail time.I can tell you wit most certainty Mr.Ryan they will find you,but I can also tell you there is a way out if your willing to take it.Now Mr.Ryan are you willing to take it?If not you may leave now and forget this ever happened,if so you will be bound to our deal,and breaking it will result in GRAVE consequinces.Now Mr.Ryan are you in? Wes:One-hundered percent,but what is it you need? Man:I represent a certain very powerful individual in a government we will leave unnamed and he has the means to make all the trouble your in just go away,you can come out of hiding and lead a normal life,all he needs is a little favor. Wes:What kind of favor? Man:You used to be a wrestler in PCW aam I correct Mr.Ryan? Wes:Yes,yes you are what does that have to do with.. Man(Cutting Wes off):You see Mr.Ryan we picked you for two reasons.One your liberal political leanings will no doubt allow you to get whole heartedly behind you mission.The second is that you are the only individual with the type of experiance we need. Wes:You mean wrestling experiance? Man:Exactly.You a certain problem has arisin with a certain individual within the PCW affilated RUSH.We figured because of your wrestling experiance and former affiliation with PCW that you could easily infiltrait RUSH and...dispose of this individual. Wes:You want me to kill someone!?! Man:No,not kill.Simply take out of commision,that's all.I;ve already seen to it that all your arrest warrents are suspended if you are successful in your mission they will be erased perminatly and you can go back to a normal life. Wes:So who exactly is this individual?.....
