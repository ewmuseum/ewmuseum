On The Edge [2]

Date: July 10th, 2002 A.D. Journal Entry: July 8, 2002 I knocked her out again, I still can’t believe it. Sleeper hold to a woman that I’m seven inches taller then, and not to mention over one hundred pounds heavier. I can’t believe I did that, I remember when I was younger, my father told me... “Son” he said to me, “there’s a few things in life a man should never do. And you know what? One of them is hit a woman.” What a load of crap that was. You see, when I ran away from home it was because of my father, after losing his job and while on a drunken binge, he bashed up my mother. Yeah, punched her in the jaw, kicked her several times in the ribs. I always promised myself I’d never hurt a woman, unless it was in a Karate fight – where I have no choice. I put a Sleeper Hold on that son of a mother fucking bitch, Deamont’s, girlfriend. I hate Deamont, not this hate you people see on Adrenaline every week, this my friends is real hate. Real hate? What I mean by this is hate like France has with Britain, we hate each other. I’ve seen what he’s done to people, hell, I helped do a lot of it. Cage burning? I handcuffed them to the cage. Me, not Brent, not Walden, and not Cassius. Me, all me. Yes, me. Who’d have thought it? Cassius is this and that, but everyone forgot about me, didn’t they? Yeah, I’m just me. Christian Matthews, from suburban Montreal. I’m not some big, fast life living former mercenary like Deamont is... I just don’t have ‘that’, which he has. But then, maybe I do have this aggression, which he seems to have flowing through his veins. In the papers I’ve read that I could be raping his poor woman, heh.. how wrong they are. I haven’t raped, the only thing I’ve done to her is knock her out twice...poor misguided tabloids making up stories, it all probably infuriates Deamont...excellent. All this enclosure, all this seclusion is taking is taking it’s toll on me, and her as well. Sleepless nights since it happened, she seems to wake up at four o’clock, start banging on my door, trying to keep me awake, yelling... “HE’S GONNA GET YOU!!” .....she’s right, he is gonna get me...but I’m gonna get him back as well. Mark my words, Deamont will lose that title, it’s mine...all fucking mine. * * * * A Dream... I walked along the rigid edges of a cliff, if I fell, it’d be to a gruesome death. If a fell, it’d be falling hundreds, no thousands, of feet down to a large mass of rocks below. As my feet trudged along the bumpy rocks of this unknown cliff face, a chilling wind creped up over my body. I looked down at my foot, my leather clad boots cracking on the rocks just underneath my shoes. Why was I up here? This unknown cliff face, well, as I looked down, staring at what some would call hell; I started to realize something. I could see water below, faintly, but it’s there. In this water below I see nothing other then the arid blue surface of the rock hard, on impact, water surface. I looked down at it, I couldn’t really see anything other then a big vat of greenish, bluish surface. I sighed, in this dream, that is. I blinked a few more times as my eyes gazed over the vat of water, I saw a ripple form in the water...maybe a rock fell from the cliff face, or something. The ripple soon dissolves into something a little weird, a shape seemed to be forming in the rippling water. I frowned in annoyance, what exactly was this forming shape? I peered down a little more, trying to see what this ‘thing’ really was. The ripples in the water began to puddle about a little more, foaming toward the edge of the small pond banks. Circulating, the water makes more ripples as I frown, from those several hundred feet above. “Odd” I said, in my dream. “Ripples forming in a pond, yet no rocks or nothing falling into it...” A shape started to form in the ripples, I frowned. Confused I was, I don’t know exactly what this shape was. As the ripples started to take more of a shape, I frowned deeper, eager and nervous to find out what this shape was... ....and then I did. A gasp leaped out of my mouth as I saw his smirking face in the water. He mouthed at me “die, die, die, die, DIE!!” I shook my head and frowned, “No...” I said, still in my dream. “You can’t be here, you can’t be here...” I went to turn around, a rock dropping off the cliff face as my knees brushed against them. What did I see when I turned around? Her. Her being Kathryn, his girlfriend. At the top of her lungs she screeched out, a piercing sound that ripped through the drums of my ears. “DIE!!” she screeched at me, I clasped my ears trying to block out her wails. “DIE!! CASSIUS IS GONNA KILL YOU, BITCH!!” /I>I clasped my ears tighter and tighter, she’s not gonna do this to me, damnit, she’s not gonna do this to me. “NO!!” I yelled back at her, but it was all in vein. Her large spiked heel shoes drove their way into my chest, with a forward thrust, her shoes launched me off the mountain face and sprawling toward the ominous and painful surface below... ....him. “AHHHHHHH!!!!!!” is what I yelled and shrieked as I plummeted, her laughing a wicked laugh at her handy work and him? A bellowing laugh of “DIE! DIE! DIE! DIE! DIE! DIE!” And now what you wonder? Of course, I wake up, only seems fitting...waking is what most humans do when they have a nightmare, and now it’s my time to do so.... * * * * Suddenly, springing up from his bed, is Chris Matthews, a face covered in sweat. That sweat trickles down from the frayed brow of his forehead as he breathes heavily. Shaking his head, his eyes look out to over the city; a few cars can be heard in the distance, that city is New York City. “What a weird dream” he begins to say as a Pigeon flies past his window, the early morning sun making a picturesque purple backdrop. “Wait, that wasn’t a dream.... more like a nightmare.” He sighs as the city of New York bellows with life, rolling over in his bed, dusty eyes and all, Matthews looks at the red strobe digits on the digital clock. “6:28” he reads out loud. A banging sound from the kitchen disturbs him, rubbing the crap that sits in your eyes while your sleeping out of his, Matthews, clad in his blue boxer shorts and loose, yet ripped, t-shirt he walked into the kitchen he thought was vacant. Rubbing his eyes, Matthews pushed the door open. At first, he didn’t notice anything. But when most of the crap is removed, brushed out of his eyes, Matthews sees the woman he’s held hostage for the last few days brandishing a large butchers knife. “What the hell are you doing?!” he yelled at her, cautious of the tired, exhausted, girlfriend of his mortal enemy. “Kathryn, put the damn knife down!” She smirked at him, holding the knife high. “Why? You scared of me? Little innocent me?” He shook his head back at the knife-wielding damsel, now dangerous as shit. “Well” he began to say, staring straight at the knife. Before he answered a second time, he lunged forward at Kathryn, eager to get the knife out of her dangerous hands. “LET GO OF ME!” she yelled, fighting with all her might to break his grip. No avail though as they struggled, struggled for several minutes until he pried the sharp and dangerous metallic knife out of her hands, throwing it over his shoulders. Using his strength and obvious size advantage over the 5’7 Kathryn, Matthews locks on that sleeper hold, once more. He held it tighter and tighter until her consciousness started to fade... ....her eyes rolling into the back of her head... ....her breathing softening.... ....her eyelids closing... .....blackness...
