El Spiffy! Takes Manhattan!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: slowly The scene opens up on a New York City night club. It’s late. Nighttime, even. Groups of rather, let’s say, ETHNIC young people gather on the sidewalks, the party in the club spilling out into the streets. Our camera takes a quick pan across the bouncing cars, neon lights, and sparkling jewelry before cutting inside. It’s even more raucous inside the club. With the Big Tymers’ “Still Fly” blasting over the speakers, and black lights distorting the colors across the room. The floor is packed with people, all gyrating with perverse rubbing and jumping that they consider “dancing.” Others in the balcony look on, drinks in hand, just taking in the atmosphere. Amongst these onlookers, two men stick out. Like the sorest of thumbs, the two men try their best to fit in, but only draw more attention to themselves. The camera catches them from across the club, sitting alone because the rest of the crowd has shied away. Slowly, it zooms in, and we get our first glimpse at the two. El Spiffy! and Bill Gates. It’s hard to recognize at first, given their attire. El Spiffy! sports a pair of black jeans and a matching black denim jacket. Underneath he wears a “Bill Gates’ Burrito Shack” T-shirt. A black Bandana is tied on over top of his mask. Gates is clad in a pair of red leather pants and a wife beater. His pale white skin glows against the black lights. On his head, a red “do rag” covers most of his hair and face, making him less recognizable. But on closer examination, it’s definitely the Microsoft CEO/fast food entrepreneur. He squirms uneasily in his seat. There they sit, El Spiffy! bouncing to the music, Gates’ glancing around nervously. No Spanish announcers, and no Shack Pack, unfortunately, to bail them out. It’s just the two of them. The music continues while Gates finally turns to confront El Spiffy! “What are we doing here, Spiff?” “El Spiffy! is doing the, the, the....research.” comes the reply. “Research? For what?” “The Spiffy! and the Shack must team up against the Player Corporation.” “You mean Playaz, Inc.?” “Yes, yes. The Players Ink.” El Spiffy! turns his attention back to the club, trying his best to pick up some moves from watching the others. Gates scratches his do rag, still confused as to why they’re around. “But El Spiffy!,” he moans, “why the hell are we here?” Without a word, El Spiffy! backhands Bill Gates, sending a resounding *SMACK* across the club. A couple of nearby patrons wince at the sound. “How can the Spiffy! understand the Players Ink atmosphere,” he reasons, “when he has whiny software-inventin’ crackas ALL UP IN HIS GRILL!” Gates pauses for a bit, rubbing his sore face. “Does grill mean face or mouth?” “Shut up.” There is another pause as the song changes over. This time, it’s some garbage by Nelly. ”It’s getting hot in here, so take off all your clothes.” El Spiffy! leaps out of his chair, pulling off his jacket in the process. “El Spiffy!’s getting so hot,” he sings along. “El Spiffy!’s gonna take his clothes off.” Bill Gates opens his man to talk, but before he can make a sound, El Spiffy! turns and tosses him come cue-card notecards in the middle of dancing. As Gates looks up quizzically, El Spiffy! gestures for him to read them. “You get to be the Ali,” he explains. Gates looks down at the first card, which has “ALI KHADAFI PRIMER COURSE” written on it. He flips to the next card and begins to read. “Fuck fuck motherfuck... fuck.” he reads. “Bitch slut fuck whore fuck asshole fuck fuckity fuck.” “Yes!” El Spiffy! shouts as he dances, swinging his hands in the cabbage patch while his oversized jeans slide down his ass, “Now you’re getting the it!” As the word “Spiffy!” on his tights becomes more and more evident with the falling jeans, El Spiffy! gyrates his way up to a group of women. With everyone in the club singing like a lemming along with the music, he quickly disappears into the crowd, his jeans flying off in the process. Back by himself, Gates continues to focus on the notecards. “Shit fuck fuck damn fuck fuck fuckeroo.” With that, two rather large African American gentlemen place their hands on Gates’ shoulders. He squeaks in fear and looks up timidly. The more burly of the two men gives him a smile, flashing some gold teeth. “It sounds like yo cracka ass need to be taught a lesson ‘bouts respeck.” Gates swallows hard, and the two men just laugh as the scene fades to black.
