Misunderstandings (debut)

8 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8th July 2002 (The scene opens with the sight of a one Joe Bananas sitting crossed legged in the centre of a beige carpeted floor. The hotel room around him is neat and generally tidy, the walls plain white colours and pretty much everything else following the same, plain pattern. Around the ex-NTICW star are masses of fruit. There are melons, mangos, bananas, oranges, apples and many others, all lying around in a large heap unorganised. Some have been eaten already, the empty skins empty and lifeless with staining juice dripping slowly onto the carpet below, multicoloured splodges of sticky patterns cover this once clean room. Joe lifts one of the mangos up close to his face, pressing the orange skin softly against his right cheek.) Joe: Mangos… ya always been der fa me an Ah never gon let dat change, ya know! (Joe uses his clutching fingers to bounce the mango up and down slightly in a nodding motion. Joe thrust his index fingernail into the orange fruit and begins to pick at the skin. His nail scratching at the peel hard until the soft, juicy flesh comes into view. He lifts the fruit above his head and opens his lips to allow the thick juice to fall into his mouth as it ebbs from the mango. As the trickle begins to slow and stop Joe brings the fruit back down in front of him and begins to pick away the rest of the skin, revealing piece by piece more and more of the inside goodness. The surrounding fruit, all collected together in a large huddle, do not seem to be important at this point to Joe, he is purely focused on one thing.) Knock, Knock, Knock (Joe looks up just as a large chunk of the mango slides down the back of his throat. He tries to ask who it is, knocking at his door but the fruit stops him and he is forced to remain silent.) Voice: Room Service! Joe (mouth still filled with fruit): Room Service? Voice: I got an order for a bucket of caviar and toast points, to be brought up to this room. Joe: Caviar? Wha ya talk wit? (Joe looks down at a bunch of green grapes, glistening in hazy glow of the bright light beaming down from above. Joe’s cheerful grin swiftly transforms into an angry scowl as he wrenches the grapes up in front of him.) Joe: Did ya bwoys orda caviar again? (Joe waits for a response from the grapes, but receives none. He lifts them up to his left ear, just in case they are talking in hushed voices.) Voice: Excuse me sir? Joe: Ah wan talkin ta ya, pickney! Ah wa talkin ta mah grapes. Voice: Sir? Joe: Ya heard meh! (The handle on the door begins to turn as the owner of the voice begins to attempt his entry into Joe’s room. Joe drops the grapes to the floor and springs up onto his feet and dives for the door ahead. He pushes his body weight against the opening entrance and forces it closed again. The man on the other side, obviously shocked, jumps back a little.) Joe: Ah ne’er said ya could come in ere, did Ah? Voice: Sir, I need to give you your order. Joe: Wha if Ah don wan it no more, wha if Ah don like caviar no more? Ah got da right ta change my opinions ya know! Voice: Yes sir. Well, we would take it back. But we would prefer it if you would take your order, we want you to enjoy your stay as much as possible. Joe: Ya mean ya got all dis caviar together an ya wan someone ta pay for it, dats da truth aint it? Well ah tell ya I din orda nah caviar. Dis some sheg up business if ya sendin ordas ta folk who don wan it! (Joe steps back and snaps the door open enough to fit his head through. His dreadlocks are caught in the space between and disallow Joe from moving any further. He gives a stern and cold stare at the man before him, standing nervously in a dark red uniform. He then looks down to the floor of the hallway, the maroon carpet supporting a metallic bucket filled with black fish eggs and a white plate, holding triangular points of toast, beside it. Joe only looks at the bucket for a snap second before continuing his stare out with the uniformed man. With breaking his gaze, Joe snatches the bucket from the ground and steps back into his room, closing the door. He drops the bucket next to his fruit, spilling a small amount of the fish eggs onto the already badly stained carpet below. He then stumbles back over to the door and opens it, creakily, at full length. He steps through the open doorway, the uniformed man shocked to see him wearing nothing but a string vest and pair of white boxer shorts with red hearts printed onto them. Joe looks at the man again as if to say ‘what you lookin’ at?’ and seizes the plate of toast.) Joe: Ya can ave some if ya wan, ya know! Ah don mind sharin wit ya. Man: No, it’s ok sir. I have to get back to work. Please enjoy you caviar. Joe: It aint mah caviar, I ne’er wanted none of it! I jus tekkin it so ya don look like da bruck up kid ya are! Man: Please sir, stop shouting. You’re disturbing the other customers. (A few doors can be seen opening, various people sticking their heads out to try and catch a glimpse as to what the commotion is all about. A few old men were wiping their glasses and holding their wives back as the angry Jamaican continues his discussion about his room service.) Joe: Who gi a damn bout dem? I gon cause as much ruckus round here as Ah can, and make sure ya bruck up rass get fired. Ah sure they dey don wan kids like ya bruckin dey image, it such a nice otel as well. Man (lowering his voice): Please sir, if you would just stop this nonsense I’m sure the hotel will be happy to cut the charges and take the caviar back. Joe: Ya can take it back, Ah already started eatin it. Man: You have? Joe: Yeah, it don taste like chicken like Ah thought it would. Man: Well sir, we would be happy to cut the charges on the caviar due to this misunderstanding. Joe: I wan it free! Man: Free? Joe (shouting so the other customers can hear): Dis hotel is sheg up an teif, tekkin away mah money straight from unda mah… Man (interrupting): Please sir, lower your voice. I’m, sure we can arrange something. Joe (smiling): Ah respec ya decision an accept da offer. (Joe raises his hand in a salute position as he chews on one of the toast points. He awaits the man’s respectful return of this honour filled gesture. The man instead sighs and hangs his head as he walks along the corridor towards the elevators, Joe remains with the edge of his hand spread on his forehead. His eyes widening and his mouth wide open, the half chewed toast spilling from his pink tongue.) Joe: Wha? Nah salute? Foo foo pickney! Ah gi ya mah oda salute fa dat! (Joe proceeds to raise the middle finger on his left hand and raise it in the air towards the closing doors of the elevator. He then steps back into his room and slams the door shut with a loud slam. He then turns back round, the plate in hand and a grin spreading back across his voice. He rests the toast next to the bucket and lifts the bunch of small, green grapes up to his face.) Joe: Well, ya got ya caviar like ya wan. Enjoy bwoys! (Joe dangles the grapes over the black mass for a few seconds before dropping them down. The grapes slowly sinking into the eggs Joe turns his attention back to his juicy mango, eager to get stuck into it yet again. He lifts the fruit back towards his round lips, ready to take the second bite.) Ring… ring… ring (Joe sighs heavily and shrugs his shoulders as if to say ‘what now?’ to his group of fruit friends. Joe clumsily pulls himself up and picks up the smooth receiver from the phone resting on his bedside table. He pushes it towards his ear and listens to the calm and gentle voice that is projected through the telephone line.) Voice: Hello, Mr Bananas. We have been receiving complaints from several other residents that you have been making loud noises. The man who brought your room service also confirmed that you were causing a commotion. Can I ask you if… Joe (interrupting): Hey, da pickney wa givin me some of his cheekiness. I don stand fa dat. Voice: I assure you that all our employees are as polite as possible at all times. Joe: Well ya shunt beh usin lil kids fa jobs ya know! Voice: Excuse me? Joe: Dere are laws against pickney labour ya know! Voice: P-Pickney labour? Joe: Yeah, usin kids. Voice: Oh, you mean child labour. Joe: Hush up ya noise! Ah aint a satisfied customer, ya sendin me tings Ah don even want! Voice: If we have made a mistake we will gladly take the item away and will not charge you for it. Joe: Well, da grapes are enjoyin it too much. Ah can’t take it from dem now, it be cruel. Voice: Grapes? Joe: Well, Ah tink one of da melons might wan some too. Ah doubt any of em could fit in, not proper anyways. Voice: Sir? Are you ok sir? Joe: Course Ah ok foo! Ya soundin a bit too cheeky like ya other pickney! Voice: Sir, if you have been drinking then we can have this conversation at a later date. Joe: Wa da bumbaclot!? Ah never been so insulted! Voice: Please sir, just… Joe: Firs ya get ya lil kids ta bring mah caviar, which Ah dint even order, an now ya sayin Ah drunk! Voice: Please sir, if you would just calm down. We will not charge for this mistake made on our part and let me offer my apologies. Joe: Ya damn well should be sorry! Voice: Is there any way we can make it up to you, as an apology for the mix up. Joe: Well… Voice: Yes? (Joe looks back over his shoulder, grinning to the mass of fruit still laying on the floor or sinking into the caviar bucket.) Joe: Well, da bwoys here are a lil lonely. Ah aint exactly great entertainment. So could ya provide some female ‘company’ fa dem? If ya get wha Ah mean. Voice: Oh, I understand perfectly sir. They’ll be sent up as soon as they get here. Joe: Get here? (The line goes dead; Joe shakes the recent confusion from the last statement out of his head and replaces the receiver back onto the phone. He spins back on his heels so he faces the fruit again. His grin grows wider as he thinks about the company for his fruit that is being sent up, lots more fruit companions for them.) Joe: Good news bwoys, dey sendin up more fruit an Ah got mah caviar fa free! Ah love dis place. *20 minutes later* (Joe looks down at his left wrist; a cheap watch glaring up at him shows the time almost clearly. Joe sighs loudly as he realises it’s been twenty minutes since they promised him the fruit, he didn’t understand why it was taking so long to get here. Even if the elevator had broken down and they were forced to go up the stairs, it would have gotten here by now. How far away was the kitchen?) Knock, knock, knock (Joe grins, it must be here, at last. He springs up quickly to his feet, eagerly making his way to the door. He rubs his hands together and places his hand on the handle and turns it slowly, the door creaking open. On the other side stands a dark skinned woman wearing very revealing clothing, her lips moving up and down as chews at a pink chunk of gum. Her over usage of makeup startles Joe and he steps back.) Joe: Ya nah fruit! Woman: Hey, you’re Jamaican. I just love the accent. (Joe scratches his head as the woman steps through the doorway into his room, she reaches back with her right arm and shuts the door, smiling. Her long, straight black hair glistens in the overhead light. Her highly revealing black outfit leaves little to Joe’s imagination. She steps closer to Joe, noticing the fruit.) Woman: Fruit! Kinky. Joe: Wha? (Joe steps back away from the woman, his mouth wide open and his eyes full of shock. He has no clue what is going on; obviously she is not someone who works for the hotel.) Joe: Who da rassclot are ya? Woman: Well, my names Latoya… you? Joe: I’m Joe, but wha da bumbaclot does dat matter? Why ya here, in mah room? Latoya: Huh? (Joe slumps down onto his bed, covering his eyes up with his hands and fingers. He breathes in deeply, rubbing the area around his nose.) Latoya: hey sugar, you payin’ you can do whatever the hell you like. I’ll just go and sit over there. (Latoya wiggles herself towards a seat on the other side of the room, her buttocks moving in almost perfect unison with each other like two pendulums. She notices the caviar and takes a piece of toast, dipping it deeply into the bucket of fish eggs. As she pulls it back out again she lifts it towards her mouth, but stops, picking off a caviar covered grape.) Joe: Dere mus a been a mistake or suttin, Ah dint order a woman… how could ya order a woman anyway? Latoya: Huh? Joe: How could ya order a lady ta come up ta ya room? Latoya: Lady? Sugar, aint nobody callin’ me a lady these days. (Joe lays on his side and reaches down to the mass of fruit and lifts up his mango that he had started earlier. He sinks his teeth deep into it and begins to eat away greedily at the fruit. The juice falling down onto his string vest and dribbling down his arms. As he swallows the last pieces he throws the remains to the floor below and wipes at his chin with the back of his hands.) Latoya: Well, let’s get this started sugar. (Latoya gets out of her chair and moves closer towards Joe. Joe looks up, confused and bewildered and shrieks as he is pushed back down onto his bed. He covers his eyes with his hands as the cleavage of Miss Latoya becomes more and more visible, crawling over on the bed so that she is above him. Joe slides sideways off the bed onto the ground and dashes towards the door, slamming it behind as he makes his way to the elevator. He leaves a confused Latoya laying on his bed, looking around at all the expensive things around her.) (Joe stands inside the elevator with the annoying music barely audible to him in this state. The cold gushing into his bloodstream, still wearing nothing more than underwear and a vest. He bobs up and down as he waits for the gentle bell noise of the doors opening and he can sort this mess out. As the elevator comes to wobbly stop, and the doors open, Joe storms angrily towards the front desk where the man is talking with customers. The family of four’s mouths open wide in shock and they turn around to leave the hotel, hoping to find one where crazy Jamaicans don’t run around in their underwear.) Man: What the hell? Joe: Wha da bumbaclot ya sen up ta ma room? Man: Sir, please keep the noise down… you’re disturbing the other residents. Joe: Hush up! I asked fa fruit ta be sen up ta ma room an ya sen a woman? Man (quietly): Sir, you asked for some ‘female company’ remember? I just gave my cousin Gary a call and he got you sorted out; keep it hush though sir we don’t want to tarnish the look of this hotel. Joe: Ah don care bout ya chupid hotel! I wan know why ya sen up wha ya sen, Ah wan mah fruit! Man: Sir, if you keep your voice down I’m sure I can sort you something out. Joe: Ya better had! Ah wan free fruit from ya, an get dat woman out mah room! Man: She has already left, look. (Joe turns around to see Latoya scurrying away wearing a long coat, the pockets seemingly bulging. She quickly leaves through the main doors and away into the night. Joe turns back to the man on the other side of the counter.) Joe: So, where mah fruit? Man: Sir, just go to your room and I’ll have as much fruit sent up to you as quickly as I can. Joe: Ah give ya 5 minutes! An dats all! Man: Ok, ok. Just don’t let word out, this hotel as a reputation you know! (Joe smiles and begins his journey back to his room. He is still yet unaware that everybody he passes is staring at him, still walking in nothing more than underwear. He gets back to his room and opens up the door once more. He slumps down onto his bed and grins widely as he awaits his delivery to arrive.) *Later* (Juice dripping from his chin at a tremendous rate, Joe gorges himself stupid full of fruit. Skins, peels and the remains of eaten fruit almost fill the entire room. Juice stains practically covering the wall, Joe eats well into the night. He lifts the first grapefruit and tries to peel the skin away, his hand aching and his muscles tire he realises he must use a spoon. He stands up out of the mess and walks towards the cabinet nearby. He pulls the top draw out full length, only to find it empty. Joe’s face screws up with confusion. He closes the draw and opens the one below, also finding it empty. All the other draws, after careful inspection, turn out to be the same. Joe shrugs his shoulders and decides to go to plan B. He places the grapefruit on the side of the cabinet and punches the top of it with his bare knuckles as hard as he can. The fruit squashes down and juice squirts out in all directions, some flying into the unprepared eye of Joe. Joe holds his stinging eye in pain and runs to the bathroom. After pushing the door open he finds that the bathroom is empty too, all the small bottles and accessories… and all the towels and flannels. Joe splashes some water onto his eye, but the pain continues.) Joe: Wha’s goin on here? (Joe continues to search the rest of his room, finding that the alcohol cabinet is also empty. All the alcohol gone, along with all the macadamia nuts, towels, shower-caps and the shampoo. Where could it have all gone?) Joe: Where all mah stuff gone? It all gone! (Joe continues to look only to find more things, which should be there, are gone. He slumps down onto the floor and supports his fuzzy chin with the palms of his hands.) Joe: Who took all mah stuff? (The scene fades out to black, leaving a bewildered Joey Fruit behind who is scratching his head obviously not adding two and two together and figuring out who the culprit of this robbery is.)
