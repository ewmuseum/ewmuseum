Representing the worlds evils, one day at a time.

Date: not entered (The scene fades in to show the outside of Brock Newbludd’s fully functional babe lair on a crisp March morning. The birds can be heard chirping in the background and everything seems to be cheerful out today. Even the homeless bum named Ned who lives next to the apartment building is in a more than merrier mood. The reason for this is when Brock returned to Milwaukee from the latest PCW card late last night he left the old bum a present of sorts. While the bum was sleeping in his box during the night Brock left him a few bottles of warm beer that they had left over from the trip home. So, when Ned woke up this morning he was greeted to two bottles of piss warm Old Milwaukee sitting by him. Now, we see Ned sitting on a garbage can next to his cardboard box sipping one of the beers with a smile on his face. The camera pans out to show the whole scene, we see that the city streets are very busy on this warm morning. People also clutter the sidewalks, most are just taking a morning walk, enjoying the beautiful morning. We see an old woman dressed in a fur coat and a large billow cap walking down the sidewalk following her poodle that’s on a leash. The poodle stops in the middle of the sidewalk, right in front of the large apartment building. The dog circles in one place, sniffing the air, the dog then looks at the old lady…) Old lady: Right here? Ok… (The old woman pulls the latest issue of PCW magazine out of her coat pocket, she flips through it, before ripping out a page and nodding her head in approval…) Old lady: This should work just fine! (With that the old woman pulls the torn out page from the magazine and sets it down on the cement, right directly behind the small dog. Upon further inspection we see that is a full color picture of “Amazing” Mike Storm posing in a sharp business suit. The dog steps back a bit and squats, centering itself over the picture. Then, in a instant it drops a load of feces all over the picture, most of it lands on Mike’s face. The dog gets done and kicks some snow that was on the sidewalk on top the literally crappy picture.) Old Woman: That a boy Skippy! That’s all that Mike Storm whippersnapper deserves to have after ruining the PCW like he has. A big pile of dog sh*t on there heads! Now, lets go Skippy, its getting cold out! (The old woman and her dog trot off casually down the street as the camera oans out. It then zooms in onto a window on the second story window of the complex. The camera keeps on zooming in until we are looking directly in the window. We see that is Brock Newbludd’s apartment aka “babe lair”. The camera cuts out quickly and cuts back in so that we are now inside of the apartment. The camera pans around to set the surroundings. We see that the living room is messier than usual, empty beer cans and bottles lay strewn across the floor along with the cardboard cases they came in. Other miscellaneous items like potato chip bags, cases for Playstation games and the occasional kernel of popcorn strewn across the floor. We see that the large 47” TV is still on, and showing on the screen is a blank pause screen from the Playstation game “PCW Alliances”. In the background we can hear “Stupified” by Disturbed playing through the speaker system. But, above all else we can hear an audible snoring. The camera pans around to see that the snoring comes from Brock Newbludd, who is passed out on the couch wearing only a pair of boxers and a wife beater. A burnt out cigarette hangs from his top lip and a Playstation controller hangs from his hand. A half empty bottle of Heineken lays on its side, on his chest. The liquid in side of it sways back and forth in motion as Brock inhales and exhales. All of a sudden one of the bedroom doors swings open and Stephen “The New F*ckin’ Legend” Blood, clad in a pair of flannel pajama bottoms and no shirt. Blood stands there for a moment and rubs the sleep out of his eyes before shuffling his way over into the kitchen and opens the refrigerator and grabbing two beers out of it. He slams the door and walks into the living room, Blood sees Brock passed out on the leather and shakes his head. The New Legend walks across the floor trying to avoid stepping any of the beer cans on it and plops down in the recliner that sits in the corner of the room. Stephen cracks his beer and tosses the other one on to Brock’s chest causing the beer that was already on his chest to be knocked off and spill on the floor. Brock opens his blood shot eyes and rubs them a bit, before sitting up. He rubs his temples and groans…) Brock: Ugh…what time is it? Stephen: Two o’clock in the afternoon. (Brock leans back and cracks open his beer, he takes a swig and shakes the cobwebs out of his head…) Brock: What happened last night? Stephen: Well, you were playing a season on Alliances on PS2™ and you got pissed because you kept losing to Miyagawa via run-ins every time you got a shot at the TV title. You got so pissed you slammed the Playstation controller on the ground and drank a case of beer, I went to bed at 1:00 in the morning and you were still playing, so who know what time you finally passed out. (Stephen lets out a little chuckle…) Brock: What? Stephen: It was kinda funny, you’d work your way up the rankings and then when you’d get a shot against him you’d kick his ass the whole way, then Mike Storm or some other goon would do the run-in and he would retain. Brock: Well, at least the game is true to life. Stephen: Hey man, I have a feeling, Sunday will be your night to right fully take your place among the TV title division. Brock: Well, everybody’s predicting him to beat me, man, maybe I’m starting to lose a step. Stephen: Quit sounding like a little b*tch man! You haven’t lost a step! Hell, who cares what people are predicting. You remember the first time we lost the tag straps? (Brock rubs his eyes and nods his head…) Brock: Yeah, to Sex and Violence? Stephen: Yep, remember how know one thought that we would ever win them back? (Brock lights up a cigarette and takes a long soothing drag of it…) Brock: Yeah, so? Stephen: Well, what happened? Brock: We won it back? (Stephen cracks his knuckles and grins…) Stephen: Dude, there’s something that you’ve got to realize, whether you like it or not. (Brock sits up on the couch and stretches out lazily, but we can see a serious look in his eyes…) Brock: What? Stephen: Brock, no matter how much you doesn’t like it, you’ll always be an underdog in PCW. People will always look over you, around you and underestimate you. Brock: Don’t you think I’ve realized this? Stephen: Oh, I know you have, but dude instead on moping around complaining like you always do, why don’t you do something about it. (Brock puts out his cigarette and looks at Stephen…) Brock: Ya know what, I am gonna do something about it, and that something is Mike Storm. Stephen: Atta boy. Brock: I dunno if I have a jinx, a curse, or just bad luck. But whatever it is, it will end on Sunday with a win over Mike Storm. The man that thinks I should be in prison. The man who thinks that I represent everything that is wrong in the world. Ah shit Stephen, Mike’s right I do belong behind bars, and I do represent everything wrong in the world today. But, hey I’m a f*cking blast doing it and that’s all that counts, right? Stephen: F*ckin’ A right! (Brock looks up at the clock and stands up excitedly…) Brock: Shit! I gotta get to the airport, my flight to the PPV leaves in twenty minutes! Stephen: Let’s go! (We fade to darkness as Brock and Stephen frantically get ready to go to the airport…)
