Chapter 004 - Going Bananas

28 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08-28-2002 To my mother, to my father,It's your son or it's your daughter,Are my screams loud enough for you to hear me?Should I turn this up for you? Life is what? Life is stupid... life is overrated and I don’t want to live a life anymore. Just take me away… take me far away.. From the pain I must endure. I dream of this land… where I do not love or think. Fuck It All… Yoshio Akinaga… Yoshio Akinaga.. Yoshio Akinaga! Ai Yah!!!! What the hell is going on? Your coffee, 2 sugars and a crème with a touch of French vanilla? Oh yes… leave it here and I’ll drink it in a minute. Could I get a banana please? Umm.. Let me check in the back if we have any, please excuse me. Yeah sure whatever… Yoshio Akinaga is sitting on a stool holding the paper to his face while reading the news section. His scar over his white eye begins to look bigger as he opens his eyes wide looking to his left. He spots a young asian female looking at children passing by going to school. She is sipping her coffee as Yoshio mumbles to himself. Jina looked a bit like her but she was much prettier. Sir… Yes? Your banana? My banana? Yes, would you like your banana? You sick perverted white man! Excuse me? I don’t believe it… you want my banana don’t you? No… I was going to give you the banana you asked for My banana? No.. the chef’s banana! Yoshio Akinaga looks as the waiter that looks to be a small lobby for hotel guests slams the banana onto the table, shaking tables nearby. And there is your banana… thank you.The waiter leaves the receipt flipped the other side for Yoshio to ponder at as he walks off with a smile as he fixes his shirt and walks off back into the kitchen. Yoshio Akinaga mumbles as he looks at the banana as if it was speaking to it. Akinaga! Akinaga! Its your birthday tomorrow… What are you going to do? Like it matters? But its your birthday and you should be happy! You’re a year older and everybody’s going to celebrate! Who? Your friends? Your Family? I don’t have that…. I’ll celebrate with you, with kiwi, mango, papaya, apple, orange, grape, and everybody else. Wo he le shem me? Yoshio Akinaga peels the skin of the banana. And If I don’t? AHHHHHH it hurts! Stop Please STOP! Yoshio Akinaga smiles as people nearby watch him peeling the banana slowly, mumbling to himself. OH yes… feel the pain… oh slow and painful, suffering… just suffer… make me smile… make me happy! Ahhhhh! Yoshio Akinaga stands up and with the banana’s head missing in his mouth. He walks off dropping the money he owed. Bananas… your friends are going down just like you. (WTF is this? I don’t know.. Im sleepy good nyte)
