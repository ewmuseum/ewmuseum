Date: Today (Our scene fades in to show the outside of “Vinny’s Place”, a small pub located in the heart of scenic Milwaukee, Wisconsin on a cold, windy fall night. As we pan down the streets, we see that they are mostly empty, except for a few sparse people either walking to one of the four bars that line the streets, including “Vinny’s”. We can see an old bum walking down the street, with a bottle of unidentifiable booze in one hand and a white cat under his other arm. We notice that the old man is dressed in a tattered jacket that doesn’t look very warm. We can see that he is also wearing a pair of ripped up and torn overalls and a pair of Pony tennis shoes that are way too small for him, for we can see his toes sticking out of the front of them. As he walks down the sidewalk, the man takes a swig of the booze, and pets the cat. As he passes people, he asks them for money, giving a line about how he was once a great wrestler who was screwed out of his career buy a evil dictator. Most people just shuffle past him quickly, ignoring him, while other toss him a few cents hoping that he will go away happy. We then pan further down the street, and see a young couple, a muscular dark haired man and a beautiful young woman, walking down the sidewalk, talking to themselves. The old bum sees the young couple coming towards him and he begins to shuffle towards them. The young couple, lost in conversation, doesn’t even notice the old homeless man coming towards until the old man comes up and grabs the young woman gently by the arm. The young blond haired woman jumps back and wrenches her arm away from the old man, who just looks back at her with pleading eyes…) Old Bum: Please madam, could you spare me a few cents, anything will do… (The old bum reaches out his hand apathetically, but the young woman slaps it away with her purse and steps away from the bum, as she looks at him disgustedly…) Woman: No! Stay away from me, you no-good street scum! Old Bum: But…I’m hungry…please… (The old bum takes a step toward the couple, when this time the young man steps in between the bum and what appears to be his girlfriend. Now that we are closer, we see that the young man is quite bigger than the bum, almost a foot taller. We can see from the tight white t-shirt that he wears underneath his brown suede jacket that he is well built and muscular. He shoves the old man back a few feet. The old man stumbles back a bit and regains his ground…) Old Bum: Please sir, I mean no harm… Young Man: Listen you old bum, why don’t you find a box to live in or something, huh!? (The young man takes a step forward, rolling up the sleeves of his coat in the process. The old man takes a step back, and holds up his hands in a innocent fashion…) Old Bum: Listen mister, I don’t want no trouble! I ‘m just really hungry, that’s all! Young man: Well, then maybe you’d like a knuckle sandwich, huh? (The young woman peeks her head over the massive shoulders of her boyfriend and sneers at the cowering bum…) Woman: Rough him up good Jimmy! (The young man just grins at his girlfriend and winks at her…) Jimmy: Don’t worry babe, this will be kinds fun… (The dark haired boy who we now know as Jimmy, starts to advance towards the old man, who now has a look of fear in his eyes. The old man back pedals quickly, and without watching where he was going, trips over a garbage can that was sitting on the sidewalk. As the man looms over the bum, he lets out another plea…) Old Bum: Please sir, I beg you, just leave me alone! (Jimmy just snarls at the old bum as he balls up his fists…) Jimmy: I don’t think so pop, do you know who I am? I’m Jimmy “The Muscle” Russell, the best independent wrestler in the whole state of Wisconsin and probably the whole USA! And that gorgeous young woman over there… (Points to his attractive girlfriend…)…is my valet, “Luscious” Lucy Valentine. And do you know what I do to people who touch my Lucy, do you!? Old Bum: …no…I’m sorry… (Jimmy “The Muscle” Russell lets out a laugh at the bums expense as he cracks the knuckles on his large hands…) Jimmy: Well then, let me show you… (Jimmy bends down and picks up the bum, lifting him off the ground by his raggedy shirt so that they are eye level. We see that the old bums feet are off the ground now…) Old Bum: Leave me alone, I’m sorry! Man: You will be sorry when you wake up from the beating I’m about to give you! (Jimmy tightens his grip on the old bum’s shirt and rears back his arm, clenching his fist. The old bum lets out a cry of terror and closes his eyes. Jimmy grins and starts to go for the old man’s face with his fist, but is stopped by the squeal of tires and the roar of a muscle car engine. Jimmy stops his fist in mid-punch and raises a eyebrow. The old man does the same, opening his eyes. Jimmy and the old man both look over to see a jet black 1969 Ford Mustang pulled up to the curb. The windows are tinted, so we cannot see who is inside, but none the less this car looks familiar to Rush television. We can hear the thunderous sounds of “Who Made Who” by AC/DC coming from inside the car. The ground around the car literally shakes from the maxxed out bass of the system inside the car. Jimmy drops the old bum back to the ground, but still hangs on to his shirt. We pan over to the car to see that it is now shut-off, the driver and passenger side door both swing open simultaneously. Stepping out from the drivers side door is none other than “The Innovator” Brock Newbludd, stepping out from the passengers side is his partner in crime, “The New Legend” Stephen Blood, who is still retired or so he says. Brock is clad in a pair of baggy fitting camouflage pants with black jump boots. We see a tight fitting white t-shirt showing off his impressive physique underneath his trademark black leather jacket. Brock’s hair is spike up in his familiar fashion, and we now see that his goatee has grown a little bit longer and he now has long sideburns. Stephen is wearing a pair of baggy fitting black denim jeans with snakeskin, steel tipped, cowboy boots. He also wears a black leather jacket that matches Brock’s. We notice that both men have beers in their hands, while Brock has a cigarette hanging from his mouth. Brock shuts his door and walks around to the front of the car, while Stephen does the same. Both men lean against the front of the car, surveying the scene. Brock takes a drag of his cigarette and strokes his goatee curiously…) Brock: What bar are we gonna hit first tonight? (Stephen shrugs his shoulders and throws some beer down his throat…) Stephen: Vinny’s place? Brock: Nah, we ALWAYS get into fights there, let’s same that place for last.Stephen: Ok… Brock: How about Deano’s, we always score with the chicks there! And besides, I’m looking for some play tonight… (Stephen slugs Brock in the arm before he can finish…) Brock: What!? Stephen: We’re married now dude, no more bar sluts! (Brock sighs, takes the last drag of his cigarette and throws it on the ground…) Brock: Yeah, but bar sluts are like totally cool and kinky in the sack! Stephen: No Brock! Brock: F*ck! Fine, what bar do we go to? (Before Stephen can answer, we pan back to the old bum and Jimmy “The Muscle”. Jimmy picks the old man back up and sneers at him…) Old Bum: C’mon, let me go mister! (Jimmy shrugs his shoulders and picks the bum up higher off the ground…) Jimmy: Whatever you say! (Jimmy gorilla passes the man up over his head and tosses him away, but it just so happens that the bum flies directly toward the Brock and Stephen who are still arguing over which bar to go to first and aren’t even paying attention to the situation going on behind them. We watch as the old man flies across the sidewalk and lands face first onto Brock’s vintage Mustang and then bouncing off of it and hitting Brock and Stephen in the back, spilling Brocks beer in the process…) Brock: What the f*ck!? My beer! My car! (Brock turns around to see the old man knocked out on the hood of his car, and then looks up to see Jimmy chuckling at him and Stephen…) Jimmy: Woops, my bad! Stephen: What a f*cking assclown, eh? (Brock doesn’t even acknowledge Stephen and walks right up into Jimmy’s face…) Brock: That was my car dickweed… (Jimmy just grins at Brock…) Jimmy: I guess it was the wrong place to park now wasn’t it? (Stephen raises his eyebrows at this and watches contently to see what Brock will do next…) Brock: Gee, I guess I did…I should have known that you were going throw the old bum right there now shouldn’t I have? Jimmy: That’s right little man, you just better move along with your bald headed buddy there, before I, Jimmy “The Muscle” Russell have to throw both of you to the curb… (Brock backs down a little bit from the bigger man, showing fear in his eyes, Stephen gives a look of curiosity at this, for usually Brock backs down from no man…) Brock: Jeez, you must be some big superstar with a name like that…. (Jimmy grows a cocky grin…) Jimmy: You damn right little man, I am the cream of the crop of independent wrestlers in Wisconsin… (Brock now grows a cocky grin and steps back up into the face of Jimmy…) Brock: Really now Jimbo, I happen to know a little of wrestling my self… Jimmy: Yeah, high school doesn’t count assclown… (Stephen gets up off the car and sneers at Jimmy…) Stephen: What did you call him? Jimmy: An assclown, the catchphrase that I coined… Stephen: You’re the assclown you piss lapping pissclam! Jimmy: Hahahaha…OOF! (Brock cuts Jimmy off in mid laugh by a punch to the ribs Jimmy keels over, but stays on his feet. Brock grabs Jimmy by the head and hits him with his patented “Old Style DDT” on the pavement, knocking him out in the process. Brock gets up, dusts off his clothes and stares down at Jimmy who is laying face down on the pavement. Brock then looks over at the old bum that is sitting on the curb, holding his head. Stephen then walks next to Brock and looks down at the downed “Muscle”…) Stephen: (Looking down at the knocked out Jimmy…) Spread the word, assclown, the Blood Brothers are back in town. Brock: F*ckin’ A… (Brock and Stephen highfive, then Brock turns around and walks over to the old bum and then digs through his jacket pocket. He then pulls out a hundred dollar bill out of it and tosses it to the old man…) Brock: Go get drunk somewhere, huh? Oh, and maybe some new pants, since your balls are hanging out of them… (The old man frowns and looks down to see that, indeed, his nuts are hanging out of a hold in the crotch of his pants. Thankfully, it’s censored from our eyes. The old man stuffs his nuts back into his pants and stands up…) Old Bum: Thanks mister! Brock: No problem.(The old man gets up, and starts walking down the street, sniffing the hundred dollar bill as he walks. He walks around the corner and out of sight, as we pan back to the Blood Brothers who look at each other and shrugs their shoulders. Brock lights up another cigarette and Stephen pulls another beer out of his jacket pocket…) Brock: Where were we? (Stephen scratches his head…) Stephen: Talking about which bar we were going to go to… Brock: That’s right, how about Deano’s, they always got some fine pussy, disease free most of the time too. (Stephen sighs and throws his arms up in the air…) Stephen: I give up! Brock: Sweet! Then Deano’s it is! (Brock and Stephen start walking towards the bar, as we slowly fade to black…)
