F*ck Deadlines

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: I write wheneva I want! Wheneva I want! “But El Spiffy! doesn’t want the World Title.” *sigh* “I don’t think you’re getting the point here….” “El Spiffy! wants the Syndicate Title.” “If you get the World Title, you don’t NEED the Syndicate title.” “But El Spiffy! wants the Syndicate Title!” “Jesus Christ…” Juan Carlos Smith stands up from the table, sending his chair skidding across the floor. The room is dark, with the exception of, you guessed it, El Spiffy!, sitting at a small card table in the center, bathed in the light of a pseudo-interrogation lamp. Juan Carlos disappears into the shadows, but can be heard cursing under his breath and kicking a wall. Out of the darkness comes the figure of Julio Diaz Perez O’Neil, the other Spanish commentator. He sits down at the table, remaining calm, and gazes at the luchadore opposite him, his hands folded patiently. “Spiffy!. Buddy. Pal. Senor. Tonight you have a shot at the World Heavyweight Title of RUSH.” “El Spiffy! doesn’t wan-“ “This is the best belt in RUSH. If you win it, you are the best guy in RUSH. The Syndicate title, if you’re lucky, makes you third or fourth best.” “El Spiffy! wants the Syndicate Title.” Silence. “OK, Spiffster, we’re going to try a little experimant here. You’re going to pretend for once that your mind has more than one track, alright?” El Spiffy! pauses, to contemplate, then nods. “And in this gloriously multi-tasking mind of yours, you can focus on more than one thing at a time. For instance, [he holds up one finger] you can want the Syndicate title and [holds up a second] you can want the World Title, too! See, you can do that, because your brain is capable of such complex functions! Do you follow?” Another pause. He shakes his head. Julio sighs loudly. “El Spiffy wants-“ “Yeah, you’ve made that pretty clear bud, thanks. Get back to me when you get another original thought.” Silence. Out of the darkness Juan Carlos Smith comes stomping back up to the table. He has a marker in one hand and a sheet of paper in the other. On the paper are drawn two championship belts. The one at the top of the paper, labeled “wORLd” in hastily scratched letters, looks very fancy and appealing, with fancy designs nad little drawn diamonds in it. The one at the bottom, labeled “SYNdIcATE” in equally poor handwriting, looks much less attractive, with the plates falling off and those little wavy lines above it to tell everyone that the belt smells. “You see how this belt up here looks so much better than that one? That’s cause the World title is MUCH better!” Silence. “El Spiffy! just wants the Syndicate title.” “Goddammit! But WHY?” “Quo.” Silence. “Oh.” The commentators look at each other, then turn and walk away, disappearing into the dark. Soon, a door opens, and their frames can be seen leaving as the room is bathed in light. The door swings closed again, and there is only El Spiffy! in the room. Staring. Silence.
