Why does it always after be books

29 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29/7/02 The sun continued on its inhumane coarse had it had down for almost two months in New York. The heat at that moment at its worst in the Hospital and RUSH’s resident magician Andi the Magnificent was sweating like a cob as he sat in the high backed green leather chair. The young magician continued to ponder on the suggestion that Scott Taylor had given him over the phone last week, Andi was sure they had to be a way of helping Fluffy but how he was going to find that out was a mystery unto its self. Still he was not only tormented by killing that thing known only as a ‘Welcome’. But now he was being haunted at the by the fact he couldn’t help the one he loved. Time slowly dragged on as he ploughed all his mental abilities into finding a way round his current problem. But alas still it eluded him. Andi was quickly pulled out of his reprieve as a Chinese nurse walked in to check on Fluffy, Andi only briefly looked up at the busy nurse before his mind took over his fleeting thoughts. It was some ten minutes after the busy nurse left had the young magician came up with a solution to his little problem. It was some five or six months ago since he had seen this man and the last time being when he needed a incantation to save his beloved Fluffy from what was a life and death situation. Quickly the bulky magician flung himself out of the room and ran down the long hospital corridor almost knocking over her old woman who hobbling down the hallway using her little steel Zimmer frame. “You little hooligan, my god kids today always in a rush.” The little irate woman screamed out as Andi continued running down the corridor and into a just closing elevator. No sooner had the magician got out of the hospital was he in a yellow taxi cab and making his way toward china town to see the man he had almost killed. It took almost an hour in the busy New York traffic before Andi arrived at the place he had hoped he would not after to return to seen as he was so ashamed of his actions that night. Slowly the magician paid the smiling Cab driver who had tried to speak to the brooding Andi as the travelled but soon got the hint after five minutes of no answers. Andi quickly got out of the car and took a good luck round the busy China town area before slowly walking towards the little basement shop on the corner. The strange foreign smells hit his nose as soon as the big man walked in causing a large wooden wind chime to start up announcing his arrival to the little white haired Chinese man who is stood behind the little tall wooden counter. “Ah Mr Magnificent I presume and why may I ask has it taken you so long to come and seek my aid?” The white haired Chinese man says without lifting his head up from the little thing he was doing. Andi stood back in amazement at the little Chinese mans observation and struggled to get his words out. “How did you know that I would come and seek your help?” The little Chinese man finally looked his deep black beady eyes onto Andi before casually smiling at him before answering in what could only be described as sarcastic. “I do have a TV and I do try and keep a track on what is happening and if the rabbit one is in trouble then I know that you would come to me eventually and again I was proven right so do me a favour and get on with it because I do not like you anymore than you like me.” Andi again was flabbergasted as the man who seemed so small and fickle could almost through using words make the big man seem rather small. “Well you see I need some help in bringing Fluffy out of her coma.” The little man smiled for a second before locking on a look and speaking in a tone that could only truly be described as threatening. “You wish me to help you mess around with the force that drives the very fabric of not only magic but life its self. You apprentice do not and would not even begin to comprehend the complexities of the type of magic you would be dealing with.” Andi shrugged at the magician before pouring his heart out just like he had done before. “Listen I know I cant understand everything about Magic but I just cant sit there waiting for her to get better its doing my nut in will you just help me out please or I am going to go mad.” As if something in Andi’s words had trigged something from his past or scared the living smoke out of him, the young magician wasn’t sure but with a sigh the small Chinese man spoke. “Very well, you must search for the book of Nastramus in there you shall find what ye looks for but be warned great perils beset the way to the lost book and only the strong-willed and strong at heart will succeed, and if you do find the book then use it with caution because nothing every turns out like it seems.” With that Andi saw a swirling mass of light appear before him. “Go know young magician because it is clear that your heart is set on thy task.” Slowly Andi stepped into the lights and a great feeling of uncertainty came over him before finally his tall muscular body was enveloped in swirling lights.
