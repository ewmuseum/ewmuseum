CrossRoads Chronicles #5: Rich Cannon

12 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8.12.02 “Ignorance is not bliss, it is oblivion.”--Philip Wylie CrossRoads almost finally here. Months of waiting and weeks of preparing, and what do we have before us? Just like in RUSH, the I.R.S. Faction is doing everything possible to continue their reign of power. How you ask? By stacking the odds in the Brawl for it All, inevitably high and difficult. With who? Well, let us just take a look shall we? Rich Cannon...better known as Steve Carnage Jr. How about G.O.D.? A great wrestler in his own right, even though it is unknown whether he is male, female, or unknown. How about Statuz Quo and Dante Jackson? Perhaps a more famous name in Tyler Valentine. Is that it? Are those all the names? Of course not, for that could be just a coincidence. A question I ask to you people now. What else is new? Nothing. Barriers to break, bridges to cross and another battle royal to win. Do not think that I have named all of the names in that rumble, I have saved the biggest and most important name in there, because it cannot just be told to you. No, it needs to be given the entrance, the promotion, the build up that it deserves. So without further ado...the Most Important Name…is Scott Cage. 'Nuff Said. I don't really know how to begin here, I mean, I am near positive that I have created confusion and near havoc with my actions as of late. I will not come out here and be a hypocrite, and say that the fans do not persuade me anymore, because they do. I will not come out here and say that what I did, I did because you forced me into it. What I will say is this: while I have always said that I do everything for the fans, and that I got into this business partly to have you all feel the way I did as a child. However, at the risk of having everything crash down on my head, at the risk of having the Syndicate Division fall down on my shoulders until the day that I die. On the risk that I could cost myself my own career for being stubborn, I have decided that I can still please all the fans out there, as well as have the opportunity to do whatever I truly want, with the backers that I now have. I hope that that can explain to you the reasoning of my actions, and I hope that you can still feel the same way towards me. Have the love and affection that you did towards me, have the enthusiasm and the slight tinge of jealousy. But truly the most important is that you keep the same utmost respect for me. What happened you ask? What brought on the change of feeling, the change of heart, the change of self-actualization that I have taken? What really happened was Scott Cage retaining the title from G.O.D. and *gasp* Rich Cannon. Over the months that I have been in RUSH Wrestling, I have had more then one set of Network Division misfits clawing at my back. I have had more then one mid-carder suit trying to drown me, to hold my head down. I have had enough of it, and the one sure fire way to put an end to this injustice, other then killing Statuz Quo, is to outsmart him in the Battle Royal. Now most, if not all of the fans are questioning themselves, as well as myself, wondering if giving there faith to me was something a tad extreme, and wondering if what I did was a tad extreme. What I am doing is simply a necessity, a way to become confident, to be able to not need eyes in the back of my head, or have to use a fool like Rich Cannon to add to my win streak. Having to wander around in the same division with the same nitwits, fools who truly believed that they could defeat me. You thought Rich Cannon, that you were going to take the Network Title from me, twice. And so twice you were wrong, terrible wrong. See, evil can be a two-way street, and unfortunately, you just went a little too slow. Maybe next time you will out-smart me...as impossible as it may be, the answer is no. Outsmarting me, Scott Cage? You have better luck growing a set of breasts…and they might look dandy on a bitch like you. BA-ZING! (Fade in to a shot of Scott Cage’s arms coming down as he briskly walks down the hall of a local high school. He grins, still smiling, the Network Title still over his shoulder, towards a door. The door reads HISTORY 101. He opens the door and a bald professor sits at a desk…) Scott Cage: Is this History 101? Man: It is. Scott Cage: And you are Mr. Henderson, I presume? Mr. Henderson: I am. Scott Cage: It’s good to meet you. Mr. Henderson: I presume you're Mr. Cage? Scott Cage: Yes I am. Do you have the tape I requested? Mr. Henderson: Ah yes, the tape, well...uhh...not really... Scott Cage: Excuse me?Mr. Henderson: I…I don’t have the tape. Scott Cage: Henderson, you do realize that's the whole reason I came here, don’t you? Mr. Henderson: W-well, Mr. Cage, it was very hard to get. (Scott leans in and grabs Mr. Henderson by the shirt…) Scott Cage: You listen here little man, you're going to go get that tape or else I'm going to have to tear off your arms.Mr. Henderson: You…You know, this is no way to treat a teacher. Scott Cage: And I suppose you're really a pivotal part of society?Mr. Henderson: As a matter of fact, I am. Without teachers like me, the society would crumble, much like that of the Ancient Romans. Scott Cage: Oh? If you're so important, then the educational system should be flourishing. Mr. Henderson: But it is! Scott Cage: Then explain Keanu Reeves to me. (Henderson lets out a bitter laugh…) Mr. Henderson: Well he must have slipped through the cracks. Scott Cage: Could be. Mr. Henderson: Yes, well anyway Mr. Cage, if you'd let me go, I'd be happy to go and find the tape. I'm sure they're some where in the basement. (Scott lets go of Henderson’s shirt, eyeing the teacher with suspicion…) Mr. Henderson: Thank you. (Henderson goes back to his desk and pulls something out, and hides it under his shirt…) Scott Cage: Is that...? IT IS! HAND OVER THAT VIDEO! (Henderson takes off, with the video in his hands and Scott quickly follows. As the corny race music begins, Scott begins to chase Henderson around the school. Suddenly, the scene fades to show Scott, looking in classrooms as Mr. Henderson sneaks behind him. He turns and begins to chase him as Henderson continues to run. Suddenly, Mr. Henderson bursts onto the scene again. He looks around and doesn't see Scott anywhere in sight. He turns and behind him walks forwards as he looks backwards. Needless to say, he runs into the wall, quickly falling to the floor unconscious, as Scott comes into view. He walks over to him and tries to wake him up as the corny race music stops…) Scott Cage: Wake up, old man. (Henderson lays limply on the floor, as Cage kicks him in the side…) Scott Cage: I SAID WAKE UP, OLD MAN! (Henderson lays motionless, as Scott grabs him by the collar of his shirt, and begins slapping him viciously…) Scott Cage: I…SAID…WAKE...UP...YOU...STUPID...GEEZER! (The elderly teacher is still unconscious, as Cage tilts his head, thinking of what to do…) Scott Cage: All right...I need to think of something. Wait a second...what's that...WHAT THE HELL?! GET OFF MY LEG! (Scott looks down and sees Mr. Henderson gnawing on his leg! He shakes off the teacher with ease, then pins him to the wall…) Scott Cage: WHAT DO YOU WANT FOR THOSE VIDEOS?! Mr. Henderson: I want you to pay my way through cooking school. (Cage lets go of Henderson, as the teacher brushes off the dust and grime on his brown suit…) Scott Cage: You're serious? Mr. Henderson: I am 100 percent serious, Mr. Cage. I want to become a chef. Scott Cage: All right, how much will it cost me? Mr. Henderson: Five thousand dollars. Scott Cage: FIVE THOUSAND DOLLARS TO TEACH YOU TO HOW TO FLIP A BURGER? Mr. Henderson: You better believe it. (Scott pulls out his checkbook from his jacket, and begins writing the check for five grand…) Scott Cage: All right, I'll put the check the floor and you do the same with the tape. Mr. Henerson: Fine... (Scott places the check on the floor and Henderson does the same with the video. Cagepicks up the video and opens it…) Scott Cage: This is worth five grand. It's going to completely bankrupt Starr Industries...WHAT THE?! THE CASE IS EMPTY! (Scott looks up and Mr. Henderson is long gone…) Scott Cage: I’ll kill him. I’ll kill that old geezer if it’s the last thing I do. (Scott throws the case to the floor and it shatters. He walks off into the horizon, as the camera fades...)
