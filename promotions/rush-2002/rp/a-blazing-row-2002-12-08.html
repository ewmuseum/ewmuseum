A blazing row

8 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-08-02 (Voice of Livewire) Well, the challenge starts here. No more shows, no more interviews, no more laughable comments from Kris Raves and James Williams, business starts here. August 18, 2002, a day that will be more significant than any other for one particular twenty-one year old Englishman. August 18, 2002, the day that destiny will be conquered head on and one man will rise above all adversity to become the number one contender to the World title. One man will stand on the top of the mountain that is the wrestling food chain and be able to look down on all the others. So, why should I believe that, that person is me? Well why not? Usually I would not rate my chances very highly in a clusterfuck such as the one, which will take place at Crossroads. Despite my obvious size and strength advantage there would be an immense amount of difficulty in me winning a brawl containing over 20 other wrestlers. That is because I lack experience and the same ring sense as many of my adversaries. Looking into Crossroads there are many names that will be more hotly tipped to win than my own. Wrestlers such as the former five-time PCW tag-team champion Brock Newbludd, wrestlers such as the unbelievably talented and dedicated Tyler Valentine and wrestlers as sneaky and under-handed as my former ally Biohazard or the heinous D-kay Calyx. But I will stand in the ring with men like these with the exact same chance as they. No longer will past wins, form, confidence or booking placement on match cards be important. Now is the chance for me to stake my place in immortality on August 18, 2002, a certain debonair Englishman may just be the man to step forward as the number one man in RUSH. The scene opens with Livewire sitting in the drivers seat of his jet black alfa romeo spider. His right hand is positioned around the rim of the leather steering wheel, while his left arm rests on the car door. The car is parked outside one of Washington’s largest department stores and the engine continues to run as time passes by. Next to Livewire in the passengers seat of the car is his absent-minded but very good-looking girlfriend Fiona Johnston. Fiona is staring out of the cars window, and is trying her best not to look in the direction of her boyfriend. Livewire likewise is looking directly in the opposite direction of his girlfriend and has a pissed off look on his face as if he is chewing a swarm of bees. There seems to be a fidget in Fiona’s chair as Livewire glances across to look at her. He tuts and goes back to looking out at the road in front and the passing cars before finally breaking the silence and turning to the still fidgeting Fiona to talk. “Why did you have to do it Fiona?” he asks in a questioning tone “Surely even you would know how stupid it was to think that you would never exceed the limit at the rate you were buying clothes on the card”. Livewire once again turns to look out at the road as Fiona slowly stops fidgeting and takes off her seat belt, knowing that she is obviously not going to be driven anyway in the near future. “Huh, it was like….totally a like…surprise to me too” she replies innocently. “Do you think I honestly wanted for what to have happened a few minutes ago to have happened honey? That stupid women behind the desk was lucky I didn’t slap the taste right out of her mouth”. Livewire mockingly smirks at his girlfriends reply and slams his right hand hard onto the steering wheel causing Fiona to jump from the shock. “You’re lucky that, that women behind the desk didn’t rip up my card and ban us from the store” Livewire roars back angrily before placing his head in his hands and groaning in a frustrated state, frightening Fiona furthermore. Ok, so lets take a slight quick re-cap. Why is Livewire so angry with his beautiful redheaded damsel? Well it seems that Fiona’s incessant urge for buying all things Prada, Gucci or Armani has finally landed her in trouble. A recent shopping spree filled with designer goodies was abruptly cut short as both she and Livewire tried to purchase the goods at the check-out, only to be told that they had exceeded their limit on the department stores card. Both had to leave the expensive department store empty-handed and red-faced with the indignity of knowing that they would probably be the topic of conversation with employees and customers in the store for weeks to come. So Livewire feels embarrassed, his girlfriend is an over-spending shopaholic and the whole matter has infuriated Livewire somewhat to the point where he is unable to drive without berating his girlfriend first. Ok, lets get back to the happy couple or should I say not so happy couple as they remain seated and bad-tempered in Livewire’s sleek, expensive parked Alfa Romeo spider. “I like…haven’t spent that much on the card. I’ve like…spent only about $5000” pleads Fiona with all the innocence of a car salesman sealing a deal with a blind man. Livewire shakes his head and grins insanely at Fiona. “Fiona the limit on the card was $1000” he replies now close to breaking point. Fiona shrugs her shoulders and bites her lip as her slow-witted mind finally conjures up the conclusion that she hasn’t spent as much as she thought on clothes, but nevertheless she has spent enough to heap embarrassment on herself and her image conscious boyfriend. “Alright, I’m sorry, I apologise” says Fiona belatedly “But gees, you’ve recently been really….” Livewire cuts Fiona off and runs his hand through his pony-tailed hair, causing a few strands to move out of place. “Moody!” Livewire sneers as he brings his face closer to Fiona’s. “You were going to say that I’ve recently been really moody weren’t you?” Livewire slouches back in his chair and mutters to himself as Fiona sits quietly next to him. Fiona is about to get up and leave the car, most probably thinking that a bus ride home is a far safer trip but Livewire suddenly starts talking to himself, causing her to stop and listen. “Can you blame me for being a little moody so to speak? Can you blame me for not being entirely pleased about how my life is going?” whines Livewire. Fiona sits back into the car and takes her boyfriends hand and rubs it gently. “Fiona, this week poses to me the biggest obstacle that I’ve had to contend with in the wrestling ring. Two massive battles, one victor”. Fiona tries not to day-dream and politely nod as she usually does when Livewire talks about wrestling but instead listens to every word he has to say. “Plus all this in-fighting with Shack-Pack, heck in recent weeks I’ve had to become something like an unofficial leader for them. Fiona I’m twenty one years old, how many twenty year olds do you know that have to try and control two grown men like I do?” Fiona of course doesn’t know the answer as it has been asked in a rhetorical way. So she instead just frowns blankly and lets Livewire ramble on. “Tsk, tsk, yep I sure do have the troubles of the world on my shoulders right now. The only thing that has brought a smile to my face of late was my win with Joe last week against Biohazard and his brainwashed goon.” Livewire says with a smile as he remembers himself and Joe Bananas masterfully dealing with their far inferior opponents. Fiona still has that stupid frown on her face and continues to look at Livewire blankly. “Huh, like..who is like…biohazard? Isn’t Biohazard a like…a chemical no it’s like…a rock band isn’t it?” Livewire smiles and tries to laugh off Fiona’s child like enthusiasm for all things confusing. “Yes babe, Biohazard is a chemical and it’s also the name of a rock band” Livewire replies much to Fiona’s glee as she claps her hands excitedly. “But it’s also the nickname for that guy that I told you about before. You know, William Perry, the guy that I used to wrestle with a couple of years ago” he says hoping that it propels one of Fiona’s brain cells into life. Fiona puts her finger to her mouth and thinks hard before speaking again. “Oh yeah, wasn’t he the disfigured guy who has an ass for a face?” Fiona asks in all seriousness. “No, that’s George Bush” Livewire grins wickedly “Biohazard is the guy who has a pile of shit for a face. Aaahh, it was so good to slam his deranged body into the canvas last Thursday on Adrenaline. I suppose that tag-team victory proved the point, that 1999 was so last decade and that any victories that he did have over me have now well and truly been put to rest”. Livewire leans back on the leopard skin cover of the car seat and puts his arm around Fiona. She playfully pokes him in the ribs and snuggles up to him with sly grin. “Does this mean I’m forgiven? she asks. Livewire nods his head and looks out onto the passing motorway again. “Yep, you are darling, but I’m still feel a little pissed off, I think it’s something to do with the thought of Biohazard’s ugly imperfect head in my face. Let’s go to that small bar on 52nd street. I need a drink, a stiff drink.” And with that said the personification of cool turns on the cars ignition and drives off into the Washington early evening dusk. The scene closes with the view of Livewire’s car number plate which reads LIVELY 1 . Six men one prize, the Network title. Will Livewire finally get the one title belt that has eluded him from day one in RUSH? Take care Livewire-ites. The End
