CrossRoads Chronicles #3: Ricky Starr

10 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8.10.02 'It is a sin to believe the evil of others, but it is seldom a mistake.'- H.L. Mencken This week begins the unveiling of a battle royal at CrossRoads, a crowning of a new era so to speak, as we find out who will win the coveted Number One Spot. The name on that spot, will be one that is as deserving of it. A man that is as talented as anyone in RUSH, and a man who will hold that crown high above his head, holding it equal to that he already holds dear. Ladies and gentleman, boys and girls, wrestlers of all federations…I give to you now the solemn swear, that I will not rest because I chose not to. I will not cry because I do not believe in it. I will not die, because I am immortal inside of the ring. I give you my word that at the end of this fight, when the dust is settled, you will have a New Number One Contender for the RUSH Heavyweight Title, and I will be he. I plan on walking into this reign, and creating a blissful ascent that nobody has ever seen before. To those of you who have not been hindered from your opinions of me, I will prove to you with this fight, that I do not get anything handed to me. I will be victorious in this, over so many the greats, because I am the best, I am the apex of wrestling today, and the future as well as the present. I look at the list of competitors and I see names that I know, Names that I don't, and names that really have some backing behind them. But what I don't see, while looking through that entire list of competitors, is that I don't see a Real Champion. I look in the mirror, and I see a Real Champion. I see a Winner, and that is the most basic way that I can tell you that. I have been silent recently, trying my very best to keep myself composed, to keep control of my emotions as well as my tongue, which can in fact be the dagger in anyone's heart. I knew that I needed to do something, to really slay the doubts in anyone's mind of whether I have had everything handed to me, whether or not I have been served up my titles, my accomplishments here in RUSH on the proverbial silver platter. Ricky Starr, the man who wished me malice more then any other, the man who nearly beat me in an attempt to get the title away from me because I was undeserving. The only thing I can say, is that I have never been more focused on what I have to hold. I have never been more focused on holding the RUSH Heavyweight Title. Possibilities are endless now that I am not alone, now that I have my backup, now that I have everything in control; I am prepared for the inevitable as well as the unconscionable. I am ready for the steps that I need to take, especially the ones in the Battle Royal which lays just a few days away. I am a little giddy at the thought of my dream being under my belt, and it brings a little smile to my face. (As the scene fades in, there is an empty room, no occupiers in sight, just tables with chips and such and a few couches and chairs. The camera focuses in, moments later, a man is standing in the corner, his back turned. The camera zooms in closer, and two more people appear. The process continues until the room begins to fill. The camera zooms to a banner…labeled 'AWA REUNION PARTY'. Many big names pop up from the now defunct AWA, including the idiotic David Parker. Then, we see Scott Cage talking with Vinnie Catante and David Parker, the RUSH Network Title, slung over his shoulder. Catante and Cage look confused, as Parker has a white wig on and what looks like a blue robe on, over his suit.) Scott Cage: Uhh, are you okay? David Parker: OF COURSE! I'M THE 14 TIME WORLD CHAMP! WOOOOOO! Scott Cage: He's back to this? Vinnie Catante: I dunno. Scott Cage: What do you mean, 'I dunno'? He used to be our boss. Vinnie Catante: He was? What happened to Parker? Scott Cage: Do you EVER pay attention? Vinnie Catante: What did you say? I wasn't paying attention. Scott Cage: Man, Parker, you are one delusional President…David Parker: Delusional? DELUSIONAL? Pal, you're not talk'n to Michael Jackson. You're not talk'n to the Uni-bomber! Your talk'n to the NAT'CHER BOOAY! WHOO! WHOO! (cough) WHOOOO!!!Scott Cage: Riiiight. David Parker: Jax screwed me, pal! And the aliens are real! They live, pal! They live, but we're gonna fight back. We're gonna walk that isle, style and profile- and they're gonna bleed ALLLLLLLLLL Night-Voice: Flair?! Where da' fuck 'dat bitch at?! Ima' whoop his motherfuckin' ass. (We turn to see a rather sizeable black, his eyes saggy and his hair in a small afro. You quickly realize that it's none other than rapping/wrestling superstar, Carl Cash…) Vinnie Catante: I dunno. Carl Cash: Don't gimme 'dat bitch. Tell me where the mothafucka' is! David Parker: I'm right here! WOOOOO! Scott Cage: Damn bitch, since when are you taller than 5 feet?! David Parker: Watch it pal! I'll take your girl friend there and make a woman out of her. WHOOO!Carl Cash: That's it bitch. You're mine! (Carl Cash jumps on Parker and begins to punch him squarely in the face as he drags him down to the floor. Scott and Vinnie stand there, their hands in their pockets.) Scott Cage: Should we tell him it's really David Parker? Vinnie Catante: Whatever. Scott Cage: All right, well seeing as you can't put together a sensible conversation, I'm moving on. Vinnie Catante: Uh-huh. Scott Cage: You gonna break this up? Vinnie Catante: Of course.... Scott Cage: Whatever, man. (Scott walks away as Catante finally wakes up and sees his former boss being beaten by Carl Cash and attempts to break it up...as Scott is walking, he bumps into someone in ragged and torn clothing. He turns, and he looks familiar, but still lost to you, as he been gone for a long time.) Scott Cage: Excuse me... Man: Hello. Scott Cage: DEREK KRONIC? How did YOU get in here? Man #2: I brought him. Scott Cage: TOKEN WEED?! And how did YOUR ass get into MY party?! We don't allow people on the corporate payroll. Derek Kronic: I'm not on the corporate payroll. Scott Cage: Yes, but you sure are annoying. Derek Kronic: Now that wasn't nice, Scott. I'm hurt. Scott Cage: And I'm leaving. (Scott walks away, looking for greener pastures. He stumbles across the door and sees two very familiar faces.) Scott Cage: Wow, I never expected to see you two here. The USA Kid and Triple X: Well, we do our best to keep up to date with personal appearances. The USA Kid: You stupid whore. You always stole the spotlight. Triple X: Did not! You're the fat one! Scott Cage: Boys, boys, while you're at the party, let's TRY and keep the fighting to a minimum. Triple X: You're lucky the XXX didn't run wild on you! The USA Kid: Yeah, or else I would have had to find a pillow and a couch and take a nice long nap. (As Triple X and the USA Kid get face to face, Scott laughs and walks away as the two sides argue and come close to a brawl. He comes to a corner and NO WAY! IT'S THE JUDGE!) Scott Cage: Oh hell no. Syphon X: HEY SCOTTY! Scott Cage: *sigh* What do you want?! Syphon X: What's going on, bro? Scott Cage: Don't call me bro. Syphon X: No need to be so mean. Scott Cage: I thought you committed suicide.Syphon X: Nope, for some reason though, jumping into a tank full of lobsters wasn't the best idea… Scott Cage: Uh, you don't say. Well, I've got to be going... Syphon X: Wait a minute, bro. I've got a surprise for you. Scott Cage: No, Syphon, it's been, uhhh, fun, but I've got to go. Syphon X: Oh okay, but one question. Scott Cage: What? Syphon X: Did you change your phone number? Because for some reason every time I call you all I get is some weird message telling that it's the wrong number. Scott Cage: Uh…yeah…maybe. (Scott takes off into the crowd, getting as far away from Syphon X as possible. He stops at the food table, talking to himself as he goes.) Scott Cage: Who the hell invited him. It's not like-WHAT THE HELL?! (Scott looks at the table. Titan and Ox O'Henry are obliterating the snack table, eating everything in sight. The USA Kid and Triple X are doing their best to stop them. Unfortunately, it isn't working too well…) The USA Kid: Scott! HELP! Triple X: YEAH! WE'RE NOT GETTING VERY FAR! Scott Cage: Guys, if there's one thing I've learned...it's to stay out of their way when they're eating. Titan: Do you feel something? Ox O'Henry: Yeah, it's like I have this itch on my back...oh hey Scott Cage, right? Scott Cage: Uhhh, yeah... Ox O'Henry: Haven't seen you in a while. Great party...although the food choice is a bit sub-par. Scott Cage: Oh, well you guys have fun... Titan & O'Henry: We will. The USA Kid: Help us! PLEASE! Scott Cage: Sorry guys...I'm leaving. (As Scott laughs and begins to leave, he turns and bumps into…former AWA Disgrunted Reporter Todd Downs!) Scott Cage: Oh Todd! Hey man! What's going on? Todd Downs: Eat shit. Scott Cage: Yeah…same old Todd. (Scott walks out the door, and into the night. The camera fades...)
