Freedom.

19 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 19th, 2002 A.D. I lay upon the slab of stone which I call a bed. I am awake, yet I dreams. I dreams of days past, and days yet to come. What is that word? That word which is the embodiment of happiness... the embodiment of suffering... the embodiment of life? Freedom People fail to understand the meaning of the word. To those it means a flag. Those are to be pitied. Freedom is life, freedom is death, freedom is existence. It is that which I wish for. I wish to be free. People tend to think of things such as fame, power, and even wealth as the most important. What is power without freedom to use it as you see fit? What is Fame, while attached to the reins which society fastens upon the famous? What is wealth? Gold can buy many things... but it cannot buy love. Freedom is all, and yet it is nothing. Freedom is best used when it is given away. That is what I have done. I have forgotten about fame, fortune, power... and given all... even freedom away for what I believed in. All because I was free to do so. I cry. I remember a time when I could fight...when I could do as he pleased. All I can do is sit here... and dream. I have dreamt of many matches... and yet won few. What is this nightmare? What is this thing which others call reality? IT frightens me, it frightens me more than the prospect of death. Consequence for actions... If it was all more than a dream, I would win...but that which they call reality is nothing more than that. Freedom? An illusion. People like Tyler Valentine and The Masked Mullet think they know the outcome...think that they have control.... think that they can conquer fear... death... and just survive. Things which seem so obvious are almost never so. I remember. I once knew of a man... Chris. Now Chris wasn't real, but who truely is? As Chris slowly matured, he went from a quiet kid who always kept himself away from the jocks, to a guy who took a town of cowardly jocks, and made the jocks his bitch. I remember that he was once like Chris was, one who was frightened of all... and gave in to his emotions... and let it all happen. I think that some, like that Mullet fellow are still like that. They are afraid, yet never let it show. Someone really wise once said 'Behind every dark person there hides a frightened nerd'...someone who's initials had to do with the number 3.14159265......well anyways, Back to Chris.. He had a shotgun, and in the end of the trilogy of movies, he was very proficient with it. He had a quote...what was it?...oh yeah... 'Hail to the king, baby'. I know. I am Chris.
