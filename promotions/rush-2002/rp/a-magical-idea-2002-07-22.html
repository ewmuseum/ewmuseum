A Magical Idea?

22 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22/7/02 The sun shone through the small gap in the curtains, a rustling of bed covers can be heard as Andi begins to moan and grumble as the suns powerful rays almost blinded the poor magician. Andi sigh’s as he sits up in the bed that the helpful hospital staff had put on for him. As usual the RUSH Superstars first thoughts where immediately on Fluffy as he looked at her grey matted face, the sweat had been pouring off her for so many nights now that the doctors had to add to add fluid intravenously which had given Fluffy the look of a walking advertisement on the reason to get Health Insurance. The young magician slowly got out of bed before quickly diving under the covers when he realised that a nurse was just walking in to do her hourly check on Fluffy’s progress. Now Andi normally wouldn’t mind but as he had started to get out from underneath the covers he had realised instead of having his shorts on he was completely naked, although he was completely sure that he had fallen asleep on top of the bed fully clothed and his suspicions where raised even more where he saw his clothes folded neatly in the chair. “Excuse me Nurse, But how did I get into bed last night?” Andi said with a puzzled look on his face. The nurse who was dressed in her typical Nurses outfit and was in her late 50s just simply smiled and gave a little wink before leaving the room. The young magician continued to look puzzled, as he quickly got dressed. It was about 15 minutes after the old nurse had walked out before a younger nurse walked in. The nurse, who was in her mid to late twenties, had long brown hair that was tided into a ponytail. The nurse looked at Andi with her deep hazel eyes and pushed her chest out before she spoke to him in her deep American accent. “Hi, My name is Amy and I thought I would come in and explain my actions last night.” Andi’s mind raced as he struggled to comprehend what the young nurse has said because he was sure he wasn’t drunk last night tired yes but drunk he knew for sure he wasn’t, but the nurse continued. “What I mean is when I came in at two last night you where fast asleep so and I hope you don’t mind I decided to take your clothes of and make you a little more comfortable.” The young wrestler looked at Amy before nodding and thanking her for being so nice to him, but still he couldn’t help but feel slightly violated. The young nurse smiled and blushed before backing out and making her back to the nurse’s station just a little way down the corridor. Andi sat on the edge of the bed and continued to hold onto Fluffy’s hand. Slowly the hours drifted by and Andi drifted in and out of his thoughts continuously until he was pulled out of his reprieve when the phone at the side of his bed rang. Slowly the young magician reached over and picked up the phone. “Hello.” A voice familiar to Andi spoke which for the first time in weeks made the wrestler smile. “Hi Andi how’s it going?” “Good Scott thanks for the phone call man. How’s OSW treating you?” Andi heard a sigh escape from Scott before he replied. “Good but I didn’t ring you to talk wrestling but I was wondering how Fluffy was doing.” Andi felt a little tug at his heart as he looked over at Fluffy. “She’s still very ill man, but hopefully she will pull through this.” Andi heard an annoyed grunt escape from Scott’s lips before he spoke. “Andi is there nothing you can do with your magic to help her?” Before Andi could reply Scott spoke again. “Damn Andi I’m sorry man but I got to go I got a press interview to do, ring me man if there’s any news.” With that Scott and Andi said there goodbyes before Andi slowly thought on what Scott had said before a large smile crossed his lips as a somewhat magical idea appeared in the magician’s mind.
