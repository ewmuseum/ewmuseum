Date: Today (The scene fades into show the inside of a fitness center somewhere in suburban Milwaukee, Wisconsin. We see that we are located in the weight room section of the fitness center. The room has the faint of order of sweat in it. The camera pans around to show that it is a fairly large room, exercise bikes and treadmills are lined up on one side of the room, facing a large bay window that shows the Olympic sized swimming pool and hot tub area through it. We can see that the swimming pool is mostly filled with beautiful young women wearing skimpy bathing suits. Which probably explains why all the exercise bikes and treadmills are filled with middle aged men and teenage boys. All the men and boys stare out through the window with a zombie like stare at the voluptuous bodies of the women in the pool. Drool is even dribbling out of some of their mouths and down their chin. None other than the five time and PCW Tag Champions and all around bad asses, Rush superstar Brock Newbludd and his best friend, now retired wrestler Stephen Blood, better known as The Blood Brothers. A white wife beater tanktop clenches tightly to his wet perspired skin. His usually spiked up hair is matted down with sweat, and beads of it run down his face.
