Back (RP #1 For Adreniline)

14 July 2002

Date: 7-14-02 Starting from scratch is horrendous. Losing time is worse. “Gene,” G.O.D. looked up to his creator with slight intrigue, “We need to talk.” The great doctor sat on the edge corner of the cot The Genetic Oriented Drone had been resting on. The cot had been placed in what had become ‘G.O.D.’s Room’ so that he could use it as a couch, but since a couch wasn’t long enough for Gene to lay down on they used the cot instead. Gene had been laying down on the cot previously for almost thirty minutes just thinking. He was thinking about fireworks mainly. He had seen them on the fourth of July in extended passing. They were a sight to see indeed. Their suspenseful uprising. The smoke forming a lengthy tail. The explosion of lights and colors. The loud BOOM that followed it all in a thunderous ending. They truly had been stupendous. “I want to talk about your match this week.” The doctor said. G.O.D. asked: “What about?” “Well, it’s just that you haven’t wrestled in quite some time. Actually,” Dr. Jenkins ponders, “You haven’t wrestled since you LOST your Extreme title in NTICW.” He emphasizes. “But I have been practicing. I have continued my normal workout and sometimes surpassing it.” “Gene, the point is that you have not wrestled-“ “I have wrestled the trainees,” the clone interrupts. Dr. Jenkins sighs slightly. “You have not wrestled real competition. You haven’t faced anyone of true stature or worthiness. You have three men to overcome in the match. J. Rish, Ricky Starr, and Vulcan Flare. You do in fact have to beat all of them to win. Can you do this after such a lengthy break?” G.O.D. sits up. “Of course!” “I will not hold it against you if you do not. I know you will try. I know under normal circumstances you would crush them all, but this scenario is slightly off,” he says showing some rare compassion. “I will do my best.” G.O.D. says. “And my best is better than anyone else because they all will feel my wrath.” Dr. Jenkins chuckles under his breath. “G.O.D.’s wrath… hehehe… G.O.D.’s wrath.” He pats The Genetic Oriented Drone on the knee and stands up to leave. “Good luck Gene, you’ll be on top soon enough.” G.O.D. lays back down on the cot and reaches over. He grabs his book with the Belmont Street Public Library bookmark inside on page 189. The book is entitled The Red Dragon and was by Thomas Harris. Mysteries were now what Gene had been reading. Since the closure of New True Internet Championship Wrestling he had more time to read and began to read more novels than non-fiction books. He began to read the book slowly and picked up the pace as he continued. As Dr. Jenkins left he looked back at Gene Otto Droidman and grinned. It was possibly the sickest most egotistical grin he would make in his life. Of course his life had aged greatly with experience and wisdom… G.O.D.’s life had only begun…
