CrossRoads Chronicles #1: William 'BioHazard' Perry

9 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8.9.02 'It is good to be without vices, but it is not good to be without temptations. '- Walter Bagehot Temptations... seduce... persuade or tantalize. No matter how many synonyms I use to describe it, it will always come out the exact same way. Something that you want, that you need to have. Some people's temptations revolve around chocolate. Some people's temptations are women or men or whatever is 'Politically Correct'. Some people's temptations could be food, or booze, or drugs or even sleep. The possibilities are endless, because we are dealing with the world as a whole, not singling out anybody. Temptations, no matter how much you chose to fight them, will always fight to resurface, and in 90% of the time, you cannot overcome them, and in more then 50% of the cases, nobody wants to. Is that a good thing? Is that a bad thing? It is whatever you want it to be, because the temptation is something that only you have, control and chose to deal with. What am I getting at you may be asking? What could I possibly be trying to say? I am saying that everybody has something that they cannot do without, everybody. Whether they want to divulge what it is, or whether they hide it from the seeing eye and listening ear. Whether anything or nothing. Everybody has them. I have one, and it is to be the most recognized voice in the world, to be heard and loved more then anyone else. To be known not for what I look like, but for what I say, for my intelligence. But what do I matter now? I am here because I am finding my temptation, something that may not be tangible, but is there nonetheless. My temptation is not bad for me, physically or mentally, it will not hurt my health or brain. It is something that will only aid me in my life and confidence. My temptation is winning, and I will certainly do everything that I can to feed my need. I have had my wants quenched recently, I have had them fulfilled, beginning with an breathtaking record in the Best Wrestling Federation of them all, RUSH, the bricklayer of greatness. Continuing on with my unexpected victory, an upset in many people's eyes, but a truth in ours, over G.O.D, over Marvin Harris, over Rich Cannon, over Livewire. Continuous winning. Now before us, standing at the brink of perhaps the culmination of another Great One, we stare directly into the face of the career making, endurance testing and utilitarian skill. We stare into perhaps the largest battle royal ever seen in RUSH History. Seventeen men, battling for one World Title contendership. This is the real deal, all the way to the end of the line, until the final two and then the Final Winner. Winning is something now that it has never been, with the back-stabbing, and the backing of the Top Execs, or even the wrath of the RUSH President. It has become something more then a necessity now, as before it all came down to getting paid, as long as you got the money, you were happy, but in today's society, everybody wants the same thing, other then money. Power and Recognition. It is about being known, being a name, that people will know, it is about having the reputation. Being somebody. People know me, whether they believe I am a great Champion, or whether they think I am worthless. Whether they believe I am an incredible worker, or whether they think I am a lazy guy who gets pushed straight to where I want to go. You know who I am. Plain and Simple. But adding to that my temptations of winning, I will focus heavily on making an even grater name for myself, fulfilling my wants...and becoming a Living Legend. William 'BioHazard' Perry...I can honestly say that I have no idea who you are, where you came from or what you are capable of. I can also honestly tell you that I don't care who, what, where, why or how about you. Quite frankly, you are going to be just a stepping stone, just another road block that I have grown so accustomed to stepping over. Perry, I hope that you do not walk in thinking that because I have a reputation of being a winner, that you have no chance, because that is the thing I hate the most in wrestling today. People believing that they have been beaten before they truly have, that is when you get beaten. I believe Perry, I believe inside of my heart and head that I am the best wrestler that the world has ever produced. I believe that nobody in the entire world is equal to what I can do. I believe that whomever I step into the ring with, whoever is my opponent at the time, will lose, because I believe that I am unbeatable. Where has it taken me William Perry? It has taken me to possibly the most stellar record that any RUSH Federation member could ever boast about. Do I have a reputation? Yes, I have built it with years of hard work, excellence and intelligence. I do not let my reputation push me through the great abyss, I drag with me my reputation, slung over my shoulder because I am in control, I decide everything. Not my reputation. Now listening to the words that the fans have said, they seem to think you have no chance against me, no chance of advancing...I look to you and see only a small, disheveled rookie standing, peering through the window looking at all the superstars. Cleverness is something that you definitely aren't lacking. I guess I already hold an advantage over you BioHazard, for while you are being born into a large battle royal, I have been a resident for many times, even winning another in another federation packed with 20 participants. You have won matches though, and that must be re-assuring for you, that must put you at the top in your mind, knowing that you have as good a chance as any...right? Wrong. Nobody in this Battle Royal cares anything about what you have accomplished, what I have accomplished or what we might accomplish in the future. They care about themselves, about winning, about stepping out at the end, hand raised high, perhaps a single tear of joy rolling down your cheek. BioHazard, you held titles. Big Deal. I held titles. Big Deal. Everything begins now, no past to speak of, only the present to focus on. Unfortunately, the present is all you have, especially when you are going up against the Future. I guess it comes down to this... I Have to Win. (As the view fades in, you can see the bright light of the sun in mid-day. It lies high in the sky, not a single cloud in the vicinity. Your emotions calm, however, as you notice the two men who have stayed so loyal to our Hero, Lars DeVille and Leo Hassler. They flank your Network Champion on both sides. They stare at the giant infra-structure behind you, the RUSH Head Offices. They walk forward, moving you to the side as a dialect begins.) Lars DeVille: So, WHY exactly are we here? Scott Cage: It's simple, really. Lars DeVille: Oh? Care to explain it to us 'simpletons'? Scott Cage: Okay guys, no need to act so innocent. I know what's going on. Leo Hassler: What's going on? Tell me! Scott Cage: Shut up simpleton! Anyway, since you guys seem to be in denial, I'll say it. As we all know, I'm the RUSH Network Champion. Lars DeVille: Yes, and…? Scott Cage: Well, as you recall, Cassius Deamont got a party when he won the World Title. Lars DeVille: And Cassius Deamont IS the World Champion. What are you? (Scott's eyes go wide, and he goes berserk...) Scott Cage: WHAT? YOU QUESTION MY ACCOMPLISHMENTS? Lars DeVille: Boss, calm down, let's just go to the mall in town and barge in on the AA meeting. I'm sure we'll find someone to heckle. (Scott smiles and folds his arms in two…) Scott Cage: Stop fooling, let's go inside. I'm sure that I'll find everyone inside. (Scott lets go and walks in front. The bodyguards worry, with frightened looks on their faces.) Leo Hassler: What the hell do we do?! Lars DeVille: Please, he'll never find the right room. Leo Hassler: He better not. (Scott begins yelling from a distance…) Scott Cage: Are you guys coming or would you rather sit around in a circle jerk with Steve Carnage and Jonathan Drachen? Lars DeVille: No, we're coming. (Scott turns back around as the bodyguards run up to meet him as they walk inside. The view fades to show Scott and Lars running to the first door they see. Scott opens it and…Jonathan Drachen is sitting in a leather chair as a noise comes from his desk…) Scott Cage: WHAT THE HELL IS GOING ON IN HERE?! Jonathan Drachen: Uhhh...Uhhhh... Scott Cage: Is that...NO WAY! It's…STEVE CARNAGE! (A head peaks out from under the desk, taking something long and ugh...wet, out of his mouth. He looks at Scott, smiling.) Steve Carnage: HEY THERE, PAL! JOIN THE PARTY!Scott Cage: HOLY SHIT! CLOSE THE DOOR! CLOSE THE DOOR! (Leo Hassler slams the door shut as the noise resumes. Scott stands there for a moment, extremely shaken and generally freaked out.) Leo Hassler: You know, you would think that seeing that would deter you from opening another door. Scott Cage: As FRIGHTENING and SICKENING as that was...I WANT MY PARTY! Lars DeVille: Fine, but don't blame us if something goes wrong again. (Scott reaches for another door handle…) Scott Cage: Here we go... Lars DeVille: WAIT! Scott Cage What? Lars DeVille: I don't think you should go in there. Scott Cage: And why not?Lars DeVille: Well, let's just say, it's not a place for the three of us. Scott Cage: This is it, isn't it? You're just trying to keep me from my party! Well you can't do it. I'm going in! Lars DeVille: Don't say I didn't warn you. (Scott opens the door and walks in as the camera looks to the side. There is a sign…'WOMEN'S RESTROOM'. Suddenly, you hear screams and yells and Scott comes roaring out of there as purses, bottles, and even a few shoes come rocketing past his head. Scott slams the door shut as the bodyguards laugh hysterically…) Lars DeVille: I told you. Scott Cage: Shut the hell up. (Scott punches Lars in the arm as the trio ventures forward, looking for that one room, as we fade out…) TO BE CONTINUED...
