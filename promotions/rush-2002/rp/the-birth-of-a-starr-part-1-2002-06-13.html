The Birth of a Starr (part 1)

13 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13/6/02 To be human is to have the freedom to control one's fate--Karl Marx 'Economic and Philosophic' We all know what happened to Steffan We all know why a Slatkovic became a Starr and for what reasons Now we shall pose the next question on the agenda How? How did Remus become Ricky and Slatkovic become Starr? What happened during the time between his present life and his distant past? Ladies and gentleman Presenting to you all… The Birth of a Starr Joshua DuBois, or better known as Joshua “The Starr” Dubois, or simply Josh The Starr is a man in his mid-50’s, carrying with him a stern yet strangely charismatic countenance. His face although aged through the years, remains handsome for a man his age with soft blue eyes, perfect face and jaw structure and a smile worth more than six figures. None of them bore any signs of being cosmetically enhanced or altered, being as natural as natural can be. His head of hair was rich, the envy of many other men in his age group, although it no longer retains the color of days gone by, it still looked good on him nonetheless with a lush white-silver color, probably the gift granted with the effects of aging unto him. He also happens to be the rightful owner of a multi-million dollar fortune. An enterprising individual, Joshua DuBois was an opportunist since he learned how to talk. His conservative Christian mother who believed heavily in the merits of charity often mistook this aspect of him for the vice of greed and often took measures to discourage this side of her youngest son. However, the sayings of old still proved true until now “You can take the tiger out of the jungle but you can’t take the jungle out of the tiger”. Her efforts proved all too superficial as a young Joshua mastered the art of controlling his facial expression when confronted by his mother, an art that would prove useful to him in time to come. The same went to his father, an honest man who believed that the only way to make a decent living was the hard way, bit-by-bit, day in and day out with no shortcuts on the way. Josh knew better than to talk back during one of his lectures about family values and the virtues of being a hardworking man. What did Josh really learn from his father? One must say the right thing at the right time and the right place while not giving in to the urge to say the honest thing at the wrong time to the wrong person. Josh decided from the day he could think rationally that the only person who could figure him out was himself. Not even his parents and certainly not his elder sister, an All-American blonde who didn’t know any better, being blood of their blood and flesh of their flesh could fully comprehend who he is. While they were droning away at their insulated world, occasionally harping about the merits of being good middle-class Americans, Josh would be furiously thinking in his head about how best to do things in a quicker and more efficient way while getting the most out of them in the shortest time-span possible. All while keeping that fresh suburban All-American pearly white smile on his face. Surprisingly for a person who calculated so much, he was the object of disgust by all his math teachers. He just simply didn’t like they way they taught To him, knowledge did not just need to be dense, complex and perplexing at first glance, it needed to be efficient, practical, proven to be effective but most importantly… It needed to be useful Young Joshua just decided that what they were teaching him just didn’t meet that criterion. As a result of that, Joshua DuBois was almost consistently on the receiving end of punishment from his parents, which came in the form of grounding and the taking away of various “privileges”. This merely strengthened his resolved and re-enforced his belief that the last people who he could count on to take him anywhere better in life were his parents. It’s not like they were bad parents or anything. It’s not like they never tried hard for their son Josh. They didn’t even favor his All-American elder sibling ahead of him (although she was truly more to their standards and tastes). They just didn’t understand him and flowed with him well enough to grasp and even tolerate what he deep down believed in. The REAL American Dream. It was a fantastic yet absurd dream at the same time. It wasn’t the fact that the widely known story was that a person could walk in with rags and stand tall the next day with an expensive thee-piece suit and a full wallet. It wasn’t the fact that everything seemed so much bigger and better in American than anywhere else. It wasn’t even the fact that it had grown in a relative short space of time in history to be a major world power, if not THE world power. It was the simple blatant fact that everybody was given a chance A chance to put their measly selves on par with almost everybody to start with A chance to attain whatever is it their hearts and heads desire A chance to be the best they can be A chance to control their own destiny. A chance to be free Those were the very things Josh truly desired deep within the recesses of his mind and nobody understood them more than he did. His life then was a paradox, a contradiction of sorts. He resided in the very land that embodied those principals yet his life was being pruned and controlled by his overbearing parents; the very embodiment of what the American Dream is not Conservatism They wanted him to live up to THEIR expectations. It wasn’t that their expectations were too high for their boy or anything along those lines. In reality He was beyond their expectations To live a middle-class American life, a slave to the 9-to-5 routines just like his father, slugging it off bit by bit with the work and all for what? For just a minuscule percentage of company profit? Was there actually a moral or a plausible point to what his father had been harping about all this time? Was this the life his family envisioned from him? A sort of decent suburban home with a housewife to clean, cook, love in exchange for financial support and the possibility of a child as well? An honest job with long hours and a below average sum that passes for a paycheck? Joshua DuBois could never bear to imagine himself leading a life like that. In the end, at the very root of it all after countless of near-sleepless nights calculating as a 12-year old, he knew he had to do one thing from an early age… Defy his destiny “Did Josh ever tell you much about his childhood days?” A slim and attractive lady in a crisp suit sits across a café table from the self-proclaimed “Natural Born Talent” of RUSH, Ricky Starr. Her face seemed focus as she anticipates a response from the man across her as she presses a button on her dicto-phone. “Lets just say that in many ways he differed from me a lot in terms of his childhood lifestyle. He led a better life than I did, was given the many comforts I could only faintly imagine in my childhood, taken care of well by parents who loved him but whether he really loved them back, well that is another question…” Ricky Starr sips on his iced tea as he pauses for a short moment. The lady looks on at him, anticipating a finish to his statement “But at the very core of it all, when it came to hopes, wishes and dreams…” A reminiscent smile comes across Ricky’s face “I guess he was just like me”
