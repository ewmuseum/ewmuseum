On The Edge [5]

24 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 24th, 2002 A.D. “What if I said no?” Matthews responded, unfolding his arms. Kathryn let out a small, and soft little chuckle after Matthews’s bold question. “Then you’ll be sorry” Kathryn replied to his question, spitefully indeed. Matthews’s eyebrows raised after her spiteful comment, he walked around to her right hand side, but only for her to turn her back to him. “What ever do you mean?” he asked her, eyebrows raised still. Click...Click What’s this clicking sound you wonder? A trigger of a gun. Kathryn had a gun hidden in her pockets, it wasn’t a large gun, just your standard handgun. Matthews’s eyes grew wide as Kathryn turned around, holding the small black weapon. “Put it down...” is what he uttered to her, “....Kathryn, put the damn gun down!” A smirk spread across her face, an evil smirk as Matthews double backed against the wall. With a smirk spread widely across her face, Kathryn waved the gun in front of her face, then at Matthews. “Let me go!” she yelled at him, fire blazing in her eyes. “LET ME GO NOW!!!!!!” * * * * As we return to what we saw last time, Kathryn, the gun wielding girlfriend of Chris Matthews’s bitter rival, had loaded a gun and pointed it at Matthews. Matthews was now double backed into the corner of the balcony. “Kathryn” he said to her, trying to act as calm fully as possible, no such luck really. If you had a crazed, hostage kept, gun-wielding woman in front of you, wouldn’t you be a little scared? Yeah, you would. “Kathryn, please, put down the gun.” “NOT A CHANCE!!” she yelled loudly, people bellow the balcony would of heard it had it not been peek hour traffic time, people coming home from work and the like. Matthews’s eyes dart all over, looking for a way out...if there is one. He swallows some of his own saliva, it hits the bottom of his stomach with a thud. “Just think about what you’re doing!” Matthews pleaded, obviously not wanting to be shot by the gun. Kathryn still smirked with the gun in her hand. “Oh I thought about it for a while” she said, smirking. “You know, after keeping me locked up for days, never knowing if I’d get out? I thought looooooong and hard about this, Chrissy my boy. See, I don’t know when I’m gonna next see my boyfriend....” “That piece of shit....” Matthews quietly said, turning his head and looking a large tree. “....while you kept me up here, I had no idea what was happening to my boyfriend.” She said, a tear started to roll down her cheek. Kathryn began to shake, still with the gun in her delicate little hands. He stood just a few small, insignificant, inches away from the tearful damsel. Kathryn began to sob more, and more every couple of seconds. Tears splattered down on the floor…. splat….splat….splat….splat …Kathryn began to wobble on her delicate legs, and then she fell to the floor. She fell to the floor and began to weep. Small little puddles of tears began to form in front of her face, Matthews, still standing over her. He sighed a sigh of relief. “Oh god” he thought inside his worried mind. “I thought she was going to open fire on me, I thought I was going to die.” The gun that Kathryn had been holding was scattered on the floor, left dormant by the weeping women known as Kathryn. With her hands held over her face, Kathryn didn’t even see….or care that the man above her, Matthews kicked the gun away. “Come on” he peacefully said to her, holding her arm. “This is getting stupid, come on Kathryn… lets go inside.” She still sobbed… Each sob… It rang in his ear…. Her crying… Continued crying….until she spoke back at him. “I hate you” she coldly said at him. He smiled down at her, tightly gripping her arm. She still hung her head as he held onto her, trying to pull her up. “Hate is such a strong word” he said poetically down to her, breathing out of his nose. “but not nearly strong enough.” Kathryn scoffed at him, Matthews smirked while still holding tightly onto her arm, her head still hung low looking at the floor. “Kind of like how you hate Cassius, eh?” she asked, his smirked broadening. * * * * “Hate...Hate...Hate...Hate” he thought in his mind, sitting on the large leather chair. “I hate that piece of shit...” “I’m going to end him.' 'Going to destroy him.' 'By taking his title.' 'That’s what will kill him.”
