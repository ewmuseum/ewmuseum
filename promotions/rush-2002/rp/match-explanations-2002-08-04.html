Match Explanations

4 August 2002

Date: 8/4/02 'But Dr. Jenkins, why must I fight quickly?' Dr. Jenkins sighed. 'Gene, fatigue will grant you your losses if you do not. Do not try and be the wall that others cannot break. If you are to win either of these important matches you must, under all circumstances, be wary of your body.' Jenkins goes back to reading his book, The Ruler of Penguins: The King Penguin. G.O.D. lays sprawled across the couch, confused. 'So you're saying that I will become too tired to compete?' he interrupts the great doctor's reading. 'Yes... well... sort of. You see Gene, the way your first match works is this: the first person to pin another is the winner. Statistically this is the worse of matches.' He explains, 'Now if you try and destroy all of your opponents so as they do not conquer each other, thus conquering you, you will not be able to hit all consistently and powerfully. You will not be able to beat them all with quicker moves.' G.O.D. ponders this dumbly. Dr. Jenkins continues, 'So that would make it seem that the best tactic would be to divide and conquer, which is simple considering that they are already divided. And it you focus on one opponent and destroy him you will already have a pinned candidate.' G.O.D. springs up to a sitting position and raises his finger in the hair with a smile. 'So I will do that and win! Thank you doctor!' The great doctor shakes his head. 'No, no, no. The problem with this theory is that if someone distracts you and tries to take you down, you will have to deal with two adversaries while others have to deal with one or only half a man. This would give others the oppurtunity to pin someone, thus beating you.' 'I see.' 'Do you really?' 'Kind of,' Droidman admits. 'Okay, well, the thing is that the odds are against you in that match.' G.O.D. questions: 'So I shouldn't try?' The doctor's eyes light up with fear of loss. 'No, no, by all means try. Just don't overdue, save your energy for later on. Okay?' 'Okay,' G.O.D. replies. He lays back down, sprawling across the large couch that he does not fit upon. His feet dangling over an arm while his head rests on the upper corner so as to conserve room. After some time he queries the great doctor again, who has dove back down into his book of arctic creatures. 'Excuse Dr. Jenkins...' The doctor looks up curiously at his creation. 'What about the next match? The 'brawl for it all'.' 'Gene, just don't get pinned.' Then he begins reading once more. 'Oh.' G.O.D. thinks about that for a moment. 'Okay, I won't.'
