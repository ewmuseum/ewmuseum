Preparing for the post-PPV party.

3 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/03/02 (Our scene fades in to show the outside of the Savvis Center located in St. Louis, Missouri on a bright and sunny SUnday morning. We see that large arena’s parking lot is mostly barren of cars. But, we can see a few large semi’s with the PCW log on the side of them unloading equipment near the building. For, the Savvis Center will be holding one of the premier shows in the world today, PCW: March Madness. As we pan over the large building, we notice that big, heavy snowflakes start to slowly fall from to the sky onto the cold concrete. As we watch the PCW crew workers unload the equipment from the semi’s trailer, we hear a low rumble of what sounds like a muscle car’s souped up engine. The rumble rows louder as we watch the crew continuing to unload the equipment. The rumble that was once in the background, now is the only thing we can hear. We pan around to see a jet black 1969 Boss 429 Mustang with a license plate that reads “Blood-2” roar into the parking lot of RAC at high speeds. As we zoom in on the car as it careens across the parking lot, we can see it’s driver and passenger bobbing their heads in rhythm to the sounds of “Killing in the Name Of…” by punk band Rage Against the Machine. The music can be heard even over the large roar of the Mustang’s beast of an engine. As we zoom even closer to the car, we can see that the driver and passenger in the car are none other than five time PCW tag team champions of the world, “The Innovator” Brock Newbludd and the now retired “The New Legend” Stephen Blood. As the car speeds across the large parking lot, Brock suddenly slams on the brakes and the car does a powerslide the now slightly snow covered parking lot. The car slides nearly twenty feet before stopping perfectly in a open parking space. The classic car shuts off, and Brock and Stephen both step out of the car, shutting the doors behind them. We see that Brock is clad in a pair of baggy fitting blue jeans and black Doc Marten boots. A tight fitting plain black t-shirt can be seen his black leather Harley jacket. Brock's hair is spiked stylishly as always. Stephen is clad in a pair in a pair of baggy black denim pants and black jump boots. His also wears a black leather Harley jacket that is zipped up all the way. Both men walk to the back of the car and Brock starts opening the trunk…) Stephen: You did get the party supplies, right? Brock: Yah, two half barrels baby! This will be the sweetest party yet, man. And it will be extra sweet party after I make Mike Storm my pantyhose bitch. Stephen: No doubt dude… (Brock pops the trunk of the car, and we see two kegs of beer sitting in the trunk…) Brock: Grab a barrel bro… (Brock picks up one of the kegs and throws it up onto his shoulders, while Stephen garbs the other one and does the same. The two begin to trudge across the parking lot with the half barrels on their backs…) Brock: You got my back if Mikey tries to cheapshot me before the match? Stephen: Abso-f*ckin’-lutely! Brock: Good man, I have a good felling about this match, it’s like that feeling I had right before I beat Novak for the Extreme strap, and the best thing is I’m saving all my drinking for after the match… (Stephen grows a devilish grin…) Stephen: I know that feeling, I had it right before I beat you for the Extreme strap… Brock: Shaddup! (Brock and Stephen share a laugh as they continue to walk across the parking lot…) Stephen: You weren’t serious about not drinking before your match, were you? Brock: Dead serious my friend, you know I’m always on top of game sober… (Stephen shrugs his shoulders some what awkwardly with the keg on them…) Stephen: But dude, you know it’s much funner wrestling drunk off your ass… Brock: Yeah, but then I don’t remember the match in the morning! Stephen: True… (Brock and Stephen finally make it to the front doors of the arena and walk through them. As we follow them walk in, Brock and Stephen are greeted to PCW reporter and lackey Cal Seaver with his cameraman Jacques LeFiere. Cal sees Brock and Stephen walk in and smiles at them…) Cal: Hey guys! Brock: Shove it Seaver! Stephen: You suck, Cal! (Cal and Jacques walk in step with Brock and Stephen as they head towards Brock’s dressing room. Brock and Stephen trying ignoring Cal, but that’s like trying to have sex with Tiffany Jacobs and not getting a STD…) Cal: So…you mind I can get a interview? (Brock rolls his eyes…) Brock: If I give you a interview Cal, will you promise not to bother us the rest of the night? (Cal shrugs his shoulders in a agreeing fashion…) Cal: Sure, so does that mean I can get a interview? Stephen: Duh! Cal: Yippee! Jacques get the camera ready! (Jacques shoves the rest of the pastry he was eating into his mouth and slings his camera onto his shoulder. He adjust the zoom on it and gives Cal the thumbs up…) Cal: Great! You guys ready? (Brock sets his keg of beer on the ground and sits down on it. Stephen keeps walking towards his and Brock’s dressing room…) Stephen: I’m going go to our dressing room and tap this keg, have fun! (Brock waves Stephen on, and we pan back to show Cal adjusting his hair in the reflection of the camera lens, he then gives Jacques the thumbs up. LeFiere turns the camera on, and we go from live to dead air…) Cal: Hellooo PCW fans! Cal Seaver here and I’m standing inside of the Savvis Center, the site for one of PCW’s biggest PPV’s, March Madness. This PPV should be a hot card indeed with many exciting match ups. One of them being a ladder match for the number one contendership of the PCW TV title , in which the self proclaimed rolemodel “Amazing” Mike Storm will face off against the man I am standing with now, longtime PCW fan favorite, “The Innovator” Brock Newbludd. Brock, what are your final thoughts with the match looming closer and closer? (Brock lights up a cigarette and takes the mic from Cal hand…) Brock: Well Cal, you little turd, you. I think you’ve hit the nail right on the head with Mike Storm. You said he was the self-proclaimed role model. Cal, I’m impressed by your showing of a logical brain. Self proclaimed, is exactly what he is, because, nobody else thinks he’s a rolemodel, especially the fans. Most people think Mike Storm is what I think he is, a overzealous obsessive lunatic. He seems to obsess about ridding the world of all it’s wrong things. And he wants to start with me, the beer drinking, bar fighting sonuvabitch who could give a sh*t less about what other people think about him. The fact is Mike you’re as pathetic as a rolemodel as you are a wrestler. (Brock shrugs his shoulders in a innocent like fashion and hands the mic back to Cal…) Cal: Brock, have you heard Mike Storms latest on you? He has also said that you are jealous of him. (Brock lets out a laugh and strokes his chin goatee…) Brock: Cal, what would I have to be jealous of Mike Storm for? (Cal thinks a little and shrugs his shoudlers…) Brock: That’s right, absolutely nothing. Mike Storm has nothing that I want, nothing that I admire. (Brock takes a last pull of his cigarette and puts it out on the floor, he then looks into the camera with a confident look in his eyes…) Brock: Mike, you say that I’m jealous of your “greatness” in the cruiserweight division. On how you are so much better than me. Do I sense someone starting to get a little overconfident? Mike, I have no jealousy towards you, because the fact is I think you’re a pathetic puke that I would never want to be like You wear a mask for Christ sakes! Why would I be jealous of a mask wearing weirdo who looks like he should be on Super Friends? (Brock grins cockily at the camera and shakes his head…) Brock: Mike, just because you beat me on my first match coming back from a lacerated kidney which made me lose twenty pounds from being bed ridden for three weeks, don’t get your hopes up on beating me tonight. You may have won the first battle, but you have not won this war… (Brock hands the mic back to Cal, grins at the camera one more time and throws the keg of beer back on top of his shoulders. We fade to darkness as we watch Brock walks down the hall with a look of determination on his unshaven face…)
