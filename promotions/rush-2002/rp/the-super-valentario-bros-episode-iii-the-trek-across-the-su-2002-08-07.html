The Super Valentario Bros. - Episode III - The Trek Across the Sunderian Sea...

7 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 07, 2002 The last time we checked in with our heroic trio of…er…heroes, they had finally met with King Dante Crunkstool II and rented three fly jetskis, perfect for crossing the Sunderian Sea. Why would they cross this perilous, treacherous, and overall not very nice body of water? I’LL TELL YOU WHY, BUCKO! It’s ‘cause they must find the vile Scott Taylorio, who has stolen Marsh Mallowbludd’s Golden NOS Injector of Happiness. So here are our benevolent bunch of badasses, riding their personalized jetskis in the midst of the cerulean Sunderian Sea, underneath a scorching summer sun. Something about the heat seems a little TOO intense for the three – but thanks to the cool refreshing breeze of the sea, they don’t have much to complain about. Without further adieu… Tyler Valentario: This way, guys! The waves careen out from underneath Tyler’s custom cherry-red Kawasaki jetski, kicking up water on either side as Marsh Mallowbludd and Waldo Valentario follow him on either side. Waldo Valentario: (expletive), dawg, we been ridin’ for like…ten (expletive)ing minutes, ain’t we there yet? Marsh Mallowbludd: Relax, Waldo! We should be there soon. Tyler’s map says it’s only about ten more minutes away. As the impatient heroes ski along, they suddenly begin to notice the temperature getting exponentially hotter and hotter… Tyler: Ugh…man, I’m sweating like a pig here. What time is it? Waldo unbuttons the cuff of his lime green shirt, and checks his sparkling Rolex. Waldo: Well (expletive), bro…it’s high (expletive)ing noon. Suddenly, a magic paintbrush comes and paints white skulls on all three of their faces. All three: AHHHH! A LOOK OF FEAR~! Okay, bad joke. But seriously, these fellas are scared, and BOY HOWDY DO I MEAN SCARED. Tyler Valentario, the leader of the bunch, looks up into the sky, only to see a vile face on the hot midday sun, the heinous face of the evil… Tyler: KID SUNDER!!! Kid Sunder: Yes, it is I, you imbeciles. And what I see here is a complete violation of the trespassing rules of the Crunkstool Kingdom! You see, you are in the Sunderian Sea – it belongs to I, this Sea’s PREMIERE Sun, Kid Sunder! I’m in like Flynn, damnit! You can’t ride your filthy little jetskis on MY sea! I should report you to King Dante Crunkstool II immediately! Now THAT would be wonderful! I say… The three sit on their jetskis, waiting for Kid Sunder to finish his extremely long soliloquy. Kid Sunder: You see, I, being this Sea’s Premiere Sun, Kid Sunder, have the final say in who gets to travel on my Sea and who doesn’t! And I, the Quintessential Crunkstool Heat Source, say that YOU cannot travel on my sea! Know why? TOO BAD! I’LL TELL YOU WHY! You see… Waldo: (EXPLETIVE)IN’ A, DAWG, THIS AIN’T NO (EXPLETIVE)ING PCW PROMO! Y’ALL IN (EXPLETIVE)ING OSW NOW! Kid Sunder seems taken aback by this comment! How dare someone insult his promo! Kid Sunder: Oh…uh…right…angle fed, angle fed…ummm…GET THE HELL OFF OF MY SEA! HAVE AT YOU! Suddenly, much to the horror of our heroic harem (these are REALLY getting cheesy, aren’t they?), the VILE KID SUNDER ZOOMS OUT OF THE SKY, CAREENING TOWARD THE GROUP AT HIGH SPEEDS! All: Ahhh! Kid Sunder spins! He swirls! He does a corkscrew hurricanrana! Okay, maybe not. But he comes inches within our heroes’ heads, nearly searing the hats off Waldo and Tyler! Waldo: (EXPLETIVE)ING HE JUST TRIED TO (EXPLETIVE)ING BURN MY (EXPLETIVEx2) HAT OFF JESUS (EXPLETIVE) CHRIST! Kid Sunder: Hahaha! You’ll never leave my sea of despair! Wonder Piranhas, go get them! Suddenly, four gigantic, yet strangely handsome with blonde hair, piranhas splash out of the salty water, coming straight for… Director: HOLD UP!!! *SCREECH!* Director: Now wait a minute, Kid Wonder, you could sign up for ONE part in the promo, not two. You can’t be the sun AND the piranhas! Kid Wonder: Uh…shit. Tim Walden: DUDE! WHAT THE (EXPLETIVE) IS THIS (EXPLETIVE)!?! HOW COME HE CAN (EXPLETIVE)ING CURSE BUT ALL MY (EXPLETIVE)ING CURSES GET BEEPED THE (EXPLETIVE) OUT!? Director: Walden, stop your damn whining. He’s a four-time PCW Cruiserweight Champion! Besides, it adds flavor to your character, jackass. Tyler Valentine: Yeah, Walden. Calm the fuck down. Brock Newbludd takes his awkward, lemon-yellow marshmallow costume off in order to pipe in. Brock Newbludd: Yeah, Walden. Your acting fucking sucks! Tim Walden starts to seethe red in anger, nearly popping the buttons off his green plumber’s suit! Tim Walden: GOD (EXPLETIVE)! I WANT TO (EXPLETIVE)ING SWEAR WITHOUT IT BEING BEEPED THE (EXPLETIVE) OUT…aw, (expletive) this (expletive). Tyler Valentine: GUYS, GUYS, come on...we have a promo to shoot. Newbludd puts his marshmallow costume back on, and the three heroes finally take their places on the color-coded jetskis. Director: Okay, Kid Wonder, you CAN ONLY BE THE SUN…got it? TAKE TWO! Kid Sunder: Hahaha! You’ll never leave my sea of despair! Uh…um…DAMNIT, PLOTHOLE! Fortunately, our main hero is a very good improvisational actor! Tyler: WE’VE GOT HIM STUCK IN A PLOTHOLE OF DOOM! Mallowbludd: Just like Clyde? Tyler: YES! QUICK, MEN, ESCAPE! A large, black hole envelops Kid Sunder, trapping him in place as a slew of bookers fly through the air, stabbing him with pens and shoving contracts in his face. Our three heroes just barely escape the vile heat source just in time, and FINALLY reach land… …ONLY TO DISCOVER THEY ARE NOT AT TAYLORIO ISLAND! Tyler: Wait a minute…the map says we’re supposed to be at Taylorio Island. What could be wrong? At Taylorio Island they are not, indeed. An ominous storm cloud floats in, obscuring the normally bright and cheery sky of the Sunderian Sea in a shroud of dark grey. Mallowbludd: Hey, Tyler…why is that map folded over at the bottom? Tyler: I don’t know…oh my God. Look at this. Tyler unfolds the small flap on the bottom of the map, shielding the map from the impending rain as he reads a small note: “Heh, hope you found your way on my special map, brother…hell, I couldn’t with a…” *DRAMATIC DRUM ROLL* DUN DUN DUN… “REVERSED MAP! DYAAAAHAHAHAHA!! - Scott Taylorio” Tyler: Dang nabbit! We’ve been had! We’re now at… Tyler looks up at the giant gothic castle erected before him, now being poured upon by a thunderous rainstorm, as the realization of this predicament sets in. All: Dark Gimmick Island… WILL OUR HEROES FIND THEIR WAY OUT OF THE ISLAND AND RETRIEVE MARSH MALLOWBLUDD’S GOLDEN NOS INJECTOR OF HAPPINESS? Probably, but you should read on anyway! TO BE CONTINUED!
