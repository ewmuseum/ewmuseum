Date: .... “Get your facts first, and then you are free to distort them as much as you please.”--Mark Twain It's like a pull really. It isn't that I love the nature, although it helps. It isn't that I enjoy being outdoors, although it's true. It isn't that I need fresh to think, but it has never hurt. It isn't that I had something happen here in my life, because I have never come here with anyone other then myself. I can't even describe it really, all I know is that I am drawn, sort of magnetically, and I can't tell that feeling no, I can't not do what it says. It controls me, like a curse that you would miss if it left, like a sickness that you have grown used to, and when it clears up you wish only for it to come back. Like a jailbird his whole life only to be let out. He will still follow the rules he learned in the house. The fact is, that if I didn't have this pull, I would not be myself. This park has been the place where I have realized myself, where I have figured out what I am, and what I need to do in the ring, what I have to do and what I cannot do. It is an essence inside of me that I love...that I Love. El Spiffy, you seem to have assumed to many things for my liking, and soon enough for your liking as well. You assume that we are one and the same, you assume that you are on my level and that the struggles you face against yourself will be what our tussle shall be like. You and I may be a little alike on the outside, the way we seem to present ourselves, but inside, the way we think. The way I think, is nothing like anybody else. Look at these arms Michael, they are nowhere near the biggest, they are average to below average. Look at my legs Spiff, they might not be able to lift for then a high school kid. Look at my size, I have never been the biggest but I have beaten the biggest and the best. Why? Because of what is inside of my mind, because of how I think. You may try to pawn off to the fans around the world that you are like me, but you are not giving the fans the due they deserve, you are not giving them the respect that they have earned. Your charade is bland Spiff, it is transparent and you are doing a terrible job of trying to cover it up. You seem to keep saying that we are the same, but what evidence do you have to back up your accusation, what proof do you hold with it? If I say I have three legs it doesn't make it automatically true, does it? You got a few things right on why I wrestle, but you forgot the most important one. The people that you have tried to trick are the number one reason, even though some of them may not be that fond of my right now, they are still the reason that I keep going in this wrestling thing, the same reason why I started in the beginning. You are doing something now that many people have tried, and better people have failed at. You are trying to get inside of me which I will admit is a good tactic, but if Marvin Harris couldn't do it when he threatened my brother, then how do you expect to do it with empty thoughts? I am a stone Spiffster, I am solid and cannot be broken, and I will not lie to you, but your attempt is not even scratching me. Life isn't about giving 110% Spiffy, you are wrong. It is about going to that limit, that spot where you are steady at 110%, and then taking it a hell of a lot further. It is about being a stand out, not being a part of a crowd. It is about being true to yourself as well as the people who deserve the same treatment that you give yourself. It is about not only being rare and unique, but being special, being extraordinary. Spiffster, if you come into the ring with me and give 110% you will lose, plain and simple, because it isn't enough. There are people in this world that give there life for everything they do everyday, and that is what I base myself on, knowing that if I do not do what I think is right for me and the fans, then I do not want to go on living. I can risk anything and everything and that is what I do every single night. Spiffy, 110% is something that will get you destroyed in RUSH, and it is something that will show you how much better you need to be. Good luck I guess, I really have nothing against you and I don't think I ever will. You seem to know what you want, yet just don't know how to grab onto it and hold it close to you. I was at that spot once, and I felt lost, I felt a little empty, and now when I see how I feel now, now that I have everything, I can see how much of a shell I was before. You want to know how to get rid of that emptiness inside of you? Stop trying to 110% and find your untapped ability, let it out and don't think about anything other then winning. See, that's where we differ, you may not want to lose, but I cannot lose. I am not trying to sound like a Prophet or a Cardinal, I am trying to sound like an experienced wrestler who has learned every trick and how to use it to perfection...110% isn't perfection. We are special in ourselves, Spiffy, so take that as it is. (The camera fades back to Beverly Hills...this time with Sirius and Godfather Silvio sitting on the couch…) Sirius Cage: Where is he? Godfather Silvio: I'm not sure...he told me he was going to a park or something... Sirius Cage: Well that was hours ago...he should have been back by now, he still has yet to get ready for the event. Godfather Silvio: About that...how does he match up on Sunday? I don't want him getting injured… Sirius Cage: Scott shall fair fine... Godfather Silvio: Can he win? Sirius Cage: Win? Perhaps Godfather, it appears as if he does have a chance. Godfather Silvio: We'll just have to make sure he isn't inebriated before he gets to the match... (Godfather Silvio gives a comforting smile to Sirius...as the door bursts open...and Scott comes running in...) Godfather Silvio: Scott, where have you been? Scott Cage: You'll never guess where I've been! Godfather Silvio: I don't know Scott, how about you tell me? Scott Cage: I went to Brazil! Godfather Silvio: Sirius, check his breath for alcohol. (Sirius walks up to Scott, and he just pushes her aside...) Scott Cage: I haven't been drinking! Godfather Silvio: Scott, just how, out of curiosity, how did you get to Brazil? Scott Cage: Grandpa took me. Godfather Silvio: Bugsy took you? Scott Cage: Yeah, Bugsy. Godfather Silvio: Bugsy Cage, the New York Gangster, who's been dead for over forty years, took you to Brazil...Scott, sit down. (Scott hops over the couch, and puts his feet up on the coffee table...) Godfather Silvio: Sirius, can I see you for a second? (Sirius nods and the two walk into the back hallway...while Scott turns on the TV...) Godfather Silvio: Sirius, I believe the stress has finally gotten to him. Sirius Cage: Do you think so? Godfather Silvio: His talking about having conversations with your grandfather, and traveling to Brazil...they just don't come out of no where. Sirius Cage: Do you think he's having another one of his drug spells? You think that could be the problem? Godfather Silvio: I'm not sure, but if I were a doctor...I would think that all of Scott’s actions as of the past few months... Sirius Cage: Are you saying he's lost his mind? Godfather Silvio: I don't know...I just don't know... (While Sirius and Godfather Silvio continue their discussion on Scott…our camera fades gracefully…)
