From Hell, Spawns Evil...

1 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8.1.02 'Everyone has a price!'--Ted DiBiase Less then one day away from my match, from my time, from my era as the retaining Network Champion. G.O.D. and Rich Cannon, you are both now going to be part of a dying breed, part of a reckless wave which has no business being in RUSH today. In a federation filled with talented wrestlers who believe in what they are doing. Although I may not get along with all the wrestlers I believe in, that does not mean that I do not respect what they are doing and why they are doing it. G.O.D., you are still trying to live in the world where the gimmick is what makes the man, not in the present time where the man is what makes the wrestler. You are wishing with all of your might that the fact that you are an new name, a famous name, that since you have worked so long at being your gimmick, being your dream, that now when RUSH has moved into the future already, and wrestlers are making the most of what they can do, you can't keep up. G.O.D., I am fitting to end your gimmick this coming Thursday, I am planning on retain my title and ending your career all in the same night. G.O.D., normally this would be the time, and I wish that it wouldn't be, where I would say It is nothing against you, but unfortunately the times, and your callow acting as led me to have to make this personal. To hold this match more then close to my heart. Did you think that with everything you have done as well as attempted to do, that I would just lay down and let you walk all over me? G.O.D., It may sound a bit ridiculous listening to me say this, and if you chose not to believe what I am saying, then I urge you to try. I have taken everything you have given me with a grain of salt. I have not whined, I have not bitched, and I have not cried, and the reason that I didn't do any of those things that more then three quarters of the roster would have done, because I am glad you did it. I am glad that you gave me everything I could handle, because now, taking everything that you have, taking everything that you can find, taking everyone you could find and everything that you could dream of stopping me with...I took at all and look at me standing here before you. Having no worries about you, no quams about my match, no uneasiness because I am still standing. You cannot keep me down, not before, not now, not ever, and that is my key to keeping my belt around my waist. Over the last few months, I have watched every single promo you have done, every single interview your unknowing little trainer has completed, every little word you have let escape from your lips. I have seen everything you have done and payed attention to everything you have thought. I know you G.O.D, which is sometihng you cannot say the same about in the same content towards myself. I have heard each time you attempt to pawn off some bogus victory over me which you never accomplished. So then why, in front of the world, do you keep attempting to make yourself equal to an Champion who wishes only to have his voice heard. Why do you not attempt to be what made you what you are, your intellegent speaking and near laughable wit. Perhaps a more factual and important question I should be asking you is: Why are you being forced off of your normal strategy, off your normal approach to a match, off your normal tactics? The answer, which all my fans are begging to hear, is because I am not the normal man you are used to fighting. I do not make mistakes, I do not set myself up, I do not play into you like all of the tedious and tiresome wrestlers you have run over in the past. What has the one thing I have praised about myself since I arrived, what was the one thing that I have focused on making sure everybody knew. What was the one thing that I wanted everyone to realize before facing me...that I am the smartest, most intelligent, never equalled by anyone else in that regard, and I am always using my head more then my muscles. Quite simply, I have been playing your fiddle, I have been your puppeteer and you have done everything I have manipulated you to do. G.O.D., I am aware that you are not like the rif-raf that float through here week-in and week-out, and I know that you are one that will occasionally use his head. Meaning that at Adrenaline I expect you to have something up that sleeve, but let it not be thought that I do not. Because I will play you again G.O.D., but this time the tune will be for you. Rich Cannon, in all my days of following after you, trying to catch up and get my once chance to destroy a rookie in the ring, I used to think you were something original. Something that although I may think is very ridiculous and contrived, it is still original. The words that I would like you to focus on Rich Cannon is 'Used To'. The day you would pull a ridiculous stunt, a ludicrous attempt which has been known to be a winner in the minds of the simpleton wrestlers who always seem to wonder why they can't catch a break. You fell into that world Rich, and the day that you get your shoulders pinned by me for the last time, do not wonder how it happened, because it happen thanks to yourself. It happened because you are are far to ignorant to even wrestle anymore. You are a disgrace to the rest of the men in RUSH. Cannon, I am not lying when I tell you this, and I have honestly never said something more truthful in my entire life, but Cannon, I look forward to beating you again. I look forward to hurting you. I look farward to ending you, and I most certainly look forward to taking that little shred of dignity away from you. People say that sunsets are the most beautiful thing that you will ever see. To those people I say only this, watch Adrenaline this Thursday and you will see a what beauty is all about. I always thought that the nursery rhyme or lullaby 'Rock-a-bye-baby' was never as sweet as it was supposed to sound. You listen to the lyrics and all throughout you hear that a baby is falling from the top of the tree, and we are supposed to sing this little tune to our children. To help them sleep at night. To help US sleep at night. I am certain you are all wondering what I am talking about, and it is simple. Do not always think that by making sometihng sound nice it will be. Cannon, I am putting this out for you as politely, intelligently and as courteously as possible, but do not think that when we step into the ring together I will not be an animal just waiting to be unleashed, I will not be waiting in any sense. I will be an animal ravaging and devouring every ounce of your being. I want you to focus in on one word Cannon, while you are trying to get back up to your feet, while you are trying to take my title and put it around your waist...Smile. It will make you feel much better. Or maybe not. (Our camera fades into the hotel restaurant...where Scott Cage is sitting at the bar. He reaches for his glass, when Leo Hassler comes running in...) Leo Hassler: BOSS! BOSS! COME HERE! Scott Cage: What is it Leo? Leo Hassler: They're having a wet t-shirt contest!!! Scott Cage: Just keep on moving Leo... Leo Hassler: But boss…I thought you liked-Scott Cage: No, no. I'm feeling a bit under the weather today, Leo… Leo Hassler: Oh. Sorry, boss. Scott Cage: It's okay Leo...run along... (Leo Hassler starts to slowly back away from Scott...and turns and leaves altogether. Suddenly, a man in a trenchcoat and fedora walks in, his face fully hidden in the shadows…) Trenchcoat Man: They don't suspect a thing. Scott Cage: You know, I'm not to fond of this setup... Trenchcoat Man: We agreed, when I felt something important needed to be said...I would say it. Scott Cage: I know, I know. But man...I haven't even been able to go out with the guys. And I really wanted to go see Atlantic City. Trenchcoat Man: Well Scott, sometimes things have to be sacrificed to gain something else. Scott Cage: You mean like Rich Cannon sacrificing his intelligence, confidence, and respect for my title?Trenchcoat Man: In a way...but, you aren't sacrificing anything important, just some site seeing. Scott Cage: I guess I can sacrifice that...I mean, it's just New Jersey. And, I've watched some awesome shows this week... Trenchcoat Man: It's comforting to realize that we're acting as a team... Scott Cage: I know...it feels weird. Trenchcoat Man: That's alright Scott, it may feel odd...but you'll be fine. Come next week, I'll hide myself from your sight, and return to Miami Scott Cage: I knew that. Trenchcoat Man: Than you would also know that people are believing you're intelligent. Scott Cage: I know, isn't it great? We've got them duped. Sirius Cage: Scott! (We see Sirius Cage walking briskly towards Scott, as the Trenchcoat Man swiftly leaves the scene without a trace…) Scott Cage: Uh...hey bro… Sirius Cage: Scott, where have you been? Scott Cage: Ah...I um...moved. I have to be going. Sirius Cage: Going? You just got here. Scott Cage: But Sirius...I really have to be going. Sirius Cage: And where is it that you could be going...that is of such importance? Scott Cage: They have an exhibit on...on…Bugsy Siegal. I just wanted to see if I was going to be in it. Sirius Cage: You? Scott Cage: Uh...yea. You know, with my…title and all. Sirius Cage: Well Scott, I at one point would have thought otherwise...but I know you. So go...go observe the exhibit. Scott Cage: Ah...thanks, Bro.Sirius Cage: And if you happen to be in it...come get me. Scott Cage: Will do... you're the best. (Scott slaps Sirius on the back and walks out the door. The Trenchcoat Man appears out of the shadows, walking next to Scott…) Trenchcoat Man: That was a close one Scott. Scott Cage: You're telling me. Trenchcoat Man: The exhibits...quick thinking. Scott Cage: Yeah, I thought that would be able to keep him thinking... Trenchcoat Man: It probably did the job...it was effective. And people say you're useless... Scott Cage: Yeah, I get that a lot. Trenchcoat Man: And then people push you aside. Scott Cage: It's like you know me...oh wait... Trenchcoat Man: Yes, well...it is indeed time to push you aside for our personal, collective gain... (Scott displays a wide grin as he shakes hands with the mystery man, and the camera fades to black...)
