Smoke and Mirrors...

30 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7.30.02 'One death is a tragedy, but a million deaths is just a statistic.' --Joseph Stalin Even though this man was hated, even though this man was evil, even though this might have been one of the most awful man to ever step foot on this earth, that doesn't mean that he never said anything intelligent. The words that Joseph Stallin said, that sentence, makes more sense then possibly any other of it's kind, and while it is said with hatred filled in his voice, it is said with the truth backing it completely. You might be wondering why I used such an obscure quote to begin this interview, and I will tell you why... The day that Scott Cage dies, it will be a day filled with tears, a day filled with sorrow, a day that will be filled with mourning fans, mourning fans all around the world. However, after a few more attempts by the other Network Title Contenders, it will be proven that many deaths will just be a statistic. With every attempt that Rich Cannon and G.O.D. sends toward me, I lose it just a little bit more, and as we get closer to the edge, as I tip-toe lightly along that edge, as I look over that edge at what is right infront of me, it is nothing more then homicidal instincts and a wake of bodies as I continue moving forward, and nowhere else. So while I try to hold off those instincts, as he tries to supress the urges inside of him, as he tries to quell those impulses... then many you won't be only looking at one large statistic. First of all, is it so much of a problem, so much of a headache to have a respectible Network Champion? A man whom the fans can not only cheer for, not only enjoy watching, but can relate to? Is it such a problem to have someone succesfully dethrone myself, who has not only not been in RUSH for a very long time? It sickens me to be even remotely involved in a situation like this, a situation where you are only trying to stop the progress of a young stud, because you are held up and are being controlled by, quite honestly, utter childishness and stupidity. It is obvious to even you that more money will roll in with me at the helm, leading RUSH higher then it has ever been. It will be more respected then it has ever been. There will be no possibility of some other lowly fed like NTICW trying to moan that we suck. There will be no possibility of loss of ratings, like you receive now thanks in part to the ridiculously boring interviews spewed out by Rich Cannon and G.O.D. Even you, Boda, you know that, even you can see that, and even you want that... yet you keep sending jobbers to me…why? Since you have no fucking answer, nothing to say in response to my rantings of the truth, I lay in front of you a challenge, no, a Dare. I dare you to let me become your Champion, I dare you to let me lead this company, and I dare you to not enjoy reaping the benefits from it all. I dare you to finally realize what you are stopping, and then I dare you to stop it. Understand? Life is a large dare, one that you are hesitant to accept not knowing what the outcome of it may be, not knowing if you will possibly get yourself into sometihng that you can't handle, or whether you will accomplish it without a flaw. You always have to take chances, and you always have to live on the edge. So do it. Live on that edge, live something that ins't a piece of cake, that isn't a fucking walk in the park. Do something drastic...live a little. Now I look in front of me, and I see two men once again. Two accomplished wrestlers inside the ring. G.O.D., do you think that I do not know who you are? Do you not think that I know you will do anything your boss, Boda, says in an attempt to finish me? Now he puts you in front of me again, in perhaps one final chance to stop me, to slow me down, to liberate Cassius Deamont and Statuz Quo and their Titles from the only threat in RUSH…Me. A lesson to you now, and I wish that you listen in great detail because now I will begin. It is blindingly obvious that while your past is colorful and filled, it is all against mediocre men and ordinary fighters. It is all against common wrestlers with sub-par talents, and people who got handed the title as a way to quiet the whining. I am none of those G.O.D., I am someone who deserves everything that I get, because I earn it with my blood. I am someone who fights for every ounce of blood that I lost, for every ounce of heart that I have and for every little fan out there that is marking out for me. I deserve the Syndicate Title, and that is the reason that I will have it. Understand? Not to mention Rich Cannon, you skip into RUSH from NTICW, thinking that you have got better things in front of you, better life ahead of you, more prestige preceding you. Yet you find yourself here again after a failing once more in your life. Did you think that by coming here, your failures would be complete? That you could propser here in RUSH? Times have changed, Rich, no longer are the days of you being anything of a name in RUSH, as now we have a flurry of talent infiltrating RUSH, now we do not need your lowly self slacking along at the top of the ladder. We have fought before, and I came out on top, and lightning will most definately strike twiceDo you think that I am fearful of what you can do? If that is what you truly believe, then you are mistaken. If you cannot step past an opponent like Ricky Starr, then how do you expect to defeat someone of my calibur, someone who has more talent, more skill and more ability then you can dream of? Cannon, you disgust me. I see what you do and wonder how you can live with yourself… I am not all about talk, like everyone else. I am not all about lies, like everyone else. I am not all about commoness and being the Standard. I am about Individuality, I am about distinctiveness, I am about standing out, and that Is exactly what I do. I look out over the rest of the people in RUSH, and although they all try, although they never give up, They, much like you Cannon and G.O.D., aren't in my league. Now, I have realized over the past few weeks, that although I usually try not to be too cocky or arrogant, I have been doing just that, and while I usually dislike being this way, I have realized that it is the only plausible thing I can do. While I am a tad cocky, and while I am a shade arrogant, I hope that you all realize that I am not over-confident, not in the slightest. I look forward at my opponents, I analyze them, I study them and I strategize against them, and although that may not be what you are used to doing, I have one question for you. Which one of us is better? Exactly. (Our camera fades into the New Jersey airport, where Scott and Sirius are deboarding…) Scott Cage: I want to gamble my title in Atlantic City. Sirius Cage: Huh? Scott Cage: Yeah, I’m sick of this title. I’m going to gamble my title in Atlantic City. Sirius Cage: First things are first Scott, you're entering into a big night coming up. Scott Cage: Really? Sirius Cage: Adrenaline, Scott… Scott Cage: Oh yeah...the pests are going to be on my ass. Sirius Cage: By pests, you mean Cannon and G.O.D.? Scott Cage: Pffftt…I don't need a title to tell people I'm a champion... (Scott tries to give a high five to an Indian Taxi Driver...but he just turns away, as Sirius sighs…) Sirius Cage: Come Scott, before you make a fool out of yourself.. Scott Cage: Make a fool out of myself? These are my people! (Scott raises his title in the air as Sirius Cage walks on out, passing the security...and Scott gives chase…) Scott Cage: Hey! Wait up, bro!Sirius Cage: Scott, I’m sick of your fucking games… Scott Cage: What the hell? You sound just like my old girlfriend! Sirius Cage: Scott? Scott Cage: Yes? Sirius Cage: Do not ever...EVER...say that to me again. (Sirius cringes...as a wide grin crosses over Scott…) Scott Cage: Come on Sirius...let's get out of this airport... Sirius Cage: I would like that, Jersey has many casinos I'd like to visit, we can start today. Scott Cage: Uh...sure...how many of these casinos can we see from the hotel room? Sirius Cage: What are you saying? Scott Cage: Well...I really don't want to see any casinos...unless they'll have those Golden Toilets. Sirius Cage: You're telling me, you would rather sit in a hotel room, drinking the alcohol in those tiny bottles, and not see Hawaii in it's fullest? Scott Cage: Well…yeah. (Scott motions towards his bag…as Sirius sighs…) Sirius Cage: Fine Scott, fine. Let's go. Scott Cage: Are you upset? Sirius Cage: Upset? No...disappointed. Scott Cage: Well ... as long as you're not upset. (Sirius begins to mumble under his breath as Scott plays around with his Network Title…) 30 Minutes Later… (The camera fades and shifts to the room floor, where Sirius is opening up the door to his suite. He throws his bags on the floor, and heads towards the mini-bar, grabbing a small vodka, and sits down on the couch…) Sirius Cage: Thursday is approaching...and here I am, alone and drinking. The perfect of the perfect situations, as my heart falls content to speak my mind, upon deaf ears. But whom to preach to myself first? Scott is almost a mirrored image of myself, only lacking in the actual intelligence. But, he's since moved on, breaking his ties...severing connections. He's moved in on his own, and has taken part to focus his attentions on the 'common' enemy of RUSH, Boda. Tranquillity, versus that of a brutal, bloodletting match. Two against one…could the odds be in Scott’s favor? Rich Cannon, has never been able to defeat him one on one, on numerous occasions. G.O.D., the rebel from NTICW, has yet to be graced. Could Scott still be the champion, if my predictions hold? Or am I just giving bullshit to pass the time away? (Sirius stands up, and walks towards the balcony...) Sirius Cage:: Today is Tuesday. Thursday is Adrenaline. That gives me plenty of time to stow away from the entourage, and let my mind wander onto the open palette that is my tongue. Yet right now, the world is mine to take in, as this beautiful scenery has yet to be touched by the unjust that is about to occur... (Sirius sits down on a chair on the landing...as the camera fades to black...)
