The Seven Saga: Antioch

30 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/30/02 A thick fog covered the plant that evening. Maybe it was Mother Nature adding a bit of foreshadowing. After all, there was going to be a battle there tonight. For the sake of humanity, there would be a battle. This particular battle was taking place in the strangest of places, too – meat storage/shipping plant. It stood at a bitter 20 degrees. Caleb stood outside, against a brick wall, the fuzz collar of his leather trench coat wrapped tightly around his neck. A hooded cloak was draped over his body, the hood covering just above his eyes. Hiding in the shadows, he waited for the door to swing open. You needed a key card and a pin number to get inside the building. The doors swung open – someone was headed home. McGlocklan peeled himself off the wall and ducked inside, the sword on his back gleaming like a star in the sky. A sudden blast of cold air hit him like a wave in the pacific. He staggered for a moment, taking in the cold, and leaned up against the wall – security cameras were everywhere. McGlocklan: Destromethus… clever. Destromethus was a rather large demon, bluish in color, who thoroughly enjoyed the cold weather – the colder, the better, actually. Which is strange, of course. Most demons love the heat, which is why they stay in hell, naturally. Destromethus was a rare exception. He couldn’t stand hell. He took long trips away from there – mainly to unpopulated polar caps. This was genius by Lucifer… hiding the sword in a plant that could freeze a normal man in moments. McGlocklan spat out, his phlegm crackling before it hit the ground. He swore aloud… it was quite cold, indeed. Reaching into his cloak, he pulled out a hip flask, and took a deep swig. Energy swirled through his hands and gave him the breath of life. This place was enchanted, indeed. Enchanted only to those who opposed the demon, however. Clever witchcraft. Caleb slipped the flask back in its rightful patch and gazed at the ceiling. Several surveillance cameras were rotating back and forth. Fortunately he was in a blind spot. Now. The cameras pointed away and he rolled away from the wall and into the middle of the hall, flat on his face, he scurried forward, stopping occasionally for a passing guard. Rolling forward, he climbed to his feet and flat out ran through the hall and through a pair of closing sliding doors. He found himself in the freezer. The walls were thick with frost and ice and dew. Caleb frowned and touched the wall. They were melting… Destromethus knew of his arrival and was becoming… angry. McGlocklan frowned and placed a hand on the hilt of his katana. He slowly advanced, taking two, maybe three steps every minute. Destromethus was completely temperamental. If you looked at him the wrong way, he’d kill you. A fog came from Caleb’s nostrils as he took each breath… it was that chilly. He was deeper into the freezer now. Huge slabs of meat hung from rustic hooks and dribbled blood. This was heaven for a demon, all right. All the raw meat you could handle, a cool environment, and a huge ass sword to protect yourself with. Sinister Voice: Why do you come here, angel? McGlocklan stopped his advance. He had forgotten Destromethus’s best trait – heat vision. He was as blind as a bat otherwise. It was a shame Caleb had quaffed that potion before. Caleb drew out his sword and held it forth. It shone with the blood of Prometheus. Destromethus swore aloud and scurried across the floor. Caleb didn’t know where the demon was… it was fairly dark in the freezer, pretty wide, and there were rows upon rows of flesh dangling off spikes. However, he had an impeccable sense of sound and rolled forward once he heard something wipe towards his ear. Another Destromethus trait – skilled in carving. Thus, a carver. He carried a pick with him most of the time. Narrowly avoiding the poker, Caleb rolled to his feet and turned around, thrusting forward with his sword – meeting nothing but air. McGlocklan: Filth! Where have you hidden the sword? I might go easy on you if you hand it over. Destromethus: Behind you… ‘Fuck!’ Caleb turned his head and caught a pick across the cheek. Swearing loudly, he stumbled to his feet and swung his sword over his head, rotating his wrist. The demon stepped out behind a rather large hunk of meat, his tongue moving back and cross the tip of his ice pick. Caleb roared, spit flying from his mouth and crackling. The demon snickered, brandishing a second pick and motioning his arms like a wild boxer. That was the second time in as many days Caleb’s cheek had been cut open. Destromethus cackled and charged forward, swinging his arms in a rotary fashion, slinging the sharp edged of the picks forward with each step. Caleb sneered, and readied his blade horizontally. The demon screeched and leapt, a pair of black wings tearing through his back. Soaring at Caleb, he shouted in a distant language. Caleb narrowed his eyes, dropped his sword, and backhanded the over-grown imp clean in the jaw. The imp swore as it traveled in a completely different direction, losing all control. Destromethus: FUUUCCCCKKKKEEERRR! McGlocklan grinned and kicked up his sword. Catching it with his right hand, he walked toward the direction where the imp was standing. It suddenly charged forward again, screaming at the top of its lungs. Persistent little bugger. Caleb sidestepped it, and watched it run by. Unwise. A decoy. This particular imp had the power to “record” an image of itself and send it forth. A blade across his neck made him well aware. ‘Shit!’ Avoiding under the second skull piercing poker, Caleb kicked a long casing of ribs, twirling it around. Spotting the imp dangling from the heavy ribs, Caleb readied his sword, and slashes the chain the hook was lying on clean in half. The ribs fell atop the demon, crushing him under the weight. McGlocklan walked over and stared Destromethus in the eyes, raised his sword, and buried the blade though his head. An explosion of dust flew in Caleb’s face. Rubbing a sleeve across his eyes, he spat at the ground – unfrozen this time. McGlocklan: Filth… One sword bound the shape shifters to hell. Another bound the carvers. Caleb found the sword – Antioch – wedged in a block of wood. Wandering over towards the sword, he gripped the hilt, and drove it out of the ice. Lifting it up, it shone greatly above his head. And with a flash of light, it was gone. Two swords retrieved… five more remained. With a swirl of his cloak, Caleb was gone… and once again, another piece of Lucifer’s masterful plan was destroyed. End.
