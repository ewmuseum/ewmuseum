Poems from the shadows

27 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27/10/02 I serve no kings and I bow down to no queensNevertheless I'll blind your eyes and steal your dreamsHave no need to play your gameNot interested in your worthless fameThis life is mineBe it not fair or fine You try to intimidate me with your vengeful desiresYet I rest easy in the knowledge that their uttered from liarsTry to entice me into your snareSoon you'll be rotting, decayed and bare You think I'm misguided, well maybe your rightYet try to act out your judgements on me and you'll be encompassed from lightYou have met nothing like me, so don't even contemplate or thinkNevertheless you'd be surprised how many strands of my mind tend to link I'm that darkness, that shadow, that myth and that shadeLet me enter your mind, and you'll see how one day everything will fadeI'm that being you pray won't enter your lifeAll the while knowing I'll bring chaos, suffering, turmoil and strife I've met and defied Lucifer and emerged from his hellOn my way from there I stumbled, crawled, bled and fellNow here I stand, a man, a rebel, a shadow and the symbol of your every fearNow here you stand, corrupt, broken and afraid to shed a tear I am the last true warrior of my twisted breedThe ones who see that this world is nothing more than a bitter sadistic seedNow I walk the earth with only a few twisted intentionsYet still I will not subsist with your modern day inventions I neglected my past and have no futureNow I follow no master and have become my own tutorMy past personal grief has become my life long lessonThe invisible teacher went by the name of depression None of you will know how I truly feelYet soon you will all wither blister burn and peelThen when this world has entered it's final fallYou will all realise.............. Victims...... aren't we all. * * * * * He read through the poem several times, absorbing every last word and emphasising every pronunciation as he whispered it while studying it under candlelight. The elderly evil Jeremiah O'Barr didn't bear his usual sly menacing devilish grin as he read through it, instead his eyes pulsated with what appeared to be anger and fury. 'Like what you see old man?' inquired a mature dark voice from the darkness. Jeremiah turned round sharply, staring into the shadows that clung to the walls of the abandoned shack that he and Gideon lately called home. 'Very interesting, have a knack for poetry I see young Gideon, following in the footsteps of your surname eh?' croaked the dark puppet master, his usual menacing grin had returned. 'I wouldn't go that far' came the voice from the shadows again, Jeremiah almost wondered whether it was Gideon speaking or the shadows themselves. Ten again what difference did it make, that was what Gideon was, The Shade, a constant looming dark shadow over the earth. 'I didn't know you wrote poetry?' 'I didn't know you read it' The anger ridden hate filled responses between this dark duo were frequent and often, with Gideon's comments bellowing with darkness and decay, with Jeremiah's oozing with menace and malevolence. 'So... you serve no master and have become your own tutor?' The eerie silence that followed was enough to answer his question. 'Well?!' '............ That's right.' 'Care to explain that theory to me?!' 'Certainly. Your an old worthless withering sick twisted old man with nothing better to do than poison me and take me unwillingly as your companion. Your not a master, and your not my master.....' Gideon Daray Poe then came emerging from the abyss full shadows, to tower of his unwanted sensei Jeremiah O'Barr. 'I serve not you or no man. But if it makes you sleep easier at night thinking that I do, then carry on , I deprive you of that ignorant pitiful privilege. But just remember...' The Shade then dropped to one knee so he was level with Jeremiah's petit frame. 'One day in the not so distant future... I will rip that black shrivelled heart of yours up and feed it to you.' Jeremiah's menacing grin did not wince or fade. 'Intimidating words my dear boy, very, but preaching and acting out those preaching's are two different things' Gideon merely grinned, when he rarely did it was one of the only times his handsome face was free of all the mystery and depression it inhabited. 'We'll see old man, we'll see' With that, The Avatar Of Death rose to his feet. 'Come Roland' he whispered into the darkness. Then out of the shadows came soaring the beautiful eagle, who then calmly perched itself on his shoulder. The scene then fades with the Gideon and Roland leaving the elderly evil Jeremiah O'Barr with his usual sick grin on his face.
