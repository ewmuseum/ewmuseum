Vulgarity and Violence

15 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15th August 2002 The sky was a watery wash of royal blues dripping down with the sun. The white hot rays beating down on the unsuspecting people below, melting ice creams in the hands of those desperate to cool down. The bustling streets were filled with overheated life, all going about their day trying to ignore their thirst for the chilled atmosphere they could find somewhere near Alaska. With their hair pasted to the top of their skulls and their lips dry and crackling, the people were not happy and sought out methods of relieving themselves rather than simply panting away and wiping the sweat from their brow. Every jingly jangly music that sounded in the distance drew a mob with money in their fists, frantically scrambling for the chance that their tongues could meet with the frozen desserts sold in such vans. Amidst this stifled and uncontrollable crowd was Joe Bananas, garbed in a sweat drench Bob Marley t-shirt and blue shorts that left little the imagination. Clasped in his hand, a small strawberry to keep him company. Joe, never too fond of the company provided by humans such as his stable mates El Spiffy! and Livewire, preferred the company of such fruits as Samuel Strawberry. They may not have limbs and faces, which is the main downfall for most people, but their loyalty and devotion have provided Joe with comfort and a warm feeling inside for many years. Joe held Samuel tightly in his hand, using his free arm to push himself through the crowd as he made his way to a small store at the side of the street which sold magazines and small snacks. The owner, standing behind the main surface with his arms crossed across his chest, was short and stocky with thinning black hair atop his shiny head. His skin, pale and hair, was beginning to turn a slight red shade under the scorching waves of sunshine. His name, Darren, meant nothing to Joe as he peered over at the various items being sold. “Hot today, aint it?” Darren asked politely, eager to draw Joe in. “Nah mon, ya should live in Jamaica if ya t’ink dis is hot.” Joe replied without looking directly into Darren’s eyes. “You’re Jamaican eh? I bet you don’t get much bad weather down there.” Darren retorted with a lining of jealousy and a grin on his face. Joe simply ignored the last statement and continued browsing through the overabundance of useless things he could purchase with his hard earned green paper. There were candy bars, bags of sticky sweets, soft drinks, packs of bubble gum, baseball cards. Joe shrugged his shoulders and reached down with his free hand and took the first thing he could get a hold of. He looked in a dissatisfied sort of way down at the bag of M&Ms he had. Joe placed it down on the counter before him and began to look at the piles of glossy magazines and comics around him. “We have some… top shelf magazines, if that’s what you’re interested in.” Said Darren out of nowhere, a slight nervous twitch in his voice. “Wha ya mean?” Joe asked, confused as usual. Darren edged his eyes upwards as he awkwardly pointed his index finger at the higher shelves. Joe followed the direction with his eyes as he peered upwards. Rows of magazines covered with images of naked ladies, with white stars printed across offensive parts of their bodies, were highly abundant it seemed. Joe furrowed his brow as he moved closer for a better look. He scratched the top his head as the titles flashed before him. College Girls Gone Wild, Nasty Italian Housewives, Big Bazookas and many more all zoomed through Joe’s eyes and into his brain. He reached out and grasped at one of them randomly, pulling it down from the ledge and bringing it closer to him. He stared down at the bold yellow writing on the front cover which read ‘Big Black Booty!!!’ with three exclamation marks. As he went to flick through the glossy pages and old woman steered over to the store next to Joe and made her request for ‘Gardener’s Weekly’ magazine to Darren. She looked to her side and smiled at Joe. “Hello young man, what’s that you’re reading?” She asked inquisitively. Joe, his thumb set on the middle page, titled the magazine 180 degrees so that the old woman could see clearly. The eardrums of all around almost burst as the old lady screeched her lungs out, terrified by the sight she had just seen. “You dirty dirty man!” She wailed past her dentures and over-usage of lipstick. Joe didn’t understand and looked down at Samuel Strawberry, looking for some kind of answer. “You shouldn’t have shown her the magazine dumbass.” The small red berry squealed to Joe. “Why not?” Asked Joe. “Because that, my dear friend, is a dirty magazine. She does not appreciate being shown such material, it would be a good idea to apologize to the poor lady.” Joe nodded accordingly down his scarlet companion. He turned his attention to the blue haired woman again and opened his lips. “I’m sorr…” Began Joe, his voice being muffled. The old lady, now with a scorned and angry look on her face, had retrieved her handbag after paying for her Gardening magazine and had proceeded to strike the Jamaican wrestler with it; repeatedly. Joe yelped as the purse filled with money connect hard with his chest and arms. He dropped the magazine to concrete below and began stumbling backwards, shrieking as the barrage of handbag related attacks continued. He carefully cradled Samuel in his cupped hands as he began his retreat, protecting him from any danger from the assault. Joe took a few hops back before spinning on his heel and running away as fast as his legs would take him away, the old woman shaking her wrinkled fist in the air behind.
