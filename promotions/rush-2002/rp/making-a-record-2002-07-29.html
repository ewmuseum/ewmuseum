Making a record

29 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29-07-02 (Voice of Livewire) Some people seem to be putting two and two together and coming up with the answer five. For those of you who watched last weeks Adrenaline, let me put an end to these silly rumours now. I will not and I repeat the words WILL NOT ever tag or align myself with the wrestler named William “Biohazard” Perry or as I like to call him William “big-head” Perry again. Biohazard is a devious, unscrupulous nasty individual whose recent actions have somewhat disappointed and disgusted me of late. He has manifested from being a hard-working and intelligent young man into a self-absorbed megalomaniac who quite frankly I am ashamed to have called a friend. William was my friend, Biohazard, the evil mad scientist that he has become is a mockery and a sham to all the hard work and effort that he had made during his years wrestling alongside me on the indy circuit. We wrestled for ludicrously low amounts of money just to pay our dues to the sport we loved so much, knowing that one day we could both hopefully end up in a dream federation like RUSH. Now that we both have, I hope I can stay as faraway from him as possible and concrete on furthering my own personal career. Now onto a lighter note, the stable formerly known as Shack Pack has recruited a new member, the fruitiest man in wrestling, Joe Bananas. Joe is a highly talented man who will be a fine addition to our little multi-national family. So welcome aboard Joe, and I hope you enjoy our stables quest in our bid to rid the world of Playaz Inc and other men who resort to cheating and other dirty tactics to win matches. “Are you sure this is ok, I mean this isn’t another one of your hair-brained schemes is it?” “Ok course not Wire, trust me this will aid your career, not hinder it.” “I’ll believe that once this fu*king meeting is over, what’s the time Ted anyway?” “1.30 man, if we don’t find Mr Baron’s office in the next 5 minutes we’ll be late and Mr Baron is a very impatient man so quit talking and start running.” As the two tall men start to run with effortless expressions on their faces it is clear to see that the conversation we stumbled across was between RUSH superstar Livewire and his scheming agent Ted Wanstead. The location of this sure to be entertaining promo is Sacramento, Florida and the hall-ways that Livewire and Ted are hurriedly running through are the glossed halls of Sony records headquarters. Why is a professional wrestler and his agent running through the halls of a world famous music headquarters? Good question, you are all about to find out. As Livewire and Ted turn another corner of offices they accidentally bump into a small black spectacled man who is carrying a stack of papers. The man kneels down and picks up his mass of papers with a disgruntled look on his face. “What the hell do you think your doing, you cant run around is a place like this.” moans the man who has now risen back to his feet and is burning a hole through both Livewire and Ted with his stare. Livewire being the gentleman that he is apologises and offers to give the man his autograph. “Here my man…Livewire smiles…let me autograph that newspaper for you as a show of my sincerest apologises”. And with that Livewire takes a fountain pen from out of his pocket and scribbles his signature onto the newspaper. “There you go, something to tell your family and friends about” grins Livewire thinking he has done this man a great favour. The black man who has now loosened his top button in order to fully let off some steam cuts Livewire and Ted confused looks before pointing to the newspaper that Livewire had just autographed. “This isn’t just any old broad sheet you idiot. This is Mr Baron’s early afternoon financial paper. Damn it, he’s going to kill me.” whines the small black man who is now pushing his spectacles further up his nose in order to examine the newspaper properly. Ted takes a step closer to the small man and winks at him, the wink is replied by the man angrily flipping Ted the bird. Ted tries to laugh it off and pats the man on the back. “Say, what’s your name kid?” Ted asks. “Moore, Ian Moore” replies the man with a forlorn look on his face. “Ok Ian…Ted smiles….you say you know Mr Baron right?” Ian looks again at the autographed newspaper and winces before looking up at Ted. “Yeah, and what of it” he answers. Ted wraps his arm around Ian and chuckles wickedly. “So, you know where his office is?” Ian fakes a yawn and pulls away from Ted’s grasp. “Turn, right here and walk to the end of the hall, it’s right there.” Mumbles Ian as he tucks the stack of papers under his right arm. “Excellent..smiles Ted…ok Wire, it’s 1.34. We have a to get there in a minute, lets go. Just as Ted and Livewire are about to jog away Ian Moore shouts out to them. “Why do you want to know where Mr Baron’s office is anyway?” Ted turns around and smiles “It doesn’t matter kid, you’ll soon find out” and with that the two tall men run down the hall in pursuit of Mr Baron’s office. As they turn the corner Ian Moore stands with his head hung down. “I hope those two dim-wits aren’t Mr Baron’s new personal assistants, or else Sony Records is in for a whole heap of trouble” he says with a wry smile. Finally Livewire and Ted reach the end of the hall-way and after much panting and heavy breathing come across a blonde receptionist who is sat behind a wide desk in front of a gold door. The gold door has a star in the middle of it with the words Mr Baron etched across it. Ted catches his breath as the more athletic and fitter Livewire wipes a bead of sweat from his head and approaches the desk. The blonde with long curly hair and a tight fitting low cut blouse which exposes her cleavage looks up at Livewire and in between chews of gum manages to mumble out a “Hello, how may I help you”. Livewire leans forward and places both his hands onto the receptionists’ desk, he peers down her cleavage for a moment before looking up at the receptionist giving his trademark gleaming smile. “Hey sugar…he answers in his strong London accent….could you tell Mr Frank Baron that Livewire and Ted Wanstead have arrived albeit a few minutes late”. The pretty blonde receptionist immediately nods her head and presses a black intercom button that is by her telephone on the corner of the desk. “Mr Baron, there is Mr Livewire and a Mr Wanstead here to see you. Should I send them straight in? “ She asks speaking slowly and clearly into the intercom. There is a delay of about 15 seconds before a crackle can be heard on the intercom with a deep voice in an Australian accent replying. “Strewth Tess, I was sleeping. How many times have I told you Tess, DON’T DISTURB ME WHEN I’M SLEEPING!!!” The young receptionist sheepishly looks up at Livewire who now has a heavily perspiring Ted Wanstead by his side. The two look somewhat surprised and perplexed by the blunt rudeness of Frank Baron’s reply. Once again the receptionist speaks into the intercom, this time with a more tentative and timid tone in her voice. Err, sorry Mr Baron, but the two gentlemen are still waiting outside. Once again there is the customary 15 second delay before the crackle and boom of Frank Baron’s voice through the intercom. Yeah, fair dinkum, send them in and get me a coffee. The intercom goes dead and the young blonde receptionist stands up and walks towards the door. It is now visible that she is also wearing a very short skirt in accordance with her tight blouse, and just like her blouse her skirt is very revealing. Livewire and Ted both smirk and check out the receptionists tight ass as she opens the office door. “Gentlemen…she says as she turns around…this is the office of Sony records president Mr Frank Baron, you may now enter.” The door fully opens and the inside of the office is shown. The walls are a dark cream colour and are highlighted by a large fish tank with a green light inside that illuminates the tank and one corner of the room. The tropical fish in the tank swim blissfully most probably unaware that they are swimming in the fish tank of one of the richest men in the world. On the walls of the office hang platinum discs of various different music artists, groups and bands. It is now that we come into view of the great man himself, Frank Baron. Baron is seated at his desk with a cigar in his mouth. He is dressed in a colourful Hawaiian shirt, which clashes, with his tanned bronze skin. His black hair is slightly thinning at the front and around his neck hangs a thick medallion chain. His casual look is finished off with a stylish pair of Oakley sunglasses that cover his eyes and make him look deceptively younger than he actually is. Livewire and Ted slowly enter the room and the two figures look quite out of place in a place so full of prestige and wealth. Baron immediately rises to his feet and shakes both mens hands. Livewire and Ted both take a seat and wait as Baron’s receptionist comes through the door and hands all three men a coffee with a sachet of cream. As the blonde receptionist leans over to hand Baron his coffee, he slaps her on her pert ass. She cuts Baron a scornful look but holds her tongue in fear of another berating from her ruthless boss. She pulls down her skirt a little more and walks out of the office a tear welling up in both her eyes. As she shuts the door Baron moves forward over the desk and nudges both men in their arms and speaks keeping his voice down. “That sheila is one hell of dumb receptionist, but strewth she sure does makes the best coffee I’ve ever tasted, if ya know what I mean”. And with that Mr Baron takes a nauseating slurp of his coffee, causing Livewire to look away and shake his head. (Livewire’s thoughts) There is something about Australian businessmen that is rather shady. Just like Rupert Murdoch I have the funniest of feelings that Frank Baron isn’t the most likeable or honest of people. He’s arrogant, unbelievably rude and a chauvinistic, sexist pig after the way I’ve just seen him treat his employees, particularly his receptionists. Something tells me that maybe I shouldn’t have listened to Ted about conducting this deal. “Alright fellas…Frank Baron sniggers as he lifts a gold sealed contract from out of his draw….you want to make a mark in the music business hey?” Baron takes another slurp of his coffee this time causing both Livewire and Ted to turn their heads. Baron then proceeds to stub out his cigar into the cup of coffee and takes off his sunglasses and replaces them with his reading glasses to read through the contract. As he does this Ted whispers something to Livewire “This guy will scrutinize every last detail of this contract Ted whispers. “Lets hope that he agrees to the conditions, I don’t want our lawyers having to get involved again”. Livewire whispers back. All this whispering and hush hush actions prompts Baron to look up from the contract and move his reading glasses further down his nose. “Is everything alright fellas?” Baron asks, the gleam of the light now bouncing of the balding crown of his head. Both men nod and reply with cheesy smiles to insinuate that everything is perfectly fine and running smoothly. Baron grins and glances back at the contract with a look of joy on his face. “I have nothing to dispute what so ever fellas, which is strange for a hard bargainer like myself” chuckles Baron, simulating both Livewire and Ted to break into the routine fake laughter that accompanies a bad joke made by a person who has more authority and power than yourself. Ted wipes an imaginary tear from his eye and stops his laughing which is quite an easy process. He then asks Baron a question. “Ok Frankie, seeing as the formalities have now been cleared by yourself can we complete proceedings and get this contract signed by all three parties?” Frank Baron shuffles the contract forward on the desk and directs it into the direction of both Livewire and Ted. He puts his sunglasses back on and puts his hands behind his head. “Ok sport, it’s up to you and the pom now…laughs Baron…heck, you know that I’m more than pleased to sign on the dotted line but you and your client have to sign things first.”. Ted brushes the comment off with a roll of the eyes and eagerly snatches the contract from the desk and grabs a pen from a pot also on the desk and signs the contract. Ted’s eyes glow as he sees the 7 digit figure that is written in small print at the bottom of the contract and quickly ushers the contract into the direction of Livewire who as always is sitting with a relaxed smile on his face. He looks at the contract and then at both Baron and Ted. Like a spotlight on a convicted villain who is getting interrogated by a cop, the Lively one is being stared at by both Baron and Ted with blood thirsty snarls on both their faces. Livewire takes a moment to assess just exactly what he is about to do and places his head into his hands, much to the dismay or Frank Baron and Ted Wanstead. (Livewire’s thoughts) So what do I do? Ok lets analyse this: Name: Frank Richard Baron Age: 47 years Occupation: President and figurehead of Sony Records Pros: Shrewd businessman with a great mental acumen. Has launched the career of many talented musicians and could be the man famed for generating me even more millions. Has a wealth of experience and wouldn’t let other projects disrupt my wrestling career. Cons: Has just called me a pom, the fu*king Aussie moron. Has a history of launching careers but adding now help or backing when they start to plummet. Is undeniably a smarmy son of a bitch who would want a large cut of any money I make. Name: Edward Jeremiah Wanstead Age: 34 years Occupation: Agent to many sports entertainers Pros: My best friend and the only person who I can still trust despite my sudden fame. Would make sure that Baron, doesn’t screw me over and rinse me dry financially. Is the only person I know who can make a good old-fashioned English roast dinner. Cons: Has serious flatulence problems. Could potentially become a nuisance if this new career becomes more profitable than my wrestling career. Never told me that his middle name is Jeremiah, ok I know it’s embarrassing to admit, but it does sow a few seeds of doubt, can I really trust this man? Livewire raises his head from his hands and takes the pen from off the desk and hesitantly signs the contract, much to the approval of Ted and Frank Baron who both duly take the acclaim and pat themselves on the back. Livewire hands Baron the contract and he finalizes the deal by adding his own signature. All three men then rise to their feet and Baron shakes Ted’s hand. He then turns to Livewire and nods his head before breaking into a massive smile. “Well done sport…Baron smiles…you have just taken your first steps to becoming a POP STAR!!!” There you have it, I told you that you would be amazed. It seems that our hero and favourite wrestling star is about to launch a side project as a pop star. Since when did the personification of cool develop a singing voice? Let’s just hope that Frank Baron doesn’t exploit this man for all the money that he is worth. Take care Livewire-ites. The End
