Pet Happenings Part Two

2 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/2/02 “Gene! We can’t just walk into a pet store and say, ‘I’d like one dragon please!” G.O.D. pouts. His hopes of a lovable little flying fire-breathing green pet extinguished. The little critter of his dreams not coming to life. The Genetic Oriented Drone had first heard about dragons in a book. That book was The Eyes of The Dragon by Steven King. The book is ancient tale about kings, princes, scandals, evil magicians, and many other fun adventures. There is a secret passage in a castle that looks through a plaque’s eyes. The head on that plaque? Niner, King Roland’s greatest killing. Niner was a dragon that the king himself brought down bravely. G.O.D. enjoyed hearing about the dragon most of all and soon found more books about dragons. These mythological creatures fascinated him. Previously he had liked to hear about unicorns, but these were dangerous. Dragons were power. Dragons were Gods. G.O.D. wanted a dragon. He wanted one badly. And so he thought of what to do. How could he get one? Well, it so happens that G.O.D. himself was a creation. Maybe, just maybe, Dr. Jenkins could make a dragon too. And so he asked, “Can you make one?”
