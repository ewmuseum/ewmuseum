Date: Today (Our scene fades in to show Rush superstar “The Innovator” Brock Newbludd sitting on the end of a doctors examination table inside of the Milwaukee County Hospital. We pan up to the digital clock hanging on the wall to se that the time is 12:00, midnight. Brock has a weary look on his face as he rubs his temples and lets out a yawn. We pan around the room to see that it is your fairly normal doctor’s room. A little cheap looking wooden desk and plastic chair sit in one corner of it. Assorted papers sit on it, most of them are just little brochures about the prevention of some diseases and other conditions that could be hazardous to your health. A clipboard sits on the table among all of the brochures, we see written on the yellow piece of paper in bold letters is “Medical History for, Brock A. Newbludd.” We pan around more to see a few plaques and medical charts hanging on the wall here and there. The wall are painted all white, that combined with the overhanging light makes the setting feel very bright and luminescent. We pan back to Brock and to see that he is clad in a pair of baggy fitting denim blue jeans with black jump boots. A tight fitting plain white t-shirt shows off his quite impressive physique. His brown hair is spiked up as it always is, and we see that Newbludd hasn’t shaven for a couple days, for he now has a short stubble beard that gives his already tanned face a darker shade to it. Newbludd digs through his pants pocket, and pulls out a pack of Marlboro Lights cigarettes and a black Zippo lighter that has “Blood Brothers” written on it in red. Brock grabs a smoke out of the pack and sticks it in-between his lips and lights it up, taking a long drag as he puts the lighter and cigarettes back into his pocket. We then notice a bandage from where it appears a needle was stuck into Brock lower right back, near the kidney. Brock tries to lean back on the table but jumps back up when his bandaged back hits it…) Brock: I hate f*cking hospitals! (Brock punches the wall in anguish and then gets a surprised look on his face, for he has just punched a whole through the plaster on the wall…) Brock: Woops… (Brock looks around the room, to see what he can use to cover up the hole in the wall, so he doesn’t have to pay a even larger doctors bill than he probably will already have to pay. Brock looks around the room inquisitively, before he eyes a large poster that’s hanging on the wall that contains the basics of having hemorrhoids removed. Brock hops off of the exam table and grabs the poster, and the pulls the tack off the wall that it was hanging from. Newbludd then grabs the tack and the poster, hanging the poster over the hole so it will cover it up. As Brock sits adjusting, the doctor, a middle aged black man, walks in holding a clipboard. He sees Brock playing with the poster and gets a curious look on his face…) Doctor: Are you having hemorrhoid problems Mr. Newbludd? Brock: Huh? (Brock then gives a closer to the poster that he hung up, and winces into a face of embarrassment as the doctor keeps his curious look…) Doctor: If you have problems with hemorrhoids, I can check them out.
