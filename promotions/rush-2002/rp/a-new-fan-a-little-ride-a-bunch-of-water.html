A new fan. A little ride. A bunch of water

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: TODAY Tex Kelley is in his big Red Ford pick-up truck. The old ’77 Ford has seen better days. Driven hard and put away wet, this truck is clearly worse for the wear. The numerous dents on the truck would make it look old but the caked on dirt move it from old to plain old ugly. The rain beats down on the truck and yet every dead bug on the windshields is discernible. It is a dark day as the rain falls hard. The roads are drenched and the one working windshield wiper moves back and forth across the windshield. Coach Tex Kelley is wearing a University of Nebraska red tank top with Huskers written across the chest. His white ADIDAS™ wind-pants are lined with three red stripes. His blond hair is covered by a tan visor has a large red “N”. The truck is rumbling along a dirt road. CTK turns the truck down Main Street. He is headed towards the YMCA in Waverly, Nebraska. Waverly is an incredibly small town north of Lincoln. Coach pulls into the back of the YMCA. He walks directly into the office of his close friend, Steve Graf. Graf and Kelley played for together for 2 years at University of Nebraska. Graf speaks, “Damn it! I know I should have locked the damn door!” “You said I could drop by you – jerk!” responds Tex. These two men have been friends for a long time. Steve Graf arrived at Nebraska 2 years before CTK and his career ended on a stretcher. “I was surprised when you called, I never thought the RUSH superstar would ever have time to visit me out here in Waverly!” “Well, Coach O talked some sense into me and I realize that I can’t forget where I came from!” says CTK. Tex takes a seat in one of the mismatched couches that line Steve Graf’s office. Gaming equipment, board games, and paperwork cover almost every surface of this office. Tex takes it all in. “Steve- you have done a wonderful job decorating this place in the grand style of the best pile-o-logists of the world.” Coach laughs at his joke. Graf rolls his eyes, “You are here and thirty seconds later, you re busting my chops!” Steve adjusts his black T-shirt by tucking them into his NJ Nets shorts. He stands up as there is a knock at the door. A young boy comes running in, “Graf you have to see this pile of crap in the parking lot! I think someone donated that heap of crap to the Y!” The young man is an African-American kid with a shaved head. He is wearing a DMX T-shirt with huge Tommy jean shorts. “Hey – that piece of junk is my ride” responds a very defensive CTK. Steve introduces the young man to the old man –“Nate this is an old friend of mine Tex Kelley.” An awkward silence ensues as the young man processes the information and the kid looks over the superstar! “Damn, you are the dude I watch on TV! You are making bank – why you driving that piece of crap?” As Nate asks the question, he reaches out his hand and Tex and he perform some complicated hand-shake that ends with a snap. The kid is very excited to meet the coach. “Nate, too many times people forget where they came from. I have enough money to buy a new ride but I am all about the ’77 red Ford pick-em up!” The kid walks around the superstar sizing him up. “Damn – you look much smaller on TV! I saw when you were almost killed and then this week! I went nuts when Coach O talked to you.” Tex shrinks in his seat as his friend interrupts the interrogation from a 15 year old. Steve speaks, “Nate, leave my old friend alone!” Tex responds by shooting his friend a look, “No, it is ok!” Nate pulls up a stool and sits at the feet of CTK. “Sir – Mr. Kelley – are you going to stay in the side of red and white?” “Listen up! Say Red & White fast – rwhite! Get it? I am permanently on the side of right – the red and white. Nate have faith in the old coach. I am permanently going to heed the words of Coach O!” Steve Graf interrupts, “Nate your mom’s car is out front and Tex we have a reservation at McCarthy’s. I called them after you called me! You won’t believe it but Elaine is still working there!” Nate quickly gets CTK to sign an autograph and sprints out to his waiting mother. Steve Graf quickly locks the Y office up as he leaves. Tex and he walk out back. They debate about which car to take to McCarthy’s. Finally, Tex wins out and they get in his old truck. “I can’t believe that you still drive this piece of crap! I remember puking in the back of it after the win over Oklahoma.” Tex laughs, “I know but you know this truck is like an extension of my style. Plus, I have not paid off all the debt from the Arvin Incident.” “God, I read about that and the CNN special was brutal on you – what the hell were you thinking?” This question had set CTK off in the pass but he smiles and reflects. “I made a mistake, I was going through a ton of crap at the time and I just snapped.” The two drive for a few miles in silence. Coach Tex Kelley finds a song on the radio by Tim McGraw. The two men quietly sing along. “Tex, Nate was telling about your situation in RUSH. He said you have a chance at winning the belt. Why not just buy a belt?” “You are a clown! He meant the title belt. Yes, I can not believe it but I have found myself in the middle of the title mix at RUSH.” Steve smiles, “That was bad – I am sorry! Nate also mentioned something about a group called the Solution being after you.” Tex bears down as he drives, turns down the radio. He says nothing for a few seconds. It may be the falling rain but CTK seems to concentrate on issues outside of the car. “Yeah, Deamont and Matthews are in the Solution and presently, I have a laundry list of reasons to go after them. Deamont has tried numerous ways of killing me.” Tex slowly rubs his still red arms. The pinkish red bubbles still appear on his left arm as he heels from the scathing burn. “Matthews is nothing more then a Mr. Miyaga student, the little Daniel-son is a kung-fu fighter. You know everyone is kung-fu fighting these days.” “Why do they attack you?” asks Steve Graf. “I have no idea but the have crossed the man who now wears the colors of rewhite! They will have to pay. Deamont suffers from the greatest of all sins – hubris. The excessive pride is the cause of my starting my career in such a terrible way! It is time for me to set things right!” Steve laughs, “How can you be stuck up? You are a National Champ, an All-American.”. Tex interrupts, “No, I acted like a horse ass, like Coach O said.” Steve is excited, “How is the old Coach?” “Well, he did not stay that long – he told me to change my ways and just left. I was lucky that he even talked to me.” “This week, I team with a decent guy, Jonathon Drachen. He is a Pizza Delivery boy! Very cool guy and we face Demo and Math-pew!” Steve seems interested, “You and a pizza guy against them?” “Yes, I am pretty sure that we can win. Matthews is a little karate boy. As long as he doesn’t do the Miyaga crane, I should be fine! These tow have no idea about the new and improved CTK that awaits them!” The two men speed a long in the car. Tex intentionally drives through puddles. Water and mud splash in every direction as the truck rumbles towards the restaurant. Suddenly, a little water squirts through the floor boards and gets Steve Graf wet. “What the hell?” Graf is pissed. Tex is shocked but quickly figures that the old floor boards are giving out. CTK speeds up and aims the truck towards another huge puddle. “You jerk!” Tex Kelley responds by laughing harder. The second puddle sends a huge amount of water in the face of Graf. He is swearing his head off. Tex is laughing another body part off! A third puddle is in the line of sight and CTK speeds up. Tex smiles at Graf. As the truck hits the third and biggest puddle, water enters the car. Water shots through the car, it looks like they are on a log flume ride! Coach is drenched from head to toe! Graf is entirely saturated. A wet leaf is stuck to the interior roof of the truck, next to the dome light. “Well, that did not work out as I planned” says CTK. The two men laugh very hard as we fade to black. FIN.
