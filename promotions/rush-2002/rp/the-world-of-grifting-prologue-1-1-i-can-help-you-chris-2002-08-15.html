The World of Grifting - Prologue 1.1 - I Can Help You Chris

15 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 15th, 2002 A.D. For Prologue 1.1 Click Here Alright, I think we need to recap what kind of a dilemma I’ve gotten myself into over the last 6 hours, shall we? Can’t see why.... Tyler Valentine and I left Boston early in the morning; we flew to Vegas on a crappy American Airlines flight. When we arrived we basically lounged around the pool outside of the gaming rooms and the like, at night we were going to enjoy the gaming rooms. When night time rolled around, we entered the gaming rooms. Tyler and I walked around a little bit, went and watched a few of the shows they have their but once we found the gaming room? It was fun time. I just wandered around for a bit before standing back and watching some fat guy play a game of craps, wasn’t all that entertaining until I saw him lose a load of cash, sucked in. No one wanted to step up and take a shot at the game, I thought I’d give it a go. I rolled the dice, more, and more, and more and I began to win, more, more, and more! My feelings were one of endless luck; I actually thought I’d keep on winning more and more money. But then, it ran out. My seemingly eternal luck had just run out and I was left with minimal choices. Some guy pushed me onto the floor after I asked his wife a few questions, rather rational questions, though. When I got back to my feet, I just began to walk. Walking away, trying not to think about it. And that brings me to now. * * * * Las Vegas Streets2200 hours. “Fuck” I said to myself as I walked around a dark corner. I had just spent the last few hours in the lively rooms of the Caesars Palace Casino, drinking over a game of Craps. Right now, I wasn’t quite sure what was going on, really, I’m very confused. The added sedatives of Alcohol really don’t help. “What in the hell have I done to myself?” I kept asking myself as I walked the lively streets of Vegas. I walked past rows, and rows of people as I walked down this footpath, the massive Palace of Caesar...or Caesars Palace left in my shoe dust. As I walked, I didn’t know where I was walking, what was my destination in the city of sin, Las Vegas? I just don’t know. I walked on further; Caesars Palace was long behind me. Passing the hordes of people I just walked, no particular direction. I walked past this middle aged scum of a man stealing some Hookers purse, I didn’t do anything about it. Other people’s petty problems aren’t my concern. I walked past a bunch of Hookers, too, to my surprise I didn’t do anything to them or about them. Wow. “Hey hot stuff” one said to me as I past them, absent minded. “Wanna get lucky tonight?” I kept on walking, even if I had the cash I wouldn’t even dream of doing something with a diseased filled whore of a woman. “Fuck off” was my response to the woman. Was I right in saying that to an ‘innocent’ stranger? Probably not. I kept on walking after the offended hooker just flipped her hair and went back to selling off her body to the cheap, pathetic losers who couldn’t get a date with any girl, or in some cases with a guy. Yuck. Time begin to pass, how far had I really walked? “Damn” I muttered under my breath as a Taxi breezed past the sidewalk. “How the hell could I have gotten myself into this predicament?” “I don’t know” suddenly chimed in a voice from a place I couldn’t identify. I spun around completely in a circle trying to find out where that voice came from. My eyes squinted toward the sound of the voice, but I couldn’t find anything. I went to walk away, but something grabbed me on the shoulder. Spinning around I could see a full view of this person, it was the fat-man from Caesars Palace. In his hand, for some odd reason, was a small plastic Visa Credit Card. Odd. “Who are you?” I enquired, confused. “I am” he began to say, sliding the small credit card into his pocket. “I am, a man who can help you get your money. I saw you lose that game of craps, I’m here to help you.” “Yeah right” I spat back. “Like there are decent people out here in Vegas.” The fat guy seemed to be a bit offended by what I had just said, I don’t know why, it’s not like it was a lie. I don’t lie. “Oh there are” he said back, with a chubby little smile. “You just don’t know where to find them.” “Yeah” I said back, not at all interested in what this man had to say. “Just how can you help me? “Well” he began to say, a sly smirk spread across his disgustingly fat face. “How do you feel about, swindling people?” Swindling people? No way. I may have lost god knows how much money, but I still have the common decency to earn the money back myself, the proper way. “No.” I said back coldly, walking away immediately. “BUT I CAN HELP YOU!!” yelled the fat man at me. I kept on walking, my back turned to him. Walking. Walking further away from sin. I need to get this money on my own.
