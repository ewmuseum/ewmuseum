Things Are Going To Change...

3 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/3/02 We open to a small, rundown one-bed motel room. A broken air conditioner gives off warm, stale air, flapping sheets of peeling wallpaper from off the wall. A fan dangles wildly from the wires in the ceiling, swaying to and fro and ready to fall at any moment. The carpet is stained and littered with articles of soiled clothing, several used and bloodied syringes, and a number of worn out sexual “barriers.” A television lies with a hole through its screen and its antenna replaced with a metallic coat hanger. A shadowy figure, curvy in all the right places, emerges from moist, vulgar smelling mattress of the nearly broken bed. Slowly, careful not to wake her snoring partner, she reaches across and pulls his wallet from off the table. Flipping through a dismal amount of money, she muffles a cackle of laughter before cleaning him out and tossing his coach on the floor. Straightening the top to her dress, she slides the money into her brazier and slips on a pair of her lustful high heels. Exiting the room, she tosses her hair out with her wrist, and walks down the hall, never to be heard from or seen again. The man continued to sleep on, his loud snores sending dust from off the walls and into the air. Meanwhile, a roach made it’s way from the bathroom sink. Skittering across the floor, it came upon the bedroom and wandered around aimlessly, searching for a crumb to eat. Its beady little eyes came upon the man, and he went after it, crawling under a pair of pants and up his hand that draped over the side of the bed. Up his arm, he trotted across the man’s chest and across his neck, and finally up to his mouth. Peering into the abyss before him, the roach took one step in, when suddenly… Man: WHAT THE FUCK?! FUCKING… AGH! The man was awake. He reached up with his hand, grabbed hold of the revolting little creature, and whipped it across the room. The man rolled out of bed coughing and spitting and wandering over towards the bathroom. Crack! He screamed in agony as he stepped on the bottle the syringe holds. The plastic cracked and went into his foot, not breaking the skin, but causing a considerable amount of pain anyway. He groaned and hopped on one foot to the bathroom, where he sat on the crusted toilet and massaged the palm of his foot. With each stroke of the foot, his arms bulged forward, revealing a number of sore injection wounds. This man was an addict. When the pain was gone, he dropped his leg and peered into the mirror. A small shamrock lied under his bloodshot right eye. With a sigh, he spoke to himself… Man: Caleb… what have you gotten yourself into? He stood from the seat and stepped towards the mirror, which was hard to see through. A white powder was caked on it, cocaine probably, and made it virtually impossible to see one’s reflection. McGlocklan ran his hand across the reflective surface, clearing a spot for him to see himself perfectly through. Was this him? It was hard to tell. It was him in the flesh, yes, but in personality… uncertain. Even in person, it was hard to tell. Bags were forming under his red eyes, his cheeks were droopy and thin, he was in need of a shave, and his hair was in tangles. This was the first time in his life where he looked into a mirror, and didn’t like who was staring back. McGlocklan: Tch… some angel you are… He turned his gaze away from the mirror and towards the bacteria encrusted faucet. Reaching up with his shaking hands, he turned the handle. After a long wait, water splurged out of the end. Brown, disgusting water, thick with mud and the like. Shivering, McGlocklan waited until it cleaned before rinsing his hands and splashing water on his face. Feeling very disgusted, but quite refreshed, he returned to the bedroom and gathered up his clothing in a pile. Gazing at the drug paraphernalia on the floor, he realized… no one knew. No one knew about the long nights, the drinking, the narcotics… no one knew. McGlocklan: Things have got to change… Crouching to the ground, he began scooping up the needles and what not, reminiscing about days gone passed. He climbed to his feet, eyeing the things that turned his world into a living hell… but no longer. He wouldn’t succumb to the evils of the world… he would vow to live his life like it was meant to be lived. There was to be no more Caleb McGlocklan, warrior of God… no purifications, no helping, no remorse for those who had gone and fucked up their lives. He was no angel… we was no better. But things were going to change. McGlocklan: Things are GOING to change… He whipped his hand forward, tossing the syringes into a waste pail before shoving his clothing in a duffle bag. Sliding the strap over his shoulder, he picked up his wallet from off the floor – clean out. Grimacing, he slipped the leather into his back pocket and wandered over to a window. Desperate times call for desperate measures, after all. He slid it open, put one leg out, and grabbed hold of a fire escape. Sliding down, he was out. He took off from that shit hole, and ran down that dark, deserted, rainy alley. He wouldn’t return… he would be different… he would change.
