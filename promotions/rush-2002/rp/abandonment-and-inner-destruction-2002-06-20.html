Abandonment and Inner Destruction

20 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 20th, 2002 A.D. A stroke of genius is perhaps what’s saving Chris Matthews’s life at the moment. After over-hearing what Cassius had been promising for weeks, Matthews had decided he didn’t want Cassius to attack while not in the ring. So Matthews, in a somewhat cowardly way, acquired a restraining order held against Deamont. It’s no secret to the world that Cassius is a very dangerous person, working as a hit man for rich mobsters, before turning to wrestling. Matthews, a wrestler for, going on 7 years now, is no where near as dangerous as Deamont is. This “feud” people see, is it a professional feud? Yes. But is it a personal feud? Even more so. It became personal when Matthews kidnapped Deamont’s girlfriend of numerous years, as Deamont said, a cowardly act by Matthews, but it worked. He wanted to hit Cassius where it would, and it did. Kidnapping his girlfriend, will be the end of Cassius Deamont’s mental structure. * * * * “This is killing him” the voice of Chris Matthews said, inside of a dark car, in an almost deserted parking lot. Sitting next to Matthews is his friend, Tyler Valentine. Both men are inside Chris Matthews personal car, his prized black Imported (from Japan, silly.) Toyota Supra. Matthews sits in the drivers seat, the keys snapping together as the car sits in neutral. “What the hell are you talking about?” questioned Tyler, still very puzzled after he walked in on the end of a phone call Matthews had with the Police Officer. “Oh never mind” Matthews quietly said to Tyler, resting his jaw on the steering wheel of the impressive looking Supra. “Well actually, if you wanna know.... I saw Deamont leave this place about 3 hours ago, the guy just looked...nuts.” “What do you mean?” Tyler questioned, once more. “He was just sitting in his car, looked like he was shaking or something” Matthews spoke, in a sombre tone. Still with his jaw resting on the ice-cold leather steering wheel, he once again replies to Valentine’s question. “I know him better then a lot of people, I was his friend. We were stable-mates on-camera, but off-camera we still hung out.” Valentine nods, and then reaches down for a magazine while Matthews sighs. “I never knew him that well, but I still talked to him a lot. When I.....when I kidnapped his girlfriend, it really ate at him. He’s killing myself from the inside, it’s all because of me.” “Dude...” Tyler starts to say, flipping through the magazine. “...can we go to McDonalds now? I’m hungry!” A sigh is Matthews’s response as he leans back into his seat, turning the ignition key. “You’ll never understand anything....” Skidding away, the tires of the Supra spun rapidly as Matthews hit the accelerator and the Supra sped off toward the dark and black exit of the parking lot of the Hudson Valley Civic Centre, in quiet Poughkeepsie, New York State. * * * * I had no idea that kidnapping his girlfriend would do this to him.... to Cassius. We use to be friends, we use to hang out together after shows. Kidnapping Kathryn, I never knew it would do that to him. He’s killing himself from the inside; Cassius is mentally destroying himself because of me. He’s building up so much anger. So much hate. He’s going to explode with rage and anger, and I’m going to be the man to pay for it. It won’t be Brock. It won’t be D-Kay. It won’t be Tyler. It won’t be Boda. It won’t be Statuz Quo. It’s gonna be me. Chris Matthews. I’m going to pay for this, and I’m ready. Cassius will hurt me, he will beat the living hell out of me, but I’m going to return the favour regardless of what people say. * * * * Inside of the almost vacant McDonalds’ restaurant in Poughkeepsie sits Tyler Valentine, burying his face into a large box of Chicken Nuggets, and his friend Chris Matthews; who is sitting there staring out over the parking lot with a half eaten cheese burger in front of him. “Dude” Valentine said, between mouthfuls of Chicken Nuggets. “What’s the matter with you?” Matthews continued to stare out the window, mainly looking at his prized, and expensive imported Toyota. The car’s shining glimmer in the parking lot light catches Valentine’s attention too. “How much did that thing cost you?” Valentine asked, Matthews suddenly snapping out of his boredom induced trance. “Huh? Oh right.” He replied, quickly shaking his head. “Eh, about $50,000. Why?” “Nuttin`” Valentine said, stuffing a nugget in his mouth. “Just curious.” Matthews, suddenly, gets up from his seat and throws away the half-eaten cheese burger. Tyler, still eating away on some Chicken frowns as Matthews walks out the door, Valentine quickly racing after him. “DUDE! WHERE ARE YOU GOING?!” Valentine yelled at Matthews, but Matthews just shrugs it off and climbs into his impressive small car and speeds out of the parking lot leaving a frustrated Tyler Valentine stranded at the McDonalds Outlet. “MAN!” Valentine yelled annoyingly, kicking the concrete steps with his shoes. “Now what the hell am I supposed to do?!” OOC Note- Tyler Valentine was used with full permission from Kenny (Tyler Valentine).
