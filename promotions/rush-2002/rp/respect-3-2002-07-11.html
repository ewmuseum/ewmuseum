Date: 7/11/2002 Title RP: 'Respect' # 3Date: 7/11/2002Location: ... Respect is one thing, disrespect however, is another. Think of ways to disrespect an Englishman, then go slap yourself, you dirty AmericanJ Mr.Uk. gets slapped in terms of respect daily, if not hourly. ‘Wanna go see the big football game today Alan?’ one of his friends would say. ‘Sure,’ would come the oh so charismatic and social voice. ‘Great, the Browns and the Steelers are playing, Let’s go to the stadium early, bring a waterproof pair of pants.’ Do they not feel his damn pain? That’s what? Two disrespects in one sentence, one friggin sentence! But however, there is a line, stepping over the line is not acceptable. Saying something such as, ‘Sorry sir, you’re driving with your pants replaced with a grocery bag, and a spatula celotaped to your fore-head’ isn’t too bad. But you can still overstep the mark. The national snack/toasted food on the British is the CRUMPET~!, also thought by 63% of Australians to be a half bird/half ant eater variant, I can confirm this is completely and utterly incorrect. So, you ask the Brit, a world wide famed star, returning to a wrestling federation on a 7 figure a year contract, to appear at your awards show, fair enough. Theirs Currywurst[Sausage with curry sauce for you Yanks], for Matt Hunt, fresh fruit for Joe Bananas, and what for Mr.Uk.? Crab sticks? No, that’s complete and utter disrespect, asshole! Wheres the CRUMPETS~! One simple request for CRUMPETS~! To be put on the buffet table, and what happens? They disrespect you, go kick their ass UK! Do it for The Former Yugoslav Republic of Macadonia, do it for Malta, do it for ummm….Swaisiland, do it for everyone! Damn Yanks, complete and utter disrespect. ‘Respect’
