On The Edge [1]

8 July 2002

Date: July 8th, 2002 A.D. Journal Entry:July 2, 2002 Why am I doing this? Does my hatred, my inner rage toward Cassius Deamont want to make me, a simple man, hurt his ladylove? I don’t know anymore...I don’t think I’m capable of doing the kind of things he’s done to other humans, to other people. I just can’t do things like that; maybe it’s a sign of weakness. Maybe, I just don’t have that RUTHLESS AGRESSION that he, Cassius Deamont, has. Like how he set a steel cage alight with 4 men inside of it, I don’t think I am capable of doing something like that...but... I kidnapped her. I stole her from him....to get at him, not her.... .... all in the name of Cassius, all in his name. Die Cassius, you will die at MY hands. * * * * A few hours ago.... The early summer morning sun of New York City seeped into the hotel room of RUSH Wrestling’s very own, Chris Matthews. His hotel room is covered in his own belongings. 2 large suitcases lay open on a desktop, clothes spilling out of their openings. A large red bandana spread out on the floor, a small burned in RUSH logo appearing in the middle. On the wooden table sits a sprawled copy of the monthly Wrestling Tattler magazine, the page open to an article about Old School Wrestling. The Television set in the far right corner is turned off; the normal static sound from the unit is no more. Sitting on the black leather couch, arms folded across his chest, is indeed Chris Matthews. His usual blonde hair that’s tied back in a pony tail is frayed about, out of it’s tie. Matthews looks up at the clock once more, his eyes darting all over the ticking handles, watching the tick and the tock of the clock. Tick.. ..tock. Tick... ....tock Tick, tock the large and small black handles went as Matthews’s emerald green eyes stared up at them. An intense look on them, darting around every few seconds, surveying the area. An obstruct painting, care of the hotel, sits on the wall...Matthews looks at the painting for a few seconds, until suddenly a phone rings, startling him. “AHHH!” Matthews yells out as the phone rings for the second time, he shakes his head composing himself. He slides up off the couch and walks over to the phone, ringing on the bench next to the Kitchen counter. The metallic black phone vibes it’s sound throughout the silent, deadly quiet, hotel room. “Hello?” answers Matthews, frowning as soon as the words escape his lips. Matthews waits a few seconds before there’s an answer from the end of the line.... ...no answer. Matthews frowns at the emptiness of the other line. He taps the phone a few times, making sure it’s working. Nothing. Matthews taps the phone with his index finger again, nothing returns as his finger smash’s into the plastic of the telephone. “Hello?!” he asks the dead end once more, his frown on his forehead intensifying. Matthews yells back into the dead end of the phone once more. “Is anyone there?! Hello?!” Still no reply from the other end of the endless leads of wires under the plastic covering. Matthews grows annoyed, the blankness of the other end of the phone growing on his nerve. “What,” Matthews begins saying, before tapping the phone...once more. “What the hell is going?!” Matthews taps the phone once more, small, barely hear able breathing seeps through the holes on the phone. “Hello?! Is anyone there?! Hello?!” Suddenly, the annoyed and frustrated Matthews slams down the phone, clicking it back onto it’s plastic hook. “Fucking phones” he spits out. Matthews walks over toward the window, which looks over the city. Through the curtains he can see cars, he can see buildings....he can see people. A sigh escapes his lips as the red lined curtains flow over his skin, the warm New York summer wind flowing over his body. Those emerald green eyes that most of us have accustomed to know peer out of the city, piercing a hole through the thin atmosphere. “Why is this happening?” He asks himself, his eyes look out over the city again. Sighing. “Why didn’t I think this through before I...before I kidnapped her?” He shakes his head as the words escape his lips. Matthews slowly turns around the curtain drapes slapping back against the walls. As soon as he turned around though his eyes grew wide, startled by the sight of....her. “WHAT?!” He yelled at her, his eyes wider every second. “I THOUGHT YOU WERE KNOCKED OUT?!” Matthews screams at the unknown figure, for our uneducated eyes can’t see. A short, and raspy response comes back at him from the unknown woman. “I woke up, obviously.” From what we can see, this woman has long hair, we don’t know the color...yet. “Yes, I can see that.” Matthews replies, angrily to the woman. “But how? I thought you were knocked out good!” We can hear a small chuckle from the woman as Matthews seems proud of his ‘triumphant’ attack on her. “I wouldn’t be brimming with confidence if I was you” she speaks back, still all we can see is strands of her hair, dark, very dark brown color indeed. “You know.... he does have connections...” Matthews frowns back at this unknown woman, curious, yet annoyed as by what she’s talking about. “He has them, all over the USA...” “But I’m Cana-” he interjects back at the shadowy figure of this woman. “He’s from Toronto.” She tells him back, he hangs his head, remember this. “You now live in Toronto...you won’t escape him, Chris.” She speaks back at him once more, her voice raspy. “He’s all over the place, watching you.....” Suddenly, we see her. She is the woman whom was stolen...no...kidnapped by Chris Matthews 2 weeks ago on RUSH Wrestling’s syndicated program, Adrenaline. She is the long-time girlfriend of Matthews’s archenemy, one Cassius Deamont. Matthews looks straight at the drowsy, and sleepy, Kathryn tilts her head and folds her arms as Matthews’s eyes dart all over... Then his lips open, and he utters the word “....fuck....” as Kathryn smirks back at him, a sly, yet cunning smirk on her pretty face. Her reply? “Cassie's gonna kill you, Cassie's gonna kill you, Cassie's gonna kill you, Cassie's gonna kill you, Cassie's gonna kill you... BITCH!” Over and over again, going faster and faster, louder and louder each word falls into his mind. She’s trying to send him into insanity...it might just work. “AHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!” yells Matthews suddenly, and then just as sudden...he leaps forward and puts his arm around Kathryn’s neck, if this was a normal RUSH Adrenaline, James Williams would be screaming ‘SLEEPER HOLD O’DOOM~!’ and Kris Raves would be saying ‘BOOOOORING!’ but alas, none of that this time. Kathryn attempts to scream but the well trained Matthews knocks her back out within a few seconds of apply the sleeper hold. Kathryn begins to breath heavily, just as Matthews lets her body fall gracefully to the floor... ....leaving her there....laying unconscious on the floor....dragging her away....into a dark, cold and empty room, once more... Poor, poor Kathryn...
