Principal's Office or Battlegrounds?

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today (Our scene fades in to show a new face to RUSH television sitting on a plastic chair in what appears to be a waiting room. This face is of Charles “Chili” Newbludd, the little brother of RUSH veteran superstar, “The Innovator” Brock Newbludd. As we pan around, we see that Chili is sitting in a small room with white walls that are covered with motivational posters and the like. We see that on the wooden door that is in one of the corners of the room, a sign reads “Principal” written on it. In the middle of the room a large middle aged woman with gray hair sits behind an old rickety looking wood desk. The secretary sits behind the desk, shuffling papers and not paying any attention at all to Chili. Another student sits next to Chili, a nerdy looking boy with a scrawny frame and bad acne. We pan back to Chili who sits in the chair with his legs sticking out, with a smile from ear to ear on his face. He plays around with his pierced bottom lip, and looks around at all the motivational posters on the wall, most of them about kids achieving there dreams and staying away from drugs. Chili just rolls his eyes at these with the same grin on his face. Chili is clad in a pair of baggy fitting khaki shorts that are cutoff well below the knees with a ratty looking pair of Vans on his feet. A cutoff Rage Against the Machine shirt shows the tattoo of a woman sitting with her legs spread eagle on his upper arm, most likely put on illegally. Chili looks at the door that leads to the door that leads into the principal’s main office and gets an impatient look on his face. He snaps his fingers at the old lady and she nearly jumps out of her chair…) Chili: Hey old lady, what’s the hold up here, is that old geezer principal getting a handjob from a cheerleader in there or something? (The old secretary gets a abashed look on her face and puts her hand to her mouth…) Secretary: Watch your language young man, have you no decency? Chili: My bad, it must be your duty to the handjobs, eh? (The ancient looking secretary’s mouth drops open, she glares at the smiling Chili for a few moments and then she spins back around in her chair and goes back to shuffling her papers…) Old Lady: Cocky little whippersnapper…. Chili: Old hag… (Chili looks over to the kid next to him and sees him reading the latest issue of Computer World Magazine…) Chili: Whatju in for pizza-face? Wait, let me guess you’re in charge of giving the ol principal blowjobs, right? (The nerdy kid looks up from the magazine with a frown on his face…) Nerdy Kid: What’s a blowjob? (Chili just stares at the nerdy kid for a long moment before slapping across the face and turning his back on the kid, Chili begins to rub his temples…) Chili: I hate f*cking school! (The nerdy looking kid wipes the tears from his eyes and glares at Chili…) Nerdy Kid: You’re a big meanie you know that!? (Chili turns around to slap the kid again when all of a sudden the door that leads into the principals office opens up. Chili stops in mid-swing to see a short fat man with a round head and rosy cheeks looking at him. Too small of a sweater really accentuates the mans pot belly. The man looks at Chili and nods his head towards his office for him to come in…) Man: You must be Chili, come in here son, you must be ashamed of your self. (Chili shrugs his shoulders and walks into the mans office, we follow Chili as he enters. The fat man shuts the door behind him…) Man: Take a seat. (We pan around to see that we are now inside of a small office, about half the size of the room we were in before. The walls of the room are lined with shelves of books and a few plaques are hung up as well. In the center of the room lies a nice looking wooden desk with a plush leather chair behind it. In front of the desk are a couple of plastic chairs similar to the ones in the room before. Chili sits down on one of them with an arrogant look on his face. The fat man goes and sits behind the desk. We see a large tan portfolio sitting on the desk. The man begins to flip through the file, letting out a “oh” or a “ah” every once in awhile. He looks through it for a few moments before shutting and looking up at Chili, who was in the process of picking his teeth with a toothpick…) Man: Before we start out, I’m Principal Shaugen and I am the power that hangs over this school. I deal with all the crap, the turds that filter through this school, and Charles, you’re one of them. (Chili looks up and grins at the principal…) Chili: Excuse me Sir, but you more resemble a turd, you’re big and round. Principal: Listen here, I’m about two seconds away from expelling you and sending you back to the streets. Chili: I did nothing wrong… (The principal throws up his arm in anger…) Principal: You did nothing wrong!? Chili: You got it. (The principal flips open the portfolio and pulls out a piece of paper and begins reading it aloud…) Principal: According to this, you called your English teacher a “donkey raping sh*teater” and proceeded to stand up and show her your genitals and your backside in front of the class. Chili: That’s called a “fruit basket”, sir… Principal: That is unacceptable!? And besides that, you came to school intoxicated!? That’s downright despicable! (Chili lets out a audible yawn and flicks his toothpick onto the teachers desk…) Chili: What are you gonna do Dough Boy, spank me? Principal: No!? Here is what I’m gonna do, I’m going to suspend you for two weeks, I’ve already called your legal guardian and he should be here to pick you up any minute. (Chili grows a big smile and gets a excited look in his eyes…) Chili: Really!? Sweet! No school for two weeks!? WHOOO! (Just then, the door opens and a familiar face walks in, it is none other than Chilis brother and RUSH superstar, 'The Innovator” Brock Newbludd. Brock walks in with a cigarette in his hand and takes a long drag of it as he shuts the door behind it. Brock looks down at Chili and nods his head at him…) Brock: Hey poopdick. Chili: What’s up f*ckface? (Principal Shaugen sees Brock’s cigarette in his hand and frowns…) Principal: Sorry sir, no smoking is allowed on school property, please put the cigarette out. (Brock shrugs his shoulders and takes one last pull off the smoke…) Brock: Whatever dude… (Brock puts the cigarette out on the wall and tosses it to the ground…) Principal: Who are you? Brock: Chilis brother, you called me remember? (A look of shock comes over the principals face…) Principal: You…you’re the legal guardian? Well that explains where his bad traits come from. Brock: F*ck off fatty, what the f*ck did you need? Principal: Well, your brother here has been suspended for two weeks for “fruit basketing” as he calls it a teacher and coming to school drunk. (Brock lets out a laugh and highfives the grinning Chili…) Brock: You fruitbasketed her!? Awesome! Chili: F*ck yeah dude, she was a b*tch anyway. (The principal gets another surprised look…) Principal: You accept this sort of behavior? I think both of you need serious help! (Brock starts laughing and looks at the principal with a blank face…) Brock: What’d you say? (The principal clears his throat and repeats…) Principal: I think you both need serious mental help! Now, if will take your brother and escort yourself out of my office, it would be much appreciated. (Chili stands up and shrugs his shoulders at Brock…) Principal: Good riddance, sheesh… Brock: Yeah, ok we’re leaving… (Brock and Chili begin to walk out the door when the principal mutters under his breath…) Principal: Damn street rats… (Brock quickly spins around and slams the principals head on the table and pushes him over on his chair. The principals moans in pain as Brock and Chili exit the office…) Brock: Street rat, that’s like something those ignorant assclowns Chris Matthews or Tex Kelley would call me. (We cut out for a moment and cut back in to show Brock and Chili getting into Brock’s 1969 Ford Mustang, when Brock looks over to Chili and says…) Brock: I don’t think you need to go to school anymore, it’s over-rated… Chili: Good call, bro… (And to that, we slowly fade to black….)
