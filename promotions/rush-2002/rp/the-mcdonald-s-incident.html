The McDonald's Incident

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today (Our scene fades in to show “The Innovator” Brock Newbludd and his pregnant, but still quite hot, wife Lindsey Francis sitting in a booth, inside of a local McDonalds somewhere in the booming city of Milwaukee, Wisconsin. It’s your typical McDonalds fast food restaurant, so I really don’t have to divulge into what the outlay of the building is like. And if you’ve never been inside of a McDonald’s live a sad, sheltered life. As the superstar couple sits and enjoys their meals, a young boy walks up to them holding a small notebook and a pen. He looks like he is about seven or eight years old. He is clad in a pair of black Nike shorts and a Blood Brothers t-shirt that reads “We Are The Blood Brothers, AKA: The PCW”s F*ckin’ Tag Division!” Brock sees him, and smiles at him, even though his mouth is full of French fries…) Brock: What’s up bro? (The boy grows a big, hearty smile and sticks the book and pen out towards Brock…) Kid: Can I have your autograph, I’m your biggest fan! (Brock shrugs his shoulders and grabs the pen and notebook from the boy’s hand, and grins at Lindsey…) Brock: Isn’t everybody? (Looks back at the kid…) Brock: What’s your name kid? Kid: Superfly! (Brock raises his eyebrows and looks at Lindsey excitedly…) Brock: See people name their kids Superfly! Why can’t we name our kid that!? (Lindsey just shakes her head as she wipes off her mouth with a napkin…) Lindsey: Because! Now, just sign the damn thing! (Brock just rolls his eyes and looks at the kid…) Brock: Women, weird huh? (The kid just gives a dumb smile…) Kid: I like boobies! (Brock gives the kid a pat on the shoulder…) Brock: Yes, they are nice, especially when they’re bouncing up and down when she’s riding your… Lindsey: Brock! Sign the autograph! Brock: Erm… (Brock signs the kid’s notebook and hands it back to him…) Brock: There ya go Superfly and keep loving them nips! Kid: I sure will thanks Mr.Innovator! (Brock gives the kid a highfive and the boy runs off, holding the autograph close to his chest. As the boy runs by older woman, he grabs her breasts and gives Brock a thumbs up, before the old woman begins to beat him over the head with her purse. The boy lets go and runs out of the McDonald’s, probably saving his young life. Brock just shakes his head and chuckles…) Brock: Ah, I love teaching America’s youth the basics of life… Lindsey: That kid will live a troubled life now because of you… Brock: Naw, my guess his love of titties will get him a job as a pornography photographer… (Lindsey puts her hand on her head and sighs…) Lindsey: I give up… (Brock takes a bite of his Big Mac sandwhich and shrugs his shoulders. We notice that Brock is clad in a pair of black baggy denim pants with black jumpboots. A tight fitting plain black t-shirt shows off his chiseled physique. We see that Brock has got a new tattoo on his forearm, it’s of the mighty mythical character’s Thor’s hammer that is dripping with blood. Brock hair is spiked up in a stylish fashion as always and he wears a pair of expensive looking Oakleys sunglasses covers his rare crystal blue eyes. Lindsey is wearing a pair of baggy denim blue jeans and a large Blood Brothers t-shirt to cover her pregnant stomach…) Brock: Think of any names for the baby? Lindsey: I was thinking maybe Shane, it’s a nice name… Brock: That name sucks! Lindsey: Does not, it’s a sweet sounding names, because our boy will be a sweet little boy! (Brock rolls his eyes and stuffs some french fries in his mouth…) Brock: Well, I’ve thought um some new ones… Lindsey: (Sarcastically…)Oh, great, here we go… Brock: Ok, how about Zeus? Lindsey: Absolutely not! Brock: Luigi? Lindsey: No… Brock: Bowser? Lindsey: What!? Brock: What would you think of the name Fonzie? Lindsey: Do you want our kid to get beatup when he gets older? Brock: Here’s a badassed name, how about Lobo? Lindsey: Lobo? Is he a cartoon character? C’mon, Brock, get real!(Brock sips on his pop, which he mixed a little vodka in, and sighs…) Brock: Well, it was better than Shane! Lindsey: Whatever, maybe we’ll let Stacey decide on the name. Brock: No way! She’ll name him Harold or something! (Stacey finishes off her third cheeseburger and starts unwrapping her third when Brock lights up a smoke…) Lindsey: You can’t smoke in here, honey… Brock: I’m the “Inno-F*ckin’-Vator” I can do what I want… Lindsey: Please refrain from using your catchphrases towards me… (Brock shrugs his shoulders and takes a drag of his smoke…) Brock: Sorry, but I’ve been under a lot of stress lately. With Adrenaline and all, I gotta big six-man match against Kelley, Matthews and Valentine on Thursday, which should be pretty easy, but you never know. I mean, the fools might actually put up a fight and show some wrestling skill. Although, I do have Boda and Cassius on my side and with all three of us combined, nobody can stop us. Lindsey: Isn’t Valentine from Milwaukee too? (Brock shrugs his shoulders…) Brock: Yeah, but nobody’s ever heard of the fool. This town has only one hometown hero and that’s me. And as far as Tex and Matthews go, I’ve already proven that I can beat both of those useless tools. So… (Brock is cut off by Lindsey stands up with a shocked look on her face, holding her stomach…) Brock: What’s wrong!? Did they serve you bad fries again? Those bastards, I told them… Lindsey: No…My…my…water broke! Brock: I thought you were drinking a Sprite!? (Lindsey hits Brock over the head…) Lindsey: No you idiot! The baby’s coming! (Brock’s mouth drops wide open and he steps back a bit, shocked…) Brock: You mean little Gohan is coming!? We gotta go! To the hospital! (Brock picks Lindsey up and heaves her over his shoulder as she yells is pain. We fade out as we watch Brock’s Mustang tear out of the parking lot and head towards the nearest hospital…)
