Soul Searching- Part II: And She Dies

27 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 27th, 2002 A.D. He breaths in loudly as he looks down from the stare of the large metallic skyscraper. He sighs and swallows his stomach, breathing loudly his last words are... “Time to get this sorted out” Walking through the rain, barging through a pack of people Matthews pulls open the door to the large café, looking for the person he seeks for the truth.... * * * * The talk of strangers fills the café, bouncing the sound off the walls and into the minds of all within. Coffee cups crash together as Waitress’s collect them off table, rushing around to wait on the hands and feet of other customers. Our view swings around to see the rain soaked face of one Chris Matthews. His blonde hair is frayed in front of his eyes, those emerald green eyes fans across the world have come to know and love...well, not love. Matthews’s breathes softly in the bustling café. His eyes dart around the room, watching, looking for his friend, that betrayed him not so long ago. “Where is she?” Matthews asks himself, blazing a gaze over the café with fire in his eyes. He looks around the bustling room once more, his eyes looking over the dwelling. And then he spots her. Sitting, alone, in a booth alongside the window’s looking out at over the rain. He starts to walk over to her, thoughts racing in his mind. His breathing begins to subside, like he’s calming but still, nervous as an acne-ridden teen on his first date with a girl. Matthews’s feet scrape along the floor as he walks toward the booth near the windows. The rain splats down against the window, harder and faster every few seconds. The thunder crackles high above the city of New York as electrical cords shoot down from the clouds. He takes a long breath as he approaches closer to her, and then....the words leave his lips. “I think..” he begins to say. She swings around her head around, her long brown hair flowing through the air. He repeats; “I think we need to get something sorted.” She sighs back at him. “As do I.” She replies back, her mascara in her eyes thick; like she had been crying recently. He takes a seat down, opposite her in the booth. “Well” he begins to say first, his eyes very solemn. The normal bright emerald green has faded, to one of a dull green. A color that looks as if all the life has faded out from it, a dying colour. She opens her mouth, about to say something to him but he puts up his index finger, his fingernail gnarled and bitten back. She closes her mouth, pressing her lips back together. “I want to ask the questions.” She nodded back, a defeated women looking for an accepted apology from her best friend of so many years. “First of all” Matthews tried to say, choking the words out. His hatred for his younger brother still burning in his heart and soul. “...just tell me. What the HELL were you doing with my ‘brother’?” Leah drops her head in disgrace as her once best friend questions her actions, his eyes still that dull green. Matthews shakes his head as Leah picks up her bag, tears start to roll down her cheeks causing Matthews to grow very uneasy. She slides out of the booth, tears streaming down her face. Leah begins to quickly walk, or slowly run, through the café toward the exit leaving behind one Chris Matthews, cupping his hands over his face. He sighs with his hands covered over his face, the laughter and the talk in café still sounding through his ears. Matthews hands leave his face, he slides to start out of the booth and walk toward the exit until suddenly a loud screeching sound from the street outside fills through the café and then a thud follows it. Commotion fills the café, everyone within the room almost makes a mad dash toward the exit....Matthews slowly walks over to see, curious as to what had happened and to quench his fears. He draws near the doors crowds of people and looks out on the road the crumpled body of a young woman.... ...that fear is a truth. Matthews’s friend, long-time friend, Leah lay spread out on the road. Her arm hanging over her chest, her other arm at an awkward angle. Matthews’s eyes fill up in emotion as he sees her lifeless body spread out across the road. He runs up to her body and kneels down, trying to hear her breathing. But he hear’s nothing, a tear begins to fall down his check as he holds her head in his arms, a lifeless, soulless body looking up at him. She had died, died in his arms. Matthews looks up at the sky, if a man believed in him, he was looking up for god...but he never found it. One last sentence escaped his lips, just as a new spurt of rain spat down from the grey clouds above. “WHY? OH GOD WHY?!” he yelled up at the clouds, clutching his best friends body, a tear splash’s down onto her white cheek, just missing the pool of blood under he lips. Matthews looks at the grill of the car that claimed the life of his best friend, then at the driver....a tear hits the ground.... Her life is gone. Now, he must avenge the man who caused this.....his brother.....
