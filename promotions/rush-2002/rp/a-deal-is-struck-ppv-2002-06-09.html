A Deal is Struck! (PPV)

9 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/9 After his recent week that included the total failure of the floor boards in his truck and a main event match in Toronto, CTK has a lot to take care of. He arrived to the meeting via a taxi cab because of the damage done to his truck. His head is still throbbing after taking a steel pipe to the head from Steve Brent of the soon to be defunct Solution. His hands still are swollen from all the punching he did into the face of Chris Matthews. His pride is still basking in the cheers of the fan. He is in the stately offices of his best friends and agent. This is an actual business meeting Tex and Mike Beardslee wit on one side of the huge mahogany desk. Two men in very dark business suits sit across from them. Tex is dressed in a very unusual fashion. He is wearing a solid red button down shirt. The red shirt is perfectly complimented by a solid white tie. The desk blocks our view of his pants. “Mr. Beardslee and Mr. Kelley, we asked for this meeting because we at FORD™ think that Coach Tex Kelley could be the perfect spokesman for our company. We think he would play very well in the mid-west” says the older Gentlemen. The younger Gentlemen speaks, “I am Bob Klepp and my co-worker James Salisbury, have the negotiating power for the FORD™ advertising division.” Kelley gets a huge smile on his face. He blurts out, “Awesome, what is the plan guys?” His agent, Mike Beardslee, grabs his arm and cuts him off. “Gentlemen, my client would be very interested in working with you.” Mr. Klepp slides a large book across the desk. It contains block-outs (scene descriptions) of several of the ad ideas for the Coach Tex Kelley & Ford campaign. “We believe the image of CTK and FORD™ are a perfect match; these story boards show some of our ideas for your spots.” Tex is flips through the box and laughs at many of the ideas. He points to a few of the ideas and notes that filming these could be a lot of fun. He reads the list of things they will ask him to do. The list includes: grand openings, radio spots, and TV spots. He cracks up when CTK learns that FORD™ wants to do at least 3 different photo shots of him and FORD™ vehicles. “Me? At a photo shot, that is just damned silly!” Mr. Klepp and Mr. Salisbury smile at CTK’s reaction to the book of information that they had passed him. Mr. Salisbury, the one in the darker suit and the younger of the two FORD™ reps speaks, “Mr. Beardslee, here is the number figure that we see as fair part of a two year deal.” Beardslee looks at the paper and quickly excuses him self and CTK from the table. The recess into the hallway and approach Mr. Beardslee’s secretary’s desk. CTK chats up the young lady as his agent speaks, “Hey, here is our plan, we are going to hold out for a little more. Act upset by the offer.” “Mike – How mush is the offer?” Mike responds, “I ain't telling you – you will screw up the deal. Just act pissed off.” The two friends return to the negotiating table after CTK winks at the young secretary. “Gentlemen – my client and I think you can do better.” “We can and will – this is our last offer and it includes a customized FORD™ F-150 truck if Tex signs today.” The new offer is slid across the desk. Mike looks at it, “Done deal”. All four men smile and quickly shake hands. Tex still does not know how much cash he is going to make. As Mike, Mr. Klepp, and Mr. Salibsury discuss the legal semantics. Tex reaches over and sneaks the offer slip into his pants pocket. Mr. Salisbury points to the door. “We have to do two other things before the deal is done; go to lunch and take CTK to his surprise.” Tex opens the door and allows his agent, Mr. Klepp and Mr. Salisbury to leave. He shakes each mans hand and slaps them on the back on the way out of the office. The get in the elevator and begin the small talk. Mr. Salisbury asks Tex about his most recent match. “What was it like when Brent hit you with the steel pipe?” “It was not the high light of the night; I can tell you that much but like dirty dogs, the Solution moves in packs!” Klepp chirps in, “My son is a big fan of RUSH and he loves you! He thinks you really have a shot at the World Title.” “Tell your boy that I said hi! That would be great for you if I win the belt! You could make some cool ads. I feel pretty good about it! I have a chance to scout the other 3 men involved in person! I think if I train hard and the lethal lottery tag match works out, I just might have the gold on Monday morning!” Beardslee interrupts, “In fact Gentlemen, my client has a training session set for later today, so we will take Tex to see the ‘surprise’, then the three of us will adjourn for lunch and to finish the deal.” As the four men leave the office complex, a gorgeous black stretch Navigator™ waits for them. The driver opens the door and the four men climb in. The back of this Stretch SUV is appointed with leather furniture and the glasses are pure crystal. As the impressive vehicle leaves the lot, Salisbury pours 4 rather healthy glasses of Johnny Walker blue. This very expensive Scotch goes for $35 a shot. Beardslee grabs the drink intended for CTK, “I will drink two, my boy is in training.” Tex does not object, he cracks open a bottled water. Klepp speaks, “To long and prosperous friendship.” The men clink their drinks and Tex’s water bottle makes the rounds.” They pull into a warehouse outside of town. “Why are we here?” asks CTK. In unison, the Ford spokesmen speak,”Just wait.” They give the driver some very specific directions. The older salesman hands CTK a blind fold. Tex rolls his eyes; implying that this might be a little too cheesy for even him! The car stops and his agent and 2 new “friends” help him out of the truck. His steps echo loud. He even yells a little. He is aware that he is a huge but maybe empty warehouse. The 3 other man gasp as they see the gift. Tex is left asking, “What? What is it?” “Mr. Kelley as a testament to our good faith regarding our new relationship, the FORD™ family would like to offer you this gift!” Tex opens his eyes and lets out a huge belly laugh. His breath is taken away. A fire engine red Supercab F-150 truck is the gift. It has all black tinted windows. Tex runs his hands along the white racing stripes that run from the headlights to the tail lights. The white wraps around the lights and CTK is spelled out in the normal FORD lettering in a solid white! The custom gate impresses the hell out of CTK as he takes not 1, not 2, but 3 looks at it! The men from FORD™ are busy trying to show the wrestler all the new bells and whistles but Tex is not listening. He is walking around his new ride in an utterly joyful silence. Tex hugs his agent, “This is awesome, know I even drive in red, white and on the side of right!” “Well, I took care of one your concerns this week, now you must take care of the other 3; Deamont, Drachen and Matthews.” Salisbury hands CTK the keys, “Tex drive it in good health!” Klepp shakes Tex’s hand and opens the door for him, “The only thing this truck doesn’t have is a championship belt, can you go get that for us?” As Tex fires up the engine and gets set in the seat, “I will try my damnedest to get that done. I am off to train. Mike call me tonight regarding the other issues!” Tex drives off with a smile on his face, a dream in his heart, and a game plan in his mind! FIN.
