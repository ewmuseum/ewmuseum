The problem with balls.

8 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/8/02 “Very well, you must search for the book of Nastramus in there you shall find what ye looks for but be warned great perils beset the way to the lost book and only the strong-willed and strong at heart will succeed, and if you do find the book then use it with caution because nothing every turns out like it seems.” With that Andi saw a swirling mass of light appear before him. “Go know young magician because it is clear that your heart is set on thy task.” Slowly Andi stepped into the lights and a great feeling of uncertainty came over him before finally his tall muscular body was enveloped in swirling lights. The young magician felt his stomach lurch as his body began the earth-shattering task of moving between one dimension and the other. The sun shone down in the deep red sky causing Andi to scrunch his eyes up with the sudden intense light, slowly his eyes adjusted to the harsh light giving him chance to take in his somewhat strange surroundings. The trees or what he believed them to be were a bright orange colour while what should have been grass actually looked more like a harsh blue coal along with the deep red sky and pink sun left the young magician somewhat overawed. Slowly the tall wrestler started to make his way forwards totally unaware of what he was doing or why he was doing it, but still he continued on, well that was until a loud booming voice sounded. “So you have finally come in search for the lost book of Nastramus?” Andi being totally brought from out of his reprieve by the voice that came form nowhere. Standing still the young magician looked around for some idea of where the voice was coming from. “FOOL, you will not find me for I am everything in this world and in order for you to gain what you wish is by proving to me that you indeed do deserve it.” The large voice boomed once more in a mocking tone. Andi grinned in as he spoke his reply, “Well instead of ranting about it why don’t we just get on with it then.”The voice boomed out an evil sounding laugh, “Very well as you wish we shall begin.” Andi felt the ground underneath him move and was surprised as he entered a large corridor with a door at the far end. “Enter the door and your first task shall begin.” Slowly Andi entered the room and was greeted with a 9 spheres of different sizes and colours rotating around a large clear sphere where a small key floated in the middle. “As you can see this is pretty simple task get the key and unlock the door at the other end.” The voice said sarcastically. Andi smiled as watched the balls spinning around and started to makes his way forward only to be shot back with an electrical jolt as soon as he had stepped forward and broken the current that actually kept the spheres levitated. The voice laughed manically as it watched form its safe haven. “Not as easy as it looks mortal.” Andi glared ominously into the air before dusting himself off and concentrating on the task at hand.
