Nightmare - Beginning (part 1)

6 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6th August 2002 The white clouds, high above in a deep sea that was the sky, began to darken. The fluffy and delicate texture, which had held them in such regal form, sharpened and forced them into cold spikes to threaten those below. Behind was the hidden sun, an ancient face which once smiled was covered with the thick cloths and kept secret. The truth and past was to be hidden, this was one of the best kept secrets. The image of Joseph Banner floated on the grassy plains, bare scenery all around that stretched for endless time. The thick mane, which hung from his skull, was that of a proud and powerful lion. A crown resting, as if made of sunlight, so gently on him. he knew not of the storm above, the brewing terror and fear that was slowly falling closer. This was the beginning, of the end. The bleached white skull, which emerged dexterously from the thick fog which he wore as a dark robe, cackled with fire. He spoke no comprehensible language or dialect. His bony fingers were the ones to communicate, with a long metal rod pointed down at the proud land. Flames spewed from the end as the spectre scorched the earth and took the souls to New Spain. Joseph looked up, his eyes quivering with tears, as the crowns were melted down to coins. * Joe snapped awake into his hotel bed, sweat dripping from him like a stream. The sheets around him were screwed and thrown around him, his body barely covered. His breath was deep and heavy, his throat clenching at any air it could grasp and pulling it down into his lungs. His mind confused, thoughts seemed to take no form for any time longer than a few seconds. He was still taking in what was happening and where he was. he tightened his eyelids and blocked out all sight, he had to make some sense of it all. Every muscle in his body felt tense and restricted, he was unaccustomed and still unsure. “A dream.” Were the only words he could muster, his voice a tiny whisper in the air. He only had a blurry memory of the sights that had passed through his mind before, his sleep had locked away the clarity. “Just a dream.” Were the last words that poured from him as he lay back on the mattress. He curled the covers up to his chin and gripped them tightly with the edges of his fingers. He turned his neck to his left, starring at the warm rain patter against his window. With the white cotton curtains drawn he could still see the dark spots splattering onto the glass. This brought back the image of the darkened cloud from his dream and the bleached skull which had come forth from it. Joe shivered as he picture the horrific sight sauntering behind his window, waiting to strike and reproduce what he had seen before. Fright twisting around his spine, Joe shifted the white covers over his face and covered his eyes. He lay still for a few moments as nothing happened, he had expected an explosion of flames or the crackling cackle. He brushed the covers from his body and slid off the side of his bed, standing on unsteady legs. The long, baggy black jeans being his only item of clothing helped him to remain cool but he could still feel the beating heat against his skin. He twisted at his sides and searched the room for company, for some reason he thought there would be some sort of lurking figure around, just waiting to attack. Seeing nothing but his empty shell of a room, Joe had no choice but to proceed on to the window. He was reaffirming the fact that this was all a charade and some form of strange wrinkle in his mind. He closed his eyes once more and pulled the curtains back to reveal the true picture of the outside world. He slowly lifted one eye open, only letting a small crack of light in. he saw nothing. Nothing but the darkness of night and the pattering rain falling from the sky. “The sky!” Joe screeched at the back of his throat as he leaned closer to the window. He pressed his palms against the glass, craning his neck up in a search for any such monsters as he had expected. There were none, just the lack of stars and the falling precipitation. Unsure whether to smile or to frown, he slowly crept back over and sat on the edge of his bed. He continued to stare through the transparent shield before him. The sweaty handprints soiling the once perfect cleanliness that the maids had left. The gnarled tree outside left twisting shadows, like cracking fingers around a victims throat. This did not scare him at all, the shadows were no threat to him and did not pose any sort of disturbing image in his mind… but the clouds did for some reason. He fell backwards, laying on his bed once more with his feet dangling off the side. The ceiling seemed to slowly shrink, being overtaken by the colour black, as he was swept back into sleep once more. * “So tell me, Joe, could you describe this ‘skull’ for me again?” Asked Dr. Adams, a specialist in dreams and their hidden meanings. “Well, it wa bleached white wit da dark clouds surroundin’ him. He had a gun in his hands and when he laughed, dere was fire comin’ out of his mout.” Explained Joe Bananas, swiftly. Dr. Adams, a middle-aged man with thinning grey hair and a bristly silver beard adorning his chin. The round spectacles, which rested on his pointed nose, reflected the white paper which he held on his lap as he scribbled down notes. “Skulls and skeletons and a regular occurrence in nightmares, we usually associate them with death and evil. It’s interesting that you mention it was a bleached skull, this could mean a cleanliness or high regard for itself.” Theorised Dr. Adams, not looking up from his pad as he continued to write. Joe sank his teeth into the perfectly round green apple which he held in his hand, ripping away a large chunk of white flesh and chewing it for as long as possible to absorb as much taste as he could. “Dis don’t mean I’m crazy or nuttin does it?” Joe asked, a little worried. “Of course not!” Dr. Adams bellowed, a grin on his face, “We all have dreams, it’s just our brains sorting out information whilst we sleep. We pick up on it as we do and it can come out very strange, the point is to try and understand what all the information means.” “Ah get ya. Da got ta be some kinda meanin’ ta it.” Joe said to reassert the point to himself. “Yes, yes. Well, I’m afraid our time is about up. I’d really like to continue talking about this, tell me if you have anymore dreams similar to the one as well.” Dr. Adams said, looking down at his watch. “Yeah, well, I’ll call da place up an’ make arrangements for when I’m free.” “Ok, well see you then Joe.” Joe pulled himself from the brown leather chair and made his slow exit from the well decorated room, closing the door behind as he entered the refreshingly cool corridor outside. He used his forearm to wipe a thick film of sweat from his forehead, whisking his hand around to try and dry himself. The heat had been searing him for many days, feeling it more than others it seemed. He felt his throat become coarse and dry, his lungs felt as if filled with cotton wool and crackled seed pods. His fingers weakened and the half eaten apple fell to the polished floor, bouncing a little and rolling away from the near-choking Joe.
