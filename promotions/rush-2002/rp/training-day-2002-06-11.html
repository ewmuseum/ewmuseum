Training Day

11 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/11/02 The new custom red and white FORD™ Supercab F-150 recently given to Coach Tex Kelley is parked in front of Hirohito Tojo’s private home. Mr. Tojo is the number one martial arts instructor in the mid-west. His home is located in a suburb of Chicago. Tex has made a planned stop at his home on the way to the first ever RUSH PPV- Ignition. Tex is presently wearing jeans, a black RUSH T-shirt. The black shirt has the Ignition artwork on the front. The faces of Deamont, Drachen, Matthews, and Kelley appear on the front. The back has the phrase, “Are you ready for it to explode?” written in huge block letters across his back. Tex is leaning back in a white wicker chair, he right leg crossed over his left. Mr. Tojo is wearing an all black karate outfit. His blank pants made by the Phoenix Company are trimmed with red, blue and yellow motif designed to look like the rising sun. His black top is closed very tight. The black gi material is snug to his chest and abdomen. Mr. Tojo is a relatively well-built man. His dark hair is long and gelled back into a ponytail. He has that air of confidence and wisdom that Westerners have come to expect from Eastern teachers. The two men are sitting in white wicker chairs. They are on the back porch of Mr. Tojo’s home. The porch overlooks a small creek. The sun-porch or Florida room is big and spacious. There are several Asian screens placed around the room. The art on the wall is gorgeous and the small tables are covered with jade or ceramic sculptures. “What do you want from our training?” asks Mr. Tojo. “I want a better understanding of the Martial arts.” Responds CTK “No, what do you want from our training? asks Mr. Tojo. His facial expression and overall attitude has not changed. He is very stoic and quiet. He seems either very bored or very reflective. “I need to learn more about the Martial Arts. I have a chance to become the first ever RUSH world champion, but if I am going to do that; I need to understand martial arts to beat Chris Matthews. Suddenly, the teacher perks with interest. He is incredulous. Confusion and annoyance run across his face. He asks, “You are fighting the MSNBC guy? That is sick. He is a chubby old man.” “No,no, no – Chris Matthews is a wrestler in the federation that I work!” “That is impressive n may levels, Mr. Matthews is intellectual enough to host a political talk show and wrestle in the RUSH federation. I think you should be wary of such a diverse opponent” responds Hirohito Tojo. “Mr. Tojo – 2 guys with the same name. They are not the same person!” “Fine!” Mr. Tojo looks disappointed when the reality that the name is shared by two different people. Although they both do share the similar qualities of lacking talent and talking too loud. “Why do you want this training?” “If I beat Mr. Matthews – the wrestler this weekend, I could have the honor of being the newest RUSH world champion.” “So – you want this training for pure glory?” CTK squirms at his seat. He is aware now that Tojo is not a wrestling fan and has much to explain or clarify. “No, there is more then that, he and his gang – the Solution have betrayed many evil acts since RUSH has reopened and I am just one of the lucky guys that gets a shot at taking them down. Tojo looks pissed, “So this training is for revenge?”Coach Tex Kelley looks his trainer in the eye, “it is bigger than revenge. The whole thing about it is hard to explain. I want to win this match desperately. I would like to have the belt, I can not deny that would be awesome. I really think though this match is bigger then me, bigger then Drachen, bigger then Deamont, bigger then Matthews.” Silence. An awkwardly long and protracted silence envelops the room. Tojo gets up and walks around the room. He shuffles some Asian looking playing cards. They may even be some form of Tarot cards. He sips a small ceramic glass filled with tea. He approaches CTK. Instinctively, Tex knows not to move. The only thing Tex does is lower hie eyes to the floor and uncrosses his legs. CTK sits silently as the older man walks around him. Tojo even places his hands on Tex’s shoulders. He then starts an inspection. He orders Tex to open his mouth and Tojo inspects his mouth. Tojo’s inspection of CTK lasts for seemingly hours. Tojo sits down and is quietly reflective for moments. During which, Tex respectfully sits still. Both men take sips of their respective drinks at the same moment. “Very impressive, therefore may I say that you are a wise man. One that has vision that goes today, reflects yesterday and deals with tomorrow! I see a powerful force inside you. This force can be both good and evil – how do you deal with that?” CTK reflects, “In the past, the evil has won out! I lost a job because of a violent outburst. I took another man and bashed his head through three clocks. When I was dancing with the evil, I almost lost it all. My family, my university, my mentor and my fans all hated or were disgusted by me.” Tojo interrupts, “How did you fix it?” “My mentor, Tom Osbourne – US Senator and Hall of Fame football coach challenged me to change. He reminded me of where I came from and I moved to a very comfortable place.” “Very well, to be honest, Mr. Kelley, the training has already begun and I am impressed with your candor and insight into your own being. I have received a very large donation from your agent. One, Mr. Beardslee has paid me well. I will donate that money to my temple. You will train hard with the me. The next 48 hours will change you.” Tex drops his head. “Sir, I appreciate your willingness to help me.” Tojo directs Tex to get his gear out of his truck. Tex moves into the guest suite on the second floor of the Tojo residence. As CTK finish folding his T-shirts, Tojo knocks at the door. “We must begin!” “One second, I just have to,,,,” Tojo leg sweeps Tex so that he lands face down in his suitcase. Tojo grabs CTK neck, “First rule of Tojo Training is that you never talk about Tojo training.” Muffled by the suitcase, CTK says, “Ok?” “Second Rule is when I say begin, we begin!” Tojo turns and walks out of the room and down the stairs. A shocked CTK follows closely. This new trainer has captured his attention. As the descend from the main floor to the Tojo Training basement, Tojo advises, “Rule 3 is never break rule 1 or 2.”TIME LAPSE PHOTOGRAPHY/ MONTAGE The two men train intensely for the next few hours. Tojo teaches Tex many new moves and many counters to the most popular forms of Martial Arts. They spend the late night hours reviewing films of Matthews, Drachen and Deamont. Scenes show CTK actually making his own banzaii tree. Several shots show an exercise where CTK has a blindfold on and he must block strikes from Tojo. We see Tex doing roadwork – running while carrying Tojo on his back. Tojo also is seen giving CTK a small book to read. We see CTK staying up all night finishing the book. We see a cool friendship develop in these quick edited clips. We see a mutual respect grow between the trainer and the trainee. THE END OF TRAINING. Tex smiles as he knows that the training has ended but a friendship has started. Tex is wearing a solid Red T-shirt with CTK written on his left chest. He has on black karate style pants and his ADIDAS™ sneakers. Tojo is dressed in full RUSH gear. The RUSH gear was a gift sent in from Beardlees’ office. Tojo has on a Jeremy Howard RUSH T-shirt. One of the earliest T-shirts printed after the reopening. The navy blue shirt has JHowards face on the front and the RUSH logo on the back. “DID you feel the RUSH” is written in script below the logo. Tojo is also wearing a pair of the RUSH sweatpants. The black sweatpants have RUSH written across the seat of them and down the left leg. He is bare foot as usual! “Mr. Tojo – I can not thank you enough! You are truly a great friend and a better teacher!” Tex embraces Tojo. Tojo hands CTK an envelope, “Be like a rock, strong and sold. Be like the wind, quick and unseen. Be like the sea, swift and unforgiving. Be like a tiger, controlled and filled with rage. Be like the night, give nothing away.” Tex takes the envelope and looks at it. “Do not open it until you get to the arena on the night of your match!” Tex grabs the handle to his rolling suitcase and heads out the front door of Tojo’s home. He has many miles to go. He has many fights to face. He has enemies to conquer. He has dances to dance. He has fears to overcome. He has fans to entertain. He has songs to sing. He has believers to gratify. He has sponsors to appear for. He has skills to use. He has jokes to tell. He has lessons to learn. He has friends to aid. He has changes to acknowledge. He has wrongs to right. He has 1 dream to chase – CTK WORLD CHAMP? Do you believe? He does. FIN.
