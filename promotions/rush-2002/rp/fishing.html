FISHING

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: WED Coach Tex Kelley is sitting in a diner outside of Lake Hopatcong, NJ. Lake Hopatcong is the biggest lake in New Jersey and it is a very popular recreation site. Many people own summer homes on the lake. Even more people have boats docked in the lake. Boats that range from small canoes to huge cigarette style power boats. Tex is going to do some fishing and water skiing with Bryan Edwards. Bryan and Tex were friends at the University of Nebraska. He recently flew into Newark airport. It is before sunrise. Tex and Bryan are sitting at the counter area of the dinner. Tex is wearing a pair of red TOMMY(tm) bathing suit and a black RUSH T-shirt. His addidas sandals are black. Bryan is a slight man with sandy blonde hair. His T-shirt is a white one from Parsons Industries and he is wearing linen slacks. Bryan is eating a gigantic breakfast with eggs, pancakes, sausage, bacon, hash browns, and toast. Tex is eating an egg-whites omelet with peppers and onions. “You still eat like a bird!” says Bryan. “Nope, I eat like a champion- Thursday night; I get a shot at my dreams against Cassisus Delmont for the RUSH world title.” As he shovels more food in his mouth Bryan states, “I know, dude, I am so psyched for that!” “You are excited about it? It is everything my fans, my friends, my family and I have always dreamed about!” “Tex – what have you done this week to prepare? Are you sure we have time to go fishing?” Tex smiles as he opens his day planner and reads; “6am road work,7am breakfast7:30 am tape work8:30 am lift10 – 4 ring work4pm road work6pm tape work8pm motivational work10pm focus activities.” “Damn, I forgot how hard you work at these things! Can I get an extra ticket to MSG tonight?” Tex sips his glass of water, “Sure, I bought 50 tickets. I dropped 40 of them at the YMCA over in Randolph Township. You can be in charge of the other 10!” “Cool, thanks”. 'What an honor to get a chance to preform in Madison Square Garden! The home of the New York Knicks, the home of the Rangers. Ironically, for Cassius it is also home of the Big Apple Circus. I remember when you and I saw Bruce Springsteen there! I can not believe that I am going to be part of the main event in the greatest arena on earth! This is a dream come true. I am an All-American and a national Champion and now the dream of wrestling at MSG comes true - I am truly blessed.' Bryan listens and nods agreement with CTK. Bryan gets back t his meal! The two men sit there quietly eating breakfast. They discuss University of Nebraska memories. They discuss annoying professors. They discuss funny memories. They discuss the hot girls. They discuss their four great years at University of Nebraska. Bryan scrapes the ketchup, bacon, egg scrap mix off of his plate and finishes his meal, “We gotta hit the water now buddy!” Tex takes a last huge gulp of water and is ready to head out. Tex and Bryan argue over the check. “I will pay!” Tex grabs Bryans wrists – “I got it!” “Fine – I will leave the tip!” As the pay the check – there is a small TV over the register. The TV is set to CNN. The reporter is talking about a recent federal court decision; “Stunning politicians on both the left and right, a federal appeals court declared for the first time Wednesday that reciting the Pledge of Allegiance in public schools is unconstitutional because of the words 'under God' inserted by Congress in 1954. The ruling, if allowed to stand, would mean schoolchildren could no longer recite the pledge, at least in the nine Western states covered by the court.” “What the hell?” Bryan is also upset, “That ain’t right” Tex combs his hair with his hand and reflects, “See this is exxxactly what is wrong with this culture. America is a great nation. We give everyone so much. Who the hell would want to take the pledge out of America? The pledge is a small way to say thank you for the rights, privileges and safety that America offers. I can not get over that crap.” As Tex rants, everyone in the dinner rallies to listen to the pro-wrestler! “Another minority forces change down the majorities’ throat! This is not kosher! I am not happy about this!” The dinner erupts in applause as Tex and Bryan walk out. We see Tex and Bryan launch the boat from its pier. Tex and Bryan drive around the water for a few minutes. They drop anchor and do a little fishing. Bryan has three or four lines in the water. Tex is working two lines at the same time. Bryan breaks the fishing silence after a while. “So you face Cassius tonight?” “He is only a speed bump between me and the title! Thursday night will be so special. I hope I am lucky enough to get that belt!” “Who was that punk that attacked you? I never heard of him.” Tex laughs and rubs the bump on the back of his head and we realize that he has a bandage over a few stitches on the back of his skull. “That undisciplined slob does not matter! He is a drunken fool – Newbludd is his name. Brock or Brody or Brent – I forget – he really does not matter this week. I will deal with him in good time!” The two friends spend a few more hours fishing before heading in. They dock the boat and Tex is now ready to go train for his night of destiny.
