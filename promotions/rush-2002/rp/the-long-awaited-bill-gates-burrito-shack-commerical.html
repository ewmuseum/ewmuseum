The LONG Awaited Bill Gates Burrito Shack Commerical!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not as good as I would have liked. The scene fades in to a family eating in a restaurant designed sneakily to look like Taco Bell. The walls are gray and boring, and the floors look dirty. Workers behind the counters are made up to look mean and filthy. The children in the family, both of them, look sadly at their food…limp, bland tacos that look like they’re filled with feces. The parents look at the kids longingly, then exchange a worried glance before an announcer interrupts them. “ARE YOU SICK AND TIRED OF LOW QUALITY MEXICAN FAST FOOD?” The parents faces light up. They look back at each other, then straight into the camera, and nod enthusiastically. “WELL THEN HAS BILL GATES GOT AN OFFER FOR YOU!” Suddenly, the family is transported to a lavish restaurant designed to look like a Mexican ballroom (whatever they look like). Massive decorations cover the walls to eliminate that industrial feel. A wandering Mariachi band plays “La Cucaracha” as the family looks around. “COME ON DOWN TO BILL GATES’ BURRITO SHACK!” The family is swept up as a booth pops out of thin air in front of them. They sit down to a full table of colorful Mexican cultural food, all smiles. “OUR STAFF OF TRAINED MEXICAN GOURMET CHEFS…” The camera shows the kitchen, where several run-down looking Hispanic men try to force smiles. Former luchadores Silver King and Damien are noticeable amongst them. “…IS DEDICATED TO BRINGING YOU THE FINEST MEXICAN FOOD THEY CAN OFFER! BECAUSE IF THEY DON’T, THEIR ASSES GET DEPORTED!” Silver King attempts to spit in a pot of taco meat, but is immediately swept off his feet by two INS officials. He kicks and screams as they drag him out the door, his cowboy hat falling to the ground. Damien picks it up and puts it on as he happily packs a gordita. (Get your minds out of the gutter) The shot switches back to the Taco Bell ripoff, where people are dying at the tables and the employees take turns pissing in the guacamole. “SO INSTEAD OF WASTING YOUR TIME IN A HELLHOLE LIKE THIS…” With a CRASH, the shot of the Burrito Shack slams into the picture, knocking the other one out. Families pack the tables, all eating happily and having a good time. The mariachi band wanders from table to table…and there is a HUGE line waiting for the men’s room. (True sign of a good Mexican restaurant) “…WASTE YOUR TIME IN A HELLHOLE LIKE THIS!” As the families all have a good time, Bill Gates walks into the picture, dressed as Zorro. He beams at the camera. “Hi, I’m computer warlord Bill Gates. I sure hope you can all join us here at my Burrito Shack. Isn’t that right, RUSH Wrestling superstar El Spiffy!?” With that, the camera cuts to old file footage of El Spiffy! from RUSH. He’s backstage at some event dressed in his gang gear…looking like he’s in the middle of a promo. “-yes-” The shot immediately cuts back to the smiling Gates in his mask and cape. “So come on down to Bill Gates’ Burrito Shack…senor. Ask about our new Playaz Inc. Special: fried chicken and collard green quesadillas with a 40 oz bottle of malt liquor, all for only $5.95! Gates holds out his arms and the meal appears via the magic of freeze-frame editing. The little boy from the first family tugs at his cape. “Can I have some, Zorro?” “Sure you can!” Gates hands the kid the bottle of Colt and watches him run off as the announcer takes over. “BILL GATES BURRITO SHACK!!! IT”S JUST AROUND THE CORNER!
