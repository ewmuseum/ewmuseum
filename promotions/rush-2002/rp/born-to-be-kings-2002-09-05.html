Born to be Kings...

5 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9.5.02 Out with the old, in with the new... A saying that brings smiles to so many little faces, that brings joy to so many fans, and not only to the younger generation alone. The adults and the decision makers also feel the same bliss since the old has been ushered out by the up and coming, by the future... by the deserving. That is not the thing that I wish, as even though we are fully ecstatic over the timely ass-kicking Rich Cannon received. Yet what strikes us more is how we said that I would be the one toppling the Network Division from the very beginning. That I have accomplished what I set for myself. My goals achieved, my mountains conquered, my limits far from reached and I stand high above everyone else, listening to the deafening cheers from the fans as they stare at their reigning Network Champion… RUSH Wrestling…one solid, untouchable federation, the envy of all others, the pinnacle of the mass, and the tip of the proverbial mountain. The place where struggling rookies failed because they just couldn't hack it. A world unlike any other, because nothing is as tough as this. A string of talent, and an endless pool of the future stars. The old apparently, the unneeded. One step behind everyone else, while still being two steps ahead. Changes are always going to occur, changes will always present themselves, yet it is how we deal with these changes that we are recognized for. RUSH Wrestling…the epic of all federations, now something new... something different. To further finish the short discussion that I just began, is it time for the rookies turn to shine perhaps? The New Generation of youngsters who truly believe in there heart that they are the future of the wrestling industry? That in just a few short years, in just a span of some months they will be the top billing on the marquee? That they will be the fuel that keeps the fire burning high and hot, much more fierce then all the other fires burning at the same time? All the visions in there heads, all of the images in there minds, all of the dreams that they live inside of there little heads. Surprise, surprise my rookies, because I have some bad news for you. Dreams are often shattered, as yours will be to match the bones which I will also aid you in. In with the New? Preferably not for long. Don't get me wrong, I am not one to back away from competition, yet the word that you should have underlined in the previous part of the sentence was, competition. By bringing in the new rookies, and keeping them far out of the reach from the intelligent, hoping that they will somehow be magically taught everything they need to know. Unfortunately that is not how things work around here. In this business, you must find the learning tree and climb every branch and watch every twig. You must pay attention to the ones that know what they are doing and what have been accomplished already. The Reign of the New Generation? Far from it, my friends. The Reign of the Current Generation? Sounds more like it… (Fade in to a shot of young Sirius Cage, sitting poolside at his Godfather's mansion…) Godfather Silvio: Sirius… Sirius Cage: Huh? Godfather Silvio: Sirius, I thought it was you. Sirius Cage: Oh, hey Godfather Silvio...I was just soaking some sun... (Godfather Silvio gives Sirius a pat on the head, and pulls up a lounge chair next to him...) Godfather Silvio: So…how are you holding up, my child? Sirius Cage: And that's supposed to mean…? Godfather Silvio: Well, nothing really. Scott mentioned that you've been isolating yourself lately... Sirius Cage: I have not. Godfather Silvio: Well, that's not what Scott has said. Sirius Cage: Godfather...if keeping myself away from everyone, because I wanted to be alone is isolation...than so be it. Godfather Silvio: Sirius…what the hell is wrong with you? You're talking like your drugged-up brother… Sirius Cage: I know...I know. That is what isolation is, all right? No need to tell me... Godfather Silvio: Are you feeling all right? (The Godfather places his hand on Sirius's forehead...than pulls it away...) Godfather Silvio: Sirius, you're burning up... Sirius Cage: Probably because I've been in the sun all day.Godfather Silvio: Sirius, you've letting things get to your head...and you need to fix that. Sirius Cage: And who are you to tell me what I need to do? Godfather Silvio: I am your Godfather, and I brought you and your brother off the streets to live in the comfort of my home. Sirius Cage: So? Godfather Silvio: Tell me, tell me you did not just say that. Sirius Cage: Oh...I did. I did just say that. (Godfather Silvio smacks Sirius upside the head...) Godfather Silvio: You may think you're bigger than me Sirius Cage...but if you ever cross me like that...I will destroy you. Sirius Cage: I'm sorry Godfather...it's just, well...I'm a little stressed. Godfather Silvio: Crossroads, isn't it Sirius? (Sirius wipes the sweat beads from his forehead...and stares at the blue pool…) Godfather Silvio: Sirius? Sirius Cage: Yes, Godfather? Godfather Silvio: What troubles you, my son? Sirius Cage: Right now? Godfather Silvio: Yes...right now. What are you thinking about? Sirius Cage: I'm trying to figure out what kind of trouble I can get in by what I was thinking. Godfather Silvio: Come now, Sirius...what are you thinking? Sirius Cage: I'm just pondering life... Godfather Silvio: Sounds boring...you wanna come to the beach with me and Scott?Sirius Cage: That's alright Godfather...I'd much rather just sit out here... (Godfather Silvio frowns and leans into Sirius…) Godfather Silvio: Are you sure, Sirius? Sirius Cage: I'm positive Godfather...how about you run along? I'd like to be alone... (Godfather Silvio grabs his cane, and walks off...) Sirius Cage: Like I was going to tell him what I was thinking. Do you look like a fool? Two people knowing what one is thinking...that can't be good. God knows I have enough demons within me to where thinking for myself is bad enough. And then to be subjected to people who want to analyze you. No one likes to have their head analyzed, let alone lobotomized. But, hell...you can't help but wonder whom around us have been victim to the brain probings. I look around RUSH, and I look into the Britney Spears PPV, Crossroads...and all I see is uniformity and conformity. It wasn't too long ago, when we were blessed by the presence of only the pure RUSH Wrestlers...and now look at where we have been left. The creative minds have all left, the NTICWers have charged in, Boda has taken control...and I'm left as the sole heir to that throne. And now, we're left with the misfits and oddities. Well, maybe not the misfits and oddities. I prefer to call them…the commons. They place themselves...in front of a camera...and talk about how extreme they are, or how disturbed they are, or how amazing they are. Those are a few of my favorite things. Not raindrops on roses...no, my favorite things are the commons. When you just can't fall asleep...you take the commons. When you just don't feel smart...you take the commons. When you just don't...well, when you don't feel normal...you take the commons. But shall I not mock the commons...as they come in hordes, like locusts. They swarm in, bore you, snore you, and then rape you of your intelligence. Than they move on...proceeding to attack the next innocent victim. Sirius Cage: My God if this place isn't beautiful...natural, pure...virginity. Almost virginity. However, like some of us know, rape can ruin a perfectly good environment. Pillage, plundering, war, alcohol, mass confusion...all can allude to people, places, or things. All can be the reason for the downfall of a beautiful civilization, an ancient, respected nation. Evolution, civilization, warriors always come and destroy what was not there. But, some of us know rape better than others, and some of us know that idiocy can cloud vision, obstruct justice. Maybe I'm just rambling on to myself...for the sun is setting, and then we just have until Thursday...the days are closing in. The day of reckoning...where we savages once again destroy this beautiful land...just so we may try to gain a pointless, meaningless title. David and Goliath...except this time...there is no slingshot... (The camera begins to fade to black...) Sirius Cage: No slingshot...but I do have the stones. (Camera fades to black...)
