Soul Searching- Part I: Deception & Lies

22 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 22nd, 2002 A.D. The thunder crackles through the sky, sending sound waves into the impulses on any mans inner mind. Lighting shoots out from the dark, black clouds. The golden strobes of electrical energy shoot down from above, like a golden dome erupting over this city…. New York City. Raindrops fall down from the black clouds above the city of New York, the Big Apple. The largest city in the United States of America, busiest city in the world too. Businessmen and women, complete with their suitcases and essentials. All of them with umbrella’s risen high into the air to protect themselves from the rain dancing its way down to the now dark, damp concrete. Footsteps echo up and down as cars horns blast, bouncing off the walls of the buildings. We look around, getting a view of each person on the wet concrete, most have umbrella’s raised into the air shielding them from the rain, all except one man… A man with long, tied back blonde hair hangs toward where his shoulders end, all damp from the rain. Strands of hair hang down over his eyes, his clothing saturated with rain. A trickle of water starts to slide off from one the strands of hair dangling in front of him; it falls gracefully from his hair splashing against the water stained concrete. We all know this man to be RUSH Wrestling’s very own, Chris Matthews. Matthews’s eyes stare around the street, glaring all around the people in front, he turns around and looks up. His head looks straight up toward the large skyscraper, his eyes peering up the metallic face. His eyes close in, blinking a few times. As his eyelashes meet water fly’s off them into the air. He sighs, his face somewhat contorted with one of anger. “I can’t believe she did this…” he speaks. *** A door opens into a room with all the lights turned, only two crystal clear blue, green eyes can be seen piercing their way through the darkness. Shining in the dark, blonde hair is barely even seen, small little spots of blonde shine in the barely visible evening night. The door creaks open, it’s sound echoing into the light stricken room. I breath softly, as to not disturb whoever else might be in the room, out of sight. Two voices whisper in the darkness as the voice creaks... “Who do you think that is?” Whispers the first voice, softer, most likely one that is female. Her voice is very soft, I know who it is. “Who do think it is?” I spit out from the shadows, startling her. Her you must wonder? She is my friend, Leah Fei. I stay in the shadows, keeping the purple drapes over the majority of my body. “Ch-Chris?” she coughs out, like she didn’t know it was me. How could she not? Who else has keys to his own apartment other then me? “Yeah, who else would it be? It is MY apartment, after all.” I reply back, my voice full of hatred and lust to hurt. “So...” Rustling is heard on the couch, moving through sheets and blankets. Leah tries to get up but she stumbles into the coffee table, making a thump sound as her leg smashes into the hard wooden table leg. She saunters over to where I am hidden, in the darkness of my apartment. “Just...” I began to say, pain not allowing me to do so. “.... tell me...no....don’t. Why the hell are you with him?!” As I ask this question, I point into the darkness where a silhouette sits.... Leah reaches her hand just behind mine and the lights turn on, erupting the room in a sea of light. And there he sits. My brother. I can feel the rage building up in my blood, flowing through my veins like electricity. I open my mouth to speak but my muscles are so tight, my jaw locks and no words come out, just a soft sigh. “It’s not what you think, Chris.” My brother, Andrew speaks vaulting up off the couch. He stumbles around the room, clutching onto the makeshift pants he’s made with the quilt I have always sitting on the couch. I sigh again and reach for the doorknob, my fury and rage intensifying by the sight of my brother, from years past. “Fuck this!” I spit back at the two ‘lovers’, I pull the door open, swinging it with all my might and immediately head for the staircase. I start to run down the stairs; one by one fleeing from a scene I regret more then anything I’ve ever been through in my life.... *** He breaths in loudly as he looks down from the stare of the large metallic skyscraper. He sighs and swallows his stomach, breathing loudly his last words are... “Time to get this sorted out” Walking through the rain, barging through a pack of people Matthews pulls open the door to the large café, looking for the person he seeks for the truth....
