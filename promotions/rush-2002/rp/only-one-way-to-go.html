Date: Today (The scene fades in to show Stephen Blood’s 1971 Fire Red Dodge Charger nicknamed “The Blood Bullet” pull in front of the most popular strip joint in Milwaukee,“Shooters” on a cold night. It is a chilly night, showing that spring is not quite here yet. The wind blows sub arctic airs down the streets and into the alleyways, making even the homeless guys seek better shelter than their cardboard boxes. A mixture of snow and rain falls from the sky blanketing the streets with ice. But, this place looks to be exceptionally busy for how bad the weather is, we can hear the loud rock music from the punk band and some hoots and hollers from inside the building. There is even a waiting line of about twenty people outside of the door, a extremely large bouncer, appropriately nicknamed “Crusher” stands at the door, checking Ids and letting people in. He is a well-built man, about 6’5, 290 pounds. He has a shaved head and sports a long braided goatee nicknamed Charlie. He has been working this joint for years and knows the ins and outs of the strip joint and bouncer business. A couple of rowdy kids try getting in the door past Crusher. The huge bouncer picks up the two young men and tosses them out on to the icy street in one swift motion. The two kids slide across the street and slam into the side of a car parked across it. They sit against the car with a dazed and confused look on their faces. They mumble some halfhearted profanities before walking down the street towards their car. They got their skinny college asses kicked…inDEED! The camera cuts back over to The Blood Bullet. The muscle car sits running in front of the strip joint for a few more seconds, before finally shutting off. Some movement can be seen inside of it, before “The New Legend” Stephen Blood steps out of the drivers side door, while Rush star “The Innovator” Brock Newbludd gets out of the passengers side. The people in line notice Milwaukee’s hometown heroes step out and let out a massive roar. Brock and Stephen acknowledge the crowd as they make their way up to Crusher, who just stares at the two with a unimpressive look. The Blood Brothers try to proceed through but Crusher steps in their path…) Crusher: Let me check your ID’s. (Brock and Stephen look at each other and smirk, they then walk forward again brushing off Crusher. The large bouncer steps in their way once again, this time putting his finger in Brock’s chest…) Crusher: I said, Let me check your ID’s! (Brock and Stephen’s smirks turn to serious looks as they stare at Crusher…) Brock: You’re serious? Crusher: Yes, let me see them, or else I’ll throw your asses out of here… Stephen: Well, I think we have our ID’s, don’t we Brock? Brock: I believe we do Stephen…. Brock and Stephen begin digging through the pockets of there coats…) Brock: I didn’t find my ID, but I did find something else… Stephen: Yeah, I found the same thing… Crusher: What? Stephen and Brock: THIS! (With that, Brock and Stephen both deliver a punch straight to the big mans face. The blow sends Crusher reeling back a few feet but still standing. The bouncer shakes off the blows and charges the Blood Brothers. Stephen ducks down real quick, causing the big man to trip over him and directly into a Brock Newbludd dropkick that sends Crusher down and out. Brock and Stephen stand over the fallen bouncer and high five as the waiting line cheers them on…) Brock: You silly jackass! Stephen: You should know not to f*ck with The Blood Brothers! Brock: Even though we’re retired! (Crusher mumbles a few profanities, only to have Brock kick him the jaw. Brock and Stephen high five again, then make there way into the strip club as the scene fades out…) FADE OUT FADE IN (The scene fades in to show the inside of the strip joint that we last saw our ass kicking duo entering. The camera pans around to show the strip joint, it is a very nice place, a large open area with many tables, all with people sitting at them. A stage is set up on the side with a live band playing at it. But, most people’s attention is directed towards the large front stage of the building, pole dancers, strippers and many other beautiful women dance on this stage. A large group of men circle around the stage, stuffing dollar bills into the dancers thong underwear whenever they get the chance. Girls also walk around the table area, giving out lap dances to patrons if they have the money. The camera swings around to show a bar area that is on the side of the stripper’s stage. It is a very large bar, with two bartenders working out. The camera pans over the bar patrons, some drunk, a couple passed out and others talking with there fellow patrons. The camera finally stops on Brock and Stephen sitting at a table, not even paying attention to the woman dancing around them. A hot looking brunette waitress walk up to the Blood Brothers, who have their heads turned away from her…) Waitress: You guys need a refill on the beers? (Brock and Stephen turn their heads and the waitress nearly drops the tray she’s holding…) Waitress: Oh my god! You’re the Blood Brothers! I’m your biggest fan… Stephen: Interesting, yeah get us a pitcher of Heineken… (Brock lights up a cigarette as he checks out the waitresses ass…) Brock: Better make that two pitchers… Waitress: Ok! Right away! But, can I have your guys autographs, I’m the president of The Blood Brothers Fan Club! (Brock and Stephen looks at each other curiously…) Brock: We have a fanclub? Stephen: I guess so… Brock: You got some paper, we’ll sign you one? Waitress: No, I don’t, but sign these! (The waitress pulls up her shirt to show a massive set of breasts. Brock and Stephen’s eyes get as nearly as big as the jugs. The waitress tosses Brock a black marker from her waitress pouch…) Waitress: Hurry up, it’s getting kinda cold! Stephen: As I can see… Brock: It is a bit nippy in here… (Brock leans over and signs the waitresses left breast, drawing a smiley face on the nipple…) Waitress: Oh! That tickles! Brock: Heh… (Brock gets done and hands the marker to Stephen who does the same, signing the right breast…) Waitress: Thanks! Your beers will be right up! Brock: Uh huh… Stephen: Yeah… (The waitress walks away, and Brock looks at Stephen with a cocky grin…) Brock: I betcha Chris Matthews, D-Kay Calyx, or Tyler Valentine never gets to sign tits like that! Stephen: The only thing those three sign are the welfare checks… (Brock chuckles and highfives Stephen…) Brock: Dude, I can’t wait to get my hands on those three. I’m sick of their sh*t, I should be the #1 contender for the belt, no questions asked. (Brock takes a drag off his smoke and leans back…) Brock: But, oh well, its not like I’m gonna turn down a fight against those guys, I mean hell, that’s what I do best, fight! Stephen: Dude, don’t worry about the match, you got it in the bag. Brock: True, true. I’m not doubting that, because the fact is besides maybe Cassius I’m the best wrestler in Rush. And after I’m done with the three jobbers I have to face on Thrursday, there’s only one place to go. Stephen: Up? Brock: Exactly. (Brock lets out a cocky grin. We slowly fade out as the waitress comes with his and Stephen beer…)
