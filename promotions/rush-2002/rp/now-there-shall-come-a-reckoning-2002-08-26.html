Now there shall come a Reckoning...

26 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8.26.02 “Sweet are the uses of adversity.”--William Shakespeare How is it that the people that do all of the good in the world, the real role models are given such disrespect, such a tenaciously horrible shake, when all they really deserve is a fair one at that. I have always been one to want to play by the rules, to give the children someone to look up to like I had growing up. To have someone to want to be like, like I did, and to be able to look at someone and list off the things that impress you of them. Now that I have done everything that I can to do that, now that I have accomplished everything in my life and have never taken the fans or the talent for granted, Now that I am at the apex of my career, my climax. Everybody around me only wishes me harm, even the men who sign my checks. If any of you wonder why somebody in the wrestling world becomes hated, losses all of there morals and does what they feel is best for them, that is the reason why. Disrespect. El Spiffy, I always respected you for your accomplishments, for being a fighter, for being such a name, for being a fellow RUSH employee. But I will knock you on your ass for a reason Spiff, and that reason is not personal, it is business. Yes, this is not a game, because what it is, is life. Wrestling for me, for you, for the real wrestlers, is life. It is all about reaching the next level without dying, it is all about surviving. When you fail to make the next level, you try again, when you fail to survive, you die. Spiffster, life is a game that only the greatest players will prosper in, let alone survive, and wrestling is the essence of this game. Being in control, being able to decide your own fate and not have it handed to you by someone else. I am fairly sure that you have never wrestled anyone like me, because there is nobody like me. I wrestle, like you, because it is the only thing I know how to do. I wrestle because it is a chance to prove that you are the best at something. I wrestle because I love it Spiff, I love every second of the action and will die having the exact same feeling. However if you think that you are the same as me, you are far from accurate. You see, I am a sort of Prototype, I am something that nobody has ever been before and that many try to be like now…but why? Simple Spiffy, nothing impresses me, Nothing. Spiff, you have come up against someone who only cares about the world he lives in, and nothing in it, something that you don't see at every corner no matter how much people masquerade themselves. I, much like you, Don't know anything about you, but the difference between us Spiffy, is I will not waste my promo time learning about it then, I will not try to create a promo out of nothing and hope to God that it pans out decently. I can see you Spiffy, I can see that while you are fishing for something to say, while you are fishing for a handle to grasp, you are nervous. I can see you quivering like an alcoholics liver and I have to smile, because as much as you want to believe that you can beat me, as much as you listen to your friends tell you that you can win, you know that I am one step above you. You know it El Spiffy, and you are scared. No worries. However Spiff, for many people before you have fallen and many people after you will fall. Many people think that this life is about the money, about the paychecks that come every week sporting more zeroes then scoreless baseball game, but they are wrong. Dead wrong. This Life is about us Spiff, about you and me, about the aspiring stars. It is about the people who are willing to give much more then there all and risk there own bodily harm for advancement. It is about the fans, it is about the opportunity of being something that only few can be, and it is the opportunity of being real...and being yourself. Do not try to hide behind a group of people Spiff, come out alone and be yourself, be free. What I care about is people who say they are in wrestling for the life, not the money, to prove themselves in front of the world. Prove it kid, tell me to my face, alone, anything you have to say. I ask you, no, I implore you. Prove to me that it is life, prove to me that to you this isn't a game. Keep your vignettes and your songs to yourself, keep your theories and research to yourself. Be yourself, be free...be Real. Like it or not, facts are facts, and all of you have to live by them. Sirius Cage: Where is this place at Scott? Scott Cage: It's coming up Sirius. (Scott walks in front of Sirius, and suddenly stops...) Scott Cage: Here...we...are... (Scott looks up...as Sirius follows…) Sirius Cage: Planet Hollywood? Scott Cage: This has stars all over the place! Sirius Cage: Like who? Scott Cage: Well...Hollywood stars! Sirius Cage: I'm sure stars that live in Hollywood fly out to San Diego just to eat here. Scott Cage: Well, I would if I was from Hollywood, and since this is the only Planet Hollywood, it seems logical. Sirius Cage: Scott...have you looked at this sign? Scott Cage: What sign? Sirius Cage: The sign that says that this Planet Hollywood has closed down due to lack of interest. Scott Cage: Well...that's odd. I wonder what is going to fill it's spot... Sirius Cage: It says here...a Starbucks. Scott Cage: Really? Wow! I love that place. And having a Starbucks is the greatest thing in the world... Sirius Cage: And it isn't like San Diego has any... Scott Cage: You can say that again. Sirius Cage: ...yes, I mean, the nearest one is...well, Scott, is that one across the street, or the one two stores down closer? Scott Cage: That's a tough decision...I mean, what if we walk five feet further than we have to? Sirius Cage: Would that imply we're going to go... Scott Cage: To.... Sirius Cage: ...oh no… (Scott runs across the street…and enters into a tiny, crammed little store, with a line longer than the one at the World Series…) Scott Cage: Uh…I think this is the quick line...Sirius Cage: Right. But to be safe, you hold our spot... Scott Cage: What? But... Sirius Cage: Just save our spot, Scott. (Sirius slaps him across the back and walks out of the Starbucks, and sits himself on a bench outside...while a possessed look crosses his perplexed face...) Sirius Cage: Man, how the time flies. But, who am I to judge? He's trying; he's off on his own. It is more than I can say for two undeserving creations. For it is nothing like a Syndicate Champ and his seemingly 'top notch' opponent to completely go off into seclusion. I've seen many people do it Spiffy, you surely are gearing yourself for the battle to come. For I, and Scott, are more than prepared...and if I have to, I will drag you down, make you an example, just to make this interesting. Well Spiffy, I must credit you...you've tested me. I don't know right now, how I can lower my sanity. But...I'll think about it...truly I will. But it shall be death defying of myself to find some way to out beat the insanity of El Spiffy. That...may be too insane for even I. (Sirius lowers his head...as Scott walks out of Starbucks...) Scott Cage: Sirius, we're next in line...so let's go. Sirius Cage: See, I told you it'd be quick...but I have a question... Scott Cage: What is it? Scott Cage: If you're here...who's holding our spot? (Scott opens his mouth...than smacks himself in the head repeatedly, as the camera fades to black...)
