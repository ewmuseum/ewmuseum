Home Sweet Home

19 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 19th HOME HOME HOME It was a place that I hadn’t seen enough these past few months. Hell, it was a place that I hadn’t seen AT ALL these past few months. I was either off, stopping Sven from his plans or stopping in city by city for the NTICW events. Now, as my first RUSH event approached, I finally had gotten a little break from all of the crime-stopping in the under-cover business and the wrestling..... for a week, to go back to my old, huge apartment that I had shared with my four friends, and a girlfriend. We had always been tight, the six of us, being friends from all the way back to grade four when I had moved to New York. We had always been there for each other so of course we had to live together and we were still friends to this day. First, there was JJ, my best friend through all of the times, everything. What can I say about JJ. He had short hair, almost bald but enough hair to see that covered his head. He was more of a cool person that even me! With his vests and tank-tops he was a ladies man, everybody knew him but too me, he was just funny, old Justin Jacob McAnton. When I was down about losing a match or something else, he always cheered me up with his impressions or jokes that cracked me up or made me smile every time. Best friends forever, were we. Next there was Justin. Two Justin’s were my friends, what were the odds! Justin Lang and JJ were friends before I came from Germany and when I came, I was in there group immediately. Justin would probably be my best friend but he was more quiet but I still liked him a lot and we were really good buds. Justin was training to get into the navy. He had buzzed hair, shorter than JJ’s. He was more of the formal guy and not like a slob as much so he wore clothes accordingly. With me involved, that made three boys, leaving three girls, how perfect that it fit that way. Lets just start with my girlfriend, Celeste. Celeste. It was a name that I had liked a lot. That wasn’t what attracted me to her for the most part though. It was her beautiful baby blue eyes that made me smile when I looked at them. She had blonde hair, down to her shoulders, usually in a pony-tail. I looked at her with ease and she was interested in me too. We have been going out for over three months now and........... don’t tell her this.....but........ I was going to ask her to marry me at the right moment and right time. I was so excited to see her again and I knew she was too. Celeste Duran, my girl. The second girl was Mandy Liton. She and Celeste were best friends ever since they were born. They had lived and grew up beside each other and there mothers had also worked together at a local bank in the city. Mandy had long, hair down to her shoulder that was a darker red colour. It was black but she died it. Mandy was the party girl and if she got drunk, don’t let her get a hold of anything fragile or anything that you want cause it won’t be there if she gets a hold of it. It was her personality and she didn’t remember what she did the next morning anyways. The last girl, but not least was Brittney Trochier. She was more of a quiet girl. She had short, black hair. She was into books and usually read big, long books that were about nothing at all. Just because she was quiet most of the time, it didn’t mean she was quiet all the time. In fact, she worked as an announcer for the Arena Football Announcer and was very energetic with her job. Brittney was into literature as well and was going to be an author at one point or another in her life. Those were basically my friends, all different kinds with different interests and such and such and such. Now, as I stood outside the door or my six room apartment that me and my friends had shared for the past three years, I couldn’t wait to see them. They knew I was coming home but they didn’t know when so I expected them to be excited to see my face again. I slowly opened the door, turning the knob to my apartment. I could hear faint voices inside, probably the voices of them chatting in the living room. I walked inside, nobody noticed. It was sudden quietness through the whole house like nobody knew if they should attack this person that just opened the door or cheer. All of a sudden, a shadow came forward from the kitchen to the left. “Hello.” I recognized the voice immediately. It was a girls voice. As the girl stepped into the entrance way from the kitchen, I recognized the blonde hair immediately. “Celeste!” She saw me and a smile spread across her face. She wrapped her arms around my shoulder, something that I had messed a lot. After embracing with Celeste briefly, I joined the rest of the group that was waiting for me. Usually men didn’t hug but today was a happy day so I hugged JJ and Justin happily, along with Mandy and Brittney. The smell in the house was that of warmness. We sat down on the couches and talked. Just talked. We talked about RUSH and how it would go and be. We talked about things at home in New York. It was a loving time. We then Barbequed some hamburger’s and ate our faces off, like little kids. After supper, Justin kinda of said it out loud while everyone was in the room to me. He asked. “Yo, Steffen. We all chipped in for a lottery for fun. We were just going to put you in anyways cause we knew you would join but now that you’re here, we can put you in, right?” Justin. He always was spending his money on all those lotteries. He always thought that he would become rich and win it one of these days. Most of us played along with it and joined in with him usually. It was really no big deal I guess. “Yeah, of course you can put me in.” I kinda of side-eyed the others and they looked at me goofy like they knew it was stupid but polite to join anyways. Oh well, Justin got his jollies. “Great!” Justin turn down the hallway to our apartment to go probably to his room. The rest of us just sat there, talking and laughing like we always did. Soon after, Justin came back and joined the conversation. Home Home, sweet, home!
