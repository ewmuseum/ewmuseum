01 - Introducing...Bagwell

24 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 24, 2002 Every man has the opportunity to pave his own pathway through life. The possibilities for the directions in which the pathways are paved remain endless, giving each individual the freedom and power to do whatever they please. It gives each individual a very likely chance to be different and to be the only one with that selected lifetime pathway. However, there always come that rare occassion in which the selected pathway gets cut off. A sudden obsticle obstructs the chosen road causing an unexpected, and in most cases unplanned, change of course. The result of that sudden change varies from being a good move...or one that will later be regret. Its hard to believe that even thought there is new ownership, the same auro still floats within. Yes, I'm speaking about Five Star Wrestling. Years ago, before the closing, the very same environment influxed the federation. I thought that after new ownership took over with a new spawn, things would change. Attitudes, especially. But I guess I was quite wrong. Quite frankly, I'm over it. I met with Rush Mason, the higher up of RUSH Wrestling the night that Adrenaline went head to head with Glory. I knew as soon as Meanstreak walked through the doors of Five Star Wrestling that I was doomed, career-wise. I mean lets face it, they were hiring in all the headliners. The guys who were already on the roster were being pushed to the bottom of the card. Sure, the cards would open great and be well rounded, but who really wants to spend their career open cards? Certainly not me. And I'm more than positive a lot of the original roster feels the same as I do. However, I was the first and so far only one to make the jump. I would not be surprised to see others follow in my footsteps when they see me succeed. Anywho, after speaking with Mr. Mason I was rather impressed at his views of talent and his way of operation. And what better place to jump ship? Its no secret that RUSH Wrestling is sitting at the bottom of the ratings war right now. Why would I care to jump to PCW or OSW when they're in the same position as FSW. It would basically be a repeat of what I tried to escape. RUSH has the potential to be larger than life. RUSH has the potential to walk over PCW, OSW, and FSW. And it won't be long after they do walk straight to the top. I've been pretty eager to step in the ring, lately. I've taken a whole new approach on things. I'm not going to do this nice guy crap any longer. The hell with partnerships or friendships in the back. The hell with satisfying other people. I made friends in Five Star Wrestling and what happened? They turned their backs on me when I needed them the most. It may not have seemed so apparent on television, the alliances and all. But thats because we were friendly backstage. I gave everyone a fair shot at friendship. None of them helped me to save Rebecca. None of them stopped me from going over the edge. None of them saved me from... Meanstreak. So the hell with them. People always expect you to be there for them but they never even think twice about being there for you. I would have put my body and well being on the line to save any of their asses. Obviously the feeling wasn't mutual. Whatever, I'm over it. I'm well over it. Theres always the 'fans'. Good for nothing, back stabbing, low life, sons of bitches! They simply go along with what the media feeds them. Sure, I had their support when things began. But what did it transpire into? As they were fed a new superstar, they got choked up on his ego and tended to forget about the people who were responsible for rebuilding the foundation of Five Star Wrestling. Hopefully they all choke on that and die. Thats all they're filled with these days. Egotistical freaks. FSW, that is. I can't wait to watch them crumble. All those egos and only one championship title. We'll see just how long FSW's power trip lasts. Someone has to get that title soon. And only one of them can hold onto it. Lets just watch and see what happens after one man is named champion. So its been a bit of time since I last was in Five Star Wrestling. How great it does feel. I got a somewhat significant raise in yearly pay. That's a definite plus. I was walking around the house the other night, skimming through the contract that me and Mason agreed upon. I figured I better read the small print to ensure that I'm not going to be screwed out of a job or something of that nature. I didn't find anything which seemed anything like those terms, but I did find something else that caught a glimpse of my eye. It was something of a promise. A promise of spotlight if I showed the appropriate skill in the ring. It went into detail about how I'd be assessed but I really wasn't in the mood to go in depth with the details. I'm not much of a reader. Normally, I'd have Tommy go through it and read all the specifics then give me a summary of everything. He's good with that kind of stuff. I guess thats kind of why I keep him tagging along. But I haven't seen his little ass in quite some time. I honestly have no idea where he disappeared to. Ever since we left the building on the 12th, during Glory, he's basically been out of sight. I know that he didn't follow me to meet with Mason. I know that as a fact. He apparently had wanted to get home and he's not the type of person to act all shady. Perhaps I should give him a call to make sure the little fucker is alright. But that would be putting in more of an effort than I'd want. I'm actually pretty eager, never the less, to brag about the jump. I know he'll share the joy with me to have escaped that hell hole. Even if he isn't excited, he'll at least act it! A good kid, he is! Yet that still doesn't fuel me to get off my ass and check on him. I'm sure he can handle himself. He's a big boy. And if he can't handle himself ... well then I guess he's fucked, now isn't he? But quite frankly, I'm over that too. Fact of the matter is that I've been fucked many times before in my life too. And I do not mean sexually. The most apparent rapings being the RPGod Tournament and even Five Star Wrestling, by my so called 'allies'. God damn self-centered bastards. If theres one thing I can't stand, it is bandwagoners. The people who jump to the hot commodity and never stick with one thing. Prime example of how I was disgraced and brought to new lows. But I'm away from those hell holes. The only hell left that I plan to deal with is the hell in which I bring upon other men. Speaking of which, I just got a call from Mr. Mason telling me that I get to debut in the ring against Tex Kelley and Matt Elia. As if Kelley didn't get enough of me on my unofficial debut! I sure as hell made an example out of that bastard. And I'm sure glad of that. There is no better way to get yourself noticed besides taking out the icons. And Kelley just happened to be in the wrong place at the wrong time. Sucks to be him, now doesn't it? And it's sure as hell going to suck more after I unleash all the rage bottled up inside of me this Friday. Let it be known and let it be sworn... ...someone WILL be TORN! End.
