Going to the Disturbed Concert...

Date: Today (Our scene fades in to show “The Innovator” Brock Newbludd’s jet black 1969 Boss 429 Mustang careening down a highway in a location unbeknownst to us. It is quite cold and windy out telling us that we are probably somewhere outside of Milwaukee. We watch as the classic American muscle car weaves in and out of heavy traffic, not slowing down for anything. The car bobs in and out of lanes in a manner that would make any professional racecar driver envious. The car then passes under a sign that indicates where we are; the sign reads, “Now Entering Chicago City Limits”.As the car continues to speed along, we zoom in closer on it, until we are nearly inside the car, then the camera feed cuts out for a instant and now we are inside of the Mustang. We are in the perspective, as if we were sitting in the backseat of the car. The first thing we notice is the adrenaline pumping sounds of “Turn Me On Mr. Deadman” by The Union Underground blaring through the cars juiced up stereo system. Sitting in the passenger’s seat is Brock’s wife and former RollerJam star, Lindsey Francis, who is looking as good as ever, almost back to her sultry shape after having Brock’s baby, Brock Jr. Lindsey is clad in a pair of tight black leather pants with black high heels. A tight fitting yellow t-shirt with “Sex Machine” written across the chest of it shows off her quite supple “assets”. Her blond hair is held up in a ponytail, while her blue eyes are covered up by a pair of Oakleys sunglasses. She sits, flipping through the latest issue of PTC: The Magazine. We pan over to see driving the muscle car is not surprisingly the cars owner, PCW legend and Rush superstar “The Inno-F*ckin’-Vator” Brock Newbludd. Brock sits clad in a pair of baggy blue jeans with Doc Marten boots. A tight fitting plain white t-shirt shows off his chiseled physique. Brock’s crystal blue eyes are hidden behind a pair of red and black Oakleys. We see that he has a can of Old Milwaukee beer in the hand he’s steering with, and a cigarette hangs from his top lip. Brock bobs his head to the music, as he weaves in and out of traffic at a harrowing rate of speed.…) Lindsey: I can’t believe you got tickets to a Disturbed concert! (Brock shrugs his shoulders casually…) Brock: Wasn’t that hard, Stephen’s agent hooked me up with them. Lindsey: You’re such a sweetheart. (Brock flashes a confident smile at his wife…) Brock: I can’t believe Stephen agreed to babysit little Brock, while we went to the concert, he loves Disturbed! (Lindsey grows a quirky little grin that Brock notices…) Lindsey: Well, Stacey really likes little Brock and well… (Brock grows a inquisitive look on his face…) Brock: Yeah, so? Lindsey: Well, Stacey wants a baby now too… (Brock burst out in laughter and almost drives off of the road. Lindsey looks at Brock angrily obviously flustered…) Lindsey: What’s so funny, huh? (Brock takes a sip of his beer, still chuckling…) Brock: Stephen? A dad? HAHAHAHA! Lindsey: I think he would be a good dad! (Brock bursts out in laughter again, this time almost choking, he stops once Lindsey hits him in the shoulder…) Brock: I’m sorry, I’ll stop…(Chuckles a little bit…) Lindsey: How long till we get to the concert, anyway? (Brock looks at the clock on his CD player…) Brock: About twenty minutes, just enough time for a quick promo! Lindsey: Huh? (Brock reaches under his seat and pulls out s small camera, it almost looks like a webcam…) Lindsey: You just HAD to bring that along didn’t you! Brock: Yep! (Brock sticks the webcam on the dash of the car and pulls a small antenna from it and plugs it into the cigarette lighter. The camera flashes for a second and then a green light comes on for a second…) Brock: Hello fans, welcome to what’s sure to be another kick ass promo by the man who always puts out kick-ass promos, “The Inno-F*ckin’-Vator” Speaking of promos, lets talk about Ricky Starrs. Those have to be the most mindless f*cking things I have ever watch. I would rather watch educational videos than watch that sh*t. I mean, c’mon, the guys doesn’t even mention his opponent in them. What did he forget he’s a wrestler and that’s what the whole point of a promo is? I dunno, the guy sure is a f*cking pissclam. (Brock lights up a cigarette and continues…) Brock: It kinda really pisses me off that he doesn’t mention. What, does he think that I’m not important enough to be mentioned? That he’ll do all his talking in the ring? I’m the “Inno-F*ckin’-Vator” and I do all the talking in the ring, period! That little f*ckin bastard might think that I’m one to be ignored, but he won’t be thinking at all when I punch his teeth through the back of his head. Ricky Starr, you’ve picked the wrong man to piss off and all you did was be quiet. Your ass is in some deep sh*t now, because you’re going be just a mat stain when I’m done with ya’. And Lindsey, what is that? Lindsey: Sad but TRUE! (Brock finishes off his beer can and throws it out of the window as the camera shuts off, we watch the cans flight as it lands on the road behind the Mustang. We slowly fade to black on the beer can as the roar of the muscle cars engine slowly fades away…)
