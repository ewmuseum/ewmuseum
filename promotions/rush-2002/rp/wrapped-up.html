Wrapped Up

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: we'll show you The camera shot fades in on what seems to be a nightclub, back in what seems to be New York. The crowd is hopping, bouncing to the beat of something. I don’ know what it is. Maybe techno. Look, fuck you, I don’t have to give you every goddamned detail of where we are. Use your goddamned imagination. Anyway, through the crowd of, uh, people, one man seems to stand out the most. Dressed in street clothes, the 6’8 frame of Livewire pushes his way through the drug-ridden clubbers. He doesn’t look very happy with his situation at the time, and does his best not to create a scene while forcing a path to a door near the back, with “VIP” printed in bold letters upon it. He signals to the bouncer at the door, and the guard lets him pass through. Inside the room, a few scantily clad women mill about, having their fill of drinks and grinding to Eminem’s “Without Me” as it blares from speakers above. The crowd parts, however, at the sight of the massive Brit. Let’s just say that L-Dub ain’t exactly someone you make Austin Powers or bad teeth jokes around. The Red Sea-esque division of the crowd exposes the center of the room, and the center of attention. Amidst a slew of promiscuous women, available drinks, and a private room… …is El Spiffy!, playing FIFA World Cup Soccer 2002 on Xbox. Next to him, in the understandable position of “Player 2”, is the enigmatic RoboGates. The Spanish-announce duo of Juan Carlos Smith and Julio Diaz Perez O’Neil sit at the private bar, each holding a rather empty glass, with a bottle of Jack Daniels between them. As always, their faces are less than amicable. They raise their glasses at Livewire as he approaches. He nods in return. Livewire walks up behind the videogamers, and clears his throat loudly. Not surprisingly, they continue to jam the buttons. Well, ok, El Spiffy! jams on the buttons. RoboGates taps them with a methodical perfection…being an android and all. The luchadore remains excited, however, eagerly pounding his controller, jumping up on the couch he’s sitting on in an attempt to gain leverage. “Viva La Senegal!” El Spiffy! shouts, oblivious to his partner behind him. “Viva La Senegal!” Livewire clears his throat again, still without a response. Finally, RoboGates turns his head to acknowledge the visitor….giving El Spiffy! just enough time to lob a kick in underneath his goalie’s arms. “GOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAALLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLL!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!” In a scream that echoes the Univision commentators (or Juan Borro, if you ever followed him…I didn’t, sorry), El Spiffy! leaps up off the couch, pulls his SeanJohn shirt over his masked head, and begins running around in circles, arms outstretched. The rest of the room just sort of watches in a awed/horrified shock. This act comes to a screeching halt, however, when the Spiffster runs headlong into a wall…a wall known as Livewire. He bumps, and falls back like a child, frantically pulling his shirt back on to see what interrupted his celebration. Upon freeing himself, he sees LW, and immediately jumps back to his feet. “Hey everybody! Livewire’s here!” Most of the braindead occupants of the VIP room murmur in apathy. El Spiffy! turns to look at Juan Carlos and Julio. “Why didn’t you tell me Livewire was here?” Neither man responds. Instead they pour another round. El Spiffy! ignores them anyway and turns back to his partner. “What is it that is up, Liveywire? Come to play World Cup? El Spiffy! calls Senegal!” He turns to vault over the couch and back into the game, but a hand on his shoulder stops him. “We need to talk business, Spiffster,” Livewire says grimly. “Shack Pack business.” El Spiffy!’s face turns slightly serious. “The Shack Pack deal is over. Burger King discontinued it.” “You know what I mean.” “The Shack Pack deal is over.” El Spiffy! responds quietly. “So what the hell are we now?” “You could call yourselves the Backporch Grillers!” shouts Juan Carlos. “Homestyle Cheddar!” chimes in Julio. Livewire does not seem amused. El Spiffy!, as usual, is oblivious. “El Spiffy! will take care of everything, Liveywire. You want play soccer now?” “I think we should talk strategy. We’ve got a match on Thursday that pretty much everyone expects us to lose.” “El Spiffy! is working the strategy! If Senegal moves up a defender, it strengthens the attack on the Gates’ robot French!” Livewire furrows his brow at the luchadore and looks past him to the announcers. “Does he always prepare like this?” “You’re lucky he’s even working his thumbs.” Trying his best to keep a calm head, but still cursing Shackelton for leaving him in this position, Livewire continues to question El Spiffy!. “Fine, we can talk about that later. But we still need to do group planning…Where’s Joe Bananas?” El Spiffy! looks confused at the name. “Joe who?” “Joe Bananas.” It still doesn’t ring a bell. El Spiffy! swings around to look at the commentators. Juan Carlos sighs loudly and puts down his drink glass. “Joe. They guy from NTICW that joined the Shack Pack.” El Spiffy! doesn’t recognize it. “He joined on Thursday, at Adrenaline.” Still staring. “You were there.” Placing his hands on his hips in a decidedly “wait a damn second” manner, El Spiffy! turns back to Livewire, drawing a blank. He opens his mouth to speak, then turns back to Juan Carlos. “The black guy?” Sigh. “Although I’m almost positive he would disapprove of such a horrid generalization, it’s true that Joe is a Jamaican.” Pause. “The black guy?” “Yes. The black guy.” Content with his identification, El Spiffy! turns back to Livewire with a beaming smile, one that is not reflected by anyone who just heard that exchange. “El Spiffy! will talk to him later. Right now is time for SENEGAL!” With that he turns and hops over the couch, simultaneously kicking the metal head off of RoboGates. It clangs to the floor as El Spiffy! grabs his controller and goes back to work vigorously pounding the buttons into submission. With great patience, Livewire looks up at the commentators once again. “You gentlemen will contact me when he’s ready to have a conversation that means something right?” They raise their glasses once again. “Terrific. I’m going back to my hotel room. I don’t need any more of this garbage.” He turns and makes his way back through the small crowd of the VIP room. As he disappears, the voice of El Spiffy! can be heard shouting above the cacophony. “Don’t worry, Liveywire! El Spiffy! has a plan!” And we fade.
