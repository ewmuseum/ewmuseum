Cleaning Out Paul Pluto's Closet

29 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Oct 29, 2002 It was a bright, hot and muggy day in Lemmon, South Dakota, as it always was. All two hundred and fifty people in the small town came out and greeted each other, in their drunken hillbilly manner. Of course, they all ran back into their shoddy little homes, as a high pitched, and girly scream rang out of the powder blue house on the corner. The shriek continued to ring out, over and over. The residents of the small town, slowly peeked out their windows and doors, looking at the house, wondering if Ned Witte, or Scott Minks were back in town. The screaming, of course, was from the man known as Paul Pluto. He sat there in horrified disbeleif, as The Masked Mullet, and his red neck crew, who were carefully pulling down poster, and items of memorabilia from the walls. Paul Pluto: Why are you doing this? He screeched, as he ran around in circles, trying to stop each man. TMM: Whe'll.. in RUSH, we don' take too kindly to those FSWers. Paul Pluto: So, why are you taking it out on my John Jupiter memorabilia?! TMM just slowly popped a beer, and glanced at his friend. He gave a slight shrug. TMM: Sylkk told me to? Billy Conners: ...You.. still.. believe.. Sylkk? TMM: Ain't ne'er steered me wrong be'fer. Mullet slowly turned, and ripped a signed John Jupiter poster off the wall, crumpling it up, and tossing it into the garbage can. Paul Pluto: NOOOOOOOOOO! Tears broke from the frail eyes of Paul, as he fell to his knees, as the jagged edges of sorrow stabbed into his John Jupiter loving heart, but he gasped soon, and tried to stop Crazy Migs, from gnawing any more on the John Jupiter cut out. Bobby Connely: Well... Clem.. (he took a long breath)Why are ya' still trustin' that Sylkk feller? TMM: Well, he was Cianan's friend, ah' reckin'. Ah'm sure he probably has a plan, tha's why he's been makin' such a ruckus. He nodded slowly, and scratched his cheek, as he tossed a limited edition John Jupiter, PCW Extreme Champion, action figure. Paul shrieked, and ran, diving into the garbage can to save it. Bobby Connely: Ah' dunno Clem.. Bobby stopped to scratch his head, tilting it to the side, to look at his masked friend, a sigh passign through his lips. Bobby Connely: Last Adrenaline, was the second time he beat'cha up. I dunno if he's exactly on yer' side. TMM: Ah'm sure he is! We're both from CC-Dub after all! Just like Josh Marshall! Ah know that ah can trust em'. Clem said with firm resolve, nodding his head once, firmly. Bobby Connely: But'cha couldn't trust Cianan! Anyways.. Where's Skillz? Ah' thought he wanted ta help with this? Clem shrugged his shoulders, and ran his hand through the short part of his might, might, mullet. TMM: Ah, ya know Skillz, he wanted to do sompthan' Canadian. So he went ta go hang out with Chris Matthew's again. Paul stops his frantic running about to stop, and look at his friends, placing his hands on his hips. Paul Pluto: He isn't going to try and make Matthews breakfast in bed again, is he? TMM: Yeah, ah think so. Ta make up fer the other night.. Paul Pluto just sighs, and starts preparing his lecture. Paul Pluto: Now guys, I think we should go.. Paul Pluto is cut off by the sound of a monsterous.. RIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIP Paul shrieks in a girlish fashion, and finally loses it, passing out. The Masked Mullet just sighs, holding up a limited edition John Jupiter comic book, and nods his head once. TMM: Ah really didn' wanna hear a lecture. With that, The Masked Mullet continues on through the day, destroying any traces of John Jupiter stuff in Paul Pluto's house. Still continuing to believe in his 'friends,' and trusting in what was good with America. Oh, and I made it through the whole RP with out calling Paul Pluto, PP! Yay for me!
