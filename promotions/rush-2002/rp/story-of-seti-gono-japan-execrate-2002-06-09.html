Story of Seti Gono: Japan: Execrate

9 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 9th, 2002 A.D. My eyes stirred open with a start, but I don’t really feel conscious. My head aching after my loss, my loss in the Wrestling God tournament the other day left me hollow, like a part of my soul had been twisted ripped out of me. Like it was being cut out of me and stepped on, soon to be thrown in the trash. I am not used to losing, especially on such a large scale of a match so importance. I flew back to America just for this, and for what? A loss. And just like I came out losing on this week’s edition of Adrenaline, this sixth to be exact. My so called stable mate, Cassius Deamont, turning his back on me after I made ‘advances’ toward his two cheap corner sluts, that bastard. My head is aching, two consecutive beatings within two days of each other, the last was in my hometown. Toronto is a town where I am proud to be from, and in front of my friends and foes I am treated like dirt from a man I can only call evil. Cassius Deamont is evil, in my eyes; he is Satan, Lucifer and Azrael. He has a black heart, a heart which I will destroy in one week, and claim my throne as champion of the world. He treated me like dirt in Toronto, the town where I seek residence after I ran away from Montreal all those years ago.... December 17, 1995 A.D. As I walked down the footpath, my school slung over my shoulder, I had no care in the world. School had just finished for the Christmas holidays. My two friends walking on my side as we casually walk down the path, my house only a few blocks away and there’s just a short distance as well. “Man” said my friend Damien, on my left. “I can’t wait for Christmas this year!” I chuckle as my other friend, Scott, on my right sighs. Poor Scott, his family isn’t one of the richest. “I hate Christmas” I spits out. Both Damien and I snicker at Scott’s remarks as we turned a corner, my house now coming into view. “Yeah, there’s my house over there so I’ll see you two later” Damien said, point to the large two-story house on the corner. Scott ran up to Damien. “Wait” he said, “I’m coming with you, and you’ve got my Science Book.” I watched as Damien and Scott turned right down a long tree filled road and head toward Damien’s massive house, my pace began to pick up as I walked toward my house at the end of the street. “Excellent” I thought, “that ‘brother’ of mine won’t be home for a few hours, TV access ahoy!” I started to walk up our driveway, passing both my parent’s cars. I suddenly hear yelling coming from inside my house. My eyebrows fold together cringing at what I will walk into see. “Oh god...” I mouthed out loud, “Dad’s drunk again.” With a slow and cautious walk my feet barely making a sound, I slowly creped up to the front door. I twist the handle slowly and quietly as to go unrecognised by my arguing parents. The door slams behind me as I walk in, the yelling seems to be coming from the kitchen. “SHUT UP!” yells my father. “JUST SHUT YOUR MOUTH UP NOW BITCH!” I hear crying. My mother’s desperate cries for help and release. “PLEASE!” she yells back, her voice hoarse through her cries. “STOP!” The yelling and the cries began to grow louder as I slide along the white walls in the hallway of my home, a shadow looming at my feet from my body rest on the white, pearly white walls of my home. That’s right, my home. My head leans out, peeking into the kitchen to see what has transpired. What I see is a scene that shall haunt me for the rest of my life. A scene, which I dream about like no other. My father, him in his drunken state, his large Bear like hands wrapped around my petite mothers throat. Asphyxiating her, cutting her air supply off, trying to kill her. Trying to make my mother away from my sisters, and I. But my brother Andrew can go to hell. Acting without thinking I leap into the kitchen, throwing my backpack onto the kitchen. It skids away, slamming into a mirror stand in one of the four corners. My dad’s head snaps over to me. “STOP IT!” I scream at my father, “LEAVE MOM ALONE!” His eyes, I’ve never seen such fire and rage before. His drunken state of mind not all there, his hands still tight around my mother’s throat, her life fading away.... “What did you say?!” he yelled at me, letting go of my mother’s throat at last. Her breath’s are heaving as she staggers away. “I said leave Mom alone!” I yell back, my fists clenched tightly. Anger subsiding and flowing through my veins like electricity. My father scoffs, a drunken scoff of course. “What are you gonna do to me?” He spits back out, literally spitting on the floor. He lets out a loud belch; I think I smelt it from over here. My Dad looks back down to my Mom, an evil grin spreading across his face. He walks over to her and puts his hands around her delicate neck. “You wanna protect her like a mommies boy?” He spits at me, my anger and rage rising in my blood and heart. “Then I’m gonna make you sit through this!” Dad locked his hands around Mom’s neck again, her desperate gasps for air, our elixir of life, almost tearing me up inside. Tears began to appear in my eyes as my mother fights to live from my childhood devil, Dad. “Chr-is” she struggles out. “help me...” He smiles a wicked and evil smile at me, like he was enjoying this. “You’re gonna watch your precious mother DIE!” He tightened his grip around her neck, choking more and more air out of her lungs. My fists begin to shake with anger, rage and hate for my father.... the man who spawned my brother. I can feel it flowing through my body, like a ticking time bomb waiting to explode.... “NOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!” I yell out at him, his eyes snapping back to me. I lunge forward, his fists tight and my arms out stretched. As I reach my evil father I slam my arm straight into his head. My father in his drunken state almost collapses into the stove.... His words chilling, I looked on in horror as my father got back to his feet and my mother lay unconscious on the floor. His last words to me before I ran away, words which I remember every day of my life. “GET OUT OF MY HOUSE NOW YOU FILTHY GOOD FOR NOTHING BRAT!” Suddenly I see a face in front of me, long strands of brown hair dangle in front of me. Black eyelash’s blink, once, twice and again. A smile spreads across her face as I awake from a dream? “Bad dream” she asks me, I blink as I get back up, blinking a few times. I start to walk across the room where I had ‘slept’; the door opens as I turn my head back to her. “You don’t want to know,” I tell her coldly. The rest of that story is even worse.....
