The Muscle Car Emporium

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered (The scene fades in to show ex-PCW superstar, “The New Legned” Stephen Blood’s1971 fire red Dodge Charger pull into the parking lot of “High Society Muscle Car Emporium” in suburban Milwaukee on a bright sunny March morning. The camera pans around to set the scene for this promo. We see that this is a fairly small car dealership, with a small number of cars. But, oddly enough, most of the cars here look to be mint condition muscle cars ranging from the mid 1960’s to the late 1980’s. Everything from Chevrolet Corvettes to Plymouth Roadrunners are sold here. A few people are walking around the parking lot, checking out these beasts of cars. Upon further inspection we see that most of these people are from the more “privileged” south side of Milwaukee and are probably car collectors or looking for a toy to mess around with on weekends. They look over the cars meticulously making sure there are no imperfections on them like dings in the paint or other minor things. Most of these people are dressed in sharp business suits or flashy sport coats of some sort. Which would make the two men stick out like a sore thumb with their usual garb of leather jackets and blue jeans. We can see that is a fairly warm March day, causing the mounds of snow to melt a little, forming pools of water here and there. There are blue tarps put around the cars that are outside sitting in the lot, so that water doesn’t form under them. We pan back to the Blood Bullet, which is still running in the parking space. The car sits running for a few more seconds before shutting off, killing the low growl that the idling engine produces. Then, almost simultaneously the passenger and drivers side doors fly open. The first thing we see step out is not what you except, not a Blood Brother, but their pet wolf Skoll. The wolf jumps out of the passenger’s side and tromps around on the snowy parking lot before sitting down on the ground waiting for Brock and Stephen. Then, “The Innovator” Brock Newbludd steps out of the passengers side door, beer in hand. While Stephen Blood steps out of the driver’s side door, beer in hand. Upon closer examination we see that Brock is clad in a pair of baggy fitting blue jeans with his trademark Doc Marten boots. A Blood Brothers: “Kick Ass and Drink Beer” T-shirt can be seen underneath his black leather Harley jacket. His usually spiked brown hair is concealed by a Wisconsin Badgers baseball cap that sits backwards on his head. He gets out and shuts the door behind him. Brock pats Skoll on the head and takes a drink of his beer. Stepping out from the drivers side door is “The New F*cking Legend” Stephen Blood, dressed in a pair of baggy black denim jeans with biker boots. The same Blood Brothers shirt that Brock is wearing can be seen underneath his leather jacket. His shaven head is covered with a black and red skulls and roses bandana. Stephen gets out and shut the door behind him. Then him and Brock followed closely by Skoll walk up the front steps of the car dealership and enter the main building.) (The camera cuts back to show the two men and Skoll walking through the sliding doors of the dealership and strolling up to the front desk where a beautiful looking red headed secretary sits shuffling through papers, not even noticing the PCW superstars stroll up to the desk. As Brock and Stephen stroll up, the camera pans around to set the scene. We see that that are inside of the large floor showroom are much nicer and much rarer than the ones that are inside. The same snobby rich people that were outside checking out the cars are in here also. There are about fifteen or so cars parked in the showroom, each one shining and sparkling more then the next one. The salesmen that wander throughout the building, haggling potential buyers are sharply dressed and always greet the potential buyers with that cheesy 'I’m your friend, NOW BUY MY CAR!” smile. The camera shifts back to show The Blood Brothers standing in front of the desk with Skoll sitting behind him licking his paws. The secretary hasn’t noticed them standing there yet, so Brock shrugs his shoulders and pounds his fists on the desk, making the secretary nearly fall out of her comfy office chair. The young woman composes herself and gives The Blood Brothers a fake grin.) Secretary: Hi, welcome to the “High Society Muscle Car Emporium”, home of the finest and rarest cars in all of the Midwest. My name is Cindy and how may I help you gentlemen? Stephen: Um, yeah, my bro here wants to buy a car. Brock: Yeah, no cheap s*it either! I crashed the last one you douches gave me! (The young secretary is aghast from Brock profanity laced comment…) Cindy: Um…well, just take a look around the showroom, one of our fine salesmen will help you with any questions you have. Stephen: Thanks. (Cindy notices the large wolf Skoll sitting behind the Blood Brothers and she raises her eyebrows at this…) Cindy: There are no animals allowed on the showroom floor. Stephen: That’s fine.(Looks at Skoll) You stay here and don’t cause any trouble now. (The wolf lets out a agreeing growl and lays down on the welcome mat, letting out a yawn…) Stephen: Lets go Brock. Brock: Ok. (Brock turns and faces the beautiful secretary and gives her a cocky smile) Ya know, Cindy, if you’re not doing anything later I’m sure I can give you a spin on the new wheels I’m buying today. Maybe I’ll let you have a ride on something else… (Stephen smacks Brock in the back of the head causing a loud “SMACK” to be heard…) Stephen: You’re married to Lindsey now, remember! Brock: Oh, right… Stephen: Now c’mon, lets go get you some wheels so I don’t have to drag you drunk ass all over the place anymore. I can’t believe you crashed your ride! Brock: Yeah, ok. (Brock and Stephen walk off into the showroom. We see that the secretary’s eyes don’t leave Brock as him and Stephen walk into the showroom. They stop in the middle of the showroom and survey all the cars in it. Brock looks over Corvettes, Bel Airs, Mustangs and all other types of muscle cars. Brock looks each car carefully, seeing which ones he likes. Brock’s eyes then settle and lock on to one car, the one sitting in the corner of the show room. The car that Brock’s eyes gaze on is a 1969 Jet Black Boos 429 Mustang with tinted windshields and a sticker across the windshield that reads “Terror Personified” in blood red print. Brock grows a grin and points at the car…) Brock: Look at that f*ckin ride dude! (Stephen looks at the car and frowns…) Stephen: Um, dude, that looks just like the car that you had before.(Brock nods his head in a excited fashion, not putting his finger down from the car…) Brock: I know! What are the odds!? Stephen: Don’t you want something different though? (Brock rolls his eyes in a exaggerated manner towards Stephen…) Brock: No! Look at that car, it’s f*ckin’ sweet! It’s black and bad-assed! I mean, you wouldn’t see a little fairy like Mike Storm driving something like that would you? (Stephen opens his mouth to speak, but Brock cuts him off right away…) Brock: No! You wouldn’t it! Mikey boy probably rides around on a pink moped, or a tricycle or something. (Stephen starts laughing and high fives Brock. Then, one of the greedy looking salesmen wearing a nice suit with his thinning blond hair slicked back walks up to the two. He grows a frown as he looks Brock and Stephen up and down…) Salesman: Excuse me…gentlemen, but the openings for mechanics have been all filled up, but there are a couple of openings for janitors, the applications are on the front desk… (Brock and Stephen stare at the salesmen with blank looks on their faces. They look at each other and then look back at the smiling salesmen. Brock puts a finger on the mans chest and grins…) Brock: Do we look like f*cking janitors or mechanics? Do we look poor? (The salesman smiles meekly…) Salesman: What else would you be here for, certainly not looking for car. Stephen: That’s exactly what we’re doing buttcheese. (The salesmen gets a confused look on his face…) Salesman: You do know that most of the these automobiles here are above the fifty thousand dollar price range. (Brock gets a irritated look on his face…) Brock: Listen dick, we can afford all of the cars here, and I already know which one I want. (Brock points at the Mustang…) Brock: We’re getting that one, and I just have one question. (The salesman puts his hands on his hips defiantly…) Salesman: What would that be? (Brock digs through his jacket pocket and pulls out a large roll of money bills with one hand and a credit card with the other. The salesman gets a surprised look on his face…) Brock: Do you want paper or plastic? (The sly salesman grows a dumb grin…) Salesman: Heh…plastic will do… (Our scene slowly dissolves out and we fade back in to show Brock and Stephen sitting inside of the new Mustang outside of the car lot. Brock sits in the drivers seat and Stephen in the passenger’s seat. The same salesman from before stands by Brock on the outside of the car, handing him some papers and documents….) Salesman: Thanks fellas, its been a pleasure doing business with you. (Brock rolls his eyes at the man as he shoves the papers the salesman gave him in the glovebox. Stephen straightens up and looks around…) Stephen: Where’s Skoll? Brock: I dunno… (The two look around before Stephen spots the large canine sleeping under a car…) Stephen: Skoll! Come on, boy! (Skoll looks up and sees Stephen, he gets up and runs towards him. He makes it to the car and stops suddenly. The large wolf puts his hind leg up and proceeds to urinate on the salesman leg before jumping in the backseat of the car. Brock laughs at the salesman and gives him the middle finger…) Brock: Smell ya later jackass! (We fade out as Brock slams on the gas pedal and the muscle car tears out of the car lot, leaving the salesman with a abashed look on his face and a wet leg…)
